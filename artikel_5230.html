Arthur Hayes is back on the VVV radar, but the transaction needs careful wording. He reportedly received 15,000 VVV tokens worth approximately $306,000 from Flowdesk, three days after predicting the token could reach $40.

That transfer has attracted attention as VVV rebounds from a key technical support area. Still, receiving tokens isn’t automatically the same as buying them on the open market.

The reported 15,000-token transfer is worth around $306,000 at the stated valuation. Hayes had previously outlined a $40 target for VVV.

The timing has fuelled speculation about his market positioning. But the transfer alone doesn’t establish whether he purchased the tokens, received them for another reason, or intends to hold them.

VVV reportedly reversed near its 200-day EMA around $17.14, a level that also aligns with the lower boundary of an ascending wedge.

The recovery has since pushed the token above its 50-day EMA band, trading above $22 according to the chart analysis.

The next upside levels are $26 and $35. A stronger rally could bring the $40 target into focus, although none of these levels is guaranteed.

The VVV price bullish setup still has a clear risk. If selling pressure overwhelms buyers, $21.34 is the next important support level. A break below it could expose the $17.14 region again.

For now, the Arthur Hayes VVV transfer has renewed attention around the token, while its technical recovery offers a possible path toward higher targets. But the $40 prediction remains a target, not a confirmed destination.
