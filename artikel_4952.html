The New Apple Watch Ultra 4 Is at Its Lowest Price Ever Right Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Plus other fitness trackers we like on sale.

If you’ve been waiting for a deal on an iconic premium fitness tracker that does it all, the Apple Watch Ultra 4 is worth checking out. Right now, the 49mm GPS + Cellular model with a natural titanium case and translucent gray ocean band is currently $100 off at Amazon (it’s lowest price ever), but this deal won’t last long.

The new Ultra 4 is designed for those who want much more than a basic activity tracker. It combines health and fitness features with a large display, cellular connectivity, GPS, and a battery that can keep going for more than two days between charges, depending on how you use it.

In her comparison of the Ultra 4 and Series 12 for Women’s Health, associate health and fitness editor Addison Aloian found that the larger screen made it easier to read texts and check workout stats while running or lifting. The Ultra 4 also has an extra button she used to pause and resume workouts without having to swipe through the screen.

Longer battery life is another highlight of the Ultra 4. It’s rated for up to 50 hours of normal use, compared with up to 24 hours for the Series 12. Aloian reported that the Ultra 4 hadn’t died on her during testing, and noted that it charged quickly. According to Apple, actual battery life will vary depending on which settings you’re using.

The smartwatch offers an impressive suite of features such as heart rate tracking, sleep tracking, cycle tracking, and a readiness score. The Ultra 4 has Apple’s S11 chip and a health-sensing system designed to improve heart rate tracking. Its Readiness feature combines fitness, vitals, and sleep data into a score from 1 to 10, intended to help you gauge how prepared you are for physical effort that day.

For outdoor adventures, the Ultra 4 also offers precision dual-frequency GPS and Emergency SOS via satellite, making any excursion safer. One potential trade-off is size: Aloian found the 49mm watch bulky on her small wrist at bedtime, even though she loved the larger screen during the day.

So if you want a spacious display, longer battery life, and an Apple Watch that’s ready for just about anything, check out the deal happening on the Ultra 4 right now on Amazon, just in time to get a head start on holiday shopping.
