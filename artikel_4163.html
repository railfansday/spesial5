Brawlhalla set to collaborate with My Hero Academia as Izuki Midoriya and co join the brawl

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Say what you like about Brawlhalla, but Ubisoft's Smash-like fighting game has chugged along quite nicely whereas other crossover-focused fighters like Multiversus have flagged. Now, Brawlhalla is reaching outside their usual crop of guest fighters to bring in the cast of none other than My Hero Academia!

Set to launch December 9th, the Brawhalla x My Hero Academia crossover features the hotblooded Katsuki Bakugo, coldblooded Shoto Todoroki and, of course, Izuki Midoriya aka Deku himself as brand-new brawlers. Each boast abilities based on their Quirks as shown in Horikoshi's hit superhero manga.

As for Brawlhalla, I wasn't kidding when I gave it praise for outlasting Multiversus. And I think part of that is the fact their increasing number of crossover fighters actually have some relevance to the world of gaming and brawling in general. That, and the fact they already had a solid core roster of original fighters means that the novelty of crossover characters remains just that; novel.

In contrast, when Midoriya, Todoroki and Bakugo arrive in My Hero Academia come December 9th, they'll be bringing exciting abilities and fighting styles that'll fit right in with the rest of the Brawlhalla cast already!
