Flamelit Marks Five Years of Turning AI Into Operational Capability

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Company evolves from AI advisory into an AI Operations Accelerator that embeds practical intelligence into the work teams do every day

HOUSTON, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Global Clean Energy, Inc. (OTCID: GCEI), an AI-powered resilience and sustainable-infrastructure company, today announces Flamelit's five-year anniversary of helping organizations move beyond AI experimentation and put artificial intelligence to work in daily operations. What began as advanced AI and data-science consulting has evolved into the company's AI Operations Accelerator which is a repeatable approach for identifying a high-value workflow, deploying an AI-enabled capability, and improving it against agreed business outcomes.

Flamelit's work reflects a central challenge facing organizations adopting AI: a compelling demonstration does not automatically become a better business process. Companies often need to connect AI to live data, existing systems, clear decision rights, and the people accountable for results before value can be realized at scale.

From Advice to DeliveryOver the past five years, Flamelit's role has expanded from advising leaders on AI strategy to taking responsibility for delivering and improving operational capabilities. Early Fractional Chief AI Officer engagements helped clients set priorities, establish governance, and align leadership around AI opportunities.

Client work then revealed a recurring delivery gap: even a strong model or recommendation still required reliable data, integration with the systems people already use, a team able to act on the output, and ongoing support. That experience shaped the AI Operations Accelerator, which begins with one workflow and an agreed outcome, then builds and improves the capability through real use.
