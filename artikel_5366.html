U.S. weekly jobless claims edged up to 203,000, a second week above 200,000

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Initial claims were revised up to 202,000 for the prior week, and continuing claims rose 70,000 to 1.763 million

The Labor Department reported that initial jobless claims rose by 1,000 to a seasonally adjusted 203,000 in the week ending Nov. 14. Economists in a Reuters survey were expecting a figure of 197,000.

The department revised the prior week's level up by 2,000 to 202,000 from 200,000. That puts claims above 200,000 for two straight weeks. The four-week moving average was 197,750, up 2,000 from the prior week's revised average of 195,750.

Before seasonal adjustment, states recorded 197,492 initial claims, a decrease of 11,941, or 5.7%, from the previous week. Seasonal factors had called for a decrease of 12,534, or 6.0%. The comparable week in 2025 had 218,228 initial claims.

Continuing claims, which count people still collecting benefits after an initial week, rose by 70,000 to a seasonally adjusted 1,763,000 in the week ending Nov. 7. The seasonally adjusted insured unemployment rate was 1.1%, unchanged from the prior week. The jump hints that some people let go from their jobs are taking a long time to land new ones, according to Reuters.

For the week ending Nov. 7, the largest increases in unadjusted initial claims were in California (+4,800), New Jersey (+3,679), Missouri (+3,168), Texas (+2,631) and Minnesota (+2,583). New Jersey cited layoffs in educational services, and Missouri cited layoffs in manufacturing. Michigan had the largest decrease (-2,949).

Last week's release put claims at 200,000 for the week ending Nov. 7, before the revision. Claims were at or below 200,000 in each week from Sept. 12 through Oct. 31, according to the department's data. They reached 189,000 in mid-July, a low last seen in 1969, and the highest weekly reading this year was 230,000.

The unemployment rate fell to 4.1% in October from 4.5% in November 2025, and nonfarm payrolls rose by 165,000 jobs in October. The latest claims report spanned the period when federal officials conducted their business survey used for November's payroll count, according to Reuters.
