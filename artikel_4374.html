Honda Issues Yet Another Recall for 810,592 Cars Equipped with Takata Airbags

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The recall population includes some Acura RDX, ILX, Honda CR-V, Civic, and Accord vehicles supplied by the company that sold millions of deadly airbags.

Somehow, after all these years, there are still a considerable number of vehicles equipped with Takata airbags, though their number should be considerably lower in the near future. Honda just issued a recall for 810,592 cars equipped with Takata airbags that could rupture due to propellant degradation caused by high humidity.

According to documents filed with the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), the recall covers some 2013 Acura RDX, ILX, Honda CR-V, Civic, and Accord models. The affected Accords and Acuras are all from the 2013 model year, while the CR-V and Civic populations include both 2012 and 2013 vehicles.

The NHTSA documents show that the affected vehicles are equipped with airbag inflators that use a phase-stabilized ammonium nitrate propellant, which is intended to control moisture within the inflator. Over time, exposure to high humidity and repeated temperature changes can cause the inflator to become brittle. When the brittle inflators are deployed, they could turn into projectiles that shoot metal fragments through the cabin, potentially injuring or killing occupants.

The recall comes after years of investigation and analysis by Honda, which was routinely shared with NHTSA. The recall documents show that on November 19, 2026, Honda determined that the inflators posed enough of a risk to warrant the new recall.

Owners of affected models will be contacted by mail and urged to bring their vehicle to a Honda or Acura dealership. Dealer technicians will replace the driver and passenger airbag inflators with a different model for free. Dealers have already been notified of the recall, with owner notification letters scheduled to be sent on December 18. Owners concerned that their car might be included in the recall can check on the NHTSA recalls site as well as NHTSA’s dedicated Takata airbag recall page.
