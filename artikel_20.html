Season's eatings! Celebrating Thanksgiving in France

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

This Thursday marks Thanksgiving in the United States, the biggest travel holiday of the year there and arguably the most important on US calendars. But here in France, it's just another day. However, Americans living in Paris do try to capture the Thanksgiving spirit, though it can be tricky to track down the turkeys and pies required. Joining us to discuss how Americans do Thanksgiving in Paris is Cordon Bleu-trained chef and the owner of Boneshaker Donuts, Amanda Bankert.
