Gadi Eisenkot unveils agenda to reverse Netanyahu’s policies

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The bloc of Israeli prime ministerial hopeful Gadi Eisenkot introduced a series of bills at the start of the legislative session that target Benjamin Netanyahu and his policies. They include launching an official government-led inquiry into the Hamas-led Oct. 7, 2023, attacks, setting term limits on the position of prime minister and overturning changes the outgoing Netanyahu-led government made to limit the powers of the judiciary. FRANCE 24's Sharon Gaffney speaks with Rina Bassist, Editor at Al-Monitor.
