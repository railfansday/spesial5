29 Vintage Photos of a Young Meg Ryan, Fall Style Icon

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Every celebrity has their season: Mariah Carey is the “Queen of Christmas”; Nicole Kidman is apparently a “True Spring,” according to color analysis theory; and Megan Thee Stallion permanently owns hot girl summer. While one could certainly make the argument that fall belongs to Alexis Bledel and Lauren Graham, personally, I’ve spent enough picturesque autumn afternoons rewatching Gilmore Girls to last me the rest of my life. So I’m nominating Meg Ryan—queen of cozy vibes and choppy layers—for the title of high priestess of fall.

Ryan turns 65 today, which makes perfect sense; I mean, would you expect someone who starred in basically every Nora Ephron movie about the glorious, sensorily overwhelming wonders of fall in New York to have a birthday in July?

Like present-day Ryan, I live in California, much of which looks and feels the same in November as it does in high summer, so I’m celebrating the fall season with a deep dive into the affinity for preppy separates, tailored silhouettes, and killer accessories that has always put Ryan ahead of the style curve, onscreen and off.

In her honor, join me in appreciating 29 vintage photos of the great Meg Ryan.
