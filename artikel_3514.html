StackSocial has a refurbished, factory-unlocked iPhone 16e with 128GB of storage on sale for $354.99 right now, down from a $599 list price. The catch, and it's one worth understanding before you click buy, is that this particular unit carries a Grade C refurbished rating, meaning that it may arrive with visible scratches or dents on the housing. If that matters to you, you can get a Grade B-rated model for $399.99 for a B- grade or $434.99 for a B+ grade.

Cosmetic wear aside, the hardware is recent, and the 16e launched in 2025 as Apple's lower-cost entry with much of the same internals as the standard iPhone 16. That means you still get the A18 chip, 8GB of RAM, and a 16-core Neural Engine—enough to run Apple Intelligence features and handle games without feeling like an entry-level phone. Its 6.1-inch OLED screen is similarly well suited to everyday use, whether you’re reading, browsing, or streaming, with a sharp 2532 x 1170 resolution and up to 1,200 nits of brightness. The battery should also be able to keep up with a full day of typical use, with Apple rating it for up to 26 hours of video playback, while charging is handled through USB-C or a Qi wireless pad.

The potential downside shows up around back, where a single 48MP camera with optical image stabilization and a 2x telephoto option covers everyday photos and closer shots—though you lose the ultrawide lens that makes group shots and landscapes easier on the regular iPhone 16. Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.3 also put it a step behind newer models on wireless, and while its IP68 rating covers immersion up to six meters for half an hour (so spills and rain aren't a worry), it isn't a dust-proofing guarantee. But for this sale price, it's a great opportunity to upgrade your older iPhone.
