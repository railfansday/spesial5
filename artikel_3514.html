Apple Stores roll out holiday decorations to kick off the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The holidays are coming, and Apple Stores around the world are rolling out decorations today to celebrate. Here’s a look at this year’s holiday decor.

Apple is officially kicking off the holiday season today.

The online Apple Store and physical retail stores have now debuted holiday decorations featuring a colorful array of products and accessories.

Like in years past, Apple’s new imagery arranges products into shapes like snowflakes, trees, stars, and more.

Beyond decorations, today also kicks off an extended holiday return policy.

If you buy a new product (or receive a previously ordered one) starting today, it is eligible for an extended holiday return. That means instead of the typical 14-day return period, the product can be returned as late as January 8—over two months from now.
