Calafiori opens to Roma return: “I’d be very happy.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A special day for Riccardo Calafiori, honored at the Capitoline Hill by Mayor Roberto Gualtieri.

The Arsenal defender received the “Son of Rome” award for his international achievements, particularly for winning the Premier League with the Gunners. It was an opportunity to discuss his ties to the capital, as well as his past and a possible future in the Giallorossi jersey.

During the ceremony, Calafiori thanked the mayor and shared his emotion: “I feel like thanking the Mayor. I consider myself a clean-cut person, and I felt a bit emotional. I felt a stomach ache, but I’m grateful for this recognition, and it makes me proud. I always try to represent my city abroad. I miss home, but when I return, it always feels good, and it’s a thrill.”

Calafiori then spoke about Gasperini’s Roma and the project for the new stadium in Pietralata: “We talked about the stadium project, and it’s incredible, and I can’t wait to see it finished. Speaking with the Roma guys, everyone speaks highly of Gasperini. We know how much he can develop players, and I hope he can achieve something important at the end of the season.”

Finally, the young Gunners and Italy national team defender didn’t close the door on a possible return to Roma: “I hope to win as much as possible in the coming years and achieve the best I can. I’ve always said in the distant future that we didn’t part on good terms. I don’t hold a grudge, in fact, I think it served me well. I’d be very happy.”
