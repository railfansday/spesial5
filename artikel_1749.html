Man City XI vs Fulham: Confirmed team news, predicted lineup and injury latest for Premier League

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Premier League football will soon resume after the international break, with Manchester City hoping to rectify their recent troubles against Fulham in Saturday’s lunchtime kick-off.

Since suffering their first loss of the Premier League season to Aston Villa, City have fallen to two further defeats at the hands of Brighton and Nottingham Forest.

With time to think over the international break, Enzo Maresca has decisions to make when it comes to his starting XI for the visit of Fulham to the Etihad this weekend.

He has headaches in defence. Marc Guehi missed international duty with England after coming off with an issue against Nottingham Forest, while he has since confirmed Abdukodir Khusanov and Matheus Nunes are both doubts for the match, too. Antoine Semenyo is a long-term absentee with a knee issue.

In goal, Gianluigi Donnarumma has come under fire for his performances, but it seems unlikely that Maresca would drop the Italian international just yet.

With Guehi and Khusanov doubts, it makes sense to predict a centre-back pairing of Ruben Dias and Josko Gvardiol. Left back Nico O’Reilly is returning to both form and favour with Maresca, while Nunes may be forced to drop out for Rico Lewis.

Enzo Fernandez is definitely a favourite of Maresca, and while the Argentine travelled to South America to play two friendlies with his national side, he was rested for 35 minutes in the second game.

Elliott Anderson received a similar amount of rest while away with England - coming off early during their 3-2 victory over Spain courtesy of a red card.

City paid over £250million to overhaul their midfield this summer, and the duo’s quality should see them start.

Phil Foden was dropped for the defeat to Forest, but City’s performance might encourage Maresca to restore the England international to the starting XI. In order to fit both Foden and Rayan Cherki into the side, the former will have to go on the right wing, with the latter playing centrally.

We’re predicting that Jeremy Doku will get the nod on the left - with Erling Haaland leading the line up top.
