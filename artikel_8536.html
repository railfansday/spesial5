Personal Investments • Re: How to incorporate an inheritance into retirement plan??

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

We're in a similar situation but our anticipated inheritance is about half of what we have in our portfolio. We are retired (67/64) and based our retirement planning on our accounts only. We don't "need" the inheritance to sustain our retirement lifestyle.

The amounts of our pending inheritance are known by account as of 2020 which have likely changed since then. Because the largest piece of the inheritance is coming in the form of an inherited IRA, I modelled it separately in the future based upon the 2020 amounts. I set up RMDs over the following 10 years so that I could see the effects of the increased taxable income by year against our plan which will likely overlap with our SS draws and RMD. This helped me make some Roth conversion decisions that I have completed. That's about all I've done which seems appropriate.

You could probably do the same pretty quickly in the new retirement optimizer app > https://retireoptimizer.web.app/#/dashboard
