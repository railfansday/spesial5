Get the Discover it Balance Transfer Credit Card if you’re moving a balance from another bank’s card and will use its 5% quarterly categories once it’s paid off. Transfers get 0% for 18 months for a 3% fee, so pay the balance off before that ends.

Capital One, which bought Discover in May 2025, issues this cash back card, and its 0% period on transfers starts the day the account opens, not the day you transfer. Transfers can start 10 days after opening, so a $5,000 balance moved then costs a $150 fee, and 17 monthly payments of $303 clear the $5,150 before the 0% ends.

After the first year, it beats a flat 2% card only for those who put at least 25% of their card spending in the 5% categories, which stop at $1,500 a quarter. Readers with nothing to transfer get the same rewards plus a welcome bonus on the Discover it Cash Back, and those under 25% earn more on the Citi Double Cash, a flat 2% card with its own 0% transfer offer.
