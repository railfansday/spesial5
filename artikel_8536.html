Before you sign up for survey sites that pay in Amazon gift cards, collect the credit Amazon pays you directly. Trade in an old Kindle, Echo, or tablet for an Amazon gift card balance, and get on the waitlist for the Amazon Shopper Panel, which pays up to $10 a month for receipts from other stores.

If you’re replacing a Kindle, tablet, or Echo, the old one can help pay for the new one. Amazon Trade-In also takes phones, headphones, Bluetooth speakers, streaming players, home security devices, routers, and game consoles.

The trade-in value depends on the device’s make, model, and condition, and Amazon pays it as a gift card balance. Some trade-ins also come with a discount of up to 20% off a qualifying new Amazon device, limited to one discount per device category each calendar quarter. A device too worn to resell may still earn the discount, Amazon says.

Look up your device on the Trade-In page, answer its questions about condition, and ship it free in your own box with Amazon’s prepaid label, or drop it off at a participating location. If Amazon’s inspection adjusts the offer, you can accept the new value or have the device sent back free.

For receipts, download the Amazon Shopper Panel app. Amazon invites a limited number of shoppers, and anyone else can join the waitlist in the app. Once you’re in, each eligible receipt from the last 30 days pays an amount set by the type of store. Receipts from electronics, clothing, home improvement, sporting goods, and department stores pay 70 cents each, so 15 of them reach the $10 monthly cap. Grocery, drug, dollar, big-box, and other store receipts pay a dime. Amazon, Whole Foods, and restaurant receipts don’t count, and every receipt you share shows Amazon what you bought and where.

Amazon also offers one-time bonuses now and then, such as extra credit the first time you load $100 or more onto your gift card balance. Only some accounts get each offer, so look for one on Amazon Reload, the page where you add money to the balance, before you add any.

You can spend Amazon credit only at Amazon, so for a phone or game console, compare cash offers before you trade it in.
