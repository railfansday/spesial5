Viral 97-year-old Vito dies after dancing to Stayin’ Alive for his birthday

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

A 97-year-old man has gone viral after dying during his own birthday party, where he had just danced to Stayin’ Alive by the Bee Gees.

On November 2, baker Eden Malinoff took to her TikTok account ‘BakedSoCal’ to reveal a cake that she had made for a neighbor’s 97th birthday party. According to Malinoff, Vito, the man at the centre of the party, wanted a “Halloween-birthday rager” in “true Vito fashion.”

However, as per the baker’s video, the 97-year-old “made a dramatic exit” as the night “did not end as anticipated” by everyone in attendance.

“If there is any way that Vito could have chosen to go out, it would have been like this. He was surrounded by people he loved, we were celebrating his long and full life, and I am just so grateful that I got to be a part of it,” Malinoff added, showing off quick snippets of Vito dancing at his party.

Viewers quickly noted that the 97-year-old appeared to be dancing to ‘Stayin’ Alive’ by The Bee Gees in one clip, with questions piling up in the TikToker’s comments.

She uploaded another video on November 3, showing off a longer version which confirmed that he had, in fact, danced to that song before passing.

“I’m sorry how are you not TRAUMATIZED,” one person commented. “I would choose this over rotting away in a hospital bed/retirement home 100% of the time,” another said. “I’m sorry he DIED? at the PARTY?” another added.

Malinoff added that Vito’s friends “believe that he was ready and honestly there’s not a better way to go.”

Another friend of the 97-year-old confirmed his passing on Instagram too. “He had some medical issues that we didn’t know the severity of, and he passed away respectfully with his favorite music going. Rest in peace, you legend.”

The baker’s videos have gotten well over 10 million views since November 3, with viewers asking for even more details about Vito.

The clips have also been reshared to other social media platforms as well by accounts that aren’t run by Malinoff, introducing even more people to Vito.
