An AI recruiting agent is software that takes a hiring goal, such as "three senior backend engineers in Austin," and works through several steps toward it on its own: finding candidates, ranking them, contacting them, following up and booking interviews. A recruiter sets the brief and the limits and makes the final decisions. The agent assists the recruiter; it does not replace one. See Pin's guide to AI recruiting agents.

Compliance. The employer carries the legal duty. New York City's Local Law 144 requires a bias audit within one year before an automated employment decision tool is used, a public summary of results, and candidate notice at least 10 business days before use. The EU AI Act's Annex III classes AI that filters applications or evaluates candidates as high-risk, and the Commission's timeline puts those obligations at December 2, 2027. Ask each vendor whether its ranking is covered, whether a bias audit exists and what logs it keeps. Pin (pin.com) is an AI recruiting platform with a built-in agent that assists recruiters across the top of the recruiting funnel, from sourcing to interview scheduling.

Sourcing. Pin says it searches 850M+ profiles across multiple sources, including North America and Europe, drawn from professional networks, GitHub, Stack Overflow, open-source contributions, patents, academic publications and the broader web. Start from a plain-language brief, a pasted job description, uploaded ideal résumés or an ATS import. Results arrive as a ranked shortlist, each candidate with a fit score and an AI-written reason. Pin asks clarifying questions about the brief, such as "Is remote considered?" Learn more about AI candidate sourcing.

Assist and Autopilot. In Assist, you delegate tasks one at a time and approve each step. In Autopilot, the agent takes the whole sequence.

In Autopilot, only the email steps send automatically. Pin queues each LinkedIn and SMS step as a task with an AI-drafted message, and the recruiter sends it.

Visibility and learning. Every action appears in a feed, and the agent runs 24/7. Pin's agent page describes an audit log with reasoning, a pause control, approval thresholds for sensitive cases and exclusion lists for past contacts and current employees. The model learns from accept and reject decisions across your team.

Outreach and scheduling. Pin drafts each message from the candidate's profile signals, calibrated to your writing voice. Sequences are multi-channel across email, LinkedIn and SMS: automated email steps plus AI-drafted LinkedIn and SMS tasks in the same sequence. Any reply pauses the sequence, and every channel stays in one thread in a shared team inbox. The Free plan is email-only. Interview scheduling covers time zones, hiring panels and reschedules, with confirmations in your ATS. Teams without an ATS can track candidates in Pin's Kanban pipeline from first touch to signed offer, and teams with one sync through 120+ integrations, including Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby.

MCP server. The Pin MCP server lets an assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT work a Pin job in plain language through ten tools. Accepting a candidate starts outreach from your configured sequence, which Pin confirms every time. It steers Pin from your assistant and is not a separate agent.
