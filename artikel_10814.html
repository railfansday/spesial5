Ethan Klein adresses affair, the end of H3 Podcast, and return to YouTube

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Ethan Klein has broken his silence on his affair and Hila Klein filing for divorce, as well as bringing the H3 Podcast to an end.

Back on October 22, after Ethan Klein announced that the H3 Podcast would be going on an “indefinite hiatus,” Hila Klein revealed that he had been having an affair for over six months. Ethan handed over his ownership stake in the podcast, as well as other business ventures, and his wife also filed for divorce in the aftermath.

The H3H3 co-founder has made some suggestions that he could make a comeback in the near future, but has now issued an apology for everything going on, calling it the “ultimate betrayal” for Hila.

“The disappointment and despair I’ve caused is immense, and I have so much to reckon with in coming to terms with what happened,” he posted on November 24. “I’m most sorry to Hila, to whom the word ‘sorry’ likely has become the weight of ash, and so I will refrain from getting too deep into apologizing to her here. I could never say or do enough to make-up for what I have put her through.”

In a lengthy statement, Ethan noted that an explanation for his affair “will come in time” and he wants to “thoughtfully address the seriousness” of it.

He also took blame for the H3 Podcast coming to an end, but hit out at “certain crew members” for treating the situation like “Game of Thrones and not a comedy podcast,” adding that he had been “growing concerned” about the “viability of things working out” between everyone.

Ethan also added that there is “no plan” right now for a return, but suggested the podcast had become too “automated” at times.
