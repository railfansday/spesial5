The industry races into Las Vegas for RE+

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The largest tradeshow focused on the U.S. solar, storage and clean energy markets is back in Las Vegas for 2026. RE+ was the 24th largest tradeshow in the United States in 2025 by attendance, and this year’s showing (Nov. 16-19) is expected to be just as large.

The Formula 1 series will race the Las Vegas Grand Prix the weekend after RE+ concludes. Photos courtesy: F1 and the Las Vegas Grand Prix

The U.S. solar and storage industries have gotten so big that it appears only the Las Vegas Convention Center can accommodate a tradeshow bringing together nearly 40,000 clean energy professionals in one place. RE+ will be held at the Las Vegas Convention Center each November for the foreseeable future.

The convention center is tucked away from the main strip of casinos, but there is still plenty in the area to see and do. The site is also easily accessible, with a stop on the Las Vegas Monorail.

Conference education programming begins on Monday and sessions continue throughout the show at various stages on the exhibit hall floor. The exhibition opens on Tuesday.

Exhibitors will be spread across three halls in the convention center — the main entrance is at West Hall which extends via an over-street walkway to North Hall, and then Central Hall is accessed beyond a lobby area. There will also be a few outdoor booths to peruse located near food trucks. Visiting outdoor exhibits in November will be more comfortable, as the average high temperature is just 70°F, unlike the late summer heat of September.

Special events at RE+ include a golf tournament on Monday and the Run with the Sun 5K on Tuesday. The “Energy in Motion” block party will be at the Convention Center on Tuesday starting at 7 p.m. in the West Hall.

Formula 1 will race the Las Vegas Grand Prix the weekend following RE+. The course takes drivers down the Las Vegas Strip and around the Sphere, and road closures to the public will begin on Thursday. The Las Vegas Convention Center is several blocks north of the racing circuit, so the conference shouldn’t experience disruptions. After-conference activities might be affected later in the week, and attendees should plan a little extra time getting to the airport if leaving before the weekend.
