Get The Blue Business® Plus Credit Card from American Express only if you’ll transfer its points to airlines or hotels. It earns 2 points per dollar on your first $50,000 a year with no annual fee, but other uses value a point at a cent or less. That’s 2% at most, what the Blue Business Cash pays in cash.

American Express says freelancers, gig workers, and other self-employed people can apply for a business card, using their Social Security number if they have no employer identification number (EIN).

It suits owners who charge under $50,000 a year and will move the points to airline or hotel programs. Owners who’d rather redeem for cash or flights booked through American Express Travel should get the Blue Business Cash, which pays 2% in cash on the same first $50,000.
