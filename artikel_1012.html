Tottenham boss Roberto De Zerbi defends Mykhailo Mudryk for saying he wanted to join Arsenal

Roberto De Zerbi has defended Mykhailo Mudryk despite acknowledging the frustration among Tottenham supporters regarding the Chelsea loanee’s recent comments about rivals Arsenal.

Mudryk recently featured on the Guest of Gordon podcast – an appearance which it is understood Spurs were unaware of – where he spoke about his drugs ban and revealed how close he was to joining Arsenal in 2023 during the transfer window in which he ultimately moved to Chelsea.

The Ukraine winger labelled Arsenal "the club I wanted to play for" and added that "moving to the Emirates Stadium was my dream". The remarks have angered Tottenham fans, particularly as the 25-year-old has featured for just 16 minutes since arriving on deadline day due to a persistent ankle issue.

Speaking ahead of Saturday’s clash with Ipswich, De Zerbi said: "Yes, I can understand the fans (feeling) but Mudryk is a good guy and is a good player.

"He suffered a lot for the situation in the last couple of years. He has just one thing to do. Show on the pitch his qualities and show on the pitch that now he is playing for Tottenham. And Tottenham is a big club like the other clubs – not less at all.

"Now his focus is just on football and he has a big chance to play with us because Tottenham is a big club, he knows very well the coach, he knows very well the style of play and he knows I give him total confidence to show what he is.

"So, he has to move the focus in the right direction, in a good direction, but I think he is working hard to recover and to play earlier."

With Mudryk still two to three weeks away from resuming training, De Zerbi avoided giving a direct answer when asked whether Tottenham might terminate his season-long loan deal in January.

"It is not my part, no? I am a coach and now we have to help him to recover in the best way because I think and I believe he can be very important for us for the specific characteristics he has and for the level of the player," De Zerbi said.

Mathys Tel is out with an ankle problem, but De Zerbi is handed a boost with Lucas Bergvall and Savio both returning after their recent injury lay-offs.

Destiny Udogie and James Maddison are also fit to play, though the Italian cautioned that the pair need to be able to train on a more consistent basis.

De Zerbi added: "James Maddison worked very well from last Wednesday after Leeds. He worked very well every day. Before Leeds, from the Chelsea game to the Leeds game, he worked just three times on the pitch and training ground.

"If you don’t work during the week, you risk injury, and in any way you lose the normal physical condition you need to play in the Premier League. The same for Udogie.

"When they work, when they are fit to work, they have many chances to play and especially Maddison because he is a special player."

De Zerbi will watch Saturday's game against Ipswich from the stands as he serves a touchline ban after picking up three yellow cards this season.
