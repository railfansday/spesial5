Liverpool's updated January transfer plan as Bradley Barcola sets off alarm bells - Transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Liverpool will not spend big in the January transfer window as they plan to cut back their outgoing fees after recent failings.

The Reds have not been shy to spend big in recent seasons, with fees over £100million dropped on all three of Alexander Isak, Florian Wirtz, and most recently, Bradley Barcola.

So far, none of the three big-money signings have lived up to expectations, with injury issues, struggles with confidence, and dips in form clouding their Liverpool spells.

As a result, former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Liverpool are unlikely to spend such excessive fees on new signings in the near future.

Alarm bells are ringing about their success rate when it comes to spending big, with a toned-down approach likely to weild better results when it comes to strengthening the squad.

In recent years, the likes of Ryan Gravenberch, Alexis Mac Allister and Ibrahima Konate have been signed for relatively low fees and gone on to make significant impacts.

Brown told Football Insider on 9 November that Liverpool have not been convinced by Barcola so far, and they had expected more given the money spent to bring him in.

This is a similar story to their feelings on Wirtz, whose performances have improved this season, but have yet to justify the huge fee paid to Bayer Leverkusen last year.

Those difficulties have forced Liverpool to change their transfer stance, with a focus on bargain deals likely to emerge in the summer when they step up their interest.

"I can't see Liverpool spending that kind of money on another player any time soon," Brown told Football Insider.

"Their success rate hasn't been good at all, Wirtz is still struggling after a year in the Premier League and Barcloa hasn't started anywhere near as well as they'd hoped.

"The only one of these £100million signings you could say has been worth it is Alexander Isak, but even then, he has struggled a lot with injuries.

"The problem is, when you spend upwards of £100million on a player, there's much higher potential that you aren't going to get what you think you're paying for.

"I don't think Liverpool will be prepared to take that risk and be disappointed again, so when the next window opens, they'll be looking at cheaper deals.

"They've shown before they can do it, some very influencial players have come in for fees of a lot less than £100million, and that's where they need to focus.

"There are bargain deals to be done, players who might not be wanted elsewhere but could do a job for Liverpool, so it's not like they have to go out and spend big."

It has become clear in recent transfer windows that Liverpool are willing to splash the cash when it comes to agreeing deals for players who they believe will improve their side.

Andoni Iraola still believes there are areas of his team that need strengthening, with the midfield believed to be a priority ahead of the January transfer window.

As a result, Brown told Football Insider that if Liverpool were to spend £100million on another player, Real Madrid star Federico Valverde should be the one.

The 28-year-old has established himself as one of the most impressive, consistent and reliable midfielders in world football, and has previously been eyed by Liverpool.

Valverde would be a "dream" signing for Liverpool and would resolve the glaring weaknesses in the squad, but it is highly unlikely he will leave Real Madrid any time soon.
