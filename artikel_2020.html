The Young Lions have made it two Elite League wins out of two in the space of a week.

Last midweek it was stunning 6-1 home victory for the England men’s under-20s against Czechia.

The Young Lions maintained their perfect record in the Men’s U20 Euro Elite League and were 4-0 up at half-time, before then easing to an eventual 6-1 scoreline with two more goals in the second half and Czechia with just one late consolation goal of their own.

The Young Lions then headed to Portugal for the second match of this international window for a game at the Estadio Municipal de Mafra.

The England Under 20s with an impressive Elite League victory away against Portugal.

England men’s under-20s signed off for the November international window and for 2026 with a 2-1 win against Portugal.

Brian Madjo found the back of the net in the first half to ensure that England led at the break.

Francisco Silva though with the equaliser just before the hour mark.

Newcastle United rising star Sean Neave then one of the subs brought on in the 63rd minute and he helped the Young Lions to eventual victory.

On loan at Wolves, Neave helping to pile on the pressure and the Portugal goal leading a charmed life until eventually Harry Amass got the winner ten minutes from time.

This is proving a great time for young rising Newcastle United star Sean Neave, he came off the bench and helped England Under 20s to that 6-1 win against Czechia.

Now helping to make it two wins in a week with this away victory over Portugal.

This England Under 20s call up for the November internationals swiftly followed Sean Neave getting his first two goals in senior football. The teenage striker is on loan at Wolves and with them trailing 1-0 at Bristol City, Sean Neave coming off the bench to score the two late goals that totally turned the match around ahead of these internationals.

Sean Neave hoping for another call up for the England Under 20s, their next game against Poland on Friday 26 March 2027.
