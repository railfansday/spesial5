James Penrice Rangers penalty earns ex-referee verdict with VAR shout

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

James Penrice should not have been awarded a penalty for Rangers in Saturday evening's 2-0 victory over St Mirren.

That's the view of former SPFL referees Steve Conroy and Des Roache who both reckoned the decision from Nick Walsh should have been overturned by VAR.

The Gers left-back went down under pressure inside the area in Paisley last night with whistler Walsh immediately pointing to the spot. And despite demands from the home support to rethink the call, the spot-kick shout stood.

It would be Ryan Naderi who stepped up to send goalkeeper Jacob Chapman the wrong way after Samuel Ramos had been found guilty of the foul.

Very soft penalty, doesn’t look to be a lot of contact. VAR should be overturning that.

— The Ref’s View (@TheRefsView) November 7, 2026

But posting to their social media account on X, Roache and Conroy's shared opinion was clear.

They wrote: "Very soft penalty, doesn’t look to be a lot of contact. VAR should be overturning that."

The Light Blues dominated the action at the SMiSA Stadium and midfielder Dan Neil would double his side's lead in the second-half to ensure all three points headed back to Glasgow.

Chris Sutton, on punditry duties for Sky Sports, claimed Penrice had "played Walsh like a piccolo" to win the penalty.
