Celtic player ratings as 9/10 sub duo complete Europa League rescue mission

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Started well with a tremendous save to deny Leandro Trossard a wonder goal, before the offside flag was raised. Saved Junior Olaitan's penalty but couldn't track the ball from his stop as the rebound was hammered over the line. Beaten all ends up by Orkun Kokcu's drive to make it 2-2.

A positive performance from the left-back, recalled to Scotland duty after missing out through injury last month. Was so determined in his one-v-one against former Rangers rival Vaclav Cerny. Tried to join the attack where possible and was eventually rewarded as his shot turned into an assist for Camilo Duran's winner.

Becoming a leader for Celtic in defence. Never shirked a duel and had to win a number of key tackles throughout a battling 90 minutes at Parkhead. Was wasteful with the ball a few times and not at his commanding best when Celtic retreated in first half but regrouped after break. So close to scoring with a towering header in second half.

A fairly comfortable evening for the centre-back who was largely on top of Besiktas' attacking output, with Celtic standing up to some serious pressure in spells. A little hesitant in his use of the ball at times but defensively sound throughout. Withdrawn for the closing stages.

An unexpected first start for Celtic but settled into the match pretty well. Earned early praise for his swashbuckling defending and charges forward. But all that credit was wiped by a moment of stupidity as he lunged in on Poku to gift Besiktas a penalty for their opener. Would rally with a surging run to the opposition penalty box but fluffed his lines at decisive moment.

A better night for the Celtic captain in Europe. Didn't start particularly well as Celtic struggled to take any control of things but he was relentless in his efforts. Demanded more from his side and found a groove of sorts after triple sub in second half - guided team-mate Mika Baur through the night, too. But perhaps could have been out quicker to block Kokcu goal.

Similar to McGregor, the German was not at his best in the first half but grew into the match and emptied the tank in a tireless second half. Strong in the tackle, composed on the ball and a proper competitor as Celtic turned it round to answer critics.

So often a player more effective than involved, but was the opposite against Besiktas. Saw plenty of the ball and a couple of openings but didn't have any conviction or composure. Slashed a huge early chance wide of target and proved it wasn't his night as he picked up a booking for a lunge after miscontrolling as he was yards from the Besiktas box.

Such a frustrating performance from the winger. Clearly, has the quality to be a creative spark for Celtic but was repeatedly let down by a slack touch or poor delivery when it came to the crucial moment. Would be a surprise to see him starting regularly ahead of any of the other options on the flanks.

Too often the ball bounced off him or his link-up play wasn't on point as the ball just kept returning to Besiktas.
