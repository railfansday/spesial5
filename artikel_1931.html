‘A lot better now’ - Sheffield Wednesday’s Jaden Heskey makes loan spells and Manchester City comparisons

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Jaden Heskey has described his first loan spell at Sheffield Wednesday as a “mad experience” - and claimed the club’s situation is now much-improved.

The 20-year-old was thrown in at the deep end in January, when Manchester City loaned the midfielder to Wednesday.

He joined a club in crisis, becoming a regular fixture in a side nosediving towards League One against a backdrop of off-field uncertainty.

Heskey returned on loan in the summer transfer window, after a takeover by Arise Capital Partners had put the Owls on a more stable footing.

“I spent the end of last season with them,” Heskey told From My Left. “But I’d say the situation’s a lot better now with the club.

“It was crazy last season. It was crazy but it was a mad experience with the team. This season, I think, we’re going obviously for promotion, naturally. [We’ve got] a very, very strong team.”

An attack-minded midfielder, Heskey has been getting quite the education in South Yorkshire. He has already experienced relegation and a managerial change, with Dave Challinor now at the helm in place of the sacked Henrik Pedersen.

There is also the challenge of playing in League One, a division notoriously difficult for relegated clubs to immediately escape from.

“It’s tough,” he said. “I’d say it’s similiar to the Championship to be fair.

“But a lot more teams go direct than try to play. It’s getting used to that, obviously from playing at City. I’m enjoying it. At City especially, we’ve always got at least 60 per cent possession. It’s more proper football this now. I’m enjoying playing it.”

Heskey, the son of former England international Emile, has made 12 appearances across all competitions for Wednesday this season.
