Wayne Rooney reveals he contemplated suicide while at Manchester United

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Wayne Rooney has revealed he contemplated suicide while playing at Manchester United.

The ex-England striker, who remains United’s record goalscorer, has spoken publicly of his struggles with mental health and alcohol consumption in the past.

But he has now revealed in a candid interview that at his ‘lowest point’ he spent days at home drinking and considered taking his own life.

Speaking on the ‘Life At The Midpoint with Gabby Logan’ podcast, Rooney explained: “I can look back and say I was selfish, but I knew I was going through something which I was really struggling to deal with.”

“Then I didn’t want (his eldest son) Kai seeing me like that, so that’s why I’d hide away as well.

“I got to a place where you’re contemplating suicide. You just didn’t want to be around anyone. I didn’t want to be see people in the streets. I didn’t want to go into shops.

“I was always comfortable on a football pitch, but everything else, (I) really struggled with it.”

Kai Rooney, the eldest of Wayne’s four children, is now 17 years old and impressing in United’s academy ranks.

Asked to remember the lowest point of his life, Rooney continued: "When I was drinking, I would lock myself in my house for two days and just drink. This is probably in my best part of my career playing wise."

He added that he “struggled to deal with it all” and acknowledged he “needed help to get through it” after a conversation with his wife Coleen.

The heyday of Rooney’s playing career saw him lift a wealth of domestic and European titles with United. It was during that time, though, that he revealed tried to “mask” his struggles with mental health to avoid “showing weakness.”

“Even though I knew the position I was in, it was difficult to actually admit that I was in that position because I felt like you feel less of a man, if you like, for doing that.

“I was speaking to a therapist to try and figure out what was up with me because when I look back I was probably at the height of my career, I've just had a young son, I've just been married.

“It should be the happiest point in my life and it wasn't.

“It was probably the lowest point because of the selfish acts I was taking.

“Coleen helped me massively. She could have walked away, it was that bad, but she stuck by me and helped me get through it.”
