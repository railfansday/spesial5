Walmart Sees Weakest Global Ad Sales Growth in 2 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

$188.8 billion: Walmart’s revenue, up 5.2% from a year ago.

25%: Year-over-year growth of Walmart’s global advertising business in the third quarter—its weakest quarter of growth since the first quarter of 2024.

37%: Walmart U.S.’ year-over-year advertising growth, including Vizio and Vibe.co. Excluding those two acquired businesses, Walmart Connect grew 36% within the U.S.

17%: Growth in Walmart’s global ecommerce sales. Walmart U.S. ecommerce sales grew 23%.

$120: Annual price of the new combined membership bundle of Walmart+ and Sam’s Club Plus.

17%: Global year-over-year growth in membership fee revenue.
