Sonic 06 Is Now Playable On PC Thanks to Fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

There are a lot of contenders for the 'worst' Sonic game: Sega's bizarre attempt at Mario Party with Sonic Shuffle; the missed opportunity of Sonic Forces and its custom character; or maybe the Kinect-exclusive Sonic Free Riders—but none are quite so infamous as Sonic 06.

And yet, for its 20th anniversary, fans have painstakingly worked to bring what the community calls "the worst Sonic game in history" to PC, ending its two-decade streak of Xbox 360 and PS3 exclusivity. Now, an entirely new audience can revisit this reviled corner of the Blue Blur's gaming legacy and leave just as annoyed and confused.

Marathon Recompiled version 1.0 launched on November 14, but as the team explains, you must provide your own legally acquired Xbox 360 copy to install the game. Typical recomp and emulation boilerplate; lest they risk the wrath of Sega lawyers. And no, I'm not talking about Takayuki Yagami.

You have to respect the dedication of Sonic fans to recompile what is widely regarded as the worst game in the entire franchise, but what's really impressive about Marathon Recompiled is that it's not another heap of vibecoded slop regurgitated by AI bros.

"Marathon Recompiled is an unofficial PC port of the Xbox 360 version of Sonic the Hedgehog (2006) created through the process of static recompilation," the GitHub description reads, citing ga2mer as the project's creator, Isaac Marovitz and squidbus as graphics programmers, Hyper as developer of system level features—like achievement support—Rei-san and Desko as the developers behind several quality-of-life patches, LJSTAR as the project logo designer, and a number of others for localization. Crucially - no Claude credit.

You might've noticed in that lineup that a couple of the devs worked on quality of life patches, but that doesn't mean that the game's original bugs have been patched out. Instead, bugs present on original hardware have been "intentionally preserved"—the idea is to archive Sonic's history, not sanitize it.

With that being said, there are optional settings that can be enabled in the configuration file to patch several of the Xbox 360 version's bugs and "other annoyances" if you so choose. Otherwise, the default experience strives to be as close to the original version as possible, and as such, bug reports that note issues present on original hardware will be rejected.

Bizarrely, Sonic X Shadow Generations producer Shun Nakamura wants to remake Sonic 06.

So, if you download it, expect Sonic 06 in all its glitchy glory, with slippery movement, Sonic falling through floors, and paradoxically slow and clumsy controls. There's a reason this game has a 46 on Metacritic; to hear GameSpot say it, "Sonic the Hedgehog may share its name with the Genesis original, but this game is even more broken than most of the other recent Sonic games have been."

Sonic Unleashed has never come to PC, but this port from a fan might as well be an official release.
