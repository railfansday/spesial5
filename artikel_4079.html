Hollow Knight: Silksong’s Sherma Gets A Singing Plush

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Now that GTA 6 is here, studios can start to assess the impact and figure out when they can get back to business as usual. For Team Cherry, that day is November 27, when it has promised to reveal more about its Hollow Knight: Silksong DLC, and it paired a reveal of a special Sherma plush with that announcement.

Hornet might be the hero in Silksong, but the award for most beloved character in the Hollow Knight sequel almost certainly goes to Sherma. You meet the optimistic little bug early on in your journey and bump into them frequently throughout. You know they’re nearby because of the catchy tune they’re always singing.

Out of the frying pan into the Sea of Sorrow fire.

Now you can hear that catchy tune everywhere you go, as not only has Sherma finally been turned into a plush, but that plush sings the same song that he sings to alert Hornet he is, once again, nearby.

The officially licensed Sherma plush was revealed by Fangamer, which has handled most of Silksong’s official merch. The reveal was coupled with a clip of the plush in action, and it appears that you have to squeeze Sherma’s head to make him start singing. Better than it activating whenever you’re nearby, like in the game. Sherma’s cute, but that might start to get annoying after a while.

Mario Wonder’s Talking Flower toy serves as evidence of that.

There is no pre-order period for Sherma’s singing plush. You can just go to Fangamer right now and buy it for $36. That presumably means it will be shipped right away, so the perfect holiday gift for the Hollow Knight fan in your life if you’re still trying to figure out what to buy them.

The Sherma plush is around nine inches tall, and even though the biggest takeaway is that it can sing to you, its other finer details should not be overlooked. The plush looks exactly like the character on which it's based, and is even holding the needle and chime he clinks together to play the tune that accompanies his song.

As for November 27’s Silksong stream, Team Cherry has promised multiple exciting announcements alongside an update on the sequel’s Sea of Sorrow expansion. There was hope that the DLC might be released before the end of 2026. Considering how little notice the studio wound up giving between the reveal of Silksong’s release date and that release date’s arrival, it is still entirely possible that Sea of Sorrow’s date gets revealed tomorrow, and that it will be here by some point in December.
