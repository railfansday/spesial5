Poll: Xbox Turns 25 This Week, But Which Console Generation Had The Best Launch Titles?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

This Sunday, Xbox will celebrate 25 years of great games and console experiences. Without giving away just how ancient we are, Fraser and I have been Xbox players since the beginning, enjoying each new generation of Xbox consoles and the games that launched alongside them.

With November 15th drawing closer, we thought we'd look back on each generation's launch lineup and ask for your verdict: which Xbox console had the best launch titles?

Was it the original Xbox, with Halo: Combat Evolved, Project Gotham Racing, and Fuzion Frenzy? How about the Xbox 360, with Condemned: Criminal Origins, Kameo: Elements of Power, and Call of Duty 2? Perhaps the Xbox One, with Ryse: Son of Rome, Dead Rising 3, and Forza Motorsport 5? Or is it the Xbox Series X|S, with Gears Tactics, Forza Horizon 4, and Sea Of Thieves?

This'll be fun to watch, I was PS only on the earlier years

Well, these are the games that I got with each console upon launch:

Assassins Creed Valhalla Borderlands 3 (next-gen upgrade) Bright Memory 1.0 Devil May Cry 5: Special Edition DIRT 5 Forza Horizon 4 (next-gen upgrade) Gears 5 (next-gen upgrade) Gears Tactics (next-gen upgrade) Manifold Garden (next-gen upgrade) No Man's Sky: Next Generation (next-gen upgrade) Warhammer: Chaosbane Slayer Edition Watch Dogs: Legion (next-gen upgrade) WRC 9 FIA World Rally Championship (not the upgrade)

In fairness, I would have got each of the next-gen upgrades, but I wouldn't really count them as launch titles. However, as I owned each of them then I would have got the upgrades too...

Original Xbox by a colossal margin. None of the following consoles even came close.

I picked the Series X because I feel the games shone immediately; better graphics, faster, smoother. It was just superb.

When it comes to the 360 though, I literally walked out of the shop with a bunch of those titles and no console! So, I had placed a deposit in Game for all the games and for the console, but when I got to the store late afternoon, they had my reserved games, but not my reserved console! I had been at work that day, and even though the Police Station at which I was Detective was nearby, I just had not had chance to get there any earlier, but I remember leaving the store clutching all these games and having nothing to play them on. Talk about annoyed!

Anyway, I remember going home, and my now sadly departed wife took pity on me. The console was intended for me for my birthday, which was in December, but I was going to be allowed to pick a couple of the games to play early and have access to the console (obviously!).

Thus, I resorted to eBay, and a hurried search on there for 360 consoles revealed a couple of students whom, in the early days of scalping, were flogging one not far from where I lived. So I duly paid £100 more than the asking price, and drove up to their flat to pick up the console (and a couple of games too; Kameo: Elements of Power and King Kong, which I had not bought myself).

All's well that ends well, though I never did play either of the games the students sold to me, and in fact, I still own them both still factory sealed too...

I tend to look at exclusives for these things more than third party releases that are available everywhere like AC, COD or Madden.

Although I didn't have it, purely based on those lists, it has to be the original Xbox - Halo, Project Gotham Racing, Tony Hawks Pro Skater 2x that's three worldies right there.

Next for me is X360 with Kameo, PGR3 & Amped 3 etc.

XBO in third with Dead Rising 3, Forza Motorsport 5 & Ryse.

XSX had the weakest launch lineup, I remember having it Day 1 and unsure what games to buy and play other than backwards compatible and upgrades, which I don't really count. There simply wasn't a new and worthwhile first party exclusive at launch.

so I bought the 360 for oblivion then it got delayed. but I enjoyed kame and need for speed and a couple of others. but man oblivion made the console for me. series x was probably better over all.

I'd say Series X had the best launch day lineup because of Gears Tactics. I played it on XB1 (released same day as the Series X|S version) but it was easily one of Xbox's best games.

The full order for launch day lineup would be Series X > XB1 > Original Xbox > 360

OG Xbox. Halo made it feel like an essential console, honestly.

360’s launch didn’t quite have the same blockbuster feeling to it, even though the 360 grew to have a much better catalog over time.

The Xbone’s launch was really meh for me. I remember the Xbone was sort of just a cooler 360 for me until Ori came out.

I didn’t get the Series S til Halo Infinite. The current generation launched sort of weakly. I remember being sort of underwhelmed and dragging Valhalla out since there wasn’t much out there to start the generation on PS5 and there was even less I was interested in on Series X/S. I didn’t give a current gen only game a personal GotY til 3 years into the generation. 😂

My nostalgic blinkers say the OG Xbox or 360 but as someone who skipped the Xbox One generation, Series X has been an incredible generation to catch up on Xbox exclusives and the launch lineup was excellent (I had 24 months of Gamepass bundled with my interest free purchase).

Seeing all those next-gen upgrades, for the Series S/X generation, is just sad. What a horrible start of a generation.

For me, it was the OG Xbox release, that blew me away, with Halo.

I love Amped 3 and Need For Speed Most Wanted so Xbox 360 for me. King Kong is really good but I’ve only played the pc version. THAW is also good but not my favourite Tony Hawk game

For me, it was probably the 360 as I felt it had the most games I wanted to play but only because both the XB1 and Series consoles had a lot of games I already had on he previous Gen hardware. That worked better with the Series Consoles but I ended up buying quite a few games again on XB1 that I'd played (or started playing) on my 360.

I didn't need to buy ANY games with my Series Console despite pre-ordering and getting it day 1 because all the Games I played on it that very day were carried forward with a Series X 'update' - games like Gears 5, FH4 and AC: Valhalla. When I bought my XB1, I had to buy games like BF4, CoD Ghosts etc again to play Day 1.

It was close between the OG and 360 era for me, but I gave it to the 360 just because it had a few more games I remember buying and playing. I bought the OG Xbox for Halo primarily and got PGR a bit later. I got Fusion Frenzy with it too but never liked that...

Because most, if not all the Launch games I wanted to play on the XB1 and Series Consoles were games I already owned on the previous Console, particularly the Series Console, they don't really feel like they had a great line-up of 'unique' titles, just more of an upgrade to the game performance - like buying a new GPU for your existing PC rather than buying new hardware for exciting new games that couldn't or wouldn't release on the older hardware.

Halo CE speaks for itself. One of the best games ever made. Throw in Fusion Frenzy and Oddworld and that's a trifecta for launch titles IMO.

Series X, perhaps. The nostalgic power of Xbox 360 is strong, and I enjoyed a lot of that a bit later on Xbox One. The original Xbox is the most mysterious console for me, because not many titles are currently available. Xbox One had some very cool games from the start, too. It's difficult to choose just one when you have been playing games from different generations at the same time, I guess. My opinions are much stronger about Nintendo consoles' line-ups.
