November 2026 PS Plus Essential Games Available to Download Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Tour a Dead Island, board a Monster Train

Today's the day for Sony's penultimate PS Plus Essential update of 2026, which delivers a further three PS5, PS4 games for members to download and play.

Here's what PlayStation has to offer this month, available to redeem until 1st December 2026:

The games are available now in the UK and across Europe, and availability will reach the USA later in the day.

As long as you add the games to your PSN account and maintain an active membership, you will always have access to the titles — they always remain playable and be tied to your profile. Again, you've got until 1st December 2026 to redeem them to your account.

Dead Island 2 is the standout title, and represents a fantastic open world romp through an undead LA. If you enjoy your time with the game, it's worth noting the Expansion Pass, which provides access to two DLC packs, is on sale right now for just £3.89 / $4.79. You've got until 5th November 2026 to buy it at that price.
