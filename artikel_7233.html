Who What Wear Sells AI Visibility to Brands, Helping Fuel Double-Digit Revenue Growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Few digital fashion publishers from the early 2000s are still around, let alone growing, but Who What Wear is a rare exception.

Revenue at the U.S. outpost of the fashion publisher, which was acquired by the U.K. media firm Future, rose by “double digits” in fiscal 2026, according to its parent company.

That’s partly thanks to a new business line, centered around helping brands ensure they surface more readily in answer engines, which has become an object of focus, said Hillary Kerr, its cofounder and senior vice president.
