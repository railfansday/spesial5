Security Engineer, Infrastructure Security at LiveKit

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

LiveKit is building the infrastructure layer for the voice-driven era of computing. Our platform gives developers everything they need to build, test, deploy, scale, and observe agents in production. Founded in 2021, LiveKit powers voice AI applications for OpenAI, xAI, Salesforce, Coursera, Spotify, and thousands of others, collectively facilitating billions of calls each year.

Our customers trust us with audio, video and data belonging to their own end users, so a security failure on our side puts their data and their business at risk. We are hiring an Infrastructure Security Engineer to improve the security posture of the production platform that carries that data. You will join a small Trust team that covers security, IT and compliance, and you will own real parts of the program from your first week. You will build our understanding of infrastructure security posture, then prioritize and drive remediation by business impact. Where the same class of problem keeps coming back, you will build the guardrails and security building blocks that let our security scale with the business.

think like both a builder and a breaker, and understand security from first principles

take an automation-first and pragmatic approach to risk mitigation

translate security concerns into engineering action

can clearly explain complex technical concepts to others

are a fast learner and thrive in ambiguity

Map the security posture of our cloud and production infrastructure and turn it into a documented baseline.

Find the gaps that could lead to customer data loss or infrastructure compromise, agree a prioritized security debt backlog with engineering, and close the highest-risk items first.

Harden identity, secrets and network boundaries, including cloud IAM, environment separation and credential hygiene.

Secure the infrastructure that runs customer-deployed voice AI agents: tenant isolation, secrets for third-party model providers, and media and recording storage.

Build guardrails and secure defaults into our infrastructure as code so the secure path is the easy one for engineers.

Partner with our Compliance team to map infrastructure controls to PCI-DSS and ISO 27001 and automate the technical evidence those audits depend on.

5+ years in security engineering, infrastructure engineering or SRE, with a significant share of that focused on infrastructure security.

Hands-on experience securing a major cloud provider (AWS, GCP or Azure) and containerized workloads including Kubernetes.

A track record of owning infrastructure security for a production platform.

Fluency with infrastructure as code such as Terraform, and strong scripting and automation skills in a language such as Go or Python.

A pragmatic approach to risk: you can explain why a finding matters to the business and how fast it needs to be fixed.

Experience with real-time media, networking or WebRTC systems.

Experience in AI security, such as securing LLM applications or agent infrastructure.

Incident response or application security experience.

Experience supporting compliance audits such as PCI-DSS, ISO 27001, SOC 2 or HIPAA.

An opportunity to build something truly impactful to the world

Contribute to open source alongside world-class engineers
