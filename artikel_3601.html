Nicola Peltz Beckham Addresses ‘Mean’ Body-Shaming Comments

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Nicola Peltz Beckham is appreciating the support of her fans after receiving negative comments about her body.

Peltz Beckham, 31, took to Instagram on Wednesday, November 11, to share a series of selfies wearing purple scrubs inside a doctor’s office. She captioned the upload, “🎀 hyperbaric chic post facial 🎀.”

In one image, Peltz Beckham pulled up her shirt with her stomach visible — which led some users to comment on her body. One fan, however, clapped back at the negative comments and asked people to “stop body shaming” the actress and others.

“Are you all perfect?” the user questioned, to which Peltz Beckham replied, “Thank you 💖. Some people are actually so mean 💔.”

“Your [sic] welcome sweetheart. I’m so sick of ppl and there [sic] opinions of others. I’m overweight and I struggle with it do [sic] to my health. You are beautiful. I adore you and Brooklyn ❤️,” the user wrote back, referencing Nicola’s husband, Brooklyn Beckham, to which the model responded, “Your comments are so kind thank you sm i read them and it means a lot to me 💖 you’re so beautiful, you’re perfect.”

Earlier this year, Nicola got candid about living a life in the spotlight and navigating comments about herself online.

“I’m very sensitive, and I need to work on not reading everything: online news, social media comments,” she told Elle. “I try not to pay attention to what people say, but it affects me. I need to learn to distance myself.”

In August, Brooklyn, 27, made headlines when he came to his wife’s defense and clapped back at body-shamers.

At the time, Brooklyn shared an Instagram Reel of himself making a cottage pie and received a comment that read, “Do you comment on everyone who is concerned about your wife and how sickly she looks? She puts herself out there; feed her.”

Brooklyn replied, “Don’t ever talk about my wife. She’s absolutely beautiful and you should never comment on a woman’s body. It’s disgraceful.”

Brooklyn and Nicola began dating in 2019 and tied the knot three years later. The pair renewed their vows in 2025. Last month, Brooklyn shared his love for Nicola by uploading a candid photo to his Instagram Story — and took aim at trolls before receiving negative comments.

“Yea I am obsessed with my wife. Bring on the comments, I don’t care, she’s perfect,” he wrote in October, ending the message with a middle finger emoji and a red heart emoji.
