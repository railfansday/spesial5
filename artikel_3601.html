GTA 6 Leads TGA Nominations, And Reviews Aren’t Even Live Yet

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Keighleys, or more formally, The Game Awards, are right around the corner. Ahead of its December 10 air date, host Geoff Keighley announced the nominees for the myriad categories, including Game of the Year. Rockstar Games' Grand Theft Auto 6 made the cut, but in an unusual circumstance, it did so before any reviews of the game have been published.

Before anyone has read any official take on GTA 6, Rockstar Games' action-adventure game has eight nominations, making it the most-nominated game at this year's The Game Awards.

On top of being spotlighted for Game of the Year, the game also has nominations for Best Art Direction, Best Audio Design, Best Game Direction, Best Action/Adventure, Best Narrative, Best Performance (for Manni L. Perez's portrayal of Lucia), and Best Score, bringing the crime sim's total nominations to eight.

We already knew GTA 6 would be eligible for this year's ceremony. After all, per the TGA FAQ, the cutoff date for eligibility is November 20, one day after the game comes out. With a "jury of over 100 global media and influencer outlets" selecting the nominees, and many of these folks likely having plenty of hands-on time with Rockstar Games' latest release, it isn't much of a shocker to see GTA 6 at the top spot. Still, it is a little awkward that it has so many nominations before its release that may reveal what to expect from the critical reception..

Maybe it shouldn't be too surprising or weird, though. After all, the game isn't officially out yet, and it's already sold approximately 16 million copies and generated nearly $2 billion in revenue. Maybe that expensive sick day caused by the GTA Flu won't be so expensive.
