SoulCalbur Is Getting Review Bombed By Salty GTA 6 Fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Two of the best-reviewed games of all time were released this month. Those who don’t like them, or one specific thing about them, have been trying to ruin that by leaving bad user reviews. Now, for some reason, SoulCalibur, which was released in 1998, is getting review bombed, seemingly by angry GTA fans looking for a fanbase to take their frustrations out on.

With a 99 on Metacritic, GTA 6 is now the highest-rated game ever. It pushes the original Ocarina of Time down to second, and SoulCalibur into third, both of which have 98s. Despite the negative user reviews being lobbied towards GTA 6, barring some late and unlikely negative reviews from websites and outlets, nothing is going to change that.

GTA 6’s user score doesn’t match. Hit by a review bomb for various reasons - including a certain subset of “fans” outraged that the game asks you to treat a woman with respect - it’s currently sitting on a 5.6. That appears to be the leading reason why, after 28 years, SoulCalibur is suddenly getting an influx of new, largely negative, user reviews.

GTA 6 was a monumental launch for Rockstar, with over 50% of all active console users playing the game on day one.

I say appears - although some have taken the extra step to mask the fact that they’ve probably never played SoulCalibur, a few have been more overt in why they believe the iconic fighting game deserves 0/10. “For GTA 6, child, you are starting a war, wait for it, Jason’s nail will bury you, kid, wait for it,” one SoulCalibur user review reads. I don’t understand what they’re trying to say, but it sounds bad.

“GTA number 1 forever. GTA number 1 forever. GTA number 1 forever. GTA number 1 forever,” another well-written SoulCalibur review reads. Like I said, many didn’t even try to mask the fact that the zeroes they’re leaving are in retaliation to others doing the same to GTA 6. Although in that instance, while most of the reasons for disliking the game are just as embarrassing, at least they actually played it.

Although the bad reviews will likely drag SoulCalibur’s user score down, fans of the series have already started to dilute its effect by giving the game some perfect scores to match. “I have to say it, but this game is far more superior than GTA 6,” one more recent 10/10 user review reads. “gta6 fanboys are sofa king we todd ed Soul Calibur > GTA6 GTA6 is dumb lololol,” reads another, which suggests to me that some of the people trying to diffuse SoulCalibur’s review bomb might actually get along with the people leaving negative reviews if they were to ever meet in real life.

It is all relatively meaningless though, particularly for SoulCalibur, because, again, that 98 isn’t going anywhere and it never will. That also means it will never creep up to a 99 and threaten GTA 6’s newly-won Metacritic crown. I’m also assuming the reviews that openly admit this is all about getting revenge for the GTA 6 review bomb will eventually be removed.

The GTA 6 reveal was "practically perfect," but there's one thing fans aren't sold on - the UI.
