Everything We Know About <em>The Batman: Part II</em>

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Batman: Part II director Matt Reeves has officially confirmed several major additions to the sequel’s cast, including Scarlett Johansson, Sebastian Stan, Brian Tyree Henry, and Sebastian Koch. In May 2026, Reeves announced the actors one by one on X, using a series of GIFs.

The follow-up to Reeves’s superhero reboot will once again star Robert Pattinson as Bruce Wayne, alongside returning cast members Jeffrey Wright, Andy Serkis, Colin Farrell, and Jayme Lawson. While many plot details remain tightly under wraps, the growing cast offers the clearest indication yet of where Reeves may be taking Gotham next—even if the movie could potentially come out later than previously anticipated.

Reeves confirmed on May 14 that Johansson, Stan, Henry, Koch, and Charles Dance have all joined the sequel.

Stan is widely expected to portray Gotham district attorney Harvey Dent, though Reeves has not publicly confirmed the role. Meanwhile, Henry and Koch’s roles have not yet been revealed. Dance is rumored to play Dent’s father, while Johansson is believed to be portraying Gilda Dent, Harvey’s wife.

The new additions join returning stars Pattinson as Batman, Wright as Jim Gordon, Serkis as Alfred Pennyworth, Farrell as Oz Cobb/The Penguin, Lawson as Mayor Bella Reál, and Gil Perez-Abraham as Officer Martinez.

Johansson opened up about her role in The Batman: Part II with Entertainment Tonight and revealed that the film was much more challenging than she expected. “It gave me a lot of existential angst for some reason,” she shared. “It was very hard to figure out. I don’t know why, whether it was just the role or where I’m in my life or what’s going on, but it was just like hard to crack it. And that’s all I can say about it. It was a tough role in a lot of ways.”

Reeves has shared very little about the plot, but he previously hinted that the sequel’s central villain is a character who has “never really been done in a movie before.”

In May, Reeves shared a behind-the-scenes image of the Batmobile driving through snow, confirming that at least part of the sequel will take place during winter.

The first film ended with Gotham partially flooded following the Riddler’s attacks, so the colder setting could reflect a city still recovering from political and physical devastation.

The image also marked one of the first signs that production was officially underway.

The film is currently scheduled to arrive in theaters on Oct. 1, 2027. Pattinson confirmed in a September interview with GQ that he was filming in London “till the end of the year” for the Batman sequel.

By October 11, Pattinson revealed that filming halted. “We’re pausing for a little bit and then we’re going to figure it all out,” he said during an interview with Official Pop Drop while on the red carpet for the premiere of Primetime in London.

Pattinson offered the update after representatives for DC Studios and Warner Bros. shared on October 5 that “production on The Batman Part II has been temporarily paused while Matt Reeves focuses on a family matter” in a statement, which was shared with multiple outlets. “Matt has the full support of DC Studios, and we look forward to continuing the journey soon.” The studios did not mention if the pause on filming will affect the movie’s release date.

On October 15, Warner Bros. confirmed in a statement that The Batman: Part II will proceed with a new director. “As Matt Reeves continues to attend to an ongoing family matter, Steven Soderbergh, as an admirer of Matt’s work, approached Matt and will step in to help the writer-director complete the final weeks of principal photography on The Batman Part II,” the statement read. “Matt and Steven will work closely together with the actors and crew to fulfill Matt’s vision of the film.”

Soderbergh’s work includes Ocean’s Eleven, Magic Mike, Black Bag, and The Christophers.
