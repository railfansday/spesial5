Adobe expands AI assistant into creative workflows

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Adobe Inc. today announced an expansion of its CX Enterprise Coworker agentic artificial intelligence platform into its GenStudio content supply chain platform.

The company said the integration move allows creative professionals to bring AI agents into production workflows to develop, manage and deliver personalized marketing content at large scale.

The announcement, made at its Adobe MAX conference in Miami, extends CX Enterprise Coworker beyond its initial focus on customer analytics and marketing into content planning, creation and distribution. The technology provides a conversational interface for coordinating AI agents across Adobe applications and third-party systems.

Adobe said it’s aiming to reduce the manual work involved in producing marketing campaigns while maintaining human oversight and brand consistency. It’s also introducing several new capabilities across its GenStudio portfolio, including AI-assisted campaign planning, automated brand compliance, reusable creative workflows and expanded 3D product visualization.

Central to the initiative is Adobe Brand Intelligence, a machine learning system that captures an organization’s brand standards, including the unwritten practices and preferences that traditionally have resided inside employees’ heads.

“As we move to a world where creatives are directing agents, the context that’s locked in the hearts and minds of creatives is made available to agents so they can start to produce content that is on brand,” said Varun Parmar, Adobe’s senior vice president of GenStudio and Firefly for Enterprise.

Brand Intelligence will be integrated into Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign and Firefly Creative Production for Enterprise. New intelligent asset adaptation capabilities can automatically resize and rearrange campaign designs for different media formats while preserving brand standards.

The system also incorporates continuous learning to keep guidelines current as creative practices evolve.

“It’s ingesting reviews, approvals and rejections by the creatives,” Parmar said. “Based on that information, it’s able to infer the latest guidelines.”

Administrators can inspect and override those conclusions to prevent outdated or incorrect practices from becoming embedded in automated workflows.

Another addition is Ideation Space, a collaborative AI-powered canvas within Adobe Workfront. It combines historical campaign performance, audience information and other enterprise data to help marketers develop campaign briefs.

In a demonstration, Adobe showed how Coworker could retrieve project assignments, research audience characteristics, coordinate with an AI copywriting agent and evaluate proposed designs using synthetic audience simulations. The platform can also be used to run surveys of human respondents.

Adobe is extending Firefly Graph for Enterprise with a feature called Capsules that packages complex creative workflows into simplified applications that other employees can run without mastering the underlying tools. Another feature, Node Publishing, allows teams to share reusable workflow components.

New 3D Digital Twin capabilities enable enterprises to modify product imagery, materials, lighting and camera angles and generate animations without repeatedly photographing physical products.

CX Enterprise Coworker supports external applications and custom-built systems through the Model Context Protocol, enabling enterprises to connect their existing technology investments.

Adobe emphasized that the expansion isn’t intended to give AI agents unrestricted authority over creative decisions. “CX Enterprise Coworker enables human-led agent-accelerated work,” Parmar said. “At every step when the agent is making a decision, it’s coming back to the human and asking.”

Early deployments have accelerated audience definition, traditionally a time-consuming part of campaign development, according to Adobe, although executives provided no quantitative productivity figures. The company expects similar improvements in content production.

The new GenStudio integrations are being made available today, with some Firefly Graph capabilities remaining in beta test. Adobe said the expanded Coworker functionality won’t require additional charges for customers with existing subscriptions.
