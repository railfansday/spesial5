Ross Stores stock plunges after cutting its profit forecast on higher fuel costs

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Ross Stores raised its full-year earnings forecast to $8.75 to $8.84 a share while it trimmed its fourth-quarter outlook

Ross Stores $ROST lowered its fourth-quarter profit forecast on Thursday, and its stock dropped as much as 12% in extended trading.

The off-price retailer reported third-quarter results and said it expects fourth-quarter earnings per share of $2.16 to $2.25, or growth of 8% to 13%. The prior range was $2.17 to $2.26. The new range falls below the $2.27 analysts expected, according to Bloomberg.

Higher fuel costs drove the reduction, with added spending on holiday staffing and advertising also contributing, Chief Financial Officer Bill Sheehan told analysts on a call, according to Bloomberg.

Ross Stores kept its fourth-quarter outlook for comparable store sales growth of 4% to 5%, on top of a 9% gain last year. The company raised its fiscal 2026 earnings forecast to $8.75 to $8.84 a share. That range includes a benefit of about 60 cents a share from refunds of tariffs imposed under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act, recognized in the second quarter.

For the third quarter ended Oct. 31, sales rose 12% to $6.3 billion from $5.6 billion a year earlier. Comparable store sales rose 8%, on top of a 7% gain last year. Net income was $606 million, compared with $512 million a year earlier, and earnings per share were $1.91, compared with $1.58. The company had guided for earnings of $1.75 to $1.83 a share.

Operating margin was 12.3%, up 70 basis points from a year earlier and above the company's plan of 11.7% to 12.0%.

"Customer traffic remained the primary driver of our comparable store sales growth," CEO Jim Conroy said in a statement.

Sales peaked at the start of the period, when shoppers hunting for back-to-school bargains filled the aisles, while weather that ran warmer than forecast dampened demand as autumn went on, Conroy said on the call, according to Bloomberg.

Ross Stores opened 51 stores in the quarter, completing its 2026 plan of 115 new locations. Ross Stores stock was up 22% for the year as of Thursday's close.
