Signe Bruun double lifts Real Madrid to 3-1 Champions League win over ten-player Inter

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

To help deliver timely coverage of women's football, we may use AI tools to support content creation and editing. While we strive for accuracy and editorial quality, readers should independently verify important information.

Real Madrid beat Inter Milano 3-1 in the fifth round of the UEFA Champions League Women league stage on Wednesday 18 November, with Signe Bruun scoring twice in the closing minutes at the Ciudad Real Madrid. Inter played more than half the match with ten players and still went in level at the break.

Lineth Beerensteyn opened the scoring for Alberto Toril’s side in the 28th minute. Three minutes later the hosts thought they had a chance to double the lead, but a penalty involving Elisa Senss was cancelled after a VAR review.

The game turned again before half-time. Vilhjálmsdóttir was sent off for serious foul play in the 42nd minute, after VAR confirmed the upgrade of her card. Senss was booked two minutes later.

Inter answered anyway. In first-half stoppage time, Michela Cambiaghi finished from a Brittany Cyrenie Raphino assist to make it 1-1, a goal scored after Gianpiero Piovani’s team had been reduced to ten.

Real Madrid dominated the numbers across the 90 minutes. They had 58% of the ball, took 30 shots to Inter’s 14 and forced 12 corners against 5. Their expected goals figure of 5.95 dwarfed Inter’s 1.09.

Rúnarsdóttir kept Inter in the contest for a long time. The goalkeeper made 9 saves in all, while Laia López had to make just 1 at the other end. Real Madrid put 12 attempts on target; Inter managed 2.

Bookings came steadily. Silvia Cristóbal was cautioned in the 48th minute, Pau Marc Quesada Tormos was booked for dissent in the 65th and Marija Ana Milinković saw yellow in the 79th.

The breakthrough finally arrived in the 85th minute. Bruun put Real Madrid back in front, and Lina Magull’s booking two minutes later summed up Inter’s growing strain. A VAR check then confirmed a penalty involving Linda Caicedo in stoppage time, and Bruun converted it in the fourth minute of stoppage time to seal the win.

The win extends Real Madrid’s unbeaten run to at least six matches in all competitions. That sequence includes a 2-2 draw with Bayern Munich, a 3-0 win over Paris FC and a 3-3 draw with FC Levante Badalona.

Inter are heading the other way. This was their third straight defeat, after a 4-1 loss to Manchester City and a 3-2 loss to Juventus. Earlier, they had beaten Lazio and AC Milan 3-0 each.

Both sides return to league action on Sunday 22 November. Real Madrid travel to Granada in the Primera División Femenina, while Inter visit Ternana in Serie A Women.
