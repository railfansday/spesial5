X is doubling down on short-form vertical video with a series of updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Earlier this month, X launched a new feature that allows users to clip moments from their favorite videos, add their own takes, and hit post without leaving the platform.

In theory, that tool (inventively named “Clipping”) is a win-win for everyone involved. Clips credit and link back to the original videos, and both original creators and clippers can earn revenue through X’s engagement-based creator rewards program (aka Original Content Rewards).

X isn’t stopping there, though. On November 20, the platform shared that “video is the fastest growing format on X,” adding that “Posts with video get 5x the reach and engagement, and 36% of all impressions now go to video.”

X is also beefing up monetization options for video creators. In addition to Original Content Rewards (which replaced the platform’s Revenue Sharing program earlier this fall), X has introduced Video Kickstart Bonus, “an invite-only pilot that pays creators a bonus for posting original vertical video under three minutes with no watermarks.”

TikTok, of course, didn’t stay offline for very long—but that hasn’t stopped X from continuing to refine its video offerings. In February of this year, the platform rolled out a redesigned immersive player intended to make video more watchable on mobile.

With its latest slate of video features and monetization options, X finally appears to be putting its money where its mouth is—and offering creators the tools they need to take advantage.
