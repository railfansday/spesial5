Nordstrom’s 2026 Black Friday Sale Is Full of Travel Essentials and Luxury Accessories

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Deals and pricing updated as of 3:04 a.m. on November 27, 2026.

We've officially made it to Black Friday, which means the deals are piling up and it’s time to start taking advantage of them. Whether you're determined to knock out all of your Christmas shopping or are finally ready to check things off your personal wish list, there is no better starting line than Nordstrom. You’ll find fabulous deals on everything from women’s clothing and high-tech skin tools to home goods and stocking stuffers for even the most difficult-to-shop-for relative.

Even better, some of our editors' favorite brands are the most significantly marked down during this year's Nordstrom Black Friday sale. Tried-and-true companies like Levi’s, Tumi, Skims, Dyson, and more are offering discounts of up to 60% off your dream pieces. As much as I wish Black Friday could last forever, unfortunately, the clock is ticking, and some of the best picks are selling out quick. Hurry up and get shopping before it’s too late!

Nordstrom is always my go-to when I need a travel uniform refresh, a stock-up on basics, or just feel like browsing. If you’re interested in an end-of-year wardrobe revamp but don’t want to break the bank, now is the best time to dive in. Brands like Skims, Ralph Lauren, Vuori, and Free People are all having terrific deals. Whether you're after an elegant wool coat to keep warm in the chillier months or a buttery-soft pair of leggings, you'll find exactly what you are looking for and save a pretty penny at the same time.

Nordstrom’s men’s section also has some unbeatable deals. Whether you're shopping for yourself or a loved one, you’ll find the best gifts and everyday essentials from top-tier brands like Lands' End, Carhartt, Levi's, and more. Looking for the perfect pair of trousers for the office? These discounted Bonobos dress pants will have you looking sharp every day of the week. Planning your next snowy adventure? This Outdoor Research winter jacket will keep you toasty and warm no matter how much the temperature drops.

Shoes are the cherry on top of every outfit (and they happen to be required in almost every public place), so curating the right collection is of utmost importance. Luckily, Nordstrom’s Black Friday shoe sale is offering some great deals. You can snag discounted Frye boots and Tory Burch loafers for a chic office-to-happy-hour evening, or if your Christmas plans involve planes, trains, or long car rides, a comfy New Balance sneaker or easy-to-slip-off Ugg boot is the way to go. Whatever your needs are, Nordstrom has your feet covered.

'Tis the season of travel, and whether you're off to visit family or finally take that bucket-list trip, you'll need something to pack your things in. Luggage can get expensive quickly, especially if you need multiple pieces at once, and that’s where Nordstrom’s Black Friday deals are changing the game. If you’re after an entire luggage collection revamp, this Brussels three-piece luggage set is over 50% off. Just looking for a luxe upgrade to your personal item? The Tumi Montclair tote bag will be your new favorite travel companion.

Though luggage is a crucial element of any trip, the accessories are what really make or break the travel experience. Sometimes the tiniest item can save you from an otherwise grueling travel day, and Nordstrom has no shortage of deals on these small-but-mighty accessories. Overpackers, these Ecotex packing cubes will save you from sitting on your suitcase to get it closed. Movie lovers, this in-flight wireless audio transmitter lets you avoid those flimsy, terrible-quality plane headphones. If I were you, I'd grab a new pair of Beats headphones to go with it (yes, they're also on sale!).

If you are a beauty lover, now is the time to stock up on skincare or buy that gadget you have been eyeing for ages. Nordstrom’s deals on tools like the NuFace Mini+ and the Omnilux Contour Mask are almost unbeatable, so it's time to treat yourself. If you're looking for a gift for someone in your life who loves luxury beauty products, this Prada hand cream and lip balm set is the perfect mix of practical and luxurious.

There's no better time to get started on Christmas shopping than Black Friday. Every year, it comes way faster than we can imagine, and it never fails to be at least a little overwhelming. Thankfully, Nordstrom has your gift-giving needs covered. Whether you’re looking to hide diamond earrings in a loved one's stocking or are excited to treat someone to their first Le Creuset pot, Nordstrom has something for every kind of person you need to shop for. Or, treat yourself! It never hurts to make your nighttime routine at home a little more cozy, and this robe, hair towel, and fuzzy blanket will do just that.
