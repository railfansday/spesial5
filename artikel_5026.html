The Butter Capital Limited, an active company limited by shares incorporated in the British Virgin Islands under Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) 98450051EB909E909F56, has announced the public launch of Butter Capital (https://www.butter.capital), a managed 30-day USD stablecoin staking platform supporting USD Coin (USDC) and Tether (USDT) across six EVM networks: Base, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Optimism and Ethereum.

Built for digital asset holders seeking structured stablecoin deployment without manually managing funding intervals, cross-chain bridges, or margin liquidation ladders, Butter Capital allows participants to open 30-day stakes starting from $10 USD. Staked capital backs active execution through Butter Cortex, the platform’s quantitative cryptocurrency perpetual futures consensus desk orchestrating Mythos, Claude, Grok, OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek, Perplexity, and Llama.

Users onboard in seconds via cryptographic Web3 wallet sign-in (MetaMask, Rainbow, Trust Wallet and Coinbase Wallet) or email and password, receiving one dedicated personal EVM deposit address (0x…) valid across all six supported chains. Confirmed USDC and USDT transfers credit at exact 1:1 USD parity with zero swap spread, $0 deposit fees, $0 idle management fees, and $0 platform withdrawal fees from a $10 USD minimum on Available Balance.

Before committing live capital, prospective users can test the complete staking workflow through a prefunded $10,000 virtual USDC Demo Sandbox simulating 30-day stake cycles, 24-hour accruals, and compounding controls in full isolation from live protocol balances.

Each 30-day stake accrues on an independent rolling 24-hour clock from activation within a variable applied daily yield band of 0.10% to 0.30% per day. Rather than charging fees on principal, Butter Capital deducts a flat 5.00% Cortex Performance Fee strictly from positive gross daily yield, waived to 0.00% across the entire first 30-day cycle for users joining via a partner or referral link.

Participants can adjust a live 0% to 100% Auto Compound setting on any active stake mid-cycle to either reinvest daily net USD credits into active principal or route payouts directly to their liquid Available Balance, alongside an optional Day 30 Auto Restake toggle. Active staked principal remains committed for its 30-day cycle, while Available Balance funds remain withdrawable on chain 24/7.

Butter Cortex monitors 12 liquid USDT margined perpetual markets, including BTC, ETH, SOL, DOT, OP, LINK, AVAX, SUI, LTC and NEAR, across five quantitative layers. Grok and Perplexity track live market catalysts and funding extremes; OpenAI, DeepSeek and Gemini score L2 order book depth and basis skew; Claude and Llama stress test correlation risk and enforce deterministic invalidation stops; and Mythos stages execution strictly when at least five of eight models align.

Open intraday positions remain private until session close to prevent front running and stop hunting. Every morning at 00:15 UTC, 100% of closed trades from the prior UTC session, including both profit fills and controlled stop-loss exits, publish at https://www.butter.capital/trades with entry and exit prices, USD spreads, UTC timestamps, quorum tags, and direct TradingView (BINANCE USDT.P) verification links. Internal session returns are accounted separately from customer staking rates, with surplus alpha retained in a reserve buffer.

Butter Capital offers a single-level, two-sided partner program from a $10 stake: referrers earn a 3.00% instant USD commission into Available Balance on eligible manual stakes plus an ongoing 5.00% share of invitees’ daily staking yield from platform revenue, while invited friends pay 0.00% Cortex Performance Fees across Cycle 1.

Public web servers derive user deposit addresses exclusively from watch-only extended public keys (xpub) without holding private mnemonics or signing keys, paired with optional TOTP authenticator 2FA withdrawal gates, 24-hour email change cooldowns, and mandatory 48-hour withdrawal freezes after any 2FA reset. Documentation and Whitepaper v1.3 are live at https://www.butter.capital/docs.

Butter Capital, operated by The Butter Capital Limited (British Virgin Islands, LEI 98450051EB909E909F56), is a managed 30-day USD stablecoin staking platform powered by Butter Cortex, an eight-model crypto perpetuals consensus desk publishing closed trading sessions daily at 00:15 UTC. The platform supports 1:1 USDC and USDT deposits and withdrawals from $10 across Base, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Optimism, and Ethereum with $0 platform fees.
