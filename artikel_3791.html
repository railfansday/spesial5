It's weird to type this out, but the GTA 6 release is finally upon us after a 13-year wait.

Red Dead Redemption 2 launched to acclaim at the time to ease the wait, though few things in gaming feel as big an event as a Grand Theft Auto release from Rockstar.

And thankfully, the wait looks to be worth it. The GamesRadar+ GTA 6 review lands on a perfect score, and says "Rockstar hasn't just set a new benchmark, it has delivered the highest-fidelity sandbox of the generation." It's not just us, either. The GTA 6 Metacritic score is a good 'un. In fact, only the OG Zelda: Ocarina of Time can match it.

So, we're probably on the same page. It's time to get the download going, and thoughts to our gaming brethren on PC who don't own a console.

To celebrate the moment, we're firing up the live blog to put everything you need to know in one, evolving space – from review buzz to launch requirements to exactly how the GTA 6 release time rollout is working. On that note, let's crack on.

And here we go! It may be GTA 6 release time for some sooner than others, but we'll all end up there eventually, right? So let's get into some things you'll need to know.

First things first. The main talking point today, as we count down the hours to launch, is critics' verdicts on GTA 6. As per our own GTA 6 review, we've landed on a perfect score.

"GTA 6 is the highest-fidelity sandbox of its generation," he says. "Rockstar has built an open world of truly astonishing scale, scope, and quality. There's a degree of sophistication to everything GTA 6 does, and there's no aspect of the series' gameplay that hasn't been improved over past installments."

We're not the only ones who dig this one either. The GTA 6 Metacritic score ranks it among the most popular games of all time. The only competition is The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. The original one, that is. Mind you, the remake is pretty nifty, too.

So! The game is good, though I have an inkling that plenty of you have already pre-ordered it. According to Sensor Tower, Rockstar has already made $1.45 billion from GTA 6, aided by nearly 90% of us buying the controversial $100 Ultimate Edition. Oof!

I imagine a few more people will be tossing some more money Rockstar's way.

So, for many of you who are jumping in, some housekeeping. First, the GTA 6 release time.

Rockstar isn't setting a singular time for people to jump in, so some of you will be playing sooner than others. Namely, congratulations to those of you who live in New Zealand or who know the trick. If you don't, our guide on how to play GTA 6 early with the New Zealand trick will sort you out.

If you've got some hours to wait, you're not alone. That said, you can still be productive. There's a new PS5 update you'll need to download to play GTA 6.

Sony hasn't confirmed what's behind the update, though we've got a good guess. Amid an AI explosion, PS5 jailbreaks have become more frequent in the run-up to GTA 6. Not only does that risk the latest Grand Theft Auto game being released before its official release date, but it could also result in it unofficially ending up on PC. Not ideal, no.

As we near release, though, it looks like Sony has managed ot hold this one off.

If you weren't already convinced, the GTA 6 reviews put Rockstar's latest in a good position for the upcoming Game Awards. Just yesterday, we found out that GTA 6 is up for several awards, including Game of the Year.

While that has led a few people to ask how that can be the case when it's not out yet, host Geoff Keighley has chimed in to add that reviewers behind the nominations have been playing it for three weeks. So, there is some reasoning to the inclusion.

You know it's a big day when none other than Rockstar president Sam Houser issues a rare statement. Here it is in full:

"Rockstar Games was founded on the belief that games could achieve the same distinction as all other forms of storytelling, become essential to modern culture, and to be thought of as stories and worlds that live on with all of us.

"This week, Grand Theft Auto returns to Leonida and modern-day Vice City. While things have changed and time has passed, one aspect holds true: We have tried to create an experience that will exceed your expectations while showing what this medium is capable of, and why we believe games are so special. Whether you can trace your journey to this game through the entire series or are experiencing Grand Theft Auto for the first time, this is a narrative set in a world full of possibilities — and it is up to you as the player to shape how it ultimately feels, and concludes. We hope it felt both familiar and entirely new, and above all, we hope that you had fun.

"We are incredibly grateful to you and everything that you do to celebrate games. We look forward to many more years of sharing these experiences with you."

As we wind down the live blog for the day, here's a fun one for you. One of the many mad details of GTA 6 is that all of its refrigerators sound different. It's all in the details, baby.

"Much of the dynamic soundscape is informed by the incredibly detailed art throughout the world," says audio VP Alastair MacGregor.

"One small example is that convenience store refrigerators all have their own unique sounds. In a run-down store, the refrigerators and freezers are loud and sound like they are badly maintained. Their compressor units can be heard mechanically whirring away from years of neglect.

"As a result, the bottles behind the doors can be heard shaking and the units click on and off more frequently. In an up-market store, the refrigerators are quieter, adding a subtle hum to the environment."

Game of the year, people. Game of the year.
