Brandy and Monica Tease Joint Las Vegas Residency Will Be on 'Another Level'

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Brandy and Monica are ready to roll the dice and take fans back to the ‘90s with their upcoming joint Las Vegas residency.

“This is going to be another level,” Brandy, 47, exclusively told Us on Tuesday, November 10, after performing alongside Monica, 46, during Dancing With the Stars’ Grammy Night.

The musician teased that the residency, which comes after their 2025 The Boy Is Mine Tour, will be “elevated and just great and phenomenal, and I’m just so excited.”

Brandy added that she “can’t wait to get to work” on the 10-show experience ahead of the tour kickoff over Memorial Day weekend in 2027.

“We have a lot to prepare for, but we look forward to it,” Monica revealed, noting that she and Brandy “really start to create when we get together.”

She promised that the pair will get into Resorts World Las Vegas and “make it an experience” that fans won’t soon forget.

“[We’ll] make sure we take them into what it was like to be a part of music from 1994 up until this very moment,” Monica told Us.

Brandy and Monica initially collaborated on “The Boy Is Mine” in 1988. The duet was featured on both artist’s second studio albums, Brandy’s Never Say Never and Monica’s The Boy Is Mine.

The track and its accompanying music video made a massive splash in the music industry, being nominated for two MTV Video Music Awards, including Video of the Year and Best R&B Video.

The song won a Grammy for Best R&B Vocal Performance by a Duo or Group and received two other nominations in 1999.

Monica and Brandy reunited in 2012 to record the “It All Belongs to Me” single. They also made a cameo in Ariana Grande’s 2024 music video for her song “The Boy Is Mine,” which is a reimagining of the ‘90s classic.

Amid the song’s resurgence, Brandy and Monica embarked on their The Boy Is Mine Tour, which ran from October to December 2025 across the U.S.

It was revealed during Tuesday’s DWTS that Brandy and Monica will be coheadlining The Boy Is Mine residency in Las Vegas. The shows will take place in Resorts World Theatre beginning on May 29, 2027 and will run through July 17, 2027.

When asked why fans are craving all-things ‘90s, including their music, Brandy told Us, “Because the ’90s is where it’s at. It’s the best. I just feel like that’s where it’s at.”

The Moesha alum pointed out, “The most incredible artists came out in the ’90s. Whitney [Houston] was in the ’90s. Mariah Carey‘s in the ’90s.”

Monica couldn’t agree more, saying that all the ‘90s artists were “individuals” who flourished in their respective lanes.

“We all had our own sound, our own vibes, and it was so welcomed. It wasn’t where everything sounded the same,” she recalled. “That’s what I think people are craving, those differences and those nuances between all of us that made us all so different.”

Monica added that by teaming up with Brandy, fans will get the best of both worlds — past and present.

“Coming together, it just brings you to that moment two times over,” she concluded. “So that’s how we want people to feel when they come to Resorts World.”

Tickets for The Boy Is Mine residency go on sale Friday, November 20.

Dancing With the Stars airs on ABC and Disney+ Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET.
