The PC specs for Mass Effect-like RPG Exodus are basically bearable for a blockbuster space opera in 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Not sure how much RAM Matthew McConaughey requires these days

Between Matthew McConaughey, the flesh cathedrals, the space bears, FemJun and the tragedy of Biggs "The Bolt" Keil, the pre-release reveals for Mass Effect-like space RPG Exodus have been a real emotional rollercoaster. It's almost disappointing to learn that the recommended PC system specs are just sort of… fine. Broadly within expectations, by 2026 standards, anyway.

True, you'll need one of those Nvidia RTX 5080 things if you want to play it in 4K at 60fps, but the True Gamer understands that 4K/60FPS is a dangerous and blasphemical overreach, akin to building the Tower of Babel out of cats. 1080p/30FPS was perfectly good enough for Virtua Tennis 3 on Xbox 360, and it'll be perfectly good enough for this game about *squints*… octopus mercenaries. For that, you'll only need an i5-10400, an Nvidia 2060 and a mere 16GB of RAM. Granted, I think new PCs with those components currently cost $200,000, but that isn't Exodus's fault. It's that Jensen Huang who's to blame. Here's the complete spec rundown, anyway.

Will you be playing Exodus? I think it looks swankier so far than Owlcat's surprisingly unconvincing The Expanse: Osiris Reborn, that other great hope of people who mourn Mass Effect 5, but I haven't spent any time with a build. It's out 7th April 2027 - I imagine these recommended specs might alter as they stuff in more McConaughey and layer up the space bears.
