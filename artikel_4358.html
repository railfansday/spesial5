Plants Vs Zombies: Replanted set to land on Apple Arcade with new Party Mode

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Out of the cornerstone mobile game series out there, Plants Vs Zombies has occupied a special place in the hearts of many players. With so many fans devoted to the series, I'm sure there's more than a few of you who'll be excited to learn that Plants Vs Zombies: Replanted is coming to mobile, exclusively via Apple Arcade!

Plants Vs Zombies: Replanted reinvents and remasters the hit 2009 original, with widescreen support, hi-res art and a variety of quality-of-life features to boot. Basically, it's all you loved about the original, as you battle to defend your lawn from hordes of invading zombies with your trusty plants, just brought up to modern standards!

However, for Apple Arcade fans, it's a definite win. The Arcade version of Plants Vs Zombies: Replanted even comes with Party Mode, an optional cosmetic mode that sees the zombies don neon shirts, transforms Cherry Bombs into pinatas, and generally adds a festive flavour to proceedings with plenty of confetti.

PvZ: Replanted for Apple Arcade also comes packaged with the new Cloudy Day and Rest In Peace modes exclusive to the remaster. So, if you're looking to relive 2009 all over again, set your watches for December 3rd to join forces with Crazy Dave and save the garden once more!
