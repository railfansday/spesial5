The 6 Best Floor Scrubbers for a Deeper Clean on Any Surface

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

There's no need to get down and dirty when you have one of these powerful machines.

There's no reason to get down on your hands and knees to clean your floors. If you want to get the job done with minimal effort, invest in a floor scrubber to do the dirty work for you. Floor scrubbers lift out set-in grime, stains, and surface mildew quickly and efficiently on practically any hard surface—without the back-breaking labor.

While a few non-powered floor scrubbers are worth considering (we recommend one), most have powerful electric motors. The full-size machines on our list are corded, and we’ve also included a battery-powered spin scrubber for tighter spaces like bathrooms. These machines use floor-safe abrasive pads, bristle brushes, and microfiber rollers to deliver a level of deep clean that's virtually impossible to get with a mop or hand tools alone. Some add steam to clean and sanitize or suction like a vacuum, and some even tackle carpet.

We used our cleaning and home care expertise to find models worth your attention and investment. These are the best floor scrubbers we recommend.

The floor scrubbers we cover work on just about any hard surface. This includes hardwood, laminate, tile, stone, concrete, and brick, though note that some scrubbing bristles may be too coarse for certain types of vinyl or unfinished wood. Also, if your hardwood floorboards have lots of space between them, it may be best to go with a model that cleans without water.

Some of the models we cover—including our Best Overall pick—are safe for carpet. While floor scrubbers may be great for deep cleaning some carpets and rugs, our recommendations below are tailored to hard surfaces. To deep clean your carpet, check out our story on the best carpet steam cleaners.

Non-powered floor scrubbers are essentially heavy-duty push brooms with features that make cleaning easier, like telescoping poles and squeegees on the side opposite the bristles. They're far more affordable and compact than electric floor scrubbers, so if you’re short on space or don’t want to spend $100 or more on a new product, opt for the manual model we recommend.

If you go electric, corded and cordless models use a spinning or suction mechanism for fast, deep cleaning. When paired with water or a cleaning solution, they remove grime and scum much faster than a non-powered floor scrubber. Some models add in steam to break up baked-in dirt and sanitize.

Many floor scrubbers include features that enhance cleaning, including multiple scrubber heads to tackle different spaces, like tight corners or bathroom floors. Some models, like our Best Overall pick, are all-in-one cleaning machines that clean, sand, and finish floors with buffing pads or polishing heads.
