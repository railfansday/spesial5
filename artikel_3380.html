Skydance to Combine Warner Bros. Television Group and Paramount Television Studios as One Big Studio

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The corporate shakeups keep coming at Skydance, this time to the TV department. Warner Bros. Television Group and Paramount Television Studios are being combined as one big studio under Skydance, with WB’s Channing Dungey and Paramount’s Matt Thunell serving as presidents of the combined studio.

The new studio, which officially launches in early 2027 (the two entities will keep operating independently until then), will be known as Warner Bros. Television Studios. The entire studio will report to George Cheeks, co-chair and Chief Content Officer of Skydance TV. As previously announced, Dungey also serves as Chair of Skydance Global Cable Media, with oversight over all 50+ WB Discovery and Paramount cable brands. Together, Dungey and Thunell will lead all of scripted and unscripted programming, overseeing all creative departments. They will also oversee BET Studios and Warner Bros. International Television Production’s studios around the world.

CBS Studios will continue to operate independently, headed by President David Stapf, who also reports to Cheeks. CBS Studios International, Skydance Sports Entertainment and See It Now Studios will operate under the CBS Studios umbrella.

The new Warner Bros. Television Studios will be home to more than 120 active television series and some of the industry’s biggest global franchises, including “Harry Potter and The Philosopher’s Stone” (HBO), “The Pitt” (HBO Max), “Ted Lasso” (Apple TV), “Georgie & Mandy’s First Marriage” (CBS), “Reacher” (Prime Video), “Marshals” (CBS), “Landman” (Paramount+), “Emily in Paris” (Netflix) and the upcoming series “Neuromancer” (Apple TV).

Yesterday, Skydance made some big changes to its film side of the business, appointing Don Granger and Kira Goldberg to lead Paramount’s Motion Picture Group and Jesse Ehrman to lead Warner Bros. Pictures. It also recently shook up DC Studios, with James Gunn and Peter Safran exiting and giving way to Jeremy Latcham.

“Bringing together two of the industry’s most prolific television studios creates a formidable creative engine, uniting extraordinary talent, resources, acclaimed programming and iconic franchises with the global scale to compete at the highest level. With Channing and Matt at the helm, we have two exceptional leaders who understand what it takes to empower creative talent, foster bold ideas and deliver programming that resonates with audiences everywhere,” Cheeks said in a statement. “Alongside CBS Studios, led by the incomparable David Stapf, Warner Bros. Television Studios gives us two world-class studios, with the breadth, depth and creative ambition to deliver for Skydance platforms and third-party partners across the industry.”

“Matt has extraordinary creative instincts, and a deep understanding of great storytelling. I couldn’t ask for a better partner as we bring these storied studios together,” Dungey said in a statement. “We have a unique opportunity to create something truly special for our creative partners and audiences around the world. I’m very excited to begin this new chapter.”

Thunell said, “Channing is one of the most accomplished and respected executives in our industry, with an extraordinary track record of building successful businesses, developing enduring partnerships and delivering some of television’s most acclaimed programming. Ever since our time together at Netflix, I’ve hoped we’d have the opportunity to work together again, and I’m thrilled that day has finally come. We’re bringing an unmatched roster of talent under one roof, building on the remarkable legacies of two storied studios. The possibilities ahead are extraordinary, and I couldn’t be more excited to partner with Channing to champion the ambitions of our creative talent and bring exceptional stories to audiences around the world.”
