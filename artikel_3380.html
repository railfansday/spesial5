Elden Ring: Raging Wolf Officially Releases Spring 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Elden Ring may very well be FromSoftware's magnum opus, a feat that has, consequently, led to it getting numerous spin-offs, expanded media releases, and merchandise, all of which are treasured by fans worldwide. From its game-sized DLC to the highly anticipated big-budget Elden Ring movie, there truly is no escaping the magnitude of this incredible game. So, perhaps it should come as no surprise that fans are being treated to yet another Elden Ring release in 2027.

It comes at a rather opportune moment, as fans have been left waiting for the next FromSoftware game and the ever-elusive Bloodborne sequel that will, surely, release at some point. This new release, aptly titled Raging Wolf, looks absolutely incredible and will almost assuredly be the centerpiece of any fan's collection. However, as is often typical of Elden Ring merchandise, it comes with a rather significant caveat that fans should be aware of.

The Raging Wolf release joins a pantheon of upcoming Elden Ring merchandise that has fans excited, if only because it means even more statues to adorn one's shelf with. It is a 1/6 scale statue of the game's protagonist, the Tarnished, wearing the iconic Raging Wolf armor set as seen on the game's box art. Produced by premium figurine manufacturer GECCO with close supervision from FromSoftware, this is a premium statue, packed with details that fans are sure to appreciate.

The Elden Ring: Raging Wolf statue comes fully painted, with everything from the scales on the metal armor to the leather straps beneath expertly sculpted. Even the statue's base has had additional consideration given to it, as it has been designed to resemble the path leading to the legendary Stormveil Castle. This is truly a piece of Elden Ring merchandise designed for and aimed at fans, something that is unequivocally represented in the Raging Wolf statue's exorbitant price.

If you want to pick up this 1/6 scale statue (which is approximately 12.9 inches tall), you'll need to spend an eye-watering $469.99. Of course, you're getting an intricately detailed and painted statue for that price, something that typically does not come cheap. Still, it's an expensive purchase, and one that is definitely targeting dedicated fans with a lot of disposable income. For those ready to take the plunge, you can pre-order it right now via the official Bandai Namco website.

The Elden Ring: Raging Wolf statue has a tentative release date of January 30, 2027. This is according to the countdown on the official Bandai Namco store listing for the statue. However, this is, as always, subject to change, and the statue's release date could be further delayed.

Regardless, there isn't too long to wait until you can get your hands on this incredible new Elden Ring statue. Of course, one can hope that, beyond the likes of these highly detailed and niche collectibles, FromSoftware elects to release another game within the Elden Ring series, or, indeed, elevates its ideas in an altogether new IP.
