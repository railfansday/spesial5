Taylor Sheridan’s Hit Crime Series Was Saved From Cancellation By Major Netflix-Paramount Deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Taylor Sheridan's Mayor of Kingstown is bidding farewell with season 5, but it almost ended even earlier.

Co-created by Sheridan and Hugh Dillon, who also stars in the Paramount+ series, the crime drama has followed Jeremy Renner as Mike McLusky as he works as the eponymous power broker in the fictional Michigan prison town, striving to keep the peace between the various gangs and the law in the area. Though starting with a mixed response from critics, Mayor of Kingstown became a critical sensation in subsequent seasons, though its streamer announced in January 2026 that the show would end with season 5.

In a new report from Deadline chronicling Netflix's recent partnerships with different studios for licensing streaming deals, sources report that Mayor of Kingstown was actually going to be cancelled outright after season 4. It wasn't until Netflix inked its major deal with Paramount for 20 shows that Paramount+ elected to keep Sheridan's hit crime series going, as the former streamer was "not willing to take the other titles without it."

This, per Deadline's sources, led to Mayor of Kingstown's producers using Netflix's confidence to "leverage the series' inclusion to get an extra season" of the Jeremy Renner-led show. Paramount+ subsequently renewed the crime drama for its abbreviated fifth and final season, which the publication points out "was announced just days before the Netflix licensing deal was announced" that included the first four seasons of the series.

A crime thriller series set in a small Michigan town where the McClusky family wields influence among police, criminals, and politicians. As power brokers, they navigate the complexities of a community reliant on the business of incarceration.
