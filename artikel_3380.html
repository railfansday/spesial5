The Spy Thriller That Inspired ‘Reacher’ Officially Sets 2027 Return With First Season 2 Footage

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Although The Terminal List has since taken the spotlight on the streamer, Reacher is still going strong as the king of Prime Video action. The Alan Ritchson-led series debuted its fourth season back in August and has a long future planned out, with Seasons 5 and 6 already locked and loaded, and the former set to adapt Make Me, the 20th book in the thriller saga penned by Lee Child. On top of that, viewers got a double dose of Ritchson's mountainous former major thanks to the debut of the Maria Sten-led spin-off Neagley back in September. Filming is now halfway done on Reacher's latest outing as of this month, according to Ritchson, setting up the series for a potential return as soon as late 2027. Before then, though, a spy franchise that Child cited as an inspiration for his own writing will redeploy.

Peacock recently confirmed that The Day of the Jackal wouldn't be back for Season 2 before the new year, but the good news is that the wait won't be much longer. Eddie Redmayne first took up the mantle of the titular assassin almost exactly two years ago, bringing Frederick Forsyth's thriller story into the modern day. Since Season 1 wrapped up, the show has gotten a globe-spanning upgrade under new showrunner David Harrower, with an expanded cast that includes Weruche Opia, Pablo Schreiber, Matt Bomer, and Pia Tjelta as newcomers alongside returning faces like Úrsula Corberó as the Jackal's wife, Nuria, Charles Dance as his billionaire employer, Timothy Winthrop, and more. Now, a proper release window is set for his next operation.

The first teaser for Season 2 has been unveiled, taking a scene directly from the opening moments to demonstrate the newfound sense of scale the series is working with. It opens with the Jackal atop a mountain range, glancing out over the scenic vista while a narration reminds viewers just how dangerous he is. Most importantly, the voice confirms that they, and their organization, have their eyes on the assassin from now on as he leaps from the peak in dramatic fashion. Along with the short video, Peacock confirmed that new episodes will arrive in March 2027. An exact date will be revealed as the return draws closer.

No exact details about The Day of the Jackal Season 2's story have been revealed, but the Season 1 finale gives a good idea of where Charles is heading next. After assassinating the tech giant UDC (Khalid Abdalla) on behalf of Winthrop, the Jackal learns from Zina (Eleanor Matsuura) that Winthrop has deceived them both, not just refusing to pay the price for his enemy's head, but paying to have the two of them dealt with as "loose ends." While they want to go after the billionaire and his associates, Charles has a higher priority — finding his wife. Reuniting with his family will be the main goal, though it won't be easy with a powerful enemy like Winthrop against him and new foes coming out of the woodwork on his mission. However it unfolds, it's expected to be a much different story from the cat-and-mouse thriller that defined the first run.

The Day of the Jackal Season 2 premieres in March 2027. Check out the first teaser in the player above.
