All 7 Episodes Of Netflix's 10/10 Western Are Perfect From Start To Finish

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

While the small-screen Westerns of the 2010s might be hit-and-miss, Netflix’s seven-episode masterpiece hasn’t got a single weak episode in its entire run. Hard as it might be to believe in 2026, there was a time when Westerns were the biggest genre on TV. Admittedly, this was back in the days of Gunsmoke and Rawhide, when shows set in the Wild West produced record numbers of live-action episodes and set early TV ratings records.

However, as the decades went on, cop shows gradually replaced Westerns as the most-watched genre on TV, and the genre wouldn’t regain its mainstream popularity until the rise of the Yellowstone franchise. While the Yellowstone spinoff Marshals is CBS’s biggest show of 2026, it is important to note that the spinoff is part Western and part police procedural. Sharing many building blocks in common with cop show franchises like CSI, NCIS, FBI, and Law & Order accounts for a lot of the show’s mainstream popularity.

Even Yellowstone itself and its sequel Dutton Ranch are contemporary crime dramas as much as they are Westerns, since Sheridan’s greatest trick was selling a modern-day spin on Dynasty and Dallas as a “Neo-Western” rather than the trashy, soapy family saga that the original series really was. To find a traditional historical Western, viewers need to look to Yellowstone’s prequels 1883 and 1923, or Netflix originals like American Primeval, The Abandons, and the underrated seven-episode masterpiece Godless.

Released in 2017, Godless was inexcusably missed by many viewers upon its original release. Set in 1884 New Mexico, Godless stars Sinners supporting star Jack O’Connell as the outlaw Roy Goode, who hides out in the New Mexico town of La Belle as he attempts to avoid a murderous mob boss, his former employer Frank Griffin. In an unsettling subversion of his usual genial screen presence, The Newsroom star Jeff Daniels wows as the icy, terrifying Griffin.

However, the story of Godless isn’t defined by male characters like O’Connell’s Goode, Daniels’ Griffin, or even Scoot McNairy’s local sheriff, Bill McNue. Thanks to a mining disaster, McNue is one of the only male citizens left living in La Belle, a town that is populated almost exclusively by women. These women include Michelle Dockery’s steely wealthy widow Alice Fletcher, Merrit Weaver’s more welcoming Mary Agnes, and a host of other well-sketched heroines with complex motivations and backstories.

Thus, Godless sets up a story that functions as both a tense, gripping self-contained small town siege Western and also a daring subversion of the genre at the same time. While most traditional historical Westerns sideline their female characters in favor of stories focused on sheriffs and outlaws, Godless forces viewers to think about the women left on the margins of these old-fashioned stories.

However, no one should mistake the Netflix show for a dry, academic deconstruction of the genre. Even compared to Sheridan’s more famous Yellowstone, Godless is a dark, daring, and thoroughly enthralling Western through and through. Unpredictable and inventive, Godless is as satisfying as any earlier Western show like Deadwood or AMC’s underrated drama Hell on Wheels.

Sadly, this didn’t do anything to help the show’s performance. While Godless was a big, well-deserved hit with reviewers, the show seemingly failed to find a major mainstream audience as the series arrived shortly before the modern Western revival was kickstarted by the success of Yellowstone in 2018. As noted above, Yellowstone’s success spawned the spinoffs 1883, 1923, Marshals, and Dutton Ranch.

However, the show also helped get the green light for further Neo-Westerns from Sheridan, such as Landman, Lawmen: Bass Reeves, and arguably even the crime drama The Mayor of Kingstown. Outside of Sheridan’s work, Netflix’s Ransom Canyon proved that Godless might be better known if the iconic show had just debuted a decade later.
