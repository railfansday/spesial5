John Belton: This Volatility Isn’t Healthy for the Market

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

John Belton, Gabelli Funds Portfolio Manager, Growth Portfolios MD, discusses a sharp reversal in markets after last week’s broad selloff: Nvidia added about $950 billion in market value, the largest one-day increase for any stock, while the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rose 13% and CoreWeave gained 21%. Investors treated Jensen Huang’s return as a pivotal event for the AI economy, with Nvidia’s outsized influence likened to that of a central bank. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
