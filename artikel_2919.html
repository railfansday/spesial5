Katapult (KPLT) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Merger synergies and a shift to e-commerce shaped this quarter's results.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Katapult Holdings, Inc. (KPLT +8.21%) reported its first quarterly results following the merger of Katapult, Aaron's, and CCFI, a transaction that closed on Aug. 11, 2026. Management reported that the integration process has identified $30 million in annualized cost synergies, primarily through consolidated leadership, vendor rationalization, and shared corporate platforms. The company stated that it operates two primary segments: Consumer Finance and Lease-to-Own & Retail, serving a combined customer base of more than 2.1 million active users. Financial results for the quarter were influenced by merger-related costs and a higher interest expense environment. The company indicated that its strategic focus is centered on optimizing its proprietary data set for underwriting and simplifying its complex capital structure to reduce funding costs.
