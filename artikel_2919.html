Kicker Smyth elevated to Saints' playing roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Kicker Charlie Smyth has been elevated to the New Orleans Saints' playing roster and has signed a new deal with the NFL franchise.

The former gaelic footballer, from Mayobridge in Northern Ireland, has started the last two NFL games for the Saints after being promoted from the practice squad.

The 25-year-old was handed a second opportunity to be the Saints' kicker after Dan Carlson, who started the season as first choice, was released at the start of November due to mixed form.

Smyth helped the Saints to an overtime win over the Cleveland Browns on his return to the team and then kicked a late 55-yard field goal in the 20-17 defeat by the Carolina Panthers on Sunday.

NFL rules state a player from a practice squad cannot be used more than three times in a season, and Smyth has been rewarded for his impressive return.

He made his NFL debut for New Orleans last November but was released by the franchise in August.

Smyth was retained on their practice squad due to having an exemption as he came through the NFL's international player pathway.

His first game since signing his new deal is at the Chicago Bears on Sunday at 18:00 GMT.
