Steelers’ DK Metcalf ejected after heated Eagles skirmishes — as Kevin Harlan takes brutal ‘brawls’ jab

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Steelers wide receiver DK Metcalf was ejected from their loss to the Eagles on Sunday following a pair of unsportsmanlike penalties, marking his second time being tossed from a game in the last two seasons.

The second penalty occurred following an incomplete pass from Aaron Rodgers to Germie Bernard in the end zone, when Metcalf and Marcus Epps got into a skirmish near the area as a group of players gathered around.

DK Metcalf is ejected for unsportsmanlike conduct. CBS Sports

"He's done nothing statistically but continues to get in these brawls," CBS play-by-play broadcaster Kevin Harlan said.

CBS broadcast cameras also captured Metcalf putting his hand near Eagles defensive back Cooper DeJean's face mask, too.

Metcalf also drew an unnecessary roughness penalty in the first half after a skirmish with DeJean.

It marked the third time in his career that Metcalf has been ejected, according to ESPN — tied for the most across that stretch with 49ers linebacker Dre Greenlaw.

Metcalf had collected just one catch for two yards during Sunday's game, and he entered with just 432 receiving yards and a pair of touchdowns this season.
