4 takeaways from Kansas State basketball's bounceback vs CSUN

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

MANHATTAN — That's more like it. Well, at least the first half was.

Kansas State rebounded from its opening night loss with a 95-78 win over Cal State Northridge on Friday, Nov. 6, at Bramlage Coliseum. The win is the first for K-State under Casey Alexander.

The win follows a loss to South Dakota State on Monday, Nov. 3. The Wildcats (1-1) will host Creighton (2-0) on Tuesday, Nov. 10, for their first high-major game of the season.

Here are our immediate takeaways from Friday night.

The Wildcats made only eight 3-pointers in their loss to SDSU earlier in the week. The Wildcats made their eighth 3-pointer on Friday against CSUN with 5:05 left in the first half.

K-State showed Bramlage Coliseum the efficient 3-point-shooting offense that Alexander is known for. His Belmont team was No. 1 in the country from beyond the arc last year.

As a team, K-State finished 17-for-41 from beyond the arc. Eleven of the makes came in the first half.

Brandon Rechsteiner, a Colorado State transfer, was the orchestrator of the Wildcats' offense. He caught fire early, making his first four 3-point attempts.

Confidence isn't an issue for him, and he got in a groove quickly. He also showed he's got some physicality to his game, willing to lower the shoulder a little bit and initiate contact to create some blocking fouls. That's no surprise when his brother spent some time in the NFL, and his dad is a WWE Hall of Famer.

Rechsteiner finished with a career-high 26 points while going 6-for-15 from deep. He cooled after halftime, scoring 19 of his points and making five 3-pointers in the first half.

The majority of the positive vibes came from the first half. The second half was disgusting.

Turnovers, missed shots, fouls on 3-point attempts and lengthy scoring droughts led to some gross play. The Wildcats, thankfully, went into halftime up by 28.

Seven of their 17 turnovers came in the second half. K-State only scored 38 after halftime, while the Matadors cut the Wildcats' lead to as few as 15.

The Wildcats will play their first high-major when they host Creighton on Tuesday, Nov. 8, at 8 p.m. CT. It's the backend of a home-and-home that saw K-State beat the Bluejays in Omaha, Nebraska, last year.

Creighton hasn't played anyone of note. It opened the season with a 15-point win over Kansas City before winning 83-55 over Le Moyne on Thursday, Nov. 5.

Notably, Creighton is led by Alan Huss, who was involved in Kansas State's coaching search this past offseason. Huss was already on Creighton's staff as its coach-in-waiting, and opted to take the job after Greg McDermott's retirement.
