Milwaukee Bucks vs. Detroit Pistons Preview & Game Thread: Time to get back on track

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Milwaukee Bucks are taking on the shorthanded Detroit Pistons in their fourth NBA Cup game, looking to get back on track after a disappointing performance against the Brooklyn Nets. This will be the first meeting between the two teams and won't do the Pistons justice, as they are missing many of their key players. However, that's good news for Bucks fans, as it opens the door for Milwaukee to secure a win that otherwise would have been unlikely.

The Pistons' record hasn't matched their expectations after finishing atop the Eastern Conference last season. A key factor has been the absence of All-NBA big man Jalen Duren, who has been out with a hamstring strain and has appeared in only two games this season. The Pistons are a top-five defence, so the Bucks could run into a lot of issues, especially with their tendency to turn the ball over. But things aren't getting any easier for the Pistons either, as they face a handful of key players out for this game. Detroit runs many small-ball lineups, considering their two big men, John Collins and Paul Reed, are both just 6'9"; therefore, Kel'el Ware and Myles Turner could be in for some big games if they can establish themselves down low. The Bucks will need to protect the ball and lean into a team-first brand of basketball to beat a Pistons team looking to prove it can win without its stars.

The Pistons are undersized at center; Myles Turner could be in for a big game if he can get his offence going and play with high physicality.

Who: Milwaukee Bucks (2-17) vs. Detroit Pistons (11-6).

Myles Turner continues the form he showed in the Raptors game, and the Bucks keep leaning on him. The big fella goes for 20 points and 10 rebounds.
