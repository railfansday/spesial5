“If the devil is in the details, then this is the most unholy tent we’ve ever occupied.” That’s how we started our review of the Sea to Summit Telos TR2 when it won a Backpacker Editors’ Choice Award in 2021. This tent seems to excel at everything it does: At a trail weight weight of 3 pounds 4.3 ounces, it’s light, and with a floor space of 28.2 square feet and 43.5 inches of headroom, larger than average for the category. But the numbers don’t tell the whole story about why we love this tent. Smart features, including dual doors and vestibules, stuff sacks that turn into clip-on storage and a hanging light bar, and a roll-up fly for stargazing, abound. Bonus: The tent body, poles, and fly pack into separate stuff sacks, so you can split the Telos’s already-modest weight with your partners.
