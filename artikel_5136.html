Cardano is trading near $0.15 today, down -5.8% overnight amid broader market weakness. In an over-the-top bullish scenario, Claude AI predicts that ADA could stage one of the more dramatic recoveries among large-cap Layer-1s by January 1, 2027.

The landmark catalyst driving this extreme upside is a highly unlikely but not impossible supercharged outcome of Cardano’s real progress on regulated assets.

(SOURCE: Claude AI Predicts a Huge ADA Rally is on the Way)

Building on the recent mainnet launch of CIP-0113 (the programmable token standard that allows issuers to embed transfer restrictions, identity checks, and compliance rules directly into assets), a major international consortium of banks, asset managers, and a key regulatory body formally designates Cardano as a preferred settlement layer for large-scale tokenized real-world assets and regulated securities.

In this hypothetical, the network becomes the default rail for a significant portion of institutional RWA issuance, creating sustained demand for ADA as the native gas and collateral token and triggering a wave of treasury accumulation and ecosystem inflows.

ADA has been under steady pressure, sliding from levels near $0.18–$0.20 earlier in the month into the mid-$0.14s before stabilizing around $0.15.

The chart shows a series of lower highs, with key support now resting in the $0.14–$0.145 zone and stronger demand potentially waiting near $0.12–$0.13 if selling resumes.

Resistance sits at $0.17–$0.18 and then the more significant $0.22–$0.25 area. Momentum indicators remain weak after the recent decline, leaving substantial room for a reversal if buying interest returns.

In a conventional recovery, a sustained break above $0.18–$0.20 would be needed to shift the structure higher and open a path toward $0.30–$0.40.

Under the extreme institutional RWA-adoption catalyst outlined above, those intermediate levels would be cleared rapidly, allowing multi-cycle measured moves and Fibonacci extensions from the current multi-month base to project far higher

While present price action reflects broader risk-off conditions and limited near-term catalysts, the combination of Cardano’s regulated-asset tooling and the theoretical potential for large-scale institutional selection creates a setup from which a parabolic move remains possible in a strong risk-on environment.

Several developments provide a foundation for this scenario. The Cardano Foundation confirmed that CIP-0113 went live on mainnet in October, enabling programmable compliance features for tokenized assets.

Development updates continue on scaling Leios (via DijkstraNet) to support higher throughput. On the real-world side, Cardano has been used in India’s Uttar Pradesh police forensic evidence system, with discussions of broader expansion.

These items keep Cardano in conversations around institutional readiness, regulated assets, and governance maturation even while price action remains subdued.

Anthropic’s Claude AI predicts ADA could reach between $4.50 and $7.00 by January 1, 2027, with a stretch target above $9 in the most aggressive outcome.

This is deliberately aggressive and highly speculative. The catalyst would require a fundamental change in how global markets operate, something that remains a long shot.
