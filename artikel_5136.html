Shiba Inu (SHIB) printed its first bullish RSI signal on the daily chart since September after rebounding from its lowest price since 2021. According to TradingView, SHIB's market cap was back above $2.1 billion on Friday and stood at $2.19 billion, about $500 million below its moving average at $2.67 billion ahead of Christmas.

SHIB was trading at around $0.0000037 on Friday, up 1.8% over 24 hours, according to TradingView. The token ranked No. 34 on the platform with a market cap of $2.18 billion.

The relative strength index (RSI) on the chart fell below 30 on Nov. 14, which is usually considered an oversold reading. The indicator stayed under that level until this week and reached 35 on Friday.

On Nov. 20, TradingView's RSI divergence tool marked a "Bull" signal on the chart. A day later, SHIB fell as low as $0.00000337, and its market cap briefly dropped below $2 billion.

SHIB's market cap fell below both the $2.35 billion and the $2.67 billion moving averages in October, according to the chart. The third moving average on the chart stood at $2.89 billion on Friday.

On Nov. 17, the number of SHIB holders on Ethereum passed 1.7 million, according to Etherscan. In mid-November, CryptoQuant data showed that around 312 billion SHIB left crypto exchanges.

SHIB lost 17.6% in December 2025, according to CryptoRank. The token also lost 29.5% and 13.5% in December of 2021 and 2022, respectively, and fell 21% in December 2024.

SHIB gained value in December once, in 2023, when it rose 24.6%. SHIB's median December return is -17.6%, and its average December return is -11.4%, according to CryptoRank data.

In 2021, the December drop came right after SHIB's record high of $0.00008845 on Oct. 28. On Friday, the token traded more than 95% below that level.

SHIB has lost 20.6% in November so far, according to CryptoRank. The token also lost 24.8% and 16.2% in November of 2022 and 2025, respectively. In November of 2023 and 2024, SHIB gained 6.6% and 48.8%, respectively.

SHIB's median November return is -18.4%, and its average November return is -6%, according to CryptoRank data. In 2026, SHIB has lost value in six of 11 months so far.

SHIB's monthly candle for November will close on Monday, Nov. 30.
