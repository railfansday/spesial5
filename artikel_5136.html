Hong Kong’s SWIFT Push Puts XRP Back In Focus

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Home » Ripple (XRP) » Hong Kong’s SWIFT Push Puts XRP Back In Focus

Programmable blockchain payments are coming: Swift is plugging its ledger into Hong Kong’s Project Ensemble.

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority announced on 16 November, 2026 a strengthened partnership with Swift to explore how tokenised deposits can improve the speed and end-to-end experience of cross-border transactions.

The work builds on collaboration that began in 2023 through the Project Ensemble Sandbox, where the two organisations tested interoperability between central bank digital currency systems.

The latest phase brings in major Hong Kong banks, including Bank of China (Hong Kong), Standard Chartered, and HSBC, to examine how the Swift ledger can connect with Project Ensemble’s settlement layer.

The goal is to develop mechanisms that use multi-currency tokenised deposits for cross-border payments, using Swift’s global network to link international and local systems more tightly.

HKMA Deputy Chief Executive Howard Lee described it as another step toward supporting real-time, always-on, and programmable cross-border transactions.

Swift’s Chief Business Officer Thierry Chilosi noted that as domestic systems evolve to handle new forms of digital value, the organisation is exploring how its ledger can connect these platforms across borders so institutions can settle more efficiently.

This is not Hong Kong’s first experiment with tokenised assets and digital money infrastructure. In 2023, Ripple and Fubon Bank participated in the HKMA’s inaugural e-HKD Pilot Programme.

Their use case combined a hypothetical e-HKD with tokenised real estate (property liens) and lending protocols to enable faster access to home equity.

The solution ran on a private ledger built with the same technology as the XRP Ledger, with Ripple’s CBDC platform handling the digital currency layer.

That earlier pilot focused on domestic retail and asset-tokenisation scenarios. The new HKMA x Swift x XRP work shifts the emphasis toward wholesale interbank settlement and multi-currency cross-border flows using tokenised deposits.

Both efforts share the same underlying requirement: different systems, whether CBDC platforms, tokenized deposit ledgers, or traditional messaging networks, must be able to interoperate without forcing every participant on one rail.

Public blockchains such as the XRP Ledger have long been positioned by their proponents as neutral settlement layers that can sit between otherwise incompatible systems.

XRP itself is frequently discussed as a potential bridge asset for currency conversions in corridors where no single national digital currency is suitable as an intermediary.

Ripple’s earlier work with the HKMA already demonstrated that XRPL-derived technology could support tokenized asset and CBDC-related use cases inside a regulated pilot.

The current Project Ensemble-Swift collaboration does not name any public blockchain or bridge asset. The programme focuses on connecting existing institutional infrastructure with emerging tokenised-deposit systems.
