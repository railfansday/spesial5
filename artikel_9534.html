Cornbread Cookies With Salted Honey Buttercream

Soft and chewy inside with an unexpected cornmeal crunch outside, these cornbread cookies are a little something different, in the very best way. These cookies combine the texture of a chewy drop-style cookie with the flavors of cornbread and honey butter, and you won’t believe how delicious they are until you try them! Finished with a drizzle of honey and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt, these frosted cornmeal cookies are impossible to resist.
