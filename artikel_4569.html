Which pollen is found in honey? Automated analysis closely matches conventional testing

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Pure rapeseed honey—or not quite? Single-flower honeys must contain a certain proportion of pollen from the respective plant. This is assessed by pollen analysis, which is time-consuming and requires specialist expertise. As part of a collaborative project, a research team from the Helmholtz Centre for Environmental Research (UFZ) and the German Centre for Integrative Biodiversity Research (iDiv) in Leipzig worked with several partners to develop an automated, AI-based pollen analysis method for honey testing.
