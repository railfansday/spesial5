Former CFO Sues NAR for Withholding Retirement Payments

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The National Association of Realtors® (NAR) may spend the holidays in court, as the association is being accused of failing to pay a former employee promised retirement based on the financial strain of previous legal issues.

John Pierpoint—NAR’s former and long-time chief financial officer, who was succeeded by Matthew Cendella in 2025—filed a lawsuit against NAR in the Circuit Court of Cook County in Chicago on Wednesday, Nov. 11. He claims that NAR has refused to pay him a retirement benefit that the association regularly provides to retiring senior executives.

According to a release from Pierpoint’s attorneys, NAR “improperly withheld one year of salary in retirement compensation despite having recorded the anticipated payment in its own financial records for more than a decade.”

The release also points out that NAR has maintained the executive retirement compensation practice for approximately two decades, and pays retiring senior executives a year’s worth of their base salary.

In 2024, Pierpoint received over $872,000 for his role as CFO, according to NAR’s 2024 990 form.

While the Burnett settlement burdened NAR with a $418 million payment, Pierpoint’s attorneys claim that retirement compensation is held in reserve, specifically set aside for those payments.

“Pierpoint helped guide NAR through an extraordinarily challenging period in its history, including the financial consequences of the antitrust litigation,” David Fish, an attorney representing Pierpoint, stated. “After nearly four decades of service, he expected the organization to honor the same retirement compensation practice it had followed for other senior executives. We believe the evidence will establish that the financial pressures following the antitrust verdict did not relieve NAR of its obligation to pay compensation earned by its employees.”

NAR had not yet commented on the lawsuit at press time.
