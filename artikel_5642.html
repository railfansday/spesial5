Alix Earle Opens the Doors to Family’s $4.1 Million New Jersey Mansion in Season 2 of Drama-Filled Netflix Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Social media sensation Alix Earle is preparing to once again welcome fans into her family's sprawling New Jersey mansion, this time in the second season of her hit Netflix series "Earle Meets World."

Earle, 25, initially rose to stardom on TikTok, where she amassed a legion of followers with her candid "get ready with me videos," which were filmed inside her student housing at the University of Miami.

After graduating, her career continued to go from strength to strength—while the bubbly blonde expanded her work from short-form video to podcasts, drinks brands, and numerous ad campaigns.

Then, she took her success to "next phase" by partnering with Netflix to showcase all of the most "vulnerable," "hard," and "fun" moments in her life, many of which she experiences with her family by her side in the series, which hit the streaming giant on Sept. 4.

Now, the internet star has revealed that fans will get an even closer into her life away from the camera's during the second season of the show, which Earle announced in a joint Instagram video with Netflix.

In the clip, Earle can be seen strutting into the Netflix office while on the phone with her dad.

"Okay dad, when I said, 'What's the worst that could happen?' I didn't think it would be that bad. I know they aren't talking. I'm telling you, we have to just show it all, it will be fine," she said on the call before dumping a box of football-themed items onto the floor.

"Earle Meets World is coming back for season two, I'm just making myself at home," she added while lounging in the office.

In a press release shared by Netflix, Earle revealed, “Since Season 1 aired, some s— has hit the fan. But hey, that makes for a good Season 2. I couldn't be more excited to be back and get the cameras rolling. Lots to catch up on and share with our Netflix family!”

Meanwhile, Vice President of nonfiction series, Brandon Riegg, added, “Our goal with Earle Meets World was authenticity and going deeper than social media ever could. We’re thrilled that viewers connected with that and grateful to Alix for trusting us to tell her story. We’re excited to welcome her and her family back for Season 2!”

The influencer is once again handing over control of the cameras to Netflix producers, who will continue to focus their lenses on Earle and her family, including her TikTok star sister Ashtin, her mom, Alisa, her father, Thomas "TJ," her stepmother, Ashley Dupré, and her younger half-siblings, Izabel, Penelope, and Thomas.

Those who follow Earle's content will no doubt already be familiar with many of these characters, who regularly feature in the TikTok star's videos, particularly when she returns home to Red Bank, NJ, to spend time with her loved ones.

While the online starlet has continued to reside in Miami since graduating, while also taking on a property in Los Angeles, where she regularly travels for work, her family is largely located in and around New Jersey, where TJ and Ashley live in a sprawling mansion that they share with their three youngest children.

Records show that the couple, who wed in 2013, purchased the enormous property in 2013 for $2.25 million, scoring quite the deal on the home, which had previously been listed for $3.35 million. It is currently estimated to be worth $4.1 million, according to Realtor.com®.

However, the family appears to have made numerous upgrades to the dwelling in the six years since they bought it, with Earle's videos showcasing a much more modern and elegant interior than featured in the original listing images.

Meanwhile, records show that Earle's mother, Alisa, resides in a newly-built dwelling in nearby Point Pleasant.

When they are in New Jersey, the TikTok star and her sister, Ashtin, who resides in New York City, split their time between the two dwellings and it is understood that both parents will feature heavily in the Netflix series.

Earle announced the news of the first season of the show via a joint Instagram post with Netflix, in which she was filmed on the phone with her dad—who serves as her manager—outside of the Netflix offices.

In the clip, she tells her dad that things will be "fine," adding, "Dad, it's gonna be fun! It's gonna be fine. What's the worst that can happen?"

The video was captioned, "From your FYP to your TV: Alix Earle, her family, and friends will star in an unscripted series dropping later this year on Netflix."

In a press release shared by the streaming giant, the TikTok star confessed that partnering with Netflix felt "unreal," adding that she was excited to show fans her life behind the scenes.

"It honestly feels unreal to be partnering with Netflix, and I’m so excited to be creating this show with them," she shared. "I’ve shared my life for years through my own camera, but this felt like the next phase because it’s a chance to go deeper.

"Not just the highlights, but the real moments, too. The vulnerable ones, the hard ones, and the fun, chaotic ones in between. It’s the same authenticity people know me for, but now with a different lens."

The social media mogul revealed that she was most "excited" for her fans to see her "fun" and "modern family."

"People either think we’re insane or they’re like, wait, this is exactly like my life. And honestly, both are true. I’m excited for fans to experience the full picture as it happens. The chaos, the emotional moments, the stressful parts, and all the little things in between that you don’t always see online," she added.

She admitted that the process was a "little scary," adding, "I’m so used to being in control of what I share and what I keep private, so having cameras around in moments I normally wouldn’t post is definitely an adjustment.

"But the real, human moments are what connected me to my audience in the first place. If letting people in even more makes someone feel less alone, or even just makes them laugh, then it’s worth it."

Although she is hesitant to give up power over he content, Earle noted that she finds comfort in the relationship she has with her followers.

"My relationship with my followers has always felt really personal because I’ve been sharing my life the same way I would with my best friends. With this show, I want it to feel like you’re even more in it with me, in a way that’s real and not curated.

"The goal is not to be someone different on camera. It’s to show more of who I already am, and who my family really is. The fun, the messy, the growth, while capturing the way we help each other through it all," Earle added.

The news of her family's reality TV show comes a mere month after her and Houston Texans player Braxton Berrios called it quits after two years of dating.

Earle and Berrios, 30, began dating in February 2023 but kept their romance coy on social media, with the TikTok tycoon referring to her now-ex beau simply as "NFL man," when she started discussing their relationship with her followers.

They then took things to the next level in April, when Earle revealed to her followers that she had moved out of her rented Florida condo and into her boyfriend's $2.7 million Miami home, just as former Miami Dolphins star Berrios relocated to Texas, where he now plays for the Houston Texans.

Meanwhile, Earle moved to Los Angeles temporarily earlier this year, when she landed a role on "Dancing With the Stars," ultimately finishing in second place alongside professional dance partner, Val Chmerkovskiy.

Throughout her time on the competition, Berrios failed to make a single in-person appearance in the ballroom—however, Earle insisted to fans that his absence was the result of his intense football schedule.

In December, Earle admitted to fans that they had indeed split, insisting that there was no bad blood between them.
