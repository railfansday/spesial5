Starting the 100-mile wilderness (Day 162)

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

After having an amazing birthday yesterday, it’s time to start hiking again. We’re starting the 100-mile wilderness today. Poet from Shaw’s hiker hostel shuttled us to the start, reminding us that we haven’t hiked hundreds of miles, but thousands of miles by now. He sent us off with the following haiku:

“Drink your deepest now, For the richest stuff settles In the final swig”

This poem really put us in the right mindset for the last part of our journey. After thanking Poet for everything, we went into the woods for a final time.

The 100-mile immediately greeted us with beautiful ponds, inviting us to sit, write and reflect. The flatter hiking is a treat too, but it’s also quite treacherous. The rocks are slippery and it’s very rooty. Both me and my dad slipped on one of these rocks. I was fine, but my dad fell and folded his knee, making it hurt pretty bad. He’s still going though. We can’t let our focus slip too much here, accidents can still happen.

We reached the shelter, had dinner and went to bed at 7:30. The daylight is becoming more rare by the day and we’re still very much programmed to sleep when the sun sets.
