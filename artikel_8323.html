Gear Check – Appalachian Trail 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Now a days everyone has there own way of carrying gear.  Frameless pack, internal frame, or external frame.  I do not see many external frames these days.  You do see some hikers packing a 42 liter external frame, not this guy anymore.  I switched to a frameless pack about three years and never looked back.  Pack weight seems to be a great conversation starter around the shelters, but I always say, “Hike and be comfortable”.  I try to stick to the 20% rule.  Which is carry 20% of your body weight.  Might not work for everyone, but works for me and my feet are not blistered up at the end of the day.

JRB Big Meadows 40:  13 oz &  REI Helix:  1lb 5oz

Sleeping Quilt & Air Pad Nemo Pillow and BA Foot Booties

I try to keep this section really simple.  Hot meals and coffee in the morning.  I will have another post about Town Food later.  I never cook in my pot for easy or no clean up, so I’m only boiling water.  In 1997 cooking was messing and sometimes dirty.  Between resupplies one Backpacker meal, ramen creations, stovetop stuffing, instant everything, and grits or oatmeal.

I’m starting in April so planning on some cooler mornings at first.  I do have the advantage of being home at mile 110, so I can drop some items off.

Two Patagonia Capilene shirts:  4.5 oz each

Patagonia Capilene base layer bottoms:  3.5 oz

Patagonia Capilene base layer top: 3.3 oz

I have to add some weight to my pack.  Having morning coffee is one of my favorite times.  Either in my tent on a rainy morning or watching the sunrise on a mountain peak.  A camp chair is a must and makes relaxing in the tent a plus.

Total Pack weight or base weight:  14 lbs.
