The Top 10 Most Authentic Ski Resorts in the West (2027)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A great ski resort has a personality all its own, whether it’s anchored by laid-back mountain town where locals welcome you like an old friend or home to a quirky base village with deep ski-culture roots. Whatever gives a place its character, that local flavor can be just as memorable as the skiing itself.

The 10 resorts on this list stand out for feeling like themselves—from the people and the atmosphere to the character of their base areas. Voted the most authentic by SKI readers in our annual Reader Resort Survey, these are the places where the mountain experience goes beyond the runs and reflects the people who make it special.
