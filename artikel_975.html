Littler hits triple-bullseye finish on way to last 16 despite first Grand Slam defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Luke Littler took out 150 with three bullseyes in the eighth leg of his match; Despite the showstopping moment, he lost to Niels Zonneveld; watch the Grand Slam of Darts in Wolverhampton live on Sky Sports with the final taking place on Sunday November 22

Luke Littler was beaten at the Grand Slam of Darts for the first time ever by Niels Zonneveld but still progressed to the second round as he produced a sensational triple bullseye finish.

The reigning champion needed to win just three legs to make it through from a tough Group A and after going 1-0 down, he rattled off three in a row to ensure his title defence continues.

The highlight of the match came in the eighth leg as Zonneveld was waiting to come for a second match dart on 20 and Littler was on 150.

Not only did Littler take it out but he did it in the most sublime style with three bullseyes, asking his family in the crowd beforehand if he 'should do it'. With the answer of yes, he nailed all three and sent the Wolverhampton crowd wild.

Although Littler had come up with one of the greatest finishes you can see, Zonneveld held his nerve to take the match and take a piece of history home as the first player ever to beat Littler at this major. He was eliminated alongside Beau Greaves in Group A.

Asked whether he thought Littler going that route with the match still on the line was disrespectful, Zonneveld said: "I don't think it was disrespectful because I was 4-3 up, he was already through so could go for the three bullseyes.

"It was brilliant, of course. But for me it was really tough because of the group, but I don't see it as disrespect."

In the last 16, 'The Nuke' will take on Jermaine Wattimena. Wattimena won a straight shootout with Alan Soutar and progressed from Group B off the back of eight missed match darts from the Scotsman, finally taking the contest and a place in the last 16 on D8.

The other big moment of the night came in Group E in which fourth seed Gerwyn Price was clinical in a 5-2 victory over Sebastian Bialecki but the win came with some controversy.

In the fourth leg, it seemed like Price took out 135 for a hold of throw, but he had in fact taken out 145 due to a miscalculation from referee Charlie Corstophine.

Price went the route of T19, T16, and D20 and after his first dart at T19, he asked the referee what was remaining and was told 88.

However, he in fact needed 78 which led to the error of him being awarded the leg despite busting his throw.

Next up, he faces Ryan Searle who rattled off five legs in a row for a 5-2 victory over Andrew Gilding in the straight shootout in Group F.

Cameron Menzies is through from Group D and eliminated Stephen Bunting in the process with a 5-2 victory in the penultimate Monday match.

His 103.39 average was too much to handle and in a composed showing in a pressurised straight shootout, he clinched a brilliant 144 finish and then took the contest with a 135 checkout on the bull.

In the last 16, he faces Kevin Doets who blew Tommy Morris away with a scintillating 5-1 victory to take top spot in Group C and finished the match in style with a 121 checkout on the bullseye.

Eighth seed Josh Rock had already been eliminated after two defeats and despite Morris impressing earlier in the tournament, he could not find his rhythm and Doets punished to make it into the next round on his Grand Slam debut.

Fifth-seed Jonny Clayton was already out of the running in Group G and Rob Cross took full advantage and progressed with a 5-1 victory over Anton Ostlund.

With the doubles troubling Ostlund, Cross made sure to make his opportunities count and took out five of the six legs played for a clinical win.

Cross now faces a mouthwatering clash against Niko Springer who came out on top against Wessel Nijman in what was a tussle between two young darting prospects in Group H.
