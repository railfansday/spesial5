It’s okay that the Daiyan Henley trade fell through

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

I was excited as the next fan about the possibility of the Denver Broncos adding a solid coverage inside linebacker like Daiyan Henley. However, late last night when the news broke that the deal fell through, I was find with it. I trust the Broncos' medical staff far more than I would ever trust the Los Angeles Chargers staff — you know, the one whose team doctor accidentally punctured Tyrod Taylor's lung that one year.

Not to be a jerk about it, but hey, when it comes to track record the Broncos' medical team has a very good one. If they raised concerns and the front office decided it needed to re-negotiate the terms of the deal… then so be it.

What is done is done. Maybe we see Henley again in free agency.
