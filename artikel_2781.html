Jensen Huang to appear before House panels Dec. 1; Nvidia lawyer slated to testify Dec. 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang will appear before the House Foreign Affairs, Intelligence and China Select Committees on Dec. 1, the leaders of those congressional panels said on Tuesday.

Nvidia general counsel Tim Teter will also appear before the committees on Dec. 2, a statement from the lawmakers said.

"We look forward to hearing from Jensen Huang and Tim Teter on their roles in a potential breach of our national security," said Reps. Brian Mast, R-Fla., John Moolenaar, R-Mich., and Rick Crawford, R-Ark., the chairs of the Foreign Affairs, China Select and Intelligence Committees.

"The American people need answers on Mr. Huang and Nvidia's role in allowing dangerous PRC actors to access our most advanced chips, as well as the role of a potential Chinese intelligence asset in this scandal," they said.
