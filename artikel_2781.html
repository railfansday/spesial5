Former Miami Star Carson Beck Gets Major NFL News With Arizona Cardinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Former Miami Hurricanes quarterback Carson Beck has received the biggest opportunity of his young NFL career.

The Arizona Cardinals have named Beck their starting quarterback for Sunday's game against the Los Angeles Rams, setting up the rookie's first regular-season appearance. ESPN's Adam Schefter reported the development, which the Cardinals also confirmed in a team announcement on November 10.

Beck, Arizona's third-round pick in the 2026 NFL Draft, will take over after veteran Gardner Minshew suffered a season-ending arm injury during the Cardinals' 17-3 loss to the Seattle Seahawks.

For Beck, who guided Miami to the College Football Playoff National Championship Game last season, the promotion represents a major step forward after spending the first nine weeks of his rookie campaign on the sideline.

The Cardinals announced Tuesday that Minshew had been placed on injured reserve following an injury to his throwing arm against Seattle.

The veteran was struck by Seahawks linebacker Dante Fowler Jr. while attempting a pass late in the first half. Although Minshew returned for the second half, the injury ultimately ended his season.

Arizona's quarterback situation has changed dramatically in less than two weeks.

Minshew replaced Jacoby Brissett during the team's comeback victory over the Dallas Cowboys on November 1 and was subsequently named the starter. Beck was promoted to the backup role, while Brissett was eventually traded to the Baltimore Ravens.

Cardinals head coach Mike LaFleur indicated Monday that the transition should not require a significant overhaul of the offense.

"Foundationally we do similar stuff," LaFleur said, according to the Cardinals' official website. "A lot of the stuff Carson likes and is good at is the stuff Gardner likes. They like this system."

Arizona also added veteran Josh Johnson and rookie Joey Aguilar to provide depth behind Beck.

The opportunity comes with considerable challenges. Cardinals receiver Michael Wilson was also placed on injured reserve with a neck injury, leaving Beck without one of Arizona's leading receiving threats.
