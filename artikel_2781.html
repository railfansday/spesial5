WTC 2027 Final: Qualification scenarios, chances and probabilities for World Test Championship final

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Oval will host the 2027 WTC final from June 9 to June 13.

South Africa lead the table at 75%, with New Zealand and Australia chasing hard.

India sit fourth and need near-perfect results from their seven remaining Tests.

With less than a year left before the next World Test Championship final, nine teams are still in the race for two places at The Oval, and the table is starting to separate the contenders from the rest. Australia hold the top spot, South Africa have found form again after their win in Gqeberha, and India are now fighting an uphill battle.

The Oval has been confirmed as the venue, and the match will be played from Wednesday, June 9 to Sunday, June 13. The ICC announced last year that England had won the hosting rights for the next three finals, in 2027, 2029 and 2031, and the England and Wales Cricket Board picks the ground for each edition.

This will be the second time The Oval stages the showpiece, after Australia beat India there in 2023 to lift the mace.

The first three editions have each produced a different champion. New Zealand won the inaugural title in 2021, Australia took the mace in 2023, and South Africa ended their long wait for a major ICC trophy in 2025.

Points are awarded on a simple scale, with 12 for a win, six for a tie, four for a draw and none for a loss, and slow over-rate penalties can reduce a team's total.

After their latest victory in Cape Town, South Africa is on top, with a 80% chance of qualifying, which is up 18 points.

New Zealand follow at 75% and Australia are now at 60%. India and Bangladesh each stand at 3%, Sri Lanka at 0.2% and England at 0.1%, while West Indies and Pakistan are out of the race.

The "wins needed" column shows how many victories each side requires to reach the likely cutoff by the end of the league stage.

Australia are the top contender, with a points percentage of 75.00. Their remaining fixtures include a four-match Test series against New Zealand, followed by the five-Test Border-Gavaskar Trophy in India in early 2027.

They need roughly three wins from their remaining 9 Tests to cross the 65% mark, and even a split of results across Cape Town and India should keep Pat Cummins' side in the top two.

South Africa stand on 75.00%, with their 2-1 series win over Australia. If Temba Bavuma's team dominate their series against Bangladesh and Sri Lanka, they could finish above 68%, which would confirm their place in the final.

New Zealand are on 66.67%, and they have played seven matches.

After drawing the first Test against India at home, the Black Caps will hope to win the second match and clinch the series before a tough away fixture against Australia.

India are fourth with 50.00% after a draw in Wellington in the first Test against New Zealand. They have six Tests left in the current cycle, and they must win all of those matches to stand a solid chance of qualifying for the final

They cannot afford any more draws or losses if they want to remain in the race for a spot in the final

A clean sweep or a 4-1 win over Australia at home, along with a series win over New Zealand, remains the ideal route to The Oval.
