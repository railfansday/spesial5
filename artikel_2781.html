Texans activate CB Jaylin Smith

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Texans announced a handful of roster moves ahead of Thursday night’s game against the Colts.

Cornerback Jaylin Smith has been activated from injured reserve. Smith has been out since Week 4 with a hamstring injury and he had two tackles in the first four weeks of the season while seeing most of his playing time on special teams.

Smith was listed as questionable on the team’s final injury report. Linebacker Jake Hummel (groin) and guard Wyatt Teller (back) were also listed as questionable.

The Texans also elevated linebacker Chad Muma and defensive end Chris Braswell from the practice squad on a temporary basis. Muma has now been elevated for three straight games while Braswell has been called up for the first time this season.
