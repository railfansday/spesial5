Kirk Cousins and Fernando Mendoza are keeping in touch

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Las Vegas Raiders moved to Fernando Mendoza last week after nine weeks with Kirk Cousins. Cousins was brought in to help mentor Mendoza and help him grow as a rookie quarterback.

The bond became more than a mentorship and a true friendship. It helped Mendoza become ready to play faster than the Raiders' coaching staff expected. It led to Cousins being shipped to the Minnesota Vikings for a third-round pick.

After the trade, the two players are keeping in touch. On Wednesday, Mendoza said he texted Cousins after the game for advice. During his press conference, Cousins shared more about the conversation.

"Just wanted to debrief with him, get his thoughts. I'm a big fan of his, and me and my boys will be following him for a long time. When I'm long done playing, we'll be cheering for Fernando and for his team. So just wanted to get his thoughts. I remember my first career start and that's one that kind of always stick with you, but I think he did some great things and he's got a bright future ahead."

It is good for Mendoza to have this consistent mentorship and bond with a veteran player. It will give him something to lean on and help him improve every week.
