We just saw why the San Francisco 49ers are the best team in the NFL

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Yes, you read that correctly. In fact, saying the San Francisco 49ers are the best team in the NFL isn't a hot take or "clickbait." Every single thing you toss at this team bounces off them, and they continue to pile up wins. Sunday night was the biggest statement to the NFL and media members waiting to pounce on the 49ers and Brock Purdy.

Let's start with the obvious. Brock Purdy is playing like an MVP. I'd argue top two, and he isn't number two. Fine, you want to throw Joe Burrow into this? Very much deserved, but I wonder if people will ding him for having the best receiver combo in the NFL, as they do with Purdy and his "Avengers." I love hypocrisy, so this argument is the weakest.

So Brian Flores? Well, the 17-point curse is over. It was over at halftime. Once the 49ers passed the opening script, you wondered if the counter punches would come from Flores. They didn't, and Kyle Shanahan was dialed in. Purdy had the football out in a blink, and it always seemed to be in the right spot.

Sunday night was a glimpse into what the 49ers offense looks like with all the available weapons. George Kittle had two touchdowns, Mike Evans and Christian McCaffrey had one each, and it felt like the 49ers could do whatever they wanted in Mexico City. An offensive performance like that will be impossible to stop for any defense. Yes, Seattle, you're next.

On the defensive side, the line was after Kirk Cousins from the initial snap. The secondary made plays and took the football away. The 49ers just looked like far and away the better football team. The expectations of "game of the year" came crashing down as the 49ers dismantled the league's best defense on national television.

This isn't recency bias to say this is the best 49ers offense under Kyle Shanahan. If De'Zhaun Stribling even steps on the field again this season, I would shudder to think about how good this offense could be. As long as Shanahan and Purdy perform like this, nobody can stop them.

So, yes, the 49ers are the best team in the NFL. Debating otherwise is foolish. There's only one thing left to do.

"Win the whole F'ing thing." Sorry, I couldn't resist. Major League is my favorite baseball movie of all time.
