What Deion Sanders said following Colorado's loss at Arizona State

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The offseason will bring a lot of questions, especially after another loss by the Colorado Buffaloes to a Big 12 team in their 20-10 loss to Arizona State.

It is a shame the team's defensive talent is being wasted on an offense that cannot do much. It took until the second half for the Buffaloes to score. Their touchdown came only after Arizona State got a 15-yard penalty for roughing the kicker, which kept the drive alive when Colorado otherwise would have missed a field goal. They had a chance to make this a game, but the offense is not functional.

Penalties were also a problem, despite only five. Colorado was called for an ineligible man downfield that negated a 74-yard touchdown pass from Isaac Wilson to Ernest Campbell on their second drive. It was an instant momentum killer, and Arizona State scored their first touchdown two drives later.

When Sanders spoke to the media after the game, he did not mince words about his disappointment with the offense, their need for better play in the secondary, and his frustration with another non-winning season for the Buffaloes.

Here is everything Deion Sanders said after Colorado's fifth conference loss of the season.

Deion Sanders: "I didn't see quit, I didn't see lethargic. I didn't see any giving up by any means … This is not how it is supposed to be … If anybody's in that locker room that's not hurting right now, this ain't a sport for you. This is the wrong sport. It's the wrong game. It's the wrong team. Wrong everything. This is not how it's supposed to be."

Sanders: "It's almost like, 'I don't want to stop the clock and give them an opportunity.' We don't want to give the opposing team an opportunity to score. You got to think about the other side too. So, that's a different thought process."

Sanders: "We got to score. That's it. We just got to score. I don't think it's a destination type of thing. On the road, at home … We're not scoring points … You don't score points, you're not going to win in any profession, any game, period. That's the problem. We're not scoring, and we need to stop people as well. We got to do a better job of stopping people. That's on me."

Sanders: "Sometimes one complements the other and unless the passing game is going, sometimes the running game is not, and it's vice versa … We couldn't get anything going really offensively."

Sanders: "The season don't look good. It don't look good. I'm not crazy, man. I can see, I can see clearly. You think I'm happy? I'm not happy with outcomes. I'm not a loser by any means and I hate the fact of that we're coming up on the short end of these games, tremendously."

Sanders: "I hurt because you guys deserve better and the team deserves better. We should win. We should win. We should put our best foot forward, and I'm not seeing that."

Sanders: "There's no one in that locker room that is giving up and is not fighting, coaches included and support staff included."

Sanders: "I want these kids to graduate and provoke changes in their lives and families. I am not worried about me."
