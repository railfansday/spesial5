The first look at Latveria in Avengers: Doomsday is here over a month early – as part of a Jeep commercial.

As spotlighted by New Rockstars' Erik Voss on Twitter, the latest round of Doomsday marketing features Captain America, Johnny Storm, and The Thing each taking a Jeep through a portal in a handful of familiar locations – including Avengers Tower and Wakanda.

The Avengers Doomsday tie-in commercials keep showing us interesting world details from the film. Here's our first-ever look at LATVERIA in the MCU! Doctor Doom's towering Doomstadt castle towers over the city in the mountains of Eastern Europe. Hey, thanks Jeep! https://t.co/DQHZFZEihNNovember 5, 2026

Their final destination is the real talking point, however. There, the Jeeps converge into the snowy climes of Latveria, a mountainous nation-state that appears to show Doom's castle and the capital Doomstadt.

In the comics, Castle Doom is frequently depicted as Doctor Doom's base of operations, upon which he rules over the fictional nation of Latveria. But much like most of Doomsday, Doom's HQ has been under wraps.

In fact, Doomsday marketing has been a most unlikely source of spoilers. Already, we have had a Dunkin' Donuts commercial reveal Gambit's new accent and dive deeper into the universe-altering influx of incursions.

A Funko Pop has also confirmed that Doom will spend at least some part of the movie with Reed Richards and Sue Storm's son, Franklin – a likely follow-up to the Fantastic Four post-credits scene which saw the Marvel supervillain meet the gifted, super-powered child.

What next, a T-shirt that spoils a surprise cameo? A burger that ruins the ending? At this rate, we wouldn't be surprised. Keep your eyes glued to screens to see what other Avengers: Doomsday promotions might have in store for us next.

Avengers: Doomsday hits cinemas on December 16 in the UK and December 18 in the US.
