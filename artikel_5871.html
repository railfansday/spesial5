US pilot tells Trump which fighter jet is ‘world’s greatest’ during Thanksgiving phone call

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

President Donald Trump, during a Thanksgiving call with Air Force Lieutenant Colonel Brant Smith, asked which fighter jet was the best. Smith told him it was the F-16. Trump made calls to service members of all six U.S. military branches.

President Donald Trump learned what the "world's greatest" fighter jet is, according to an F-16 pilot, during a phone call on Thanksgiving Day.

"So could I ask, before I give my little speech to you — a little quick one — could I ask, so the F-16, you love it, right?" Trump asked U.S. Air Force Lt. Col. Brant Smith.

"World's greatest fighter jet, sir," Smith replied.

Trump spoke to Smith from his Mar-a-Lago resort in Florida as the president and first lady Melania made calls to service members from all six branches of the U.S. military.

Trump asked Smith to compare his F-16 to other fighter jets like the F-22 or F-35, to which Smith replied: "The F-16 all day."

"You couldn't pay me to switch airplanes," Smith added.

"I love flying this airplane. And I think there are very few pilots in this room that would argue with me. Both of those airplanes (F-22 and F-35) are great, and they have their place in the world and in the war. But, this is a lot of fun. We get to do it all, and it does it all very well," Smith said.

The president also asked Smith about his service and queried about what he was doing at the time.

"We are pre-installing the air base in Saudi Arabia," Smith replied.

"You have a pretty large crowd of pilots, maintainers and ops support personnel from the 510th Expeditionary Fighter Squadron, the Buzzards. We're an F-16 unit. We've been on here since about ten days before Epic Fury kicked off. And we're flying combat operations out in the straits every day," he continued.

Trump complimented Smith on the military's efforts in the Middle East and in the war with Iran.

"And you're seeing some big victories, aren't you? Knocking them out left and right, knocking out the launchers, knocking out the missiles, knocking out left and right. Amazing job you're doing, Brant," Trump said.

Smith also told the president he believes Iran was "starting to run out of things." Trump concurred.

"Yep. I think they are. I think they are. And that'll continue. And we're getting stronger. We sent an extra aircraft carrier," Trump said, referring to the Nimitz-class USS Theodore Roosevelt, which arrived in the Middle East on Nov. 16 to relieve USS George Washington.

"In my opinion, they're not very smart. They're making big mistakes. They've made a lot of mistakes. Their economy is down the tubes," the president said of Iran.

"Every day the stellar F-16 squadron is conducting missions across the Middle East to defend our interests and our assets. Like few people could ever even think about. This year, you've flown more than 2,000 sorties over more than 12,000 hours across eight different operations, including Operation Epic Fury. And you're getting plenty of time out there, aren't you, Brant?"

"Yes, sir," Smith said, adding, "Nine months and 10 days."

"Yeah, we're having fun," Smith said. "This is what we trained for."

"Amazing. The best in the world," Trump replied.
