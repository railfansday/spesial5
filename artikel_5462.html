U.S. mortgage rates fall to 7.21%, lifting home-purchase applications

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The Mortgage Bankers Association's purchase index rose 7.5% for the week ended Nov. 6, while its refinance index gained 4.5%

In the week ended Nov. 6, the Mortgage Bankers Association said, the contract rate for a 30-year fixed loan dropped 9 basis points to 7.21%, according to Bloomberg. The decline was the fourth in a row, and the data came out Wednesday.

The group's index of home-purchase applications rose 7.5%, the largest weekly gain since mid-April, according to Bloomberg. It was the second time since the end of August that the weekly figure climbed.

The refinance index rose 4.5%, its third straight weekly gain, according to Bloomberg. The measure remains close to the weakest reading recorded since January 2025.

The latest reading follows a decline the week before. The 30-year rate had eased to 7.30% from 7.37% in the week ended Oct. 30, when purchase applications slid 2% from the prior week. That reading was the third straight weekly drop in the rate.

The rate had reached 7.50% in the week ended Oct. 9, the highest level since November 2023. Rates climbed back above 7% in mid-September, and the housing market has weakened since.

The National Association of Realtors said last month that pending home sales fell 7.3% in September from August. The index slid to 66.0, a record low for a data series that dates to 2001.

"Market conditions weakened in September due to fast-rising mortgage rates," NAR Chief Economist Lawrence Yun said in a statement. "Buyers and sellers may simply be holding back until mortgage rates move favorably," Yun said.
