Collibra: Manager, Professional Services Federal

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

At Collibra Public Sector (CPS), the Manager, Federal Professional Services is responsible for leading all services roles and functions to ensure the long-term success, adoption, and satisfaction of government clients. The role drives a cohesive customer journey, aligns services delivery with other CPS delivery to mission outcomes, and ensures operational excellence across the entire customer lifecycle. This role safeguards account health, revenue, and customer satisfaction by proactively identifying and mitigating technical risk, coordinating services engagements, and building operational frameworks for GTM clarity and alignment amongst the services teams.

Customer Journey Mapping and Operational improvement

Customer Risk Prevention, Assessment & Management

The standard base salary range for this position is $128,000 - 160,000 per year. This position is eligible for additional commission-based compensation. Salary offers are based on a combination of factors, including, but not limited to, experience, skills, and location.

In addition to base salary, we offer a competitive total rewards package, including bonus potential, equity for eligible roles, a Flex Fund monthly stipend, pension/401k plans, and more.
