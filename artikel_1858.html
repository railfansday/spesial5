The FA Cup draw for the second round is coming up live tonight.

Seventh-tier Wimborne Town were the headline-grabbers as they toppled EFL basement club Crawley, while Kidderminster knocked out Doncaster and Carlisle defeated high-flying Salford.

Barrow took down Gillingham, while elsewhere Sheffield Wednesday fell to Oldham, Guiseley beat Sutton, Dover overcame Wealdstone and Exeter eliminated Huddersfield.

Charlie Savage also got the better of dad Robbie as Reading thrashed Peterborough in a one-sided battle of League One teams.

The first round proper got under way on Friday night with 2021 winners Leicester seeing off Oxford City. Elsewhere, 2013 winners Wigan took care of business against Boreham Wood.

The dream is over for AFC Rushden & Diamonds after they were beaten by Burton Albion, while eighth-tier Stafford Rangers went two goals up against Grimsby, only to get knocked out on penalties.

The FA Cup draw for the 2026/27 second round will take place tonight on Monday November 9, 2026, before the clash between non-league clubs Plymouth Parkway and Berkhamsted at Bolitho Park.

Television coverage of the game starts at 6:30pm GMT, with the draw itself expected to begin shortly afterwards at around 6:45pm. Kick-off in the match is at 7:30pm.

The draw will be hosted by Jules Breach and conducted by Karen Carney and Joel Ward.

TV channel: In the UK, the draw will be televised live on TNT Sports 1.
