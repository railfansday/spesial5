Mauricio Pochettino sends message to Manchester United fans in first interview as manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Mauricio Pochettino has issued a message to Manchester United fans in his first interview as manager.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea manager has replaced the sacked Michael Carrick, and flew in from the United States on Wednesday morning to begin his two-and-a-half-year contract.

Pochettino on Thursday oversaw his first training session as head coach, and has a quick turnaround before his first game in charge - which comes on Sunday at Anfield to face Liverpool.

The Argentine is aware he faces a baptism of fire at the home of a rival this weekend, and hopes to earn his connection with the fans by playing with "fight" and "for the win".

The fans need to see that we are going to fight ... and we are going to play for the win

"I don't want to ask nothing because I think always we demand, demand, demand to the fans, but we need to provide to the fans before we demand," he told the club's official website.

"I think we need to be the next to connect. We need to be the part that we create that energy that connect to the fans, to provide the fans.

"The fans need to see that we are going to fight, that we are Manchester United and we respect our history and we are going to play for the win. And for sure then our fans will be supporting us because what I hear and what I saw in the past, the supporters are amazing."

He continued: “I know very well what that game means and how important it is. The history talks for us and to what we need to expect.

“We need to make sure that we are brave and we are protagonists and go there with no fears and challenge a very good team like Liverpool.”
