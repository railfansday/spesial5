Liverpool vs Manchester United LIVE: Premier League result, latest updates and reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Mauricio Pochettino’s Manchester United reign got off to a miserable start as Liverpool punished Leny Yoro’s red card with a pair of early goals on their way to a comprehensive 4-0 win against their relegation-threatened rivals. A whirlwind week for the former Argentina international ended in all too familiar disappointment for the faltering Red Devils, whose hopes of enjoying a new manager bounce were extinguished within 13 nightmare minutes at Anfield.

Dominik Szoboszlai’s fierce free-kick Liverpool put ahead after United defender Yoro was sent off for denying a goalscoring opportunity and Florian Wirtz’s smart finish quickly put Pochettino’s side further behind. United temporarily stemmed the tide thanks to some last-ditch defending, wayward finishing and key Senne Lammens stops, but Andoni Iraola’s Reds redoubled their efforts in the second half.

In truth, Pochettino’s 10 men were fortunate that Liverpool only added a further two goals though Rio Ngumoha and substitute Victor Munoz, such was their dominance.

There we have it. A nightmare debut for Pochettino.
