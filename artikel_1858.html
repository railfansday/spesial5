Richard Kone injury update after QPR star forced off against Burnley

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Richard Kone is expected to be fit and available to face Lincoln this weekend after suffering an injury scare during QPR’s 1-1 draw with Burnley.

Kone was forced off just before the hour mark at Loftus Road, and walked straight down the tunnel instead of taking his place on the bench alongside the rest of his team-mates.

The 23-year-old striker had been moving awkwardly before he was substituted, prompting injury fears.

Standard Sport, though, understands the Ivory Coast international came through the game unscathed and is set to be available for selection against Lincoln on Saturday.

Kone has been in superb form, scoring four goals in his last three games, since returning from a freak injury he sustained during a development game against Bolton's Under-21s.

He was off the pace as QPR could only take a point at home to struggling Burnley on Tuesday night, but he remains the club’s top goalscorer this season.

QPR head coach Julien Stephan was left frustrated by his side’s performance against Burnley, but said the West Londoners had enough chances to win the game.

"I'm frustrated with the result, not with the performance. I think we started very well and in the first 20-25 minutes we had enough chances to score a minimum one or two times,” Stephan said.

"We conceded probably from the first shot they had during the game. They shot only four times, touched the ball only five times in our box. We had 20 shots and 45 ball touches in the opponents' box.

"We were very dominant, and the minimum in the second half was to score and equalise, but I think we had enough big chances to win today. That's why it's a frustration based on the result, but not a frustration based on the collective performance."
