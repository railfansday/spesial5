Last year's Apple Watch Series 11 is now $100 off at Walmart. Shoppers can snag a GPS-only base model with a jet black aluminum case and a black Sport Band for only $299, or 25% off. If you need one with a cellular connection, it'll cost you $399 (normally $499 — a 20% savings), and you can take your pick from four case and band combinations:

Jet black aluminum case with black Sport Band

Rose gold aluminum case with light blush Sport Band

Silver aluminum case with purple fog Sport Band

Space gray aluminum case with black Sport Band

The Series 11 earned a Mashable Choice Award for its big battery life upgrade. Compared to the Series 10 from 2024, it lasts up to six hours longer for a total of 24 hours. The brand-new Apple Watch Series 12 has the same battery life, though it charges a bit faster.

The Series 11 doesn't have the same upgraded optical and electrical sensors as the Series 12, and its processor is a generation behind. So, it doesn't support consistent heart rate readings or features like Readiness and Siri Recap. But their designs and core feature sets (via watchOS 27) are identical; the Series 11 offers hypertension notifications, a Sleep Score metric, and Workout Buddy support. It remains a good option for folks who aren't interested in the most cutting-edge health tracking tools — and those who are a little sketched out by Audio Intelligence.
