Accsys Technologies PLC (ACSYF) Q2 2027 Earnings Call November 24, 2026 4:00 AM EST

Martijn den Drijver - ABN AMRO Bank N.V., Research Division

Welcome everyone to the Accsys Technologies PLC interim results presentation for the six months ended 30 September 2026. Today's speakers are Dr. Jelena Arsic van Os, Chief Executive Officer of Accsys Technologies, and Sameet Vohra, the Company's Chief Financial Officer. Jelena and Sam will take you through an overview of the business and financial performance for the half year. [Operator Instructions] With this, I would like to hand over to Jelena for the presentation.

Good morning, everyone, and thank you for joining us. In the first half of FY 2027, we were and still are navigating challenging markets. We updated full year expectations in September and we are maintaining that full year guidance. In a tough market, we have focused to protect profitability, actioning on what we can control. Our September update was a difficult message. Since then, the first half has come in where we said it would. Distributor destocking has stabilized, and October trading and the Q3 order book are in line with the board expectations.

Adjusted EBITDA was at EUR 10 million, broadly in line with the strong first half last year, with underlying EBITDA of EUR 10.6 million, only 1% down from last H1, even though group volumes were 5% lower. That came from decisive management action. We maintained our pricing discipline, we cut costs by EUR 1.8 million through tight cost control of overheads and further R&M operational productivity gains. Barry also matched color production to demand because of softer market and color production transitioning to the USA.
