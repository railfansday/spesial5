Why Are Jen and Zac Affleck Getting Divorced? She Just Revealed the Betrayal That Led to Their Split

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Jen Affleck is back in the spotlight thanks to her new Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County spin-off—and her messy divorce from Zac Affleck.

After recently admitting she’s “struggling” with the split, Jen revealed the specific betrayal that became the final straw in their marriage. In true SLOMW fashion, there’s apparently more than meets the eye.

“It seemed like he wasn’t being honest, so I went to check our bank, like, the money is gone,” Jen said in the fourth episode of SLOMW: OC. “There [was] over $100,000 in there.”

Jen, who’s pregnant with her and Zac’s fourth child, further accused Zac of draining funds from their joint bank account to “invest a ton of money in Bitcoin.” He allegedly “lost” the money by gambling in “online poker” and “investing in crypto.”

“He was like, ‘I fucked up. You wanted to separate so I didn’t tell you because I knew if you found out you would divorce me,'” Jen continued. “I said, ‘Yeah, I’m done. I’m divorcing you.'”

Jen’s allegations came to light around the same time Zac released a lengthy statement on his Instagram Stories. The DadTok member took accountability for his “unacceptable” actions and behavior towards Jen—particularly, when he criticized and commented on her body after giving birth to their third child.

Zac also revealed why he didn’t partake in SLOMW: OC, which was filmed “during an especially difficult time for Jen and I.”

In the end, he claimed that he has already privately apologized to Jen, since they talked things out behind-the-scenes.

“Jen and I are constantly working to be on great terms and we share a deep sense of respect and appreciation for one another,” he closed his statement. “My children may see this online someday, and all I want them to know is that their parents love them more than anything, and I am committed to showing up as the best version of myself for our family unit.”

With Jordan Ngatikaura’s recent comments and the rest of Jen’s SLOMW: OC press tour, we’ll be staying tuned for more details on the divorce as it unravels.
