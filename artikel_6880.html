Prince Harry Says Moving to England Has Given Archie and Lili a Big Confidence Boost

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The royal sat down for his first TV appearance since returning to England

Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet are all settled into their new life in England, it seems—but it wasn’t a simple transition. Today, Prince Harry sat down for a rare chat on U.K. daytime show This Morning—his first television appearance since returning to England—and opened up about the changes his kids have gone through since the move in late August.

Harry said of his children: “They love being back! I say back, they never really...”

“It’s a big culture shift ... The kids in school uniforms is a big change for them, but it’s certainly helped,” he added. “The confidence in both of them has gone through the roof since being at a U.K. school, which is really, really nice.”

Harry also touched on his kids’ accents, which seem to be leaning British these days. The prince joked that he is “squeezing the accents,” and Archie and Lili’s “friends at school are doing the same.” He noted that “Lili does a good impression of ‘Mummy.’”

Harry’s TV appearance came a couple days after he and wife Duchess Meghan were seen out with their kids for the first time since their move (though the couple has made some public outings without the children). The family went to Winter Wonderland, an annual six-week Christmas festival and fair in London’s Hyde Park.

Elsewhere during his TV chat, the Duke of Sussex discussed the upcoming Invictus Games, which are set to take place in July 2027 in Birmingham, England. Harry has been participating in related events leading up to the games, and over the weekend even made an appearance at the Invictus Games in Lviv, Ukraine.
