Senior Server Engineer, Foundations at Raya

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Raya Foundations is a new platform team that owns the shared foundation every other eng team builds on - AI infra (LLM gateway, model routing, spend tracking, code review agents), server architecture, shared libs and dev tooling, CI, security remediation. We own our own roadmap, but our goal is to listen to other developers' needs and take on work that makes our apps safer and easier to build in.

As a Senior Server Engineer on Foundations you'll drive server architecture modernization and build the AI tooling the whole eng team uses. Small team, huge surface area. ~80% roadmap, ~20% keeping the lights on. Node.js + TypeScript at consumer scale.

The ideal candidate has 5+ years of experience, has been the technical leader on past teams, thinks like an owner, builds with AI (not just alongside it), and would rather fix it for everyone than just for themselves.

We offer comprehensive medical and dental coverage, $50 a day food delivery budget, equity based employment, a great culture, learning opportunities, unlimited vacation, 12 weeks paid parental leave, and we pay all employees $1,000 a year to go somewhere in the world that they've never been because of our values of human connection, empathy, and curiosity.

Platform Ownership: Collaborate, build, and lead key components of the shared foundation every Raya engineering team builds on: AI infrastructure (LLM gateway, model routing, spend tracking, code review agents), server architecture, shared libraries, developer tooling, and CI

Mentorship: Help engineers grow through pairing, code review, and direct feedback

5+ years of hands-on experience building and operating production server software, with a track record of delivering scalable and maintainable systems and growing into a technical leader within your organization

Demonstrated expertise in system design, architecture, and mastery in Node.js and TypeScript

Proven experience in building and scaling backend systems in production at a startup or fast-moving environment

Hands-on with MongoDB and/or PostgreSQL in production, including performance tuning

Experience owning platform, infrastructure, or developer-productivity work that other teams depend on (shared libraries, CI/CD, dev tooling, observability, or security remediation) and driving its adoption

Enthusiasm around new AI tools and frameworks - and a willingness to quickly adopt emerging best practices

Modernizer: You've led a large architecture migration across a codebase other teams depend on, and you got it adopted

AI Infrastructure: Experience with LiteLLM, Amazon Bedrock, or similar model-routing layers, and with prompt engineering, evals, or fine-tuning in production

Infrastructure-fluent: Comfortable with AWS, Docker/Kubernetes, and CI/CD, and with the security & infra side of running a consumer product at scale
