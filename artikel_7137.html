Please Enjoy Jenna Johnson’s Perfect Clapback to Critics and Broadway Rumors After Her ‘DWTS’ Elimination

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson may have been eliminated ahead of the Dancing With the Stars finale, but the pro dancer isn’t quite finished yet.

After they bid farewell to the ballroom on Tuesday night, Jenna posted a hilarious clapback to critics and, in the process, seemingly debunked all those Broadway rumors. In a video shared to her Instagram account on Wednesday, November 18, Jenna and the rest of the eliminated DWTS pros performed dramatized choreography, poking fun at folks who claim she kept “putting the emphasis on herself” throughout the season.

Her song choice for the post, “I Can’t Make You Love Me” by Bonnie Raitt, was pointed and aspirationally petty, too. Some of the lyrics? Ahem: “Cause I can’t make you love me if you don’t / You can’t make your heart feel something it won’t.”

As the other dancers lifted her and danced circles around her, Jenna also referenced Harry’s polarizing “scream” during their week two Viennese waltz to “Iris” by the Goo Goo Dolls…which the internet made fun of at the time.

Harry himself even chimed in with a comment, writing, “Scream. Solo after solo. It’s like I was never there… 😂😂😂.”

Another pro, Britt Stewart, added, “It’s the scream for me.”

ICYMI, one of The Sun’s sources previously alleged Jenna had been “gunning for a Broadway role, 100 percent” when she performed a mini solo at the end of her and Harry’s performance of “Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious” earlier in the season.

“She has become more and more focused on her getting a Broadway offer after this season and after the DWTS tour ends than she is highlighting Harry and it shows,” a source told the outlet. “She put a solo of herself into the routine for Disney Night—which is basically unheard of for a pro to do. Jenna is doing a disservice to [Harry] by putting the emphasis on herself, and the other pros know it.”

Welp…seems like Jenna got the last laugh. 10/10 comeback!
