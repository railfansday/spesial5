Stock Market Today, Nov. 16: Snap Falls Amid Tech Sector Sell-Off and Ongoing Youth Social Media Addiction Lawsuit Exposure

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

On Nov. 16, 2026, Snap shares slid as roughly 1,600 youth addiction lawsuits kept legal liability front and center for investors.

Snap (SNAP -3.23%), a visual messaging and augmented reality social platform, closed at $5.09, down 3.23%. A broader tech sector sell-off added to ongoing concerns around roughly 1,600 active youth social media addiction lawsuits, weighing on shares. Investors continue to watch Snapchat's path toward one billion monthly users and its monetization potential.

Trading volume reached 41.5 million shares, coming in nearly 8.1% above its three-month average of 38.4 million shares. Snap IPO'd in 2017 and has fallen 79% since going public.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC -1.92%) fell 1.92% to 7,416, and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -2.49%) dropped 2.49% to 24,510. Amid internet communication and social media stocks, Meta Platforms (META -2.38%) closed at $626.41, down 2.38%, while Pinterest (PINS -3.81%) finished at $18.42, down 3.81%, highlighting pressure across digital peers.

On Monday, Nov. 16, Snap stayed under pressure as roughly 1,600 active youth addiction lawsuits remained a central overhang. That legal risk is keeping investors focused on liability exposure and settlement costs. Moreover, Snap may need to change how its products operate to address concerns around the alleged addictive design and youth mental health risks of its apps. If so, that could impact future user growth or advertising revenues.

In addition to the legal concerns, Snap shares fell as part of a broader Wall Street sell-off in technology stocks. The benchmark 10-year Treasury yield climbed past 5% today as persistent inflation remained a key macroeconomic factor. Higher interest rates put pressure on tech sector stock valuations, especially for lower-margin growth companies like Snap.

Earlier this month, Snap reported third-quarter revenue growth of 14% to $1.72 billion and positive net income. However, Q3 average daily active users in its key North American market fell from 98 million to 92 million year over year. This suggests Snap is struggling to retain users in its most important market.
