WhatsApp is raising group chat limit to 1,500 participants

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

WhatsApp is gradually rolling out an increase to its group chat limit, allowing up to 1,500 participants to join in, instead of the previous 1,024 cap. Here are the details.

While there are exceptions, there are generally two kinds of WhatsApp users: those who hate group chats, and those who can’t seem to be in enough of them.

If you’re in the second category, you’re likely aware that WhatsApp has steadily increased the number of participants allowed in group chats over the years, jumping from 100 to 256 in 2016, then to 512 and, soon after, to 1,024 in 2022.

Now, as spotted by WABetaInfo, WhatsApp is once again expanding that limit, raising the maximum number of participants in a group chat to 1,500.

According to the website, the new limit is rolling out to users on iOS and Android, though the rollout is gradual, meaning “not every account will see the higher limit immediately.”

WhatsApp is automatically updating existing groups to support the new limit. This means that admins do not need to take any action to unlock the higher capacity. As soon as the update becomes available to their accounts, their groups will support up to 1500 members. As a result, the update lets admins add up to 476 new members to their existing groups.

This rollout comes as WhatsApp has been working to make group chats easier to manage. Just last month, the company announced four new features, including the ability to pin messages indefinitely, share status updates within groups, filter conversations by mentions and replies, and share recent message history with new members after they join.
