The fourth-gen Apple TV 4K is now twice the price of Roku's top streamer.

Apple’s latest streaming box, the Apple TV 4K (fourth generation), with a faster processor and onboard microphones for Siri AI, goes on sale Nov. 13 for $299.

Early reviews of the unit are in, and PC Mag’s Will Greenwald has praised the on-board Siri and speed bump, while at ZDNet Maria Diaz says owners of the old version shouldn’t rush to upgrade. Look out for my own hands-on thoughts on launch day, Nov. 13.

In term of improvements, the new Apple TV has an updated A19 chip, which Apple says is 50% faster than the existing A15 processor and has twice the graphics performance. The TV 4K boasts 128GB of storage space which is the same as the previous flagship.

The new set-top box has three onboard far-field microphones for conversing with Siri, as well as a familiar-looking remote with a traditional microphone button. The remote also includes Find My with Precision Finding if it gets lost behind the cushions.

All of the relevant formats are included, such as Dolby Atmos, Dolby Vision and HDR10 Plus. You can now also create a four-speaker surround system by pairing the Apple TV 4K (fourth generation) and the new HomePod Mini 2.

At $299, though, the Apple TV is more expensive than every other premium streaming box on the market. For instance, it’s $150 more than the Roku Ultra and the Google TV. The outgoing Apple TV 4K (2022) recently had a price hike of its own, with the entry-level model leaping from $129 to $199, and the company blamed the increase on an electronics shortage also known as RAMageddon.

While it’s the most expensive streamer, the fourth-gen Apple TV 4K is also likely to be capable, especially if you’re a gamer. The outgoing TV 4K is still great for streaming games via Steam Link, and the new box should be even better. For everyone else, who simply want to watch TV, a $50 streamer from Amazon or Roku should do just fine.

The new Apple TV 4K is still available for preorder at Apple’s website, and it’s due in stores on Nov. 13. Amazon shipping appears to be delayed until Nov. 17, based on my own experience.
