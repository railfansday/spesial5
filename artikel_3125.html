Stripe’s valuation set to top $200bn in a new share buyback, WSJ reports

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Stripe’s annual tender offer for employee shares would likely close early next year. Existing investors are expected to buy some of the shares this time, the Journal reports.

Stripe’s valuation is set to rise above $200 billion in its next offer to buy back shares, The Wall Street Journal reported on Tuesday. Ben Glickman wrote for the Journal, citing people familiar with the matter, that the new figure would be at least 20% above last year’s $159 billion.

The privately held payments company is discussing a tender offer for outstanding shares, according to the report. The deal would likely close early next year. The offer is not final, and the valuation could change as Stripe talks with investors. TNW has not independently verified the report.

Stripe buys back employee shares every year, often announced early in the year, so staff can cash out stock. This time, existing investors are likely to buy some of the shares, one of the people told the Journal. In last year’s offer, Stripe said it used some of its own capital to repurchase shares.

Brothers Patrick and John Collison founded Stripe in 2010. It has stayed private partly because it can give employees and investors cash for their stakes from time to time, the Journal reported. Its executives have said that staying private is preferable while AI brings so much uncertainty.

The new valuation follows a run of deals that put Stripe in the middle of how businesses use AI. In August, it sealed its OpenRouter deal, buying the startup that routes users’ requests to different AI models. The deal, worth more than $7 billion, was its largest acquisition.

Stripe also bought Metronome, which lets software companies bill customers by usage, according to the Journal. It added Parafin, a lending platform for small businesses. Its largest potential move did not happen: Stripe and the private equity firm Advent International made a bid for PayPal.

Stripe’s checkout tool and digital wallet, Link, is now one of the default payment options in AI shopping agents. Meta’s Muse is one of them. Meta had to ask Stripe to raise its limits as Muse shoppers poured in last month.
