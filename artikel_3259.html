Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Delayed orders hit sales, but new awards point to a stronger second half.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Motorcar Parts of America, Inc. (MPAA -26.62%) reported a decrease in quarterly revenue attributed to delayed customer orders and lower tariff pass-throughs, prompting management to lower its full-year sales guidance. Despite these near-term headwinds, the company secured significant new business awards and completed a strategic manufacturing relocation to Mexico to improve cost structures. Management expects a stronger second half of the fiscal year as new programs launch and inventory liquidations from competitors subside. The company remains focused on leveraging its diagnostic technology and expanding its brake product line to drive long-term market share gains.

Our JBT-1 diagnostic testing equipment has an established presence with major automotive aftermarket customers across the United States, Mexico and Canada.

Higher new vehicle prices also reinforce the economic incentive to maintain and repair existing vehicles. These dynamics are particularly relevant to our business because we supply essential components required for vehicles to start, stop and operate safely. Although repairs and purchases can be deferred temporarily, the underlying need for replacement parts remains.
