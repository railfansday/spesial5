At $1.99 per month ($55.72 upfront pre-tax), CyberGhost is one of the cheapest VPNs you can get this Black Friday. What's more, you'll have 45 days to make up your mind, with one of the longest money-back guarantees of any VPN.

CyberGhost is excellent as a streaming VPN. It offers servers optimized for different streaming platforms, and during our recent tests, it unblocked almost every major service we threw at it, from Disney+, Prime Video, Paramount+, Apple TV+, Netflix, and DAZN. If you’re a fan of local content, CyberGhost can also unblock regional platforms such as BBC iPlayer, 9Now, ITV, and 10Play.

It also has torrenting-optimized servers in 88 countries, while its overall network covers 100 countries. On top of that, there are dedicated gaming servers in the UK, US, Germany, and France, allowing you to simply pick a server for whatever you’re doing. While CyberGhost no longer discloses its total number of servers, it’s impressive that it offers connections – albeit via virtual servers – in censorship-heavy countries like Russia and China.

Furthermore, its Smart Rules feature lets you set the VPN to connect automatically when you launch a specific app or join an unsecured network. This is especially handy on Windows, where you get the most options. CyberGhost is also plenty quick for everything you’d want to use it for, including 4K streaming and gaming.

One major gripe we’ve had with the service, though, is its pesky device limit. CyberGhost only lets you register seven devices per account, so you’ll have to log out of one device before adding another once you hit the limit. PIA, which is now even cheaper this Black Friday ($1.89 per month), offers unlimited simultaneous connections.

Overall, CyberGhost's latest Black Friday deal – just $1.99 per month – makes it a solid bang-for-buck VPN, particularly if you’re happy with a reliable service that does the basics well, and little else. It also comes with a 45-day money-back guarantee, which is significantly longer than the industry-standard 30 days.
