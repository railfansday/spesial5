Moretto: Deal done, compensation paid – Krösche will be Milan’s new Head of Football

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

It seems that the finish line has finally been reached in the Markus Krösche saga, with compensation now agreed between the clubs.

The first links between Krösche and Milan date back to June, when it looked certain that the German would arrive alongside head coach Ruben Amorim. However, Eintracht Frankfurt blocked him from leaving, and the Rossoneri chose not to appoint a new head of the management.

The story never went away though, and it picked up new life when Eintracht announced that Krösche was departing his role there. Over the past few days, various sources have claimed that Milan remained in touch and that an agreement is in place with the director.

However, what needed to be resolved was the compensation to be paid to the Bundesliga side to release him from his deal. Now, Matteo Moretto has claimed that everything is done on that front too.

“A deal has been reached for Markus Krösche to become AC Milan’s Head of Football. The Rossoneri club is paying Eintracht Frankfurt a financial compensation,” he said on X.

“The German executive has always been the number one option for Cardinale; barring any changes in plans, Krösche will officially begin his Rossoneri adventure in January.”

While waiting for official announcements, it is worth remembering that Moretto also suggested Krösche’s right-hand man Timmo Hardung could follow him to Italy.
