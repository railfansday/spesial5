Horsham FC ‘well worthy’ of FA Trophy success over Torquay

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

James Hammond’s 21st minute spot-kick proved to be the difference as the Hornets beat the Gulls 1-0 at home on Saturday.

In an end-to-end Trophy tie, Horsham hit the woodwork three times in the first half and had plenty of opportunities in the second.

Torquay saw a fine effort superbly saved by Hornets stopper Sam Howes before clattering the crossbar – but Horsham held on to advance to the third round.

Di Paola told us: “It was a good game. I thought we played well. I thought we were dominant for 75 minutes of the game and then they came at us. They kept bringing on good players to try to get back into the game.

“I thought we were well worthy of the win for our first half performance if nothing else. We should have been further ahead over the course of the game. Meeky [Reece Myles-Meekums] has had a good chance to kill it at the end, but they’re a good side and we’re happy to get the win.

“When the draw came through you’re thinking, oh no! It was almost a head in hands sort of thing – but the boys have done really well to go through.

“It’s a bit of extra money for the club, so it was all good. It was a really good performance, so we’ve got to be happy with that one.”

The Hornets, who picked up £3,750 for the win, will host Isthmian North outfit Hashtag United in the next round on Saturday, December 12 – and the tie will invoke fond memories for Horsham supporters.

Needing to beat Hashtag and score two more goals than title rivals Billericay Town on the final day of the 2024-25 Isthmian Premier season, the Hornets thumped the Tags 5-1 to leapfrog Ricay on goal difference and secure promotion to the National League South.

Di Paola added: “We’ve had a lot of away draws in the cups this year so I was quite pleased. We’ve had Walton [& Hersham], Plymouth [Parkway, both in the FA Cup], Pagham [in the Sussex Senior Cup] – a few stinkers in terms of away journeys – so I’m just happy to get a home draw.

“I’m sure they’ll come down and be bang up for it. I briefly had a look at the Isthmian North table and they’re having a good season.

“I think they took voluntary relegation last year, but that seems like it’s worked for them. They’re a step three team playing at step four which always helps.

“But a home draw is all you can ever ask for. We’ll park it for a couple of weeks, but I’m just glad to be through to be honest.

“It’s another game, which is a challenge for a small squad, but it got us the prize money and the prize money is important.”

Horsham visit Dover Athletic in National League South this Saturday before welcoming Truro City on Tuesday.
