Arsenal fans will be concerned about Declan Rice’s fitness after he was substituted against Hull City.

The Gunners were victorious 1-0 against last year’s Championship play-off winners, with Martin Odegaard netting the winner in a tense affair.

There was worry around the Emirates Stadium just after the hour when Rice, the England international, was taken off in favour of Mikel Merino on 63 minutes.

Mikel Arteta had already faced questions from the media about whether he had overplayed the No41, suggestions which he laughed off.

With that in mind, here is the latest Arsenal injury news and return dates…

There was concern in the north Londoners’ camp when Rice was taken off at the weekend.

The midfielder did not look in discomfort, as he was replaced by Merino, but it did come after Arteta had been forced to answer suggestions of fatigue for the former West Ham captain.

The Gunners boss, however, insisted that there was nothing wrong with his midfielder, and as such, he is set to join up with the England squad this week as expected.

“The only player, the only player in this squad that for two games in the Carabao Cup has been left out of the squad,” Arteta said, having insisted that Rice was “fine”.

“At home with two days off. That’s my concern.

“We are really looking after him more than any other season. The way he’s training and all the load. That’s it.

“Here are the demands of this football club and you have a player like him. Not only him because there are many others that have even less time.”
