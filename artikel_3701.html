Special Forces' Collin Gosselin Is Interrupted by DS in Awkward Position

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The bathroom issues on Special Forces continue with Collin Gosselin being interrupted in an awkward position.

During the Thursday, November 5, episode of the Fox show, the Directing Staff called the recruits over for their next challenge. Gosselin, 22, however, was occupied when production showed him in the outside bathroom.

“Oh s***, I’m taking a deuce,” Gosselin said before the DS told him to “pinch it off.” The former reality star was given 20 seconds to get ready while comedian Hannah Stocking was instructed to count Gosselin down.

Season 5 of Special Forces has often addressed various toilet-related issues as some recruits adjusted to having to go to the bathroom outside. Earlier in the season, former NFL star LeSean McCoy got into a conversation with his fellow recruits about his issues.

“I’m not s***ting outside,” he told the group. “I won’t s*** for three days or eight days.”

Despite hoping to push through, McCoy’s visit to the restroom, which was out in the open, caused him to officially withdraw, saying, “I am not ready for all this thing. I am not taking no s*** here.”

McCoy, 38, then called the Directing Staff over to deliver the news.

“Staff, your bed is getting mosquitoes all night, but it’s cool. This stuff is great, but I am not s***ting outside,” he noted. “I am cool. I am not doing nothing else.”

McCoy later spoke to Us about having to deal with the elements.

“We’re out here with little stuff like this. But in real life, I saw a big mosquito, just as big as this, and they were doing a lot of biting. When I woke up the next morning, I had so many bites on my back,” he exclusively told Us about the challenges. “On my first day there, we got into the jungle and the bathroom was outside. Now that was hard. Like, whoa, we gonna use the bathroom outside? So in my head, I said, ‘I gotta hold this thing in for as long as I’m here.’ That’s what I’m thinking.”

He continued: “When I looked into the bathroom, it really was just like a hole, some wood, a circle and a bucket. That’s it. And there was a big snake in there. I’m like, ‘Oh, hell no.’ So I said, ‘I’m not using the bathroom.’ They got snakes. I saw a king cobra.”

McCoy found the conditions to be the “hardest” part.

“Obviously the missions and tasks were hard, but it’s the stuff you’re not prepared for,” he continued. “I saw that stuff in zoos and on TV — I never saw those types of animals or creatures in person. That was scary.”

Special Forces: World’s Toughest Test season 5 airs on Fox Thursdays at 9 p.m. ET.
