When everything's gone sideways at 3000ft you're going to need a parachute in GTA 6. Skyscraper diving is actually what got me into playing GTA 4 online with my friends so I'm definitely buckling in for some high altitude gliding in GTA 6. Here's how to sail the skies in Leonida.

Hop out of a plane mid-air or off the top of a building and while you're in freefall press the button prompt on screen to pull your chute and float to the ground. You can steer left and right, and angle yourself downward to descend more quickly or pull back to descend more slowly.

You only need to buy a parachute one time, but you may need to reset it between uses. Head to a gun store and select it from the rack to add it back to your inventory before you go skydiving again. You can also just re-equip a parachute that you find on top of a skyscraper.
