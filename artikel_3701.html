One Chicago Stars Who Have Confirmed They Would — Or Wouldn't — Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The One Chicago universe has become a revolving door of actors — and fans are still hopeful some of the past characters will make a comeback.

Dick Wolf started the Chicago-based family of shows with Chicago Fire in 2012. Two years later, Chicago P.D. gave fans an inside look at how police work in the Windy City. Chicago Med joined the group in 2015.

After more than a decade on air, each show has lost significant actors such as Jesse Spencer, Monica Raymund and and Joe Miñoso on Chicago Fire, Jesse Lee Soffer, Sophia Bush and and LaRoyce Hawkins on Chicago P.D. and Brian Tee and Nick Gehlfuss on Chicago Med. Luckily, a few of the stars are ready and willing to reprise their roles.

Scroll down to see which One Chicago stars have confirmed whether they want to return — or have no plans to come back — to the universe:

Character: Firefighter Joe Cruz on Chicago Fire When He Exited: Season 15, episode 5 Whether He’ll Return: Joe Miñoso exclusively told Us in November 2026, that his goodbye on the NBC series left the door open “more than a crack” for him to return as Cruz somewhere down the line. “[Showrunner] Victor [Teran] has spoken about it, and I proudly would return in any capacity if I can be of service to the show,” he shared. “It’s my absolute pleasure.” Miñoso reiterated, “I think the door remains fully open, and I look forward to the potential of that happening as soon as possible and as many times as possible.” The actor is set to direct an episode of season 15 of Chicago Fire.

Character: Dr. Natalie Manning on Chicago Med When She Exited: Season 7, episode 1 Whether She’ll Return: Torrey DeVitto announced in May 2021 that she was leaving Chicago Med at the end of season 6. However, her last episode was actually the season 7 premiere. The actress returned as Dr. Natalie Manning briefly during the season 8 finale when she met Will (Nick Gehlfuss) at the airport in Seattle, hinting they were getting back together. Fans were treated to another cameo — a full episode — during season 11 of the series in 2025 when both DeVitto and Gehlfuss’ characters returned to Chicago. When asked whether she’d return for more Med episodes, DeVitto exclusively told Us in October 2025, “I would never say ‘no,’ but you never know. I guess I'll have to watch and find out.”

Character: Emily Foster on Chicago Fire When She Exited: Season 8, episode 20 Whether She’ll Return: “I would be interested,” Annie Ilonzeh exclusively told Us in April 2025 when asked about bringing the paramedic back to Firehouse 51 in some capacity. “I would so be interested to see where Foster has landed and what's the tea and just get back with my people. I mean some of them have keys to my house, that's how much I love them.” When fans last saw Foster, she was leaving to pursue medical school. As a result, the actress is all for a reunion with some of her “family” members she still keeps in touch with from the show. “Chicago will always have my heart, Chicago Fire will [too],” Ilonzeh added. The actress recalled having a “hard time leaving” the show when her character was written off “for seemingly no reason.” She explained, “We left on good terms, which is great. And that world is very special to me, and I still watch them. I mean I support them.”

Character: Dr. Ethan Choi on Chicago Med When He Exited: Season 8, episode 9 Whether He’ll Return: “There’s always conversation [about] returning on screen,” Brian Tee told TVLine in June 2024, noting that he gets to “play with all the cast and crew” when he directs for the show. Tee said it would take the “right time or the right story line” to come along for him to jump back in front of the camera. “I think probably the hospital needs help. Some kind of tragedy is about to happen, some kind of major catastrophe drama,” he pitched as his return arc, “And maybe they bring back myself and Will Halstead [played by Nick Gehlfuss] and [Ethan’s wife] April [played by Yaya DaCosta] and bring back some of the old crew to kind of fix things to what it was.”

Character: Jay Halstead on Chicago P.D. When He Exited: Season 10, episode 3 Whether He’ll Return: After nine plus seasons of Chicago P.D., Jesse Lee Soffer exited the series in 2022. He insisted during a February 2023 interview with Variety that he’s “definitely not” opposed to reprising the role. “Never say never. I feel so bad for the fans that are like, ‘Is he going to come back?’” Soffer confessed. “‘What’s he going to do?’ ‘What’s going on with him and Hailey?’” The actor explained that he likes to think that his character is “just in another country right now.” Soffer continued, “I still love it. I still love the fans. And I still love Halstead. Halstead’s always going to be in my blood. That’s never going to change.” Soffer has since returned to P.D. as a director for an episode in both season 10 and season 11. He reprised his role as Halstead during the March 2026 three-part One Chicago crossover, where he reunited with love interest Hailey Upton. Soffer exclusively told Us in March 2026 he would “absolutely” be open to more cameos. “If there was ever a last episode of P.D., which I hope there isn't. I hope it goes 25 years or something. But if there ever was, Halstead has got to be in it, just because it's in my bones,” he shared. “It's a special thing.”

Character: April Sexton on Chicago Med When She Exited: Season 6, Episode 16 Whether She’ll Return: Yaya DaCosta reprised her role as April during three episodes of season 8 of Chicago Med after exiting the show after season 6. After her character married Ethan (Brian Tee), DaCosta told TV Insider that she wouldn’t be back in a full-time capacity unless April was a doctor. "I always thought April was going to finish her schooling and become a doctor. … I think the idea was always that we would kind of tap into some woman’s empowerment and now that he was all set, go back to her fulfilling her dream, and I guess just as time went on, everyone was comfortable seeing her as a nurse,” DaCosta explained in July 2023. “The way that she was in the hospital, the way that she interacted with the patients, the wisdom that she brought to the cases, the way that the doctors could trust her to just finish their sentences and start doing what was needed before they’re even asked, I think that kind of gave her a lot of credibility and expectation that she would be a doctor. So if ever in this or any other dimension, April Sexton were to return to screen, it would have to be as a doctor. "
