£8m Southampton outcast should be 'incredibly ambitious' Charlton Athletic transfer target

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Charlton Athletic have been told to target Southampton’s Tom Fellows as they look to strengthen their squad in the January window.

It has been a crazy season so far for the Addicks, who followed up a brilliant start to the campaign with an 11-game winless run.

However, a 2-1 victory over Sheffield United means the Londoners went into the international break on a high, and whilst they are 17th in the table, they do have a seven-point advantage over the relegation zone.

Nevertheless, it’s clear that Jones’ squad is lacking in certain departments, and the January window will provide an opportunity for the boss to add quality.

However, the January window is notoriously difficult, and with Charlton not working with the biggest budget in the division, they will have to be smart with how they recruit.

And, when asked by FLW about one player the Addicks should try to bring in, Charlton fan pundit Chris Woodward explained why he feels the club should pursue a loan move for Southampton’s Tom Fellows, as he compared it to a previous deal involving James Bree that worked for all parties.

“We haven’t exactly got the budget to splash the cash. I think we’re limited in the quality moves we can make, especially in an inflated window. Therefore, I think we will have to look to the loan market," Chris told FLW.

“One position we lack right now is a natural right winger, and the squad in general is lacking creativity. It’s incredibly ambitious, but I’d look to try and take Tom Fellows on loan, as he ticks both boxes.

“It seems silly to suggest, but 12 months ago we had James Bree on loan, and now he is one of the best full-backs in the league!

“Fellows is out of form and out of favour at Southampton, and he’s not exactly loved by the fans at the moment. So he needs to build his confidence back up and get back to the player he was.

“Fellows is above Charlton’s level, but he may look at it as a chance to be a key player in a team where he would get a lot of the focus, and Southampton may look at the work we did with Bree, and some of our previous loans, and see how we turned them around. It's an ambitious signing, but it would be an incredible one.”

This may seem like a bold move, but the reality is that Fellows is enduring a difficult period right now, and he is not getting the game time he would’ve wanted a year on from his initial £8 million move from West Brom.

You can understand why Tonda Eckert is playing Kuryu Matsuki, as he is doing very well right now, but Fellows still isn’t getting the minutes he would’ve wanted, as he has been an unused sub in five of the last seven games.

At 23, Fellows is at a stage in his career where he needs to be playing regularly, and Southampton will know that they risk stalling his career if he stays on the bench.

So, if they can bring in a winger during the window themselves, a loan shouldn’t be ruled out for Fellows. And, as mentioned, the way Charlton helped Bree would give them a shot at making this happen if Fellows was available.

Most would agree that Charlton do need attacking additions in January as the lack of creativity and goals was a major problem in their poor run prior to the Blades win.

There was a reliance on Tyreece Campbell earlier in the campaign, and he was always going to struggle to sustain the numbers he was producing at that stage.

As well as his goals, Campbell is one of few players in the squad with genuine pace, so, even if they can’t get Fellows, he is the profile that they need to try and get in January.
