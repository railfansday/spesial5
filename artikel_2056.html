Stoke City urged to sign Sheffield United star who would ‘fit the bill’

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Excitement and optimism is brewing at the Bet365 Stadium, with Stoke City firmly in the promotion mix heading into the winter run-in.

The Potters are joint-top of the Championship table, currently sitting third behind Middlesbrough and Birmingham City on goal difference following a stellar opening 15 league games.

Mark Robins’ men have only tasted defeat once in the league since early October, and have also won four out of their last five outings heading into the most recent international break.

It’s a welcome change for all connected in the Potteries, with Stoke failing to climb any higher than mid-table in all the previous eight second tier seasons since their relegation from the Premier League.

If Stoke can maintain such excellent form heading into 2027, then the January transfer window could be the time to make some statement signings, highlighting their intent to get back to the top-flight come May.

Therefore, further ambition is going to have to be shown, with the Stoke faithful eager to see what the recruitment team can get through the building.

Stoke City have been one of the standout teams in the Championship so far this season and have rightly put themselves in the frame for promotion back to the Premier League.

It’s amazing to think that the Potters lost their opening three games of the league campaign, with Robins firmly under pressure heading into September, but the team managed to put together some momentum that has snowballed over the course of the last two months.

Ever since a heavy 4-1 defeat away at Wolves at the end of August, Stoke have only gone on to lose one of their next 12 league games, incidentally against fellow promotion rivals Middlesbrough.

Apart from that agonising loss, Stoke have picked up nine victories over that period, with Robins firmly getting the best out of the current crop of players he has and building a fierce winning mentality that is needed to get out of the right end of the division.

When domestic action resumes, Stoke have some games coming up that will thoroughly test their promotion credentials, firstly hosting league leaders Birmingham City, before going on to face the likes of West Ham, QPR and West Brom heading into mid-December.

With Stoke’s excellent start to the season in mind, FLW’s Stoke fan pundit has named one player he would like to see sign on the dotted line in January to help get them over the line in their quest for promotion.

Speaking to Football League World, Stoke City fan pundit Ryan Beresford has backed the Potters to reunite with Sheffield United forward Tom Cannon in January, believing the 23-year-old would ‘fit the bill’ in a Mark Robins system, just like he did when on loan at the club during the 2024-25 campaign.

When asked who Stoke City should be signing in the January window, Ryan told FLW: “Realistically, it has to be Tom Cannon.

“Sheffield United are on a down spiral, pinch him while we can, and I think he exceeds and excels in this squad.

“Playing in the right formation, we’re 100% after a striker, so he would fit the bill.”

Ryan also mentioned targeting inform Blackburn Rovers frontman Andri Gudjohnsen, but did concede that a deal in January may not be possible.

He added: “If not, Gudjohnsen from Blackburn is fairly ambitious. Whether he leaves I’m not sure, but it would be amazing to have a top-class striker in this squad to push us even further.”

There is every chance Tom Cannon could make a return to Stoke City in the January transfer window as his spell at Sheffield United continues to fall flat.

The Blades are clearly unable to get the best out of the frontman, demonstrated once again by his underwhelming stats so far this season.

Cannon has only netted three Championship goals this term, and it’s not helped by a lack of continuity in the side, with the 23-year-old starting just eight of the 13 second tier matches he’s been available for.

Meanwhile, Stoke City are expected to be on the lookout for a striker in January given their recognised number nine options of Sam Gallagher, Róbert Boženík, George Hirst and Lamine Cissé have a combined total of just five goals so far this season.

It’s clear the Potters need more of a goalscoring presence leading the line to keep their promotion push intact, and Cannon could be the ideal candidate given he scored nine times from 22 Championship matches during his brief time in the Potteries under Mark Robins.

Over a full season, Cannon would be pushing to hit the 20-goal mark, so it’s evidence enough that he can score regularly in the division and just needs to be under the right coach to achieve it.

Whether on a loan or permanent deal, a move for Cannon is what could ultimately keep Stoke situated towards the top end of the division, with the Potters needing a more regular source of goals from a frontman if they are to stand the best chance of ending a nine-year exodus from the big time.
