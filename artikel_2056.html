Charlie Austin hails £4.5m QPR deal with Southampton a 'steal'

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Ronnie Edwards was signed permanently from Southampton by Queens Park Rangers and has had particular praise from former QPR and Saints striker Charlie Austin.

Having played as both a defensive midfielder and a centre-back since his initial arrival on loan to Queens Park Rangers from Southampton, Edwards has since made one of the two central defensive positions his own this season.

Jimmy Dunne has also been excellent as his partner for the majority of games, but Jake Clarke-Salter is also a strong performer at Championship level when fit. Between them, they are an excellent combination of options for Julien Stephan.

Speaking via his show on The Charlie Austin Football Channel on YouTube, Austin talked up the 23-year-old and said: "Well, Ronnie Edwards at £4.5 million, come from Southampton. I mean, what a steal that is from Southampton.

"Look, I played against him, Peter. I want to say, when I was at West Brom against Peterborough [United]. And I played against him, I thought, 'This young man, he's tidy. He's good him'. Then I played against QPR I think it was again.

"I always thought he would go on to bigger things other than Southampton, if I'm being honest without being rude. Or he would use that as a stepping stone and move on but he's gone to QPR.

"He went to QPR on loan, he had a great time there. I mean, listen, I don't think you change a winning team, but listen, I've had my frustrations with Julien at times because he hasn't kept certain players in the team when they performed the game previously.

"Like with [Rumarn] Burrell, we scored two won one game midweek and then he was dropped at the weekend. I couldn't get my head around it if I'm honest as a centre-forward."

Edwards emerged at Peterborough, making his senior breakthrough as a teenager before establishing himself as one of League One's most exciting young centre-backs.

His performances earned him a £3 million move to Southampton in 2024 (BBC Sport), having also helped England win the European Under-19 Championship. Edwards is an assured operator in possession, comfortable carrying the ball forward, and breaking lines.

The 25 top scorers in the Championship since the second tier was rebranded back in 2004/05, including Billy Sharp and Aleksandar Mitrovic.

He can find teammates between the lines by punching passes into forward areas, too. He is also proactive in defensive duels, although his aerial ability and tendency to be over-aggressive remain areas for improvement if he is to become a Premier League player.

Now one of the Championship's outstanding defenders at QPR, Southampton may regret selling him for a reported £4.5 million initially (Daily Echo). Had they been a bit more patient, he could be their asset worth a lot more than he is now.

Austin is spot on with his praise of Edwards, who looks increasingly like the sort of defender Southampton should have been building their future around. Allowing a young, homegrown, English talent to slip through their fingers to QPR for so little will be particularly frustrating.

Southampton are understandably focused on securing promotion, and their recruitment reflects that, but sustainable clubs cannot afford to neglect player development, even in the midst of that.
