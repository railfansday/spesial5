Poll: Which Dr. Mario & Puzzle League Box Art Is The Best?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

We are back with another edition of Box Art Brawl!

It was a Zelda blowout last time, as we matched up every cover from Ocarina of Time across its various releases. Somewhat surprisingly, the newest Switch 2 design took the win quite comfortably with 31% of the vote — it could be recency bias, but it really is a rather lovely cover.

With Europe and North America opting for the same look on this one, we've got a good old East vs. West match-up on our hands.

There's no misunderstanding what's included in this game, eh? The Dr. Mario cover is up the top, the Puzzle League one is below. A red line bisects the pair with a clear bit of "2 games in one!" text for anyone who misunderstood the cart's contents up to that point. Hey, it gets the job done.

The Japanese cover goes for something a touch more abstract, and we like it! The dual logos along the bottom make it clear what's included, of course, but the game covers have been replaced by some cool dotted art. Hang it on the wall!

Thank you for voting! We'll see you next week for another edition of Box Art Brawl!
