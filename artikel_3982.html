Pikmin 4 Nintendo Switch 2 Edition launches 12th November and a demo is out tomorrow

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Pikmin 4 Nintendo Switch 2 Edition will launch on 12th November, bundled with a new mode called Dandori Academy.

Nintendo confirmed the date during yesterday's surprise Pikmin Day Direct, which also revealed that a demo will land on the Nintendo eShop on 2nd November. Any progress you make in the demo will transfer to the full game if you buy it.

Dandori Academy is a timed exam mode. The Direct showed off one of its exams, Gargantuan Garden, which challenges players to collect gold nuggets before time runs out (thanks, Insider Gaming).

The Switch 2 Edition will also let you give voice commands to Oatchi, the game’s dog companion. If you already own Pikmin 4 on the original Switch, you can buy an upgrade pack from the eShop.

Nintendo had warned ahead of the broadcast that there would be no new game announcements. Instead, Shigeru Miyamoto introduced Japanese actor Fuka Koshiba as a new ambassador for the series, and Nintendo confirmed that 1st November, today, is now Pikmin Day.

Pikmin 4 originally launched on Switch in July 2023. The Switch 2 Edition is one of two big Nintendo releases this month, alongside The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake on 5th November.
