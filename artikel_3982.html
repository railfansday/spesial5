Final Fantasy VII Revelation Has Been Rated For Switch 2 By The ESRB

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The third and final chapter arrives next April

One of the most anticipated games of 2027 is the final chapter in Square Enix's Final Fantasy VII Remake trilogy.

Revelation is currently scheduled to arrive on 8th April 2027, and it seems the Switch 2 release and other versions of the game remain on track, as the third title has now been rated by the Entertainment Software Rating Board (ESRB).

The classification also shares some details about what fans can expect when it does arrive. According to the site, it's received a rating of 'T' for 'Teen' and contains blood, language, mild suggestive themes and violence.

You can see what exactly the ESRB had to say about this title in its official rating summary:

"This is an action role-playing game in which players follow the story of a mercenary on a quest to save the planet from evil. From a third-person perspective, players traverse locations across the planet, interact with characters, and battle human enemies and fantasy creatures (e.g., robots, monsters, mutants). Players use swords, pistols, and magic attacks to defeat enemies; battles are accompanied by realistic gunfire, light effects, and large explosions. A handful of sequences depict additional acts of violence and blood: a character impaled by a sword; a character shot at close range; a character detonating a bomb to kill soldiers. Some female characters are designed with low-cut outfits and deep cleavage; the dialogue also references suggestive material (e.g., “Bad little girls like you two deserve a good spanking”; “My, what sharp teeth you have…All the better to eat me with, perhaps?”). The words “sh*t,” “tw*t,” and “a*shole” are heard in the game."

Final Fantasy VII Revelation will be launching for the Switch 2 on the same day as other versions of the game.

Nintendo also recently featured Revelation as one of the major games on the way to the Switch 2 in 2027, alongside titles such as The Duskbloods and Resident Evil: Veronica.
