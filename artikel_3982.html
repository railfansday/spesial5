Hozy for PS5, Xbox Series launches November 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Cozy renovation and cleaning game Hozy will launch for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series on November 9, publisher tinyBuild and developer Come On Games announced.

Hozy first launched for PC via Steam on March 30. A Switch version is also planned for release in 2026.

Here is an overview of the game and “Hideaways,” via tinyBuild:

Wrap yourself in a cozy atmosphere where every small action and detail matters. Hozy is about slowing down. There are no timers, scores, penalties, and absolutely no stress or pressure. Just you, your tools, and the simple joy of making spaces beautiful again. Whether you’re wiping windows, repainting walls, or finding the perfect spot for a sofa, everything is designed to keep you in a calm, satisfying flow.

Skip the endless catalogue overwhelm. Hozy offers a carefully curated selection of furniture and decor for each level that work well together. Place items freely, rotate and arrange them however you like—there is no right or wrong and no room is ever the same. Use the tools at your disposal wisely, and you’ll create spaces worthy of an interior-design magazine!
