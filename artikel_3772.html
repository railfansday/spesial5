The Regreening launches December 7

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Cozy restoration adventure game The Regreening will launch for PC via Steam on December 7, publisher ByteRockers’ Games and developer Positive Impact Games announced.

“This fall has been all about breaking the law and tearing up virtual cities, which is fun in its own right—but sometimes your soul just needs to plant a tree and watch a family of deer move into a meadow you built by hand,” said ByteRockers’ Games CEO Maik Reichelt in a press release. “The Regreening is a love letter to cozy gaming and optimism. On December 7, we’re inviting everyone to leave the crime spree behind and help heal the world, one sprout at a time.”

Here is an overview of the game, via ByteRockers’ Games:

In The Regreening, players take on the peaceful task of revitalizing damaged landscapes, planting native flora, restoring waterways, and welcoming charming wildlife back to their natural habitats. Designed from the ground up as a stress-free sanctuary, the game encourages players to move at their own pace, unwind with acoustic soundscapes, and experience the magic of environmental stewardship with a touch of Baltic folklore.
