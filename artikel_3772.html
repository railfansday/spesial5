Marvel Rivals The Acts of Kurt Wagner event guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Decipher the prophecies to stop catastrophes.

Nightcrawler has finally made his way into Marvel Rivals, and he’s brought with him a fresh event to tackle. The Acts of Kurt Wagner event has been added to the event page with plenty of rewards you can unlock by progressing through it.

Every Marvel Rivals event is unique, with various special mechanics to navigate and lots of exclusive rewards tied to each one. To help you fully work through this one, here’s a complete walkthrough for The Acts of Kurt Wagner event in Marvel Rivals.

To successfully complete The Acts of Kurt Wagner event in Marvel Rivals, you need to work through a total of four event phases, with eight sections to tackle across all of them. Each part of the event comes with two parts: the Prophecy section, which has three missions, and the Disaster Relief portion, which features another three quests that unlock after you check off the Prophecy part.

All tasks tied to this event task you with completing specific objectives while playing in Quick Match, Competitive, Arcade, or Practice vs. AI. The nature of each specific mission varies, but generally includes relatively straightforward goals like dealing a specific amount of damage, landing a certain number of final hits, and completing a required number of matches.

To unlock additional missions so you can progress through The Acts of Kurt Wagner event, you first need to complete all available unlocked objectives. All tasks in this event unlock progressively, which means you must work through each individual section to gain access to the next part.

Some parts of this event are locked behind a timer, which means even if you get everything done leading up to it, you’ll have to wait for it to be added. All sections that are locked for a specific amount of time have a countdown on them to help you keep track of this.

There are four exclusive rewards you can earn by progressing through The Acts of Kurt Wagner event. You also get to claim two key currencies for checking off missions throughout it. Here’s a breakdown of all prizes.

The Acts of Kurt Wagner event is available to complete from Nov. 13, 2026 to Jan. 15, 2027. This means you have about two months to work through it and claim all available rewards.

Progressing through this event is a great way to check off key tasks that earn you Chrono Tokens to buy season 11 battle pass skins. There are also lots of other events running alongside The Acts of Kurt Wagner, so if you haven’t completed them all, it’s a good idea to get started on them next. This includes the Night of Illusions event, Path to Doomsday: Avengers Endgame, and Path to Doomsday: Avengers Infinity War.
