What are we all playing this weekend?

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Ah, yes, Saturday. Traditionally a day for relaxing. Why is it then I'm packing, doing last-minute shopping, and going to a wedding all in the space of eight hours? Because, Julian, you are a fool who, as ever, has left everything far too late.

What about the team, though. I imagine they've managed their time much better.

JamesI've got a date with Derelikt, a retro survival-horror FPS with just a dash of immsim keycode hunting. I'm not expecting gee-oh-tee-why material but I do respect any game whose minimum system requirements read, in their entirety, "Sound Card: Toaster Spec".

MarkLast weekend of freedom before I, both cheekily and I assure you temporarily, abandon my PC in order to play a certain game I've been waiting for since I was about 14. I should probably play something strategy-oriented or with extra swanky graphics to get in some more preemptive atonement for my sins. I might end up playing Sleeping Dogs instead.

OllieI want to spend more time in Marathon this weekend. The PvE roguelite-ness of Cryo Archive is intriguing, and I want to see how different the runs can get.

EdwinThis weekend I'm going to shop for a fan heater. I have an unhealthy love of fan heaters. They are my spirit animals. I fancy upgrading to one of those big Dyson things that looks like a mini hyperspace jumpgate, but I assume they're costly both to buy and run.

CallumWith GTA 6 now looming on the horizon, I shall spend this weekend with as few video games as possible. I’ll sit and meditate, like a great warrior prepping for the war to come. Many guides shall be written. Many hours shall be spent looking at virtual guns. Many digital policemen's lives will be taken with big bazookas. But for now, I rest… and probably eat a lot of food.

JeremyThis is the last weekend I must work on our Castlevania walkthrough after almost 50 days of continuous effort. The end is in sight and only then will the morning sun come to vanquish this horrible night.

JulianWhy have I still got too much to do? Because for some reason I decided to start a new BattleTech campaign this week and didn't use my evenings productively crossing items off my to-do list. If I get a free hour (ha!) I'll settling into my mech and cause a ruckus.

And you, reader dear, what are you playing this weekend…?
