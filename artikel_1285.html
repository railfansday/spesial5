Wayne Rooney’s I’m a Celebrity Fee Compared to Other Contestants

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Former Manchester United striker Wayne Rooney is set to appear on the 2026 edition of 'I'm a Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here', and the pundit's eye-watering appearance fee has now been revealed.

The hit reality TV show kicks off next week, with the first episode set to air on Monday night on ITV. Fans of the cult classic, hosted by presenting duo Ant and Dec, are avidly awaiting this year's iteration, with football supporters particularly intrigued by how Rooney will fare in the jungle.

The legendary centre-forward, one of the best English footballers of all time, heads down under alongside a host of other stars from different fields, including the likes of Ricky Wilson, Leigh Francis and Chloe Burrows.

Arguably the biggest name in the show's initial group, it's no surprise that Rooney is set to earn substantially more than everyone else travelling to New South Wales next week.

Hanging up his boots in 2021, Rooney has already enjoyed a number of different ventures since retiring from football. He's overseen four separate clubs as a manager, including Derby County, Birmingham City and Plymouth Argyle, while he's also worked extensively as a pundit, mostly for BBC Sport.

His next adventure is now imminent, with the prolific goalscorer set to enter the world of reality TV at the start of next week.

Rooney's wife, Coleen, famously appeared on 'I'm a Celebrity' back in 2024, finishing second behind winner Danny Jones. The former footballer will be looking to go one better than his partner when he heads to Australia.

While his name speaks for itself, Rooney's reserved and down-to-earth persona has sparked huge amounts of interest from the public about how he might deal with the various obstacles that are thrown at contestants in the jungle.

For these reasons, the ex-number nine is set to take home a significant appearance fee, far more than any of the other starting participants.

From Gary Neville to Alex Scott to Mark Chapman, UK pundits are paid very handsomely for the roles of television and radio.

According to SuperTV247 on X, Rooney will be paid £800,000 for his presence on the reality show, a figure that will add to the Manchester United legend's net worth. This is £625,000 more than the next best-paid contestant, Kaiser Chiefs frontman Ricky Wilson.

Behind Rooney and the musician come presenter AJ Odudu, actress Joanna Page and comedian Leigh Francis, all on £150,000. Meanwhile, the lowest earner is set to be internet personality Jack Skipper, who will take home £50,000.

The eyeballs Rooney's antics in the jungle will bring to the show likely justified this enormous fee, although interestingly, Coleen earned a reported £1.5 million for her presence on the 2023 edition. This was on par with politician Nigel Farage's fee in 2023.
