Goodison Park Ready For A Halloween Showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A Halloween thriller awaits when Everton Women take on Manchester United at Goodison Park. 

The frightfully fun matchday for all the family kicks off at 12.45pm on Saturday 31 October.  

Supporters of all ages can get into the spooky spirit of the Blues with a host of themed activities throughout the afternoon, including a special Halloween half-time dance, performed by local dance groups. 

We will have prizes for the best costume, and a host of themed activities throughout the afternoon, including face painters and arts and crafts tables for younger fans to create their own scary masks!  

Spin the wheels on our concourse carousels for a trick or treat, against a backdrop of live, Halloween-themed music from our ghoulish guitarists.  

Whether you're planning a family day out, introducing the next generation to Everton or simply looking for something special to enjoy together this half-term, Goodison Park is the perfect place to make lasting memories.   

And with a combination of Barclays Women’s Super League football, costumes, music and plenty of Halloween fun, it promises to be an afternoon the whole family can enjoy!   

Tickets are just £12 adults and £6 concessions.

Secure your tickets now and join us for a Halloween to remember - BUY TICKETS - Event Information Screen - Everton Ticket Office   

In the run up to the game, our hugely popular ‘Goodison Park After Dark’ tours return for two exclusive nights on Monday 26 and Tuesday 27 October. Visitors will uncover fascinating stories, historic moments and eerie tales hidden within Goodison's walls, experiencing the stadium in a way few ever have - Goodison Park After Dark can be booked here.
