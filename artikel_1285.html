Romano: Man Utd Made ‘Very Aggressive’ Move To Sign Aurelien Tchouameni

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Manchester United made a "very aggressive" move to sign Aurelien Tchouameni, with Fabrizio Romano revealing just how close they came to signing the Real Madrid star in the summer.

Needing to add some real quality to their midfield to take it to the next level, the Red Devils made Real Madrid star Tchouameni their priority target in the summer transfer window.

However, a move never came to fruition, with United instead signing Andrey Santos, Youri Tielemans and Carlos Baleba to try and improve their midfield.

Amid the possibility of a move to United, Tchouameni remained at Real Madrid and on Thursday the Spanish side confirmed a new contract for the player until the summer of 2032.

Transfer expert Fabrizio Romano, when speaking on his YouTube channel, discussed Manchester United's move for the French international and made it clear why a transfer never materialised.

"Because Manchester United were very interested and very aggressive, intense in the conversations to try and get Aurelien Tchouameni," revealed Romano. "When Manchester United in January 2026 understood that Casemiro decision was final and he was not going to stay at the club, the internal conversation at Manchester United was around Aurelien Tchouameni.

Man United also have a good relationship with the agents of Tchouameni, especially the CEO of Manchester United, Omar Berrada, has a very good relationship with the agents close to Aurelien Tchouameni.

"United started trying the understanding around the French midfielder, so United wanted to understand if there was any chance to negotiate, to bid, and to try and get Aurelien Tchouameni.

"The deal was always 90% impossible because of the salary. Tchouameni is on a very big salary, and also because Tchouameni is very happy at Real Madrid."

Manchester United have endured a dreadful start to the Premier League season, currently sitting in the bottom three after ten games of the 2026/27 campaign.

New boss Mauricio Pochettino has a major task on his hands to get the Red Devils back to their previous heights, starting this weekend with a huge clash against Liverpool at Anfield.

Much has been made of the team's midfield woes, and it is something the new man needs to solve ASAP as he bids to get his new team up the Premier League table.

The new midfield signings have not worked out at all just yet and things could have panned out rather differently had they managed to complete the signing of Tchouameni, who was described as "phenomenal" by former Man United player Nicky Butt, as he would have added some real quality in the middle of the park.

The 26-year-old, described as the 'best DM in the world' by former teammate Chema Andres, would comfortably be alongside Bruno Fernandes as the club's best player had he signed on the dotted line at Old Trafford.

That is currently not the case, however, as he is instead starring for Jose Mourinho's Real Madrid and Manchester United are at present in the bottom three.

It is once again yet another disaster for the club in the transfer market.
