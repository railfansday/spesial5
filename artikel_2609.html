2026-27 MLB free agent tracker: Yankees reunite with righty; Tarik Skubal, Teruaki Sato remain unsigned

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

An unusual MLB offseason is underway. The current Collective Bargaining Agreement is set to expire on Dec. 1, and MLB owners are expected to lock out the players. It will be the second MLB lockout in five years, and it will mean all transactions will come to a halt.

So what should we expect before the lockout begins at 11:59 p.m. ET on Dec. 1? Free agency is underway, and a few notable signings and trades have happened so far. The Yankees, for instance, agreed to a two-year deal with right-handed reliever Jonathan Loáisiga on Friday, bringing the pitcher back to the Bronx.

But we're still waiting on the first major move, and it could very well come before the end of the month. Tarik Skubal is the clear best player available on the market, an ace among aces who will play the entire season at age 30. The Mets, Blue Jays and Dodgers are seen as the frontrunners to sign the lefty, and he's recently started meeting with teams.

Behind him are lesser but still solid options in guys like Jazz Chisholm Jr., Tyler Rogers, Randy Arozarena and Freddy Peralta.

Sonny Gray (Twins), Evan Phillips (Braves), NLCS MVP Jake Bauers (Red Sox) and Dustin May (White Sox) have all also landed with new teams in November.
