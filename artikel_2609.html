Chargers WR Ladd McConkey ruled out for MNF vs. Ravens

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

5:00 p.m. ET: The Chargers ruled McConkey out on Sunday afternoon, moving Quentin Johnson into the WR1 role. With Thompson also sidelined, they will likely promote one, if not two wideouts from their practice squad. Tight ends Charlie Kolar and Oronde Gadsden could also see more targets if Mike McDaniel turns to more 12 and 13 personnel.

7:30 a.m. ET: Finally, the biggest name on the Baltimore Ravens' weekly injury report belongs to the other team. Los Angeles Chargers wide receiver Ladd McConkey is listed as questionable for Monday night's primetime matchup due to a back injury that kept him from practicing all week.

McConkey, 25, was a last-minute scratch for last Sunday's game against the Houston Texans due to back spasms that began during pregame warmups. His lack of progress this week suggests he is unlikely to suit up, depriving Justin Herbert – who is dealing with an injury to his non-throwing hand – of his best receiver. That is good news for a Baltimore secondary that has struggled to stop opposing passing games this year, making even the league's lowest-scoring offense a potential threat.

That secondary, though, will be without Jaylinn Hawkins (knee) for another week. The former Patriot occupied a 81% snap share but seemed to struggle as the Ravens' third safety before fore going down in Week 6. He returned to practice in a limited capacity this week but will still miss his fourth straight contest.

The Ravens also listed running back Adam Randall (foot) as questionable pending his activation from injured reserve. The sixth-round rookie was a full participant in practice all week, and the Ravens have open spots on their 53-man roster. The stage could be set for owner Steve Bisciotti's personal draft pick to make his NFL debut.
