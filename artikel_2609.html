Former NFL QB feels Daniel Jones signing with Patriots is 'inevitable'

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A potential quarterback move could be on the brink of taking shape for the New England Patriots.

NBC Sports' Chris Simms recently stated that it feels inevitable that Daniel Jones will eventually end up with the Patriots, who suddenly find themselves in need of help at the position with Drake Maye out for the foreseeable future.

"Get to New England now," Simms said regarding the adamancy that the Patriots should have in signing Jones right away. "Learn the offense now, so you can actually get comfortable and maybe you can get into the playoffs... So the door's open... Plus, here's the other aspect of it. [Brian] Daboll's offense has got a lot of the same language as Josh McDaniels'. It's rooted in New England. So Daniel Jones is going to know a lot of what's going on right away. I just feel like it's inevitable."

Maye suffered a fractured fibula in Week 10 against the Detroit Lions and is expected to miss at least six weeks, leaving New England with a major void under center. While Tommy DeVito is the next man up at quarterback for the Patriots, bringing in an experienced veteran makes sense for the team to help keep things afloat and potentially remain in the playoff hunt.

Jones was recently released by the Indianapolis Colts following a disappointing showing this year which saw him throw for 1,974 yards, seven touchdowns and an NFL-leading 13 interceptions through 11 games played. Jones had previously thrown for 3,101 yards, 19 touchdowns and eight interceptions across 13 games played last season.

Nevertheless, for New England, signing Jones in free agency could provide the Patriots with a considerably more established option under center than DeVito. Jones brings more starting experience and better passing ability to the field, making him an intriguing candidate to stabilize the position while Maye recovers.
