Theo Benedet, Kyler Gordon listed as out of Bears practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Bears listed three players as out of practice on their first injury report ahead of Thursday afternoon's game in Detroit.

Left tackle Theo Benedet is on that list. Benedet had to leave Sunday's win over the Saints in the fourth quarter with a quad injury.

Running back Roschon Johnson was also injured on Sunday and was listed as out of practice with an ankle injury. Defensive back Kyler Gordon was the third member of the group. He did not play on Sunday due to a shoulder injury.

Linebacker D'Marco Johnson (elbow) was listed as a full participant.

All of the participation levels were estimates as the Bears did not practice a day after beating the Saints to move to 8-2 on the season.
