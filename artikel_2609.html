NFL predictions: 3 bold takes on the Cowboys vs Eagles game

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Dallas Cowboys annihilated the Tennessee Titans last Sunday by a score of 51-30. It was a complete game by the offense, elite really, but even the defense has a 'decent' day by their standards.

The Cowboys are at home for this week's Thanksgiving game against the 3-7 Philadelphia Eagles, and are 8.5-point favorites according to our friends at FanDuel. The over/under is 48.5 points.

Last week wed did okay on our bold predictions. Tony Pollard did go over 100 yards, but he did not score twice. Mike Pettine's defense got one turnover, not two, and they almost scored as predicted, until Markquese Bell ran out of gas. CeeDee Lamb did turn out to be the star and he did get a touchdown, but only had 132 receiving yards, not the 150 predicted.

Former Cowboys QB Joe Milton returns to Dallas as the starting signal-caller for the Eagles. We know his M.O. from his Dallas days – dangerous runner with a rocket arm, struggles with consistency and accuracy.

Even with the Dallas defense picking up its pass rush and putting QBs under pressure, they still have problems letting them escape the pocket. We'll see some of that, but the Eagles will cook up a play on Thanksgiving to allow Milton to score a touchdown on a planned run.

When the Cowboys lost Tyler Goodson, the position of RB2 appeared to be an issue. But last week, Jashaun Corbin showed he's ready to claim the spot going forward. He'll get another chance this week.

Corbin had five carries for 32 yards, and impressive 6.4 yards per carry. He also delivered a crushing block on a blitz pickup to allow Dak Prescott to strike the Titans' secondary. This week his reward will also include a touchdown on offense.

The NFL has been pretty lenient about throwing flags for celebrations involving the kettle on Thanksgiving. But they aren't shy about fining players later on. Our bold prediction guarantee is that some player on the Cowboys will get fined after the game for some kind of kettle celebration.

Ok, over to you. What is your one bold prediction for the game? Hit the comments and let us know. Happy Thanksgiving!
