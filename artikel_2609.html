The NFL is back and so are the Yahoo Fantasy x Arena Club Slab Packs!

Loaded with top performers and fantasy stars, the Yahoo Fantasy x Arena Club Slab packs will be available each week during the regular season for collectors and fans to digitally rip.

For those new to Arena Club, it is a new way of collecting. As the premier tech-powered marketplace and grading platform, Arena Club brings the physical experience of card collecting into the digital age. Users can purchase and open virtual packs of real, pre-graded cards ("slabs"), buy and sell on the marketplace and manage their card showrooms all in one place. Each Arena Club Slab Pack displays the full lineup of potential cards and the likelihood of ripping each.

Yahoo Fantasy Slab Packs contain graded NFL trading cards curated by Arena Club. Fans can purchase a slab pack, each containing a graded card of an NFL player, via the Arena Club platform. This week, we're upping the ante with grails of some of the most collectible players — and top fantasy performers — being available as chases in both tiers of packs. For the Silver ($70) tier, each grail is worth roughly 35-60x of the pack price. In the Gold ($200) tier, each grail has an estimated value of 25-40x the pack price.

The Yahoo Fantasy Slab Packs will go on sale at kickoff of the Packers-Rams game (8:00 p.m. ET, Wednesday) and be available until the conclusion of the Monday night game (or until they sell out).

CeeDee Lamb, Cowboys – Lamb leads the NFL in both targets (133) and receptions (88) and is on a three-game touchdown streak. In just 11 games so far this season, the Cowboys' undisputed No. 1 receiver has surpassed every major statistical total from last season and has been the linchpin for one of the league's highest-scoring offenses.

Jahmyr Gibbs, Lions – Calling a 118-yard, two touchdown performance average would sound bizarre for any player in the NFL not named Gibbs. The Lions star has either scored or compiled at least 100 scrimmage yards in every game this season and has four games of at least 150 yards and multiple touchdowns. Gibbs isn't just making good on his No. 1 draft pick status for fantasy managers, he's putting together an MVP-caliber season.

Patrick Mahomes, Chiefs – Reports of Mahomes' demise were greatly exaggerated, as the three-time Super Bowl champion has followed up back-to-back zero touchdown performances with a four game stretch that has featured 13 TDs and no interceptions. The scarier part of all of this, Mahomes has been without Kenneth Walker III as of late and the Chiefs offense hasn't missed a beat.

Caleb Williams, Bears — Williams may not have the gaudy stats of some of the other quarterbacks in the league, but all he has done this season is win. The third-year QB is 6-1 in games he's started this season and has completed a career-best 68.3% of his passes through Week 11. Williams and the Bears enter a Thanksgiving clash with the Lions on a five-game winning streak and will need to stay hot in order to claim a hyper-competitive NFC North.

Josh Allen, Bills — At this point, we know how good Allen and Buffalo are in the regular season and in a dismal AFC East, the playoffs are seemingly a lock for the 9-1 Bills. Allen has cooled off a bit from his torrid September pace, but he is still ranked No. 2 in rushing touchdowns (11), is top-10 in QBR (70.2) and has just two games this season where he hasn't had multiple touchdowns either through the air or on the ground.

Saquon Barkley, Eagles — Although Barkley's days as a game-breaking running back appear to be in the past, the Eagles star has found the end zone in four straight games. As Joe Milton continues to find his groove at the helm of Philadelphia's offense and the receiving corps gets healthy, Barkley should be able to find more success in the rushing attack.
