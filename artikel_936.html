Scotland are aiming to finish their Nations League campaign with a flourish after a mixed start to Sebastien Pocognoli's reign as manager.

Their best performances in Group B1 have come on the road, in a 0-0 draw with Slovenia and 2-0 win over North Macedonia, while the Scots' displays at home have left a lot to be desired. Defeats of 3-0 and 2-1 by Switzerland and Slovenia respectively mean they look set to finish third in the table, which would send them into a relegation play-off against a team in the tier below.

Pocognoli will still believe his side can catch second-place Slovenia, though, and a first home win in charge would keep them in the hunt as long as Bostjan Cesar's team do not beat Switzerland.

Influential midfielder Scott McTominay returns to the squad after a successful heart procedure, while striker Che Adams is back from injury and Rangers' James Penrice is included for the first time. Meanwhile, captain Andy Robertson is set to equal Sir Kenny Dalglish's national caps record as he draws level on 102 appearances.

North Macedonia travel to Glasgow more in hope than expectation after a chastening opening four games in Group B1, in which they scored no goals and conceded ten. In fact, the minnows have failed to find the net in any of their past eight matches, with their most recent goal coming in a 7-1 thrashing by Wales 12 months ago.

Only a victory will keep alive North Macedonia's hopes of overtaking Scotland into third place. Among the players aiming to do that is former Leeds United defender Ezgjan Alioski, who could win his 93rd senior cap, but Rangers forward Bojan Miovski has been left out.

The Scots eased past these opponents in their own backyard and we expect them to do the same on home turf.
