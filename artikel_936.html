Prince Harry Pleads for Child Safety After Meghan Markle Dad’s Arrest

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Prince Harry asked for more child safety regulations just days after Meghan Markle’s father Thomas Markle was arrested on suspicion of possessing child sex abuse materials.

Four days after Meghan Markle’s father Thomas Markle Sr. was arrested for possession of child sex abuse materials, the Duke of Sussex spoke out about the dangers minors face on social media.

“How did we reach a point where we can build technology capable of extraordinary things, yet still struggle to make it safe for a child?” Harry said during his Nov. 10 speech at the Lost Screen Memorial, which honors children who lost their lives due to the harms of social media, in Belgium, per People. “I’m not here as an expert or a politician. I’m here as a father. My wife, as a mother.”

That’s why Harry—who shares kids Prince Archie, 7, and Princess Lilibet, 5, with Meghan—is calling for reform.

“We rarely ask technology to be less ambitious,” he said. “We’re told it will transform education. Cure disease. Solve problems we once thought impossible. Why should our ambition stop there?”

“Why, when we ask whether it can keep a child safe, does everything suddenly become complicated?” he continued. “I am not asking for a world without technology. That isn’t possible, it isn’t what young people want, and it certainly isn’t what I want for my own children.”

Rather, the 42-year-old hopes that social media can one day become a place where kids nurture their curiosity without consequence.

“All children should be able to go online and discover something wonderful," he said. “A friend. An interest that becomes a passion. A place where they feel understood.”

“They deserve to feel safe wherever they are,” he added. “In a classroom. On a playground. At any time, no matter where they are.”

Though Meghan, 45, didn’t join her husband onstage, the event marked her first public outing since her father was arrested Nov. 6 by the FBI in Florida on suspicion of possessing child sex abuse materials, the U.S. Attorney's Office confirmed in a release.

“Mr. Markle is accused of possessing child sexual abuse material, including photographs depicting himself with minor girls,” said First Assistant United States Attorney Bill Essayl. “These allegations are extremely serious, and we will prosecute Mr. Markle to the fullest extent of federal law.”

Thomas has been charged with one count of possession of child pornography. If convicted, per the statement, Thomas would face a maximum sentence of 20 years in federal prison.

Thomas’ arrest comes mere weeks after his son Thomas Markle Jr., Meghan’s half-brother, accused their father of possessing child sex abuse materials, which he said he found in his house in the Philippines.

“I'm shocked, I'm hurt and I'm very upset that my son is making these accusations against me,” he told The Daily Mail on Oct. 23. “I could never do what I'm being accused of. It's the worst thing anyone could accuse me of.”
