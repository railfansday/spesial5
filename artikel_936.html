David Raya MISSES training ahead of Arsenal's crunch clash with Man City in blow for Mikel Arteta

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Published: 14:47 EST, 27 November 2026 | Updated: 14:53 EST, 27 November 2026

David Raya didn’t take part in Arsenal’s training session today, plunging into doubt his availability for Sunday’s Premier League showdown against Manchester City.

Raya appeared to be in agony as he was forced off in the 70th minute of Arsenal’s 2-2 draw at Newcastle last Saturday, hurting his left shoulder in an accidental collision with William Osula.

There were initial fears the goalkeeper could be facing an extended spell on the sidelines, with Kepa Arrizabalaga playing in Tuesday’s Champions League match against Borussia Dortmund.

Yet Mikel Arteta this afternoon alluded to there being a chance Raya would be fit enough to play on Sunday, replying ‘yeah’ when asked if he was hopeful of the goalkeeper’s availability.

He also said: ‘Yeah, they're all training. Bukayo (Saka) as well is training…’, alluding to the individual work the winger is doing away from the main group.

When asked, ‘is he (Raya) diving around?’ Arteta replied: ‘(On) that, I'm not sure.’

His absence from the training session doesn’t rule the goalkeeper out of Sunday’s fixture, but significant doubts remain over whether Raya will be fit to face Man City.
