Lakers exceeding expectations this season, but roster isn’t championship material: insiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Lakers have emerged as one of the Western Conference's top teams early in the 2026-27 campaign.

But not everyone is convinced Los Angeles' level of play is sustainable.

Luka Doncic (77) and Austin Reaves (15) of the Los Angeles Lakers look on during a game against the Toronto Raptors on Nov. 1, 2026, in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

On The Hoop Collective, ESPN insiders Brian Windhorst, Tim MacMahon and Ben Golliver discussed the heavy workload for Luka Doncic in Tinseltown.

The trio believes the team is simply too reliant on Doncic and can't win a championship with the way the roster is currently built.

"Nobody whose closet isn't full of purple-and-gold clothing thought the Lakers were a championship team going into the season," MacMahon said.

"I mean Vegas had them in the mid-40s for wins. You don't look at this roster and say, 'Hey, that's a championship-or-bust season for the Lakers.' They are exceeding expectations at this point early in the season."

Luka Doncic of the Los Angeles Lakers reacts after a foul during a game against the Utah Jazz in Los Angeles, Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026. Getty Images

Los Angeles Lakers head coach JJ Redick looks on during an NBA game against the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

The Lakers saw several of their free agents find new teams in the offseason, including superstar LeBron James. While general manager Rob Pelinka orchestrated a complete revamp of the roster, many felt as though the team fell short of championship aspirations.

The squad has already impressed early this season, but the Lakers evidently still have much to prove among those who doubt their ability to make a deep playoff run.
