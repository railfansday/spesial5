Wizards’ AJ Dybantsa left bloodied in scary sight after wild collision vs. Pistons

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

AJ Dybantsa handed the Washington Wizards an epic dunk versus the Detroit Pistons three nights ago. Thursday's game, however, witnessed the first scary moment involving the top pick of the 2026 NBA Draft in the 144-140 Detroit win.

Dybantsa was playing perimeter defense and watched Cade Cunningham flow his way. Cunningham collided with Dybantsa, though, in the head. Dybantsa fell to the floor holding his head.

But that wasn't the scary part, as Dybantsa started bleeding visibly from his mouth.

That moment arrived with 25.7 seconds left in regulation. Washington later asked for a coach's challenge regarding a possible technical foul.

But Dybantsa refused to leave the court. He stayed in and forced double overtime thanks to this pull up shot from the free throw line.

That basket made it 132-132 before both teams played an extra period inside the Capital One Arena.

Detroit, however, withstood a feisty Washington team by outscoring the Wizards 12-8 in the sixth period. Dybantsa managed to play 44 total minutes, but became ineffective as a scorer in settling for just five points. He added eight rebounds on the defensive end while dishing out four assists.

Alex Sarr and Anthony Davis helped spark the scoring change for Washington, dropping 30 points each. The latter hit a double-double by grabbing 15 rebounds.

But Trae Young rose up as the leading scorer for the Wizards in hitting 37 points. Young also delivered 14 assists despite the defeat.

Cunningham hit an astonishing 52 points to lead all scorers while passing out 11 assists in the epic overtime contest. Daniss Jenkins added 21 to place second on Detroit.
