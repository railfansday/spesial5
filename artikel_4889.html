The 11 Best Lip Balms for Dry, Cracked, and Peeling Lips in 2026, Tested by Beauty Experts

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Even if you don’t have an elaborate skincare routine, you probably still use lip balm. Chapped lips aren’t just a winter issue—they can happen year-round. Cranking up my personal space heater creates a warm, dry environment that dehydrates my skin and lips, leaving them dry, flaky, and sometimes painful. While drinking water and not licking lips can help, dermatologists say those aren’t the only reasons they get chapped.

Our lips have a unique anatomy. They have thinner skin, low water retention, and fewer oil glands than the rest of our face. That makes them more prone to dryness, says dermatologist Teresa Song, MD.

A good lip balm hydrates, seals in moisture, prevents cracking, and protects the skin barrier from harsh elements, she says. That’s where the ingredients shine and separate good lip balms from great ones. Once you order one of these editor-tested, dermatologist-recommended lip balms, reapplication is key. Dr. Song advises reapplying every two to six hours, and once before bed.

The bottom line: You need something that's going to keep your pout hydrated and supple all year long. Thankfully, we tapped four dermatologists to get their input on what to look for in the best lip balms and which ones they recommend to their clientele. Then, we took matters into our own hands and tested them all ourselves, narrowing down our list to the 11 best lip balms for a wide range of different concerns. Before diving into our list, keep reading to discover what to look for when shopping for your new balm.

Dr. Song name-checked three types of ingredients to watch for—humectants, emollients, and occlusives—and shared how they keep the lips hydrated, smooth, and protected.

You’ll want to stay away from alcohol, which causes excessive dryness. Mineral oil may clog pores around the lips, and strong fragrances can lead to irritation and rashes. Though menthol, camphor, and phenol have a cooling sensation, they will irritate and dry the lips over time.

Dr. Song also recommends swapping out lip balms when seasons change and when you're traveling to a different climate. Use lighter formulas such as Rhode’s Peptide Lip Treatment in warm or humid climates and thicker formulas like the Laneige Lip Mask in cold or dry weather.

To find the best lip balms, I reevaluated my massive collection of products. I considered their ingredients and consistency, as well as their functional and cosmetic benefits. For example, I evaluated how long the hydration lasted and how sticky each swipe felt.

While my favorites range from $5 to $30, I didn’t notice a decline in the quality or feel of more affordable lip balms, nor did pricier picks equate to dramatic hydration.

Additionally, I ensured there was a mix of lightweight formulas for daily wear and heavier balms for overnight use. Because I love experimenting with my lipstick and lip liner combos, I awarded bonus points to balms that come in a variety of tints. I also factored in how vibrant or sheer each hue was on its own and how they layered with makeup. There are clear options for anyone who prefers a more natural look.
