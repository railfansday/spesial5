Chick-fil-A Is Saying Goodbye To Its Chicken &amp; Waffles—But There’s A Twist

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The waffles aren’t going down without a fight.

If you’ve been loving Chick-fil-A’s new Chicken & Waffles menu (me, I have), you might want to make another breakfast run before it’s too late. The chain’s limited-time lineup is officially on its way out, but a few lucky cities are getting an expanded version with even more options.

Chick-fil-A's nationwide Chicken & Waffles offerings are scheduled to disappear after November 14, while supplies last. But starting November 16, select restaurants in Tampa, FL; Chicago; and Raleigh, NC, will test five different waffle breakfast sandwiches.

The extended menu includes the Chicken & Waffles Breakfast Sandwich, which customers can order with Chick-fil-A’s Original, Spicy, or Grilled chicken fillet. So whether you're looking for something crispy, a little heat, or a grilled option, there’s a version for you.

But the real excitement comes from the four additional sandwiches being tested.

The new Chicken, Egg & Cheese Waffle Sandwich combines chicken with a classic breakfast pairing, while the Bacon, Egg & Cheese Waffle Sandwich swaps the usual breakfast bread for waffles. There’s also a Sausage, Egg & Cheese Waffle Sandwich, and for anyone who wants a little bit of everything, a Grilled Chicken, Bacon, Egg & Cheese Waffle Sandwich.

According to Chick-fil-A, the expanded test follows a positive response to its Chicken & Waffles menu this fall.

The expanded breakfast menu will be available at participating restaurants in Tampa, Chicago, and Raleigh from opening until 10:30 a.m., while supplies last. Customers can check the Chick-fil-A app to see whether their local restaurant is participating.

As for the rest of us, we have until November 14 to enjoy the nationwide Chicken & Waffles lineup before it disappears.

Here’s hoping the test goes well, because I’d happily trade my usual breakfast sandwich bun for a couple of waffles.
