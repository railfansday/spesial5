GarageJam doesn’t have its own device picker. It records from whatever your system has set as the default input. To choose the mic, open GNOME Settings Sound, pick the device you want under Input, and then hit record again in GarageJam. If you’d rather switch inputs for GarageJam only and leave the system default alone, use pavucontrol: start recording, open the Recording tab, and pick the source for GarageJam from the dropdown there.
