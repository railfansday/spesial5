Google Gemini rolling out ‘Memory summary’ with everything it remembers about you

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Google Gemini now offers a “Memory summary” to “view, edit, and delete what Gemini Apps understands about you.”

On the web, you can access this new experience from Settings > Personal Intelligence (or gemini.google.com/personalization-settings).

This loads a list with categories like: The Essentials (“snapshot of your core background, location, and key relationships”), Career & Professional Projects, Travel & Daily Gear, Media & Entertainment, and Fitness & Lifestyle. Each has several bullet points with specific details.

The text field at the bottom lets you “Add or remove details.” For example:

Google says “Gemini may ask follow-up questions if your request is ambiguous to ensure your summary remains accurate.”

The pencil icon in the top-right corner lets you make broader edits. In the three-dot overflow menu, you’ll find “Memory history” to see “when specific information was added or modified.”
