Mistakes doom Royals in semifinal loss, but the identity has been established

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

“As long as we come out with the grit … that’s the biggest piece to me.”

Utah Royals FC were handed an early gift on Saturday at Audi Field, but eight days after a near-perfect display of playing from in front, they were inhaled by a ferocious Washington Spirit attack that found goals in each half of a 2-1 victory that spelled the end of the Royals’ first playoff appearance.

Royals goalkeeper Mandy McGlynn, after playing the self-proclaimed best game of her life, allowed Trinity Rodman’s cross to sneak under her arm. Easy equalizer for Sofia Cantore. Nuria Rábano, a solid outside back, played a weak ball toward Kate Del Fava that Clara Luvanga pounced on to score the deciding goal.

A game of mistakes that ended a season of hope in the Wasatch Valley.

“I’m so frustrated,” Del Fava said after the game. “I just feel like we gave them the win. It feels like they didn’t really earn that win from us.”
