Many of us started running in a Brooks trainer, and with Black Friday deals in full swing, you can stock up on must-have Brooks gear or gift that friend you’re pushing to run their first 5K.

What makes Brooks trainers so popular? Their plush, slipper-like fit and comfy cushioning put them at the center of a Venn diagram where dad shoes and serious runner trainers converge.

Several top-rated models, like the Adrenaline GTS 25, are on sale at brooksrunning.com and sites like REI and Amazon. But sizes and colors are limited, so stock up now before they’re gone for good!

The stability counterpart to the Ghost 18, the Adrenaline GTS is the shoe many of us ran in when we fell in love with running. The 25th iteration is the lightest yet, with a foamy midsole, the brand’s DNA Loft v3, to absorb shock and provide soft landings. The guide rails system is Brooks’s unobtrusive stability tech for neutral runners and overpronators alike. Dense foam on the medial and lateral sides acts as bumpers to help align your knee and ankle.

“This is a fantastic shoe,” said tester Chris Garges, an overpronator and heel striker. “I’ve run in every model Adrenaline dating back to the Adrenaline 3, and this is by far the best shoe in the series. I loved everything from the fit to the ride to the cushioning.”

Brooks wanted to change what we should expect when running in a max-cushioned trainer. “We noticed that for some people, they would start running in a neutral shoe and then their body would work extra. So the internal cost was actually increasing,” said Brooks Footwear PLM and BlueLine Director Nikhil Jain.

The team set out to create a shoe that would cut that internal cost. The Glycerin Flex has a decoupled heel and Flex Zone; these components allow your foot to flex naturally instead of working against a stiff, beefy platform.

“Testing the Brooks Glycerin Flex was a novel experience for me on multiple fronts,” said Editorial Assistant Michael McDonough. “Not only was this the first Brooks running shoe I’ve ever run in, but it was also the first hyperflexible shoe that I have ever seen—and overall, it was a positive experience. The Glycerin Flex combines the benefits of a daily trainer that allows your feet to work and get stronger with the comfort and protection of a maximalist shoe.”

We like the Glycerin Max 2 for run/walking and recovery days. The shoe has 45 mm of DNA Tuned foam in the heel and a 6 mm offset. Its beefiness was off-putting for some, especially for runners hoping to get in some tempo work on their long runs. But testers said the cushioning was extremely comfy on slow days.

“I loved how comfortable the sneaker was. I instantly felt supported and it felt like I was walking on a bouncy pillow,” said wear-tester Steph Norman. “This sneaker would be great for easy/long miles where you do not have to worry about your feet hurting.”

The popular Brooks Ghost hits the trails. The shoe is outfitted for going off-road with 3 mm lugs on the TrailTack rubber outsole.

Our tester, David Hinkl, appreciated how the shoe protected his feet on gnarly surfaces. “The best part of the shoe for me was the sole,” he said. “It was beefy enough to prevent sharp rocks and pointy things from getting to my feet. Training is more fun when I don’t have to tiptoe across the trail.”

The Canopy is iconic. Despite the curtains of rain, monstrous winds, and cold temps, Des Linden was the first American woman to win the 2018 Boston Marathon after 33 years. And she wore the Canopy Jacket. This version of the Canopy (the original is also on sale with 30 percent off) can be packed into a little pouch that’s easy to store in the depths of an overpacked duffel.

The High Point keeps out the rain without overheating. Venting in the back allows airflow, while waterproof fabric and sealed inner seams keep you from getting soaked during a downpour. The jacket also packs down into a pocket.

These joggers are the softest joggers we’ve tested. They’re great for warmups, cooldowns, and traveling. I should know—I trekked Japan after running the Tokyo Marathon in these.

The Run Visible not only ensures you’re seen with reflective detail, but it also keeps you warm thanks to Thermolite insulation. Three pockets provide storage. That hi-vis orange color also helps you pop out in low light.

The Chaser is for runners who love a soft compression short with ample storage. The high-rise Chaser has side pockets for stashing your phone and leg-opening grippers to prevent them from riding up.
