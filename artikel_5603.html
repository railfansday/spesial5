Bitcoin’s cycle bottom is likely already in or very close, as a Mark Zuckerberg Meta AI Bitcoin bottom prediction highlights a final low expected in the $60,000–$68,000 zone by late December 2026 or early January 2027 at the latest. As of November 11, 2026, Bitcoin trades near $73,700–$73,900, roughly 41% below its October 2025 all-time high around $126,000.

The market has been grinding lower after a failed recovery attempt into the low $80,000s, with recent sessions testing the psychologically important $73,000 level. While the short-term trend remains soft, longer-term structure and historical cycle patterns suggest the deepest part of the correction is largely complete.

Bitcoin is consolidating in a tight range after a multi-week decline. Immediate support sits at the $73,000 round number, which coincides with recent daily lows, the approximate 200-day moving average, and prior structural levels from earlier in the year.

A daily close below $73,000 would open the door to the next support cluster near $71,500 (prior swing area) and then the 50-day moving average zone around $68,500. Deeper support resides in the $62,000–$65,000 region, with the July 2026 low near $57,700–$58,000 acting as the major cycle floor.

On the upside, resistance is layered at $75,000, followed by the more significant $80,000 area. Momentum indicators such as the RSI have cooled into neutral-to-oversold territory on daily timeframes, leaving room for either a relief bounce or one final liquidity sweep lower before a more durable base forms.

Volume has been elevated on down days, consistent with distribution by short-term holders, but longer-term holder metrics and the 200-week moving average (historically a strong demand zone) continue to provide a structural floor.

My base-case view is that the primary cycle low was printed in July 2026 near $58,000. That level marked a roughly 54% drawdown from the prior peak, shallower than the 70–80%+ declines of previous bear markets, reflecting Bitcoin’s growing institutional base and ETF demand.

The current pullback into the low $70,000s looks more like a higher-low retest or secondary correction within a broader bottoming process rather than the start of a new leg down. If $73,000 fails, a final flush into the $62,000–$68,000 zone remains the most probable destination.

This area aligns with Fibonacci retracements of the 2025–2026 rally, prior demand zones, and the upper end of the July low region. I expect any such move to occur relatively quickly, within the next four to eight weeks, driven by residual profit-taking, macro uncertainty, or year-end positioning.

By late December 2026 or the first half of January 2027 at the latest, this process should be complete. A sustained defense of the $68,000–$70,000 area (or a higher low above $70,000) would strongly support the thesis that the bottom is in.

Conversely, a decisive break below the July low near $58,000 would invalidate the constructive view and open a path toward $50,000–$55,000, though that outcome currently carries lower probability given the maturity of the correction and the strength of long-term holder support.

In short, Bitcoin appears to be in the late stages of its post-2025 correction. The most likely final low sits in the $60,000–$68,000 range, timed for the remainder of Q4 2026 into very early 2027. Once that floor is confirmed with a higher low and a reclaim of $80,000, the stage would be set for a more meaningful recovery phase.
