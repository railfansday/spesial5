Miley Cyrus Shares Sweet Memory of Godmother Dolly Parton as They Earn a Grammy Nod Together

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Dolly Parton received three posthumous Grammy nods when nominees for the 69th awards were announced on Monday including a nomination for Best Country Duo/Group Performance for her rerecording of “Light of a Clear Blue Morning,” which originally appeared on her 1977 LP, New Harvest … First Gathering.

To celebrate her 80th birthday, Parton brought together her goddaughter Miley Cyrus, Reba McEntire, and Queen Latifah (scoring her first country-music Grammy nomination) to sing on the recording, which was released as a charity single to raise funds for pediatric cancer research at Monroe Carell Jr. Children’s Hospital at Vanderbilt in Nashville.

Following the news of the nomination, Cyrus took to social media to reflect on the moment. In the post, Cyrus included a photo of the cover of New Harvest … First Gathering and wrote, “A couple Christmases ago Dolly gifted me this vinyl knowing how much I’d love an original.” Cyrus continued, “My Aunt Dolly is one of the greatest songwriters the world has ever known. She never made music for recognition but to make the world a happier place through her songs.”

The singer said that to be nominated alongside her godmother and the rest of the all-star artists for “Light of a Clear Blue Morning” is a “beautiful way to honor her enormous heart.” Cyrus added, “I miss her calling on big days like this but feel her in every moment. Love you Aunt Dolly. Parton was also nominated in the Best Country Duo/Group Performance for her “Butterfly” duet with Zac Brown and his eponymous Zac Brown Band. The song was featured on Brown’s 2025 album, Love & Fear. Brown performed it during the recent Parton tribute special, and has been making the song, with Parton’s recorded vocals, part of his live shows.

Parton’s third Grammy nomination this year is all her: She’s in contention for Best Audio Book, Narration, and Storytelling Recording for the reading of her 2025 memoir, Star of the Show: My Life On Stage.
