Tom Brady Addresses Aaron Rodgers Meltdown on Thanksgiving Day Broadcast

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Tom Brady says Aaron Rodgers violated the sacred rules of an NFL locker room ... blasting the dude for airing the Pittsburgh Steelers' dirty laundry.

The GOAT addressed Rodgers' controversial comments during Fox's broadcast of the Dallas Cowboys and Philadelphia Eagles matchup Thursday afternoon ... telling play-by-play man Kevin Burkhardt it's a privilege to be in an NFL locker room, and players shouldn't forget that.

He says private talks need to stay private ... and disagreements -- which happen all the time -- shouldn't be played for ratings during candid on-camera interviews.

TB12 says he was lucky enough to play with a ton of great teammates over the years ... stars who were great because they cared about each other and put the team's goals first.

Looking back, Brady says the relationships he built in football mattered far more than the on-field accolades ... and players shouldn't forget to focus on the personal side rather than the professional.

As for where the Steelers -- who are in playoff contention -- go from here ... Brady says he hopes this galvanizes the squad to make a late-season push.

In case you missed it ... Rodgers was cut from the Pittsburgh Steelers shortly after he ripped Coach Mike McCarthy on Pat McAfee's show -- alleging he dozed off during training camp meetings and claiming he's not 100% there during practices and games.

McCarthy told his own version of events ... claiming Rodgers gave him an ultimatum, so the team cut him. Several former teammates of Rodgers -- like Clay Matthews and T.J. Lang -- have voiced their displeasure with the future Hall of Famer.

Sounds like Rodgers isn't going to get much support here ... especially not from the league's legends.
