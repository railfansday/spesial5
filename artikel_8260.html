Thank you for the kind words everyone.
Upthread I offered some advice on more tax efficient investing with regard to your munis. Have you looked at the math in your state? Does the after tax return exceed that of a regular bond? It usually makes more sense to keep bonds in pretax, but that may force you to re-evaluate your target date funds. Once you retire and convert your 403b into an IRA making changes will be very easy as your IRA will have a lot more options.

Have you had a chance to consider ACA subsidies vs Roth conversions? It can be impossible to have both.

I also asked you a very important question. Your original post and question was about 1 more year, but you have more wealth than you need for your current expected spending of $120k a year. What is your goal for the extra money you have accumulated. This is a very serious question. You have been employed for 27 years. You have chosen to deny yourself some of life's pleasures so that you can save money which you have done with exemplary results. Now what? If you don't have a plan for this extra money, it will grow and grow and grow. Maybe that is the goal which is fine. Some people take comfort and solace in a portfolio that far exceeds their needs and the ever growing number makes them happier. Is that person you? Maybe you want to donate millions after your demise which is an admiral goal. Maybe you want your heirs to live in the lap of luxury in their retirement. Maybe there are things you are denying yourselves today which you can afford to do and should do while you are at the healthiest point in your life as of today. This is a very serious topic that I don't think should be ignored.

Take a look at this post from 2021 and then the follow up post from a few days ago. This retired lawyer had $6 million, debt free and could not get himself to enjoy his wealth. Looks like he finally figured it out. This will be you one day but with a heck of a lot more than $6 million when you turn 70
