Newsletter Perfectly Imperfect, on Track for $2M This Year, Launches an Agency

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Perfectly Imperfect, the taste-driven recommendations brand that began as a Substack newsletter in 2020, is turning its brand work into a full-service creative shop called the Perfectly Imperfect Agency, according to founder Tyler Bainbridge.

The agency is just the first of several upcoming services from Perfectly Imperfect, which is also building a newsletter platform to rival Substack and a dating app, all tied together by an “anti-algorithm” focus on human curation and taste.

As it evolves from a newsletter into an ecosystem of products, Perfectly Imperfect reflects a new route for modern media companies. Rather than chase scale through search or social platforms, Perfectly Imperfect built a direct audience around a distinct point of view, which it is now expanding.

Mark Stenberg is ADWEEK's senior media reporter.
