Manchester United injury update: Latest news and return dates as Mauricio Pochettino takes charge

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Mauricio Pochettino could be boosted by some returnees as he faces the first match of his Manchester United tenure on Sunday.

Today, Wednesday, November 18, is officially the first day of the Argentine’s tenure with United, and once he arrives at Carrington he will throw himself headlong into preparing for his first match.

That will come just five days after his arrival in the form of a trip to Anfield, to face Liverpool.

High on his to-do list will be figuring out who is or is not fit to make that journey across the north-west, and there is no shortage of patients in the physio’s room.

Marcus Rashford, Luke Shaw and Carlos Baleba are the most notable absentees, but Pochettino stands to welcome a number of fit-again stars back into the squad this week.

With that in mind, here is all the latest Manchester United injury news and potential return dates...

Marcus Rashford has not featured for United since the defeat by Atletico Madrid in October, when he picked up a persistent hip issue.

He had been pushing to return against Aston Villa but did not feature in the matchday squad, and has spent the international break building up his fitness at Carrington.

Michael Carrick had described Rashford’s injury as “day-by-day” before he left the United job, and it is hoped he will be able to return at Anfield.

Potential return date: Sunday, November 22 vs Liverpool (A)

Luke Shaw has been sidelined with a muscle injury since early October, but is thought to be pushing for a return to fitness.

As of the Aston Villa draw, Shaw had yet to return to training, but reports during the international break suggest he is pushing for a comeback at Anfield.

His return would be a huge boost for Pochettino and United, given the squad lacks a clear backup for Shaw at left-back.

Potential return date: Sunday, November 22 vs Liverpool (A)

The summer signing Carlos Baleba has made a stuttering start to this campaign as he struggles for fitness.

He arrived from Brighton with an ankle injury, which he shook off to make a run of appearances, before tweaking his hamstring against Roma earlier this month.

Speaking at the time, Carrick explained Baleba is facing weeks out and would not return during the international break.

Patrick Dorgu missed a stretch of this campaign after suffering a hamstring injury in the September international break, but returned to put in an impressive cameo against Aston Villa last time out.

It seems, though, that he is not yet fully fit. He joined up with the Danish national team for the November international break and traied with his team-mates, but was promptly sent home due to fitness concerns.

“He's still on his way back. The fact that he got those minutes against Aston Villa and did a fantastic job should be taken with a grain of salt,” said head coach Brian Riemer. “There are still some lingering issues with the major muscle injury he suffered. We need to make sure it doesn't flare up again. We're optimistic, and we also believe things will go well.”

A statement issued by the Danish FA when he left camp said he was not match fit, but it is hoped he will be able to feature in the Anfield match.

Potential return date: Sunday, November 22 vs Liverpool (A)

Harry Maguire was forced off at half-time of the 4-4 draw with Aston Villa with a back injury.

He had come into the match with a knock but pushed through it to start, but did not last long thereafter.

Carrick explained it was a back spasm which forced hi off, adding: "I don't know how bad it is but, going into the internationals, there should be plenty of time for him to recover."

Potential return date: Sunday, November 22 vs Liverpool (A)

Benjamin Sesko has been ruled out until the new year with a niggling shin injury.

The big Slovenian missed the latter stages of last season and the early days of the current campaign after suffering an injury at Old Trafford in May.

He enjoyed a run in the side and appeared to be back at full fitness, but has since been sidelined again having reaggravated the initial issue.

It is hoped a lengthy rest will allow him to fully recover without undergoing surgery.

Speaking before being sacked, Carrick said: “We're not exactly sure yet how long [he will be out for], but it's going to be a significant amount of time, unfortunately.

“So I feel for Ben, he had the issue at the end of last season that we looked after and tried to give him as much time to recover and then unfortunately he's had the issue coming into this season.

“So, we've got to do what's best for Ben and he's going to have to have some time on the sidelines, unfortunately.

Manuel Ugarte is currently recovering after undergoing surgery on a ruptured ACL suffered at the World Cup. He is expected to miss most, if not all, of the 2026/27 season.
