Sources: Brighton prepare to terminate Harry Howell's deal

Brighton are considering terminating Harry Howell's loan deal at Leicester City, sources have told Football Insider.

The 18-year-old joined the Foxes on a season-long loan deal during the summer, but has since started just two of his 13 appearances across all competitions under Russell Martin.

Brighton had hoped to see the attacking midfielder play more regularly, and are now considering recalling him from his loan with a view to another loan move in January.

A number of clubs in the Football League have expressed an interest in the England Under-19 international, and would be willing to take him in for the rest of the season.

Brighton have now been left with a difficult decision to make, with discussions underway behind the scenes about their next steps and what's best for Howell's future.

Leicester have given the teenager opportunities to perform in League One, but a lack of regular starts and the club's turbulent form may not be the best platform for him.

Brighton agreed to send Howell on loan during the summer as they were keen for him to continue his development by playing regular senior football at a high level.

The teenager has featured regularly for Leicester, but largely as an option from the bench, sparking concerns about the Seagulls' long-term plans for their young star.

Howell remains extremely highly regarded, not just at the Amex but within English football, having made seven senior appearances for Brighton before his loan move.

Now, clubs are said to be queuing up for his signature and could swoop if Brighton decide to pull the plug on his loan spell at the King Power Stadium.

Leiecester will surely be reluctant to lose a player who has featured heavily under Martin's guidance, but unless his situation changes between now and January, he could return to Brighton.

That would then allow the Seagulls to assess their other options, with a move to another EFL club most likely, as they aim for him to start regularly at a high level.

Howell is not the only player who could be on his way out of Leicester City in January, as the club are at risk of losing key players after their slow start to the season.

The Foxes currently sit seventh in the League One table, nine points shy of top spot, and alongside their first-team stars, some of their best young players could be tempted away.

Football Insider revealed on 25 November that Leeds United and Brentford are both plotting potential moves to sign Bismark Owusu in January.

The 18-year-old winger has impressed for the Foxes U18 side so far this term and is being tracked by multiple Premier League clubs as a result.

European outfits Parma, Utrecht and Copenhagen are also paying close attention to the starlet's progress, and could potentially launch a move for him this winter.
