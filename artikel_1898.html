Sheffield United v Watford: Chris Wilder's Championship promotion prediction plus recruitment admission

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

A highly experienced manager who has lasted the course in that regard in two previous spells at Sheffield United, Chris Wilder's views on one of the most competitive leagues in world football hold weight.

After taking nine points from a possible 24 in eight opening league games before the elongated late September/early October hiatus, Wilder's side have upped things in the next seven matches, claiming 13 points from an available 21.

Without being picture-perfect, United are well-placed just outside of the top eight ahead of today's resumption of business, with United starting out with a Bramall Lane double-header against former club Watford and the club where he started out in football, Southampton.

A third of the way into the campaign, just eight points separate current leaders Birmingham City from 13th-placed Blackburn Rovers. Plenty can happen - and will - between now and January 1.

Wilder said: "I've got to say, I don't think there is a Leeds United, Burnley and Sheffield United situation this year (like in 2024-25).

"It wasn't last year. Coventry were obviously the standout team. Everybody tipped Ipswich at the start of the season and they took the time to get going.

"That's the nature of the division. Everybody's looking for that consistency and it's been that rollercoaster ride for everybody (so far). We've had to endure it and suffer it as well.

"We've enjoyed some good wins as well, but I think we're all after that consistency. I'm looking for us, off an average start and a better last nine games, to push on into January and then to kick on into the second part of the season."

Behind-the-scenes, this period is also critical for instigating plans for the winter window.

Should further incremental progress in results dovetail with a strong mid-season window, then the Blades will be a team to watch in the new year.

If remains a big if, more especially with the well-documented off-the-field situation at the club.

On his transfer budget, Wilder continued: "That's fluid and that'll be in conversations with the owners. But the (recruitment) guys have been working right the way through since the last window.

"I've got an idea what I need. It's not four, five and six, but I do think we might need a little bit of help and a bit of a push with a couple of players coming in.

"We've got a loan slot available. The landscape's changed a little bit now with Femi, but I think we can cover that.

"It'll be two or three with my recommendations to push on and make us better and hopefully give us the opportunity of extending our season."

Seriki has had an operation after rupturing his patella tendon in the recent game at Charlton Athletic - with the 'devastated' right-back/wing-back sidelined for the next 12 months.

Wilder added: "We're devastated for him because this is a big injury for him. One hundred per cent, he will make a full recovery.

"The medical team here are absolutely spot on, but we've had big injuries in the past. Blaster (Oli Arblaster) had his ACL and there's other injuries that we've had and we've had to get over, as everybody else and other clubs will have to do.

"But it's a real big blow; it's a hammer blow for us and even more so for the boy, especially at his age and when he's starting to kick on."
