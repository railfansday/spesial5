De Zerbi 'angry' over Savio injury after physio talks - ex-Tottenham employee

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Savio's injury has left Tottenham Hotspur boss Roberto De Zerbi furious with the decision to let him play.

That is the opinion of former senior Tottenham scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 22-year-old wide forward made his return from injury during Saturday's 2-0 win over Ipswich Town, having spent more than a month out with a muscle injury.

But after just 25 minutes on the pitch, Savio pulled up and was forced to limp off the pitch, with De Zerbi confirming after the game that he has suffered the same injury.

Tottenham agreed a deal worth up to £85million to sign the Brazilian from Manchester City in the summer, but have had just seven appearances out of him so far this season.

The emergence of Luca Williams-Barnett may hand the forward another problem on his eventual return, with the youngster impressing in his position.

“IT’S THE SAME INJURY!” 😤 Roberto De Zerbi on Sávio’s injury setback on his return against Ipswich pic.twitter.com/VfJzxuZ2Gy

Injuries have been a constant issue for Tottenham this season, but none have been more frustrating than Savio's.

The winger has looked exciting whenever he has been on the pitch, but the Lilywhites have struggled to keep him on it.

"Those types of injuries are very risky, you've got to be very sure [that they are ready to return]," King told Football Insider.

"I could imagine the physio would have said to the player, 'look do you want to give it another week or another two weeks?' 'No, I feel fine'.

"Well, now you've got to make your decision - are you fine or are you not? Do you feel confident about going to play in the Premier League? If you make that decision, it's on your head then.

"I think the thing that De Zerbi is angry about is that he obviously wasn't ready, because he came on before the 70th minute and he went off about 25 minutes later.

"Injuries are very, very risky. You come back too early and, if you've been out five weeks, you're obviously desperate to come back. You want to get back in the team like he obviously did, and it's backfired on him and the club because he could be out now for another five weeks.

"I understand De Zerbi being angry because that's down to the physio and the player himself."

Savio's injury left a bad taste after what had been a really positive day for Tottenham.

The Lilywhites had won their fourth Premier League game from five and are now just one point behind Chelsea.

After the game, a clearly frustrated De Zerbi said: "Yes I'm very angry about that because Savio is a crucial player for us.

"He is there to make the difference for us. It's the same injury and it's the worst thing from today."
