Back in July, Kyle Healy-Matthews was tempted away from West Ham by Newcastle United.

The highly teenage starlet was a young player the Hammers were desperate not to lose.

Newcastle United fighting off a number of other Premier League clubs who had been competing to attract the teenage right-back.

However, personal terms were agreed between NUFC and Kyle Healy-Matthews and the deal completed

Obviously one for the future he looked a very positive addition, the young right-back is quick and with very good defensive ability, he’d been a stand out player in the West Ham young set-up.

Since the October 2021 takeover, Newcastle United having signed a lot of promising young players from other clubs, which will hopefully pay longer-term benefits.

Now Newcastle United have revealed on Wednesday morning that Kyle Healy-Matthews has signed his first professional contract with the club on his 17th birthday.

Official Newcastle United media stating: ‘The Romford-born youngster joined Newcastle’s Academy from West Ham United last summer and initially linked up with Chris Moore’s Under-18s squad as a first-year scholar before agreeing professional terms with the Magpies. Healy-Matthews, who primarily plays as a right-back, expressed his delight in committing his future with Newcastle and has relished his time at United’s Little Benton Academy to date.’

Kyle Healy-Matthews: “I’m very pleased to sign my first professional contract,” he told newcastleunited.com. “It’s a special moment for myself and my family, seeing all the hard work which has led up to this. Newcastle is a massive club with goals and ambitions that I feel I can contribute in. I believe I can play a big part for this club. I’ve enjoyed my time here a lot. I’ve had a lot of support from my peers and coaches with helping me to settle in as well as progressing along the way.”

The official NUFC media going on to say: ‘Kyle Healy-Matthews, who cites fellow right-back Kyle Walker as his footballing idol, has made a positive start to the current campaign at Under-18 level, with the Toon teenagers winning six of their opening seven league matches and currently occupying third spot in the division. The rapid full-back believes he has handled the challenge of relocating hundreds of miles away from home for the first time and is determined to advance through the Magpies’ youth ranks. Healy-Matthews, a member of West Ham’s Under-17 Premier League Cup winning side last season.’

Kyle Healy-Matthews: “I’ve only been here for a few months but I think I can say the move has paid off. Moving very far away from home could have been tricky but with all of the support I had around me from my peers, that really helped me to settle in quickly. I’m loving my time with the Under-18s. This season, my main aim is to progress into the Under-21s and long-term, but hopefully not too long, would be to progress into the first-team.”

Steve Harper is Newcastle United Academy Director: “Kyle has made an excellent impression since joining the Academy in the summer and we’re pleased to see him rewarded with his first professional contract. Moving away from home and settling into a new environment can be a significant challenge for any young player but Kyle has handled the transition brilliantly. He’s worked extremely hard since arriving at the club and his attitude, commitment and willingness to learn have stood out throughout his time here so far. This contract is recognition of the progress he’s made, and we’re excited to continue supporting his development as he takes the next steps in his career at Newcastle United.”
