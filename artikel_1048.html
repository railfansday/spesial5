Arsenal earn impressive Women’s Champions League draw at reigning champions Barcelona

Barcelona 1-1 Arsenal: Frida Maanum scored for the Gunners before Barca hit back but it was a well-earned draw for the English side

Frida Maanum scored as Arsenal held Women’s Champions League holders Barcelona to a creditable 1-1 draw at the Nou Camp.

The goal came against the run of play, with both Irene Paredes and Patri Guijarro having gone close for the hosts before half-time.

Caroline Graham Hansen then levelled for Barcelona in the 59th minute, but the points were ultimately shared after a more even second half

The result leaves Renee Slegers’ side on eight points after four matches in the league phase, and extends their unbeaten run to six matches in all competitions.

The fixture was a rematch of the 2025 final, in which Arsenal triumphed to claim a maiden European title, and Slegers’ side produced a similarly dogged defensive display on this occasion to leave with a point.

Arsenal could have gone in front after just four minutes when Caitlin Foord raced away down the right and had a shot deflected into the path of Alessia Russo but the England forward inexplicably headed wide with an open goal at her mercy.

That chance was immediately followed by wave after wave of Barcelona attack, with Misa Rodriguez forced to deny Graham Hansen and Sydney Schertenleib in quick succession.

After Rodriguez also thwarted Esmee Brugts, Arsenal made the breakthrough on 22 minutes. Russo did well down the left and crossed in for Foord, who was denied by Cata Coll. Maanum was first to the loose ball and fired under the Barcelona goalkeeper to make it 1-0.

Barcelona turned to three-time Ballon d’Or winner Aitana Bonmati early into the second half and equalised in the 59th minute. Brugts spotted the unmarked Graham Hansen, who controlled and cut inside before rifling into the corner.

Slegers reacted by introducing Ona Battle and Olivia Smith, which was followed by Russo sending an effort over. Schertenleib went close next, but Leah Williamson produced a timely block and it finished all square.
