Tenerife paid an emotional tribute to striker Jesus de Miguel as the club returned to action for the first time since his death.

De Miguel died in hospital a day after colliding with Andorra goalkeeper Jesus Owono during a Spanish second-tier match on 3 November.

For the visit of Ceuta, players of both teams as well as referees came onto the pitch donning “De Miguel 9” shirts and wearing black armbands. A wreath was placed on the pitch before a minute of silence was observed, which was bookended by a standing ovation.

In the ninth minute, play was stopped and a further round of applause followed, while a video of De Miguel was shown around the stadium, as occurred in all first and second tier games in Spain last weekend.

Tenerife fans also unfurled a banner that read: "Jesus De Miguel, 9, eternally blue and white.”

Tenerife forward Nacho Gil opened the scoring in the 40th minute and celebrated by holding up De Miguel's shirt to the crowd, before then revealing a message written under his shirt that read: "Demi (De Miguel's nickname), always in our hearts."

Neal Viereck added a second four minutes from time to seal a 2-0 win for Tenerife, ending a run ‌of six games without a win.

The result moved Tenerife up to 14th in the Segunda standings, while Ceuta are bottom.
