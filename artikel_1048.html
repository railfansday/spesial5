Fan who racially abused Kylian Mbappe at LaLiga match avoids prison time

A fan who racially abused Kylian Mbappe during a LaLiga match last year has been handed an 11-month suspended prison sentence after being convicted of a hate crime.

The suspension of the sentence is conditional on the unnamed fan not attending football stadiums for any official sports competitions for two years.

The individual has also been handed a fine along with “various disqualification penalties”, Real Madrid said in a statement.

“This ruling adds to the criminal convictions already issued for racist insults against Real Madrid players at the Mestalla, Son Moix, Vallecas, and José Zorrilla stadiums,” the club statement continued.

“Real Madrid, which has acted as a private prosecutor in this case and continues to do so in others involving similar incidents, reaffirms its commitment to combating racism and eradicating any racist behavior in football and sports.”

The incident occurred during Real Madrid’s 3-0 win over Real Oviedo at the Carlos Tartiere Stadium in August 2025, during which Mbappe bagged a brace.

Vinicius Jr, who has been persistently targeted with racist abuse at multiple stadiums in Spain as well as online, also scored in the match.

After the match, LaLiga reported alleged racist chanting towards Mbappe and Vinicius to Spain’s National Police, with the league alleging that chants imitating monkey sounds were heard on multiple occasions.

In March 2024, Vinicius broke down in tears during an emotional press conference as he bemoaned that racism was getting worse as perpetrators were going unpunished.

“I just want to play football but it’s hard to move forward… I feel less and less like playing (due to the racist abuse),” he said.
