Open a CIT Bank Savings Connect account for savings you’ll add to or withdraw from, since it pays 3.65% annual percentage yield (APY) on any balance. Choose Platinum Savings instead only if you’ll keep at least $5,000 there, because on any day the balance ends below that, all of it earns its standard 0.25%.

CIT Bank, an online division of First Citizens Bank with no branches, suits savers who move money by transfer and can set some aside for months. By our math at standard rates, Platinum Savings’ 3.75% top tier earns about $10 a year more than Savings Connect’s 3.65% on $10,000, and a month at $4,900 earns about $1 in Platinum Savings against $15 in Savings Connect.

CIT Bank works best for online savers who split their money, with Savings Connect for cash that comes and goes, Platinum Savings for a balance that never drops below $5,000, and a 1-year or No-Penalty CD for cash set aside. Anyone who deposits cash or writes paper checks needs a bank with branches, and savers locking money away for 2 years or more should find a CD paying more than CIT’s 0.50%.
