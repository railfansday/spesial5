Azkona double and assist carry Athletic Club to 4-1 Liga F win at Real Sociedad

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Athletic Club were three goals clear by half-time at Anoeta in San Sebastián on Sunday 15 November, and Real Sociedad never got close enough to threaten the result. Ortega, Azkona with two and Pinedo scored for the visitors. Keefe replied for the hosts six minutes after the restart.

The opener came in the 21st minute. Elexpuru supplied the assist and Ortega finished to put Athletic Club ahead, two minutes after Camino had been booked for a foul on the Real Sociedad side.

Facila picked up a yellow card for Athletic Club in the 29th minute, but it did nothing to slow the visitors. Azkona doubled the lead on 35 minutes and added a third on 39, this time from an Ortega assist. That gave Ortega a goal and an assist inside the first half.

Real Sociedad found a way back in early in the second half. Marcos provided the pass for Keefe to score in the 51st minute. The revival was short-lived. Landaluze was booked for Athletic Club two minutes later, and on 57 minutes Azkona turned provider for Pinedo to restore the three-goal margin.

Referee Trujillano’s last act of note was a yellow card for Valero in the third minute of stoppage time. Azkona finished with two goals and an assist, a hand in three of the four.

The win leaves Athletic Club third in the Liga F table with 19 points from 11 matches, on a record of five wins, four draws and two defeats. Atlético Madrid also have 19 points in fourth but have played a game fewer. Real Madrid are five points ahead in second, and Barcelona lead on 27.

It was Athletic Club’s first win in four matches. Their previous three had brought a 1-1 draw with Real Madrid, a 2-0 defeat to Granadilla Tenerife and a goalless draw with Sevilla.

Real Sociedad are ninth with 13 points. The defeat ended a run of five matches without a loss in all competitions, a sequence that included two 2-0 wins over Fenerbahce and a 2-1 victory against Eibar.

This was also a far more one-sided meeting than recent editions of the fixture. Each side had won two of the previous five league encounters, with one draw, and none of those games was decided by more than two goals. Real Sociedad had won the most recent of them 2-0 on 15 February 2026.

Both clubs return to league action on Sunday 22 November. Athletic Club face the toughest test in the division, a trip to leaders Barcelona, who have won nine of their 10 matches. Real Sociedad host Sevilla, who are 14th with 10 points and have drawn four of their last five.
