No shade to those who love their subscription services, but viewing physical movies is a time-honored tradition that shouldn’t go away. Watching them doesn’t feel all that different, but deciding which movie to watch feels less annoying; sifting through a movie collection on your shelf or in a storage binder is so much more fun (and less overwhelming) than scrolling endlessly in apps.

While standard Blu-ray players that display movies at up to 1080p are fairly inexpensive, 4K Blu-ray counterparts that can show movies in all their 4K HDR glory are significantly pricier (but totally worth it if you’re a movie buff). No matter the player you own, there are always Blu-ray deals, and the discounts are especially good during Black Friday. We’ve highlighted some of our favorites below, though Amazon, Barnes & Noble, and Gruv are each overflowing with more deals than we could realistically wedge into our coverage.
