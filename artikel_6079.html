Between Black Friday deals, gifts for family and friends and all the other expenses that come with the holiday season, it's easy for spending to add up.

But with the right credit card, it can also be one of the most rewarding times of the year.

Whether you're shopping online or in-store, choosing a card that aligns with your spending habits can help you earn valuable points, miles or cash back on your purchases. Some cards also offer valuable perks, like purchase protection, that can come in handy during the holiday season.

Here are our picks for the best credit cards for holiday shopping in 2026.

The information for the Prime Visa has been collected independently by The Points Guy. The card details on this page have not been reviewed or provided by the card issuer.

The Capital One Venture Rewards is a great option for those who want to earn flexible rewards without worrying about different spending category bonuses. The Venture Rewards earns 2 miles per dollar spent on all purchases — ensuring that you earn bonus miles no matter if you’re buying stocking stuffers or perusing Cyber Monday deals.

There’s also flexibility in how you redeem those miles you earn. You can use your miles to “pay” yourself back for travel booked in the past 90 days. Or, you can transfer your miles to 15-plus airline and hotel loyalty programs to book new travel. You can also redeem miles for gift cards or statement credits for eligible purchases.

To learn more, read our full review of the Venture Rewards.

The is a great option for earning bonus rewards year-round, thanks to its rotating quarterly categories.

For the fourth quarter of 2026, cardholders can earn 5% cash back on grocery store purchases and American Red Cross donations, plus an elevated 7% cash back on dining purchases. That could come in handy if you're stocking up for holiday gatherings or dining out with family and friends.

These bonus rates apply to up to $1,500 in combined purchases across the quarterly categories through Dec. 31, after which you'll earn 1% back. You must activate the categories by Dec. 14 to qualify.

While 5% cash back is appealing on its own, the Freedom Flex can be even more valuable when paired with a Chase card that earns transferable Ultimate Rewards points, such as the (see rates and fees), or the (see rates and fees).

By combining your rewards, you can transfer points to airline and hotel partners, including United MileagePlus and World of Hyatt, potentially getting more value from your holiday spending. Note that the Sapphire Preferred offers a 4:3 transfer ratio for World of Hyatt.

To learn more, read our full review of the Freedom Flex.

If you’re a regular Amazon shopper, the Prime Visa is the card for you, with no annual fee (though it requires an Amazon Prime membership).

With Black Friday and Cyber Monday coming, you want a card that gives you the biggest bang for your holiday shopping buck. The Prime Visa offers unlimited 5% on Amazon, Whole Foods and Chase Travel, 2% on restaurants, gas stations and local transit and commuting and 1% on all other purchases. Additionally, earn 10% or more on a rotating selection of items and categories on Amazon.com.

If you don’t have the card already, new applicants will instantly get a $150 Amazon gift card upon approval, exclusively for Prime members.

To learn more, check out our full review of the Prime Visa.

Without question, the is one of the best cards for foodies. The card offers an impressive 4 points per dollar spent at U.S. supermarkets (up to $25,000 spent per calendar year, then 1 point per dollar spent) and restaurants worldwide (on up to $50,000 in purchases per calendar year, then 1 point per dollar spent).

While the earning rate for nonbonus purchases yields 1 point per dollar spent, you may want to consider paying with the Amex Gold this holiday season for its strong purchase protection policy. Eligible items are covered for up to $10,000 in theft, damage, or loss within the first 90 days of purchase (up to $50,000 per calendar year).*

That’s considerable, especially if you buy big-ticket items like electronics or furniture.

The Amex Gold also comes with extended warranty protection, which prolongs a U.S. manufacturer’s warranty by an additional year on eligible warranties of five years or less.*

You’ll never know when you need to rely on these protections, but they can be quite valuable and may be worth sacrificing the bonus points you’d earn from the other cards on this list, even after factoring in the $325 annual fee.

*Eligibility and benefit levels vary by card. Terms, conditions, and limitations apply. Visit americanexpress.com/benefitsguide for details. Policies are underwritten by AMEX Assurance Company.

If you have the ability to park a significant amount of your investments and savings at Bank of America or Merrill, the is a great option for holiday purchases.

While the card comes with reasonable (though not earth-shattering) earning rates of 2 points per dollar spent on travel and dining and 1.5 points per dollar spent on all purchases (which can be redeemed at the rate of 1 cent per point), the real value of this card comes when you can make it to either the Preferred Honors or Premier award tiers in the BofA Rewards™ program.

By doing so, the everyday earning rate on this card can jump all the way up to roughly 2.6 points for every dollar spent, making it a terrific choice for holiday gifts.

To learn more, read our full review of the Bank of America Premium Rewards.

The final card on our list earns a spot in part for its simplicity. The offers a flat 1% back on every purchase plus another 1% back as you pay your bill (up to a 2% total return).

While this return rate on everyday purchases doesn’t compare to the potential returns highlighted above, this is automatic; it results in cold, hard cash in your account. For many shoppers, a no-annual-fee card like this fits the bill.

To learn more, read our full review of the Double Cash.

Holiday spending can do more than check gifts off your list — it can also help you reach big credit card goals. If you’ve recently opened a new card, consider putting your holiday purchases toward the minimum spending requirement to earn a valuable welcome bonus. Just be sure to budget responsibly and pay off your balance in full as often as you can.

If you already have multiple cards, check whether any offer calendar-year spending bonuses or perks that reset soon. Concentrating your purchases on one card could help you unlock statement credits, free nights or elite status before the year ends.

Another easy way to earn extra rewards is through online shopping portals. These portals let you earn additional points, miles or cash back just by clicking through before you buy (no special credit card required).

To find the best offer, use CashBack Monitor, which compares earning rates across dozens of portals for the same retailer. You can also install your preferred portal’s browser extension, so you’ll never miss out on bonus rewards during checkout.

The holiday season doesn’t have to drain your wallet, and with the right credit card strategy, your spending can actually pay you back. Focus on cards that match your shopping habits, whether that means flat-rate cash back, bonus rewards on popular retailers or flexible travel points you can redeem later.

Pair your card with online shopping portals and seasonal category bonuses to earn even more rewards on every purchase. And if you’re opening a new card, use your holiday spending to hit minimum spend requirements and unlock valuable welcome offers.

With a little planning, your holiday shopping can turn into a rewards windfall, from extra points and cash back to future flights, hotel stays or statement credits, making this season both festive and rewarding.
