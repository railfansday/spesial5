Reports: Maignan will have MRI after early substitution for France – the suspected issue

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Last night, AC Milan saw their captain Mike Maignan removed after 45 minutes in France’s final international game of the break.

As international breaks go, Milan were having a great one. Players were, for the most part, playing well and they’d had no injury issues. However, that changed last night with Maignan forcibly taken off after suffering a knock.

It creates a bit of worry about how long he could be sidelined for, and he’s expected to go for tests in the next 24 hours.

According to L’Equipe last night, the goalkeeper felt discomfort in his right thigh and Zidane’s coaching staff didn’t want to take any risks. So, he was removed from the game after the first half, as MilanNews relays.

Then, this morning, Daniele Longo has provided a further update, stating the pain came in his right hip – in the adductor area – after a challenge. He will now return to Milan this evening (Monday) and then go for an MRI on Tuesday to assess things.

It could well offer Lorenzo Torriani a chance in the next few games, but he’d have hoped it would have come under different circumstances. Nevertheless, it’s a huge opportunity and he must take it.
