Sheffield Wednesday boss Dave Challinor provides update on key defender Max Lowe

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The left-back recently suffered a recurrence of the hamstring problem which afflicted him earlier this season - ensuring that he faces an additional spell out.

Challinor, whose fourth-placed side welcome Reading in League One this weekend, also confirmed that Mason Burstow and Reece James are progressing well in their rehabilitation.

Tyler Onyango is a long-term absentee alongside academy prospect George Brown, who is to undergo hamstring surgery, which will rule him out for three months.

Challinor, whose side are unbeaten at league level since he took over - taking seven points from an available nine - said: "We've had a couple of games and then you see Mason and Reece out there today, they've been handed over and are now doing football-specific stuff, so that's a real positive and we obviously need to get them back as soon as possible.

"The more longer-term ones, we've probably got a bit of a gap now. Max isn't too far behind, he's probably due the end of December and then you've got the couple of long-term ones.

"With them, it's just making sure they're in a good place themselves. They've not got football realistically on the horizon, but that doesn't mean they're not important to make sure we're giving them the best chance when they come back."

Specifically on Brown, who has had an injury-time time in the past year at the club, Challinor added: "George - and probably I'll use Tyler as well, although Tyler's a little bit older - the last four years for Tyler have been riddled with serious injuries and operations to recover from. They're not little injuries, they're big ones.

"So you've got someone in a similar camp who's been through that, so that'll be good and important that they form a bond.

"It helps massively when they're in here because the work that's been done and the work that will continue to be done will make this a really enjoyable place to be.

"A place to work but also a place to bond and get to know people and will be an enjoyable place.

"It'll be important that when you are in moments of need - dark and down moments if you like - that the people around you allow you to talk about that and ultimately come out the other side.

"These things have to make you stronger and that's difficult for young players at times to comprehend."
