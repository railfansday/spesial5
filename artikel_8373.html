Why I’m Walking Away From Nursing

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

​I’m Cat. I work as a registered nurse, and starting in January, I’ll be trading in my scrubs for trail runners and a backpack. My husband and I are known to be spontaneous and challenge ourselves when life starts feeling boring. Packing a U-Haul with whatever fits and moving to a state we’ve never been to, hiking to the top of a mountain in Colorado to exchange vows, buying a house 14 hours away we’ve only seen pictures of online; you could say we live for the spontaneity of life. My motivation for hiking the trail goes far beyond last-minute decision-making; it comes from more recent experiences while working as a nurse.

Exchanging vows on the summit of Mount Bierstadt (14,065 feet)

​Nursing has this way of showing you just how fragile life really is. Having worked as a float tech in all units of the hospital and recently as a nurse in ICU step-down and rehab, I’ve cared for adults, both young and old, who lost their health and independence in an instant. I quickly realized I’ve been taking my own health for granted. I didn’t quite know I was in search of a challenge in life, but as always, my husband was at the driver’s seat of a new adventure. This time, it was all on me.

​It all started sitting by the fire in our backyard. My seasoned thru-hiker husband asked if I wanted to hike the Appalachian Trail; I shrugged and said okay. A week later, he suggested adding the Florida Trail first to get my trail legs ready; I quickly agreed to that, too. By the third week, he suggested I hike the entire ECT. Just like that, a casual backyard fire turned into a 4,500-mile hike.

​I’ll be hiking the Eastern Continental Trail (ECT), which is a connection of trails spanning from Florida to Canada. I decided to blog my adventures to have something to look back on and to share my travels with friends and family. I don’t know what lies ahead of me, but what I do know is I’m about to make so many memories, connections, and mistakes!

If you’d like to follow along with the journey, I’ll be sharing updates, photos, and stories right here while I head north.
