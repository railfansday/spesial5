JetBlue Raises Bag Fees for the Second Time This Year

JetBlue’s second bag fee increase this year tests how much more travelers will absorb.

For travelers in economy, the cost of checking a bag is now $50, up from $45, if purchased more than 24 hours before departure, according to its website. Bags checked in within 24 hours of departure will cost $60. During peak periods, checked bags will cost $55, up from $49, and a bag checked in less than 24 hours before departure will cost $70.

The carrier is the first in the U.S. to raise baggage fees twice in 2026 as surging fuel costs wipe out much of the airline industry’s profits.

JetBlue has raised checked bag fees for the second time in 2026, making it the first U.S. carrier to do so twice this year. Economy travelers now pay $50 for a first checked bag bought more than 24 hours before departure, up from $45, and $60 if bought within 24 hours. Peak-period bags rise to $55 from $49, or $70 inside the 24-hour window. A JetBlue spokesperson said the increases are meant to offset rising fuel and operating costs while keeping base fares competitive, and co-branded cardholders and Mosaic loyalty members keep free checked bags. Jet fuel averaged $4.41 a gallon as of Tuesday afternoon, and most major U.S. carriers already raised bag fees earlier this year. Carriers are also pushing fares higher: domestic airfares have risen an average of 30% this year, Delta CEO Ed Bastian said demand held up after a 20% fare increase, and JetBlue president Marty St. George said pricing has not reached equilibrium relative to cost increases since 2019.
