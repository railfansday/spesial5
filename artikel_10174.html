Meta brings Conductor on board its model train

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Agent orchestration startup gets its exit

Meta has acquired AI coding startup Conductor, signaling interest in applying AI to software engineering tasks.

Charlie Holtz, the startup's co-founder and CEO, made the announcement in a blog post. He also revealed that the company's hosted Conductor Cloud, a sandbox for hosted agents, will go read-only on November 13 and shut down on November 20.

Conductor, the eponymous company's macOS app for running multiple coding agents simultaneously in isolated copies of Git projects, will continue working for the next few months, and the biz expects to provide a way to transfer Conductor Cloud workspaces to local machines.

But in early 2027, Conductor will no longer receive new models or critical bug fixes.

A Meta spokesperson confirmed that the acquisition will strengthen the company's developer-oriented AI capabilities but declined to elaborate further. Perhaps Conductor will play nicely with Muse Code, Meta's recently introduced coding agent.

Having spent heavily on strengthening its AI research lab, Meta has moderated its appetite for big acquisitions since April when China blocked its $2 billion offer for Manus AI, a developer of software agents.

Meta's other acquisitions this year, Moltbook, Assured Robot Intelligence, and Stilla, are also AI-oriented but more modest in scope.

The Conductor deal comes without an announced price, and follows in the footsteps of Meta regaining credibility as a model provider: Llama 4 disappointed but Meta's recent Muse Wonder and Spark models rank respectably among the top LLMs at the moment.

In Meta's most recent conference call for investors [PDF], CEO Mark Zuckerberg said his company aims to provide AI agents that non-technical people can deploy for business tasks.

"What we see is a lot of people are actually using Muse today to help run especially small businesses," he said. "And we think that there's both an opportunity to help create an agent that people use to really enable them to automate and do a lot of the parts of spinning up a small business that are just kind of difficult and time-consuming but aren't the core thing that a person is trying to do."

Meta's AI strategy, as Zuckerberg describes it, is focused on collecting transaction fees from businesses for enabling agentic commerce, selling advanced AI capabilities to developers in the form of model access or tokens, charging for Muse to automate business tasks, and applying AI to personalize and improve results from ads.

It's a good time to run an AI-related startup, as leading model companies seek to add value beyond offering AI models. Models themselves have become commoditized to the point that only a few leading model makers can get away with charging a premium. In an effort to maintain that lead, top model makers have been purchasing innovation (and talent) while they are still flush with investor funds.

On Wednesday, OpenAI said it plans to acquire Pierre Computer Company, maker of distributed storage service Code Storage⁠ and open-source developer tools. And a few months earlier, the ChatGPT maker snapped up Ona, which makes cloud development environments for agents.

Anthropic's most recent developer tools deal involved Stainless back in May. Its purchase of Opaque Systems, which offers confidential computing technology, could also be squeezed into the developer tool category.

At some point, when it becomes clearer what kinds of AI services people will pay for, startup acquisitions may become more focused. But for the moment, with software development being the most successful use of AI, makers of AI-oriented developer tooling have a shot at a glorious exit. ®
