DL Gabriel Truss survived severe car accident to earn second chance at college football

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Garden City Community College defensive lineman Gabriel Truss committed to Eastern Michigan this weekend. The Eagles have a good class of JUCO additions coming in, but Truss is a little different.

He is a 6-foot-3, 320-pound run stuffer who has college experience having played at Missouri Western State prior to coming to Garden City. What makes him extra unique are the circumstances that brought him to Garden City and have made him so appreciative of every day he gets to play football and simply be alive.

"I got in a car accident and thought I'd never play football again," Truss said. "I had split my jaw almost in half, I was missing my bottom four teeth, I fractured the top of my spine, broke my leg, fractured rib, I had a concussion, and had emergency jaw surgery."

The early days in the hospital were tough.

"There were days when I was in terrible pain," Truss said. "I'd be in my hospital bed, pressing my button and waiting for the nurse to come bring me pills to take away the pain and I tried not to question why God put me through this. But my faith played a big factor. I'm so thankful now for getting offers to play at the next level."

Eastern Michigan proved to be that right opportunity, with his official visit to Ypsilanti sealing the deal for Truss.

"I would say the coaches, their hospitality, and their interest in me made me want to be there. And I liked the facilities, the meeting rooms and all that," Truss said.

With that, Truss now prepares for the next step of his journey, which is enrolling mid-year at Eastern Michigan.

"Getting this opportunity, coming from where I'm from and being through what I've been through, I just am very thankful," Truss said.

Truss had 16 tackles (2.5 for loss) this season for Garden City.
