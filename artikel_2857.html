Shane Steichen: Daniel Jones didn't ask to be released

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Colts abruptly released quarterback Daniel Jones on Friday. When coach Shane Steichen spoke to reporters on Friday, he was asked whether Jones asked to be cut.

“No, this was a decision that was just made for both, you know, the best interests of both parties,” Steichen said, via James Boyd of The Athletic.

Steichen also explained the timing by saying, “It’s just where we’re at in our season. . . . That’s just the situation that we’re at right now.”

The situation was clear. Jones was going to be benched, in order to ensure that he won’t suffer an injury that will trigger a $10.51 million guarantee for 2027. If that had happened, he would have been asked to be cut. And they likely would have done it.

So they accelerated it. Skipped steps. Got to where they would have landed after doing the dance.

Steichen also said that, as expected, Anthony Richardson will become the starter.

So instead of squatting on Jones and not letting him play, Jones gets a chance to land with a contender as a backup. Likely on the practice squad, which would give him the ability to jump to an active roster if/when a starter gets injured.
