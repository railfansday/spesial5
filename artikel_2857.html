Steelers activate WR Roman Wilson

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Wide receiver Roman Wilson will be back in the Steelers' lineup for Sunday's game against the Eagles.

The Steelers announced that they have activated Wilson from injured reserve on Saturday. Wilson has been out since injuring his hand in Week 5. He had 14 receptions for 188 yards and two touchdowns before his injury.

Wide receiver Michael Pittman and offensive lineman Gennings Dunker have also been designated for return, but neither one of them was activated for this weekend's game.

The Steelers also signed linebacker K.J. Henry to the active roster. Wide receiver Max Hurleman and offensive lineman Doug Nester have been elevated from the practice squad on a temporary basis.
