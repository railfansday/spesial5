Joe Burrow tosses 3-yard TD pass, Bengals lead Steelers 10-0

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It's been all Bengals so far on Sunday night.

Quarterback Joe Burrow connected with Drew Sample for a 3-yard touchdown, giving the club a 10-0 lead early in the second quarter.

The Bengals got a field goal out of their first drive to open the game and after got a three-and-out from their defense.

That's part of why Cincinnati leads time of possession 14:13 to 1:00 through just over one quarter of game time.

The Bengals have 12 first downs to Pittsburgh's zero.

On Cincinnati's second possession, a defensive pass interference penalty helped the Bengals, as the ball was put on the 3-yard line. On the next play, Burrow bought time and hit tight end Sample on the left side for the score.

Burrow is 15-of-18 for 125 yards with a TD to start the night. He now has 24 touchdown passes on the season.

Ja'Marr Chase has four catches for 47 yards.
