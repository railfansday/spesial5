Jazz make roster moves to bolster their rotation

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Utah Jazz announced today that they waived two-way guard Trey Alexander.

It looks like that move might have been related to an ankle injury Trey Alexander is dealing with.

It's too bad Alexander went down with an injury, he might have been a player the Jazz could have used down the road. But Utah needed to add someone who could help immediately with the struggles they're going through. Which leads us to the other news of the day. The Jazz are signing center Jonas Aidoo to a two-way contract.

Aidoo was a prospect we saw in summer league with the Jazz, and now he joins the Jazz to help with their center rotation. Utah has been dealing with multiple injuries that have derailed their season so far. Recently, Utah added Tony Bradley, and now they have added Aidoo as they wait for Jusuf Nurkic to get healthy. Aidoo might not play, but he provides depth if Mo Bamba or Tony Bradley gets hurt now that Jaxson Hayes is injured.

It's been a rough season for the Jazz so far, they can only hope they can get some wins soon while they wait for the return of some of their core players.
