Fired by Orlando, Seb Hines lands Houston Dash job 11 weeks later

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Seb Hines won the NWSL Shield and the NWSL championship with the Orlando Pride in 2024. On Aug. 31, the Pride fired him.

Eleven weeks later, he has a new job. The Houston Dash named Hines the seventh head coach in Houston Dash history on Nov. 17. He takes over a franchise that has reached the NWSL playoffs once in 13 seasons.

"Seb brings a track record of success that truly aligns with out ambitions for the Houston Dash," said Angela Hucles Mangano, the Dash's president of women's soccer, who led the search. "He understands what it takes to compete for championships in the NWSL, because he has done it before."

He has. The 2024 Pride piled up 60 points and 18 wins, both league records at the time, and went 23 games without a loss to start the season. Then they won the Shield for the best regular-season record and the championship, the first trophies in club history. Hines was named NWSL Coach of the Year.

He had already made history once. When Orlando named him interim coach in 2022, he became the league's first Black head coach. The 2024 trophies made him the first Black head coach to win one.

Orlando reached the semifinals again in 2025, losing 1-0 to Gotham FC, which went on to win the title. FIFA put Hines on its five-coach shortlist for The Best FIFA Women's Coach that year.

The 2026 fell apart. The Pride had lost 12 games by the end of August. On Aug. 23, Hines got his 50th regular-season win, 1-0 at Washington, and became the sixth coach in league history to reach 50. Eight days later, he was out.

He went 50-40-21 in the regular season with Orlando and 4-1 in the playoffs.

The Dash opened 2026 at 3-1-0 and then faded. They fired coach Fabrice Gautrat on Sept. 13 and went 2-3-1 the rest of the way under interim coach Jaime Frias. They finished 9-15-6. Their only playoff appearance came in 2022.

Hucles Mangano said one playoff trip isn't the bar.

"Our ambition is bigger than a single season or postseason appearance, we want to build a team that consistently competes at the highest level and contends for trophies," she said. "we believe Seb is the right leader to help us realize that ambition."

Hines, 38, grew up in Wetherby, England. He spent eight seasons as a player in the Premier League and the Championship, England's second tier. He came to Orlando City on loan before the club's first MLS season in 2015 and stayed. He started to coach in Orlando in 2018, joined the Pride staff as an assistant in 2020 and holds a U.S. Soccer Pro License.

"I'm incredibly grateful for the opportunity to lead the Houston Dash and excited about what we can accomplish together," Hines said. "I look forward to working closely with Angela, out ownership group and the players as we turn that shared vision into results and that work is underway."

"Ultimately, we want to build something this city and the fans can be proud of, give them a team they are excited to watch and create sustained success for this club."
