Toyota is bringing a kei truck to America that only goes 25mph

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Toyota is bringing a tiny kei truck to America in 2027 that’s only 11 feet long and has a top speed of 25mph.

Whether you’ve traveled to Japan or played Forza Horizon 6, you’ll likely have seen your fair share of kei trucks. These little vehicles are known for their small size and ability to squeeze into tight spaces with very little problem. They’re both practical for those with minimal space and, well, they’re also rather adorable.

Now, Toyota is bringing this pint-sized approach to the US with a new kei truck model that will be released in the US next year.

Announced on November 10, the KEI-01 measures just over 11 feet long and less than five feet wide. The miniature pickup also comes with a 660cc three-cylinder engine, selectable four-wheel drive, an enclosed cabin, and a cargo bed with three folding sides.

Despite its small size, the truck can carry up to 600 pounds and is capable of towing small trailers/equpment. Inside, drivers get heating, air conditioning, sliding and reclining seats, and storage behind the seats for tools and other gear. Oh, and it also retains its signature right-hand-drive layout.

There is just one catch, though: the KEI-01 tops out at just 25mph. It’s not exactly the fastest truck on the market, but Toyota says the little truck has mainly been designed for use on farms, university campuses, and businesses.

If you’re hoping to buy your own tiny kei truck, you’ll have to wait, as Toyota will initially only sell it to businesses and organizations through selected dealers. However, the Japanese car company has said it may open sales to everyone else later if there’s enough demand.
