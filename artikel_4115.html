Dicevaders is a roguelike dice-roller about overwhelming Earth's defences, coming to mobile

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Time to defend the Earth! Wait, no. In today's game, you're doing anything except saving the planet! Because this time around, in this follow-up to Starvaders, you play not as the saviours, but the Dicevaders, as this roguelike dice roller is set to arrive November 26th on iOS and Android!

In Dicevaders, your objective is simple: overwhelm humanity's defences as you hop from planet to planet with the ultimate aim of Earth itself. To do so, you place troops onto the board, play cards and, of course, roll dice. You'll need to synergise your choices to build up combos, while deciding when to deploy your units, nudge the outcome of dice or lock the min place. Think Balatro or Dice of Kalma, mixed with destroying the Earth.

Graphics-wise, it's also quite pleasant with pleasingly crunchy retro pixels, and an art style that'll be instantly familiar to fans of Starvaders, which is set in the same universe as Dicevaders.

Overall, it's definitely one to watch when it lands on iOS and Android, with an expected launch of November 26th. Especially if you were a fan of Starvaders, or the aforementioned Dice of Kalma.
