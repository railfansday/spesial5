Michael Carrick has now been sacked by Manchester United.

An official announcement on Thursday morning revealing the news.

Less than six months ago Manchester United agreed a contract with Michael Carrick for him to be manager until the end of June 2028.

The Geordie manager now the latest victim of Sir Jim Ratcliffe and the Glazers.

Manchester United now sitting in the relegation zone three months into the season.

Only one win in the Premier League this season, three in fifteen in all competitions.

The official Man U statement starting: ‘Manchester United can confirm that Michael Carrick has departed his role as men’s first-team head coach. Michael rejoined the club in January and guided the team to a third-placed Premier League finish by winning 12 out of the remaining 17 games of the 2025/26 season, accumulating the highest points tally in the league during that period. After a disappointing start to the season, the club has come to the extremely difficult conclusion that a change is necessary in order to achieve our ambitions.’

The statement going on to add: ‘Michael is, and always will be, a Manchester United legend. Just as he did as a player, he has conducted himself with the highest standards of dedication and professionalism throughout his time back with the club. His leadership and achievements last season will always be fondly remembered. The club intends to appoint a permanent successor as quickly as possible.’

It looks like Maurico Pochettino will be the new Manchester United team boss.
