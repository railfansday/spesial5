Scott Parker damns his own players with this painful one-word home truth | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Scott Parker could do little else other than state the obvious last night as West Ham plumbed new depths of mediocrity against their south coast opponents. For the first time this season, the league’s top scorers failed to find their opponent’s net despite again hitting the woodwork twice.

Speaking after the game, Parker came up with a new expression for ‘pulling their finger out’ – when speaking to West Ham tv:

“We need to fix up as a group and as a team and give ourselves a platform.”

This is an honest group. They’re probably too desperate, really and I think that’s weighing quite heavy and you can see that.

it’s just certain moments we’re a little bit soft in certain moments. We’re easy to play against in certain moments.”

Which means, as we can all see for ourselves it is less about the quality of the play, more about the mental resilience of the eleven on the pitch who have seemingly lost – for the time being – the ability to take another low blow and fight back. Conceding one goal away at Portsmouth is no disaster. Failing to register two goals against them is the real failing.

Another year, another hit: Soucek’s commitment to this team stands head, shoulders, torso and boots above some within the limp lettuce line up. Right now it ss more about the character of those selected than the ability.

At times earlier in the season West Ham would have romped down the other end and banged in three in quick succession to redress the balance and grab the points. And that is what Parker needs to find again. It isn’t the magic fix of a ‘perfect’ defensive performance, our players aren’t good enough to give us that. It is a mental resilience that ten didn’t have last night.

Eleven Tomas Souceks, players who are prepared to bleed for this club, will do nicely.

Clearly some ( c’mon, we know who they are) aren’t prepared to dig in, to battle, to die for the shirt ( metaphorically speaking of course) ands they’re the ones who need to be sidelined. Forget the (huh) best footballers. Give us 10 fighters alongside Soucek.

The Czech showed again why, without being the best footballer, he wins the admiration of every West Ham fan. Because he bleeds for the team. The rest would do well to follow his example.
