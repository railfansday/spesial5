The West Ham teenager who stunned Arsenal is back after four years

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Former West Ham United midfielder Reece Oxford has returned to English football, as he signed a deal with League One side, Bromley.

The midfielder first caught the eye with the Hammers in the early stages of his professional career, as he produced an eye-catching display in his senior debut against Arsenal in August 2015.

Oxford became West Ham's youngest-ever Premier League player at 16 years and 236 days old, and his performance looked as though he'd been playing regularly in the top-flight.

West Ham ran out 2-0 winners at the Emirates against Arsenal in that match, and Oxford came in for high praise from Sky Sports' co-commentator on the day, Niall Quinn.

“He’s been phenomenal. A star is born,” Sky Sports co-commentator Niall Quinn said. “He’s been mature, he’s ordering people around. It’s unbelievable to see. He’s mobile, he gets around and he knows the game. He’s a natural.”

Oxford went on to label his performance as a 'dream debut' in the win over the Gunners, and an 'honour' to play for West Ham in the top-flight.

“It was a dream debut,” Oxford told Sky Sports. “It’s an honour to play for West Ham and to be the youngest.”

Oxford is now 27-years-old, and it's fair to say that his career hasn't quite hit the heights that he and supporters would have originally hoped to see.

Whilst contracted to West Ham, he had loan spells with Reading whilst they were in the Championship, before two brief spells with German side Borussia Mönchengladbach, before having his final loan spell with FC Augsburg.

He seemingly made a good impression with Augsburg as well, as he went on to sign for them on a permanent basis in 2019. Oxford then spent six years in Germany with them, making 80 appearances in total across all competitions.

The former England youth international's time with Augsburg came to an end at the end of the 2024/25 season, when he departed when his contracted expired.

The midfielder had a year out of competitive football, and it'll be interesting to see if he can recapture his best form, as he's a player with real quality when at his best, which we saw with both West Ham and Augsburg.

There will be some doubts whether he can do that though, as Oxford hasn't played a competitive match since November 2022, as he's had numerous injury problems which have stalled his development in senior football.

A move to Bromley could be the ideal move for him, although the League One side are struggling in their efforts to avoid relegation back into League Two.

Bromley are next in action on Saturday, when they travel to the Select Car Leasing Stadium to take on Reading, in what will be Oxford's first trip back to his former club.

Bromley have found it tough to build any sort of momentum this season, as they're bottom of the League One table, as well as being three points adrift of safety heading into this weekend's fixtures.

This dismal run of form includes being winless from their last 11 matches, and it's a tough ask for them to end that run against a Reading side that have only lost once at home so far in the 2026/27 season.

Oxford has a point to prove at this level, having not played this far down the English football pyramid, but if he can hit the ground running, then Bromley will have a great player on their hands.
