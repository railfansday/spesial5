The KNOX Newcastle United Player of the Month nominations have now been revealed.

The club making public the three nominations for Newcastle United fans to vote on.

In August the first KNOX Newcastle United Player of the Month of the season was Amar Dedic.

Then in September it was Lewis Hall who picked up the second accolade of this 2026-27 campaign.

Newcastle United announcing: ‘We’re giving you the chance to vote for your KNOX Player of the Month for October. The KNOX Player of the Month campaign offers supporters the chance to recognise the player who has impressed them the most.’

Newcastle United outlining the contributions of each of the trio.

‘In October, Nico González emerged as a regular starter for Matthias Jaissle’s side, impressing in the victory over Crystal Palace.’

‘Lewis Hall was an ever-present figure in United’s XI, and continued to deliver strong performances at both ends of the pitch.’

‘Meanwhile, Harvey Barnes was back among the goals, further adding to his tally in black and white.’
