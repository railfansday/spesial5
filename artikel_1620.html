He is joining Liverpool, commentator confirms African sensation is coming to Anfield

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Liverpool look to have completed an exciting signing.

These are exciting times at Anfield. Julian Ward has been promoted to be the main man in charge of the club, taking on the sporting director's role, and he is already building an exciting team.

The evidence of that could be seen in the success of Liverpool's 4-0 demolition over Manchester United.

It was the stars who showed up on the afternoon who Liverpool are building their future on.

Florian Wirtz excelled. Rio Ngumoha was simply astonishing. Both of them scored and registered an assist as well. The former created the most chances (10) in the game and the latter completed the most dribbles (4). So essentially both of them were doing what they were supposed to be doing.
