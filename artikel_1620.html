'I reckon:' Liverpool verdict on 'physically dominant' sensation revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Liverpool have a lot of decisions to make before the end of the 2026-27 season.

Right now, they don't really look like they're going to be able to compete for the Premier League title or the Champions League.

Indeed, there are only seven points between them and first-placed Arsenal but, considering the Reds have already been beaten by Mikel Arteta's men once this season, that gap is going to be hard to break down.

Obviously, if they end up drawing or losing a couple of games like they did against Newcastle on Saturday, they could be in with a chance but, if it ends up being down to Liverpool, fans wouldn't really hold out much hope.

That is, especially since they were beaten 3-2 in the Champions League by Fenerbahce.

Changes are clearly needed at Anfield which is why the upcoming transfer windows are going to be massive for them.

Liverpool would have to slash Ronald Araujo's buy clause in half to sign him permanently

Nobody knows what Liverpool are going to do in the winter or summer transfer windows.

They have thrown their hat into many different rings with almost every position among their ranks covered by a new target or two.

Some players that the Reds have been linked with have been exciting for fans but others, not so much.

There are also some decisions to make about their current crop of players with Ronald Araujo's future being one of the most talked-about deals in the world.

Within their season-long loan deal, Liverpool have a €55 million option to buy the Uruguayan and, even though he's performed rather well, the Reds are still undecided about whether they want to activate that option to buy or not.

And, now, thanks to an update from David Lynch via Anfield Index, it's now clear what Liverpool's plans are.

"That figure [€55 million] is far too high for Liverpool to be interested," he said.

"You’re talking it would have to be, I reckon, about even half that much to even give a sniff of Liverpool being interested."

Liverpool need to make their decision on Ronald Araujo soon

Although there is still a long while until June comes around, a decision on Araujo needs to be made.

He's currently Liverpool's first-choice right-back and, without him among their ranks, even more problems would be coming the Reds' way.

Obviously, his prior experience of La Liga and the Champions League has massively helped him adjust to Premier League life but, since he's 27 years old and has a rather ropey injury history, it feels like a lot of money for Liverpool to commit to.

That is, especially when they don't know how good Conor Bradley is going to be upon his return from injury.

Everything is up in the air at then moment but, even if they don't want to, a decision is going to have to be made soon.

Otherwise, the Reds could end up missing out on the "physically dominant" defender or going for someone who's worse than the Uruguayan which would be far from ideal.
