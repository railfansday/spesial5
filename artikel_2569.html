South Carolina Basketball Suffers First Loss Of The Season In Thriller

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

South Carolina basketball came within a few possessions of pulling off an impressive upset Wednesday afternoon, but the Gamecocks ultimately fell 71-69 to No. 18 USC in the Acrisure Series in Palm Desert, California.

The Gamecocks entered the matchup undefeated at 5-0 and faced their biggest challenge of the young season against a talented USC team. South Carolina controlled much of the contest, held a halftime lead and appeared to be in position to hand the Trojans their first loss.

However, USC found a way to turn things around late, using a decisive run in the final minutes to escape with the victory.

With the loss, South Carolina dropped to 5-1, while USC improved to 7-0 and kept its perfect record intact.

South Carolina came out ready to compete, showing it was not intimidated by the nationally ranked Trojans.

The Gamecocks used strong outside shooting to keep USC's defense off balance. Camden Heide was especially effective from beyond the arc, knocking down four three pointers and finishing with 12 points.

Aleksas Bieliauskas also delivered a major performance inside. The forward scored 18 points and grabbed 12 rebounds, giving South Carolina an important presence on both ends of the floor.

The Gamecocks built an early advantage and continued making shots throughout the opening half. Their three-point shooting helped them stay ahead of USC's high powered offense.

South Carolina entered halftime with a 37-35 lead, putting itself in a strong position to compete for another important victory.

The second half featured plenty of back and forth action, with South Carolina continuing to find ways to slow down the Trojans.

USC entered the game averaging more than 94 points per contest, but the Gamecocks did a strong job limiting its offensive production. The Trojans struggled from the field and particularly had trouble finding their rhythm from three point range.

Alijah Arenas became the biggest offensive weapon for USC. He finished with a game-high 25 points and consistently found opportunities to attack the basket and get to the free throw line.

Rodney Rice added 13 points, although he struggled with his shooting and finished with five turnovers.

South Carolina continued to lead for much of the afternoon and built a 10 point advantage with just over eight minutes remaining. At that point, the Gamecocks appeared to be closing in on a major upset.

Unfortunately for South Carolina, the final minutes belonged to the Trojans.

USC increased its defensive pressure and began taking advantage of opportunities at the free-throw line. The Trojans closed the game on a 10-2 run, holding the Gamecocks scoreless over the final 4:05.

South Carolina had opportunities to regain the lead, but late missed shots prevented the Gamecocks from completing the upset.

Arenas led the comeback and finished 12 of 13 from the free throw line. His ability to create offense and convert important free throws helped USC escape with the 71-69 victory.

South Carolina showed plenty of positives despite suffering its first defeat. The Gamecocks competed with a ranked opponent, shot 52.4% from three point range and controlled much of the game.

The biggest disappointment was allowing USC to take control during the closing minutes.

The loss drops South Carolina to 5-1, but the Gamecocks have an opportunity to learn from the experience and respond in their next matchup against Arizona State.

This was a difficult way to lose, especially after holding a late lead. However, South Carolina proved it can compete with some of the best teams in college basketball.
