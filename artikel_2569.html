Roman Wilson practices in full Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Pittsburgh Steelers WR Roman Wilson (hand) fully participated in practice Wednesday, Nov. 18.

Why It Matters: Wilson seems to have a good chance to return to the lineup this week, but fantasy players will know more later in the week. He will likely see a few targets per game once he returns and can be a No. 4 or No. 5 receiver in fantasy leagues.
