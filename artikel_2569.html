Bayern Munich confirm new contract for Harry Kane

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Harry Kane has signed a new contract with Bayern Munich, extending his stay at the German champions until 2029.

FC Bayern officially confirmed the Ballon d’Or winner’s contract extension on Thursday.

“I feel right at home at FC Bayern. I love the club, the fans and the city of Munich," said Kane, who joined FC Bayern from Tottenham in 2023.

"We have a fantastic team and, in Vincent Kompany, a fantastic manager. It makes me very proud to be part of this club. My whole family is very happy here. I was keen to stay. I’m now looking forward to the future and I want to win many more titles with this great club.”

“What began as a major transfer has, over time, become a special chapter in FC Bayern’s history. Harry Kane lives up to every promise football has to offer, and he has secured his place in the hearts of the fans as one of the greatest figures in the sport of our time. My compliments to Jan-Christian Dreesen and our sporting management team. This is a special moment for FC Bayern today.”

Bayern board member for sport Max Eberl added:

“This key contract extension gives us certainty in our planning. It sends a message that the biggest goals are within reach at FC Bayern. Players like Harry Kane offer a team unique opportunities. We’re delighted that he will continue to be a key figure who helps shape the direction of the club, and with whom we can continue to build our squad for the future.”

Kane has scored 156 goals in 161 games for FC Bayern.
