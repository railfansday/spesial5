NBA Week 6 Injury Report: Cameron Boozer to miss at least four weeks; don't panic on Cade Cunningham

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The injuries keep stacking up across the league, and this stacked rookie class hasn't been immune from it. Cameron Boozer is set to miss at least four weeks with an ankle injury that will force him to miss a third straight game on Wednesday. He has been one of the best rookies in the league this year, and he has been instrumental in the Grizzlies' hot start. This would be a great opportunity for Jerami Grant to step into the starting lineup, but he is slated to be re-evaluated around Dec. 6 after suffering a calf strain.

Kris Murray and Jahmai Mashack have each gotten a start in place of Boozer, so far. Mashack didn't do much in his start and was completely out of the rotation for their last game, while Murray was able to do a little more with his opportunity. Taylor Hendricks and Olivier-Maxence Prosper have also taken on extra minutes off the bench with Boozer, Grant and GG Jackson all sidelined.

It's hard to identify a Grizzlies player that's worth picking up. I'm not really confident in any of them providing consistent value, even if the minutes are there. Feel free to take a chance on one of them, but it may be best to look elsewhere. The good news is if you have any other Memphis rotation players, they should see a bump in usage.

Here are 10 other injury updates from around the league.

The latest news on Nurkić has him being re-evaluated on Friday, so hopefully his return isn't far off. Jaxson Hayes has missed three of Utah's last six games with a left knee strain, and he has started two of the three games he has played during that stretch. Mo Bamba has seen some starts as well, though his last one wasn't nearly as productive as the few before that. Kyle Filipowski had his best game of the season on Monday with 21 points and 12 rebounds after not really being in the rotation before that. When Nurkić returns and gets up to speed, he'll be the team's starting center, so the time to stream any of the other options is coming to a close, though managers shouldn't have much confidence in any of them.

Cunningham missed Tuesday's game against the Magic, which was the second time he missed a game over their last four due to right knee soreness. There's no reason to be alarmed right now, but this is worth keeping an eye on. Isaiah Joe slid into the starting lineup and had a season-high 21 points, which was great to see after missing so much time already this year. Daniss Jenkins dished out 10 assists as the primary ball handler. Again, keep an eye on the injury report ahead of their matchup with the Bucks on Friday, but don't panic.
