Why Securitize Stock Surged Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

More real-world assets are moving onchain.

Shares of Securitize (SECZ +22.38%) spiked 22% on Tuesday after the tokenization platform announced an intriguing acquisition.

Securitize's revenue rose 13% year over year to $18.6 million in the third quarter.

The financial technology provider helps convert ownership of real-world assets into digital tokens stored on a blockchain, bringing greater access and transparency to previously illiquid markets.

Average tokenized assets on Securitize's platform jumped 39% to $5 billion.

"That growth came from tier one asset managers bringing real products onchain including new ones from Ark Invest, BlackRock, and Neuberger, and to me it's the clearest sign yet that institutions are past the experimenting stage," CEO Carlos Domingo said.

Still, investors should note that Securitize has not yet achieved sustained profitability. The company's adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) checked in at a loss of $2 million.

Securitize also struck a deal to acquire River One to enter the $6 trillion asset-backed finance market. River One specializes in connecting the $321 billion global stablecoin market with the traditional lending industry.

The deal is projected to close in the fourth quarter.

"Credit is one of the most exciting emerging asset classes for blockchain-based financial markets, and acquiring River One represents an important step in expanding our business into this space," Domingo said.
