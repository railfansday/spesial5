Codere Online: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Spain's new deposit rules add near-term friction, yet stable acquisition costs show marketing spend can grow without eroding profitability.

On its third quarter of 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 12, 2026, Codere Online (CDRO -1.56%) announced revenue grew 31% year over year to 62.6 million euros, driven by expanding player spend. Online gaming operators depend on converting user volume into profitable retention rather than burning capital on low-value users. Managing player value determines whether top-line expansion generates durable earnings. Three things management said stand out.

Management prioritized player quality over mass volume, lifting average monthly spend per active user 24% to 134 euros. Higher spend per user translates directly into improved profitability without forcing the business to overspend on customer acquisition.

Spain introduced new identification and deposit rules that apply across operators, creating temporary operational hurdles. Established companies with proper infrastructure can adapt while smaller competitors struggle to comply.

Management kept cost per acquisition, or CPA, which measures the marketing expense required to sign up a new paying player, steady at 196 euros over the last 12 months. Maintaining stable acquisition costs lets the business protect adjusted EBITDA, or earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, a measure of core operating profitability, even as total marketing spend expands.

Revenue momentum continues across key markets like Mexico, and leadership remains comfortable aiming for the upper end of its full-year targets. The business holds structural advantages in its debt-free balance sheet and negative working capital model, while management closely watches regulatory developments, and foreign currency fluctuations.

Looking beyond near-term regulatory adjustments in Europe, leadership intends to keep its capital allocation framework grounded in measured spending and disciplined underwriting.
