Greif (GEF) Q4 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Management outlines a bigger savings target and higher capital spending for a tough industrial market.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Greif, Inc. (GEF -5.60%) reported fiscal 2026 results and issued guidance for 2027 focused on structural cost reductions and capital allocation in a weak industrial demand environment. Management reported the achievement of its multiyear cost-saving targets one year ahead of schedule and established a new cumulative goal for the upcoming fiscal year. The company stated that its strategy involves divesting noncore assets, such as the Sweetwater mill and previous containerboard operations, to concentrate on high-return segments like polymers and closures. Capital allocation priorities for fiscal 2027 include increased organic growth investment, maintaining leverage below 2x, and continuing shareholder returns through dividends and share repurchases.
