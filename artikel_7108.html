Vultr is seeking a Principal Engineer, Network Software (Network Traffic & Modeling) to architect and build the platforms that tell us, and our customers, what is happening on the Vultr network. You will own the end-to-end architecture of our network modeling/source of truth (SOT) and traffic analytics platforms. You will enable revenue-generating features, such as traffic analytics and differentiated billing, as well as build distributed systems for key platform abilities. You will also build data-driven tooling for backbone, peering, and transit capacity planning and vendor engagement, and model network resilience against defined SLOs as we bring new sites online around the world. The ideal candidate is a senior software engineer who has built flow and telemetry pipelines at service provider scale and understands the network those pipelines describe. This is your opportunity to join our fast growing team and build the data foundation for how Vultr plans, sells, and protects its global network.

Own the holistic architecture of Vultr's traffic analytics and network modeling/SOT platforms, from data collection and enrichment through storage, APIs, and customer-facing products

Build destination-based billing: classify traffic by destination, path, and cost and deliver accurate, auditable usage data to billing systems

Integrate with existing platforms and APIs; build services for data enrichment, reconciliation, and automation on top of them

Lead buy vs. build analysis and overall architecture for foundational infrastructure such as flow collection/storage/presentation and network modeling

Build capacity planning tooling for backbone, peering, and transit: traffic matrix generation, demand forecasting, what-if and failure simulation, and upgrade timing recommendations

Build foundational infrastructure and tooling for measuring network performance and security, including synthetic/real user monitoring (RUM)

Develop network resilience models that test topology and capacity against defined SLOs, quantify risk of single points of failure, and guide design for new site builds

Produce data-driven analysis that supports carrier, IX, and transit vendor negotiations, such as cost per bit, traffic by ASN and region, and peering opportunities

Set engineering standards for your team: code quality, testing, observability, CI/CD, and operational readiness; mentor engineers and lead design reviews

Partner with Network Engineering, Platform, Operations, Security, Billing, and Product to turn requirements into reliable, scalable production services

10+ years of software engineering experience, including 5+ years building network data, observability, or automation platforms at a cloud provider, ISP, CDN, or hyperscaler

Expert in flow and telemetry data: NetFlow/IPFIX, sFlow, BMP, SNMP, streaming telemetry (gNMI/OpenConfig), and enrichment with BGP, RPKI, geo-IP, and customer metadata

Proven experience designing high-volume data pipelines and stores: Kafka or similar streaming systems, ClickHouse or other columnar/time-series databases, and stream processing frameworks

Strong programming skills in Go, Python, Rust, or similar; experience building REST/GraphQL APIs and multi-tenant services with strong data isolation

Experience with network SOT and automation tooling, such as NetBox/Nautobot, and integrating SaaS platforms like Kentik into production workflows

Deep knowledge of IP networking, including BGP, peering and transit economics, Segment Routing, MPLS, and Traffic Engineering; SLOs and SLAs

Experience with capacity planning and network modeling: traffic matrices, demand forecasting, failure simulation

Experience building usage metering or billing data pipelines where accuracy and auditability matter

Comfort with cloud-native infrastructure: Kubernetes, containers, infrastructure as code, CI/CD, and agentic coding tools

Familiarity with modeling data sources and tooling, including BGP-LS and BMP for real-time topology and routing state, graph databases such as Neo4j for modeling topology and dependencies, and network digital twin or simulation platforms

Familiarity with GIS development and building tools to enable network planning at layers 1 and up

Track record of strong cross-functional leadership, including mentorship and driving results with colleagues in adjacent organizations

Excellent written and verbal communication - able to produce crisp briefs for executives and detailed design docs for engineering teams
