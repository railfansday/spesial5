Bundesliga Fantasy Show: Matchday 10 draft team

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

With the international break bringing unlimited transfers to Bundesliga Fantasy, Bulinews.com editor-in-chief Rune Gjerulff and writer Ultan Corcoran sat down on Tuesday to put together a joint draft team ahead of Matchday 10.

The duo selected a 15-man squad costing just over 178 million, set up in a 3-4-3 formation. Their team looks as follows:

Nathaniel Brown (FC Bayern, 14.9M, 10%) ©

Ezechiel Banzuzi (RB Leipzig, 6.85M, 35%)

Niclas Füllkrug (Werder Bremen, 15.87M, 40%)

Patrik Schick (Bayer Leverkusen, 19.81M, 18%)

This is, of course, just one possible approach to building a Bundesliga Fantasy squad, with plenty of other combinations and player selections worth considering ahead of Matchday 10.

Rune and Ultan break down their team selections and highlight other promising options in Episode 10 of the Bulinews Bundesliga Fantasy podcast, available on Spotify and Apple Podcasts, as well as in the YouTube video below.
