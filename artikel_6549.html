Salted Caramel Eggnog Bars

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

If you love the warm, cozy flavors of eggnog during the holidays, then these salted caramel eggnog bars are the perfect dessert to make that’ll hit all of those notes. They have all the comforting flavors of a classic glass of eggnog (vanilla, nutmeg, cinnamon, and dark rum) & are transformed into a rich and creamy cheesecake bar with a buttery gingersnap crust. The salted caramel on top adds the perfect balance of sweetness and depth, making every bite feel indulgent and festive.

The bars:What I love most about these bars is how they capture the feeling of the holiday season in dessert form. The spiced gingersnap crust gives them warmth and texture, while the creamy filling stays light and smooth thanks to the sour cream and cream cheese combination. The salted caramel layer takes them over the top and gives them a bakery-style finish that feels impressive without requiring complicated techniques.

Make-ahead:These are the kind of bars you bring to a holiday gathering when you want people to ask for the recipe before they’ve even finished their first bite. They’re rich, cozy, and perfect for making ahead, which is always a bonus during the busy holiday hosting season.
