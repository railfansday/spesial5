iOS 27.3 gives Apple One’s high-end tier a compelling upgrade

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

iOS 27.3 is coming next month and is packed with new features, including some that make Apple One Premier more compelling than ever.

Apple One launched in 2019 with Individual, Family, and Premier plans. And in the seven years since, the service bundle has stayed remarkably consistent.

Every plan has always included Apple Music, Apple TV, Apple Arcade, and iCloud+. And the high-end Premier option adds Apple News+ and Fitness+ alongside more iCloud storage.

Apple News+ has gotten a lot better over the years. Following the New York Times playbook, Apple has added daily puzzle games, food-focused content like recipes, and audio stories, all while continuing to improve the basic news experience.

Apple Fitness+ keeps improving too, but there’s an upgrade in iOS 27.3 that makes it far more compelling in my view: Gym Strength.

Built-in video demonstrations accompany your personalized plan, making strength training accessible even for newcomers. And you can track your progress in the app, with key Apple Watch integration too.

I’ve used Fitness+ plenty and enjoyed it, but a comprehensive strength plan like this was a big gap in the service previously. With iOS 27.3 though, Fitness+ takes a big leap forward and makes Apple One Premier more compelling than ever.

Also relevant: with the new HomeView product and other Apple Home accessories almost here, Apple One Premier’s support for Apple Intelligence video summaries and clips with HomeKit Secure Video becomes more valuable. All of this together amounts to a stronger-than-ever Premier plan.

Apple One starts at $21.95/month and includes services like Apple TV, Apple Music, iCloud+, and more.
