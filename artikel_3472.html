‘John Wick’ Mastermind Unleashes Brutal Samurai Action in New Thriller Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

In the last few years, there have been dozens of action franchises coming out of nowhere from countless producers at different studios, but one franchise that stands above the rest is John Wick. The John Wick franchise launched all the way back in 2014 with a simple revenge tale starring Keanu Reeves, but flash forward all these years later, and it has several sequels and spin-offs spread across various platforms with more projects in the works coming soon. Reeves is the one who gets most of the credit for the John Wick franchise, and rightfully so, but writer/director/producer Chad Stahelski also deserves his fair share of the rewards. It's not an exaggeration to say that the John Wick franchise wouldn't be around today if not for Stahelski, who used his years of stunt expertise to bring to life one of the greatest action stories in history.

The John Wick franchise has produced plenty of copycats over the years as well, and while some have worked out better than others, there's a new John Wick-style action thriller coming soon that's worth being excited about. We're talking, of course, about Karoshi, the new action thriller coming to theaters on February 5, 2027. The movie features some widely recognizable stars such as Giancarlo Esposito (Breaking Bad) and Cynthia Erivo (the Wicked movies), but the most intriguing name is one who sits behind the camera, not in front of it. Karoshi is produced by Chad Stahelski, the same director behind the John Wick movies. The new actioner was written and directed by Takashi Doscher, and you can find the first gripping trailer full of brutal samurai action below.

After 12 years and five movies, the John Wick franchise is finally moving on from Keanu Reeves, at least for a little while, with its next movie, Caine. The film is directed by Donnie Yen, who also stars as Caine, the blind assassin who was first introduced in John Wick: Chapter 4. The movie does not yet have an official release date, but it's expected in theaters before the end of 2027. Keanu Reeves will also reprise his role as John Wick in a fifth mainline film, despite the ending of the fourth, but few details are known about the movie at this time.

Check out the new trailer for Karoshi and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of the future of the John Wick franchise.
