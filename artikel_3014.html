When the markets feel shaky, investors often look for ways to soften the blow of sudden losses. The search has helped fuel greater interest in "just-in-case" exchange-traded funds (ETFs). Specifically, investors look for funds designed to provide some defense when economic conditions, geopolitical activity, or inflation become harder to predict.
