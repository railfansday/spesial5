Huang Says Agentic AI Systems Need Monitoring, Containment

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang called the development of agentic AI systems without containment and monitoring the biggest risk he sees to the development of the technology. He spoke with Bloomberg’s Emily Chang on Oct. 14, just weeks before he revealed an extramarital affair with a woman the US reportedly suspects to be a Chinese spy. (Source: Bloomberg)
