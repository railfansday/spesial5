CAE reports second quarter fiscal 2027 results

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

MONTREAL, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- (Nasdaq: CAE) (TSX: CAE) - CAE Inc. (CAE or the Company) today reported its financial results for the fiscal second quarter ended September 30, 2026. For more information, please refer to the annex for the second quarter fiscal 2027 available at cae.com/investors.

"We delivered another solid quarter in Q2, with improved adjusted profitability, strong free cash flow and continued progress on our transformation priorities," said Matthew Bromberg, CAE's President and CEO. "Given our performance year-to-date and increased confidence in our ability to execute our second-half plan, we are raising our full-year fiscal 2027 outlook for adjusted EPS and adjusted segment operating income margin. We remain focused on advancing our transformation and building a higher-growth, higher-return CAE.

In Q2, Civil results were in line with our expectations, with strong business aviation performance and higher yearâoverâyear utilization across both commercial and business aviation training offset by a lower contribution from Civil products. Defense delivered another strong quarter, with double-digit year-over-year growth in adjusted segment operating income and continued underlying margin expansion.

We also made progress across each of our three transformation priorities: portfolio, capital discipline, and operational performance. In October, we announced an agreement to sell Flightscape to SITA for US$500 million. We expect the transaction to close in the first half of calendar year 2027 and intend to deploy the proceeds in accordance with our disciplined capital allocation priorities, including funding our transformation, supporting growth in our core businesses, and returning capital to shareholders. In Q2, we accelerated our share repurchase activity, bringing year-to-date repurchases through the second quarter to approximately 3.0 million shares for $106.1 million.

Overall, our second quarter performance included strong execution, Defense margin expansion, higher free cash flow, increased share repurchases and meaningful progress on our transformation. We are building a more disciplined CAE, with stronger margins and cash generation supporting long-term value creation. We look forward to providing further details on our strategy, transformation and growth opportunities at CAE's Analyst and Investor Day on February 25, 2027."

The Company is increasing its fiscal 2027 outlook for adjusted EPS and adjusted segment operating income margin.

Fiscal 2027 consolidated financial outlook

Adjusted segment operating income margin(1)

In fiscal 2027, management continues to expect consolidated revenue to increase by a low-single digit percentage, with Civil revenue expected to be flat to slightly down and Defense expected to grow at a mid-single digit rate.

Management now expects fiscal 2027 adjusted segment operating income margin of 14.9% to 15.4%, up from 14.6% to 15.1% previously, reflecting higher expected margins in both Civil and Defense and increased confidence in the Company's ability to execute its second-half plan. Adjusted EPS is now expected to be $1.28 to $1.35, up from $1.21 to $1.28 previously, reflecting the stronger operating outlook as well as lower expected finance expense and a lower effective tax rate.

In Defense, for the full year, CAE continues to expect higher year-over-year profitability, supported by bid discipline, structural cost reduction and strong program management. In Civil, for the full year, profitability is still expected to remain below prior-year levels, reflecting lower revenue, the near-term impact of network optimization actions and elevated investment levels intended to support stronger long-term performance.

The total cost of the transformation plan is anticipated to be approximately $200 million to $250 million, with approximately $100 million arising from non-cash charges. Of the total cost, $42.6 million was incurred in the second quarter of fiscal 2027, bringing the total costs incurred to date to $175.3 million. The majority of the remaining costs are expected to be incurred in fiscal 2027. We remain on track for our transformation targets, including $125 million to $150 million of annual transformation run-rate savings.

Ongoing geopolitical uncertainty in the Middle East continues to put pressure on the global aviation industry. The Company has undertaken mitigation actions, including redeploying certain training activities within its global network. To date, these actions have been effective at mitigating the direct operational impacts from the conflict. Our outlook assumes that these mitigation actions remain effective for the balance of fiscal 2027. However, an intensification of the conflict or a further deterioration in regional conditions, including sustained increases in fuel prices or disruptions to airline activity, customer operations, or supply chains, could result in additional pressure on performance.

Civil Aviation (Civil)Second quarter Civil revenue was $652.3 million compared to $670.0 million in the second quarter last year. Operating income was $61.3 million (9.4% of revenue) compared to $108.7 million (16.2% of revenue) in the same quarter last year. Adjusted segment operating income was $103.9 million (15.9% of revenue) compared to $124.1 million (18.5% of revenue) in the second quarter last year. The decrease in adjusted segment operating income was mainly due to a lower contribution from simulator sales, primarily due to a lower contribution from contracts accounted for based on progress toward completion, and lower contribution from the sale of simulator capacity structured as finance lease arrangements. The decrease was also due to higher selling, general and administrative expenses. The decrease was partially offset by a higher contribution from business training services, driven by higher utilization. During the quarter, Civil delivered 11 full-flight simulators (FFSs) and second quarter Civil training centre utilization was 65.0%.

During the quarter, Civil signed training solutions contracts valued at $580.6 million for a range of long-term commercial and business aviation training agreements, including 7 FFS sales.

The Civil book-to-sales ratio(1) was 0.89 times for the quarter and 1.07 times for the last 12 months. The Civil adjusted backlog at the end of the quarter was $8.3 billion.

Defense and Security (Defense)Second quarter Defense revenue was $552.4 million compared to $566.6 million in the second quarter last year. Operating income was $53.1 million (9.6% of revenue) compared to $46.6 million (8.2% of revenue) in the same quarter last year. Adjusted segment operating income was $75.4 million (13.6% of revenue), compared to $52.6 million (9.3% of revenue) in the second quarter last year. The increase in adjusted segment operating income was mainly due to higher profitability from products recognized upon delivery and increased profitability on our contracts in North America. The increase was also due to the recognition of $11 million in investment tax credits identified and claimed in the current period in respect of prior years.

Defense booked orders for $586.0 million this quarter for a book-to-sales ratio of 1.06 times. The ratio for the last 12 months was 1.03 times. The Defense adjusted backlog, including unfunded contract awards and CAE's interest in joint ventures, at the end of the quarter was $11.3 billion.
