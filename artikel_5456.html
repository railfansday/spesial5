CITGO Names Erik Uitenbogaart Vice President Finance and Chief Financial Officer

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

HOUSTON, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- CITGO Petroleum Corporation today confirmed Erik Uitenbogaart as its new Vice President Finance and Chief Financial Officer, effective Nov. 16, 2026. Uitenbogaart brings nearly three decades of global finance, strategy, business leadership and governance experience across the energy, petrochemical and chemical industries.

"Erik combines financial discipline with strategic vision, and he has a proven record of driving profitable growth and long-term value across the energy and chemical industries," said Carlos JordÃ¡, President and Chief Executive Officer of CITGO Petroleum Corporation. "His experience in strategy, capital allocation and governance will be a tremendous asset as we continue to invest in our business and strengthen our financial position. We are pleased to welcome him to CITGO."

Uitenbogaart joins CITGO from LyondellBasell, where he held roles of increasing responsibility since 2013, most recently as Vice President Finance, Strategy & M&A for Sustainable Solutions, and previously led the company's global Financial Planning & Analysis organization of 150 finance professionals. Prior to that, he was Controller EMEA at Univar Solutions and spent more than 15 years with PricewaterhouseCoopers.

Uitenbogaart holds a Master of Science in Auditing & Accounting and a Post-Master of Auditing degree (Dutch Chartered Accountant/CPA) from Nyenrode Business University.

CITGO Petroleum Corporation is a recognized leader in the refining industry and operates under the well-known CITGO brand. CITGO owns and operates three refineries located in Lake Charles, La.; Lemont, Ill.; and Corpus Christi, Texas, and wholly and/or jointly owns 43 active terminals, eight pipelines and three lubricants blending and packaging plants. With approximately 3,300 employees and a combined crude capacity of approximately 829,000 barrels per day (bpd), CITGO ranks as the fifth largest and is one of the most complex independent refiners in the United States. CITGO transports and markets transportation fuels, lubricants, petrochemicals and other industrial products, and supplies a network of approximately 4,000 locally owned and independently operated branded retail outlets. CITGO Petroleum Corporation is owned by CITGO Holding, Inc.
