Chase launches Cyber Monday rate sale for purchase, refi loans

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Chase Home Lending is offering a limited-time mortgage rate discount of up to 0.25% for eligible purchase and refinance loans. Borrowers must lock a rate from Nov. 30 through Dec. 13, and discounts can pair with select programs.  AI Summary

Chase Home Lending is offering a limited-time mortgage rate discount for eligible home purchase and refinance loans as borrowers continue to search for affordability.

The promotion runs from Nov. 30 through Dec. 13. Qualified borrowers could receive a rate discount of up to 0.25%, depending on their location and loan type.

Existing and new Chase customers must qualify for a purchase or refinance loan and lock in their rate during the promotional period. The company said there are no additional criteria to qualify for the sale.

“As millions of holiday shoppers look for the best online deals, the biggest purchase most people will ever make deserves the same kind of careful shopping,” Erik Schmitt, head of consumer direct sales at Chase Home Lending, said in a statement.

The rate discount can be combined with other available Chase Home Lending programs and discounts, subject to their terms and eligibility requirements. These include the Chase Homebuyer Grant and the bank’s Relationship Pricing Program, which recently expanded eligibility to more than 7 million small-business customers.

Maximum discounts and other limitations may apply depending on how the rate sale is combined with other programs, the company clarified.

November’s rate sale is not Chase’s first promotion this year.

In September, the lender offered qualified borrowers up to 0.25 percentage points off mortgage rates on purchase and refinance loans. The company also ran promotions in May, covering purchase and refinance loans, and in March, when it offered discounted rates on new purchase and refi loans.

In August 2025, Chase offered temporary discounts on purchase mortgages.
