Jude Bellingham and Real Madrid in advanced talks over new five-year deal - with England superstar set to become one of Europe's HIGHEST earners on par with £500,000-a-week team-mate Vinicius Jr

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Published: 17:22 EST, 23 November 2026 | Updated: 18:40 EST, 23 November 2026

Jude Bellingham and Real Madrid are advancing in talks over a new contract that will make him one of the best paid players in Europe.

Madrid have been eager to tie down the in-form England midfielder beyond his current contract, understood to be worth around £12.5million a year after tax, which runs until 2029, in recognition of his performances.

Talks were initiated in September over an extension until 2031 and have been progressing well since with sources at Madrid confident they are close to reaching a conclusion.

Bellingham is expected to be handed improved terms to put him on par with team-mate Vinicius Jr, who agreed a new deal last summer reputedly on around £500,000 a week after tax.

Kylian Mbappe remains the best paid player at the club while talks are also advancing over a new contract for Fede Valverde. Real have also recently renewed the contracts of Turkish winger Arda Guler and France international Aurelien Tchouameni on deals until 2032.

The contract for Bellingham will be just reward for the 23-year-old who has flourished under coach Jose Mourinho who recently described the former Birmingham City academy graduate as ‘one of a kind’.

1. Erling Haaland, Manchester City: £510,000 per week

2. Kylian Mbappe, Real Madrid: £503,000 per week

3. Vinicius Jr, Real Madrid: £500,000 per week

4. Harry Kane, Bayern Munich: £402,000 per week

5. Virgil van Dijk, Liverpool: £350,000 per week

Bellingham, who has scored six goals this season, could be rested for Madrid’s Champions League tie with PSV Eindhoven tonight but was still the topic of conversation as goalkeeper Thibaut Courtois praised his influence on the team on Monday.

‘Jude is someone who really wants to win,’ said Courtois. ‘That's why on the field he swears a lot! He's really a winner, and when he's not happy, he will make it known. He's also a leader on the field. He's a great player, a great mentality. You need those kind of people like that in your team that go for everything.

‘Jude is a young guy but when you see him, it feels like he's a few years older.

‘It seems like he has an engine to play 200 minutes. He keeps on going, running with the ball, it's really hard to dispossess him. And on the outside he just works hard, he goes to the gym. With a good Jude, we play better.’
