Today's Champions League predictions include Real Madrid's clash with AEK Athens, and a contest between Liverpool and Fenerbahce.

Real Madrid are flying at the moment, and we are fully expecting Los Blancos to secure all three points against an AEK Athens team currently struggling for form.
