Report: Liverpool offered massive boost ahead of January window

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Liverpool have been extremely unfortunate with several injuries over the last 18 months.

Alexander Isak missed the majority of his first season in Merseyside after suffering a leg break whilst scoring, and both Hugo Ekitike and Giovanni Leoni remain sidelined by injuries they sustained last season.

One of the biggest losses for Liverpool in that time has been Conor Bradley, who suffered a devastating injury against Arsenal in January.

The right-back is expected to regain his status as Liverpool's first-choice right-back when he is fully fit, but his return has taken longer than expected and he is yet to return to training.

Few expected the 23-year-old to be out for so long, but it now appears he could face close to a full calendar year on the sideline, with a return targeted for January.

That's according to a new report from Football Insider, which claims that Bradley is edging closer to a full recovery and could return to action in the New year.

Right-back has been a massive issue for the Reds in Bradley's absence, with Dominik Szoboszlai and Curtis Jones often filling in last season and Ronald Araujo now the de facto first-choice.

Although the Uruguayan has done an admirable job filling in, his natural position is as a central defender and Bradley's return will take the pressure off Andoni Iraola's defence.

It is a cliché, but the 23-year-old's return would be like a new signing for the Reds, and having an end in sight to the fullback's injury is a massive boost.
