The 10 greatest games to never get a GOTY nomination

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Game Awards announced its 2026 nominations yesterday, though not without controversy. Grand Theft Auto 6 — a game that isn't even out yet — leads the pack with eight nominations, including Game of the Year. No indies managed to get a GOTY nomination this year, despite the release of acclaimed games like Mina the Hollower and Big Walk. Several blockbusters — including Polygon favorites like Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave and Pokémon Pokopia — were also snubbed for the night's biggest award.

While there's a lot to be said about 2026's Game Awards snubs, some of the greatest games of all time never received a GOTY nomination. While the games below may have been nominated (or won) in other Game Awards categories, they never got their fair shot at Game of the Year honors.

Where to play: PlayStation 4, Windows PC, Xbox One

Dark Souls 3 was a gorgeous, somber conclusion to FromSoftware's groundbreaking Dark Souls trilogy. By speeding up the pace of combat, Dark Souls 3 bridged Bloodborne and Elden Ring, streamlining an already iconic gameplay loop. To close out its trilogy, FromSoft proved it had complete mastery over the gameplay style it innovated years earlier.

Despite widespread acclaim, Dark Souls 3 was not nominated for Game of the Year in 2016, only getting a nod for Best RPG. Fans should take comfort in the fact that 2016 was an absolutely stacked year, though, with Overwatch, Doom, and Inside all competing for top honors. There's also the fact that very difficult games face an uphill battle in the GOTY race.

Where to play: Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, Windows PC, Xbox One

Outer Wilds is hard to categorize. At once an open-world explorer, an action adventure title, and a puzzle game, it dropped a traditional combat focus in favor of rewarding curiosity and knowledge. Couple that with a tightly written story, and you have one of the most immersive, combat-free gaming experiences of the decade.

Outer Wilds faced even stiffer competition than Dark Souls 3. 2019 was a competitive year, with Control, Death Stranding, Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice, Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, The Outer Worlds, and the Resident Evil 2 remake fighting for top honors. Several other great games, like Disco Elysium, Fire Emblem: Three Houses, and Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order also failed to secure nominations. So, for an indie game — which tend to build popularity via word of mouth — Outer Wilds was always a deserving but unlikely nomination.

Where to play: Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, Windows PC, Xbox One

NieR:Automata this relatively obscure spin-off of Square Enix's Drakengard series to mainstream audiences. Its intricate, existential story and seamless transitions between bullet-hell, hack-and-slash, and side-scrolling gameplay left a mark on millions of gamers worldwide.

Automata lost the nomination race to games like Breath of the Wild, Super Mario Odyssey, and Horizon Zero Dawn — games with enormous mainstream appeal. Though Automata was a breakout critical and commercial success for its franchise, it still came from a relatively niche series. What's more, the game's heavy philosophical themes and dozens of endings may have been a bit opaque for voters.

Ghost of Yotei, the highly anticipated follow-up to 2020 GOTY nominee Ghost of Tsushima, is about as cinematic as a game can get. With influence from filmmakers like Takashi Miike (13 Assassins) and Shinichiro Watanabe (Cowboy Bebop), Ghost of Yotei even has a game mode inspired by the work of Seven Samurai director Akira Kurosawa. And, it shows off its stunning graphics and cinematic influences without compromising on fluid, intense gameplay.

Ghost of Yotei earned seven Game Awards nominations in 2025, including Best Direction, Narrative, Audio Design, Action/Adventure Game, and more. So many people were confused about how a game that, apparently had some of the best direction, design, action gameplay and storytelling of the year could miss out on a GOTY nomination. Polygon's Oli Welsh even said "you could have grown it in a lab to win Game of the Year." Unfortunately, Ghost of Yotei paid a sequel tax — critics thought it didn't distinguish itself from Ghost of Tsushima quite enough.

Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze deserved better. It's an absolute masterclass in platformer design, with carefully themed levels and gimmicks that feel purposeful. Developers mastered a sense of momentum, getting players to chain satisfying rolls, jumps, and precise landings. Also, everything is set to an absolutely classic soundtrack by composer David Wise.

Tropical Freeze came out in 2014, the first year of the Game Awards, alongside titles like Bayonetta 2, Dark Souls 2, and Dragon Age: Inquisition. So, it didn't quite fit in with the kind of vibe the early Game Awards honored. Also, Tropical Freeze was initially a Wii U game. While a Nintendo Switch port has brought the game to platformer fans nationwide, the commercial failures of the Wii U hindered its success.

Where to play: Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, Windows PC, Xbox One

Considered one of the best games of the 2010s, Hollow Knight dropped players into a vast, rich world with minimal guidance. Since 2017, millions of gamers have uncovered Hallownest's secrets, pieced together its tragic history, and died a lot. (It's a hard game!)

Hollow Knight didn't make a ton of headlines when it first came out. Like many other indie titles, it spread slowly via word of mouth. So, it lacked the kind of blockbuster momentum that can propel a game to a GOTY nod. Hollow Knight received an indirect nod last year through the nomination of its sequel Hollow Knight: Silksong, which lost out to Clair Obscur: Expedition 33.

Where to play: PlayStation 4, Windows PC, Xbox One

Konami and Bloober Team's Silent Hill 2 revisits one of the greatest psychological horror games of all time. It faithfully recreated the suffocating atmosphere and emotional weight of the 2001 original, while upgrading its gameplay to modern survival horror standards. Stronger performances and updated graphics made the terror and tragedy of Silent Hill 2 accessible to a new generation of gamers.

But horror games can be a tough sell at The Game Awards. Though high-profile blockbusters like Resident Evil 2 have been nominated, the only horror-adjacent game to bring home GOTY was The Last of Us Part II in 2020. Silent Hill 2 was also a remake; remakes are rarely nominated and have never won GOTY.

Fire Emblem: Three Houses was the best-selling mainline Fire Emblem game of all time, catapulting the series into Nintendo flagship status. Its iconic route split drew players into an intricate tale of war, love, and betrayal. It also sparked years upon years of discourse and fan arguments, which continue to this day. That ought to count for something.

Like Outer Wilds, Three Houses faced stiff competition amid a stacked 2019 gaming lineup. Also, Three Houses was a bit rough around the edges, particularly graphically. Couple that with a monastery hub that can feel repetitive (and the fact that you need to play the game four times to get the full story), and it's easy to understand why Three Houses failed to secure a GOTY nomination. That doesn't make it hurt less, though.

Where to play: Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Windows PC, Xbox One

Yakuza 0 took on a lot. It features two protagonists, a high-stakes crime drama, over-the-top combat, and a quirky sense of humor, all without losing the series' integral "cool factor." It succeeded with flying colors, telling an emotional story about two iconic characters while leaving room for an all-important disco mini game.

When it was released overseas in 2016, Yakuza 0 was hampered by what Parasite director Bong Joon Ho called "the one-inch tall barrier of subtitles." Without an English dub upon release (it would eventually get one via its 2025 Director's Cut release), Yakuza 0's viral popularity was a slow burn with Western audiences. Once it launched on Steam in 2018, Yakuza 0 nearly doubled its past sales, and is now the best-selling game in the series. It may be the game that inspired a thousand memes — and took the Yakuza series into the mainstream — but it wasn't an overnight blockbuster.

Where to play: Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox Series X

Of course, this list would not be complete without Mina the Hollower, one of the most baffling GOTY nomination snubs of this year. Its intricately designed levels took an insane amount of effort, rivaling any AAA game out right now. That effort resulted in one of the year's best games, a loving homage to early Zelda games, the Game Boy Color, and Gothic horror.

While it received a nod for best indie game, Mina the Hollower was left off an indie-free GOTY nomination list. Though it was, critically speaking, one of the best received games of the year, it looks as though The Game Awards voters focused on mainstream blockbusters this year. It's also very, very hard, which may have dulled its mass appeal.
