Influencer in tears as burglars steal “absolutely everything” days after winning 4-way boxing match

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Colombian influencer Yaya Muñoz broke down in tears after discovering burglars had ransacked her home, stealing her jewelry and savings just days after she won a four-way influencer boxing match.

The 33-year-old content creator, whose real name is Dahian Lorena Muñoz, is best known for appearing on Colombia’s version of Celebrity Big Brother, La Casa de los Famosos.

On November 22, Muñoz shared a series of emotional Instagram Stories showing the aftermath of a burglary at her home in Medellín, Colombia.

“How is it possible that I come home, where there is supposed to be so much security, and it turns out I’ve been robbed?” she questioned, according to El Tiempo.

“They took absolutely everything,” she added.

In the footage, Muñoz showed empty jewelry boxes and revealed that the thieves had broken into her safe, stealing cash she had been saving.

“I can’t believe this. They took absolutely all my jewelry. My God, how can people be so evil?” she said through tears.

Muñoz also showed police around her home, suggesting the burglars may have entered through a window after noticing that a curtain had been moved while the door remained locked.

She later confirmed that authorities were investigating, adding that it still hurt to lose possessions she had worked so hard to acquire.

The burglary comes just over a week after Muñoz emerged victorious at Stream Fighters 5, a major influencer boxing event organized by popular Kick streamer WestCOL on November 14.

Held at Bogotá’s Coliseo MedPlus, the event featured an unusual four-person Royal Rumble-style boxing match involving Muñoz, Karola Alcendra, Beba de la Cruz, and Alexa Torrex.

Rather than having all four women fight simultaneously, the contest featured a series of rotating one-on-one rounds, with the winner ultimately determined by the judges.

The fight produced a viral moment when Beba appeared to dislocate her shoulder and attempted to put it back into place in the ring before eventually withdrawing.

Muñoz also landed a powerful punch on Karola that prompted a referee’s count before going on to defeat Alexa Torrex in the final by unanimous decision.

Following her victory, Muñoz embraced Karola, putting an end to a feud between the two influencers that had lasted for months.

The event was the latest installment of WestCOL’s influencer boxing series, which has previously broken Kick viewership records.

Meanwhile, the investigation into Muñoz’s burglary remains ongoing. The influencer has not disclosed the total value of the stolen belongings, and no suspects have been publicly identified.
