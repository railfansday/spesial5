GTA 6 on PS5 Is PS Store's Most Pre-Ordered Digital Game in History

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

It was always going to be big, but with a week until release, Rockstar has now confirmed that GTA 6 is the most pre-ordered digital game in PS Store history.

Confirmation comes courtesy of the PS Blog, in a post from Sony’s Tim Turi.

“GTA 6 is now the most pre-ordered game in the history of PS Store. Given this unprecedented demand, we’ve joined with Rockstar Games to allow players to start pre-loading the game nine days early, so they’re ready to play on launch day. We’ve also adopted a rolling midnight launch by country, beginning at 12:00AM NZDT in New Zealand and running across the globe as respective countries hit midnight on 19th November.”

You can learn more about when you’ll be able to play in our dedicated GTA 6 release times guide, so keep that bookmarked for 19th November.

Digital pre-orders for GTA 6 have been available since 25th June, so Rockstar has had a long time to build up numbers.

It’s worth reiterating that physical versions are following a “code-in-box” format, so there’s less incentive to purchase at retail, too.

(Although in a recent Push Square poll, a remarkable 23% of our readers did say they’d be plumping up for the boxed version.)

Record-breaking pre-orders like this are significant as the PS Store has been selling digital games for over a decade now; that it’s set a new record with over a week to spare and hype still building is remarkable.

While we don’t have actual numbers, Rockstar’s said they’re “exceeding” its external expectations.

Analyst firm Sensor Tower predicts 9.69 million pre-orders have been placed, with an astonishing 75% of those sales commanded by the PS5 version.

It’s worth noting that this milestone comes at a time of deep unrest among PS5 players, who’ve continuously spammed the platform holder with proclamations of ‘No Disc, No Buy’ in the aftermath of its decision to stop shipping physical games in 2028.

Sony recently reported year-over-year increases to its monthly active users, during a period in which enthusiasts mobilised a “blackout” of its platform.

Irrespective of fan frustration, it looks like Rockstar’s latest open world is days away from registering the biggest digital launch in industry history.
