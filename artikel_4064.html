Persona 6 Gets A New Domain Update, Prompting Game Awards Rumors

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Ever since Persona 6 was first revealed back in June, fans have been eagerly awaiting any details about the highly anticipated sequel. While Persona hasn't exactly been short on spinoffs and remakes in the 9 years since the release of Persona 5, it's still a far cry from the full-blown original entry that fans have been dreaming of for years now.

Following a disappointing series of teases and trailer reposts, however, a recent discovery by one dedicated fan has been making waves in the community for possibly hinting at a brand-new trailer reveal sometime in the next month.

First discovered by Reddit user ronakwya, who has been tracking Atlus' public domain certifications, the studio recently updated their domain for none other than Persona 6 just a few days ago.

While this doesn't really mean much on its own, the post details how not only do the domain certifications used by Atlus let them "build a massive website makeover behind closed doors before making it live," but that the exact same thing happened to the newly inspired Persona 4 Revival one month before its first major gameplay trailer dropped.

Persona 6 looks like it might be bringing back the spooky vibes, but not everyone is trusting Atlus just yet.

Combined with the fact that the 2026 Game Awards are just about a month away at this point, the newest update is offering even more support that Persona 6 will not only appear at this year's showcase, but that it could even be the first look at a new mainline Persona game in nearly a decade.

Although The Game Awards is filled with some tough competition for world premieres and extended looks at upcoming games, it's hard to imagine many titles that could beat out Persona 6 in terms of sheer player popularity, with the event having the perfect audience size for such a massive and highly anticipated gameplay reveal.

Given that Persona 6's first teaser also came not long after its protagonist and cast were supposedly leaked, a new gameplay trailer wouldn't be too unexpected as a way to distract fans from the inadvertently confirmed Persona 1 remake that dropped at the start of November alongside the new anniversary merchandise.
