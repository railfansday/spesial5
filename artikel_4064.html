ICYMI: Limited Run Games Announces Restock Of Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition For Switch 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Physical game distributor Limited Run Games is all about "limited" releases, but it's making an exception for Lara Croft.

Following the recent release of the Tomb Raider 'Survivor' trilogy on the Switch 2, it's now announced it will be offering a second batch of Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition physical releases. So, if you missed out on this game the first time it was made available for Nintendo's new system, it's available once again.

This follows a Limited Run poll, where 90% of respondents voted for the physical distributor to "make more" copies of this game. The other entries in the Survivor trilogy are also available on Limited Run's website.

This lines up with the series' 30th anniversary celebrations and the recent shadow drop of Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition on the Switch 2 eShop. You can find out more in our previous coverage here on Nintendo Life:
