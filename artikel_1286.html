Gazzetta: Club Brugge set Tresoldi price tag for Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, Club Brugge will consider selling Milan target Nicolò Tresoldi in the January window, but only for €60m.

The U21 Germany international has already drawn plenty of attention in Italy after his recent hat-trick in a Champions League match against Inter, and because of his dual citizenship, which makes him eligible for both the Italy and Germany national teams.

It is no secret that Milan have been tracking him for several months, and the 22-year-old would gladly move to San Siro, as the Rossoneri are his favourite club.

“Milan is a dream that pushes me to work every day. I’m really happy here at Club Brugge, but I know I have a long way ahead of me,” Tresoldi said in an interview this past February.

“The dream of playing for Milan is inside my head, and I think about it every morning when I wake up.”

Gazzetta reports that Club Brugge are ready to listen to offers in the January window, but warns they have no need to sell the player, especially mid-season.

Therefore, the minimum asking price for their striker is €60m. Tresoldi has scored 14 goals in 17 appearances across all competitions this season, and his contract with Club Brugge runs out on June 30, 2029.

Talks between the two clubs have not started yet, but the Rossoneri remain keen on the Cagliari-born striker, who has reportedly attracted interest from other clubs as well, especially in Germany and in the English Premier League.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.

Deducting the inflated base amount (€10m pre-2020; €20m post-2020) suggests the player costs €40m on the open market.

Considering that he’s just 21 years old, and has a possible full decade of injury-free football ahead of him, it seems like a reasonable deal.
