Gifts for Teens That’ll Make Them Say, “Wait, This Is Actually Cute”

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Stop scrolling TikTok for inspiration. These gift ideas, from beauty to fashion and more, will impress even the pickiest teen this holiday season.

Back in the day, we used a pen and paper to write down our holiday gift lists or circled items in highly coveted retailer magazines. These days, teens make full-blown PowerPoint presentations for their parents.

If you're wondering what's on these presentations, we're here to provide a little insight. Whether you're a parent, aunt or uncle, big brother or sister, we rounded up trending teen gifts that will impress even the pickiest people on your list (even if they don't show it).

There's one thing we guarantee will show up on any teen's holiday wishlist and that's '90s or Y2K-inspired fashion, beauty, and technology. Think digital cameras, low-rise jeans, and wristlets. You also can never go wrong with beauty gift sets (bonus points if they include mini versions of viral products) or comfy cozy sweats.

Stop spending hours scrolling on TikTok for inspo. Shop these gifts instead that will have them saying, "wait, that's actually cute" (and not at all cringe).

You can never go wrong with Summer Fridays lip butters, especially this adorable 4-piece mini set. Their petite size is perfect for throwing in a bag to hydrate dry, chapped lips on the go.

Teens are embracing analog-inspired technology and this screen-free digital camera. The point-and-shoot pick features a built-in memory card, 6 filters, and rechargeable, but the real standout is its colorful transparent design.

From travel to sleepovers and errands, teens will wear a sweat set just about anywhere, so this one from LDMA is sure to be a hit. Both the hoodie and matching straight leg pants are made from soft cotton terry with a comfy, slightly oversized fit.

These Emi Jay brushes are so gorgeous, they'll double as vanity decor. The mini boar bristle paddle brush can be customized and personalized for a truly special gift.

This portable printer uses ink-free technology to print out photos wherever you are. It connects via Bluetooth so you won't have to fumble with a bunch of cords and you can customize images using the app.

Looking for something viral that actually works? WONDERSKIN is a perfect option. This set comes with four fan-favorite shades of the brand's bestselling longwear lip liner stains plus a clear gloss for a shiny finish.

The younger generation has fully embraced Y2K denim styles, including low-rise fits (sorry, millennials). This dark wash pair boasts a looser, baggy fit that's also trending.

These TikTok-loved e.l.f. lip stains are way more affordable than higher-end picks but definitely just as good. Buy them in a convenient customizable set for $12.

This sweet pointelle long-sleeve top is crafted from 100% cozy cotton and features a scalloped neckline. Boasting a snug fit, the versatile pick can be worn both in and out of the house.

Charlotte Tilbury's cult-favorite soft-focus powder gives skin that airbrushed, camera-ready finish. The weightless powder helps reduce shine, blur imperfections, and seamlessly set makeup.

Your new favorite perfume might be hiding in this set, which includes five sample-sized versions of DIME Beauty's bestselling scents. It features everything from warm gourmands to soft florals.

The perfect purse alternative when you want to travel light. The wristlet comes with two zippered (and detachable) pouches to store keys, credit cards, DIME Beauty scent samples, and mini Summer Fridays lip butters.

Salt & Stone’s aluminum-free deodorant is beloved for its long-lasting odor protection and signature scents that feel far more elevated than your standard drugstore stick. Formulated with niacinamide, seaweed extracts, and prebiotics, this set of minis includes three equally yummy scents.

From SKIMS and KHY to Cécred and BÉIS, these are the celeb brands we’re adding to cart during Black Friday.
