Don Toliver Delays London Gig After Manchester Set Evacuated for ‘Unknown Substance’: ‘Stop Bringing Bulls–t’ Into My Shows!’

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

His Saturday Manchester set was evacuated mid-show, with attendees treated by medical staff.

Don Toliver has postponed a show in London on Monday (Nov. 23) after his Manchester concert was disrupted over the weekend following the release of an “unknown substance” in the crowd.

“I’m very sad to sit here and say I have to reschedule The O2 London show to December 15th due to unfortunate incident last night Manchester [sic]” he wrote on his Instagram Stories Monday.

“I just wanna make sure everyone is safe and the next show goes on properly. My heart and prayers are with with [sic] everyone that was injured last night @ co-op live. The only thing I love more than creating music is performing for my fans ! STOP BRINGING BULLS–T INTO MY SHOWS!! it ain’t cool see you soon London. Love you guys. DT.”

The Houston-born rapper was performing at Manchester’s Co-op Live on Sunday (Nov. 22) when roughly 40 minutes into the set, the incident took place. Greater Manchester Police believe the substance to be tear gas, per the BBC.

More 12,000 people were evacuated from the venue and a major incident was declared. The North West Ambulance service said 80 people were treated for “eye and respiratory symptoms,” with one person admitted to hospital.

In a statement to Billboard U.K., the GMP said, “Officers first were alerted by venue staff shortly after 10 p.m. and attended alongside other emergency services on a priority response. Members of the public began to be evacuated when safe to do so, to ensure those that were suffering the effects of this incident could be assessed by medical professionals.”

It added, “Anyone at tonight’s event who has since noticed side effects or become ill should attend their local hospital. Enquiries are underway into what the substance is and what caused the incident.”

Toliver’s next show is due to take place at London’s OVO Arena Wembley on Tuesday (Nov. 24).
