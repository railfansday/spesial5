What We Missed: Feds Lasso Texas Laptop Farms

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Cybersecurity In-Depth: Feature articles on security strategy, latest trends, and people to know.

In this video, Dark Reading editors discuss some of the news they didn't get a chance to cover, from busting up North Korea's stateside infrastructure in "Operation Lonestar Justice" to Staples' gift card bonanza.

The US government is striking back against the ever-persistent threat of fake IT worker schemes run by the Democratic People's Republic of Korea (DPRK).

In this episode of "What We Missed," Dark Reading's Rob Wright and Alex Culafi discuss some of the recent news events and topics that didn't make it into the publication, starting with FBI raids across more than 40 Texas residences that were allegedly running "laptop farms."

The law-enforcement action, dubbed “Operation Lonestar Roundup,” seized 187 devices, charged 14 people, and arrested individuals accused of facilitating North Korean threat actors running fake IT worker schemes. The schemes disrupted by Operation Lonestar Roundup impacted more than 70 US companies, according to the FBI.

Fake IT worker schemes typically involve DPRK actors masquerading as IT professionals and developers, either using fake or stolen identities, to gain employment at US companies. The threat actors then use their positions to exfiltrate sensitive data, steal intellectual property, and gather intelligence.
