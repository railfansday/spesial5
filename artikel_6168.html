Update: United Resolves System Outage

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

United said it has resolved a system outage that delayed flights across its network late Monday afternoon.

“We have resolved a technology issue that delayed flight departures,” the airline said in a statement. “While some flights were delayed, there was no impact on flights in progress, or on United Express flights. There is no indication that this was a cybersecurity-related issue.”

Between 4 p.m. and 6 p.m. Eastern time, crews at multiple airports reported they could not get the final weight-and-balance figures required for departure.

United acknowledged a “widespread system outage” in a post on X. In a separate statement, the carrier said it was “troubleshooting a technology issue affecting departing flights.”

The first report identified in radio audio captured by the ATC App came at about 4:02 p.m. ET, when the crew of United flight 2787 in Denver reported trouble getting its final weights. At Chicago O’Hare, the system used to request pushbacks also went down. At Newark, a ramp controller said the outage affected how ramp crews received gate changes.

“We are having an extreme system-wide outage… which is preventing us from closing out flights, which is affecting pretty much all of our mainline departures at this point,” an O’Hare ramp controller said.

Radio reports named at least 30 United flights across 12 airports. Some crews at O’Hare and Los Angeles reported receiving final weights between 4:40 and 5:09 p.m. ET. At 5:11 p.m., an O’Hare ramp controller canceled a United flight’s pushback after it had waited about 20 minutes.
