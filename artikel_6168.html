From bonus points on your next hotel stay to sales on award tickets, we're always looking for travel deals that help you earn more points and miles and redeem them for maximum value. In other words, we want to help you book the vacation of your dreams.

This time around, we've got Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Travel Tuesday deals galore. From hotels and all-inclusive resorts to cruises, now is the time to save on your next big trip.

We've also rounded up our top tips to help you save on your Thanksgiving trip to the airport. Plus, a popular luggage brand is running its biggest sale of the year right now.

Each week, we help our readers by rounding up promotions so they don't have to scour the internet for the best reward redemptions and offers. This includes everything from frequent flyer programs and hotel rewards programs to credit card offers and rental car deals. Here's our latest list of noteworthy new points and miles promotions for early November.

Airline loyalty programs can offer trip discounts on specific dates, the opportunity to earn more points or miles for future travel and exclusive deals to accumulate points or miles toward elite status. Below are some of the best offers we found for November.

If you value United MileagePlus miles, you're hopefully already earning miles on your Lyft rides thanks to the partnership between the two programs — and you can earn even more right now.

With this promotion, riders who set United miles as their earning preference in their Lyft account can earn 8 miles per dollar on rides to and from airports in the U.S. through Nov. 30. That's double what you would normally earn.

Plus, new riders can snag an additional 1,000 bonus miles after linking their accounts and taking two eligible rides in the first 30 days.

If Delta SkyMiles is your frequent flyer program of choice, you won't want to miss this offer — especially if you have an eligible Delta Amex card with a Delta Stays credit that you still need to use this year.

Until Dec. 15, members can earn 5 miles per dollar (five times the usual miles) on hotels booked through the Delta Stays platform. You have until Sept. 30, 2027, to complete travel.

Dreaming of a beach getaway this winter? Atmos Rewards is awarding double points on Alaska Airlines and Hawaiian Airlines flights to and from Hawaii.

Register and book by Dec. 31 for travel between December 1-17, 2026 or Jan. 5-March 10, 2027.

Related: How to pick the best Atmos Rewards accrual method for you

Hotel loyalty programs regularly offer discounted award stays and new ways to earn extra points. Here are some November deals to consider when booking your next stay.

Homes & Villas by Marriott Bonvoy, the hotel giant's vacation rental collection, is offering a whopping 27,000 bonus points for a single stay of two or more nights. Your stay must cost a minimum of $1,500 to qualify.

Register and book by Jan. 3, 2027, and stay between Nov. 23, 2026, and Dec. 31, 2027.

If you decide to jump on this deal, be sure to check your Amex Offers first. We've seen an offer for $350 back on a Homes & Villas purchase of $1,000-plus by Dec. 31, which you could stack with this deal to save money.

That's not all Marriott Bonvoy has in store this holiday season. Members who have yet to link their Marriott and Uber accounts can also earn an easy 2,000 bonus points: 1,000 points for linking your accounts, plus another 1,000 for taking your first qualifying ride.

Act fast, because this offer ends Nov. 24.

Related: From Starbucks to Uber: Airline and hotel partnerships that earn points and miles

Sonesta Travel Pass may fly under the radar in the points and miles world, but this deal is worth a look.

The hotel loyalty program is offering an extra 5,000 points per stay, up to a maximum of 30,000 bonus points. Plus, you can save up to 25% off the best available rate, or up to 30% when you pre-pay.

Book with code JOYFULSTAY by Dec. 15, 2026, for travel through June 30, 2027.

Earn a $60 statement credit after spending at least $300 on flights or Getaways by Southwest, in one or more transactions, by Dec. 31.

Need a vehicle for an upcoming trip? Check your Amex account for these offers.

You could be eligible for 10% cash back, up to $50, when you spend $100 or more in the Hyatt Essentials portfolio by Nov. 23. That includes Hyatt Place, Hyatt House, Hyatt Studios, Hyatt Select, Caption by Hyatt and Unscripted by Hyatt brands.
