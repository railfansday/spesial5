SHoP Architects designs Novak Djokovic-backed Miami supertall skyscraper

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

New York studio SHoP Architects has released designs for a ribbed supertall skyscraper in Miami, announced alongside the launch of a luxury brand for which Serbian tennis player Novak Djokovic is developing amenity facilities.

SHoP Architects, who are behind some of New York City’s tallest buildings, designed the 1,049-foot-tall (320-metre-tall) skyscraper around ATMA, a luxury brand being launched by hospitality operator Aman Group.

If completed at the stated height, it would be taller than the city’s current tallest skyscraper, and have the same projected height as the in-progress Waldorf Astoria Hotel & Residences Miami, which has been cited as the city’s future tallest.

The skyscraper will include a hotel, residences and a members’ club. According to Aman Group, ATMA’s “advanced wellness programming and world-class sport facilities” will be developed “exclusively” with Djokovic, who is credited as a global partner of the new brand.

Designs for the skyscraper itself show a ribbed form of balconied terraces organised around a central void that runs from the crown to the podium.

It is planned for Miami’s Brickell neighbourhood, and Aman Group said that the floor-to-ceiling windows of the skyscraper will merge the “futuristic architecture with the bay”.

Toronto-based interiors outfit Studio Paolo Ferrari has been chosen as the interiors lead on the project. It will include condos with one to five bedrooms, with six-bedroom penthouses pushed to the top of the skyscraper.

The structure will have setbacks at the upper floors, presumably to create more panoramic views for residences in the penthouses.

The ATMA concept is based around what Aman Group calls “performance-driven lifestyle”.

“Monolithic architecture meets biomorphic design, furniture is shaped naturally to the human form and ambience adjusts sensitively to residents’ and guests’ circadian rhythm,” said Aman Group.

Djokovic’s programme, meanwhile, will include a 120,000-square-foot spa and wellness centre.

“ATMA Miami will sit at the pinnacle of what performance living can achieve,” said Djokovic.

OKO Group and Oak Row Equities were announced as developers for the project.

SHoP Architects is no stranger to supertall luxury residences. It designed New York’s skinniest supertall skyscraper to have single-floor apartments on the city’s so-called Billionaires Row. It also designed the Brooklyn Tower, the tallest structure in the borough.
