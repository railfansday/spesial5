‘Exceptional’ - Former Leeds and Man City man shares bold Brighton and Arsenal title prediction

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Former Leeds United man Danny Mills believes 'exceptional' Brighton can achieve Champions League football this season.

The Seagulls have emerged as surprise title contenders this term and are currently second, five points adrift of leaders Arsenals.

Fabian Hurzeler's Brighton are the leading scorers in the division with 26 goals from 10 matches. They have also been impressive at the back with seven clean sheets from 10 matches.

Mills, speaking to BOYLE Sports, who offer the latest football odds, said: "Brighton would need a little bit of luck, possibly with injuries. We've seen Leicester do it in the past.

"Are they likely to win it? Probably not. But is Europa League or Champions League football a possibility with the league the way it is in flux at the moment. Absolutely.

"[Brighton] are incredibly well coached. If you listen to some of the best players that I've ever been around, the likes of Paul Scholes, Nicky Butt, that have seen these up first hand and seen some of the coaching that Fabian Hurzeler is doing, it's exceptional.

"It's clear, it's concise, they have a specific plan that they work to, and it's working for them. I think they will keep it up but they will be tested at some point.

"Undoubtedly there will be a dip at some point within that. But at the moment they're playing with great confidence in what they're doing, great confidence and belief in their manager."

Mills was also full of praise for Brighton transfer model and the fact they give new players and youngsters a chance to shine on the biggest stage.

"I think we've seen in recent years the business model has changed somewhat. I think you have to look at it, you know, teams understand now that bringing in young players and making them better, EPPP ripped the heart out of the academy system, really.

“So all the top clubs took all the best young players very, very early and clubs were not getting huge fees for them at that stage. So I think what they're trying to do now is hold on to these players a little bit longer, develop these players, give themselves a chance.

“I think we've seen it across the leagues, that's the model. It's going back to maybe the old days of let's give players an opportunity, let's give them plenty of game time, improve them, and then sell them on and keep doing the same thing.

“It's no different from the Brentford model, the Brighton model. It's exactly what they're doing. So then identification and recruitment in all that is absolutely key.”
