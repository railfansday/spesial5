Lamar Odom Says He Made Over $50,000 in 8 Days After Joining OnlyFans

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Lamar Odom is earning some major cash with his foray into OnlyFans.

A representative for the former NBA player told People on Sunday, November 8, that Odom, 47, has made over $50,000 in just eight days on the content creation platform.

Odom said in a statement to the outlet, “The response to my OnlyFans has been incredible. I’m connecting with fans in a whole new way, building something big, and now I’m looking to bring other athletes along for the ride.”

“There’s a real opportunity here and we’re just getting started,” Odom added.

Odom launched his OnlyFans account in October, writing in his bio, “You’ve seen me on the court. You’ve seen me on TV. Now get to know the REAL me. A little more personal. A little more playful. And a side of Lamar you haven’t seen before.”

The former Los Angeles Lakers power forward also told TMZ that he expected judgment for signing up to OnlyFans, which is popular among adult content creators.

“People hear OnlyFans and automatically think one thing, but I’m not here to tell you what you will or won’t see. I’ve lived a pretty wild life,” he said.

TMZ reported that Odom’s girlfriend, L.A. real estate agent Rachel Bradley, has also signed up for OnlyFans and the couple may share joint content in the future.

Odom was married to reality television star Khloé Kardashian, 42, from 2009 to 2016.

During a recent episode of Hulu’s The Kardashians, Kardashian and mom Kris Jenner recalled how they caught Odom having an orgy during the height of his substance abuse issues. (The former basketball player was found unconscious and went into a coma in 2015.)

“We’re going to extract him from the situation because he’s being held hostage by these hookers,” Jenner said.

“Khloé’s security burst into this room,” she added before Kardashian said, “And all I saw [was] they were literally having a circle jerk. There were 20 people in the room.”

“This is solidifying how I will never date a freak again,” Kardashian said on camera. ”I needed to put this on my manifest list [for a man]. Maybe I should add no circle jerks and no orgies. Also maybe no hardcore drugs.”
