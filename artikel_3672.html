'Alpha Sadist' Convicted of Murdering Woman, 32, He Met in Fetish Chatroom

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

A self-declared “alpha sadist” in Florida was found guilty of killing and kidnapping a British piano tutor after they connected on a fetish chatroom and allegedly arranged for him to kill her.

Dwain Hall was convicted on Friday, November 20, in the death of Sonia Exelby in October 2025, according to the Daily Mail.

A jury of 12 people deliberated for about an hour before they told Judge Timothy McCourt that they had reached a unanimous verdict. During the deliberation, the jury concluded that the murder was premeditated.

Hall, 53, immediately learned his fate and was given two consecutive life sentences in prison.

Just days before her murder, Exelby, 32, flew from the United Kingdom to Florida to meet up with Hall.

Authorities in the U.K. considered her to be endangered and missing when she didn’t make her return flight to England on October 13, 2025. The Florida Department of Law Enforcement eventually got involved in the case, and authorities found evidence on Exelby’s computer that indicated “she was suicidal and traveling to the United States to be possibly sexually abused, tortured, and murdered,” according to Hall’s arrest affidavit obtained by Us.

Florida investigators learned that Hall picked Exelby up from the airport on October 10, 2025, and they traveled to an Airbnb in Reddick, Florida.

Amid the investigation, Hall told police that Exelby promised to pay him $4,000 to kill her. He also explained that they had known each other for two years after they connected in online chat rooms on websites such as motherless.com. The now-defunct aforementioned website allowed anonymous users to uploaded disturbing photos of women and children being tortured, raped and killed.

Exelby appeared to express regret over her arrangement with Hall, and she reportedly sent concerning messages to a friend while at the Airbnb.

“I’m sorry he keeps taking my phone he doesn’t trust me with it,” she wrote in messages to a friend sent via Discord, according to the affidavit. “He made it clear there was no way out unless I shoot him. I was questioning it last night. I can’t kill anyone.”

“I’m so so scared I’m so broken and in so much pain all I can do is lay here and doing what [he] wants makes him respect me enough not to do the things I really hate,” she continued. “Everything but some are.”

Hall’s verdict was revealed in front of eight of Exelby’s family members, and four of her siblings made statements before the defendant learned his sentence.

“Dwain, you pretended to care for Sonia,” Barry Wilton said in court, per the Daily Mail. “But you didn’t at all. You used her for your sick fantasy. You had the opportunity to save her. Instead, you took her from us. May your actions weight heavily on your mind for the rest of your pitiful life.”

Luke Wilton added, “This year has been terrifying for the family. We were terrified coming here. What we found, however, is decency and care. People met here were stunning, seeing how the state of Florida fought for us, seeing all these people came here together in harmony for us.”

“Thank to the jury for their patience and attentiveness, and to the prosecutors who left nothing to chance, and staff who looked after us for kindness, humanity and dedication,” Luke said.
