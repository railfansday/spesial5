GTA 6: What Will Review Scores Be? You Can Win A $500 Gift Card For Getting It Right

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Rockstar's long-awaited open-world action game Grand Theft Auto 6 finally releases on November 19, and among the many things people are excited to find out is how the game will score with reviewers.

Nintendo's The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake is currently the No. 1 most highly rated game of 2026 on GameSpot sister site Metacritic, but could GTA 6 dethrone it?

You can make a prediction of your own via GameSpot's parent company, Fandom, and score a chance to win a gift card for up to $500.

Now through midnight on November 17, you can submit your prediction for GTA 6's score on Metacritic by dragging the slider on the GTA 6 Metascore Prediction page. Selections are editable up until the contest closes.

There are three prizes up for grabs in the form of Visa gift cards. The first-place prize is a $500 gift card, with $250 going to second place, and $150 for third. Winners will be drawn at random from eligible entries whose prediction exactly matches the GTA 6 Metascore on November 25.

You can see the full terms and conditions of the contest here.

For some context, here's how some of the recent GTA games have fared on Metacritic over the years:

  • GTA 5 (2013) - 97
  • GTA: Chinatown Wars (2009) - 93
  • GTA 4 (2008) - 98
  • GTA: San Andreas (2004) - 95
  • GTA: Vice City (2002) - 95
  • GTA 3 (2001) - 97
