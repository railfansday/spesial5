Diddy’s 57th Birthday Meal in Prison Revealed After Recent Transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Sean “Diddy” Combs will be turning another year older behind bars — and his prison meal is a far cry from his former caviar-and-champagne lifestyle.

The disgraced rapper turns 57 on Wednesday, November 4, and Us has exclusively obtained FCI Allenwood Low’s food menu, revealing what he will be served on his birthday.

Diddy — who is serving his 50-month sentence at the Pennsylvania low-security prison — will begin his day with a healthy breakfast, including hot oatmeal, scrambled eggs, fruit, whole wheat bread with jelly and whole milk.

The Bad Boy Records founder’s birthday lunch will be his choice of a hamburger or black bean burger and a bun with all the fixings: lettuce, tomato, sliced red onions and condiments like mustard and ketchup. His meal will come with oven-browned potatoes, fruit and a beverage to wash it all down.

Diddy will end his 57th birthday eating pork roast, mashed potatoes with gravy and mixed vegetables served with whole wheat bread.

Of course, there’s always commissary if he wants to treat himself to snacks, too.

According to FCI Allenwood Low’s commissary list viewed by Us, Diddy can buy himself a three-pack of birthday cupcakes for just $1.25.

If the business mogul has already made friends inside his new prison after his transfer last month, inmates can buy him a birthday card from the commissary. A pack of six birthday cards costs $1.50, per the list.

Diddy was transferred from FCI Fort Dix in New Jersey to FCI Allenwood Low in Pennsylvania on October 16, days after reportedly suffering a head injury. The reason behind the transfer was not publicly shared.

“We appreciate the Bureau of Prisons taking the safety and security of Sean and everyone involved seriously,” Diddy’s rep, Juda Engelmayer, told Us in a separate statement. “His transfer from one low-security facility to another is an important distinction, and while we cannot speak to the BOP’s specific reasons for the move, it is consistent with a decision made with safety and security in mind. We are grateful for the attention being given to his well-being and hope he can continue serving his time without unnecessary disruption.”

The move also came following an NBC News report that he lived a “pampered” life at FCI Fort Dix, which allegedly included contraband cellphones and a secret stash of liquor.

Diddy was sentenced to 50 months on October 3, 2025, after being convicted on two charges of transportation to engage in prostitution. He was acquitted on two counts of sex trafficking and one count of racketeering conspiracy.

Amid his sentence, his legal team has filed to appeal the conviction.

Diddy’s time behind bars continues to be shortened. The Bureau of Prisons’ website currently lists his projected release date as February 6, 2028.
