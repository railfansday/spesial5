Grand Theft Auto 6 Includes A Joke About GTA 7

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Grand Theft Auto 6GTA 6GTA 7Rockstar Games

After a very long wait, Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here. And the new game contains a joke that pokes fun at how long it took Rockstar to make GTA 6 and also references the franchise’s past and future.

In GTA 6, players can scroll through Snapmatic, an Instagram-like social media platform found on Jason and Lucia’s in-game phones. Snapmatic contains hundreds and hundreds of posts, videos, and comments. As you play the game, more posts are added as people react to the events of GTA 6 and also just post memes, conspiracies, funny videos, and other random junk.

And very early in the game, if you check Snapmatic, you can find a post complaining that gamers in the world of GTA 6 got a video game based on an in-universe kids’ cartoon before Race and Chase 7.

For those not aware, Race and Chase is a reference to Grand Theft Auto‘s original name. Before the original top-down GTA became what we know today, it started life as a game that would have let players race around as street racers or chase down those drivers as cops. Hence the name Race’n’Chase. As the idea evolved, so did the name.

It seems very likely that Race and Chase 7 is a nod to GTA 7. And the post on Snapmatic joking about getting a Barnyard Blue video game before Race and Chase 7 seems to be Rockstar acknowledging the years of people posting comments online like “I can’t believe we got [insert game sequel/remake/etc] before GTA 6.” Apparently, in the world of GTA 6, gamers have been waiting a long time to play the new Race and Chase. I understand their collective pain.

This isn’t the first time Race and Chase has popped up in a GTA game, either. In GTA Online, players can find and play three variants of an arcade racing game titled Race and Chase. If we assume those are the first three games in the series, then I’d assume that RAC 4 and RAC 5 moved the franchise away from being just a basic arcade racing game and into something big enough to have people super-hyped for Race and Chase 7. Good luck with the wait. Hope it’s worth it.
