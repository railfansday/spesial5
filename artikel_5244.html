XRP Price Plunges to $1.05 After a Hot CPI Report: Will the Support Stand?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Ripple XRP fell to $1.05 after a hot CPI report. After 7 of the last 10 hot reports, XRP was higher a day later. Can $1.05 hold?

Ripple XRP fell to $1.05 on Tuesday within five minutes of the U.S. October consumer price index (CPI) report, which showed XRP USD price rising faster than economists expected.

XRP crypto traded at $1.0797 when the report came out at 8:30 a.m. in New York, or 13:30 UTC. By 13:35 UTC it had touched $1.0500 on Binance, a 2.8% drop and its lowest price since August 19. It has since bounced to about $1.07. Bitcoin fell with it, dropping to $73,525 in the same five minutes.

The drop puts XRP back on a level it has tested before. Between late June and mid-August, XRP traded around $1.05 for weeks. The level held the first time and broke the second.

So will the support stand this time, and what do the CPI numbers tell us?

BREAKING: October CPI inflation rises +0.4% month-over-month, above expectations of +0.3%.

Core CPI inflation rose +0.3%, also above expectations of +0.2%.

BLS will not be releasing year-over-year figures due to the October 2025 government shutdown.

Prices rose faster than forecast in October. The Bureau of Labor Statistics said consumer prices rose 0.4% in the month, against the 0.3% economists expected.

Core prices, which leave out food and energy because they swing the most, rose 0.3%. The forecast was 0.2%. Core inflation has now run at about 0.3% for three months in a row.

The report is missing one usual figure. The bureau did not publish a yearly inflation rate because it never collected prices in October 2025, during the government shutdown. That leaves the monthly numbers to carry the whole report.

Those monthly numbers matter for the Federal Reserve. Fed Governor Christopher Waller said in a speech this morning that he already leans toward a 0.25 percentage point rate hike in December. If core CPI came in around 0.3% again, he said, a 0.5 point hike “should be considered”.

Core CPI did come in at 0.3%. Before the release, traders saw almost no chance of a hike that large. Higher rates make cash and bonds pay more, which tends to pull money out of risky assets like XRP.

The first move after a hot report has often faded. We measured XRP’s price on Binance in the 24 hours after each of the last 24 CPI reports, from October 2024 to October 2026.

Ten of those reports had core inflation of 0.3% or higher, like today’s. After seven of them, XRP was higher 24 hours later. The median move was a 1.4% gain.

The record is weaker this year. Of the four hot reports in 2026, XRP was higher a day later only twice. After the last one, on October 14, it fell 4.4% in 24 hours.

The difference this time is the Fed. A hot report that only confirms a small hike is easier for traders to shrug off. One that puts a larger hike on the table, as Waller laid out, gives sellers more to work with.

The level has a history this year. XRP has spent two long stretches around $1.05 since June.

Today’s low of $1.0500 sits right on that zone. The first test of a level often holds. Each new test tends to leave fewer buyers there.

XRP trades at about $1.07 on Binance after the CPI drop, down 2.8% in 24 hours and 17% in a week. It is 71% below its July 2025 record of $3.65.

On the daily chart, where each candle covers one day, XRP sits well below both of its exponential moving averages (EMAs), averages that give recent prices more weight. The 50-day EMA stands at $1.294 and the 200-day EMA at $1.350.

For Ripple XRP, the first test of $1.05 has held so far. The daily close decides it: a close above $1.05 keeps the support alive, while a close below it opens the way to $1.
