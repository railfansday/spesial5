A crypto savings account on a centralized exchange lets you earn daily interest on idle USDT, USDC, Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and Solana (SOL). You do not need to run validator nodes or manage external DeFi wallets. Unlike bank deposits, exchange savings programs generate yield through principal-protected Simple Earn pools, margin lending, Real-World-Asset (RWA) instruments, and fixed-rate loans. Comparing flexible vs fixed terms, VIP rate boosters, Proof of Reserves, and Auto-Earn idle-balance sweeps helps you pick the right account across Binance, OKX, BloFin, Bybit, Kraken, and Bitget.

Published staking and Earn product specifications and fee schedules reviewed November 1, 2026. APRs move with network conditions and regional eligibility applies.

Crypto savings accounts on centralized exchanges are not bank deposits. They are not covered by government deposit insurance such as FDIC or FSCS protection. Savers face exchange custodial counterparty risk and borrower utilization fluctuations on flexible lending pools. Fixed Simple Earn plans also lock principal until maturity, and BTC, ETH, and SOL balances carry market price volatility.

A traditional bank savings account holds fiat currency insured up to statutory limits (such as $250,000 FDIC coverage in the United States). It lends deposits through regulated banking channels. A crypto savings account (such as Simple Earn on Binance, OKX, BloFin, or Bitget, or Easy Earn on Bybit) holds digital assets like USDT, USDC, BTC, and ETH under exchange custody without government deposit insurance.

RWUSD is BloFin Earn’s flexible Real-World-Asset (RWA) yield product, paying a published 3.68% APR on a flexible duration. Unlike peer-to-peer margin lending pools whose rates swing hourly with trader leverage demand, RWUSD provides a steady dollar-referenced yield stream with anytime subscription and redemption.

BloFin VIP 1 (unlocked at 50,000 USDT in assets with zero platform token requirement) offers 7-day VIP Fixed Simple Earn rates of 4.80% APR on USDT, 3.00% on USDC, 3.20% on SOL, 3.20% on ETH, 0.80% on XRP, and 0.40% to 1.60% on 14/60-day VIP BTC. By comparison, Bitget’s published VIP Simple Earn rates pay 4.00% APR on USDT, 2.28% on USDC, and 0.15% on BTC.

Fixed-term Simple Earn contracts lock your deposited tokens until the scheduled settlement date in exchange for a guaranteed fixed APR. On BloFin Simple Earn Fixed plans (which run from 2 to 180 days depending on the coin), early redemption is not supported, so savers who want instant liquidity should use Simple Earn Flexible, RWUSD (3.68% APR), or Flexible Onchain Earn.
