In spite of the fact that Zcash (ZEC) price ranked among the top cryptocurrency losers on Monday, November 9, 2026, Polymarket, a leading web3-based prediction market platform, has set the odds of further decline by the end of this year higher than the odds of a possible uptrend.

As of press time, Polymarket had set the odds of Zcash price plunging to $700 by December 31 at 21%, up 9% over the past 24 hours. This contract had attracted approximately $31,571 in traded volume, according to data Coinpedia fetched from Polymarket.

Contract for Zcash price prediction for December 31, 2026. Source: Polymarket

Meanwhile, the prediction platform had placed the chances of ZEC reaching $2,000 by the close of this year at 17%, down 3% today. Notably, this contract had attracted the highest traded volume of $77,834, signaling that more cryptocurrency traders are focused on this price target.

With Zcash price hovering at $1,169.24 at the time of writing, Polymarket believes the altcoin has a 17% chance of rallying over 71% by the end of the year. Additionally, the platform has set the likelihood of ZEC plummeting over 40% by the end of the year at 21%.

Zcash price fell by more than 6% over the past 24 hours, driven by weakness in the broader cryptocurrency market. As the altcoin saw its 24-hour traded volume surge by over 85% to $1.19 billion, its market capitalization declined by nearly $1.1 billion to $19.82 billion.
