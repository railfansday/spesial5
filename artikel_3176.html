Anthropic's IPO Is Just 1 Week Away. Should You Buy the Stock?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The business is impressive. The value is still a question mark.

Anthropic is now set to go public in a trillion-dollar mega-IPO late next week, and there are now answers to most of the questions investors have been asking about the Claude developer. We now know how fast it's growing, how much it spends on computing power, and who will control the company after the IPO.

In the latest version of Anthropic's IPO prospectus, we also know the initial price range and valuation the company is targeting. With the IPO reportedly set to begin trading on Nov. 19, here's what I'm thinking as I analyze whether it's a smart buy.

To put it mildly, Anthropic's growth is staggering. The AI giant's revenue grew 1,368% year over year in the second quarter, and the company brought in $16.3 billion in the first half of 2026, more than three times its revenue for all of 2025. Its amended filing showed third-quarter revenue of $17.0 billion, more than it generated in the entire first half of the year. Existing business customers are spending about nine times as much as they did a year ago, and more than 1,900 of them now spend at least $1 million a year.

It's also becoming a better business. A year ago, Anthropic spent about $2 on computing power for every dollar of revenue. In the first half of 2026, that fell to about $0.55, and the company's adjusted operating margin reached 15% in the third quarter. Add $131.8 billion of cash and short-term investments, and Anthropic has plenty of room to keep investing.

Of course, there's plenty to worry about. Anthropic's top five customers account for about a quarter of its revenue; the company has signed enormous long-term compute commitments, and public shareholders will have very little say in how it's run. The co-founders will control 50.1% of the voting power, and the company can issue additional shares to its Public Benefit Fund as the stock price rises.

Anthropic filed an updated prospectus on Tuesday, and it plans to sell 825 million shares at $70 to $78 each, with a separate $26 billion private placement with several big tech companies. About 13.1 billion shares will be outstanding after the IPO, which works out to a market cap of about $916 billion to $1.02 trillion. That's roughly in line with Anthropic's $965 billion valuation from its private funding round in May, and well below the $1.5 trillion to $1.6 trillion investors were pushing for just a few weeks ago, according to The Wall Street Journal.

It's worth noting that you may see higher valuation figures elsewhere, and they aren't wrong. When including stock options and other equity awards, Anthropic's valuation on a fully-diluted basis would be in the range of about $1.3 trillion to $1.4 trillion. The range may not hold, either. The Financial Times reports that strong demand has bankers considering pricing the shares above $78. It is not uncommon for popular IPOs to price significantly above the initially disclosed range.

Anthropic generated $17.0 billion of revenue in the third quarter. Annualized, that's about $68 billion. On a fully diluted basis, investors are paying about 19-21 times sales.

To be sure, a company growing this quickly can grow into a lofty valuation faster than you'd think, and at about 20 times sales, Anthropic doesn't exactly look like it's unreasonably expensive. But if strong demand pushes the final price well above the range, the stock could leave much less room for disappointment.

First, I wouldn't chase the stock on its first day of trading. Individual investors rarely get shares at the IPO price, and hot IPOs often open well above it. Waiting a few weeks costs very little, and doing so could give you a look at Anthropic's first earnings report as a public company.

Second, I'd keep any initial position small. Even if you believe Anthropic will be one of the most important companies of the next decade, a single stock priced for perfection deserves a modest spot in your portfolio.

Finally, there are indirect ways to own a piece of Anthropic. Alphabet (GOOGL +2.11%)(GOOG +2.00%) and Amazon (AMZN -1.74%) are both investors in the company, and each gives you exposure to Anthropic's success alongside a profitable core business.

Anthropic is one of the most impressive businesses I've seen go public, and I'm likely to own shares eventually. Having said all that, a great business can still be a poor investment at the wrong price, and right now we have no idea what the final price will be, especially by the time the shares are actually available to trade. I plan to wait for the final IPO pricing, watch how the stock trades in its first few weeks, and start small if the valuation makes sense.
