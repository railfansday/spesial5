Timothée Chalamet Addresses Possibility Of A ‘Call Me by Your Name’ Sequel: “Never Say Never”

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Nearly a decade later, a Call Me by Your Name sequel isn’t looking too likely, but another Luca Guadagnino/Timothée Chalamet team-up could be in the cards.

The 4x Oscar-nominated actor recently commented on the possibility of the once-percolating sequel to Guadagnino’s 2017 adaptation of André Aciman’s 2007 novel happening, following co-star Armie Hammer’s fall from grace.

“A great question,” he told Vanity Fair. “Never say never, but… I’ve grown up with the mindset that you don’t really go near something that is so near and dear to people.

Chalamet added, “Now, to work with Luca again? In a heartbeat.” The pair last worked together on 2022’s Bones and All, which starred Chalamet and Taylor Russell. Watch on Deadline

Set in 1983 rural Italy, Call Me by Your Name follows 17-year old Elio Perlman (Chalamet), who falls in love with Oliver (Hammer), a young graduate student who has been hired to assist Elio’s father (Stuhlbarg). In the heartbreaking conclusion, Oliver winds up returning to the United States at the end of the summer, breaking off his romance with Elio as he gets engaged to his girlfriend back home.

Following the release of Aciman’s 2019 sequel novel Find Me, which picks up 10 years after the original, Guadagnino announced the following year that he was planning to adapt the followup with Chalamet, Hammer, Michael Stuhlbarg and more set to reprise their roles.

The likelihood of the original cast reuniting seems to have gone out the window when the LAPD investigated accusations against Hammer of rape and sexual abuse in 2021. Although he was dropped from several projects and his representation, Hammer maintained his innocence and ultimately faced no charges.
