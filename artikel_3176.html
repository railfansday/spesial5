Don't panic: All your devices should transfer over to Google Home fairly seamlessly.

Google Home is replacing Nest and bringing new devices.

If you’re a Nest smart home user, a major change is coming in early 2027 that will affect how you manage your smart home. Google has officially confirmed to CNET via email that both the Nest app and Google Assistant are being sunset, and current users will lose access to both next year.

All Nest devices will lose Nest app support in favor of the Google Home app, and the Nest app will be retired on Jan. 25, 2027. Google Assistant will be removed from home devices on Aug. 2, 2027, with all support moving to the Gemini for Home voice assistant.

The change is part of Google’s effort to consolidate its various apps and devices into a single app and voice experience. In a statement sent to CNET, Google confirmed that “every device supported today works with these updated experiences.”

Nest Thermostats should all seamlessly port over to Google Home.

Google has confirmed that Google Home will support all your Nest devices, including thermostats, cameras, doorbells, smoke detectors and locks. Existing schedules and settings should transfer over automatically, which is often a hassle when you switch between apps and devices. You should also be able to pair your Nest devices with any Works with Google Home and Matter smart home devices, as well as Google Home speakers and smart displays and TV streamers, all with the same app.

As part of the switchover, you should be able to access Nest devices on various platforms outside of the mobile app. It’ll support iOS and Android widgets, the Home Panel on Android, smart displays, speakers and the Google Home web page. That includes being able to check live camera views, adjust your thermostat and lock your front door, right from a TV or smartwatch.

Google Assistant may be leaving, but Google is releasing newer, more powerful devices.

However, to continue controlling and receiving notifications from your Nest devices remotely, you will need to switch to the Google Home app or home.google.com if you prefer to control them via a web browser.

You can change Google voice assistant voices, but not the wake words.

On Aug. 2, 2027, Gemini for Home voice assistant will fully replace Google Assistant on speakers and smart displays. The switch to Gemini makes a lot of sense if you’ve used Google Assistant at all. Assistant is very much an older voice assistant, lacking the smarter follow-ups and natural-language capabilities that make Gemini more useful.With Gemini, you should get answers to more complex questions, and you can speak more naturally without barking commands, letting it handle conversation context and remember multiple interactions.

Getting ready for Google Home is easy, though Google has an entire Nest App Transition Support Hub if you need step-by-step instructions to walk you through everything that’s needed.

For most people, it should be a simple matter of downloading the Google Home app. Naturally, a Google Account is required, but as mentioned, your Nest devices, including the cameras and video history, will automatically transfer from the Nest app starting on Jan. 25, 2027.

Gemini’s Google Home integrations are useful, but command options can include some risks.

The other thing you may have to do is download any custom video clips you’ve created on home.nest.com. Manually saved clips won’t transfer to the Google Home app along with your video history. To make sure these are backed up, you’ll want to visit home.nest.com before Jan. 25, 2027, and use the Download All button to save clips you want to keep.

The final deadline to migrate your Nest account to a Google account before the company starts data deletions March 25, 2027.

Google says Nest devices, including cameras and video history, will automatically transfer from the Nest app to the Google Home app. Nest Aware (1st Gen) subscriptions should also automatically switch to Google Home Premium so that coverage continues without interruption. Google Home Premium is replacing Nest Aware (1st Gen), and users will receive an email closer to the transfer date detailing your subscription information.

Google’s smart displays should all work perfectly with the various Nest devices.

Gemini for Home is already available in many countries and languages. Google announced earlier that it’s expanding to more regions over the coming months. Users in a supported region can upgrade to Gemini Home right now.

On your phone, open the latest version of the Google Home app, tap your profile icon in the top right to open Home Settings, then tap “Get Started” on the “Set up Gemini for Home” banner. On your computer, just visit home.google.com/gemini.
