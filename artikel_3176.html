1 Reason to Buy e.l.f. Beauty Stock

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

e.l.f. Beauty stock is up 53% in a year, but a raised sales outlook and early international expansion suggest the run may not be over.

By John Ballard – Nov 22, 2026 at 1:34AM EST

10 stocks we like better than e.l.f. Beauty ›

e.l.f. Beauty (ELF -5.01%) has been on a tear. This top cosmetics stock closed at $111.21 on Nov. 17, up 53% from a year earlier.

After a run like that, investors might be hesitant to buy it in fear of buying at the top before a pullback. But the best reason to buy it is the business's momentum in a challenging consumer spending environment.

e.l.f. just posted its 31st straight quarter of net sales growth. Net sales jumped 51% year over year in the September-ended quarter. Sales were inflated by the addition of Rhode, the beauty brand e.l.f. bought in August 2025. Excluding Rhode, sales grew about 35% year over year.

Management expects the strong results to continue. "With this momentum, we are raising our full-year net sales guidance to 22% to 24%," CEO Tarang Amin said. This works out to organic growth — which excludes the impact of acquisitions — of 9% to 11% for the fiscal year, up from the previous guidance of 6% to 7%.

The risk is that e.l.f. relies on a small number of retailers for a large share of its sales. It sells affordable makeup and skin care through retailers such as Target, Walmart, and Amazon. A loss of just one major store that sells its brands could dent the company's sales and momentum.

Still, the stock looks reasonably priced. It trades at about 27 times next year's consensus earnings estimate, while analysts expect earnings to grow about 15% a year over the next several years. That's a premium, but analysts have raised their long-term growth estimates for this company that has grown sales for nearly eight years straight.

I believe patient investors buying today could be well rewarded, especially as Amin believes the company is in the "early days of international expansion."
