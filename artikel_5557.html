Cisco beat earnings expectations and raised its AI revenue target for the year

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Cisco raised its fiscal 2027 revenue forecast to $74.8 billion to $75.8 billion as quarterly sales rose 24% from a year earlier

Cisco $CSCO now anticipates $9 billion in fiscal 2027 from hyperscalers’ purchases of AI infrastructure, more than double the fiscal 2026 total, the company said Thursday as it reported first-quarter results.

Revenue for the quarter ended Oct. 24 was $18.5 billion, up 24% from a year earlier. Non-GAAP earnings per share were $1.40, up 40%. GAAP net income was $4.6 billion, or $1.14 per share, up from $2.9 billion, or 72 cents per share.

Analysts expected revenue of $18.1 billion and adjusted earnings per share of $1.33, according to CNBC.

Cisco raised its fiscal 2027 revenue guidance to a range of $74.8 billion to $75.8 billion and its non-GAAP earnings per share guidance to $5.47 to $5.53. The prior outlook for revenue was as much as $73.4 billion, according to Bloomberg.

For the second quarter, Cisco expects revenue of $18.0 billion to $18.2 billion and non-GAAP earnings per share of $1.34 to $1.36. Analysts had expected revenue of $17.88 billion and earnings per share of $1.23, according to CNBC.

Chief Financial Officer Mark Patterson said in a statement that revenue, non-GAAP operating margin and non-GAAP earnings per share all exceeded the high end of the company's guidance ranges. He said that performance gave Cisco confidence to raise its fiscal 2027 guidance.

Product revenue rose 32%, led by networking, which climbed 43% to $11.1 billion. Security revenue rose 7%, collaboration 8% and observability 2%. Services revenue rose 1%. Revenue grew 28% in the Americas, 18% in EMEA and 21% in APJC.

Product orders rose 21% from a year earlier, a slower pace than the 35% increase recorded one quarter before, according to Bloomberg. A UBS analyst said on the earnings call that AI orders from hyperscalers edged down. CEO Chuck Robbins responded that "it's going to be lumpy and there's going to be some quarters that are going to be big, others that are going to be smaller," according to Bloomberg.

Cisco stock closed at $102.01 on Thursday.
