Ubisoft stock drops 11% as GTA VI competition dims its holiday forecast

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The Assassin's Creed maker expects about €250 million in net bookings for the quarter ending in December and kept its full-year targets

Ubisoft forecast net bookings of about €250 million ($280 million) for the quarter ending in December, and its stock fell about 11% in Paris trading on Friday.

The company said on Thursday that the outlook reflects a crowded holiday season. Ubisoft confirmed its full-year targets: net bookings down by a high single-digit percentage, a high single-digit negative non-IFRS operating margin and free cash flow consumption of no more than €500 million ($560 million).

The €250 million forecast is a 26% decline from €338 million a year earlier, according to Bloomberg. The forecast falls short of the €361 million analyst consensus that TD Cowen pointed to, according to Reuters. Reuters also said the outlook reflects a thinner slate of content releases than is normal.

Ubisoft stock fell to €3.72 after dropping as much as 15% during the session, and it is down 42% this year.

Rockstar Games, a label of Take-Two Interactive $TTWO, launched “Grand Theft Auto VI” on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X $TWTR|S, with the U.S. release on Thursday. The game earned a Metacritic score of 99. Ubisoft’s holiday-quarter titles will compete with it, according to Bloomberg. The game is believed to have taken in $2 billion on its opening day, according to Reuters.

Ubisoft’s second-quarter net bookings were €389 million, above its guidance of around €370 million. The company credited “Assassin’s Creed Black Flag Resynced” for the outperformance. Second-quarter net bookings were down 20.8% from €490.8 million a year earlier.

For the first half of the fiscal year, net bookings were €644.7 million, down 16.5% from a year earlier. Ubisoft posted an IFRS operating loss of €189.2 million, compared with a loss of €120.2 million a year earlier. Headcount fell by close to 1,100 over the six months, and the fixed cost base was €660 million, down 6%.

Ubisoft said the second half of its fiscal year will benefit from the releases of “Rayman Legends Retold” and “Just Dance: Decades of Hits,” along with continued “Black Flag Resynced” sales and a stronger fourth quarter for “Rainbow Six Siege.”

The company expects a stronger content pipeline in fiscal 2027-28 and 2028-29. It expects to return to free cash flow generation and positive non-IFRS operating income in fiscal 2027-28.
