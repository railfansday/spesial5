Former SEC chair Gary Gensler warns AI bubble is likely to cause recession before 2028 election

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The former SEC chair told CNN he expects a downturn as significant as the dot-com crash, with bankruptcies among neocloud firms

Former Securities and Exchange Commission Chair Gary Gensler told CNN that the AI boom is a bubble likely to burst and drag the economy into recession ahead of the 2028 presidential election.

"It's already a bubble," Gensler said, according to CNN. He led the SEC from 2021 until 2025 and is now a professor at MIT.

Gensler pointed to the money flowing into AI infrastructure, which totals $800 billion a year by some estimates, according to CNN. "The Big Tech companies are spending five times more in 2026 than they were just three years ago," he said.

“History tells us that when you have a new technology and a significant investment ... you come to a point where it plateaus. It retrenches,” Gensler said. “The only question is if it’s a soft recession or something more dramatic.” He cited railroads, 1920s electricity and the 1990s internet as earlier technologies that suffered steep declines once their infrastructure had been built on expectations of future revenue.

He also raised concerns about how the spending is financed, pointing to circular deals and intricate off-balance-sheet structures that make the true scale of exposure hard to see. "I think history will show that they loaned too much money, too fast," he said.

Gensler named neoclouds, which lease supercomputing capacity to AI developers, as the likely first casualties. "The major companies will survive. It's these neocloud companies where we're going to see some breakage and bankruptcies," he said.

He said he expects the downturn to rival the dot-com bust of the early 2000s in significance, though not to reach the severity of the subprime housing collapse that triggered the 2008 financial crisis. "I think we're going to have this repricing and a softening of the economy that happens before November 2028," he said, according to CNN. "I can't say exactly when and how stormy, but I would be battening down some hatches."

Ed Yardeni, president of Yardeni Research, called Gensler's scenario "plausible" but disagreed that a burst is coming, according to CNN. "It's a self-correcting bubble," Yardeni said, adding that chipmakers now trade at lower price-to-earnings multiples than the market overall. "There is no irrational exuberance in valuations," he said.

The warning comes days after Nvidia $NVDA said CEO Jensen Huang had resigned with immediate effect. Nvidia has committed roughly $10 billion to a private placement of Anthropic stock that is set to close alongside Anthropic's IPO.

Anthropic plans to price its IPO on Nov. 18. Combined with the private placement, the offering would bring in $90.4 billion if it lands at the top of the marketed range. The stock is expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq $NDAQ Global Select Market on Nov. 19 under the symbol ANT.
