Sotarrá House / FORarquitectura

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Text description provided by the architects. The neighborhood of "Las Playas de El Palo" is an extensive stretch of coastline made up of more than 500 properties located on the beachfront in the eastern district of Málaga. In this area, squeezed by urban growth, tourism pressure, and the tension generated among residents fighting to maintain their unique, picturesque, substandard housing, an opportunity arose in 2021 to embark on a complex journey through administrative procedures. This process was marked by clashes with disconnected administrative bodies and the defense of logic and coherence in carrying out work on one of these peculiar properties.
