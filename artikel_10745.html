30,000 guests turn up to wedding after groom was worried they wouldn’t have enough people

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Around 30,000 guests turned up to a wedding in China after the groom extended an invitation to people via social media.

When it comes to inviting people to your wedding, you might have to make some tough decisions on who makes the cut – especially if you only have a small party with close friends and family.

However, in the case of Xu Chenghong, he extended an invitation to people on social media due to the fact that he has very few relatives who could actually attend the ceremony. “Besides my older brother, there’s been no one else. Just myself, saving 167,500 RMB for the wedding,” he said.

Viewers quickly replied to Chenghong’s video, saying that they’d be there, and while the internet isn’t always great at pulling through on planning, around 10,000 people actually showed up days before the wedding.

As per local reports, the October 31st gathering was described as possibly “China’s biggest ever wedding,” with the sheer number of guests causing disruption in Yiliang County.

One report stated that hotels had no rooms available for all the people who had turned up from different parts of China. Other reports noted that local businesses saw massive boosts too, with some people sticking around for a few days.

Content creator Ur.Chinese.Unc added that people from Hong Kong, Sichuan, and Chongqing turned up with live ducks, cows, apples, home appliances, and a used car for the newlyweds, too.

A post shared by Ur Chinese Unc (@ur.chinese.unc)

As for the actual day, Ur.Chinese.Unc noted that around 30,000 guests actually turned up for Xu, with more than 40 chefs also travelling to help keep everyone fed on the day.

Guests even cleaned up the town square so that you would never have known that it had hosted a massive wedding just the day before.

Plenty of viewers said that he, in fact, had “millions” of guests from around the world because of how viral the story has gotten on social media.

“Video of the year. A day he will never forget,” one said. “Humanity at it’s best,” another commented.

China has a history of rallying around newlyweds. Back in March, a man went viral after telling other drivers to get out of the way so he could propose to his partner.
