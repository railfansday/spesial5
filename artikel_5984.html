Hegseth rips impeachment push from outgoing Republican as 'farce,' 'stunt'

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

War Secretary Pete Hegseth on Tuesday condemned a House impeachment effort, saying he stood behind "lawful" military operations in Iran, Venezuela and Yemen.

War Secretary Pete Hegseth, facing eight articles of impeachment in a privileged House resolution, remains defiant amid kinetic war and political warfare.

Hegseth said he stood behind military operations his department conducted in Iran, Venezuela and Yemen, saying they were lawful.

"What one disgruntled and defeated congressman is attempting is not oversight, it's not accountability. It's a political spectacle, a personal manifesto driven by ego and resentment," Hegseth said in a Tuesday morning conference. "Time for one man's final days in Congress to try to do damage to this president, this country and most irresponsibly, the warriors who defend it."

"It's a farce, and it's a stunt, and everybody knows it."

President Donald Trump gave a real-time review of Hegseth's defiant tone and remarks in a post Tuesday morning on Truth Social.

"Pete Hegseth gets an ‘A’ PLUS, PLUS, PLUS for his defense against a disgruntled, angry, defeated, former Congressman, Thomas Massie, a TOTAL LOSER!" Trump wrote.

Hegseth took a few questions at the news conference, mostly from conservative media outlets, but struck a warrrior's tone with his defense of his War Department and their operations during Trump's first two years.

"As for the articles themselves," he continued, "Iran, the cartels, Maduro, Yemen, every one was a lawful operation ordered by the commander in chief, then ordered by myself, notified to Congress and executed by American warriors who did their jobs."

"We destroyed terror regimes' capabilities. We sank the boats carrying poison into our communities. We put a narco dictator in handcuffs. These are not high crimes. This is the job, and it's the job well done."

"I was proud to execute them, and I would do it again tomorrow," he added.

Hegseth said the effort hands U.S. enemies a "propaganda victory."

"This is not patriotism; this is sabotage. It tells our enemies that the United States will actually punish its own warriors and its own civilian leadership for successfully executing lawful military operations," Hegseth added.

Trump defended Hegseth against outgoing Rep. Thomas Massie's impeachment effort in a Truth Social post Monday night, too.

HEGSETH'S FUTURE DRAWS OMINOUS PREDICTIONS FROM INSIDERS AFTER GOP LOSSES, BUT TRUMP ISN'T BUDGING YET

Trump said his war secretary "should be praised, not impeached."

"Defeated Republican Congressman Thomas Massie, a total loser, is trying to Impeach perhaps the most popular and successful military official in the Country, Secretary of War, Pete Hegseth," Trump declared Monday on Truth Social.

"With recruitment at an ALL-TIME HIGH, from an all-time low under Sleepy Joe Biden, and TWO VICTORIES, Venezuela and Iran, Pete Hegseth should be praised, not Impeached. Congress, stand up for Pete!!!" he added.
