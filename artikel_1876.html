Fabian Hurzeler reveals what frustrated him most about Brighton's defeat at Hull

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Seagulls looked on course for at least a point at the MKM Stadium, before two stoppage-time goals from Oli McBurnie condemned them to defeat.

Hurzeler – who was booked by the referee late on in the match – felt Brighton had been the better side for much of the contest. But he criticised their performance after the opening 65 minutes, admitting they had allowed Hull back into the game.

Speaking to Match of the Day, he said: "For 65 minutes we showed a good performance then we chopped our intensity, levels and standards.

"In the Premier League they can punish you especially after a red card. Overall a frustrating afternoon, a frustrating game but we have to keep going."

Brighton were reduced to ten men in the 89th minute, when Costinha received a second yellow card for pushing Sorba Thomas to the ground.

McBurnie then struck in the 92nd and 95th minutes to secure all three points for Hull.

Asked whether the dismissal had contributed to the late goals, Hurzeler said: "Of course when you are one man down, the red card plays a role."

The Brighton boss also acknowledged the outstanding performance of Hull goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis, who made several important saves to keep his side in the game.

"We have to give credit to him. He made a good performance but in the end it's our responsibility," Hurzeler said.

Speaking to Sky Sports, Hurzeler described Hull's opening goal as a ‘lucky punch’ but insisted Brighton had to take responsibility for the result.

"That's football. The Premier League is ruthless. Hull scored with a lucky punch, we have to accept it and be better next time.”

He suggested his players had become impatient as they struggled to make their chances count.

"We rushed things, we lost a bit of intensity, we tried to force things because we weren't patient enough," he said.

"For 65 minutes we could easily win the game.

"You can easily get frustrated if you miss so many chances."

Hurzeler was also full of praise for Tzolakis, describing him as a ‘great goalkeeper’ who had produced a ‘big performance’.

The Brighton manager was asked about a penalty appeal involving Pascal Gross but said he had not watched the incident back.

"The referee wasn't the reason why we lost today.”

Hurzeler insisted his team would analyse what went wrong but stressed the importance of maintaining their standards in the Premier League.

"We will analyse it – that's the process we follow," he told Match of the Day.

"I always emphasize keep staying humble. We have to outwork the opponent otherwise the opponents are too good in the Premier League and that's what we have to do better next time."
