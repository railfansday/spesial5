13 brilliant photos of Scotland fans during 2-1 win over North Macedonia - including a famous face

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Scotland edged a narrow Nations League game against North Macedonia thanks to two John McGinn strikes.

Scotland kept their hopes of qualifying for the Nations League promotion playoffs with a 2-1 win over North Macedonia in front of an attendance of 41,585 at at Hampden Park on Friday night.

Two second-half goals from John McGinn were enough to seal a first home win for new head coach Sebastien Pocognoli on a chilly Glasgow night, with Elmin Rastoder’s header just before the hour mark not proving to be enough for the visitors.

The result means that Pocognoli’s men can still claim second spot in the group if they can beat Switzerland on Monday, and Slovenia fail to beat North Macedonia, in their final Group B1 game.

Here, we take a look at the best photos of the Tartan Army - and the travelling support from North Macedonia - at Hampden Park last night. Can you spot yourself in our gallery alongside a famous face or two?
