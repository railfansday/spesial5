Dimitar Berbatov names the three midfielders who are better than Arsenal star Declan Rice

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Dimitar Berbatov claims Declan Rice is not the best midfielder in the world and must still improve before he can be truly considered among the game’s elite.

The Arsenal powerhouse has made a superb start to the new campaign and delivered another man of the match performance in the Premier League champions’ 1-0 win over Liverpool at Anfield last weekend.

Rice was to the fore again last night as the Gunners ground out a 2-1 win at Slavia Prague to cement their position near the head of the fledgling Champions League table.

The England international’s excellence was rewarded with a 15th placed finish in the Ballon d’Or rankings leading to the suggestion he is now among the best central midfielders on the planet.
