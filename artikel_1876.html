‘I feel like swearing’: Roy Keane issues damning Manchester United verdict after Liverpool thrashing

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Roy Keane was scathing in his verdict of Manchester United after the Red Devils’ 4-0 thrashing by Liverpool.

Mauricio Pochettino endured a nightmare debut at the helm after his recent arrival at Old Trafford, and he entered a baptism of fire at Anfield on Sunday.

The enormity of the task ahead of him was laid bare for the Argentine, as Andoni Iraola’s hosts ran riot on Merseyside.

Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz got the goals to secure all three points after Leny Yoro’s early red card.

Keane in the Sky Sports studio afterwards lambasted his former side’s performance.

"This team two years ago finished 15th,” he explained.

“We're blaming the board, blaming ownership, blaming managers. When do the players take responsibility for some decision-making?

"Liverpool probably should have scored eight, probably eight or nine, unbelievable chances they had.

"And everyone's coming off the pitch saying four [goals] is not too bad? I feel like swearing.

"I thought United were desperate. So bad.”

He took aim at ex-team-mate and fellow Sky pundit Gary Neville, who had described United’s current predicament as “the worst of it” as Pochettino looks to get his teeth into his new project.

"Neville's ok with 4-0?” Keane asked rhetorically.

"Are you telling me they could have done better? 4-0 is not too bad with 10 men? Has anyone ever got a result with 10 men? It can be done!

"People who played for the club said 4-0 is not too bad? Wow.

"Fans are applauding. Applauding what? Performing rubbish."

The result leaves United 19th in the Premier League table, in the relegation zone, with just one victory in 12 matches in all competitions.
