Vote. Top 25 Arizona high school girls basketball players in 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

With the Arizona girls' high school basketball season starting Nov. 18, The Arizona Republic looks at some of the top players in the state entering the 2026-27 season. Here is a breakdown of The Republic's top 25 who are competing in the Arizona Interscholastic Association, regardless of conference or school year. Coaches provided input.

Vote in our poll (scroll down) below for who you think the top player is. The poll will close on Sunday, Nov. 22 at noon. Print readers can find the poll online with this story at azcentral.com/sports/high-schools.

2. Jaiyana Bogan-Jacobs, Gilbert Campo Verde, G, So.

An incredible two-sport athlete. One of the state's top flag football players who is in the midst of trying to help lead the team to its second consecutive 5A state championship. But basketball is her main sport, where she is ranked No. 2 in the class of 2029 by ESPN. A top-tier scorer who can rebound like very few. Holds a number of offers, including Arizona State, Arizona, UCLA, Oregon, Michigan, North Carolina, Baylor and Florida.

3. Kolbi Brooks, Goodyear Millennium, G, So.

Made the transfer from Phoenix Desert Vista. Also nationally ranked by ESPN in the class of 2029 at No. 8. Averaged 23.2 points as a freshman. She might lead the state in scoring this season, which says a lot, since the team is loaded. Has over 15 Division I offers at the moment.

4. Lily McCracken, Scottsdale Notre Dame Prep, G, Sr.

The Creighton commit is electric with the ball in her hands. Can score from inside or outside. Very shifty and agile with the ball in her hands. High motor that never seems to slow down. Defends at a high level as well. Averaged 17.1 points, 7.5 rebounds, 3.0 assists and 2.5 steals per game as a junior.

5. Amaya Ribbens, Goodyear Millennium, F/C, So.

Part of the very talented 2029 class here in Arizona. Ranked No. 24 for her class by ESPN. One of the top players in the paint in the state, but she is also developing a nice touch from outside. Sparked the team in the comeback win in the Open Division state championship and was named MVP.

6. Kinsey Murray, Goodyear Millennium, G, Sr.

Made the transfer from Surprise Valley Vista after the departure of coach Brooklyn Hinkens. Ultra-athletic guard who can get downhill with the best of them. Great first step and finishes well around the rim. Very good passer. Lockdown defender.

7. Elleigh Molinar, Gilbert Perry, G, Jr.

One of the top point guards in Arizona. She is a knockdown shooter who can create her own shot and find ways to the basket. Molinar is also an excellent passer and defender. It's easy to see why she is one of the most recruited uncommitted players in Arizona.

When it comes to pure athleticism, the Northern Arizona commit might be the top in the state. But her improvement in her basketball skill set over the last year has been eye-opening. She's one of the best rebounders in the state, can get to the basket and make the open three.

On the court, she is a dynamic scorer. Averaged 20.3 points per game last season. Helped lead Tuba City to the Copper State championship, the first state title in 22 years for the school. Picked up an offer from Oklahoma State in the offseason. Off the court, she has quickly developed into a sensation on the Navajo Nation.

10. Ava Schooler, Phoenix Sandra Day O'Connor, G, Sr.

Physical on both sides of the ball. Can score from all levels efficiently. Plays calm under pressure, super composed. Was a key part of last season's run to the Open Division state championship game.

11. Amaya Rodgers, Chandler Hamilton, G, Jr.

Made the transfer from Queen Creek. The daughter of former ASU football star Derek Rodgers, who went on to play in the NFL, is forging her own path. She's a raw, athletic and physical wing who is just scratching the surface of what she can become as a player. Ability to play at all three levels.

12. Tahjie "TT" Freeman, Phoenix North, G, So.

An explosive, versatile and highly competitive player who continues to establish herself. Outstanding athlete – who also plays flag football – with the ability to impact the game on both ends. Offensively, she is a dynamic scorer who can create her own shot, attack the basket, finish through contact and score in a variety of ways. Defensively, her quickness and instincts allow her to disrupt opposing offenses.

13. Cadence Murdough, Gilbert Higley, F, Sr.

She's an elite athlete who's deceptively strong and faster than most at her size. Her hands and finishing ability around the rim are some of the best in the state. She plays with a high motor and rebounds aggressively. She spent this summer working on some of her perimeter play, including attacking off the bounce and extending her shooting range.

14. Terrais-Joi Coleman, Chandler Basha, G, Sr.

Uses her length extremely well to make plays offensively and defensively. She is confident with the ball in her hand, which allows her to make some elite passes. She has been working on extending her shooting range this offseason.

15. Mariah Brownlee, Goodyear Millennium, F, Jr.

Long, active, athletic wing who impacts winning in every way possible. Guards one through five. Great first step and finished well around the rim. Very good passer with the vision to match it.

Long, athletic guard who can defend multiple positions. The reigning 6A Fiesta region defensive player of the year. Deadly from the 3-point line. Gilbert's leading returning top scorer.

17. Destinee Benway, Chandler Hamilton, G, Jr.

The Air Force commit is a potent shooter who is improving from beyond the arc, shooting 34% from the 3-point line. She's turning into a deadly slasher and has a high IQ for the game. Developing into a true point guard.

18. Amira Reynolds, Chandler Basha, G/F, Sr.

Her ability to make plays on the defensive end due to her athleticism and IQ is impressive. She can guard all positions. A three-level scorer who hasn't played at her full potential yet.

19. Avery Olson, Scottsdale Horizon, C/F, Jr.

A 6-foot-2 wing who can play inside and out. She can run the point and post up. Her shooting has improved greatly and her range is tremendous. She dominates on the boards, averaging just under 10 rebounds a game last year. Her shot-blocking is first-class, with her length and timing.

The New Hampshire commit has turned herself into one of the top wings in the state. Her mix of shooting ability, passing, basketball IQ and ability to get to the basket makes her a tough player to guard.

21. Brooklyn Williams, Goodyear Millennium, G, So.

A complete guard who can handle it, shoot it and pass it very well. Great length and versatility. Defends extremely well. Great shooter.

22. Shannon McIlroy, Phoenix Sunnyslope, G, Sr.

A tenacious competitor who has worked hard to add to her game. Her playmaking ability is incredible and her defense can stifle opponents. She knows how to hit the big shot and find her teammates when it matters most.

23. Risi Lancaster, Phoenix Sandra Day O'Connor, G, Sr.

Intensity is her game. Plays fast with a motor that keeps on running. On-ball defense is elite. Creative offensive player who finds different ways to the rim.

One of the most prolific scorers in the state who also distributes the ball at a high rate. Put up a whopping 28.1 points per game last season, including one game with 58 points. Also had 11 games with at least 10 steals.

A big, strong guard who creates matchup problems for opposing teams. Give her a guard and she's going to bully them in the post; give her a post and she's going to pull them out and attack off the dribble with creative footwork and attacking. She has a very quick release, allowing her to get shots off without much space.
