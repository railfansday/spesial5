49ers vs. Vikings live updates: Start time, injury news for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

One of the biggest NFL games of the season takes place Sunday night at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City where the San Francisco 49ers will play host to the Minnesota Vikings.

A win for the 49ers will keep them in the driver's seat and in control of their own destiny for the No. 1 seed in the NFC. A loss for the 49ers would drop them into the quagmire of two-loss NFC teams and achieving the No. 1 seed would require San Francisco to continue winning and get some help around the league.

Niners Wire is bringing you live updates, scores and highlights from the game. Follow along.
