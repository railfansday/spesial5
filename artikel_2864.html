Why one NBA GM reportedly shied away from trading up to draft Yaxel Lendeborg

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Why one NBA GM reportedly shied away from trading up to draft Yaxel Lendeborg originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

A Western Conference general manager had Yaxel Lendeborg fifth on his 2026 NBA Draft board, considered trading into the top 10 to take him and then shied away, according to ESPN's Tim MacMahon.

The executive was not identified, and his biggest concern was Lendeborg's age.

"He has to be really good right away or it's a problem," the GM told MacMahon who also reported the unnamed exec's hesitancy to draft Lendeborg was "in part due to worries about how the rookie's 'goofy' personality would fit in his team's locker room."

Lendeborg turned 24 before making his NBA debut, which was the "biggest knock" on him among NBA execs, MacMahon reported. The last lottery pick that old was Chris Duarte, the 13th pick in 2021, who averaged 13.1 points as a rookie and was out of the league after four seasons. Lendeborg has addressed the age talk himself, saying that winning Rookie of the Year would come with a "he's 24" caveat no matter what.

So far, the Warriors rookie has not given anyone a reason to worry. The No. 11 pick ranks second among rookies in scoring (19.7 points) and rebounding (7.3) and fifth in assists (3.9) through 16 games, with MacMahon calling him the likely steal of the draft.

Another West executive told MacMahon it is hard to rule him out as the best player in the class, and an Eastern Conference executive told them he could not have predicted Lendeborg would be this good.

His shooting has helped. MacMahon noted that Lendeborg has improved as a 3-point shooter at every stop, from 33.3 percent in his first season at UAB to 37.2 percent at Michigan and 37.8 percent in the NBA, even with a slump since Steph Curry's injury made him the main focus of opposing scouting reports. Curry has an oblique abdominal tear and was set to be re-evaluated in two weeks.

For the GM who passed, the early verdict is clear: Lendeborg has been really good right away.
