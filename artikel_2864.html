Indianapolis Colts Injury Report: Week 9 Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Indianapolis Colts today released their Thursday injury report for Week 9 of the NFL season against the Dallas Cowboys.

Wide receiver Keenan Allen, running back Jonathan Taylor and cornerback Charvarius Ward were all full participants today after being rested yesterday. Defensive tackle Grover Stewart was rested today at practice.

Linebacker Akeem Davis-Gaither (knee) was again unable to practice today. Davis-Gaither has now missed both practices this week and looks more than likely to miss Sundays game against the Cowboys. If he is unable to go expect a rotation of Jaylon Carlies and Bryce Boettcher at the WILL spot with CJ Allen manning the MIKE.

Cornerback Justin Walley (knee) missed practice again. Today. Walley looks set to miss back to bs k games due to the knee injury. If Walley is unable to go expect rookie cornerback Japena Gushiken to start at the nickel spot in place of Walley.

Tight end Drew Ogletree (knee) was limited at practice today. Ogletree missed last weeks game and Wednesdays practice due to the injury so him managing a limited practice today is a positive step for him. He may not be able to play this week but it looks like he will return soon. Expect Tyler Warren, Mo Ali's-Cox and Will Mallory to rotate at the right end spots this week.
