Cunningham-Pemberton scores 17, Quinnipiac downs Bryant 66-56

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

HAMDEN, Conn. (AP) — Tayeshawn Cunningham-Pemberton had 17 points in Quinnipiac's 66-56 victory over Bryant on Thursday.

Cunningham-Pemberton shot 6 for 11 (3 for 6 from 3-point range) and 2 of 5 from the free-throw line for the Bobcats (1-1). Keith Mcknight scored 16 points and added nine rebounds. Nas Hart had 10 points and went 4 of 7 from the field.

Keegan Harvey finished with 14 points, two steals and two blocks for the Bulldogs (1-1). Onyx Nnani added 13 points for Bryant. Andre Pasha also had eight points, eight rebounds and two steals.

These two teams both play Sunday. Quinnipiac visits Vermont and Bryant hosts Fairfield.
