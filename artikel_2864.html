What’s next for Rangers after calling up Marc Del Gaizo from Hartford

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

After remaining intact for the first 14 games of the season, the New York Rangers defense corps continues to undergo plenty of change the past week or so. The latest move happened Sunday, when the Rangers recalled Marc Del Gaizo from Hartford of the American Hockey League.

This very well could be a precautionary move so that the Rangers have a healthy extra defenseman on the roster. Matthew Robertson slotted into the lineup Saturday for New York's 4-3 win over the Seattle Kraken because Alberts Smits was a late scratch due to an upper-body injury.

Del Gaizo's recall likely means that Smits isn't an option to practice or play, at least for a bit, so the Rangers needed another defenseman, especially with the team in the Pacific Northwest with three games remaining on the current road trip.

The Rangers practiced in Edmonton on Sunday ahead of their game Monday night against the Oilers. However, no reporters were at practice, therefore no injury-related updates were announced by the Rangers nor coach Mike Sullivan.

Sean Durzi landed on IR with an undisclosed injury this week, and Scott Morrow drew into the lineup after being a healthy scratch the first 14 games of the season. Morrow has one assist in four games with Durzi sidelined, and led the Rangers with five shot attempts (three on goal) against the Kraken.

When Durzi landed on IR, the Rangers recalled Robertson to be the extra defenseman. The 25-year-old played 72 games as a rookie last season, but lost his spot in the lineup this year to Smits, and his spot on the roster to Morrow.

Robertson partnered with Morrow on New York's third defense pair Saturday, and logged just under 12 minutes TOI. That was the first game Smits missed this season. The 18-year-old rookie from Latvia is averaging 14:29 TOI in 17 games and remains without a point so far in the NHL.

Without speculating too much, it appears Smits' injury could more than a game or two thing, and Durzi isn't quite ready to be activated off injured reserve just yet, even though he's currently on the road trip.

Each of New York's top-four defenseman played at least 20 minutes against the Kraken, leading one to assume that no one from the group is injured, leading to Del Gaizo's call-up.

Del Gaizo was named the 16th captain in Hartford Wolf Pack history a couple of weeks ago. The 27-year-old leads all Hartford skaters with nine assists in 15 games, and his 11 points are tops among the defensemen.

The Rangers signed him to a two-year, $1.75 million contract on July 1. The Basking Ridge, New Jersey, native immediately made his presence known with his physical play in training camp, where he got under the skin of Matt Rempe during practices and scrimmages.

That the 5-foor-10 defenseman didn't back down from the towering forward, who's almost a foot taller, showed the type competitor Del Gaizo is.

"I'm an Italian kid from New Jersey. So you know I have some piss and vinegar in my game," he told reporters during camp.

You might remember a certain game between the Rangers and Nashville Predators back in December 2024, when Del Gaizo got into it with another big, tough Blueshirts forward, Will Cuylle.

A fourth-round pick by the Predators in the 2019 NHL Draft out of UMass, Del Gaizo has 55 games of NHL experience — all with Nashville over two seasons.
