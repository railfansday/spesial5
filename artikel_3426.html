Apple to stream Las Vegas Formula 1 race for free in the United States

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Apple today announced that the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix will be streaming for free on the Apple TV app, no subscription required.

This means anyone in the US can log on to the Apple TV app on their device of choice and watch practice, qualifying and the race — without having to pay for an Apple TV subscription or anything else. The Las Vegas F1 Grand Prix is coming up next week, running Thursday through Saturday.

You can find all the racing content in the Formula 1 hub on the Apple TV app on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Android, Windows, Xbox, PlayStation and more platforms. You can also watch in a web browser at tv.apple.com.

The Las Vegas race is a night race, so you can expect the broadcast to portray the neon-lit atmosphere of the city.
