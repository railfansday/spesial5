Money Heist’s charm was putting together a group of criminals, who were then led by a charismatic, geeky professor into the most well-planned, meticulous, adrenaline-loaded heists. Berlin tried to keep up that same pace, too, using the wits of just one character, but it didn’t quite work out as well as its predecessor did. But another show, starring Omar Sy in the lead role, recently had its Season 4 rollout on October 23, 2026, and it managed to do what Berlin couldn’t. It managed to fill the same gap Money Heist left when it ended in 2021.

The show follows Assane Diop (Sy), a fictional French gentleman thief and master of disguise. Instead of the character directly being Arsène Lupin, a fictional character created in 1905 by writer Maurice Leblanc, Assane is actually inspired by the famous fictional character in the show and follows his mannerisms. The Netflix Original series is currently trending in the Top 10 (and that too, primarily within the Top 5) in dozens of regions across the world.

The show’s title is Lupin, and Lupin Season 4 picks up a bit after a time gap while Assane is still in jail and a woman is constantly framing him for thefts. Later on, he works with the police to figure out who the actual thief is. The bigger plot of Lupin Season 4, however, follows Assane and his daughter Adele Kapoor, played by Laïka Blanc-Francard, working together to get their hands on a bigger treasure.

Netflix has not yet renewed Lupin for a fifth installment. If renewed, the new season, as per previous patterns, will likely not arrive until 2028. For reference, Lupin Part 4 arrived on October 23, 2026, which was three years and 18 days after Part 3 premiered on October 5, 2023.

Lupin’s fourth installment, however, ends with a major cliffhanger which leaves room for a solid Part 5 story. Assane’s son, Raoul (Etan Simon), turns against his father out of long-standing resentment over his father's absences and jealousy toward Assane’s newly revealed daughter, Adèle. The season ends with Assane’s future left in question as his son coldly left him behind after getting his hands on the treasure.
