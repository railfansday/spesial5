When Are The Game Awards 2026: Date, Time, and How to Watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Game Awards is hitting some unexpected platforms

The biggest annual video game awards ceremony, The Game Awards, is almost here, likely bringing big wins, a few upsets, too many game trailers, and maybe even an acceptance speech or two.

The event will likely have some big game announcements. For context, last yearBaldur's Gate 3 developer Larian Studios announced its new Divinity game, Lucasfilm teased a new Star Wars Old Republic game from Mass Effect director Casey Hudson, multiple Tomb Raider sequels were revealed, and some of this year's nominees were shown for the first time or further teased.

As for the awards themselves, Grand Theft Auto 6 is the clear frontrunner in the Game of the Year conversation, but an outside upset from something like Control Resonant or Resident Evil: Requiem is always possible. 2025's TGAs felt more like a traditional award show, thanks to the organizers giving winners more time for acceptance speeches. Hopefully, that tradition will continue.

Here’s everything we know about The Game Awards 2026, including start times and where to watch.

The Game Awards 2026 begins on Thursday, Dec. 10 at 7:30 p.m. EST / 4:30 p.m. with a 30-minute pre-show. The show proper kicks off at 8 p.m. EST / 5 p.m. PST.

The Game Awards 2026 will be livestreamed via The Game Awards’ official channels on YouTube, Twitch, TikTok Live, Steam, X, Kick, Facebook Live, Instagram Live, andPS5’s Live TV. The show will also stream on Disney Plus and Roblox for the first time.

While The Game Awards organizers have not announced how long the 2026 event will be, the show typically runs for roughly three and a half hours, including the 30-minute pre-show. Last year's event ran for almost exactly that long.

The Game Awards announced the 2026 nominees on Nov. 17. Grand Theft Auto 6 is currently the clear frontrunner for this year's Game of the Year race. It is up for Best Action/Adventure Game, Best Narrative, and Best Art Direction, among other categories, showing voters were quite impressed with it across the board.

Meanwhile, Control Resonant, Resident Evil: Requiem, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time also have a chance at taking home the big prize. Resonant and Requiem are tied with six award nominations each, while Ocarina of Time is one of the best-reviewed games of the year.

Fans can vote for their favorite games on The Game Awards' official website. These votes will determine the Players' Voice award and contribute to what wins Game of the Year. Awards are determined by a blended vote, which is split between the voting jury (90%) and the public (10%).
