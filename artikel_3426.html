When it comes to setting up a home audio system, Sonos is a go-to for a reason. The brand makes everything from speakers to soundbars, and they’re all designed to work together, whether you’re setting up a multiroom music system or a home theater. But Sonos speakers typically command a steep price tag, which is why we’re always on the lookout for deals. Right now, Sonos speakers, headphones, and soundbars are on sale.

Discounts include savings on the Era 100, Sonos’ entry-level speaker, which works well as a standalone speaker or as part of a multi-room listening setup. The Era 300, Sonos’ oddly-shaped but powerful speaker, is also discounted, and you can save on soundbars like the Ray, the Beam, or the powerful Arc Ultra.

Sonos is also running discounts on bundles, letting you save more when you buy multiple speakers. You can check out the full list of deals here, or check out our picks below.

The new Ace Ultra headphones aren’t discounted during Black Friday, but you can take significant discounts on the first-generation Sonos Ace headphones. They feature up to 30 hours of battery life, with features like Dolby Atmos, lossless audio, and dynamic head tracking.

The Sonos Play speaker can be integrated with other Sonos speakers at home or taken on the go and used with Bluetooth. It’s waterproof and boasts up to 24 hours of battery life.

The Sonos Era 100, the brand’s entry-level speaker, is $179. In addition to WiFi, the Era speaker can be used with Bluetooth or a line-in connection. It can also be paired with a Sonos soundbar for a surround sound experience.

The Era 300 is one of Sonos’ most powerful speakers, with Dolby Atmos capabilities for room-filling sound. It’s currently $100 off.

The rugged Sonos Move 2 speaker features Bluetooth and WiFi capabilities, meaning you can use it around the house or on the go. Plus, the handy charging base makes it easy to remember to keep it powered up.

The Dolby-enabled Beam 2 Sonos speaker is suited for music and movies alike. Typically $499, it’s available for $339 right now.

Sonos’ powerful Five speaker can be used for streaming via WiFi, and the auxiliary cable lets you connect an external device like a turntable for listening to music. The speaker has handy touch controls, and you can stream with your preferred app, such as Spotify, Tidal, and Amazon Music.

The powerful Arc Ultra soundbar is 46″ wide, making it best suited for larger TVs and rooms. It features Dolby Atmos for an immersive audio experience, and it can be paired with devices like Sonos subwoofers for deeper bass.

Subwoofers aren’t just for homes with dedicated theater rooms. The compact Sub Mini boosts the bass without taking up too much space, and it pairs with either the Beam or Ray soundbars.
