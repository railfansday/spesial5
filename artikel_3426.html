GTA 6's First Big Patch Finally Fixes A Major Fan Complaint

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

One of GTA 6's most hated features literally left some players in tears

There's a lot to love in Grand Theft Auto 6, but many of those strengths were overshadowed by basic game features. Take, for instance, GTA 6's hated autosave feature. Normally, autosave is something that players don't have to worry much about as it happens entirely in the background. Yet GTA 6's implementation of this basic feature literally brought some players to tears.

The issue was one of frequency. Previously, GTA 6 seemed to mostly autosave between missions, or at major mission checkpoints. This meant that players could sometimes make lots of progress on ambient world tasks, like sticking up a store or going shopping, only to lose it all after closing the game down improperly or reloading.

GTA 6 is overflowing with distractions from the main missions, though — you could theoretically play it for hours on end without actually getting further into the story. Unfortunately, this led to some high-profile cases in which streamers and average players alike lost half a dozen hours or more of progress​​​​​​​. In one particularly egregious case, Twitch streamer JasonTheWeen lost 12 hours of progress and then was swatted​​​​​​​ shortly after the shocking discovery.

Fear no more, because Rockstar is on the case. One of the bullet points on the Nov. 23​​​​​​​ update reads, "Fixed an issue that resulted in the game not autosaving correctly." The update hasn't been out for long, so we can't verify exactly how GTA 6 addresses the issue. Given the nature of the complaints leading up to the update, though, it seems reasonable to assume that the game's autosave is not strictly tied to mission progress. Perhaps it even autosaves a little more often?​​​​​​​

Whatever the case, it's nice to see that Rockstar has its fingers on the pulse of what players want most. Beyond some nuts-and-bolts fixes​​​​​​​, GTA 6's first major patch also ensures that the game's dynamic weather system actually works as intended. Previously, players had put many hours into the game without seeing so much as a thunderstorm.

The 01.002.002 update is rolling out gradually for players on PlayStation 5 and Xbox, so if you don't see it yet, hang tight — and keep saving manually — and often​​​​​​​!
