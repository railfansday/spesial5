USDT buyers face 22% VAT when Moscow Exchange opens crypto trading

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Buyers will pay VAT on top of the trade price in December because Russia’s tax code has no explicit exemption for foreign digital instruments.

Buyers of Tether’s USDt (USDT) stablecoin will pay a 22% value-added tax (VAT) on top of the trade price when Moscow Exchange (MOEX) opens crypto trading on Dec. 1, according to the exchange’s clearinghouse.

The National Clearing Centre (NCC) said it would apply VAT to USDT purchases, sales and swaps through Dec. 31. A buyer purchasing 100,000 rubles worth of USDT would pay an additional 22,000 rubles in VAT.

Unlike Bitcoin (BTC) and Ether (ETH), which Russia classifies as digital currencies, USDT falls under a separate legal category known as foreign digital instruments. Russia’s tax code excludes sales of digital currencies from VAT, but it provides no explicit exemption for foreign digital instruments, according to the NCC.

The NCC said it had asked Russia’s Federal Tax Service and Finance Ministry for guidance. It said it would update market participants if it received clarification or if lawmakers exempted trades conducted before Jan. 1, 2027.

Bank of Russia rules that took effect Nov. 17 allow non-qualified investors to buy Bitcoin, Ether and USDT through intermediaries, with annual purchases capped at 300,000 rubles (about $3,650) per intermediary.
