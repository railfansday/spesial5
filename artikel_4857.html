Can Testosterone Add Inches to Your Penis?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

There are a lot of misconceptions about what testosterone boosters can and can’t do.

IF TESTOSTERONE HELPS increase your penis size during puberty, surely taking TRT will help you get a bigger package?

Unfortunately, testosterone works a bit differently once you’re out of your late teens. Here’s what to know about that.

Testosterone, which is produced by your testicles, “plays a key role in the development of characteristics like libido, and growth of the penis and testicles,” says Jamin Brahmbhatt, MD, urologist at Orlando Health. The hormone surges in your body during your tween and teen years to achieve all of these changes (along with deepening of the voice, facial hair, and so on).

The “blast” of testosterone is usually over by your mid- to late teens. Reproductive urologist Paul Turek, MD, says this is because fertility is achieved as the testicles and penis reach adult size.

YOUR PENIS DOES go through some interesting changes as you age, but it doesn’t get bigger.

After testosterone stops surging in your late teens, your sexual development is set for life. Genetics are the main reason your penis grows to its permanent size. “You’re born with the size already determined,” says Dr. Brahmbhatt.

Now testosterone is still being produced in your body when you’re an adult, but it won’t have any further effect on penis size. That biological fact holds true no matter your penis length. One study done by researchers at the University of Utah School of Medicine found that stretched penile length did not have any association with testosterone levels in adult men.

Since the penis consists of spongy tissue, not muscle, there’s no way to build it up on your own. “There are no safe and effective methods for permanently increasing penis size,” says Dr. Brahmbhatt. Penis pumps might help with stronger erections, but they do nothing for overall size.

WHILE TESTOSTERONE HAD an effect on your penis length early on, more won't help now. It might even hurt to take any sketchy T boosters or penis enlargement pills that promise to increase your girth or length. You have no idea whether they’re safe to take or whether they contain harmful ingredients.

A Stanford University study found that the average erect penile length has grown 24 percent worldwide over the past 29 years. The researchers report this is not due to an increase in testosterone levels.

Any change in development in a short amount of time is concerning. One of the authors of the study noted that chemical exposure could be interacting with the hormone makeup of guys—which may also explain lower sperm counts, increases in testicular cancer rates, and lower T levels in men nowadays.

The bottom line is that size doesn’t matter. Your penis health—especially function—does. If you notice any changes in how your penis looks or functions—patchy spots, bumps, curves—definitely don’t ignore them.

And while it’s natural to be curious about how your penis stacks up with other guys, don’t worry. The average penis length is not as big as you think.
