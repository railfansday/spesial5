Get the Business Edition® Visa® Card with Absolute Rewards® if you bank with Flagstar and want to move another bank’s card balance, since it gives 12 billing cycles at 0% on transfers and purchases. Without a balance to move, a no-fee card paying 2% on your first $50,000 a year beats this card’s 1.5% until your spending passes $100,000.

First National Bank of Omaha (FNBO) now issues Flagstar’s credit cards, and this is the rewards card in Flagstar’s business lineup, which no longer includes the old Visa Business Cash card. Flagstar’s other two business cards are a no-rewards card with 18 billing cycles at 0% and a secured card that earns 1 point per dollar.

It suits Flagstar customers paying down another bank’s card while earning on new purchases, since moving $10,000 at account opening costs a $500 fee and no interest through the first 12 billing cycles. Owners who pay in full earn more with the no-fee American Express Blue Business Cash Card until yearly spending passes $100,000, since it pays 2% on the first $50,000 a year and 1% after, against this card’s 1.5%.
