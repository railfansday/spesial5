Board games that make players pay bills, borrow, and invest let kids practice money decisions where a mistake costs only play money. Pick one that fits your player’s age and attention span. Afterward, ask why a move worked or didn’t, and connect a rent payment or loan in the game to your family’s own.

A kid can watch a parent pay with a tap of a card all week and never see a bill, a loan balance, or an investment account.

A board game puts all three in front of them. In PayDay, a month of bills comes due on payday, and in Monopoly, a mortgaged property stops collecting rent. The game shows a player what a choice cost. A question afterward about why they made it gets them to plan differently in the next game.

Each entry gives the ages its publisher recommends and what the game has players do with money.

Start with the ages on the box, then match the game’s length to how long your player will sit still. A game of Monopoly Deal can be over in 15 minutes, and Acquire runs about 90 minutes. PayDay’s rules let players choose how many months to play and say a three-month game with four players takes about an hour. Rich Dad says first-timers might need three to four hours for CASHFLOW.

For a shorter game of Monopoly, the rules in the box say to skip house rules: auction any property the player who lands on it won’t buy, never lend money between players, and put no cash on Free Parking.

For a preschooler, a pretend store with real coins is the place to start. A younger sibling can join an older one’s game as a teammate who handles the money.

Keep the talk short, and have it right after the game. Ask about one move that cost or made money, and let the player explain it before you add anything.

Picture a 10-year-old who comes up short at the end of a PayDay month. She borrows, then pays 10% of the loan every month after that. If she decides to set aside part of each month’s salary in the next game, she has worked out why a budget plans for bills before they arrive.

Tie the game to your family’s money too. A Monopoly rent payment is the moment to say what your household pays in rent or on its mortgage each month. A PayDay loan is the moment to show the interest rate on a credit card statement.

Set up a rematch within a week or two, so the player can try the change they described.
