Personal Consumer Issues • Re: When to replace yubikey

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

I actually replace Yubikey 4 with Yubikey 5 a long time ago when I needed to gain access to the new feature. I feel that when that time come, I will ugprade to the newer version. Also if it looks like the yubikey is malfunctioning, I would replace it.

The older version of the firmware only allow 25 keys and may not allow you to erase old ones. However, I only save critical passkey on the key so this is not an issue.

The older firmware has a known vulnerability that may allow someone with a lot of expensive equipment to clone my keys. I am not particularly bother by the vulnerabiklity because they still wouldn't know my pin and they would need expensive equipment that only government agency would have. I am not a high value target individual.

As for quantum hacks, I have to question if the quantum computing threat is overhyped, do we even have working quantum computer that people are using to do actual work or is it just mostly companies thumping their chest to get investment money. Company have been adding quantum resistent algorithm. However, we don't control much. I would be more worry about encryption on our drive and on your network than on yubikey.
