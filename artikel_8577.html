I have never been a fan of under counter microwaves. We use the microwave often, and I hate the idea of bending over to get things in and out.

Please share your experience and why you like it.
It's not under-counter. It's a countertop microwave on a built-in shelf above the built-in oven. Being a convection oven/combo microwave, it offers the availability of more convection oven space for large meals (thanksgiving) etc. It is mounted higher than counter height, so I suppose there is spill risk (as others have previously posted), but in 25 years we haven't had any issues. You don't have reach issues you have with an over-stove mounting. The microwave shelf height is at about 8 inches above the kitchen counter height, so it is quite comfortable for compartment access and controls.

The microwave is probably the most used appliance in our kitchen.
