‘VisionQuest’ May Have Just Revealed ‘Avengers: Doomsday’s Biggest Twist

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Editor's Note: The following article contains spoilers for the VisionQuest finale.

VisionQuest ended the "WandaVision trilogy" on a high note, reuniting Vision (Paul Bettany) with his sons Tommy (Ruaridh Mollica) and Billy (Joe Locke) while also giving him closure with his "father," Ultron (James Spader). Audiences were in for one more surprise during the credits, as VisionQuest addressed the lingering absence of one important character. The post-credits scene flashes back to the final moments of Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, where Wanda Maximoff (Elizabeth Olsen) seemingly sacrifices herself by demolishing Mount Wundagore.

The scene then reveals Doctor Doom (Robert Downey Jr.) approaching the rubble, declaring: "Scarlet Witch, awaken. Your power will be mine." Wanda jolts awake, seemingly teeing up the character's appearance in Avengers: Doomsday. This scene could also be connected to a long-running fan theory that Wanda will be part of the "Latverian Witches" who accompany Doom, as one of them wears a bright red cloak that perfectly fits her aesthetic. It might also confirm that Doom is collecting certain figures from across the Marvel Cinematic Universe that all share something in common.

Avengers: Doomsday has largely been shrouded in mystery, with only an official trailer and "special look" hinting at the menace that Doom poses to the Avengers and other heroes. However, recent MCU projects have been hinting that Doom is collecting different heroes. Avengers: Endgame Encore features a collection of post-credit scenes, including one depicting a battle between Doom and Bruce Banner (Mark Ruffalo). The post-credits for Spider-Man: Brand New Day also hint that Tom Holland's web-slinger is no longer on Earth, potentially teeing up a Spider-Man appearance in Doomsday or Spidey getting his symbiote suit in Avengers: Secret Wars. Avengers: Doomsday co-director Joe Russo previously hinted that Doom was looking for other heroes:

"I mean look, his interest may extend beyond just Banner. So, that's what we'll say about that."

So what's the connection between Banner, Wanda, and Peter? All three of them caused events that led to world-shattering "incursions," which threaten to destroy all reality. Banner used the Infinity Stones in Endgame to reverse Thanos' decimation of the universe, and took part in a "time heist" that led to the splintering of different timelines. Wanda "dream walked" across different worlds in Multiverse of Madness, which is revealed to be a cause of the incursions. Peter accidentally split open reality in Spider-Man: No Way Home, leading villains from different universes to invade the MCU. These incursions might have changed Doom's life, so he's targeting the people who caused them to force them into "correcting" their mistakes.

Four powers, and four completely different kinds of person behind them. Here is what you would wake up with, what it would cost you, and why the MCU keeps handing it to somebody exactly like you.

You get the power that is really about taking the hit so that nobody else has to. Strength in the MCU always lands on people who were already carrying too much, which is exactly why it suits you.

You get the power that never switches off. Spider-sense is a gift and a nervous system on permanent alert, and the people who get it in these films are always the ones who were already worrying about everybody.

You get the quietest power in the building, and quietly the most useful. Invisibility comes with force fields for a reason, because the people who prefer not to be seen are usually the ones holding a shield around everybody else.

You get the biggest and least comfortable power on the list, the one that runs directly on feeling. Chaos magic is not really a superpower in the MCU so much as an emotional state with consequences attached.

With Wanda now slated to play a role in Avengers: Doomsday and Avengers: Secret Wars, the question remains: what's next for the Scarlet Witch? The answer might lie in VisionQuest's ending, which sees Tommy and Billy reunite, with Tommy wondering whether Wanda can make the same kind of return he and his brother (and father) did. Most fans know Tommy eventually gets his answer in Avengers: The Children's Crusade by Allan Heinberg and Jim Cheung, which sees the Young Avengers on a quest to find a missing Scarlet Witch—and running afoul of Doctor Doom in the process.

The timing has never been more right for an adaptation of Avengers: The Children's Crusade, or a Young Avengers project. Most of the characters in that team have been introduced in previous MCU shows and projects, and having a younger group of heroes would provide a direct contrast to the Avengers' adventures. There's also Wanda Maximoff's current status in Marvel Comics canon; as a result of Doctor Doom's arrogance, she is now the Sorcerer Supreme of Earth. Having Wanda defend Earth from magical threats could be a great way for her to re-enter the MCU and build on the events of Doomsday.

VisionQuest is available to stream on Disney+. Avengers: Doomsday will premiere in theaters on December 18, 2026.
