Doomsday's Missing Avenger Snub Is Even Worse After Scarlet Witch's Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

While a lot of MCU fans might have been waiting for this to happen for a long time, Scarlet Witch's return from the dead for Avengers: Doomsday is still one of the biggest swings the franchise has taken in years. After her sacrifice at Mount Wundagore at the end of Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, bringing Wanda Maximoff back into the fold, and shockingly as a recruit of Doctor Doom, changes everything for the upcoming Avengers crossover. It also means we're going to see even more trauma piled onto an already tragic storyline, as Wanda will once again potentially be involved in major deaths.

Despite speculation of her return, at some point her late addition to the Avengers: Doomsday cast was genuinely a surprise, especially given how tightly Marvel has guarded the movie's biggest secrets. We may be in for more, as the Russo brothers have openly teased that the roster probably still isn't finished and there will be more surprises to come, even if they're only revealed when Doomsday is released.

There are some fairly major characters still missing from Avengers: Doomsday who fans expect to see: Captain Marvel being one of them, Deadpool and Wolverine, and even Spider-Man as a possibly wishful further inclusion, while the complete absence of the entire Young Avengers cast is just confusing at this point. With several heavy hitters still notably absent from the officially confirmed lineup, it feels like more characters will definitely turn up before the multiverse starts collapsing. But with Scarlet Witch back in play, one missing Avenger in particular stands out above the rest as a glaring omission.

Despite some rumours of his involvement, Clint Barton being left out of the Avengers: Doomsday cast so far has already been a bit of a tough pill to swallow for fans of the original Avengers. With the narrative suggesting that Doomsday and Secret Wars may be the farewell for the remaining original Avengers, him being snubbed doesn't really make sense. Now that Scarlet Witch is officially returning as one of Doctor Doom's personal weapons, Hawkeye's lack of involvement also means there's a big gap in the story.

Going back to Wanda's full debut in Avengers: Age of Ultron, she and Clint shared a genuine bond almost immediately, as he was positioned as the somewhat unwilling handler of the future Scarlet Witch and her brother, Quicksilver. He was positioned as the veteran hero who looked after them during the Sokovian firefight with Ultron and effectively stepped in as a surrogate father, especially after Pietro died. He also stood by her during the fallout of Captain America: Civil War and rescued her from isolation when the Avengers' fear locked her up.

From Wanda's point of view, assuming she still believes Vision is gone forever and her kids with him, Clint is arguably the most significant personal connection she has left in the world. Removing him from the equation robs Doomsday of an excellent emotional moment. Just imagine the gut punch of Clint stepping onto the battlefield to face Doctor Doom's forces and seeing a corrupted Wanda standing across the lines alongside Victor, without Hawkeye there to confront her or at least for an added reaction to what she has probably become. The movie loses a crucial human element that would ground one of its biggest in-universe shocks.

And it isn't just Clint Barton who was a glaring omission. Now that we know that Scarlet Witch is back, having Vision sit on the sidelines is now almost unthinkable too, especially after the events of VisionQuest's ending, thanks to a solo season.

White Vision has effectively had a total reset, with all of his original memories restored, and he has been reunited with both of his sons. He remembers Wanda, the life they built together, and the grief that drove Wanda towards the Darkhold in the first place. Leaving him out of Doomsday may be necessary at first to keep Wanda on Doom's side, but it makes zero narrative sense for them not to come back together.

Bringing both Hawkeye and Vision back into the fold also isn't just about ticking boxes for who's missing in Doomsday. It's about giving Scarlet Witch a path back to genuine redemption. Clint can be an advocate for her when some of the other heroes may defer back to suspecting that she's been evil all along, while Vision represents the future that she thought she'd lost. If Doom is weaponizing her trauma and isolation to use her as a weapon to his own ends, having Vision (ideally alongside both Billy and Tommy) be the one to break through his hold and pull her back from the brink is the best way to complete her redemption arc.

She cannot die again, and she needs and deserves this happy ending. Avengers: Doomsday already has enough spectacle; it needs this smaller, quieter moment of personal emotion to resolve one of the most complex and compelling story arcs in the entire franchise.

Avengers: Doomsday is scheduled to release worldwide on December 18.
