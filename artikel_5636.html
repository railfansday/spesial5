Jamie Foxx’s Halloween Festival Draws Police to His California Home After Reported Stabbing

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Police were called to Jamie Foxx's California home after a reported stabbing at his annual Halloween bash.

On Nov. 7, the Ventura County Sheriff's Office responded to a call at the sprawling mansion during the Hollywood star's party, dubbed Skvlk Fest.

As first reported by TMZ, a man was stabbed in the neck and had a superficial wound, which paramedics treated at the scene.

According to the outlet, one man was arrested in connection with the assault with a deadly weapon.

A video of the aftermath of the incident that began circulating on Nov. 8 showed that multiple people were put in handcuffs by police while everyone else ran out of the home.

The outlet reported that the home had several signs that indicated guests could not enter with  “real or fake weapons,” even if they were props for their costumes.

Skvlk Fest is the Halloween festival hosted by Foxx's daughter Anelise, 18, and featured carnival rides and performances from Tyga, Ty Dolla $ign, Sexyy Red, and more.

The actor shares his 18-year-old daughter with his ex Kristin Grannis. He is also father to Corinne, 32, whom he shares with his ex Connie Kline.

Although the event was meant to take place on Oct. 31, it was postponed due to heavy rains on Halloween.

This isn't the first time the event was disrupted by violence. Just last year, an attendee threw a glass bottle at rapper GloRilla.

The rapper had been performing when the bottle came flying. Foxx took the microphone and quickly came to her defense.

“Why would you do something like that?Why would you throw something at the stage, man? This is for free. Y’all don’t deserve this s--t. That’s f---ed up, bro. I’m so disappointed. I love y’all but hate whoever the f--- that was. That ain’t cool, bro. You throw some s--t at my house? That ain’t cool," he said.

Foxx resides in a 17,000-square-foot property, which is situated on 40 acres and located in Thousand Oaks, CA.

The home comes with 10 bedrooms, 12 bathrooms, a resort-style pool, sports courts, and a recording space.
