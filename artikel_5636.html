<a href="https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/payers/optum-rx-rolls-out-new-pharmacy-tools-aimed-transparency" hreflang="en">Optum Rx rolls out new pharmacy tools aimed at transparency</a>

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Optum Rx rolls out new pharmacy tools aimed at transparency" style="max-width:100%; height:auto; border-radius:8px;" loading="lazy">

Earlier this year, Optum Rx teased the rollout of new pharmacy tools designed to address medication cost and access.

Now, the company is offering a look at how those tools work.

The pharmacy benefit manager said Tuesday that the tools build on Optum's new and more transparent pharmacy model, which the company announced in May and shifts to a fee-based structure. The model moves away from more traditional approaches linked to manufacturer pricing and prescription volume and offers clients a clearly defined fee structure.

The first platform, called Shop MyScript, allows members to compare prices for their medications as well as their options for pharmacies or delivery before the prescription is dispensed. The tool will notify members of potential cost savings shortly after a script is written.

In addition, Shop MyScript embeds in the prescribing workflow, Optum said, so that the member does not have to make comparisons after the fact. If they choose to keep the existing prescription, it will then be sent to the pharmacy.

Optum said that early adopters have averaged $28 in savings through the program.

"People should have clear information about their medication costs and options when they can actually use it, not after a decision has already been made," said Katte Carey, president of Optum Rx, in the announcement. "These tools give people more information earlier in their journey, so they can make choices that work for them and get the support they need along the way."

In addition, the pharmacy benefit manager announced Optum Assistant, a conversational AI guide that helps members complete routine tasks and track down key information. The tool can triage concerns on a range of topics, from questions about medication and benefits to pharmacy experience challenges.

If the AI guide can't address the individual's needs, they can choose to be connected to live support to find a solution. Optum said about 80% of conversations using the tool led to a resolution without needing a live advocate.

The final new launch aims to support cash-pay options, according to the announcement.

Optum Price Wise will offer members a complete cost breakdown, including the price of a drug and any fees. That total, all-in price allows members to fully understand what their options may be and make the choice that works best for them at the pharmacy counter.
