GTA 6 Is Officially Making Boyfriends and Husbands Step Up Their Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Lucia's GTA 6 missions are actively encouraging players to be more thoughtful partners

At its heart, Grand Theft Auto 6 is a game about a relationship between two people, Jason and Lucia, trying to lead a good life while navigating an unjust system. Controversially, early segments of the game give the players the option to do nice things for their partner, like buying Lucia jewelry and flowers. Those missions gained a surprising amount of mainstream attention due to the contrast between inattentive boyfriends treating their digital girlfriends "better" than their living, breathing partners.

It seems that the game's thoughtful message has gotten through to some of its players, judging from social media posts and reports from real-world florists.

In one Instagram post with over a million views, the account pappasparlor shows someone reaching into the flower display at a local grocery store. "Getting my wife some flowers since I already bought a bouquet for Lucia," reads the text overlaid on the footage.

In another viral Instagram post with 2.8 million views, a woman named Montana recorded herself holding a bouquet of roses while her partner sat on the couch holding a PlayStation 5 controller. "POV: he bought Lucia flowers in GTA 6 so he bought me flowers too," the post reads.

Yet another popular Instagram post shows a young man walking into the frame holding up a display of pink flowers in a vase. "Got a haircut and brought her flowers since I got a haircut and bought flowers for Lucia in GTA 6."

Of course, some of these posts might be performative or exist​​​​​​​ for clout — especially if the account is one focused on relationships to begin with. The phenomenon is widespread enough to be noticeable, which means that average GTA 6 fans are being influenced as well.

Over the weekend, one popular post on Threads by @muvashutta described a scene at a grocery store that was so jarring​​​​​​​, they got worried they had forgotten​​​​​​​ about an important flower-related occasion, like Mother's Day. "There is an unprecedented number of men buying flowers in this Publix tonight," the post stated.

The post itself does not attribute the sighting to GTA 6, but the comments — which include a real florist — do.

"CAN CONFIRM," reads a reply from a florist on muvashutta's post. "The boys are buying flowers for their Lucias."

Another commenter claimed that they saw "three youngish men picking out flowers at Publix tonight and the produce guy walked by them and said Ooooooo Lucia will appreciate this!"

Sensing an opportunity, some flower shops are goading men to take action as well. In a recent advertisement by Atelier Flowers, the company used an AI-generated image of a GTA protagonist walking into a florist. "GTA 6 can wait ... but your girlfriend's flowers can't. Gentlemen, consider this your next mission: keep your girl happy. Skip the wanted level and earn some boyfriend points instead."

​​​​​​​While there are many posts from flower shops on social media, an unexpected number of these posts​​​​​​​ aren't directed at boyfriends or husbands. Florists are instead targeting women by suggesting that if their boyfriend can buy GTA 6 or treat Lucia well, then they can afford a real bouquet for their loved ones as well. One video showing a flower arrangement states, "If he remembered to pre-order AND pre-download GTA 6 he can remember to pre-order your flowers."

While some men are learning how to be more thoughtful partners, others are experiencing a role reversal. Steven Trombetta on X.com recently shared a photograph of a colorful floral arrangement​​​​​​​ tied to a heart-shaped balloon. The bouquet was themed around GTA 6's primary colors. "My wife bought me flowers to celebrate the game's launch ... I'm the luckiest man in the world."

Players weren't expecting that keeping Lucia happy would require any actual work
