Digimon Alysion Passes 200,000 Registrations With New Rewards Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Digimon isn't a new TCG, but a brand-new mobile version of the game, Digimon Alysion, is coming before the end of 2026, and the pre-registration phase just hit a major milestone.

It's launching in a market stacked with competition, but Digimon has a pretty hardcore fanbase behind it, and this new app will make the game far more accessible to new players who have never played and returning players who haven't engaged with the game for an extended period of time. Fans have been waiting a long time for this game to finally launch, so we expect there is a very good chance that these new milestones will be hit.

Pre-registration for Digimon Alysion started on October 27, 2026, meaning this huge milestone has taken just over a month to achieve, and developer Bandai Namco isn't stopping there. There are rewards laid out to cover up to 400,000 pre-registered users, ranging from in-game tickets to a guaranteed card, Omnimon.

The game is available to pre-register on the App Store and Google Play, and the total number of registrations covers the cumulative figures from both platforms.

Four rewards from previous milestones are locked in and will be delivered to every player who pre-registers when they first open the app. These rewards include more of the same, tickets, and guaranteed cards. There is also a separate reward scheme running that is related to the number of players who have joined the official Discord server. If 10,000 members join the community by November 30, all players who log into the game will receive five BD-03 tickets.

Digimon fans will soon have their own Pokemon TCG Pocket competitor.

It's been confirmed that Digimon Alysion includes plenty of single-player content, including a Story Mode where players earn rewards as they get more familiar with the game. From there, multiplayer modes will make up the majority of the game, ranging from casual to competitive modes.

Early looks at the game show a lot of similarities to Yu-Gi-Oh! Master Duel, at least in the way that content is presented and menu navigation works.
