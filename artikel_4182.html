Bethesda Will Sell You Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel T-Shirts and Hoodies, Even Though It Hasn't Shown the Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

You won't play The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel for at least another two years, Bloomberg journalist Jason Schreier claims, but that's not stopping Bethesda from selling you something today: merchandise.

Yes, not a single second of gameplay — or any actual footage of the RPG whatsoever — has been made public.

However, the Official Bethesda Gear Store will still sell you a t-shirt or a hoodie with the game's logo plastered across it.

The t-shirt is $34.99 and the hoodie is $64.99 before shipping:

"Your next journey through Tamriel will be in The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel. Show off your excitement with the official tee," the t-shirt description states. Do you plan on showing off your excitement by purchasing a piece of merch for a game you haven't seen in action yet?

Of course, plenty of developers sell merch for their games, but you'd think they would wait for it to actually be out before doing so, right? Or at least show the title off first? Still years away, Bethesda is straight in there with Sentinel t-shirts and hoodies.

The merch isn't even exciting: it's literally just a t-shirt with the logo on it — you're basically just a walking advertisement if you wear it.

All we know is The Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel is set in the Hammerfell region, and the suggestions are it's still years away from releasing. In the meantime, you can wear the logo across your chest.
