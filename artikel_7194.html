Dr. Alex Cooper Prescribes Less Screen Time in Google Pixel&#8217;s Holiday Ad

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Dr. Alex Cooper will see you now. Well, at least she will in Google Pixel’s holiday campaign.

The Call Her Daddy host and founder of media brand Unwell takes a starring role in Pixel’s “Phonehead Therapy” campaign, putting a fun spin on the digital distractions that can pull people away from living in the moment during the holidays.

The hero film features actors Sam Lerner and Victoria Justice as a husband and wife seeking help from “Dr. Cooper” for the former’s addiction to his phone screen.

A post shared by Alex Cooper (@alexandracooper)

Channeling her mom, Laurie Cooper, who is an IRL therapist, Cooper prescribes the Pixel 11 Pro as the antidote to Lerner’s character’s “Phonehead Syndrome.”
