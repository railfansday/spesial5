New eyes in the sky make timber harvesting and forest conservation easier and more efficient

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Flying thousands of feet above northern and eastern Maine, Dave Sandilands searched for illegally dumped trash. On behalf of the New England Forestry Foundation, the University of Maine pilot was surveying both the 760,000-acre (308,000-hectare) Pingree Easement in northern Maine, home to the state's most productive peregrine falcon nesting area, and 335,000 acres (136,000 hectares) in Washington County through the Downeast Lakes Forestry Partnership.
