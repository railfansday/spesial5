How likely West Ham are of selling Konstantinos Mavropanos revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

West Ham are determined to retain Konstantinos Mavropanos beyond the January transfer window after he was linked with a move away.

The centre-back has been key to the Hammers’ this season, scoring five goals in 13 games, as well as putting in some dominant displays at the back, even if he has struggled at times recently during their poor run.

Nevertheless, such form overall, combined with how Mavropanos performed under Nuno Espírito Santo last season, meant interest in the Greek international was inevitable.

It has been claimed that West Ham could be open to cashing in on the 28-year-old in the New Year, with Besiktas said to be admirers.

As well as that, with his contract expiring in 2028, it could be a time to get maximum value for the player.

However, Claret & Hugh have stated emphatically that Mavropanos is not for sale going into the January window.

Furthermore, they explain that whilst the ex-Stuttgart man will enter the final 18 months of his contract in the New Year, the Londoners have an option to extend that by another year, which eases any fears about needing to sell.

This is the stance that you would expect West Ham to take, as the reality is that they can’t afford to cash in on any key players during the winter window.

This season hasn’t gone the way that the Hammers had expected so far, but they are still firmly in the race for a top two finish.
