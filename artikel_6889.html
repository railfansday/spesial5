The First <i><em>Day of the Jackal </em></i>Season 2 Teaser Is Finally Here

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Peacock’s hit thriller is on the way, with Eddie Redmayne once again taking the lead as Frederick Forsyth's infamous assassin.

With James Bond back in the headlines—at long last, we finally have our 007, even if a certain crowd is upset about it—The Day of the Jackal is also gearing up for a return.

Based on Frederick Forsyth’s bestselling 1971 thriller, the Sky and Peacock series—starring Eddie Redmayne as the world’s most elusive assassin—earned much acclaim when it arrived in 2024. Now the British actor is reprising the role in a second season arriving next March.

Peacock has just released a first-look teaser, taken from the opening of the new series. Plot details are being kept under wraps for now, but the Jackal is back in action, and there should be plenty of globetrotting, elaborate schemes, and unfortunate encounters for anyone who gets in his way.

If the first season of The Day of the Jackal is anything to go by, Peacock has another hit on its hands. After its first 75 days on streaming, NBCUniversal reported that it was the most-watched new original drama series ever recorded on Peacock. Plus, Redmayne earned Golden Globe, Critics’ Choice and SAG Award nominations for his performance.

Redmayne won’t be the only familiar face returning. Úrsula Corberó, Charles Dance and Chukwudi Iwuji are also back, while Matt Bomer, Pablo Schreiber, Weruche Opia and Pia Tjelta join the cast. That’s quite a few new faces for the Jackal to get acquainted with.

The Day of the Jackal season 2 arrives on Peacock in March 2027.
