Harry Shum Jr. Confirmed His ‘DWTS’ “Valerie” Performance Is a Direct Nod to ‘Glee’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Dancing With the Stars semifinals kick off tonight, which means six couples are still in the running for the coveted Mirrorball trophy. I’m sorry to everyone else competing, but the night already belongs to Harry Shum Jr.

Before Harry graced our screens on Grey’s Anatomy, he had a major role on Glee until the show ended in 2015. Day-one Gleeks know he played their go-to dancing dude, Mike Chang. He performed memorable numbers during his time on the show, and his dances in the DWTS semifinals seem to call back to his days in the William McKinley High School glee club.

Not only is he bringing us back to Mr. Shue and Emma’s synchronized swimming proposal with a paso doble to “We Found Love” by Rihanna feat. Calvin Harris, he also confirmed that he and Jenna Johnson’s quickstep to “Valerie” by Mark Ronson feat. Amy Winehouse is a direct callback to that iconic sectionals performance. Pls indulge while you’re here:

In an Instagram Story on Monday, November 16, Harry said (via Entertainment Weekly), “I wanted to also just clarify, I’ve seen you guys’ excitement from ‘Valerie,’ and it’s so much fun. It’s so much fun to hear it. And, you know, what we made sure to get is the Glee version to be able to hear the [a cappella moments].”

While his late co-star, Naya Rivera, covered the song on the Emmy-winning show, Harry confirmed that her vocals will not be featured in tonight’s performance.

“It’ll be sung by the band, the wonderful Dancing With the Stars band,” he added. “And just that energy, feeling that for a quickstep…It’s gonna be beautiful. Can’t wait for y’all to see it.”

Ahead of the semifinals, he shared a clip of him and Jenna dancing to Naya’s version of the song during rehearsals.

“We are soooo excited!!!!!” he captioned the post. “Been holding onto this song, hoping we would make it to this point!! SEMIFINALS BABY!!!!!”

My Gleek heart truly can’t handle this. Here’s to hoping some New Directions make an appearance, too!
