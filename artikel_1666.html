Arsenal dropped points from a winning position for the second time in as many games following a 1-1 Champions League draw with Borussia Dortmund at the Emirates Stadium.Mikel Arteta’s men led at the interval after Viktor Gyokeres scored his first goal on home soil in 206 days with a wonderful 25-yard opener inside 10 minutes.

But just as they did in the Premier League at Newcastle on Saturday, Arsenal were unable to see out the victory, with Maximilian Beier netting for the visitors after half-time. Jobe Bellingham had a goal ruled out for offside, while Gyokeres could perhaps count himself fortunate not to give away a penalty when a Dortmund corner struck him on the arm.

Gyokeres then wasted a golden opportunity to restore Arsenal’s advantage when he headed wide unmarked from six yards, while the ball cannoned off Serhou Guirassy to twice strike the Dortmund post in the closing stages. Declan Rice hit the woodwork again for Arsenal with five minutes left.
