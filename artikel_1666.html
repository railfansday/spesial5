Leeds injury, suspension list and return dates vs. Tottenham Hotspur: Joe Rodon, Noah Okafor latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

By Darren Plant | 04 Nov 2026 11:40

Leeds United are back in action on Saturday afternoon when they play host to Tottenham Hotspur in the Premier League.

Despite making a strong start to the campaign, Leeds now sit in 12th position in the Premier League table courtesy of winning just two of their opening nine matches.

However, last weekend's defeat to Bournemouth represented just the second top-flight loss for Daniel Farke's side in 2026-27.

Here, Sports Mole rounds up all of Leeds' latest injury and suspension news before welcoming the in-form North Londoners to Elland Road.

Mateo Joseph has not played since sustaining a knee injury on loan at Mallorca earlier this year, and the attacker remains a long-term absentee for Leeds.

Possible return date: November 7 (vs. Spurs)
