Barcelona vs. Villarreal: Head-to-head record and past meetings ahead of La Liga clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

By Matt Law | 20 Nov 2026 09:23, Last updated: 20 Nov 2026 09:23

Barcelona will resume their 2026-27 La Liga campaign with a home fixture against Villarreal on Sunday afternoon.

Hansi Flick's side are currently top of the La Liga table, boasting a record of 11 wins and one defeat from their 12 matches to collect 33 points, which has left them three points ahead of second-placed Real Madrid at this stage of proceedings.

Barcelona have won their last three games in all competitions, including a 2-1 success over Atletico Madrid in their last fixture before the November international break.

Villarreal, meanwhile, have a record of four wins, three draws and five defeats from their 12 league matches this season to collect 15 points.

The Yellow Submarine are currently eighth in the division, three points behind sixth-placed Sevilla.

Here, Sports Mole looks at the head-to-head record between Barcelona and Villarreal ahead of their clash at Camp Nou this weekend.

Barcelona have tackled Villarreal on 58 occasions throughout history, and they comfortably lead the head-to-head record, posting 36 wins to Villarreal's 12, while there have also been 10 draws.

Lionel Messi is the all-time leading goalscorer in this fixture, having found the back of the net on 16 occasions against Villarreal during his time at Barcelona, while Neymar is second on nine.

As for the Yellow Submarine, former Manchester United and Atletico Madrid star Diego Forlan is their leading goalscorer against Barcelona, having netted five times against the Catalan outfit during his Villarreal career.

In La Liga, there have been 52 meetings, with Barcelona's winning 32 times to Villarreal's 11, while the points have been shared on nine occasions in Spain's top flight.

Villarreal incredibly recorded a 5-3 victory away to Barcelona in January 2024 and 3-2 win in May 2025, either side of a 5-1 triumph for the Catalan team in September 2024.

However, they were most recently downed 2-0 by La Blaugrana at Estadio de la Ceramica in December 2025, with centre-back Renato Veiga being sent off for a reckless tackle on Lamine Yamal, who went on to score the second for Hansi Flick's side.

Villarreal's last three wins over Barcelona have all come away from home, and they have not actually managed to beat the Catalan side in front of their own supporters since October 2007.

Barcelona's dominance in this fixture is perhaps best highlighted by the fact that they have won 12 of the last 15 La Liga meetings between the two clubs, even if Villarreal have enjoyed strong spells such as when they recorded three straight victories between April 2007 and March 2008.

The last meeting between the two sides took place at Camp Nou in February 2026, with Barcelona recording a 4-1 win.

Barcelona will now be looking to post a third straight victory over Villarreal.
