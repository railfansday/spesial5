Play-Doh is releasing an adult ACOTAR flower bouquet kit — how to buy yours

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

TL;DR: Hasbro’s new $49.99 adult Blooms by Play-Doh craft kit lets fans sculpt a 15-flower ACOTAR centerpiece complete with a book-cover vase and Truth-Teller dagger. Preorders are live at Amazon ahead of its Jan. 2027 release.

If you are still daydreaming about starlit skies and shadowed mountain courts, your bookshelf is about to get a major upgrade. Just a week after the arrival of A Court of Splintered Harmony, Hasbro is giving Sarah J. Maas readers a whole new way to step into Prythian: an adult floral craft kit under its new Blooms by Play-Doh line, designed specifically for ACOTAR fans.

Aimed at crafters 17 and up, the all-in-one set lets you build an entire 15-flower arrangement from scratch — no glue guns, wire clippers, or sculpting experience needed. Everything you need comes in the box, making it easy to spend an evening decompressing without staring at a screen.

Rather than classic neon clay, the kit uses a moody, Night Court–inspired color palette packed with enough compound to build 12 large blooms alongside three smaller, hand-formed accent flowers.

Putting the entire bouquet together is surprisingly simple, guided by an illustrated booklet filled with full-color photos and tips. You place the compound into the blooming tool, press down, and watch the petals open up automatically as you shape them. Once the sculpted petals dry, you can mount them onto the included wire stems and weave in artificial greenery sprigs to fill out the volume.

A light mist of the included finishing spray seals the compound to prevent cracking, fading, or drooping on your shelf over time. The completed arrangement slots directly into a clear display vase styled after the series' book covers, topped off with a miniature Truth-Teller dagger pick that slides between the stems for a sharp fantasy accent.

Whether you are looking for a weekend craft project, a centerpiece for your next book club meetup, or an activity for a bachelorette party, locking in a pre-order is the safest bet to dodge the inevitable sellouts that usually hit Maas merch.
