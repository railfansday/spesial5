The 45 best Black Friday tech deals actually worth buying, picked by a tech editor on a budget

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Thanksgiving Day is done. Finito. The turkey is eaten, the potatoes mashed, the football won (Editor's note: Go Bills!), and the Macy's parade, erm, postponed. All of which means Black Friday 2026 is officially here.

As Mashable's tech editor, I get to review some of the coolest gadgets in the world — and I genuinely love my job. The downside to covering tech in 2026? Having to constantly update Mashable readers on an endless stream of price hikes. Well, Black Friday is your chance to get some well-deserved relief for your wallet.

The best Black Friday tech deals are counteracting many of the price hikes from the past 12 months, and in some cases, these deals go even further. I know a lot of people are wondering whether the discounts will be any good this year. Let me just say that so far, I've been pleasantly surprised. I've been covering Black Friday sales as a journalist since 2019, and this year, I've found discounts of 50% and up on flagship LG OLED TVs, noise-cancelling headphones from Bose, and new Amazon Echo products.

Some of my favorite tech gadgets are also on sale. I'm talking about items I've personally tested and reviewed and now use all the time — in some cases every day — including the DJI Osmo Pocket 3, Apple MacBooks, Kindle Paperwhite e-readers, and the Sonos Arc Ultra soundbar. I also found discounts on trending products and holiday gift ideas, like the Kodak Charmera blind box (another personal favorite of mine).

This year, Black Friday falls on Friday, Nov. 27 (the day after Thanksgiving). However, the sales actually started a lot sooner. Amazon and Best Buy launched their official Black Friday 2026 sales on Nov. 19, Target began its savings event on Nov. 22, and Walmart kicked things off on Nov. 23.

Cyber Monday falls on Monday, Nov. 30 this year. While some stores will have a one-day-only sale on that date, most online retailers have evolved into offering a full "Cyber Week" sales event.

This year, you can find discounts of up to 50% or more on Bose QuietComfort Headphones, Hisense and LG TVs, and Amazon Echo smart home devices. You can also find big discounts on virtually every Apple products, including record-low prices on the entire Apple Watch lineup, plus savings on MacBook and AirPods. For budget shoppers looking for the best tech deals under $50, we recommend checking out the JLab JBuds Lux ANC Headphones and Anker charging products.
