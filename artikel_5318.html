Major crypto exchange Coinbase shared the options-implied seven-day ratios for major cryptocurrencies, highlighting that options traders might be preparing for an unusually large price move for XRP.

In an X post, Coinbase Markets shared the outcome of dividing the options-implied 7-day move by the median 7-day move for XRP, Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana.

Options-implied 7d move, divided by each asset's own median 7d move: $XRP 1.94× $BTC 1.69× $ETH 1.56× $SOL 1.46× $XRP is priced furthest above its own history, $SOL the least. Which one is asking for more than its own history delivered? https://t.co/WgWbqjcgwI

This suggests how much movement options markets are pricing in relative to each asset's own historical behavior. However, it does not compare their expected dollar or percentage moves directly.

According to the figures shared by Coinbase, XRP's options-implied seven-day move stands at 1.94 times its own historical median seven-day move. Bitcoin follows at 1.69 times, while Ethereum records 1.56 times and Solana comes in at 1.46 times.

XRP, therefore has the highest relative reading among the four assets, suggesting options traders are pricing in a more pronounced move than the token has typically delivered over a comparable period.

At the time of writing, XRP was down 1.93% in the last 24 hours to $0.98 amid the continued profit-taking in the market. XRP briefly reached $1 on November 18, 2026, but fell afterwards as traders took profits. XRP is down 21.57% so far in November, but its history says more than its current price action does.

XRP’s average November gain is skewed upward by massive outlier rallies—specifically +550% in 2013 and +282% in 2024. Its median November return is about -10%, meaning it has closed lower in most cycles in history. XRP dropped roughly 23.42% in November 2019 and lost 12.18% in November 2025 (opening at around $2.51 and closing at around $2.20).

A weak November doesn’t always mean a weak December: for instance, after falling 9.1% in November 2015, XRP jumped 46.8% in December 2015.

In the near term, XRP’s price recovery will target $1 before moving to the $1.24–$1.26 range. Alternatively, if the price falls further, it is likely to find support at $0.89.
