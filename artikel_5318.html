Anthropic stock wavers as rest of AI sector rallies on Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Nvidia stock jumped 30% after Jensen Huang's reinstatement, while Anthropic stock was 6% above its $83 IPO price at 9:48 a.m. ET

Michael Nagle / Bloomberg via Getty Images

Anthropic stock wobbled between gains and losses in early trading Monday as other AI-related stocks rallied after Nvidia $NVDA reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, according to Bloomberg. Anthropic traded at $87.98 as of 9:48 a.m. ET, 6% above its $83 IPO price.

The stock was little changed in its third session after spiking briefly before the market opened. It rose 17% when it first traded Thursday, then fell 8.5% by Friday's close.

Nvidia stock jumped 30% Monday after a slump of more than 40% over the past month. Alphabet $GOOGL stock rose 6% and Meta $META stock gained more than 5%, while Amazon $AMZN stock moved close to 5% higher and Microsoft $MSFT stock advanced 4%. SpaceX stock rose 9% and AMD $AMD stock added over 8.5%. Nebius stock rose more than 30%, while CoreWeave stock and Iren stock each gained 28%.

The Nasdaq $NDAQ opened more than 6% higher Monday after a 10% slide last week, its worst week since 2020, according to CNBC. Concern about how quickly AI spending is growing pressured stocks last week, according to Bloomberg. Anthropic's filings show it has pledged to spend nearly $901 billion through 2045.

David Busch, chief investment officer at Trajan Wealth, compared Anthropic's trading with SpaceX's debut in June. "With the Anthropic volatility, I'm not as concerned there," Busch told Bloomberg in an interview Friday. "This typically happens on an IPO."

SpaceX stock peaked at 67% over its IPO price, then slipped under that level within six weeks of debuting, according to Bloomberg. SpaceX stock was under its offering price on Friday.

Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO on Nov. 12, days before Anthropic's public offering. Nvidia has committed roughly $10 billion to purchase Anthropic stock in a $26 billion private placement priced at the IPO price. Nvidia named Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress interim CEO while its board searched for a permanent successor.

Anthropic opened trading on the Nasdaq at $111.50 on Thursday under the ticker ANT, 34% above its IPO price. It closed that day at $97.17.
