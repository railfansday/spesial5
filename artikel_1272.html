Belgium vs. Italy – Official schedule, including Azzurri tribute to Heysel victims

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Italy national team trained in Milan on Friday morning and will fly to Belgium this afternoon, where the official schedule includes a tribute to the victims of the Heysel tragedy.

Italy trained this morning, Saturday, November 14, at the Inter training centre ahead of their away Nations League match against Belgium, while their opponents are training at Proximus Basecamp in Tubize as we write.

Later today, at 14:00 CET [13:00 GMT], Belgium coach Mark Van Bommel will hold a pre-match press conference, joined by a Belgian player.

The Azzurri fly to Brussels from Milan at 16:55 CET, while the pre-match press conference and the Walk Around at the King Baudouin Stadium are set for 19:00 CET.

As usual, Mancini will talk to the media alongside an Italy national team player.

As reported by Sky and Tuttosport, on arriving at the King Baudouin Stadium, Italy’s players and staff will pay tribute to the victims of the 1985 Heysel disaster, in which 39 Juventus fans died before the European Cup final between the Bianconeri and Liverpool.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
