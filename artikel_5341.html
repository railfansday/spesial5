The rally Bitcoin made after the US midterm elections is gone. BTC is trading near $76,115, down 5.8% in 24 hours and 3.1% over the past week, according to market data. Ethereum has fared worse, dropping 7.8% to $2,242.

With buyers struggling to hold the line, investors are asking how far Bitcoin could fall before it finds a real bottom.

In the near term, $76,680 to $78,117 is the first resistance zone. If Bitcoin stays below $78,117 on its next bounce, that would support the case for another leg lower. A move above it would make the picture more complicated and could mean a longer sideways phase.

The outlook also carries a bullish scenario. If the correction ends inside the $63,326 to mid-$70,000s support zone, the next rally could be the strongest of the current cycle. A standard projection from a low near $67,868 points to a first target above $100,000.

Bitcoin would first need to set a low and then show a strong five-leg rally from it to confirm the bottom is in.

For now, there is no confirmed bottom. Bitcoin could stay in its $75,600 to $81,238 range for a while longer, or slide into the deeper $63,000 to $75,000 zone before buyers return. A daily close above $81,238 would be the first real sign that the worst is over.
