Nvidia sheds more than 3% premarket a day after closing 30% higher on Huang's return

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Nasdaq-100 futures are down 2% after a Monday rally that lifted the S&P 500 by 4.7% and the Nasdaq Composite by 6.5%

Photo Illustration by Matteo Della Torre/NurPhoto via Getty Images

Nvidia $NVDA stock shed more than 3% in premarket trading on Tuesday, a day after it jumped 31% on the return of Jensen Huang as CEO.

The Monday gain boosted Nvidia's market value by close to $1 trillion and pulled other semiconductor shares higher. By the close, the Nasdaq $NDAQ Composite had gained 6.5%, with the S&P 500 up 4.7% and the Dow Jones Industrial Average up 3%. It marked the strongest single day for both the Nasdaq and the S&P 500 since April 9, 2025, according to CNBC.

U.S. stock futures fell in early trading. Nasdaq-100 futures were 2% lower and S&P 500 futures fell 1%. Dow futures slid 259 points, a 0.5% drop.

Anthropic stock fell more than 4% in premarket trading, dipping below its IPO price.

Monday's rally reversed a steep sell-off on Friday. The S&P 500 and the Dow closed in correction that day as AI-related stocks fell and Nvidia stock dropped.

Tom Hulick, CEO of Strategy Asset Managers, told CNBC's "Squawk Box Europe" that Nvidia has "a lot of things to worry about" on corporate governance.

Markets in Asia closed mostly higher after the first trading day following Huang's return. South Korea's Kospi rose 6.3% to 5,006.03, and Taiwan's Taiex, home to chipmaker TSMC $TSM, rose 9%. Mainland China's CSI 300 gained 0.1% to 3,963.82. Hong Kong's Hang Seng Index traded 0.6% down during the final hour of the session. Australia's S&P/ASX 200 rose 1.8% to 8,726.4, ending an eight-day losing streak.

In Europe, equities slipped at the open, and technology shares gave back ground a day after their rally. Dutch chip stocks ASML $ASML, ASM International and BE Semiconductor hovered at or just under the unchanged mark.

Dell $DELL, Analog Devices $ADI and Autodesk $ADSK are among the companies due to report earnings on Tuesday, according to The Wall Street Journal. Home-price and consumer-confidence readings are also scheduled for release.
