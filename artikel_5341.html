SuperKlean Announces CRN Registration for Three Industrial Washdown Mixer Designs in Six Canadian Provinces

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

BURLINGAME, Calif., Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- SuperKlean Washdown Products announces Canadian Registration Number (CRN) design registration for three industrial washdown mixers in Alberta, British Columbia, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, and Saskatchewan. The registered designs give plant managers, engineers, and distributors options for facilities supplied by plant steam or an existing hot water system.

DuraMix 8000GM-B and 8000GM-S | Steam and cold water mixing stations

Available in bronze and stainless steel, respectively, the DuraMix 8000GM-B and 8000GM-S is an instant hot water generator using steam and cold water. This process is very efficient and energy loss for this process is minimal to none. Output temperature can be easily set and maintained.

3600M-S | Hot and cold water mixing station

The stainless steel 3600M-S blends hot water with cold water to reach the desired washdown temperature for the purpose of proper washdown.

Based in Burlingame, California, SuperKlean supplies industrial washdown equipment for food processing, dairy, beverage, pharmaceutical, and other industrial facilities. Its product line includes washdown mixing stations, spray nozzles, industrial hoses, fittings, and accessories.
