Xabi Alonso gives Moises Caicedo injury update as Chelsea crisis grows

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Xabi Alonso has provided an update on Moises Caicedo after the midfielder was forced off in the first-half of Chelsea’s 4-1 defeat to Leeds United.

The Blues fell to a dismal defeat at home on Saturday afternoon with Leeds scoring three times in the second-half to seal a famous win in west London.

Caicedo started for the hosts having asked to come off at half-time playing for Ecuador on Tuesday against Bolivia, later explaining he ‘didn’t feel very well’.

During an aerial dual with James Justin in the first-half at Stamford Bridge, the midfielder suffered a heavy landing after twisting in mid-air.

While he was initially able to carry on, Caicedo went down again off the ball a few minutes later, covering his face with his shirt in disappointment.

He was replaced after just 22 minutes with Jordan Henderson onto replace him. Caicedo did not go straight down the tunnel and instead watched the action from the bench with an ice pack.

Asked for an update after the game, Alonso explained the former Brighton star was in ‘pain’ and would require further assessment.

‘He has a knock and we need to wait until tomorrow to see how he is,’ Alonso said in his post-match press conference.

‘He was in pain and let’s see how bad it is.’

Chelsea are already without a number of key players. Joao Pedro and Maxence Lacroix were absent again against Leeds, still recovering from respective thigh and hamstring issues with Alonso unable to out a timeframe on their returns when he spoke to the media on Friday.

Reece James meanwhile is also still absent as he recovers from minor knee surgery.
