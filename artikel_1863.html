Tottenham injury update: Mathys Tel, James Maddison, Mykhailo Mudryk latest news and return dates

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Tottenham return to Premier League action on Saturday, but those fitness concerns heading into the international break remain ahead of a visit from Ipswich.

Defeat to Leeds last time out not only halted Spurs’ momentum after three straight wins and a draw, but it was a costly one for Mathys Tel as he left Elland Road wearing a protective boot and needing the aid of crutches.

There has since been an update on the French youngster, and not a positive one at that. Furthermore, James Maddison’s fitness has been called into question after twice failing to be called upon from the bench this month.

However, there is better news on the likes of Savio, Lucas Bergvall and, potentially, Xavi Simons.

Here is all the latest Tottenham injury news and return dates.

Mathys Tel could be out for a significant period after suffering a “bad injury” during the defeat at Leeds, which left him on crutches after the game.

The forward was on the receiving end of a heavy challenge from Leeds defender Jayden Bogle, who landed with his studs up on the Frenchman’s left Achillies but escaped even a booking for the foul despite a VAR review.

Despite initially trying to play on, Tel eventually signalled to come off and was replaced by Dominic Solanke. The young striker was later seen leaving Elland Road on crutches and with a protective boot on as he made his way to Spurs’ team bus.

As confirmed by French Football Federation (FFF), Tel did not link up with the France U21s as a result of the injury, meaning he missed the loss to Italy and will sit out Tuesday's showdown with England.

A statement reads: “Having suffered an injury with his club Tottenham, Mathys Tel is forced to withdraw from the France Under-21 team training camp which began this Monday at the CNF Clairefontaine.”

It still remains to be seen how long Tel will be sidelined for, with a further update likely to follow during the De Zerbi addresses the media in the pre-Ipswich press conference later this week.

Fitness concerns remain regarding James Maddison after failing to come off the bench in Tottenham’s last two games.

Maddison has not played since picking up an issue during the win at Chelsea last month, and De Zerbi opted to turn to teenager Lucas Williams-Barnett instead of the experienced midfielder with Spurs chasing a goal at Elland Road.

“James, I thought [about bringing him on] in the last substitution before Luca Williams-Barnett, but I saw [Mohammed] Kudus was very tired,” De Zerbi explained.

“And James didn’t [train] until Thursday. On Thursday he worked half [and] part of the training yesterday.

“And in the last 15 minutes maybe could be a good solution, but I decided to make that change because Luca in the winger position, is better than Maddison.”

Potential return date: Saturday November 21, vs Ipswich (H)

There was slight hope that Savio would return for the trip Leeds, which would have been ahead of schedule.

This was after Savio was spotted training with his team-mates ahead of the game, having seemingly recovered from the muscular injury picked up against Manchester United last month.

However, the Brazilian was again absent heading into the international break.

The good news, though, is he could now be available against Ipswich, thus bolstering De Zerbi’s attacking options even further.

Potential return date: Saturday November 21, vs Ipswich (H)

Tosin Adarabioyo has been sidelined with a calf injury since making his first start of the season last month, in a win over Coventry, but was back in contention heading into the international break.

Tosin was not involved in the squad at Leeds, having only days earlier returned to first-team training.

De Zerbi said on Friday: “Tosin is available, yesterday he came inside with the group.”

Potential return date: Saturday November 21, vs Ipswich (H)

Lucas Bergvall will be hoping to return from injury after the international break, after missing the last four games for Spurs with a knock.

The initial prognosis was a two-week absence, but now Bergvall will have a fortnight get back up to speed after being left out of Sweden’s latest squad.

Potential return date: Saturday November 21, vs Ipswich (H)

The reason for Martin Dubravka’s absence from the Tottenham squad against Leeds is down to a minor knee injury which prevented him from being named in the Slovakia squad for their Nations League double-header.

Spurs’ back-up goalkeeper is not expected to be sidelined for too long, so a return on the bench against Ipswich is likely.

Potential return date: Saturday November 21, vs Ipswich (H)

Signed on loan from Chelsea in the summer, Mudryk played just one game before picking up an ankle injury in training back in September.

De Zerbi stated the timeframe for the “stupid” injury was six to eight weeks, with it originally hoped that the Ukrainian could be involved by late October. That was then updated to after the Novemver international break.

Potential return date: Saturday November 21, vs Ipswich (H)

New reports suggest that Xavi Simons will enter the final phase of his recovery this month and return to first-team training, having only ruptured his anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) back in April.

Simons suffered the injury against Wolves and was predicted to return early in 2027.

The Dutchman posted footage of himself training on the grass last month and his early comeback would be a massive boost for De Zerbi.

Frenchman Wilson Odobert is another Spurs player recovering from an ACL injury. The former Burnley man sustained his tear against Newcastle in February.

The typical timeframe for an ACL recovery would have the winger available to Spurs in December, but De Zerbi’s recent comments put that timeline under some doubt.

The Italian stated: “I don't know, because he has not started yet to work with us. I have no idea [when he will return].”
