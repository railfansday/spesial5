Michael Carrick sack news LIVE: Man Utd latest as meeting booked to decide manager's future

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

All eyes are once again on Manchester United today as fans await a decision on the future of under-fire manager Michael Carrick. Man Utd’s hierarchy have a huge call to make once more in the aftermath of Sunday’s remarkable and chaotic 4-4 draw with Aston Villa at Old Trafford, in which Carrick’s side twice came from two goals behind to seal a dramatic point in stoppage time. That was just days after they had also overturned a 2-0 half-time deficit to defeat Roma 3-2 in the Champions League and end a dismal nine-match winless run across all competitions.

The players are still fighting for their boss and fans once again showed their strong support, though it remains to be seen if these terrible starts to games will ultimately prove his downfall. United head into the November international break crucially sitting inside the bottom three amid genuine fears over a potential relegation battle this season, having won just one of their first 10 matches - at home to newly-promoted Ipswich back in August - and collected only eight points with a tough trip to Liverpool to come next.

Carrick was in uncharacteristically spiky mood when answering the latest questions on his future on Sunday night, insisting that he would definitely not resign as a crisis meeting is reportedly now planned to discuss his future, with the likes of Eddie Howe, Julian Nagelsmann and possibly even Mauricio Pochettino waiting in the wings. Manchester United have now conceded 19 goals in just 10 Premier League games this season, leaving the Red Devils sitting in the relegation zone heading into the final international break of 2026.

Gary Neville was a guest pundit on NBC Sports for the draw with Villa, with the ex-Man Utd captain criticising the club’s recruitment over the summer.

Roy Keane compared Michael Carrick to a “politician” on Sky Sports after yesterday’s chaotic clash with Aston Villa, warning that he needs to “get moving” in terms of the improvements he has promised at Manchester United.

"It's obviously difficult for the manager. Under pressure, no doubt. He talks a lot but doesn't say much. I don't know if that's a tactic. Like a politician," Keane said.

“He keeps saying we need to tighten up and there's things we need to work on.

“But they better get moving on that. It's the worst start in how many years, all these stats aren't very good. If you keep giving up goals and chances, it's just going to continue.

"What are we expecting to change over the next few weeks and months? What are they working on? They need to do a lot better than that."

Keane added: “I think we've said previously there have been question marks over this team but they're showing a bit of fight, a bit of desire and, as bad as the game was from a footballing point of view, they kept going to the end.

"So, on the one hand, you say dreadful to give up four goals at home, but they kept going, so we have to give them a little bit of praise.

"In terms of the bigger picture, it's that rollercoaster of watching United. Some bits were so, so bad.

“Defensively the way it started, the start of the second half, but they found a way to get a point in a game where they looked dead and buried.

"Still nowhere near good enough to try and get towards the middle of the table."

Gary Neville has had his say on the future of Michael Carrick, insisting that the Manchester United owners “don’t have a clue what to do” as he took fresh aim at their approach.

"When the new manager comes in, everybody's happy for a couple of months and all of a sudden, six months later, it happens again," Neville told NBC Sports in America, where he appeared alongside brother Phil as a pundit on Sunday.

"You can keep changing the manager and thinking that's going to be the answer. But this has been proven now over eight managers in 12 years, that's not the answer.

"Michael could leave, Michael might stay, we don't know, the ownership don't have a clue what to do.

"It's rinse and repeat. [Ruben] Amorim, [Erik] Ten Hag, Ole Gunnar Solskjaer, Carrick, Jose Mourinho, Louis van Gaal, David Moyes, Ralf Rangnick - some of them are world class managers who have had success, there are a lot of promising young managers there, ex-players who know the club as well.

"None of it has worked because if you recruit badly, if you've got [a] poor performance department, poor recruitment department, you've not got leadership that is strong right through the spine of the football club you will fail.

"Every organisation in the world will fail if you've not got an aligned leadership from top to bottom - your board and your ownership right the way through to the person who is stood on the pitch trying to play football.

"There are real issues within that football club, you've got people doing those jobs who have never done those jobs before in senior positions."

A fresh update now on Michael Carrick’s Manchester United future.

Mauricio Pochettino has emerged as the new betting favourite to replace Michael Carrick as Manchester United manager.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea boss has been linked with the Old Trafford job several times in the past and shot to the top of the odds market over the weekend.

Pochettino now sits ahead of the likes of Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann with some bookmakers.

Antonio Conte is the only other realistic-looking option from the shortlists that are doing the rounds, with Unai Emery reportedly set to sign a new long-term contract at Aston Villa.

Fabian Hurzeler has already distanced himself from the role and does not have a release clause in his contract at high-flying Brighton, meaning United would have to pay a world-record fee for a manager to prise him away from the Amex Stadium.

None of Kieran McKenna, Gareth Southgate or Cesc Fabregas seem like genuine options.
