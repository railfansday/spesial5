Is Wigan Athletic v Barnsley FC on TV? Channel and streaming details ahead of League One meeting

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Barnsley are set to return to League One action with a trip across the Pennines to Wigan Athletic.

The FA Cup took centre stage at Oakwell last week and the Reds were thorough in their 7-2 dismantling of fifth-tier Aldershot Town.

They were then in EFL Trophy action in midweek, defeating Oldham Athletic 5-2 despite the next round being out of reach.

Barnsley are back to their bread and butter this weekend with a trip to Wigan in the calendar. It will be a meeting of two struggling sides, but both have shown signs of life recently.

Reds head coach Daniel Stendel said: “It’s more pressure maybe than in our situation normally, but it doesn't matter, it's one game.

“Pressure is part of football, but we need also a little bit of calm to play our game. We're looking forward to it. We're in good shape and in a positive momentum.”

Kick-off at the Brick Community Stadium is scheduled for 3pm on Saturday, November 14.

Yes - the match will be shown live on TV. It has not been given top billing on any of the main Sky Sports channels but can be viewed as an alternative broadcast via the Sky Sports+ service. Subscriptions can be purchased online.

The 3pm blackout can only be enforced when half of the matches across a country’s top two tiers are played at the same time and there are none scheduled to take place.

Supporters can stream the action via the official Sky Sports app or on NOW TV with a valid subscription. There will also be live audio commentary on BBC Radio Sheffield.
