‘Only he knows’ - Sheffield United legend opens up on Doncaster Rovers exit and ‘ruthless’ Grant McCann

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Billy Sharp has described Doncaster Rovers manager Grant McCann as “ruthless” - but does not hold any grudges regarding his departure.

The 40-year-old left Doncaster at the end of last season, signing off in style with a final-day hat-trick away at Peterborough United.

He later hung up his boots, but did recently come out of retirement to play for AFC Rushden & Diamonds in the FA Cup.

Sharp had expressed interest in a coaching role at Sheffield United while still on Doncaster’s books and believes it had a factor in the decision to release him.

He told the DN4 Podcast: “I don’t hold any grudges at all. He’s a ruthless manager and you’ve got to make decisions. Sometimes you get decisions wrong. I’m sure it did have a part to play, I think so, but only he knows.

“I think you have to be ruthless as a manager. I’m stubborn as well. If I ask myself, if I was manager and a player came to me... but I don’t know. It was different because I was coming to the end of my career, I’m a Sheffield United fan, I was trying to think about the future.

“It wasn’t that I wasn’t enjoying training or playing. It had been arranged to retire. But I don’t know, I really wanted the opportunity. I thought it was a great opportunity for me to be in the game for the next 15, 20, whatever years.”

Sharp has repeatedly expressed a desire to move into coaching and with his playing days now seemingly behind him, the iconic marksman can shift his focus.

Given the wealth of experience possessed by the Sheffield United legend and ex-Leeds United man, he would arguably be a shrewd addition to an array of coaching set-ups.

“Grant always said to me about doing a bit of coaching at Donny,” he said. “He obviously brought Greeny (Paul Green) up.

“When I was injured, towards the back end of the season, looking out of that physio room, wanting to be on that training pitch, I could see myself doing a bit of coaching as well.”
