Mad five minutes costs Worthing so dear at Woking

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

After Ashley Nathaniel-George cancelled out Roy Syla’s opener just before half-time, a five minute spree including two goals from John Marquis and an own-goal from Harvey Griffiths turned the tide heavily in favour of the hosts after the interval.

Dominic Johnson-Fisher’s superb showing from the bench brought the Rebels within touching distance of leaving Surrey with a point after the number nine netted twice, but the deficit could not be overturned before the full-time whistle.

It was the hosts who enjoyed the better start of the two side through 20 minutes, as experienced forward Marquis had two sights on goal early on. A driving run down the right flank by Jaiden Putman inside the first minute led to the former Portsmouth striker being picked out at the far post, yet he could not get an effort away under pressure from multiple Worthing bodies.

Five minutes later, another breakaway for the Cardinals saw Marquis latch onto a lofted ball in behind the Rebels backline. Shaping onto his left side, the 34 year-old’s strike was kept out by the midriff of Harrison Foulkes.

Worthing had spells of promise during the early exchanges, with the weekend’s match-winner Jordan Young instrumental once again in most of the away side’s attacking play. The former Torquay United man had his best effort of the half just after quarter of an hour, linking up with Kendall through the right channel before firing over Vicente Reyes’ crossbar.

Putman’s adventurous play down the Woking right posed a persistent threat to the left of the visitors’ defence throughout the first half. The QPR-loanee tested Foulkes with an attempt of his own with 20 minutes on the clock drifting inward from the right side before being kept out by another Foulkes intervention.

With half an hour on the clock, a double substitution from Hinshelwood to bring on both Cory Adjetey-Brew and Nathaniel-George proved the spark for a crescendo of action to close out the half.

A loose ball following an aerial challenge allowed Putman to drive forward down the right. The ball found the feet of Josh Kelly at the by-line, who swivelled before picking out the run of Syla into the box. With Syla unmarked, the midfielder side-footed the ball past Foulkes to open the scoring.

Woking’s first half advantage did not last long, and it was one of the two first half changes in Nathaniel-George who fashioned an equaliser with a minute of normal time remaining in the period. Cutting in from the left flank, the substitute curled a right-footed strike which nestled into Reyes’ bottom left corner from 20 yards out. A spectacular way to open his goalscoring account for the Rebels.

While Worthing’s rapid response may have spurned optimism amongst the travelling faithful heading into the break, the start of the second half spelled nothing but turmoil from a Worthing perspective.

Less than a minute after play resumed, Marquis notched his second of the night after turning the ball home from the edge of the six-yard box with a flicked finish amidst a sea of away defenders.

The hosts doubled their advantage almost immediately after regaining their lead. A poorly-executed pass from Foulkes invited Woking to spring an attack, and with the visitors’ keeper stranded off his goal-line, Marquis swept home into an empty net to make it 3-1.

Five minutes of horror to start the half culminated with the home side adding a fourth goal. Another giveaway in midfield presented Josh Grant the opportunity to drive towards the box. Grant’s eventual effort fizzed off the foot of the retreating Griffiths, with Foulkes helpless to watch the ball trickle over the line.

The away contingent stunned following a flurry of concessions, Hinshelwood looked to the bench yet again for inspiration in giving the Rebels a chance of coming away with something. One of those introductions was Johnson-Fisher, who netted two brilliant goals to bring the visitors back to within one.

In the 64th minute, a ball in from the left side was brought down astutely by the former Whitehawk striker, who fired the ball home on the turn off the underside of the crossbar. Then, 12 minutes later, another fine finish from Johnson-Fisher gave Worthing their third, this time getting on the end of a low pass in behind from Nathaniel-George before slotting a low drive underneath the body of Reyes.

Ultimately, despite the visitors’ best efforts in the closing stages. three goals in rapid succession to start the second half proved too much for the away side to overcome on a cold Tuesday night of madness at the Kingfield Stadium.
