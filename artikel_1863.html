Barcelona star wants Man Utd move in January as agent flies in for transfer talks

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Alejandro Balde is keen on joining Manchester United in January with his agent holding talks with Barcelona over a move this week.

United explored a move for the Spain international late on in the summer transfer window with Balde eventually opting to stay and fight for his place at the Camp Nou.

The 23-year-old started the season behind Joao Cancelo and Xavi Espart in the pecking order at left-back but his prospects under Hansi Flick have not improved.

Balde has not started a single match this term, managing just 124 minutes of action in five appearances off the bench. The situation has prompted fresh speculation over his future with United still in desperate need of another option at left-back.

New United boss Mauricio Pochettino will be backed in January with a new striker and a left-back to be targeted.

Mundo Deportivo report Balde’s agent Jorge Mendes arrived in Barcelona on Tuesday with his client’s future one of the topics on the agenda.

Balde is unhappy with his role at the Camp Nou and while there are no offers on the table as of yet, that could soon change.

Bayern Munich were also credited with interest in the summer but the report adds Balde is eager to test himself in the Premier League.

Barcelona are said to value Balde at €40million (£33m) and are reportedly prioritising a permanent sale, but a loan deal with an option to buy could also be considered.

Balde broke into the first-team back in 2022 under club legend Xavi, replacing Jordi Alba in the starting XI.

In November that year, he took part in a quick-fire Q&A with Mundo Deportivo on TikTok. Balde was asked, ‘A club other than Barcelona?’, replying, ‘United’.

Balde started 21 games as Barcelona won the Spanish title last season, starting a further 10 in the Champions League and cup competitions. Hamstring and muscle issues saw him miss 10 games through injury.
