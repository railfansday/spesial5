‘Ramayana’ VFX House DNEG Partners With Meta on 3D Movies for VR Glasses, Including ‘The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping’ (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A catalog of popular films is being converted into stereo 3D for Meta VR Glasses ahead of the device’s spring 2027 launch, under a new technology partnership between Meta and DNEG, the visual effects and animation company whose slate includes “Ramayana: Rise of a Legend.”

DNEG’s Stereo team has already begun work on several of the titles. Among them is “The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping,” which Variety reported earlier this month is being converted to 3D by Meta and Lionsgate with DNEG and director Francis Lawrence. The film opens in 3D in theaters on Nov. 20 and will be available on Meta VR Glasses after its theatrical run. The glasses are built around a 5K micro-OLED screen, branded the Infinite Display, with 37 pixels per degree. According to DNEG, that level of sharpness lets its team produce 3D versions with theatrical-quality clarity. In some cases, the team will also step outside conventional film framing to build new immersive moments.

DNEG Global CEO Namit Malhotra said, “I’m excited and enthused to announce DNEG’s partnership with Meta to deliver never-before-seen 3D content for Meta VR Glasses. The launch of Meta VR Glasses is set to be a game-changer and will give cinema fans a new way to experience their favorite movies in stunning 3D. The creative possibilities opened up by this new technology align perfectly with DNEG’s stated ambition to ‘Transform the Future of Storytelling,’ and we look forward to exploring further opportunities with the Meta team to deliver the highest-quality immersive experiences for this exciting new technology.”

DNEG has worked in stereo conversion for more than 15 years and was the first to turn a full-length Hollywood feature into stereo 3D. Its 3D work has since won multiple awards, including for Alfonso Cuarón’s “Gravity” and Christopher McQuarrie’s “Mission: Impossible – Fallout.” More recent conversions include “Spider-Man: Brand New Day,” “Supergirl” and the live-action “How to Train Your Dragon.”

With close to three decades in the business and studios in North America, Europe, Asia and Australia, DNEG has won eight visual effects Oscars since 2011, along with BAFTA, Primetime Emmy and Visual Effects Society honors.

The Meta deal comes during a crowded stretch for the company. Its projects include “Ramayana: Rise of a Legend,” “Blade Runner 2099,” “The Cat in the Hat” and Season 3 of “The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power,” with “Dune: Part Three” and “The Angry Birds Movie 3” following in December.
