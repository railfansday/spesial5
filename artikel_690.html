Chris Pratt to Star in Sci-Fi Movie ‘The Traveler’ as He Inks Paramount First-Look

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The film has Zach Lipovsky and Adam Stein set to direct.

By Aaron Couch, Borys Kit November 18, 2026 4:26pm

Chris Pratt is taking a trip to Paramount. He will star in The Traveler for the studio, where he has also signed a first-look deal. The three-year producing pact could involve starring in projects as well for his Indivisible production banner.

The Traveler is based on Joseph Eckert’s novel, which was published over the summer. It’s a father-son story, about a man who is flung through time every morning at 7:52 a.m. The first jump pushes him 24 hours into the future, but every day, the jump doubles in time, meaning soon he’s hurtling years in a single day. His son, meanwhile, grows up to study quantum physics as he attempts to save his father, who has long been missing through time. Final Destination Bloodlines filmmakers Zach Lipovsky and Adam Stein are directing The Traveler from Justin Rhodes’ screenplay. The feature, one of the more original projects on the runway of any major studio, is gearing up to shoot next year.

Pratt’s first-look deal, meanwhile, also includes Miami Heat, which has a script from Brad Copeland and has Pratt starring and producing with Hutch Parker.

Pratt became an A-list star more than a decade ago with Guardians of the Galaxy, and went on to appear in multiple Avengers movies for Marvel. He also led the Jurassic World franchise from 2015-2022, grossing billions in the process. Earlier this year, he led the animated Super Mario Bros. Galaxy movie, which grossed $1 billion globally, and had a new season of The Terminal List out last month on Amazon.

Up next, he stars in Way of the Warrior Kid for Apple TV, which he also produced. The family opens debuts Nov. 25.
