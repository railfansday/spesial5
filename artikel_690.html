ESPN Taps John Legend to Warm Up Crowds for CFP With Original Music

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

John Legend has sung “The Star-Spangled Banner” at the World Series, the NBA Finals and the NBA All-Star Game. After going pro, he’s turning to college sports.

ESPN has tapped the popular musician to draw attention to the looming College Football Playoff, which kicks off on Friday, December 18. In a new promo that beings airing Thursday, Legend offers a musical number with what he calls “Rat Pack energy” to highlight the fact that t post season play will end in Las Vegas with the CFP National Championship. “it was easy for me to pick that as the musical direction, he tells Variety, because he’s a fan of Frank Sinatra, and extremely familiar with his music, “That was my North Star for where I was going.”

This sort of performance “is just a different challenge” Legend says. “You can’t do lots of repetition, but you have to make it hooky, and that was the compositional challenge. “He found the process fun, “because I think sometimes constraints make creativity easier in some ways. You have the boundaries. It’s like coloring within the lines rather than just drawing something freehand.

ESPN has new reason to call attention to post-season college football. It will soon be showing a lot more of it. The Disney sports-media giant will be the exclusive worldwide rightsholder to an expanded 24-team College Football Playoff, starting with the 2027-28 season. ESPN gets media rights to eight CFP first round games, eight CFP second round games, four CFP quarterfinals, two CFP semifinals and the CFP national championship — compared with 11 games in the current 12-team Playoff.

“John Legend is the perfect fit for this campaign,” said Maggie Lewin, ESPN senior director of marketing. “He is a true college football fan. He can truly talk the talk, while also bringing a sense of showmanship and entertainment value to the spectacle of the CFP in Vegas this year. The campaign is bold, fun and a little wild – making it the perfect message for this year’s CFP.”

Legend’s only complaint? There’s a “sad irony” to the project. His favorite team is fighting to keep its post-season dreams alive. Legend is a fan of Ohio State, which just broke a two-game losing streak.
