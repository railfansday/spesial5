We’re rounding up last week’s news, from Anthropic’s new safety agreement to a proposed rule’s impact on school meal access.

With the growth of school choice programs in Florida, Broward County Public Schools is “no longer the only game in town,” says Valerie Wanza, the district's chief strategy and innovation officer.

To stay financially healthy, the district is now taking a comprehensive approach that includes closing and consolidating some schools, cutting at least 1,000 staff positions, and implementing other cost-saving measures. It’s also working to increase enrollment by vigorously recruiting new students via media campaigns, personalized phone calls, and events aimed at raising interest in and excitement for Broward schools.

Broward’s story is part of a bigger trend schools nationwide are navigating as they grapple with declining enrollment and the impact of school choice programs. Read more in our series on the issue, "Scrambling for Students."
