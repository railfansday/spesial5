Rob Kardashian Shares Glimpse Inside $21 Million California Home as He and Ex Blac Chyna Celebrate 10-Year-Old Daughter’s Birthday

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Rob Kardashian and his ex, Blac Chyna, threw a fun-filled birthday bash for their daughter Dream's 10-year-old birthday at Kardashian's $21.7 million California home.

Kardashian, 39, and Chyna, 38, took to Instagram to share heartfelt birthday tributes to their daughter on Nov. 10.

“Happy DREAM Day!!! Happy golden Birthday to my Dream girl I can’t believe 10 years have gone by already goodness Lord please slow down time," Rob penned.

The TV personality then shared a snap of him holding Dream as a baby, before sharing a current picture of the 10-year-old standing in front of large balloons that read, "Happy 10th Birthday Dream."

Dream could be seen smiling from ear to ear while sitting on the kitchen island by a bouquets of flowers.

“Being your dad is the greatest gift of my life! You make me proud every single day! I love you more than you’ll ever know!! Love, Daddy," he added.

Another snap also showed off the sweet treats Dream got for her birthday, which included doughnuts from Blinkie’s Donuts.

The donuts featured glazed and white frosting with sprinkles that spelled out, ‘Happy 10th Birthday Dream.’

In the comments, Chynna added, "Happy birthday to our beautiful girl. Thank you Robert.”

Meanwhile, Dream's aunt, Khloe Kardashian also chimed in, penning, "She's the best girl ever."

In her post, Chynna—whose real name is Angela White—added, "Happy 10th Birthday to my dearest baby girl, Dream. Thank you, Jesus for everything your doing in our lives. I don’t take these moments for granted and I appreciate your grace, mercy and guidance.”

Rob also penned in the comments section, “Happy birthday Dream!!!!!!!!!.”

They held the party at $21.7 million Los Angeles mansion, which he snapped up in December 2025.

The palatial pad comes complete with a tennis court, resort-style pool, and perimeter fencing.

The outdoor area boasts a basketball hoop, a jungle gym, and an outdoor bar.

The home also features six bedrooms and 10 bathrooms. It is also within walking distance from his mom Kris Jenner's home and Khloe's pad.

Rob was previously residing in Jenner's home, however, was booted out when the mom put the home on the market.

Rob and Chyna tied the knot in 2016, they then announced that they were expecting their daughter. They announced their split the following year.

Jenner also shared her own tribute for her granddaughter, penning, "Happy birthday to our beautiful Dream Girl!!! 💖🎂 I can’t believe you are 10 years old today! You are the sweetest, smartest little girl with the biggest heart and the most incredible personality!!!

“You are so loving, kind, funny, creative, and full of life, and watching you grow into the amazing young girl you are becoming has been one of my greatest blessings. I am so proud of you, and I hope you always know how deeply you are loved and how much joy you bring to all of us. Lovey loves you forever and ever!!!!
