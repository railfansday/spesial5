Inspiring Hearts & Minds on Solar

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Since the invention of the solar cell seven decades ago, the story of American solar has seen its fair share of twists and turns on its journey to becoming the country’s most affordable and fastest-growing energy source. While the market continues demanding more solar and energy storage at a rapid pace, public opinion is still catching up. Bill McKibben is an author, activist, and longtime advocate of solar energy. He joins SEIA’s Good Energy podcast from RE+ in Las Vegas—North America’s largest clean energy trade show—to discuss a lifetime of solar advocacy and why Americans should look to the sun for an affordable, reliable, and clean energy future.

From in-depth interviews with industry leaders to insightful discussions on the rapid growth of solar and storage in the United States, Good Energy connects you with the heartbeat of the solar industry. Whether you’re a seasoned professional or just curious about the future of energy, tune in to stay informed and inspired about the bright future of solar. Listen to full episodes on YouTube, Spotify, and Apple Podcasts.
