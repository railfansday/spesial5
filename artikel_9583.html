The 12 best recipes to bake in October: It’s peak baking season — here’s what to make.

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

What do jobs, school, sports practices, and commuting have in common? They all prevent us from baking as much or as often as we’d like. But when we do get the chance, we want a slam-dunk recipe — or 12. Here’s a dozen of the favorite fall recipes we’ll be making this month: from pumpkin cake bars to spooky fudge brownies to a crowd-pleasing vegetarian quiche.

It’s the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown! Only this version comes in cake form. The decoration is deceptively easy: Stack two small Bundts, frost the entire thing, and add a chocolate-dipped ice cream cone for the stem.

Get the recipe: The Great Pumpkin Bundt Cake

To make this recipe, you’ll need: Golden Wheat Flour and Pumpkin Pie Spice

If you like this recipe, you may also like: Pumpkin Espresso Bundt Cake, Gluten-Free Pumpkin Cake

To make this recipe, you’ll need:  Black Cocoa and Supernatural Plant-Based Food Colors

These bite-sized pies are a charming addition to any occasion. The boiled cider and spices in the filling ensure a full depth of apple flavor for maximum fall vibes. If you're tight on time, swap the lattice treatment for a buttery crumb topping instead.

To make this recipe, you’ll need: Boiled Cider and Apple Pie Spice

If you like this recipe, you may also like: Apple Pie Bars, Tiny Apple Tarts for Two

Step aside, soup: Butternut squash truly shines in this autumnal quiche. Caramelized onions finished with balsamic vinegar and fresh baby spinach round out the filling, while our Classic Single Pie Crust recipe ensures a tender, golden crust. Elevate the edges with the pie crust decorating technique of your choice.

Get the recipe: Roasted Butternut Squash & Spinach Quiche

To make this recipe, you’ll need: 9" Pie Pan

If you like this recipe, you may also like: Vegetable Quiche, Delicata Squash Galette with Cheese and Herbs

Ready to test the theory that only white flour can make rolls light and fluffy? A bit of bread flour and vital wheat gluten give these whole wheat rolls a lofty rise, while the milk, butter, and honey create a plush crumb that stays soft for days. Add a salted honey butter wash to the tops of a freshly baked batch, and you may not have any leftover.

Get the recipe: Super-Soft Whole Wheat Rolls

To make this recipe, you’ll need: Golden Wheat Flour and Bread Flour

If you like this recipe, you may also like: Whole Grain Dinner Rolls, Soft White Dinner Rolls

"Tassie" means "small cup" in Scottish, and these dainty pecan pies pack a nutty punch. The cream cheese pastry crust is cut into rounds and placed in a mini muffin pan before the pecan pie filling is spooned into each well. With only 30 minutes of hands-on time, these two dozen tassies come together quickly on a crisp fall afternoon.

To make this recipe, you’ll need: Mini Muffin Pan

If you like this recipe, you may also like: Easy Pecan Pie Bars, Old-Fashioned Pecan Pie

Need a spooky treat, stat? You can transform a plain batch of brownies into a Halloween-themed dessert in a pinch. A simple piping technique creates a spider web effect and adds a touch of rich tang to chocolatey brownies.

To make this recipe, you’ll need: 9" x 13" Pan

If you like this recipe, you may also like: Fudge Brownies, Chocolate Cheesecake Brownies

You’ll notice a lot of similarities between this pumpkin cake and carrot cake: moist, spiced cake, toasted walnuts, and rich cream cheese frosting. These bars are great for delighting a crowd, as this recipe yields three to four dozen bars, depending on how big you slice them. You can easily make them gluten-free, too.

Get the recipe: Pumpkin Cake Bars with Cream Cheese Frosting

To make this recipe, you’ll need: Pumpkin Pie Spice and Maple Syrup

If you like this recipe, you may also like: Pumpkin Spice Latte Bars, Chocolate-Pumpkin Crumb Cake

'Tis the season for hand pies to be mummified! A shortening-based pie crust helps the lattice strips stay together, while jam or pie filling allows for customizable flavors. Pop on a few sugar eyes, and you'll be sure to have treats instead of tricks.

To make this recipe, you’ll need: Double Pastry Wheel or Pizza Cutter

If you like this recipe, you may also like: Flaky Apple Hand Pies, Autumn Apple Turnovers

With a luxuriously creamy maple filling and a buttery pecan crust, this cheesecake balances richness with subtle flavors. Meanwhile, the candied pecan garnish adds visual interest and a complementary crunch. Be sure to leave time for an overnight chill and keep your cheesecake from cracking with these tips. (Find more seasonal maple recipes in our Fall Flavor Edit.)

To make this recipe, you’ll need: Springform Pan

If you like this recipe, you may also like: Caramel Pecan Cheesecake, Maple Cream Pie

No need to choose between chocolate or caramel; these cupcakes bring both bold flavors together in a luscious combination. Making caramel from scratch is worth it for the irreplaceable flavor, and those few extra steps are easily done ahead of time. Substitute in Gluten-Free Measure for Measure Flour to make these cupcakes gluten-free. (Find more seasonal caramel recipes in our Fall Flavor Edit.)

Get the recipe: Chocolate Cupcakes with Whipped Caramel Frosting

To make this recipe, you’ll need: Muffin Pan and Pastry Brush

If you like this recipe, you may also like: Chocolate Cupcakes with Pecan Caramel , S’more Cupcakes

This milk bread incorporates all the flavor and color of pumpkin, while still keeping an airy texture. You can shape the dough into pull-apart rolls or a loaf, and topping with a milk wash and pumpkin seeds adds a nutty crunch. Wrap it well at room temperature, and this bread will stay soft for days.

To make this recipe, you’ll need: Round Pan or Loaf Pan

If you like this recipe, you may also like: Japanese Milk Bread, Pumpkin Yeast Bread
