The unexpected pan you need this holiday season : The case to make everything mini.

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Lining every well takes a little extra time, but the smaller format pays off: for these mini pumpkin pies and quiches, you can skip parbaking step and still get a crisp, flaky crust. Plus, they’re ready to serve out of the pan — no pie cutter required.

Not feeling pie? Consider Molten Chocolate Mini Cakes instead. And when houseguests wake up, bring the pan back out for Mini Quiches or Protein Pancake Bites.

Get the most out of this last month of fall baking with an oven-baked version of a fried favorite: warm apple cider doughnuts. Making and frying them from scratch can feel like more work than they’re worth, but a mini muffin pan offers an easier route. These Apple Cider Doughnut Holes are baked entirely in the oven, then tossed in butter, boiled cider, and spices and served warm for the same cozy appeal as a freshly fried doughnut.
