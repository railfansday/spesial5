Fall Freezer Meals #2

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

These Fall Freezer Meals have been SO helpful for us. The prep is shockingly easy!

Published by Lindsay Ostrom on October 14, 2026

A few months ago, I discovered what feels like a little life hack.

I started spending an hour each month getting a few meals prepped for the month ahead. We’re talking just assembling stuff for my favorite recipes into bags and freezing it!

And I can’t tell you how much this system makes me feel like I have my life together! It’s the best.

The recipes this month are absolute family favorites for fall!

And they all have cook times for Crockpot, Instant Pot, and stovetop methods. 🙂

Hope these help make your life easier this month like they have for us!

Everything you need is right on them. I print the cards out and attach them to the bags so I don’t have to use my phone at all for the prep or cooking.

For the streusel: mix 4 tablespoons melted butter, 1/3 cup brown sugar, and 3/4 cup flour into small crumbles.

Spray a muffin tin with avocado oil. Fill tins two-thirds of the way full with batter; top with streusel.

Store muffins in an airtight container or bag in the freezer (or fridge). To reheat from frozen, microwave 45-60 seconds.

Remove chicken from the sauce and shred. Blend sauce until smooth. Add chicken back in. Serve in tortillas with all the taco fixings you want!

Add 1-2 cups heavy cream and 1-2 cups chicken broth (as needed). To thicken: whisk 2 tbsp. cornstarch with 2 tbsp. cold water; add and simmer.

Whisk 1 1/2 tbsp. cornstarch in 1 1/2 tbsp. cold water; add to bubbling pot and let simmer until slightly thickened.

Stir in 2 cups of fresh spinach and fresh cilantro just before serving.

Serve with rice and chili crisp. I also love this with cucumber salad (thinly sliced cucumber and red onion, tossed with vinegar, avocado oil, salt, and sugar).
