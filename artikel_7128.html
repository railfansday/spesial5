Account Manager at Atticus

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

At any given time, 16 million Americans are experiencing a crisis that requires urgent help from our legal system or government. The right assistance could transform their lives. But today, most never get it.

Atticus makes it easy for any sick or injured American to get life-changing aid. Our mission is to tear down barriers between people in crisis and the aid they deserve. In the last six years, we’ve become the leading platform connecting people with disabilities to government benefits. We also help victims of accidents, misconduct, and violence get compensation from insurance.

So far, we’ve helped hundreds of thousands of people access over $10 billion in life-changing aid —and earned over 20,000 five-star reviews. And we’re just getting started.

We've raised more than $100 million from top VC firms like Forerunner, Google Ventures, and True Ventures, and we’re on our way to creating a category defining business assisting Americans in need.

In the next two years, we aim to help over 250,000 Americans get access to life-changing benefits. Much of that help reaches people through our Lawyer Network: our network of vetted partner law firms that represent clients alongside Atticus. The Lawyer Network (LN) team, led by Erin Casale, is the bridge between Atticus and those firms, and our job is to improve their ability to serve clients and help them grow.

We're hiring a Senior Account Manager to own our largest partner firms. You'll carry a deliberately small book of high-volume firms (roughly 10) and act as an extension of their team: diagnosing where and why they lose cases, coaching them on operations and legal execution, and getting even the most technophobic firm to adopt new Atticus tools.

The ideal candidate has spent years making small business customers measurably better at what they do through a mix of operations and tech, and wants to do that work somewhere it changes lives.

Make firms better. Own our enterprise-tier partner firms and improve their capacity, conversion rates, and win rates. Find best practices in firm operations and Social Security Disability legal execution, spread them across the network, and coach them relentlessly.

Drive technology adoption. Get every firm, including folks who are tech-hesitant, using new Atticus features well. That means making the case for the value (think: sales motion but the product is free), building and implementing change management plans, and running training and support.

Know your firms from the inside. Build relationships beyond the managing attorney, down to intake, case development, and operations leads. Understand a firm well enough to help with hiring, software, and staffing decisions, not just Atticus problems.

Be the voice of firms inside Atticus. Bring what you learn back to Product, Customer Experience, and Operations so we serve firms better and learn from their experience.

Deliver hard news well. Hold firms to clear performance standards and push back on attorneys—even those with decades of experience in the field—with finesse and confidence. If needed, cut ties gracefully.

Shape how the team works. Own strategic projects end to end, from scoping the question and gathering data to sharing recommendations with leadership and rolling out what works across the network.

5+ years in Account Management, Customer Success, Partnerships, or a similar role serving small businesses, where your mandate was improving your accounts' performance (not just retention)

A track record of driving product adoption in a tech or tech-adjacent setting

You read data comfortably, spot trends before results move, and show up with both a diagnosis and a plan

You're persuasive in conversation and in writing, and you'd rather pick up the phone (or get on a plane) than send another email

You're outgoing and build trust quickly, with colleagues and with skeptical partners alike

Adaptable: resourceful and steady when priorities shift

You pick up new tools fast, from cloud software to building a clean deck

We covered above why this job might be for you, and think it’s equally important to tell you why it might not be for you. You won’t enjoy this job very much if:

You prefer a large book of light-touch accounts over depth with a few

You get frustrated when projects and priorities change quickly (we obviously try to limit this, but are a quickly-growing company and it does still happen)

The role is a rare opportunity to join a fast-growing Series C startup that doubles as a B-corp social enterprise. Our team is still small enough for you to have an outsized impact on how we operate and how our firms grow.

We offer competitive pay, including equity, and generous benefits:

Medical and dental insurance with 100% of employee and family premiums covered

15 vacation days and ~20 paid holidays each year (including two weeks at end-of-year)

$1,000 reimbursable stipend for education and training outside of work

Up to $1,200/year student loan repayment assistance

Humble, thoughtful, smart, fun colleagues

We anticipate the base salary band for this role will be between $110,000 and $130,000, in addition to equity and benefits. The salary at offer will be determined by a number of factors such as the candidate's experience, knowledge, skills and abilities, as well as internal equity among our team.

This job is fully remote and we're committed to empowering everyone with flexibility. Work remotely, and travel to LA (on the company dime) as needed to be with your colleagues, usually quarterly, plus offsites. We care a lot about building a great culture and we think some interactions need to happen in person, so we put a lot of thought into retreats, offsites, and other ways to gather. You'll also visit partner firms in-person occasionally. Overall, expect to travel up to monthly between internal get togethers and firm visits.

New remote jobs delivered to your inbox, every week.

Location independence is the future of work. It serves companies with best talent from around the globe. And benefits employees with a greater work life balance and the ability to work in their pyjamas.- Nithur, founder.
