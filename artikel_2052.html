Rangers face battle to keep Ryan Naderi after source's scout reveal - verdict

Rangers are sure to find it increasingly difficult to keep hold of striker Ryan Naderi as his impressive form continues in the Scottish Premiership.

The Czech international has been like a man transformed at Ibrox so far this season, and is quickly becoming one of the first names on the teamsheet.

In the absence of Youssef Chermiti, the 23-year-old has stepped up and taken on the attacking burden for Derek McInnes' side, and to great effect of late.

Naderi is the Gers' top scorer in league action so far with six strikes, while his tally of nine goals in all competitions has already set a new best for a single season in his career.

The former Hansa Rostock man is finally beginning to prove himself at Ibrox, but that will naturally be attracting attention from elsewhere.

With Naderi's stock beginning to rise consistently in recent weeks, it surely won't be long before Rangers have to worry about losing him.

Naderi is in a simply remarkable vein of form at Rangers right now, and is spearheading their push for the league title.

Across his last 10 club games, the young forward has seven goals and three assists to his name, and has played almost every minute for his team.

McInnes is placing great importance on him right now, and his faith in Naderi is being repaid in spades.

However, there is also reason for Rangers to approach with caution moving forward.

Former Manchester United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider last week that Premier League clubs have scouts watching Rangers "weekly" and are sure to be impressed by Naderi.

The ex-recruitment boss outlined that while Rangers are doing everything they can to keep hold of him long-term, that prospect will become increasingly tricky once concrete interest appears.

Former Aberdeen chief executive Keith Wyness told Football Insider on Sunday that Rangers chairman Andrew Cavenagh is "tempted" to personally fund a permanent move for loanee Badredine Bouanani.

The Stuttgart loanee is quickly becoming a fans' favourite at Ibrox, but the option to buy him for £15million feels distant at the moment.

However, Wyness believes that Cavenagh's imminent sale of his £4.5billion-rated company ParetoHealth could potentially see him invest a big chunk into Rangers once again.

The boardroom expert suggested that could be used to fund a permanent move for Bouanani, provided that he continues to impress.
