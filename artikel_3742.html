First-person fishing adventure DREADMOOR is coming soon to gamers worldwide, but if you think it’s a cozy adventure, think again. The game is set in a dark, submerged world rotting from past events. Hence, fishing in these perilous waters won’t be easy, and there will be dangers to stay away from.

DREADMOOR is an atmospheric first-person game where you have to survive in a flooded world. To do so, you’ll need to use your fishing skills, combat abilities, and knowledge of exploration. In this decaying world, taking charge of your trawler is only the first step.

As you travel across scattered settlements, you can trade your catch and make money. This lets you upgrade your vessel and craft essential equipment.

The creatures in the world of DREADMOOR are not your ordinary fish. Many of them have mutated across the sea and land, and they can be a threat to your survival. If you are careless, you can end up becoming the prey yourself.

Dozens of dangerous fish live beneath the surface. You have to scour the waters and find the best spots. To catch different species, you must bring unique baits and cast them alongside your line. You also need to look out for scraps, resources, and other materials that have been decaying in the barren lands and water.

While survival is the core gameplay loop of DREADMOOR, there is no single way to do so. With an ever-lurking threat at every corner, you will have to do your best to outlast the dangers on land and sea.
