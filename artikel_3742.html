Darkest Dungeon 2's next bit of DLC is a short run, combat focused gauntlet where you must do everything in your power to please the audience

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The original Darkest Dungeon may have gotten some new DLC earlier this year, but that doesn't mean that Red Hook Studios are abandoning their sequel. No, in fact the next bit of DLC for Darkest Dungeon 2 was teased only a couple of weeks ago, with a new trailer shared today actually showing off what it's all about.

It's called The Butcher's Asylum, as pleasant a name as any other, and it's a "stand alone gladiatorial arena game mode," that's due for release sometime in 2027. "The long-silent bells of the remote Asylum ring once more - souring the valley below with discordant and unsettling vibrations," the DLC's Steam page describes. "Garish heraldry, hastily stitched by unskilled hands, festoons the hilltop. Deep within the crumbling masonry of the Asylum, frenzied inmates compete in merciless bloodsport with monsters corralled the kingdom over. And to the victors of these debauched contests, the Butcher’s favour: a cure for their madness."

This DLC isn't veering too far away from its roguelite origins, as it is still run-based, just on the shorter side and focusing more on the combat system. On your runs you'll need to prepare for the fights ahead, come across "insane enemy combinations never before possible," and spin a fun wheel which nets you prizes like new battle modifiers, charms and jinxes. There's also a new crowd meter, "a unique feature to the Asylum, can grant powerful effects to your team if you cater to the Crowd's demands. Failing to do so will only earn their scorn which raises the stakes and puts your success in jeopardy!"

The Butcher's Asylum also introduces a new playable character, The Ringmaster. You pick her up by winning bouts in the arena, and then you can recruit her in any game mode. She also serves as the narrator for the DLC, likely because of the passing of the main game's narrator Wayne June; Red Hook made clear earlier this year that they would never use generative AI to recreate June's voice, so I'm glad to see they haven't backstracked here.
