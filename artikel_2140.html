Wolves could hit January jackpot with Brighton transfer agreement for Femi Azeez

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Wolves have had a positive season so far as they look to bounce back to the Premier League at the first attempt.

The decision to replace Rob Edwards with Cesar Peixoto in the summer was a bold one, but the Portuguese head coach has settled well to life in England, even if Wolves’ performances have been inconsistent.

The 1-1 draw with Wrexham last time out means they go into the break in a strong position, with Wanderers unbeaten in six and just one point off top.

Yet, there are clear areas Peixoto’s side can improve, and the recruitment team will already be aware that a few new additions are required in January to help the group in their promotion push.

Of course, the January window is always difficult, so it’s not going to be a case of overhauling the squad. Instead, the focus will be on bringing in one or two quality players that can enhance the group.

Whilst fans will all have their own opinions, the consensus is that Wolves need attacking reinforcements, so a new winger and striker will be the priorities.

And, Femi Azeez would be a smart signing for Wolves to strengthen their options out wide, as the likes of Rodrigo Gomes and Mateus Mane have been inconsistent this season, with Bertrand Traore yet to make his league debut due to injury issues.

With Jean-Ricner Bellegarde looking uncomfortable out wide, Wolves are clearly lacking a natural winger apart from Rafiki Said, so Azeez could slot in and become a key player immediately.

After starring with Millwall last season, Azeez got his Premier League transfer in the summer, joining the Seagulls.

But, a hamstring injury means he is yet to feature for Fabian Hurzeler’s side, although Azeez is closing in on a return to full fitness.

However, with Brighton flying, it’s hard to see where Azeez will fit in, as Hurzeler is obviously not going to want to disrupt a team that is playing so well.

Plus, Yankuba Minteh is now back involved after his own injury issues, and, given his quality, he will presumably stay ahead of Azeez in the pecking order. When you add in that the classy Kaoru Mitoma is scheduled to return this month as well, it’s difficult to see how Azeez gets involved regularly.

Obviously, things can change quickly in football, but, if Azeez is struggling for game time, then Brighton will recognise that a loan move in January could be just what he needs, otherwise there is a very real chance he will have spent the entire 2026/27 campaign without a run of games, which could stall his development.

From Azeez’s perspective, that’s far from ideal as well, and he will be desperate to get back out on the pitch after a frustrating lay-off.

So, a move to Wolves may appeal, as they are a club that will give him the platform to play week in, week out, and the style of play that Peixoto adopts should suit him.

There is an emphasis on wide play from the Wolves chief, who wants his wingers to hug the touchline, stretch the play and get at their opponents.

That makes Azeez the ideal fit, and the prospect of the Nigerian international linking up with the likes of Fer Lopez and Raul Jimenez in the New Year is one that would worry rivals at this level.

Following on from that, as outlined above, the January window is about Wolves making a few tweaks that can help them win promotion, and there’s no doubt Azeez fits the bill.

This is a player that scored 11 goals and registered seven assists in 37 Championship games last season with the Lions, so he is proven in the second tier.

Again, that’s something that is crucial in January, as you need players to hit the ground running, and Azeez should be capable of doing that given his pedigree at this level.

As it stands, Wolves will head into the New Year desperate to add more quality in the final third, and Azeez is the sort of statement signing that could make the difference during the final months of the campaign.
