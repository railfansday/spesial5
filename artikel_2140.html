QPR and Bradford City won’t be shocked by Jack Wilshere, Luton Town situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Luton Town have turned around their recent form after making a slower start to the new League One campaign compared to their rivals.

The Hatters went into the new season targeting a top two finish, with their sights set on returning to the Championship.

And they have worked their way back up to fourth in the standings after the opening 14 games of the term, winning seven and drawing three.

This has left Jack Wilshere’s side five points behind leaders Huddersfield Town, and only two adrift of second place Stockport County.

One aspect of the new campaign that hasn’t quite worked out for Luton is the arrival of Harrison Ashby from Newcastle United.

The full-back joined on loan from the Magpies, and has so far featured in eight of their 14 league games this season, including making three starts.

The 24-year-old hasn’t made much of an impact for the Hatters, and is struggling to break into Wilshere’s starting lineup on a consistent basis.

He played the full 90 minutes of the 2-0 win against Forest Green Rovers in the FA Cup last weekend, but that was his first time in the starting XI since 19 September, where he played against his former side Bradford City in a 1-0 loss.

Ashby previously spent the second half of last season on loan with the Bantams, where he played just eight times for Graham Alexander’s side in the league.

The defender struggled to prove his worth for the Yorkshire outfit, who were hoping his arrival could bolster their promotion hopes.

Yet he didn’t even feature in the play-offs, as they lost to Bolton Wanderers in the semi-finals.

And his previous loan stint prior to moving to Valley Parade was also quite underwhelming for QPR, where he was unable to show he could compete at a Championship level.

Ashby is behind Isaiah Jones in the pecking order under Wilshere, and it’s difficult to see that changing in the immediate future.

But he has also fallen behind Hakeem Odoffin as well, even though the midfielder is not naturally a full-back.

The 28-year-old has found himself moved into that position while Ashby was out with a groin injury, even playing there with Jones moved into a more advanced role as a winger.

Wilshere has found this solution, and this may leave Ashby out in the cold, unless he can find a way to convince the manager that he deserves a place in the team now that he is back fully fit.

If Ashby is unable to earn his way back into the Luton team between now and the January transfer window opening, then Newcastle may have to act.

The 24-year-old doesn’t look capable of being able to reach the level needed to become a part of their plans in the Premier League, even with their disappointing start to the new season.
