Celtic vs Besiktas: TV channel, live stream and kick-off time

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Celtic return to Europa League action on Thursday night as Besiktas visit Glasgow.

Martin O’Neill’s side are looking to get off the mark in the league phase after losing their opening three matches, while Besiktas have seven points from their first three games.

Here is everything you need to know ahead of the match.

Celtic face Besiktas in the UEFA Europa League on Thursday, November 5.

The match takes place at Celtic Park and kicks off at 8pm.

Yes. The match will be shown live in the UK on TNT Sports 3, with coverage scheduled to begin at 7pm.

Yes. The game is available to stream in the UK through HBO Max.
