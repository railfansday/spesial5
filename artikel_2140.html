Six goals in 11 consecutive wins: Ex-Norwich City man stars for European club after £14m sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Norwich City bid farewell to Borja Sainz in the summer of 2025, following a fruitful two-year stay with the Norfolk outfit. NCFC signed the Spaniard from Turkish club Giresunspor and he enjoyed a steady first season with eight goals and two assists in 39 outings.

However, it was the 2024/25 campaign when Sainz would emerge as a real Championship star. The winger scored 19 goals and laid on four assists in 43 appearances for the Carrow Road club that season, prompting significant transfer interest before Porto paid a little over £14m to bring him in.

Sainz is now in his second season with the Portuguese giants. And, just like he did at Norwich City, the 25-year-old has grown into a starring role in his sophomore year.

Sainz had a solid debut year at Porto after joining from Norwich City, managing seven goals and six assists in 47 games across all competitions. He was not a nailed-on starter in Liga Portugal with 12 of his 30 league outings coming from the bench, but he settled well.

He has had to wait for his chances this term too, with all of his first eight league appearances coming off the bench despite scoring in three of those outings. Sainz has started the last three in the Portuguese top-flight though, and he has rewarded Francesco Farioli’s faith with three goals and an assist.

The ex-NCFC man has scored in back-to-back-to-back Liga Portugal games against Gil Vicente, Vitoria Guimaraes and Estoril, providing an assist against the latter too. He now has six goals and an assist to his name in 11 league matches this term.

Porto have won all 11 of those games to move into a commanding position at the top of the table. Sainz has made four appearances in the UEFA Champions League as well, but is yet to get off the mark in that prestigious competition.

Norwich City have had some real hits in the transfer market in recent times. Like Sainz, Gabriel Sara starred at Carrow Road and ended up earning a big move to Europe, while Mo Toure and Pelle Mattsson have become highly influential players at Carrow Road.

However, in terms of the money made and the level of success he found, Sainz takes some beating. He joined for nothing from Giresunspor and was sold for over £14m, making for a sizeable profit.

He’s gone on to prove his abilities after NCFC too, emerging as a key scorer at Porto. Sainz will be eager to keep delivering as Farioli and co see how long they can retain their perfect start to the Liga Portugal campaign.
