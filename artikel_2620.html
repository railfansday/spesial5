Le’Veon Bell Goes on Hilarious Rant About DK Metcalf

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Former Pittsburgh Steelers running back Le'Veon Bell once again ripped DK Metcalf after another subpar performance in Sunday night's loss to the Cincinnati Bengals.

Metcalf had a crucial offsides penalty and another drop in the first half.

"DK Metcalf don't give one f*** about catching a football bruh," Bell wrote on X.

Bell then went on a video rant, ripping the Steelers' offensive approach and Metcalf.

"What the f*** are we doing? We came out wanting to pass the ball. Mike McCarthy wants to come out first drive passing! Why are we passing the ball? We got Jaylen Warren in the backfield. He ready to run, but we came out passing. Not only did we come out passing. Guess who we're passing to? DK f***ing drop Metcalf!"

Metcalf finished with two receptions on six targets for 54 yards and a touchdown. The TD came when the game was pretty much over, however.

"Gotta love garbage time points," Bell wrote on X.

— Le'Veon Bell (@LeVeonBell) November 16, 2026

Last Monday, Mike McCarthy said that he looks at Metcalf's recent lack of production firstly as a reflection on himself as a play-caller and said he's focused on how he can get Metcalf more involved.

"Well, I think just like anything. I look at it as a play-caller. That's where I always start anything that I'm looking at offensively. Am I putting DK in position to be successful? McCarthy said. "So, that starts with our game plan process with our game plan process, and that's definitely one of many things that we look at each and every week."

Steelers offensive coordinator Brian Angelichio also touched on how to ignite Metcalf's production.

"We got a tremendous amount of confidence in DK and we got to look at, are we putting DK in the right position to be successful?" Angelichio said Monday.

"So there's some things that we're going to try to do to help him and certainly get him more ops to be effective because certainly he's a really dynamic player and we have a lot of confidence in him to be that player."
