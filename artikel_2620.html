Kennedy Blair nears triple-double in MSU women's basketball win

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Kennedy Blair fell just short of a triple-double while helping the Michigan State women's basketball team pick up a second road win.

Blair had 19 points, 10 assists and added eight rebounds for the Spartans in their 75-53 victory over Harvard on Sunday, Nov. 15.

Blair was 7-of-12 shooting from the field while recording her second double-double of the season. She was among three players in double figures scoring for MSU (4-0).

Theryn Hallock knocked down three 3-pointers and had 17 points and five steals and Monique Bobongie finished with 15 points for the Spartans. Blair, Hallock and Bobongie combined to shoot 58.8% (20-for-34) from the field while leading MSU.

The Spartans held Harvard to 3-of-12 shooting in the second quarter while owning a 16-6 advantage that quarter to own a 39-25 lead at halftime.
