Cooper DeJean exits with concussion and Cowboys take lead over Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Cowboys have taken an early lead in Dallas and the Eagles have lost a top defensive player.

Eagles defensive back Cooper DeJean took a hit to the head while making a tackle on the fourth play of the game, went to the locker room for a concussion check, and was quickly ruled out.

The Cowboys’ offense marched down the field on an 11-play, 77-yard drive that ended with a Javonte Williams one-yard touchdown run, and the Cowboys took an early 700 lead.

Eagles quarterback Joe Milton is back in Dallas to face his old team, and if the first drive is any indication, Philadelphia is going to need to Milton and the offense to score plenty of points.
