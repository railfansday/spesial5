‘Hates it here’ – NFL fans in awe as Dallas Cowboys star repeats answer 12 times in press conference

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Dallas Cowboys DT Quinnen Williams was speaking to reporters ahead of their Week 11 game against the Philadelphia Eagles.

Being on the Cowboys means players will face constant media attention and scrutiny, as they represent one of the most popular franchises in the country.

However, Williams didn't give the reporters much. In fact, he just kept repeating the same six words as a response whenever he was asked a question.

Strange press conferences are far from a rare occurrence in the NFL, but Quinnen Williams' press interaction may have set a new record for strangeness.

Regardless of the question being asked of him, the Dallas Cowboys DT repeatedly gave reporters the same non-answer.

"I'm gonna keep that to myself," Williams repeatedly told reporters whenever he was asked a question.

Understandably, this press interaction caught the attention of several NFL fans, who were taken aback by Williams' overstated lack of candour.

One fan joked: "Most consistent Cowboys performance all season."

A Dallas Cowboys fan didn't see the humour, pointing out an issue with the franchise: "We are not a serious team."

Another fan pointed out an important strategy that the reporters missed: "Could have just asked if there is anything he will NOT keep to himself."

A fourth fan suggested Williams' behaviour is in pursuit of a Cowboys exit: "He wanted to be traded – he never talked like that with the Jets. With reporters!"

A fifth fan echoed these sentiments with a far more succinct statement: "He hates it here."

And lastly, one final fan claimed Williams' frustration extends beyond the media: "These guys are obviously tired of the fans and media at this point. I'm sure being one game from .500 and watching your fans sell their tickets by the truckload to the opponent isn't helping any of this"

The Cowboys have had a frustrating season, currently sitting second in the NFC East with a 4-6 record. For a team with the history and fan following of the Cowboys, this is far from ideal.

And it appears their poor record and overall struggles this season have started to impact their players, with Williams' press conference raising serious questions about his commitment to the team.
