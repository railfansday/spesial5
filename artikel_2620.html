Buffalo weather forecast calls for cold and rain on Thanksgiving night

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

One of the best games of this NFL season may be played in some of the worst weather.

With the Chiefs in town to play the Bills on Thanksgiving night, Buffalo is expecting a cold, rainy evening.

The current forecast for Orchard Park, New York, where the Bills’ Highmark Stadium is located, calls for an 88 percent chance of evening rain. The Thanksgiving high temperature is expected to be 40 degrees and the low is forecast to be 36 degrees.

The conditions won’t be favorable for either Patrick Mahomes or Josh Allen to put up big passing numbers, but sometimes bad weather games are fun to watch in the comfort of home after a big Thanksgiving dinner. The Chiefs-Bills game kicks off at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC.
