LIVE DISCUSSION: Brooklyn Nets at Minnesota Timberwolves, 7:30 PM ET

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Brooklyn Nets are on national television tonight, and we're in for a treat: Mikel Brown Jr. will make his first career start with Nolan Traore deemed out at least 8-10 weeks earlier on Monday.

While Brown Jr.'s start is fun, there isn't anything positive about the Traore news. The sophomore guard was averaging 13 points on 40.4% shooting from three, proving to be a downhill point guard with a growing arsenal. The timing of the news is simply brutal for a Nets team that needs just about anything to feel good about.

Julius Randle is also back in Minnesota on the big stage… as a Net. The forward spent two seasons with the Timberwolves, helping them reach the Western Conference finals before joining the Knicks and eventually landing in Brooklyn. He'll return to Target Center on Monday night with the Nets looking to snap a four-game losing streak.

On the other side, the Timberwolves are 12-5, have one of the league's toughest defenses, and are coming off a 104-95 win over Memphis. Anthony Edwards is out with a right knee injury, giving Brooklyn a little more room to work with. But that's about it.

Who: Brooklyn Nets (3-12) at Minnesota Timberwolves (12-5)

Where: Target Center, Minneapolis, Minnesota

OUT: Nolan Traoré (right hamstring strain)

OUT: Jaylen Clark (right hamstring strain)

OUT: Donte DiVincenzo (right Achilles tendon repair)

"The Wolves have arguably the best defense in the NBA to start the season, and a lot of their success is due to their ability to limit three-pointers. Minnesota is only allowing 35 three-point attempts per game, fifth-fewest in the league. Even more impressively, they're holding teams to 32.8% from downtown, third best in the league. It's hard to get shots up against this team, and without Traore, scoring will be a bit harder for the Nets to come by tonight. Look for Egor Demin to return to the starting five and for Mikel Brown Jr to get all the minutes he can handle."
