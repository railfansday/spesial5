"Going to be tough" for Steelers LB Alex Highsmith to play this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

It looks like the Steelers will be without linebacker Alex Highsmith for their Black Friday game against the Broncos.

Highsmith did not practice on Tuesday and head coach Mike McCarthy said, via the team’s website, that it is “going to be tough for him to make it this week.” Highsmith had two sacks against the Eagles last Sunday and has 5.5 sacks on the season.

Wide receiver Ben Skowronek (calf) was the only other Steeler listed as out of practice on Tuesday. Linebacker Nick Herbig (hip, ribs) was listed as a limited participant.

Wide receiver Michael Pittman (foot) and guard Gennings Dunker (knee) were full participants. Both players remain on injured reserve, so the Steelers will need to activate them if they’re going to play against Denver.
