Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sitting here on the outskirts of our Milky Way galaxy, it's hard to imagine the galactic machinations that led to our Goldilocks spot around a very welcoming yellow dwarf star. But for decades, astronomers have suspected that the internal dynamics of massive galaxies like ours play an outsized role in regulating star formation.
