Vegan Stuffing

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Listen, we all know that stuffing is the best part of Thanksgiving dinner, but because of the butter, eggs, and chicken broth that usually go into classic stuffing, it's a dish that vegans can't always enjoy—until now! We created a stuffing that's so delicious, you might even be convinced to make a few of your other holiday classics into vegan Thanksgiving versions.

Start your stuffing off by preheating the oven to 200° and slicing the bread into roughly 1” cubes. Spread the cubes in a single layer on a large baking sheet and bake for about 20 minutes, until the bread is nice and crisp.

Remove the bread from the oven and turn the temperature up to 350°. Grease a 13” x 9” baking dish with cooking spray and heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add onion to the skillet and cook for about 5 minutes until lightly charred in some spots, then add the celery and garlic and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the celery starts to turn translucent. Pour in the vegetable stock and scrape up any browned bits—this is where all the flavor is. Season with salt and pepper.

Mix the bread cubes and chopped herbs in the vegetable stock mixture until well combined, then transfer to the prepared baking dish.

Cover the dish tightly with aluminum foil and place it in the oven to bake for 40 minutes. Carefully remove the foil and continue baking for another 35 minutes, until the stuffing is golden brown and crisp on top.

Any leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. You can also prepare this dish ahead of time, as it’s the kind of dish that we think tastes even better after sitting for a day.
