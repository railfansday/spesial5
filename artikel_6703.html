The Editors’ Table: New York

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Executive Members are invited to join our annual Editors’ Table dinner in New York on 17 November, hosted by Vogue Business executive editor Hilary Milnes and US editor Madeleine Schulz.

This intimate, curated evening brings together a distinguished group of brand founders, decision-makers, and creative leaders from across fashion and beauty. Set in a relaxed yet sophisticated setting, the dinner is designed to provoke thoughtful conversation, shared perspectives and genuine connection among peers.

To request an invitation, please complete the form below. Spaces are extremely limited and will be allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

This event is exclusively open to Executive Members. You can find out more about Executive Membership here.

Location: Midtown Manhattan (exact location will be shared with confirmed guests)

If you have any questions, please contact: VBevents@vogue.com
