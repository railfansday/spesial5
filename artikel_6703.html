When Does Spotify Wrapped Come Out? Find Out the 2026 Release Date and How to Make Sure You Don’t Miss Yours

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It’s nearly Thanksgiving, so Spotify users are expecting their Spotify Wrapped any day now. The campaign—which includes a summary of your listening habits for the year—usually drops around the end of November or the beginning of December, so it can’t be long before it’s released.

So, when will Spotify Wrapped come out this year? How can you make sure that you don't miss yours? And when is the data collected?

Read on to find out everything you need to know about Spotify Wrapped.

The streaming service has yet to confirm an exact date for this year’s Spotify Wrapped, but based on previous years, we can make some educated guesses on when it might be.

Over the years, Wrapped has been released in late November or early December, so we expect it to follow the same pattern for 2026.

A majority of these dates fell on a Wednesday, so December 2 is looking very promising for 2026.

Spotify Wrapped is an annual campaign provided by the streaming site, giving readers a summary of their listening habits for the past year.

It usually includes five musicians, songs, and musical genres that the user has listened to over the year in a fun graphic that you can then share on social media.

Spotify Wrapped first dropped in 2016, as a rebrand of 2015’s “Year in Music.”

Speaking ahead of the 2024 drop, Spotify’s vice president of partnerships and marketing Marc Hazan said, “What started as a look back at the music and audio that defined the year has become a global cultural phenomenon in its own right, a moment driven by the passion of hundreds of millions of fans. We’re thrilled to debut 2024 Spotify Wrapped to more fans than ever before this year, celebrating the hundreds of millions of ways that they’ve streamed.”

You’ll want to make sure that you have the most updated version of the app so that you can get your Spotify Wrapped for 2026.

To ensure that you're using the newest version of the app, go to the app store on your phone and check for available updates.

With artists like Bad Bunny, Drake, and Taylor Swift predicted to top the 2026 lists due to major album releases and tours, you won’t want to miss your roundup.

Spotify has never confirmed the exact start and end dates for its listening data window for Spotify Wrapped. However, we do know that they start collecting data at the start of January and conclude sometime in November.

What is known is that Spotify begins collecting data at the start of January and concludes sometime in November.

Speaking to Mashable during a Wrapped press presentation in December 2024, the senior director of product Molly Holder explained, “We start at the beginning of January with all of your listening that begins right then at the beginning of the year, and it goes all the way through collecting data up until a few weeks before we launch the Wrapped product.”

This means Spotify likely stops collecting data for its annual Wrapped campaign in early to mid-November, so the music you listen to after Wrapped and before January 1 may not count.
