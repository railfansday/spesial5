Vista Social AI Analyzes 1.2 Million Brand Posts: Opening a Video With a Question Lowers Engagement on Every Network Measured

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Free 2027 report: 1 to 3 minute videos get 36% more engagement on YouTube, and videos with a voice beat music-only videos on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube

NEW YORK, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Opening a video with a question reduces audience engagement on all seven social networks measured, according to a new analysis of 1.2 million posts by Vista Social. The research, which used Vista Social's AI to label images and videos from more than 22,000 brand profiles, found that opening with a bold statement does better than a question hook on all seven networks.

The full research report is free to read at vistasocial.com/research/social-media-content-performance/

The findings challenge several common assumptions about content length and format. While short-form video is often prioritized, YouTube videos between one and three minutes long got 36% more engagement than the same brand's other videos. Instagram videos under 15 seconds got 17% less. A human voice also made a clear difference: videos with someone speaking got 18% more engagement on YouTube and 14% more on TikTok than the same brand's videos without a voice, while videos with music only did 10 to 15% worse.

Every figure compares a brand's posts with its own other posts.

"Most advice about what content works comes from a few viral examples or from what marketers say they do," said Vitaly Veksler, CEO of Vista Social. "Our AI looks at every image and video our customers upload, so we can measure what actually works across more than a million posts. The lesson is practical: let people talk, put the point in the first seconds, and save the call to action for the end."

The analysis is part of Vista Social's 2027 research series, which also covers social media benchmarks by industry, the best times to post, how often brands should post and how agencies run social media, at https://vistasocial.com/research. Unlike surveys that rely on what marketers say they do, the research uses AI to label the creative features of each post and measures how each one relates to engagement. The data is anonymized and aggregated. No figure is published unless it rests on at least 50 brand profiles from at least 10 Vista Social accounts, and no customer or brand is named.
