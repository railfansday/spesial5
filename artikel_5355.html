10 of the best-selling video games of all time, from Tetris to Minecraft

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

A selection of 10 best-selling video games of all time, from Tetris to Minecraft, with publisher-reported sales figures where available and the history behind each title

A falling-block puzzle created in the Soviet Union in 1985 and an island-building life simulation released on the Nintendo Switch in 2020 have something in common. Both are among the best-selling video games of all time. The 35 years between them cover several generations of consoles and a wide range of genres.

This article gathers 10 of those games. It is a selection, not a complete ranking, and the titles appear in order of first release rather than by sales. That order also tracks the hardware they arrived on. The earliest entries ran on the Nintendo Entertainment System and the Game Boy. Later ones launched on the Wii, the Switch and consoles from Sony $SONY and Microsoft $MSFT.

Game sales figures are moving targets. Most of the totals here come from the publishers or their parent companies. Those totals keep climbing as new copies sell. A number reported in one year can be out of date the next. Each figure in this piece is tied to the company that reported it.

The figures do not always measure the same thing. Take-Two Interactive $TTWO, the parent company of Rockstar Games, reports units shipped for Grand Theft Auto V and Red Dead Redemption 2. Shipments and sales are separate measures. Nintendo, Microsoft and CD Projekt describe their totals as copies sold. Most figures in this piece carry the phrase "more than." That wording marks each one as a floor at the time it was reported, not a final count. Nintendo's figure for Wii Sports is an approximate one instead. Two entries, Tetris and the original Pokémon games, appear without any sales figure.

Hardware bundles shaped several of these totals. When a game comes packed in the box with a new console, every system sold in that package adds a copy to the game's count. Nintendo paired Super Mario Bros. with the NES this way. Wii Sports came with the Wii in many regions. In 1989, Nintendo also packed a version of Tetris with the Game Boy.

The selection also spans many genres. It moves from puzzle and platform games to creature-collecting role-playing, motion-controlled sports and sandbox building. It also includes open-world crime, fantasy role-playing, kart racing, a frontier western and a life simulation. Each slide that follows covers what the game is, who made it and when it arrived.

Tetris is a falling-block puzzle game that Alexey Pajitnov created in the Soviet Union in 1985.

The whole game rests on one mechanic. Blocks drop into the playing area, and the player arranges them on the way down. There is no story to follow and no cast of characters to learn.

In 1989, Nintendo put a version of Tetris in the box with its Game Boy handheld. That pairing carried a puzzle game designed in the Soviet Union to Nintendo's portable audience.

Tetris did not stay tied to the Game Boy. Versions of the game have been released on many platforms. Its popularity carries across those machines rather than resting on any single one.

Super Mario Bros., which Nintendo released for the Nintendo Entertainment System in 1985, has sold more than 40 million copies, according to Nintendo. The game established the side-scrolling platformer format that later Mario games built on.

Many of those copies came from bundling. Nintendo packaged Super Mario Bros. with the NES console itself. Many players got the game in the hardware box instead of buying it as a separate cartridge.

That history affects how the figure compares with other best-selling Nintendo games. The total tracks the reach of the NES as much as demand for Super Mario Bros. as a stand-alone purchase.

Super Mario Bros. is also the only 1985 release in this selection with a reported sales total. Tetris arrived the same year, but it carries no figure here.

The design is the game's other lasting contribution. In a side-scrolling platformer, the screen moves sideways as the player runs through a level. The player jumps between platforms to reach the end.

Super Mario Bros. built its levels around that structure. Later entries in the Mario series returned to the template that the 1985 NES release had established.

Pokémon Red, Green and Blue are the Game Boy games that launched the Pokémon series. Game Freak developed them, and Nintendo published them. They first went on sale in Japan in 1996.

The work was divided between the two companies. Game Freak built the games. Nintendo released them on its own hardware.

Each version centers on one activity. The player catches creatures called Pokémon and then trains them. That catch-and-train loop places the games in the creature-collecting role-playing genre.

The Game Boy was Nintendo's handheld console. It ran on batteries rather than plugging into a television. A player could carry the whole game in a pocket and keep building a collection away from home.

Wii Sports has sold about 82.9 million copies, according to Nintendo. The company released the game in 2006 alongside its Wii console.

The disc collects five sports: tennis, baseball, bowling, golf and boxing. Every event runs on the Wii's motion controls. Players swing, throw and punch with the controller in hand. The game turns those physical movements into actions on screen.

That design made the game inseparable from its hardware. Each event was built around the Wii's motion-sensing controller, so Wii Sports could only be played on the console it launched with.

In many regions, buyers did not pick up Wii Sports as a separate purchase. Nintendo packed it in the box with the Wii. In those markets, the game reached every household that bought the console in that package.

That means its sales total reflects demand for the Wii as well as demand for the game itself. Both the design and the distribution of Wii Sports traced back to the same machine. It is one of the best-selling Nintendo games.

Minecraft had passed 300 million copies sold, Microsoft $MSFT said in 2023. The game is commonly cited as the best-selling single video game.

Mojang Studios released the full version of Minecraft on November 18, 2011. That finished release came three years before Mojang changed hands.

Minecraft is a sandbox game. Players dig into the world to mine resources. They then turn those materials into structures of their own design.

That loop of gathering and building is the core of the game. The player decides what to collect, where to dig and what to make from it. In a sandbox, the player sets the goals.

Microsoft bought Mojang, the studio behind Minecraft, in 2014. Microsoft reported the 300 million milestone nine years after that acquisition. The total therefore covers copies sold both before and after the game came under Microsoft's ownership.

Grand Theft Auto V has shipped more than 200 million units across its platforms, according to Take-Two Interactive $TTWO.

Rockstar North developed the open-world crime game. Rockstar Games published it.

The game launched on September 17, 2013. Its first release came on two consoles — PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360.

Take-Two's shipment total combines every platform the game has appeared on. It does not describe sales on any single console.

The story takes place in San Andreas, a fictional state. Instead of following a single lead, players control three protagonists.

Those three characters are Michael, Franklin and Trevor.

CD Projekt has reported sales of more than 50 million copies of The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt. CD Projekt Red released the fantasy role-playing game on May 19, 2015.

The game is based on the Witcher books by the Polish author Andrzej Sapkowski. Its setting and characters come from that series of novels.

That literary source sets it apart from the other nine games in this selection. Each of those began as an original video game property. None was adapted from a book series.

Mario Kart 8 Deluxe is the best-selling game in Nintendo's Mario Kart series. Nintendo has reported sales of more than 60 million copies.

Nintendo released Mario Kart 8 Deluxe on the Nintendo Switch on April 28, 2017. That reported total counts copies of the Switch edition.

The game did not begin life on the Switch. It is an expanded version of Mario Kart 8, a kart racer that first came out on Nintendo's Wii U.

Its place at the top of the Mario Kart series came from that expanded version. Nintendo's best-selling kart racer is a reworked earlier release rather than an entry built from scratch for the Switch.

Red Dead Redemption 2 has shipped more than 60 million copies, according to Take-Two Interactive $TTWO. Rockstar Games released the western on October 26, 2018.

The story is set in 1899. Its central character is Arthur Morgan, an outlaw.

Morgan rides with the Van der Linde gang. The player follows events from inside that group, through Morgan's eyes.

Rockstar Games also published Grand Theft Auto V, which launched in 2013. Red Dead Redemption 2 arrived from the same publisher five years later. It traded a modern fictional state for a frontier setting at the close of the 19th century.

Animal Crossing: New Horizons is a life simulation that Nintendo released on the Switch on March 20, 2020. Nintendo has reported more than 45 million copies sold.

The premise is simple. The player arrives on a deserted island and develops it.

That design places the game in the life simulation genre. Progress comes from shaping one place over time rather than from winning a race or finishing a level.

New Horizons is the latest release in this selection. It arrived 35 years after Tetris and Super Mario Bros., which both date to 1985.

It is also one of two Switch games here. The other is Mario Kart 8 Deluxe, which came out on April 28, 2017. Nintendo has reported more than 60 million copies sold for that racing game.
