In late August, Christian Boothe was persuaded to move from Leicester City to Newcastle United.

The exciting teenage midfielder swapping the Foxes for the Magpies.

Now it has been revealed he has signed his first professional contract with Newcastle United on the day of his 17th birthday).

(ED: Players generally cannot sign a professional contract until their 17th birthday. Under The FA’s Rule C11, players must be 18 or over to enter a playing contract unless they are 17 and not in full-time education.)

A club statement on 28 August 2026 revealed that the young Leicester City starlet was one of three that Newcastle Unuted had persuaded to swap clubs as NUFC look to recruit the best young talent possible for the future: ‘Newcastle United can announce that promising youngsters Kyran Thompson, Christian Boothe and Kyle Healy-Matthews have joined the club’s Academy.  Thompson joins the club from Arsenal, with Boothe and Healy-Matthews arriving from Leicester City and West Ham United respectively.’

Having signed his Newcastle United pro contract, a now 17-year-old Christian Boothe reacting: “It is a surreal moment for me and my family, I’m so grateful to sign it with such a big club like Newcastle. I can’t wait to see what the future holds for me. I wanted to sign because there were better opportunities for different pathways into the first-team. I’m loving my time here. The name, Newcastle United, speaks for itself. Who wouldn’t want to join Newcastle? It’s a lovely club with so many good footballers that have played here. I’m also so lucky that I knew a few of the boys before I joined; Jayden (Kouossu), Muawiya (Ghanem) and Kyle (Healy-Matthews) as well because my brother played at West Ham. To have those friendships already definitely helped me to settle in.”

The exciting teenage midfield talent looking to take inspiration from Lewis Miley and Elliot Anderson: “You’ve seen the likes of Lewis and Elliot play for Newcastle’s first-team after coming through the Academy. I try to study their game and learn what I do differently to them and vice-versa, to try and follow in their footsteps. I’m really enjoying it at Newcastle so far and I think I’ve been performing well for the Under-18s. This season, I want to try and break into the Under-21s squad as well as train with the first-team. If I do get my shot with the first-team, it’s about making sure I express myself.”

Academy Director Steve Harper (pictured at the top of this article): “Christian has settled into life at Newcastle United really well since arriving last summer and we’re delighted to see him sign his first professional contract with the club. Joining a new environment always brings challenges, but he has adapted quickly, embraced the standards we expect and shown a strong commitment to developing his game. He’s worked hard to build relationships with his teammates and coaches, and that positive attitude has helped him make a strong start to his time here. This contract is recognition of the progress Christian has made so far, and we’re looking forward to continuing to support him as he develops and works towards achieving his potential.”
