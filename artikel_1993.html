Milos Kerkez’s stats prove Hungarian media wrong about Liverpool star

Milos Kerkez shone for Hungary against Northern Ireland but was barely even praised by their national media after a disappointing draw.

Kerkez has come in for some criticism at Liverpool this season after more inconsistent performances under Andoni Iraola.

The 23-year-old struggled throughout his debut campaign at Anfield and now concerns have been raised over whether he is the best long-term fit to replace Andy Robertson.

The international break has seen him impress for Hungary at left-back as he looks to nail down a regular spot in the Reds' starting eleven.

Hungarian media outlets were hardly even complimentary of Kerkez's showing against Northern Ireland on Tuesday, even though he was a standout performer for his country.

He will be looking to carry over the strong form shown to club football as Liverpool face rivals Man United this weekend.

Hungary needed to beat Northern Ireland on home turf in midweek to finish top of their Nations League B group.

Barnabas Varga put his country ahead in the first half, but Justin Devenny clinched a famous point for the Green and White Army with a rocket of a strike in the 65th minute.

Kerkez featured down the left flank despite the disappointing draw, but national outlets Nemzeti Sport and NB1 each only handed him a rating of six out of 10.

The former described his performance by writing: "In the middle of the first half, he made a great run down the left flank, but his cross was neutralised by the Northern Irish – we should have seen more of that. Of course, we took the lead even without that, so his crosses into the box weren't really missed for a long time. It seemed much more important to shut down the Northern Irish right flank, and he did that."

While the latter said: "Midway through the first half, he showed that he still had the drive to support the attacks. He wasn’t afraid to get stuck in."

Sources have exclusively revealed to Football Insider that Liverpool boss Iraola is keen to stick with Kerkez for the time being despite recent inconsistencies.

His latest performance should prove why, after the 23-year-old created the most chances (4) of any player on the pitch, had 94 per cent pass accuracy and completed two successful dribbles.

He also won four of five attempted ground duels, the second most of any player, and won both of his attempted aerial duels, with the only thing letting him down being his 38 per cent crossing accuracy (3/8).

Kerkez deserved heaps of praise for being the Magyars' main attacking threat, yet the media barely even recognised his impressive exploits across the pitch.
