Brighton issue official fan safety statement ahead of Getafe clash in Madrid

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Brighton and Hove Albion fans are expected to travel in numbers for their Conference League clash at Madrid based club Getafe.

It's the Seagulls third continental away trip this year having already competed in Norway at Tromso and the Czech Republic at Jablonec - both matches ended 0-0.

Brighton will seek to get their first Conference League away victory in Getafe, who are 12th in La Liga.

Brighton & Hove Albion Supporter Services posted: "Police have advised that Brighton fans arrive to the fan meeting point by 17:00 for your own safety.

"There will be a police escort to the stadium. The fan meeting point is the Avenida María Zambrano area, where you will find bars and restaurants.

"This is a 20 minute walk to the stadium. All bars and restaurants have been made aware of the club's presence and will be welcoming.

"Please note that the slightly longer route (turning left at the roundabout at the FMP) has been advised by Spanish police."

Brighton & Hove Albion Supporter Services added: “All Brighton fans attending the Getafe game will be entering through Gate 7 (Visitantes).

“Please check what section your ticket is in before going to your row and seat. Section 27 is on the left, section 28 is on the right.”

On facing Getafe, Brighton head coach Fabian Hurzeler said: “We expect a tough opponent because I think when you play here in Getafe and when you see them playing also, for example, at the weekend against Sevilla, you know it's a team that knows how to win.

"It's a team that plays quite vertical, long balls, second balls, [who are] good in personal duels, and can be very intense in personal duels. Therefore, we expect a team that has a clear style of play combined with individual quality."

The Seagulls will be back in Premier League action this Sunday at th Amex Stadium against Brentford.
