Sources: Sheffield United respond to Newcastle and Sunderland's Sydie Peck interest

Newcastle United and Sunderland will find it difficult to sign Sheffield United midfielder Sydie Peck in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The Blades are "aware" of the growing interest in the 22-year-old from the North East, but are not prepared to sell him in the middle of the season.

The England U21 international has already started 13 games in the Championship this term, and has even chipped in with four goals and two assists.

Sources say that Newcastle and Sunderland will have to wait until next summer for the chance to land Peck, as he is crucial to Sheffield United's play-off push.

The South Yorkshire outfit are aware that more Premier League clubs are likely to come to the table if they fail to get promoted next season.

They are planning to stop Newcastle and Sunderland in their tracks in January, but it could be a different story with Peck next summer.

Peck is building a growing reputation for himself at Bramall Lane, and that has attracted attention from Newcastle of late.

That interest is not expected to fade any time soon, with the starlet continuing to catch the eye on a weekly basis in the second tier.

Peck's contract situation also creates an issue for Sheffield United moving forward.

The midfielder's deal is due to expire in the summer of 2028, which is likely to leave next summer as their final chance to cash in on him.

Chris Wilder's side won't want to risk losing their star man on a free, which could play directly into the hands of Newcastle and Sunderland.

The duo are already keeping a close eye on Peck's development, and are both likely to be ready to pounce on him next summer.

Former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness told Football Insider last week that Newcastle must find a clear role for new signing Matias Fernandez-Pardo if he is to flourish on Tyneside.

The 21-year-old joined from Lille in a £51million deal in the summer, but is still yet to register a single goal involvement for his new club.

Fernandez-Pardo has already been used as a central striker, a winger and an attacking midfielder by the Magpies, something Wyness believes must change moving forward.

The boardroom expert feels that Newcastle must nail down a settled role for the Belgian in order to get the best out of him.
