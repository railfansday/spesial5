Aston Villa youngster TJ Carroll attracts ‘interest’ from Barnsley FC, Portsmouth and Swansea City

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Barnsley are reportedly among the clubs showing interest in Aston Villa prospect TJ Carroll.

The 19-year-old is a centre-back who has featured regularly for the Midlands club at youth level, but a first-team breakthrough has not yet materialised.

With competition for places proving stern in Villa’s star-studded squad, a temporary move could prove beneficial for the young defender.

According to the Daily Mail’s Tom Collomosse, Carroll is set to depart Villa Park on loan in the winter transfer window.

League One strugglers Barnsley are said to be among his admirers, but could face competition from higher up the pyramid.

Championship outfits Swansea City and Portsmouth have both been credited with interest, as have Norwegian side Viking.

Posting on X, Collomosse said: “TJ Carroll set to leave Villa on loan in Jan. Has interest from Viking (Villa’s CL opponents) plus Barnsley, Portsmouth and Swansea. Featured in pre-season but not featured in a senior game this term.”

Barnsley are thin on the ground and desperately need to get some new faces through the door when the next window opens.

Teenage defender Arley Kay has been used at centre-back of late despite being a right-back by trade, partnering Sam Gale.

Reds head coach Daniel Stendel has spoken to the club’s hierarchy about potential reinforcements in January, with Barnsley sat dangerously in the relegation zone.

After his side’s 2-1 defeat to Wigan Athletic last week, he said: “I talked with some players right after the game. We have some ideas. I will speak with Neerav tomorrow and then we'll have a look at what we can do to help the players.

“The one and only thing we can do is stand up. The derby [against Sheffield Wednesday] is a big game, not only in our situation, but also for the club and for the supporters. It's a big opportunity for us to give a little bit back from what we got today from our supporters.”
