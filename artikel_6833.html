28 Winter Wedding Guest Dresses for Cold-Weather Ceremonies

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

If summer nuptials call for layers of floating chiffon and bright prints befitting a beach ceremony, winter wedding guest dresses are decidedly more pared-back. Velvet gowns stand in for linen maxis; fluttery cocktail dresses are replaced by floor-length options with long sleeves; the palette gets moody, rich, and inky. If you have a cold-weather dress code on the horizon and want to add a new piece to your closet, start with our edit.

These 28 options are geared toward guests (though, attention, bridesmaids: If your prompt is black tie and you’re on a deadline to find a dramatic silhouette in an appropriate color, you’ll find something here too) and feature standouts from contemporary brands like Staud and Aje, as well as designer favorites. Tove’s popular Zenaida dress glows in golden satin, Erdem makes jewel tones interesting with floral detailing and a fringe hem, and Fforme’s long-sleeve stunner is perfectly apropos for the dressiest of weddings. We even found a short dress that works: It’s a beaded metallic midi from Cos, and a great pick for an upcoming courthouse ceremony. Don’t let the chilly temperatures deter you—but do ensure you have a proper coat to assist with backless numbers and short-sleeve options.
