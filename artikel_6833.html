We Tested Dozens of Perfumes to Find the 5 Best Baccarat Rouge 540 Dupes—Here’s Our Ranking

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

We may earn commission from links on this page, but we only recommend products we love. Promise.

"Are you wearing Baccarat Rouge?" This might be the greatest compliment—someone noticing that I've doused myself in a perfume that cost more than my very first cell phone. This expensive fragrance is a total status symbol and has gone viral more than a handful of times. The poetic blend of spicy saffron and effervescent jasmine mingle with warm amber and cedar to create a luxe, mysterious, soft perfume that automatically makes anyone more attractive, IMO. It's pretty iconic if you ask me. But the price, at $360 for a 2.4-oz bottle, is high enough to give someone a heart attack.

Now that the perfume is so popular, a wave of Baccarat Rouge 540 dupes has hit the market from brands like Dossier and Fine’ry. Plus, many brands have been inspired by the fresh and woodsy DNA of the original to create their own iterations that smell shockingly similar. Can they really hold a candle to the O.G.? I set out on a mission to sniff all of the so-called "dupes" on the market and rank them for how similar they smell to the original. I found some really good options.

Now, there’s no replicating something as iconic as BR540, but these five affordable picks come pretty darn close if I do say so myself.

We can't break down the dupes without acknowledging the original, okay? Baccarat Rouge 540, by Maison Francis Kurkdjian (one of the most famous French perfumers of the 21st century who owns his own brand but is also the creative director of perfumery at Christian Dior), was developed to celebrate the 250th anniversary of the Baccarat crystal line. The note breakdown is actually defined by three "auras," according to Kurkdjian: A sparkling opening, followed by a warm middle, and a woodsy and sweet base.

I've been using this perfume on and off for the last five years, and it's still one of the most unique scents I own. The way it smells upon first spritz versus how it develops on my skin is truly a nod to perfume artistry (sorry, I'm getting nerdy here). There's a reason everyone is so obsessed with this jasmine-dominant fragrance: It's light and airy yet warm and enveloping—it's really unlike any other perfume (you know, until everyone started making similar versions). Yes, it's extremely expensive, but all you need is one spritz to last all day long, meaning the bottle will last a pretty long time.

If you're a little scared of the price tag, we've got a slew of cheaper options below.

Although Dossier now creates many of its own original fragrances, the brand started with creating affordable dupes to expensive, iconic perfumes. So it's no surprise that Ambery Saffron, its counterpart to Baccarat Rouge 540, smells the most similar of every option I've sniffed. It's not exact when you place them side by side, but on skin, I can't really tell that it's two different perfumes.

I'd say Ambery Saffron is a bit more woodsy than Baccarat, which dries down extremely sweet on my skin. But that's likely because it doesn't have the ambroxan and sugary notes that give BR540 that syrupy quality. However, I really do love the addition of orange blossom in this Dossier perfume. It creates a teensy hint of citrus that works well with jasmine and saffron.

Everything Sol De Janeiro does is tropical in nature. So the Cheirosa '68 body and hair mist is basically what you’d get if you swirled Baccarat Rouge with some beachy notes. This fruity scent is much juicier than the original BR540, all thanks to pink dragonfruit. It's more mouthwatering and brighter than the sparkling scent of Baccarat Rouge, too, making it perfect to spray on alongside your Baccarat Rouge (or a dupe) or if you want something a bit more luminous. Consider it Baccarat’s carefree, summery cousin.

Surprisingly, this celebrity perfume bears a striking resemblance to Baccarat Rouge. While the notes appear slightly different, the sparkling quality and hints of sweetness are reminiscent of what you get with the jasmine-based juice. But what I loved most about this when I spritzed it all over is how long it lasts. Ariana is busy, so she can't be reapplying her scent all the time. I get it! And this fragrance proves it, with impressive longevity on skin and clothes. One spritz on the top of my hand, and I'll be sniffing the pear, praline, vanilla, and musk notes for days.

Fine’ry is known for creating similar versions of our favorite perfumes at super affordable prices. And, bonus, you can easily add to your Target orders. (Score!) The New Rouge—their take on Baccarat, of course—is a sexy, warm scent for everyone.

The sandalwood gives this a much deeper profile than the original. If Baccarat Rouge smells too traditionally "feminine" to you, then you'd probably love this. It's not nearly as sweet, leaning more into the warm, spiciness with an undertone of woodsiness. I would wear this more in the fall/winter or in the evening for that reason.

Sure, the bottles take me right back to high school. But this scent feels a lot more grown up than what I spritzed back then. In fact, this was the first-ever Baccarat Rouge 540 dupe I tried—and it's still one of the best to this day.

The notes are mostly different, but hit a lot of the same ideas. There's sandalwood for a grounding scent, amber for the cozy factor, and a subtle sweetness from sugar and musk. Unlike BR540, though, this one uses starflower (fitting, considering the name) and tangelo to create the sparkling quality of the original. It's definitely not the same scent, but you could probably fool some unsuspecting noses. And best of all, this has some serious lasting power and projection. You'd think you were wearing an expensive eau de parfum, not an under-$20 body spray.

If you’re looking for a dupe of the iconic Baccarat Rouge 540, chances are you’re probably on a budget. So before you start your shopping journey, make sure you set a target price range and stick to it. All of the options on our list are under $100—and some are even under $30—so there’s a truly excellent alt for everyone.

Disclaimer: While all of our picks are worthy alternatives to the O.G. Baccarat Rouge 540, none of them smell exactly the same (even if they come v close). If you want to be as close to the original as possible, pay close attention to the key notes to make sure they match up. BR540 features a mix of saffron, jasmine, ambergris, and cedar, and plenty of the perfumes listed above also contain all or a mix of the same.
