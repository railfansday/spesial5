Imagine a Parker House roll having a tryst with Asian-style milk bread and a classic French croissant—that’s the essence of salt bread, a.k.a. shio pan in Japan and sogeum-ppang in Korea. Each soft bun holds a rich secret: a nub of salted butter bundled within the shaped dough. I like to tear it in half to revel in the buttery, hollow center, then unravel each flaky layer, just like a croissant. —Hana Asbrink, deputy food editor

Lemons are a powerful ingredient. So why miss out on their full potential by only using their juice and zest? That was the thesis behind these bars. Think Lemonhead candy. It’s everything I love about classic lemon bars with the volume turned up to a reckless decibel level.

With a few pantry staples and a little time, this recipe yields a dozen delightfully chewy bagels that rival the most beloved of NYC shops. The trick?
