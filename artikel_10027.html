While I applaud the sentiment, the current generation of "AI" literally *cannot* "do our thinking for us" -- it can *simulate* what we *think* thinking is, but it cannot actually think, by definition, since (among other reasons) we're unable to explain to it (or ourselves) in any precise and complete way, what thinking actually is (or even is not) for us. It also can't truly create anything. It can only reorganize and distill the thinking that went into what humans have already written, drawn or photographed, that make up the its training data. It may prove useful in finding things people have thought up, that we've simply missed as being important, or that we thought were wrong at the time, but none of that is "thinking" -- it's association, which is an aspect of thinking, for sure, but not the whole thing.

Ultimately AI will have no effect on "what it means to be human". It can't, since it isn't, and won't ever be anything like us. If people are thinking that it will have such an effect, they're completely deluded, which admittedly is a common malady.

The only sense in which "AI" is "changing how we talk about being human" is just that: talk. It can't change how we *are* human, or how we do things as humans. If it's changing how we talk about being human (I remain unconvinced of that fact) then possibly that's a good thing, since our understanding about "what it means to be human" and other similar philosophical questions are important, and (IMHO) in all of modern history, those kinds of questions have been insufficiently valued and explored.

It's not going to help us *answer* any of those questions, except to regurgitate, combine and reframe the kinds of answers that philosophers have already come up with over the centuries, though the answers aren't really even all that important: it's the ability to form the questions in the first place that's important and understand how the possible answers to our own questions make us feel and decide what that means for us, individually and collectively. Even if AGI someday in the far-distant future becomes a thing, it won't be able to do that "for us" and if it's truly intelligent, that's what it'll say: "The only way for you to answer that is by answering it for yourself."
