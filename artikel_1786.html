Transfer news LIVE: Arsenal in ‘serious’ midfield move; Chelsea learn Scott price; Man Utd, Liverpool latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The transfer window will be thrown open again in just a few weeks, with Arsenal, Chelsea, Manchester United, Liverpool and Tottenham all getting ready to bolster their ranks for the second half of the campaign. Arsenal continue to be linked with Real Madrid’s Vinicius Junior despite the Brazilian signing a new deal with the La Liga giants in the summer. The winger featured heavily on the Premier League champions’ wishlist in the previous window and could be on the move again here. The Gunners also like the look of Bournemouth’s Eli Junior Kroupi and Antonio Nusa, as well as Paraguay international Eduardo Delmas.

Chelsea have learned that Bournemouth want a seismic transfer fee to part with Alex Scott at the turn of the year, while the Blues are also investigating other midfield targets for Xabi Alonso’s squad. Mauricio Pochettino’s Manchester United are plotting to raid Fulham for Calvin Bassey and Antonee Robinson as the Argentine’s first two signings.

Liverpool are also eyeing Mexican wonderkid Gilberto Mora, and they are also monitoring Lamine Camara, who almost joined Chelsea in the summer. Follow all the latest transfer news, rumours and gossip live below!

Virgil van Dijk is unsure where he will be playing his football next season, with his Liverpool contract due to expire in the summer.

The Dutchman, 35, has plied his trade at Anfield since 2018 and is now a two-time Premier League champion.

He will be out of contract in June, though, and has revealed in a new interview with Sky Sports that it remains unclear whether he will extend his stay. He is, however, clear that he expects to continue his playing career for several more years.

Per reports from BBC Sport, talks regarding a new deal are underway but an agreement is not close, and no resolution is expected before the new year. From January, Van Dijk will be free to sign pre-contract agreements with clubs outside England.

“Whatever's going to happen after this season, we'll see,” he told Sky Sports.

Bayern Munich have opened the door to selling Jamal Musiala, or renegotiating his contract, amid concerns he may struggle to get back to his best form after being diagnosed with a fainting condition.

Musiala, 23, left the footballing world deeply concerned in August when he collapsed on-pitch twice in the span of just a few days, during pre-season friendlies against Heidenheim and RB Leipzig.

He later revealed in a statement that he had been diagnosed with a “neurological dysfunction”, adding: “(These episodes) are currently simply part of my everyday life. I am receiving the best possible medical care in this regard, and I am very optimistic... there is no additional health risk beyond this.”

Musiala has since appeared in four league matches, three cup ties, and one Champions League match, tallying two goals and two assists in those appearances.

Bayern Munich’s decision-makers, though, are beginning to worry that Musiala, one of Bayern’s best-paid players, may not be able to replicate the form of his youth.

The club has opened the door to selling the former Chelsea youngster Musiala in upcoming transfer windows, though they acknowledge it may be hard to find a buyer given the player’s condition.

Arsenal are making a ‘serious’ move for Olimpia midfielder Eduardo Delmas.

The Paraguay international has yet to leave his home nation but has reportedly caught the eye of the Gunners’ scouts, and they have bene making enquiries regarding his quality and availability.

At 19, Delmas might come as a long-term investment, with reports in Paraguay saying the Gunners are very interested in a deal.

Despite his meagre years, the player has already made his senior international debut, notching an assist in a 4-1 friendly defeat of Singapore earlier this month.

Crystal Palace have confirmed the arrival of Bernardo Cueva from Chelsea.

The Mexican was the Blues’ set-piece coach, but he has now moved to Selhurst Park to become a more general first-team coach under new boss Thomas Frank.

Mykhailo Mudryk has revealed that he wanted to join Arsenal.

The Tottenham winger, on loan from Chelsea, admitted that before he signed for the Blues in January 2023, he had wanted to move to north London.

Before he made the move to Stamford Bridge, he had been heavily linked with a move to Mikel Arteta’s side, but ultimately, he decided to join Graham Potter’s Blues instead.

Now, he has declared that his real desire was a move to Arsenal, which never materialised.

Arsenal are searching for a new left winger, and a surprise target of theirs is Brentford’s Kevin Schade.

Teamtalk reports that the Gunners have registered an interest in the German, liking in particular his versatility across the frontline.

Arsenal are not giving up in their pursuit of Vinicius Junior.

Fichjaes claims that the Gunners are still monitoring the Brazilian, despite the forward signing a new contract at Real Madrid in the summer to stave off any further interest from the Premier League champions.

Chelsea have been told it will take over £100million to sign Alex Scott from Bournemouth.

That comes from Teamtalk, who claim that the Cherries are fully expectant of bids around that figure come the January window.

The Blues lead the race for the England midfielder, who is slowly making his anticipated return from injury.

The i Paper reports that Manchester United are plotting to raid Fulham for Calvin Bassey and Antonee Robinson.

Mauricio Pochettino is looking to strengthen his backline and has reportedly identified the Cottagers’ pair as the two to do so in the January window.

Standard Sport understands, however, that Fulham have opened talks with Bassey over a new contract in a bid to keep him at the club.

Fulham are in talks with captain Calvin Bassey over a new contract as they look to fend off interest from Manchester United, writes Standard Sport’s Sam Tabuteau.

The 26-year-old centre-back, whose contract expires next summer, is understood to be on United’s shortlist as they look to sign defensive reinforcements in January.

Fulham hold the option to extend Bassey’s current deal by a further year.

The Cottagers are keen to keep hold of Bassey, especially after Alvaro Arbeloa promoted him to captain in the summer.

Nothing has been agreed yet, but Fulham have opened talks with Bassey over a new contract and do not intend to sell him in January.
