GdS: Krösche announcement could come next week – how he will operate at Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Further confirmation has arrived that Markus Krösche will be joining AC Milan, and an announcement could come before the month ends.

La Gazzetta dello Sport confirm the news that been circulating for weeks now: an agreement has been reached with Krösche, and next week could be the perfect time to announce it to the world, at least as per the German director and the club’s plans.

Owner Gerry Cardinale made it clear that he will never again hand the club’s management over to a single executive. He will make the decisions himself, and in the summer he chose Ruben Amorim as the head coach, working closely with him and listening closely to his transfer desires.

In short, he wanted to avoid introducing the traditional role of sporting director, who at many clubs decides the coach’s fate. However, the intention to strengthen Milan’s sporting side has always been there, and Krösche has always been held in high regard by Cardinale.

Krösche officially stepped down from his role at Eintracht Frankfurt on October 18. This separation came after five and a half years of working together, with the highlight being winning the Europa League in 2021-22.

His best acquisitions were Randal Kolo Muani (bought for free after his time at Nantes and sold to PSG for €95m), Willian Pacho (bought for €13.65m from Antwerp and sold for €40m, also to PSG), and Omar Marmoush (bought for free after a year at Wolfsburg and sold to Manchester City for €70m).

“Over the past few months, I’ve been intensely reflecting on my personal situation and my professional future,” Krösche said upon his departure. “I’ve come to the conclusion that I need a change. I also believe that a fresh impetus could be helpful to Eintracht.”

The club recognised his great work: “Krösche has decisively shaped Eintracht Frankfurt. Winning the Europa League, participating in the Champions League, and the further growth of our team are testament to this extraordinary period.”

Milan are his next chapter. Krösche will be responsible for transfer operations, likely taking the title of Head of Football. He will work closely with Head of Football Intelligence Bobby Gardiner and Head of Recruitment Tiago Estevão, key figures who enjoy Cardinale’s trust.

Krösche will liaise with clubs around the world and with agents. He will have a lot of responsibility, but certainly not the final say, which will rest with Cardinale after the traditional consultation with the club’s internal working group.

Above all, it will be fascinating to see what Krösche will pursue and how he will balance the pursuit of high-potential young players (his strength in Germany and a long-standing objective for Milan) with the need for short- and medium-term results.
