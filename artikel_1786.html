Gary Neville delivers scathing Manchester United verdict after heavy Liverpool defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Gary Neville believes there has been a “remarkable” drop in Manchester United’s standards after the Red Devils sank to a 4-0 thrashing by Liverpool on Sunday.

The club were looking to propel themselves out of the Premier League relegation zone, helped by a bounce from the arrival of new manager Mauricio Pochettino, who took charge of his first match at Anfield.

The Argentine, however, was dealt a nightmare start, as Leny Yoro was sent off inside eight minutes for denying Alexander Isak a clear goalscoring opportunity, and Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz got the goals for Andoni Iraola’s brilliant hosts.

Pochettino bemoaned Yoro’s red card as “unfair” in his immediate post-match reaction, but he could not complain about his side’s performance on the night, as they were swept aside emphatically by their historic rivals.

On Sky Sports, former United defender Neville said that Pochettino had now seen “the worst” of his new club’s predicament.

“It's really desperate and it's tiring watching them,” he said.

“This is the worst Manchester United have ever been in 53 years.

“There's no time to work on this. Maybe this will be a good thing in the long run that Pochettino sees the worst of it.

"He's got a big job on here. There'll be an element of 'Let's just forget about this game because Yoro got sent off and it's with 10 men', but it rides into the game in midweek.

"From a defensive perspective, I've never seen anything like this.

"I grew up watching great defenders that were fantastic players that were aggressive. They were brilliant people, great fighters and had spirit.

"You watch this lot, honestly, it's unbelievable. They're going to have to advance money to [Pochettino] in January for defenders. They're probably going to need another couple next summer.

"Forget the rest of the squad for now, they've got to build from the back.

"Manchester United are the worst team in Europe defensively right now. You can't say anything more than that. It's unbelievable. 34 goals [conceded] in 15 matches.

"This is where we've got to where we're starting to write off 4-0 defeats at Anfield as if it's just the norm and what should be accepted.

"The club standards have dropped so far, it's incredible.

"But the fans’ standards haven't dropped. They expect a Manchester United team to be competitive every single week, and what they're seeing now, Manchester United have got the worst defence in the whole of Europe, and are currently second from bottom in the Premier League.
