Prediction: Hull City vs Brighton

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Simply looking to carry on from where they left off prior to November’s International break, Brighton will travel to Yorkshire on Sunday afternoon when they meet Hull.

With a stacked schedule from across England’s top-flight, there are plenty of attractive Premier League betting promotions available.

Ahead of Sunday’s showdown from Yorkshire, you can find all of the key Hull City vs Brighton H2H stats here.

Verdict: Pascal Gross over 0.5 shots on target

Starting with the hosts, while Hull might have received plenty of plaudits for their rugged approach since making their famous Premier League return this season, the Tigers are now struggling to put points on the board. Eventually falling to a gutsy 1-0 victory away at defending champions Arsenal prior to November’s international break, Sergej Jakirovic’s side have come tumbling down the standings and mark their return this weekend holding just a three-point buffer over the drop zone. Signing off October with a rollercoaster 2-2 stalemate against fellow Premier League newcomers Ipswich, Hull have now failed to win any of their previous nine consecutive appearances across all competitions (five draws, four defeats). Looking to claim a first Premier League victory since the end of August, the former Dinamo Zagreb boss will be bracing himself for a stern examination on Sunday afternoon.

Only making a single Premier League appearance since completing a summer switch to Yorkshire, former Newcastle defender Matt Targett remains on the sidelines with an ankle injury. However, missing out against Arsenal prior to November’s fortnight interval, Matt Crooks will return to Hull’s squad here.

Signing off October with back-to-back Premier League strikes, talismanic striker Oli McBurnie will lead Hull’s attack this weekend. Reportedly attracting plenty of interest despite only arriving in Yorkshire over the summer, Greek goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis will start between the sticks.

As for the visitors, astonishingly opening this weekend’s fixtures sat in silver medal position among England’s elite, Brighton have only continued to shatter a string of records since opening up the 2026/27 campaign. Last seen heading into November’s international break with a deserved 2-0 victory at home against Brentford, Fabian Hurzeler’s side have already claimed some heavyweight scalps so far this season. Along with signing off October with a 1-0 victory away at Manchester City, Brighton also laid down a remarkable marker on September 19th as they stormed their way to a famous 3-0 romp against defending champions Arsenal. Already putting 22 points on the board and with their sights set on punching an iconic Champions League ticket this season, Sunday’s guests have also gained a real reputation for their clinical displays in the final third. Notching 26 goals in their first 10 Premier League outings, Hurzeler’s high-flying squad hold the best attacking tally among England’s top-flight.

Working his way back to full fitness over the international break following a long-term hamstring injury, Kaoru Mitoma could be included in Brighton’s squad for the first time this weekend. However, Greek starlet Stefanos Tzimas remains on the sidelines with a serious knee injury.

German international Pascal Gross has registered eight direct goal contributions in his 10 top-flight appearances (four goals, four assists) so far this season and the veteran playmaker could once again play a leading role in Yorkshire. Attracting plenty of heavyweight interest over the summer, Yankuba Minteh could make a maiden Premier League start on Sunday afternoon.

Simply looking to carry on from where they left off prior to November’s international break, Brighton have rightfully enjoyed a string of plaudits from across the continent. Opening this weekend’s fixtures sat in silver medal position, Sunday’s guests are riding a serious wave of momentum. Meeting a Hull squad who make their respective return in Yorkshire in the midst of an extended nine-match winless rut across all competitions, the Seagulls will be smelling the chance to make another Premier League statement outside of Sussex. Likewise, signing off October with a sensational 1-0 victory away at Manchester City, Hurzeler’s record-breaking squad will feel they have been handed a potential opportunity on their travels.

Verdict: Pascal Gross over 0.5 shots on target
