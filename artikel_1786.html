League One table forecasted with Bradford City, Sheffield Wednesday, Huddersfield Town & Barnsley FC calculations

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Data experts Opta have forecasted League One finishes for Bradford City, Sheffield Wednesday, Huddersfield Town, Barnsley and Doncaster Rovers

After a busy weekend of League One action, data experts Opta have crunched the numbers.

The weekend kicked off with a South Yorkshire derby and a Jamal Lowe brace fired Sheffield Wednesday to a 2-1 win over Barnsley.

There was also a derby clash in West Yorkshire as Bradford City and Doncaster Rovers played out an entertaining 2-2 draw.

Huddersfield Town, meanwhile, were beaten 1-0 on home turf by Leicester City and head coach Martin Drury was left to rue the absence of their “usual spark”.

Away from Yorkshire, Reading were edged out by Bromley, Burton Albion lost to Luton Town and Leyton Orient thumped AFC Wimbledon. Mansfield Town and Milton Keynes Dons shared the spoils, while Peterborough United claimed a 2-0 win away at Plymouth Argyle.

Stevenage were 1-0 victors away at Notts County, Stockport County breezed past Cambridge United and Wigan Athletic defeated Blackpool.
