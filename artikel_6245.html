10 Spacious Villas in Morocco Fit for a Relaxing Group Getaway

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The bustling capital city has perennial charm, but beyond its walls, there’s so much more to Morocco worth exploring. Whether you’re gearing up for a hiking trip to the Atlas mountains, looking to immerse yourself in the sights and sounds of the buzzy souks or headed out to the coast, there’s a fantastic nearby address to know. Most of our favorite Morocco villas pair idyllic views with in-house chefs, housekeepers, and even security staff for a hotel-worthy break in your own space. Below are 10 of the best villas in Morocco to rent for your next trip.

We've vetted these listings based on Superhost or Guest Favorite status, ratings, amenities, location, previous guest reviews, and decor.

Bed & bath: 5 bedrooms, 5 bathroomsTop amenities: Heated pool, home cinema, sauna and steam room

This is the kind of holiday home that keeps everyone happy, making it perfect for a large family with different ideas of what a great holiday looks like. The interiors are modern, but Moroccan charm is preserved in the details. The five spacious bedrooms all feature en suite bathrooms and are spread across both floors. The large living area, with its circular arrangement of comfortable sofas, and the home cinema invite guests to relax and enjoy each other's company during their stay. A fitness center and a traditional hammam add a wellness touch to the home. There is a golf course right next door, should you fancy a round of golf.

Bed & bath: 8 bedrooms, 7 bathroomsTop amenities: Pool, hammam, pickleball court, pétanque, barbecue

Located just north of Marrakech, this villa gives groups a soft, quiet place to land after a busy day in the city—and plenty of activities to keep everyone busy on days spent on-site. Beyond the resort-style heated swimming pool lined with lounge chairs, the pétanque and pickleball courts encourage some healthy competition. Afterwards, spend a bit of relaxation time in the hammam or massage room, or kick back in the garden. Of note, two of the eight bedrooms are located in an annex of the villa and share a bathroom.

Bed & bath: 6 bedrooms, 6 bathroomsTop amenities: Heated pool, outdoor dining space

To reach this villa, you have to drive 30 minutes from Marrakech into the stunning Ourika Valley. On arrival, you’ll immediately notice the snow-capped peaks of the Atlas Mountains rising around the property. The 8,000-square-meter garden dotted with olive trees and cacti is the perfect setting for days spent lying by the large pool, playing pétanque, or cooking up a storm with produce from the organic kitchen garden. Inside, the house is bathed in sunlight and particularly stunning at dawn. There are six bedrooms, all featuring en suite bathrooms for ultimate comfort. Five of the bedrooms are in the main house—as well as the kitchen and the spacious living room featuring a dining space with a fireplace—while the final bedroom is in the annex, alongside the playroom and wellness area with a hammam.

Bed & bath: 6 bedrooms, 6 bathsTop amenities: Terrace, pool, hot tub, gym

Bed & bath: 6 bedrooms, 7 bathsTop amenities: Pool, terraces, Atlas Mountains views

Palais Les Orchidées is a palace-like six-suite villa just outside the center of Marrakech, combining grand architecture with pared-back interiors and views towards the Atlas Mountains. The property is surrounded by leafy gardens, with a large swimming pool and several terraces providing plenty of space to relax outdoors. Inside, all six suites have king-size beds and en suite bathrooms with bathtubs and showers, making this a particularly comfortable option for a large group or several couples traveling together. Despite its peaceful setting, you won’t be too far from the action: the villa is around 15 minutes by car from some of Marrakech’s best-known sights, including the Koutoubia, Bahia Palace, and Jardin Majorelle, and around 20 minutes from Marrakech Menara Airport. Breakfast can also be served at the villa for an additional charge.

Bed & bath: 5 bedrooms, 5 bathsTop amenities: Rooftop terrace, private pool, breakfast chef

This classic Marrakech riad is a bohemian dream with vibrant, patterned tiles, a mishmash of colorful, soft furnishings, and a beautiful outdoor courtyard—bringing good vibes only. You’ll be well taken care of here; daily housekeeping, a breakfast chef, and a night guard will ensure everything goes swimmingly. Take an alfresco lunch before a dip in the plunge pool, then settle on the daybed for a leisurely afternoon with a view—there’s a panoramic vista of both the buzzing medina and the Atlas mountain range. Take note: You’ll have to climb some steps to reach this villa, and there’s no parking outside, so if you’re taking a car, you’ll need to leave it a short distance away. However, there are a slew of excellent restaurants and hammams within walking distance and good public transport links right outside.

Bed & bath: 9 bedrooms, 9 bathsTop amenities: Outdoor kitchen, sauna/steam room, gym

Like most experiences in Morocco, stepping into this palatial villa’s vast grounds is a sensory feast from the get-go. Plaster pink walls, an eclectic mix of bright furniture, ornate artwork, patterned rugs, and glittering chandeliers are wrapped around the central turquoise jewel of the outdoor pool. It has nine huge bedrooms, at least one of which has a statement roll-top tub in the corner, plus nine bathrooms—so no waiting in line for the shower here. It’s a great choice for bachelor or bachelorette parties and big group getaways, with multiple comfy gathering spaces for catch-ups with a view. First, head to the meeting room to grab a drink from the bar, but take them to the rooftop terrace to maximize the experience—the views at sunset are fantastic. Extra bookable spa and hammam treatments make a stay in this Moroccan palace all the sweeter.

Bed & bath: 8 bedrooms, 8 bathsTop amenities: Private pool, courtyard, outdoor shower, record player

This isn’t a riad, but a former fondouk—a 19th-century inn that used to accommodate travelers before they set sail on trading voyages. Now, however, it has been transformed into a chic villa for 15, set around four distinct courtyard spaces decorated with fragrant lemon trees and mature prickly cacti. Locally sourced clay walls bring traditional flair that keeps you cool on hot Moroccan nights, and there’s a pool to jump into for welcome midday dips. Active types, take note. This villa sits in the ancient walled city of Taraudant, which offers easy access to both the High Access and Anti-Atlas hiking routes. It's the perfect place to wind down after a long day of mountain treks.

Bed & bath: 4 bedrooms, 4 bathsTop amenities: Terrace, courtyard, fireplace, private cook

Bed & bath: 2 bedrooms, 3 bathsTop amenities: Private chefs, private heated pool, terrace

With a hilltop location looking down onto sparkling sea views, even by Moroccan standards, the views here are unbeatable. A clever design means the home provides ample opportunity to totally immerse yourself in the landscape. Run a bath in the outdoor tub right on the beachfront, and awake to sunrise light streaming through floor-to-ceiling windows framing gentle ocean waves. Mealtimes are sorted thanks to the in-house private chefs who’ll cook up anything you please to be enjoyed alfresco with a glass of wine on the terrace next to the private heated pool. There’s a slew of activities on offer in Essaouira—tell the hosts what you’d like to do and they’ll make it happen.
