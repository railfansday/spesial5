8 players set to miss Wrexham vs Middlesbrough ft £4.3m ace

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Championship season is now in full swing as we enter November, and midweek action is back on the agenda for the division's 24 clubs.

One of three second-tier offerings on Wednesday night is Wrexham's clash with high-flying Middlesbrough at the Racecourse Ground, with three points much-needed for both Phil Parkinson and Kim Hellberg, albeit for very differing reasons.

The Welsh side have been extremely below-par so far this term, and having lost three of their last four fixtures, pressure is firmly starting to build on Parkinson's shoulders with results nowhere near matching the club's lofty ambitions.

Boro, meanwhile, have been unstoppable in recent weeks, winning four of their last five and scoring 14 goals during that timeframe, including Saturday's 3-0 triumph over Charlton Athletic.

However, whilst both head coaches will be relying on the services of several star names to achieve a crucial victory, there are a total of eight players who are set to miss the encounter in total, and Football League World looks at all of their respective predicaments.

Sebastian Revan is the first of four players Wrexham are set to be without for Wednesday's encounter, with the defender still on the road to recovery from a serious hamstring issue.

The 23-year-old suffered such misfortune during his loan spell with Burton Albion last season, with his last competitive appearance for the Red Dragons coming all the way back in March 2025.

As such, Parkinson recently expressed his hopes that the left-back is fit enough to secure a loan move in the January window.

Fellow left-back, Liberato Cacace, has endured an injury-hit spell at the Racecourse Ground since joining from Empoli, with the New Zealand international still yet to feature this season.

Cacace, 26, featured at the FIFA World Cup three times after making just 13 appearances last term, with it being revealed that the defender continues to suffer recurring calf issues.

However, the timescale of his latest recovery is still not yet known.

Wrexham's poor start to the campaign has also coincided with an injury sustained by Welsh international forward, Nathan Broadhead, who posted 14 goal contributions last season.

Having featured in the opening three matches of the league season, the 28-year-old hasn't featured since due to a thigh injury which also ruled him out of Craig Bellamy's squad for the three-week international break between September and October.

Callum O'Hare missed Friday night's 1-0 defeat against his former club, Sheffield United, with a reported ankle issue, and remains a doubt ahead of the clash with Boro.

Wrexham paid £6.5m for the playmaker on Deadline Day in September, but he is yet to get off the mark for his new club in terms of goal contributions after netting in his final league appearance for the Blades.

However, in yet another cruel tale of misfortune for Parkinson, he revealed on Tuesday that Sam Smith will miss a prolonged period of action after sustaining a knee injury.

The striker replaced Ollie Rathbone at half-time, but after playing through the pain barrier in the game's latter stages, "his knee swelled up significantly the next day," said the Wrexham boss.

Smith, 28, will now see a specialist to learn the full extent of the setback.

The last absentee for Parkinson comes in the form of Josh Windass, whose experience and quality was vital in the club's play-off bid last season.

Windass has endured an injury-hit start to the season, having missed the entirety of pre-season and the competitive campaign's early weeks, and after just six appearances this term, was forced off in the early stages of the 1-1 draw at Derby County with an ankle issue.

The injury prognosis wasn't as bad as first envisaged though, albeit the 32-year-old has missed the subsequent four matches.

Alfie Jones is one of two players set to miss this encounter from the Middlesbrough camp, with the experienced Canadian international suffering more injury misfortune at the Riverside Stadium.

Having missed a fair chunk of his first season on Teesside, the 29-year-old suffered a blow to the calf in the dramatic 4-3 victory against Norwich City back in September.

It was expected that Jones would only miss a matter of weeks, before Hellberg revealed that he would be sidelined for longer due to an untimely setback.

The North East side also look set to be without summer signing, Rokas Pukstas, for a sixth successive encounter.

Having been recruited from Hadjuk Split for a reported £4.3m, the American youth international has played just six minutes of Championship football since his arrival, which came against Queens Park Rangers on September 5th.

Pukstas was then an unused substitute for three straight games before suffering a hamstring injury whilst partaking in private training, leaving him to miss, at least, the entirety of last month's action.
