Sunderland plot move for ex-Southampton and West Ham star to replace Le Bris

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Premier League side Sunderland could target ex-Southampton and West Ham United player Jose Fonte to replace Regis Le Bris.

Now in November, sackings are happening more and more across English football. Regis Le Bris has credit in the bank for his promotion from the Championship with Sunderland in 2024/25, while also guiding them into the Europa League in 2025/26, but pressure is mounting.

His side are bottom of the Premier League heading into today's game against Chelsea at the Stadium of Light, with Sunderland having also lost to Dinamo Zagreb at home on Thursday. Now, it could mean Jose Fonte comes in to replace him.

Fonte spent the most significant period of his English career with Southampton, joining from Crystal Palace in January 2010 and making 288 appearances across seven years at St. Mary's.

He was instrumental in the Saints' remarkable rise from League One to the Premier League, becoming a hugely respected figure before earning a move to West Ham in January 2017. His spell in east London was much shorter, however, lasting just over a year and producing 24 appearances.

Fonte went on to enjoy successful spells with Lille and Braga before finishing his playing career at Casa Pia in 2026, bringing the curtain down on an extraordinary 24-year senior career. Since then, he has been coaching under Le Bris at Sunderland, joining back in July.

Now, Alan Nixon has revealed Sunderland are plotting for him to replace Le Bris in the dugout. He says that the 42-year-old is highly regarded by the club's chiefs and that they are eyeing him if Le Bris moves on.

Fonte was far more than just a dependable centre-back during his playing days because he was a genuine leader, particularly at Southampton, where he became one of the most influential figures in the club's modern history and rise through League One and the Championship and to the Premier League.

He captained the Saints through their rise from League One to the top flight, helped establish them and later enjoyed success with Portugal and Lille. Given his presence, communication skills and ability to command respect in a dressing room, it is hardly surprising that he wanted to move into coaching.

Sunderland might be a step too far for him as a head coach at this early stage, but his personality and leadership qualities suggest he could eventually become much more than an assistant. He has the attributes to develop into a manager in his own right but Sunderland not going for a more established and experienced alternative will be a head scratcher for many.

Back in July, speaking to The Sunderland Echo, it was revealed why Fonte was brought in by the club’s director of football Florent Ghisolfi. He said his experience of football at the highest level would be a big benefit to the club this season, as they are competing in Europe for the first time in over 50 years.

Ghisolfi explained Fonte's "strong leadership skills" which would help complement the current coaching group. Speaking about the former Southampton and West Ham figuure, he said: “Jose joins our coaching team with a wealth of experience as a player at the top level.

“He will bring that experience, along with tremendous energy, to a technical staff that is already performing outstandingly under the guidance of Regis.

We believe those qualities, combined with his strong leadership skills, will add great value to our club as we look to push forward both in the Premier League and on the European stage. We’re delighted to welcome Jose to Sunderland AFC.”
