Birmingham City set to welcome back James Beadle for Stoke City clash, but doubts remain over six-goal star

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Birmingham City have gone into the November international break top of the Championship. BCFC have been really impressive to start the year, and have continued winning games, even with some injuries to key players.

Chris Davies’ side have gone the their last two games without goalkeeper James Beadle and top-scorer August Priske. However, as reported by Birmingham Live, Davies has provided an update on the pairs fitness going into the internationals.

“James (Beadle) definitely will be (back),” Davies confirmed but he couldn’t be so sure on Priske. “August (Priske) did everything to get ready but he couldn’t,” the Blues manager said.

“I presume he is going to make more strides in the next week or so. I’d hope he was back after the international break but I couldn’t be definite on that because it’s how it settles down.”

Davies also provided an update on Marc Leonard who is not part of the Birmingham City squad after being omitted at the start of the season following an Achilles injury. The BCFC boss said he is looking to be back in action after the New Year.

Both Beadle and Priske could return for Birmingham City’s trip to Stoke City on 21 November, but the striker’s availability would be the bigger boost.

Beadle is the established first-choice goalkeeper after missing the wins over Millwall and Swansea with a knock. Davies has confirmed he will be available for Stoke, although Ryan Allsop has impressed in his absence.

Priske’s return is less certain, but potentially more significant. The forward provides their main focal point in attack. Davies is hopeful the international break will give him enough time to recover, but has stopped short of guaranteeing his availability.

While BCFC have continued scoring without him, having their main goal threat back for such an important away fixture would be a considerable boost.

Priske has been a key player for Birmingham City this season and has really come into his own after a slow start last term. However, his injury has not slowed up the Blues as many might’ve thought.

Davies has adjusted his side well and in Priske’s absence, BCFC have still been an attacking threat. Max Bird has come into the side as part of the front four with great success.

Jay Stansfield has also found his scoring touch with three goals in his last two games. The Blues have adjusted very well to Priske’s absence, and while they were prefer to have their top-scorer in action, the club have shown they are still strong enough without him.
