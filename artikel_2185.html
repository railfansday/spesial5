Portsmouth must take action over 22-year-old’s League One loan as Mousinho confirms clear ‘need’: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Portsmouth signed Hayden Matthews as a highly-rated centre-back prospect in the winter of 2025. He had emerged as one to watch at Sydney FC and quickly made a good impression at Pompey too, with his performance away at Oxford United in the 2024/25 season one fondly remembered.

But, Matthews was loaned to Bradford City for the 2025/26 season after a string of injury blows and centre-back signings. Unfortunately, the 22-year-old has struggled for minutes at Valley Parade, and manager John Mousinho has stressed his need for game time.

Speaking to The News on Matthews’ situation, Mousinho said earlier in November: “He’s gone out on loan with every intention to play, but unfortunately Bradford’s (defensive) record is one of the best in the country. We’ll see what happens – they (Matthews and Josh Knight at Reading) need to play.”

Matthews is a highly promising centre-back. His time at Portsmouth has been disrupted, but there are few doubts over his potential having displayed his composure on the ball, progressive abilities in possession and knack for reading the game.

A loan to Bradford City felt ideal, allowing Matthews to play in a solid defensive setup at a level that would require him to develop his physical game. However, he didn’t get any opportunities under Graham Alexander, and his struggles have continued under Michael Duff too.

All of George Pratt, Macaulay Gillesphey, Aden Baldwin, Reece Welch and Matthew Pennington have ranked ahead of Matthews in the pecking order. Given that they have kept nine clean sheets and conceded only eight goals in 13 League One matches, it seems unlikely that the setup is changed to make way for the Pompey loan man.

Portsmouth can not allow this to continue for the entire season, and Mousinho’s words suggest they won’t. Barring a miraculous turnaround, the Championship side must take action and recall Matthews this winter.

Matthews’ Bradford City stay has not gone as hoped, and his struggle for game time may seem like a put-off for some admirers. However, the Portsmouth man is clearly a talented defender, and he has plenty of room to grow with meaningful minutes in the right setup.

It means that other League One clubs should not hesitate to move for Matthews when Pompey recall him. Ideally, he can play in a team similar to Bradford City, who prioritise a strong defensive base.

That will ensure Matthews has plenty of opportunities to develop the areas of his game he needs to focus on.

Matthews is under contract at Fratton Park until 2029, and there is still scope for him to forge a future at the Championship side. However, finding minutes to grow as a player is a must for 2027.
