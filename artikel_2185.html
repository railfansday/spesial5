What Alex Neil must learn from Millwall’s 3-0 defeat to West Brom heading into the international break

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Millwall have had a terrible opening third of the 2026/27 season. The Lions suffered their seventh loss of the campaign and their second this week as West Brom were 3-0 victors at The Hawthorns on Saturday.

Alex Neil’s side did well to nullify a WBA side who were unbeaten in five before the weekend inside the first 45 minutes. However, the second half saw the South London outfit capitulate defensively as they made it 27 goals conceded this term.

Millwall will welcome the pause in Championship action for two weeks as they reset. There are certainly many lessons learned from their latest defeat that the Lions must use as fuel over the international break to come back stronger heading into the busy winter period.

This time last year, when Millwall headed into the international break, they were sat 6th in the table and just four points off the top two. Now after their second consecutive 3-0 loss, the Lions are 16th in the Championship and six points from the top eight.

Neil’s side have scored six more goals than they did this time last season. However, they have conceded seven more times. Saturday highlighted the gulf in quality and momentum between a side in real play-off contention like West Brom and a team in disarray like Millwall.

The Baggies had an xG of 2.98 whereas the South London outfit only had an xG of 0.62. The Lions also had just nine touches inside the opposition’s box and won just 48% of their ground duels.

All three goals Millwall conceded against West Brom weren’t entirely at the fault of the New Zealand international. However, it’s safe to say that the Lions won’t achieve anything this season with him as their number one.

Max Crocombe has conceded 22 goals this term out of 10 games and has a save percentage of 55.1%. He is currently averaging 2.2 goals conceded per match, and the 33-year-old is struggling to cope with the demands of the Championship.

Against WBA, the New Zealand international made an error leading to a goal and made just three saves. With Lukas Jensen reported to be nearing a return to fitness and his save percentage standing at 100% from his two games this term, Neil won’t select Crocombe again if he doesn’t have to.

One positive Millwall can take from the clash against West Brom is the return of duo Josh Coburn and Tairyk Arconte. Coburn played 54 minutes before being substituted, whilst Arconte featured for the last 10 minutes.

Both players have been crucial to the limited success Neil’s side has had this season. Coburn especially had a strong start to the term before an injury sidelined him for two months. He has won 69.2% of his aerial duels this campaign and has an xG on target of 3.14.

Arconte was also missed by the Lions. He has netted three times in nine games for Millwall in his debut Championship season. The 22-year-old has a successful dribble rate of 60% and has made 45 touches inside the opposition box.
