Countries race to secure Cop31 deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

COP31 climate talks have overrun their schedule as countries race to agree on a new global climate deal, following criticism of an earlier draft that made no mention of fossil fuels. The EU has warned that the current proposal risks turning the summit into a “fossil fuel summit”, as negotiators work towards a revised, potentially final agreement expected later today. France 24 International affairs editor Jamie Smith-Maillet tells us more.
