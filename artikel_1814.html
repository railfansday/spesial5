​Women’s Super League predictions: Arsenal, Chelsea and Man City backed as big favourites on MD11

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

The 2026-27 Women's Super League season is already 10 games in, and both ends of the table are taking shape.

Charlton Athletic remain without a point to their name at the foot of the standings, but above them, Birmingham City, Crystal Palace, Brighton and Liverpool are all clustered within five points of each other.

At the summit, Chelsea sit one point above reigning champions Manchester City, while London City Lionesses are flying high in third place.

Arsenal have a lot of ground to make up, but they are made the biggest favourites for MD11 when they host West Ham this weekend.

Chelsea face Liverpool, and Man City take on Tottenham in some intriguing matchups. Here, we use Opta insights and the supercomputer to look ahead to the WSL action.

Chelsea have won 17 games against Liverpool in the WSL (D4 L5), their joint-most victories over any one side in the competition (also 17 wins v Everton).

And Opta's model is forecasting another Chelsea victory at St Helens Stadium, with the Blues handed a whopping 70.4% chance of victory.

That makes them the most favoured away team on MD11 by some distance, with Liverpool's 13.1% win probability coming in higher than only two other sides.

Liverpool have won only five of their 26 WSL meetings with Chelsea (D4 L17), though each of the most recent four of those have come at home, most recently in May 2024 (4-3).

Chelsea have won each of their last six WSL away games, only once before having a longer winning run on the road in the competition, eight straight wins between August 2015 and September 2016.

The Blues' expected goal difference of +25.3 is more than twice that of any other side in the WSL this term, with London City next on +11.7. Sonia Bompastor's team have both attempted the most shots (228) and faced the fewest (72), taking more than three for every shot allowed.

It feels unfair to label London City as a surprise package this season, given the investment into their squad this past summer. Can a team with a Ballon d'Or holder in their ranks really be considered a surprise package?

Putellas has created 18 chances, provided one assist and scored three goals already in the WSL, and her form has been integral to London City sitting in third place ahead of hosting Crystal Palace.

London City are the clear favourites, with a win likelihood of 67.2% to Palace's 14.8%. There is an 18% chance of a draw.

Palace have, however, won their last two away league games against London City without conceding (1-0 in 2022-23, 2-0 in 2023-24), though they have already lost away to the Lionesses in this season's Players Cup (2-4).

But Palace are winless in their last 14 away games in the WSL (D5 L9), since a 2-0 victory at Leicester City in October 2024. Having drawn each of their first three away matches this season, the Eagles have lost both of their last two.

London City have won six of their last seven WSL home games (L1), scoring at least twice in every victory.

Manchester United are fourth, eight points behind leaders Chelsea, and they are the favourites to take three points against next-to-bottom Birmingham.

The Red Devils have a 60.1% win probability, while Birmingham have a 19.2% chance.

After a difficult start under new boss Eva Olid, United are unbeaten in their last eight WSL games (W5 D3), having lost their opening two this season.

The Red Devils have conceded just four goals during this run, compared with seven in those first two matches, and have kept a clean sheet in each of their last three.

Ella Toone has scored (three goals) or assisted (two assists) in all four of United's WSL games against Birmingham. No fixture has been played more often in the competition with the same player registering a goal involvement in every meeting.

Spurs are unbeaten in three league games, and that form has taken them to fifth in the standings.

However, they are not given much of a chance against Manchester City, who have a 74% win probability. Spurs come out on top in just 10.8% of the supercomputer's simulations.

City have won 12 of their 13 WSL games against Tottenham, with the exception being a 2-1 home defeat in September 2021.

And Spurs have lost six of their last seven away matches against City in all competitions (W1), averaging over four goals conceded per defeat (26 in six).

City have won each of their last 17 WSL home games, including all 10 in 2026; only Chelsea have ever won more home matches in a calendar year, winning 11 in both 2022 and 2023.

Charlton Athletic are only the third side to lose their opening 10 games of a WSL season, after Doncaster Rovers Belles in 2016 and Yeovil Town in 2017-18. Those teams did not collect their first points until their 16th and 13th matches, respectively.

And while the supercomputer expects it to be close between Charlton and Aston Villa, the Villans are anticipated to take the points (42.6% to Charlton's 32%). There is a 25.4% probability of a draw.

Villa have taken four points from their last two league away games (W1 D1), twice as many as across their previous five (D2 L3); the Villans have lost just one of their five matches on the road this season (W1 D3).

Charlton have conceded nine set-piece goals in the WSL this season, at least three more than any other side and accounting for 30% of their goals against (9/30). Six have come from corners, also the most in the division, while the Addicks are yet to score via one themselves.

Everton sit eighth on the back of three straight draws, but those all came against teams above them in the standings (Spurs, Chelsea and Man Utd).

They now host Brighton. Everton have never drawn four consecutive games in the competition, with their only other run of three coming in their first three WSL fixtures in April 2011.

The Toffees are the favourites, with a 43.5% win probability, but at 26.8% this is the game that is most likely to be a draw, with Brighton's chances ranked at 29.7%.

Everton have kept a clean sheet in three of their last five WSL home games, as often as across their 23 home matches prior; the Toffees themselves could go goalless in back-to-back home matches in the league for the first time since November 2023, however.

Between the 2018-19 and 2023-24 seasons, the head-to-head record between Everton and Brighton in the WSL was perfectly balanced with four wins each (D4) – since then, however, the Seagulls have won all four of their top-flight meetings with the Toffees.

The most likely winners on MD11 are Arsenal (76.1%), as they host West Ham, who are handed just a 9.7% win probability.

With just three wins from their opening 10 matches (D5 L2), Arsenal have recorded their fewest victories at this stage of a season. Their 14 points are also their joint-lowest tally after 10 games, alongside 2014.

West Ham have won three of their last five WSL games (L2), having won just one of their opening five this season (L4); the Hammers' four victories are already three more than they had recorded at the same stage last term (one).

Arsenal are the first team to draw each of their opening five home games of a WSL season, with the last four all finishing 1-1. The Gunners are nevertheless unbeaten in their last 24 home matches in the competition (W16 D8), since a defeat to Chelsea in October 2024.

But they will now be looking to bounce back from a disappointing 2-0 home loss to Lyon in the Women's Champions League on Thursday.

The Gunners have won all eight of their WSL home games against West Ham by an aggregate score of 27-8, always scoring at least twice in those matches.
