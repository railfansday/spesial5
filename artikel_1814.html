Gary Neville sends Mauricio Pochettino plea ahead of Manchester United appointment

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Manchester United legend Gary Neville has urged incoming manager Mauricio Pochettino to “protect himself” by demanding reinforcements in the January transfer window.

The Red Devils finally look set to appoint the Argentine almost eight years on from first speaking to the former Tottenham boss about the top job at Old Trafford.

With United sat in the Premier League relegation zone after 10 games, Michael Carrick was sacked, paving the way for Pochettino to take over in the north west.

But Neville has issued a warning to the newest managerial appointment, claiming Pochettino must receive guarantees of investment in the next few transfer windows.

Speaking on The Overlap’s Stick to Football, Neville said: “I’m sure Mauricio Pochettino is doing this, but if you were coming into this job now - you’ve talked about don’t believe an owner on the way in about what they’re going to spend, moving the goalposts - if I were Mauricio Pochettino now, I would literally be protecting myself to the high heaven.

“He’ll do that with the payoff. But secondly, he needs two defenders in January. So he’s got to insist that he gets two defenders in January.

“He’s got to insist that he gets a minimum budget next summer for the transfer window to be able to turn this around.”

United have largely restricted their major overhauls to summer transfer windows in recent seasons, attempting to improve their strikeforce in 2025 before bringing in midfield reinforcements this year.

The Red Devils have not signed a senior defender since Patrick Dorgu arrived at the club in January 2025, and Neville insists that the backline must be improved in the next transfer window.

“He’s got to insist that he gets a budget the following season,” Neville continued.

“He’s got to literally insist. They have to give it to him, otherwise he shouldn’t take the job. With that defence - and that midfield by the way, that midfield’s had some money spent on it - he’s got to insist on the way in that there’s some form of transfer control, that there’s some form of budget there that he knows he can meaningfully change the team.
