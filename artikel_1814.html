Roberto De Zerbi issues James Maddison injury update surprising Tottenham no-show

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Roberto De Zerbi has provided an update on James Maddison’s fitness after the England midfielder was an unused substitute during Tottenham’s 1-0 defeat to Leeds.

Maddison has continually struggled for fitness this season and has not played since coming off with a suspected groin injury against Chelsea last month.

De Zerbi has insisted Maddison is fit and has named him in the matchday squad in each of Spurs’ last two games against Crystal Palace and Leeds.

The Italian, though, neglected to turn to the 29-year-old in either game, raising concerns that he was still struggling for fitness.

Asked about Maddison’s situation, De Zerbi said he had thought about bringing him on in the closing stages against Leeds before opting to bring on 18-year-old Luca Williams-Barnett instead for just his second ever senior appearance.

“James, I thought (about bringing him on) in the last substitution before Luca Williams-Barnett, but I saw (Mohammed) Kudus very tired,” De Zerbi said.
