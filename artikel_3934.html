Police arrest suspect after drive-by shooting at office campus housing Xbox studio inXile

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Police are still investigating the motive.

Police have arrested a suspect after a drive-by shooting at an office campus in Tustin, California, which is home to Xbox-owned studio inXile Entertainment.

According to Aftermath, officers responded to reports of a drive-by shooting at Flight at Tustin Legacy, a campus that houses inXile and other businesses, on 11th November.

"Located multiple shell casings. Nobody was hurt," Tustin Police Sergeant Christopher Peterson told the outlet. "Processed the crime scene, and then sometime [Friday] morning, a little before 11 [AM], they took a suspect into custody. And the investigation is still kind of ongoing as to where and why."

"We're relieved that no one was hurt at our inXile office," an inXile spokesperson said. "Our focus is on supporting our team, and we are working closely with law enforcement as they investigate."

Police have not given a motive, and the suspect has not been named.

InXile is the studio behind the Wasteland series, and is currently working on Clockwork Revolution.
