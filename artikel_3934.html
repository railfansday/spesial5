Only two days out from GTA 6, its publisher Take-Two lay off staff at XCOM and Civilization developer Firaxis

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

About a third of the workforce has been cut, reportedly

Upsettingly, it is that time of the week again, as Take-Two are laying off staff at Firaxis, the developer behind Civilization and XCOM, only a little over a year the last time the studio suffered from layoffs. Firaxis are, notably, owned by Take-Two, the publisher of GTA 6 which comes out in, oh, two days time? Interesting timing, 'ey.

Word of the layoffs came from Bloomberg, who report that about 60 people are losing their jobs, apparently roughly a third of its staff. The studio's head, Heather Hazen, is also leaving. A spokesperson for 2K Games (the Take-Two label that manages Firaxis) shared a statement on the layoffs: "Firaxis is restructuring and recentering around the studio’s core strength - designing world class strategy games that have set the industry standard for the genre for the last 30 years.

"As part of these changes, the size of the studio is being reduced to focus on design, technology and the player experience, which means saying goodbye to talented colleagues who helped build Firaxis. These decisions are never easy, but they’re essential to build the next chapter of Firaxis." I don't feel personally confident in the idea that the only way to make a company succeed is by laying a bunch of people off thereby making it much harder to make anything in the first place, especially in the lead-up to what will likely be the best selling game of the decade, but what do I know!

Bloomberg also report Firaxis have been working on a new XCOM, which is yet to be announced, but the project has apparently had to contend with numerous development issues, missing a "significant milestone over the summer." This new XCOM game hasn't been cancelled at the very least, but anonymous sources that spoke with Bloomberg said it may have to deal with a shakeup due to the cuts. As always, solidarity with all those affected by this situation.
