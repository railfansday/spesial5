Crypto Market Today, Nov. 11: 2. Bitcoin Climbs Back Above $74,000 as Wall Street's Crypto Outlook Begins to Shift

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Cryptocurrencies recovered some of Tuesday's losses as institutional developments and easing selling pressure offered encouraging signs.

Cryptocurrency markets rebounded on Wednesday, November 11, recovering some of the previous session's losses as selling pressure eased and new institutional disclosures highlighted growing Wall Street exposure to digital assets. As of early evening, Bitcoin (BTC +0.35%) climbed 1.24% to $74,621.63, while Ethereum (ETH +0.08%) gained 1.30% to $2,077.30 and Solana (SOL -0.03%) advanced 1.94% to $84.87.

The recovery comes despite persistent inflation concerns and uncertainty surrounding Federal Reserve monetary policy, suggesting investors remain cautiously optimistic about the cryptocurrency market's longer-term outlook.

Bitcoin showed signs of stabilization on Wednesday as selling pressure from U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs began to ease. The slowdown in outflows offered some relief after several sessions of sustained selling.

Meanwhile, new regulatory filings revealed growing exposure to Bitcoin ETFs among major Wall Street banks. Barclays (BCS +0.22%) increased its holdings in BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF (IBIT +1.34%) by 48% during the third quarter, bringing its position to approximately $187 million. Citigroup (C +1.59%) also disclosed a new $119 million position in Fidelity's Bitcoin ETF while increasing its BlackRock holdings by roughly 50%.

The disclosures point to growing institutional participation in regulated cryptocurrency products, even as near-term market conditions remain challenging. However, the filings reflect holdings as of September 30, rather than fresh buying during November's sell-off, making current ETF flows a more timely indicator of investor demand.

iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF rose 1.34%, Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC +1.30%) rose 1.30%, and iShares Ethereum Trust ETF (ETHA +1.23%) rose 1.23%.

Wednesday's rebound offered some relief for cryptocurrency investors, but the broader market remains under pressure following five consecutive weeks of declines in Bitcoin. While signs of stabilizing institutional flows are encouraging, a single positive session does little to reverse the recent deterioration in market momentum.

For Bitcoin, reclaiming $76,000 will be an important first step toward restoring its short-term market structure and establishing a more sustainable recovery. Failure to recover that level could leave the cryptocurrency vulnerable to further selling, particularly if ETF outflows accelerate or macroeconomic conditions deteriorate.

Ethereum faces a similar test, with $2,000 emerging as a critical support level. Holding above that threshold could help stabilize sentiment, while a sustained break lower would increase downside risks. Solana, meanwhile, needs to reclaim approximately $98 to signal a more meaningful recovery from its recent decline.

The coming weeks will be crucial in determining whether Wednesday's gains mark the beginning of a broader recovery or merely a temporary rebound.
