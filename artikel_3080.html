GoDaddy's 2027 Outlook: AI-Driven Platform Adoption Drives Sustained Margin Expansion

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

This under-the-radar tech stock boasts a Superscore of 81 from our Hidden Gems Primary database, part of The Motley Fool's Moneyball Database system. Here's why.

By Robert Izquierdo – Updated Nov 27, 2026 at 3:38PM EST

Picture a small-town baker who needs a way for customers to find her shop, pay for her croissants, and order for pickup without needing a degree in computer science. She heads to the web, types in a name, and clicks buy. For more than 20 million customers, that first step happens on GoDaddy (GDDY -1.97%).

As a provider of domain registration, hosting, and commerce tools for entrepreneurs, the company acts as the digital storefront for the micro-business economy. The stock trades at $102.09 as of November 19, 2026, down roughly 18% over the past year despite recent operational gains.

Although a mature tech company, the rise of artificial intelligence has given GoDaddy novel opportunities. Its Airo AI platform is becoming its flagship product, as the new solution hit an annualized bookings run rate of over $100 million in the third quarter, and began contributing to bookings growth.

The company's historically robust retention rate above 85% is boosted by the fact over half its customers pay for at least two products, adding to revenue growth. For those who adopt Airo, 70% have at least two paid products, suggesting its shift to the new solution is contributing to even stronger sales expansion.

GoDaddy management is also adept at maintaining strong financials. It produced free cash flow of $480.8 million in Q3, representing a 9% year-over-year increase, while its balance sheet sported $1.1 billion in cash and equivalents. This gives the company flexibility to perform share repurchases while investing in new opportunities.

These factors position the company for growth in the coming years. Combined with a reasonable trailing P/E of 14.1, well below the 24.4 multiple shares were at in Q3 of 2025, make the company a compelling stock to consider buying now.
