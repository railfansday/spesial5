Napoli set asking price for De Bruyne following €2m PSV offer

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Newspapers in Italy claim that Napoli have a price in mind when it comes to the potential sale of Kevin De Bruyne in the January transfer window, following reports of a €2m offer from Eredivisie side PSV Eindhoven.

De Bruyne has less than a year remaining on his contract with Napoli, and although he has an option to extend by a further 12 months, the assumption in Italy is that this clause will not be exercised.

Reports over the last few days have indicated that PSV are interested in a deal for the 35-year-old superstar, a move that would allow De Bruyne and his family to return to their home in the bordering Belgium if it were to come off.

Reports in the Netherlands claim that PSV have offered a fee of €2m to sign De Bruyne from Napoli in January. If this were the case, Napoli would reportedly raise around €10m through the transfer fee on top of the player’s remaining wages and bonuses.

According to Thursday’s updates from La Gazzetta dello Sport, a €2m transfer fee will not be enough to convince Napoli into selling De Bruyne in January. The Pink Paper suggests that a figure closer to €7m will be required to get the deal over the line.

Head coach Massimiliano Allegri also needs to be convinced: The Partenopei boss is not keen on the idea of sending De Bruyne packing given that he is the most experienced and most technically gifted player available in his squad.

However, much depends on De Bruyne himself and how strongly he will push for a move away from Napoli in January.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
