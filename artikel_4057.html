The Skull Mask, the Spooky Mask, the Bunny Hood, and more are part of the Happy Mask Shop quest in OOT Remake

The Happy Mask Shop in Zelda Ocarina of Time has you run around Hyrule trading masks with characters for Rupees, including the Skull Mask, Spooky Mask, Bunny Hood, and the Keaton mask. Though these masks only serve as commodities to be traded with specific characters, if you can allocate all four to the right characters and complete this OOT quest, the Happy Mask Shop will give you access to four new masks that all have a very specific utility. Below I'll show you how to hand out all the Masks and the specific locations you need to look.

Keep in mind that the Switch 2 version is the same as the original, in that the Happy Mask Shop won't open in The Legend of Zelda Ocarina of Time until you open the passage to the Goron City. Fortunately, the guard who opens the passage will actually tell you that the Mask Shop is now open, flagging that you can start this questline.

Bought from the Mask Shop for 10 Rupees, you need to give this to the Guard in Kakariko Village who opens the passage for you, the same one who lets you know that the Mask Shop is open. He'll give you 15 Rupees in return.

Bought for 20 Rupees, head back to the starting area of Kokiri Village, where you start off and get the first Shield and Sword in Zelda Ocarina of Time, and turn North into the Lost Woods.

The moment you enter, head left through the hollow trunk to meet The Skull Kid, playing a tune on top of a log. He, understandably, will offer to take the Skull Mask from you, though only pay you ten for it. Cheapskate.

After buying the Spooky Mask for 30 Rupees, head back to Kakariko Village, and this time take the East exit to the Graveyard, but do so during daytime. Here you'll find a boy wandering around. Speak to him to give him the Spooky Mask, for which he'll give you 30 Rupees back.

The final mask in this sequence is bought for 50 Rupees. You can only sell it once you've progressed to the point where you have obtained the Three Spiritual Stones in the main story.

Once this happens, head to Hyrule Field. There'll be a man who runs in circles around the field, practicing his running. If you go during the night, he'll be sitting beside the road and you won't have to keep pace. Either way, if you give him the Bunny Hood, he will entirely fill your wallet with Rupees, so it's a good idea to have expanded it as much as possible prior to the trade to really capitalize on that.

Likewise, the cash infusion delivered by the Bunny Hood NPC can make Link incredibly rich, a great reward in and of itself.
