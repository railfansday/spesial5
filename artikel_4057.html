The Witcher 3 Remastered Patch 5.02 Fixes Annoying PS5 Stutters, Improves Performance, and Much More

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Witcher 3 Remastered has a new update on PS5, and it's available to download right now, weighing in at a reasonable 1.6GB.

Patch 5.02 is the one many have been waiting for, as it addresses the game's most annoying technical issue on console. I am, of course, referring to the momentary stutters that occurred when autosave was triggered.

This problem was especially noticeable when quest information popped up on the screen, both when you began a quest and finished it. Thankfully, CD Projekt Red's finally taken action.

The patch also improves v-sync and VRR "behaviour" on PS5, which should result in a smoother overall experience. Screen flickering has also been addressed.

On top of those more-than-welcome adjustments, the update is packed with bug fixes — some of which are related to the remaster's new lighting techniques, which could result in some areas looking poorly or incorrectly illuminated.

And in terms of gameplay, we're looking at a bunch of balancing tweaks to a number of Geralt's skills. Some of these perks simply weren't being calculated properly, so you should be more effective in combat right out of the gate with this update installed.
