How To Remove Advanced Trackers In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

You'll really need to put in the work if you want GTA 6's best vehicles. Here's how it's done.

One of the most surprising things about Grand Theft Auto 6 is how much more effort it makes you put into pretty much every core tenant of a GTA game, from shooting to controlling a character.

The most obvious example of this is with stealing cars, which is no longer as simple as just taking whatever you want and respraying it. Advanced trackers make that tougher, so here's how to deal with them.

Advanced trackers are a special type of security that are put on high-end cars in Leonida, making it impossible for Jason and Lucia to keep them at first. You'll be able to see if a car has one by scanning it with the WaiNK app, which will show how much security the car has, including the level of tracker it's got, if any.

While they're usually paired with Key Cloners that make even driving the car impossible without the right tools, if you manage to get into a car with an advanced tracker, it will go off and alert the police if you don't drive fast enough. This in turn makes it so you can't really stop using the car once you've stolen it, making expensive cars a very temporary solution at the start of the game.

All of this is to say that Jason and Lucia can't just hop into the fastest cars and take them for themselves, as advanced trackers put a swift end to that tactic. You'll need to find a way to remove them, and there's one baked into a lengthy sidequest that you should try to complete as soon as possible.

Fast and Furious fans have an extra special easter egg to find in GTA 6: Paul Walker's Mitsubishi Eclipse from the first film.

Unfortunately, it's going to be a very long while until you can remove advanced trackers, as it's tied to completing nearly all of Randy's high-end car list. You'll meet Randy in Chapter 3 as Lucia, and can then choose to work with him by stealing specific cars off a list. There's a total of 19 cars on the list, and you'll need to collect 18 before Randy gives you the ability to remove advanced trackers.

While that sounds like quite the task, Randy does give you specific locations for many of the cars, and the rest are very easy to find if you go to Capri's neighbourhood and look at the fancy cars there. I managed to knock out nearly every single car on the list that way, so it's not as tough as it may seem.

The list starts off as 17 cars, but two more get added once you've found that lot. Those two are basically their own side missions, though.

Once you've found 17, Randy will say he needs some time before adding more to the list. Once he does a little later on in the game, get the first of the two new vehicles for him, and he'll call you afterwards to let you know you can now remove advanced trackers. From there, it's as simple as taking any car you want to a Pay 'n Spray and selecting to register it to remove the tracker.

Unsurprisingly, you should expect to pay some pretty hefty sums to remove those trackers from nice cars. At least all the money you made from Randy's wishlist should make things easier.
