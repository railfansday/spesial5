Dad Killed Son by Setting Car on Fire After He Shot the Boy's Mother

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

An Illinois man was arrested after he allegedly killed the mother of his child and left their son to die inside a car that he set on fire.

Jeremiah Duncan has been charged with three counts of first-degree murder after he allegedly killed Heaven J. Wood and their 2-year-old son, KJ Duncan-Wood, according to a bill of indictment viewed by Law & Crime.

Two of the counts Duncan, 24, was charged with are related to KJ’s death.

Prosecutors claimed that Duncan “knowingly started” the fire and “knowingly left” his son inside the “burning vehicle” before the toddler died from smoke inhalation.

The Peoria County State’s Attorney’s Office said that Duncan was arrested in September and was initially charged with one count of murder after he fatally shot Wood, 20, on September 25. The former couple shared custody of KJ at the time of the killing.

“On September 15, 2026, the court granted Wood an emergency order of protection against Duncan, which was served the next day,” prosecutors said in a press release shared on November 2. “On September 18, 19, and 20, Duncan called the Peoria County Sheriff’s Office to stand by during custody exchanges with Wood.”

Deputies responded to reports of a vehicle fire on September 25, and they found a car in flames. The vehicle was then traced to a nearby home, and deputies went to the residence for a welfare check.

Upon their arrival, deputies found Wood with a gunshot wound. She was then transported to a local hospital, where she was ultimately pronounced dead. Authorities said that two other children were found inside the home and they were unharmed.

“The preliminary investigation indicates the vehicle had been driven from the area of the shooting before crashing,” the sheriff’s office said about the murders in a press release. “After extinguishing the flames, first responders located a deceased infant inside the vehicle. Investigators determined the infant was the child of the female found deceased in the residence.”

The Peoria County Coroner’s Office conducted an autopsy and concluded that KJ died from severe smoke inhalation.

Duncan was quickly named a suspect in the killings, and detectives set out on a search for him. Police eventually found Duncan several hours later and he was taken into custody. Authorities claimed that he was armed with a knife at the time he was found.

Police noted that Duncan suffered “serious burns” in the fire, and he was treated at a medical facility before he was taken into custody.

“Nearby deputies recovered a pair of burned pants and a revolver with three spent rounds,” prosecutors said. “They also recovered a recording from Wood’s phone on the day of the murders. In the recording, the two can be heard having a discussion. Wood tells the defendant to leave, or she will call the police. There is a brief struggle, followed by three gunshots.”

Prosecutors added that witnesses reported seeing a man running away from the vehicle as he was “partially on fire.”

On October 30, Duncan was discharged from the hospital and was transferred to the Peoria County Jail. He remains in custody as of time of publication.

It’s not currently clear if Duncan has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following his arrest. The Peoria County State’s Attorney’s Office had no additional updates to share when contacted by Us about the case.
