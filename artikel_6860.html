Often when we think about it, some of the most-worn pieces in our closets involve some form of practicality. Perhaps it’s that reversible raincoat, that cozy office-to-weekend cardigan, or a pair of utility jeans, which you may also know as cargo or work jeans.

When it comes to practicality and nonchalance, few pieces deliver quite like this one. Deep pockets, sturdy hardware, and a relaxed, easy fit mean you can run errands, commute, or settle in at a café wearing a polished version of your favorite work pants. The best pairs look just as good dressed up with a sleek knit and boots as they do dressed down with a favorite tee and sneakers, and they only get better with wear. Consider them proof that the hardest-working item in your closet can also be the most stylish.
