A Definitive Guide to the Best Black Boots

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Out of all the fall fashion purchases you make this season, there’s one item that is guaranteed to give you maximum mileage in the months ahead: a pair of black boots.

For those who favor a sleek silhouette to elevate and anchor your outfits, a classic knee-high boot complements dresses and skirts across the board. If you’d like your boots to serve as more of a personality piece, why not test drive a Western-inspired pair or one detailed with moto hardware? Of course, timeless shapes like square-toe booties and Chelsea boots will always be reliable go-tos; trendier styles like the slouchy boot still feel just as versatile as they did decades ago.

Ahead, we break down nine different black boot styles, from equestrian-inspired riding boots to all-weather lug-sole boots. Find the right one for you in our guide to the best black boots of the season.

A quintessential knee-high black boot can be worn layered beneath dresses and skirts or styled over slim trousers and denim. Let your personal preference inform the height. Flat or block heels are best for commuters, while a higher heel can transition from the workday to evening seamlessly.

If you’re not yet a Western-boot convert, the easiest way to style these is to contrast them with wardrobe staples that don’t feel cowboy-adjacent. Whether you’re letting your boots peek out from beneath a pair of wool trousers or opting for a pair with subtler motifs, there’s always a way to make them feel your own.

Saint Laurent Dylan 45 Patent Leather Boots

A traditional Chelsea-boot silhouette is the workhorse of many wardrobes. While a classic take on this style makes a great investment, don’t miss modern interpretations, like the Row’s, which are cut extra low.

Manolo Blahnik Chelsa Leather Chelsea Ankle Booties

Whether you’re going for moto-inspired hardware or something with a bit of downtown edge (Coach and Vince, for example), a silver buckle is the kind of detail that adds dimension while staying versatile.

Manolo Blahnik Calica Buckled Biker Boots

For instant polish, slip into a pair of black riding boots. This equestrian-inspired silhouette is both classic and distinct, ideal with preppy separates like pleated skirts and boxy blazers.

The Row Nobilis Leather Knee-High Riding Boots

Khaite, Chloé, and several other designers included the slouchy boot in their fall 2026 collections, highlighting how this style has shifted away from its ’80s associations and in a much more compelling direction (particularly when rendered in classic black).

Preparing for the winter means being ready for the storms ahead, and a pair of lug-sole boots is a must. The season’s best styles offer all-weather protection (and are a stylish snow-boot alternative) while still feeling versatile enough to wear on glorious days when the weather is agreeable.

Not quite an ankle boot or a knee-high either, the mid-calf boot is shaping up to be one of the season’s most interesting shapes. Need proof? Just look to the designers embracing it, from cool-girl favorite Maryam Nassir Zadeh to buzzy newcomer Jude.

There’s a time and place for a pointed boot, but a sleek square toe always manages to feel a touch cooler. Look for styles with a slimmer, subtler square toe so you can style them for any occasion.
