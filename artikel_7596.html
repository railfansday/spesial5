Tigard Man Sentenced to 22 Years in Federal Prison for Sexual Exploitation of a Child and Possession of Child Sexual Abuse Material

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Jacob Michael Lewis, 51, of Tigard, has been sentenced to 264 months in federal prison, followed by a fifteen-year term of supervise release, for surreptitiously recording sexually explicit images of a minor victim and possessing thousands of files of child sexual abuse material, U.S. Attorney Scott E. Bradford announced today.
