Quick history refresher: Nvidia tried to buy Arm for ~$40B back in 2020. The FTC sued 4-0 to stop it [ftc.gov] in Dec 2021, the UK and EU had their own investigations going, and Nvidia gave up in Feb 2022. SoftBank even got to keep the $1.25B Nvidia had already paid. So not technically "denied", but it sure wasn't going to happen.

The FTC's big complaint wasn't Nvidia owning the IP. It was Nvidia getting a look at all the confidential roadmap stuff that Qualcomm, Apple, Samsung, etc. hand over to Arm because Arm is supposed to be a figurative Switzerland. Guess who knows all of that? Sure, Haas has an NDA and a non-compete, but you can't NDA what's in a guy's head. Can't buy the company, so hire its brain? Same trick as the $20B Groq "license and hire" acqui-hire deal the DOJ is already poking at.
