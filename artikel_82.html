A Visual Tour of President Trump’s Visits to Asia

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

President Trump disembarking from Air Force One in Manila on Tuesday.Credit...Doug Mills/The New York Times

President Trump landed in the Philippines on Tuesday, the first stop on a 48-hour Asia trip during which he is expected to meet with leaders including the presidents of Russia and China.

Mr. Trump began in Manila, where he is participating in a summit for Southeast Asian nations and is scheduled to meet with the president of the Philippines.

From there, Mr. Trump will travel to Shenzhen, China, for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit. He will finish with a highly anticipated trilateral meeting with China’s top leader, Xi Jinping, and President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia.

The trip comes as Trump administration officials feud over the White House’s approach to China.

When Mr. Trump met Mr. Xi earlier in his current term, there was plenty of pageantry, but little progress on points of contention.
