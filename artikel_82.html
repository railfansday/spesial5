Xi, Trump, Putin: historic summit ends, outcome unclear

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The unprecedented trilateral summit between Xi Jinping, Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin ended on Thursday in Shenzhen after about an hour and 20 minutes. No details have emerged yet, but the three leaders talking face to face is already an event. Each has their own agenda: Putin wants to be back on the world stage, Trump needs a win at home, and Xi is asserting China’s status as an economic superpower. What comes out of it remains unclear, as FRANCE 24's journalists explain.
