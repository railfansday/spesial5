FIFA says a cyberattack against it targeted a 'limited set of personal and business information'

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

World football's governing body says the cyberattack targeted personal and business information. They struck at a sensitive time for the under-fire organization and its president, Gianni Infantino.

FIFA told news agencies on Thursday that it had been targeted by a person or group seeking access to its data.

"FIFA can confirm that it has been the victim of a criminal cyberattack resulting in unauthorized access to a limited set of personal and business information," a FIFA spokesperson told the Reuters news agency, issuing similar comments to other outlets.

Reuters cited a person "with direct knowledge of the matter" as saying that less than 0.1% of FIFA's total data had been compromised.

This person also said that no files from the controversial, abandoned FIFA Forward Enterprise project that looked to sell a stake in the World Cup to private investors had been accessed.

FIFA said that it was working closely with the relevant authorities, including law enforcement, in the aftermath of the event.

It did not say when the attack took place, list the precise nature of the compromised data or specify how unauthorized access to FIFA data had been sought, amid reports of people having posed as FIFA officials rather than actually hacking the organization or otherwise compromising its cybersecurity systems.

Last week, a group calling itself the Handsome Boy Hacking Club posted a screenshot of what it said were FIFA files. Three days later, it appeared to take credit for the cyberattack when responding to a post saying that more than 12 gigabytes of internal documents had been posted.

FIFA did not explicitly state whether it was talking about the same supposed incident.

The breach comes at a sensitive time for the organization. Still reeling from the scandal following this summer's World Cup of its now-aborted plans to sell off World Cup commercial rights shares, it's now trying to secure another term for its embattled president, Gianni Infantino.

Infantino is the favorite for an election on March 18, when he is expected toface challenges from Kohzo Tashima of Japan and Philippe Diallo of France. But until the turbulence of the last six months, it seemed most likely he would simply stand unopposed and extend his 11-year tenure without a fight.
