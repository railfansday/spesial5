Stock Market Today, Nov. 10: AMD Rises on Plan to Invest $2.5 Billion in Anthropic

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Chip designer AMD gained as investors weighed its AI deal push, with trading well above normal, today, Nov. 10, 2026.

Advanced Micro Devices (AMD +1.30%), a data-center and PC chip designer, closed at $515.21, up 1.30%. Tuesday's gains followed a premarket upgrade and fresh AI-chip deal chatter, and investors are watching data-center demand and AI spending next.

Trading volume reached 46.7 million shares, coming in about 102% above its three-month average of 23.2 million shares.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.38%) closed at 7,521, down 0.38%, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -0.74%) ended at 25,066, down 0.74%. Among semiconductors, x86 CPUs, GPUs, AI accelerators, and data-center/server chip stocks, Nvidia (NVDA -5.47%) fell 5.47% to $168.89 and Intel (INTC -2.09%) dropped 2.09% to $58.13, while AMD outperformed both chip rivals.

AMD stock rose today after artificial intelligence giant Anthropic disclosed in an amended IPO filing on Nov. 10 that AMD would be part of a group involved in a $26 billion private placement alongside some of the world's biggest technology companies. AMD plans to invest $2.5 billion worth of non-voting Class N common stock in Anthropic, which counts toward an earlier commitment of up to $5 billion linked to chip deliveries.

AMD shares have been on a run this week after its chief rival, Nvidia, disclosed an investigation into its CEO, Jensen Huang, over his relationship with an alleged Chinese spy. Wall Street's fears that Huang could be forced to step down caused investors to shift into AMD stock.

This added to analyst upgrades of the company's shares, as its business continues to boom. AMD's Nov. 3 earnings results showed record quarterly revenue of $13.4 billion and Q4 revenue guidance of about $17 billion, which kept AI-driven growth in focus.
