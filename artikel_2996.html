Brock Bowers is questionable to play Sunday at Denver

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Las Vegas Raiders star tight end Brock Bowers is questionable to play Sunday at the Denver Broncos, according to head coach Klint Kubiak.

Bowers didn't practice Wednesday, but he was limited in practice on both Thursday and Friday. Kubiak said Wednesday there would be a chance the 2024 first-round draft pick could potentially play at Denver even if he missed practice all week.

Bowers left a Week 10 home loss to the Seattle Seahawks on Sunday in the second quarter with an ankle injury and he did not return. On Wednesday, it was disclosed he was also dealing with a quad injury as well.

Kicker Matt Gay (hip) and defensive tackle Foley Fatukasi (toe) are also questionable. Defensive tackle Tonka Hemingway didn't practice all week with a stinger and was ruled out.

Right guard Trey Zuhn returned from an illness Friday and he should be ready to play Sunday.
