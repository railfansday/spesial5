Embiid, Brown have big scoring nights as Sixers down Nets, sweep back-to-back

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

BROOKLYN — That second half is probably best forgotten, but that's another win stacked. The Sixers escaped with a 135-123 win over the Brooklyn Nets Monday night.

Joel Embiid put up a season-high 37 points shooting 13-of-22 from the floor along with five rebounds. Tyrese Maxey had a more reserved night, going for 22 on 7-of-12 shooting with nine assists.

Jaylen Brown hit some big shots again as he finished with 31 points for the second straight night, going 9-of-19 from the field and pulling down 13 rebounds as well. VJ Edgecombe once again hit a big three late, finishing with 14 points along with nine rebounds and four assists shooting 4-of-10 from the floor. Julius Randle led the Nets with 33.

LeBron James missed the second half of the back-to-back with lower back management, as well as Adem Bona and Dom Barlow.

The Sixers wasted no time trying to create fast breaks as Maxey picked off an errant pass on the first possession of the game for an easy dunk. Edgecombe ran quickly off the Nets' first miss of the night, missing his layup but Dean Wade was there to put it back. Embiid had a weird start, picking up his first basket of the game picking some cherries down the end of the court. He and Edgecombe picked up a silly backcourt violation as Embiid did not get down the court all the way, but Edgecome still threw it to him.

With that being said, Embiid made four of his first seven shots, all of them being pretty easy looks near the basket. He finally knocked down a three later in the quarter. In some very intriguing rotation news, Jabari Walker played some four next to Embiid, despite being the most reliable backup five option at the moment. Expectedly, Walker did slide down to the five when Embiid first subbed out of the game.

The Nets' shot diet was similar to start the game, scoring 10 of their first 15 points from the paint, and putting some size next to Embiid in Walker didn't help that effort all that much. Walker picked up a hustle bucket on the other end, but couldn't finish the quarter after picking up two fouls. Wade replaced him to finish the quarter at center.

After a quiet start to the game, Brown took over the offense with Maxey and Embiid on the bench, getting to the line a couple of times. Edgecombe also had a strong take to the basket as the quarter closed, helping the Sixers get out to a seven-point cushion.

Anfernee Simons continued his stretch of tough shotmaking with a contested midrange pull-up to start the second. The Sixers got down the floor quick on their next trip, and Brown was able to find Simons wide open in the corner for a three. Brown got Danny Wolf to foul him on a three-point attempt, then stopped Wolf on a post-up, helping Philadelphia build an extended lead for the first time of the night.

It was a bit of a relief to see the Sixers just look flat out better than their opponent, and, yes, it took one of the worst in the league to do so. Edgecombe hit Walker with a high-low pass, and that gave him easy driving access on the next possession. Brown hit a nice crossover to leave his defender in his tracks and get to the basket. Embiid once again tried the self lob off the backboard, and thankfully enough players got in the paint to prevent him from attempting a dunk.

Embiid settled into a mode of just trying to draw a foul every time down the floor, which he was able to do pretty easily. Brooklyn is tall at a lot of positions but center, ironically, isn't one of them. Embiid took 10 free throw attempts as he put 24 points in the first half.

If there was one complaint for the half, it was that Nick Nurse only played eight guys against this Nets team on the second leg of a back-to-back. They set themselves up well for the end of bench guys to get plenty of run late, but it would have been nice to see some Labaron Philon Jr. minutes. Brown came off a screen to drill a three off a side out of bounds as the half came to a close. But Nolan Traore was able to draw a foul just before time expired, cutting the Sixers' lead to 19.

Embiid picked up right where he left off, opening the second half with a turnaround fadeaway. Wade kept doing all the little things, fighting for an offensive rebound to keep a possession alive for a Brown three. Maxey hit a quick stepback three and found Brown for another corner threee. It was a great start to the half offensively that offset Brooklyn coming away with three points on their first four possessions of the third.

That streak did end for the Nets, but they were able to build the tiniest bit of momentum after Embiid got called for a transition take foul on Randle. The Sixers sandwiched that with a couple of turnovers. The bucket they came up with to stop it was Embiid's best look of the night, driving past his defender and spinning to the hoop for an easy layup.

On the very next possession, Embiid went up aggressively but got blocked by Michael Porter Jr. and hit the deck hard flat on his back. He remained on the ground for a bit, long enough for the Nets to get an open three and have Nick Nurse call a timeout. As bad as it looked, Embiid seemed more concerned arguing with the officials that he should have been fouled during the timeout, and did remain in the game.

Maxey hit a three coming out of that timeout, but the Sixers had started to cool down as Brooklyn continued to inch back in it. They came within six before Brown buried a much needed midrange pull-up. After a rare stop he was able to get to the line on the next possession, but split the pair.

To make matters even trickier, Walker picked up his fourth foul which looked pretty cheap, but the Sixers had already lost their challenge earlier in the quarter. Moe Wagner might have missed both free throws, but Kentavious Caldwell-Pope missed a three on the ensuing possession and Porter drilled one coming back down the floor. Walker made a great finish once again getting found by Edgecombe and also got to the line and split a pair. He pumpfaked a defender that left him all alone on the wing and he buried a three as time expired to keep the Sixers up by 10 after three.

After the quarter started with a Wolf three to bring it back down to single digits, Brown picked up a foul on each end to bring him to four on the night as well. Edgecombe had a really nice driving dunk, but the Nets kept getting closer as the Sixers couldn't stop Randle.

As Brown was turning around with the ball, he didn't see Randle right behind him and slammed into him pretty hard. A foul was called but Brown hit the deck pretty quickly and was down for quite a bit. He left the game for three minutes of gametime but was able to return.

Walker tried to slide over and take a charge, but got whistled for his fifth, bringing Embiid back perhaps a minute or two earlier than intended. The Sixers got very lucky on the ensuing free throws. After Wolf missed both he got his own offensive rebound and missed the putback layup right at the rim. Embiid knocked down a pair of midranges, but got called for his fourth foul trying to block the shot of Day'Ron Sharpe.

Sharpe ended up missing both of those free throws as well, and Embiid followed that up with another pretty driving layup. Brown seemed to be OK as he knocked down a midrange, but the Sixers just couldn't keep the Nets down. Randle got an and-1 and Traore got to the line to remain within striking distance.

After some good ball movement, Maxey found Edgecombe wide open on the perimeter for a big three, but Traore came right down the floor and hit a three of his own. Despite some second chances the Sixers came up empty on the following poessession and Egor Demin came flying right down an open lane for a dunk, prompting a timeout from Nurse with the Sixers up only seven.

Demin hit a three after another empty Sixers possession to cut it to four. Embiid got the ball in the corner and calmly pumpfaked his defender, took a dribble and buried a much needed three in response. Embiid made more threes in this game (two) than he had over the past three weeks. Wade made a great play to block Randle and after Edgecombe won a jump ball and got fouled, the Sixers were able to win this one from the free throw line.
