Keisha Lance Bottoms Makes History: Elected First Black Female Governor

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Keisha Lance Bottoms won the Georgia Governor race … and made history in the process.

On Tuesday, Keisha, the former Atlanta mayor, was named the winner in her fight with billionaire Republican, Rick Jackson.

Rick said he spent more than $200 million of his own money on his campaign. This win makes Keisha the first black female governor.
