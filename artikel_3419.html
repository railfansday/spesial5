30 Years Later, Power Rangers Is Finally Bringing Back One Of Its Most Iconic Villains

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Three decades after one of Power Rangers' best series, a forgotten antagonistic force returns for a rematch. The Power Rangers franchise has introduced an unforgettable gallery of iconic villains whose larger-than-life designs left a mark on Saturday morning television for generations. Lord Zedd's exposed brain and booming voice instantly set the tone in Mighty Morphin Power Rangers alongside the theatrical Rita Repulsa and the cowardly Goldar. In later eras, In Space's Astronema, Time Force's Ransik, Dino Thunder's Mesogog, and RPM's Venjix expanded the Power Rangers' rogues gallery with very different twists and gimmicks.

The sheer number of Power Rangers villains introduced throughout the years has left several antagonists underused. For instance, Scorpina vanished mysteriously from Mighty Morphin Power Rangers despite matching the Rangers blade-for-blade. Over in Jungle Fury, Jarrod’s internal struggle while possessed by the ancient dragon spirit Dai Shi and offered great character drama that ultimately flew under the radar, and Dino Thunder's Zeltrax showcased a compelling personal grudge against Tommy Oliver that deserved a deeper payoff.

Three decades after the franchise's peak in popularity, overlooked Power Rangers heroes and villains alike are finally getting a second chance to shine, courtesy of BOOM! Studios' Power Rangers Unlimited.

In BOOM! Studios’ upcoming Power Rangers Unlimited #6, the Machine Empire makes a chilling return from the ashes following their defeat at the hands of the Zeo Rangers years earlier. The automated facilities are now churning out fresh battalions of Cogs and heavy weaponry for the Power Rangers franchise's new main antagonist, the Black Turbo Ranger. As teased by Power Rangers Unlimited #6's solicitation, when the Phantom Ranger sends out a distress signal, Trini Kwan and Minh cut their long-awaited reunion short to infiltrate the reactivated complexes and cross paths with both the Red Omega Ranger and an ancient warrior corrupted by the Black Turbo Ranger's dark influence.

The Machine Empire was introduced in 1996’s Power Rangers Zeo, where King Mondo, Queen Machina, Prince Sprocket, and their mechanical legions forced Rita Repulsa and Lord Zedd to flee the moon. The empire later fell to Zordon's energy wave, though its remnants eventually resurfaced. In BOOM!’s continuity, the empire previously appeared in major storylines like Shattered Grid and the Unlimited Power era. Now, Power Rangers Unlimited #6 brings the Machine Empire back under the control of a rogue Ranger alongside Louie Kaboom, the volatile robot created by Rita and Zedd during Zeo, which offers another sign of how the Black Turbo Ranger is weaponizing every corner of Ranger history.

Power Rangers Unlimited #7 leads the franchise's biggest crossover into exciting new territory with a Wild West-inspired showdown at the Onyx Corral in an adventure that brings together a fan-favorite squad of sharpshooters across Ranger eras. Lost Galaxy Red, Lightspeed Pink Space Silver, Dino Charge Black, and White Tiger Wild Force in a mission centered on acquiring a vital cache of Power Rangers data. Like previous issues have done for a variety of Power Rangers series, Power Rangers Unlimited #7 puts the spotlight back on beloved and overlooked series outside the usual Mighty Morphin focus.

Power Rangers' most underrated Green Ranger comes back after more than twenty years to avenge his fallen teammates and his forgotten world.

BOOM! Studios leaves anticipation sky-high for what awaits in Power Rangers Unlimited's issues #8 through #12, which have yet to receive official solicitations. Although official information hasn't been revealed, the second half of the series will likely continue to rotate squads made of solo members from underexplored teams, such as Time Force, S.P.D., RPM, Ninja Storm, and Mystic Force, as each mission recovers another piece of the puzzle needed to stop the Black Turbo Ranger. Of course, the mystery behind the Black Turbo Ranger's true identity will also be revealed at some point in these upcoming issues.

Power Rangers Unlimited #6 is available December 16 from BOOM! Studios
