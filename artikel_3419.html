Netflix’s 10/10 Sci-Fi Masterpiece Officially Releases First Season 2 Footage After 3-Year Delay

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

When one of the densest sci-fi shows ever made arrived on Netflix in March 2024, it made one thing abundantly clear: humanity is absolutely screwed if aliens ever decide to pay us a visit. From scientists mysteriously dying to the laws of physics apparently deciding they'd had enough, the first season gave us plenty to worry about before revealing that the real trouble was still 400 years away. Of course, that hasn't stopped fans from getting impatient, especially with almost three years having passed since the series first premiered. Now, with the countdown to humanity's potential extinction officially underway, we're finally getting a better idea of just how bad things are about to get.

Netflix has officially dropped the first teaser for 3 Body Problem Season 2. The new footage arrives just a day after Netflix confirmed that Season 2 will premiere in January on the streamer. The series has also been renewed for a third and final season, allowing David Benioff, D.B. Weiss, and Alexander Woo to complete their adaptation of Liu Cixin's bestselling trilogy.

Netflix's official synopsis for the first season reads: "Beginning during China’s Cultural Revolution in the 1960s, the series introduces a young Ye Wenjie (Zine Tseng), who makes a fateful decision that reverberates across space and time into the present day. When the laws of nature inexplicably unravel, a tight-knit group of brilliant scientists known as the Oxford Five must join forces with an unflinching detective and a mysterious intelligence agency to save the world."

Season 2 will see humanity preparing for the San-Ti's arrival, both on Earth and in space. A fleet sets off on a mission that will take centuries to complete, while a secret hidden inside one man's mind could be humanity's best chance of surviving the invasion. And what's more, the show's creators have warned fans that if you thought Season 1 was out there, they have no idea what they're about to get themselves into, previously telling Deadline:

“The story gets really wild in the best possible way. With something that’s that wild, there are a lot of choices to be made and a lot of things to be figured out. We’ve been putting our heads together to figure them out recently especially the past couple of months.”

The returning cast includes Jess Hong (Inked) as Jin Cheng, Benedict Wong (Doctor Strange) as Da Shi, Liam Cunningham (Game of Thrones) as Thomas Wade, Jovan Adepo (Watchmen) as Saul Durand, Eiza González (Baby Driver) as Auggie Salazar, and Marlo Kelly (Dare Me) as Tatiana Haas. New additions include Claudia Doumit (The Boys) as Captain Van Rijn and Ellie de Lange (Wolf Hall) as Ayla, while Alfie Allen (Game of Thrones) has also joined the cast in a recurring role.
