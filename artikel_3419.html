‘Slow Horses’ Future Beyond Season 8 Officially Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Slow Horses is the most consistent show on streaming. In a time when multi-year gaps between seasons are becoming the norm and network sensibilities are a thing of the past, Apple TV's crime favorite has maintained a strict one-season-per-year standard that other hit shows can only dream of. Between 2022 and 2026, this adaptation of the series of novels by Mick Herron has blessed audiences with six seasons and 36 episodes.

But it isn't just in quantity that Slow Horses has thrived. The Apple TV hit has also consistently drawn critical praise, regularly being cited as one of the best shows of the year. The most recent Season 6 scored a perfect 100% on Rotten Tomatoes after debuting and was called an "even twistier and more unpredictable" season in Collider's review. Before the most recent batch of episodes paced towards a cliffhanger finale with River's (Jack Lowden) life hanging in the balance, it was confirmed that the series would return for an eighth season following next year's seventh.

And now we officially know the fate of the series beyond Season 8. Apple TV has today confirmed that Slow Horses has been renewed for Seasons 9 and 10. There is no mention of exactly what the new seasons will be based on, and with Herron having not yet penned anything beyond Season 8's Clown Town, the show might do a Game of Thrones and steer in its own creative direction. There is also no direct mention of Lowden in the press release, which makes sense following his character's potential fate at the end of Season 6, but could hint at his move to Prime Video as the new 007.

Recently, prior to the renewal, House of the Dragon star Olivia Cooke, who plays Sid Baker, raised concern among fans, suggesting that the end could be nigh. In a recent interview, after announcing, "I am shooting the last season," Cooke said, "never say never" regarding the show's future. Cooke continued:

"But I think that’s what we’ve been told and it definitely feels like we are ending. We’re all melancholy, distracted. Yesterday, I got in the make-up chair and cried. The show’s been a shining light and I’ve told my partner I need two weeks to get over it. I’m going to be depressed."

Season 8 "sees Jackson Lamb and Diana Taverner face off in their most explosive confrontation yet," an official synopsis has revealed. "When a botched attempt to blackmail the Park puts Taverner on the offensive, it is up to Lamb to unravel one of MI5’s darkest secrets and put an end to it once and for all." Slow Horses is expected to return in September next year for Season 7.
