Kung Fu Tea launches Cyberpunk Edgerunners boba tea with collectible stickers

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Kung Fu Tea launched two Cyberpunk: Edgerunners drinks inspired by David and Rebecca, with collectible stickers for fans who move fast.

The collab arrived during a busy stretch for Night City, since Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 premiered on Netflix on October 20 and Cyberpunk 2077 quietly added free Witcher 3 gear shortly after. Rather than chase the sequel’s brand-new crew, though, the boba chain went straight back to the originals.

Kung Fu Tea announced the collaboration on Instagram with a cheerful “WAKE UP, CHOOM!” and slung both drinks onto its menu from November 9 to 22.

David’s Passion Fruit Overdrive Slush blends icy passion fruit slush with strawberry popping boba, while Rebecca’s Coco Riot Milk Tea pairs creamy coconut milk tea with chewy brown sugar boba, which the chain describes as “sweet with a chaotic side.”

Kung Fu Tea is also handing out exclusive collectible stickers, but only while supplies last at participating locations, so anyone hoping to snag one may want to visit sooner rather than later. Fans can find their nearest store through the link in the chain’s Instagram bio.

The pairing isn’t Kung Fu Tea’s first brush with gaming, either. The chain, which bills itself as America’s largest bubble tea brand, previously served Monster Hunter Wilds and Metaphor: ReFantazio drinks, and ran three Pokémon Legends: Z-A themed drinks from December 2025 to January 2026.

This time, though, picking David and Rebecca, hearkens back to the 2022 anime that helped send Cyberpunk 2077 sales soaring. Edgerunners 2, by contrast, swapped them out for an entirely new cast featuring D and Talia.
