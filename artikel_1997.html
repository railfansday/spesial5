What channel is Dundee v Hearts? TV and live stream plus team news, referee and VAR

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Hearts travel to Dundee on Saturday evening as the Scottish Premiership swings back into action following the international break.

Wouter Vrancken’s injury-hit side are looking for a major turnaorund after collecting just one win from their last eight matches in all competitions, a run which included an Edinburgh derby defeat to Hibs in the Premier Sports Cup semi-final at Hampden.

The 5-2 hammering at the hands of Falkirk at Tynecastle before the break - Hearts were 5-0 down inside 47 minutes - also shattered their unbeaten home record stretching back 18 months.

Injuries have been a huge factor in Hearts’ downturn with a full starting team of players ruled out in recent weeks.

Hearts go into the match in fourth place in the table, three points ahead of fifth-placed Dundee, who have earned plaudits this season after collecting 17 points from their opening 12 matches.

Steven Pressley’s side have been particularly strong at home, where they are unbeaten in four matches, with the vast majority of their points accrued from their games at Dens Park this season.

The Scottish Premiership match between Dundee and Hearts takes place at Dens Park, Dundee on Saturday, November 21, 2026. Kick-off is 5.45pm.

The match will be broadcast live on Premier Sports 1 with coverage starting at 5.15pm. Highlights will be shown on Sportscene on BBC One Scotland at 10.20pm.

Dundee are still without long-term absentees Bobby Wales (ankle), Simon Murray, Finlay Robertson and Jack Moorhouse (all ACL), but Steven Pressley expects everyone else to be available.

Hearts’ injury list has eased slightly, with Ageu, Christian Borchgrevink and Rogers Mato back in contention, but Malachi Fagan-Walcott (muscle) joins Stephen Kingsley (hamstring), Calvin Miller (knee), Tomas Magnusson (concussion), Harry Milne (knee), Oisin McEntee (muscle), Craig Halkett (Achilles), Blair Spittal (shin) and Tom Renaud (ankle) in being unavailable.

Steven McLean is the match referee. Jonathan Bell and Ross Nelson are assistant referees, while Lewis Hogarth will serve as the fourth official. Grant Irvine is the VAR room assisted Calum Scott.
