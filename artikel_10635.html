Viral monkey Punch is now caring for a baby monkey after being rejected by his own mom

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Punch, the viral monkey from Japan, is now looking after another monkey as Ichikawa Zoo has given an update on his well-being.

Back at the start of 2026, the internet found a new animal to rally behind – Punch. The now-infamous Japanese macaque went viral after news that his mother had abandoned him within the troop at the Ichikawa City Zoological and Botanical Garden in Chiba Prefecture, Japan.

Without a mother to cling to, Punch found solace in a stuffed orangutan toy that had been given to him by the zookeepers. Images of him hugging the toy quickly went viral online, with fans from all over the world wanting to visit Punch.

Over the last few months, things with Punch have settled down, but the Ichikawa City Zoo has given updates on his life from time to time – including when he found himself a girlfriend.

On November 8, the zoo uploaded a new video of the viral macaque, noting that he now cares for another monkey within the group.

In the video, Punch can be seen hugging onto the infant monkey, as well as grooming their head and neck, too.

“Punch and the other animals’ conditions change from day to day. We observe them, interact with them, and provide care, and we also adjust their feeding to suit the season and weather,” they said. “Punch is able to live so healthily thanks to the care our keepers provide.”
