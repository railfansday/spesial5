Obsessed fan breaks into YouTuber’s home demanding marriage, dies after family beats him

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

An obsessed fan who broke into an influencer’s home and demanded that she marry him has died after being beaten by her family members and neighbors.

According to NDTV, Abhinav had developed one-sided romantic feelings for Bisht after regularly watching her content. He was reportedly upset after learning she was engaged and preparing to marry another man in December.

On November 7, Abhinav allegedly forced his way into Bisht’s home and insisted that she marry him instead.

Police reports indicate that Abhinav appeared intoxicated when he entered the residence and allegedly threatened to stop Bisht’s upcoming wedding.

Her family members and neighbors confronted him, with the situation escalating into a violent assault that left Abhinav with serious injuries.

When police arrived, they found him suffering from injuries to his back and right foot. He was taken to hospital, where he died approximately a week later.

However, this wasn’t the first time Abhinav had targeted the influencer.

According to The Indian Express, he previously visited her home in July, claiming to be a friend of her brother to gain entry.

He reportedly declared his love for Bisht and begged her not to marry anyone else. Her family confronted him, but no police complaint was filed at the time.

Bisht also claimed that she had received threatening messages from anonymous Instagram accounts discussing her movements and daily routine.

Authorities are investigating the case as culpable homicide not amounting to murder and reviewing CCTV footage to determine whether other family members or neighbors were involved.
