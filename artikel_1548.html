Champions League (F): Bayern comes to beat PSG again

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Bad evening for the French clubs involved this Tuesday in the Women’s Champions League. In the wake of the defeat logically conceded by Paris FC on Real Madrid’s pitch (3-0), it was PSG who fell at home against Bayern Munich (1-2, 4th day).

Like a year ago, the Bavarians came to take three points on the Parisian pitch. And they were helped by their hostesses of the day who conceded a csc just before the hour mark. It was the goalkeeper, Katarzyna Kiedrzynek who deflected a shot from Pernille Harder into her cage (0-1, 29th).

Dominated, PSG logically conceded a second goal shortly before the break, this time on a winning shot from Klara Bühl, well served by a Pernille Harder who was definitely very comfortable this evening (0-2, 40th).

Unable to score a single shot in the first period, the Parisiennes will do better in the second and manage to reduce the gap thanks to Paulina Dudek (1-2, 65th). Bayern will have opportunities to make the break, like this too-cross shot from Linda Dallmann (82nd). PSG will get the last opportunity of the game, but Tara Elimbi Gilbert’s twenty-meter full-axis shot will miss the target (90th + 7).

This defeat, the first in four matches for PSG in this league phase of the Women’s Champions League, places the club in ninth place in the provisional ranking. Bayern are second with three more points. On November 18, PSG will go to Chelsea. Not a piece of cake.
