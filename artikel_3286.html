Charlie Sheen’s ‘Mostly Sheenius’ Podcast Lines Up Michael Douglas As First Guest: Rob Lowe, Martin Sheen & Denise Richards Also Set To Appear

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

EXCLUSIVE: Michael Douglas will be the first guest on Mostly Sheenius, the new podcast from Charlie Sheen. The weekly interview pod, from Fox-owned Red Seat Ventures, bows on November 12.

Douglas is fresh off publishing his well-received memoir ‘One Helluva Ride’. His sit-down with Sheen will cover the book, Hollywood and his time working with Sheen on the iconic movie Wall Street.

Sheen has also lined up a roster of other big names for his pod series. It’s a crowd he has some strong connections with, which promises some prime insider stories. His father, the actor Martin Sheen, is set to appear, as is his actor brother, Emilio Estevez. Denise Richards, Charlie Sheen’s ex-wife and with whom he has two daughters, will also be a guest. Watch on Deadline

The other confirmed interview subjects include Jennifer Grey, who starred with Sheen in the classic movie Ferris Bueller’s Day Off. Sheen’s longtime friend Rob Lowe will also be on the show, as will comedian and actor Bobby Lee.

The team behind Mostly Sheenius promised honest, humorous and unexpected conversations. Sheen will bring his own perspective as an actor and celebrity to proceedings, sharing tales and insights along the way.

Mostly Sheenius will launch across the big podcast platforms as audio and full video episodes. The show is produced by STRAC Media with Charlie Sheen and Todd Christopher serving as Executive Producers. Eric Pearce, EVP of Creator Operations, exec produces for RSV.

The news comes ahead of Chris Peterson VP of Business Development at creator-led studio Red Seat Ventures taking part in a Deadline-moderated session titled ‘How Podcasting Rewired The Talent Deal’ at the Web Summit event in Lisbon.
