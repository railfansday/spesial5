Premier League predictions: Pochettino backed to get first win on Old Trafford bow

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

The Opta supercomputer has delivered its match predictions ahead of another weekend of the 2026-27 Premier League season.

The top three all dropped points last weekend, with Arsenal and Manchester City drawing with Newcastle United and Fulham, and Brighton stunned by two stoppage-time goals from Hull City.

Liverpool took advantage of that to close the gap with their 4-0 thrashing of 10-man Manchester United – with Mauricio Pochettino's disappointing start seeing the Red Devils stay in the bottom three.

Thomas Frank also got off to a losing start at Crystal Palace, who remain bottom of the table, after a 3-2 loss to Coventry City in his first game.

Matchday 12 sees fixtures take place across Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Ahead of what should be another intriguing set of Premier League games, let's check out the Opta supercomputer’s match predictions.

The weekend kicks off at the City Ground on Friday, with Nottingham Forest and Chelsea both looking to get back to winning ways.

Since the start of March, no side has lost more Premier League games than Chelsea (13), who were beaten by Sunderland and Leeds United in their last two outings. Similarly, among ever-present sides, only Crystal Palace (17) have earned fewer points than the Blues (23) over the same period.

Chelsea are at least unbeaten in their last five away league games against Forest (W4 D1), winning each of the last three, though our predictive model gives them just a 33.5% chance of stretching that run to four.

Forest, who won 39.9% of the pre-match simulations, are looking to win consecutive league games against Chelsea for the first time since April 1979, though there is also a 26.6% chance of a draw.

A quiet Saturday slate begins at Elland Road, with Leeds United aiming to close in on the top four when they host Coventry City.

Leeds are given a 56.8% win probability, the highest of any team on MD12, unsurprising considering given they've lost just one of their last 23 home league games against Coventry (W16 D6), going down 3-1 in December 1996.

Coventry lost both league meetings with Leeds without scoring the last time they met in the 2024-25 Championship.

While a lot has obviously changed since then, the supercomputer still only gives Coventry a 20.8% chance of victory this time around.

Manchester United will be looking to quickly put their nightmare start under Pochettino behind them against high-flying Brentford.

Pochettino heads into his first home game as United boss on the back of that 4-0 drubbing at Liverpool and an unlucky 2-2 draw with Sporting CP in the Champions League, but they are given a 44.1% chance of victory to Brentford's 29.1%.

In United's league history, only John Chapman (1921) and Erik ten Hag (2022) have lost their first two games in charge, but this match also has a 26.8% chance of a draw, making it the likeliest stalemate this weekend.

United have won all five of their Premier League home games against Brentford. However, they haven't won any of their last nine league matches (D5 L4), their longest-ever winless run in the competition.

The final game of the day sees Aston Villa looking to end their run of three straight league draws. Ipswich Town will be their hosts.

Villa haven't drawn four consecutive Premier League games since between August and September 2011.

The supercomputer gives this game a 25.5% chance of ending all square.

Unai Emery's side, who lost 1-0 to Galatasaray in the Champions League on Tuesday, are unbeaten in all 22 of their league games against promoted teams under the Basque coach (W15 D7); they are given a 41.9% win probability here. Ipswich, meanwhile, have a 32.6% chance of victory.

And it's a landmark match for the Villans, as it will be their 5,000th in the top four tiers of English football (W2,050 D1,199 L1,750).

The Merseyside derby kicks off Sunday's action, with Everton looking to bounce back from a 4-0 loss of their own when facing rivals Liverpool.

Brentford soundly beat Everton following James Tarkowski's sending-off, and more red cards could be on the table at Hill Dickinson Stadium as this fixture has had more dismissals than any other in Premier League history (25).

The omens aren't great for Everton, as they've won just two of their last 31 top-flight meetings with Liverpool (D14 L15).

The Toffees have also conceded six 90th-minute winners against the Reds, including in this exact fixture last season, when Virgil van Dijk won it.

Liverpool, who beat Club Brugge 3-0 in the Champions League on Wednesday, have lost just one of their last 15 Premier League trips to Everton, though 10 of them finished level.

The Reds are given the edge for this one, with a 42.7% win probability, which is the highest of any away team on MD12. Everton won 32%, with a draw coming in at 25.3%.

Brighton (56.3%) were shut out for just the second time this season last time out, but they're looking to consolidate second place against Newcastle United (21.1%).

Brighton are unbeaten in all nine of their Premier League home games against Newcastle (W4 D5).

In this competition, it's both the most they've faced a side at home without losing, and the most the Magpies have visited a side without ever winning.

Newcastle, who drew 2-2 with Arsenal last Saturday, haven't won any of their last 39 Premier League away games against teams starting the day in the top two (D5 L34), and they face a tough task as Brighton have conceded the fewest home goals in the competition this term (one).

Hull City's win over Brighton last time out ended an eight-game winless streak in the Premier League, while Crystal Palace have lost each of their last five.

Hull goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis was the hero against Brighton. He's made the most saves (48) and boasts the best save percentage (81.4%) in the top flight this season.

However, he will likely need to be at his best again. Hull have won just one of their last 13 away games against Palace in all competitions (D5 L7), and they are only given a 31% chance of getting another victory at Selhurst Park.

Palace are enduring their longest losing run since between June and July 2020 (seven), but they are backed to end it with a 42.3% win likelihood.

Bournemouth's five-game Premier League winning streak has seen them close in on the top four, but Fulham are also on the up with a five-game unbeaten run.

It's the Cherries who are expected to extend their respective run, however, with their impressive form coinciding with Evanilson's hot streak.

The Brazilian's been involved in more goals than any other player in the Premier League this season (six goals, four assists), scoring in six consecutive appearances.

Fulham have won just one of their last 11 home league games against Bournemouth (D5 L5) and are given a 33.4% chance of getting their first such victory since February 2024.

Tottenham have enjoyed their own climb up the table after winning four of their last five Premier League matches (L1), more than in their first 26 games in 2026 combined (W3 D10 L13).

Now, Roberto De Zerbi returns to the site of his first game in charge of Spurs when they go to Sunderland, who've moved off the foot of the table after avoiding defeat in their last two outings.

Sunderland's 1-0 win in this exact fixture on 12 April ended a 15-game winless run against Spurs in the league (D5 L10), but since that date, only six teams have won more points in the division than Tottenham (26).

Spurs are given the edge by the supercomputer here, winning 39.3% of the simulations to Sunderland's 34.8%.

All eyes then turn to the Emirates Stadium as leaders Arsenal tussle with struggling challengers Manchester City for the second time this season.

The Gunners prevailed 3-0 in the Community Shield back in August, but a lot has happened since then.

Arsenal have a six-point lead at the top of the Premier League, while City are winless in their last four league games (D1 L3) after winning their first seven.

Both teams were held to midweek draws in the Champions League, with Arsenal's profligacy costing them in a 1-1 stalemate with Borussia Dortmund, while City had to come from behind twice to earn a point against Napoli.

Arsenal are unbeaten in their last three home meetings with City (W2 D1). As such, the supercomputer gives the Gunners the edge with a 54.5% win probability.

Indeed, City, who have a mere 22.1% win likelihood, have won just one of their last six Premier League meetings with Arsenal (D3 L2), though that was in their most recent clash in April (2-1 at the Etihad Stadium).

City have won 12 of their last 14 Premier League games against reigning champions (D1 L1), completing the double over Liverpool last season. Since MD8, however, only Palace (0) have earned fewer points in the competition than Enzo Maresca's side (one).

Arsenal, meanwhile, are the only side yet to drop a point at home in the top flight this season, winning all five games at the Emirates.
