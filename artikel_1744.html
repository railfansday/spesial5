Report: 'Incredible' Premier League defender eyed by Liverpool, it's his dream

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Liverpool have been linked with many different players over the years.

However, their last couple have consisted of the most spending that they've ever done.

Around £600 million has been splashed on new players by Liverpool and, there are still a lot more areas among their ranks that could do with an upgrade or two.

Right now, it's unclear what their main priorities are but, since they're fourth in the Premier League and find themselves nine points adrift of first-placed Arsenal, it's clear that change is needed.

Obviously, big deals don't often get completed in January but, if Iraola wants to give his men the best chance possible this term, some big transfers are going to have to be agreed.

Liverpool are eyeing up a move for Josko Gvardiol

Right now, left-back is probably one of Liverpool's main problem areas.

Milos Kerkez and Kostas Tsimikas have both failed to meet expectations with neither of them managing to prove their worth.

Of course, the former of the two is still young so he still has a lot of his career ahead of him but, since he's their first choice, you'd have expected a lot more from him.

Links with left-backs such as Alejandro Balde and Julian Ryerson have emerged but, thanks to an update from El Nacional CAT, an even better option has entered the discussion.

"Barcelona are calmly considering the possibility of trying to sign Gvardiol, although this would involve a significant investment," they wrote.

"The Sky Blues won't let go of a player for whom they paid over €90 million, making him one of the most expensive defenders of all time. Nor will they allow him to leave mid-season, so they would have to wait until the summer transfer window.

"Furthermore, there is another obstacle that makes it extremely difficult to see the former Dinamo Zagreb and RB Leipzig player at the Spotify Camp Nou, and for him to be available to Hans-Dieter Flick.

"There will be a lot of competition for Gvardiol The situation surrounding Gvardiol and City has attracted the attention of many clubs , who are willing to try their luck and fight for his signing.

"For example, he has been linked with Paris Saint-Germain, Liverpool, Bayern Munich, Chelsea, and Al-Nassr. In other words, Barça will face several rivals in the fight to secure his services."

Josko Gvardiol's dream move has always been Liverpool

Before Manchester City jumped in and prised Josko Gvardiol away from RB Leipzig, the Croatian always spoke about how it was his dream to join Liverpool.

There have been several interviews where the "incredible" defender has shared that the team he supports is Liverpool.

His favoured position is obviously centre-back but, with the Reds still having now idea what they're doing with that area of their squad, he could come in as another option at left-back for Liverpool.

Not only would this help shore up Iraola's defence like Ronald Araujo has but, it would also give Kerkez someone to properly compete against.

Of course, the window isn't open yet so deals can't be struck right now but, the fact that Gvardiol has re-entered Liverpool's thinking is excellent news.
