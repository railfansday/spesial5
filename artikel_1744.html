Man Utd open talks to make Premier League star Mauricio Pochettino’s first signing

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Manchester United have held talks over a January move for Fulham defender Calvin Bassey.

United’s defence shipped another four goals last weekend against Liverpool with only Crystal Palace conceding more in the Premier League this season.

With the club slumped in 19th place after 11 games, Mauricio Pochettino will be backed in the January transfer window with a centre-half, a left-back and a striker targeted.

While they are also interested in Everton’s Jarrad Branthwaite and Brentford star Nathan Collins, Bassey has emerged as a leading target for United.

His current contract at Craven Cottage expires in the summer with Fulham holding the option to extend that by a further 12 months.

The Cottagers are also in active talks with their captain over a new contract. But United remain hopeful of prising him away from west London with The Athletic reporting talks are taking place to ‘understand the conditions’ of a January move.

Bassey turned in another fine performance as Fulham held Manchester City to a 1-1 draw at the Etihad Stadium on Saturday afternoon.

Speaking after the game, the former Rangers star was asked about his future, telling TNT Sports: ‘Of course [it is something we want to get done quickly.]

‘We are negotiating, we are still talking. It’s an amazing place to be, I have settled in nicely and the manager has put a lot of faith and trust in me. It’s nice to be here and hopefully we will see what happens with the negotiations.’

United are also looking at signing a left-back with Sporting CP star Maxi Araujo one option. Alejandro Balde is also open to a move with his first-team prospects at Barcelona not improving since the summer.

Up front, the Athletic report United are not expected to make a move for Monaco and USA forward Folarin Balogun. Dominic Calvert-Lewin was also of interest but he has now signed a new contract with Leeds United.
