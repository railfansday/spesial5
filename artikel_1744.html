Roy Keane names Arsenal’s biggest rivals for the Premier League title

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Roy Keane has predicted the biggest threat to Arsenal’s hopes of retaining the Premier League title is unlikely to come from any of their traditional rivals.

The Gunners displayed unfamiliar signs of vulnerability at St James’ Park on Saturday evening where they let a half-time lead slip, before eventually battling back to earn a 2-2 draw.

The loss of two points was compounded by an injury to goalkeeper David Raya who is now nervously anticipating the results of a forthcoming MRI scan on his damaged arm.

Manchester City’s failure to win at home against Fulham meant the failure to cling onto the lead Arsenal gained through Eberechi Eze’s first-half opener represented a missed opportunity.

With Enzo Maresca’s side in the midst of a disappointing run and the likes of Chelsea and Liverpool struggling to come to terms with the demands of their new managers, Arsenal are widely expected to defend the title they won back in May.

Indeed, their nearest challengers at present are Brighton, who are thriving under the management of Fabian Hurzeler, and Keane sees no reason why the Seagulls can’t continue to mount a challenge.

‘At this moment, yes,’ Keane told Sky Sports when asked if Brighton could emerge as the only team capable of preventing Arsenal from claiming back-to-back titles.

‘That’s because of the form they’re in, they’re goal threat and defensively they’re very, very good.

‘They’ve got a top quality manager but obviously over the next couple of months the pressure and injuries and whatever (will come).

‘They’ve got a young team and have a lack of experience. But at this moment in time they’ve got to enjoy it and keep that pressure on Arsenal.’

Hurzeler, who masterminded a 3-0 win over the Gunners back in September, has plenty of admirers and was recently linked with the Manchester United job.

‘His time will come,’ Keane said when it was put to him that the German would’ve represented a better appointment than Mauricio Pochettino.

‘We talk about Poch getting close a few times and sliding doors moments. He’s done a great job and Brighton and if he left now you’d be looking at him and thinking he should’ve stuck at it a bit more.

‘We talk about loyalty in football and it’s got to work both ways. So I’m glad he continuing at Brighton and hopefully they can continue having a good season.’
