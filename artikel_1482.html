Bayern sharply rejects Barça for one of its executives

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Barça will have to review its plans to strengthen its defense during the next winter transfer window. While Deco and Hansi Flick had activated the track leading to Jonathan Tah according to SportBayern Munich quickly cooled Catalan ambitions. Following a meeting between Pini Zahavi, the German defender’s agent, and Max Eberl, Bavarian sports director, the Blaugrana leaders received a categorical refusal. Despite less playing time than expected since his arrival, the former Leverkusen player is considered an indispensable element by Vincent Kompany.

This seriously complicates the search for Deco, who wishes to offer Flick an experienced defender, capable of quickly integrating into the Barcelona system, and to supervise Pau Cubarsí and Gerard Martín. The club’s financial constraints, however, limit the possibilities on the market. The priority therefore remains to find immediately operational reinforcement, even if it means exploring other avenues if no agreement is possible.
