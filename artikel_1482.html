Zhegrova plays 82 minutes in Kosovo’s 0-0 with Israel before Dublin decider for second

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

With Edon Zhegrova in the starting line-up, ten-man Kosovo held Israel to a goalless draw, a result that leaves them second in Nations League Group B3. The run-out gives a Juventus forward who rarely starts for his club valuable match fitness while Pierre Kalulu is injured.

In Pristina, the Juventus forward Edon Zhegrova was Kosovo’s main attacker against Israel.

Zhegrova tested Israel’s defence a couple of times in the first half but faded after the break, according to TuttoJuve. Dion Kacuri came on in his place.

Below Kosovo, Ireland have 5 points and Israel 3. Austria beat the Republic of Ireland 1-0 on the same Saturday.

Kosovo’s campaign has been uneven. They opened by beating the Republic of Ireland 1-0 on 24 September. Three days later they lost 3-1 away to Austria on 27 September. On matchday 3, they had also drawn 0-0 with Israel.

The final round pairs the Republic of Ireland with Kosovo and Israel with Austria. That leaves Ireland, sitting just below Kosovo, hosting the side directly above them. Zhegrova is set to be central to Kosovo’s hopes of holding on to second place.

For Juventus, the significance lies in the minutes. Zhegrova has started only once since joining the club. He sits low in Spalletti’s pecking order and has mostly featured in short spells off the bench.

More than 80 minutes as Kosovo’s focal point gives him match fitness. That could prove useful before Juventus face Venezia in Serie A and Omonia in the Europa League.

The squad is stretched. Scans at J|medical on 9 November found a low-grade injury to Pierre Kalulu’s left biceps femoris. The defender had felt muscle discomfort in his left thigh after the draw at Fiorentina. France withdrew him from their squad. The midfield is also short of bodies.

Spalletti has made clear he sees potential in the winger. “Edon se si completa un po’ può diventare un crack,” the coach said. If Edon rounds out his game a little, he can become a star.

Other clubs are paying attention too. Near the end of the summer window, Al-Ittihad made a move for the winger, and Monaco are also interested, Eurosport Italia reported. A strong finish to the international window would do his standing no harm, in Turin or elsewhere.

First comes Dublin. Kick-off at the Aviva Stadium is at 7.45pm UK time, with second place in the group at stake.
