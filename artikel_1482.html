Roma interested in Bologna’s Cambiaghi

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Roma is expanding its list of targets for January. Among the names being pursued by Tony D’Amico is Nicolò Cambiaghi.

The Bologna winger is one of the players being considered to strengthen Gasperini’s attack, as he has been seeking a right-footed player capable of starting on the left for months.

This is a situation that remained unresolved during the summer transfer window and which the management intends to address in the upcoming transfer window.

The top choices remain Jamie Gittens and Endrick, but landing one of the two won’t be easy.

The Chelsea player has been hampered by an ankle injury that has prevented him from finding consistency this season.

AC Milan, meanwhile, has also made an interest in the Real Madrid Brazilian, reportedly considering an offer of around €60 million. These obstacles are pushing D’Amico to keep other options open.

According to TuttoMercatoWeb, Cambiaghi meets Gasperini’s expectations, and his performance hasn’t gone unnoticed at Trigoria. The winger is enjoying a strong run with Bologna, as confirmed by his recent call-up to the national team.

He was among the players who stood out most in the match against France. For now, however, there have been no direct contacts between Roma and Bologna.

The Cambiaghi deal is still a possibility, with no negotiations underway. The coming weeks will determine whether D’Amico will decide to take a concrete step, especially if the difficulties in securing the services of Gittens and Endrick continue.
