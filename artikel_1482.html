Romano: Milan ‘very attentive’ and Cardinale ‘directly involved’ – the latest on Endrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

AC Milan are waiting for updates on Endrick’s future at Real Madrid, with owner Gerry Cardinale seemingly ready to pounce.

Milan and Roma have both been linked with a swoop for the Brazilian forward since the summer window, when any chance of an exit was ultimately kiboshed by the new head coach Jose Mourinho. However, ahead of the January window, things are moving.

Endrick has not been given the playing time he hoped for thus far, leading to strong suggestions that an exit could be on the cards. Not only that, but several sources in Brazil have claimed that the Rossoneri are ready to put a package worth €70m on the table.

In a video posted on his YouTube channel (via MilanNews), Fabrizio Romano commented on the future of Endrick, who is highly regarded by both Roma and Milan in view of the winter mercato.

“After the PSG-Roma match, both Gasperini and Wesley spoke to TNT Sports Brasil, a Brazilian television station, about Endrick. Both were asked what’s happening with Endrick. Gasperini dodged the question and said he doesn’t talk about players from other teams,” he said.

“Wesley, on the other hand, let himself go a little more and admitted that in the national team he told Endrick to go to Roma and that everyone is waiting for him.

“He then added that obviously it will be up to him to decide what he wants to do with his career, but also that in Rome the people would go crazy for him, the fans would go crazy for him and his teammates would also be very happy to welcome him.

“So Endrick has also received messages from the Brazilians on the Roma side. On the other hand, we must never forget Milan in the matter because we know that the Diavolo are very attentive to the Endrick situation and also that Gerry Cardinale is directly involved.

“Milan are absolutely keeping its antennas up, waiting to see what will happen. All the various possibilities, however, pass through Real Madrid and José Mourinho.”
