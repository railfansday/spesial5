Phelan Provides McNamara & Gabarro Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Speaking in his pre-Chelsea press conference on Thursday afternoon, head coach Scott Phelan provided an update on the fitness of Inma Gabarro and Holly McNamara.

McNamara faces a spell on the sidelines, though the forward could return before Christmas. Gabarro continues to recover from an anterior cruciate ligament injury and has been progressing well. However, the Spain international remains a long way away from making her return – with no timeline set on her comeback.
