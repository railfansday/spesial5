The ranking of European top scorers

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

This Monday is the seventh report in the ranking of European top scorers for the 2026/2027 season. We thus find the scorers who shook the nets in Germany, England, Belgium, Spain, France, Italy, Netherlands, Portugal and Türkiye. And it is Raphinha who remains in the lead. The Brazilian has 15 goals in 10 matches with FC Barcelona and scored twice in the big victory against Deportivo Alavés (6-1).

Just behind, we find Kylian Mbappé who maintains his pace. Scorer against Racing de Santander, the French striker allowed Real Madrid to win 3-0. He now has a nice total of 12 goals in 11 matches. The podium is then completed by Lamine Yamal. The FC Barcelona winger also shone during the demonstration against Deportivo Alavés (6-1). With his double, he brings his statistics to 10 goals in 11 matches.

Victor Osimhen fails at the foot of the podium. The Nigerian managed to shine again with a new achievement against Konyaspor (4-0). He has now scored 9 goals in 8 matches. Niclas Füllkrug finds himself fifth with the same standards. The Werder Bremen scorer scored a new goal on Saturday against Hoffenheim (2-0). Sixth, Vangelis Pavlidis will play this evening against Alverca and has 9 goals in 9 matches. Identical figures for Gustavo Varela who comes seventh. Saturday against Bologna, he scored despite his team’s 2-1 loss. Eighth, Erling Haaland remained silent during Manchester City’s 1-0 defeat against Brighton. Gift Orban comes ninth with 9 goals in 10 matches following a new goal against Eyüpspor (5-1). Finally, Donyell Malen finished tenth with 8 goals in 9 matches. The Dutchman scored in AS Roma’s big victory against Udinese (5-2).

Luis Suárez (Sporting CP), Mohamed Salah (Trabzonspor), Wout Weghorst (Twente), Nicolò Tresoldi (FC Bruges), Eldor Shomurodov (Başakşehir), Lassine Sinayoko (Paris FC), Antonio Raimondo (Frosinone), Yassir Zabiri (Racing Santander), Søren Tengstedt (Go Ahead Eagles) and Brynjolfur Willumsson (Groningen) failed to reach the top 10 with also 8 goals. Estéban Lepaul (Rennes), Guus Til (PSV Eindhoven), Norman Bassette (Westerlo), Sergio Camello (Rayo Vallecano), Patrik Schick (Bayer Leverkusen), Thom van Bergen (Groningen), Milan Zonneveld (Sparta Rotterdam), Dusan Vlahovic (Besiktas), Ante Budimir (Osasuna) and Vedat Muriqi (Fenerbahçe) follow with 7 goals.
