35 Gifts for Coworkers That'll Make Them Say, "Wait, How Did You Know?"

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Every item on this page was hand-picked by a House Beautiful editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

We found items for everyone, from the new hire to the retiree.

A lot of us commiserate about commuting into the office, but there are a few positives to seeing your coworkers regularly, like finding your fellow movie buffs to discuss the latest films with, or bonding with the colleagues you grab drinks with after work. Another upside? Your group may do white elephant gift exchanges, Secret Santa, or at the very least, have an office holiday party. If you are doing a present swap this season, finding creative, affordable gifts for your coworkers could be tricky.

First, there’s determining the budget; then it’s figuring out whether to get them something practical, fun, edible, or just plain silly. Our thoughtful recommendation is to get a gift they can use immediately or leave at work, like a cute tissue box cover, hand warmers (because the office is always cold), or even a small diffuser that makes the air around them smell better. We found 35 such items they’ll love, and if they truly want to, they could bring and use them at home too.

I even gave them to a coworker whose hands were chronically cold in the office, and she used them all year long!”

It packs just the right amount of style while still feeling functional, so if your coworker always brings their lunch from home, give them a lunch box they won’t cringe at.

For the days when work gets stressful, this easy-to-use Buddha Board is the perfect at-desk escape. With a refillable water brush, they can paint any design they choose on a canvas that evaporates in minutes. No muss, no fuss, just endless creativity to reset their brain.

We can't get enough of these cheeky cocktail napkins with the ingredients for a perfect Caesar salad. The coworker who's always shaking up a salad bowl around lunchtime will get a kick out of this.

The personality hire is a key role in any office dynamic. Say thank you to the person who keeps things fun with this chic game box, complete with playing cards, dice, and a classy velvet lining.

Putting together the staff holiday party is no easy task. Thank the coworker responsible for this finishing touch for the office Christmas tree: a set of scented tree stems. They complete the festive atmosphere by bringing the scent of fir and spruce to the office.
