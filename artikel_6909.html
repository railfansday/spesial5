Rihanna’s Daughter Rocki Makes Her Dior Campaign Debut Alongside Her Mom

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Rihanna’s daughter, Rocki, already joined her parents on two magazine covers before her first birthday, and now she’s starring in her first campaign at age 1. Rihanna and Rocki front Dior’s Lady Dior 2027 campaign. Per the fashion house, the series is designed to showcase Rihanna’s “multidimensionality, with her various poses and expressions suggesting the many facets of her identity.”

Rihanna posted several videos and photos from the shoot, including a clip where she wears nothing but the purse while holding her daughter.

All of Rihanna’s children have now appeared in fashion editorials. Last week, Rihanna shared photos of her middle child Riot’s debut magazine cover for Arena Homme+.

Her oldest son, RZA, appeared with her and A$AP Rocky on the cover of British Vogue in 2023.

While Rihanna hasn’t spoken at length about Rocki in an interview yet, she did discuss her style as a mother in her 2025 Harper’s Bazaar cover story.

“I feel like getting dressed is a fight on its own [now],” Rihanna said. “Everything is so … logical. What makes sense? What’s easy? What’s fast? I try not to overthink all that stuff, but you’re leaving the house. It kind of stops you from going out. How much energy do I have to put into getting ready? Doing my makeup, doing my hair, and then going to the closet and figuring out which three things in this entire room make sense together? You go through a fog. And fashion is so much fun, and I miss the fun.”

But at that point, she was getting back into dressing up. “Now I’m starting to just remember what I loved about it: the juxtaposition, putting the things together that don’t make sense,” she said. “My fashion has always been driven by my mood, and my mood was on mom mode for a minute.”
