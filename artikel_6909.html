These Are the Only Deals on AirPods Worth Shopping

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Every product was carefully curated by an Esquire editor. We may earn a commission from these links. Here’s how we test products and why you should trust us.

If you’ve been eyeing a new pair, now is the time to buy.

While the Black Fridays of yore involved a sea of people rushing into a big-box store to claw each other’s eyes out over a new TV or gaming console, the biggest shopping holiday of the year looks a bit different these days. Now, the madness is largely confined to the Internet—and the sales, like everything else, never actually stop.

Case in point: Amazon’s 12-day Black Friday sale has officially landed a week before Thanksgiving, and with it comes some of the year’s best Apple prices, including impressive deals on AirPods. Every time Apple gadgets go on deep discount, they tend to sell out quickly. It’s just like those Black Friday mad dashes, just from the comfort of your own couch, so it would behoove you to shop sooner rather than later.

Right now, both the AirPods Pro 3 and the AirPods Max 2 are hovering close to their lowest prices ever. If you’re looking for the cheapest possible pair, the brand-new AirPods 5 are still at full price, but considering that’s just $129, it’s still a solid deal, especially because they’re Apple’s first standard AirPods to include noise cancellation. That said, we wouldn’t be mad if they go on sale for the first time and will keep you posted if they do. Amazon, your move.

AirPods Pro 3 are Apple’s most advanced earbuds yet. We called them “pretty damn cool” back when they first dropped in September 2025, and more than a year later, we stand by it.

Not only do they come armed with the noise cancellation Apple is famous for, but they also boast both adaptive and transparency audio features that adjust to the environment around you. Krista Jones, senior commerce director, is partial to the adaptive mode, which she says is “slightly less noise cancelling and always tunes into my surroundings to turn things up or down accordingly.”

Jones said that the Live Translation feature has become particularly useful in her bilingual home. Heart Rate Sensing is also of note—it keeps you plugged into your heart rate during workouts without having to wear a fitness tracker.

While a fourth generation of AirPods Pro were rumored to drop in fall 2026, there’s been no sign of them in Apple’s most recent slate of releases, so if you’re looking for the most sophisticated AirPods on the market to date, these are it. Right now at $179, they’re just $10 shy of their lowest price ever.

Should you prefer over-ear headphones, the AirPods Max 2 are $120 off as part of Amazon’s Black Friday sale. Back when the first generation of these dropped in 2022, editor-at-large Dave Holmes called them “Apple’s best product in years.” Four years later, Apple’s second stab has only improved the superb noise cancellation and sound quality we already loved, and added in a slate of beloved features from the AirPods Pro (think Live Translation, Conversation Awareness, and Personalized Volume), without sacrificing the sleek build.

Since the second generation was released in March 2026, the lowest we’ve seen them go is $398. That said, a $120 price cut is nothing to scoff at, especially in an age where our favorite tech has fallen prey to price increases. Note that these tend to sell out pretty quickly when they’re more than $100 off.
