One Piece Chapter 1199: Release date & major spoilers

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

One Piece Chapter 1199 reveals Luffy’s new mysterious transformation, so here’s everything you need to know.

Undoubtedly one of the greatest chapters in the Elbaf Arc, the new installment follows the events of Chapter 1198, where the Straw Hat captain debuts a new form. This comes after his futile attempt to defeat Imu with the help of Loki and Hajrudin.

As Killingham is about to be defeated by Sanji, the Rain God Zaza tries to board the giant children on the ship. This is when Luffy appears in his new form, resembling a monkey with marks around his body, and saves the children.

One Piece Chapter 1199 also promises to reveal more about the Void Century. With Robin, Saul, and Chopper receiving the centuries-old chest from Biblo, they learn about a human named Lili who used to live in Elbaf, which may unveil some mysteries of the forgotten era.

Titled ‘Things Passed Down Through Generations’, One Piece Chapter 1199 focuses on Luffy’s new monkey-like form and its powers. The cover page features Vivi asking Morgan to take her picture for her wanted poster.

After ripping Zaza away from the ship, Luffy beats his chest. It creates a beat that the giants recognize as the Drums of Liberation. However, they soon realize that the rhythm is a little different, hinting that Luffy’s Hito Hito no Mi, Model: Nika, has more in store than we previously thought.

Meanwhile, Sanji is still chasing Killingham, aiming a kick at his heart but missing. While Killingham is still alive, his nightmare monster Zaza is beaten by Luffy. After he defeats her, he turns his attention to the flying ship Dorry and Brogy are riding.

Luffy rips a giant house from the ground and throws it at Dorry and Brogy’s ship. However, they cut the house in half in a single slash before it can crush them. Not one to lose easily, Luffy jumps into the air to attack them, wielding lightning as a sword in his right hand and a cloud as a shield in his left.

As this goes down, Imu is seen approaching the village, while his minion shadow monsters carry the giant children to the ship.

There’s a lot more happening on the island, too. Inside the Owl Library, Robin finds Nefertari Lili’s letter in the chest., in which she asks: Nefertari? Isn’t that the name from Alabasta?”.

One Piece Chapter 1199 ends with Robin declaring, “I’m going to read the letter.” There’ll be no break next week, meaning One Piece will continue with its usual release schedule.
