TELA Bio (TELA) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Revenue fell 8% as surgeon turnover hit OviTex, but cost cuts stretch the runway.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

TELA Bio, Inc. (TELA -7.47%) reported a year-over-year revenue decrease of 8% for the third quarter, primarily attributed to volume declines in its plastic and reconstructive surgery segment. Management initiated a $17 million annualized cost reduction plan, which included a 20% headcount reduction, to extend the company cash runway into 2028. The company regained compliance with NASDAQ minimum bid price requirements and received 510(k) clearance for its Liora Monofilament Scaffold. Management indicated that U.S. hernia products saw sequential growth in both volume and revenue, while the international business grew 18% behind strength in the United Kingdom.
