S&P puts Nvidia’s AA credit rating on downgrade watch over governance

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

S&P says the Nvidia credit rating could fall one notch, to AA-, if governance gaps remain. Huang’s exit and return did not answer its questions about board oversight.

Nvidia’s Voyager building at its headquarters in Santa Clara, California

S&P Global Ratings has put Nvidia’s AA credit rating on watch for a possible downgrade. The Jensen Huang saga exposed weak spots in how the company is run and overseen, the agency said in a note on Wednesday. It could cut the rating within 90 days if those problems remain.

Any cut would likely be a single notch, to AA-, S&P said. It could go further if investigations or disclosures showed harm to Nvidia’s reputation or future business. The watch covers both the company’s rating and its senior unsecured debt.

“News regarding the CEO and questions regarding potential export control violations have exposed vulnerabilities in NVIDIA’s management and governance framework, especially in regard to its board structure, composition, and effectiveness,” S&P wrote.

The agency said the long tenure of some directors may have compromised their independence. Nvidia’s governance may also not have kept pace with its fast growth, it added.

“While NVIDIA management has delivered significant returns for its stakeholders over decades, weak executive oversight and lack of succession planning have brought questions around the ‘key man’ risk to the forefront,” analysts Andrew Chang and David Tsui wrote.

S&P cut its management and governance score for Nvidia to neutral from positive. Huang’s departure and his reinstatement do not answer its questions about how the board oversees executive conduct, it said. The agency plans to meet the board and management to discuss board independence, oversight and a credible succession plan.

Nvidia’s competitive position, technology lead and finances are unchanged for now, according to S&P. Stricter export controls could still slow its growth. The agency would take a harsher view if it came to believe Nvidia broke or got around export rules. The same goes for any sign that past disclosures understated its exposure to China and nearby regions.

S&P’s move follows Moody’s. On 18 November, Moody’s lowered its outlook on Nvidia to stable from positive over governance concerns, CNBC reported. Moody’s kept that outlook after Huang returned. Nvidia shares have fallen about 12.5% since Monday’s close, CNBC added.

A lower rating could raise Nvidia’s borrowing costs. The company sold $25bn of senior notes in June, shortly after S&P raised it to AA, according to CNBC.

Nvidia also lends its AA rating to less creditworthy companies by backing their debt, leases and hardware purchases, Semafor reported. On Wednesday, measures of credit risk for Nvidia and CoreWeave hit record highs, Bloomberg reported, as fears over AI debt returned.

Some critics say the agency is late. Nvidia had no board chair until Huang resigned on 12 November and Harvey Jones became its first, Martin Peers wrote in The Information. That weakness was public in Nvidia’s filings, and S&P could have flagged it earlier, he argued.
