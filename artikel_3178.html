Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Precipio targets a $40 million run rate by 2027 as it expands its sales team.

Precipio, Inc. (PRPO +6.53%) reported third-quarter results characterized by a transition from achieving financial independence in 2025 toward a strategy of accelerated revenue growth for 2027. Management reported that the company achieved a quarterly cash balance exceeding $3.5 million while maintaining positive adjusted EBITDA and operating cash flow. The company stated its intent to leverage established distribution agreements with four major industry partners to scale its product division, targeting a combined annualized revenue run rate of $40 million by the end of 2027. This growth plan includes doubling the sales force and hiring a senior marketing executive to enhance clinical credibility and market awareness. Management noted that current facility infrastructure is already equipped to handle up to $100 million in annual revenue without further major capital expenditures.
