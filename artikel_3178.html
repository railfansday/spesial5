Audible’s Best Deal Is Back — Get 3 Months for $1 a Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

If you’re looking to get ahead on New Year’s resolutions — and your goal is to read more — Audible is launching an early Black Friday deal. You can get the first three months for $0.99/mo., which is an 80% discount on the regular $8.99/mo. price. When you sign up, you also get a $20 bonus on Audible audiobook purchases. The offer ends on Dec. 28.

In addition to a wide selection of books, Audible has exclusives and originals. Originals include the Andy Weir-produced Exoplanet, written by Benjamin Percy and read by Anthony Mackie, Ariana DeBose, and John Leguizamo. Audible features podcasts as well, including A Great Book With Barack Obama, a podcast hosted by the former president with guests including Tom Hanks and Ta-Nehisi Coates.

The top titles on Audible include Suzanne Collins’ Sunrise on the Reaping, narrated by Jefferson White, Matt Dinniman’s Dungeon Crawler Carl, narrated by Jeff Hays, and Exoplanet.

With a standard monthly subscription, Audible members can choose one audiobook a month to listen to. The selected audiobook stays in your collection, meaning you can revisit favorite titles. Additional books can be purchased at full price.

The premium membership starts at $14.95/mo. In addition to one book credit a month, members get access to any of the titles in the all-you-can-listen-to catalogue and credits that roll over month to month.

Memberships can be cancelled at any time, and members can keep any title they purchased with a credit even if their membership is no longer active.

As for the Audible app, it features adjustable playback speeds, and you can set a timer to pause playback if you’re listening before bed. Other features include car mode, which enlarges app icons for easier one-handed control. Users can also download titles for offline listening while traveling.
