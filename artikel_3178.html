Here's How Many Shares of Procter & Gamble You'd Need for $1,000 in Yearly Dividends

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The consumer goods leader is one of the best dividend stocks investors can own.

By Neil Patel – Nov 20, 2026 at 4:00PM EST

Procter & Gamble (PG +0.91%)shares have climbed only 103% in the past 10 years (as of Nov. 13). The company certainly isn't drawing in growth investors, but it can be a top choice for income seekers.

Here's how many shares of this consumer staples stock you would need to generate $1,000 in yearly dividends.

Procter & Gamble currently pays a quarterly dividend of $1.0885 per share, which amounts to $4.354 on an annualized basis. This means you would have to own 230 shares to collect $1,000 in yearly passive income. At the current stock price of $169.83, the investment required totals over $39,000.

At 2.56%, the dividend yield is healthy. That's much higher than what the S&P 500 index (^GSPC -3.70%) offers.

Investors should be blown away by the payout's durability. In April, the board of directors raised the dividend, which marked the 70th straight year that a hike was approved. What’s more, the business has paid dividends in 136 consecutive years, a mind-boggling track record.

Shareholders can sleep well at night knowing that their income stream is safe. Procter & Gamble reports sizable earnings, with an operating margin in the first quarter of 2027 (ended Sept. 30) of 25.5%, that results in meaningful free cash flow. The company has also stood the test of time, a sign that its products have stable demand throughout various economic scenarios.

The stock isn't a home run. It's a safe portfolio addition, however.
