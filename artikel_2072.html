SPFL reveals Rangers vs Hibs final date, time, ticket split and prices

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Rangers and Hibernian have been handed a 50/50 split of Hampden for next month’s Premier Sports Cup final.

The sides will meet at the national stadium on Sunday, December 13, with the showpiece scheduled for a 3pm kick-off and broadcast live on Premier Sports.

The SPFL has confirmed the ticketing arrangements following discussions with both clubs and the relevant authorities.

Hibernian supporters will be housed across the South, East and North Stands, while Rangers fans will be located across the South, West and North Stands.

Ticket prices will be the same for both sets of supporters.

The majority of adult tickets will cost £42, with adult seats in the North and South Stands priced at £49.

Concessionary tickets will also be available across the stadium, priced at £27 or £30 depending on the section.

Rangers secured their place in the final by defeating Aberdeen last Saturday, with Ryan Naderi scoring a late winner in the semi-final.

Hibernian booked their Hampden return a day later with a 2-1 victory over Edinburgh rivals Hearts.

All tickets will be issued digitally through the Barclays Hampden app, with both clubs set to announce their respective sales information through their own channels.

It will be only the second time Rangers and Hibs have met in the final of the competition.

Their previous meeting came in the 1993/94 final at Celtic Park, when Rangers won 2-1.
