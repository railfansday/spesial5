From Brazil: Roma and Milan the most concrete options for Endrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Endrick’s entourage is positively considering a possible move to Italy.

This is according to ESPN Brazil, which reports that those close to the Real Madrid striker are positively considering a move to Serie A, especially given the player’s limited playing time in the first half of the season.

The Brazilian continues to find little space in José Mourinho’s rotation, despite reiterating just yesterday his desire to remain at Real Madrid for the rest of his career.

Roma and Milan have been tracking him for some time, with the Giallorossi having maintained constant contact with the 2006-born player’s entourage ahead of the January transfer window.

According to ESPN Brazil, AC Milan is preparing an offer to acquire Endrick, while Real Madrid is considering a fee between €60 and €70 million. Furthermore, the Spanish club and the striker are not interested in another loan spell, instead considering a permanent transfer.

This is a different story from the one Matteo Moretto provided this morning to Retesport, where he had identified a loan deal as Real Madrid’s preferred option.

Moretto also emphasized that Roma have moved more decisively than AC Milan since September, reiterating to Endrick in the last few hours their intention to bring him to the capital.
