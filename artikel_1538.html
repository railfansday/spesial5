CorSport: Mourinho has ‘no intention’ of letting Endrick go as Milan prepare €70m move

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Endrick is the big target for AC Milan in January, but they may have some difficulty agreeing on a deal for the youngster.

Real Madrid’s attack is stacked with talent and Endrick’s minutes have been extremely limited so far this season. So, Milan have hoped that this would pave the way to a deal being worked out in the January mercato.

However, it seems that it will be increasingly difficult to pry him away from the Bernabeu.

According to Corriere dello Sport, via MilanNews, Jose Mourinho is proving to be a huge problem in the Rossoneri’s pursuit of Endrick. The Portuguese has ‘no intention’ of allowing the star to leave in January.

His position is clear and it would ‘outweigh the financial offer’ being prepared, which is believed to be worth €60 million, with a further €10m available in bonuses and an €80m buyback clause. So, whether his stance will change is something that will be key to any potential deal.

The Diavolo will not give up, though, and the new recruit Brett Yormark could play a big role in his arrival. Formerly of Roc Nation – Endrick’s agency – he has links to the company himself and through his brother, Michael, who is the president of the agency itself.

Through that relationship and Gerry Cardinale’s, there could be conversations with his entourage and that could change the situation. Privileged contacts, as MilanNews writes, will have no impact if Mourinho’s stance doesn’t change, though.
