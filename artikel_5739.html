NAED Unveils PV-1 PECVD-V, a U.S.-Engineered Vertical Deposition Platform Built for 96,000-Wafer-per-Hour Tandem Solar Manufacturing

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Patent-pending vertical PECVD/PVD architecture doubles wafer throughput over NAED’s previous platform and is designed to produce perovskite/HJT tandem cells targeting 30% efficiency

NAED Energy Holding Inc. (“NAED”), the U.S. aerospace, engineering, and utility-energy company behind NAED Nexus Core™, last week unveiled PV-1 PECVD-V, a vertical in-line deposition platform for high-volume solar cell manufacturing. PV-1 combines plasma-enhanced chemical vapor deposition (PECVD) and physical vapor deposition (PVD) on a single shared-carrier line. It is designed for a throughput of 96,000 wafers per hour, double the 48,000 wafers per hour of NAED’s previous XL-class platform.

In PV-1, wafers travel upright on a shared carrier through every PECVD and PVD stage. To reach the second side of the wafer, the system never flips the substrate: the entire carrier is lifted and turned 180 degrees as a unit. This keeps wafers seated in the same carrier from first deposition to last, cutting direct wafer handling between process steps. The PV-1 production line, its 180-degree carrier turn, its wafer-retention carrier, and the T2X tandem cell are the subject of pending U.S. patent applications assigned to NAED Energy Holding Inc. NAED’s intellectual property portfolio has been independently appraised at $179 million under an ASC 820 Level 3 fair-value methodology, as of November 11, 2026.

PV-1 was engineered from the start for tandem solar. The full line, including perovskite top-cell process tools, is designed to produce NAED’s T2X two-terminal perovskite/heterojunction (HJT) tandem cell, which targets 30% cell efficiency. NAED plans to bring T2X cells to both terrestrial and space solar markets, alongside a family of glass-glass T2X modules starting at 600 W.

Every PV-1 tool is designed in-house by NAED and machined in its own aerospace-grade CNC shop in the United States. The first six of PV-1’s thirteen process tools are complete, and the remaining seven are in production. Engineering and design for the full PV-1 line and its production facility are complete.

“Going vertical, let us double throughput and build the line around tandem cells from day one, rather than retrofitting tandem onto a platform designed for something else,” said Malcom Longair, Media & Development Lead, NAED Energy Holding Inc. “With PV-1, manufacturers anywhere in the world can reserve U.S.-engineered deposition equipment built for the next generation of solar cells.”

NAED is now opening reservations for PV-1 production tool slots to solar cell manufacturers in the United States and internationally. Manufacturers can request specifications and reserve a slot at NAED. energy or through the media and partnership contact below.
