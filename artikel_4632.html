Give This Nike Gear to Your Favorite Training Partner

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

We do the heavy lifting for all your gifting.

YOUR TRAINING PARTNER is there for you, all year long. When you’re struggling under that final press, he lends a finger or two. When it’s time to get psyched, he’ll say just the right thing. And when you’re looking woozily at the alarm clock, wondering whether you “need” the gym today, the thought of leaving him hanging snaps you into action.

So with the holidays upon us, isn’t it time you showed him a bit of gratitude? If you’re strong on spotting but weak on your gift-giving acumen, we’ve got you covered with an assist. From shoes and clothes to an array of gifts under $50, plus some stylish bags to hold it all, here’s the iconic Nike gear to bring good cheer to your brother in iron.

If your training partner is still rocking the same sneaks in the gym that he throws on to mow the lawn, it’s time to upgrade his workout fit. The Nike Metcon 10 Workout Shoe is meant to handle everything from deadlifts, bench presses, and biceps curls to an all-out leg day with ease. It provides unmatched stability through the built-in Hyperlift plate (directly under the heel), plus a generous toe box for those heavy exercises that require you to spread those digits and really dig in.

The midsole is lightweight, more so than you’d expect from a typical training shoe, due to the ReactX foam—which not only helps enhance energy return on traditional powerlifting moves but boosts the bounce-back on high-intensity plyos like box jumps, burpees, and even between-set sprints. He’ll also appreciate the “sticky” texture and traction pattern underfoot for a no-slip session.

Another Option: On squat day, you need shorts that you can truly depend on, with enough give to bottom out and enough sweat-wicking action to keep you dry and comfortable. Nike’s Men’s Flex Dri-FIT Training Shorts, available in an array of colors and sizes, is a great short to wear while strength training.

The Hybrid RN is Nike’s most versatile training shoe, aimed at guys who love to run and lift during HIIT and other conditioning sessions. It’s a strong yet airy shoe that can take on any challenge in the weight room, thanks to a stable heel suited to jump squats and sled work and a forefoot that pairs an Air Zoom unit with ReactX foam for responsive cushioning on runs. Add the X-shaped overlay on the upper for ample midfoot containment, and you have a trainer that switches effortlessly from broad jumps to HIIT cardio without a slip.

Another Option: Give him a new set of go-to workout clothes made with Dri-FIT technology that can handle the most sweaty and intense workouts that are his favorite while keeping him dry. Replace a favorite well-worn t-shirt with Dri-FIT Legend T-Shirt, which is soft and relaxed and keeps sweat away. Add Nike N.A.C. Dri-FIT Woven Shorts, which provide a roomy fit and the freedom of movement needed during HIIT and conditioning workouts. Between the shirt and shorts, you’ve created a performance pair where strength and style come together.

Sure, your training partner could keep sporting his slightly pungent, faded sweatshirt that’s been seeing action since his college days for his bar league. Or, give this avid outdoor player a wardrobe upgrade with the Nike Stride Men’s Therma-FIT Repel Winterized 1/2-Zip Running Top. Its water-repellent finish and Therma-FIT technology help manage body heat in cold-weather conditions, while the soft, brushed waffle-like interior adds lightweight warmth. Raglan sleeves help reduce underarm chafing, thumbholes help keep the sleeves in place, and reflective details add a practical touch for runs. It’s a much more considered layer than his so-called “lucky” pullover that’s survived one pub crawl too many.

Another Option: Sure, it’s a running jacket, but it’s great for so much more than that. The Nike Therma-FIT ADV AeroLoft innovates with down insulation at the chest to keep the core warm in wicked weather, while lightweight insulation everywhere else gives you just enough protection to not get overheated when gutting out a cold-weather boot camp in the park.

Imagine a shoe so responsive that it feels like you’re being propelled forward with every step. That’s the Nike Pegasus Plus 2, designed to deliver lightweight speed and maximum comfort for most kinds of runners. The unique “Air Zoom” feature—actually visible in the forefoot of this eye-catching sneak—delivers powerful energy return, while the updated waffle-style outsole provides increased traction and just the right amount of ground feel for confidence on asphalt paths, paved streets, and sidewalks.

Another Option: The Nike Men’s Repel Woven Running Pants are stretchy yet water repellent, with a quick-drying waistband and two zippered pockets.

Depending on when the two of you train, Nike has the backpack or duffel to carry your gear. If it’s before or after work, the Nike Brasilia backpack is a solid choice—it’s extremely roomy, with more than enough space for a laptop, change of clothes, and a pair of dress shoes, along with a zippered front pocket to keep the sweaty togs sequestered from everything else. For wet climates or long winters, the Nike Heritage Eugene 2.0 backpack is made with water-repellent materials, with three zippered accessory pockets, an open side pocket for that necessary water bottle, and a grab-and-go loop on top to hold it on a crowded bus or subway train.

As for duffels, the Jordan Element Duffel is an all-timer, just like its GOAT namesake. It comes in two sizes, both versatile and spacious with room for everything from a laptop to a vented “shoe garage” on the left side to hold an extra pair. It also has a hidden velvet-lined pocket on the front (behind the iconic Jumpman logo) for phones, wallets, passports, or whatever else you need to keep safe and dry.

If your training partner is one of those people who skips out of recovery, try enticing them with a shoe that puts a slide to shame. The Nike ReactX Rejuven8 will caress their foot in ReactX foam that is soft and responsive to the foot’s every movement. Extra room in the forefoot allows toes to move freely while ventilation holes allow the foot to breathe. And unlike slides that can make walking challenging, the Rejuven8’s sticky rubber sole will help grip even the slipperiest of surfaces. They’re the ultimate go-to footwear after every workout.

Nike offers many budget-friendly training gifts under $50. The Jordan Element Gym Sack is simple and stylish, with drawstrings that give the option to sling it over a shoulder or wear it as a backpack, plus hidden zip pockets on the front to secure the important stuff. There’s also the Nike ACG Dri-FIT Knit Beanie, with three cool colors to choose from, or the Nike Dri-FIT ADV Club Structured Swoosh Cap, which wicks away sweat and keeps the head cool in all conditions. If your training partner wears a smartwatch, the Nike Sport Loop can handle sweaty workouts. And if you think socks are an uncool gift, you haven’t checked out the Nike Run Wool socks, with sweat-wicking wool fabric and well-placed cushioning and support mile after mile.

The best gym bag gift combines both style and functionality. The Nike Heritage Eugene 2.0 and Jordan Element Duffel check all the boxes. The ideal gym bag, whether it is a backpack or a duffel, fits everything you need, with various pockets and separate compartments to help keep everything organized. If you typically carry valuables, zippered pockets are essential, as is venting, and options to place wet clothes or dirty shoes away from your clean gear. And an easy-access slip-in-and-out space for a water bottle will ensure you’re always hydrated.
