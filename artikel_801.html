Why Kacey Musgraves, Miranda Lambert Song Cut Out at CMA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

During Kacey Musgraves and Miranda Lambert’s performance of “Horses and Divorces” at the CMA Awards 2026, their vocals were partially cut from broadcast. Here’s why.

Kacey Musgraves and Miranda Lambert might like to cuss (and smoke and drink) but the CMA Awards had reign them in.

During the duo’s rendition of their song “Horses and Divorces” at the CMA Awards Nov. 18, many country fans noticed that their performance was partially obstructed from the broadcast—and that’s all due to the language in the song.

While some stars opt to perform a clean version of their songs to follow the Federal Communications Commission guidelines, Musgraves and Lambert performed the original lyrics, which sees Lambert sing, “I’ve done my fair share of s--t talkin,’ too.”

Those present at the ceremony in Bridgestone Arena in Nashville were able to hear Lambert’s entire vocals, but during the ABC broadcast, there was a few seconds of white noise that confused viewers—especially those unfamiliar with the duet.

Luckily, the country singers’ hook, “hell just froze over” is permitted by the FCC and it exemplifies just how momentous the song—which is featured on Musgraves’ album Middle of Nowhere—truly is, considering the stars were previously at odds which each other.

Musgraves admitted during an interview with CBS Mornings earlier this year that it was “common knowledge there was a bit of soft tension” between her and Lambert—and so she decided to bury the hatchet once and for all by featuring the “The House that Built Me” singer on her latest album.

Lambert, for her part, shared she was surprised by Musgraves’ request.

“I hadn’t heard from her in a while,” Lambert—who split from Blake Shelton in 2015—explained in a separate interview during the same CBS Mornings segment. “She texted me and was like, ‘I need to ask you something but I need to ask you on the phone, like I want to talk about this.’”

Musgraves—whose breakup with husband Ruston Kelly occurred five years later in 2020—noted that while she was putting together her album, she saw a video of Lambert riding her horse online and thought, “‘Hey, at least we’ve got two things in common.’”

And so, she thought of the concept for “Horses and Divorces.” When Musgraves pitched the song to Lambert, now married to Brendan McLoughlin, and said “we need to write it,” the 43-year-old said she was immediately game, saying, “Hell yeah we do.”

As she put it, “It was an immediate yes.”
