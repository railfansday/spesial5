Crystal Palace Women deducted three points for use of subs

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Crystal Palace Women are being punished for making substitutions at four separate windows in a win over Everton in September - the last of which came in the 97th minute - which is in breach of IFAB Laws that state clubs have no more than three substitution opportunities in each match

Crystal Palace Women have been deducted three points in the WSL with immediate effect.

Promoted side Palace, who now drop into the relegation play-off spot, are being punished for making substitutions at four separate windows in a 1-0 win over Everton in September.

This is in breach of IFAB Laws, which state clubs can make five substitutions, but have no more than three opportunities in each match to do so.

Palace made a substitution in a fourth window in the 97th minute of the match. Kirsty Howat, who was brought on for Shannon O'Brien, did not touch the ball before the full-time whistle was blown.

Palace admitted to the breach but felt the sanction sought by the WSL was "excessive", according to the written decisions published with the sanction.

However, the independent tribunal determined that a sporting sanction was necessary to avoid damaging the integrity of the competition.

In response, Palace said in a statement on Tuesday: "Everyone at the club is obviously frustrated that such a minor infraction, given that the player was on the pitch for only 46 seconds while the ball was in play, has led to such a significant penalty - a penalty which we do not agree is reasonable or proportionate.

"We are disappointed for the players and staff who worked so hard to secure a deserved victory on our return to the WSL."

Everton's points total is unaffected by the sanction. They remain 11th in the table on 12 points after 11 matches. Palace, meanwhile, drop to second bottom, falling behind Birmingham on goal difference.

Everton disagreed with the assertion of Palace head coach Jo Potter that the outcome of the match was unaffected by their use of a fourth substitution window.

In a letter to the independent tribunal, Everton set out a four-point argument for how the non-compliant substitution had a direct sporting impact on the match.

"Palace did not make an extra substitution but used an extra substitution window, making changes in the 63rd, 83rd, 90th and, crucially, the 97th minute.

"This is devastating for Palace because Kirsty Howat, who came on for Shannon O'Brien, was only on the pitch for a matter of seconds and she didn't touch the ball.

"It is recognised [by the independent tribunal] that this hasn't created any advantage for Palace when it comes to the result, they have to deter other teams from choosing not to adhere to this rule.

"This will be upsetting for Palace, who have dropped down to second bottom in the table. The team in 14th at bottom of the table, currently Charlton, will be immediately relegated.

"Whoever is 13th goes into a play-off against the winner of a match between the second and third team in WSL 2.

"This is gutting for Palace, who were above Birmingham before this deduction. Now they've dropped down and they're in danger. And, mentally, we know how hard it is for promoted teams to stay in the WSL."
