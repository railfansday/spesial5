SKIMS’ New Cryo Globes Look Like Snow Globes and They Sculpt Your Face

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Kim Kardashian’s SKIMS collabs on a limited-edition beauty tool that looks like snow globes and it’s made to save winter skin. The glass tools help sculpt and depuff, and make a luxurious gift.

SKIMS continues to innovate, now with a newly launched facial tool with a festive twist. Teaming up with celebrity makeup artist Jenny Patinkin on limited-edition Cryo Glow Globes designed to soothe, sculpt and depuff the face for a glowing complexion.

Beauty holiday gift: Jenny Patinkin x SKIMS Cryo Glow Globes, $78

Patinkin, founder of Eco-Luxe Beauty Tools, called the collaboration a natural fit in an exclusive interview with E!. “Both brands are fundamentally about making women feel more comfortable, confident, and beautiful, without asking them to change who they are,” she said.

The hand-blown glass globes contain floating glitter shaped like snowflakes, candy canes and SKIMS logo sequins, bringing snow-globe energy to your vanity.

Skims' brings its sleek aesthetic to your vanity with these hand-blown glass globes, featuring floating glitter and sequins in festive winter-y motifs.

This marks the first-ever collaboration for the Jenny Patinkin brand, of which has become known for its luxe beauty tools.

“Cryo tools are a quick, easy way to give skin an instantly refreshed, more lifted and more radiant look," said Patinkin, founder of Jenny Patinkin Eco-Luxe Beauty Tools. "The goal is for your skin to look and feel its best, the same way SKIMS makes you look and feel your best.”

Created exclusively for the collaboration, the hand-blown glass globes feature snowflake, candy cane and SKIMS logo sequins floating like in a snow globe. The collaboration brings together Patinkin’s expertise in beauty tools and SKIMS’ signature aesthetic, with the goal of making a sculpting facial ritual easy to incorporate into your everyday routine.

"Cryo Globes are a great way to quickly help get the look of less puffy, more lifted skin," says Patinkin. "The cold temperatures cause a vasoconstriction response in the skin, which can help to create a depuffing effect, and can also help to lock off redness and soothe irritation.

"You can use these tools to give yourself a little lymphatic massage which can aid with the drainage of excess fluids in the skin, and I also find them soothing for sinus pain, headaches, bug bites and sunburns."

Cold, dry weather and a packed holiday calendar can leave skin feeling irritated or puffy. Patinkin says the globes’ cooling effect can help soothe irritation, while gently massaging (similar to a guashua) reduces puffiness for a more sculpted look—an easy at-home facial refresh during the craziest time of year? Now that's a gift worth giving, especially in winter.
