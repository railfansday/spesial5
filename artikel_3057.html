Ackman on Huang's Exit, Anthropic IPO Roadshow

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Bill Ackman, Pershing Square founder and CEO, says it's sad to see Jensen Huang leave Nvidia and he'll go on to do something great. Ackman also says he was impressed with the presentation given by Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei at the company's IPO roadshow event in New York. He speaks to Bloomberg's Isabelle Lee. (Source: Bloomberg)
