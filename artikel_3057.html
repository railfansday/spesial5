Bengals vs. Commanders odds, expert picks, and our prediction for Week 11 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Cincinnati Bengals have been one of the hottest teams in the NFL and are tied with a handful of teams with a five-game win streak. That culminated in an absolute whooping of the Pittsburgh Steelers at home on Sunday Night Football that wasn't even as close as the 20-point win made it seem. Now they are taking their show on the road to play the Washington Commanders in their house on Monday Night Football.

Joe Burrow is 0-2 so far in his career against the Commanders, but that comes with some pretty big caveats. The first loss was the game in which he injured his knee midway through the game as a rookie. The other loss came in an absolute monster of a shootout where the Bengals defense did not stop the Commanders or Jayden Daniels a single time in 2024, with the game ending 38-33. That would not be the first time Burrow would helm a losing team despite putting up more than 30 points over the next few seasons.

These teams are drastically different as the Cincinnati defense has had a major overhaul, and Washington is far from the team it was during Daniels' rookie season. There is no doubt Daniels is a talented quarterback, but the Commanders themselves seem to be a very average to below-average team that needs a combination of home run hits on offense and a handful of turnovers to spark a win.

Meanwhile, Burrow, Ja'Marr Chase, and Tee Higgins are giving their best impressions of the Colts' offense during Peyton Manning's heyday by trusting their quarterback to put up points with his top weapons over a balanced attack. That has led to four straight games of four touchdowns for Burrow, which ties a record set by Manning.

The real x-factor here will be the Bengals defense. Will the version we have seen since the Madrid game against the Atlanta Falcons show up that has been able to contain opposing offenses that tried to test their linebackers and safeties? Or will the young players at those positions allow a few too many big plays?

Here is how the oddsmakers and experts feel about the game.

The Bengals are currently 6.5-point road favorites against the Commanders, according to FanDuel Sportsbook. It really is a fairly big deal when a road team gets almost a touchdown advantage over a home team. It clearly shows that there is a step between where these two teams are currently at.

The point total is set at 53.5 is the second highest of the week. That is fair because even when this team has faced teams like the Tennessee Titans, they have given up opportunities to score.

10 of ESPN's 11 experts are going with the Bengals

CBS Sports has all seven experts taking Cincinnati to win

All five of NFL.com's experts are expecting the Bengals to extend their win streak to six games

NFL Pick Watch — which takes into account picks from across the web — has Cincinnati receiving 99% of the picks for this game.

I think this team has really turned a corner as far as this defense is concerned. The young linebacker duo of Demetrius Knight and Barrett Carter have grown enough to prove to be a competent — maybe not the most consistent — duo. That is the level of play this defense needs when the defensive line led by Boye Mafe and Dexter Lawrence is able to get penetration. We will have to see how well they contain Daniels, who can really cause some damage by extending plays with his legs.

All of that more or less will determine how much the Commanders lose by. Washington has no way of slowing down or throwing off this offense. The Bengals are getting into at least the 30s. Can the Commanders keep pace?
