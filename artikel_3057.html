Stock Market Today, Nov. 19: Alphabet Shares Fall as Anthropic Nasdaq Debut Puts Fresh Value on Google's Stake

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Today, Nov. 19, 2026, Alphabet's slide came as investors weighed its private AI holdings and a heavy trading session.

By Howard Smith – Nov 19, 2026 at 4:58PM EST

Alphabet (GOOGL -3.46%), the search, advertising, cloud, and AI platforms provider, closed at $272.66, down 3.46%. Anthropic's Nasdaq debut and Google's Blue Origin partnership update were cited as catalysts, while investors are watching how AI-linked valuation gains affect Alphabet's investment portfolio.Trading volume reached 48.1 million shares, coming in about 58% above its three-month average of 30.4 million shares. Alphabet IPO'd in 2004 and has grown 10,763% since going public.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC -2.02%) closed at 7,176, down 2.02%, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -2.25%) closed at 23,879, down 2.25%. Among internet media and digital advertising peers, Meta Platforms closed at $596.22, down 3.27%, and Amazon.com closed at $257.37, down 3.87%, showing broad pressure across large-cap AI and cloud names.

Anthropic's market debut and IPO pricing put fresh value on Alphabet's nearly 14% stake. The Google parent already owns close to its 15% ownership cap, which is now worth about $175 billion based on Anthropic's initial closing price.

In other news, Google has picked Blue Origin's New Glenn rocket to launch data center satellites for its Project Suncatcher program in 2029, the company said today.

Google first revealed its plans to run AI systems in space through orbiting data centers about a year ago, calling the effort one of its research "moonshots." That project will garner investor attention, but Anthropic's debut was the focus today. Given Alphabet's meaningful ownership, it's likely that some investors pulled money from that stock to buy Anthropic today.
