GTA 6 Actor Says Rockstar Made Their Non-Binary Character Unkillable

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Jesse James Rose speaks about their experience on set for GTA 6

The state of Leonida has thousands of inhabitants, each one with their own story. One of them is Cygnus, not only Vice City's museum curator, but also the first non-binary character in Grand Theft Auto history. In a detailed Instagram post, actor Jesse James Rose revealed that they played Cygnus and shared their experience working with Rockstar Games on the set of GTA 6.

Grand Theft Auto allows players to lead a hectic life of crime with lots of killing, shooting, and stealing, but of course, that doesn't reflect the values of the people who made it. Rose began filming for GTA 6 three years before its release and spent weeks on set. They shared that the set of Grand Theft Auto 6 was among the most inclusive and caring sets they'd worked on. However, at the time, they had no idea what they were filming for; to ensure confidentiality, they had to sign 17 different NDAs.

Considering that Jesse James Rose spent weeks filming, Cygnus is expected to play a role in the story of GTA 6 or a major sidequest, so players will likely encounter them at some point in the campaign. To prevent anti-trans violence in Grand Theft Auto 6, Rockstar assured Rose that players would not be able to harm Cygnus in-game.

The trans actor (who now uses she/they pronouns but preferred they/them pronouns at the time) said the developers made sure to portray the non-binary character Cygnus accurately and respectfully. Rockstar relied exclusively on motion capture to create Cygnus, highlighting that it would be impossible for AI to come up with the same faces as a real person. Given the growing use of AI in the video game industry, this stood out to Rose: "Remember, in a world consumed with Generative AI, the largest video game in history chose human labor to preserve the integrity of the game."

While Jesse James Rose played the first non-binary character in Grand Theft Auto, they were not the only trans person working on the game. Trans people held other crucial roles in the making of GTA 6, such as writers, designers, and more. Rose notes that although they were "paid well" and are still "thrilled to be a part of this project," they stand by their values and continue to advocate for workplace unionization, workers' rights, and fair compensation, which have been issues at Rockstar Games.
