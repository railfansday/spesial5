The Video Game Hardship Fund Raised Almost $300,000 For Laid-Off Developers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The United Video Game Workers-CWA union has released a report with details on the hardship fund it started in collaboration with Necrosoft Games earlier this year benefiting laid-off developers. The UVW reports that through its itch.io bundle and from monies received via union dues and outside donations, it raised $290,000 over two weeks. All that cash has been dispersed to over 150 devs from across the industry to help with expenses like rent, medical bills, and other debt.

"This came at a time when I deeply, deeply needed a hand to pull me up while I'm drowning."

Perusing the report’s list of studios from which applicants to the fund have been laid off is depressing. Among them are some of the biggest names in the industry: Xbox, Activision Blizzard, EA, Ubisoft, and more. It’s the human reckoning, the terrible price to all the industry upheaval that’s often glossed over in reports on resets, booming financials, and robust pipelines. The other equally sad detail is just how long many of these out-of-work people have worked in the industry, with 32 percent of applicants reporting 10 years or more.

The industry is experiencing a brain drain that will be incredibly difficult to recover from. Nintendo is able to make the games it can because it retains its employees, training them up to eventually lead games of their own. Jason Schreier reported at Bloomberg that 21 percent of Rockstar staff are vets with 10-plus years of experience, and they’ve just released what has the potential to be the best-selling, best-reviewed game of all time.
