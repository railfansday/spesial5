Waiting for the first big storm is the riskiest time to buy a snow blower, since popular models sell out fast. Black Friday deals at The Home Depot, Lowe’s, Walmart, Amazon, Ace Hardware, and Tractor Supply cut prices on gas, cordless, and electric snow blowers by up to 50%. The biggest dollar savings is the PowerSmart 26-Inch 208cc Two-Stage Gas Snow Blower for $1,019.99 (was $1,699.99) at Lowe’s, $680 off a self-propelled machine with a Briggs & Stratton engine. To compare battery models, see BobVila.com’s tested guide to the best cordless snow blowers.

For smaller driveways and walks, the Litheli 40V 20-Inch Brushless Cordless Snow Blower for $194.99 (was $389.99) at Walmart is half off and includes two batteries and a charger. Power snow shovels for steps and decks start at $99 at Lowe’s.

Long driveways and deep, heavy snow call for a two-stage machine like this self-propelled gas snow blower. A Briggs & Stratton 208cc engine powers a 26-inch clearing path, and the self-propelled drive does the pushing. At 40% off, it’s the biggest dollar savings in this roundup.

This cordless snow blower clears a 20-inch path with a brushless motor and runs on two 20V batteries (included, along with a charger). It’s a budget-friendly pick for paved driveways and walks that get light to moderate snow, with no gas or pull cord to deal with.

This cordless dual-battery snow blower runs on two M18 batteries at once and comes with six, four 12.0Ah and two 8.0Ah packs, so a fresh pair can take over mid-driveway. It’s $550 off, and M18 owners can use the batteries in their other Milwaukee tools.

Single-stage blowers handle up to about 8 inches of light snow on paved surfaces. For deeper or heavy, wet snow, or for gravel drives, step up to a two-stage machine. Cordless models skip the gas and oil but need charged batteries, and power snow shovels are best for steps, decks, and short walks.
