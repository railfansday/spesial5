Amazon has a new way to shop — and you can get $5 off your first order for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

SAVE $5: Amazon is giving you $5 off your first order when you shop by text through Alexa.

And now, the retailer has introduced a new way to shop with Alexa, allowing you to find products, reorder essentials, and even sort out your grocery shopping, all through a simple text message.

You can use the service to send an entire shopping list at once, ask Alexa for product recommendations, or reorder something you've previously purchased. You can even upload a photo of an item and ask Alexa to find a similar one, saving you the trouble of searching for it yourself.

Alexa can also help with grocery shopping. Simply send over a recipe, and it'll add the ingredients to your cart. It can even remember your preferences for future orders.

To get this deal, head to Amazon's Alexa Shopping page, tap 'Text Alexa,' and accept the terms. The $5 discount is available for a limited time on eligible products and excludes gift cards, digital content, and subscriptions. You'll also need a personal U.S. Amazon account, a verified mobile number, and a phone that supports RCS messaging.
