Trump’s “Noisy Name-Calling” Mocked By NYT After Bored POTUS Starts Thanksgiving Ranting Against Reporter’s Nvidia Dispatch

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Executive Editor, Legal, Labor & Politics

Like death and taxes, you can always rely on there being an annoying relative at the table on Thanksgiving, and you can always depend on Donald Trump losing his marbles on Turkey Day.

Down in his Florida compound (which no one but Trump calls the Southern White House), the former Apprentice host took time early this AM local time to go on social media to blast off about the New York Times‘ reporting on Nvidia and the reinstating of scandal plagued CEO Jensen Huang. As well as likely keeping aide Natalie Harp busy posting for him, Trump pleaded with Vladimir Putin to “pull back all Military aggression against territories throughout Europe,” approving disaster relief for parts of the Sunshine State, boasting about the state of the economy and his extensive and pricey White House redecorations. However, with the possible of that last topic, Trump’s real vigor seemed to be reserved for chastising the Gray Lady and veteran journalist David Sanger. Watch on Deadline

Trump termed Sanger as a “mediocre reporter” after the Times ran a piece bylined by him on Trump’s much bragged about direct role in Huang rejoining the AI chip company he founded. Stinging Trump likely more, Sanger also wrote about the lack of stock market success that Huang’s return has brought Nivida, a company POTUS owns millions in stock in. Revealing his thin skin, Trump went on to say: “David Sanger is a lowlife that only writes what he is told to write by his bosses. Also, until the Election, best Market, EVER, 82 days, settled 8 Wars, and the USA is respected again like never before!”

Having had some success of late in Florida courts against the NYT in a $15 billion defamation suit, Trump had to , as he so often does, list off his assumed accomplishments — many of which are questionable to put it nicely.

In their own way, the Times were equally blunt and all-incompassing in their response today.

“If readers want to understand the market effects of President Trump’s interventions, including Nvidia’s reversal, his disruption of international trade, and his decision to go to war with Iran, then David Sanger’s latest report is an essential read,” the Ochs-Sulzberger family-owned outlet said this Thanksgiving Day. The NYT added: “David has covered five American presidents in more than four decades at The Times; his coverage of the ways in which the administration is shaping foreign policy and national security is unmatched. Noisy name-calling is just an attempt to distract from the news.”

That last line kinda sums up this second Trump administration, and the first, in PR poetry.
