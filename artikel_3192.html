Tanggal: 2026-11-14

AI now drives nearly half of revenue as the Columbiana facility nears launch.

Digi Power X Inc. (DGXX +3.17%) reported a transition toward AI infrastructure operations, as AI-related services now represent nearly half of the total revenue mix. Management stated that the company is focusing on completing its Columbiana facility and scaling the NeoCloudz platform to capture increasing demand for inference processing. The company confirmed that Phase I tenant access is scheduled for December 15, 2026, while the forward capital plan prioritizes growth through customer prepayments and project-level debt to minimize equity dilution. Management reported that the scarcity of permitted power assets is increasing the value of its current infrastructure portfolio.
