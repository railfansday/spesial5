Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Joe Public, sorry I just read your question.

I retired in a low cost community in Middle Tennessee and had no debts. At the time I was also in the Vanguard Wellington fund plus the Vanguard Value Index Fund. It gave us ample finances plus SS to live on. For years I always worried and emphasized cash flow when teaching entrepreneurship. Thus, I was also bias and conservative in my value equity index fund with dividends received and the Wellington fund. I also had shifted some 403b money to a traditional TIAA that also gave me interest only withdrawals. I did not touch any equity funds during the downturn.

In retrospect my asset location in the brokerage account (Vanguard Wellington fund plus the value index fund) was a mistake since taxes were a problem. I finally sold the Wellington fund a few years later and bit the bullet by paying the taxes. Lastly, I was too conservative by shifting some CREF money to a traditional TIAA fund. I would have been better leaving the money in CREF and then doing a rollover to my Vanguard IRA via the Wellington fund in the qualifying account. In short, the economy and noise did not alter any last minute changes…I ignored that downturn.
