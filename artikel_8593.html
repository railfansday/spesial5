Get the Slate® Credit Card if you need close to two years to pay off a big purchase or a balance moved from another bank’s card. Purchases and transfers cost no interest for 21 months, and the card adds purchase protection and an extended warranty. It earns no rewards, so take a cash back card if you’ll pay in full.

The Slate is Chase’s no-annual-fee card for financing furniture, an appliance, or a big repair. It’s a different card from the Slate Edge, which Chase makes for fair credit.

The Slate is worth getting for a big purchase or a transferred balance that will take more than 15 months to pay off, since it charges no interest for 21 months. For a payoff within 15 months, the Chase Freedom Unlimited is the better card, because it also charges 0% for that long and earns at least 1.5% cash back.
