Gérard Depardieu Rape Case Sent To Trial In Criminal Court After Actor Loses Appeal

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Gérard Depardieu will stand trial in a criminal court on a rape charge related to accusations by actress Charlotte Arnould, after an appeal by his lawyers failed.

The Paris Court of Appeal said on Thursday that Depardieu would be tried for one part of the accusations levelled by Arnould against the actor.

The actress, whose father was an old friend of the actor, accuses Depardieu of raping and sexually assaulting her twice in his Paris mansion in the summer of 2018 after she went to him for acting advice. The court has ruled that Depardieu will be tried for the first alleged rape on August 7, 2018, but not the second alleged rape on August 13, due to insufficient evidence.

The decision to proceed with the trail comes eight months after Depardieu was found guilty of sexual assault and given an 18-month suspended sentence related to events on the set of Jean Becker’s The Green Shutters in 2021. Watch on Deadline

The actor last week announced he was abandoning plans to appeal the sentence and issued a statement saying that he recognized his culpability and apologized for the hurt he had caused.

Just days after the letter was published, investigative newsite Mediapart published details of a fresh sexual assault and rape complaint against the actor related to events on the shoot of Guillaume Nicloux’s Thalasso in 2018.

His 18-month suspended sentence related to assaults on two female crew members on the set of The Green Shutters.

A number of women, who were not pressing charges, also gave evidence at the trial, including an actress who alleged Depardieu had shoved his hands into her knickers during a photo-call on the set of Netflix show Marseille.

Arnould was the first woman to pursue Depardieu in the courts in relation to sexual assault allegations, but her move came against a backdrop of growing rumors around the actor’s sexually inappropriate treatment of female extras and crew members on film sets.

Her complaint was initially dismissed by the courts following a nine-month investigation for lack of evidence, but she filed a second complaint as a civil party, which resulted in an investigating judge being appointed in 2020, and Depardieu being indicted for rape and sexual assault in 2021.

In between times, Depardieu’s public star plummeted following an exposé of his alleged sexual misconduct by state broadcaster channel France 2’s investigative show Complément d’Enquête, entitled “The fall of the ogre”, which aired in December 2023.

The allegations and 2026 guilty verdict have brought his acting career to a grinding halt. Since 2022, he has worked on only three productions, one of them directed by his longtime friend Fanny Ardant.

The criminal court is France’s highest level of court reserved for crimes carrying sentences of more than five years, if the accused is found guilty.
