‘Eternal Bangers’: See Taylor Swift’s Speech Honoring Brooks & Dunn at 2026 CMA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Taylor Swift made her long-awaited return to the CMA Awards stage on Wednesday night, appearing with icons like “Whispering Bill” Anderson, Brad Paisley, and Keith Urban during her performance. But she didn’t stop there: the superstar presented Brooks & Dunn with the CMA Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award.

Swift arrived onstage in a deep blue and green sequined dress, explaining how the award is given out to an artist who has “ascended to incomparable heights in country music,” with “a massive amount of aura points.” Swift said that Kix Brooks and Ronnie Dunn have “provided the world with eternal bangers,” citing hits like “Boot Scootin’ Boogie,” “My Maria,” and ‘Red Dirt Road,” adding that “they still approach their music with the mischief and excitement of little kids just discovering their love for this.”

“That’s the thing about Brooks & Dunn,” she added. “They don’t take very much too seriously, except for all the important stuff: their families, their fans, and country music.”

Earlier in the evening, Swift tore through her Toy Story 5 hit “I Knew It, I Knew You” and “Our Song.” It marked her first performance of the latter in three years, and only her fifth time in 13 years. There’s a long history of musical appreciation between Brooks, Dunn, and Swift. Swift performed onstage at the 2010 ACM Brooks & Dunn The Last Rodeo tribute concert, alongside Reba McEntire, Keith Urban, George Strait, and Faith Hill. She also covered the duo’s radio hit “Ain’t Nothing ‘Bout You.” During Swift’s 2011 Speak Now tour, Dunn was a special guest at her Nashville show, where the two performed Dunn’s solo song “Bleed Red.” In past interviews, the duo has praised Swift’s ability to straddle genres.

The awards ceremony also included a special tribute for Brooks & Dunn featuring Halestorm, Brothers Osborne, Luke Bryan, Hardy, and Marcus King. The all-star band performed a medley of B&D hits that included “Boot Scootin’ Boogie,” “Rock My World (Little Country Girl),” “Play Something Country,” and others.
