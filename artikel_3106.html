Bragg Gaming Group Inc. (BRAG -6.24%) reported a 23% decline in total revenue resulting from the expected roll-off of legacy platform contracts in the Netherlands and regulatory shifts in the Brazilian market. The company appointed Morten Tonnesen as Interim Chief Executive Officer to lead a transition toward a simpler business model focused on proprietary content and disciplined execution. Management stated that recent restructuring programs have reduced the global workforce by 27% to align the cost base with current revenue levels. The acquisition of Drayton International added five game development studios and three proprietary technology platforms to the company's portfolio. Management indicated that future growth will be driven by expanding proprietary content distribution in North America and using artificial intelligence to optimize operational efficiency.
