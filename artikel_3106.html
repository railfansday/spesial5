Spotify suffered from some unexpected problems today, according to a sudden massive spike on Downdetector. Thousands of users started reporting issues with the popular music streaming service at 5:30 am ET / 11:30 am GMT today, November 24.

The majority of the problems appear to be login-related, but the good news is that Spotify was able to implement a fix in a little over an hour.

Today's issues were just a small blip — let us know in the comments box below if you're having problems with Spotify today — and check below to see how things unfolded.

Welcome to our coverage of today's Spotify troubles. This could be an extremely short-lived outage, as Spotify says that any problems that users have experienced this morning should already be fixed...

Everything’s looking much better now. Thanks for your patience. If you still need help, get in touch at https://t.co/jmAt5MtcNJNovember 24, 2026

Around an hour ago, the outage reports hit a substantial peak over over 15,000. As you can see from the graph above, they fell back down almost as sharply. Suggesting that Spotify was able to isolate and solve whatever today's problem was pretty quickly.

Today's issue seems to be mostly a login problem, rather than a playback issue. 70% of those affected say gaining access to the platform is what's gone wrong for them. Are you able to access Spotify? Let us know in the comments.

Reports are dwindling away to very little right now, with plenty of folks posting that today's login problems have been solved for them and they've regained access to their account.

If you're still having problems yourself, you can ask for help over on the Spotify Support page to get the company to give you a steer.

We can put today's outage down as just a technical hiccup from the looks of things, but given the recent swathe of outages across services, you'd be forgiven for thinking it could have been much more serious.

Ok, I think we can definitively say today's Spotify problems are over at this point.
