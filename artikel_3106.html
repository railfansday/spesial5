SAP staffer allegedly gave a startup partner’s demo to its rival

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

SAP did not tell the British startup Conduct after a whistleblower flagged it, Bloomberg reported.

SAP’s headquarters building in Walldorf, Germany, with the company logo on its roof.

An SAP employee allegedly passed confidential material from British AI startup Conduct, an SAP partner, to Berlin rival Qorelo, then joined Qorelo, Bloomberg reported. SAP did not tell Conduct after a whistleblower flagged it in October; SAP says the parties deny the claims.

An SAP employee allegedly passed confidential material from Conduct, a British AI startup that SAP works with and has invested in, to a rival, Bloomberg reported on Wednesday. SAP did not tell Conduct after a whistleblower flagged the matter in October, people familiar with it told Bloomberg.

The employee allegedly gave a Conduct demo and other documents to Berlin-based Qorelo, which also partners with SAP. After the complaint, SAP banned him from its offices. He joined Qorelo in November, according to Bloomberg.

The whistleblower report claims he added about 800 contacts to Qorelo’s customer database while still at SAP. It says he also shared a recording of a call between SAP, Conduct and their customer DHL.

Conduct learned of the matter this month from a whistleblower, and its lawyers then contacted SAP.

“In this case, we contacted the relevant parties, and they have denied the allegations,” SAP said in a statement.

Qorelo said it takes the claims very seriously and is reviewing the employee’s move from SAP. Conduct declined to comment, and the former employee did not respond.

Conduct was founded in 2024 by three former Palantir staff. It raised $60M this year with SAP among the investors, and helps companies move off older SAP systems, a market Qorelo also serves.

The claims could strain the startup ties SAP is using to rebuild around AI. The company has also frozen most hiring to free up cash for that push.
