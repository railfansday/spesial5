Three xAI co-founders launch Epochal to teach AI to predict the future

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Their first product lets AI agents browse a trillion-page archive of the web, frozen at any moment since the 1990s.

Guodong Zhang, Zihang Dai and Manuel Kroiss, three of xAI’s co-founders, have launched Epochal, a startup building AI that forecasts events. Zhang has put in $100M of his own money, Bloomberg reported. Its first product, Time Portal, lets agents browse the web as it was at past dates, so they can practise forecasting without seeing the outcome.

Three of xAI’s co-founders have launched Epochal, a startup that wants to teach AI to forecast events. Guodong Zhang, Zihang Dai and Manuel Kroiss announced the company and its first product on Tuesday.

Zhang has put in $100M of his own money, Bloomberg reported. No other investors have been named.

All of xAI’s co-founders have since left the company, as Elon Musk rebuilds it after its merger with SpaceX. Another co-founder, Igor Babuschkin, raised $1.1bn for his own startup, River AI, in August.

Today’s AI models learn from an internet where they can already see how things turned out, Epochal says. People, by contrast, have to make choices before they know the result.

Its first product, Time Portal, lets AI agents browse the web as it looked at any moment over the past 30 years. Epochal says it covers more than 1 trillion pages, down to the minute on busy sites. A matching Linux sandbox sets its clock, software and downloads to the same date, so agents cannot spot the trick.

To test it, Epochal ran forecasting agents on 347 live questions from prediction markets Kalshi and Polymarket. Weeks later, it replayed the same questions through Time Portal. In its own test, the replayed forecasts closely matched the originals, with a correlation of 0.98.

Developers can try a limited version for free. Epochal has not said how it gathered the archive or whether the pages are licensed.
