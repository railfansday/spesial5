Paradium AI (PAAI) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Referral losses drove the revenue drop as the company pivots toward AI tools.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Paradium AI, Inc. (PAAI -0.66%) reported a 31.2% year-over-year revenue decline, which management attributed to a volatile distribution environment and reduced referral traffic from platforms such as Google Discover. The company stated it is transitioning from a traditional publishing model to a diversified technology platform focused on AI-powered content creation and SaaS-based revenue streams. Management indicated that this shift is intended to reduce reliance on third-party traffic sources while improving operational efficiency through proprietary tools. During the quarter, the company implemented monetization experiments and expanded its AI capabilities through the acquisition of InfoSentience and the launch of Cutter Studios.
