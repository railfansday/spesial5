Prediction: Brentford vs Everton

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Two camps who will mark their respective returns from November’s international break on Monday night, Brentford will welcome Everton to west London.

With a Monday schedule from the likes of Serie A, La Liga and the Premier League, there are plenty of attractive betting promotions available.

Ahead of Monday’s showdown from the Gtech Community Stadium, you can find all of the key Brentford vs Everton H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, while Brentford might have enjoyed a rugged start to proceedings when opening up the 2026/27 campaign back in August, Keith Andrews’ men are searching for some recent stability. Last seen heading into November’s fortnight interval with a 2-0 loss away at Brighton, the Bees have come tumbling out of the European spots. Despite signing off October with a much-welcomed 2-0 victory at home against Nottingham Forest, that is the only win that Brentford have mustered from their previous six appearances across all competitions. Watching their EFL Cup adventure come to a rollercoaster last month as they fell to a thrilling 3-2 loss against Sunderland, Monday’s hosts will be excited to make a return to west London here. In fact, a 2-1 defeat against Liverpool on October 17th is the only loss that Andrews’ side have suffered in front of their own supporters since back in February.

Shown a first-half red card away at Brighton prior to November’s international break, attacking talisman Igor Thiago will miss out through suspension on Monday night. However, despite dealing with a reported hip issue prior to the fortnight interval, Rico Henry could return to Andrews’ squad in west London.

Featuring for Denmark over the past fortnight, Mathias Jensen could be handed a full recall in the middle of the park. Despite still waiting to open his Premier League account this season, summer recruit and veteran striker Callum Wilson will be hoping for a chance to lead the line.

As for the visitors, opening the weekend fixtures sat within touching distance of the Champions League spots, Everton have certainly cemented their spot as one of England’s ultimate surprise packages. Last seen heading into November’s international break with a 3-1 victory at home against Premier League newcomers Coventry, David Moyes’ men will simply be looking to carry on from where they left off prior to the fortnight interval. Extending on their respective EFL Cup charge last month as they signed off October with another 3-1 win against Newcastle, the Toffees have won each of their previous three consecutive appearances across all competitions. However, despite opening November with a gritty 1-0 win at St. James’ Park, that is the only domestic victory that Everton have clinched outside of Merseyside since the end of February.

Recently returning to first-team training after being handed an internal suspension due to reported issues behind the scenes, Manchester City Jack Grealish could be included in Moyes’ travelling squad. Former Freiburg starlet Merlin Rohl could once again start in a makeshift right-back role.

Bagging yet another Premier League brace against Coventry prior to November’s international break and remarkably already notching 10 goals across all competitions so far this season, the red-hot Thierno Barry will lead Everton’s attack. Completing a permanent switch to Merseyside over the summer, ex-Brentford star Christian Norgaard could feature against his former teammates.

Watching Monday’s opponents play out a lively 2-2 stalemate when they last met for a Premier League showdown back in April, neutrals will be hoping for another frantic affair from west London. Winning each of their final three appearances prior to November’s international break and opening the weekend fixtures sat within touching distance of the Champions League spots, Everton are more than capable of springing some real headaches in the capital. Meeting a Brentford squad who found themselves at the heart of an extended wobble prior to the fortnight interval, we could be treated to an enjoyable battle on Monday night.
