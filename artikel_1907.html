After being benched for Celtic loan star, my on-field response was emphatic

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Lucas Clearie has issued another reminder of his goalscoring potential after hitting a first-half hat-trick for Celtic B.

The 17-year-old was benched for the midweek KDM Evolution Trophy clash with Ross County, with Shim Mheuka preferred through the middle.

But Clearie responded in emphatic fashion just four days later with a first-half hat-trick against Beith Juniors. The striker opened the scoring in the 18th minute before adding another in the 42nd minute. He completed his hat-trick in the 45th minute.

His latest haul takes Clearie to an impressive 23 goals across Lowland League and KDM Evolution Trophy football this season.

Eighteen of those have arrived in the league, while he has also struck five times in four KDM Evolution Trophy outings. It was Clearie’s second hat-trick of the campaign after he previously scored three in a dramatic 4-3 victory over Stranraer.

His prolific form comes as he attempts to force his way into the thoughts of first-team manager Martin O’Neill.

Clearie was among seven Celtic youngsters handed their first professional contracts in the summer of 2025 and has continued to enhance his reputation with the B team.

The teenager’s latest treble also rounded off an impressive week after he was included alongside Celtic teammate Corey Shaw in the Scotland Under-19 squad.

Scotland are due to face Kazakhstan, Kosovo and Belgium during the international window.
