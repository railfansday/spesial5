Sources: Kevin de Bruyne’s most likely destination revealed amid Everton whispers

Kevin De Bruyne is likelier to move to the MLS or the Saudi Pro League than return to the Premier League, sources have told Football Insider.

The 35-year-old has been linked with a move to Everton in recent days, with his contract at Napoli set to expire at the end of the season.

The Italian side have an option to extend it by an additional year but are yet to exercise the option, with the Belgian's future still uncertain.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, De Bruyne is unlikely to make the move to Everton if he does decide to leave.

The legendary midfielder left Manchester City in 2025, having won the Premier League six times and recorded nearly 300 direct goal involvements in an extremely successful 10 years.

De Bruyne struggled with injuries in his debut campaign with Napoli but looks to have returned to his best this season, scoring four goals and providing two assists in his first 15 appearances.

For the likes of Everton and the queue of other clubs who would dream of signing him, it is De Bruyne's wages which would be a sticking point in contract negotiations.

The midfielder reportedly took a pay cut to join Napoli, but he is still on a highly lucrative contract in Italy.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "If De Bruyne was to leave Napoli any time soon, it's maybe more likely that he would go to a different league.

"Maybe the MLS could be something that would be of interest to him, and I would imagine there would be a lot of Saudi Pro League clubs who would love the opportunity to sign the Belgium international as well.

"So as things stand right now, I would say any move back to the Premier League is probably unlikely for De Bruyne."

While De Bruyne's move to Napoli appears to have been fuelled by his competitive spirit, he has previously admitted to being open to a move to the Saudi Pro League.

Speaking in 2024, he said: "At my age you have to be open to everything. You're talking about incredible amounts of money in what may be the end of my career.

"Sometimes you have to think about that. If I play there for two years, I will be able to earn an incredible amount of money. Before that I had to play football for 15 years. I may not even reach that amount yet.

"Then you have to think about what that could mean next. But at the moment I haven't had to think about that yet."
