Generali Stadium: A closer look at AC Milan and Inter’s new 71,500-seat home

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

In the latest bonus podcast episode, Oliver and Ben discuss the renderings of AC Milan’s new stadium, the naming rights deal and potential concerns.

It was on Thursday evening that Milan and Inter unveiled the design of their new stadium, which will be called Generali Stadium. A naming rights agreement was reached with the insurance company and the clubs are expected to earn €13m each per year from it.
