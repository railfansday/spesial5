Hyundai is shaking up its 2027 electric vehicle lineup: Here’s what’s changing

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Hyundai’s US electric vehicle lineup will look a bit different in 2027, including the new IONIQ 6 N, a new range-topping IONIQ 9 trim, and a handful of minor updates to the IONIQ 5.

Hyundai announced its 2027 model-year changes on Wednesday, including the new IONIQ 6 N and a few slight changes to the IONIQ 5 and IONIQ 9.

While Hyundai discontinued the standard IONIQ 6, it added the high-performance IONIQ 6 N for the 2027 lineup.

The IONIQ 6 N is Hyundai’s second performance EV, following the IONIQ 5 N. Powered by a dual-motor all-wheel-drive powertrain, the electric sports car delivers a combined 641 hp and 568 lb-ft of torque.

Hyundai said the IONIQ 6 N can sprint from 0 to 60 mph in 3.2 seconds, beating the IONIQ 5 N at 3.25 seconds.

Like the IONIQ 5 N, it also includes fun added features, like N e-Shift and an N Active Sound+ system that simulates the sounds and sensations of a traditional gas-powered sports car with an 8-speed dual-clutch transmission.

It uses an 84 kWh battery that provides up to 245 miles of pure electric driving range. Thanks to its 800V ultra-fast charging architecture, the IONIQ 6 N can charge from 10% to 80% in about 18 minutes using a 350 kW DC fast charger.

Like Hyundai’s other electric vehicles, it comes standard with a native NACS charging port and a CCS adapter.

The 2027 IONIQ 5 and IONIQ 9 are mostly carryover models with a few minor changes, while the IONIQ 5 N remains the same as the 2026 model year.

Changes to the IONIQ 5 for the 2027 model year include roof rails, which are now available on the SEL trim and above; previously, they were only available on the XRT and Limited trims.

Hyundai HomeLink will be available exclusively on the Limited trim, rather than SEL and above, in the current 2026 model year, while the Cargo Cover will be offered as an Accessory only.

Regarding color options, the Gravity Gold Matte and Sage Silver Matte will no longer be available for the 2027 IONIQ 5.

The 2027 Hyundai IONIQ 5 starts at $35,250, making it one of the most affordable EVs in the US.

That’s for the standard-range IONIQ 5 SE with an EPA-estimated range of up to 245 miles. The extended-range SE RWD model is $2,500 more, starting at $37,750, and offers up to 318 miles of range.

Hyundai’s three-row SUV also received a handful of updates. The 2027 Hyundai IONIQ 9 will be available with a new Calligraphy Black Ink trim, while the Calligraphy Design will no longer be offered.

The 2027 IONIQ 9 Calligraphy and Calligraphy Black Ink trims gain a Virtual Gear Shift and heated third-row seats. All variants also gain a Tailgate Emergency Lamp.

On the one hand... the Ioniq5 is due for an update. On the other... I still absolutely love the way it looks, and notice just about every one I pass on the road.

Hyundai has yet to announce prices for the 2027 model. The base 2026 IONIQ 9 S starts at $58,995 and offers an EPA-estimated driving range of up to 335 miles.

The previous top-spec Performance Calligraphy Design AWD started at $76,490, so you can expect the new Calligraphy Black Ink to be around the same, if not even more.
