Ads of the Week: 12 Campaigns That Caught Our Eye, From Disney to Maybelline

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

It was a week filled with ads featuring big names, from Sabrina Carpenter raising a glass for Johnnie Walker (in a spot directed by Damien Chazelle), to Miley Cyrus reworking a Maybelline classic, to David Schwimmer chasing his dream knitwear for Quince.

Plus, Kendall Jenner very nearly bares all for 818 Tequila and John C. Reilly haunts those constantly online during the holidays for Xfinity.

Elsewhere, Disney’s holiday ad features an invisible horse (or is it?), while Apple’s festive offering is the stuff of dreams. Netflix supplies a cinematic trio of shorts and Santa takes a few big bites for Oreo.

Matt Barker is a contributing writer for ADWEEK. He has previously written for Campaign, Marketing Week, and Condé Nast.
