Loaded Green Bean Casserole

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Green beans deserve to have a friend in the classic casserole, and by friend, we mean bacon. This mashup dish takes its cue from loaded baked potatoes and adds bacon, chives, and SO much cheese (just the right amount) for the perfect upgraded side. Whether you're looking to amp up the classic side at your Thanksgiving dinner or need a fun potluck dish for your Friendsgiving, this loaded-up side is the answer.

Tip: If you've got vegetarian company, feel free to leave the bacon out—it's got plenty of cheese to make up for it. 😏 Just use 4 tablespoons of unsalted butter to make up for the bacon fat.
