Workforce Shifts and Validation Deficits: What the 2026 Data Actually Shows

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Honest technical leadership requires confronting friction alongside progress. Stanford HAI’s 2026 AI Index documents that AI’s workforce disruption is targeted: employment among software developers aged 22–25 has fallen nearly 20% since 2024 while older engineers’ headcount continues to grow, and only 6% of U.S. K–12 teachers report clear AI policies despite 4 in 5 students using AI. In clinical AI, only 5% of 500+ reviewed studies evaluated models on real patient data, highlighting a massive validation gap before clinical deployment.
