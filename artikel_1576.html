Bayern: Harry Kane talks about his change in dimension since the Ballon d’Or

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Crowned Ballon d’Or ahead of Kylian Mbappé after a fierce battle, Harry Kane has undeniably moved into another dimension under the colors of Bayern Munich. Freed from the weight of years without a title and finally installed at the top of world football, the English captain spoke in all sincerity at the microphone of Téléfoot. Between the relief of having won the highest individual distinction and his perfect development in Bavaria, materialized by his recent contract extension, the talkative pyrotechnician played the card of transparency when discussing his transformation.

“I am very happy to extend my contract, as you see I am very fulfilled at this club, it shows in the way I play and score. It’s truly a privilege to feel wanted and I intend to assert myself over time here. My Ballon d’Or? I didn’t really believe it, but the more my career progressed, the more interested I became, winning it is beyond belief. I just thought I was a good player and since arriving in Munich I’ve reached a milestone, I hope to give the best of myself. When I went on stage, I felt a sigh of relief. My heart rate was too high”confided the former Spurs player.
