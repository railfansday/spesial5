Beyond Nen Hunter Exam Guide & How to Get Nen

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Become a Hunter and unlock your Nen abilities!

Before attempting the Hunter Exam to activate your Nen make sure you’ve farmed a good weapon, have some decent skills and are on an appropriate server for good ping since there will be PVP matches. I recommend playing through the Hunter Exam with a friend via voice comms since it makes Stage III drastically easier. Here’s how to complete the Hunter Exam and how to get Nen abilities in Beyond Nen.

Before you can unlock Nen in Beyond Nen you have to complete the Hunter Exam which you can join via the lobby from the Main Menu. This is where you can invite friends if you’re not going solo. The First Stage is The Stamina Race and it isn’t necessarily a race since you don’t have to be first but simply finish the obstacle course by reaching the end in time. Here are some notes:

The second stage of the Hunter Exam is the Trick Tower where the first part is an easy labyrinth with traps and the second is mini-boss fight. In terms of the labyrinth:

The second part is an NPC fight which is fairly straightforward. You just need to practice blocks, parries and dodges as you DPS with for your M1+uppercut combos and skills. Use air attacks to close the distance. Dodge backwards when he’s about to do his fire tornado skill and overall try to zone him with your M1s.

The Zevil Island stage is an open zone PVP match where you need to survive until the end with 2 badges. You have your own and you need to grip a player for one of theirs and just run and play defensively with 2 until the timer runs out (Skateboard or Jump slide for more speed). This is your first real skill-check and if you’re feeling confident with your ping and parries try to isolate a player for a 1v1:

Don’t Grip Players Without a Badge: If you got a badge from a player and they attack you to get their badge back, either run or down them again but don’t grip them. Just run away after downing them since this gives you plenty of time to retreat as they recover. If you grip a badge-less player you don’t get anything and they’ll just instantly recover and attack you again.

Co-op Makes This a Breeze: I highly recommend doing Zevil Island with a friend or two for an easier time via voice comms. It makes it much easier since you can coordinate to find each other and then gank players for an easy badge grip for you and your friend. Then it’s also much easier to go on the defensive with multiple people once all of you have 2 badges.

The last stage is just a simple 1v1 against a random player in the lobby. Make sure you have good ping and follow the 1v1 tips from the Zevil Island section above. Defeating that player will get you your Hunter’s License thus successfully completing the Hunter Exam and becoming a Hunter with a special mark in the top right. This now allows you to get Nen abilities in Beyond Nen by finishing a short quest (more on that below). Note that if you’ve played with a friend up until now they might be matched against you so you can either 1v1 for real or ask to grip them and then help them out in return later.

After finishing the Hunter Exam and becoming a Hunter, you can activate your Nen via Rohan’s quest (The Librarian in Port Town sends you to him) by going and talking to him at Wayfarer Island in the Nen Cave. Simply press P, hide all trackers and then select only the “Active Quest” tracker and follow the “Activate Nen” prompt. Buy a Meditation Mat from the Merchant in Port Town before you go since you’ll need it for the quest. Once you reach Rohan, talk to him and follow the dialogue to activate your Nen.
