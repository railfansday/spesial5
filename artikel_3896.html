Herdling – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition now available

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Publisher Panic and developer Okomotive have released Herdling – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition. It is a free upgrade from the Switch version.

Herdling first launched for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC via Steam and Epic Games Store on August 21, 2025.

Here is an overview of the game, via Panic:

Herdling – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition features higher resolution and frame-rate improvements, and is a free upgrade to owners of the original Switch version of the game. Players who have not set out on this adventure can pick up the original Switch 1 game at a limited time 50 percent-off discount and then receive the free Switch 2 upgrade. Traverse through awe-inspiring scenery as you strengthen your bond with your pack of Calicorns, solving light environmental puzzles on your way to the mountain’s peak.

Also available on Nintendo Switch, Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam and the Epic Games Store, Herdling follows a young girl named Via who shepherds mysterious animals called Calicorns through a dangerous mountain path, encountering surprising threats and environmental challenges along the way. Forge an unbreakable bond with your pack in this emotional tale as you ascend the mountain and attempt to keep your herd intact.
