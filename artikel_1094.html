Drone operator deletes footage of Mauricio Pochettino’s first Man Utd training session and issues apology

A drone operator ​who filmed Manchester United's training session this week has apologised for what he called a "total error of judgment" and removed the footage from ⁠his YouTube channel.

The footage, filmed at United's Carrington training ground during new manager Mauricio Pochettino's first training session on Thursday, was uploaded online under the username "CC OVERVIEW".

In a statement ⁠posted on his YouTube channel ​on ⁠Friday, the drone operator said that while the flight was legal under drone regulations, filming ⁠the session had been "unethical".

“My aim was to picture Mauricio Pochettino in his United training kit for the supporters to see for the first time,” he wrote.

“I had wrongly presumed that with it being close to the start of the session that no meaningful training sessions would have started so would have been some harmless warming up shown.”

The operator added that he had wrongly assumed that the training session would be open to various media outlets. Pochettino’s first session was closed to the press.

“I would like to apologise to Manchester United, Mauricio Pochettino and the players for my actions and sincerely hope those actions do not affect the outcome of any match,” he said.

"I would also like to state that Liverpool Football Club or any other football club were not involved in my decision to film at Carrington yesterday. The video has been removed from my channel.

“I have spent the past four years building up my YouTube channel and were well regarded in the fields I film and would now like to get back to building up my damaged reputation and making well received responsible videos.”

The footage has nevertheless been shared across social media and garnered millions of views. United have not commented on the matter.

United have a strict no drones policy and have signs around Carrington warning that anyone caught using a drone "will be reported for prosecution and subject to civil action".

Pochettino arrived from ‌the US men's national team ⁠this week after United sacked Michael Carrick, ⁠and faces an immediate test with a trip to Liverpool on ‌Sunday.

United are languishing 18th in the league table after winning just one of their 10 games, while Liverpool sit fourth.
