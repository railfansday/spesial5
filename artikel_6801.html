Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Sunscreen is always important, but when one product can also brighten your complexion and take the place of your favorite concealer, it becomes impossible to forget. Tinted SPF offers the best of both worlds, helping protect your skin from the sun while giving it the appearance of a more even complexion. And when it’s made specifically for the eye area, it can make one of the most delicate parts of your skin care routine feel a whole lot easier.

“Tinted sunscreens are one of those rare beauty products where the cosmetic benefit and the dermatological benefit really align,” says Dr. Pearl Ugwu-Dike, a board-certified dermatologist. “You’re protecting your skin from tomorrow’s sun damage while making it look good today.” As for why they’re especially useful under the eyes, the skin in that area is some of the most delicate that we have on our body. “That makes it particularly susceptible to visible signs of photoaging, like fine lines, crepiness, hyperpigmentation,” Dr. Ugwu-Dike says. “It’s also often a place that gets neglected when applying sunscreen, conventionally.” That’s why the best tinted SPFs for under-eyes are so special: They offer protection while providing immediate color correction and a brighter-looking appearance, helping to camouflage under-eye bags and dark circles.

Of course, the right formula depends on how much coverage you want, your skin type, and how your under-eye area tends to behave throughout the day. Some options are sheer and brightening, while others can pull double duty as a true concealer. Ahead, we’ve rounded up the best tinted SPFs for under-eyes so you can find the one that fits seamlessly into your morning routine.
