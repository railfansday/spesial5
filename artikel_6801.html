Sweatpant Jeans Are the Most Controversial (and Comfortable) Denim Trend

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Level up your casual looks with these cozy and stylish picks.

We all remember the jeggings era—it was a traumatic one. But believe it or not, the same concept has come back around in a fresher, more stylish manner. Typically cut from cotton terry cloth or a lightweight Tencel blend, “sweatpant jeans” combine the appearance of real pants with the comfort of loungewear for a result that you really have to see to believe. Plus, most of our favorite styles are shoppable on Amazon.

Don’t let the jeggings of the early 2010s deter you; today’s versions look far more realistic. Take Rag & Bone’s viral Miramar silhouette, which kicked off the trend, as proof. With a faux zip fly, button closure, and rivets, it’ll fool even the most discerning eye—even Oprah loves them. They’re ELLE editor-approved, too. Associate fashion commerce editor Meg Donohue loves the drawstring iteration, seen below.

To help you navigate the category, we’ve rounded up nine of the best sweatpant jeans below. From long travel commutes to casual Fridays, these comfortable bottoms can truly handle it all.

Rag & Bone has made variations of its viral Miramar sweatpant jeans for years, and they remain the gold standard to this day. Printed to look like a pair of faded medium-wash jeans, this wide-leg style feels just right for the current season.

Prefer a slightly more polished style? Made with DL1961’s latest fabric innovation, a lightweight cotton-tencel blend, these easy-going baggy jeans are the perfect solution.

This striped version of Rag & Bone’s viral sweatpant jeans was recently featured in Oprah’s Favorite Things. Should you be seeking a cozy gift for someone stylish, this is a great pick.

Drawing inspiration from denim washes, these Z Supply pants offer an elevated take on traditional sweats. Snag the coordinating button-up overshirt (cut from the same plush French terry material) for the ultimate cozy travel outfit.

Cut from cotton-lyocell chambray, these drapey Favorite Daughter pants work particularly well in warmer weather. The elastic waistband and wide-leg silhouette make them comfortable enough for long commutes, too.

Gap’s French terry baggy jeans are another viral favorite, so naturally, we had to try them ourselves. “Paired with a biker jacket, leather flats, and a studded belt, these pants looked suspiciously like regular ol’ blue jeans—all while providing the soft, stretchy comfort of sweatpants,” commerce editor Lauren Gruber wrote in her review. “It’s a solid, affordable alternative that offers the illusion of denim with the right styling.”

It’s hard to believe these Faherty pants are made from stretchy cotton terry fabric and not traditional denim—unless you’re the one wearing them, of course. The patch pockets and zip fly are quite deceptive.

For sweatpants jeans that are elevated enough for the office or dinner, this new style from Frame won’t miss. A particularly refined take on the balloon pant trend, they feature an airy yet slim silhouette and flattering high waist that make them easy to style with everything from a silk cami to a crisp-button and blazer.

Darting at the knees and a slightly tapered hem give this relaxed-fit style a subtle barrel-leg silhouette. Grommet details at the waistband and a deep stone wash only add to its edgy, laidback appeal.
