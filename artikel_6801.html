We Tested Luxury Sheet Sets for Three Years—These Are the Only Ones Worth the Splurge

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Every item on this page was hand-picked by a House Beautiful editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

For hot sleepers, restless sleepers, and anyone tired of scratchy bedding.

You deserve better than itchy, scratchy sheets on your bed at night. Upgrading your bedding is more than a bedroom aesthetic choice; it’s an investment in your well-being. I know this firsthand—as both a design editor and an insomniac. I’ve tested over a dozen of the best luxury sheets on the market in my journey to fix my sleep routine. The right sheet set lets me drift off much more easily, and I’m here to help you find one of your own.

What sets “luxury” sheets apart from your run-of-the-mill bargain bedding? It's a combination of texture, quality, aesthetic, and price point. The set should feel good the moment it hits your skin. The fabric, too, should be durable and constructed to avoid pilling, tearing, or any of that less-than-best feel. As the name implies, luxury sheets often come with a higher price tag, so you'll want to make sure they're worth the extra money.

I have every sleep issue you could think of: I'm a hot sleeper, I snore, I toss and turn, and I have eczema, so I have to be picky about what fabric goes in my bed. Luckily, my fellow editors, testers, and I have spent over three years sleeping on every kind of sheet under the sun, from fluffy flannel to silky eucalyptus. And now we’re revealing the ones that totally upgraded our sleep routines. Scroll on to meet our MVBs (most valuable bedding).

I first tested the Buffy Breeze sheets in my evaluation, and beyond the beautiful sheen that made the sheets look luxe, the cooling feeling of the eucalyptus fibers completely won me over. After months of trying other sheets, these were still my favorite.

I'm an incredibly hot sleeper, and I can't stand waking up to sticky sheets. These sheets from Buffy, made from moisture-wicking eucalyptus, gave me the most refreshing sleep I've had in my life. The first quality that impressed me about these sheets was how well they shifted with my constant tossing and turning. Even as they gathered underneath me, I was never uncomfortable. They're lightweight and breathable without the gauzy texture of other airy sheets. I genuinely looked forward to sinking into them at the end of the day.

I also tested Buffy's Cloud pillows under the pillowcases in this set and was impressed with how well they held their shape, even as I bunched them up under my neck. I was convinced I’d have horrible neck pain any time I slept on a pillow that wasn’t the butterfly shape I have for side sleepers. Imagine how shocked I was by how comfortable and long I slept.
