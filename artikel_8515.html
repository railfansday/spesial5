Personal Investments • Re: Can I retire now, and how can I simplify portfolio?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Just wanted to chime in as someone who took a career break for caretaking. You’re in a much more comfortable position than I was and with the numbers, you should be okay, especially after you start collecting Social Security. There’s no need to rush, but most on this site will recommend consolidating the smaller holdings in the tax-sheltered accounts into a total stock market or total bond market fund. If you’re not too fussed on international stock allocation, you can go with VT, which is balanced to global market caps and doesn’t require much analysis from you. There’s some tax efficiency arguments to go another way, but if you start with the IRAs/401ks there’s no real reason you can’t change your mind later.

You may actually be in a uniquely good position to consider Roth conversions, as your HoH filing status means you have a fairly low rate for now. If nothing else, take some time to consolidate all of your various IRAs in a couple of firms to simplify your life.

Hopefully you’re already aware, but you should be deducting any medical expenses above 7.5% of your AGI for you and your dependents. It can help defray some of the rising costs that will come with coming off employer-sponsored healthcare.
