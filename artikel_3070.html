Breakfast News: Did Nvidia Just Favor Marvell?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Nvidia’s likely role in the Marvell-Lumentum deal, AI stocks hit by midterms, and more…

In June, Nvidia (NVDA +2.29%) CEO Jensen Huang interrupted Marvell’s keynote to call Marvell Technology (MRVL +0.33%) the next trillion-dollar company. Five months later, on Monday, Marvell agreed to buy Lumentum (LITE -0.62%) in an all-stock deal valued at $115 billion. All three companies are Fool recommendations, and we plan to hold the combined Marvell and Lumentum indefinitely.

Both companies play a key role in a change inside artificial intelligence (AI) data centers, where more data now moves between chips as light instead of electricity. Every light-based connection needs a laser, and Lumentum is one of the few companies that can make them at scale. Marvell already designs the chips that work alongside those lasers, so this deal gives Marvell a laser supply of its own.

We believe Nvidia “helped” bring these two companies together, because a stronger Marvell gives Nvidia a heavyweight ally against Broadcom (AVGO +3.07%).

The companies’ upcoming merger filing will list every meeting that led to this deal, including any involving Nvidia. Until then, we’re holding both stocks and plan to own the combined company for years.

In a single 24-hour window on Wednesday, three distinct forces – corporate security, global diplomacy, and electoral politics – converged to prove that the biggest threat to AI growth isn't model performance, but friction.

The AI game is changing. Revenue won't flow to who builds the biggest model. It will flow to who builds the strongest governance, runtime security, and infrastructure to keep it running safely.

Team Hidden Gems sees EMCOR Group (EME +3.61%) reaching $1,523.33 within five years, more than double today’s price of about $669. Our first Team Hidden Gems rec in November 2024 is already up nearly 50%. EMCOR wires and cools hospitals, factories, and data centers, and it wins by bringing a Hidden Gems favorite, technical superiority, to an industry built on hard hats and blueprints.

At about 21 times trailing earnings, EMCOR’s tech edge comes at a fair price, and we expect it to carry the stock toward our target.
