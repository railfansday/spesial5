Universal Corp (UVV) Q2 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Delayed tobacco shipments and Ingredients cost pressure weighed on quarterly profit.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Universal Corp. (UVV -4.05%) reported a decrease in second-quarter financial results, citing a delayed tobacco market cycle that pushed shipment volumes into the second half of the fiscal year. Management indicated that customer commitments remain aligned with full-year sales plans and that uncommitted inventory levels are within the target range. The Ingredients Operations segment recorded an operating loss during the period due to supply constraints in the apple market and higher raw material costs. The company is implementing a reorganization of its Ingredients business to improve operational efficiency and facility utilization. Management stated that emerging customer interest in additional tobacco volumes is being driven by concerns regarding potential weather impacts from El Niño.
