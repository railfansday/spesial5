Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Record ACH volume and a new Harris partnership drove margin expansion this quarter.

Usio, Inc. (USIO -5.21%) reported its third consecutive quarter of GAAP profitability and a record year-to-date adjusted EBITDA performance. Management stated that revenue increased across all four business lines for the first time in 2026, with consolidated gross margins expanding 110 basis points to 24.1%. The company attributed this performance to significant operating leverage, noting that selling, general, and administrative expenses grew only 2% compared to a 12% increase in top-line revenue. Executive leadership highlighted the commercial launch of Usio Ion and a new strategic partnership with Harris Computer as primary catalysts for growth expected to contribute to 2027 results.
