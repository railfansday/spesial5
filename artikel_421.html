The best early Black Friday MacBook deals: The Neo and OLED MacBook Pro are both on sale already

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Shopping for a MacBook this Black Friday? No need to wait until Nov. 27: Retailers like Amazon and Best Buy have already started their holiday sales, ushering in plenty of early deals on Apple's entire laptop lineup. Even the MacBook Neo is on sale right now (and it never goes on sale).

Fair warning: This year's Black Friday MacBook deals may not look as good as usual because Apple raised its prices over this summer. However, most of the discounts we're already seeing match the best offers we encountered over Black Friday 2025.

Just to be sure you're getting the best MacBook deals possible this Black Friday, we've also cross-checked listings across various retailers to find the lowest prices on every model. Read on for our top picks so far.

The Mashable Choice Award-winning MacBook Neo is our favorite budget laptop on the market. It has the same premium build quality as Apple's other MacBooks, and its A18 Pro processor — a pared-down iPhone chip, wouldn't you know — makes it much faster than competing Windows PCs. "For students, young people, and casual users, there's nothing else like it," said Mashable Tech Editor Timothy Beck Werth, who rated it a 4.7/5. In the lead-up to Black Friday, it finally got its first real discount.

We think the 15-inch model is the better buy for most people, but if you frequently work on the go (or don't need as much screen real estate), the smaller 13-inch model might be ideal.
