Thanksgiving Day: When Walmart, Target, Best Buy, and more stores open for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

TL;DR: On Thanksgiving Day (Nov. 26), Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Costco, and most major retailers are closed all day, while select grocers (like Kroger, Wegmans, and Whole Foods) and drugstores (Walgreens and CVS) stay open with modified hours.

Whether you realized halfway through prepping dinner that you forgot the cranberry sauce or need an emergency prescription refill, finding an open store on Thanksgiving Day is trickier than it used to be. For the seventh straight year, almost every major big-box, warehouse, and department store — including Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Costco, Lowe's, and Kohl's — is keeping its doors locked all day on Thursday, Nov. 26.

That does not mean you are completely out of luck, though. A handful of grocery chains, drugstores, dollar stores, and outdoor retailers will stay open on Thanksgiving with modified holiday hours (mostly closing by mid-afternoon, often with their pharmacy counters shut).

We rounded up which stores are open and closed on Thanksgiving 2026 below so you don't waste a trip to an empty parking lot and can plan your Thursday errands accordingly.

If you need last-minute ingredients on Thursday morning, several major supermarket chains are staying open with shortened hours. Keep in mind that most in-store grocery pharmacies are closed on Thanksgiving Day, and exact opening and closing times vary by store — so check your local store locator before leaving the house.

Harris Teeter: Stores open at 6 a.m. and close early at 2 p.m. local time (pharmacies closed).

H-E-B: Open from 6 a.m. to noon local time (pharmacies closed; curbside pickup runs 7 a.m. to 11 a.m. and delivery runs 7 a.m. to 10 a.m.).

Kroger (and banner stores): Most Kroger, Fred Meyer, Ralphs, King Soopers, Mariano's, Smith's, and QFC stores are open with modified hours — typically closing at 4 p.m. or 5 p.m. local time (pharmacies and clinics closed).

Meijer: Open from 6 a.m. to 5 p.m. local time.

Safeway: Most locations are open with adjusted holiday hours, typically closing at 4 p.m. local time (pharmacies closed).

ShopRite: Select stores are open with modified hours that vary by location.

Sprouts Farmers Market: Open from 7 a.m. to 2 p.m. local time.

Wegmans: Most stores close early at 4 p.m. local time (pharmacies closed).

Whole Foods Market: Most stores are open with modified hours — typically 7 a.m. to 1 p.m. local time — though locations in Massachusetts, Maine, and Rhode Island are closed all day due to state blue laws.

Need pain relievers, batteries, foil roasting pans, or a quick coffee? These national pharmacy, discount, and specialty chains are keeping their doors open on Thursday, Nov. 26:

Bass Pro Shops: Open from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. local time.

Big Lots: Open from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. local time.

Cabela's: Open from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. local time.

CVS: Most locations are open with regular or reduced holiday hours (pharmacy hours vary, so check your local store before heading out).

Dollar General: Most locations are open with adjusted holiday hours (hours vary by store).

Dollar Tree: Open from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. local time.

Family Dollar: Most locations are open from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. local time — though stores in Maine, Massachusetts, and Rhode Island are closed.

Starbucks: Most standalone cafes are open with modified holiday hours (locations inside closed stores like Target will also be closed).

Walgreens: Nearly all 8,000 stores nationwide are open on Thanksgiving Day — with regular hours at 24-hour locations and 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. at most others.

Don't bother driving to any of these big-box, warehouse, department, home improvement, or specialty chains on Thursday, Nov. 26 — all physical locations (along with curbside pickup and in-store services) are closed all day.
