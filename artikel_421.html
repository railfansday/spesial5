Black Friday is the best time of year to buy noise-cancelling headphones: Shop our favorite 27 deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Whether you're a frequent flyer, a commuter, a remote worker, or just someone who often needs to turn down the volume of the outside world, noise-cancelling headphones are a must. And Black Friday week is the best time of year to pick up a pair.

What do we mean by the best? We mean that every flagship pair of headphones from a major brand is at or near a record-low price. That's the Sony XM6 headphones, the Bose QuietComfort Ultra 2 headphones, the AirPods Max 2.

That's not to mention all the great deals on headphones under $300, or budget favorites that are even more affordable. While there may be some more price dips on the actual day of Black Friday on Nov. 27, all of the ANC headphones deals we've included on the list below are some of the best deals we've seen this year.
