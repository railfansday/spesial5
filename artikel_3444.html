Chris Pratt's New Karate Kid-esque Movie Yields Stellar Rotten Tomatoes Audience Score

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Chris Pratt's new movie, Way of the Warrior Kid, is streaming and gaining traction.

In what has been a busy 2026 for the Marvel actor, he stars in his final movie with Apple TV's new drama, playing a Navy SEAL uncle who trains his middle-school nephew to become a warrior of his own. The story is based on Jocko Willink's 2017 children's novel, which became a five-part bestseller. Ahead of the streaming release, reviews of Way of the Warrior Kid were mixed to negative, earning a low 22% on Rotten Tomatoes. However, with the movie now available on Apple TV, the audience reception has been different.

With over 100 ratings, Way of the Warrior Kid landed a solid 84% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes, a 62-point difference from the critical score. As a result, Pratt ended the year with all three of his 2026 movies dividing critics and audiences. In fact, viewers were more favorable to his movies, as in the case of The Super Mario Galaxy Movie (42% vs 87%) and Mercy (24% vs 81%).

Reviewers criticized the adaptation's preachy tone, absurd third act, and inconsistent pacing. Some felt Pratt's role overshadowed Jude Hill's presence, and the movie failed to balance its cheesy moments with its emotional arc. Others asserted that the story read more like military propaganda than a true coming-of-age story and tried to be a Karate Kid knockoff. However, viewers responded differently, with many finding it a good, inspirational family story that emphasizes discipline and hard work.

Nevertheless, Pratt's new movie was expected to resonate with audiences. It's a pattern in his movies lately, dating back to 2024's The Garfield Movie, which also divided critics and viewers. That trend continued into Netflix's The Electric State and his recent 2026 movies.

Pratt has been a household name since appearing in Parks and Rec, and his career ballooned after he was cast as Peter Quill in Guardians of the Galaxy. Since then, he has starred in major franchises like Jurassic World and The Lego Movie and has helped expand the Terminal List universe with Amazon.

Meanwhile, he has a few projects in the works, including The Traveler and the espionage series Savage Son. He's also expected to reprise Star-Lord sometime in the future, although Marvel Studios has not announced his next project and he has yet to be confirmed for the next two Avengers movies.

McG directed Way of the Warrior Kid, while Will Staples penned the script. Willink and Pratt are producers, while Linda Cardellini, Levi McConaughy, Ava Torres, and Darien Sills-Evans round out the supporting cast.

Way of the Warrior Kid is now streaming on Apple TV.
