Prime Video Officially Has a New Sci-Fi Hit Thanks to Michael Fassbender

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The early 2010s were a great time to invest in Michael Fassbender stock, as he was soon to be nominated for two Oscars in three years. In 2014, Fassbender scored his first Academy Award nomination for his supporting performance in 12 Years a Slave, the powerful biopic directed by Steve McQueen. He followed it with a Best Lead Actor nod for his role in the Steve Jobs biopic, and while he didn’t win for either role, he will almost certainly be back on the stage at one point, at least of his talent is any indication. Fassbender is also known for his blockbuster role as David in Ridley Scott’s Alien franchise, who he played in both Prometheus and Alien: Covenant. He is also reportedly set to return for a new Alien movie that will likely be released sometime in the next 4–5 years.

Michael Fassbender is also widely known and loved for his role as a young Magneto in the Fox X-Men movies, and while there is speculation he’ll return in Avengers: Doomsday, this has yet to be confirmed. It’s been a somewhat quiet few years for Fassbender, but earlier this year, he returned to the screen for a new sci-fi movie that’s finally getting its due on streaming. We’re talking, of course, about Hope, the well-liked alien invasion movie written and directed by Na Hong-jin. The movie hit theaters in the second week of September, and after just over a month in theaters, it managed to scrounge together nearly $50 million at the global box office. The movie has since been added to VOD platforms like Prime Video, where it’s surged into the top 10 in several countries.

In addition to Michael Fassbender, Hope also stars Hwang Jung-min as Beom Seok, Zo In-sung as Sung Ki, Hoyeon as Sung Ae, Taylor Russell as Ai’dovor, Cameron Britton as Va’migere, and Alicia Vikander as J’aur. Fans may recognize Britton from his role as Gary Jones in the second season of Paradise on Hulu, and he also starred as Donegal in the first and only season of Spider-Noir on Prime Video. Hope follows a police chief, police woman, and a group of hunters who try to track down and kill a mysterious creature that’s laying waste to a Korean village.

Check out Hope on VOD platforms like Prime Video and Apple TV and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of the film.

Hope is a 2026 film set in the remote harbor town of Hope Harbor, near the Demilitarized Zone. The story unfolds as a mysterious discovery in the area intensifies into a larger enigma, compelling the town's inhabitants to face an ominous and unfathomable threat.
