Target Officially Reveals Big Pokémon TCG Deals On Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Target expected to offer a wide range of Pokémon 30th Anniversary products in November

November is here, and retailers are finally revealing how they'll handle the year's biggest shopping event: Black Friday. Target has offered Black Friday deals on the Pokémon trading card game in previous years, and 2026 looks to be no different.

The retailer's BF deals initially leaked in late October, but the retailer has since begun putting out fliers advertising the incoming Pokémon promos. Target also just broke the news that some stores will have their stock delayed for after Black Friday. Here's what you need to know.

The news is sourced from Target's own weekly ad sneak peek, which you can peruse here. Target locations are expected to receive shipments of the following products starting on Nov. 15:

According to leaked images, these products will be stocked on new shelf displays that advertise Pokémon's Electronic Dance Music event, which still has a couple of dates left on the calendar.

Initial reports from those leaks indicated that the 30th Anniversary TCG products won't be put up for sale until Nov. 27, or Black Friday proper. However, the weekly ad as well as signage at individual Target stores suggest these TCG products will go on sale on Nov. 28, the day after Black Friday. Limits for these products will vary by store, if they're enforced at all. Targets that do observe purchasing limits typically only allow one product type per customer for 30th Anniversary products. Other products, like Delta Reign, come with more lenient limits of two per person.

It's worth mentioning that none of these products are technically cheaper than their normal price tags. That's in contrast to last year, when Target offered two Pokémon TCG exclusive bundles for a decent price. Though Target charges a markup for its Pokémon TCG products, its prices are cheaper than some competitors and not more prone to price fluctuations than retailers like GameStop.

It's still an (apparent) guaranteed drop date for a highly in-demand product that Target locations are struggling to keep on shelves. By that fact alone, the rumored Pokémon product display will motivate people to visit the store — and perhaps, while they're there, entice them to check out other Black Friday offerings.
