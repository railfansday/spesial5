Taylor Sheridan’s ‘Yellowstone’ Spin-Off Officially Returns After Surprise Break

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

When it was reported that Yellowstone was ending, no one knew what the future would look like for what was once the biggest show on television. There were internal wrangles, and after five years, it seemed like some cast and crew members were ready for something different. So it came as a bit of a surprise when CBS and Paramount announced a new spin-off, but it wouldn't be like anything viewers had seen before. The new series would take a procedural approach and would follow one of the least explored characters in the flagship series, Kayce Dutton (Luke Grimes).

The problem was that Grimes wasn't interested in playing Kayce after Yellowstone, and he had been vocal about it. "I didn’t think I wanted to come back to it, because I felt like Kayce’s story ended so perfectly in the first show," the actor admitted in an interview with THR. "And I was like, well, what are we gonna do now? He’s got his dream life. Everything he’s been fighting for, he’s gotten. He literally rode off into the sunset. And what sort of breaks that world open for him is that that dream life became no longer possible." With a bit of convincing from showrunner Spencer Hudnut and support from Taylor Sheridan, Grimes put the cowboy hat back on, and Marshals arrived earlier this year, becoming a ratings juggernaut for CBS.

Season 2 airs this fall, but the network announced the show would take a two-week break, meaning no new episode on Sunday, November 8. However, Marshals is back on schedule this Sunday, November 15, with an episode that could become a turning point for some character dynamics. In Marshals Season 2, Episode 5, "Roughstock," Cal (Logan Marshall-Green) goes to Salt Lake to see a cancer specialist, and Belle (Arielle Kebbel) tags along without anyone else knowing. Is this the beginning of a new romance, and what effect will it have on the team? Meanwhile, Andrea (Ash Santos) temporarily takes the reins in Cal's absence, and problems arise when Kayce steps outside the marshal's jurisdiction to help his friend Mo (Mo Brings Plenty) after Mo's nephew goes missing.
