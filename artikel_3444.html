Jennifer Lawrence’s Most Candid Comments About Getting Plastic Surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Jennifer Lawrence is done playing coy about cosmetic work.

The actress has gotten candid about what she has done, what plastic surgery procedures she wants to do and what she swears she hasn’t done — from new breast implants to a future facelift. Keep scrolling for Lawrence’s honest quotes about going under the knife:

Lawrence revealed on a November 2026 episode of Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen that she recently got a breast augmentation.

During a “hot takes” segment about Bravo stars, Cohen asked whether it was really a mystery that a breast implant could break Real Housewives of New York City star Hailey Glassman‘s nose.

“Yes, and I have implants,” Lawrence responded, gesturing toward her chest. “Yeah, they’re new.”

When Cohen asked what size she had gone with, Lawrence replied, “It’s a full C. I think? It’s the same size that they were” in a nod to her pre-baby cup size. (Lawrence and husband Cooke Maroney share two sons.)

In a Vogue interview published in September 2026, Lawrence walked readers through her current cosmetic routine and explained why she keeps Botox to a minimum.

“I need to have movement in my face. I get baby Botox, but I can’t do a lot,” she told the outlet. “If my skin is going to be a little saggier than I would like, at least it looks dewy and pretty.”

Lawrence further noted that she is curious about fat transfers, a procedure that restores volume and softens wrinkles, but isn’t ready to try them herself.

“I’m really nervous about these fat transfers,” she admitted. “I support other people doing them because I want to continue to watch and see what happens, but I don’t know about that.”

Buccal fat removal, the trendy procedure that slims the lower cheeks, is another story, telling the outlet, “I would love to have a buccal fat removal. I can’t because everybody would know, but just know that I want that.”

In her October 2026 cover story for The Cut, Lawrence laid out a new personal philosophy for the school-drop-off set.

“I want to be a sexy mom,” the Die My Love star said. “At drop-off, I want to look like a woman that has her s*** together even when I’ve just woken up and don’t have my s*** together.”

In the same interview, Lawrence confirmed that getting a facelift is somewhere in her future, though she didn’t attach a timeline to it.

Not every change to Lawrence’s face is cosmetic, she insists. After her photo shoot for The Cut and a recent red carpet appearance for the New York City premiere of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, chatter picked up online that she had gotten a blepharoplasty or eyelid lift.

Lawrence pushed back by reposting a photo from her makeup artist Shayna Goldberg on Instagram Stories with the caption, “Iykyk she’s the one who did my bleph.”

She made a similar case about her eyes — and her nose — in a 2023 Interview magazine conversation with Kylie Jenner, that time crediting makeup artist Hung Vanngo.

“I also think it’s incredible what makeup can do because I work with Hung, who overlines the lip, and I call him a plastic surgeon, because everybody in the last few months since I’ve been working with him is convinced that I had eye surgery,” Lawrence said at the time. “I’m like, ‘I didn’t have eye surgery. I’m doing makeup.'”

As for the long-running nose job theory, Lawrence told Jenner, “I started at 19, so I get the before and after pictures from when I’m 19 to 30, and I’m like, ‘I grew up, I lost baby weight in my face, and my face changed because I’m aging.’ Everybody thought I had a nose job, and I’m like, ‘I’ve had the exact same nose. My cheeks got smaller. Thank you for bringing it up.'”
