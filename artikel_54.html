French ex-PM Philippe pledges to slash spending, hike retirement age as Elysée bid falters

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Former prime minister Édouard Philippe pledged on Wednesday to slash public spending, scrap France’s statutory 35-hour workweek and raise the retirement age to 65 from the current 62.9 years as he unveiled a slew of measures aimed a staving off economic "catastrophe" for France. An early frontrunner in next year's presidential election, Philippe is now trailing rivals in opinion polls and at risk of missing out on the second-round runoff.
