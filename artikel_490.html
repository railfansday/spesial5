Trump’s Asia Trip Leaves Key Issues Unresolved

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

ABC News State Department reporter Shannon Kingston tells Bloomberg This Weekend that Russia’s increasingly provocative actions near NATO countries are complicating President Donald Trump’s push for a negotiated end to the war in Ukraine, while Moscow remains publicly defiant. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Kingston says Trump’s Asia trip produced few concrete results and puts greater pressure on the upcoming G20 meetings to deliver progress on China, trade and other unresolved diplomatic issues. (Source: Bloomberg)
