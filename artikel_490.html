Nvidia Sinks as Analyst Downgrades Start to Come In: Street Wrap

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Nvidia Corp. shares fall as much as 8.4% in premarket trading following reports the AI chipmaker’s board is working with an outside firm to identify potential replacements for Chief Executive Officer Jensen Huang. The move follows reports of extramarital affairs, including with an alleged Chinese spy. If the premarket losses hold, the stock is set to drop below $4 trillion in market capitalization when cash trading resumes.
