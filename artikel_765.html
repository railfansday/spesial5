Will Smith Makes Surprise Cameo for Tatyana Ali in DWTS Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Will Smith supported his Fresh Prince of Bel-Air costar Tatyana Ali during the Dancing With the Stars finale with a sweet message of support before winners were crowned.

Now this is a story all about how Will Smith and Tatyana Ali melted hearts during the Dancing With the Stars finale.

Before Ezra Frech and professional partner Daniella Karagach were crowned the winners of DWTS season 35, the Fresh Prince of Bel-Air actor showed face to support his former costar.

“I am amazed,” Smith said in a video package that aired just before winners were announced during the Nov. 24 episode. “I am in awe. I am inspired. Keep doing what you’re doing. I love you.”

While Ali was floored by the support she received—which also included Elmo and The Black Eyed Peas’ will.i.am—she was visibly shocked by Smith’s brief cameo, falling to the ground and putting her hand over her mouth as she stood by her professional dance partner Jan Ravnik.

“I was surprised by all of them,” Ali later told ABC News after the finale. “I didn’t know about Will, I didn’t know about Elmo.”

Although she and Ravnik didn’t come out with a Mirrorball trophy, Ali insisted she had a “magical” experience competing on the series.

“From the first performance all the way to this one,” she mused. “Tonight was amazing. Tonight was really miraculous.”

Smith’s appearance may have briefly turned DWTS into a Fresh Prince of Bel-Air reunion—Alfonso Ribiero, who played Carlton Banks, has been hosting the reality series since 2022—but it was far from the only must-see moment of the night.

Case in point: Frech seamlessly recovered during his final performance to Linkin Park’s “Numb” alongside Karagach after a malfunction with his leg prosthetic. As Frech seamlessly reattached the piece mid-performance, Ribiero shared his awe in a commentary, noting, “What an incredible recovery.”

Naturally, Frech earned plenty of praise from the judges following his final performance.

“Ezra, you are a hero,” Derek Hough said in his feedback. “This performance will have a ripple effect far beyond this show. What you did tonight—forever, kids will look up to you and see what’s possible. The fact that the worst-case scenario might have happened and you wouldn’t have even known.”
