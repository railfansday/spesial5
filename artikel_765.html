Pepe did not injure eyes staring at sun, say Villarreal

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Nicolas Pepe (right) wore an eye mask on his return to the side following his eye operation in October

Villarreal say reports striker Nicolas Pepe needed eye surgery because he stared directly at the solar eclipse are "completely false".

In a statement, the Spanish club said an operation in October that ruled the 31-year-old out for six matches was "scheduled in advance" and "entirely unrelated" to the eclipse, which took place on 12 August.

Reports on social media suggested the former Arsenal forward had injured his eyes staring directly at the sun during the lunar event, with a video then emerging - purportedly from one of Pepe's social media accounts - showing him watching it in Spain.

"Villarreal CF, in response to reports published in various media outlets recently, clarifies that it is completely false that Nicolas Pepe's eye surgery has any connection to viewing the solar eclipse," said the club.

"The surgery had been scheduled in advance for reasons entirely unrelated to this event.

"The club urges accuracy and responsibility when reporting on matters related to its players' health."

Staring at an eclipse without protection can damage the retina, which sits at the back of the eye, and particularly those cells responsible for sharp central vision.

In the build-up to this summer's event, experts warned only genuine specially-designed solar eclipse glasses should be used, and that ordinary sunglasses or other devices were not enough to prevent potential damage.
