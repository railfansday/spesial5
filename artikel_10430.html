Anthropic stock is already falling today: Why AI giants are seeing a painful sell-off after record IPO

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Despite Anthropic’s (Nasdaq: ANT) blockbuster IPO yesterday, it’s been a bad several weeks for AI-related stocks.

And today things are even worse. Nearly every major hardware manufacturer that makes components for the AI pipeline—from CPUs to GPUs to memory—is seeing significant share price declines. 

And then there’s Anthropic itself, the world’s most valuable pure-play AI company. After a strong debut yesterday, its share price is down significantly this morning. Here’s what you need to know.

What’s happening?

In premarket trading this morning, the tech-heavy Nasdaq is seeing red, along with some of the largest AI players that trade there.

As of this writing, Nasdaq futures are down nearly 1%. That’s after a significant fall of more than 550 points, or 2.25% yesterday. As of Thursday’s close, the Nasdaq was down around 5% for the week. 

Yesterday’s Nasdaq drop is all the more striking because it was also the day that Anthropic made its eagerly awaited market debut. The AI company’s IPO was priced at $83 per share, but those shares quickly popped to nearly $115 in early trading before ending the first day at $97.17—a 17% gain.

Anthropic’s IPO was the largest in history, raising around $81.3 billion. The company’s market cap now sits at around $1.27 billion, making it the 12th most valuable company on the planet.

Yet Anthropic’s success did little for the rest of the market. Throughout the day, tech stocks started falling, including those of other major AI players like Nvidia.

This morning, Asian markets and major AI and tech players are down significantly. And on this side of the Pacific, the sell-off is continuing with major American AI-related stocks.

As of the time of this writing, the following AI-adjacent stocks are down in premarket: 

  • Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (Nasdaq: AMD): down 6.5%
  • NVIDIA Corporation (Nasdaq: NVDA): down 2.3%
  • Intel Corporation (Nasdaq: INTC): down 5.2%
  • Micron Technology, Inc. (Nasdaq: MU): down 4.8%
  • SanDisk Corporation (Nasdaq: SNDK): down 6.7%

And yes, Anthropic’s stock price is down nearly 10%.

Why are Anthropic’s shares sinking?

Anthropic and hardware-related AI companies are both seeing their share prices sink in premarket trading, but maybe for different reasons.

When it comes to AI hardware companies, those that make the components needed to power the massive server farms AI data centers require, there has been significant negative sentiment around them since at least October.

Investors are increasingly getting uneasy with the major hyperscalers’ capex spending.

Companies like Google, Meta, Microsoft, and Amazon are spending hundreds of billions to build out data centers, and most aren’t yet showing a clear path to monetizing AI enough to recoup those costs.

If those companies cave to investor pressure and start scaling back their capex plans, it will hit AI hardware component makers (like Nvidia, Intel, and AMD) and memory makers (like Micron and SanDisk) pretty hard.

Investors seem to be bracing for an inevitable capex pullback, which has weighed heavily on hardware-adjacent AI stocks recently.

Other factors that have affected AI stocks lately include the Jensen Huang scandal and the ongoing fears that we are firmly in an AI bubble.

But Anthropic doesn’t make hardware, and it just had its successful IPO only yesterday—so why are its shares down so much today?

It likely has less to do with significant fears of capex spending and more to do with other factors.

The primary factor is likely profit-taking. The company’s stock price rose more than 17% on its first day of trading. Many shareholders who aren’t bound by lockup periods are likely taking some of those profits this morning.

Another factor—and one that could have significantly more long-term consequences—is that fears of an AI bubble and an AI slowdown are also likely weighing on investors’ minds. 

Anthropic has a massive valuation north of $1.2 trillion, but in the first nine months of 2026, the company generated only around $33 billion in revenue. Its net loss for the same period was $104 billion. Investors may be able to live with that discrepancy while the AI hype train is riding high, but enthusiasm only goes so far, and if the AI bubble bursts, Anthropic could be hit hard.

Where do Anthropic shares go from here?

That’s something everyone wants to know, and no one can tell you.

The company’s share price has already fallen from its intraday debut high of nearly $115 per share to its current premarket price of $88.44.

Contrast that with the company Anthropic just dethroned as having the largest IPO in history: SpaceX. 

When SpaceX (Nasdaq: SPCX) went public in June, it was, until yesterday, the biggest IPO in history, raising $75 billion. Its IPO price was $135 per share, and the stock quickly jumped to more than $225 per share.

But then it began falling.

A month after its debut, SPCX shares were trading below their $135 IPO price as the hype wore off, and people began questioning the company’s future profitability. As of the time of this writing, SPCX shares are trading at just $131.66 in premarket trading.

If ANT’s early life plays out the same way—and there’s no way to predict whether it will or won’t—the share price could be underwater for even IPO investors in just a few months. And if the AI bubble does burst, things could get even worse.
