Hull City unlikely to keep Vaz beyond January

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

From a future investment to a potential transfer problem: Robinio Vaz’s time away from Roma risks ending sooner than expected.

The French striker, who moved to Hull City during the final days of the summer transfer window, has failed to establish a significant role in the English side.

According to Tuttomercatoweb.com, the Premier League club is even considering terminating his loan deal early.

Roma acquired Robinio Vaz from Marseille last January for approximately €25 million, banking on the qualities of the 2007-born striker, considered an interesting prospect.

The deal was designed with the player’s future development in mind, but the size of the investment also suggested a potential immediate contribution.

In the second half of the season, however, the Frenchman failed to convince Gian Piero Gasperini, finding very few opportunities to shine. After spending the summer in Rome, the club therefore chose to send him out on loan to help him develop through increased use.

The agreement with Hull City included a €2 million loan with a €30 million option to buy, a deal that could have guaranteed Roma a significant financial return in the event of positive performances. However, things aren’t going as expected.

Vaz continues to find little playing time, and the English club isn’t reportedly considering a permanent purchase of the player.

Instead, the possibility of terminating the deal during the next transfer window has emerged, potentially returning the striker to the Giallorossi early.

Roma is also reportedly dissatisfied with the situation, especially since the lack of playing time risks further slowing the young Frenchman’s development and compromising his value.

The initial goal of the loan was to allow Vaz to gain experience and demonstrate his qualities more consistently, but so far the move to England hasn’t yielded the desired results.
