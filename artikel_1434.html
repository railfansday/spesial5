Jorge Jesus praises Rodrigo Mora: “He’s been very good for Roma.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Rodrigo Mora is preparing for his first experience with the senior Portuguese national team.

The Roma talent has received his first call-up from coach Jorge Jesus, who has named him in the squad for the matches against Denmark and Wales.

During the press conference, Jesus was asked if Mora could play behind the center forward, in the position occupied by Joao Felix.

Two players with different technical skills, but who, according to the coach, can be deployed in the same area of ​​the pitch: “They have the characteristics to play that position very well.”

The Portugal coach then focused on the young Giallorossi’s performances for Roma in this first half of the season: “At Roma, Rodrigo was very involved in the attack, scoring goals and providing assists. He’s part of what I have in mind for these two matches.”

The first match is scheduled for November 14th in Braga against Denmark, while three days later the Portuguese side will close out their Nations League campaign in Cardiff against Wales.
