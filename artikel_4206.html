How To Get Blessed Jade Pendant In Phantom Blade Zero

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Sometimes, the truth comes at a great cost.

In Phantom Blade Zero, one of the main storylines has to do with Yu'er, a young girl trying to heal her father. You meet her right at the start of the game, and she pops up as you continue on, all the way until you get to Pangzhen. She has been trying to take a Flesh Lily to her father, as it is the only thing that can heal him.

However, once you get to the city, you'll find that she's been killed. After avenging her death, her body is taken by the Herald, who seeks to use her as a puppet going forward. When you finally make it to her home in the backstreets of Pangzhen, you're faced with the choice of telling her father the truth or shielding him from it.

Interestingly, you can avoid any dialogue with Yu'er's father, which is technically easier. The downside is that you don't get a positive ending or closure, and you also don't get the accessory he would give you as a reward.

In the Weeping Creek Valley, there is a missable boss that will affect your gameplay going forward. If you go to the Sha Chi Tower, on the first floor, you can find a section of the floor that you can slide down to reach a lower area of the map.

In this area, you'll meet a few enemies before getting to Hua, Deadly Betrothed. Defeating this boss grants you a White Lily, which affects whether you can interact with Yu'er's father or if you don't get a say in the matter.

You don't get the White Lily if you defeat Hua, Deadly Betrothed after meeting Yu'er's father.

If you don't face this boss at all, when you defeat Chang Hong and go to see Yu'er's father, you won't get the choice between telling him that Yu'er is gone or that she is hiding. You also won't get the Blessed Jade Pendant accessory; instead, you'll get the Tarnished Jade Pendant, which is shown as a story item and can't be used.

Once you're face-to-face with Hua, the name of the game is dodging as much as possible and parrying only when you see a clear opening. Many of her attacks involve rings of lilies on the ground that sap your health and Sha Chi, and her grab attack is devastating.

She'll also emit giant white lilies that fly through the air and follow you for a frustratingly long time, so keep running and dodging to avoid taking damage. She won't stop attacking while they float at you, so try not to put yourself between the flowers and Hua.

This fight will be much easier if you do it after speaking to Yu'er's father, since it's an early-game boss you'll be tackling well into the campaign. However, doing so will essentially make the fight pointless, because it'll be too late to save Yu'er's father if you wait that long.

If you do defeat Hua before you go to see Yu'er's father, you can take the white lily to him. When you speak with him, he will see Yu'er being controlled by the Herald and fall back asleep, but Soul will use the white lily to bring him back to consciousness. He will ask you where Yu'er is, and you'll be given two options: "She's no longer with us" or "She's still alive, but hiding."

If you tell him the truth that Yu'er is dead, he will say that he knew it all along, before you're shown a cutscene of Yu'er saying goodbye to her father in his dreams.

If you choose to spare his feelings and tell him that she's alive, he will thank you for your kindness before telling you that he knows that she is dead, also citing that she came to him in a dream.

Regardless of your choice, you'll get to speak with him about the state of the city, where he'll tell you what he knows before offering you the Blessed Jade Pendant.
