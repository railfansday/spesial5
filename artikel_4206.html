Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen New Update & Anime Collab Announced, Here Are The Full Patch Notes

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Capcom has this week announced a new update for its recent release Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen. It's already available on other platforms and will be live for the Switch 2 next week on 11th November 2026.

It comes loaded with added features and adjustments, and whole bunch of bug fixes, making updates to NPCs, the UI, Vocations, Pawns, Enemies and more. We'll provide an update when this is officially available on the Switch 2.

"The update for the Nintendo Switch 2 version is scheduled to release on November 11 PST / November 12 GMT. We apologize for the wait and appreciate your patience and understanding!"

In some other news, Capcom has also announced a new collab with the manga and anime series "Delicious in Dungeon" this week. It will include special pawns and outfit sets, and is available for a limited time.

And here's the full rundown of the latest game update via Capcom's official Dragon's Dogma website:

Availability: Until the January 28, 2027 Update Note: The Outfit Sets will be added to your storage after loading a saved data file during the period of availability.

If you haven't played Dragon's Dogma 2 (or its expansion Dark Arisen), be sure to check out our review. You can also download the game's character creator from the eShop and transfer your creations over to the full release.
