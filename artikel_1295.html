Three Everton December Fixtures Moved For Live Television

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Three of Everton's Premier League fixtures in December have been moved for live television coverage on Sky Sports.

The Blues' clash away to Aston Villa on Wednesday 2 December will now kick off 15 minutes later at 8.15pm (GMT), while the visit of Fulham to Hill Dickinson Stadium – initially pencilled in for Saturday 5 December – has been switched to Sunday 6 December at 2pm (GMT) due to the Cottagers' match at Tottenham Hotspur on Thursday 3 December.

Everton's away trip to Brighton & Hove Albion has also been put back a day to Sunday 13 December at 2pm (GMT) because of the Seagulls' participation in the UEFA Conference League three days' earlier.

The Toffees' match at Nottingham Forest will remain on Saturday 19 December at 3pm (GMT).

Ticket details for all December games will be announced in due course on Everton digital channels.
