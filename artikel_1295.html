Champions League Liveblog: FC Porto vs. Napoli, RC Lens vs. Como

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from the Champions League, as Napoli visit Francesco Farioli’s FC Porto and Como are away to RC Lens.

They both kick off at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

The Partenopei finally got their first European points with a 4-3 victory over Bodo/Glimt, but are coming off the weekend’s 2-0 Serie A loss to Juventus.

Cesc Fabregas and Como won both home games in the Champions League, but lost away to Feyenoord, so the pressure is on against Florian Thauvin’s Lens.

Max Allegri admits Napoli have ‘limitations’ after a 4-1 defeat to FC Porto, as their Champions League campaign hangs by a thread. ‘These are the mistakes that cost you matches.’

Cesc Fabregas admits the 9-1 win in Lens was ‘a wonderful night for Como, but we need to keep our feet on the ground’ as Champions League ambition starts to genuinely build.

Como made history with an astonishing 9-1 victory away to RC Lens in the Champions League, including a Tasos Douvikas hat-trick and 18-year-old Matteo Papaccioli scoring on his debut.
