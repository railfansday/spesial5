Report: Milan and Genoa discussing 3 young Italy talents including Camarda and Calvani

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Sunday’s reports claim that Milan are interested in a potential deal for Italy U21 defender Gabriele Calvani, on loan at Frosinone from Genoa, and that the Grifone are interested in deals for both Francesco Camarda and Christian Comotto.

Milan are hoping to add a new central defender to their ranks during the January transfer window, and whilst recent reports claim that Sporting CP’s Goncalo Inacio is their priority target, updates on Sunday claim that Calvani is also a potential option.

According to reports from Calciomercato.com, the Rossoneri have recently sent scouts to watch Frosinone in action ahead of their game against the newly-promoted side coming up next weekend, and that those scouts kept a particularly close eye on Calvani.

Calvani is represented by Giuseppe Riso, who is also the agent of several young Italian talents including Camarda and Comotto. Riso is expected to meet with Rossoneri representatives in a meeting at Casa Milan in the coming weeks. Milan and Genoa are also said to have ‘excellent’ working relations.

Meanwhile, there have also been reports on Sunday that Genoa are interested in pinching two young talents from Milan, namely Camarda and Comotto.

As per Calciomercato, Milan do not want to ‘lose control’ of either player and would therefore only be interested in sending them out on loan.

Genoa are reportedly keen to include some form of buy option, but in order to convince Milan, this would likely need to include a favourable buy-back clause to get a deal off the ground.

Parma, Monza and several other Serie B clubs are said to be interested in Comotto as well.

Pisa, Benevento, Catanzaro, Empoli and a selection of clubs abroad are also said to be interested in Camarda.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
