13+ Xbox Games Are Releasing Today, Including A Retro-Inspired Shooter (November 13)

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Happy Friday 13th everyone. To celebrate, let's have a look at all the new games launching on Xbox today.

Three games caught my eye this morning: a retro FPS, a simulator, and a social co-op game. DERELIKT is the shooter. It's giving serious old-school Xbox and PS1 vibes, drawing from games like System Shock and DOOM.

As for the simulator, well, you're basically just blowing leaves around with a leaf blower. Makes sense why they'd call it Leaf Blower Co. Finally, the co-op game BOMBANANA has you playing as three monkeys each restricted by the see no evil, speak no evil, and hear no evil, as you attempt to defuse a bomb. Oh so silly stuff.
