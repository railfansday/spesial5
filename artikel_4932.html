Bitcoin Faces 6-Year Moving Average Test Only Seen During FTX Collapse

Since 2016, the only time Bitcoin closed below its six-year moving average was the FTX crash. That lasted about two months, with the worst closes about 9% under the line, and a year later Bitcoin was up 128%. Sean Hagan explains why this cycle has only wicked below it, and why FTX is the one that looks out of place on the chart.
