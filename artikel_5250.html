XRP Ledger Posts 370% Payment Spike After Sharp Price Rebound

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Although XRP has begun to cool from the sudden price recovery witnessed at the beginning of the week that allowed it to reclaim the $1 price level, payment activity across its network has remained high.

After multiple days of extreme caution from traders, the latest on-chain data from XRPScan shows a sharp resurgence in payment volume on the XRP Ledger.

The data shows that the total volume of XRP transferred between accounts on the XRP Ledger surpassed a massive 997.2 million XRP on November 23rd.

Further data shows that this marks an increase of about 370.1% in XRP payment volume over the last 24 hours; this means that the amount of XRP transferred between accounts has soared significantly after the major drop in activity seen the previous day.

The rapid spike in volume has drawn attention as it far exceeds the network's 30-day average payment volume of 791.8 million XRP.

While the surge has coincided with a sharp rebound in the price of XRP, it appears that transaction activity has picked up considerably as the market regains confidence.

It is important to note that the surge in XRP payment volume has come after XRP witnessed a parabolic price move, showing a daily increase of over 10% after reclaiming its $1 price level.

However, it appears that the rally has cooled as XRP has briefly fallen back into the red, retesting $0.9 once again.

Although its price movement has become weak again, on-chain data shows that its network activity remains strong as holders continue to move the token across accounts.
