Longbridge promotes Melissa Macerato to president and chief commercial officer

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Longbridge Financial promoted longtime executive Melissa Macerato to president and chief commercial officer, recognizing her role in building sales, marketing, training and product development as the company expanded beyond reverse mortgages into broader home equity solutions for homeowners 55 and older.   AI Summary

Melissa Macerato has been promoted to president and chief commercial officer at Longbridge Financial, moving up from her prior role as chief revenue and marketing officer, the company announced Monday.

Macerato has been a key member of the company’s leadership team since 2012, helping guide its evolution from an emerging reverse mortgage lender into a broader home equity lender serving homeowners 55 and older, according to the company announcement.

Longbridge reported a 31% market share and annualized growth exceeding 40% since January 2024 in its core business of serving older homeowners. The company said Macerato’s promotion recognizes more than a decade of contributions to its business strategy, organizational development and culture.

“Melissa has represented the heart and soul of Longbridge since joining us during our startup phase, when we didn’t have a single loan officer. She led the development of our entire sales, marketing, and training departments, which together now account for more than 40% of our employees,” CEO Chris Mayer said in a statement.

“Over the years, Longbridge’s sales team has reached new highs in productivity, even amid the challenges of a high-interest-rate environment. Under Melissa’s leadership, Longbridge has achieved and maintained its No. 1 position as the industry’s largest third-party originator, with continued gains in volume and market share and a deep commitment to helping our partners grow their businesses. Equally important, she has been instrumental in building our unique culture, where 94% of employees say Longbridge is a great place to work,” Mayer said.

Throughout her tenure, Macerato has overseen sales, training, marketing and human resources, along with Longbridge’s product development division. The company said she has been responsible for building teams and infrastructure to support Longbridge’s expansion and diversification.

Working with Mayer and the executive leadership team, Macerato helped take HELOC For Seniors, a home equity line of credit for borrowers 62 and older, from concept to market. She has also helped grow Longbridge’s proprietary Platinum reverse mortgage portfolio, supporting the shift toward a broader mix of home equity products.

Macerato brings more than 20 years of leadership experience across banking, mortgage lending and financial services. She began her career at MBNA America as a vice president before joining GE Capital as a senior director. At GE Capital, she was selected as one of the original participants in its Six Sigma quality process management program and achieved Black Belt status.

She later joined MetLife, where she eventually led reverse mortgage sales. There, she hired more than 150 salespeople and managers, helping to establish the company as a major reverse mortgage lender. After MetLife began winding down its bank, she joined Longbridge in 2012, a few months after the lender’s founding.

In her expanded role, Macerato will help lead Longbridge’s next phase of growth and oversee the rollout of new products and tools designed to help borrowers 55 and older understand how home equity can fit into a retirement strategy. For lenders and brokers working in the senior housing market, the company’s focus on new product suites and education tools could broaden partnerships and add options for borrowers who may not fit traditional reverse mortgage profiles.
