PHYSINT Star Bill Skarsgård has Become ‘Enamored’ with Hideo Kojima and His Works

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Daniel is a lifelong gamer with a love for various genres and gameplay styles who enjoys exploring games and playing from various angles.

If a single person could be considered to be the definitive face of auteur game development, it would be Hideo Kojima, hands down. The man was already showing his chops in putting together unforgettable productions back in the Metal Gear Solid days, and ever since going into business for himself with Kojima Productions, his worlds and stories have only gotten bigger and bolder.

All of those qualities alone more than qualify Kojima for auteur status, but there’s also a little bit more to it than that. Kojima possesses, for lack of a better term, a certain magnetism, both in his person and his production, that inexplicably draws powerful, prominent individuals from across the entertainment spectrum. You just have to look at the original Death Stranding and its star-studded cast to see the kind of magnetic pull a Kojima trip possesses.

Very little is currently known about PHYSINT, one of Kojima’s next big-budget productions that was rescued from the brink by XBOX when Sony bailed out over the summer, but apparently, its star, Bill Skarsgård, was already well aboard Mister Kojima’s wild ride well before he signed on. He spoke to this during a recent appearance on the Happy Sad Confused podcast, as well as the inherent power that comes with Kojima’s works.

Joining an already stacked cast for PHYSINT, which features Charlee Fraser, Don Lee, and Minami Hamabe, is none other than Bill Skarsgård.

As Skarsgård explained, he grew up playing Kojima’s games during his time at Konami, chief among them the Metal Gear series. By his own admission, Skarsgård has played every single Metal Gear game that is commercially available, finding himself repeatedly drawn into the elaborate, intricate world that Kojima was piecing together story-by-story.

Skarsgård has played every single Metal Gear game that is commercially available.

As Skarsgård became more interested in Kojima’s creations, he became more interested in Kojima himself. “I was very enamoured by him and the sort of the mythology or the universes that he creates, but also the sort of mystique and allure about him as well,” he explained.

Hideo Kojima shared several new details about PHYSINT, and it looks like the legendary developer is going back to full stealth.

Later in the interview, as Skarsgård described the process of motion capture that was going into PHYSINT’s development, he marveled at how the planned game will feature both eight hours of narrative alongside an entire, living world populated by photorealistic people and settings.

“I think he's a pioneer, and keeps going out to untrodden waters,” he said of Kojima.

Skarsgård briefly recalled a discussion he had about filmmaking with Kojima during PHYSINT’s motion capture process. It’s no secret that, in addition to video games, Kojima is well-versed in the cinematic arts, both on a technical level and just as a good old fashioned lover of movies, as you can tell from the many film references and direct shout-outs that pepper his games.

Interestingly, though, when Skarsgård asked Kojima if he ever wanted to make an actual movie someday rather than a game, Kojima said no, because he wants to make something with the cinematic potential of traditional film combined with the inherent interactivity of a video game, and a regular film can’t do that. “At this point, what he's doing is inventing a new medium,” Skarsgård said.

All we currently know about PHYSINT, aside from its major stars in Bill Skarsgård, Charlee Fraser, Ma Dong-seok, and Minami Hamabe, is that it’s apparently going to be an action-espionage game in a similar vein to the Metal Gear series. The promotional tagline on its initial reveal poster is “The Next Cold War Begins.” Based on Skarsgård’s gushing, though, it’s going to be quite the ambitious multimedia project, the kind of elaborate, once-per-generation creation that only an auteur like Kojima could bring to life. So, y’know, business as usual.
