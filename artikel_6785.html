Prince Harry Says Archie and Lilibet’s Accents Are Already Changing

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Prince Harry made a guest appearance on ITV’s This Morning today and gave the hosts a little insight into how his children, Archie and Lilibet, are adjusting to life in the U.K.

He and Meghan Markle moved back to England at the end of August, with their rep confirming that they had “decided to spend an extended period in the U.K. this autumn. They are excited to have their children spend more time in the country that’s such an important part of their family’s story.”

The kids are doing well in their new home, Harry said: “They love being back.”

Archie and Lilibet have spent most of their lives in California (Lili was even born there). The Duke of Sussex acknowledged that being in England is “a big culture shift. I think that the kids in school uniforms is a big change for them, but it has certainly helped. The confidence in both of them has gone through the roof since being in U.K. school, which is really, really nice.”

The hosts asked about the kids’ American accents. “I’m squeezing the accents out of them and their friends at school are doing the same,” Harry quipped. “Lili does a good impression of ‘Mummy.’”
