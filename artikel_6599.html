Sam Adams' New Cherry Chocolate Stout Is The Winter Beer Fans Can't Stop Talking About

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The 6% ABV brew joins Winter White Ale, Winter Lager, and Old Fezziwig in a seasonal variety pack.

Samuel Adams isn’t just beloved for its classic Boston Lager—people across the country stock up every time a new seasonal beer hits shelves. And this winter is no different. The craft beer brand recently launched its new Cherry Chocolate Stout, the latest addition to its winter lineup, and customers are already singing its praises.

While many brands lean into cranberry during the holiday season, Sam Adams is going a different route by embracing cherry. The new beer has notes of cherry cordial, vanilla, roasted malt, and fudge chocolate, making for a smooth yet decadent sip.

At 6% ABV, it’s a hearty brew. But can it measure up to the Winter White Ale? It’s easy to find out, as both are included in Sam Adams’ Winter Break variety pack, along with Winter Lager and Old Fezziwig.

“I’m not looking forward to winter but am looking forward to this pack 😀,” one fan commented on the brand’s social media. “The whole variety pack is a banger! Can’t wait to get my hands on a few cases,” another wrote.

And those who have already tried the stout are giving it particularly good reviews. “The Cherry Chocolate Stout steals the show in that variety pack. It is ridiculously good!” one customer declared.

Others are still eager to get their hands on it. “Winter white is amazing but not gonna lie can't wait to try that Cherry Chocolate,” one person shared. “Yeah I need this in my life,” another agreed.

Now the only question is: Will you be sipping this out on the slopes or sitting next to the fireplace?
