18 Splendid Winners From the British Photography Awards

The British Photography Awards, a competition open to every photographer based in the U.K., have announced the winners for 2026.

A glitzy award ceremony took place last night at the Royal Lancaster in London, where the category winners and People’s Choice Awards were handed out.

To see all the winners, and for info on next year’s competition, head to the British Photography Awards’ website.
