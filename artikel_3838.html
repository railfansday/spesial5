All FC 27 Ultimate Gauntlet 3 rewards and tasks

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Ultimate Gauntlet 3 is now live in FC 27, and you can complete it to win some wonderful rewards. This gauntlet also kicks off the RTTF promo and rewards you with a guaranteed promo item. However, there are more packs to win, and let’s find out what’s on offer.

The Ultimate Gauntlet 3 has ten different rewards. This includes promo card, evolution, coins, and packs. However, you need to complete any eight out of the 10 tasks to finish the whole set.

Once you complete the objective set, you’ll also get the Aleix Garcia RTTF item. The card might not be overpowered, but you can use it in more casual modes. Moreover, the card will be eligible for upgrades based on how Bayer Leverkusen performs in the Europa League.
