Demon Tides coming to PS5, Switch 2, and Switch on January 21, 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Publisher PM Studios and developer Fabraz will release open-world 3D platformer Demon Tides for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and Switch on January 21, 2027, the companies announced. PlayStation 5 and Switch 2 physical editions will launch later in 2027.

Demon Tides first launched for PC via Steam on February 19, 2026.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:

Race across oceans and uncover the kingdom’s dark secrets in 3D platformer Demon Tides!

Navigate the open seas and explore each distant structure, uncovering every hidden nook and cranny with your diverse, expressive move-set. Beat challenges or find hidden chests to collect talismans along the way that modify and even expand your abilities or switch up your drip with a plethora of discoverable outfits!

And never forget, you’re not alone in this journey! Compete with other players for speedrunning times by challenging their ghosts or tag a wall and share your message in the form of graffiti with the entire world! Are you ready to conquer this kingdom and expose all of its mysterious secrets? Or will Beebz be crushed by what awaits her… Only one way to find out!
