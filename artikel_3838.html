Feature: Everything You Need to Know Ahead of the Delta Force Invitationals Grand Final

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The tactical shooter’s 3-day LAN championship kicks off in Sweden this weekend - with huge in-game rewards for tuning in

After last year’s inaugural event in Hangzhou, this weekend sees the Delta Force Invitational make its first move outside Asia, as an official competitive title at DreamHack in Stockholm, Sweden, from 27th-29th November. The Grand Final of the tournament will take place on November 29th at 11am UTC+0 through to 4pm UTC+0 after three days of global competitive action.

It’s the first Chinese esports title to reach DreamHack, featuring teams that have been selected through a full professional league system. Six regions offered a route to the offline finals, and the twelve squads competing this weekend are as follows:

Fnatic are, of course, well known to DreamHack audiences, having won the first League of Legends World Championship there back in 2011, as well as the first CS:GO Major in 2013. Alliance, being a Swedish team, will be playing on home soil, but only time will tell if this gives them an additional boost. They, and the ten other teams to have gone the distance, will once again be competing for a share of a $500,000 prize pool.

One core aspect of Delta Force that’s attracted new fans over the last twelve months is its commitment to extraction as an esport. Whereas in traditional FPS competitions there’s an obvious focus on winning fights, in Delta Force’s Operations mode, information gathering, firefights, resource management and extraction decisions all play out within a single match.

In the two years since its launch, Delta Force has steadily gained momentum, with developers Team Jade determined to grow the tactical FPS into a globally recognised esports title. With three days of intense competition on the horizon, its future certainly looks bright.

Delta Force is free to play and available now on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Android, iOS, Google Play, Epic Games Store, and Steam.
