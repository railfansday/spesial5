All FC 27 Campaign Mix Upgrade SBC rewards

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Campaign Mix Upgrade SBC is now live in FC 27, and it’s a guaranteed shot at a promo item. The SBC features promo cards from previous campaigns that were dropped earlier in Ultimate Team, and there are some valuable cards to be won.

Let’s find out all the available items before you decide to spend your fodder.

The Campaign Mix Upgrade SBC features all items released in the Destined for Glory and Future Stars promo.

The SBC costs about 40,000 coins worth of fodder, and most of the promo items are valued above it. However, you have no way to guarantee the card you get. Hence, only try this SBC if you’re willing to take the risk.
