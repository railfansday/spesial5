Why Pizza Has Become an Unofficial Thanksgiving Tradition

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Turkey gets Thursday, but Wednesday is all about the 'za.

It’s Thanksgiving Eve. The turkey is hogging an entire fridge shelf, every baking dish is spoken for and the cousins just pulled into the driveway. Then somebody asks what’s for dinner, and the only reasonable answer comes in a cardboard box.

If that sounds familiar, you’re in excellent company. As it turns out, the night before the big feast is one of the busiest times of the year for pizza orders, and our collective craving for a good slice is practically a holiday tradition of its own.

Ridiculously popular. According to DoorDash’s latest trends report, pizza was the No. 1 pick this time last year, with orders jumping more than 50% compared with other Wednesdays in November. And the ‘za of choice? Plain cheese, up a whopping 70%.

The craze isn’t limited to the couch, either. Toast data shows that pizza orders at sit-down restaurants and bars spiked 48% that evening. Salads, meanwhile, dipped 5%, which sounds about right.

National chains feel the rush, too. “Across the industry, it consistently lands in the top handful alongside Halloween and the Super Bowl,” Greg Hamilton, chief marketing officer at Little Caesars, said in a statement.

There’s a certain satisfaction in wiping down the counters the day before, knowing the casseroles are assembled and you’re as ready for tomorrow as you’ll ever be. The last thing anyone wants to do is pull another pan out of the cupboard.

“Thanksgiving Eve is about making life easier,” Hamilton said. “The kitchen is already prepped for Thursday, the house is filling up and nobody wants to cook twice.”

But there’s more to it than giving the dishwasher a break. College kids are back in their hometowns, and childhood friends are making plans to reunite. Even siblings who usually communicate via memes in the family group chat are finally in the same room.

With AAA expecting 81.5 million Americans to travel this week, plenty of those reunions kick off a day before anyone carves anything. Order pizza, and whoever’s hosting actually hears the surprise engagement announcement instead of yelling “Wait, WHAT?” over a sizzling skillet.
