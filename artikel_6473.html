Bose has long been synonymous with noise cancellation—before it was cool. Its QuietComfort headphones remain top-of-class, and that doesn’t appear to be changing anytime soon. The second-gen QuietComfort Headphones are its best iteration yet, and while they are a little pricier than past models, they’re typically well worth the investment. And right now, you can get Black Friday-level savings on the brand’s latest (and greatest) model.

Right now, Bose’s latest QuietComfort headphones are 45 percent off, which amounts to nearly $200 in savings. $200 for our best-tested noise-canceling headphones is an absolute steal.

The QuietComfort 2.0 makes some significant improvements on Bose’s best-selling QuietComfort noise-canceling headphones. For starters, they feature a modernized design, offering more comfortable pill-shaped earcups and a sliding, adjustable headband. They’re also slightly wider than the previous model, with cozier synthetic leather that strikes a perfect balance between a soft and secure fit.

Performance has also improved. Bose’s new proprietary TrueSpatial Technology adds another layer of immersive, natural sound alongside its best-in-class noise cancellation. It also adds improved high-fidelity lossless audio via a USB-C connection.

Senior Tech Editor Stefan Vazharov also raves about the latest QuietComforts (which, for what it’s worth, are also my go-to over-ear headphones)—we have them in our latest tech gift guide, as well.

“A sequel to a legendary product, the Bose QuietComfort (2nd Gen) headphones add welcome upgrades, headlined by spatial audio and lossless audio playback over USB-C,” Vazharov said. “I'd take them in a heartbeat for less than $200, and use the savings to grab an extra set in one more of the many available colors.”

Speaking of noise cancellation, that has gotten better with age, too. The second-generation headphones feature updated ActiveSense Tech for adaptive acoustic noise reduction, which evens out sound spikes when listening in Aware Mode. There’s also improved background noise reduction—perfect for meetings and calls.

We’re looking at a more intuitive interface, including updated button placement, easier volume control, and an easier-than-ever way to cycle through listening modes. This is as important as ever, as Bose added new modes to the equation. In addition to its usual QuietMode for full noise cancellation, there’s now Cinema Mode with layered sound for enhanced video content, Immersion Mode, and ANR off.

These are our favorite noise-cancellation headphones at full price—although at almost $400, we’d even admit that’s pretty steep. But $200 is a steal—especially for one of our top 2026 tech gift picks. Get these while you can; we don’t expect this deal to last long.
