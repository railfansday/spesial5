Parke’s Collab With Stoney Clover Lane Is Back by (Very) Popular Demand

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The two internet-famous brands return with a new iteration of their sold-out collab.

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, The Hollywood Reporter may receive an affiliate commission.

Parke’s latest inevitably viral launch comes in the form of a collaboration with beloved accessories brand Stoney Clover Lane.

The limited-edition drop, available on both Parke’s website and Stoney Clover Lane’s website starting Friday, Nov. 6 at 7 a.m. PT/10 a.m. ET, is chock-full of each brand’s customer favorites: denim, duffels, pouches, bag charms and, of course, a reimagined version of Parke’s signature Varsity Mockneck.

“When my two worlds COLLIDE!,” writes one Instagram user. The sentiment is clearly widespread. The latest release comes on the heels of Parke’s Halloween drop, which similarly revived sold-out favorites. Other recent campaigns include Parke’s Pink Aid partnership timed to Breast Cancer Awareness Month, its Fall ’26 Collection and Parke’s Collegiate Collection, the brand’s first licensed apparel launch, created in partnership with sports lifestyle brand ’47.

Since coming to market in 2022, Chelsea Parke’s direct-to-consumer clothing line has taken social media by storm. What started as reworked denim quickly turned into limited-release loungewear, most notably, Parke’s signature mockneck, known to sell out within minutes of online drops. By April 2026, the viral fashion brand made its way into Target stores nationwide. As for Stoney Clover Lane, the internet sensation launched in 2009 by two Palm Beach-based sisters. The brand has since collaborated with entertainment and retail giants like Disney, Sanrio, the NFL and American Girl, to name a few.

Stoney Clover Lane x Parke will be available in limited quantities starting Friday, Nov. 6 at both Parke.com and StoneyCloverLane.com. As always, product is expected to move fast.
