Blumhouse Atomic’s ‘Sleepover’ to Star Michelle Monaghan, Justin Theroux

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Filmmaker Jacob Chase is behind the project, due in theaters next year.

Michelle Monaghan and Justin Theroux are packing their sleeping bags for the night. The duo are set to star in Sleepover for Blumhouse Atomic, the movie from Insidious: Out of the Further filmmaker Jacob Chase.

The logline is being kept under wraps for the film, which Chase wrote with Night Swim director Bryce McGuire, based on McGuire’s original short story. It’s slated for a wide release from Universal on Oct. 8, 2027, one of eight projects Blumhouse Atomic has in theaters next year.

Blumhouse Atomic’s Jason Blum is producing with Chase and McGuire executive producing. Bea Sequeira is also executive producing for Blumhouse and Lilly Tyson overseeing for the company. Cinescape Entertainment is backing the movie with Blumhouse Atomic and Universal.

Monaghan earned praise for the most recent season of HBO’s The White Lotus, and returns next month for season two of Bad Monkey on Apple TV. Other credits include the Netflix’s drama series The Sticks and Apple’s Your Friends and Neighbors.

Two-time Emmy winner Theroux was recently on screen with Netflix’s Running Point and The Devil Wears Prada 2. Next he has the Apple TV Siegfried & Roy limited series Wild Things with Jude Law and Andrew Garfield. During his long career, he starred in HBO’s The Leftovers and wrote Iron Man 2, Tropic Thunder and Zoolander.

Monaghan is repped by Gersh, Anonymous Content, Entertainment 360, Narrative, and Sloane Offer. Theroux is repped by CAA, Lighthouse Management & Media, Ingenuity Group and Toczek Suddleson Law Group.
