NBC to Air Princess of Wales Cancer Charity Documentary

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The special follows the princess as she completes a challenge to climb the highest peaks in England, Scotland and Wales.

NBC will air a documentary special following the Princess of Wales as she completes a charity challenge to raise money for cancer patients.

Heal: A Princess of Wales Special is set to air at 8 p.m. ET/PT Nov. 30 on NBC, a day after it premieres on the BBC in the U.K. The special will also at 11:30 p.m. Nov. 30 on Bravo and stream on Peacock starting Dec. 1.

Heal will explore “life beyond diagnosis and the many ways people find comfort, strength and hope in the face of serious illness,” a description reads. Cameras followed Catherine as she completed the Three Peaks Challenge — climbing the tallest mountains in England, Scotland and Wales in the course of 24 hours — in June in support of the Royal Marsden Cancer Charity. It will also feature the stories of other people facing cancer (the princess was diagnosed with a form of cancer in 2024 but was in remission as of 2025).

“[Cancer] changes your body, it changes you physically,” Catherine says in a trailer for the special (watch it below). “But there’s a much, much deeper part to what a cancer diagnosis shifts in you.”

All money from distribution of the special (it will also air in Australia on Dec. 1 and Canada on Dec. 7) will go to the Royal Marsden Cancer Charity to help people living with and after a cancer diagnosis. Detail Films produced the special, with Benji Jackson directing.
