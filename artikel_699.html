‘Spider-Man: Brand New Day’ Extended Cut Sets December Release Date in Theaters

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The re-release of “Spider-Man: Brand New Day,” which features footage previously left on the cutting room floor, hits theaters on Dec. 4. Tickets go on sale Nov. 19.

News that “Brand New Day” was returning to theaters with an extended cut first broke in late September. While audiences will have to wait another month to know everything that’s in the “Brand New Day” re-release, Rosario Dawson is set to show up somewhere in the cut. The “Ahsoka” star first revealed during a “Daredevil” fan Q&A in August that her MCU character, Claire Temple, aka Night Nurse, had a cameo in “Brand New Day,” but the scene was removed. “There’s a frame in one of the new comics that has a young Spider-Man talking to Daredevil, and they made it so that the young Spider-Man goes to PS19 in the Lower East Side, which is where I went to elementary school,” Dawson said. “So when I tell you my post was already ready with my BTS, with my grandson wearing all his Spider-Man stuff … Then I got the email that was like, ‘Yeah, nah.’”

Dawson’s Night Nurse is a medic who helps treat injured superheroes. The character first appeared in the first season of “Daredevil” in 2015. Dawson reprised the role in several MCU shows, including “Jessica Jones,” “Luke Cage,” “Iron Fist” and “The Defenders.”

“Spider-Man: Brand New Day” first arrived in cinemas on July 31 and skyrocketed as the summer’s biggest blockbuster success. The superhero tentpole debuted to $927 million worldwide on opening weekend and has since extended its global tally to $2.5 billion. Directed by Destin Daniel Cretton, the film is currently the third-highest-grossing movie of all time behind 2009’s “Avatar” ($2.92 billion) and 2019’s “Avengers: Endgame” ($2.88 billion) and ahead of 2022’s “Avatar: The Way of Water” ($2.3 billion) and 1994’s “Titanic” ($2.2 billion).

In less than two months of release, “Brand New Day” overtook 2015’s “Star Wars: The Force Awakens” as the highest-grossing domestic film in history. “The Force Awakens” set the bar at $936 million domestic, and the record stood for over a decade. “Brand New Day” snatched the crown after grossing $936.773 million in 47 days in North American cinemas.

“Spider-Man: Brand New Day” sees franchise star Tom Holland face off with a mysterious telepath and try to rebuild his life as a completely anonymous web-slinger. In the previous film, Benedict Cumberbatch’s Doctor Strange wiped the memories of the entire world so no one could remember that Peter Parker was Spider-Man. Other cast members include Zendaya, Sadie Sink, Jon Bernthal, Jacob Batalon, Mark Ruffalo, Tramell Tillman and Florence Pugh.
