MatPat announces surprise YouTube return with brand-new channel

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

YouTube star MatPat has announced his return to the platform nearly three years after retiring from his popular Theory channels, revealing plans for an entirely new project alongside his wife, Stephanie.

Matthew Patrick, better known as MatPat, stepped away from hosting his Theory videos in 2024 after spending 13 years building one of YouTube’s most recognizable gaming and entertainment brands.

Now, after more than two years away from regularly appearing in videos, the creator is returning with a new channel that promises a different approach to content, without the demanding upload schedule that defined his previous career.

In a new video announcing his comeback, MatPat reflected on the 943 days since his final Theory video and explained how retirement allowed him to spend more time with his family, travel, and pursue projects outside YouTube.

Despite enjoying his time away, he admitted there was one aspect of creating videos that he missed.

“It’s because I missed you, okay?” he told viewers.

MatPat explained that while he and Stephanie have enjoyed exploring new opportunities, they realized how much they valued sharing their experiences and discoveries with their audience.

“What we learned was that one, we loved learning and the channels gave us an excuse to do that. But more importantly, two, that we loved getting to share what we learned with other people,” he said.

Their new channel will feature discussions about internet culture, entertainment, technology, and other subjects that interest them, including the growing popularity of puppet-based content, artificial intelligence, and the entertainment industry.

Stephanie, who previously appeared on GT Live and helped manage the Theory channels behind the scenes, will also play a major role in the new project.

The pair explained that their expanding YouTube business eventually left them with less time to work together on videos, something they hope to change with their new channel.

“It’s meant to be a reset. It’s back to basics,” Stephanie explained. “It’s back to simple videos where we just get to hang out, we get to have fun, and we get to talk about weird, fun, memish, and yeah, sometimes important stuff, too.”

Unlike the Theory channels, however, MatPat emphasized that the new project will not follow a weekly upload schedule.

“This channel is about being here and having real conversations. It’s not about feeding the algorithm and every time we put something up, it’s because we really think it’s worth checking out,” he said.

Alongside announcing his YouTube comeback, MatPat shared details about several projects he and Stephanie have been developing since leaving their previous roles.

One of the biggest announcements involved his growing partnership with horror production company Blumhouse, which is responsible for films including Five Nights at Freddy’s, Get Out, and Paranormal Activity.

MatPat confirmed that the two have been working together for nearly a year, with their first announced collaboration being a film adaptation of the indie horror game Amanda the Adventurer.

He also revealed that two additional movie projects are in development, although he cannot disclose their details while contractual arrangements are being finalized.

Beyond filmmaking, the couple have written a musical based on an existing franchise, but are still working to secure the rights to make it an official adaptation.

They’re also developing a podcast intended to make classic literature more entertaining and accessible to children and families.

MatPat discussed his work in Washington, DC, where he and Stephanie have advocated for protections involving creators’ voices and likenesses, as well as greater recognition of the online creator industry.

Despite the growing list of projects, he made it clear that returning to YouTube doesn’t mean returning to the demanding workload he left behind.

“We went for 13 years almost never missing a weekly upload across many channels and this channel is not that,” he explained.

Instead, MatPat and Stephanie plan to release videos whenever they have something they want to share, with some episodes potentially arriving close together and others separated by longer breaks.

The announcement concluded with MatPat reviving his familiar introduction, telling viewers, “For the first time in 2 and 1/2 YEARS. HELLO, INTERNET. IT’S GREAT TO see you again.”
