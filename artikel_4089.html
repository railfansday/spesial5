Microsoft Is Giving Away The Most Insane Xbox Series X Ever Created

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

We've seen all kinds of Xbox Series X console designs over the past six years, but this one is specifically advertised as Microsoft's "wildest" design yet, and they're giving it away to one lucky sweepstakes winner.

Here's how it works - it's an Xbox Series X inside of an alligator. A functioning Series X, no less.

Don't believe us? Check out the trailer and accompanying description of it:

"Measuring more than five feet from snout to tail, the console sits inside a meticulously crafted alligator, visible through an illuminated XBOX Nexus in its body. A custom XBOX Wireless Controller with bite marks rests between its jaws, complete with XBOX-green irises and Nexus-inspired pupils, making the XBOX Series X-BITE ready to play some of the biggest games of the year. The result is part-XBOX Series X, part-creature fabrication."

Microsoft says this console was created by John Nolan Studio, which has worked on loads of famous projects including Jurassic World Dominion and the upcoming Harry Potter series for HBO Max.

There's only one of them as mentioned, and the way to enter the sweepstakes is by following these instructions:

It's worth noting here that the giveaway is only for residents of the United States, and entries close in exactly a week. The chances of securing it are extremely slim, of course, but you've got to be in it to win it!

We'll leave you with more details and images down below.

"This November, XBOX is heading to Florida – home to incredible wildlife, unforgettable places, and every now and then, stories you have to see to believe. That felt like the right inspiration for a custom XBOX Series X unlike anything we’ve made before: the XBOX Series X-BITE. The idea is simple: an alligator swallowed an XBOX Series X whole. And yes, it still works.

Keep your account public so we can see your entry. That’s it. Choose your platform, make your move and you’re in for a chance to win. Keep your notifications on and your eyes open after entering. If you’re the potential winner, you’ll have 48 hours to make your move and claim the prize. Miss the window, and this gator makes a clean getaway."
