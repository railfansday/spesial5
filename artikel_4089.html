Dave The Diver's Latest Crossover Update Sees Another Beloved Indie Join The Fray

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Since its arrival on Switch a few years back, Dave The Diver has crossed over with just about every other indie game imaginable (a slight exaggeration, but you get our point). Today, developer Mintrocket has announced yet another of these crossovers for its roster, as it is teaming up with Fireshine Games and Pugstorm on some new two-way Core Keeper content.

This crossover update launches on 30th November in both games, with the sandbox survival title gaining some new Blue Hole paraphernalia, and the beloved diving/bar management sim going all mine-y. It's all entirely optional, but it'll be hanging around permanently, so you are free to check it out whenever you fancy.

We'll start with Dave the Diver. As part of the crossover, the game will net a brand-new minigame, where you'll have to pick up a pickaxe and help defend Otto and Sammy's farm from hordes of Core Keeper Cavelings.

You'll be able to dive into the minigame after unlocking the farm through Sammy's VIP event, and there are new charms and figurine rewards for those who play.

The additions aren't quite as grand in Core Keeper, but they're very sweet, nonetheless. Cobra will arrive as a new underground merchant, selling all manner of Dave-themed goodies. There are Momo and Mini Dave pets, a new Bancho-inspired sushi armour, additional furniture items and sound effects, and more.

All of this will arrive in Core Keeper on all platforms on 30th November, while Mintrocket has confirmed that console rollout for the Dave the Diver update might be a little slower (though it will arrive on PC on 30th).
