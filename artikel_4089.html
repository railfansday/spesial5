GTA 6 PS5 Physical Edition Includes a Poster, But It's Still Not the Map

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Retailers are beginning the process of shipping out their boxed copies of GTA 6, which include a digital download code instead of a physical disc. As they receive their stock, it's now been confirmed there is actually a poster inside the case.

However, it's still not the map of the game world previous GTA games have shipped with.

UK retailer The Game Collection has been able to confirm there's a double-sided A3 poster included in the case, as it plans to ship boxed versions later this week. Those who do get the physical version can start preloading the digital edition ready for release on 19th November 2026.

With physical copies now out in the wild, the contents have been posted online, and the poster included has the GTA 6 key art on one side, and the cover girl on the other:

If you want that physical map of the GTA 6 open world, then you'll need to wait until after release. Rockstar has revealed a map poster will be available from its official online store. It's $0.99 if you just want to buy it, or it'll be included free of charge with a purchase of anything else on the storefront.
