Stuff Your Face with PS5 RPG Atelier Karia's Story Trailer, Pre-Orders Live Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Atelier Karia: The Night Kingdom & the Guide of Memories attracted attention recently for including a system where its eponymous protagonist could get fat.

That’s because this upcoming PS5 RPG from Koei Tecmo takes the “you are what you eat” mantra and makes it a gameplay mechanic, with the heroine absorbing all kinds of abilities depending on what you feed her.

It’s a bit like Kirby but with anime waifus – and yes, there’s a swimsuit costume set if you plump up for the Ultimate Edition.

Speaking of which, pre-orders are live now on the PS Store, in the following formats, as per the press release:

The game launches on 25th February, and all purchases made before 10th March will come with an ‘Early Purchase Bonus’ of the ‘Crimson Requiem’ costume and the recipe for ‘Training Badges’. The latter boost your XP gain after battle.
