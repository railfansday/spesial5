Cypherpunk: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Cypherpunk's 25% hash rate target and $56.5 million equity raise show how it plans to fund expansion without selling mined tokens.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 26, 2026 at 3:22PM EST

Management launched its mining operation in August 2026, generating $9.9 million in revenue over its first 44 days. By deploying machines directly, the company produces tokens below market spot prices, adding to its treasury.

Cypherpunk accumulates ZEC tokens through both active mining and open-market purchases. The business raised its holdings to about 2% of total circulating supply, keeping capital allocated toward long-term network exposure.

Management signed a letter of intent (LOI), a preliminary agreement, to purchase up to $30 million in next-generation hardware from Bitmain. The commitment supports the long-term target of capturing 25% of total network computing power.

To fund these initiatives, management raised $56.5 million through an at-the-market (ATM) equity program, an arrangement that lets a company sell shares directly into the public market over time. This structure provides capital for operating expenses so the company does not have to sell the ZEC it mines.
