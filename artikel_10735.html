Pokemon GO leak reveals which Legendary Pokemon would live closest to you

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A Pokemon GO datamine has paired 53 Legendary Pokemon and Ultra Beasts with real-world locations, from Mewtwo in New York City’s Times Square to Kyurem on the Antarctic Peninsula.

The list comes from The Pokemod Group, which first spotted a reference to “WorldwidePokestop” in the game’s data. The group followed up with a list of Pokemon and their associated landmarks, including volcanoes, parks, and observatories. The dataminers said they’re still unsure how the locations will be used in-game.

One of the pairings has already appeared in Pokemon GO. Players at the July 9 anniversary event could catch a Mewtwo with a Times Square background, a reward that drew backlash from players who missed out. One of those Mewtwo later sold for thousands on eBay.

The United States has nine entries, including Raikou at Niagara Falls on the Canadian border, while Japan has four and Australia has three. Several landmarks fit the Pokemon paired with them. Fire-type Entei gets the Silvestri Craters on Italy’s Mount Etna, while the muscle-bound Buzzwole gets Venice Beach in Los Angeles.

Two entries are less precise. Pokemod gave both Bermuda and the Cayman Islands for Nihilego, and each has a Crystal Caves attraction. Blacephalon’s entry specifies a Quarry Wharf in New South Wales, which may refer to the site on the Moruya River.

Earlier Pokemod findings offer a clue about what the landmarks might be for.

The group previously found references to Location Cards, the artwork that can appear behind a Pokemon on its summary screen, for all 53 Pokemon in the new list. Those earlier findings also included Latias, Latios, Poipole, and Naganadel, whose locations remain unknown.
