7 Brew adds food to its menu for the first time with five new $3 treats

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

7 Brew is expanding its menu beyond drinks for the first time, introducing five new bakery items inspired by some of its most popular coffee flavors.

The drive-thru coffee chain launched its new food lineup on November 1, giving customers the option to grab a snack alongside their usual coffee, energy drink, or other beverage.

The expansion comes as 7 Brew continues to grow across the US, with the company introducing the bakery treats alongside six dessert-inspired drinks for November.

The new bakery menu includes five items, three of which take inspiration from existing 7 Brew drinks. Each treat costs $3 and is available at participating locations.

“Our guests have been asking for craveable snacks to accompany their favorite 7 Brew drinks, and we wanted to do more than simply check a box,” said Nick Chavez, Chief Marketing Officer at 7 Brew.

“We wanted to create bakery treats so good that people would go out of their way to get them. Our new lineup delivers exactly that.”

Alongside the food launch, 7 Brew is featuring six dessert-inspired beverages throughout November as part of its “Joy in Every Sip” lineup.

The seasonal selection includes a Pecan Pie Breve with Almond Roca, Irish cream, and salted caramel, as well as a French Silk Mocha combining dark chocolate and vanilla.

Other drinks include a Tiramisu Cold Brew with salted caramel, almond, and chocolate macadamia nut, an Amaretto 7 Brew Energy with blackberry, almond, and cherry, a Butter Brew 7 Fizz Soda with Almond Roca and salted caramel, and a Cinnamon Roll Cocoa featuring brown sugar cinnamon and white chocolate.

While the drinks are being featured throughout November, 7 Brew confirmed that French Silk and Tiramisu will become permanent flavor options at all stands.

7 Brew isn’t the only coffee chain bringing its signature drink flavors into food. Dunkin’ recently partnered with General Mills to introduce three coffee-inspired cereals, including Cinnamon Toast Crunch Cinnamon Caramel Latte, Reese’s Puffs Peanut Butter Mocha, and Golden Grahams Vanilla Graham Latte.

The limited-edition cereals are scheduled to arrive in December 2026, with the Golden Grahams variety available exclusively at Walmart.
