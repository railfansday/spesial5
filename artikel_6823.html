Why Taylor Swift Hasn’t Been Seen at Travis Kelce’s Chiefs-Bills Thanksgiving Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Travis Kelce and the Kansas City Chiefs are celebrating Thanksgiving with their prime-time football game against the Buffalo Bills at Highmark Stadium in Orchard Park, New York.

While Taylor Swift has attended all of her husband’s home games this season, the singer wasn’t spotted this evening. As of 9:51 P.M., no footage or photos of her have surfaced. The couple has kept the rest of their holiday plans private.

Swift hasn’t been seen at any of Kelce’s away games for the last two seasons, due in part to security risks. The star has maintained a low profile this week as well. She made her last public appearance at her husband’s game against the Arizona Cardinals on Sunday, where she cheered him on alongside her parents at Kansas City’s Arrowhead Stadium.

In December 2023, Swift addressed the attention she gets at her partner’s games. “I don’t know how they know what suite I’m in,” she told Time. “There’s a camera, like, a half-mile away, and you don’t know where it is, and you have no idea when the camera is putting you in the broadcast, so I don’t know if I’m being shown 17 times or once.”

“I’m just there to support Travis,” she added. “I have no awareness of if I’m being shown too much and pissing off a few dads, Brads, and Chads.”
