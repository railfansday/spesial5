This ‘DWTS’ Pro Accidentally Revealed He Was Told “Not to Discuss” Ezra Frech and Hailey Bills Dating Rumors

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The rest of the cast clearly cannot stop teasing them.

No matter how much Ezra Frech and Hailey Bills try to avoid dating rumors, their Dancing With the Stars co-stars aren’t letting them escape.

During a TikTok livestream on the official DWTS account earlier this week, Alan Bersten and Brandon Armstrong were tasked with interviewing backstage at the ballroom—and they wasted no time in teasing Hailey about her rumored romance with Ezra.

According to screen recordings reposted by fans, Alan and Brandon jokingly cornered Hailey as she laughed and tried running off to her mark.

“The director just told us not to mention anything about relationships, so we won’t,” Alan said, poking fun at the situation before he reassured her, “What I’m saying is we won’t.”

This isn’t the first time Hailey—or Ezra—has been teased about the dating rumors during DWTS’s TikTok livestream. Earlier this season, Emma Slater and Sharna Burgess tried being Hailey’s wingwomen by bringing up the Paralympian unprompted.

“Do people want to see Ezra Frech?” Sharna asked, to which Hailey responded, “They probably do!” Emma then turned the attention to Hailey, asking, “How much do you wanna see Ezra Frech?”

“Me? I would love to see Ezra Frech!” Hailey responded between laughs, while Emma winked, “Ezra, Hailey would love to see you!”

What’s more: Sharna and Emma attempted to get some intel on Ezra’s relationship status during the same livestream, though Ezra confirmed he was “single” and “locked in on dancing.”

Sharna further pressed him, jokingly asking if he would be “interested in that changing.” He doubled down, saying, “I’m locked in on dancing right now. That’s all I’ll say.”

^^^ This all comes after a source previously told The Sun that they are allegedly dating in secret and “plan to reveal their relationship after the season ends.”

“They are being really careful not to post each other right now because they don’t want the chatter about them potentially dating to overtake everything else,” the insider said at the time.

Ezra and Hailey haven’t publicly addressed their rumored romance aside from the occasional teasing from their DWTS castmates, but honestly? Wouldn’t be mad at this pairing!!
