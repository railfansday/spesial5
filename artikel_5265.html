Bitcoin ETF outflows and nearly $3.02 billion in liquidations offer a measurable picture of the latest market damage. But whether the approaching Anthropic IPO has anything to do with it remains unproven.

The Bitcoin price fell to $62,021 on November 15. Meanwhile, US spot funds recorded approximately $1.91 billion in net withdrawals across seven trading sessions from November 5 to November 13.

BlackRock’s IBIT accounted for roughly $1.42 billion of those withdrawals, around 74% of the total. November 13 alone saw approximately $185 million leave the funds.

Ethereum ETFs also recorded about $398 million in net withdrawals during the week ending November 13.

Moreover, Derivatives data from November 5â15 shows approximately $2.56 billion in long liquidations, against $458.75 million in shorts. Combined, that’s around $3.02 billion.

The biggest long-liquidation days were November 13, at $692.16 million, and November 14, at $629.09 million. November 15 added another $165.13 million in wiped-out long positions.

Dan Gambardello drew a comparison with June, claiming the market fell 19% into the SpaceX IPO, bottomed after its listing and then rallied. He sees a potentially similar setup ahead of Anthropic’s planned Thursday listing.
