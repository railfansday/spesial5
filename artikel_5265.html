Why Ethereum faces a liquidity test as Binance reserves drop to annual lows

An Ethereum [ETH] Foundation-linked wallet has dumped 6,700 ETH since Friday, the 13th of November, according to Lookonchain data.

The blockchain analytics firm also observed that four whale-linked wallets withdrew 13,408 ETH in recent hours. Even so, the ETH price was down 0.5% in the past 24 hours and 11.95% over the past week.

According to SoSoValue data, U.S. Ethereum spot ETF flows totaled -$77.23 million as of the 16th of November. This week’s ETF outflows reflected bearish sentiment in the market.

Yet, the exchange ETH reserves were also dropping alongside the price. AMBCrypto reported that the low reserves could help set up a Q3-style rally.

Analyst CryptoOnChain reported that the 7-day average Ethereum reserve on Binance was 2.97 million ETH. This reading put it in the 1st percentile of the past year, with a Z-score of -2.79.

The 7-day average Binance USDT reserve was $29.71 billion, which was once again in the 1st percentile of the past year, with a Z-score of an even more astonishing -3.37.

The values meant that, compared to the past year, the ETH, and USDT reserves were at an annual floor. The Z-score drew further attention to the anomaly; readings beyond ±2.0 are major statistical outliers.

The analyst also noted that the outflows were concentrated on Binance, as all-exchange flows of ETH were only down 2.1% in a month, compared to Binance’s 11.3% drop.

It is possible that the Binance ETH outflows were going toward self-custody or staking. The record staking numbers reflected long-term investor belief despite recent price losses.

Lowered stablecoin reserves also indicated reduced buying power on Binance. Overall, the falling stablecoin and ETH reserve were a cautious combination, but the next price move’s direction is not yet confirmed.

Ethereum co-founder Vitalik Buterin’s push to position ETH as a store of value was another hopeful sign for long-term investors.
