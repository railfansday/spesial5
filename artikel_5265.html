Alphabet shares gained more than 3% as Google introduced two new AI chip variants, intensifying competition with Nvidia.

The new TPU 8tx and 8i Max target secure AI computing and memory-intensive workloads, expanding Google's enterprise AI offering.

GOOGL has rebounded toward $291, but its daily chart remains below key moving averages, leaving the recovery vulnerable.

Alphabet (GOOGL) stock gained momentum on Monday, November 9, as Google’s latest AI chip announcement strengthened its position in the semiconductor market. The company introduced two new Tensor Processing Unit (TPU) variants designed to challenge Nvidia’s dominance in AI computing. The move comes as Google looks to turn its in-house chip technology into a larger cloud business.

Can Google’s expanding AI chip business drive GOOGL stock higher, or will the recent recovery lose momentum?

Google has introduced two new TPU variants, 8tx and 8i Max, as it expands its AI infrastructure offering to enterprise customers. The TPU 8tx targets government agencies and regulated industries that require secure data processing. It combines Google’s chip technology with AMD processors and hardware-level encryption, with Google claiming up to twice the speed of its predecessor.

The TPU 8i Max is designed for memory-intensive AI applications, including coding assistants, mathematical reasoning and drug discovery. Its architecture combines two chips in one package to deliver greater memory and computing capacity.

The announcement strengthens Google’s efforts to compete with Nvidia in the AI accelerator market. More importantly, Google is increasingly making its custom chips available through Google Cloud rather than limiting them to internal workloads.

Google Cloud revenue reached $30.71 billion in Q3 2026, increasing 103% year over year, while TPU system sales made a meaningful contribution to growth.

Alphabet stock is trading near $291.14, after recovering from a sharp decline toward its $270–$280 support zone.

However, the broader technical structure remains weak. GOOGL is trading below its key moving averages, clustered around the $325–$335 region, indicating that the stock has yet to establish a sustained bullish reversal.

The $300 level is the immediate psychological hurdle. A sustained move above it could strengthen recovery momentum, while the $270–$280 region remains an important support area. A breakdown below this demand zone could expose Alphabet to another leg of selling.

Alphabet’s AI chip expansion provides another potential growth driver beyond search advertising and cloud services. Growing enterprise demand for custom processors could strengthen Google Cloud’s competitive position and create additional opportunities to monetize its AI infrastructure.

However, investors still face concerns over rising capital expenditure, cash flow and competition in AI-powered search. Google’s ability to convert infrastructure investment into sustainable revenue and profitability will remain central to its valuation.

Technically, GOOGL needs to reclaim its moving averages to establish a stronger recovery. Until then, the latest rebound may remain a corrective move within a broader downtrend.
