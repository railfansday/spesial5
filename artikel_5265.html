Strategy Inc (NASDAQ: MSTR) has witnessed one of its biggest corrections in recent years. After hitting an all-time high near $540 in late 2025 and rallying above $200 during the first half of 2026, the stock recently regained momentum and retained the critical $100 mark.

While MSTR remains one of Wall Street’s most closely watched Bitcoin-related stocks, the recent sell-off has significantly compressed its valuation despite continued treasury expansion and growing institutional participation. The company’s long-term outlook continues to depend on Bitcoin’s performance, treasury growth, and investor demand for Bitcoin-linked equities.

In this MSTR price prediction for 2026-2040, we analyze the stock’s recent performance, treasury growth, analyst outlook, and whether Strategy Inc can recover from current levels and eventually reach the $500 milestone.

Strategy’s Inc valuation is increasingly driven by its Bitcoin treasury. With more than 843,000 BTC on its balance sheet, Bitcoin price movements, BTC Yield, and treasury growth directly influence MSTR’s value.

As the largest corporate Bitcoin holder, MSTR has become one of the market’s primary Bitcoin-linked equities.

Wall Street increasingly values Strategy Inc based on its ability to grow its Bitcoin treasury rather than its software business. The company prioritizes BTC Yield, Bitcoin-per-share growth, and capital allocation, supported by expanded equity programs, preferred securities offerings, and continued access to capital markets.

Investors continue to monitor financing costs, share issuance, and market liquidity, as these factors affect the company’s ability to fund additional Bitcoin purchases. As more public companies adopt Bitcoin treasury strategies, Strategy’s Inc ability to maintain its scale and grow Bitcoin exposure per share is likely to remain a key driver of MSTR’s valuation.

Strategy stock (MSTR)  has recovered sharply after finding strong buying interest around its lower demand zone, triggering a sustained rebound toward the upper resistance area. However, the rally lost momentum near the major supply zone, leading to a pullback and a period of consolidation.

The stock faces immediate resistance near $125–$130, where a sustained breakout could strengthen recovery momentum toward $140–$150. A broader supply zone remains around $165–$200. On the downside, initial support lies near $110–$105, while the major demand zone is positioned around $100. Holding above the immediate support could help MSTR stabilize and attempt another recovery. However, a breakdown below $100 may expose the stock to renewed selling pressure toward the lower demand area.

Coinpedia forecasts MSTR to trade between $100 and $250 in 2026, with an average price target of $180.

Strategy reported its third-quarter 2026 earnings on October 29, posting $19.53 billion in net income, $20.91 billion in operating income, and diluted EPS of $44.75. However, revenue came in at $125.4 million, below analysts’ expectations of $128.03 million. The results were largely driven by a $20.91 billion fair-value gain on Bitcoin holdings, which reached 848,517 BTC as of October 25. Strategy also reported a $5.07 billion USD reserve, providing approximately 3.4 years of coverage for preferred dividends and interest obligations.

Investors searching for the MSTR stock dividend should note that Strategy does not pay a regular dividend on its common stock. Its preferred security, STRC, carries a 12% annualized dividend rate, with daily accruals beginning in November 2026. The aggregate November dividend is $1.00 per STRC share.

Strategy is facing a critical period as MSCI prepares to remove MSTR from its global equity indexes after the November 30 market close. The decision could trigger passive fund selling, with estimates pointing to approximately 13 million shares potentially affected. More importantly, the exclusion raises questions about Strategy’s ability to maintain its Bitcoin accumulation strategy through equity financing.

Bitcoin’s decline below Strategy’s average acquisition cost of $75,456 adds another challenge. The company held 850,364 BTC as of November 8, following its latest purchase of 1,036 BTC for $81.9 million. However, its latest filing also showed $188.3 million allocated to repurchasing STRC preferred stock, compared with $81.9 million directed toward Bitcoin purchases. Its $4.77 billion cash reserve provides an additional liquidity buffer.

Meanwhile, JPMorgan reported a 36% increase in its MSTR shareholding during Q3, although its filing also disclosed substantial put-option exposure. This makes the development less straightforward as a bullish institutional signal.

Coinpedia’s MSTR price forecasts are based on a combination of historical stock performance, Bitcoin market cycles, treasury growth trends, institutional analyst forecasts, and technical market structure. Since Strategy trades as both a Bitcoin-linked equity and a Nasdaq-listed company, both crypto and traditional market factors were considered.

Strategy Inc remains one of the strongest Bitcoin-linked equities in public markets, with its long-term performance largely dependent on Bitcoin adoption and treasury growth. While short-term volatility and financing risks may continue to pressure the stock, the company’s aggressive accumulation strategy and growing institutional interest could support higher valuations over the coming years. Coinpedia expects MSTR to remain a high-conviction Bitcoin play for long-term investors.

MSTR is the Nasdaq-listed stock of Strategy Inc (formerly MicroStrategy), the world’s largest corporate Bitcoin holder. The stock largely trades as a Bitcoin-linked equity because the company’s valuation is increasingly tied to its BTC treasury.

According to Coinpedia’s MSTR price prediction, the stock could reach a maximum target of $1,000 by 2030 if Bitcoin adoption continues to expand and Strategy maintains its treasury growth strategy.

3. Can MSTR benefit from institutional Bitcoin adoption?

Yes, growing institutional demand for Bitcoin and Bitcoin-linked equities could support MSTR over the long term. Increased corporate adoption and treasury expansion may further strengthen the investment case.

4. What are the major risks for MSTR investors?

Bitcoin volatility, share dilution from capital raises, higher interest rates, and regulatory changes remain the biggest risks. A prolonged decline in Bitcoin prices could significantly pressure MSTR’s valuation.

MSTR offers investors indirect exposure to Bitcoin through a publicly traded stock. Its long-term outlook depends on Bitcoin prices, treasury growth, institutional demand, and the company’s capital allocation strategy.
