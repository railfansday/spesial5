Colette Kress is Nvidia’s current Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer (CFO), who has been appointed Interim CEO following Jensen Huang’s immediate resignation. Kress joined Nvidia in September 2013 and has led the company’s finance operations for more than 13 years. Before joining Nvidia, she spent nearly 25 years in finance leadership roles at major technology companies, including Cisco and Microsoft. During her time at Nvidia, Kress played a key role in managing the company’s finances through its expansion in accelerated computing and artificial intelligence. She will continue serving as CFO while taking on the interim CEO position. Nvidia’s board has started searching for a permanent successor and will consider both internal and external candidates.
