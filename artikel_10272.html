Gemini 4 Carbon is now available in Google Antigravity for AI Pro

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Google shared today that it’s “upgrading models across tiers,” starting with how paid AI Pro ($19.99 per month) users “now have access to Carbon” after previously requiring AI Ultra ($99.99/month). Meanwhile, “Hydrogen will be available to all users as promised.”

As part of this announcement, Google is “resetting Gemini quotas for everyone on Antigravity.” This follows another reset on Saturday after the team introduced caching improvements that “increased the amount you can get done in quota by 22%.”

The Orchestrator in Antigravity was also updated with a better interface for choosing the Orchestrator and Executor models. Go to Settings > Models > Orchestrator Settings.
