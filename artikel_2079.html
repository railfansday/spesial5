Blackburn Rovers fans should be excited but worried at new Andri Gudjohnsen exploits

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Blackburn have had a positive season under Tony Mowbray, with the side sitting in mid-table going into the international break.

Many had tipped Rovers to be fighting relegation before a ball was kicked this season, but they are just three points away from the play-off places, and they hold a healthy 11-point advantage above 22nd-placed Burnley.

So, all connected to Blackburn will be delighted at how the campaign has begun, and there will be a belief that they can maintain this form, especially due to the form of striker Andri Gudjohnsen.

Whilst it has been a team effort at Rovers, it’s hard not to single out Gudjohnsen, who is the son of former Chelsea man Eidur, for individual praise.

That’s because he has been superb in front of goal, scoring ten times in 14 games, with a shot conversion rate that is better than anyone in Europe, which includes the likes of Raphinha and Erling Haaland.

The 24-year-old is in the form of his career right now, and he continued that on international duty with Iceland on Friday night, as he scored a hat-trick in their 5-0 win at Bulgaria.

This was Gudjohnsen at his devastating best, as he was once again ruthless in front of goal, and it means he now has nine goals in his last six games in all competitions, which is a remarkable return.

Gudjohnsen will hope to extend his goalscoring run when Iceland host Luxembourg on Monday, but, for Blackburn fans, the focus will be on their big game against Burnley when club football returns.

This is the game that all Rovers fans would’ve looked for as soon as the fixtures were released, and Mowbray’s men will make the short trip to Turf Moor next weekend.

Burnley were expected to be in the mix for a top two finish this season, but they have had a disastrous season so far, with Nickey Hayen having already lost his job, and the terrible collapse against Cardiff last time out shows the size of the task facing Fabio Celestini.

So, Blackburn will head to their bitter rivals with genuine confidence, and if they are to return home with all three points, you get the feeling that Gudjohnsen will have a significant role to play.

His form, combined with Burnley’s defensive woes, means the striker will fancy his chances of making a difference in the derby.

Of course, Gudjohnsen will be focused on Blackburn when he returns from international duty, and he will be determined to help the team close the gap on the top eight.

However, it’s unrealistic to think that his goalscoring exploits won’t have attracted attention, so the January window could be a challenge for Rovers.

Finding a number nine who can score goals is a major challenge for all clubs, but Gudjohnsen is proving himself to be someone who can do just that.

His ball-striking technique is superb, and Gudjohnsen is proving time and time again that his movement and anticipation is excellent, as he knows how to find space in and around the box.
