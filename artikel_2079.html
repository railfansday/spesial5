'From all I hear...' - Insider shares Conor Gallagher 'dressing room' reveal amid Tottenham exit latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Tottenham Hotspur midfielder Conor Gallagher is hugely unfortunate to find himself down the pecking order in North London.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the 26-year-old deserves to keep his place at Spurs long-term.

This comes amid reports that Gallagher could potentially be sold by the Lilywhites in January, with the likes of Leeds, Everton and Aston Villa all keen on him.

Wyness believes that Gallagher is not a potential troublemaker in the Spurs dressing room, and is unlikely to rock the boat due to his lack of football.

The former Atletico Madrid ace has started just three Premier League games so far in 2026-27, and has quickly fallen into a substitute's role.

Tottenham invested heavily in their midfield in the summer, and it has undoubtedly hindered Gallagher as a result.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that it is "strange" that Gallagher doesn't go long without being tipped for an exit from a club.

The ex-Chelsea academy graduate enjoyed several loan spells earlier in his career, but has since spent just two seasons with Atletico, and potentially now just 12 months with Tottenham.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness couldn't fathom why Tottenham would sell Gallagher so soon.

"From all I hear, he's not a problem in the dressing room or with his own ego, et cetera. So I just don't get it," he said.

"Whether the wrong managers are picking him, playing in the wrong way, look, there's a talent there, and he should settle somewhere.

"I remember when Crystal Palace fans were fighting over themselves to try and get him back after his loan. It's just strange."

Wyness also told Football Insider on Friday that Tottenham made a huge mistake by selling young striker Will Lankshear to Middlesbrough in the summer.

The Lilywhites offloaded the starlet for a fee that could rise to £20million, but he has already netted 13 goals in his opening 13 Championship games.

Wyness believes that Lankshear is already proving that he could become a £60m player in future, which would see Spurs potentially miss out on a huge sum of money.

They also have no buy-back clause as part of the sale, something which Wyness feels is simply "unforgivable."
