BREAKING: Michael Carrick sacked as Manchester United prepare for Mauricio Pochettino

Manchester United have sacked Michael Carrick as head coach following a disastrous start to the 2026/27 campaign that has left the club in the Premier League relegation zone.

The former Manchester United midfielder was informed of the decision today after travelling to the Carrington after meeting chief executive Omar Berrada, director of football Jason Wilcox and co-owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe.

Michael Carrick initially took charge on an interim basis after Ruben Amorim was sacked last season. He had a great start in the interim role, guiding Manchester United to third place and securing a return to the Champions League after missing the last two seasons.

His first full season at Old Trafford has been a completely different story.

Manchester United have managed just one Premier League victory from their opening 10 matches, collecting just eight points from five draws and four defeats. Their only win came against Ipswich Town in August, while a disastrous run of results has pushed the club down to 18th in the table.

Sunday’s chaotic late 4-4 draw against Aston Villa was another setback, with United failing to climb out of the relegation zone before the international break.

Manchester United have already identified Mauricio Pochettino as their preferred replacement, with reports indicating that an agreement in principle has been reached with the former Tottenham and Chelsea manager.

The Argentine is currently in charge of the United States national team but is understood to be interested in taking the Old Trafford job.

Pochettino’s contract reportedly includes a release clause worth around £7-7.5 million, which United would need to activate. Talks over personal terms are also understood to be advanced with Pochettino’s camp.

United are now preparing for another change in the dugout, with Pochettino expected to be handed the responsibility of rescuing their season.
