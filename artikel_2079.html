William Saliba earns Arsenal U21s praise as defender continues comeback from injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

William Saliba Arsenal return: William Saliba has made a great impression during his return to action, according to Arsenal’s coaching staff. The U21s manager, David Horseman, praising his attitude and professionalism.

The 25-year-old played the first 45 minutes of Arsenal U21’s 5-2 win at Boreham Wood against Aston Villa on Sunday. Slowly making his way back from his three-and-a-half-month layoff.

Saliba was clearly a quality player, but it was his attitude which impressed Horseman. He worked well with the younger players and was a great example to follow throughout the morning.

The defender had been scheduled to play, and there were no new injury concerns behind his half-time withdrawal. He will return to the first team group as he continues to build up match fitness.

Saliba was praised by Horseman for his positive attitude since joining the club. His preparation, involvement in team meetings and ability to communicate with the younger players stood out.

He also showed his quality with the ball. His passing range helped the Arsenal attack and he made a good weighted pass that led to Marcell Washington running into space before Andre Harriman-Annous’ first goal.

Saliba didn’t see it as below his level to play in the game and really got into helping the academy players.

It set an example for the younger players in the squad. With an established first teamer keeping up the standards, there wasn’t much of an excuse for anyone else not to.

Saliba was not the only senior player to feature, with Marli Salmon as captain and Max Dowman also playing. Dowman, Maalik Hashi and Evan Mooney all scored, and Harriman-Annous added two goals.

It was another key step in Saliba’s return, as he featured in the matchday squad for Arsenal’s game against Slavia Prague after playing 45 minutes in the behind-closed-doors friendly.

He wasn’t selected in the senior squad for their game against Hull City. With only nine subs allowed in the Premier League, Arsenal decided it was best to play him for the U21s.

The decision means that Saliba has been able to play competitive minutes to build up his fitness.

Arsenal will be hoping that he can now make a full recovery from his injury so that Mikel Arteta has another defensive option heading into a busy period for the club.

However, the U21s will have to maintain their high standards from the Aston Villa game without first team players such as Saliba, Salmon and Dowman.
