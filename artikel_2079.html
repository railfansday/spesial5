The Young Lions kicked off the first of two Elite League matches this month with a stunning 6-1 victory on Thursday.

England men’s under-20s in action at the home of Mansfield Town.

Kicking off the November international window with this emphatic 6-1 win over Czechia.

The Young Lions maintained their perfect record in the Men’s U20 Euro Elite League and were 4-0 up at half-time.

Before then easing to an eventual 6-1 scoreline with two more goals in the second half and Czechia with just one late consolation goal of their own.

There were two goals each for  Shea Lacey and Shumaira Mheuka in the first half.

Then Brooke Nfonkeu-Feuba and Ben Broggio after the break, scoring in the 79th and 91st minutes respectively.

This is proving a great time for young rising Newcastle United star Sean Neave. he came off the bench in the 73rd minute last night with the score 4-0, then showed great promise in the 20 minutes or so he had on the pitch as he helped the Young Lions score a couple more and see out an eventual 6-1 victory in this Elite League match.

This England Under 20s call up for the November internationals swiftly followed Sean Neave getting his first two goals in senior football. The teenage striker is on loan at Wolves and with them trailing 1-0 at Bristol City, Sean Neave coming off the bench to score the two late goals that totally turned the match around.

The Young Lions now head to Portugal for the second match of this international window for a game on Monday 16 November (4pm GMT) at the Estadio Municipal de Mafra.
