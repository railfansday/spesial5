I Didn't Expect to Love This Prada Cologne. But This Is Why It's My Go-To Fall Scent

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Prada Paradigme looks loud. The cologne inside is surprisingly refined.

WHEN PRADA PARADIGME Eau de Parfum was released last year, the first thing I noticed was the bottle. It has a stark lime-green-and-black design that can best be described as Green Goblin chic, which is ironic considering Spider-Man himself, Tom Holland, fronts its campaign. The fragrance itself, however, is no joke. I dug the scent so much, I quickly featured it at the top of my next new releases column before giving it a 2026 Men’s Health Grooming Award.

Prada clearly felt similarly, as it dropped a more concentrated Prada Paradigme Le Parfum this May. After wearing the OG cologne regularly for over a year, I can confirm my early impression of it wasn’t t misguided. Here’s how the Eau de Parfum performed over 12 months and how the new Le Parfum stacks up.

At its core, Paradigme is an amber-woody cologne, which is definitely up my alley. But it’s also bold and spicy—two cologne descriptors I tend to avoid. Paradigme pulls off hitting this intersection of vibes with a scent profile that is refined and powerful yet still grounded and casual.

It opens with a warm blend of Peru balsam, benzoin resin, and guaiac wood before settling into a musky finish with some hints of citrusy bergamot and rose. There’s also a lingering bourbon note that I especially appreciate as someone who loves the smell (and taste) of whiskey. In the Le Parfum, the citrus and floral notes come through a bit clearer and definitely stick around longer. But it has the same rich amber-woody base.

To me, this is ideal for wear at a low-lit cocktail bar, that’s potentially haunted in the best possible way. In short, this is what I’d wear if I was hitting up Overlook Hotel’s bar in Stephen King’s The Shining—not to be confused with the Stanley Hotel in Colorado, on which that hotel is based, which I have visited and wore something lighter at (it’s pretty tight in there). Prada Paradigme Le Parfum, on the other hand, is better suited for wandering around the Overlook’s extensive hedge maze.

This more concentrated iteration is best suited for spring and summer, as its deeper floral notes could clash with the cozy vibe of fall and winter. While the EDP is best-suited for the cooler, moodier months, I dig it when temps are high too. There’s enough bergamot and rose in there to reflect the brightness of the season, and the guaiac wood gives it a slight earthy edge that works well during the warmer months. I’m also someone who gladly enjoys sipping on bourbon when it’s 90 degrees out, so my view may be skewed.

After applying the cologne with two to three sprays to my neck, I get about eight to nine hours of quality wear. The Le Parfum lasts about an hour longer and maintains its intensity more strongly than the EDP. It also builds more in intensity over the first couple of hours before plateauing, whereas the EDP settles after about 45 minutes and stays consistent for the next few hours. I usually stick to two sprays with the Le Parfum, as the projection is stronger.

Prada Paradigme Eau de Parfum strikes a rare balance between bold and easygoing, blending amber woods, spice, bergamot, rose, and bourbon. It feels refined without being stuffy, works across seasons, and lasts roughly eight to nine hours on my skin—making it an easy everyday fragrance with enough personality for nights out. If you’re looking for something stronger and slightly spicier, Prada Paradigme Le Parfum is the pick. It lasts longer, but the scent isn’t as well-suited for year-round wear as the OG.
