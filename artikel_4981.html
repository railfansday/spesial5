<b><strong>Alo Super Sleek Bra Tank Review: A Fitness Editor’s Honest Thoughts</strong></b>

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

I’ve worn this top on runs, at the gym, and beyond.

As a fitness editor, I’ve racked up quite the collection of built-in-bra tanks and sports bras for working out. And while they all do their job, there’s one that I love more than the others: my Alo Super Sleek Bra Tank. The main reason? It’s versatile—but also because it’s incredibly stylish and flattering.

I am a big believer in the bra-top combo. It’s way easier to slide on something with a built-in bra and go (and it even helps reduce my ever-growing pile of laundry). But, over the past year, this one has outperformed all the rest and become my go-to top for all kinds of workouts. Whether I’m heading to barre, getting ready to lift, or lacing up my sneakers for a run, I know this top will do exactly what I need it to.

I’ve worn the Super Sleek Bra Tank in quite a few contexts: to the gym, to different types of workout classes, to physical therapy, and even while running. It’s also cute enough and offers enough coverage to wear around town running errands, but I tend to reserve it for working out.

While I wouldn’t usually trust something with a built-in bra on its own for higher-impact workouts, the Alo Super Sleek Bra Tank can handle it. Trust me, I’ve worn it for a 10K race. It’s got enough compression and support to get me through the miles. To be fair, I don’t have the highest demand when it comes to sports bras (if you know what I mean), but thanks to the support features, I think quite a few chest sizes could wear this as a standalone bra-tank combo.

When it comes to other kinds of workouts (like a high-energy class or lifting on my own), the high-compression fabric moves with me through every twist and bend, offering the right balance of stretch and support. It also stays put and requires minimal adjusting after big movements, if at all.

This top is made with Alo’s signature Airlift material, which is made from a sleek blend of durable polyester and stretchy elastane—aka it offers high compression with just enough stretch. Compared to that super soft material you might get in a comfy yoga pant, this top feels more like what you’d get in a classic athletic compression short. It’s a bit thicker, slicker—and it is opaque, so you can feel comfortable wearing it around in public.

The compressive material is also why the top is so supportive. It fits snugly to the body, keeping everything locked in place, while also managing to not feel restrictive or too tight. It also has removable pads (which I lost in the washing machine months ago!), but I’ve found that I don’t need them because the material is so snug. To note: Because it has a tighter fit, the brand recommends that you size up if you have a bigger cup size. (My usual size worked just fine for me.)

Part of the reason I wore this for my 10K race was simply that it looks better on me than just about every other workout tank I own. I think that’s mostly thanks to the compression of the garment, which gives it a smoothing effect and helps make me look snatched.

It has a low scoop neck cut with a racerback silhouette. The longline fit hits a bit lower on your torso than a regular bra would, giving it the look of a cropped tank with the fit of a supportive bra. I love that I can throw this on without worrying about an extra layer and feel a little more covered up than when I head to the gym in a regular sports bra. That said, sometimes I wish it were a bit longer, but most of my workout bottoms are high-waisted, so it ends up feeling long enough.

If you are looking for a bra-tank combo, the Alo Super Sleek Bra Tank is just about the top of the line. The garment’s high-compression, stretchy fit is perfect for a wide range of activities, while still staying comfortable and looking cute. My next move? Buying this top in every color.
