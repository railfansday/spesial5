Influencer & Media Manager at Yodo1

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

You will: Build Spicy Koala's reputation as a credible publisher and curator of premium indie PC and console games through effective PR, media relations, creator partnerships and community development.

Must have: A current network of influential media and content creators and demonstrable experience of managing marketing communications, as well as building gaming communities.

At Yodo1, we don't just build and grow games, we turn them into global successes that become household names.

With 14+ years in the games space, we've reached 3.5 billion+ players and supported 6,000+ developers worldwide. From games like Transformers: Earth Wars, Rodeo Stampede and Ski Safari, to bringing iconic IPs from Hasbro, AMC, Netflix, Paramount and Sony Pictures to leading studios like Rivergame, Candivore, 37Games and Tencent Studios, we connect creativity with global expertise.

Our work spans game development, publishing, IP licensing and ad monetization across mobile, PC and console. We build games, grow franchises and help developers reach players worldwide. We love games. We play games. And together, we grow games that last.

We believe in the power of games and that everyone deserves the opportunity to build a life they love, from anywhere in the world.

We're fully remote by design. We embrace Work From Anywhere, with no fixed hours or limits on time off. We trust our people to own their work and deliver results.

We're driven by performance, curiosity, and continuous improvement. We experiment often, challenge assumptions and value impact over titles.

Yodo1 is seeking an experienced, commercially minded Influencer & Media Manager with a track record of generating positive coverage via their current, active network of games media and content creators. Must be able to provide evidence of successfully establishing, growing and actively engaging player communities on Discord and Steam Communities.

Focused on impact. You understand the 80/20 rule and focus on activities that matter.

Performance-driven and growth-oriented. You have an intrinsic will to succeed while unafraid to try, fail, and learn by experimenting with new approaches and testing ideas.

Cooperative. You help others succeed; you give and receive feedback well.

Humble. You ask for help; you are quick to admit and learn from your mistakes.

SMART. You work and communicate in ways that are Specific, Measurable, Actionable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

Develop and execute PR and communications strategies for the publishing label and individual game releases, working closely in partnership with our Social Media Manager to effectively promote our gaming portfolio and achieve strong commercial launches.

Build and maintain relationships with games media, journalists, influencers and content creators to secure coverage, reviews, features and creator-led promotion.

Create compelling stories and narratives around our games, developers and releases, translating their distinctive qualities into effective press releases, announcements and promotional opportunities.

Plan and coordinate PR activity & creator campaigns around key milestones, including reveals, demos, Early Access launches and full releases.

Establish, grow and actively engage player communities on Discord and Steam Community, where appropriate, creating sustained engagement before and after release.

Work closely with developers and internal publishing, production and marketing teams to ensure consistent messaging, maximise visibility and respond to player feedback.

Monitor coverage, creator activity, community sentiment and campaign performance, using insights to improve reach and engagement.

An active, current network of games media contacts and content creators, with demonstrable evidence of having recently leveraged these relationships to secure coverage, exposure or content creation for games you have represented.

Practical, hands-on experience of planning, hosting and delivering Steam livestreams.

Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with experience producing press releases and communicating effectively with games media and content creators. Equally, must be comfortable establishing, developing and actively engaging gaming communities, primarily through Discord and Steam.

A strong understanding of the PC and console games market, indie developers and gaming audiences.

A proactive, hands-on approach, comfortable working across multiple games and release campaigns in a small, growing publishing team.

Compensation. We pay based on qualifications, experience, and fit for the role. We provide an allowance to purchase your own device or equipment and offer variable compensation through our annual bonus program.

Growth. We offer an environment where you will have the trust and freedom to try, fail, learn fast, and make a real impact while accelerating your career, and where anyone with a great idea can pitch it directly to our management team.

WFA. Work from anywhere in the world, travel when and where you like, get paid in your currency of choice, and enjoy flexible hours and unlimited paid time off.

Community. Work with an energetic, fast-learning, supportive, and diverse team of Yodies across six continents and 30+ countries who share and support new ideas and get things done.

New remote jobs delivered to your inbox, every week.

Location independence is the future of work. It serves companies with best talent from around the globe. And benefits employees with a greater work life balance and the ability to work in their pyjamas.- Nithur, founder.
