Affirm: Compensation Analyst I

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most.

As part of Affirm’s Early Career Program, you’ll join a community of early career employees and receive dedicated support as you grow your career. You’ll be paired with a mentor from your team and collaborate in person three days a week from our New York office.

Throughout your first year, you’ll also take part in learning opportunities, networking events, and social activities with your ECP community. Past activities have included things like escape rooms, boat tours, mini golf, and other opportunities to connect with fellow early career employees outside of your day-to-day work. After your first year, you’ll have the flexibility to remain hybrid, transition to remote work, or continue working from an office.

The People team helps Affirmers cultivate a high-performing, inclusive organization. We focus on three core areas: attracting talent aligned to our mission, enabling growth through people and team development, and retaining employees through thoughtful experiences and rewards. We develop Affirm’s people programs and systems to create a workplace where all employees can thrive and contribute meaningfully. By investing in our people, we create a long-term advantage for the business.

The Compensation Programs & Partnerships (P&P) team designs, executes, and maintains some of the people programs and partnerships that enables Affirm to provide thoughtful experiences and rewards to Affirmers.

We’re excited to launch our early career program and are seeking someone passionate about learning, growing, and contributing within the Compensation area to help drive Affirm’s success. In this role, you’ll have the opportunity to build your knowledge of Affirm’s business functions, partner closely with the broader Compensation team on impactful initiatives, and build strong cross-functional partnerships through hands on projects that allow you to create scalable solutions that make a difference in the day-to-day experiences of our employees.

You’ll report directly to the Senior Manager of Compensation and be paired with an experienced compensation partner who will provide guidance, share their expertise, and help you navigate your early career while giving you the opportunity to take ownership of projects and bring your own ideas to the table.

Come join us if you’re excited about embarking on a new program that drives meaningful outcomes.
