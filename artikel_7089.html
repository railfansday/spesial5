TRM Labs: Global Investigator, Asset Recovery

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

TRM Labs provides AI-powered intelligence solutions that help public and private sector agencies investigate and disrupt crime. TRM's platforms enable investigators to trace illicit activity, build cases, and construct operating pictures of threat networks. Leading agencies and businesses worldwide rely on TRM to make the world safer and more secure.

The Scam Investigations team is TRM’s tip of the spear against pig butchering syndicates, romance fraud networks, and investment scam operations that steal billions from victims each year. The team operates an integrated workflow — from AI-supported complaint triage to time-sensitive investigation, escalation, and recovery support for victims.

As a Global Investigator, you will work at the recovery-focused layer of scam investigation. You will validate high-priority blockchain leads, conduct forward and backward tracing, assess whether funds touch regulated platforms or other actionable endpoints, and produce clear investigative documentation that supports time-sensitive escalation and victim-centered recovery outcomes.

Support time-sensitive asset-preservation workflows with regulated platforms and ecosystem partners.

Own the recovery side. After a freeze, run the backtrace that ties frozen funds to specific victims and produce the documentation that drives restitution. The freeze is the start. Victim recovery is the goal.

Trace victim funds end-to-end — forward to the wallets that consolidate them, backward to the platforms they came from. Your work feeds directly into the legal packages that recover them.

Validate time-sensitive blockchain signals and move high-confidence matters through the appropriate escalation workflow. Speed and accuracy both matter.

Support other teams on time sensitive issues that might be unforeseen

React timely to the movement of funds during an ongoing investigation
