Kroger Names Melanie Zimmermann to Lead Advertising Business

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Kroger’s advertising business has named its next leader, who joins the division after it recently reported record growth.

Kroger Precision Marketing has named Melanie Zimmermann to the role of group vice president. She succeeds Christine Foster, who joined The Home Depot’s Orange Apron Media as gm in September.

Zimmermann joins from Criteo, where she served as gm of retail media business strategy and client solutions. Before Criteo, she spent more than a decade at Macy’s and was vp of Macy’s Media Network from 2020 to 2023.

Mitchell Parton is ADWEEK's senior commerce reporter, covering retail media as well as the rise of agentic and social commerce. Before joining ADWEEK in 2026, he was a reporter at Digiday Media's Modern Retail and before that wrote for several regional publications in Texas.
