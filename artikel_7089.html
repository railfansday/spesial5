Come and See: Product Engineer, Full Stack

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Great stories only matter when they reach people, and you build the way they get there. If you love shipping software that holds up in production and see engineering as a way to serve people well – we’d love to meet you.

Come and See is the faith-based nonprofit media ministry behind The Chosen. Our mission is inviting everyone to encounter Jesus through exceptional storytelling that brings the Bible to life. Our Team Members work together as collaborators in the greatest mission on earth.

The Product Engineer, Full Stack will build and ship features across our streaming apps on web, mobile, and connected TV, the platform services behind them, and the tools our partners rely on, working with Product and Design from user problem to production. This is a full-time, remote role open to candidates in the USA.

BONUS POINTS: Mobile development (Kotlin or Swift), connected TV platform experience, nonprofit or ministry/faith-based organizational experience

BENEFITS: Medical, dental, vision, retirement match, HRA, disability and life insurance, generous and flexible time off, holidays, parental leave, and more.

At Come and See, every staff member is a critical and valuable part of our mission and ministry. We consider ministry readiness and an individual’s capacity to represent our culture and Christian beliefs during the selection process for all staff positions. An essential function within every position held by a staff member at Come and See is to uphold and represent Jesus in how we live and behave.

While we unite around our mission, we know unity doesn’t mean uniformity. Our calling is too great, and our mission is too important not to be intentional about strengthening our team with people from all backgrounds. We believe that varied perspectives—across race, ethnicity, life experience, age, and gender—enrich our ability to share the story of Jesus with the world and help everyone, everywhere, experience the authentic Jesus.

Come and See is an equal opportunity employer and as a religious organization, lawfully exercises its right under Title VII and applicable state law to employ individuals who share its religious beliefs.
