Gevo, Inc. (GEVO -5.44%) reported record third quarter results, achieving profitability and positive operating cash flow through the expansion of its carbon credit and low-carbon fuel operations. Management stated that the company is transitioning from a project-dependent model to a diversified operating platform focusing on capital efficiency and asset optimization. The company is prioritizing near-term debottlenecking and ethanol capacity expansion at its North Dakota facility over immediate large-scale investments in sustainable aviation fuel (SAF). Management reported that this strategy allows the company to strengthen its balance sheet and fund future growth using cash generated from operations.
