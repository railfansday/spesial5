Your FitBit Versa and Sense smartwatches will lose some features this month – what to know

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Time's running out for some Versa and Sense models. After Nov. 28, you can no longer install or update third-party apps or clockfaces on these Google smartwatches.

If you have a Fitbit Versa or Fitbit Sense smartwatch, you’ll soon be unable to update the third-party apps on these devices. Google announced on Monday an update to its older lineup of devices that may signify the beginning of obsolescence. Users can no longer install or update third-party apps or clocks on these Fitbit smartwatches, beginning on Nov. 28.

The apps will continue to work after that date, but if users delete any of these apps, they will not be able to reinstall them. The changes affect the Fitbit Sense and Fitbit Sense 2, as well as the Fitbit Versa, Fitbit Versa Lite, Fitbit Versa 2, Fitbit Versa 3, and Fitbit Versa 4.

Google noted that users will still have access to Fitbit- and Google-developed apps and clocks, and that they should update to the latest app versions to keep those apps functioning properly.

The Nov. 28 support cut-off comes right on the heels of this season’s Black Friday sales. (Google’s Pixel Watch 5 just got a $100 discount at Amazon.)

So the smartwatches aren’t dead — they can still perform the same primary functions, like recording workouts or receiving or responding to messages and calls. Still, the news is a downer for Versa and Sense users who’ve been using their smartwatches and the many third-party apps on them for anywhere from two to eight years now.

All signs point to these devices being slowly phased out in favor of Google’s latest lineup of fitness trackers and smartwatches.

Within the past few years, Google has unveiled an impressive new lineup of health trackers, including the screenless Fitbit Air, the screened Fitbit Edge, and the Pixel Watch 5. For those looking to ditch their Versa or Sense, a few affordable options exist in the ecosystem to take their place.

The earliest and most recent Fitbit Versa models were launched in 2018 and 2022, respectively, and the first and last Fitbit Sense models launched in 2020 and 2022, respectively — making the oldest of these devices eight years old.
