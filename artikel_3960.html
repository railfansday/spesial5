Capcom Is Delisting Exoprimal In A Few Months, Then Killing It In 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Can't believe the dinosaurs are going extinct, again!

Capcom has today announced that its dinosaur PvPvE shooter Exoprimal will end service on September 29, 2027, meaning it will no longer be playable after that date. The game is also set to be delisted from digital storefronts, including the Xbox Store, on March 30, 2027.

"We regret to announce that service for Exoprimal will be discontinued as of 8:00 PM (PDT) on Wednesday, September 29, 2027."

Exoprimal launched on Xbox Series X|S on July 14, 2023, and suffered a pretty pants opening with only 4,995 players logging in through Steam to play Capcom's new team-based shooter.

Following Capcom's mighty 2026 releases, Resident Evil Requiem and Onimusha: Way of the Sword, I wanted to understand what went wrong for Exoprimal, so I hopped back on. Funnily enough, I was actually enjoying the game, despite it being a ghost town. Only nine players were active at the time, and it really felt like it. It's still a fun title though, which makes the end of service announcement quite sad, at least for me.
