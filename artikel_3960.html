DOOM: The Dark Ages and Horde Mode Is a Match Made in Heaven

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Available for DLC owners from 16th November

id Software has revealed it's updating DOOM: The Dark Ages with a horde mode next week, called Slaughter Mode. It's a free addition, but there's a catch: you have to own this year's Revelations expansion to access it.

Slaughter Mode releases on Monday, 16th November 2026, and brings with it "an all-new endless survival challenge designed to put even the most seasoned Slayers to the test". It's got randomised enemies, bosses, and arenas, and the ability to record your personal high scores and compete in weekly challenges.

The trailer confirms there are 19 arenas in total, and every round of every run features a new combination of enemies to defeat. At checkpoints, you can cash out your total earnings so far in exchange for rewards. Or, you can risk losing everything by continuing your run and trying for a higher score.

You'll mostly be unlocking new character and weapon skins, profile icons, and music.

This looks to be the full Dark Ages combat system placed into Slaughter Mode, so after a fantastic base game and supposedly even better DLC in Revelations, the title provides even more reason to return.
