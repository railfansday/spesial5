Four OG Xbox Games Are Getting Achievements For The First Time Ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

"We’re introducing one of the top requested features"

It's time for achievements in original Xbox games! Microsoft is officially rolling them out for four games today, with more to come over time. They're referring to this as "one of the top requested features" from fans in recent memory.

The four games that are getting Xbox achievements today are as follows:

You'll be able to access achievements for these games when playing them on Xbox One, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S - and the same applies for PC as well, as they're all now part of the PC backwards compatibility programme.

News 21 Original Xbox Games Are Coming To PC Via Backwards Compatibility Today (November 12)

There's one key thing you'll need to do to see these achievements though - update your Xbox console by going to Settings, System and then Updates. If you see a new update ready to download in there, you'll need to grab it.

Microsoft says that more original Xbox games will be getting achievements in the future, and that they've worked with the developers and publishers of these initial four titles to craft their achievement lists. If you've already made progress in any of the aforementioned games, you may find that some achievements pop automatically.

We'll leave you with a few more details on what Xbox is saying about them:

"The addition of achievements also apply when you play these games on XBOX One and XBOX Series consoles. Make sure to take the latest system update so you can start earning them. In some cases, achievements may unlock based on progress you’ve already made. We worked closely with each game’s developers and publishers as we designed the achievements to make sure they fit the spirit of each title. We plan to bring achievements to more original XBOX games over time."

"The games you’ve played are part of your history with XBOX. Bringing more of them to PC and adding achievements are a few ways we’re ensuring you can continue to play the games you love, wherever you may choose."
