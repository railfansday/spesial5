Imax Ups Giovanni Dolci To Newly Created COO Role, Mark Welton To Head Strategic Partnerships

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Imax has promoted a pair of executives to newly created roles as it expands its leadership.

Giovanni (Gio) Dolci was named the big screen exhibitor’s first chief operating officer, reporting to CEO Rich Gelfond. The executive, currently based in London as EVP and chief commercial officer, will transfer to New York with expanded oversight of Global Theatres and Technology in addition to his current leadership of the Global Sales, and Streaming and Consumer Technology teams.

Dolci joined Imax in 2012 as managing director of Europe and Africa and chief sales officer. Previously, he was Director of Business Development and Commercial Operations at Arts Alliance Media and worked in film financing in the U.K., Italy and New Zealand. Watch on Deadline

“Imax is a stronger, more global business than ever, and we continue to evolve and grow our leadership team to capture the many opportunities ahead,” said Gelfond. “Gio has a deep understanding of our business, our partners, and our people at every level. He’s been central to our growth for more than a decade, and his leadership will be critical in this next era of success.”

Imax also promoted Mark Welton, currently President, Imax Global Theatres, to the newly created role of President, Strategic Partnerships, also reporting to Gelfond. Welton will lead partnerships outside Imax’s core theatrical business, working closely with the company’s content team on initiatives across sports, events, and consumer brands.

The 30-year Imax veteran has led theater operations and key partner relationships worldwide including the NHL, MLB and NBCUniversal, which partnered with Imax to show the opening ceremony of the 2024 Paris Olympics. “Mark has been instrumental in building IMAX, and few people know our company and industry like he does,” said Gelfond. “He is exactly the right person to turn the strength of our brand into new sources of growth.”

Imax has been on a tear over the past few years as PLFs and event cinema became a key part of the post-Covid theatrical experience for consumers and studios. The company reported strong quarterly earnings earlier this month powered by The Odyssey.
