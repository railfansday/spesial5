Following recent allegations of sexual assault, Investigation Discovery is now releasing a four-part docuseries about “manosphere” influencer Andrew Tate.

Titled Andrew Tate: Toxic Empire, Rolling Stone reported that “The new series will attempt to trace [Tate’s] evolution, talking with insiders, including men who participated in the War Room — the high-priced, private network Tate promoted, where he spoke directly with followers — as well as women who were in his orbit.”

The first episode airs Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 9 p.m. ET, followed by the second at 10 p.m. The remaining episodes will air over the next two Wednesdays. You’ll need access to Investigation Discovery (ID) to watch. But if you don’t have cable, you can still tune in with a free trial through DirecTV or Philo. Sling and Hulu + Live TV are other paid streaming options, which we’ll break down below.

The series has already sparked online conversation, with some Reddit users expressing hope that “this is a serious one,” compared to other Andrew Tate documentaries. Whether you’re curious about the investigation or want a closer look at the allegations, you can stream Andrew Tate: Toxic Empire through DirecTV, Philo, Sling, or Hulu + Live TV. Here’s a full list of each service’s plans and prices.

Investigation Discovery is easy to access through DirecTV, with the channel included in several of its signature and genre packages. You’ll also get a five-day free trial, after which plans start at $34.99 per month.

Philo is another great option, offering one of the longest free trials at seven days. After the trial, you’ll pay $25 per month for the Essentials plan to keep access to the channel.

Sling offers a better long-term option compared to some of the other platforms. While it doesn’t offer a free trial, you can access Investigation Discovery through its Blue tier, starting at $45.99 per month.

Hulu + Live TV doesn’t currently offer a free trial, but it’s one of the best options if you want to bundle your streaming services, including ESPN and Disney+. Plans start at $89.99 per month with ads.
