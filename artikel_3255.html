PPL Corporation (PPL -0.56%) raised its long-term annual earnings growth target and updated its four-year capital investment plan to $25 billion. Management reported that the higher growth is supported by an expanded investment program primarily funded by large load customers. The company reached an agreement with QTS to provide 2 gigawatts of generation capacity for a data center project in Pennsylvania, which management described as a significant milestone for the Invitium Energy joint venture. Management stated that the project uses a regulated-like risk profile with a 20-year initial term and cost reimbursement provisions. The company is also targeting further upside in Kentucky, where additional generation needs could increase the compound annual growth rate for earnings per share to over 9% through 2030.
