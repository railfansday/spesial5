It's been another excellent year for gaming with new hardware, accessories and so many essential new games that playing them all is practically impossible. If you're not immersed in the world of gaming, knowing what the best gaming gifts are can be extremely overwhelming.

That's why I've done the research for you. I'm one of Tom's Guide's gaming experts, and I've picked out a selection of gifts that any gamer would love to receive this year. I've got options with a range of prices, from affordable picks to premium products, with each broken down in simple categories.

Whether the gamer in your life plays on PC, PlayStation, Nintendo, Xbox or mobile, these are the best gaming gifts in 2026.

Looking for more gift inspiration? Check out our guide to the best holiday gifts.

Choose to follow your gaming destiny with the powerful Victus Gaming Laptop leading you to immersive, fun-filled experiences. Enjoy playing with great, stable performance with an Intel Core processor and NVIDIA GeForce RTX Laptop GPU. OMEN Gaming Hub's performance toolkit elevates play with software enhancements and personalized hardware control.

The Xbox Series X remains the pinnacle of Microsoft's console family. It's a powerful, chunky black box that can run the latest games at 4K resolution at 60 frames per second without breaking a sweat. Plus, Xbox Game Pass is the best subscription service in gaming. It also offers the most robust backwards compatibility features of any console, letting you experience classic games from every Xbox generation.

Thanks to unwelcome price hikes, the Xbox Series S isn't quite the budget-friendly machine it was once. Nevertheless, it's still the cheapest current-generation console on the market. It plays all the same games as its more powerful Xbox Series X sibling, but with some graphical sacrifices. It's a great pick if you don't want to spend eyewatering sums on a console, and is the cheapest way to play Grand Theft Auto VI in 2026 — which is an especially big selling point.

A second controller is always useful for co-op gaming on Xbox consoles. You could get the standard black or white model, but the Forza Horizon 6 Limited Edition is way snazzier. Released in conjunction with the latest Forza game, its neon-bright yellow/pink/blue color scheme celebrates the title's vast open-world map of Japan. It also features translucent plastic, which is clearly back in style in 2026.

The latest title in the Gears of War franchise is a prequel set during Emergence Day (aka E-Day), the fateful moment when the Locust hordes first attacked humanity. You play as series stalwart Marcus Fenix alongside his best buddy, Dom Santiago, and a new crew of COG soldiers battling on the front lines of this future war. In TG's Gears of War E-Day review, we gave it an 'Editor's Choice' award, calling it "the must-own Xbox exclusive of 2026."

If the gamer in your life hasn't already picked up Grand Theft Auto VI, then they're probably asking for it for Christmas. It's the long-awaited return of the GTA franchise after 13 years. This blockbuster is set in Vice City and introduces the franchise's first playable female protagonist in the story mode. Note: this is a "code in box" edition; it doesn't come with a physical game disc.

The PS5/PS5 Slim is the best-selling console of this generation and, in 2026, continues to make an excellent case for itself. Its library is overflowing with great games, many in 4K resolution at 60 frames per second. Its DualSense controller is packed with novel features, like haptic feedback and adaptive triggers. Plus, PlayStation is proudly boasting that "PS5 is the best place to play" GTA 6. There's also the PS5 Pro, for those demanding the most powerful console, but finding the already pricey Pro in stock is very difficult at present.

Sony's latest official PlayStation headset is the PlayStation Pulse, not to be confused with the PlayStation Pulse 3D, which arrived back in November 2020 alongside the PS5 console. It's a slick wireless headset with a built-in microphone and full UI integration on PS5. Its look also mirrors the PS5's two-tone aesthetics, so it's a great buy for gamers who want their entertainment setup to match.

The DualSense controller is great, but if you want a proper "pro" pad, the Nacon Revolution 5 Unlimited is designed to give competitive gamers an edge. Its biggest upgrade is the built-in display, which lets you customize any of its seven shortcuts on the fly, and it also sports TMR joysticks, dual-mode actuation triggers, and a docking station for easy charging. It's also compatible with PC.

PS5's big blockbuster exclusive for 2026 is Marvel's Wolverine, the latest superhero romp from Spider-Man developer Insomniac Games. It's quite repetitive, but it's essentially a playable summer blockbuster that does have its appeal. Just be aware, it's not one for younger gamers. Wolverine's claws are used for all manner of violence. Trust me, Wolverine earns its "M" rating.

Grand Theft Auto VI is not just the biggest game of 2026, but the most anticipated game of the whole decade. Sony and Rockstar have confirmed it'll "play best on PlayStation," making the PS5 the place to experience this open-world crime epic 13 years in the making. As with the Xbox version, this is a "code in box" edition, without a physical disc, but it gives you the full game, and an actual box is nicer to wrap than some digital store vouchers.

The Nintendo Switch 2 was recently voted the console you should buy in 2026 by the Tom's Guide staff, and it's not hard to see why. Like its predecessor, it's a hybrid system, letting you play on a TV or undock for portable play on its 7.9-inch LCD display. Now in its second year, the Switch 2's library has really swelled in size, which makes deciding what to play first quite a challenge.

The Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller is a must-have extra if you plan to use your Switch 2 in TV Mode for extended periods. It's the best Switch 2 controller around, offering a traditional button layout, ludicrously long battery life, and even comes with two back buttons. The Joy-Con 2 controllers work well for portable play, but if your Switch 2 is docked, you'll want to play on a Pro pad.

The Nintendo Switch 2's internal storage fills up very fast, with some games requiring more than 50 GB to install. For a portable system, this is a major pain, as you don't want to be deleting and redownloading software all the time. Fortunately, this Samsung 256GB microSD Express Card gives the Switch 2 plenty of additional storage, and comes sporting a charming little Mario design.

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time has been remade for the Nintendo Switch 2, and in a word, it's stunning. This complete remake of the classic Nintendo 64 offers overhauled visuals, improved controls, and a few smart tweaks to make the timeless game even more modern. Ocarina of Time was considered a masterpiece back in 1998, and this new version on Switch 2 has earned the same plaudits.

If you want a Switch 2 game that the whole family can play this holiday season, Nintendo Switch Sports Resort is my top pick. It's a part of the spiritual successor series to Wii Sports, offering a large collection of sports mini-games that use motion controls to mimic the real thing. It's approachable even for non-gamers, and is sure to delight players of all ages. Just don't blame me if it causes a few overly competitive rounds of virtual bowling.

Upgrade your mobile gaming with the excellent Backbone Pro controller. It turns your smartphone into a device resembling the Nintendo Switch 2, giving you greater control of your mobile gaming experience and saving you from trying to play AAA games on a touchscreen. It's super portable, and with 40 hours of battery, the Backbone Pro will always have juice when you need it.

Xbox celebrated its 25th Anniversary this year, leading to a slew of limited edition products; one of my favorites is this GameSir X5 Lite mobile controller. It's an officially licensed accessory that, just like the Backbone Pro, turns your smartphone into a proper portable gaming device. It comes decked out in an attractive colorway that's a nod to the classic Xbox green. Its more wallet-friendly price means some compromises, but it's a capable, cheaper pick compared to the Backbone Pro.

The Steam Deck OLED lags behind its competition when it comes to internal components, but it's still among the finest portable gaming devices around. It's great for tearing through your Steam backlog or enjoying the best indie games around; just don't expect to be playing the latest 2026 releases on the Deck. Unfortunately, post-launch price hikes have made this a tougher recommendation. At $789 for the 512GB model, it's overpriced, but its overall quality gets it a nod in this roundup.

If you've got very deep pockets (and can find it in stock), you could make somebody's holiday season by gifting them the cutting-edge Asus ROG Xbox Ally X20. It's an upgrade on the ROG Xbox Ally X, with a stellar OLED display and fantastic ergonomics. However, it's hard to get over the sticker shock of the price. It makes the Steam Deck look affordable, which is really saying something. This is only for a gamer in your life who has been positively saintly in 2026.

The Evercade Nexus is a handheld device designed for those who love retro gaming. It includes a 5.89-inch IPS display, dual analogue sticks, and wireless headphone support. There's no digital store to download games; instead, it's compatible with more than 80 physical Evercade cartridges (that's 700+ games). To get your collection started, the Evercade Nexus comes bundled with the Banjo-Kazooie Double Pack, collecting a pair of classic 3D platformers on a single cartridge. It's a nostalgia gamer's perfect handheld system.

The VR gaming boom never quite exploded. Even PlayStation seems to have given up on the PSVR 2. However, the Meta Quest 3S is still a great, more budget-friendly virtual reality headset. Ideal for a gamer who already has all console/PC hardware they need. It comes bundled with Golf+, a VR game that lets you step onto the green in the most immersive way possible outside of paying for an overinflated membership at your local golf club.

The music rhythm game genre is making a big comeback this year thanks to Stage Tour. Taking inspiration from Guitar Hero and Rock Band, you play along to a setlist of more than 90 tracks either on a regular controller or for the full rockstar experience on a plastic Guitar. This $129 bundle includes the Stage Tour game and a Kramer Wireless Guitar Controller and is available on PS5, Xbox Series X/S, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC.

Lego has released a few gaming-themed sets this year, and while the Lego PlayStation 1 is very novel, my pick of the bunch is the Lego Donkey Kong Arcade machine, which recreates a classic arcade cabinet playing the game that introduced the world to Mario. It looks great on display and comes with a play feature that lets you simulate the game in motion, with Mario jumping over barrels.
