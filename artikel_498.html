Matthew McConaugheys Pantalones Tequila made an iPhone case that holds a mini shooter — where to buy

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

TL;DR: Matthew McConaughey's Pantalones Organic Tequila launched the Phone Phlask, a vegan-leather iPhone case designed to strap a 50ml mini-shooter directly to the back of your phone. Shop for $36.99 starting Nov. 10.

Phone cases have spent the past few seasons evolving into wearable utility belts, morphing to hold everything from lip gloss and perfume to card sleeves and kickstands. Matthew McConaughey's spirits label, Pantalones Organic Tequila, is taking that trend into considerably more spirited territory for the holidays.

The Pantalones Phone Flask is an iPhone case engineered to holster a mini tequila shooter on the back of your device. Crafted from vegan leather, the case features pants-inspired stitching and detailing designed to keep your drink locked down on the go.

The Apple iPhone 18 Pro is free right now with T-Mobile

The case drops alongside the nationwide rollout of Pantalones 50ml minis, which are hitting store shelves for the first time. They look just like shrunken versions of the brand’s regular bottles and come in both organic blanco and reposado.

As for the case itself, it is made from vegan leather and fits both the iPhone 16 Pro and iPhone 17 Pro. The back features a functional buckle strap with cheeky "Buckle Up" stitching that holds the bottle in place, but you can also slip your hand through it to use it as a regular phone grip.

Pantalones is selling the whole thing as a holiday bundle. Along with the phone case, you get two 50ml bottles, one Blanco and one Reposado, so you have everything you need right out of the box.

It is ridiculous, completely unnecessary, and guaranteed to be the most popular item in the room. Grab the Pantalones bundle for $36.99.
