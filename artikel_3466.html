Demon Slayer Drops First Infinity Castle Part 2 Release Date Hint, But Fans Are Split

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Demon Slayer has delivered the most successful theatrical release in anime, with Infinity Castle securing a place in the global top 10 highest-grossing films of 2025. Naturally, with such massive success, expectations surrounding the next film installment are exceptionally high, with fans incredibly eager for official news regarding Infinity Castle Part 2’s release date.

While Ufotable has remained quiet about official news surrounding the sequel, a recent development involving Sony Pictures might just offer fans a hint as to when they can expect the series to return. Last month, The Numbers revealed that Sony Pictures, along with Crunchyroll, has an untitled animated movie slated for September 2027.

Following this reveal, Sony Pictures is giving anime fans an exciting possibility to speculate about. While the project isn’t currently named or accompanied by any identifying details as of yet, the listing remains an intriguing development. Given that Sony and Crunchyroll are the global distributors of the Demon Slayer films, it’s not too far-fetched to suspect this title to be Infinity Castle Part 2.

A two-year interval between the first and second parts gives Ufotable the time to deliver the animation quality fans expect without stretching too far beyond previous patterns.

Given the untitled film is expected to premiere in mid-September 2027, the release window aligns with already-existing expectations surrounding Demon Slayer’s next installment. A two-year interval between the first and second parts gives Ufotable the time to deliver the animation quality fans expect without stretching too far beyond previous patterns.

Since the series’ animated debut in 2019, the gap between seasons has often ranged from one to one and a half years before the next installment arrives. Considering the fact that Infinity Castle Part 1 released in Japan on July 18, 2025, and in North America two months later on September 12, 2025, a 2027 release date for a film that could become even more successful than its predecessor is perfectly consistent with the series’ historical release windows while leaving room for delivering exceptional animation quality.

While fans are incredibly excited and filled with anticipation for Demon Slayer’s next theatrical release, nothing is set in stone just yet. Official news regarding Infinity Castle Part 2 likely won’t be announced until Jump Festa 2027, taking place across December 19 and 20, 2026. That said, the untitled film could potentially be about another hit series that broke streaming records in 2025.

After becoming the most-viewed and most-rated series on Crunchyroll, Solo Leveling went on to secure the Anime of the Year Award, cementing its rapid rise in popularity and place among some of anime’s most successful modern titles. Following its second season, the series officially announced an upcoming film, Solo Leveling: Beyond the System, on July 3, 2026, during the Crunchyroll Showcase at Anime Expo.

Demon Slayer's finale movie trilogy that will cover the Infinity Castle Arc has finally announced the release date for its first film.

Like Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle Part 2, Solo Leveling: Beyond the System is also expected sometime in 2027, though no official release window has been announced. Since both Demon Slayer and Solo Leveling have upcoming theatrical films on the horizon, it’s impossible to determine precisely which series, if either, will ultimately be revealed as the untitled film for September 2027. While it is still a possibility that the expected film is the next installment of Demon Slayer, it’s equally possible that it will be Solo Leveling’s next film or a different project altogether.

All in all, Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle Part 2 remains one of the most anticipated upcoming anime films, and fans can rest assured knowing that the team behind the franchise will deliver a viewing experience that meets or even exceeds every high expectation. Sony’s September 2027 listing is an exciting development to speculate on, but until an official announcement is made, Demon Slayer’s next installment doesn’t yet have a release window.
