Kings of Leon to play at Apple Music Hall in December, tickets go on sale November 18

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Apple Music Hall has added Kings of Leon to its lineup of upcoming gigs at the newly opened London venue. Here are the details.

In September, Sir Elton John gave the first-ever performance at Apple Music Hall, a new live music venue in Battersea, London.

Since then, Baby Keem, Skye Newman, Troye Sivan, Arlo Parks, and Burna Boy have performed at the venue, with Chase & Status and Charli xcx scheduled to take the stage in the coming weeks.

A few days later, on December 10, R&B and soul singer-songwriter Jorja Smith will also perform at Apple Music Hall. As with the Kings of Leon concert, tickets will go on sale November 18 at 10 a.m. GMT, priced at £40.
