Bond 26 Director Teases The Next 007 Casting Reveal In Unexpected Moment

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The director of Bond 26 almost names the next 007 before retracting his claims to reveal the new actor who will take on the role.

Bond 26 is the upcoming installment in the James Bond film series, which follows the adventures of 007, a British secret agent working for MI6. Although specific plot details remain shrouded in mystery, the movie is expected to feature a different take on the titular character, bringing the agent back to the big screen with a new face and story.

Bond 26 director Denis Villeneuve has found a humorous way to address the rumors that have been swirling on the internet regarding the iconic franchise's next actor. With Daniel Craig having concluded his five-film run as the secret agent, speculation about his successor has been spreading like wildfire. Although many insiders have claimed that several actors have auditioned for the role, Amazon MGM Studios has not confirmed any casting information about the new leading man.

According to IGN, Villeneuve poked fun at all the reports about who the next Bond was that have been surfacing on the internet. The director, best known for Dune, Dune: Part Two, Arrival, and Blade Runner 2049, was at the Los Angeles County Museum of Art's annual Art+Film Gala, where he decided to play a little joke at the expense of the studio.

IGN stated that Villeneuve unexpectedly brought up the next James Bond during the closing moments of a speech. After expressing his appreciation for Amazon, the director joked that he was about to announce the identity of the actor taking over the role. However, just as he was about to say a name, Villeneuve abruptly stopped himself. He playfully explained that revealing the information would be enough to give producer Amy Pascal a heart attack.

To express my gratitude with the blessing of my new friends at Amazon, I will reveal to you tonight who will be the next James Bond. The new James Bond is… and I will stop right now because Amy Pascal is about to have a heart attack. Thank you very much.

Although Villeneuve wasn't being serious and wasn't seriously considering revealing anything, his comments arrived during a transitional period for the franchise. The upcoming film will introduce the first new James Bond since Craig's departure in 2021's No Time to Die, which ended his run as the character after 15 years.

Recent reports have connected actors including Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor, Robert Pattinson, and Nicholas Hoult to Bond 26. However, none of these performers has been officially announced as Craig's successor, and their alleged involvement should not be interpreted as confirmation that any of them have received the role.

There is no official release date for Bond 26 at the time of this article's publication.
