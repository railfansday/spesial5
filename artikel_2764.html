Not much has worked for the Houston Texans on the ground. Here's what has: Woody Marks and run right

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Houston Texans entered the season hoping for a revamped running game after struggling on the ground in 2025. That hasn't happened.

Entering Sunday's game against the Los Angeles Chargers, the Texans rank fourth-worst in the league in yards per carry and have the second-fewest carries through eight weeks.

"I want us to move the football," coach DeMeco Ryans said. "Run, pass, it doesn't matter. I want us to move the football and score points."

Houston has mostly done that through the air, with most of its total yardage coming from the passing game. But even with the run game less involved, some trends have emerged.

The Texans' 27-21 overtime loss to the New York Giants on Oct. 25 ended two streaks that had lasted through the first two years of Woody Marks' career.

Houston had been 10-0 when Marks carried the ball at least 11 times in a game since the Texans drafted him in 2025. The team had also been 11-0 when he rushed for at least 44 yards.

Marks rushed 19 times for 66 yards against the Giants, but it wasn't enough to help the Texans win or keep either streak alive.

"He sees the hole and hits it hard," Texans center Evan Brown said. "It's been fun blocking for him. We've got to do a better job giving him a little bit more of a crease to hit sometimes, and we'll keep working at that as we go."

David Montgomery, acquired in an offseason trade with Detroit, got off to a fast start in his first game with the Texans. He scored three touchdowns, and Houston rushed for 124 yards in an overtime loss to Buffalo in Week 1. But the Texans followed with three straight games of 70 yards or fewer. They had just one regular-season game with fewer than 70 rushing yards last season.

The rushing attack has been better over the past three games, especially in the win over the Jacksonville Jaguars, when Marks found success running to the right side of the offensive line.

Nearly 74% of Marks' rushing yards over the past three games came on runs inside the right side of the line or to the right. The Texans' more experienced starters on that side are right tackle Trent Brown and right guard Ed Ingram.

"I'm not sitting there intentionally trying to run right every single time," second-year offensive coordinator Nick Caley said. "Like, I can promise you that."

Marks' longest run of the season has been 12 yards, though he has had longer runs called back by penalties. A 27-yard run against the Giants was negated by a holding penalty, and his 24-yard touchdown against the Tennessee Titans was nullified by another holding call.

"When you get them big runs and it finally opens up and you turn around and it's a flag, it's just like, dang," Marks said.

It's not just a problem for Marks. The Texans' longest run of the season so far was a 25-yard touchdown by Jawhar Jordan against the Jaguars.

Every team in the league except the Cincinnati Bengals has had at least one run longer than 25 yards. The Bengals and Texans are the only teams with fewer than two runs of 20 or more yards.
