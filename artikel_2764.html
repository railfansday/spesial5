Steelers OL Mason McCormick carted off field vs. Bengals

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Down 17-0 in the first half versus the rival Cincinnati Bengals on Sunday Night Football, the Steelers' rough night continued with starting left guard Mason McCormick being carted off the field.

With 1:27 remaining in the half, McCormick was seen lying on the field with his right ankle at an uncomfortable angle.

His leg was placed in an air cast according to the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette's Ray Fittipaldo, and McCormick was carted off the field. The play was a fourth down, with Steelers kicker Chris Boswell bringing the score to 17-3 on a 45-yard field goal on the following play.

Spencer Anderson is expected to replace McCormick in the starting lineup.

After Boswell's field goal, the Bengals again drove down the field to bring the first half score to 24-7 in Cincinnati's favor. A battle between the two top teams in the AFC North has quickly turned sour for Pittsburgh, which is now without one of its top players on offense.
