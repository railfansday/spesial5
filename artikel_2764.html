Pat Fitzgerald talks MSU football's loss to No. 13 Oregon

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Michigan State football ended their home slate with the worst loss of the 2026 season, losing to No. 13 Oregon, 45-7.

Following the game, Pat Fitzgerald spoke about the tough loss from his team, giving praise to his team for showing fight, but also the disappointment from the outcome of the game.

See what the Michigan State head man had to say about the game against the Ducks:

"I want to say thank you to all the great seniors (listing everyone, players to bank to dieticians), we had them be part of our Spartan Walk today. The game got away from us there. Proud of our seniors, they've been through a lot. One more guaranteed chance."

"I'm worried about finding a way to go 1-0 next week,"

"Five years from now, we are going to look back on this group of guys that laid the foundation for the future."

"I was just wishing the refs Happy Thanksgiving,"

"We had a guy see it, get tackled. How many people were there? 61,000 people saw it. TV saw it."

"Really proud of Kayd Coffman. I thought he prepared really well. Settled down after the first drive, we have confidence in him."

"Our students were unbelievable today. I cannot wait for the season opener in Spartan Stadium next year, but that is the future. I'm focused on the guys right now."

"We're hopeful that he (Cam Fancher) is going to be able to play next week."

"My focus is on us, we have to defend those plays."
