Tennis legend Rafa Nadal accepts role at La Liga’s Real Madrid

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Tennis legend Rafael Nadal is now officially a Galácticos. Legendary La Liga Club and one of the most popular soccer clubs in the world, Real Madrid recently officially announced the arrival of Nadal as a part of the club's Board of Directors.

"The Board of Directors of Real Madrid CF, meeting today Wednesday, November 4, has agreed to submit to the General Assembly of Delegate Members the ratification of the appointment of Rafael Nadal Parera, member number 67,778 of our club, as a new member of the Board of Directors.

"Rafael Nadal, an honorary member of Real Madrid, is one of the greatest legends in the history of sport and one of the most admired and recognized Spanish athletes in the world,Real Madrid shared this via their official announcement. [Quotes translated via Google] In their official statement, Real Madrid also paid homage to the legendary tennis player and his achievements.

Real Madrid expressed its pride at the news of the 22-time Grand Slam champion and longtime Real Madrid fan joining the "Los Blancos." This statement comes after Nadal, who holds honorary membership at Real Madrid, dismissed on social media earlier in 2026 rumours suggesting he might run for the club's presidency.

Shortly after Real Madrid shared the official announcement, Nadal reacted to it on social media and wrote, "I want to thank President Florentino Pérez and the Board for proposing that I join the Board of Directors of [Real Madrid]. I have accepted this proposal with great excitement, a desire to learn, and a willingness to contribute everything I can." Nadal also added two pictures of him as a Madrista: one, a childhood picture of him in all white holding a soccer ball, and another of him at the legendary Santiago Bernabéu.

Despite hailing from Manacor in Mallorca, Spain, Nadal grew up a Real Madrid fan and has now become a part of the club's management.
