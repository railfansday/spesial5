Hawks sign big man Rafael Castro to a two-way contract

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Michael Scotto: The Atlanta Hawks and Rafael Castro have agreed to a two-way deal, agents Kevin Bradbury and CJ Ward of LIFT Sports Management told @hoopshype. Castro was a 2-time All-Atlantic 10 Team selection and 2-time All-Atlantic 10 Defensive Team selection at George Washington University.
