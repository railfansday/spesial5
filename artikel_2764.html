Celtics extend losing streak to 4 games with 88-87 loss to Warriors

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

For the first time since May of 2021, the Boston Celtics have lost 4 games in a row, falling to the Golden State Warriors 88-87. This is now the longest losing streak in the Joe Mazzulla Era of the Celtics.

Jayson Tatum led the way for the Celtics with 27 points, 10 rebounds, and 6 assists, but similar to the last three losses, Boston could not find their offense late in the fourth quarter.

On the second half of a back-to-back, the Celtics were without both Paul George (rest) and Mitchell Robinson (illness). Boston started Derrick White, Hugo Gonzalez, Sam Hauser, Jayson Tatum, and Neemias Queta. Tatum and White returned after missing the game against the Kings on Saturday while it was also the second consecutive start for Gonzalez and Hauser. The Warriors were once again without superstar Stephen Curry, along with guard Brandon Williams, former Celtic Kristaps Porzingis, Jimmy Butler, and Moses Moody. Golden State would start De'Anthony Melton, Brandin Podziemski, Yaxel Lendeborg, Draymond Green, and Charles Bassey.

Jayson Tatum was welcomed back early by the Celtics, scoring the first 10 points of the game for Boston. He was in an unlikely scoring battle early with Draymond Green, who hit a season high 3 threes in the first quarter after beefing with Phoenix Suns broadcaster Eddie Johnson on X before the game.

Boston allowed 4 corner threes in the same exact place to Green and former Celtic Al Horford, causing a timeout from Joe Mazzulla at the 3:51 mark of the first quarter.

The Celtics played 11 different players in the first quarter but their shooting struggles across this west coast trip continued. Boston shot 7-18 from the field and 3-11 from three, trailing Golden State 27-20 after the opening frame. Jayson Tatum carried the load, finishing with 10 points on 3-4 shooting while the rest of the team was 4-14.

With Tatum on the bench, Ron Harper Jr opened the second quarter with an impressive dunk after cutting from the corner off a nice pass from Neemias Queta. This came after he hit a three to end the first quarter and this was the second game in a row that Harper Jr has seen minutes early in the game.

Harper Jr continued his impressive play in this game after he stole a pass from Brandin Podziemski and grabbed an offensive rebound on the next possession that led to a Payton Pritchard layup. This forced the Warriors to call a timeout at the 9:24 mark of the second quarter.

After turning back the clock in Sacramento in the previous game, Mike Conley checked in at the 7:41 mark of the second quarter for the Celtics. He checked in alongside Al Horford, 2 of the 4 remaining players of the 2007 NBA Draft Class.

Jayson Tatum continued his carry job for the Celtics, finishing the first half with over half of Boston points. He had 23 points and 8 rebounds on 8-13 shooting from the field and 4-6 from three. Tatum scored the final 11 points of the second quarter for Boston and made history on his last shot, passing Bill Russell for 8th on the Celtics all-time scoring list with his 14,523rd point of his career. It felt fitting that Tatum passed Russell in San Francisco, where Bill Russell played his college ball.

Tatum received no help from his supporting cast, as the second highest scorers for Boston in the first half were Payton Pritchard, Luka Garza, and Ron Harper Jr with 5 points. As a team the Celtics shot 16-43 from the field and 6-22 from three.

Mike Conley replaced Hugo Gonzalez in the third quarter starting lineup and has continued to find the fountain of youth. He hit back-to-back three pointers to open up the scoring for the Celtics in the second half, with his second three coming off a beautiful pass by Tatum.

Conley's presence alone looked to calm the Celtics out of the half as Boston open up the third quarter on a 10-2 run. After Sam Hauser hit his first three of the night at the 8:08 mark of the third quarter, it capped off a 22-6 run for Boston since the 38.0 second mark of the second quarter.

The Celtics bench made much more of an impact in the third quarter. Outside of Conley, midway through the quarter Hugo Gonzalez hit two threes, Luka Garza scored 6 points, and Baylor Scheierman hit his first three of the game.

Boston led 75-69 at the end of the third but Golden State finished the quarter on a 7-0 run with Podziemski hitting a three with 5 seconds left. The Celtics shooting as a team bounced back in this quarter, finishing 11-22 from the field and 7-17 from three.

Payton Pritchard opened the fourth quarter with back-to-back steals, leading to a Derrick White floater after the second one. This did not deter the Warriors offense. Gui Santos hit back-to-back threes and Yaxel Lendeborg hit back-to-back floaters to give Golden State an 81-79 lead at the 8:13 mark of the fourth quarter, forcing a Celtics timeout. This extended a 12-4 run by the Warriors to open up the quarter.

Out of the timeout, Neemias Queta grabbed an offensive rebound and went up for a thunderous dunk over Yaxel Lendeborg.

Jayson Tatum returned to the game at the 6:32 mark and grabbed his 10th rebound for a double-double. After a Warriors challenge, Derrick White scored 5 straight points to give the Celtics a three point lead with 4:21 left in the game. Brandin Podziemski responded with a 5 point swing of his own, including a wide open corner three that gave the Warriors the lead again with 2:38 left in the game. With under two minutes to go, Joe Mazzulla replaced Payton Pritchard with Baylor Scheierman in the lineup.

Podzemski looked to have given the Warriors a 5-point lead with an And-1 three with 1:08 remaining, but he grabbed Baylor Scheierman on the way down. This led to the Celtics challenging the call and overturning it, keeping it a two-point game.

Jayson Tatum proceeded to go 1-2 at the free throw line to make it an 88-87 Warriors lead with 55.6 remaining.

After the Celtics forced the Warriors into a shot clock violation, Mazzulla called a timeout with 31.5 seconds to go. Out of the timeout, Boston went for the two-for-one, but Jayson Tatum missed a wide open three and Neemias Queta missed a put back, leading to a Warriors rebound and timeout with 19.7 remaining.

After the Warriors timeout, Hugo Gonzalez made an incredible play, stealing the inbound pass from Draymond Green to give the Celtics a chance. After holding on to the ball for a little bit, Mazzulla called timeout with 8.2 seconds remaining.

Out of the Celtics timeout, the ball was inbounded to Derrick White, who found Jayson Tatum at the top of the three-point line. He drove on Draymond Green and shot a fadeaway jumper that hit the front of the rim as time expired. Celtics lose 88-87.

Another heartbreaking loss as the Celtics move to 0-3 on their current road trip. Boston's next game is on Tuesday November 24th against the Washington Wizards in a NBA Cup game. This will be the first time the Celtics face off against 2026 Number 1 Overall Pick and Brockton, MA native AJ Dybantsa.
