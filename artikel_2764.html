When the season expands to 18 games, reseeding will likely follow

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

With the NFC South destined to send a none-of-the-above team to the playoffs and to have that team host a playoff game, there inevitably will be criticism of the current structure of the postseason. And it inevitably will dust off the proposal the Lions made two years ago, with a nudge from the league office, to reseed the seven teams based on record and not on division championship.

When will the talk become action? When the NFL expands to regular season to 18 games.

That’s the current thinking, as we’ve consistently heard it. Expansion of the regular season will result in reseeding of the playoffs,

Winning the division will still get a team in. But whether that team hosts a playoff game will depend on whether its record is one of the four best in the conference.

The best approach would be to realign the league to four eight-team conferences. Four division champions, 10 wild-cards. No arbitrary separation of AFC and NFC.

We won’t hold our breath for that one to happen. The league takes baby steps, not giant leaps. And when it takes its next step to 18 games, wild-card teams likely will be able to leap lesser division champions.
