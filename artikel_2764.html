Lakers vs. Spurs Preview: A big first test

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

After nearly a full week off, the Lakers (15-3) have their first true, big test of the season against the Spurs (15-4) on Friday. The two teams will meet three times this year, though not again until February after this duel.

Who: Los Angeles Lakers at San Antonio Spurs

For more than a week now, this matchup has loomed over the Lakers' schedule. Each win during their recent homestand meant more and more of an eye turned toward this contest.

The Spurs stand alone as the measuring stick in the Western Conference after the Thunder were decimated by injury. It means every team is going to give them their best shot with the Lakers the latest team loading up for their chance at a defining win.

And while the Lakers have had the better part of a week to prepare, the Spurs were already on the receiving end of one of those haymakers on Wednesday. In an NBA Cup match that impacted LA's chances of advancing, the Blazers knocked off the Spurs, dropping them into a four-way tie atop the group with every non-Kings team sitting at 2-1.

The Spurs losing has been a rarity since the opening week of the season. After dropping their first two contests to the Thunder and Rockets, San Antonio won 12 straight games and 15 of 16 games prior to Wednesday's defeat.

Victor Wembanyama is, obviously, at the center of that. Through 18 games, Wemby is averaging 30.7 points, 12.5 rebounds, 3.2 assists and a staggering 3.8 blocks per game. He's doing it on 54.3% shooting, a career-best mark despite shooting just 31.7% from three, a career-worst rate.

Perhaps unsurprisingly, they sport the best net rating in the league with the fourth-best offensive rating and third-best defensive rating. Their damage comes in the paint and at the rim where they rank top five in points in the paint per game (second), rim field goal makes (third), attempts (fifth) and percentage (fifth) and overall paint field goal percentage (third).

Their three-guard approach of De'Aaron Fox, Dylan Harper and Stephon Castle around Wemby allows them to relentlessly attack the rim at the cost of shooting, where they are 21st in 3-point rate and 28th in 3-point percentage.

In short, it will be a long night for Walker Kessler as a rim protector.

The good news is that all signs point to an Austin Reaves return. His absence will span more than two weeks, but he was a participant in practice on Wednesday and spoke with the media after, which is typically a giveaway that a player will return from injury.

After watching Luka Dončić run on empty in the final games of that aforementioned homestand, Reaves' return couldn't come at a better time. The rust will need to be kicked off and it's unrealistic to expect Reaves to continue his All-Star-level play out of the gate, but having him available is a huge boost.

It also gives the team a full picture look at how they stack up against the Spurs. While this is still just one game and there are still 60+ remaining on the schedule after Friday, this is a big one and both teams will treat it as such.

Reaves is officially listed as questionable for the game. It's the first time he hasn't been listed as out since suffering the injury.

San Antonio could be without two of their guards. De'Aaron Fox hasn't played in the last two contests and is questionable with a shin contusion. Dylan Harper, who has not played in the last four games, has been upgraded to questionable with a hiop external rotator strain. Tobias Harris (calf strain) is out.
