Spain Moves to Give Authorities Power to Freeze Crypto Accounts Without a Court Order

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Key takeaways Spain’s Royal Decree-Law 44/2026 would let financial intelligence unit Sepblac freeze crypto accounts for up to five business days, and halt pending transactions for up to ten, without a court order. The powers take effect January 25, 2027, subject to parliamentary validation; a court must confirm or extend any suspension at the deadline. […]
