Why XRP Analysts Flag $8.30 Macro Price Target

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Home » Ripple (XRP) » Why XRP Analysts Flag $8.30 Macro Price Target

The wild SWIFT replacement claim resurfaces on social media for a good reason: XRP-tied banks are in.

Two recent posts have put long-term XRP price projections back in view. One centers on a multi-year chart pattern with an $8.30 target, while the other argues that full replacement of SWIFT would support valuations above $15,000. XRP is trading near $0.95.

Analyst EGRAG CRYPTO highlighted a weekly chart showing a large symmetrical triangle that XRP broke out of in prior years. The X post stated that the original white triangle’s measured breakout target of $8.30 has not yet been reached and suggested the current price action could represent a macro retest of that breakout before any larger expansion.

#XRP – The $8.30 Number 🎯:This remains one of my most powerful macro charts.The original white symmetrical triangle breakout target has STILL not been reached.What if everything happening right now is simply the macro retest of that breakout before the real expansion?My… pic.twitter.com/F0tdM5mxtl

The chart marks $1.65 as a major reclaim and confirmation level, with $8.30 labeled as the primary macro target. EGRAG noted a personal connection to the number.

Being born in 1983, and said that level is where a large portion of holdings would be sold if reached. The analysis frames the target as a technical outcome rather than an emotional one. XRP remains well below its 2025 high near $3.65, and the pattern’s interpretation is subject to debate among traders.

A separate post by Ripple_Mino claimed that if XRP fully replaces SWIFT, the token could be valued beyond $15,000. The argument rests on XRP’s divisibility: one XRP equals one million drops, so even extreme valuations would still allow practical fractional use.

When XRP replaces Swift, it will be valued beyond $15,000 per token.The original token design allowed for such a valuation as 1 XRP can be divided into 1,000,000 smaller units known as drops.So 1 Drop = 0.000001 XRP(one millionth of an XRP) pic.twitter.com/WcBEPSh0XA

Official and recent developments do not support the premise of XRP’s SWIFT replacement.

SWIFT has launched its own blockchain-based shared ledger for tokenized bank deposits, with more than a dozen major banks participating in trials. SWIFT has not announced any integration of XRP as a settlement asset for that system.

Former SWIFT innovation leadership has publicly rejected claims of XRP integration, and the network continues to emphasize its own infrastructure and tokenized deposits rather than a single external token.

The $8.30 figure is a technical target drawn from a multi-year chart structure, while the $15,000 figure is a hypothetical based on a scenario that SWIFT and major participating banks haven’t endorsed yet.

Neither projection is reflected in current market pricing yet or officially top-confirmed institutional commitments. XRP’s price action continues to trade well below both levels as of late November 2026.

The link between SWIFT as a traditional financial intermediary and XRP’s native chain comes multi-layered, with intersection across liquidity provision and instant settlement environments.
