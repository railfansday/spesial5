Alejandro Balde gives priority to Roma move

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Roma will be very active in the transfer market in January. Gian Piero Gasperini has primarily requested offensive reinforcements, but the Giallorossi continue to monitor Alejandro Balde.

The Spaniard is finding less and less space at Barcelona, ​​and his departure in January is increasingly likely.

Roma is delighted by some news from Spain: according to Spanish newspaper Sport, the 2003-born player has put the Roman club at the top of his list of options should he leave Camp Nou. Negotiations between the clubs, however, are an uphill battle.

Roma and Barcelona are struggling to find a formula that everyone can agree on; the clubs will continue to work until January to find a compromise. Balde’s salary is also a problem: approximately €5.5 million net is beyond the reach of the Trigoria club.

The deal could be finalized by someone who helped the Roman club close a deal this summer. After Rodrigo Mora, Jorge Mendes could also intervene to bring the 2003-born player to the capital. All discussions are postponed: the Giallorossi have other priorities.
