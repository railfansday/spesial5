Can you name Man Utd’s XI from Sir Alex Ferguson’s first game in 1986?

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

On November 08, 1986, Alex Ferguson’s legendary reign at Manchester United began with a not-so-legendary 2-0 defeat away to Oxford United. Do you know the names on his first teamsheet?

Long before he was knighted, Ferguson arrived at Old Trafford to turn around the dormant giants’ fortunes.

Ron Atkinson had been sacked and in came Ferguson, fresh from his success with Aberdeen and taking Scotland to the 1986 World Cup in Mexico.

In 2013, auctioneers Henry Aldridge and Son valued the original teamsheet valued at £2,000, describing the match as “an inauspicious start to a dynasty that yielded a record 13 Premier League titles and two Champions Leagues among its haul of over 30 trophies”.
