Olise, Kane, Neuer… Bayern Munich is hit by a terrible massacre!

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Nothing seems to spare Bayern Munich. Already faced with a long list of absentees, the Bavarian club saw two new executives leave the pitch due to injury this Friday evening against Cologne: Harry Kane and Michael Olise. Hit in the ankle after contact with Joël Schmied in the area, the English striker had to give up his place in the 20th minute of play. For his part, the French international was the victim of contact with Tom Krauß in the first period. Despite an attempt to continue the match, he was unable to return after the break.

Olise’s situation also arouses particular concern. According to the German press, the former Crystal Palace player left the stadium on crutches, a sign which raises questions about the seriousness of his injury. For the moment, no official diagnosis has been communicated concerning the Frenchman, any more than for Kane even if Max Eberl affirmed that the Bavarian striker was suffering from a torn syndesmosis (which could keep him away from the field for 3 months, announces Sky Sports Germany). Bayern could therefore lose two of their main offensive assets as they enter a particularly busy period in their calendar.

Because the Bavarian infirmary is already very full. Manuel Neuer suffers a partial tear in a tendon in his right foot, while Serge Gnabry is affected by the muscles in the back of his left thigh. Ismaël Saibari was the victim of a rupture of the syndesmotic ligament in his right ankle, an injury which required an operation. Finally, Alphonso Davies suffered a torn muscle in the back of his left thigh with Canada, while Luis Díaz is still recovering from a torn muscle in his left thigh.

With six players already unavailable before the match and now the uncertainties surrounding Kane and Olise, Vincent Kompany sees his options reduced considerably. The return of Jamal Musiala, recently recovered from his thigh injury, constitutes a brightening in this particularly dark context. But the next few hours will be decisive for Bayern, who are now awaiting medical examinations in order to know the duration of possible new absences. For its part, LOSC, which is preparing to find the Munich residents on the European scene, can rub its hands…
