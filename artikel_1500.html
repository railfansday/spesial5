Ancelotti advises Endrick to join Roma in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Roma is ready to move forward in the January transfer window to strengthen Gasperini’s attacking line.

The Giallorossi’s priority remains the acquisition of a player capable of operating on the left flank, a request made by the coach during the summer transfer window.

Among the options being pursued by sporting director Tony D’Amico, Endrick currently appears to be ahead of Jamie Gittens, a Chelsea winger who remains a favorite with the club.

As reported by La Gazzetta dello Sport, the Brazilian remains one of Roma’s top targets heading into January.

Mourinho had declared him unsellable, but the limited playing time afforded to the 2006-born player could lead to a reconsideration during the next transfer window.

This situation is also being closely monitored by Carlo Ancelotti, Brazil’s coach, who would favor a move to the capital to allow him to play more consistently. Two factors could favor a possible move to the Giallorossi.

The first is the possibility of working with Gasperini, a coach who has demonstrated his ability to develop numerous young players throughout his career.

The second is the presence of Wesley, Endrick’s compatriot, who has already settled into his role in the capital and with whom he could form a new partnership on the left flank.

These are two elements that, according to the newspaper, are also being viewed positively by the player’s entourage. Real Madrid, however, still need to be convinced, while Roma continues to keep the possibility of signing Gittens open.
