Supercomputer Ranks Every 2026/27 Champions League Team’s Chances of Winning

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Data analysts have judged every Champions League club based on their winning probability for the 2026/27 season. And, such is the difficulty of predicting Europe's premier competition, holders Paris Saint-Germain currently have just a 12 per cent chance of making it three wins in a row.

Les Parisiens are always going to be among the favourites, but with so many managerial changes and high-profile names passing through each club's arrivals and departure lounges this summer, the chasing pack is a mixed bag of potential dark horses, emerging powers, and those that are usually expected to be among the best but fall spectacularly short.

Arsenal, Manchester City, Liverpool, and Manchester United will all be gunning to make deep runs, while Bayern Munich and Barcelona will be expected to do the same. Current odds as per Opta, however, following gameweek four of eight in the League Phase, spring a few surprises into the mix.

There can be very few surprises in this section. With Sabah venturing into their first-ever Champions League campaign after just nine years of existence, Slovan Bratislava struggling under Yaya Toure, and Shakhtar Dontesk not as good as they once were, the bottom three are as expected - though each will be hoping to forge an underdog story.

Four teams have been given a 0.01% chance of glory: AEK Athens, LASK, Slavia Prague and Feyenoord. The Czech club have only picked up a solitary point in the competition this season, while the other three are sitting on three.

French duo Lille and Lens have been given a slightly better chance, at 0.03% and 0.04% respectively. Both teams have had disastrous starts to their campaign. Lens were crushed 9-1 by Como in their last outing, while Lille are still waiting for their first victory of the League Phase.

England, Wales and Scotland are all in the top 27 nations that boast the most Champions League winners.

Viking pulled off a surprise when they held Bayern Munich to a 3-3 draw in their very first Champions League outing. They have been given a 0.05% chance of winning the trophy after picking up four points from their opening four games.

PSV (0.06%), Fenerbahce (0.07%), Napoli (0.11%) and Galatasaray (0.11%) follow them. Galatasaray can blow very hot or very cold, but with Victor Osimhen up front, they will always be a dangerous team.

Villarreal are 22nd in the probability rankings, while German side RB Leipzig are 21st, and Bodo/Glimt, who enjoyed a fairytale run to the last 16 last season, crack the top 20.

Porto, who won the Portuguese league last time out, round out this section. They have been given a measly 0.2 per cent chance of adding to the Champions League titles they won in 1987 and then again in 2004 under Jose Mourinho.

Every Champions League club's chances of winning (27-19)

Stuttgart, Betis, and Roma being among the top half of the rankings suggest they could be the teams to look out for this season by way of surprise packages. Sporting CP made it to the quarter-finals last year, and they've been handed a 0.53% chance of going all the way this term.

Manchester United were considered among the contenders to win the Champions League before a ball was kicked this season. However, after a dismal run of form under Michael Carrick, their chances of becoming European champions for a fourth time this season have significantly dropped.

Atletico Madrid are arguably the biggest team to have never won the Champions League. Diego Simeone's side have been given a 0.86% chance of ending that drought this season.

Como are the first side to have been given a greater than 1% chance of lifting the trophy. Cesc Fabregas' side have been mightily impressive in their first season in the Champions League, beating Man Utd 4-1 and crushing Lens 9-1.

Borussia Dortmund just miss out on the top 10, while Club Brugge round out this section. The Belgian giants are enjoying a successful League Phase, having picked up nine points from their opening three matches.

Every Champions League club's chances of winning (18-10)

Inter Milan, who reached the Champions League final in 2023 and 2025, are hoping to go one better this term. They have been handed a 2.45% chance of becoming European champions for the fourth time.

Unai Emery's Aston Villa are eighth favourites. The Villains are hoping to become European champions for the first time since 1982.

Liverpool are seventh favourites, Real Madrid are sixth, and Barcelona are fifth - but the first club to break the 10 per cent chances is Manchester City in fourth. Enzo Maresca has had an impressive start to life at the Citiziens after replacing Pep Guardiola in the summer.

Four of the five Premier League clubs competing are tipped to finish in the top eight.

Surprisingly, back-to-back Champions League winners Paris Saint-Germain only have a 12.0% chance of making it a three-peat. That is despite PSG starting their campaign with four successive wins, beating Man City and Barcelona in the process.

The two teams that have been given a better chance than the French giants are Bayern Munich (17.74%) and Arsenal (22.56%). Mikel Arteta's side are the clear favourites to go all the way this season and become European champions for the first time.
