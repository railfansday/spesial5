JJ Gabriel: Spanish Media Cast Strong Verdict on Man Utd Youngster

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

JJ Gabriel hasn't even kicked a ball for any of Real Madrid's teams yet and the former Manchester United starlet has already made an impression on Spanish media.

The 16-year-old stunned United when he asked to leave the club in September, and he became the most in-demand teenage talent in the months that followed. As soon as the news dropped that he wanted out of Old Trafford without even making a senior appearance, the likes of Madrid, Barcelona, Bayern Munich, Chelsea and Manchester City were all linked.

Madrid eventually won the race to sign Gabriel, a move still to be completed and one that confused United's fans because of his supposed wish for regular senior football. His family, including his father, are thought to believe he is a similar superstar talent as Lamine Yamal, but he might have to work hard convincing the Spanish media that he's the real deal.

Gabriel bid farewell to United with a social media post this week, confirming his departure with a video that veteran players often use when wishing fans an emotional goodbye after lengthy stays at clubs. Given that the England youngster didn't even feature for the club's first-team, it caused a stir not only at Old Trafford.

Spanish commentator Juanma Castano discussed the video during an appearance on El Partidazo de Cope and he reacted in astonishment. Pointing out a lack of prominence while at United, he said:

"I say this with the slight fear that this kid might go on to become the greatest player of all time. But it’s just unbelievable. This lad hasn’t played a single minute for Manchester United. Yet he’s said goodbye as if he were a United legend."

Castano added: "Someone leaving the club, the sort of player whose departure would have people in Manchester in tears." He explained that Gabriel is just "a 16-year-old kid who hasn’t even made his debut. He’s a prospect."

When Gabriel pushed to leave United, questions arose about his attitude and those advising him at this stage of his career. Jamie Carragher particularly warned about the influence family can have on a youth prospect and demanding too much, too soon.

Castano echoed this when explaining his concerns about Gabriel and his mindset. He believes that the English winger isn't "what he’s being led to believe he is" and that will lead to “difficulties this boy will face, believing he’s already a football star.”

The Spaniard's colleague, Manolo Lama, has partially blamed the media for building Gabriel up as a superstar in the making at the age of 16:

"We in the media are largely to blame for this. Every week, I see young players, 14 or 15-year-olds, at Real Madrid. Some go on to succeed, others don’t. But we almost always attribute far more value to them than they actually possess."

He added: “Just pick up any newspaper. Every day you’ll find a ‘diamond’ from somewhere or a ‘gem’ from somewhere else.”

Gabriel caught the eye throughout his journey through United's youth ranks, putting in dazzling displays that had the UK press believing him to be Carrington's biggest talent in decades. That included handing him the nickname 'Kid Messi' and constantly reporting on his ventures such as his major sponsorship deal with Nike.

United have yet to even confirm Gabriel's exit and, from their perspective, the club did all they could to convince him to stay and sign a new contract. They are frustrated to have lost one of their academy's most exciting teenagers, but there are others whom they hold in high regard.

Take for example, Shea Lacey, who, unlike Gabriel, has broken into the senior team and has already won over fans with cameo appearances. The 19-year-old came off the bench to set Matheus Cunha up in a 3-2 comeback win over AS Roma in the Champions League earlier this month.

The Red Devils don't have to worry about Noah Ajayi's future as the German starlet signed his first long-term professional contract with the club in September. Gabriel got most of the plaudits for the U18's last season but Ajayi was equally important in attack under Darren Fletcher.

Tynan Thompson is another name United will be hoping can fulfill his potential after forking out an initial £4 million to sign the 18-year-old from Tottenham Hotspur. The English winger may well be viewed as Gabriel's like-for-like replacement at youth level and a talent who could step up to the senior level like Ayden Heaven.

United's main concern right now should be about their first-team struggles that have led to the sacking of Michael Carrick. Mauricio Pochettino has replaced him and the Argentine's first match back in the Premier League is a trip to Anfield to face Liverpool on Sunday (November 22nd).
