Fish with a sniper rifle takes aim at Tim Sweeney's hopes and dreams, as genAI-free meme game tops Epic Games' vibe-coding megajam

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Epic Games are, as you're probably aware, going big on genAI at the moment. To the extent that, last month, the Tim Sweeney-helmed company announced the Epic AI MegaJam. Build a playable game in Unreal Engine or Unreal Editor for Fortnite with help from as many AI agents or tools as you like was the pitch.

So, naturally, sorting the submissions by 'Popular' turns up a game about a fish with a sniper rifle whose creator says they deliberately made it without using any generative AI.

The game's called LSD:FIH and sees a cigarette-smoking bit of shoe-wearing marine life go full MLG and start pulling off trickshots on blobs. If you struggled to parse that, you'll probably struggle even more with developer PolyCube's description of it as being a "computer game about a fih aura-farming". "No AI was used to make this game," is the other line in their description, which is far easier to parse.

"I made a game for the Epic's 'vibe-coding gamejam' megajam without using any AI in four days," they expanded on not Bluesky. "Sorta ran out of time so gameplay didn't turn out how I wanted, but hey it's still pretty aura."

Other submissions the megajam included a game called THIS GAME IS AI SLOP and one dubbed Goon Patrol - Director's Cut. If you're wondering how a game without genAI use could be allowed to stand among the slop masterpieces, it's because Epic ended up having to make clearer not long after the jam was announced that submissions didn't have to make use of genAI as a hard rule, leaving it up to the developer how much or little was worked in.

Still, I imagine noted genAI advocate Tim Sweeney's not best pleased that a game deliberately swimming against the slop tide shows up at the top if you hit up the Popular filter. Then again, he's been very busy posting important things about the situation with Jensen Huang and Nvidia lately, so maybe he hasn't noticed.

Yes, the last post I've linked there is the Sweens giggling at an AI image of him, Huang, Johan Andersson, and John Carmack as the heads carved into Mount Rushmore that he himself asked Grok to generate. As you do.
