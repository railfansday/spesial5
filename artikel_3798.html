WoW Forever PvP Explained: Honor, Ranks, and World PvP

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

There's a reason "War" is in "Warcraft," and WoW: Forever PvP tries to give us some of that Alliance vs. Horde action again.

World of Warcraft: Forever being a redo of OG WoW means going back to the good ol’ days of the never-ending conflict between the Alliance and the Horde. You’re either lok’tar ogar or you scream “for the Alliance!” There was hardly any in-between, and we missed that from the older expansions.

And since WoW: Forever does want to recapture that 2004 vanilla “spirit” without messy expansions getting in the way, part of that effort is to make PvP a lot more enjoyable and replayable. But first, let’s take a quick look at what Forever offers in the player-versus-player department.

Not everyone is playing WoW to fight other players, and we don’t want to ruin anyone else’s time. So the best way for you to enjoy PvP is to find others who are willing to duke it out just as much as you do.

All PvP activities in Forever give you something called Honor Points. You earn this exclusive PvP currency through Battlegrounds, PvP Quests, and even defeating other players. Spend your Honor by finding PvP vendors at:

You can only carry a maximum of 15,000 Honor at any given time.

PvP battles are no walk in the park, and Forever plays its part in honoring (pun intended) that participation. You start your journey as a Civilian and earn Rank Points as you do PvP activities. Earning enough points will raise your rank for the Season, which is separate from the MMO’s core leveling dynamic.

To check out your current rank, open your Character sheet and find the “Player vs. Player” tab. This will show you your current rank, the Rank Points you have, and rewards you can purchase based on your ranking.

You might be confused about why your PvP actions may or may not earn Rank Points in Forever. How come your Battlegrounds skirmishes net you Rank Points, but somehow a difficult duel against this Alliance Rogue didn’t give you anything?

The key distinction is that you don’t earn Rank Points in casual combat.

The prospect of PvP giving Epic Gear can make a weekend marathon worthwhile, but you might want to hold your horses. You only get to earn a certain amount of Rank Points per week, based on your current rank. This means you’ll have to, at minimum, engage with the PvP Season for at least 10 weeks before completing it.

As you progress as a PvP enthusiast in Forever, you can also indulge in the rewards of fighting other players. But you can’t just spend your Honor willy-nilly, as these rewards are also locked behind rank tiers. Here are shop items you raise your rank:

You might notice the word Upgrade Token there. That’s because there are two different PvP gear sets you can purchase with Honor. By default, you can buy a Rare Set and an Epic Set. The catch is, you can only access the Epic Set by bringing in its Rare equivalent and upgrading it with the Emboldened Seal, which is the upgrade token you can purchase for that specific gear type.

Blizzard wants to give Forever players a reason to go back to the game all the time, and they are using PvP Seasons for our dueling enthusiasts.
