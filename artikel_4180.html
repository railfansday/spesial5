Reminder: The Final Kirby Air Riders amiibo Are Now Available

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Since the arrival of Kirby Air Riders on the Switch 2 last year, Nintendo has been releasing amiibo for the game on a regular basis.

In case you missed the previous updates, this week marks the arrival of the "final figures" in this series. It's Sword Kirby & Dragoon, and Noir Dedede & Hyrda. This particular line allows you to swap the riders and machines around. Here's a little more about what else they do:

"Tap this amiibo featuring in Kirby Air Riders to summon Figure Players in-game that you can train and level up. You can even swap riders just like in the game to choose your rider and machine."

They'll set you back $54.99 / £41.99 each (or your regional equivalent) and can be purchased on the Nintendo Store and from retailers online and in-store. There are a total of seven Kirby Air Riders amiibo to collect.

This follows a game update for Kirby Air Riders last week, which included some bug fixes. The update before this in June (Version 1.3.3) made these latest amiibo figures compatible.
