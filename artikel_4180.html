Every Game Coming To And Leaving PS Plus In November 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

PlayStation Plus got a makeover in 2022, when the subscription service was split into three tiers: Essential, Extra, and Premium. Essential is the cheapest option and gives you access to online features, plus a handful of free games each month. Extra costs a little more and gets you everything that Essential comes with, plus access to a library of PS5 and PS4 titles. Then you have Premium, the most expensive tier, which includes all of that, a selection of classic games to play, and other features, including PS Portal cloud streaming capabilities.

Three games this month with some big names among them, the biggest being Lara Croft. The original Tomb Raider games have been getting remastered and bundled into trilogies. The latest example of that is Tomb Raider 4 through 6, and from November 3, that collection will be available to claim and keep through Essential.

Joining it on the same day are a couple of very different, sequels. Dead Island 2 also comes to Essential on November 3, as does the fantastic, and still very new, Monster Train 2.

If you have PS Plus Extra, you get access to a library of great PS5 and PS4 games with new titles added every month. There are some big games coming to Extra throughout November too, starting with the original Hell Let Loose on November 11.

Three more games will join it on November 17, including by far the biggest of the bunch, Death Stranding 2. Not only one of 2025's biggest games, but one of the biggest PS5 exclusives full stop. Atomfall and Dragon Quest 3's HD-2D remake will be added to Extra on the same day.

Another big game arrives on Extra on November 24 as Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii, the Like A Dragon spinoff, is added to the library. Still the newest game in the series if you don't include the Yakuza 3 remaster. Trepang2 will also be added to Extra on the same day, and a couple of titles for the sports game fans among you courtesy of MotoGP 26 and Cricket 26.

PS Plus Premium is the tier you need if you want access to PlayStation's Classic Catalog, along with everything else mentioned above, and we are well and truly back to getting two Premium games every month after an extended period of having to settle for just one monthly game.

Both of this month's Premium games will be added to the Classics Catalog on November 17, and even though we've been told some of the games coming our way in the future, we didn't know either of these titles were inbound. Soul Reaver 2 and Freedom Wars Remastered.

Classics Catalog games are typically also added to the PlayStation Store for those who don't have Premium but still want to play them.

As noted above, PlayStation has started to make a habit of showing us what games are coming to Plus, particularly Premium, ahead of time. The original Ratchet & Clank has been confirmed to be coming to Premium soon; we just don't know when. The earliest time it can now show up will be December.

Earth Defense Force 4.1: The Shadow of New Despair

Each month, PlayStation removes games from its Extra and Premium libraries, sometimes for incredibly clear reasons, while other times, not so much. In what is a bumper month for Plus departures, the most high-profile games leaving the service are doing so for a very obvious reason. GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 leave Extra on November 17. Clearly, a ploy by Rockstar to get as many people as possible buying and playing GTA 6 two days later. Ten other games will be leaving on the same day, including Football Manager 26, since its successor, like GTA 6, also releases in November.
