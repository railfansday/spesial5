Rubix Battery: Storage Designed for Solar Installers

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

By James Mast, Managing Partner, Rubix Battery

AI and data center growth, extreme weather, and baseload retirement are straining the US electric grid. For homeowners and businesses, reliable access to electricity is increasingly important. That need is driving solar battery adoption. Solar power with battery storage can help keep systems running when grid power is unavailable and give property owners greater control over energy.

Energy storage is becoming an increasingly important part of residential, commercial, and off-grid power systems. In fact, the off-grid power systems market is expected to increase to USD 7.35 billion by the year 2032, while growing at a Compounded Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of 15.0%. For solar contractors, that growth brings new opportunities, but they will need battery solutions that accommodate different project requirements without adding needless complexity. As more customers consider storage, solar installers need battery solutions that can be configured for different loads, applications, and long-term goals.

Rubix Battery is a U.S.-based energy storage manufacturer specializing in lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) solar batteries. Drawing on personal, hands-on experience with solar and off-grid installations, the company develops battery systems with an emphasis on practical installation, scalable capacity, and long-term performance.

Battery specifications are important, but they’re only one part of what makes a battery good for a project. Installation requirements, wiring access, inverter compatibility, and the ability to expand a system all affect how well a product fits.

Rubix Battery knows what makes installation easier because the batteries were designed by installers with hands-on, real-world experience. They bring that knowledge and experience into product design. Features like enclosed wiring compartments, magnetic covers, modular stacking, and closed-loop communication with compatible inverters help simplify system setup. Integrated safety components and designs that eliminate the need for external racking or exposed cabling further streamline installation.

This installer-focused approach also gives contractors flexibility when planning systems for customers with different energy demands. No matter what type of project, both residential and off-grid, Rubix offers two battery platforms to address a range of storage needs while giving customers reliable energy storage that grows as their needs do.

Battery performance over time depends in part on how effectively individual cells remain balanced. Rubix Battery uses Impactiv Balancing™ technology in every battery to help maintain consistent voltage throughout the battery pack.

Unlike traditional passive balancing, which dissipates excess energy from higher-voltage cells as heat, Impactiv Balancing™ transfers energy from higher-voltage cells to lower-voltage cells. The system also incorporates dissipative balancing when a cell reaches a critically high voltage.

With Impactiv Balancing™, Rubix batteries balance cells at up to 20 times the speed of passive balancing and can add up to 30% more battery life. Effective cell balancing is an important consideration when evaluating battery capacity, performance, and long-term reliability for installers and system owners.

Rubix Battery offers two primary product lines: the modular Stack Series and the high-capacity R-Series. Both use LiFePO4 chemistry and include Impactiv Balancing™ technology, closed-loop communication with compatible inverters, and a 10-year warranty.

Rubix Stack Series solar batteries are modular LiFePO4 energy storage systems designed for residential and light commercial applications. Each Rubix Stack Series battery module delivers 5.12kWh of energy storage, scalable up to 32 batteries in parallel for a maximum system capacity of 163.84kWh.

Rubix R-Series solar batteries provide higher-capacity storage for demanding applications, including off-grid, agricultural, and larger energy systems. Each Rubix R-Series battery module delivers 14.336kWh to 16kWh of energy storage across three configurations (24V/560Ah, 48V/280Ah, and 48V/314Ah) and is scalable up to 63 batteries in parallel for a maximum system capacity of 1,008kWh.

Selecting a battery platform involves balancing capacity, installation requirements, and a customer’s long-term energy needs. Rubix Battery’s Stack Series and R-Series give installers two approaches to system design, from modular residential storage to high-capacity off-grid applications.

With LiFePO4 chemistry, Impactiv Balancing™ technology, and features designed around installation realities, Rubix Battery offers battery options for contractors working on a range of solar projects.
