Chef Rachel Khoo: 'You don't need fancy kitchen gadgets to cook delicious food'

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

One of the world's best-known chefs has spoken to FRANCE 24 about her new book that proves you certainly don't need fancy kitchen gadgets to cook delicious meals. The book "The Smallest Restaurant in Paris" tells her own story of starting out in a tiny Parisian flat cooking food just for two people at a time. That then led to a BBC series and a decade of writing and filming cooking stories. Rachel Khoo is now a judge on "The Great Australian Bake Off". She spoke to us in Perspective.
