French presidential debate: 'Le Pen exposed xenophobic and authoritarian aspects of her policies'

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Eve Irvine is pleased to welcome Damien Lecomte, French political scientist, researcher, and lecturer at the University of Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne. With five months to go before France's presidential election, Marine Le Pen leads the opinion polls, while Jean-Luc Mélenchon emerges as her most likely second-round opponent. Lecomte says that "the other candidates are basically fighting for the second spot" and that the center and right are "desperately trying to find a way to avoid a second round" between the two. He also warns that a Le Pen presidency could "really change the political life with more xenophobic and nationalistic policies,"even if her ability to secure a parliamentary majority remains uncertain.
