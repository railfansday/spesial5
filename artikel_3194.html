XPEL: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Dealer channel relief and a China plant feeding non-U.S. markets could reshape XPEL's margins, but both depend on a 2027 rollout.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 4, 2026, XPEL (XPEL -0.85%) reported revenue growth of 9.9% to $137.8 million alongside expanding gross margins. The automotive protective film maker faces short-term regulatory friction in U.S. auto dealerships, but it's scaling direct international distribution and expanding domestic manufacturing to drive long-term operating leverage (growing revenue faster than operating expenses).

Three key takeaways from management's comments stand out for long-term investors.

Federal scrutiny and state regulations targeting undisclosed vehicle add-ons created uncertainty for dealership partners, causing sales through that channel to flatten. Management expects fourth-quarter performance to mark the trough as dealers update sales software and offer protective films as optional accessories rather than preinstalled packages.

XPEL is transitioning to direct distribution and in-house manufacturing, expanding its footprint through a new plant in Texas and an acquired facility in China. Supplying non-U.S. markets from the Chinese facility in early 2027 will lower production costs and expand gross margin (the percentage of revenue remaining after direct cost of goods sold).

Opportunities with original equipment manufacturers (OEMs, vehicle makers) have expanded rapidly, with a project pipeline double the company's historical total. The acquisition of the graphics supplier VISCO adds vehicle personalization capabilities and opens access to new auto manufacturer accounts.

Short-term headwinds in the Middle East create friction, yet operational progress stays on track. Internal manufacturing investments in Texas remain scheduled for a mid-2027 launch, supporting long-term operating margin goals while disciplined overhead cost control provides steady efficiency.

With regulatory resets nearing completion and direct international operations scaling up, execution across key markets positions the business for broader momentum.
