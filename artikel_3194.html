Park-Ohio Holdings Corp. (PKOH -8.94%) reported record third quarter revenue and increased its full-year outlook based on sustained demand in high-growth industrial sectors. Management stated that growth is being driven by strategic positioning in the aerospace and defense, semiconductor, and AI data center markets, which complemented stability in traditional industrial segments.
