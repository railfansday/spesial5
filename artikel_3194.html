CMA Awards Voters Choose Ella Langley, As Singer Walks Away With Six Trophies

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ella Langley took home six CMA Awards tonight, including Entertainer of the Year, Female Vocalist of the Year, Single of the Year, Music Video of the Year and Song of the Year for “Choosin’ Texas,” and Album of the Year for Dandelion.

Other winners include Chris Stapleton, Brooks & Dunn, Hardy, Eric Church, Morgan Wallen and Tim McGraw.

The night also saw Taylor Swift’s first CMAs performance in 13 years. She took the stage for a medley of “I Knew It, I Knew You” from Toy Story 5 and “Our Song” from her debut album, with Keith Urban on banjo.
