‘No Me Without You’ Director Janno Jürgens Dives Into the Complex Love-Hate Relationship Between Sisters in the Tallinn World Premiere

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

“No Me Without You” director Janno Jürgens became fascinated by the love-hate relationship between sisters.

“There’s something deeply primal about it,” he said.

“A sister or brother can be the person closest to you, but also your very first rival. He wondered: “Perhaps the line between love and hate isn’t as clear as we’d like to believe?”

In the film, written by Jürgens and Anti Naulainen, a tragedy brings two estranged sisters (Eva Koldits and Katariina Unt) together. Ridden with guilt and moral ambiguity, they embark on a haunting emotional journey.

Jürgens wasn’t immediately drawn to the story, he admitted. But eventually, he found his way in.

“After my first feature, ‘Rain,’ I realized I really enjoyed watching darker psychological thrillers. I had always been drawn to the genre. Anti’s idea could naturally fit into this genre framework.”

“We carry grievances, guilt and memories with us, and we may eventually lose that closeness with the people who matter most to us. This leads me to a very important question: is it possible to restore that unconditional closeness we once had? Can we find our way back to another person, even when an entire abyss has formed between us over the years?”

He wanted the actresses to participate in the creative process.

“We had many meetings before filming, discussing the screenplay and the characters in great detail. I was particularly interested in the women’s perspective. As a result, we made quite a few changes,” he explained.

“I had the feeling that, in making this film, we were all venturing into uncharted territory. It was an incredibly fascinating journey, but also a difficult one. When you’re moving through unfamiliar terrain, the usual safety nets no longer apply.”

He wasn’t interested in a straightforward drama.

“I’ve never been drawn to what happens to be popular, mainstream, safe or easily understood. For me, the interesting things have always been somewhere else,” he said, revealing that the odd, supernatural elements in the story brought him a sense of comfort.

“Introducing a broader, almost incomprehensible dimension places these events in an entirely different perspective. It reminds me how small we really are within this vast system that we understand so little about. And how far we still are from fully understanding it.”

“Considering the immense scale of the universe, how can we ever claim anything with absolute certainty? How can we be so sure that something that happens to us is merely a coincidence? I’m not saying that a greater plan exists. What interests me is the possibility that our understanding of reality may be extremely limited.”

Jürgens would like to see more films that dare to be “bold, unexpected and genuinely interesting.”

“Everything has become so instantly and easily accessible. People often justify this by saying that life is already difficult enough, and that what people need is simple entertainment. I don’t particularly like that idea,” he observed.

“I don’t believe that a difficult life should mean simple art. Films increasingly seem to blend into one another, and it’s becoming harder and harder to experience something that genuinely surprises or touches you. I miss a certain sense of displacement, unpredictability and uniqueness. Something that takes me, even for a moment, outside the world I already know.”

His characters certainly go to dark places. But he wanted to explore whether there’s a way out of that.

“The extremity of these situations and the question of how far two sisters would go to achieve their goals fascinated me. If I couldn’t see some kind of hope in all this darkness, I’m not sure portraying it would hold much meaning for me.”

People can hurt each other terribly, and yet love can still exist between them.

“Family is a very particular kind of place in that sense. What has interested me most is the question of whether love can survive even when almost everything else has been destroyed.”
