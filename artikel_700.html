Hello Sunshine’s Lauren Neustadter Stepping Down at Year’s End (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The longtime president of film and television is leaving after nearly a decade at Reese Witherspoon’s company and on the heels of a recent ownership change.

Changes continue at Reese Witherspoon’s Hello Sunshine, where the company’s top creative executive, Lauren Neustadter, is stepping down at year’s end. The longtime collaborators announced the move on Friday afternoon.

“Lauren’s taste, passion and leadership have been integral to Hello Sunshine’s film and television success,” said Witherspoon, founder of Hello Sunshine. “I’m so proud of what we accomplished together and grateful for her partnership and the lasting foundation she built around telling powerful, complex female-driven stories that resonate.” Related Stories Business Reese Witherspoon's Hello Sunshine Breaks Away From Candle Media Business From 'F1' to 'Slow Horses' to 'Call My Agent!': Mediawan Executives Take Madrid Spotlight

“Building Hello Sunshine alongside Reese and our incredible team has been the privilege of a lifetime,” added Neustadter. “I’m deeply proud of all the shows and movies we’ve made, the doors we’ve opened for new voices, and the ways we have put women in the spotlight both in front of and behind the camera. I am grateful for every moment of it, and I will always be cheering for Hello Sunshine.”

Neustadter has served as president of film and television at Hello Sunshine since she joined the company in 2017. Over her tenure, she executive produced a number of TV series, including The Morning Show, Big Little Lies, Little Fires Everywhere, The Last Thing He Told Me, Truth Be Told and Surface — also spearheading adaptations of Reese’s Book Club picks Tiny Beautiful Things and Daisy Jones & The Six. This past summer, Hello Sunshine premiered Legally Blonde prequel Elle at Prime Video and Lucky on Apple TV. Neustadter also worked on features Where the Crawdads Sing, Your Place or Mine, You’re Cordially Invited and TriStar’s upcoming adaptation of The Nightingale. She will remain with the company through the end of the year to support the transition.

Her departure comes during a period of transition for Hello Sunshine. In October, Witherspoon teamed up with Mediawan to buy the company back from Candle Media. Hello Sunshine, which Witherspoon founded with Seth Rodsky in 2016, was acquired by the Blackstone-backed company in 2021 in a deal valued at $900 million.

In partnering with the growing French media company, Hello Sunshine joins a sprawling portfolio of entertainment brands that includes Chernin Entertainment and Plan B Entertainment. But the shift has also meant changes at the company. Hello Sunshine’s sale was followed by layoffs its direct-to-consumer division.

Prior to her near-decade at Hello Sunshine, Neustadter worked at old Fox — where she was senior vice president of programming at the broadcast network, overseeing such series as The Mindy Project and Brooklyn Nine-Nine. She joined the network after a tenure at 20th Century Fox, where she worked on such films as Parental Guidance and Witherspoon vehicle This Means War. While at 20th, she was included The Hollywood Reporter’s Next Gen list — the magazine’s annual assessment of the 35 fastest rising entertainment executives under the age of 35.
