Indie App Spotlight: ‘Salon’ is an Apple TV app that turns your TV into a picture frame

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Welcome to Indie App Spotlight. This is a weekly 9to5Mac series where we showcase the latest apps in the indie app world. If you’re a developer and would like your app featured, get in contact.

You likely have a TV on your wall that generally sits idle throughout the day. While the Apple TV does offer loads of built in screensavers, you might be looking for something a bit more artsy. Salon offers a picture frame experience for the Apple TV, where it has over 1000 open-access paintings to display.

In short, this app replicates the Samsung Frame TV experience but for anyone who owns an Apple TV. As mentioned above, you can choose from over 1000 hand-picked art pieces to choose from the world’s museums, but you can also import your own photos from iCloud.

Salon lets you add a frame to the edge of your photos, or you can display them edge-to-edge if you’d like. You can also add a clock and date to the corner.

I bought a Samsung Frame a few years back and was pretty appalled by Tizen and the fact that they charge a subscription for the art mode. It annoyed me enough to build my own, at first just for my own TV. I also noticed that tvOS gets very little love from indie developers. Outside Apple’s own apps and the big streaming apps, it’s mostly aquariums and fireplaces. Although now with the whole vibe coding craze, there are a few more interesting additions as well.
