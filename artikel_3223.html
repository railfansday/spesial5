Fidelity National Financial, Inc. (FNF +2.21%) reported quarterly results supported by record commercial title performance and growth in its F&G annuities segment. Management reported that the title segment generated an industry-leading 17.1% margin despite a low-transaction environment in residential real estate driven by elevated mortgage rates. The company is managing through the current cycle by focusing on operational discipline and long-term investments in technology and recruitment. Management stated that the F&G segment has surpassed $75 billion in assets under management before reinsurance, contributing 22% of the company's adjusted net earnings for the first nine months of the year. The company continues to prioritize capital returns, having returned $561 million to shareholders year to date through dividends and share repurchases.
