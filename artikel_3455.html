Robert Pattinson's Heist Thriller Officially Sets Netflix Release Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Robert Pattinson is everywhere currently. After an extensive search, the actor was selected as the new James Bond, drawing wide social media commentary about his career trajectory. From Harry Potter to Twilight and Batman, it's hard to miss Pattinson in some of Hollywood's biggest movies. He appeared this year in Christopher Nolan's blockbuster The Odyssey, and come December, fans will see him again in Denis Villeneuve's Dune: Part Three. And that's not all. The actor also has another project coming out next year, but this time on streaming.

The film is a heist thriller in which Pattinson stars as former Captain Noah. Fresh out of the Army, he is trying to find his footing in civilian life when charismatic master thief Christian Carter recruits him to join his crew for the score of a lifetime: robbing the richest bank in Manhattan. The otherwise meticulous plan has one hitch: Noah's girlfriend, Rose, works at the bank as a teller. With time and love on the line, Noah must figure out who he can trust if he hopes to make it out of the heist alive.

Titled The Take, the thriller was directed by Fernando Meirelles (The Two Popes) and written by Simon Kinberg (Mr. & Mrs. Smith). Other cast members include Denzel Washington as Carter, the master thief behind it all; Daisy Edgar-Jones as Rose; Danai Gurira (Oaklynn); Sean Harris (Logan); Moisés Arias (Gabriel); and Justin Kirk (Anderson). The Take streams on Netflix on Friday, January 15, 2027.

The streamer has started unveiling next year's releases, and The Take is one of two films releasing early in 2027. The other is Danny Boyle's INK, which debuts in theaters on December 11, 2026, before streaming on January 8, 2027. TV shows include The Twin (January 1), Jamie Dornan and MacKenzie Davis' crime thriller; Old Dog, New Tricks Season 2 (January 1), a Spanish dramedy; 3-Body Problem Season 2 (January 14), the hit sci-fi drama from Game of Thrones creators that is returning after three years; Grown Ups (January 14), a family drama costarring Robert Sheehan (Umbrella Academy); Blue Eye Samurai Season 2 (January 19), an animated series; Ginny & Georgia (Season 4) returns with more mother-daughter and YA drama; and The Palace of Secrets (January 22), a Spanish period romance drama starring Carlos Cuevas (Merli) and Carla Campra.

Stream The Take when it hits Netflix in January, and stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
