BYD is in exclusive talks to buy Ineos Automotive's French car plant

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Hambach would keep building the Grenadier and Quartermaster for the medium term if talks conclude, as Ineos plans U.S. output

Ineos Automotive, a subsidiary of Ineos, is in exclusive negotiations to sell its Hambach assembly plant in France to to BYD, Ineos Automotive said on Tuesday.

If the talks conclude successfully, Ineos Automotive would continue to produce the Grenadier and Quartermaster at Hambach for the medium term, the company said. Hambach sits near France's eastern border with Germany and formerly built Smart cars, according to Bloomberg.

BYD said it intends to use the site to serve European buyers. "Our aim is to build vehicles there with European drivers in mind," the company said, according to Bloomberg.

Ineos Automotive disclosed the talks alongside plans to start producing the Grenadier and Quartermaster in the United States in 2028. The company has shortlisted U.S. sites and expects to make a final decision on the facility within the next few months. The U.S. accounts for 70% of Grenadier global sales, the company said.

The shift to U.S. production is meant to avoid trade tariffs, according to Bloomberg.

"This is arguably the biggest moment in the history of INEOS Automotive," Lynn Calder, CEO of Ineos Automotive, said in a statement. "Setting up production there makes so much sense. It will empower us to invest, expand, and reach even more customers around the world."

The company did not state terms for the proposed sale in its release.

BYD Executive Vice President Stella Li said in October that France was on the shortlist for a future BYD factory in Europe. Li said BYD was looking closely at setting up a plant in France and wanted people there to think of it as a local company. BYD plans to open additional European factories after its first plant, in Szeged, Hungary, begins mass production.

BYD registered 13,935 passenger vehicles in France in the first half of 2026, a 136% increase from the same period a year earlier.
