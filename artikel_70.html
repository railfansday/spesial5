Spain’s political crossroads, Europe’s wider stakes: Spaniards to vote in key election

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The stakes are high – not only for people in Spain, but also for Europe. On November 29, the country holds a parliamentary election that could see the end of the Socialist-led government and potentially a coalition between the right and the far right, in one of the EU’s biggest and most influential member states. It could also signal the end of Pedro Sanchez’s third term in office. Among other things, the prime minister may pay the political price for two shocking train crashes and the migrant crisis in Spain’s North African exclave of Ceuta.
