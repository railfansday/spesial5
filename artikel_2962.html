Newsmax: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Newsmax's streaming library more than doubled to over 700 specials, and engagement time per account rose 19%, a sign viewers are staying longer.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 12, 2026, Newsmax (NMAX +31.41%) announced underlying revenue grew 21.6% year over year to a record $55 million, after excluding a $14 million nonrecurring service termination fee. The media company generated $5 million in adjusted EBITDA, which represents earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization. This marks its second consecutive profitable quarter on that basis.

Expanding contractual distribution deals and international partnerships are strengthening higher-margin cash flows. Three takeaways from management's comments on the call stand out.

Affiliate fees, which are distribution payments from television operators to carry channel programming, expanded 77.4% year over year to $14.3 million. Re-negotiated multiyear agreements set higher pricing floors that compound across future reporting periods.

Licensing revenue, which represents fees earned by allowing foreign broadcasting partners to use channel branding and content, rose 343% year over year. Contracted international agreements provide high-margin revenues that insulate total sales from domestic political advertising cycles.

Newsmax+, the company's paid video streaming application, expanded its library from 300 titles to over 700 specials. Account engagement time rose 19% year over year as viewers spent more time consuming original and licensed video programming.

Distribution expansion and contractual rate repricing are driving profitability improvements across digital and broadcast operations.

Capital allocation priorities center on programming assets and multi-platform reach while management reduces legacy newsletter overhead. With zero balance sheet debt and $137.6 million in total cash and short-term investments, financial flexibility supports long-term strategic execution.
