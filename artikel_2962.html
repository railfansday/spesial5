Nvidia’s Leadership Shakeup Tests AI Chip Giant

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Bloomberg senior technology and strategic industries editor Mike Shepard tells Bloomberg This Weekend that Nvidia’s appointment of longtime CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO may offer investors some stability after Jensen Huang’s abrupt departure. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Shepard examines how Nvidia moves beyond a leader who defined the company for more than three decades and whether the upheaval creates an opening for AI chip rivals. (Source: Bloomberg)
