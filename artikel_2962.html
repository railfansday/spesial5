Pistons vs Raptors preview: 2nd NBA Cup game

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Early season Fridays = NBA Cup day! The Detroit Pistons can climb to 2-0 in group play with a win over the worse-than-expected Toronto Raptors (1-1 in group play). The NBA thought this would've been a big game, handing out the Prime Video first game slot, but injuries, injuries, injuries.

Ron Holland, Duncan Robinson, and Jalen Duren are out again, but Cade Cunningham is expected to play. Injuries have slowed down the Raptors a ton.

RJ Barrett and Collin Murray-Boyles won't suit up in Detroit. Kawhi Leonard remains out. His absence is the main source of Toronto's struggle, but I still expected more.

The Raptors are still live even though they should be better. That said, Detroit isn't a team that takes opponents lightly because of who's in or out. The Pistons play the same brand of ball regardless of who's out there on either side.

Wednesday's 122-109 win over the New Orleans Pelicans tripled down on that point. Even a shorthanded Pistons squad without Cade manages to find a way.

Daniss Jenkins. Javonte Green. Ausar Thompson with the ball. Paul Reed with extended minutes — trustworthy things.

We got Isaiah Joe back in the lineup in that one! One of the marquee signings of Detroit's offseason let it fly. Joe went 3-for-5 from range and put it on the floor, aiding Detroit's flow. Bball Paul said Joe had more in his game during media day. Paul knows ball.

The ability to leverage your shooting with live dribble decisions is an invaluable skill come playoff time. Love seeing Joe back out there, and more of that would be cool.

John Collins was on a heater (4-6 on 3s). He should get six 3s up every night with the room he has to fire. Collins will be matched up with Jakob Poeltl tonight. Poeltl is arguably the biggest hacker in the league. His 5.6 foul percentage ranks in the fourth percentile among bigs.

A lot of those Cade drives should end with trips to the charity stripe. Cade's .37 FTr is a career high. He's getting the calls and shooting ~86 percent at the line. That works. Cade is also putting together a strong All-Defense case, being a big-time contributor on the sixth-best defense.

Per Stathead, only Cade, Nikola Jokić, Matisse Thybulle, Scottie Barnes, and Caleb Wilson are averaging at least 0.9 blocks and 1.4 steals. Cade is doing this without fouling. He's leveling up even more on that end.

Barnes recently declared himself the best defender in the world. I have no problem with that. He is up there. He's a legit 1-5 defender. Anybody that good at something should feel they're the best. That confidence got them where they are.

Barnes is Barnes, but I thought Toronto would have a more intimidating team defense. Cleaning The Glass ranks them 17th overall and 19th over the past two weeks + they are on a six-game losing streak.

The injury bug cannot be ignored, but I thought Toronto would be immune to long stretches of defensive lapses because of their suffocating gap principles.

Those principles still force turnovers at a frightening rate (2nd), but they haven't dug in and clamped down like last year's Raptors team. They're still feisty enough to muddy up a game, but I expected more.

Toronto's off-the-bounce creation takes a big blow with no Kawhi and Barrett. Barnes has more shot-making responsibilities this season. His 23.8 points per game are a career high. Immanuel Quickley will get them up. Catching fire vs. Ausar and the Pistons' defense isn't easy, but Toronto is looking to Quickley to provide immediate offense.

Jamison Battle is one of Toronto's consistent snipers. He's not a shooter you can disregard and is coming off a 19-point, 5-for-10 from 3 game. Ja'Kobe Walter can shoot it too and is quietly turning into a dependable heavy-minute wing.

With a win, Detroit could be in first or second in the East Group A division. The Orlando Magic (2-0) take on the struggling Brooklyn Nets (0-1) tonight. Should Orlando snag a win, they stay atop the group.

One game and one week at a time for Detroit in this tournament. They smacked around the Nets in their first cup game and look to do the same against the Raptors.

Detroit Pistons (10-4): Cade Cunningham, Daniss Jenkins, Kevin Huerter, Ausar Thompson, John Collins

Toronto Raptors (5-10): Immanuel Quickley, Ja'Kobe Walter, Jamison Battle, Scottie Barnes, Jakob Poeltl

The New York Knicks won the NBA Cup tourney and the real thing last year. Did that add any validity to the tournament?
