Geron Corporation (GERN +16.13%) reported its first quarter of profitability since its transition to a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company, supported by RYTELO adoption and reduced operating expenses. Management stated that the company is focused on deepening its penetration into the U.S. community oncology market, where it sees thousands of additional eligible patients. The company confirmed its full-year revenue targets while lowering expense projections following a strategic workforce reduction implemented in late 2025. Strategic priorities now include evaluating business development opportunities to expand the hematology pipeline following the discontinuation of the Phase 3 IMpactMF trial.
