VENU Honors Jorge Juárez With Disruptor Award at 2026 Billboard Live Music Summit | Billboard

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Billboard and Live Music Summit presenting partner VENU honored Jorge Juárez with the Disruptor Award at the 2026 Billboard Live Music Summit. The festivities began the night before at VENU’s Music & Money Cocktail Hour, where industry executives gathered for drinks and conversation, including a fireside chat with VENU Founder, Chairman and CEO J.W. Roth and Ron Bension, former President and CEO of ASM Global, veteran entertainment executive and strategic advisor to VENU, moderated by Billboard Co-Chief Content Officer Jason Lipshutz.

Last year, we partnered with Billboard to create the Disruptor Award. We honor people at our company that break barriers and bring change. Jorge, you have earned this, and I am super excited to award you the 2026 Disruptor Award.

Thank you very much. I’m really happy to accept this award. After all these years, I’ve seen that it’s not just about selling tickets; it’s about creating these experiences for the fans.
