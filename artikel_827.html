‘Pa’ Él’ Lifts Los Ángeles Azules & Tini to No. 1 on Regional Mexican Airplay Chart

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

It's a first for Tini and a ninth chart-topper for the Mexican siblings.

Sr. Charts & Data Analyst (Latin & Billboard Español)

Los Ángeles Azules and Tini join on their first collaborative No. 1 with “Pa’ Él” on the Regional Mexican Airplay chart (dated Nov. 14). Their new team-up leaps 2–1, giving Tini her first chart-topper and the Iztapalapa-born siblings their ninth champ. Explore Los Angeles Azules TINI See latest videos, charts and news

“Pa’ Él” comes in first on Regional Mexican Airplay following a 23% gain in audience impressions, earning 7.2 million in the United States in the week ending Nov. 5, according to Luminate. It bumps Karol G’s “Alguien Que Te Amaba” from the lead, after a one-week reign, as the latter dips 1-7, down 32%. Tini’s new peak surpasses her previous best on Regional Mexican Airplay: “Vuelve,” with Ricky Martin and Los Ángeles Azules, reached No. 5 in May.

The Los Ángeles Azules siblings score their ninth chart-topper—and third of 2026—making this their most successful year on the chart. They earned one No. 1 each in 2000, 2019, 2023 and 2025, and two in 2024. Their longest-reigning hit remains “Nunca Es Suficiente,” with Natalia Lafourcade, which led for three consecutive weeks in 2019.

Beyond its Regional Mexican Airplay coronation, “Pa’ Él” nearly misses the No. 1 spot on the overall Latin Airplay chart across its 152 Latin-reporting stations in the United States. There, the song climbs 5-2 with 7.5 million audience impressions, up 21% during the tracking period.

The new No. 2 mark becomes Tini’s best chart performance to date among her four entries. The pop singer reached No. 4 through “Vuelve.”

Thanks to its radio haul, “Pa’ Él” flies from No. 11 to No. 5 on the Hot Regional Mexican Songs chart, which combines streams, audience impressions, and sales into its formula.
