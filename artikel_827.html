Ellen White and Ben Haines take the pod on the road to the home of the ‘WSL disruptors’, London City Lionesses! They’re joined by special guest Jana Fernandez to discuss London City's massive shift in mindset this season, growing their fanbase, and her own impressive form. Plus, the Spanish defender reveals who she’s battling with in fiery training sessions ahead of their blockbuster WSL clash against Arsenal this weekend. Ben catches up with Jana's LCL teammate Alexia Putellas following her historic third Ballon d’Or win and to wrap up, Ellen and Ben preview two massive top-of-the-table clashes that could shake up the title race!
