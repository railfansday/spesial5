Vrancken proud of Hearts players despite last-gasp defeat to Monaco

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Hearts vs Monaco. UEFA Conference League Round 1.

Tynecastle ParkAttendance: Attendance15,164.

Hearts lost 3-2 at home to Monaco in the Conference League; Jordi Altena opened the scoring; Sabah Kerjota pulled the Jambos back level after goals from Folarin Balogun and Edan Diop; Balogun netted again in stoppage-time; the Jambos have one point from their opening four games

Wouter Vrancken expressed pride in his Hearts team after an agonising 3-2 defeat at home to Monaco left their Conference League hopes hanging by a thread.

The Jambos looked on course to pick up their second league-phase point after stunning first-half strikes from Jordi Altena and Sabah Kerjota either side of goals from visiting pair Folarin Balogun and Edian Diop.

But in the fourth and final minute of added time, injury-hit Hearts were denied what would have been a hugely commendable result when USA forward Balogun found space in the box to slot home and maintain Monaco's perfect record in the competition.

"The biggest thing is, of course, to be really proud of the performance we put on the park today, because it's against a really good side," said Vrancken, keen to reiterate that his team remains without several key players.

"Knowing that we have still nine injuries, I will mention it, and we have a lot of players not on the European list today and to still do this against that team, I'm really proud of what we did."

A point would have significantly boosted Hearts' hopes of reaching the knockout phase, but they can now only finish on a maximum of seven points - the same tally they managed two years ago before being eliminated on goal difference under Neil Critchley.

With their goal difference currently standing at minus five, Hearts will likely need high-scoring victories against Bosnian side Borac and Cypriot outfit Pafos in their final two matches to stand a chance of breaking into the top 24 and extending their European campaign beyond Christmas.

Vrancken, however, does not believe the late goal will make a significant difference to his side's prospects.

"I think the point doesn't make a difference," he said. "One or no points doesn't change anything in the next games. There's also something that really was pleasing for me, that when it was 2-2, we still wanted to go.

"We wanted to go for the win here, and against a side like this, in the situation we're in, so really proud of this also, because three points would make a difference.

"Zero or one point doesn't make a difference towards the next games, so you have to win the next games."Vrancken believes the quality of the goals scored by Altena and Kerjota, both exquisite finishes from range, highlighted the renewed belief coursing through his squad after their 8-0 win over Dundee last weekend.

"They were quality goals," he said. "You see the confidence those guys have, and this is what we wanted after the weekend, of course, that you take some confidence of the dynamics we did there.

"I think the conviction around the box was there, also in both boxes actually. It's an important thing to take with us towards the next games coming, especially in this run of games."
