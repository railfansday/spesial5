Bitcoin crashes to $59,000 as Anthropic falls 25% from yesterday’s IPO-day high

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bitcoin slid to its lowest price since July 1, while the Nasdaq Composite fell nearly 3% in Friday morning trading.

Bitcoin fell below $60,000 on Friday for the first time since July as US technology stocks slid and Anthropic shares fell about 25% from their IPO-day high.

Bitcoin traded at $59,470, down 5.66% over the previous 24 hours, according to CryptoSlate’s Bitcoin price data.

Anthropic’s Nasdaq debut Thursday drew strong demand for the maker of Claude. Shares opened at $111.50, 34% above the $83 offer price, and climbed to $115.10 before closing at $97.17, still 17% above the offer. Anthropic said Friday the IPO totaled $93.5 billion in gross proceeds after underwriters exercised their option to buy more shares.

The listing gave stock investors a direct stake in a leading AI lab, making Anthropic’s shares a gauge of investor appetite for AI stocks. By Friday afternoon UTC, the stock was down 11% on the day at $86.48, about 25% below its debut high and just 4% above the IPO price.

The Nasdaq Composite was down 2.85% after losing 2.25% Thursday. Nvidia shares were down 6.3%. The S&P 500 fell 2.02% Thursday, its sixth straight decline.

The sell-off followed the Wednesday release of Federal Reserve minutes showing almost all policymakers expected further rate increases would likely be appropriate under their baseline outlooks. The Fed held its benchmark rate at 3.75% to 4% in October.
