A Coinbase price projection showing XRP at only $1.57 by 2035 has drawn mockery and debate across the XRP community. The tool lists XRP at $1.06 in 2027, rising slowly to $1.23 by 2030 and $1.57 by 2035. However, the figures are not a Coinbase forecast. The calculator applies a fixed annual growth rate of about 5% by default, which users can change, to the current price near $1. The post reignited the argument over Ripple’s monthly XRP releases. One holder argued that adding supply naturally pushes prices down, much like inflation. Lawyer Bill Morgan rejected that view, pointing out that Ripple was releasing around 300 million XRP a month in late 2024 and early 2025, yet the price still climbed from about $0.50 to $3.60. He said Ripple’s sales are too small a share of total trading to explain price moves and that no one has produced evidence otherwise in six years.
