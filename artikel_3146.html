Kodak SlideTag is a $35 AirTag alternative with an E-Ink display for your photos

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Offering support for iPhone and Android’s tracking networks, the new Kodak SlideTag offers the neat added functionality over an AirTag of being able to show your photos and more on an E-Ink display.

The Kodak SlideTag is inspired by classic 35mm slides from Kodak cameras, and is built by the same company, Reto, that launched a keychain digital camera inspired by old Kodak cameras too. The tracker’s E-Ink display is designed mainly to showcase your photos, with storage built-in for up to 12 photos, but also the ability to show lists and QR codes.

The SlideTag’s extra features are controlled by the Kodak Memory Link app, while tracking is handled in the Find My app on iPhone just like an AirTag (and the Find Hub app on Android phones).

There are nine total styles for the SlideTag, all sold in a blind box format. You can order one tracker for $35 with the whole set also available for purchase.

Eight of the styles have a one-in-eight chance of finding, while the ninth style is only found in one out of every 64 boxes.

Kodak SlideTag is available today from Amazon.
