Allot Ltd. (ALLT) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Recurring security revenue is becoming a growing share of Allot's business.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Allot Ltd. (ALLT -13.27%) reported a return to sustainable profitability driven by double-digit growth in its core security and network intelligence business lines. Management highlighted the transition toward a recurring revenue model, with Security-as-a-Service (SECaaS) now contributing one-third of total revenue. The company raised its full-year revenue outlook and emphasized its strong cash position and lack of debt as foundations for both organic growth and potential acquisitions. Management stated that the growing sophistication of cyber threats, particularly those utilizing artificial intelligence, is increasing demand among communication service providers for network-native security solutions.
