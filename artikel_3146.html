What it's about: Set in 1982, "The End of Oak Street" follows the Platt family (Anne Hathaway, Ewan McGregor, Maisy Stella and Christian Convery), whose ordinary suburban neighborhood is suddenly transported to an unknown prehistoric world following a mysterious cosmic event. With dinosaurs and other dangerous creatures roaming their once-familiar streets, the family must stick together, find a way home and fight to survive their bizarre new surroundings.

Why you should watch it: "The End of Oak Street" taps into the kind of old-school adventure that made creature features so much fun in the first place. Anne Hathaway and Ewan McGregor make an entertaining pairing, while the arrival of prehistoric creatures leads to some exciting and genuinely tense encounters. What it's about: "12 12 12" is an eight-part heist thriller following a disgraced FBI agent (Anthony Mackie) and an American career criminal (Jamie Dornan) caught in a dangerous game of cat and mouse across Europe. Their paths collide during an ambitious attempt to rob a heavily secured bank vault beneath Zurich. The story unfolds across three timelines: the 12 months of planning, 12 hours of execution and 12 days following the heist.

Why you should watch it: If you're a fan of heist thrillers, "12 12 12" is worth adding to your watchlist. Anthony Mackie and Jamie Dornan bring an entertaining dynamic to the story, while its three-timeline structure gives the central robbery a few unexpected turns. The series also makes good use of its European locations, with slick action sequences and tense encounters adding to the excitement. It's a solid option for anyone who enjoys a heist movie that keeps you guessing.
