The ‘French Milei’? Presidential candidate David Lisnard aims to reshape France’s right wing

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Having secured reelection victory Wednesday as leader of France's mayors, Cannes Mayor David Lisnard turns once more to build on his recent bid for the Élysée. As his Nouvelle Énergie movement gains momentum, questions swirl over whether his campaign will reshape the French political landscape, or if his party will ultimately cosy up to the far right ahead of France’s 2027 race to the polls.
