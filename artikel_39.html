Spain's Alcaraz beats error-prone Shelton to win Paris Masters for first time

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Spanish tennis star Carlos Alcaraz avenged his loss to American player Ben Shelton in the US Open in September as he inched ahead of his rival Sunday to win the Paris Masters for the first time. It was Shelton's first defeat in a Masters final.
