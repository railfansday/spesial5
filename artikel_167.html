Why car keys are so expensive to replace

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Where Readers Share Great Links. Join Free

Modern car keys have evolved into encrypted fobs packed with security tech, which makes replacement and reprogramming far more costly. Carmakers’ anti-theft systems, restricted programming tools, and dealer service fees all push prices up, and some models can cost hundreds or even over $1,000 to replace.
