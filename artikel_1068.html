Two weeks is a long time in football. Before Bournemouth faced Sturm Graz in the Europa League 16 days ago, they had yet to taste victory in the Premier League this season and were coming off the back of a 5-1 defeat at Chelsea.

Suddenly, things appear very sweet indeed for the Cherries. They welcome Leeds United looking to extend a run of five successive wins, in which they have scored 14 goals and conceded none. AC Milan, Manchester United and Aston Villa have each been dispatched 3-0 in Bournemouth's past three games, the latter to send Marco Rose's side into the Carabao Cup quarter-finals.

The German manager has well and truly found a winning formula following a slow start to the campaign, and they will surely climb into the top half of the table before too long.

Last season's top scorer Junior Kroupi returned from injury in the 3-0 win over United last Sunday but is not yet ready to start, while midfielder Alex Scott also misses out despite nearing the end of his recovery from a thigh issue.

Leeds have shown plenty of resilience this season, so it will be fascinating to see how they fare against the free-scoring Cherries.

Daniel Farke's men have drawn five of their eight Premier League games, including three of the last four, with the 2-1 loss against champions Arsenal earlier this month their only defeat of the campaign.

The Whites have had some positive team news since drawing 0-0 at Sunderland last Sunday, with Nico Elvedi available after recovering from illness and both Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Noah Okafor in contention after shrugging off fitness issues.

Dan James could return to the squad following a back problem, but Anton Stach is doubtful after suffering a foot injury.

The Cherries are in the groove, so we reckon they will breach Leeds' mean defence and claim a sixth straight win.
