The Softest Part of my Day Starts with On’s Cloudsurfer 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

When life feels hard for influencer Aliyah Grissett, she lightens it up with her daily run.

A day full of hard things calls for a running shoe that’s soft. Fitness and travel influencer Aliyah Grissett experiences that every afternoon in On’s Cloudsurfer 3. When she laces up and starts her warmup, the pavement beneath her practically melts away thanks to the Cloudsurfer 3’s Surreal™ superfoam and CloudTec®.

For Grissett, running is more than a workout. It’s a slice of the day she can claim for herself, away from all the work and other obligations demanding her attention. “Some days, everything feels like work,” she said. “But then there’s that moment when you start running…and everything just feels soft. This is my time to let the feelings out and take all the beauty in.”

On’s Cloudsurfer 3 is a daily runner, designed for a smooth ride on the road. Its re-engineered warp-knit upper is breathable and holds the foot securely during every run. But what really makes it perfect as a daily runner is the technology under foot: Surreal™ superfoam and CloudTec®. The combination allows for a soft, cushioned underfoot feeling when running. This makes Cloudsurfer 3 a natural choice for Grissett’s everyday miles, giving her comfort and cushioning she looks forward to.

After the run, she cools down and carries that effortless vibe into the rest of her day, whether that means grabbing a coffee or relaxing by the beach. “I’ve found the ultimate comfort in my On Cloudsurfer 3,” she said. “They make it easy to show up for myself each day.”
