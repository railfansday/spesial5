Rayman Legends Retold Reveals Rabbids Outfit Exclusive To Switch 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Rayman Legends Retold arrives this December and you'll be able to dress him up in all sorts of costumes. We've already got a look at the Assassin's Creed one and some other designs, and Ubisoft has now revealed another exclusive costume.

If you're getting the game on the Nintendo Switch 2, you'll be able to unlock a Rayman Rabbids costume for free, the first time you visit the costume shop. The original Rayman Raving Rabbids game was released as a Wii launch title in 2006, and was followed by a sequel a year later.

"Rayman’s latest fashion statement? BWAH! Exclusive to Nintendo Switch 2, the Rayman Rabbids costume is yours for free the first time you visit Victor Velour’s costume shop in Rayman Legends Retold, available Dec 3!"

While this costume is exclusive to the Switch 2, it seems there'll also be some exclusive costumes on other platforms. On PlayStation, players will receive an Astro Bot outfit.
