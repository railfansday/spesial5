Games that belong to this genre used to give PC owners the chance to prove just how dour and serious they were. They were the reasons to justify shelling out large quantities of cash for their expensive rigs.

And while it might seem that these realistic simulacra would be poor fits for mobiles - requiring bags of computing power and complex input devices - they're actually proliferating on iOS.

After all, the modern mobile packs a walloping great processor and a hugely versatile touchscreen. You just have to get the user interface right.

You will spend your days purchasing small boxes of items from locals, stocking your shop, and hosting events inside your building. As you continue to manage this shop, you will also learn more about the townsfolk, what items they are looking for and why they are selling items. It's quite fun to manage the shop, get to know your community, and help them grow.
