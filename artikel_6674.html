The 12 Best Viral Beauty Products of 2026, According to Our Chronically Online Beauty Editors

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Not everything you see on TikTok is worth the hype, but these products sure are.

We may earn commission from links on this page, but we only recommend products we love. Promise.

I try to spend as little time as possible on my phone, but alas, as a beauty journalist, it is part of my job to stay on top of the trends and test out the most viral products that pop up on my FYP to let you know if they do, in fact, deserve the hype. It seemed like an infinite number of beauty products went viral in 2026—from scientific perfumes and cool new beauty tech to celebrity-founded brands and glowy makeup-skincare hybrids. I’ll be the first to tell you that half of the things were meh, but there were also several that completely blew team Cosmo away.

I polled our beauty editors to pull together this list of the best viral beauty products of 2026 that are 100 percent worth your hard-earned cash. These innovative items wowed us, earned permanent spots in our vanities, and even changed our lives in one way or another (no joke). You may not be able to trust everything you see on TikTok, but you can have confidence in our top viral beauty picks. Keep reading to discover them all.

One Cosmo editor says: “Few fragrances caught my—and the internet’s—attention quite like Cloud Reverie this year. The whole concept is incredible: Future Society wanted to bottle the scent of clouds, so they stuck a petri dish out of a plane to collect actual cloud microbes (!!), then viewed them under a microscope to create the most realistic cloud fragrance. The result is a soft, slightly powdery dream that swirls together fluffy vanilla, sparkling aldehydes, a touch of sugar and iris, and musk. This makes me want to smell like a cloud every day.”—MARY HONKUS, CONTRIBUTING BEAUTY EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: “I’m a sucker for beauty tech tools, and the Shark Depuffi became part of my daily routine this year. This amplifies the effects of traditional gua sha with hot-and-cold contrast therapy. The InstaChill setting instantly depuffs and sculpts, while the InstaHeat offers a relaxing tension-melting sensation. I understand why it sold out multiple times, especially when new colors were released.”—MH

One Cosmo editor says: “These are my personal holy grail lip balms, and they come in the most gorgeous chrome packaging, making them an ideal gift. My everyday go-to shade is Fig Sauce for a juicy pink finish.”—JASMINE HYMAN, ASSISTANT BEAUTY EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: “I’m not always one for celebrity-founded skincare, but as an acne-prone girl, Alix Earle’s products have been a game changer for my breakouts this year. The whole brand is worthy of its virality, trust! My personal favorites are the cleansing balm and mandelic serum, and you can try the entire lineup with this travel-ready set.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “I can’t use many bronzing drops because they ultimately break me out, but Saie’s Super Glowy Gel is my non-comedogenic go-to. Their newest shade Beachglow gives me the ideal sun-kissed glow—I wear it both under makeup and on its own for minimal glam days.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “I never thought I’d have a go-to body wash. First of all, I love changing up scents. But also, after going through a big bottle, I want something new. The Cyklar body wash changed everything for me. This makes my skin feel hydrated once I rinse it off, and I really love how sudsy it gets in the shower. I always keep a bottle of this in my bathroom now, and I get my fix for newness by swapping scents. I started with Vanilla Verve, just finished Rose Bud, and now I’m moving onto Sex Musk, the fragrance that broke the internet.”—BETH GILLETTE, BEAUTY EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: “The way this foundation melts into my skin is nothing short of magnificent. I pump out a dollop on the back of my hand and pick some product up on the M.ph Beauty Foundation Brush (a fluffy brush that gives my skin a soft focus finish while keeping coverage airy). The consistency quickly evens out my skin, effectively hiding acne and dark spots. With such a lightweight texture, I’m used to streaking. But picking up product on the brush and gently swirling it over my face gives me an even finish.”—BG

One Cosmo editor says: “We all know and love the original Coco Mademoiselle, but dare I say this new flanker is even better and will no doubt become an instant classic. The first time I tried this, I spritzed it once on my wrist and immediately left for a CorePower class. I kept getting whiffs of the scent throughout workout, which surprised me because I was drenched in sweat. Afterward, multiple people came up to ask me what perfume I was wearing because it made the room smell so good. ‘I thought they were pumping something through the vents,’ one of them told me. Hours later, when I got home and rinsed off, I could still smell remnants of this patchouli-based, slightly sweet scent."—BG

One Cosmo editor says: “This is a sunscreen for people who hate wearing sunscreen. It is so lightweight; it really feels like a milky toner more than an SPF product. I reach for it almost every day, especially if I'll be wearing makeup. Since it absorbs so quickly, it layers well with color cosmetics and doesn't lead to any pilling.”—KARA MCGRATH, CONTRIBUTING EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: “I mean, is it really a surprise that every product master makeup artist Katie Jane Hughes puts out is just plain good? I've always hated how my lower lashes looked with mascara, but this changed that: The smaller-than-usual brush makes it easy to apply product to those wispy hairs without getting black smudges everywhere. And, of course, the tubing formula makes for easy removal, which is a huge bonus.”—KM

One Cosmo editor says: “The OG Glazing Milk launched in 2023 and was an instant hit, both on social media and on my own skin. Since then, it's been a staple in my morning and evening skincare routine. So when its shimmery sister Highlight Milk launched, I knew I—and TikTok—would fall in love immediately. And that we did. It's the perfect primer for my dry skin (shoutout to ceramides and glycerin), it helps my makeup go on sooo smoothly, and it gives me reflective, glassy skin. Sometimes, I even mix it with a tinted moisturizer or skin tint for an instant dewy look.”—CATHARINE MALZAHN, CONTRIBUTING EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: “If you really need to lock your makeup in place all day long, then this is the setting powder I’ll always recommend. Inspired by the also-viral Airbrush Flawless Finish Setting Powder, this loose version kept my makeup in place for a 14-hour day in the Texas heat and humidity, not one touch-up required. It's lightweight, doesn't crease, totally erases my pores, and to me, the cherry on top is the included heart-shaped powder puff. And even though my skin's on the drier side, it doesn't cling to any dry patches. She really is that girl.”—CM
