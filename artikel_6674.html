The Gap Black Friday Sale Is a Holiday Miracle

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Gap Black Friday Sale kills two birds with one stone. The stone? Up to 60% off sitewide. The birds? Gifts for loved ones and gifts for you. Since its founding during the Summer of Love in the epicenter of the hippie revolution, the American fashion label has outfitted generations with stylish clothes both timely and timeless. And while it’s come a long way since its early days slinging Levi’s jeans and vinyl records, Gap’s designs are as relevant now as they were during Haight & Ashbury’s heyday. The prices, too, have remained accessible for the average American, even with inflation going insane.

Gap’s recent seasons have put itself back in the limelight, too, with the recent appointment of creative director Zac Posen as well as a string of killer collaborations and good ol’ fashioned Gap ads. Now that the best Black Friday clothing deals are here, you can score cartfuls of great menswear from hard-wearing denim to baby-soft cashmere sweaters—all for up to 60% off. But enough chatter. The best things to buy at Gap? Start with the hits. The brand has been on a heater lately, mining its deep archive for all-star classics while dialing in modern silhouettes and workwear staples. Outerwear (especially the lighter layers), pants, shirts, sweaters, and sweats are all strong plays—especially if you haven’t checked in for a while.

This year in the US, Black Friday is on Friday, November 28, a day after Thanksgiving. While that’s the official date, many retailers roll out deals throughout November. You can expect the biggest discounts to happen between Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday, but be on the look out for retailers to launch sales early.

Cyber Monday lands on December 1 this year, the Monday after Thanksgiving. Most brands keep the deals rolling through the week—but don’t bank on it.
