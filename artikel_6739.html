Hailey Bieber’s 30 Best Looks in Honor of Her 30th Birthday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

From polka dots and fur to red-carpet statements

Happy birthday, Hailey Bieber! The star has officially turned 30, and to mark another turn around the sun, she kicked off a week of celebrations with a new navy-blue Rhode drop, a festive campaign, and an intimate video series that followed a week in her life. Throughout the vlogs, we saw the founder in fittings and makeup chairs, spending time with son Jack Blues, and reflecting on the past decade. “Becoming a mom definitely changed me the most in my twenties,” she told the camera. “I always knew I wanted to be a mom, I always knew I wanted kids, and it just totally transforms you as a person for the better.”

Of course, the birth-week celebrations have also come with a wardrobe to match. From shearling coats to archival statements, she ushered in the new season with everything from an ivory satin Oriori coat trimmed in gray-white fur to a black silk Armani Privé Fall 2005 set to a Yohji Yamamoto Spring 1998 jacket previously seen on Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy. And we did not expect anything less from the star.

Throughout the years, Bieber has become as known for her style as for her Rhode lipgloss phone cases, playing with understated “quiet luxury,” classic Los Angeles athleisure, and tons of Saint Laurent leather. As she grew from a teenage model into a business owner and, later, young mother, her taste has evolved over time—but through it all, she has given us some major fashion inspiration.

So, in honor of her 30th birthday, we rounded up our 30 favorite looks from the star. And between her plethora of Met Gala ensembles, polka-dot prints, and mismatched moments with husband Justin, there was a lot to pick from.

For the New York Times Dealbook Summit, Bieber takes a page from Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy and wears a Yohji Yamamoto Spring 1998 jacket previously worn by the late It girl.

Stepping out in New York City, Bieber pairs her Phoebe Philo cropped fur coat with blue jeans and vintage Manolos.

The star wears the coat of the season: Conner Ives’s reconstituted fur robe in white silk mikado.

Bieber wears an iridescent jacket from Matthieu Blazy’s Chanel Fall 2026 collection. With a medley of silver, red, and turquoise mesh, the piece puts an aluminum chainmail twist on the house’s signature tweed.

For the Academy Museum Gala, Bieber wears a custom bubblegum-pink Chanel dress.

At the 2026 Vanity Fair Oscar after-party, she hits the carpet wearing a shimmering leopard-print gown, made custom by Armani Privé. She accessorizes with Lorraine Schwartz jewelry and Manolo Blahnik heels.

While in Tokyo, Bieber wears a textured Issey Miyake egg-carton skirt suit from the Fall/Winter 2000 collection.

Doing press in Australia, Bieber wears a green Valentino Spring 2026 minidress, with structured shoulders, ruching, and a sweet bow at the hem.

Bieber attends the GQ Men Of The Year party in a naked dress and sparkly Gucci thong, bringing back the ’90s trend.

At the 2025 Academy Museum Gala, Bieber steps out in a striking, sculptural gown from Schiaparelli, constructed from brown moiré vintage ribbons for the house’s Spring/Summer 2025 Haute Couture runway.

Supporting Kylie Jenner at the Kylie Cosmetics anniversary event, Bieber looks to Wiederhoeft for a belted satin midi dress, designed with a chunky belt and thigh-high slit.

While out for an evening in New York, Bieber turns heads in an electric green minidress from Dolce & Gabbana’s 2003 collection.

Stepping out in New York, Bieber embraces two of the year's biggest trends—polka dots and capris—all in one go.

Bieber wears a Marine Serre dress made out of metallic-silver watch straps for a weekend in Miami during the Grand Prix.

Bieber spends an NYC evening at Cafe Zaffri, wearing a silky ice-blue minidress—a vintage Gucci piece from the fashion house’s Spring/Summer 1998 collection, designed by Tom Ford.

Leaving Funk in Los Angeles with Justin, Hailey is seen in an oversized gray suit with strong shoulders and baggy trousers, paired with a burgundy tie and matching clutch.

Stepping out with Justin, who matches his wife's Adidas with a pop of red, Hailey layers on the leopard print patterns—pairing a matching trench and shoulder bag from Saint Laurent.

Bieber highlights her bump with a silky white asymmetrical minidress and matching square-toe heels.

For her pregnancy announcement, Bieber poses in a bridal long-sleeve Saint Laurent maxi dress and matching lacy veil, with chunky sunglasses for a full old Hollywood glam look.

Captured in what have since become iconic photos, Hailey and Justin take couples dressing to new contrasting heights, with her in a little red dress and him in a gray hoodie and Crocs.
