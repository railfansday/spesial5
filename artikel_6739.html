Nathan Fielder’s Elizabeth Holmes Documentary, <i><em>You Can See Everything</em></i>: What We Know So Far

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Elizabeth Holmes’s story is getting told in a new project with Nathan Fielder at the helm. The comedian has released his new documentary titled You Can See Everything about Holmes, founder of the now-defunct biotech company Theranos, before she began serving her 11-year prison sentence for committing fraud.

Unlike a typical documentary, the film features Fielder chatting with Holmes in a Q&A format, while an unnamed A-list actress reenacts the events of the documentary in asides as Holmes. Here’s everything we know about You Can See Everything.

The official logline for the documentary reads: “34 days before she’s sent to prison, notorious Silicon Valley founder Elizabeth Holmes invites a skeptical film crew to document her every move. What begins as an intimate portrait of an enigmatic felon becomes a mind-bending three-year journey into the abyss. From boundary-pushing directors Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim comes You Can See Everything.”

Audiences were buzzing at Telluride Film Festival, where the doc premiered with a surprise screening on September 6. Variety called the doc “a shameless, fascinating, reality-TV-eats-its-tail look at entitled duplicity” and said “the sensation of ‘WTF? I’ve got to watch this!’ did not subside as the movie went on. It only intensified.”

Deadline reported that “the nearly three-hour-long film drew continuous laughter, frequently triggered by hilarious reaction shots of Fielder...attempting to process what Holmes and Evans [Holmes’s partner] are pushing.” And the L.A. Times said the movie contains “mind-bending twists that meld reality and performance.”

The documentary is currently available to watch in theaters, but it will likely hit HBO Max when it comes to streaming. Warner Bros. Discovery owns HBO and HBO Max, and the company has an agreement that states A24 titles will automatically go to HBO Max as their first stop on streaming after the theatrical window and PVOD (Premium Video on Demand) release windows are over. You Can See Everything currently has no streaming release date.

In addition to Fielder and Holmes, The Hollywood Reporter confirmed that an unnamed “A-list actress” has a role in the making of the documentary. Her identity has not been revealed, as “the filmmakers and fest [Telluride Film Festival] requested that her identity be kept secret until more people have a chance to see the movie.”

As for what viewers can expect from the A-list actress, THR reports her cameo is “the craziest twist of a crazy film,” but her involvement is accidental. After agreeing to meet with Fielder, she finds herself wanting to be involved in the filming process and “winds up becoming a full-fledged collaborator in the film, appearing throughout the rest of it alternatingly as herself, giving commentary on the insanity of the situation, and then in reenactments—long Fielder’s go-to way of making sense of things—as Holmes.”

The first official trailer for the documentary arrived on September 8, featuring Fielder and Holmes having an unsettling conversation.

“I don’t have anything to deceive you on. Of course I’m not deceiving you. I’m engaging with you as a human being. Why would I deceive you? There’s no...there’s no reason for me to do that,” Holmes tells Fielder.

“You’re being real right now?” he asks her, to which Holmes replies, “I’m always being real.”

A first-look image was also released with the trailer, which shows Holmes preparing to give an item to a child, while Fielder looks at her from the background.

The first official trailer arrived on September 23 and provides a close look into some of the conversations that Fielder and Holmes had with each other throughout the film.

“You really feel you did nothing wrong?” Fielder asks her, to which Holmes replies, “I did not.”

He presses her and follows up the question with, “So where did things get lost? How are you where you are right now?”

Holmes finds herself at a loss: “I wish I knew. I don’t know. I don’t feel like I understand that,” she tells him.

At another point in the trailer, Fielder questions how Holmes could be so calm about his filming her every move. When he asks Holmes if she has any “worry” about him being at her home 24/7, she answers, “I don’t think so. I think this is the moment in time to capture this story before I’m gone.”

The comedian also manages to interview Holmes’s partner, William “Billy” Evans, who reveals he fell in love with her in their first conversation. “Weren’t you worried she was scamming you?” Fielder asks.

Evans coolly responds, “100 percent, but what does she has to gain from me?”

When Fielder reminds him that his family is wealthy—Evans is the heir to the Evans Hotel Group—he responds, “I think there’s a lot of different levels of wealth.”

Neither Holmes nor Evans have seen the doc, Oppenheim revealed during an October Q&A after the film’s Los Angeles screening. “Neither of them have seen the film. Elizabeth is still incarcerated; Billy hasn’t seen it,” the director said, with The Hollywood Reporter adding that Evans has “a strained relationship with the crew.”

Fielder added that Evans “started trying to exert a level of control over the process that made it very hard to engage with him, and it was uncomfortable for us and also A24 and everyone. And I think we sort of had to stop—I think communication broke down basically, and we’ve been in that like state for a little bit. So it’s been challenging in that way.”

On November 2, Evans revealed the “one misconception” about his relationship with Holmes following the release of the movie. “That I somehow control Liz. Anyone who actually knows her would find that idea hilarious,” he responded to an X user.

Evans added, “She’s fiercely independent, strong willed, and my best friend. We are partners in every sense. We challenge each other, disagree, and face the world together. Her closest friends are also some of my closest friends. If I ever mistreated her, they’d have my head.”
