GTA 6: The Big Review Round-Up

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Sam Woods is TheGamer’s Senior Reporter. He’s been in games journalism since 2020 and has undertaken a variety of roles in that time. These have included roles such as SEO Editor, Managing Editor at DualShockers, and Global Head of Gaming, and he's also been seen on the BBC. He was promoted to the Senior Reporter position in 2025 following a series of high-quality reports and exclusives for TheGamer.

Since 2020, Sam has written over 3,000 articles and specializes in telling the stories of the people behind the games. He’s interviewed the biggest game directors, some of the industry’s top talents, told the stories of how indie games came to be, and spoken to players about what really makes them tick. He’s also interviewed some of the world’s biggest musical artists to find out how the medium has inspired their work, and TV and Film actors about their roles in video game adaptations. He even hosted a PAX panel in 2021 and made a documentary in 2024 surrounding the creation of a Disney game.

Alongside this, Sam focuses on breaking news. He got the scoop on Perfect Dark’s cancellation, provided context on some AI drama, and revealed that Lady Dimitrescu’s actress would love to step back into the character’s giant shoes. These articles have been picked up by the biggest gaming sites, including IGN, GameSpot, and PC Gamer. He also loves to dive into the numbers behind the games.

Sam’s journey in games journalism has taken him all over the world. He’s covered Gamescom in Germany, Summer Game Fest in Los Angeles, and Tokyo Game Show in Japan, each on three occasions.

He’s been learning Japanese since 2024 with the intention of identifying and translating Japanese-language news and bringing it to English-language audiences first, something he’s done countless times.

Sam’s first review was 2020’s Battletoads reboot, and he’s since reviewed games in The Legend of Zelda, Super Mario, and Pokemon franchises.

With less than 24 hours until Grand Theft Auto 6 launches, the game's review embargo has lifted, and it's everything we expected it would be. The game opened to a staggering 99 on both Metacritic and OpenCritic, making it officially the best-reviewed game of all time. If there were any doubts that Rockstar might fumble the bag, they have now been put to bed.

Its glowing reviews are best captured in quotes from critics around the world. We've trawled through these reviews and gathered them into a full round-up featuring the thoughts from some of the industry's most notable reviewers. Hopefully this will amp up your excitement another notch ahead of tonight's release.

We'll start off with our very own George Foster, who delivered an astounding 5/5 review. He called it the most "high-pressure thing" he's ever taken on, but it seems like it was worth it. The game constantly left him "speechless," and he says it sets "an impossible new bar for the medium," but his thoughts are perhaps best summarized in the comment below:

"Without a shadow of a doubt, Grand Theft Auto 6 is the best open-world game I’ve ever played, and one of the best games, full stop, with only incredibly minute nitpicks barely worth mentioning that do nothing to drag it down from masterpiece status."

Unsurprisingly, GameSpot's Tamoor Hussain also scored the game a 10/10. He said, "This game is the culmination of everything the developer has previously achieved in the open-world space that pushes the genre to heights that, in all likelihood, no other developer will be able to reach. GTA 6 is an astonishing open-world action game that I’d wager won’t be outdone until Grand Theft Auto 7."

Josh West had the honor of reviewing GTA 6 for GamesRadar+. They were seemingly just as blown away by the game as everybody else, saying, "With impossible production values, details that matter, and an interconnected set of world systems that bring this world to life, GTA VI is unlike anything we’ve ever seen. Jason and Lucia are the beating heart of this game, and the story they tell is worth living. This isn’t just a good game, or a great game, it’s the kind that comes just once a generation."

The 10s keep rolling in, with Luke Reilly giving it another perfect score for IGN. They said, "I was simply not ready for just how much richer, deeper, and more satisfying Grand Theft Auto 6 has proven to be. After three weeks and over 120 hours playing it, I’m still hopelessly hooked, absorbing the world at walking speed like a tourist in a new town. I’m dazed by just how consistently I find myself noticing a new animation, unique pedestrian, or standout vehicle. I am completely enamoured with its series-best car handling and weaving through the realistically dense traffic. Put simply, Grand Theft Auto 6 is the single most impressive video game I have ever played."

VGC's Jordan Middler said the game "does so much at such a high bar" in their 5/5 review. Like many of his fellow reviewers, Middler felt that it's a game that will likely never be matched. "This is Rockstar operating at the absolute peak of its power. The scale of Grand Theft Auto 6’s achievement won’t be felt this week, or next week, or even next year. It’ll be felt in the next decade of games that come after it, as developers, like so many before them, attempt to emulate the Rockstar magic. But nobody else can. No one has the budget, this number of developers, and this level of cultural impact."

One of the few 9/10 scores came from The New York Times and Yussef Cole. While not quite a 10, they were still enamored with the game, saying, "The surprising depth to which themes are explored makes for a memorable and endearing experience in Grand Theft Auto VI, which largely lives up to its sky-high expectations by inverting the series’s reputation for destruction."
