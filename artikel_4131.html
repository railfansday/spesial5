"You Don't Want To Interrupt The Player's Experience" - Here's Why Zelda's Switch 2 Remake Cut Some Mario References

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Nintendo is known in recent times for dropping Star Fox (and even Pikmin) into the Super Mario universe, but with the Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake on the Switch 2, it decided to take a little more care.

In the castle courtyard, where Link meets with Zelda, you might have noticed how the character portraits for Mario and friends are not in the Switch 2 version of the game. If you are wondering about the thought process behind this, Zelda series producer Eiji Aonuma has explained how the team didn't want to break the immersion.

Here's what he had to say during the same chat with Game Informer:

Eiji Aonuma: "Certainly, at the time, when we put those [portraits] there, I don't think anybody thought it was too big a deal. Maybe there was a more carefree or comfortable approach to that sort of thing. But looking at it now, like if a player is coming through that area, and given the overall way that we're, you know, creating this world and giving the player that form of expression, if they look up and they think, "Why is Yoshi there?” We don't want that moment to interrupt their experience of the world in that way. So we're feeling a little bit differently about it this time."

If you recall, the 3DS game also had Skyward Sword art hidden in certain locations, and this is also gone from the Switch 2 remake. According to Aonuma, the same reasoning applies here - the team didn't want to "interrupt the player's experience by seeing something that's unrelated to what they're currently doing in the game".

Although these easter eggs are gone, certain references to the Mario series remain intact as they are part of the character designs:

"You will remember that there is one design element on Talon with Bowser on it, and that's part of the original design of the character. So we didn't change that. That stays as is."
