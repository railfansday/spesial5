UK Charts: Zelda: Ocarina Of Time Is The Second Biggest UK Launch In Franchise History

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The latest charts data from the UK is in, and as expected, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time has debuted at the top spot, beating out the likes of Modern Warfare 4 and FC 27.

While not surprising, it's nevertheless a satisfying result, and we'd be keen to see what the actual split is between physical copies and digital. We suspect that, with the pre-order bonuses and midnight launches, physical has likely vastly outsold digital in this case.

In fact, Chris Dring from The Game Business has stated that Ocarina of Time is the second biggest UK physical launch in franchise history, beating Breath of the Wild by around 35% but falling short of Tears of the Kingdom by about half. However, he states that there are "plenty of install base-related reasons for both of those things". He also states that this is easily the biggest physical launch of 2026 so far.

Elsewhere, Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered has made its debut at number 9, with Switch 2 putting up a solid fight against the PS5. Stellar Blade Complete Edition has also done rather well to land at number 12; a solid indication that there is decent demand for proper physical games on Switch 2.
