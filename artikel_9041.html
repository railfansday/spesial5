Florian Residence 1 / Benthem Crouwel Architects

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Text description provided by the architects. Florian Residence 1 is the first completed building and marks the beginning of a new urban district. Located on a prominent site near the National Bank of Slovakia, the building comprises eight floors, shops and amenities, and 73 homes ranging from compact studios to spacious apartments. High technological standards – triple glazing, high-performance thermal insulation, and acoustic engineering – ensure residents' optimal comfort, while the generous entrance and light-filled atrium lend the building an authentic sense of place and community.
