Understated, Earthy, and Endlessly Wearable: The Best Olive Green Polishes

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The earthy shade adds an edge to fall manicures while still wearing like a neutral.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Like all nail polish colors, green has a vast spectrum, one that runs from electric neon to bold kelly to soft sage and all the way down to deep, moody olive. Of all the greens out there, olive sits in a quieter place. The hue is earthy and a little mysterious, somewhere between a vintage army jacket and an actual juicy olive. It’s a shade we love for fall, since it pairs so seamlessly with black, rich browns, burgundy, red, and, of course, cream.

Once reserved for the brave few, olive has now gone mainstream. Part of the appeal is how wearable it is, offering an unexpected twist without ever feeling too loud. It’s also soft and soothing to the eye, making it an easy shade to wear day after day.

If you’re new to the shade, the easiest way in is to treat it like a neutral. Try it with a camel coat and chunky knits when the weather turns, or a crisp white shirt for something fresh and unexpected. A creamy, opaque finish feels polished and modern, while a glossy jelly or a hint of shimmer adds depth in low light. However you wear it, olive looks effortlessly considered and adds an edge to your style.

Below, we’ve rounded up the best olive green nail polishes to shop now.
