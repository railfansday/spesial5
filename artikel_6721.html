15 Best Zip-Up Hoodies for Men 2026, According to GQ Editors

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Surveying the best zip-up hoodies for men takes me back to my younger days as a hopelessly romantic high school kid. My formative years were marred by angst, soundtracked by pop punk, emo, and indie rock. The piece of clothing that got me through it all? A zip-up hoodie. It was core to the indie aesthetic, with every forlorn frontman sporting one over a thrashed band tee, skinny jeans, and shredded Chucks. But nostalgia never seems to stay in the past, especially when fashion is involved.

Gen-Z has revived every minute piece of 2000s pop culture, which means zip-up hoodies are back and have all but usurped the pullover hoodie as the sweat du jour. The zip-up is inherently versatile in both practical wear and sheer styling dexterity. With the flick of the zip, you can put your beloved Death Cab for Cutie tee on full display while letting your pits breathe, or keep it zipped up when it's just a bit too nippy out. To find the best zip-up hoodies on the market, I dusted off Transatlanticism and went on a deep dive. The good news is that the old classics are still good. The better news is that there's a lot of great competition to choose from.

Uniqlo’s no-nonsense clean aesthetic and low price point has made it a go-to for any menswear enjoyer looking for good style on a budget. While the brand excels at cashmere sweaters, T-shirts, and a slew of homerun collabs, the Japanese label’s sweats are criminally underrated. We’d go so far as to say that they should be talked in the same breath as brands like Champion and Sunspel. This zip up hoodie took us by surprise thanks to its comfy midweight, all-cotton fabric and solid construction. But the main selling point is the impeccable fit.

So many other brands cut their hoodies too long in the body and leave you looking like Jack Skellington’s pubescent brother. Uniqlo managed to cut its French terry hoodie to sit right at the hips which makes for a more classic and natural silhouette that’s neither lanky nor sloppy. Because of the fabric, the feel, and the overall fit, this is my favorite zip-up hoodie by a sizable margin. It’s my most-worn hoodie and has yet to show signs of any fraying.

Hoodies were originally meant for sportswear, as warm up gear before a grueling game of football. These days, they’re more often worn for lounging on the couch to watch the kickoff. And if you want to kick it in comfort while saving your pennies for booze and amuse bouche for the big game with the boys, the best budget zip up hoodie to buy is Hanes’ EcoSmart zip hoodie. The fit is spot on—relaxed, but not sloppy, comfy in the body and the sleeves without swallowing you like a sand pit.

The fit is impressive for the price, and the overall quality means you’re getting a little more than what you pay for. It’s not a hoodie that’ll last you for too long, but it is a good quality piece for just 15 bucks. And when other hoodies offer lesser quality and worse-fitting options for about the same price? The answer is clear.

It’s hard not to sing the praises of Reigning Champ, a brand that built its fleece empire on sweatpants and hoodies. The Canadian label’s flagship midweight terry fabric has drawn the attention of streetwear royalty and menswear buffs alike and its dedication to quality has elevated the humble hoodie to oxygen-deprived heights. We named Reigning Champ’s crewneck sweatshirt and pullover hoodie as among our favorites in their respective categories, and the same goes for its zip-up hooded sweatshirt. It’s made of the same 100% cotton French terry fabric as its superlative-earning brethren and is cut with high-quality flatlock stitching for a smooth, un-bulky feel.

I had high expectations for Reigning Champ, given my previous experience, and I’m not surprised at all with how nice this zip-up is. And while I'm partial to the standard fit, it’s also available in a slim fit version, too.

Hoodies live in a universe well beyond the sportswear world from which they originated. And while we still enjoy the OGs and the classics that made the hoodie a bona fide menswear must-have, we also love seeing all the ways the style has been pushed, tweaked, and freaked. Our Legacy's Level Hood has been a successful avenue for the Stockholm-based brand to explore the limits of the hoodie and this particular version strikes a delicate balance between the freaky and the fashion-curious. It's made of an English wool knit into an all-over ribbed texture for a throwback '90s feel. The cropped, boxy silhouette and elongated sleeves harken back to the grunge-y era, while the sultry dual-tone nightshade colorway throws you off in the best way possible.

Menswear influencers will be happy to regale you with their TedTalk on the golden ratio of clothing proportions. Essentially, that means your outfit should be divided into three equal sections where the top takes up one third of the outfit. Basically, your tops should be cropped and sit closer to your natural waist. Follow it up with some high-waisted pants with a wide silhouette and you’ve instantly garnered 300K views on TikTok (RIP). With so many indie brands and influencers scrambling to make their own versions of “the perfect cropped hoodie” you’re probably tired of wading through all the choices. Do yourself a favor and go to Abercrombie.

The Moose’s version is backed by actual trend forecasters, actual designers, and actual fit models. What does that mean, exactly? It means that its cropped zip hoodie fits really well and uses surprisingly beefy fabric. It was a revelation for me, too—Abercrombie wasn’t a name I associated with hoodies, but after I saw this in person, I had to readjust my menswear word cloud.

Some of the best zip-up hoodies for men are made by a brand you never see in brick and mortar stores. Camber has been crafting the most durable sweats for decades, right here in America and have been the shining example of the difference between good quality and great quality. Sure, you have to order through an old-school web 1.0 website and wait for several weeks to actually get your hoodie. But trust us when we say that the relatively minor inconvenience is completely worth it.

The heavyweight fleece is almost as thick as a leather jacket and can stand in as outerwear if you wanted it to. While other hoodies are washed to hell in giant industrial washing machines to reach a super soft texture by the time it gets to you, Camber’s actually require you to do the work yourself. It sort of feels like you’re being swaddled by an anaconda, which means if you’re a proponent of hard clothes like raw denim, you’ll like Camber’s hoodie a lot.

Cashmere zip hoodies, once the exclusive province of Silicon Valley unicorn founders and PJ-flying nepo babies, have been democratized, none better than Naadam's take. You know the brand for bringing thoughtful, sustainable cashmere pieces at “really, only that much?” prices. Its zip hoodie is as soft as it gets, with extra credit points awarded for coming in a classic fit (too many cashmere hoodies err on the side of slim) and six colors, including this decidedly handsome olive. Wear it on weekends, work-from-home Fridays, and long flights—even if it's just Economy+.

Shoutout to brother in arms Louis Cheslaw, GQ Recs contributor, whose writing for the newsletter Magasin put us on to young brand Cease. From seed to sewing, it's beefy 20-ounce cotton is fully American-made. Assembling the fabric vertically means the hoodie will shorten up to a slightly cropped, oversized fit. Act fast if you want some of the less-boring colors—they move fast.

We can’t deny Russell Athletic’s contributions to the hoodie space. And while its formula has changed over the years, the new hoodies are still pretty good for the price. The fabric is soft and lightweight, and it’s available in a rainbow of colorways. Plus, if you’re looking for a hoodie with a little more length, this is a great option.

Bold graphics aren’t usually deemed classics, but Gap’s retro-leaning collegiate-style block logo has staying power almost as much as the Levi’s red tab. Add to that a wonderfully fluffy midweight fleece material and a tailored straight fit and you’ve got something you’ll see on moodboards for generations to come.

Champion’s Reverse Weave fabric is iconic for its nearly shrink-proof quality and almost unsettlingly rough exterior. Flip it to the inside and you’ve got a luxuriously brushed interior that’ll have you feeling like you’re melting into a cloud. Heads-up that pricing varies kinda wildly by size on Amazon.

The secondhand market is chock full of navy-blue hoodies that have been sun-faded and beaten down with time. They'll cost you just as much as this Mfpen version, but will also fall apart faster than you can zip 'em up.

Les Tien's dedication to all things sweats results in a catalog of luxurious fleece and terry cloth with a modern approach. Featuring high-end Cobrax hardware, RiRi zippers, raw-edge hems and sleeves, and a heavyweight brushed fleece, no detail is phoned in.

The Real McCoy's is considered by many to be Americana repro wizards. This revival of an early 20th-Century hoodie is just one small piece of the brand's preservation prowess. It's made using beefy sinker-knit fleece, old-school Talon-style zippers, and vertical patch pockets.

A.Presse's chokehold on the menswear illuminati refuses to let up and it's pieces like this vintage-inspired hoodie that keep the pressure on. Based on very rare “after-hood parkas", this made-in-Japan hoodie features an idiosyncratic attached hood, super dense and lofty fleece, with artfully distressed details throughout.

Think of the zip-up as the pullover hoodie's elder brother, but with way more layering potential. In its most natural state, it can cuddle up to a matching set of sweatpants and a baggy tee with flair. (Top it off with a swanky necklace, crisp sneakers, and some stellar shades and you’re left with a fit on par with Shai Gilgeous-Alexander at his tunnel-trotting best.)

Unzipped and left open, it can also take on the role of a picture frame ready to display your sickest graphic tee—much like the cardigan, another treasure of 2000s fashion. Or make like a mid-Aughts garage band ironically donning corporate attire and wear your zip-up over a dress shirt and baggy tailored trousers, a high-minded fashion move with a jolt of kooky Scandinavian cool.

GQ Recommends heavily emphasizes our own editorial experience with those brands, how they make their clothes, and how those clothes have been reviewed by customers. Bottom line: GQ wouldn’t tell you to wear it if we wouldn’t.

We lean on that collective experience to guide our search, culling a mix of household names, indie favorites, and the artisanal imprints on the bleeding-edge of the genre. Then we narrow down the assortment to the picks that scored the highest across quality, fit, and price.
