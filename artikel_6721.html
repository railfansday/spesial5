Bring a little opulence into his everyday life

If gift-giving for the guy in your life has you stumped, our guide to the best luxury gifts for men is the answer. We’ve curated a variety of presents to satisfy each and every personality, whether you are shopping for a father figure, sibling, friend, or partner. These luxe gifts, which cover every price point, make excellent ideas as we inch closer to the holiday season. Consider this well-rounded edit for thoughtful ways to celebrate the many men in your life, with something personal for everyone, all selected through our discerning eye.

For your favorite jet-setter, there are sumptuous leather goods, both big and small, to keep him stylish and organized while on the go. Gucci’s luxe weekender bag is a one-of-a-kind investment and Brunello Cucinelli’s Dopp kit will withstand plenty of wear and tear as he continues to fill up his passport with stamps from all those far-off destinations—especially with lots of holiday and winter travel on the horizon. Alternatively, treat your resident fitness fanatic to some new gym gear and post-workout recovery with HigherDose’s infrared sauna blanket or Beast’s personal blender. The tech enthusiast in your life certainly deserves the latest Apple iPad Mini or Meta’s AI-infused Ray-Ban sunglasses—effortlessly cool within the iconic Wayfarer frames.

Make sure to shop our favorite picks now with these 72 best luxury gifts for men—long before the final shipping windows close this holiday season.

High-tech noise-cancellation headphones will surely impress. Immersive listening? Check. Elevated design? Of course. Mega comfort? No question.
