There's a seriously good price on a HP gaming laptop right now with an RTX 5070 Ti and a top spec AMD Ryzen CPU. HP has cut this HyperX Omen Max 16 gaming laptop down to $2,099.99 right now, or slightly more if you fancy the OLED, giving you a $600 saving in the process.

The big selling point here is the current-gen Nvidia GPU, giving you the performance you'll need for gaming. The mobile version of the Nvidia GeForce RTX 5070 Ti isn't quite a match for the desktop version, and ships with a set of 5,888 CUDA cores and 12GB GDDR7 VRAM. This is still a 115W GPU with plenty of power for high-resolution gaming, however, especially compared to the CPU's integrated GPU you'd be stuck with otherwise.

There won't be much of an issue here hitting high frame rates on this HP laptop's 1600p display. DLSS support, meanwhile, means you've got breathing room to boost to the best settings, with multi-frame generation able to cut some of the slack with AI-generated frames to give you an uplift in the most power-intensive games.

This is an extremely competitive option for gaming with this discount, offsetting some of the criticisms we made in our HyperX Omen Max 16 review, and the build quality will remain the same, whatever spec you choose. If you change other components, like the RAM, GPU, and display, then performance will vary.

This HyperX Omen Max 16 gaming laptop features a powerful (and customizable) spec sheet that includes the AMD Ryzen AI 7 450, Nvidia GeForce RTX 5070 Ti GPU with 12GB of GDDR7 VRAM, 16GB of DDR5 RAM, and a 512GB SSD.

OLED and IPS LCD versions are available for this 16-inch laptop, offering a 1600p max resolution and refresh rate up to 240Hz, depending on your preferred option.

That's because this laptop is heavily customizable, so you can switch up most of its specs, although that will impact the price.

The rest of the specs include an AMD Ryzen AI 7 450. This Zen 5 CPU has eight cores, with a boost clock up to 5.2 GHz. This is a solid enough mid-tier mobile CPU and won't be a huge bottleneck for your ambitions at 1600p. If you need more juice, though, you can choose the 12-core AI 9 HX 475 instead for an extra $140.

The build for $2,099.99 includes 16GB of DDR5 RAM, but you can upgrade to 32GB or 48GB if you want to pay a higher price tag. Likewise, the base model here comes with a 16-inch IPS display with a refresh rate up to 240Hz. If you have an extra $110, though, you can upgrade to an OLED with a refresh rate of 165Hz, or $190 will get you an OLED with a 240Hz refresh rate.

A 512GB SSD is a compromise for the price, offering Gen 4 speeds. You can upgrade to 1TB or 2TB models for higher prices, though. Other areas of the laptop's build quality are worth mentioning, however, with a full-size keyboard that has per-key RGB lighting, as well as liquid metal cooling and a vapor chamber that helps to improve heat dissipation. You've also got several ports to choose from, including two USB-A and USB-C ports each, as well as HDMI 2.1, DisplayPort 2.1, and Ethernet, as well as Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 5.4 connectivity.
