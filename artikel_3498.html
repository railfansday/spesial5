New 4-Part Crime Thriller Officially Arrives on Netflix

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The best crime dramas can keep audiences guessing about who committed a murder. But the truly elite? They're better off when they tell us what happens when it seems like the investigation is over. While shows like Mindhunter and True Detective have built enormous followings by exploring the darker corners of criminal investigations, one of the most critically acclaimed dramas of the 2010s took a much quieter approach. And despite earning the sort of reviews most television creators could only dream of, it somehow managed to remain relatively overlooked. Until now, perhaps.

All four seasons of Rectify have officially arrived on Netflix in the United States, joining the streaming service on November 1. The acclaimed drama originally aired on SundanceTV between 2013 and 2016, producing 30 episodes and earning widespread critical acclaim throughout its run, even winning one of the most prestigious awards going.

When Daniel Holden is released after nearly 20 years of complete isolation on Death Row, he returns as an outsider to his family, to his community, and to the times. Somehow, he survived the mental and emotional strain of his imprisonment, but now the walls have suddenly come crumbling down, and Daniel is set free in a world he no longer understands. He’s an outsider, lost in a once familiar world, struggling to navigate his newfound freedom. Combined with two decades of technological and social changes to his small town and the larger world, everything he encounters is a puzzle.

The cast stars Aden Young (The King Tide) as Daniel Holden, Abigail Spencer (Timeless) as Amantha Holden, Adelaide Clemens (Silent Hill: Revelation) as Tawney Talbot, Clayne Crawford (Lethal Weapon) as Ted Talbot Jr., J. Smith-Cameron (Succession) as Janet Talbot, Bruce McKinnon (Lawless) as Ted Talbot Sr., Luke Kirby (The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel) as Jon Stern, and Sean Bridgers (Get Shorty) as Trey Willis. The show is created by Ray McKinnon (Deadwood).

Rectify was met with universal acclaim from critics and audiences, receiving a score of 96% on Rotten Tomatoes, with the audience score not far behind at 92%. Its third and fourth seasons both earned perfect 100% critics' scores, while the final season achieved an extraordinary 99 out of 100 on Metacritic; it also received a coveted Peabody Award, which shows just how highly regarded it was.

All 30 episodes of Rectify, spanning four seasons, are streaming now on Netflix in the United States.
