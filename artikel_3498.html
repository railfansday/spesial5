3 Elite Netflix Series to Binge This Weekend (Nov 21-22)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Who can take The Body's crown? One of the best new shows on streaming this November, Kristina Bogic and Sara Boustany star in a brilliantly twisted horror-thriller, reminiscent of Cruel Intentions and The Craft. "The Body is like nothing else on streaming right now," wrote Gabrielle Moss in Collider's review, a feeling shared by much of the streaming population, as the series tops the Netflix charts in the U.S. at the time of writing. But that shouldn't mean you don't have other Netflix options on your radar. With that in mind, here's a look at three shows you should binge-watch on Netflix this weekend.

For more recommendations, check out our list of the best shows and movies on Netflix.

Disclaimer: These titles are available on US Netflix.

Of all the new content added to Netflix in the past seven days, one that might've gone under your radar is the former Starz series Run the World, which ran between 2021 and 2023. The series follows Whitney (Amber Stevens West), Ella (Andrea Bordeaux), Renee (Bresha Webb), and Sondi (Corbin Reid) as they forge strong friendships, tackle messy romance, and fuel their hunger for Harlem dominance.

Created by Leigh Davenport, this vibrant, fiery series is packed with great drama and plenty of laugh-out-loud comedy. All 16 episodes were made available on Netflix earlier this week, three years on from the show's cruel cancellation, despite earning plenty of acclaim. This acclaim includes a coveted, perfect 100% score on review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes and an NAACP Image Awards nomination for Outstanding Comedy Series.

Four Netflix characters, four completely different ways of showing up for somebody. This is the one who fits how you actually do friendship, and what having them in your life would be like.

You do not do friendship at half volume, and neither does he. What you want is somebody who says yes to the plan before you have finished describing the plan.

You are the friend who makes things happen, and so is she. Together you would be mildly exhausting to everyone around you and completely delighted with yourselves.

You have no patience for pleasant lies, and she has no ability to tell them. What looks like coldness from the outside is the most reliable friendship on this list.

You look after whoever is having the worst day. So does he. It is the least strategic way to move through the world and the best possible quality in a friend.

Sometimes a story is so hard to believe it simply has to be true. One of the most exciting new series on Netflix this month is exactly that, as Graham Moore, the Oscar-winning writer of The Imitation Game, adapts the story of a strange, whirlwind romance between FTX founder Sam Bankman-Fried (Anthony Boyle) and former Alameda Research CEO Caroline Ellison (Julia Garner), during the rise and fall of their cryptocurrency trading firm.

This Bonnie and Clyde crypto tale will keep you hooked throughout all of its eight episodes. The cast is stacked beyond Boyle and Garner, including Alex Lawther, Karan Soni, Naomi Okada, Madison Hu, Matt Rife, and Eugene Young. Described as a "fascinating, personal, deeply heartbreaking" story by Moore himself, this is a new miniseries you simply can't afford to miss.

As the end of 2026 edges closer, it's time to catch up on one of this year's best shows on Netflix. An adaptation of John Steinbeck's celebrated 1952 family saga, East of Eden follows two families (the Trasks and the Hamiltons) as they live within an echo of the story of Cain and Abel, only this time set in California's Salinas Valley. Florence Pugh stars as Cathy Ames.

Christopher Abbott, Mike Faist, Hoon Lee, and Ciarán Hinds also feature in this star-studded adaptation of a celebrated novel, although Pugh, as ever, steals the spotlight. By debuting with 6.5 million views in week #1, followed by more than 12 million views in its second week, the show has continued to prove to be one of this year's most enduring hits, as the world indulges in Zoe Kazan's whirlwind adaptation.
