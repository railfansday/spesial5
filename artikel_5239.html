Indonesian police found server racks but no Nvidia processors when they raided the Jakarta office of Megaspeed’s local subsidiary earlier this year, The Straits Times and Reuters reported. The search was carried out at the request of the FBI. Import records reportedly show the company brought in Nvidia H100 chips between 2024 and 2026, yet none were found on site. An Indonesian official said investigators suspect most of the hardware was sent on to China, possibly using private jets, though this has not been confirmed. Megaspeed said its business is legitimate, confirmed that founder Alice Huang left the company in August 2025, and said it has started an internal compliance review. The firm also faces investigations in the US and Singapore, but no breach of US export rules has been proven. The raid adds pressure on Nvidia, whose former CEO Jensen Huang resigned last week after admitting an affair with Alice Huang.
