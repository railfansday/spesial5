The Numbers Game: Arsenal look to strike title blow against Man City

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Arsenal versus Manchester City has emerged as the standout Premier League fixture in recent years.

And even before the end of November, Sunday's showdown at the Emirates Stadium could have huge ramifications for the rest of the season.

After City started their first campaign under Enzo Maresca with seven straight victories, a four-game winless run has allowed Arsenal to pull six points clear at the summit.

Will they put further distance between themselves and last season's runners-up on Sunday, or will City turn things around?

Here, we delve into the best Opta insights to provide a data-powered preview of this weekend's standout fixture.

An Arsenal victory would give them a huge advantage even at this very early stage in the title race, and the supercomputer fancies them to get the job done.

Across 10,000 data-led simulations conducted by the model, Arsenal were victorious in 54.4% of scenarios.

City, meanwhile, are assigned a 22.2% chance of taking all three points and a 23.4% probability of leaving north London with a draw.

City traditionally dominated this fixture, but Mikel Arteta has managed to tip the scales in Arsenal's favour in recent years.

Having lost six consecutive home league games against City between 2018 and 2023, Arsenal are now unbeaten in their last three against them at Emirates Stadium (W2 D1).

Overall, City have only won one of the last six Premier League games between the sides (D3 L2), though that victory did come when they last met in the competition this April (2-1 at Etihad Stadium).

A better omen for City is the fact they have won 12 of their last 14 top-flight games against the reigning champions (D1 L1), completing the double over Liverpool last season.

They have lost three of their four games when facing Arsenal as champions in the competition, though, drawing the other 1-1 in January 2005.

Things to work on for Arsenal after drab draws

Arsenal's lead over City in the table could have been even more commanding, if not for a 2-2 draw with Newcastle United at St. James' Park last weekend.

Arteta's side have been tremendous on their own turf this season, though – they are the only Premier League side yet to drop a single point at home in 2026-27.

The Gunners are on an eight-match winning run in home Premier League games, their longest since a 10-match streak between April and December in 2022, though they were held to a 1-1 draw by Borussia Dortmund at the Emirates in the Champions League on Tuesday.

Arsenal have, of course, already beaten City once this season, running out convincing 3-0 victors in the Community Shield in August.

But the Gunners have not beaten City twice in the same campaign since 2014-15 – with one of those wins coming by a 3-0 scoreline in the Community Shield – and aspects of last week's performance on Tyneside will certainly have displeased Arteta.

In the first half of that game, Arsenal only gave up three shots, none of which were on target, but in the second period, Newcastle had nine attempts and hit the target with three of them.

Though Noni Madueke's goal salvaged a point for the champions after Newcastle fought back to go 2-1 up, that was Arsenal's only shot on target after half-time, from two total attempts.

Arsenal's issue against Dortmund was not chance creation, though; it was their finishing, having put up 30 shots and 2.8 expected goals to BVB's 13 and 1.12 xG.

Captain Martin Odegaard has been one of the league's best playmakers this season, and he is now just two assists away from tying Morten Gamst Pedersen's record for the most by a Norwegian player in the Premier League (38; Pedersen managed 40).

Since the start of 2023-24, only Bruno Fernandes (41) and Mohamed Salah (35) have bettered Odegaard's 25 Premier League assists.

Odegaard will, of course, be going up against his Norway team-mate Erling Haaland, who has been a constant thorn in Arsenal's side ever since he arrived in the Premier League.

Haaland has scored in each of his last four Premier League games against Arsenal and could now become just the fourth player to net in five straight matches against them in the competition, after Jimmy Floyd Hasselbaink in September 2000, Harry Kane in April 2017 and Charlie Austin in December 2018.

The goals have dried up for Haaland lately, however, after he found the net nine times in his first eight Premier League outings of 2026-27.

He has since failed to score in his last three Premier League games, despite totalling 25 touches in the opposing area, 12 shots and exactly 2.0 xG versus Brighton, Nottingham Forest and Fulham.

He could now equal his worst-ever goal drought in the English top flight, having previously failed to score in four consecutive games between February and April this year, against Newcastle, Forest, West Ham and Chelsea.

But Haaland is certainly not the only City player whose performances have dipped lately, and a 2-2 Champions League draw with Napoli in midweek will have done little to lift their spirits.

Since the start of matchday eight, bottom side Crystal Palace (0) are the only team to pick up fewer points than City (one).

They are just the second side in English Football League history to win their first seven games of a season before going winless in their next four, along with Sheffield Wednesday in the 1904-05 top flight.

And their defensive record of late has also proved a real worry. City have conceded the first goal in each of their last four Premier League games.

They last had a longer run in March/April 2012 (five), the fifth game of which was a 1-0 loss at Arsenal with current Gunners boss Arteta netting the winner.

City's terrible run has seen their title chances – in the eyes of the Opta supercomputer, at least – dip to just 9.1%, compared to Arsenal's 76.7%. If they are beaten this weekend, a recovery may already be beyond them.

Havertz scored Arsenal's second goal when they thrashed City in the Community Shield three months ago, maintaining his strong record against the Citizens.

The German has been involved in four goals in his last four games against City in all competitions (three goals, one assist).

Viktor Gyokeres started up front against Dortmund, but given how much Arteta trusts Havertz out of possession, he should return following his midweek rest.

Having dropped out of the XI that drew with Fulham and been reduced to a substitute role against Napoli, Doku will likely return to Maresca's line-up here.
