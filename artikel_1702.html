Moreira discusses starring in the derby, wearing Kaka’s number and De Winter declining call-up

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Diego Moreira has admitted the last few weeks have been ‘complicated’ for AC Milan, but he was delighted to star in the derby.

Having arrived in the summer window for a fee reported to be just over €50m, Moreira has hit the ground running at his new club. The brace against Lazio was a big introduction, inspiring a comeback, then his three goal contributions made it four derby wins in a row for the Rossoneri.

There is even talk that the fee paid is beginning to look like a bargain, given that the former Benfica man is capable of playing multiple positions but appears to be decisive no matter what. Now, a key role with the national team will follow.

Moreira gave an interview to RTL Sports from the Belgian national team’s training camp, where he would normally be joined by Alexis Saelemaekers and Koni De Winter. However, the former is injured and the latter turned down the call-up.

How have the past few weeks been for you?

“These were quite complicated weeks, because we played so many games back-to-back. We also had some players injured, so it was a very intense period. But it was up to us to prepare well for the derby.

“We know how important a derby is in Italy. We played the match to our fullest, imposing our pace and our game. I was decisive in that match, but, as I said, it was also thanks to the help of my team-mates. It’s something that will remain forever etched in my memory.”

“It’s an honour to wear his number and to represent such a player. But, as I said, I’m building my career and also trying to establish myself. It’s still an honour to wear that number.”

What do you think of De Winter’s decision to decline a call-up?

“It’s a personal matter, his choice. It’s something between him and the coach. I’m not the one who should discuss it, neither in terms of his life nor his playing time.

“It’s a question you should ask him directly. Van Bommel’s trust is certainly a good thing, but that doesn’t mean I should rest on my laurels.”
