Official: Starting XIs for AC Milan vs. Frosinone – one change from Genoa win

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

AC Milan head coach Ruben Amorim has named his starting line-up for the Sunday evening San Siro clash with Frosinone.

Milan went into the international break inside the top four, but Como’s win on Saturday has pushed them back out, so there is some added pressure tonight. It will not be an easy game against a newly-promoted Frosinone side who have started the season very well and are full of confidence.

Strahinja Pavlovic is back in the matchday squad after a kidney issue sidelined him, but Amorim has chosen not to risk him from minute one. A back four of Mario Gila, Koni De Winter, Sankhoun Diawara and Davide Bartesaghi will operate in front of Mike Maignan.

It looks like Luka Modric will be flanked by Adrien Rabiot and Yunus Musah in a midfield three, though the Frenchman could play a bit further forward. Diego Moreira and Christian Pulisic are the wingers either side of Goncalo Ramos.

We have become accustomed to line-ups appearing a certain way and being used differently under Amorim though, so there may be some surprises compared to how the team looks on paper.

The bench features the likes of Pavlovic, Ardon Jashari, Omari Hutchinson, Alphadjo Cisse and Francesco Camarda, all of whom will hope to be able to lend a hand in a winning cause if called upon.

Official Milan XI (4-3-3): Maignan; Gila, De Winter, Diawara, Bartesaghi; Musah, Modric, Rabiot; Moreira, Ramos, Pulisic.

Bench: Pittarella, Torriani, Estupinan, Hutchinson, Comotto, Jashari, Pavlovic, F. Terracciano, Cisse, Camarda.

Official Frosinone XI (4-2-3-1): Palmisani; Citizens, Calvani, Monterisi; Oyono, Calò, Masini, Tchato; Schmidt; Ghedjemis, Kvernadze.

Bench: Pisseri, Desplanches, Amey, Birligea, Cichella, Zerbin, Akpoguma, El Azzouzi, Fini, Hasa, Terzic, Fayed, Bobcek.
