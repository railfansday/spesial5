Journalist confirms Liverpool have been 'in contact' with €70m midfield sensation

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Andoni Iraola finally seems to be turning things around at Liverpool.

His squad are just coming off the back of a 4-0 win against arch-rivals Manchester United which proved how good they can be.

Indeed, the Red Devils did end up with a red card within the first 10 minutes of the clash but, the football that Liverpool played on Sunday was some of their best of the season.

If they continue performing like that, there's a good chance that they could get themselves back into the race for the Premier League title.

At the moment, seven points separate them and first-placed Arsenal and, while that's still quite a big gap, it's a number that they can start hacking away at fairly easily.

This task would be made an awful lot easier if, during the January transfer window, Liverpool manage to add one or two new faces to their ranks.

Liverpool have been 'in contact' with Lamine Camara

Lamine Camara is one of the main players who was linked with Liverpool in the summer.

The Senegalese midfielder was rumoured to be one of the Reds' priority targets with them, alongside many other clubs vying for his transfer, despite his €70 million price tag.

Liverpool were the first ones to be linked with the 22-year-old but, when all the other clubs entered the race, the deal got too messy and in the end, they pulled out.

However, there were some rumours on transfer deadline day which claimed that Liverpool had told AS Monaco and Camara to wait for them until January.

And, to the surprise of many, they did just that and pulled out of their deal with Chelsea at the last minute.

The ending of negotiations led to many different questions being asked and several rumours being shared but now, David Lynch, while speaking to Anfield Index, has confirmed that "contact" has been held between the two parties.

"Liverpool did have a word with his representatives and just said kind of hang on for us," he said.

"I’ve got much more solid information that he’s someone that they really like.

"[He is] a player that they like that they’ve been in contact with."

Liverpool must decide who they want their next central midfielder to be

As of now, nobody knows exactly what Liverpool's plans for the January transfer window are.

Many different rumours have been shared with fans, pundits and journalists all making up their own theories.

But, only those within the Reds' recruitment department will know exactly what lies ahead.

However, if their plans don't include a new central midfielder, some serious questions will need to be asked.

Many different players have emerged as options for Liverpool, and, if a new one is on their shortlist, they need to figure out who it's going to be.

Whether it's Camara, Scott, Wharton or even an out there option like Gavi, a decision needs to be made quickly.
