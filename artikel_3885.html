All Fortnite Treasure Hunters loot odds and drop rates explained – Pirate Shop, Weekly Gold Outfit, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

In Fortnite Treasure Hunters, there’s quite a vast collection of special loot you can find and permanently add to your collection. This includes special Treasure Chest items, which have been revealed to have specific odds and drop rates.

The hunt for these rare assets is quite tough and time-consuming, so if you’re unsure whether it’s worth keeping at it, reviewing the specific statistics for acquiring each item may help make this decision easier. Here are all loot odds and drop rates for Treasure Chest items like the Pirate Shop Access, Weekly Gold Outfit, and more in Fortnite Treasure Hunters.

The odds and drop rates for each item you can find in Treasure Chests around the Fortnite Treasure Hunters island vary, with the Weekly Gold Outfit and Pirate Shop Access having the overall lowest numbers. Each time you open a chest in Fortnite Treasure Hunters, there’s a chance you may find one of the special Treasure Chest loot items. Whether or not you find one of these items is essentially RNG and comes down to random luck landing in your favor.

For example, when opening a chest, your odds of acquiring the Weekly Gold Outfit are the worst, with only a 1 in 1,562,500 chance (about 0.000064 percent) of finding this item. For Gizmos, on the other hand, the drop rates look a lot better, with a 1 in 127 (about 0.787 percent) chance of acquiring one while looting.

Technically, the more you loot, the more shots you have at finding these rare items. Opening more chests does not increase your odds or guarantee you will eventually find what you’re seeking, but the more you do try, the more chances you have of getting lucky.

All drop rates and odds for Treasure Chest loot items consistently stay the same throughout every day of the season. Nothing causes them to drop or get lower. The only changes you’ll ever see to these rates are special, helpful boosts to the odds that occur during Treasure Hour events.
