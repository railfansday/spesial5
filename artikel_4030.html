New Witcher 3 DLC Uses AI to Touch Up Geralt's Polish Voice Due to Actor's Health Issues

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

CD Projekt Red has worked with an AI voice generation company on next year's The Witcher 3 expansion, Songs of the Past, so the Polish voiceover for Geralt can match the original game.

Jacek Rozenek, the original Polish voice actor for Geralt has returned to voice the character for all the content in Songs of the Past. However, the actor unfortunately suffered a stroke in recent years that impacted his voice. This has left his voice unrecognisable compared to the lines he recorded for the original RPG.

Having received Rozenek's blessing, CD Projekt Red recorded all his voice work for Songs of the Past, and has taken that work to AI company Respeecher, who will make it sound like Geralt from the base game.

Again, this only affects the Polish language option. Doug Cockle has returned to voice Geralt for the English language version.

Rozenek suffered a stroke in 2019, and he was unable to speak following it. Through hours of training every day, he was able to return to voice acting in 2022, when CD Projekt Red released the PS5 version of The Witcher 3. There was a new quest in that edition, and the work he had to put into the voice lines accompanying it left Rozenek "exhausted" after the recording.

Rozenek has recorded his Polish voice lines for Songs of the Past as usual, but he's supportive of the developer's decision to involve Respeecher to make them sound like Geralt: "The point is that this voice should be as close as possible to my voice from 12 years ago. I have great trust... for the creators of this game."

Respeecher has used AI tools trained on Rozenek's previous work as Geralt to match the voices as much as possible.

"We basically delivered to Respeecher all the lines that Jacek recorded for us for The Witcher 3,” explained localisation producer Mikołaj Szwed. “They created an algorithm of his voice, based on his lines."

Szwed added that CD Projekt Red only considered this route because of Rozenek's health issues, and having gained his blessing, thinks this is an "ethical" use of AI that can do some good.

Songs of the Past is slated to launch for PS5 at some point in 2027, off the back of the recently released The Witcher 3 Remastered.
