The Best Gold Nail Polishes, From Soft Champagne to Molten Chrome

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Gold nail polish has a way of making any manicure feel intentional. It can read as quiet luxury on a bare hand, as festive sparkle at a holiday party, or as a modern accent against deep neutrals. But “gold” isn’t one shade. It’s a whole family, and the right pick depends on your skin tone, your style, and the finish you love.

Some golds are warm and buttery, with a champagne glow that flatters nearly everyone. Others lean rich and antique, with the weight of real metal. Then there are glitter-packed formulas that catch the light with every movement, and chrome finishes that look like liquid foil poured over your nails.

To save you the trial and error, we’ve rounded up the best gold nail polish colors, from soft, sheer shimmers to bold metallics that make a statement. Whether you want a subtle hint of shine for everyday wear or a standout shade for the season’s celebrations, there’s a gold here worth adding to your collection.

This antique gold glitter polish shines in two coats on its own or layered over your favorite color.

Golden Cinnamon, a limited-edition shade timed for the holidays, is every bit as luxurious as it sounds: a sparkling gold that catches the light with every gesture.

This gold metallic dries to a rich, opaque finish that gleams like molten metal, no layering or base color required.

For when you’re short on time, this classic quick-dry polish from Sally Hansen delivers fast-drying color and high shine.

Subtle yet radiant, this light pearly gold adds a soft, luminous glow to bare nails or pairs effortlessly with any neutral.
