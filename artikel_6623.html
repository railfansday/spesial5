Crock-Pot Green Bean Casserole

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Your oven this Thanksgiving is probably booked solid with baking turkey and sweet potatoes, so to lighten the load, this green bean casserole can be made entirely in a slow cooker. The cream of mushroom soup, milk, and spices get whisked really well in the slow cooker first to avoid pulling out a separate bowl, then the green beans get folded in after. Depending on your schedule, you can choose your cook time (2 1/2 hours on high or 5 to 6 hours on low). Bonus, the warm setting on your slow cooker will keep the casserole at perfect serving temperature until you’re ready to eat!

Grab your 6-quart slow cooker and whisk together the soup, milk, onion powder, and a teaspoon of pepper right inside. Next, stir in the green beans and a cup of fried onions. Pop the lid on and cook. Set it to High for 2 to 2 1/2 hours, or Low for 5 to 6 hours, depending on your schedule.

While that’s cooking, grab a medium skillet and toast the rest of the fried onions over medium-high heat. Give them a stir now and then, and they’ll be perfectly golden brown in about 3 minutes.

Once the casserole is ready, season it with a little salt and pepper to taste.

Then sprinkle those toasted fried onions on top for the perfect finish.

Store leftover green beans in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3–4 days. When ready to serve, reheat in the microwave or in a saucepan over low heat.
