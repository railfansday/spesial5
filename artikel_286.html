The cast of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power is well-accustomed to battling Sauron (Charlie Vickers) onscreen. But if the series' stars Vickers, Cynthia Addai-Robinson, Jamie Campbell Bower, and Daniel Weyman had to face off against the Dark Lord himself, which Middle-earth characters would they want fighting at their side?

Find out as the show's s cast, as well as series co-creator Patrick McKay, stop by Mashable's Say More studio for a game of Choose Your Squad, in which they draft their own personal army to face off against Sauron.

Who will be the first draft pick? Which team would be the most fun hang? And of course, which team stands the best chance of taking down Sauron?
