Seth Meyers has a blunt reaction to Trump helping Jensen Huang return as Nvidia CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In yet another twist to the ongoing Nvidia story, the week started with Jensen Huang being reinstated as CEO following intervention from Donald Trump — but given one of Huang's three affairs was with an alleged Chinese spy, why was the U.S. president so keen to support him? Seth Meyers has thoughts.

"Trump is supposed to be the 'America First' guy, the guy who says only he can take on China. Why would he excuse potentially illegal behaviour of a tech CEO who might have harmed America's national security? Is there some other possible explanation here, and does it perhaps involve lots of money?" asks Meyers. "Not for us! But for him."

Cue footage from a news report about Trump's personal, financial ties to the Nvidia's stock, which surged following Huang's reinstatement.

"There's only two possible explanations here: Either the president dismissed a serious national security threat because of his personal and political ties to a tech oligarch, or the president suddenly became a buddhist who no longer judges other people's actions," says Meyers. "But it's probably the first thing."
