Dolan defiant as Hastings United look for turn-around

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Last aturday’s 2-0 defeat at Broadbridge Heath was the latest serback in a run which has left them in sixth spot – so still in the play-off places but now nine points behind leaders Faversham.

Their next bie to emerge from their tricky run comes at ninth-placed Punjab United – and they’ve been boosted by the arrival of ex-Crowborough goal ace Ellioot Duncan, although have lost Mitch Chapman to Margate.

Dolan dissected the week’s events with us and said: “You’d have to say Broadbridge Heath deserved the win. First half, I don’t think they caused us too many problems. Momentum is important – like when you’re doing well and a goal goes in against you, which was an unfortunate error from Harry (Haines). It just went under his hand – there wasn’t much power in it from 20 yards.

"I’m not going to hold that against Harry, he’s been good for us this season. But when things aren’t going for you, little things like that go against you. It kills the momentum you had in the game and it does knock the stuffing out of people a bit.

"Without being disrespectful, I didn’t really see that Broadbridge Heath caused us too many problems, not until the second half when we were 2-0 down and chasing it – they probably could have scored a few more then.

"We chucked bodies forward, we had four strikers on the pitch. We had chances but there was maybe only one that was clear-cut in the second half which we needed to do better with.

"At the moment we are in bad form. It does happen in football – teams don’t win week in, week out, especially at this level, or even at the top level in the Premier League.

"This level is definitely more inconsistent this year. You can see from results, anybody can beat anybody.

"You’ve just got to stay on it and that’s what we’re doing with the boys in training – back to basics, try to keep things simple, don’t over-complicate things, try to make the right decisions, get in the right areas. We’re working hard on putting it right.

"Our boys are prepared for that, we’ve been going through it in training.

"Once we do get a win I genuinely think we’ll be okay. It’s about getting confidence back – and winning games gives you confidence.

"I’ve still got confidence in this squad. If you look at what we did in those fist 10 or 15 games, there’s no reason why we can’t go on another good winning run with the squad we have, with one or two additions."

On addition already on board is hotshot Duncan, who made big FA Cup scoring headlines with the Crows earlier in the season.

"You have to have freshness and competition for places and we’re trying to add to the squad for competition in areas where we feel we need it,” Dolan said.

"Elliott coming in is great competition for the frontline boys. He can play anywhere acorss the frontline and knows the level really well. He’s very good with the ball at his feet and knows where the goal is. He’ll get firing for us.

"He came in for his debut (at Heath) not really knowing any of the boys and will need a game or two to get up to speed with how we play."

Of Chapman’s switch to league rivals Margate, Dolan said: "Mitch has moved on. We have had three approaches for him in the time we have had him, from step three and step four, and this one was in line with where he lives and where they train.

"Mitch did a great job for us – he filled in for me when I was injured – but players move and I have a lot of time for Mitch and have no issue with him moving on.

"We’re hoping to have another one or two added to the squad ready for Saturday. Hopefully we can get them over the line and move on and get the win Saturday.”

Hastingsknow a visit to Punjab United will not be easy. Dolan said: “It will be a hard game away from home. I’ve played there and their pitch is on a bit of a slope. "They’re a very big, physical, direct side, from what I know.”
