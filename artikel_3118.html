Chase wass down for thousands — live updates on massive outage

Chase bank appears to be suffering an online banking outage. On the outage tracking site, Down Detector, reports slowly starting rolling in this morning before suddenly spiking around 10 am Pacific.

Chase released a pair of statements throughout the nearly eight hour outage. The bank claimed that a technical issue brought its services down and was clear that it did not consider the issues a cyberattack.

Tom's Guide tried to confirm with the company but customers claimed that tellers at Chase locations couldn't operate their systems either. At least one support tweet on X seemed to confirm this.

Chase never said if its non-digital locations were compromised.

Here's what we learned throughout the day.

From what I can tell, the Chase outage finally appears to be over. Reports are still over 500 but most comments and social posts are indicating that people have access to their accounts again.

I have not a seen a "We're back" post from Chase or JPMorgan.

For awhile it seemed reports were in steady decline, but things have remained study for the last hour.

I am seeing mixed comments from people saying they have access, while others say they still can't login to the app.

Reports on Down Detector have halved in the last hour dropped from about 3,800 to 1,780.

Its been a slow decline but it appears things are coming back online for people.

People who've regained access have said they've been able to use Zelle, withdraw money and successfully login to the app or the browser.

I just received an update from Chase. Here's what the bank is saying now.

Per the bank's spokesperson, JPMorganChase apparently experienced a software bug in "on-premise hardware system", which the spokesperson reiterated there is "no evidence this is cyber-related."

The statement claims that customers still have access to their accounts and funds. They did not respond to questions about issues affecting brick-and-mortar banks.

"We’re working diligently to resolve this by the end of the day today." It's unclear which timezone that end of day actually refers to.

Chase has been suffering a technical outage for more than 4 and a half hours.

Again, we saw an initial stream of reports around 7 am PT/10 am ET, with the real spike hitting two hours later.

Chase has said in statements that the problem is a technical one, not an attack. It's not clear what the issue is and it appears to affect both online banking and in-person transactions at their banks.

Reports have declined but not enough to declare the outage over. People are still reporting that they can't access their accounts.

We have asked Chase for clarification but haven't received any updates since the first statement.

After an initial decline, reports on Down Detector hit a momentary plateau. For the last half an hour, they've started a slow but steady decline.

Let me know if you're able to access your Chase account since reports started going down.

As of this writing, reports are under 3,200 for the first time since 10 am PT, about 3 hours ago.

Yahoo! Finance is tracking statements from different banks. Wells Fargo and Bank of America have received some outage reports, but not as many as Chase, which again suggests that it's an issue between Chase and other banks.

"You can still access your accounts through our ATMs, banking locations, and customer service," the Wells Fargo website reportedly reads.

I have a Wells Fargo account and could not find that statement, but I've also had no issues logging in or using my accounts online, so my experience may not be the same.

Bank of America has not released a statement.

A Chase spokesperson has responded to our inquiries with a similar statement as the one received by the San Francisco Chronicle.

“Due to a technical issue, some transactions are taking longer than usual to complete but customers still have access to their accounts and funds. We are working to resolve this as quickly as possible."

However, our statement had one extra line saying that the issue was not cyber-related, meaning it is something in the Chase systems and not a malicious attack.

"This is a technical issue, not cyber related.”

The spokesperson did not indicate how long it would take to resolve the issue or any potential causes.

Reports are starting to go Down Detector, though as of this posting they are still around 4,000 after a peak of 5,000 reports about 30 minutes ago.

Analyst Bruce Schneier (Security Technologist; Fellow at Harvard Kennedy School) is an internationally renowned security technologist.

We asked him why companies don't have redundancies to prevent outages:

"Redundancies reduce profitability, and it is the goal of shareholders these days to wring all excess profits out of any and every company. Thatreduces resilience in general, and redundancy in particular.”

With the Outlook outage last week, and more every day, Schneier expects more will come.

"I guess I’m not surprised. Our systems are very fragile these days. Fragile systems are the most profitable in the near term. And there is no incentive to focus on the long term.”

Chase has not responded to Tom's Guide as of this posting; however, a spokesperson did give a statement to the San Francisco Chronicle.

“Due to a technical issue, some transactions are taking longer than usual to complete, but customers still have access to their accounts and funds,” a Chase spokesperson told the paper. “We are working to resolve this as quickly as possible.”

This does seem to contradict users on social media claiming they can't log in.

I'm seeing reports and comments that people are having trouble logging into other banks including Bank of America and Wells Fargo.

I've been able to login into Wells, but that may not be universal.

Lets u know, are you having problems with your bank?

According to posters on Reddit, people have said that after attempting online transactions they ended up going to their location branch.

Multiple people said that the Chase tellers told them all their systems were down and they couldn't do anything.

"Then went to branch to ask about it they said they can’t do anything because their system is down smh," said one representative poster.

Tom's Guide has reached out to Chase for comment.

I am seeing hundreds of reports for Bank of America, JP Morgan, Wells Fargo and Zelle, but based on the amounts and the ability to log in to accounts with those banks, I currently believe this incident is isolated to Chase.

However, if you're trying to send or receive money from or to a Chase account, you will likely have problems.
