“Not True”: Antoine Fuqua Alleged Gun Assault Of Bar Manager Over Daughter Rebuked By Reps

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Executive Editor, Legal, Labor & Politics

Accusations in a recently filed assault and battery complaint against Antoine Fuqua from a Hollywood assistant bar manager got a concise and blunt response today from the Michael director’s rep.

“What’s claimed in that filing about Antoine is not true,” publicist Allan Nierob told Deadline Monday.

The PMK Entertainment boss wouldn’t add more, but it was clear that Fuqua and his team plan to meet the allegations and $50,000 in damages demand from Good Times at Davey Wayne’s William Rosenthal head on. Under California law, defendants usually have around 30 calendar days from being served to respond to such a civil complaint. Fuqua has not yet been served, sources tell me, thought Rosenthal’s filing in LA Superior Court occurred on November 20. Watch on Deadline

In the 10-page jury trial seeking document, from El Segundo firm Greene Broillet & Wheeler, Rosenthal says that Fuqua and his brother Brando came after him on the night of February 11 after the Training Day filmmaker’s daughter and some friends wee refused entrance to the N El Centro Ave venue for being “visibly overly intoxicated.” The 61-year-old Fuqua supposedly showed up later and accused Rosenthal of pepper spraying his daughter, which the bar employee denied.

The complaint then says: “As a result, Defendant ANTOINE FUQUA pulled out a handgun and struck Plaintiff in the face. After striking Plaintiff, Defendant ANTOINE FUQUA directed a racial and antisemitic slur at Plaintiff and threatened to kill him. As Plaintiff staggered back in pain, Defendant BRANDO FUQUA, appeared and approached Plaintiff and forcefully swung a handgun at Plaintiff’s face. Defendants ANTOINE FUQUA and BRANDO FUQUA then pointed their handguns at Plaintiff, placing him in imminent fear for his life, before fleeing down the street. Due to the acts of Defendants ANTOINE FUQUA, BRANDO FUQUA, and DOES 1 through 50, Plaintiff sustained severe physical and mental injuries.”

Law enforcement sources tell Deadline that an investigation of the months ago incident resulted in no further action from them — so take that as you will. At the same time, there does seem to have been some chemical spray or something similar in the situation.
