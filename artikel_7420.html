Sumo Logic: Principal Product Marketing Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

As a Principal Product Marketing Manager — AI Systems & Content Architecture, you will determine the architecture of our narrative and collateral engine. Rather than operating as an artisanal, bespoke content creator, your mandate is to establish our foundational positioning architecture and engineer high-leverage, AI-driven workflows that generate enterprise-grade sales collateral at scale. You will own the strategic narrative for Sumo Logic’s Log Analytics Platform across Observability and Security personas, establishing automated knowledge loops, scalable asset generation engines, and self-serve frameworks that allow a focused team to deliver the output and velocity typically associated with a much larger company.

Sumo Logic, Inc. helps make the digital world secure, fast, and reliable by unifying critical security and operational data through its Intelligent Operations Platform. Built to address the increasing complexity of modern cybersecurity and cloud operations challenges, we empower digital teams to move from reaction to readiness—combining agentic AI-powered SIEM and log analytics into a single platform to detect, investigate, and resolve modern challenges. Customers around the world rely on Sumo Logic for trusted insights to protect against security threats, ensure reliability, and gain powerful insights into their digital environments. For more information, visit www.sumologic.com.
