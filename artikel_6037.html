News and notes from around the interweb: Somewhat similar to Bucholz Army Airfield on Kwajalein Atoll, Marshall Islands which is a stop on the United Airlines island hopper. Yanji Airport is jointly used by military and civilian aircraft, so passengers are required to close their window shades during takeoff and landing. Because military aircraft have priority, our flight that day was not cleared to land for quite a while, even after the final approach announcement was made. In the end, we spent nearly 30 minutes circling the airport at low altitude with the window shades closed.
