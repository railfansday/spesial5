Booking Holdings has carved Priceline out of its new Booking Partner Division because of a potential U.S. Federal Trade Commission complaint against the brand. Omri Morgenshtern, head of the new division and CEO of Agoda, told Skift at Phocuswright in Hollywood, Florida, that the investigation created "operational complexities" and that Priceline "will keep serving their partners." The original plan was to merge Rocket Travel by Agoda, Booking.com partnerships, and the Priceline Partner Network into one B2B unit serving banks, OTAs, airlines, hotels, loyalty programs, and travel agencies. Instead, Booking.com partnerships and Rocket Travel by Agoda will unify, and the Priceline Partner Network will operate on its own. Booking Holdings took a $1 billion charge in the third quarter tied to the FTC matter, after disclosing earlier this year that the FTC intended to file a complaint against Priceline and a B2B affiliate partner over "unfair or deceptive business practices related to disclosures, fees, customer support, and billing practices." Morgenshtern said including Priceline is not the goal "at the moment," and that Booking is likely considering breaking out the financials of its B2B segment, as Expedia Group does. He is relocating to New York to run the division while staying on as Agoda CEO, supported by recently promoted Chief Operating Officer Ittai Chorev.
