CM: Krösche given ‘no limits’ for January as Cardinale prepares €100m budget

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

AC Milan are going to have a big January and Markus Krösche has been given his brief by Gerry Cardinale, it seems.

After a solid start to the season, Cardinale plans to invest more in Ruben Amorim and he will do so at the first opportunity, in January. So, alongside making his new Director of Football hire, he will invest heavily in the mercato.

According to Calciomercato.com, Krösche will be given ‘no limits’ by Cardinale when he steps into his role in the new year. The German will be given a substantial budget to help transform the squad in the mercato and this has also been made clear to his staff already at the club.

€70 million has already been put aside for a striker, with two huge names linked. Then, another €30m will be made available for other positions. There are, as the report writes, another two or three priority moves that need to be completed, too.

A full-back and a winger are desired, but the second priority will be a central defender. Goncalo Inacio is the main target and he’s set to cost between €40-45m. In short, it’s going to be a big month for the incomings.

However, Krösche’s main task will be sorting the outgoings. Cardinale hopes that the German will be able to sell a few players who haven’t convinced this season to help make funds available. Additionally, he will be responsible for sorting out the futures of Francesco Camarda and Christian Comotto.
