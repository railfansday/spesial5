How to watch Slavia Prague vs Arsenal: TV channel and live stream for Champions League today

Arsenal face Slavia Prague in the Champions League tonight, exactly a year on from their last visit to the Czech Republic.

When these two sides met on November 4, 2025, it finished 3-0 to Arsenal as Mikel Merino added to Bukayo Saka’s penalty with a second half brace.

While, the Gunners go into this meeting on the back of a huge win over Liverpool to end their long Anfield hoodoo, last week’s shock Carabao Cup exit at the hands of Fleetwood Town serves as a reminder against complacency.

That said, Mikel Arteta’s side are big favourites to maintain their unbeaten start to this season’s league phase, having beaten Napoli and Lille, and earning a 2-2 draw with Bayern Munich a fortnight ago.

Prague, meanwhile, have picked up just one point from their opening three Champions League fixtures, losing to Lens and competition debutants Sabah, but did claw back a two-goal deficit against Fenerbahce to earn a point.

The Czech side have never beaten Gunners in five previous attempts, failing to score in any of the home meetings which also includes losing 4-0 in an Europa League quarter-final second leg back in April 2021.

Here’s everything you need to know about where to watch the match...

TV channel: In the UK, the game will be televised live on TNT Sports. Coverage starts at 7.45pm GMT on TNT Sports 1 ahead of kick-off at 8pm.
