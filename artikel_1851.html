Scotland’s World Cup award can be game-changing for the nation

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Scotland captain Caroline Weir says the country being named as a host nation for the 2035 Women’s World Cup is a “pivotal moment” for the sport.

The United Kingdom’s four-nation bid was given the official seal of approval in a vote by acclamation at an Extraordinary FIFA Congress on Monday, having been the sole valid bid in contention for the event.

Nineteen stadiums are proposed, with 14 in England, one in Northern Ireland, two in Scotland and two in Wales in a tournament which will feature 48 teams.

While the 2022 European Championships were held in England, Scotland has never hosted a major women’s football event. Hampden Park in Glasgow (capacity 52,032) and Hibs’ Easter Road in Edinburgh (capacity 20,093) are Scotland’s Candidate Stadiums and would stage at least group-stage matches. Weir feels that having the competition on Scotland’s doorstep will show youngsters in this country that “anything is possible”.

Midfielder Weir, who currently plays for Lyon and has won 122 caps for Scotland, said: “Hosting the 2035 FIFA Women’s World Cup in the UK is a pivotal moment for women’s football in this country. Playing at the 2019 Women’s World Cup in France was an incredible moment in my career, so knowing that the next generation of Scottish players will have the chance to experience that on home soil is so special.

“The women's game in Scotland has so much potential and this tournament is a real opportunity to drive momentum and help take it to another level both nationally and internationally.

“For young girls and boys across the country, seeing the best players in the world right on their doorstep will show them that anything is possible.

“We’re so proud to play our part in welcoming the world in 2035 and I know I speak on behalf of all of the players and staff when I say that we are ready to rise to the challenge and help deliver a truly memorable, world-class tournament.”

Scottish Football chief executive Ian Maxwell hailed the decision to award the event to the UK. “Securing the 2035 FIFA Women’s World Cup alongside our Home Nations partners is a momentous milestone for Scottish football,” he said. “Scotland has a proud footballing heritage and a rapidly growing women’s game, and welcoming the world to Barclays Hampden and Easter Road will turbo-charge the progress we have made across our grassroots, domestic, and international game.

“This tournament will be the single-biggest sporting event ever staged in the UK, and our ambition goes far beyond the matches themselves. It gives us a powerful platform to inspire participation in a new generation of girls and women across Scotland, empower more female leaders and match officials and deliver a sustainable commercial and community legacy for many years to come.”

It will be the first time that Easter Road, the home of Hibs, has been selected to host a major international event and the club say they are hugely excited at the prospect.

Hibs Women’s head coach Joelle Murray said: “This is fantastic news for women's football in Scotland and something we should all be extremely excited about.

“To welcome the best players in the world to Easter Road would be incredibly special and provide a brilliant opportunity to showcase the women's game. It would also give young girls across the country the chance to experience a World Cup on their doorstep and to see what is possible to achieve through football.

“Having played and coached at Easter Road throughout my career, it holds a very special place in my heart. To see it named as a Candidate Stadium for a Women's World Cup is a hugely proud moment for me, and the opportunity to inspire the next generation makes it even more exciting.”

Hibs chief executive Dan Barnett added: “The inclusion of Easter Road as a Candidate Stadium for the 2035 FIFA Women's World Cup is fantastic news for the Club and the City of Edinburgh.

"This is something we've been working hard on for several months, with various positive discussions with the Scottish FA and key stakeholders. In recent years, we have been delighted to host Scotland Women's National Team matches at Easter Road, so to be named as a Candidate Stadium for the finals in 2035 is a huge honour.

“To have the opportunity to showcase our stadium on the world's biggest stage would be an immensely proud moment for the Club. It presents a unique opportunity to welcome tens of thousands of supporters from across the world to Edinburgh and create a lasting legacy for the women's game.

"We're excited by the next steps and the prospect of working alongside the Scottish FA, the City of Edinburgh Council and our partners to help bring this opportunity to life.”

The bid team projected the tournament would attract 3.5 billion viewers worldwide, and that 4.5 million tickets would be sold. FIFA’s evaluation of the bid, published earlier this month, rated it 4.3 out of five.

The tournament will feature 48 nations and 104 matches across 39 days, with the final host cities and stadiums to be confirmed by FIFA in due course.
