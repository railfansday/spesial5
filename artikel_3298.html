Some holiday traditions are classics for a reason: reading The Night Before Christmas on Dec. 24, putting up your favorite decorations, cueing the seasonal playlist, and gifting a watch to someone special.

Timepieces are practically foolproof holiday gifts. There’s no complicated sizing to figure out (the average man’s wrist measures 6 to 7.5 inches, and even if you get it wrong, resizing is usually a simple fix), they feel luxurious without necessarily requiring a big budget, and there are enough styles out there to match just about anyone’s personality. Plus, unlike that sweater you weren’t sure they’d actually wear, a good watch has a pretty solid chance of making it into their regular rotation.

The only tricky part? Deciding which watch brand to shop. There are hundreds out there, all claiming to be the best. Fortunately, you’ve landed on the gift guide of someone who quite literally shops around the clock. Don’t worry, it’s in my job description. I’ve tested thousands of brands and personally gifted watches for just about every major milestone, from weddings to anniversaries, and I always find myself coming back to Bulova.
