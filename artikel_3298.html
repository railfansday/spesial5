Sasha Lane, Alia Shawkat & Simon Rex Wrap On Horror Thriller ‘Nepo Babies’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Sasha Lane (Twisters), Alia Shawkat (Blink Twice) and Simon Rex (Red Rocket) have wrapped on the Mexico City shoot for Nepo Babies, a darkly comic horror thriller written and directed by Brian McGreevy.

Described as Entourage by way of David Lynch, Nepo Babies unfolds over a New Year’s Eve pregame in Los Angeles, following a close-knit circle of privileged young Hollywood friends whose glamorous celebration takes a sinister turn when an unwelcome outsider arrives.

Others in the cast include Gabriella Kessler (The Beast in Me), Pretty Lonesome podcast host Madeline Argy, who makes her acting debut, Samuel Farnsworth (Dickinson), Corrado Martini (Vampire Carnival), and comedian Lucas Zelnick (Pizza Movie). The film is produced by McGreevy through BPM Video, Braxton Pope (Paul Schrader’s The Card Counter, The Basics of Philosophy) under his Film Maudit banner, Gabriella Kessler through Betwixt, Stacy Perskie (Blink Twice) of Redrum, Samantha Sneider of Cine Alquimia and Cameron Parker. McGreevy is coming off his feature debut with The Technique, a thriller he wrote and directed, which stars Kelsey Asbille, Nicholas Alexander Chavez, Ben Platt, Emma Roberts, Laura Harrier and Noomi Rapace. He is represented by Dissident and The Nord Group.

American Honey breakout Lane has most recently been seen in Ira Sachs’ Rami Malek-led The Man I Love, which premiered at Cannes this year. Also: the films Corporate Retreat and Twisters. She is repped by Untitled and Granderson Des Rochers.

Shawkat stars alongside Kristen Stewart in Neon’s stoner comedy The Wrong Girls. She’ll next be seen in Tony Gilroy’s Behemoth!, out December 4. She is repped by UTA, Mgmt Entertainment and Lichter, Grossman, Nichols.

Rex is coming off films including The Leader, Operation Taco Gary’s and Easy’s Waltz. He is repped by Buchwald and Range.

Kessler is repped by IAG and Reel Talent; Argy by UTA and Yorn Levine; Farnsworth by BRS/Gage and Venture Entertainment Partners; Martini by UTA and Untitled; Zelnick by WME, Mosaic and Ziffren Brittenham; and Perskie by WME and Hirsch Wallerstein Hayum.
