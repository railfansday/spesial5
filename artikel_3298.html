CAA Inks With Hybe To Rep Korean Firm’s Artists, IP & Brands

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

CAA has signed Hybe to rep its artist roster including K-Pop behemoth BTS, along with the South Korean giant’s IP and brands. The companies will collaborate across content, brand partnerships, sports, licensing, merchandising and live experiences.

Hybe’s acts also include such groups as Katseye, Tomorrow X Together, Seventeen and many more.

“This partnership with CAA marks an important step in expanding creative possibilities for Hybe’s artists,” Hybe CEO Jason Jaesang Lee said in a statement. “By bringing together our complementary strengths and global networks, we aim to extend the reach and value of our artists and their IP across content, brands, and live experiences. Together, we will explore new creative opportunities that transcend borders and formats, bringing audiences closer to our artists through more immersive and meaningful experiences.”

Added CAA CEO and co-chair Bryan Lourd: “Hybe has created a cultural movement. Hybe artists do not just entertain; they connect with audiences in extraordinary and innovative ways that define the future of our industry and the intersection of art, influence and fandom. We are honored to help carry that forward, building on CAA’s experience and relationships across the region, to show the world what is possible when two organizations that believe in creative talent come together.”

Hybe, which rebranded from Big Machine Entertainment in 2021, is a global entertainment lifestyle platform company built on music and technology. With operations across Korea, Japan, the U.S., Mexico, China, and India, it runs numerous record labels including Bighit Music, Belift Lab, Source Music, Pledis Entertainment, Koz Entertainment. Hybe America is behind Blue Highway Records, music publisher Big Machine Music and Quality Control Music, along with Hybe’s labels in Latin America and the U.S. Hispanic market.

Beyond music, Hybe continues to expand across concerts, video content, gaming, original stories, retail, and fan platforms.
