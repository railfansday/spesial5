Fall’s signature cool, crisp temperatures have me reevaluating more than just my wardrobe. My skincare routine is due for a seasonal refresh—but one beauty ritual that I won’t skip is body hair removal. There’s nothing wrong with body hair, of course. But if you’re like me and prefer smooth, dolphin-like skin brushing against your matching workout sets and cozy sweater dresses, then chances are that you’ve tried a slew of hair removal methods.

Shaving is cheap and easy, but it requires daily maintenance. Depilatory hair removal creams are convenient, but they can easily irritate sensitive skin. Waxing results last up to six weeks, but the discomfort can also be unpleasant for those with sensitive skin. Some of our editors adore laser hair removal devices, but they’ll set you back at least $300. Many aren’t designed with Brown and Black skin tones like mine.

However, we’re all in on one hair removal technique: epilators. They resemble electric razors, but instead of trimming hairs at the surface, they pluck them from the root for long-lasting, smooth skin. Because the hair-free results last for two to four weeks, epilators are a great alternative to shaving and hair removal creams. Repeated sessions slow down hair growth, and your unwanted strands will be finer when they regrow, says dermatologist Hadley King, MD. They’re also gentler than laser hair removal devices, which is why Dr. King recommends them for anyone with sensitive skin or who isn't a good fit for laser hair removal.

My favorites start at just $9, so you can enjoy hair-free skin without putting a major dent in your beauty budget ahead of the holiday season. Epilators are unique because they’re equipped with built-in tweezers to pluck hairs from the root.

The smaller heads have fewer tweezers, but they're fantastic for targeted areas like the face, underarms, and bikini line.

Larger epilator heads are better for covering bigger areas like your legs. Having a lot of tweezers helps speed up your hair removal routine, but it also means you’ll have a more painful experience because it’s plucking dozens of hairs at a time. But like ripping a bandage off a hairy arm, it’s uncomfortable at first, but not too rough. And FWIW, Dr. King promises the pain level noticeably improves after multiple sessions. So be brave and patient, and it’ll all be over before you know it.

For the most effective hair removal, your body hair should be about the length of a sprinkle or grain of rice (about 0.25 inches or 6.35 mm). This standard for epilators is the same as waxing and sugaring. When your hair is too short, it's challenging for the epilator to grasp and remove the strands. You also don't want them to be longer than about two rice grains to help prevent hairs from getting tangled and minimize pain. Everyone's hair regrowth is different, but for most people, stubble typically occurs within one to two weeks.
