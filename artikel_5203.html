WBTC is expanding its presence across confidential DeFi, STRK reward opportunities and institutional lending through integrations with Zama, Twinstake and Two Prime.

Zama brings Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE) to public blockchains, enabling smart contracts to process encrypted data while supporting publicly verifiable on-chain financial activity. Through the Wintermute Confidential WBTC vault, curated by Armitage and accessible through the Zama application, this technology introduces confidential financial positions to WBTC-based DeFi strategies.

Built on Morpho, the vault encrypts individual deposit and withdrawal amounts, as well as user share balances. Wallet addresses, transaction timestamps, aggregate vault metrics and underlying lending allocations remain publicly visible on-chain. When users convert standard WBTC into its confidential representation, the initial amount may also be visible, depending on how the conversion is performed.

Eligible WBTC holders can participate in Starknet’s native staking ecosystem by depositing WBTC into an ERC-4626 smart contract vault operated by Endur. The vault routes funds through delegator contracts to institutional validators, including Twinstake.  Staking rewards are generated in STRK and automatically converted into WBTC, with rewards reflected in the exchange rate of xWBTC relative to WBTC. Rewards are variable and are not guaranteed.

This arrangement connects WBTC with Starknet’s native staking infrastructure without requiring holders to stake directly into the network’s consensus layer or use liquidity pools or leveraged strategies.

The Two Prime Axiom WBTC Yield Vault, available through Pareto, gives eligible WBTC holders access to an institutional lending strategy. The vault lends to a network of vetted institutional borrowers, including public companies, credit-rated entities and diversified financial institutions. Two Prime conducts borrower underwriting and ongoing credit reviews.

The strategy combines Two Prime’s institutional lending approach with Pareto’s on-chain private credit infrastructure. ICE Digital Trust serves as the primary custodian for the underlying assets, while Copper provides smart contract signing and asset security infrastructure. Participation is subject to the vault’s eligibility requirements, terms and associated risks.
