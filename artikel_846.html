Are the Cowboys wasting another year?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Watch the NFL's Thanksgiving triple-header live on Sky Sports from 5pm Thursday, starting with Chicago Bears at Detroit Lions, followed by the Philadelphia Eagles against the Dallas Cowboys and Patrick Mahomes's Kansas City Chiefs versus Josh Allen's Buffalo Bills from 1.20am Friday

The Dallas Cowboys are in danger of wasting another prolific year from star quarterback Dak Prescott as they continue to fight for their playoff lives.

Prescott and the Cowboys are 5-6 and second in the NFC East as they face their divisional rival Philadelphia Eagles on Thanksgiving Thursday, live on Sky Sports NFL.

Dallas leaned on their offense once again in Week 11 when they stormed to a 51-30 win over the Tennessee Titans behind four touchdown passes from Prescott and 100-yard receiving games for both CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens.

It is an offense ranked second in passing and third in scoring, but hindered by a defense that has slumped to second-worst in yards, fifth-worst against the pass, dead last in rushing and fourth-worst in scoring. Deja vu in Dallas.

"It never has been Dak's fault," said Sky Sports NFL's Phoebe Schecter. "For years now, Dak Prescott has done a phenomenal job.

"Originally it was only him and CeeDee Lamb. You bring in George Pickens last year, they've upped the game. He is now able to spread that ball out, both of them contested catchers, yards after the catch.

"They're so much fun to watch, but there's always one side that's letting them down. And it's so hard to not look at this defense."

Prescott is currently 276 of 420 [65.7 per cent] passing for 3,049 yards - second only to Joe Burrow in the league - and 25 touchdowns to six interceptions. Lamb, meanwhile, is ranked second behind Jaxon Smith-Njigba with 1,108 receiving yards, followed by Pickens down in 11th with 780 as part of one of football's most dominant aerial trios.

But it will mean nothing if the Cowboys continue to be haunted by long-term issues on defense.

"And even with everything that's gone on with defensive coordinator Christian Parker and Mike Pettine, and you get Caleb Downs, who's supposed to be one of the best [young defenders] out there," Schecter added.

"You must be so frustrated if you're Dak Prescott in this offense. And Brian Schottenheimer is doing all he can to keep that positivity.

"But man, when you've got a quarterback that can do that and then a defense that can't stop anything, it is so frustrating."

The Cowboys switched play-calling duties from under-fire defensive coordinator Christian Parker to senior defensive assistant Mike Pettine for the first time against the Titans in Week 11. Still, they shipped 30 points against one of the NFL's struggling teams.

They now take on an Eagles team coming off a 19-16 win over the Pittsburgh Steelers to end a seven-game losing streak.

Dallas are trailing the New York Giants (6-3-1) at the top of the division, and face fresh competition from the Washington Commanders after they moved to 4-5-1 with a shock win over the Cincinnati Bengals.
