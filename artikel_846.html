How Hailey Bieber, Justin Bieber's Son Is Following in Dad's Footsteps

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Hailey Bieber shared a new clip of her and Justin Bieber's son Jack Blues, 2, following in his footsteps by playing with a keyboard.

That's Hailey Bieber and Justin Bieber's baby, he's iconic.

The Rhode Beauty founder shared a new video of her and Justin's son Jack Blues, 2, that revealed he may already be following in the "Sorry" singer's footsteps when it comes to music.

Hailey posted a quick clip of her son on her Instagram Stories Nov. 12, showing him being mesmerized by a keyboard as he plays with the keys. In fact, zooms in on Jack's fingers in the video taken from the couch behind him as he leans against a coffee table and plays his tune while wearing a salmon-colored sweatsuit.

She wrote over the video that "blues loves the keys."

Jack's apparent penchant for music shouldn't come as a surprise, of course. The pint-sized tot has gotten a front row seat to his dad's music career, like when he cheered Justin on as he prepared for his headlining set at Coachella this spring—even rocking a temporary Bieberchella tattoo.

Hailey had previously shared insight about what it's like "seeing this little human evolve before your eyes" as she raises her first child.

"They grow so fast and they change so much and there’s always so much excitement," she told Interview in April. "You’re watching them experience life for the first time, so you kind of feel like you’re experiencing it that way with them."

Hailey—who recently celebrated her 8th wedding anniversary with Justin—also gave some insight into their plans to potentially expand their family.

"I definitely want more than one, but I’m taking it one at a time," she shared at the time. "Some days I want two. Some days I want five."

"I always wanted to be married and have kids and a family from a super young age," she added before demonstrating how much she loves being a mom. "Kids are just so yummy in the morning when they first wake up. They’re just so smushy."
