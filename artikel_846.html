Isaiah Washington Debuts New Romance One Year After Wife’s Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grey’s Anatomy alum Isaiah Washington revealed he’s in a new relationship with content creator Kei Bennett one year after his wife Jenisa Garland’s death: “She is all mine now and I am all hers."

It’s a beautiful day for Isaiah Washington’s personal life.

More than a year after the Grey’s Anatomy alum shared wife Jenisa Garland had died suddenly in June 2025, he detailed his next chapter with content creator Kei Bennett.

“I have known many incredible women in my life,” Washington wrote on Instagram Nov. 17 alongside a photo of the new lady in his life, “but not a single one of them can hold a candle to the deep Southern charm of @keibenet.”

“I am not only thrilled to have gotten to know her in the last 9 months talking to her for hours every single day,” he wrote, “I am absolutely baffled how any man in her past could have harmed her, belittled her, disrespected her, or even betrayed her in the most deplorable ways.”

As for how she’s pulled at his heart strings?

“She has an incredible mind, heart, and spirit and I have grown to respect her intelligence, green thumb, hair stylist talent, entrepreneurship, holistic approach, and her black business owner's business acumen immensely,” he explained. “Yes, she has earned her place at my creative table and I love her dearly.”

And while Bennett may have kissed her fair share of frogs in the past, he feels they were all leading to her prince.

“I am so glad that all of the men who treated her like she was less than a Queen, all left her heartbroken, but not broken,” he wrote. “Don’t worry I will take it from here and I will never let her down as a friend and partner in love and creativity.”

“There has been no other woman who has made me laugh and has encouraged me as she has,” he added. “Yeah, I don't play about Kei Bennett, and just know that she doesn't play about me. Yeah…she is all mine now and I am all hers. Deal with it.”

And it’s clear Bennett is just as smitten, commenting under the post, “I love you to pieces. Thank you for this. My protector, my best friends, my love, Mousie’s poppop…you exceed every goal you set and have the most pure heart I’ve ever seen.”

The couple’s hard launch comes after Washington’s wife of 29 years died following a weeklong health battle in June 2025. Just last month, he shared deeper insight into the tragedy.

"It came as a huge shock to me and our entire family," Washington—dad to kids Isaiah, Tyme and Iman with Garland—captioned his Oct. 3 Instagram post. "She succumbed to a massive hemorrhagic stroke and never recovered."

And while her death came as a shock to their family, Washington shared how grateful he was to have his three kids by his side during the devastating chapter. As he put it, "She has been gone 16 months now, but she left 3 incredibly amazing children behind to continue her legacy."
