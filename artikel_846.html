Jennifer Lopez Cried for 6 Months When Her Twins Left for College

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Jennifer Lopez admitted that she felt a range of emotions when her twins Max and Emme, 18, went off to college in the fall: "It goes so fast, just like everybody says."

Jennifer Lopez is getting loud about her emotions.

The “On the Floor” singer revealed that she took it quite hard when her and ex-husband Marc Anthony’s 18-year-old twins Max and Emme left home to start college earlier this fall.

“I cried for six months straight,” Lopez admitted on The Tonight Show With Jimmy Fallon Nov. 15. “From two months before they graduated until I dropped them off and a few weeks after that.”

“I still have on my phone them on my lap as babies,” she shared. “It goes so fast, just like everybody says,”

And the 57-year-old’s her emotions took several laps around the block, with her detailing the mixed emotions that come with seeing her kids enter the world. Lopez explained, “It’s one of those moments in life where you’re just like, you’re sad, you’re melancholy, but you’re excited and you’re hopeful. There’s just so many feelings that go with it.”

The Grammy nominee also warned host Jimmy Fallon he would likely have a similar reaction when his two daughters with wife Nancy Juvonen reach that milestone, quipping, “You’re gonna die.”

Still, despite the sorrow, there was a bit of joy, as becoming an empty nester opened up a new level of freedom for Lopez.

“[It’s] the first time since I was like, in my early 20s, that I haven’t been with someone, had my kids,” she noted. “I’m alone. I’m free. I can do whatever the f--k I want. It’s kind of amazing, but also weird, but also amazing.”

And while she misses the twins “so much,” she’s also found herself inspiration to create new music, leading to a new era of her life.

“When the kids were leaving, I was having so many feelings and so many emotions that I went into the studio and started kind of like playing around and making music, and now I’ve made 20 songs,” Lopez said. “I feel like I’m only halfway done—I need to be in there longer.”

“This does feel like a happy era where I feel unencumbered with anything kind of unhealthy,” the Selena actress added. “It’s just a very healthy time in my life. I’m on my own. I feel like it’s another coming of age that happens in our lives.”

And that feeling of “coming of age” was one she can relate to, saying that she first felt it when she left home, much like Max and Emme are feeling now.

“You have it again when your kids leave, because for 20 years that's all it's about. And now it's just me,” she shared. “So it’s a very happy time, in a lot of ways, even though it had a lot of other emotions involved with it.”
