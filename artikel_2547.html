Frankfurt captain Laura Freigang will miss Women's World Cup after 16-month suspension for anti-doping rule violation

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Laura Freigang is set to miss the 2027 Women's World Cup after a 16-month suspension for committing three reporting and/or testing failures of anti-doping protocols. The Eintracht Frankfurt captain will only be able to return to the pitch from October 8th, 2027, which will be more than two months after the end of the biggest competition in women's football.

Freigang, WADA, NADA and the DFB agreed on this settlement on Thursday as a more severe punishment of two years was also a possibility. The failures to comply with the responsible organizations committed between March 11, 2025 and February 23, 2026.

The ban applies retroactively from June 8 as all of her results since February 23 will now be nullified. The attacker will be allowed to train with a team again starting from August 8, 2027.

The Kiel-native scored 17 goals in 45 appearances for Germany, including playing in the previous World Cup, Euros and the Olympics. She was initially named in Christian Wück's squad for the South America tour.

Frankfurt are currently third in the Frauen-Bundesliga as they head into the last international break of the year with three successive wins in all competitions. They will face Real Sociedad in the quarter-final tie of the UEFA Europa Cup on February 2027.
