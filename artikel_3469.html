Hila Klein Announces She's Walking Away From 'H3 Podcast' Amid Divorce

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

“H3 Podcast” host Hila Klein is stepping away from the popular podcast after filing for divorce from husband cohost Ethan Klein, whom she publicly accused of cheating on her.

“I’m so sorry to share that I’ve made the difficult decision to stop working on the new show,” Hila, 38, shared in a statement posted via the podcast’s Instagram account on Monday, November 23.

She continued, “I truly tried with every ounce of strength I had to make this work, but after everything that has happened, I’ve come to realize that I can’t continue down this path while also giving myself the space I need to heal and protect my mental health.”

“Right now, I need to focus on myself, my healing, and what’s best for me and my kids,” Hila wrote. “I’m devastated that things have come to this. I love this community we’ve built together so much, and I love the crew like my own family. Walking away from something that has been such a huge part of my life is incredibly painful.”

“I will miss you all more than I can put into words. Thank you for all the love and support over the years,” she concluded.

Hila accused Ethan, 41, of having a six-month affair in a shocking Instagram post on October 23, which led to the “H3 Podcast” going on an “indefinite hiatus.”

At the time, Ethan said he was “working with Hila to urgently remove my ownership and financial stakes in any podcasts” and other joint ventures created through their production company, h3h3 Productions.

On November 13, Hila and some of the “H3 Podcast” crew shared a photo on Instagram from a team meeting, suggesting that they were planning to get a new version of the podcast — sans Ethan — back on track.

Nearly a month after her affair allegations, Hila posted a 30-minute YouTube video on November 18, detailing Ethan’s alleged infidelity.

She said, “This is a person that I’ve been in a relationship with for 20 years, since I was 19, and I thought I knew him more than anybody. … When I found out, it was like my whole entire life just shattered in front of me. And it was just like the floor just disappeared. All of a sudden, I realized that this person has been lying to me every single day.”

“Now I just don’t even know him at all,” she said. “I don’t know who is this person? I’m now divorcing Ethan. I need to figure out what is my life going to look like? How do we coparent when I can’t look at him or talk to him? Where am I going to live? What is going to happen to my career, my job, my employees who I’m responsible for?”
