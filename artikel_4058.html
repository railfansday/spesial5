Romancing SaGa 3's Great Looking PS5 Remake Moves Its Release Date Away from Persona 4 Revival

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United will no longer launch just two days before the relative juggernaut that is Persona 4 Revival.

Previously set to release on the 16th February, the promising JRPG remake has actually been moved forward. It now drops nearly two weeks earlier, on the 4th February.

Square Enix must have read our coverage of the initial announcement, where I suggested that its release date was a real problem!

It'll almost certainly benefit from the increased breathing room — there's just no way it can compete with Revival's Xbox-partnered marketing heft — but it's still launching during a very busy period for new games.

The hope, then, is that Destinies United can stand tall on the basis of its quality. The original Romancing SaGa 3 was ahead of its time in terms of player choice and gameplay freedom, offering up an adventure that's split between a sizeable roster of protagonists.

Your actions determine the story's direction — and the fate of its characters — with the remake promising a particularly grand retelling thanks to its fully 3D visuals.

The remake also features a reworked turn-based battle system, difficulty settings, and a freshly arranged soundtrack. It certainly doesn't have the budget of Persona 4's, er, revival, but it's still packed with classic JRPG potential.
