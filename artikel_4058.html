Talking Point: How Often Are You Willing To Play Games At 30FPS?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

While GTA 6 has been receiving unbelievable praise and is technically considered one of the best video games of all time according to Metacritic, that certainly doesn't mean it's immune to criticism.

One of the big talking points both before and after launch has been that it's a 30FPS experience across all Xbox and PlayStation consoles, and if you're going from 60FPS back to 30FPS, there's definitely an adjustment period. Some people are absolutely fine with that, while others are finding it extremely difficult to go back.

For what it's worth, our friends at Digital Foundry have been analysing this in depth today:

Feature Why Isn't Grand Theft Auto 6 60fps?

Digital FoundryAnd could frame generation be the solution?

Rockstar Games has already confirmed recently that even the PS5 Pro doesn't come close to running GTA 6 at 60FPS due to the system's processor not being powerful enough, so we might be forced to wait until the next generation of consoles (and PC) before the game will become playable at 60 frames per-second.

"The PS5 Pro features an upgraded graphics processor and upscaling technology that we leverage to increase the game’s visual fidelity. While the PS5 Pro’s central processor is slightly better than the PS5 base console, it is not powerful enough to run the game’s complex simulation at higher frame rates.”

With all of this in mind, we're interested to hear what you think about playing games at 30FPS on Xbox, PlayStation or anywhere else. It's becoming much more common that 60FPS is the default these days, but there are still some phenomenal 30FPS-only experiences out there, and it's a shame to think people are missing out on them.

I personally much prefer playing at 60FPS if I can, and there are certain games I will avoid at 30FPS until they get a patch down the line (like Mafia: Definitive Edition), but when it comes to industry-defining masterpieces like Red Dead Redemption 2 and Grand Theft Auto VI, the last thing I'd want is for them to scale back the detail in order to squeeze in a 60FPS mode.
