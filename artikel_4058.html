Bonus PS Plus Premium Game Is Out Now for PS5

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

You have got to wait another couple of weeks to gain access to the official set of new PS Plus Premium games for this month, but in the meantime, there's been a bonus addition to the service.

Not announced or highlighted by Sony or publisher Deep Silver, the new PS5 version of Metro Redux — which improves and enhances Metro 2033 and Metro: Last Light — has been added to PS Plus Premium. The old PS4 version was on there already, but now the listing has been updated to support the PS5 edition too.

This new PS5 version brings significant improvements over the original PS4 bundle of the two intense shooters, so they are worth revisiting again if you haven't already. Here's what to expect on PS5:

The third game in the series, Metro Exodus, was added to PS Plus Extra a few months ago, so now the entire franchise is playable natively on PS5 through Sony's subscription service. Now would also be a great time to catch up, with Metro 2039 out in February 2027.
