Price roars to Grand Slam of Darts title to end four-year major drought

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Gerwyn Price defeated Gian van Veen to win Grand Slam of Darts for fourth time; it is Price's first major title in four years; it moves him up to world No 3 above Luke Humphries

Gerwyn Price picked up his fourth Grand Slam of Darts title with a dominant 16-11 victory over Gian van Veen in Wolverhampton.

The win continues what has been a brilliant 2026 for Price. The Grand Slam marked his fourth major final of the year after making it to the World Grand Prix, World Matchplay and World Series Finals deciders, but this was his first success.

Price also maintained his perfect record in finals at this tournament, adding to his 2018, 2019 and 2021 triumphs.

It is Price's first major victory since 2022. The win also moves him up to world No 3, overtaking Luke Humphries, with Van Veen world No 2.

With his eighth major win, Price also moves alongside Gary Anderson and Peter Wright in terms of majors won.

Price will now be hoping to continue a four-year-long trend where the winner of the Grand Slam goes on to win the World Darts Championship.

"It's amazing. I've had some close calls this year where I probably should have won games and tournaments, but when it comes along, it's nice," Price told Sky Sports.

"I was probably more nervous in this game than ever. I've lost so many finals and just to keep the media off my back. Now they can stop talking about it and I can go to Minehead next week with a major, so they can leave me alone.

"The sweet thing about this is the last couple of years, whoever won has gone on to win the World [Championship], so...

"If that happens, then I'll take six months out!"

With the Wolverhampton crowd at fever pitch, they were treated to a to-and-fro opening session. It looked like it was all going to go the way of Price when he powered in a sensational 150 checkout for a second break of throw, but Van Veen held his nerve and broke straight back to go into the first break just 3-2 behind.

Van Veen thought he was on for a two-leg cushion in the second session after breaking Price's throw in the ninth leg but, in typical 'Iceman' fashion, Price fired straight back with a 145 checkout on tops to level things at 5-5.

The standard was high and there was nothing separating them.

While both players were finding some clutch doubling, neither could initially find a strong spell to run away. Van Veen had the first real opportunity to break the throw in the 15th leg but, with both finding trouble down on D2, Price eventually took it to hold and lead 8-7.

Rattling off the next five legs, Price found three breaks of throw and a clutch tops-tops checkout for 100 that left Van Veen without any answers.

In no time, he was three legs away from glory and six legs ahead of Van Veen, his 102.95 average compared to Van Veen's 83.71 for the third session the key statistic.

Van Veen managed to stop the rot with a break of throw but just as he thought he would be able to make it back-to-back breaks, Price took out a clutch 132 checkout on the bull to move 14-9 ahead.

Although Van Veen continued to hold his throw, that was not going to be enough.

The moment arrived at 15-11 and Price decided to win the Grand Slam in some style. With a 118 checkout on tops, his fifth ton-plus finish of the match, Price's crowning moment arrived.

Runner-up Gian van Veen speaking to Sky Sports:

"It has been a very positive week but I am very disappointed. It was a close game and he took out a lot of good finishes. I missed chances.

"I think 16-11 is a flattering scoreline but that's what Gezzy does. He always puts everyone under pressure. He deserved to win."

Sky Sports' Dan Dawson on Price's Grand Slam win:

"Price has had to battle to this title and I think he's had to battle to win this Grand Slam more than he has in any other since his first, because we've seen him play unbelievable stuff to win Grand Slam titles.

"But I think that's what he's had to do to become a winner again, and seeing him smile and seeing that pressure lift, just hearing him talk, I think that is an enormous sign because he's already looking ahead. What can I win next?

"We know that at Ally Pally he is up into No 3 in the world, with Luke Humphries and Luke Littler on the same side of the draw provisionally.

"That game there between Gian and Gerwyn Price, that would provisionally be the semi-final in the other half of the draw.

"So that has done him a favour if he is looking to become a world champion again."
