Mercedes set to run ADUO engine upgrade in Las Vegas with one driver

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Silver Arrows expecting to run ADUO update on Kimi Antonelli's car only as use of another turbo for George Russell would trigger fresh grid drop; watch the Las Vegas GP this weekend on Sky Sports F1, with qualifying at 4am on Saturday and the race at 4am on Sunday

World champions Mercedes will bring their ADUO turbocharger upgrade to this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix, although George Russell will not use it to avoid having to take another grid penalty in his bid for second place in the Drivers' Championship.

Mercedes in June were granted one chance to upgrade their power unit this season after being found to be between two and four per cent behind the best-performing internal combustion engine - which was unexpectedly judged to be Red Bull's - by the FIA in the year's first ADUO (Additional Development and Upgrade Opportunities) assessment window.

While rivals Ferrari, Audi and Honda, who were all found to be more than four per cent behind Red Bull's output and granted two upgrade opportunities, have all updated their respective power units at least once since, Mercedes had continued to keep their sole update chance in reserve.

But that ADUO upgrade - which Mercedes have focused on their power unit's turbo - is now ready to use in Las Vegas, although the team have played down the significance of its likely performance.

Given that expectation, Mercedes say that only world champion Kimi Antonelli is expected to use it given the Italian, who clinched the drivers' title two races ago in Mexico, remains one turbo within his penalty-free allowance of the season.

Russell has already used five turbos, one more than is permitted, due to unreliability earlier in the year and a sixth would trigger a further five-place grid penalty. The Briton, who dropped five places on the Sao Paulo Grand Prix grid due to an engine change, is in a five-way battle to finish runner-up to Antonelli in the standings with three races to go.

"We will have the ADUO enabled turbo specification available from this weekend," confirmed team principal Toto Wolff in Mercedes' pre-race preview.

"This is primarily about proving out the development in race-weekend conditions, with the expected performance benefit relatively small.

"We anticipate running it with Kimi, whilst with George our priority is protecting his grid position with the performance benefit likely too limited to justify taking a grid penalty."

As Wolff said after Mercedes wrapped up the Constructors' Championship last time out in Brazil to go with Antonelli's drivers' crown from the week before in Mexico City, helping Russell finish second in the standings is now a priority for the team over the season's final three events.

Russell is currently third in the standings, 17 points behind Red Bull's Max Verstappen and only one point ahead of Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton. Charles Leclerc, Hamilton's Ferrari team-mate, and McLaren's Lando Norris are also not out of the running for second place yet either.

Wolff added: "Winning both world championships was an incredible achievement and we have taken the time to celebrate that.

"There is still plenty we want to achieve this season though, and now it is back to work. Our targets are clear: helping George finish as high as possible in the Drivers' Championship, ending the year with momentum, and continuing to unlock more performance from the car.

"There is more work to do on that front, and the final three races give us three more opportunities to learn and improve."

Mercedes have won 12 races this season but only one of those has come in the last five rounds with the challenge having grown during the year from Ferrari, Red Bull and McLaren.

The Silver Arrows were the dominant winners of the second Las Vegas GP in 2024 and Wolff admitted: "We know what it feels like to win there and we would certainly like to experience that again."
