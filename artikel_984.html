If someone would've said to me I'd be doing this as a job when I was 12, I wouldn't have believed them.

My name's Jacob. I'm a content creator, and I work with Bolton Wanderers.

My role here at Bolton is coming up with new ideas for social media, to try new concepts, filming stuff, and then posting them onto Bolton's pages and trying to help people follow the club. In my opinion, I think it's one of the funnest jobs in football, and it's brilliant.

My passion for football when I was growing up started at a very young age. My dad used to take me to the football matches, and I got my very first season ticket when I was five years old in the 2010/11 season, when Bolton was in the Premiership. Being something I loved as a kid, to turn it into my job is amazing.

I work here at least two days a week, obviously filming content, producing content,and then on match day, I'll be filming mostly with fans, and I love it.

Some of the skills I use, I think one of them's got to be communication. You've got to be able to talk to people, talk to fans, and if you're watching this and you're a bit shy, or you think you can't communicate with people as well, I was like that inhigh school, so you learn it as you get older. But working in football, you've got to be adaptable because one minute you're doing a new signing, next minute you're working with the players, next minute it's a match day, next minute the season's over.

So when I was 16 in high school, that's where my journey started. I did videos, which got me a number of followers. I always knew I had a passion for creative pieces of work and being able to now do it as a job, it suits me down to the ground.

I started doing some videos here at Bolton with the fans and that, and then it turned into going to the training ground and filming with the players, working with the commercial team, retail team, all the different departmentsaround the football club to eventually getting a paid role in football.

Making videos with fans and players at his local football club is all in a day's work for Jacob.

He's a content creator for Bolton Wanderers FC, coming up with new ideas for social media, filming videos and posting them onto official pages. The aim is to encourage people to follow the club.

There are many different ways to develop a career in football content creation, from apprenticeships to freelance work and full-time employment. It isn't just about making videos - content creators need to understanding their audience, respond to analytics and adapt material for different platforms

Jacob works as a content creator for Bolton Wanderers and his career progression has followed this path:

Voluntary work at Bolton --> Professional content creator

While it might look simple, he's had to be resilient - and one of the biggest obstacles he faced was convincing Bolton to create his role. "Don't wait for an opportunity to come to you," he says. "If you're good at it, it does then sometimes lead into getting a role. If it doesn't, you've created a portfolio and it's really good for your CV and future opportunities."
