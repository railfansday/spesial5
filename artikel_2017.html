Oliver Glasner 'owes' Nottingham Forest 'data team' after Liam Delap revelation - Expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Nottingham Forest manager Oliver Glasner "owes" the club's data analysts for urging him to persist with struggling striker Liam Delap in recent weeks.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who has compared the Reds' record buy to the Toffees' frontman Thierno Barry.

Delap had been underperforming in front of goal compared to his xG, prior to Forest's recent win over Manchester City when he netted twice.

Wyness believes that the data on the 23-year-old shows that he had been getting in the right positions, and it was only a matter of time before he began to find the back of the net.

Glasner has opted to stick with Delap in the starting XI in recent weeks, despite him facing criticism from the fans at times.

Wyness believes that Forest's analytical department will have been in his ear about Delap coming good, and they have now been proven right.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that there are clear parallels between Delap's finishing skills and those of Barry at Everton.

The Frenchman has missed more big chances than any other Premier League player in 2026-27, but has already surpassed his tally of eight goals in all competitions from last term.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how Delap has been in a similar situation.

"Well, Glasner's gone from a dead man walking to almost a messiah, which is quite some move. Now let's just think about it though, there's a parallel here with Liam Delap and Thierno Barry," he said.

"Forest and Everton are both way behind on their xG, and it was taking the strikers a chance to finish, but we kept saying that if they're in the right position, that's the main thing.

"It's getting in those positions which is the key. Now, Delap is starting to turn that round, as is Barry.

"Delap, if you looked at the data, was definitely underperforming compared to where he should have been and maybe, you know, Glasner owes all those data guys a case of wine because it's proven to be right."

Former Manchester City financial advisor Stefan Borson told Football Insider last week that he feels Delap is absolutely worth the £50million that Forest paid to sign him this summer.

Despite it feeling like a "crazy" argument, the football finance expert pointed towards the shortage of top-level strikers in the market as evidence that Forest got a good deal.

Borson believes that Delap was undoubtedly worth £50m when he left Ipswich back in 2025, and only his poor stint at Chelsea has changed the perception of him.

He still believes that the youngster can come good at the City Ground, and develop into a top-level frontman.
