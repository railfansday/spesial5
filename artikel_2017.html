Sources: Celtic's updated stance on Martin O'Neill revealed

Celtic are still planning for Martin O'Neill to see out the season despite recent disappointing results, sources have told Football Insider.

The 74-year-old is under fire as a result of a string of inconsistent displays at Parkhead, with serious questions asked over his position following last week's defeat against Falkirk.

Since then, a 3-2 win against Besiktas secured the Hoops their first Europa League points of the season, and the board are hoping that result will kickstart a ressurgence.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Celtic remain firmly behind O'Neill and hope he will see out the remainder of his contract.

Regardless of how their results go this season, the manager is expected to leave next summer, having penned a one-year deal to stay on following his initial interim spell.

That will then allow Celtic to take their time and bring in the ideal replacement, rather than being forced into a decision by pulling the trigger mid-way through the season.

Football Insider warned on 6 November that O'Neill's job is on a knife's edge as things stand and dropping off of top spot in the Premiership could see him sacked.

However, while they remain on high alert, Celtic are hoping the boss will turn their form around so they are not forced into a decision, with their stance viewed as optimistic.

Idaelly, Celtic would not be forced to pull the trigger any time soon, and they are hoping a return to form in the league should place them in a strong position to earn success.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Martin O'Neill is well aware that the pressure is building on him and the team because of their disappointing run of form.

"But he has still got credit in the bank after he stepped in at a real difficult point on two occastions last season and somehow led them to win the league so late on as well.

"So he has still got plenty of credit in the bank for that, O'Neill, and Celtic are hopeful he can turn things around in the near future.

"He's an experienced manager so he will know that if results don't improve that there will be serious question marks over his long-term future.

"But for now, Celtic are optimistic that they can get back to winning ways and make a success of what has been a difficult season so far."

Despite Celtic's current plan to stick by O'Neill, they are putting contingency plans in place in case they are forced to pull the tirgger earlier than they had hoped.

A number of potential replacements have already been assessed, and while there is a belief a chance will be made at the end of the season, they could make a change soon instead.

Football Insider revealed on 6 November that Celtic have set their sights on Kieran McKenna as their first-choice option to become the next Hoops manager.

McKenna would be available to step straight into the role, having left his position at Ipwich Town following their promotion in the summer, and is high on Celtic's list.

However, any approach could be complicated by the £8million compensation fee which would be due to Ipswich if Celtic were to appoint McKenna now.
