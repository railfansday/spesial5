How Airlines Can Own the Whole Trip in the AI Era

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Damien Pfirsch, Chief Commercial Officer, Agoda

Moderated by Jeremy Kressmann, Vice President, Skift Studio

Airlines have an opportunity to extend their customer relationships beyond flights as AI agents reshape travel planning and booking. The strategic choice is how to combine airline-owned interfaces, external AI platforms, and specialist partners to deliver more of the journey while maintaining a direct connection with travelers.
