Here’s Your Complete Release Schedule for <i><em>The Rings of Power</em></i> Season 3

Because we know your time is ... precious.

One does not simply walk into Mordor, but it is rather easy to binge a new season. The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power is back on Prime Video for season 3 to continue the Second Age of Middle-earth. The Rings have been forged, yet there is still one more ring to rule them all. But how can anyone from the realms of men keep up with Rings of Power?

In The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power season 3, Sauron (Charlie Vickers) has finally dropped his facade. No longer hiding behind fake names like Halbrand or Annatar, he is truly himself: Sauron, the Dark Lord. And the Dark Lord now races to forge the fabled One Ring from his seat in Barad-dûr.

Meanwhile, as his orc armies eclipse Middle-earth, his war rages with the Elves—concentrated in Lindon and Rivendell—who begin an alliance with Dwarves and men. And a few wizards thrown in for good measure.

Four years since it premiered on Prime Video, The Rings of Power is officially at the halfway mark of its predetermined five-season run. While it’s still hard for the show to escape the shadow of Peter Jackson’s immortal trilogy, Rings of Power is still a handsome effort at probing all that went down before a Hobbit picked up a ring in a cave.

You don’t need Palantir to know when The Rings of Power drops new episodes on Prime Video. Here’s how and when you can watch new episodes of the Lord of the Rings series.

The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power season 3 premiered its first four episodes on November 11, exclusively on Prime Video. They are available to watch right now. The rest of the season will roll out weekly in batches, with two episodes per week until November 25. Which is awesome! Once you’ve seen every episode, you can roll right into your annual post-Thanksgiving trilogy rewatch.

As a quick note: With Prime Video originals, there’s a favorable chance you can watch new episodes a few hours earlier. Most of the platform’s shows actually drop new episodes the night before. That’s what happened with The Rings of Power season 3, as the first few episodes were made available to stream on November 10 at 8:00 p.m. ET even though Prime Video promoted November 11 as the official date. So if you’re really impatient, check for new episodes the night before.
