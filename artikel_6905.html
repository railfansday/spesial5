Kendall Jenner and Jacob Elordi Are Raising a Dog Together

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Just months into dating, Kendall Jenner and Jacob Elordi made a huge commitment: They’re now raising a puppy together. People learned that the couple recently got a Dalmatian.

“They are both huge dog lovers; it’s something they’ve bonded over,” a source close to the pair told the outlet. “They’re both already in love with their new puppy.”

Jenner and Elordi were first romantically linked this spring. In April, a source told People that they had actually been hanging out privately for “the last couple months.”

The two traveled together this past summer, visiting Hawaii, Japan, and Elordi’s native Australia. They were even seen walking a dog in Byron Beach months before they acquired a pet of their own.

In late July, a source told Entertainment Tonight that Jenner was falling for the Australian actor. “Kendall has been telling her closest friends that she’s in love with Jacob,” the insider said. “She feels like Jacob is everything she’s been looking for, and the past few months have felt like something out of a movie. She’s happier than she’s been in a long time. He brings out a side of her that even surprises her.”

Elordi had really impressed her, the source said: “With him, everything feels different. He’s thoughtful in all the little ways. He’s always carrying her bag, opening doors, and making sure she feels taken care of.”
