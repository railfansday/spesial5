Collibra: VP, Product Marketing & Executive Thought Leadership

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

At Collibra, we believe the next decade of enterprise value will be decided by whether AI can be trusted to act. Agents are moving from demos to decisions, and the leaders deploying them need certainty at the speed of AI. It's why we built the Enterprise AI Control Plane, over eighteen years of ontology engineering, to govern context and ensure control across any data, any model, any agent. We are builders who come from a wide range of backgrounds to do what has never been done before.

We are looking for a VP, Product Marketing & Executive Thought Leadership to own the narrative for Collibra's business, and to lead the team that takes it to market. This is a category-defining role at a defining moment: the vocabulary of AI context is being written right now, and we intend to author it.

You will make our CEO the recognized voice of the category, on every stage and in every byline that matters. You will build the joint story with the platforms the world runs AI on — Snowflake, Databricks, AWS, Google Cloud, SAP. And you will grow and coach a team of product marketers into a multi-layer org that ships market-moving launches without you in the room. You manage managers: directors own the day-to-day launches; you own the bar, the narrative, and the few things that cannot be delegated. This is a 0→1 category role inside an 18-year-old company — you'll define how a new market is positioned, messaged, and brought to the world.

You are a builder of leaders, not a soloist. You've run a PMM org through managers and you know the difference between setting a standard and taking back the pen — you coach the launch from spec-sheet to stake, and you let your directors own it. You move fluently between a whiteboard with engineers and a green room with a CEO, translating architecture into a story technical and business leaders both lean toward. You've moved a category before, ideally from the challenger's seat, not the incumbent's. You have a passion for proof — every number you ship traces to a source — and an unapologetic focus on customer truth over internal belief. You thrive where the assets are good but the narrative and the discipline are the gap, and you'd rather open a new frontier than chase a competitor into theirs.

Collibra is building the Enterprise AI Control Plane — the layer that governs context and ensures control so enterprises can deploy AI with certainty. Eighteen years of ontology and semantic engineering sit underneath it. We are at the center of the most important shift in enterprise software: the move from data governance to governing the context and agents that act on it. We are an entrepreneurial company with a founder who wants to be the author of this category, and a marketing team rebuilding around the AI growth motion to make that real.
