Marvel Has Successfully Recast Tom Holland's Spider-Man For The MCU's Multiverse

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Marvel Studios has a new Spider-Man for its multiverse releases, with Tom Holland having been recast as Peter Parker for certain projects, and it has worked out pretty seamlessly for the comic book giant despite the actor's popularity in the role. Spider-Man was always one of the world's most popular heroes, with every major Spider-Man movie to date being successful.

That said, there is no denying that Holland's Spider-Man franchise has put the character on a new shelf. After all, earlier this year, Spider-Man: Brand New Day became the 3rd highest-grossing movie of all time, earning over $2.5 billion worldwide, a number that will rise when its re-release starts playing in theaters on December 4. The Marvel movie's theatrical return will feature an Avengers: Doomsday scene.

All this success comes down to audiences loving what Holland has brought to the table as Spider-Man and his MCU journey from a newcomer in the middle of a superhero civil war to a young, but experienced hero who carries a lot of trauma, pushing through the pain and inspiring others, such as Sadie Sink's Jean Grey and Jon Bernthal's Punisher. Holland's Spider-Man is an icon.

As such, with Avengers: Secret Wars expected to reboot the MCU in some form, Holland is most likely to stay on as Spider-Man, even though other characters should be recast. Marvel is not insane to replace the one who is arguably its most popular hero. That said, the MCU's multiverse slate of projects has already recast Holland's Spider-Man, and I have to say that it worked out great for them.

While Holland has starred in four Spider-Man movies and other MCU projects, the actor has never given life to Peter Parker on the small screen. Fans have been asking for Spidey to show up in Daredevil: Born Again for years, and even though several MCU actors have voiced their characters across Marvel Studios' animated projects, Holland did not return as the wall-crawler in them. All of this likely boils down to the fact that Sony owns Spider-Man's movie rights, while Marvel can do as it pleases with animation, and the gray areas in which Holland could play Spidey are probably too complicated to sort out.

Well, this contractual dilemma has led to Marvel recasting Spider-Man, with the same actor voicing Peter Parker in three animated TV shows on Disney+. Hudson Thames started his MCU multiverse journey as Spider-Man's voice in What If…?, with the actor returning for that show's zombie-themed spin-off, Marvel Zombies, as well as a Spider-Man series created by Marvel Studios, Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man. While one might say that Thames' Spidey is part of the multiverse, not directly connected to the MCU, which is correct, it is clear that he is essentially Holland's Spider-Man, but on TV and without contractual issues.

Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man is pretty much an alternate universe that is close enough to the MCU, while also forging a new path for itself. In the animated Marvel series, Thames' Spider-Man is mentored by Colman Domingo's Norman Osborn, rather than Iron Man, and that event sets Peter up for an unpredictable chain of events, though the superhero Civil War and other MCU elements also play out in the series. Scorpion looks like Michael Mando, Charlie Cox voices Daredevil in the show, and above everything else, Peter Parker is, personality-wise, the same as Holland's version. Even Aunt May is pretty much the same.

Thames brings that same boyish charm and charisma, as well as the quip machine Holland made his version of Spider-Man, but also all the emotional depth the character needs. It is a masterclass in how to bring Spider-Man into animation with all of his core traits, while forging a new path for him in relation to the adventures Holland has in the MCU, be that through having Harry Osborn as his best friend, surviving the zombie apocalypse, or much more throughout those three animated shows in the MCU multiverse. Marvel Studios has left Spider-Man in good hands in all mediums.
