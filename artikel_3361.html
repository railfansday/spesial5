Disney’s ‘Rings of Power’ Replacement Is Officially a No. 1 Streaming Hit

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Fantasy franchises excel because they transport their audiences into realms that seem impossible. Arguably the cream of the crop, J.R.R. Tolkien built out The Lord of the Rings and the realms of Middle-earth, Valinor, and beyond. Prime Video's mega-bucks series, The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power, now into its third season, and only just renewed for a fourth season, has been the go-to fantasy show in recent weeks. As the darkness deepens across Middle-earth on Prime Video, the Greek gods seem to have their own thing going on a different streaming service.

In the past week, Disney+ returned once more to the fantasy genre conversation with the return of its most beloved series. The third season of Percy Jackson and the Olympians premiered with a perfect 100% score on Rotten Tomatoes, reflecting critical approval. The show adapts Rick Riordan's popular fantasy novels based on the Olympian gods, and it follows the titular hero, Percy, played by Walker Scobell. While Riordan's novels have been adapted previously, The Lightning Thief and The Sea of Monsters, to be exact, Season 3 marks the first time the author's third book, The Titan's Curse, is making it on screen.

Created by Riordan and Jonathan E. Steinberg, Percy Jackson and the Olympians has become one of Disney+'s most acclaimed fantasy shows. Now, according to data obtained via FlixPatrol, the dominion of Olympus has been to spread wide as the series has become an instant streaming hit. The series is currently the most-watched show in the world on Disney+, with the top spot secured in countries like Australia, Belgium, Brazil, Denmark, France, Germany, Italy, and more. While Percy Jackson and the Olympians held the number seven spot only a couple of days ago on Disney+ in the United States, the show has since taken the top spot in recent days.

Besides Scobell, the fantasy drama also stars Aryan Simhadri, Charlie Bushnell, Nick Boraine, and Jason Mantzoukas. Season 3 saw the likes of Levi Chrisopulos and Holt McCallany join the adventure in a tale that sees Scobell's demigod Percy return to, with help from his friends, embark on a dangerous rescue effort to free his demigod friend Annabeth Chase (Leah Sava Jeffries) and the Greek goddess Artemis (Dafne Keen) from an army of Kronsos acolytes. Given the success of Percy Jackson & the Olympians, over its first two seasons and even into this third adventure, discussions have begun regarding the Greek mythology show's future, with Scobell hinting at a continuation:

"Let's say we don't get another season. I'm just going to binge all of Percy just to watch it. But since, like, I know we're coming back next season, I can't."

Percy Jackson and the Olympians streams on Disney+ and Hulu. Stay tuned to Collider for updates.
