PGA Create Lab Announces 2026 Fellows

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

EXCLUSIVE: Now in its sixth year, the Producers Guild of America‘s Create Lab has announced the producers selected for its 2026 fellowship, which runs Nov. 17-20.

The PGA Create Lab’s scripted cycle has selected nine emerging and mid-career producers from underrepresented backgrounds, representing eight scripted feature projects, including Jason Fitzroy Jeffers (Arc), Annie Huang (Bobbie and Diana), Lea Mende (Cold Blooded), Cristobal Abugaber & Felipe Torres Urso (Keel Over & Die), Selena Leoni (Nine Night), Yuki M. Ledbetter (The Panacea), Claire Brooks (Total Landscaping) and Alison Week (Wanle). The four-day program will allow the producers in active development, financing or packaging of a feature to work with a one-on-one mentor to hone their pitches, attend master classes led by experienced producers and expand their network of fellow producers. Watch on Deadline

Developed through PGA’s One Guild inclusive initiative, the One Guild Task Force Chairs Javier Chapa and Shelby Stone said in a statement, “For six years, PGA Create has provided steadfast support to up-and-coming producers with a range of personal backgrounds, work experience and innovative storytelling that are invaluable to the future of our industry and profession. We could not be more proud of the Guild’s investment in this program, the colleagues who generously share their time as speakers and mentors, and the fellows who have shared their work with us.”
