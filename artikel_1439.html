PSG’s Nuno Mendes applauds Roma: “They’ve been one of the hardest teams we played this year.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Nuno Mendes acknowledged the difficulties Paris Saint-Germain faced in their 2-2 draw against Roma at the Parc des Princes.

The Portuguese full-back, playing in the mixed zone, praised the tactical work of Gian Piero Gasperini’s team, which caused considerable difficulty for the French and European champions with intense pressing and precise organization.

“There are teams that will try to play this way in the future, and we’ll be ready. We already know what we have to do for the full 90 minutes. They caused our team a lot of problems, but I think we managed to overcome their pressure at times.”

“There were also moments when they managed to find space, win the ball back, and cause us problems. They were one of the most difficult teams to face. We have our own way of playing, and we never change. They analyzed it carefully, just as we had studied Roma, but they still managed to create a lot of problems for us. There are games where we can’t always be at our best, but we’ll keep trying.”
