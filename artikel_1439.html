Sky: Milan dealt Modric injury blow but Tomori set for return – the latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

This evening, AC Milan have been dealt some good and bad injury news ahead of their game against Olympiacos.

It’s been a particularly unfortunate season for Milan where injuries are involved, and today, they have suffered a new problem in midfield, with Luka Modric out of this week’s crucial game in the Europa League.

Read SempreMilan ad-free and get access to exclusive news. Click here for a free trial!

MilanNews relays a report from Sky, and it’s best to start with the bad news: Modric has suffered ‘a muscle strain in his right rectus femoris’. The Croatian will definitely miss this week’s game against the Greek outfit and the game against Cagliari is in doubt.

However, he should be back for the game against Parma. It offers a big chance for Ardon Jashari to potentially step up, especially after his goal at the weekend. Nevertheless, it brings up questions about who is fit to actually replace the Croatian in the squad.

It’s not all bad news, though. The report also confirms that Fikayo Tomori will be back in full training tomorrow, alongside the group. Amorim had hoped to have him back for the win against Frosinone, so whether he’ll play on Thursday remains to be seen.

Nevertheless, it’s a welcome boost, especially with the news of Pavlovic’s new check-up. Of course, with that check-up, he’s expected to miss Thursday’s game as well.
