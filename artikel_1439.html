You're Not Nineteenth Forever! Sacked Man United manager Michael Carrick looks relieved to be out of the relegation fight as he watches The Courteeners at gig in London

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Published: 04:29 EST, 16 November 2026 | Updated: 05:44 EST, 18 November 2026

Former Man United boss Michael Carrick was seen enjoying some time out of the spotlight as he watched The Courteeners at a gig in London over the weekend.

Carrick, 45, was relieved of his duties as United boss on Thursday following a dismal start to the season that has seen his former side pick up just eight points and one victory in ten matches.

That came despite his incredible impact in the second half of last season when he replaced Ruben Amorim and led the Red Devils to third place in the Premier League.

And on Saturday night, a few days after his tenure ended, Carrick attended a tour date of The Courteeners.

According to the band's schedule, they were performing at Alexandra Palace in London.

One fan shared a photo on X from the gig, with the caption 'One hell of a day out'.

Michael Carrick was spotted watching The Courteneers at a gig in London over the weekend (Source X/adamkearey)

In the shot, Carrick looked relaxed and was smiling as he tried to make the most of a rare Saturday off.

The Courteeners were formed in Manchester and their frontman Liam Fray is known to be a lifelong United fan.

He regularly shared praise for Carrick during his time in the Old Trafford dugout, including backing him to get the best out of Marcus Rashford on his return to the club after his loan spell at Barcelona.

Some of The Courteeners' most famous songs include Are You In Love With A Notion? and Not Nineteen Forever, ironically the position United were in under Carrick until their 96th-minute equaliser against Aston Villa before the international break took them up to 18th place.

Following his sacking on Thursday after ten months in charge, Carrick issued a heartfelt statement reflecting on his tenure.

Posting on social media, he said: ‘I’ve had the immense privilege of leading Manchester United, the football club that I love.

‘There are very few who get to live that and feel it, and for that I’m incredibly grateful. I want to say a big thank-you to the supporters. I must say, you are a special, rare breed.

‘I know we’ve not had the results lately, but you’ve shown me nothing but love and support in times when the easiest thing, and what most end up doing, would be to turn the other way.

‘That’s what makes you who you are. That’s why I fell in love with this club all those years ago.’

Carrick was relieved of his duties at Man United on Thursday after a dismal start to the season

The axe ultimately fell on Carrick on Thursday, yet the writing was on the wall for several days beforehand as speculation spread and United's hierarchy worked towards an agreement with Mauricio Pochettino.

Many former United stars were hugely critical of their former club, with Gary Neville calling their treatment of Carrick 'disgusting', while Peter Schmeichel labelled it 'very disrespectful'.

Rio Ferdinand also compared United's 142-word statement announcing his sacking to 'like ChatGPT' after there was no comment added by club chiefs.

United are back in action on Sunday when they travel to Anfield to take on Liverpool in what will be Pochettino's first game in charge.
