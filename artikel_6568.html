The best men’s nose hair trimmer is Philips Norelco’s Nose Trimmer 5000 Series, according to GQ. The powerful device offers dual trimming options (for internal and peripheral nostril hairs) and also functions as a facial hair, eyebrow, and ear hair trimmer.

Whether you are using a rotary head or a linear head, nose hair trimmers are built with a protective guard, which ensures the blade doesn’t come in direct contact with the skin. The protective guard also helps you get a clean cut so the device doesn’t pull or pluck at your nose hairs. Generally speaking, nose hair trimmers do not hurt.

Not only can nose hair trimmers be washed, but it is important to disinfect them between use, first by flushing out stray hair clippings. Let them dry before storing, too, to avoid rust and bacterial proliferation between each use. It’s a good idea to clean the device again before using it, since it is going into your nose, after all.

Do you need a nose hair trimmer, and how often should you use it?

If visible nose hairs bother you, a trimmer is a simple way to keep them tidy. There’s no fixed schedule to follow – a quick trim when hairs start showing is usually all that’s needed. Concentrate on those visible hairs around the nostril opening, rather than trying to remove everything inside. Ear hair and eyebrows can be tidied as needed, using the appropriate attachment and comb.

The cleaning systems for nose hair trimmers vary. “Some nose hair trimmers are waterproof, so it’s easy to just rinse under water, if you are the sole user, and just let it air dry,” says Simone. If you share the device with somebody else, then it’s going to be all the more important that you disinfect between uses (ideally before and after each trim). “Make sure you have tapped out the hair trapped inside to avoid clogging,” adds Simone. “For a disinfectant, I would use a sterilizing alcohol pad or barber’s disinfectant, and just wipe the tool gently dry.”
