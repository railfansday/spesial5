Cranberry Apple Quinoa Salad

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

You’ll be pulling out this salad recipe all fall, and maybe even (because it’s that good) giving it a spot on your Thanksgiving spread. With the crunchiest apples and tartest cranberries, every bite is the perfect taste of fall. The slightly sweet and lemony dressing is easy to make, and you’ll want to have extra on hand to pour it over everything. Feel free to use your favorite apples for this. I like extra-crunchy, sweet, and tart ones, so I used a Granny Smith and Honeycrisp. You can also use goat cheese instead of feta or walnuts in place of the pecans. It’s easily customizable!
