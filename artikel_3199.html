Wolverine World Wide, Inc. (WWW +4.29%) reported consolidated revenue growth for the third quarter, marking its eighth consecutive period of top-line expansion. Management stated that performance was led by the Active Group, with Saucony and Merrell achieving significant growth across both wholesale and direct-to-consumer channels. The company reported that strategic pivots toward upper-funnel marketing and "key city" activations in international markets contributed to brand heat and improved search interest. While revenue for the Wolverine and Sweaty Betty brands declined, the company indicated that structural improvements in gross margin and a 26% reduction in net debt have improved the overall health of the balance sheet. Management stated it expects these improvements to provide greater capacity for brand investment and potential shareholder returns in future periods.
