HBAR Enters Falling Wedge As Hedera ETF Sees Record

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Home » Altcoins » HBAR Enters Falling Wedge As Hedera ETF Sees Record

Hedera-powered Spot ETFs pulled in the biggest daily figures since the beginning of the year. Will HBAR bounce hard?

Investors poured capital into Canary’s HBAR ETF on November 4, resulting in a net inflow of $2.73 million, according to the statistics from SoSoValue. This marks the best-performing day for Hedera-based exchange-traded funds (ETFs) since mid-January, 2026. Capital rotating at such a fast speed doesn’t occur without reason: institutions are typically buying the dip.

Pulling in millions of dollars a day is rather rare for Hedera-based stock on traditional markets: Canary Capital’s ETF product saw 10 dry days in a row back in October, while the daily value traded only exceeds $1 million on a rare occasion. This now puts the cumulative net flow at $116.66 million, pushing the ongoing-week inflows to $3.01 million.

This week’s shift hints at corporate buyers believing in Hedera Hashgraph’s (HBAR) long-term value and may be possibly looking for the local bottom. Judging from the latest HBAR price analysis from analyst Gopal, HBAR presents a cautiously-bullish case with bulls needing to defend the $0.065 support one and reclaim wedge resistance for confirmation.

The veteran analyst’s 4-hour HBAR price chart depicts the price consolidating near $0.068 support amid fading selling pressure, presenting a cautiously bullish outlook. For the falling wedge to hold weight, Hedera’s price would have to conquer the confluent resistance from $0.075 to $0.08. If bears dominate, $0.058 provides the next demand territory.

Another key hurdle is HBAR’s moving averages. Judging from the spectre provided by the Moving-Average Ribbon, HBAR’s price is presently trading well below all four thresholds. The massive cluster of moving averages is located between $0.07545 and $0.08665, while HBAR’s SuperTrend price was situated slightly above $0.08. Any close below keeps it in the red zone.

Now, Hedera Hashgraph (HBAR) has to break out of the aforementioned falling wedge by reaching $0.10, which is also a historical confirmation point for HBAR’s bulls. Going upward, momentum could take the altcoin’s bulls towards retesting the double top located at $0.20. Measuring by the Moving-Average Ribbons, roughly $0.25 becomes plausible in the long-term.
