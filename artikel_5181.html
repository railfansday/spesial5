Bitcoin eyes a cycle bottom – Could Anthropic’s IPO put BTC’s $60K at risk?

If history repeats, Bitcoin could be getting close to the bottom.

In the last two market cycles, Bitcoin [BTC] formed a bottom after roughly six consecutive weekly red candles, followed by strong rebound rallies. This time, the pattern is starting to look similar. Bitcoin has now had six red weekly candles, with its price dropping from $85k to $65k. The RSI is also showing oversold levels, adding to the case for a potential rebound.

Notably, the stakes couldn’t be higher. As the chart shows, Bitcoin’s most recent correction has hit new whales hard. STH whales amassed BTC over the last 155 days but are currently sitting on the largest amount of unrealized loss in history, amounting to almost $8 billion.

After a rally of over 51%, some big investors likely rushed into the market only to get caught up in the almost 30% BTC correction since October.

In this context, Bitcoin’s failure to stage another rebound could trigger deeper capitulation.

Notably, there are several catalysts over the coming week, such as Fed Chair Warsh’s speech, Nvidia earnings, and Anthropic’s $1.4 trillion IPO, which will cause increased volatility in risk assets. If macro FUD increases but support buying is weak, BTC could face another leg down.

Interestingly, this trend is already visible. On-chain data reveals that selling activity is picking up on both Binance and Coinbase, while Binance founder CZ has flagged Anthropic’s upcoming IPO as a potential reason behind this pressure and capital outflows from crypto. The key takeaway? This FUD is not new.

Ahead of SpaceX’s listing, Bitcoin fell roughly 19%, with losses eventually reaching 25.2% by day 18. It then traded sideways for weeks before staging a strong recovery by day 100. However, with bearish on-chain signals and increased macro uncertainty, BTC may struggle to repeat its previous recovery, putting that $60k support at greater risk.

Could a break below $60k trigger a deeper correction?

As discussed earlier, strong buying support is still missing. Weak ETF flows, a negative Coinbase Premium Index, and extreme fear are all indicative of weakening demand. On top of that, STH whales also have almost $8 billion in unrealized losses to sell ahead of Anthropic’s IPO. Without much new buying activity, Bitcoin might struggle to absorb the selling pressure, which puts the $60k support at risk.

The key takeaway? The risk does go beyond a price breakdown. If Bitcoin breaks below $60k, that could cause more than $2.6 billion in long liquidation. This can cause a chain reaction of liquidation, which will cause more downwards pressure. While such a flush may bring BTC closer to a cycle bottom, a quick rebound is far from guaranteed.

Ultimately, BTC’s ability to hold $60k will be a key test, especially with Anthropic’s IPO adding to the FUD.

However, with weak buying demand, mounting whale losses, and more than $2.6 billion in potential long liquidations, it all leaves BTC vulnerable to further selling. While oversold conditions offer some hope for a rebound, the combination of IPO-driven FUD and weak market support could make a break below $60k the start of a deeper correction rather than an immediate bottom.

Bitcoin’s oversold RSI and six red weekly candles hint at a possible bottom, but whale losses and selling pressure remain high.

Anthropic’s IPO, weak buying demand, and $2.6 billion in potential liquidations could push BTC below $60k and trigger further losses.
