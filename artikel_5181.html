Binance Founder Denies Selling as Bitcoin Breaks Below $63K

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Binance founder Changpeng (CZ) Zhao pondered the reasons behind the sharp market decline that pushed Bitcoin’s price below $63k. Though views differ, several commentators reiterated macro positions and industry security concerns.

In a social media note, CZ said Binance has not sold any crypto during this market crunch, while he personally bought some assets, including stablecoins. The bear market forced traders to reconsider their positions, with some retail investors liquidating up to 80% of their assets after whale jitters.

This was an expected reaction as the market consistently dipped across the board. CZ tried to pin the root cause on multiple factors but backed the entire market for mid-to-long-term growth.

First, the Anthropic IPO has investors rotating assets in line with the wider artificial intelligence (AI) drive. Strive CEO Matt Cole explained that Bitcoin plunged 19% during the SpaceX IPO and is now down 18% for the second-largest public listing.

Institutional traders are major determinants of investor trajectories, as seen in recent traditional finance and technology rallies. Spot ETF outflows are also behind the decline in market prices over the last 30 days.

Across seven trading sessions, spot Bitcoin ETF volume declined by $1.91 billion while BTC liquidations skyrocketed. The free fall dragged the asset to a new three-month low, negatively impacting altcoins and similar products. During bull phases, spot crypto volume is expected to peak, reflecting the impact of traditional funds in crypto.

“Hot U.S. inflation has raised expectations of another Fed rate hike. Higher yields and a stronger dollar can weigh on “riskier assets” such as crypto. Political uncertainty: Midterm results have raised concerns about potentially stricter U.S. crypto policy. No, Binance did not sell crypto. I bought crypto in the past few days, as I got some stablecoins recently,” he added.

Furthermore, CZ linked the red wave to low sentiment due to recurring cryptocurrency hacks. At the top of the list were the infamous Binance incident that drained billions and the iPhone-linked digital asset wallet exploit.

There are also fears around Q-Day, a phase when supercomputers become powerful enough to break cryptography. On the flip side, bulls like CZ still back a turnaround, driven by increased whale confidence and inflows.
