First Look: BGA Cottonwoods Xpore LT G2 Jacket and Bibs

The new Backcountry Cottonwoods kit. (Photo: Backcountry)

Utah’s Cottonwood Canyons are home to legendary backcountry lines and iconic resort runs alike, so it’s fitting that BGA Cottonwoods Xpore LT G2 Jacket and Bibs (Shop Men’s, Shop Women’s) channel that same kind of go-anywhere vibe. Outfitted with Xpore technology, this kit delivers reliable waterproof protection, breathability, and freedom of movement wherever your days—and the weather—take you. It’s comfortable and stylish enough for resort riding, with features that save you time and effort in the backcountry, making it an easy choice for anyone who wants a kit that can do it all.

The lightweight stretch fabric gives you more range of motion whether you’re skinning up the mountain or carving down it. The oversized pit zips on the jacket ditch the previous version’s mesh lining to allow for unrestricted airflow, while a ventilated storm flap allows you to cool down while keeping out snow. Meanwhile, the updated bib features a mid-chest height that gives you more flexibility with layering on the go while still protecting you from deep powder.

The pockets have also been redesigned with packs in mind, offering serious backpack-compatibility. Your pack’s hipbelt can slide comfortably beneath the jacket’s flaps to keep them secure and tucked away so you can focus on your turns. The oversized chest pockets can comfortably fit climbing skins, and the additional accessory pocket has routing for corded headphones. Other details, like the jacket’s wrist gaiters and stowable hood, as well as the bibs’ self-adhering straps for streamlined adjustability, round out the dialed design.

The no-compromise performance comes with no-compromise sustainability. Both pieces are made without intentionally added PFAS (“forever chemicals”) and use recycled, Bluesign Approved materials, so you can feel better about the gear you’re laying tracks in.
