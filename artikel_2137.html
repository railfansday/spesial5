Middlesbrough, QPR & West Brom have all dodged Bamba Dieng transfer bullet

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Middlesbrough, Queens Park Rangers, and West Bromwich Albion were all linked with Senegal international Bamba Dieng during the summer transfer window, but none ultimately pursued a deal.

The 26-year-old instead joined Serbian giants Red Star Belgrade on a free transfer, where he has enjoyed some encouraging moments in front of goal after not joining Middlesbrough, QPR, or West Brom.

However, there is a growing argument that the Championship trio might have been wise to steer clear, particularly when looking beyond Bamba Dieng's domestic scoring record and assessing how he has performed on the European stage.

Dieng has certainly found the net a reasonable number of times since arriving in Serbia, but there is a significant difference between scoring in the Serbian SuperLiga and delivering consistently in the Championship.

More importantly, his performances in the UEFA Conference League have hardly suggested he would have been a transformative signing for the likes of Middlesbrough, West Brom, or QPR this season. Boro were even in talks with Dieng at one stage of the transfer window.

All three clubs should arguably be targeting promotion and, given their size and stature, would surely fancy their chances of competing for glory in a European competition of that nature if they were playing at that level.

A high-wage arrival such as Dieng should be demonstrating genuine upside against continental opposition, rather than simply padding his statistics and goal involvements in domestic fixtures for a club the size and stature of Red Star Belgrade in Serbia's top flight.

Red Star have faced Lugano, Copenhagen, and Inter Club d'Escaldes, with that trio being opponents that Championship sides would reasonably expect to beat or at least give a very good account of themselves against.

Dieng has failed to register a goal or assist in any of those three matches. Perhaps all three English clubs had the right idea by ultimately looking elsewhere or sticking with what they had in the end. The fact he limped off the pitch assisted by medial staff in their most recent fixture underlines this, too.

Dieng began his professional career in Senegal with Diambars before earning a move to Marseille in 2021, where he scored eight goals in 40 appearances. He subsequently joined Lorient permanently in January 2023, spending time on loan at Angers before enjoying his most productive campaign yet in 2025/26.

The 24-cap Senegal international may well have made the move to the Championship over the recent summer but he has also experienced missing out on a Premier League move, having agreed to join Leeds United from Marseille in September 2022 before a dramatic deadline-day change of plans saw Nice attempt to hijack the deal (BBC Sport).

Since joining Red Star on a free transfer in August, he has scored five goals in seven Serbian league appearances. However, he has no goals or assists in three Conference League outings, totalling just over 200 minutes, against opposition of the stature and quality of Lugano, Copenhagen, and Inter Club d'Escaldes.

Dieng would not have been somebody who could comfortably arrive as an expensive substitute at any of these three clubs, meaning his signing would have carried significant consequences for their existing attacking options.

At Middlesbrough, he could have been brought in instead of Will Lankshear, which would have been a considerable mistake given the former Tottenham Hotspur youngster's extraordinary goalscoring form in the Championship.

QPR might have been tempted to install him ahead of Richard Kone, potentially stunting the development of one of their most exciting young attackers. Meanwhile, West Brom already have Jimmy-Jay Morgan performing well, with Aune Heggebo also contributing positively.

All three clubs have younger forwards worth developing rather than blocking with an expensive, high-wage alternative. Dieng might yet prove to be a success in Serbia, but the Championship trio arguably made the right decisions for their respective squads.
