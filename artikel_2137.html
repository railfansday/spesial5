Motherwell ace feeling 21 again as he embraces senior role

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Motherwell striker Callum Hendry aims to be a key player on and off the park after getting back to fitness.

Hendry made his first appearance of the season in Saturday’s 1-0 win over St Mirren following knee surgery, having come back from a four-month lay-off in April last season.

Speaking ahead of Saturday’s trip to face Dundee United, the former St Johnstone player said: “I sorted out the double hernia and then playing and training consistently probably covered the injury that I had.

“At the start of summer I started running again and I had really bad, really angry tendonitis, so it just needed fixing. I got the operation and I feel good now and hopefully I can put it behind me.

“I’m very optimistic. The more minutes I get, the fitter I’ll get, the sharper I’ll get. And I’ve seen that the team’s creating a lot of chances.”

Hendry, Paul McGinn and Oscar Priestman all returned off the bench against Saints to give the squad a lift.

“I’ve actually felt like there’s a bit of a glow around training having us back, because it’s such a tight-knit group,” the 28-year-old said. “It was just nice for us all to come back for the same game, and win it.

“The culture hasn’t changed, which I think is important, because we created such a good culture last year and we’ve kept it somehow, which is quite rare for such an overhaul of players.

“There’s a few younger lads in there, different changing room really, but still got the same feel to it. The boys are still close.

“It’s important that you stick together. They have had to stick together through some tough results the last couple of months where they haven’t really deserved them.

“I’ve had a few boys speak to me and ask me for advice, more this time than the last few seasons.

“I still feel 21, but my body tells me different sometimes. But it’s nice to be that guy because I always leaned on the older pros when I was younger.”
