QPR must take January action over 24-year-old who has not made a league appearance since August: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

QPR refined their squad with a handful of incomings and outgoings in the summer transfer window. It has yielded good results at Loftus Road, but some of those who headed out the exit door have not found the fortunes they would have hoped for.

Among those in that category is goalkeeper Joe Walsh. The 24-year-old saw a loan move away from West London sanctioned, with Pierce Charles becoming the new No. 1 while on loan from Manchester City and new signing Calum Ward locking down the spot as his backup.

Walsh was sent to League One with Wigan Athletic, but things have unravelled fairly quickly for him in the North West. And, having lost his starting berth, QPR should be recalling him when the winter transfer window comes around.

Walsh took the No. 1 shirt upon arrival at Wigan Athletic and looked poised to be their starting ‘keeper. However, a tough start to the season saw the Latics lose their first three League One games while conceding six goals in the process, and they were knocked out of the Carabao Cup too.

Then, just before the end of the transfer window, Joe Whitworth was signed from Crystal Palace. The 22-year-old has since locked down the place as Gary Caldwell‘s starting shot-stopper, leaving Walsh to watch on from the bench in every league match since.

The difference in their stats is rather glaring. It must be noted that Walsh’s numbers are from a smaller sample size, but he recorded a save rate of just 40% compared to Whitworth’s 69.8%, and the QPR loanee’s xG prevented sits at -2.03 compared to Whitworth’s impressive +4.45 (all stats via FotMob).

In truth, Whitworth’s importance in goal means it feels unlikely that Walsh will reclaim his place. He has started in the EFL Trophy games – all of which have ended in victory – but the Hoops man was on the bench for the FA Cup clash at Boreham Wood.

The purpose of Walsh’s loan move away from QPR was to find game time. However, evidence shows he is not finding the opportunities needed.

He did not play for the Hoops before joining the Latics, so would not be restricted by the two-club rule. As such, the Championship side should look to send the goalkeeper out again.

A drop to League Two could offer Walsh the starting berth he desires. Opportunities in Scotland could also prove fruitful as he bids to find the game time he needs to develop.
