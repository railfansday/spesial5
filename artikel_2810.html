Lindsay Davenport applauds Madison Keys’ broadcasting work at 2026 WTA Finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Lindsay Davenport and Madison Keys go way back. Davenport was Keys' mentor and coach during multiple stints of her playing career. Davenport remains close to Keys and her husband and current coach Bjorn Fratangelo.

Now Davenport and Keys are working side-by-side in the Tennis Channel broadcasting booth, alongside Chanda Rubin, for Tennis Channel at the 2026 WTA Finals. Davenport gushed about how well Keys was doing only three days into the gig while they were calling the Aryna Sabalenka and Mirra Andreeva match. Keys is pulling double duty working at Sky Sports and Tennis Channel in her tennis broadcasting debut.

From her stint with Jessica Pegula, Jennifer Brady, and Desirae Krawczyk on The Player's Box podcast, we have learned that Keys is articulate, funny, and has a pleasant-sounding voice.

At the WTA Finals, we see that she can break down tennis strategy in easy-to-understand nuggets for the viewer. Her insight, analysis, and personality make her an immediate media star.

We do not want Keys to retire anytime soon. The 31-year-old hopefully has plenty left in the tank and plays until at least the 2028 LA Olympics, but time will tell.

When she does hang up the racquet, a media career could certainly be in her future.

Interviewing her friend and podcast partner Pegula provided sweet moments between the two. However, she shone the brightest when addressing Andreeva as "diva," a nod to the hot mic Pegula outburst regarding Andreeva.

If Andreeva is not an upcoming The Player's Box guest, a big opportunity will be missed.
