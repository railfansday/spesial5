Jazz vs Heat Preview: The rise of Darryn Peterson is here

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The situation in Utah has become pretty dire. After their 8th consecutive loss against the Minnesota Timberwolves, the Utah Jazz will face the Miami Heat in Salt Lake City.

Utah's injury report just keeps getting worse with the addition of Keyonte George. That leaves Darryn Peterson as the Jazz's only hope to end the losing streak.

If we're being honest, this has a chance to be epic.

If you had told me a year ago that the Jazz would have gotten lucky in the lottery and came away with the player many pegged as the number one pick, I would have lost my mind with excitement. If you had also told me that the price would be the entire starting roster around him would have injury problems, I would have taken that in a heartbeat. What's exciting about Peterson? He has the talent to overcome those odds because he's that good. Peterson's last two games have been eye-opening. They've shown he has all the talent Jazz fans were excited about. Can he put it all together when all eyes are on him? The last two performances showed he has the juice to do it. We'll find out soon.

The Utah Jazz added Keyonte George to the injury report and have him as OUT. Lauri Markkanen is QUESTIONABLE.

Miami has listed Giannis Antetokounmpo as PROBABLE.
