Ravens rule out S Jaylinn Hawkins, DT Nnamdi Madubuike vs. Panthers

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Baltimore Ravens ruled out safety Jaylinn Hawkins and defensive tackle Nnamdi Madubuike.for their Week 11 matchup against the Carolina Panthers. Cornerback Marlon Humphrey will also miss the game after head coach Jesse Minter benched him over his comments about Baltimore on Thursday.

Madubuike missed every practice this week with a hamstring injury, leaving a hole in the middle of a defensive front that the Los Angeles Chargers pushed around on Monday night. His 26 pressures are second on the team behind Trey Hendrickson, per Pro Football Focus, though both have struggled to finish sacks this year.

Hawkins will miss his fifth straight game with a knee injury. He practiced for the last two weeks in a limited capacity but was absent on Friday, now raising questions about his ability to return at all. K'Von Wallace and Keondre Jackson should continue to split his snaps moving forward.

Here is Baltimore's full Week 11 injury report:

CB Marlon Humphrey (non-injury related/personal)

The Panthers ruled out linebacker Bobby Okereke, who will miss a third straight game, and safety Isaiah Simmons, a core special teamer. Tight ends Darren Waller and Tommy Tremble are both questionable after limited practices on Thursday and Friday, which could leave Bryce Young without his top two options over the middle. Jonathon Brooks, Brady Christensen and Mike Jackson practiced fully all week and are questionable as they work back from injured reserve. Here is Carolina's full injury report:

RB Jonathon Brooks (groin, designated to return from injured reserve)

OL Brady Christensen (hamstring, designated to return from injured reserve)

CB Mike Jackson (groin, designated to return from injured reserve)
