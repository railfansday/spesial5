NFL Week 11 expert picks, score predictions: Bucs vs. Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Returning to the United States with a win, the Detroit Lions look to get back into their daily routine at home against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. Despite their record, the Bucs are still alive in the playoff hunt as they're second place in the NFC South, just one game out of first. Their defense has been dominant and a big reason this team is still competing.

"This should be a big one." said Lions head coach Dan Campbell. "It's a physical team, these guys play with violence. (Bucs head coach) Todd Bowles has always done a great job. The record is not indicative of the type of team that they are. They're in the hunt for the (NFC) South. We know what we have in front of us. No excuses. We know this is number two here in this little stretch we got and our guys will be ready to play."
