Ravens QB Joe Fagnano has full participation in Tuesday's practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Lamar Jackson (right thumb) and Tyler Huntley (toe) are expected to land on injured reserve this week. That leaves undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano to make his first start Thursday night.

Fagnano, who played 40 snaps in Sunday’s loss to the Bills after Jackson and Huntley went out in the first half, has an ankle injury. But after the Ravens estimated him as a limited participant on Monday, Fagnano had full participation Tuesday while wearing a brace.

Outside linebacker Trey Hendrickson (finger/ribs) also returned to full participation as did running back Justice Hill (groin), offensive guard Vega Ioane (knee), linebacker Roquan Smith (foot) and offensive tackle Ronnie Stanley (rest/toe/ankle).

Defensive lineman Calais Campbell (rest) and safety Jaylinn Hawkins (knee) remained out of practice a second day.

Nose tackle John Jenkins (knee), offensive tackle Roger Rosengarten (ankle) and offensive guard John Simpson (back) were again limited.

Tight end Mark Andrews (hand), tight end Matthew Hibner (hand), running back Adam Randall (neck) and cornerback Nate Wiggins (stringer) were full participants.
