Fernando Mendoza in line to make first start vs. Seahawks on Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

So you want to be a quarterback in the NFL.

How about making your first start against the defending champions with a famously good defense?

That’s the situation No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza is facing for Week 10 after the Raiders agreed to trade Kirk Cousins to the Vikings for a third-round pick on Tuesday.

Having served as the backup, Mendoza is now in line to make his first start against the Seahawks on Sunday.

Though the Seahawks are currently 5-4, the team’s defense still ranks No. 4 in points allowed and No. 1 in yards allowed. The unit has allowed just 139 first downs, which ranks atop the league. And Seattle sports the league’s No. 1 pass defense.

Mendoza has made just one appearance so far as a rookie, entering the Oct. 11 loss to the Patriots once the game had gotten out of hand. He completed 6-of-8 passes for 48 yards in the contest.

The Raiders also have Aidan O’Connell on their 53-man roster at quarterback. They may be inclined to add another QB, as the club currently doesn’t have one on the practice squad.
