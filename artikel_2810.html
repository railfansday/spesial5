Pistons vs. Wizards Discussion: Game Time, TV, Odds, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Rarely have I been so interested in an immediate rematch as I am in tonight's game, which sees the Detroit Pistons taking on the Washington Wizards for the second consecutive contest. The Pistons prevailed in Monday's game 122-111. The game was also just dang weird. Anthony Davis played out of his mind, scoring 44 points in just 34 minutes. Rookie AJ Dybantsa also enjoyed the best game of his young pro career, scoring 21 and securing eight rebounds. Everyone else on the Wizards was pretty stinky.

Conversely, Cade Cunningham had one of the worst games of the season, and Detroit was really propelled by some excellent play from John Collins and Daniss Jenkins in a new-look starting lineup. Gone are the mismatched pair of Paul Reed and Collins sharing the floor. In is Collins as a center with room to operate and Jenkins as a secondary ball handler. It'll be interesting to see if they build off that success or if it was temporary. If Detroit can get both Collins and Jenkins playing at a high level and a locked-in Cade, it could be a blowout. If instead the Wizards bounce back, it could get messy.
