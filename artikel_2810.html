How to watch Warriors vs. Mavericks

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Golden State Warriors enter the final stretch of their road trip with a back-to-back beginning Wednesday night against the Dallas Mavericks. Tip-off is scheduled for 5:00 PM PT in Dallas and can be watched on NBC Sports Bay Area.

The Warriors' struggles continued Monday night as the team suffered their third consecutive defeat, losing to the New Orleans Pelicans, 115-94. Much like Friday's loss to the San Antonio Spurs, Golden State had difficulty generating any offense in the opening quarter. After Brandin Podziemski made two early free throws, the Warriors went scoreless for nearly six minutes as New Orleans rattled off a 21-0 run. Golden State trailed 35-11 at the end of the first quarter and never recovered, as slow starts have become an increasingly troubling trend in the absence of Steph Curry.

As has been the case in years past, the Warriors are once again struggling to establish an offensive identity without Curry. The team's inability to manufacture points has been a recurring theme throughout this road trip, prompting head coach Steve Kerr to acknowledge that changes to the lineup may be necessary following Monday's loss.

Specifically, the Warriors are in desperate need of players who can create their own offense, and the absence of backup guard Brandon Williams has only made that more difficult. With limited options in the backcourt, Kerr turned to LJ Cryer in the starting lineup Monday night, but the young guard struggled to get going, finishing with seven points on 2-of-10 shooting from the field. How Kerr reshuffles his rotation in search of an offensive spark will be something to keep an eye on, especially against a Dallas team featuring a budding superstar in Cooper Flagg who leads the Mavericks in points, rebounds, and assists.

Enjoy the game Dub Nation. GO WARRIORS!!!

Where: American Airlines Center — Dallas, Texas
