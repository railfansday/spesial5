📅 CBF announces dates and kick-off times for final Brasileirão rounds

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

On Friday evening (6), the CBF announced the dates and kick-off times for the final two rounds of the 2026 Brasileirão.

The penultimate round, Matchweek 37, will be played over four days.

The opening match will feature Palmeiras, who will face Chapecoense four days before the Libertadores final against Estudiantes.

In the final round, all ten matches will kick off simultaneously on the same day.

Notably, Flamengo and Vasco will play at home in stadiums just 5.3 km apart.
