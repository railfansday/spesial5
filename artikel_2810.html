3 threats that could prevent the Browns from reaching the playoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

As the Cleveland Browns return from their bye week, the team will prepare for their playoff push as the season begins to wind down. Going into the season, nobody believed that the Browns would be in this position, but they are. Thanks to a couple of teams regressing (whether it's due to injuries or overall performance), the Browns were able to take advantage.

If the season ended today, the Browns would be traveling to the Denver Broncos, a hated historic rival.

Granted, we have seen teams collapse before, and there are three threats that could derail the Browns postseason hopes.

Despite the Browns defeating the Texans two weeks ago, Houston finds themselves back in the playoff picture for now after a win on Thursday Night Football and all the other Week 11 results.

Make no mistake, the Texans have been one of the biggest disappointments in the NFL this season. Defensively, the unit has regressed despite the amount of talent the unit boasts. Offensively, quarterback CJ Stroud has been playing at a high level, but the unit has some flaws in terms of a running game.

In terms of their schedule, the only two tough games that Houston has left on their schedule are a home meeting against divisional rival, the Jacksonville Jaguars, and a trip to Lambeau to take on the Green Bay Packers. It's hard to trust the Texans right now because this team hasn't shown any signs of being trustworthy, despite the talent on the roster.

Losing a double-digit lead happens, but if it happens more than once, then it's not a coincidence. It feels as if the Texans have dug too deep a hole and they will have to rely on other teams to help them out, but the talent and recent history of success are a threat to Cleveland, until they are not.

A year after making the Super Bowl, the New England Patriots could find themselves missing the playoffs entirely. Losing quarterback Drake Maye for the season was a massive blow, but the Patriots haven't been the same team, even before Maye's injury, that caught everyone by surprise last season. A harder schedule, the lack of depth on the roster, and regression to the mean are some of the many factors for why New England hasn't been great this season.

The trade for wide receiver AJ Brown can be considered a miss at this point, but the Patriots getting blown out the past two weeks have been alarming. It's hard to envision the Patriots automatically turning the corner unless the team finds some consistency and they upgrade the QB room with the injury to Drake Maye. Not great, but Mike Vrabel's group was able to put things together last year and remain a threat, again, until they are not.

It sounds cliché, but the biggest threat to stopping the Browns from reaching the playoffs at this point is the team themselves. Considering how nobody expected this team to be in the playoff picture going into November, the fact that they are should be considered an accomplishment. However, some fans are still expecting Cleveland to give this spot away only because the fans are wired this way.

The Browns will only play two teams (three games) over .500 to finish the year (they play the Cincinnati Bengals twice and they travel to the Meadowlands to play the New York Giants). Cleveland is not in a position to overlook anyone, so it's imperative that the team stays on task and does not have any slip-ups.

It's not to say that the Browns will, but the team has to take care of business and beat the teams that they are supposed to beat because they are their own biggest threat of missing the playoffs.
