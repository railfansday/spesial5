Justin Hartley's Colter Sees Blood in 'Tracker' Case After Season 4 Changes

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Justin Hartley‘s beloved Tracker character, Colter Shaw, is once again on the case after the hit CBS series shook things up in season 4.

In Us‘ exclusive clip from the Sunday, November 15, episode of the show, Colter does a walkthrough of an apartment, saying, “Erica’s comforter is missing, the place smells like bleach and now I am looking at a missing curtain.”

He adds: “Looks like there is blood on this window that someone tried to clean up. Then it looks like someone removed a bullet from the window frame.”

According to the official synopsis, Reenie (Fiona Rene) “enlists Colter’s help to locate her mentor’s estranged daughter, and the search uncovers a dangerous conspiracy.”

The hit CBS series, which premiered in February 2024, introduced fans to survivalist Colter, who travels the country to help solve various missing persons cases. As Tracker has continued to find success, it has also gone through changes after cast exits — and a relocation to Los Angeles in season 4.

“I’m proud of what we built in Vancouver. I’m also very excited we’re bringing Tracker to L.A.,” Hartley, 49, said in a June statement. “I’m looking forward to continuing to tell these stories alongside the new, fresh places we’ll be heading to next. Most importantly, I want to thank the fans for showing up for us every step of the way. We couldn’t do this without you.”

Hartley later shared more in-depth thoughts on the change.

“In terms of tone and character, I don’t think it’ll impact the show at all,” he told The Wrap in May. “Our show is a road show. Our character goes from town to town across the United States of America and meets people from all different walks of life and helps them out. These strangers become kind of his family in a way.”

The actor expressed excitement about making the move to Los Angeles.

“To be able to shoot in a different place that gives us different landscapes, we’re able to go to places — New York, D.C., the desert, Texas, the beach,” Hartley noted. “It just opens up our world in terms of landscape, which is such a big character of our show.”

Tracker airs on CBS Sundays at 9 p.m. ET before streaming the next day on Paramount+.
