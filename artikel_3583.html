Derek Hough Addresses Criticism Over His 'Dancing With the Stars' Judging

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Derek Hough is weighing in on criticism surrounding his Dancing With the Stars judging as season 35 comes to an end.

“This season, there’s a lot going on. There’s the whole thing about me [giving out] the nines,” Hough, 41, exclusively told Us after the semifinals on Tuesday, November 17, referring to how he gave every routine the same score one week prior.

Despite the conversations online, Hough has remained unbothered.

“The reason why that doesn’t bother me at all is because I think anybody that knows me, if you’ve been to one of my shows, if you see me do what I do, if you see me host, if you see anything you see me do, it’s one thing I’ve always been,” he noted. “I consistently show up.”

The judge defended his choice to “come with 110 percent energy” at all times.

“You come to any of my shows, anybody can attest to that,” Hough added. “So I know that I give my absolute all no matter what. Do I sometimes have an off night? Sure, absolutely.”

Hough has been willing to accept feedback, adding, “I’m not exempt from imperfection. But one thing I do know about myself is, I show up. I show up no matter what and I’m a professional in what I do.”

After spending years as a DWTS pro — and winning six mirrorball trophies — Hough moved behind the judges table, offering his perspective on how the show has changed. “But I do feel like the energy around the show is a reflection of the popularity of the show, right? People are having conversations and things like that,” he said. “I would hope that moving forward, we can move toward more enjoyment and what people like about it versus what you don’t.”

He concluded: “Because I feel like what’s bad is always available. So is what’s good. And where our focus goes, our energy flows. So we want to put more energy and focus into what’s good, into each other, and into the show and into the world. There’s enough bad stuff out there. We don’t need to add more to it.”

In addition to judging on DWTS, Hough is also looking forward to the debut of his and wife Hayley Erbert‘s documentary, The Symphony of Dance, which follows the aftermath of her cranial hematoma. (She underwent emergency surgery in 2023 after a life-threatening brain bleed.)

“For us, it’s a beautiful piece of archival footage to have that just tells her story, tells her story really of resilience and overcoming something that was terrifying,” Hough noted. “But also it is a beautiful journey of returning to the stage, reclaiming a part of herself. That was incredible.”

Erbert, 32, admitted she was initially hesitant about the project. “I always say that I’m a private person living a public life, and so it’s a little interesting sharing something that’s so raw and so horrible,” she told Us. “But honestly, it was receiving messages after the fact when I’ve inspired somebody in some way, whether it was mentally, physically, emotionally. And after that, I was like, ‘You know what? If this can help one more person, then absolutely, I’m all for it.’”

Dancing With the Stars airs on ABC and Disney+ Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET.
