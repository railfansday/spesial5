The 17 Best Airbnbs in Copenhagen Embrace Hygge

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

If you’ve spent years saving photos of Copenhagen street style and Danish living rooms, the best way to really experience Scandi fashion and design is to go straight to the source—moodboards can only take you so far. Watch locals pedal past in color-blocked coats and billowy blouses, browse the racks at Hay House, and keep tabs on how many cafés have a Louis Poulsen pendant over their counter. Winter may be cold, but it’s a great time to go and see the city in action. Tivoli Gardens lights up for Christmas and the city settles into the candlelit coziness of hygge season.

A Copenhagen apartment delivers Scandinavian style up close. Think herringbone floors, tall windows, blonde wood, and a kitchen that’s set up to actually cook in. From canal-side flats in Christianshavn to a family house a short bike ride from the center, these are the Copenhagen Airbnbs at the top of our list.

The sunny striped mural in the dining room says it all: This home has personality. This 170-square-meter apartment takes up the first floor of its building and is located in one of the oldest squares in the city, once belonging to a duke. The interiors use warm ochre tones and terrazzo tiles in both bathrooms. French balcony doors open onto a quiet inner courtyard, but for evenings and nights, the square is a cozy place to be, with candlelit restaurant windows (including one right downstairs) just around the corner.

Amenities: Fully equipped kitchen, dressing room, Wi-Fi, TV, free gym access, 24/7 support

Set in an 18th-century house on Amaliegade, a block from Amalienborg Palace, this apartment has high ceilings and old-world details like wood-paneled walls and a large hearth grounding the living room. Step outside and the Marble Church, Designmuseum Danmark, and the noon changing of the guard are just a few minutes’ walk away. If you visit during the holiday season, the Nyhavn Christmas market is just down the street.

Clean lines and a restrained palette rule in this two-bedroom apartment, located right in the middle of most of the city’s big attractions. That’s not to say it doesn’t have its quirks: Look closely, and you’ll notice more than a few duck figurines (as well as a brass pretzel hanging in the kitchen). With a full cooking and dining space, you’ll feel right at home on the nights you want to stay in—and when you don’t, Copenhagen’s best restaurants are right on your doorstep.

Amenities: Washer, TV, sound system, balcony, backyard, outdoor furniture, elevator, EV charger

Though it can technically accommodate up to four with the help of the living room sofa, this spot is ideal for the couple craving solitude. It’s located in Frederiksberg, a more residential city-within-a-city known for its green space, and is itself a charming old wood house. (The fact that its surrounded by a fruit garden only amplifies its oasis-like feel.) Accented by rustic wooden furniture and a cozy fireplace, the whitewashed interiors are anything but stark.

Amenities: Wood-burning fireplace, fire pit, hammock, outdoor dining area, loungers

Somewhere between eclectic treehouse and antique treasure trove, this penthouse apartment definitely makes for a memorable stay—if only for the decor. Between the lacquered paint, bold wallpaper, and personality lighting (the fuchsia fringe lamp in the living room is of particular note), this place will soothe the maximalist traveler uninspired by Scandinavian simplicity. It has a spacious balcony if you’re visiting in the warmer months, and is well-located in the heart of the historic district; City Hall is a quick five-minute walk away.

Amenities: Washer/dryer, TV, balcony, outdoor furniture

Sunny, centrally located, and ideally sized for a couple, this newly renovated apartment is perfect for a first-time stay in Copenhagen. (The space is a five-minute walk from the metro and next to two big supermarkets.) You’ll have access to a shared garden to unwind in the evenings (if your trip is in the summer, the sun doesn’t set until 10 p.m.) and a dining table for nights in when the temperatures dip.

Amenities: Bathtub, washer, dryer, TV, dishwasher, shared backyard

Built in 1904, this light-filled apartment is full of historical touches like high ceilings and intricate crown molding. It’s a one-bedroom, but spacious enough so that you can fully stretch out: The living area is divided between a sitting room and an open kitchen-dining room. Fælledparken, the city’s largest park, is conveniently located nearby—be sure to make a pit stop at the famous Juno bakery on your way.

Amenities: TV, washer, dryer, backyard, dishwasher

For a more colorful take on Danish style, stay at this color-blocked three-room in the middle of Nørrebro. Start your day off with a tea on the terrace that’s right off the bedroom before heading out for a day of exploring: You’ll be less than a five minute walk from the lakes, where you’ll find plenty of cafés and shops to peruse.

Amenities: TV, dishwasher, private balcony, shared backyard, paid parking

The tall windows light up a calm, pared-back space with a dining table big enough for group dinners (the space sleeps to 10) and a living room with both a sofa and a daybed for extra guests. The furnishings and fixtures read classic Scandi style. Illum and Hay House are a few minutes’ walk away for shopping, and the holiday market at Højbro Plads is close enough that you can drop your bags at home between rounds of gløgg. Climb the Round Tower’s spiral ramp at dusk for a view of the city.

Amenities: Fully equipped kitchen, dedicated workspace, Wi-Fi, crib and high chair, shared courtyard, small pets allowed

A short 10-minute bike ride out of the center lands you in the Amagerbro district, in the northern (and less touristy) part of the city. With a dining table that can fit six and a large, open-concept kitchen, it's ideal for a group stay.

Amenities: TV, washer, dishwasher, room darkening shades

For a standalone house that's close to the action, opt for this three-bedroom home that’s a five-minute bike ride from Nørrebro. It’s great for longer stays (there’s a desk for remote work, too) and anyone traveling with small children will especially appreciate the backyard. There, you’ll find playhouses, swings, and even a trampoline.

Amenities: Bathtub, washer, dryer, TV, crib, pack ’n play, high chair, baby bath, playroom, outdoor playground, fireplace, dishwasher, balcony, private backyard, fire pit, outdoor kitchen, BBQ, bikes, free parking

Nestled amongst wine bars and tiny boutiques, this one-bedroom apartment is in a pretty unbeatable location. A real highlight is the kitchen: those who love to spend hours combing local markets when they travel will find plenty of space to whip up something with fresh ingredients, to be enjoyed either in the dining nook or on the balcony that overlooks the picturesque street.

Amenities: Washer, TV, BBQ, paid parking,

Situated in the old red-light district of Copenhagen—a neighborhood that now functions as a creative hub—this roomy apartment overlooks the main square of Enghave Plads. As such, the balcony provides prime seating for starting and ending each day eavesdropping on the busy streets below; though we can also envision many quieter evenings spend indoors taking advantage of the impressive library.

Amenities: Bathtub, washer, TV, outdoor playground, dishwasher, private balcony, backyard, fire pit, BBQ, paid parking

For a weeklong stay, this apartment right near the buzzing street of Jægersborggade (home to around 40 art galleries, vintage shops, and coffee shops) will feel like home. It has a balcony for your very own slice of outdoor living, as well as amenities if you’re traveling with small children.

Amenities: Piano, record player, crib, high chair, outdoor playground, dishwasher, balcony, backyard

Light and airy, this sunny apartment is quintessentially Scandinavian, right down to the wishbone dining chairs and the IKEA storage units. You’ll have access to both a balcony and a backyard during your stay, and homebodies will especially appreciate the cozy touches—from a movie projector for a DIY cinema to a wood-burning stove, this is a vacation rental that’ll feel like your own.

Amenities: Washer, TV, fireplace, dishwasher, balcony, backyard

When you look up the word “hygge” in Pinterest you may as well get a referral to this very image. With low lighting, warm wood furniture, and lots of cushy textiles, this two-floor home is the ultimate cozy stay. During the warmer months, take advantage of the courtyard; it’s a great spot to sit with a glass of wine under the leafy trees.

From the wide-plank floors to the oversized windows to the two (!!) balconies, everything about this bright apartment is charming. Bonus points for the massive green kitchen, where we can imagine kicking off slow mornings with a cup of coffee and maybe a spandauer pastry purchased at one of the many bakeries that line the street below.
