The Toyota Supra and BMW Z4 Are Officially Sold Out: Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Production for the sibling sports car pair has ended, and the last units in the U.S. have, apparently, left dealer lots.

When BMW and Toyota first joined forces to reimagine two iconic sports car nameplates—the Supra and the Z4—the collaboration was a surprise, but also turned out to be quite a success, at least in terms of the smiles both cars delivered. Six years later, Toyota and BMW are sunsetting production of the sibling sports cars, and shoppers have apparently bought out all the remaining inventory.

National inventory data analyzed by CarsDirect brought to broader attention by Motor1 indicates that all new Toyota GR Supra and BMW Z4 units have been sold. (Road & Track found a handful of new Supra and Z4 listings on Autotrader, for what it’s worth, but that info is subject to dealers remembering to update their listings.) Both manufacturers ended production of the sports cars, which were built at the Magna Steyr factory in Austria, back in March.

Despite genuine enthusiast love, neither the GR Supra nor the Z4 sold particularly well. Toyota sold 2953 GR Supras in 2025, while BMW sold 2113 units of the Z4. All told, around 31,228 GR Supra units were sold in the U.S. since 2020. Similarly, BMW sold about 15,282 units of the fifth-generation Z4 in the U.S.

BMW offered a Z4 Final Edition in December 2025 with Individual Frozen Black Metallic paint and a Moonlight Black soft top. Toyota also offered a Final Edition GR Supra in May 2026, featuring larger brakes, adjusted suspension, and matte black paint. 1300 Final Edition GR Supra units made it to the U.S.

Toyota has not officially announced plans for a sixth-generation GR Supra, though company executives said the sports car will likely return. “It would be logical that we would have a next-gen Supra. But when and how is still TBD,” senior vice president of Product Planning and Strategy for Toyota Motor North America Cooper Ericksen said to MotorTrend.

BMW, however, has no current plans to bring a sixth-generation roadster to production. It remains unclear if BMW will bring another roadster to market.
