The Toyota GR GT3 Is Heading to Its First Race This Weekend at Motegi

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Toyota supercar is making its race debut in the Super GT series in Japan.

At long last, the Toyota GR GT3—the racing version of the new GR GT supercar—is officially hitting the racetrack. Seen here in photos at Motegi Circuit outside of Tokyo, the number 32 Eneos X Prime GR GT3 is taking part in the final round of the Super GT sports car racing series in Japan.

Up until now, we’ve only seen the GR GT3 in Toyota’s all-black reveal spec. Now, we finally get to see it dressed in proper race trim, and at the track where it belongs. It has Eneos (a Japanese petroleum company) as the title sponsor, and it wears a bright and colorful orange and red livery with yellow accents aplenty. To say the design is eye-catching would be an understatement.

Rookie Racing team is fielding the car. In case you weren’t familiar, the team shares about as close ties as one car with Toyota, as it’s owned and backed by Akio Toyoda himself. Typically, the team would be fielding a Mercedes-AMG GT3 car, but it’s swapped out this GR GT3 for the double-header event at Motegi this week. It will be the first wheel-to-wheel action the GR GT3 is put through in a racing series.

As of now, we’re expecting the GR GT3 to show up in the U.S. at some point during the 2027 IMSA season; then, the road car is scheduled to launch toward the end of next year. For now, we’re left to drool over these incredible photos of the race car hitting the track for the first time. More photos and videos will surely arrive as the week turns into the weekend, so keep an eye out for updates from Rookie Racing to see more.
