Digimon Alysion now available

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Digimon Alysion, the free-to-play mobile game based on Digimon Card Game, is now available for iOS via App Store and Android via Google Play in 183 countries and regions, Bandai Card Games announced.

To celebrate surpassing 450,000 pre-registrations, all players will receive tickets that can be redeemed for game-exclusive cards, card packs, and a secret rare card, with more rewards planned.

Here is an overview of the game, via Bandai Card Games:

Digimon Alysion is finally here! Enjoy a brand-new Digimon card game world!

And that’s not all! There’s plenty of other features that make the Digimon Card Game easy and fun to play! Whether you’re a seasoned veteran or just getting started, come and experience the Digimon Card Game by playing Digimon Alysion!
