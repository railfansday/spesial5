Meteor blast scars hint at Venus's gradual volcanic makeover

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

In a radar image from NASA's Magellan spacecraft, a dark spot about 20 kilometers (12 miles) wide sits inside two bright halos on Venus (see images C and D above). The fainter outer halo spans some 180 kilometers (110 miles), yet there is no obvious crater at the center. A space rock may have created the entire pattern by exploding in the planet's thick atmosphere.
