You Can Now Download and Print GTA 6's Official Leonida Map

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

If you miss the days of unfolding a paper map before heading into a new GTA game, Rockstar has something for you. The official map of Leonida is now available to download for free, and there's a print-ready version for anyone who wants a physical copy.

Rockstar has unveiled The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection, a special bundle packed with physical merchandise celebrating the game's setting.

Rockstar added the map to its GTA 6 website on November 19, the same day the game launched. You can also download the game's cover art as a poster, so there are actually two things worth grabbing if you've got a printer handy.

Head to the official GTA 6 media page and look for the section labeled 'Leonida Map & Cover Art Poster.' Select 'View Options' to open the downloads. Rockstar has separate choices for the Leonida map and cover art, including printable versions of both.

Pick 'Leonida Map - Printable' if you want to put it on paper, then use the download button. There's a regular map download as well, which is handy if you just want to keep a copy on your phone, tablet, or second monitor while you play.

The map gives you a proper look at the shape of Leonida, from Vice City's dense roads to the waterways, coastlines, and smaller areas further inland. If you've spent the last few years comparing trailer shots and fan-made maps, being able to look at Rockstar's official version is a pretty nice change.

If you want the entire thing rather than a cropped section, choose a matching paper size or ask a print shop to scale it to fit. The print-ready download is the version to use for that.

The other download is the game's cover art poster. Rockstar has made it available in a print-ready format too, so you can hang it up instead of just using it as a desktop wallpaper.

There's a bit of GTA history behind the appeal. Physical copies of GTA 3, Vice City, San Andreas, GTA 4, and GTA 5 all came with paper maps. Even spin-offs like Liberty City Stories, Vice City Stories, and Episodes from Liberty City followed that tradition. You could spend ages looking over the streets and place names before you'd actually explored them in-game.

I've lost the map that came with my copy of GTA: Episodes from Liberty City, and I'm sure some of you have misplaced yours too. Even the maps we've managed to keep aren't necessarily in great shape. Leaving them folded inside a game case for years can damage the paper along the creases. Having an official file you can print again is a pretty good solution to that.

You can obviously save the new map and check it on another screen, but printing it is half the appeal. Having something physical to look at while you explore Leonida feels a lot closer to those older releases.

I can already see the Leonida map and GTA 6 cover art decorating plenty of fans' rooms. I'd be surprised if a fair few people didn't end up printing both.

If you'd rather have a professionally printed copy, Rockstar has another option coming. In its November 3 announcement, the company said a double-sided physical poster would be sold through the Rockstar Store shortly after launch.

That version will cost $0.99 on its own, or come free with another Rockstar Store purchase. Rockstar hasn't given an exact date for when it will go on sale, though, so don't confuse that upcoming physical item with the digital files you can download right now.

Boxed copies of GTA 6 are available, but as DualShockers previously reported, they contain download codes rather than discs. Reports on the PS5 edition also confirm that the case includes an artwork poster, not the traditional fold-out map. Whether Rockstar will ever bundle a map with a later boxed release is still unclear.

It would've been even better if Rockstar had confirmed that a physical Leonida map would come with the game, like it did with the older releases. Still, making high-resolution printable versions of both the map and cover art available for free is a good move. And unlike an old folded map, you can always print a fresh copy if yours gets damaged.

There's something nice about having an official paper map beside you while playing GTA, and now anyone who wants one can print it without having to track down a boxed copy.
