Marvel Tokon's Magik Gets Fired Up with Free New Costume for All Players

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

If you're playing Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls, you'll probably be aware of the recent DLC and updates to the superhero fighter.

Phoenix Cyclops has joined the roster as the first post-launch character, and to celebrate his arrival, developer Arc System Works has provided all players with a fun bonus.

A new costume for Magik is now available to everyone for free, and here's what it looks like:

This is based on her outfit when she became part of the Phoenix Five — a storyline from the comics in which she and four other mutants became possessed by the Phoenix.

It gives Magik some fiery red and gold attire, and her sword gets a fresh appearance too.

The costume is only available to claim for a limited time; you have until 30th November 2026 to grab it via the in-game newsfeed.

It's a neat freebie, for sure — will you be rocking this look for Magik in Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls? Tell us in the comments section below.
