Bills’ Thanksgiving win matches the best start in franchise history

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

We're 11 games into the 2026 NFL season, and the Buffalo Bills have lost just once. With their 13-10 win over the Kansas City Chiefs on Thanksgiving night, the Bills have matched the best 11-game start in franchise history.

It was ugly. It was wet. It was cold. It was supposed to be an epic showdown between two NFL MVP quarterbacks. But in the end, it was a 57-yard field goal in the rain that sailed straight and true off the foot of Tyler Bass as the clock expired.

In 67 seasons of Bills football, only three other teams have made it through their first 11 games with just one loss. If you're looking for a little reason to be optimistic about where this season could be headed, let's take a look at what those three teams went on to accomplish.

The 1964 Bills, under head coach Lou Saban, finished their regular season with a 12-2 record before defeating the San Diego Chargers, 20-7, to win the first of back-to-back AFL championships.

It would be another 24 years before Buffalo got off to another 10-1 start. In 1988, Marv Levy's Bills finished the regular season at 12-4, winning the AFC East for the first time since 1980 and making it to the AFC Championship Game, where they lost to the Cincinnati Bengals.

Three years later, the Bills were at it again. The 1991 team turned its 10-1 start into a 13-3 regular-season record and made the second of Buffalo's four consecutive Super Bowl appearances.

Now we can add Joe Brady's 2026 team to that list.

Brady has won 10 of his first 11 games as an NFL head coach. To put that into perspective, two of the more successful first-year head coaches in recent NFL history, Jim Harbaugh (2011, San Francisco 49ers) and Kevin O'Connell (2022, Minnesota Vikings), were both 9-2 through their first 11 games.

Of course, getting to 10 wins hasn't always looked the same for this year's Bills. Early in the season, we were talking about how much the offense was going to have to do to compensate for the defense. Over the past few weeks, it's been the defense that's given us some of the biggest reasons to believe this team could be playing football into February.

With six games remaining on Buffalo's regular-season schedule, the franchise record of 13 regular-season wins is well within reach. And while the Bills have won 13 games on several occasions, they've never won 14.

There's still plenty of work to be done, beginning with a trip to Foxborough next week. But on Thanksgiving night, Bills Mafia can enjoy something it hasn't experienced in 35 years — a 10-1 football team.
