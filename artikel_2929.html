Bears WR Luther Burden has cleared concussion protocol

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Bears wide receiver Luther Burden cleared concussion protocol, coach Ben Johnson said Tuesday.

Burden was concussed late in the Bears' 27-24 win over the Buccaneers on Nov. 8 after taking a hit to the head from linebacker Reuben Bain.

Burden practiced on Monday after the team's bye week and is on track to return Sunday against the Saints.

He has 49 receptions for 644 yards and four touchdowns in nine games.

Slot cornerback Kyler Gordon injured his shoulder in Week 9 and didn't practice Monday, Johnson said.

"We'll see as the week goes along," Johnson said, via Patrick Finley of the Chicago Sun-Times. "It's more a day-by-day thing for us right now."

Gordon has 13 tackles, four sacks, one pass defensed and two forced fumbles.
