Class M girls soccer: Lauralton Hall tops previously unbeaten East Hampton

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

HARTFORD – The East Hampton girls soccer players arrived at Trinity Health Stadium for the Class M state championship game Saturday, undefeated and full of hope that they would join their school's boys team, which won the first championship game in the morning, to bring two state titles back to East Hampton.

But it was not to be. The top-seeded Bellringers lost to No. 3 Lauralton Hall, 2-1 in the Class M final.

Lauralton freshman Isabella Lalvay scored the first goal with 28:43 left in the first half and Alivia Chappell had the second for the Crusaders (14-5-1), who won their first state title.

Mia DonAroma had the only goal for East Hampton (20-1) with 26:03 left in the game.

"Today we just didn't play our best game," East Hampton coach Andrea Giuliano said. "Bad day not to play your best game but at the same time, this entire season we've been building to this point."

East Hampton had dominated most of its games this season and had only fallen behind one other time, against RHAM.

"In previous seasons, we've been in that position but this season, not so much because we have had a dominating type of play this year," Giuliano said. "I think ultimately that was the catalyst that put us a step behind today. It's a tough place to be when you're not there that often. I think that's where we just couldn't catch up.

"Once we put the goal in, I thought we had a little bit of a chance but Lauralton Hall outplayed in the midfield every second basically; that's where we lost it."

Lauralton Hall had only been in the finals once before, in 2001, where the Crusaders lost to Avon.

"I was not expecting this," Chappell said. "It's been the first time we've been in the finals for 20 years or something like that.

"We're in the SCC, it's super competitive. We faced a lot of adversity; we played great teams but I have to thank them for this win because it was their challenge that helped us improve as a team and help us come out here and win."
