J.P. Morgan Asset Mgmt’s Rosenbaum on 2027 AI Spend

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Nathaniel Rosenbaum, JPMorgan’s Head of U.S. High-Grade Credit Strategy, expects another busy year of AI-related borrowing but says issuance growth could crest during 2027. High-grade supply surged nearly three and a half times this year, he said; next year, it could reach a record $2.1 trillion without repeating that jump. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
