De la Fuente has ‘nothing but pride and gratitude’ for Spain despite unbeaten run ending

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Luis de la Fuente insists he has "nothing but words of pride and gratitude" for his players as Spain's incredible 43-match unbeaten run came to an end on Sunday.

The reigning world champions were defeated 3-2 by 10-man England at the Metropolitano in the Nations League, meaning they finished second in Group A3, having already qualified for the quarter-finals due to their perfect record before this game.

Harry Kane put the visitors ahead from 12 yards out just before the break, while Ezri Konsa and Ben White gave England a healthy lead before the hour mark.

La Roja fought back after that, though, with Elliot Anderson given his marching orders for a desperate foul on Lamine Yamal, and Mikel Oyarzabal calmly converted the resulting penalty.

Nico Williams pulled another back in the 75th minute, but Spain were unable to salvage a draw, though De la Fuente could not fault his players.

"I have nothing but words of pride and gratitude for the players and to value what has been done so far," the Spain manager said.

"All that is, whoever comes after me should improve upon it. I am proud of a fantastic journey, and all that lies ahead.

"You have to know how to win and how to lose, and for the first 30 minutes we were overwhelmed.

"[England] were better; they established their dominance. Then things changed; we had some chances, and at 0-3 down, we have to acknowledge what we did.

"But there are no excuses. Their goalkeeper was crucial. That 0-3 scoreline and the first half-hour really hampered us against a very strong opponent."

Spain suffered only their second home loss across their last 41 such matches, coincidentally dating back to a 3-2 defeat to England in October 2018.

Sunday's loss also marked Spain's first in Madrid since February 1987, when they were likewise beaten by England.

Indeed, the defeat brought an end to Spain's 968-day unbeaten streak, which stretched back to a friendly defeat against Colombia in March 2024, with La Roja's 43-match run (excluding penalty shoot-outs) the longest by a European nation across all competitions.

"When matches don't start well, adverse situations accumulate," De la Fuente added. "[Marc] Pubill had an ankle problem, [Pau] Cubarsi had a muscle strain, [Martin] Zubimendi too…

"But we were able to recover, and the players gave their all.

"It's a shame we didn't at least get a draw, but I'm proud, and I insist again and again, of this fantastic group of players."
