Former Robinhood Employee Pleads Guilty To Fraud

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

United States Attorney for the Southern District of New York, Jamie McDonald, announced today that HUAISONG XIANG, a former software engineer at Robinhood Markets (“Robinhood”), pled guilty to wire fraud in connection with a scheme to misappropriate confidential business information from Robinhood, and use that information to trade perpetual futures on Hyperliquid, a decentralized trading platform.
