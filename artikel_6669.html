Ciara and Russell Wilson Help Macy’s Usher in the Holiday Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Last week, Macy’s welcomed guests to the eighth floor of its Herald Square flagship for an exclusive preview of Santaland and the reimagined holiday experience. Upon entering, a crowd of all ages was greeted by a sprawling red carpet surrounded by Christmas trees, setting the festive mood for the evening.

Co-hosted by Ciara and Russell Wilson, the celebration took place across two main spaces. In one room, holiday-inspired cocktails flowed as attendees, including Nicky Hilton Rothschild, Candace Bushnell, Tiler Peck and Roman Mejia, and Alec and Hilaria Baldwin, enjoyed light bites and a DJ set by Brendan Fallis. In the other, families stepped into the magic of Santaland, boarding the new North Star Express before visiting St. Nick himself.

Macy’s Santaland opens to the public on November 27. Ahead of the holiday season, the couple shared more with Vogue about festive dressing, gift-giving, family traditions, and what’s next.

What are you most excited for this holiday season?

Ciara: This time of year has always been so special to me. Our schedules are so busy, and I really cherish the opportunity to slow down, spend quality time with our family, decorate the house together, and see the excitement on our kids’ faces throughout the season. This year, we are especially looking forward to soaking up those special moments together and getting ready to welcome Baby Cinco.”

You’re wearing a head-to-toe Macy’s for tonight’s celebration. How would you describe your personal style during the holiday season?

Ciara: Tonight I wanted something that felt elevated, but still comfortable for running around with the kiddos. I knew Macy’s would have exactly what I was looking for. I’m wearing a Lauren by Ralph Lauren look, with a cozy cableknit tank, satin skirt, and sweater that felt perfect for this time of year.

Russell: I’m wearing Alfani. The style I always feel my best in is classic, timeless, polished, and comfortable. I love a great tailored look that also has a fun spin to it, especially during the holidays. Macy’s is the perfect shopping destination because it has such a great range of styles, I love putting together different pieces and making them feel unique to me.

With a busy career and a full house, are you someone who has all your holiday shopping done by November, or are you more of a last-minute, Christmas Eve kind of shopper?

Ciara: I’m definitely a planner and take holiday shopping very seriously! With a big family, you need the time to find something special and thoughtful for everyone. Especially with my kids, their interests are always changing, so I’m always paying attention to what they’re into and thinking about what would make them happy. I just love finding those special things that I know they’ll be excited to open on Christmas morning.

When it comes to gift-giving, what’s your signature style?

Ciara: For me, the best gifts are the ones that show how well you know someone. I’m a sentimental person, so I always think it’s more about the thought and intention behind the gift than how much you’re giving.

Russell: When it comes to giving gifts to my family, I get so excited. I love to go all out—go big or go home! I also love surprising Ci and the kids with something they aren’t expecting: Their reactions are always the best. At the same time, I always make sure gifts are thoughtful and personalized. I love giving them things that remind us of a special moment in our lives, an inside joke, or something they can cherish for years to come. So for me, it’s really a mix of everything.

When you’re hosting for the holidays, what’s your secret to throwing a memorable celebration?

Ciara: I like to plan ahead, and I actually have a lot of fun with that part of hosting. There’s always a lot that goes into putting a party together, but a good playlist is a must. You can usually count on a few of my songs making the list, especially “Level Up” or “Yes.” I also love having games and activities for everyone to enjoy. We’re big UNO fans in our family, so that’s always a fun one to bring out. Lastly, it’s not a party unless the host is having fun, so I make sure to relax and spend time with my guests.

As the year winds down, what are you most looking forward to in 2027?

Ciara: 2026 was such a special year for me with so many personal and professional achievements, so I’m really excited to continue creating and growing in 2027. This year, I released “Yes” and its remixes alongside some incredible artists I admire, so I’m excited for what’s next musically. I’m also looking forward to continuing the work we’re doing through the Why Not You Foundation and finding more ways to support communities and make a meaningful impact. And beyond all of that, just spending quality time with my family, making memories, and enjoying all the little moments. That’s always what matters most to me.

Russell: There is so much to look forward to in 2027. I’m excited to continue working with my amazing CBS Sports team while also exploring some exciting personal projects, continuing to grow the Why Not You Foundation, expanding 3BRAND, growing our family, and prioritizing our health and wellness with the help of QALO. We have some incredible projects in the works that we'll be announcing soon, so stay tuned. It’s going to be a special year!
