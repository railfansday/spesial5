<i><em>Focker In-Law</em></i>: What We Know About the Latest Installment in the <i><em>Meet the Parents</em></i> Franchise

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Ariana Grande’s latest foray into film has finally arrived. Though the project comes from Wicked’s home studio Universal, it perhaps isn’t the most obvious choice for a recent Oscar nominee and global pop icon. Nevertheless, make no mistake, Grande recognizes beloved IP when she sees it. Thus her latest news: The “we can’t be friends” singer stars in the Meet the Parents sequel Focker In-Law, which officially hits theaters today, November 25, ahead of Thanksgiving.

According to Deadline, “Grande has had her fair share of scripts come her way” in Wicked’s wake. “Ultimately,” the outlet reported, “her strong ties to the studio led her back to the lot on a project Universal is excited about.”

Last year marked the 25th anniversary of Meet the Parents, which memorably starred Ben Stiller as Greg Focker, who meets his girlfriend’s parents, Jack (Robert De Niro) and Dina Byrnes (Teri Polo), over an eventful wedding weekend on Long Island. The comedy was a box-office success and was later followed by Meet the Fockers (2004) and Little Fockers (2010), helping the franchise gross $1.13 billion worldwide, per Deadline.

On July 12, 2025, Grande shared a carousel of photos on Instagram, which included a shot taken at a table read on June 17 for the new film.

On Oct. 31, 2025, Grande celebrated wrapping the film with a carousel of behind-the-scenes photos. In the caption, she wrote, “these past few months have been so, so unimaginably special. i love my Fockers, and i love my Byrnes… so, so very much ♡ i will miss this bunch terribly. see you next november ! ♡”

The Meet the Parents sequel arrives in theaters today, November 25, almost exactly a year after the Wicked sequel, Wicked: For Good, was released.

In a November 2025 cover story with Interview, Grande shared new details about filming the sequel in New York. “I’m really enjoying this role and this cast,” she said.

“It’s with the original Meet the Parents crew, so it’s Mr. De Niro, Ben Stiller, Teri Polo, and some new additions—Skyler Gisondo and Beanie Feldstein,” she added. “Everyone is so incredible. And Blythe Danner’s here as well.”

The actress also spoke about what it has been like to work on the movie while promoting Wicked: For Good at the same time: “It’s such a special experience, and I’m really loving getting to work with these people and this character. And then we’re simultaneously doing Wicked promo on the weekends—we just recorded a concert in L.A. So it was a little bit of bouncing around.”

At the 2025 Emmy Awards on September 14, Stiller spoke with E! about Grande’s role in Focker In-Law. “What’s surprising—maybe not really surprising—is how amazing [Grande] has blended in [with the rest of the cast],” he said. “She’s such a pro. She’s so funny. She’s so talented, obviously, as a singer, but also she’s so funny and amazing...what she’s doing in this movie is kind of very unique. It’s a very unique character, and we’re having a great time, and just to go to work with her and De Niro—and Owen Wilson the other day decided to drop by, and that was fun.”

The first look at Grande in the movie arrived on April 14, 2026. In the clip, Grande is hooked up to a lie detector, administered by De Niro.

The full trailer arrived on April 15—it kicked off with Grande in character as Olivia Jones hooked up to a lie detector test and answering questions about the Focker family. “I test all the potential family members with this machine,” De Niro says in the clip.

After Stiller interrupts and asks her, “Do you think I hold Henry emotionally hostage?” She responds, “I mean, yeah. You call him ‘wee-wee.’”

The trailer also revealed some interesting background about Grande’s character. While seated at a family dinner, she tells the Fockers, “People underestimate me, but actually I used to be an FBI hostage negotiator. I pull the strings of the hostage takers with strategic emotional puppetry. That’s how I’m going to free Henry from you guys.”

The rest of the trailer sees Grande and Stiller going head-to-head, as she tries earning her place in the family.

Tickets to Focker In-Law are currently available for purchase on Fandango.

There is no confirmation that Grande will star in another Meet the Parents sequel movie after Focker In-Law, but the movie’s ending did hint at the possibility of another installment.

After Grande and Stiller’s characters make up at the end of the movie—after feuding for most of the film’s run time—she tells Stiller, “Well I guess there only one thing left to do—meet my parents!” He sarcastically responds, “Great.”
