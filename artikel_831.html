Nicola Peltz Beckham Addresses "Mean" Body Shamers

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Nicola Peltz Beckham—who married Brooklyn Peltz Beckham in 2022—chimed in after a fan hit back at body shamers commenting on her appearance in new photos.

Nicola Peltz Beckham is abs-solutely blocking out the noise.

After the Bates Model actress showed off her bare stomach in a selfie, she addressed body shamers commenting on her appearance, while also expressing her gratitude toward supportive fans.

In a Nov. 11 Instagram post, Nicola—who has been married to Brooklyn Peltz Beckham since 2022—shared photos of herself posing in a mirror wearing blue scrubs after an oxygenating cosmetic procedure, with the 31-year-old lifting up her shirt to reveal her flat stomach.

She captioned the post, “hyperbaric chic post facial.”

After a handful of commenters made critical remarks about the Last Airbender star’s looks, one came to her defense to shut down the haters.

“Stop body shaming her and other ppl,” the user wrote. “Are you all perfect?”

The comment didn’t go unnoticed by Nicola, who replied to the fan, “thank you. some people are actually so mean.”

The heartfelt exchange continued as the commenter wrote back, “your [sic] welcome sweetheart. I'm so sick of ppl and there [sic] opinions of others. I'm overweight and I struggle with it do to my health. You are beautiful. I adore you and Brooklyn.”

The Transformers: Age of Extinction star once again thanked the user for their “kind” words.

“I read them and it means a lot to me,” she added. “You’re so beautiful, you’re perfect.”

This isn’t the first time Nicola and Brooklyn have faced body shamers head-on.

Over the summer, an online user made a negative remark about her body in the comments section of one of her husband’s Instagram posts.

And David Beckham and Victoria Beckham’s eldest son didn’t hesitate to defend his wife.

“Do you comment on everyone who is concerned about your wife and how sickly she looks?” the since-deleted comment, per Us Weekly, read under Brooklyn's Aug. 25 Instagram post, which featured him baking a cottage pie. “She puts herself out there; feed her.”

In response, Brooklyn clapped back at the user, writing, “Don't ever talk about my wife. She's absolutely beautiful, and you should never comment on a woman's body.”

The 27-year-old added, “It's disgraceful."

As for Nicola, she’s previously admitted that it’s difficult not to let the unkind words get to her.

“I’m very sensitive, and I need to work on not reading everything: online news, social media comments,” she told Elle España in a March interview translated from Spanish. “I try not to pay attention to what people say, but it affects me. I need to learn to distance myself.”
