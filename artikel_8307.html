Azure Standard is interesting. Its different around the country but in my area it basically functions as a co-op but with no storefront. Someone in each area is the receiver and everything is delivered to that one person. My receiver piles all the boxes together by purchaser in her garage and then texts everyone to come and get it. Over the next few hours everyone show up and takes their pile. This lets Azure avoid 'last mile' pricing and issues.
