HoYoverse's Petit Planet Launches in December, Switch 2 Port Coming 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Daniel is a lifelong gamer with a love for various genres and gameplay styles who enjoys exploring games and playing from various angles.

In the gacha gaming scene, HoYoverse, international division of Chinese game developer miHoYo, has become one of the definitive faces, offering games like the high-grossing Genshin Impact, the stylish Zenless Zone Zero, and my personal favorite gacha game, Honkai: Star Rail. Even as new challengers constantly appear in the gacha scene, and there are a lot of them, HoYo maintains a powerful presence thanks to its games, and especially its character designs.

We were able to get some extensive time with HoYoverse's upcoming life simulator, Petit Planet, and were left grinning from ear-to-ear.

While all of its other games are merrily chugging along, HoYo is still working on even more titles to add to its catalog and appeal to an even larger variety of potential players. One such title is Petit Planet, a life sim game in a similar vein to titles like Animal Crossing. Petit Planet has run several beta tests since its initial announcement in September of last year, and it seems HoYo is finally ready to launch the proverbial rocket.

In a pre-launch special program that aired over the weekend, HoYo officially revealed Petit Planet’s global launch date as December 4, 2026. As is typical for HoYo games, it’ll be launching on PC, both via HoYo’s own HoYoPlay launcher and the Epic Games Store, and mobile devices via Android and iOS. However, in a first for HoYo, the game will also be launching on the Switch 2, albeit in 2027 after the initial launch, as announced today on a post to the game’s official X, formerly Twitter account.

Several of HoYo’s other titles, primarily Genshin Impact, Honkai: Star Rail, and Zenless Zone Zero, have been made available on gaming consoles before, with their primary affinity being the PlayStation 4 and, later, PlayStation 5, though Genshin and ZZZ specifically also made it over to the XBOX Series X/S. However, none of the developer’s titles have made it to either the Nintendo Switch or Switch 2, at least until now.

Honkai: Nexus Anima is shaping up to be a stand-out monster taming game from HoYoverse.

The post on the official Petit Planet X that announced the Switch 2 port didn’t specify when, exactly, we’ll be seeing this port beyond a broad window of 2027, but it is still an interesting addition to HoYo’s wheelhouse, and hopefully a sign that its other titles are on the way as well. Maybe we can start getting  more Star Rail crossovers…

Even if you plan on playing the game on the Switch 2, you might want to make your account ahead of time anyway, as due to the large number of pre-registrations the game received during its developmental period, there will be a swath of rewards for all players upon initial log-in. All players will receive 2 Blissful Floral & Fruit Tea, 2 Red Star Fireworks, 2 Sweet Specials, 100 Bazaar Jelly, the Sproutling Outfit, and the Bestie Star Light.

With this latest announcement, it’s shaping up to be quite the winter season for those interested in gacha games. Following Petit Planet’s launch on December 4, an entirely new gacha game, Silver Palace, will be launching a little over a week later on December 14 for PC, PS5, Android, and iOS, as that game’s developers announced a couple of days ago.

NetEase Games and Naked Rain revealed a brand-new trailer for Ananta during ONL's gamescom.

Not only that, but the following month, the long-anticipated city sandbox gacha game Ananta will be launching on January 15, 2027 for PS5, Android, iOS, and PC specifically via Steam, as opposed to Petit Planet and Silver Palace’s places on the Epic Games Store outside of dedicated clients.

It’s also worth a reminder that HoYo, in particular, still has a lot of irons in the fire even with Petit Planet officially poised for launch. The most recent test for its creature battling game, Honkai: Nexus Anima, ran this past summer, while one of its other new brand-new IPs, Nodusfall, was announced at Gamescom back in August, showing a much darker fantasy aesthetic compared to the more anime-based stylings of Genshin, ZZZ, and the Honkai games.
