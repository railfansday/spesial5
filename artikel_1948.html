Matthias Jaissle may already be losing the support of his players at Newcastle United, sources have told Football Insider.

The 38-year-old has endured a rocky spell since taking over from Eddie Howe in the summer, with the Magpies having won just three of their last ten games in all competitions.

A 1-1 draw against struggling Fulham last time out has piled the pressure on the manager's position, with frustrations growing behind the scenes at St James' Park.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, there are concerns that Jaissle may be losing the support of his players in the dressing room.

Recent reports elsewhere have suggested there is discontent behind the scenes, and if the noise coming out of the club is accurate, it could lead to the manager's exit.

Large sections of the supporters at St James' Park are already losing patience, and if the players do share those sentiments, it will become a concern for Newcastle.

Football Insider revealed on 6 November that Jaissle could become the first Premier League manager to be sacked this season amid dressing room concerns.

This comes amid comparisons with Crystal Palace's under-fire boss Pierre Sage, many people's favourite to be the first sacked, whose players have pubicly backed him.

That has not been the case for Jaissle at Newcastle, with reports elsewhere suggesting the players are asking questions of his tactics, leading to doubts over his position.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "It's going to be interesting to see what happens with Jaissle at Newcastle because things don't seem particularly settled.

"There's now sort of reports coming out of Newcastle from elsewhere, suggesting that the players aren't particularly happy and different issues behind the scenes.

"There have been suggestions that those players maybe aren't behind him or are questioning his decisions, and at the moment that is all sort of rumours and speculation.

"But it will be an interesting thing for us, to keep an eye on this and see whether the manager has the backing of all of his squad and everybody at Newcastle."

Amid rumours of potential discontent behind the scenes at St James' Park, a number of players have also come under fire for their inconsistent displays this season.

Among those is Harvey Barnes, who former Man United coach Mick Brown warned Football Insider could cost Jaissle his job if he does not step up in the coming weeks.

The 28-year-old's inconsistency is starting to become a problem, as his four goals and assists have come across just four of his 12 appearances while the Magpies' form has dipped.

Meanwhile, the club's inability to put a positive run of displays together has heaped pressure on Jaissle's position, with large sections of the fanbase now calling for his head.

Barnes' form has been representative of his teammates', ramping up the pressure on Jaissle as he struggles to turn around their recent displays and results.
