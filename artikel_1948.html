Celtic hit out as Hearts cut away ticket allocation for Tynecastle clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Celtic have expressed “surprise and disappointment” after Hearts gave the Premiership champions a reduced away allocation for their league match at Tynecastle on November 29.

The teams are due to meet in Gorgie for the second time this season, with Celtic having defeated Hearts 7-1 last month at Parkhead.

It will be the first clash between them in Edinburgh this season and in a communication to their fans, Celtic revealed that they have been given approximately 20 per cent less tickets than is usually the case.

A statement from Celtic read: “We have been informed by Heart of Midlothian FC that we will receive an allocation of 508 tickets for our forthcoming match at Tynecastle Stadium at the end of this month.

“Clearly we are surprised and disappointed at this move, which represents another cut of around 20% on our previous allocation and follows significant reductions across recent years.

“However, to be very clear, this is outwith our control and is the decision of the home club.”

Hearts later responded with a statement given to our sister title The Edinburgh Evening News, which explained that the move has been done for segregation purposes.

“As part of the security arrangements for this fixture, we have increased the segregation within the away section at Tynecastle,” a Hearts spokesperson said. “This will result in a reduction in the visiting allocation and is intended to help ensure the match can be managed safely and effectively.”

Celtic pipped Hearts to the Premiership title last season, defeating the Jambos 3-1 at Parkhead on the final day of the season.

The end of the match was marred by a pitch invasion by some Celtic supporters, with fans accosting Hearts players on the field of play. The visiting players left the stadium still in their kit, having not showered or conducting post-match interviews.

Celtic currently lead the Premiership by four points under manager Martin O’Neill, with Hearts under Wouter Vrancken in fourth place, 11 points behind but with a game in hand.
