Armstrong nets twice in Wolves win

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Wolverhampton Wanderers vs Derby County. EFL Championship.

Molineux StadiumAttendance: Attendance28,970.

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Wolverhampton Wanderers and Derby County at Molineux on Tuesday night; Adam Armstrong scores twice as Wolves sweep to victory past the Rams.

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

Adam Armstrong's brace made it six goals in six games to give dominant Wolves a 3-0 win over Derby in the Sky Bet Championship at Molineux.

Armstrong's double sandwiched Mateus Mane's first of the season as Wolves' fourth straight win stretched their unbeaten run to eight games to climb a place to second in the table.

And despite a couple of first-half scares from Derby, Wolves could have had several more goals, while Derby are now bottom after one win in 13.

Clean-through Carlton Morris had the chance to give Derby a surprise second-minute lead, but Nasser Djiga made a vital tackle.

Wolves soon started creating chances as Derby sat back.

Ladislav Krejci glanced wide from a corner before Hugo Bueno's 25-yard angled drive was deflected wide.

Mane and Fer Lopez both then side-footed straight at Derby goalkeeper Jacob Widell Zetterstrom.

A cross missed by Krejci gave Derby's Filip Bilbija a sniff of goal but Krejci desperately hacked clear before the winger could shoot, Mohammed Fuseini lobbing over the loose ball.

Wolves returned to the attack where Andre lifted a rising effort comfortably over the bar.

Derby breached Wolves' back line again only to hit the woodwork.

Widell Zetterstrom's free-kick was won by Matt Clarke and Morris' shot hit Krejci before Fuseini crashed a shot against Jose Sa's head then against the outside of the bar.

Wolves responded by taking a 29th-minute lead through Armstrong.

Mane did brilliantly to shake off Clarke on the touchline before pulling it back for Tommy Doyle, whose shot was blocked, Armstrong tucking away the loose ball.

Armstrong was inches away when he stabbed wide from Bueno's diagonal pass.

Rafiki Said missed two chances to have doubled Wolves' lead just after half-time.

He headed over unmarked from Kieran Trippier's cross. Then he took the ball too close to Widell Zetterstrom in a one-on-one.

Wolves continued to threaten and Djiga headed powerfully over from Trippier's corner before Doyle's deflected chip sailed just wide.

An inviting cross from Mane was missed by Widell Zetterstrom but hit Said's thigh with the goal at his mercy, before Lopez and then Armstrong fired just wide.

Despite their dominance, Wolves had not forced a serious save from Widell Zetterstrom until he palmed Bueno's rising shot upwards.

Wolves finally got their long overdue second goal in the 74th minute.

Substitute Rodrigo Gomes drove in a fierce low cross and Mane gleefully steered home.

Armstrong made it 3-0 two minutes later, volleying into the far corner from just inside the box, though Widell Zetterstrom got a hand to it.

"It was a perfect night. We deserved the win and could have scored more goals but it was a great team performance.

"In the first few minutes they created two or three situations because we didn't have the right balance.

"But we deserved the win because the players were enjoying the game and we wanted to win for the fans. The lads did a great job.

"I spoke to the players because we know our opponents stay in a low block on the edge of the box and sometimes that's difficult, so we have to shoot more from outside the box.

"In the second half the space opened up a bit more to allow us to create more chances."

"I was happy with the first-half performance apart from the last five or six minutes.

"We had two or three chances which we didn't take and against a top team you can't afford to let them off.

"Then we tired in the second half and we didn't come out at all really.

"We looked leggy and we continued in that rhythm. But that will improve with Matt Clarke, Shane Duffy and Jack Robinson coming back.

"I said to the lads you can't feel sorry for yourselves. We had a really solid performance against Southampton but when you come to a place like this, you can't afford to not do the hard work and take a break."
