WSL roundup: Chelsea thwarted by Liverpool keeper, London City move to third

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Keating’s resilience keeps Chelsea at bay in 0-0 draw

Van de Donk and Putellas score twice to sink Palace 6-2

Khiara Keating’s resilient performance in goal denied Chelsea, the Women’s Super League leaders, and earned Liverpool a point, while London City Lionesses moved to within four points of the top by beating Crystal Palace 6-2.

After hard-fought 1-0 wins against Manchester City last Saturday and Paris Saint-Germain on Wednesday night, Chelsea drew a blank. Against Gareth Taylor’s side, Chelsea’s lack of cutting edge was clear a week after they beat City to go top. The calf injury picked up by Melvine Malard against City, the absence of Lauren James, who has a thigh injury, and the form of Aggie Beever-Jones, who scored her first WSL goal in more than a year in the 6-0 defeat of Charlton, have left Chelsea’s attack short.

Despite almost 70% possession and 47 touches in the box to Liverpool’s 12, Sonia Bompastor’s side amassed only 19 shots to Liverpool’s 13 and seven on target to their opponents’ four. Although the Blues remain unbeaten in the WSL, their third draw in five games in all competitions means City can leapfrog them at the top if they beat Tottenham on Sunday.

Liverpool had chances, particularly in the first half, but were hanging on after the break and Keating helped them to a fourth clean sheet, a tally bettered only by Bompastor’s side.

“It was a bit stressful; we don’t want to sit back but in games like this you have ‌to ride it out,” Keating told Sky Sports. “A clean sheet is ​always on our minds every game. We knew the mission. It was such a close game, it could have gone either way.”

The January transfer window will give the Blues an opportunity to strengthen their attack, but with three games before the winter break they need to step up in the final third, starting with their tricky fixture against London City Lionesses on 12 December after the international break.

London City came out on top in a thrilling end-to-end encounter with Palace. Having earned five of their nine points from losing positions this season, Palace did not cower when Eder Maestre’s team took the lead in Bromley. They twice came from behind to level the score before London City put the game beyond them.

The hosts had to weather some early Palace pressure, with Lexi Lloyd-Smith testing Elene Lete in the opening minute, with the goalkeeper starting in place of Mary Earps.

London City then took control and were rewarded in the 14th minute, when Daniëlle van de Donk turned in Isobel Goodwin’s cutback from close range. Palace were carved open, but they were not down and out against the team with ambitions of earning a place in next season’s Champions League.

Jo Potter’s side drew level via an Abbie Larkin volley into the bottom corner to stun the home team, with the visiting fans in full voice telling Alexia Putellas what she could do with her Ballons d’Or.

It felt fated that the three-time winner of the trophy would have the last laugh and within six minutes of the restart she restored her team’s lead, the ball falling kindly for her to turn into an empty net.

London City’s third came three minutes later and the scenario was similar. Shae Yañez failed to deal with a cross and Van de Donk swept it goalwards. Molly Bartrip was there to hook it away but the referee deemed it to have crossed the line.

The home team’s lead was cut to one in style by Palace when Fuka Tsunoda’s reverse pass was met by Annabel Blanchard and her side-footed volley looped over Lete and in. It was a stunning move and a stunning finish.

London City’s squad depth and fresh legs eventually helped them stretch the game beyond the stubborn visiting team, with Alanna Kennedy heading them back into a two-goal lead 15 minutes after coming on.

They killed the game off four minutes later, this time Goodwin rising highest to head in from another set piece, and Putellas put the cherry on top with her second at the death, looping the loose ball in after a big save from Yañez.

There were positives for Palace, despite remaining a precarious three points clear of Birmingham City, who play Manchester United on Sunday, but big-spending London City are just a cut above.
