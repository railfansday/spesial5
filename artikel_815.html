How to Watch Billboard’s 2026 CMA Awards Red Carpet Livestream

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The carpet will be hosted by Tetris Kelly & Ellie Thumann ahead of Wednesday's night's Nashville awards show.

The 2026 CMA Awards are just around the corner, and Billboard is ready to give you cowboy-boots-on-the-ground access to the red carpet.

Our livestream of the pre-ceremony arrivals at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville will be hosted by Billboard‘s own Tetris Kelly, who will be joined by content creator Ellie Thumann. She and Tetris first teamed up six months prior for Billboard‘s red carpet coverage at the 2026 ACM Awards.

The livestream will kick off at 6 p.m. ET and last until 7:30 p.m. ET, when the official CMA Awards begin. It’ll be available to watch in real time on Samsung TV Plus, Amazon Fire TV, the Billboard News YouTube channel and the Billboard Country X account.

This year’s show is sure to be a memorable one. For starters, Taylor Swift is scheduled to perform on the CMAs stage for the first time in 13 years, as her Billboard Hot 100-topping Toy Story 5 soundtrack hit “I Knew It, I Knew You” scored a nomination for single of the year. That means she also might grace the red carpet — Swifties will have to tune in to see.

Lainey Wilson will definitely be in attendance, given that she’s set to return as host of the awards ceremony. Album of the year nominees Ella Langley — whose Hot 100 record-breaking smash “Choosin’ Texas” notched nods for song of the year and single of the year — Kacey Musgraves and Luke Combs are also locked in to perform, while presenters include everyone from Michael Bublé to Joe Buck, Jessica Chastain, Vince Gill, Little Big Town, Martina McBride, Carly Pearce and Glen Powell.
