James Gunn Says “No One From Skydance Has Seen” ‘Man Of Tomorrow’: “There Are A Lot Of Untrue Rumors Going Around”

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

James Gunn is clearing up some rumors following his exit as co-head of DC Studios.

With Skydance making changes amid the Paramount and Warner Bros. merger, Peter Safran and Gunn announced they would not be heading DC Studios.

Amid their exits, many rumors have circulated. One prominent story was that Skydance saw the Superman sequel, titled Man of Tomorrow, and planned to make some edits, but Gunn has shut down those allegations.

“There are a lot of untrue rumors going around, but one of them is that Skydance has seen Man of Tomorrow and that all sorts of sweeping changes are being made,” Gunn posted on Threads.

He continued, “No one from Skydance has even seen the movie – the only people that have are Peter Safran and a very small trusted group of friends and family. So any stories around this narrative are false.” Watch on Deadline

Man of Tomorrow wrapped filming in August, with Gunn sharing a joyful post and celebrating everyone involved in the project.

“That’s a wrap on principal photography for Man of Tomorrow,” Gunn shared on Threads. “Truly a joyful experience, and I have immense gratitude to my cast and crew.”

Man of Tomorrow is set for release on July 9, 2027, with David Corenswet reprising his role as Clark Kent. The cast also includes Nicholas Hoult as Lex Luthor, Lars Eidinger as Brainiac, Rachel Brosnahan as Lois Lane, Skyler Gisondo as Jimmy Olsen, Sara Sampaio as Eve Teschmacher, Isabela Merced as Hawkgirl, Nathan Fillion as Guy Gardner, Edi Gathegi as Mister Terrific, and Adria Arjona as Maxima.

Aaron Pierre, who plays John Stewart in HBO’s Lanterns, is set to jump to the big screen in the Superman sequel. Also set to reprise his Blue Beetle role is Xolo Maridueña, who will play the superhero once again since the 2023 film, directed by Ángel Manuel Soto.

Amid the executive shakeup at Skydance, former Marvel exec Jeremy Latcham is now set to run DC Studios.
