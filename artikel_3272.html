iOS 27.3 gives Apple’s Calendar app new design updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

iOS 27.3 beta 2 arrived today, most notably giving the iPhone Duo a face unlock feature. The update also includes some design updates for Apple’s Calendar app.

When you open Apple’s Calendar app in iOS 27.3, the interface will look largely familiar to what was there before. But there are some key design changes that make the app better suited for one-handed use.

Most notably, the buttons for search and for adding a new event have both been moved from the top of the screen to the bottom.

This is a welcome change for anyone who uses their iPhone one-handed a lot, as both important buttons were previously in the top-right corner. I normally use my iPhone in my left hand, so they were especially hard to reach before.

Additionally, Apple has moved the current month to the top-center of the screen and added the year too. Because the year is now always prominently visible, Apple removed it from the back button in the top-left.

Finally, the ‘Today’ button that takes you back to the current day has been changed to always show the actual date. So right now, it reads ‘Mon 16.’
