Billy Ray Cyrus Spills on Miley's Wedding, Says Her Love Will Last Forever

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Billy Ray Cyrus is still riding high from daughter Miley Cyrus' wedding ... and he says there's one thing about her big day that'll stick with him forever.

The country singer was spotted running errands in Beverly Hills Monday, just two days after Miley tied the knot with Maxx Morando in Palm Springs ... and he couldn't stop gushing about the newlyweds.

Billy Ray sidesteps a question about whether he walked Miley down the aisle while struggling with a parking meter ... but once he gets that sorted out, he's happy to share his favorite part of the wedding.

"Knowing that the love is gonna last forever," Billy Ray says ... repeating, "It's gonna last forever."

He also calls the ceremony beautiful and says he's so happy ... though he never gives a straight answer about walking his daughter down the aisle.

BRC also shared some photos from the wedding on social media ... he says he's a proud papa, honored to welcome Maxx to the family and says they're "a match made in heaven."
