The stand out feature of any Tempur-Pedic mattress is the Tempur Material foam. This is the brand's signature material, a squishy comfort material that's often seen as the original memory foam.

Based on the foams used by NASA to cushion pilots during test flights, Tempur Material is known for its dense foam build. The tight foam cells are able to easily adapt to your body, cushioning every joint, bump and crease to deliver Tempur-Pedic's famous pressure relief.

Dense foam also helps limit motion transfer. When another person moves in bed, the foams absorb the excess vibrations before they get to you. As a result, you aren't woken up by your partner tossing and turning.

Tempur Material is used across Tempur-Pedic's top-rated luxury mattress line-up. We love how it 'hugs' the joints to relieve pressure, which can help reduce aches and pains in the morning. (No more getting out of bed with a creak.)
