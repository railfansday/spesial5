You've got expertise worth sharing, a project worth talking about, or a story people should hear. But getting that story published often means pitching journalists, chasing opportunities, and waiting for someone to take notice.

Introducing Journalistic.tech, a new way to get interviewed and share your expertise with the HackerNoon community, without having to find or pitch a journalist.

We've put together 273 interview templates, each designed around a specific role, achievement, company milestone, product launch, or area of expertise. Whether you're a developer sharing something you've built, a founder announcing company news, or a professional reflecting on your career, there's probably an interview waiting for you.

Writing about your day-to-day work? Try Commit History, the software engineer interview. Sharing what you just shipped? Take the product launch interview. If your company just raised a round, start with the funding announcement interview. For a career change, try the career change interview. There’s an interview for everyone in tech.

No journalist hunting, no awkward interview scheduling, and no need to squeeze everything into a single call. Just around 20 minutes to get started and a chance to turn your knowledge into a published story.

Getting your ideas, experience, and work in front of a wider tech audience shouldn't require knowing the right journalist or having a PR team behind you. Journalistic.tech makes it easier to share what you know, build your professional presence, and contribute your perspective to the tech community.

The About page walks through why we publish interviews, how HackerNoon editors write the questions and review the answers, and where your story goes after approval. It also suggests when to start an interview, from the day you ship a product to the week you start a new job, and links to published Q&As so you can see how other people tell their stories.

Our Example Interviews library brings together more than 2,500 published HackerNoon interviews. Search by name or topic, sort by most read, or browse categories like AI & data, Women in Tech, and Startups of the Year. Each story points you to a template you can use to write one like it.

Interviewing has long been a part of Hacker Noon, two people working together to better document their expertise on the internet.

We're also bringing Journalistic.tech to Product Hunt, and we'd love your support. Check out the launch, share your thoughts, and give us an upvote to help more people discover a new way to get their stories out there.

You have a story. We have the questions. Let's get it published!
