Welcome To One Mile At A Time, Daniel Ross! Here’s What’s Going On…

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It’s a big day here at OMAAT, as I’m delighted to welcome our first full time contributor in the site’s nearly 19-year history. Let me start with a little bit of background, and then I’ll talk about Daniel Ross, who is joining the team. As a creature of habit, this feels like a whole new frontier, but I’m excited!

I’m incredibly fortunate that I’ve never had a “traditional” job — I started OMAAT my freshmen year of college as a hobby (after having been passionate about miles & points in my teens), and nearly 19 years later, that’s still what I get to do full time.

I’m blessed that I can wake up every day and cover an industry that I love. While OMAAT is a business that pays my bills, it has never been exclusively about maximizing profitability, coming up with some five-year business plan, or seeing what opportunities there are to sell. It’s also my hobby.

Now, the downside to this — at least if you ask my family — is that I also “work” seven days per week, 365 days per year. Other sites in the space that are this size might have one or two dozen contributors, but for me, that has never sounded very appealing. My passion is actually getting to cover the industry, rather than managing a huge group of people.

Let me be clear, I don’t do this alone, as I have two great people who do a ton of to work to handle everything in the background, much of which readers don’t see — Jeremy oversees business development (basically anything having to do with the business side of things), and Howie manages things related to credit cards and tech (which largely helps pay the bills!). I’m incredibly grateful for their contributions, because it allows me to focus on content.

In the past, I’ve had some part time contributors, and I also very much appreciate the contributions they’ve made. However, given the volume of content at OMAAT, it’s hard for that to make a meaningful difference in terms of workload, etc.

Recently I finally accepted the fact that it’s time to add a full time contributor. That’s not because I want to meaningfully change anything about the site in terms of the content mix, or because I want to step back from things. Quite to the contrary, it’s because I want to do the best possible job I can.

As many people can surely attest to, if you take pride in what you do (rather than just turning your site into an AI slop machine), it just takes a lot more effort than in the past to accomplish the same results, given how everything on the internet has shifted. Aside from spending time with family, this site and industry is basically my entire life — it’s not a means to an end, but the passion as such is the “end.”

So my hope is that by having someone join the team full time on the content front, I can actually put more time into the areas that I’d like to improve — engaging with the community, responding to comments and emails, etc. These are all things I’ve gotten very bad at, not due to a lack of caring, but due to a lack of hours in the day, especially as someone who has two small kids.

For that matter, I’m planning on imminently launching a new video series (which I wrote about over the summer), not to replace written content, but to complement it, as I think we have a unique angle for it. I just couldn’t carve out the time for that without getting some more help. So I’m finally no longer putting this off, and I’m ripping the band aid-off.

Let me emphasize that the goal here is to genuinely do a better job of providing useful and interesting content to community members, rather than anything else. This isn’t a “we’re cutting free food & drinks in economy due to customer feedback, it’s an enhancement” situation!

I’d like to welcome Daniel Ross to OMAAT, as he’s joining full time as of today! Daniel isn’t new to the miles & points world, as has he contributed to many sites over the years (The Points Guy, Business Insider, Upgraded Points, etc.), so odds are good that you may be familiar with him (and you can follow him at cometravelwithdan on Instagram).

Shortly Daniel will be publishing his first post introducing himself, but let me provide a little background on how this came to be, and how I envision this evolving.

I’ve actually never met Daniel before in person. I’ve always had respect for his work and think he has done a great job with writing, social media, video content, etc. We hadn’t connected until not that long ago, and hit it off.

When it comes to working with people, and especially adding them to the OMAAT team, I try to follow my gut, and I think Daniel will be a good fit. I know OMAAT readers value opinions, analysis, and someone with interesting takes, and I think Daniel checks all those boxes. For example, he was just telling me about Aerolineas Argentinas’ quirky baggage allowance, so I’m excited for the expanded content coverage, as that’s something I would’ve never written about (I’m more into covering Air Côte d’Ivoire’s insane first class carry-on allowance).

One of the specific reasons I don’t want a dozen contributors is because I think it’s important for people to have a unique voice, and for readers to get to know that, and I think people will enjoy hearing Daniel’s takes on things.

Daniel also does a great job with social media and video content, which is something that I’ve historically struggled with. My general philosophy is that if I can’t do something right, I won’t do it at all, and that basically sums up the OMAAT strategy for social media up until now. 😉 I think Daniel strikes the unique balance of being interesting without being obnoxious and clickbaity, and I appreciate that.

Transparency is important to me, and Daniel is someone who understands how “particular” the OMAAT community is. 😉 Obviously the OMAAT community is very much about written content, so that’s why I think it’s important that this is where he focuses to begin with, since that’s the medium people are most familiar with. Then we’ll see where things go from there…

It’s an exciting (scary?) day here at OMAAT, as Daniel Ross is joining the team, as the first full time contributor of the site in its nearly 19 year history. I’m excited, and the goal is to add someone with a unique and interesting voice, to expand coverage on things, etc.

My hope is also that it frees up a little more time for me, so I can focus a bit more on the community aspect of things. In the long run, you can also expect a bit more of a focus on video and social media, but we’re starting with what we know here!

I’m excited about this, and Daniel will have a post soon introducing himself. Thanks to everyone for their patience, and stay tuned!

PS: For those wondering about the British Airways 747 featured image, it’s because I noticed Daniel had that as his model plane in the background behind his desk during our video calls, so we’ll throw him that Easter egg. 😉
