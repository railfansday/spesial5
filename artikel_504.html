On Aug. 12, 2026, the UK witnessed its best solar eclipse since 1999, wowing the entire country with coverage ranging between 88-96%. I feel so blessed to have witnessed it and honestly, I owe that opportunity to the Unistellar eQuinox 2. After hiking up Kelston Roundhill in Somerset and setting up, I was able to safely watch the eclipse on my iPad through all of its stages and capture images at every moment.

After attaching the solar filter to the Equinox 2, I simply clicked "Go-to" on the sun from the Unistellar app and waited for it to find the blinding solar disk, which showed up on my screen as an orange disk. There was no issue with the eQuinox 2 finding the sun, so I could focus on capturing images or watching it with solar eclipse glasses.

It not only gave me the opportunity to photograph the event, but it gave me a closer view than I had with my solar eclipse glasses. For something that is unlikely to happen again in my lifetime, I am amazed to get such a front-row seat.
