KPop Demon Hunters announces Golden World Tour. Heres how to get tickets.

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

If you've ever wanted to catch KPop Demon Hunters' HUNTR/X live (and let's be honest, after hearing "Golden," who hasn't?), next summer is your chance.

Starting in June 2027, HUNTR/X's Rumi, Mira, and Zoey will step out on the Golden World Tour, a global celebration of KPop Demon Hunters that will bring fans up close and personal to the beloved animated characters. It's not clear whether artists EJAE, Audrey Nuna, Rei Ami, the singing voices behind HUNTR/X, will be performing.

According to a Netflix press release, HUNTR/X will "interact with the audience through cutting-edge performance innovations that bring this fan-favorite K-pop girl group to life like never before."

HUNTR/X won't be alone on tour. Their rival boy band, the Saja Boys, will also take the stage, so you can get your "Soda Pop" on — again Netflix hasn't announced whether or not Andrew Choi, Neckwav, Danny Chung, Kevin Woo and samUIL Lee, the Saja Boys' real life singers, will appear. Plus, expect appearances from fan-favorite Derpy Tiger, as well as serious stunts, choreography, and more.

According to Netflix, the tour will last from 2027 to 2028, with over 150 performances across almost 50 cities. Here's how you can get in on the Golden World Tour.

Tickets for the Golden World Tour will be sold in three waves.

First up is the Mastercard presale, which begins Wednesday, Nov. 18, at 10 a.m. local time.

Next up is the "For the Fans" presale, which starts Thursday, Nov. 19, at 10 a.m. local time. You can register for that presale at the Golden World Tour website.

General tickets go on sale Friday, Nov. 20, at 10 a.m. local time. Eligible Mastercard World Elite and World Legend holders will get access to preferred tickets during this sale.

So far, the Golden World Tour has only announced tickets for tour stops in North America, the U.K., and Europe. However, further dates for the Asia Pacific and Latin American regions will be dropping soon.
