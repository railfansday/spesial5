The Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Knock Out Collection is back in stock for Black Friday — buy for under $30

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Knock Out Collection is on sale for $29 at Amazon, down from its $32.50 list price.

With Black Friday merely days away, Amazon has dropped one of the Pokémon TCG's more affordable 30th Celebration boxes to a new record-low price — making the Knock Out Collection significantly cheaper than it was back in September.

As of Nov. 23, the Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Knock Out Collection is available for just $29 at Amazon, shaving $3.50 off the $32.50 list price. It's now $20.99 cheaper than the $49.99 deal we covered back in September. Better still, it's sold directly by Amazon instead of a third-party seller.

This 30th-anniversary box is now only $9.72 above its $19.38 TCGplayer market price.

For your money, you're getting two 30th Celebration booster packs, an exclusive foil Eevee promo trading card, and a plastic coin — bringing the cost to $14.50 per booster before counting the extras.
