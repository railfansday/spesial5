Peruvian Bull: The Yen Carry Trade and Bitcoin’s Road to $300k

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Roberto Rios, the Peruvian Bull and author of the Dollar End Game Substack, joins Spencer Nickels to explain how the yen carry trade connects to Bitcoin's road to $300,000.

Roberto Rios, the Peruvian Bull and author of the Dollar End Game Substack, joins Spencer Nickels to explain how the yen carry trade connects to Bitcoin’s road to $300,000. He traces the trade from the Plaza Accord to today’s estimated $3 trillion to $6 trillion. He explains why the Bank of Japan’s aggressive rate hikes are starting to crack it, and how Japan’s yen interventions push up U.S. Treasury yields. In the short term, he warns that a carry trade unwind like August 2024 can “break Bitcoin lower.” Long term, he argues that high rates force the government to borrow more and the Fed to eventually print more, which he calls “massively bullish for Bitcoin.” He also says “this cycle we’re going to go to $300,000.”
