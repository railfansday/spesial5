City fire blanks in shoot-out as Maresca’s opening game ends in defeat by Inter

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Manchester City lost the Asahi Super Dry Trophy 3-1 on a penalty shoot-out against Inter Milan.

Enzo Maresca took charge of the Premier  League runners-up for the first time since his arrival as Pep Guardiola’s replacement, though he was without many of his stars who had yet to return from World Cup duty.

One of the players missing was Elliot Anderson, the £116m signing from Nottingham Forest, though Jeremy Monga, 16, was an unused substitute after being brought in from Leicester City in the summer transfer window.

City took the lead on 14 minutes through Divin Mubamba as he tapped in an Antoine Semenyo cross, but Inter’s experienced defender Benjamin Pavard levelled just six minutes later when City failed to clear a corner.

The Premier League side dominated possession in the second half after an even first 45 minutes but could not find a way through. Phil Foden, the captain, was again underwhelming after a difficult year last season, but with the season still to come he had plenty of time to show his best form once more after signing a new contract at the Etihad.

The game went to penalties after full-time with no extra time played, and only Claudio Echeverri held his nerve for City as Inter Milan scored three of theirs, Matteo Lavelli netting the winning spot-kick.

Both teams learnt little from the match as Inter made 11 changes and City eight, though Maresca will have been impressed by Ryan McAidoo’s performance as a second-half substitute, as he regularly threatened from the right wing. The 18-year-old forward will surely be in demand from other clubs looking to loan him, and City may yet be tempted to keep him around for the coming season.

Welcome to Telegraph Sport’s live coverage of the pre-season friendly between Manchester City and Inter Milan.

The two sides meet  at Kai Tak Sports Park, Hong  Kong as their pre-season work kicks up a gear ahead of the new campaign.

City are still getting used to life without Pep Guardiola, the coach who left after a decade of high spending and high performance.

In his place is Enzo Maresca, who is not shy with the chequebook himself.

Ayyoub Bouaddi, the outstanding 18-year-old Moroccan international, should soon arrive from Lille when a fee is agreed, but Elliot Anderson and Jeremy Monga have already been signed.

City were runners-up last season, so Maresca is under pressure not just to keep things going without Guardiola, but to get the team back to the top of English football. That looks to be a tough prospect with Arsenal likely to sign Bruno Guimaraes from Newcastle in the coming days.

Inter, meanwhile, have swooped to pick up John Stones on a free transfer after his contract ended at the Etihad Stadium, and he is joined by Manuel Akanji, another former City centre-back whose loan turned permanent in the summer.

Inter won Serie A last season under head coach Cristian Chivu, and they are still one of the best sides in Europe.

While this friendly is likely to be played at a reduced pace, and will be disrupted by rustiness and substitutes, we could still learn a lot about both sides going into the new campaign, and there may be the odd surprise thrown up by a young player looking to stake a claim ahead of his senior colleagues this year.
