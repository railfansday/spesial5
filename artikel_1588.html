Watch: Jashari scores first Milan goal within three minutes of coming on

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

AC Milan restored their two-goal advantage not long after the interval, as Ardon Jashari scored mere minutes after coming on.

Amorim opted to make an intriguing change at half-time, bringing off Bartesaghi and replacing him with Jashari. The change proved to be an absolute masterstroke, as the midfielder scored inside three minutes.
