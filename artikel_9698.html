Abstraction in C++ - Explained in Simple Words

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Abstraction means showing only the important things and hiding the complex details.

Think of a car. When you drive a car, you only see steering, brake, and accelerator. You don't see how the engine works inside. You don't need to know it. You just drive. This is Abstraction!

In C++, we use Abstraction to make our code simple to use.
