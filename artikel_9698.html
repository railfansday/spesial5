Google's t5-small was downloaded 17.3M times on Hugging Face in the last 30 days. Meta's 200-language nllb-200-distilled-600M got 709.4K. Below are all 143 translation models in the TextModels directory: open weights ranked by downloads, including 73 Opus-MT language pairs from Helsinki-NLP, plus the historical models that started neural machine translation.

Downloads and capability tell different stories here. NLLB-200 covers 200 languages, and HackerNoon's look inside the AI that translates 200 languages explains how it lifted quality for low-resource languages. But t5-small is a 60M-parameter multitask model that tutorials and test suites load by default. For production in specific language pairs, teams still reach for dedicated models, as in this production voice stack for Igbo, Yoruba and Hausa that chose NLLB over an LLM.

Everything comes from the TextModels directory as of November 23, 2026, filtered to translation models (143 entries). Hugging Face download counts are the last 30 days from the Hugging Face API. "Verified" means the directory fetched and confirmed that model's metrics. Hosted models have no public download counts, so they're listed by price or provider, and historical models link to their papers.
