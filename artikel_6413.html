11 Best Vacation Rentals in Riviera Maya for Beach Access, Private Pools, and Jungle Views

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

From the bustling city of Cancún to the somewhat more tranquil bohemian beach town of Tulum, the Riviera Maya is home to some of Mexico’s most idyllic beaches along the Caribbean. Featuring turquoise waters, windswept bays, and wide swaths of verdant jungle harboring hidden cenotes, this region of the Yucatán Peninsula delivers equal parts serenity and adventure. After living in Mexico for over 10 years, I’ve stayed in and driven through many of the towns and beaches that make up this region, and it's hard to choose a favorite.

As wonderful as the beachfront views, jungle tranquility, and local activities are here, the vacation rentals in the Riviera Maya are equally as impressive. They have a way of making the surrounding nature feel even more pristine. Whether you’re venturing to the region solo or traveling with friends and family, there are gorgeous options for nearly every type of traveler, each offering a personalized way to visit this corner of the Caribbean. Read on for my favorite vacation rentals in the Riviera Maya, from city escapes in Cancún to beachy stays in Tulum and everything in between.

We've selected these listings based on Superhost and Guest Favorite status, ratings, amenities, location, decor, editor stays, and previous guest reviews.
