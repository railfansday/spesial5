Pokemon cards can now be delivered in 15 minutes with massive discounts on rare cards

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Gopuff is launching a new way for Pokemon card collectors to purchase sought-after trading cards, offering weekly drops that can be delivered to their doors in as little as 15 minutes.

The delivery service announced the initiative on November 5, revealing plans to release limited quantities of Pokemon Trading Card Game products every Friday for six consecutive weeks.

Some drops will also give collectors the chance to purchase valuable cards at prices significantly below market value.

The new feature comes as demand for trading cards continues to grow on the platform. According to Gopuff, trading card sales increased 335% between the fourth quarter of 2025 and the third quarter of 2026.

Starting Friday, November 6, Gopuff will release limited quantities of Pokemon TCG products every Friday at 3 PM ET for six weeks.

The lineup will include products such as the Mega Evolution Pitch Black Elite Trainer Box and the 30th Celebration Poster Collection, with additional items revealed ahead of each drop.

Gopuff is also partnering with Card Vault by Tom Brady to launch a weekly promotion called “Hit of the Week,” letting customers buy a high-value Pokemon card for significantly less than its market value.

Only one of these discounted cards will be available each week, meaning collectors will need to act quickly when the drop goes live.

The company has outlined a weekly schedule for customers looking to participate. Every Monday, a countdown timer will appear on the Gopuff homepage, teasing the upcoming release without revealing which products will be available.

On Thursdays at 3 PM ET, exactly 24 hours before the drop, the company will reveal the products included in that week’s lineup. Customers can then enable notifications or add items to their carts ahead of time.

Checkout will open on Fridays at 3 PM ET, with products sold on a first-come, first-served basis until supplies run out. Quantities will vary by location, and purchase limits may apply.

Unlike traditional online trading card purchases that require customers to wait for shipping, Gopuff will fulfill orders through its network of local fulfillment centers, allowing eligible customers to receive their purchases in as little as 15 minutes.

“Our goal is to make Gopuff the fastest way to get the internet’s most-wanted products,” said Tyler Stewart, Head of Marketing at Gopuff.

“Our new drops give people something to look forward to, from the anticipation to the reveal, with a few surprises along the way. And the best part? No lines, no waiting for shipping. Just the products you want, delivered in minutes. We’re excited to continue building on this experience, with more iterations to come.”

The Pokemon promotion also marks the introduction of Gopuff’s broader product drop feature, which is designed to offer limited-quantity releases of high-demand items through scheduled shopping events.

Collectors can access the drops through the Gopuff website or its mobile app, although product availability and delivery times will depend on their location.
