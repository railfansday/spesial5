Burger King’s feedback campaign takes aim at its own restaurants

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

CMO Joel Yashinsky explains how the chain is focused on listening to customers, a push that has helped the chain deliver its sixth consecutive sales increase.

Burger King is asking consumers to help the brand determine which of its restaurants need a remodel or renovation, per details shared with Marketing Dive. “Rebuild My BK” continues the recent efforts to turn feedback into action as part of the brand’s larger turnaround plan.

“One of those key pieces [of feedback] that we heard — and we certainly had an inkling to this, as well — was our restaurants needed to be updated,” said Burger King CMO Joel Yashinsky. “It's a natural evolution of this campaign that takes us beyond just a marketing or promotional event, but is really about the betterment of the brand.”

Along with a microsite for soliciting feedback, the campaign includes a 60-second spot that showcases Burger King President Tom Curtis as he tours the country, meets with consumers and pitches in on construction. The chain has remodeled over 60% of its footprint and is working to open a new or remodeled location every day this year, per the spot.

Curtis has been central to Burger King’s marketing efforts this year, including a push for feedback that saw the chain give out his phone number, a move that has generated 120,000 calls, per press materials. The chain this summer also rolled out a reimagined restaurant manager role and a Whopper Guarantee that yielded free burgers for unsatisfied customers.

“[Tom’s] commitment to listening to the guests, to wanting to hear from them and then being out front in a lot of our creative and our content — he's a natural,” Yashinsky said. “His humility comes across, his authenticity comes across… he didn't want to be the face of the brand, necessarily.”

“Rebuild My BK” is scheduled to run through Jan. 5. Members of Burger King’s Royal Perks loyalty program who complete a submission earn 100 bonus Crowns. Guest feedback is one of several factors the chain may consider when making its remodeling and renovation plans.

Restaurant upgrades and marketing investments have been the major focus of a Reclaim the Flame turnaround plan that Burger King parent Restaurant Brands International launched in 2022. Burger King this year surpassed Wendy’s to reclaim second place in the U.S. burger business behind McDonald’s. The chain saw its sixth-consecutive comparable sales increase, up 8.4% in the U.S. last quarter.

“Our guests are engaging with us. They're on this journey with us, and we're trying to be really true and authentic to them by making these changes,” Yashinsky said. “That's the special piece — the special ingredient, if you will — in what we're doing with this campaign: it's really putting listening to the guest at the forefront.”
