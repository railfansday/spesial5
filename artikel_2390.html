Spirit defeat Royals 2-1 in semifinal to set up home Championship

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Clara Luvanga scored solo-effort game-winner in the 56th minute

The first game of the National Women’s Soccer League semifinals brought the dramatic goalscoring that was missing for much of the quarterfinals. In front of a soldout crowd at rowdy Audi Field, the Washington Spirit defeated the Utah Royals 2-1 to advance to the 2026 NWSL Championship at home in D.C.

The first ten minutes of the game jam packed the biggest moments of first-half action.

It started with the fastest goal in NWSL playoff history and an Olimpico at that. Just one minute and 57 seconds in, Utah Royals forward Cloé Lacasse took a corner kick that snuck in at the front post. Lacasse’s set piece contributions have been crucial all season, but this time she was helped by a miscommunication between Washington’s goalkeeper and defender. The goal was Utah’s 15th scored off a set piece this season.

Washington scored an equalizer before the clock hit eight minutes. Following up her two-goal and one-assist performance in the quarterfinals, Trinity Rodman found space running down the left side into the box. Her cutback cross was missed by Royals goalkeeper Mandy McGlynn, an uncharacteristic mistake, and finished off by a cheeky touch from Sofia Cantore. She became the first Italian to score in the NWSL playoffs.

Sofia Cantore levels it early for the Spirit with a cheeky touch 👀NWSL Playoffs pres. by Google Pixel

The rest of the first half, the momentum belonged to Washington. They kept applying the pressure, taking 12 shots to Utah’s five, but only three of those 12 were on target, and the Spirit did not score a go-ahead goal.

Washington were not content coming out of halftime though, so they once again found a goal less than 10 minutes into the half. This time it was a disastrous mistake from the Royals, as Nuria Rábano played a pass back too softly and Clara Luvanga — coming off her own one-goal and two-assist quarterfinal performance — pounced and finished.

All it takes is a moment. Clara Luvanga makes it 2-1 for the Spirit!NWSL Playoffs pres. by Google Pixel

The Royals were nearly punished for another costly mistake five minutes later when Luvanga sent in a great cross to an entirely unmarked Mélissa Bethi who put the ball in the back of the net. Lucky for Utah, Luvanga was called offside in the buildup.

Utah made their second substitution of the game while the call was reviewed, bringing back Midfielder of the Year finalist Mina Tanaka from injury. The Royals did not win in the regular season while Tanaka was unavailable down the stretch. The first change had been bringing Cece Delzer on for an injured Madison Hammond.

Despite the return of their leading goalscorer from the regular season, Utah could not break through Washington’s control on the game. The home team continued to push forward for more goals while finding the right moments to slow things down and eventually saw out the victory.

Entering their third straight NWSL Championship, the Spirit will hope to turn things around. Washington won the 2021 NWSL Championship in Rodman’s rookie year with Andi Sullivan, Ashley Hatch and Aubrey Kingsbury the only other remaining players. Since then, they lost in two straight Championships.

Washington are the first club since the 2019 North Carolina Courage to play an NWSL Championship at their home venue as well as just the second club in league history to reach three straight NWSL Championship matches (2017-19 North Carolina).

The Spirit remain perfect in postseason matches at Audi Field and will hope that continues with the home field Championship. Across eight postseason matches, they are now an undefeated 6-0-2, with both draws resulting in advancement after a penalty shootout.

Washington will face the winner of Sunday’s semifinal between the Kansas City Current and NJ/NY Gotham FC, at 8 pm ET on Nov. 21 at Audi Field.
