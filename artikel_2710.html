Packers pick off Drake Maye, jump out to 7-0 lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Packers are out to a quick lead in New England.

Drake Maye threw an interception on his first pass of the day and the Packers turned it into their first points of the day. Jordan Love scrambled for a 13-yard score and the Packers are up 7-0 with just over two minutes off the clock.

Maye's pass for Mack Hollins was snagged by Packers safety Xavier McKinney near midfield and he returned it inside the Patriots' 15-yard-line. Love scored two plays later and the Patriots' second drive didn't go much better.

A short run and an incompletion were followed by an Isaiah McDuffie sack, so the Patriots will need to figure out something different if they want to keep this one from turning into a Packers runaway.
