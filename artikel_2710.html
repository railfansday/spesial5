Andy Reid: New York overturned Rashee Rice catch, not a call I agree with

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Chiefs coach Andy Reid was not happy with the NFL’s officiating office in New York over a crucial call in the final minutes of the Thanksgiving night loss to the Bills.

With the Chiefs facing third-and-10 at their own 9-yard line with 1:51 remaining in the fourth quarter, Patrick Mahomes hit Rashee Rice over the middle for what the officials ruled was a sensational 11-yard catch. But the officiating office reviewed the play and ruled that Rice lost control as he was going to the ground. Reid said after the game that the on-field officials told him they thought it was a catch but the officiating office overruled them. Reid agreed with the officials on the field.

“Not one that I agreed with, but they said it came from New York,” Reid said. “It’s not from these guys, our guys called it a completion — the guys who were working the game. I’m not sure what they saw. I saw he had control of the ball, then his elbow hit, then the ball hit, but he had control. Normally, in those kind of situations, it’s got to be clear and obvious. And to me, that was not clear and obvious.”

It was a big play, as it went from the Chiefs having first-and-10 at the 20-yard line with all three timeouts and a real chance of driving for a game-winning score on that possession, to the Chiefs having fourth-and-10 at their own 9-yard line and punting the ball back to the Bills. Ultimately, after an exchange of punts, it was the Bills who kicked the game-winning field goal as time expired. And it was Reid left to wonder what might have been if the call on the field had stood.
