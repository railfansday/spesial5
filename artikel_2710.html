Fantasy Basketball Top 200 Rankings: How concerned should Trae Young managers be?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

With the Washington Wizards boasting a revamped rotation headlined by first-overall pick AJ Dybantsa and veterans Anthony Davis and Trae Young, there were expectations that the team would take a step forward in 2026-27. While the team's current record of 7-11 doesn't scream out "progress," it is an improvement compared to how the Wizards started last season. But among the concerns has been Young's play, as he is going through one of the most inefficient shooting stretches of his NBA career.

Through 18 games, Young is averaging 17.1 points, 2.8 rebounds, 8.8 assists, 1.3 steals and 2.1 three-pointers per game. The assists, steals and three-pointers have been fine, but the points and percentages have taken significant hits. Young finished Sunday with shooting splits of 36.4 percent from the field and 32.5 percent from beyond the arc. If it held, the former would be the lowest of Young's career by a significant margin, and he hasn't shot this poorly from three since his rookie season (32.4 percent).

Despite Young's continued struggles, Washington went 3-1 last week, including a blowout win over the Magic on November 22. In those four games, Young averaged 12.8 points, 2.3 rebounds, 9.0 assists, 1.8 steals and 2.5 three-pointers per game, shooting 28.6 percent from the field.

He was better from three, making 40 percent of his attempts, and Young has kept his turnover count in check on most nights. Washington's improved rotation should be acknowledged, as it allows Young to take a step back as a scorer. But given his second-round ADP, fantasy managers expected a bigger return than what they've seen so far.

Week 6 Fantasy Basketball Week Ahead: Nuggets pick up two big wins; Dylan Harper and Jaylen Brown injured

A look back at Week 5 and a look ahead to Week 6 in the NBA.

C Joel Embiid, Philadelphia 76ers: The only two games Embiid has missed this season were part of back-to-backs. And in three games last week, he averaged 29.7 points, 9.3 rebounds, 5.7 assists, 1.0 steals and 2.0 blocks per game while shooting 47.4 percent from the field and 86.8 percent from the foul line.

C Nikola Jokić, Denver Nuggets: The Joker capped Week 5 with his fourth triple-double of the season, recording 37 points, 18 rebounds, 11 assists, one steal, one block and one three-pointer in a November 22 win over the Bulls.

C Jalen Smith, Chicago Bulls: In three games, Smith averaged 19.0 points, 7.0 rebounds, 2.3 assists, 1.7 steals, 1.3 blocks and 3.7 three-pointers while shooting 64.7 percent from the field. There may come a time when Nic Claxton replaces him in the starting lineup, but that hasn't happened yet.

G De'Aaron Fox, San Antonio Spurs: While Wembanyama performed well last week, Fox struggled. While he did record 15 assists in the two games the Spurs played without Dylan Harper (hip), Fox scored 15 points total in wins over the Clippers and Pelicans. There will be room for him to score more while the Spurs await Harper's return.

C Alex Sarr, Washington Wizards: Young isn't the only Wizards starter whose production has underwhelmed. Outside of three-pointers, Sarr's averages have decreased across the board compared to his 2025-26 numbers, with the third-year center averaging 11.2 points on eight shot attempts per game.

G Derrick White, Boston Celtics: After appearing to turn a corner with his shot to close out Week 4, White struggled in Week 5. In three appearances, he shot 30.6 percent from the field and averaged 11.7 points per game. Also, White totaled three three-pointers, one steal and one block, categories that have boosted his fantasy value in seasons past.
