Giannis Antetokounmpo, Heat host Tyler Herro, Bucks for GTA 6-inspired 'Vice City' night

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Miami Heat played host to the Milwaukee Bucks on Wednesday night. While this was already a big game — the first time Giannis Antetokounmpo is facing his former team — it carried even more meaning.

The long-awaited "GTA VI" releases nationwide on Thursday. It is arguably the most highly anticipated video game of all time, coming out 13 years after GTA V was released back in 2013.

The game, which has already received near-perfect reviews, is set to upend the world Thursda. With estimates that roughly 16 million copies of the game were pre-ordered, employers could be dealing with a lot of "sick" days ahead of the weekend.

That might sound far-fetched, but it's true. Experts are estimating businesses in the United States could lose over $1 billion in productivity due to worker absences.

"Vice City" is a fictional city from the franchise. It is not a direct replica of Miami, but it is heavily inspired by the city, while the state of "Leonida" in the video game is inspired by Florida.

The Heat's marketing department took the chance to host a "Vice City"-inspired night in honor of the game's release, and it just so happened to coincide with Anteokounmpo getting his first crack at his old team.

To celebrate the game's release, the Kaseya Center got a "Vice City" makeover with a new paint job. Music from the video game was also played throughout the arena.

The game also featured a unique halftime show for fans' entertainment. The arena even brought some of the fictional fast-food joints from the video game to the concession options for Wednesday night.

ESPN reporter Cam Wolf showed that fans lined up four hours before tipoff in the rain to get access to the "Vice City"-inspired gear that was selling in the team store.

Wolfe also added that the arena completely sold out of the gear just 90 minutes after it went on sale.

ESPN released a video package for the game.

Milwaukee traded Antetokounmpo and Bobby Portis to Miami in exchange for Tyler Herro, Jaime Jaquez Jr., Kel'el Ware, Kasparas Jakučionis, and five total draft picks on July 6, 2026.

Antetokounmpo totaled 27 points, 13 rebounds and 9 assists to lead the Heat to a 137-130 victory over the Bucks on Wednesday.

The Heat are 9-6, while the Bucks enter with an NBA-worst 1-14 record.
