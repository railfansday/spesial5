Lakers to honor beloved announcer with commemorative banner

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Lakers will honor Lawrence Tanter with a commemorative banner on Star Plaza outside of Crypto.com Arena before the Lakers play the Jazz on Monday.

Fans are invited to share personal messages and memories from 4 p.m. to 8 p.m. in tribute to Tanter, who served as the Lakers' P.A. announcer for 43 years.

Los Angeles Lakers public address announcer Lawrence Tanter works during an NBA basketball game against the Portland Trail Blazers on Dec. 23, 2017. MediaNews Group via Getty Images

Los Angeles Lakers announcer Lawrence Tanter stands before a game against the Timberwolves at Staples Center in Los Angeles on Friday, March 18, 2011. Los Angeles Times via Getty Images

Tanter, who suffered a stroke in March and moved into an advisory role in June, died at age 76 in September.

Tanter was the voice of the Lakers during 10 championships, making calls for Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Magic Johnson, Shaquille O'Neal, LeBron James and Luka Doncic.

Announcer Lawrence Tanter attends the game between the Los Angeles Lakers and the Denver Nuggets at STAPLES Center on Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2015, in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

"We are deeply saddened to share the news of the passing of legendary Lakers public address announcer Lawrence Tanter," the Lakers said in a statement in September.

"For 43 years, LT's voice served as the soundtrack to Lakers games at the arena. He will be remembered as a cherished member of the Lakers family."
