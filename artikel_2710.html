2026 F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix starting grid & lineup after penalties

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

We are just hours away from the 2026 Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix, after a wild day of qualifying. After five drivers were handed grid penalties for exceeding their power unit component allocations, the starting grid and lineup is set.

Max Verstappen and Red Bull Racing inherited the pole after Kimi Antonelli's five-place penalty, while Antonelli's Mercedes teammate will start in second. In the second row, McLaren's Lando Norris and Verstappen's Red Bull Racing teammate, Isack Hadjar, will get their starts.

Saturday's 50-lap race around the 3.8-mile Las Vegas Strip Circuit is set to begin at 11:00 p.m. ET, with free live streaming available in the United States on Apple TV.
