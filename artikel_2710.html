Jakobe Thomas gave Kirk Cousins No. 8 for nothing (other than wins)

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Raiders arguably could have driven a harder bargain over the trade that sent quarterback Kirk Cousins back to the Vikings. Vikings safety Jakobe Thomas could have driven a harder bargain over the return of Cousins’s preferred number.

Thomas told reporters that he gave Cousins No. 8 without payment.

“No money needed,” Thomas said, via Ben Goessling of the Minneapolis Star Tribune. “I just need him to come here and win games. A lot of fans have his jersey anyway, so they could pull him out of the closet and put him back on. So that’s all it is. Kirko’s got a lot more memories and history in that number than I do, and it’s rightfully his. So I just gave it back to his rightful owner.”

Thomas moved to No. 6, the jersey number he wore at Middle Tennessee State.

It makes sense. Thomas, a third-round pick out of Miami in April, has yet to be active for a regular-season game. Cousins is returning with the goal of winning a lot of games.

Starting with Sunday at Lambeau Field, the site of the final game of his first stint with the Vikings.
