Sixers Bell Ringer: Rough night for all as Sixers crushed by barrage of Celtics triples

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Bell Ringer Standings:Tyrese Maxey – 3.5Jaylen Brown – 2.5VJ Edgecombe – 2Joel Embiid – 1LeBron James – 1Anfernee Simons – 1

Well, that could have gone better. The Philadelphia 76ers were absolutely buried by a barrage of three-pointers from the Boston Celtics and fell 123-101 on Tuesday night.

It was a highlight anticipated matchup of Eastern Conference teams, with Jaylen Brown facing his former squad for the first time as a member of the Sixers. This also meant the return of Paul George, who now sports Celtics green… and he made himself known in this one, to say the least.

It was a rough first half for the Sixers, who looked completely flat offensively and lacked any foundation on defense. They'd manage a bucket, then immediately allow the Celtics to knock down another three. Looking back, Philadelphia were fortunate to only be down 11 points going into halftime. A minor miracle.

The Sixers came out a much better team in the third, finally knocking down some shots and chipping away at the Celtics lead to bring things back even… for a moment.

Boston stayed red hot from beyond the arc, and rebuilt their lead to nine going into the final frame. And they just didn't stop. Three after three after three. They hit nine threes on a run spanning from late in the third into the fourth. It was relentless. Meanwhile, the Sixers didn't score for nearly five whole minutes to start the fourth. Hard to keep a game close with that kind of math going against you!

The Sixers are off Wednesday night and will return on Thursday evening hosting the Toronto Raptors.

Until then, let's get to the Bell Ringer, if we must.

Tyrese Maxey: 24 points, 1 rebound, 4 assists

(Photo by Jesse D. Garrabrant/NBAE via Getty Images)

Tyrese Maxey got his night going quickly with a 3-for-3 start from the floor for eight quick points in the first. He ended up leading the Sixers after their lackluster first half with 12 points on 4-for-6 field goal, 1-for-1 three-point shooting. It was a rough first half for really every Sixer, but his contributions were part of why they were only down a dozen at the break. And he wasn't done. In the third, things really started to heat up for Maxey, who put up another dozen points in the frame with some silky smooth 4-for-5 field goal shooting to help give the Sixers their only real taste of momentum all game, including knocking down two big threes.

It wasn't nearly enough to survive the Celtics absolutely onslaught from long range, but Maxey was the only Sixer to shoot well throughout the night and was critical to the brief, fleeting moment the Sixers looked like they might turn this one around.

Maxey finished leading the Sixers with 24 points on 8-for-12 field goal, 3-for-4 three-point shooting with a rebound and four assists.

(Photo by Jesse D. Garrabrant/NBAE via Getty Images)

Honestly, Dean Wade was one of the only players I thought put in good minutes throughout the entire first half, rather than just a moment here or there. Wade put up some incredible defense on Jayson Tatum at times in the first frame, was grabbing boards left and right and even knocked down a few buckets. At the half, he actually led the Sixers in +/- as a 0. Not bad for a game they trailed by 11 at the time. He got right back to work in the third, putting up more some extremely-respectable defense and adding a triple that briefly tied the game up.

Wade has done this a few games now where he really just does all the little fundamental things right. Even if it's just going up for every defensive board he can get his hands on, or just putting up solid defense that never really shows on basic stat sheet, to simply knocking in a few points when the opportunity presents itself. Wade has been an absolute asset for the Sixers off the bench and he deserves some recognition for it.

Wade finished with eight points and nine rebounds (two offensive). And that's part of what I mean, it hardly sounds like a crazy stat line, but he was truly one of the more consistently productive players for Philadelphia all night.

VJ Edgecombe: 8 points, 4 rebounds, 5 assists, 1 steal, 1 block

(Photo by Jesse D. Garrabrant/NBAE via Getty Images)

Of the rough first half performances from the Sixers, VJ Edgecombe put up one the of the least-rough. Thinking positive, people! The sophomore filled the stat sheet with six points, three rebounds, two assists, one steal and one block by the break. Edgecombe came out to start the second half and hit a truly insane finish through contact from Neemias Queta. It was part of the only little run the Sixers could really string together tonight in the third frame.

The bar was pretty low tonight, so I honestly was giving a nomination to VJ regardless just to show off this play. It was one of the very few exciting moments of the night for Philadelphia. Edgecombe finished with eight points, four rebounds, five assists, a steal and a block.
