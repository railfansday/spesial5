“Wemby Coming for Luka MVP Trophy”, “Statpadding in Garbage Mins”: NBA Fans Divided After Victor Wembanyama Drops 50 Points Against Zion-Less Pelicans

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Victor Wembanyama produced another monster performance as the San Antonio Spurs blew out the New Orleans Pelicans 114-91 on Saturday. Wembanyama posted 50 points, 19 rebounds, four steals, and five blocks.

While some fans downplayed his numbers due to the stature of the Pelicans, who fell to 5-13, others were thrilled to see Wemby's all-around brilliance.

"Wemby coming for Luka MVP trophy," one fan commented.

"Statpadding in garbage mins," another fan claimed.

With Zion Williamson (thigh) and Trey Murphy III (knee) sidelined, the Spurs established control in the first half, taking a 61-45 lead into the break.

In the second half, San Antonio continued to shoot at a high rate, doing enough to maintain its sizable advantage. The Spurs held the Pelicans to 36.7% shooting from the field and 22.9% from beyond the arc.

Helping Wembanyama on the scoring end were Stephon Castle (19 points, six assists), Devin Vassell, and Keldon Johnson, who each put up 10 points. The win keeps the Spurs in step with the Lakers, with whom they share a Western Conference-leading 14-3 record.

In Victor Wembanyama's eyes, he wasn't putting up numbers for the sake of putting up numbers.

In his post-game interview, Wembanyama seemed to take a shot at other NBA players who might have been toying with their stats.

"Unlike many of my peers, I'm not very good at stat-padding."

While other NBA players might have something to say about that, it's hard to argue when the 7-foot-4 star dominates games as thoroughly as he did in the Spurs-Pelicans outing.
