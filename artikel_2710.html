Who do you think will win the Browns vs. Saints game? Reacts survey

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year we ask questions of the most plugged-in Browns fans and fans across the country.

This week, our first question focuses on what you think the outcome of this week's Cleveland Browns vs. New Orleans Saints game will be. It's easy to be down after the team's bad loss to the Steelers last week, but one thing I have preached all season is how the coaching staff seems tuned in to making the right adjustments week-to-week. With that said, a few storylines are proving to be immune to adjustments lately: the Browns' run defense, and Deshaun Watson taking too many sacks. Will those issues be covered up by facing one of the league's worst teams in the Saints? Tell us if you think the Browns or Saints will win, and by what magnitude.

Our other question is the weekly confidence question. I wouldn't blame any Browns fans if they lost a bit of confidence after seeing Cleveland lose their 23rd straight regular season game in Pittsburgh. But, that also should erase the progress this young team has made under Todd Monken. Maybe it's a loss that leaves a sour taste in your mouth for a week, but you remain confident in the direction of the team.
