Broncos suspend RB J.K. Dobbins for one game. Here's why

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Denver Broncos have suspended veteran running back J.K. Dobbins for their Week 12 game against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Sean Payton announced Dobbins' suspension in a news conference on Monday, Nov. 24. He clarified the one-game ban was a "head coach's decision" stemming from "conduct detrimental" to the team but declined to offer further details about the suspension.

"Nope," Payton said, when asked for potential additional information. "I think it's better that way."

Payton further clarified Dobbins would not be available to practice with the team because of his suspension.

And previously, Payton offered during a media availability on Monday, Nov. 23, that Dobbins was "healthy" and remained a key part of the team's plans at the running back position.

"J.K. is going to be super important for what we're doing in this stretch," Payton said when discussing his options in the team's running back room.

Dobbins' suspension comes after the 27-year-old played a season-low one snap against the Las Vegas Raiders. He handled a single carry for no yards during his action while rookie Jonah Coleman handled the bulk of the rushing workload, turning 24 carries into 111 yards and three touchdowns.

Dobbins, who served as Denver's backfield leader in 2025, has played in 10 games thus far during the 2026 season. He has handled 99 carries for 324 yards and three touchdowns but saw his average touches per game drop from 14.8 across the first six weeks to 4.8 over his last four appearances.

Dobbins' suspension will render the Broncos thin at running back ahead of their Week 12 game. He and Coleman were the only running backs on Denver's 53-man roster entering the game against the Steelers, as RJ Harvey and Tyler Badie are both presently on IR, though Badie was designated to return ahead of Week 12.

The Broncos have Cody Schrader and Jaret Patterson as running back options on their practice squad, so one or both could be called up for Friday's game. Schrader would seemingly be in line to serve as Denver's backup for the game after playing in Week 11 against the Raiders and generating three yards on his lone carry.

Either way, Payton did not seem worried about the situation behind Coleman when asked about it during his media availability Monday.

"We'll have depth. We'll let you know," Payton said. "I'm not gonna get into who's playing."
