DEAD OR ALIVE series 30th anniversary trailer; DEAD OR ALIVE 6 Last Round free update now available

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Publisher Koei Tecmo and developer Team NINJA have released a new trailer for the DEAD OR ALIVE series in celebration of its 30th anniversary, as well as a free anniversary update for DEAD OR ALIVE 6 Last Round. The trailer provides a brief glimpse at the February-announced next entry in the series, DEAD OR ALIVE New Project.

The DEAD OR ALIVE series, a 3D fighting game franchise first launched in 1996, has been enjoyed by fans across the world, selling over 11 million units and amassing 23.8 million downloads of the free-to-play version.

To mark DEAD OR ALIVE‘s 30th anniversary, fans can now enjoy a free content update in the series’ most recent entry, DEAD OR ALIVE 6 Last Round. This content update includes anniversary costumes for 10 characters (Kasumi, Ayane, Hayate, Ryu Hayabusa, Marie Rose, Honoka, NiCO, Jann Lee, Zack, and Minato), the 30th Anniversary Specs accessory, which can be equipped by all characters, a new anniversary stage, a 30th anniversary music track, as well as commemorative titles comprised of the anniversary logo, title logos, and series catchphrases.

DEAD OR ALIVE 6 Last Round Core Fighters Free Character Access

Furthermore, players can look forward to free access to 25 additional characters for a limited time in DEAD OR ALIVE 6 Last Round Core Fighters, the free-to-play version of DEAD OR ALIVE 6 Last Round.

Additionally, a special 30th anniversary DEAD OR ALIVE sale is now being offered, with discounts on DEAD OR ALIVE titles as well as downloadable content. Fans can find further information about the 30th anniversary sale on the official sale website.

Fans can also anticipate the DEAD OR ALIVE 6 Last Round Championship, an official international tournament featuring DEAD OR ALIVE 6 Last Round. Beginning in 2027, global players will go head-to-head in Online Qualifiers for one of eight places in the Finals, scheduled to take place in Japan on April 25, 2027. More information about the DEAD OR ALIVE 6 Last Round Championship can be found on the official website.
