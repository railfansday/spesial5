Marriott’s website and app briefly blocked loyalty member log-ins Tuesday. The company says the outage wasn’t a cyberattack. United Airlines and many non-travel companies have suffered various tech disruptions in recent days.

Members of Marriott's loyalty program had trouble signing in Tuesday, with problems affecting access through its website and mobile app.

Outage-tracking service Downdetector first began receiving complaints about login issues from users around 9:15 am ET, and escalated to hundreds of complaints in the following hours. The pace of complaints appeared to be declining after 1 pm.

Marriott Bonvoy members couldn't sign in to Marriott.com or the Bonvoy app for several hours Tuesday, cutting off the login-dependent features that run a modern hotel stay — mobile check-in, digital room keys, messaging with hotel staff, and room-service ordering. Downdetector began logging complaints around 9:15 a.m. ET, reports climbed into the hundreds, and the pace slowed after 1 p.m.; by 2 p.m. Skift's tests found sign-in working again. A Marriott spokesperson said the company resolved a technical issue affecting sign-in on both the website and the app, said teams worked with its technology partners to restore functionality, and said its review found no indication the disruption was cybersecurity-related. The outage landed on a day when Downdetector also showed problems at Gmail, iCloud, and Apple payment services, and CNN reported a National Security Agency communications network went offline; United Airlines, Microsoft, and Chase had tech disruptions over the past week. Marriott is in the middle of a multi-year overhaul of its property-management, central-reservations, and loyalty technology.
