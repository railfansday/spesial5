President Donald Trump has named his pick to run Nvidia after Jensen Huang’s resignation: former Apple chief executive Tim Cook. In a Truth Social post on Thursday, Trump praised Huang for doing a fantastic job and said Cook is the only person who should take his place, calling it a case of “A Legend replaces a Legend.” Cook stepped down as Apple’s CEO earlier this year and now serves as executive chairman of its board. Trump has long praised Cook, highlighting during his first term that the Apple boss called him directly rather than relying on consultants. When Cook’s departure was announced in April, Trump described his career as almost incomparable. Nvidia’s board has not commented on Trump’s suggestion. It has named CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO, while Kalshi traders currently favour Nvidia’s Ian Buck to take the role permanently.
