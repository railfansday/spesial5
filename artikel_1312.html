Everton Announces Partnership With Global Power Technology Brand NOCO

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Everton has announced a new multi-year partnership with global power technology brand NOCO.

The agreement will see the US-headquartered company become Everton’s 'Official Battery Charging Partner', further strengthening the Club’s growing portfolio of international commercial partners.

Founded in Cleveland, Ohio in 1914, NOCO has established itself as an internationally-recognised name in power technology, specialising in battery chargers, portable jump starters, tyre inflators, lithium batteries and related accessories.

With more than 110 years of innovation behind it, the company has developed a significant international presence, with offices and operations spanning North America, Europe, Asia and Australia.

As part of the partnership, NOCO will benefit from prominent brand visibility across Everton’s Hill Dickinson Stadium, with LED perimeter advertising and exposure through the Club’s digital and social media channels.

The agreement will also provide NOCO with opportunities to host international clients and business guests and events at Everton fixtures at Hill Dickinson Stadium, using the Club’s matchday experiences to develop relationships and support its global business network.

Mark Rollings, Chief Partnerships Officer at Everton, said: “We are delighted to welcome NOCO to Everton.

“NOCO is a well-established and respected international business with a proud heritage, a strong reputation for innovation and clear ambitions for continued global growth.

“Securing a brand of NOCO’s standing in a new commercial category is another positive step in the development of our partnership portfolio. It also demonstrates Everton’s growing profile and the opportunities we can offer international businesses looking to build their brand through the global reach of the Premier League.

“NOCO joins an impressive group of globally recognised businesses already working with Everton, and we look forward to building a successful relationship that supports its continued growth.”

The partnership comes at a time of continued international expansion for NOCO, which has strengthened its presence in key overseas markets, including a recent agreement with AUTOBACS Global, one of Japan’s leading automotive retailers.

NOCO was also recently recognised at the 2026 Circana Automotive Aftermarket Performance Awards, winning the Maintenance & Repair category for achieving the highest increase in US market share.

Zachary Koehler, Partnership Marketing Manager at NOCO, said: We’re proud to partner with Everton, a club with a rich history and one of the most passionate fanbases in world football.

“Like Everton, NOCO is built on more than a century of heritage and a drive to keep moving forward. As we continue to grow internationally, the global reach of the Premier League gives us a powerful platform to connect with new audiences, customers, and partners.

“We can’t think of a better way to make our debut than the Merseyside derby, and we look forward to a successful partnership with everyone at Everton."

The agreement will see NOCO branding formally make its debut at Everton’s next home fixture, the Merseyside derby, and further underlines the Club’s growing commercial momentum and international appeal. NOCO joins an expanding portfolio of globally recognised brands associated with Everton, including Pepsi, Budweiser, Castore, Red Bull, Heinz, Nestlé, JYSK and Christopher Ward.
