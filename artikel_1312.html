Premier League: Man City Facing New Charges After Guilty Verdict

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Manchester City are currently going through the appeal process after being found guilty of 114 breaches of the Premier League's financial regulations. But things could still get worse for the Citizens.

The Premier League announced in September that City had been found guilty of artificially inflating sponsorship deals during the nine years from 2009 to 2018.

City have maintained their innocence and the outcome of the appeal process is expected in the coming months. However, The Independent is now reporting that multiple executives want further investigations.

The Premier League are said to be facing a 'dilemma' as English football chiefs are pushing for further investigations into Man City's conduct.

More specifically, they are asking questions about the years that followed 2018. It is believed that directors are questioning whether the inflated sponsorship deals simply stopped in 2018, despite commercial income appearing to remain at the same level.

Manchester City could be in hot water with UEFA if they fail in their appeal against the Premier League's 115 financial charges.

In the original investigation, the Premier League found that Man City and the Abu Dhabi-based sponsors were 'related parties'. If it was proven that was still the case after 2018, then there would be a basis for further sanctions related to the seasons that followed.

The ongoing legal case began in 2023, when the Premier League accused the Manchester-based club of breaking115 financial charges after documents were leaked.

Now, executives don't believe the period after 2018 haven't been addressed adequately. However, the English top flight has taken a stance of not addressing the court case until it has reached a conclusion.

Potential punishments have been widely discussed since the guilty verdict. They have included large fines, transfer bans, titles being stripped, relegation and points deductions.

According to Football Insider, a single-season points deduction is the 'likely' course of action if the charges stick after City's appeal. The number of points is reported to be 'around 40 to 50 points'.

"I don’t know whether it’s this season. I can’t see how that can be this season because of the timing, when they get it through it’ll be too late. "Is it next season? But they are talking about a 60-point deduction."
