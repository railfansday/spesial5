Defense attorney in Tupac Shakur murder trial discovered dead inside his Vegas home

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Attorney Michael Sanft, in court last August during the trial of Duane “Keffe D” Davis, was found dead in his Las Vegas home on Friday. (Daniel Cole/Pool Photo via AP)

The lawyer who defended the man convicted of killing rapper Tupac Shakur was found dead Friday inside his Las Vegas home, a short time after filing an appeal in the case.

Michael Sanft, 55, was the attorney for Duane “Keffe D” Davis, who was found guilty Oct. 13 of first-degree murder in the killing of Shakur. Davis, 63, was sentenced to 40 years to life in prison for being the architect of the drive-by shooting targeting Shakur in 1996 when the rapper was 25 years old, according to the New York Post.

Sanft filed an appeal of the conviction on Oct 30. He had taken Davis’ case pro bono.

Sanft leaves behind two children. The cause of death is pending. Police said Sanft’s death doesn’t appear to be suspicious.
