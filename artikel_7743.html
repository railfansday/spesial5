Ecuadorian National Pleads Guilty to Committing Several Truck Thefts, Including Stealing $100 Million in Jewelry from Brinks Truck

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

An Ecuadorian national pleaded guilty today to committing several thefts, including a July 2022 heist in which approximately $100 million worth of jewelry was stolen from a Brinks semitruck stopped in the Central Valley while it was en route from an international jewelry show in Northern California.
