Vultr is seeking a skilled Senior Technical Program Manager to drive program-level initiatives across our Physical Security organization. You'll partner with security, data center operations, facilities, engineering, and vendors to deliver programs that protect our global infrastructure. This role is hands-on in conversations: you'll join calls, facilitate decisions, and keep cross-functional work moving.

Own end-to-end delivery of physical security programs, such as access control, surveillance, and perimeter security rollouts across data center sites

Lead working sessions, vendor calls, and stakeholder reviews, driving conversations to clear decisions and next steps

Define program scope, milestones, dependencies, and success metrics, and keep them visible to stakeholders

Coordinate security requirements across site build-outs, expansions, and new data center launches

Manage vendor and contractor relationships, including security integrators, guard services, and hardware suppliers

Support compliance and audit readiness (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI) by tracking physical control requirements and remediation items

Identify risks and blockers early and escalate with clear options and recommendations

Report program status to technical and non-technical audiences, including leadership

Build repeatable processes, templates, and operating cadences for the organization

8-10 years of program or project management experience, with 4+ years in a technical program management role

Experience delivering complex, multi-team or multi-site programs

Confident communicator who can lead calls, challenge assumptions, and align senior stakeholders

Strong written skills: concise status updates, program briefs, and decision docs

Comfort with ambiguity and the ability to create structure where none exists

Ability to work independently in a remote environment and partner across time zones

Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with technical teams, operations personnel, executives, vendors, customers, and first responders.

Ability to work independently and make sound operational decisions within established security policies and procedures.

Ability to pass required background checks and obtain applicable site access or clearances.

Willingness to work flexible hours and participate in after-hours security escalations when required.
