Stayd Collective: Cleaning Operations Coordinator

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Schedule: Monday–Friday, 8:00am–5:00pm ET | Occasional weekend coverage as needed

Compensation: USD $1,200–$1,500 / month depending on experience.

About the brandHaven manages cabins and mountain homes in the Great Smoky Mountains and around Knoxville, Tennessee. Steep driveways, hot tubs, wood stoves, and a season that swings from quiet weeks to a full house every weekend. It is a market where property care is guest experience: a cold hot tub is a ruined anniversary, and a turnover that runs late is a family waiting in the driveway.

The roleYou are the person who makes sure every cabin is guest-ready before the next family arrives. You are the main communication point between Haven operations and our Tendwell cleaning teams. Your day starts with the departures, arrivals and cleaning schedule, and from there you follow up with cleaners on progress and ETAs, clear whatever is blocking a turn, coordinate re-cleans and supply fixes, and confirm each home is ready before a same-day check-in.

When a guest checks out late, you work out what it does to the cleaning schedule and tell operations what timing is realistic. It is a role for someone who is highly responsive and calm under same-day pressure. Asking for an update is not the same as getting the home ready. If a turn is yours, you own it until it is confirmed complete.

Where you sitYou sit in Housekeeping & Field Ops and work every day with Tendwell cleaners and Haven’s operations team. This is not a role where you are handed a queue and left alone.

This role is remote and based in LATAM, covering U.S. Eastern Time working hours. Because Stayd runs shared systems across every brand, a process you improve here tends to end up running in six other markets, which is the closest thing we have to a promotion path.
