Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan: A Synthetic Dataset Capturing Japan's Demographics and Financial Context

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

A compound AI approach to Japanese personas grounded in real-world distributions

To address these challenges, Elith and NVIDIA have released Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan, a financial domain extension of the open-source persona dataset "Nemotron-Personas-Japan." To our knowledge, it is the first open persona dataset at million-record scale that pairs Japan’s official statistics with household finance and financial literacy data. Built upon official government statistics, this dataset was developed using NeMo SDG-PGMs, a probabilistic graphical model framework for synthetic data generation (SDG), and NeMo Data Designer, an enterprise synthetic data generation system. Furthermore, to enhance the practical utility of the dataset in financial use cases, it was designed by incorporating insights and feedback provided by leading domestic financial institutions and industry associations, including Sompo Japan, Tokio Marine Holdings, Bank of Fukuoka, Mizuho Financial Group, Meiji Yasuda Life Insurance, and the Financial Data Utilization Association (FDUA).

This dataset expands the environment where Japanese developers and enterprises can develop and evaluate AI tailored to local and industry requirements, without using real customer data. Released under the CC BY 4.0 license, it is available for commercial use. From building conversational agents to customer simulation and model evaluation, it supports practical financial AI development. The Nemotron-Personas collection, including Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan, is designed to seamlessly integrate with the user simulation framework NeMo User Sim, enabling instant execution of simulations drawn from diverse distributions consistent with the real world.

It was built using NVIDIA’s framework for synthetic data generation, NeMo Data Designer. This compound AI system enables complex Jinja templating, Pydantic validation, structured outputs, automated retries, and support for multiple generation backends. Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan is built first for teams creating financial AI in Japan, at banks and insurers and at the startups and integrators that serve them. Each persona carries household finances and financial literacy alongside demographics. Teams can use them to build customer-service agents, evaluate sales conversations/marketing initiatives, run user simulations in NeMo User Sim, and red-team customer-facing AI.

The release also serves anyone building for Japanese users. It holds 9M personas, 50% more than Nemotron-Personas-Japan, across 50 fields instead of 22. That gives any Japanese-language project finer slices of the population to train and test on.

Access to diverse, high-quality training data that reflects real-world populations has long been a challenge in AI development. Enterprise AI development has predominantly relied on private data, creating barriers for researchers, startups, and particularly AI developers in regions with limited available data.

Nemotron-PII-Japan launches the same day. It holds 100,000 synthetic Japanese business documents with labeled personal information, for training and testing detection and redaction models. Its documents are built on Nemotron-Personas-Japan. Use Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan to simulate customers, and Nemotron-PII-Japan to test how your pipeline protects their data.
