Sitting out the storm and going to Shaw’s (Day 159 & 160)

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Because of the looming threat of the Nor’Easter, we decided to not hike today. We spent the day in Rangeley, having brunch at the pub, seeing the Spiderman movie in theaters and generally hanging out.

Not the most adventure packed day today, but it’s not too bad. In hindsight, the storm wasn’t that bad, but even the best weather forecasts didn’t know that beforehand.

Halfway between the equator and the North Pole

This morning is the day we officially skip up towards Shaw’s hiker hostel in Monson, just before the 100 mile wilderness. A lot of the time was spent in a shuttle and making ourselves home.

Shaw’s is probably the most well known and renowned hostel on the AT. I probably don’t have to explain to you that we were very excited to get there! And it is a very nice hostel. But, it’s still just a hostel. Don’t get any crazy expectations. I still can’t beat my dad in chess, but we had a great time regardless!
