Install CashWalk only as a small perk for steps you’d take anyway, if you don’t mind ads and gift cards in place of cash. Every 100 steps earns a coin, and card prices aren’t posted outside the app, so check what a card costs in coins before you count on one.

By our math, a 30-day month of 10,000-step days earns 3,000 coins, worth $3 if a $5 card still costs the 5,000 coins App Store reviewers reported in April 2024. At that pace, your first $5 card takes 50 days.

CashWalk suits walkers who log 10,000 steps most days, collect their coins before midnight each day, and will put up with ads in return for gift cards. Walkers who want cash for the same steps should pick WeWard, which pays by PayPal or Venmo.
