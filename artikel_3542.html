WoW: Forever’s Dungeons Have Gotten Yet Another Big Change

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

World of Warcraft: Forever's dungeons have changed once again, with developer Blizzard's most recent adjustment requiring an emergency hotfix to address players' outcry.

Dungeons, specifically how much experience points players earn from them, have been one of Forever's most debated topics going back to the game's beta. Make them too lucrative, and players will just stay inside dungeons instead of questing out in the world. At the same time, Blizzard doesn't want dungeons to be one-time affairs, with players completing all their quests in a single run and never going back.

Blizzard opened a new front in the dungeon wars on November 13, making a change so that nobody in a dungeon group would receive any experience if enemies were considered "trivial" to to any player in the group. The idea behind this appeared to be an effort to prevent high-level players from running low-level ones through dungeons, in the process curtailing players from selling character "boosting" services to lowbies.

Unfortunately, far too many players playing the game normally were caught in the crossfire. Many of Forever's dungeons have double-digit level ranges, meaning even if a player might find enemies early on in the dungeon trivial, enemies and bosses later on won't be. It also created a scenario where players were leveling up mid-dungeon, only to suddenly find themselves fighting trivial enemies and bricking the experience gains for everyone in the group.

As it turns out, committing to a multi-hour adventure, only to suddenly find nobody is earning experience points and everyone is mad at you because you happened to level up, is not a great social experience. Blizzard was at least quick to admit the change "missed the mark" and made adjustments a day later on November 14.

"With hotfixes that are now live, we've just deployed a further change to this restriction, making it so the XP nullification only kicks in when the high-level player is more than 12 levels above the level of the enemies," Blizzard said in a forum post. 

At least judging from forum comments, that seems to be a more reasonable compromise, even if it still does prevent higher-level players from coming to help their lower-level friends.

It's just the latest chapter in what has become one of the biggest ongoing discussions in the Forever community. During the Forever beta, Blizzard made changes to experience earned from dungeon quests, nerfing them significantly. 

Come launch day, it became apparent Forever's dungeons weren't worth running more than once, as after completing quests, experience earned from kills was extremely low. Blizzard agreed, and issued a change on November 8 to increase the amount of kill experience earned in dungeons by 50%. It was a sizable increase, but is still lower than the experience amount players earned from doing dungeons in WoW Classic.

Forever's launch broke WoW concurrent-player records, and after one week of adventuring through Azeroth once more, classic WoW feels fresh again. Despite the record-breaking launch, Blizzard hasn't released official stats, but fansites have already pieced together the game's most popular class, race, and faction.
