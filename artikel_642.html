Rihanna Shares Adorable BTS Footage With Her 3 Kids on Set for Fenty Holiday Campaign: ‘With the Actual Gifts’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Rih and the Mayers family got a head start on spreading holiday cheer.

It was bring your kids to work day at Fenty. Rihanna had her and A$AP Rocky’s three children (RZA, Riot and Rocki) on set for a holiday-themed campaign for her Fenty Beauty brand and the Grammy-winning superstar posted adorable behind-the-scenes footage from the shoot on Tuesday (Nov. 24).

With a Christmas jingle providing the soundtrack, the heartwarming footage finds the kids causing havoc on set in cute matching pajamas with their momma. Rocki attempts to eat a microphone while Riot believes he’s the next Peter Parker, shooting fake spiderwebs with hilarious sound effects.

“Behind the scenes with the actual gifts,” Rihanna captioned the clip.

The Navy loved seeing the rare footage of Rih in mommy mode, giving a peek into her chaotic family dynamic chasing around her little ones.

“Riri made 3 copies of herself and is just having a blast with them 😂her personal baby dolls,” one fan wrote in her comments on IG.

Another added: “Rihanna got them babies workin so hard they sleepin on set lmfaoooooo!”

Rihanna and Rocky’s children aren’t camera shy and are following in their parents’ footsteps, as all three have graced the covers of magazines before turning 3 years old.

Baby Rocki shared the W magazine cover with Rih in April, proving she gets it from her momma, while Riot Rose notched the first solo cover of the Mayers kids, as he was on six different magazine spreads for Arena HOMME+ earlier in November.

While Rihanna has her hands full with three kids, she’s not ruling out going for baby No. 4 with Rocky. Earlier this year, she hinted about having pregnancy on the mind. “Wait! So I’m not crazy then?” she wrote in response to Love Island alum Montana Rose Brown, who was debating having another child herself.
