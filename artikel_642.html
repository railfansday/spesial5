The film captures the spectacular finale of Lopez’s sold-out Jennifer Lopez: Up All Night Live in Las Vegas residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace, which ran from Dec. 30, 2025, through March 28, 2026. The official trailer, published in September, previews a narration from Lopez as she reflects on growing up watching musicals and how they inspired her career as a singer, actor and dancer. The concert itself showcases the grand finale of her Vegas run, with four acts packed with her most famous hits, musical numbers from Gypsy, West Side Story and Funny Girl, plus covers like Lana Del Rey’s “Young and Beautiful.”
