Terri J. Vaughn Signs With Zero Gravity Management

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Actress, director, producer and creator Terri J. Vaughn has signed with Zero Gravity Management for acting and literary representation.

Her signing with Zero Gravity Management will focus on expanding her acting and directing opportunities while also developing and packaging original film, television and new media projects through the company’s literary division.

She most recently co-created, executive produced and starred in the Netflix comedy series Miss Governor playing Antoinette Dunkerson, Mississippi’s first Black female lieutenant governor. Vaughn co-created the series with Niya Palmer, which was produced Tyler Perry Studios. Perry wrote the first eight episodes of the series before passing the comedy to an all-women writing team to tell the story of a Black woman finding her footing in a political world that isn’t quite ready for her. Her breakout role was starring as Lovita Alizay Jenkin on The Steve Harvey Show, for which she received three NAACP Image Awards for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Comedy Series. Additional credits include UPN’s All of Us, the TBS sitcom Meet the Browns, and HBO’s Insecure. Watch on Deadline

Behind the camera, Vaughn has spent years building her production company, TJV Enterprises, directing, producing and developing film, TV and new media projects while championing opportunities for women and underrepresented storytellers. Her most recent directing credits include two episodes of Hulu’s hit series Reasonable Doubt for Seasons 3 and 4.

“As the landscape of our industry continues to evolve, I’m thrilled to join the Zero Gravity family as I embark on this exciting new chapter of my creative journey.” Vaughn shared. “With their exceptional expertise and global outreach I look forward to expanding possibilities, building meaningful partnerships, and bringing even more compelling stories and characters to life.”

Vaughn will continue to be repped by Lily-Black Entertainment and Strategic Public Relations.
