10 Video Games To Play Before Kicking the Bucket

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Video games were once a niche medium that only the nerdiest of fans would play, but over the years, they have become a global sensation that almost everyone has experienced at some point. Recent titles such as Red Dead Redemption 2 and The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild have further expanded the appeal, but there are still plenty of must-play video games.

With such a vast catalog of games, it can be difficult for a newcomer to get into the medium, which is why this list will feature 10 must-play video games before you leave this world. Based on aspects such as design, gameplay, visuals, narrative, originality, fan opinion, critical acclaim, and overall quality, these are the most essential video games that everyone has to play before they die.

Plenty of renowned sci-fi franchises let players explore worlds, and one of the greatest is Mass Effect, with the second game, Mass Effect 2, standing out in particular. With Commander Shepard presumed dead, he is revived by an organization that wishes for him to go on a mission and investigate a mysterious race of aliens that are capturing colonies of humans.

Mass Effect 2 is a popular game, but not one that the masses would be familiar with. Still, this game has earned its title as a bucket list experience because of its originality and narrative prestige. Mass Effect 2 is a masterclass of worldbuilding, exploring this through the rich history and lore of the characters and races, with every choice the player makes affecting the future and story meaningfully.

The indie genre is one of the fastest-growing, with more and more fans coming to love the passion and creativity put into these games, especially since they are getting better, as proven by Outer Wilds. Stuck in a 22-minute time loop while investigating the lore of a planet, gamers must figure out the history and origins before their time runs out.

Outer Wilds is an underrated video game that is absolutely perfect, doing so with stunning originality. Its unique style of progression is entirely knowledge-based, with players able to solve everything right away if they know how. But it isn't so simple, with the natural progression of knowledge uncovering mechanics, lore, and worldbuilding to create an experience unlike any other that everyone needs to play.

Nintendo is home to some of the most creative video games of all time, and their flagship franchise is Mario. While there are plenty of great games in the franchise, the newest 3D game, Super Mario Odyssey, is the best. When Bowser kidnaps Princess Peach, Mario must travel across many kingdoms in order to stop the wedding and save the princess.

Any 3D Mario game should be on fans' bucket list, but especially Super Mario Odyssey, as this modern masterpiece features the best of the classic style and new formula. The platforming mechanics are polished, plus the controls are fluid and engaging, working together to create an imaginative masterpiece. The result is the definitive platforming experience and further proof that Mario still has the ability not only to entertain but also to challenge boundaries.

As the Resident Evil franchise celebrates its 30th anniversary, it has never been more popular, meaning now is the perfect time to get into it, especially Resident Evil 4, the must-play game of the franchise. When a cult in Spain kidnaps the president's daughter, Leon S. Kennedy travels to the country in order to save her and defeat the cult, which has been taken over by a parasite.

Like many franchises on this list, fans should start with the first one, but that is because they need the context to fully appreciate Resident Evil 4. By reinventing the franchise and steering it toward the action realm, it became more widely regarded and easier for new fans to get into, which is exactly why it is a near-perfect video game that every fan needs to play at least once.

Some of the most popular video games are also the most polarizing, and that is especially true for Fortnite, the defining game of the modern age. As a Battle Royale, players are dropped into an eccentric world full of eclectic characters, powerful weapons, and loot for players to fight over. By shooting and building, gamers battle it out until only one remains.

Fortnite is a polarizing game because, while it is extremely popular with a solid formula and foundation, it is rather simple and gimmicky, which makes it something of an acquired taste. But still, when a game reaches a certain level of fame, it becomes almost an obligation to play. Chances are, your favorite franchise is in Fortnite, and if that isn't enough to try it out, the addictive loop should do it.

It may take a long time to come out, but The Elder Scrolls VI is on the way; in the meantime, gamers have to play The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim. After barely avoiding execution, players create a character and set out on an adventure across the titular province to kill Alduin, the World-Eater, a dragon that is prophesied to destroy the world.

The fantasy genre is arguably the best in video game history, and with so many open-world RPGs, Skyrim stands out as the most essential. It has the perfect balance of classic style and modern polish that is perfect for any gamer, not to mention the worldbuilding, side quests, and sense of discovery are flawless. This blends together to make Skyrim a near-perfect fantasy video game.

Valve is one of the largest video game companies, mainly known for their gaming platform, but they also have a handful of must-play games, like Portal 2. Years after the first game, Chell wakes up in an abandoned scientific facility, using the help of a robot to escape. However, when they accidentally wake GLaDOS up, this task becomes much more difficult.

Video games aren't just about shooting and fighting; some of the most engaging experiences come from solving puzzles, and there isn't a better game than Portal 2 in this regard. The portal gun mechanic is innovative and fun to toy with, adding plenty of imaginative and stimulating puzzles to play through. Portal 2 is one of the most polished gaming experiences and is essential for any gamer.

Rockstar is a famous game company best known for Grand Theft Auto, and after 13 years, the next entry, Grand Theft Auto VI, is finally coming out. Going back to Vice City, Lucia and Jason plan to rise up the ranks from ordinary thieves to underworld kingpins, but the chaotic streets of this city and its eccentric residents make this much harder than it has to be.

Grand Theft Auto VI was just released, but it's already breaking records and living up to the hype. Moreover, and at the very least, it is a cultural event. It is arguably the best video game of 2026, showcasing its unbridled ambition that took over a decade to make. There is so much to do, and the sheer amount of detail and hype add up to the necessity of playing this video game before fans kick the bucket.

As mentioned, Nintendo has a slew of great games, like The Legend of Zelda, and the best in the franchise is The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. After Ganondorf kidnaps Princess Zelda, he is now in possession of two-thirds of the Triforce, meaning it is up to Link to defeat him, which he must do by travelling between past and present to wake the sages up.

The remake is arguably the better version, but the original is regarded as the best video game of all time, and that alone is a good reason to play it. Ocarina of Time redefined the gaming landscape, defining itself as an essential experience because of its innovative mechanics and sense of scale. It is iconic, legendary, and still holds up, proving that gamers need to write it down on their bucket list.

Fortnite is arguably the most popular video game right now, but it has some competition, including Minecraft, which has been in the limelight for over a decade. Gamers are dropped into a procedurally generated world made of blocks, with their goals relying solely on their drive and curiosity. From mining to crafting, building, exploring, and beating the Ender Dragon, there are endless things to do in this game.

More than likely, many fans have already played Minecraft because it is the best-selling video game of all time. But on the off chance that they haven't, this is their reminder to join the masses and experience this gaming sensation. This unique video game became so popular because of the agency it gives the player, along with the plethora of options for them to experience, highlighting the joy that gamers need to have before they kick the bucket.
