Tesla’s Cybercab, with no steering wheel, starts carrying riders in Dallas

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Dallas is the second city for Tesla's two-seat robotaxi, after Austin in September.

A Tesla Cybercab on display at the Auto Zürich show in Zurich in November 2026.

Tesla has started offering rides in its Cybercab in Dallas, the second city after Austin for the two-seater with no steering wheel or pedals. Tesla already runs Model Y robotaxis in Dallas and plans to start delivering Cybercabs to private buyers in Texas in December.

Tesla’s Cybercab, its two-seat robotaxi with no steering wheel or pedals, is now giving rides in Dallas, InsideEVs reported. “Cybercab has arrived in your city,” Tesla’s Robotaxi account posted on X on Thursday.

Dallas is the second city to get the Cybercab, after it began giving rides in Austin in September. Tesla already runs driverless Model Y robotaxis in Dallas. Riders book both in the Tesla Robotaxi app.

Tesla’s robotaxis also run in Houston, Fort Worth, San Antonio, Tampa, Orlando and Miami, mostly with the Model Y. It has focused on Texas and Florida, where the rules are looser than in states like California.

The US road safety agency, NHTSA, is looking into how Tesla decided a car with no wheel or pedals meets US safety rules. Unlike Amazon’s Zoox, Tesla did not ask for an exemption. NHTSA is also reviewing claims that Tesla robotaxis, including Cybercabs, sped in school zones.

Tesla is also starting to sell the Cybercab to some customers for $29,990. Owners must pay a $299 monthly fee, or the car will not run. The first cars should reach buyers in Texas in December.

To build more of them, Tesla plans to wind down Cybertruck output at its Texas plant around mid-2027.
