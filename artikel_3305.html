Princess Of Wales Documentary ‘Heal’ Set At BBC & NBC; Special About Princess’ Three Peaks Challenge & Cancer Journey Gets Release Date & Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Kate Middleton is starring in her first documentary, and it’s a personal one. Heal, which will premiere on the BBC in the UK on November 29 and NBC in the U.S. on November 30, follows the Princess of Wales as she undertakes the National Three Peaks Challenge and reflects on her life as a cancer survivor.

In June, Middleton completed the National Three Peaks Challenge, climbing the highest mountains in Scotland, England and Wales within 24 hours to support The Royal Marsden Cancer Charity. She had a crew filming her for the documentary, the existence of which had not been revealed until now.

In Heal, set against the backdrop of the challenge, Middleton reflects on her cancer experience alongside others who have faced major health issues, discussing the impact serious illness can have on the afflicted person and those around them as well as places and experiences that can help in the healing process while exploring life beyond diagnosis and recovery. Watch on Deadline

You can watch a trailer below. It features Middleton’s brother James, who accompanied her on a climb, and her husband, Prince William, and children George, Charlotte and Louis embracing her at the end of the challenge.

In the U.S., the documentary will run at 8:00 p.m. on NBC and 11:30 p.m. on Bravo on November 30, streaming on Peacock next day. In the UK, it will air on BBC at 6:15 p.m. GMT on November 29. Additionally, Heal is set for release via NBCUniversal on Channel 9 in Australia on December 1 and CTV in Canada on December 7.

Produced by Detail Films, Heal is directed by Benji Jackson. All proceeds from the distribution of the documentary are going to the Royal Marsden Cancer Charity.

This is Middleton’s first time headlining a documentary. Prince William has done it a couple of times, tackling subjects he is passionate about with projects such as Prince William: We Can End Homelessness. He and his brother, Prince Harry, partnered on Diane, Our Mother: Her Life and Legacy almost a decade ago.

Harry and his wife, Meghan Markle, have starred in a number of docuseries under their Netflix deal, most notably Harry & Meghan.
