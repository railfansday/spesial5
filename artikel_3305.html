I’m a Deals Writer, and These Are My Top 10 Tech Deals This Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

As Lifehacker's deals writer, I'm always looking for the best bargains on TVs, speakers, laptops, and other tech. Now that it's Friday, I've gathered together this week's sales highlights, all of which I've vetted using my favorite price-tracking tools to make sure they are actually good deals.

This week, you can already feel the early Black Friday deals heating up with price cuts on the M5 MacBook Air, Bose speakers and headphones, a Samsung OLED TV, and more.

The MacBook Air with the M5 chip comes in both 13-inch and 15-inch sizes, and if you're interested in either, it's a good time to buy: Amazon is selling both right now at a $200 discount. That brings the 13-inch MacBook Air to $1,099.99 (down from $1,299) and the 15-inch MacBook Air to $1,299 (originally $1,499), reaching their lowest prices since the June price hikes.

The WH-1000XM5 headphones were at the top of their class until they were supplanted by the XM6s. On the plus side, by virtue of being older, they're currently discounted to $198 (originally $399.99). These are still incredible headphones in 2026, and with a $200 discount, they are a steal.

Bose’s second-generation QuietComfort Headphones are down to $199 from $359, saving you $160 and bringing them to their lowest price yet. Weighing around 8.1 ounces, they’re among the lightest premium noise-canceling headphones, with generously padded earcups and a cushioned headband that spreads pressure without gripping too tightly. They’re also easy to pack for travel, as the earcups fold and swivel for storage in the included hard-shell carrying case.

The Bose SoundLink Plus hasn’t been on the market for long, but it’s already earned strong reviews—CNET labeled it “the Goldilocks of Bose Bluetooth speakers,” and it’s down to $179 (originally $269). This is a great opportunity to snatch a quality portable speaker with a big discount.

Amazon has cut the price of its latest Fire TV Stick 4K, dropping it to $24.99 from $59.99 and bringing it to its lowest price ever. At this price, it's an affordable way to bring 4K streaming to an older television or give a sluggish smart TV a performance upgrade.

The Sony WH-CH730N are $128 (originally $179), the lowest price they've ever been. They use the same Sony Connect app as the premium headphones; as a result, you get a 10-band slider on the EQ menu, a battery care feature that extends the longevity of your battery life, 50 hours of juice, ANC, Ambient Sound (transparency mode), Multipoint to connect to two devices at once, and Bluetooth 6.0.

I got the Roborock Qrevo CurvX because it seemed like the perfect robot vacuum for me, as I have a dog, a mix of different floor types, and a gym with a workout mat, and it excels at handling them all. Amazon currently has it for $749.99 (originally $1,499.99), a 50% discount, and the lowest price it has ever been.

Samsung’s HW-Q600F soundbar is down to $379.97 at Amazon from $499.99, a $110 discount that brings it to its lowest price ever. This 2025 model sits in the middle of Samsung’s Q-Series lineup and pairs a sleek, angular bar with a subwoofer in a 3.1.2 setup. Dolby Atmos also brings a modest sense of height, and effects track cleanly from left to right. You get a graphic equalizer and sound presets for adjusting the audio to your preferences through the included remote.

The Ultimate Ears Miniroll is down to $49.99 (originally $79.99), the second-lowest it has sold for so far. It’s a small, flat, puck-shaped Bluetooth speaker that weighs just over half a pound, with a removable rubber strap you can attach to a backpack, bike handlebar, or shower caddy. Despite its size, the Miniroll can get surprisingly loud—which PCMag confirms in its review—and at moderate volume, it has enough sound for a kitchen or backyard without sounding thin. As for its battery life, you get about 12 hours of playback before it needs a charge over USB-C, while its IP67 rating protects it from dust and a brief dunk in water.

If you're looking for a premium model, Samsung’s 65-inch S90H OLED TV is down to $1,397.99 from $2,697.99 at launch, saving you over $1,000. The sale price is easier to justify when you consider that this is a new 2026 model. If the 65-inch model does not suit your space, Samsung also sells the S90H in 42-, 48-, 55-, 77-, and 83-inch versions, and those sizes are discounted as well.
