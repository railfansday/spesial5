3 tips for shopping for a mattress for back pain in the Black Friday sales

I've had recurrent lower back pain for over a decade, so I know how much it can impact your ability to stay asleep all night. If you are a shopping for a mattress for back pain in the Black Friday sales, there's a few things to keep in mind. 1. Choose a mattress with lumbar support Your lumbar region is the curve at your lower back. A mattress with enhanced lumbar support will support this natural ‘S’ shape, comfortably filling this gap and distributing your weight evenly. The Saatva RX, Saatva Classic and Helix Midnight Luxe are just a handful of mattresses that support the lumbar beautifully. 2. Opt for the right firmness It’s a common misconception that when it comes to back pain, the firmer the mattress the better. Instead, most chiropractors recommend a medium-firm mattress as the sweet spot between pressure relief and support. However, those who weigh over 230lb or are back or stomach sleepers might find relief from a firmer mattress. Check out our guide on how to choose a mattress for more advice on mattress firmness.

3. Make sure it comes with a sleep trialSometimes the mattress you buy ticks all the right boxes on paper, but doesn’t quite meet your sleep needs. That’s why I always recommend buying a mattress with a sleep trial. Should you buy a mattress from Amazon this Black Friday?

You can buy anything at Amazon, even a new mattress — but should you? I'm going to have a quick scout around to see if any new deals have dropped at Amazon overnight. In the meantime, check out my pros and cons of buying a new mattress from the online marketplace below.

✅Cheap deals: You can often pay less for a mattress on Amazon than if you were to buy it directly from the brand, even if it is only on certain sizes.

✅Faster shipping: The Black Friday sales can cause an increase in demand, leading to shipping delays and back orders. Amazon, on the other hand, is known for their speedy delivery — a huge plus if you need a bed before Christmas.

✅More options: Brands run out of stock or discontinue certain models when revamping their collections. Amazon gives you the chance to buy discontinued models and items that are sold out on the brand’s website.

❌No guaranteed sleep trials or warranties: The beauty of buying directly from a brand is that their benefits are clearly outlined, such as a sleep trial. While Amazon usually offers 90-100 day returns, they don’t offer official trials which allow you to sleep on your purchase for an allotted number of weeks to see if it's right for you.

❌Less reputable: While you can find mattresses and bed toppers from trusted brands at Amazon, there are a lot of cheaper beds from less well known brands that you may be tempted by, but aren’t always the best quality.
