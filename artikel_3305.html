Rihanna Breaks Silence on EJ Johnson Feud Rumors: 'I Just Felt Bad for Him'

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Rihanna is setting the record straight after her viral clap-back at EJ Johnson during Paris Fashion Week ... and said there's no bad blood between the two.

In a video taken outside the L.A. hotspot Celine Saturday night ... Rihanna -- who might be the only girl in the world who doesn't think she intended any drama -- admitted ... "There's no beef."

To prove how much their past drama had been settled, RiRi even said she felt EJ deserves to walk the runway at one of her Fenty shows.

As you know ... the shade went down at PFW last month when EJ complained about Rihanna being late to the Dior show, causing the show to be delayed by an entire hour, saying they "didn't care to wait for her."

Then after a video surfaced of EJ being denied entry into their hotel ... Rihanna commented underneath ... "damn."

We're glad they were able to work, work, work, work, work it out.
