iPhone Duo durability tested on unfolding livestream, now over 260,000 folds

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

CNET built a ‘Fold-a-Phone’ machine that’s been constantly folding and unfolding iPhone Duo for over a week to test durability, while livestreaming daily. Here’s how it’s going.

How durable is the iPhone Duo’s hinge and folding display? Several early reviews tried to answer that question, including in some cases by building machines that constantly folded and unfolded the device.

But perhaps the best test yet is still ongoing.

CNET built a custom ‘Fold-a-Phone’ machine that has been folding and unfolding the iPhone Duo 24 hours per day, 7 days a week for over a week now.

The issue with this is that it is kind of pointless, it is like the IP rating tests - very precise in lab conditions, but of limited value in the real world. As has been shown with all the recent foldables, in labs using machines they can do hundreds of thousands of folds. But in the real world, with varying conditions, dust, being put in and out of pockets, and with humans opening/closing, who do not repeat identically every time, these things will break more often. This isn't Apple related, it is just the reality of all foldables.

It’s livestreaming the process here on YouTube every weekday from 1-5 p.m. ET.

When the livestream wrapped up yesterday, iPhone Duo had hit just over 260,000 folds.

So far, the hinge and crease seem to be holding up extremely well. But CNET says it will continue testing daily until at least November 20 to see if anything might change over time.
