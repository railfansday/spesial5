Sheila Mangubat Dies: ’90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days’ Star Was 35

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Sheila Mangubat, who appeared in TLC’s 90 Day Fiancé franchise, has died. She was 35.

David Dangerfield, whom Mangubat dated on 90 Day Fiance: Before the 90 Days, announced that she died on Saturday in the Philippines, according to her family.

“It breaks my heart to share the news from her family that Sheila passed away on Saturday in the Philippines,” Dangerfield wrote on Instagram. “I am devastated and still trying to process this loss. Please keep her son, her family, and me in your prayers. Thank you for all your love and support.”

In 2023, Sheila and David appeared on Season 6 of Before the 90 Days, having met through an online sign language group. They bonded over the fact that Sheila was hard of hearing and David is deaf, communicating primarily through ASL. Watch on Deadline

David followed Sheila to the Philippines, where she lived with her mother, her father and her son Jhonreil. The season ended with Sheila accepting David’s proposal.
