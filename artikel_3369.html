Every MCU Character Doctor Doom Has Met Before Avengers: Doomsday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom will face off with the Avengers for the first time in Avengers: Doomsday, but he's already crossed paths with several notable characters in the Marvel Cinematic Universe ahead of this showdown. Hailing from Earth-828, Victor von Doom arrives in the Phase 6 event film ready to battle the Avengers, Thunderbolts, X-Men, and Fantastic Four as the multiverse collapses.

The Avengers are accustomed to fighting villains they have little to no familiarity with. Thor was the only original Avenger who knew Loki before The Avengers. Ultron didn't even exist before Tony Stark and Bruce Banner created him. Meanwhile, Thanos was a new threat to everyone but the Guardians of the Galaxy members Nebula and Gamora.

But thanks to Doctor Doom's rising MCU appearance count and confirmed details about his backstory, it has become clear that multiple MCU heroes have met him before Avengers: Doomsday. Some have done so in rather unusual circumstances.

The likes of Thor, Steve Rogers, the X-Men, and more will still first encounter Doom as the movie unfolds, but the following Avengers: Doomsday characters become connection points between the villain's past and his present multiverse-destroying plot.

It is confirmed that Reed Richards (Pedro Pascal) has a long history with Doctor Doom before we even get to see them share the screen in Doomsday. This is due to Victor and Reed coming from the same universe. Their history goes beyond just knowing about the other brilliant mind on their Earth, as they were friends and developed a closeness.

The film's marketing hasn't touched too much on their history, but it has been seen briefly. In one of Doomsday's trailers, Reed screams at Victor, questioning if he is responsible for what has happened. It's clear that the two have crossed paths previously, even if the MCU hasn't shown that.

According to Pascal, Doom and Richards are "bonded in a lot of different ways." As he told Empire, this is rooted in their scientific and mathematical minds. They have an extensive relationship in the comics, going back to their days as college roommates. While it's unknown if that is part of RDJ's Doom's backstory, Pascal teased that the friendship they share makes them "feel less alone in the world."

Considering Reed's history with Doom, it is hardly a surprise that Sue Storm (Vanessa Kirby) also interacted with him previously. While Reed and Victor may have bonded over science, Sue seems to have a better grasp on who he really was.

Her relationship with Doom is strong enough that she's the one who explains to the film's other characters how he used to be. A special look at Doomsday began with Sue telling her new allies, "Victor was always the smartest guy in every room. He used to be different. He used to be kind. He used to be caring. And then, everything he loved was taken from him. I knew he was lost, but I didn't realize that he was broken."

She has also already been seen in the same room as Doom thanks to The Fantastic Four: First Steps' post-credits scene. The two don't talk at all in the brief sequence, but their proximity and the suggested familiarity Doom has with the whole Fantastic Four family reiterate that Sue and Victor are not strangers. In fact, it sounds like they were once close and then grew apart.

Despite his very young age, Franklin Richards is technically the first MCU character to meet Doctor Doom on screen. The Fantastic Four: First Steps showed Reed and Sue's child face-to-face with Victor unmasked, with him even touching his scarred figure that's usually concealed.

The question is whether this is Franklin's first time meeting Doom. The young boy seemingly has no fear of Victor, and Doom is comfortable enough around him to take off his mask and let him touch his face. Perhaps this is because they've met multiple times before this, possibly even before Doom's accident, or the villain's way of making the all-powerful child feel safe with him upon their first encounter.

Whereas Doctor Doom knew members of the Fantastic Four long before Avengers: Doomsday, he made a point to meet Scarlet Witch (Elizabeth Olsen) ahead of time. VisionQuest's post-credits scene showed the powerful sorcerer at the ruins of Mount Wundagore as he resurrected Scarlet Witch after her demise in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness. He awakened her to gain access to her powers.

While the exact placement of this scene within the wider MCU timeline is not yet confirmed, it is clear that this is prior to Doom battling a multiverse full of heroes. Victor is recruiting the Scarlet Witch so he has more power on his team, ensuring he will win Doomsday. It will mark Wanda's second time working for an Avengers movie's main villain, following her original allegiance to Ultron.

Doom meeting Scarlet Witch like this implies that she is one of the Latverian witches supporting him throughout Doomsday. The others could also be recognizable MCU characters he has previously encountered, but so far their identities are not confirmed. We do know that Victor's recruiting didn't start or end with Wanda.

It is confirmed that Victor has already met Bruce and his Hulk alter ego. Avengers Endgame: Encore's post-credits included a look at Bruce's life in isolation after the events of Spider-Man: Brand New Day. However, his self-imposed exile from humanity is interrupted when Doom teleports into the military base. They have a brief exchange before Victor transports Bruce away, seemingly as he transforms into Hulk.

Even though the Russo brothers elected to neither confirm nor deny Hulk's role in Doomsday, it is abundantly clear that Victor and Bruce's first meeting comes before the film. Doom isn't going to take a detour from the main plot to abduct Hulk, and the Russos confirmed Endgame: Encore's new footage was filmed specifically to set up Doomsday. That can't be true if this is meant to setup Avengers: Secret Wars, as some have speculated.

The other giveaway that Doom collects Hulk before Doomsday is the setting. It's expected that the upcoming movie will conclude with the multiverse collapsing and Doom rewriting reality as he deems fit, with Earth-616 destined to be destroyed by an incursion. That has not happened yet if Bruce is still alive and well on Earth.

The MCU has yet to show Johnny Storm (Joseph Quinn) or Ben Grimm (Ebon Moss-Bachrach) interacting with Doom, but it is suggested that they know each other. It would be quite odd for Reed and Sue to have such an extensive history with Victor and for that to never lead to the other Fantastic Four members meeting him.

Of the two, Ben is the much safer bet to have met Doom. Given his age and history with Reed, its likely that Ben would've met Victor many years ago. Depending on the timing of Doom's accident and his relationship with Reed and Sue potentially becoming more distant, there is a chance Johnny didn't cross paths with him.

Even though we've never seen Doom with either of them, all signs point to him having met Johnny and Ben before Avengers: Doomsday.
