Feid Previews New Song With Snoop Dogg at Latin Grammys

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Feid tore up the stage at the 2026 Latin Grammys. The Colombian reggaeton star put on a voltaic performance featuring Divino, Lil Supa, Baby Ranks, and DJ Premier. Feid also took the moment to preview a new song with Snoop Dogg.

Feid made the stage glow in the dark with his neon-colored aesthetic. Feid was up for three awards at this year’s ceremony, including Best Urban Performance and Best Rap/Hip-Hop Song alongside Lil Supa. Surprisingly, he won Best Reggaeton Performance for “Verano Rosa,” his feature with fellow Colombian star and former partner, Karol G. He won his first Latin Grammy back in 2023 for his hopping on Bad Bunny’s “Perro Negro,” which earned the duo Best Reggaeton Performance.

It’s not Feid’s first Latin Recording Academy performance this week. The Colombian artist was a part of the gala celebrating Daddy Yankee as the Latin Grammy’s Person of the Year on Wednesday night. “I’m so happy. It’s crazy to have the opportunity to sing for him in Person of the Year and a song that was the first song that I sang live,” Feid said in Spanish during rehearsals. “I’m so inspired by everything he did throughout his career, his disciple, his hard work, his talent, his success. Aside from his numbers, he broke so many barriers as a Latino in the era that he did it, it’s crazy.”
