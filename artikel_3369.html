Apple's AirPods Pro 3 Are $100 Off During Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Apple AirPods Pro 3 are not only my favorite earbuds I've ever owned, they might also be my favorite headphones of all time. The Pros sound great, come with excellent noise cancellation and transparency modes, and are packed with modern features that go beyond listening to music. I'd recommend these to any Apple user looking for the best overall audio experience at full price, but when they're $100 off, I actively encourage you to buy these now.

On the surface, Apple's third-generation AirPods Pro are among the best ways to listen to audio on your Apple devices. The earbuds sound great: I'm not an audiophile or a headphone reviewer, but to my ears, the Pros deliver excellent sound quality, with plenty of bass and great sound separation. I frequently turn to mine when I want to pay close attention to a new album, or watch a show or movie with intricate sound design. Plus, like other AirPods, the Pros are great for jumping between your various Apple devices. I'm constantly switching between my iPhone, Mac, and Apple TV with mine, and they seamlessly move between each—at least, most of the time.

The noise cancellation on these AirPods is also stellar. I used to take my AirPods Max with me on planes, but lately, I've been fine with just my Pros, since the noise cancellation seemingly blocks just about as much background sound as my over-the-ear headphones. Equally great are both the transparency modes, which pump in outside sounds while you're wearing your AirPods, and Adaptive Transparency, which automatically adjusts the level of transparency based on how loud or soft your surroundings are. This is a great mode for wearing your AirPods in loud environments, so you can still hear everything without the noise becoming overwhelming. To that point, these AirPods are actually FDA-certified hearing aids.

There are plenty of other features available here as well, especially if you have a compatible iPhone. AirPods Pro 3 have a built-in heart rate sensor, so you can track workouts without an Apple Watch; you can translate conversations in real time with Live Translate; "Conversation Awareness" lowers the volume of your audio and turns on transparency mode when you start speaking; and they run for eight hours on one charge with noise cancellation enabled.

This isn't an in-depth list of the AirPods Pro 3's set of features. But suffice it to say, these earbuds are worth their full MSRP of $249. So not only can I wholeheartedly recommend them at $149, but I'd say they're an easy buy if you've been thinking about picking up AirPods for yourself.

Yes, Apple participates in Black Friday, though you may want to compare their sales with other retailers like Best Buy and Walmart. So far, the best Black Friday sale on an Apple product is the M5 MacBook Air on sale for $200 off, the lowest price since the price hike this summer.

Black Friday sales officially begin Friday, November 27, 2026, and run throughout “Cyber Week,” the five-day period that runs from Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday, November 30, 2026. But Black Friday and Cyber Monday dates have expanded as retailers compete for customers. You can get the same Black Friday sales early, and we expect sales to wind down by December 2, 2026.
