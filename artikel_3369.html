HBO Max’s 3-Part Harry Potter Spin-Off Is Officially One of the Most-Watched Shows in the World

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The countdown to HBO's new Harry Potter adaptation continues, with the series now just over one month away. Controversy surrounding the Wizarding World's creator, J.K. Rowling​​​​​​, certainly isn't going away and continues to cast a shadow over the adaptation's debut. Paapa Essiedu, who is portraying Professor Snape in the series, has spoken out once again about Rowling's divisive views, saying, "I don't want to talk about Jo at all. I believe, in my bones, that trans people, queer people, people of all creeds and color have got the right to be treated with respect and dignity and safety at all times."

This follows Essiedu previously signing an open letter criticizing a UK Supreme Court ruling concerning the legal definition of sex, with his involvement in the series, despite having outspoken opinions on this subject, causing much debate online. But all the noise around Harry Potter's big-budget return hasn't stopped the show's newest spin-off from quietly continuing to prove popular.

The first season of Harry Potter: Wizards of Baking, a reality series based on the franchise, scored over 5.2 million total viewers across linear TV in its premiere alone, proving that audiences' hunger for Harry Potter is as strong as their hunger for cake. Now, after a successful second season, the show has returned for a third, which debuted on Wednesday, November 4, at 8 p.m. ET/PT on Food Network and HBO Max. This time, four teams were competing for the most attractive prize yet: The Wizards of Baking Cup and a VIP trip to Warner Bros. Studio Tour Tokyo – The Making of Harry Potter.

Hosts James and Oliver Phelps, who played Fred and George Weasley in the original movies, have found success yet again with the third season of Wizards of Baking. At the time of writing, the reality series ranks in tenth place on the list of the most-watched titles on HBO Max worldwide. The current most-watched show on the platform is The Gilded Age, the lavish costume drama in the midst of a dramatic fourth season.
