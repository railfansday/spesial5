Prime Video’s Military Thriller Masterpiece Officially Hits a Major Streaming Milestone

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

2026 has been a year full of ups and downs for Chris Pratt, who starred in one of the first sci-fi movies of the year in Mercy. Pratt teamed up with Rebecca Ferguson for the AI sci-fi thriller, which bombed at the box office before going on to find redemption as one of the most-watched titles of 2026 on Prime Video.

Pratt found box office redemption thanks to his return as Mario in The Super Mario Galaxy Movie, the sequel that was the first movie of 2026 to hit $1 billion at the global box office. The movie is available to stream on Peacock, and it's also coming to Netflix at the end of the month. Pratt even has a new Apple TV military movie, Way of the Warrior Kid, set to begin streaming later this month.

However, amidst all these new releases, the one that most fans had on their radar for Pratt coming into the year was the second season of The Terminal List. The first season of The Terminal List premiered on Prime Video back in 2022, alongside the first season of Reacher (starring Alan Ritchson), and while Prime Video has managed to release four new seasons of Reacher, The Terminal List only just returned for Season 2 a few weeks ago.

Much of the cause behind the delay can be credited to the debut season of The Terminal List spin-off, Dark Wolf, starring Taylor Kitsch. Many fans have been watching The Terminal List: Dark Wolf throughout the year to prepare for The Terminal List Season 2, and the spin-off series has now been in the Prime Video top 10 for over 200 days in 2026.

The Terminal List is going to continue with a new season, which will be based on the Jack Carr title Savage Son, one of the many books in The Terminal List series centered on James Reece. However, it's unclear at this time if the new season will be developed as the third season of The Terminal List, or simply a new standalone series. Either way, it will likely feature The Terminal List title in some capacity, to help with marketing and familiarity to general audiences.

Check out the first two seasons of The Terminal List and The Terminal List: Dark Wolf on Prime Video and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage on the future of the show.
