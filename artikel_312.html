16 Best Black Friday Headphone Deals (2026): Up to 57% Off

It is a great time to buy a new pair of headphones. Brands have largely finished launching their latest models for the year, and I'm seeing some stellar Black Friday headphone deals on new and older headphones alike. I've been reviewing headphones (and covering Black Friday deals) for more than four years, so I have a good handle on what's worth opening your wallet for. (Spoilers: it's Sony and Bose this year).

All the deals below are WIRED-tested products we stand by, including wireless earbuds and over-ear headphones. For more Black Friday deals, check out our guides to the Best Black Friday TV Deals and the Best Black Friday Mattress Deals.

Apple AirPods Pro 3 are the best wireless earbuds not only for anyone with an iPhone—but also for anyone who wants to keep things simple. They sound great and have some of the best active-noise-canceling (ANC) and transparency modes on the market. They also seamlessly sync with all of your Apple devices. At $100 off, it's the lowest price they've ever been at.

This is the lowest price I’ve ever seen on one of my favorite pairs of wireless earbuds. The Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro have one magic trick: call quality. To put it simply, I sound better talking on calls using these earbuds than any other I’ve tested, and it’s not a close competition. Beyond that, they’re solid all-rounders, with good noise-canceling, sound quality, and versatility.

Want workout earbuds that won't fall out? Get these Beats Powerbeats Pro 2. They're some of the more secure-fitting earbuds I've ever tested. Plus, they have great noise-canceling, transparency, and even built-in heart-rate sensors, so you can track your workout even if you left your fitness tracker at home.

The Nothing Ear (3a) are one of our favorite pairs of earbuds under $100, and 30 percent off is the biggest discount I've ever seen for them so far. They have a semi-transparent design and bold colors that stand out from the crowd (particularly the yellow version). While the automated noise-canceling isn't the best, it's hard to fault them at this new low price.

My main gripe with the Bose QuietComfort Headphones (2nd Gen) is price. At 45 percent off (their lowest price ever), that complaint vanishes, making this a top headphone deal to shop. The headphones are exceedingly comfortable and have excellent ANC—far better than basically every other set of headphones at this new low price of $200.

Did you hear the news? Sony made a version two of their cult-classic Sony WH-1000XM4 headphones. This is the first time they are on sale, and at $200, are one of the best deals this Black Friday. They have outstanding sound, great noise-canceling, a comfortable fit, and a convenient, folding design well-suited for travel.

The Nothing Headphone (1) are a home run in looks and sound, and they are at their lowest price ever ahead of Black Friday. They’re solid noise cancelers with a bold, transparent design on the ear cups that's somehow techy, retro, and futuristic-looking. Among their best features are the on-ear controls, namely the clicky wheel that adjusts volume and the flat rocker paddle that you can push back and forth to move to the previous track or skip ahead, respectively.

The budget Nothing Headphone (a) and premium Nothing Headphone (1) Pro are also on sale, but the Headphone (1) is the most appealing deal in my view.

The more premium XM6 and the newly revived (and cheaper) XM4C have effectively replaced the Sony WH-1000XM5. But I am still seeing the blue colorway of these excellent headphones at the lowest price I've clocked for these older cans. When they launched in 2022, they were our top pick for over-ear headphones. They remain excellent and far outperform other options at this limited price.

Sony recently launched the WH-CN730N to replace these headphones, hence the rock-bottom price of 50 percent off. While these headphones can't compete with most specs of more premium options on this list, they're ultra-lightweight at just 192 grams. That weight plus 35 hours of battery life with ANC on is nothing to scoff at.

AirPods Max 2 are excellent over-ear headphones for Apple fans, but the $550 sticker price is frankly absurd. Thankfully, they are almost always a little bit on sale, including now. This is far from the best deal I've seen on these headphones, and I do expect the price to dip further during Black Friday sales events, so I would add them to my cart and wait to pull the trigger.

My top over-ear headphone pick, the best-in-class Sony WH-1000XM6, is at its lowest price ever this Black Friday. These headphones are amazing across the board, with their high comfort, superior sound quality, and great noise-canceling capability.
