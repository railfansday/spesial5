‘American Doctor’ and ‘You Can See Everything’ Lead 2027 Cinema Eye Honors Film Nominations

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Cinema Eye Honors have revealed nominations for the 20th annual awards ceremony. Leading the pack are Poh Si Teng’s “American Doctor” and Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim’s “You Can See Everything” with seven nominations each.

The A24 Elizabeth Holmes movie also made Cinema Eye Honors history as the first film to be nominated for Nonfiction Feature, Direction, Editing, Production, Cinematography, and Original Score — along with the Audience Choice Prize.

Other films that fared well in nominations for the ceremony honoring outstanding artistic achievement in nonfiction and documentary films and series, which is often a bellwether for the Best Documentary Feature Oscar race, are Sara Dosa’s “Time and Water,” which scored six nominations, and recent Neon acquisition “NAZA,” which earned five nominations. Yuval Abraham and Rachel Szor, directors of the latter film, won at the 2025 Cinema Eye Honors for their previous film “No Other Land,” which won the Oscar for Best Documentary Feature. For individual recognition, “American Doctor” filmmaker Poh Si Teng has the most nominations this year, with five. “You Can See Everything” directors Fielder and Oppenheim received four apiece. Meanwhile, documentarian Jimmy Chin, who earned three nominations for “Everest: The Other Side,” which he co-directed with Elizabeth Chai Vasarhelyi, moves into third on the all-time nominations list with 14 total nods.

Among distributors, HBO, National Geographic and Netflix tied with the most nominations, with each receiving 12 nods across all categories. Neon followed with nine nominations, while A24 and Watermelon Pictures both had seven.

After a first round of voting that saw nonfiction fans worldwide cast tens of thousands of votes for their favorite films, the Audience Choice nominees were announced. The last eight winners of the Best Documentary Feature Oscar— “Mr. Nobody Against Putin,” “No Other Land,” “20 Days in Mariupol,” “Navalny,” “Summer of Soul,” “My Octopus Teacher,” “American Factory,” and “Free Solo”— were all first contenders for the Audience Choice Prize. Second-round voting to determine this year’s winner will take place in December.

In addition to the previously announced Unforgettables Honorees, Broadcast nominees, and the Shorts List, Cinema Eye announced that Steve James’ documentary masterpiece, “Hoop Dreams,” will receive this year’s Legacy Award, presented to a classic nonfiction film that continues to inspire today’s working filmmakers.

This season, Cinema Eye will return to the Times Center in New York City, the home of its second and third editions, for its 20th Awards Ceremony on Monday, January 11, 2027. Cinema Eye Week, which includes events and activities for Cinema Eye nominees and honorees, kicks off in New York on Friday, January 8.

Wednesday, December 2: Shorts List Screenings, DCTV, New York

Saturday, December 5: Shorts List Screenings, Vidiots, Los Angeles

Monday, December 14: Shorts Nominees Announced and Final Audience Choice Voting Opens
