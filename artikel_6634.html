Amelia Dimoldenberg Brought Her Chicken Shop Date Break-Up Tour to Metrograph

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

“All good dates must come to an end.” This is how Amelia Dimoldenberg announced to the world, back in September, that she was signing off from her beloved YouTube series Chicken Shop Date. To officially say goodbye, she gathered a crowd that boasted past dates, friends, New York City crushes, and media personalities at Metrograph.

What started as a youth club project has, over the last 12 years, garnered Dimoldenberg cult status and a devout following. The Londoner’s intentionally awkward and endearing deadpan interviews with stars like Cher, Paul McCartney, Billie Eilish, Jennifer Lawrence, and Daniel Kaluuya weren’t just entertaining, they also successfully re-contextualized the typical celebrity interview setup—with a side of nuggets, chips, and neon lighting in humble fast-food spots for a dose of levity. Whether she was asking Jack Harlow if he could read, matching Alex Consani’s frenetic energy, creating ear-worms with Louis Theroux (who could forget “My money don’t jiggle, jiggle, it folds”), or fueling romantic fan-fic with her off-the-Richter chemistry with Andrew Garfield, Dimoldenberg consistently created memorable viral moments.

The finale is set to premiere in theaters across the U.K. and U.S. on November 24, before dropping on YouTube on November 27. Final-season guests have included Jorja Smith, Noel Gallagher, Nemzzz, Role Model, and Jack O’Connell (a.k.a. Cook from Skins: every millennial’s formative bad-boy pin-up). But on this night, in Metrograph’s theater one, Dimoldenberg let a few people in on the secret before the rest of the world, revealing the last episode and the much-anticipated closing guest.

“It’s bittersweet, but I feel so ready to do the next thing,” Dimoldenberg told Vogue ahead of the screening. Sitting in the empty theater in a sparkling lace dress by Mandalay, she explained that the decision to end the show was 18 months in the making. “It’s been a process to get there. And that’s why I feel like this is more of a celebration. It would be a weird vibe if I was just sad,” she joked.

For the Thursday night outing, attendees including Sean Evans, Richie Shazam, Jon Caramanica, Joe Coscarelli, and Gabby Bryan laughed and made fast friends over dirty martinis, tart margaritas, and Chicken Shop Date-branded bags of popcorn. As Dimoldenberg made the rounds, she lit up and squealed when Harlow sauntered into the room wearing baggy pants and an oatmeal-hued knit pullover. The two embraced with a warm hug, as Mel Ottenberg—who swapped his denim uniform for a slick suit—chatted with comedian Stavros Halkias. Eventually, the giddy crowd began slinking toward their seats for the main event.

“You’ve become something of a celebrity, Amelia,” The New Yorker’s Naomi Fry remarked, in a short panel before the show. “I know, it’s so embarrassing,” Dimoldenberg replied dryly.

The series was aired in its entirety, and as the final episode concluded, there was some unmistakable sniffling, and even some tears. The closing credits gave way to a swell of applause, as cheers for the star filled the room. “I think that final episode had to be about me,” Dimoldenberg confessed earlier. In hindsight, it all made sense. With a rom-com in development, which she’s also featuring in, Dimoldenberg has plans beyond the internet now, hoping to one day direct, too. It was a farewell that not only centered her trajectory as the show’s host but also closed the chapter on this part of her career.

The theater had emptied, and upstairs a party had begun. Guests stretched across leather booths, sipped on cocktails, and excitedly exchanged their reviews of the final episode. The only thing seemingly missing was chicken. Not for long, though, as chicken parm sliders soon arrived. All good dates must come to an end, but this one ended the right way.

Ashley Hamilton, Stewart Fullerton and Halle Robbe
