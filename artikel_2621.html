Thanksgiving Eve football: Packers vs. Rams discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Happy Thanksgiving Eve, Bleeding Green Nation!

The NFL scheduled the Week 12 opener for a Wednesday night, and will feature a matchup between the Green Bay Packers (6-4) on the road against the Los Angeles Rams (6-4).

The Packers lead the all-time series between these teams, 49-46-2, and have won their four most-recent meetings. They last faced off back in 2024, with Green Bay winning on the road in Los Angeles, 24-19.

After missing nearly a full year, Packers edge rusher Micah Parsons is set to make his 2026 debut against the Rams. His return kept getting pushed back, but he was finally activated ahead of the Week 12 matchup, and should be close to 100 percent.
