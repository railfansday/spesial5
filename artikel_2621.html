What Packers teammates are texting each other after Aaron Rodgers’ bombshell interview: ‘None are on 12’s side’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Aaron Rodgers said Mike McCarthy's alleged troubling behavior dated back to their time together in Green Bay — but it appears some former Packers are taking issue with it.

Former Packers receiver James Jones said he is on a group text with teammates of his and Rodgers' and they are far from enthused with the QB's public shaming of their former coach.

While Jones said he did not see what Rodgers claimed while playing under McCarthy, his issue was Rodgers' stunning decision to air his grievances on "The Pat McAfee Show."

Rodgers #12 of the Green Bay Packers talks with James Jones #89 prior to the game against the Chicago Bears at Soldier Field on September 13, 2015 in Chicago, Illinois. Getty Images

"How do you jump on Pat McAfee's show and say all this? Come on, man," Jones said on the "Speakeasy" podcast.

"My text thread is full of texts and group chats with a lot of my teammates right now, and none are on 12's side, especially the ones that know Mike. I don't care if it's the truth. I don't care if he has 15 people bringing him ice cream sandwiches… I would not do this to people that I love [or hate]."

Jones said that Rodgers should have gone directly to McCarthy with his issues — which included allegations that the Steelers coach falls asleep in meetings, is generally unprepared and led the team on a "1, 2, 3 Packers" chant at halftime of their most recent loss to the Eagles.

Pittsburgh Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy speaks at a news conference after an NFL football game against the Cincinnati Bengals in Cincinnati, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/Joshua A. Bickel

The hosts of the show explained that Rodgers and McCarthy had multiple conversations — and confrontations — before Rodgers was benched, went on McAfee and was subsequently cut. Jones, who played under McCarthy from 2007-'13, was unmoved.

"How does that go to this? How do you go on Pat McAfee's show and say all this," Jones said.

He said that while Rodgers is in the twilight of his career that the 63-year-old McCarthy, who is in his first season in Pittsburgh, potentially had a lot of coaching in front him.

Aaron Rodgers on "The Pat McAfee Show" on Nov. 25, 2026. PatMcAfeeShow/X

"Coach Mike is still trying to coach. This could end him," Jones said.

Another former Packers teammate, offensive lineman T.J. Lang, called Rodgers' actions "despicable" and echoed a similar sentiment as Jones about messages between former teammates.

"I've been in a group chat with a whole lot of former teammates that feel the exact same way that I do," Lang said.

While Lang said he is not close with Rodgers, Jones claimed to have a good relationship with his former quarterback.

Green Bay Packers quarterback Aaron Rodgers (12) talks with NFL Network host James Jones during 2021 Training Camp at Ray Nitschke Field on July 31, 2021 in Ashwaubenon, WI. Icon Sportswire via Getty Images

"I love 12. Twelve is my dog. This here wrong," Jones said. "This here is weak to me… When teammates came out and talked about Aaron Rodgers I was pissed at them, too. Because if you are from where I'm from and I got a problem with you and you my dog, I'm going to call you and tell you that.

"I am not going on 'The Pat McAfee Show' to talk bad on my dog. Coach Mike and Aaron have been together for almost 15 years, more than half of his career. The most disappointing thing in all this is AJ Hawk (another former Packer and McAfee co-host) is sitting there, I don't ever remember none of this at the Packers."
