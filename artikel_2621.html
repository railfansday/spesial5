Why Lakers must figure out how to support Luka Doncic late in games

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Luka Dončić continues to dominate as a scorer this season, but the Los Angeles Lakers will have to find a way to support their star player.

Lakers coach JJ Redick referred to Dončić as one of the league's best scorers before a game against the Phoenix Suns on Sunday, a sentiment shared by Giannis Antetokounmpo of the Miami Heat earlier this month.

Dončić nearly produced a double-double with 43 points on 13-of-28 shooting and nine assists for Los Angeles in the 131-123 road victory, living up to his coach's remarks.

Despite having one of the best records in the league (12-2), the third-year coach had other remarks and expectations for his team; he used the recent three-day break to share a 21-page analytics presentation with his players to serve as an internal audit on both offense and defense.

"A lot of good things and a lot of things to clean up," Lakers forward Ziaire Williams told reporters on Nov. 13. "Being first in the (Western Conference), with the stuff that they showed us, you would think we were almost last."

It is the Lakers' eighth straight win, with the last four coming on the road, but one of those aspects of the game that needs to be cleaned up is apparent.

Dončić and Austin Reaves, who was out Sunday due to injury, have proven they can win games together and can also take on a role as the high-scoring threat with the other out of the lineup.

Beyond the two stars, there's some inconsistency with the team's depth to help complement that scoring and allow its superstars an opportunity to rest.

Dončić ushered the Lakers to a quick start once again this season, scoring eight of the team's first 10 unanswered points. He finished the first quarter with 20 of the team's 33 points in the first quarter.

"We're working too hard and we're playing too hard to build these leads," Redick said during the postgame press conference on Sunday. "It's a disservice to each other to take your foot off the gas."

Los Angeles has held the lead in 76.8% of the game time this season, which is the best in the NBA, according to the Lakers. The team also has displayed its offensive dominance during its recent four-game stretch, having led for 92% of the game time.

The Lakers went wire-to-wire on Sunday, leading by as many as 31 points during the game, but allowed the Suns to battle back into the game with 43 points in the final frame.

"I wasn't planning to play in the fourth quarter," Dončić told reporters after the game. "We got to get better at that. I know we won eight in a row, but we got to get better at that.

"Once we get up, we got to stay there. We got to work on that."

Dončić gave credit to the Suns, who played aggressively while Los Angeles started the fourth quarter "a little bit sloppy."

Redick has stated multiple times this week that the Lakers' desire is to work toward being a top 10 defense in the NBA. The Lakers rank near the middle of the pack in terms of defensive rating, allowing close to 114 points per game.

After the game, Redick recalled a moment that could be featured in his next audit for the defense.

"We did a lot of good things through three quarters," Redick explained on Sunday. "... In the fourth, when (the Suns) are walking it up and we don't even talk and get matched up.

"They hit wide-open 3s and get going. You just can't allow that. You have to play for 48 minutes. We are going to need our defense to win us games."

The Lakers will have another chance to work toward Redick's defensive expectations against the Charlotte Hornets on Tuesday, Nov. 17 at 11 p.m. ET (8 p.m. PT).
