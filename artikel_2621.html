Roshon Johnson unable to practice Monday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Chicago Bears running back Roschon Johnson (ankle) was listed as a DNP for Monday's estimated practice ahead of the Week 12 game against the Detroit Lions on Thursday, Nov. 26.

Why it Matters: Johnson, the team's third back behind D'Andre Swift and Kyle Monangai, turned an ankle injury in the Week 11 game against the New Orleans Saints. With Johnson unavailable, and Monangai limited after a return from his own ailment, Salvon Ahmed saw some additional work behind Swift. Johnson is only worth using in deeper fantasy leagues when he is healthy.
