UNC Basketball center Cameron Fens files lawsuit

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The 2026-27 college basketball season is two weeks old, and some eligibility questions are still left to be answered from the offseason. That includes North Carolina center Cameron Fens who has yet to play a game this season for the Tar Heels as he's inelgible.

Fens' case was filed in Tennessee, and after uncertainty over the past three months, he's now taking it to another court.

Per Inside Carolina, Fens has filed for a temporary restraining order and a preliminary injunction against the NCAA in the Orange County Superior Court on Wednesday, according to court documents. The site is reporting that the lawsuit contends that amended NCAA Bylaw 12.6, which sets the five-year period of eligibility, makes Fens eligible by its own terms.

He's hoping that he can receive a decision soon and potentially be ready to play Friday against Marshall. The emergency motion for the temporary restraining order will have a hearing that is scheduled for Thursday morning.

North Carolina should know Fens' decision by the afternoon.

For a team that has struggled with rebounding this season, getting Fens eligible could be huge. It should boost the Tar Heels frontcourt which has really struggled this season.

Fens committed to North Carolina this Summer after spending time with South Dakota.
