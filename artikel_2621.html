Boston Celtics (11-6) at Washington Wizards (7-11) Game #18 11/24/26

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

he Celtics complete their 4 game road trip with a stop in Washington to take on the Wizards. This is the 1st of 4 games between these two teams this season. They will play 2 consecutive games in Boston on December 14 and 16 and they will play a final time in Washington on January 18. The Celtics won the series 3-0 last season and they have won 11 straight games against the Wizards. They are 209-120 overall all time against the Wizards and they are 86-74 all time in games played in Washington.

This is the third NBA Emirates Cup game for the Celtics (2-0) and 4th for the Wizards (2-1). They are part of East Group C, which also includes Chicago, Atlanta and Charlotte. The Celtics beat Chicago and Charlotte. The Celtics will host Atlanta on November 27 to complete group play. The Celtics have to win this game and Friday's game against Atlanta in order to win the group. If they win the group, they will advance to the quarterfinals on December 4 and 5. The Semifinals are December 8 and 9. The Championship is December 11. The Celtics have only made it out of the group stage once, back in 2023.

After 4 straight losses, the Celtics have dropped to 3rd place, 2.5 games behind 1st place Philadelphia and they are tied with 2nd place Atlanta and 4th place New York. They are 10-2 against Eastern Conference opponents. They are 5-5 on the road and 6-4 in their last 10 games. They have lost their last 4 games. The Wizards are 9th in the East, 7.5 games behind 1st place Philadelphia. They are 7-8 against Eastern Conference teams. They are 3-5 at home and 3-7 in their last 10 games. They have won their last 3 games.

The Wizards have made a few changes since last season. They won the draft lottery and chose AJ Dybantsa with the #1 pick. They added Deandre Ayton, Tre Mann, and Khris Middleton via trades. They added Reece Beekman and Felix Okpara on two-way contracts. They lost Leaky Black, Sharife Cooper, Jaden Hardy, Julian Reese, D'Angelo Russell, and Cam Whitmore.

After this game, the Celtics will home for one game against Atlanta before playing at San Antonio and Dallas. Then it's home against Charlotte before the break for the NBA Cup tournament. Then, they play a game at Brooklyn before they have a 7 game home stand against Washington twice, Utah, Detroit, Portland, Miami and Cleveland. Then it's back on the road for a 6 game road trip through Chicago, Utah, Portland, Phoenix, the LA Lakers and the LA Clippers.

After this game, the Wizards will host Orlando before playing at Charlotte and at Miami. Then they break for the NBA Cup tournament after which they will host Charlotte before 2 consecutive games at Boston. Then they will host New York, play at Brooklyn, and host Toronto, Cleveland and Portland. Then they will play at Cleveland and at Toronto.

Since the Celtics had a day off before this game and will have 2 days off before they play Atlanta on Friday, I don't expect any players to be rested. Since Hugo Gonzalez started the last 2 games, I'm going to guess that he will once again start this game. However, Mike Conley started the 2nd half against the Warriors so he may get the start here. Or, Joe may go back to Jordan Walsh. Washington's starters from their game notes are Young, Dybantsa, Sarr, Davis and George.

I don't foresee any players out for rest in this one. Mitchell Robinson will miss his 3rd straight game due to an illness. For the Wizards, Reece Beekman is out due to a right acromioclavicular joint injury. Justin Champagnie is out after undergoing surgery to repair a fractured foot. Tristan Vucevic is out and has yet to suit up this season due to a left hip injury. Jamir Watkins is out after surgery for a torn ACL that he suffered in Summer League.

Key Matchups Jayson Tatum vs Anthony DavisDavis is averaging 25.1 points, 9.9 rebounds, 2.0 assists, 1.2 steals and 1.1 blocks per game. He is shooting 53.3% from the field and 35.1% from beyond the arc. Davis is able to score both in the paint and on the perimeter and the Celtics need to defend him well wherever he is on the court. He is also a good defender and so Tatum will need to work hard to get open and also to find the open man if he is tightly covered. The Celtics also need to keep Davis off the boards.

Derrick White vs Trae YoungYoung is averaging 17.1 points, 2.8 rebounds, 8.8 assists and 1.3 steals per game. He is shooting 36.4% from the field and 32.5% from beyond the arc. Derrick White has been struggling in recent games and hopefully he can find his shot and come out of his slump against Young.

Honorable MentionHugo Gonzalez vs AJ DybantsaDybantsa, the #1 pick in the draft, is averaging 16.5 points, 6.9 rebounds, 2.8 assists, and 1.3 steals per game. He is shooting 42.3% from the field and 23.5% from beyond the arc. The Celtics need to defend him well as he is capable of having a big game. He is also a good rebounder and the Celtics need to keep him off the boards. Hugo may or may not start here.

Keys to the GameDefense – Defense is always the key to winning games. The Celtics are 2nd in the league with a defensive rating of 105.3 The Wizards are 20th with a defensive rating of 115.3. Although the Wizards have an offensive rating of 110.3 (26th), they can get hot at any time if they are not being closely defended. In spite of being 2nd in defense, the Celtics too often slack off in games and allow way too many easy shots. They need to commit to playing lock down defense for 48 minutes with no let up.

Rebound – Rebounding is also a key to winning every game. The Wizards are 19th with 42.7 rebounds per game while the Celtics are 10th with 45.2 rebounds per game. They simply have to fight harder to grab rebounds than the Wizards. The Celtics started the season as one of the best rebounding teams in the league but have slipped in recent games. They are 19th with 31.2 defensive rebounds and 28th in the 4th quarter for defensive rebounds. They allowed the Warriors 19 offensive rebounds, with 5 of them in the 4th quarter, which the Warriors turned into 22 second chance points. The Celtics must put out extra effort to crash the boards in this, and every, game to get back to winning. Move the Ball Carefully – The Celtics are at their best when the ball moves and they make the extra pass. When the ball sticks and players try to do too much, the Celtics struggle. But they have to make careful passes and not get sloppy because the Wizards average 15.8 points off turnovers and 12.4 fast break points per game. The Celtics need to be careful with their passes and ball handling because if they aren't the Wizards will make them pay.

Focus on Making Shots – The Celtics have been struggling to score in recent games, especially from the perimeter. Against the Nuggets they shot 40.8% from the field and 25.0% from three. Against the Kings they shot 33.7% from the field and 22.4% on threes. Against the Warriors, they shot 38.6% from the field and 29.8% from beyond the arc. It may be fatigue or the travel or just a lack of focus, but they have to get their focus back on taking good shots and making them. They are struggling especially on threes but yet they keep shooting, and missing, them. If they continue to shoot 20% on 3's, they need to focus on getting into the paint and not keep shooting bricks from outside.

X-FactorsOn the Road and Fatigue – The Celtics are playing in the final game of a 4 game road trip. The last game of a road trip is always hard to win. They are also playing in the 3rd game in 4 nights so fatigue may come into play. They have to overcome the distractions that come with playing on the road like travel, missing family, an unfamiliar building and a hostile crowd. They have to stay focused on playing Celtics basketball the right way and not on the crowd, the court, or the Wizards' record.

Officiating -Officiating is always an x-factor in every game. Every crew officiates differently. Some call it tight, others let them play. Some favor the home team. Some favor the road team. Some just make bad, head scratching calls. The Celtics need to adjust to how the refs are calling the game and not allow bad calls or no calls to take away their focus on playing the game.
