Utah’s Offense Seals The Deal Against Arizona In Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Utah enters Saturday as the only unbeaten team left in the Big 12, sitting at 9-0 while Arizona fights to keep its own story alive at 7-2. The Utes have surged to the No. 2 spot in the national rankings, a feat that demands attention when they face their conference rival. This matchup defines the weekend for college football fans who want to see if a perfect record can survive a true test.

Ben Portnoy of FOX Sports has made his stance clear on which team will win this high-stakes game. He acknowledges the hard work Arizona head coach Brent Brennan has put in this season. The Wildcats have had a great run, but Portnoy sees a specific flaw in their defense that Utah can exploit.

The difference comes down to the Utah offense and quarterback Devon Dampier, according to Fox Sports. Dampier is a key piece of the puzzle. The Utes need to win to stay in the conversation for the title.

They have won seven games and lost only two, proving they can compete with anyone in the conference. For guiding his team to this point this season, Head coach Brent Brennan merits significantly greater credit.

But the focus on Saturday belongs to the battle between two of the best offenses in the country. The game will test if Arizona's defense can slow down the Utah attack led by Dampier.

While Utah and UCLA focus forward to look to stay perfect after Friday, other teams are trying to recover from recent setbacks. Miami fell to No. 7 after a loss to Notre Dame, and they now host their former quarterback. The Hurricanes want to bounce back, but the Utah game stands out as the most critical for the playoff picture.
