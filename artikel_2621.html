UTEP football fires Scotty Walden after once-promising hire bottoms out

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Once among the youngest head coaches in Division I college football, Scotty Walden now finds himself out of a job.

The 37-year-old Walden was fired by UTEP in the waning stages of a third consecutive losing season, according to multiple reports.

The Miners are winless this season in Mountain West Conference play and against FBS opponents and have gone just 6-28 since Walden took over the program before the 2024 season.

At the time of his hiring, Walden was viewed as one of college football's more intriguing young coaching prospects. He first became a head coach on an interim basis at Southern Miss after the program parted ways with Jay Hopson one game into the 2020 season. Walden went 1-3 before leaving amid uncertainty over whether he would be considered for the full-time job.

He soon landed at FCS program Austin Peay in Clarksville, Tennessee.

Walden produced four winning seasons in as many years with the Governors, highlighted by back-to-back outright or shared conference championships in 2022 and 2023. His final Austin Peay team won nine games and reached the FCS playoffs before losing in the opening round, finishing the season ranked inside the top 15 in both major FCS polls.

But that success never translated to the FBS level.

Walden won three games in his first season at UTEP, then slipped to two victories in 2025. His third season failed to produce the breakthrough the Miners needed.

Now UTEP, which has made just six postseason appearances this century, will begin another coaching search as it pursues its first winning season since 2021.

The change also comes at a cost. Walden was under contract through the end of 2028, according to documents reviewed by USA TODAY Sports, leaving UTEP owing him just more than $1 million.
