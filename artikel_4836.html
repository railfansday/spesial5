Tanggal: 2026-11-12

While a classic ring of evergreens will never go out of style, there’s no shortage of modern takes on traditional winter foliage that will earn you points for both holiday spirit and good taste.

If cleaning up pine needles isn’t your thing, fear not: Faux wreaths are getting closer to the real thing by the season, saving you a little maintenance during an already busy time of year. Choose one accented with velvet ribbons for a touch of nostalgia, or opt for something that looks as though it was plucked straight from a whimsical snowscape, complete with pinecones and berries. And if you’ve historically never considered yourself a “wreath person,” take this as your sign to make this the year you become one.
