The Best Matching Sweat Sets, Tested by Style and Fitness Editors

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Just in time for Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals.

Back in the day, a sweatsuit was all about comfort. It wasn’t so much a fashion staple, but the kind of thing to throw on and lounge in. With Y2K style back in vogue, matching sweatsuits are more commonplace than ever—just with a little less sparkle than the ones previously sported by icons like Paris Hilton and J.Lo in the early 2000s. And, with the rise of athleisure, these simple sweats have gotten the luxury treatment: they’re softer, more flattering, and pricier than ever.

Whether you’re the kind of person who considers it a cozy wardrobe essential, or you’re just looking to treat yourself to something comfortable to wear around the house, there have never been more choices when it comes to the best matching sweatsuits. Since we’re heading into winter (and Black Friday is just around the corner!), Women’s Health editors tested the most popular styles.

Here are our thoughts on the matching sets after months of lounging in them, working from home in them, and even wearing them to work.

Oner Active’s workout clothes have had our hearts at WH for a while. That’s why we were excited to put their loungewear to the test. Associate Health and Fitness editor Addison Aloian wore this set while traveling, chilling at home, and even working behind the scenes on a photoshoot.

“I absolutely love this set from Oner,” she says. She loves it for its comfort, warmth, and versatility (Aloian says she knows she could wear it outside in the chilly NYC winter, layer it under her ski clothes, or pop it on over leggings for her warm-up at the gym). “But the standout feature is the color,” Aloian says. “So many sweat sets are gray or black, but the turquoise is stunning and makes it a standout in my closet when I want a monochromatic moment.” She opted for the “oil blue” color, but Oner also has options like alpine green and toasted red, plus your neutral staples.

It’s also one of the more affordable options we tested—and on sale for half off this Black Friday. But that doesn’t mean it skimps on quality: The material feels super plush since it’s made from 67% cotton and 33% polyester, for a super plush feel that doesn’t pill or shed after one wash.

The set’s one flaw? The bottoms are a little long. At 5 '1, Aloian says she’d love a “short” option as well. Oner does offer a “long” length, though, for the taller girlies.

Old Navy Bounce Fleece Half-Zip Pullover Sweatshirt

Old Navy High-Waisted Bounce Fleece Jogger Sweatpants

In a world of expensive sweat sets, Old Navy’s Bounce Fleece line provides a budget-friendly—and stylish—option. “You’d never know these are super affordable,” says Fitness editor Cori Ritchey. They hold their own with the rest of the pricier sets from their fit to their feel.

The highlight for Ritchey is the material, a mix of polyester, rayon, and spandex. “It moves with you, and the fabric feels like it breathes compared to other sweatsuits that feel too heavy and hot.” One small caveat: Be sure to follow the wash instructions and keep your cycle on cold and gentle, tumble dry low, and keep the garments inside out. Ritchey says she noticed a bit of pilling after the first wash, but says that after following the wash instructions during the next few washes, the set stayed in great shape.

The half-zip and jogger combo makes for a put-together look and is cozy enough for traveling, running errands, or lounging around the house, Ritchey says. The set comes in plenty of neutral colors, plus some fun ones like orchid. It’s also the first on our list that doesn’t have a prominent logo, for anyone who wants a clean-looking set with no branding. Another point for Old Navy: It’s the most size-inclusive on the list. Sizing goes from XS to 4XL, with regular, tall, and petite options.

Alo is another longtime favorite at WH. The brand is known for its cozy, stylish (and premium-priced) loungewear. The fit is what we love most. The set, which comes in over a dozen colors, has an oversized feel, yet still manages to look intentional, says Deputy Features editor Carina Hsieh. “Unlike other sweatsuits, this one doesn’t look or feel unflattering or sloppy,” she says. She especially likes how it falls on her shoulders.

It’s also the “perfect transition-weather sweatsuit.” The French terry material is made up of 65% cotton and 35% polyester, for a “really soft, thick, and high-quality” feel. The fabric doesn’t feel ultra-heavy, though, so it’s a great pick for fall and spring when you might overheat in thicker sets. Another perk? The pants have pockets.

However, the sizing isn’t the most intuitive. Hsieh, who is typically a small or medium in other brands, is an extra-extra small in these pants. The pants also run a bit long. Hsieh says sizing down is likely the way to go for this set.

Aritzia Cozy Sweatfleece Mega Funnel Neck

I had been hearing great things about Aritzia’s Sweatfleece line, and after testing for over a month, I can attest that it’s truly the coziest sweat set I’ve worn. Made from 78% cotton and 22% polyester, the material is soft with a fleece-lined interior. They’ll definitely keep you warm; that fleece inside is perfect for chilly temps. But they don’t feel too heavy. The material is still breathable, and I don’t get overheated when I’m wearing them.

I also love the look of this set, especially with the funnel/mockneck style—which is very on-trend. I have been wearing the suit when I work from home, and it makes me feel put together and chic, even though I’m in the coziest clothes I own. I’m also a runner, and I've noticed that this is the first thing I want to put on after I shower post-run. It’s the ultimate comfy outfit for recovery.

When it comes to sizing, these sweats have a “mega” fit, which means they’re oversized. I still sized up just to make sure mine would be roomy, but learned that I didn’t need to. These give you an oversized fit at your true size. Plus, as a shorter girlie (5’2!), I was thrilled that they came in short, regular, and long lengths. They’re still just the tiniest bit long, so I flip the elastic on each ankle under to shorten the legs. You can’t tell a thing!

Set Active Staple SET Uniform Layered Crewneck

Set Active Staple Straight Leg Sweatpants

This vintage-inspired sweatsuit from Set Active is made from 100% cotton, for those who want to prioritize more natural fibers. “I love how soft they feel,” Hsieh says. “Sometimes 100% cotton feels stiff to me, and I’ve found that cotton sweats in particular lay weirdly at the chest when they’re oversized, but these maintain their plushy softness.”

This set is mid-weight, cozy, and warm. “I love a crewneck and sweatpants to sleep in, but sometimes find other sweats to be too much underneath my many layers of comforter and other blankets. These are the perfect weight to be warm and let me sleep comfortably,” Hsieh says. The crewneck isn't too bulky either—a challenge for a lot of sweatshirts—and fits easily underneath a jacket.

This set comes in tons of bold colorways, with contrasting collars and retro logos on the crewneck—so it’s great for anybody who's into the vintage activewear look. The only con? Hsieh, who is 5’3, found that pants run slightly long.

lululemon Scuba Oversized Full-Zip Hoodie

This lululemon hoodie was named the best zip-up in our guide to the best hoodies for its comfy, classic fit. Where the hoodie shines, according to Associate Style & Commerce Editor Shannen Zitz, the pants are just as impressive. Both stand out for the waffle material: A soft, stretchy blend of cotton, modal, and polyester. She finds it more breathable than some of her buttery-smooth cotton sweats. Another perk? It’s basically immune to pilling.

“Both pieces are incredibly soft and cozy,” Zitz says. “I love the slightly oversized, waist-length fit of the top. It pairs really well with the matching sweatpants, which are equally comfy and have a flattering drape with a straight-leg fit.”

This set styled with a plain white tee underneath and a pair of comfortable sneakers “happens to be my travel fit of choice,” she says. “The hoodie keeps me warm and cozy during cold flights and early morning departure days, but I can easily unzip it or tie it around my waist if I get too warm.”

The Restore hoodie is one of Vuori’s best sellers, and for good reason. “I love the material,” Richey says. “It’s very soft and feels super durable.” The French terry material (a cotton blend that’s smooth on the outside with brushed fleece on the inside)is made of breathable cotton and sturdy polyester.

As far as sweat sets go, this one has a lot of heft. The Restore line is thicker and denser than the label's BlissBlend or DreamKnit lines, making this set incredibly warm with a high-quality, structured feel. “The softness seems to also stay wash after wash, which is a huge win!” Richey adds.

When it comes to fit, the hoodie has a perfectly oversized shape, but you might benefit from sizing up in the bottoms, particularly if you’re thicker-thighed, Ritchey says. The pant comes in short, regular, and long lengths, giving you even more options to find the right fit.

This Spanx set stands out as a lighter-weight and more polished option. “This set feels so light and airy,” says Zitz. Spanx’s Air Essentials is made of their signature Spacer Fabric (a blend of modal, polyester, and elastane), for an ultra-smooth feel with plenty of stretch.

“It’s one of the most comfortable lounge sets I own, but also one of the nicest looking,” Zitz says. The structured half-zip top and tailored (yet roomy) wide-leg pant have an almost dressed-up look, so you can wear the set to lounge or out of the house. It also comes in nearly 20 colors—a range of neutrals to bright options like red, blue, and plum.

There is one downside: Neither the top nor bottom has pockets. While they might take away from the streamlined look, being able to slip your phone or wallet into a small pocket would be a bonus.

Gap VintageSoft Oversized Henley Pullover

Gap VintageSoft Baggy Wide-Leg Sweatpants

Gap certainly holds its own in the loungewear category. Aside from the cozy feel (thanks to a blend of cotton and polyester), it really shines when it comes to style.

“I love a wide-leg sweatpant,” Zitz says. “I find they look less loungey than a jogger, and this pair is roomy without swallowing up my entire frame.” Even better is the henley top: The button-up design and mock neck make this top look more chic than your average crewneck or half-zip. “It’s unlike any other sweatshirt I own in terms of style,” Zitz says.

One area for improvement: The color options. Our tester opted for a soft blue color, but would have preferred a more neutral gray—it just wasn’t in stock due to the limited colorways.

Rhone is another label that the WH fitness and style editors love. I have personally been a fan of their DreamGlow line for a while. It’s warm, but also has a sleek feel to it. There’s no heaviness to this set at all.

Made primarily of polyester and a small amount of elastane, the fabric also contains lyocell, a semi-synthetic fiber that helps to wick moisture away from the body, creating a slightly cooling effect. This set is ideal for anyone who runs hot or for transitional spring or fall weather. I like to wear it around my apartment on work-from-home days or as a travel outfit.

This is also the set for someone who is a little sweatsuit-reluctant. The top has a slightly cropped and boxy fit, while the pants are a barrel-leg shape (a.k.a. a flared mid-leg and tapered ankle). Combined with the ultra-smooth material, this set looks less frumpy than your old-school sweat suits.
