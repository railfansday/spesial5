Weekend Warm-Up: Hurricane Chase

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

This episode of the storm-chasing TV series Storm Rising is titled “High Plains Insanity.” Produced by National Geographic, it follows Reed Timmer and Mike Theiss as they attempt to navigate and document an increasingly unpredictable storm season.

The storm-chasing segments are filmed like an action movie but, as Timmer makes clear, thrills are not the primary objective. As the climate changes and disrupts weather patterns, tornadoes are no longer limited to traditional seasons and ranges.

“That’s why we need to be on the ground directly in the path of these tornadoes,” Timmer explains, “to record data that no one else is getting.”

Driving into a potential tornado in Montana. Photo: Screenshot

With hurricane and tornado seasons now overlapping, the duo is forced to split up to cover the widest possible area and collect the most data. For the tornadoes, this means deploying a probe into the funnel.

Up north in the High Plains of the U.S. West and Midwest, Timmer and his dog Gizmo narrowly miss storm after storm. Gizmo is better company but is a very poor navigator, while Timmer isn’t much better. He constantly makes wrong turns as he tries to navigate, drive, and prepare equipment. He does take the time to explain the basic weather science behind what he is failing to catch, such as the conditions that created the baseball-sized hail pummeling his windshield.

Timmer and Gizmo drive through the aftermath of a powerful tornado in the Black Hills of North Dakota. Photo: Screenshot

In Florida, Theiss sleeps in his car to maintain a constant vigil along the coast. Our current scientific abilities, he explains, allow us to track the movement of hurricane systems with decent accuracy. But predicting their strength is much more difficult. Systems can suddenly turn deadly or fizzle out with equally short notice. In this case, the terrible-looking storm comes to nothing. He heads back up north to rejoin Timmer and Gizmo.

Gizmo buffeted by fierce winds. Photo: Screenshot

The reunited trio prepares for a final effort, complete with a windshield-replacement sequence. They immediately drive into the gathering storm, passing through a hail core which, of course, destroys the new windshield. The Lord giveth and taketh away, et cetera. Soon they’re racing on a near-empty tank, as the powerful storm threatens to touch down at any moment.

It’s their last chance this season to deploy the probe. When, after a long, tense chase, they actually manage to do it, both erupt into cheers. Gizmo appears unmoved, despite the fact that this ground probe, deployed in a storm of this type, in this area, has never been deployed before and is sure to yield valuable scientific data.
