The Eliminated: North Carolina Courage, Denver Summit

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The NWSL’s most decorated franchise and its newest expansion club came so close to playoff berths, and both fell just short.

Decision Day has come and gone in the National Women’s Soccer League, and the playoff picture is set. What should have been the most electric game of the year turned out to be the dullest, as the North Carolina Courage and Denver Summit — both of whom needed to win in order to have a chance of making the postseason — played a grubby 0-0 affair until the last moments of second-half stoppage time, when Kaleigh Kurtz let in an own goal against her former club. Making this all the crueler was final whistle blowing on the Seattle Reign mere seconds later, eliminating both the Courage and the Summit from playoff contention.

On this week’s edition of The Eliminated, we go through the ups and downs of two squads that, barring some bad luck at the wrong time, could have found themselves in the quarterfinals. We’ll compare preseason expectations to final results and underlying performances, using an average of two different projection models: a player-performance-based model from American Soccer Analysis and an historical team performance model from Trevor Larner. Expected points are an average of models from ASA and Opta.
