Terry Bradshaw calls Aaron Rodgers 'strange' and hopes he goes away

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Terry Bradshaw called the Pittsburgh Steelers a classy organization Thursday evening. The Steelers Hall of Famer had much harsher words for Aaron Rodgers.

"Am I surprised? Absolutely not. In my opinion, he's a strange guy anyway," the NFL analyst said during Fox's Thanksgiving pregame show.

Bradshaw, who won four Super Bowls as the Steelers' quarterback from 1970 to 1983, also brought up Rodgers' vow to disappear once he is done playing.

"And I hope this is one time he is telling the truth," Bradshaw said.

Rodgers gave Bradshaw plenty of reason for the spicy comments.

A day earlier, after the Steelers benched him for Mason Rudolph, Rodgers went on "The Pat McAfee Show," accused coach Mike McCarthy of sleeping through team meetings and said he wasn't coming back. The Steelers waived him that afternoon.

Bradshaw said he has been friends with McCarthy for years and didn't know all the details, but he knew enough.

"None of us turn on our teammates," Bradshaw said to the panel of former players with whom he co-hosts. "We don't turn on our coaching. We don't turn on our ownership. We show class on the way out.

Bradshaw had never wanted Rodgers in Pittsburgh. In May 2025, while the Steelers were courting Rodgers, Bradshaw told 103.7 The Buzz in Arkansas that the quarterback should stay in California and "chew on bark."

Now, the Steelers continue their season with Rudolph at quarterback, starting with the Black Friday game against the Broncos. Rodgers' future is up in the air. He's already said on McAfee's show that he's not going to be calling teams searching for new opportunities.

"When this is done, it's Keyser Soze," Rodgers said. "And you won't see me."
