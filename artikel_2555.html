Christian Anderson Jr. goes off as Hornets fall short vs. Memphis

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Following a four-game road trip that resulted in four consecutive losses, the Charlotte Hornets hosted the Memphis Grizzlies Monday night, looking to get back into the win column. Despite missing key players due to injury, both sides were evenly matched, as the Grizzlies entered halftime with a two-point advantage. After the break, the Grizzlies attempted to pull away, stretching their lead out to 12 in the fourth. Although the Hornets hung around, they ran out of time for a comeback, falling 117-111.

Rookie Christian Anderson Jr. was the team's leading scorer, posting a career-high 25 points off the bench on 9-for-12 shooting, including 6-for-8 from 3-point range. Naz Reid, Coby White and Brandon Miller each chipped in 20+ points as well, while Moussa Diabate added a 10-point, 10-rebound double-double.

For the Grizzlies, it was their guards who did the damage, as Ty Jerome (29) and Cam Spencer (24) combined for 53 points. Cedric Coward (16) and Isaiah Stewart (11) were the only other players to reach double-figures.

Looking ahead, the Hornets host the Bulls Wednesday in their final game before a brief Thanksgiving break.
