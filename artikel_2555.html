Jordie Barrett to captain All Blacks for first time in England rematch

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Jordie Barrett will follow in the footsteps of elder brothers Scott and Beauden as he captains New Zealand for the first time in their rematch with England.

Hooker Codie Taylor, who had been deputising for regular skipper Ardie Savea, has been ruled out of the encounter with a tight calf, with the youngest Barrett brother installed as Dave Rennie seeks a response following a record defeat last week.

The All Blacks were beaten 37-12 by England, and are bidding to avoid becoming the first New Zealand side to be beaten by their opposition in three successive contests.

Richie Mo'unga takes over at fly half from Damian McKenzie, who shifts to full-back with Beauden Barrett displaced from the starting side.

Leicester Fainga'anuku partners the captain in midfield with Anton Lienert-Brown another injury absentee, while Anton Segner is the replacement for Tupou Vaa'i on the blindside flank.

Cam Roigard also drops to the bench with Cortez Ratima preferred as Mo'unga's half-back partner.

New Zealand XV: 1 Ethan de Groot, 2 Asafo Aumua, 3 Fletcher Newell; 4 Scott Barrett, 5 Fabian Holland; 6 Anton Segner, 7 Peter Lakai, 8 Wallace Sititi; 9 Cortez Ratima, 10 Richie Mo'unga; 11 Josh Moorby, 12 Jordie Barrett (capt.), 13 Leicester Fainga'anuku, 14 Rieko Ioane; 15 Damian McKenzie.

Replacements: 16 George Bell, 17 Xavier Numia, 18 Pasilio Tosi, 19 Josh Lord, 20 Ethan Blackadder; 21 Cam Roigard, 22 Timoci Tavatavanawai, 23 Beauden Barrett.
