NBA trade rumors 2026-27: Will Anthony Davis get traded? Clippers as sellers.

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

For Thanksgiving, here is, if not a feast, at least an appetizer of NBA trade rumors as we move toward the end of the month.

The answer depends on who you ask. As league sources from other teams have told NBC Sports, they expect Washington to at least entertain offers from other teams — the Wizards need to build long-term around AJ Dybantsa and Davis would bring back a haul (while Trae Young has the most untradable contract in the league right now.

However, it's no lock Davis will end up on the market, reports Jake Fischer of The Stein Line. Davis isn't pushing for it, he's playing at a high level, and if the Wizards look like a playoff team they may not be looking to deal, even though he can be a free agent next summer (Davis has a $62.8 million player option and has said he doesn't want to talk extension during the season). If Davis becomes available, keep an eye on the Toronto Raptors, Golden State Warriors and Atlanta Hawks, Fischer adds.

Maybe this is all posturing by the Wizards. Either way, what is clear is that this conversation is happening close to the trade deadline, not before.

While the parents are away — owner Steve Ballmer and head of basketball operations Lawrence Frank are suspended — GM Trent Redden will play.

Multiple reports say the Clippers are expected to be sellers at the deadline, most recently from Jake Fischer of The Stein Line. LA is willing to move their deep group of veterans — pay particular attention to center Brook Lopez and wing Derrick Jones Jr., who are on expiring deals — to get back picks and young players to pair with the backcourt they like of Darius Garland and rookie Keaton Wagler. Specifically, the cross-town Lakers like Lopez and Jones, reports Brian Windhorst and Tim MacMahon at the Hoop Collective Podcast (the players would love that, then they wouldn't have to move).

Nothing is imminent. With Brandon Ingram back, the 4-12 Clippers want to see if their fortunes turn, but don't be surprised if they are dealing by the deadline.

• One player "widely expected by league personnel to be dealt by the trade deadline" is Brooklyn's Michael Porter Jr., reports Brian Lewis at the New York Post. The one obstacle to trading Porter? He makes $40.8 million this season.

• Denver would like to trade Zeke Nnaji, Fischer reports. Nnaji is under contract for $7.5 million this season with a player option for the same amount next season.

• The Utah Jazz might be a team to watch at the deadline, owner Ryan Smith wants to start winning and the Jazz are not so far, Fischer reports.
