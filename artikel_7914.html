Don't put up with wet feet when you can garden in comfort with a pair of waterproof garden boots. Whatever the size of your budget, we've found the perfect pair for you and your garden style.

My ancient pair of gardening boots sits permanently beside the back door – except when I'm wearing them in the garden, of course. They're waterproof which means I never have to worry about my feet getting wet, whether that's from dew-soaked grass on an early morning garden wander, spray from the hose when I'm watering the veggies, winter snow when I need to fill up the bird feeders, or spring rain when nothing can stop me from spending time outdoors again. And if I need to nip to the garden center, I simply stick my feet beneath the outside tap to wash off the mud, and off I go.

So if you don't already have a pair of waterproof gardening boots among your garden wardrobe, maybe it's time you treated yourself. And if you're searching for memorable Christmas gifts for the gardener in your life, I can't think of a present that would be more happily received than a stylish pair of garden boots. I've rounded up all sorts of waterproof women's gardening boots from lightweight ankle booties to cozy mid-calf rainboots. There are boots with rugged grips if your garden soil sits soggy and non-slip boots that are better for a mostly paved urban plot. So, whether your budget is big or small, and your style is no-nonsense navy or neverending daisies, there's a design to suit.

Here's to enjoying our gardens with a pair of waterproof boots that keep feet dry and comfy!
