What it's about: Based on Enid Blyton’s beloved children’s books, "The Magic Faraway Tree" follows Polly (Claire Foy), Tim (Andrew Garfield) and their three children as they relocate to the English countryside. With no Wi-Fi or electricity, the children explore their new surroundings and discover an enchanted tree inhabited by eccentric characters, including Moonface and Silky. Climbing to its top transports them to extraordinary fantasy worlds, where the family embarks on magical adventures.

Why you should watch it: If you love "Paddington" or "Wonka," this whimsical family adventure deserves a spot on your watchlist. Andrew Garfield and Claire Foy lead a fantastic ensemble, while Nicola Coughlan and Rebecca Ferguson add plenty of personality. Its colorful fantasy worlds, funny characters and playful humor make it a lovely escape for viewers of all ages.
