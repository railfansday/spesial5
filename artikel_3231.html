I love to travel, but I also like to be comfortable. Howdy, I'm Dan, and I cover consumer tech gadgets and outerwear for Tom's Guide. I'm also an avid adventurer (more Rick Steves than Indiana Jones) with a penchant for saying "yes" to pretty much any opportunity to check out new places or learn new things.

This year, I made my first visit to Scandinavia for a behind-the-scenes tour of the cutting-edge MIPS helmet test facility in Stockholm, Sweden. I also ran my first 5K while testing out new Garmin watches in Durham, North Carolina. And I was fortunate enough to cover several international trade shows for TG, including Mobile World Congress in Barcelona, CES in Las Vegas, and IFA in Berlin.

To keep spirits high during even the most head-scratching travel kerfuffles, I turned to my time-tested toolkit of accessories that make even 13+ hour flights (and the days that follow), all-day car rides, epic train delays, etc. feel like the smallest of potatoes.

This list (below) includes everything from $12 inflatable seat cushions that will save your butt to remarkably versatile and durable luggage; also, the best cheap (and splurge-worthy) entertainment devices and even my favorite fancy-yet-thrifty travel slippers. Happy trails!
