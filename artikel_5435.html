Fundstrat co-founder and BitMine chairman Tom Lee expects the price of Ethereum to bottom shortly before the Nasdaq debut of Anthropic. "If ETH follows the same path as the SPCX IPO, ETH bottoms shortly before the ANT IPO," Lee wrote on X.

Lee shared a Bloomberg and Fundstrat chart that compares the price of ETH ahead of the SpaceX IPO in June with the current decline. According to the chart, ETH lost 39% over eight weeks before the SpaceX offering, including 29% in the final two weeks. Ahead of the Anthropic IPO, ETH has also lost 39% over two months, including 28% in the last two weeks.

SpaceX raised a record $75 billion in its IPO in June. The bottom for ETH came three trading days before SpaceX priced its shares, and the price of ETH went up after that.

Anthropic is set to price its IPO on Nov. 18 and aims to raise up to $64.4 billion. It would be the second-largest IPO in history after SpaceX.

Lee blamed the decline of ETH on a rotation of money into the Anthropic IPO, and Binance founder Changpeng Zhao and Strive CEO Matt Cole have pointed to the same flow of liquidity. On Monday, ETH is trading at $1,823.

BitMine Immersion Technologies (BMNR), which Lee chairs, has bought ETH every week since it launched its treasury strategy on June 30, 2025. In its Nov. 16 update, the company disclosed fresh numbers: it added 7,653 ETH to its reserves last week and 9,587 ETH the week before.

BitMine now holds 6,077,745 ETH, or 4.97% of all coins in circulation (about 122.2 million ETH), and it is the world's largest corporate Ethereum treasury. According to the company, it is 99% of the way through its "Alchemy of 5%" program, and fewer than 35,000 ETH are left to reach the target.

The total portfolio of BitMine, which includes crypto, cash, securities and venture stakes, has shrunk from a peak of more than $14 billion to $11.8 billion. According to BitMine, 5,227,037 ETH, or around 86% of the reserve, is staked.

Lee expects the growth cycle for ETH to last two to three years, driven by bank tokenization, and his base-case targets for ETH range from $12,000 to $22,000.
