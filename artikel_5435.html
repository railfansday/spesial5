NEW DELHI, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- SunTec India today announced the appointment of a full-time Chief Data Security Officer (CDSO), creating a single accountable owner for data security across project delivery, IT infrastructure, and third-party audits.

Security Before Scope: What Client Inquiries Revealed

An analysis of inbound inquiries revealed a consistent pattern: before discussing scope, timelines, or budgets, most prospective clients first want to know how their data will be protected. Only once those concerns are satisfied does the conversation move to project requirements.

SunTec India has maintained ISO 9001, ISO 27001, SOC 2, and HIPAA compliance for more than 15 years and is currently certified to ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 27001:2022, with SOC 2 Type II attestation. Given the weight clients place on data security, the company's management unanimously decided to elevate it from a shared compliance function to a full-time executive mandate.
