Raid: Shadow Legends lets you know what is best in life with its Conan the Barbarian collaboration

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

For whatever reason, Robert E. Howard's Conan has come back into vogue for mobile publishers recently. First, UnGodly saw the original Conan announced, and now Raid: Shadow Legends has kicked off its own collab featuring none other than Schwarzenegger's interpretation of Conan the Barbarian from 1982.

Kicking off today and running through February 23rd, Raid: Shadow Legends' Conan the Barbarian collaboration features Conan himself, of course. But you can also claim Valeria, the heroic swordswoman who accompanies Conan in the film, as a free character just for logging in to add a bit of Hyborian flair to your roster.

Personally, I've had a bit more fondness for the original short stories featuring Conan than the 1982 film. But I can certainly appreciate its appeal, and Conan's hack-and-slash attitude fits very well in the world of Raid: Shadow Legends.

That being said, it's not hard to see why some people aren't the biggest fans of Raid: Shadow Legends, since gating off such an exciting collab behind a pass, especially with only one character in it, seems a little exploitative. But if you do fancy giving it a go, you can jump in and experience the collab for yourself until February 23rd.
