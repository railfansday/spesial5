Verizon&#8217;s Latest Heartthrob Is Jonathan Bailey as a Sexy Scrooge

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Verizon is serving up hot deals and a sexy Scrooge this holiday season.

Jonathan Bailey, Bridgerton star and People’s 2025 Sexiest Man Alive, plays Ebenezer Scrooge in “A Verizon Christmas Carol.” He’s joined by actress and former Saturday Night Live cast member Ego Nwodim, who plays the Ghost of Holiday Present.

The spot opens on Scrooge in a candlelit Victorian bedroom. The ghost suddenly appears and is promptly distracted by his good looks. “You didn’t tell me you were the Sexiest Man Alive?” she says.

“I’m not anymore,” Scrooge replies begrudgingly.

She then shows him Verizon’s holiday deals, which she says are “so hot they may even melt your cold heart.”
