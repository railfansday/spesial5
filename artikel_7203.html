Upstart: Director, Internal Communications

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

At Upstart, we’re united by a mission that matters: to radically reduce the cost and complexity of borrowing for all Americans. Every day, we bring creativity, experimentation, and advanced AI to reshape access to credit, helping millions move forward financially with clarity and confidence.

As the leading AI lending marketplace, we partner with banks and credit unions to expand access to affordable credit through technology that’s both radically intelligent and deeply human. Our platform runs over one million predictions per borrower using more than 3,000 signals, powering smarter, fairer decisions for millions of customers. But the numbers only hint at the impact. Every idea, every voice, and every contribution moves us closer to a world where credit never stands between people and their financial progress.

We’re proudly digital-first, giving most Upstarters the flexibility to do their best work from wherever they thrive, alongside teammates across 80+ cities in the US and Canada. Digital-first doesn’t mean distant. We’re intentional about in-person connection through team onsites, planning sessions, and moments that spark creativity and trust. And whether you choose to work primarily from home or collaborate in-person from one of our offices in Columbus, Austin, the Bay Area, or New York City, you’ll have the support to work in the way that works best for you.

If you’re energized by tackling meaningful problems, excited to innovate with purpose, and motivated by work that truly matters, we’d love to hear from you.

Upstart is looking for a Director of Internal Communications to evolve how employees understand the business we are building, how the company is performing and what our next chapter requires from them. Internal communications here is not a distribution function. It is a strategic capability that connects company decisions to our strategy, creates clarity during consequential moments and helps employees act with confidence.

You will join Upstart’s Communications, Culture & Development team, which helps employees understand where the company is going, feel connected to its purpose and build the capabilities to do their best work. You will evolve the strategy and operating rhythm for enterprise communications, serve as a senior partner to executives and functional leaders, and independently lead some of Upstart’s most consequential employee-facing work.

You will also own Upstart’s company knowledge strategy and core internal content portfolio, ensuring employees can find clear, current and trusted information across Guru and Glean. As our ways of working evolve, you will bring greater depth and thought leadership to our employee-facing GenAI communications and enablement strategy.

This is a role for a senior player-coach who sees beyond the immediate request, brings a strong point of view and stays close enough to the work to make the strategy real.

Position Location - This role is available in the following locations: Remote, Burlingame, CA, and Columbus, OH

Time Zone Requirements - This team operates on the East/West Coast time zones.

Travel Requirements - As a digital first company, the majority of your work can be accomplished remotely. The majority of our employees can live and work anywhere in the U.S but are encouraged to to still spend high quality time in-person collaborating via regular onsites. The in-person sessions’ cadence varies, but in this role you can expect to travel 1-2 times per quarter.
