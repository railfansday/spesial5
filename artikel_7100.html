Vultr is seeking a Principal Engineer, Network Security to build the infrastructure and policy framework that detects and stops abuse on the Vultr network. This is a key role which sits at the intersection between network engineering, security, and distributed systems, working cross-functionally to drive successful outcomes for the business. You will design and lead systems that find intrusion activity, botnet command-and-contro, spam, and proxy farming across our global footprint, using our flow platform and AI/ML-based analytics. You will also strengthen our DDoS defenses by developing sensors, detection analytics, and backbone best practices that work with our scrubbing infrastructure. We are looking for someone who has built network abuse detection at a large service provider or hyperscaler. Your work protects the reputation of Vultr IP space and ASN, and gives enterprise and AI customers proof that their network is protected. This is your opportunity to join our fast growing team and build network security that customers can see and trust.

Architect and build a comprehensive network abuse detection platform covering intrusion activity, botnet C2, outbound spam, scanning, proxy farming, and compromised or malicious tenant workloads

Develop detections from flow telemetry (NetFlow/IPFIX/sFlow), BGP, DNS, and platform metadata; design for a distributed, large-scale, backbone network with hundreds of egress points

Apply AI/ML and statistical methods (anomaly detection, behavioral baselining, graph analysis, classification) to find abuse at scale, with clear handling of false positive and false negative trade-offs

Define the abuse and security policy framework: detection thresholds, automated response actions (rate limiting, null routing, Flowspec, port blocking, tenant suspension), escalation paths, and appeal processes

Build DDoS sensors and detection analytics that feed our scrubbing infrastructure; improve time-to-detect and time-to-mitigate for volumetric and application-layer attacks

Set backbone network security best practices, such as RTBH, BGP Flowspec, uRPF/BCP38 anti-spoofing, RPKI/ROV, control-plane protection, and edge ACL standards

Integrate threat intelligence feeds, IP reputation sources, and abuse reports into automated detection and response workflows

Protect and restore the reputation of Vultr IP space; work with email providers, CDNs, and blocklist operators to delist legitimate customers quickly and programmatically

Reduce manual work for the abuse and support teams through automation, tooling, and clear signals they can act on

Identify and build revenue-generating security features, such as customer-facing DDoS and threat visibility, security add-ons, and compliance evidence for enterprise and AI customers

Mentor engineers, lead design reviews, and partner with Network Engineering, Operations, Trust & Safety, Legal, and Product

10+ years in network security or infrastructure engineering, including 5+ years building abuse, threat, or DDoS detection at a large service provider, CDN, or hyperscaler

Proven track record of detecting botnet C2, spam, scanning, proxy/residential-proxy abuse, and compromised hosts from flow and metadata at scale

Expert in flow telemetry (NetFlow, IPFIX, sFlow) and its limits, including sampling, and in using it for security analytics without relying on full packet capture

Deep knowledge of DDoS attack types and mitigation: volumetric, protocol, and application-layer attacks; scrubbing center architecture; RTBH and BGP Flowspec

Strong grasp of IP networking and routing security, including BGP, RPKI/ROV, uRPF/BCP38, MPLS, Segment Routing, and Traffic Engineering; SLOs and SLAs; peering and transit

Experience applying ML and data science to security problems, such as anomaly detection, clustering, and supervised classification, and running them in production

Strong programming skills in Go, Python, Rust, or similar, with experience building high-volume data pipelines (Kafka, ClickHouse, or similar) and real-time detection services

Familiarity with threat intelligence, IP reputation and blocklist ecosystems

Strong experience working cross-functionally with peers in other teams and functions, driving successful outcomes

Excellent written and verbal communication - able to produce crisp briefs for executives and detailed design docs for engineering teams

Willingness to take part in escalations for major security incidents
