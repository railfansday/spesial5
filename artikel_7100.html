Virtual Peace of Mind: Licensed Clinician | VPM $1500 Sign On Bonus

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Job Title: Licensed Therapist/Counselor 
Employment Type: (W-2)  10-30 client hours/week
Location: Remote (U.S.)


Important — Insurance Paneling: This is a licensed position. Before starting, all licensed clinicians must be credentialed (paneled) with VPM's insurance providers. The paneling process can take up to three months. We are looking for clinicians who are comfortable applying now and joining once paneling is complete.

Virtual Peace of Mind is looking for a 16-month post-insurance-paneling commitment. VPM will make an initial investment to panel all its clinicians. Due to this upfront cost, there is a required 16-month commitment to work at Virtual Peace of Mind, beginning on the date paneling is complete.


Scheduling & Availability (ET): This is a full-time role at 30 client hours per week. Clinicians set their own schedules — sessions can be held anytime, days, evenings, or weekends, based on clinician availability and client need.


Clinician Availability: You provide your weekly availability upfront, and VPM assigns cases to fill it. Sessions can be scheduled anytime — days, evenings, or weekends — based on clinician availability. Clinicians will build to a full caseload of 30 client hours per week.


Applicant Note: Please do not apply for this position if you are unavailable to attend onboarding on your date of hire. Onboarding is required on day one — four hours starting at 9:00 AM (2 hours organizational onboarding + 2 hours documentation onboarding) — and will most likely fall during your current job's business hours. Onboarding hours are paid. If you are unable to take off during business hours, this is not the job for you.


Compensation: $60–$65/hr for scheduled client sessions (rate based on experience/credentials).

Sign-on bonus: $1,500. Payment schedule and terms are provided in the offer letter.


About Virtual Peace of Mind (VPM)

Virtual Peace of Mind (VPM) is a fast-growing, fully remote mental health practice dedicated to providing evidence-based mental health services to children, adults, and families across multiple states. Our mission is to expand equitable access to mental health services through high-quality virtual care — and to support the clinicians and professionals who make that care possible.

We're building a culture that is mission-driven, accountable, supportive, and built for sustainable remote work.


Why Join VPM

At VPM, you'll be part of a team transforming access to mental health through virtual care — with a remote model designed to support excellent work and real life.


What you can expect:

  • Mission-Driven Impact: Contribute to meaningful work improving access and outcomes for youth and families.
  • Remote by Design: Fully remote environment with clear expectations and supportive infrastructure.
  • Collaborative Culture: Work with a multidisciplinary team that values integrity, empathy, and shared learning.
  • Growth & Leadership Pathways: Opportunities to expand scope and take on leadership or consulting responsibilities as we scale.
  • Professional Development: Access to training, resources, and continued learning opportunities aligned to role scope.
  • Diverse Caseloads: Work with children, youth, adults, and families across a range of needs and backgrounds.


Role Overview:

VPM is seeking Licensed Therapists to provide tele-therapy to children, adults, and families across multiple states.

This position focuses on delivering culturally responsive, evidence-based therapeutic services through a secure, HIPAA-compliant virtual platform. Therapists will play an essential role in expanding access to care for clients regardless of insurance coverage, immigration status, or language barriers.


Ideal Candidate Profile:

We are seeking clinicians who:

  • Have the real capacity to carry a full-time caseload of 30 client hours per week.
  • Strong on documentation — completes clinical notes within 24 hours.
  • Are looking for a home, not another stop — they want to settle in and provide compassionate care, not move from role to role.
  • Are self-directed and organized, with a strong virtual presence in a fully remote setting.

Licensed clinicians will have opportunities for leadership and program development within our expanding clinical framework.


Who This Role Is Not For:

This role may not be the right fit for clinicians who:

  • Cannot commit to a full-time caseload of 30 client hours per week.
  • Tend to say yes to more than they can realistically carry.
  • Are looking for a short-term stop rather than a place to settle in.
  • Struggle to complete clinical notes within 24 hours.
  • Are not comfortable working fully remote and independently.
  • Are not comfortable waiting for insurance paneling to complete before starting.


Key responsibilities include but limited to:

Direct Client Care

  • Deliver individual tele-therapy sessions to children, youth, adults, and families
  • Utilize evidence-based modalities such as CBT, DBT, and solution-focused therapy
  • Address a range of concerns including anxiety, grief, trauma, academic stress, and family or peer conflict
  • Provide services to diverse client populations, including multilingual clients and those without insurance


Collaboration & Communication

  • Coordinate with internal clinical teams and administrative staff
  • Engage in family outreach when appropriate to promote continuity of care
  • Participate in case consultations, supervision, and ongoing professional development
  • Maintain responsive and professional communication with internal VPM partners
  • Check VPM email daily for changes, updates, or cancellations from clients and VPM, and respond promptly


Documentation & Compliance

  • Complete all clinical documentation (intake, progress, and discharge notes) within 24 hours
  • Ensure compliance with all licensure board requirements, HIPAA, and program-specific standards
  • Contribute to quarterly outcomes reporting through standardized metrics and case summaries
  • Other duties as assigned.


Qualifications & Preferred Experience:

Licensure

  • Active Virginia license as LPC, LCSW, LMFT, or equivalent


Experience

  • Minimum of 1 year of clinical experience
  • Prior experience in therapy, community mental health, or tele-health preferred
  • Experience with trauma-informed care and crisis response a plus


Skills & Competencies

  • Proficiency in evidence-based interventions (CBT, DBT, solution-focused, narrative therapy)
  • Strong time management, organization, and documentation skills
  • Commitment to culturally responsive, strengths-based, and trauma-informed care
  • Self-directed with strong virtual presence and professionalism in remote settings

What the Employment Process Looks Like

  1. Submit an application.
  2. If there is interest, get a response from VPM for an initial interview.
  3. Receive notification to participate in a 15-minute AI demonstrative session.
  4. Receive notification to build a schedule to demonstrate availability and capacity.
  5. Meet with the clinical staff for a second interview.
  6. If selected, receive an offer of employment.
  7. Complete background check and license verification.
  8. Complete insurance paneling (up to 3 months).
  9. Attend paid day-one onboarding (4 hours, starting at 9:00 AM).
  10. Begin seeing clients and build to a full caseload of 30 client hours per week.
