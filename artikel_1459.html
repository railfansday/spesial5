The night that several streaks ended: All the key stats from Olympiacos 2-4 AC Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

AC Milan produced a brilliant second-half comeback to overhaul a two-goal deficit and beat Olympiacos 4-2 in the Europa League.

Milan trailed 2-0 after 50 minutes, but Omari Hutchinson was not in the mood to taste more European misery. He scored a brilliant individual goal to get the Rossoneri back into it, before setting up Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic to complete the comeback.

Three goals in 15 crazy minutes overturned the deficit and Diego Moreira made sure of the points late on, helping Ruben Amorim’s side jump into the play-off spots with three games and nine more points up for grabs.

The general stats makes for interesting reading, as Milan had 59% of the ball and generated 2.25 Expected Goals compared to the 1.56 that their hosts did. They also had 4 big chances to 2, which of course is exactly what the scoreboard reads too.

Yet, Olympiacos outshot the Rossoneri 18 to 12, but they had only 5 on target to the visitors’ 6. The Greek side did generate 6 corners to 1, scoring from their first.

Last night brought a fourth goal in the Europa League for Alphadjo Cisse, who at the age of 20 years and 35 days has become the youngest player in Milan’s history to score three goals in the major European competitions.

For the first time in their history, Milan have conceded at least two goals in three consecutive matches in Europe’s top competitions.

Olympiakos had lost just one of their last 13 UEFA Europa League matches (W8 D4) before last night, keeping nine clean sheets. In the group stages, they were unbeaten in 11 games (W7 D4), conceding just twice.

The Greek side had also won each of their two previous Europa League contests at the Stadio Georgios Karaiskákis this season, and they had a six-match home unbeaten streak in all competitions broken.

Milan had never won a competitive game against a Greek side in Greece, losing 2 and drawing 4. However, they have lifted the Champions League trophy twice in Athens (4-0 vs. Barcelona in 1994 and 2-1 vs. Liverpool in 2007).

The Rossoneri were scoreless in their last 198 minutes in the competition going into the game in the Europa League, and hadn’t found the net since Gonçalo Ramos’ goal against RB Salzburg. By the time of Hutchinson’s goal, that had reached 254 minutes.
