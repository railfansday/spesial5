Fermin Lopez does better than Diego Maradona

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

This weekend, FC Barcelona offered a shock against Atlético de Madrid. A 2-1 victory which consolidates the Catalan club’s leadership in La Liga. Author of a double in the match, Fermin Lopez burst the screen. The attacking midfielder once again showed that he is decisive in important moments and has 8 goals and 7 assists in 15 matches with the Blaugrana.

He has now reached 40 goals in 151 matches with FC Barcelona. A bar reached which allows him to do better than certain legends like Romario and Diego Maradona who respectively scored 39 and 38 goals with the Catalan club during their career. Fermin Lopez is close to players like Cesc Fabregas (43 goals), Luis Figo (45 goals) and David Villa (48 goals).
