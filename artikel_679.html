Jodeci’s K-Ci Apologizes to Fans After Last-Minute Cancellation of Kentucky Show Due to ‘Severe Dehydration’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The singer born Cedric Hailey said he had to receive "immediate medical attention" on Sunday (Nov. 22) before Louisville show.

Jodeci singer Cedric “K-Ci” Hailey sent out a heartfelt thank you to all the fans supporting him after the beloved New Jack Swing star was forced to pull the plug on the group’s planned show in Louisville, Ky. on Sunday (Nov. 22). “I just want to take a moment to personally reach out and say thank you for all the love, prayers and well-wishes,” Hailey said in an Instagram video posted on Monday (Nov. 23). “Unfortunately, I experienced a medical emergency due to severe, I mean severe, dehydration and I wasn’t able to perform with my brothers in Jodeci last night in Louisville.” Related 'Do You Have What It Takes?': KATSEYE Appears in Trailer for K-Pop Film Inspired by Their Experience Rosalía Reacted to a Rauw Alejandro TikTok Video, and The Internet Lost It Karol G's 'BbY WOW' Leads Billboard Global Charts, 'Christmas' Carols Climb

Jodeci pulled the plug on their Forever My Lady 35th anniversary tour at the Louisville Palace on Sunday after fans had already gathered, calling off a show that’s part of the celebration of the trio’s 1991 debut album of the same name. Hailey, along with members JoJo, DeVante Swing and Mr. Dalvin, is slated to take the stage again on Tuesday night (Nov. 24) at the Orpheum Theatre in Memphis.

“I just want you to know that I am truly, truly sorry to everyone that came out expecting to see us. Believe me, there’s nothing I love more than getting on that stage singing for you guys and feeling all the love … [that you] give us in return, so your support means so much to me than words can express,” Hailey said in the video statement. “I never, ever take a single fan, a single ticket, or a single opportunity to perform for granted. So I just want to thank you for the medical staff last night — you guys were amazing.”

Hailey seemed to provide proof that he’ll be back on stage tonight, informing fans that he was already in town. “Memphis, I’m here, baby,” he said. I see y’all tomorrow night. God bless you.”

Forever My Lady hit No. 1 on the Billboard R&B/Hip-Hop Albums chart in May 1991, spinning off the No. 25 Billboard Hot 100 hit “Forever My Lady” and the No. 11 hit “Come and Talk to Me.”
