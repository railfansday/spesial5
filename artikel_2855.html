Veteran safety becomes available for Steelers after Saints release

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Pittsburgh Steelers are 6-3 and well within the AFC playoff picture after 10 weeks of football. However, the Steelers could improve at a position of need after a shocking release by the New Orleans Saints Thursday afternoon.

Per Jordan Schultz, safety Justin Reid and the Saints organization mutually agreed to release him. Reid has struggled on a New Orleans defense that is in the bottom half of the league in many major statistics. Despite his struggles this season, Reid is expected to have plenty of interest once he clears waivers. Schultz even goes as far as to say, "He's expected to land with a contender."

His release comes just a day after the safety missed practice Tuesday due to what the team called "personal reasons." Just a day later, the safety was granted his release.

Reid is looking for a new opportunity, and the Steelers could be the perfect place for the safety to have an impact on a playoff team. The Steelers have been thin at safety all season long with injuries to DeShon Elliott. Seventh-round rookie Robert Spears-Jennings has played well for the Steelers in relief, but the depth could provide a much-needed boost to a Steelers secondary that was exposed by Joe Burrow last weekend as he threw for 409 yards.

Given where they are in the standings and safety being a major position of need, keep an eye on Reid potentially landing in Pittsburgh in the near future.
