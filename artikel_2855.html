Broncos activate WR Pat Bryant

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Broncos have wide receiver Pat Bryant back for Sunday’s game against the Panthers.

Bryant was activated from injured reserve on Saturday afternoon. He missed four games with an ankle injury and was designated for return when he resumed practicing with the team this week.

Bryant had nine catches for 133 yards and a touchdown in four games before his injury.

The Broncos also signed cornerback Sean Fresch off of the practice squad and placed defensive lineman Eyioma Uwazurike on injured reserve. Uwazurike was ruled out for Sunday with a shoulder injury.

Safety Taylor Rapp and running back Cody Schrader were elevated from the practice squad in other moves.
