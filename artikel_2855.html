‘Not good enough’ – Stephen A. Smith unleashes on Giants RB Cam Skattebo

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Cam Skattebo has proven himself to be a controversial figure in the NFL, with his attitude towards the game not sitting well with everyone.

There will be some who feel Skattebo gives his all to the game through his physical style of play, but the running back has also shown, as many feel, a real immaturity towards his career. The 24-year-old recently came under fire for admitting his recent illness was due to eating a pre-game hot dog.

For many people, Skattebo needs to show some more professionalism. Stephen A. Smith is one of those people, with the ESPN analyst sending a message out to the Giants' running back after what he has recently heard him say.

Earlier this week, Skattebo admitted he will bring an end to his backflip celebration after seeing what happened to Aidan Hutchinson over the weekend. Smith still wants more professionalism from Skattebo, though.

"That's not good enough. Don't do it. Listen, just don't do it. What is this, Dr. Phil? I don't want to hear that: don't do it. Listen, the kid's got potential. Showed me some speed the other week, too. He ain't just somebody looking to run through a brick wall; he's got some speed on him, he's skilled, and he's needed," Smith said on First Take.

"Yo, you did a backflip over the summer at Fanatics Fest and lost your footing then, you understand what I'm saying? Now, what could have happened? This is football. You play it; you guys know what I'm talking about. The slightest thing can derail you. You could be running routes in practice with no contact whatsoever and find yourself in that kind of situation.

"Not only do you have yourself doing it, now you've got your teammates mimicking your behaviour and doing it too. We saw what happened to Aidan Hutchinson. All he did was jump in the air to celebrate, came down, and the knee buckled. I'm quite sure he wasn't feeling like that before that play, or when the game started. You don't know what's going to happen to you."

Skattebo's last two performances have certainly impressed, with three touchdowns scored and over 200 rushing yards across his last two games against the Houston Texans and Washington Commanders.

"So what are you doing? You're making millions, you've got an opportunity to have an NFL future, and you're going to do something that stupid? It makes no sense. If I'm his coach, I better not see it. If I'm the owner, I better not see it, because I need you. It's that simple. I don't understand why this is hard. I really don't."

There is clearly a lot of expectation that Skattebo can go on to become a really impressive player, but Smith has clear concerns about his personality and how seriously he takes his job.
