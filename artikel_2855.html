Patrick Mahomes, Chiefs News Announced After Week 8 Loss to Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Kansas City lost their second consecutive on Sunday. Fans tuned in to their game against the Denver Broncos, and CBS announced an update about Patrick Mahomes and his team two days later.

The star quarterback completed 30-of-41 passes for 321 yards, three touchdowns and zero interceptions. He finished with a 73.2 completion percentage and a 120.1 QB rating. However, his efforts weren't enough to avoid the 28-24 defeat.

Kansas City had the opportunity to make it a six-point game with less than three minutes remaining. However, Harrison Butker missed a 54-yard field goal attempt and gave Denver good field position. Bo Nix and Co. only needed 35 seconds to score a touchdown and converted the extra point.

Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes (15) stands on the field after the game against the Las Vegas Raiders at Allegiant Stadium.

The Week 9 matchup became the second-most watched game of the season. "NFL on CBS" soared as the most-viewed program with 28.175 million fans tuned in.

Many fans looked forward to the clash between the AFC West rivals, especially with the division's top spot up for grabs. Mahomes returned to action this season after suffering an ACL and LCL injury last December. Meanwhile, Nix has established himself as one of the best young quarterbacks in the NFL today. Their showdown intrigued fans around the country, adding to the anticipation all-week long.

Mahomes and the Chiefs have some more marquee matchups to be played this year. They will take on the Josh Allen and the Buffalo Bills in Week 12, with CBS also airing the matchup. It won't be surprising if it breaks this season's viewership record again.
