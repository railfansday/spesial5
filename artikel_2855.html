NFL Week 10 picks: Packers prevail over Vikings; Steelers beat Bengals

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Los Angeles Times NFL writer Sam Farmer examines the matchups and makes his predictions for Week 10 of the NFL season.

All lines and over/under numbers are according to FanDuel Sportsbook.

Last week, Farmer posted an 15-0 record. Through the first nine weeks of the season, he is 89-47 (.654).

Using point spreads with the scores Farmer predicted, his record against the spread in Week 9 would have been 12-3 (.800). For the season, his record against the spread is 65-71 (.478).

All times are Pacific and TV reflects broadcasts in the Los Angeles area. The Bears, Broncos, Buccaneers and Eagles are off this week.

New York Giants running back Cam Skattebo carries the ball against the Houston Texans on Oct. 25. (Maria Lysaker / Associated Press)

The last time these teams met, they tied. The Commanders haven't won on the road this season, and the Giants are getting J.J. McCarthy and Cam Skattebo back. The Giants defense shines.

The Patriots defense started strong but has fallen apart, and last week it was embarrassed by Green Bay. Offense is beat up. Detroit isn't scoring a ton but has won four in a row.

Patrick Mahomes' elbow is in question. The Chiefs are coming off a get-right win over the Jets. The Falcons pulled off a stunner against San Francisco three weeks ago but that was their Super Bowl.

C.J. Stroud is coming off a five-touchdown game against the Chargers, and the Texans' defense had eight sacks. Deshaun Watson starts against his old team. The Browns have problems scoring in the red zone.

Green Bay Packers quarterback Jordan Love passes against the New England Patriots on Nov. 8. (Greg Cooper / Associated Press)

Can the Vikings solve all their QB problems by trading for Kirk Cousins? That's a big ask. Jordan Love is having a great season, and the Packers' defense is second in the league in takeaways with 19.

Jacksonville might be the best team in the AFC and it just beefed up its defense at the trade deadline. Cam Ward is coming off his best game for the Titans, but Jeffery Simmons being traded to Chicago is a major loss for the defense.

The Colts had a fire sale but at least they kept their quarterback and running back. That might be enough to hold off the Dolphins, who have played a bit better lately by keeping games fairly close.

Bryce Young looked good against Denver last week — much better than his two previous games — but the Panthers have still lost three in a row. The Saints are coming off a clutch performance in an overtime win over the Browns.

The Bills are coming off a short week but they have the NFL's highest-scoring offense. The Jets have lost four in a row and kick a lot of field goals. Jets can run the ball, though, and Breece Hall is back.

Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford passes against the Arizona Cardinals on Oct. 18. (Eric Thayer / Los Angeles Times)

The Rams aren't going to be swept by Arizona. They're in trouble at receiver, though, and need to find a way to limp through. Myles Garrett makes a scary defensive front terrifying. Tough draw for Carson Beck.

Fernando Mendoza makes his starting debut, and does so against a defense ranked No. 1 in yards allowed. Drew Lock starts again for the Seahawks, who are 2-0 with him at the helm.

The Cowboys can score but they can't stop anyone, least of all San Francisco, which might be the best team in the league. Despite a bunch of injuries (like a lot of teams) the 49ers keep finding ways to win.

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers scrambles against the Cleveland Browns on Nov. 1. (Matt Durisko / Associated Press)

First place in the AFC North is at stake, and these teams are evenly matched. Both have won four in a row, and Pittsburgh won the last meeting. The Steelers are 12-4 in Cincinnati since 2010.

The Ravens have lost four in a row and have a new quarterback in Jacoby Brissett. Hard to bet on the Chargers until they show a pulse, however. They've gotten into holes early and can't dig out.
