The biggest game yet of the Lane Kiffin era in Baton Rouge called for some pomp and circumstance.

LSU's home matchup against Alabama is the first of a massive three-game stretch for the Tigers, needing at least two wins over the Crimson Tide, Texas and Tennessee over the coming weeks to be considered for a CFP spot.

Ahead of Saturday's huge game, Kiffin and his team brought out some of the biggest-name LSU alums out there for a "Tiger Walk" featuring Shaquille O'Neal, Angel Reese, Paul Skenes, Livvy Dunne and more.

Here's a look at the viral clip of Kiffin walking with some of LSU's most well-known names.

Saturday's game is essentially must-win for LSU, which already has two losses and still has matchups against two ranked SEC teams remaining. No team has ever made the CFP with three losses — and even if the Tigers want to become the first, a win over Alabama would be key for their resume.

Hosting the game at Tiger Stadium, Kiffin led the team's "Tiger Walk" — a pregame tradition with the team, cheerleaders, band and more marching down Victory Hill into the stadium — alongside some huge names.

O'Neal and Reese, two former LSU basketball stars, and Skenes, a former LSU baseball star, all accompanied Kiffin on the "Tiger Walk."

Actor Kevin Costner was another surprise guest in the star-studded lineup.
