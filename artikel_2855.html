Dolphins news: Bills previews, injury updates, and GTA VI release

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Miami Dolphins are preparing to face the Buffalo Bills for the first of two games against their AFC East rivals in the 2026 NFL schedule. Miami, 1-8 this season, faces a tough battle against the Bills, who are 8-1 this year and currently in position to claim the top spot in the AFC playoff picture.

Miami will also be starting quarterback Kyle McCord for just the second time in his career as he continues to fill in for the injured Malik Willis. The injury concerns do not end there, however, as Miami has seen three defensive tackles, Zach Sieler, Zeek Biggers, and Jordan Phillips, land on the injured reserve over the past week. On the first injury report of the week, the Dolphins were without center Aaron Brewer (concussion), punter Bradley Pinion (right groin), cornerback Jaylin Simpson (knee), safety Dante Trader Jr. (shoulder/groin), and wide receiver Jared Wayne (quad), continuing the injury issues the team is currently battling through.

As the preparations for the Bills game continue, the Dolphins are also supporting the release of Grand Theft Auto VI, perhaps the most anticipated video game ever. Scheduled to release today, GTA VI is set in a fictionalized version of Miami called Vice City and features a local football team, the Manatees, whose primary colors are aqua and pink. While the Dolphins have not broken out a "Vice City" jersey combination like the Miami Heat have, they are still partnering with Rockstar Games, the game's developers, to ensure the game is represented in Hard Rock Stadium.
