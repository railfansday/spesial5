Browns home win streak: Undefeated in Cleveland is on the table, with one big risk

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Cleveland Browns have performed well in front of their home crowd this season, going 4-0 so far. Their most recent game was a 27-24 win over the Houston Texans. This is the first time the Browns have been 4-0 at home since 1963. Cleveland has four more home games remaining on the schedule. Looking at the list of opponents, which game are they most likely to lose?

The Browns have three consecutive home games scheduled against the Las Vegas Raiders, Cincinnati Bengals, and Atlanta Falcons coming out of their bye week. The final home game comes in the second-to-last week of the season against the Indianapolis Colts.

The matchup against the Cincinnati Bengals is high on the list for most likely losses. The Bengals are coming off a dominant 38-18 home win against the Pittsburgh Steelers. Cincinnati currently sits atop the AFC North with a 7-2 record, giving the team every incentive to keep its momentum going and further solidify its playoff position. The game will definitely be competitive, but the Bengals will be coming ready to play.
