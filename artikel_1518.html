Roma maintain contacts with Endrick’s entourage

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Roma is already looking ahead to the January transfer window, particularly to strengthen Gian Piero Gasperini’s attack.

Among the names the Giallorossi are following is Endrick, the Brazilian striker from Real Madrid.

Despite public and official statements, the 2006-born player continues to find little room in the Spanish club.

This situation is keeping Roma interested, as they are evaluating the opportunities offered by the winter transfer window.

According to journalist Matteo Moretto, D’Amico is continuing to maintain contact with Endrick’s team, without abandoning the pursuit of the Brazilian talent.

His name therefore remains among those being monitored by the Giallorossi ahead of the January transfer window.
