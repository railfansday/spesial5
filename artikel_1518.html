Rodrigo Mora becomes third U20 player to score in four consecutive games

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Rodrigo Mora continues to surprise and rewrite the Serie A statistics.

Roma’s young Portuguese talent, born in 2007, became the third player under 20 to score in four consecutive games in the Italian top flight since the introduction of three points for a win.

Before him, only Alexandre Pato and Moise Kean had achieved the feat, two names who, at different times, have shaped the recent history of Italian football with AC Milan and Juventus.

The next chapter in the Portuguese’s development began at the Ennio Tardini Stadium, where Roma swept Alberto Gilardino’s Parma 5-1. Mora also made his mark in the Giallorossi’s impressive five-goal campaign, once again a key player in front of goal.

After some initial moments of obvious difficulty given his young age and the change from Portugal to Italy, the time has now come for Mora to take center stage, and the numbers confirm his rise in Italian football.
