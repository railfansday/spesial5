Thomas Tuchel's 'best night' as England manager: Three Lions boss hails heroes after Spain victory - but hits out at Elliot Anderson decision made by referee he previously labelled a 'one out of 10'

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Published: 18:10 EST, 15 November 2026 | Updated: 19:58 EST, 15 November 2026

Thomas Tuchel hailed the 'best performance of my time as manager' after England beat world champions Spain in a crazy, brilliant see-sawing drama of a Nations League tie.

England played the best football of Tuchel's two-year reign as coach to end Spain's world record 43-match unbeaten run.

England raced into a 3-0 lead with a penalty from Harry Kane, and further goals from Ezri Konsa and Ben White.

But they played the last half an hour of the game with ten men after Elliot Anderson was sent off for pulling back Lamine Yamal.

Spain got two goals back to set up a nerve-wracking finish but goalkeeper James Trafford, in the team ahead of Jordan Pickford, pulled off a miracle double save in the dying minutes to seal the win.

'What a performance we put out there for 60 minutes to be deservedly 3-0 up,' Tuchel said after the game. 'Thirty minutes against Spain with ten men feels like three hours.

Thomas Tuchel said England's display in the win over Spain was the best in his time as boss

'It was a statement victory. It was on the highest level football-wise. It is a wonderful feeling for us in the dressing room.

'It is the best performance of my time and they are unbeaten for 43 matches and that was a complete performance in that environment. To impose our identity on them was a real achievement.

'Declan Rice said he knew we were going to win and I could feel the true belief. This was the moment we wanted to prove ourselves. You need to rely on your teammates. That is why I am very proud of the win.'

Tuchel also questioned the decision to give Anderson a straight red, made by a referee he once labelled a 'one out of 10'.

The Man City midfielder was sent off because Clement Turpin adjudged him not to have been making an attempt to play the ball when he fouled Yamal.

'The red card and the penalty is always a big decision and I needed to understand why it was a double punishment but... OK,' Tuchel told ITV Sport.

'From then it was a long 30 minutes but I don't want to focus on that because there were so, so many positives.'
