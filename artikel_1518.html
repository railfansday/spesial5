England quiz: Name the XI from Harry Kane’s debut, 2015

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

In March 2015, Harry Kane made his senior England debut in a Euro 2016 qualifying match against Lithuania – but how many of his team-mates that day can you recall?

And the striker scored 79 seconds into his debut, the first goal of a record-breaking international career.

Now England’s all-time record scorer, Kane is about to become England’s all-time leading appearance maker too.
