What should I think about?
depending on your job stability would it be more beneficial to take a 401k loan you will pay back so as to avoid the penalty and tax? Some plans let you pay back over 5 years. That'd be $80,000 a year (plus interest) for 5 years but would save you a lot in taxes/penalties and the interest payments are credited to your 401k. Downsides are you're out of the market (but you would be anyway if you sell/distribute) and that can be mitigated by taking $400k out of fixed income, not stocks AND if you lose your job the amount not paid back becomes taxable (but you were prepared to pay the tax/penalty anyway).

Only other option is get a mortgage and hope rates drop and refinance into a lower rate.

If you had taxable investments you could have drawn from those and the cap gains would be more favorable and no penalties.

Not generally advisable but if you had Roth account you can take out contributions (not earnings) tax and penalty free.
