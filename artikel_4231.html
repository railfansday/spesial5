11 Bit Studios Signs Deal To Release Three 'Day One' Xbox Game Pass Titles In 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

We've already heard about many titles coming to Xbox Game Pass in 2027, including the likes of Stranger Than Heaven and Fable in the first couple of months, and now we know that 11 Bit Studios will be joining the lineup next year too.

The publisher (and developer in some cases) behind such games as Frostpunk 2, The Alters and Moonlighter 2 has officially entered into an agreement with Microsoft to release three upcoming games on Xbox Game Pass.

Here's the confirmation, as shared on social media:

The deal unfortunately doesn't mention what the three games will be, but confirms they're from the "publishing portfolio" (so not necessarily developed by them), and are "scheduled for release in 2027".

Plans for 11 Bit Studios are mostly unknown at the moment, but they are confirmed to be working on a new version of Frostpunk called Frostpunk 1886, along with a psychological horror game called Trainwatch and a narrative-driven survival farming simulator known as Crop. Who knows whether any of these will make the cut for Game Pass though.

Let's wait and see! We've had a lot of fun with a whole bunch of 11 Bit Studios titles on Xbox Game Pass over the years, and these three upcoming titles should prove excellent additions to the service in 2027 - whatever they may be.
