GTA 6 True Crime Podcast Discusses GTA 5 Protagonists

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It has never been officially confirmed that all Grand Theft Auto games take place in the same shared universe. There are reasons to believe that’s the case, though, thanks to countless easter eggs across multiple titles, and players have already found a big one that effectively confirms GTA 6 and GTA 5 share a timeline.

As famous as the GTA games are for the music featured on their radio stations, GTA 6 is the first game in the series to have been released during the podcast era. That’s why there is a healthy mix of podcasts alongside the usual radio setlists in GTA 6, and a true crime podcast not only mentions GTA 5’s protagonists by name, but it’s a secondary character from the last game saying them.

Is Jason bridging the gap between GTA and Red Dead, or are fans reaching?

That character is Wade Herbert, who, in the “true” ending of GTA 5, is framed for the murders of Deborah and Floyd - a crime that was actually committed by Trevor. Even now, as demonstrated by his guest appearance on a GTA 6 true crime podcast, not only is Wade unaware that Trevor was the one responsible, but he still sees him as his “BFF” and doesn’t believe he, Michael, and Franklin could have killed Deborah and Floyd.

The host of the podcast reveals she showed Wade a photo, which resulted in the GTA 5 tritagonist excitedly identifying everyone in it. “Oh my lord, that fella there is my BFF,” Wade exclaims. “I will not say his name, but I can tell you that that there is Michael De Santa; he’s a clever sort and a real family man, and that young ‘un is Franklin Clinton, and he is, what we may call, a street rascal.”

“But like I said to those detectives, none of those folks could have killed Floyd and Deborah, or robbed all them banks. They were respected businessmen and entrepreneurs,” Wade continues, the tone of his voice becoming slightly more aggressive.

The host then retakes the reins and comments on the change in Wade’s voice, questioning whether he was scared when being reminded of his connections to Trevor, Michael, and Franklin. That’s when she spins a conspiracy theory that perhaps they were the ones responsible for the murders at the end of GTA 5, along with countless other crimes. Typical true crime stuff, except we’ve got the inside track, as we know it isn’t a conspiracy theory at all - it’s the truth.

Michael and Franklin are confirmed to have left their criminal lives behind them post-GTA 5, with the latter even showing up in GTA Online. What has happened to Trevor since then has been left a little more ambiguous. He might even be dead, although Wade seemingly being too afraid to even utter his name suggests that he, at the very least, seems to believe he’s still alive.

I really hope there’s more to that particular easter egg than just the one clip, and that the same podcast dives even deeper into the past of the three lead characters in GTA 5, attempting to out them as the criminal masterminds that they become.

It’s far from the only easter egg in GTA 6 that references other Rockstar games. It has been two days, and players have already found Arthur Morgan’s hat, which Jason can buy and wear, and confirmed that the photo of a random house in the GTA Trilogy remasters really was a shot of a house from GTA 6, hidden in plain sight in a different game five years before it was released.
