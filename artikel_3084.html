During our mattress testing process, we found the DreamCloud out-performed some luxury rivals costing double the price in certain areas. For example, the DreamCloud is one of the best hybrid mattresses you can buy online. Its hybrid construction means it has been crafted from a combination of coils and foam comfort layers.

When compared to the Bear Elite Hybrid, our top cooling mattress of the year and with a current queen sale price of $1,370, the DreamCloud performed better in our motion isolation and edge support tests, making a compelling argument that the DreamCloud is a better fit for couples.
