TL;DR: For a limited time, sign up for T-Mobile 5G Home Internet from $30 per month with a voice line and AutoPay and get a month on them plus up to $200 back via a virtual prepaid Mastercard.

T-Mobile has dropped an offer on its T-Mobile 5G Home Internet that's much needed ahead of the upcoming holiday season. For a limited time, you can sign up for a plan starting at $30 per month (with a voice line and AutoPay bundled) and get up to $200 back as a virtual prepaid Mastercard. That's not all, either. In total, there are three T-Mobile 5G Home Internet plans available with this offer: Rely, Amplified, and All-In. With the former, you can enjoy download speeds up to 354 Mbps, while both Amplified and All-In get you up to 498 Mbps. All three offer upload speeds up to 55 Mbps. The latter plan also includes additional perks, such as Advanced Cyber Security (in the TechEdge Suite) and a gateway upgrade after three years.

If you're curious which plan is the best fit for you and your online activity, it's worth checking out T-Mobile's plan page to learn more.

As for the cash-back part of this deal, up to $200 will be credited to your virtual prepaid Mastercard when you bundle your voice plan. Just make sure to allow seven weeks from rebate submission for it to show up. This card can be used everywhere Debit Mastercard is accepted, so it's great to have on hand when spending cash ahead of the holidays.

This offer is only available for a limited time. If you've been hoping to sign up for T-Mobile 5G Home Internet, now is the time to do it.
