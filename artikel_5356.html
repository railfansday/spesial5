Nvidia's new boss to staff: 'Let's move forward'

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Interim CEO Colette Kress addressed staff at the first all-hands since Jensen Huang's exit; the board does not intend to reinstate him

Colette Kress, Nvidia $NVDA's interim CEO, kicked off Wednesday's gathering, the first full-staff session since Jensen Huang stepped down, by telling employees, "let's move forward," according to CNBC. The meeting took roughly 60 minutes, with employees attending in person or watching a Microsoft $MSFT Teams stream.

Nvidia made clear at the meeting that Jensen Huang, the company's co-founder, will not return as CEO, according to Bloomberg. Harvey Jones, Nvidia's board chair, said on Tuesday's earnings call that the board "unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO," according to CNBC.

Kress raised the subject of pay raises for employees, according to CNBC. Jones then turned to the search for the next CEO. Ian Buck, who leads hyperscale and high-performance computing, and several other executives spoke. Co-founder Chris Malachowsky urged staff to be intellectually honest and direct, according to Bloomberg.

Kress walked through the prior day's financial results and described the period as a "great quarter," according to CNBC. Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion on Tuesday, up 103% from a year earlier. Nvidia stock was down 2% on Wednesday.

Employee sentiment at the meeting was divided, according to CNBC. One attendee described the general mood as a desire for Jensen Huang's return, while another person said employees welcomed the remarks and the view into company leadership. Some current and former staff, along with Silicon Valley allies of Jensen Huang, are pushing for him to come back, according to Bloomberg.

Jensen Huang resigned as CEO and left the board on Nov. 12 after reports of affairs with at least two women. U.S. officials suspect one of them, Alice Huang, of relaying information to China's intelligence services. Jensen Huang and Alice Huang are not related. The board is searching for a permanent CEO and will consider internal and external candidates. Kress keeps her CFO role while serving as interim CEO.

On Tuesday, Jensen Huang said he would be "honored" to return if the board decides it is in the best interest of shareholders, employees, the company and America.
