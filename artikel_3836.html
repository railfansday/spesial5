All Captain Aganmi’s Missing Crew member locations in Queen’s Domain

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Queen’s Domain has several difficult quests to complete, and one of them involves finding four lost raiders across the map. All of them belong to Captain Aganmi, and he will task you to find their whereabouts.

In this guide, I will briefly discuss the locations of all four of them so that you can get the quest done easily.

To receive the quest, travel to Aganmi’s Camp in the Ombuer Temple region. Here, you’ll find the captain sitting near a bonfire. After serving his introduction, he will tell you about his crew, and you can begin the quest.

To find the first person, head to the Knight’s Encampment in Isle of Neasied. Take the stairs to the left of the portal and continue heading straight. Keep following the same path, and you will come across the ocean in front of you.

Turn left, take a few steps forward, and you will find a tree with a crew member sitting under it.

You can find the second crew along the path to the Temple, and you’ll need a few air dashes to reach the spot.

Navigate the tough spots with the air dashes and continue past the opening shown on the image above.

From there, you’ll find a broken structure that allows you to travel upwards. Continue past it (don’t go on the structure), and you’ll find the body on a small piece of land.

To find the third crew member, you have to defeat the zone’s boss. You’ll find him near the top of the teleporter. From there, turn right, and continue along the path that goes downwards. Go straight, and you’ll find an opening on the floor. Drop, and you’ll find the crew member.

To reach the final crew member, head to the Cemetery Precipice teleporter at Isle of Neasied. Go past the teleporter, continue straight, and then turn right. You’ll come across the structure shown on the image above.

From there, take the path that goes left, continue straight, turn right, and use an air dash. Again, continue straight, and use a second air dash to jump into the wall opening. There, you’ll find the final missing crew.
