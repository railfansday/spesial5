The Game Awards 2026 nominees announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Game Awards has announced its nominees for 2026, as selected by a global jury of more than 160 media publications and creator outlets.

This year’s nominees are led by Grand Theft Auto VI with eight nominations, including Game of the Year and Best Game Direction, followed by CONTROL Resonant and Resident Evil Requiem each with six nominations, and Phantom Blade Zero with five nominations.

Users can vote now in all categories via authenticated online voting on official website. Voting will end on December 9 at 6:00 p.m. PT / 9:00 p.m. ET.

The Game Awards 2026 will air live on December 10 from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, California with awards, world premiere announcements and trailers, musical performances by The Game Awards Orchestra led by conductor Lorne Balfe, and other surprises. It will be viewable across more than 40 digital video platforms including Disney+, Live TV on PS5, Prime Video, Roblox, Steam, TikTok Live, Twitch, Twitter, YouTube, and many more.
