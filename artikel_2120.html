Harry Kane embraces his family during special Ballon d'Or presentation at Bayern Munich - before England skipper MISSES a penalty in chaotic draw!

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Published: 06:28 EDT, 1 November 2026 | Updated: 06:29 EDT, 1 November 2026

Harry Kane received a hero's welcome as he returned to action for Bayern Munich for the first time since winning the Ballon d'Or.

Kane was crowned as the best player in the world on Monday night, becoming the first English player to win the award since Michael Owen in 2001 on what was an emotional evening for the former Tottenham star.

His success came off the back of a stunning year with Bayern and England, with the forward netting a staggering 73 goals during the 2025-26 season.

Kane's goals spearheaded the German side's domestic Treble, while he also captained his country to third place at the World Cup.

And, ahead of Bayern's clash against Borussia Dortmund on Saturday night, Kane received a special presentation at the Allianz Arena.

With both sets of players and match officials lined up on the pitch, Kane was welcomed out of the tunnel while holding his new prized possession in his hands.

Harry Kane presented his Ballon d'Or trophy to Bayern Munich fans and players on Saturday

Kane received a hero's welcome from Bayern fans and his team-mates as he walked out

Kane was also greeted by his family, including his four children, ahead of the match

Bayern's players and fans gave Kane a standing ovation as Land of Hope and Glory played over the stadium's sound system.

The England skipper was serenaded by the home faithful, although some Dortmund fans could be heard to be booing him.

Waiting on the pitch were his family, including his wife Kate, four children, his parents and his brother Charlie, and he greeted them all as he came out.

Kane then took a microphone and addressed the home fans by saying: 'I’m extremely proud to be a Bayern player.

'The support you’ve shown me since I arrived is incredible. I’m extremely proud to present this to you. I hope to win many more titles with you.'

Unfortunately for Kane, his on-pitch return didn't perhaps go as planned as he missed a penalty as Bayern drew 2-2 with Dortmund.

Kane had the chance to mark his honour further after Konrad Laimer had put Bayern ahead, but he smacked his spot kick against the bar.

Serhou Guirassy then equalised for Dortmund, and although Kane assisted Dayot Upemecano's response, which put his side 2-1 up, former Wolves flop Fabio Silva equalised for the visitors in the 89th minute.

Kane addressed the crowd on the pitch where he highlighted his pride at playing for Bayern

Meanwhile, after winning the award on Monday, Kane had made sure to pay tribute to Bayern.

He said: 'Without you guys [Bayern Munich team-mates] and the rest of the team it wouldn't be possible, the way you guys push me, the way we compete, so thank you very much.

'To Vinny [Vincent Kompany]. I know he is at home or still in the hospital. Wishing you well. I hope this gave you a bit of energy. To all the staff, the we way train and the way you push us, it is a credit to yourself.

'Since I have been with you I have improved as a player, improved as a person and a leader.

'It is an honour to play for Bayern Munich, to represent you is a real privilege and I am glad to be bringing this trophy back to Bayern Munich.'
