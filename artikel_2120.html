Who are Mauricio Pochettino’s assistant coaches at Manchester United?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Shumail Sajid writes about Manchester United for Read Man Utd and Crystal Palace for Read Crystal Palace, with occasional pieces on other Premier League clubs. He has also written for FanSided's Red Devils Dispatch, Yardbarker, Anfield Central and The Sports Side.

Manchester United parted ways with Michael Carrick after a disastrous start to the Premier League season with just one win from 10 games.

Manchester United have spent a reported 12 million to bring Mauricio Pochettino and his coaching team from the United States to Old Trafford, with the Argentine determined to work alongside several familiar faces.

United paid around $10 million to secure Pochettino’s release from the US team and with a further $2 million reportedly required to secure members of his backroom staff.

The 54-year-old has built a close working relationship with his coaching team throughout spells at Espanyol, Southampton, Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea. Several of those staff members have followed him to Manchester United.

Jesus Perez is Pochettino’s long-time assistant and is widely regarded as his right-hand man. Their relationship began at Espanyol, where Perez initially worked as an analyst before becoming his assistant at Southampton. He has followed the Argentine throughout his career with spells at Tottenham, PSG and USA. He is now set to take the same role at Old Trafford.

Jimenez will work as United’s goalkeeping coach. He first joined Pochettino’s coaching setup at Espanyol in 2011 and has remained alongside him throughout his managerial career, including their time in England, France and the United States.

The manager’s 31-year-old son has also joined the coaching group. Sebastiano started working with his father during their time at Tottenham and has continued as a sports scientist and fitness coach with him with the USA team. He is also joining Pochettino at Manchester United.

The former Argentine defender has worked with Pochettino since his first managerial job at Espanyol. He has been involved in training sessions, video analysis and tactical preparation, helping the manager develop game plans.

The arrivals mean a major change behind the scenes at Carrington following the departures of Michael Carrick and his coaching staff.

Mauricio Pochettino is set to face Liverpool in his first game as Manchester United head coach on Sunday.
