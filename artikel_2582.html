Patriots News: Pats need defense in top form to tackle Chargers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Literally. The Pats defense needs to be in top-tackling form. Not because the Chargers are all that and a bag of chips, but because the defensive unit has had actual trouble tackling. Here's hoping they have seen enough and had enough watching cringey film of opposing receivers catching balls at will and making too many yards after the catch, over the bye week. I'm pretty sure Pats fans want to push away from that table and say "I've had enough" too.
