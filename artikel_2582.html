'He can't be the lead bowler': Cheteshwar Pujara warns Punjab Kings against relying on Hardik Pandya

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Punjab Kings (PBKS) will need to make the right bowling acquisitions at the upcoming IPL auction if they are to end their long wait for a maiden title in 2027, according to former India batter Cheteshwar Pujara. While the arrival of Hardik Pandya has strengthened their all-round options and added depth to the batting order, the departure of Arshdeep Singh to Mumbai Indians has left a significant void in their pace attack.Pujara believes the identity and quality of Arshdeep's replacement could prove decisive in determining Punjab's title prospects, with the franchise needing to build a bowling attack capable of delivering consistently throughout the tournament.Speaking to JioStar, Pujara highlighted the contrasting impact of Hardik's arrival and Arshdeep's departure, stressing that Punjab must prioritise their bowling requirements at the auction.

"The arrival of Hardik Pandya has been one huge positive for Punjab, though alongside it there is a slight negative as well, that Arshdeep Singh isn't there. Even though his performance last season wasn't as special as expected, given the way he is bowling right now, he is back in form."So that could be a big blow for Punjab, and who's going to be his replacement will be the deciding factor. Of course, with Hardik Pandya coming in, their batting strength will increase. How his bowling is coming along, we don't know yet, because he has been recovering from an injury."While his bowling will also be important, he can't be the lead bowler, he can be a supporting bowler. The bowlers they pick at the auction and how they shape up will determine whether they can go on to win the trophy," Pujara said.

Pujara also believes Hardik's presence could address one of Punjab's long-standing batting concerns, particularly their inability to find stability in the lower middle order when early wickets fall."With Hardik in the team, the slight issue they used to face of needing someone to stabilise the innings after early wickets fall is a role he can fulfil very well, solving Punjab's No. 6 and No. 7 problems," Pujara added.
