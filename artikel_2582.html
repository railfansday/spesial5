High School National Esports Championship: Two teams tied atop Week 6 standings

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The race for the USA TODAY Sports National High School Esports Championship heats up as Thanksgiving weekend approaches with a tie atop the leaderboard.

Chaparral High School (CO), La Cueva High School (NM), and Bob Jones High School (AL) hold the No. 1 position in "Rocket League," "Madden NFL," and "Marvel Rivals," respectively. But Bob Jones and Gadsden High School (NM) share the overall lead.

Which high school esports power will become the inaugural national champions?

With plenty of head-to-head matchups left, the answer is still very much an exciting unknown.

The tournament's round-robin portion kicked off on Oct. 12 on the PlayVS platform and will run through Dec. 4. Each school's performance in the three titles will determine the leaderboard, with overall points awarded as follows.

The two schools with the highest point totals will move on to play in the national championship on Dec. 11.
