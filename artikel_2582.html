Thursday Cannon Fodder: rubber meet road

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Good morning, TSFers! Once again, we're just a day away from the weekend. Isn't it wonderful how time marches on like that. Speaking of, we've reached the thick of the Premier League season. There are no more international breaks until March. We're in range of the festive period. The grindiest part of the PL grind starts now.

Arsenal are in great shape on the eve of battle. They're five points clear of Brighton in second, six points (plus whatever deduction is coming) ahead of Manchester City, and nine points up on Liverpool in fourth. They're third in the Champions League table with three of four away matches played. The Gunners were knocked out of the Carabao Cup, which will likely help things as the schedule slogs on by alleviating a bit of congestion.

The fitness situation is in a decent spot, too. William Saliba should be ready to return to first team action imminently, if not this weekend. Martin Zubimendi picked up a minor hamstring niggle on international duty, but it's not thought to be serious.

The big question is when will Bukayo Saka be ready to return. The star winger injured his quad on a non-contact movement against Bayern Munich last month and has missed 10-straight matches. His recovery is going well, according to Mikel Arteta, but it will still be weeks until he's ready. The club haven't said much at all about the nature of the injury nor have there been leaks to the press, which is a bit odd.

Saka is a big miss, of course. All told, entering the marathon portion of the schedule with only one long-term absence to go along with a short-term one (Zubimendi) isn't bad. Famous last words, right? We know that the congestion and fatigue will inevitably lead to more niggles, knocks, and injuries. But it's the same for every club and the Gunners have the deepest roster in the PL. We'll be fine, right? *knock on wood*
