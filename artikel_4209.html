A Complete Guide To The Star Trek Set Phasers To Superdrop Secret Lair Drop

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

As we've come to expect with new Magic: The Gathering Universes Beyond sets, a whole Secret Lair superdrop is launching alongside the new release. For Star Trek, we're getting five bundles in total, covering everything from full-art basic lands to an entire Klingon language set.

Admittedly, this Universes Beyond superdrop has some really cool cards, so if you're not financially ruined by two sets releasing close together and the endless stream of Secret Lair releases, it offers great options for Trekkies who want to expand their Magic card collection.

The Star Trek Set Phasers To Superdrop Secret Lair bundles are dropping on Monday, November 9, at 9 am PT, 12 pm ET, 5 pm GMT, and 6 pm CET.

This will follow the same structure as typical Secret Lair superdrops, meaning all bundles will be available to add to a cart from 8 am PT, 11 am ET, 5 pm GMT, and 5 pm CET. At that point, you will be asked to log in to your Wizards account, then placed in a waiting room until the products go live.

At that time, the entire waiting room is randomized into a checkout queue. You will be given an approximate waiting time based on your position in the queue. When you are admitted onto the Secret Lair website, you have ten minutes to check out your cart. Some products may sell out, in which case they will be removed from your cart.

As for prices, each bundle costs $29.99 for the nonfoil variant and $39.99 for the foil version. Wizards of the Coast has yet to confirm any complete bundles, but we fully expect these to be available on the day. This includes bundles of just the foil or nonfoil cards, plus an everything bundle with both copies of each.

These bundles are rarely sold at a major discount; instead, they're conveniently packaged to make checkout easier.

Finally, this drop's promo card is Transporter Boondoggle, a reskinned version of Return of the Wildspeaker. You will earn one copy of this card for every $199 spent on the superdrop. Free shipping is available when you spend $99.
