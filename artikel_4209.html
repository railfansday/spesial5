14 Games Are Leaving Xbox Game Pass In Early November 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Microsoft has revealed the next 14 games leaving Xbox Game Pass in early November 2026, with all of these being highlighted as "leaving soon" on the official Xbox phone app.

There are some pretty decent ones in here, including 1000xRESIST, Wreckfest, and the iconic GoldenEye 007, so it's well worth giving those a try before the middle of November if you haven't yet!

Here's the full list of games set to leave Xbox Game Pass in early November 2026:

Football Manager 26 is leaving Xbox Game Pass on November 10th, whereas everything else is scheduled to depart on the 15th. You'll find discounts of at least 20% on most of these Xbox Game Pass titles until they leave the service.

The good news is there's still plenty of time to check out these games before they go. Also, there's a whole bunch of games coming to Xbox Game Pass in the coming weeks, too.
