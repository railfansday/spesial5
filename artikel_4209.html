Lara's New Tomb Raider Game Is Apparently On Its Way To Switch 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis (a reimagining of Lara's original 1996 game) was confirmed for the Switch 2 earlier this year, and will be launching alongside other versions next February.

Now, in an update, it seems her "next all-new entry" Tomb Raider Catalyst could also be on the way to the hybrid platform. This new title, developed by Crystal Dynamics, arrives in 2028, and while there's no Nintendo announcement just yet, an updated Amazon News post might have already confirmed it.

According to the article, which was updated earlier this week on 9th November 2026, "both" of these Amazon-published titles are coming to the Switch 2.

When do the new ‘Tomb Raider’ games come out?

Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis arrives on February 12, 2027, while Tomb Raider: Catalyst launches in 2028. Both games arrive on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, and Steam.

The Tomb Raider series is currently celebrating its 30th anniversary. Most recently, Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition was shadow dropped on the Switch 2, rounding off the Survivor Trilogy.

If we hear anything else about Tomb Raider Catalyst coming to the Switch 2, we'll be sure to provide an update.
