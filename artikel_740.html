Robinson signs for Nottinghamshire on three-year deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Former Sussex captain Ollie Robinson has signed a three-year all-format contract with Nottinghamshire; the England seamer has represented Sussex in the County Championship since 2015, taking on the role of red-ball captain in 2026; Robinson had a year left on his Sussex contract

Nottinghamshire have announced the signing of England bowler Ollie Robinson on a three-year all-format deal.

The signing signals the end of an 11-year spell for Robinson at Sussex - whom he captained during the 2026 County Championship Division One season - despite having a year left on his contract.

Robinson joins the 2025 Division One title winners with a stellar first-class record of 520 wickets at an average of 21.09, while boasting 104 wickets at an average of 19.25 for England's Test side.

"I'm absolutely delighted to be joining Nottinghamshire. I've always loved playing at Trent Bridge, and I'm really looking forward to calling it home," Robinson said.

"I have a real desire to win silverware, and with the quality of the squad at Notts, there's no doubt in my mind that we can do that.

"Everyone has told me the environment at Trent Bridge is as close to an international one as you can get, so I'm really excited to see that and make the most of it."

Robinson posted figures of 5-85 in his first and only Test appearance at Trent Bridge, taking 22 first-class wickets in four appearances at the ground, averaging 19.63.

"I'd like to give Sussex a lot of credit throughout this whole process," Nottinghamshire director of cricket Mick Newell added.

"They've been constructive and cordial to deal with, and we're grateful to them for that. We're all thrilled by the statement signing of Ollie, and we know our members and supporters will be too."
