Gracie Abrams’ ‘Daughter from Hell’ Gets Deluxe Treatment: Stream It Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The expanded version of her Billboard 200 leader now features five fresh cuts.

Gracie Abrams’ Daughter from Hell is now, well, more hell-ish.

The Grammy nominated singer and songwriter today (Nov. 20) drops the deluxe edition of Daughter from Hell, an expanded version that now features five fresh cuts.

Daughter from Hell (Deluxe) collects newbies “Out of Nowhere,” which became a fan fave after Abrams debuted it during her 2025 Glastonbury Festival spot (where Paul McCartney was watching on from the sidelines); and “Crazy Girl,” which appeared on the vinyl exclusive and is now available digitally for the first time. Other bonus tracks into the previously revealed “Here You Come, There You Go,” produced by Jim-E Stack and co-written with Amy Allen; “Love Letter;” and “Lost It In.” Explore Gracie Abrams See latest videos, charts and news

It’s not the first expansion for Daughter From Hell. In late July, Abrams added two additional tracks from the DFH sessions to the standard version: “Out of Nowhere” and “Love Letter”. As it stands, the album blows out to 21 songs, with a playing time of 70 minutes. The “Daughter From Hell” in the title really is Abrams. “That song was the last one that I wrote for the album, and it is like a, you know, decades overdue apology to my mom, basically,” she told Jimmy Fallon during an episode of NBC’s The Tonight Show, which aired in July. Was she a beastly kid? Not exactly, just a “defiant person,” which can be “turbulent” for mom and dad (the film director J.J. Abrams). “This is a thank-you note to her.”

Abrams scored her first No. 1 album on the Billboard 200 chart with Daughter From Hell, which opened atop the Aug. 1-dated ranking.

She’ll support the album with a North American arena tour which runs from December through late March, followed by dates in the United Kingdom and Ireland in April and May, then Australia in July and August.
