ROSÉ of BLACKPINK Covers ‘One of My Favorite Songs’ by The Killers at Star-Studded LACMA Gala

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The star also played three of her own tracks, including solo release "New Trick."

ROSÉ got out of the APT. on Saturday (Nov. 7), attending the 2026 LACMA Art+Film Gala to perform four songs — including a cover of The Killers — and enjoy a star-studded evening celebrating Dune director Denis Villeneuve and artist Vija Celmins.

As captured in clips from the night, the BLACKPINK star rocked out to “Read My Mind” from the Brandon Flowers-fronted band’s 2006 Billboard 200 No. 2 album Sam’s Town, her live vocals sounding more powerful than ever as she let the emotion of the lyrics lead the way. “This next song is one of my favorite songs to cover,” she said before the tune. “Please sing along if you know it.”

The Los Angeles County Museum of Art also shared a video snippet of ROSÉ jumping up and down and whipping her hair while energetically singing “New Trick,” her new solo song released in September. Dressed in a dainty white dress with a pale pink flower decal on the shoulder, the K-pop superstar also played 12-week Billboard Global 200-topping Bruno Mars duet “APT.” as well as “Number One Girl” from her 2024 debut solo album, Rosie, according to Vanity Fair.

ROSÉ’s mini-concert was for a high-profile audience, with Hollywood elites including Nicole Kidman, Timothée Chalamet, Anya Taylor-Joy, Monica Barbaro, Salma Hayek, Kim Kardashian, Sarah Paulson, Kylie Jenner and more attending the 2026 event. This year, the gala raised a record-breaking sum of nearly $7 million for the museum’s programming.
