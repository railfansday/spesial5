Why I Think Privacy-First Personal AI Is Worth Watching

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The pattern I'm seeing in AI agents lately is that they keep asking for more access to your data, but nobody's really thinking about who controls that model of you.

When an AI knows your relationships, habits, and priorities, that's essentially a digital twin. The data involved is incredibly sensitive, and most products just... store it wherever. That's the gap First Person Labs is targeting with their approach to keeping that model on your device with you in control.

The technical challenge of running these models locally while still being useful is substantial. But if you pull it off, you solve a real adoption blocker for personal AI assistants.
