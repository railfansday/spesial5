Motorola’s flagship Signature 27 sounds quite expensive in retail leak

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Motorola’s return to the proper flagship space is coming in the Signature 27, but early leaks suggest this new device, as well as the company’s wide foldable, won’t exactly be cheap.

GSMArena and “Android Treasure“ spotted retail listings in Europe that give us some idea of what Motorola’s upcoming releases will cost.

The Motorola Signature 27 is listed at €1,594.90 through a retailer website based out of Portugal. For comparison, the Galaxy S26 Ultra is sold for €1,299 in Portugal. The listing calls out some marketing points including the 50MP Sony Lytia L910 main sensor, 200MP telephoto camera, as well as a “unique cooling system uses thermal gel with diamond particles and a new 3D vapor chamber to maintain performance for longer.” It also mentions weight of “0.4kg” which seems… unlikely given that would make for a phone that weighs 400g. Perhaps that’s the shipping weight. There’s also mention of a 12GB/512GB configuration.

Circling back to the price, it’s unclear whether or not that’s strictly accurate, but it’s not exactly out of the question.

With Motorola Signature 27 packing top-tier specs including Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, it was always going to be pricey. And if €1,600~ is the final price, it could be a lot worse. As a reminder, Oppo Find X10 Pro Max recently made its debut in the EU for €1,800.

The same retailer also lists the “Razr Flex,” Motorola’s wide foldable, at €1,510~. This one very much seems like a placeholder listing given there’s no imagery and a generic description compared to the Signature’s listing. The price also seems borderline impossible seeing as the Galaxy Z Fold 8 sells for over €2,200 in Portugal.

What these listings do suggest is that Motorola is gearing up for launch on these new products pretty soon.
