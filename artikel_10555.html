YouTuber Charlotte Dobre says YouTube should make people use real names to comment

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

YouTuber Charlotte Dobre would make YouTube commenters use their real names, if she had the power to implement a new rule on the platform.

There has been plenty of debate online over the years about whether or not people should be allowed to use personas and fake names to make comments on social media.

Like everywhere else, YouTube gives you the option to use your real name or a pseudonym, with plenty of people opting to reveal their true identities. They don’t always leave comments, so it makes things easier on that front.

Speaking to Dexerto, YouTuber and podcast host Charlotte Dobre revealed that, if she had the power to add a new rule to YouTube, it would be to force people to expose their true identities when leaving comments.

“Some people aren’t going to like this; I think that you should have to use your real name to comment. Everybody’s very brave behind a keyboard, but I’m curious how mean people would be if their real name was attached to it,” Dobre, who has just launched a pilot for her show Textuationships, said.

“I think people are emboldened by the fact that there are no repercussions for the stuff they say. Like, everything I say, there are repercussions for. I have to be so, so careful with what I say.

“I do think if there were consequences, ergo, like your name was attached to it, people would be a lot more careful.”

The YouTuber noted that people have gotten angry at her in the comments over some of the advice she’s given out, especially when they believe she hasn’t given the ‘right’ advice.
