Big Walk review bombed after House House admits AI touched “well under 0.1%” of code

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Big Walk, the co-op adventure from Untitled Goose Game developer House House, has been hit by a wave of negative Steam reviews after the studio disclosed that large language models were used during the final stretch of development.

House House added an AI section to Big Walk’s FAQ explaining that chatbot responses were occasionally used as starting points for modified game code. The studio estimates that “well under 0.1%” of the game’s code had anything to do with that usage and says generative AI was not used in any other part of development.

Big Walk launched on August 4 and remains rated Very Positive overall on Steam. However, its review history changed sharply after the disclosure spread. Steam recorded 135 positive reviews and three negative reviews on November 9.

The following day, that shifted to 426 positive and 366 negative reviews. The November 11 figures were 358 positive and 90 negative when checked.

House House said its primary use of LLMs involved researching problems with third-party code. The studio gave one example involving Unity’s Universal Render Pipeline, where shadow casters behind the player were reportedly rendered at an infinite distance.

According to House House, the issue made Big Walk run around 50% slower for approximately 20 seconds during sunrise and sunset scenes. The developers said they investigated the problem themselves, spoke with a Unity engineer and eventually asked a chatbot for another possible solution.

The chatbot suggested a fix that House House then modified extensively. The studio said the final change represented around 10 lines of code inside URP.

House House said it did not disclose the coding assistance on Steam because Valve does not request disclosure of code or chatbot usage. It said the FAQ statement was originally prepared for its Independent Games Festival submission and was published after the matter became public.

The studio stressed that the statement was not an endorsement of AI tools, apologized that the use might not meet some people’s standards and called the lack of an internal AI policy a failure. House House said it plans to create a more comprehensive policy going forward.

The debate began after 19 IGF jurors said they would decline to vote for any game that used generative AI during development. Their statement included AI-generated code and said it did not speak for the entire IGF organization or every juror.

House House disclosed the AI use as part of its IGF submission, but no official announcement has confirmed that Big Walk was disqualified from the awards.
