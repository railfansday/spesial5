There are just weeks left in 2026, and if you haven't quite reached the United MileagePlus elite status level you're hoping to secure for 2027, the clock is ticking.

From earning bonus Premier qualifying points with the right credit card to taking a last-minute flight, here are five ways to close the status gap before Dec. 31.

United's elite status qualification year runs from Jan. 1 through Dec. 31. Status earned in 2026 is valid through Jan. 31, 2028.

To qualify, you must fly at least four flights operated by United or United Express and meet one of two thresholds: a combination of Premier Qualifying Flights and PQPs or a higher PQP-only requirement.

You can check your progress in United's Premier Status Tracker, including how your upcoming flights will count toward qualification.

Some United credit cards offer bonus PQPs toward status as part of their welcome offers, in addition to redeemable United miles.

For example, the (see rates and fees) is currently offering 500 PQPs after your first purchase and 80,000 redeemable United bonus miles after spending $4,000 on purchases in the first three months from account opening. Plus, an additional 10,000 bonus miles when you add an authorized user in the first three months from account opening.

The card has a $350 introductory annual fee.

It's still possible to get bonus PQPs in 2026 as part of a new cardmember bonus on select United credit cards, but you should act quickly.

The good news is you can earn PQPs through everyday spending on United credit cards. The bad news is that most United cards earn them at a relatively slow rate of 1 PQP for every $20 spent.

That means $1,000 in purchases on most United cards would earn you just 50 PQPs.

However, the (see rates and fees) earns PQPs faster than the other personal United cards, at a rate of 1 PQP for every $15 spent. So, that same $1,000 in purchases would earn you about 66 PQPs with that card.

Every little bit helps, especially if you're close to your next status level.

If you already have United flights booked before the end of 2026, you may be able to earn additional PQPs by upgrading your seats.

Purchasing Economy Plus seats, United's preferred seating or paid upgrades to first class can earn 1 PQP per dollar spent (excluding taxes and fees).

You can also earn 1 PQP for every 100 MileagePlus miles redeemed for eligible seat upgrades on United and United Express flights.

Just remember that PQPs from upgrades purchased separately from your original ticket may post separately after you fly, so keep an eye on your account.

One of the most obvious ways to close the gap toward United elite status is to book another flight (or two) before the end of the year as part of a modern-day mileage run. Unlike in the old days, how far you fly doesn't matter if you're on United for status. It only matters how much you spend.

Ideally, you're going somewhere fun to see a friend, see some attractions or have a great meal. But if you turn around and don't leave the airport before your next flight, you won't be the first — or last — status-seeker to do so. I've certainly done it when I needed a few more segments at the very end of the year.

If you're chasing PQFs, remember that you generally earn one for each eligible flight segment on United, United Express or partner airlines.

For United-operated flights, you'll generally earn 1 PQP per dollar spent on eligible base airfare and carrier-imposed surcharges, excluding government taxes and fees. You can see the estimated PQPs and PQFs a flight will earn when selecting a fare during booking.

If you need a lot of United PQPs and want to take this to the next level, it is possible to earn PQPs at a faster rate with some United partners. You can learn all about that strategy here.

Finally, if you have an upcoming United flight, you may be able to purchase additional PQPs through United's Premier Accelerator program.

To check, open an existing reservation and look for the Award Accelerator option, which lets you purchase additional redeemable miles.

Depending on your reservation, you may also see a Premier Accelerator option that lets you purchase PQPs along with redeemable miles, as shown below.

This isn't usually a cheap way to earn status, but it can be useful if you're just short of your goal and don't want to take another flight.

United is making some big changes to elite status qualification beginning in 2027, including eliminating the PQF-plus-PQP qualification pathway and raising the PQP-only requirement for Premier 1K.

That means earning status could get harder for some United travelers next year.

If you expect to rely heavily on United credit card spending to earn PQPs toward status, as I do, there's another reason to consider getting a United card before the end of 2026.

If you have an eligible United card by Dec. 31, 2026, you'll get a 5% PQP boost in 2027. For example, if you earned Platinum status in 2026, you'll get a 900-PQP boost to start 2027.

And remember, the earns PQPs faster than United's other personal cards, so it might be the best choice if every PQP is going to count.

It also currently offers an increased welcome bonus of 90,000 miles after spending $5,000 on purchases in the first three months from account opening. Plus, an additional 10,000 bonus miles when you add an authorized user in the first three months from account opening.

But no matter what United status level you are going for or how you plan to get there, just remember that all of your activities must be completed by Dec. 31, 2026, to count toward status this year.
