’Frozen’s Kristen Bell and Idina Menzel Officially Reunite for a New Disney Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Disney's rich history of animation success continued earlier this year, as its latest collaboration with Pixar proved hugely popular. Toy Story 5, the latest installment in the groundbreaking animated series, scored more than $1 billion in a mammoth box office run, as audiences in their many millions booked tickets to see the return of Woody (Tom Hanks), Buzz (Tim Allen), Jessie (Joan Cusack), and the rest of the gang.

Called "a necessary response to how much the world has changed in the past three decades," in Taylor Gates' review for Collider, this timely animated favorite was considered a likely contender for the Best Animated Feature prize at the Academy Awards, only for LAIKA to return with the fantasy masterpiece Wildwood a few months later.

Either way, Disney struck gold once again with Toy Story 5, 13 years after producing another pop culture phenomenon with Frozen. Unable to let the IP go, the company is returning to the Frozen pool for a brand-new series that features several returning favorites. The Real Frozen (currently a working title) is the latest natural history series collaboration between National Geographic and Walt Disney Animation Studios.

Lion, based on The Lion King, Emmy-winner A Real Bug's Life, and the upcoming The Real Finding Nemo are the first three entries into the growing series, which expands with one of Disney's biggest IPs. Perhaps most excitingly, Kristen Bell, Idina Menzel, and Jonathan Groff will return as Anna, Elsa, and Kristoff, respectively, with it reported that Josh Gad will also appear as Olaf. This news comes following the first plot details for Frozen 3 being revealed at D23 in August. Frozen 4 is also in development.

According to a synopsis, The Real Frozen reimagines the characters of Frozen "as if they were inhabiting the real worlds depicted throughout the series." The synopsis continues:

"Set against the breathtaking icy landscapes of Iceland, Finland, Norway and Sweden, The Real Frozen follows real-life animals whose fortitude, loyalty, resilience and relationships echo the emotional heart of the Frozen franchise. These animals navigate the harshest winters, revealing extraordinary behaviors, survival strategies and bonds."
