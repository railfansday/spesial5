Stardew Valley Officially Returns January 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Stardew Valley might just be the greatest cozy game of all time. Created by solo developer ConcernedApe (AKA Eric Barone) more than 10 years ago, it has since gone on to conquer a very large corner of the cozy gaming world. With its chill vibes and focus on farming and relationship mechanics, it was one of the first cozy games to truly grip a large gaming community. ConcernedApe's next game, Haunted Chocolatier, still has an unknown release date, so it's nice to know that new content for Stardew Valley is coming very soon.

Whether you are into farming, mining, fishing, romancing or just socializing, Stardew Valley is a game that has something for everyone. It offers a laid-back approach and allows players to slow down, remove themselves from the rat race for a few hours and simply perform the chilled tasks that best suit their mood. Carrying that laid back vibe forward then, a new release on Amazon might just be what the doctor ordered. If you're looking for a little less screen time and perhaps want a new relaxing hobby, the book that's landing in January might just be the ticket.

Inspired by the creatures and animals of Stardew Valley, The Official Stardew Valley Crochet Book will be available in January 2027 for all budding hobbyists. This book will give enthusiasts an extra reason to slow down and create wonderful crochet projects that look like they were lifted straight from the video game itself. With 25 patterns to learn and over 40 projects to work through, it should offer challenges to both advanced users and beginners alike.

Created in collaboration with ConcernedApe, The Official Stardew Valley Crochet Book is filled with original illustrations, character tips and headnotes. It's the perfect way to pick up and master a new hobby while also creating friendship-boosting gifts for your loved ones. Who wouldn't love to receive a Mermaid pendant just in time for the holidays, or perhaps some some infamous purple pants instead to display on your shelf?

The Official Stardew Valley Crochet Book goes on sale on the 26 January 2027 and is available digitally for your Kindle for $13.99, or you can purchase a hardback version for $32.00. In a world where physical media seem to be disappearing, that feels like a much-needed purchase for fans of Stardew Valley, along with a fun opportunity to whip out the needles and knock out a few geodes or perhaps a legendary Galaxy sword or two.

With January ushering in the season of 'New Year . . . new me', it might be the perfect time to pick up a new hobby, and sitting down with a cuppa and some crochet needles sounds easier than signing up to the gym. With a little patience and a bit of grinding, you too could have hundreds of cute Junimos scattered around your living room. The Official Stardew Valley Crochet Book feels like the perfect companion piece for any Stardew Valley fan seeking that cozy gaming lifestyle in the real world.
