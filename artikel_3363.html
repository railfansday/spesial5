Netflix has confirmed the new movies and shows heading to the popular streaming service this week. I’ll be upfront: it’s not exactly the most packed slate of the year, but there are still new arrivals that you’ll want to add to your watchlist (and one to watch live), so I’m picking out the highlights below.

Let’s start with a new WWII movie. This one debuted in theaters (a rarity for a Netflix original movie), and received largely lukewarm reviews. But its theme of bravery and brotherhood on the front line of a devastating conflict should still resonate. And it incorporates a very important football game, so it actually ties in with my second pick of the week, Netflix's NFL gameday.

On the eve of Thanksgiving, Netflix will be the place to watch the Packers vs. Rams. Both teams have identical records this season, so it could be a closely contested matchup. And if football isn’t for you, how about the return of a Netflix original drama that explores an even more cutthroat world than professional athletics: an upper-society clique in East Texas.
