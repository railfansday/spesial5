Falcons place DE Samson Ebukam on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Falcons placed defensive end Samson Ebukam on injured reserve Tuesday, the team announced.

Coach Kevin Seiftert already said the Falcons were likely without Ebukam and wide receiver Zachariah Branch this week in Madrid. Both players have a hamstring injury.
