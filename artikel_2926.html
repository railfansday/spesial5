49ers’ optimism grows for Mike Evans’ return in massive Vikings game

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The 49ers might have one of their best offensive players back after a lengthy absence Sunday night against the Vikings in Mexico City.

Receiver Mike Evans was expected to be a limited participant in practice for the second day in a row on Friday and listed as questionable, 49ers coach Kyle Shanahan told reporters, giving him a chance to be available for one of the biggest games of the regular season.

Mike Evans (5) of the San Francisco 49ers runs on the field against the Washington Commanders at Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara, Calif., Oct. 19, 2026. Getty Images

"He had a good day of practice yesterday. As always, he's a little sore today but going in the right direction," Shanahan said.

Evans has missed San Francisco's last three games after leaving near the end of the first half of the Oct. 19 win over the Commanders with Achilles tendinitis. Yet despite missing the future Hall of Famer, the 49ers have scored 38 and 46 in their last two games in wins over the Raiders and Cowboys, respectively.

Shanahan was asked what kind of boost Evans would give the offense if he could return against the Vikings' vaunted defense that enters the weekend ranked second in the NFL in EPA per pass attempt and first in quarterback pressure rate, according to Next Gen Stats.

"A big one," Shanahan said. "Having Mike out there, everyone knows how good Mike is. So I think the guys have done a good job of holding it down without him out there. But Mike's our guy and a very unique player, been doing it at a high level for a very long time and played really unbelievable in the games he played in this year. It'd be great to get him back."

Shanahan spoke before practice and noted Evans would be limited after practicing for the first time since his injury on Thursday with the team in Colorado Springs, Colo., preparing for the altitude at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City.

NFL Network's Ian Rapoport said the 49ers are optimistic Evans can play on Sunday night. Though he compared Evans' injury to what running back Christian McCaffrey dealt with in 2024, when he missed the first eight games due to bilateral Achilles tendinitis.

Mike Evans (5) of the San Francisco 49ers runs after a catch against the Washington Commanders in Santa Clara, California, Oct. 19, 2026. Getty Images

"It feels like to me there's a really good chance," Rapoport said on "The Pat McAfee Show." "It's a tough injury. When Christian McCaffrey had it, and obviously, he had it on both feet, both legs. So a little bit different. But I don't think anyone saw McCaffrey being out as long as he did.

"So it's a tough injury [for Evans], and I don't really want to assume because you never know how it's going to react. But there is optimism that he goes this week. And that would be pretty significant. If he could go, and this is an issue that's in the past for him, not something he's going to continue to worry about, then a very, very good receiver joins a very, very good team. I think we were kinda wondering, how would this 49ers' offense be without him? And they looked pretty good. So adding him to the mix would only help."
