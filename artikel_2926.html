Chiefs give injury status updates for TE Travis Kelce, WR Rashee Rice

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Tight end Travis Kelce and wide receiver Rashee Rice fully participated in the Kansas City Chiefs' practice session on Thursday ahead of the team's highly anticipated Week 9 matchup against the New York Jets.

The star duo was limited during workouts on Wednesday, which threatened their status for the Chiefs' fast-approaching showdown with New York.

Kelce is said to be dealing with a lingering ankle injury. Rice has injuries to his hamstring and knee that kept him from playing in Weeks 6 and 7.

An official update with status changes for Kansas City's injured players was posted to Twitter by the Chiefs' official account on November 5:

Unless something changes during Kansas City's final practice session in Week 9 this afternoon, fans should expect to see Kelce and Rice take the field to face the Jets on Sunday as the Chiefs look to end their two-game losing streak in front of a home crowd at Arrowhead Stadium.
