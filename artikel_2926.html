Former Arizona wing Dalen Terry signs with Thunder

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Dalen Terry is on an NBA roster after starting the season on the sidelines.

The former Arizona Wildcats wing has signed a two-year deal with the Oklahoma City Thunder, according to ESPN's Shams Charania.

The contract comes at a good time for both sides: Terry was without an NBA team after getting by the Golden State Warriors in the preseason, and the Thunder are in need for an athletic swingman after suffering multiple injuries.

Terry is on his third NBA team after playing the first four-plus years of his career with the Chicago Bulls before getting traded to the Philadelphia 76ers.

In 218 career games, Terry is averaging 3.5 points, 1.7 rebounds, 1.2 assists and o.5 steals per game.

The 6-foot-6 Terry arrives in Oklahoma City following injuries to the Thunder's Jalen Williams and Cason Wallace. The Thunder are also without big man Chet Holmgren for at least two months.

Terry was drafted 18th overall by Chicago in the 2022 NBA Draft. He comes the 17th former Arizona basketball player to be on an NBA team this season, the third-most of any school behind Kentucky and Duke.
