Texas A&M vs. Oklahoma State live updates: Score, highlights, analysis

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Texas A&M is 3-0 on the season after defeating UL Monroe 108-67 on Monday night, led by transfer guard PJ Haggerty (22 points) and Jordan Pope (19 points) scoring efforts, combined with fellow transfer guard Tyshawn Archie's breakout performance, as the former McNeese standout scored 12 points, which included his emphatic dunk that resulted in becoming SportsCenter's No. 1 clip.

On Thursday night, second-year coach Bucky McMillan and his revamped roster face their first legitimate challenge of the season, hosting 2-0 Oklahoma State after falling to 87-63 in Stillwater last season. Both teams are averaging over 100 points per game, while the Aggies hold a slight talent edge, with PJ Haggerty and Jordan Pope both averaging 18 points per game and able to take over at any moment.

In the frontcourt, senior forward Mackenzie Mgbako is close to reaching his full potential, while transfer Jalen Shelley is averaging 10.7 points and 9 rebounds per game, making an immediate impact on defense.

However, transfer forward Cade Phillips suffered an apparent knee injury during Monday's blowout win and is not expected to play on Thursday night. This means freshman center Josh Irving, who scored eight points and grabbed five rebounds against UL Monroe, will see increased playing time against Oklahoma State.
