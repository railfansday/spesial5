Puka Nacua will undergo knee surgery and likely miss the rest of the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

With the football-following world still processing the fact that Rams receiver Puka Nacua will be able to serve a three-game suspension while on injured reserve (and missing at least four games), Nacua apparently will miss more than the minimum.

Via Nate Atkins of The Athletic, coach Sean McVay said Friday that Nacua will undergo surgery to repair a torn MCL and likely will miss the rest of the season.

Per McVay, Nacua is out “a couple months.”

In two months exactly, the divisional round will be looming. The Rams will have to get to the playoffs — and past the divisional round — without him.

Then, the question will be whether he’s ready for the round of eight. And so on, for as long as the Rams survive.

Meanwhile, Rams receiver Davante Adams remains on IR with a hamstring injury.

This week, the Rams are on a bye. They host the Packers on Wednesday night, followed by games against the Chiefs, 49ers, Cowboys, Seahawks, Buccaneers, and Seahawks.
