Texas Tech football RB Cameron Dickey aggravates ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Running back Cameron Dickey aggravated an injured ankle and left the Texas Tech football game against West Virginia early in the second quarter on Saturday, Nov. 7, at Galaxy Stadium.

Dickey hobbled off after he'd carried for 6 yards, alternately putting little or no weight on his left ankle. The play took place with 12:31 left in the second quarter.

Dickey suffered a high-ankle sprain in an Oct. 3 game at Colorado. The Red Raiders had an open date the following week, and Dickey missed the next two games.

Going into the game, the 5-foot-10, 215-pound junior from Austin Crockett had carried 67 times for 276 yards and 4 touchdowns.

Dickey was a Doak Walker Award semifinalist last year when he rushed for 1,124 yards and led the Big 12 in rushing touchdowns with 14 and total touchdowns with 16.
