Sixers Bell Ringer: Sixers undone by Portland’s blazing hot shooting

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Sixers Bell Ringer Season Standings:VJ Edgecombe – 4Jaylen Brown – 3.5Tyrese Maxey – 3.5Joel Embiid – 3LeBron James – 1Anfernee Simons – 1Dean Wade – 1

The Philadelphia 76ers simply could not stop the red hot hands of the Portland Trail Blazers in a 128-111 loss on Monday night.

Joel Embiid did end up missing this one due to illness after spending the day as questionable on the injury report. Jabari Walker started in his absence. The Sixers were otherwise healthy.

This was a shootout early with some hot shooting from both teams in the first half. The Sixers hit 57.8% from the floor and 37.5% from long range, with the Trail Blazers hitting 50.0% and 46.3% respectively. It all culminated in a LeBron James buzzer-beating three to end the half and knot things at 69-69 going into the break.

The Trail Blazers, unfortunately, remained red hot from long range. It was a dual problem: partially due to the Sixers' help defense leaving way too much room for guys on the perimeter, and partially due to Portland just knocking down absolutely everything. They continued firing as the Sixers went cold, building a double-digit lead early in the final frame. Shooting regression certainly had hit Philadelphia… but Portland seemed immune. The Sixers couldn't hit a damn thing and the Trail Blazers were able to pull away fairly comfortably.

The Sixers are now 14-4 on the season as their win streak has been cut short at five games. They're off Tuesday and will be back on Wednesday evening for a Thanksgiving Eve game.

Until then, let's get to the Bell Ringer. Most of these candidates are here for their first half effort more so than the rough, cold second half that affected pretty much everyone in a Sixers uniform.

Lebron James: 23 points (8-for-13 field goal, 4-for-5 3PT shooting), 6 rebounds, 3 assists

(Photo by David Dow/NBAE via Getty Images)

Especially in the absence of Joel Embiid, there was extra work on LeBron James shoulders to really be the engine of the Sixers' offense. Of course he was up to the challenge, he's the King. After a first half of efficient shooting and some patented incredible dimes to teammates, LeBron had 16 points on 6-for-8 field goal, 3-for-3 three-point shooting with three assists going into the break. Those three triples included a buzzer beater to tie the game at the end of the second frame. He suffered from the same cold stagnant streak that hit the rest of the Sixers in the second half, but it was an incredible few periods from LeBron.

James finished with 23 points on 8-for-13 field goal, 4-for-5 three-point shooting with six rebounds and three assists.

Jaylen Brown: 20 points, 2 rebounds, 4 assists, 2 steals

(Photo by Jesse D. Garrabrant/NBAE via Getty Images)

Jaylen Brown just makes it look so easy sometimes. And he made it look easy on a night returning from a few games off due to a hamstring strain. Whether it was being in the right place at the right time or working isolation to create shot opportunities out of nothing, Brown looked healthy and ready to work tonight. By halftime, Brown was leading the Sixers with 19 points on 8-for-10 field goal, 1-for-2 three-point shooting with three assists. He unfortunately picked up his fourth foul early in the third which sent him to the bench and, to be honest, the Sixers' offensive momentum took a seat with him. That was the beginning of the end for the Sixers. Really illustrates how critical Brown can be to this team's offensive groove.

Brown finished with 20 points on 8-for-14 field goal shooting with two rebounds, four assists and two steals.

Tyrese Maxey: 22 points, 2 rebounds, 5 assists

(Photo by Jesse D. Garrabrant/NBAE via Getty Images) | NBAE via Getty Images

It was far from Tyrese Maxey's best night, I'll be honest. He had some inconsistencies shooting the ball so he couldn't really get going for any of the one-man streaks he tends to string together. But he wasn't discouraged, and instead kept waiting for better opportunities to come and chipped in points where he could get them to fall. This was especially true in the third period — critical, as everyone else for the Sixers went relatively cold during that frame — where he knocked in a a number of big buckets to keep the Trail Blazers close as they tried to pull away. After scoring just 12 in the first half, Maxey added 10 points in the third to give the Sixers a fighting chance. They didn't take advantage of that by any means, but they still had a chance.

It was an up and down night, but Maxey ended up one of the Sixers' best scorers — 22 points on the night with two rebounds and five assists.
