Journalist says Juventus has been forced to focus on top four finish

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Juventus are regarded as one of the biggest clubs in Italian football history, and expectations are always high whenever they begin a new season. Challenging for the Serie A title should be a regular ambition for the Bianconeri, given their history and financial strength.

The club consistently invests heavily in its squad to improve its chances of success, while the manager receives considerable backing to deliver results. However, Juventus have struggled this season and are no longer being seriously discussed as title contenders.

Their immediate concern is securing a place in the top four, especially after missing out on Champions League qualification last season. Another failure would represent a significant setback for the club and increase the pressure on the manager.

With the title race seemingly beyond their immediate reach, attention has turned towards securing a return to Europe's biggest club competition.

Journalist Gianni Balzarini believes the club's priorities have changed, with financial considerations now making Champions League qualification particularly important.

Returning to regular Champions League football would provide Juventus with an opportunity to restore some stability and generate the revenue needed to strengthen their position.

(Photo by Valerio Pennicino/Getty Images)

Discussing the club's situation, Balzarini explained the importance of finishing in the top four, via Tuttojuve:

"Now, for the club, winning a Scudetto isn't so important anymore; what matters is finishing in the top four, to get the Champions League revenue, and the proof is in the fact that this capital increase, which will be approved, responds precisely to this need. The club is 66 million down, eight more than last year, due to lost Champions League revenue. I mean, you always start from that base."

His comments highlight the financial consequences of missing out on Champions League football and explain why Juventus are prioritising qualification.

Although challenging for the Scudetto should remain a major ambition, the Bianconeri must first secure a return to Europe's elite competition.
