Hawkeye Women’s Basketball: Montana State at Iowa Fan Discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Iowa women's basketball team returns to the comfortable confines of Carver Hawkeye Arena to host Montana State tonight at 7:00 PM CT. Thursday's matchup against Montana State will be their first ever meeting. The game will air on B1G+ and the Hawkeye Radio Network.

The Bobcats are coached by Tricia Binford who is entering her 22nd season at Montana State. They are 2–0 entering Thursday's matchup and are coming off an 11-day layoff after their game at UNLV on November 15 was canceled due to travel complications caused by the nationwide FAA ground stop. Last season Montana State finished 27–8 overall (16–2 in the Big Sky) and played in the NIT, reaching the quarterfinals.The Big Sky Conference is a middle-tier, single-bid mid-major league in Division I women's basketball (meaning only the conference champion is selected into the NCAA tournament). The Bobcats made the NCAA tourney in 2025 and 2022.Montana State will be challenged to match Iowa's size, athleticism, and scoring depth. The Bobcats do not list any centers on their roster, and run an up-tempo, perimeter-oriented five-out offense that relies on mobile, versatile forwards & guards who can space the floor and run in transition. Players to watch are sophomore forward Addison Harris (20.5 PPG, 6.5 RPG), sophomore guard Taylee Chirrick (8.0 RPG, 3.5 APG), and returning senior guard Dylan Philip. They do have one 6-foot-5 player, Emma Dongelman, but she is a first-year player with a lean frame versus a true center in the paint who sees limited minutes.For Iowa, keeping Ava Heiden out of foul trouble will be a key to the game, and really a key throughout the season. Backup Layla Hays has not progressed from her first season to year two as we normally see post players evolve under Jan Jensen. Chit Chat Wright broke out as a scoring threat in the game against UConn, and for the new (to this season) 4-out 1-in offensive scheme to work, Iowa needs the guards to score and give the opponent multiple threats they must guard.
