2027 NFL Draft order: Titans move into top spot

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

With the Los Angeles Chargers' 23-20 win over the Baltimore Ravens last night, the Tennessee Titans now sit atop the 2027 NFL Draft order. Both the Chargers and Titans are 1-8, but the Titans have the easier schedule, so they own the tiebreaker. The Miami Dolphins are the only other 1-win team. They sit at #3 based on strength of schedule.

There is still a long way to go before this is final, obviously. It is tough to see the Dolphins winning another game. The Titans aren't going to win many games, but they will have a good chance to beat the Colts at home in December. I still see the Chargers getting to 3 or 4 wins, so they probably will be a little further down.

Right behind those teams, the New York Jets and Philadelphia Eagles sit at two wins. The Jets still have games with the Chargers and Dolphins. They will likely win at least one of those. As for the Eagles, it is impossible to know what the rest of their season is going to look like with their quarterback situation being the way that it is.

As you can see, the Titans don't lose the strength-of-schedule tiebreaker until you get down to the Texans, who have three wins and will likely win a couple more games.
