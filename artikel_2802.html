Gael Monfils appointed captain of France's Davis Cup team following retirement

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Gael Monfils has been appointed France's Davis Cup captain a month after calling time on his playing career.

The flamboyant Frenchman, known for entertaining both on and off court, quietly retired in October - appearing on the International Tennis Integrity Agency's inactive list without an explanation - amid the breakdown of his marriage with Ukrainian player Elina Svitolina.

Having initially planned to bid farewell to the sport with one last appearance at the Paris Masters, he was instead honoured with a ceremony in the French capital earlier this week, joined by fellow fan favourites Jo-Wilfried Tsonga, Gilles Simon, Richard Gasquet and Benoit Paire.

He now transitions into a courtside role after succeeding Paul-Henri Mathieu as captain for France's upcoming Davis Cup campaign, with qualifying in February.

"It is a great source of pride to become captain of the French national team," Monfils said. "This blue jersey represents so much to me. I want to build a real collective project and create the dynamic that will allow us to win titles together."

The French Tennis Federation (FFT) thanked Mathieu for his commitment in the role for the past three years, having guided France to the quarter-finals in 2025.

The 40-year-old represented France in eight editions of the Davis Cup during his 22-year professional career, winning 12 of his 15 singles matches.
