CSC Reacts Results: What’s the biggest obstacle preventing the Saints from becoming playoff contenders?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

In this week's reacts poll, we asked our readers: What is the biggest obstacle preventing the Saints from becoming playoff contenders?

An overwhelming 63% of voters said that the Saints biggest issues lie with coaching and playcalling.

15% of fans said they have confidence that the team is headed in the right direction.

With the Saints currently sitting at 3-7, frustration continues to mount by fans as coaching and offensive playcalling remains a major concern. Slow starts, a lack of consistency, questionable decisions, and missed opportunities have raised questions about the direction of the offense.

With 7 games remaining, there is still time for the Saints to address their issues, particularly on the offensive side of the ball. However, 10 games into the season, the same questions remain: Can these issues be resolved, and are coaching changes necessary?
