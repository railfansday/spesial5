4 takeaways from Rams’ first NFC West win in 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Los Angeles Rams' first NFC West victory of the 2026 season delivered several important takeaways that could shape the team's outlook for the remainder of the year. Their Week 10 win over the Arizona Cardinals was more than just another divisional victory; it offered a look at how much depth the Rams have beyond their superstars. From defensive dominance to offensive consistency, the Rams showed areas of strength while providing a few developments worth monitoring moving forward. Here are four takeaways from Sunday afternoon's road win…

Beaux Limmer. Bill Murray. Xavier Smith. CJ Daniels. Konata Mumpfield. Davis Allen. Yup, a group of Day 3 picks and undrafted free agents. Smith, Daniels, and Mumpfield combined for 143 yards and one touchdown. That doesn't include Tutu Atwell who was not on the team during training camp until after the final round of cuts. He accounted for 1 reception for 31 yards. The four receivers each had an explosive play for the offense.

Beaux Limmer started the game at left guard and found his way under center when Coleman Shelton (hamstring) and Dylan McMahon (knee) were knocked out of the game. Bill Murray then filled the left guard vacancy.

I predicted the Rams defensive front would get going. The defense notched four sacks in the first half. With Aaron Donald recording his first sack since 2023.

This doesn't even include Kobie Turner who should return within the next 2-3 weeks at most.
