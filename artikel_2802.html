Warriors’ Steve Kerr Delivers Blunt Assessment Amid Injury-Hit Skid

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It's been a rollercoaster start to the season for the Golden State Warriors. They began the campaign with three straight losses before turning things around and rattling off an eight-game win streak. Now, though, Steve Kerr's team finds itself on a five-game skid and without Stephen Curry, who is absent from the rotation due to a right oblique abdominal tear.

When speaking to the media on Thursday, November 19, ahead of the Warriors matchup with the Memphis Grizzlies, which Golden State ultimately lost 126-98, Steve Kerr provided a blunt assessment of the issues his team is currently facing.

"This is just where we are right now. We're wiped out with injuries," Kerr said, via Dalton Johnson. "The guys are really competing and playing hard and staying together and that's all we can do. Things are going to turn, but we've got to give ourselves the best chance."

Right now, the Warriors need patience. At some point this season, Jimmy Butler, Moses Moody and Curry will all return to the rotation. Once that happens, Golden State's rotation will look far stronger. Until then, the Warriors are going to endure some difficult stretches of basketball.

Fortunately for the Warriors, rookie Yaxel Lendeborg has looked fantastic to begin his NBA career, and with so many high-usage players sidelined, he's being given the room and the green light to figure things out on the fly. The results might not be what fans are expecting, but the experience Lendeborg is currently gaining will be invaluable to his development.

Of course, Warriors fans won't want to hear that until the franchise has halted its latest skid down the Western Conference standings.
