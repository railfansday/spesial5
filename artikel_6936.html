Montu: Nurse Practitioner

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Alternaleaf is Australia’s largest telehealth provider of its kind, delivering safe and trusted services to more than 100,000 patients each year. Our goal is to help Australians access alternative healthcare with ease via telehealth consultations with experienced nurses and doctors. Join us in an Australia-based, 100% remote role where exceptional clinicians bring their purpose to life.

Most clinics talk about expanding access to care. At Alternaleaf, you’ll help shape how we do it. As a new part of our sector-leading Commitment to Care patient model we're looking for experienced nurse practitioners who want their scope of practice to count for something new.

The Alternaleaf clinic is part of Montu, one of the largest healthtech companies in Australia. As a new Nurse Practitioner, you will help strengthen our sector-leading clinical governance, continuity of care, and patient safety standards that we pride ourselves on at Alternaleaf.

You’ll be part of the group that helps define how NP-led care works here, so your clinical judgement, feedback and ideas will directly influence what comes next.

Conduct telehealth consults. You’ll conduct clinical assessments including reviewing patient histories and symptoms (including DASS and QOL), talking patients through their treatment options, and making sure their consent is clearly understood and documented.

Prescribe safely and compliantly. In line with your clinical assessment, you’ll prescribe S4 and S8 medications in accordance with the NMBA NP Standards for Practice, submit SAS applications and adhere to RTPM requirements.

Own continuity of care. You’ll run new and follow-up consults, develop and adjust treatment plans as patients progress, and pick up patients transferred from other practitioners so their care continues without a gap.

Educate and improve. You’ll help patients understand their treatment, side effects and access pathways, and you’ll contribute to internal education and cross-functional projects that lift clinical standards.

Keep compliance tight. You’ll document accurately, meet TGA reporting requirements, and ensure compliance isright every time.

Run your own consult list. You’ll assess, treat and follow up with patients via telehealth, reviewing histories, chronic conditions, and DASS and QOL assessments to build treatment plans with them.

Current AHPRA registration and endorsement as a Nurse Practitioner with prescribing rights

3–5+ years of post-registration clinical experience, ideally in primary care, chronic disease or mental health

Solid knowledge of TGA guidelines and controlled-substance prescribing, including S8

Sharp clinical assessment skills and meticulous documentation

The confidence to hold boundaries and keep consults clinically focused, plus the warmth to make telehealth feel personal
