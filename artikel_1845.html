'Long may it continue' - Crawley Town boss believes fans can play key role in fight for survival

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Goals from Kai Jennings and Pemi Aderoju were enough to secure a 2-1 victory at the Broadfield Stadium.

It was a first win at home for the Reds since March 28 – Colin Kazim-Richards’ first game in charge and a 2-0 win over Gillingham.

Blackwell, 67, was appointed manager until the end of the season on Thursday (November 19).

And the 67-year-old has made a strong impression with the Crawley faithful so far.

“They were the 12th man today,” he said, after the game. “Because when we needed them, they gave us that vocal support that really, really helped us.

“They were that loud at times, they [the players] couldn't hear what I was trying to shout across the pitch. So, listen, long may that continue, but we've given them something to shout [about] – that's the thing.

“The fans will respond if we do our job as professional people, footballers, and they've done their job today and the fans have responded.

“And I made this comment in our previous meetings that we've had – if we're going to get out of the trouble we're in, it's all of us. Me, the players, the staff, the owners, the fans – it's us. It's Crawley Town Football Club, that's who'll get out of this.”

Will Davies’ 79th-minute goal for the Cod Army made it a nervy finish to the contest.

The Broadfield erupted in a mixture of elation and relief at full time – the latter particularly understandable given the difficult start to the season.

Blackwell said: “If you don’t win, going into late November and you haven’t won at home yet, I think everyone was nervous. Because to win the game is a rarity – that can’t be right.

“We've got to make sure that becomes the norm. Your home form is what normally keeps you in the league. So, our home form has got to improve. As I said, I'm under no illusion here. We know it's going to be a tough one, but we know that if we work like that, and we've had 21 days since the last game, and let me tell you, they have worked very, very hard.

“And today you saw a lot more powerful running, a lot more aggression in tackles and overall a good shape to the team. So, a lot of pluses today, but as I said to the boys in there after the game, ‘we've won, but that wasn't right and that wasn't right’, having complimented them on how well they did. So, there's still things to improve on and we'll keep doing that.”
