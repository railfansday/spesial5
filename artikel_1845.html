‘I’m very healthy’ – Kompany returning to the dugout when Bayern host Koln

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Vincent Kompany will return to the touchline for the first time since October, when Bayern Munich host Koln in the Bundesliga on Friday.

Kompany has been recuperating after being taken to hospital following a collapse at the club’s training ground last month. He was subsequently diagnosed with meningeal irritation.

In his absence, Bayern have continued their strong start to the season and sit top of the table after nine games with 21 points (W6 D3).

They went into the international break on the back of a 2-1 win over Mainz, and they are looking to move clear of Borussia Dortmund before the rest of the weekend's action.

"It was very intense," he said. "At the time, it was a new experience.

"Looking back, everything went perfectly. I received support right away from the club and the doctors. I'm very grateful.

"You try to stay calm and wait until you have the results. It was a positive process; all the tests were positive.

"I have to say, I'm very healthy. We did a lot of tests, and they all came back fine. In those quiet moments, you also try to learn.

"Now I feel very good again, and I'm happy to be back with the team."

While Bayern are enjoying an unbeaten start to the season, Koln are sat down in 15th, having accumulated just nine points so far.

They are winless in their last four matches (D2 L2), but no matter what, Kompany is relishing the contest.

"There's one person at Bayern who is really motivated for this game against Koln. I've been waiting for four weeks," he said.

"It wasn't difficult to get myself in the right frame of mind. I've had plenty of time. Motivation is always high for every match, and it certainly is for this one.

"I hope I can convey that to the team. They're just back from international duty. It's a Friday game, so anything can happen. If the players can sense my motivation, we'll have a good foundation for the match."

Kane has failed to score in his last four matches in all competitions, his joint-longest goal drought for Bayern.

However, he has already provided eight assists in the Bundesliga this season, a new record for a player after nine matchdays since Opta began recording this data in 2004-05 and already equalling his personal record for a single Bundesliga season (also 2023-24 and 2024-25).

El Mala recently scored his 16th Bundesliga goal for Koln; only Dieter Muller (32) and Lukas Podolski (22) scored more Bundesliga goals for the club before their 21st birthday.

Among all U21 players in Europe's big-five leagues since the start of last season, only Barcelona's Lamine Yamal (26) has scored more goals than El Mala (16).

Bayern have not lost any of their last 22 Bundesliga matches against Koln (W20 D2) since a 2-3 away defeat in February 2011.

Among current Bundesliga teams, Bayern only have a longer unbeaten run against Schalke (24 matches).

Indeed, Bayern are the only team from Europe's big-five leagues to remain unbeaten in all competitions this season (W11 D5). They have lost only one of their last 41 matches in all competitions across two seasons, a 4-5 defeat at Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League semi-final first leg in late April.

Of all teams to have played continuously in the Bundesliga since the start of last season, Koln have won the fewest points over the last 34 matchdays (27), while Bayern have won the most (83).

Over the last eight matchdays, only Borussia Monchengladbach have won fewer points (four) than Koln (six).

Koln are also winless in their last 17 Bundesliga away games (D7 L10), the longest current run of any team in the competition and equalling their negative club record set in 2003-04.
