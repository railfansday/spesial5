Arsenal: David Raya and Jurrien Timber on the verge of signing new deals as Mikel Arteta gets his wish

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Jurrien Timber and David Raya are set to commit their long-term futures to Arsenal.

The Gunners have made a push to agree new deals with the core of their Premier League-winning squad, as the club look to kick on and dominate domestically and in Europe.

Declan Rice has reached an agreement on a contract that will bring him in line with Bukayo Saka as the club’s best-paid player on around £350,000-a-week.

Andrea Berta has been working on several other extension alongside that and two more players are on the verge of signing new deals.

Timber is understood to have agreed improved terms to stay at the club, having entered the final two years of his existing deal.

The Dutchman has been hampered by injury issues during his time at Arsenal but when fit has been consistently trusted by Mikel Arteta.

Speaking after the Netherlands were beaten 5-0 by Germany on Monday night, shortly after reports of a contract agreement broke, Timber effectively confirmed that new was correct.

“Those are good reports,” Timber said. “They are definitely on the right track. It will all work out, yes.”

In addition to that, Raya is also expected to sign a new deal with the club.

The Spaniard has established himself as one of the best goalkeepers in the world and last week was named in the 2026 FIFPRO World 11.

Raya became only the second Arsenal player to make that team, 20 years on from Thierry Henry being included in 2006.

The 31-year-old was handed an improved salary last year, though that adjustment to his contract did not extend the length of the deal.

Raya is now close to finalising a new long-term contract that will keep him in north London into his mid-30s.

It is an emphatic response to recent discussions on Raya potentially eyeing a move to Spain at the end of his current contract.

Speaking to Marca last month, Raya said: “I would love to play in La Liga one day. Right now, I’m very happy at Arsenal. But it’s true that I’d love to play in LaLiga one day. It’s a very competitive league.

“I’m Spanish and I’d love to play in Spain, that’s obvious.”

Arteta, though, has been adamant that he wants Raya to remain at Arsenal in the seasons ahead.

“I think David has to play in Spain at some point… on the beach, with his friends in the summer,” Arteta said in response to Raya’s interview.

“But for the next few years he needs to play here for Arsenal.”

Arteta looks set to get his wish, with Raya joining Rice and Timber in ensuring he spends the prime years of his career at Arsenal.
