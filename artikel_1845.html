Chelsea international watch: Blues stars shining in bid to impress Xabi Alonso

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Chelsea players have been starring for the England youth teams already during the international break.

While Morgan Rogers is the only Chelsea representative in Thomas Tuchel’s England senior team this month, a number of younger Chelsea players have been impressing for the England age groups.

The 16-year-old midfielder Mahdi Nicoll-Jazuli has been in and around Xabi Alonso’s first team since pre-season and it was easy to see why on Wednesday as he struck a hat-trick for England’s Under-17s beat Montenegro 6-1.

Reggie Watson and Josh Henry also started that match, with Nicoll-Jazuli also grabbing an assist to go with his treble before coming off after the hour mark.

England’s U19s then beat Slovenia 4-1, with Chizaram Ezenwata earning an assist. Reggie Walsh started that game, while fellow Chelsea youngsters Landon Emenalo and Freddy Bernal came off the bench as substitutes against the Slovenians.

Riley Ebho scored the winning goal for England U18s against the Netherlands in a 3-2 victory, with Ryan Kavuma-McQueen, on loan at Portsmouth, scoring the opening goal with a mazy run towards the end of box and stunning effort into the top corner of the net.
