Shaboozey & 'Summer House' Star Ciara Miller Get Cozy on the Dance Floor

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Shaboozey and "Summer House" star Ciara Miller were spotted getting extra cozy on the dance floor ... before dancing together.

In the video taken at RAISING CANE’S F1 party at Fontainebleau Las Vegas ... Shaboozey can be seen whispering something in Ciara's ear, before pulling some serious moves.

You may remember ... Ciara starred in the music video for Shaboozey's "Cowgirl" back in June.

But their creative collab didn't end there ... Ciara later came on "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon" in July to dance alongside the singer.

We reported earlier ... Ciara and fellow "Summer House" star Kyle Cooke shared a kiss during a spin the bottle game on the show.

The smooch was shared in the teaser clip for the upcoming season ... months after Ciara's ex West Wilson and Kyle's ex-wife Amanda Batula confirmed they were dating.

Who knows ... maybe Ciara has a new romantic interest in her life.

We've reached out to their reps for comment ... so far, no word back.
