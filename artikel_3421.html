Skydance's New Batman Could Launch DC's Best Shared Universe After James Gunn's Exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Skydance has a new Batman project on the way, and amid the uncertainty about the DC Universe's future right now, it could be what sets up a new shared universe for DC. James Gunn and Peter Safran revealed in 2023 a 10-year plan for DC Studios, with the first projects in the DCU Chapter One including movies and TV shows.

Since then, the franchise has had major hits, such as Superman and HBO's Lanterns series, modest success with projects like Clayface, and an outright box office bomb in Milly Alcock's Supergirl movie. More than the result of those projects, the biggest change to the DCU's plan rests in the exit of the stewards of the franchise, as Gunn and Safran are no longer running DC.

Skydance has replaced them with Jeremy Latcham, a studio head who brings along vast experience in the superhero business from his years working on getting the Marvel Cinematic Universe up and running alongside President Kevin Feige and others. For now, there have been no concrete updates on whether the DCU will continue without Gunn's creative vision or whether the DC franchise will be rebooted yet again.

Amid this uncertainty is DC's slate of Batman releases. In theory, Batman will make his DCU debut in The Brave and the Bold, though that project could shift entirely under the new leadership. Robert Pattinson will also return to the big screen in The Batman - Part II on February 18, 2028. That said, it is a TV show, Absolute Batman, that could launch DC's best shared universe after Gunn's exit.

DC has a packed slate of animated TV shows on the way, including DCU projects, such as Tom King's Mister Miracle series starring Josh Hutcherson and Mackenzie Davis, and Elseworlds releases, of which the Absolute Batman series is part of, existing in its own standalone pocket of DC. Absolute Batman is based on the DC Comics series of the same name, which is written by Scott Snyder and drawn by Nick Dragotta. Snyder returns as the showrunner of the upcoming Absolute Batman animated series, with Dragotta serving as a producer. Absolute Batman was one of the DC series announced at this year's Annecy International Animated Film Festival.

Given how Skydance's plans for DC long-term have yet to be revealed, with Latcham expected to make a slate announcement and explain the future of the DCU by the end of the year or the start of 2027, things could still change, so Absolute Batman is not guaranteed to be happening even though it was announced. That said, given just how monumental a release the DC comic book series has been for that medium, with issue #1 alone selling hundreds of thousands of copies in its first few weeks of release, it is likely to be made — Gunn or no Gunn — for either HBO Max or Prime Video.

With the DCU in disarray after losing Gunn's guiding hand, having a steady shared animated universe based on one of DC's most popular comic books ever and centered on Batman would be nice. Skydance and DC Studios have the whole Absolute Universe line of DC Comics to pull from, with key titles including Superman, Wonder Woman, Green Arrow, Martian Manhunter, and more. No matter what Latcham ends up doing with the DCU, the Absolute Universe on TV could thrive by banking on already beloved stories that reinvent DC's biggest heroes.

The first solo run in the Absolute Universe was Snyder's Absolute Batman, with the creator also being one of the masterminds behind the entire DC Comics Absolute franchise. As such, starting with an animated series adapting Absolute Batman, with Snyder as showrunner no less, seems like the perfect launch pad for a full-on shared universe on TV, and it would be nice to have some consistency after Gunn's DC exit.
