Pinterest simplifies parental controls

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The platform has updated its parental control dashboard to offer a more streamlined way to manage devices and access options.

Pinterest has updated its parental control elements to offer a more integrated, streamlined process and make it easier for parents to manage their child’s in-app experience.

As shown in these example screens, Pinterest’s updated parental controls dashboard now includes all of the app’s oversight and management options in one place, including profile visibility, content recommendations and connection controls.

The updated parental controls also include direct links to manage device-level screen time and location settings. Parents will also be able to make their kids’ profiles private by default and set limits on ad personalization and social features.

In addition, Pinterest added tools that allow parents to control the amount of artificial intelligence-generated content that teens see in the app. There’s also new notification management functionality.

Parents can also decide whether their teens have autoplay video switched on or not, which could help to combat compulsive viewing behaviors.

Pinterest CEO Bill Ready said the platform is focused on building an experience that’s age-appropriate for teens from the start, with protections that are built in, “not bolted on.”

“For parents who want more involvement, the controls are easy to find and simple to use,” Ready said. “Families can choose the experience that is right for them, while teens can continue to explore their interests and feel inspired.”

Pinterest has been working to present its app as an alternative to potentially harmful, engagement-focused approaches that have been adopted by other social media platforms.

In September, Pinterest published a report on how teens use the app, which showed that more than 77% of its teen audience uses Pinterest to explore creative interests.

More teen control options will provide additional peace of mind for parents, which could help to further establish Pinterest as a safer space for young users.
