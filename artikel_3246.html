The company sold the Hyatt Regency San Francisco at an attractive private market value and redeployed the proceeds into common and preferred stock repurchases, which management indicated increased net asset value and funds from operations per share. The company updated its full-year 2026 guidance upward to reflect strong performance in the urban and resort segments and higher expected contributions from recently renovated assets. Looking ahead to 2027, the company highlighted a stronger group booking calendar and reduced fixed charges resulting from its recent capital structure improvements.
