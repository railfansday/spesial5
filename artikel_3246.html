Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Gold and copper output held guidance while buybacks and net cash stood out.

Barrick Mining Corporation (B +4.22%) reported third quarter results that matched or exceeded the company's operational targets across its gold and copper portfolios. Management stated that safety and operational execution allowed the business to capture higher realized metal prices, leading to a 41% year-over-year increase in adjusted net earnings per share. The company reported a net cash position of $900 million and confirmed its 2026 production guidance ranges for both gold and copper. Management also indicated that the company is proceeding with a restructuring of North American assets, including an initial public offering for a new entity to hold those interests. Capital allocation remains focused on growth projects like the Lumwana expansion and returning cash to shareholders through dividends and a $3 billion buyback program.
