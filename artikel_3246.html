Billy Evans Claims He “Did Not Give Consent” For ‘You Can See Everything’ Nudity, Still Loves The Doc: “It’s Entertainment”

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

After finding out you really can see everything in A24’s Nathan Fielder/Lance Oppenheim doc, Billy Evans is sharing his side of the story.

Elizabeth Holmes’ husband has recently given his first interview about You Can See Everything, claiming that he “did not give consent” to feature his full-frontal nudity in the documentary, which has sparked Oscar buzz since its premiere last month.

“Now, did I know they were filming me in the shower? Yes. But we would film 16 hours a day,” he explained on CBS Sunday Morning. “Like, all sorts of things were filmed with assurances made. I was just as surprised as the rest of the country to have my whole body on camera for the world to see, and it’s been an awkward experience in having, like, your kids’ teachers see that. It’s really uncomfortable.

A24 told CBS in a statement, “Everything that was filmed was filmed with their consent.” Watch on Deadline

Evans confessed “I love the film,” despite being made out as a villain. “I would have preferred for that not to be the case, but it’s entertainment, you know, and I didn’t get to control that edit,” he said.

“I’m deeply passionate about my family and about the work that I do,” Evans said in the interview. “But no, I am not some controlling monster who’s leveraging his wife for insert-whatever-anybody-is-saying. No, I am not those things.”

The Haemanthus founded added of being villainized, “It’s super-painful, but me being angry about it doesn’t help change it. Look, I’m going to live my life with integrity and with who I am, and I hope people can see that over time. But if they don’t, what I care about is the people that I know, people that love me.”

Evans thinks A24 is “probably worried I’m gonna sue ’em” over his depiction, but he has no intention of taking legal action. “Because maybe I’m a different person than most people think I am,” he said.

In You Can See Everything, disgraced Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes invites Fielder into her and husband Billy Evans’ home to document everything in the month leading up to the start of her 11-year prison sentence on fraud charges.
