Massara on Juventus’ January plans: ‘If there are opportunities to improve, we’ll take them’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Frederic Massara has backed Jeff Ekhator to deliver for Juventus, insisting the young striker’s start against Omonia Nicosia tonight is “not an experiment”.

With quotes via TuttoMercatoWeb, the Bianconeri’s chief football officer was speaking to Sky Sport Italia before tonight’s Europa League clash at the Allianz Stadium.

Kick-off is at 21:00 CET [20:00 GMT] and you can follow all the action, as it happens, on our Live Blog.

“Reaching the play-offs is an important objective. I don’t think it can be achieved tonight, but a win is essential for us,” Massara said. “On paper, it might look like an easy game, but we have to be very wary, because Omonia have the quality to cause us problems.”

Asked whether Juventus are working on a new striker for January, he backed Ekhator.

“Tonight isn’t an experiment. Ekhator is a strong Under-21 player who has already shown his qualities in this league. He was born in 2006, and we know how heavy the Juventus shirt is. We mustn’t put excessive pressure on him, but we’re confident he can give us satisfaction.”

Ekhator was Juventus’ first signing of the summer. He joined from Genoa for a reported €16m transfer fee, plus bonuses, and signed a contract running until 30 June 2031.

Massara refused to rule out a move for another forward, but stressed that any deal would have to fit the club’s financial limits.

“As for the transfer market, it’s always very early. We have to see what can be done within the financial parameters we must respect. If there are opportunities to improve, we’ll be ready to take them,” he said.

He also defended Luciano Spalletti, who has been dealing with a long injury list that includes Kenan Yildiz, Khéphren Thuram and captain Manuel Locatelli.

“First of all, we hope to get some players back. The coach isn’t looking for excuses, but he’s had to deal with a big emergency. He’s been good in the past at finding solutions in difficult moments.”

Spalletti had recently suggested his squad lacked height, but Massara disagreed.

“We’re not short of centimetres in this team. I think he was referring to physical characteristics. I’d say we’re pretty well armoured.”
