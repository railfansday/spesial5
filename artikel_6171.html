Who Are the Republican Gatekeepers?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

MAGA saw Russell Brand’s con job but welcomed him anyway.

Russell Brand arrives at Southwark Crown Court during the fourth week of his trial on charges of rape and sexual assault, in London, England, November 3, 2026.

Seeing a celebrity in person is always a disorienting experience. Once you set eyes on them, it takes your brain a moment to calibrate where you are. I am seeing this movie star, yet I checked both hands and I am not in possession of popcorn, nor am I in a supine position on my couch. Something is way off here.

It was even more confusing in July 2024 when I (quite literally) almost bumped into the notorious comedian Russell Brand at the Republican National Convention in Milwaukee. I turned a corner, and there he was, the three-time recipient of The Sun’s “Shagger of the Year” award in the flesh, working the convention and interviewing GOP stars for his online show.
