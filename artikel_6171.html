Points Path Pro Is Free Through Thursday—Search 18 Airline Programs In Google Flights, No Credit Card Needed

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

I’ve written about Points Path and its Points Wallet before. The tool puts award prices next to cash fares in Google Flights, so you can compare paying cash and spending miles without running separate searches on each airline’s website. It also flags whether it thinks you’re getting a good deal for your points.

Use the free access to search a trip you’re planning, especially where you have bank points to transfer and different program options to book through. If you haven’t used it before, install the extension and sign into a free account.
