Simone Ashley and Nicola Coughlan Have a <i><em>Bridgerton</em></i> Reunion on the <i><em>Only Murders in London </em></i>Red Carpet

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Dearest gentle reader... last night, the sixth season of Only Murders in the Building, better known as Only Murders in London, brought the cast and crew together for a global premiere in the very city it was filmed. And among them, joining Martin Short and Steve Martin, were guest stars Simone Ashley and Nicola Coughlan.

The actors, both known for their respective roles in Bridgerton, hit the red carpet hand in hand, making for a (somewhat) unexpected crossover episode. For the occasion, Ashley—whose Only Murders character has yet to be revealed—worked with stylist Rebecca Corbin-Murray to source a Tom Ford-era Gucci minidress. The high-neck, short-sleeve piece, pulled from the Spring/Summer 2003 collection, drew inspiration from Japanese embroidery, with cherry blossoms and birds stitched atop light-blue ombré satin.

The 24-year-old piece has been having quite the comeback lately, with Kim Kardashian having worn it just last month. Here, Ashley leaned fully into its history, wearing it with silver peep-toe slingback heels from the same collection.

Lady Whistledown—sorry, Coughlan—has identified her Only Murders character as Essex-born fashion designer Gemma Joyce, a public figure who had been falsely accused of murdering royal Tippy Windsor. Embracing the on-screen drama, the actor wore a Richard Quinn gown from the Fall/Winter 2026 collection. The look was composed of a structured, floral-embroidered peplum top with a contrasting white sweetheart neckline and a long black velvet skirt. Stylist Aimée Croysdill added shimmering Tiffany & Co. jewels for an extra ounce of glamour.

Series lead Selena Gomez was notably absent from the event, but alongside Short, Martin, Ashley, and Coughlan, actors such as Olivia Colman, Martin Freeman, David Tennant, and Spice Girls’s Geri Halliwell-Horner walked the red carpet.
