Everything We Know About Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando’s Wedding

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando just said “I do.” On Saturday, November 21, the former Disney Channel star and her drummer fiancé tied the knot in Palm Springs, surrounded by family and friends.

The newlyweds first met on a blind date back in 2021, and have been linked for the five years since. In the time between, they have kept their relationship fairly private and out of the spotlight, simply popping up for the rare red-carpet appearance or supporting one another at a performance. Behind the scenes, the two have proven avid collaborators, working together on Cyrus’s Something Beautiful and Bass Persuades records.

In December of 2025, Cyrus announced her engagement in the subtle (yet not-so-subtle) manner of debuting a chunky gold engagement ring at the Hollywood premiere of Avatar: Fire and Ash—letting the shimmering diamond do all the talking.

Despite keeping things out of the public eye, Cyrus offered a rare glimpse into her life with Morando during her Harper’s Bazaar December 2024/January 2025 cover story interview. “He’s very similar to me,” she said of her beau, a drummer for the rock band Liily. “We just don’t take life too seriously.”

Ahead, we take a look at everything we know about Cyrus and Morando’s wedding.

Cyrus and Morando’s wedding ceremony took place in Palm Springs, California. More specifically, it was held at Frank Sinatra’s former home, which was transformed with hundreds of white roses for the occasion. Photos obtained exclusively by DeuxMoi showed the couple exchanging vows by the pool, in front of an intimate crowd of guests.

The couple’s official photos showed the “Can’t Be Tamed” star wearing a white long-sleeve Saint Laurent gown with an open back and long train. She paired the look with an updo, drop earrings, and pointed-toe heels, and had her Jacquie Aiche-designed engagement ring on full display. Standing with her at the altar, Morando wore a classic suit—matching the guests, who were all dressed in black.

At the ceremony—which was officiated by Flaming Lips frontman Wayne Coyne—Cyrus and Morando were surrounded by friends and family. Among the guests were Cyrus’s parents, Tish and Billy Ray, with their respective partners, Dominic Purcell and Elizabeth Hurley.
