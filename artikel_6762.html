Andrew Garfield and Monica Barbaro’s Complete Relationship Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Awards season brought love into Andrew Garfield’s life. On Feb. 21, 2025, People reported that Garfield has been “quietly” dating A Complete Unknown’s Monica Barbaro after the two were seen together in London.

A source told People that “they’ve been really low-key and have been spending time together quietly.” The actors’ reps did not respond to People’s request for comment.

In October 2024, Garfield told Esquire that he protects his love life, so don’t expect him to comment on any dating rumors. “I have never, and I won’t ever, speak about or confirm or deny anything about my personal life with anyone, ever,” he said.

Garfield, 43, was last romantically linked to spiritual adviser Dr. Kate Tomas; she confirmed their breakup on Oct. 11, 2024.

Barbaro, meanwhile, is a 36-year-old rising star who hasn’t spoken much about her love life either. She has touched on some offscreen relationships though, telling L’Officiel Australia in July 2023 that she has “a lovely community of friends. I love to host at my place and have everyone over for a Sunday morning, wear your PJs and just come, hang out and watch whatever, sports game or chat, catch up....Luckily, after abandoning my friends so much and leaving town, they’re still willing to come over and hang out.”

And in February 2025, she told Hollywood Authentic that attending events during awards season can really “change an actor’s life.”

When she went to the 2023 Oscars with the Top Gun: Maverick cast, “I just kept thinking: ‘Embrace it. See it. Feel it,’” she said.

“One of the coolest things about being in that conversation is getting to have that sense of community in a space that can be quite intimidating professionally,” she continued. “It was just so exciting to be there, and to watch people make speeches and honor their fellow nominees, and really truthfully do so. It’s not fake. That was so cool.”

Here, the complete (public) history of Barbaro and Garfield’s pretty private romance so far.

On January 4, Barbaro and Garfield were photographed with Eddie Redmayne and W magazine’s editor-at-large Lynn Hirschberg at the outlet’s Best Performances party in Los Angeles. Garfield had an arm around Barbaro in the photo:

On February 20, footage of Barbaro and Garfield sitting together at a performance of Jonathan Bailey’s Richard II in London circulated on social media. It sparked dating rumors that were confirmed a day later by People.

A source said the two actors had been dating quietly but did not specify when their relationship began. “They’ve been really low-key and have been spending time together quietly,” the insider said.

Neither commented on dating reports when they came out. Garfield does not have social media, while Barbaro has remained silent on the topic on Instagram.

On March 2, Barbaro and Garfield attended the Oscars but arrived separately. Fans noticed they walked down the red carpet at the same time, fueling speculation they may have traveled there together.

The couple was not photographed interacting at the ceremony, nor did they pose together at the Vanity Fair Oscar Party that they both attended. An eyewitness told Entertainment Tonight that they hung out inside there, though.

They were later photographed leaving the Vanity Fair Oscar Party and arriving at Beyoncé and Jay-Z’s Oscar party.

A source there told Entertainment Tonight that Garfield and Barbaro were glued to each other the entire time. They were seen “kissing and dancing the whole night,” the insider said.⁠

On March 16, gossip Instagram account DeuxMoi posted photos of Garfield and Barbaro shopping at a grocery store in Malibu. Garfield wore a red baseball cap, white T-shirt, and wide-legged denim jeans. Barbaro was in a white tank under a green cardigan and a blue cap. They took turns pushing a cart full of household items.

In early April, Garfield and Barbaro were spotted on vacation in Kyoto, Japan. A TikTok user shared footage of the couple holding hands and matching in gray as they walked around the city. At one point, Barbaro held on to Garfield’s arm in a sweet show of PDA.

On May 3, the couple was seen backstage at John Proctor Is the Villain on Broadway. The production shared photos of the two actors posing with the cast, writing in the caption, “new students at helen county high: thanks for joining us andrew garfield, monica barbaro, and bonnie milligan!!”

The pair was first seen at the Mark Hotel, according to Just Jared, before attending the popular new show. In the pics, they’re both wearing blue baseball caps and black jackets.

On July 6, Barbaro and Garfield enjoyed watching the match between Sonay Kartal and Anastasia Pavlyuchenkova on day seven of the 2025 Wimbledon Championships in London. The couple was engrossed in the game and one another, pausing to share some sweet PDA in between points.

They arrived in matching white Ralph Lauren looks, with Barbaro in a summer dress and heels and Garfield in white pants, a button-down, and a white cardigan around his shoulders.

On August 29, Barbaro and Garfield were photographed holding hands while at the Venice Film Festival. Garfield was wearing a pale blue button-down shirt with navy pants and sneakers. Barbaro had on a dark green leather jacket, black pants, and black flats. They both made appearances on the red carpet but did not walk together.

The couple signaled they’re still going strong in the new year during a Las Vegas date night. Barbaro and Garfield went to see Penn and Teller’s magic show and posed backstage with them. Garfield notably debuted blond hair.

“Great to have #andrewgarfield and @monicabarbaro at the show @riovegas over the weekend!” Penn and Teller wrote on January 12.

Garfield and Barbaro were photographed kissing while having dinner at Rome’s Pierluigi restaurant on June 27, marking their first major sighting in months. They were later seen walking back to their hotel together.

On July 4, they shared another kiss at Wimbledon.

On August 3, Garfield joined Barbaro in New York City following the premiere of her romantic comedy One Night Only. The couple was photographed arriving at an after-party together at The Crane Club.

For the evening, Garfield wore a deep green suit over a coordinating top, finishing the look with white sneakers. Barbaro opted for a black minidress with a plunging neckline, pointed black pumps, and a matching clutch.

Barbaro reflected on how her perspective on relationships has evolved in an interview with InStyle. “I feel like if you’re hard-up avoidant about love, it’s because, deep down, you’re actually super romantic, and you’re just afraid of getting hurt,” she said. “I dip my toe in all ideologies around romance, but mostly I’m a romantic.”

She continued, “I’m also kind of a workaholic, so I’ve never given my life over to it, which, as a woman, I’m quite proud of. But there’s also the other side of that coin, which is: You can get to a point in your life where you wake up and you look around and you’re sort of like, Oh, have I been self-protecting by [adopting] so much autonomy and independence?”

“I’m definitely shifting some of that perspective now,” she added.

The actress also spoke briefly about working with Garfield on Artificial, praising him as both a scene partner and an actor. “I mean, he’s one of the best actors of his generation, our generation,” she said. “I just learned a lot from his approach to the material and to developing character, and I felt very fortunate to get to witness that in real time.”

In his Who What Wear interview published on September 9, Garfield spoke briefly about his partner, who is also his co-star in Artificial. “I think Monica is a supremely talented actor,” he said. “I think what she did as Joan Baez [in A Complete Unknown]—what she accessed in that project—is nothing short of transformational, extraordinary, and deeply mysterious. I think she is truly incredible. I could wax lyrical more, but I’ll leave it there.”

On October 11, Garfield and Barbaro attended a screening of their upcoming film, Artificial, at the BFI London Film Festival. The couple posed together on the red carpet.

For the occasion, Barbaro wore a shimmering, pale blue Dior dress with thin straps and a softly flared skirt. She styled her brunette hair in loose waves. Garfield opted for an olive-colored blazer over a coordinating shirt, paired with dark trousers and wire-frame glasses.

On November 2, Garfield and Barbaro took in the Los Angeles Lakers’ game against the Miami Heat at Crypto.com Arena. They were photographed sitting courtside together, with Garfield wearing a burgundy top and khakis, while Barbaro chose a white button-down and jeans.
