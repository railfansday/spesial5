Nina Garcia’s Insider Guide to Los Angeles

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

You’re reading Nina Garcia’s Insider Guide newsletter, exclusively available on ELLE.com to All Access members. This edition was originally published in November 2026. Sign up to receive the next edition here.

There isn’t just one way to experience Los Angeles, a place where glitz and glamour sit next to the natural beauty of California mountains and beaches, and the possibility of a movie star being around every corner brings a thrill to every day. As the iconic American designer Isaac Mizrahi once said, “I like to think glamour was invented in Hollywood,” and I tend to agree. There’s a thread of style in every aspect of life.

I recently headed to Los Angeles for ELLE’s Women in Hollywood event, one of our favorite nights of the year, and had a chance to revisit all this city has to offer. Our seminal Women in Hollywood issue honors the year’s most talented actresses, and each fall, in celebration of the issue, we host a star-studded party at the Four Seasons Los Angeles in Beverly Hills. This year, I am bringing you along for the ride. There is something for everyone, including a world-class dining scene that spans from elite Korean restaurants to a universe of Mexican food. (Many say Guelaguetza has the best Oaxacan menu in the country.) If you’re anything like me, you might spend a day checking out the innovative architecture and vibrant gallery scene or opt for a visit to Rodeo Drive for a sun-soaked day of shopping.

For this month’s Insider Guide, I deliberated with true Angelenos, my friends Heidi Klum, interior designer Michael Smith, and fashion designer Sarah Staudinger, about the places they love the most in this classic West Coast destination.

Any time of year. Summer can be overly warm, and the perfect wintertime weather makes it an ideal getaway for East Coasters, but every season has something to offer. With LAX’s proximity to the city center, Los Angeles makes for an easy trip. I recommend renting a car to be able to visit all the beaches and experience the joy of driving down the Pacific Coast Highway, but Uber works just as well. Just be wary of the infamous Los Angeles traffic.
