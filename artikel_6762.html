‘Dancing With the Stars’ Fans Are Upset Over “Grammy Night” Dance-Off Pairings

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

It’s Grammy Night this week on Dancing With the Stars, and aside from the remaining couples dancing to Grammy-winning songs, we’ve entered dance-offs! And fans are...not thrilled about the pairings.

In case you’re new to dance-offs, “couples face off against one another on the ballroom floor as they dance head-to-head in the same style at the same time.” Judges then pick the winners, who get extra points added to their scores.

Sounds simple enough, but the pairings are causing some backlash:

Amber and Pasha vs. Ezra and DaniPerforming rumbas to “The Boy Is Mine,” performed live by Brandy and Monica.

Jenna and Val vs. Tatyana and JanPerforming cha-chas to “The Middle,” performed live by Maren Morris.

Connor and Rylee vs. Maura and MarkPerforming cha-chas to “All I Wanna Do,” performed live by Sheryl Crow.

Basically, fans feel like couples with similar scores and dance backgrounds should have been paired together. Example: Jenna should go up against Amber due to them both having previous experience with complex routines (Jenna was a professional dancer and Amber is an Olympic skater). Meanwhile, Tatyana should have gone up against Maura, and Ezra should have gone up against Connor, since they have similar overall scores.

Guess we’ll see what happens tonight (November 10), but expect some backlash. Reminder: Dancing With the Stars returns to its original schedule after airing on Monday night last week. Tune in live at 8 p.m. ET on Disney+ and ABC.
