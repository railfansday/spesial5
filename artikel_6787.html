Here’s How to Watch ‘Sunrise on the Reaping’ ASAP

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping tells the story of how Haymitch Abernathy (Joseph Zada) became District 12’s only remaining victor by the time Katniss Everdeen (Jennifer Lawrence) volunteered to save her sister. Even if you haven’t read Suzanne Collins’s book, you know this one is going to be extra brutal and especially emotional. Here’s what you need to see Sunrise on the Reaping in theaters now and watch it at home when it’s streaming.

It’s a good thing we don’t live in Panem all of the time, but especially when President Snow (Ralph Fiennes) throws a Quarter Quell in the works. Aren’t the Hunger Games devastating enough on their own?

Sunrise on the Reaping hit cinemas on November 19, 2026. If you can see it in theaters, definitely try to make that happen for that aspect ratio change alone!

The Hunger Games franchise, including The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, is currently streaming on Hulu, Disney+, Tubi, and Peacock. So your guess is as good as mine. All of the above? A secret, fifth place? Who can say??

Let’s use the most recent film in the franchise as an example. The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes was released in theaters on November 17, 2023, and started streaming on Starz (maybe that’s the secret, fifth streaming service) on May 14, 2024. So that’s a six-month waiting period. That would mean that Sunrise on the Reaping will likely be streaming by May of next year, 2027. At some time in between, maybe around the holidays, it will be rentable on PVOD.

Well, as you already know just from glancing through this guide, it’s incredibly easy to stream all five previous Hunger Games movies whenever you want. That’s the easiest way to keep yourself busy. Definitely watch the prequel The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes if you haven’t already. There’s murder AND music!

If you’re willing to spend a little money, or call your local reps to get all four seasons streaming, I recommend watching the Lifetime satire series UnREAL about the cutthroat producers of a dating reality show similar to The Bachelor. It will give you a new perspective that will totally enhance your Sunrise on the Reaping experience.

Finally, if you want to get to know the cast of Sunrise on the Reaping, here are some movie and television suggestions. Watch The Speedway Murders for Joseph Zada, Regretting You for Mckenna Grace, Chilling Adventures of Sabrina for Whitney Peak, Gail Daughtry and the Celebrity Sex Pass for Ben Wang, Game Night for Jesse Plemons, The Menu for Ralph Fiennes, and The Great for Elle Fanning.
