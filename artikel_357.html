Right now, Amazon has the Bambu Lab P1S Combo for $479, down from $549. That’s $70 in savings, or about 13% off, ahead of Black Friday. The bundle includes the same printer sold in the standalone P1S listing, plus Bambu Lab’s AMS, which automatically feeds and switches compatible filament. The included system has four filament slots, letting you print with up to four colors without manually swapping spools between color changes.

Both versions have an enclosed design, automatic bed leveling, and a 256-by-256-by-256-millimeter build volume, with advertised printing speeds up to 500 millimeters per second. You can also browse the MakerWorld model library to get your creative juices flowing. The main thing you have to decide is whether you want multicolor printing (the Combo includes it, whereas the standalone doesn't).
