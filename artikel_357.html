To preserve our sense of self, some scientists are working on ways to keep our brains going—even beyond death. In their quest for cognitive immortality, these “mind savers” are exploring everything from head transplants and brain freezing to conscious software avatars.In the latest MIT Technology Review Roundtables event, senior editor for biomedicine Antonio Regalado joined science editor Mary Beth Griggs to examine these ideas. After following the money, the science, and the philosophy, he came back part skeptical, part awestruck. Here’s a taste of what Antonio found—and what he made of it.
