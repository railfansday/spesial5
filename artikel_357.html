Kohls Black Friday 2026: See the ad and early deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Get your Kohl's Cash ready: the Black Friday Kohl's ad scan is here.

Here's what you need to know: Kohl's savings event starts Nov. 22 both in stores and online, five days before the actual day of Black Friday on Nov. 27. While the sale runs through Monday, Nov. 30, shoppers can save up to 50% off, plus an extra 20% off with coupons. And because it's Kohl's, shoppers are also able to earn $15 of Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent.

Across their catalog, there will be deals on apparel, electronics, kitchen appliances, toys and games, beauty products, and even vacuums. Looking for a preview on what to shop? We have you covered.

Note: Kohl's hasn't confirmed specifically what will be on sale in some categories. Products with a 🔍 emoji are ones we hope to see go on sale (or receive a better deal than it has currently) within confirmed product categories. All other products have been announced officially as deals.

The Shark CryoGlow is Mashable's favorite red light mask, period. While pricey (as a red light mask is wont to be), it is one of the more accessible options in a product category that can easily run into the $450 plus category. Still, we recommend picking it up on Black Friday because it doesn't go on sale often, so this it is one of the best times to buy it. Treat yourself or a loved one with a mask that can emit blue, red, and infrared light, work on well as acne as it does anti-aging, offer a soothing under eye experience with the chilling pads, and generally be extremely easy to use.

Shark Beauty will be available for up to 30% on select styles. Coupons will not apply.

The Beats Pill is Mashable's favorite Bluetooth speaker to grab on sale, and though it's $20 off at the time of writing, we suggest waiting until Kohl's Black Friday deals properly arrive. Why? Well, this speaker is almost never at its full price, so the $129 sale price is the more accurate list price. We're betting on the Black Friday savings to bring it more down to below $110 or even $100. The speaker itself has a sleek look, great room filling sound, and actually works well to make calls on thanks to the built-in mic.
