Today's Bundesliga predictions include Borussia Monchengladbach's clash with Borussia Dortmund and Schalke's meeting with Stuttgart.

The next instalment of the Borussen derby takes place at Borussia Park when Borussia Dortmund travel to face Borussia Monchengladbach in the Bundesliga on Saturday.

Dortmund come into the clash in second place with 21 points, trailing league-leaders Bayern Munich due to goal difference, whereas the hosts are last in 18th place with just four points.

It is difficult to look past Gladbach's poor defensive record given they are the most porous team in the division.

Bayer Leverkusen will hope to keep pace with the Bundesliga's top four on Saturday, when they take on visitors SC Paderborn at BayArena.

The hosts come into the clash with 16 points after nine matchweeks and could end the weekend five points from the top four, whereas 13th-placed Paderborn have 10 points.

Leverkusen's recent form at home is concerning, though they have proven in the past that they are capable of regularly finding the back of the net at BayArena.

Paderborn have been stubborn at times, though they have struggled for results on the road, so perhaps they will fail to take any points on the weekend.

Hoping to pick up where they left off in the Bundesliga, Hoffenheim will travel to face Eintracht Frankfurt at Deutsche Bank Park on Saturday.

However, Hoffenheim are likely to pose a threat given Die Adler's lack of defensive solidity, so expect to see a close-fought contest even if the hosts prevail.
