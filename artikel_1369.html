Everton are reportedly facing a significant financial obstacle in their pursuit of Villarreal striker Georges Mikautadze, with David Moyes keen to strengthen his attacking options ahead of the January transfer window.

The Toffees are looking to bring in another centre-forward, but their current ownership situation could complicate their efforts to secure the Georgian international’s signature.

According to a Football Insider report, Everton are interested in signing the 26-year-old, but his £33 million valuation is a major stumbling block.

The Friedkin Group are reportedly exploring the possibility of selling the Merseyside club and are reluctant to invest heavily in new signings during the winter transfer window. Consequently, Everton’s recruitment team may have to consider alternative financial arrangements to make the deal possible.

Moyes is keen to add more firepower to his squad, and Mikautadze could be an exciting acquisition for the Premier League outfit.

The Villarreal forward has established himself as an impressive attacking talent following successful spells at Metz and Lyon. He scored 15 goals last season and has already found the net five times this campaign, including twice in the Champions League.

His pace, movement and clinical finishing could make him a valuable addition to Everton’s attack. He also possesses the versatility to operate in different attacking positions, giving Moyes greater tactical flexibility.

However, Everton will need to find an affordable way to complete the transfer.
