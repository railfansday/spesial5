Ex-England defender Conor Coady emerges as managerial candidate for League One side Blackpool just two weeks after retiring

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Published: 09:12 EST, 25 November 2026 | Updated: 09:12 EST, 25 November 2026

Conor Coady has emerged as one of the candidates to become new head coach at Blackpool.

The struggling League One side are looking for a new manager after firing Ian Evatt on his birthday last week.

They salvaged a draw against Leicester City on Tuesday to stay just above the drop zone in 20th place with caretaker Steve Thompson in charge.

The Tangerines are understood to have been inundated with applications with the likes of Conor Hourihane, who left Leicester City as assistant manager earlier this month, Matt Bloomfield, formerly Oxford United boss, and Richie Wellens of Leyton Orient among the leading names in the frame.

However, Daily Mail Sport understands former England, Wolves and Everton defender Coady is also being considered.

Conor Coady, pictured here working as England Under-21s assistant, could take over at Blackpool

The 33-year-old retired from playing on November 6 after stints with Wrexham and Charlton Athletic and took up a role as assistant to Lee Carsley in the England U21 set-up in September, replacing Ashley Cole.

Coady had also been helping out within his first club Liverpool's U21 set-up, prior to his deadline day departure from Wrexham.

He is keen to make the step up in club coaching and was linked with a role at Barnsley in recent weeks.
