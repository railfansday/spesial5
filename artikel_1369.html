Cool Palmer has turned into Complaining Cole... his sulking as his club lurches into crisis doesn't say a lot for Chelsea's cultural reset, writes KIERAN GILL

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Published: 01:47 EST, 22 November 2026 | Updated: 04:11 EST, 22 November 2026

We do not know why Cole Palmer headed for the tunnel in a huff after full-time while others in the same Chelsea side paraded the pitch to thank those supporters who had stayed to the end.

Perhaps he was frustrated with himself after a performance in which he did not score, assist, or even test James Trafford in Leeds United’s goal. Maybe he did not fancy thanking those who had been booing as Chelsea trailed 4-1. It is possible he simply does not like losing.

Most superstars can feel the same, and they can show their stroppy side when the game is not going their way.

An extreme example is Bruno Fernandes. Manchester United fans have long accepted his reputation as the Premier League’s temperamental nonpareil because of the numbers he can produce with the whingeing, albeit not so much this season during their downturn.

Such names can get away with petulance when their performances are proper, though Palmer has no such justification currently. The 24-year-old Englishman, unfortunately for Chelsea, put up another anonymous 90 minutes.

We could see he was frustrated even while on the pitch towards the end, with the way he was performatively kicking passes backwards as if to tell everyone else he had no other option. We have seen that before when Chelsea are losing, and it can be somewhat unhelpful when the pressure is being piled on the manager. It indicates the tactical set-up is not to his liking.

Cole Palmer and Joao Pedro were involved in a frosty exchange following Chelsea's 4-1 home defeat by Leeds. Source: Instagram/farihaxchels

As the biggest name within this Chelsea team and a player whose profile transcends his team-mates’, like it or not, he is the totem when it is all going wrong.

We wax lyrical when he is at his brilliant best. It can be as if there has been an explosion at a praise factory when he is dropping that shoulder to down a helpless defender. We know how terrifically talented he is. He is still compared to that 2023-24 season when his statistics were, in a wonderful way, stupid.

But when he is not performing, so too does he need to take the medicine of disapproval, which came in the form of a chorus of boos aimed at all those in Chelsea shirts at Stamford Bridge.

Leeds had not won at this ground since 1999. They corrected history, then Complaining Cole showed up at full-time.

His reaction did not speak to the cultural reset which Chelsea hoped they had orchestrated in the summer when they finally signed some experience for the changing room, bringing in Danny Welbeck, the 35-year-old whom Palmer has nominated as their new ‘Unc’ after Tosin Adarabioyo’s departure, and Jordan Henderson, 36.

It was a poor show from someone who, in his fourth season at a club that gave him his chance when spending £42.5million to sign him from Manchester City in 2023, should be seen as a leader by now.

Palmer is, probably, still undroppable. Alonso would create an almighty problem for himself if he suddenly relegated his No10 to the substitutes’ bench when playing Nottingham Forest next week. But he is in danger of such conversations cropping up at a time when he should want to be impressing enough to get back into Thomas Tuchel’s England squad at the next opportunity.

We are told both Palmer and Levi Colwill are desperate to represent their country again, but neither are performing to that standard of late. Colwill is a defender devoid of confidence, questioning himself and making mistakes. There was one moment during this demoralising defeat by Leeds when he booted a long ball straight out of play and reprimanded himself for it.

Colwill is perfectly capable of playing a good game. You do not have multiple clubs including Bayern Munich making enquiries with Chelsea without being considered elite. He is in a bad spell, and can come back from it, just like Palmer.

Inevitably, the response of some on social media was to say that Alonso’s time should be up.

For clarity, he is not under threat of suddenly being sacked by those at the top of Chelsea. He is 11 Premier League games into his career, and, clearly, deserves time.

The tactics are on him, absolutely. But he is also dealing with a few issues outside of his control, such as holes in his squad and injuries inflicted, like his highly-influential vice captain Moises Caicedo having to be substituted after 22 minutes in this match.

Xabi Alonso has made errors but was rightly considered a huge coup when Chelsea landed him

When appointed head coach of Bayer Leverkusen in October 2022, they went through patches in the Bundesliga. First, they beat Schalke 4-0, which was good. Then they failed to win any of their next three fixtures, which was bad.

Then they won five in a row – happy days. Then they won only one of their following five – bad days. Then they won five in a row again – perfect. Then they won only one of their next seven – pitiful.

But the following season, Leverkusen won the Bundesliga without losing a single game. Alonso was considered an almighty coup when secured in the summer. A little patience is deserved from my perspective, even if Chelsea are currently in a dismal spell.

It is hard not to think Malo Gusto’s time at Chelsea is almost at an end.

He was priced out of a move in the summer when the club asked for £75million, with Liverpool, Manchester City and others across Europe indicating their interest in that window.

Malo Gusto cut a lonely figure after being beaten for Leeds's fourth goal

But the 23-year-old Frenchman has shown little worth as the season has progressed.

Gusto started in five of Alonso’s first six games in all competitions at the beginning of the campaign.

Since then, he has been handed one start in eight, and that was in the Carabao Cup at Liverpool. Coming on as a substitute in this loss to Leeds, he tried and failed to play Brenden Aaronson offside and was then beaten by his man as the visitors made it 4-1.

We should expect to hear more on Gusto’s situation as January approaches.
