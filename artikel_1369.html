Two Names In Frame To Become Next Swansea City Boss

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Rob Edwards and Tommy Elphick are both in the frame to become the next Swansea City manager, GIVEMESPORT understands.

The Swans are on the hunt for a new manager after Vitor Matos left the Welsh outfit earlier this week to take up the vacant role at Ligue Un side Strasbourg.

Matos had been in charge at Swansea for just under a year, taking over in November 2025 and the Championship side were reluctant to let him leave for France, with a £5million compensation package having to be agreed to allow him to make the move.

Strangely, while Matos has been announced as the Strasbourg boss, Swansea have yet to confirm that he has left his position as their manager.

The Swans had an incredible start to the Championship season, winning seven of their first ten matches to sit pretty at the top of the table. However, they have since lost four of their last five to drop to sixth and the next manager has a job on his hands to pick the squad up and get them back firing as they were at the start of the campaign.

GMS understands that Edwards is on the list of contenders to replace Matos at the helm. The 43-year-old spent the second half of last season at Wolverhampton Wanderers, where he was unable to turn things around, with the club eventually finishing bottom of the Premier League and suffering relegation to the Championship. He was sacked at the end of the season and is currently out of work.

Another name in the frame to replace Matos is Liverpool assistant manager Tommy Elphick. The 39-year-old has not had a managerial job as yet, having spent time as first team coach at Bournemouth. He followed Andoni Iraola, one of the Premier League's highest-paid managers, to Anfield and rejected the opportunity to become Bristol City boss and Burnley manager in the summer. However, GMS understands that he is keen to take up a managerial post.

Swansea have a big week of action coming up and will want to get a new manager in place to hit the ground running. They face West Bromwich Albion on Saturday, the team directly above them in the table, before a midweek trip to second-placed Middlesbrough.

After that they have the small matter of the WElsh derby with Cardiff City on November 29, one of the key dates in the 2026/27 season.

Charlie Morgan, the Swansea City ball boy kicked by Eden Hazard, now co-owns a vodka company that is now worth millions.
