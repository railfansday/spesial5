Patriots vs. Chargers predictions: Who will win the battle of the backups?

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It's time for a good old battle of the backups. In one corner, you have Tommy DeVito leading the New England Patriots offense in place of an injured Drake Maye; in the other corner, it's D.J. Uiagalelei replacing Justin Herbert as starting quarterback for the Los Angeles Chargers.

Who will win this marquee matchup? If the odds at FanDuel Sportsbook are to be believed, the Patriots: despite opening as 1.5-point underdogs, the line evolved over the week — first to 1.5 points in New England's favor, and now to all the way to 3. So, let's see what the Pats Pulpit staff and community think.

Bernd Buchmasser: Let's be clear: the Patriots should win this game even with Tommy DeVito under center. The Chargers have been bad all season and on top of it all will be without Justin Herbert on Sunday; they also had two weeks to get ready for this matchup. That doesn't mean this will be an easy win, because L.A. still has some good individual talent, but that if New England wants to remain a serious competitor for a playoff spot a games like this should leave no doubt regardless of the personnel available. | Patriots 20, Chargers 13

Pat Lane: Am I going with the absolute homer pick here? 100%. However, if the Patriots have any chance of staying competitive, they have to win this game. For the second game in a row, I will say that if the locker room has any sense of pride, that they will show up with their hair on fire. I think the defense will step up and win them this game. | Patriots 13, Chargers 10

Dylan Klayman: The Patriots need to win. This game is one of the three remaining on their schedule that would be a "must win," along with the Jets and Dolphins, in order to stay in contention for the playoffs. While having Tommy DeVito at quarterback makes it a challenge to win any game, the Chargers will also have a backup going on Sunday. The Chargers do not have a high-powered offense, and you would have to think the Patriots defense doesn't continue to look as bad as it has the past two weeks. I also think the Patriots' pure hunger to win this game should ultimately lead to a victory for New England. | Patriots 16, Chargers 13

Matt St. Jean: While the Chargers have the worst offense in football and will be down to their third-string quarterback this Sunday, this offense is designed to get you out of man coverage and punish you with play action passes. Zone defense and play action defense have been the two big weaknesses for New England this year, and I am not confident they have the personnel to stop it. What I do know is this game is a big litmus test for me with the Patriots defense. Your starter is out but you're still in the playoffs race against the worst offense in football. Can you fly around, attack the football, and win a game for the team? I'm not particularly optimistic, but I hope I'm wrong. | Patriots 10, Chargers 20
