Thunder rumors: Don’t expect another trade anytime soon after Nique Clifford deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Thunder rumors: Don't expect another trade anytime soon after Nique Clifford deal appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Oklahoma City Thunder have addressed their wing depth by acquiring Nique Clifford from the Sacramento Kings, but another trade may not be coming anytime soon. With Chet Holmgren sidelined by a knee injury, Oklahoma City could use additional frontcourt help. However, the team appears comfortable relying on its current big men rather than making another move.

According to NBA insider Marc Stein, Oklahoma City does not feel the same urgency to strengthen its frontcourt as it did its wing rotation. Stein reported that the Thunder are "bullish on the idea" that Isaiah Hartenstein, Jaylin Williams and rookie Aday Mara can fill in until Chet Holmgren returns. Oklahoma City traded two second-round picks to Sacramento for Clifford after injuries to Jalen Williams and Cason Wallace threatened to sideline both players for an extended period.

Holmgren is expected to miss at least eight to 10 weeks after the Thunder announced he was beginning treatment for a small tear in his patellar tendon. That timeline gives Oklahoma City a clearer idea of how long it must manage without its starting center.

The available options have also given the team reason to remain patient. Mara, the 12th pick in the 2026 NBA Draft, has made an encouraging start, while Hartenstein continues to provide rebounding and rim protection. Jaylin Williams has also been a reliable contributor, although he is currently dealing with a lower-back injury.

With several players still recovering, Oklahoma City appears content to assess its current roster and give its available big men an opportunity to step up while Chet Holmgren remains sidelined before considering another trade.
