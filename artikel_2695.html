NASCAR O’Reilly Auto Parts Series Championship Preview: Five Drivers Chase the Title at Homestead

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Justin Allgaier enters the championship race as the points leader after finishing runner-up at Martinsville. The veteran has enjoyed one of the best seasons of his career, collecting six victories and 19 top-five finishes. He also has plenty of experience at Homestead, which could give him an advantage over his championship competitors.

Allgaier controls his own destiny and can secure his second career series championship with a strong performance. However, he cannot afford to take his foot off the gas with four drivers close behind.

Love, Day and Kvapil Are Ready to Challenge

Reigning champion Jesse Love sits 22 points behind Allgaier. Love has already won twice during the postseason and knows what it takes to deliver when the championship is on the line. He will attempt to become the first driver since Tyler Reddick to win consecutive titles.

Corey Day is only one point behind Love and enters the finale with plenty of momentum after back-to-back victories at Las Vegas and Charlotte. The Hendrick Motorsports driver has exceeded expectations in his first season and has an opportunity to capture a championship.

Carson Kvapil also remains in contention, sitting 31 points behind the leader. After winning at Martinsville, Kvapil has given himself another opportunity to make history. Sheldon Creed rounds out the championship contenders, trailing by 42 points.

Homestead is known for its multiple racing grooves, making tire management and handling extremely important. Drivers who can run near the wall and maintain speed through the corners could have a major advantage.

With 200 laps available, teams will need to make smart pit-road decisions while avoiding mistakes that could end their championship hopes.

Allgaier has the points advantage, but Love, Day, Kvapil and Creed are not out of the fight. With five drivers still mathematically eligible, Saturday's finale has the potential to deliver an incredible finish to the 2026 season.
