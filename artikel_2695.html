Celtics announce starting lineup ahead of Mavericks game

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

BOSTON — The Celtics will start Derrick White, Jordan Walsh, Paul George, Jayson Tatum, and Neemias Queta when they face the Dallas Mavericks on Sunday night.

Jayson Tatum has led the team so far this season, averaging 26.3 points, 7.5 assists, and 6.1 rebounds. Paul George has been the team's second-leading scorer, averaging 22 points, 5.2 rebounds, 3.8 assists, and 2.1 steals per game.

Derrick White, who has started every game so far this season, is averaging 12.8 points and 5.3 assists per game. Neemias Queta, who has started the last 7 games after coming off the bench in the first 5, averages 9.2 points and 6.7 rebounds per game.

Walsh has started 7 of the team's 12 games so far, averaging 4.5 points and 3.4 rebounds in 16.2 minutes per game. But though he's been a regular starter, he's averaging fewer minutes (16.2) than Sam Hauser (21) and Baylor Scheierman (20.8), both of whom have come off the bench for all but one game.

Without Kyrie Irving, the Mavericks will start Naji Marshall, Caleb Martin, PJ Washington, Cooper Flagg, and Daniel Gafford.

The Mavericks are 7-6 and have lost their last two games. They are coming off of a Saturday night loss to the Cleveland Cavaliers.

The Celtics are 10-2 and have won 5 straight games.
