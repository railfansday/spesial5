Bills lead Chiefs 7-3 at halftime on Thanksgiving night

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

There is some nasty weather in Western New York for Thanksgiving night and the Bills look like they have a home-field advance over the Chiefs.

Buffalo leads Kansas City 7-3 at halftime.

With steady freezing rain falling throughout the first half, it was difficult for both teams to get much going offensively. The game-time temperature was 35 degrees but the feels like temperature was down to 21.

The Bills have relied on running back James Cook for most of their offensive production. Cook rushed for 71 yards on 19 carries in the first half with a 5-yard touchdown.

Josh Allen is just 5-of-10 passing for 41 yards with an interception. Safety Alohi Gilman picked off Allen in the end zone on second-and-8 from the Kansas City 9 midway through the second quarter. At that point, it seemed like if the Bills scored a touchdown, they might have an insurmountable lead.

While the Chiefs didn’t score off of that interception, they at least kept Buffalo off the board.

But Kansas City had several miscues from the wet ball. The team had trouble fielding kicks, punter Matt Ariza had trouble catching a long snap before a punt, and Patrick Mahomes had trouble fielding a snap.

But Kansas City finally got a little something going late in the second quarter, with Harrison Butker sending a 26-yard field goal through the uprights to narrow Buffalo’s lead to four points.

Through 30 minutes, Mahomes is 10-of-15 for 97 yards. Travis Kelce has four receptions for 71 yards, including a key 21-yard catch at the end of the first half to get Kansas City into scoring position.

The Chiefs will receive the second-half kickoff.
