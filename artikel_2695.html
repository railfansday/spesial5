Steelers place WR Ben Skowronek on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Pittsburgh Steelers are set to welcome AFC West-leading Denver Broncos to town for a pivotal Black Friday matchup. Pittsburgh will do so without one of their leaders on special teams in Ben Skowronek.

Nick Farabuagh of Penn Live reported on Thursday that the Steelers have placed the receiver and special teams ace on injured reserve. Skowronek will now miss at least the next four games with a calf injury. The receiver recently returned from a calf injury that caused him to miss three games.

Skowronek has been listed as a non-participant in each of the Steelers' three practices this week leading up to their Friday game. While Skowronek's injury will certainly have an impact on the Steelers' offense and special teams, the team will be getting one of their top receiver back in Michael Pittman Jr., who has not played since early October. The team also signed wide receiver Max Hurleman to the active roster on Thursday. The undrafted free agent was the Steelers' leading receiver in Week 11 while also scoring the team's only touchdown in his NFL debut.

Skowronek has had a limited role on the Steelers offense this season, recording four catches for 32 yards and a touchdown, and is now out for at least four weeks.
