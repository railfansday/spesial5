Why one early sack created the turning point versus the Titans

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Dallas Cowboys' offence was already on fire, and James Houston ensured Tennessee couldn't keep pace. Late in the first quarter, with Dallas leading 13-3, Houston sacked Cam Ward on third down, forcing a three-and-out.

The Cowboys capitalized on this opportunity, turning the next possession into another touchdown and opening a commanding 20-3 lead. This crucial stop helped turn a promising start into firm control in Sunday's 51-30 victory.

Dallas had scored touchdowns on its opening two possessions. Javonte Williams finished the first drive from one yard out, while KaVontae Turpin's 22-yard touchdown run capped the second. Tennessee had managed a field goal between those scores, but the Cowboys needed their defense to give Dak Prescott an opportunity to stretch the lead.

With Tennessee facing third down deep in its own territory, Dallas brought pressure. DeMarvion Overshown helped force Ward toward Houston, who finished the play with the sack. The Titans were forced to punt after just three offensive snaps, unable to answer Turpin's touchdown.

It was a useful example of defenders working together. Overshown's pressure disrupted the quarterback, and Houston was there to finish. Dallas needed that kind of coordinated effort under new defensive play-caller Mike Pettine, particularly after the breakdowns against San Francisco.

The defense still had problems after the break. Tennessee scored twice in the third quarter, including Carnell Tate's 59-yard touchdown reception. But the Dallas offense answered both scores, with a touchdown pass to Williams and a Prescott rushing touchdown restoring an 18-point cushion each time. The Titans never got the defensive stop they needed to turn those scores into a serious comeback.

Houston's early sack was not the game's flashiest moment, but it provided exactly what Dallas needed, a quick stop while the offense was hot, and gave the game its turning point.
