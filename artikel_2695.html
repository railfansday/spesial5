Texans place Azeez Al-Shaair on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Texans played without linebacker Azeez Al-Shaair in last Sunday’s loss to the Browns and they’ll remain without him for the next month.

Al-Shaair was placed on injured reserve on Tuesday. Al-Shaair was listed with a groin injury on the team’s injury report leading into the Browns game.

Al-Shaair also missed Week 4 with a groin injury, but he started the team’s other seven games this season. He had 68 tackles, a tackle for loss and three quarterback hits in those appearances.

The Texans also announced that they have signed defensive end Sebastian Harsh and released defensive tackle Junior Tafuna in practice squad transactions.
