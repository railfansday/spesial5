Dolphins news: Injuries mounting, draft implications vs. Colts, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Miami Dolphins are dealing with multiple injuries this week as they prepare to face the Indianapolis Colts. Last week, the Dolphins had a relatively healthy active roster, with nearly every player available for their game against the Detroit Lions. As the schedule moves into Week 10, the injuries have piled up, however.

After placing starting quarterback Malik Willis and starting defensive tackle Zach Sieler on injured reserve earlier in the week, Miami is already making changes to their starting roster. Now, it looks like center Aaron Brewer and wide receiver Chris Bell could miss the game as well. Brewer is working through the NFL's concussion protocol, while Bell was downgraded from limited in Wednesday's practice to not practicing on Thursday due to a knee issue.
