Bears vs. Saints: How to Watch and Listen, Odds, Previews, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Chicago Bears (7-2) will resume their quest for their first back-to-back NFL North titles in 20 years when they host the New Orleans Saints (3-6) on Sunday. The Bears are currently third in their division, behind the Detroit Lions and Minnesota Vikings, who are both also 7-2.

The Saints are also third in their division, the NFC South, behind the Carolina Panthers (4-5) and the Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-6).

Here's everything you need to know for the game!

New to Windy City Gridiron? It's SB Nation's community for Bears fans. Use the app's NEW game chat to follow every play and Bear Down together.

Easy access to communities and conversations

The Bears and Saints kick off at 12:00 pm CT on Sunday, November 21, from Soldier Field in Chicago, Illinois. The high will be 58°, and it'll be cloudy with a few rain showers during the day and breezy in the afternoon.

The game will be on FOX, with their No. 1 team, Kevin Burkhardt and Tom Brady, on the call.

If you live in the blue area on the 506Sports map below, you can see the game!

ESPN Chicago (1000 AM, 100.3 FM HD2) with Jeff Joniak (play-by-play), Tom Thayer (analyst), and Jason McKie (sideline) on the call, and LATINO MIX 93.5 FM (Spanish) with Omar Ramos.

The Bears are 11.5-point favorites, with the game total over/under at 48.5.
