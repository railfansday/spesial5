Saints waive ex-Chiefs DB, two-time Super Bowl champion in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The New Orleans Saints waived former Kansas City Chiefs defensive back Justin Reid on November 19, clearing the way for the nine-year veteran to join a new team before the NFL playoffs.

Reid started his career with the Houston Texans as a third-round pick in the 2018 NFL Draft before finding his way to Kansas City, where he was a member of the Chiefs' Super Bowl LVII and LVIII rosters.

When his time in Kansas City came to an end in 2025, Reid caught on with the Saints and was a key member of New Orleans' secondary in the 24 games he played in the Big Easy.

News of the Saints' decision to waive Reid was announced by Michael Ginnitti of Spotrac.com in a post to his official Twitter account:

While it is unclear if the Chiefs are interested in a reunion with Reid, general manager Brett Veach is sure to be doing his due diligence on the former Stanford Cardinal standout as Kansas City prepares for its playoff push in the second half of the 2026 NFL regular season.
