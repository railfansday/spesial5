Fiona the hippo dies after accidentally swallowing hat at Cincinnati Zoo

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Fiona, the beloved hippo at the Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden, has died after accidentally swallowing a hat that became lodged in her habitat.

The zoo announced her death on November 19, revealing that its veterinary team had spent days attempting to save her after the incident occurred last week.

Despite multiple attempts to remove the hat from her system, Fiona’s condition became a medical emergency, and she ultimately did not recover.

In a statement shared on Instagram, the Cincinnati Zoo explained that Fiona had accidentally ingested a hat that ended up in her habitat last week.

“It is with extreme sadness that we share with you the devastating news that Fiona did not make it after multiple attempts from our amazing vets and keeper team to help her through what ended up being a medical emergency,” the zoo wrote.

According to the statement, veterinary staff made several attempts to remove the object, but were unable to do so successfully.

“The vet staff tried everything they could to help her after she ingested a hat that ended up in her habitat by accident last week. Despite multiple attempts to move the hat out of her system, it was ultimately too much to overcome.”

The zoo confirmed that Fiona received necessary supportive care on November 19 in a final attempt to save her life, but the treatment was unsuccessful.

Fiona became an international sensation after being born prematurely at the Cincinnati Zoo in January 2017. Weighing just 29 pounds at birth, she required round-the-clock care from zoo staff before eventually growing into one of the facility’s most recognizable animals.

Her early development was documented extensively on social media, attracting millions of followers and making her a prominent figure in the zoo’s conservation and educational efforts.

The zoo acknowledged the impact her death would have on the community, writing, “We know everyone in the community will be crushed by this news, as we all are.”

“Please keep her keepers and everyone who tried to help her in your thoughts as they try to navigate and come to terms with this shocking news. They are also, understandably, asking for space at this time.”
