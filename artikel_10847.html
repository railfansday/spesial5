hireEZ is an AI sourcing and engagement platform aimed at larger talent acquisition teams. Its pricing page (October 2026) cites 1B+ profiles across 45+ channels and 40+ native ATS integrations. Enterprise pricing is by quote, so there is no per-seat figure to model, and solo recruiters start at $494 a month with a 7-day trial, the highest published entry price here. Good for: larger teams consolidating several tools on a quote-based budget, on top of an existing ATS.

SeekOut is a talent search platform built on a 1B+ profile database. Its pricing page (October 2026) puts SSO, advanced filters such as inferred security clearance, and ATS rediscovery on custom plans. Sourcing Core is $179 a month billed monthly or $149 billed annually, with 3 seats, 500 contact credits a month and a 14-day trial. Good for: sourcing-heavy teams with specialist filter needs that can budget for custom plans.

Bullhorn is an applicant tracking and CRM platform for staffing and recruitment firms, with an AI layer called Amplify. Amplify Chat is a staffing-specific model that can answer questions, launch screens, start outreach and update records. Its pricing page (October 2026) lists Pro, Max, larger-agency plans and Amplify products as quote-only, while Core is $165 and Starter $99 per user per month for small agencies. Max adds agentic screening, multi-step AI outreach and interview scheduling. Good for: staffing firms that want ATS, back office and AI from one vendor and accept quote-only pricing for advanced AI.

Loxo is an AI-native ATS and recruiting CRM with a built-in sourcing database. Its pricing page (October 2026) lists Professional by demo, which adds Loxo Source (its talent graph), AI agents and 2,500 credits per seat per month. Core is from $149 per user per month billed annually. SSO/SAML is an Enterprise feature, and usage is metered in credits. Good for: firms ready to run ATS and sourcing in one system and willing to price the advanced tier through a demo.

Recruit CRM is an AI-powered ATS and CRM built for recruitment agencies and executive search firms. Its pricing page (October 2026) lists Enterprise at $215, Business at $135 and Pro at $95 per user per month, with annual billing saving up to 17%. Credits are pooled across the account (100 per user per month on Pro), and data enrichment and AI sourcing draw from them. Good for: small agencies that want client portals and a low entry price, with sourcing limited by pooled credits.

Crelate is a recruiting CRM and ATS for staffing firms and direct-hire agencies. Its pricing page (October 2026) lists a custom Business Plus tier, Business at $119 and Essentials at $85 per user per month (up to two users). Automation, sequences and full API access start on Business Plus. Text messaging, email campaigns and contact data enrichment carry extra fees. Good for: small desks that want a CRM-first system and can pay extra for outreach add-ons.
