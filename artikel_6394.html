Frame TVs are one of the more interesting home theater innovations in recent years, mostly because they prioritize “form” as much as “function.” They take a two-pronged approach: offering high-definition 4K entertainment and decor in one fell swoop. In other words, you can finally get use out of your TV, even when you’re not, well, using it. That’s where the art function comes in.

We’ve recommended quite a few art TVs over the years, but one stands out amongst the pack: Samsung’s The Frame. It’s high-performing, sleek, and boasts a curated art gallery for when you’re not streaming or playing GTA VI. And right now, for Black Friday, you can get one for under $1,000.

Now’s the perfect opportunity to upgrade your living room, master bedroom, or mancave with Samsung’s best-selling 4K TV. The latest model is almost 30% off—and under $1,000. All things considered, this is one of our favorite Black Friday deals so far. And the two-day shipping definitely helps.

The Frame offers a discreet, tasteful centerpiece option. It’s a pioneer of sorts, first debuting back in 2017. But Samsung has upgraded it quite a bit since then. For starters, it implemented an anti-reflective matte coating, ideal for both regular viewing and art mode. The latest model offers glare-free QLED technology with vibrant colors and deep contrast.

Samsung’s custom gallery includes over 5,000 curated pieces of artwork, providing you plenty of options when it’s time to switch modes. But don’t let the art component fool you—this is a legitimately great TV. It’s even built for gaming, thanks to DLG's 240Hz performance and an impressive 144Hz variable refresh rate technology. It features smooth transitions, quick response times, and virtually no lag time.

The only small downside of The Frame: the customizable bezel and the art gallery are sold separately. You can always curate your own art, too, so there are workarounds.

All things considered, this is a home run deal and represents a rare opportunity to get the best frame TV on the market for under $1,000. You might not see this deal again until Black Friday, so we suggest acting sooner rather than later.
