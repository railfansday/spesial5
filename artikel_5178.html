Xash Brings Gold Rewards to Solana Stablecoin Users

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Home » Altcoins » Solana (SOL) » Xash Brings Gold Rewards to Solana Stablecoin Users

The platform aims to tackle DeFi’s reliance on short-term incentives.

Xash, a Solana-based application that rewards users with tokenized gold for stablecoin activity, has announced its public launch at Solana Breakpoint 2026 in London, scheduled for November 15–17.

The platform aims to change how users earn rewards in decentralized finance (DeFi) by offering gold instead of temporary token incentives. Users can earn rewards by lending stablecoins, providing liquidity and trading across established Solana DeFi platforms.

The launch comes as DeFi protocols continue to face a challenge: attracting users who stay after promotional rewards expire. Xash is betting that gold-backed rewards could offer a more sustainable alternative.

Many DeFi platforms use token rewards to attract deposits and trading activity. However, users often move their funds elsewhere once those incentives end, creating a cycle known as mercenary capital.

Xash wants to address this problem by rewarding ongoing stablecoin activity with tokenized gold rather than short-lived token campaigns.

The company says the approach is intended to encourage longer-term participation in DeFi while giving users an opportunity to accumulate gold.

It also aims to benefit DeFi platforms seeking sustained activity and stablecoin issuers looking to expand the use of their tokens.

“Stablecoins don’t have a supply problem. They have a usage problem. Incentive campaigns rent users for a few weeks, then they’re gone,” said Kevin Huynh, co-founder and CEO of Xash.

Xash aggregates DeFi opportunities into a single interface, evaluating available strategies daily and presenting users with options based on potential gold rewards.

Before committing funds, users can review associated costs and estimated 30-day rewards. They can then select an opportunity and authorize transactions directly through their own wallets.

As part of the launch, the company is introducing direct withdrawals of accumulated tokenized gold rewards to users’ wallets. It is also adding a curated liquidity vault built on Kamino, allowing users to deploy stablecoins through a single deposit instead of managing individual transactions.

Xash says its trading activity does not involve directional price bets or borrowed funds. However, this does not eliminate the risks associated with DeFi. Users remain exposed to the underlying protocols and market conditions, including potential losses when using the Kamino vault.

The project was founded by Kevin Huynh, who serves as CEO, alongside Vinny Lingham, co-founder of Civic and Gyft and a former general partner at Multicoin Capital; Alen Ribic, co-founder of SweepSouth; and Llew Claasen, former executive director of the Bitcoin Foundation.

Xash’s launch comes amid growing demand for tokenized real-world assets (RWAs), particularly gold-backed digital tokens.

According to the World Gold Council, the tokenized gold market has expanded fivefold since late 2024, reaching approximately $6 billion.

Trading activity has also accelerated. Tokenized gold generated more than $90 billion in spot trading volume during the first quarter of 2026 alone, surpassing the total recorded throughout 2025, according to CoinGecko research.

Xash tests whether real-asset rewards can retain DeFi users better than short-term token incentives. Its launch also reflects rising demand for tokenized gold within the Solana ecosystem.
