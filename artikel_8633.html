A working device usually brings more in a private sale than Back Market pays, so check recent sale prices for your model before you accept.

Back Market offered $294 for a working M1 MacBook Air with 256GB in Good condition on Nov. 17, 2026, while that model sold on Swappa, a used-tech marketplace, for $386 on average, or about $350 after fees by our math. That was the highest price any of the more than 250 refurbishers buying through Back Market would pay.

Back Market Trade-in suits owners of a cracked or dead device and anyone who’d rather ship for a known price than deal with buyers. Sellers of a working laptop, tablet, or phone worth a few hundred dollars keep more than Back Market pays by listing it on Swappa or eBay.
