Mixpanel: Deal Desk Analyst (Eastern Time Zone Preferred)

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Mixpanel is the leading product intelligence and analytics platform, trusted by more than 29,000 companies to help understand how people use the products they build. By combining powerful analytics with AI that knows your business, Mixpanel helps teams see what’s working, diagnose what’s not, and decide what to build next. Learn more at mixpanel.com.

The Deal Desk team keeps quote-to-cash fast, accurate, and in line with company policy. We help Sales structure deals that grow revenue and limit financial risk. The team is growing, covers multiple time zones, and works every day with Finance, Sales, Legal, Partnerships, and executive leadership.

As a Deal Desk Analyst, you’ll own quote review and approval for your book of deals, work through non-standard terms, and run Salesforce CPQ improvements from idea to rollout. You’ll handle unusual contract scenarios on your own and escalate only when risk calls for it. You’ll also start shaping how Sales, Finance, and Legal work together on deals. You’ll report to the Deal Desk Manager in San Francisco.
