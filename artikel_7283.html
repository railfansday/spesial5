Intuit offloads Mailchimp to Bending Spoons to deepen bets on AI

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Let Marketing Dive's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

Bending Spoons, a technology conglomerate whose portfolio includes AOL and Eventbrite, will acquire the email automation company for $3.68 billion.

Intuit has entered into an agreement to sell email automation business Mailchimp to technology conglomerate Bending Spoons for $3.68 billion, the company confirmed in its latest earnings report. Intuit acquired Mailchimp in 2021 for $12 billion.

Intuit’s decision to sell comes as Mailchimp continues to be a drag on its business, which includes financial services QuickBooks, TurboTax and Credit Karma. The move is also positioned as a way to sharpen Intuit's focus on artificial intelligence and its other core products.

“We have built AI-native experiences to help businesses win new customers, manage their pipeline and help them grow through our lead-to-cash capabilities,” said Sasan Goodarzi, CEO of Intuit, on a call discussing the company’s latest earnings. “Selling Mailchimp sharpens our focus behind that strategy, while positioning Mailchimp and its team for continued success in their next chapter with Bending Spoons.”

Mailchimp, founded in 2001, was once the gem of the email marketing industry, but in the years following its acquisition by Intuit has struggled to maintain growth. Mailchimp’s revenue decreased 1% to $313 million during Q1 of the company’s fiscal 2027 for the period ended Oct. 31, a contrast to Intuit's overall revenue growth of 12% over the same period.

In May, Intuit announced that it was reducing its full-time workforce by 17% and “rightsizing” its investment in Mailchimp. As struggles persisted, the company in August revealed that it started running Mailchimp as a separate operating segment, a move that was intended to offer investors more transparency as Intuit tried to manage the unit for profitability.

The Mailchimp deal is expected to close in early 2027, at which point Bending Spoons plans to invest “substantially” in Mailchimp and build on its existing tools, according to a separate statement. Bending Spoons, an Italian company founded in 2013, acquires businesses and works to improve them with the goal of keeping them long-term. Other businesses acquired by the company include Vimeo, WeTransfer, Eventbrite, Streamyard and AOL.

Bending Spoons went public in July at a value of over $18 billion. Holding company Berkshire Hathaway commands a 4% ownership stake of the company.

Intuit last week launched a campaign — its first work with agency Mother New York — promoting the integration of its Intuit Intelligence agentic platform into QuickBooks. The campaign, “More Brains for your Business,” positions the AI capabilities as a helping hand instead of a replacement for human talent.
