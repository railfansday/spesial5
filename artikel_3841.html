First GTA 6 PS5 Patch Live Now, Get All the Details Here

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Update [Mon 23rd Nov 2026, 2:50pm]: Rockstar has now published the patch notes for GTA 6 PS5 update 01.002.002. Here they are in full:

Guide GTA 6: Patch Notes - All Updates & Fixes (November 2026)

Original Story: Rockstar Games has today published the first GTA 6 post-launch update on PS5, bringing its open worlder up to version number 01.002.002.

It's a small 603.8MB patch on PS5, and a 1.6GB download on Xbox Series X|S consoles. However, you might not be able to download it right away on PS5. It appears the developer is utilising a rollout system where owners of the game receive the patch in waves.

According to Rockstar dataminer Tex2, the update is live right now for 33% of GTA 6 owners on PS5. Another third of the title's userbase gets the patch in another few hours, and then everyone will have it by the end of the day. This is apparently to avoid server overload.
