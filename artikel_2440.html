KC Current advance to NWSL Championship with 2-1 win over Gotham

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Brilliant tallies from Croix Bethune and Haley Hopkins overcome an own goal

The Kansas City Current got their revenge against Gotham FC Sunday with a 2-1 semifinal win in New York to advance to the 2026 National Women’s Soccer League Championship.

In the 2025 NWSL Quarterfinals, eighth-seeded Gotham FC stunned the Shield-winning Current at CPKC Stadium behind a dramatic extra-time winner. This time, the fourth-seeded Current took down the Shield winners and reigning Championship winners.

Kansas City had the better first half, creating a couple of chances for Temwa Chawinga that she would usually put away. They took the lead in the 20th minute with a brilliant direct free kick from Midfielder of the Year finalist Croix Bethune. It was the fourth direct free kick goal in NWSL Playoffs history.

The Current continued to control the game and create the better chances, though the halftime introduction of the minutes-limited Rose Lavelle did immediately spark more dynamic attack and momentum for Gotham.

In the 69th minute, Gotham benefited from a similar stroke of luck to the one that helped them advance past Seattle in the quarterfinals. A cross from Midge Purce slipped past Current goalkeeper Lorena before defender Laney Rouse inadvertently turned it into her own net. Gotham finished the match without a shot on target, having also failed to register one in their quarterfinal win over Seattle.

While Gotham struggled to create, the Current struggled to make the most of their opportunities and set pieces. That changed in the 89th minute when Haley Hopkins put the perfect finish on a free kick delivery from Izzy Rodriguez, banging it off the crossbar and over the line.

In a matchup between one of the league’s best defenses and one of the league’s best offenses, set pieces made the difference. Now the Current will make their second-ever appearance in the NWSL Championship game, and their first since 2022, facing the Washington Spirit at Audi Field on Saturday, November 21, at 8 pm ET.
