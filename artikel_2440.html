The Mets have reportedly agreed to a contract with ace left-hander Tarik Skubal for nine years and $450 million. As expected, the deal is a record-setter, so let's dive in.

The nine years here for Skubal fall short of the pitcher record by three years, as Yoshinobu Yamamoto's 12-year deal with the Dodgers still holds firm and probably will for a long time. Those were special circumstances, however, as he was coming over from Japan at such a young age rather than domestic free agency.

As for Skubal's deal, though, he does have the record for dollars, both in terms of the total contract and the average annual value.
