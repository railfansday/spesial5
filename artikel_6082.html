Hyatt And Delta Launch Extra Points On Flights And Hotel Stays—But Standard Coach Tickets Don’t Qualify

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Hyatt and Delta have launched extra points on flights and hotel stays, with airline status, lounge passes and hotel free night awards coming next year. But there’s a big restriction: even a Hyatt Globalist buying a regular Delta coach ticket earns no Hyatt points. That’s not ‘basic economy doesn’t count’ – it’s listerally standard main cabin coach that earns nothing. The companies announced their partnership in September. Now we know how the benefits actually work. This brings back some of what Hyatt members lost when American Airlines stopped reciprocal points earning at the end of 2024. But Delta excludes regular coach, gives Hyatt’s lowest elite tier half as many points, and pays hotel guests in a less valuable airline currency (SkyMiles). Who Earns Extra Points And Miles Start by linking your World of Hyatt and Delta SkyMiles accounts. Ongoing extra earning depends on your status: Your Status   Additional Earnings Delta Silver, Gold, Platinum or Diamond Medallion   1 SkyMile per eligible dollar spent with Hyatt, on top of Hyatt points Hyatt Discoverist   1 Hyatt point per $2 of eligible Delta premium cabin spending, on top of SkyMiles Hyatt Explorist, Globalist or Lifetime Globalist   1 Hyatt point per eligible dollar of Delta premium cabin spending, on top of SkyMiles Hyatt status qualifies you for Hyatt points on eligible Delta flights. Delta status qualifies you for SkyMiles on eligible Hyatt spending. For flights, Delta Comfort, Premium Select, First Class and Delta One qualify. Basic, Classic and Extra fare versions qualify when they’re sold in one of those cabins. So basic extra legroom coach qualifies, full fair regular coach doesn’t. . The flight earning rules also require: A Delta marketed and operated flight, with a Delta-issued ticket beginning 006. Your SkyMiles number attached before departure and Hyatt elite status when the flight is completed. A qualifying paid ticket. Eligible spending includes the base fare and airline imposed surcharges, but excludes government taxes, airport fees and amounts paid with miles. These are Hyatt bonus points. They don’t count as base points toward Hyatt status or lifetime Globalist. For hotel spending, eligible room charges, incidentals and qualifying purchases without an overnight stay can earn SkyMiles. Mr & Mrs Smith and The Venetian qualify when booked through Hyatt. Homes & Hideaways and FIND experiences are excluded. Hyatt Nights Can Earn Delta Credits, Lounge Visits And Status Beginning January 1, 2027, linked Hyatt members will have Delta options among their Milestone Rewards. These are choices, so you’ll need to compare them with the other rewards available at the same threshold. The specific awards are: Hyatt Qualification   Delta Reward Choice 20 qualifying nights or 35,000 base points   $25 Delta eCertificate 40 qualifying nights or 65,000 base points   $75 Delta eCertificate 60 qualifying nights or 100,000 base points   $100 Delta eCertificate 70, 80 or 90 qualifying nights   Two single visit Delta Sky Club passes 100 qualifying nights   Delta Gold Medallion status 150 qualifying nights   Delta Platinum Medallion status, without Choice Benefits The flight certificates and lounge passes expire one year after issuance. Each lounge pass covers one person for one visit with a qualifying same-day boarding pass (basic economy passengers excluded). The status awards are for your own account. They don’t provide anything extra if you already hold that tier or higher, and selecting the same status again doesn’t extend it. Platinum at 150 nights is potentially attractive, albeith without the Choice Benefits that normally come with that status (e.g. four Regional Upgrade Certificates). Delta Elites Can Choose Hyatt Free Nights Starting February 1, 2027, for the 2028 Medallion Year, Delta is adding: A Hyatt Category 1–4 free night as a Platinum Choice Benefit. A Hyatt Category 1–7 free night as a Diamond Choice Benefit. These aren’t extra certificates automatically handed to every Platinum and Diamond. You select one instead of another Choice Benefit. No new option to redeem SkyMiles directly for Hyatt free night certificates was announced, like we’d seen with the American partnership. Perhaps the biggest loss is Hyatt redemptions for American Airlines status for a day. Launch Bonuses Don’t Require Elite Status All linked members can participate in the launch offers: November 17–December 31, 2026: Register separately and complete a qualifying Hyatt stay to earn one additional qualifying night credit. That’s one bonus night total, not double nights for the entire stay. January 4–February 15, 2027: Complete your first eligible paid Delta Comfort-or-higher flight to earn 1,000 bonus Hyatt points. January 4–February 15, 2027: Complete your first qualifying Hyatt stay to earn 1,000 bonus SkyMiles. Paid and award stays qualify for the hotel promotions. Mr & Mrs Smith and The Venetian bookings through Hyatt qualify, while Homes & Hideaways doesn’t. The year-end stay must finish after registration and by December 31. For the January hotel offer, checkout must be by February 15. There’s also an Alila Mayakoba trip sweepstakes through November 23. Hyatt says reciprocal status challenges are coming. Is This Better Than Hyatt’s American Airlines Partnership? There are two different American partnerships to compare against. The original version, which ran through 2024, was better for everyday earning. Hyatt Discoverists earned the same one point per dollar on American airfare as Globalists, and regular coach counted. American elites earned a mile per eligible Hyatt dollar, and those miles also counted as Loyalty Points once that status system was introduced. Delta’s hotel earning has the same nominal mileage rate, but American miles are worth more. And there’s no status credit from those points. American’s final double earning terms also covered eligible FIND and Homes & Hideaways spending, both excluded here. On the flight side you’re earning Hyatt points either way, so the problem isn’t SkyMiles valuation. It’s the cabins that don’t qualify and the halved Discoverist rate. The 2025 American redesign removed double dipping and largely made members pay for partner benefits with their own points or by giving up another reward. Delta restores some actual additional earnings. The milestone comparison is mixed: Flight credits can be more useful than seat coupons. American offered two Preferred Seat coupons at 20 and 30 nights, then two Main Cabin Extra coupons at 40 and 50. Delta status starts later. American Gold was a choice at 70, 80 and 90 Hyatt nights. Delta offers lounge passes there, with its first announced status award at 100 nights. At 150 nights, Delta offers a higher tier. American’s offer topped out at Platinum. Delta Platinum is its third tier, although withholding Choice Benefits takes away a valuable part of the package. American also gave ConciergeKey members Hyatt Globalist and offered Hyatt status choices to other AAdvantage members. We’ll see what reciprocal status offers look like. It’s worth linking accounts and registering for the extra Hyatt night if there’s a stay coming up.
