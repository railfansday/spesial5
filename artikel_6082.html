Small Business Saturday can bring the kind of rush every owner wants. Orders roll in, and sales climb.

But for many companies, that influx of orders can also mean an influx of bills. Extra costs for inventory, shipping or temporary help can all add up.

This year's Small Business Saturday falls on Nov. 28.

Whether you're stocking up on inventory, investing in advertising or preparing to fulfill a surge in orders, the right business card could turn those related expenses into a welcome bonus or a stronger return. It can also bridge the gap between paying suppliers and receiving your sales proceeds.

Here are eight business credit cards worth considering, depending on your spending habits, cash flow and rewards goals.

Small Business Saturday is a shopping holiday for customers. For owners, it's an opportunity to grow sales — but it can also bring significant operating expenses.

Whether you're investing in advertising, replenishing inventory or shipping orders to customers, the right business credit card can help you turn those expenses into valuable cash back, points or miles.

Look for a card that rewards the expenses you already incur and fits your cash flow needs. Instant access can also help if you have holiday bills to cover right away, while earning rates and other card benefits will sustain you long after Small Business Saturday is over.
