Infosys: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Large deal wins provide multiyear revenue visibility even as near-term pricing pressure forces guidance cuts.

On its second-quarter fiscal year 2027 earnings call, held on Oct. 23, 2026, Infosys (INFY +2.60%) revised its full-year revenue growth guidance to 1% to 2% in constant currency terms. Competitive intensity and client demands for artificial intelligence (AI) productivity gains are pressuring pricing, but expanding deal pipelines show where future revenue will come from. Three takeaways from management explain how the company's navigating this shift.

Enterprise adoption of automated tools is accelerating across client operations, even as buyers demand productivity discounts on standard technology contracts. AI revenue grew 29% sequentially to account for over a tenth of total sales.

Management secured $5.2 billion in total contract value (TCV), the total dollar value of signed commitments, during the quarter. For the first half (H1) of the fiscal year, total large contract wins reached $8.8 billion.

Reduced client spending in retail and high-tech segments, combined with lower volume growth, forced management to lower its annual growth outlook. The company didn't change its operating margin (operating profit as a percentage of revenue) target of 20% to 22%.

Client spending remains cautious across several sectors, but expanding engagements in banking and energy help balance retail headwinds. The company holds structural advantages in offshore delivery models, while management tracks pricing discipline and client cost-optimization requests.

Long-term execution hinges on converting large contract commitments into active billing while expanding higher-margin technology deployments.
