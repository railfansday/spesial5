Is Cam Skattebo playing this week? Latest injury update on Giants RB

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The New York Giants are dealing with an injury to their clear-cut top running back coming off their bye week.

Cam Skattebo is dealing with a hamstring injury that he suffered on the final carry of his Week 7 game against the Houston Texans. He pulled up lame at the end of a 42-yard run in overtime and ceded the field for the final snap, which resulted in a game-winning, 30-yard Devin Singletary touchdown run.

Skattebo didn't seem concerned about his injury after the game, so many believed the second-year running back would be good to go for the team's Week 9 game against the Philadelphia Eagles.

Instead, Skattebo has continued to be absent from practice, which has thrown his status into flux.

Here's what to know about Skattebo's injury and when he might return to the field for the division-leading Giants.

The Giants are listing Skattebo as "doubtful" to play in the team's Week 9 game against the Eagles. The second-year running back was unable to practice leading up to the game, being listed as a non-participant at all three of New York's sessions.

John Harbaugh didn't rule Skattebo out during his media availability following Friday's practice, per ESPN's Jordan Raanan. However, the new designation would suggest Skattebo is unlikely to play, a fact that could be strengthened if the Giants elevate a running back from their practice squad on Saturday.

If Skattebo doesn't play in Week 9, his next chance to suit up will be the Giants' "Thursday Night Football" game against the Washington Commanders in Week 10.

The Giants have not yet provided a definitive timeline for Skattebo's return to the field.

Harbaugh didn't seem overly concerned about Skattebo's absence being of the long-term variety. He referred to the running back as "day-to-day" entering the practice week while saying the team would take a wait-and-see approach with the 24-year-old.

During a Wednesday, Nov. 4 press conference, Harbaugh also alluded to the tricky nature of predicting recovery for soft-tissue injuries, like hamstring problems.

"Skatt's got a hamstring, so you know how that goes," Harbaugh said, referring to Skattebo by his nickname. "We'll see."

Devin Singletary is currently entrenched as the clear-cut backup to Skattebo. The 29-year-old veteran is in his third season with the Giants but has played sparingly to date, racking up 80 yards and a touchdown on 18 carries.

Singletary produced exactly 437 rushing yards in each of his first two seasons with the Giants while serving largely as a backup or third-string option. The 2019 third-round pick posted three seasons with at least 1,000 scrimmage yards from 2021-23 and would get a lion's share of New York's backfield workload if Skattebo can't play.
