Evolution Petroleum Corporation (EPM -3.64%) reported fiscal first quarter results that were centered on the integration of its $16 million Permian minerals acquisition and the conversion of nonproducing acreage into cash through farm-outs. Management reported that revenue increased 6% year over year as higher realized commodity prices offset unplanned downtime in legacy working interest assets. The company is prioritizing a capital allocation strategy that emphasizes the growth of high-margin mineral and royalty interests, which do not require company-funded drilling or completion costs. Management stated that near-term financial priorities include utilizing asset monetization proceeds to reduce debt while sustaining the quarterly dividend and evaluating potential share repurchases.
