TJX (TJX +8.05%) reported third quarter financial results that exceeded its internal plans for sales, profitability, and earnings per share. Management reported that consolidated comparable sales grew 4% as customer transaction counts and average basket sizes increased. While the company faced headwinds from higher freight and fuel costs, it maintained its full-year pre-tax profit margin guidance and raised its full-year revenue outlook. The company indicated that execution issues at the Marmaxx division are being addressed through additional oversight, with performance expected to continue improving through the holiday season. Management stated that the company is well-positioned for the fourth quarter with high liquidity to chase merchandise deals in a market currently loaded with quality branded goods.
