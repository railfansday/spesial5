Here's How Many Shares of Starbucks You’d Need to Cover $744 in Coffee Using Dividends Alone

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Making a five-figure investment for free coffee may prove to be a tall order, especially after this stock's recent rally.

By Thomas Niel – Nov 21, 2026 at 7:40AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Starbucks ›

"Stop buying coffee out" has become commonplace personal finance advice, but is there a way to invest and cover your monthly Starbucks (SBUX -1.28%) bill? Let's say you go to Starbucks a few times a week, which, over the course of a year, can really add up: 150 trips a year at $4.96 per cup, or just under $5, comes out to $744 annually.

That said, for a five-figure investment, you could pay for these coffees from your dividends alone. However, in terms of potential gains from holding the stock, is it worth it? Not necessarily.

Following Starbucks' latest dividend increase, shares in what is one of the largest consumer discretionary companies now pay $0.63 per share quarterly. That comes to $2.52 per share in annual dividend payments. Divide $744 by $2.52 per share, and you get 296 Starbucks shares needed to cover this expense. Starbucks stock currently trades for around $106.50 per share, meaning you would need around $31,524 to enter this position.

Any way you slice it, that's a tall order. Even as annual dividend growth has averaged nearly 8% over the past five years, the latest dividend increase was only 1.6%. If low dividend growth persists, the price of a cup of Starbucks coffee could outpace the payouts. Also, don't assume buying today means trading lower dividend growth for the potential for greater gains down the road.

The company's "Back to Starbucks" turnaround, focused on improving the in-store experience, has been in motion for over two years now. Recent results, including a 7.2% increase in same-store sales during the September-ended quarter, have investors very confident about further improvements in growth and profitability.

However, after this rally, the stock trades at 33.5 times forward earnings, a big premium to peers like McDonald's. Although further strong quarterly results could help sustain this valuation, any sort of earnings hiccup could quickly send shares back in the wrong direction. Considering this, I would skip buying Starbucks shares for now.
