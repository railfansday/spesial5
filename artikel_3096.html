Apple claims six of China’s 10 bestselling smartphones during Singles’ Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Counterpoint Research says Apple led smartphone sales in China during this year’s Double 11 shopping festival (also known as Singles’ Day), despite a broader slump in smartphone demand. Here are the details.

Last year, Counterpoint Research found that during China’s Double 11 shopping period, strong demand for the iPhone 17 lineup helped drive a 3% year-over-year increase in smartphone sales in the country.

This year, Apple once again came out on top, though rising smartphone prices and limited promotional discounts weighed on overall consumer demand.

According to the report, Apple saw an 8% year-over-year decline in iPhone sales during the promotional period. Still, compared with the market-wide 17% drop, Apple fared considerably better than most of its competitors.

Furthermore, Apple accounted for six of the 10 bestselling smartphone models during the promotional period, including all five of the top spots.

The iPhone 18 Pro Max topped the ranking, while the newly launched iPhone Duo came in ninth despite going on sale only on October 23, nearly two weeks after the shopping festival kicked off on October 12.

Counterpoint also noted that Huawei “was the only major OEM to record growth, up 29% YoY, driven mainly by the Enjoy 90 Pro Max and Pura X View, with the new Enjoy 100 series providing an additional boost.” The Enjoy 90 Pro Max was the only Huawei model to make the top 10, coming in sixth.
