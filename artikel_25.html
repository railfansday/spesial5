Kremlin declines comment on reports 700,000 Russian soldiers killed in Ukraine war

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov on Wednesday declined to comment on reports in Russian and international news outlets that around 700,000 Russian soldiers have been killed in the Ukraine war so far. The reports were based on hacked data from Russia's official statistics agency. Peskov said only the defence ministry can disclose figures and told reporters "not to pay attention to any other data".
