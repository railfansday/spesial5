France's socioeconomic crisis: Macron 'trying to impose fiscal discipline on his successor'

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Charli James is pleased to welcome Andrew Smith, Historian of Modern France and Lecturer in Liberal Arts at Queen Mary University of London. He argues that Emmanuel Macron is seeking to "impose fiscal discipline on his successor as president" after "presiding over quite a substantial deterioration in the public finances." For Smith, the difficulty is that "it's easier to agree on the need for fiscal discipline than on who should pay for it or should have the authority to actually determine it." He suggests that the far right "may play the deciding role" in whether the proposal succeeds, potentially presenting itself as "solving the problems created by Macron's two terms."
