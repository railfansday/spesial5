Ella Langley and Tucker Wetmore’s Full Relationship Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Ella Langley and Tucker Wetmore are country music’s newest couple.

After months of speculation about their relationship, multiple sources confirmed to People on Sept. 23, 2026, that Langley and Wetmore are dating. The news came one day after the two country singers attended the Nashville Songwriter Awards, where Langley celebrated another major moment in her career.

Neither Langley nor Wetmore has publicly commented on their relationship yet. But their history goes back to at least late 2025, when they began appearing at some of the same industry events before sparking dating rumors during a trip to the British Virgin Islands that December. Since then, they’ve also shared the stage.

Below, a full timeline of everything we know about Langley and Wetmore’s relationship so far.

Langley and Wetmore were both at the BMI Country Awards in Nashville on November 18. The two appeared together in a group photo from the event.

At the time, neither had publicly suggested that they were romantically involved.

Dating speculation began the following month after Langley and Wetmore were photographed together at the Soggy Dollar Bar in the British Virgin Islands.

Langley and Wetmore reunited onstage on March 10 at the Heart Strings for Hope charity event.

During the event, Langley joined Wetmore for an acoustic performance of his song “Sunburn.” The two sat across from each other with their guitars as they performed the track together.

On September 22, Langley and Wetmore both attended the ninth annual Nashville Songwriter Awards at Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium.

It was a particularly big night for Langley: “Choosin’ Texas” won Song of the Year at the ceremony.

After the awards, Langley and Wetmore were photographed leaving the Ryman together through a side door.

The following day, multiple sources confirmed to People that the two are dating. Neither Langley nor Wetmore has spoken about the relationship themselves.

Langley and Wetmore appeared to take another trip to the Virgin Islands together. A man dining at a restaurant they went to posted a video of them arriving there.
