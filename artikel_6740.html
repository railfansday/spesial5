Time to Discuss Whatever’s Going on With Alix Earle and Zack Bia Amid Reports They’re “Casually Dating”

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

From a Sardinia yacht hang to a rumored Vegas dance floor makeout.

Even if you’re not familiar with Zack Bia, you’re definitely familiar with the women he’s (reportedly) dated. The DJ and producer has been linked to everyone from Olivia Rodrigo to Madelyn Cline to Madison Beer, so it was only a matter of time before rumors started about him and fellow influencer-about-town Alix Earle. What began with a (seemingly) friendly hang on a yacht in Sardinia has evolved into an alleged dance floor makeout in Vegas—but naturally, things are a little more complicated than that!

Let’s take a walk through Alix Earle and Zack Bia’s rumored romance from the very beginning (including how Julia Mervis and Shaboozey fit into the whole thing). Starting with...

Dating rumors first kick off after Alix and Zack are spotted together on a yacht off the coast of Sardinia along with mutual friend Stassie Karanikolaou, model Nadine Leopold, and entrepreneur Poppy Jamie (as well as Below Deck’s Fraser Olender), where they look kinda cozy.

They even make a viral TikTok together...but (aside from the photos where Zack’s hand is on Alix’s lower back) the vibes seem pretty friendly.

The two are photographed together yet again, this time grabbing dinner in Beverly Hills with brand strategist Cait Bailey. There is a hug—but again, it all seems pretty friendly.

Zack sparks romance rumors with Alix’s friend Julia Mervis after fans spot them looking cozy in a suite at a Toronto Maple Leafs game...even though Alix is also in attendance.

Later in the month, Zack attends Darren Dzienciol’s Haunted Hotel Halloween party hosted by Vanessa Hudgens, and while Julia is nowhere in sight, Alix is definitely there!

Following the LSU Tigers versus Texas Longhorns football game in Baton Rouge on November 14, a Deuxmoi tipster reportedly sees Alix with musician Shaboozey at a Raising Cane’s afterparty, where the two are apparently “all over each other.”

That said, while they were both at the game, there’s no photographic evidence to support the above claim.

Less than two weeks later, Alix and Shaboozey both attend the Sports Illustrated Circuit Series party at XS Nightclub during the weekend of the F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix...except Alix is reportedly spotted “making out” on the dance floor with Zack.

Soon after, Page Six enters the chat, claiming that—according to sources—Alix and Zack are “casually dating, if not exclusively.”

The insider says that “he’s been chasing her for a long time,” adding, “He’s been persistent. Is it exclusive? I don’t think so. But she’s like, ‘I’m here for it right now.’”

However, other sources dispute the alleged hookup, insisting the pair did not kiss, are not dating, and are “just very good friends.”

Oh, if you think Alix and Julia are now beefing, think again, because they already got ahead of the rumors with a TikTok of themselves fake-fighting (set to Sabrina Carpenter’s “Taste,” of course).
