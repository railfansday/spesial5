The 10 Best Lipsticks for Women Over 40 That Deliver Instant Hydration and Lasting Color

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

A swipe of lipstick is one of the easiest ways to look instantly put together with minimal effort. Even if you have nothing else on your face, a hint of lip color can lift your complexion. It also works as an accessory or finishing touch for even the simplest outfits.

As we get older, lips naturally feel drier and lose volume. A good balm nourishes and comforts parched lips, but if you want more pigment, many lipsticks are designed for mature skin.

First, steer clear of ultra-matte formulas, as they tend to dehydrate lips and highlight every fine lip line imaginable. They also tend to look flat.

For extra long wear, try a velvety, soft-matte formula instead. It delivers a similar, statement-making finish that's elegant while creating a pillowy, plush effect.

Satin formulas perform well, too. The gentle shine creates the illusion of healthier-looking lips, and it feels less drying throughout the day. That said, brighter shades like reds and oranges can be a little messy to apply, so apply one layer, blend with a fingertip, and blot with a tissue. Repeat to build intensity. If feathering (when the color "bleeds" into fine lines around your lips) is an issue, a clear lip liner around the perimeter should create a barrier.

It's always best to get your skincare benefits from your lip balm, but nourishing plant oils and hydrating ingredients such as glycerin and hyaluronic acid in your lipstick will help maintain hydration as the day goes on.

For makeup artist and Jones Road founder Bobbi Brown, there are no hard-and-fast rules—no matter your age. "It's about your style, what you feel most comfortable in," she tells Bazaar UK.

If you're keen on neutral tones, remember that neutral doesn't mean beige. "Pay attention to what your natural lip color is. I have a slightly bluish-purple tone to my lips. You might have more of a pink tone. It's really great to find a lipstick that really looks like your lips, and the only way is to test it on your bottom lip," says Brown, who notes that a great neutral should not wash you out.

Revered makeup artist Bobbi Brown is no stranger to creating iconic lipsticks. The Classic Lip has all the hallmarks of a modern formula: creamy, intensely hydrating, and richly pigmented. The 12-color range offers enough variety without overwhelming consumers. Expect skin-enhancing neutrals and bolder tones for any occasion.

Trinny London excels at makeup for mature skin, and this recent launch is certainly no exception. It's a glossy, mega-hydrating formula that reflects light beautifully, making lips look plumper, and it's formulated with peptides to help reduce fine lines over time. There's a full spectrum of nude shades, but we love the bold red Poppy for festive nights.

A richly pigmented, buttery-smooth formula that promises to minimize dryness without sacrificing impact. It contains plenty of nourishing oils to keep moisture lasting without feathering into fine lines. It has a rosy scent, so keep that in mind if you're sensitive to fragrance.

Everything Victoria Beckham does delivers instant polish to the lips without extra effort. This slimline lipstick is no exception. It glides on like a tinted lip balm, adding a touch more color while infusing skin with hydration. VB has perfected the art of neutrals, so expect to find easy-to-wear hues for all skin tones. Glide on, then pat to blend any hard edges. It's one of those formulas you won't need a mirror to apply.

Refillable makeup isn't new, but Lisa Eldridge has painstakingly ensured every part of her Rouge Experience lipstick is recyclable, while it still looks like it belongs on a movie star's dresser. The lipstick formula combines oil, gel, and cream to ensure all-day comfort. The color pigments build easily and sit between a soft matte and a shiny finish. It's unfragranced, too.

Creative and image director of Christian Dior Makeup, Peter Philips, reviewed Dior's bestselling lip colors worldwide to create a tightly curated collection of 10 sophisticated, wearable shades for every skin tone. Nude doesn't mean beige, according to Philips, and this line includes a complexion-lifting neutral, whatever that looks like to you. The emollient formula glides on like balmy, satin lipstick but won't budge once it sets. Expect long-wear color without the drying side effects.

When Hourglass creates matte lipsticks, rest assured they feature elevated colors and sit beautifully on the lips without highlighting fine lines, feathering, or flaking. This elegant formula feels velvety soft, with buildable pigments that stay put.

Sisley's interpretation of a shimmery, luminous lip color is as elevated as you've come to expect from this storied French brand. Phyto Rouge Shine is infused with the finest pearlescent particles in a translucent base, delivering sheer, buildable color and a plumping effect that still looks understated and grown-up.

Hermès Beauty creative director Gregoris Pyrpylis drew inspiration from Hermès scarves when designing this collection. This translates into buildable, luminous color and a smooth, tactile finish that feels like a second skin. Even the deepest shades look refined, with a radiance that brightens the face. Plus, the wide, angled bullet makes application as easy as wearing a lip balm.

Not all liquid lipsticks are created equal. Violette's soft, whipped texture is truly modern, delivering intense colors without a heavy feel. The gel-cream texture blurs out fine lines, too, so lips appear plush and smooth.
