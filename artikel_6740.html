Meet the Cast and Characters of ‘Blade Runner 2099’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The ’80s film Blade Runner is the latest to get the streaming series treatment, after the legacy sequel Blade Runner 2049 revitalized the science fiction noir franchise about humanoid robots called “replicants” that slowly took over society back in 2017. The new series takes place 80 years after that original film. What’s changed? Who’s in charge now? Before you press play on Blade Runner 2099, let’s run through the cast and characters.

This is the official description of the series from Prime Video:

Which of the new characters are human? Which ones are replicants? Does it even matter anymore? Here’s what you need to know.

Olwen is an old-school replicant Blade Runner on the verge of retirement.

Michelle Yeoh is a former pageant queen, ballet dancer, and Academy Award-winning actress. You know her from Everything Everywhere All At Once, Wicked, Crazy Rich Asians, Memoirs of a Geisha, Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings, Star Trek: Discovery, American Born Chinese, and Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon. She is also a Golden Globe, Critics Choice, and Actor Award winner. Before all of that, she had a prolific Hong Kong action and martial arts filmography. Legend!

Cora is a replicant who goes undercover as a human.

Hunter Schafer is a model and actress best known for playing Jules on Euphoria. She played President Snow’s cousin Tigris in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes and followed that up with the films Cuckoo, Kinds of Kindness, and Mother Mary. Next, you’ll see her in the upcoming Tom Ford movie Cry to Heaven, adapted from Anne Rice’s historical novel.

Bebeto is a human who teams up with Cora.

Dimitri played Reaper, a District 11 tribute, in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes. Some of his other credits include four episodes of the Netflix series Warrior Nun, the Prime Video series Love Addicts, the Apple TV+ series Hijack, and a decent smattering of productions in his home country, Germany.

Lewis starred as Danny in the drama series Somewhere Boy. You might also recognize him from Masters of the Air, Generation Z, Shetland, and Silent Witness. He played a young version of Peter Capaldi’s character in the Black Mirror episode “Plaything.” His film credits include gnarly horror movies like The Severed Sun and The Damned. Next you’ll see him in the ensemble cast of Lorna Tucker’s Bare.

Matthew is best known for playing Larys Strong on House of the Dragon. (His co-star Freddie Fox also appears in the Blade Runner 2099 pilot.) He also played Lucien Bonaparte in Ridley Scott’s film Napoleon, and starred in the medical drama series Casualty. If you’re a historical drama junkie, you might know him as Mr. Crowe from Sanditon and/or Mr. Drummie from the 2023 Great Expectations.

Helena is a very mysterious character you’ll glimpse in the first episode.

Sheila Atim is an Olivier Award-winning actress for her supporting role in the Bob Dylan jukebox musical Girl from the North Country. You might recognize her from films and shows like Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, The Woman King, Harlots, and The Underground Railroad. Atim debuted as Akasha in The Vampire Lestat, aka Interview with the Vampire season 3, and will take center stage when the AMC series rebrands to adapt Anne Rice’s Queen of the Damned in season 4.

Tom Burke is a stage and screen actor who played Praetorian Jack in Furiosa and Orson Welles in Mank. His other film credits include The Souvenir, The Souvenir Part II, Living, The Wonder, and Black Bag. Some of his television credits include Jerico, The Hour, and classic literary adaptations like War & Peace, Great Expectations, and The Musketeers. You’ll see him next in Robert Eggers’ Werwulf and the upcoming Elden Ring movie.

Daniel is a BAFTA-winning actor for the television film Eric and Ernie. You might recognize him from Flowers, Jericho, Watership Down, Sick Note, and Renegade Nell. He also does a lot of stage acting, including a one-man show, and previously worked as a stand-up comedian.

Amy Lennox is a prolific West End theater actress who was nominated for an Olivier Award in 2016. She played Chloe Godard on 100 episodes of the medical drama Holby City and also starred in the short-lived sitcom Only Child.

You’ll meet this mysterious character in the first episode.

Katelyn is an Irish actress you may have seen before in The Princess with Joey King, the murder mystery series Clean Sweep, or The Nun II.
