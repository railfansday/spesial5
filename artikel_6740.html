Beauty Marks: The Best Beauty Looks of the Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Welcome back to Beauty Marks: Vogue’s weekly edition of the best moments in celebrity beauty, from Vogue editors’ IG feeds, and all the glam of the fashion and pop culture landscapes. Each week, we curate the nail art to pin for your next nail appointment, new recruits to ‘Team Bob’, and major red carpet moments from the week’s most glamorous affairs. As always, it’s as much about celebrity beauty as it is about the makeup artists, hairstylists, and nail artists, as well as the creators crafting the trends you’re about to see everywhere.

This week in beauty was a study in contrasts: sculptural hair met soft-focus makeup, while barely-there lips shared the spotlight with high-impact eyes. Sienna Miller made a romantic case for the blurred lip, Emily Blunt embraced a wintry shade of blonde, and Kim Petras dialed up the drama with graphic liner and ice-blonde bangs. Elsewhere, Kate Young turned a diamond into the ultimate hair accessory, Kris Jenner revisited the razor-sharp bob, and Lily Allen served up a masterclass in hair transformations. From elaborate nail art to gravity-defying chignons, there was plenty to look twice at.

Scroll through the week’s best of celebrity beauty and pop culture’s glam below, and head to the app to vote for your favorite.

Jennifer Lopez was in full bombshell mode, with glossy, caramel-swirled waves by Chris Appleton, bronzed, softly sculpted makeup and a pillowy nude lip by Ernesto Casillas, and a gleaming posy pink and almond-shaped manicure courtesy of Tom Bachik.

Emily Blunt embraces the winter blonde: her icy, champagne-toned lengths fashioned into an immaculate sculptural updo by Renato Campora. A wash of rosy blush, softly defined eyes, and a precise red lip by Jenn Streicher complemented the cool-toned elegance, with pale glossy nails by Julie K.

Natanya went full celestial showgirl for her London performance, with individually placed silver Swarovski crystals tracing razor-sharp jet-black wings, icy metallic inner corners, and cherub-pink blush by Georgia Hope. Essa sculpted her glossy reddish brunette hair into a sweeping side part, with a delicate, forehead-framing kiss curl.

Lily Allen’s West End Girl tour has been a veritable hair marathon, with six distinctly different looks dreamed up by Chad Maxwell. Our favorite? A tousled, rock-chick half-updo with choppy bangs, teased volume, and loose, face-framing tendrils. Smoky ’60s eyes and a muted rose lip by Lilly Keys completed the look.

A diamond-studded twist on the classic chignon: At the CFDA Awards, Kate Young wore her platinum blonde hair slicked into a sculptural spiral bun by Jacob Rozenberg, with a sparkling, mesmerizing black-and-diamond Taffin jewel nestled at its center. This season, it doesn’t get much chicer than this piece of hair jewelry.

Nail artist Mei Kawajiri turned a set of extra-long, square-tipped acrylics into a miniature cabinet of curiosities. Scribbled illustrations, pastel washes, and translucent 3D charms—including a tiny beer mug, a white cross, and a miniature tart—made each nail its own surreal little artwork.

Once known for her signature pixie, birthday girl Kris Jenner has been enjoying the sleek, jet-black micro-bob for some time now; here, tucked behind the ears and flicked out at the ends by Justine Marjan. Ash K Holm went for fluttery lashes, softly bronzed cheeks, and a pink-nude lip, with the full, feathery brows taking center stage.

Sienna Miller was all soft-focus romance at the Madden premiere. Nina Park gave her a beautifully blurred rose-pink lip, flushed cheeks, and delicately smudged eyes, while Jenny Cho swept her honey-blonde waves into a loose, half-up style, with wispy tendrils just touching her clavicle.

Kim Petras served icy pop princess for a rave at False Idols, with waist-skimming platinum lengths and wispy, brow-grazing bangs by James Lear. Helen Rose sharpened her baby-blue eyes with graphic black wings, spiky lashes, frosted inner corners, and a glossy, overlined pink pout.

A lesson in hair origami: George Curran crafted Victoria Turner’s buttery blonde lengths into an intricate, double-tiered chignon, with two sculptural loops stacked at the nape and a single strand left loose around the face. Hair by way of hero products from Kérastase, with makeup by Amir.
