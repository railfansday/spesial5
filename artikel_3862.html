Marvel Rivals season 11 countdown: Exact start time and date

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Marvel Rivals’ next big season is nearly here, and after things slowed down a bit in season 10 with only one new playable character, the arrival of season 11 feels especially exciting. If you’re looking forward to this one, you’ll want to know exactly when season 11 is launching.

Although Marvel Rivals sees minor updates weekly, it’s the big seasonal updates like this one where the real changes and additions occur. There’s lots to look forward to as always, so to make sure you’re ready to dive in, here’s the exact time and date when season 11 of Marvel Rivals begins.

Season 11 of Marvel Rivals arrives on Nov. 13. We don’t have a precise launch time, and we likely won’t get one ahead of release. Despite this, the game sticks to a very reliable and consistent release schedule, which means there’s an unofficial but generally very dependable timeframe for the update maintenance and release.

As long as everything stays on track and sticks to the same schedule as usual, season 11 should go live at 5am CT. Maintenance should start around 9am UTC, and after about two hours of downtime, servers should be back up so you can start playing.

Marvel Rivals season 11 will be approximately one month long. This is about how long all seasons are. Halfway through this period, the game will see another big update and a transition into season 11.5.

As of the time of writing, the season 11 heroes have not been officially revealed. However, we do have some key clues that may hint at who’s to come in the final Gallery Card for season 10.5. Every season, the final card provides clues for what’s to come next, so it’s a fairly reliable way to predict the featured characters coming soon.

The first character who seems to be teased for season 11 is Nightcrawler. In the Gallery Card, Moon Girl is holding his sword. Since this clue is the boldest, he’ll likely be our launch hero.

The second character isn’t quite as clear, but based on all of the Doomsday tie-in events, the name of the Gallery Card, which is “Doomsday is near,” and the details of the story featured with this card, it might be Doctor Doom himself. If not, there’s also a mysterious bat symbol featured prominently in this art, which could point to someone like Dracula, or perhaps Green Goblin.

Before season 11 arrives, it’s a good idea to check off all the current events in season 10.5, as not all of them will be sticking around. Make sure you fully complete all Night of Illusions puzzles, jump into Avengers: Endgame, and test out Avengers: Infinity War.
