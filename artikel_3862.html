'I Mean, Guys, Really?!': GTA 7 Questions Batted Aside by Take-Two Boss

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

While it’s been available for several days now, I imagine you’re only just beginning to scratch the surface of GTA 6.

I’m now comfortably over 200 hours into my game, and it boggles my mind how much I’ve still got left to see. (I have been writing our GTA 6 guide simultaneously, though, so that is inflating my number a fair bit.)

I almost can’t believe business podcast TBPN is already asking Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick about GTA 7, then – but it is.

The executive was fairly polite in his first response, saying:

“Once you get through several hundred hours of the most incredible fun you’ve ever heard, ask the question again.”

You’d have thought that would shut down the conversation, but no.

Zelnick was later pushed on how big the map in GTA 7 could be, and he launched a wry response:

“Let me check my watch. Oh, it’s still November 19th. And you’re asking a question about GTA 7. I mean, guys, really?”

The transcript makes Zelnick sound more agitated than he actually was, but I still find it crazy the hosts of TBPN thought it appropriate to mention the next game, when this one took 13 years to release.

Obviously the next step for Rockstar is the new version of GTA Online, but I think it’d be nice to reflect on what we have for a moment, before we start thinking about what’s to come.
