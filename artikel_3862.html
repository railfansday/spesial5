DOOM: The Dark Ages DLC ‘Revelations’ – ‘Slaughter Mode’ update launches November 16

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Publisher Bethesda Softworks and developer id Software will release a free update for DOOM: The Dark Ages paid downloadable content “Revelations” on November 16 that adds the endless combat mode, “Slaughter Mode,” the companies announced.

Push your slaying skills to the limit with Slaughter Mode, a new endless combat challenge available November 16 as a free update for owners of DOOM: The Dark Ages downloadable content “Revelations.”

Tear through relentless, escalating and unpredictable waves of demonic hordes, carving out your personal best scores and unlocking rewards using the Skulls collected from the carnage. With each checkpoint reached, players must ask themselves if they want to cash in their run or risk it all for more slaughter.

No two runs through Slaughter Mode are identical as you fight escalating rounds of random encounters, bosses and arenas, turning up the heat as you proceed further into your run.

Complete rounds to earn Skulls, which unlock rewards such as Slaughter Mode-only Slayer skins, weapon skins for all modes and profile icons. You can also unlock tracks from Andrew Hulshult’s IDKFA soundtrack to play in both Slaughter Mode and the Ripatorium! Take on weekly challenges for more opportunities to earn rewards and show off your slaying prowess.

Survive long enough to reach a Round Checkpoint and you’ll have the chance to Secure your current Skulls against death. Choose Risk It instead to add to your Skull Multiplier, increasing the Skulls you can earn during the run—but there’s a catch. If you’re slain while Risk It is active, all your unsecured Skulls are forfeited!

Death isn’t the end, however: every Round Checkpoint you complete also unlocks another starting point for future runs, letting you resume the carnage in those later encounters sooner.

DOOM: The Dark Ages is available now for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC via Steam and Microsoft Store. It is also available via Game Pass.
