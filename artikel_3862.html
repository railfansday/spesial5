The GTA 6 Tourist Trap mission signals the end of chapter 1, meaning there a few things you'll want to make sure you finish. If you don't do them before this point of no return they'll be lost for the rest of the game.

I won't go into details to avoid spoilers, but you'll understand why once you've played it as some unavoidable stuff happens that significantly advances the main story (in fact, check out our GTA 6 Tourist Trap walkthrough if you’re stuck on what to do, or the GTA 6 Chapter 1 Walkthrough for a bigger breakdown). So, with that all in mind, here's a breakdown of everything you need to do in Chapter 1 before you get locked out of it by doing this mission and eventually reaching Chapter 2.

I've kept this guide as spoiler-free as possible but consider yourself warned!

In short, focus on wrapping up all the pink missions in the Key Lento area, and grabbing a few missable collectibles.

Illegal Operations, such as the GTA 6 Exotic Animal Front and the two Kingfisher robbery opportunities, will remain accessible.
