Svilar’s condition improves ahead of Parma-Roma

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Mile Svilar suddenly stopped playing, leaving the entire Roma fanbase breathless.

It was early October, and his little toe gave way: a stress fracture, a bothersome injury that needed to be overcome as quickly as possible.

The Giallorossi goalkeeper was sidelined for nine games, with Gollini and Vasquez trying to replace him. It’s impossible not to miss him, given that the former Benfica player, in terms of performance, is the best goalkeeper in Serie A.

Svilar is a sure thing for Gian Piero Gasperini’s Roma, almost always among the best on the pitch. Precise, cool, quick, capable of turning the impossible into the possible, but also of shrinking the net to the point where opposing attackers begin to suspect they’ve chosen the wrong profession.

As Corriere dello Sport reports, the 1999-born player’s little finger has stopped hurting. There’s no longer any discomfort. Yesterday, Svilar returned to defend the Trigoria goalposts, darting from post to post without fear.

The worst is now behind him. His foot is fine, his head and reflexes are there: he’ll be back in the starting lineup against Parma. This news reassures Gasperini, the team, and especially the Roma fans.
