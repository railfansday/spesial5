Pato admits Nesta’s injury ‘fury’ could’ve been handled better: “Young and didn’t understand”

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Alexandre Pato is one of the greatest ‘What If’ players of his generation and AC Milan will always hold him in high regard.

For many, even non-Milan fans, Pato is one of the defining players of the ‘What If’ term. The Brazilian had everything required to make him a modern great. Fantastic in front of goal, skilful, fun and genuinely exciting, he seemed like a potential world beater.

With the Rossoneri, there are only fond memories and he’s one that most will have loved growing up.

However, his record with injuries prevented him from ever becoming the player that most hoped he’d be one day. Yet, the relationship never soured and he remains a fan favourite, even to this day.

Speaking to Luca Toni for the Prime special Fenomeni column, Pato responded to some questions about his injury troubles and one encounter with Alessandro Nesta in the dressing room, as MilanNews relays.

“I remember that after yet another injury, Nesta came into the locker room furious and said I shouldn’t have played. I have enormous respect for AC Milan, who gave me everything, but perhaps that moment could have been handled better.

“One of the things that hurt me the most, and which I’ve never spoken about before, was hearing people at the club say I’d never play again. That was sad, especially because as players, we know that a club is like a company, and if you don’t play, someone else comes in to replace you.

“At that time, I couldn’t speak out, especially because I was young and didn’t understand. With today’s mentality, I’d handle myself differently.”
