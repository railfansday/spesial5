Opinion: Liverpool finally have their Jurgen Klopp replacement

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

When Jurgen Klopp announced his departure from Liverpool in January 2024 many supporters worried what the future might look like at Anfield.

Concerns were initially unfounded, with the Reds lifting the Premier League title in their very first season post-Klopp, but things have become complicated since then.

Arne Slot, the man who delivered the club's 20th league title, was relieved of his duties over the summer after a disappointing fifth place finish and Andoni Iraola was appointed as his replacement.

The Spaniard has enjoyed a steady start to life in L4, and the Reds are looking more like the team Klopp left behind with every week that passes.

Iraola is the next KloppLiverpool's dominating victory over Manchester United on Sunday was Iraola's biggest result since taking the reins in Merseyside. The win saw the Reds move just one point behind second place Brighton and seven points off league leaders Arsenal. A title challenge is still a bit optimistic, but the team are certainly starting to close the gap to the league's best.

The only manager to go on a longer run was Klopp, who was unbeaten against his bitter rivals for eight consecutive league games between 2018 and 2022.

Of course, it is too early to say that Iraola's tenure will be as successful as Klopp's, but early signs are positive and the 44-year-old has managed to get more out of many of his players than Slot did.

Some kinks in the midfield still need to be ironed out, but overall the team are starting to consistently deliver at a level that the Anfield faithful have become accustomed to over the past decade.

Supporters will be hopeful that Iraola can continue to mirror Klopp, and that he can bring as much success to Anfield as the legendary German boss did.
