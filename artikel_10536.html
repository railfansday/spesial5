Jujutsu Kaisen’s Gojo voice actor had no idea he was recording for GTA 6: “Sit on my lap daddy”

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Kaiji Tang, the English voice of Satoru Gojo in Jujutsu Kaisen, says he recorded dialogue for GTA 6 without realizing he was working on Rockstar’s game.

Tang also voices Sanemi Shinazugawa in the English dub of Demon Slayer. He’s played plenty of video game characters, too, including Ichiban Kasuga, the lead in Yakuza: Like a Dragon.

Tang announced his involvement on November 19, the same day GTA 6 launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. He followed up by explaining that he had no idea which game he’d recorded for, adding, “imagine my shock lol.”

One fan brought up the line “sit on daddy’s lap” and asked what Tang thought he was recording. Tang joked, “i dunno demon slayer?”

When Tang first announced he was in the game, he gave fans a clue about where to find him. “if you want to find me, watch cartoons in game,” he wrote in a reply.

Tang has three roles in The Johnsons, one of the cartoons players can watch on TV in GTA 6. He voices Jax, a therapist, and a character listed as “Guy in Parking Lot” in Rockstar’s official credits. The episode and character behind the line quoted in the X thread are still unconfirmed.

The Johnsons is one of several shows made for GTA 6’s in-game TVs. There’s also Macca & Friends, a Saturday morning-style cartoon on Weazel starring the pink alligator featured in the game’s Collector’s Box.
