Sophia Laforteza Reunites With Fellow Katseye Members on a Stylish Evening Out

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

She’s been on hiatus from the group since August

It’s a girl’s night! On Monday evening, Katseye member Lara Raj celebrated her 21st birthday with a belated party at Soho Warehouse in Los Angeles, where she felt the love from her fellow girl group members, including none other than Sophia Laforteza.

This was a notable appearance given that Laforteza has been on hiatus from the group since August, when HYBE announced that she would be stepping away from public activities until 2027 in order to prioritize her “mental health and wellness.” Now, it appears she’s slowly making her way back into the public eye.

Laforteza didn’t return to the spotlight quietly—she entered the fête in a dramatic fur overcoat in a merlot shade, which enveloped her like a blanket. The piece added serious dimension to her look thanks to its voluminous shape and the fluffy collar that hugged her neck like a ruff.

To ground the look, the singer wore an inky skirt, sunglasses, and patent-leather heels from Christian Louboutin, with the red soles giving a subtle wink to her statement jacket. She added further intrigue to her cameo by entering the venue alongside her rumored boyfriend, Leon Barretto, who dressed in head-to-toe black.

Fellow Katseye member Megan Skiendiel was also seen in attendance, wearing a camouflage camisole layered underneath an olive bomber jacket and paired with black cargo pants and taupe basketball sneakers.

Other stars who were present included actors from Netflix’s The Body, like Sofia Wylie and Emily Roman, alongside musicians like Jade, reality stars like Melanie Moreno, and more.

No matter what happens professionally, it’s clear that these young women will continue to show up for each another as friends.
