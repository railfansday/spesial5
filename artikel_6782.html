The Woven Tote Bag Trend Is Taking Over Runways and Workdays

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Woven bags have officially reached icon status.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Is there any other bag that works as hard in real life as the tote bag? When it comes to being on duty and looking good, probably not. The humble tote has come in all iterations: lightweight cotton, supple leather, and suede. But more recently, the runways have been obsessed with making the oversize tote-style bags in luscious jumbo woven iterations. We saw them on the spring/summer 2027 runways at Dries Van Noten, Courrèges, Bottega Veneta, and Altuzarra. And then, labels like Bembien, Naghedi, and Dragon Diffusion are pumping out woven totes like there’s no tomorrow.

A big woven bag offers up texture and interest in a sea of ubiquitous canvas logo totes. It also gives you a bit of resilience so you don’t have to baby your bag on, say, a subway commute or a brisk morning walk where the risk of bumping into others is second nature. It is the working girl’s best friend. That’s just one of the reasons why fashion retailers have fallen head over heels for the style lately.

“Regardless of lifestyle, everyone needs a bag that carries a lot, and potentially the kitchen sink,” Ryan Kleman, Moda Operandi’s VP of buying, accessories, tells ELLE. The great news about a woven bag is that it also seamlessly goes with everything. Tone it down for a day at the office with a button-down top or a casual dress, or take it out for a nice dinner; a silk scarf tied about the strap can be the pop of color or embellishment you might crave for a more formal occasion.

In the case of some of these brands, the woven tote is literal fashion history. Bottega Veneta, for example, first introduced its signature “Intrecciato” woven leather technique in 1975. The fashion house’s spring/summer 2027 collection modernized the technique by applying it to enormous, hulking bags that swayed when worn over the shoulder. Paired with layered sweaters and matching leather belts, the bags felt like a decidedly fresh take on wearable artisanship by creative director Louise Trotter.

In the case of Dries Van Noten, one model carried an absolutely huge woven raffia-effect foldover clutch. Styled with a heather gray hoodie rolled up to reveal leather bubblegum-pink opera gloves with a biker-style cutout and buckle at the hand, the bag itself proved just how versatile a woven piece can be.

If you’re indeed thinking of upgrading your everyday work bag for a woven tote, it’s better to think bigger. “The oversized scale of the bags and the woven construction provide the perfect details to convert a chore into a fashion statement,” says Kleman. “At Moda, we have dubbed this trend the Big A** Bag and our customers are loving it.” This, too, is true: the bigger the weave, the better the carryall.
