It does not feel like fall or the holidays are anywhere near here in New York. It’s muggy, raining, and 70 degrees. For me, though, that’s what Christmas was growing up. And the impending holidays really, really started to get into my mind this past week. Flights are booked for Thanksgiving; rental car (because flight prices are so insane) booked for Christmas. You can feel the back two months of the year slipping away already. Part of that is down to the volume of products that are getting released.

This week, we were inundated with things coming out. If we’re talking on the whole, plenty of it was bad. But within our little world, Esquire’s editors found plenty to love.

For example, was stopped in my tracks by these first two jackets—one from Edward Sexton and one from Ralph Lauren, both some of my favorite places for tailoring. There’s that stunning La Marzocco espresso machine, a gorgeous Thuma sofa in collaboration with Pendleton, my dream Lucchese blanket, candles, colognes, and calendars. It’s a lot, but we parsed through the rough to find you 27 flawless fancy diamonds. Check them out.

So you have a jacket that can finish a tuxedo or be thrown over jeans and a T-shirt for brunch. It’s a masterclass for how men dress now. —Luke Guillory, commrece editor
