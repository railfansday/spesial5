Middlesbrough FC striker Will Lankshear hands Championship high-fliers a boost - plus Kim Hellberg vow

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Championship top-scorer, who has netted 15 times for Boro this term, was missed badly in Boro's last two games before the international break at Wrexham and Watford, with Hellberg's side failing to find the net in either.

Lankshear missed both away appointments due to a hamstring concern and was not involved with England's under-21 squad during the break.

Hellberg said: "He trained today. It's the first training with him and that's all I know. We'll have to see how he goes from there and how much he can play in the games and how much we balance that."

After hosting Bristol City, Boro welcome Swansea City next Tuesday before visiting Southampton next Friday.

Meanwhile, Hellberg has reiterated that the £10m signing remains fully committed to the club - amid speculation linking him with a January move to the Premier League.

Hellberg continued: "I am not worried about him being distracted by that. Of course, there will always be interest from clubs for a striker with what he has done.

"But I think he is very committed with the work he's done here and I think his only aim is to finish as high as possible in the table and score as many goals as possible here for this year.

"That's what I am thinking about. As a coach, you always have those (other) things. You cannot go around and think 'this will happen' and 'that will happen'. You can see his commitment to try and do everything for Middlesbrough.

"He's been handling every question asked of him since the first day in a brilliant way, and nothing has affected him."
