Mikel Arteta confirms Arsenal plan to combat drones after Manchester United incident

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal are alert to drones flying over the club’s training ground after footage of Mauricio Pochettino’s first session at Manchester United was captured.

The drone operator involved in that incident at Carrington has since apologised, but not before 12 minutes of the training session was filmed and then uploaded publicly on YouTube.

The United players and staff were clearly visible, as was the shape they were working on ahead of their trip to face Liverpool this weekend.

Arsenal have had a run-in with a drone themselves this season, when their Carabao Cup clash with Fleetwood last month was delayed for eight minutes as the authorities worked to locate the operator.

Asked about the latest incident at United, Arteta said: “Well, very delicate, obviously.

“We had an episode as well against Fleetwood; you see what is happening around the world as well. So I don’t know what the solution is.

“Obviously, the technology is growing, the ability to get into systems is growing at a really rapid rate. We need to be very aware of that, but I cannot tell you what the solution is to that.”

Clubs regularly use their own drones to film training sessions and later review footage.

Arteta confirmed that Arsenal have people in place to ensure there are no outside sources watching their sessions and that the team are protected, but he emphasised that was well beyond his list of responsibilities.

“They are looking after that constantly just in case it happens, but obviously what happened yesterday [at United], it’s a very serious matter,” Arteta said.

“As I said, I’m not a specialist, I don’t have a solution, I don’t have a clue. We have people that are in charge of that.

“There are authorities there now to try to prevent that, and that’s the only thing that we can do. Collaborate with them and try to avoid it.”
