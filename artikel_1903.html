'They do not deserve this' - BBC pundit on Hull's late victory over Brighton

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Hull scored twice in stoppage time to secure a 2-0 Premier League win at the MKM Stadium on Sunday (November 22), leaving high-flying Brighton stunned.

Oli McBurnie broke the deadlock in the 92nd minute before completing his brace three minutes later, with the hosts taking full advantage of Brighton being reduced to ten men following Costinha's 89th-minute dismissal.

Morrison's reaction to Hull's opening goal on BBC Radio 5 Live captured the drama of the occasion.

The former striker suggested the Tigers had not necessarily done enough to deserve the breakthrough, but said that would not matter to the home supporters.

He said: "This is why you've got to love this beautiful game.

"They do not deserve this. But who cares if they deserve it?

"They have a man in Oli McBurnie who can find the net at times and this could be a big goal for Hull City.

Albion dominated possession throughout the match and had multiple chances to open the scoring. Diego Gomez could have had a hat-trick and the usually efficient Pascal Gross was also wasteful.

Costinha also had a big opportunity to put the visitors ahead early on, with the red card compounding a poor afternoon for the Portuguese full-back.

Hull took full advantage and found a way through at the death.

With time running out, Regan Slater played in Lucas Gourna-Douath – who squared it to Oli McBurnie. The veteran made no mistake, despite Ferdi Kadioglu’s last ditch attempt to save his team.

And there was more to come, with McBurnie tapping home a second in the 95th minute after Mohamed Belloumi beat Maxim De Cuyper and played the ball across goal.

What had looked like a frustrating afternoon for Hull turned into a memorable victory, while Brighton were left to reflect on a costly late sending-off and a dramatic collapse.

Morrison said: “The Brighton players will be disappointed. They were by far the better team. The Hull goalkeeper produced saves like that and had a brilliant game.

“Brighton's finishing wasn't good enough. As much as Konstantinos Tzolakis had a brilliant afternoon, I think Brighton could've done better in front of goal. But credit to Hull.

"No team is going to go toe to toe with Brighton and you have to show them respect and that's what Hull did. They stayed in the game.

"They needed their goalkeeper to have a good game and the defenders to defend well. And when you go and smell danger and smell blood you have to go and take it.

“Once Brighton went down to ten men this place lifted. The fans got behind and they knew this was their opportunity and they grabbed it with both hands.”

The Seagulls remain second in the table but missed a chance to move within three points of leaders Arsenal. Hull, meanwhile, moved up to 11th place.
