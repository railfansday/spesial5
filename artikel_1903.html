Sources: Coventry's chances of selling Jack Rudoni in January revealed amid Newcastle and Leeds interest

Newcastle United and Leeds United are among a number of clubs tracking Coventry City midfielder Jack Rudoni, sources have told Football Insider.

The 25-year-old will have just one year remaining on his contract at the CBS Arena next summer, with an agreement over a new deal yet to be found.

The London-born talent has adapted well to the Premier League, netting three goals in his last five league games as Frank Lampard's side have stabilised after a difficult start.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Rudoni's future will not be decided in the January transfer window.

A move next summer is more likely, although Coventry will continue to hold out hope of striking an agreement over a new deal for the midfielder.

Their chances will lie with their Premier League status, with relegation likely ending any hopes of keeping hold of the Englishman.

Football Insider revealed this week that Coventry are confident of keeping Rudoni at the club if they stay up, but the likes of Newcastle will be ready to pounce if they do not.

The 25-year-old is of particular interest to the Magpies, with space set to be opening up in Matthias Jaissle's midfield next summer.

Joe Willock's contract is due to expire, and Joelinton could join him in leaving the club with his own deal having entered its final two years.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "If they don’t stay up and they go down there’ll be a number of clubs, not just Newcastle, who would be interested in Rudoni.

"There’s been talk of Leeds interested and there’s interest from abroad as well in Rudoni as well. But look, Coventry aren't even contemplating losing such a key player like Rudoni in January.

"He will be key for Frank Lampard's side as they look to try and secure their Premier League survival as well. The interest is not surprising and obviously his contract, he has only got about 18 months left on his current deal as well.

"That’s why clubs will be sitting up and taking notice of Jack Rudoni as well. But Coventry won’t be entertaining any offers for him for sure."

Despite Coventry's struggles in the Premier League this season, Rudoni has enjoyed a rapid adaptation.

Remarkably, the midfielder is averaging 2.08 chances created, 1.95 successful crosses and 1.10 successful dribbles per game.

He also wins 4.88 duels per game and ranks in the 80th percentile for defensive contributions among Premier League midfielders, making 8.30 per game.

While it is unclear if Coventry will survive the drop this season, Rudoni looks certain to remain in the Premier League.
