Barcelona respond as Alejandro Balde pushes for Man Utd transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Barcelona have confirmed they have no plans to sell Alejandro Balde and insist the Manchester United-linked left-back remains an ‘important player’ for the club going forward.

Balde has been with Barca since the age of just eight, making a total of 173 appearances in all competitions since breaking into the first-team squad back in 2021.

In that time, the 23-year-old has helped the Catalan giants win seven pieces of silverware, including three La Liga titles – the most recent coming under Hansi Flick last season.

However, Balde has become an increasingly peripheral figure at the Camp Nou in recent months, with all five of his appearances this term coming from the substitutes bench.

Balde currently finds himself behind both Joao Cancelo and Xavi Espart in the pecking order at Barca and the Spain international’s career appears to be at a crossroads.

According to Spanish publication Mundo Deportivo, this has forced Balde to consider his options and the full-back is understood to be keen on joining Mauricio Pochettino’s Manchester United in January.

Other reports suggest that ‘everything Balde has heard about his situation has come through press conferences’ and the defender views the Premier League as a possible escape route.

The subject of Balde’s future has proven a major talking point both in the media and amongst Barcelona’s fanbase – and the topic was raised during Catalunya Radio’s joint interview with club chiefs Bojan and Deco on Wednesday.

Asked about the possible sale of Balde this winter, football coordinator Bojan replied: ‘Balde is a fantastic lad.

‘Obviously, he’s in an uncomfortable situation. The player wants to play.

‘He’s one of our own and he’s still an important player. It’s true that he’s not playing right now, although he’s ready if the manager needs him.’

Deco, Barca’s sporting director, was similarly unequivocal when asked if a transfer deal could be on the cards for Balde.

‘No,’ the ex-Barca, Chelsea and Portugal responded.

‘There is also the player’s side, but he hasn’t told us anything about being unhappy, that’s not the case.’

Manchester United had been actively looking to strengthen at left-back in the summer in order to provide support and competition for Luke Shaw, the club’s only specialist in the position other than academy product Harry Amass.

The Red Devils ultimately failed to bolster their full-back options, though, which has come back to bite them already this season, with Shaw sidelined for the last month through injury.

Diogo Dalot was therefore forced to fill in as a makeshift left-back in the bleak final weeks of Michael Carrick’s tenure at the Theatre of Dreams.

It’s understood Lewis Hall had been identified as United’s number one left-back target ahead of the 2026/27 campaign, but Newcastle made it clear the England international would not be sold for anything other than an astronomical price.

Hall would go on to put pen to paper on a new long-term deal at St James’ Park, tying him to the Magpies until 2031.

Bournemouth’s Adrien Truffert, Fulham’s Antonee Robinson, RB Leipzig’s David Raum and Crystal Palace’s Tyrick Mitchell have also been linked in recent months.
