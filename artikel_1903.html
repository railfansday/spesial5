Enzo Maresca is unlikely to be sacked in the immediate future despite frustrations at Man City, sources have told Football Insider.

The boss has come under fire after a poor run of form of late, which has seen City win just one of their last seven games across all competitions in normal time.

Fans made their voices heard during the 1-1 draw against Fulham as they booed and jeered the players mid-game, calling for more intensity and urgency to their play.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Maresca is under no pressure internally as Man City aim to turn their form around.

City have now dropped six points behind Arsenal in the Premier League title race, further raising concerns among the fans, but the board at the Etihad are said to be unfazed.

Instead, they are focusing on backing Maresca to bring success to Man City this season, and are not panicking about the situation they have found themselves in of late.

Former Man United assistant manager Mick Brown told Football Insider on 25 November that "alarm bells" are ringing over Maresca's future at Man City due to their recent form.

Their latest result at the Etihad has set alarm bells ringing both amongst the supporters and behind the scenes, and questions are now being asked about his role.

However, sources say Man City are not overly concerned about Maresca's performances and believe he will get them back on track before too long.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I don't think he's under immediate pressure, not from anybody within the club anyway.

"Results haven't been great of late, they've had just one win in their last seven games across all competitions, so obviously that brings its own pressure with it.

"The fans are not happy, obviously with that 1-1 draw against Fulham and then the Champions League draw against Napoli midweek.

"It’s an unenviable job trying to replace Pep Guardiola but you've to remember he's just new into the job himself so it's going to take time for to get his methods across and his ideas.

"So yeah, I think there's a bit of a frustration from supporters, but no pressure from inside this City camp really."

Maresca's struggles and the frustrations among the Man City supporters have come as a result of a lack of intensity and energy amongst the players in his setup.

However, worse than that lack of intensity is their lack of defensive stability, which has left them open and raised concerns as a result of their poor structure at the back.

Brown told Football Insider on 26 November that Maresca has got to come down hard on his Man City players and instill some dicipline to their displays.

As things stand, there is a sense of freedom to City's style which can often lead to players, particularly defenders, being caught out of position and unable to get back.

Maresca has now told major changes will be needed on and off the pitch, with pressure growing on his position in the duout and fans already voicing their frustrations.
