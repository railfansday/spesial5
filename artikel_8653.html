Fidelity Charitable runs the Giving Account, a donor-advised fund that lets you take a tax deduction the year you donate, if you itemize, and recommend grants to charities over time. Its donors recommended $18.3 billion in grants in 2025, the most of any U.S. grantmaker by its own count, and the account suits givers who want to donate stock that has gained value or private company shares.

Givers who keep $16,700 or more in the account pay the standard 0.60%, and nothing extra to donate private shares or crypto. Anyone keeping a few thousand dollars there pays the $100 minimum, 2% on $5,000, while DAFgiving360 charges the same 0.60% with no minimum fee, which comes to $30 on that balance.
