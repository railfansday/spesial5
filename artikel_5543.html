AI CRM startup Dreamteam raised $53M to take on Salesforce and SAP

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Accel led the round for the year-old startup, which has hundreds of enterprise customers three months after its beta launch

Dreamteam AI closed a $53 million financing, with Accel in the lead, to develop a CRM platform that competes with software from Salesforce $CRM and SAP, according to Bloomberg.

Premji Invest, the firm of billionaire Azim Premji, and Together Fund also put in capital. Dreamteam said on Monday that the money will help scale its platform, according to Bloomberg.

The software logs details from emails, meetings and conversations, including what buyers want and which rivals they bring up, and then works through a group of AI agents, according to Bloomberg. The agents are self-directed programs that decide what to do and complete work with minimal oversight from people.

Incumbents including Salesforce, SAP and HubSpot have depended on sales reps to remember what was said and type the details into predefined fields, according to Bloomberg. Dreamteam aims to take over some of that work by automating it, reviewing the conversations salespeople have with buyers and pushing deals ahead.

“The CRM was supposed to give companies a complete picture of their customers, yet the richest parts of that picture have always lived outside the CRM,” said co-founder and CEO Anand Venkatraman, according to Bloomberg. “People can spend their time on relationships, judgment and closing deals.” Venkatraman has worked for Microsoft $MSFT, Salesforce and Akamai $AKAM Technologies.

Venkatraman, Ramesh Parthasarathy and Senthil Kanthaswamy founded the company in 2025, according to Bloomberg. It is headquartered in San Francisco, and its development team is based in Chennai.

Dreamteam gives every user a set of four pre-built AI colleagues, each with its own specialty, according to Bloomberg. Their jobs include researching and preparing for meetings, following up on commitments, flagging risks and keeping deals on track.

Within three months of its beta launch, the company has signed hundreds of enterprise customers across healthcare, manufacturing and consulting, according to Bloomberg. Dreamteam said it displaced HubSpot's CRM at Plex, a streaming platform; Salesforce at DMS Health Technologies, a medical technology provider; and Attio at Invideo, an AI video creation platform.

Salesforce has been building its own AI agent business. The company announced an expanded partnership with Anthropic in August and said combined annual recurring revenue from its Agentforce and Data Cloud AI products reached nearly $3.9 billion, up more than 210% from a year earlier.
