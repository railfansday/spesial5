Emirates expects at most 3 to 5 Boeing 777-9 widebodies next year

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Emirates will decide within two months whether to follow through on an order for 35 Boeing 787 planes, Tim Clark said

Emirates anticipates receiving only three to five Boeing $BA 777-9 jets next year, and that is the optimistic scenario, President Tim Clark said at a press event in Berlin on Tuesday, according to Bloomberg.

Emirates has purchased more of the 777-9, a reworked take on Boeing's 777 widebody, than any other airline. The airline forecast only a small number of the jets arriving in late 2027, with perhaps double that volume to follow a year later, according to Bloomberg.

Boeing said Monday it would trim part of the workforce at its commercial division. Clark called the move a "recognition" that Boeing cannot speed up output across its different programs as quickly as it hoped, and "therefore, it's pointless keeping people idle," according to Bloomberg.

Emirates requires the 777-9 as a successor for older jets, among them the Airbus A380, a model no longer being built. "As we move forward, we are desperately short of capacity," Clark said on Bloomberg Television. He said the supply chain "is not where it needs to be."

Clark is holding an order for 35 of the smaller Boeing 787 in reserve. He said he will decide within the next two months whether to follow through, depending on Boeing's progress with 777-9 handovers.

"If we decide to take them, I'm not sure Boeing would be able to deliver them to us until the early '30s," Clark said of the 787 models, according to Bloomberg. "They have such a logjam."

Boeing is eliminating 5% of the jobs in its commercial airplane unit as 737 Max production runs behind plan. The company has also had trouble raising 787 output from eight aircraft a month to 10.

The 777-9 has slipped by a minimum of six years from the date it was first meant to enter the market, according to Bloomberg. Deutsche Lufthansa, one of the jet's launch customers, is leaving it out of its plans for the summer timetable.
