Wayfair posted its strongest sales growth since the pandemic, beating expectations

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

U.S. revenue rose 11.4% to $3.0 billion, while international sales fell 2.3%, and net income was $62 million versus a loss a year earlier

Wayfair reported its strongest quarter of sales growth since the pandemic on Wednesday, as net sales in the third quarter climbed 9.7% over the prior year, reaching $3.42 billion.

Adjusted earnings per share came in at 93 cents, up from 70 cents a year earlier. Analysts had expected 79 cents a share and $3.38 billion in revenue, according to CNBC.

U.S. net revenue rose 11.4% to $3.05 billion, an increase of $312 million. International net revenue fell 2.3% to $380 million.

Wayfair posted net income of $62 million, or 45 cents a share, compared with a net loss of $99 million, or 76 cents a share, a year earlier, the company said in a filing with the SEC. Adjusted EBITDA was $236 million, for a margin of 6.9%.

Active customers stood at 22.2 million by Sept. 30, which is 4.7% more than the year-earlier tally. Orders delivered rose 12.2% to 11.0 million. Average order value fell to $311 from $317.

Free cash flow was $136 million, compared with $93 million a year earlier. Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments totaled $1.1 billion at the end of the quarter.

CEO Niraj Shah said in a statement that Wayfair had predicted it would gain U.S. market share more quickly than it has in a long while. "With U.S. revenue growing double digits against a roughly flat category here in Q3, we've done exactly that," Shah said. He said the adjusted EBITDA margin was the company's best third-quarter result since 2020.

Chief Financial Officer Kate Gulliver told CNBC that Wayfair has attracted more shoppers thanks to loyal repeat customers, its newly opened stores, a high-end furnishings line and a focus on affordability. She said Wayfair's discounting this year has matched last year's levels, and that it plans to take a comparable tack through the holidays and the final three months of the year.

"We're able to meet the customer all the way from the opening price point up to luxury," Gulliver told Bloomberg.
