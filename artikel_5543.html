Hosting Guests Is Reshaping What Americans Want in a Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Hosting friends and family may be doing more than filling the house. It’s also changing the way Americans think about what they want from their homes.

A new survey commissioned by KB Home found 58% of Americans say hosting guests has made them rethink what they want in their current or future home. The research, conducted by Talker Research, surveyed 2,000 U.S. adults in October.

Gathering spaces emerged as particularly important when respondents considered what makes a home comfortable for holiday hosting and special occasions. Open living areas led the list, selected by 35% of Americans, followed by outdoor entertaining spaces at 30%.

Kitchens also figured prominently. Twenty-four percent prioritized a spacious kitchen, while 21% selected an open kitchen. Another 21% pointed to a large or dedicated dining space.

“Hosting friends and family can highlight what people love about their homes and what they wish worked better,” says Gena Kirk, vice president of corporate design at KB Home. “From spacious living areas and open floor plans to finishes that reflect personal style, a KB home works for everyday life as well as special occasions.”

Having guests over also appears to sharpen homeowners’ perceptions of their existing spaces.

Nearly one-third of hosts, 31%, said entertaining makes them think about ways to make their homes more welcoming or comfortable, and 25% said it makes them want to declutter or clean more often.

The experience isn’t always positive. While 29% of hosts said entertaining makes them feel proud of their homes, 20% said it makes them self-conscious and 24% notice things they would like to change.

Shared spaces receive much of that scrutiny before guests arrive. Among hosts, 45% said they focus most on tidying the living room, followed by bathrooms at 35% and kitchens at 31%.

For builders, the findings reinforce the continued importance of flexible gathering spaces that can serve both everyday life and larger get-togethers. They also point to the role kitchens, outdoor areas, and living rooms play not simply as individual rooms but as interconnected entertaining spaces.

Talker Research conducted the online survey for KB Home between Oct. 9 and Oct. 13 among 2,000 U.S. adults with internet access.

Leah Draffen is a senior editor at Builder. She earned a B.A. in journalism and minors in business administration and sociology from Louisiana State University.
