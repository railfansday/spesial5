XTI Aerospace Successfully Completes Restructuring of Approximately $4.7 Million of Promissory Notes and Secures Waiver and Amendment of Revolving Credit Facility with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A.

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

FORT LAUDERDALE, Fla., Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- XTI Aerospace, Inc. (Nasdaq: XTIA) ("XTI Aerospace," "XTI," or the "Company"), parent company of Drone Nerds, LLC, ("Drone Nerds"), a leading drone solutions platform serving commercial, enterprise and government customers, today announced the successful completion of an agreement to restructure and amend the terms of certain promissory notes issued on November 10, 2025 in connection with the acquisitions of Drone Nerds, LLC and Anzu Robotics, LLC (the "Seller Notes").

In conjunction with the successful restructuring of the Seller Notes, the Company has also secured a cross-default waiver on its $20 million revolving credit facility with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A.

Under the terms of the restructuring of the Seller Notes, the parties mutually agreed to modify the repayment schedule and certain financial terms associated with the original promissory note. The revised structure extends the maturity date and establishes a repayment framework that provides additional financial flexibility as the Company continues to execute its business strategy.

Additional details on the restructuring of the Seller Notes and the credit facility waiver and amendment will be available upon the filing of the Company's Form 10Q for the period ended September 30, 2026, which will be filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission and available on the Company's website once filed.

XTI Aerospace, Inc. ("XTI") (Nasdaq: XTIA) is focused on building a leading platform for commercial and government drone adoption in the growing unmanned aircraft systems ("UAS") industry. Through its subsidiary, Drone Nerds, LLC, XTI supports enterprise and government customers in sectors including public safety, agriculture, construction, and energy across the drone program lifecycle, from hardware sourcing and deployment to training, service, repair, and the proprietary Always Flyingâ¢ program. Its established U.S. customer base and reputation as a trusted solutions provider position the company as a go-to-market partner for drone manufacturers and software developers seeking to scale in the domestic market.
