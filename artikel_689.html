Luca Guadagnino Says His ‘American Psycho’ Movie Not Moving Forward

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Scott Z. Burns penned a script for Lionsgate's new take on the Bret Easton Ellis novel, which was previously adapted into a 2000 film starring Christian Bale as Patrick Bateman.

Luca Guadagnino‘s adaptation of American Psycho is no longer moving forward.

While appearing on Deadline‘s Crew Call podcast with Anthony D’Alessandro, Guadagnino confirmed that his new adaptation of Bret Easton Ellis’ bestselling novel is no longer happening.

“Scott Z. Burns wrote a fantastic script. Unfortunately for me, as we speak, it’s not happening,” Guadagnino said.

The Call Me by Your Name filmmaker did not elaborate on why the project is no longer moving forward. Lionsgate did not respond to The Hollywood Reporter’s request for comment. The project was first announced in October 2024, with Frenesy Films producing. At the time, it was described as a new interpretation of Ellis’ novel rather than a remake of the original film.

“We are thrilled to add another elite filmmaker to our upcoming slate,” Lionsgate Motion Picture Group chair Adam Fogelson said in a statement at the time. “Luca is a brilliant artist, and the perfect visionary to create a whole new interpretation of this potent and classic IP.”

The original 2000 film starred Christian Bale as Patrick Bateman, an investment banker living a secret life as a serial killer. Its supporting cast included Jared Leto, Willem Dafoe, Chloë Sevigny and Reese Witherspoon.

When asked about the possibility of another actor taking on the role, Bale told THR in March, “Whoever wants to give it a shot, give it a pop. I loved making it with [director] Mary Harron so many years back, fantastic memories of it all. Bold choice of anyone to try to do a — I don’t know if they’re doing a remake or what, I don’t know anything else about it. But all the best to ’em; I like brave people.”

Up next, Guadagnino’s Artificial, starring Andrew Garfield, follows the rise of OpenAI and the 2023 firing and rehiring of CEO Sam Altman. The film is set to hit theaters on Christmas Day from Neon.
