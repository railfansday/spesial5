Report: Barcelona set price and now Liverpool deal can move forward

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Liverpool can move forward with a transfer after Barcelona named their price for the player. The ball is firmly in the Reds' court now.

Liverpool need a new left-back. That much is abundantly clear to everyone and it’s something that will inevitably happen in 2027 - it’s just a matter of when.

They might move in January, depending on how Andoni Iraola views the current squad. It’s clear he’s not enamoured with either Milos Kerkez or Kostas Tsimikas by the way he’s constantly rotating the pair.

Kerkez, though, is here for the long term. Liverpool invested in him and he’s still so young - there’s zero reason to write him off at this stage and we’re still confident that he’ll make it at Anfield.

However, Tsimikas’s contract expires next summer and we see no real chance that he gets a new one. Liverpool will have to find competition for Kerkez, both to give him a reason to keep pushing and to hedge the bets here.

Liverpool, after all, are far more likely to find an elite left-back if they’re investing in two, rather than going all-in on Kerkez.

Barcelona set Alex Balde priceBarcelona have reportedly set their price for Alejandro Balde. The full-back is wanted by several clubs across Europe, including Juventus. Corriere dello Sport reports on the back of that. They're saying that Barcelona are open to a loan deal, which may suit Juve, but would also sell permanently at €40m and above.

Corriere dello Sport reports on the back of that. They're saying that Barcelona are open to a loan deal, which may suit Juve, but would also sell permanently at €40m and above.

Liverpool are also interested in Balde. The player wasn't keen on leaving Catalonia back in the summer but things have reportedly changed. His agent, Jorge Mendes, has held tentative talks with the Reds, supposedly.

The door is now open, then. A deal with the Reds has room to move forward with Barcelona's price now known and the real question is whether Liverpool actually want to pay that.

It's essentially what they paid for Kerkez and the idea of having two left-backs worth over €40m does seem a bit mad. Especially when so many other areas of the squad require consideration and probably investment.

Balde, though, may be as sure of a thing as Liverpool can get at that price point. One thing's for sure - it'll tempt them.
