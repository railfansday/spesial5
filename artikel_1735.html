Arsenal face nervous wait for David Raya injury news after weekend scans

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

David Raya is believed to have had an MRI scan on a potential left shoulder injury on Sunday, as Arsenal wait nervously to see whether he can feature against Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday night.

It remains to be seen whether the Spaniard will feature as Arsenal stage an open training session on Monday morning. Mikel Arteta is also due to hold a press conference in the afternoon, where an update will be proffered.

Raya was forced off during Saturday’s 2-2 draw at Newcastle after colliding with William Osula following a corner.

The keeper required lengthy treatment on the pitch from Arsenal’s medical team, before the decision was made for him to come off.

Kepa Arrizabalaga played the final 20 minutes as Arsenal fought back to earn a point.

But the result was marred by an injury to who many consider to be an irreplaceable player.

Asked for an update after the game, Arteta confirmed that the decision had already been made for Raya to have an MRI scan on the problem area.

He told reporters: “Unfortunately, he had to come off, which is not good news.

“He will have an MRI assessment tomorrow, but at the moment I don't know.”

He added: “It was a knock, I don’t know how it happened and what the injury is at the moment. We have to wait and see tomorrow.

“We know how important David is for us. Tomorrow we will have more news.”

Arsenal will hope to have their first-choice keeper involved on Tuesday as they visit Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League. They could potentially end the week atop the league phase table with a win, though they would need a number of other results to go their way.
