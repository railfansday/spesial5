Chelsea: Joao Pedro and Moises Caicedo injury latest as Xabi Alonso issues three-man update

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Joao Pedro remains sidelined but Moises Caicedo is available as Chelsea prepare for Friday night’s trip to to Nottingham Forest.

The Brazilian has not played since last month’s 2-0 win at Everton due to a thigh injury, and his absence has seen Chelsea’s form take a hit.

Joao Pedro began this season in fine form, scoring five times and assisting a further three goals in the Blues’ opening six games of the campaign before injury struck.

Caicedo has endured an injury-hit start to the campaign, and was last week forced off during the first half of the 4-1 home defeat to Leeds. Alonso confirmed at the time that the midfielder was “in pain” and would need to be assessed before the trip to the City Ground.

Asked if Joao Pedro, who visited former club Watford on Wednesday night, was fit, Alonso simply replied: “No.”

On Caicedo, he said: “He is going to be okay for tomorrow.”

Alonso was more coy when pushed for an update on Maxence Lacroix, who it was reported this week could be return from injury before the end of the year.
