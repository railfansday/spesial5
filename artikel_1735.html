Nico Paz sends message to Manchester United after transfer interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Nico Paz is open to joining Manchester United next summer and does not favour a return to Real Madrid, according to reports.

The 22-year-old joined Madrid in 2016 but made only eight first-team appearances before he made the move to Como two years ago.

During his time in Italy, Paz has played a pivotal role in Como’s rise, scoring 25 goals and registering 25 assists in 91 appearances for the Serie A club.

Paz has also delivered several impressive performances in the Champions League this season, including a man-of-the-match display in Como’s 4-1 win over Manchester United.

Earlier this month, reports in Spain claimed that Real Madrid intend to activate their €80 million (£67m) buyback clause to re-sign Paz from Como next summer.

Madrid did have the option to re-sign Paz this year but the midfielder personally requested to stay at Como for another season.

‘I thought this was where I would grow the most. I think I made the right decision and I’m happy I did,’ Paz said in September.

However, The Athletic reports that Paz ‘does not currently favour re-joining Madrid’ and the midfielder has not yet been given any assurances over playing time if he does return.

The report adds that Paz is on United’s shortlist of candidates to replace Bruno Fernandes, whose contract expires next summer but the club have the option to extend it for a further year.
