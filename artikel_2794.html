Cavs at Thunder: How to watch, odds, and injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Cleveland Cavaliers won't have to wait long to avenge the Oklahoma City Thunder for a frustrating loss on Monday night. The Cavs are heading on the road to conclude their two-game season series with them on Wednesday evening.

The Cavs had chances to win on Monday, but they couldn't come down with a key rebound or hit a clutch shot when they needed it. The margin between winning and losing in the NBA is tight. Right now, the Cavs seem to find themselves on the wrong side of these margins against upper-echelon teams.

Both teams will be without several key players in this rematch. The Cavs are down much of their wing rotation as Peyton Watson, Jaylon Tyson, and Nae'Qwan Tomlin have been ruled out of this game. The Thunder will be getting Alex Caruso back for this matchup, but they will still be without Jaylen Williams, Chet Holmgren, Cason Wallace, and could be without Jaylin Williams as well.
