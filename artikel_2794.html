Nike Revs Up Formula 1 Comeback With 2027 Partnership Deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

After stepping away from Formula 1 in 2002, Nike is reentering the sport after much speculation.  This time, Nike will be the official apparel and uniform supplier of the motorsport organization beginning in 2027, the brand announced on Thursday and ahead of the Las Vegas Grand Prix. "United by a relentless drive to push boundaries," Nike and Formula 1 said the partnership is uniting two brands sitting at the intersection of sport, speed and culture. No confirmation was given about how long the "long-term partnership" was inked.  Nike will create capsule collections, featuring sportswear styles, spanning footwear, apparel and accessories alongside co-branded uniforms worn by Formula 1 staff at its global events. Moreover, the brand will create race-weekend experiences, activations and products for fans to engage with the sport trackside and beyond.  A first look at the partnership will be released Friday with a limited-edition Las Vegas Grand Prix and Nike capsule collection at the F1 Hub and at the Nike Las Vegas store.
