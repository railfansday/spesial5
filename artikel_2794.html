Seahawks sign OL Cordell Volson to their practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Seahawks have added a veteran guard to their practice squad.

They announced the signing of Cordell Volson on Thursday. They released safety AJ Finley in a corresponding move.

Volson was a 2022 fourth-round pick in Cincinnati and started 51 of the 53 games he played in his first three seasons. He missed 2025 with a shoulder injury and signed with the Titans this offseason, but failed to make the team out of camp. He also spent time on Green Bay’s practice squad in September.

Volson’s signing comes as the Seahawks wait for Quenton Nelson to make his return from knee surgery. Grey Zabel and Anthony Bradford are the current starters at the guard spots in Seattle.
