Tigers DFA Matt Vierling, Jace Jung, clear roster space for prospects

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

DETROIT – The Tigers made some significant alterations to their 40-man roster on Tuesday, Nov. 17.

To clear space for a trio of top prospects and veteran reliever Emilio Pagan, the club designated four players for assignment, including super utility man Matt Vierling, a vital piece of the Tigers' playoff run in 2024.

"It's really hard," president Scott Harris said earlier in the day Tuesday, before the moves were made official. "It sounds cliché, but it is a reality. It's getting harder and harder every year and these are good problems to have."

Besides Vierling, the Tigers also DFA'd infielder and former first-round pick Jace Jung, shortstop Trey Sweeney and left-handed pitcher Jake Miller. Sweeney and Miller have missed significant time with injuries.

The moves cleared roster space for infielders Max Anderson and Peyton Graham and catcher-first baseman Josue Briceno. The Tigers also officially announced the signing of Pagan to a one-year, $11.5 million deal.

"I don't know that we're always going to make the right decision with these," Harris said. "But the fact that it is really hard and that we're having these meetings and having passionate arguments on both sides of the aisle for every player at the bot/tom of our 40-man roster, it's a good sign of health for us as an organization."

It was assumed that Briceno, the team's fourth-ranked prospect by MLB Pipeline, and Anderson (No. 10), were going to be added to the roster to protect them from the Rule 5 draft.

Graham, a second-round pick in 2022, was more of a surprise, mostly because he just completed his first season in Double-A and the Tigers' system is deep in terms of middle infield talent.

"One thing that is a real strength for us, our roster is very flexible in terms of positional flexibility and (minor league) options," Harris said. "We have a lot of different ways to configure our team and we think that is going to lead to a much deeper group on both the position-player side and the pitcher side to atone for some of our shortcomings from last year."

The writing was on the wall for Vierling. After posting a career year and helping to spark the Tigers' late-season surge in 2024, he's battled injuries the last two years. He played in 144 games in 2024 but only 123 total the last two.

At age 30, he was entering his second arbitration year and per industry estimates was likely to get a deal worth between $3.5 million and $4 million. With the emergence of young outfielders Max Clark, Ben Malgeri, Brett Callahan, plus the expected return of injured players Parker Meadows and Wenceel Perez, Vierling was likely to be non-tendered before the deadline Friday.

Jung's offensive production hasn't carried over from Triple-A to the big leagues. In 61 games (160 plate appearances) over three seasons with the Tigers, he slashed .193/.319/.230 with a .548 OPS. In the meantime, he's been passed in the organization by infielders Kevin McGonigle, Hao-Yu Lee, John Peck and now Anderson and Graham.

"We're just a lot deeper as an organization," Harris said. "The fact that we have a lot of hard decisions at the bottom of our 40-man and also a lot of flexibility is going to help us moving forward. As hard as these decisions are, I wouldn't want it any other way."

Earlier on Tuesday, the Tigers announced that two veteran pitchers, right-hander Sawyer Gipson-Long and lefty Bailey Horn, both of whom were DFA'd earlier in the offseason, had resigned with the Tigers on minor league deals.
