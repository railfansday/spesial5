NFL Injury Report, Week 10: Latest news, updates for Puka Nacua, George Pickens, more players for fantasy football

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

It's the halfway point of the 2026 NFL season and by now you likely have weathered plenty of injuries on your fantasy football rosters. We have a handful of big names we're tracking going into Week 10 while four teams are on bye — the Bears, Broncos, Buccaneers and Eagles. Below we'll update you on the latest injury news, plus provide some useful fantasy football analysis.

Sam Darnold, SEA (quad/ribs) — Out for Week 10

Drew Lock, SEA (oblique) — Should be good to start

Deshaun Watson, CLE (concussion) — Set to start

Gardner Minshew, ARI (elbow) — On IR with torn UCL

Mahomes: At the time of Mahomes' injury in Week 9, it looked as though we were watching a QB who may be done for the season. But after exiting due to an elbow injury, Mahomes was able to return in the Chiefs' 35-12 win over the Jets on Sunday. Mahomes completed 23-of-31 passes for 242 yards and two scores, posting fewer than 20 fantasy points for the fifth time in the past six games.

Kenneth Walker III continues to be the focal point of the offense as the passing game lacks depth and a vertical threat. The Chiefs face the Falcons in Week 10 and Mahomes was a full practice participant Wednesday, a good sign for him being able to play. If he's able to go, it'll be a chance for him to work with new receiver Josh Downs, who came over in a trade with the Colts on Tuesday.

Seahawks QBs: Head coach Mike Macdonald already went on the radio Monday and said Darnold will be out Week 10 against the Raiders. Lock underwent tests on an oblique injury he suffered Sunday and those came back "good," so he's in line to start again. The Raiders have been a great fantasy matchup for QBs this season, allowing the fifth-most points to the position. However, the Seattle offense has struggled a bit with lack of depth behind Jaxon Smith-Njigba. Lock is back in the QB2 streaming convo for Sunday and isn't a bad spot start if you have Caleb Williams or Bo Nix on bye.

Minshew: The Cardinals QB is on IR with a torn UCL. That means rookie Carson Beck will start. If you remember back to the preseason, Beck was actually pretty good. There's a chance this is a good move overall for the offense, particularly TE Trey McBride. But Arizona is down WR Michael Wilson (neck) and Beck won't have many other options in the passing game. In Superflex, Beck is worth a speculative add to see how he does in Week 10 against the Rams.

Willis: The Dolphins surprised us before the trade deadline by placing Willis on IR, which could mean he's done for the season this late in the year. He'll miss at least the next four games, which would mean the soonest he could be back is Week 14 against Chicago. 2025 sixth-round pick Kyle McCord is the backup and will start in Week 10 versus the Colts. Brady Cook will be the backup.

Miami's passing offense enters Week 10 ranked 30th in the NFL. Chances are it doesn't get much better with McCord under center, but we'll need to see. It's a downgrade to receivers like Malik Washington, Greg Dulcich and Caleb Douglas in the short term until we assess things. Ollie Gordon II could be leaned on a bit more, and McCord may use the RB and Dulcich as safety valves.

TreVeyon Henderson, NE (illness — ruled out)

Josh Jacobs, GB (finger) — Report: Out a max of three weeks

Travis Etienne Jr., NO (hamstring) — Chance to return this week

Bhayshul Tuten, JAX (ankle/foot) — DNP on Wednesday

Etienne: This is one to watch going into the weekend. Etienne got in a limited practice on Thursday and will likely hold a questionable tag going into Sunday's game versus the Panthers. If Etienne is active, he could be on some sort of pitch count. But he could still leech some touches away from Audric Estime, who was a trending waiver-wire add this week. Alvin Kamara also got nine touches last week against the Browns. Estime likely leads the backfield with Etienne out. But if the veteran returns, it could water down the touches between the three backs.

Patriots RBs: Stevenson exited Sunday's blowout loss to the Packers early and did not return. Henderson has missed the past two games after being hospitalized due to "unexplained neurological symptoms." Stevenson missed practice Wednesday and was limited on Thursday, but will reportedly be sidelined multiple weeks.

The Patriots face the Lions in Germany this week, which means Corey Kiner is likely to take over as the lead back. He finished with a solid 8-55-1 rushing line against Green Bay, though most of that production was on a fourth-quarter drive when the game was essentially over. Lan Larison would be the RB2 for New England. Keep an eye on this as Stevenson getting back on the practice field Thursday is telling. Maybe there's a shot he's active. But if he's out, Kiner is a decent RB3/flex play.

Jacobs: On the other side of that game, Jacobs suffered a fractured finger in the win over the Patriots and didn't return. Rushing duties were then split between Pierre Strong Jr. and Kaleb Johnson with MarShawn Lloyd out. Johnson rushed for 68 yards on 14 carries with a score. He looked pretty good running the ball for a team that has struggled to do so all season. But it was also in a blowout. Even so, Johnson is a proactive add off the waiver wire with Jacobs expected to miss time. Green Bay faces the Vikings in Week 10 before the bye and it would be hard to trust any Packers RB against one of the best defenses in the NFL. Johnson will be a risky flex play against Minnesota in Week 10. Strong isn't a bad add in deeper PPR formats and keep an eye on Lloyd, who could be back soon.

Puka Nacua, LAR (knee/turf toe) — Placed on IR

Michael Wilson, ARI (neck) — Out for season

Luther Burden III, CHI (concussion) — on bye

Nacua: The wideout was placed on injured reserve Thursday, meaning he will miss at least the next four games with knee sprains and turf toe. The Rams are already without Davante Adams, but McVay did offer hope that the veteran WR could return from a ruptured hamstring as soon as Week 13. For now, there isn't much WR depth. Konata Mumpfield, Tutu Atwell and the trio of tight ends — Tyler Higbee, Colby Parkinson and Davis Allen — will need to step up. We'd throw Terrance Ferguson into that group, but he remains on IR and isn't expected back in Week 10. L.A. should lean more on its run game with Kyren Williams and Blake Corum.

With Nacua out, it's a downgrade to the passing game and Matthew Stafford's fantasy outlook. It's a slight bump up for Williams and Corum, both of whom see plenty of work in the passing game plenty. The Rams take on the Cardinals before their bye in Week 11, which is an easy draw for the run game. Mumpfield isn't a bad waiver-wire add, along with Atwell in deeper formats. But don't expect any receiver to get the type of volume Nacua did.

Michael Wilson: The WR will miss the rest of the season due to a neck injury. It's a tough blow for managers who were hoping for more following the Marvin Harrison Jr. trade. Instead, Wilson struggled, failing to reach double-digit fantasy points in four of the past five games. With Jalen Brooks and Kendrick Bourne on injured reserve, Arizona is very, very thin at WR.

Simi Fehoko, Reggie Virgil and Devin Duvernay all played significant snaps in Week 9 against Seattle. None of them did much, while Trey McBride was obviously the main target for Gardner Minshew. Though, Minshew is on IR with a torn UCL and the team will start rookie Carson Beck at QB this week.

Pickens: The Cowboys held Pickens out of their win over the Colts in Week 9. As a result, CeeDee Lamb went off for 10-195-1 while third-string WR Ryan Flournoy was quiet with two catches for 53 yards. Dallas leaned on the run game, leading most of the first half. It's clear the offense missed Pickens and will need him ready for a key NFC matchup with the Niners in Week 10. Pickens was a limited practice participant Wednesday. If Pickens is out again, Flournoy remains a flex option, while Lamb would remain a target hog and elite WR1.
