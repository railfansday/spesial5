Jon Jansen blasts Michigan leadership after failed Trev Alberts hire

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

You know it's bad when Jon Jansen speaks up about how rudderless Michigan is.

The All-American, team captain, and current radio color commentator and in-house podcast host is about as Mr. Michigan as you can get, reveling in all things maize and blue. But with the week that's been, with the decision being made not to hire Trev Alberts after a controversial statement in a Dec. 2025 Title IX deposition, Jansen is speaking up.

The decision to hire Alberts and to part ways with him were both made by incoming University of Michigan president Dr. C. Cybele Raver, according to multiple reports. And Jansen -- on his morning show on 97.1 The Ticket -- reacted to the situation his alma mater is in.

"Being a former student-athlete, now being a parent of a student-athlete, my experience at Michigan was life-changing because my coaches taught me about hard work, teamwork, integrity, and character," Jansen said. "And we have completely lost our way. Everybody is talking about this leadership and that leadership and this standard, all that stuff.

"We've completely lost our way. And to me, it's obvious right now that there are people making decisions for Michigan that don't know Michigan, that don't know the standard, that don't understand what it is supposed to be."

His co-host, Jim Costa, went further, saying that he believes that Kyle Whittingham will be retained now given the uncertainty at the athletic director position. While we here don't agree -- there was a reason to fast-track an AD post -- it's certainly more possible than it was just a week ago.

"Yeah, it's a clown show. It's an embarrassment," Costa said. "And you know what it also means? Whittingham's probably staying regardless of what happens on Saturday. They could lose by a hundred points to Ohio State. They don't have anybody in leadership to make a change. Part of bringing in Trev Alberts and the timing of it was you could get out in front. If a change was going to be made, you would do it Sunday or Monday, and then you could go through the process, but you're not going to have an AD in place.

"So by the time you get someone in place, the carousel is already going to have cycled, and you're going to have to be stuck with Whittingham regardless. I think Whittingham is safe now after we just spent a week talking about, 'Hey, legitimate change could be coming to Michigan.' I don't see it happening. And maybe you don't want change at this point because every move you make's wrong. Every decision they make is a laugher."
