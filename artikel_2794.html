UFC 334 live results, highlights, play-by-play | Gane vs. Hokit

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) is live TONIGHT (Sat., Nov. 14, 2026) with UFC 334, taking place inside the famous Madison Square Garden in New York City, NY, streaming exclusively live on Paramount+. UFC 334's main event is a big one: Ciryl Gane vs. Josh Hokit is the featured attraction for UFC's Heavyweight crown. The co-main is a big deal, too, with Kayla Harrison vs. Amanda Nunes for UFC's women's Bantamweight belt.

The Josh Hokit experiment reaches its zenith tonight (heaven help us all) with his chance to take UFC's most valuable title from Ciryl Gane. Hokit is only 4-0 in UFC, but his wild brawl with Curtis Blaydes coupled with his demolition of Derrick Lewis on the White House lawn have landed him in MSG. The lack of depth at Heavyweight (and fresh match-ups), along with Hokit's odious persona, have also contributed to this moment.

Gane won the interim title after drubbing Alex Pereira at UFC White House. That title became legit when Tom Aspinall vacated his belt because of long-standing eye issue caused by Gane (knuckle deep!). Gane will attempt to defend a UFC belt for the first time in his career here and prove he's a deserving champ (and not just a dirty fighter).

There's history on the line in the co-main event with Amanda Nunes trying to replicate Georges St-Pierre, in stepping away from the sport at their peak and then returning a few years later and proving they are still the gold standard. Her American Top Team teammate, Kayla Harrison, will try and stop that. Harrison will be trying to show that, despite her lack of exposure in UFC, she deserves to be in the G.W.O.A.T. conversation.

Rounding out this particularly stacked main card is Caio Borralho vs. Yousri Belgaraoui, Uros Medic vs. Kevin Holland, and Bilal Hasan vs. Luis Gurule.

The featured 'Prelim' is the retirement fight of Stephen Thompson. Wonderboy draws Charles Radtke for his swansong. The Late 'Prelims' also have Drew Dober vs. Chris Duncan, Jim Miller vs. Terrance McKinney and Donte Johnson vs. Cesar Almeida.

The Early 'Prelims' have some bangers, too. They include Melquizael Costa vs. Jared Gordon, Bia Mesquita vs. Macy Chiasson, Juan Diaz vs. Alatengheili and Evan Elder vs. Samuel Sanches.

It's standard operating procedure tonight. Here's when the various portions of the latest numbered event goes live:

UFC 334's Early "Prelims" will begin at 5 p.m. ET.

UFC 334's Late "Prelims" are scheduled to start at 7 p.m. ET.

UFC 334's PLE main card goes live at 9 p.m. ET.

All of UFC 334 airs on Paramount+, which you can stream here.

Round 1: Medic lands a punch of Holland's shoulder and then goes for a high kick. He misses and hits the deck. He gets up quickly and Holland is talking to him. Holland throws up some front kicks. Medic comes in and cracks him with a left hook. Holland pumps his jab and flaps his gums again. Medic falls on another kick attempt. Holland lands an uppercut shortly after. Holland leans out the way of a Medic straight and then fires back, knocking Medic down. Medic gets up, but Holland throws him down and then jumps on his neck. He goes for a guillotine, but then switches it to a D'arce. He takes Medic down and there's a minute left. Medic is able to gut it out, but Holland gets it again as Medic tries to stand. Medic is again holding on and is able to get out of it.

Round 2: Holland catches a kick to the cup. Holland takes about a minute before saying he's good to go. He's smiling and talking to Medic. Not a lot of action here, just single shots and nothing landing hard. Medic gets close and throws a combo, but Holland is able to block most of it. Holland blocks a head kick. They get close and Medic lands another kick to the cup. This time Holland takes his sweet time. Marc Goddard deducts a point from Medic and we're back on. Holland goes for a takedown, gets on Medic's back, Medic rolls and somehow ends up standing again. Holland grabs him and puts him on the fence.

Round 3: Medic catches Holland with a short counter. Holland wraps him up, possibly to get some recovery time. Holland puts Medic on the fence. He uses some knees and shoulder strikes. Medic is finding Holland's size really tough to deal with the in the clinch. Goddard breaks the clinch. They trade body kicks. Medic lands some nice shots again, but Holland is able to roll with them. Medic lands another good shot, a straight left. But Holland is able to slide back as it lands. Holland level changes right into a kick attempt and gets a shin right on his ear. He's wobbled, but Medic is very hesitant. medic tries to get the finish, but there's not enough time.

Gurule is beating him up.

Stephen Thompson vs. Charles Radtke: Wonderboy gets his walk-off moment! Radtke seemed like he had a chance to out-wrestle Thompson in the first round. He got tired quickly, though. In the second round Thompson was able to get into a groove and started to chip away with solid single shots. He almost finished Radtke at the end of the second. In the third he put him away with a spinning heel kick, making maybe the best highlight of his career.

Official decision: Stephen Thompson def. Charles Radtke by KO (spinning hook kick), round 3 (2:54)

Drew Dober vs. Chris Duncan: Duncan kept Dober at leg's length for the first two rounds with constant head and body kicks. Dober blocked most, but they succeeded in preventing Dober from blitzing forwards and starting a war. In the third round Duncan slowed down and Dober tried to get a finish, but after hurting Duncan late he gave up a takedown and Duncan was able to ride out the fight in top position.

Official decision: Chris Duncan def. Drew Dober by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27. 29-28)

Jim Miller vs. Terrance McKinney: McKinney got to work quickly, of course. He hurt Miller with his jab, ripped a kick to the body and then landed a flush left on the chin that put Miller down. McKinney pounced with ground and pound for the win. Miller was able to tag McKinney with some counters, but he didn't have the power to stop McKinney doing what he does best.

Official decision: Terrance McKinney def. Jim Miller by KO (punches), round 1 (1:53)

Cesar Almeida vs. Donte Johnson: Johnson was very aggressive in this fight, winging punches at Almeida. They mostly found his shoulders and forearms, though. After blocking all that, Almeida landed right on the button, with a perfect straight punch, and dropped Johnson. Johnson survived the follow ups and got back to his feet. Johnson tried to fight fire with fire, but Almeida was able to sting Johnson again and again until the ref had seen enough.

Official decision: Cesar Almeida def. Donte Johnson by TKO (punches), round 1 (3:20)

Melquizael Costa vs. Jared Gordon: Costa lit up Gordon with kicks from all directions in the first. In the second he ended up on the bottom and then threatened Gordon with a deep triangle attempt. Gordon survived and Costa seemed to gas out from the effort. Still, Costa did enough to survive and bank the decision victory.

Official decision: Melquizael Costa def. Jared Gordon by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28)

Bia Mesquita vs. Macy Chiasson: Total domination from Mesquita. She got a body lock takedown and then toyed with Chiasson on the ground. She hurt her with ground and pound, flattened her out and then put the rear naked choke in for yet another submission win. Look out Kayla/Amanda.

Official decision: Bia Mesquita def. Macy Chiasson by submission (rear naked choke), round 1 (3:22)

Juan Diaz vs. Alatengheili: Diaz had so much success boxing with Alatengheili that he didn't need to use his grappling. He just kept clubbing the Alatengheili with two shot combos, landing quick and slipping out before he get receive any payback. He basically tripled up Alatengheili on sig. strikes. The only thing that cost him a finish was how tough Alatengheili proved to be.

Official decision: Juan Diaz def. Alatengheili by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 30-27)

Evan Elder vs. Samuel Sanches: Elder hurt Sanches early in the fight and also showed off a superior ground game. The youngster Sanches showed some nice striking in return, though. He wobbled Elder and also caused a massive cut. For all the good things Sanches did, though, Elder did more — in each round. Sanches was also deducted a point for fence grabbing.

Official decision: Evan Elder def. Samuel Sanches by unanimous decision (30-26, 30-26, 30-26)
