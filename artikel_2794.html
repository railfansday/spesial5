Brandon Jones listed as questionable; Brock Bowers also questionable

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Nothing changed for the Denver Broncos during the final day of practice. All but one player were full participants, with that lone exception being Brandon Jones who is still nursing a hamstring injury. Those can be annoyingly nagging to come back from, but he is listed as questionable. We'll see if he is a go or not against the Las Vegas Raiders.

Speaking of the Raiders, the big name on their questionable list is tight end Brock Bowers. When asked about the quad and ankle injury, Bowers said he was feeling 'alright' but when pressed on if he was optimistic about playing on Sunday he said, "I don't know. We still have some conversations to have."

That doesn't sound like a guy who is sure he'll be ready to go in two days, but I am sure we won't know for sure until right before the gameday inactives report comes out.
