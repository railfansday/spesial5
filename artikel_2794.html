Colts-Texans opening odds: Can the Colts keep it going?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Indianapolis Colts won. Let me repeat. The Indianapolis Colts won. That has been a phrase that hasn't been uttered much in 2026. One of the few wins this year came against this week's opponent in the Houston Texans. FanDuel Sportsbook isn't predicting a repeat as the Colts are 9.5-point underdogs. With that line, Thursday Night Football could be rough.

The over/under comes in at 43.5, and the money line is +440.

The Indianapolis Colts won, but that doesn't mean it looked good. Daniel Jones was atrocious, but luckily for him, the opponent was the Dolphins. Jones threw multiple interceptions, was sacked, and barely broke the 100-yard mark. It's getting out of hand at this point, but the Colts did find a way to win. At this moment, it will have to do.

Meanwhile, the Texans have been playing much better. They came out hot against Cleveland, going up 17-0 to start. These aren't your dad's Browns, though. They fought back, and C.J. Stroud failed to create further distance. They did retake the lead in the fourth but gave it up late. A comeback wasn't in the cards, so the Texans will be doing what they can to save their season on Thursday.

The Colts and Texans know each other well. The Jaguars are putting the division out of reach, but a win could put some wind in either team's sails. The Colts have to figure out the quarterback situation. It doesn't look like things are going to change, however. Let's hope the winning doesn't either.
