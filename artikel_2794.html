No close game this time as Eldorado routs Albuquerque High

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Eldorado Eagles finished business this time.

Two-and-a-half months after a memorable overtime tie game with Albuquerque High, No. 6 seed Eldorado rolled to a 29-0 lead at halftime and went on to defeat the 11th-seeded Bulldogs 50-35 on Saturday afternoon in the first round of the Class 6A prep football playoffs at Wilson Stadium.

Eldorado and Carlsbad, which defeated Hobbs in overtime, advanced to next week's quarterfinal round with victories on Saturday.

The Eagles (6-4-1) will face rival La Cueva on Friday at 7 p.m. at Wilson in the next round.

The seventh-seeded Cavemen (8-3), who won 42-35, are at No. 2 Volcano Vista in the quarters on Saturday at 1 p.m. at Nusenda Community Stadium.

For Eldorado on Saturday, Jahari Morehead was a force on multiple fronts for the Eagles. He returned a kick for a touchdown, caught two touchdown passes, recorded an interception and also blocked a PAT.

"He was a freak," Eagles coach Charlie Dotson said.

At Ralph Bowyer Stadium in Carlsbad, Cavemen quarterback Marley Munoz, on the opening possession of OT, scored from the 1 on a fourth-down run for a 42-35 lead.

On the ensuing Hobbs' possession, Carlsbad senior Lennon Ananins intercepted Hobbs QB Junior Medrano to clinch the victory.

Hobbs (4-7) overcame a 35-20 deficit in the third quarter with a short touchdown pass by Medrano, and a TD run by Demarcus Hill, plus a 2-point conversion.

In Class 5A's first round, No. 9 Cibola and No. 10 Highland were dismissed from the tournament on Saturday.

At Ivan Head Stadium in Santa Fe, the eighth-seeded Demons (7-4), hosting a postseason game for the first time since 1985, defeated Cibola 23-7 with almost all the scoring in the first half.

In a 7-7 game, the Demons scored two unanswered touchdowns in the second quarter, with Manuel Pena throwing a touchdown pass midway through the period, and Tristan Ramirez returning a punt 64 yards for a score and a 21-7 halftime lead.

Santa Fe is at No. 1 Roswell in the quarterfinals on Friday.

At Wool Bowl in Roswell, No. 7 Goddard beat the Hornets 35-22 to advance to the next round against Piedra Vista.

The Rockets scored two touchdowns in the final 6½ minutes of the first half to take control, including a touchdown pass from Nate Vazquez to Noah Lujan with just over a minute to go in the second quarter. Lujan had earlier scored on a 3-yard run for a 14-8 Goddard lead.

Vazquez tossed another scoring pass in the third quarter for a 28-8 advantage.

William Anchondo was responsible for two touchdowns for Highland, one throwing, one rushing.

Valley, Valencia and Manzano won Class 4A first-round games on Saturday in the metro area.

The fifth-seeded Vikings (8-3) got down early to No 12 Albuquerque Academy but rallied to overwhelm the Chargers 62-20 at Milne Stadium.

Cole Rawlings and Estevan Cordova Romero both had pick-6 scores for the Valley defense. Zach Perea ran for a pair of touchdowns as the Vikings advanced to play at No. 4 Lovington next Saturday in the 4A quarterfinals.

Ethan Brady, Jonathan Silva and Romero added rushing touchdowns for the Vikings.

No. 8 seed Valencia (6-5) won one of the game's closest games of the weekend, 19-14 at home over ninth-seeded Espanola Valley.

Santino Mares' touchdown run for the Jaguars with about 8 minutes to go gave Valencia the lead, and the Jaguars secured the victory late with an interception by Santiago Perea in the final seconds.

Aiden Farless and Malachi McCoy also had touchdowns in the win for Valencia, which visits No. 1 Bloomfield next Saturday in the 4A quarterfinals.

In the final game of the weekend, the seventh-seeded Monarchs rallied to defeat No. 10 Bernalillo 39-24 in the second half of the doubleheader at Wilson Stadium.

Broden Price had two long touchdowns, one on a punt return, one on a carry, for Manzano, which overcame an early 10-0 deficit to the Spartans, a team they had just beaten by 30 points last week.

Price's 72-yard punt return for a TD put Manzano ahead for good at 18-17 in the third quarter.

Price later scored from 65 yards out, and a 32-24 lead. Vincent Varela added an insurance score, a 12-yard run, for the Monarchs.

Manzano will play No. 2 St. Pius in the quarterfinals at 6 p.m. Friday at Community Stadium.

In 3A Saturday, No. 5 Robertson, No. 6 Kirtland Central and No. 10 Hot Springs (which routed No. 7 West Las Vegas 34-7) had first-round victories on Saturday.

No. 6 Raton and No. 7 Tucumcari also were victorious in the 2A bracket Saturday.
