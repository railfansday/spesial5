NBC Uses Thanksgiving TV to Kickstart 2028 LA Olympics Marketing Push

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Olympic gold medalists Sydney Levrone and Rai Benjamin star in the spots, which sees the Olympic rings appearing in some of the city's most iconic locations.

It’s still 2026, but NBC Sports is using a stacked Thanksgiving TV lineup to tee up the 2028 summer Olympics, which will be held in Los Angeles, California.

The network is debuting a pair of spots on today’s (surprise rebroadcast of last year’s) Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade, and they will re-air on the National Dog Show Presented by Purina and in Thursday night’s national NFL game between the Kansas City Chiefs and Buffalo Bills.

The spots star Olympic track & field gold medalists Sydney Levrone and Rai Benjamin encountering enormous Olympic rings at famous L.A. locations, like in Griffith park overlooking the city, or on the coast at sunset.

“For LA28, the city itself is going to be one of the stars of the Games, and we wanted this creative to capture what it will mean to welcome the Olympics back to the United States,” said Jenny Storms, CMO of NBCUniversal Television & Streaming. “Sydney and Rai have delivered unforgettable moments for Team USA and seeing them discover the Rings in iconic Los Angeles settings is the perfect way to remind America that the Games are coming home. Thanksgiving is annually one of NBCUniversal’s biggest days and an unmatched platform to continue building excitement for the Olympics.”

NBC officially kicked off its 2028 Olympics marketing at the end of the 2026 winter games in Milan-Cortina in a spot featuring actress Kate Hudson and set to the tune of The Mamas & The Papas’ 1966 classic “California Dreamin.’”

The new spots build on that, teeing up what is sure to be aggressive 2027 push ahead of the 2028 games.
