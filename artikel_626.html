Olivia Rodrigo Shares Second Holiday Song ‘Miss This Christmas’ on Amazon Music

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The new track follows the pop star's debut holiday song, "Take Me Home (4 Christmas)."

Olivia Rodrigo is keeping the holiday spirit alive with another festive release.

Just weeks after making her Christmas debut with “Take Me Home (4 Christmas),” the 23-year-old pop star has returned with a second holiday offering, “Miss This Christmas,” exclusively through Amazon Music.

Rodrigo debuted “Miss This Christmas” live during her concert at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena on Monday (Nov. 23), introducing it to the crowd as a “really sad Christmas song” and noting that “no one’s ever really heard it before,” according to Rolling Stone.

The track was also included as the B-side on the vinyl edition of “Take Me Home (4 Christmas),” which arrived in early November. The holiday tune peaked at No. 32 on the Billboard Hot 100 in mid-November.

While “Take Me Home (4 Christmas)” leans into festive pop with a nostalgic feel, “Miss This Christmas” takes a more melancholy approach, exploring the heartache of missing someone during the holiday season.

“Happy Christmas to the lonely/ Broken hearts that feel just like me,” Rodrigo sings before adding, “Yeah, next year’s gonna look real different/ Tell your folks I’ll miss their Christmas.”

The new release arrives during a particularly busy stretch for Rodrigo, who earned seven nominations at the 2027 Grammy Awards, including album of the year for her third studio album, you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love.

“I am over the moon!!!!!” Rodrigo wrote on Instagram after the Grammy noms were announced. “I love this album so much and for it to be recognized in this way is the biggest honor.”

You seem pretty sad for a girl so in love also recently earned its fifth nonconsecutive week at No. 1 on the Billboard 200.
