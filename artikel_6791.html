Teyana Taylor Kicks Off the Holidays in True Maximalist Fashion

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

She attended the Burberry holiday party in fringe-trimmed fur

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

The temperatures are dropping, string lights are hanging from the trees, and Christmas music is taking over shopping malls, which means the holidays are just around the corner—and Teyana Taylor is getting in the spirit early. Showing up to the Mark Hotel in New York City, the actor celebrated the forthcoming season with Burberry, wearing a cozy statement coat from the house’s Fall/Winter 2026 collection.

The piece in question—the Linley wrap coat—put a winter twist on the classic Burberry trench. Crafted from brown shearling, it featured a long-length silhouette that mirrored that of its rainwear precursor, reinterpreting the signature style just in time for the label’s 170th anniversary this year. Where khaki-colored epaulettes and belted cuffs might typically sit, the outerwear featured deep-brown leather alternatives. And while the traditional trench’s edges would be hemmed, here, they were trimmed with fringe. The number was finished with a silk lining.

Underneath, Taylor wore leather pants, which she tucked into matching knee-high leather boots with a sculptural flared heel. She added rounded sunglasses, while pulling her curly hair into an updo. As for her glam, she went full-holiday mode with sparkly lipstick and zig-zag nails. She posed in front of an oversize Christmas tree covered in red ornaments, from tartan bows to berries.

At the Burberry event, the star—who recently celebrated her graduation from Auguste Escoffier School of Culinary Arts—was joined by sibling duo Apple and Moses Martin, The Summer I Turned Pretty’s Christopher Briney, Claudia Sulewski, Rob Lowe, and more friends of the house.
