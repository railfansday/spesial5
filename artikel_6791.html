All the Looks From ELLE’s 2026 Women in Hollywood Event

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

From this year’s honorees to rising stars, everyone showed up and showed out on the red carpet.

ELLE’s Women in Hollywood event has officially begun, with stars already starting to make their appearances on the red carpet at the Four Seasons Hotel in Beverly Hills to celebrate tonight’s honorees: Florence Pugh, Inde Navarrette, Julianne Moore, Rosalind Eleazar, Penélope Cruz, Elizabeth Debicki, Carrie Coon, and Samantha Morton. As some of the entertainment industry’s biggest names, these women know what it means to make a statement on and off the red carpet. Naturally, tonight was no exception—and they weren’t the only ones who set the tone for the occasion.

This year’s honorees were joined on the red carpet by their equally stylish peers, from Karen Pittman in a metallic hooded dress to Louisa Jacobson, who donned a fitted white jacket with trousers from Ralph Lauren. Not to mention, the men also showcased their sartorial chops, just take a look at Alexander Skarsgård’s colorful suiting ensemble. Keep scrolling to see all the red carpet looks from Hollywood’s brightest stars at ELLE’s 2026 Women in Hollywood celebration, presented by Ralph Lauren, Bulgari, and Evian.
