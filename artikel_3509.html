‘Mormon Wives: OC’ Cast Then vs. Now: See the Stars Before They Got Famous

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County fans have watched the cast change on and off screen before the series even debuted.

Season 1 of the hit reality series, which premiered in November 2026, served as a spinoff to The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives.

But while the OG series followed a group of Mormon mom influencers in Utah, the Orange County version introduced a new group of young mothers who navigate challenges in their personal lives and at times find themselves at odds with each other.

Keep scrolling to see where the Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County cast started out:

Before being cast on The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives, which premiered in 2024, Jen was a wedding videographer. She built a following on TikTok by documenting her life as a mom and wife to now-estranged husband Zac Affleck.

Aspyn has been sharing fashion, beauty and lifestyle content since 2011.

Bobbi started posting content on TikTok in 2021 before transitioning to podcasting and interviewing celebrities including Drake and Jessica Alba.

Before Mormon Wives: OC, Salomé’s focus has been on content creation on TikTok.

McCall likely looks familiar because she is related to Mayci Neeley from The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives.

Madison was a child model and has since become an entrepreneur. She is also the founder of the health food brand Wittles.

Ashleigh is an entrepreneur and founded the business Get Fit for Kids in 2021.

Avery was a pediatric nurse before shifting over to content creation — including starting a podcast.

Jordan joined the show after originally being a fitness instructor.
