Ridley Scott’s 157-Minute ‘Gladiator’ Replacement Officially Gets a Second Chance on Apple TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Based on the sci-fi novel of the same name by Peter Heller, there was plenty of hope heading into Ridley Scott's adaptation of The Dog Stars, as one of the genre's most creative minds took on an acclaimed story. Not only that, but he had assembled a strong cast for the adaptation, including Jacob Elordi, Josh Brolin, Margaret Qualley, and Guy Pearce. Alas, as has been the case with a lot of modern Scott movies, The Dog Stars was far from the hits of the director's old filmography.

Against a reported $110 million budget, the movie made less than $40 million in a disastrous theatrical run that stands as one of the year's worst. This was no doubt due to the film receiving such poor reviews from critics, including Collider's Gabrielle Moss, who said that it has "a little something to please everybody, but a whole that will likely only genuinely thrill a few." The Dog Stars is one of Scott's most disappointing movies of the past few years, but some would say his most underwhelming is the polarizing historical epic, Napoleon.

The story of Napoleon Bonaparte is no small tale, and Scott would've once been considered the best to tackle such an ambitious project. Alas, he and stars Joaquin Phoenix and Vanessa Kirby were unable to pull it off, with critics calling the film "flat," tedious," and "too long." Collider's Ross Bonaime was one of the few critics to offer a more positive reception, scoring the film 8/10 and saying that few of Scott's films "have had quite the level of size and ambition that Napoleon has." Three years later, Napoleon is a hit at home. At the time of writing, Scott's epic is one of the ten most-watched movies on Apple TV in the world.

Napoleon was far from the disaster of The Dog Stars, but it still massively underperformed at the box office. Made for a reported $200 million, the movie returned just under $220 million worldwide, split between $61 million in domestic revenue and a further $156 million from overseas markets. The film debuted second in the box office charts, dropping quickly to sixth in weekend #2.
