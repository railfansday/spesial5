Spotify to charge users to remix songs with AI, but it’ll pay the real artists more

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Spotify is reportedly set to launch a new paid add-on that will allow users to make remixes of songs with AI, and pay artists on top of it.

Like most platforms these days, Spotify has had to deal with AI-generated content being uploaded. A number of ‘new’ songs have been uploaded to the profiles of deceased artists, while AI artists have blurred the lines between what’s real and what’s not.

Now, according to The Financial Times, Spotify is considering the implementation of a paid add-on that will let users remix songs and make covers of popular tunes using AI.

As per the report, the new feature would come with an additional charge – around $4-$5 on top of your usual premium subscription – and will allow users to “create their own versions of songs on the platform.” Additionally, the report states that this feature could roll out in early 2027.

The FT noted that users could easily turn a country track into a more upbeat, dance tune with the new tool.

According to the report, these remixed songs could be uploaded to Spotify and added to different playlists, but all plays and royalties would be credited to the artist whose track has been used.

However, artists who don’t use AI near their songs would have the ability to opt out. Multiple bands have come out against the use of AI recently, including Fontaines DC, who were surprised when they noticed AI had been used in the music video for the song ‘Marianne.’

This tool has been in the works for quite some time, with the streaming service striking a deal with Universal Music back in May so that their tracks can be used.

Some AI-generated artists have also struck massive deals with record labels off the back of their popularity. Xania Monet, who is the AI project of a poet, Talisha Jones, signed for $3 million with one label.

Spotify has given users the ability to generate playlists with AI prompts in the past, but this new feature would be a big step.

It may also bring into question how the tracks would be labeled, seeing as Spotify now has verified tags for real artists versus AI-generated ones.
