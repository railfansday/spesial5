LEGO Dragon Ball designer reveals hidden Shenron summoning Easter egg

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The designer behind Lego’s first Dragon Ball set has revealed a hidden Easter egg that lets fans collect all seven Dragon Balls before bringing Shenron to life.

Collecting all seven Dragon Balls to summon a wish-granting dragon has been at the heart of Akira Toriyama’s series since its earliest adventures. While Lego’s Shenron & Goku set recreates that moment as a display piece, its designer also found a way to weave the anime’s iconic ritual into the building process.

Speaking to Dexerto, Lego Senior Model Designer Jae Won Lee revealed how a detail hidden inside the base lets builders follow in Goku’s footsteps.

According to Lee, seven orange 2×2 round bricks serve a dual purpose within the set’s base. They help support the model, but their number and placement also give the opening stages of the build a connection to the anime.

“As you build the base, you are essentially gathering all seven Dragon Balls in one place,” Lee explained. “Once the base is complete, you begin building Shenron, which symbolises the moment he is summoned. That’s the hidden Easter egg in this set.”

These hidden bricks are separate from the visible Dragon Balls, which use ball joint elements to keep them in proportion with Shenron. Choosing Shenron alongside Kid Goku was also a deliberate nod to the original Dragon Ball series, with the team looking to capture the adventure that introduced fans to Toriyama’s world.

“We wanted to capture Shenron’s majestic presence alongside Kid Goku’s innocence and adventurous spirit in a single model,” Lee said.

Rather than copying a single animation frame, the team drew inspiration from Shenron’s summoning scenes, particularly the way his body stretches into the sky and his commanding expression.

However, recreating the green dragon’s iconic appearance proved to be the trickiest part of the design process.

“The biggest challenge was recreating Shenron’s long, organic body using Lego elements,” said Lee. “Unlike many other dragons, the overall flow of his body is a defining part of his character. We needed to achieve natural-looking curves while maintaining structural stability and creating an appealing silhouette from different viewing angles.”

Getting there involved numerous mock-ups and a wide range of poses, as the team experimented with how high Shenron should rise, which direction his body should follow, and how he should interact with Goku.

When asked what his favorite part of the build is, Lee said it was Shenron’s face. “The face is where a character’s personality and emotion really come through. I love how a combination of small Lego elements and subtle changes in angles can create his distinctive, commanding expression. For me, the most satisfying moment is when you look at a collection of Lego bricks and suddenly see the character you remember from the anime.”

The Lego Shenron & Goku set is available to purchase now from Lego Stores and Lego’s website for $179.99.
