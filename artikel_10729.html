Wendy’s plans major Frosty and bacon overhaul after sales plunge

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Wendy’s is preparing to make major changes to its iconic Frosty and bacon as the fast-food chain looks to bring customers back following a prolonged sales slump.

The company has experienced seven consecutive quarters of declining same-restaurant sales, prompting a multi-year turnaround strategy aimed at improving food quality, attracting customers, and restoring profitability.

The changes are part of a new initiative called “Dave’s Way,” which Wendy’s unveiled on November 5 alongside its third-quarter financial results.

The strategy includes upgrades to several popular menu items, with a broader refresh planned for 2027.

As part of the overhaul, Wendy’s has begun testing thicker, crispier bacon and a richer, creamier version of its signature Frosty dessert.

The company is also experimenting with whole-leaf lettuce on sandwiches and improvements to its salads, while evaluating the removal of less popular menu items to simplify restaurant operations.

According to Fox News, the upgrades are intended to improve the quality and value of Wendy’s food as the chain attempts to reverse declining customer traffic.

The changes are still in testing, and Wendy’s has not announced specific nationwide release dates. However, improvements are expected to roll out as they become ready, leading to a refreshed core menu in 2027.

The menu overhaul is just one part of the company’s Dave’s Way strategy, which also focuses on marketing, restaurant operations, digital ordering, and improving restaurant profitability.

Wendy’s is additionally making breakfast service optional for US franchisees, allowing individual operators to determine whether serving breakfast makes financial sense for their locations.

The decision follows a difficult period for the company, which has struggled to maintain customer traffic amid growing competition in the fast-food industry.

According to Wendy’s official third-quarter earnings report, US same-restaurant sales declined 5.7% compared to the same period in 2025, while international same-restaurant sales fell 2.2%.

Customer traffic at US locations dropped 11.9% during the quarter, according to QSR, while operating profit fell 39.3% and net income declined 65.9% year over year.

The company has also been closing underperforming restaurants, with hundreds of US locations shuttered as part of its efforts to improve profitability.

Wendy’s CEO Bob Wright acknowledged the challenges facing the business while outlining the company’s plans to restore growth.

“We’re confident that we have the right strategy, execution and resources to restore Wendy’s to its full potential and drive meaningful shareholder value,” Wright said during the company’s November 5 earnings announcement.

Despite the declining sales, Wendy’s reported approximately $547 million in third-quarter revenue and remains profitable.

Speaking to The Wall Street Journal back in August, Wright said the company needed to rebuild its menu at the ingredient, item, and category levels, with a renewed focus on burgers, chicken, salads, and Frostys.

The upcoming changes to Wendy’s bacon and Frosty are among the first steps in that effort, with the company targeting a fully refreshed menu experience in 2027.
