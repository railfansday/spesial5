Why your favorite candy tastes different now

It’s candy—I mean, Halloween—season. But, I mean, let’s be real: The two are kind of interchangeable at this point. But have you ever bitten into your favorite candy bar only to think, “This really doesn’t taste the way it used to?”

Well, it turns out, you might be onto something. Candy companies really do sneakily change their recipes. Our taste buds also change as we get older. So what’s really going on here? Are candy companies changing their recipes or do we just taste things differently now? To find out, Popular Science’s Ask Us Anything podcast took a deep dive into the surprisingly complicated science of candy.
