The Blood Of Dawnwalker Just Got A Free 1.2 Update, Adding New Game Plus

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Blood of Dawnwalker is one of the year's biggest, most ambitious games, and the developers over at Rebel Wolves have done a good job cleaning up bugs and performance issues in the weeks since launch. Today, that trend continued; the new 1.2 update is out now on PS5, Xbox Series X/S, and PC, and it adds New Game Plus.

The new mode is uniquely ideal for a game like Dawnwalker. The narrative is very player-driven, whether it has to do with your literal dialogue options, your ability to manage your vampiric hunger, or the order you complete quests, and all of these elements can cause your experience to vary wildly. It makes sense, then, that players would want to jump back in to try things again and see what might happen if the game is played differently, and New Game Plus allows them to do that while keeping combat fresh.

According to the update notes on the Dawnwalker website, "NG+ unlocks after you complete the game’s Epilogue and lets you experience the story again while keeping your progress – mainly your character level, skills, and inventory." You don't gain access to these skills until you complete the prologue, however, which marks the adventure's proper start.

The new mode will also rebalance the world's level scaling. In the base game, certain areas are much harder than others, but in New Game Plus, all enemies will rise in power to match your level, meaning you can explore the world in whatever order you want. On that note, the level cap has also been raised to 100, and protagonist Coen can collect new, more powerful versions of weapons marked with an "NG+" label.

That's basically everything in this update. Rebel Wolves also added some slight tweaks to combat in the base game and adjusted some geometry in the open world, but the main focus here was New Game Plus. This is in contrast to the 1.1 update, which added a much wider suite of bug fixes and made the game run more smoothly.
