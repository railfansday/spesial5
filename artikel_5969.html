PETA’s Ridiculous Protest During the Rockettes’ <i>Christmas Spectacular</i>

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The animals in the production aren’t exploited. I know because I was a cast member.

A PETA protester holds up a sign reading “Take Cruelty Out of Christmas” as the Rockettes perform “The Twelve Days of Christmas” during the Christmas Spectacular at Radio City Music Hall in New York City.

The Christmas Spectacular Starring the Radio City Rockettes is famous for its merry dances and spirited kick line, but perhaps the simplest scene is the most memorable: A narrator describes the birth of Jesus while performers walk across the stage alongside sheep, a donkey, and a camel as a flying angel appears. Those brief moments where dynamic choreography ceases and the lavish special effects subside re-center the true story behind Christmas, serving as a good reminder during the holiday season that can so often be spent erroneously focusing on material extravagances or Santa mythology.

Since the nativity scene is a nice Christian thing that people of all ages enjoy, activists naturally want to ruin it. Affiliates of the People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals (PETA) organization disrupted a performance of the Christmas-themed show at Radio City Music Hall this week. A video shows one activist running onstage with a sign saying “TAKE CRUELTY OUT OF CHRISTMAS” during the “Twelve Days of Christmas” tap number. At least one protester also stood up in the audience during the show and chanted things like “No more animals on stage!” Some footage shows staff successfully wrangling the disruptive attendees. The president of PETA has defended the protest, saying, “Unlike the Rockettes, the camels, sheep, and donkeys exploited for the Christmas Spectacular didn’t sign up for bright lights, roaring crowds, and the chaos of the stage, which can be confusing and downright terrifying for them.”

Abigail Anthony is a staff writer at National Review.
