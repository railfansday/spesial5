Queen's Domain has, by my count, four categories of collectible you have to hoover up if you want to see its more hidden endings, and some of them are real bastards to find⁠—I'd resigned myself to queueing up guides on YouTube to get it all done.

Guides are great, you might still need them for some of the collectibles, and if there's one game that feels like it was designed with Google/Reddit/forum help in mind, it's Queen's Domain. But I was thrilled to discover that, very late in the game, Queen's Domain provides a waypoint tool to guide you to its hidden collectibles. It turned what I worried would be a tedious process into yet another example of this game's boundless capacity for surprise and discovery.

I know I would have appreciated the heads-up, the permission to not worry about finding them all earlier in my playtime. If that's how you felt, and you aren't looking for spoilers, great: You can stop reading here. Granted, the spoilers in Queen's Domain are so absurd and out there as to be incomprehensible to someone early on in a playthrough.

There are two requirements to get homing compass guidance in Queen's Domain: You need a fully kitted-out Mask of Second Sight, and to discover the hidden Petrified Garden zone. You can technically reach the latter from very early in the game, but you won't be able to open the necessary path until you acquire all of the lenses for the mask. Here's a quick checklist:

That's basically, like, the entire third act of the game and encompasses most of the requirements for Queen's Domain's second of four endings⁠—this is super lategame stuff.

You can reach the Petrified Garden by jumping off the teleporter platform while you're in the spacey void warping between zones. The large platform to the side you can dash to is a boss arena⁠—kill the familiar-looking guy here to reawaken the very high-level enemies in the garden.

From there, you can find four god seals scattered through the zone with the Mask of Second Sight. Breaking them all opens a trap door to a secret area below the garden. At the end of the path, you can talk to a mysterious entity behind a sealed gate who spells out the criteria for unlocking endings three and four: Finding all mementos and silver-etched skulls in the game.

They'll also, helpfully, provide a final duck to unlock new items at Umbada's shop. Among this last tranche of material, Umbada will now sell six charms that attach to the Mask of Second Sight. These charms reveal the locations of silver skulls, unopened treasure chests, treasure lizards, Gagi meals, data modules, and Orbewa eggs.

The respective tchotchkes appear as little holographic waypoints onscreen when you're within a few dozen meters of them and wearing the Mask of Second Sight. It can still take some sleuthing to reach the general proximity for these waypoints to appear, and there were some tricky placements that required me to think things through or circumnavigate the level and approach the waypoint from a different direction. But generally, the mask charms changed this collectathon portion of Queen's Domain into another fun part of the experience, rather than a completionist chore.
