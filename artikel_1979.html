Rustington FC enters new era as Tom Barnes steps down and Darren Prior takes charge

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Rustington Football Club have announced a major change in their management team, with successful First Team Manager Tom Barnes stepping down after three outstanding years at the helm and Darren Prior being appointed Interim First Team Manager until the end of the 2026/27 season.

Barnes leaves behind an incredible legacy at Rustington, having guided the club through one of the most successful periods in its history.

During his three-year reign, Barnes led the team to an impressive collection of silverware, including back-to-back Intermediate Cup triumphs, the Division Two Challenge Cup and a remarkable unbeaten league title-winning campaign.

His achievements have helped raise standards both on and off the pitch, with the club praising his professionalism, dedication and commitment to developing Rustington Football Club.

Barnes will take charge of his final match this Saturday before bringing his memorable spell as First Team Manager to an end.

With Barnes stepping aside, Rustington have moved quickly to ensure continuity by appointing current goalkeeping coach Darren Prior as interim manager.

Prior is no stranger to Sussex football, having previously represented Rimmer FC and East Preston FC as a goalkeeper before moving into coaching roles at East Preston and Horsham YMCA.

He later continued his coaching development with Clymping FC before joining Tom Barnes' coaching team at Rustington.

Prior has agreed to take the reins until the end of the 2026/27 campaign, with the club expressing confidence in his knowledge of the players, understanding of the dressing room and commitment to the club's ambitions.

In a further boost to the transition, Barnes has agreed to remain involved for the coming weeks, offering guidance and support as Prior settles into his new position.

The departure of Barnes marks the end of a hugely successful chapter for Rustington.

His trophy-winning achievements, unbeaten league campaign and influence throughout the club have cemented his place in Rustington's recent history.

The club paid tribute to their departing manager, thanking him for his tremendous hard work, commitment and passion over the past three seasons.

Attention now turns to Saturday's 7th November SCFL Division Two fixture against TD Shipley at the Woodlands Recreation Ground, with a 2pm kick-off.

The match will provide an opportunity for the Rustington faithful to show their appreciation for Barnes before Prior officially takes charge of first-team affairs.

For Prior, the challenge will be to build on the foundations established by his predecessor while continuing to push Rustington forward.

Taking over from a manager who has delivered so much success will be no easy task, but Prior's familiarity with the squad and coaching experience could prove crucial in maintaining momentum.

One thing is certain: Tom Barnes leaves with the gratitude and respect of the Rustington faithful, while Darren Prior begins a new chapter with the backing of the club.

A trophy-laden era comes to an end, but a new opportunity begins at Rustington Football Club.

Thank you, Tom Barnes. Welcome to the hot seat, Darren Prior.
