Google buys Howie, an AI assistant that books your meetings

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The scheduling agent was already built on Gmail and Google Calendar, and its team is joining Google Labs.

Google Labs has bought Howie, an AI scheduling tool that users copy into emails to arrange meetings, Bloomberg reported. Terms were not disclosed; PitchBook valued Howie at $14M in 2024. Google said it wants to build AI tools for small businesses with the team.

Google has bought Howie, an AI tool that schedules meetings and manages calendars, Bloomberg reported. Google Labs, the company’s experimental arm, announced the deal on Monday in a post on X. It did not share the price.

Howie works like a human assistant. Users copy it into an email thread, and it finds a time and books the meeting. PitchBook valued the startup at $14M in 2024, according to Bloomberg.

Google Labs said it wants to build “the next generation of intuitive AI tools” for small businesses with the Howie team.

Howie’s co-founder and CEO, Austin Petersmith, said the team will keep building the tool at Google. He noted on LinkedIn that Howie has always run on Gmail and Google Calendar. Google has not said whether Howie will be built into its own apps.

The deal adds to Google’s push into AI agents that act for users, not just answer questions. In May, it launched Gemini Spark, an always-on agent that takes tasks by email. In October, it gave workplace agents their own email addresses.

OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and xAI all offer rival agents, and OpenAI bought travel agent Odessia last week.

Google Labs has been busy. Last week it released Tabletop, a tool for building group planning apps with AI. In February, it bought music app ProducerAI, which runs on Google DeepMind’s Lyria model.
