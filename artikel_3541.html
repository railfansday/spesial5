Jenelle Evans Tearfully Hits Back at Claims She’s ‘Faking a Disability’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Teen Mom 2 alum Jenelle Evans has addressed social media claims that she’s pretending to be disabled.

“Saying I’m faking a disability and that I don’t like disabled people when I’ve actively been in a wheelchair since April? I am still disabled,” Evans said in an Instagram video from bed on Tuesday, November 24, before she became overwhelmed with emotion. “All I know is that I’m severely depressed. I cry almost every day because I cannot walk.”

Evans’ candid video was posted three hours after she recorded another message about a Louisiana plantation canceling her scheduled filming event allegedly due to her “haters” sending the organization complaints.

“I have a message for the plantation. I understand you have decided to pass on the filming project. What I take issue with is announcing that decision in a reply to a comment making serious accusations about me,” Evans said in her first video. (In the comment she was referring to, the organization wrote that it “made the decision not to move forward” while replying to a social media user who stated that Evans “hates the disabled and our veterans.”)

Evans argued to the plantation that responding with cancellation news “gives people the impression your organization is backing those claims.” She added, “I respect veterans, and as someone who uses a wheelchair, accessibility is personable to me.”

She later shared an Instagram Story that showed the plantation’s Instagram response in her DMs, which claimed that the “full concept” for Evans’ planned live shoot “did not align” with its mission statement. “Our decision was based solely on the show’s concept, independent of your participation or any public comments from you,” the response noted.

Evans’ ongoing health battle comes after an April injury that led to surgery on her left knee. In September, she took to social media to explain that her knee, and leg mobility, had not shown any signs of improvement.

“I was later diagnosed with CRPS. This is complex regional pain syndrome,” she told fans in her September 7 Instagram video. “The doctor that performed my surgery confirmed the CRPS and sent me to a pain management clinic. I went there about two weeks ago, and he tried giving me a steroid shot injection in my lower back, which wasn’t the best idea because I have been in excruciating pain ever since, which led me to go to the hospital.”

She continued at the time, “I am literally in pain from the base of my skull to my tailbone, and it is every single day, every day on top of my leg pain.”

Evans then recalled an MRI in 2022 that discovered a cyst filled with cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) in her back. A follow-up MRI amid her recent health issues revealed that the cyst has grown to nearly four millimeters and could lead to paralysis if it continues to expand.

After Tuesday’s two videos, she posted a final Instagram Story that read, “Taking a detox from social media.”
