Peak Design makes some of our favorite tech bags and EDC gear, and they’re up to 40% off

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

I’ll admit it – I write about Peak Design quite a bit. It’s solid gear, and it doesn’t go on sale often. The entire Peak Design website is running a 40% off Black Friday sale, and these are some of our top recommendations.

The Travel Backpack 20L is my favorite backpack of the year from the company. Peak Design finally put together a minimal bag that has the ability to expand and carry more than I need, but takes up the profile of a simple pack in every other circumstance. It’s completely replaced the Everyday Backpack for me when I don’t need to take a lot of gear somewhere.

Across several sizes, it takes on a few designs. But the 20L feels like the sweet spot to me. Of course, your mileage may vary. It has cool features like a hidden water bottle pocket, specialized organizers, and magnetic strap keepers with a pull-through slot for your roller bag.

Peak Design’s Travel Backpack is currently $159, down from $199 while it’s on sale.

I’ve come to love Peak Design’s first-ever roller bag – the Roller Pro. It’s definitely aimed at the professionals, with the right space for multiple Packing Cubes and pockets up top for not only a laptop, but accessories like any other bag from the company.

The best part is that even though the outer shell is 550D fabric, it feels incredibly solid and rolls like butter down terminals or on cobblestone. The main compartment opens in a clamshell motion, making it a little easier to get things out on the go.

Overall, it’s a wonderful bag – especially at $120 off.

The Outdoor Sling has been a go-to crossbody bag for a while. I’ve talked about it on end because I do love using it. It has the storage organization of a Tech Pouch with the comfort of a dedicated sling. Not to mention that it’s expandable, too.

I use the 7L model, but if I had to choose again, I’d buy the smaller 4L variant. The 7L version is a lot of bag, even if it doesn’t feel like it. You can even get a 2L version now! It starts at $47 during the ongoing sale.

Peak Design also just launched the City Sling 2L, which sits right in that minimal sweet spot for city travel. It’s a second pocket, but it carries a surprising amount. It’s $62 right now.

Honorable mention goes to the Travel Crossbody, which my wife has been using as her go-to bag for a good while now. It still looks brand-new. It’s $79 right now during Peak Design’s sale.

More recent than the City Sling, Peak Design launched several charging accessories. Obviously, they’re included in the Black Friday sale. It’s a good time to get a few bucks off what are, admittedly, pricy chargers and magnetic batteries.

The 2-in1 Mobile Charging Station is my favorite of the bunch. It charges two wireless devices using two magnetic coils. That means you can snap two Qi2 or MagSafe devices on it at the same time and hit Qi2 fast-charging speeds. Use it as a stand or as a charging pad. It’s more like a 4-in-1.

Peak Design’s 5,000mAh Kickstand Travel Battery is also on sale at $89. It meets the same Qi2 magnetic charging standards, and it has a rather slim profile. It’s another solid option.
