50 Cool Gifts to Get the Girl Who Has Everything (But Definitely Doesn't Have These)

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Consider this a list of things she didn't know she needed.

You’ve reached the point in your holiday gift-shopping journey when you have to figure out what to get the woman in your life who already has everything. If your mind feels like absolute mush and you genuinely have no idea where to start, don’t worry—that’s completely normal (trust us, we’ve been there). This list of unique gifts for the gal who seems to own it all already is here to save the day.

As someone who would consider herself that girl (come on, I review products and shop for a living!), I can tell you that thinking outside the box will always win me over. And it doesn’t have to be anything extravagant, either! Maybe it’s a monogrammed pouch, a cozy blanket, a cool journal, a beautiful claw clip, or something she’d never think to buy for herself. It’s all about finding the surprises that feel thoughtful, personal, and just unexpected. Ahead: 50 gift ideas she won’t see coming.

Look at you thinking ahead, you! Great question. To keep things short and sweet: It’s almost too late, but you can still beat the clock with a few methods. USPS recommends shipping by December 17 for its Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail services, December 18 for Priority Mail, and December 19 for Priority Mail Express. UPS and FedEx can get a package there in time for Christmas as long as you ship by December 23 at the very, very latest (and shell out some extra cash for overnight shipping). And then there’s always Amazon Prime: If you’re a member, you might be able to score two-day or even same-day shipping, depending on the item. But don’t wait!
