Shop Our Favorite Celebrity Looks of the Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Fall is finally here! If you’re wondering what to wear to embrace the drop in temperatures, we’re here to simplify matters with outfit ideas, courtesy of our favorite celebrities spotted on the streets. Consider this week’s edit your guide to everything from transitional fall looks to clever layering techniques, all infused with plenty of off-duty flair.

This week, celebrities embraced the seasonal chill, stepping out in their fall outerwear best. Jennifer Lawrence wore Auralee’s navy barn jacket for a walk in New York, styled with a graphic T-shirt, red pants, and a plaid button-down tied around her waist. Naomi Watts chose a double-breasted pinstripe blazer over a crisp white T-shirt and relaxed denim, finishing the look with playful accessories, including a floral brooch, an oversized-buckle belt, and a printed silk scarf. Both looks reinforced how a classic topper is truly the easiest way to pull together every fall outfit.

Meanwhile, guests at the CFDA Awards in New York offered a glimpse of what’s to come for occasion dressing as the holiday season approaches. Alexa Chung arrived in Tory Burch’s velvet robe dress and leather pumps in mustard tones, accessorizing with Elsa Peretti for Tiffany & Co. jewelry for a decadent finish. Hailey Bieber, too, embraced a gilded approach, leaning into the rich brocades and sumptuous embellishments seen on the spring 2027 runway. Her Saint Laurent skirt set, trimmed with faux-fur cuffs, was accented simply with strappy metallic heels for a touch of extra shine. Gold is having a moment, and there's no better time to embrace a little evening opulence for upcoming weddings, cocktail events, or festive celebrations.

Keep reading to discover these ideas and more, bringing timeless classics to life with a few standout finds, all reworked by the stars and completely shoppable.

Ahead, our favorite celeb outfits of the week.

Jennifer Lawrence was spotted wearing Auralee’s navy barn jacket for a walk in New York, styling it with her signature eclectic approach. Take cues from her layering technique, wearing it with a graphic Sesame Street T-shirt, baggy red pants, and a plaid button-down tied around the waist. Vans Authentic sneakers and oval rimless sunglasses rounded out the cool off-duty feel.

Alexa Chung arrived at the CFDA Awards in New York wearing Tory Burch’s mustard-toned velvet robe dress and leather pumps straight from the fall 2026 runway. Recreate the look with textural pieces in satin and velvet for a lesson in refined evening dressing. A black clutch bag and classic Elsa Peretti for Tiffany & Co. jewelry completed the look with a decadent touch.

Chloë Sevigny broke out her Fair Isle knitwear early, wearing a patterned sweater over a white polo shirt and denim midi skirt while out and about in New York. Tall leather boots with a Western-inspired spin, a slouchy hobo bag, and classic Wayfarer sunglasses gave the look a distinctly ‘70s-inspired aesthetic, with just the right amount of bohemian flair. Is it too early to start thinking about après-ski outfits? We think not.

Hailey Bieber brought a modern sensibility to old-world glamour at the CFDA Awards, leaning into the rich brocades and sumptuous embellishments seen at Anthony Vaccarello’s final spring 2027 show. She paired her Saint Laurent skirt set with strappy gold heels. We’d simply add a sleek clutch and delicate jewelry—a perfect festive look for any upcoming holiday cocktails or wedding celebrations.

While promoting the new season of All’s Fair, Naomi Watts stepped out in a double-breasted pinstripe blazer. The actress paired the jacket with a white T-shirt and relaxed denim, instead of the matching trousers, giving the tailored piece a more laid-back feel. When it came to accessories, however, she went all in, adding a floral brooch, an oversized-buckle belt, and a printed silk scarf. Here, more is more.

Rihanna made a case for easy monochrome dressing, wearing Phoebe Philo’s brown crewneck sweater with khaki trousers for a night out in Los Angeles. The trick is mixing complementary shades within the same color family. Minimalist pumps, oversized sunglasses, and understated jewelry—including diamond hoops and a metal choker necklace—take the look effortlessly from day to night.

Also on the press tour for All’s Fair, Sarah Paulson embraced maximalist dressing in a Michael Kors Collection look straight from the spring 2027 runway. Until it’s shoppable, recreate the idea with a striped rugby top, white button-down, and embellished satin skirt. Gray pumps lend an unexpected counterpoint, while diamond drop earrings and a ladylike top-handle bag round out the mix.
