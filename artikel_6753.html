Everything We Know About ‘The Body’ Season 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Need more of The Body? The Netflix series is off to an excellent start, with fresh new characters and spooky, sexy situations that really take some turns. Here’s what we know about whether or not The Body has been renewed for season 2.

Set at a Catholic high school in the so-called innocence of 2004, the first season of The Body is about two girls who can’t express their feelings for each other, an ambitious dance team heading to regionals, and a mass hysteria that leads to prophetic visions and Satanic rituals. Totes normal, right? Once you’ve sped through season 1, you’ll be on the edge of your seat waiting to see what happens next.

As of the season 1 drop, The Body has not yet been renewed for season 2. It’s still early, however, and the buzz around the show has been good. Fingers crossed!

The Body is an original idea from Quinn Shepherd, not based on any pre-existing IP. So what happens next is entirely up to her and her team of writers and producers. Luckily, the first season ends with a cliff-hanger and a lot of unanswered questions.

“We always knew that that was where the show was going, Shepherd said in a post-mortem interview with Deadline about *that* thing with Grace. “From the beginning, that was the arc of the first season, and I think a lot of that has to do with things we would love to do next season and how to keep carrying the story.”

Executive producer Gina Gammell also mentioned Anya confessing to Father Franklin, Maddie’s prophecy, and the mystery over whether Tyler and Lisbeth actually slept together as potential avenues for season 2.

Given that the show doesn’t have a lot of movie stars with busy schedules in major roles, and filming on season 1 took place in 2025, it stands to reason that The Body could easily return in late 2027. But sometimes Netflix shows take a little longer. So again... cross your fingers and do everything short of ritual sacrifice to ensure The Body comes back for season 2 as soon as possible.
