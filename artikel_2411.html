U20 Elite League: Germany U20 end 2026 with late draw against Czechia

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

HSV youngster Otto Stange scored his first goal of the season to help Germany U20 drew 2-2 with their Czech Republic counterparts. The 19-year-old converted a penalty-kick in the 86th minute after the hosts recovered from an early setback with Vojtech Hranos’ goals either side of the half-time break.

Hannes Wolf's side took the lead with Deniz Zeitler's first goal at this level before the half-hour mark. The 19-year-old striker made his Bundesliga debut for Paderborn before the international break against Stange's HSV. Stange’s late strike is also a first U20 international goal for the player who starred in last summer's U19 Euros.

Germany stay in second place behind England, who are facing Portugal, after their first draw in this season's friendly competition. The 2024/25 winners will play against Switzerland and holders Portugal in March 2027.
