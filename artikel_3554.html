The director of Home Alone made a surprising comment about whether the franchise should have another sequel only minutes before news broke that another film is being developed.

The legendary director Chris Columbus wrote or helmed beloved movies like Gremlins, The Goonies, Mrs. Doubtfire, and the first two Harry Potter adaptations, among others. Despite that, many people think of him first and foremost as the director of the best Home Alone movies, the original and its New York-based sequel. As a result, the director has a strong connection to the movie, making the timing of his latest statement even more fascinating.

On November 20, 2026, the news broke that a new Home Alone sequel, which will mark the return of Macaulay Culkin to the series, is in the works. On the same day, Variety reported that mere minutes prior to the world learning about that project, Columbus told the outlet why he believed that a true Home Alone sequel became impossible following the January 2026 death of Catherine O'Hara. Check out Columbus' comments below:

Catherine, as I’ve always said, is 50% of Home Alone. There’s the yin and yang of Home Alone: Catherine O’Hara and Macaulay Culkin. Without those two in the film, there is no film, because they create the emotional epicenter of that film.

Since Variety published its report about Columbus' comments after details about the potential sequel were revealed, the outlet reached out to the director for further comment. However, he declined to make any additional remarks on the subject.

According to information that was originally broken by Puck News, Culkin is set to return to the role of Home Alone's Kevin McCallister in a new film. The outlet reported that Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg have closed a deal to develop the movie with the beloved former child star attached. Rogen and Goldberg have previously worked together on a slew of successful movies. Some of their most popular titles include Superbad, Pineapple Express, This Is the End, Neighbors, 50/50, and The Disaster Artist.

Since it is one of the most iconic movies of its era, many people have been overjoyed anytime they have seen members of the cast of Home Alone reunite. Similarly, it has been heartwarming to see the film's former stars speak publicly about the genuine affection they still have for one another decades later. Given how strong those bonds have been and how closely Culkin and O'Hara are linked in the minds of moviegoers, Columbus' comments that a sequel without her doesn't feel right are very easy to understand.

Still, the cast of the last Home Alone sequel only featured one actor from the original film in a small role, Devin Ratray as Buzz, and the prospect of seeing Culkin as Kevin again is very exciting. Hopefully, the team behind the original film and moviegoers alike ultimately feel that the Home Alone sequel currently in the works does the franchise justice.
