Rockstar's new game is indeed "the pinnacle"

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

GTA 6 has already more than proven itself, standing as the highest-rated game of all time on Metacritic and – as per Sony – the "biggest launch in PlayStation history" to date.

It's not going unnoticed by developers outside of Rockstar, either. Emil Pagliarulo, longtime Bethesda developer – with Elder Scrolls gems like Skyrim and Fallout titles under his belt – stands out, taking to X to share his thoughts on the new Grand Theft Auto. "So many articles/posts by game devs saying GTA 6 is SO great," he starts, referencing all of the perfect scores (as our own GTA 6 review gives, too) out there. "Like, the pinnacle of video games! There will never be one better!" I mean, he's not exactly wrong – it's something we've heard a lot. Naughty Dog veteran and Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet world design director Michael Barclay recently said, "GTA 6 appears to me as a Bugatti Veyron would to a nervous Victorian child" – so, yeah. And, unsurprisingly, Pagliarulo would seem to agree with the (evidently popular) sentiment.

"Let me tell you something," he says. "I've been making games for 27 years, in the industry for 30. And that is… Absolutely true. It's pure sorcery." There you have it folks – GTA 6 is "pure sorcery," as per an RPG sorcerer himself. Comments, of course, see fellow fans of the latest Grand Theft Auto toast to that. "It's like playing a video game using a Time Machine to see what games will be like a decade from now," one reads. "It's truly unparalleled."

All of this does beg the question, though – what will future ambitious titles, like Cyberpunk 2, for example, look like in a post-GTA 6 world? Baldur's Gate 3 publishing lead Michael Douse previously said that "not every game needs to be GTA," but it's a "rare" cultural moment nonetheless… A cultural moment with an impact I doubt the world will forget for many, many years to come.
