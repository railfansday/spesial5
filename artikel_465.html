Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Ready or not, the holiday shopping season is underway. Choosing the best Christmas gifts for the ones you love is either an epic quest that you look forward to every year — or one you dread. Luckily, we spend our days testing all the latest gadgets, trending products, and gift-worthy gear, and we know exactly what to recommend.

For 2026, our Christmas wishlist is simple: it's all the awesome stuff we loved most this year, including the products we named Mashable Gift of the Year winners. That includes the newest gadgets, the swoonworthy items we just had to buy for ourselves, and some classic all-time Mashable favorites. With input from our own tech reporters, shopping editors, and product reviewers, every single Christmas gift idea we recommend is an item we use, love, or have given to others ourselves.

Whether you're searching for the best Christmas gifts for mom and dad, your sweetheart, cool teens, or stocking stuffers everyone will love, start with our Christmas gift guide. So, join us as we put on our Santa hats and share the very best Christmas gift ideas of the year.

Several Mashable reporters dramatically curbed their screen time with the help of the Brick, a pocket-sized gadget that locks certain apps on your phone. That physical element makes it way more effective than traditional screen-time apps. "My favorite use of Bricking my phone is before bed, as it ensures social media isn't the first thing I see when I wake up," said lead shopping reporter Samantha Mangino. Throw in a cute Brick Case while you're at it, too. Read our full review of the Brick.

First, the ultra-thin Nano MagSafe Power Bank attaches magnetically to compatible phones for convenient wireless charging. Then, there’s the Nano Smart Display Charger, which can fast-charge smartphones and let you know when it's done with a built-in smart display.

Senior shopping reporter Haley Henschel replaced her uncomfortable AirPods Pro 2 with them and never looked back. "Join me on the wired side," she wrote. "The sound quality is good enough [and] the cover charge is cheap."

Multiple Mashable staffers have one of these hanging on their keychains or purses' hardware.
