Manchester United add another Premier League defender to January transfer wish list

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Manchester United have added another experienced Premier League centre-half to their list of targets ahead of the transfer window opening.

After leaking four goals in another humbling defeat at Anfield on Sunday, Mauricio Pochettino has inherited the worst defence in any of Europe’s top five leagues.

Michael Carrick’s successor is said to be keen on adding a striker to his ranks to cover for the injured Benjamin Sesko, but tightening up his backline will also represent a priority.

Fulham’s Calvin Bassey emerged as a potential target last week, but the versatile defender is reported to be close to signing a contract extension with the London club.

According to talkSPORT, United have also identified Brentford’s Nathan Collins as a possible solution to a problem that has plagued successive managers.

The Red Devils also have maintained an interest in Everton’s Jarrad Branthwaite but he is likely to prove too expensive, given they are reported to have a budget of just £50million to work with.

Collins, 25, is under contract with Brentford until the summer of 2029 and would also command a significant fee.

While reinforcements would certainly aid United’s cause, former club captains Roy Keane and Gary Neville fear it is not just defenders their old side are in need of.

‘My worry for United’s defenders [is] they’re not getting great help,” said Keane after the Anfield drubbing.
