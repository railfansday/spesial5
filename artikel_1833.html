Barnsley FC player ratings: 'Relentless' star takes top mark in FA Cup rout of Aldershot Town

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Barnsley’s players rated following their FA Cup clash with Aldershot Town

Barnsley delivered a ruthless performance as they eased into the second round of the FA Cup at Aldershot Town’s expense.

There was a clear gulf in class between the third-tier hosts and fifth-tier visitors, who were operating at wildly contrasting levels.

Cameron McGeehan grabbed a hat-trick, Vimal Yoganathan notched a brace and Reyes Cleary and Rylan Brownlie found the target in the 7-2 win, while Aldershot’s consolations were scored by Annisuis Lewis.

Here are The Yorkshire Post’s player ratings from Oakwell.
