Arsenal receive positive Piero Hincapie injury update ahead of return

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Piero Hincapie has provided some good news for an Arsenal team that have struggled at the start of the season by confirming that he is now fully fit.

The defender has had two separate injury issues since joining Mikel Arteta’s squad but feels he is now over the setbacks and ready to play regularly.

Hincapie is playing for Ecuador in their November international fixtures. His inclusion in the squad was also a positive, as he was unable to play in their last camp due to injury.

Hincapie initially missed Ecuador’s September squad because of a groin problem. He had been named among the substitutes for Arsenal’s final match before that break, against Brighton.

After recovering, the 24-year-old returned to action in the 2-1 Premier League win over Leeds United on October 10. However, he suffered another setback during the warm-down after the match.

The ankle injury was particularly unfortunate, with Hincapie having to leave the pitch with assistance from goalkeeper Illan Meslier. He subsequently missed Arsenal’s matches against Lille and Nottingham Forest.

Mikel Arteta had at first suggested that Hincapie could be out for a matter of weeks, but the defender has since rejoined individual training.

He has now given a much more positive update on his injury whilst on international duty. He is now “100% fit” and does not have any niggles.

This is great news for Arteta. He has had to be cautious with his defensive choices throughout the campaign.

Hincapie will have a chance to get some much-needed game time while he is included in the squad to play Ecuador.

He has been included in the squad by Marcelo Gallardo to face Costa Rica (13th November) and Bolivia (17th November) in friendly games. This will be the first games Ecuador play at home under their new head coach.

Hincapie has also expressed his optimism for his initial discussions with Gallardo. The player is excited to work under the former River Plate manager and help the national team.

He should prove to be a valuable asset in the squad, considering he can play in both roles. He is a natural centre back but can also play as a left back.

Hincapie has shown that he is happy to play anywhere in the team his manager requires. This can be an important factor for Arsenal as the fixture list gets tougher.

He has only featured three times in all competitions this season and has yet to play in the Premier League. Consistent playing time will be key if he is to become a regular in Arteta’s plans.

However, his latest update shows that he is no longer struggling with the physical issues. If he can stay fit over the international break, he will provide another key defensive option for Arsenal in the coming weeks.
