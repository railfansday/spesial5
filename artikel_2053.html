Your chance to win Newcastle v Sunderland tickets and at the same time support a great cause.

Wor Flags are the ones giving you the chance to win.

They have announced that they have two Newcastle v Sunderland tickets as a prize, courtesy of Knox Hydration, the club’s main sponsor.

As a second prize, they also have a Newcastle United 2026-27 Home Shirt signed by the whole squad.

Every single penny raised will go to help Wor Flags fund their superb stadium displays.

How to win the Newcastle v Sunderland tickets:

Enter the Wor Flags raffle here, the details you will find there are

‘1st Place: 2x Tickets to Newcastle United vs Sunderland at St James’ Park (Sunday 6th December)

2nd Place: 2026/27 Newcastle United Home Shirt signed by the whole squad

Price: £5 per entry (Multiple entries allowed)

Wor Flags goal is straightforward – to create displays at St James’ Park that are worthy of this great club. We will achieve this in the following ways:

Hundreds of black and white hand-held waver flags

Larger custom design one and two poler waver flags

Large text banners featuring messages of support

On behalf of everybody who visits The Mag, we signed up in summer 2022 to make a modest regular monthly donation to help fund Wor Flags and that is ongoing.

If you would like to join us and make a personal individual contribution, then please go to Wor Flags, where you can set up a monthly donation (from as little as £1 per month and upwards) to Wor Flags, or a one-off contribution.
