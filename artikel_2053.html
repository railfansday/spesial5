Arsenal are plotting a move to sign Rangers sensation Kai Urban, sources have told Football Insider.

The young right-back, 15, is widely regarded as one of the Gers' outstanding academy talents coming through the Ibrox ranks.

That has prompted interest from elsewhere, with Arsenal now keen to take him to their famous Hale End academy setup.

Urban is an athletic right-back who has regularly represented Scotland at Under-15 level.

The young defender is building a growing reputation for himself within Scottish football circles, and he is beginning to garner attention south of the border.

Arsenal are hoping to snatch Urban away from his boyhood club, and feel that he could be a future Premier League player.

Rangers have brought countless young starlets through their academy ranks over the years, and the likes of Findlay Curtis and Bailey Rice have made impacts for their first team in recent years.

Urban is another starlet who the Ibrox outfit feel has the potential to go to the top in the future, but they are now at serious risk of losing him.

Arsenal are now lurking around the 15-year-old, as part of their attempt to snap up the best young talent from across the UK for their youth teams.

Urban has made significant progress at Rangers of late, and was one of three young Gers talent called up to represent Scotland at the U15 World Cup at the end of October.

His stock is continuing to rise within youth football, and Arsenal are now working to try and take him to England.

Former Tottenham scout and Norwegian football expert Bryan King told Football Insider earlier this week that Arsenal are genuinely in the running to sign Erling Haaland if Manchester City are relegated.

The former recruitment man is close to the Haaland family, and feels that an exit will be inevitable if City are hit with a relegation as punishment for their 114 financial charges.

King believes that Arsenal are part of a select group of clubs who could move to sign the prolific frontman, alongside Real Madrid, Barcelona and PSG.

However, he is also adamant that Haaland won't make any plans for a potential exit from the Etihad Stadium until the full outcome of the ongoing investigation is clear.
