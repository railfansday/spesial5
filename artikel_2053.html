Insight: The stats that prove Exequiel Palacios is the perfect fit next Liverpool midfielder

Liverpool should be seriously considering a move to bolster their midfield ranks by signing Ipswich Town ace Exequiel Palacios in January.

The Reds are in the market for a new centre-midfielder this winter, having faced issues in that area of the pitch so far this term.

The Merseysiders have had the likes of Monaco's Lamine Camara on their radar in recent months, but they should perhaps be looking a little closer to home.

Palacios has starred for Ipswich since joining from Bayer Leverkusen in the summer for an initial £19.3million, and has quickly proven his credentials at Premier League level.

The 28-year-old is performing exceptionally well in a struggling team at the moment, and his displays are sure to be attracting interest from elsewhere.

Liverpool are planning to add a new midfielder to their group, and Palacios could yet emerge as an unexpected solution for them.

The trio of Ryan Gravenberch, Alexis Mac Allister and Dominik Szoboszlai have been crucial components in Liverpool's team for several years now.

However, so far this season, Palacios is outperforming all three Liverpool stars.

His tally of 17.3 line-breaking passes per 90 is greater than every member of Liverpool's midfield group, while he also bests the trio with his 15.5 defensive contributions per 90.

Palacios also wins more tackles per 90 (2.85) than the trio, while only Gravenberch can beat his success rate when it comes to duels.

Overall, the stats prove that he would be a perfect fit for Andoni Iraola's high-octane style of play, which relies on quick transitions in possession.

Palacios' ability to break up play and play incisive, forward-thinking passes could make him an ideal candidate for the Reds, and he should not be overlooked on their list of targets this winter.

Football Insider revealed last week that there could potentially be a "willingness" from all parties for Camara to move to Liverpool in January.

The Senegal international has grown frustrated after Monaco pulled the plug on his move to Chelsea at the last minute in the summer.

Sources say that the French side still require a big sale in order to balance their books, with Camara a prime candidate at the moment.

That plays perfectly into Liverpool's hands, but it also shouldn't stop them from weighing up Palacios as an option.
