Liverpool: Ronald Araújo’s agent breaks silence on his future

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Loaned by FC Barcelona to Liverpool until the end of the season, Ronald Araújo could finally extend his adventure in England. Author of convincing performances since his arrival at the Reds, the 27-year-old Uruguayan defender seems to have found his best level and above all the pleasure of playing. A situation which could push Liverpool to consider a permanent transfer. Asked by WinWin Sportshis agent Eduardo Crnjar has rightly taken stock of the future of his player. “ Ronald is performing well, we are very satisfied with what he brings, but it is still too early to talk about his future “, he explained, before insisting on his development in England: “ He feels very good, he is happy to play for Liverpool and help the club and his teammates, as he has said before. » Statements which therefore leave all possibilities open regarding the rest of his career.

It must be said that the former Barcelona player quickly established himself as an important element in Liverpool’s defense. Mainly used as a right-back, Araújo has had a string of solid performances and notably shone on Wednesday during the qualifier against Chelsea in the Carabao Cup. Already voted man of the match against Brighton in the Premier League, the Uruguay international has also scored two goals in the Champions League this season, against LASK and Villarreal. His commitment and performances allowed him to quickly win the affection of Anfield supporters. Liverpool would now consider permanently recruiting Araújo if he maintains his current level. For the moment, no decision has been made, but the English club could well try to keep a player who seems to have found a new balance away from Catalonia.
