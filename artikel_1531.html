Roma involved in race for Rennes starlet

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Roma is looking to Ligue 1 to strengthen its left flank and has reportedly added Mahamadou Nagida, a young Cameroonian full-back currently owned by Rennes, to its radar. ]

Born in 2005, the full-back is making a name for himself in the French league thanks to performances that have attracted the attention of numerous European clubs.

At just 21 years old, the full-back is considered one of the most promising prospects in his position and could become a name to watch in the upcoming transfer window.

According to AfricaFoot.com, Roma isn’t the only Italian club interested in the player.

AC Milan, Inter Milan, and Juventus are also said to be monitoring the Cameroonian’s development, fueling particularly fierce competition.

Interest in Nagida has also reportedly spread beyond Serie A, involving top international clubs. Rennes could therefore find itself dealing with several requests for one of their most promising youngsters.
