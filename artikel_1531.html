Marco Materazzi’s disturbing parallel between Zidane’s headbutt and the death of Jesus de Miguel

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Twenty years after the 2006 World Cup final, Marco Materazzi has still not turned the page on his altercation with Zinédine Zidane. A few hours before the clash between Italy and France, this Thursday (8:45 p.m.) in the League of Nations, the former Italian defender returned to the famous headbutt received in the chest, drawing a parallel with the recent death of Jesus de Miguel. The Tenerife striker died on November 3 after a violent collision with opposing goalkeeper Jesus Owono. A tragedy which pushed Materazzi to wonder about the consequences that Zidane’s gesture in 2006 could have had. The death of Jesus de Miguel made me thank God that nothing serious happened. In the case of this goalkeeper, we are talking about involuntary homicide following a game action… What if that had happened to me? “, he confided to La Gazzetta dello Sport. A surprising comparison, as the circumstances of the two incidents are different. During the 2006 World Cup final, Zidane deliberately hit Materazzi after a verbal provocation from the Italian, causing his exclusion before the Nazionale’s victory on penalties. Last October, the former number 10 of the Blues returned to this episode by admitting that he was not proud of his gesture, without directly apologizing to his former opponent.

A position that Marco Materazzi continues to regret. “ In his place, I would have said: “I made a mistake, but I was provoked. Judge for yourselves what happened, I apologize.” He would have passed for a great lord, and Materazzi for the usual moron “, explained the former Inter Milan player. The Italian world champion also recounted an unexpected meeting with Zidane in November 2010, in Milan, on the eve of a match between AC Milan and Real Madrid. According to his account, he spontaneously went to shake hands with the Frenchman, who replied: “ Don’t worry, these things happen, it’s in the past. » Materazzi, however, regrets that Zidane then claimed to have taken him for a simple supporter. Despite this persistent dispute, the Italian does not hide his admiration for the coaching qualities of the former playmaker. I like everything about his approach. He knows the players, understands the champions, gives them space and creates empathy. It’s fundamental to winning “, he stressed. He particularly praised his ability to manage the big personalities of the Real Madrid locker room, citing Cristiano Ronaldo, Gareth Bale, Marcelo and Luka Modric, with whom Zidane won numerous trophies.
