Bayern Munich, Vincent Kompany: “I don’t want to minimize the facts, it was a shock”

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

On October 23, two days after the draw conceded in the Champions League against Arsenal (2-2), Bayern Munich announced the emergency hospitalization of its coach Vincent Kompany. The 40-year-old Belgian was attending reserve team training at the Bavarian club’s training center when he felt unwell. Victim of a dislocated shoulder in his fall, the former Manchester City defender ultimately remained away from the field for four weeks due to meningitis. This morning, he made his comeback in front of the media, on the eve of the reception in Cologne, as part of the 10th day of the Bundesliga.

“I am very grateful for the support received. It was an intense period. At the time, it was a unique experience. Looking back, everything worked out perfectly. I immediately received support from the club and the doctors. I am very grateful for that. We try to quickly regain our composure and wait for the results. Everything went well; all tests were positive. I must say that I am in great shape. We have had numerous tests, all of which were conclusive. In this moment of serenity, we also try to learn. I also found it fascinating to observe the team from another angle, to see how the players and staff work. Now I feel great again and I’m happy to be back with the team.”he declared, before explaining that he did not know how to listen to his body just after the C1 shock against the Gunners.

“Against Arsenal, I was transparent about what happened: I was simply sick and maybe I didn’t listen to my body enough. Everything that followed flowed from that. I don’t want to minimize the facts, it was a shock. But it was the consequence of my perseverance when I needed a break. Now I feel good, I’ve been through a long process since then. I hope the situation returns to normal as soon as possible. I am infinitely grateful for the reaction of the club and all the medical staff, who were there day and night. They went out of their way to help me. I would also like to thank my staff, who persevered and allowed us to draw strength during this difficult period. I loved the match against Atlético. It was one of the most memorable moments of my coaching career, even though I wasn’t there. We will remember this memory, and it will give us strength for the future. »

Finally, at a time when current football displays a very busy schedule and ever-increasing pressure, Kompany responded to doubts about his state of health. Is the Belgian sufficiently armed to withstand the shock at the head of a club like Bayern? For the main person concerned, what happened to him is not due to the status of the club he coaches. “I understand the questions that will arise. The truth is that we always tend to overthink when we don’t have all the information. We are undefeated and I took the longest vacation of my life. As a manager, I can’t say there is more pressure than at Burnley in the Championship. It can happen to anyone, and we can learn from it. » The message got through.
