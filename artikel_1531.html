Bayern Munich – Borussia Dortmund: the official line-ups

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Klassiker Day! Bayern Munich faces Borussia Dortmund at the Allianz Arena, a match of the 8th day of the Bundesliga. Currently leader of the standings, Bayern are in great form and still undefeated since the start of the season. Opposite, BVB is 2nd, but with the same number of points as their opponent of the day. A match at the top between the two greats of Germany, a meeting to follow live with commentary on the French Football Weekly website.

The Bavarians start in 4-2-3-1. Urbig in goal, Upamecano and Tah in central hinge, Davies and Laimer on the sides. Kimmich and Pavlovic are associated in the middle, Saibari at the top. For the attack, Olise and Gnabry on the wings, Ballon d’Or Kane at center forward.

The visitors are organized in 3-4-2-1. Kobel as the last defense, Gadou is with Anton and Schlotterbeck in the axis. Beier and Svensson pistons, Nmecha in the heart of the game with Veerman. On the offensive side, Bellingham and Chukwuemeka will support Guirassy.
