Ronaldinho officially played again (video)

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The president of Ravenna succeeded in his bet. By securing the services of Auriverde legend Ronaldinho – even if only for a few minutes – the Serie C club (the third Italian division) has achieved a huge marketing coup.

This Sunday on the occasion of an Emilia-Romagna derby against Forli, as part of the 14e championship day, the 46-year-old Brazilian put on his crampons again – for half-time – 11 years after officially hanging them up.

Holder and flanked by the number 10 on the back, the former Parisian was able to demonstrate that he had lost none of his technique. Without creating any illusions due to his physical condition. The person concerned still participated in the construction of the first of his team’s two goals. With a 2-0 victory and a great story to tell.

“ Will I come back? It is the president who will decidesays Ronaldinho, hotly, at the microphone of Sky Sport. If he calls me, I’ll be there! Being here in Ravenna, feeling the excitement before the match, it was really great… »
