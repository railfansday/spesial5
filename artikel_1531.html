Lucas Da Cunha’s new status does not worry Como

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Having become in a few months the outfield player most used by Zinédine Zidane in the France team, notably playing the entirety of the last two matches against Italy and Turkey, Lucas Da Cunha has suddenly changed dimension. A meteoric rise which nevertheless does not worry Como, where the peaceful environment allows the 25-year-old midfielder to keep his feet on the ground. Asked by The Teamits sports director Carlalberto Ludi was completely calm about the management of this new notoriety for an element under contract until June 2031: “There was nothing in particular to manage. Lucas is an intelligent and extremely humble boy. We are just happy for him”.

Same story with his coach Cesc Fàbregas, who nevertheless ensures that his captain mentally digests the sequence of trips and the media exposure: “Lucas is experiencing these dynamics: marketing, Zidane’s fine words, etc. Besides, I didn’t expect him to play so much in blue, as a sole sentinel no less. Back here, he has to put himself back into our context, it’s a mental aspect. But the more he goes to rallies, the better he will be able to manage all of this.”. Installed very close to the training center and described internally as a very calm boy, the former Rennais thus approaches the reception of Cagliari with the same simplicity as before his international debut.
