resembling a geometric flower, 10-meter aluminum lookout frames views in australia’s victorian alps

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

flower of the alpine sun is a monument to the sensational light of the alps, a thoughtful abstraction in dialogue with the surrounding environment.

Gio Veronesi edited by christina vergopoulou November 25, 2026 4 min

Installed at an elevation of 1,750 meters in Falls Creek, within Australia’s Victorian Alps, Flower of the Alpine Sun is a 10-meter-diameter occupiable lookout and public sculpture designed by Gio Veronesi. Unveiled in June 2026 following a 2018 commission by Alpine Resorts Victoria, the Melbourne Prize Trust, and Regional Development Victoria, the project was relocated at Veronesi’s recommendation from a lower parking area to a chairlift-accessible rock outcrop above the village.

Accessible via a pedestrian walkway, the structure by designer Gio Veronesi invites visitors into its interior platform to view the surrounding mountain range through an open geometric lattice. Referencing the alpine sun and the seasonal shift from winter snowfields to summer wildflower meadows, the circular volume reads as a marker on the skyline from the valley below, shifts in profile upon approach, and aligns into a radial frame when viewed from the center of the platform.

A single geometric system governs the structure from overall layout down to individual nodal junctions. Engineered to withstand heavy alpine snow loads and high-altitude winds on the exposed ridge, the primary load-bearing frame and visitor platform are fabricated from galvanized steel. The outer structural frame and cladding elements are constructed from aluminum finished in a custom powder-coated color developed in collaboration with Dulux.
