These Ski Resorts Let You Ski for Free on the Day You Arrive

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

November 28, 2023; Bozeman, MT, USA; Horizon Airlines operations at Bozeman Yellowstone International Airport (BZN). Mandatory Credit: Joe Nicholson-Alaska Airlines (Photo: Courtesy of Alaska Airlines)

A ski vacation usually starts with a travel day. Between catching flights, collecting luggage, and making your way to the mountains, it’s easy to lose an entire day before you ever click into your skis. But what if you could hit the slopes the same day you land, without paying for a lift ticket?

While free arrival-day skiing might sound too good to be true, it’s not a new concept. Park City, Utah, previously offered a program called Quick START, which allowed travelers flying into Salt Lake City International Airport to exchange their same-day boarding pass for a complimentary lift ticket at Park City Mountain Resort, Deer Valley Resort, or Canyons Resort. Although the program has since ended, the idea of turning a travel day into a ski day hasn’t disappeared.

Today, several ski resorts and airlines offer similar incentives. Some require flying with a specific airline, while others are available to travelers booking an eligible resort stay.

Through its Atmos Rewards Alpine Getaways program, Alaska Airlines partners with ski destinations across the western United States and Canada to offer travelers special perks. For the 2026–’27 season, the airline has confirmed partnerships with Crystal Mountain in Washington and Palisades Tahoe in California, with additional resorts potentially joining as the season progresses.

In previous seasons, the airline has also partnered with Alyeska Resort, Arctic Valley Ski Area, and Eaglecrest Ski Area in Alaska; Bogus Basin and Schweitzer in Idaho; Jackson Hole Mountain Resort in Wyoming; and Sun Peaks Resort in British Columbia.

While some participating resorts have offered complimentary lift tickets, others have provided discounted rentals and additional perks. At Palisades Tahoe, travelers can present a same-day Alaska Airlines or Hawaiian Airlines boarding pass or proof of Atmos Rewards membership to access the resort’s offer. Details for Crystal Mountain’s 2026–2’7 promotion are still being finalized.

“Skiing is a natural fit for Alaska because we serve so many great mountain destinations across the West,” Sarah Lasky, Senior External Communications Manager at Alaska Airlines, noted.

“Through Atmos Rewards, we want to make the journey more rewarding and connect our members with the experiences they love, including making it easier to turn the day they arrive into part of the adventure,” she continued.

Travelers can find the latest participating resorts and offers on Alaska Airlines’ website.

For the 2026–’27 winter season, travelers flying into Jackson Hole Airport can receive a complimentary lift ticket at one of three nearby ski resorts, regardless of which airline they choose: Jackson Hole Mountain Resort, Snow King Mountain, or Grand Targhee Resort.

Each resort offers something slightly different. Snow King Mountain provides a full-day lift ticket, while Jackson Hole Mountain Resort and Grand Targhee offer half-day tickets. Snow King also offers night skiing on select evenings, giving travelers who arrive later in the day another opportunity to get on the mountain.

To redeem the offer, visitors must present their boarding pass or flight confirmation showing their arrival at Jackson Hole Airport, along with a matching photo ID, at the participating resort’s ticket window.

The complimentary ticket is only valid on the day of arrival, and blackout dates vary by resort.

For the 2026–’27 winter season, guests who reserve at least five nights of lodging through Big White Direct Bookings qualify for one free lift ticket on their arrival day. The offer is available for both new and existing eligible reservations and doesn’t require flying with a particular airline. To receive the complimentary ticket, guests must present their boarding pass.

Big White is about a 50-minute drive from Kelowna International Airport, and there is airport shuttle transportation available for those who prefer avoiding driving in the snow. For travelers arriving later in the afternoon, the resort also offers night skiing, allowing them to make use of their free ticket after dark.

Free arrival-day skiing isn’t limited to North America. In Chile, Ski Portillo offers guests the opportunity to ski on both their arrival and departure days as part of its all-inclusive vacation packages.

Located in the Andes Mountains beside Laguna del Inca, Ski Portillo operates during the Southern Hemisphere’s winter season, typically from June through September.

Unlike programs that require travelers to present an airline boarding pass, Ski Portillo includes arrival and departure day skiing in its seven-night packages and three- or four-night Mini Week stays.

Before booking, be mindful of your arrival time. Between collecting luggage, picking up rental equipment, and traveling from the airport to the resort, you may have less time on the mountain than expected. Booking an early flight can help maximize your ski day, while resorts offering night skiing may be better options for travelers arriving later.

It’s also important to check blackout dates and other restrictions, as some offers are only available during certain periods of the ski season. Additionally, some resorts may require reservations or specific booking arrangements to qualify for complimentary tickets.

Finally, don’t forget to keep your boarding pass. Many of these programs require proof of same-day air travel, and having everything ready when you arrive can help you get on the slopes that much faster.
