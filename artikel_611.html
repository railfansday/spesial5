Whats the weather forecast for GTA 6? The Weather Channel has a preview.

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

How's the weather in Leonida? The Weather Channel has your Grand Theft Auto VI forecast.

Teaming up with developer Rockstar Games, the weather provider had a little promotional fun on X on Monday, posting a series of stills from the highly anticipated game paired with a forecast for areas of the Florida-based fictional city.

From heavy rain in the Leonida Keys to high humidity in Gambit Bay, the in-game weather systems for GTA 6 are looking complex.

"Grand Theft Auto VI takes place in Leonida — a fictional world inspired by modern-day Florida, a landscape with one of the most unpredictable weather systems in the U.S.," reads a statement by Rockstar's Owen Shepherd, VP of Art: Light and Rendering. "To match the fidelity of all aspects of what is the deepest, most immersive take on the Grand Theft Auto series yet, we had to completely reimagine our in-game weather systems to feel more localized, persistent, and above all dynamic."
