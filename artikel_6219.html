Save On Southwest Airlines Flights With Amex Offers (Targeted)

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Amex Offers program has just rolled out a great new targeted offer for the purchase of Southwest Airlines flights, which could be worth considering for anyone planning travel. While we see Amex Offers deals all the time for travel on select airlines, this is the first time I recall seeing an offer for Southwest, so that’s pretty cool.

With a new targeted Amex Offers deal, you can earn a $60 statement credit when you spend $300 or more with Southwest Airlines by December 31, 2026. (Enrollment required)

It’s possible that there’s also a version of the offer for 6,000 bonus Membership Rewards points rather than a $60 statement credit, and if so, I’d consider that to be more valuable. That’s because based on my latest miles & points valuations, Amex points are worth 1.7 cents each, so I’d value the 6,000 points at $102.

Keep in mind that Southwest doesn’t charge fees to change most kinds of tickets, with the exception of the most basic fares. So you could always use this offer to book a $300+ ticket, and if your plans change, that balance would stay in your Southwest account. Just keep in mind the expiration policy, and the fares that are eligible for free changes.

Also be sure you maximize rewards by ideally finding this deal on an Amex card that offers bonus points on airfare purchases, since that will allow you to earn the statement credit and as many points as possible.
