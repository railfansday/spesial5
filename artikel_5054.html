Tether and Tron Quietly Scrapped T3 Crime Unit Two Months Before $6.3 Billion Binance Hack

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The T3 Financial Crime Unit, which said it froze more than $450 million in illicit USDT with authorities in 23 jurisdictions, wound down in late August.

The T3 Financial Crime Unit, the anti-crime alliance Tron, Tether and TRM Labs launched in 2024 and later expanded to Binance, quietly shut down in late August, Bloomberg reported Wednesday, about two months before the record Binance hack.

The alliance broke down over the past year amid slow response times and a lack of coordination among its members, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter. One of them said T3 was terminated in late August. None of the companies announced the shutdown at the time. Tron, Tether and TRM confirmed that the initiative had wound down, without commenting on the details of the report. Binance declined to comment.

“While the very successful T3 program has concluded, Tron remains committed to working to help stop illicit activities on the blockchain, in partnership with law enforcement,” a Tron DAO spokesperson said to Bloomberg. The spokesperson said Tron will share details of a new anti-crime initiative later.

A Tether spokesperson said the company will keep working with TRM to disrupt illicit funds and has been taking part in Beacon Network, a real-time intelligence-sharing network TRM launched in August 2025. Tether is also working on the Financial Access and Systemic Empowerment Foundation, or FASE Foundation, which CEO Paolo Ardoino unveiled on X on Oct. 31.

TRM’s global head of policy and government affairs, Ari Redbord, said “T3 achieved extraordinary things” and that “Beacon Network builds on that work.”

Tron, Tether and TRM set up T3 in September 2024 as a public-private unit aimed at illicit use of Tether’s USDT stablecoin on the Tron blockchain. The pitch was speed. TRM supplied blockchain intelligence, Tron supplied network visibility and Tether supplied the power to freeze tokens at the issuer level.

The unit said in August 2025 that it had frozen more than $250 million in illicit assets in less than a year. At the same time, it launched T3+, a program for outside partners, with Binance as its first member. Its first joint effort with the exchange froze almost $6 million tied to a pig-butchering scam.

The totals kept climbing. TRM put the T3 tally above $300 million in February, when it announced a $70 million Series C round. In May, the unit announced that it had frozen more than $450 million and was working with authorities in 23 jurisdictions on five continents, including the U.S., Spain, Germany, Brazil and the U.K.

That May release also said the unit had frozen funds within 24 hours of law enforcement requests in account takeovers and violent crime emergencies. It made no mention of the coordination problems Bloomberg has now described. TRM wrote that the Financial Action Task Force cited T3 in November 2025 guidance as a leading model for public-private enforcement work.

The timing matters because the shutdown predates the largest theft in crypto history. Binance put its Oct. 29 loss at $6.34 billion, and blockchain analysis firms have linked the attack to North Korea.

Tron has been a main laundering route. Most of the laundering so far, about $220 million in stolen TRX, has run through the Tron blockchain, blockchain analytics firm Elliptic said in an update Thursday.

The industry still froze a large share of the haul without T3. Tether alone said it froze more than $1.567 billion in USDT after the attack, and roughly $2.85 billion of the stolen assets has been frozen or seized, CNBC reported, citing Elliptic.

The power to freeze USDT sits with Tether, not with the alliance. What T3 offered was a single channel for police in 23 jurisdictions to reach the issuer, the chain and an analytics firm at once.

Whether Tron’s promised replacement brings that structure back, and who else joins it, remains open.
