Ripple’s 126.8 million XRP valued at $0 in Evernorth filing as $505 million write-down emerges

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Ripple's massive XRP contribution carried no accounting value, raising questions about what Evernorth's reported assets actually represent.

Ripple contributed 126.8 million XRP to Evernorth at a recorded value of zero, according to a Nov. 17 SEC filing.

The disclosure adds another wrinkle to the valuation of Evernorth's XRP treasury, which has already suffered roughly $505 million in cumulative write-downs.

The zero-cost lot reflects the basis inherited from Ripple, while the impairment reflects reductions in the carrying amount of Evernorth's historical XRP position. Neither figure establishes what the combined business is worth today.

Evernorth's Sept. 30 financial statements report 346.3 million XRP with an original cost of $846.6 million and a carrying amount of $341.9 million.

Only $6.9 million of impairment was recognized in the third quarter. The nine-month charge was $271 million, while the rest of the cumulative reduction predates 2026.

Under the policy disclosed in the filing, later price recoveries do not reverse the written-down basis, so the gap between book value and market value can persist.

The new amendment clarifies the distinction by adding financial statements for Pathfinder, Evernorth's XRP-holding subsidiary, and updated combined pro forma accounts.

Pathfinder's statements said Ripple contributed 126,791,458 XRP on Oct. 19, 2025. Because the transfer was a transaction between entities under common control, Pathfinder recognized the tokens at Ripple's historical carrying value, which was zero.

The statements also report a merger-related transfer of 346.5 million XRP from Evernorth to Pathfinder. Adding that transfer to Ripple's historical contribution reconstructs approximately 473.3 million XRP from the two disclosed transactions.

The combined pro forma accounts continue historical-cost-less-impairment treatment without a fair-value adjustment. They show $342.2 million of net digital assets, a carrying amount based on that accounting policy.

The 10-Q describes Evernorth's ownership of Pathfinder common units as approximately 71.5%. For the Oct. 9 closing, the pro forma table separately reports 55 million Evernorth shares across both classes and 23 million outside Pathfinder units.

As of Nov. 9, Evernorth reported 58.4 million Class A and Class C shares. Evernorth defines its XRP-per-share KPI as tokens attributable to the public parent divided by the combined number of shares in both classes.

The pro forma balance sheet reports $76 million in cash and $46.3 million in total liabilities, including a $30 million convertible note. It also presents $229.5 million of redeemable noncontrolling interest separately.

The balance sheet presents that outside interest separately from debt, and the note is already included in total liabilities.

The 10-Q subsequently reports $35.3 million in gross warrant proceeds through Nov. 9, plus 8.7 million warrants still outstanding at an $11.50 exercise price. The convertible note can also accrue 4% payment-in-kind interest and has conversion provisions.

The amendment explains why a large write-down and a zero-book-value XRP lot can coexist, while a current market valuation still requires the dated financial bridge.
