The Best Early Tech Deals From Amazon’s 2026 Black Friday and Cyber Monday Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

With early Black Friday sales in full swing, one of the main places you should be looking at is Amazon's Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale. I already did and found many tech deals worth highlighting. I've put my favorite ones here for you to consider, but if you want to check them all out, go to Amazon's page or check out our complete Black Friday coverage for the best tech deals throughout the sale.

The CMF Watch 3 Pro is Nothing's latest smartwatch, and it incorporates AI features powered by ChatGPT; you can currently pick it up at a major discount: Originally $99, you can get the CMF Watch 3 Pro for $55. This is the lowest price this smartwatch has been.

The latest AirPods Pro are a big step up. The third iteration of Apple's iconic earbuds comes with a number of quality-of-life and software improvements that make them worth the typical premium over the AirPods Pro 2—and right now, the AirPods Pro 3 are even cheaper than the previous generation. They're $179 on Amazon (originally $249) as an early Black Friday deal.

The Bose QuietComfort 2nd Gen is currently down to $199 (originally $359), its lowest price ever. The Bose QuietComfort line has been around for a long time, excelling in comfort, ANC, and minimalism. As PCMag mentions in their review, their sound profile leans warm and bass-forward, with deep lows that suit bass-heavy genres, while the companion app adds Bose Immersive Audio for spatial sound and lets you create customized listening profiles—the three-band EQ is fairly basic, though, so anyone who likes fine-tuning their sound may find the adjustment options limiting.

The Sony WH-1000XM6 came out in 2025 and are the best over-ear headphones I tested last year (or ever, really). These headphones go with a companion app that's packed with a lot more features that you can nerd out on. The audio quality is second to none, with the fully customizable EQ giving you plenty of options to get the sound how you like it. Right now, you can get the WH-1000XM6 headphones for $378 (originally $459.99), their lowest price ever.

Samsung’s 32-inch Odyssey G5 (G51F) gaming monitor has dropped to $179.99 on Amazon, which is the lowest price it has reached so far. It’s a flat panel (unlike Samsung’s many curved gaming displays), with a matte coating that helps minimize glare in brighter rooms, and comes with a stand that supports height, tilt, and pivot adjustments. The G51F’s combination of 180Hz refresh rate, 1ms response time, and AMD FreeSync support makes fast-paced games look smoother and feel more responsive than they do on standard 60Hz displays, especially in shooters, racing games, and competitive multiplayer titles.

If you're interested in the new M5 MacBook, Amazon is the only major retailer offering a $250 discount. The 15-inch MacBook Air is $1,249.99 (originally $1,499), and the new M5 chip is powerful enough to likely carry all of your workload without hiccups.

Budget TVs have improved dramatically over the years, and the Hisense U6 Series is a great example of what you can get today for a "budget" price. This is a QLED Mini LED TV, so while it's not at the quality that OLED TVs can offer, it makes up for it with a much lower price. Right now, the 65-inch Hisense Class U6 Series TV is $529.99 (originally $1,099.99), the lowest price it has been. Don't let the budget price fool you; this is a quality TV. The best one of 2025, according to PCMag's "outstanding" review. Its main attributes are its incredible levels of brightness, peaking at 1,024 nits, and its beautiful colors. This is expected from a QLED Mini LED TV, but not for under $550 for a 65-inch TV. It is also packed with features and specs that you won't find in other TVs at this price.

Right now, the 65-inch QM8L is $997.99 (originally $2,499.99), its lowest price since it launched in April. The QM8L has a “Zero Border” bezel design, which gives it a modern, minimalistic look. When you turn it on, you'll likely note how bright this TV gets. TCL claims it reaches 6,000 nits; no OLED can come close to this unit's brightness. Another important spec to note here is the local dimming zones: TCL claims this TV has up to 4,000, which means it gets top-tier backlight control, limited light blooming, and contrast levels that some might not be able to distinguish from an OLED.

The Hisense E7SF QLED is in the low to mid-range of the OLED category, but with its 45% discount, the price is much lower. Amazon currently has the 65-inch E7SF QLED for $499.99 (originally $999.99), which is especially notable given this model came out in April of this year. The first thing you'll notice about the Hisense E7 is how thin and lightweight it is. It comes with four HDMI connections, one of which is an eARC connection so you can plug in a soundbar, and two with a native 144Hz refresh rate in game mode.
