Trump Is Already Crying That He’s Not to Blame for Republican Wipeout

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

When things go wrong, we look to our leaders for answers. And in what appears to be a historic wipeout for Donald Trump’s Republican party, the President has a clear idea of what went wrong: his idiot lackeys failed him.

Republican candidates faced a brutal national climate in the 2026 midterms. Donald Trump’s war in Iran, harsh tariff policy, and overall instability had rocked the global economy, making gas and consumer goods prices skyrocket, cratering his own popularity. Candidates who had spent years fighting for his favor now found themselves desperately trying to distance themselves in the eyes of voters. But in Trump’s opinion, none of that was his fault. In fact, it’s the candidates who were weak.

“Candidate quality matters, and President Trump did a better job promoting many of the candidates on the ballot than the candidates did themselves,” one White House official told Fox News’s Peter Doocey on Tuesday night, amid the continued fallout. The official closed with a defiant note: “President Trump’s resolve to stop the Radical Left is only going to become stronger,” they said. “Buckle up.”

Trump himself weighed in a few minutes later.

“I wasn’t on the Ballot and if I was, we would have won IN A LANDSLIDE!” Trump posted on TruthSocial at 10:37 p.m. ET, shortly before multiple news networks called the Texas Senate race for Democrat James Talarico.

But Trump was, in some ways, on the ballot. It was his impact on the economy that Republican candidates were fighting against, his agenda that they were trying to defend, his rhetoric that they were trying to mimic. All of those factors had won the GOP elections before, in different economic conditions. But this year, voters saw only the worst. It also appears that while Trump might not admit fault, he does realize he’s been beaten. In an interview with the New York Times on Tuesday night, he said he was “absolutely willing to accept those results.”

Trump finished his Truth Social post with a familiar refrain: “Thank you for your attention to this matter.” His party is surely paying attention. With their losses in the midterms looking even worse than expected, the party will be searching for a way to reinvent itself with only two years left in its unpopular figurehead’s last term. It should be clear to them, by now, that Trump himself isn’t willing to take any responsibility for what comes next.
