John Turturro, Carrie Coon, Guitarricadelafuente, and Peter Gamble to Receive SFFILM Honors

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Awards season will be making a stop in San Francisco on December 7 for the annual SFFILM Awards Night, honoring four artists who’ve made an impact this year.

John Turturro, acclaimed this year for his performance in “The Only Living Pickpocket in New York,” will receive the Maria Manetti Shrem Award for Acting, named for the Italian-American philanthropist. (Turturro will also receive IndieWire Honors recognition on December 3.) Carrie Coon, who made a big-screen splash this year in “The Liberation,” will receive the Katie & Todd Traina Award for Acting. The SFFILM Award for Virtuosity will go to “La Bola Negra” breakout star and beloved Spanish pop singer Guitarricadelafuente. Finally, the Nion McEvoy & Leslie Berriman Award for Storytelling goes to “I Play Rocky” screenwriter Peter Gamble. Presenters include “La Bola Negra” directors Javier Calvo and Javier Ambrossi, and “I Play Rocky” star Anthony Ippolito (all of whom are also being feted by IndieWire Honors in December).

SFFILM Awards Night will take place on Monday, December 7, 2026, at San Francisco’s Gateway Pavilion at Fort Mason Center for Arts & Culture.

Anne Lai, SFFILM’s executive director, said, “Film has a rare power to change how we see the world. SFFILM exists to celebrate it and to build its future. At Awards Night we do both. We honor artists whose bold, generous storytelling defined this year in cinema, and we raise the resources that power our work all year long, from students in Bay Area classrooms to the filmmakers we fund and mentor. I’m thrilled to welcome our honorees to San Francisco, where the future is always being created, and film is the gateway.”

“Carrie, Guitarricadelafuente, Peter, and John each bring a distinct sensibility and an uncommon command of their craft to the screen this season,” said Jessie Fairbanks, SFFILM’s director of programming. “We selected this group because they represent the range of talent and creative perspectives that make cinema so compelling, and their work speaks to the thoughtful, nuanced, and often unexpected films that make a season memorable. They bring a fearlessness, determination, and depth of perspective that we are proud to celebrate at SFFILM.”

Last year, Benicio Del Toro, Kristen Stewart, Wunmi Mosaku, and Scott Cooper all received SFFILM prizes. The event serves as a fundraising opportunity for the Bay Area nonprofit film organization that also hosts the San Francisco International Film Festival.
