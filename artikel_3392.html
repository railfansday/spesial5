Netflix's 3-Season Drama Just Surged 1,735% With Nearly 1 Billion Minutes Watched

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

With so much to choose from in Netflix's streaming library, it takes a special series to really stand out. Though many shows have found success, one show that has recently become popular with viewers is Nobody Wants This. The 2024 series takes a rom-com approach to its love story, consistently delivering a hilarious but nuanced exploration of the romance at its heart. With a bold premise and talented cast, the series quickly gained attention, but it has only grown from there. Nobody Wants This is still going strong on Netflix after the recent release of Season 3, proving that fans still want more.

Led by Kristen Bell and Adam Brody, Nobody Wants This revolves around a chaotic podcaster and her romance with a devout rabbi, telling a unique story about modern relationships and the obstacles they often face. Season 3 quickly earned a spot in Netflix's Top 10, all but guaranteeing a Season 4 renewal — which the streamer officially announced last week.

After Joanne (Bell) and Noah (Brody) have an unexpected first meeting, they can't seem to go their separate ways. Noah is a religious leader, while Joanne considers herself agnostic, creating a conflict of faith despite their undeniable chemistry. Joanne has a messy dating history that she discusses on her podcast, while Noah has recently broken up with his long-term girlfriend, much to his family's frustration. Yet, the biggest issue is their families, who have plenty to say about the relationship, especially Noah's mother, Bina (Tovah Feldshuh). This forces the couple to question whether their feelings are enough to overcome the many obstacles their relationship is facing.

The single-season series was just ahead of its time.

On the surface, Nobody Wants This has a simple premise, but it doesn't shy away from complicated issues through the clashing of Noah and Joanne's lifestyles. While Joanne's agnosticism creates problems for Noah's profession, she begins to question her own belief system, allowing the show to explore religion from multiple perspectives. Despite a focus on romance, Nobody Wants This takes a realistic approach, and that's what separates it from similar shows in the rom-com genre.

While Noah and Joanne are the show's central characters, the cast is full of hilarious personalities, from Noah's meddlesome mother Bina to Morgan (Justine Lupe), Joanne's dramatic sister. Both leads are part of two loving but imperfect families that function very differently. Joanne's is messy, between her quirky divorced parents and her dynamic with Morgan, whom she is close to but often at odds with. Meanwhile, Noah's family is overly involved and strict, leaving Joanne feeling unwelcome.

The series also dives deeper into marriage through Noah's brother, Sasha (Timothy Simons), and his wife, Esther (Jackie Tohn), who struggle to see eye to eye despite their history together. With these two families filling out the show's ensemble, Nobody Wants This features a range of personalities that interact in hilarious ways, keeping the audience laughing while addressing more serious topics.

Four love stories, four completely different ideas of what it means to be somebody's person. This is the one that matches how you actually fall, and what it would ask of you.

You do not fall for strangers. You fall for the person you have already been talking to for years, which is the slowest and by some distance the most durable way anybody has found to do this.

You want a partner, not an audience. Somebody who wants their own life as badly as you want yours, and who would never ask you to be smaller to make the relationship easier.

The intensity was never the problem. The secrecy is. You are drawn to a love that lives entirely in private, and to the particular ache of wanting somebody you are not supposed to want.

You do not do this cautiously. When it happens for you it is immediate and total and it rearranges everything, and you would not accept a smaller version of it.

Even after three seasons, viewers clearly want more of this show, and luckily, Netflix plans to deliver. Though Season 4's release date has yet to be confirmed, there's still a lot left for the series to tackle. Joanne and Noah have come a long way since Season 1, but that doesn't mean life will be easy for them. Meanwhile, the show has introduced other complicated relationships to explore, setting up plenty for another batch of episodes. Ultimately, Nobody Wants This is the perfect combination of humor and heart, making it a can't-miss rom-com.
