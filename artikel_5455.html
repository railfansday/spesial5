Voters reject AI data centers, Shield AI’s $5.3B Echodyne deal, and a Seattle startup’s rare double exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

by Todd Bishop on Nov 7, 2026 at 8:07 amNovember 7, 2026 at 9:49 am

This week: Shield AI, the defense tech company building its X-BAT autonomous fighter jet south of Seattle, agrees to acquire Kirkland-based radar maker Echodyne in a deal valued at up to $5.3 billion.

We discuss what it means for the Seattle region’s rise as a defense tech hub, how the midterm results could affect the sector, and the Mercer Island roots of Shield AI co-founders Brandon and Ryan Tseng.

Voters backed limits on data center development in 40 of 41 local ballot measures across the country, more than half of them in Ohio. We talk about the backlash, reports that foreign actors are amplifying it, and what it means for Amazon as it plans its first data centers in Washington state.

Seattle startup Suplari is acquired by Docusign, nearly three years after Microsoft spun it back out to its founders. Along with the Echodyne deal and Agility Robotics’ public listing, it could be a sign that M&A is picking up.

And in the GeekWire Trivia Challenge: how did Nobel Prize winner Mary Brunkow, one of this year’s GeekWire Uncommon Thinkers, find out she had won the Nobel? She’ll be honored at the GeekWire Gala on Dec. 10.
