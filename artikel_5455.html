AEWIN Showcases Server Portfolio with AMD EPYC 9006 SP7 Processors at AMD in Depth: EPYC "Venice"

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Comprehensive AI, General-Purpose, and Storage Server Portfolio Highlights Platform Readiness and Upcoming System Shipments

LAS VEGAS, Nov. 10, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- AEWIN Technologies Co., Ltd., a member of the Qisda Group and a server and network appliance provider, today announced new servers powered by AMD EPYCâ¢ 9006 SP7 Server CPUs. Originally previewed on November 5th during the AMD in Depth: EPYC "Venice" event in Las Vegas, the BG28-A10710 and BX28-A10710 highlight AEWIN's platform readiness as the company prepares to bring its latest EPYC servers to the market in alignment.

In addition to the systems demonstrated at the event, AEWIN's Server portfolio spans AI, general-purpose, and storage servers designed for a broad range of infrastructure requirements. Building on its previously announced lineup, AEWIN offers servers featuring high-performance computing power, high-speed connectivity, and flexible configurations to support AI workloads, enterprise applications, scalable storage, and cloud infrastructure.

For AI computing, the BG18-A10710 1U AI Server supports up to two single-width 600W GPUs and integrates two-phase direct liquid cooling (2P DLC) solutions for CPU and GPU thermal management. The BG28-A10710 2U AI Server accommodates up to four full-height, full-length GPUs, supporting demanding AI training, inference, and accelerated computing workloads through PCIe 6.0 connectivity.

For enterprise and cloud infrastructure, the BX28-A10710 2U General Purpose Server combines balanced compute and storage capabilities with support for two PCIe 6.0 slots, one OCP 3.0 slot, and up to 12 U.2 NVMe SSDs. For performance-critical storage workloads, the BS28-A10710 2U Storage Server supports up to 16 E3.S Gen6 SSDs, providing high-density storage capacity and high-bandwidth connectivity for data-intensive applications.

By translating AMD EPYC 9006 SP7 Server CPU capabilities into purpose-built platforms, AEWIN enables customers to address diverse workload requirements. Its offerings deliver the versatility needed to master shifting market dynamics, seamlessly balancing extreme performance with cost-effective scalability. With its expanding server portfolio and the broader technology capabilities of Qisda Group ecosystem partners, AEWIN is well positioned to empower customers to build reliable, efficient, and future-ready rack-scale AI infrastructure.
