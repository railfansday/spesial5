Port Vale confirm TV decision and one thing is causing a real backlash

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Port Vale supporters have learned the new Sky Sports TV schedule just after the new year, and it would be to suggest that it has not gone down particularly well amongst the fanbase.

Port Vale, after a tricky start back to life in League Two following relegation from League One last year, are starting to finally starting to find their footing, as Jon Brady's side aim to claw their way back up the table.

There remains hope amongst the Valiants that this year could have a dream conclusion, though for those that often travel away with the club, they have just received some bad news.

Which sets of EFL fans have the most misery induced by their clubs?

Port Vale has confirmed via X that some fixtures around January and February 2027 have been moved to fit in with Sky Sports' TV coverage, with one of those being the away trip to Exeter City.

The initial kick-off time at St James' Park was a standard 3 pm on Saturday, February 20, but now that has been brought forward by two-and-a-half hours to a 12:30 pm kick-off time, giving Vale supporters an incredibly early morning to look forward to.

From Vale Park in Stoke-on-Trent, it's a near four-hour trip down to Devon without any stops and perfect traffic, as now supporters will have no choice but to get an even earlier night to be wide awake for the trip down south.

Naturally, this news hasn't gone down well with Vale fans, who have taken to X to react to the update.

An eight-hour round journey, at best, is already difficult enough as it is, but make that an early kick-off, and it will no doubt prevent a lot of supporters from attending who otherwise would've.

The changing of schedules is completely out of any supporters' hands, and Vale have been shown absolutely no consideration or mercy in this instance.

'Joke' is extremely apt to describe this situation.

This change in kick-off time can be enough for anyone to fall out of love with football, especially if you're on the receiving end of it.

You'd do well to find a supporter, Vale or not, who would agree that this fixture change is a good idea, as once again, the supporters are not considered in this instance.

What do you make of the fixture change? Have Sky considered the Vale supporters in this? How would you feel if your team was on the receiving end? Share your thoughts below!

Whilst Vale supporters don't have to worry about the trip down to Devon for another few months, in the meantime, head coach Brady may well have helped his side turn a corner.

The Valiants won just one game in their opening nine fixtures this season, the sole victory incidentally coming against Exeter, but with five wins and one defeat in the following seven, Vale finally look to have found their feet.

By the time they travel to Exeter, supporters hope to be looking up instead of over their shoulder, as an immediate return to League One shouldn't yet be ruled out.
