Derby County must turn to 43-year-old if they sack John Eustace: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Derby County have won only one of their first 14 league fixtures and they are in the drop zone along with Preston North End and Burnley. DCFC are seven points from safety behind Watford and Charlton Athletic following their 4-0 loss at home to Norwich City last time out.

The Rams’ hierarchy have a big decision to make about the future of head coach Eustace. If they are to sack the ex-Birmingham City and Blackburn Rovers man, there is only one man for the job at Pride Park.

Step forward Rob Edwards, who has been available since his shock summer sacking by Wolves and he has been weighing up his options in the game since. He has been carefully waiting for the right opportunity to arise and hasn’t rushed back into work yet.

The Telford-born man is a vastly experienced coach in the EFL and may feel he has a point to prove after his surprise dismissal from Molineux in June. At the age of 43, he is still young for a manager and he has overseen over 250 games from the dugout so far in his career.

If Derby County cut ties with Eustace, there aren’t that many available options at the moment. It seems unlikely DCFC would get someone like Steven Gerrard, Sean Dyche or Steve Cooper.

Paul Heckingbottom, Gary Rowett, Matt Bloomfield or Liam Manning would all be underwhelming and it costs money to get someone already employed. Therefore, Edwards seems the most obvious candidate.

He knows the league well, understands what it takes to get out of it and is someone who could help transform the Rams’ fortunes. As well as Wolves, he has also managed the likes of Forest Green Rovers, Watford, Luton Town and Middlesbrough.

Edwards’ best spell came at Kenilworth Road and he guided the Hatters to promotion to the Premier League against the odds during his time in Bedfordshire. They beat Coventry City on penalties in the play-off final.

Derby County need to act fast if they are to land Edwards. The longer DCFC leave it, the further adrift they could be cut and the less attractive they would become for prospective Eustace replacements.

Edwards has been linked with a switch to Wrexham recently and was also in the frame for the recent Burnley post. If the Rams lose again on Friday night to Bolton Wanderers, it would be hard to see Eustace surviving.
