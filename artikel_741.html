Chris Rock Gushes to Kimmel About Inducting Wu-Tang Clan into Rock & Roll Hall of Fame: ‘They Are the Top of the Top’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

As far as Oasis is concerned, though, the Misty Green director understands that the Gallaghers are "too cool" for the RRHOF.

Chris Rock movingly introduced the Wu-Tang Clan at Saturday’s 2026 Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony in Los Angeles, and even days later the Misty Green writer/director/star was still floating on cloud nine about the whole thing.

Bed-Stuy, Brooklyn-bred Rock seemed to be still pinching himself over the honor of inducting the legendary Staten Island crew into the Hall of Fame. “I had the pleasure of doing that,” Rock told Jimmy Kimmel on Monday night (Nov. 16). “They’re the greatest, most incredible … the best group of all time,” he continued, running out of superlatives.

Then he found another one. “The worst guy in the Wu-Tang is better than every rapper you know,” Rock stated. “They are the top of the top.” Kimmel and Rock then discussed the logistics of such an expansive group getting microphone time to say their thanks yous, describing group musical mastermind RZA going up first, followed by Method Man, another member of the group and then … a cut microphone.

“Because there’s nine guys,” Kimmel explained of the group that also includes GZA, Raekwon, Ghostface Killah, Inspectah Deck, U-God, Masta Killa and Cappadonna. “I had nothing to do with that,” Rock assured any disgruntled Wu-Tang fans. “There’s a lot of Wu in the house.”

The men also discussed the long list of no-shows for the ceremony, including Oasis, who very pointedly declined the invitation. “Oasis can’t come,” Rock said, stating the obvious. “They’re too cool. You would have been mad if they came.” Kimmel noted that British heavy metal icons Iron Maiden also skipped out. “Well, they’re old,” Rock suggested. “They’re like Rusty Maiden now.”

“To me, the message is ‘we’re cooler than the Wu-Tang Clan,'” Kimmel said of Oasis and Iron Maiden’s decision to no-show. “If the Wu-Tang Clan can come, everybody else can show up.”
