Dark Florals Are Giving Thanksgiving Outfits an Unexpectedly Romantic

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

This Thanksgiving, we're swapping autumnal browns and oranges for deep plums, rich burgundy and dark navy florals.

Thanksgiving Day outfits probably conjure up images of chunky brown and burnt orange knits, suede knee-high boots, and corduroy mini skirts.

But this year, Thanksgiving is taking an unexpectedly romantic route. Rather than traditional autumnal hues and fabrics, seasonal styles are embracing dark florals this holiday season.

If you're unsure what to wear this Thanksgiving Day, dark floral maxi dress, blouses, and bottoms could be the twist your yearly outfit needs, even if all you're going to do is sit in the living room with your cousins.

With a gothic, almost witchy appeal, dark floral dresses, tops, and even pants offer a moodier alternative to harvest-inspired hues.

Instead of the bright colorways you'd see in spring and summer, winter and fall florals are defined by dark plums, rich burgundy, deep navy blues, often paired with '90s-inspired velvet overlays or warm corduroy.
