Jeff Bezos Was ‘Heavily Involved’ in Casting Robert Pattinson as James Bond and Admits Initial Fears ‘Because He’s Already So Famous’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Amazon founder and chairman Jeff Bezos revealed during a recent appearance at Semafor’s Silicon Valley & The World event that he was “heavily involved” in Amazon MGM casting Robert Pattinson as the next James Bond.

Bezos said that because Bond is traditionally a star-making role, they were at first “slightly biased” against casting Pattinson because he’s already “so famous.” However, after narrowing down the candidates to five and conducting screen tests, he was the “obvious” choice. “We ran a really tight process and made the final decision on Friday,” Bezos said. “There was a big group initially, and we down-selected to five finalists. We did real, old-fashioned screen tests of those five finalists. It was interesting. Full costumes and real sets and other actors with them. We did three different scenes with each of the five finalists, and then we got together on Friday to spend a couple of hours looking at all the tests and reviewing all that.”

He continued, “By the time we got to this list of five, they were all amazing. But there was something special about Rob and his performance. It was immediately undebated. Denis [Villeneuve], the director … we all gravitated toward Rob. Initially, we were slightly biased against him just because he’s already so famous, like maybe we should have somebody more unknown, but in the screen tests, it was so obvious he is James Bond.”

Amazon MGM announced Pattinson as the next James Bond on Monday, ending weeks of rabid media speculation. “James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime,” the “Batman” and “Primetime” star said in a statement after the news broke. “I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”

While Bezos never mentioned them by name, industry analysts seemingly had a handle on the Bond finalists heading into the last weekend of casting. The shortlist of top choices included Callum Turner, Josh O’Connor, Jack Lowden and Nicholas Hoult. Other names rumored to be in the running at one include Jacob Elordi, Tom Francis, Aaron Taylor-Johnson, Harris Dickinson and even Harry Styles.

Amazon finalized its deal for MGM, the studio behind James Bond, in 2022. However, it didn’t have creative control of the franchise until it bought out longtime Bond producers Michael G. Wilson and Barbara Broccoli for $20 million. Denis Villeneuve and Pattinson’s film will be the first released after the deal.
