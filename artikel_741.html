Hibernian finally register home win after battling past Dundee

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Hibernian vs Dundee. Scottish Premiership.

Easter Road StadiumAttendance: Attendance15,108.

Report as Hibernian win at Easter Road for the first time this season; Martin Boyle opened the scoring from the penalty spot; Nicky Cadden added a second; Joe Wright pulled a goal back for Dundee; Hibs move up to ninth in the Scottish Premiership; Dundee remain seventh

Hibernian ended their long wait for a Premiership home win after overcoming Dundee 2-1 at Easter Road.

Martin Boyle and Nicky Cadden - who curled home a stunning effort from outside the box - had the hosts in command before the interval.

But Dundee showed plenty of fight after the break and pulled one back through Joe Wright.

The visitors tried in vain to grab an equaliser and it was Hibs who were left satisfied by their first three points at home since beating Kilmarnock on April 4.

Despite being thrashed by Dundee United 4-0 at the weekend, Hibernian head coach Marink Reedijk decided to make only two alterations to his team, with Cadden and Thibault Klidje coming in.

Dundee manager Steven Pressley changed half of his outfield XI following the 8-0 humiliation to Hearts.

Owen Bevan was suspended and in came on-loan Bournemouth defender Matai Akinmboni for a full debut while Joe Wright, Brad Halliday, Joe Bevan and Ashley Hay started.

Hibs played on the front foot as they looked to start repairing the damage from the weekend's demoralising loss.

On a rare start, Togo international Klidje was lively and saw a header crash into the side-netting before hitting a harmless snap-shot straight at Dundee goalkeeper Owen Goodman.

From a short corner routine Hibs worked space for Cadden on the edge of the box from Josh Mulligan's lay-off and his driving effort whistled just past a post.

Dundee were desperate to keep it tight at the back but offered little going forward. Just when it looked like they were beginning to settle Hibs hit them two goals in three minutes before the break.

The first came in the 40th minute when Boyle fired a penalty past Goodman after being tripped by Ryan Finnigan.

Cadden then found the top corner with a sublime curling effort from his left foot just outside the box. It was another short corner routine that saw Miguel Chaiwa set up the wing-back.

Despite the hammer blow of losing two goals in quick succession before the break, Dundee demonstrated at the start of the second half that they were not going to roll over.

Pressley's side upped their intensity as they searched for a goal to get themselves back in the game and that arrived in the 67th minute courtesy of Wright.

Substitute Callum Jones' free-kick should have been put away by Ryan Astley but the ball broke back to Wright and he slammed the ball high into the net from six yards.

That set up a tense finish but Hibs desperately held on for a rare home victory.

"It's always nice to win, it's a good one again in order to make sure we get confirmation that if we show our identity about intensity, aggression and proactivity and are brave, that anything can happen here.

"I hope we get another three points next match and that we go again in a way that we do the things we are good at and what our identity and what we stand for is.

"The next match against Motherwell is not at home, I know, but there will be a match at home against Rangers, where this can also come to life again. But that is far away.

"First things first, Saturday lies another challenge."

"Both teams are a bit fragile and there was a lot of trepidation in the game but the one thing about us we got a degree of pride back. It's not the result but a little bit of stability and we showed a lot of courage.

"We wanted to give ourselves a foundation to build from and we gave ourselves that.

"We were much more cohesive in the second half and when you suffer the defeat we suffered on Saturday and you come here and you find yourself 2-0 down at half-time, it asks a lot of questions of you as a group and I thought they responded brilliantly.

"They showed courage and spirit and it's not been easy because there's a fragility about us. We responded in the proper manner."
