Cardano Hits 57.6% Staking Bid While Price Compresses

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Home » Cardano (ADA) » Cardano Hits 57.6% Staking Bid While Price Compresses

ADA price continues to bounce around the historical demand points while staking sees a new high.

Cardano continues to show strong on-chain participation even as ADA trades near multi-month lows. The Cardano Foundation noted that 57.6% of circulating supply is currently staked, with roughly 21.62 billion ADA delegated to stake pools.

The Foundation highlighted that every staked ADA backs a pool involved in block production and L-1 network security. Cardano’s liquid staking model lets holders retain full control of their tokens, including the ability to spend or move them at any time.

57.6% of circulating $ADA is staked.Every staked coin backs a stake pool that helps produce blocks and secure the network. Cardano’s liquid staking allows holders to keep full control and spend or move their funds anytime.That’s how a network stays resilient. https://t.co/b1zWSViLmy pic.twitter.com/ip0D0a66kC

The design has kept participation rates high relative to many other proof-of-stake networks, supporting resilience even during weaker price periods.

On the technical side, pattern analyst Crypto With Gopal observed a falling wedge pattern forming on the 15-minute chart. ADA has been consolidating between roughly $0.172 and $0.173 as selling pressure eases.

$ADA / USDT 👀Cardano is forming a Falling Wedge pattern on the 15-min chart! Price is consolidating near $0.172–$0.173 as selling pressure gradually weakens.A breakout above $0.176 could trigger a bullish move towards $0.180–$0.195 📈🔥Compression is building, and bulls… pic.twitter.com/4VQvgbGuVB

A move above $0.176 could open a path toward $0.180–$0.195 in the near term, according to the analysis. The setup reflects tightening price action after a period of decline, though confirmation still depends on a sustained break higher.

Gopal’s outlook focuses on the immediate recovery zone above current prices. Beyond that range, some longer-term scenarios place $0.50 as a more extended target if broader market conditions improve and network activity continues to expand.

Hitting that level would require ADA to more than double from present levels and clear intermediate resistance that has limited upside in recent months.

The contrast remains clear. ADA staking participation near 58% of circulating supply indicates continued holder commitment to securing the network, while price action has compressed near the $0.17 area.

For now, the network’s security metrics and the short-term technical structure are the primary points of focus.
