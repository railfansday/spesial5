Wizards at Pistons final score: Washington loses 122-111

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Washington Wizards lost to the Detroit Pistons, 122-111 on Monday night to fall to 4-7 in the 2026-27 season.

While the Wizards led 32-31 after the first quarter, Detroit outscored Washington 37-26 in the second. The oddest part was that Anthony Davis scored 19 of his 44 points in the second quarter. Davis ws Washington's brightest spot tonight shooting 16-of-21 from the field and grabbing 13 rebounds.

Still, things didn't get much better in the third quarter when the Pistons shot 63.2 percent from the field and out-rebounded the Wizards 127 in the period. Washington also committed 19 turnovers in the game as a whole which also made things harder for them tonight.

John Collins led the Pistons with 24 points and 10 rebounds while Daniss Jenkins and Duncan Robinson added 22 points each for Detroit.

AJ Dybantsa added 21 points while Trae Young added 20 more for Washington.

The Wizards' next game is on Wednesday where they will play against the Indiana Pacers. Tip off is at 7 p.m. ET. See you then.
