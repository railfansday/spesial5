Three Games You Can Play For Free This Weekend On PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Don’t want to pay $80 for Grand Theft Auto 6? Don’t have a console to play it on? There are alternatives. Here are three games you can play for free on PC this weekned.

Let’s start with Crusader Kings 3. It’s a grand strategy game in which you leverage diplomacy, marriage, personal development, and economic expansion to become a dominant imperial power in the Middle Ages. Whether through statecraft or scheming, you’re basically doing fantasy football for geopolitics while navigating your character’s personal legacy and lore like you might in a visual novel. It’s pretty good! And easy to lose your weekend to. It’s free to play on Steam from now until November 23.

Then there’s Mistfall Hunter. This one is also medieval but decidedly less well-received. It’s a dark fantasy extraction ARPG with both co-op and PvP multiplayer. Unlike most of the extraction games out there, which are shooters, this one is melee-focused. It launched to a 55-percent positive Steam review rating but has been slowly improving. Players have praised the combat and upgrade system despite lots of complaints about balance and server issues. “It be good,” one Steam player wrote today in the review section. “imo its not fun,” wrote another. It’s normally $25 but currently free to play until November 23.
