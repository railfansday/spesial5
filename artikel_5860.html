Sen-elect Julia Letlow announces marriage: 'We feel so blessed to have found one another'

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Fox News anchor Trace Gallagher reports on projected victories for Sen. Marsha Blackburn, R-Tenn., in Tennessee's gubernatorial race and GOP nominee Julia Letlow in the Louisiana U.S. Senate race on 'FOX News Democracy 26.'

Rep. Julia Letlow, R-La., who won the 2026 Louisiana U.S. Senate contest this month, recently announced that she had gotten married.

"Kevin [Ainsworth] and I were married over the weekend, surrounded by our families," the lawmaker noted in social media posts on Sunday.

"We feel so blessed to have found one another, and the kids and I are so excited to start this new chapter as a family. To everyone who has prayed for us over the years, thank you for sharing in our joy," she continued, adding a heart emoji.

The pair got married on Saturday in Calhoun, Louisiana, with their immediate families present, a Letlow spokesperson said, according to nola.com.

House Speaker Mike Johnson, R-La., congratulated the couple.

"Congratulations, my friends! Kelly and I could not be happier for you. So much to be thankful for! May God bless and keep you in the exciting days ahead," Johnson said in a Monday post on X.

Letlow took office in 2021 after winning an election to fill the remainder of the term that would have belonged to her late husband, Rep.-elect Luke Letlow, 41, who died in late 2020, less than a week before he would have been sworn into office in early 2021.

President Donald Trump urged Letlow to run for U.S. Senate earlier this year, pledging to endorse her. Days later, Letlow announced her Senate bid.

Incumbent Sen. Bill Cassidy, R-La., placed third in the GOP U.S. Senate primary, failing to advance to the runoff.
