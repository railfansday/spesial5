6 Ways Brands Are Capturing Buzz Around GTA 6’s Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

After numerous delays and countless memes immortalizing the “we got X before Grand Theft Auto VI” format, the most-anticipated video game of the year, launching tomorrow (Nov. 19) is finally almost here.

According to data from Sensor Tower, preorders have already surpassed $1.5 billion, translating to almost 17 million copies. Conversely, economists estimate that $1 billion in productivity is at risk on release day as thousands of employees plan to call out of work.

Developed by Rockstar Games, the GTA franchise is notorious for rejecting in-game brand integrations, opting instead for parodied restaurant chains, fake car manufacturers, and a host of made up, pun-filled businesses.
