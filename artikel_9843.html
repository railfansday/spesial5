A Practical Workflow for AI-Generated Website Images

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

AI image generation can speed up website illustration, but a useful image starts with a page's purpose. An editorial cover, an explanatory visual and a product photograph have different requirements. A generated scene may communicate an idea; it should not automatically be presented as documentary evidence of a real product, office or result.

Define the placement, audience, message, aspect ratio and devices. For a wide article cover, decide where the subject should sit and whether space is needed for a heading. Check which part of the image will remain visible when the layout crops it on a phone.

Describe subject, action, environment, viewpoint, lighting, palette and level of realism. For a series, share the visual rules while giving each image a distinct job. A brief also needs exclusions: no invented logos, no fake software screenshots and no fabricated chart when accuracy matters.

For an exact interface, data visualization or technical diagram, use source material and tools that let you verify the details. Image generators can produce convincing but inaccurate labels, numbers and components.

Use reference material only when you have the necessary permission. Review the generator's current terms and your organization's rules before uploading client designs, personal data or confidential material.

A reference can convey palette or mood; it does not by itself grant permission to reproduce another company's logo, a recognizable character or an existing work. When an exact brand mark is needed, use the approved original as a separate layer in an appropriate design tool. Do not ask a model to invent a substitute.

A useful prompt lets the team compare the output to the brief. For example:

This is a starting point, not a universal recipe. Adjust the subject and dimensions for the page. When revising, specify the intended change—such as moving the subject to the right—instead of simply asking the model to “make it better.” Capabilities and settings vary by tool, so check the current documentation.

First check meaning. Does the scene support the article, or could it imply a factual claim the page cannot substantiate? Look closely at equipment, packaging, maps, labels and numbers. Do not present a generated dashboard as a real software screenshot or an invented chart as measured data.

Use accurate product imagery when appearance or specifications matter to buyers. A generated atmospheric visual may be suitable when clearly used as an illustration and when it does not mislead. For health, financial or safety-related pages, involve a qualified reviewer where factual details matter.

Inspect the image at full size for unwanted marks, illegible writing and artifacts. Consider whether it resembles an identifiable person or protected asset without permission. Review the current terms of the generator and reference sources, and consult an appropriate specialist when rights are uncertain. AI generation alone is not a blanket guarantee of commercial rights.

If a fictional office, employee or outcome could be mistaken for documentary evidence, label its illustrative nature in a way appropriate to the context.

Test the image in the actual layout, not only in an image viewer. A wide cover may be cropped differently on a phone. Keep important objects within the visible area and make sure text or controls remain readable over the background.

Choose dimensions, compression and file format for the site's requirements. Compare quality and size: excessive compression can damage gradients and fine detail. Where appropriate, prepare responsive variants and reserve display space to reduce layout movement. Test on real devices and constrained connections.

Write alternative text for a meaningful image based on its purpose in the surrounding content. Decorative images need different handling. An alt attribute is not a place to repeat a list of keywords. Add a visible caption when the context or attribution requires one.

Maintain a small asset register so the team can replace or correct an image later. Before deleting a media file, check whether other pages use it. Keep an earlier version when a quick rollback matters.

The goal is not to generate the greatest number of images. Start with one page, a clear brief and a review in the real layout. Once the process works, extend the visual system to a series without copying an error across the whole site.
