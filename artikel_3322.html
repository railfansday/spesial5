Beyoncé Makes History as First Artist to Earn 100 Grammy Award Nominations

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

When Beyoncé sang “I’m one of one, I’m number one, I’m the only one/Don’t even waste your time trying to compete with me” on the Renaissance song “Alien Superstar,” she wasn’t exaggerating. Following the announcement of the 2027 Grammy Awards nominees Monday morning, the musician has reached a new history-making milestone at the Grammys. She is the first artist to earn 100 Grammy Award nominations.

Beyoncé’s 100th nomination came in the Best R&B Solo Performance category for “Morning Dew (Donk),” the archival release she surfaced in time with the 20th anniversary of her second solo studio album B’Day, though the song itself dates back to the Beyoncé era.

She set the record for most nominations in Grammy Awards history back in 2024 when she earned 11 nods for the 2025 Grammy Awards, breaking her 88-nomination tie with her husband Jay-Z and bringing her total to 99 nominations. The pair are trailed in nominations by Paul McCartney with 84, Quincy Jones with 80, and Chick Corea with 79.

At the 2023 Grammys, Beyoncé became the most awarded artist in Grammy Awards history with 32 total wins, having won Best Dance/Electronic Album, Best Traditional R&B Performance, and Best R&B Song that night. When she returned for the 2025 Grammys, having released Cowboy Carter, her country opus and the second part of a supposed three-act album trilogy, she extended her record with three more awards, including her first Album of the Year win, bringing her total to 35. It stands as the third most-nominated album with 11 nods.

This year, Raye tied the record Beyoncé set at the 2025 Grammys for most nods in a single year by a woman. Her album This Music May Contain Hope is nominated across 10 categories, plus she has earned a nomination for Songwriter of the Year. Earlier this week, Beyoncé spoke about Raye in an interview with i-D, saying, “I’m also so proud of Raye, who wrote some of the songs on The Gift. She has had such an anointing. You hear the vulnerability and wisdom in her voice.”

Two more artists she shouted out scored big acknowledgments for the 2027 Grammy Awards, too. Sienna Spiro, the bluesy UK singer who broke through this year, is up for Best New Artist. “I’ve known her family well for quite some time,” Beyoncé said. “I heard her sing when she was a kid, discovering her voice, and I can’t articulate the pride she brings me. She extended her praise to Olivia Dean, who won Best New Artist at the 2026 Grammy Awards and has earned six nominations for next year’s show. “I also love and respect Olivia Dean,” she said. “These beautiful women are all kind, have grace and write and produce their own music. It gives me goosebumps. I just burst with pride when I see them.”

It will be a long while before anyone conquers the records Beyoncé has set, but she seems hopeful the future is in good hands.
