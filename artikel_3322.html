Instagram launches Effect Builder for making AI Stories filters from text

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The free web tool, for US adults, turns a written description or a photo into a Stories effect.

Instagram’s Effect Builder lets users create and share AI effects for Stories

Instagram has launched Effect Builder, a free web tool that lets people create their own AI effects for Instagram Stories by describing what they want.

For now, it is only available to users in the US who are 18 or older. No design or coding skills are needed, and creating and publishing effects is free.

To make an effect, users type a description of the look they want, such as vintage film grain or a pastel watercolour style. They can also upload a photo as a reference, and the AI uses its colours, lighting and texture to shape the result.

To get started, users go to the Effect Builder page on the Edits website. Before publishing, they can test the effect on their own photos and adjust it. Each effect then goes through a safety review by Meta, which usually takes less than 24 hours.

Once approved, effects appear in the Stories effects browser, where anyone can find them and apply them to their own photos. Each effect also shows its creator’s Instagram handle, and other users can tap through to the creator’s profile.

To use someone else’s effect, viewers tap ‘Try it’. Creators, meanwhile, can find the effects they have made under ‘My effects’ in Stories.

“Describe the look you want, preview it on your own photos, and publish,” said Cheechee Lin, a product designer for Edits.

Instagram tested the tool with creators including photographer Dave Krugman and Karen X Cheng.

The tool also brings back creator-made effects to Instagram. In January 2025, Meta shut down Meta Spark, the platform outside creators used to build filters for its apps. Most of their effects disappeared.

Unlike Spark, which needed design and technical skills, Effect Builder relies on AI to do that work. Instagram says that if users can describe an effect, they can build it.

Effect Builder also builds on AI Restyle, which Instagram launched in October 2025 and which already lets users create effects from prompts. The new tool adds more controls and a way to share effects with other users.

The launch is part of Instagram’s push to turn Edits into a full set of creative tools. Last week, it brought the Edits app to Mac, its first step onto desktop computers. A Windows version has not yet been announced.
