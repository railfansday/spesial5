Al Jardine Writes Open Letter to Fellow Beach Boy Mike Love: ‘Praying For Your Recovery’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Al Jardine has written a public note to fellow Beach Boy Mike Love, who has been dealing with serious health issues in recent months.

“An open letter to my Beach Boys brother, Mike Love,” Jardine wrote, before quoting from the 1968 Beach Boys songs “Friends.” “‘We’ve been friends now for so many years, we’ve been together through the good times and the tears.’ Mike, those lyrics ring especially true now, don’t they? ‘We drift apart for a little bit of a spell, one night I get a call and I know that you’re well.’ I’m thinking about you a lot, and praying for your recovery Sending you the warmth of the sun and Good Vibes.”

Jardine and Love are the last two surviving members of the original Beach Boys lineup. But they were never particularly close, and Jardine was removed from the band in 1998 shortly after the death of Carl Wilson. It sparked a legal spat when Jardine began touring under the moniker the Beach Boys Family and Friends.

“Mike just came down on me with a shitload of lawyers,” Jardine told Rolling Stone in 2025.  “And pretty soon I was down about a million bucks. It was pretty devastating financially and emotionally. He showed me no mercy…Mike has better lawyers. They wrote my epitaph: ‘You may not use “beach” and “boy” in the same sentence to describe your band.'” (A rep for Love disputed Jardine’s characterization of the lawsuit, noting the dispute was technically between Jardine and Brother Records.)

They briefly put aside their differences in 2012 for a Beach Boys reunion tour, but that ended in acrimony when Love carried on with his own version of the group that didn’t include Jardine or Brian Wilson. “He cut us right off there,” Jardine told Rolling Stone in 2025. “We were hopefully going to continue on doing some more, but he insisted on going back to his handpicked band, and basically left Brian and I in the dust.”

In subsequent years, Jardine and Love only came together at Brian Wilson’s funeral and a handful of promotional events commemorating significant anniversaries or the 2024 Disney+ Beach Boys documentary. Earlier this year, Love’s health took a serious decline, even as the Beach Boys continued to tour. Love hasn’t been seen in public since the Nashville concert on October. Their remaining 2026 concerts have been cancelled, though a new group called John Stamos and the California Sound, which included the Beach Boys touring band, substituted for them at six mini-concerts over two nights at Epcot Center earlier this month.

Jardine, meanwhile, is continuing to play shows with veteran members of Brian Wilson’s backing group that he calls the Pet Sounds Band. They play a mixture of hits and deep cuts, including the vast majority of the 1977 cult classic The Beach Boys Love You. Their tour resumes December 4 at Ruth Eckerd Hall in Clearwater, Florida.
