Travis Scott & Model Haylee Mack Spotted Together at L.A. Hotspots

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Travis Scott and model Haylee Mack were spotted out and about at two different L.A. hotspots in one night.

In the photos taken around 1 AM Friday morning ... Travis and Haylee left the iconic Chateau Marmont to get some sushi over at the historic Yamashiro, which overlooks Hollywood.

While Travis wore a black shirt, gold necklaces, and some shades ... Haylee donned a black dress for their late-night meal.

The two were at the Chateau attending GQ's Men of the Year party Thursday night -- which was a star-studded event that featured Matt Damon, Andrew Garfield, Chris Rock, Tinashe and more.

They seemed to be having a great time separately before linking up ... in one pic, Haylee dances the night away, and in another, Travis is all smiles.
