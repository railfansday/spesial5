Harry Styles, Billie Eilish, Cher to Headline Glastonbury 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Harry Styles, Billie Eilish, Cher, and Sam Fender will headline the 2027 edition of Glastonbury. It marks Eilish’s second time at the top of the Pyramid Stage, while Styles will be making his debut appearance at Glastonbury. Cher, who has not toured since 2020, is performing in the traditional legends slot on Sunday night.

Other artists on the bill include Massive Attack, Sombr, Lily Allen, the XX, Robyn, Chvrches, Kacey Musgraves, Lola Young, and Adéla. The festival will run June 23-27, 2027 at Worthy Farm in Pilton, U.K.

“We are so happy to be coming back after our fallow year and, as we head into winter after that peaceful period, we wanted to give you an early musical taster of what’s to come at Glastonbury 2027 with this spread of incredible artists. Glastonbury 2027 comes after a customary fallow year at Worthy Farm. Next year’s festival will mark the first time that ticket prices have broken the £400 mark at £408, an increase from £378.50 in 2025. Tickets sold out instantly when they went on sale on Oct. 4.

Although more acts are set to be announced, the lineups puts to bed rumors that Oasis and Madonna might headline the festival. The Queen of Pop is instead heading to Coachella in April, while Oasis have their own global tour that kicks off in May.
