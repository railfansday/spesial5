Cardinals officially place Jeremiyah Love, Kendrick Bourne on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Cardinals made the move of running back Jeremiyah Love to injured reserve official on Tuesday. He will miss at least the next four games with his new ankle injury.

Love missed two games with a left ankle injury before returning against the Cowboys on Sunday. He now has a high ankle sprain in his right ankle.

Wide receiver Kendrick Bourne also went on the injured reserve list Tuesday. He has a broken tibia that will end his season.

The Cardinals signed rookie wide receiver Harrison Wallace III from the practice squad to the active roster in a corresponding move.

The Cardinals also re-signed veteran tight end Rivaldo Fairweather to the practice squad. Fairweather, who was with the team in training camp, replaces practice squad tight end Kenny Yeboah. Yeboah, who was elevated for Sunday’s game, went on the IR/practice squad on Tuesday.

The Cardinals released defensive lineman Cam Horsley from the practice squad.
