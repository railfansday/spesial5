Aaron Rodgers heaps praise on Steeler Max Hurleman after strong game

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Not all the news around the Pittsburgh Steelers was bad on Sunday. Everyone is talking about DK Metcalf struggling and then getting ejected, but on the flip side of it, rookie Germie Bernard and practice-squad call-up Max Hurleman had strong performances.

Hurleman got the call in favor of Kaden Wejen, who was a healthy scratch for the first time this season. Hurleman finsihed with six receptions on seven targets for 61 receiving yards and a receiving touchdown and drew the praise of quarterback Aaron Rodgers.

"I've been a big Max fan since last year, so nice to see him get an opportunity," Rodgers said. "He dealt with some kind of crazy kicks on the kickoff. That guy's damn good at it. But he's a damn good route runner, and I thought he played his butt off, and I'm very proud of him."

After Sunday's performance, we expect to see Hurleman on the roster for the remainder of the season.
