Trent Williams breaks down in helmet-throwing rage after Colton McKivitz’s gruesome injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

San Francisco left tackle Trent Williams couldn't contain his emotions after seeing his teammate Colton McKivitz's gruesome lower leg injury he sustained during the 49ers' 35-10 rout of the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City on Sunday.

McKivitz's ankle got tangled under a pile at the end of a run by running back Christian McCaffrey in the second quarter and it was pointing the wrong way.

Williams walked over to McKivitz and looked at the injury as he was being treated by the medical staff, and immediately spiked his helmet on the field.

The NBC broadcast focused on a closeup of Williams, who was visibly emotional with his hands on his head.

Williams hugged McKivitz before he was carted off of the field with his leg in an air cast.

"As he's on the cart, Trent's emotions as he saw what happened right next to McKivitz," Tirico said. "Remember, these guys sit right next to each other in meetings. They line up next to each other every day — they're teammates, they're brothers."

Several players took a knee, including McCaffrey.

McKivitz suffered what is believed to be a season-ending fractured and dislocated left ankle — similar to the injury that 49ers linebacker Fred Warner sustained that ended his 2025 season.

"We think it's broken and dislocated, similar probably to Fred [Warner]'s last year," 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan said. "That was really crushing."

McKivitz sustained the injury with 6:38 left in the second quarter and the 49ers leading the Vikings 14-10.

Vederian Lowe replaced him as San Francisco secured the win and improved to 9-1.

McKivitz, 30, was in the midst of a career year as a starter on the Niners' offensive line after he signed a three-year, $45 million deal this offseason.

He also hadn't missed a game as a full-time starter since 2023.

The 49ers host the defending Super Bowl champion Seahawks (6-4) at Levi's Stadium on Sunday.
