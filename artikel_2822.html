Florida Tops Notre Dame in Players Era 8 Quarterfinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The No. 1 Florida Gators improved to a perfect 5-0 by taking down Notre Dame, 86-78.

As part of the Players Era 8 tournament, the Gators punched their ticket to the semifinals thanks to a 24-point bench effort from Urban Klavzar.

Florida is still undefeated this year, though the Fighting Irish put up a good fight in the second half.

Todd Golden's squad enjoyed a comfortable 47-32 lead at halftime, but there were points in the final 20 minutes when things got a bit dicey.

In the end, Florida prevailed, and now knows they'll be taking on Houston, who beat Rutgers earlier in the day.

At the very least, Florida is now guaranteed a spot in the 3rd place game as all eight teams will play three games from the Michelob Ultra Arena in Las Vegas, Nevada.

Winning the rebound battle always helps in a game like this, but the Gators absolutely blew the Fighting Irish out of the water.

Florida ended this game with 38 rebounds, an astonishing 16 more than Notre Dame finished with.

The Gators put on an absolute clinic in a game where they grabbed 15 offensive rebounds. Those second-chance opportunities made a huge difference, especially since Notre Dame only had four offensive boards themselves.

Shockingly, Florida turned the ball over 14 times compared to Notre Dame's 10. The Fighting Irish scored 14 points off those 14 turnovers, and Florida only scored eight points off the Fighting Irish's 10.

Despite losing the turnover battle, Florida still came out of the first day of the Players Era 8 tournament with an eight-point victory.

Somehow, one Gators player had more bench points than the entire Fighting Irish unit.

Notre Dame got a solid 21 minutes of production out of Jonathan Sanderson, who finished with 14 points, but it was nothing compared to Klavzar's 24.

The Fighting Irish had just 23 bench points as an entire team, so those players all combined to be one shy of the Slovenian senior.

Elsewhere, Alex Condon had a double-double with 19 points and 10 rebounds. Thomas Haugh finished one board short as he put up 14 points in this one.

The only other Gators player to finish in double digits was Denzel Aberdeen who added 13 points.

It's extremely notable that Boogie Fland only played eight minutes in this one, so his condition will be one to monitor throughout the tournament.
