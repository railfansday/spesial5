Sixers look to get back on track as they resume NBA cup play vs. Miami

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It appears the Sixers' next test without Joel Embiid will be a challenging one. Philadelphia hosts the Miami Heat the Wednesday before Thanksgiving, tipping off at 5 p.m. to start an entire evening of NBA Cup action. Miami leads Eastern Conference Group B at 2-0 coming into this one. The Sixers are in third place at 1-1 and have a minus-19 point differential.

The Heat have gotten out to a lead in the group while riding a seven-game winning streak entering play today. After a slow start to the season, their average margin of victory has been 11.2 points over those seven games. Like the Sixers, this new Heat team hasn't looked quite as expected to start the year. They're 6th in points scored per 100 possessions but 17th in points allowed per CleaningTheGlass.

They come to town at a bad time for the Sixers, likely to be without Joel Embiid for the second straight game. Embiid is listed as doubtful with an illness. For Miami, their big acquisition Giannis Antetekounmpo is questionable with groin tendinitis. Antetekounmpo did miss their last game against the Oklahoma City Thunder, but seems set to return.

The pairing of Antetekounmpo and Bam Adebayo projects to give an undersized Sixers' frontcourt plenty of problems. In the six games Antetekounmpo has played during this winning streak, he's averaged 29.5 points per game with a 68.4% effective field goal percentage.

Dominick Barlow is probably the best option to guard Giannis, but the Sixers have yet to give him a heavy workload since returning from his back injury, and for good reason. After playing 11 minutes in his debut, Barlow did get up to 16 in his second game back, but was favoring his back after taking a couple of spills.

The Heat have been hot from outside as of late, shooting 39% from three or better in each of their las three games. That could be a problem for a Sixers team that has not been sharp defending the point of attack as of late. The good news is after shipping out some of their younger wings, namely Jaime Jacquez Jr., this Miami team isn't the offensive rebounding machine they have been in years past. They rank 21st so far this season in offensive rebounding percentage.

The key for the Sixers might be a big game for Tyrese Maxey, who's been quiet for his standards offensively. In his last three games he's averaged 21 points per game.

Jaylen Brown did look fantastic in the first half of his first game back. He shot 8-of-10 from the floor for 19 points, easily getting to whatever spot on the floor he wanted. When he picked up his fourth foul he had to sit for nearly the entire third quarter and that greatly disrupted his rhythm. Brown didn't make a field goal in the second half and admitted after the game he felt physically different.

Getting more of that first half Brown would greatly help the Sixers' chances against Miami, but more importantly, Sixers fans should hope that isn't a sign of Brown's hamstring injury lingering.
