Harrison Armstrong passes Stones and Barkley in Everton's teen PL starts list

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Harrison Armstrong during Everton's 3-1 win over Coventry City

Harrison Armstrong has now surpassed John Stones and Ross Barkley in terms of Premier League starts for Everton as a teenager (16), though I recently stumbled across a statistic online revealing I actually top that list with 61, a full 21 ahead of Wayne Rooney in second place.

It genuinely caught me off guard, as it's something I was completely unaware of. I can't help but wonder, had I been best mates with Walter Smith and he hadn't brought in three other left-backs in David Unsworth, Alessandro Pistone and Gary Naysmith, just how high those numbers could have climbed.

That's one of the genuine upsides of social media — you discover things about yourself you never knew. It was a welcome little positive and gratifying to see my name at the summit of that list, particularly given the caliber of players below me who went on to forge outstanding careers.

Armstrong is getting his chance. I don't believe Harrison will be taken aback, but he'll certainly appreciate the volume of playing time he's receiving and the level of confidence David Moyes is placing in him.

At the beginning of the season, there was genuine uncertainty over whether he'd head out on loan again. Evertonians were eager to see more of him, but ultimately it came down to his dedication throughout pre-season, his work in training, and the quality he's consistently demonstrated, reports the Liverpool Echo.

Performing well every single week isn't realistic, and that's something every young player experiences. I recall numerous matches where I walked off the field thinking I couldn't remember doing anything particularly good or bad.

Getting on the ball is the key priority. Harrison operates in the engine room, giving him every opportunity to make that happen.

Playing at left-back limited my own ability to get on the ball as much as I would have preferred.

He has demonstrated real leadership qualities at a young age. That booking he received for what I considered an outstanding tackle against Chelsea galvanized the crowd and the entire Everton squad — he was stepping up and taking responsibility.

Harrison has the tools in his arsenal, and with each additional game comes greater experience, greater knowledge, and continued development. You absorb lessons from the best players around you in the locker room and incorporate that into your own game.

This is a tremendous moment for the academy. We've endured years of frustration watching talented players denied their shot, and in some cases, we've allowed them to leave.

Seeing one of our brightest prospects finally given his opportunity — and seizing it — is exactly what this club needed.
