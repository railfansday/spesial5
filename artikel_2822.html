J.J. McCarthy tosses TD in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

New York Giants QB J.J. McCarthy completed 18 of his 38 passes for 173 yards and a touchdown and rushed seven times for 33 yards against the Jacksonville Jaguars in Week 11.

Why it Matters: McCarthy has yet to throw for more than one touchdown in a game this season, but he has been providing 20-plus rushing yards on a routine basis. McCarthy hasn't shown much upside, but he has been doing just enough to be in the low-end QB2 mix in Week 12 when the Giants face the Colts.
