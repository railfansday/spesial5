State of the NFC South: Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The gap between first and second place is widening, and Week 10 featured one of the biggest NFC South matchups of the year.

The Bucs were on their bye week, and after not playing, they are in second place in the division. Next week, the Bucs will be facing the Lions, but QB Baker Mayfield's availability is up in the air.

To our only winner of the week, the Panthers are now the only team with four wins in the division and sit atop the NFC South. With a mixture of a very inexperienced referee group, some bad penalties, and horrible pass blocking, this team should have lost. But the Saints couldn't take advantage of anything, and the Panthers were just the better team on Sunday.

Their next game will be against the Ravens, who are without Lamar Jackson. Eventhough Carolina's defense did well against the Saints' RBs, this is still a group that ranks very low in the NFL in run defense, and that's a worrying stat while facing Derrick Henry.

Atlanta had a really bad week. They were forced to go away from Bijan Robinson in this matchup against the Kansas City Chiefs, and eventhough Michael Penix finished with 286 passing yards, the passing game couldn't score a touchdown. The defense is the real issue in this game, allowing Patrick Mahomes to throw for 348 yards and 5 touchdowns. Allow is probably not the correct word here because we are talking about Mahomes, but this was his best game of the season so far.

The Falcons will be on their bye week next.

And to the Saints. Ugh is the best word I can use to describe the Saints right now. Two things couldn't happen this season: The team being as bad and Tyler Shough regressing. Well, both are true right now. Kellen Moore, even after firing T.J. Paganetti, still hasn't figured out the run game, even against the Panthers' run defense. If it wasn't for Brandon Staley and this pass rush, the Saints would have lost this game by 30, even with all the positive calls they got from the refs.

As for Shough, he is playing with a broken hand and is probably playing as bad as we have seen from him. He is missing wide-open throws, not seeing wide-open receivers, and is playing scared in the pocket. This is why it was so important for Moore to figure out the run game and offensive line this past offseason. Keeping Shough a bit more comfortable in the pocket while also freeing up the passing game would cause Shough to improve. We have seen what Shough can do, and that's good enough for him to be a starter, but he needs a new head coach.
