Raiders-49ers game at Levi's Stadium resulted in 11 arrests and 45 ejections

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The last time the 49ers hosted the Raiders in a regular-season game at Levi’s Stadium, there were 32 arrests. This time, the number dropped significantly.

Via Matt Barrows of The Athletic, there were 11 arrests during and after the Week 9 game between Las Vegas and San Francisco. There were also 45 ejections.

It was the first Raiders regular-season game played in the Bay Area since they moved from Oakland (for the second time) after the 2019 season.

Per Barrows, six arrests involved public intoxication, four entailed disturbing the peace by fighting or challenging someone to a fight, and one involved possession of narcotics.

It’s unclear whether any of the arrests were connected to the parking-lot brawl that was captured on video.
