49ers announce Mac Jones will start vs. Seahawks, Brock Purdy is out

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Mac Jones will start at quarterback for the 49ers on Sunday against the Seahawks.

Brock Purdy, who is an MVP candidate this season, has not practiced all week with an ankle injury, and on Friday, head coach Kyle Shanahan confirmed that Purdy has been ruled out. Shanahan said Jones is ready to go and will play well on Sunday.

"Mac's been awesome," Shanahan said. "He's had a great week. We went through this with him last year so everyone's got a ton of confidence in Mac. Mac's had a hell of a camp and he's the same guy every day. It's been a good week with Mac."

Last year Jones started eight games while Purdy was hurt, and although Jones didn't play as well as Purdy, he did play well enough for the 49ers to go 5-3 in those eight games.

Now the 49ers will hope Jones can play well enough to beat the defending Super Bowl champions in a game that has major implications for the NFC playoff race.
