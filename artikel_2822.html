Pat Bryant is all the way back; Talanoa Hufanga listed as questionable

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Head coach Sean Payton announced that wide receiver Pat Bryant has progressed enough to play on Sunday against the Carolina Panthers and will be removed from the injured reserve list. That is huge news for the Denver Broncos.

Also questionable are edge rusher Dondrea Tillman and safety Talanoa Hufanga. The Broncos released safety Taylor Rapp, so I'm not sure if that means Hufanga is a go or not. The team officially ruled out cornerback Kris Abrams-Draine, Brandon Jones, and Eyioma Uwazurike. Heading into the bye week, those guys will have a little extra time to get things right before Week 11.
