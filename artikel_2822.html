Buffalo Bills elevate CB Walker from practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Buffalo Bills have elevated rookie cornerback Kani Walker from the practice squad ahead of tonight's game against the Kansas City Chiefs.

Walker, 23, was also elevated for Buffalo's lone loss of the season, a 29-26 defeat at the hands of the New England Patriots back in Week Four. Walker did not see a snap on defense during that game, but he did make one tackle on 11 special teams snaps.

During the preseason, Walker was outstanding for the Bills. He consistently impressed during training camp practices before playing lights-out during the exhibition slate. Walker had 3 tackles, 2 pass breakups, and 1 interception in Buffalo's three preseason games. Ultimately, he did not make the 53-man roster, but he has remained with the team's practice squad throughout this season. He has one elevation remaining before the Bills would have to sign him to their 53-man roster in order to have him appear on game day.

With Walker's elevation, that might give a hint as to the availability of top corner Christian Benford, who missed Sunday's game with a groin injury. Veteran Tre'Davious White came up from the practice squad to replace Benford in that contest. Joining White last Sunday was linebacker Shaq Thompson, who replaced Dorian Williams at linebacker. Given that Walker is the only elevation, it's safe to assume that Williams will be ready to roll tonight.

Does Walker's elevation mean that Benford is definitely out? We'll find out when inactives become official 90 minutes before kickoff, which should be right around 6:50 p.m. EDT. Hopefully, our collective turkey-induced food comas will be wearing off by then, and you'll be able to check back with us before the game begins at 8:20 p.m. EDT.
