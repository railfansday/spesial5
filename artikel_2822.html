MMBets Game of the Week: The Dallas Mavericks look to course-correct against the reeling Golden State Warriors

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Mavericks face the Golden State Warriors at home on Wednesday night. It will be the first meeting between former Michigan teammates Morez Johnson Jr. and Yaxel Lendeborg, but the story will be about both of these teams righting the ship. The Warriors have dropped their last two games without Stephen Curry and did not eclipse 100 points in either contest. The Mavericks have lost three in a row in embarrassing fashion and have to get back on track against a bad Warriors team. It should be a "get right" game for Dallas, if they can muster any effort at all.
