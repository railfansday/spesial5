Lions agree to acquire S Andre Cisco from Jets

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Detroit is bringing in some secondary help.

The Jets are trading safety Andre Cisco to the Lions in exchange for a 2028 sixth-round pick, ESPN’s Adam Schefter reports.

Cisco, 26, is in the final season of his contract.

Cisco has appeared in nine games with four starts this year, recording 23 total tackles with two tackles for loss, a sack, a forced fumble, and a pass defensed.

A third-round pick in the 2021 draft, Cisco played his first four seasons with the Jaguars.

In 80 career games with 59 starts, he’s tallied eight interceptions, 26 passes defensed, 2.5 sacks, and six tackles for loss.

With the trade, Cisco is now in line to potentially play 18 games this season. The Lions had their bye in Week 6 and the Jets are not slated to have theirs until Week 13.
