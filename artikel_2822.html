Pearl-Cohn football seniors win final game at home for coach's 200th victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Pearl-Cohn quarterback LJ Funk's body language wasn't translating.

The Toledo-bound senior's face wasn't wearing the look of a kid who just led his team to a TSSAA football Class 4A state semifinal game for the third consecutive season.

He had just thrown for 162 yards and four touchdowns on 10-for-13 passing during the two quarters he played during the Firebirds' 48-14 quarterfinal rout against Marshall County on Nov. 20.

And it turned out to be coach Tony Brunetti's 200th career victory at the school.

But, thanks to Lexington's 7-3 win against Springfield, it was the last game Funk and the team's other seniors would ever play at Pearl-Cohn.

Funk found out his team would travel to Lexington for its TSSAA Division I Class 4A state semifinal on Nov. 27 during the waning minutes of the victory.

"We were on the sideline. It's an interception (late in the Lexington-Springfield game)," said Funk said. "I'm like, 'Oh, man. It's over with.'

"That hit me. It was a bunch of emotions. We just won and I'm over here like we lost."

Funk thought back to his sophomore season and fought back tears as he sat far outside the Firebirds' postgame huddle. He thought about his first start at Firebirds Stadium. Remembered how he felt than. Remembered to remember how he felt in this moment.

"It's our last ride," Funk said of the team's seniors. "It's a weird feeling. I'm excited to play football next week, so I can't wait."

Pearl-Cohn senior Landan Left, a Western Kentucky commit and Mr. Do It All, embraced Funk on the sideline shortly after the news landed there and shared the same sentiment.

"Last game at Pearl," said Left, who had three carries for 81 yards and a touchdown to go with two catches for 40 yards and a touchdown while playing defense in his two quarters on the field. "That hurts a little."

"Not as much as it should, because we still have more football to play," Left said. "But they took me in my 10th-grade year. They made this a home for me. Knowing it's my last game (on this field), there's some emotions behind it. But we have to keep our head up and keep playing football."

The Firebirds will do so against Lexington, a team that plays nearly two hours to the west of Pearl-Cohn. A team that is 13-0 this season compared to Pearl's 10-3.

Funk, Left and the team's other seniors are not on an unfamiliar stage.

The group feels like it has one more step left to make: A state championship.

When the seniors were sophomores, the Firebirds fell in a state semifinal.

Last season, the Firebirds lost to Alcoa in the championship game.

"I'm not trying to speak two weeks in advance," Funk said. "But after we beat Lexington, take care of business going to state, that's our last game ever of high school football."

"I feel like we have the team to do it," Left said. "And we're going to do it."
