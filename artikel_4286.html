Wuthering Waves, Azur Lane, Chaos Zero Nightmare - We've Gacha Covered

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Love 'em or hate 'em, gacha RPGs are everywhere - so much so that they dominate our mailboxes on most days since they're just filled to the brim with all sorts of shiny new things (and I do love shiny new things). Whether it's a fancy SSR character joining the lineup or a cool new region to explore, we've got a nifty list of updates here where we go through what's caught our eye so far!  Wuthering Waves I feel like it's only been a week since we had an update for Wuthering Waves, but Kuro Games seems keen on spoiling us with yet another massive update announcement for the action-packed RPG. In particular, Version 4.0 will be dropping on December 22nd just in time for Christmas, so if you've been looking for new Resonators to add to your lineup during the holidays, this is it!

Even more exciting is that a whopping seven new characters will be joining the fray, with more narratives dropping throughout 2027. It's all about a Western-inspired world this time around, so it's safe to say Wuthering Waves is ending the year with a bang.

Wuthering Waves Wizardry Variants Daphne If you're more interested in plunging into dark dungeons before the year ends, Wizardry Variants Daphne has launched its latest update with a New Alternate Style Adventurer: Clearwater Bride Lanavaille. Featured Adventurers include Chronicle of Battlefront Heroes [Season 2], while there's a brand-new facility for you to develop your heroes at the Hall of Apprentice Training.

The aforementioned featured banner includes Rinne, Shelirionach, Raffaello, and Erwin, with each one enjoying a boosted 1.25% rate from the summons pool - and the best part of it all is that this'll run until January 13th next year.

Azur Lane Arknights Speaking of collabs, Arknights is welcoming a pretty massive one as literally massive monsters join the fray with its "Thunder in the Azure Dream" Monster Hunter crossover. With the arrival of the popular Capcom franchise comes new Operators and themed stages until December 7th, plus a special limited-time login event where two "Song of the Bow, Leap to the Sky Headhunting Permits" will be up for grabs. The former allows you to experiment with your tactics without the pressure of the rankings, while the latter will keep your competitive spirit burning all throughout!

Nine collab-exclusive dungeons are available for you to take on, so once you've strategised the best lineup for your squad, you can put them to the test accordingly - and with free pulls from the Bleach Egg Machine at that. No better reason to draw your Zanpakuto, I'd say.
