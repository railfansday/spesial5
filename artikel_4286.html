Hyundai’s new Kona N Line is already out testing [Video]

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Hyundai isn’t just giving its compact SUV a fresh coat of paint. The next Kona is getting a major overhaul, and now we’ve caught an early glimpse of the N Line variant.

The Kona is getting an upgrade. Hyundai launched the second-generation Kona in 2023, so like most of its vehicles, a mid-cycle refresh is due around next year.

Apparently, that won’t be the case. Hyundai appears to be skipping the typical mid-cycle refresh for the third-generation Kona, with plans for a complete redesign.

Several disguised test vehicles have been spotted over the past few months in the US, Europe, and South Korea with a noticeably different design. The test mules sit higher off the ground and have a much more commanding, SUV-like shape, close to the new Santa Fe, Palisade, and Tucson.

Two versions have been captured thus far: a standard Kona and a more rugged XRT variant. For the first time, the new Kona N Line was spotted in Korea by HealerTV on a car carrier.

Since it’s still covered and an early-stage prototype, it’s hard to see many changes outside of the exclusive wheels and the added lower side skirt.

The new Kona N-Line is expected to receive other exclusive N Line design elements, including a more aggressive, sporty front and rear design with unique bumpers.

The early prototype sighting suggests Hyundai is keeping the N Line trim in the third-gen Kona lineup.

Hyundai skipped the 2026 Kona Electric in the US but said it would return for the 2027 model year, when the third-generation model is expected to arrive.

Like the current generation, the new Kona is expected to be available in gas, hybrid, and fully electric powertrains. It’s expected to debut in 2027 as a 2028 model year. Earlier this month, a prototype was captured in Korea that was expected to be the EV version (see the images here).
