Derrick Henry cleared after concussion evaluation

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Ravens running back Derrick Henry exited today's game early to get checked for a concussion, but the evaluation provided good news.

Henry was cleared and will not be in the NFL concussion protocol this week.

The back of Henry's head hit the ground on a 15-yard touchdown run in the fourth quarter, and he did not return to the game after that. He went into the locker room with the medical staff and hadn't been cleared by the time the game ended.

Although Henry's touchdown gave the Ravens a 33-32 lead, the Panthers scored a field goal on the ensuing possession and won 35-33.
