Drake Maye’s wife Ann Michael gets emotional in first message since injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The New England Patriots are still holding their breath regarding the ankle injury to Drake Maye. The star quarterback injured his ankle after a sack by Detroit Lions star Aidan Hutchinson, and he is currently out indefinitely.

New England is fearful that Maye suffered a fractured ankle, but there has been no official word.

While the NFL world waits for an update on the Maye front, his wife, Ann Michael Maye, shared a heartwarming message on social media for the first time since the Patriots signal-caller went down.

Mrs. Maye posted a photo of the two sharing a sideline kiss with the message, "[I love you] always," along with a heart emoji.
