How the ATP and WTA top 10 looked when GTA V was released compared to now

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Grand Theft Auto (GTA) VI has finally been released after a 13-year wait since GTA V, when the ATP and WTA top 10s were filled with legendary players.

It has arguably been the most highly anticipated wait for a video game ever, with users now able to enjoy all that the exciting experience has to offer. The release coincides with a significant update in the tennis world, with the WTA year-end rankings having just been confirmed following the conclusion of the WTA Finals. The ATP will soon follow suit after the ATP Finals, which are currently underway in Turin.

But how did the ATP and WTA top rankings look when the previous edition of the famous video game was brought into the world? Tennis Gazette looks at the top 10 men's and women's players when GTA V was released on 17th September 2013.

GTA V was released on a Tuesday, one day after the ATP rankings received an update. But there was only one change that week, with Roger Federer climbing one spot ahead of Tomas Berdych into fifth.

Ahead of them, a 26-year-old Novak Djokovic topped the standings, followed immediately by fellow icons Rafael Nadal and Andy Murray. David Ferrer was in fourth place, with Juan Martin del Potro, Jo-Wilfried Tsonga and Stan Wawrinka completing a legendary top 10.

Meanwhile, the WTA Tour top 10 was equally iconic. Aged 31, Serena Williams was ranked as the world number one, with Victoria Azarenka in second and Maria Sharapova in third.

Agnieszka Radwanska and Li Na completed the top five back then. And ranked sixth to 10th respectively were Sara Errani, Marion Bartoli, Caroline Wozniacki, Angelique Kerber and Jelena Jankovic.

Fast forward 13 years to the release of GTA VI and the ATP and WTA top 10s currently contain another group of iconic figures, and undoubtedly some future legends. Recently crowned world number one Alexander Zverev tops the men's list, followed by Jannik Sinner in second.

Carlos Alcaraz is third, Ben Shelton fourth and Daniil Medvedev is fifth. They are joined in the latest ATP top 10 by Flavio Cobolli, Arthur Fils, Frances Tiafoe, Taylor Fritz and Rafael Jodar. Remarkably, having featured in the list back in 2013, Serbian legend Djokovic is currently just two spots outside the top 10 in 12th.

On the WTA Tour, like Zverev, Elena Rybakina recently climbed to world number one for the first time in her career. Meanwhile, WTA Finals champion Mirra Andreeva is the new world number two after her stunning run in Indian Wells.

Aryna Sabalenka has fallen one spot to third, followed by American pair Coco Gauff and Jessica Pegula in fourth and fifth respectively. Completing the current WTA top 10 are Elina Svitolina, Linda Noskova, Karolina Muchova, Iga Swiatek and Marta Kostyuk.

Most WTA stars will now be lying low after another demanding campaign, but work will soon begin for the 2027 season. And the top 10 could be in for a big shakeup early in the year, with Andreeva possibly overtaking Rybakina to rise to world number one. Meanwhile, Swiatek could be eyeing an early rankings rise too, with the Pole already back in pre-season training after ending her 2026 campaign early.
