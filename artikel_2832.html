After a dismal start to their season, the Orlando Magic (5-7) have figured a few things out of late as they prepare for tonight's game against the leaders in the Eastern Conference, the Boston Celtics (11-2) tonight in Beantown. Dealt an early blow when Paolo Banchero suffered a high ankle sprain in Game 2 this season, the Magic have reeled off three wins in a row over the last week to climb into the Top 8 in the East. Led by Jayson Tatum and Paul George, the Celtics have won six in a row over the last two weeks. The foundation of their success is their defense. Boston is allowing only 102.4 points per game

Orlando comes in after a 107-99 victory over the Milwaukee Bucks, a performance highlighted by 35 points, nine rebounds, and 12 assists from Franz Wagner. Orlando's defense held Milwaukee to 39 percent shooting and forced 16 turnovers. Wagner has emerged as the centerpiece of the offense, averaging 24.4 points, 6.8 rebounds and 8.3 assists, while Desmond Bane is averaging 18.3 points and shooting 40.9 percent from three. Wendell Carter Jr. is averaging 9.3 rebounds per game. With Banchero expected to miss upwards of another month, the Magic will need those three to continue to churn out numbers if they are to remain even remotely relevant in the playoff race.

Boston, meanwhile, is coming off perhaps its most dominant performance of the young season, a 131-94 rout of the Dallas Mavericks on Sunday. Hey led 42-9 at the end of the first quarter. That followed wins over Toronto, Philadelphia, Milwaukee and Charlotte. The Celtics have held five of their last six opponents to 102 points or fewer. Tatum is healthy and remains the headliner, averaging 26.8 points, 6.1 rebounds and 7.3 assists, while George is averaging 20.6 points. Unlike Orlando, Boston enters tonight with no injuries listed, giving the Celtics a significant depth advantage as they try to earn their seventh win without a loss at home this season.

These teams have already met once this season, with Boston earning a 108-97 victory in Orlando on to begin the month. Tatum controlled that game with 32 points, while Tristan da Silva led Orlando with 23 and Wagner was held to just 14. That result provides the blueprint for tonight: Orlando needs Wagner, Bane and Carter to generate enough offense against an elite Boston defense, particularly with Banchero unavailable, while Boston will look to turn its superior depth and defensive efficiency into another comfortable result.
