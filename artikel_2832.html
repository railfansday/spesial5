PSG forward Doué out for months due to ligament rupture

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Paris Saint-Germain will be without forward Désiré Doué after he ruptured the anterior cruciate ligament in his right knee.

The Champions League title holders said in a statement on Sunday that Doué will undergo surgery before the end of the month.

During Saturday's Ligue 1 match against Nice, Doué fell to the ground shortly before half-time without sustaining any heavy contact. He scored PSG's second goal in the 3-2 win.

His injury is a major blow to coach Luis Enrique, who is already without forward Khvicha Kvaratskhelia.
