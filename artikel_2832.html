Ex-Notre Dame and current Washington Commanders QB Sam Hartman to be the next 'Bachelor'

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

"The Bachelor" is featuring a quarterback again.

The ABC dating show announced Friday morning that current Washington Commanders practice squad QB Sam Hartman would be the lead on the show's next season. The 30th season of the show premieres on March 21.

Hartman, 27, has been with the Commanders since he went undrafted in the 2024 NFL Draft, largely on the team's practice squad. He has not appeared in a regular season game.

He played collegiately for four seasons at Wake Forest before transferring to Notre Dame for his final season of college football in 2023. Hartman was one of the most productive passers in the country over his final two seasons with the Demon Deacons. In 2021, Wake Forest went 11-3 as Hartman threw for nearly 4,300 yards and 39 touchdowns. He also added 11 rushing TDs that season.

In his only season with the Fighting Irish, Notre Dame went 9-3 as Hartman threw for 24 TDs and eight interceptions.

Hartman is the second NFL QB to lead "The Bachelor" after former Florida and New York Giants quarterback Jesse Palmer. The current ESPN college football analyst — and "The Bachelor" host — was the main character for the show's first season in 2004. Palmer was in his third season with the Giants at the time of the show and played one more season in the NFL before getting into a media career.

Another former QB and current ESPN analyst, Jordan Rodgers, was on "The Bachelorette" and ended up being chosen by JoJo Fletcher, the season's lead.

Palmer, who got married in 2020, did not meet his future spouse in his time on the show, but Rodgers and Fletcher got married in 2022. Perhaps Hartman will follow their lead. Over 20 couples who met on either "The Bachelor" or "The Bachelorette" are currently together.
