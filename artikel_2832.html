Mike McCarthy had ‘very direct’ meeting to keep Steelers power after shocking Aaron Rodgers accusations

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Mike McCarthy was quick to act for damage control.

The Steelers head coach, who came under fire after a since-released Aaron Rodgers went scorched earth on him on "The Pat McAfee Show" Wednesday, had a "very direct" conversations with the team's leadership following the ongoing drama.

Per multiple reports, McCarthy said he believes he still has the support of the team despite his former quarterback calling him out for snoring during meetings, not putting in an effort with the defense and having brain fog about what team he is leading.

"It was a meeting, very direct, very honest. I appreciated the opportunity to talk through numerous things that have been on their mind. … I'm proud of our leadership," McCarthy said, according to Steelers Now's Alan Saunders.

Pittsburgh Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy speaks during a news conference after an NFL football game in Philadelphia, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Chris Szagola

After McCarthy backed up Rodgers as his quarterback on Monday despite a tough loss to the Eagles on Sunday, the 42-year-old was announced as benched and inactive for Friday's game against the Broncos late Tuesday night.

By Wednesday morning, Rodgers skipped an 8 a.m. team meeting at Pittsburgh's facilities.

He followed that up with a scathing appearance on McAfee's show, which ultimately led to his release from the Steelers.

It was a turn of events that has seemingly left the locker room in shambles, despite McCarthy's confidence.

Players watched Rodgers roast their head coach live from the locker room.

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers (8) checks with head coach Mike McCarthy during a game against the Cincinnati Bengals in Pittsburgh, Sept. 27, 2026. USA TODAY Network via Reuters Connect

Safety DeShon Elliott also signaled to current third-string quarterback Drew Allar that he should stop talking to reporters at such a heightened time.

Mason Rudolph is set to start for the Steelers on Friday and the new QB1 addressed the media on Wednesday as well.

When asked about how the team is handling McCarthy's alleged sleeping in the job, the 31-year-old gave a professional response.

"We have a job to do as players, as men. We're going to love each other and support our coach," the veteran said. "It's pointless for me to play mediator or decipher what was said. It's important to block the noise out, go play football games … and support the black and gold."

Following the team blowup, Rodgers' potential landing spots have dwindled.

The Super Bowl champ is on waivers after being let go and can be claimed by another team up until Thursday at 4 p.m. ET.
