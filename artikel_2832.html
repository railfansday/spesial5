What channel is the NFL game on today? Time, schedule for 'Thursday Night Football'

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Week 10 of the NFL season kicks off with a "Thursday Night Football" showdown at MetLife Stadium, as the Washington Commanders face off against the New York Giants on Nov. 12.

The Commanders appeared to have reversed their fortunes following a difficult start to the season, highlighted by an impressive 27-10 victory over the Philadelphia Eagles in Week 8. However, their momentum stalled in Week 9 with a disappointing 20-13 loss to the Los Angeles Rams. Quarterback Jayden Daniels put up 274 passing yards, but his performance was marred by inconsistency and missed opportunities.

Meanwhile, the NFC East-leading Giants have found their stride, riding a two-game win streak after rebounding from a 34-27 defeat to the New Orleans Saints. In Week 9, the Giants narrowly edged past the Eagles after quarterback Jameis Winston came off the bench for an injured J.J. McCarthy and delivered a touchdown pass that secured a 19-11 victory.
