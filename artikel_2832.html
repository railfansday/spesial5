Bears vs. Saints Injury Report: Kyler Gordon DNP, Jeffery Simmons Limited

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Chicago Bears will take on the New Orleans Saints on Sunday afternoon, and we have both team's first official injury report for the week.

The Bears did not actually practice on Wednesday after holding practices on Monday and Tuesday of this week, so the injury report is merely an estimation. Even so, the injury report is mostly positive for the Bears at this point in the season given how clean it is. Just two players were listed with injury limitations, though both names are key contributors to the Bears defense.

DB Kyler Gordon (shoulder) was listed as DNP. He had previously been hurt in the Bears' win over the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, the team's last game. Reports emerged earlier this week that Gordon was not seen practicing during open availability. Head coach Ben Johnson has stated that Gordon is considered day-to-day with the shoulder injury.

Only one other player was listed as on the report with an injury, and it's a big one as well. DL Jeffery Simmons (knee) was listed as limited. At this point, it's probably nothing to worry about as the weekend gets closer, but we'll also be paying attention to his status as the week continues.

Malik Muhammad, Luther Burden, and Kyle Monangai were all listed as full participants. Burden has cleared concussion protocol.

The Saints' injury report is much more significant. 10 players are on the New Orleans report, including five players as DNP due to injury.

Listed as DNP: CB Martin Emerson, Jr. (concussion), T Taliese Fuaga (knee), RB Alvin Kamara (rest), C Erik McCoy (biceps), S Justin Reid (personal reasons), T Asim Richards (knee), and LB Pete Werner (hamstring).

CB Decamerion Richardson (thigh) and WR Jordyn Tyson (chest) were listed as "limited" in practice.

The Bears take on the Saints Sunday at Soldier Field in Chicago, IL. The game takes place at 12:00 p.m. (Chicago time) and will be broadcast on FOX.
