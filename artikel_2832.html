C.J. Stroud's second TD gives Texans 14-3 lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Texans took advantage of Kamari Lassiter’s interception of Daniel Jones, driving 85 yards in 12 plays for their second touchdown. They lead the Colts 14-3 with 7:38 remaining in the second quarter.

C.J. Stroud hit Kayshon Boutte, who was well covered by Kenneth Harris, for a 24-yard touchdown on third-and-2.

It was Stroud’s second touchdown pass of the night and his 21st of the season.

The Texans have 155 yards, with Stroud going 11-of-13 for 118 yards with two touchdowns and an interception.

He has completed a pass to seven different receivers, led by Nico Collins’ 48 yards on two catches. Collins scored the team’s first touchdown on a 42-yard reception.
