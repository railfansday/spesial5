Thunder vs. Trail Blazers predictions: Odds, recent stats, trends and best bets for November 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

They have already suffered injuries that would derail most teams' seasons and yet the Oklahoma City Thunder (8-2) head to Portland tonight riding a four-game winning streak into Moda Center, while the Trail Blazers sit at 6-3 after dropping two straight. With Chet Holmgren and Jalen Williams sidelined, OKC has relied on its defense. Led by two-time MVP Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, the Thunder is allowing a paltry 104.2 points per game. Damian Lillard is back in Portland after a dismal stint in Milwaukee. He and Deni Avdija lead the Trail Blazers in scoring.

Oklahoma City's latest win was its most lopsided of the season, a 114-75 rout of Washington on Saturday. The Thunder shot 48.9 percent from the field, piled up 55 rebounds and 33 assists, and held the Wizards to just 29.6 percent shooting, including 7-for-43 from three-point range. SGA was his usual efficient self, scoring 30 points on 11-of-20 shooting with nine assists in only 29 minutes. Portland, meanwhile, will try to regroup after a 125-103 home loss to San Antonio on Saturday, its second defeat in as many nights following a 124-118 loss to the Lakers. Those setbacks snapped a three-game winning streak in which Portland had beaten Utah, Denver and Phoenix, including a 132-119 victory over the Suns.

This is the first meeting of the season between these teams. Their most recent meeting came on December 31, 2025, when Oklahoma City cruised past Portland 124-95 at Paycom Center. The Thunder took three of four games against Portland last season also winning 123-115 in Portland on November 30 and 122-95 at home on November 23. The Trail Blazers win in the series last year came was a 121-119 win in Portland on November 5.
