The 10 Most Family-Friendly Ski Resorts in the East (2027)

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Skiing is one of those rare activities that brings generations together. There’s something special about introducing your kids to the slopes, watching them find their ski legs, and eventually keeping up with them as they race ahead of you. It’s a family tradition that never gets old.

If you’re planning a ski getaway with the whole crew, you want a resort that makes it easy for everyone to have a good time, from first-timers to seasoned skiers. Here’s how SKI readers ranked the East Coast’s most family-friendly resorts.
