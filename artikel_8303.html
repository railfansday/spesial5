Continuing on without my dad (Day 163)

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

My dad fell and hurt his knee yesterday. Today, his entire knee swelled up to about twice the size and causes him a lot of pain. For my dad to keep hiking today, doesn’t seem like the smart thing to do. We’re in the 100-mile wilderness, not the best place for stuff like this to happen. We have a slither of reception at the shelter and decided to call Shaw’s to see what our options are and if there are possible bailout points.

They told us there’s a dirt road in 0.5 miles and one in 5 miles where they can pick him up, after that it’s a four day hike to the next possible pick-up. After taking everything into consideration, my dad decided to get off at the 0.5 mile crossing. He’ll rest, see how his knee heals and if everything goes well, he might join us again at the food drop halfway through the 100-mile. I truly hope that works out, but if not, at least we have been smart about it.

I hiked with my dad to the exact pick-up point half a mile off trail. Dreamcatcher and Small Bites said their – hopefully temporary – goodbyes and hiked on. I’ll catch up to them later.

Because there’s no resupplies in the 100-mile, we have had to be smart about food. Shaw’s will do a food drop for us halfway through the 100-mile, but you pay by the bucket. Also, we don’t want to rush through the 100-mile or carry a thousand pounds in our backpacks. We only brought what we really needed. We’re on a pretty tight ration on the final stretch all things considered.

At the pick-up spot, my dad gave me some of his snacks to share with the others, that should cheer them up. With my headphones in and friends to catch, I hiked back to the trail and kept going.

Within an hour, I already encountered them sitting on a rock, with Dreamcatcher playing the ukulele. Even though the terrain is pretty flat, it’s like Northern Pennsylvania; rocky, rooty and very irregular, making the hiking more challenging than we expected.

We encountered multiple perfect swimming holes. It’s a shame that it’s already so cold in Maine, but I really couldn’t resist taking a cold plunge in one of them. We’re all pretty tired, going slow and still have some ground to cover, but we shouldn’t forget to enjoy our experience either. That’s the whole point of us being here.

After seeing more stunning views and soaking it all in, we took a pretty long blue blaze to the shelter at the end of the day. It’s so worth it though. It’s alongside a beautiful lake. Maine truly is mesmerizing.
