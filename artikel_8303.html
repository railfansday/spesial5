Top 10 Resorts in the West for Terrain Variety (2027)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

One of the best things about a ski vacation is never knowing exactly where the day will take you. Maybe you’re cruising groomers in the morning, ducking into the trees after lunch, or convincing your group to try one more steep pitch before the lifts close. The best resorts offer enough variety to keep every run feeling fresh—and every skier in your crew, from first-timers to experts, happy.

If a mountain with seemingly endless options tops your vacation wish list, start with these 10 Western resorts. From wide-open cruisers and playful glades to challenging steeps, bumps, and everything in between, they offer terrain for every ability and mood. These are the resorts SKI readers rated highest for terrain variety in our annual Reader Resort Survey.
