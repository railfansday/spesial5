Spalletti hails Juventus’ team attitude for second-half thumping of Venezia

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Luciano Spalletti says Juventus played like a team in the second half to secure their thumping 4-0 win over Venezia in Serie A.

Juventus were underwhelming in the first half, but blew Venezia away after the break, with the half-time introduction of Nick Woltemade for Kerim Alajbegovic proving to be key.

The German got the scoring started before teeing up Teun Koopmeiners, with Juan Cabal and Randal Kolo Muani also getting on the scoresheet.

Juve eventually finished with 1.8 expected goals but registered just 0.08 of that before the break from their four shots.

But they were a different side after half-time, with this the first time they have scored at least three goals in the second half of a Serie A match since March, when they netted four against Pisa.

"They came on for the second half, making choices, taking decisions, with greater fluidity in the passing, trying to have a team idea," Spalletti told DAZN Italia.

"In the first half, we gave the ball away too cheaply; there was nobody moving from their positions, nobody following the flow of the pass, so it was fundamental the players recognised we were missing something, which we provided in the second half."

This was also Spalletti's 600th Serie A match as a manager, though he did not want to get stuck focusing on the landmark for too long.

"With everything people say, what is your career objective after hitting 600 games?" he said.

"Reaching the 601st is the answer, because anything can happen in football."

Kolo Muani (10 goals in Serie A) is the sixth French player to reach double figures for goals with Juventus in Serie A.

And against no side has he scored more goals in the competition than he has against Venezia (two, also against Como and Empoli).

He took the penalty, which added the late gloss, with Woltemade handing him the ball.

"Last time, he gave me the ball; we both need confidence," Woltemade explained to DAZN.

"When we have the chance to help each other, we help each other. I don't want to act like I'm a good guy who gave him the ball.

"It was normal for me. He's a good guy; he took the penalty, and he took it.

"It's an important win. After the international break, it's always difficult to find the rhythm again.

"We knew it was a tough game, but I think we did quite well in the second half and scored at important moments."
