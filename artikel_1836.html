Rashford returns and Fernandes misses another penalty as Man Utd humbled by Amorim

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Bruno Fernandes missed a penalty for the second successive pre-season friendly as Manchester United were beaten 4-2 by former head coach Ruben Amorim’s AC Milan.

Fernandes, who rolled a spot-kick wide in a shoot-out against Leeds in midweek, was gifted the chance to open his pre-season account in Wroclaw, Poland.

His effort was on target this time, but Milan keeper Lorenzo Torriani dived to his right to beat the shot away.

Fernandes did at least get an assist, taking the second-minute corner which was powerfully headed home by Harry Maguire to give United the lead.

Amorim’s Milan equalised after a defensive mix-up allowed Gonçalo Ramos to break into the area and square for Samuel Chukwueze to roll home.

United were awarded their penalty just before half-time after Koni De Winter tugged back Youri Tielemans, but Torriani guessed correctly to leave Fernandes frustrated.

United retook the lead after Patrick Dorgu pounced on a loose backpass from Filippo Terracciano, but Milan equalised again when Chukwueze’s wayward shot was poked in by Alphadjo Cissè at the far post.

Marcus Rashford, who was frozen out by Amorim and loaned out to Aston Villa and then Barcelona, came on as a substitute on the hour for his first United appearance since returning from Spain.

But Milan went ahead in the 68th minute when Chukwueze’s cross was headed home by Ramos, and three minutes later former Chelsea midfielder Ruben Loftus-Cheek raced through to hit the fourth and cap a performance that underlines United’s need for reinforcements before their season starts in earnest next Saturday, away at Hull City.

United’s pre-season ends in defeat. A wretched second half proves their undoing as Amorim’s Milan score four. Worth reminding that United led this match, twice.

Milan are playing keep-ball in these latter stages and although Mainoo regains possession, his attempt to play forward is cut out and Modric’s outside-of-the-foot pass finds Comotto, who heads straight at Lammens.

Four minutes of added time to be played...

United now raining crosses down on Milan’s box but Torriani equal to it every time.

For the second time this afternoon, Shaw directs a lovely ball into Fernandes’ path in behind Milan’s back line, but the Portugal international can’t bring it under his spell.

Martinez has added a little bit of urgency to United’s build-up. Mbeumo tries to feed Shaw down the line but the ball is cut out.

More substitutions... Diawara, Vladimirov and Comotto enter the field of play in place of Pavlovic, De Winter and Loftus-Cheek.

Big chance for Rashford! Martinez lofts a fantastic ball over the top but Rashford is not quite able to bring it under his spell, with Torriani and De Winter combing to clear their lines.

Milan are now sitting off, settling into a back five as they look to preserve their advantage. Fernandes, Tielemans and Dalot combine well but such has been United’s fortunes this afternoon, the ball ricochets off the referee and play is halted.

Good work from Rashford in the corner to regain possession, but his ball into the box is not picked up by a team-mate.

More changes... Ramos and Musah make way for Camarda and Ricci.

United have looked bright in the opening moments of this half. They win a free-kick midway into Milan’s half. The ball is delivered to the back post and, after some 18-yard-box pinball, a relieved Torriano pounces on it.

Both teams remain unchanged as the second 45 minutes begin.

All square at the break. Tough to know what to make of that half – United looked solid enough and could have taken a lead into the interval had Fernandes converted his penalty, but they failed to create a wealth of clear-cut chances. It will be interesting to see how Carrick approaches this second half.

Milan have enjoyed just a tick over two-thirds of possession, which United have contributed to in part through some lackadaisical passing.

Fernandes misses a spot-kick for the second time in two friendlies! Here, Torriani guesses correctly, diving to his right to palm Fernandes’ strike away.

Manchester United 1 AC Milan 1 (Chukwueze) It’s all a bit messy. Jashari causes Maguire and Santos to crash into each other (Kaka at Old Trafford, anyone?) off a throw-in, Ramos taking over with the United pair out of commission. He finds Chukwueze who finishes the move off at the far post.

Santos clips Loftus-Cheek near the byline and the referee awards Milan a free-kick. Chukwueze cuts the ball back to an unmarked Jashari on the edge of the box, but his side-footed effort flies wide.

Milan supporters unfurl a banner paying tribute to the late, great Franco Baresi, who passed away on July 31 aged 66.

Michael Carrick believes Manchester United can push for the Premier League title, but has underlined the urgent need for signings.

The United head coach has taken heart from his squad’s promising preparations for the new campaign and, speaking at their Co Kildare summer base, he explained why he was confident they could build on last season’s third-place finish.

Carrick has been thrilled to hear United’s players discussing what they might achieve and has dared supporters to dream that they can get closer to last year’s champions Arsenal. But he is also a realist and recognises he is not blessed with options.

So far United have added Youri Tielemans, Andrey Santos and Karl Darlow, a reserve goalkeeper, but their £83.3m spend is significantly less than what newly promoted clubs Ipswich Town (£109.4m) and Coventry City (£92.2m) have invested in their squads.

Amorim’s side have gone intercontinental this summer, travelling from Scotland to Indonesia, via Australia, before arriving in Poland for the last of their four pre-season games.

At his AC Milan unveiling, Amorim was asked about the learnings from his United tenure that he would take into his new role. He said: “The first thing is it’s hard to explain the mistakes because for that I would have to explain the context of the last adventure.

“It’s hard to say to you every mistake. The only thing I can say is that I learned a lot and I made some mistakes.

“I didn’t have the opportunity, and I’m sorry for that, I didn’t have the opportunity to say something to the Manchester United fans at the moment and I’m really proud to be their coach for a year in that time.

“But now it’s a different history. There are a lot of things that I could do better in the last experience but sometimes it’s like that, you need to learn to find the right spot to reach a different level and that’s my thinking here.

“Everyone learns from their experience. I learned a lot, I did some mistakes I will try to change, there are some thing you will never change but I think I will be a better manager, that’s all.”

Welcome to live coverage of Manchester United’s final pre-season match, against AC Milan in Wroclaw, Poland. Michael Carrick’s side will look to apply the finishing touches to a promising summer on the pitch, which has included victories over Rosenborg and Atlético Madrid, plus a draw against European champions Paris St-Germain. The team will fly back to Manchester with the big ones, the ones they have always wanted – the Snapdragon Cup and the Boyle Sports Cup – in tow.

At the Tarczynski Stadium there will be a reunion with former manager Ruben Amorim, who was appointed AC Milan’s head coach in June, five months after being sacked by United. Following a frustrating season under Massimiliano Allegri in which Milan fell out of the Champions League spots on the final day, the Rossoneri said Amorim would bring a “modern dominant tactical approach”. His methods failed to cut through at Old Trafford, though, during a tumultuous 14-month reign which finally ended after the Portuguese “blew up” during a heated meeting with director of football Jason Wilcox. At Milan, Amorim has persisted with the 3-4-2-1 formation that was doomed at United.

Carrick made clear this week that United must strengthen if they are to challenge for titles. So far the club have brought in Youri Tielemans, Andrey Santos and Karl Darlow, but they are still in the market for a left-back and another midfielder. The future of Marcus Rashford has also been a topic of conversation among United fans. The forward has not played for his boyhood club since December 2024 but has joined up with his team-mates in Poland and it remains to be seen if he features in Carrick’s forward planning.

Kobbie Mainoo, who did not play a minute at this summer’s World Cup, and Lisandro Martínez have also joined up with the team this week and could be handed their first minutes of pre-season having been named in the matchday squad. For Milan, new signing Gonçalo Ramos – bought from PSG for £63m – will make his first appearance in the famous red and black.
