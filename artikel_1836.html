QPR youngster Leon Scarlett attracting interest from European giants after joining from Arsenal

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

QPR youngster Leon Scarlett is attracting interest from Eintracht Frankfurt and AC Milan after breaking into the first team this season.

Scarlett, who can play on the left and right wing, has made six Championship appearances for high-flying QPR after joining the club on a scholarship deal from Arsenal’s academy last summer.

The 17-year-old has already been capped by Wales at Under-21 level, and Standard Sport understands several clubs in England and Europe have registered an interest.

Scarlett started for Wales U19s during their 2-2 draw with Finland last week, and it is understood his performance caught the attention of several scouts in attendance.

QPR head coach Julien Stephan gave Scarlett his senior debut last season, bringing him off the bench as the R’s were beaten 3-0 by Ipswich at Portman Road in May.

Scarlett had been in and around the first team before his debut, after impressing for the club’s development teams, and Stephan revealed his performances in first-team training had caught the eye.
