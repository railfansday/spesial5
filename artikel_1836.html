More FA Trophy drama for Eastbourne Borough - win over Chertsey in pictures

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

After needing a penalty shootout to get through their first round tie at Leatherhead three weeks ago, they needed a late Tyano Wilson strike to see off Chertsey Town and avoid another heart-stopping finale from 12 yards.

Wilson stuck with almost the last kick to send Priory Lane wild and put the Sports into Monday’s third round draw.

Earlier Paul McCallum had headed Borough in front before the hosts had Nathan Laleye sent off and Nathan Minhas levelled.
