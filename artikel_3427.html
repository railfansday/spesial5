This New Chrome AI Feature Reads Your Gmail to Make Filling Out Web Forms Easier

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Google Chrome is making it easier to autofill online forms with more than just contact information and credit card numbers. For users with Personal Intelligence enabled, Gemini will now suggest items like order numbers, tracking information, membership IDs, and booking confirmations from Gmail messages when completing forms in Chrome.

Chrome's expanded autofill capabilities use Gemini to read your emails and surface suggestions, meaning you don't have to click back and forth between tabs or manually search for codes or confirmations in your inbox. When you click a form field that has a corresponding piece of data, a pop-up menu appears and indicates that an item is "Suggested by Gemini." You can click this to autofill the suggestion as well as open an additional menu to see the specific source (the original email) or remove the suggestion. The latter doesn't delete anything from Gmail; it simply dismisses the suggestion in Chrome.

As Android Police reports, Google started testing enhanced autofill in Chrome this summer using data from other connected apps like Google Photos. The feature currently rolling out is specific to Gmail and is coming first to U.S. users who have Personal Intelligence enabled on desktop as well as in the Android and iOS apps when using Gemini in Chrome.

In the blog post announcing the enhanced autofill feature, Google states that "sensitive data is encrypted" and that users can manage autofill, including enabling or disabling enhanced suggestions, via Chrome's settings.
