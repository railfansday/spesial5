Anne Hathaway’s 'Jurassic Park' Replacement Officially Dominates Streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Anne Hathaway has had a brilliant run this year. From playing Penelope in Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey, reprising Andy Sachs in The Devil Wears Prada 2, playing a troubled pop star in Mother Mary, to the titular character in Michael Showalter’s Verity. Hathaway’s range is on full display for her fans. However, one of her performances stayed underrated, and it’s in a Jurassic Park replacement film!

The Princess Diaries’ actor isn’t new to the sci-fi genre, having carried the emotional core of Nolan’s Interstellar and black comedy Colossal, and she proves her mettle again in this year’s The End of Oak Street, where she stars alongside Ewan McGregor. Written and directed by David Robert Mitchell, the film explores themes of working together and rediscovering a lost bond. It criticizes the human tendency to prioritize personal comfort and normalize surrounding horrors rather than face harsh truths, while exploring themes of time, evolution, and reinventing oneself.

Set in July 1982, we follow the Platt family — Denise (Hathaway), Greg (McGregor), and their children Audrey and Brian — living in a suburban neighborhood. The family is on the brink of dissolution due to marital strain and financial struggles. However, things change when a mysterious cosmic event involving wormholes rips their entire neighborhood from suburbia and transports it 200 million years into the past. The residents find themselves trapped in a hostile prehistoric landscape overrun by terrifying, aggressive dinosaurs. The Platts then fight to survive and find a portal back home, put aside their differences, and rediscover their bond.

After its theatrical run, the feature released on PVOD and streaming platforms and received its due attention. Currently, The End of Oak Street is the top movie on HBO Max’s global top 10 list. It ranks above titles like Matilda, The Legend of Tarzan, and The Hunger Games franchise. The sci-fi actioner was praised as a suspenseful, frightening, and pulpy creature feature for its practical and CGI visual effects, and Hathaway's strong performance. It grossed $121.6 million worldwide. Critics gave it an 86% Rotten Tomatoes rating, though audiences were stricter with their 76% rating. Check out the feature for Hathaway and McGregor’s compelling performances, a thrilling story, and a brilliant third act. The movie also stars Christian Convery, Maisy Stella, Jordan Alexa Davis, P.J. Byrne, Chris Coy, and more.

The End of Oak Street is streaming on HBO Max. Stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
