Two and a Half Men is entering a new chapter in its history, which will give viewers a fresh way to watch the iconic sitcom.

Between 2003 and 2015, 12 seasons of Two and a Half Men aired on CBS as the series became one of the most successful sitcoms in modern television history. Despite all the drama that surrounded the show at its height, it seemed unlikely that it would continue to garner much attention once its finale aired. However, that has changed, as a new deal involving the Charlie Sheen sitcom has given long-time viewers a reason to talk about the series once again.

Variety has broken the news that all 12 seasons of Two and a Half Men will become available to stream on Pluto TV as of November 20. That marks the first time the sitcom will be available on an on-demand streaming service that is completely free for users to enjoy. Up until now, the show could only be streamed in America on Peacock with a paid subscription, since that was its exclusive home.

The move will bring the Sheen, Jon Cryer, and Ashton Kutcher show to its new home as part of a wider deal between Pluto TV and Warner Bros. TV. While the arrangement is surprising, it makes sense since both companies are now part of the holdings of Skydance Corporation. According to Variety's report, the currently announced series are part of what's being called the "first wave of programming" that will arrive on Pluto TV. That makes it fascinating to consider what other notable TV titles could make the leap in the future. For example, many of Chuck Lorre's other shows could become free to stream, given that most of his series are owned by Warner Bros. TV.

Thanks to the appeal of Two and a Half Men's characters and storylines, it was one of the most popular shows on television at the height of its success. Now, the avid viewers who helped make the sitcom a ratings behemoth will be able to enjoy the show for free in a matter of days on the Pluto TV website and through its app.
