The Expanse’s Official Post-Season 6 Sequel Is An S-Tier Sci-Fi Gem

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Fans of James S.A. Corey’s The Expanse know that the television adaptation, despite its success, never covered the entire original novel series. When the show concluded in 2022, many fans were left wondering what other stories the franchise still had to tell. Fortunately, after a four-year wait, fans can finally revisit that unfinished chapter with two series that, together, serve as an official, direct sequel to the television series.

The Expanse series consists of nine main novels, along with nine short stories and novellas, with Prime Video primarily adapting the first six novels for its television series. Corey's sixth book, Babylon's Ashes, provided the foundation for the show's final season. However, the seventh novel, Persepolis Rising, which Prime Video did NOT adapt, jumps approximately 30 years into the future. That's a substantial gap between the two books, but this missing chapter of the franchise's history has since been filled in with two comic-book series: The Expanse: Dragon Tooth (2023) and The Expanse: A Little Death (2025).

The end of an era is nearly here for Event Horizon, as the franchise prepares to deliever one last epic adventure, closing out its ongoing trilogy.

Together, these two series continue the story after the television show's sixth and final season, serving as official, canon continuations of the TV series' storyline. Overseen by creator James S.A. Corey and developed in collaboration with Alcon Television Group, the studio behind the television adaptation, these sequels are genuine continuations that fit into The Expanse's broader story.

With both sequels now complete, fans can binge them and discover what happens next in the franchise. Better yet, both continuations have earned exceptional reader ratings, making them worthy additions to The Expanse's legacy. For more details on what to expect from these series and where they fit within the franchise's continuity, read on.

Brought to life by superstar comics writer Andy Diggle (Green Lanterns, James Bond) and artist Rubine (Voltron: Legendary Defender), The Expanse: Dragon Tooth (2023) is a 12-part series that picks up directly after the events of the television show's final season, following the crew of the Rocinante. The series fills in the missing years between Babylon's Ashes and Persepolis Rising, exploring a previously untold chapter in the franchise's history.

In this series, fans can expect an epic expansion of The Expanse universe that dives into the secret histories of fan-favorite characters, including Holden, Naomi, Amos, and Bobbie. With this series, The Expanse's story doesn't end with Season 6; it continues in a major way. The series earned a “very positive” rating on League of Comic Geeks, with readers giving it an impressive average score of 4.3 out of 5 stars.

The Expanse: A Little Death (2025–2026), on the other hand, is a four-part canon sequel that picks up directly after Dragon Tooth. Remarkably, it received an even higher rating from fans, earning an exceptional 4.5 out of 5 stars. The series sees Andy Diggle return, this time alongside fellow writer Wes Chatham and artists Francesco Pisa and Sarah Stern.

A Little Death introduces another major adventure for Holden, Amos, and the rest of the Rocinante crew: “Following the fallout of The Expanse: Dragon Tooth, the Rocinante crew is plunged into a high stakes conspiracy when an old acquaintance of Amos Burton's comes to him for aid.” What's particularly exciting is that Wes Chatham, the actor who portrayed Amos Burton in the television series, makes his comic-book writing debut with the miniseries.

Chatham's understanding of the character shines through in this series, making it all the more special and strengthening its connection to Prime Video's show. While Dragon Tooth and A Little Death are both must-reads for fans looking to explore the missing chapter between Books 6 and 7 or continue the story beyond Season 6, there are other Expanse comics worth checking out. These include The Expanse: Origins (2020), a prequel to the television series, and The Expanse (2019–2020), a four-issue story set between Seasons 4 and 5 of the adaptation.

So, if you've been craving more of The Expanse, there's an entire comic-book universe that ties directly into the canon of the acclaimed television series, just waiting for fans to dive into.
