10 New Movies to Watch This Weekend on Netflix, Prime Video, and Hulu (November 27-29)

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The biggest documentary of the year and a really strange romance

Happy Thanksgiving! As expected, it’s a big one for movies. Disney’s latest, Hexed, is out in theaters. There’s also Focker-In-Law, the newest addition to the Meet the Parents franchise. The most intriguing film this weekend, though, is The Further Misadventures of Cliff Booth, which finds Quentin Tarantino continuing the story of Brad Pitt’s character from Once Upon a Time… in Hollywood through David Fincher’s lens. It at least has to be an interesting experiment in different cinematic styles.

But if you’re planning on staying home and stepping away from GTA VI for a movie break, there are plenty of options. It’s a big weekend for VOD releases, with movies like You Can See Everything, Wicker, Fjord, and The Social Reckoning available to rent.

When it comes to streaming, The Mosquito Bowl is out on Netflix, about a 1944 football game played between Marine factions before they headed off to fight in the Battle of Okinawa.

The Cycle premiered in October on Shudder and AMC+, but it’s now available on VOD. The film stars Deborah Ann Woll as a forensic anthropologist investigating a case with ties to her late father.

Angelina Jolie’s Couture, which follows a group of women with intersecting stories during Paris Fashion Week, is out on Hulu.​​​​​​​ In the film, directed by Anna Winocour, Jolie plays Maxine​​​​​​​, an American filmmaker who receives a breast cancer diagnosis. The cast includes Louis Garrel, Vincent Lindon, and more.

There are also two releases from major action stars: Way of the Warrior Kid stars Chris Pratt as a retired Marine who teaches his bullied nephew all of his tricks, and Runner, starring Alan Ritchson as a courier who partners up with a medical courier (played by Owen Wilson) to make an important delivery.

You Can See Everything has grown into one of the biggest independent hits of the year. A surprise release​​​​​​​ by A24, the film follows Nathan Fielder as he documents the last days before Elizabeth Holmes’ imprisonment while staying at her home with Holmes, her partner Billy Evans, and their children.​​​​​​​

The story follows a fisherwoman (Olivia Colman) disliked by other members of her village. Feeling lonely, she asks the local basket maker to weave her a husband​​​​​​​. The basket maker delivers, crafting a perfect man who challenges​​​​​​​ the norms of the fisherwoman’s conservative village.

Professional hot Viking Alexander Skarsgård is an expert at sending up or subverting his own studly image while also completely embodying it. In 2025's brilliant S&M romance Pillion, he deftly exposed the emotional weakness of a strapping, leather-clad biker dom. In Wicker, he achieves something similar but more tender, which is all the more remarkable for being summoned from somewhere within a truly amazing assemblage of practical and digital creature effects.

Fjord follows Mihai (Sebastian Stan) and Lisbet (Renate Reinsve), a Pentecostal couple who move with their five kids from Romania to Norway so Lisbet can spend time with her family. When their daughter arrives at school with a bruise, a Child Protective Services investigation begins, prompting a custody battle.

Aaron Sorkin is continuing the work he began with David Fincher on The Social Network, this time for a new generation.​​​​​​​ This time around, he adapts the story of former Facebook engineer Frances Haugen (Mikey Madison), a woman who shared internal company documents with The Wall Street Journal, revealing Facebook’s prioritization of engagement and profit above all.

Angelina Jolie stars in a French-American film following the lives of intersecting women during Paris Fashion Week, with the story finding the characters at personal and professional crossroads.

The Cycle is one of the smallest yet most discussed horror films of the year, with numerous critics naming it one of their favorite horror films this year​​​​​​​. The story follows a forensic anthropologist looking into a double murder in Oregon as she uncovers a connection to her father's death.

The Mosquito Bowl is set in the '40s, depicting a morale-boosting football match that took place on Christmas Eve in 1944, months before the Battle of Okinawa​​​​​​​. The film is based on Buzz Bissinger’s nonfiction book about the match between two groups of Marines; it highlights the cost of war by noting that many of the players died in action.​​​​​​​

Based on the series of children’s’ books, Way of the Warrior Kid follows Marc (Jude Hill), a bullied fifth grader who’s struggling with the death of his dad. When his uncle Jake (Chris Pratt) arrives from a mission overseas, the two work together and learn from each other, as Marc gains confidence and resilience through Jake’s training.​​​​​​​

John Early’s feature debut is a satirical comedy-drama​​​​​​​. He plays Maddie, a dishwasher at a restaurant who achieves overnight fame when one of her cooking videos goes viral.

Runner follows Alan Ritchson as a retired Navy SEAL​​​​​​​ living in Australia as a courier. The film finds him assigned to deliver an important package that could save a girl’s life, pairing him with a chatty medical courier played by Owen Wilson.
