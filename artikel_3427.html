Netflix’s Major Crime Franchise Starter Officially Becomes a Streaming Juggernaut

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The crime genre is a staple of television. Some of the most popular shows of all time include crime elements, from The Sopranos to Breaking Bad to Law & Order. The enduring appeal of the crime genre lies in its ability to explore the darkest corners of human nature while maintaining high stakes. Whether following the ruthless rise of a cartel boss or the methodical work of homicide detectives, viewers are drawn to the moral ambiguity and tension inherent in breaking the law. Television allows you to root for bad and dangerous people without having any skin in the game. It can also allow you to hate them as much as you want to the point of wishing some characters would meet an untimely end.

12 years ago, Starz aired one of the most popular crime shows in the world. Titled Power, the series was created by Courtney A. Kemp and executive produced by Curtis "50 Cent" Jackson. The crime thriller focuses on James "Ghost" St. Patrick, a New York drug kingpin trying to escape the game through his upscale nightclub, Truth. However, it is easier said than done, as he is caught up between the ruthless drug game, a failing marriage, law enforcement, and the resurfacing of the love of his life. Power delivered six exhilarating seasons full of excellent performances, good music, and jaw-dropping twists. And recently, all seasons just landed on Netflix.

Your nemesis does not want to beat you. He wants to know you completely, and he is patient enough to wait for it. The frightening part is how good the conversation is.

Your nemesis is not after money, territory or revenge. He is after a demonstration, and you are the person he has chosen to prove his point on.

Your nemesis respects you, which sounds flattering until you work out what it means. He chose you because nobody else was worth his time.

Your nemesis has no personal interest in you whatsoever, and that is precisely what makes him unsurvivable. You are not a rival. You are a stop on the way.

As the mothership courts more viewers, the universe is set to continue with two more shows. Power: Origins turns back the clock to late 20th-century New York as Ghost and his friend Tommy rise in the game. Spence Moore II and Charlie Mann have been cast as Ghost and Tommy, with Mekai Curtis reprising his role as Kanan Stark from Power Book III: Raising Kanan. Meanwhile, in the present timeline, Tariq St. Patrick and Tommy reunite after the events of Power Book II: Ghost and Power Book IV: Force. The series is titled Power: Legacy and brings Tommy back to New York after his Chicago exploits.
