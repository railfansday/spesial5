Senator Grassley Should Heed His Own Warning

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Were the longtime Iowa senator to leave office before January, the outgoing governor, Republican Kim Reynolds, would be able to appoint his successor.

Senator Chuck Grassley (R., Iowa) listens as U.S. Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem testifies before a Senate Judiciary Committee hearing on “Oversight of the Department of Homeland Security” on Capitol Hill in Washington, D.C., March 3, 2026.

Iowa Senator Chuck Grassley is the oldest member of Congress at age 93, but he remains sharp and focused.

This month, his office announced that he will not be retiring from the Senate, even though a Democrat will take office as governor in January and have the power to appoint someone if any of the state’s two U.S. Senate seats fall vacant.

John Fund is National Review’s national-affairs reporter.
