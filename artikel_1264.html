Steven Gerrard Has Theory About Why Man Utd Hired Mauricio Pochettino

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Former Liverpool and England midfielder Steven Gerrard has offered an eye-opening theory as to why Manchester United opted for Mauricio Pochettino over Eddie Howe in their search for a replacement for Michael Carrick.

The Red Devils announced their agreement with the then-USMNT head coach on Friday, officially ushering in a new era at Old Trafford after the previous one burned out almost as quickly as it began. United had managed just three wins from their opening 15 games of the 2026/27 season, including just one in the Premier League, leaving them languishing in the relegation zone by November.

While it initially appeared that United's hierarchy were leaning towards Howe as their next manager, they ultimately moved quickly to get the paperwork over the line for former Tottenham and Chelsea boss Pochettino.

His ability to build a strong dressing-room culture, develop young players and oversee steady progress makes him more of a long-term project manager than someone whose candle burns at both ends. However, Gerrard believes there was another, perhaps even more significant, reason why Pochettino ultimately got the nod.

On a recent episode of The Overlap, Gerrard was asked why United opted for Pochettino as opposed to other available candidates. He claimed: "I think he would have been on their list for a long time, so they've admired him for a long time. They probably haven't been able to get him for different reasons, timing.

He's a good man manager. Players like to work for him. You listen to all the interviews when he left Spurs, Harry Kane, Dele Alli, Kyle Walker, they all loved having him as their manager. And if he can hit the ground running at Manchester United, you can see it being a real positive appointment.

"But the other thing to mention, I think, which is interesting, is if you look at Manchester United's main players, I know some of them are coming to the end of their contracts, but Lisandro Martinez, Bruno Fernandes, Matheus Cunha, your Amad Diallo's of this world, he will speak Spanish.

"So, if he can get these players back into top form and enjoying themselves and smiling again, that's a large chunk of this Manchester United team. "I think that will have had an impact as well, because if you look at Eddie Howe, he was available as well and it seemed as if they wanted the Pochettino road."

He continued: "You wonder whether the Spanish-speaking thing has had a bit of an impact, because you look at a lot of the main players in that Manchester United team and squad.

"The Spanish-speaking could go a long way, I would say. And him being able to speak different languages might have been the reason why he's just been picked over Eddie.

"I don't know whether Eddie was a serious contender or not, but from afar, I think they would have been stupid not to have him on the list. I'm a massive fan of Eddie Howe as well, but you can understand maybe why they've gone down the Pochettino road."

A likeable, well-to-do manager who has a track record of helping teams improve over time - there's a lot to love about Pochettino, and there are a lot of things that show he could be the one to finally get United back on track in their disastrous post-Sir Alex Ferguson era.

Taking Tottenham from outside of the European qualification places in 2014 to the Champions League final five years later is good evidence of that. But it's also that timeframe that may suggest there's a missing piece to his record.

Besides a stint with PSG, who are always expected to win league titles at ease, the Argentine has never won a major honour in his managerial career.

That will absolutely have to change if he is to make a success in what has become known as 'football's most stressful job.' But, at the same time,ime will also have to be given more time than his predecessors for any chance of that, given he's a slow-burner rather than a quick burner like that of other candidates such as Antonio Conte.

His first test will come in a trip to Anfield to take on Liverpool next Sunday afternoon - a place he has had joy at in the past with Southampton, and a place he could make a dream start to lfie as United boss.
