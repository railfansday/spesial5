He was Team-Mates with Wayne Rooney and Steven Gerrard - Then he started Talking to Water

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Footballers are a strange breed, aren't they? Whether it's screaming Ballon d'Or several times down a microphone as if in some kind of Vinicius Junior trance, slapping yourself live on Sky Sports after another Manchester United defeat, or even talking to water. Yes, talking to water.

Just ask Matt Le Tissier and he'll insist that there's plenty of time to think about anything and everything in life after football. For some, that means starting major businesses and dominating the podcast industry. For others, it means grabbing their closest tin foil hats and beginning life as a conspiracy theorist.

It's fair to say that Rickie Lambert falls into the latter category.

Before life on the conspiracy frontline, Lambert was leading the line for Southampton, Liverpool and England, enjoying time with Steven Gerrard and Wayne Rooney as teammates. The forward quickly became one of the surprise packages in the Premier League as a late-bloomer who found his feet and earned a dream move to boyhood club Liverpool.

It wasn't an easy rise to the top, however. At 23 years old, Lambert was still in the lower tiers of the EFL, playing for Rochdale and Blackpool in League One and League Two.

It wasn't until Southampton came calling in 2009 that he made his Championship debut at 29. Even he may have settled for that. No one predicted what would happen next.

After almost a decade, the forward finally made his Premier League debut at 30 years old, playing a crucial role for Southampton.

From the depths of the EFL, Lambert became Mr Reliable for the Saints, scoring 15 Premier League goals in his debut campaign before 13 in the next. Things then got even better when he made his England debut in 2013 and scored the winning goal against Scotland. Lambert was in dreamland.

It was enough to earn interest from boyhood club Liverpool, who snapped up Lambert's signature in 2014 for around £4m. While he then went onto underwhelm at Anfield, no one can deny that the striker lived the dream when many thought it was already out of reach.

Lambert enjoyed a heroic career. He could have taken the usual route into punditry or coaching in the aftermath of that career. Instead, when COVID-19 struck, he dived head first into the world of conspiracy theories. He joined the likes of Le Tissier by taking to social media with some interesting takes.

The former Liverpool man later described himself as a "critical thinker" and posted in protest of lockdowns during the pandemic.

In 2024, he then aimed a dig at Gary Neville and the former Manchester United right-back's push for an independent football regulator, replying by saying: “Instead of using the footballing community to come together and stop what is being implemented onto us, you are using your position to try and persuade them to accept the corrupt, cowardly shadow government's plan to destroy our way of life and society.”

Neville simply brushed off Lambert's response, posting: “You ok Rickie?” alongside two crying laughing emojis.

His most absurd theory came in 2023, however. Lambert claimed that, by talking positively to water and be grateful towards it, you could make it clean. He even claimed that scientists have done tests to prove it.

"They’ve [scientists] done a test where you spoke positively to one glass of water, froze it, spoke negatively to another glass of water, froze it. Then [they] examined the ice. The negativity water was full of holes and blackness. The glass of water that was spoke to positively was full of crystals."

Coincidentally, Lambert also scored 31 goals with his head throughout his career.

When he's not baffling fans with his conspiracy theories, Lambert has been working towards becoming a coach. In 2023, he became a part-time youth development coach at Wigan Athletic. As of 2026, there has been no announcement of his departure, but it remains unknown whether he is still in the role.

Away from coaching, Lambert is also regularly a podcast guest on platforms such as Saints Social, After The Final Whistle and Le Pod by Le Tissier. He also makes speaking appearances, plays in charity games, and is currently the vice-president at Newport IW on the Isle of Wight.

Fans can also see his conspiracy theories across social media, as they await his next bizarre opinion seemingly backed by science.
