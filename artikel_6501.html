23 Healthy Breakfasts That’ll Keep You Full Till Lunch

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

When it comes to healthy breakfasts, free yourself to eat more than plain yogurt and granola bars. And honestly, those $19 acai bowls from the smoothie shop aren’t that nutritious. A truly healthy breakfast has plenty of carbs for energy (this includes fiber-rich ingredients like whole grains, berries, legumes, and seeds); muscle- and bone-supporting protein; and plenty of healthy fats for nutrient absorption. Many of these recipes can easily be made ahead and doled out from the freezer, so before you swing by the drive-through for bland egg muffins, consider these more flavorful options. Here are 23 of our favorite healthy breakfasts.

When it comes to healthy breakfast ideas, my mind immediately goes to oats. Also chia seed pudding. So the newest edition of my column Good for You is a chia-overnight oat hybrid, which also uses up a can of pumpkin purée (I bet you have one in the pantry.)

Frozen or fresh berries work great in these accidentally vegan pancakes (thanks, silken tofu!) from Good for You. A good drizzle of maple syrup is all they need for accompaniment, but since I’m not vegan, I enjoy them with a swipe of Greek yogurt.

Another Good for You hit is this speedy, protein- and collagen-rich soup, which is something in between Italian stracciatella and Chinese egg drop soup.

OK, after this one, I’ll stop shamelessly promoting my column. But seriously, don’t sleep on this hydrating mixed berry smoothie, especially if you’re lukewarm on eating plain cottage cheese.

This tricked out avocado toast is loaded with hot smoked salmon and creamy scrambled eggs.

You maybe haven’t seen an omelet filled with sliced apple and cheddar, but I recommend trying before knocking.

Oatmeal is an ideal choice in the morning for carbs and fiber. I like to slowly stir in egg whites while it’s simmering on the stove for extra protein and top it with ground flax seeds.

A great option for weekend mornings. You could top it with feta or yogurt, dunk whole wheat toast in the sauce or spoon it over rice, anything goes.

Nothing hits quite like a breakfast burrito, but I can’t buy one every day and still pay rent. DIY to the rescue: I line the tortilla with salsa or hot sauce, and sometimes add leftover refried beans if I have them.

The texture of chia seed pudding took me some time to get used to, but now I’m obsessed. Strange how the body adapts! I love that this recipe makes enough for a work week of breakfasts. Head here for our favorite matcha powders.

If you don’t have time to make the hot honey mixture, these simple breakfast tacos are great with any hot sauces or chile-based condiments that happen to be in the fridge.

This Persian egg dish is similar to a frittata, which means it makes for great meal prep. It takes a while to wash all the herbs, so make sure you have time, but leftovers will last a few days in the fridge. I like it cold with a swipe of Greek yogurt, but you do you.

Meet the bran muffins you’ll actually crave. Squidgy dates and chopped chocolate make these more like a hearty breakfast cake than the dense pucks you may have eaten in the past.

If it’s not heirloom tomato season, opt for smaller cherry or Campari tomatoes in this recipe—your taste buds will thank you.

To bulk up this green smoothie into a filling meal, add some nut butter and cottage cheese or plain yogurt, plus a scoop of protein powder if you have it.

Leftover cooked quinoa mocking you in the fridge? Stir it in to these blueberry muffins. Win, win.

Speaking of leftover quinoa, if you’re looking for a savory option instead of sweet, throw together this morning salad with a few boiled eggs and smoked trout.

While I personally love mushy porridge, I understand that cakey is a more pleasant texture for many. If that’s the case for you, baked oatmeal is the solution.

Serving brunch to a crowd? Make this breakfast casserole. Just grab a nice loaf of sourdough to slice alongside.

If you haven’t yet tried fried eggs that were based with turmeric oil, now is your moment.

For the savory oatmeal fans out there, try simmering with instant dashi powder and salt-softened vegetables.

A homemade gluten-free loaf that you’ll dream about. Try it with butter and a slice of aged cheese like cheddar, or cream cheese and smoked fish.
