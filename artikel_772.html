How to Stream the 2026 Latin Grammys Online

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The 27th Annual Latin Grammy Awards air live on Nov. 12 from the MGM Garden Arena in Las Vegas. Here's how to catch the livestream.

The 27th Annual Latin Grammy Awards, hosted for the 10th time by Roselyn Sánchez, air on Thursday, Nov. 12 at 8 p.m. PT/ET across Univision, UNIMÁS and Galavisión (TelevisaUnivision’s U.S. networks), available to stream live via TV streaming services like DirecTV (with a five-day free trial) and Hulu + Live TV.

The ceremony will be preceded by the official Noche de Estrellas red carpet pre-show, kicking off at 6 p.m. PT/ET on UNIMÁS and Galavisión, and at 7 p.m. PT/ET on Univision. The pre-show will similarly stream live via DirecTV (with its five-day free trial). Since the ceremony and red-carpet pre-show air across Univision, UNIMÁS and Galavisión, the star-studded evening is also available to watch live on any streaming service that carries said networks, including DirecTV (with a five-day free trial) and Hulu + Live TV.
