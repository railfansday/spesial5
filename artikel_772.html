Truro City game at Folkestone called off after Yassine En-Neyah has medical emergency in warm-up

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Player ‘required medical assistance during the warm-up’

Truro’s National League South game at Folkestone Invicta was called off after the visitors’ midfielder Yassine En-Neyah had a medical emergency and was taken to hospital.

A club statement said: “Truro City can confirm that this afternoon’s match against Folkestone Invicta has been postponed.

“Yassine En-Neyah required medical assistance during the warm-up and was transferred to the William Harvey hospital in Ashford for further treatment.

“We will provide a further update in due course.”
