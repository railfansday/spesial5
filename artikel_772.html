Taylor Swift is riding back into country music territory.

After 13 years since she last performed on the CMA Awards stage, the superstar is returning to sing at the 60th annual CMA Awards on Wednesday, Nov. 18, from 8 to 11 p.m. ET. Broadcasting live from Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, Tennessee, the milestone celebration will be hosted by Lainey Wilson and air on ABC and Disney+, marking the streaming platform’s first time hosting the ceremony live.

The 60th Annual CMA Awards will bring together an exciting mix of country legends and rising stars. Confirmed performers include Taylor Swift, Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert, Kacey Musgraves, Cody Johnson, Tim McGraw, Zach Top, Tucker Wetmore, The Red Clay Strays, Lainey Wilson, Luke Combs and Megan Moroney. Fans can also look forward to special collaborations featuring Mumford & Sons with Chris Stapleton and Stephen Wilson Jr. with Chris Stapleton.

Another highlight will be a tribute to legendary country duo Brooks & Dunn, who are receiving the 2026 CMA Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award. Brothers Osborne, HARDY, Luke Bryan, Marcus King and Halestorm will join forces for that celebration. Following her death in August, the show will also honor Dolly Parton with a special tribute and recognize her lasting influence on country music.

Ella Langley leads the 2026 CMA Awards nominations with an impressive nine nods, making her one of the night’s biggest contenders. Miranda Lambert follows with seven nominations, while Kacey Musgraves earns five. Some of the biggest categories to watch include Entertainer of the Year, where Luke Combs, Cody Johnson, Ella Langley, Chris Stapleton and Lainey Wilson are competing for the top prize.

Meanwhile, Taylor Swift earns a Single of the Year nomination for “I Knew It, I Knew You,” going up against Ella Langley’s “Be Her” and “Choosin’ Texas,” Lainey Wilson’s “Somewhere Over Laredo” and Cody Johnson’s “The Fall.” Other major categories include Album of the Year, Female Vocalist of the Year and Male Vocalist of the Year.
