Cameron agrees contract extension with Jake Paul's MVP

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Chantelle Cameron, the unified super-welterweight champion, signs a multi-fight extension with Most Valuable Promotions; Jake Paul said: "Chantelle Cameron has established herself as one of the true GOATs of women's boxing and what makes her special is that she's still hungry for more"

Chantelle Cameron, the unified super-welterweight world champion, has agreed a multi-fight extension with Jake Paul's Most Valuable Promotions.

The reigning WBO, WBA and WBC super-welterweight world titlist is one of the world's top pound-for-pound fighters.

The new agreement extends MVP's partnership with Cameron following a landmark 2026 campaign that saw the English fighter capture world championships in a second weight division - further cementing her position among boxing's elite - and beat American star Mikaela Mayer.

A former undisputed super-lightweight world champion, and the only professional opponent to beat Katie Taylor, Cameron will continue pursuing the biggest fights in women's boxing, starting with her rematch with Mayer on December 24.

Nakisa Bidarian and Paul, founders of Most Valuable Promotions, said: "Chantelle Cameron has established herself as one of the true GOATs of women's boxing and what makes her special is that she's still hungry for more.

"Look at her resume and it is clear she is the pound-for-pound No 1 fighter in the world. In just over a year with MVP, she's unified three world titles at 154 pounds, headlined a major event in her home country, and is now preparing for another massive championship fight on Christmas Eve in Dubai as part of one of the biggest combat sports events of 2026.

"Chantelle has made it clear that she wants to keep challenging herself against the toughest competition, and we're committed to delivering those fights. This extension is about building on everything we've accomplished together and making sure the next chapter of her career is even bigger."

Cameron said: "I'm delighted to extend my contract with MVP. Since I first signed with Nakisa and Jake they have delivered on every single promise they made to me.

"They have looked after me in and out of the ring and signing a long-term extension was a very easy decision on my part.

"I am boxing on the biggest cards all over the world and I look forward to the rest of my career with MVP."
