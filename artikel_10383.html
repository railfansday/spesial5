Are the Galaxy Buds 4 Pro the best earbuds for you?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Picking the right earbuds is a tough task, but Samsung’s Galaxy Buds 4 Pro are among the best on the market, and in this guide, we’ll be looking at whether or not they’re the best earbuds for you this holiday season.

In short, yes, the Galaxy Buds 4 Pro sound great. Samsung’s flagship earbuds have a balanced sound profile that’s customizable by the listener to suit their tastes, and is generally recognized as having excellent sound quality by experts and owners alike.

Galaxy Buds 4 Pro are award-winning earbuds with balanced sound quality for music lovers, offering a clean and crisp profile that lets you hear music the way you’ll enjoy it most.

Samsung’s flagship earbuds deliver their sound through a new, larger main driver that’s paired with a dedicated tweeter. They support 24-bit/96 kHz UHQ audio when paired with a compatible Samsung Galaxy device. That’s paired with active noise cancellation that adjust to your surroundings in real-time. They also sound good for those on the other end of a call, with Samsung’s Super Clear Call tech reducing noise from your environment, and the blade stem design positioning the microphones closer to your mouth.

Like everything else in Samsung’s product lineup, Galaxy Buds 4 Pro are packed with useful features.

This includes active noise cancellation (ANC) to filter out noise in your surroundings. Samsung even adapts ANC strength to what’s around you, raising the ANC level when you’re somewhere like on a plane, but lowering it when you’re in a quiet park or café. Voice and Siren Detect can automatically switch the earbuds to Ambient mode when you begin a conversation or when an emergency siren is detected.

Live Translate offers support for 22 languages to help you have a conversation beyond the language barrier, with speech translated into your ears via Buds 4 Pro and the transcript shown on your phone. Bixby and Gemini are accessible through the earbuds for AI assistance, too. Samsung also offers an FDA-approved hearing aid feature to help amplify sounds around you.

Head gestures allow you to answer or decline calls with a simple shake of your head. Pinch controls manage playback, while swiping adjusts the volume; pinch-and-hold can be customized for supported shortcuts.

No, you don’t have to have a Samsung Galaxy smartphone to use Galaxy Buds 4 Pro.

Because they work over standard Bluetooth, Samsung’s best earbuds work with any smartphone, tablet, and other devices. Galaxy Buds 4 Pro can also connect to Windows PCs as standard Bluetooth audio, although advanced features are more limited outside the Galaxy ecosystem.

That said, you’ll get the best experience with Samsung devices. That’s in part because EQ, noise cancellation, and other controls are baked into One UI, but also because Samsung’s best audio codec on Galaxy Buds 4 Pro is proprietary to Galaxy devices. In other words, you’ll get the best sound quality on these earbuds when paired to another Samsung device.
