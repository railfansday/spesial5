Expedia Group confirmed Thursday that attackers reached its systems through a compromised third-party data platform provider, part of a supply chain attack that hit multiple companies. The acknowledgment followed reports on cybersecurity sites this week about claims by an extortion group known as n0n that it had stolen Expedia data. Expedia said it contained the issue as soon as it became aware, is investigating with outside security experts, and that its platforms remain safe for travelers and partners. The company did not identify the compromised vendor or say what information may have been exposed, and the scale of the breach is unconfirmed. Separately, researchers at Wiz and Sentra documented an attack between October 14 and 26 involving cloud data platform Qubole, a onetime Expedia vendor, in which attackers used compromised credentials to enter multiple customers' cloud environments and download data. Sentra found that former Qubole customers never revoked the access permissions they had previously granted, leaving a way in long after the business relationships ended. Expedia did not confirm whether its breach is related to Qubole, and outside researchers have not publicly identified Expedia as one of the affected customers.
