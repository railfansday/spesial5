Guide: GTA 6: Who Is the Real Actor Jason Is Based On?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Of the two playable characters in GTA 6, the first you'll spend significant time with is Jason. You might be wondering about who exactly it is that has played the part of this co-lead character. In this GTA 6 guide, we answer the question: who is the real actor Jason is based on in GTA 6?

Jason in GTA 6 is portrayed by the actor Dylan Rourke, who lends his voice and likeness to the character.

Much like with Lucia, his involvement as Jason was heavily rumoured for a very long time. Following the game's launch, however, it was finally revealed that Rourke is in fact the actor behind Jason via this Instagram post:

The post shows a picture of Rourke with Manni L. Perez, who plays Lucia, as well as stuntwoman Victoria Lee.

His name appears in the game's credits too, of course, and he has confirmed his involvement in his own social post:

Rourke, 34, got his first acting credit in 2013 in the short thriller The Lone Wolf. Since then, he has featured in television, movies, and theatre.

Like Perez, he's a relatively unknown performer, and his turn as Jason in GTA 6 will be his biggest role to date.
