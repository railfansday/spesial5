Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds - Year Two 2.0.0 Patch Now Live - New Additions, Changes & Improvements

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Update [Wed 4th Nov 2026, 1:15am]: The Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds Year Two Update (Version 2.0.0) has now gone live. It kicks off with the Godzilla Pack, which is the first paid Year Two DLC. Bayonetta also joins the race today as the first free character update of Year Two.

Here's the full rundown from Sega's website:

Duels let you take on other racers in 1-on-1 showdowns. This mode can be played both offline and online. While racing, you'll have an extra ace up your sleeve: Duel Boost. This gauge fills up as time passes and you use items. Once it's full, press the button to boost ahead. Time your Duel Boost well to pull off clutch comebacks!

Face off against CPU CrossOver racers in offline 1-on-1 matches. By defeating these powerful rivals, you'll earn unique rewards. Keep on progressing to unlock the new racer Bayonetta!

Test your skills in a 1-on-1 match against players worldwide. Give it everything you've got in an adrenaline-pumping race where it's just you, your opponent, and the track.

Compete for the most points within a limited number of races. Join these limited-time events and earn Duel Medals. Exchange them for exclusive horns, auras, and other rewards!

Face off against a friend or family member with this Friend Match option. *On Nintendo Switch™ and Nintendo Switch™ 2, you can play this mode in Local Wireless.

The Super Slot amplifies the effects of your Gadgets. *To use the Super Slot, you must first unlock the Grand Master Plate by racing at least 40 times. Players who have already fulfilled these conditions will be able to use the Super Slot upon installing the patch.

We're adding various missions that you can complete by playing regularly. Clear them by the deadline to earn Donpa Tickets and Duel Medals. *You must be connected to the Internet to participate in Play Missions.

Exchange your Donpa Tickets and Duel Medals for auras, horns, decals, characters, titles, and Jukebox tracks at the new in-game Shop. We'll be updating the store lineup over time.

Free Matches* don't affect your rank, so feel free to jump in without any pressure.

*Head over to Ranked Match for the original World Match experience.

Matchmaking will now pit you against Rivals of similar skill level. Come out on top to earn bonus Donpa Tickets!

We've overhauled the main menu to make room for all the new modes. Now when you select World Match, Legend Competition, Ranked Match, and Free Match will show up as options.

All new Festivals will be 10 days instead of 3. There's more time to join the fun and rack up all of the Festival Point Rewards.

Alongside the extended Festivals, win streaks will now determine Festival Rankings rather than Festival Points. When a Festival is live, your longest win streak will appear on the leaderboard. If your team finishes in 2nd place, don't worry. Your win streak won't reset, allowing you to keep up your run.

AI Rival teams can now appear in your matches at random. Defeating them earns you bonus Festival Points!

Win even more crowns while playing Festivals!

Awarded to players who have a 5+ win streak.

These can be obtained once your Gadget Plate reaches Grand Master Plate or higher.

Previous Festival rewards added to the Shop

The following Gadgets, previously distributed as Festival rewards, can now be purchased* with Donpa Tickets from the Customize Gadgets screen.

*These can be purchased once your Gadget Plate reaches Master Plate or higher.

Super Slot added: Powering up the Gadget System

The Super Slot, the 7th Gadget slot, gives players even more ways to customize their racing strategy.

Alongside this addition, we've adjusted underpowered and overpowered Gadgets.

We've also introduced 1-slot variants of existing Gadgets that are compatible with the Super Slot.

To add more ways to drive, the update adds Mod Gadgets, which offer bigger stat bonuses than existing Tuner Gadgets, and the new Charge Gadgets.

Plus, we've adjusted the overall balance, shortening the time it takes Rings to spawn and buffing Acceleration Type Machines.

These changes will give racers in the middle of the pack chances to stage a comeback.

Going forward, more Gadget and Machine adjustments will be made based on usage and win rates.

Rings now take less time to respawn after players collect them.

In Ver. 1.4, it was difficult to collect Rings when racing alongside others. As a result, Gadgets that let players obtain Rings independently held a significant advantage.

We've implemented this change to shake up the meta, and give players in middle positions more chances for success.

Since these adjustments will make the Ring Engine Gadgets and Ring Evolution more powerful, we have rebalanced their performance accordingly.

*These adjustments do not apply to offline modes, such as Grand Prix and Race Park.

Shortened recovery time after taking damage.

Based on usage and win rates of Acceleration Type Machines, we have made adjustments to improve their signature trait of recovering quickly.

They should excel on courses with many enemies and obstacles, as well as in races where attacks are frequent.

Fixed an issue where Power Type Machines had a higher maximum speed bonus cap than other Machines.

Originally planned for Ver. 1.4.1, we postponed this fix to prevent an issue making it impossible to update your records in Time Trial. However, as the new Gadget Slot is likely to impact records in Ver. 2.0, we have proceeded with this change.

Adjusted Omochao's Ring placement so that Rings are easier to collect mid-jump.

While holding either Boost or Double Boost, the other item can now still spawn from an Item Box. Previously, holding either a Boost or Double Boost made both items less likely to appear.

Adjusted the explosion effect to better match the duration of its attack hitbox.

Adjusted the placement of Rings and Item Boxes to improve the risk-reward balance of certain paths on the following courses:

Since their introduction, Ring Engine and Hyper Ring Engine have maintained high win rates, particularly in Emerald Grade of Legend Competition

Since Ver. 2.0 makes it easier to maintain Rings due to the shortened Ring respawn times and the ability to equip an additional Gadget that grants Rings with the Super Slot, we've lowered the top speed bonus from these two Gadgets.

In parallel, we've adjusted Gadgets that make it easier to maintain your Rings:

Slipstream Bounty, Acceleration Character Kit, Slipstream Kit

The following Ring Gadgets have been enhanced. Since these Gadgets don't continuously collect Rings, these enhancements should have only a small impact on Ring Engine builds:

Increased the number of Rings you can receive from 15 to 25.

Increased the number of Rings you can receive from 4 to 6.

Held off on adjusting Mini Ring Thief due to its use in Ring Engine builds.

Ring Gain Boost/Acceleration Character Kit

The following Driving Gadgets were useful on certain courses and in certain situations, but in many cases ended up being underpowered We have enhanced their performance in Ver. 2.0 to make their unique abilities more distinct:

After a Perfect Landing, the duration of the maximum speed boost now increases proportionate to the number of Air Tricks performed.

Added an effect that makes all Levels charge 1.7x faster while in flight form.

Enhanced the Gadgets below to make them more useful in the current game environment:

Since balance adjustments can weaken Gadgets and make them less viable for setting new records in Time Trial, we may make separate Gadget adjustments for Time Trial alone starting in Ver. 2.0. For Ver. 2.0, the following Gadgets perform differently in Time Trial.

In Time Trial, reduced the top speed bonus for acquiring a Ring has been reduced from 3 km/h to 2 km/h.

*When equipped in the Super Slot, the bonus remains unchanged at 3 km/h.

In Time Trial, this Gadget will have the same performance as in Ver. 1.4. However, you will no longer get an additional stat boost by equipping both the Ring Engine and Hyper Ring Engine. You can get the same boost by equipping Quick Starter or Starting Boost Bounty as your 7th Gadget.

In Time Trial, this Gadget will have the same performance as in Ver. 1.4, and will evolve every 10 Rings.

In Time Trial, this Gadget will have the same performance as in Ver. 1.4.

Celebrate Year Two with a new Sonic character decal each week! To commemorate Ver. 2.0, we're launching a login campaign featuring "I ♡" Sonic character decals. Grab your favorite character's decal and deck out your ride! More details coming soon on the Sonic socials.

Decals in partnership with The k4sen, an event held by popular streamer k4sen, are now available.

New Brand Collab Decals! Added collaboration decals from the brands listed below. 3 Yoshinoya Decals 7 Imuraya Decals 3 AUTOBACS Decals

Due to the recent influx of new Gadgets and the introduction of Duel Mode, we've expanded the following customization features.

To make the game more approachable for new players, lowered the unlock requirements for Gadgets and Slots from Bronze Plate onward. These lower requirements will be applied for current players the next time they launch the game.

In addition, to open up more competitive builds early in the game, we've changed the acquisition timing for Slipstream Bounty and Dash Panel Mini Bounty from Gadget Unlock 3 to when Master Plate is unlocked.

The latest version of the game's credits are now live.

We have updated content for future live operations.

In Ver. 2.0, prior to the start of Best-of-10 in Duel Match, "End Challenge" and "Round -1" are displayed.

This issue will be resolved after the first Best-of-10 competition starts.

In addition, if the event period ends while a player still has races remaining in the Best-of-10 competition, "X Tries Remaining" will be displayed even though the event has ended.

This issue will be addressed in a future update. We will let you know as soon as it has been resolved.

Thanks to all the CrossWorlds players out there for rocketing through the multiverse with us throughout the World Championship and our major Year Two update.

We’re committed to delivering new content to keep the game fresh, and we’ll continue making improvements based on your feedback.

Thank you for your continued support of Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds!
