Here are the best Accessories in Phantom Blade Zero

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

The best Phantom Blade Zero Accessories are usually the ones that grant you a noticeable damage reduction effect. From my time playing, and talking to other GamesRadar+ writers, we agree that some of these Defensive Accessories are the most useful, generally making combat feel a lot easier – mostly because it makes Soul about as spongey as some of the foes he faces. But there are also Offensive and Support Accessories to consider and a lot of them at that, so here are the Phantom Blade Zero Accessories I recommend equipping.

While you can wear up to six Accessories in Phantom Blade Zero once you have all the Boundless Capacity abilities, you're still capped at wearing up to three of the same type, so use the list below to help you put together a varied selection:

Collect all four Guardian Idol Fragments around the Mandate Encampment, which can only be reached if you have the Phantom Blade Zero Mandate Encampment Key.

Destruction-type Phantom Edges use 1 additional Remnant Spark to deal substantially more damage. Fully upgrade this to +2 to massively increase the damage buff.

Found just outside the Crimson Manor front door in Weeping Creek Valley.

Grants damage resistance based on your HP where more HP equals more damage resistance. Arguably the best Accessory in the game.

Reward for completing the 'Apprentice Healer' quest for Yu'er in Weeping Creek Valley.

Found in a gold chest next to Jewel, who you meet at the start of the Village of Lu.

Reward for beating the Phantom Blade Zero Gold-Masked One at the Crimson Forest Long Pillar.

Gain damage resistance for three seconds after switching weapons.

Reward for beating the Phantom Blade Zero Bai Chilian boss.

Found in the Village of Lu cellar which requires the Phantom Blade Zero Village of Lu Cellar Key.

Gives you a chance to regenerate a Remnant Spark upon triggering a Phantom Edge follow-up attack (this is from the Flowing Chi Ability). Fully upgrade this to +2 to turn the chance into a guarantee.

I've also got some honorable mentions for Accessories that are worth bearing in mind for specific scenarios, especially boss fights. The Cavalrybane is well worth using against any mounted bosses, and the Shieldbreaker Talisman is pretty much essential against any bosses that have a pale-blue shield health bar.You might also want to consider one of the damage reduction accessories for a specific damage type if it's a damage type a particular boss uses a lot. For example, the Lion Dance Grandmaster that you fight for the Phantom Blade Zero Flaming Mane weapon uses a lot of fire damage, so the Flameward can come in handy.
