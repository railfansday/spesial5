Pokémon Pokopia's Limited-Time 'Apples To Applin' Event Begins This Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The next limited-time in-game event for Pokémon Pokopia is almost upon us, and it comes with a fresh batch of new 'mon to add to your towns.

The 'Apples to Applin' event begins later this month on 16th November and will be sticking around until 1st December.

As the name suggests, the adorable Gen VIII apple Pokémon takes centre stage in this one, and you'll be collecting Syrupy Apples in exchange for sweet apple-themed furniture.

With this new furniture under your belt, you'll be able to build habitats for newcomers Flapple, Appletun, Dipplin, and Hydrapple.

The Pokémon Company also confirmed today that the game's next DLC wave, 'Dazzling Decorations', will launch on Switch 2 next month. The expansion is all about putting cute little accessories on your Poké pals, and you know what, we're already picturing what kind of headwear would best suit Applin.
