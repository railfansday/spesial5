Every host knows the lead-up to the gathering involves careful planning, which includes giving your home a deep clean and perhaps getting sidetracked or two with a quick touch-up project. Whether it be filling that small hole in the wall, zhuzh-ing up the guest room, or buying a sleeper sofa or extra air mattress, there’s a lot to do. We have one more suggestion if you feel like you need more storage or to create more space: get a buffet cabinet. We found one at Costco we loved last year, but it has since sold out, which sent us on a mad hunt to find a replica.

Luckily, Scurrty's Buffet & Bar Cabinet from Amazon offers the same look but also for a third of the price at $250. (Costco’s original cabinet is $650.) It has the same bright blue shade and ribbed fronts. While this Amazon find is only 62 inches wide, it delivers. The pair of dual-door cabinets features an adjustable, removable shelf, allowing you to store whatever you need for your parties.

The engineered wood used here is reinforced by high-quality hinges, plus there's even a wall nail just in case you want to mount the cabinets. And if blue doesn't complement your home's color scheme, the Scurrty cabinet is also available in brown, white, black, and green. If you’re hosting this season, this is the perfect piece to get to store away all your items quickly and have it be a place to place your apps and snacks.
