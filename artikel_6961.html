Here’s Who Won ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Season 35

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

And the winner of the Mirrorball Trophy is...

Dancing With the Stars season 35 has crowned a winner! After a fiercely competitive season finale with each couple performing an elaborate Freestyle routine, a Judges’ Choice dance, and an Instant Dance, the scores were tallied, the viewers’ votes were counted, and the winner is....

Sooo, what did Ezra win? That’d be the Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy, as well as a cash prize. Though, the payout is the same for everyone who makes it to the finale thanks to Dancing With the Stars’ strict payment structure. According to Variety, everyone on the show got $125,000 for the rehearsal period + the first two weeks of the show; those who made it to weeks three and four got another $10,000; those in weeks five and six got an additional $20,000, plus another additional $30,000 for weeks seven and eight, and then another $50,000 for weeks nine and ten. The total? $295,000.

As for Daniella, pros get paid weekly, but Jenna Johnson confirmed the winning pro gets a bonus. As she explained to The Morning After podcast, “Whoever makes it to the finale, you all get paid up until then. And you do get a nice bonus. If you win, it’s not like you get a million dollars. You’re just getting a cute trophy together.”

Congrats again to Ezra and Daniella on that shiny trophy and an incredible season!
