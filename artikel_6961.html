3 Major Awards Shows Won’t Nominate Amanda Seyfried for 'You Can See Everything'

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Awards season is almost upon us, which means celebrities are about to start campaigning hard—including Amanda Seyfried, whose brilliant performance as Elizabeth Holmes in You Can See Everything has earned a ton of Best Supporting Actress buzz.

While Variety previously reported that Amanda can be nominated for a Best Supporting Actress Oscar, apparently there are three major awards she will be ineligible for.

Per The Hollywood Reporter, Amanda is unable to be nominated for a Golden Globe, a BAFTA, or an Actor award due to her performance technically being in a documentary—though apparently SAG-AFTRA has told A24 they can appeal the decision.

Amanda told THR that she fully views her participation in the film as a performance, saying “The reenactments were definitely a challenge. But it’s not like I ever considered that I would be up for any kind of accolade. I understand the politics and the luck and the timing of awards stuff. I don’t really know if we can fully consider it a doc—it’s very genre-bending.”

Amanda added that she’s “fully present” for the awards season campaign, noting that “the amount of events and the amount of presence you need to have when campaigning for these things is pretty extreme—you saw me go through it last year—but it’s still all very positive because getting a seat at the table is the thing.”

She went on to explain that “A campaign is a lot. It’s time-consuming. It takes all your energy. And I’m totally willing to do it. A24 is great. They’re excited, and I’m very proud of the film, so I’m fully ready to engage in that. And I love these filmmakers! I had a great time with this tiny crew. And I think because [the experience] is so rare, and it will never happen again, I’m doubled-down on it.”
