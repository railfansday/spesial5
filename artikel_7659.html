Bryan County Resident Pleads Guilty To Abusive Sexual Contact In Indian Country

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

MUSKOGEE, OKLAHOMA – The United States Attorney’s Office for the Eastern District of Oklahoma announced that Gregory Taylor Garrett, age 59, of Calera, Oklahoma, entered a guilty plea to a Felony Information of one count of Abusive Sexual Contact in Indian Country, punishable by imprisonment for any term of years or Life, and a $250,000 fine.The Felony Information alleged that beginning in or about December 2025, and continuing until on or about May 14, 2026, Garrett knowingly engaged in and caused sexual contact with a victim who had not attained the age of 12 years.
