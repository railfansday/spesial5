FiEE, Inc. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Results

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Revenue for the First Nine Months of 2026 Surged 382.1% Year-on-Year to Approximately $9.6 Million

Profit Turnaround for the First Nine Months of 2026 with Net Income of $3.6 Million

OSAKA, Japan, Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- FiEE, Inc. (NASDAQ:FIEE) ("FiEE," the "Company," "we," "our," or similar terms), a technology company that leverages IoT, connectivity, and AI to deliver SaaS solutions for content creation and brand management, along with phygital art advisory services that connect artists, collectors, and brands, today announced its unaudited financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

Operational and Financial Highlights for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026

Operational and Financial Highlights for the Three Months Ended September 30, 2026

Rafael Li, Chief Executive Officer and President of FiEE, commented, "We delivered solid financial results during the period and made meaningful progress in building a more diversified, scalable digital services platform. By expanding our AI-driven content offerings, phygital art advisory services, and software services, we continued to strengthen our market position and open up new avenues for growth. We were also pleased with the success of our recent art exhibition and collector engagement, which highlighted the increasing value of connecting creators, audiences, and collectors through our ecosystem."

Mr. Li added, " Building on this foundation, including our recently completed art exhibition, we have begun generating revenue across art advisory, acquisition assistance, exhibition services, post-transaction support, and MCN brokerage services, and expect this momentum to continue. We are excited about what this means for our creator ecosystem, as these services have given creators a platform to showcase their work and a direct line to collectors who value their work. By broadening our services and deepening engagement with our community of creators and collectors, we believe we can further expand our market reach, scale our platform, and deliver sustainable, long-term value to our stockholders."

Financial Results for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026

Revenue was approximately $9.6 million, compared to $2.0 million in the same period of 2025.

Gross profit was approximately $7.3 million, compared to $1.6 million in the same period of 2025.

Gross margin was 76.3%, compared to 83.0% in the same period of 2025. The decrease primarily reflected higher amortization costs included in cost of revenues.

Operating expenses were approximately $3.5 million, representing an increase of 26.0% from $2.8 million in the same period of 2025.

Operating income was approximately $3.8 million, marking a turnaround from a loss of $1.1 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

Net income was approximately $3.6 million, representing a turnaround from the $1.3 million loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

Diluted earnings per common share was $0.24, compared to a diluted loss per common share of $0.25 for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

FiEE, Inc. (NASDAQ:FIEE), formerly Minim, Inc., was founded in 1977. The Company has a historical track record of delivering a comprehensive WiFi/Software as a Service platform in the market. After years of development, FiEE made the strategic decision to transition to a Software First Model, rebranding itself as a technology company leveraging its expertise in IoT, connectivity, and AI to improve artwork circulation, as well as engagement with artists and collectors.

Notably, FiEE has introduced its innovative Software as a Service solutions, which integrate its AI and data analytics capabilities into content creation and brand management. Building on its established pool of KOLs and artist network, FiEE further extended its offerings to phygital art advisory services, providing a platform for artists, collectors, and brands to connect through exhibitions and other value-added services.
