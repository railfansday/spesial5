From Turkey: Roma interested in  Ernest Muçi

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Trabzonspor’s Ernest Muçi is in the sights of Roma and Lazio.

According to transfer expert Ekrem Konur, the Giallorossi, in particular, are closely monitoring the 25-year-old Albanian international and continue to evaluate his situation.

Ernest Muçi’s profile is considered particularly suited to Gian Piero Gasperini’s 3-4-2-1 formation, a system in which Roma is seeking a playmaker capable of increasing the squad’s depth and increasing competition in attack.
