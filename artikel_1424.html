Dovbyk’s agent looking for suitors in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Artem Dovbyk also watched Bologna’s last match from the bench.

The Ukrainian center-back is still waiting for his first start for the Felsinei; first Tedesco and then Palladino aren’t impressed by the former Girona player. Quite the opposite.

The 1997-born player moved to the Rossoblu on loan this summer with an option to buy for €17 million, an option that will never be exercised and forces Roma to take direct interest to avoid further devaluing a €41 million investment.

The Capitoline club, therefore, is looking for the best solution for Dovbyk, who is increasingly distant from Bologna.

One of the issues to be resolved is his €3 million net salary, of which €2.5 million is paid by the Rossoblu. Interested clubs would have to pay half of that.

Dovbyk appears destined to leave Bologna. As reported by Resto del Carlino, Venezia is looking for a striker and has made contact to explore possible options for signing the Ukrainian striker.

The 1997-born player’s agent, however, is scouting clubs in Spain. Dovbyk, in fact, was pichichi (top scorer) in La Liga before moving to Italy and beginning his downward spiral.

Valencia is considering a move, but there’s work to be done given the player’s substantial salary, to which Bologna is paying 2.5 for the entire season. The Spanish club, if they acquire Dovbyk, could release Duro. The first moves ahead of the winter transfer window are underway.
