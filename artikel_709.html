‘Slow Horses’ Actress Ruth Bradley to Be Honored at the Oscar Wilde Awards (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Irishwoman just concluded a three-season stint on the Apple TV spy drama as the security official Emma Flyte.

Ruth Bradley, the Dublin-born actress who portrayed the MI5 “head dog” Emma Flyte on the Apple TV spy thriller Slow Horses, will be honored at the Oscar Wilde Awards.

The 21st annual event, organized by the US-Ireland Alliance, is set for March 11 at the Ebell in Los Angeles. The party celebrates the work of those from Ireland — and some who are not — who contribute to film, television and music.

Actors Jack Reynor and Thaddea Graham also will be feted, and Irish singer Shobsy will perform. Bradley, 39, just wrapped a three-season run on Slow Horses as Emma, the principled security official who this fall clashed with Kristin Scott Thomas’ Diana Taverner.

The daughter of Irish actress Charlotte Bradley, she will next be seen in Walk the Blue Fields, Netflix’s adaptation of Irish author Claire Keegan’s short story, alongside Emily Blunt, Andrew Scott and Ciarán Hinds; in Bender, an Irish coming-of-age comedy written by Adrian Cunningham and Ciaran Bartlett, directed by Adam Bernstein and starring Bill Burr and Chris O’Dowd; and in The Last Days of Rabbit Hayes, the film adaptation of Anna McPartlin’s novel opposite Niamh Algar, Sam Claflin and Tom Glynn-Carney.

Bradley has also stood out on television on Love/Hate — for which she twice won the best actress prize at the Irish Film and Television Awards — The Fall, Electric Dreams, Humans, Ted Lasso, The Gold and The Guilt, and in such films as In Her Skin (2009) and Embers (2023).
