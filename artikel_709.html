‘VisionQuest’ Finale Hits 3.7 Million Views in One Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The finale of “VisionQuest” reached 3.7 million views globally in one day.

Measured by Disney, that total puts the episode above the finale of every other Marvel Cinematic Universe TV series in the last three years, since Season 2 of “Loki” in 2023. The other MCU series released since then include Seasons 2 and 3 of “What If…?,” “Echo,” “Agatha All Along,” “Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man,” “Daredevil: Born Again,” “Ironheart,” “Eyes of Wakanda,” “Marvel Zombies,” “Wonder Man” and “Daredevil: Born Again.”
