Ina Garten Says She Turned Down ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Because the Thought of Competing Gave Her ‘Nightmares’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Celebrity chef Ina Garten will not be cooking up a freestyle for “Dancing with the Stars” in the near future.

Garten told Rose Byrne on a recent episode of her “Happy Hour” podcast that she would be too nervous to compete. She opened the floor by asking Byrne if she had ever “not done something because it was too scary,” before answering her own question.

“Somebody asked me to do ‘Dancing with the Stars,’” she said. “I thought, ‘Nobody needs to see that. That’s not happening.’” “The closer I got to the day when I had to do it, the more I’d have nightmares,” Garten added. Garten may be shying away from the ballroom, but she is still dancing around the kitchen. Her Food Network show “Be My Guest with Ina Garten,” where she hosts guests at her house in East Hampton for food and conversation, entered its ninth season in October.

While it wasn’t Garten, “Dancing with the Stars” still welcomed a celebrity chef this season. Giada De Laurentiis joined Season 35 with Alan Bersten as her partner. The couple competed two dances before being eliminated on Sept. 22 during “Viral Hits Night.”

“Dancing with the Stars” will host its live finale on Tuesday on ABC and Disney+. The remaining competitors include podcaster and comedian Connor Wood partnered with Rylee Arnold, singer and actress Tatyana Ali partnered with Jan Ravnik, model Maura Higgins partnered with Mark Ballas, Olympic figure skater Amber Glenn partnered with Pasha Pashkov and Paralympic track star Ezra Frech partnered with Daniella Karagach.
