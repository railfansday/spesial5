Variety’s 10 Comics to Watch Bring Stand-Up, Sketch and Songs to Hilarious New York Comedy Festival Showcase

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Variety’s 10 Comics to Watch for 2026 brought laughter, cheers and ’90s rock parodies to the New York Comedy Festival on Thursday.

The comedians, who represent the funniest and most exciting rising voices in comedy, each performed a seven-minute set for a lively audience at the Venue on Music Row at the Hard Rock Hotel in Manhattan, where they also accepted awards.

Variety TV reporter Ethan Shanfeld delivered opening remarks before introducing Caroline Hirsch, founder of the New York Comedy Festival. Rafael Espinal, the city’s media and entertainment chief, also spoke and introduced Jake Lambert, who hosted the fete. Josh Ocean Thomas kicked off the showcase with an energetic punch and jokes about growing up mixed-race and going to Christian school. Adam Friedland brought a lectern on stage and performed a partly improvised bit that involved a lengthy acceptance speech for the award he had not yet been given. And Eilise Patton performed a solo sketch, playing a Goldman Sachs executive preparing to make a difficult phone call.

Shapel Lacey, Wally Baram, Usama Siddiquee and Olivia Carter delivered standout stand-up sets, and Kyle Gordon closed out the show with a full-band performance of his alt-rock satire “If You Break Up With Me” and the ode to nu metal “Crawl to Me,” in which he led the crowd in a NSFW chant: “Suck the dick, drink the piss.”

The comedians accepted their awards on stage and took a class photo with Shanfeld, Hirsch and veteran comedy talent booker Louis Faranda. The performers, attendees and industry folks then headed to the rooftop bar at the Hard Rock for drinks and light bites.

Variety started its 10 Comics to Watch franchise in 2000, honoring the likes of Zach Galifianakis, Maria Bamford, Patton Oswalt and Louis C.K. It’s become a prestigious industry accolade that the magazine has bestowed upon comics like Wanda Sykes, Fred Armisen, Nathan Fielder, Nate Bargatze and Nikki Glaser.
