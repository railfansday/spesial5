Video: Furious Lucca confronts De Bruyne on the pitch as Napoli tension escalates

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Tension at Napoli is skyrocketing as Lorenzo Lucca openly confronted Kevin De Bruyne during last night’s loss against Porto: ‘Why are you speaking? F*** off!’

Napoli crisis continued on Wednesday as the Partenopei suffered a 4-1 loss to Francesco Farioli’s Porto in Champions League Round 4.

Tension escalated in the second half after an ugly on-pitch confrontation between Lucca and De Bruyne.

Footage from Amazon Prime Italia shows the Italian striker hitting back at his teammate, going face-to-face with him and saying, ‘Why are you speaking?’ before repeatedly shouting, ‘F*** off!’

Napoli have lost seven of their 14 games across all competitions this season and sit 27th in the Champions League standings, outside a play-off place.
