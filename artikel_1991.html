Double boost for Matthias Jaissle on Newcastle United injury and fitness front

Matthias Jaissle hadn’t had the best of luck when it comes to injuries with his Newcastle United squad.

However, for the Arsenal match last weekend it looked like the treatment/recovery room was almost cleared.

Ewen Jaouen and Joelinton appearing to be the only senior players unable to be considered for the match against the league leaders, with Matthias Jaissle appearing to omit Aladji Bamba and Bazoumana Toure purely  by choice, with others favoured.

So after getting to that point of all but clearing the treatment room out and on the back of such a positive second half performance and excellent result against Arsenal, the last thing the United boss needed was to see any new injuries.

Just as an absorbing Arsenal game had gone into the nine minutes of added time, Lewis Hall was left limping after he had blocked a cross and tried to carry on, was then having to limp off a minute or so later and be replaced by Sven Botman.

Following the match, Matthias Jaissle asked whether Lewis Hall was now an injury worry: “Hopefully it is only just a cramp that meant Lewis Hall had to come off. That is what we are hoping.”

Newcastle United have now posted clips from the training ground ahead of the Brighton match and the great news is that they show Lewis Hall sprinting out with the rest of the first team squad for group training, clearly no lasting impact from Saturday and Jaissle’s cramp diagnosis/hope proved correct.

There is also other good news on the injury and fitness front.

It was feared that Joelinton would be out for a considerable time when suffering a muscle injury during the home defeat to Everton in early November.

However, a new media exclusive from The Mail has now reported that their inside sources have informed them that the prognosis isn’t as bad as had first been feared and that Joelinton is now expected to resume full training next week, ahead of the Man U and Sunderland matches at St James’ Park.

You would assume Man U is almost certainly too early but if he doesn’t have any setbacks, it sounds very much like a chance the Brazil international could potentially feature against the mackems.

Not that there were any fears raised about him after the weekend but it’s still great to see Tino Livramento heavily involved in the training footage released by the club on Wednesday.

He reminded us against Arsenal just how good he can be and a huge boost for everyone if he can now stay fit and be in the kind of form he showed on Saturday.

Matthias Jaissle was asked about the right-back after the Arsenal match: “Tino can definitely give us another dimension to our play but we also have to be careful and smart. He was really on the limit of what he could do. I was really proud that he could deliver 45 minutes with that physicality in such an intense match. It was really well played, a top performance, and also something that he can build on.”

A huge eight days of matches coming up and Matthias Jaissle needs as many of his key players as possible available, as United take on Brighton, Man U and Sunderland.
