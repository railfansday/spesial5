Sources: Jules Kounde pushing for January transfer amid Chelsea latest

Chelsea target Jules Kounde is becoming increasingly keen to leave Barcelona in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The right-back has fallen out of favour at the Camp Nou of late, and has started just three games in La Liga so far this season.

Chelsea are long-term admirers of the France international, having tried to sign him before he moved to Barcelona back in 2022, and they have continued to keep tabs on him.

Sources say that Kounde is desperate to be playing regular football at this stage of his career, and feels that his situation at Barcelona is unlikely to change.

The 28-year-old is angling for a move away from the Spanish giants, which could play perfectly into Chelsea's hands.

Kounde is vastly experienced, with three La Liga titles to his name, and he has also made 48 appearances in the Champions League.

Kounde is firmly on Chelsea's radar ahead of the January window, but he will not be their first port of call once the window opens.

He is not seen as a "priority" target for the West London club, who are hoping to strengthen in other areas before adding at right-back.

Xabi Alonso's side are already well-stocked in that area of the pitch, with Josh Acheampong, Reece James, Malo Gusto and Marca Palestra all adept at right-back.

However, a late move for Kounde is not off the table if Chelsea have money left over, or if one of those players departs Stamford Bridge.

The club have tracked the Frenchman for several years now, and may find it too good an opportunity to turn down in the end.

Football Insider revealed last weekend that Chelsea are set to demand £75million from Manchester City if they want to sign Gusto in January.

Enzo Maresca's side have kept a close eye on the 23-year-old across recent transfer windows, and are now being linked with him once again.

Chelsea are under no pressure to sell Gusto, but could be tempted if a lucrative offer were to be put on the table.

However, sources say that City's chances of meeeting that £75m asking price in the middle of the season are hugely unlikely.
