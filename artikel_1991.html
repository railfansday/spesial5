Portuguese media wrong about Man United’s Diogo Dalot after what he did vs Wales

Portuguese media were wrong to criticise Diogo Dalot's performance for his country against Wales despite his recent struggles at Man United.

Dalot, 27, has come under fire for his sub-par performances in a United shirt so far this season.

The Portuguese right-back is out of contract at Old Trafford in 2028 and recently surpassed 250 appearances for the club.

United are considering letting him leave in the coming months but he will be given chances to impress under new boss Mauricio Pochettino.

He impressed away from Old Trafford at the start of the Argentine's reign with a decent showing in Portugal's 3-2 win over Wales on Tuesday.

National media wrongly picked him out as a lacklustre performer, but the stats show that he impressed compared to his teammates.

Dalot is bound to attract interest from across Europe heading into the final 18 months of his United contract.

He remains a regular starter for Portugal, despite struggling at Old Trafford, and was vital to their Nations League win over Wales.

Dalot completed 92 per cent of his passes, created a chance, made six defensive contributions (third-most on the pitch) and completed the most dribbles (3) of any player.

He also made seven ball recoveries, the most of any outfielder, won four ground duels (third most) and kept Wales' right side quiet until going off in the 71st minute.

Dalot was easily one of the visitors' best performers on the night, yet his reputation seemed to earn him some bad reviews in the aftermath of their Welsh capital win.

Portuguese media painted him in a negative light despite those impressive stats, as GOAL.com said: "Dalot had a tough night dealing with the Welsh wingers' runs. He finished the match with little influence in the final third of the pitch."

While PortuGOAL wrote: "He was much improved compared with his disastrous performance against Denmark. However, his lack of pace was exposed during Wales' dangerous counter-attacks."

Sources have revealed to Football Insider that Pochettino will decide Dalot's future at United in the coming months as he gets used to his new squad.

He can certainly take confidence from his latest showing in a Portugal shirt, despite ongoing negativity from the media.
