Jurgen Klopp once again putting his trust in the Newcastle United defender.

In his first two internationals in charge, the new Germany boss saw his team concede in both Nations League matches and only pick up one point from a possible six.

Jurgen Klopp then selecting Malick Thiaw in all of the next three and after two clean sheets and four points from a possible six last month for Germany, it was another three points with Thiaw in the starting line up on Friday night.

Germany defeating Serbia 3-1 in Belgrade and the United defender having an excellent game and not at fault for the home side’s consolation goal which came when Germany were already 3-0 up.

There had been eyebrows raised in the German media when Jurgen Klopp selected Malick Thiaw ahead of Nico Schlotterbeck on Friday but that faith in the Newcastle player proving justified.

After the 3-1 win, the Goal player ratings showed only three (midfield/attacking players) with a higher rating than Malick Thiaw’s 7 out of 10, with Goal commenting on Thiaw: ‘Calm and composed at the heart of the defence. Thiaw’s ability to defend in large spaces allowed Germany to push their line high. He recorded several key clearances as Serbia attempted rare counter-attacks.’

Malick Thiaw looked excellent last season for Newcastle United when arriving in summer 2025 from AC Milan but mistakes started creeping in at times during the final months of a tough 58 game season, when too much was probably asked of the defender due to so many injuries.

An up and down start to this season for both player and team overall, saw Matthias Jaissle bring in Schar and Burn for Thiaw and Botman. However, Jaissle brought Malick Thiaw back into the United team at Fulham in the last match and I think paired with Dan Burn this could be the combination to go with for Newcastle this season.

Last night will have been huge confidence boost for Malick Thiaw, his performance against Serbia and the fact Jurgen Klopp kept faith with him for a third start in a row and now only one goal conceded by Germany in their last five hours of football with Thiaw in the team.

Germany are home to the Netherlands on Monday, interesting to see what his selection will be then, last night Malick Thiaw was paired with Jonathan Tah.
