Manchester United have agreed a deal in principle for Mauricio Pochettino to become their new manager, sources have told Football Insider.

A verbal agreement is already in place with the Argentine, who is set to leave his job in charge of the USA national team, following a breakthrough in talks on Tuesday.

Sir Jim Ratcliffe is set to rubber-stamp Michael Carrick's sacking on Wednesday, with Pochettino already in line to take the reins.

Sources say that the former Tottenham boss will move to Old Trafford alongside his long-standing assistants Jesus Perez and Miki D'Agostino, as well as goalkeeping coach Toni Jimenez.

United are set to pay Pochettino's £7.44million buy-out clause, with the club adamant that the fee is "not a problem" for them. They will also have to pay for his coaching staff to leave the USA team.

A decision regarding Carrick's future has been imminent in recent days after executives Omar Berrada and Jason Wilcox decided on Monday to recommend to the club's ownership that he should be dismissed.

United have been linked with moves for several bosses in recent weeks, but Pochettino has come from outsider status to win the race.

Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann have both been extensively linked with the role, while Aston Villa's Unai Emery was also a target at one stage.

However, it is Pochettino who has now come to the fore and will step into the home dugout at Old Trafford.

The 54-year-old is a long-term target for the United hierarchy, and they have finally been able to convince him to join.

Pochettino has never hidden his desire to return to the Premier League, and he will now get the chance to lead United back to glory.

Former Tottenham scout Bryan King told Football Insider earlier this week that United's players have "wanted Carrick out" for several weeks now.

The ex-recruitment man believes that there was a clear "breakdown" in communication between the 45-year-old and his group.

King outlined that the majority of the squad had completely lost faith in Carrick, and have not been playing for him in recent games.

The writing has been on the wall for the former midfielder for several weeks, and his exit is now set to be sealed.
