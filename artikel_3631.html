The studio is supposedly shopping the rights for $100 million

Rockstar is reportedly looking to adapt Red Dead Redemption for the big and small screens.

According to The Ankler, Rockstar has been "throwing around a purchase price of more than $100 million minimum for licensing rights" to the Red Dead Redemption franchise with the hopes of making a movie or a TV show.

In Red Dead Redemption, the player controls a former outlaw named John Marston whose wife and son are held hostage by the government in exchange for Marston's bounty hunter services. It's an open-world game that's set in the year 1911 and has some horror elements (and some weird things happen when you let the game idle for a bit, depending on what you're doing in that moment). Red Dead Redemption 2, a prequel to the first game, was released in 2018.

A Red Dead movie has been in demand for quite some time, dating all the way back to its 2010 release (with Brad Pitt initially tapped by fans as the perfect John Marston). Jack Black even said that Red Dead had a "better story than The Last of Us" and therefore deserved its own live-action adaptation.

Given that Far Cry, God of War, and Assassin's Creed are all in the works, and the fact that The Last of Us received some Emmy nominations... I'd say now is as good a time as any to turn your blockbuster video game into a live-action something or other.
