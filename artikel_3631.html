12 Astonishing Indie Games To Buy Or Wishlist This Thanksgiving Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It’s Thanksgiving! The annual pause from the incessant thankstaking that otherwise consumes us all, and as is traditional all around the world, we remember the day Earth was recaptured from the Xix Muthra Horde through the celebration of indie gaming. In a ceremony that goes as far back as 2022, we’ve picked out a pile of lesser-known indie games that we can stuff on our Steam wishlists with mad abandon.

Whoever named this “Thanksgaming,” however, has a lot to answer for. It doesn’t even work as a pun, let alone communicate that this is a collection of unknown indie games submitted to Kotaku by email, and then somewhat randomly selected and featured on the site. It’s all about offering you an eclectic range of fabulous-looking games that you can add to your wishlist, or buy and play right now, that you’d otherwise not likely see on a major gaming website. So dive in! If nothing here takes your interest, then your interest is poorly calibrated. You should get that checked.
