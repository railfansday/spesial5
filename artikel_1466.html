Gasperini puts Balde talks with Barcelona on hold

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Roma has other priorities and is not making any moves for Alejandro Balde, at least for now. With January fast approaching, Tony D’Amico is working to identify reinforcements to make available to Gasperini.

The primary objective remains improving the attack, which is why the option to sign the Barcelona full-back has been temporarily shelved.

Balde’s name was brought to the table by Jorge Mendes, who proposed the player to the Giallorossi.

However, no concrete negotiations have yet been initiated. Roma has considered the player, without pursuing any further contact regarding a possible transfer.

According to Tuttomercatoweb, the Barcelona full-back has also attracted the attention of other clubs. In Italy, AC Milan and Juventus have inquired, while abroad, Paris Saint-Germain has also expressed interest.

Roma, however, has chosen to focus its resources on other positions for now. D’Amico continues to have Endrick and Jamie Gittens at the top of his list, as the two main targets for strengthening the attack. These will be the options the club will focus on ahead of the winter transfer window, leaving the potential arrival of a new full-back on the back burner.
