The ranking of European top scorers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

This Monday is the ninth report in the ranking of European top scorers for the 2026/2027 season. We thus find the scorers who shook the nets in Germany, England, Belgium, Spain, France, Italy, Netherlands, Portugal and Türkiye. And as since the start of the season, Raphinha takes the lead. Mute against Villarreal during a 3-2 victory with the Catalan club, the Brazilian has 15 goals in 12 matches.

Just behind, we find Kylian Mbappé. The Real Madrid striker faced Celta de Vigo this Saturday and provided an assist in a 2-1 success. Not scoring during this match, the French striker has 13 goals in 13 matches. The podium is completed by Donyell Malen. The Dutch striker walks on water with AS Roma. Against Parma this Saturday, he scored a quadruple and brought his tally to 12 goals in 11 matches.

Fourth, Dušan Vlahović continues to shine in Türkiye. The Serbian striker bagged a brace against Konyaspor in a 4-2 win with Besiktas. Here he is now credited with 11 goals in 10 matches. Vangelis Pavlidis comes fifth with 11 goals in 11 matches. The Greek did not play in the league since Benfica beat Torreense in the Portuguese Cup (3-1). Sixth, Yassir Zabiri impresses in La Liga with 11 goals in 13 matches. The Moroccan scored a double with Racing Santander in the 3-2 success against Real Sociedad.

Niclas Füllkrug comes seventh with 10 goals in 10 matches and scored a new pawn with Werder Bremen against Hamburg (4-0). He is thus ahead of Gift Orban who was silent during Amedspor’s 2-0 loss against Rizespor. Ninth, Nicolo Tresoldi maintains the pace with 10 goals in 12 matches. The Bruges striker remains on a double against Mechelen (5-1). Finally, Lamine Yamal completes the ranking even if he did not score against Villarreal in a 3-2 victory.

Victor Osimhen (Galatasaray), Sven Mijnans (PSV Eindhoven), Gustavo Varela (Monza), Erling Haaland (Manchester City), Patrik Schick (Bayer Leverkusen), Lassine Sinayoko (Paris FC), Søren Tengstedt (Go Ahead Eagles), Thom van Bergen (Groningen), Brynjólfur Willumsson (Groningen), Wout Weghorst (Twente), Mohamed Salah (Trabzonspor), Luis Suárez (Sporting CP) and Adrian Benedyczak (Kasimpasa) failed to reach the top 10 with 9 goals. Amine Gouiri (Olympique de Marseille), Norman Bassette (Westerlo), Dennis Eckert (Standard de Liège), Eldor Shomurodov (Başakşehir), Antonio Raimondo (Frosinone) and Ante Budimir (Osasuna) follow with 8 goals.
