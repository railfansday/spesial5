Leonard Fournette calls out NFL hypocrisy as he eviscerates Mike McCarthy and backs Aaron Rodgers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Leonard Fournette has launched a furious attack on Pittsburgh Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy while defending Aaron Rodgers amid their ongoing fallout. Fournette also highlighted what he sees as media hypocrisy: players are routinely held accountable for their actions while coaches escape the same scrutiny.

Rodgers was initially benched in favour of backup quarterback Mason Rudolph after the Steelers' frustrating defeat to the Philadelphia Eagles last weekend. The 42-year-old then publicly criticised McCarthy in an interview with Pat McAfee, giving Pittsburgh no choice but to release him on Wednesday.

Fournette was particularly critical of reports that McCarthy had fallen asleep during team meetings when he should be setting the standard for everyone in the building. He also praised Steelers players for supporting Rodgers and suggested the media often takes a different approach when criticism is directed at coaches.

Fournette ranted: "When you have players backing up Aaron Rodgers, saying he falls asleep in meetings… you're supposed to be the example; you're supposed to be the head. On with your a–! I'm glad these players are coming out saying this and I'm glad Aaron Rodgers came out saying this because, when it's the players' fault, the media is on the players' a–; people believe everything the coach tells them."

The former Jacksonville Jaguars and Tampa Bay Buccaneers running back then suggested Rodgers has little reason to stay quiet after everything he has achieved. Fournette believes the four-time MVP had every right to reveal what has happened behind the scenes in Pittsburgh.

Fournette continued: "Aaron Rodgers is a Hall of Famer in the back end of his career, has made all the money and got everything. He does not give a d— about what anybody is talking about. He's about to spill the beans. Not only that, [McCarthy] has a personal ice cream sandwich assistant. Coach, get your fat a– on."

Fournette's comments add to the growing criticism surrounding McCarthy following Rodgers' departure. The Steelers head coach now faces questions not only about his handling of the quarterback, but his coaching of the entire team.
