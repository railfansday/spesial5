148 balls for a hundred! Tilak Varma enters unwanted record books after India A's defeat to Pakistan A

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Tilak Varma's century against Pakistan A in the Asia Cup Rising Stars tournament went into the record books, but for an unwanted reason, as the India A captain registered the second-slowest hundred by an Indian batter in List A cricket.Tilak took 148 balls to reach his century, putting him behind only former India batter Manoj Prabhakar, who holds the record with a 151-ball hundred. The knock came during India's 48-run defeat to Pakistan A in a Group B encounter on Sunday.Chasing a challenging target of 314, India A were bowled out for 265 despite Tilak's hundred and an explosive 70 from Abhishek Sharma. While Tilak eventually reached the three-figure mark, his innings was a slow-burning effort, with his half-century itself taking 95 deliveries.The contrast between the two batters was evident from the beginning of the chase. Abhishek went on the offensive, smashing six fours and as many sixes in his 42-ball 70, while Tilak struggled to find his rhythm. At one stage, when Abhishek had raced to 60 off 34 balls, Tilak had managed just six runs off the same number of deliveries.Tilak's slow start put the middle order under pressure as the required run rate continued to climb. Abhishek's dismissal proved costly, with the remaining batters finding it difficult to maintain the scoring momentum against Pakistan's bowling attack.The India A captain eventually found some boundaries towards the latter stages of his innings and combined with Shahbaz Ahmed for a 112-run seventh-wicket partnership. Shahbaz contributed 56 off 41 balls, but his dismissal in the 43rd over left India with too much to do.India A eventually folded for 265 in 49.1 overs, falling 48 runs short of the target. Tilak's century, therefore, could not prevent his side from suffering defeat, while his scoring rate became a major talking point after the match.
