Dylan Harper could be back in action when Spurs host Lakers on Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Spurs guard Dylan Harper could make his return to the court as soon as Friday night after the team listed him as questionable for its showdown with the Los Angeles Lakers at the Frost Bank Center.

Harper has missed the last four games after he suffered a left hip external rotator strain late in the Spurs' 105-93 loss to Detroit on Nov. 17. The injury was expected to sideline him for two to three weeks, but the Spurs upgraded him to questionable after Wednesday night's 116-111 loss at Portland.

Harper, 20, ranks as the Spurs' second-leading scorer behind Victor Wembanyama at 18.4 points per game. He's also averaging 5.4 rebounds and 4.5 assists in 15 appearances.

The Spurs are 3-1 without Harper, who has been replaced in the starting lineup by Julian Champagnie.

In other injury news, the Spurs upgraded guard De'Aaron Fox to questionable after he sat out the last two games with a bruised right shin. The Spurs started Harrison Barnes the last two games to round out a starting lineup that included Stephon Castle, Devin Vassell, Champagnie and Wembanyama.

Forward Tobias Harris remains out with a left calf strain. The 15-year-veteran suffered the injury early in training camp after the Spurs signed him in the offseason to a two-year, $31 million contract.

Friday's NBA Cup in-season tournament group play matchup features the two top teams in the Western Conference in the Lakers (15-3) and the Spurs (15-4).

Set to start at 8:30 p.m., the game will be televised by Prime.
