Syria Weighs Sending Troops to Aid Saudi War With Houthis

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Syrian reconnaissance teams have visited Saudi Arabia and Yemen ahead of a potential deployment, but a final decision has not been made, officials said.

Syria is weighing whether to send thousands of troops to Saudi Arabia and Yemen to support the kingdom in its war with the Iranian-backed Houthi militia, according to three Syrian military and Yemeni officials with direct knowledge of the government’s plans.

Military reconnaissance teams have visited Saudi Arabia and Yemen in recent weeks to scout positions and coordinate with their counterparts in both countries, according to the officials.

The Syrian government has not made a decision to deploy troops, the Syrian officials said. If it chooses to do so, they added, it would probably be as part of the Mecca Joint Defense Agreement, a mutual-defense pact signed by Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Pakistan in August. The agreement includes a collective defense principle that considers an attack on any of its members as an attack on all. Syria’s foreign minister said last month that the country was considering joining.

The main objective of any deployment would be to protect Saudi borders from any ground incursions by the Houthis, the Syrian officials said. The potential deployment has been previously reported by other media, including Agence France-Presse, the news agency. Any engagement, however, risks embroiling Syria in the kingdom’s widening conflict with the Houthis.

Last month Saudi military officers, soldiers and some 150 Syrian soldiers and commanders visited the Thamud district in northeastern Yemen, along the Saudi border, according to a Yemeni officer in the area.
