Matkahuolto selects Aurionpro to advance seamless, sustainable public transport payments across Finland

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Aurionpro's contactless validation and payment technology will support Matkahuolto's cEMV project, enabling acceptance of EMV and local contactless card standards across Finland

MUMBAI, India and HELSINKI, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Aurionpro Solutions, a global technology solutions provider serving the banking, payments, mobility, and transit sectors, today announced that Matkahuolto, Finland's long-standing provider of parcel and digital transport services, has selected Aurionpro as the device vendor for its contactless EMV (cEMV) project following a competitive RFP process.

Under a Master Service Agreement, Aurionpro will provide its SCV 500 Validator and ECR-one contactless card reader, together with integration to Matkahuolto's Transport Management System (TMS) and APIs for payment-application connectivity. The solution will enable the acceptance of EMV payment cards alongside Finland's local contactless card standards, delivering a seamless and reliable fare-payment experience across the public transport network.

Petri Lindqvist, Director, Digital Transport Services, Matkahuolto, said:

"We are delighted to partner with Aurionpro. The collaboration will help us deliver a more seamless and convenient travel experience for passengers across Finland. By adding cEMV capability to our solution, we can further improve support for tickets from different operators on Matkahuolto's onboard solutions and, in doing so, help build more flexible and accessible public transport across Finland."

Sanjay Bali, President & Global Head, Tech Innovation Group, Aurionpro, said:

"We are grateful to Matkahuolto for choosing Aurionpro as its partner for this important initiative. We are proud to support Matkahuolto's vision to make public transport more accessible, convenient, and attractive for people across Finland. This engagement marks our entry into the European market and is a significant milestone in expanding our global transit business. We are confident that this is the beginning of a strong pipeline of opportunities across Europe. Our contactless validation and payment capabilities, combined with TMS and payment-application integration, will help enable a seamless payment experience while supporting the acceptance of international EMV cards and local contactless payment standards."

Matkahuolto is a modern service company that has been bringing people and goods together seamlessly for more than 90 years. The company's business is divided into Parcel Services and Digital Transport Services. Parcel Services transport 20 million parcels a year in Finland and abroad, and Matkahuolto's customers are served by a nationwide network of 2,550 service points. Digital Transport Services focus on sustainable mobility services, timetables and tickets, enabling over 7 million journeys annually. For transport operators, the company provides digital tools for business development. Matkahuolto is committed to science-based climate targets, and the company aims to halve all its emissions by 2030. In 2025, Matkahuolto's turnover was EUR 98.5 million. www.matkahuolto.fi
