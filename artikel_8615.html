Choose Kikoff if you can spare only $5 a month to build credit, since its Basic plan reports a $750 credit line to all three credit bureaus. None of the $60 a year comes back, so if you can pay more each month, pick a credit-builder loan, which returns most of what you pay.

Each month, Kikoff charges your plan’s fee to the Kikoff Credit Account, a revolving line from Kikoff Lending, LLC, and you repay it. Signing up takes no credit check, so it suits someone with thin or damaged credit.

Kikoff is worth $5 a month for someone who can spare no more and wants a revolving account on all three credit reports. A reader who can pay $25 a month does better with Self’s smallest credit-builder loan, which costs $89 in interest over 24 months, against $120 for 24 months of Kikoff Basic, and returns $511 of the $600 paid.
