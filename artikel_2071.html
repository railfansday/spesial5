Aladji Bamba (pictured above with Borussia Dortmund’s Ethan Nwaneri) started in midfield for the France Under 21s at Ashton Gate on Monday night against their English counterparts.

Moore and Mubama put the England Under 21s 2-0 up within half an hour at the home of Bristol City.

Aladji Bamba then one of three French players to be subbed on 64 minutes.

The Newcastle United midfielder watched on from the sidelines as his teammates staged a comeback, scoring in the 80th and 85th minutes.

However, in the 89th minute it was Arsenal’s Max Dowman who got the winning goal in this Under 21s friendly.

Elsewhere, a remarkable night in Berlin as Jurgen Klopp saw his Germany side play out their sixth match under his leadership.

His reign had started in dismal fashion with defeat to Greece and a draw with the Netherlands in their opening two Nations League group games.

Jurgen Klopp then made a number of changes which included bringing in Malick Thiaw and in their next three games, Germany picked up seven points from a possible nine and only conceded one goal.

Having played the full match in the win over Serbia on Friday, the Newcastle United defender was rested last night in Berlin.

Germany pulling off a stunning 5-0 victory over the Netherlands as they somehow came back from what looked an impossible position to win their Nations League group.

Serbia ended the group without a single point but the other three nations all with 11 points, Jurgen Klopp and his players just edging out the Netherlands and Greece.

Earlier on Monday, another full match and another international victory for Newcastle United’s Ousmane Diabaté, the exciting midfield prospect played the full match as the 19-year-old helped Guinea to a 3-1 AFCON qualifying group win over Eritrea.

Ousmane Diabaté was still only 18 when signing for Newcastle United this summer but already a full international for Guinea. He was also a first choice regular for his club side in the Turkish top tier, United agreeing Diabaté could stay with Gençlerbirliği this season in order to continue to get regular first team football at club level.
