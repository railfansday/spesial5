'It will end in tears' - Expert slams Jim Ratcliffe after 'diabolical' Man United decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Sir Jim Ratcliffe has only made the situation at Manchester United worse since his arrival.

That is the opinion of experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

INEOS, led by the billionaire, became minority owners of the Red Devils in 2024, and have since been in control of the majority football operations.

Ratcliffe was involved in the decision to dismiss Michael Carrick last week, but had reportedly wanted Eddie Howe appointed to replace him.

Majority owners, the Glazer family, are said to have overruled him by appointing Mauricio Pochettino instead.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea boss will take charge for the first time on Sunday, when Man United visit Liverpool at Anfield.

Pochettino will hope to bring stability to Man United, but he will need to get those above him on board.

Manager after manager has failed at Old Trafford, with the board and the ownership seemingly incapable of creating a good environment.

"I just think that the whole palaver here will end in tears, and I hope it does," King told Football Insider. "I really hope it does.

"Because the way they've gone about running that football club for the last 10 years is diabolical. Ever since Ratcliffe came in, it's gone from one bit of chaos to the next.

"What you have to look at here is the way he and his company are running it. Great businessman, but football as a business is, you have to have a totally different approach to the way you're running your own company, INEOS.

"You can't take that strategy into a football club like Man United. Through him, the people that have gone, and he's got rid of a lot of people, the new people he's brought in are certainly not up to the job.

"So why are they still there? And they are still there. Now he's replaced Carrick and at least six members of staff at God knows what cost, and he's bringing in this fella who's going to be on big wages, by the way."

Pochettino has insisted that he has the support of those above him at Man United.

"I feel all the support. We feel all the support from the directors and the owners," the Argentine said after his appointment was confirmed.

"And of course, now it's up to us to provide what they expect from us.

"We have clear idea what the club expect from us and we want to be in the level of the demand of this football club."
