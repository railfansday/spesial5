Aston Villa owners could face 'legal issues' over 'threat' to Jamie Carragher - Stefan Borson

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Aston Villa's owners could have landed themselves in hot water after threatening to take legal action against Jamie Carragher.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that there was no legal standing to the club's "empty threat".

Carragher, formerly of Liverpool, sparked a lot of anger online after suggesting that Villa will "never be Manchester United" in a debate around Unai Emery's future.

It would appear that Emery is going to put pen to paper on a new deal until 2029 at Villa Park, but he remains a target for those at Old Trafford.

Mick Brown has told Football Insider that the Spaniard's new contract is unlikely to stop his exit before 2029, suggesting that Carragher's comments might be correct.

That didn't stop Nassef Sawiris, the Villa chairman, from threatening legal action against the Sky Sports pundit, which caused him to issue an apology.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson believes that Villa could be have been the ones to face legal consequences from the situation.

"It was a legal threat. Actually, it probably would have given them some issues from the Solicitors Regulatory Authority," he said.

"It looked like an empty threat to me, designed to suppress opinion. There are no laws against Jamie Carragher giving a football opinion, and one that generally is probably right anyway.

"I think you'd be pretty hard-pushed to say that there isn't a hierarchy and that Real Madrid aren't on top of it, that Manchester United are one of the leading English clubs and Aston Villa are not.

"It seems pretty uncontroversial to me. They couldn't have stopped him legally. Nobody likes the negative opinions being out there, but it's just the way of the world, right?"

All points currently point towards Emery putting pen to paper at Villa Park, but nothing has been confirmed just yet.

Fans at Old Trafford would love for him to replace Michael Carrick this season, but that now looks almost impossible for the Red Devils.

It has been suggested that Emery would relish the opportunity to play an even bigger role behind the scenes on a long-term basis.

He has already achieved so much on the pitch, and it is clear just how much he loves the Villans. Those at Villa Park love him just as much too.
