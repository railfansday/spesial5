Stefan Borson drops Unai Emery bombshell after Aston Villa latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Unai Emery's future as Aston Villa manager is still not totally secure despite him being set to sign a new deal this season.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that the only plausible move away would be back to Spain.

Emery appears set to put pen to paper on a new contract at Villa Park, extending his stay with the Villans until the summer of 2029.

The 55-year-old had been heavily linked with the Manchester United job, but Villa's owners are sending a huge message of intent instead.

Having lifted the Europa League title in 2025-26, it is easy to see why Emery is so sought-after, and also why he could become one of the highest-paid managers in Europe.

A new deal doesn't automatically rule out a move in the coming seasons, though, with some giant clubs surely keeping an eye on his achievements.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson believes the only way that Emery leaves Villa Park is if Real Madrid or Barcelona come knocking.

"I think the risk really is probably only Real Madrid or Barcelona. Those clubs are very hard to turn down," he said.

"He's got it pretty good at Villa. He's got a team that he's happy with. He somehow managed to get them continuing to do pretty well, having sold all of those players in the summer.

"Obviously they bought a lot of players as well, but it's an amazingly challenging situation to have to sell the spine of your team and still perform on the field, especially in the Champions League.

"I don't think that he's necessarily completely unable to ever move again, but I don't think the circumstances are right for a move anywhere except back to Spain."

There were some serious questions being asked about the manager's future at the start of the season after a poor run of results.

All of that has been left in Villa's rearview mirror, though, with Villa now sitting 10th in the Premier League table and sixth in the Champions League.

As for their chances of Premier League success, Opta has given Villa a 0.36 per cent chance of winning the title, while Champions League glory stands at 2.65 per cent.

A top-four finish and a decent run in Europe would likely be classed as a success this season, especially given the difficult summer window.
