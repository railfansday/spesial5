Why One Agency Traded Billable Hours for a Stake in a DTC Steak Brand

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

It’s not every day that a glittering gold briefcase, or “beefcase,” with a marbled 55-ounce Tomahawk steak winds up on one’s doorstep. But that’s exactly what restaurateur David Einhorn is delivering in his new direct-to-consumer steak brand called Papi Ranch, a joint-venture with creative agency ImaginaryFriend.

ImaginaryFriend has a 6% equity stake in Papi Ranch, Einhorn’s latest brainchild launched in October, an offshoot of his two Papi Steak “foodtainment” steakhouses in Miami and Las Vegas.

As part of the deal, ImaginaryFriend will serve as the lead creative and strategic partner for Papi Ranch, offering the brand a reduced agency fee in kind.
