Salesloft: Salesforce Business Systems Administrator

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

This is a fully remote opportunity and can be worked from any location in the United States.

Salesloft is building the next era of enterprise revenue — one where teams make confident decisions powered by AI and real signals. By combining our scale, insights, and AI innovation, we're building the industry's first Predictive Revenue System, enabling humans and AI to work together to make smarter decisions and drive consistent growth.

With thousands of customers using our platforms every day, we have an unmatched view into how revenue is actually won — the Revenue Context that reveals what happens, when, and with what outcome. This gives us a unique opportunity to transform an entire category and set a new benchmark for how modern revenue teams operate.

Join us to help transform how companies around the world run revenue — and build the platform that will guide leading revenue teams into the future.

At Salesloft, our Business Systems Administrator, Customer Experience is critical to our company’s success. You will be a key member of our fast-growing and high-performing Business Systems team and will lead generating, scoping, solutioning, and executing process and efficiency improvement efforts across customer-focused systems. You will have a passionate, capable team supporting you.

You will be responsible for the support, development, and maintenance of Salesforce and its related Customer Experience systems. You must own the pursuit of a world-class technology experience for our customers, while enabling scale and efficiency for internal customer experience teams - Support, Services, and Success. You need to have pride in the work you do and a passion for customer support and success.

This role lives at the heart of how we Make Your Mark: you'll treat systems administration as a discipline — automating, documenting, and fixing root causes so the same issues don't recur. And in the spirit of how we are Customer Inspired — where every decision has a true north, and it's our customer — you'll administer access and configuration as the security controls they are, protecting the company and the data customers trust us with.

We are seeking a Salesforce CRM, and related customer experience systems, expert. Someone that can see the big picture, future state but has a keen eye for details. You will have a knack for spotting opportunities to improve systems and process, and are excited to work cross-functionally to make it happen. You are passionate to build relationships to help others succeed.

If you’re looking for an opportunity to learn more, do more, and become more, then becoming aBusiness Systems Administrator is the career path for you!

Our Salesloft’s Revenue Operations team is comprised of seasoned and up-and-coming operational experts.

Values: Move as One, Evidence over Ego, Lead from the Front, Make Your Mark, Customer-Inspired.

The Revenue Operations team consists of team members that work with Sales, Marketing, Customer Services, Finance, and IT to create systems to more efficiently prospect, forecast, close, onboard, and support customers. Team members conceptualize the strategic priorities for the company, develop execution plans, execute plans cross-functionally, and operationalize executed strategies for continuous improvement.
