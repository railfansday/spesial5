Cardi B Reveals Name of Baby No. 4, Her First With Stefon Diggs

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Cardi B announced the name of her and NFL player Stefon Diggs' baby boy, who she revealed last November that they had welcomed.

Cardi B revealed the name of her and NFL player Stefon Diggs' baby boy in honor of his first birthday. While Cardi shares kids Kulture, 8, Wave, 5, and Blossom, 2, with ex Offset, her youngest son is her first child with the New England Patriots wide receiver.

"Happy 1st birthday to Grey Marsean Diggs!!" Cardi wrote on Instagram Nov. 4 alongside sweet pictures of Grey. "It’s only been 1 year since meeting you but it feels like I’ve had you at my side forever. I carried you through one of the most uncertain high pressured times of my life but having you inside of me through it all gave me a extra strength."

That’s why her little guy’s name serves as a special reminder to keep her spirits up.

“I was growing you inside of me and at the same time you were giving me life!” she added. “You gave a new meaning to what a Grey day feels like. You light up my life and every room with your smile your laugh and your precious spirit.”

And, naturally, the mom of four made sure her son’s first Halloween had a sweet link to his moniker. After all, she also shared an adorable pic of the toddler sporting scrubs while sitting in front of a Grey’s Anatomy sign.

The "Hello" rapper’s post also marked the reveal of her baby's birth date. Last year, she shared that she welcomed her newest addition through a message marking her life changes in November.

"A new baby into my world, and one more reason to be the best version of me, one more reason to love me more than anything else or anyone else so I can continue giving my babies the love and life they deserve," she captioned her post at the time, a video of her strutting and striking poses to her song "Hello." She continued, "This next chapter is Me vs. Me!"

And the next chapter already looks good on her. Just hours after the announcement that she's now a mom of four, Cardi stepped out with Stefon for the launch party of baby brand Cybex’s flagship store in New York City, rocking a dramatic all-black ensemble featuring a corseted puffer coat, bubble skirt and fur detailing.

While Cardi opted to keep her lips sealed regarding her baby boy's name at the time, she did hint that it was one she really loved.

"I'm not sharing the name yet," she told Highsnobiety at the Cybex event. "It's cute."

While the name stayed under wraps, the 34-year-old did share a bit into what her birth and post-partum experience has been like, adding, "You know, I was a little bit on bed rest because that baby, oh, that baby was heavy."

However, her toughness is what she hopes her children will learn from her.

"I will stand up for myself," she said of how she leads by example. "Sometimes people be like, 'Oh no avoid it.' No, I will stand up for myself. Then my kids can see that they mama ain't no punk."
