Jenna Dewan Claps Back at "Know It All" Claim After DWTS Elimination

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Less than a week after Jenna Dewan was sent home from Dancing With the Stars, the Step Up alum clapped back at a comment claiming she was eliminated for being a “know it all.”

Outside of the ballroom, Jenna Dewan is taking no critiques.

Less than a week after the Step Up alum—along with her partner Val Chmerkovskiy—was eliminated from Dancing With the Stars, she clapped back at a comment claiming she was ousted for being a “know it all.”

In a comment on a since-deleted Instagram video, Dewan cheekily wrote, “If I knew it all, I probably would’ve known I was going home.”

The video—which was deleted on Instagram but still up on TikTok—saw a viewer dancing in front of her horse stables as she shared her “hot take” that Dewan was not only a know-it-all, but also “had zero chemistry” with Chmerkovskiy and the audience because she “was so hard to relate to.”

While Dewan didn’t address the fan’s other concerns, the 45-year-old—mom to Everly, 13, with ex Channing Tatum as well as Callum, 6, and Rhiannon, 2, with fiancé Steve Kazee—did take a moment to send some love to the post as well, nodding toward the fan’s animal, “PS beautiful horse.”

Dewan was eliminated during the Nov. 10 episode of DWTS, a decision that many were shocked by considering she was firmly on the leaderboard throughout her nine-week run on the show.

And although The Rookie alum was visibly gutted to be sent home, she emphasized how amazing the experience had been for her.

“Dancing again, getting to share my joy with my family, who's over here every single night—thank you, my baby—I had a great time,” she said. “And I loved getting to share that with the world. Honestly, all love.”

Meanwhile, Chmerkovskiy shared some sage advice from his and Jenna Johnson’s 3-year-old son Rome in wake of the duo’s elimination.

“It’s OK to lose right?” Chmerkovskiy asked the toddler in a video shared to his Instagram Nov. 11, to which Rome replied, “Yeah, you try your best. If you don't win sometimes, you try to do it so many times.”

Chmerkovskiy admitted he was focusing on the good, telling his son, “Even though I lost and I'm sad, I feel already better because I have you in my life.”
