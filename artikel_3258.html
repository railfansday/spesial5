Amanda Seyfried To Host ‘Saturday Night Live’

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Amanda Seyfried will make her Saturday Night Live hosting debut in the December 5 episode, joined by musical guest Don Toliver. The NBC sketch comedy made the announcement during the Nov. 14 episode hosted by John Mulaney with musical guest Phoebe Bridgers, the last before a Thanksgiving hiatus that will strretch to Dec. 5.

While Seyfried has a new movie to promote, Netflix’s animated Steps, which debuts Nov. 20, it is her unexpected double turn as herself and Elizabeth Holmes in Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim’s hit documentary You Can See Everything that has everyone talking about the Oscar-nominated actress who won an Emmy for portraying Holmes in the Hulu limited series The Dropout.

Saturday Night Live is produced in association with Broadway Video. The creator and executive producer is Lorne Michaels. The show airs live on NBC and Peacock Saturdays at 11:30 p.m. ET/8:30 pm. PT. Watch on Deadline
