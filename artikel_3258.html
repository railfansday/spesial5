Melissa Gilbert Gives Thanks To Those Who “Spoke Up, Testified” In Support Of Husband Timothy Busfield

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It was an extra special Thanksgiving holiday for Melissa Gilbert. The Little House on the Prairie actress gave a shoutout Thursday to all those “who checked in … spoke up, testified and wrote letters” after her husband Timothy Busfield was charged with two counts of criminal sexual contact with a minor and child abuse. A New Mexico judge dismissed them in October.

The charges stemmed from alleged incidents involving two minor boys on the New Mexico set of the Fox/Warner Bros TV series The Cleaning Lady. Busfield, who directed several episodes of Cleaning Lady during its four-season run from 2022-25, said the claims were “absolutely one gazillion percent untrue. “This Thanksgiving I”m incredibly grateful for all those who came running toward us this year,” Gilbert wrote on Instagram. “Our undying gratitude also, to our extraordinary team who bravely fought and continue to fight so hard for what is right and true. On this day, as we all acknowledge the blessings in our lives, we send our love and eternal gratitude to each and every one of you.” Watch on Deadline

The charges were tossed last month after the “prosecuting attorney failed to act in a fair and impartial manner, including the failure to properly instruct the grand jury on the law for calling witnesses and in instructing the grand jurors on a procedure that effectively withheld requested witnesses,” Judge Joseph Montano wrote in an order quashing the indictment. “In doing so, the grand jury proceedings were conducted in a manner that violated grand jury statutes designed to protect the structural integrity of our grand jury system and the presumption of prejudice applies.”

Montano gave prosecutors the option to “institute new criminal proceedings” against Busfield; each count in the tossed indictment carried a maximum sentence of six years behind bars.
