‘Yaga’ Creator Wanted To Sexualize Men “The Way Female Bodies Are Looked At” In Thriller Genre

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

As the dark mystery at the center of Yaga unfolds, the Crave series has turned up the steam with some guy-on-guy scenes.

Series creator Kat Sandler explained that “it definitely was a choice” to have “objectified” Charlie (Noah Reid) and Henry (Hudson Williams) in the episode “The Horns,” in the same “way that female bodies are looked at” in the thriller genre.

“We’re presenting these two stunning men in this way that you’re used to seeing women,” said Sandler on the show’s official podcast.

“You look at Basic Instinct—it’s not the same thing—but it’s a choice to look at male bodies in this show the way that female bodies are looked at it thrillers and in horrors and in mysteries, repeatedly,” she added. “So, it was definitely a choice, but I stand by that it’s important to the story.” Watch on Deadline

In Yaga, private investigator Charlie Rapp (Reid) is hired to find Henry Park (Williams), the heir to a commercial fishery who has gone missing, while suspicion grows around Dr. Katherine Hargrove (Carrie-Anne Moss), a university professor with an interest in the Slavic witch legend Baba Yaga.

New episodes of Yaga are available to stream Fridays on Crave in Canada and AMC+ in the US, with the final two episodes set for Nov. 27.
