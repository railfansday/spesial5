7 Best Oushak Rugs Fit for Royalty, Vetted by AD (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Few home furnishings can make an ordinary room feel quite so grand as Oushak rugs. Named for the Turkish town of Uşak, where the weaving tradition began, these hand-knotted floor coverings gained popularity during the Ottoman Empire and were eventually exported across Europe. Their designs are recognizable for their large-scale medallions, geometric borders, and floral motifs, many of which drew inspiration from Ottoman art and architecture. The result is a style that’s decorative but never overly fussy, which helps to explain how it has withstood the test of time.

An Oushak rug can sit comfortably beneath a traditional dining table, add some contrast to a pared-back living room, or bring some personality to an otherwise neutral bedroom. And while antique examples are certainly worth seeking out, plenty of modern Oushak rugs capture the same Old-World grandeur. Put simply, you may not be a sultan, but you can certainly decorate like one.
