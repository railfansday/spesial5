Ads of the Week: 11 Campaigns That Caught Our Eye, From McDonald’s to Bumble

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

This week’s big starring roles go to Macaulay Culkin, all alone (again) for Christmas at McDonald’s, and Pete Davidson and Alix Earle, who go on a date for Bumble. Pamela Anderson tends the garden for her Sonsie brand, and two Tom Bradys face off for Chase.

NHL heroes Connor McDavid and Terry Ryan team up for BetMGM, and a trio of famous felines appear for Meow Mix.

Elsewhere, PayPal salutes all the work families put in to make the holidays special. Dunkin’ brings more than a little sweetness to your daily coffee, and QuickBooks doubles your business brainpower. Meanwhile, Bark swaps Black Friday deals for adoptable dogs named Air Fryer and Robot Vacuum.

Matt Barker is a contributing writer for ADWEEK. He has previously written for Campaign, Marketing Week, and Condé Nast.
