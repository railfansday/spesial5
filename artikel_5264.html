Strategy MSCI Verdict Nears as Anthropic IPO Puts Billions on the Line

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Wall Street faces two major tests of institutional appetite as the Strategy MSCI decision approaches. The index provider will publish its November review results shortly after 11:00 PM CET today. Strategy’s inclusion remains a major concern for Bitcoin investors.

Meanwhile, Anthropic is preparing one of the largest IPOs in history. The Claude developer is targeting up to $64.4 billion from its share offering, with a valuation reaching $1.02 trillion before dilution. Its listing will test if institutional investors still have an appetite for enormous technology bets after SpaceX.

The timing creates an interesting contrast for financial markets. Strategy could face billions in potential passive fund selling, while Anthropic is asking investors to commit billions more to artificial intelligence. Each could reveal where Wall Street wants its next wave of capital to go.

Strategy position in MSCI indexes faces scrutiny under new eligibility rules targeting non-operating companies. The methodology could affect businesses whose financial structures resemble investment funds rather than traditional operating companies.

Strategy holds an enormous Bitcoin treasury, and its business model revolves around raising capital through equity and debt offerings to accumulate more BTC.

MSCI previously identified Strategy and Japan’s Metaplanet among companies potentially affected by its review. Now, other digital asset treasury companies also face scrutiny under the new framework.

The potential financial consequences are substantial. JPMorgan previously estimated that removing Strategy could trigger $2.8 billion in selling from passive funds tracking MSCI indexes. But exclusion would not force Strategy to liquidate its Bitcoin holdings. Index-tracking funds would sell MSTR shares, potentially weakening its valuation and access to equity financing.

Yet, a weaker share price could make future fundraising more expensive. This could eventually reduce the company’s ability to accumulate Bitcoin at its previous pace.

MSCI’s announcement is scheduled for shortly after 11:00 PM CET on November 11. Any changes will become effective after the November 30 market close.

While Strategy faces potential exclusion, Anthropic is preparing to raise an extraordinary amount of institutional capital. The company plans to sell 825 million shares at $70 to $78 apiece, potentially raising $64.4 billion. Its valuation could reach $1.02 trillion before dilution, or $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis.

The offering also includes a separate $26 billion private placement involving major investors, including Google, Nvidia and AMD. Combined proceeds could reach $90.4 billion.

Anthropic’s growth makes the offering difficult for investors to ignore. Its annualized revenue run rate reached $17 billion quarterly, according to its IPO filing.

However, the company also faces enormous infrastructure commitments. Its computing agreements with partners approach $900 billion, showing the extraordinary costs behind the AI expansion.

The Anthropic IPO arrives at a complicated moment for technology stocks. Investors are questioning whether AI companies can generate enough revenue to justify their infrastructure spending and enormous valuations.
