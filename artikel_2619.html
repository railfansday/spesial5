What Aaron Rodgers ‘took most issue’ with before burning it all down

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

There was one thing that set Aaron Rodgers over the edge as he thought about the news of his benching circulating the NFL.

"It was the benching he took most issue with," ESPN's NFL insider Ian Rapoport reported while on "Good Morning Football" on Thanksgiving morning — a day after Rodgers' release that resulted from the roast of Mike McCarthy.

"There was a lot of football issues going on," Rapoport said. "Rodgers, obviously, was removed as the starting quarterback, seemed to take issue with anyone thinking what the Steelers insinuated that Mason Rudolph was a better option at starting quarterback than Rodgers."

Head coach Mike McCarthy talks with Aaron Rodgers #8 of the Pittsburgh Steelers during the game against the Cincinnati Bengals at Acrisure Stadium on September 27, 2026 in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Getty Images

"The reality is, this isn't, is Aaron Rodgers as good as Mason Rudolph? That's really not the issue. The issue started with how fit is Mike McCarthy to be the head coach and play caller."

Rapoport added Rodgers, along with quarterbacks coach Tom Arth, called plays during the second half, and some in the second quarter, of last week's loss against the Eagles in Philadelphia.

Afterwards, the insider reported that the Super Bowl champ went to the organization "to change things" because he didn't believe that McCarthy "should be calling plays."

"I want to call and if I can't call, Tom Arth needs to be the one to send me the plays and have a heavy hand in calling it because Rodgers did not believe Mike McCarthy could do it," Rapoport reported before adding the two parties had several "intense" discussions.

McCarthy backed Rodgers as his quarterback following their 19-16 loss to the Eagles on Monday. Yet, the Steelers announced Rodgers' benching and that he would be inactive for Friday's game against the Broncos late Tuesday night in a social media post.

Wednesday is when everything hit the fan.

Rodgers skipped a team meeting in the morning and later was a surprise guest on "The Pat McAfee Show" where he ripped McCarthy for snoring during team meetings and for saying "1-2-3 Packers" in the Steelers locker room during the Eagles game.

Rudolph, 31, is set to start for the Steelers on Friday.

The dramatic series of events comes with the Steelers in the hunt for the playoffs as the No. 7 seed tied with the No. 6 Browns with a 6-4 record.

Cleveland holds the better division record over Pittsburgh, and now has an opening against its fellow AFC North team.
