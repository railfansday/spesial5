Ravens DT Nnamdi Madubuike misses first practice of Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Baltimore Ravens only listed six players on their first injury report of Week 11 – a marked improvement over the last month – but Nnamdi Madubuike could be at risk of missing the team's next game against the Carolina Panthers.

After Monday night's loss to the previously-winless Chargers – yes, that phrasing is sticking around – the Ravens have one less day to prepare for their trip to Carolina. Baltimore's week-to-week injury situation has improved, but that cannot make up for the absence of star quarterback Lamar Jackson (and backup Tyler Huntley). Jacoby Brissett, who was acquired from the Cardinals before the trade deadline, performed admirably in his first game in Baltimore, but he cannot carry the Ravens' porous defense to victory.

That unit could struggle even more without Madubuike. The seventh-year defensive tackle has just one sack and zero tackles for loss in his eight games since coming back from last year's season-ending neck injury, but he ranks second on the team with 26 pressures, per Pro Football Focus. Madubuike might be struggling to finish plays – so is Trey Hendrickson, for that matter – but he is still a disruptive force in the trenches who is better than the next man up.

That could be 2025 sixth-round pick Aeneas Peebles, an undersized lineman with burst and agility in the pass rush. He was active for the Ravens' first four games with Madubuike still ramping up but has been a health scratch since his star teammate's return. Giving Peebles some much-deserved developmental snaps could be a small silver lining if Madubuike is absent.
