Rams star Aaron Donald records first sack since coming out of retirement in game vs. Cardinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Aaron Donald, for the first time in years, has sacked a quarterback.

The Los Angeles Rams star, who came out of retirement earlier this fall to rejoin the franchise, brought down Arizona Cardinals quarterback Carson Beck early in the second quarter of their matchup on Sunday afternoon. It marked his first sack since Dec. 31, 2023, or 1,050 days ago.

The sack forced a fourth down, and got the Rams the ball back in the contest at State Farm Stadium.

Donald retired shortly after his final sack in 2023. He finished with 53 total tackles and eight sacks in that campaign. But earlier this fall, after rumors for weeks, Donald rejoined the Rams to team up with fellow defensive star Myles Garrett and bolster the Rams' chances at making a deep postseason run.

The 35-year-old made his debut on Sept. 21 in a win over the New York Giants, and had three tackles in that contest. While he's been impressive in his return, especially considering he had been away from the game for so long, Donald failed to record a sack in his first seven games this fall. Donald entered Sunday's contest with 13 total tackles and seven solo tackles.

The sack on Sunday marked the 112th of his career. That's good for 42nd on the league's all-time list. While he's well back from the 200 that leader Bruce Smith recorded in his career, there are only seven active players in the NFL with more sacks than Donald has.

The Rams, who will have a bye next week, carried a 5-4 record into Sunday's game in Arizona. They've won three straight, however, and finally got above .500 for the first time all season with their 20-13 win over the Washington Commanders last week.

Though they still have a long way to go to actually make a run at another ring, Donald finally getting his first sack out of the way is a big step for him in his comeback.
