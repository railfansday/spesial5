Akron football score, live updates vs. Buffalo. Zips aim for MAC spoiler

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

No Black Friday deal will extend the University of Akron's 2026 football season.

This iteration of Akron (2-9, 1-6 in the Mid-American Conference) will be done after it faces Buffalo (8-3, 6-1) on Friday, Nov. 27, at InfoCision Stadium.

However, the Zips have one last chance to do something of note. They could play spoiler in their season finale.

With a victory at Akron, Buffalo would clinch a spot in the MAC championship game on Dec. 5 in Detroit against the winner of the Ohio-Toledo showdown on Nov. 27. But if Buffalo were to lose to Akron, the door would be open for Western Michigan or Miami to meet Ohio or Toledo.
