Warriors star Steph Curry claps back at weird podcast criticism of wife Ayesha Curry

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

HOUSTON - Stephen Curry was evidently none too pleased with social-media chatter around wife Ayesha Curry's first feature-film leading role in the romantic comedy "Second Chance Holiday."

A clip from the film, featuring Ayesha Curry giving an on-screen kiss to co-star Matthew Daddario, went viral this past week and served as fodder for a wide variety of podcasts. Former NBA All-Star Gilbert Arenas and former Golden State Warriors backup Nick Young held a 17-minute segment Saturday on the kiss, with Arenas saying "the only blemish in the (Curry) family is the wife." Media personality Emmanuel Acho also devoted a good bit of airtime to the topic.

On Wednesday, Curry took to Instagram for a notably pointed response.

"I've been hearing a lot of noise, and usually the noise doesn't bother me, doesn't bother Ayesha, but this one in particular - when you're trying to step on the accomplishments of my wife, and obviously needing podcast tapings and topics and giving opinions on somebody else's wife - Nick, (Gilbert), Emmanuel, and anybody else who feels the need to want to have to jump in and give an opinion on something you don't really know about and kinda speaks to a bigger conversation on how you view women in this world and what they're allowed and what they're not allowed to do, even the ignorance behind how acting works," Curry said, cycling through an array of mocking faces.

"Obviously, I tried to laugh this one off," he continued. "But something needs to be said. And anytime Ayesha Curry shows up in your headline, I kindly suggest you just give the happy hands, the congratulations, and maybe, just maybe, the apology can be as loud as y'all leading y'all shows with this topic."

The video was clearly filmed handheld, at the spur of the moment. A source with direct knowledge of Curry's thinking said that the video response wasn't planned or organized by his team in any way, and came directly from Curry.

The majority of Arenas and Young's content centered on the odd idea of Ayesha Curry potentially bringing a negative perception to the Curry family due to her acting career, visibility and independent personal success, a narrative that has existed on social media for years as her husband has simultaneously become one of the most famous athletes on the planet. Curry has increasingly dipped into the Hollywood space in recent years, starring in the animated film "GOAT" and directing the short documentary "The Baddest Speechwriter of All" this year.

Back on the court, Curry has led the Warriors to an 8-3 start in 2026-27, averaging 30.6 points, 4.1 rebounds and 4.3 assists in one of the hottest early-season stretches of his career.
