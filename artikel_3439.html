Where to find strip clubs in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

There are several strip clubs in Grand Theft Auto 6, but they're hard to find, especially if you're spending most of your time in Key Lento. Have no fear; if you can't wait to watch scantily clad women move to music in GTA 6, there's an early-game strip club that offers private dances.

Here's where to find every strip club location in GTA 6, and what you can do at strip clubs.

To locate strip clubs on your map, scroll to "activities" on the legend and select "clubs." Note that this category also includes regular pubs and dance halls, such as The Rusty Anchor. If a club has strippers, it usually says so in the description.

Here's every strip club location we've found in GTA 6:

While you can visit Delights Cabaret and NINE1NINE as early as Chapter 1, Jack of Hearts is unlocked at the start of Chapter 3.

Though this may not be the main reason for your visit, you can use the strip clubs' many ATMs to deposit or withdraw money from BuckMe, buy drinks at the bar (which offer temporary stat boosts), or dance. Dancing isn't much of an activity; you'll just hold R2/RT to see your character move to the rhythm. If Jason and Lucia dance together, though, it can improve their relationship.

The main activity at a GTA 6 strip club, which you won't find at regular clubs, is paying for a private dance — remember that you can only get them at NINE1NINE and Jack of Hearts. Once there, find a counter (there are usually several) and select "private dance." On top of the initial price, you're expected to tip the stripper during the performance, though you can spend as little or as much as you want. You can only pay for a private dance in cash (both the starting price and the tips). If you don't have cash on hand, use one of the ATMS.

Private dances aren't just reserved for Jason; Lucia can also pay for them. The strippers are always female, and there is some touching involved — but nothing beyond that. If you've visited the strip club before, you can request the same stripper again, or choose "surprise me."

Even if you have a good relationship with your partner, and want to keep it that way, you can still pay for private dances. At some point during the private dance, you may get a message from your partner asking where you are. If you reply honestly and keep spending time with your partner outside the strip club, the relationship won't be hurt. What really damages Jason's and Lucia's relationship isn't the strip club, but ignoring each other. Not replying to messages is far worse!
