Dragon Ball Super: Beerus Episode 6 Release Date & Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Time is officially up for Goku's divine transformation, but Beerus is nowhere near finished with him. Episode #5 of Dragon Ball Super: Beerus ended with Goku's fiery red Super Saiyan God aura flickering out, dropping him right back into his regular golden Super Saiyan form in the middle of their fight. What dragged on for four whole episodes in the original 2015 Dragon Ball Super broadcast (episodes #10 through #13) has now been condensed into a single, hard-hitting episode, taking the battle from the ocean floor straight into Earth's ozone layer without wasting a minute.

Gone are the stiff frames, dragged-out beam struggles, and uneven animation that plagued the 2015 release. Toei Animation has clearly put serious work into making this remake feel like the version fans should have gotten all those years ago. Under chief animation director Tadayoshi Yamamuro and episode #5 director Ayaka Noro, early Super has never looked this sharp.

Now, with Goku preparing to trade final blows with Beerus without even realizing his god timer has expired, Dragon Ball Super: Beerus episode#6 is set to bring the opening six-episode God of Destruction arc to an explosive finish, clearing the way for the next saga and the return of one of the franchise's most iconic villains, Frieza.

Meanwhile, the English dub finally kicked off on Crunchyroll on November 12 after a month-long wait, bringing back Sean Schemmel (Goku), Christopher R. Sabat (Vegeta), Jason Douglas (Beerus), and Ian Sinclair (Whis). With new dubbed episodes arriving every Thursday, episode 6's English dub is scheduled for December 17.

With the God of Destruction arc wrapping up, the remake is already preparing to bring back one of Dragon Ball's most iconic villains. Episode #7 kicks off the six-episode Golden Frieza arc on November 22, bringing back the tyrant who was sliced apart and vaporized by Future Trunks. This time, Frieza actually spends four months training after being revived by the Frieza Force remnants Sorbet and Tagoma, eventually unlocking his Golden Frieza form.

But Frieza isn't the only one getting a new transformation. Vegeta joins Goku on Beerus's planet after bribing Whis with Bulma's instant ramen, and both Saiyans begin training to master God ki. This leads to Super Saiyan Blue, while the arc finally gives Vegeta a much bigger share of the spotlight. Considering how rough the original 2015 Resurrection 'F' TV arc looked, Toei's revamped animation and tighter pacing should make this rematch worth watching.

If Toei Animation can maintain this level of animation and pacing, the future of Dragon Ball Super: The Galactic Patrol is looking pretty bright. The sequel was officially announced at January 2026's Dragon Ball Genkidamatsuri 40th-anniversary event, confirming that the long-awaited Galactic Patrol Prisoner saga is finally getting an anime adaptation.

There's still no official release date or year, though Toei's financial schedules point toward a possible late 2027 or 2028 launch. With Dragon Ball Super: Beerus expected to finish its 19-episode run in early 2027, that could be the perfect time for Toei to share a proper release window and full trailer.

Dragon Ball Super's anime is returning to tell its next major story arc, so get ready with us ahead of its release!

The original Dragon Ball Super ended its television run in 2018, right after Goku's battle against Jiren introduced Ultra Instinct to the world. That silver-haired transformation became an instant fan favourite, but Goku still couldn't use it at will, even after Dragon Ball Super: Broly. The Galactic Patrol Prisoner saga finally takes that story further. Goku and Vegeta join the galaxy's peacekeepers to hunt down Moro, the magic-wielding Planet-Eater, while Goku trains under elite Galactic Patrolman Merus, who is secretly an Angel-in-training. For fans waiting to see Goku truly master Ultra Instinct, there's a lot to look forward to.
