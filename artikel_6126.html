In Pictures: Riyadh Air’s First Airbus A321neo In Bedouin-Inspired Pearl Livery

The first of Riyadh Air's Airbus A321neos have rolled out of the paint shop, debuting the airline's signature pearlescent livery. This is the first of 91 Airbus aircraft for the Saudi carrier. It marks another important step in the airline's evolution and fleet expansion as it builds on the Kingdom's aviation ambitions.

This latest milestone will add to Riyadh Air's mixed fleet of Airbus and Boeing aircraft, supporting its domestic and international growth ambitions. The jet, which will eventually be registered as HZ-RXFA, emerged from the Airbus paint factory in Hamburg, Germany, on Friday, and the airline shared photos on its LinkedIn page.

The plane carries a modern-inspired livery from traditional Saudi heritage, which combines flowing lines that are influenced by traditional Bedouin tents. This includes elements that reflect Arabic calligraphy. On the exterior of the plane, there are shades of indigo and lavender, while inspired patterns dress the fuselage toward the rear.

For the A321neo, it will now undergo a series of test flights in the coming weeks, before it is officially delivered to Riyadh Air. This jet will complement the existing Boeing 787 Dreamliners that are set to form the backbone of the airline's widebody expansion plans. For those interested in the inside cabin configuration and design of the Airbus jet, these are set to be formally debuted in the coming weeks.

Simple Flying reached out to Riyadh Air for comment on its newest aircraft on Saturday; however, an airline spokesperson could not immediately be reached.

As already implied, the plane will now undergo a series of test flights under German Airbus test registration D-AZWL. The plane, which will then carry HZ-RXFA, should be delivered to the airline in the coming months. This Airbus A321neo is the first of 60 of the type to be delivered to the new airline, which aims to revolutionize how passengers travel to and from the Saudi Kingdom.

With ambitions for the airline to serve up to 100 destinations by the end of the decade, these narrowbody aircraft will support thinner, niche routes, or shorter international services, building on the long-haul expansion plans that the carrier is already exploring with its fleet of 787 aircraft.

Riyadh Air is the second flag carrier for the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. It operates domestic and international flights and plans to serve 100 destinations in six continents relatively quickly. The airline began commercial operations in October 2025 and is owned by the Saudi Public Investment Fund (PIF).

The aircraft will be part of the static display lineup at Le Bourget alongside other Airbus types throughout the Paris Airshow.

Riyadh Air is not part of any major airline alliance. It has signed cooperation and codeshare agreements with several airlines.

The airline currently has just ten active 787-9 aircraft, with one leased from Oman Air and used as a technical spare and training aircraft. The airline has an order for more than 40 of the widebody jets, which will eventually be complemented by 31 Airbus A350-1000s.

With the first of 60 Airbus A321neo aircraft set to begin deliveries to Riyadh Air very soon, these narrowbody aircraft will fly short- and medium-haul routes of up to five hours, likely to other Gulf countries and India, feeding passengers through Riyadh.
