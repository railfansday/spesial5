China 2027 Growth to Top 4.5%, Ex-Official Says

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

China’s economy is on track to grow more than 4.5% in 2027, according to former Vice Finance Minister Zhu Guangyao. Speaking to Bloomberg TV’s Avril Hong in Singapore on Wednesday, Zhu noted that Beijing still has room to loosen fiscal policy next year, while also stressing the need for AI regulation. (Source: Bloomberg)
