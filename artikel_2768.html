Knicks’ win streak doesn’t stop Mike Brown from being blunt about team: ‘We haven’t arrived’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Mike Brown isn't letting his Knicks team get comfortable.

They have responded emphatically to their shaky start to the season — after losing their first three games of the year, the Knicks have gone 12-3. They have won six in a row and are tied for second in the Eastern Conference ahead of their game against Giannis Antetokounmpo's Heat Friday night at Madison Square Garden.

They are starting to resemble the team that finished last year with a championship. But Brown holds the same sentiment he did during those first few games.

Mike Brown reacts on the sidelines during the Knicks' win over the Cavaliers on Nov. 25, 2026, at Madison Square Garden. Jason Szenes for New York Post

"Our s–t still does stink," Brown said. "We haven't arrived. We still have to embrace the little things and all those things. We may do this [go up], but eventually we're gonna do this [go down]. When we do that, handling the challenges in front of us is gonna be big. You can't get too high in this league because you're that close to going the other way. Just like if you're down, it's not the end of the world, you're that close to flipping it. You try to be even-keeled."

Friday's game is the Knicks' fourth and final NBA Cup group-stage game. And they face an uphill battle to defend their title.

To win their group and advance to the knockout rounds, they need to beat the Heat by at least seven points and have the 76ers lose to the Pacers. Advancing as a wild-card team is much more complicated and far more unlikely.

The Knicks lost their first two group-stage games to the 76ers and Pacers, respectively, before beating the Cavs on Wednesday.

Friday will be the first time they face Antetokounmpo with the Heat. He has been battling a groin ailment but is listed as probable to play.

OG Anunoby, who has missed the past three games due to a left calf strain, is doubtful for Friday's game. That is a slight improvement, as he was ruled out for Wednesday's win over the Cavs from the start.

Jordan Clarkson, who has missed the past four games with right plantar fasciitis, is questionable — also an improvement after being ruled out of Wednesday's game from the beginning.

Miles McBride, who has missed the past two games due to left adductor tightness, is questionable.

Andre Drummond, who injured his head during Wednesday's game, is questionable as well.
