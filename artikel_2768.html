The early reviews for the Madden movie aren't good

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Madden movie, starring Nicolas Cage as the legendary coach, broadcaster, and video-game mogul, has landed on Prime Video. In the eyes of critics, it has landed with a thud.

The current consensus at Rotten Tomatoes, based on 16 reviews, is 13 percent.

The folks at Sports Business Journal have collected and summarized some of the feedback. Owen Gleiberman of Variety calls it a “stunt biopic on cinematic amphetamines,” adding that “it’s like an SNL sketch shot and edited by Scorsese.”

Then there’s this incredible lede from David Rooney of the Hollywood Reporter: “Imagine being cornered at a party while sober by a wired cokehead determined to tell you a long, painstakingly detailed story without pausing for breath and you might have some idea of the exhausting experience of watching Madden.”

Bryan Curtis of The Ringer says "[i]t’s not quite as awful as the trailers made it look,” but that it still “isn’t good.” Curtis also says Christian Bale is “mesmerizing” as Al Davis, which raises the question of why they didn’t just make an Al Davis movie?

Of course, I’ll still watch it. You should, too. But I have a feeling that, throughout the experience, I’ll be wondering how much John Madden himself would have hated it.
