Cavs suffer familiar loss to Knicks; Mitchell’s 30 points not enough

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Cleveland Cavaliers have all but been eliminated from the NBA Cup after the New York Knicks, who outscored them 39-17 in the fourth quarter to pick up their second win over Cleveland this season.

The Cavs are now 2-2 in the cup, making it almost certain they will miss out on advancing to the next stage. There's always next year.

Cleveland's defensive focus and energy were much improved in the first half compared to their previous loss in Chicago. The Cavs played on a string, trusting each other by not overhelping — while seamlessly communicating through switches and containing New York's off-ball screening actions by working together.

Donovan Mitchell was relentlessly getting downhill throughout the first half. Mitchell opened the game shooting 8-for-9 from inside two-point range. He entered halftime with 20 points.

James Harden chipped in with 14 points on 8-for-8 shooting from the free-throw line in the first half. Mikal Bridges led the Knicks with 17 points on 8-of-11 shooting overall.

The Cavs hit the ground running in the third quarter, controlling the tempo and putting New York on the back foot before a myriad of fouls slowed the game to a crawl. This abrupt shift allowed the Knicks to storm back and make the game competitive again.

Cleveland found themselves trailing by double digits in the fourth quarter as the Knicks began turning defense into offense. A handful of errant turnovers from the Cavs' backcourt fueled the run.

Winning the possession battle has been an emphasis for the Cavs, especially in this matchup. The Knicks have historically punished Cleveland by grabbing offensive rebounds and making it impossible to end defensive possessions.

The Cavs started off on the right foot in that regard. They were outscoring New York 11-to-1 on second-chance points and had full control of the possession battle.

The Knicks grabbed every key rebound, forced backbreaking turnovers, and hit all of the big shots down the stretch to put this game out of reach. A fate the Cavs have suffered more than once to this team. As stated, it was a 22-point bludgeoning in the fourth quarter that closed the door.

Harden and Mitchell combined for 58 points. Both struggled in the fourth quarter after playing stellar basketball before. Their six shared turnovers felt like they came at the worst times.

The Cavs end the night officially below .500 with a 9-10 record. Not how anyone hoped this season would start.
