Jerry Jones on fan's death after fight at 49ers-Cowboys game: "We had no record of an incident"

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

This week, a fan died after falling during a fight at AT&T Stadium. The fan was there to watch the 49ers-Cowboys game.

On Friday, Cowboys owner and G.M. Jerry Jones was asked about the incident during an appearance on 105.3 The Fan.

“I just got that information, and so we had no record of of an incident, and we’ll be obviously looking into that,” Jones said. “My understanding is that the individual went to the game and didn’t feel good at the game, and then left, and [had] been hospitalized. But we weren’t aware of any incident that involved a fan at the game.”

They know about it now, and they have the resources to fully investigate.

“We really do have quite a — eons above what it was 10, 15 years ago,” Jones said. “We have quite a surveillance of what’s going on at every aspect of our game. And so it is really rare if we didn’t know of any incident. We’ve got too many people out there. We have 6,000 workers on game day.”

Jones said he’ll pass along any surveillance footage the Cowboys have to investigators.
