YouTube Gold: Cameron Boozer Goes Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Cameron Boozer is having an incredibly impressive rookie season with the Memphis Grizzlies. Obviously, he's not the only player on the team, but consider this: last season, the Grizzlies finished 25-57 and finished 13th in the Western Conference.

This year, they traded their greatest talent, Ja Morant, and drafted Boozer. So far, they are 10-6, and in fifth place in the West.

As noted, it's not all because of Boozer, but he's had a big impact on the team. Take Monday night's win against Washington.

Boozer finished with 29 points, hitting 10-21 overall and 7-13 outside. He also grabbed 7 rebounds and passed out 7 assists.

His draft-night rival, AJ Dybantsa, finished with 21 points, 5 rebounds, and 3 assists.

Similar lines, but, as has been the case for most of his career, Boozer's team won.

It's still early, but if Memphis continues to play this way, it could put together one of the best turnarounds the NBA has seen in years.
