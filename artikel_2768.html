Things look bleak for Warriors, but they can survive this

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Warriors are in free fall, losing five games in a row, including their last four by 20 or more points.

Golden State Warriors guard Stephen Curry, right, confers with head coach Steve Kerr during an NBA game in Denver, Monday, Oct. 26, 2026. AP Photo/David Zalubowski

Of the 15 Western Conference teams, only 11 are going to have a real shot at securing the 10 playoff spots unless something drastically changes.

In other words, the West cellar is so weak that even if the Warriors can't arrest their precipitous slide without Steph Curry, they'll have more than enough time to recover when he returns.

So, while what's happening to the Warriors is unfortunate, it's far from catastrophic.

Curry is expected to be sidelined close to a month after suffering a right oblique abdominal tear on Nov. 11.

The 38-year-old had carried the Warriors to eight straight wins while playing some of the best basketball of his career, averaging 27.8 points (50% shooting from the field, 45.7% from beyond the arc), 3.7 rebounds and 3.9 assists per game.

Before Curry sustained the injury, he and Yaxel Lendeborg were looking like one of the NBA's best duos. Lendeborg, whom the Warriors drafted with the No. 11 overall pick in June, was having a Rookie of the Year campaign, averaging 21.6 points on 52.2% shooting.

But with defenses collapsing on him without Curry, the rookie has wilted, averaging 13.7 points on 26.8% shooting over the last four games.

Not only is Curry going to be back in the lineup soon, but Jimmy Butler (torn right ACL) is expected to return midseason.

The Warriors could still be really good. They could still have a deep playoff run. They could still be a contender.

This has been a roller coaster for Warriors fans.

Entering the season, it was questionable whether the Warriors would make the playoffs after they pretty much stood pat during the offseason. And after dropping their first three games, it seemed all but guaranteed that this was going to be a wasted season for Curry, a two-time MVP who carried the Warriors to four championships in eight years from 2015-2022.

Lendeborg emerged as his sidekick. He sprinted past his expectations. He became a force.

Yaxel Lendeborg, left, and Golden State Warriors head coach Steve Kerr smile during a game against the Houston Rockets on Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, in Houston. NBAE via Getty Images

All of a sudden, Curry and Lendeborg were stomping on their competition, beating contenders such as the Lakers, Timberwolves and Nuggets over a stunning stretch that had pundits questioning whether the Warriors were championship contenders.

But the short-handed Warriors were always one Curry injury away from disaster.

And when their nightmare was realized, the Warriors went from being one of the league's most fun teams to one of its biggest duds.

Golden State Warriors head coach Steve Kerr watches play during an NBA game against the San Antonio Spurs on Friday, Nov. 13, 2026, in San Antonio. AP Photo/Darren Abate

Curry and Butler will return, and the Warriors will have more than enough runway to correct course.

So the excitement the team was generating a little over a week ago?
