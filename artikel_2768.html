Colin Cowherd trolls Republicans after Democrats’ blue wave on election night

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Colin Cowherd couldn't begin his Wednesday Fox Sports Radio show without needling Republicans over what was a big election night for Democrats.

Four years ago, Cowherd was roasted for predicting a "red wave" in the 2022 midterm elections, only to see the result end up vastly different than many expected. Cowherd opted against making any bold predictions ahead of the 2026 midterm elections. But when Democrats obliterated Republicans on election night to take control of the House and Senate, Cowherd appeared to take some pride in poking the right.

"I was gonna wear a red shirt today, but I woke up and just felt like blue was the more appropriate color," Cowherd said with a chuckle. "I told the people in the back, I'd get some blue shirts ready for the next two years, it's just a theme."

After losing the presidential election two years ago, Tuesday night was a big rebound for Democrats, winning control of the House and Senate, and the majority of governorships for the first time in 16 years. The blue wave that occurred might not relegate Trump to being a powerless president, but it strongly limits his agenda for the remainder of his term. Perhaps worse than that, Republican fans of The Herd were forced to suffer through seeing Cowherd in blue.

Cowherd largely avoids politics on his FS1 and Fox Sports Radio show, but he loves to drop little quips that are just enough to cause a stir. Whether it's announcing why he's wearing a blue shirt, praising Kamala Harris for being pleasant, chiding Democrats for losing the presidential election, ripping Donald Trump as a "con artist" or crediting him for being an "appealing" leader, Cowherd routinely tries to spark a response from both sides of the aisle.

Maybe Cowherd would have been a successful cable news provocateur if he landed at CNN or MSNBC as he has previously discussed. Or maybe Cowherd is just weighing in on the topic of the day more than he is offering an informed political opinion. But he's good at sparking a response. Just as he says things that tend to provoke sports fans, his little political quips and comments tend to get a reaction on social media.
