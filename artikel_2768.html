Sean Payton suspends J.K. Dobbins for Week 12 for conduct detrimental to the team

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

J.K. Dobbins will not be on the field for the Broncos when they play the Steelers on Friday afternoon.

Head coach Sean Payton announced in his Tuesday press conference that Dobbins has been suspended one game for conduct detrimental to the team.

Payton did not elaborate or answer any follow-up questions, though he did note that the suspension was his decision.

Dobbins began the season as Denver's starting running back but has now filled that role in just one of the last four games, with rookie Jonah Coleman taking over as the Broncos' lead back.

Coleman rushed for 111 yards on 24 carries with three touchdowns and caught four passes for 18 yards in Sunday's victory over the Raiders.

Dobbins was on the field for just one offensive snap in Week 11, taking a carry that was stuffed for no gain.

Dobbins will not practice this week while he is suspended.

In 10 games with seven starts this season, Dobbins has rushed for 324 yards with three touchdowns and caught nine passes for 31 yards.
