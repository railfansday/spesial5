De’Anthony Melton stuffs the box score in Warriors’ pre-Thanksgiving win

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

On the first Thanksgiving, the overconfident Plymouth Rock settlers had to lean on the Wampanoag tribe to survive their first harvest in the New World. There's scant historical evidence about that first Thanksgiving, but some scholars believe that the Pilgrim colony found itself at a loss after Miles Standish suffered an abdominal oblique strain, one that wouldn't be evaluated until the Friday following Thanksgiving, a holiday that wouldn't exist for another quarter-millenium.

On the Wednesday before Thanksgiving 2026, the Golden State Warriors got 61 points from their bench players and eight three-pointers from De'Anthony Melton as they beat the Sacramento Kings, 129-110. Thanksgiving may not be a holiday in Brazil, but Gui Santos still feasted Wednesday night, putting up 22 points in 24 minutes.

Draymond Green had 11 assists, Yaxel Lendeborg had 15 points and eight rebounds and Brandin Podziemski notched a +16 in 32 minutes, but the Warriors won their game by dominating the bench minutes. Granted, bringing Leaky Black and Precious Achiuwa to an NBA game is like bringing a knife to a gun fight, especially if the knife turned the ball over 18 times, but the bench dominated, 61-19, to get the Warriors their second straight win.

That's intended in no way to minimize Melton, who is now shooting 44.9% from three-point range this season. He went 8-for-12 from distance Wednesday against the Kings and added seven assists, which just goes to show that any time you get the chance to have surgeries on both thumbs in one summer, you pull the damn trigger.

It was a game when the Warriors played well against type, taking a large 1st-quarter lead instead of falling hopelessly behind after eight minutes, a hallmark of their recent road trip. But they were back at the Chase Center, where the Warriors had won seven straight games after choking the home opener against the Memphis Grizzlies. And as the poet says, there's no place like home for the holidays.

Melton shot 4-for-5 from behind the arc in the opening period as the Warriors raced out to a 25-point lead after 12 minutes. They went 9-for-13 as a team and 14-for-22 overall, with Brandin Podziemski and Gui Santos each shooting 3-for-3 with two triples in the quarter.

Brandon Williams wasted no time in his first game in 16 days, scoring 14 points in 13 minutes, while putting up 14 shots and delivering three assists. That's officially a usage rate of "Thank God," and should help Williams when he lobbies the Chase Center's operations staff to change the current "road runner" sound effect when he scores to a Ferrari engine revving. He's now played 150 NBA games yet he already thinks he's too good for the road runner, a bird that has faced off against Wile E. Coyote and survived over a hundred times. Show him some repect!

In the second quarter, Melton made two more threes and Yaxel Lendeborg scored eight points to keep the halftime lead at 19 points. Melton also allowed Williams to use his laundry facilities this week, likely giving his teammate the confidence to dominate the ball the way he has. Williams does have his own apartment now, and more than perhaps any other Warrior needs them to advance to the prize money stage of the NBA Cup.

On the Sacramento side, Domantas Sabonis scored 27 points while taking only nine shots, also picking up 10 rebounds. Unfortunately for Sabonis, it's not clear if playing well or badly will get him what he truly wants: The chance to play for a team who isn't the 2-16 Kings.

Rookie Darius Acuff, Jr. had a high-volume, low-efficiency game, scoring 22 points on 9-for-24 shooting and logging nine assists. It's been a Make-A-Wish type season for Acuff, where the 20-year-old has been able to live his NBA dream of shooting whenever he wants, only it's also like he wished on a monkey's paw, because he's on a team that has won only two games all season.

But Gui Santos stood out with his first game of 20+ points all season, punishing smaller, slower, or just plain lazier Kings defenders to shoot 8-for-11 and score 22 points.

The Warriors are back up to 10-8, stuffing themselves on victories before heading to Portland Friday, where they'll face a team likely woozy from eating vegan turkey and giving out land acknowledgements all day Thursday. Could it be a three-game win streak for the Warriors? Maybe it's enough that the team is all together on a holiday. And still alive on the NBA Cup, which could even let Williams finally own his own washer.
