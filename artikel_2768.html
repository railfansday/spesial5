Everything you need to know about the MLB vs. MLBPA CBA negotiation situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

As the MLB offseason continues, with multiple players flying off the free-agent board, it is time to acknowledge the biggest headline that will certainly plague the offseason and potentially the regular season: the possibility of an impending lockout due to negotiations over a new CBA agreement.

On this episode of the Baseball Bar-B-Cast, Jake Mintz and Jordan Shusterman are joined by senior writer for The Athletic Evan Drellich as they break down everything you need to know regarding the negotiations surrounding a new CBA agreement, a possible lockout and when a deal could potentially get done. While both sides seem drastically apart on what they want, is there a possibility of a resolution without any games being missed?
