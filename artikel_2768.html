NFL confirms Aidan Hutchinson's tackle of Drake Maye was not a hip drop

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson and Patriots quarterback Drake Maye were injured on the same play Sunday. Hutchinson sacked Maye on third down with 9:28 left in the first quarter.

He ripped Maye down from behind, with his right arm reaching around Maye’s right shoulder pad. Hutchinson landed on Maye’s lower leg, injuring the quarterback’s ankle. Hutchinson seriously injured his right knee in the celebration.

Fox rules analyst Mike Pereira said on the broadcast that Hutchinson’s tackle was “not a hip drop.”

“Three things have to happen: You have to unweight yourself, swivel all around the back and land on the back of his leg below the knee,” Pereira said. “This was more of a horse collar-type pulled down from behind. [But] certainly not a horse collar penalty.”

Walt Anderson, the NFL’s officiating rules analyst, confirmed Pereira’s explanation through a league spokesman, via Mike Reiss of ESPN. The league said it was not a hip-drop tackle because Hutchinson did not swivel or unweight himself.

Patriots coach Mike Vrabel called Hutchinson’s tackle “just part of the game.”

“However it happened, I don’t think it was anything intentional,” he said postgame.

Maye remained in Germany for further evaluation, but he could miss the rest of the season with his injury. Hutchinson’s injury was season-ending, as he will need surgery to repair his torn ACL and other damage.
