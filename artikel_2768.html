Vikings release WR Odell Beckham Jr., but will re-sign him to the practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Vikings terminated the contract of wide receiver Odell Beckham Jr. on Tuesday, the team announced.

The team needed a roster spot for Kirk Cousins after the trade with Las Vegas.

The Vikings will re-sign Beckham to the practice squad, according to multiple reports.

Beckham made his Vikings debut on Monday after signing from Minnesota's practice squad but played only two snaps.

The Vikings previously elevated Beckham from the practice squad in Week 5, but he did not appear in the game against the Saints.

The Vikings also announced they released guard C.J. Hanson and tackle Corey Robinson II from their practice squad.
