Which SWFL teams made FHSAA football playoffs? Who's out?

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The playoffs have arrived for high school football. The FHSAA released its football brackets on Nov. 6, and 14 Southwest Florida programs earned the opportunity to extend their seasons.

The Southwest Florida postseason participants include four district champions: St. John Neumann, Bishop Verot, Naples and Cape Coral.

Friday's bracket reveal featured a pair first-round matchups between area teams, including Naples facing South Fort Myers in a playoff rematch and Canterbury hosting ECS.
