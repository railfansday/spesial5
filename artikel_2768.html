Saints vs. Bears: The good, the bad, and the ugly from Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

All things considered the Saints defense played pretty well on Sunday. They held one of the better offenses in the NFL in the Bears to just 19 points and held Caleb Williams to 181 yards. The Saints defense also sacked Williams twice, and got four tackles for loss. They also got two takeaways, both through the efforts of Carl Granderson who tipped a pass that was intercepted by Danny Stutsman and forced a fumble that was recovered by Julian Blackmon.

Tyler Shough was under duress all day as the Saints allowed 6 sacks. Shough had a hand in some of these as he left the pocket too early and scrambled into the defenders arms, but the Saints allowed Jeffrey Simmons to live in the backfield almost the whole game and allowed a total of 10 tackles for loss.

Feels like I am writing the same thing every week, but the Saints continue to make no adjustments and simply nothing is changing. Once again they fell behind early, once again they turned the ball over multiple times, once again they squandered opportunities to get back in the game late, and once again they failed to adequately get the run game going. Travis Etienne Jr. actually became the first Saint to run for over 75 yards this year, but 46 of those yards came on one play. Take that away and he only averaged 2.3 yards per rush. They once again missed a field goal, and did not use Alvin Kamara in any meaningful way despite him scoring the only touchdown of the afternoon. The direction of the team is completely backwards and unfortunately, until changes are made, the results on the field will be the same.
