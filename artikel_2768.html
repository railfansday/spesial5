Boston Celtics (11-3) at Denver Nuggets (7-5)Wednesday, November 18, 20269:30 PM ETRegular Season Game #15 Road Game #8TV: ESPN, NBCSB, Altitude SportsRadio: 98.5 Sports Hub,92.5 Altitude Sports Radio, Sirius XMBall Arena

The Celtics begin a 4 game road trip in Denver to take on the Nuggets. This is the first of 2 games between these two teams this season. They will face off again in Boston on February 9. The Celtics were 0-2 last season against the Nuggets. They are 62-42 overall, all time, against the Nuggets and they are 21-31 in games played in Denver.

With their loss to the Magic, the Celtics have dropped to 2nd in the East, half a game behind the first place 76ers. They are 2 games ahead of 3rd place Detroit. They are 5-2 on the road and 8-2 in their last 10 games. They are 1-1 against Western Conference teams. They are coming off a loss in their last game.

The Nuggets are 8th in the West, 4 games behind 1st place San Antonio and 1.5 games behind 6th place Portland. Surprisingly, they have a better road record than home. They are 2-3 at home and 4-2 on the road. They are 6-4 in their last 10 games and they are 2-0 against Eastern Conference opponents. They are coming off a loss in their last game.

The Nuggets have made a few changes since last season but no major changes. They have added free agents Marvin Bagley III, Alpha Diallo, and Tyus Jones. They signed Bryce Hopkins, Ryan Nembhard, and Cam Whitmore to 2-Way contracts. They added Trevon Brazile with the 35th pick in the draft. They lost Tim Hardaway, Jr, Jonas Valanciunas, Bruce Brown, Jalen Pickett and Payton Watson.

After this game, the Celtics will continue their West coast trip through Sacramento, Golden State, and Washington. They then play one game at home against Atlanta before playing at San Antonio and Dallas. Then it's home against Charlotte before a game at Brooklyn. Next they have a 7 game home stand against Washington twice, Utah, Detroit, Portland, Miami and Cleveland.

After the Celtics, the Nuggets will host Houston before one game at Chicago and a game at home against Dallas. Then they have a 6 game road trip through Utah, Toronto, Detroit, Philadelphia, Utah (again), and Portland. Then they host Atlanta and the Lakers before playing at the Clippers, Lakers and Golden State, They then host Indiana and play a home and home series vs Minnesota.

I'm going to guess that the Celtics will be starting their regular starting lineup of Derrick White, Jordan Walsh, Paul George, Jayson Tatum and Neemias Queta. Of course, Joe can always surprise us with an unexpected change at any time. I'm going with the Denver starting line up of Jamal Murray, Christian Braun, Cam Johnson, Aaron Gordon and Nikola Vucevic from their Game Notes.

The Celtics have no one on their injury report at this time. Since they aren't playing back to back games, they should not have to rest anyone. The Nuggets have just Julian Strawther on their injury report. He was was diagnosed with a left knee sprain in a preseason game and has yet to make his debut in the regular season.

Keys to the GameDefense – Defense is the key to winning every game. The Celtics are 2nd with a defensive rating of 106.1. The Nuggets have a defensive rating of 114.9, which is 20th. The Celtics are 5th in the league with an offensive rating of 118.2 and the Nuggets are 10th with an offensive rating of 115.1. The Celtics have made defense their identity but against the Magic, their effort on that end was lacking. They need to go all out every game on the defensive end to win this and every game.

Rebound – Rebounding is another key to winning every game. The Celtics are 10th in the league, averaging 44.9 rebounds per game. The Nuggets are 25th in the league with 41.7 rebounds per game. Rebounding takes effort and the Celtics have to work harder to grab rebounds to limit the Nuggets second chance points and fast breaks. The Celtics are 7-1 when they have more rebounds than their opponents. They have to put in the effort to beat the Nuggets on the boards.

Effort and Energy for 48 Minutes – The Celtics must be the more aggressive team. They have to fight for loose balls, defend aggressively, be aggressive in driving to the basket, and just play harder than the Nuggets for the entire game. They have to be the team that wants it more and plays like it. They especially have to begin the game with energy and not allow the Nuggets to race out to a big lead and then, they can't let up throughout the game. They also have to come out strong in the 3rd quarter and play hard until the final buzzer. They allowed the Magic to play harder than them, especially in the 4th quarter and they can't do that in this game if they want to win it.

Move the Ball Carefully – The Celtics need to keep moving the ball to find the open man. Doing that makes it much harder to guard them. However, they have to be careful with their passes and they also need to be careful when they dribble the ball and not drive into a crowd and turn the ball over. The Celtics are usually very good at taking care of the ball but at times they lose focus and get sloppy. They can't afford to do that against the Nuggets.

X-FactorsMile High on the Road– It's tough to win in Denver because of the altitude that the teams are playing at. Denver is called the Mile High City because it is 5,280 feet above sea level. Nuggets players are used to the altitude but visiting teams often struggle with playing there and often are fatigued before the end of the game. It is a very unique home court advantage. The Celtics also have to shake off the distractions of travel and playing in front of a hostile crowd and play hard and play their game.

Officiating – I know that I say this every game, but the officiating can always be an x-factor. Every crew calls the game differently whether they call every little ticky tack foul or they let a lot of contact go and let the teams play. We have already seen games where the officiating made a difference in the outcome, both good and bad. The Celtics have got to adjust to the way the game is being called and not let the calls affect their focus or their play. The Celtics also have to be aware of the new points of emphasis for this season and try to avoid flopping, off‑arm push‑offs, reckless closeouts, delays before free throws, and illegal screens.
