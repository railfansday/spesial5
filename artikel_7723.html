The Election Integrity Scorecard: Progress and the Path Forward

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Election integrity remains essential to ensuring that Americans have confidence in the electoral process. This program will review the 2026 Election Integrity Scorecard, highlighting key results, notable progress, and areas where further work is needed. Experts and state leaders will discuss election reforms, lessons learned across the country, and the challenges and opportunities ahead. The conversation will explore how states can continue strengthening election laws, improving election administration, and promoting transparency and accountability in the democratic process.

A distinguished lineup of speakers will also preview the upcoming Supreme Court oral argument in Mi Familia Vota v. RNC, addressing whether documentary proof-of-citizenship requirements are preempted by the National Voter Registration Act and whether noncitizens may be removed from voter rolls during the 90-day quiet period under federal law. Panelists will discuss opportunities for continued reform of citizenship-verification requirements in voter registration and voting.

Join us for a discussion of the current election integrity landscape, the significance of the latest Scorecard results, and how policymakers and election officials can build on recent progress to help ensure elections are secure, fair, and worthy of public confidence.
