National Security Division Announces First “Near Miss” Non-Prosecution Agreement Under the Department-wide Corporate Enforcement Policy

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Department of Justice announced that it has entered into a non-prosecution agreement with Extreme Networks, Inc. The company agreed to pay criminal penalties totaling $975,721.84 to resolve the Department’s investigation of sanctions and export control offenses.
