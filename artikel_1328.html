Everton Announces Partnership With Global Risk & Insurance Specialist WTW

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Everton has confirmed a new multi-year partnership with WTW, a leading global advisory, broking and solutions company, as the Club continues to expand its commercial portfolio into new categories.

The agreement sees WTW become Everton’s ‘Official Insurance Partner’, bringing specialist expertise in insurance, risk management and analytics to support the Club’s operations and long-term ambitions.

Operating across more than 140 countries and markets, WTW becomes the latest internationally-recognised business to join Everton’s growing partnership portfolio, following recent agreements with NOCO, Nestlé and JYSK. 

Through the agreement, WTW will benefit from brand visibility at Hill Dickinson Stadium and across Everton’s digital and social media channels as well as commercial activation opportunities through access to the Club’s waterfront home for client hospitality, business events and engagement activities. The agreement allows for wider collaboration, including community initiatives, that will also be explored.

As Everton’s exclusive partner in the insurance brokerage category, WTW will also provide tailored risk and insurance advice and solutions, drawing on its global insurance-market relationships and extensive experience across the sports and entertainment industry.

Mark Rollings, Chief Partnerships Officer at Everton, said: “We are delighted to welcome WTW as our Official Insurance Partner.

“WTW is a highly respected global business with an outstanding reputation for expertise and innovation. This is a relationship that brings together two organisations with shared ambitions and a commitment to long-term success.

“Alongside the international profile and opportunities Everton can offer WTW, the partnership will bring valuable specialist knowledge to the Club and create opportunities for collaboration beyond traditional sponsorship.

“Welcoming another business of WTW’s calibre is a further demonstration of our commercial momentum and the growing appeal of Everton to leading international organisations.”

WTW has an established presence across the global sports and entertainment sector, working with some the world’s best-known professional clubs, governing bodies, sporting events and venues to manage complex risks, strengthen resilience and support performance.

Ian Tomlin, Global Sports and Entertainment Leader at WTW, said: “High-performing organisations, whether in sport or business, depend on resilience, specialist expertise and the confidence to make informed decisions.

“Our partnership with Everton brings together the Club’s ambition and distinctive identity with WTW’s global insurance, risk and analytics expertise.

“Together, we will help create greater resilience, improved risk management and performance, and lasting value on and off the pitch.”
