Baldini advises Camarda ‘father to son’ and has great expectations for Bartesaghi: ‘A luxury’

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Italy U21s head coach Silvio Baldini says that he has spoken to Francesco Camarda ‘like father-to-son’ and has tipped both Alphadjo Cisse and Davide Bartesaghi to reach the highest heights in the professional game: ‘If I were the coach of any Serie A club, I would take him with me’.

Baldini shared his thoughts on Milan’s cohort of young Italian talents in an interview with La Gazzetta dello Sport, published on Saturday.

The Azzurrini head coach says that he is impressed with the development and maturity that Cisse has shown over the last year.

It has been a rapid rise to the top for the Rossoneri winger, who was playing in Serie B with Catanzaro six months ago, has already been fast-tracked through the Italy U21 side and was called up to the senior national side by Roberto Mancini earlier this month.

“Compared to a year ago, I see a completely different guy, stronger, more mature and more prepared with a lot more self-esteem,” Baldini told La Gazzetta dello Sport. “He has Milan to thank for taking him from Catanzaro in Serie B, believing in him and giving him an opportunity to play consistently. If he moves up to the national team and does well, even better.”

Baldini credited Milan with scouting and developing Cisse, but La Gazzetta dello Sport suggested that it is Baldini who discovered that Cisse could be equally as effective through the middle as he is off the wing.

A humble Baldini shrugged off those suggestions: “After analysing the training sessions, there were lots of situations where he managed to score by attacking space from depth. If he can do it off the left and right flanks, it means he can do it from the centre as well.

“But still, the credit goes to the staff who had this insight, who understood that Cisse could be useful to our game plan in that role. I try to be honest and meritocratic in my decisions.”

Baldini was then asked for his thoughts on Camarda. Tipped for greatness from the age of 15 onwards, some feel that the now-18-year-old has stalled in his development and that he has had a lack of opportunities in the Milan first-team.

“I can only talk about the things that we do together,” said Baldini. “I spoke to him at length, dedicated some time to him, had him explain his concerns, his fear of not being able to express himself in the way that he would like.

“I responded by telling him stories about myself, my life and how I got out of it. A conversation that was less coach-to-player and more father-to-son, or rather a grandson as he could be for me!

“I try to help him understand what I see, I watch a lot of his small movements. He’s a clever boy who shouldn’t become obsessed with scoring, but should play football to enjoy it, have fun. If it becomes more of a test, then you start to get worried, and if it worries you, you won’t enjoy it and won’t perform at your best.”

Baldini was asked if he has or if he would advise Camarda to make a move away from Milan to find that enjoyment he mentioned.

“I won’t intervene on that,” he said. There are clubs, agents, families, they know what’s best for him. Let’s remember that he’s 18, he plays for Milan and with the Italy U21s and he also came with me to play two games for the senior national team. Of course, the more he plays the better it is for Milan and for everyone.

“We’re talking about a great player, a champion for me, who needs to finish his development. He’s still growing, he already finds the goal very well and sacrifices himself for his team.”

Does Baldini think that Cisse and Camarda could be the future of the Italy national team’s attack?

“For me, yes, I see them as regulars for the national team. And they have the time to earn it and prove it on the pitch. They have the characteristics to do it. I think Alpha is doing really well with his decisions, Amorim has done well to get the best out of him.”

Baldini is also a fan of one of Milan’s other young Italian talents, Davide Bartesaghi.

“Davide is one of the best players I’ve coached in that position for many years,” said Baldini. “He’s very good, very professional, very mature. He’s an introverted character but still open, and that’s not a contradiction. He has enormous potential and he shows it every time he’s with us. If I were coach of any Serie A club, I would have him with me.

“Bartesaghi is a luxury for the U21s. He plates with heart, passion, intelligence and has high-level technical, physical and running skills. He can play in all positions on the wing. For me, if he starts 50m away from goal, he performs at his best.”

Baldini’s interview ended with a big statement of intent, referring back to a promise he made in an interview with La Gazzetta dello Sport earlier this season.

“It’s a divine gift to be in this role. Kids and family are the most precious things I have. Choosing who to send to the stands is always the hardest part.

“I said that I wanted to win the European Championship and the Olympics. We’ll work twice as hard to try for that.”

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
