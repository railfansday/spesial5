Football Italia Show: ‘Italy has 60 million coaches’ – We met the two best

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Football Italia YouTube Show is back with interviews with Cristian Chivu, Massimiliano Alvini and Giovanni Malagò during Monday’s Panchina d’Oro in Coverciano.

“What we did confirm is that Italy has 60 million coaches; everyone has their own view on how things ought to be done,” FIGC Special Commissioner said earlier this week after Italy’s 4-1 loss to Belgium.

Malagò spoke to reporters, including Football Italia’s, during the Panchina d’Oro award, where Cristian Chivu was named the best Serie A coach for 2025-26, and Massimiliano Alvini of Frosinone received the same recognition for Serie B.

Under Chivu, Inter won the domestic double last season, while Frosinone were promoted to Serie A after finishing second in Serie B.

Fellow coaches in each of Italy’s top two divisions voted to determine the winners. Awards were given to coaches and directors across every tier of Italian football, including women’s football and futsal.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
