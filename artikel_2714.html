Jozy Altidore makes National Soccer Hall of Fame 1st year of eligibility

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Jozy Altidore has been elected to the National Soccer Hall of Fame in his first year of eligibility.

Altidore, a Boca Raton considered one of the greatest American soccer players of all time, scored 42 goals in 115 appearances for the U.S. Men's National Team, placing him third on that list, from 2007-2019. He made two World Cup squads in 2010 and 2014, and scored the opening goal in a 2–0 upset of Spain in the 2009 FIFA Confederations Cup semifinals.

Altidore helped the U.S. win the Concacaf Gold Cup in 2017 and also led Toronto FC to the Supporters' Shield and the MLS Cup, scoring the winning goal in the final.

Family and friends gathered in Boca Raton earlier this week to surprise Altidore with the news of his election.

Altidore's parents emigrated from Haiti and he was born in New Jersey. But the family moved to Boca Raton when he was young and his soccer skills were apparent from the beginning. He started playing organized soccer at 5.

Altidore started his club career in Major League Soccer with the New York Red Bulls (2006-2008) before spending eight seasons abroad with stops in the Spanish Primera, English Premier League, Dutch Eredivisie and the Turkish Süper Lig. He returned to MLS in 2015 and helped Toronto FC win the 2017 MLS Cup and Supporters' Shield, as well as three straight Canadian Championships (2016-2018).

He retired after a two-year stint with the New England Revolution (2022-2023).

Altidore will be inducted during a private ceremony on April 16, 2027, at the National Soccer Hall of Fame, located at Toyota Stadium in Frisco, Texas.
