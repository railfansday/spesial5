Dalton Kincaid, Christian Benford listed as out of Bills practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Bills listed starters on both sides of the ball as out of practice on Wednesday.

Tight end Dalton Kincaid (thumb, ankle) was one of them. Kincaid was on the injury report because of the ankle last week, but picked up the thumb injury while catching two passes for 58 yards last Sunday.

Cornerback Christian Benford (groin), cornerback Dee Alford (concussion), right guard O'Cyrus Torrance (personal), and linebacker Dorian Williams (calf) were also listed as out, although all the designations were estimations because the team only held a walkthrough.

Safety Cole Bishop (shoulder), running back Ray Davis (hamstring), safety Damar Hamlin (toe), and safety Amani Hooker (knee) were listed as limited participants. Wide receiver Keon Coleman (ankle), running back Ty Johnson (knee), center Connor McGovern (neck), defensive lineman T.J. Sanders (shoulder), and wide receiver Tyrell Shavers (knee) were listed as full participants.
