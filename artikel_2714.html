Police say Titans' Jaylen Harrell allegedly assaulted driver, causing accident

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Tennessee Titans linebacker Jaylen Harrell, who was hospitalized with "serious injuries" Nov. 20 after he was involved in a car accident, allegedly assaulted the driver of his rideshare while the vehicle was in motion, according to a statement obtained by The Tennessean from the Tampa Police Department on Nov. 21.

Tampa police, who are still investigating, said the assault caused the driver of the white Kia Sorento to swerve into another vehicle before crashing into a utility pole. The impact forced Harrell forward and his head struck the windshield, leaving him unconscious. He was taken to a nearby hospital, where he remains, police said.

The driver of the rideshare was treated for minor injuries at the scene but was not hospitalized, while the driver of the vehicle he struck was not injured.

The crash occurred at about 7:46 a.m. CT on Nov. 20, police said.

The Titans released a statement Nov. 21 saying that Harrell, 24, had been involved in a "serious car accident that resulted in him being taken to the hospital in his hometown of Tampa, Florida."

Harrell tore his anterior cruciate ligament on Aug. 13 during the Titans' preseason opener against the San Francisco 49ers. He was put on the season-ending injury reserve list.

The Titans picked Harrell in the seventh round of the 2024 NFL Draft out of Michigan.

He has played 34 career games, including two starts, in two seasons with the Titans. He also has one forced fumble, 4½ sacks and 38 tackles, including seven for a loss.
