How to watch: TCU Football vs. No. 2 Utah Utes

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

TV: FOXStreaming: FOX Sports App / Fox OneTalent: Connor Onion, Robert Griffin III, Alexa LandestoyRadio: KZPS 92.5 FM, TuneIn, Varsity App, Sirius XM 167Talent: Brian Estridge, Ken Seals, Elvis Gallegos, Dave ShookSpanish Radio: KAMV-FM 107.1-2; KLNO-FM 94.1-2Talent: Miguel Cruz, Roberto Ramirez

TCU closes out the regular season home slate with a visit from No. 2 Utah. The Utes are the highest-ranked opponent to visit Amon G. Carter Stadium since No. 2 Texas in 1970.

A win at home on Saturday would give the Frogs their fifth home win of the season and consecutive seasons with five home victories for the first time since 2014-15 (7-0, 6-0). The Frogs were 5-1 at home last season.

The game also marks the first home kickoff with true night conditions at the start of the game since hosting Oklahoma State on Nov. 9, 2024. An 8 p.m. kick is the latest home kick off since hosting Northwestern at Thursday, Sept. 2, 2004 at 8:30 p.m.

TCU has hosted just two undefeated teams in November since joining the Big 12 – falling both times to No. 11 Baylor (2019) and No. 3 Kansas State (2012).

With 7 receptions, TE Kari Ashley would tie Kelly Blackwell's 1990 and 1991 school records for receptions by a TE in a season (64)

Punter John Hoyet Chance is downing 54.5% of his punts (24 of 45) inside the 20 – the secondhighest rate in the P4.

QB Devon Dampier has been responsible for multiple touchdowns (pass + rush) in all 10games this season, the most in the FBS. He has rushed for and thrown for multiple scoresin the same game in back-to-back contests and has recorded multiple TD passes in nine ofUtah's 10 games.

Dampier is the third power conference QB since 2000 with 20+ passing TDs and 8+rushing TDs in a 10-0 start to the season, joining Justin Fields (Ohio State) in 2019 and VinceYoung (Texas) in 2005.

In four combined games against the SEC and AP-ranked opponents (Arkansas, No. 14Houston, No. 7 BYU, No. 22 Arizona), Dampier has had a pass efficiency of 144.4, 62.3%completion rate and has averaged 211.5 passing yards per game. He rushed for twotouchdowns in all four games (for a total of eight) and had 85-plus rushing yards in threeof the four, including 100 rushing yards in the road win over Arizona and 150 at home vs.Houston.

Utah has led in 74.4 percent of regulation time this season, tied for third highest in the FBSbehind Notre Dame (80.3%), Georgia (80.0%) and Ohio State (74.4%).

Utah has outscored opponents by 87 points (103-16) in the first quarter this season. Thatis the second-highest point differential in the first quarter in the FBS (Georgia, +115) and tiedfor the second-fewest points allowed (Georgia, 14).
