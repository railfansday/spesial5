Former Steelers cornerback announces retirement

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Former Steelers cornerback Arthur Maulet is retiring at 33 years old, NFL Network's Cameron Wolfe reported Wednesday evening.

Maulet was an undrafted free agent in 2017 out of Memphis who started his college career at Copiah-Lincoln Community College in Mississippi. In the NFL, the 5'9 cornerback played games for six different teams over his career.

He recorded his most games played (33) and second-most games started (eight) with the Steelers over the 2021 and 2022 seasons after recording 11 total starts with the Jets in 2019 and 2020.

Over his time in the black and gold, Maulet logged 106 total tackles, two sacks, seven tackles for loss, four passes defensed and one interception.

For his career, Maulet played in 94 regular-season games as well as five in the playoffs. After his time with the Steelers, Maulet spent two seasons with the Baltimore Ravens and one with the Detroit Lions.
