Cowboys rally late to avoid disaster vs. Colts after blowing lead, multiple second-half miscues

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Dallas Cowboys almost blew it for another tough loss on Sunday.

But they rallied for a late touchdown to secure a 27-23 win over the Indianapolis Colts.

Dallas blew a 17-7 halftime lead and allowed the Colts to take a 23-20 advantage in the game's final two minutes. But it recovered for a game-winning touchdown drive in the final 1:37 set up by a big pass from Dak Prescott to Ryan Flournoy.

On the first play after a Colts go-ahead field goal, Prescott found Flournoy on a 46-yard deep ball into Indianapolis territory.

The play ultimately set up Javonte Williams for a one-yard go-ahead touchdown with 1:08 remaining. A beleaguered Colts offense helmed by Daniel Jones didn't mount a response on its final possession. Jones threw an interception on third-and-22 to Caleb Downs that sealed the Dallas victory.

With the win, the Cowboys improved to 4-5 to remain afloat in what should be a wide-open race for the NFC East. The Giants beat the Eagles Sunday, 19-11, to improve to 5-2-1 and remain in control of the division.

The win for the Cowboys avoided potential disaster that was set up by multiple second-half mistakes.
