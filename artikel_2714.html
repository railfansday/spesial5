Seahawks need to be wary of Mac Jones possibility

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Sunday's Week 12 matchup between the Seattle Seahawks and San Francisco 49ers is being dominated by quarterback-related headlines. Sam Darnold will return to the Seahawks' starting lineup after healing during the bye week. Meanwhile, 49ers starter Brock Purdy missed Wednesday's practice with an ankle sprain injury.

49ers backup quarterback Mac Jones took first-team reps at Wednesday's session. We'll learn more on Thursday and Friday, but it sounds like Jones could possibly start versus the Seahawks this weekend. If so, Mike Macdonald's defense needs to be wary of the possibility while respecting his ability.

Jones has only attempted one pass this season, a five-yard completion in a blowout victory over the Minnesota Vikings. The former 2021 first-round pick played far more extensively last season, when Purdy was nursing an injury. In 2025, Jones completed 201-of-289 passing attempts (69.6%) for 2,151 yards, 13 touchdowns, and six interceptions.

Jones looked perfectly capable of executing Kyle Shanahan's offense at a high level. The Seahawks are all too familiar with productive backup quarterbacks. In Seattle this season, No. 2 QB Drew Lock has done an excellent job filling in for Darnold.

Ironically, both Jones and Lock are pending free agents. They are being projected as the most sought-after quarterbacks in free agency. The Seahawks wouldn't mind if Purdy is sidelined on Sunday, but Jones has proven capable of playing high-level football.
