Open Thread: Happy birthday to Maliq Brown

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Casual Spurs fans may not be able to pick Maliq Brown out of a lineup of 2026 NBA Draft picks, but this isn't a site for casual fans. We're Pounders, and we take our San Antonio Spurs seriously, so we know who Maliq is. More importantly, we look for how he can impact the team whether it be now or in the future.

The Virginia native spent two years at Syracuse before transferring to Duke where he played in the NCAA Final Four. Dunks and defense are two big facets of the Spurs playbook and Brown's highlight reel is chock full of both.

He's just getting started with the Spurs predominantly in Austin for development. His potential is high and hopes are his evolution brings him to San Antonio as this young team continues to grow in the future.

He's most likely celebrating his 23rd birthday on the road as the Austin Spurs play the Rio Grande Valley Vipers on Tuesday.
