Celtics starter on injury report ahead of game vs. Magic

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

BOSTON — Neemias Queta could miss Monday's Celtics game against the Orlando Magic. Queta, the Celtics' starting center for most of the season, is listed as questionable with rest. He has yet to miss a game this season, though the Celtics have rested Jayson Tatum, Paul George, and Mitchell Robinson for one game each.

Joe Mazzulla spoke glowingly about Queta after Sunday's game.

"I feel like his growth and his role has gone kind of under the radar," Mazzulla said. "He's more important than just for defense. He's important in the locker room. He's important on the offensive end. He just continues to get better and better. It's easy to overlook it because of how easy he kind of makes it sometimes. And you can feel it when he's not — a couple times he's not playing well, it's so noticeable because of how good he is."

"He's just a guy that I have a lot of joy watching because of who he was when he got here and who he is now. And everybody wants to become the player that you have to rely on. And he's been that for us."

Queta is averaging 9.1 points and 6.6 rebounds this season, while shooting 57.3% from the field. He's also made 4 of his 5 three-point attempts this season as he continues to expand his game.

If Queta sits, Mitchell Robinson, who started the Celtics' first five games of the season, could slide back into the starting lineup, with Luka Garza is available as well. Robinson is averaging 5.2 points and 5.5 rebounds per game in 17.7 minutes off the bench.

The Celtics have managed their centers on back-to-backs before this season. Robinson sat on the front end of the team's first back-to-back in Orlando on November 1st, and Queta started in his place. Queta has started every game since.

The Celtics are on the second night of a back-to-back after blowing out the Dallas Mavericks 131-94 on Sunday night at TD Garden. Boston outscored Dallas 42-9 in the first quarter, the largest first-quarter point differential in franchise history.

Jayson Tatum was the only Celtic to play more than 25 minutes on Sunday; he erupted for 32 points, 6 assists, and 6 rebounds in 30 minutes.

"We got a really, really good team," Tatum said after. "And I think it really just starts with our depth."

The Celtics are 11-2, lead the Eastern Conference, and have won six straight games.

Orlando, who is 5-7 on the season, arrives in Boston riding a three-game winning streak, most recently a 107-99 win over the Milwaukee Bucks on Friday night.

The Magic remain without Paolo Banchero, who is out with a right high ankle sprain. Jonathan Isaac (left knee injury management), Noah Penda (partially torn right hamstring), and Izaiyah Nelson (left ankle injury recovery) are also out.

Jalen Suggs and Tristan da Silva have both returned from injuries and are off the injury report. Franz Wagner has carried the load without Banchero, averaging 24.4 points, 8.3 assists, and 6.8 rebounds per game.

The Celtics beat the Magic 108-97 in Orlando on November 1st.

Celtics-Magic tips off on Monday night at 7pm ET.
