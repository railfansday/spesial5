Week 12 Fantasy Football Waiver Wire: Chris Rodriguez Jr., Antonio Williams among top pickups

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Every Monday during the season, Justin Boone breaks down the recommended waiver-wire pickups.

Roster percentages are generated from Yahoo leagues. Free-Agent Budget (FAB) amounts are based on a $100 salary cap. Only players rostered in less than 50% of leagues are considered.

Mendoza's first start came in one of the toughest possible matchups against the Seahawks in Week 10. Unsurprisingly, he struggled in that contest, but there were signs of fantasy upside, including his six carries for 22 yards and a touchdown.

This week, we got a better idea of what Mendoza could become for fantasy and the results were excellent. Even with Brock Bowers out of the lineup, Mendoza threw for 267 yards and three scores versus the Broncos, adding another 22 yards on the ground. That locks him in as a top-five fantasy QB on the week.

The Browns aren't the most fantasy-friendly opponent and the Raiders have a bye in Week 14, but over the final month (including the fantasy playoffs) Mendoza has one of the five best schedules at his position with dates against the Chargers, Broncos, Titans and Cardinals. He's firmly in the high-end fantasy QB2 mix with low-end QB1 potential given his late season slate.

Watson is also someone worth considering as a fantasy starter this week and beyond.

The Browns have one of the softest schedules the rest of the way, including the Raiders, Bengals, Falcons, Giants, Ravens and Colts. That stretch begins with a Raiders defense that's top-five in fantasy points allowed to quarterbacks.

Cleveland hasn't had many favorable opponents for Watson, but he finished as a top-10 weekly fantasy QB in the two games against easier defenses (Titans in Week 5, Texans in Week 10).

You can expect that to happen at least a few more times given his rushing stats and the Browns' strong, young pass-catching corps of Denzel Boston, KC Conception and Harold Fannin Jr.

Cam Ward is 52% rostered but would be the No. 1 QB add, if available.

Somehow Black remains available in over 50% of Yahoo leagues, despite my endless pleas for managers to stash him on their benches. I'm not including him on the Top-10 pickups list, but just know he would be my top add.

In better matchups, Black has shown solid RB3/flex value while backing up Christian McCaffrey and if the veteran ever misses time we would see Black emerge as a top-10 fantasy option.

If you need a real-life example of what Black could do if CMC were ever sidelined, just look at what happened with Emmett Johnson when Kenneth Walker III sat out the last two weeks. Don't let players with that kind of upside remain on the waiver wire.

Bhayshul Tuten is one of the breakout stars of the 2026 season, but he's missed or been limited in several practices over the last few weeks. To his credit, the sophomore back has managed to suit up every week. Unfortunately, he missed time on Sunday while battling the injuries, leading to just a 43% snap share and 13 touches for 53 scoreless yards in the loss.

If the Jaguars are serious about contending for a Super Bowl, they need their star runner healthy for the stretch run, which makes you wonder if they will ever give him a week off. This week's matchup with the Titans seems like the perfect opportunity to get Tuten some rest, since Jacksonville is 14.5-point favorites.

In that scenario, Rodriguez would step into the lead role and be a potential fantasy RB1 given the opponents and likely positive game script.

Sampson hasn't had the biggest stat lines since returning from injured reserve, but he has been productive with yardage totals 77, 69, 48 and 37 over the last four outings.

More importantly, he out-snapped Quinshon Judkins in three straight outings, turning this backfield into a 50-50 committee.

Without a Judkins injury, the ceiling is limited with Sampson, so keep your expectations in check. But he's definitely worth considering as an RB3/flex option.

Even without Lamar Jackson, Hill has been productive over the last three games. The veteran pass-catcher has at least eight touches in each of those outings, while amassing 92 scrimmage yards and a touchdown in Week 9, 38 yards and five receptions in Week 10, as well as a season-high 98 yards and a TD on Sunday.

Derrick Henry cleared concussion protocol after the loss to the Panthers, so he should be fine for Week 12 barring any symptoms popping up over the next 24 hours. However, even with Henry starting, we've still seen Hill have a set role in the offense, with the duo splitting snaps fairly evenly over this three-game span.

Jacoby Brissett has given Hill 12 targets during his two starts and that doesn't seem to be going away. Fantasy managers can target Hill as an RB3/flex with some upside thanks to his pass-catching and occasional big plays.

Saquon Barkley appeared to injure his shoulder and potentially suffered his second stinger of the season on Sunday. After the game, Barkley admitted he won't be 100% for the rest of the season, but remained confident he would play Thursday against the Cowboys.

If Barkley is right, then Shipley would only be a flex option thanks to a good matchup with the Cowboys. However, if Barkley ends up being held out on Thanksgiving, Shipley would become one of the top pickups of the week.

Knight hasn't made a huge dent in the box score over the last two games, but it's worth remembering that the Cardinals squared off against two tougher run defenses in the Rams and Chiefs.

The reason I'm mentioning Knight now, is due to the Cardinals' remaining schedule. The Commanders and Eagles are on deck the next two games, followed by a bye in Week 14. Then things get more interesting with the Jets, Saints and Raiders during the fantasy playoffs. Each of those defenses ranks inside the top-10 for most fantasy points allowed to running backs.

If Jeremiyah Love isn't activated from IR, Knight has a chance to produce as a sneaky fantasy option in those matchups.

Kenny Gainwell is 54% rostered but would be the No. 3 RB add behind Black and Rodriguez, if available.

Williams had his best outing of the season on Monday night, totalling nine receptions and 122 yards on a team high 11 targets.

The rookie has now posted at least 50 yards or a touchdown in four of his fives games since Terry McLaurin was lost for the season. However, this most recent performance shows a new ceiling in a Commanders' passing attack that has a very favorable schedule in the coming weeks. Washington will take on the Cardinals, Titans, Texans and Falcons over the next month.

First-year players often improve in the second half of the season as they get more comfortable in the offense and earn the trust of teammates and coaches. Williams is on that exact script at the moment and should be considered a fantasy WR3 with upside the rest of the way.

It's not a great week for fantasy receivers on the waiver wire, which is why Douglas is leading this section despite only managing 30 yards against the Bills.

The rookie has posted at least 70 yards in four of his seven appearances this season. That includes some very strong volume in the last three weeks, with at least eight targets in each of those outings.

It's hard to trust anyone catching passes from Kyle McCord, but Douglas remains in the WR3/flex mix. However, just like Kaelon Black, I'm going to exclude him from the top-10 pickups since I've highlighted him enough in past weeks.

Hurst's five catches, 56 yards and two touchdowns on seven targets was one of the more shocking outcomes from Week 11. I was high on the rookie prior to the season, but his playing time had dwindled down to just 2% of the snaps in Week 9 and he hadn't caught a pass in any of his last three outings.

Coming out of the bye, the Buccaneers clearly made an effort to emphasize him and his snap share rose to 64% — his highest mark since Week 3.

After the contest, Todd Bowles told reporters the team plans to feature Hurst more moving forward, especially early in games. With Emeka Egbuka disappointing and Chris Godwin Jr. fading, it only makes sense to see what they have in Hurst. This performance puts him in the WR4/flex conversation as a boom-or-bust option.

With Wan'Dale Robinson out for the season, Dike becomes a very intriguing fantasy add in an uptempo Titans' offense that's come to life over the last month.

In the first game with Robinson on IR, Dike led the team in targets (seven) and played the second-most snaps among the receivers behind only Carnell Tate. The result was six receptions and 69 yards with another four yards on the ground. It's his second solid stat line over the last three outings, after he posted a 5-71-1 day in Week 8.

While it might feel odd to invest in the Titans' passing attack, this is a team that's finding its identity as the season progresses. Dike can excel as a playmaker in this environment. He also has a decent schedule coming up with the Jags, Commanders, Lions, Colts and Raiders over the next five weeks.

Mumpfield hasn't taken advantage of the opportunity he's been given this season with Puka Nacua and Davante Adams missing so much time.

Now that Nacua and Terrance Ferguson are done for the season and Adams isn't expected back until mid-December, there is another chance for the 24-year-old wideout to step up. Based on where I have him placed in this column, you can probably tell I'm skeptical. Mumpfield has been held to 20 yards or less and really only popped up for bigger days twice thanks to big plays in those games.

I expect the Rams to lean on their defense, rushing attack and tight ends to grind out wins until Adams returns in a few weeks. But if you're in a deeper format and searching for help at receiver, Mumpfield has a path to production.

Jakobi Meyers is 52% rostered but would be the No. 2 WR add behind Douglas, if available.

Tre Tucker is 53% rostered but would be the No. 4 WR add behind Douglas, Meyers and Williams, if available.

It's tough sledding at tight end this week, but as I mentioned in the Konata Mumpfield section, Puka Nacua and Terrance Ferguson are out for the year and Davante Adams isn't expected back for another couple weeks at the earliest.

Higbee hasn't produced much in the way of yardage over the last month, but he did have that strong three-game stretch earlier this year with stat lines of 8-62-1, 5-67-0 and 5-83-0. He also caught a touchdown in their last game before the bye. The Rams are running out of bodies and Higbee is one of the few healthy players who has a proven history with Matthew Stafford. If you need help immediately though, Higbee might not be the best option since the Packers are a tough matchup this week.
