Aaron Rodgers was ‘barred’ from Steelers training facility before release, Al Michaels reveals

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Another twist to the Aaron Rodgers saga was revealed Friday, and it adds another level of intrigue to the quarterback's departure from the Pittsburgh Steelers.

According to a report by long-time broadcaster Al Michaels, Rodgers was barred from entering the Steelers facility on Wednesday. Michaels read a statement that he received from Rodgers. "They took this public, I never heard from the head coach, then they leaked it at 11 p.m. and barred me from the facility. That's why I took it to (Pat) McAfee," per the statement from the former Steelers' quarterback that was read by Michaels.

Rodgers had been the Steelers' starting quarterback, but the team made a decision to bench him, and the controversy ensued. Rodgers went on McAfee's ESPN television program and revealed embarrassing information about head coach Mike McCarthy and then was released by the team.

The Steelers had lost two games in a row and their offense had struggled badly. McCarthy decided to have Mason Rudolph start at quarterback, and then the move went public.

The Steelers met the Denver Broncos in a post-Thanksgiving Day game on Friday, and the Steelers responded with a surprising 27-5 triumph. Rudolph completed 9 of 15 passes for 142 yards in the victory for 142 yards with one touchdown and he did not throw an interception.

The Steelers recorded two defensive touchdowns in the game, as the favored Broncos were not able to get their offense untracked. The Broncos improved their record to 7-4 with the victory, and they are 1/2 game behind the first-place Cincinnati Bengals in the AFC North.
