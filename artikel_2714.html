Bills RT Spencer Brown is doubtful with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Bills right tackle Spencer Brown (ankle) is doubtful for Sunday’s game against the Jets.

Brown missed the Week 8 win over the Ravens with a back injury but returned for Monday night’s win over the Vikings and played 77 of 80 snaps before his injury.

He had limited work Wednesday before sitting out Thursday and Friday. Tylan Grable will start another game if Brown can’t go.

“As always, I still feel the same with a guy like Spencer, so we’ll see what it looks like tomorrow. That’s why we’re not ruling him out,” Bills coach Joe Brady said Friday, via Alec White of the team website.

Cornerback Christian Benford (wrist), defensive back Sam Franklin Jr. (shoulder), wide receiver Tyrell Shavers (knee) and defensive back Jordan Hancock (hamstring) are questionable. Shavers is in his 21-day return-to-practice window.

Left tackle Dion Dawkins (ribs) and left guard Alec Anderson (ankle) are on track to play Sunday, with full participation on Friday and no injury designation.
