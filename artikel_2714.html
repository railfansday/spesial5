Former All-Pro WR seems to wish he played for Liam Coen

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Liam Coen has the best regular season winning percentage (0.762) of any head coach in franchise history. He could move into third place in all-time wins by the end of this season. The beginning of the Coen era has been a thorough success.

This week, Coen told his team to stay hungry.

"How great do we really want to be? Because we could sit here and say, 'Alright, we're at 6-2, just had a good win.' OR. We can say, 'We're gonna f—ing be great. And I don't mean on Sunday in a game. I'm talking about today."

"I'm not asking you to go from good to great in a f—ing day. I'm asking us to go and pursue greatness with a relentless f—ing attack."

If that doesn't make you want to run through a brick wall, I don't know what will.

Coen's message did fire up a certain former three-time Pro Bowl wide receiver who spent seven of his nine NFL seasons coached by Jason Garrett.

Could you imagine prime Dez Bryant in an offense designed by Coen?!
