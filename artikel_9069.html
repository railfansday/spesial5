vincent leroy installs kinetic cloud wireframe in morocco’s desert

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

building upon previous molecular cloud series, the project transforms the cloud-like volume into an open metal lattice, where lines and voids become inseparable.

Vincent Leroy edited by christina vergopoulou November 12, 2026 3 min

Sited in Morocco’s Agafay Desert, Molecular Cloud Wireframe is a kinetic installation by French artist Vincent Leroy that investigates spatial perception, movement, and structural transparency. Building upon his previous Molecular Cloud series, including the installation presented at Coachella, the project translates a cloud-like volume into an open, three-dimensional metal lattice where linear elements and empty voids share equal structural weight.

The interwoven metal framework outlines a constellation of intersecting spherical geometries through curved linear ribs. This porous configuration functions similarly to a three-dimensional moucharabieh, filtering sightlines and fragmenting views of the surrounding desert terrain as the viewer changes perspective. By dematerializing volume into a network of lines, the sculpture by artist Vincent Leroy operates simultaneously as a physical object and a spatial drawing. Diurnal shifts in natural light continually alter this reading: direct daylight emphasizes the structural depth and overlapping spherical volumes, while dusk increases contrast against the sky, flattening the three-dimensional assembly into a graphic, two-dimensional silhouette.

the semi-transparent metal cloud merges with the heavy, cloud-filled skiesin constant motion, the cloud shifts between fluidity and form
