Bubbly lamp by Canalside Studio among nine new products on Dezeen Showroom

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

A table lamp comprising a plastic base topped with five magnetically attached glowing orbs is among the products recently published on our Showroom section.

Magnetic connector points are placed inside the five globe-like bulbs that light this table lamp by Hong Kong-based Canalside Studio.

The Bubbly lamp comprises a statuesque base with five bulbs attached, which can be removed and positioned around a room – including attached to metal surfaces – to spread out its ambient glow.

Other products published last week include a tile collection by Ceramiche Keope, acoustic pods by Actiu and an outdoor rug range by Rima Dain.

Read on to find out about some of our latest products:

Grace Lithos tiles by Ceramiche Keope are intended to mimic the striated appearance of natural Villeneuve quartzite quarried in Italy.

As well as being striped with differently coloured striations, the tiles feature the stone’s abundance of mineral inclusions and the glinting mica microcrystals that it is studded with.

Amber and white veining streaks across the surface of Italian stone company Antolini‘s aptly-named Blueroots marble.

The slabs can be used in kitchens and bathrooms as interior cladding or to create furnishings, imbuing interiors with visually stimulating surfaces.

Office furniture brand Actiu has evolved its Qyos acoustic pods, expanding on its configuration options and capacity for personalisation.

Described by the brand as “adaptable microarchitecture”, new options include those that enhance both aesthetics and sound absorption.

Italian company Ceramiche Refin‘s latest surface draws on the faceted appearance of traditional terrazzo.

Palladia comes in a trio of colourways – Iride, Luce and Aurea – and a choice of two finishes, glossy Lucida or plain Matt.

Office brand Mute‘s Jetson acoustic pods have evolved to encompass a wider range of colourways, wall layouts and levels of transparency.

Also enhanced is the pods’ acoustic credentials, which the brand says now meets Class A acoustic standards in accordance with ISO 23351-1, allowing individuals and small groups to work and converse in a quiet environment.

A range of styles, shapes and sizes are available in the catalogue of Spanish brand Rima Dain, which specialises in outdoor rugs.

Rather than being an afterthought, the rugs aim to help zone and enhance outdoor spaces in both residential and hospitality contexts.

Enhanced and cutting-edge new features aim to make Swedish brand Asko‘s Steel Seal washing machines more seamless, hygienic and pleasant to use.

Two new signature technologies in particular feature in the new machines. Steel Seal, which substitutes rubber components for ones made of steel, and Quattro Construction 2.0, an integrated suspension system that reduces movement and resulting noise when in use.

Finnish company Framery has released an internal layout option for its One Pod, featuring a corner sofa in miniature.

One Lounge aims to make users feel at home with the cosy, thickly-padded corner seat, in place of a harder stool.
