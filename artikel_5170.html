Charles Hoskinson Says One Viral App Could Bring Millions to Cardano as India Police Probe ADA-Linked Evidence

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Cardano founder Charles Hoskinson believes the network is far from reaching its full potential, arguing that a single successful application could completely transform market sentiment and attract millions of new users.

As pointed out by Cardanians, Hoskinson stressed that market narratives can change quickly, stating, “Facts and circumstances change. It takes just one viral application to bring millions of people in and change the narrative and tone of everything.”

He reinforced his confidence in the ecosystem, declaring, “ADA’s not done. Cardano’s not done.”

Interestingly, Hoskinson recently noted that Cardano was entering a new phase, with priorities shifting from building blockchain infrastructure toward developing products that encourage meaningful adoption. He acknowledged that Cardano’s Midnight network would take the driver’s seat in this transition.

What’s Cardano’s bigger picture? Hoskinson seems to acknowledge that turning capabilities into applications that attract users and deliver practical value should receive close attention.

Cardano is also demonstrating its potential through real-world deployments. The Cardano Foundation recently highlighted an initiative in India where police are using the blockchain to track and preserve evidence throughout criminal investigations.

The project has reportedly recorded 163,000 transactions, while Trivolve says the system can help cases reach trial 29% faster.

By documenting the entire chain of evidence, from crime scenes to courtrooms, the initiative illustrates how blockchain can improve transparency, traceability, and accountability in public institutions.

Therefore, this development reinforces Hoskinson’s idea that Cardano’s future will be catapulted by adoption, not simply technological progress.
