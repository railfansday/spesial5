House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries has secured his party’s nomination to become the next speaker of the House, winning a 251-11 vote among Democrats, according to a person familiar with the private meeting. The New York Democrat still needs a formal vote when the new Congress begins in January, but the wide margin leaves him in a strong position. If confirmed, Jeffries will become the first Black speaker in US history. The vote ends months of tension within the party over whether to keep its current leaders or shift toward a more progressive direction. Democrats won a commanding majority in this month’s midterm elections and are expected to hold at least 257 seats. The new House leadership will shape upcoming oversight of tech and finance, including hearings with former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, who is scheduled to appear before House panels on December 1, and the future of crypto legislation such as the CLARITY Act.
