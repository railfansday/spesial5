2026 Q3 earnings roundup: Utilities seek to reassure investors amid election fallout

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Executives went from touting their data center pipelines to emphasizing the diversity of sources driving load growth and their shared concern over energy affordability.

Utilities that were counting on data center-fueled anger dying down and a return to business as usual after the midterm elections now face the likelihood of prolonged scrutiny of their AI-fueled growth plans.

The election, which fell in the middle of third-quarter earnings season, saw historic gains for Democrats up and down the ballot, with many promising to rein in data centers. It also prompted a shift in tone among executives, who went from touting data center pipelines to emphasizing the diversity of sources driving load growth, their shared concern over energy affordability and their good relations with incoming officials.
