AI Video Cost: 100 Product Videos Priced on 14 Models

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Posted on Nov 24 • Originally published at blog.siray.ai

The AI video cost of 100 five-second product videos on Siray AI ranges from $20.30 to $130.50 at 720p and from $40.50 to $319.90 at 1080p, depending on the model. We priced 14 image-to-video models from Kuaishou, ByteDance, Alibaba, Google, MiniMax, Vidu, PixVerse, xAI, Lightricks and Black Forest Labs with the same recipe: one product photo in, one 5-second clip out.

These tiers group the models by what 100 five-second clips cost. They don't rank quality. We haven't compared all 14 models side by side, so run a few clips on your own product before you commit.

Not every clip is usable. Product shapes warp, labels blur, or the camera move is too small. A simple way to plan:

Total = clips you need × takes per clip × price per clip

Failed tasks that end with FAILURE and no video are a separate case. In our Black Friday Seedance 2.5 test, three requests failed because of an aspect_ratio setting, and resubmitting fixed them. Check the task status before you resubmit, so one bad request doesn't turn into a batch of retakes.

If a placement needs 4K, upscaling the 500 output seconds of 100 five-second clips with Topaz video upscaling on Siray AI (topaz/topaz-upscale-video-precision) costs about $30 at the default 4K rate of $0.06 per second, and up to $60 for 60 fps (prices checked November 24, 2026).

To see what the price difference looks like, we ran one product photo through five of the models above with the same prompt, 5 seconds and 720p. Four clips were made on November 24, 2026. The Seedance 2.5 clip is from our November 19 test with the same photo and prompt. Times are from submit to finish. Each is a single take, so it shows one result, not how often a model gets it right.

The source photo for all five clips, made with Seedream 5.5.

Slow, smooth dolly push-in toward the amber glass perfume bottle in the open black gift box. The camera moves straight forward from a medium shot to a close-up of the bottle and its brushed gold cap. A soft highlight glides across the gold cap and the amber glass glows from the rim light behind it. The warm string lights in the background twinkle gently as out-of-focus bokeh. The bottle, the box, the tissue paper and the burgundy ribbon stay still and keep their exact shape. Premium commercial look, steady camera, no shake. No people, no text appears.

In all five clips the bottle, the cap and the box keep their shape. The clips differ most in how far the camera travels. Kling 4.0 ends closest, with the bottle filling the frame. The others end a little wider, with the ribbon and tissue paper still in view.

All 14 models use the same endpoint and the same asynchronous flow. Submit a task, read the task_id, poll every 3–5 seconds until the status is SUCCESS, then download the MP4 from data.outputs. To switch models, change model and the parameters that model accepts.

On Siray AI, a 5-second image-to-video clip costs $0.203 to $1.305 at 720p and $0.405 to $3.199 at 1080p across the 14 models we priced (November 24, 2026).

At 720p, PixVerse V6 without audio is the lowest total we found: $20.30 for 100 five-second clips, with a 10% discount at time of writing. Budget extra for retakes.

On the same model, 1080p costs 1.3× to 2.5× the 720p price. Vidu Q4 has the smallest increase (1.3×) and Seedance 2.5 the largest (2.5×).

The Veo 3.1 image-to-video endpoint on Siray AI accepts only duration: 8. Each clip costs $2.56, so 100 clips cost $256.00.
