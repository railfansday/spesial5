Alexander Skarsgård weaves a Wicker story on audio erotica app Quinn

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Wicker watchers keen for a little more steam in the woods, consider your hot basketry autumn sustained.

Announced Tuesday, star Alexander Skarsgård has narrated spicy audio fan-fiction that channels the raunchy fantasy film, newly launched on audio erotica app Quinn.

Written by Emma Parkinson, Felled is an erotic romance tale inspired by Ursula Wills Jones' short story "The Wicker Husband," upon which the film is based. It was chosen as the winner of Quinn's fan-fiction contest, launched ahead of Wicker's October release, which called for original erotic stories from the world of Wicker. A fellow tale of sentient wood-based desire, Felled follows a seemingly hot willow tree that bonds with a farmer's daughter over the seasons. When tragedy (an axe) strikes, their connection seems severed forever — until thick stands of osier later, they reunite.

Felled is now part of the company's celeb-thirst catalogue Quinn Originals launched in 2022, which has already seen the likes of Andrew Scott, Heated Rivalry's Hudson Williams and Connor Storrie, Manny Jacinto, Victoria Pedretti, Matt Smith, Lili Reinhart, Jesse Williams, Jamie Campbell Bower, and more read original audio erotica tales.

In a statement, Skarsgård praised Quinn's fan-fiction initiative, saying, "It was quite refreshing to have something sensual and erotic that wasn't androcentric for a change!"

At the time of the fan-fiction contest's launch, Quinn did not share the winning tale would be narrated by Wicker star Skarsgård, a surprise for both listeners and the author.

"Wicker fans have been so passionate about the film since day one, so partnering was a no-brainer," said Quinn founder CEO Caroline Spiegel in a statement. "There were hundreds of incredible, creative stories about mermen, porcelain men, witches and more. Felled stood out and we knew our users had to hear Alexander narrate it."

Between Felled and Wicker 4DX (and its extended run), it seems "The Wicker Husband" has fans woven in.
