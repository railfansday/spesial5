NBA Cup 2026: LeBron James nabs triple-double as 76ers snap Heat's 7-game win streak despite losing Jaylen Brown to hamstring injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Early in the second quarter Wednesday night in NBA Cup group play, LeBron James pulled up from Broad Street.

He drilled a 35-foot 3-pointer amid a torrid stretch that saw him make a trio of triples and score 14 straight points for the 76ers on Thanksgiving Eve. Before that sequence, the Sixers were trailing the Miami Heat by three points at Philadelphia's Xfinity Mobile Arena. Afterward, they were up eight.

While becoming the oldest player in NBA history to notch a triple double — at 41 years and 330 days old — James continued his recent hot streak from beyond the arc, going 4 of 6 on 3-point attempts. Philadelphia, as a team, shot 14 of 35 from long range en route to a 111-104 victory.

The Heat (10-7) would have clinched East Group B had they come out on top, but Philadelphia (15-4) denied Miami an eighth consecutive win, despite losing Jaylen Brown to a hamstring injury late in the first half and Joel Embiid being sidelined by illness.

Nick Nurse's squad is now 2-1 in NBA Cup competition, whereas Erik Spoelstra's Heat dropped to that same mark.

James finished with a season-high 25 points and 12 assists, plus 10 rebounds, with maybe his most notable dime taking place in the infancy of the fourth quarter. The 22-time All-Star flexed his elite court vision, timing a halfcourt lob perfectly to connect with VJ Edgecombe for a layup. James later dialed up an alley-oop for forward Dominick Barlow to throw down.

Edgecombe was among the Sixers players stepping up in the absence of Brown and Embiid, as Philadelphia protected its nine-point halftime lead and kept Miami at arm's length over the final two quarters. He had 18 points, and Tyrese Maxey poured in 30.

The Heat, who wound up trailing by 16 late in the final frame, were led by Giannis Antetokounmpo, who clocked out with 32 points, 14 rebounds and 4 assists.

Note: * denotes teams that have been eliminated from knockout round contention.
