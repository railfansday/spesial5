NWSL facing venue conflicts ahead of playoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The NWSL semifinals could see a change in venue for the top three seeded teams.

The National Women’s Soccer League is facing a venue crisis ahead of the league’s playoffs.

According to ESPN, “none of the top three seeds can host at [their] usual venue” on Nov. 14, which is the day of one of the semifinals. The schedule conflicts affect Gotham FC, the San Diego Wave and the Washington Spirit next Saturday.

For Gotham, who play at Sports Illustrated Stadium, the conflict is with the New York Red Bulls of Major League Soccer, who have a friendly against Club América at 7:30 p.m. For the Wave, who play at Snapdragon Stadium, San Diego State football plays host to Utah State at 9:30 p.m. Meanwhile, for the Spirit, a Division III football game between the U.S. Coast Guard and U.S. Merchant Marine at 1 p.m. conflicts at Audi Field.

The NWSL has been aware of potential conflicts and “working closely with clubs, broadcast partners and key stakeholders to navigate complex stadium availability and scheduling challenges ahead of the semifinals,’ a league spokesperson told ESPN in a statement.

“While some circumstances are outside our control, we’ve proactively evaluated multiple scenarios and their tradeoffs to prepare for every potential matchup, with the goal of minimizing disruption to players, clubs and fans while protecting competitive integrity,” the spokesperson said to ESPN and Kassouf. “These challenges also reinforce why stadium infrastructure and meaningful control or influence over venue availability have been central to our growth strategy for years.”

Meghann Burke, the NWSL Players Association executive director, told ESPN that the union “has been looped into those conversations for months.”

“The matrix of choices is beyond their control because there’s a lack of political will and investment in women’s sports,” Burke told Kassouf. “Until you control your own venue, we’re gonna keep having these problems over and over again. “We need to start looking at policymakers who have no problem opening up the wallets of taxpayers for billionaires who own NFL and MLB teams, and refuse to do so for the women who have established that when you invest in them, we do a hell of a lot more than just make money.”

Regarding Gotham FC and New York Red Bulls, the friendly between the MLS and Liga MX side was only announced on Oct. 5. The Red Bulls are currently out of playoff contention, so their regular-season will end tomorrow, due to not being affected by Decision Day results.

Gotham does have a backup plan in place, but it’s a stark difference between the 25,000-capacity Sports Illustrated Stadium. Gotham “may be forced” to play at Icahn Stadium in New York, which only holds 5,000 fans.

There are other scenarios that could force the Washington Spirit to host a semifinal at Northwest Field in Landover, Md. — home of the NFL’s Washington Commanders — or for West Coast teams to host a game at 9 a.m. PT.

However, the venue dilemma is subject to results. On Friday, the San Diego Wave will host the Utah Royals to kick off the quarterfinals, at 10 p.m. ET. Gotham hosts the Seattle Reign on Sunday at 12:30 p.m. ET, while the Spirit host the Portland Thorns on Sunday at 3 p.m. ET.
