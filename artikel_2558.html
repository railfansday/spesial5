Geyoro inspires London City Lionesses to 4-1 WSL win over Birmingham City

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

To help deliver timely coverage of women's football, we may use AI tools to support content creation and editing. While we strive for accuracy and editorial quality, readers should independently verify important information.

London City Lionesses ended a four-match winless run against Birmingham City on Sunday 8 November, winning 4-1 in the Women’s Super League. Eder Maestre’s side led 2-0 at half-time in Dartford and finished with 20 shots to Birmingham’s 4. Their last win over Birmingham came on 12 February 2023.

Poppy Pattinson opened the scoring after 10 minutes. Six minutes later Delphine Cascarino doubled the lead from a Grace Geyoro assist. Cascarino was booked for a foul in the 27th minute, and María Pérez followed her into the referee’s notebook in the 37th.

Amy Merricks’s side halved the deficit five minutes after the restart. Océane Hurtré scored in the 50th minute, set up by Lisa Naalsund. It was Birmingham’s only shot on target all afternoon, and Hurtré’s display earned her a rating of 8.2.

Neve Grace Herron was cautioned for dissent in the 72nd minute. Two minutes later Geyoro scored herself, restoring the two-goal margin. With a goal and an assist, she finished rated 8.82, the highest on the pitch.

Daniela Arques was booked in the 81st minute, the hosts’ third yellow card. The last word went to Kadidiatou Diani, who came off the bench for 45 minutes and scored in the second minute of stoppage time from a Freya Godfrey assist. That was Diani’s fourth league goal of the season, eighth in the WSL scoring chart.

The home side dominated the numbers. They had 67% of the ball, completed 604 of 683 passes and put 8 efforts on target, forcing 4 saves from Baggaley. Earps did not have a save to make. Expected goals finished 1.52 to 0.19.

The win leaves London City Lionesses third with 19 points from 9 games, 4 behind Chelsea and 6 behind leaders Manchester City. It was their fourth win in six matches in all competitions, coming straight after a 2-1 defeat by Manchester City.

Birmingham are 13th on 6 points, 6 clear of Charlton Athletic. This was a second straight 4-1 defeat after the loss to Tottenham Hotspur. Liverpool, in 12th, are 2 points ahead.
