Saatva Black Friday Sale 2026: Deals on Mattresses and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

This story was updated at 4:03 am EST on 11/27/26 to reflect Black Friday pricing.

Saatva has been a routine top contender for AD staff doing mattress testing and shopping. They hold superlatives across luxury mattresses, foam mattresses, and innerspring mattresses. Their Black Friday 2026 sale is on now and runs through Cyber Monday, and you can save up to $625 across the site on a buy-more-save-more structure, so the discount grows with your order. A few category deals sit on top of that: Buy a mattress and you'll get $100 off an adjustable base or bed frame (or up to $40 off a foundation), nightstands are $200 off when you buy two, and pillows are up to $60 off in pairs. The $625 ceiling holds through Monday, though the exact tiers shift slightly as the sale moves through the weekend and into Cyber Monday, so the price in your cart today may very well not be the price on Monday. All the more reason to move quickly.

Every Saatva mattress comes with a 365-night home trial, free white glove delivery with removal of your old mattress, and a lifetime warranty. That's a full year to decide whether you love it, and plenty of time to sort out the guest room before next Thanksgiving. If you'd rather lie down before you buy, Saatva viewing rooms in cities across the country—should you be lucky enough to live by one—have nearly every model on display, and no appointment is needed. If not, our editor’s have tested a number of their mattresses, and we can vouch for their feel and quality.

Saatva makes more than a dozen luxury mattresses, including the Saatva Rx mattress for chronic back pain and a memory foam hybrid mattress that layers cooling foam over coils. The four below made our list of the best Black Friday deals, and they vary by what’s inside.

Saatva's original mattress is an innerspring mattress with more than 1,000 recycled steel coils arranged in a dual-coil system, finished with a three-inch Euro pillow top for cushioning, so it feels closest to a traditional mattress: bouncy, supportive, and padded on top. Its three firmness levels cover most sleepers: Plush Soft for side sleepers and lighter bodies, Luxury Firm for every position and for couples who sleep differently (Saatva says this is the most popular), and Firm for stomach sleepers and anyone who'd rather lie on top of a mattress than inside it. It comes in 11.5- and 14.5-inch heights; go with the shorter one if an adjustable base is in your future, since the taller version isn't compatible.

Think of this as the Classic with natural latex in place of foam on top. Five zones of organic latex sit over individually wrapped recycled steel coils, under a cover of organic cotton and New Zealand wool, and the latex is GOLS certified organic with a GREENGUARD Gold rating on top of that (shorthand for good carbon footprint!). There's one feel: medium-firm (a 6 out of 10, per Saatva), with a buoyant push-back that's different from the slow sink of memory foam. AD's global editorial director and US editor in chief Amy Astley sleeps on one. "[My husband and I] both sleep warm and appreciate the vented airflow channels, which allow for circulation," she says in her review. She also found her lower back pain went away after the switch. "That alone is mattress gold!" she adds.

In most shared beds, somebody is compromising on firmness. The Solaire adjustable firmness mattress spares you both by putting a separate air chamber under each side, controlled by a wireless remote with 50 firmness settings (on queen sizes and up) and two memory buttons for saving your favorites. The comfort layers on top flip, too: latex side up for more bounce, foam side up for more give. Saatva recommends the softer settings for side sleepers and the firmer end for stomach sleepers. Pair a split king with Saatva's split adjustable base and each side can change its position as well as its firmness.

Saatva's first all-foam mattress skips springs entirely, so it molds to your body and lets you sink in slightly. It pairs two layers of cooling gel-infused memory foam with a high-density foam base that keeps movement from traveling across the bed. Contributor Bailey Berg, who considers anything north of 70 degrees hot, chose it for that gel foam and went with the firm option over relaxed firm to sleep cooler still. The dense base was the bigger surprise. "Both my partner and I are light sleepers: If one of us rolls over, the other one generally wakes up too," she says in her review. "After the first couple nights, I realized I hadn't been woken up by his movements (or by our dogs jumping on and off the bed) at all." Four years in, she reports no sag in the center, no dips where they sleep, and an organic cotton cover that has softened without pilling. If running warm is your main complaint, Saatva's newer all-foam model, the Contour5, adds a heat-dispersing CoolVent cooling system and a phase-change lumbar layer.

A shredded latex core cushions the head, neck, and shoulders in any position, and contributor Sydney Gore found that it eased her back issues. "You won't have to buy new pillows for years afterwards," she says in her best pillows review. Buy two and save up to $60. Send the second one to the guest room.

Saatva's organic sateen set is 300-thread-count long-staple cotton, GOTS certified organic, woven one yarn over and three under, which is what gives sateen its silky hand and soft sheen. The fitted sheet's deep pockets fit mattresses up to 16 inches tall, so it works on the taller Classic, and the set comes in white only. If you'd rather sleep on something crisp, contributor Katarina Kovac swears by the brand's percale for how airy the weave feels.

If a new mattress isn't in the cards this year, a topper is the next best fix. This one is three inches of organic natural latex quilted in organic wool under an organic cotton cover, and Saatva describes the feel as plush with medium firmness. It fits any mattress, Saatva's or otherwise, and the brand pitches it for softening a new bed during the break-in period or buying an old one a few more years.

For anyone who loves memory foam but not how warm it can run, this three-inch topper infuses the foam with graphite, which Saatva says draws body heat away, and wraps it in a breathable organic cotton blend cover. It's medium-firm with the contouring hug memory foam is known for, and Saatva recommends it for every sleep position, side sleepers especially. The foams are CertiPUR-US certified.

The softest of Saatva's toppers adds about two inches of down-alternative fill split into two chambers: a finer fiber on top for loft and a sturdier one underneath so you don't bottom out. Baffle-box stitching keeps the fill from drifting into one corner, a fitted skirt keeps the topper from sliding, and the 300-thread-count sateen cover is breathable. It's hypoallergenic and OEKO-TEX certified, which makes it an easy upgrade for a guest bed when you don't know who'll be sleeping in it.

The main draw is a one-touch zero-gravity button, which raises your feet to heart level and lifts your head and knees slightly for balanced support. There's also a head, full-body, and foot massage with three intensity levels, an anti-snore preset that tilts the head up seven degrees, under-bed LED lighting, and USB ports for your phone. Buy it with a mattress for $100 off. It works with every Saatva mattress except the 14.5-inch Classic and the youth and crib models, and adjustable bases can't be returned, so measure before you order.

With rounded edges and fluted drawers in natural oak, the Arden would look as at home in a beach house as in a city bedroom. Buy two and Saatva takes $200 off, which is how most people buy nightstands anyway.

This low-profile platform frame stands on slim legs that lift it off the floor, with a kiln-dried hardwood frame and plywood slats that let you skip the box spring. The back of the headboard is fully upholstered (it's shown in graphite velvet), so the bed can sit in the middle of a room without an unfinished side facing the door. It also works with Saatva's adjustable bases, and buying it with a mattress takes $100 off.
