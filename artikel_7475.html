Meta launches shopping platform Achados in Brazil

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

Achados combines product listings from across the company’s apps into a unified display.

Meta has launched yet another experimental app, with the company’s new shopping-focused platform, called Achados, now available to users in Brazil.

Achados is a marketplace app that combines product listings from across Meta’s other platforms into a single, unified display of what’s available for purchase.

As per the app’s description in the Google Play store: “One feed for what people near you are selling: listings from Marketplace, posts from Facebook Groups, and items sellers have shared from Instagram, including listings you won’t find in a Marketplace search.”

As shown in the third image above, the app also includes a price comparison element, which shows how each listing matches up against similar products available in Meta’s apps.

Achados also enables potential buyers to view seller profiles on Facebook and IG, and includes customer reviews and sales histories.

According to reporting from El Fondo, Achados launched in Brazil on Tuesday, and is only available on Android at this stage.

Meta has been on an app spree of late, with the company launching six new apps this year alone, all focused on different elements.

Meta is also reportedly working on a prediction markets app, as another way to potentially expand its suite of tools.

Meta has always experimented with new apps, and most of them end up seeing limited engagement, with Meta mainly using them as a learning experience for features in its main app instead.

But the evolution of generative AI has enabled Meta to pump out app experiments more quickly, which lets the company try out more things and capitalize on common behaviors and opportunities as identified by its internal developers.

In this context, the focus on selling is noteworthy. Achados essentially aims to build on Meta’s broader shopping potential by merging all of the various product listings across its apps into a single feed.

That could provide a range of expanded promotional opportunities, while also potentially driving more shopping behavior in Meta’s other apps.

Also worth noting: Meta recently expanded its Creator Fast Track program to creators in Brazil. The combined announcements could suggest that Meta is looking to capitalize on rising opportunities in the country.
