Black Friday is its own holiday of sorts, especially if you’re a pathological online shopper. But if there’s one thing Black Friday has historically been known for, it’s the TV deals. People used to run over small children to get a TV on Black Friday, but fortunately, it’s much easier (and safer) nowadays. Better yet, most deals are available well before Black Friday even hits, so you can skip the anxiety of waiting until the last minute. Or, worse yet, dealing with slow load times and glitchy websites.

We’re still a few weeks away, but we’re already seeing massive savings up to 60 percent off some of our favorite brands, including Samsung, Sony, TCL, and Hisense. Shockingly, some of these units are already seeing actual day-of-Black Friday-level savings. In other words, we don’t expect the sales to get much better than this, including on some of our favorite editor-tested frame TVs. We won’t bore you with the details any longer, though; take a look at the best early Black Friday TV deals we found.
