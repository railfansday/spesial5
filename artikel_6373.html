Snag These 11 Fall Travel Deals Before It's Too Late

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Are you, like me, craving a Caribbean beach with a cocktail in hand? Or maybe a transatlantic cruise is calling your name? Consider these fall travel deals your excuse to start daydreaming about your next getaway. As we get closer to Thanksgiving, early Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales are starting to crop up, along with hot-off-the-press cruise offers and loads of airfare bargains worth keeping on your radar, including both international and domestic routes.

Now's a great time to start comparing prices, mapping out flexibility on travel dates, and seeing what’s on offer before the holiday shopping frenzy kicks in. As for where to go, the world is your oyster. Fall is an appealing time to book for a reason—especially for those opting for last-minute trips: Many destinations are between peak summer and winter holiday travel, while cruise lines are moving ships between regions, sometimes offering repositioning itineraries that make for memorable longer voyages. After sifting through dozens of sales, here are the very best fall travel deals to work into your next itinerary.

MSC Cruises is no stranger to luxury—nor is it a stranger to deals. Above is the MSC Poesia.

Looking for a value-forward cruise to the Caribbean, Alaska, or Europe? Top-rated cruise line Celebrity Cruises offers a weekly Exciting Deals page with discounts to all of the above, and the line-up is already pretty enticing. For one? Come late November, you can sail one of Celebrity's newest ships to the ABC Islands and take up to $700 off in instant savings, and your second guest can sail for up to 75% off. A new series of these exciting deals will launch on Monday, November 2, 2026, and continue every Monday through the end of the year.

Calling all northeast residents—your next ocean escape just got a little more tempting. Virgin Voyages is offering residents of New York, New Jersey, Massachusetts, and Connecticut up to 15% off eligible cruise fares with its limited-time Northeast Resident Rate (which can net out to over $1,000 in savings). From sailings to Canada and New England to ones in Charleston and Bermuda, the adults-only cruise line has plenty of ways to trade your usual routine for a few days at sea. I’ve sailed from NYC to Bermuda with Virgin myself and can vouch for the fun of swapping city views for ocean breezes. Even better? All-in voyage pricing includes Wi-Fi, dining at more than 20 eateries, genuinely fun entertainment, fitness classes, and nonalcoholic drinks. The resident rate can also be combined with select promotions; just use code NORTHEASTRATE when booking and don't forget to bring proof of residency when checking in.

Act quick on this one, folks: MSC Cruises' holiday sale runs through November 5 and features up to 35% off select sailings, plus children 17 and younger can sail free as third or fourth guests. With trips starting at $156 for four nights, get ready for a value-forward trip of sunbathing across the Bahamas and Ocean Cay, Puerto Rico, or the British Virgin Islands. For more deals far less timely, check out the site's deals page, where MSC constantly updates new offerings, from Voyagers Club perks to last-minute cruise deals.

Stunning views await at the Four Seasons Puerto Rico.

It's no secret that Four Seasons properties are absolute hotel havens in the world of travel. That said, a discount on these gems can never hurt. Through December 31, 2027, you can score a fourth night free when you book three consecutive nights at The Ocean Club in the Bahamas. If Puerto Rico is more your speed, take advantage of the Experience More spending credit of up to $500 when you book a stay of three nights or more at the Four Seasons Resort and Residences Puerto Rico. (I've been to this property and can't recommend it enough.) This promotion is valid for stays through January 4, 2027.

Save even more on your travel plans with Four Seasons promo codes.

The Mindful House at the JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort and Spa in Costa Rica

JW Marriott is entering the all-inclusive space for the first time this fall with the opening of JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort and Spa on Costa Rica’s Guanacaste Coast. Lucky for its first-timers, the property's Be First Experience offers up to 15% off the resort’s all-inclusive rate, plus a $150 spa credit per stay, a complimentary room upgrade, early check-in and late check-out (based on availability), and a signature JW experience for two—with a choice of a private mixology class or guided wellness journey. And it's certainly an opening to look out for: Set along a Blue Star–designated beach and surrounded by tropical dry forest, the 415-room resort will feature Costa Rica’s largest resort poolscape, JW Marriott’s largest on-property garden, 11 restaurants and bars, and a spa and wellness center. Book by the end of November for stays through December 20, and be sure to use promo code S5235.

If you’re planning to finish off 2026 in style, you may want to take advantage of Iberostar last-minute offers across the globe. Once you enter Iberostar promo code LASTMINUTE into the hotel website's discounts field—like magic—you'll save up to 60% on sun-soaked hotels this fall. In Miami, for one, you can save up to 35% on a sun-soaked stay in South Beach. Caribbean travelers can save up to 60% at Iberostar Selection Riviera Cancun, up to 50% at Iberostar Waves Costa Dorada in the Dominican Republic, and up to 45% off at the brand’s top-rated Iberostar Selection Paraíso Maya Suites. Looking for more ways to save? Check out more of Iberostar's limited time offers on its official deals page.

Save even more on your travel plans with Iberostar promo codes.

There's no shortage of things to do in the Big Apple—and a (discounted) tour is a fresh way to experience it, whether you're a tourist or local.

With Hilton’s latest perk, travelers can take 15% off experiences booked through GetYourGuide (including guided tours, excursions, and local activities, oh my!) when staying at participating new or newly renovated Hilton properties in top destinations like New York City, Las Vegas, Miami, and Oahu. Just book your hotel stay by December 31, 2026, for travel through March 31, 2027. Check the website to see if the city of your choice is considered a top destination—even if it’s not, all other participating global destinations still receive a 10% discount.

If you’ve got a sudden urge to get up and go, G Adventures has hundreds of last-minute travel deals with up to 30% off tours departing in the next 60 days. We’re talking discounts off some of their most-loved and top-rated trips, including Northern Thailand: Hilltribes and Villages and chasing Costa Rica: Coastlines and Clouds Forests. If your schedule’s flexible and your passport’s ready, this is the kind of deal you jump on before it disappears.

Nothing screams fall like a good old fashioned road trip

We're in the thick of fall travel, and Hertz is making it easy to hit the road. Book by the end of November to save up to 25% on base rates at participating locations all across Canada and the US for travel through the rest of the year. Whether you’re chasing snow-capped mountains or solitude in a charming small town, this is the perfect excuse to plan a road trip. (Though, do note that the deal is subject to availability, so double check your dates before booking a trip around it.)

Still need to book holiday flights? You're in the right place.

Expedia's November Flight Deals page is making it easy to find discounted flights. If you want a quick getaway before the winter chaos hits, Expedia's Air Hacks data argues that you don't have to book months in advance to score deals: The sweet spot for domestic airfare is actually a tight 15 to 30 days before departure, meaning last-minute planners get an automatic advantage. That said, if you're booking Thanksgiving travel, be sure to secure your seats on Expedia by November 5 to save up to $125. You can also score on international airfare when booking on Expedia, with off-season pricing on flights to destinations like Mérida or Brazil (and you'll get there before the crowds do).

Southwest Airlines has teamed up with MGM Resorts to launch Getaways by Southwest, offering travelers significant savings on flight and hotel packages. Jet-setters looking to explore major domestic destinations can save up to $250 on select five-night domestic packages, while international travelers heading to hot spots like Cancun and Punta Cana can slash up to $500 off select bookings of five nights or more. Whether you are aiming for the bright lights of Las Vegas, the beaches of Hawaii, or a tropical Mexican escape, this partnership bundles your essential travel needs into a single, budget-friendly price.
