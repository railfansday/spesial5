Don't Miss Starbucks Red Cup Day 2026—Here's What You Need To Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

It’s the most wonderful (and caffeinated) time of the year.

Starbucks has already unveiled its 2026 holiday cup designs, including an adorable miniature version for dogs. Now it's time for the humans to get their hands on something they can actually keep.

Starbucks Red Cup Day is officially back, and the multinational coffeehouse chain is once again giving customers a little something extra with their seasonal caffeine fix. But before you make a special trip for a little piece of festive plastic, a few things are worth knowing, as your usual coffee order might need some adjusting.

Circle Tuesday, November 17, on your calendar, as that's when Starbucks will be handing out its latest reusable holiday cups at participating locations across the country.

The catch is that it’s only happening while supplies last. And considering how much people love free stuff, I wouldn't recommend waiting until a 4 p.m. coffee run to try your hand at getting a cup.

Starbucks says it's bringing in extra baristas for the occasion, too, which should help with the inevitable rush of customers looking to add to their collections.

You'll need to purchase a qualifying handcrafted holiday or fall beverage of any size. Yes, even a tall gets you the same free 16-ounce cup as someone ordering a venti.

You don't even have to physically step inside a Starbucks, either. Orders placed at the drive-thru, through the app, or via DoorDash, Grubhub, and Uber Eats are all eligible.

And somehow, brewed Starbucks Christmas Blend and Starbucks Reserve Christmas coffee don't qualify, despite having Christmas right there in their names. Because apparently, even Christmas itself can't get you a free cup.

This year's version features coffee cherries and leafy branches tied together with a big red bow. There's also a green-and-white ribbon design around the outside that ties into the rest of Starbucks' holiday collection.

The reusable cup is made with 95 percent recycled material, and unlike the paper versions you normally toss after finishing your latte, this one is meant to stick around.

In fact, putting it back to work comes with a few perks. Bring a clean reusable cup to Starbucks on a future visit, and you'll save 10 cents on your drink, while Rewards members also get double Stars on their entire order.
