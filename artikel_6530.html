Starbucks Just Revealed Its 2026 Holiday Cups—And There's An Unexpected Addition This Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

There's a lot more to the designs than Christmas trees and bows.

It's already November, and I'm still trying to accept that I'll be leaving the house in the dark for the next several months. But at least there are festive drinks to warm us up, plenty of Christmas songs to listen to, and one annual tradition that makes the changing seasons a little easier to accept.

Starbucks' 2026 holiday cup collection has officially arrived, and this year's designs look like something you'd find among the Christmas decorations your family has been hanging up for decades. You’ll find four versions for hot drinks, adorned with trees, bows, and stars, with the usual red and green joined by shades of mint, cream, cranberry, and gold.

And for anyone who refuses to stop ordering iced coffee just because it's freezing outside (read: me), there's a cold version covered in greenery and red bows.

According to the coffee giant, Starbucks started thinking about these designs nearly two years ago. London D., who oversaw the creative work, told the company that one of the team's early inspirations was “real physical ribbons.” The idea eventually went beyond the cups, showing up on other packaging and even in the decorations inside Starbucks stores.

There's another addition this year, although the intended customers probably won't appreciate the artwork quite as much. For the first time, Starbucks has made a 3.5-ounce holiday pup cup, featuring an illustration of Junie, a real-life beagle. Personally, I don't think dogs care much about what their whipped cream is served in, but it's a cute touch nonetheless.

Red Cup Day is also returning on November 17, which gives a free reusable red cup to anyone who purchases a handcrafted holiday beverage at a participating Starbucks, while they have ‘em in stock.

As for the more permanent additions, Starbucks has a Christmas tree-shaped glass cold cup ($32.95), a Bearista ornament ($16.95), and a stainless-steel Bearista tumbler ($29.95) among its new merchandise. And after the madness surrounding the original Bearista glass, the company is releasing mystery-box versions on November 10, followed by another round of the original on December 1.

Say what you want about Starbucks, but they certainly know how to do the holidays right.
