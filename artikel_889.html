Man Utd announce financial results as revenue and wages rise

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Manchester United's latest financial results for the first fiscal quarter of 2027 covered three months up to September 30; it included the summer transfer window and their return to the Champions League, but not the sacking of Michael Carrick and arrival of Mauricio Pochettino

Manchester United have released their latest financial results, with revenue and wages increasing.

The first quarter fiscal 2027 report covered three months up to September 30. It included the summer transfer window but not the sacking of Michael Carrick and subsequent hiring of Mauricio Pochettino.

It does include the club's return to the Champions League. However, only one of these matches were at home - a 4-0 win against Sabah. They played four further games at Old Trafford, and three away.

Manchester United's total revenues rose 4.1 per cent to £146.1m compared to the same period last year, while wages are up 8.6 per cent to £79.9m.

The club's loss after tax increased by 174.2 per cent to £18.1m, with interest paid down 32 per cent to £14.5m.

Non-current borrowings - the debts associated when the Glazer family bought the club 21 years ago - are up 19.2 per cent from $650m (£481.2m) to $775m (£577.4m), primarily as a result of refinancing. Current borrowings are down from £268m to £207.1m.

In terms of personnel, player sale profits down went down 54 per cent to £20.7m. The net expenditure on intangible assets - namely players - increased by £43.3m to £143.1m.

There was 17.7 per cent in broadcasting revenue - up from £29.9m to £35.2m - which likely coincides with Manchester United's return to the Champions League.

Matchday revenue is also up 2.3 per cent from £26.2m to £26.8m, although sponsorship revenue went down three per cent from £47m to £45.6m after the men's team had a smaller pre-season tour. Retail and merchandising also went up 3.5 per cent from £37.2m to £38.5m.

Manchester United chief executive Omar Berrada said of the results: "Our revenue remains strong and on track, while we continue to grow sustainably.

"We are pleased to welcome Mauricio Pochettino and his staff to the club and our priority is to support them as we look continuously to improve our performances on the pitch.

"We are pleased with the progress Eva Olid has made with our women's team and how well the team has adapted to her approach."
