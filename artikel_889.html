Callum Turner, Jack Lowden & More Who Were Almost Cast as James Bond

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Robert Pattinson was officially announced as the next James Bond, succeeding Daniel Craig to play the iconic secret agent. See Callum Turner, Jack Lowden and more stars who circulated the role.

Looking for someone? The name’s Pattinson, Robert Pattinson.

But before the Primetime actor was officially announced as the next James Bond, succeeding Daniel Craig in playing the iconic secret agent, there were plenty of other could-be stars vying to star in Denis Villeneuve’s next movie.

Following months of speculation about the next 007, a rumored short list began circulating in October, with multiple outlets citing Jack Lowden, Josh O’Connor, Jacob Elordi and Callum Turner among the contenders. While the actors went method when it came to keeping their potential casting as the secret agent under wraps, they were, at least, willing to react to the rumors.

For his part, O’Connor admitted it was “extraordinary” to even be considered.

“It’s an incredibly bizarre pinch me moment,” The Crown alum told the BBC in October, “even being uttered in that same sentence and being considered for something like that.”

Similarly, Lowden had Slow Horses costar had Gary Oldman expressing he should be the next Bond, and that itself was an honor.

“It’s nice to be mentioned—for any role—and I’m just not really going to add to the noise,” Lowden told British GQ in an interview published earlier this month. “It’s very nice, and Gary’s a sweetheart. It’s particularly lovely, coming from someone like him. But I don’t really listen to it.”

As for Turner, he held firm that he had no information about the role in a June interview, although he did divulge that his casting was a question he couldn’t get away from—even in his most personal circles.

“I'll tell you what's so funny about the Bond thing: Even your best friends ask you, people text you that you haven't spoken to for 10 years—and you know nothing!” he told The Hollywood Reporter at the time. “It's such a weird thing of something happening and nothing happening at all. I genuinely know nothing. I just find it quite amusing.”

In addition to Pattinson, Nicholas Hoult was also recently added to the shortlist for the iconic character, but his How to Rob a Bank costar Pete Davidson gave a cheeky assist when it came to sharing any details on his audition process.

“It’s going to be me,” Davidson told E! News in an interview alongside Hoult earlier this month. “I will be the first American James Bond.”

Still, when it came to the possibility of Hoult getting cast, Davidson emphasized that, in his opinion, Hoult was “the best guy for the job.”

Meanwhile, Elordi and Aaron Taylor-Johnson also shared their perspective on the speculations that they too could be the seventh actor to officially portray the Ian Fleming character. Elordi dodged the speculation when he chalked his August visit to London to a simple weekend away—as opposed to a potential Bond audition—telling Variety, “I’m here on holiday.”

For Taylor-Johnson’s part, he reacted to rumors that he would take over the part from Craig—whose fifth and final outing as Bond was No Time to Die in 2021—by emphasizing that he was happy enough to see where life takes him.

“I don’t feel like I need to have a future drawn out for me,” Taylor-Johnson told Rolling Stone UK in 2024. “I feel like: whatever’s drawn out for me, I can f--kin’ do better.”
