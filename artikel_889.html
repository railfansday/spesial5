'It's been horrific' - Evatt says sickness bug decimated Blackpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Evatt: 'I've never seen anything like it'

Blackpool boss Ian Evatt has revealed his squad had a horrific build-up to their defeat by fellow League One strugglers AFC Wimbledon following a fast-spreading sickness bug in his camp.

The Seasiders were without 15 players and staff for Saturday's visit to the capital as they suffered from "severe diarrhoea and vomiting", with some suffering such symptoms when playing.

Evatt's side lost 1-0 at the Cherry Red Records Stadium - their seventh league defeat of the season - and are now 20th and only outside the bottom four on goal difference.

"There's some players and staff that haven't been able to to make it out of bed," Evatt told BBC Radio Lancashire.

"Some staff have had to continue to do their jobs with the vomiting and the diarrhoea and the players are the same - some players were vomiting on the pitch [or had] diarrhoea on the pitch. It's been horrific.

"I've got to be honest, I've never seen a virus spread so fast, I've never seen anything as aggressive as what it is."

Evatt said the effects of the virus began by midday on Friday, the day before the fixture, and suspected its effects had its origins at their Squires Gate training facility.

Blackpool have now won just one game out of their past 10 League One fixtures with their opponents ending a poor run of form themselves, having not won in seven attempts prior to Saturday's fixture.

"We did everything we could, we followed every protocol we could, we followed every process, we separated players, we separated staff but it was continuing to spread," Evatt added.

"There's even staff that have had the virus that haven't even travelled with us so it's obviously something that's been picked up and started at Squires and it just snowballed fast.

"We did what we could in terms of following protocol and processes with what we were asked to do but obviously the game still went ahead and in my opinion it shouldn't have."
