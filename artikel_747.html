Chase Infiniti Coming-of-Age Drama ‘The Julia Set’ Sells Out for HanWay (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

HanWay Films has sold out all territories globally for coming-of-age drama “The Julia Set,” starring “One Battle After Another” breakout Chase Infiniti, following its premiere in Toronto earlier this year.

As previously announced, Sony Pictures Classics acquired U.S. and key international territories including Benelux, Switzerland, Italy, Scandinavia, Spain, the U.K., Eastern Europe (excluding Poland), Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Israel and Asia (excluding Singapore) in a deal negotiated by HanWay Films and Gersh. Additional sales now negotiated by HanWay Films include Sphere (Canada), Cinesky Pictures (Airlines), MegaCom Films (Former Yugoslavia), SND (France), Vuelta Germany (Germany), The Film Group (Greece), Synapse Distribution (Latin America), Front Row (Middle East), Kino Świat (Poland), Nos Lusomundo Audiovisuais (Portugal), Shaw Renters (Singapore) and Bir Film (Turkey).

Directed by Niki Byrne, “The Julia Set” follows math prodigy Julia Fischer (Infiniti), who is recruited into an elite prep course culminating in the most punishing exam in mathematics. At the same time, she begins to fall for her charismatic teaching assistant Pascal (Christopher Briney). As Pascal crumbles under the weight of his overbearing father Aleksander’s (Jason Isaacs) suffocating expectations, it is Pascal’s mother, Anne (Gillian Anderson), who helps Julia recognize and seize a moment of opportunity that will help to define her future.

The film also stars Nina Hoss, Carmen Ejogo, Chloe Bailey, Nico Hiraga, Liz Lin and Alex Dillard.

“There are so many wonderful things I could say about HanWay, but nothing speaks louder than these three words: sold out, worldwide,” said Byrne. “Thank you to Gabrielle and her incredible team, and to our extraordinary partners in every territory, for believing in this film, and for giving Julia her passport.”

Added Gabrielle Stewart, CEO of Hanway Films” “We are delighted to have such great partners for ‘The Julia Set’ globally. We look forward to working with SPC and all our partners on shaping a fantastic campaign for 2027. Congratulations to Niki Byrne and the whole team.”

“The Julia Set” is produced by Potato Salad Cosmic Domination, Shaun Sanghani, in association with SSS Entertainment, G Nus Media, IF/THEN, Grapevine Films, Cinetrain SA, Melegrano Films, Hanway Films, Gotham Group, Good Future Films and Vacancy Film Fund. Producers include Jeffrey Penman, Nick Scherma, Shaun Sanghani, Anderson, Eleonora Granata Jenkinson, Andrey Epifanov, David Treatman, Max Johnson, Lee Stollman, Ellen Goldsmith-Vein and Tayler Haynes.\

“From Niki’s first draft to TIFF to today, The Julia Set has kept finding the right people,” said Scherma and Penman. “We’re endlessly grateful to Gabrielle and the HanWay team, to SPC, and to every partner who has taken Julia on. We can’t wait for audiences everywhere to meet her in 2027.”

Todd Harris, Art Williams, Chad Oliver, Daniel Sheldon, David Fensterstock and Russell Posternak. Co-producers are DJ Dodd, Paolo Pilladi, Rob Simmons and Mitchell Darwin.
