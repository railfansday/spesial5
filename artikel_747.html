Hear Hans Zimmer’s Sweeping Take on Rüfüs Du Sol’s Classic ‘Innerbloom’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

"Innerbloom XI" is out today, Nov. 20, the 11-year anniversary of the original's release.

Rüfüs Du Sol‘s emotional banger “Innerbloom” is well-established dance world canon, having provided the soundtrack to weddings, anniversaries, birthdays, engagements and other extra special moments since its release on Nov. 20, 2015 — eleven years ago today.

Today (Nov. 20) the already epic track becomes even more so under the watch of legend Hans Zimmer, who’s just released his take on the song in collaboration with Rüfüs du Sol and Bleeding Fingers, the scoring collective Zimmer co-founded to make music for films.

This version, “Innerbloom XI,” maintains the nine-minute length of the original, but takes the song to Zimmer’s characteristically sweeping cinematic scale, adding strings, percussion, xylophone, a choir and layers (and layers) of other instrumentation that makes the song fit squarely among Zimmer’s unparalleled Academy Award-winning body of work.

“Innerbloom XI” is accompanied by a new film from Rüfüs’s longtime creative director Katzki, made in collaboration with visual artist Thomás Blanchard that recreates the music video for the original. Watch the “Innerbloom XI” music video below.

Coming from Rüfüs’ du Sol’s second album, 2016’s Bloom, “Innerbloom” has generated 290 million official on-demand U.S. streams and a billion official on-demand global streams, according to Luminate. The song was a major highlight of the group’s recent Inhale/Exhale tour, a global two-year run that became nothing less than the biggest electronic tour of all time.

Beyond this “Innerbloom XI” project, new music from Zimmer will next be heard in the third installment of the Dune franchise, which is in theaters Dec. 18.
