50 Cent Jokingly Sends Diddy a Happy Birthday Message With Jail Photo: ‘Wishing You Many More LOL’

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

50 Cent is as petty as they come. Once on the G-Unit mogul’s bad side, it seems like there’s no coming back. The Queens rap legend continued to troll his nemesis Diddy, who remains behind bars, by wishing the disgraced Bad Boy mogul a happy birthday on Instagram as Combs turned 57 on Wednesday (Nov. 4).

“Happy 57th B day Diddy wishing you many more, LOL,” 50 jokingly wrote on Instagram alongside a jail photo of a disheveled Combs, who’s sporting a salt-and-pepper look behind bars. It’s unclear if the picture of Diddy is legit. “I told you he alright got his appeal going, Diddy good ! Happy Birthday LOL.”

This isn’t out of the realm for 50, who also wished Diddy a happy birthday with a post to social media last year. “I told you he alright got his appeal going, Diddy good ! Happy Birthday LOL,” he wrote on IG.

50 Cent teamed up with Netflix last December to roll out the four-part Emmy-winning docuseries Sean Combs: The Reckoning, which takes a deep dive into Diddy’s history of alleged sexual abuse.

TMZ reported in October that Combs had been transferred from FCI Fort Dix in New Jersey to Federal Correctional Institution, Allenwood in Pennsylvania following an alleged confrontation with an inmate. A spokesperson for Diddy confirmed to Billboard that Combs was moved from Dix Hills to a “low-security facility.”

50 made an appearance on Jimmy Kimmel Live! in October where he reacted to reports of Combs watching the series about his downfall in prison. “He saw the whole thing… I’m sure (he) thought it was the best documentary he ever saw in his life,” 50 said.

Combs was sentenced to 50 months in prison in October 2025 after being convicted on two charges of prostitution transportation. He’s scheduled to be released in March 2028.
