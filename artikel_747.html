Why Dolly Parton’s Niece Danielle Wore Singer’s Dress to CMA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Dolly Parton’s niece Danielle Parton—who is brand ambassador to her estate—attended the 2026 CMA Awards wearing a dress the county legend was meant to wear in the “Light of Clear Blue Morning” video.

A little piece of Dolly Parton was at the 2026 CMA Awards.

Nearly two months after the country music legend died, her niece Danielle Parton—who was named the brand ambassador of Dolly’s estate last month—wore a dress custom-made for the “Jolene” singer.

The all-white, fringe-forward frock was, of course, covered in rhinestones and included a trim of many colors around the low-cut neckline, bodice and skirt. Danielle, 50, paired the look with diamond jewelry and a pair of gold heeled boots at the CMA Awards Nov. 18.

“It was made for the ‘Light of Clear Blue Morning’ video that she never got to finish and get to do,” Danielle told E! News in an exclusive interview on the red carpet. “So I'm wearing it tonight in honor of her.”

And while Dolly’s niece may have been inspired by the “I Will Always Love You” singer’s wardrobe, she quickly found that her aunt Dolly’s words of wisdom have left a far greater impact.

“Almost immediately right after she passed, I gave myself one day to sit around and cry and feel pitiful about it,” Danielle continued. “Then the following day I had stuff to do, and I remember she'd always say, ‘Well, you just got to get your rear in gear and get to it.’ So that's what I did.”

But Danielle knows that Dolly’s impact goes far beyond the Parton family.

“Dolly was such an incredible human,” she said. “I'm a fan of hers as much as I am a family member, and it's always nice, especially as a family member, to see somebody in your own family have a positive impact on other people.”

As for the tribute to Dolly performed by Vince Gill and CMA host Lainey Wilson during the event, Danielle noted she was “really excited to see those that she's had such an influence on get to pay their respects in their way.”
