Republic of Ireland boss Ward signs new contract

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Carla Ward has been in charge of the Republic of Ireland since 2025

Republic of Ireland manager Carla Ward has signed a contract extension until the conclusion of the side's Euro 2029 campaign.

The 42-year-old's previous deal was due to expire at the end of this World Cup qualification cycle but her future has now been resolved before next month's two-legged play-off final against Poland.

Ward replaced Eileen Gleeson at the helm in January 2025 and has led the side to promotion to League A in the Nations League and a highest ever position of 21st in the Fifa rankings.

The Republic of Ireland are just 180 minutes from next summer's World Cup in Brazil, with the first leg against Poland in Gdansk on 1 December before the reverse fixture four days later at Aviva Stadium.

"From the moment I arrived, I've felt a strong connection with this country, the players, the staff and, most importantly, the people. The way I've been welcomed and embraced has meant a huge amount to me, and it's a real privilege to lead this team and this nation," said Ward.

"I'm incredibly proud of what we've achieved together so far, but what excites me most is what lies ahead. There's something really special about this group, their togetherness, their character and their willingness to fight for each other and for the jersey.

"I've always said I want to build something that people can connect with, a team that reflects the spirit of this country, that people are proud of and genuinely feel part of. We've had some brilliant moments already, but I really believe we're only just getting started."

Ward previously managed Sheffield United, Aston Villa and Birmingham City after a successful playing career during which she scored over 100 goals for Sheffield FC.

Since she became boss, the Republic of Ireland have won 12 of their 19 games and lost just one of their Nations League fixtures last year with the sole reverse coming away to Slovenia.

Football Association of Ireland (FAI) chief executive officer David Courell said Ward has helped give "women and girls' football around the country the platform it deserves" during her tenure.

"We are delighted to reach agreement with Carla to continue to lead our Ireland women's national team for the long term," he added.

"The energy that she has brought to the role has propelled the team towards, hopefully, qualifying for a second Fifa Women's World Cup and, in turn, giving women and girls' football around the country the platform it deserves.

"Extending Carla's contract shows our ambition to keep moving forward, hopefully to the World Cup in Brazil and beyond to the Uefa European Championships in Germany in 2029."

The Republic of Ireland are aiming to secure a spot at their second successive World Cup, while Poland have never qualified for the tournament before.

Ward's side defeated Kazakhstan in comfortable fashion in their play-off semi-final with Poland coming through a penalty shootout against Belgium.

Republic of Ireland must do without key defender Chloe Mustaki who has been forced to withdraw from the panel after rupturing her anterior cruciate ligament (ACL).
