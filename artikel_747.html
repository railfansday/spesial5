DWTS Winner Bobby Bones Slams Connor Wood Criticism Ahead of Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

After DWTS’ Connor Wood and his professional partner Rylee Arnold made it to the season 35 finale, season 27 winner Bobby Bones slammed fans criticizing Wood’s success.

Bobby Bones isn’t dancing around his real feelings.

The radio personality and winner of Dancing With the Stars season 27 recently came to the defense of finalist Connor Wood—partnered with Riley Arnold—after some fans were reportedly planning to boycott the finale due to his semifinals performance.

“This is ultimate loser right here,” Bones said in a Nov. 18 TikTok. “First of all, Connor, win the freaking Mirrorball, dude. Two, these fans, these really toxic Dancing With the Stars fans, they’re the biggest losers in the whole world. I’ve been around a lot of losers.”

And Bones emphasized that he and Wood are not the only person to experience criticism from the DWTS superfans.

“Not all Dancing With the Stars fans are like this,” he continued. “Actually, it’s only a fraction but I saw Harry Shum Jr. today who was just kicked out, talking about how the online bullying is. Bro, these fans here are the biggest losers in the whole world.”

Bones even went so far to say this group of DWTS fans are bluffing when they say they won’t tune into the finale, which will also feature Amber Glen, Ezra Frech, Tatyana Ali and Maura Higgins.

“They’re not boycotting because they live their whole life for this show,” Bones continued. “They don’t have any friends in real life so they think all these people here on the show, all their parasocial friends, are real life friends.”

For Bones, the way fans were reacting to Wood’s inclusion in the finale reminded him of the backlash he faced for his win in 2018 against season frontrunner Milo Manheim, with many fans saying Manheim should have won.

“Luckily, I have a job where I deal with people talking about me all the time, so my skin is pretty freaking thick,” Bones added. “But you are losers and I hope Connor wins the whole thing just to stick it in your freaking face. A lot of people were saying if Connor wins, I’d like that because I won’t be the worst—no, I’ll still be the worst don’t worry.”

Still, Bones maintained that his win alongside pro Sharna Burgess was no accident.

“Don’t forget, the greatest gap in history of first and second place on that show was me winning that show,” he emphasized. “You know what you’ve won? Nothing. You’ve won nothing because you’re losers.”

Although Bones was throwing his support to Wood in the moment, he also expressed that any of the season’s finalists will be great contenders for the Mirrorball.

“Ezra’s legit, Connor’s—all five of them in the finals that are in the finals, completely legit,” he said. "Congratulations, you all deserve it. Your body has just been put through heck, you fought the trolls and here you are.”
