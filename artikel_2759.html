2027 UFL Draft tracker, results, top prospects as Diego Pavia finds landing spot

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The 2027 UFL Draft has arrived, and football fans have one question on their minds heading into the event. Where will former Vanderbilt quarterback Diego Pavia land?

Pavia announced in October he was planning to join the UFL ahead of its 2027 season. The 2025 Heisman Trophy runner-up will instantly become one of the spring football league's biggest stars and will look to prove to all the teams that passed on him in the 2026 NFL Draft that he deserves a chance to play professionally.

While Pavia is the marquee name in the 2027 UFL Draft's player pool, there are plenty of other players with whom football fans will be familiar. A total of 120 players will be selected across the two-day, 15-round event that will help fill out the rosters of the UFL's eight current teams.

Of course, not every player selected is guaranteed to play in the UFL. The spring football draft is a rights draft, meaning teams retain the rights to negotiate with selected players should they choose to play in the UFL.

Still, spring football fans will be eager to see the crop of talent that could be poised to enter the UFL ahead of its 2027 season.

USA TODAY Sports is tracking the results of the 2027 UFL Draft. Follow along with the latest picks and a breakdown of the top prospects available in this year's draft class.

Pavia starred across two seasons at Vanderbilt, leading the Commodores to a winning record in back-to-back seasons. That included a 10-3 mark in 2025, a season during which he completed 70.6% of his passes for 3,539 yards, 29 touchdowns and eight interceptions while adding 862 yards and 10 touchdowns on the ground.

Pavia finished as the runner-up in the 2025 Heisman Trophy voting and signed with the Baltimore Ravens after going undrafted in the 2026 NFL Draft. He was released before the preseason and now will look for a shot to play in the UFL.

Martinez played spring football in 2024, when he won the USFL MVP while a member of the Birmingham Stallions after throwing for 1,748 yards, 15 touchdowns and three interceptions while adding a league-high 528 rushing yards and three scores on the ground. He parlayed that opportunity into a chance with the New York Jets and has spent the last three NFL seasons with the Jets, San Francisco 49ers and Seattle Seahawks.

Currently, Martinez is on the Seahawks' practice squad. It isn't clear whether the 26-year-old will look to return to spring football, but his dual-threat ability will certainly make him a hot commodity should he decide to do so.

Steele went undrafted out of UCLA in 2024 but quickly earned a role in the Kansas City Chiefs' backfield. He totaled 183 yards on 56 carries as a rookie but has only handled one touch, a 4-yard reception, over his last two years with the Chiefs and the Philadelphia Eagles.

Steele (6-0, 228 pounds) is a big-bodied bruiser who runs with great physicality. That could make the 24-year-old a goal-line hammer at the UFL level.

Floriea may not be a household name, but he endeared himself to Cleveland Browns fans during the 2025 and 2026 preseasons. The Kent State product notched a combined seven receptions for 125 yards and three touchdowns across four games of preseason action with the Browns.

Floriea spent the 2025 season on Cleveland's practice squad but failed to earn a spot despite a strong performance in the 2026 preseason. The 25-year-old is small at 5-8, 180 pounds, but his hands and separation skills could make him a quality UFL pass-catcher.

Cunningham, a former Louisville quarterback, began his career as a signal-caller for the Baltimore Ravens and New England Patriots. However, in recent seasons, he transitioned to the receiver position and played one game at the position for the Detroit Lions.

Cunningham, 28, has never attempt a pass nor made a catch at the NFL level, but his dual-threat skill set would make him an intriguing fit for the UFL.

Cunningham isn't the only Louisville product who could draw interest in the UFL draft. Thrash, a 2024 fifth-round pick by the Browns, is in the player pool, and UFL teams would love to get their hands on the speedy 6-0, 190-pound receiver.

Thrash recorded 13 receptions for 129 yards across two seasons with the Browns. He also spent two weeks on the Las Vegas Raiders' practice squad during the 2026 NFL season.

Bell was a seventh-round pick by the Patriots in the 2024 NFL Draft after he enjoyed a standout career at South Carolina and Florida State. Bell made just two catches for 20 yards with the Patriots, but he showed off his upside as a senior with the Seminoles, recording 39 receptions for 503 yards and two touchdowns.

Bell is a bit short for a tight end at 6-2, 241 pounds, but he has a well-rounded skill set that could make him hard to cover for UFL defenders.

Wright's name will be familiar to college football fans, as he tried to become one of the first players to see action with an NFL team and return to the college level. His efforts were denied, leaving Wright in limbo after the Browns waived him and LSU chose not to add him to its roster.

Wright (6-4, 246 pounds) may yet get a chance to play professionally in the UFL. The Ole Miss product had 39 catches for 635 yards and five touchdowns his final season with the Rebels and it's easy to see him developing into a mismatch threat in the spring game.

Pete, like Wright, made headlines during the 2026 NFL preseason. The Kentucky product spent time with the Dallas Cowboys, becoming the first NFL player from the Navajo Nation and earning rave reviews for a locker room speech following Dallas' preseason win over the Arizona Cardinals.

At 6-9, 309 pounds, Pete looks the part of a high-upside, developmental offensive lineman. Developing in the UFL could help him eventually emerge as a contributor thanks to his size alone.

Martin-Rhodes is one of the highest-drafted former NFL players in the 2027 UFL Draft's player pool. He was a third-round pick by the Lions in 2023 but was never quite able to carve out a role during his NFL stops with the Lions and Pittsburgh Steelers.

Martin-Rhodes has just six career tackles and no sacks. The 27-year-old will look to show off his abilities as a run stuffer behind his 6-5, 330-pound frame.
