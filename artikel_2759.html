Tennessee Titans sign CB Corey Thornton off Panthers practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Tennessee Titans signed cornerback Corey Thornton off the Carolina Panthers practice squad, the team announced Nov. 25.

The Titans have struggled covering receivers in coach Robert Saleh's first season, and Thornton has been productive in short time, recording eight tackles, one interception and three passes defensed in two games.

Tennessee also signed defensive back Derrick Canteen to the practice squad on Nov. 24 and released linebacker Dominique Hampton. The Titans waived Milo Eifler from the injured reserve list.

The Titans (1-9) play the Jacksonville Jaguars (7-3) at EverBank Stadium on Nov. 29 (FOX, noon CT).

Thornton signed with Carolina as an undrafted free agent out of Louisville in 2025. He had 12 tackles in 12 games last season to go with three passes defensed and a forced fumble.

The Titans have allowed more than 40 points in three straight games and more than 450 total yards in four straight games. Tennessee is allowing an NFL-worst 272.7 yards passing per game and has just four interceptions made by current players.

The team traded veteran safety Amani Hooker, who recorded five of the team's interceptions earlier this season, to the Buffalo Bills before the NFL trade deadline.

"I think the conversations that are inside the team meeting rooms, the position rooms and the unit rooms are as real and as hard as they're going to get," Saleh said on Nov. 23.

Tennessee starting cornerback Cor'Dale Flott (neck) left Week 11's game against the Dallas Cowboys and did not return. Saleh has not updated Flott's status since the game.

Through four college seasons at UCF and one at Louisville, Thornton recorded 177 tackles, four tackles for loss, a sack, six interceptions, 39 passes defensed and a forced fumble.

He was an All-ACC honorable mention his senior season after logging 38 tackles, two interceptions and nine pass breakups.
