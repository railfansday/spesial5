BTSC Monday Night Football thread: Bills at Vikings

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The final game of the NFL's Week 9 looks like a good one, with the 6-1 Bills taking on the 6-1 Vikings in Minnesota on Monday Night Football.

A big change for the Vikings this week will be the team starting Carson Wentz at quarterback while Kyler Murray sits with a hamstring strain. Most will be interested in the other side of the ball, however, with Josh Allen and one of the league's most exciting offenses taking on a hyper-aggressive Brian Flores defense.

Who are you rooting for? What are you watching for? Have anyone in fantasy football? Join the Behind the Steel community and let us know in the comments.
