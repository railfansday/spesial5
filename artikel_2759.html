Eagles coaches reportedly got in a scuffle during the Steelers game

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

It wasn't just the players fighting on the field Sunday when the Eagles beat the Steelers, with tempers flaring on the sideline, too. According to PHLY Sports' E.J. Smith, he witnessed and confirmed that there was tense moment during the third quarter involving Eagles defensive line coach Clint Hurtt and head strength and conditioning coach Fernando Noriega.

According to Smith, Hurtt turned and looked to grab Noriega by the throat, before the two were separated. He confirmed with players post-game, who explained that Noriega was trying to keep Hurtt and some of the defensive players back on the sideline, and Hurtt didn't appreciate the constant reminders. The player that Smith spoke to, also said he wouldn't be speaking on the situation if lingered or was bigger than the one moment, but also noted that it highlighted how pissed off the defense played on Sunday.

Smith later went on to say that it was a tense moment, but one that seemed to pass quickly. He noted that Nick Sirianni came over and spoke to Hurtt a few minutes later, and the head coach appeared to be kind of laughing it off, as well.

The Eagles defense definitely played with extra fire on Sunday, and Hurtt is consistently a driving force in that energy. It was a moment in which they all realized that tempers flared, and then moved on from it. As long as it's not something that leaked over into the locker room, it seems like a good thing that everyone on the team is so fired up to turn things around.
