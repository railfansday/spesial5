Aaron Rodgers’ NFL options dwindling after ugly Steelers divorce

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Aaron Rodgers might have a tough time finding another NFL job.

After being released by the Steelers following his shocking teardown of Pittsburgh head coach Mike McCarthy, the future Hall of Fame quarterback has "limited" options if he wants to get behind center again this season.

"I've asked around about Aaron Rodgers with multiple teams," NFL insider Dianna Russini wrote on X. "Right now, there's no taker. Injuries can change situations in an instant, but in what's supposed to be his final season, his options appear limited."

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers looks up during the second half of an NFL football game against the Philadelphia Eagles in Philadelphia, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Chris Szagola

Rodgers is now on waivers after being let go by the Steelers and can be claimed by another team up until Thursday at 4 p.m. ET. He will become a free agent if he goes unclaimed.

With just seven weeks remaining in the regular season and the trade deadline having passed, most teams across the league are either set at quarterback or already out of contention, making it difficult to envision a landing spot for Rodgers this season.

Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel reacts during a news conference. AP Photo/Charles Krupa

The Patriots, who are 5-5 heading into Sunday's game against the Chargers, are a potential destination for Rodgers given their troubling quarterback situation.

New England is turning to Tommy DeVito for the time being after Drake Maye went down with an ankle injury that could take him out for the rest of the season.

Before the Rodgers news, Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel indicated that the franchise wasn't looking to add another quarterback.

The Ravens could also be a possibility as they try to keep their playoff hopes alive without Lamar Jackson.

Baltimore gave up a 2027 fourth-round pick and a 2028 seventh-round pick in a trade with the Cardinals for veteran quarterback Jacoby Brissett and a 2027 fifth-round pick, but have lost both games with him as their starter.

Tarheeb Still #4 of the Los Angeles Chargers tackles Jacoby Brissett #11 of the Baltimore Ravens. Getty Images

Rodgers has said all year that the 2026 season would be his last in the NFL.

If no team wants his services, it's possible the NFL world has already watched him take his last snaps.
