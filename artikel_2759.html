Sauce Gardner questionable, Charvarius Ward ruled out for Thursday vs. Texans

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Colts may be particularly thin in the secondary for Thursday night’s game against the Texans.

Cornerback Charvarius Ward (knee) has been ruled out while fellow corner Sauce Gardner (calf) is questionable for the contest.

Ward and Gardner were both listed as non-participants on all three injury reports for the short week.

Tight end Will Mallory (hamstring), offensive tackle Bernhard Raimann (concussion), defensive tackle Jerry Tillery (calf), and offensive tackle Jalen Travis (knee) have also been ruled out for Week 11.

Receiver Anthony Gould (toe), offensive tackle Charlie Heck (personal), and long snapper Luke Rhodes (hamstring) have been listed as questionable.

Guard Dalton Tucker (shoulder) has no game status and is expected to play.
