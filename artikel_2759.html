2026 SEC football power rankings after Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Week 10 of the 2026 college football season has concluded for SEC schools.

No. 16 Tennessee (7-2, 4-2 SEC) hosted a top-25 matchup on Saturday in Week 10. The Vols defeated No. 7 Kentucky (7-2, 5-2 SEC), 34-6, at Neyland Stadium. Rankings reflect the US LBM Coaches Poll.

Tennessee's home schedule in 2026 consists of games against Furman (W, 56-9), Kennesaw State (W, 42-9), Texas (L, 20-17), Auburn (W, 24-14), Alabama (W, 26-10), Kentucky (W, 34-6) and LSU, while playing at Georgia Tech (W, 45-24), Arkansas (W, 24-17), South Carolina (L, 27-24), Texas A&M and Vanderbilt.

Following Week 10 games, SEC football power rankings and each team's schedule are listed below. All 16 SEC schools were in action during Week 10.
