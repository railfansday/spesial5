Dak Prescott: 49ers fans &quot;were more present than ours&quot;

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

On Sunday, America's Team wasn't even Dallas's Team. 49ers fans showed up in big numbers for the game against the Cowboys at AT&T Stadium.

After the game, Cowboys quarterback Dak Prescott was asked whether the home team had to use the silent count.

"No, we didn't go on the silent count," Prescott said. "[We] anticipated it. I think y'all even potentially asked me on Thursday about it, having this many 49er fans there. Tricked myself and told myself early on that the red was for my high school.

"And at the end of the day, all I asked for is a great environment to play this game. And unfortunately, their fans were more present than ours, but it created a great atmosphere. So, we take the good out of it one way or another."

For owner Jerry Jones, the colors worn by the fans don't matter; their money is the same shade of green.

Still, it's always a bad look when too many fans of the opposing team show up for a road game. On Sunday, 49ers fans found their way to buy tickets and infiltrate the venue.

And their fans got to see the Niners move to 8-1 on the season.
