RHOSLC's Mary Cosby Reveals Unseen Moments That Led to Angie Feud

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Mary Cosby claimed Real Housewives of Salt Lake City viewers don’t necessarily have a full picture of what led to her explosive season 7 confrontation with Angie Katsanevas.

“By the time we were filming, I was already built up [with anger],” Mary, 54, told Andy Cohen on the Wednesday, November 18, episode of Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen. “She kept doing things and kept dismissing it.”

The longtime friends had a shocking falling out earlier in the season when Angie, 53, argued with Mary over whether she was prioritizing her marriage to husband Shawn Trujillo. (Angie and Shawn separated over the summer after nearly 30 years of marriage.)

“All you care about is yourself. What happened to your husband? What happened to your child?” Mary asked during the heated argument, with Angie shooting back, “What happened to your husband? What happened to your child? What are you talking about?”

Angie’s comments stunned her RHOSLC castmates, as the scene was shot mere weeks after Mary’s only son, Robert Cosby Jr., died at age 23 of a suspected overdose.

Looking back on her rift with Angie, Mary maintained that their issues arose when she had told her RHOSLC castmate about Robert Jr.’s death.

“[Viewers] see a version of what happened, and that did happen. Also, building up to it, the reason I think I was so forceful was because, in the beginning when it first happened, I called her first,” Mary recalled. “The first thing [Angie] said was, ‘I knew it! I knew it!’ I said, ‘Don’t talk like that, it hurts me.'”

More disagreements followed, starting with Angie allegedly texting the whole RHOSLC cast about Robert Jr.’s death before Mary had a chance to speak to any of them.

“I wanted to tell them, but she told them. She ended [her text] with, ‘It’s a Greek tragedy,'” Mary claimed. “I thought, ‘Why are you mocking [and] putting my son’s [death], the news, and ending with ‘a Greek tragedy?'”

Mary alleged that the “worst thing that happened” between her and Angie took place at Robert Jr.’s homegoing service.

“[Angie] was in my office, with [fellow Housewives] Cynthia Bailey, Lisa Barlow, Shereé [Whitfield], trying to sit on my husband’s lap,” she claimed.

Us has reached out to Angie’s representatives for comment.

In July, Angie exclusively opened up to Us about the “very real and raw challenges” she faced with Mary throughout the filming of season 7.

“There’s a lot of ups and downs. I think that’s every year. It’s not all fun,” she pointed out. “The honeymoon phase is over as your bond deepens. You’re more honest with each other and you say what you’re really feeling and thinking. We love each other. We love each other deeply.”
