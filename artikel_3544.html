Paramount's Next Big Fantasy Franchise Officially Arrives in 10 Weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

2027 is shaping up to be another big year for fantasy adaptations. Not even two full months in, Netflix will release Greta Gerwig's much-anticipated Narnia: The Magician's Nephew in theaters on February 12, marking the first movie from C.S. Lewis' literary world to reach the silver screen in over 15 years. On December 17, 2027, meanwhile, Andy Serkis will bring viewers back to Middle-earth with The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum, complete with Ian McKellen, Kate Winslet, Lee Pace, and Elijah Wood reprising their roles from Peter Jackson's trilogies. It's not just books getting the big-screen treatment either. Sony's The Legend of Zelda will take to the fields of Hyrule on April 30, headlined by Benjamin Evan Ainsworth and Bo Bragason in the iconic roles of Link and Zelda.

For Paramount's fantasy ambitions, however, the charge will be led by Children of Blood and Bone. Based on Tomi Adeyemi's breakout hit fantasy novel of the same name, the film will introduce a new audience to the Kingdom of Orïsha and the young woman, Zélie Adebola, who seeks to restore its magic. Thuso Mbedu will play Zélie on her journey as she wields the powers granted to her by the gods themselves to strike back at the oppressive regime ruling her people and take back what was so violently ripped from them. Along the way, she'll make some powerful allies too, with the daughter and son of the king joining the fight against the monarchy to forge a better future. A full seven years after Fox 2000 initially optioned the YA book, its release is finally close at hand.

Four worlds and four completely different ideas of what a good life looks like. This is where you belong, what it would ask of you, and what you would get back in return.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

You want the sea, a crew, and absolutely nobody telling you where you are allowed to go. The Grand Line is the world for people whose main ambition in life is finding out what is over there.

You would rather be feared and useful than liked and useless. The Continent is a world where the monsters are the straightforward part of the job and the people are where it all gets complicated.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

As of writing, only ten weeks remain until the ambitious adaptation hits theaters on January 15. Gina Prince-Bythewood, who last worked with Mbedu in the Viola Davis-led epic, The Woman King, in 2022, took the reins of Children of Blood and Bone as both director and co-writer alongside Adeyemi. Davis is also part of the star-studded cast, which prominently features The Acolyte alum Amandla Stenberg as Princess Amari and F1's Damson Idris as Prince Inan, alongside Tosin Cole, Cynthia Erivo, Lashana Lynch, Zackary Momoh, Richard Mofe-Damijo, Chiwetel Ejiofor, Regina King, and Idris Elba.

While there's been plenty of anticipation to see how the New York Times bestselling West African mythology-inspired epic translates to film, one person who's not particularly excited for the film is the author herself. Adeyemi is credited as the co-writer of the screenplay, but openly distanced herself from the project over the summer, describing a "painful" falling out in a series of posts on social media. "I have not seen the film, and I will not watch it," she added, also writing that she ultimately left because she couldn't "continue being hurt and attacked behind the scenes." Much of her frustration was specifically directed at Stenberg, who had previously indicated the author herself wanted her involved in the adaptation. Exact details of what transpired are unknown, but it has sown some uncertainty about the production and how it will ultimately treat Adeyemi's source material.

Children of Blood and Bone launches in theaters on January 15. Stay tuned here at Collider for more updates as it draws near.
