Pokémon Officially Recruits Tommy Lee Jones for New Ad

Pokémon is celebrating its 30th anniversary and has partnered with many major brands. From toys to clothing and even gaming accessories, and even teaming up with astronauts, Pikachu and Eevee merchandise has become hard to avoid. Now, another brand has partnered with this large Japanese franchise, featuring Tommy Lee Jones.

Jones is an award-winning actor who has appeared in numerous projects, including the Men in Black trilogy as Agent K, Volcano as Michael Roark, and, more recently, the FX series The Lowdown as Lee Raybon Sr. Outside his acting career, which began in 1969, Jones has also served as a spokesperson for Japanese beverage company Suntory, portraying a character known as "Alien Jones" in commercials for its BOSS coffee brand. Jones has played this character since 2006; he's described as an alien who takes human form to observe daily life on Earth, and he's appeared in numerous ads promoting the product, where he interacts with and watches the Japanese people in different scenarios, such as a school teacher and a police officer. Recently, Jones reprised this character as part of the coffee's latest collab.

On Suntory's social media channels, it has released a series of videos featuring Alien Jones investigating different Pokémon. The commercials are titled "BOSS Alien Jones Pokémon Investigation," where the sketched version of Alien Jones observes Psyduck, Magikarp, Ditto, and Wobbuffet. Meanwhile, on the official Suntory Japanese website, visitors will be greeted with Jones dressed up as Alien Jones, with Pikachu's tail and ears, with a translated quote of him saying, "I tried to become Pikachu. It seems it was a bit of a stretch."

The Pokémon x BOSS Coffee collab began back in August 2026. Beyond images and videos of Jones dressing up and researching Pokémon, the brand will also release exclusive Pokémon-printed BOSS Coffee products, with cans featuring a Pokédex entry for Pikachu and other notable Pokémon, including the Gen 1 and Gen 8 starters. Additionally, buying a six-pack of BOSS Coffee gives fans a chance to win exclusive merchandise, such as T-shirts, notepads, and jackets, featuring both BOSS and Pokémon branding. The campaign is only available in Japan, but fans can still get involved, as it ends before December.

The Pokémon x BOSS Coffee collab is now underway and will end on November 30, 2026. Follow Collider for more updates.
