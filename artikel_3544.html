Bill Hader’s Feature Directorial Debut ‘They Know’ Lands at Black Bear

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bill Hader‘s first film as a director has found its distribution partner. Black Bear has acquired North American distribution rights to “They Know,” Hader’s upcoming psychological horror film that he co-stars in with Linda Cardellini. The growing distributor, which just released “Wicker” nationwide, acquired the rights from financier MRC in what was described as a competitive situation.

Per an official synopsis, “They Know” follows a divorced dad who becomes increasingly suspicious and paranoid that his ex-wife’s new mysterious partner is exerting a strange, unsettling influence on their children.

“Bill Hader is a singular talent both in front of and behind the camera, and he has delivered a film in ‘They Know’ unlike anything we’ve ever seen,” Black Bear president of production & operations Michael Heimler said in a statement. “’They Know’ is authentic, original, and bold filmmaking at the highest level, and we couldn’t be more excited to partner with Bill and re-team with our great friends at MRC, who consistently deliver boundary-pushing films that help define their genre.” Related Stories Why Good Voice Acting Doesn’t Have to Happen in Isolation Watch the ‘Backrooms’ VFX Supervisor Explain His Unique Process of Collaborating with Kane Parsons

Hader co-wrote “They Know” with Duffy Boudreau and produced alongside Bob Graf, with Alyssa Donovan co-producing.

Hader is a three-time Primetime Emmy winner for his work on “South Park” and “Barry.” He co-created the HBO comedy “Barry” with Alec Berg and began directing episodes in Season 2, gradually working his way up to directing the entirety of Season 4. He has long been open about his cinematic influences and his desire to transition into feature filmmaking after the show ended.

Hader recently shared some more details about directing his first feature while speaking at IndieWire’s Los Angeles Future of Filmmaking Summit. He explained that “They Know” emerged after he and longtime friend and writing partner Boudreau both went through divorces and discussed their biggest fears about ways the process could have gone awry.

“We’re both divorced. We were talking about how lucky we were that we were on, like, we had really good terms with our ex-wives, and we have kids with them,” Hader said. “We started talking about the worst case scenarios of, ‘Oh, my god, this person is going through this and this person is going through that.’ And then this idea came up.”

Black Bear has yet to announce a release date for “They Know.”
