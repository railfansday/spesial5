Newborn Died After Mom Allegedly Beat Him Less Than 1 Hour After He Was Born

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A Mississippi woman was arrested after she allegedly beat her newborn son less than one hour after he was born, leading to his death just days later.

Jantelle Sharon Jordan has been charged with capital murder following the death of her son, Jaiden Shane Jordan-Burkes.

The Smith County Sheriff’s Office announced in a press release that deputies and paramedics responded to a home in Polkville, Mississippi, on October 18 after they received a call about a mother giving birth.

Upon their arrival, authorities learned that Jordan, 25, had given birth about an hour earlier while still at the home. The sheriff’s office said that her son was then rushed to the hospital, and he began “experiencing medical complications that initially had an unknown cause.”

Investigators determined that Jaiden had sustained injuries after birth, and he died two days after he was hospitalized.

An autopsy was performed on the boy, which revealed that his cause of death was blunt force trauma. The sheriff’s office added that his manner of death was homicide.

Despite confirming the details of his death, officers did not describe how or why they believe Jordan fatally injured her son.

Following her son’s sudden death, detectives interviewed Jordan on October 24. The woman wasn’t arrested until Friday, November 13. She was taken to the Smith County Jail following her arrest, where she remains in custody.

Authorities said that they are still investigating the case, and they plan to forward their findings to the state attorney’s office for prosecution.

“This is a horrific case for everyone involved in working on this investigation. These are the types of cases that are difficult for anyone to comprehend, especially when they involve the loss of an innocent child,” Sheriff Joel Houston said in a statement.

Jaiden was remembered in an online obituary, in which his family reflected on their short time with him and the future they could have had together.

“Jaiden was born on October 18, 2026, in Smith County, Mississippi, and although his time here was short, he made a lasting impact on the family that loved him so dearly,” the obituary read. “He would have loved racing and riding four wheelers with his father and grandfather.”

It’s not currently clear if Jordan has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following her arrest. The Smith County Sheriff’s Office did not immediately respond to Us’ request for comment regarding updates in the case.
