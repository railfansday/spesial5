Stellar (XLM) Absorbs 92% of RWA Fund Inflows in Sudden Market Consolidation

The tokenized funds market grew by $121.2 million in a day, and practically all of this amount went to one blockchain. According to the analytics platform Token Terminal, over the reporting 24 hours the Stellar network attracted $111.5 million, securing 92% of total market inflows.

Competing L1 and L2 networks showed minimal dynamics, lagging far behind the leader. The Base blockchain took second place with $5.2 million (just 4% of the market), Robinhood Chain attracted $2 million (2%), and the remaining seven specialized networks collectively split a modest $2.5 million.

This proportion holds within the bounds of the reporting day and clearly illustrates the scale of short-term liquidity concentration.

The daily inflow coincided with a series of integrations in the Stellar ecosystem at the end of October. At the Meridian conference in Lisbon, the Securitize platform announced the completion of its technical integration with Stellar. The step simplified access to the network for Securitize’s institutional clients, including BlackRock and Hamilton Lane.

In parallel, asset management company Janus Henderson, in partnership with Centrifuge, launched the JQQQ fund on Stellar, tied to the Nasdaq-100 index.

A direct link between these announcements and the $111.5 million tranche has not been institutionally confirmed; however, the events create a backdrop for the movement of liquidity. Token Terminal analysts also updated Stellar’s classification, assigning it to the categories of tokenized assets and yield instruments.

The annual growth of assets under management (AUM) on the Stellar network amounted to 360%, reaching the $4 billion mark. The current daily anomaly confirms that major players — including Franklin Templeton’s BENJI and Spiko’s SAFO funds — use Stellar’s infrastructure for targeted settlements in the debt market, thanks to which the platform holds its leadership during periods of capital consolidation.

Retail investors, meanwhile, predominantly allocate funds within EVM-compatible L2 solutions.

Such single-day concentration should, however, be read with caution: one or two large institutional transfers can easily skew daily flow data, so the sustainability of Stellar’s lead will only become clear over weekly and monthly windows.
