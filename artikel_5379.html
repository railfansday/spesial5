Bring People Together: COSCO SHIPPING at CIIE

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

SHANGHAI, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- "Connecting some people together. That's so great," said Magdalena Ognek, a COSCO SHIPPING employee, reflecting on her experience at the ninth China International Import Expo (CIIE).

Global shipping networks and supply chains help bring new technologies and products from around the world to CIIE. Beyond keeping global trade flowing, shipping and logistics also bring people together across borders, opening opportunities to exchange ideas and build relationships.

COSCO SHIPPING, one of the world's leading shipping and logistics providers, brought employees from across its global operations to the expo, using the event as a platform for industry exchange and cross-cultural dialogue. They explored the latest technologies and products and met with international customers and partners. At the company's booth, professionals from different industries and cultural backgrounds shared expertise, developed a better understanding of one another and explored opportunities for future collaboration.
