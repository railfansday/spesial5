Gasperini: “The result doesn’t show how difficult the game was.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Gian Piero Gasperini, speaking to DAZN after Parma-Roma (1-5), was pleased with his team’s victory.

“A lot, because there’s always a bit of uncertainty when so many players leave and some return at the last minute. Then you have to figure out if you can pick up where you left off. Today’s result doesn’t reflect how difficult the match was because we faced a very determined Parma side, in excellent condition, who started very strongly. Then, over the 90 minutes, the game took a different turn, but there were moments of great balance.”

The Grugliasco coach on the final step towards growth: “There are undoubtedly many details, some team-related, some individual, but there’s no doubt that this is a team that’s growing in every aspect. Even in difficult situations, we conceded a bad goal, and there could have been a psychological impact, but instead we reacted very strongly. That’s the best aspect: this team has the confidence to score goals, and even in the face of bad luck, they always have the conviction to move forward.”

Gian Piero Gasperini on Malen: “But he was still an incredible player even before coming here, just look at his career path, first in Holland and then at Dortmund. You don’t go to certain teams. If you don’t have outstanding qualities, I probably put him where he performs best, not that he didn’t do well at other teams.”

The Grugliasco-born coach concluded on Serie A forwards and the comparison with the Dutch center-forward: “In the Italian league, the numbers speak for themselves. There’s a great lineup of forwards in Serie A too. Lautaro comes to mind. He’s extraordinary right now and he combines with us. Our team certainly values ​​a striker with these characteristics, perhaps more than another, stronger striker with different characteristics.”
