Deutsche Telekom is investing about $110 million in French AI startup Mistral AI

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Its T.Capital Tech Fund put 100 million euros into Mistral's Series D round, which lifted the startup's valuation above 21 billion euros

Deutsche Telekom invested €100 million ($112 million) in French AI startup Mistral AI through its T.Capital Tech Fund, the company said on Thursday.

The money went into Mistral's Series D funding round. Deutsche Telekom said the investment sets the stage for a strategic tie-up to bring European AI to companies and public bodies across Germany. The stake is worth around $110 million, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Mistral closed the Series D in September, bringing in €3 billion ($3.36 billion) overall and lifting its valuation past €21 billion ($23.5 billion), according to The Wall Street Journal. Samsung, the Scaleup Europe Fund managed by EQT and existing investor PSG Equity co-led the round. Existing backer ASML $ASML Holding took part, as did newcomers such as Advent, BlackRock $BLK-managed funds and accounts, and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg.

Deutsche Telekom described the investment as an initial move toward pairing its infrastructure, its ties to customers and its skill at integration with Mistral's AI models, software for enterprises and capacity to deploy them, all within a future partnership, according to The Wall Street Journal. The two companies plan to assist firms and government bodies in Germany as they take up AI, concentrating on industrial and regulated sectors.

"We built Mistral so that European enterprises can deploy frontier AI they fully control, on their own data, in their own infrastructure," Mistral AI Chief Financial Officer Johan Bergqvist said, according to The Wall Street Journal.
