Hot Dog Marketing Sharpens Focus on Public Sector, Taps Central Texas Leaders to Guide Growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Agency expands strategic communications capabilities and establishes five-member Strategic Advisory Council to strengthen its position in public-sector markets.

AUSTIN, Texas, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Hot Dog Marketing, a Central Texas-based marketing and communications agency, is sharpening its focus on professional services firms, government contractors and public entities. The firm announced a new strategic communications offering and the formation of a five-member Strategic Advisory Council composed of established Central Texas business and civic leaders.

The move marks a new chapter for the agency as it builds on its established foundation to help clients become better known and trusted in the markets they serve.

"Organizations working in and around the public sector face unique challenges when it comes to building visibility, establishing credibility and communicating their value," said Jessica Scanlon, founder and CEO of Hot Dog Marketing. "We're expanding our capabilities to help clients address those challenges more strategically while continuing to provide the marketing expertise that has been central to our business."

Through the new offerings, the company aims to provide clients with an integrated approach that will help them communicate their value more effectively and build stronger reputations. The focus is on developing relationships with public-sector decision-makers and strengthening firms' presence in their target industries.

The Strategic Advisory Council brings together five leaders whose backgrounds span public infrastructure and professional services, with deep ties to the Central Texas business community.

Advisory council members will provide strategic guidance to Hot Dog Marketing's leadership as the agency responds to evolving client needs and explores new opportunities to strengthen its position in the Texas market.

"Each of these leaders brings a perspective that will challenge our thinking and help us grow intentionally," Scanlon said. "Our goal is bigger than growing an agency. We want to build a stronger organization that helps our clients grow, creates meaningful opportunities for our team and makes a positive impact in the communities we serve."

Hot Dog Marketing is a Central Texas-based marketing and strategic communications agency specializing in professional services firms, government contractors, public entities and economic development organizations. The minority- and woman-owned agency combines branding, digital marketing, creative services and strategic communications to help organizations build visibility, strengthen credibility and support business growth.
