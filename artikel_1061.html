Switzerland sealed promotion back to League A at the first time of asking thanks to their 0-0 draw in Slovenia on Friday, after a dominant campaign in Group B1.

With 11 goals scored and only one conceded, plus a perfect record until that stalemate in Maribor, the Swiss have shown themselves to be comfortably the best team in the group — none more so than when they cruised to a 3-0 win over Scotland in Glasgow in September.

Manager Murat Yakin will be without several key players for the clash in St Gallen, with midfielders Granit Xhaka and Johan Manzambi both injured, as well as Nottingham Forest striker Dan Ndoye and former Manchester City defender Manuel Akanji.

Scotland will hope those absences prove costly for Switzerland in a game they have to win, while also hoping North Macedonia — who are yet to pick up a point in Group B1 — take points off Slovenia.

Sebastien Pocognoli claimed his first home victory on Friday against the North Macedonians, thanks to John McGinn's second-half double. The Scots will need to produce their best performance under the Belgian manager if they are to triumph in St Gallen, though.

Scotland have no new injury concerns, but captain Andy Robertson is set to become his country's record appearance-maker. The Tottenham Hotspur defender should win his 103rd cap tonight, taking him past the legendary Sir Kenny Dalglish.

The absence of key players should weaken the hosts, opening the door for Scotland to get a result — but maybe not the result they want.
