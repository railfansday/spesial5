When is FA Cup second round draw? Ball numbers, time and how to watch

Seventh-tier Wimborne are one of nine non-league teams still fighting in the FA Cup ahead of the second-round draw.

The Dorset part-timers, of the Southern League Premier Division South, stunned League Two strugglers Crawley 2-0 on Sunday to reach the FA Cup second round for the first time in their history. Stockport survived a scare to beat Cray Wanderers of the Isthmian League Premier Division 3-2, while eighth-tier Stafford Rangers also led EFL opposition 2-0 before Grimsby avoid an embarrassing exit and eventually made it through on penalties.

Saturday saw a few non-league teams get the better of EFL foes as Kidderminster thrashed Doncaster 3-0, Carlisle battled past Salford City 1-0 and Barrow won 1-0 on their long trip to Gillingham, while seventh-tier Guiseley also upset National League Sutton United 1-0. Friday night’s matches saw 2021 champions, and former Premier League winners, Leicester City avoid a potential banana skin at sixth-tier Oxford City.

Another seventh-tier side, in Plymouth Parkway or Berkhamsted, will advance to the second round and those teams meet tonight to conclude the first round, following the second round draw on TNT Sports.

Here’s everything you need to know about the second-round draw

The draw for the second round of the 2026-27 FA Cup takes place on Monday 9 November, before the clash between non-league clubs Plymouth Parkway and Berkhamsted.

The television coverage of the game starts at 6:30pm GMT, with the draw itself expected to begin shortly afterwards around 6.45pm. Jules Breach will host the draw with the teams being pulled out of the pots by Karen Carney and Joel Ward.

The draw will be available to watch live in the UK on TNT Sports 1, HBO Max, and on the TNT Sports YouTube channel. Coverage begins at 6.30pm, with the draw itself expected to start at approximately 6.45pm, before kick off at 7.30pm.

There will be 40 teams in the draw for the second round with 20 fixtures to be made ahead of the entry of the Premier League and Championship clubs from the third round onwards.

The 20 FA Cup second-round matches will be held across the weekend of 5-6 of December, with the third-round draw presumed to take place on the evening of Monday 7 December.
