Ex-PlayStation boss says PS6 needs to be cheaper not more powerful

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Former PlayStation boss Shawn Layden believes Sony should stop focusing on making its consoles more powerful, arguing that the PS6 should be like a PS5 but cheaper.

With the PS5 approaching its sixth anniversary, fans are expecting the PS6 to be announced in the coming years. However, many reports have claimed that next-gen consoles could be significantly more expensive, with prices of around $1,000 being predicted.

But Layden, who previously served as chairman of Sony Interactive Entertainment until 2019, has questioned whether players actually need a more powerful console.

Instead, he believes Sony should focus on making the current generation more affordable, potentially bringing the price of a PS5 down to just $300.

Speaking on The Trophy Room podcast, Layden argued that the improvements offered by each new console generation are becoming increasingly difficult to notice. While earlier PlayStation consoles delivered dramatic graphical leaps, he suggested that the PS6’s rumored upgrades might not have the same impact.

Discussing features such as improved ray tracing and processing power, he joked that “these are differences only dogs can hear.”

“I think we have enough power in the machine. The innovation needs to come not from making PS6 for $1,200 – it’s to make another PS5 for $300. Now, that would be true innovation,” Layden explained.

He argues that the PS5 is already capable of delivering the games players want, so Sony would be better off finding ways to make that experience accessible to more people.

“This is the first platform that I’ve seen that has gone up in price over time,” he added, referring to the PS5’s price increases since launch. “What the company needs to do is do what it’s never done, which is don’t iterate the console.”
