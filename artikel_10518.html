Police mock criminal with “Ocarina of Crime” Zelda arrest video

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Police have used The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time to mock a criminal’s arrest, calling it the “Ocarina of Time.”

Over the years, a number of police departments have gotten entrenched in social media posts, regularly mocking criminals with memes and parody videos. A few of them got on a hot streak by using GTA 6 before it was released in mid-November.

However, now attention has turned to the other joint best-reviewed game of all time: The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

In a TikTok post from November 24, Merseyside Police revealed bodycam footage from an attack that took place in May 2026, dubbing it the “Ocarina of Crime” with the Zelda graphics overlaid.

As part of the clip, police chased a 59-year-old man – Stephen Elder – on horseback, with the classic Zelda objective graphic appearing on-screen, with sound effects from the game also being used.

With the horseback officer chasing after the man, they also included a graphic of horse stamina being depleted as well. “If you didn’t think our officers would pursue you on horseback, think again,” Merseyside Police said. “A-neigh-zing work by our mounted officers and horses. Well done to all!”

The 59-year-old was handed a three-year ban from football matches, while also being forced to pay £500 compensation and a further £600 fine.

Plenty of viewers saw the funny side of the video too, chiming in with further Zelda references. “Let me know if you catch that Danondorf guy,” one said. “NGL I don’t know whether I’d be made up or mortified if it was me getting caught,” another added.

As noted, police have been using games on social media to put a bigger spotlight to on arrests. Though, some forces in the United States have been worried that GTA 6 would lead to people committing real crimes.
