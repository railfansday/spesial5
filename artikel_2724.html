Lions claim DE Tyrus Wheat off of waivers

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Lions lost Aidan Hutchinson for the rest of the season on Sunday and they moved to add another defensive end to the roster on Monday.

They announced that they have claimed Tyrus Wheat off of waivers. Wheat was let go by the Cowboys over the weekend.

It will be Wheat’s second tour of duty with the Lions. He played in 15 games for the team last season and recorded 15 tackles, 1.5 sacks and a forced fumble in those appearances.

Wheat had three tackles in five games for the Cowboys this season and he also played 20 games for Dallas during the 2023 and 2024 seasons.

The Lions waived defensive back Nick Whiteside in a corresponding move.
