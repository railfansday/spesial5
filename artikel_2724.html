UFC Fight Night 294 weigh-in results: One fighter comes in heavy

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Ultimate Fighting Championship returns this weekend, with UFC Fight Night 294 at ABHA Arena in Doha, Qatar, which will begin bright and early in the United States on Saturday, November 21.

UFC Fight Night 294, which is also known as UFC Doha, is headlined by a thrilling light heavyweight bout between former 205-pound champion Jiri Prochazka (32-6-1) and undefeated rising star Navajo Stirling (11-0).

MORE: UFC Qatar: Jiří Procházka vs Navajo Stirling, main card preview

In the co-main event, former featherweight champion Aljamain Sterling (26-5) is back in action to face off against Kevin Vallejos (18-1).

UFC Fight Night 294 will kick off at 10:00 a.m. ET with the preliminary, with the main card set to begin at 1:00 p.m. ET. John Gooden will provide play-by-play from cageside, along with UFC Hall of Famer Michael Bisping and former lightweight contender Paul Feldman.

UFC correspondent Heidi Androl will provide interviews and updates from backstage.
