Seahawks-49ers Thursday injury report: Charles Cross a full go, Brock Purdy a DNP

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ahead of a must-win NFC West rematch, the Seattle Seahawks' offense appears set to get their starting left tackle, Charles Cross, back.

Cross has been out since the Week 7 game against the Kansas City Chiefs with an ankle injury, but was a full participant in practice on Thursday. Cross' return could come at a crucial time as the Seahawks are already planning to be without star guard Quenton Nelson and starting right tackle Abe Lucas, both of whom did not practice on the holiday.

Signs continue to trend positively for the return of Seahawks' quarterback Sam Darnold, who was a full go in practice for the second day in a row. On the other side of the field, San Francisco 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy did not practice with an ankle injury on Thursday, setting the table for a potential Mac Jones start on Sunday.

Sticking with the offense, prolific wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba was once again limited in practice with an ankle injury. JSN has been the Seahawks' entire offense, so even the potential of him missing this game is enough to give any Seahawks fan a miniature heart attack at minimum.

Seahawks guard Anthony Bradford was also limited with a knee injury and an illness. Bradford has been battling injuries all season, but served in a key role of holding back disaster up front with Nelson out. Star linebacker Ernest Jones continued to be limited in practice with a knee injury, while edge rusher DeMarcus Lawrence was upgraded to limited, some welcome good news for the Seahawks front-7.

The "Dark Side" defense will likely be without Jarran Reed, who missed practice for the second day in a row with a toe injury. However, Leonard Williams is making steady progress on the stinger he suffered against the Chicago Bears, being a limited participant for the second day in a row.

For the 49ers, they got positive news about Mike Evans, who destroyed the Seahawks' secondary in the first game. Evans was elevated to a limited participant, but all eyes remain on Purdy as we look ahead to Sunday's contest.
