Our Defense vs. Their Offense: Cincinnati Bengals

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Here is the side of the matchup all New Orleans Saints fans should be concerned about. The Saints will have to slow down former national champion and current MVP favorite Joe Burrow. Not to mention the matchup between the best receiver in the league, Ja'Marr Chase, and the Saints' secondary. Oh, and Tee Higgins is a problem as well.

The Bengals are currently sixth in the NFL in total offense, averaging 383 yards per game. They are averaging 289 passing yards per game, which ranks first in the NFL. The weakness of the Bengals' offense is their rushing attack, as they are just as bad as the Saints at running the ball. The Bengals only average 84 yards per game on the ground, which ranks 30th in the NFL.

The Saints rank 27th in total defense, allowing 355 total yards per game. As poorly as the Bengals run the ball on offense, the Saints are just as bad at stopping the run on defense. The Saints' defense is allowing 131 rushing yards per game, which ranks 28th in the NFL. This will be a matchup of weakness against weakness. If the Saints allow the Bengals to establish offensive balance, this game will become significantly more difficult for them to defend.

Entering their Monday night matchup, the Bengals were averaging 36.2 points per game during their five-game winning streak, so this offense is rounding into midseason form. Burrow is on pace to become the first QB in NFL history to finish a season with over 5,000 passing yards, 50 TDs, and a 70% completion percentage.

The Saints have one advantage they can proudly lean on, and that is the pass-rushing ability of their two edge rushers, Chase Young and Carl Granderson. Now, this does not mean it will be easy for them because, unlike in years past, the Bengals have been able to improve the protection of their franchise QB. The Bengals have allowed only 16 sacks this season. This places a premium on being able to generate pressure on Burrow with just four rushers. If Brandon Staley has to bring more than four, Burrow, Higgins, and Chase will be more than willing to take advantage.
