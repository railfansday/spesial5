Oregon availability report: Davison, Hill questionable vs. Washington

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

We have finally reached the last week of the regular season, and while the stakes have risen for Dan Lanning and the Oregon Ducks, so too has the weight of the weekly availability report.

Last week, the Ducks missed starting running back Jordon Davison, who was out for his second consecutive game with a lower-body injury, and matters were only made worse when Dierre Hill Jr. went down with a leg injury on the third play of the game. Hill was seen with a boot and crutches during the second half, officially throwing his status for rivalry week against the Washington Huskies into question.

On Wednesday night, the first availability report of the week gave us a look at what to expect from Oregon's two star rushers, as well as the rest of the team when it comes to health. Davison and Hill are both listed as questionable, which would be huge news for the Ducks on Saturday if either is able to play.

On Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday evenings, the Big Ten Conference will release the statuses of several players, with teams classifying them as out, doubtful, or questionable.
