LaNorris Sellers injury update, star South Carolina QB exits Texas A&M game

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

COLUMBIA - Star South Carolina quarterback LaNorris Sellers left the game after suffering an ankle injury in the second quarter against Texas A&M Oct. 7.

Sellers dropped back to pass then was sacked by Texas A&M's Bryce Perry-Wright. The hit jarred the ball free with the Aggies recovering the ball on their own 22 with 5:23 left in the second quarter.

Sellers was helped off the field and went to the injury tent. He was then taken to the locker room.

South Carolina leads Texas A&M 7-3 with two minutes left in the first half.

"We're concerned," South Carolina coach Shane Beamer said in an ESPN interview at halftime. Beamer added that Sellers had an ankle injury and was getting X-rays.
