Vote now for Monroe County Region Athlete of the Week for Nov. 9-15

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

One Hunter topped another in the race for the Monroe County Region Athlete of the Week for Nov. 2-8.

Jefferson football player Hunter Barton drew 461 votes to finish ahead of Summerfield volleyball player Hailey Hunter, who wound up with 368

Third place went to Whiteford football player Tri Eitniear with 190.

Football and volleyball both have three winners this fall.

More: Jefferson, Whiteford football reach MHSAA semifinals with dramatic wins

Perhaps the tie will be broken this week.

There are three volleyball players and a pair of football players on the ballot for Nov. 9-15 along with a trio of swimmers.

You can vote until 11:59 p.m. Wednesday, Nov. 18.

Here are the candidates in alphabetical order.

Karis Beaton, Monroe girls swimming: Named Swimmer of the Meet at the SMISL championships after winning the individual medley and 100 freestyle and swimming on two winning relays.

Luke Beaudrie, Jefferson football: Ran for 41 of the 52 yards, including a 23-yard touchdown, on the game-winning drive in the Regional finals..

Sarah Giroux, Flat Rock volleyball: Capped her outstanding career with 28 kills and 27 digs in Regional semifinals.

Kenna Goulet, Bedford volleyball: Logged 16 digs in Regional semifinals and 11 kills in finals.

Meredith Hasley, Erie Mason-Ida girls swimming: Took first place in the butterfly at the SMISL championships.

Brek Ruddy, Whiteford football: Set Region record for points in a season and topped 2,000 rushing yards for season while running for 259 yards and 3 TDs in Regional finals.

Sophia Smith, Monroe girls swimming: Won the 50 freestyle and breaststroke at the SMISL championships.

Avery Yaeger, SMCC volleyball: Recorded 9 kills and 4 blocks in Regional finals.
