Lions battle Chicago Bears on Thanksgiving: Live updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

In one of the most anticipated Thanksgiving Day to take place in Detroit, the Lions host the Chicago Bears to see who'll take the top spot in the competitive NFC North division.

With the Lions coming into Thursday's showdown riding a six-game winning streak and the Bears coming in winning five straight, along with this being the third time that Dan Campbell will face former offensive coordinator Ben Johnson, this game already has plenty of storylines. But the Lions organization is playing with a heavy heart after the passing of Lions owner Martha Firestone Ford on Tuesday.
