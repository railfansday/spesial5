Chargers-Jets Injury Report: Ladd McConkey returns to practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

After backs spasms kept sidelined for the past two games, wide receiver Ladd McConkey returned to practice on Wednesday but participated in a limited fashion. He was one of five players who logged limited practices, joining cornerback Donte Jackson (groin), defensive lineman Justin Eboigbe (ankle), offensive lineman Trey Pipkins (knee), and safety Kendall Williamson (calf).

The six remaining players on the injury report were all DNPs. Edge rusher Khalil Mack received his usual veteran's rest day to begin the new week, but the other five players who worked off to the side were cornerback Isas Waxter (ankle), wide receiver Brenen Thompson (hamstring), cornerback Rodney Shelley (ankle), tight end Charlie Kolar (concussion), and linebacker Daiyan Henley (knee).

Henley played 61 of 69 snaps against the Ravens on Monday night and Jim Harbaugh told reporters earlier Wednesday he would sit out practice with knee soreness.

Pipkins' return could be something to watch should he be activated off of injured reserve this week. Harbaugh told reporters that rookie Logan Taylor would remain the starter after a standout performance against the Ravens. If right guard Cole Strange continues to underperform, there may be a chance Pipkins could relieve him of his duties. Kayode Awosika, who is now healthy, would also be a name to watch for that scenario, as well.
