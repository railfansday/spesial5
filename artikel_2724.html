Anthony Edwards: 'I never wanted to have surgery,' no timeline for return

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Anthony Edwards met with the media Thursday, but that doesn't mean he'll be back on the court later that night when the Timberwolves host the Knicks. When will he return?

"I have no clue. Whenever my knee tells me it's ready, I'll be ready," Edwards said in his first public words since he had arthroscopic surgery to clean up his right knee three weeks ago. The surgery is expected to keep Ant sidelined until around Christmas.

Edwards said he didn't really want to have the surgery, but had to after the pain got worse.

"I'm the type of guy, I never wanted to have surgery. I thought it would just, you know, go away. In the offseason it was actually good, like we was managing it pretty good, treating it, and it wasn't as swole. Then, as soon as the season kicked off it, I don't know what happened. It just flared back up like really bad. Then I was like, "Man, it's time"...

"It was good in [training] camp, like I said. I think after the Miami game it started to flare up, and then it went down a little bit after the Orlando game, so we thought we were going to be alright, but then after the back-to-back it was really bad, like painful to fly, so I'm like, "I gotta get it."

It's a small sample size, but Edwards arguably was playing the best basketball of his career to start the season, averaging 33.3 points, 4.8 rebounds and 3.5 assists per game while shooting 40% from 3-point range, all while the Timberwolves were 3-1.

Minnesota, behind LaMelo Ball, Ayo Dosunmu and the best defense in the league, has gone 5-2 since Edwards went out. Edwards recognized what is going on and said that when he returns he wants to fit in.

"I'm thirsty to come back, but not super fast because of how they're playing. I don't want to take them out of rhythm," Edwards said. "But I told them, when I come back, I'm not going to try to be who I usually am and try to ball hog and take all the shots. I'm just going to fit in where I get in – take a couple catch-and-shoots. When they need me, I'm there."

Edwards isn't just going to accept a role, he's an elite player, but Minnesota might be better — and a more dangerous playoff team — because of what they have learned while he is out.
