NFL power rankings: Nick’s picks for how teams stack up before Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Buffalo Bills scored 21 points in the second half to blow out the Jets, 31-12. James Cook III rushed for 157 yards with one score. The top of the NFL is competitive among the top five teams, but Buffalo remains No. 1, due to solid defense and a Josh Allen-led offense.

The Chicago Bears had their bye in Week 10.

Brock Purdy went 22 of 28 for 389 yards with three touchdowns, two of which were LONG completions to Christian McCaffrey. Deebo Samuel Sr. had a long touchdown run as well. The 46-40 game was competitive, due to the performances of Dak Prescott and George Pickens, but San Francisco was too much for Jerry Jones' Cowboys.

Jacksonville beat up the Tennessee Titans, 40-23, but the game was close until the second half. Liam Coen's offense gained 459 yards; they're one of the hottest teams in the league, and their defense is playing well too. They complete their three-game road trip in New York against the Giants in Week 11.

The Vikings traded for quarterback Kirk Cousins, and he completed 21 of 31 for 207 yards with two touchdowns and an interception, while suffering three sacks. The offense was efficiently and effectively run in this NFC North road matchup against Green Bay. The Vikings defense dominated Green Bay, holding them to just 10 points.

The Bengals went nuclear once again – 500+ total yards of offense, with Joe Burrow tossing four touchdowns for 409 yards on 25 of 44 passing. Chase Brown's receiving skills were on display; he caught eight passes for 39 yards with two touchdowns, and Ja'Marr Chase caught 11 passes for 231 yards. The Bengals defense dominated Aaron Rodgers and the Steelers. The final score, 38-18, Bengals. The Bengals have now won five games in a row.

The Chiefs were without Kenneth Walker III, but rookie Emmett Johnson rushed for 101 yards on 22 carries with six catches for 70 yards with two touchdowns through the air. Patrick Mahomes threw for 343 yards and 5 touchdowns, one to new arrival Josh Downs. The Falcons didn't stand a chance at home – 35-13 Chiefs.

Detroit beat up the Patriots in Munich. Jared Goff threw for 303 yards with three touchdowns (and two picks), while Jahmyr Gibbs gained 153 yards on the ground (7-yard average!) with a pair of rushing touchdowns. Detroit suffered a terrible loss with Aidan Hutchinson going down after a sack celebration in the first half. Still, the defense didn't have to do much against the Tommy DeVito-led Patriots' offense.

Drew Lock is 3-0 this season for Seattle; he completed 14 of 17 passes for 240 yards and two touchdowns in this low-scoring 21-13 win over the Fernando Mendoza-led Las Vegas Raiders. Lock had some DOTs to Jaxson Smith-Njigba, who secured eight of ten targets for 171 yards with a touchdown. Seattle travels to San Francisco in two weeks.

Green Bay was dominated by the Vikings' defense, which forced FOUR Green Bay turnovers. The Packers still can't run the football, and the heater that Jordan Love was on found its solution in Brian Flores' defense.

The Rams beat up the Carson Beck-led Cardinals. 34-6. Blake Corum rushed for 100 yards, and Ronnie Rivers had 81 yards on 15 straight runs to conclude the game. Los Angeles wasn't threatened in this game; their defense forced three turnovers and sacked Beck five times.

Cleveland is now 4–0 at home – first time since the 1960s! Houston took a big 17-0 lead but Cleveland rushed for three touchdowns and stayed resilient against a potent Houston offense. Cleveland won the game, 27-24 in the final minutes. Cleveland is a good, well-coached football team.

The Giants hosted the Commanders on Thursday Night Football, and they couldn't find a way to win a tight game after Dennard Wilson's defense forced two interceptions and a turnover on downs in the fourth quarter. Despite 293-yards from J.J. McCarthy, and another epic performance from Isaiah Likely, the Giants could not secure the win, allowing the Commanders to hang inch closer to the top of the NFC East.

The Steelers came out of their bye and laid a dud in Cincinnati. The offense couldn't move the football and the Steelers' defense could not slow down Joe Burrow. The Steelers' four-game winning streak is now over, and an interstate rivalry game in Philadelphia awaits them.

The Panthers defeated the Saints in New Orleans, 20-17, in a slower paced game where Chubba Hubbard had 27 rushes for 92 yards with two rushing touchdowns. Carolina's defense shut down Tyler Shough, for the most part, and the Panthers' offense benefitted from a last-second pass interference that allowed kickerRyan Fitzgerald to make up for his previous miss.

The Commanders responded to Wilson's aggressive approach and found a way to earn a pivotal road victory against the Giants 24-21. Jayden Daniels threw three third-down touchdowns against New York, and Washington's defense stiffened against a flaccid final two drives by the Giants' offense.

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers had a bye in Week 10.

Houston blew a 17-0 lead and allowed Cleveland to crawl back into the game for a 27-24 win. Nico Collins caught nine passes for 94 yards with a touchdown in the game, but this was – yet another – tough loss for Houston.

The Ravens lost a tough one on Monday Night Football to the previous winless Los Angeles Chargers. It was Jascoby Brissett's first start, and the quarterback kept the game competitive with no rushing attack. Zay Flowers' costly fumble with 2:01 left in the game all but sealed the victory for Justin Herbert and the Chargers to finally win — 23-20.

The Patriots were blown out two weeks in a row – this time in Germany, 35-10. What was more costly was the loss of Drake Maye, who will miss a significant amount of time with a knee injury. It's not good for the defending AFC Champions.

The Panthers defeated the Saints in New Orleans, 20-17, in a slower-paced game where Chubba Hubbard had 27 rushes for 92 yards with two rushing touchdowns. Carolina's defense shut down Tyler Shough, for the most part, and the Panthers' offense benefitted from a last-second pass interference that allowed kicker Ryan Fitzgerald to make up for his previous miss.

The Cowboys' defense is memed for its mistakes at this point. Caleb Downs was run over on Kalon Black's touchdown run, and defensive coordinator Christian Parker had Von Miller on Christian McCaffrey during a 70+ yard touchdown reception. Dallas has become a joke and the NFL is laughing, but the Cowboys still receive excellent offensive output due to Prescott. Dallas had 439 yards of offense, but San Francisco had 584 – wow!

Fernando Mendoza had some high points against a talented Seattle defense, including a good touchdown run. Mendoza went 17 of 29 for 143 yards and was sacked six times, though. Seattle's defense asserted itself against the former No. 1 overall pick. Las Vegas lost Brock Bowers in the game too, which must be monitored.

Atlanta couldn't do much at home against the Chiefs, but they mounted some offense in the fourth quarter. Atlanta has now lost five of their last six games.

The Jets lost Geno Smith for part of the game, forcing Cade Klubnik into the lineup. The rookie found some success with his legs, but the Jets lost in the end, 31-12. The future of the Jets coaching staff remains up in the air.

The Chargers are still the CHARGERS, but their defense SHUT DOWN Baltimore's rushing attack on the road in their first win on Monday Night Football. Los Angeles ran the football well with Omarion Hampton, and they finally found a win. Credit to Dalvin Tomlinson for recording two sacks!

The Philadelphia Eagles had a bye in Week 10.

Despite terrible quarterback play, the Indianapolis Colts were able to squeak by the 1-8 Kyle McCord-led Dolphins. Jones finished 11 of 22 for 108 yards with a touchdown and two picks. Seth McGowan was the highlight of the offense, with 12 carries for 77 yards and a score; Jonathan Taylor finished with 16 carries for 83 yards. Indianapolis is on a short week, playing Thursday night on the road against the Houston Texans.

The Titans lost 40-23 to the Tennessee Titans, but Cam Ward looked okay; 28 of 39 for 231 passing yards and two passing touchdowns.with 39 yards on the ground. Epic Ayomanor lead the Titans with five catches for 79 yards, as Carnell Tate remained out.

Arizona started rookie Carson Beck, who finished 11 of 18 for 108 yards with two interceptions and he suffered five sacks. The Cardinals could do nothing offensively and the Rams dropped 202 rushing yards, including an end of game drive where Ronnie Rivers received 15 straight touches for 81 yards. Rams easily won, 34-6. It could be a long end of the season for Arizona.

Ollie Gordon II received a heavy workload with 24 total touches, totaling 76 yards rushing and 39 yards receiving. Kyle McCord fumbled for a Colts' defensive touchdown, but the rookie wasn't inept, albeit he struggled; he went 17 of 32 for 209 yards, while suffering three sacks. Rookie wide receiver Caleb Douglas caught three passes for 71 yards.
