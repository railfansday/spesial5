Duval Dispatch: Jags earn ‘C’ in loss to Giants

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Jaguars got a bit of a reality check on Sunday after coming up short against the Giants in a 23-18 loss.

There was a controversial call on the field that negated a Parker Washington touchdown which could have made the difference for Jacksonville, but overall the sentiment is that the Jaguars left this one get away from them

For their efforts, CBS Sports' John Breech gave the Jags a "C" game grade in the loss. Here's what he had to say:

"The Giants might have just given other teams a recipe to beat the Jaguars: Punch them in the mouth and see what happens. The Jags offensive line was no match for the Giants' pass-rush, and that created a lot of problems for Jacksonville's offense. Trevor Lawrence couldn't get into any sort of rhythm, and he ended up throwing two UGLY interceptions on a day when he was also sacked three times. With Lawrence flustered, the Jags struggled in key situations, especially in the fourth quarter, when they were 0-for-9 on third-down attempts (Lawrence also threw an incompletion on a failed fourth-and-1 in the third quarter). The Jags were starting to look invincible after winning three straight games and averaging 38.3 points, but this loss was a reality check."
