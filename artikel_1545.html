Manchester United is heading towards Mauricio Pochettino!

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Manchester United are expected to part ways with Michael Carrick in the coming days. According to the British press, Mancunian leaders are considering sacking their coach during the international break, after having been in danger since the start of the season. Despite a spectacular draw against Aston Villa (4-4), the Red Devils remain in the relegation zone and have only two victories in ten Premier League days. A situation which considerably weakens Carrick, while the club would like to quickly find a new technician to turn things around.

Appointed in January to replace Ruben Amorim before receiving a two-year contract in May, he could therefore already be living his final hours on the Mancunian bench. After leading his team to third place last season and qualification for the Champions League, the former midfielder is going through a much more complicated period. Asked about a possible resignation after the draw against Aston Villa, he was categorical: “it won’t happen”. But according to the Daily MailManchester United would like to install their new coach before the trip to Anfield on November 22.

In this perspective, Mauricio Pochettino now stands out as the big favorite to succeed him. Currently coach of the United States, the Argentinian could be released thanks to a clause set at more than 8 million euros. His experience in the Premier League, with Tottenham, Chelsea and Southampton, constitutes a strong argument for the Mancunian leaders. According to the BBCMauricio Pochettino would be open to a transfer to Manchester United, even if he has just extended his contract with the United States because the clause would allow him to leave his role as coach if a prestigious offer arises.

Other profiles nevertheless remain under study, notably Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann. The former Newcastle coach had been considered during Manchester United’s previous discussions, but he would have already refused the idea of ​​taking charge of the Red Devils. For his part, Nagelsmann is available after his departure from the German selection, while the name of Antonio Conte is still circulating. Pochettino, however, remains one step ahead in Mancunian thoughts, even if his arrival would require resolving the question of his contract with the United States.
