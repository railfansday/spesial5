French team: which eleven to face Italy?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The first meeting of the Zidane era had already given rise to several withdrawals between the publication of the list and the first match played. The second forces the new coach to dig deeper and deeper into the French reserves, like Saël Kumbedi called in as reinforcements to compensate for the absence of Kalulu. Marcus Thuram and Aurélien Tchouameni also had to withdraw from the two matches against Italy and Turkey, while Kylian Mbappé is heading towards an absence for the trip to Italy on Thursday evening.

Which necessarily complicates the task of Zizou, faced with an offensive shortage. His first two choices for the number 9 position were Ousmane Dembélé and Esteban Lepaul during his first selection, both are absent due to injury, the next two were Kylian Mbappé and Marcus Thuram and both will also be absent against Italy. Elie Junior Kroupi and Senny Mayulu were called up urgently, but will they be judged as possible starters for the most important match of this November gathering?

The only team to have frustrated the Blues in September, Italy will want to repeat their performance, in Milan, and Zinédine Zidane will have to field the best possible team. In defense, the presence of Maignan in goal seems obvious, while we will have to choose between Konaté and Jacquet to accompany Upamecano. Truffert seems to have a head start on Diouf in the left lane, while Malo Gusto has a boulevard in front of him to start on the right. In the middle, in the absence of Tchouameni, Lucas Da Cunha could continue as sentry. A debate will be held between Adrien Rabiot and Eduardo Camavinga, relaunched by ZZ en Bleu. It remains to be seen whether Rayan Cherki will be threatened by Warren Zaire-Emery for the third position in midfield.

But you have understood, the real debate is at the number 9 position. Because there is little doubt that Désiré Doué and Michael Olise will be placed in the best conditions, one on the left side, the other on the right. Bradley Barcola can help out in the position of central attacker, but he was not included in the list for that by Zidane, who demonstrated during the first meeting that he wanted to respect the positions established at the time of the constitution of the list (Zaire-Emery considered as a midfielder and not as a right back, Mbappé on the left, etc.). Therefore, this could also exclude the option of a refocused Doué, with a Barcola who would take the left side.

If we follow Zidane’s logic established during the first international break, each player is called up for a specific position, and he is not afraid to send a new one into the deep end, no matter the adversity. We saw this from the Blues’ second match, against Belgium. Thus, the starting place could well be played out between Eli Junior Kroupi, 20 years old and returned to competition for two weeks, and Senny Mayulu, also 20 years old, versatile and rather brilliant with PSG in recent weeks in a role of false number 9. And since Zidane called Kroupi before Mayulu, it is perhaps he who will be propelled into the starting position against Italy…
