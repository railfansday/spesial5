PSG positions itself on the sulfurous Gianluca Prestianni file

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Times have changed since the Vinicius-Prestianni affair. A little less than a year ago, the Argentinian was at the center of a serious controversy during the Champions League match between Benfica and Real Madrid. The Real Madrid winger accused his opponent of making racist remarks to him, while covering his mouth so that his lips could not be read. Referee François Letexier was forced to stop the match for a few minutes, which ended in a bit of chaos after this affair.

UEFA still opened an investigation leading to a 6-match suspension of the Benfica player, 3 of which were suspended for comments deemed “discriminatory” And “homophobic“. This affair was even the trigger for a new law by FIFA. From, “any player covering his mouth during a confrontation with an opponent may be penalized with a red card“. This rule has been implemented since the World Cup. Miguel Almiron (Paraguay) was even the first player sanctioned.

Prestianni probably did not change his behavior after this affair. On the other hand, his rating has exploded. Having become a starter in recent months, the 20-year-old winger has become the X factor of his team since the start of the season. With 12 goals and 6 assists in 20 matches in all competitions, he does a lot of damage from his left side or in the center, where his coach Marco Silva places him most of the time. The 20-year-old Argentinian is starting to attract many recruiters from the biggest European clubs.

According to the show Record na Horaattached to the Portuguese newspaper RecordPSG is one of the contenders, along with Bayern Munich. Benfica currently values ​​him at around €100 million. There is no question of a departure this winter, specifies the media. Prestianni would therefore not be a potential “replacement” for Kvaratskhelia, injured for the end of the season, but rather a possible recruit for a more distant future. It remains to be seen how this rumor will be perceived by Parisian supporters, who are committed to the fight against racism.
