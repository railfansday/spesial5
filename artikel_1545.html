Chelsea’s England playmaker on Man Utd’s radar

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Chelsea transfer news around Cole Palmer has flared again, with Mauricio Pochettino’s arrival at Manchester United reviving the noise. Reporter Dean Jones insists the England playmaker’s future at Stamford Bridge is not in doubt, but he admits the sentiment around it is becoming harder to ignore.

Palmer, a boyhood United supporter, has been linked with Old Trafford before. United appointed Pochettino on 18 November after sacking Michael Carrick following one win in ten league games. The Argentine made Palmer one of his most used players at Chelsea, where the pair built a positive relationship, and Palmer scored 22 league goals in 2023/24.

Jones rates the chances of Palmer joining United as extremely low. United’s January budget stands at roughly £50m, and they want a left-back, a centre-back and a striker. A player FotMob values at €100m, roughly £87m, sits outside both the money and the shopping list. Jones also sees no sign that Palmer wants to leave, and says relations at Stamford Bridge would need to break down badly first.

Palmer has started all 11 of Chelsea’s Premier League games this season, playing 970 minutes for three goals, one from the penalty spot, and three assists. His 23 chances created include six big chances, and his 2.58 expected goals backs up the output. Xabi Alonso says, “The better Cole we have, the better Chelsea we have”.

Only ten of Palmer’s 31 shots have hit the target; he wins 46.9% of his dribbles, and he has collected two yellows and a red. Fitness worries more after an injury-hit 2025/26 that ended with him missing the World Cup.

No, but Chelsea should treat Jones’s condition as the real warning. Palmer’s contract runs to 2034, and United’s budget does not reach his valuation, so the danger lies in a deteriorating relationship, not a bid. That puts the onus on Alonso, Chelsea’s seventh permanent manager since 2021.

Morgan Rogers’ £117m arrival from Aston Villa creates the obvious flashpoint. If Palmer feels crowded out of the number ten role, or pushed wide right too often, the homesickness talk will return. Clear roles and managed minutes will protect him better than another forward.

The money belongs elsewhere. Alonso’s side beat United 2-0 on 31 October, then lost 1-0 at Sunderland eight days later, and defensive brittleness and thin squad depth keep undermining a front line that already scores. A back line that leaks goals will not.
