GTA 6: How to get more than $15,000 early on with almost no effort

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Everyone's journey through Grand Theft Auto VI is going to go a little differently, as that's the nature of a big, freeform open-world adventure. But one thing is certain for every player: You're going to need money, and fast.

That's especially true at the beginning of chapter 2 of the game's main story. For reasons I won't spoil in case you aren't there yet, chapter 2 finds Jason and Lucia in a tough spot with almost no money to their names, and an urgent need to get some. Thankfully, there are a couple of different methods that, when combined, can net you somewhere between $15,000 and $20,000, with almost no killing or even any real effort required.

Just understand that there may be some minor spoilers for the beginning of chapter 2 if you decide to read ahead.

We'll start with a method to get at least $10,000 (and possibly as much as $14,000), which we first learned about from GamingBible and tested ourselves to ensure it worked. Without beating around the bush, it does work.

It involves going to an abandoned warehouse outside the town of Hamlet, which is easily accessible from the very beginning of the game. However, because (spoiler warning) you lose all of your held money at the end of chapter 1, I highly advise waiting until chapter 2 to do this.

Anyway, you'll know you're in the right place if you follow the waypoint marker in the screenshot above and see a warehouse with an Apollo sign out front. There will be lots of vehicles and construction equipment outside, but don't worry, it's totally empty inside. Well, except for a bunch of rotting corpses and more than $10,000 worth of contraband lying around. Simply pick all of it up, stash it in the trunk of your car, and take it to a fence for a low-effort, no-risk payday. Just make sure you have a fence available to you when you do this. You'll unlock one early in chapter 2, so perhaps you should work your way through a few main story missions at the beginning of the chapter before taking this on. It's also logistically a little easier to do this with both Jason and Lucia together, as there is a lot to carry in there, so maybe keep playing until that's an option, too.

This second batch of cash is one we heard about from our friends at IGN, and, again, we can vouch for it ourselves. It's something you can only do after the start of chapter 2, but just to reiterate, you are pretty destitute with almost no money and no vehicles to your name at that point in the game. It's worth playing a couple of story missions to at least fix the vehicle problem first before doing this next bit of money-stealing.

To be specific, there is a "rainy day fund" of $5,000 cash hidden in Jason's bedroom in his old house. Unfortunately, by this point in the game, that house has been exploded real good, and the entire neighborhood is a heat area where cops are stationed and looking for Jason and Lucia. It's a bit of a hike from where the story takes place in chapter 2, and it's in a risky spot, so this is a super easy wad of cash to miss.

Still, you can at least drive (or take a cab) down to the area outside the heat zone and go on foot from there. Do whatever you can to sneak to the backside of Jason's house and get inside. You can easily evade the cops if you stick to nearby backyards and maybe do a little bit of swimming. Just stay crouched and quiet, and you'll get in and out easily. Once you're in, go to Jason's old bedroom, open the stash, and grab the $5,000. You can also optionally grab some old photos while you're at it, which will raise Jason and Lucia's relationship rating.

If you do both of these things, which should take maybe half an hour of real-time at most, you can roll around in chapter 2 with as much as $19,000 in your account before you get started doing real crimes. Have fun, folks.
