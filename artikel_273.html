Topics Black Friday Gaming Pokemon Trading Cards

If you spot a solid discount on a Pokémon TCG pack, box, or collection you haven’t seen before (like a 30th Celebration ETB, Delta Reign Booster Bundle, etc.), it’s worth buying now rather than waiting for Black Friday.

When you see a particular price drop, that deal is more likely specific to one or several sellers than to the industry market price across all sellers — depending on their own stock allocations. When that stock is gone, it’s uncertain whether those sellers will have any more units coming in, or even at the same lower price point.

Although more Pokémon cards are being printed than ever in 2026, the Pokémon TCG still has the same issue that sees any readily available selection of packs, or boxes of packs, being sold for above MSRP: There is nowhere near enough supply to meet the demand.

As a result, prices for readily available cards are dictated by market price: estimated by factors like pull rates, the most valuable cards in an expansion, how many print runs an expansion has had, and their overall contribution to the meta.

Day-to-day deals vary between standard Pokémon TCG retailers like Amazon, Walmart, and Target — but the best Black Friday deals are likely to be from third-party sellers operating through those storefronts. There are a lot of sellers competing for sales with one another to offload their stock, and Black Friday is still the ideal season to undercut other Pokémon TCG merchants.
