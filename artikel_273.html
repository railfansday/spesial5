Our 2027 Innovators Under 35 competition is now open

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Nominate yourself or someone you know by January 19.

Every year, the editors at MIT Technology Review select 35 of the world’s top young scientists, entrepreneurs, and inventors to honor as our newest class of Innovators Under 35. And starting today, our 2027 competition is officially open for nominations.

We’re on the hunt for researchers and founders who are engineering solutions to some of the most formidable problems society faces, including human disease and climate change. We also recognize those who have made clever and useful inventions, important new scientific discoveries, or technological breakthroughs early in their careers.

We want to hear about people, based anywhere in the world, who are responsible for a major finding or advance and can show real-world impact from their work. To qualify for the list, a nominee must be under the age of 35 as of October 1, 2027 (that is, born on or after October 2, 1992).

Have someone in mind? Or think you’d be a great candidate and want to nominate yourself? Follow this link to suggest someone who you think should make our 2027 list. It’s free and takes only a few minutes. Just be sure to get your nomination in before the deadline—which is 11:59 p.m. Eastern time on Tuesday, January 19.

After the deadline, our editors will review all nominees and select a group of semifinalists, whom we’ll invite to complete a formal application. Then, dozens of expert judges will help us evaluate those applicants and settle on the finalists. You can read more about our selection process here.

We look forward to reviewing your nominations.
