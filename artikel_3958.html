Poll: 15 Years Later, Is Skyrim Still One of the Best Open World RPGs Out There?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

11/11/11 — the famous release date that will forever be associated with The Elder Scrolls 5: Skyrim.

If you haven't already realised, today marks the title's 15th anniversary, and it's crazy to think that the open world RPG remains one of the most consistently popular games out there.

Indeed, at the time of writing this article, Skyrim's racking up around 40,000 concurrent players on Steam. Again, it's 15 years old!

There's no denying that Skyrim has a unique magic to it; an atmosphere, a sense of time and place. Bethesda struck absolute gold with this one, despite its often obvious flaws as a role-playing excursion.

Of course, the fact that Skyrim's simply never been replaced probably bolsters its legacy. The wait for The Elder Scrolls 6 has felt eternal at points, and the long, long awaited sequel is reportedly still years away from release.

I mean, would you be at all surprised if we got a PS6 port of Skyrim before Sentinel's arrival?

But Skyrim also owes so much of its longevity to its players. Modders have kept the candle burning for over a decade, and people are still forging ahead with extremely ambitious projects today.

No game maintains that level of interest without being special in the first place, however. I'd argue that even now, there's no open world RPG quite like Skyrim — it's a truly timeless experience with regards to its gameplay loop, dynamic happenings, and player-driven sense of adventure.

I've seen Skyrim dubbed Distractions: The Game, and that comedic description still rings true. I've lost track of how many times I've started a new Skyrim playthrough over the years, but somehow, it's never lost its appeal — and that's primarily because of how freeform it can feel.

But do you think Skyrim still stands tall as an all-timer RPG? Dare I ask if you're still playing it in 2026? Vote in our polls, and then tell me all about your wintry adventures in the comments section below.
