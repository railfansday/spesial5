Set pieces lift Current in semifinal as Gotham’s attack goes quiet

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Kansas City booked its place in the NWSL Championship with a 2-1 semifinal win over Gotham, capitalizing on two set pieces while the Shield winners struggled to generate an attacking threat.

The Kansas City Current took down Gotham FC 2-1 on the road in the weekend’s second semifinal. Much like both teams’ quarterfinals last week, this was another slow burn, with only a few flashes of attacking firepower from either side.

Let’s review three key takeaways from the game.

Before we break down anything that happened over the 90 minutes, we have to talk about the circumstances leading up to this game. Ahead of the match, Gotham announced that the semifinal would be played at Icahn Stadium in Manhattan instead of Sports Illustrated Stadium, the club’s usual home. The reason? The field at Sports Illustrated was deemed unfit.

There’s a lot to unpack here, but it all boils down to one thing: this is unacceptable for a league of the NWSL’s caliber.
