Looking for a MacBook and device charger that’s as stylish as it is capable? Both Amazon and Sharge are selling the Pixel 140W GaN charger for $69.88 and $69.90, respectively, a $24 discount on its usual price. If you’re not in need of that much wattage but like the style, Sharge is also selling the 67W version for $19.90 ($30 off) or the 100W version for $59.90 ($16 off).

The Pixel 140 delivers up to 140W of power when using only one of its three USB-C ports, which Sharge says is capable of taking a 16-inch MacBook Pro from a flat battery to 50 percent charge in just 25 minutes. It’s 47 percent smaller than the MacBook’s included charging brick as well, which is great if you’re tight on bag space or travel frequently. The pixel display on the side has configurable brightness and orientation, and displays your current charging wattage.
