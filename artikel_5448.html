U.S. retail sales edged up 0.2% in October, slowing ahead of the holidays

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The gain followed a 0.8% rise in September, and sales at motor vehicle and parts dealers fell 1.6% from the prior month

The Census Bureau reported on Tuesday that U.S. retail and food services sales rose 0.2% in October to $748.2 billion, a smaller gain than September's 0.8%.

The agency put the margin of error on the monthly change at plus or minus 0.4%. Its 90% confidence interval includes zero, so the data do not provide enough evidence to conclude the actual change differs from zero. Sales were up 6.8% from October 2025. The estimates are adjusted for seasonal variation and holiday and trading-day differences but not for price changes.

Motor vehicle and parts dealers posted a 1.6% decline from September. Excluding that category, total sales rose 0.6%. Gasoline stations gained 1.5%, nonstore retailers 1.2% and health and personal care stores 1.5%. Furniture and home furnishings stores fell 2.3%, and food services and drinking places rose 0.2%.

Sales at gasoline stations were up 30.3% from a year earlier.

The 0.2% gain matched what economists had forecast, according to Reuters. Households spent less on cars, trucks and other expensive purchases as prices rose, with the U.S.-Israeli war with Iran pushing inflation higher.

Retail sales in a narrower group that leaves out food services, building materials, gasoline and automobiles climbed 0.6% in October, according to Reuters. September's gain in that measure was revised up to 0.5% from 0.1%. Economists had forecast a 0.4% October increase.

The CNBC/NRF Retail Monitor, which draws on card spending data, showed U.S. retail sales rising 0.2% in October, the slowest monthly gain since November 2025. The National Retail Federation said Halloween sales and early holiday discounts prevented a weaker reading.

September's 0.8% gain was driven largely by receipts at gasoline stations. The Census Bureau's initial estimate put September sales at $745.0 billion. That figure has been revised to $746.6 billion.

The Census Bureau will release November retail sales data on Dec. 16.
