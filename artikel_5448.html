Hosting Changes How People See Their Home Designs: Survey

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Group of friends celebrating at home having dinner

Hosting for the holidays can be chaos—you have to think about decorating, cooking, baking and more. But, there’s another wrench in the planning that people may forget to consider: the setup of your home. Do you have enough space? Enough parking? Enough bathrooms?

A new survey found that more and more homebuyers are taking hosting into consideration when making decisions about the design of their homes.

Homebuilder KB Home’s survey—featuring input from 2,000 U.S. adults and conducted via Talker Research—found that 58% of respondents feel that hosting has made them rethink what they want in their current or future home. While 29% of respondents said that having people over makes them feel proud of their home, 20% report that it makes them feel self-conscious.

“Hosting friends and family can highlight what people love about their homes and what they wish worked better,” said Gena Kirk, vice president of corporate design for KB Home.

As for what people are looking for, the survey reported that 35% said an open living area is the most important feature for making a home comfortable for hosting friends and family during holidays and special occasions, followed by outdoor entertaining space (30%). Other wants include a spacious kitchen (24%), an open kitchen (21%) and a large or dedicated dining area (21%).

Kirk recommended that buyers pursue new homes in order to achieve total customization.

“From spacious living areas and open floor plans to finishes that reflect personal style, a KB home works for everyday life as well as special occasions,” she said. “Our Built-to-Order approach recognizes that no two households are alike, giving buyers the flexibility to make choices that fit their lifestyle and budget and create a home that is uniquely their own.”

Hosting also inspires new priorities, as 21% of respondents said that hosting makes them think about how to make their home more welcoming or comfortable, while 25% said it makes them want to declutter or clean more often. In addition, 24% said that when they host, they notice things about their home they would want to change.
