Target's Cyber Monday Sale Helps Families Save Big on Thousands of Must-Have Gifts

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

MINNEAPOLIS, Nov. 27, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Target Corporation (NYSE: TGT) today announced its Cyber Monday sale on Target.com and the Target app, offering up to 50% off thousands of gifts and holiday essentials. Starting Monday, Nov. 30, the sales event combines major savings with new digital and AI-powered shopping tools and an expanded online assortment of Target Plus brands to shop, giving guests more ways to discover great gifts at an incredible value.

"Cyber Monday is an exciting holiday tradition for our guests because it's a moment to discover incredible deals on gifts they can't wait to give," said Sarah Travis, executive vice president and chief digital and revenue officer, Target. "That's why we've raised our game with even more products and brands that are on sale at irresistible prices, bringing together the style, design and value that set Target apart."
