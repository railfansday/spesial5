Nick Cannon Pulls Back the Curtain on the Drama of Raising 12 Kids Across Multiple Homes in Netflix’s ‘Raising the Cannons’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Nick Cannon is lifting the lid on what it takes to raise a family across multiple homes—and his kids have a few things to say about it, too.

In “Raising the Cannons,” premiering on Dec. 18 on Netflix, the father of 12 gives viewers a glimpse of home life with his blended family.

Among those offering their own take are Moroccan and Monroe, the 15-year-old twins he shares with ex-wife Mariah Carey, who have some candid thoughts about their dad’s choices.

For a family spread across several households, the Cannons live surprisingly close together. According to Netflix’s Tudum, the six mothers of his children—Carey, “Selling Sunset” star Bre Tiesi, Abby De La Rosa, Alyssa Scott, Brittany Bell, and LaNisha Cole—all live within a 5-mile radius of one another.

That proximity comes in handy for school pickups. For Cannon, it also makes something more personal possible. He wants the time his children spend with him to give them a chance to grow up together, even when they go home to different houses.

“I want my children to have relationships with one another,” he says in the trailer.

Cannon credits the mothers with helping him bring the kids together. De La Rosa, who shares twins Zion and Zillion and daughter Beautiful Zeppelin with him, wants that for her children, too.

The radio personality and DJ is also open to getting to know the other mothers better.

She and Cannon first met through morning radio, where they bonded over music and entertainment. As parents, Cannon says, they’ve found they also share many of the same values about how they want to raise their three children.

Cole offers a different perspective in the trailer. The photographer, who shares daughter Onyx with Cannon, says she feels little need to present the entire group as one big, happy family.

Cannon tells Tudum it took him time to find his footing with Cole after their romantic relationship ended in 2022. When she moved on with a new partner, he says, he had to put his ego aside, respect her boundaries, and focus on what worked best for Onyx.

For “Selling Sunset” fans, one of the bigger surprises may be what Tiesi gets up to in her own home.

Viewers know the luxury real estate agent for showing palatial properties. Cannon, who shares son Legendary Love with her, knows her as someone who’s just as happy taking on a project around the house.

He tells Tudum that “at home she’s always decorating and baking—DIY projects all around the house, building chicken coops.”

The series also follows Cannon as he balances fatherhood and work while managing lupus nephritis, a condition that affects the kidneys. The trailer shows him in a hospital bed as a voice expresses concern about the toll stress is taking on him.

Elsewhere in the preview, Moroccan questions whether his father can sustain the way he’s living.

Moroccan and his sister were just 5 when Cannon and Carey divorced in 2016. The twins are now in high school, and Cannon says he and Carey still speak regularly about parenting and what comes next for their children. Of all his co-parenting relationships, he points to theirs as the model he hopes to follow with his younger kids.

These days, the twins have advice of their own for their father, particularly when it comes to his love life.

“They’re the parents, I’m the kid,” Cannon jokes to Tudum.

Monroe gives viewers a sense of just how frank those conversations can be. In one exchange in the trailer, she tells her father that if he hadn’t gone on to have more children, she thinks he and her mother would have found their way back to each other.
