Bitcoin: It’s So Over

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

How does today's Bitcoin bear market compare with the ones before it?

How does today’s Bitcoin bear market compare with the ones before it? Sean Hagan and Grace Remington walk through the 2018 crash, with Bitcoin falling to about $3,000 as fear and greed sat at 11, and the 2021 to 2022 unwind driven by tighter policy and a string of failures. They note that the current drawdown is about 54% from the top, which they describe as not nearly as bad as previous cycles. They also point out that this cycle has been a continuous sell-down with no recovery bounce.
