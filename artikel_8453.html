The Best Black Friday Hiking and Backpacking Gear Deals of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

If you like paying full price, you’re on the wrong post. But if you want to know which hiking and backpacking gear deals are most worth your attention and holiday spend this year, these are our favorite 2026 Black Friday deals — from cottage quilts and trekking pole shelters to packs, base layers, and bear-proof food storage.

Several sales are already live, and many more start between now and Thanksgiving weekend. Upcoming sales are labeled with their start dates, and we’ll update this list as new offers launch.

Jump to: Our Top Picks · Deal Alerts · All Black Friday Deals

Enlightened Equipment’s November sale is live, with 20% off storewide (exclusions apply, per EE’s site) plus free US shipping. Custom orders are also 20% off while supplies last. The Revelation is the obvious pick here. It’s the quilt that put EE on the map with long-distance hikers, and it unzips flat as a blanket on warm nights or cinches into a closed footbox when temperatures drop.

Pick: Revelation, 850-fill down, 20°F, Regular length / Regular width. Regular price: $365. Expected sale price: $292. Price changes with size, fabric, and fill options.

Dates: Live now through December 1. Code: None.

Gossamer Gear’s sale is live now through November 30, with 35% off packs, 20% off trekking poles and accessories, and 15% off shelters. Discounts apply automatically at checkout, and shipping is also free on all orders Black Friday through Cyber Monday (November 27–30). The Mariposa 60 is Gossamer Gear’s high-capacity ultralight pack, with a removable frame and hip belt and seven exterior pockets, and it has long been a favorite of long-distance hikers.

Pick: Mariposa 60. Regular price: $315. Expected sale price: $204.75.

Also worth a look: Deals on shelters and trekking poles mean you can get 15% off The Free, GG’s new freestanding tent, and 20% off the ever-popular he LT5 ultralight trekking poles.

Dates: Live now through November 30. Code: None (discounts apply automatically).

Hyperlite Mountain Gear’s Black Friday sale kicks off November 24. Stay tuned: we’ll share the details as soon as it goes live. Hyperlite makes Dyneema packs and shelters built for long trails, including the Unbound 2P trekking pole tent. Even if you’re not familiar with the brand, you’ve probably seen its distinctive monochrome packs if you’ve spent much time out on the trails.

Katabatic Gear’s Black Friday sale starts November 23. Check back here next Monday: we’ll share the details the moment it goes live. If you’re in the market for a premium quilt or puffy, this is a deal to keep an eye on. The Colorado brand is beloved by long-distance hikers for its high-quality ultralight sleep systems and backpacking insulation.

Osprey’s Black Friday sale is already live, with up to 40% off hundreds of select products and free shipping. This is an “up to” sale, so discounts vary by model, color, and size.

Our pick is the women’s Eja 48, which thru-hiker extraordinaire Lisa Slutsken reviewed for The Trek after carrying it on her Colorado Trail thru-hike. Its trampoline-style back panel reduces back sweat, the torso adjusts by 4 inches, and zippered hipbelt pockets are back. “Osprey makes some of the most comfortable women’s packs on the market,” she wrote. “The shoulder straps sit just right, the pack doesn’t feel too wide on my back, and now, with the adjustable torso length, I can really dial in the fit.” The trade-offs: at about 2.7 pounds, it’s heavier than many ultralight packs, the pockets have little stretch, and she found the hipbelt padding thin over a long hike. The men’s version is the Exos 48.

Pick: Eja 48. Regular price: $265. Sale price: $194.95 on Osprey’s site when we checked on November 17 (Deep Teal; colors and sizes vary).

Dates: Live now through November 30. Code: None.

Every Six Moon Designs product is 25% off through November 30 with code YULEHIKE. Our recommendation is the Lunar Solo. There’s a reason this tent has been a fixture of America’s long trails for about two decades. It’s a 26-ounce single-person shelter that pitches with one trekking pole and has lots of headroom for its weight. Seam sealing costs extra if you don’t want to do it yourself, and if you don’t hike with poles you’ll need to buy a separate pole.

Pick: Lunar Solo. Regular price: $260. Expected sale price with code: $195.

Also worth a look: The Silver Shadow Carbon trekking umbrella falls under the same sitewide discount.

Dates: Live now through November 30. Code: YULEHIKE

Dates: Live now through November 29. Code: saveszn

Zenbivy’s Black Friday sale starts November 23. Details aren’t public yet, but we’ll share ’em next Monday when the sale goes live. Zenbivy’s unique quilt-and-sheet sleep systems are built to sleep more like a bed at home than a mummy bag. We’ve been watching this brand rise in prominence over the last few years with great interest.
