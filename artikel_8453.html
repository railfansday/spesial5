Upthread I had some feedback related to beginning a transition of MYGA's to Fidelity - for me mainly for what I perceived as convenience to have MYGA's be in one place and display on my dashboard. I have a total of 7 MYGA's (2 qualified and 5 unqualified).
I have an American Equity MYGA reaching guarantee period end 12/16/26.
I am a client of BluePrint Income.

I've decided leave well enough alone - instead of transitioning to Fidelity as a Gainbridge MYGA and a Canvas MYGA ends 01/2026 just stick with BluePrint and do appropriate 1035 Exchanges when the time comes. For me BluePrint is easy accessible, efficient, great offerings, and I like the easy to follow and see the details of each offering. Good help when I need it. I feel a personal touch when I phone BluePrint - they know who I am.

I was reminded yesterday why for ME a good idea to stick with BluePrint.
I phoned BluePrint -Wednesday, 11/11 phoned in late afternoon to request a 1035 Exchange for the American Equity MYGA to an Athene (A+ rated), 5.50% - 3 yr. MYGA. Asked to forward a copy of my American Equity annual statement.
Thursday a.m. 11/12 received request to review and sign the application - along with a link and instruction to securely access and sign the application and transfer form.
Thursday afternoon notice received that my application submitted to Athene.
I have no idea how Fidelity manages 1035 exchanges, my guess just fine, but couldn't ask for better service from BluePrint - including because I'm an account holder all the identifying details were filled out - all I had to do was review, sign, and submit. And a reminder - We recommend you follow up consistently with your current financial institution until your funds are sent. We also recommend that you ask your current financial institution to send the funds electronically or express with a tracking number.

Sharing the above info. mainly for readers new to purchasing or doing 1035 exchanges - hope it's helpful.
