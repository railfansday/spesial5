After a Year of Washes, These Towels Are as Plush and Absorbent as the Day I Got Them

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Utah-based bedding, bath, and loungewear brand Cozy Earth has built a $100 million business around bamboo. The brand is perhaps most loved for its luxe sheets, but we’ve also tested (and generally loved) its pajamas, blankets, and cotton-and-bamboo towels. In fact, most of our shopping team owns at least one Cozy Earth product, regarding them as “ultrasoft pieces that will reign forever in our linen closets,” as senior shopping editor Nashia Baker puts it.

The brand’s bamboo sheets have taken best overall in both our bamboo and cooling sheets roundups. Its towels have earned their own spots in our guides on the most absorbent bath towels, the best quick-dry towels, and the best waffle towel sets. Contributor Megan Wahn was sold on the waffle bath sheets after dunking a corner in a bowl of water: “I could literally see as the parts of the towel that were not submerged soaked up the water,” she says.

Towel marketing tends to promise softness and absorbency in the same breath, but in my experience, it’s rare that both are true. Cozy Earth’s Luxe Bath Towels are the exception. After a full calendar year of countless showers, followed by washing in New York City laundromats, where the dryers have one setting (and it’s “inferno”) they are still exceptional towels.

The feel was memorable from the start: plush and not the least bit scratchy. At 600 grams per square meter (GSM), a unit of measure for a towel’s weight, it also has real heft. The towel’s blend of cotton and bamboo viscose, woven using what the brand calls zero-twist technology, leaves no fabric loops for your hand to catch on. But despite lacking the shag-carpet texture of some fluffy towels, this soft, smooth Cozy Earth model remains absorbent enough to dry you with ease.

A year later the most impressive quality of these towels is how little they’ve changed. They’ve been through more showers than I can count and a year of intense washes (the care label calls for tumble drying on low, an option my laundromat doesn’t offer), and they’re still as soft to the touch as when I first got them. Plenty of cotton towels demonstrate their best absorbency in their first months, and then never again. The Cozy Earth Luxe towels still dry me off as well as they did on day one, which is something I can’t say for most towels I’ve owned (and I’ve tested a lot of them). In my experience, plush towels go stiff and absorbent ones go thin, but these have done neither.

The one concession to the laundromat is color. My off-white pair of towels has picked up a faint blue-gray tint over the year, though they still pass for white. For this I blame the machines more than the towels, which deserve better care than I can give them….

At $118 (currently $88.50 for Black Friday), I won’t pretend the Luxe Bath Towels set isn’t an investment. You can buy a 12-pack for the same price on Amazon. But these are my favorite bath towels due to their durability as much as their feel—a luxe combination that’s hard to replicate on a tight budget. Towels that stay soft and absorbent for a month are easy to find; it’s much rarer for options to hold their quality for a year and beyond. If you’re replacing a full bath-towel set, I recommend starting here instead of cycling through cheaper towels that end up costing you the same once you’ve replaced them a few times.

To outfit the whole bathroom, Cozy Earth sells the Complete Luxe Bath Bundle. The hand towels and washcloths are just as good as the towels.
