Bolton Wanderers fans will love what James Trafford said after England heroics v Spain

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

England became the first team to beat Spain since March 2024 on Sunday night, topping Group C of the Nations League, due in no small part to Leeds United goalkeeper James Trafford. But even that double save at the end doesn't top one effort while at Bolton Wanderers.

The Three Lions weathered quite the storm in the final half hour, despite cruising at 3-0 thanks to goals from Harry Kane, Esri Konsa and Ben White.

A lapse in concentration let Lamine Yamal nick the ball and race away, and a second error seconds later saw Elliot Anderson drag the Spaniard down in the box, giving away a penalty and earning a straight red.

But despite Spain halving the deficit after converting the spot kick, England saw out the win, with Trafford proving to be the hero between the sticks. He made six saves, including a brilliant double stop at the end to deny Yamal and then Aymeric Laporte.

An incredible moment, and one that, for 99% of goalkeepers, would be the pinnacle of their careers thus far. Not for Trafford, though.

Trafford first made a name for himself as one of England's most exciting young goalkeepers while on loan at Bolton from Manchester City. He spent the second half of the 2021/22 campaign at the Toughsheet Stadium, but really broke through the following year.

The 24-year-old kept 22 clean sheets for the Trotters en route to the play-offs, making plenty of big saves to help what was then Ian Evatt's side save points, while also failing to concede in any of his four EFL Trophy matches as Bolton won at Wembley against Plymouth Argyle.

His second clean sheet back in a Wanderers shirt came at Port Vale, and despite it being over four years ago, he hasn't seemed to forget it.

Speaking to ITV after the win over Spain, Trafford was asked if that double save to deny Yamal and Laporte was "as good as he's made," and after thinking about it, the 24-year-old had to disagree.

"The moment's a brilliant moment," he said. "As in saves, I made an incredible one away at Port Vale when I was at Bolton, and that will forever be my best save. But for the moment, what it meant, yeah, it's up there."

The game ended 0-0 at Vale Park, and you'll be hard-pressed to find a fan of either side who remembers much about what happened, but that one moment will live in the goalkeeper's head for the rest of his career.

It's the old moniker, 'never fall in love with a loan player,' but it seems that the love is still mutual between Trafford and Bolton, even though his departure was over three years ago now.

As seen above, it's quite a brilliant save, and while Trafford and the Bolton fans had some brilliant memories after that third game of the campaign at Port Vale, those in the stands at the Toughsheet will love their mention after one of the best performances of the 24-year-old's life.

"I made an incredible [save] away at Port Vale when I was at Bolton" Our boy 🤍 we love you," one Bolton fan commented under the interview online. "Our boy james Trafford, loves Bolton and Bolton love him !!" was also among the comments, as was "He loves bolton . He always does."

"Makes a world-class double save to help England beat the world champions, but still puts another above it - Bolton away at Port Vale in League One. Never change!" a Bolton fan account tweeted.

"Hahahaha I love him England vs Spain? Yeah big moment, but fav save? For Bolton vs Port Vale 😂," was also commented. Trafford's matter-of-fact and somewhat unconventional interview style does lend well to some fans, especially those at Bolton, who will have immense pride towards their former loanee keeper.

Trafford is one of the prime examples of how EFL loans can help shape a player and ready them for big things in their career. The fact that the 24-year-old was playing in League One just four seasons ago is quite something.

Now, the Leeds number one is arguably one of the best goalkeepers in the Premier League, and after that performance against Spain, the number one elect for England, according to some fans.
