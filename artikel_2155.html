No surprise that Mikel Arteta was asked about Bruno Guimaraes.

The Arsenal boss speaking at his pre-match press conference on Friday.

A 5.30pm kick-off at St James’ Park on Saturday awaits.

Bruno Guimaraes potentially stepping back onto the St James’ Park pitch for the first time in over six months.

The 17 May 2026 proved to be the last time the Brazil international would play at home for Newcastle United, a 3-1 win against West Ham which effectively relegated the Hammers.

Brazil have been in Singapore, playing two friendlies against Japan and Singapore.

Mikel Arteta asked about the potential availability of Bruno Guimaraes for Saturday: “Both Gabriel and him came back [without picking up an injury and available], the same as the other internationals, apart from Martin Zubimendi. They are in good condition. We have another training session this [Friday] afternoon to make sure we nail the preparation because we know the game to expect tomorrow, how difficult it is to play against Newcastle United with a new manager, new ideas, new philosophy and Bruno knows them really well. We are ready for that.”

Mikel Arteta asked whether he expects Bruno Guimaraes will get a good reception from the Newcastle United fans at St James’ Park: “I hope so. I think that he was one of the main players there and I think he came to the club in a really important part, when they had a massive lift in performance and what they achieved together was impressive.”
