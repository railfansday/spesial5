Blackstone will buy a 15.57% stake in Indian lender IIFL Finance for about $532 million

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The investment is expected to lift IIFL's capital adequacy ratio to about 30% from roughly 17% on a pro forma basis as of September

Blackstone is set to buy 15.57% of Indian shadow lender IIFL Finance in a deal worth roughly 50 billion rupees ($532 million), according to Bloomberg.

The investment will be routed via Singapore-based Ingot SG Pte. Ltd., which is set to take roughly 83.1 million preference shares that are compulsorily convertible, priced at 602 rupees each, according to Bloomberg. Every preference share will convert into IIFL Finance equity at a one-for-one ratio.

IIFL Finance stock ended Monday's session at 627 rupees, roughly 4% over Blackstone's entry price. The stock is up roughly 3% so far in the current year.

Under the fundraising, IIFL founder Nirmal Jain will take up 15 million warrants valued at 9.03 billion rupees, according to Bloomberg. Co-founder R. Venkataraman will take up 4.5 million warrants, valued at 2.71 billion rupees. Ardent Impex Private Ltd., an entity of the founder group, will take 1.5 million warrants valued at 903 million rupees.

Jain and Mukesh Mehta, chief investment officer of Blackstone India Private Equity, said in a statement the deal will shore up the lender's finances and back its growth, according to Bloomberg. The expansion includes technology spending as well as co-lending tie-ups with banks. Blackstone said it intends to partner with IIFL's management team as the lender keeps expanding.

On a pro forma basis as of September, the capital infusion is expected to raise IIFL Finance's capital adequacy ratio from roughly 17% to about 30%, according to Bloomberg.

Bloomberg News reported back in September that Blackstone was one of several international investors weighing a stake in IIFL Finance. Fairfax Financial Holdings bought a 60.72% holding in IDBI Bank in October, paying 528.78 billion rupees, according to Bloomberg; as of the end of September, it owned about 13.6% of IIFL Finance.

Blackstone committed roughly 62 billion rupees last year in return for a stake of up to 9.99% in Federal Bank, according to Bloomberg. The firm also controls mortgage lender Aadhar Housing Finance.

IIFL Finance lends against gold and provides home loans, business loans, property-backed financing and credit for small and medium-sized enterprises. The lender runs upward of 4,900 branches spanning more than 500 Indian cities and manages roughly 1.15 trillion rupees in assets, according to Bloomberg.
