Arlo Delivers Personalization to Front Doors Everywhere with New Video Doorbells Available in Four Colorways

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Designed to Complement a Range of Exterior Aesthetics, Battery-Powered and Hardwired Solutions Offer Flexible Power and Continuous Recording Options for Smarter Home Protection

CARLSBAD, Calif., Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Arlo Technologies, Inc. (NYSE: ARLO), a leading provider of smart home security services, today announced two new third-generation video doorbells with advanced features to protect what matters most. The Arlo Video Doorbell (3rd Gen) (Battery-Powered) and Arlo Wired Video Doorbell (3rd Gen) (Hardwired) provide smarter home protection from anywhere with real-time visibility, intelligent alerts, and fast ways to respond to what is happening outside. Available now for pre-order for $79.99, both the battery-powered and hardwired solution come in four versatile colorways (White, Black, Greystone and Sand Dune) giving users more options to complement their home's exterior design aesthetic.

"Consumers want more from their front-door security than simply knowing when motion is detected," said Matthew McRae, CEO of Arlo Technologies. "Our latest generation video doorbells not only allow users to see clearly and respond instantly, but the Early Warning System's advanced intelligence goes beyond basic alerts to provide greater context and identify risks before they escalate. Threat Assessment goes a step further to decipher what deserves attention and what doesn't, and automatically enable dispatched help for critical threats faster than ever before."

Flexible Power Options for All HouseholdsArlo's new video doorbells provide consumers a choice of power solutions while delivering a more complete view of what's happening outside their home. The Video Doorbell (3rd Gen) (Battery-Powered) offers flexible installation with a rechargeable battery and an optional trickle-charge connection with in-home chime support. The Wired Video Doorbell (3rd Gen) (Hardwired) delivers always-on power through an existing doorbell connection, enabling Arlo Foresight to capture the seconds leading up to the moment motion was detected, and the option for 24/7 Continuous Video Recording (CVR) for always-on front-door visibility (with an Ultra Secure Plan).

Reliable Performance, Built to Protect What Matters MostArlo third-generation video doorbells deliver smarter front-door protection with premium materials and an array of advanced features.

Stay Ahead of Potential Threats with Arlo Secure Early Warning SystemThe Arlo Secure App allows users to stay connected to their family, pets and home from anywhere. With an Arlo Secure Early Warning System plan, they can understand more, make faster decisions, and respond with confidence. AI-powered features such as Person, Animal, Vehicle & Package Detection, Fire Detection, Event Captions, Arlo Summary and Threat Assessment provide additional context around detected activity, helping users distinguish between everyday events and emergency situations that may require action. Three Arlo Secure Early Warning System plans are available: Smart Aware, Smart Secure, and Ultra Secure.

Pricing & AvailabilityPre-orders for the Arlo Video Doorbell (3rd Gen) (Battery-Powered) and Arlo Wired Video Doorbell (3rd Gen) (Hardwired) are open now for $79.99 in four colorways, with cameras shipping in late November.

For more information on the full range of Arlo smart home security products and a comprehensive list of Arlo Secure Early Warning System plans and pricing, visit www.arlo.com.

About Arlo Technologies, Inc.Arlo is an award-winning, industry leader that is transforming the ways in which people can protect everything that matters to them with advanced home, business, and personal security solutions. Arlo's deep expertise in AI- and CV-powered analytics, cloud services, user experience and product design, and innovative wireless and RF connectivity enables the delivery of a seamless, smart security experience for Arlo users that is easy to set up and interact with every day. Arlo's cloud-based platform provides users with visibility, insight, and a powerful means to help protect and connect in real-time with the people and things that matter most, from any location with a Wi-Fi or a cellular connection.
