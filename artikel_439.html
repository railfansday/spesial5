Amazons early Black Friday deal on this Osprey travel backpack drops it to a new record-low price — save 35%

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

SAVE $38.50: The Osprey Daylite Expandable Travel Pack 26+6 is on sale for just $71.50 on Amazon as of Nov. 13. That's a 35% discount on its $110 MSRP and its lowest price ever.

Heads up, holiday travelers: Amazon just dropped an early Black Friday deal on a top-rated travel backpack. And according to our research, it's never been cheaper before.

Normally $110, Osprey's Daylite Expandable Travel Pack 26+6 is now on sale for just $71.50 — a 35% savings. Amazon has it labeled as an official "Early Black Friday Deal," so it's highly unlikely to see any further discounts as we head deeper into the holiday season. (Amazon's Black Friday Week sale technically starts on Nov. 19.)

The Daylite Expandable Travel Pack 26+6 has a huge main pocket with a wrap-around zipper that lets it open wide like a piece of luggage. It has a 26-liter capacity by default, but it can expand by two inches for six extra liters of packing room (hence the numbers at the end of its name). There are two zippered pockets on the front of it and two water bottle sleeves on its sides.

Of note, Amazon customers who have purchased the Daylite Expandable Travel Pack 26+6 have rated it highly for its compact yet roomy design and lightweight but durable construction.

Amazon's offer only applies to its exclusive "tunnel vision grey" colorway, by the way. A couple other variants of the Daylite Expandable Travel Pack were also on sale at the time of writing, but none were nearly as cheap.
