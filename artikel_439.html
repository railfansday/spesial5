Google just dropped its Holiday 100, the years most-searched gifts. See our favorite 15 gift ideas.

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Need some holiday gift ideas? Consider taking some inspiration from your fellow cybersurfers. Today, Google unveiled the 2026 edition of the Holiday 100, its annual gift guide featuring the most-searched products of the year.

According to a company blog post, this year's Holiday 100 picks range from "viral sensations that broke the internet," like squishy toys and red light masks, "to nostalgic styles making a massive comeback" (think turntables and roller skates). Content essentials like podcast mics and portable ring lights have also gained ground as the creator economy continues to grow at a rapid pace.

"With over a billion shopping searches across Google every day, we get a front-row seat to what’s actually topping everyone’s wish lists," Google said.
