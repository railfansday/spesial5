Pokémon TCG Delta Reign Booster Bundles get a price cut on launch day — shop for market price at Amazon

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Delta Reign Booster Bundle is on sale for $57.95 at Amazon, down from its $61.45 list price.

The wait for the Pokémon TCG's Delta Reign expansion is finally over, and Amazon has marked the Nov. 6 launch with another discount on its six-pack Booster Bundle.

As of Nov. 6, the Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Delta Reign Booster Bundle is priced at just $57.95 at Amazon, shaving $3.50 off its regular $61.45 price. Sure, it’s a relatively modest 6% saving in the grand scheme of things, but this means you'll be spending about $9.66 per pack.

While this deal is supplied by third-party seller Toynado Alley rather than Amazon, it still has 100% positive feedback. Also keep in mind that delivery is estimated for Nov. 14, rather than dispatching today or arriving over the weekend.

This new low price is only $5.31 above the Delta Reign Booster Bundle’s estimated $52.94 market price on TCGplayer. What’s more, this particular box has been steadily climbing in market price over the last week, and we expect that to go up even more during this launch period — so get yours while stocks last at this price point.

For $57.95, the Booster Bundle offers 60 trading cards spread across six packs, each accompanied by a Basic Energy and Pokémon TCG Live code card. Delta Reign offers over 135 cards to pull, including Mega Rayquaza ex, Mega Malamar ex, Mega Golurk ex, and Mega Golisopod ex.
