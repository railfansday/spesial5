McDonald's Moves on Makeover; Dick's Sporting Goods Up on Earnings | Stock Movers

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

On this episode of Stock Movers: - Anthropic (ANT) shares are lower and briefly traded below their $83 initial public offering price in premarket trading, wiping out gains for investors who piled into the artificial intelligence lab’s record-setting debut. - McDonald's (MCD) shares are moving on news the company's betting big on a makeover to win back customers. The chain is tearing down kitchen walls, adding hand-breaded chicken and trendy drinks, plus testing AI to take drive-thru orders. However, the nearly $800-thousand price tag per restaurant has franchisees pushing back and convincing them of the overhaul is becoming a big challenge for the fast food giant. - Dick's Sporting Goods (DKS) shares are climbing after the retailer’s third-quarter results easily topped Street expectations. Management also boosted its adjusted EPS forecast for the full year, as well as the low end of its Dick’s stores comparable sales target. (Source: Bloomberg)
