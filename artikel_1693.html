Gallery: ‘No room for failure’, ‘Amorim facing a crisis’ – Today’s front pages in Italy

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

AC Milan’s Europa League campaign resumes this evening in Athens against Olympiacos, with a win desperately needed.

Milan have won three Serie A games on the spin, but Europe has been their kryptonite. Ruben Amorim’s side have lost three of four so far, failing to score in those three defeats leaving them outside even the play-off places.

By contrast, they face an Olympiacos side who have won three of four to plant themselves firmly in the top eight. The last time this fixture was played resulted in a 3-1 win for the Greek side to knock the Rossoneri out in 2018-19, and the feeling is a defeat could again be fatal.

Each of the sports newspapers in Italy this morning reference just how big a night this is for Milan if they are to avoid being further embarrassed in Europe.

La Gazzetta dello Sport: “Milan in Athens: final call. Amorim, fielding his first-choice side, looks to Ramos for goals.”

Corriere dello Sport: “Milan at a crossroads: final call. They need a breakthrough in Greece to move up from their 28th-place position and three defeats in four matches. But Amorim is facing a crisis: nine players are unavailable.”

Tuttosport: “Jashari in the cauldron. A Greek test. The Swiss player, operating as the regista in place of Modrić, has yet to fully convince. Without a win, even the play-offs are at risk.”

QS: “Milan, no room for failure away to Olympiacos.”
