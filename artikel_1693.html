Opinion: Julian Ward has no other choice, Everton star must now join Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

An Everton star simply has to join Liverpool. Julian Ward has no choice but to buy him.

It's going to be a challenging period for the club's new sporting director. Ward will have known that when he accepted the job.

Liverpool's squad is all over the place and lacks the necessary balance to make things work if the Reds want to be competing at the very highest level.

But this was always to be expected. Liverpool are going through changes. There's a new generation coming through and an older generation heading out through the gates of the AXA centre.

It takes time to go through that metamorphosis and grow together as a team. Especially, when there's a new manager in charge of the club as well.
