Colby Donovan concern as Celtic rising star withdraws from Scotland duty

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Colby Donovan has been ruled out of Scotland Under-21 duty after becoming a trusted figure under Martin O’Neill at Celtic.

The young defender has been a regular during Alistair Johnston’s absence and played the full 90 minutes of Sunday’s 1-0 Premiership win over Kilmarnock.

Donovan has earned plenty of affection from O’Neill during his breakthrough campaign, but the 20-year-old will now sit out Scotland Under-21s’ upcoming friendlies against Ukraine and the USA.

Tony Docherty’s squad will play both games at Pinatar Arena in Spain, facing Ukraine on Thursday before taking on the USA three days later on Sunday, November 15.

Calum Adamson, currently on loan at Raith Rovers from Rangers, has also withdrawn, with Sam Chambers and Sebastian Lochhead drafted into the squad.

Donovan’s absence comes amid an impressive season at Celtic in which he has already made 21 appearances in all competitions, featuring in their Champions League qualifiers and Europa League campaign.

His emergence had previously earned him recognition at senior international level, with Sebastien Pocognoli including him in his first Scotland squad.

Donovan did not feature in any of the opening four Nations League matches, however, and was not included in the senior group for the double-header against North Macedonia and Switzerland during this international break.

Meanwhile, Switzerland captain Granit Xhaka has also withdrawn from international duty, including the Nations League showdown with Scotland.

The 34-year-old suffered a calf problem in the warm-up before Sunderland’s Premier League clash with Chelsea on Sunday, although he was still able to start the game.
