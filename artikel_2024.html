Swansea City, Burnley show interest in 12-goal star ahead of potential January swoop

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Swansea City and Burnley are in contrasting positions in the Championship table, with the Swans still inside the play-offs and the Clarets stuck in the relegation zone. While both look to rediscover form on the pitch though, work is going on to identify potential targets for the January transfer window.

Now, a report from Football League World has claimed that the Championship pair are both interested in Huddersfield Town hotshot Joe Taylor. It is said that they are ‘weighing up’ moves for the 24-year-old striker as the next window approaches.

Taylor has been in excellent form this season with 12 goals and three assists in 18 games across all competitions. He is currently under contract until the summer of 2029; that and his form means it will take a handsome fee to prise him away from the Terriers this winter.

Despite his age and the fact he has moved around clubs a fair amount, Taylor is an established name in the EFL with 143 appearances to his bame across the Championship, League One and League Two.

Peterborough United signed him from non-league side Kings Lynn Town, and Luton Town bought him from there. Taylor impressed in loans with Colchester United (27 games, 12 goals, two assists) and Lincoln City (19 games, 10 goals, three assists) but failed to score in 19 outings for the Hatters.

Strong loan form prompted Huddersfield Town to snap him up, and Taylor’s Terriers career has come to life after another successful temporary move, this time to Wigan Athletic (22 games, 11 goals). The Welshman has proven to be a prolific scorer in League One and he is starring at the level once again, scoring for fun in Martin Drury’s attack alongside fellow star Derensili Sanches Fernandes.

Given his success in League One, it feels inevitable that Taylor gets another shot in the Championship. Swansea City and Burnley could offer him such a chance, but they will have to pay a good fee for his services.

Of the two interested clubs, Swansea City are no doubt the more attractive option at the moment. Despite the fact they are currently managerless, the Swans are in the hunt for a Championship play-off finish, and with the help of Jamestown Analytics, they are capable of making some long-awaited strides forward.

But, for Taylor, a move to Burnley may be better. Swansea have all of Zan Vipotnik, Adam Idah and Ross Stewart on the books for their one-striker system, so they do not need another.

As for the Clarets, they have plenty of strikers, but none are impressing. He would quickly jump up the pecking order and into contention for a starting spot against Jamie Vardy, Andreas Hountondji and Zeki Amdouni, and on current form, he’d be backed to beat them.

Alternatively though, a stay at Huddersfield Town could be best. The Terriers will have little interest in selling someone key to their promotion push, and he could end up in the Championship in their colours sooner rather than later.
