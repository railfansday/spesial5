Anis Mehmeti's Ipswich Town situation hasn't changed - Derby County, Birmingham City will watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

As the calendar year comes to an end, murmurs are starting to swirl around which fringe Premier League players may find themselves in the Championship during the January transfer window, and Ipswich Town's Anis Mehmeti has to be one of them.

The Albanian international was on the move last January, signing for the Tractor Boys midway through the campaign for a reported fee of just £3 million, with his contract at Bristol City in its final six months.

Mehmeti had established himself over the past 18 months at Ashton Gate as a top Championship attacking midfielder and someone on the fringes of making that jump to the Premier League, and he did so at Portman Road.

The 25-year-old's top-flight dreams have been quite nighmareish so far this season, though. He's yet to make a league squad for Gary O'Neil's side, who have enjoyed a decent start to life back in the Premier League without him.

As January approaches, he'll surely be someone touted for a short-term move to get some more game time, and a few of the sides who had interest in him this past summer should be retaining that.

Discussion surrounding whether Mehmeti would be able to make the step-up to the top flight with Ipswich was swirling back in the summer. With the 25-year-old not in Gary O'Neil's plans in the first month of the season, many were expecting him to leave by deadline day.

Derby County were interested, as were Hollywood duo Wrexham and Birmingham City, but the 25-year-old declined the option to move back to the Championship, instead choosing to fight for his place at Portman Road.

Mehmeti has appeared in just one game in all competitions for the Tractor Boys this season: their 4-2 defeat in the EFL Cup against Arsenal. He did score in that game, but the performance evidently wasn't enough to warrant even a place on the bench in a Premier League outing.

Two and a half months on from deciding to stay at Ipswich, that doesn't seem to have been the right choice. With the transfer window opening soon, he may find himself tempted to prove himself once more in the league below.

Both Derby and Birmingham have experienced a stark contrast in their starts to the Championship campaign.

Chris Davies' side sit atop the second tier at the November international break, while John Eustace was close to the sack before a relieving 3-1 win over Bolton Wanderers in the final game before the break - just their second victory this season, and their first at Pride Park.

The Rams are still inside the bottom three, but have some newfound belief that the campaign can be salvaged in the coming months, especially with the good news surrounding some starters returning from long-term absences, which have contributed to this poor start.

Despite being at opposite ends of the table, though, both sides could use someone like Mehmeti on their side for the second half of the campaign.

We saw last season that the 25-year-old is comfortable coming into a side already challenging for promotion and settling in quite quickly. Mehmeti started 12 of the 19 games he was available for in Kieran McKenna's side last year, scoring and assisting twice.

Meanwhile, Derby need goals. The three against Bolton was just the third time this season they'd scored more than once in a game, and the first time since their last win at Portsmouth on September 1st. Mehmeti can provide that from an attacking midfield position.

John Eustace knows how to get the best out of a goalscoring attacking midfielder, as seen with Sammie Szmodics in the 2023/24 Championship campaign.

Even now at Derby, the Irishman has been the Rams' most dangerous goal threat this season, with four goals in 14 games before undergoing knee surgery earlier this month, which will see him out for around five more weeks.

With Mehmeti as a versatile option across the three behind a striker, bringing him in won't force Szmodics to watch on from the sidelines. They could easily both play together, and those two behind a returning Patrick Agyemeng could transform this Derby frontline in the new year.

The Albanian international netted 12 for Liam Manning's Bristol City side en route to the play-offs a couple of years ago, before starting last season with nine goals and six assists in all competitions at Ashton Gate ahead of his move to Ipswich.

If he can contribute with nearly as many goal contributions in the second half of this season, Derby won't need to worry about relegation at all.

Unfortunately, though, Derby's current concerning position in the Championship table may work against them in a potential Mehmeti deal, especially if a club like Birmingham look to reignite interest, too.

The 25-year-old wanted to prove he was Premier League quality back in August. If he's likely to do that elsewhere in January, a club near the top of the second tier would be an ideal place to showcase that.

If he can get promoted for a second time in as many years, there wouldn't be much more a player could do to be given a chance in the top flight, especially if he contributes to a strong second half of the season at that club.

At Derby, he'd be tasked with helping a club stave off relegation and climb up the table, which may not be as impressive on his resume after this season.

So, if the Rams are to put themselves in the best position possible to attract Mehemti or other leading Championship players who are on the fringes of the Premier League, a good run of form between now and the new year will help them out massively.
