CM: Gimenez could terminate Porto loan early and return to Milan – the situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Santiago Gimenez left AC Milan in the summer, but he may be returning before the end of his loan deal next summer.

It’s fair to say that Gimenez is one of the most difficult signings to assess from Milan in recent years. On paper, it made plenty of sense why he was right to arrive; he was firing in the Champions League, he had no difficulty scoring in the league and there was the added bonus of him being young and a fan of the club.

With goals against elite teams in Europe too, it made sense why he’d be able to have a successful career at San Siro. Performing on the biggest stage seemingly wasn’t an issue.

So, when he started brightly, fans thought the Rossoneri had struck gold. Yet, that form quickly came undone and Gimenez began to show glimpses, rather than actually developing a portfolio of goalscoring.

After almost being forced out of the club last summer, an injury-ridden campaign effectively ended his time with the Diavolo and he was loaned out in the summer. Yet, he could already be heading back to Italy.

As Calciomercato.com writes, it’s highly unlikely that Porto will activate the €18m buy clause in his deal, and it’s even possible that he doesn’t see out the complete spell of his loan. He’s the third-choice striker, with only four appearances in the Portuguese top flight and one penalty goal; it’s hardly form that warrants making his stay permanent.

Now, ahead of the January mercato, a meeting is planned with Rafaela Pimenta to discuss what would be best for his career. A return to Milanello cannot be ruled out, but it wouldn’t be permanent. He’s not part of Ruben Amorim’s plans, so a new destination would need to be found.

What is for sure, though, is that his value has only dropped further with his Portuguese adventure…
