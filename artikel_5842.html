Pennsylvania reports eighth measles-related death

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Pennsylvania health officials and the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) on Wednesday reported the commonwealth’s eighth measles-related death this year, one day after the U.S. lost its measles elimination status after 25 years. The state’s Department of Health said an unvaccinated Clinton County resident initially tested positive for the virus before their death.…
