Stirlingshire Investments Appoints Daniel Gicklhorn as Chief Technology Officer

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Former Apex Fintech Solutions Chief Platform Officer and Microsoft Azure product leader to lead technology strategy, architecture and AI-native engineering for Stirling ONE

NEW YORK, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Stirlingshire Investments today announced the appointment of Daniel Gicklhorn as Chief Technology Officer. Gicklhorn will lead technology strategy, architecture and engineering for Stirling ONE, the AI-native platform built to give independent advisors the technology of a large firm without giving up their independence.

Stirling ONE, which Stirlingshire introduced at Future Proof in September, brings onboarding, portfolio management, trading, compliance, reporting and client communication together on one platform, so advisors can spend less time running their business and more time on their clients.

Gicklhorn has spent more than 20 years building the platforms global enterprises run on every day, from the infrastructure behind the world's most-used online services to the modern backbone behind a new generation of investing platforms. Most recently he was Chief Platform Officer at Apex Fintech Solutions, the clearing and custody provider behind hundreds of brokerages, wealth platforms and fintechs, holding hundreds of billions of dollars for tens of millions of investors. There he led the cross-product infrastructure and trust layers behind Ascend, Apex's modern cloud-based clearing and custody platform.

Before Apex, Gicklhorn spent more than a decade at Microsoft building global infrastructure and commercializing technology that powers the modern internet. He created Azure Front Door, the commercial global edge security and delivery platform that powers more than 100,000 websites, including Microsoft's own web properties, such as Office and Xbox.

At Stirlingshire, Gicklhorn will apply his experience to the continued evolution of Stirling ONE, with a focus on secure, scalable infrastructure and practical AI capabilities that help advisors operate more efficiently and serve clients more effectively.

"The next generation of clients will expect their advisor to move at the speed of software, but no one trades trust for speed with their life savings," said Gicklhorn. "I've spent my career building platforms that deliver both. My job is to make Stirling ONE the platform advisors trust to run their practice on today and tomorrow."

Gicklhorn holds a Master of Engineering in computer science from Cornell University and advises early-stage companies on AI, cloud infrastructure and financial technology.

Stirlingshire Investments is a wealth management firm built to provide independent financial advisors with the technology, infrastructure and economics to operate more effectively. Through Stirling ONE, Stirlingshire brings core wealth management functions together in one integrated environment, while offering advisors a 100% payout and zero platform expenses.

For more information about Stirlingshire Investments, visit www.stirlingshire.com.
