Powering Ahead: Sungrow Demonstrates Resilience and Innovation at RE+ 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

LAS VEGAS, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Sungrow, a globally leading PV inverter and energy storage system provider, showcased its latest energy technologies and solutions at RE+ 2026, addressing the evolving energy needs of the North American market. Highlights included the latest advancements in Safety of Utility-scale Energy Storage, Integrated Power Solutions for AI data centers, and an Enhanced Modular Inverter for utility-scale solar, and flexible energy storage solutions for commercial and industrial (C&I) applications.

PowerTitan 3.0: Advancing Large-Scale Energy Storage Safety

A major highlight at RE+ was the first public presentation of Sungrow PowerTitan 3.0 Large-Scale Fire Test (LSFT) video, offering attendees an inside look at its system-level safety validation.

In October 2026, PowerTitan 3.0 successfully completed a large-scale fire test. The test was conducted under the lead of UL Solutions, with Fire Risk Alliance (FRA) and Jensen Hughes (JH) present. It marks the industry's first large-scale fire test of a stacked-cell BESS featuring an integrated AC-block design, demonstrating Sungrow's continued commitment to advancing energy storage safety.

Beyond safety, PowerTitan 3.0 delivers high efficiency and rapid deployment for utility-scale energy storage applications. The system achieves up to 99.3% power conversion efficiency and 92% round-trip efficiency (RTE), helping minimize energy losses throughout the charge and discharge cycles.

Its stacked battery cell design helps reduce the risks of overheating and short circuits. Combined with self-monitoring capabilities, the system manages potential thermal risks with over 99% warning accuracy, supporting safer and more reliable operation.

Designed for rapid deployment, PowerTitan 3.0 features factory pre-assembly and pre-testing to reduce on-site installation and commissioning effort. A 1 GWh project can be grid-connected in as short as 12 days, helping accelerate project delivery and improve overall deployment efficiency.

Integrated Power Solutions for AI Data Centers

As AI data centers drive growing demand for reliable and high-efficiency power infrastructure, Sungrow presented its integrated power solutions featuring the EnerNeo Solid-State Transformer (SST) and a range of Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) based power supply architectures for AI Data Centers application.

Sungrow offers EnerNeo Solid State Transformer (SST), designed to make power delivery simpler and more efficient. The modular design EnerNeo SST offers 1.5â6 MW power range and simplifies power delivery by converting medium-voltage AC directly into 800V DC. Complementing the SST, Sungrow presented three BESS architectures tailored to different data center power requirements: Grid-Forming (GFM) BESS, MV-UPS + BESS, and Dual-PCS + BESS. Built on different approaches to power management, these solutions support dynamic load smoothing, grid support, and power resilience. Together, they offer data center operators flexible options to address increasingly complex power challenges as AI infrastructure scales.

Optimized 4.8 MW Modular Inverter for Utility-Scale Solar

Sungrow also showcased its optimized 4.8 MW SG4800UD-MV-US modular inverter for the North American utility-scale solar market. Combining the advantages of central and string inverter technologies, the solution delivers 1.6 MW per unit in a flexible modular design, helping reduce balance-of-system (BOS) costs.

The split modular design enables inverter module replacement in under an hour, minimizing downtime and improving operational efficiency. Smart DC terminal temperature protection and a bottom pressure relief design further strengthen system safety. The inverter also features PV grid-forming and off-grid commissioning capabilities, supporting earlier commercial operation and helping improve project returns.

PowerStack 255CS: Supporting North America's Growing C&I Market

As Sungrow continues to expand its presence in the North American C&I energy storage market, the company showcased the PowerStack 255CS integrated energy storage system with built-in PCS.

The system delivers 257 kWh of capacity in a two-hour configuration. Designed for versatile applications, the PowerStack 255CS supports standalone energy storage, PV-plus-storage systems, EV charging integration, and microgrids. It also accommodates off-grid applications, providing a flexible energy solution for businesses seeking to enhance power reliability and maintain critical operations.

From AI data centers, utility-scale energy storage to solar power generation and C&I applications, Sungrow continues to develop technologies that address the increasingly diverse needs of the North American energy landscape.

Through its ongoing innovation in power conversion, energy storage, and grid-forming technologies, Sungrow remains committed to supporting North America's evolving energy infrastructure with efficient, reliable, and adaptable solutions.
