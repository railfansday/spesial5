Former New York State CIO Dru Rai Joins Botcopy to Redefine the AI Front Door for Public Services

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Dru Rai, who ran technology for 20 million New Yorkers, joins as Botcopy reaches eight states and ranks among Google Public Sector's top five software partners.

MIAMI, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Botcopy is the AI front door for public services: one accessible, governed place where residents actually complete what they came to do, across every agency, without forcing anyone onto one system or one agent. Today, the company announced that Dru Rai, former Chief Information Officer for the State of New York, has joined as Go-To-Market Strategic Advisor.

Right now, renewing a license, paying a bill, or applying for benefits often means hunting through a separate website for each department. Botcopy replaces that maze with one chat window where people can ask questions and finish tasks. Think of it as calling 311, built for web chat and AI. Behind the scenes, Botcopy connects the systems agencies already use, so nothing has to be replaced.

Rai joins at a moment of rapid growth: Botcopy now powers public services across eight states and has risen into a top-five Independent Software Vendor (ISV) partner within Google Public Sector.

A veteran of government technology, Rai brings more than 30 years of technology leadership. As New York's technology chief, he oversaw digital services, cloud upgrades, and AI policy across more than 50 state agencies and 20 million residents. He previously served as CIO of Goodwill Industries International and held senior roles at Quaker Houghton, Ball Corporation, DuPont, and General Electric. At Botcopy, he will advise the board and leadership on national growth and shaping the platform around what government leaders need most.

Many agencies have tested AI in small pilots. The harder part is getting AI tools to work together across departments without costly, years-long overhauls.
