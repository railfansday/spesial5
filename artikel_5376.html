Soneil Spark Launches Spark Cashback: A Home Charger, Free with a Refundable Deposit, That Pays You Back Every Time You Plug In

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Launching in time for Spark Season, the Soneil group's holiday gift to EV drivers

BRAMPTON, ON, Nov. 26, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Soneil Spark, the EV charging business of Soneil Electronics, today publicly launched Spark Cashback, a program that turns the Clean Fuel credits your home charging creates into cash in your pocket. It launches alongside Spark Season, the Soneil group's way of giving back to EV drivers this holiday season.

"We've spent more than 30 years building power electronics people can rely on, and Spark Cashback puts that to work for everyday drivers," said Sonya Jain, CEO of Soneil Spark. "The charger is free with a deposit you get back, and every charge puts money back in your pocket. Spark Season is how we give back, with a gift that keeps giving and is even better shared with someone you care about."

Under Canada's Clean Fuel Regulations, fuel suppliers must buy compliance credits, and charging an EV at home creates them. Your Spark charger tracks each session over Wi-Fi, Soneil Spark sells the credits, and you get a share as cash by Interac e-Transfer. The fuel industry helps offset your everyday charging, while you keep driving without tailpipe emissions.

The charger is free with a refundable deposit. Once you reach 1,250 kWh, the full deposit comes back, earlier than most comparable free-charger programs. You end up with a home charger that cost you nothing, apart from shipping, plus cash back on top. How soon depends on your driving.

A program is easier to trust when you know who is behind it. Soneil Spark designs and builds a complete range of EV charging under one roof, including mobile charging trailers for fleets and enterprise, all on our own technology, made in Canada, rather than rebadged off-the-shelf hardware. Soneil Electronics has reliably supplied batteries and chargers globally across a wide range of industries and applications, from golf carts and cleaning appliances to material handling and emergency power. The company has supplied more than 5 million chargers, and every Soneil Spark charger is backed by 24/7 North American-based support.

Spark Season: the gift that keeps giving back

Most gifts get unwrapped once. This one keeps showing up in your bank account every time you charge. Spark Season is the Soneil group's way of giving back, with promos throughout the holiday season, so check back regularly. But don't wait: if a better promo comes along after you sign up, just ask and we'll readjust your offer.

Pay it forward, in the most literal sense. Don't just tell the EV drivers you care about, get it for them. Family, close friends, anyone who drives electric can have a charger that's free with a refundable deposit and pays them back, with the fuel industry's compliance credits doing the giving.

Open to new customers with a Canadian address and Wi-Fi at the charger. Quebec drivers can choose the Canada-wide offer or a Quebec-specific program. Getting it for family or a friend? They need to be a new customer at an eligible address. Sign up and see this season's deposit, rates, bonus and warranty terms at soneilspark.com/pages/spark-cashback.

Free charger requires a refundable deposit, plus shipping, refunded after 1,250 kWh of charging; timing depends on your driving. Cash back is paid out at each payout milestone. Deposit and bonus amounts, rates, warranty terms and full terms are on the sign-up page.
