SMCI vs. NTAP: Which Stock Is the Better Value Option?

Investors interested in stocks from the Computer- Storage Devices sector have probably already heard of Super Micro Computer (SMCI) and NetApp $NTAP (NTAP). But which of these two stocks is more attractive to value investors? We'll need to take a closer look to find out.

We have found that the best way to discover great value opportunities is to pair a strong Zacks Rank with a great grade in the Value category of our Style Scores system. The proven Zacks Rank puts an emphasis on earnings estimates and estimate revisions, while our Style Scores work to identify stocks with specific traits.

Currently, Super Micro Computer has a Zacks Rank of #1 (Strong Buy), while NetApp has a Zacks Rank of #3 (Hold). This means that SMCI's earnings estimate revision activity has been more impressive, so investors should feel comfortable with its improving analyst outlook. But this is just one piece of the puzzle for value investors.

Value investors analyze a variety of traditional, tried-and-true metrics to help find companies that they believe are undervalued at their current share price levels.

The Style Score Value grade factors in a variety of key fundamental metrics, including the popular P/E ratio, P/S ratio, earnings yield, cash flow per share, and a number of other key stats that are commonly used by value investors.

SMCI currently has a forward P/E ratio of 3.84, while NTAP has a forward P/E of 17.14. We also note that SMCI has a PEG ratio of 0.25. This figure is similar to the commonly-used P/E ratio, with the PEG ratio also factoring in a company's expected earnings growth rate. NTAP currently has a PEG ratio of 1.53.

Another notable valuation metric for SMCI is its P/B ratio of 1.35. The P/B ratio pits a stock's market value against its book value, which is defined as total assets minus total liabilities. For comparison, NTAP has a P/B of 22.62.

These metrics, and several others, help SMCI earn a Value grade of B, while NTAP has been given a Value grade of D.

SMCI sticks out from NTAP in both our Zacks Rank and Style Scores models, so value investors will likely feel that SMCI is the better option right now.
