Aberdeen suffer major double blow as Stephen Robinson confirms injury lay-offs

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Aberdeen boss Stephen Robinson has been dealt a double injury blow ahead of the William Hill Premiership clash with Hibernian at Pittodrie on Saturday night.

Centre-back Mats Knoester and left-back Emmanuel Gyamfi will be out for at least 10 weeks with respective tendon and thigh injuries, stretching the Dons’ squad further.

Knoester picked up his injury at training before the recent 3-2 defeat away to Dundee United while Gyamfi had to go off in the first half of last weekend’s Premier Sports Cup semi-final defeat by Rangers at Barclays Hampden.

Robinson said: “We received some bad news on Mats (Knoester). He is going to be out for a minimum of 10 weeks.

“The same with Emmanuel (Gyamfi) after he got a scan. They’re two big blows to us when they were just coming back and Mats was finding really good form.

“They (Mats and Emmanuel) just can’t seem to get a run of games, unfortunately. I feel for them mentally as well. It’s so, so tough when you’re just back.

“Emmanuel had an injury previously before he came to the club, a thigh injury, and it’s gone again, unfortunately. So it’s just constant muscle injuries with him. Mats is a tendon injury, which is a big blow for us.

“The illness side of things hasn’t cleared, Ayoub Mouloua won’t be available. He’s picked up the bug now as well, so he probably won’t have any involvement on Saturday.

“We were hoping he’d be in today, but he’s still not better from it. So at the minute, he’s the only new casualty, and we hope that’s the end of it, the last of it, fingers crossed.

“Good news on Brad Lyons. He took a bad knock which looked bad, but actually didn’t transpire into anything serious. He’ll train today, so he’ll be fine for tomorrow.

“Dan (Happe) is good. He’s trained the last two days. Obviously, he had the virus and couldn’t play on Saturday. We have Toyosi (Olusanya) available again as well. He wasn’t available on Saturday.

“Some positives within that as well, and it is what it is. We just have to deal with it.”

After losing three games in a row in all competitions, Aberdeen are 10th in the table, just three points above bottom club Hibs.

Robinson said: “It’s a big game. We need to win. As I’ve said previously, every game you go into as Aberdeen manager, you need to win.

“I believe that, to give us a little bit of breathing space and let us get into the international window to reset ourselves, and hopefully maybe get Chris Cadden back and a couple of others closer to fitness, we need to win the game.

“Every game with Aberdeen, you need to win. There’s no excuses. You’re expected to win, especially home games. That comes with the territory. We’ll pick as positive a team as we possibly can.”
