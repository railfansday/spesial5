It has now been reported that Newcastle United are interested in signing Kenneth Taylor in January.

The next transfer window is only six weeks away from opening.

Kenneth Taylor has proved a bargain buy by Lazio.

He was signed by the Serie A club in the January 2026 transfer window for a reported £15m plus future add-ons and in the second half of the 2025-26 season scored three goals and got two assists in 17 league appearances.

The left footed central midfielder has now played every league game so far this season and been a key factor in Lazio surprisingly topping Serie A at the moment, Kenneth Taylor adding two league goals and one assist so far this campaign.

The Netherlands international now a target for Newcastle United according to Tuttosport in Italy, with both Brentford and Bournemouth also credited with interest.

Lazio desperate to hold on to their star midfielder, rather than cash in and double their money in 12 months, with a valuation now of around £40m on Kenneth Taylor.

Kenneth Taylor was a teammate of Sean Steur’s at Ajax and won player of the year for the 2024-25 season when with the Amsterdam club. Taylor made 37 starts in the Dutch domestic league and Europa League, scoring 14 goals and getting seven assists. A bit of a surprise really that clubs in the Premier League and beyond didn’t show more interest in signing him in summer 2025.
