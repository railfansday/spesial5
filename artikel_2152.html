3 players who Burnley should offload in January, including Armando Broja

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Burnley sit 22nd in the Championship table and still only have one win this season with the latest international break upon us. Fabio Celestini has inherited a struggling squad, with plenty of players on the fringes.

As the Clarets look to turn the tide in their battle against relegation, it seems inevitable that new players join in the winter transfer window. And, to make way for some new signings, sales are going to be needed.

With that said, we put forward three players who Burnley must offload when the January transfer window comes around…

Following an injury he sustained during his time with Southampton, Armando Broja has been unable to stay fit and display his talent on the pitch. The forward has had unsuccessful spells with Fulham, Everton and Chelsea and has continued in that manner with Burnley.

Broja was a regular feature for the Clarets last season and has played nine times this term. However, he has only scored one goal for the side and appears to be devoid of confidence.

Between a struggle with injuries, a lack of form and faltering relationship with supporters, it could be for the best that Broja makes a fresh start elsewhere in January.

Tresor was an exciting signing when he joined Burnley, having impressed with KRC Genk and broken into the Belgium national squad. However, his fortunes have changed at Turf Moor and he has spent the vast majority of his time at the club on the fringes of the squad.

Tresor is currently 27, and should be hitting his prime in his football career. However, he has only played 17 Championship minutes this season across two appearances. His last appearance for the first-team came in September after he was omitted from their 25-man EFL squad.

Like Broja, a permanent departure seems to be the best option for Tresor. He would benefit from an opportunity abroad once more as he bids to revive his career.

While Tresor and Broja commanded hefty fees when they joined Burnley, Shurandy Sambo did not. However, the right-back has also been unable to find minutes this term after he was left out of their squad.

The Clarets have Connor Roberts and Kyle Walker as options at right-back, leaving Sambo’s opportunities limited. He joined in 2024 and has only played twice for the club overall, spending last season on loan at Sparta Rotterdam.

A return back to the Netherlands could be best for Sambo and there should be little issue finding him a new home when the winter transfer window comes around.
