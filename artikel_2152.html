Millwall controversy takes fresh twist - West Ham will be watching on

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A fresh twist has emerged concerning the off-pitch drama at Millwall involving once highly-rated winger, Benicio Baker-Boaitey.

The Lions' start to the Championship season has been one of mixed fortunes, with Alex Neil's side sat 16th, having accumulated 18 points 15 matches.

Neil, one of the second tier's most seasoned managers, has been without several key names so far this season due to injury and suspension issues, which hasn't helped his side's wish for consistency.

One of the players missing in action for quite some time now is Baker-Boaitey, with it initially envisaged that the 22-year-old was also on the treatment table for a prolonged period.

However, recent developments have shed new light on the winger's situation, and it is one which could be about to get worse.

Baker-Boaitey arrived at Millwall in January 2025 after a spell with Premier League outfit, Brighton and Hove Albion, where he made six top-flight appearances for the Seagulls under Roberto De Zerbi after debuting against Chelsea in December 2023.

He would then link up with Port Vale for the 2024/25 League Two season, before his gametime in North Staffordshire was cut short due to injury, with Neil admitting after his move to SE16 was completed that Baker-Boaitey was suffering with pubic symphysis.

Almost two years later, though, the winger is yet to make a first-team appearance for the Lions, and was recently seen posting cryptic Instagram posts, one of which was captioned: "On my mumma, can't even train with the 21s."

His account has since been deactivated, and it has since been widely reported that Millwall are now undertaking disciplinary action, which could even result in the termination of his contract, whilst Baker-Boaitey is also said to be weighing up his options.

Given Brighton's recent ability for producing and unearthing several gems, there was an understandable hype when Millwall added Baker-Boaitey to their squad so soon after the club-record sale of Romain Esse - who has since returned to the club on loan - to Crystal Palace.

“Another England youth international, Benicio ticks a number of boxes we’re looking to recruit for at his young age of 21," said Lions director of football, Steve Gallen.

"He is an exciting winger who will add plenty of pace and skill to the squad, which will get fans off their seats."

However, this prediction has failed to come true, with Baker-Boaitey only making one appearance for the club's Under-21s side shortly after moving from the AMEX Stadium.

Millwall and West Ham United fans' disdain towards one another is certainly well-documented, and was displayed at The Den two months ago in a compelling 2-2 draw.

However, those from East London will certainly have one eye on how their rivals' disciplinary investigation into Baker-Boaitey's conduct plays out.

Indeed, the former England youth international came through the famous academy at Rush Green, and by 2020, aged 16, was linked with a move to Bayern Munich after rejecting professional terms at the London Stadium, eventually signing for FC Porto, with West Ham receiving £190,000 in compensation.

The German giants were not alone when it came to high-profile clubs courting his signature, with the likes of Liverpool, Paris Saint-Germain and Manchester United also credited with interest.

Since Baker-Boaitey's social media swipe, Neil is yet to issue a statement on the matter, and neither has the player.

Millwall will be hoping that this doesn't cause any form of distraction regardless of Baker-Boaitey's distinct lack of involvement since joining the club, whilst further impressions could be made on West Ham fans, even if returning to form under new head coach, Scott Parker, is their top focus right now.
