Three shows in Monterrey: live visuals for H3MBR4 with TouchDesigner and TouchDiffusion

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

On 21 and 22 November I ran the live image for H3MBR4 at Edificio Lolyta, in downtown Monterrey.

H3MBR4 is an experimental choreography by Yessica and Fernanda Patricia Villarreal. The piece takes the body as a sound generator: movement, breath, pulse and voice become material. My part was the image, in TouchDesigner with TouchDiffusion, running in real time next to the dancers and the sound.

Three functions, free entry, supported by the Convocatoria Nacional de Danza 2026 through the Plataforma de Danza Expandida y Performance, Danza INBAL.

What I keep from the weekend: the image only works when it listens. A generative system that reacts to a body on stage has to accept that the body sets the tempo, not the other way around. ``
