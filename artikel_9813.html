How to Get LinkedIn Ad Library Data into Google Sheet

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

If you have ever tried to pull LinkedIn ad data into Google Sheets, you have probably hit the same wall: LinkedIn's Ad Library shows you the ads, but it gives you no bulk export, no search API, and no native Google Sheets integration.

In this guide, we will use a LinkedIn Ad Library scraper to pull structured ad data — headlines, ad copy, creative URLs, CTAs, targeting details, impression ranges — and push it straight into Google Sheets. No paid connector, no manual copy-paste.

There are two ways to reach Make — but they don't lead to the same place, so pick carefully.

You now have a hands-off pipeline that turns public LinkedIn Ad Library data into structured rows in Google Sheets — no manual exports, no copy-paste, and no paid connector. Run the Actor manually whenever you want a one-off snapshot, or schedule it on Apify to keep the sheet continuously updated with fresh competitor ad data.
