Microchip: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Hailo's neural processing unit lets Microchip skip five years of in-house chip development and move straight into edge AI.

On its second-quarter fiscal year 2027 earnings call, held on Nov. 5, 2026, Microchip (MCHP +1.23%) reported net sales of $1.62 billion. Long-term investors should care because severe external manufacturing bottlenecks are delaying shipments of high-margin products despite steady customer demand across industrial and automotive markets.

Three key takeaways from management explain where the business is heading.

Capacity bottlenecks at external foundries that manufacture silicon wafers and at OSATs, or outsourced semiconductor assembly and test providers, are creating severe supply shortages. These constraints are delaying shipment of high-margin microchip orders and stretching delivery schedules for customers.

Microchip is expanding its presence in artificial intelligence infrastructure through high-speed connectivity parts. Rapid adoption of its PCIe, or Peripheral Component Interconnect Express, data-transfer switches and retimers, which are signal-boosting microchips, is driving its data center business toward $1 billion in annual net sales.

Management completed the acquisition of Hailo to gain an advanced NPU, or neural processing unit, designed for hardware-accelerated machine learning. Integrating this technology allows Microchip to skip five years of internal development work and immediately target edge artificial intelligence applications.

Demand across industrial, automotive, and defense markets remains stable, even as third-party assembly bottlenecks restrict short-term shipments. Management relies on proprietary chip designs and long-standing customer relationships to defend target margins while monitoring external factory capacity allocations.

Microchip remains committed to its core non-GAAP metrics, which exclude one-time acquisition costs and share-based compensation.
