Bystanders Form Human Chain To Rescue Two Hikers Who Slipped From Yosemite’s Half Dome Cables

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

In a daring rescue originally reported and verified by SFGATE, a group of hikers on Yosemite National Park’s Half Dome formed a human chain to rescue an engaged couple clinging to the steep slope after slipping from the cables.

On October 12, a woman descending Half Dome’s famous cable system reportedly lost her footing and began sliding away, eventually coming to rest just above a sheer drop-off.

Another hiker who was present at the time described hearing the woman’s screams just as he and his son finished descending the cables themselves. “I just was shouting, ‘No, no, no!’ because I knew she was dead,” the hiker told SFGATE. And then she stopped, and I just couldn’t believe it.”

Hikers on Half Dome’s cable path in 2025. Photo: Anna McKinney Smith

The woman’s fiancé reportedly left the cable path to help her but also slipped and ended up hanging onto a small ledge by his fingertips.

Hikers who witnessed the pair’s predicament called 911 and began assembling a makeshift chain of carabiners, slings, and doubled rope from their own supplies.

According to SFGATE, one hiker climbed down to the stranded man and helped him back to the cables while a human chain of hikers braced the line. Once safe, the man immediately joined the human chain to help reach his fianceé farther down the cliff.

The existing rope chain was too short to reach her, so the hikers extended it by linking together several climbing harnesses donated by bystanders.

Yosemite’s Half Dome. Photo via Katie Jackson

As the wind picked up and light snow began to fall, another hiker climbed down to her and helped her back to safety, aided by those bracing the line further up the slope.

The rescued couple reportedly embraced at the base of the cables and hugged the rescuers, with the fiancé weeping and promising to buy the group beers once they returned from the 16-mile round-trip trail.
