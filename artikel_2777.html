Can the Rams add anyone to replace Puka Nacua?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Should the Los Angeles Rams have a traded for a wide receiver at the trade deadline with Puka Nacua expected to be out? Was anyone available going to replace Nacua? Likely not. Nacua's injury is a loss to the Rams no matter who they could've added via trade.

Will the Rams be able to overcome the loss of Nacua? Anything can happen, but likely not exactly. Nacua offered an incredible offensive presence and he's out now. There is no easy answer. The Rams will just have to put in the work and play their best.
