Dolphins news: Reactions to Miami’s Week 10 loss to the Colts

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Miami Dolphins lost to the Indianapolis Colts on Sunday, falling 24-22 and dropping to 1-8 in 2026. The game featured Miami starting a quarterback in his NFL debut, a shuffle among the defensive tackles due to injuries, an All-Pro center dealing with a conucssion, and a starting wide receiver missing the game due to injury. Yet, none of those were the reason behind Miami's two-point loss.

Football is a team sport, and there are always moments on offense and defense that could have been executed better. The Dolphins' offense gave up a defensive touchdown on a sack fumble – not ideal. The defense could have been tougher against the run, stopping Jonathan Taylor and Seth McGowan from leading the team to over 170 yards on the ground. But, even with those issues, Miami was within two points of pulling off the upset win.

Miami did enough to win on offense and defense. They fell apart on special teams, specifically with placekicking. Kicker Zane Gonzalez missed a 47-yard field goal in the first quarter. After connecting on a 53-yard kick, he missed an extra point in the third quarter. He also missed a 51-yard field goal in the fourth quarter. Miami lost seven points on three kicks from Gonzalez. Even the extra point would have changed things; a converted extra point in the third quarter could have let Miami kick a tying extra point in the fourth quarter instead of attempting a failed two-point conversion.

Maybe it is too harsh to blame the loss on a single player, but Gonzalez missed kicks Miami had to have, and this is not the first misses he has had recently. Gonzalez has struggled since joining the Dolphins, first on the practice squad, then being promoted to the active roster for Sunday. In four games, he is 6-for-10 on field goal attempts and 6-for-7 on extra points. Gonzalez has also struggled on kickoffs, leading Miami to move to punter Bradley Pinion to handle those responsibilities.

Miami should have come out of Indianapolis with a win. It was there for the taking. They just could not connect on the kicks that would have changed the result. The kicking woes were among the top talking points around the web following the game.
