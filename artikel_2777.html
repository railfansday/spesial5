Lions place Cade Mays on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Lions placed center Cade Mays on injured reserve for the second time this season on Saturday.

Mays has missed the last two games with an ankle injury and he was ruled out for Sunday’s game against the Buccaneers ahead of Saturday’s roster move. Mays fractured his wrist in training camp and Mays missed the first five games of the season as a result.

Juice Scruggs has started at center when Mays has been out of the lineup this season.

The Lions filled the empty roster spot by signing defensive back Nick Whiteside off of the practice squad. He’s appeared in two games for the team this season.

Linebacker Owen Pappoe was elevated from the practice squad to round out the day’s moves.
