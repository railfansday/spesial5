Warriors' Kristaps Porzingis stepping away from basketball to prioritize health

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Kristaps Porzingis has decided to "step away from the game" as he prioritizes his health, the center announced in a statement Friday, while committing to work with the Golden State Warriors to "find a solution that makes sense."

The Warriors had stopped payments on his $20 million salary amid his ongoing absence from the organization.
