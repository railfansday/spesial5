Todd Monken: Getting flexed into Sunday night is really cool for our players

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

If someone had told you at the beginning of the season that the Week 12 game between the Browns and Raiders would get flexed into Sunday Night Football, you probably would have responded with some healthy skepticism.

But with the way the season has unfolded, that’s exactly what’s happened.

With the Browns at 6-4 and the Raiders at 4-6 — and now starting the league’s No. 1 overall pick at quarterback — the two clubs make for one of the most compelling matchups of Thanksgiving weekend that wasn’t already in a standalone window.

Browns head coach Todd Monken told reporters on Wednesday that getting flexed is “really cool for our players.”

“I said it at the beginning of the year. I mean, you want to be on Thursday Night, Sunday Night, Monday Night — you want to be flexed, means you’re having a good year,” Monken said, via transcript from the team. “As nice as it is to have a one o’clock schedule every week, I mean, I said that, early in the [year]. I mean, you’re on a trajectory to be fired at some point because you’re at one o’clock every week. So, you’re fighting like hell to get to where you’re on national TV. So, if people are going to watch football, and more importantly than that, an opportunity to get to seven [wins].”

Monken also agreed that it’s a good test for the Browns as a young team.

“I think you earn where you’re at in the present moment, and that only lends itself to what’s in front of you and how important that’s going to become,” Monken said. “You know, getting the bye at 6-4 and putting yourself in position where the rest of the games matter, and especially the one coming up.”

This will be the Browns’ first Sunday night game at home since 2019, which should make for a rowdy atmosphere on the shores of Lake Erie.

“Oh, it’s a huge advantage. Huge advantage,” Monken said of the anticipated environment. “Making it hard for the opposing offense, cadence-wise, being able to function, and making an advantage for us when we’re on offense. I mean, it’s a huge advantage, and it should be playing at home.”
