Calvin Ridley faces misdemeanor assault charge over airport incident

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Titans wide receiver Calvin Ridley faces a misdemeanor assault charge after his Nov. 7 incident with a valet at Nashville International Airport, Paul Kuharsky of paulkuharsky.com reports.

Ridley is scheduled to be booked in Metro General Sessions Court on Dec. 7.

“With this being an ongoing police investigation, we’ll have no further comment on the matter at this point in time,” the Titans said in a statement to Kuharsky.

Ridley has six catches for 91 yards in eight games this season.
