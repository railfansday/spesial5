Grizzlies guard Ty Jerome ruled out vs Brooklyn Nets with hip soreness

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Ty Jerome will sit out the Memphis Grizzlies' Nov. 14 game against the Brooklyn Nets with hip soreness.

The game at FedExForum is the second night of a back-to-back. He had 17 points on 6-of-18 shooting against the New Orleans Pelicans on Nov. 13.

The Grizzlies also confirmed that Scottie Pippen Jr., Quinten Post and Richie Sanders will be out against Brooklyn.

Jerami Grant, the Grizzlies' leading scorer against New Orleans with 22 points, is questionable with a head injury. Center Zach Edey is also questionable.

The Grizzlies (8-4) routed the Pelicans 142-111 on Nov. 13 at Smoothie King Center, where all five starters scored in double figures.
