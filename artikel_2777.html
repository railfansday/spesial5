Jason Kelce believes teams should coach celebrations after Aidan Hutchinson’s ACL tear

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Jason Kelce believes teams should coach celebrations after Aidan Hutchinson's ACL tear appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Detroit Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson went from the highest of highs to the lowest of lows in Week 10 against the Patriots as his celebration led to a torn ACL with no additional damage. Detroit added another edge rusher off waivers from the Dallas Cowboys and are moving forward without Hutchinson in pursuit of a playoff berth and division title.

Jason Kelce, formerly of the Philadelphia Eagles, lamented the Hutchinson injury on the NFL on ESPN's Monday Night Football pregame show. He proposed that teams begin coaching celebrations to prevent unforced injuries like the one 'Hutch' suffered in Germany against New England.

"I think there's enough evidence out there now…Between obviously what just happened with Aidan Hutchinson, Stephen Tulloch who was also in that same organization, Lamar Houston that same season did it, Gramatica, I believe it was Bill Gramatica, not even Martin Gramatica, but yeah, there's enough evidence now, where, maybe we should be coaching these guys. We should be coaching celebrations differently."

Jason McCourty of ESPN questioned Kelce's proposal, wondering whether teams would fine players who celebrated against their rules. Kelce said the punishment in such a scenario would likely be to point out what not to do in a film session.

Hutchinson missed the second half of the 2024 NFL season due to a fractured fibia and tibia in his left leg. He bounced back to play all 17 games in the 2025 season before his latest injury, which came after a celebratory leap and sack of Patriots QB Drake Maye. His teammates revealed that they thought Hutchinson was playing a prank on them after the injury, but it turned out to be another worst-case scenario that Lions fans everywhere feared.
