Liam Lawson baffled by FIA Safety Car call in nightmare Las Vegas GP

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Liam Lawson has questioned why it took so long for him to unlap himself at the Las Vegas Grand Prix, having already started a lap down at the Strip Circuit due to a fuel leak prior to the race.

Continuing his impressive form in 2026, Lawson was set to line up in P7 for the start of the Las Vegas GP, but the race had already begun by the time he emerged from the pit lane following a remarkable recovery job from his Racing Bulls team.

After a Lap 19 Safety Car was called after a collision between Rafa Camara and Sergio Perez, Lawson - who showed impressive pace throughout the Grand Prix - ought to have thought he had a chance of another strong race, with lapped drivers usually allowed to unlap themselves.

However, at the second longest track on the F1 calendar, Lawson struggled to catch up to the back of the pack, and by the time he was informed that race leader Max Verstappen was restarting proceedings, he was still over 20 seconds behind.

"It's f****** pointless, dude," Lawson said on team radio. "Oh my god. I cannot believe that." The best the Kiwi could do was P14 - having been bumped up a place by Isack Hadjar's 10-second penalty.

"Thank you for all you guys for putting my car together so quickly, I appreciate it," Lawson said on the post-race radio. "I'm sorry I couldn't get a result for everyone today, I really f****** tried, though."

Speaking to media, including GPblog, Lawson elaborated on his frustrations over the timing of the Safety Car, questioning why it took so long for him to be able to unlap himself. The Safety Car period was also extended after a bizarre spin for Lawson's teammate Arvid Lindblad.

"The thing is I got it back, and we actually probably could've still had a really good race," Lawson began. "I don't know why they they took four laps to release me. I get that when there's marshals on track and there's safety around it. I can understand that, but it's still a double yellow.

"I can still slow down for the double yellow zone and follow a delta. There's nothing stopping me. You know, I'm not gonna blow through that zone at high speeds, but it just means I can do the whole lap and catch back up.

"And, unfortunately, they waited four laps, and then they gave me half a lap to catch back up. I restarted more than 25 seconds behind the field.'

All the more frustrating for Lawson was the fact Racing Bulls showed impressive pace, with Lindblad fighting at the front before his Safety Car spin.

"So frustrating, man. I was watching the screens the whole race and I could see how close it was bunched up. At the end the race, we were very, very quick today. It's really frustrating to not have the chance to fight there."

Lawson later added: "I think Arvid was at the front. I don't know what happened, but he was fighting at the front as well. So for us to not score points just really sucks at a track where we're really fast."
