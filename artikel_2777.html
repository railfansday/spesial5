‘It makes no sense’ – Kurt Warner says NFL legend simply has to be inducted into Hall of Fame

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Kurt Warner has made a strong case for one of his former wide receivers to be inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame.

The former quarterback was inducted into the Hall of Fame back in 2017, which made him the only player to be inducted into both the NFL and the Arena Hall of Fame following his incredible career that proved so many people wrong along the way.

However, the 55-year-old is now making a case for one of his former wide receivers from his days with the St. Louis Rams to be inducted into the Hall of Fame. Although Warner's talent and resilience defined his career, he also had the help of receiver Torry Holt.

Holt played in the Rams team alongside Warner, which won Super Bowl XXXIV. The wide receiver scored a nine-yard touchdown in that game after catching a pass from Warner. This is just one of the reasons that Warner feels Holt should be inducted into the Hall of Fame.

"No doubt. And I think with the new rules where you have to get a certain amount of the votes, and not that that's necessarily a bad thing, but when you have a Steve Smith and a Reggie Wayne and a Torry Holt. Look at Torry, he's an all-decade player, and he can't get into the Hall of Fame," Warner said on the Dan Patrick Show.

"It makes no sense to me. But if you have all these voters going, 'Well, I think Reggie was a little better than Steve, and Steve was a little better than Torry, and Torry was like, you start axing people off and you can't get that required number of votes. Even though you look at those guys and you can make an argument that all of them belong in there. To me, Torry is a no-brainer. But he continues to wait and it's hard for me to figure out exactly why."

Holt retired with the 10 most receiving yards in the NFL, but he has now fallen down the order to 19, with current players like Mike Evans and Travis Kelce both overtaking him. The 50-year-old is also able to boast six consecutive seasons with 1,300 yards.

The former receiver has already been inducted in the College Football Hall of Fame, but he has been unable to get over the line, having already been a regular finalist for the Pro Football Hall of Fame.
