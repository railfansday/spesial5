Lions preview: Breaking down Patriots’ offensive, defensive schemes

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Detroit Lions (6-2) and New England Patriots (5-4) are headed to Munich, Germany for a Week 10 battle between interconference opponents. Despite coming off a Super Bowl appearance in 2025, the Patriots' season has been wildly inconsistent, looking unbeatable at times and completely vulnerable at others. While it's impossible to know which Patriots team will show up on any given week, the Lions expect New England at their best in this matchup.

"Real good team here," Lions coach Dan Campbell said of the Patriots. "I know the record is not the true indication of what this team is. There's a number of things that go into that. […] You look at the totality of what this team is capable of, the firepower they have, and they're a good team. They're dangerous. And they're going to clean a lot of things up."

Former Patriots quarterback and current color commentator Tom Brady covered the Patriots' last game against the Packers—a 45-27 blowout—and he was less kind in his assessment of the Patriots:

"Half the season they played like this, and the other half of the season they haven't turned the ball over, they haven't exposed the defense, have played more physical on defense, but right now it's just too inconsistent. Which team are they going to get? The team that is loose with the ball, is loose with decision-making, looking like they can be beaten by anybody. Or the team that takes care of the football, that makes the other team play left-handed, and can do what they want to do. Can they be a good team? They haven't quite done that because good teams leave the smallest margin of error."

Let's take a closer look at the Patriots' coaching staff, the scheme they run, and scouting notes from their most recently viewed games.
