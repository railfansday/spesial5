Taty Castellanos to leave West Ham? Everything we know right now

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

West Ham have had a disappointing start to the Championship season, with Nuno Espirito Santo having already paid the price for his job.

Many expected the Hammers to not only return to the Premier League at the first attempt, but to win the title, so the fact they are outside the play-off places after 15 games is a concern.

Of course, there is still time to turn things around, so new boss Scott Parker won’t be panicking, even if he knows that they must improve quickly.

When it comes to underperforming players, there are several individuals at West Ham who are not hitting their best levels, with Taty Castellanos arguably the biggest culprit, having scored just once in 14 Championship outings.

That will have come as a major surprise, as the 28-year-old was one of the few positives for the Irons as they were relegated last season.

After joining from Lazio in January, Castellanos scored six times in 18 Premier League games, which is a very decent return.

Therefore, it was widely expected that he would take the second tier by storm, but, as outlined above, Castellanos hasn’t delivered.

It’s not just his poor form in front of goal either, as Castellanos’ work-rate and intensity made him such a popular figure, and that appears to have dropped off as well.

Despite that, it has been claimed that Castellanos could move on in January, with reports stating that River Plate have made the 28-year-old a top target going into the New Year.

The chance to return to his home country may appeal to the two-cap Argentina international, and obviously River Plate are a huge club that will hope to compete for honours domestically and on the continent.

It has been said that River Plate would look to sign Castellanos on an initial loan deal, which may include an option or obligation to buy, which is hardly ideal for the Hammers, although it remains to be seen how negotiations play out.

Right now, it’s fair to think that West Ham will regret not cashing in on Castellanos in the summer window, when his value was at its highest.

His form in the Premier League understandably put him on the radar of clubs in the top division, and it was revealed that Everton had an offer worth up to £45m turned down by the Londoners prior to the summer deadline.

At the time, you could see why West Ham took that stance. There was an expectation that the striker would be huge in their promotion push this season, so retaining him was an ambition statement by the hierarchy after also keeping the likes of Konstantinos Mavropanos.

In hindsight, however, it is clearly a mistake, as whether it’s River Plate or another club, it’s clear that West Ham aren’t going to get anywhere near £45m for the ex-Girona man.

Most fans would urge West Ham not to accept a loan deal, and it’s easy to see why. Even if they won’t get near £45m, Castellanos could still command a huge fee.

That could be put towards a few new signings in January, and Parker will no doubt have his own targets that he wants to bring to the club.

But, if Castellanos does want to move to River Plate, and they’re only offering an initial loan, it could put the Hammers in a tricky spot. Do they risk keeping an unhappy player?

Ultimately, there is a lot of football to be played between now and January, so things can change, and Parker must do all he can to get Castellanos back to his best, as he could be a major asset to the team if he gets near that level.

If he can’t, though, then a January move seems inevitable, and West Ham may have to make a compromise to ship him out if it’s for the good of the club.
