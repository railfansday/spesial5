Southampton must join race for West Ham, Wolves target as ideal Cyle Larin competition: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Southampton’s reliance on Cyle Larin has become increasingly apparent this season, making the January transfer window an important opportunity to strengthen their attacking options. After Ross Stewart’s release, Divin Mubama joined on loan, but he has not made the desired impact.

Rangers striker Ryan Naderi is a man who may be available in January could be an ideal addition for the Saints. Offering the physical presence, work rate and goalscoring instincts that Tonda Eckert’s side need, they would need to beat West Ham and Wolves to his signature.

Larin has been outstanding since making his move permanent in the summer, following his productive loan spell from Mallorca. The Canadian international quickly established himself as Southampton’s main attacking threat, scoring seven goals in the early stages of the Championship campaign and earning the division’s Player of the Month award for August.

However, his recent hamstring injury has exposed a concerning lack of reliable alternatives. Lewis Dobbin has struggled for consistency since arriving for around £9m, while Mubama has yet to establish himself. With Southampton targeting promotion, relying so heavily on one striker is a considerable risk.

Naderi could help address that problem. The 23-year-old has emerged as one of Rangers’ most important players, registering nine goals and five assists in 17 appearances across all competitions this season.

His Scottish Premiership return of six goals and three assists in 11 matches underlines his growing influence. There are obvious similarities between Naderi and Larin. Both are powerful, penalty-box forwards who use their movement, physicality and finishing instincts to make an impact.

At 6ft 4in, Naderi would offer even greater aerial presence, while his relentless pressing and willingness to run beyond defenders could suit Eckert’s energetic approach. Crucially, he would provide genuine competition rather than simply another attacking option.

Larin remains Southampton’s first-choice striker, but the 31-year-old cannot be expected to shoulder the goalscoring responsibility throughout a demanding 46-game campaign. Naderi could rotate with him, provide a different physical threat or potentially complement him in a two-striker system.

There are complications, however. Rangers reportedly have little interest in selling midway through the season, particularly with Naderi under contract until 2029.

Interest from West Ham and Wolves could also increase the financial competition, meaning Southampton would likely need to make a significant investment. Nevertheless, this is precisely the type of recruitment they should be considering. Naderi is young, improving and already producing consistently at a major club.

Southampton need greater depth around Larin, and signing a striker with a similar physical and goalscoring profile could make a significant difference to their promotion ambitions.
