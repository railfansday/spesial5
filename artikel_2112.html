Sheffield United want to sign Chelsea player - 2 players should follow

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Sheffield United are continuing to keep tabs on Chelsea starlet Ryan Kavuma-McQueen, as the Blades look to put plans in place for the future of the club. Those plans should also include a couple of out-of-favour Premier League stars.

Chris Wilder's side missed out on the chance to climb into the play-offs ahead of the November international break after losing to Charlton Athletic, putting a bit of a damper on what was, overall, a good block of fixtures between breaks.

The Blades had won four of their previous six before their rather surprising defeat at the Valley against an Addicks side who were winless in 11 league games.

Now, they face a tough schedule between now and the new year, where they take on the likes of Southampton, QPR, West Ham United, West Brom and Middlesbrough, which will really test their top-eight credentials.

Regardless, though, recruitment plans are being put in place for any outcome this season, and Kavuma-McQueen remains on their shortlist, according to the Sheffield Star.

17-year-old Kavuma-McQueen has been one of the more talked-about wingers in the Championship this season, excelling in his first senior loan spell with Portsmouth.

The Chelsea loanee has shown the fearless dribbling quality that a lot of young, exciting players have, but he's completing these dribbles at a very successful rate of over 50%, per FotMob.

He definitely caught the eye amongst Sheffield United fans during their recent meeting at Fratton Park, too, as he drew both fouls which led to Hamza Choudhury's red card, and then set up Adrien Segecic to put Pompey 2-0 up.

According to the Star, Kavuma-McQueen could have signed for the Blades on loan this past summer, but at the time, Chris Wilder was wanting some more experienced loanees out wide, with the likes of Romain Esse and Amario Cozier-Duberry enquired about.

In the end, Sheffield United moved for Romelle Donovan, who has impressed during his time at Bramall Lane thus far, while Portsmouth snagged Kavuma-McQueen. At the end of the season, the talented teenager will be set to return to Chelsea.

The Blades will be keeping tabs on him for next summer, and they should have their eye on these two as well, whether that be for the off-season or perhaps even the January window.

Ahead of the 2025/26 campaign, Kaine Kesler-Hayden was one of the most exciting young full-backs in the Championship, winning Preston North End's Player of the Year, Players' Player of the Year and Young Player of the Year while on loan from Aston Villa.

That prompted the previous year's play-off semi-finalists Coventry City to move for him, with it expected that he'd replace Milan van Ewijk, who was linked with an eight-figure move away to Bundesliga outfit Wolfsburg that same summer.

The Dutchman stayed, though, and Kesler-Hayden has played just 588 minutes of league action since the start of last season, three of which have come in one solitary cameo appearance in the Premier League this year.

Sheffield United should be in the market for a right-back in January, after the crushing news that Femi Seriki will likely miss the rest of the season following picking up a patella tendon rupture just six minutes after coming on at Charlton.

The 24-year-old needs a way out of Coventry soon, and the Blades should be able to provide that. If he performs well during an initial loan spell, perhaps he could even stake a claim for a permanent move as Sheffield United look to put plans in place to move forward.

Anis Mehmeti's decision to reject advances from Birmingham City and Wrexham to stay and fight for his place at Ipswich Town following promotion to the Premier League, while admirable, has turned out to be the wrong one.

Despite scoring in his only appearance this season in the EFL Cup against Premier League champions Arsenal, the 25-year-old hasn't even made a league squad for Gary O'Neil's side so far this year.

It's a real shame, as Mehmeti has been one of the more exciting creative midfielders in the Championship over the past couple of campaigns, netting 12 times to help Bristol City reach the play-offs in 2024/25, and then scoring a further 10 and assisting seven last year for both the Robins and then Ipswich, after making a January switch.

The Albanian international is on the cusp of being a Premier League-level player, but he hasn't been able to realise that at Portman Road, and will need to prove himself back in the Championship to get back to that level.

Sheffield United should be looking for upgrades both in the January window and beyond, and Mehmeti would provide that goal threat from midfield that the Blades lost this past summer when both Gustavo Hamer and Callum O'Hare left the club.

If Chris Wilder's side can come out of this difficult run of fixtures to end the calendar year still within touching distance of the top-eight, they should be doing all they can to try and bolster the squad within their means to get into the play-offs. These two would help them do so.
