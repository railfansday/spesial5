Avengers: Doomsday Shatters All-Time Disney Record In Just 24 Hours

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

It only took 24 hours for Avengers: Doomsday to break an all-time Disney record.

Ahead of the December 18 release in theaters, the Marvel Cinematic Universe movie is continuing to generate significant anticipation. This was only further amplified with the release of the final Avengers: Doomsday trailer, which features Cyclops/Scott Summers (James Marsden) shouting, "To me, my X-Men!" as he and his fellow heroes take on Doctor Doom (Robert Downey Jr.) and numerous Sentinels.

According to Deadline, Avengers: Doomsday's final trailer is the most-watched in Disney history after receiving 563 million views in just 24 hours. The movie beat its own record held by a previous trailer that landed 503 million views in the same amount of time. Doomsday now also has the second most-viewed trailer in movie history, only behind Spider-Man: Brand New Day's 718.6 million, but the record does not solely belong to Disney due to Sony's shared ownership of the web-slinger.

An important distinction is that Doomsday's new look got three times the viewership of Brand New Day's final trailer at 131 million. Final trailers tend to be watched less than their predecessors, as was the case with Brand New Day, which makes the new feat for Doomsday all the more notable.

In other metrics, the final Doomsday trailer had nearly a million mentions on social media in the first 24 hours, ranked as number one, two, and three on YouTube Movie Trending Charts, and got the highest concurrent viewership for the Doomsday Countdown Clock on livestream on YouTube with 306,000 views.

Trailer viewership and other online metrics don't always correlate to box office results, but given the massive numbers for Doomsday, setting more than one box office record seems highly likely at this point. The record-breaking trailer definitely ended up correlating with Brand New Day's box office, which went on to become the highest-grossing movie of 2026 and third highest-grossing of all time at $2.5 billion worldwide, only behind Avengers: Endgame ($2.924 billion) and Avatar ($2.923 billion).

Beyond being the first Avengers movie since 2019's Endgame, the confirmed characters in Avengers: Doomsday have contributed to much of the excitement, from Downey playing the titular villain to Marsden and other actors from Fox's X-Men movies reprising their roles and getting to interact with MCU heroes. Chris Evans is also playing Steve Rogers for the first time since Endgame, and Hailee Steinfeld is confirmed to be back as Kate Bishop after being introduced in Disney+'s Hawkeye five years ago.

Other characters appearing in Avengers: Doomsday include Peggy Carter (Hayley Atwell), Loki (Tom Hiddleston), Charles Xavier (Patrick Stewart), Magneto (Ian McKellen), Mystique (Rebecca Romijn), Nightcrawler (Alan Cumming), Gambit (Channing Tatum), Thor (Chris Hemsworth), Ant-Man (Paul Rudd), Captain America (Anthony Mackie), Shang-Chi (Simu Liu), Falcon (Danny Ramirez), Shuri (Letitita Wright), M'Baku (Winston Duke), Reed Richards (Pedro Pascal), Sue Storm (Vanessa Kirby), Johnny Storm (Joseph Quinn), Ben Grimm (Ebon Moss-Bachrach), Yelena Belova (Florence Pugh), Bucky Barnes (Sebastian Stan), Alexei Shostakov (David Harbour), Ava Starr (Hannah John-Kamen), John Walker (Wyatt Russell), and Sentry (Lewis Pullman).

Avengers: Doomsday releases in theaters on December 18.
