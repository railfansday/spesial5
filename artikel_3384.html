The 6 Most Outrageous GTA 6 Myths, Debunked

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The internet is already drowning in GTA 6 misinformation

After a wild opening weekend, Grand Theft Auto 6 is off to the races. Millions are playing Rockstar’s enormous open-world game and discovering its complexities in real time. There’s a lot to unpack in a game that’s filled with intersecting gameplay systems across a long story, and some players are already taking advantage of that. At the same time, the opening days of GTA 6’s launch have been filled with misinformation, much of it fueled by culture warriors and content creators looking to turn outrage into clicks on social media platforms that incentivize such behavior.

Because of that, there are a lot of myths about GTA 6 currently floating around the internet. Some come from harmless misunderstandings, while others are more deliberate misrepresentations intended to scare off potential players. To help you navigate the noise, we’ve rounded up some of the most rampant myths currently circulating around GTA 6 and fact-checked them accordingly.

The claim: GTA 6’s story forces you to do chores for Lucia instead of crimes.

If you spent launch day watching reactions to GTA 6’s early hours roll in instead of playing, you might have walked away thinking that Rockstar made a chore simulator. That’s thanks to a few early missions that revolve around Jason getting his home ready for Lucia as she’s about to be released from prison. Players will paint a bedroom for her, fix a leaky roof, and repair some stairs. There’s also an optional quest where players can run errands to prepare for Lucia's arrival, like picking up groceries or buying her a necklace. All of this happens in the first few hours of the game. GTA 6 quickly ramps up after that, getting into the series’ usual flow of cop chases, heists, and shootouts. You can call it a slow start, but know that any mundane missions you’ve seen make up a very small amount of story that you’re likely to see in your very first play session.

The claim: GTA 6 doesn’t allow you to steal cars, or heavily restricts what you can steal.

While there’s a grain of truth to this, it has been heavily exaggerated on social media. There are some select cars that you can’t steal in GTA 6, like those around Jason’s neighborhood or in an NPC’s car lot. But for the most part, you can jack just about any vehicle you see in Leonida. The catch is that you’ll need a device that can copy keys to get into some of the more expensive cars, and you won’t get that until Chapter 3. Being able to steal anything requires some patience as you play through the story, but be rest assured that you can do as much grand theft auto as ever in Grand Theft Auto.

The claim: Airplanes will bounce off of buildings instead of exploding on impact.

This one is a little tricky to unpack because there seem to be two weird things colliding here. After videos of airplanes bouncing off of skyscrapers like rubber began to emerge on social media, players deduced that content creators were sharing videos where they had their Reduced Vehicle Damage setting turned on. It seemed like a textbook case of outrage farming, though there may be a slight bit of truth here. Some players have speculated that GTA 6 may have a few physics bugs that make it tough for certain airliners to blow up as expected. That may be an edge case, but I can confirm that I have crashed plenty of planes into buildings in GTA 6, and they have all exploded into a fiery wreck so far.

The claim: GTA 6 features no autosave, and you will lose progress if you don’t manually save.

When players started reporting that they were losing hours of gameplay because GTA 6 didn’t have an autosave feature, I was very confused. I can open my save file right now and see a dedicated auto save slot that is regularly refreshed as I play. I initially wrote it off as players either lying or not realizing that the auto save only triggers after completing major missions, but it turns out they weren’t hallucinating. GTA 6’s first patch fixed an issue where auto save wasn’t triggering properly. So, yes, GTA 6 does have autosave; it just wasn’t working correctly for some players at launch. It shouldn’t be a problem now, but always remember to save early and often just in case.

The claim: Lucia is cheating on Jason during the story.

This one is downright bizarre. One of the first talking points that came out about GTA 6 was that Jason is a “cuck” who does not realize that Lucia is cheating on him. As someone who has played through the story, I spent days trying to figure out where in the world that blatant lie could be coming from. It turns out it was a good old misinterpretation. Early in the story, Lucia sends a text to an unknown character, which she rushes to hide from Jason when he enters the scene. While some have interpreted that as Lucia having a secret lover, it actually turns out that she was texting a friend from prison. To players’ credit, the scene does feel like it’s built to make you think Lucia is hiding a big secret from Jason, so that reaction might have been exactly what Rockstar was hoping to provoke.

The claim: GTA 6 does not allow you to open fire and kill people in a gay club.

The ugliest rumor to come out of GTA 6 so far is that the game will not allow you to walk into a gay club in Vice City and kill people. There was even a clip going around social media that seemed to support that, sparking some grotesque reactions. The truth is that the location in the video isn’t specifically a gay club. It is a club, and it’s true that you can’t open fire in it, but that’s only because you can’t shoot inside any establishment in GTA 6. This claim would technically be true if there was a gay club in the game, but only as a side effect of the wider systems working the way they do. What is true, though, is that the game features an unkillable non-binary character, according to the actor who portrayed them.
