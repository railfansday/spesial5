Clint Eastwood Nearly Directed A Star Is Born With Beyoncé

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Bradley Cooper and Lady Gaga's chemistry helped make 2018's A Star Is Born a success, but its musical performances were just as important. Cooper made his directorial debut playing Jackson Maine, a country-rock musician dealing with alcoholism. Gaga played Ally, an undiscovered singer who catches Jackson's attention at a drag bar. Their romance develops alongside Ally's rising career, creating tension as Jackson's struggles worsen. Their performance of "Shallow" marks a turning point, as Jackson brings Ally onstage to perform her original song, introducing her talent to a much larger audience and helping launch her career.

However, the version audiences eventually saw was far from the only one being considered. Years before Cooper stepped behind the camera, Clint Eastwood was attached to direct his own remake of A Star Is Born, with Beyoncé set to play the leading lady (via Los Angeles Times). The pairing was an interesting one, especially given Eastwood's longstanding interest in jazz and Beyoncé's career as one of the biggest names in contemporary music (via Shadow and Act). It was also a serious project backed by Warner Bros.

Eastwood's remake never made it into production, and the project underwent several changes before Cooper brought his interpretation to theaters (via BAFTA). Although Eastwood's version would have followed the same basic story, his approach to the music and Beyoncé's involvement could have given the film a different identity. Each major version of A Star Is Born has reflected the entertainment industry of its time, and Eastwood had specific ideas about the music he wanted to explore. His plans for Beyoncé and the circumstances that eventually brought Cooper aboard reveal how differently the 2018 remake could have turned out.

When Eastwood signed on to direct A Star Is Born in 2011, Beyoncé had already been attached to the project for several years. According to TheWrap, the remake had gone through several potential directors before Eastwood became involved. Screenwriter Will Fetters had developed a version focused on a rising female singer and an older, fading performer, although the production had yet to secure its male lead.

Eastwood also had specific ideas about the film's music. In a 2012 interview reported by Shadow and Act, he discussed Beyoncé's interest in writing songs for the movie and his desire to introduce her to jazz. He even compared her vocal abilities to those of Sarah Vaughan and Ella Fitzgerald, suggesting a musical direction quite different from Cooper's eventual adaptation.

In Cooper's film, Jackson's country-rock sound contrasts with Ally's transition toward mainstream pop, capturing the growing distance between their careers. Eastwood's interest in jazz could have given the characters a different sound, especially if Beyoncé contributed original songs. Although the full soundtrack was never produced, his comments suggest he wanted to explore a different side of her talents.

Clint Eastwood's A Star Is Born faced several setbacks before Beyoncé officially departed in October 2012. Production was initially reportedly delayed by her pregnancy, while scheduling conflicts and difficulties securing a male lead complicated the project further. According to the Los Angeles Times, several actors were considered for the role, including Tom Cruise, Leonardo DiCaprio, and Bradley Cooper. However, the musical movie never moved forward with Eastwood directing.

Cooper had actually been approached by Eastwood about starring in the remake, but initially felt he was too young for the role. After working together on 2014's American Sniper, Cooper eventually became interested in developing his own version of A Star Is Born. Interestingly, Beyoncé remained part of his plans for a time. During a BAFTA career retrospective, Cooper explained that he had written his adaptation with her in mind before considering Adele.

Ultimately, Cooper found his leading lady after watching Lady Gaga perform "La Vie en Rose" at a cancer fundraiser. Impressed by Gaga's performance, Cooper approached her about playing Ally, and the two began developing the film together. Their collaboration ultimately brought A Star Is Born to theaters in 2018, with a different director, leading actress, and musical direction than Eastwood had originally envisioned.
