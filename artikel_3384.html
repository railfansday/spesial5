10 Best Crime Movies To Watch Before GTA 6, Ranked

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto is arguably a cultural phenomenon at this point, transcending its video game origins. Grand Theft Auto VI is one of the biggest game releases of this console generation, if not of all time. It's made mainstream news worldwide, created by Rockstar, a development team whose brand is synonymous with high-quality open worlds.

Red Dead Redemption 2 was Rockstar's most recent brand-new title. It was released in 2018 and has since received ports to newer platforms. The game tells an epic Western story, highly praised for its bustling world, impressive character building, and extraordinary attention to detail.

Grand Theft Auto V was released several years earlier, in 2013. It tells an intense crime story split between three characters, Michael, Franklin, and Trevor. One of the game's big appeals is that each lead is very different, and you can switch between them on the fly after progressing past a certain point. San Andreas is a stunning location for a crime game, full of side activities and things to do. GTA Online further expanded the experience, letting players team up with friends.

It's not hard to see why GTA has such an impact on the gaming space, nor why GTA 6 has already set so many records before it's even released. Many are eagerly waiting for the launch, and if you're looking for a way to pass the time, there's many brilliant crime movies to prepare you for a hardcore gaming session, including genre classics and busy heist thrillers.

Snatch is an incredible Guy Ritchie crime movie with many moving parts and a phenomenal cast. Jason Statham stars as Turkish alongside his partner, Tommy (Stephen Graham). He's an illegal boxing promoter who soon finds himself in the pocket of Brick Top (Alan Ford), a brutal crime boss.

The boxing story is just one aspect of the movie, as numerous stories converge into a far grander tale. Snatch opens with a diamond heist, which draws the attention of various mobsters, including Boris the Blade (Rade Šerbedžija) and Bullet Tooth Tony (Vinnie Jones).

Snatch starts off our list because it's set in the UK, with an entirely different setting from GTA. Nevertheless, the colorful cast of over-the-top characters would all fit comfortably into a GTA story, and much of the dialogue is endlessly quotable.

Miami Vice is notably slower-paced than many movies on this list, though it offers a gritty peek into the criminal underworld that makes it an easy recommendation. Jamie Foxx and Colin Farrell star as Ricardo Tubbs and Sonny Crockett, two skilled detectives who go deep undercover to bust Montoya (Luis Tosar), a dangerous crime lord.

Tubbs and Crockett learn that several law enforcement branches are compromised, so it's a high-risk mission with limited support. The situation becomes even more complicated when Crockett gets emotionally involved, and the stakes continue to rise.

Michael Mann is a legendary crime-movie director who also helmed another movie on this list. Miami Vice is notably more grounded than your typical GTA story, but it offers a gripping, raw take on a crime and shady dealings with dangerous people, where one false move could be your last.

We're dramatically switching gears with Baby Driver, which more closely matches GTA's over-the-top vibe. The 2017 movie stars Ansel Elgort as Baby. He works for Doc (Kevin Spacey) as a getaway driver, with a unique approach to his job.

Viewers are introduced to Baby's incredible driving skills from the moment the movie starts. A bank robbery quickly draws the cops' attention, and the thieves rush inside his bright red Subaru Impreza WRX. A stylish chase ensues, as Baby effortlessly drifts around corners and weaves in and out of heavy traffic. He also finds an ingenious way to lose the police despite his colorful vehicle.

The stylish driving is a massive highlight of Baby Driver, but the crime story is also thrilling and fast-paced. Players will be doing their fair share of driving in San Andreas, though they may be hard-pressed to match Baby's skills.

Den of Thieves is an intense crime thriller starring Gerard Butler as Big Nick. He's a tough detective with a fearsome reputation, hunting a deadly group of thieves.

Those thieves are skilled and ambitious, led by Ray Merrimen (Pablo Schreiber). Ray's crew is planning a heist at the LA Federal Reserve. The question is, will Big Nick stop them, or will Ray and his team pull it off? Big Nick is also targeting a bartender, Donnie (O'Shea Jackson Jr.), who could be a weak link with connections to Merriman.

Heist missions are a fundamental part of GTA, so we can expect something similar to occur in GTA 6. Den of Thieves also takes plenty of time to explore the heist preparations while the cops do everything they can to figure out what Ray and his team are up to.

Bad Boys II is a buddy cop action classic, starring Will Smith and Martin Lawrence as Mike Lowery and Marcus Burnett. The pair are Miami detectives investigating a dangerous Cuban drug cartel run by Johnny Tapia (Jordi Mollà).

The movie boasts some impressive action scenes and shootouts. It's also a lighthearted film, full of laugh-out-loud moments and some brilliant exchanges between Mike and Marcus. Our heroes may be on the right side of the law, but their methods are anything but by-the-book.

The action in Bad Boys II is far grander than the original, with a story closer to GTA than the undercover plot of the first movie. That said, if you enjoy one Bad Boys movie, you'll likely want to watch them all, as Smith and Lawrence remain charismatic leads throughout.

There's a case to be made for several Fast and Furious movies to be watched ahead of GTA 6, though Fast Five features a wild heist and crime story as over-the-top as you can expect from any Grand Theft Auto game.

Dom Toretto (Vin Diesel) and his crew plan a daring heist in Brazil, setting their sights on Reyes (Joaquim de Almeida), a dangerous crime boss. Dom's crew targets a massive vault with an elaborate plan that includes some particularly stylish driving. Agent Hobbs (Dwayne Johnson) is also after the team, which makes the situation much more complicated. It's his first appearance in the franchise, and is such a great addition he has become a franchise mainstay.

Fast Five is as ridiculous and frenetic as you'd expect from the long-standing series. The movie is an easy watch where there's never a dull moment, with a great setup and a brilliant payoff.

One huge aspect that makes GTA so unforgettable is the characters themselves. In GTA V, for example, Michael gives a shocking description of what Trevor is like early on, and he doesn't disappoint.

Pulp Fiction is widely regarded as one of the greatest crime movies of all time. It boasts a phenomenal cast, including Bruce Willis, Uma Thurman, Samuel L. Jackson, and John Travolta. The movie tells several interconnected stories involving hitmen, gangsters, and even rigged boxing matches.

Pulp Fiction is endlessly quotable, with larger-than-life characters, including Jackson's portrayal of Jules and his legendary "Does he look like a *****?" speech. There's never a dull moment, and the movie remains a classic in Quentin Tarantino's filmography.

Pain & Gain is an action comedy based on a true story. Mark Wahlberg stars as Daniel Lugo. He's a Miami bodybuilder who believes he's destined for better things. He enlists the help of two other bodybuilders, Paul Doyle (Dwayne Johnson) and Adrian Doorbal (Anthony Mackie), to participate in a criminal scheme.

Daniel plans to kidnap Victor Kershaw (Tony Shalhoub). He's a wealthy businessman, and the trio intend to kidnap him and force him to transfer his riches to them. The plan takes a dark turn, with a failed attempt on Kershaw's life.

Pain & Gain is so outlandish that it wouldn't be out of place as a mission in Grand Theft Auto VI. The setting is similar, and the characters are just wild enough to be both exciting and believable.

Heat is the second Michael Mann movie on this list, starring Robert De Niro as Neil McCauley. He's a professional thief, though a messy job draws the attention of Vincent Hanna (Al Pacino), a relentless detective. McCauley and his crew decide to go ahead with one final heist with a potentially massive payout, even though the risk is greater than ever.

Mann takes time to explore every main character's motivations, including Val Kilmer's Chris Shiherlis, who has a gambling addiction. It's a busy movie, but it makes the list for its jaw-dropping heist shootout scene.

One particular shootout is legendary in the crime genre. The scene feels grounded and genuine as gunshots echo across the street. The ensuing firefight and chase are just as thrilling, as we have no idea who will survive. Heat isn't as fast-paced as Fast Five or Bad Boys II, for example. Still, it's widely regarded as a masterpiece and arguably required viewing for crime movie fans.

Scarface is a phenomenal crime movie featuring arguably Al Pacino's greatest role. He stars as Tony Montana and arrives in Miami from Cuba. He has grand ambitions, and the movie follows his incredible rise to power in the criminal underworld. It's a feat only matched by his colossal downfall.

Pacino is incredible as Montana, delivering numerous unforgettable lines that have become legendary in the crime genre. The movie tells a rags-to-riches story in many ways. Montana climbs the criminal ladder, quickly surpassing those he once served. He eventually has everything he could ever dream of, which makes his downfall even more mesmerizing.

Montana's rise through the ranks in Scarface makes the movie a must-watch for any GTA 6 fans. It's easy to draw similarities between Montana and various protagonists in the GTA franchise, and it might just serve as a cautionary tale if you do end up running a drug empire in the game.
