'Blade Runner 2099' Stars Reveal the Secret to Being "More Human than Human"

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Prime Video show's stars explain the difference between humans and Replicants

The Prime Video show's stars explain the difference between humans and Replicants

In the world of Blade Runner, it is nearly impossible to tell the difference between a human and a Replicant, an artificial being created to serve as slave labor and marketed as being "more human than human." In Ridley Scott’s 1982 film Blade Runner and Denis Villeneuve’s 2017 sequel Blade Runner 2049, Blade Runners are tasked with hunting rogue Replicants posing as humans. That job is flipped in Prime Video’s Blade Runner 2099, where Replicants have taken over Los Angeles and hunt human fugitives. But either way, the question remains: How do you tell the difference between a human being and a near-perfect approximation? We spoke to the cast to find out.

Blade Runner left it ambiguous whether Rick Deckard (Harrison Ford) was human or Replicant, while Blade Runner 2049 focused on K (Ryan Gosling), a Replicant hunting his own kind. Blade Runner 2099 puts a new spin on that formula with Cora (Hunter Schafer), a human fugitive pretending to be a Replicant Blade Runner so she can steal evidence from their headquarters.

“It was quite the task,” Schafer tells Polygon. “I felt like I was juggling a few balls through this performance. Cora has to perform being a Blade Runner in spaces full of Blade Runners and Replicants. I can’t do anything too big, but I also need to let the audience and the camera know what’s going on inside Cora’s head. It was this tricky oscillation between smaller, more minute choices and going big when it felt right and wasn’t going to get Cora caught. It got easier the more we did.”

Lewis Gribben has far more freedom as Cora’s brother, Elias, who despises Replicants and longs to help humanity regain control.

“I was allowed to be emotional and freak out and then be really impulsive, angry, and frustrated,” Gribben says. “I was allowed to just feel normal human emotions, so that was pretty easy for me.”

Michelle Yeoh plays Olwen, a Blade Runner with only eight days before her programmed expiration date. For inspiration, she looked to the examples set by the original Replicants: Blade Runner’s Rachael, Pris, and Roy Batty.

“It seems like a Replicant was evolving nonstop, trying to discover who they were,” Yeoh says. “By 2099, the war is over and the Replicants are now in charge. They’re no longer trying to find out who they are. They are the dominant species, so they have that sense of confidence. When you are a Blade Runner, not just a Replicant, you are hunting the humans and stronger and faster than any other species.”

House of the Dragon star Matthew Needham embraced that power as Yahya, a ruthless Blade Runner who works alongside both Cora and Olwen.

“One of the things that's fun about being a Replicant is you get to be really rude,” Needham says. “I just sort of cleared my brain of any sort of desire to please anybody or to respect anyone's personal space. That's really fun when you can just get up in people's grills and not worry about it.”

While Yahya and Olwen both radiate menace and sport similar outfits and guns, Yeoh wanted their characters to be a study in contrasts.

“Her sidekick, Yahya, is very jealous of her,” Yeoh says. “He’s always making big noises and sounds like a spoiled brat because he wants to be noticed. Olwen didn’t feel the need to be noticed. She knows they know she’s there. She was almost like a scary serpent. You don’t know when she’s going to strike at you.”

That attitude is especially terrifying for Cora, who has to work closely with Olwen to find her former partner, Marek (Johnny Harris). The two meet after Cora is forced to take a version of the Voight-Kampff empathy test, a tool originally used to find Replicants but now focused on ensuring Blade Runners don’t have too much sympathy for the humans they are policing.

“Olwen didn’t know that Cora was pretending,” Yeoh says. “She passed her index with such flying colors. She was more Replicant than Replicant. Olwen was fascinated by these incredible results. I suddenly go up very close to [Cora’s] face and [she’s] like, ‘Ohh shit. What am I supposed to do? If I flinch, she will know.’ I loved that. When I turned around, I could see her flicker like ‘Oh shit.'” It was so good. Those were the neat little nuances that we worked out between us. It was like Olwen was always watching.”

Blade Runner 2099 is streaming now on Prime Video.
