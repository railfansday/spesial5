Michael Connelly’s Bosch Replacement’s Next Release Is Just 6 Months Away

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Cher Thompson is a Senior Staff Writer at Screen Rant, where she covers everything from explosive reality TV moments to cast interviews about the twists and turns of scripted dramas. With a background in creative writing, marketing, and teaching, Cher knows how to spot both the storytelling genius and the guilty pleasures in pop culture. Her work blends wit with insight, making readers feel like they’re chatting with a friend who just happens to know way too much about TV.

Over the past several decades, Michael Connelly has taken his time establishing Harry Bosch as one of crime fiction's most recognizable figures, but his closest replacement character's next release is sooner than you'd think. While Bosch was first introduced in the early 1990s with The Black Echo, Connelly has worked with the character and continued to build the universe for well over 30 years, bringing him into the present day. While the author's Bosch was a successful series in the literary world, nothing has helped boost the character into the cultural lexicon more than the Prime Video live-action TV adaptation.

When Prime Video began adapting Bosch into a series in 2014, the character broke containment and became one of the most popular TV investigators of the modern streaming era. With a specific point of view of policing in today's world and an incredible portrayal of the character from Titus Welliver, the series quickly rose in the rankings and stayed on the air for seven seasons before transitioning into a secondary series, Bosch: Legacy, which ran for another three seasons. With Bosch coming to a close in 2025, the decade that the series was on the air did wonders for the books, which have sold more than 92 million copies worldwide.

Connelly's tremendous character may be his most notable, and while the Bosch series continues on the page, the author's most promising replacement character has another release coming out in just six months. With his newest character in the Catalina series, Connelly's introduced his readers to a whole new detective in Steve Stilwell.

Although Bosch himself is still alive and well in the literary world, Connelly has branched out into other characters consistently throughout his career, most notably writing The Lincoln Lawyer series with Mickey Haller at the center, as well as the Ballard series, which follows detective Renée Ballard. Both The Lincoln Lawyer and Ballard have been adapted into successful TV projects as well, but his newest work is still finding its footing, despite already getting the green light for a TV adaptation of its own. The Catalina series, which follows detective Steve Stilwell as he solves cases in a sleepy California island town, is a much different swing than his previous work.

Connelly's Catalina series, which will be adapted for HBO Max by Connelly and TV legend David E. Kelley, currently consists of two novels: 2025's Nightshade, which introduced the character and brought his first intense investigation to life, and 2026's Ironwood, which introduced Stilwell to Renée Ballard. Connelly's larger interconnected universe of characters does mean that eventually, Stilwell and Bosch could come face to face. In building a very different franchise, the author has brought Stilwell to a more remote, rustic location on Catalina Island, and has made his work feel vastly different than the intensity of Bosch's, or the pace of Ballard's.

With Connelly's Catalina series kicking off on the right foot, another piece of the larger puzzle will be coming out in 2027, just six months from now. Stilwell is set to return in Daybreak, Connelly's next title within the series, which will explore another case on the island that's changed the detective's life. Daybreak is set to release on May 25, 2027, and while there hasn't been much news about what to expect from the plot, the novel will continue Stilwell's story, which is setting him up to be the prime Bosch replacement as he continues the series in sunny Catalina.

While Stilwell and Bosch share some commonalities because of their work, both investigators have a vastly different approach to what they do. With Connelly having explored Bosch as a character for decades, there's far fewer surprises on the table for readers when it comes to learning things about his past or his process. Stilwell, on the other hand, is a far newer character for Connelly to dive into. This means that novels like Daybreak are working hard to establish who he is, how he works, and why readers should invest themselves in his stories. Thankfully, Connelly's newest Bosch replacement isn't far off from releasing new material.
