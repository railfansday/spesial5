Chrissy Teigen Opens Up About First Christmas Without Her Dad: ‘I Don’t Exactly Know What To Expect’

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The holiday will bring a mix of joy and sadness as she honors her father through family traditions.

Chrissy Teigen is known for her candid, filter-free personality and playful, unpretentious comfort food, especially around the holidays. But this Christmas will be different. In an exclusive interview with Delish, the cookbook author shares how she's preparing for her first holiday season without her dad.

Teigen announced the death of her father, Ron Teigen Sr., over the summer in an emotional Instagram post, per People. “Yesterday I woke up with a daddy and went to bed without one,” Teigen wrote in her caption.

In the caption, she described the shock of losing him in the middle of an otherwise ordinary day.

“All the same things happened. Hurried shower. Picked up stuffing from the dog toys. Answered some emails. Avoided some. Laughed with my friends and put on a pretty dress for a friend's birthday. Took a video. How cute is this dress!” she continued. “And then my dad just like...f---ing died.”

She admitted that she wasn’t prepared for how hard the loss would hit.

“I thought that since we talk about it and I’ve come to terms with him always having been an ‘old dad’ that I wouldn't have fallen to the ground the way I did. I’ve thought about this phone call for years. I’ll be ready,” she wrote.

This will be her first Christmas without her father, and Chrissy says it will be emotionally complicated.

“[Christmas] will be filled with so much joy, but it’s going to be an emotional one for me, of course. To have that big yellow chair that my dad loved so much, empty...I don’t exactly know what to expect from me emotionally, but I can imagine it will be all the things that make the holidays so complex: sad and wonderful and special all at the same time.”

Chrissy plans to pay tribute to her dad in part by cooking the scalloped potatoes her family makes every year. “We will bake, with love, the potatoes Dad loved so, so much.”

Chrissy also reflects on other family members they have lost. John Legend’s grandmother, Marjorie Maxine Stephen, passed away in 2021 at 91. “We will clean the greens the way Granny (on John’s side) told us to. I’m sure our eyes will well up. It’ll be a testament to how much they meant to us all.”

John and Chrissy share four children—Luna, 10, Miles, 8, Esti, 3, and Wren, 3—and are looking forward to staying home for the holidays. The kids will have time to participate in one of the family’s favorite activities: crafting. “I wanted to stay home for the holidays this year because I need all my children to start hand-making their ornaments and putting them on the tree,” Chrissy says.

“I’ve entered this time in my life where I want Christmas to be completely whimsical and over the top. I want to have a cute, colorful, chaotic Christmas this year.”
