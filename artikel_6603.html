Taylor Swift Brings Friend Karlie Kloss to the Chiefs Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Taylor Swift is no stranger to attending a Kansas City Chiefs game. During the football team’s peak season, you can often find the superstar cheering on her husband Travis Kelce, a tight end, from the VIP boxes. (Usually wearing thematic red and white merch, no less.) But tonight, as the Chiefs faced off against the New York Jets at Kansas City’s Arrowhead Stadium, Swift brought along a special friend to spectate the exciting match: Her longtime friend, Karlie Kloss.

The pop singer was spotted wearing a red sweater (go Chiefs!) and a statement necklace in her private box, while sitting beside Kloss and her parents, Andrea and Scott Swift. Swift and Kloss have been close friends since the early 2010s—and they even shared a Vogue cover together in 2015—though more recently, they have been rarely photographed together. (There have been rumors for years of a brief rift between the two friends, though neither party has ever confirmed this.)

It makes sense that Swift was on-hand at tonight’s game, and brought along a special friend for the occasion. There was lots to celebrate, after all, given the game marked Kelce’s 200th match in the NFL. So, did you really think Swift would miss such a milestone? For every Taylor Swift concert that Kelce shows up for, the singer will be in the box, dressed in red with a best pal.
