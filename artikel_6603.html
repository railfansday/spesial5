Totally Buggin'! Paid Nails Are Here for Fall

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Move over, fruit nails: this festive season, which means it’s time to go in a different direction with our nail designs. The inspiration? Ralph Lauren Christmas, Linda Evangelista’s 1991 Scotland shoot, and obviously Cher’s iconic yellow suit. The trend is both warm and chic, seasonal and statement-making.

The plaid nails trend can be more than just a vibe, though. Try finding inspiration from heritage prints and textured knits—all the things you’ll be wearing in for the foreseeable. It’s sleek but striking, a combo of criss-crossed stripes in various lengths and widths. While you can go totally classic, plaid nails are also completely customizable, with lots of opportunity to get creative and add your own personal spin with the colors and line styles you or your nail tech paints on. Maybe your plaid nails come in that familiar curation of burgundy, cream, and navy blue, or you mix it up with lilacs and pinks.

The preppy trend has long been permeating fashion, where there’s been no escaping the scholarly tropes of collegiate knit sweaters, check kilts, loafers, and classic trench coats. Think Michael Rider’s cinched Argyle knits in his Resort collection for Celine, Ralph Lauren’s ever-enduring allegiance to check, and The Row’s own selection of knits. It was only a matter of time before the school of prep swept beauty too—be the best student in class, and match your next set to your polo and skirt.

Kylie Jenner looked to her go-to nail artist, Zola Ganzorigt, for her own striped manicure. Ganzorigt—who is always first on the trends or coining them herself, a la Hailey Bieber’s glazed donut nails—opted for a colorful selection of colors to create the look, with each long, almond-shaped plaid nail in a different combo: One chocolate and butter yellow, one lilac and moss green, one inky blue and gray.

Baltimore-based nail artist @nailology.101’s plaid nails take a more cool-toned approach, with a silvery base and gradient stripes in forest green, icy blue, and red—they look expensive.

Sarah Brezel showed off a colorful but classic palette, pairing plum and lilac, moss green and navy, deep cherry red and burnt orange.

Dress up a classic French mani with plaid print, like this fabulous set. Nail artist Pitrizz Gangcuangco says this was a more muted take, mixing a bold yellow plaid pattern on top of a neutral base. Gangcuangco calls the teddy bear charm a fun and cozy touch.

A cool-toned brown shade is the perfect staple for fall. Add in plaid details, like celebrity and Chanel nail artist Betina Goldstein did here, to make them feel a bit more seasonal and cozy. The white, black, and blue lines are the perfect combo that really pop against the chocolate shade.

Plaid inspo can come from anywhere. For this pink shimmer plaid look, nail artist Shannon Elias took inspiration from her client’s matching custom-made dress. To get that cat-eye shimmer, she used a magnet to add some depth. “I love how this set turned out and will never get tired of this pattern,” she says. “I’m already looking forward to recreating more coming into this festive season.”

As big proponents of mixing different designs, this combo of argyle prints and cat-eye finishes just speaks to us. Celebrity nail artist Queenie Nguyen uses deep blues and greens to really make the a bold statement with the plaid. And it’s just super fun.

This smooth, velvet finish is just so dreamy. Nail artist San Sung Kim says she was inspired by her favorite plaid sweater and opted for bright colors that still felt cozy. To get that velvet finish, she used a magnet for extra shine and glitter.

So how will you be styling your own plaid nails for fall? Whatever your aesthetic, if you’re embracing prep, be sure to prep your nails accordingly with a good shaping, cuticle tidy, and strengthening base coat. Start with a neutral base polish to make your design pop like a true plaid, and keep a super steady hand for that intricate striping. Go forth, prepsters!

How can someone create a plaid nail look at home?

“Anyone can create a plaid look with just a few velvet polishes or jelly polishes,” says Kim. She says you just need to paint on a few stripes of one color in one direction, followed by a different color in the same direction. Then finish by painting on different sizes of stripes and colors in another direction for added dimension.
