David Walliams: British Police Open Investigation Into Sexual Misconduct Allegations

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Following the allegations of sexual misconduct against David Walliams, London police announced Friday they have opened an investigation into the British comedian.

Earlier this week, The Guardian and ITV News published their report about Walliams, with more than 30 people accusing him of abusing his celebrity and star power to prey on women and teenagers.

Without naming Walliams, the Met Police confirmed to the BBC that they have opened an investigations following “a number of allegations of non-recent serious sexual offenses involving a man now in his 50s.”
