Trae Crowder Sets Comedy Interview Series ‘Help Me Out Here’ With All Things Comedy; Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz Is His First Guest

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Comedian Trae Crowder has teamed up with All Things Comedy for Help Me Out Here, a new weekly comedy/interview series set to premiere on Crowder’s YouTube channel on November 18.

The series follows Crowder as he tries to make sense of what the hell is going on, with help from big-city experts and his buddies back home in Tennessee. Each episode starts with a phone call between Crowder and a friend from back home. This sets him up for a conversation with a guest who knows the subject firsthand, before he calls back home to report what he found out. Topics range from UFOs and artificial intelligence to trad wives and money in politics. “There are a lot of things that get brought up culturally in this country that I find fascinating, but also that I know next to nothing about (AI, tradwives, the manosphere, etc.),” Crowder explained. “This show is my attempt to remedy that through conversations with people who know the answers and sharing what I’ve learned with my people back in Tennessee. Hopefully others find it as fun and interesting to listen to as it’s been to make it.” Watch on Deadline

Help Me Out Here was created and executive produced by Crowder, Sean Stack and Alex Byrnes. ATC serves as the show’s production and studio partner, with production based at its Burbank studio. Sam Griffel exec produces, alongside Billy Rosenberg and Drew Eller for All Things Comedy.

In the November 18 premiere, Crowder sits down (virtually) with Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz to discuss where Democrats go from here and what it takes to reconnect with voters in places like the one Crowder grew up in. Back on the phone, the conversation covers something more immediate: what it means to work just to cover the cost of daycare.

A nationally touring comedian, writer, best-selling author and political commentator who goes by the moniker “The Liberal Redneck,” Crowder’s comedy pairs his rural working-class upbringing with the liberal worldview he embodies. The comic released the specials Damn Boy! and Trash Daddy in 2023 and ’25, respectively. With over two million followers across Facebook, YouTube and X, and nearly 90 million views on YouTube, he is repped by Gersh, Artists First and Knol Hanly Garrett.

All Things Comedy’s podcast network is home to dozens of shows, including Monday Morning Podcast with Bill Burr, The Dollop with Dave Anthony and Gareth Reynolds, TigerBelly with Bobby Lee and Khalyla Kuhn, and Rick Glassman’s Take Your Shoes Off. Outside of podcasting, they recently produced the upcoming feature comedy Golf Trip with Live Nation and HartBeat, as well as Marie Faustin’s Why Are You Single? for Tubi and The Bad Game Show with Andrew Santino & Bobby Lee.
