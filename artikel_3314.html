IM8 Is David Beckham’s Answer to Late Nights, Long Flights, and Early Call Times

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Nobody keeps a schedule like a touring artist. Red-eyes, soundchecks, 2 a.m. sets, and a call time the next morning that doesn’t care how late the show ran. Fans see the stage, but the real work is keeping your body ready to do it all again tomorrow.

David Beckham has lived his own version of that life for 30 years, from the soccer pitch, to fashion weeks, global campaigns, and co-owning Inter Miami CF.

“I’ve often felt overwhelmed by how many different products it takes to truly support your body,” the sporting icon says. So he co-founded IM8 with health sciences company Prenetics, and the brand’s most complete product now carries his name: the Beckham Stack.

The Beckham Stack pairs two products. Daily Ultimate Essentials Pro packs 90+ ingredients across 16 supplement categories, and Daily Ultimate Longevity adds 10 compounds for long-term cellular health, including 600 mg of NMN.

Together, they cover what a demanding schedule wears down. You get 100 mg CoQ10 and B vitamins for energy, 30 mg saffron extract and choline for focus and sleep, and 2,500 mg of electrolytes to rehydrate after a night on stage or a long flight. Ten billion CFU probiotics and digestive enzymes support a gut that has been through three time zones and too much catering, while vitamin C, zinc, selenium, elderberry, and reishi round out immune support for the days you are shaking hands with a new city every night.¹

The co-owner list reads like a festival lineup. IM8’s shareholders include Diplo, Lily Collins, Jay Shetty, NBA star Giannis Antetokounmpo and tennis star Aryna Sabalenka, all of whom are co-owners through Prenetics. The brand is also the Official Health Supplements Partner of Inter Miami CF.

The star power comes with serious science too. IM8’s Scientific Advisory Board includes Dr. Dawn Mussallem of Mayo Clinic, former NASA Chief Scientist Dr. James L. Green and Prof. Suzanne Devkota of Cedars-Sinai.

In a 12-week randomized controlled trial of IM8’s Daily Ultimate Essentials formula, 95% of participants reported more energy, 85% reported less bloating, 80% reported better sleep and 75% reported sharper focus.² Essentials Pro is also NSF Certified for Sport, and IM8 holds a 4.5 out of 5 rating on Trustpilot based on more than 2,000 reviews.

Each product comes in a single-serve sachet that fits in a carry-on, a tour bus bunk, or a back pocket. Tear both open, mix with water, and your routine is done in about a minute. Flavors include Açaí + Mixed Berries, Mango Passion Fruit, and Lemon + Orange, all sweetened with fermented sugarcane instead of stevia.

The Beckham Stack is $139 per month on a quarterly subscription, compared with $261 for a one-time purchase. IM8 estimates the same ingredients bought as separate supplements would run about $502 per month.

Quarterly plans include a 90-day money-back guarantee and free shipping, HSA/FSA payments are accepted for eligible customers, and you can pause or cancel anytime.
