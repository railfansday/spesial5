EA Sports FC 27 Best Young Players: Highest Potential Wonderkids in Career Mode

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

EA Sports FC 27 is out now and that means you can dive straight into Career Mode. Here, building a successful team requires plenty of planning and one of the most important aspects in that respect is laying the foundations for the future so that a lasting legacy can be achieved.

Indeed, uncovering and nurturing the best young talents can be highly satisfying, especially if you help a player go from unheralded hot prospect to a world-class football genius known the world over. Whether it is finding the next Lionel Messi or emulating Sir Alex Ferguson by 'winning with kids', there is a lot of excitement in looking to the future on the field.

To help you on your way to trophies and acclaim in the game, GIVEMESPORT brings you all the best young players aged 20 and younger in EA Sports FC 27.

The two best young strikers in EA Sports FC 27 are, without doubt, Bournemouth's Eli Junior Kroupi and Real Madrid's Endrick. The pair each have a potential rating of 88 in the game, with Junior Kroupi's current rating marginally better than that of Endrick.

Two more intriguing and potentially affordable prospects are Italian wonderkid Francesco Camarda, who is on the books at AC Milan (known as Milano in FC 27), and German forward Alexander Staff, who plays for Bayer Leverkusen. At just 18 years of age in the game, their current rating is relatively low in the 60s, but they have a high ceiling in terms of potential.

💡 DID YOU KNOW? Endrick has been compared to Brazilian legends Ronaldo, Romario and Pele. However, the forward dismissed the comparisons, telling GQ in 2023: "Man, nobody is going to be Pele. He's the king of football."

Robin van Persie's son, Shaqueel van Persie, has been impressing for Feyenoord in the Eredivisie, and he has the potential to mature into one of the best strikers in FC 27, while one player who could have an immediate impact and also develop into a world-class star is Christian Kofane.

Seventeen-year-old Brian Madjo has shown his potential in the real world for Aston Villa. A physically imposing youngster, Madjo already possesses impressive strength and could mature into an effective target man.

Who will be taking Desire Doue's crown in 2026?

As has been the case in the real world and previous FC games, Lamine Yamal is already one of the best players, with a frightening current rating of 90, but his potential is set much higher at 95, meaning he is expected to reach heights only ever scaled by the likes of Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo, two of the greatest football players of all time, in this game series.

Chelsea winger Estevao and Real Madrid attacker Yan Diomande are the two midfield stars deemed closest to Yamal in terms of potential, with that pair each potentially reaching a rating of 90.

💡 DID YOU KNOW? Yan Diomande became the most expensive African footballer in history in 2026 when Real Madrid paid €125 million to bring him to the Santiago Bernabeu. The previous record was set in 2019 when Arsenal paid €80 million for Nicolas Pepe.

In central midfield, Paris Saint-Germain and France anchor Warren Zaire-Emery is the best young star, while the likes of Kennet Eichhorn, Lennart Karl, Nathan De Cat and Ayyoub Bouaddi are up there too, with potential ratings in the high 80s.

Two Moras - Rodrigo Mora at Roma and Mexico's wonderkid Gilberto Mora - are widely touted to be among the best CAMs in the sport in years to come. Konstantinos Karetsas has been impressive since moving to Borussia Dortmund in the summer and his value is underlined by the fact that he is in the running for the 2026 Golden Boy Award.

The most gifted young footballers on the planet have been ranked in order.

At 19 years of age, Pau Cubarsi is already a world champion with Spain and he has proven his worth as one of the best centre-backs in world football at Barcelona. He is, thus, the best young defender in EA Sports FC 27 by a distance with a 91 potential rating. Brighton's Luka Vuskovic is a close second according to the EA Sports FC 27 ratings wizards.

Feyenoord's Givairo Read and Arsenal's Myles Lewis-Skelly are deemed the best full-backs in the game, with the pair boasting a potential rating of 88 and 87 respectively. Interestingly, however, Lewis-Skelly is also capable of playing in midfield, so it will be interesting to see where his best role is in the game.

A wonderkid defender to look out for is is Georgian left-back Saba Kharebashvili. Despite having a relatively low current rating, his growth potential is phenomenal. Similar talent profiles include Kaua Prates at Borussia Dortmund and Daniel Banjaqui at Benfica. Such players may be more accessible for a mid-ranking team to recruit.

Surprisingly, Barcelona's famed La Masia doesn't rank at the top of the best academies in football.

It will come as little surprise to seasoned EA Sports FC players to learn that Dennis Seimen is the best young goalkeeper prospect in the game. The German shotstopper has a current rating of 72 and a potential rating of 87, meaning he could develop into a player of similar stature to the likes of Mike Maignan or Alisson, two of the best goalkeepers in the world right now.

Seimen's compatriot Florian Hellstern also has a high potential rating of 86, which would place him in a similar mould to the likes of Joan Garcia or Diogo Costa. Outside of Seimen and Hellstern, it is worth keeping an eye on Simon Eriksson and Aaron Yaakobishvili.
