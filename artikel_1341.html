15 Most Popular and Famous Footballers in the World 2026 (Ranked)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Football is the most universally popular sport in the world and attracts more television viewers than any other sport. But with such a level of scrutiny in the so-called beautiful game these days, it's no wonder that some of its greatest players have become some of the most famous names in the entire world.

Today, there is so much money in the sport that some of the most elite players earn even more through their brand deals and sponsorships than they do from the gigantic weekly wages they receive from their respective clubs. The more their brand increases, the more famous they become.

David Beckham was arguably the first footballer whose fame extended far beyond his footballing career, and he is still repeatedly in the limelight despite retiring over 10 years ago. There are numerous reasons for a footballer to become a genuine global superstar, and Beckham's appealing looks, coupled with his heroics in an England shirt meant he ticked almost every box.

This list looks at the 15 most famous and well-known footballers playing right now. Factors considered when ranking this list include:

The 20 best defenders on the planet right now have been ranked in order.

It would have been hard not to include Marcus Rashford's name in a list of the world's most famous players. Although the Englishman has been going through a difficult period on the green since returning to Manchester United from a successful loan spell with Barcelona, he is nonetheless one of the Kingdom's true darlings.

He owes this reputation as much to his history and career as a footballer as to his humanitarian actions, and his fight to enable disadvantaged children to live in decent conditions has been going on for many years. A role model, both on and off the pitch.

While there is no question that Luis Suarez's stock has greatly fallen in the last few years, he is still unquestionably one of the most talked about footballers of the 21st century, and not always for the right reasons. In spite of a truly glorious career, which has included well over 500 senior goals, it is the Uruguay international's scandals that make him stick in the mind so much.

Suarez disgraced himself at two consecutive World Cups, firstly denying Ghana the chance to become the first African team to reach the semi-finals of the competition after deliberately handling on the line in 2010, and then at the following tournament, biting Italy's Giorgio Chiellini, marking the third time he'd bitten a fellow player on the football pitch.

He was also accused of racism by Man United's Patrice Evra in 2011, so while he may not be the most liked footballer of all time, he is certainly one of the most notorious.

Karim Benzema may no longer be in the spotlight at Real Madrid, and doesn't have the opportunity to shine on the stage of the Champions League, but he is nonetheless one of the - French - players most appreciated and followed on the planet. It has to be said that the Ballon d'Or 2022 winner is doing everything he can to maximise his visibility.

His followers can continue to track his adventures despite leaving Saudi Arabian club Al-Hilal in 2026. After three years in the Saudi Pro League, Benzema's worldwide fame has only increased.

It has taken Cole Palmer no time at all to establish himself as one of the Premier League's true stars. A standout player in a stacked Chelsea attack these days, the former Manchester City man is continuing to hit new heights every week. In terms of marketability, he knows exactly what he's doing, too.

Trademarking his signature ‘Cold’ goal celebration, Palmer's brand is quickly becoming one of the most noticeable and popular around world football. And to think that the England international is yet to even reach his prime years. Simply put, there is still so much more to come.

Barcelona, Man United, Real Madrid, Liverpool and Bayern Munich all feature as the 16 clubs that pay the most wages in world football are revealed.

Michael Olise's rise from Crystal Palace star to Bayern Munich world-beater has been sensational to watch. Whilst his nonchalant act means he has to settle for a lower place on this list, there's no doubt that the Frenchman is quickly becoming one of the most popular players in the world.

Since joining Bayern Munich, that popularity has only increased too. The talented winger has as many as 12.2m followers on Instagram, where fans can find posts without even a caption. In what is becoming classic Olise fashion, he presents success without comment.

It's quite hard to believe that Erling Haaland is only in his mid-20s, given his formidable scoring record. The Norwegian forward started making headlines at the age of 19 with Red Bull Salzburg and was quickly snapped up by Borussia Dortmund, where he continued to shine, particularly in the Champions League.

In the summer of 2022, Haaland signed with Premier League champions Manchester City, and in his first season, he helped them win their first-ever treble, scoring 52 goals in 53 matches, and breaking the Premier League record for most goals in a single season.

He is arguably the best striker in the world right now, and also the most popular, having won an Instagram Rings Award in 2026 as one of 25 global and cultural winners.

Some might have thought that Vinicius Junior's influence would have diminished with the arrival of Kylian Mbappé at Real Madrid. They were wrong. For while he may no longer be the Spanish club's media poster boy, the Brazilian is nonetheless among the most famous.

Whilst that place in the spotlight has not helped the winger's struggles at times, it is something which comes with the part of playing one of the most talked about players on the planet.

An established star these days, the only reason that Vinicius Jr isn't higher on this list is because he's hit his ceiling.

For a long time, Harry Kane was one of the biggest faces in the Premier League, consistently challenging for the Golden Boot at Tottenham Hotspur before leaving for Bayern Munich in the summer of 2023. After nine years in the English top flight, he scored 213 goals, which places him second behind Alan Shearer in the list of all-time top goalscorers in the competition.

However, it's since then that Kane has become one of the most popular names around at Bayern Munich - winning the 2026 Ballon d'Or to confirm his status as the best player in the world.

The England captain often lets his football do the talking, but that's not been an issue in terms of reaching world fame.

Tracking the leading contenders to win the prestigious Ballon d'Or award in 2027.

Lamine Yamal is a rare case. Regarded as one of the brightest stars in world football, the Barcelona winger continues to impress fans around the world and is enjoying a sensational rise in popularity with every year

An indisputable starter for the Catalan club and the Spanish national team, which he helped to win at Euro 2024 and the 2026 World Cup, Yamal is a true star of the game.

Anyone who can predict how far the teenager will go, whose limits are still unknown, will be very clever. Only time will be able to tell. He may even top this list one day, especially if he wins the 2027 Ballon d'Or.

Mohamed Salah is arguably the pure definition of a world star. The forward is widely considered to be the best African footballer of all time, and his dazzling performances as part of Jurgen Klopp's all-conquering Liverpool side have made him one of the most recognisable sportspeople on the globe.

After failing to make it at Chelsea early in his career, Salah returned to the Premier League with Liverpool, before winning everything there is to win in English football.

Now at Trabzonspor in Turkey, the Egyption is so popular that a place from his Instagram post is now a tourist attraction. It doesn't get much more famous than that.
