DP World Tour DQs pro from Q-School for 'serious misconduct,' player responds

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The DP World Tour disqualified a pro Monday during the fourth round of its Q-School for "serious misconduct."

Frank Kennedy, a 21-year-old from England, was DQ'd following an incident where Monday Q Info's Ryan French, citing multiplesources, reported Kennedy "took a divot out of the 11th green. One player described it as a one foot divot about 4 feet behind the hole."

Kennedy was tied for 28th at 15 under following the first four rounds of the six-round event. The top-60 players made the cut after 72 holes, and then the top 15 and ties would receive DP World Tour cards via Category 18. The remainder of players will receive HotelPlanner Tour status.

Kennedy made par on the 11th, a par-5, and signed for a 3-under 69 in the fourth round. French reported Kennedy argued for a long time in scoring before being DQ'd.

He took to social media late Monday and expressed his disappointment with the penalty, eventually saying he wanted DP World Tour officials to review the punishment.

"I am hugely disappointed to have been disqualified from the Final Stage of DP World Tour Qualifying School," Kennedy posted late Monday on social media. "Reports today say this was for deliberately damaging the course.

"I did not intentionally or deliberately damage the putting surface, and any comments saying otherwise are wholly incorrect. On the 11th green I missed a birdie putt and tapped in. In frustration, as I was walking off the green, I swung my putter and it accidentally caught the surface. My playing partner and I repaired it straight away, and we were both satisfied it was properly fixed before we moved on. I should have called a referee to check the repair, and I've learned from that."
