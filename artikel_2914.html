Kim Caldwell 'loved start' in Lady Vols' win over Radford

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Tennessee (6-0) defeated Radford (3-3), 102-48, on Thursday at Food City Center.

The Lady Vols led, 67-16, at halftime and outscored Radford, 34-4, in the second quarter.

Tennessee outrebounded the Highlanders, 50-29, including 25 offensive and 25 defensive rebounds. The Lady Vols also outscored Radford, 54-20, for points in the paint.

Tennessee scored 44 bench points, 57 points off 38 Radford turnovers, 23 second chance points and 27 fast break points.

Third-year head coach Kim Caldwell discussed the Lady Vols' win after the game.

"I loved our start," Caldwell said. "I loved how hard we played. We played really well together. I think we did a much better job on the 50/50s. I think we did a much better job rebounding the ball on the box outs.

"That was a good response to see another good start on a one-day prep, so that's really good to see out of this team going forward."

Tennessee will next play on Nov. 22. The Lady Vols will host UCLA at Food City Center. Tipoff is slated for 5:30 p.m. EST (ESPN).
