Washington Commanders vs New York Giants: NFL Week 10 game chat and live discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Washington Commanders are on the road on a short week, and will be playing the New York Giants for the second time this season. The first game ended with a tie after the Commanders failed to kick a field goal before time ran out in overtime. There have been several head-scratching decisions made by players and coaches since then, and the team now sits at 2-5-1 with their season hanging on by a thread.

The Commanders are coming off a home loss to the Los Angeles Rams, and there were more questions raised during the final series where Jayden Daniels didn't attempt a pass into the end zone. The offense has been disjointed, and rookie offensive coordinator David Blough is under the microscope for the second half of the season.

The Giants are 5-2-1 and lead a weak NFC East heading into Week 10. They are down to J.J. McCarthy at quarterback, with veteran Jameis Winston backing him up. The Giants are getting Cam Skattebo back after a two-week absence due to a hamstring injury.

The Commanders are 1.5-point road underdogs, and need a win in New Jersey to maintain any hope for the playoffs. Dan Quinn is coaching for his job, and Jayden Daniels is just trying to get another win under his belt for the season. Thursday night division games can get really weird, and with the way this season is going, anything can happen during this primetime game.
