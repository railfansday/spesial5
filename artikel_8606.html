A romantic date night at home feels like a night out when you plan it like one. Set a start time, pick a theme, put both phones in another room, and spend on one small treat instead of a restaurant bill. Take turns planning, so one of you always gets to be surprised.

Dinner out costs what it does because you’re paying a restaurant to cook, serve, and clean up. The romance comes from the planning and the attention, and neither one costs anything at home.

Plenty of cheap evenings at home never feel like dates, because nobody planned them. So plan this one. Pick a start time and keep it the way you’d keep a reservation. Dim the lights, light candles, set the table with real plates, and put on music. Change out of what you wore all day, and leave both phones on silent in another room until the night is over.

Then choose one thing to spend on, such as a good steak, a better bottle of wine, or a bakery dessert, and keep everything else simple.

Say you go out once a month, and dinner for two with wine and dessert costs $120 with the tip. Cook a similar meal together at home, with a $15 bottle of wine as the treat and a homemade dessert by candlelight, and it costs about $35. You do the cooking and the dishes, and you keep $85 a month, or $1,020 a year.
