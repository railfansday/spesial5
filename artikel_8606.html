Get the Capital One Spark Classic for Business if your fair credit rules out better business cards. It charges no annual fee and reports to the personal credit bureaus, so paying on time with a low balance can help raise your score. With excellent credit, apply for the Spark Cash Select instead, which pays 1.5% back.

Capital One sells it to business owners with fair credit, which its guidelines define as a loan default in the past five years or less than three years of credit history. A side business can qualify, since a sole proprietor applies with a Social Security number in place of a tax ID.

Owners with fair credit or a short credit history get a no-fee business card, and each on-time payment goes on their personal credit report. The Spark Cash Select pays 1.5% back but asks for excellent credit, which, by Capital One’s guidelines, takes at least three years with a card or loan and a limit above $5,000.
