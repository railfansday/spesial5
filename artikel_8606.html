Sometimes scored tablets can be split easily by hand with no device. Place the tablet on a hard surface and firmly push on each side of the tablet with your fingers, with the score line in the middle. The tablet should break at the score. This is why there is a score line.

From experience, this works better with some tablets than others, but definitely worth a try as it is quick and easy.
