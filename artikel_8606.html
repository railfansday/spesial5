Open a my529 account if your state has no 529 tax break or gives one for any state’s plan. Utah’s plan charges about 0.1% a year on most of its ready-made portfolios and lets you build your own mix. If your state’s break requires its own plan, use that plan.

At a 5% yearly return, a family saving $200 a month for 18 years pays under $600 in my529’s quoted fees on a portfolio tied to the child’s college start year, by our math. The same family would pay about $1,600 at Morningstar’s 0.30% average for direct-sold plans, the ones you buy without an advisor.

Savers whose state has no 529 tax break, or one covering any state’s plan, get target enrollment portfolios at about a third of the average direct-sold fee and more say over their mix than most plans give. Residents of a state whose break requires its own plan should open that plan and claim the break.
