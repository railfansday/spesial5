If you've secured a DMZ Blackmarket Locker key, here are all the locations at which you can use it

You'll need to get yourself to one of the multiple MW4 DMZ Blackmarket Locker locations if you've found yourself in possession of a key for one, as it's the only way in. In contrast, if you've found a locker and want the goods inside, you might be wondering where to find the keys. Either way, I've got you covered below.

There are currently three Blackmarket Locker locations that I know about, and they're all marked on the map above, though you can see them in more detail in my more general guide to all MW4 DMZ keys. All of them are on the western side of the map, with two in the prison area and one in Deadtown.

Each location has two lockers in it, so if you've got two or more keys, you could take two with you each time to ensure you get a good look at everything on offer - just remember you can still only try and exfil with two MW4 guns. Alternatively, you could choose to just take one key with you and make the most of your weapon slots. I'd recommend taking a backpack with a secure slot and keeping your key in there until you use it, just in case.

Even without a key, these locations are great to check out as you can still find weapons and other valuable items in the locker room.

You can get keys for the Blackmarket Lockers in a couple of ways, one of which being finding them randomly by looting the map. A more reliable way of obtaining Blackmarket Locker keys is via Survival Kits that are unlocked through DMZ career progress, orders, and mission, as well as via the Battle Pass and events.

You'll acquire these Survival Kits, specifically Recon Equipment Kits, at levels 19 and 46 of your DMZ career. Once you've got them, you need to head to the Survival Kits menu in the DMZ lobby area, as seen in the image above, to claim them, at which point the items appear in your Stash.
