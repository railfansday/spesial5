'There Is No Truth to These Claims': Marvel 1943 Not Cancelled, But Doesn't Sound Great

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Marvel 1943: Rise of Hydra – the highly promising superhero game starring Captain America and Black Panther – has not been cancelled, despite rumours to the contrary earlier today.

A fan account claimed on X (or Twitter) that the project, fronted by former Uncharted lead Amy Hennig, had been scrapped. That post has since been deleted.

But Kotaku gained access to a message posted in dev Skydance Games’ private Slack channel, where executive producer Lu Gigliotti confirmed with leadership that the reports aren’t true.

The game – first announced in 2019 – was originally due out earlier this year, but has now been pushed back to 2027.

And based on further reporting from Kotaku, you may not want to get your hopes up too high for this one.

While the project is purportedly progressing and is poised to hit an “alpha” milestone soon, it’s allegedly over budget and has been scaled back significantly.

Curiously, journalist Rebekah Valentine reports that the title was originally intended for Netflix, but transitioned to consoles like PS5 during development.

As such, gameplay is said to be light, “primarily using only one button and lacking significant interaction”.

If you felt this year’s Marvel’s Wolverine was a bit basic, then it sounds like this may end up even more simplistic by comparison.
