Medabots Classics Plus coming to PC in December in Japan

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Publisher Imagineer and developer SUCCESS Corporation will release Medabots Classics Plus for PC via Steam in both Kabuto Ver. and Kuwagata Ver. in December in Japan for 5,478 yen each, the companies announced.

Medabots Classics Plus first launched for Switch on November 12, 2020 in Japan.

Here is an overview of the collection, via its store pages:

A historic collection of eight games from Medabots series.

This collection includes the five numbered games in the series, from Medabots to Medabots 5: Susutake Mura no Tenkousei, as well as Medabots Navi, Medabots G, and Medabots Two: CORE. Players can enjoy a diverse range of genres, spanning RPG, strategy RPG, and action.

While the original appeal of the games remain unchanged, the collection introduces convenient features like the ability to save and load at any time, a Robattle skip feature, and a high-speed mode. It also supports online battles and parts trading between both the Kabuto Ver. and Kuwagata Ver.

This is the definitive Medabots series collection.
