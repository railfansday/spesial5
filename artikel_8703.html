On Episode 310 of The Menace Podcast, host Dennis James was joined by Milos Sarcev, Chris Cormier, and special guest Terrence Ruffin, and the conversation moved towards the 2027 Mr. Olympia final. The man they call “Ruff Diesel” talked to the panel about the progress he needs to make, to finally win a title at the Olympia.

Ruffin, an iconic Men’s Physique competitor has appeared in nine Olympia finals, and qualified for his tenth by securing victories at the Mexico and Slovakia Pro events in October. Ruffin was at ninth place at the ‘O’ in 2016 and 2018 but found a career high placing of second in 2020 and 2021. He came fifth in 2026, so what does he need to work on, in order to progress in 2027?

“Do you believe in your heart that you can still win the Olympia,” asked host Dennis James. “100%” came the quick response from Ruffin, who explained that despite his vast experience, he is still very excited about that prospect. Ruff Diesel then provided a glimpse into the adjustments he may need to make, if he is to dominate bodybuilding’s biggest stage.

“My biggest issue is, I always end up doing too much,” he explained. “I told myself, this year, I’m going to say no to a lot more stuff.” Ruffin shared that his constant travel and business interests could be spreading him too thin. “But I’m trying to not do so much,” he relented.

“But you’ve started a new business too,” challenged The Menace. “You’re prepping for the Olympia and you’re opening up a gym. I mean, come on.”

Ruffin agreed that he takes on a lot. “I’m even (going to) have a meeting with the gym tomorrow, about handing off a couple of things,” he said. “Just to make sure I can focus like I need to.”

Ruffin says that he has learned to appreciate that getting to the pinnacle of bodybuilding requires his complete attention. “I’m giving up a part of the business at the gym, so I don’t have to do, like, the day to day,” he explained. The competitor now intends to keep his diary clearer by turning down events and reducing his travel. “I’m just all in, on just the gym training, and all of that.”

With a renewed focus on the prize, Milos Sarcev pushed further on what progress Ruffin will need to make with his physique, in order to win. “Conditioning, or size?” asked The Mind. “Both,” answered Ruffin, with a smile.
