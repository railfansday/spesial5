Providing remarks today are Ryals McMullian, Chairman and CEO; and Anthony Scaglione, our CFO.

Thanks, J.T., and thanks to everyone joining the call today. Our third quarter results reflect many of the challenging conditions we discussed last quarter, including pressure on household budgets, evolving consumer preferences and heightened competitive activity.

Overall, the operating environment remains demanding, and these conditions, along with areas where we need to move faster with focused execution, resulted in performance below our expectations.

As we discussed last quarter, constrained household budgets continue to drive price sensitivity with consumers placing greater emphasis on affordability in their purchasing decisions.
