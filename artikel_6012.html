What We Leave Out When We Talk About Sexism in Science

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The research on bias against women in STEM is far more mixed than the headlines suggest.

Imagine you’re a young woman considering a career in science. You’ve heard that when a man and a woman with identical qualifications apply for the same job, hiring committees tend to favor the man. You ask for evidence, and someone sends you a study. It’s a real study, published in a prestigious journal, and it says more or less what you were told. Case closed?

Steve Stewart-Williams is a professor of psychology at the University of Nottingham Malaysia.
