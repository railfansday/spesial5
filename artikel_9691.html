2 self-hosted apps for Indian businesses: CA Vault for CA firms and Toran for weddings

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Most business software today is a monthly subscription, and your data lives on someone else's cloud. For some Indian businesses that's a real problem:

CA Vault and Toran take a different approach. You pay once, install the app on your own hosting (ordinary cPanel shared hosting works), and your data never leaves your server. Neither app has telemetry, and neither needs to "phone home" to keep working.

CA Vault runs the whole CA office in one place: clients, compliance deadlines, documents, passwords, DSC tokens, notices and billing.

Price: one-time, from ₹7,999 (GST included), with unlimited users and clients. 12 months of updates are included, and after that the software keeps working.

👉 cavault.rsflowhub.com · GitHub page with screenshots & docs

Toran puts a whole Indian wedding (Sagai, Mehndi, Haldi, Sangeet, Lagna, Reception) in one app. All modules share one guest list: an RSVP updates the seating, the seating shows at the gate, and the shagun shows on the family.

Languages: English, हिन्दी, ગુજરાતી and मराठी, chosen per user.

Price: one-time, from ₹9,999 (GST included), with every module and no per-guest or per-event fees. Wedding planners get white-label plans: your name, your logo, and use for your clients' weddings.

👉 Try the live demo: a sample wedding with 300 guests, no sign-up needed · toran.rsflowhub.com · GitHub page

Both are built with Laravel 12, Vue 3 and Tailwind. The release ZIP needs no Composer, npm, Node, Redis or SSH on the server: you upload it, open the installer, and you're running in about 15–20 minutes.

Know a CA or a wedding planner still running everything on Excel and WhatsApp groups? Send them this post 🙂
