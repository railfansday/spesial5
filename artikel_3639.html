Miley Cyrus Marries Fiance Maxx Morando in Cali Wedding After 5 Years of Dating

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Miley Cyrus made good on her promise. Literally.

The “Let’s Get Married” singer, 33, reportedly wed her fiancé, Maxx Morando, in an intimate ceremony in Palm Springs, California, on Saturday, November 21, after five years together, per Entertainment Tonight. Miley’s mom, Tish Cyrus and her sister Noah Cyrus were reportedly among the loved ones in attendance.

Cyrus and Morando, 28, struck up a romance after meeting on a blind date in the summer of 2021.

“Well, it was blind for me and not really for him,” she told British Vogue in May 2023. “I thought, ‘The worst that can happen is I leave.’”

Us confirmed in December 2025 that Cyrus and Morando were engaged after she was spotted at the Avatar: Fire and Ash premiere in Los Angeles wearing a cushion cut diamond ring set in a 14K yellow gold band designed by Jacquie Aiche. An insider told Us at the time that the proposal had taken place in early November 2025.

This is the second marriage for Cyrus. She previously tied the knot with Liam Hemsworth in December 2018 before separating from the actor in August 2019 and finalizing their divorce in January 2020.

Despite her past experience with matrimony, Morando altered Cyrus’ perspective on the institution.

“Miley never thought she would get married again, and Maxx changed everything for her,” a source exclusively told Us in December 2025. “They have a really healthy and private relationship.”

Later that month, Cyrus opened up about her engagement to Morando, sharing that he had popped the question during a trip to Asia.

“I actually had never gone to Japan, which I did that purposefully. I wanted one place in the world that could be my special place that I saved for me,” she said on Good Morning America. “I am not easy to surprise because I love to control every situation, and I had completely surrendered. And I’m telling you, I was so, so surprised.”

Cyrus added that Morando “did good” when it came to selecting her engagement ring.

Despite her change of heart about marriage, an insider told Us in December 2025 that Cyrus had considered foregoing a traditional wedding with Morando.

“Miley is really happy right now and in such a good, calm place,” the source noted at the time. “She’s not super into the idea of doing another wedding.”

The “very low-key” couple planned to “definitely celebrate in their own way” and hoped to do “something fun and meaningful that feels very special to them,” according to the insider.

In her next stage with Morando, Cyrus was more focused on the “love” than the “whole production” of a wedding.

“They have talked about eloping and doing something very private with just the two of them,” the source hinted. “She is embracing this era being engaged and is not in a rush to plan anything.”

Regardless of the fact that she didn’t “necessarily want to be a ‘bride’ again or do anything traditional,” Cyrus embraced the intimacy of her relationship with Morando while preparing for their nuptials.

The insider added, “Miley loves that this chapter of her life has been more private.”
