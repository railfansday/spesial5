Mercury: Product Marketing Manager - Banking & Payments

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

In 1933, Harry Beck introduced a radically different map of the London Underground. Rather than faithfully depicting the geography of London, he simplified the sprawling transit network into clean lines, clear connections, and a system that anyone could navigate. Distances were distorted, angles were standardized, and unnecessary details disappeared. The result was something far more useful than a geographically accurate map: a way for millions of people to understand a complex system and confidently find their way through it.

Beck understood something fundamental. The best way to explain something complicated isn't to show people everything. It's to understand what matters to them and make that unmistakably clear.

At Mercury, we believe great product marketing works the same way. Banking* and payments can be complex, but the value they create for customers shouldn't be. Our job is to understand what people need, find the essence of what we're building, and help customers see how it can make their lives better.

We're a financial technology company building products that help ambitious businesses do more with their money. From everyday banking and payments to international money movement and emerging capabilities like agentic payments, we're making complex financial workflows feel remarkably simple.

We're looking for a Product Marketing Manager to help bring these products to market, communicate their value, and make sure customers discover and use what we're building.

This role sits at the intersection of Product, Marketing, and Revenue. You'll develop positioning, lead go-to-market strategies, drive successful product launches, and identify opportunities to increase adoption and revenue.

We care deeply about craft, but successful product marketing is about more than a beautiful campaign or a well-executed announcement. It's about understanding customers, building an effective plan, and driving meaningful results.

*Mercury is a fintech company, not an FDIC-insured bank. Banking services provided through Choice Financial Group and Column N.A., Members FDIC.
