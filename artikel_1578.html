Roma CEO John Galantic returns ahead of transfer discussions

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Roma continues to work at Trigoria ahead of their trip to Parma, a matchday 12 Serie A fixture.

Gian Piero Gasperini will once again have the three Argentine players Soulé, Castro, and Balerdi available today, returning from their commitments with the Albiceleste.

CEO John Galantic also met at the Fulvio Bernardini Stadium.

For the Giallorossi coach, the director is a key figure in understanding the squad’s room for maneuver ahead of the January transfer window, a crucial session for strengthening the team and dreaming big.

In addition to the various players involved with their national teams, John Galantic has also returned to Trigoria.

As Il Tempo reports, the manager is expected to exchange important information and guidance regarding the January transfer window with Gasperini and sporting director D’Amico.

The Giallorossi coach is convinced that with effort, they can compete until the end for very ambitious goals. The club’s room for maneuver remains to be seen, especially in relation to the sanctions resulting from failure to comply with the settlement agreement.
