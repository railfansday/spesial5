The stakes are eminently high for two of the NFC East's most venerable franchises ahead of a Week 9 showdown.

The Giants enter Week 9 with a vice grip at the top of the division, posting a winning record — something none of the Eagles, Cowboys and Commanders have been able to achieve. Jaxson Dart's season-ending injury appeared destined to torpedo New York's season. But J.J. McCarthy's heroics have breathed some life into John Harbaugh's side, which secured a momentous overtime win over the Texans off his back.

Harbaugh's side will get its next test in the form of a Philadelphia team that is presently on pace to miss out on the postseason for the first time since 2020. Pressure is mounting within the Eagles ranks, with a host of injuries — none more important that Jalen Hurts' season-ending blood clots — leaving Philly looking rudderless nearly halfway through the NFL calendar.
