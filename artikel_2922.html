Raider Nation has a big rise in confidence

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Las Vegas Raiders won their first game since Week 3 against the New York Jets. Last Sunday's 38-33 victory has the team back to 500 and the best record after 8 games since 2021.

Klint Kubiak has already improved from last season with the fourth win. He has this team believing they can beat anyone on any given Sunday. Fans are excited about the future and the young talent that is built around it.

Brock Bowers had another breakout game, and the young secondary made plays in the red zone. With their future franchise quarterback waiting for his opportunity, the future seems bright for the Silver and Black.

In this Week's SB Nation reacts, we ask Raider Nation how confident they are in the team's direction. Fans saw a 21-point rise this week, from 73 percent to 94 percent.

Of course, this percentage will keep rising if the Raiders pull off an upset against the 49ers. They are 10.5-point underdogs and would be a huge road win. We will see if the Raiders pull it off.
