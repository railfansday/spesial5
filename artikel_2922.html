CSC Reacts Survey: Can the Saints beat the Bears?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It's Week 11 and the New Orleans Saints are now 3-6 after losing to the Carolina Panthers last Sunday in the Superdome. This week, they are preparing for another challenging matchup as they get ready to hit the road to take on the Chicago Bears, with hopes of getting back in the win column.

As the season progresses, every game is opportunity for the Saints to improve, build confidence and establish some much-needed momentum. The NFC South is still up for grabs, but New Orleans will need a strong performance on both sides of the ball to come away with a win against a fresh Chicago team looking to improve on their 7-2 record.

For the Saints, success will depend on executing offensively, protecting the football and finding ways to sustain drives. Defensively, New Orleans must limit explosive plays, generate some pressure on Caleb Williams and capitalize on opportunities to force some turnovers.

While the Saints have faced their share of challenges this season, Sunday's matchup offers another chance to show some improvement and put together a complete performance.
