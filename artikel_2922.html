Iowa women's basketball vs Eastern Illinois, score, live updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

IOWA CITY — Iowa women's basketball team returns to Carver-Hawkeye Arena on Thursday night to take on Eastern Illinois.

Last time out, the Hawkeyes (1-0) won their season opener on Monday, 73-45, against Towson. Dani Carnegie led the team in scoring, with 21 points, 10 of them in the fourth quarter. Chit-Chat Wright added 14 points, and McKenna Woliczko scored 10 and led the team with eight rebounds.

Eastern Illinois (0-1) lost its season opener to Drake, 76-54. In the loss, Nyah Hardy scored 15 points off the bench. Janyah Bohanon, a preseason All-Ohio Valley Conference selection, scored 10. The Panthers are predicted to finish seventh out of nine teams in the OVC.
