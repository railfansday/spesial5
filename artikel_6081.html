Hermès Carry-On Costs $10,450 Used—Travelers Are Worried About Gate Checking It, And That’s The Problem

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A Hermès carry on bag with two horse heads on the front is listed for $10,450 used, and travelers on Reddit are imagining the argument with a gate agent who wants to check it. I like good luggage. I’ve been using my Rimowa Salsa Deluxe for about a decade. But I don’t understand spending $10,000 on a carry on if it means worrying more about what happens to the bag than to everything inside it. Here’s the Hermès Toile & Clemence R.M.S. Cabine 55. The asking price was $11,000, reduced 5%. Hermès currently lists its Quadrige horse print cabin suitcase for $13,400 new. Imagine the fight with the Gate Agent over checking this! What You’re Getting For The Money Reactions in the Delta thread range from hating the design to insisting that anyone with $10,000 luggage must fly private, which doesn’t make sense. They probably have no money left for even Comfort+. And there are happy owners. In a discussion among Hermès enthusiasts, one owner praises the construction and smooth wheels, while acknowledging they fly private most of the time (heh). Others worry about weight, wear and having to check it. PurseBop’s detailed owner review loves how it rolls and the ability to customize details. But the review also finds less packing space than in a Louis Vuitton Horizon because of where the telescopic handle sits. That handle arrangement also makes balancing a large tote on top less convenient. People should buy what they want. Part of being human is valuing aesthetics. A beautiful object can make someone happy every time they use it, and that has value. This particular design doesn’t do much for me, but other people don’t need to share my taste. Why I’ll Pay More For Good Luggage I used to buy whatever inexpensive bag I could find. As I traveled more, I became willing to pay for something that rolled easily, balanced properly, held what I needed and fit comfortably in the overhead bin. I wrote about that tradeoff back in 2015. A few hundred dollars spread across years of frequent travel can be a small cost per trip. Better wheels are nice every time you walk through an airport. A handle or zipper that doesn’t break saves you a hassle at exactly the moment you don’t have time for one. I still like my decade-old Rimowa Salsa Deluxe. I find Tumi overpriced for what it is, prefer Briggs & Riley for quality, and think Delsey Helium is decent at the value end. My experience with Rimowa customer service has been atrocious. When part of my handle broke, I got disconnected calls, unanswered emails and a runaround between their repair teams. By the time I reached the right people, they told me my warranty had expired in the year and a half during Covid since I’d first contacted them. It doesn’t change the size of the bins on a small aircraft or help when a late connection puts you among the last passengers to board. I don’t want my belongings lost or delayed, and I don’t want to waste time at baggage claim. When a bag comes back at the aircraft door, there’s still the wait and the extra handling. I’ve made this argument before about Louis Vuitton luggage. A bag’s job is to make its contents easy to transport and protect them along the way.
