Thom Velde, Former AMC Theatres Executive, Dies at 83

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Thomas Velde, who served as head film buyer at AMC Theatres during his 27 years at the exhibitor, died Oct. 31. He was 83.

Velde died of cancer at his home in Rancho Mirage, Calif..

For 27 years, he worked at AMC Theatres, where he negotiated film agreements with domestic and international studios while also overseeing the entertainment company’s Canadian operations and real estate ventures. Following his time at AMC, he worked independently in motion picture distribution and would consult for clients that included Microspace Communications and CJ America. Velde is survived by his wife, Deborah; his daughters, Andrea Ter-Velde and Courtney Ter-Velde, and their husbands, John McLoughlin and Greg Horton; his son, Sam; stepsons Matthew Castillo and Spencer Castillo and their wives, Crissy Castillo and Claire Castillo; grandchildren Aubrey Long and Jack Long; and step-grandchildren Jackson Castillo, Sammy Castillo, Cruz Castillo, Bianca Castillo and Capri Castillo.

A celebration of life will be held Dec. 8 at 3 p.m. at Woodland Hills Community Church. Donations can be made to the Will Rogers Motion Picture Pioneers Foundation or the Motion Picture and Television Fund, both of which serve the industry community Velde was a part of.

“Thom was an avid golfer and a devoted college football fan,” his family noted. “Above all, he loved his family and friends, who will remember his strength of character, his generosity and the way he made time for the people in his life. He was always willing to lend a hand and had a special gift for keeping friendships alive over the years.”
