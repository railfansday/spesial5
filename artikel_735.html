Pop Mart Turns Peach Riot Into HUNTR/X With New ‘KPop Demon Hunters’ Blind Box Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

From the makers of Labubu, the collectible crossover features six different blind boxes, starting at $29.99 each, or fans can grab the complete set to collect them all.

Fictional girl groups are having a moment, and Pop Mart is bringing two of them together. The latest collaboration from the brand that brought us Labubus pairs its punk-rock trio Peach Riot with Netflix’s hit KPop Demon Hunters, reimagining Gigi, Frankie and Poppy in HUNTR/X-inspired stage outfits. The collaboration arrives as Pop Mart collectibles continue to make waves across fashion and pop culture. From Doja Cat to BLACKPINK’s LISA, celebrities have helped bring these quirky collectibles into the spotlight by styling them as fashion accessories. Meanwhile, KPop Demon Hunters has become a merchandising powerhouse following its massive success in film and music, inspiring everything from Squishmallows and electronics to gel press-on nails and trading cards. So, this pairing feels right on trend, bringing together two popular collectible worlds with the potential to become the next must-have bag charm. Fans can purchase the plush pendants individually in blind boxes, with six possible designs to collect, or opt for the complete set featuring Gigi, Frankie and Poppy reimagined as Rumi, Mira and Zoey in their “How It’s Done” and “What It Sounds Like” outfits.

Alternatively, shop the whole set for $179.94 to guarantee all six pendants.

The Peach Riot x KPop Demon Hunters plush doll pendant collection features six regular designs starring Gigi, Frankie and Poppy in HUNTR/X-inspired “How It’s Done” and “What It Sounds Like” outfits. There’s also a secret Derpy Tiger-inspired Gigi design to be on the hunt for too. Each plush pendant measures approximately 6.5 inches.
