Lainey Wilson Takes Brief Break From Hosting CMA Awards To Live Debut New Song ‘Just a Woman’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The country star pulled double duty as performer and host of the 60th annual award show.

Just before hosting the 60th CMA Awards, country star Lainey Wilson dropped her newest song “Just a Woman.” Fittingly, she chose the award show stage for the song’s live debut – taking just a brief break from her own hosting duties.  Explore Lainey Wilson See latest videos, charts and news

Wilson delivered the soaring country ballad about being “just a woman trying to love a man/ you drive me crazy, but baby, I can’t get this ring off my hand.” This May, the country star married longtime partner and former NFL quarterback, Devlin “Duck” Hodges. “Just a Woman” is co-written by Wilson, Luke Dick and Shane McAnally. The song follows a handful of singles this year from Wilson, including “Phone, Keys, Wallet” with John Mayer, “Kinda Love It,” “Can’t Sit Still,” “The Jesus I Know Now” with Brandon Lake and a duet version of “Younger You” with Miley Cyrus.

Tonight (Wednesday, Nov. 18) is the third time Wilson has hosted the CMA awards. She is also nominated for entertainer of the year and female vocalist of the year. She was up for single of the year, though the trophy ultimately went to Ella Langley for her hit single “Choosin’ Texas.”Wilson has won 12 CMA Awards, including two for entertainer of the year in 2023 and 2025. She has released five full-length albums, including 2024’s Whirlwind. The album debuted at No. 8 on the Billboard 200 and No. 3 on Top Country Albums, earning Wilson her first top 10 debuts on both charts. On the Billboard Hot 100, she has scored 14 entries.
