Ring is launching an indoor home security drone, new Rover Cam robot

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Amazon's Ring Robotics announced two new security devices on Tuesday: the Ring Drone Cam for residential use and the Ring Rover Cam for commercial use. Amazon also announced the release of a new tool called AI Pilot, a cloud-based tool that will power the navigation for both devices.

Ring Robotics is a division of Amazon's home security brand Ring, which makes a popular line of security cameras, video doorbells, and related products. The Ring Drone Cam is essentially a flying indoor security camera, and it's priced at $249.99 and set to be released on Jan. 27. The Ring Rover Cam is priced at $999.99, will be released on Dec. 16, and is available to pre-order now.

This isn't the first time Ring has announced a flying indoor security camera, yet this is set to be the first Ring drone that will actually ship to customers. Ring previously announced home security drones in 2021 and 2025, but at a press briefing, Ring founder and chief inventor Jamie Siminoff stated that the new Ring Drone camera is fully ready to ship to customers this time. He also said the assembled-in-the-USA product is fully compliant with the Trump administration's recent ban on drones and robots made overseas.

Siminoff said that the new drone solves the problems the company faced with its earlier attempts to create drones.

"As we tried to fix the edge cases that we were running into to make this work inside of a home, that would add a sensor or more processing," Siminoff explained.

"More processing and sensors had more need for power. More power, more battery. More battery, more weight. More weight, you have to spin the rotors faster. The faster you spin the rotors, the more dangerous it is. The more dangerous it is, the more you have to shroud it. The more you shroud it, the less aerodynamics, and you get into this vicious cycle, where you basically keep fixing things, which then breaks things and makes them worse."

Siminoff said the company decided at the last minute not to ship the drone announced in 2025 and ultimately redesigned the product.

The Ring Drone Cam is a new flying indoor security camera that navigates via a new custom-built system called AI Pilot. The vision-based system relies on an internet connection to operate, though the drone can land safely if the internet cuts out mid-flight.

When a Ring Drone Cam is installed in a customer's home and connected to other Ring cameras, sensors, or alarms, it will automatically fly to investigate the potential disturbance. Siminoff explained that it can help provide an on-the-ground view of sensitive areas like bedrooms or bathrooms in case of emergency, without customers needing to install a permanent security camera.

Programmable custom flight routes and patrol schedules

Automatic response to Ring camera, sensor, and alarm alerts

2K video with a 148-degree field of view and the ability to rotate 360 degrees

Two built-in LED spotlights for low-light conditions

The Ring Drone Cam is unusually loud, particularly since it's flown in indoor environments. However, Siminoff stated that Ring considers this a feature, not a bug, as the LED spotlights and loud noise can act as a deterrent for home intruders. The drone is meant to be used while customers are away from home, though it may surprise any pets in the household.

The drone operates with the new Alexa Home Map, an interactive 3D map showing all internet-connected devices in a user's home. The new Alexa Home Map was announced at a recent October Amazon event, where the company introduced new Echo smart home products.

However, for the Ring Rover Cam, Siminoff said the company was specifically targeting enterprise customers with small- and medium-sized businesses.

The Rover Cam can patrol warehouses, offices, and other commercial environments after hours, through routine patrols, remote navigation, or emergency response. The Rover Cam features a periscope camera and records and streams 4K footage.

Unlike the Astro, the Rover Cam is a first-party Ring device that can integrate with other Ring cameras and security products.

In a press release shared with Mashable, Ring said the new roving robot is "like having a guard on patrol who never clocks out. Ring Rover can do a sweep of the floor after close and investigate when sensor trips at 2 a.m. — streaming it all to your phone, wherever you are. For a business with a lot of ground to cover, Ring Rover delivers security that moves."

Mashable actually criticized Ring for waiting too long to employ two-factor authentication back in 2020.

The company also faces increased competition in the space, as Apple recently entered the smart home market with a new line of video doorbells and home security cameras.

When asked about Apple's entry into the market, Siminoff didn't seem worried.

"I have always focused on the mission to make neighborhoods safer and inventing the future," Siminoff said. "And so if we keep building products that are the best for making neighborhoods safer, our customers and our neighbors will continue to reward us and buy our stuff, and that's what we have to focus on."
