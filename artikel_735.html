Juan Atkins’ Catalog Surges 1,285% on Streaming After Producer’s Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The techno founder died on Oct. 31 at age 64.

The catalog of techno founder Juan Atkins has seen a massive streaming surge following the producer’s death on Oct. 31.

Between Oct. 30 and Nov. 5, U.S. streams of Atkins’ catalog went up by 1,285%, according to Luminate. In the week of Oct. 23-29, Atkins’ catalog received 6,000 U.S. on-demand streams; following his death, the number rose to 86,000 U.S. on-demand streams. Similarly, Atkins’ global streams increased by 1,103% the same week.

Two of Atkins’ key side projects also saw significant streaming boosts. In the week of Oct. 30-Nov. 5, Cybotron — composed of Atkins and producer Rik Davis — boasted 84,000 total U.S. on-demand streams. That was up from 26,000 the week prior, representing a 228% increase. Founded in Detroit, Cybotron released its debut album, Enter, in 1983. Its key track, “Clear,” reached No. 52 on Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs on Oct. 8, 1983 and was later sampled on Missy Elliott’s Timbaland-produced “Lose Control” in 2005. “Clear” ranked No. 77 on Billboard‘s 2025 list of the 100 Best Dance Songs of All Time.

Meanwhile, streams for Model 500, an Atkins solo alias, were up 492% following his death, rising from 8,000 to 46,000 U.S. on-demand streams week over week.

On Oct. 31, Atkins was found unresponsive in his hotel room in Mexico City before a scheduled performance. He was transported to a hospital, where he was pronounced dead shortly after arriving. No cause of death has been announced.

Atkins is a founder of techno, a genre he co-created alongside Kevin Saunderson and Derrick May as part of The Belleville Three — so-called because the trio initially worked in the Detroit suburb of Belleville. His productions are credited for presenting not only new sounds but new sonic worlds, with fellow techno pioneer Jeff Mills remembering Atkins as being “among a rare and select group of visionary musicians who helped construct a bridge into the 21st century.”

Following his death, Atkins was also fondly remembered by an array of fellow electronic music producers around the world.
