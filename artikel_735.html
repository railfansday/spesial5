Sheila Mangubat, ’90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days’ Star, Dies at 35

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

She appeared on season six of the TLC reality series with her former fiancé, David Dangerfield.

Sheila Mangubat, who starred on season six of the TLC reality series 90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days, has died. She was 35.

David Dangerfield, Mangubat’s former fiancé, shared on Instagram that she died on Saturday in the Philippines. Her cause of death wasn’t immediately available.

“It breaks my heart to share the news from her family that Sheila passed away on Saturday in the Philippines,” Dangerfield wrote. “I am devastated and still trying to process this loss. Please keep her son, her family, and me in your prayers. Before the 90 Days, which launched in 2017, is a spinoff of the original TLC series 90 Day Fiancé. It follows Americans who connect with their partner online and travel abroad to meet them in person for the first time, as they decide whether to propose and apply for a K-1 visa. The original show centers on couples who are already engaged and must decide to get married or break up.

Mangubat and Dangerfield’s relationship was documented on Before the 90 Days season six in 2023. After meeting through a Facebook group for deaf and hearing-impaired singles in 2021, Dangerfield traveled from Nebraska to the Philippines to meet his then-girlfriend Mangubat in person for the first time. They eventually got engaged on the show but later broke up in October 2025 due to K-1 visa issues, financial strain and distance.

Mangubat is survived by her son, Jhonreil, from a previous relationship.
