Highwoods Properties: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

As tenant concessions expire and capital spending normalizes, free cash flow is poised to surge, potentially reshaping dividend coverage.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, Highwoods Properties (HIW -1.23%) raised its full-year funds from operations outlook, a standard metric tracking real estate cash generation. Capital recycling into top markets dictates the trajectory of property distributions.

Three statements from management reveal where the business is heading.

Highwoods Properties sells older assets outside prime submarkets to fund construction in premier locations. A low capitalization rate, which measures annual property income relative to purchase price, allows the company to harvest cash from mature buildings without adding heavy debt.

National construction of office space has dropped to historical lows. That supply shortage gives Highwoods Properties pricing power in Best Business Districts, or high-demand commercial hubs, where space is becoming scarce.

Upfront tenant concessions and building improvements hit short-term cash flow while building occupancy. As free-rent periods expire and capital expenditures, or cash spent on physical space, return to normal levels, net cash generation will surge.

Demand for premier office space is outpacing supply, driving higher rents across core submarkets. Executives are tracking pricing discipline as regional construction halts.

Disciplined capital recycling into prime locations positions the business for long-term growth.
