6 Stocks Set to Cash In on the Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Boom

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Take-Two Interactive is the obvious winner, but it's not the only company that will benefit.

By Geoffrey Seiler – Nov 18, 2026 at 6:04PM EST

Grand Theft Auto is one of the most iconic video game franchises on the planet, and its newest edition, Grand Theft Auto VI, or GTA6, has been called the most anticipated video game in history. All five previous versions of the game have been hits, and it's been more than 13 years since Grand Theft Auto V was released in September 2013. With an estimated $1 billion budget, a lot is riding on the game, and early signs are that it is set to deliver.

A day before its launch, the game had nearly $1.7 billion in preorders, and it was already drawing rave reviews. The game was already atop Metacritic's all-time gaming leaderboard with a score of 99, which had only happened one other time back in 1998, with Nintendo's The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

With excitement building, let's look at six stocks set to cash in on Grand Theft Auto VI.

As the developer of the game, Take-Two Interactive (TTWO +6.22%) is the biggest winner. It's already closing in on $2 billion in orders, and management has projected Q3 net bookings for its fiscal Q3 to reach $3.35 billion to $3.5 billion, about double from a year ago.

The key going forward, though, will be how the company monetizes the game over the long term, through online multiplayer extensions and recurring consumer spending. The up-front sales are great, but that is just part of the story.

Sony (SONY -0.04%), which makes the PlayStation gaming console, is also set to be a big winner. About 75% of preorders have been for PlayStation versions of the game. The success of the game could not only drive sales of its PS5 and higher-margin PS5 Pro, but also give it a take rate on all digital software downloads and in-game microtransactions made through the PlayStation Store.

Both Walmart (WMT -0.13%) and Target (TGT +4.01%) should also benefit from the launch of GTA6. Now, these retailers will not be selling physical discs; instead, physical boxed copies of the game will contain a digital download code. However, collectors still like to get boxed copies for the artwork and physical inserts like maps, while it's also a better gift presentation for the upcoming holidays.

Importantly, it can increase foot traffic to their stores, which can also lead to other impulse purchases that typically don't happen online.

Big video game launches are also often accompanied by strong sales of gaming peripherals. This can be a revenue growth driver for a company like Turtle Beach (TBCH +1.55%), which holds a roughly 40% share of the U.S. gaming headset market. It has also expanded into other accessories, such as controllers, chargers, and simulation gear.

Gamers tend to upgrade or replace equipment during big new game launches. The company also has strong retail distribution, with its items placed near popular games.

While all these companies could see a lift from the sales of physical items, Reddit (RDDT -0.41%) could see a strong benefit from increased community engagement on its platform around GTA6. It's common for its platform to have dedicated gaming communities, and with the launch of a game the size and scope of Grand Theft Auto VI, millions of users will likely flood gaming subreddits to share clips, potential easter eggs, map theories, and gameplay discoveries.

Young, predominantly male gamers are a highly desirable demographic for many advertisers, and brands that cater to this segment will likely be looking to spend to capture their attention for what is not just a video game launch, but a true cultural phenomenon. Reddit, meanwhile, is in a unique position to command strong ad prices, given the concentration of hyperengaged eyeballs on its gaming subreddits.
