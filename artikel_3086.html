Stock Market Today, Nov. 10: CoreWeave Stock Edges Lower as a Multibillion-Dollar Question Looms Over Earnings

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

CoreWeave's massive AI infrastructure expansion is fueling revenue growth, but mounting debt and capital spending are raising the stakes for investors.

CoreWeave (CRWV -0.51%), a leading provider of cloud infrastructure for artificial intelligence, closed Tuesday at $48.73, down 0.51%, but then jumped roughly 6% in after-hours trading following better-than-expected third-quarter results. The results revealed surging demand for AI computing capacity, with revenue jumping 168% year over year to $3.66 billion. However, a $709 million net loss highlighted the substantial financing costs associated with the company's aggressive expansion.

Trading volume reached 58.1 million shares as investors weighed CoreWeave's rapid growth against mounting debt and the enormous capital investment required to expand its AI infrastructure. Despite the financial pressures, the stock remains approximately 22% above its March 2025 IPO price.

S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.38%) closed at 7,521, down 0.38%, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -0.74%) closed at 25,066, down 0.74%. Among AI-specialized cloud computing and GPU infrastructure services peers, Oracle (ORCL -1.51%) closed at $117.98, down 1.51%, and Nebius Group (NBIS -5.27%) closed at $134.00, down 5.27%.

CoreWeave's third-quarter results reinforced the strength of AI infrastructure demand, with revenue surging 168% year over year to $3.66 billion and its contracted revenue backlog reaching $132.5 billion. The company also raised its full-year revenue outlook to between $13 billion and $13.3 billion, providing further evidence that demand for computing capacity remains robust.

However, converting that demand into sustainable profits remains the bigger challenge. CoreWeave reported a $709 million net loss, while quarterly interest expenses reached $846 million. With capital expenditures expected to approach $39 billion this year, the company remains heavily dependent on external financing to fund its expansion.

Looking toward 2027, investors should focus less on revenue growth alone and more on whether newly deployed infrastructure can generate sufficient cash flow to offset rising financing costs. CoreWeave has established itself as a major beneficiary of the AI investment boom, but its long-term returns will increasingly depend on how efficiently it can turn that demand into profits.
