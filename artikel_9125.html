Drawing Center's Laura Hoptman Wins Curatorial Excellence Award

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Plus: Joyce Awards winners announced, Agnes Gund's gift to art in schools, and Derrick Adams and LEGO make a splash in Miami.

Art Movements, published every Thursday afternoon, is a roundup of must-know news, appointments, awards, and other happenings in today’s chaotic art world.

The Center for Curatorial Studies at Bard College announced Laura Hoptman, executive director of the Drawing Center in New York, as the recipient of the 2027 Audrey Irmas Award for Curatorial Excellence, a $25,000 prize. Hoptman's curriculum vitae reads like a roll call of NYC's major institutions: She started out as an assistant curator at the Bronx Museum in the 1980s and later held curatorial roles at the Museum of Modern Art and the New Museum.

So it is no surprise that, when asked what this recognition signified for her, Hoptman chose to invoke the network and community that her career has grown alongside.

“The best kind of professional recognition comes from one's peers, and this is one of the many reasons why this award is so meaningful to me,” Hoptman told Hyperallergic in an email. “It also reminds me how damn lucky I have been to have spent the last 30 years around art and artists in one of the greatest cultural hubs in the world. After all this time, my vocation still fills me with joy.”
