Lapalma presents a cross-section of its catalogue on Dezeen Showroom

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

From colour palettes and modular seating to outdoor furniture and tables, Italian brand Lapalma has listed a selection of its most recent products on our Showroom section.

The brand collaborated with architect and designer Raffaella Mangiarotti on a new palette of colourways that can be applied across its offering of products.

Nine new shades join seven rethought existing colours in what the brand describes as a “chromatic alphabet”, which can be mixed or matched across seating, tables and accessories.

When designing the collection, Mangiarotti considered each colour’s capacity to work seamlessly whether applied to metal, wood or plastic.

The brand’s Kipu Club outdoor chair presents a new iteration of Anderssen & Voll‘s original design and can withstand life outdoors.

A curvilinear backrest partially wraps a pebble-shaped seat, creating a comfortable and friendly seat for al fresco lounging.

A family of tables have been developed for the brand by designer Mario Ferrarini, who created and named the collection after the conical profile of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Aptly named Kili, the tables are designed to be sculptural as well as functional, coming in various sizes and tabletop shapes, with each element carefully aesthetically considered.

Snaking lengths of seating are created in this new modular system designed for the brand by Francesco Rota, who wanted to make “welcoming islands for meeting, working, waiting or simply taking a pause”.

The HUB modular system is reconfigurable, allowing users to reorder the modules to create evolving landscapes in open-plan spaces.

Designer Francesco Rota collaborated with Lapalma to extend the Plus modular outdoor seating system, which now comprises additional sun loungers and facilitates all-new combination options.

Round-cornered, lozenge-shaped modules available in various bright and neutral-coloured upholstery make up the range, which creates opportunity for open-air lounging and socialising.

Lapalma is an Italian furniture company with a forty-year history of designing and manufacturing tables, seating and other furnishings for both indoor and outdoor use.

It frequently collaborates with eminent designers, both fellow Italians and those from further afield, to develop cutting-edge furniture solutions for contemporary interiors.
