Shark's newest robovac, the PowerDetect UV Reveal 2-in-1 Vac & Mop, landed in Australia less than two months ago, blending the brand's competitive pricing and suction power into an automated package. Not only that, it comes with smart LiDAR navigation, a heated mop pad, and, as mentioned in its name, it uses UV light to detect and remove stains, debris and dust on your floors.

What makes it even better is that it's gotten its first discount, and it's Amazon that outshines even the SharkNinja Black Friday sale. While the maker itself has knocked 33% off the list price, Amazon has gone even better with a huge AU$750 reduction bringing down the price to just AU$1,049.

There's no word on when this deal will end, but with a discount this good, I don't expect it to hang around for long.

Designed to spot messes the naked eye can miss, the Shark PowerDetect UV Reveal uses light to detect hidden stains and spills on your hard floors. Better yet, it also features a heated mop that can scrub the toughest stains. It's also equipped with LiDAR navigation, an anti-tangle roller brush, eight PowerDetect modes that adapt to your flooring and a NeverTouch Pro Base that handles the debris and self-cleaning for you.

Also available in Eclipse Navy for the same price, or Café Noir Mocha for an extra AU$21.
