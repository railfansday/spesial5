Spectral AI, Inc. (MDAI +0.65%) reported a strategic transition from a research-focused organization to a commercial enterprise during the third quarter. Management stated that the company is focusing on establishing clinical confidence and expanding relationships with burn centers to integrate its AI diagnostic technology into standard clinical workflows. The company began manufacturing its first 30 DeepView AI systems to support scheduled deployments and is conducting clinical studies in the U.S. and the U.K. to support diagnostic label expansion. Additionally, management updated its 2026 revenue outlook to reflect the completion of certain government programs and shifting development timelines.
