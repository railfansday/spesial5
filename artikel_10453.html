This holiday season, the best meal deals come with a side of merch

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Socks aren’t usually something kids want for Christmas. McDonald’s is betting adults will want them with their meal, though.

Instead of a plastic toy, the chain is offering free pairs of ugly Christmas socks with every purchase of its Home Alone-themed specialty meal, which will arrive in stores December 1. With each order, customers will get one of four pairs of festive socks featuring illustrations of McDonald’s characters like Grimace, or Macaulay Culkin’s Home Alone character, Kevin McCallister.

McDonald’s isn’t alone in this. The socks, designed in the style of an ugly Christmas sweater, are kitschy collectibles that fit within the broader trend of apparel releases by food and quick service brands, and it’s bigger than ever this holiday season. Burger King and Wendy’s are also giving away cold weather gear —both happen to be beanies, and Burger King’s is Peanuts-themed—with their holiday meal deals this year, too.

Restaurants and food brands have turned to clothing and apparel in recent years to capitalize on “post-ironic” cultural relevance and to deepen brand affinity even as shoppers pull back on spending. Chains like Cava have released their own ingredient-themed apparel collections, while other brands have turned to unconventional partnerships, like PringlesCrocs or Hidden Valley Ranch-flavored chapstick. For some fans, restaurant merch has become the new band T-shirt.

The happy-meal-toys-for-adults trend also comes as grown-ups are increasingly ordering kids meals, according to the market research firm Circana, which found adult orders of kids meals are up 28% since 2019. At Burger King, the No. 2 U.S. burger chain by sales behind McDonald’s, sales of its King Jr. meal are up 40% year over year, which the chain attributes in part to more adult purchases with no kids around.

Designing a kids meal for adults, then, is catering to an already growing customer base. Instead of giving them toys with their meals, apparel is a giveaway that’s usable.

Due to rising prices, customers have dined out less frequently over the past two years; to win them back, chains are focusing on quality and value. Though McDonald’s has fared better than many of its competitors, it announced a 0.2% decline in U.S. sales on its earnings call this month. To grow its domestic business, the chain is rolling out new menu items with smaller portion sizes and more protein, as well as leaning into specialty drinks and dirty sodas. Meals themed around nostalgic IP like Home Alone add up to another tactic.

McDonald’s is marketing the meal as an invitation to “leave adulthood behind” by tapping into nostalgia, and letting the customers customize their meals by picking their own entrée. It also leans into Home Alone through its menu items: french fries rebranded as “Little Nero’s Pizza McShaker Fries” for the film’s fictional pizza chain, along with a McFlurry, and pizza-flavored seasoning packet and dipping sauce.

This is what a kids meal looks like when it’s “kidulting.”
