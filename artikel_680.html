Stevie Wonder Addresses Conspiracy Theories About His Blindness: ‘Get in the Car With Me & We’ll See’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The legendary singer is in on the joke and has been for quite some time.

Stevie Wonder has seen and heard all the jokes, rumors and conspiracy theories about him actually not being blind, and he has a message for everybody.

During a performance in Chicago at the United Center over the weekend (Nov. 21), he took a break in between playing his plethora of hits to address the conspiracy theories as he sat behind his piano and had a good laugh. “If you believe I can really drive or really see,” he quipped, “Get in the car with me and we’ll see what happens to us at the end of the day.” Related 'Do You Have What It Takes?': KATSEYE Appears in Trailer for K-Pop Film Inspired by Their Experience Rosalía Reacted to a Rauw Alejandro TikTok Video, and The Internet Lost It Karol G's 'BbY WOW' Leads Billboard Global Charts, 'Christmas' Carols Climb Explore Stevie Wonder See latest videos, charts and news

Speaking of the “Stevie Wonder can drive” theories, Lionel Richie told Kelly Clarkson back in 2019 that he spent most of his life thinking that Stevie could see and told a funny anecdote about them being in a car once.

“I went to his house, he says, ‘Wanna hear a new song? I’ve got it in the car, come on, come with me.’ We got out to the car, he says, ‘You sit on the passenger side, I’ll sit on the driver’s side.’ He goes in, he cranks the car up, he puts the tape in, and then he does this [motions that Stevie turns around as if he’s backing the car up] and starts driving back down the driveway. And I scream, I say, ‘Stevie! What are you doing?’ And he says. ‘Got ya, didn’t I?'”

2019 must’ve been the height of the theories because Shaquille O’Neal also told a hilarious story about the time he and Stevie lived in the same building when Shaq played for the Los Angeles Lakers. According to him, the legendary musician walked into their building’s elevator by himself, said “What’s up, Shaq,” pressed the button to his floor and walked to his apartment.

Then there’s the time in 2017 when he told TMZ that he would reveal the truth after they asked about the rumors, which then led to this classic video in “Stevie Wonder Might Not Be Blind” internet lore. Be sure to heck out the comments for a funny tidbit about a story Charlie Murphy told in his memoir The Makings of a Stand Up Guy published in 2011.

Stevie is currently on the back leg of his 18-date Songs in the Key of Life 50th anniversay tour.
