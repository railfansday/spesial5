Paige DeSorbo Goes Social Media Official With Boyfriend Joe D’Amelio

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Paige DeSorbo debuted her romance with boyfriend Joe D'Amelio on TikTok Nov. 25 nearly two years after the couple first sparked romance rumors.

Paige DeSorbo is serving up a hard launch.

Four months after the Summer House alum denied rumors that she and boyfriend Joe D’Amelio got engaged, she made their romance social media official, sharing a funny video on TikTok of her friends making homemade pizzas.

As DeSorbo captioned the Nov. 25 TikTok video, which she also shared on her Instagram Stories, “We aren’t Italian at all.”

The clip featured the Giggly Squad podcast host’s pals cooking up pies and hanging out as they lip-synced to audio from a scene from the 1990 classic Goodfellas, in which Henry Hill (Ray Liotta) introduces the members of mafia capo Paulie Cicero’s crew.

“There was Jimmy, and Tommy, and me. And there was Anthony Stabile, Frankie Carbone,” Liotta’s Hill says in the scene. “And then there was Mo Black’s brother, Fat Andy and his guys, Frankie the Wop and Freddy No Nose. And then there was Pete the Killer, who was Sally Balls’ brother. Then you had Nickey Eyes and Mikey Franzese.”

And among the family and friends who made a cameo in DeSorbo’s video was D’Amelio, who lifted up his drink in greeting as the camera panned to him during the “Nickey Eyes” line.

While the couple first sparked romance rumors way back in February 2025, they have kept their relationship offline until now. And fans couldn’t help but get excited about the update.

“GIRRRRRRRRL it’s tiktok official okayyy honey,” one user wrote in the comments section of DeSorbo’s video, while another added, “Paige he’s so handsome and fits in so well I’m so happy for you kween.”

Although this marks the D’Amelio’s debut on DeSorbo’s social media, the Bravo alum previously shared insight into their romance, joking to Us Weekly in a February interview that while Italian men have a reputation for being “not quiet,” she’s found that’s not the case.

“They’re quiet. And I appreciate that,” she quipped. “They know when to speak and when not to, and that’s important. That’s important in a man—know when to shut it.”
