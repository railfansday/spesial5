Zimmermann Brought the Aussie Party Spirit Back to New York

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

A Zimmermann night out out was long overdue in New York City. It’s been some six years since sisters Nicky and Simone put on a party for their label around these parts—and you could hear Kylie Minogue’s voice blasting from the speakers the moment you pulled up to Indochine on Lafayette Street.

“The Irish and the Australians, it’s a lethal mix,” said Eve Hewson with a mischievous laugh. The Disclosure Day actress, who has recently relocated to Los Angeles, was more than happy to accept the invite to fly into town for the night—particularly after attending her first Zimmermann runway outing during Paris Fashion Week last month (her seat mate from the show, Charlotte Lawrence, quickly ran up and grabbed her with glee upon arrival).

Speaking of showing up to support sisters, Hewson divulged that there could indeed be a season 3 of the cult-favorite Apple TV show Bad Sisters “if people kick up enough of a fuss” to bring it back for another round of murderous fun. In the meantime, after a year where she’s been booked and busy, she’s ready to “just sleep” once the holiday season rolls around.

Around the buzzing restaurant, well-dressed groups broke off into huddles. Naomi Watts and Tommy Dorfman linked hands as they tattled, loved-up models Vivienne Rohner and Henry Rank canoodled in a corner, and sisters Jemima and Quentin Jones showed off their synchronized sultry black frocks at the step-and-repeat. Watts’s photographer brother, Ben, reunited with his fellow Aussies, and their accents audibly cranked up a notch as they cracked jokes together. DJ Amrit Tietz, also flying the flag for Australia, had rushed back from her cool mom platform Spread the Jelly’s most recent “Jellymoon” brand trip upstate to man the decks. (Hence the Kylie.)

Uma Thurman, who’s gracing screens in the just-dropped season 2 of Dexter: Resurrection, arrived in an all-black ensemble from the Cruise 2027 collection. After a quip was made about the extreme height difference between the 5’11” actress and Nicky Zimmermann, Thurman discreetly slipped her high heels off—prompting peals of laughter from the designer. “I hate heels anyway—I only ever wear them for work,” Thurman said, smiling.

As guests found their seats at a long table dressed in dramatic bundles of burgundy anthuriums, magenta dahlias, and amaranthus, a lace-clad Lawrence surprised the room with a performance of her new music, as well as Fleetwood Mac’s “Dreams.” Once the chocolate truffles and mini fruit tartlets were served, everyone was well and truly warmed up for the dance floor as Tietz returned to the DJ booth. Safe to say, more than a few pairs of shoes were kicked off.
