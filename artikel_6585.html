Knee-High Boots and Jeans Are a Classic Combo—Here’s How to Style Them

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Your guide to making the timeless combo feel polished and fresh.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

We’ve come a long way since our skinny jeans era, but even though they never entirely left, they somehow got an iffy reputation. The good news is you can still wear them this season, perhaps just tucked into a pair of knee-high boots. Jeans and knee-high boots are the classic pairing that never disappoints this time of year.

However, skinny jeans aren’t the only style that works. Nowadays, relaxed and even slightly baggy denim is also taking the spotlight in this combination. If you’re wondering how to make the look feel current, it all comes down to proportions. Pair slim jeans with a chunkier knit or an oversized coat to balance the silhouette, and let looser denim do its thing tucked under the boot shaft for a more modern approach.

Not convinced? Below, we’ve rounded up some inspiration for how to wear your favorite boots and jeans together this season.
