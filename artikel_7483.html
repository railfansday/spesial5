Among social media ads, short-form video drives the most actions

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Social media advertising continues to grow, but not all ads are created equal. The ad tech company DoubleVerify has released its 2026 Global Insights Report, a dispatch that identifies YouTube and Facebook — and their respective short-form formats — as dominant forces in the social media marketing landscape.

DoubleVerify compiled its report by collecting responses from 22,000 consumers and 2,020 marketers, spanning regions like North America, Latin America, EMEA, and Asia-Pacific. One thing those global respondents can agree on is their appreciation of YouTube. Google’s video hub is the most-used platform across all age groups and regions, with 95% of all respondents claiming to use it.

Those users are also warming up to YouTube’s ad ecosystem. Half of the respondents said they would take action based on YouTube ads; that was the highest percentage among all measured platforms. Facebook came in second on that axis (49%), followed by Instagram (42%) and TikTok (38%).

Other platforms are lagging behind in terms of actionable ads, and DoubleVerify’s findings suggest a big difference between those four industry leaders and their competitors. YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok are four platforms where short-form media has been widely adopted, and 38% of DoubleVerify respondents said that short-form ads inspire them to take action. That was 7% higher than the next ad format on the list (long-form video pre-rolls, at 31%).

The ubiquity of short-form video is one factor that seems to be making ads more palatable across social media. Since the release of DoubleVerify’s 2025 Global Insights Report, 8% more consumers have decided that social media platforms are suitable locations for ads. YouTube, at 72%, was deemed the most valid platform for those spots.

DoubleVerify’s report shows that social media spend is paying off across multiple platforms, but marketers must still pick and choose as they allocate their ad dollars. As we saw with the Pew Research Center’s recent survey of American social media habits, usage data can vary wildly depending on demographic filters like age, gender, and household income.

That assertion held true in the DoubleVerify report, in which TikTok ranked as the third most-used platform among 18-to-24-year-olds but didn’t crack the top five among the 65+ set. And most platforms skewed male worldwide, but 8% more female respondents use Pinterest compared to men.

The global scope of DoubleVerify’s report adds another wrinkle to the conversation. In the Asia-Pacific region, for example, 56% of respondents said that influencer activity “somewhat to significantly” impacted purchase decisions. In North America, that statistic is only 39%. So if marketers want to ensure that their social media activity inspires action, they should deploy their budgets wisely.
