10 Fall Dinners With 10 Ingredients (Or Fewer!)

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Fall means the start of the school year and holiday after holiday, which means busy mornings and even busier days (and chillier too!). If you feel like you never have the time to run to the grocery store and pick up tons of ingredients for endless weeknight dinners, we feel you. And with grocery prices still climbing, the last thing you want is a recipe that requires a long shopping list but still leaves you wanting more.

These 10 fall dinners check all the boxes. They're some of the easiest and quickest weeknight meals you can make, and best of all, each one requires no more than 10 ingredients, so you won't have to stock up on a bunch of extras.

The best weeknight dinners use pantry powerhouse ingredients to bring big flavor in a short time, and these creamy harissa chickpeas are a prime example. Harissa, tomato paste, and coconut milk join forces with chickpeas to create a saucy one-pan dinner that’s as quick to make as it is wildly craveable.

These cranberry brie turkey melts are precisely what they call for: rustic slices of artisan bread, tangy cranberry sauce, creamy buttery brie, and a mound of garlicky leftover turkey.

Not all sheet-pan dinners are created equal. Sheet-pan pork chops with apples falls into the camp of excellence, packing in flavor with a short-ingredient list onto one pan, all of which comes together in just over a half an hour.

Tossed in a fresh garlic-burst cherry tomato sauce and served with slabs of bubbling browned feta, these chickpeas are your weeknight ticket to protein-packed dinnertime bliss.

With the addition of one of fall’s flavor superstars—pumpkin, of course!—spaghetti carbonara gets a seasonal twist that'll have you craving it all season long. Adding pumpkin puree gives a creamy, silky, and subtly sweet boost to the otherwise simple sauce.

In this easy, hands-off version, we’re cooking thinly-sliced steak low and slow in a rich broth until it’s fall-apart delicious, and then throwing in the broccoli near the end so it cooks just long enough to become tender. After that, all you need is a pot of rice, and dinner is served!

Don't let chicken breasts have all the fun! Chicken thighs are super-flavorful, easy to cook, and SO delicious with sweet potatoes and apples. Try it for dinner any night this fall.

We’re big fans of one-pot pastas and French onion soup-flavored everything, so it was just a matter of time until we combined those two awesome things in one dish. Meet this one-pot French onion soup pasta, which is destined to become one of your favorite weeknight dinners.

Our creamy Tuscan chicken has consistently been one of our most-popular recipes since we published it, which has inspired our test kitchen to create countless iterations. Practically anything is delicious swimming in this creamy Tuscan-inspired sauce, but this vegetarian white bean dinner is our favorite.

This is a true 30-minute meal, and it calls for ingredients that you might already have on hand, saving you a trip to the grocery store. This saucy one-pan dinner features a base of fresh garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, thyme, heavy cream, and Parmesan tossed with pillowy-soft and quick-cooking gnocchi.
