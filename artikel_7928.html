28 Christmas Baby Names That Aren't Noel

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Whether your baby will be born in December, or you just love the holidays that much.

Of course when parents are thinking about what to name their new baby, they look toward the things they value most. Whether it’s a lifelong love of literature, a sentimental meaning, or simply a certain vibe that fits your lifestyle, choosing a name you really adore often starts with thinking about what already brings you happiness. And for some parents, that means Christmas. It’s a holiday all about togetherness, giving, joy, tradition — all wonderful values on their own. For many, Christmastime is also a source of some of their best memories of a magical childhood. It’s easy to see why parents might choose a Christmas-adjacent baby name, and Noel is not the one and only option. Here are some of our favorites:

This beautiful, classic girl’s name means “bright” or “clear.” And yes, Christmas is all about being merry and bright, but the real Christmas connection here is that Clara is the main character of the iconic Christmas ballet, The Nutcracker.

Douglas is a Scottish name meaning “dark stream.” But if you’re a Christmas lover, you’ll know that Douglas firs are an American evergreen tree species. This name is perfect if you love traditional boy names and would prefer one with a built-in nod to your favorite holiday.

Like the North Pole, of course! Kim Kardashian made the name popular when she gave it to her eldest child back in 2013, but now it feels like enough of a name that it wouldn’t be automatically associated with the young celeb.

If the Christmas story holds meaning for you, but naming your baby Mary or Joseph is a little too on the nose, consider Shepherd. It’s an occupation name (very trendy in recent years), and kind of doubles as a last name baby name too. But really, the shepherds in the fields were the first to hear the news of baby Jesus’s birth.

Do any of these names feel like the perfect pick for your little cherub?
