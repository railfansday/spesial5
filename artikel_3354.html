Daisy Ridley, Darren Criss, Travis Fimmel & Judy Greer Join Catherine Zeta-Jones In Tate Taylor’s Marriage Counselor Thriller ‘Cupid’; Filming Underway — AFM

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

EXCLUSIVE: Here’s some nice casting. Daisy Ridley (Ebenezer), Darren Criss (Glee), Travis Fimmel (Dune: Prophecy) and Judy Greer (Ant-Man) have joined Catherine Zeta-Jones in Tate Taylor’s psychological thriller Cupid, which is now filming in Natchez, Mississippi.

Tate Taylor (The Girl On The Train) is directing from a screenplay by the Van Dyke brothers (Don’t Worry Darling).

Cupid “follows a couple (Ridley and Criss) whose relationship is on the rocks as they try, over one weekend, to repair their marriage at the home of a seemingly helpful but unorthodox marriage counselor (Zeta-Jones). What starts out as a hopeful endeavor quickly turns dark as her methods become crueler and more dangerous.”

Taylor and John Norris are producing alongside Ryan Donnell Smith. Executive producers include Brian Edwards, Zack Sherman, Jon Hamm and Jaan Bains for Time Zone Capital. Watch on Deadline

Upgrade is handling international sales and CAA Media Finance, which arranged financing for the film, is handling U.S. distribution rights. Sales discussions continue at the AFM.

Ridley is represented by CAA and Sloane, Offer; Criss is represented by Gersh, Hyphenate Creative, Gersh, and Hansen, Jacobson; Fimmel is represented by Paradigm, Entertainment 360, & Sloane Offer; and Greer is represented by CAA, Artists First, and Felker Toczek.
