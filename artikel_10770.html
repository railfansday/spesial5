Dragon Ball Z composer elected Texas Attorney General

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

A former Dragon Ball Z composer has been elected Texas Attorney General, marking an unlikely career transformation from creating music for Goku and Vegeta to becoming the state’s top lawyer.

Democrat Nathan Johnson defeated Republican Mayes Middleton in the November 3 election, marking the first time in 32 years that a Democrat has been elected Texas Attorney General, with Dan Morales being the last to win the office in 1994.

Johnson, who has served in the Texas Senate since 2019, will take office in January 2027, replacing outgoing Attorney General Ken Paxton.

While his election is a major political upset, anime fans might recognize Johnson’s name from an entirely different career.

Before entering politics, Johnson worked as a composer for Funimation’s English dub of Dragon Ball Z, providing music for the first 67 episodes of the 2005 Ultimate Uncut edition.

He also contributed music to five Dragon Ball Z movies, including Broly: Second Coming, Fusion Reborn, and Wrath of the Dragon.

In a 2012 interview with The Dao of Dragon Ball, Johnson revealed that he had never watched the anime before being hired and initially struggled to connect with its characters and story.

However, he eventually developed an appreciation for the series, particularly through Gohan, whose relationship with his father resonated with Johnson as a parent.

His connection to Dragon Ball Z even became part of his political identity when he first ran for the Texas Senate in 2018.

Speaking to The Dao of Dragon Ball following that election victory, Johnson revealed that younger voters were surprised to discover his anime background.

“Along the way some of them found out about my music background, and specifically about Dragon Ball Z, and they got really excited about it,” he explained.

Johnson previously said that Dragon Ball’s themes of unlikely allies working together reflected his own political philosophy.

“I can never not be a part of Dragon Ball Z,” he said in a 2017 interview.
