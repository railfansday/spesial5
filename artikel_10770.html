sWooZie defends AI video after animator Ashley Nichols calls out use of her character design

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

YouTuber sWooZie has responded to backlash after animator Ashley Nichols accused his latest video of using a character design she created in an AI-assisted sequence.

Nichols initially posted, “THAT’S MY FING DESIGN I MADE AND HE’S USING IT WITH AI???? FING EW.” She later clarified that she had created the design for sWooZie and was paid for the work, but said she “wouldn’t have wanted it used this way.”

THATS MY FUCKING DESIGN I MADE AND HE'S USING IT WITH AI???? FUCKING EW https://t.co/ISajisV79G pic.twitter.com/eloPQR3iUu

Nichols is an animator, voice actor and creator of Far-Fetched, with credits including Hazbin Hotel, Animaniacs and Lackadaisy.

Her comments appeared after viewers noticed AI-assisted animation in sWooZie’s new video. The criticism focused on the use of a design she had originally made for him, rather than a claim that the original commission itself was unauthorized.

I did make it for him to use and was paid for it, but I wouldn't have wanted it used this way

The post quickly drew attention from other artists and longtime viewers. Former sWooZie collaborator RushLight Invader called the decision “a massive slap in the face to your audience and all your former artists,” while Nichols’ follow-up made clear that her concern was how the design had been repurposed.

In a pinned YouTube comment, sWooZie acknowledged that “AI raises a lot of concerns about artists’ livelihood” and said he understood why people were worried.

He also defended his relationship with the artists who have worked on his channel, writing that they “give my characters life” and that all of his character designs are commissioned, paid for and owned by him.

sWooZie said he had spent three years trying to develop the project with animators, but the artists attached to it were ultimately unable to finish. He described the current situation as difficult to navigate and said he did not want to start a debate or anger anyone.

His stated plan is to test making more frequent videos with AI, use the increased output to generate revenue and put that money back into budgets for hiring artists. “This isn’t me being cruel or cutting corners,” he wrote.

However, the comment did not directly address Nichols’ specific concern about using her design with AI. He ended by asking viewers to suggest a better way forward, writing, “If u have a better idea pls lmk!”
