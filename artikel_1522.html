Exclusive: Julian Wards ramps up dream Liverpool transfer, this move changes everything

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Liverpool are looking to build a dynasty and Julian Ward has found the perfect way to do that.

The demolition of Manchester United was the perfect demonstration of what the Reds could become. A mighty dominant force full of the future superstars of the planet.

That being Rio Ngumoha, Jeremy Jacquet and of course Florian Wirtz.

What a sensational display it was for the three of them, highlighted by the individual brilliance of Dominik Szoboszlai as well as elecrifying moments from Bradley Barcola and Victor Munoz.

The future looks bright at Anfield. Brighter than it has looked for well over a year now when the bleak slump commenced under Arne Slot.

Andoni Iraola alongside Ward is now steering Liverpool in the right direction. This is a team that can once again dream about conquering the biggest tournaments on the planet.

Liverpool ramp up move to find Florian Wirtz back-upAnfield Watch understands that the Reds are exploring moves to sign a back-up for the German international. Right now the club are delighted with how well Wirtz has been playing in recent weeks. He created 10 chances against Man United all by himself - the most any Liverpool player has managed in the Premier League since records began. Wirtz now has four assists and six goals in his last nine starts for club and country. It's the kind of form that Liverpool brought him to the club for and spent a then club-record fee in order to secure his services. However, Ward and his recruitment team are well aware that Wirtz needs a deputy. Liverpool cannot rely on him alone. It's why the Reds are ramping up moves to sign a back-up for Wirtz in the January window. Among the players targeted are Wael Mohya, Gilberto Moira and now a third name has also been shortlisted by the club.

Liverpool lining up 'dream' move for Abdellah OuazaneAnfield Watch understands that the Moroccan attacking midfielder who plays for Ajax has also been looked at by the Reds and is a serious candidate.
