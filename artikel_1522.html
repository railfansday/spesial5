De Roon also closing in on new Roma contract

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Roma is preparing its next moves on the transfer market and contract renewals, with sporting director Tony D’Amico involved in several negotiations.

Among the most advanced situations is that of Mario Hermoso, for whom a new two-year contract is reportedly ready.

Additionally, Il Messaggero also writes that a contract extension is also possible for Marten de Roon, who could sign for another season.

The situation regarding Lorenzo Pellegrini and Paulo Dybala, however, is different: both matters remain temporarily pending, with a final decision postponed until the end of the season.
