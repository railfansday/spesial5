Mercato: PSG has taken information on a goalkeeper to compete with Safonov

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The goalkeeper position is probably the position that generates the most doubts and uncertainties on the Paris Saint-Germain side. If Matvey Safonov took the starting place ahead of Lucas Chevalier during last season, the Russian goalkeeper does not completely convince. And this despite the numerous declarations of Luis Enrique to praise him. “He has adapted to what we are looking for in modern football. A team like ours needs a goalkeeper who plays in conjunction with the defensive line all the time. He is still improving. He is a courageous goalkeeper. It does everything we want it to do. I am very happy with him”confided the Spaniard, for example, at the end of October.

For many supporters, the Russian goalkeeper is not necessarily a top goalkeeper, or at least a goalkeeper at the level of a double European champion club. His performance against FC Barcelona earlier this season was thus singled out, despite the Parisians’ 4-2 victory. If the latest information explained that Paris Saint-Germain had not necessarily planned to recruit for this position this winter, the situation would have changed.

As indicated Sports BildParis Saint-Germain is very interested in Gregor Kobel (28 years old), the Swiss goalkeeper for Borussia Dortmund. Indeed, the European champion would have even sought information on several occasions from the player and those around him recently. And this while the German team and the goalkeeper of the Swiss selection are currently negotiating an extension of the lease which binds them until 2028. Arriving on the Ruhr side from Stuttgart for 15 million euros in 2021, Kobel has clearly established himself as one of the best goalkeepers in the German championship.

The one who has 30 selections with Switzerland has a market value estimated at 40 million euros by the specialized site Transfermarktand inevitably, the closer you get to the end of your contract, the more the amount of a potential transfer will decrease. The German media explains that a meeting between Dortmund and the player’s clan is planned to seriously conclude negotiations for an extension, and for the moment, Kobel is rather interested in the idea of ​​returning to BVB. But PSG obviously has arguments to make him flinch…
