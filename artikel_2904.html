Why Anthropic Could Be a $5 Trillion Company By 2030

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Investors have never seen a company like Anthropic before.

By Jeremy Bowman – Nov 19, 2026 at 12:19AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Anthropic, PBC ›

Anthropic (Nasdaq: ANT) is about to make stock market history.

The AI lab will go public on Thursday at a valuation of $1.5 trillion after pricing its IPO at $83 a share. While that is slightly below the $1.75 trillion that Space Exploration Technologies was valued at in its debut, Anthropic will raise more money than SpaceX, bringing in $107.3 billion, including a $26 private placement from some big tech companies.

Anthropic is just five years old, born from former OpenAI employees, and has experienced meteoric growth, with revenue up 1,200% through the first three quarters of the year. Its revenue run rate reached $68 billion at the end of the third quarter. No company in history has come close to that mark just five years since its inception.

Anthropic faces a lot of risks as it goes public. The company has approximately $900 billion in future purchase commitments for chips, AI compute, and lease obligations. It faces competition from other frontier AI labs like OpenAI as well as open-source and open-weight models, which could eat into its market share. Additionally, CEO Dario Amodei has highlighted the risks of AI and called for a slowdown in development to ensure adequate guardrails are in place.

Despite those risks, the company also has a lot of upside potential, and reaching a $5 trillion market cap is achievable. Here's why.

Anthropic might have more growth potential than any other company in history. We've already seen this anecdotally. Software stocks plunged at the beginning of the year when Anthropic introduced a series of plug-in tools for Claude Cowork, competing with specialized software for job functions like legal, finance, and sales.

The disruptive potential for Anthropic is virtually limitless, considering potential applications that extend to drug discovery, robotics, chip design, and beyond.

That's why it doesn't seem hyperbolic for the company to claim a market opportunity ranging from $22.5 trillion to $40 trillion. In its prospectus, Anthropic said that the International Data Corporation (IDC) estimates that AI will produce a $22.5 trillion impact by 2031, and Anthropic believes it could be even greater. Management also said that work performed by knowledge workers represented more than $40 trillion in economic value in 2025.

By both increasing knowledge worker productivity and shifting it to Claude, Anthropic certainly has a multi-trillion-dollar opportunity in front of it, which could support the stock tripling through 2030.

Anthropic has delivered incredible growth over the last year as its new frontier models have grabbed market share from OpenAI and driven a surge in Claude adoption.

In the third quarter, revenue jumped 1,127% year-over-year to $17 billion, rising 47% on a sequential basis. Over a year, 47% sequential growth would translate into 366% revenue growth on an annual basis. While we don't know what Anthropic's growth rate will be going forward, it clearly has the potential to deliver breakout growth if it releases new products and expands its addressable market.

Not only is Anthropic growing year-over-year revenue by more than 1,000%, but the company is now profitable on an adjusted basis with an adjusted operating profit of $4.1 billion through the first three quarters, and $2.5 billion in the third quarter. Anthropic's business is scaling as it grows, and its margins are expanding.

Based on the company's run rate revenue of $68 billion and the IPO valuation of $1.5 trillion, the stock is valued at 22 times sales, which looks like a bargain considering its growth rate and profitability.

It's not unusual for software stocks to trade at around 20 times sales, and the ones that do don't have the explosive growth or disruptive potential that Anthropic does, and some aren't even profitable on an adjusted basis.

With a massive addressable market and soaring growth rate, Anthropic could become a $5 trillion company sooner than investors expect, especially if it maintains a strong growth rate. Don't be fooled by the dialed-down IPO valuation.
