Indiana's Curt Cignetti calls out CBS Sports over 'fake news' report

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Ever since his "Google me" introductory press conference, Indiana Hoosiers head coach Curt Cignetti has enraptured college football with his blunt personality.

Of late, Cignetti hasn't minced words when flaming what he views as SEC favoritism, whether in relation to ESPN's coverage of the College Football Playoff rankings or a new coaching carousel report from John Talty, Richard Johnson, and Chris Hummer of CBS Sports.

"Fake news created by three SEC media writers. Nice try [clown emoji]," Cignetti posted Thursday night, quote-tweeting Talty.

In the report, Talty, Johnson, and Hummer float the possibility of Cignetti leaving Indiana for the Michigan Wolverines. Michigan is 5-5 and in shambles in the first season under Kyle Whittingham, whose seat is getting awfully hot.

"Would Indiana's Curt Cignetti consider an intraconference move to a Big Ten school with deeper pockets and resources?" they wrote. "Sources have indicated that Cignetti has expressed concerns about funding and the program's ability to sustain winning at this level, and the roster outlook for 2027 is less rosy with many of the program's best players set to depart, including possible top-10 pick Charlie Becker."

The report also cites an anonymous source in characterizing Cignetti's messaging to his Hoosiers staff as, "Let's keep winning because this (roster) nest is going to be empty at the end of the year."

Also Thursday, Zach Osterman of The Indianapolis Star reported that any speculation around Cignetti eyeing an Indiana exit for Michigan is "100% false," citing an unnamed source directly refuting the CBS Sports report.

Since hiring Cignetti in November 2023, the Hoosiers are 36-3, including a historic 16-0 campaign last season resulting in the program's first-ever national title. This season, Indiana is 9-1, good for No. 8 on this week's College Football Playoff rankings.

Earlier this week, sassy Cignetti struck again when asked about Indiana's strength of schedule:

"Well, you know, we don't play anybody. We have a soft schedule, right?" Cignetti said sarcastically. "That's all I've heard around here for three years because people love to discredit us and what we've accomplished."

Cignetti then spent nearly two minutes breaking down each of Indiana's 10 games before ending on a defiant note.

"I think it feeds into our motivation, and our chip," Cignetti said. "And that's why I'm not afraid to keep it alive. And you know what? I'm 65 years old, and I really don't give a sh*t."

Dating back to his "Google me" line, Cignetti has shown it's wise to take him at his word. But his clown emoji usage suggests he might give a little bit of a sh*t. As he should.
