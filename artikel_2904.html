Addus HomeCare: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Management is weighing clinical acquisitions in home health and hospice, a pivot that could reshape how the company deploys its balance sheet flexibility.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 25, 2026 at 11:21AM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 4, 2026, Addus HomeCare (ADUS -1.20%) reported total net service revenue of $387.7 million, up 7% year over year. Expanding regional scale matters for long-term investors because geographic coverage strengthens negotiating leverage with state Medicaid programs and managed care partners.

Three key takeaways from the call highlight how the business is positioning itself for growth.

Addus signed a definitive agreement to purchase AccentCare's personal care assets for $275 million, excluding its New York operations. The deal adds about 13,700 clients and $280 million in annualized revenue while expanding private-pay operations that don't rely on Medicaid reimbursement.

Home health same-store revenue grew 2.5% year over year while admissions jumped 13.8%, breaking a two-year streak of revenue declines. Management built this turnaround on leadership changes and cross-segment referrals from its Clinical Bridge program, which routes home health patients into hospice care.

As post-acute valuation multiples moderate, management's expanding its acquisition strategy beyond core personal care services (PCS) to target home health and hospice providers. The company paid down $40 million in bank debt during the quarter, so it has plenty of balance sheet flexibility for larger deals.

Cost discipline drives margin expansion as scale creates operating leverage across regional markets. Addus relies on its balance sheet strength and market density to broaden care networks while maintaining disciplined capital allocation.

Long-term value creation hinges on expanding geographic footprint and driving cross-segment referrals.
