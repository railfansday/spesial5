Oaktree (OCSL) Q4 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Nonaccrual exposure fell 44% while the company returned to the bond market.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Oaktree Specialty Lending (OCSL -2.16%) management emphasized debt reduction and balance sheet flexibility as key achievements for the fiscal year. The company reduced its nonaccrual exposure by 44% in dollar terms through successful credit workouts and proceeds from repayments. Management successfully accessed the debt markets with a $300 million unsecured bond offering, extending its maturity profile and maintaining leverage at the midpoint of its target range. The portfolio remains centered on senior secured lending, with first-lien debt making up nearly 80% of total investments and a focus on larger, resilient companies with median EBITDA of $180 million.
