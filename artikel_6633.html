What are the best things to buy at Todd Snyder?

There really aren’t any weak links at Todd Snyder—if they make it, it’s probably good. We’re especially partial to the tailoring, which spans the silhouette spectrum and mixes classic fabrics with the occasional showstopper. The Wythe suit, in particular, nods to the fuller ’90s cuts without veering into frumpy territory. We’re also big fans of the shirting, knitwear, five-pocket pants, and outerwear, and their polos—somehow, against all odds—get better every season. For more, check out our guide to shopping Todd Snyder.

This year in the US, Black Friday is on Friday, November 28, a day after Thanksgiving. While that’s the official date, many retailers roll out deals throughout November. You can expect the biggest discounts to happen between Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday, but be on the look out for retailers to launch sales early.

Cyber Monday lands on December 1 this year, the Monday after Thanksgiving. Most brands keep the deals rolling through the week—but don’t bank on it.
