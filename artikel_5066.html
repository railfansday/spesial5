Before the bigger targets come into play, XRP’s macro chart must confirm what the indicator is hinting at.

The macroschematic argument presented by Egrag, the popular French crypto analyst, relies on the assumption that XRP is still following the symmetrical triangle on the monthly charts. Admitting that many of XRP’s bulls have been taken by surprise when the seasoned altcoin dipped below $0.98.

Now, the analyst is drawing the public’s eye towards two crucial macro indicators: the monthly Volume Profile Visible Range (VPVR) & The Point of Control (POC). The first one’s a popular TradingView indicator that shows how much volume traded at each XRP price level across the candles visible on the chart below.
