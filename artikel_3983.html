First Phantom Blade Zero PS5 Update Makes an Early, Deadly Boss Easier

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Hua, Deadly Betrothed nerfed in PS5 patch

S-GAME has today published the first major Phantom Blade Zero update for PS5 and PC, available to download now on both platforms.

It's a relatively normal list of updates and tweaks, comprising bug fixes and adjustments — until you see that one of the game's earlier bosses has been nerfed a touch. Hua, Deadly Bethrothed, an optional boss fight in the first main Weeping Creek Valley region, has had her grab attacks made less frequent.

This update applies the nerf to the Wayfarer (easy) and Pathbreaker (normal) difficulty options. If you are on Hellwalker (hard) or the 66 Days modes, then Hua will operate as she did before.

Once you reach the Sha-Chi Tower in Weeping Creek Valley, you access the boss arena by dropping down through a hole on the ground floor of the tower. From there, you round the corner and discover Hua. Her grab attack deals very heavy damage to Soul, but now that attack will be less frequent.

I think Hua is more designed as a boss encounter you return to later on in the campaign, once you're a bit stronger and have access to better, upgraded weapons and medicines. If you face her as soon as she's accessible, then the fight is doable, but it presents a big challenge.

Elsewhere, update 1.3 on PS5 adds new audio settings to the pause menu: Hit Sound Intensity and Hit Impact Sound Intensity.

You can then expect "improved performance" in certain areas, different combat behaviour for select enemies and bosses, and improved weapon feedback. Below you'll find all the patch notes for PS5 update 1.3.
