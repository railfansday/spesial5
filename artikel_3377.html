J.K. Rowling Officially Teases New Sci-Fi Book After Harry Potter

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

While J.K. Rowling has become quite the controversial author in recent years, there's no denying the sheer scale of her success, and an intriguing update suggests she has a new sci-fi project in mind. Rowling's Harry Potter franchise is arguably the most popular and globally recognized fantasy IP in history, beginning with seven bestselling novels that captivated an entire generation of readers and hasn't slowed down since.

The original books spawned eight blockbuster film adaptations, transforming the Wizarding World into a multibillion-dollar entertainment empire that continues to expand today. With HBO's ambitious new Harry Potter television series set to premiere this Christmas, the franchise is preparing to introduce yet another generation to Hogwarts.

Now, however, Rowling has hinted at a major departure from the Harry Potter franchise, teasing a mysterious "futuristic" project on X that could mark her first foray into science fiction.

Back in 2024, J.K. Rowling gave readers a look at the projects she's working on and has apparently already started writing, with plans to release after she wraps up her Strike series. For those unaware, under the pseudonym Robert Galbraith, Rowling has found considerable success with her Cormoran Strike detective novels, which have developed a dedicated following and inspired their own television adaptation.

While the crime series hasn't come close to matching Harry Potter's extraordinary popularity, it's become a bestselling franchise in its own right, with a 10-book arc planned to complete the series. Based on her recent progress, that means Rowling's next major literary project could be closer than fans might expect.

The eighth Strike novel, The Hallmarked Man, was released in September 2025, while the ninth installment, Sleep Tight, Evangeline, was officially announced in February 2026 and is currently in development. Although a release date hasn't been confirmed, Rowling has continued sharing progress updates, suggesting the next installment is coming along nicely.

With only one more Strike novel planned after Book 9, Rowling is approaching the finish line of her detective series. She's also reportedly begun taking notes for Book 10, suggesting that preparations for the finale are already underway.

While Rowling didn't explicitly describe the mysterious project as science fiction, her comments certainly suggest that's the direction she's headed. An "Earthbound" futuristic setting could mean anything from a dystopian thriller to a story exploring advanced technology, although Rowling hasn't revealed any actual plot details.

There's also no indication whether the project will be a standalone novel or the beginning of another series. Still, considering how much work Rowling has apparently already completed, and with just two Strike books remaining, her potential first venture into science fiction could arrive sooner rather than later. For now, however, there's no official release window.
