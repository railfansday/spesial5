Tech Stocks End Dismal Week On Harsh Note; Nasdaq Slides Another 5% And Is Now Flat For The Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

A massive AI correction wreaked more havoc on Wall Street, ending one of the worst weeks in recent memory for the stock market and erasing all of the Nasdaq‘s gains for the year.

About $4 trillion in market value was wiped out across the AI sector, with Sandisk, Micron, Seagate, Applied Digital, Broadcom, Nvidia, Dell, AMD and Meta all taking big hits. The Nasdaq fell 1,261 points, or 5%, logging its seventh straight down day, the longest losing streak it has had since 2020.

The Dow and the S&P 500 fared a bit better, but still slipped 2% and 4%, respectively.

The selloff in tech coincided with two major developments in AI earlier in the week: Anthropic’s IPO and a leadership change at Nvidia. Watch on Deadline

After raising a record $81 billion in going public Thursday, Anthropic fell back near its $83 opening price mark. Earlier in the week, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, was ousted after admitting to multiple affairs, including one with a woman suspected by U.S. intelligence agencies of being a Chinese spy. The scandal is likely to disrupt the AI sector for some time due to pending investigations into the affair and also a complex web of investments making Nvidia not just the leading AI chipmaker but a key investor in many emerging companies.

Against the gloomy backdrop, core Hollywood companies fared slightly better, even if it wasn’t a banner day. Disney, Skydance and Netflix all shed between 2% and 4%. Comcast, buoyed by strong internet demand from Grand Theft Auto VI, finished up a fraction.
