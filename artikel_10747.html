Genshin Impact reveals McDonald’s Japan collab with character burgers and in-game rewards

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Genshin Impact is teaming up with McDonald’s Japan for a limited-time collaboration featuring Varesa and Escoffier burger packaging, alongside in-game rewards including a wind glider.

HoYoverse’s open-world RPG is bringing two of its food-loving characters to McDonald’s restaurants across Japan, with the collaboration running from November 18 to December 8, 2026.

Varesa and Escoffier will appear on special packaging for the chain’s Teriyaki Mac Burger and Double Teriyaki Mac Burger, while fans can also redeem rewards like an exclusive wind glider.

From November 18, chibi versions of Varesa and Escoffier will appear on Teriyaki Mac Burger and Double Teriyaki Mac Burger packaging at McDonald’s Japan branches, while supplies last.

Those who purchase a Teriyaki Mac Burger meal or Double Teriyaki Mac Burger meal and spend 300 rewards points will be able to redeem a special Genshin Impact bundle through the My McDonald’s Rewards program.

The bundle includes a wind glider, a dish and its recipe, and a namecard, which can be redeemed only once per person. Separate smartphone wallpapers featuring Varesa and Escoffier will also be available for 100 points each via the McDonald’s app.

McDonald’s Japan is also running two giveaways on X on November 10 and November 11, where each giveaway will award 100 randomly selected winners a ¥1,000 ($6) McDonald’s gift card featuring an original Genshin Impact design.

For now, the collab is exclusive to McDonald’s Japan, but hopefully fans elsewhere will get a chance to join in with a future promotion.
