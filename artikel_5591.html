Cloud for Good Appoints Former Salesforce Executive Steve Dobbs to Board of Directors

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Appointment strengthens financial services leadership as the company scales its Cumulus industry solutions and Salesforce capabilities

ASHEVILLE, N.C., Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Cloud for Good, an industry-focused Salesforce consulting partner, today announced the appointment of Steve Dobbs, former Executive Vice President of Financial Services at Salesforce, to its Board of Directors. Dobbs brings more than 25 years of enterprise software leadership and deep financial services expertise to support the company's continued expansion.

Dobbs has held senior executive roles at Salesforce, Guidewire, Google Cloud, and Oracle. Most recently, he led go-to-market strategy, enterprise sales, and AI adoption across Salesforce's Financial Services and Insurance business. His appointment comes at a pivotal moment as Cloud for Good scales its financial services practice and accelerates adoption of its recently launched Cumulus Banking and Cumulus Credit Union solutions.

"Steve brings a valuable perspective on how financial institutions can translate technology investments into measurable client outcomes," said Tal Frankfurt, Founder and CEO of Cloud for Good. "He brings a powerful combination of industry expertise, strategic vision, and experience scaling technology businesses. His understanding of the financial services landscape and ability to identify emerging market opportunities will be invaluable as we chart our next phase of growth."

"Our focus is on building a category-defining partner that pairs deep domain knowledge with modern AI and data capabilities," said Gurvendra Suri, Executive Chairman of Cloud for Good and Operating Executive with Tailwind. "Steve's experience and strategic perspective will strengthen the Board as we support Cloud for Good's next phase of growth. He will be a valuable partner to Tal and the leadership team as they expand the company's financial services business and help clients realize the potential of AI and data."

The appointment builds on Cloud for Good's acquisition of EMS Consulting in March 2026, which significantly expanded its capabilities serving banks, credit unions, and other financial institutions.
