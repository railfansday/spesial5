Oklahoma City, OK, Struggles With Low Starter Home Availability Compared to Other Cities

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A recent metro-by-metro analysis by Realtor.com® reveals where buyers can still find a starter home. For many would-be homeowners, these options have felt increasingly scarce as rising prices and climbing mortgage rates compound affordability challenges.

Furthermore, research from economists supports this trend. Nationally, the share of starter homes in active inventory fell from 38.1% in August 2019 to 36.2% in August 2026. During that same period, starter home prices rose 30.8%, from roughly $260,000 to $340,000.

As a result, some markets like Oklahoma City have experienced a downturn in available starter homes in recent years.

A starter home is typically defined as a property priced at roughly 80% of a metro's median list price. This captures the relative affordability of homes at the lower end of a local market. These homes are generally smaller and less expensive, making them accessible to a first-time buyer or a household with an Income at or below the area median.

In Oklahoma City, OK, the share of starter homes has seen a steady decline. The portion of these homes was 34.8% in 2019, which then decreased to 33.1% in 2022. As of 2026, the share has fallen further to 31.4%.

Therefore, this represents a total decrease of 3.4 percentage points between 2019 and 2026. This trend indicates a shrinking market for entry-level homes in the area, presenting a challenge for new buyers.

For those weighing the decision of renting or buying, current data suggests that renting is the more financially prudent choice for now. A July Rent Report from Realtor.com found that the cost of buying a starter home in the 50 largest metros was $858 higher per month than renting one. This 50.6% premium on homeownership makes renting the more affordable option across all 50 of the largest metros.

In Oklahoma City, OK, this trend holds, offering a clear financial advantage to renters. The median rent for an apartment was $915 as of July 2026.

Moreover, the local rental market has seen prices decrease by 1.50% year-over-year, making it an even more attractive alternative to purchasing a home with a significant down payment. (A rent vs. buy calculator can help individuals assess their personal financial situation.)
