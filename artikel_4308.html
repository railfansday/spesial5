First Look At Lego's Big Buildable Yoshi Revealed On Nintendo Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Lego has been working with Nintendo to turn the Mushroom Kingdom and its residents into toy bricks since 2020, and it has never looked back. Countless sets featuring various characters have been released since then, and the collaboration is far from done. There will be more from Lego Mario in 2027, including a new Yoshi set we were just shown for the first time in a typically Nintendo way.

Most of the time, if you want to know Nintendo news, you go to Nintendo's social accounts (or you come here, of course). However, last year, it started making big reveals via its new app, Nintendo Today. That's where you'll find its latest Lego announcement, where the first image of a big Lego Yoshi, presumably releasing next year, has been shared.

If Nintendo won't say it then I will - 2026 is the Year of Yoshi.

The teaser only shows a little bit of the completed set. About 3/4 of Lego Yoshi's face, peering down the camera like it's photobombing a different Lego set reveal going on behind it. It doesn't give much away about the set other than the implication that it's going to be a big one. Likely similar in size to the Mario and Luigi Mario Kart sets. It might even be a third Mario Kart set and continue that trend.

Annoyingly, the Nintendo Today app doesn't let you take screenshots. I can direct you to where it is, though. Just scroll down on the app's homepage until you see the Lego and Super Mario logos and hit that. The Yoshi image will then appear. Failing that, someone has managed to grab a low-quality look at the set's teaser if you'd rather skip all the faff and see a slightly grainier version below.

If the new Yoshi set is going to be a part of the Mario Kart collection, then you can expect it to be roughly 2,000 pieces, so very big for a Mario set. The original Mario Kart set came in at a little under that benchmark, while this year's Luigi was a little more. There is a chance this Yoshi is just a Yoshi and doesn't come with a kart, in which case it might be a little smaller.

That seems likely, since almost everything Nintendo does with Yoshi is aimed more at kids than it is at its older fans.

We already know that 2027 is going to be a big year for Mario's Lego collection, as the plumber has got eight new sets releasing on New Year's Day that will mark a highly requested milestone for the range - Mario minifigures. Mario fans were disappointed that the original sets turned the characters into bigger, blocky interactive toys rather than minifigures. The eight sets kicking off 2027 forgo those bigger versions for traditional minifigures, with Luigi, Bowser, Peach, and various versions of Mario among them.
