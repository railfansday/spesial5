Alaska Airlines Flight Delayed Hours After ICE Jet Bridge Brawl In Seattle

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

An Alaska Airlines flight was delayed by hours after masked ICE agents stormed the jet bridge of a departing flight to arrest a Mexican national who allegedly had a work permit had overstayed his visa (you know, “the worst of the worst”). This ended up turning into a huge jet bridge melee, leading to additional arrests, as ICE claims that someone hurled hot coffee at the officers.

In the end, the flight was delayed by hours, as the crew was no longer fit to fly, and the Port of Seattle is outraged. This is quite the story, as flagged by PYOK.

This incident happened on the morning of Saturday, November 21, 2026, and involves Alaska Airlines flight AS1306, scheduled to operate from Seattle (SEA) to Los Angeles (LAX).

This was first reported by a passenger on the flight, who explained that “ICE agents just arrested multiple people at SeaTac Airport inside the jet bridge as we were all boarding,” and went on to explain that they were “deplaning because the crew is being replaced due to poor morale.”

Video taken from the jet bridge shows masked ICE agents trying to arrest people on the jet bridge.

Masked ICE agents detained two people on a crowded airport jet bridge in Seattle as passengers were boarding a flight. Alaska Airlines said ICE officers gave them no advance notice of the operation. pic.twitter.com/r44WPTvGdv

As a result of this incident, the Alaska flight ended up being delayed by hours. It was initially supposed to depart at 8:10AM and arrive at 11:01AM, but instead, departed at 11:03AM and arrived at 1:36PM.

On November 21, 2026, at approximately 8:05 a.m. PST, ICE officers conducted a targeted enforcement operation to arrest Ricardo Larrondo Mendoza, an illegal alien from Mexico who overstayed his visa in violation of our nation’s laws.

During the arrest other travelers began to obstruct and assault our law enforcement officers—a federal crime and a felony. One passenger hurled hot coffee on the officers. Other passengers began obstructing federal officers.

The individual who threw the hot coffee was identified as Sofia Hernandez Mejia, an illegal alien from Mexico and visa overstay. She was subsequently arrested as well. She will be charged with assaulting a federal officer.

The Port of Seattle expressed its outrage about the incident, releasing the following statement:

We are outraged about this morning’s incident involving ICE and two passengers as travelers boarded AS 1306 to Los Angeles. Immigration enforcement actions at SEA are conducted without the Port’s participation or knowledge. The Port has continually asked, and local federal immigration authorities have previously agreed to protocols at SEA, and we are in conversation with DHS on whether any of these protocols were breached. However, the Port remains committed to the safety of all of our travelers and staff and airline team. This incident was not only distressing for the affected family but the passengers around them, and Port officials continue to engage federal officials to understand more about what occurred.

Washington Governor Bob Ferguson was also angry, as he held a press conference about what happened, and released the following statement:

ICE is completely and totally out of control. Like many Americans, I’m outraged by masked agents, who do not identify themselves, dragging people off jetways as they board a flight, creating chaos in our communities. Today’s actions at Seattle-Tacoma International Airport are just the latest example of ICE’s deeply un-American tactics. I’ll have more to say this afternoon.
