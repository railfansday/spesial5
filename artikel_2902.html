Kansas City Chiefs: Patrick Mahomes Receives Good News Away From Football

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Kansas City Chiefs superstar quarterback Patrick Mahomes was happy for the Kansas City Current after they clinched a semifinal spot in the NWSL.

On Saturday, the Current earned a 1-0 extra-time win over Angel City FC at CPKC Stadium, punching their ticket to the semis. Current forward Michelle Cooper broke the deadlock with a penalty kick in the 96th minute, helping end Angel City's 10-match unbeaten streak.

Patrick Mahomes, a Current co-owner since 2023, along with his wife, Brittany Mahomes, and co-founders Angie and Chris Long, welcomed the good news from his soccer team. The Chiefs QB celebrated the team's triumph by sharing the update on his Instagram story.

This is the third time in franchise history that the Current have advanced to the semifinals. They now await the winner of the match between Gotham FC and Seattle Reign as their next opponent.

Meanwhile, Patrick Mahomes and the Chiefs will now turn their attention to their Week 9 game against the New York Jets on Sunday at Arrowhead Stadium.

They look to end their two-game losing streak after an impressive 5-0 start to the season. Kansas City suffered back-to-back defeats to the Seattle Seahawks and the Denver Broncos.

The three-time Super Bowl winner and two-time league MVP has been playing well for Kansas City, completing 154 of 232 passes for 1,795 yards and 12 touchdowns.

Mahomes and Co. head into Sunday's matchup with a 5-2 record and ranked second in the AFC West behind the 6-2 Broncos. Kickoff from Arrowhead Stadium is scheduled for 1:00 p.m. ET on CBS.
