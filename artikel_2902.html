Canucks Lose Vocal Locker Room Presence in Max Sasson Trade

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Vancouver Canucks have made a Friday night trade, their specialty. Max Sasson is headed off to the New York Rangers, in exchange for a 2029 fourth-round pick.

Especially following the piece from Iain MacIntyre about Brock Boeser and Sasson's relationship, this move could sting for fans and the locker room. Management gave Sasson an opportunity to be an NHLer last season, and he has looked back ever since. Sasson was a vocal part of the team and had a mic'd-up game recently with the Canucks. From the human side of things, he will be missed, 100%.

"Someone made me a sign. Gotta get him a stick."Listen in to a mic'd up Max Sasson as the #Canucks took on the Philadelphia Flyers. 🎤 pic.twitter.com/NNOsu4REfV

— Vancouver Canucks (@Canucks) November 16, 2026

"I am gracious for my time in Vancouver and the Canucks allowing me to break into the NHL," said Sasson to Canucks reporter Rick Dhaliwal. "I am looking forward to the opportunity in New York."

"Max grew a lot as a player in our system, and his development took a big step forward during our Calder Cup championship run in Abbotsford," said Canucks General Manager Ryan Johnson in a press release. "Giving him an opportunity to establish a foothold in the NHL is something we take pride in as an organization. We thank him for his time here in Vancouver and wish him all the best with the Rangers."

The fourth-round pick coming back makes sense, as Sasson, on a playoff team, would likely be a fourth-liner or even a depth forward. What is a little baffling about this trade is that Head Coach Manny Malhotra clearly likes his speedy players down the middle, and Sasson is one of the fastest skaters on the team. He had the second-fastest speed burst on the Canucks this season at 37.25 kph on Oct. 10 against the New Jersey Devils. Sasson also had five goals and eight points in 24 games this season.

This move means that the Canucks will likely be calling up Jacob Quillan from the Abbotsford Canucks. Quillan played in three games earlier with the NHL team and was impressive with his physical presence and hard work ethic, logging two fights and an assist in his brief stint.
