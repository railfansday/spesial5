Broncos open 3-point road favorites over the Steelers for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Denver Broncos extended their win stream to six games and will be hitting the road in Week 12 to take on Aaron Rodgers and the Pittsburgh Steelers on Black Friday next week. This is one of the toughest defenses they'll face for the remainder of the season, so a win here would put them firmly in the mix for race towards the top seed in the AFC.

According to FanDuel, the Broncos are 3-point road favorites against the Steelers in Week 12. The over/under stands at 39.5 for this matchup.

It seems that perhaps oddsmakers are beginning to believe in these Broncos. Just a few weeks ago they were mere 2.5-point road favorites against the sub-.500 Carolina Panthers and now they are full 3-point favorites against the playoff contender in the Steelers.

That said, Rodgers and the Steelers struggled mightily on Sunday losing the Philadelphia Eagles on the road. They seem to be a bit of good/bad at times and it entirely depends on how good the opposing pass rush can make Rodgers uncomfortable. That should be the focus for the Broncos heading into next weeks' game.
