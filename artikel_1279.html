He Played For Arsenal and Sunderland – Now He’s Joined English Football’s Worst Team

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The English Football League is made up of 92 clubs, which are separated into four divisions: the Premier League, Championship, League One and League Two.

Many players have played in both the top flight and the fourth tier of English football. For example, Kevin Davies, Billy Sharp and even Sol Campbell.

A former Arsenal player has recently joined that list, having represented the Gunners 23 times during Arsene Wenger's reign. Not only has he dropped down to League Two, but he's now turning out for the club that is rooted to the bottom of the table.

Vito Mannone didn't just play for Arsenal during his career in the Premier League. The Italian-born goalkeeper also made 67 appearances for Sunderland.

He played with the likes of Cesc Fabregas, Robin van Persie and even Mikel Arteta at Arsenal, while he shared the pitch with Jermain Defoe and Wes Brown for Sunderland.

A former Arsenal player, who played alongside Jack Wilshere and Cesc Fabregas, was sentenced to four years in prison back in 2025.

Now, he is plying his trade at the lowest-ranked club of the 92, Crawley Town. Mannone was announced as a player-coach signing in the summer, with the club confirming he'd be in charge of the goalkeeping department.

A club statement said: "Vito will head up the goalkeeping department, taking control of all sessions and player development, but will also be available for selection as a registered member of the playing squad."

Mannone also made a statement in which he expressed his pride at the opportunity he's been given. At 38 years old, it's a great opportunity to extend his playing career while gaining experience in coaching. He told the club website:

"I am delighted and proud to take on this role and can’t wait to meet everyone at the club and to start working with the staff and players.

I’ll give everything to the cause and hope to pass on my experiences as a player, leading into a successful season."

Mannone and Crawley are dangerously close to slipping out of the EFL. But despite sitting bottom of League Two, the Red Devils have games in hand over the teams closest to them.

In his 11 appearances for the club, Mannone has conceded 13 goals and kept three clean sheets. Unfortunately, only one of those shutouts has been in the league.
