Nations League Liveblog: Belgium vs. Italy

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from the final Nations League group game, as Italy visit Belgium for quarter-final qualification and potentially top spot.

It kicks off at the King Baudouin Stadium in Brussels at 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET).

The Azzurri go into this last game as leaders on 10 points, the same as France but with a superior head-to-head record, while Belgium follow on nine.

That means a draw would suffice for Italy to reach the quarter-finals, but a victory here is required to win the group overall.

It is a remarkable situation considering Roberto Mancini opened his tenure with a 2-0 home defeat to Belgium only seven weeks ago.

“There is great disappointment, obviously. We could’ve done better in the first half, we’ll work on it, but there’s not much more to say,” Kayode told RAI Sport.

“We gave our all, we can certainly do better, but this is just how it went.”

Is there a danger this defeat can wipe out all the gains in self-belief that Italy made in recent weeks, especially with those two performances against France?

“It’s a process, we did so well in these games and created a great unit, so there is regret, but we’ll work hard and be back stronger than before.”

“They were better, we suffered too much on a physical level, in one on one situations. We started well, but they were better on the night,” Mancini told RAI Sport.

“When the ball got to the wings, we struggled to have the right pressing, they caused us problems, we felt some fatigue from the France match, but they deserved the victory, there’s nothing more to say.

“These nights can happen in the growth process, you need to learn from these experiences. Considering the way we got here, we had the chance to qualify, but we knew it would be tough, as Belgium are a strong team.

“We are sad, but this night will help us going forward.”

Italy had gone in front through Pio Esposito, but crumbled in Brussels to a 4-1 defeat against Belgium and their Nations League campaign ends here with third place.
