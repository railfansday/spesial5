The Best Skin Care Products of 2026, According to ELLE Editors

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

We tried dozens of new launches to find our Future of Beauty award winners.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

New skin care products are everywhere—the minute you’ve finished trying the latest serum or eye cream, four more just like it seem to pop up in its place. That’s why we enlisted dozens of testers to find the best of this year’s skin care launches. We narrowed down 320 submissions to the 90 most innovative to test—and after vigorously trialing, we can confidently recommend 28, including a cream that helps extend the effects of Botox, a foamy serum packed with exosomes, and a liquid pimple patch. Keep reading for the game-changing Future of Beauty award winners worth adding to your skin care routine.

Bloomeffects Black Tulip RegeneLift Eye Serum

This eye serum may be lightweight, but thanks to ingredients like peptides and bakuchiol, our tester said it instantly smooths and hydrates, leaving the eye area brighter and more lifted. “My favorite part is the metal applicator. It feels cool on the skin, which is perfect for tired mornings,” she said. Another tester loved the milky texture, noting that it absorbed quickly without pilling under makeup.
