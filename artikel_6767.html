Surprise! ABC Named Their Next Bachelor, But Who Is Sam Hartman?

Sam Hartman is a 27-year-old pro football player from Charlotte, North Carolina. He went to Wake Forest and Notre Dame, and plays for the Washington Commanders. According to Deadline, he signed “as an undrafted free agent in 2024 and currently serves as a quarterback for their practice squad.”
