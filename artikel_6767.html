These Long-Lasting Tubing Mascaras Survive Sweat, Tears, and Long Days

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

I’ve always been particular about mascara. I need a formula that lengthens my lashes to the heavens, won’t flake or smudge, and stays locked in place from morning to night. After years of testing and reviewing mascaras as a beauty editor, I’ve found that there is only one type of formula that manages to do it all: tubing mascaras.

The best tubing mascaras are in a league of their own. Instead of simply coating lashes with pigment, they wrap each individual lash in tiny, water-resistant polymers that form a flexible “tube” around the hair. The result? A mascara that won’t smear, flake, or run throughout the day, yet slides off effortlessly with warm water when it’s time to take it off.

It almost sounds too good to be true, right? The best tubing mascaras do feel like magic—team Cosmo would know. We’ve tried every tubing mascara out there, and somehow we narrowed our favorites down to this list of seven foolproof (and smudge-proof) formulas, below.

Whitfield says that removing tubing mascaras feels like conducting Cinderella’s fairy godmother’s spell. “The second the clock strikes midnight, and you get in the shower or wash your face, the fibers literally crumble right off,” she says. “You’re not fighting against the current with waterproof pigment or oily eye makeup remover to get it off. The water rinses off the mascara with minimal effort and without damaging your lashes.”
