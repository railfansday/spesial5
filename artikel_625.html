Bill Maher Mocks ‘Grand Theft Auto 6’-Playing Young Men With Awful ICE Joke

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The “Real Time” host dedicated a portion of his Season 24 finale monologue to the video game that’s captivated the nation.

The Season 24 finale of “Real Time with Bill Maher” aired Friday night — it’s been renewed for two more seasons through 2028 — and the crabby comic’s monologue tackled everything from the spate of tech outages to former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s infidelity to “Grand Theft Auto 6.”

“It’s getting a little scary out there,” offered Maher. “Last week, it was Microsoft and the airlines that went down. This week: Apple — whew that’s a big one — four of the big banks, the National Security Agency. He continued: “This is just AI doing its thing: finding vulnerabilities. They’re fixing shit by breaking shit. Didn’t we try this with DOGE? Wasn’t that the same thing? OK, but here’s my question: If these are not cyberattacks, why yesterday did they fire the top official in charge of cybersecurity? It’s like your wife saying, ‘You know, I’m definitely not having an affair, but I fired the pool boy yesterday.’”

Then, the 70-year-old political pundit took aim at the cultural phenomenon that is “GTA 6,” which hit a whopping $3.79 billion in first-day sales and led to tons of people calling out of work to play it on their PlayStation 5 or Xbox.

“Apple? Oh my gosh. I mean, I worry for young men. Really, without the phone, anyone hoping to see tits will have to talk to an actual girl,” he joked. “And if you think that joke is stereotypical or unfair, I will just remind you that ‘Grand Theft Auto’ came out yesterday, and about a fifth of the young men in America stayed home and didn’t go to work to play a game. This thing has caused more workplace absences than ICE.”
