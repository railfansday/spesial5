Deadly fire erupts near Cairo killing dozens of people

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Thirty-one people have been killed and 21 others injured after a large fire broke out on a busy highway in Cairo.

Footage circulating on social media showed billowing flames and black smoke towering high into the night sky, with multiple nearby vehicles torched.

The fire occurred on a section of a ring road in the Warraq neighbourhood, north of the old city centre. Egypt's health ministry said the injured were being treated at hospitals nearby.

Officials have not given an account of how the fire broke out, but state-owned media reported that a high-voltage cable fell in front of a fuel tanker, causing the driver to lose control and the tanker to overturn, dousing nearby vehicles with petrol.
