Democrats blast Trump’s decision to appoint Stephen Miller as chief of staff in wake of Republican midterm rout – live

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Chuck Schumer says the move to promote the controversial immigration hardliner shows that Donald Trump ‘learned nothing from his historic defeat’ in the midterms

Stephen Miller, architect of Trump’s immigration crackdown, to become White House chief of staff

Stephen Miller, one of Donald Trump’s fiercest allies and architect of the administration’s hardline deportation agenda, will now be the White House chief of staff. The role is a promotion for Miller, who currently serves as an adviser. Trump said that Miller will start the job on Monday 9 November, less than a week after Republicans’ crushing defeat in the midterm elections.

Wiles will now take a role in Trump’s political operation, which includes his Super Pac Maga Inc. On social media, she said that serving the president has been “the honor of a lifetime”. Wiles has been in the president’s orbit for over a decade, and prior to her role at the White House, she managed, alongside Chris LaCivita, Trump’s winning 2024 presidential campaign.

Trump later told reporters that he decided to promote Miller because he “wanted to make a change”. He praised Wiles, and said that Miller had been by his side “from the beginning”. “Sometimes in life you have to make moves,” the president said.

Meanwhile, Democrats blasted Trump’s decision, after Republicans faced a drubbing in the midterm elections. Chuck Schumer, the Senate minority leader, said that the president “learned nothing from his historic defeat”, and Congressman Robert Garcia – poised to be the chair of the House oversight committee – advised Miller to “lawyer up”. Garcia said that in the majority, Democrats on his panel will “continue to investigate [Stephen] Miller for his misconduct, and we will fight against his hateful policies in Congress and the courts”.

Add congressman Don Beyer, a Virginia Democrat, to the ranks of those deeply alarmed at Donald Trump naming Stephen Miller his new White House chief of staff.

“Trump’s elevation of Stephen Miller doubles down on the politics of hatred, lawlessness, and failure that the American people soundly rejected three days ago,” Beyer said in a statement.

But Trump and his Republican allies who continue to embrace the politics of hate, division, and chaos are wildly out of step with what the American people want, and they will keep suffering more defeats until they listen.”

Taylor, who later resigned to join the ranks of anti-Trump Republican memoirists and podcasters, warned on Friday:

Democrats have won nine House seats in North Carolina so far, to just one for Republicans, with four races still too close to call, including one in which a Democrat leads a Republican incumbent by just 99 votes.

The elevation of Stephen Miller, the architect of Donald Trump’s brutal and unpopular mass deportation agenda, to White House chief of staff suggests that the president has no intention of rolling back the crackdown, even after voters delivered a sweeping rebuke by electing Democrats to every House seat along the US border with Mexico.

Miller’s appointment has been widely condemned by Democrats, and some Republicans, but the man himself said in a statement that he is “honored beyond words” to run the White House.

“President Trump is the most transformative political leader in American History — and he has single-handedly saved America from those who would destroy it,” Miller said, referring to the incoming Democratic majority in both houses of Congress in typically extreme fashion.

“The mission now is more clear than ever: Fight with all of our soul to support our President and defend this magnificent Republic,” Miller added, in a clear signal that the final two years of Trump’s presidency will pit a maximally combative White House against Congress.

Against the backdrop of Susie Wiles’ departure from the White House, it’s worth noting that Calley Means, a former senior adviser to health secretary Robert F Kennedy Jr who resigned just weeks before the US midterms, said that Wiles is the most “powerful chief of staff in modern American history” who has “taken a stranglehold on policy decisions”.

Means, an architect’s of the administration’s Make America Healthy Again (Maha) agenda, claimed that Wiles’ former work as a lobbyist at Ballard partners had compromised her support for various policies, particularly his proposals to make children’s diets healthier, in order to still receive financial boosts throughout the election cycle.

“Susie said to me many times: ‘We need money for the midterms, Calley. We need money for the midterms,’” Means said on a two-hour episode of Joe Rogan’s podcast.

“It is absolutely true that [Trump’s] administration has been infiltrated by the very people that he energized voters to say he would get out of DC,” Means told Rogan. “Everything in the political strategy was based on getting enough money for the midterms, not actually doing the things that would then energize voters enough.”

Congressman Hakeem Jeffries, who is poised to become the next US House speaker when Democrats retake the majority in the new year, called Stephen Miller “a dangerous xenophobe and reckless extremist who has American blood on his hands”.

Jeffries added: “His [Miller’s] presence in government should be eradicated like a malignant tumor. Fire him today.”
