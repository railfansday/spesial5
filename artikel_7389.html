Zambia formally indicts opposition leader on treason charges

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Rights groups have accused Zambian authorities of political repression since the August election, which international observers said was marred by irregularities.

(AFP) — Zambian prosecutors on Monday indicted the country’s main opposition leader on treason charges over an alleged plot to overthrow the government.

Brian Mundubile, who finished second to President Hakainde Hichilema in the Aug. 13 general election, and his running mate have been in police custody for nearly three months after claiming victory.

Prosecutors charged the two and 12 others with treason, according to court documents reviewed by AFP.

Prosecutors allege the group plotted to overthrow the government between April and August 2026 and that Mundubile and a businessman provided funding for military training in Russia as part of the plot.

Mundubile, a former government minister, has previously denied wrongdoing.

Magistrate Nthandose Chabala referred the case to the High Court for trial. It was unclear when proceedings would begin.

If convicted, the defendants could face life in prison.

Rights groups have accused Zambian authorities of political repression since the August election, which international observers said was marred by irregularities.

During Hichilema’s first term, opposition parties and civil society groups accused his government of restricting dissent. The government has denied the allegations.

In September, authorities canceled the passports of six government critics while they were abroad. Critics say the move is another sign of growing repression in the copper-rich southern African nation.
