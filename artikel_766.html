See Taylor Swift React to CMAs Joke About Travis Kelce Wedding

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Taylor Swift burst into laughter when CMA Awards 2026 host Lainey Wilson and longtime pal Brad Paisley made a joke about her and husband Travis Kelce having their wedding at Madison Square Garden.

Taylor Swift was patient zero for wedding jokes at the 2026 CMA Awards.

The 12-time CMA Award winner returned to country music's biggest night for the first time in 10 years on Nov. 18, and couldn't help to laugh when host Lainey Wilson and longtime friend Brad Paisley poked fun at her lavish nuptials with husband Travis Kelce. (Click here to see all the stars at the awards show.)

"I'll never forget what my husband said to me when we went looking for a wedding venue," Wilson, who tied the knot with Devlin "Duck" Hodges in May, shared before breaking out into song with Paisley: "'I beg your pardon, but I never promised you Madison Square Garden.'"

The quip—a play on Lynn Anderson's 1999 hit "I Beg Your Pardon (I Never Promised You A Rose Garden)" and Swift's wedding venue—prompted the "Patient Zero" singer to burst into laughter as she buried her face into pal Brittany Mahomes' shoulder. After regaining her composure, Swift mouthed to Wilson and Paisley, "That was good."

And when Paisley pointed out that he was actually one of the 1,000 guests who attended Swift's wedding, she played along with the bit.

"Remember me?" he asked as the "Blank Space" artist nodded along. "I ate all the shrimp."

But jokes aside, Paisley was touched when he and his wife Kimberly Williams-Paisley received an invite to Swift's wedding, the details of which were shrouded in secrecy in the days leading up to the July 3 ceremony.

"I took this personally when I got the invite, because in the invite, it said, ‘If you’re getting this, you mean a lot to us,’” he told The Associated Press earlier this year. “And it was really fun to watch the cat-and-mouse game of like, ‘We can tell you roughly where it’s gonna be. We’re not gonna say what building.'"

However, according to Paisley, the nuptials ended up being "one of the least-pretentious weddings I’ve ever seen, or ever will see.”

As for why Swift and Kelce picked the 22,000-seater arena for their white veil occasion?

“We wanted to do it somewhere where we could have a very intimate and genuine setting without a lot of distractions,” Kelce explained during a September episode of his New Heights podcast. “We needed it to be a private feel. MSG went above and beyond to give us that.”

"I can’t thank everybody enough that showed up but also that helped put the show on,” Kelce added. “It was so cool to see everyone from my childhood not only mesh with Taylor, but [the people] from her childhood and her family and friends.”
