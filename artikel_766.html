Carlyle, KKR Back Litmus with $475 Million in Fresh Funding

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The fast-growing catalog company owns songs by Katy Perry, Keith Urban, Maluma and more.

By Elizabeth Dilts Marshall

Catalog investment firm Litmus Music, the owner of music by Katy Perry, Keith Urban and Randy Newman, said on Wednesday (Nov. 18) it secured $475 million in new capital commitments from majority owner Carlyle and KKR.

Founded in 2022 by music industry veterans Hank Forsyth and Dan McCarroll with $500 million in equity investment and debt from Carlyle Global Credit, along with capital from management, Litmus says it now has roughly $1.4 billion in assets under management.

Its portfolio of more than 100,000 copyrights and recordings includes songs by benny blanco, Juice WRLD and Maluma, and earlier this year the company was in talks to acquire Northleaf Capital’s Crescendo catalog, which contained certain rights to music performed by The Who, T. Rex and Ingrid Michaelson.

The new capital commitments, which bring KKR on as a new strategic investor, will go to fund acquisitions of music catalogs and related rights for the upcoming years.

“This investment marks an exciting next chapter for our company, providing meaningful additional capital to pursue new deals and accelerate growth,” Hank Forsyth, co-founder and CEO, said in a statement.

Since 2017, Carlyle’s global credit division has deployed more than $6.1 billion of capital across sports, music, television and film, including in the leading sound and staging company Clair Global and Universal Music Group, an investment it has since exited.

“What began as an ambitious vision has evolved into a differentiated business with a strong foundation, established acquisition capabilities and significant opportunities for further expansion,” adds Alexander Popov, head of credit opportunities at Carlyle. “Our continued investment in Litmus reflects our conviction in the platform, its management team and the long-term opportunity in music rights.”

KKR has a long history of investing in music companies and catalog investment firms dating back to at least 2009, when it backed BMG with 250 million euros ($274 million) to acquire publishing assets. KKR and BMG teamed up again in 2021 to acquire music rights from artists including John Legend and ZZ Top. The alternative investment firm later backed Sherre Clarke’s HarbourView Equity Partners in two $500-million asset-backed securitizations (ABS) and acquired the European festivals organizer Superstruct Entertainment from Providence Equity Partners.

Prior to Litmus, Forsyth served as an executive at Warner Chappell, general manager with Blue Note and spent a decade in operations and strategy at EMI. McCarroll was president of Warner Bros. Records from 2013 to 2017, after having held a similar role at Capitol Records, where he worked closely with Katy Perry, Ryan Adams and other artists.

Evercore served as Carlyle’s financial adviser, and Latham & Watkins LLP served as its legal counsel, while Kirkland & Ellis LLP provided legal counsel to KKR.
