Grimes Makes Red Carpet Debut With Producer Elijah Weinkauf

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Grimes and music producer Elijah Weinkauf made their red carpet debut at the Nov. 12 Los Angeles premiere of Blade Runner 2099, holding hands as they posed for photos.

The “Oblivion” singer (real name Clair Boucher) confirmed her romance with music producer Elijah Weinkauf Nov. 12 as they made their red carpet debut at the Los Angeles premiere of Blade Runner 2099.

The duo hit all the right notes as they posed for cameras, with Grimes looking chic for their date night, wearing a striped black and camo green dress with long sleeves and an over-sized collar. The 38-year-old completed her look with pink glasses and pointed black and pink loafers. Weinkauf—who produces music under the name VADAKIN—looked dapper in an all black suit over a black dress shirt, finishing off the outfit with brown flip-flop sandals.

While neither Grimes nor Weinkauf has publicly commented on their relationship, the two have been dating for “some time,” according to multiple outlets. Weinkauf also co-produced the “Genesis” singer’s upcoming album PSYOPERA.

Prior to her romance with the music producer, Grimes had been dating Italian DJ Anyma, although the two reportedly split in August 2025 after going public with their romance in 2024.

The “Realiti” singer was also in an on-again, off-again relationship with Tesla founder Elon Musk—with whom she shares kids X Æ A-Xii, 6, Exa Dark Sideræl, 4, and Techno Mechanicus, 4—although the two finally called it quits in March 2022 after four years.

And while Grimes has never shied away from the spotlight, she has been careful with how much she shares about her children.

"I try to never talk abt or show my kids because they deserve the right to anonymity but i just have to say their pure aura is unmatched," Grimes wrote on X in January. "Might be biased but this seems unusual."

She also admitted that she and Musk have different views on how much to share publicly about their kids.

"I have tried begging the public and my kids dad to keep them offline, and I've tried legal recourse," she shared on X in March 2025. "The state of my children's lives being public is of grave concern to me and I think about how to solve this every day. It's insane to me that theres no way to deal with this."
