Britney Spears Apologizes to Her Family on Instagram: ‘I Just Miss Them Like Crazy’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The pop star wrote that she regretted raising past grievances publicly, five years after the end of the conservatorship that fractured her relationship with her parents and sister.

Britney Spears posted a public apology to her family on Saturday (Nov. 21), writing on Instagram that she regretted revisiting past conflicts in public.

She described her own conduct as “childish and embarrassing,” adding: “Thoughts on the past, I’m obviously human, and there actually might be something to those things I said psychology-wise; either way, I’m sorry to my family for bringing up those memories.”

Spears said the conversation had never taken place directly. “I’ve never actually spoken to them face to face on it,” she wrote. “I just miss them like crazy! And I’m very sorry!” She also called herself “blessed to have such a beautiful family.”

Spears is the eldest child of Jamie and Lynne Spears, who are divorced. She has a brother, Bryan, and a sister, Jamie Lynn Spears.

The relationship has been publicly strained for years. Spears spent 13 years under a conservatorship, established in February 2008 with her father as conservator, which gave others control over her finances, career and personal decisions. After an explosive court hearing in June 2021 in which she testified against the arrangement, a Los Angeles judge suspended Jamie Spears that September and terminated the conservatorship on Nov. 12, 2021.

In the years since, Spears has repeatedly addressed the fallout. “Honestly my family embarrassed me and hurt me deeply,” she wrote in December 2021, a month after the termination. Her 2023 memoir The Woman in Me detailed her account of the arrangement, including her view that it “stripped me of my womanhood, made me into a child,” and addressed tensions with her sister.

The sisters’ relationship had already become public: Spears sent Jamie Lynn a cease and desist letter in early 2022 over claims in her sibling’s memoir Things I Should Have Said, published two months after the conservatorship ended. The pair appeared to move toward reconciliation in mid-2023, when Spears posted about visiting the set of Jamie Lynn’s film Zoey 102.

Spears has continued releasing music since the conservatorship ended, including “Hold Me Closer” with Elton John in 2022 and “Mind Your Business” with will.i.am in 2023. Earlier this month, a 25th anniversary edition of her self-titled third album arrived with 11 bonus tracks, five of them previously unreleased.
