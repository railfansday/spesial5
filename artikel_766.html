LCD Soundsystem to Headline First of Manchester’s New Platt Fields Park Concerts in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The show will kick off a run of 25,000-capacity gigs planned at the venue.

LCD Soundsystem will headline Manchester’s Platt Fields Park next summer, marking the return of live music at the venue for the first time in more than 15 years. Explore LCD Soundsystem See latest videos, charts and news

The show, announced on Monday (Nov. 16) will take place on July 30 and be the first of a new series of 25,000-capacity outdoor gigs planned at the venue next year, with further headline acts set to be revealed in the coming weeks. The legendary New York band, led by James Murphy, will be joined by a slate of support acts including Mercury Prize-winning Scottish group Young Fathers, rising duo Cowboy Hunters and DJs Erol Alkan and Luke Una. Tickets will go live at 10 a.m. GMT on Nov. 20 and will be available via Plattform, where further information can be found.

The park’s last major shows took place in 2011, when the Parklife Weekender brought a host of acts to Platt Fields, including Chase & Status, Kelis, Two Door Cinema Club, Mark Ronson, Jamie xx and Skrillex, among others. The festival later moved to Heaton Park, its current home, in 2013 as it expanded.

Sam Kandel, event director and founder of promoters The Warehouse Project, said in a statement: “We’re so proud to be part of the team delivering the return of live music events to Platt Fields Park. Everyone in the city knows Platt Fields has its own unique character, shaped by its fantastic location and the diverse communities who live alongside it, and we’re planning to create some truly special moments and memories there for everyone.”

He added: “The return of LCD Soundsystem to Manchester, performing in the beautiful surrounds of Platt Fields Park, after nearly a decade away is, we think, the perfect place to start!”

The event will run as a collaboration between The Warehouse Project, Live Nation and SJM Concerts.

Councilor Shazia Butt, executive member for culture, leisure and libraries for Manchester City Council, added: “With 2027 already set to be yet another massive year of live music in the city — bringing with it huge benefits for the city’s economy, local businesses and our residents — we’re looking forward to adding to this next summer with the return once more of live music to our amazing Platt Fields Park, and to welcoming even more visitors to our fantastic city.

“We’ll be working closely with concert organizers to ensure maximum benefit for Platt Fields and for the local community from the gigs, both in the run-up to, during and after they’ve taken place, and we look forward to sharing more information on this in the coming months.”

LCD Soundsystem’s most recent full-length release arrived in the form of 2017’s American Dream, which peaked at No. 3 on the U.K.’s Official Albums Chart and became the band’s first No. 1 on the Billboard 200. The record arrived five years after the band announced its split with a farewell concert at New York’s Madison Square Garden; the group has since gone on to tour extensively, including multiple residencies at London’s Brixton Academy and a Glastonbury appearance in 2024.

More recently, Harry Styles has cited LCD Soundsystem as a major influence on his latest album, Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally, which dropped in March. In the run-up to its release, Styles revealed that seeing the band perform live helped shape both the record’s sound and his approach to performing. The connection will come full circle as the band joins Styles as special guests for the Rome dates of his Together, Together tour next year, playing two shows at the Circo Massimo on Aug. 7 and 8.
