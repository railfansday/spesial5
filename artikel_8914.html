medical scans and vascular cable networks construct letters in experimental bio-typography poster series

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

what happens when constant connection moves beyond the screen and into the body? across four experimental bio-typography works, cells, translucent organisms, medical scans, and vessel-like cables form letters, tracing how social media reshapes identity and perception.

Hiroshi Imaeda edited by christina vergopoulou November 24, 2026 2 min

Developed by graphic designer Hiroshi Imaeda (PENETRATE), CONNECTION / LONELINESS is a self-initiated series of four experimental bio-typography works, MEET, OOZE, PROTOCOL, and SEEP, designed to function across both digital displays and printed posters. Treating typographic characters as biological structures, the series uses cellular matter, translucent organisms, medical imaging, and vascular cable networks to translate digital social media interactions into physical, anatomical forms.

Each poster by graphic designer Hiroshi Imaeda (PENETRATE) investigates a distinct visual state: MEET intersects two bands of cellular structures into an X that fractures as connections multiply; OOZE constructs letters from intertwining, jellyfish-like organisms that expand and dissolve; PROTOCOL builds typography from cross-sectional brain scans that frame identity as diagnostic data; and SEEP replaces cerebral blood vessels with tangled cables to depict digital networks infiltrating human consciousness.

To construct the series, Hiroshi Imaeda first designed the foundational letterform structures, layouts, and art direction. AI image generation was then applied primarily to introduce organic and biological textures onto the custom typographic skeletons, followed by manual composition, material refinement, typography, and print preparation executed in Adobe Illustrator and Adobe Photoshop.
