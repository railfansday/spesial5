Clara Luvanga is the striker Washington needed, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Washington Spirit confirmed their place in the National Women’s Soccer League playoff semifinals in emphatic fashion, routing the Portland Thorns 4-1 at Audi Field last Sunday. It was an exceptional attacking display and their new signing, Clara Luvanga, was right at the heart of it all.

Luvanga only joined the Spirit on September 9, and this was just her third start for the club, but she scored one goal and set up two more, tearing Portland’s defense apart with the quality of her play on and off the ball.

Portland’s defense was under incessant scrutiny from the first whistle, with Luvanga leading Washington’s defense from the front and pressing early on in the Thorns’ build-up. Luvanga steered the press, splitting Portland’s center-backs, denying sideways passing between them and forcing them down one side of the field.
