Bear Grylls, Dhar Mann, Erin Foster, Lonnie Ali, Cameron Alexander and Sonja Warfield to Be Honored at Variety’s Third Annual Faith & Spirituality in Entertainment Honors

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Rainn Wilson will host the evening presented by the Coalition for Faith & Media on Dec. 2.

Variety has announced its Faith and Spirituality in Entertainment Visionary Awards honorees who will be recognized at the third annual Faith and Spirituality in Entertainment Honors, presented by the Coalition for Faith & Media in Los Angeles, on Dec. 2.

The Honors will celebrate individuals who are supporting often underrepresented themes of faith and spirituality across film and TV. The event coincides with Variety’s annual Faith & Media Impact Report, recognizing those who are championing belief-centered storytelling within the entertainment industry. The honorees for the evening include: Bear Grylls, two-time Emmy Award-winner and best-selling author; Dhar Mann, founder Dhar Mann Studios; Erin Foster, creator, “Nobody Wants This”; Lonnie Ali, executive producer of Muhammad Ali TV series “The Greatest”; Cameron Alexander, screenwriter, “Heart of the Beast”; and Sonja Warfield, the Emmy-nominated co-showrunner and executive producer for HBO’s highly awarded drama, “The Gilded Age.”

Grylls is a former British Special Forces soldier, who became one of the youngest climbers to summit Mount Everest. He went on to host a string of hit adventure series, including Discovery Channel’s “Man vs. Wild” and “Running Wild With Bear Grylls,” now in its ninth season.

Foster is a writer, actress, and producer best known for creating the acclaimed comedy series “Nobody Wants This,” an AFI Awards honoree nominated for Golden Globe, Emmy, and Critics Choice Awards. Under her Fatigue Sisters overall deal with 20th Television, she is currently developing the drama “These Summer Storms,” based on Sarah MacLean’s novel, for Hulu. Beyond television, Erin co-owns the fashion brand Favorite Daughter and co-hosts the award-winning “The World’s First Podcast.”

Mann is an influential storyteller and founder of Dhar Mann Studios, an audience-first global entertainment company dedicated to uplifting audiences through stories and experiences that help people feel seen and connected. The studio reaches more than 170 million followers and generates over 320 million views each week across platforms.

Ali is an executive producer on the upcoming Amazon biopic series “The Greatest” and the documentary, “The City of Ali.” She is also the creator and co-host of the award-winning podcast “Ali in Me” on Audible and will executive produce a planned documentary about the relationship between Muhammad Ali and legendary broadcaster Howard Cosell. She and her husband opened the Muhammad Ali Center in Louisville in November of 2005, where she serves as a lifetime director and vice chair of the board. The Muhammad Ali Center’s mission is to inspire greatness, cultivate curious and courageous leaders, and grow a more compassionate world.

Cameron’s original feature screenplay, “Heart of the Beast,” is a survival thriller film starring Brad Pitt that was released in September this year. He recently wrote “Dreams in Antlered Moonlight,” which he will direct in his feature directorial debut.

Warfield is a television writer, producer and author whose credits include her work on the Emmy Award-winning NBC sitcom “Will & Grace” as well as BET’s hit comedy “The Game.” Currently, she’s the Emmy-nominated executive producer and co-showrunner for HBO’s highly awarded drama, “The Gilded Age.”

The night will feature award presentations to the honorees, alongside a networking reception and seated dinner, hosted by Rainn Wilson.

Wilson is the Emmy-nominated and SAG award-winning actor best known for playing Dwight Schrute for nine seasons on “The Office.” He is also a New York Times best-selling author, whose latest book is entitled “The Soul Boom Workbook: Spiritual Tools for Modern Living.” He co-founded the digital media company SoulPancake and Lide Haiti, an arts and education non-profit in rural Haiti.

“We are honored to partner with the Coalition for Faith & Media and Fetzer Institute to advance a meaningful conversation about the portrayal of faith and spirituality in media,” said Dea Lawrence, co-president and publisher of Variety. “Media plays a powerful role in shaping cultural understanding and influencing how we see one another. Creating greater awareness of the diverse ways faith and spirituality inform people’s lives is essential to fostering more authentic, nuanced and inclusive storytelling.”

“More than three-quarters of the world identifies as religious, spiritual, or a person of faith,” said Brooke Zaugg, executive director, Coalition for Faith and Media. “Audiences want to see their values reflected in the stories they watch. It’s good business and a meaningful way to create greater understanding of those around us. We’re grateful for Variety’s leadership, honoring the diverse industry voices who are bringing faith to the screen in artful, nuanced, and entertaining ways.”

Past honorees have included Mo Amer, Tiffany Haddish, Viola Davis, Sheryl Lee Ralph, Jay Shetty, and Julius Tennon.
