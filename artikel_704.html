Jennifer Lawrence Thinks Her ‘Hunger Games’ Bow and Arrow Prop Was Stolen

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

When asked by Andy Cohen if she was able to snag the iconic prop her character Katniss Everdeen used in the films, the actress realized on-air she may have "lost it, or I guess it was stolen."

Jennifer Lawrence thinks a bow and arrow she received after appearing on The Hunger Games may have been stolen from her.

The Oscar winner appeared on Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen on Thursday night alongside Josh Hutcherson, marking their first joint interview in over 10 years. The appearance was tied to the release of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, the prequel film where they reprise their respective roles of Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark.

Andy Cohen questioned them about a number of Hunger Games-related topics, including if they agree that Catching Fire is the best movie in the franchise (Lawrence agreed, while Hutcherson said the first Hunger Games is his favorite) and their first impressions of each other when they first met to work on the film. The host also asked if they were able to keep any props from set, to which the Die My Love actress said she still has a pair of green pants Katniss wore in the original 2012 film, while Hutcherson said he also has a pair of Peeta Mellark’s sweatpants from the second movie.

Cohen asked Lawrence if she was able to obtain one of her character’s famed bow and arrows, which led her to a realization. “I think I lost it, or I guess it was stolen,” Lawrence said, dumbfounded. “Where am I going to lose it?”

“Someone stole your bow from Hunger Games?” Hutcherson said while Lawrence had the realization on-air, as she added, “I’m just actually learning this in real time that they did.”

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping centers on Haymitch Abernathy’s time in the Hunger Games’ second Quarter Quell. It is the second prequel film to be released in the franchise, following 2023’s Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

Sources told The Hollywood Reporter that Sunrise on the Reaping is likely to surpass $100 million in its box office debut this weekend.
