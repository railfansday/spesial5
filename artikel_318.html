YouTube will once again be part of Billboard chart calculations in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Billboard will give more weight to free, ad-supported listening in its methodology.

Starting next year, YouTube performance will once again factor into where music places on the Billboard charts. The Google-owned platform opted to stop sharing its data with Billboard in December 2025, claiming that the charts didn't assign sufficient value to free, ad-supported listening in their calculations.

As a result of these new equivalence rules, YouTube is now willing to share its data with the charting company again. The update to its previous announcement about withdrawing from Billboard's accounts simply states: "We are returning to the Billboard charts starting in 2027. Billboard's new methodology better reflects how fans engage with music today across both subscription and ad-supported services."
