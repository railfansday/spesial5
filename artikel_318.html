Everything to know about Best Buys Black Friday sale: Best deals, doorbusters, store hours, ad highlights

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It's time to start hatching a plan for Black Friday 2026. Most major retailers treat the entire month like one drawn-out holiday sale, and Best Buy is no exception. The electronics retailer has announced plans to roll out two waves of early Black Friday deals before a 10-day savings extravaganza around Black Friday proper (Nov. 27).

Having covered Black Friday sales for nearly a decade, Mashable's reporters and editors agree that Best Buy is the place to shop if you're looking for Black Friday deals on big-ticket items like large TVs, Windows laptops, gaming consoles, and espresso machines. This year, the pressure is on for it to combat price hikes related to the global memory crisis, which has made many popular tech gifts way more expensive than usual.

Here's everything you need to know about Best Buy's Black Friday sale before you start shopping.

Best Buy's Black Friday sale will run from Thursday, Nov. 19 through Saturday, Nov. 28. Paid members of the My Best Buy program (Plus and Total tiers) will earn double points on their purchases from Nov. 19 to Dec. 6, the Sunday after Cyber Monday. Those points are redeemable for reward certificates.

Some of Best Buy's Black Friday deals will be available early. The retailer is releasing batches of Early Black Friday Deals and Doorbusters on Friday, Nov. 6 and Nov. 13 as a way of "giving shoppers an early jump on some of the season’s best offers," per a Best Buy blog post.

Best Buy stores will open at 6 a.m. local time on Friday, Nov. 27, aka Black Friday. Stores will remain open until 10 p.m. local time. But do you need to line up hours beforehand? Not unless the product you're after is sold out online but still available in stores.

Yes, Best Buy is showcasing limited-time doorbuster deals from Nov. 19 to 28. These deals won't last long. Each one is being offered in limited quantities, so when it's sold out, it's really gone. Best Buy doesn't price-match competitors' deals during Black Friday, but it will price-match its own deals. If something you've bought there from Oct. 30 to Dec. 31 goes on sale during that period, you can request that your purchase be adjusted to the lower price through Jan. 15, 2027. The only items that aren't covered by this price-match guarantee are holiday decorations and products that come with a third-party contract (like smartphones).

Under its extended holiday return policy, Best Buy will accept returns for most items purchased from Oct. 31 to Dec. 31 through Jan. 15, 2027. That deadline stretches to Jan. 31 for paid My Best Buy members.

Similar exclusions apply here. Holiday decorations have a 15-day return window, for one. "Activatable" devices (meaning those that come with data plans) can only be returned within 14 days, and they have a $45 restocking fee if opened. Scooters, drones, digital cameras, action cameras, lenses, projectors, fitness recovery devices, cold tubs, saunas, and special-order products have a 15% restocking fee if they're unboxed.

Best Buy's Cyber Monday sale actually begins on Sunday. The sale starts Sunday, Nov. 29, and runs through Monday, Nov. 30. According to Best Buy, these two days offer savings of up to 50%, with the opportunity to score extra deals during a limited-time flash sale.

And because Best Buy is feeling generous, from Dec. 1-6, Best Buy will have more Cyber Week deals incoming.
