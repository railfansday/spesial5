Tanggal: 2026-11-04

As a woman of Italian descent (who also happens to have hormonal issues), I’m no stranger to unwanted hair growth. I’ve tried hair removal creams, wax strips, the fanciest razors money could buy, and, most recently, IPL devices. And despite my best efforts to find one thing that does it all, keeping my skin smooth and hairless takes a combination of tools. Apparently, this is a group project.

It’s also an expensive one. Between replacing razors and buying a device that promises less shaving in the future, the cost of grooming adds up. Throw in the pink tax, and I’d rather wait for a sale than pay full price for the privilege of removing hair I didn’t ask for.

Luckily, Amazon has been dropping early Black Friday grooming deals for the past few weeks, so there’s no need to wait until after Thanksgiving to see what’s on sale. Whether you’re trying to get ahead of ingrown hairs, keep a beard in check, or finally commit to IPL, these are the best early Black Friday grooming deals to shop right now. I’ll keep updating this list as prices change, so you can spend a little less on the upkeep.
