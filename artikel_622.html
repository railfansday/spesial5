‘Dancing With the Stars’ Season 35 Finale: Who’s Taking Home the Mirrorball? — Updating Live

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Season 35 of “Dancing With the Stars” is officially coming to an end. On Tuesday, Nov. 24, the five remaining celebrities — Tatyana Ali, Ezra Frech, Amber Glenn, Maura Higgins and Connor Wood — compete one last time for the mirrorball trophy. Each finalist will perform three routines: the instant dance, a freestyle, and judges’ choice.

Variety will be updating this story live, so keep refreshing through the night for updates.

Judges’ Choice:Tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik kicked off the night with a sexy Salsa to “Jamaican (Bam Bam),” directed by Carrie Ann Inaba. The duo scored a 27/30.

With direction from Derek Hough, Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov took on a quickstep to “Sing, Sing, Sing,” scoring the first perfect score of 30 of the night.

Carrie Ann suggested the Viennese waltz for Connor Wood and Rylee Arnold, and the pair danced to Noah Kahan’s “Willing and Able.” The emotional number earned the pair a 28/30.
