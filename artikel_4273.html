Fragmentary Order Looks Like Sci-Fi Escape from Tarkov, And You Can Play It This Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Covering the video games industry since 2017, with experience in news, articles, and reviews. Currently a writer at DualShockers and gaming magazines.

If you've ever wondered what Escape from Tarkov would look like if its brutal firefights took place somewhere beyond Earth, Nikita Buyanov might have just the answer. The game director behind the popular hardcore shooter has finally offered a proper look at his next project, and it seems he's not planning to make things any easier for players.

Called Fragmentary Order, the upcoming sci-fi tactical shooter just received its first gameplay reveal, featuring an impressive 14 minutes of in-engine footage. And if you're curious to see what Buyanov has been working on, you can watch the full gameplay reveal on YouTube!

Escape From Tarkov's creator reveals that his upcoming game is 'almost there.'

Set across the Solar System in 2251, Fragmentary Order imagines a future where warfare has become an outsourced business. So, instead of putting their own lives on the line, players control remotely operated clones known as RepEnts, which are deployed across large combat zones to carry out all sorts of dangerous missions.

Interestingly, losing one of these clones isn't necessarily the end of the road. In this world, death is simply another business expense, but that doesn't mean players can afford to be careless. The game features large-scale environments, changing environmental conditions, vehicles, drones, and advanced technological systems, with squads of up to four players expected to coordinate their operations.

Buyanov is also taking a more grounded approach to its sci-fi setting (as you should expect from the same mind that created Escape From Tarkov). Rather than relying on futuristic gimmicks like wall-hacks, the upcoming hardcore shooter features electromagnetic sensors that can expose players' positions, meaning every encounter requires careful planning and an understanding of the battlefield. In other words, running around without thinking twice probably won't get you very far!

Battlestate Games boss Nikita Buyanov opens up about his creative vision for his upcoming game, Fragmentary Order.

Alongside the gameplay reveal, Buyanov also announced “Prime Batch”, a paid program that gives players access to the game's upcoming closed testing sessions. So yes, some dedicated fans won't have to wait until the game's full release to get their hands on it, as closed testing sessions are expected to begin later this year.

There are two available tiers: Candidate ($44.99), which includes access to alpha sessions and major technical playtests, and Founder ($79.99), which adds earlier access to experimental builds, roadmap updates, and a private community with direct developer access. The first closed alpha wave is planned for later this year, and you can check out more about it on this page.

For now, Fragmentary Order doesn't have a confirmed release date, but its first gameplay showcase has certainly given fans plenty to discuss. If Buyanov manages to capture the same long-term appeal that made Escape from Tarkov so popular while introducing a compelling sci-fi setting, his next project could be one worth keeping an eye on.

Fragmentary Order is a next-generation multiplayer combat tactical simulator.
