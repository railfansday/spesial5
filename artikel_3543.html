Hollow Knight: Silksong’s First Expansion Details Coming Friday, Here’s How To Watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Hollow Knight: Silksong was such a highly anticipated sequel that it became a meme, so naturally as soon as it released, Hollow Knight fans started looking forward to the next big event: the promised Sea of Sorrow expansion. Team Cherry has now announced a special presentation to unveil more details, and you won't have to wait very long.

The studio has announced a "Cherry Time" for November 27 at 7 AM PT / 10 AM ET. The presentation promises "a bunch of exciting announcements, and an update on Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow." The stream is already available with a countdown, so to tune in you can come right back here and watch below.

Team Cherry Special Presentation Start Times

  • 7 AM PT
  • 10 AM ET
  • 3 PM GMT
  • 2 AM AET (Saturday)

Sea of Sorrow will be the first major expansion for Hollow Knight: Silksong, and it will be free for all players. As the name suggests, it will have an aquatic theme, featuring new areas to explore and new tools to do it with.

Hollow Knight: Silksong itself was a critical success, scoring a 90 Metacritic average and being named one of GameSpot's Best Games of 2025. Now that it's out, though, we have to start donning our clown noses for other perennial no-show game reveals.
