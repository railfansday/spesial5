Game Of Thrones: War For The Throne Officially Unveiled

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Game of Thrones has transformed many times throughout the years. What originally started as a TV show back in the 2010s now encompasses so much more, as the greater fandom has become more away of the source material and all of author George R.R. Martin's side stories. Plus, we're all still waiting for the story to actually conclude in book form.

We're knee-deep in Westeros history with shows like House of the Dragon, featuring many characters that are merely referenced in the greater mythos. Now, Game Of Thrones: War For The Throne has officially been unveiled, and you can already partake in its festivities.

Hotpie, the "Gravy Knight" is coming to Game of Thrones: Conquest as par of the War of the Throne event, as well as Mace Tyrell, "Lord of Highgarden." Fans will recall these characters being part of the show, and they're recreated with their likenesses in Conquest.

A new gear set is also coming, which focuses on ranged attack and defense, as part of the Highgarden Harvest Festival event. A new Dragon Gear set is also included called Terror of the Tundra, and predictably focuses on buffing your dragon stats.

The nobility celebrate in opulence at the Highgarden Harvest Festival, spilling wine and igniting romance. For a time, whispers of war and politics are drowned out by the mummer’s tunes. However, some still sow the seeds of discord that they intend to reap once the revelry subsides.

Look for these Featured Rewards throughout the Highgarden Harvest Festival event - New Gear: Rose of the South - New Heroes: Hotpie and Mace Tyrell - New Dragon Gear: Terror of the Tundra. Braavosi Loan trophies can be traded for prizes in the Recipes tab of your Inventory while the Maintaining Poise or Culprit Revealed events are active. Remember to use all your Braavosi Loan trophies before the Bay of Dragons event ends."

Released in 2017, this MMO mobile adventure is nearly 10 years old, and will hit a decade of service on October 19, 2027. It's hard to believe, but Game of Thrones as a brand (of course, adapted from A Song of Ice and Fire) has been around a very long time.

Never mind the age of the books (the first of which was published in 1996, and the last of which as of publication was 2011), the TV show is over 15 years old, and there have been several spinoffs either released or in the works.

Even though the ending of the Game of Thrones TV show wasn't what basically anyone wanted, it's still going strong, and has regained its spot in the limelight thanks to the success of House of the Dragon and A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms.
