Sister of Miley Cyrus Wedding Guest Wearing Yellow Reacts to Dress Code Mishap

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A wedding guest at Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando’s Palm Springs nuptials found herself at the center of social media chatter after opting for a pop of color despite the event’s strict all-black dress code.

Belgica Vargas wore a bright yellow number while joined by her husband, music producer Shawn Everett, for the romantic ceremony held on Saturday, November 21. She completed her look with a pair of black sunglasses, although that didn’t stop photos showing Belgica in her vibrant ensemble from going viral and sparking conversation online, leading her sister, Grecia, to speak out and set the record straight.

An Instagram post showing the color contrast of Belgica and other attendees read, “Even Miley Cyrus couldn’t get everyone to follow the dress code. Brides, you’re going to be okay 😅.” The clip zoomed in on Belgica as the cheeky message “GUUUUURL” appeared.

“She didn’t know guys, her husband is really good friends with Miley and if you knew my bro in law then you would totally understand,” Grecia replied in the comments section. “My sister should have known better to double check on the invite 😂. Anyway Miley is a sweetheart so she didn’t care as much as the internet does. Our family is having a few laughs.”

Grecia pointed out that it had been a while since Belgica looked at the invitation, adding, “My sister said she saw the invite 2 months ago and is gonna go home soon to see the invite and to see where they missed the memo.”

She also quipped, “Her husband is a colorful guy so we’re surprised he wore black 😂.”

Grecia later told People that Belgica was admittedly “mortified the first hour,” but luckily no one made the style snafu a bigger deal than it needed to be.

“When Miley greeted her guests and came to my sister, Miley hugged her and told her ‘You look gorgeous!’ Super genuine,” the sister added.

A source exclusively told Us that Cyrus’ big day went just as magically as she imagined, noting, “It wasn’t a typical Hollywood or traditional wedding, but was everything Miley wanted.”

The insider added, “Miley was super emotional and genuinely so happy the entire weekend. She had the biggest smile on her face the whole time.”
