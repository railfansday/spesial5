Halo: Campaign Evolved ‘Firefight’ update announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Publisher Xbox Game Studios and developer Halo Studios have announced a free update for Halo: Campaign Evolved, which will add the four-player cooperative player-versus-environment “Firefight” mode to the game.

“For nearly two decades, Firefight has been a Halo favorite, challenging Spartans to team up, hold the line, and survive wave after wave of enemies. The tradition continues this winter as we add the four-player cooperative [player-versus-environment] mode to Halo: Campaign Evolved in a free update.,” Halo Studios director of competitive engagement Tahir Hasangjekaj said.

Hasangjekaj continued, “We’re kicking things off tonight at Xbox FanFest [in Seattle], where attendees will be the first to jump in and experience Firefight across new maps and armed with plenty of weapons to help even the odds.”

Further information will be announced at Halo Fest, which will run from December 12 to 13 in Seattle, Washington.

Halo: Campaign Evolved is available now for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC via Steam and Microsoft Store. It is also available via Game Pass.
