Finding and inputting GTA 6 safe codes is a slicker way to break into the 50+ safes scattered throughout the GTA 6 map. While some safes can be opened with sheer brute force, those that require a code often offer more lucrative rewards, so finding these safe codes can be a great way to make cash fast in GTA 6.

Unfortunately, these safe codes aren't always easy to come by. While some of the safe codes in GTA 6 are set – with those critical to the story and major side missions always consisting of the same digits– many of the codes are randomized and can change from player to player. So, while I can't give you the codes for those safes, I can at least help by guiding you on how to obtain them. So read on for tips on finding these randomized safe codes and for a list of set safe codes and their locations.

There are two methods of getting GTA 6 safe codes:

Which you'll need depends on the location, but by using your Focus by pressing down on L3 and R3/LS and RS, you can highlight the location of the safe code if there is one. If you can't see one, you probably need to threaten the staff by holding down the LT/L2 button and then press X/Square to threaten. Eventually, the threaten option will turn into a 'Safe code?' option, as seen in the image above. The Avery Pawn Shop GTA 6 safe code is an example of one of these, so why not use that to try it out.Alternatively, safes that require a code can be blown open using sticky bombs. These are only unlocked once you reach the Credit Checked mission in Chapter 3, but as I've explained in my dedicated guide to getting into the Kingpin Bowling safe in GTA 6, there are a few you can pick up at the alley - and presumably other places around Leonida - before you unlock them at Ammu-Nation.

Aside from the 0420 safe code that's given to you in Chapter 1, there are three main safe codes you'll need when gathering funds in GTA 6, and they're all related to the Kolt gang in Port Gellhorn. All three codes are found in the same place, Brooks Boatworks, but since they're set, you can use them from our list above without having to go looking for them. This won't save you a lot of trouble, though, as you'll still have to get past the gang in all three locations anyway.
