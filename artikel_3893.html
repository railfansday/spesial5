All Retro Rewind New Release Unique Games and SKU Codes

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Video games have arrived in Retro Rewind, and there are some cool ones to collect when it comes to unique new releases. These are all games based on real-life titles, but their names have been changed in the game due to licensing.

Nevertheless, I will help you discover all 16 games, along with their SKU codes.

As of writing, there are 16 unique video games to discover. Just like the unique VHS tapes, all 16 of them can be identified directly using their SKU codes, allowing you to separate them in the catalog.

Just like Unique VHS Tapes, you’ll first need to unlock and own a copy of the game in your collection. Only then can you use the SKU code for orders of more copies. You can also find Limited Editions for all 16 games that appear in red.

While they have different in-game appearances, the Limited Editions share the same SKU codes as the regular version.
