One of the top premium cards, the offers many lifestyle-centered benefits and statement credits. Those include several health- and fitness-centered benefits, including an Oura Ring statement credit for up to $200 per calendar year (enrollment is required).

With the end of the calendar year fast approaching, now's a good time to decide whether you can use this credit before it resets.

Here's how this statement credit works and whether it makes sense for you.

One of the many lifestyle statement credits available through the is up to $200 each calendar year toward Oura Ring. Specifically, the statement credit applies to hardware purchases only and doesn't cover subscriptions. (Enrollment is required.)

The Oura Ring is a wearable, health-tracking smart ring. Some key data it tracks includes:

Imagine an Apple Watch or Fitbit, but in ring form. Oura currently sells the Oura Ring 4 starting at $349 and the newer Oura Ring 5 starting at $399. While this statement credit won't fully cover the cost of a ring, it will get you more than halfway there.

One notable advantage of the Oura Ring is that you don't have to charge it as often as other wearables — the Oura Ring 5 typically lasts six or more days on a full charge.

Related: The best credit cards to maximize fitness spending

Amex states that the statement credit should post within eight weeks, but some TPG staffers have seen it post within days.

If you're trying to use this statement credit before Dec. 31, don't wait for the last minute. Amex relies on the date Oura processes the transaction, which might be a day or two after you place the order.

This is particularly important if you order a sizing kit: The ring itself won't process and ship until you confirm your size in your Oura account. Place your order now to get things started.

Related: Why the new Amex Platinum changes are converting me to a cardmember

This statement credit is a more niche perk that not all cardmembers will be able to use. For starters, many people may prefer other forms of wearables. If health data doesn't interest you, this statement credit won't move the needle either.

Plus, the fact that this statement credit doesn't cover the full cost of the ring may also deter some cardmembers. The out-of-pocket cost will still run you at least $149, unless you land a good sale. You'll also need to pay for an Oura Membership at $5.99 monthly or $69.99 per year.

I hope Amex expands this statement credit to cover a subscription in the future, since it's needed to fully maximize your Oura Ring's features.

Even if Oura Ring is of interest, you may not want to buy a new one every year, meaning some years the benefit may go unused.

If you don't think you’d use the statement credit yourself, you could gift a ring to one of your health- and fitness-minded friends or family members.

It's also important to note that this is a shared statement credit, meaning authorized users do not receive their own.

This sole statement credit shouldn't be the deciding factor on whether you add this card to your wallet, but if you can use it organically, it can help recoup some of the Platinum's $895 annual fee (see rates and fees).

Related: Complete guide to authorized users on the Amex Platinum Card

The Oura Ring statement credit can be valuable for Platinum Card members who already plan to buy a fitness tracker, especially if they can stack it with a sale. However, you might find it smarter to let the statement credit go unused some or all years.

However, it would be ideal if this statement credit were expanded in the future to cover the subscription cost, as this would make it more accessible for more cardmembers to use year after year.
