Brentford knock Crawley Town out of Jersey Mike’s Trophy – here’s how the Reds players fared

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Reds had to win either in normal time or penalties to progress from the group stages and into the knockouts.

Omari Mrisho’s slip led to Brentford’s opener in the 27th minute as Domeiro Bobb-Semple poked it home at the front post. The striker scored his second early into the second half just minutes after Brentford had missed a penalty.
