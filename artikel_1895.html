'Not doing so well' - Alan Shearer's Leeds United admission as prediction for Tottenham Hotspur clash shared

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Premier League legend Alan Shearer has acknowledged Leeds United’s form has tailed off - but still believes they will wrestle a point from Tottenham Hotspur tomorrow (November 7).

Leeds are winless in five Premier League games, having lost two and drawn three since their 4-1 rout of Newcastle United on September 14.

Spurs, meanwhile, are turning a corner after a dismal start to the 2026/27 campaign.

Shearer told Betfair: “This should be a good game. Leeds are not doing so well at the minute. Spurs have found a bit of form, but Elland Road's a tough place to go. I'm going to go for a draw.

“I think he's got something going [Roberto] De Zerbi, he's got a little bit of belief there and you just need something to happen like the red cards for the opposition, but I wouldn't look too much into that. I think Spurs will be all right. I still think they need a forward, but I think it'll be a draw there, point apiece.

“De Zerbi has turned them around, but he needed to, and they've had to be patient, but he stayed calm. I think that's the most important thing.”

Leeds could be without Anton Stach and Noah Okafor, who have both seeing their preparations for the game interrupted by injury.

Dominic Calvert-Lewin also missed a session with a foot issue, but there is hope he could be fit enough to lead the line against Spurs.

Whites boss Daniel Farke said: “It's not the easiest situation at the moment especially offensive-wise, but we want to stay together and fight together and also make sure that in perhaps not the best situation, we still are able to dig in and grind out a result in this important game.”
