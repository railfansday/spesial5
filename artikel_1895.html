'Wow' - Senne Lammens blasted by ex-goalkeeper after 'shocking' error vs Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Senne Lammens must take the blame for the way in which Manchester United conceded Dominik Szoboszlai's free-kick in Sunday's 4-0 defeat Liverpool.

That is according to experienced former Everton, Tottenham and Aston Villa scout and ex-goalkeeper, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The Hungary international's strike came as a result of Leny Yoro's foul, which brought the Red Devils down to 10 men and gave them the worst possible start at Anfield.

Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz would later add to Liverpool's lead, handing Mauricio Pochettino an embarrassing defeat in his first game in charge.

Lammens would go ultimately do well to keep the score down, making seven saves as Man United faced wave after wave of attacks.

The Belgian has faced criticism at times this season, but is offered little to no protection by a clearly dysfunctional defence.

Pochettino would have already been fearing the worst when Yoro was sent off, but at 0-0, anything could have happened.

Had Man United defended the free-kick, they would have at least have had something to dig in deep and fight for.

"Shocking keeping for the free-kick, wow," King told Football Insider. "You line your wall up, four players, a guy laying on the floor, which is ridiculous as well.

"Where does the goalkeeper go and stand? In the middle of the goal. To be honest, if you're going to have a wall like that from that distance against a fellow who can hit a ball like he [Szoboszlai] can, then you've got to be within just over-touching distance of the post that you're meant to be defending as a goalkeeper.

"I blame the whole setup. The wall was fine for that post. The goalkeeper's positioning was wrong, and although he played reasonably well in the game and probably kept the score down, that goal was down to him, as far as I'm concerned.

"If the ball had gone over the wall, then fine. Great skill. I don't think the goalkeeper would have had such a full dive if he'd have taken the correct position."

In all likelihood, Liverpool would have eventually broken down the Man United defence anyway, but Pochettino will wonder.

"The keeper shouldn't be done on the side they're supposed to be," Danny Murphy said on BBC Match of the Day.

"It's a brilliant strike, we know he's technically brilliant, it's a super strike from Szoboszlai. He's more central the goalkeeper, trying to see the ball.

"Keepers will always say they want to see the ball. If he moves over to his natural position, he's not going to see the ball straight away but he will when the two Liverpool players move.

"If he's in the right position, where I think he should be, he saves it comfortably."
