Jensen Huang steps down as Nvidia CEO after affairs and spy scandal

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

CFO Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor to the Nvidia co-founder

Jensen Huang resigned as CEO of Nvidia $NVDA on Thursday, and CFO Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO, the company said. Huang also left the board, with immediate effect.

Nvidia’s board appointed Harvey C. Jones, a former CEO and executive chairman of Synopsys $SNPS, as its first-ever chair. The board is starting a search for a permanent CEO and will consider internal and external candidates. Kress will keep her CFO role while she serves as interim CEO.

"The Board has full confidence in Colette and the leadership team, and we expect the company's operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption," Jones said in a statement. Kress said in a statement that she is committed to building on the foundation Huang created over 33 years.

The resignation follows reports of two extramarital affairs. Huang, 63, admitted to infidelity in a note sent to employees on Sunday, according to The Wall Street Journal. That relationship was with Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed, a major Nvidia customer. U.S. intelligence officials suspect she is an agent of Beijing’s spy agencies.

The second affair was with Kimberly Powell, who runs Nvidia's healthcare business, according to The Wall Street Journal. Powell did not respond to the newspaper's requests for comment.

Jensen Huang rejected claims that his actions put U.S. security or the chipmaker's confidential information at risk once the reports about Alice Huang surfaced, according to CNBC. The Justice Department is heading several separate probes that touch on Alice Huang, according to The Wall Street Journal. Megaspeed and Nvidia have denied wrongdoing in a U.S. probe of suspected chip smuggling into China, according to Bloomberg. China's Foreign Ministry has called the espionage allegation unsubstantiated.

Nvidia’s board opened an independent investigation on Sunday, overseen by a committee of independent directors. The company said Tuesday that the directors retained Joon H. Kim, a former acting U.S. attorney, and the law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to conduct it. The board said it had no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised. Three House Republican committee chairs have asked Jensen Huang to appear for a private session with lawmakers on Nov. 18.

Nvidia stock closed Wednesday at $173.73, fell about 8% in after-hours trading and was down in premarket trading Thursday.

Nvidia will report third-quarter fiscal 2027 results on Tuesday, Nov. 17, as scheduled, at about 4:20 p.m. ET, followed by an earnings call at 5 p.m. ET, the company said.
