Champions League: Como vs. AEK – Probable line-ups and where to watch on TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Nico Paz is expected to return to the Como starting XI as Cesc Fabregas and his side look to maintain their impressive start to life in the Champions League with a visit from AEK at 20.00 on Tuesday evening.

Como begin the week in fourth place in the Champions League table after picking up three victories and one defeat across their first four matches in Europe’s most prestigious competition.

Como’s Champions League campaign has included impressive 4-1 victories at home against RB Leipzig and Manchester United, as well as a 9-1 thrashing of Lens, the largest winning margin by an away side in the competition’s history.

AEK, meanwhile, are currently outside of the play-off round spots, in joint-29th place with three points and three defeats to their name from their first four matches in 2026-27.

Star attacker Nico Paz is expected to return to the Como starting line-up after being given a rest during Como’s 2-0 win over Cagliari in Serie A over the weekend. The 22-year-old had only just returned from a busy international shift with Argentina in the days leading up to the Cagliari game.

There could also be a return to the starting line-up for summer signing Trevoh Chalobah following his recent return from injury, while Maximo Perrone could replace Samuele Ricci in midfield.

Como’s biggest injury news is that Italy international Moise Kean is set to spend another few months on the sidelines after suffering a setback in his return from an injury that has already kept him out of action for the last five weeks.

AEK are expected to turn to former Milan and Fiorentina striker Luka Jovic to lead their attack at the Stadio Sinigaglia on Tuesday night.

Como (4-2-3-1): Butez; Couto, Ramon, Chalobah, Valle; Perrone, Milla; Da Cunha, Paz, Baturina; Douvikas.

AEK (4-4-2): Strakosha; Rota, Moukoudi, Relvas, Pilos; Majer, Marin, Vitalis, Kutesa; Jovic, Gacinovic.

Tonight’s game will be available to watch live in the UK on TNT Sports 4 and online through HBO Max. Supporters in the USA are able to tune in via Paramount+.
