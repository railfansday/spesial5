Mamdani’s ‘Affordability’ Deception

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

It turns out affordability and socialism are incompatible. Who knew?

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani holds up bananas as he speaks at a food distribution center in Brooklyn, N.Y., July 27, 2026.

New Yorkers are coming to terms with the unenviable conditions they’ve voted themselves into, with some experiencing sticker shock and a commensurate degree of buyer’s remorse. Who could blame them? The response from Democrats to the public’s frustrations should only further reinforce the unavoidable conclusion that New Yorkers have populated city government with neophytes and ignoramuses.

New York Times reporter Emma Fitzsimmons could not have anticipated the garment-rending apoplexy that her social media post would inspire in city officials. Nevertheless, she fulfilled her reportorial duties when, after completing her Amazon.com purchase of a children’s liquid vitamin, she encountered this message from the online retailer: “NYC Council could add up to $5 per order,” it read. “Act now to protect low prices.”
