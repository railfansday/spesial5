Van Hollen scores support from another Dem amid Senate majority leader bid

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Sen. Chris Van Hollen, D-Md., looks to become the next Senate majority leader in his bid against Sen. Chuck Schumer. Van Hollen says there are 15 people to back him in this election. He also weighs in on the DSA agenda and more during 'Fox News Sunday.'

Sen. Elissa Slotkin, D-Mich., said Thursday she will back Sen. Chris Van Hollen, D-Md., as he challenges current Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer for the majority leader slot.

Van Hollen announced a bid for the role last week.

"I've been clear as well that I don't think that Senator Schumer is best positioned to be our next leader. I'm encouraged that people like Chris Van Hollen got in the race, and because I've been clear that I wasn't supporting Schumer, I will be supporting Chris Van Hollen," Slotkin stated in a video posted to social media.

"But I think no matter the outcome, it's going to be imperative for our leaders to demonstrate how they're going to incorporate that next generation, and what big and bold ideas, specifically, they're going to put forward in the first 100 days," she said.

Van Hollen shared Slotkin's post on X and wrote, "@SenatorSlotkin & I agree: the American people spoke, and we need new leadership in the United States Senate. Now is the time to act boldly, and we’re going to work together to deliver results."

Sen.-elect Abdul El-Sayed — who will become the junior senator from Michigan early next year and is backing Van Hollen for the majority leader role — also shared Slotkin's post.

"Michigan for @ChrisVanHollen," El-Sayed wrote. His post included a raised fist emoji.

Sen.-elect Peggy Flanagan of Minnesota, who is also supporting Van Hollen's leadership bid, shared Slotkin's post and added, "Americans spoke. Democrats are listening."
