How Lauren Sánchez Bezos Handles Criticism Amid Life With Jeff Bezos

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Lauren Sánchez Bezos revealed how she handles negative comments surrounding her marriage to Jeff Bezos as well as her appearance: “I’m human. I get my feelings hurt just like anyone else.”

Lauren Sánchez Bezos is focusing on the big picture.

The former Good Day L.A. anchor—who tied the knot with husband Jeff Bezos in 2025—revealed her secret to blocking out the haters, especially when it comes to criticism of her marriage and lifestyle.

“I’m human. I get my feelings hurt just like anyone else,” Lauren admitted during a Nov. 10 appearance on CBS Mornings. “But I think in this world there’s so much noise and you can’t let it get into your heart and you have to focus on the things that are really important.”

And for the 56-year-old, the issue on her mind is childhood education.

“One right now is focusing on kids with a learning difference,” she explained. “It really is important. And parents should know this that you can get your kids tested. So, if you’re in a public school and you think your kid is struggling, you just have to write a letter to your teacher and to the principal and they have to test your kid.”

She emphasized. “I stay focused on that.”

The mission is a personal one for Lauren, who’s used her experience with dyslexia as the foundation for a series of children’s books starring Flynn the Fly.

In fact, Lauren—mom to son Nikko, 25, with ex Tony Gonzalez as well as kids Evan, 20, and Ella, 18, with ex-husband Patrick Whitesell—just released her third book titled The Fly Who Flew to the North Pole.

“Flynn the fly is back—and this time, Christmas is on the line,” a synopsis for the book reads. “When her best friend Rio, a reindeer, gets chosen for Santa’s navigator training, it’s the moment he’s been dreaming of his whole life.”

“But Rio is worried: The numbers and directions keep jumbling on the page. Nothing stays still. And the test is tomorrow,” the description added. “Flynn knows that feeling. So she does what best friends do—help!”

Lauren released the first book in her The Fly Who Flew series in 2024. At the time, the philanthropist admitted she wasn’t buzzing over the idea of having her work up for public scrutiny.

"Having the spotlight on me is a little nerve-wracking," she told People that year. "I'd rather be on the other end. I'm very curious and I love asking questions. People are like, 'We haven't heard you talk in public much, and I'm like—but I was talking for 15 years on television!"

But she credited her learning difference with helping her develop tough skin.

"I think dyslexia has given me resilience," she said. "When you have dyslexia, you're so used to failing at things and it can be extremely humiliating. Especially as a young kid. But once you get through that, nothing can stop you."

"People want to put you in a box," Lauren continued. "But we don't thrive in boxes. Life is about trying new things."
