John Aloisi’s Chengdu choke to blow title chances on final day of Chinese season

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Chengdu lose their final four games to give up a 12-point lead

Dalian Yingbo FC claim title on drama-laden day in China

John Aloisi’s Chengdu Rongcheng let the Chinese Super League title slip from their grasp on the last day of the season, losing their fourth consecutive game as rivals Dalian Yingbo pipped them to the trophy following their fifth straight win.

Dalian completed a remarkable comeback by overhauling Chengdu’s lead, which once stood at 12 points, with Aloisi’s side picking up just one point in their last five matches. On the league’s final day, Chengdu lost 3-2 to Shandong Taishan, while Dalian’s 3-1 victory over Tianjin Tiger earned Li ⁠Guozu’s side ​the trophy.

The results saw the two teams finish level on 52 points, ​with Dalian winning the title on ‌a better head-to-head record ‌after recording a pair of wins over Chengdu, including in the penultimate ‌round of matches.

Dalian still needed to win on Sunday while hoping Chengdu lost, and Aloisi’s side went two goals down in Jinan when Raphael Merkies and Guilherme Madruga struck in the 15th and 23rd minutes respectively.

Felipe Silva pulled one back for Chengdu with seven minutes remaining ‌in the first half and, while Cryzan restored Shandong’s two-goal advantage in the 63rd minute, Wellington Silva’s 79th minute strike ​set up a tense finish.

Wang Ziming’s shot from close range was deflected over the bar in the third added minute as an increasingly desperate Chengdu ran out of time.

That left Dalian to secure the title in front ⁠of their own fans after Yang Mingrui gave the home ​side a fifth-minute lead.

Xadas’ ​50th minute penalty levelled ​the scores for Tianjin but Liao Jintao netted from the ​edge of the ‌area with 20 ​minutes left ​to restore Dalian’s advantage.
