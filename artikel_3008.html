Coinbase Global vs. Toast: Which Financial Stock Is a Better Buy for 2027?

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Coinbase's crypto exchange and Toast's restaurant software are growing fast, but their valuations tell very different stories for investors.

Choosing between Coinbase Global (COIN -2.92%) and Toast (TOST -0.72%) requires pitting high-growth digital assets against the steady digitization of the restaurant industry. Which path leads to better long-term portfolio growth for investors today?

Coinbase Global operates as a primary bridge between traditional finance and the crypto economy. Toast provides a comprehensive technology stack that manages everything from point-of-sale to payroll for restaurants. Both companies represent the shift toward digital-first financial services, making them frequent candidates for growth-oriented investors looking toward 2027 and beyond.

Coinbase Global generates revenue by facilitating the exchange of digital assets for retail and institutional clients. The company also offers subscription and services revenue through its partnership with Circle (CRCL -3.82%) for the creation and redemption of the USDC (USDC +0.01%) stablecoin. By providing secure storage and developer tools, it aims to be the primary gateway for the crypto economy.

In its latest annual report for fiscal 2025, revenue reached nearly $7.2 billion, representing growth of approximately 9.4% over the prior period. The company reported net income of close to $1.3 billion, resulting in a net margin of roughly 17.6%. This net margin reflects the portion of total revenue remaining as profit after all expenses are paid.

As of Coinbase's December 2025 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio, which compares total debt to shareholder equity to assess financial leverage, is approximately 0.5. The current ratio, which measures liquidity, is nearly 2.3, while free cash flow reached close to $2.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Note that stock-based compensation accounted for roughly 34.6% of operating cash flow, inflating reported cash generation, since SBC is a non-cash expense added back in the cash flow statement.

Toast provides an integrated platform that serves as the operating system for the restaurant industry. The company offers software, payment processing hardware, and financial services to more than 170,000 locations. It often partners with third parties, such as Amazon (AMZN +1.48%), to maintain its cloud infrastructure and deliver services to small and medium-sized businesses. This is a common strategy among tech stocks that focus on vertical software solutions.

In its latest annual report for fiscal 2025, Toast reported revenue of nearly $6.2 billion, a growth rate of approximately 24.1%. The company achieved net income of close to $342 million, a significant swing from its historical performance. This resulted in a net margin of roughly 5.6% for the year.

Toast's balance sheet as of December 2025 shows a debt-to-equity ratio of 0. The current ratio is approximately 2.8, and free cash flow for fiscal 2025 was nearly $608 million. Note that stock-based compensation represented roughly 36.6% of operating cash flow.

Coinbase Global faces significant revenue concentration in Bitcoin (BTC -0.49%) and Ethereum (ETH -1.84%) trading, making its results highly sensitive to price volatility. The company is also navigating intensive regulatory scrutiny, including investigations by the New York Attorney General regarding marketing practices. Furthermore, operational risks like cybersecurity threats and platform outages could affect its brand and financial stability.

Toast carries risks related to its reliance on a single bank partner for its working capital loan programs. A lawsuit from the FTC alleging deceptive pricing practices also poses a threat to its reputation and bottom line. Additionally, the company is concentrated in the small business restaurant market, which is vulnerable to inflation and broader economic downturns. Toast also relies on Amazon for platform hosting, creating a dependency on the stability of third-party services.

Toast appears cheaper based on its forward P/E and P/S ratio. These reflect future earnings estimates and sales over the past 12 months.

Coinbase Global starts at a disadvantage here because I am a crypto skeptic. Crypto has no inherent value -- it doesn't produce earnings or cash flow and has no real-world utility. Buying cryptocurrency as an investment is entirely a bet that someone will later buy it from you at a higher price.

Furthermore, Coinbase Global's fortunes are inseparable from those of crypto:

Look at how closely Coinbase's stock mimics the price movements of Bitcoin and Ethereum (except worse). To me, Coinbase seems like a second-order play on crypto, and not a very good one, to boot.

Toast has a more reasonable valuation and also recently flipped from reporting a loss to a profit. Its POS systems are ubiquitous in the restaurant industry; it feels like every restaurant either runs on Toast or Square nowadays. I don't think crypto is a legitimate investment, and Coinbase is inextricably linked to the crypto market. Toast gets my vote here.
