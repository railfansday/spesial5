Where Will USA Rare Earth Stock Be in 5 Years? This Mine Holds the Keys.

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

USA Rare Earth is building out its mine-to-magnet supply chain, and the Round Top mine in Texas will be an important part of its growth.

By Courtney Carlsen – Nov 22, 2026 at 5:30PM EST

Rare-earth elements are key ingredients in high-strength permanent magnets that enable many of today's modern electronics, clean energy technologies, and defense applications, including drones and precision-guided munitions.

The United States is taking steps to create a domestically controlled "mine-to-magnet" supply chain as it looks to reduce its dependence on China, which controls 70% of rare-earth extraction and 90% of processing, according to a research report from The Motley Fool.

The U.S. government is pouring money into rebuilding a rare-earth supply chain, and USA Rare Earth (USAR -8.73%) is one company playing a key role in unlocking America's rare-earth industry. The company made several acquisitions to hit the ground running. But over the next five years, developing the Round Top mine in Texas will be key to its long-term success.

In recent years, the U.S. has taken a pivotal role in expanding the country's rare-earth supply chains by providing funds, investing in companies in the space, setting price floors, and acting as a guarantor to break China's stronghold.

In June, the Department of Commerce finalized CHIPS Act funding of roughly $1.6 billion for USA Rare Earth, with $1.3 billion from senior secured loans and the remainder from grants.

The funding supports its efforts to enter rare-earth mining and processing, including its acquisitions of Less Common Metals and Serra Verde Group, as well as the expansion of processing and magnet-making facilities in Oklahoma and South Carolina.

In the coming years, USA Rare Earth will integrate its acquisitions, which help secure feedstock for rare-earth magnets while building out its U.S. manufacturing footprint. But a major part of its future lies in developing the Round Top deposit in West Texas.

The Round Top deposit is rich in heavy rare-earth elements (HREEs), which no U.S. mines currently produce at commercial scale. This would give USA Rare Earth a massive HREE supply, complementing its Serra Verde mine in Brazil, the largest-scale producer of HREEs outside Asia.

By owning the mine, factories, and processing, USA Rare Earth can capture margins across the entire value chain. The next five years are crucial for USA Rare Earth, and developing the Round Top project is a massive undertaking projected to cost around $1.4 billion. The company doesn't expect to begin commercial production at the Round Top until late 2028, with full-scale production coming by 2030.

For investors seeking exposure to the U.S. rare-earth supply chain build-out, USA Rare Earth is a highly speculative, risky stock, especially as it incurs gigantic costs to integrate and expand its mine-to-magnet supply chain over the next several years.
