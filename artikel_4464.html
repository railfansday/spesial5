Tesla Cybertruck had its best month in over a year. It’s still going away

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

New EV prices edged up in October, but buyers still paid less than they did a year ago. The average transaction price for a new EV in the US was $56,220, up 1% from September and down 2.7% from October 2025, according to Kelley Blue Book, a Cox Automotive brand.

Across all new vehicles, the average hit a record $51,109, up 1.3% from September and 2.1% year over year, even as prices rose more slowly than they typically do in October.

EV incentives barely budged from September’s revised 11.2% of the average transaction price to 11.3% in October. That’s up from 10.3% a year earlier and well above October’s industrywide average of 6.8%.

Tesla’s average selling price fell from September to $54,194, but it was still up 1.2% year over year. The Cybertruck helped push that average higher. Cox Automotive estimates Tesla sold 1,914 Cybertrucks in October, its best month in more than a year, at an average price of $92,380.

The sad thing is that there likely is a market for an affordable electric pickup truck in the U.S. to compete with Ford’s F150 and others. The Ford Fathom may be the best fit for that market, now.

That sales bump comes as Tesla prepares to end Cybertruck production at its Austin factory in mid-2027. Elon Musk said Tesla will dismantle the assembly line around June or July to make room for more Cybercab robotaxis.
