NSA's highly classified communications network disrupted by widespread outage

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

'Fox & Friends' examines major technology outages disrupting services nationwide after consecutive system failures hit Chase Bank and United Airlines during a 90-minute ground stop. Theresa Payton, the Fortalice Solutions CEO, joins the program to analyze the critical infrastructure concerns. The national security expert outlines essential preparedness steps following multiple high-profile tech disruptions.

The National Security Agency's internal classified communications network is experiencing an "intermittent outage," but critical communications remain intact, a person familiar with the matter told Fox News Digital on Tuesday.

The disruption affects a highly classified network known as NSA Net, which agency personnel use to communicate classified information internally.

The latest impact at one of the nation's most sensitive intelligence agencies also comes amid a string of technology outages affecting major U.S. institutions, from the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) to Microsoft and major banks, raising fresh concerns about the reliability of critical communications infrastructure.

CNN first reported the outage Tuesday, citing five people familiar with the matter.

The agency has implemented staggered arrival times for personnel at NSA to prevent tens of thousands of employees from attempting to log into their computers simultaneously as officials work to address the disruption, the person familiar with the matter said.

The issue appears to be technical in nature, according to the source, though the precise cause has not been established.

Despite the disruption, the agency has not lost critical communications capabilities and has numerous backup facilities available to maintain operations, the source said.

The NSA, which is responsible for collecting and analyzing foreign signals intelligence, plays a central role in supporting U.S. intelligence and military operations.

It remains unclear when the outage began or how long it will take to fully restore the affected network.

Fox News Digital reached out to the NSA for comment.

The disruption comes amid a string of high-profile technology outages over the last week affecting government systems and major businesses.

On Friday, the FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop after a telecommunications failure disrupted aircraft communications and radar feeds. The agency said instability spread to 17 of its 22 air route traffic control centers before flights resumed later that evening.

The disruption was attributed by the FAA to excessive data flooding its aging telecommunications network, potentially originating from two facilities in Texas and Arkansas. Officials said they had found no indication of a cyberattack.

Federal authorities are separately warning of increasingly sophisticated cyber threats. In a Nov. 13 advisory, the FBI warned that hackers are exploiting an artificial intelligence-powered penetration testing framework known as ARTEX to automate attacks against organizations worldwide.

The FBI said the technology has allowed some attackers to move from initial targeting to privileged access in less than three hours. Microsoft previously linked the framework to a China-aligned hacking group's exploitation of a ConnectWise supply-chain compromise, which involved attempts to access more than 2,250 downstream Microsoft 365 customer environments.

There is no evidence linking the NSA disruption to the ARTEX activity or the other recent outages.
