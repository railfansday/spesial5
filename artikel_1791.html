Three promotion contenders and Burnley at Bramall Lane before the end of the year – bring it on, says Sheffield United's Chris Wilder

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

After a difficult summer transfer window, his side took time to get but are now hitting their stride, with four wins in the last six matches. It feels like they might be ready to take on some of the best teams in the division at the best time of the season to get some momentum.

"I don't get fazed by that," said Wilder of the fixture list awaiting his side.

"I think sometimes they're the better games for us, where teams have to come out and open up and they are really good teams. QPR (who the Blades play away on Saturday) are playing really well. Southampton are a top side.

"Whatever happened last season, whatever opinion you've got on it (Spygate, which saw them expelled from the Championship play-offs), they would have gone up.

"I know you can't absolutely guarantee it, sometimes these things are not meant to be in the play-offs but I just think that the way they were steamrolling teams at the back end of last season, the way they dealt with Middlesbrough in the play-offs, I think they would have dealt with Hull City in my opinion.

“Not absolute guarantees because things can happen in football. But they would have been a Premier League team this season.

"I like the manager (former Barnsley coach Tonda Eckert), I think he's a really good football manager, and they've got top players.

"They've got good experience – Championship players and players that have played in the Premier League.

"Everybody looks at the fixtures and says, 'You have to deal with them' and 'That's easy.'

"They're not sometimes the easy games. Go and ask Southampton when they played Preston and they get turned over. Go and ask Middlesbrough when they draw at Watford. Preston get a result at West Ham.

"But we're looking forward to these big home games.

"Southampton at home – brilliant game for us. West Ham at home – brilliant game for us. Middlesbrough coming to Bramall Lane, Burnley coming to Bramall Lane, some top sides coming to play.

"We'll have the utmost respect for all those sides.

"And I don't think any of those sides will be thinking, 'Well, Sheffield United are just one of those teams that we can easily turn over.'

"Hopefully the crowd see it and smell it and sense it as well and give us the backing that I believe we need on Tuesday night and at times deserve."
