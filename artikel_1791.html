O’Neill salutes ‘really pleasing’ win as Celtic stay four points clear at SPL summit

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Martin O'Neill hailed "a really pleasing" victory after Celtic maintained their four-point lead at the Scottish Premiership summit with a convincing win over St. Mirren.

The Hoops were commanding 4-1 winners at Celtic Park, scoring three times in nine first-half minutes to effectively put the game beyond the visitors.

Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain, Camilo Duran and Luke McCowan put the hosts 3-0 up at half-time, while Callum McGregor added a fourth goal in the 63rd minute.

And O'Neill was full of praise for his side's performance.

"I thought we played exceptionally well, particularly in the first half," the Celtic boss told BBC Sport.

"I'm delighted to win, obviously, but the performance and the manner and way the boys took the game on.

"Alex [Oxlade-Chamberlain] played really well from the start of the game, got a little tired, which was understandable. It was nice to see Luke [McCowan] score, it was just a really pleasing afternoon."

Rangers, having moved seven adrift, reduced their deficit to four points again following a dramatic 2-1 victory over Motherwell.

It looked like the spoils would be shared at Fir Park, where Jake Girdwood-Reich cancelled out Emmanuel Fernandez's earlier header, but Ryan Naderi's deflected winner sealed all three points for the visitors two minutes from time.
