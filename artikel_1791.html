Swindon Town next manager: Ex-York City, Barnsley FC, Doncaster Rovers & Sheffield United men among favourites

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The early favourites for the Swindon Town job - including ex-York City, Barnsley, Doncaster Rovers & Sheffield United men

Figures familiar with fans of York City, Barnsley, Doncaster Rovers and Sheffield United are among the favourites to become Swindon Town’s next manager.

Ian Holloway has been relieved of his duties as Robins boss with the club sat 17th in the League Two table. A 2-0 defeat away at Newport County on Saturday (November 21) proved to be the final straw and the search for a successor is now on.

A club spokesperson said: “Swindon Town Football Club can confirm that we have parted company with first-team manager Ian Holloway.

“Since joining the club towards the end of 2024, Ian has played an important role in stabilising our on-field position and helping the club make progress season upon season. We would like to place on record our sincere thanks to Ian for his hard work, commitment and endeavour throughout his time at the Nigel Eady County Ground. We wish him every success for the future.

“However, following careful consideration of recent results and performances, the club believes that now is the right time to take a different direction. This has not been an easy decision, but the board firmly believes that it is important to make difficult decisions when necessary, always with the best interests of Swindon Town Football Club at heart.

“Our supporters are at the heart of everything we do. We want to recognise and thank every one of you for the countless miles travelled, the time, commitment and dedication shown in following the team, both home and away. Your continued support, particularly through challenging periods, demonstrates the strength and passion of this Football Club.

“The board will continue to make the decisions it believes are necessary, including difficult ones, to protect the club’s long-term interests and move us towards those shared ambitions. We ask supporters to continue standing together with us as we work towards achieving them.

“The search for a new first-team manager is already underway, and we will provide a further update in due course.

“While the board’s search for a permanent manager continues, Gavin Gunning and Steve Mildenhall will take charge of the team on an interim basis. They will be assisted by first-team coach David Carney.”
