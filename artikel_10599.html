Xfinity offers free GTA 6 speed boost to download the massive 212GB game in 30 minutes

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Xfinity handed its internet customers a free 15-day speed boost of up to 2 Gbps, so they could download GTA 6 faster.

The offer landed just as Rockstar flung open digital pre-loads on November 10, confirming the hotly anticipated game would need up to 212GB, or roughly a third of a base PS5’s usable storage.

With a download that size looming, Spectrum had already promised its customers a free gig upgrade, and it didn’t take long for Xfinity to follow.

Comcast’s November 9 announcement confirms eligible Xfinity Internet members can activate the boost through the Xfinity app or the Membership page at Xfinity.com/membership.

Once claimed, it lifts download speeds to up to 2 gigabits per second for 15 days, which Comcast says is enough to download GTA 6 in as little as 30 minutes.

The redemption window stays open until December 3, so anyone who claims it on the final day keeps the faster speeds until December 18. Meanwhile, members who don’t see the option on their Membership page can contact Xfinity Customer Service to request access.

Comcast pitched the boost as a way to beat the rush. Kohposh Kuda, Senior Vice President of Consumer Product Marketing, said millions of people would “hit download the moment Grand Theft Auto VI becomes available.” In fact, Xfinity expected the stretch from November 10 to 19 to be the busiest week ever on its network, with players barreling toward pre-loads well before launch.

The company also pointed members toward its year-round Boost a Device feature, which prioritizes the WiFi connection for up to three devices, such as a console, on a home network.
