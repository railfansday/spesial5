Seth Meyers welcomes Robert Pattinsons Bond with unreliable 007 guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond, marking a generational refresh for the 007 franchise and prompting hot takes worldwide.

On Late Night, Seth Meyers responded to the news with a quick and not-entirely accurate guide to James Bond. "Now, young people might know Robert Pattinson, but do you know James Bond?" asked Meyers. "Don't worry, we're here to help with our segment 'Getting to Know James Bond.'"

Less a list of facts and more an increasingly silly rundown of one-liners, Meyers' list of tidbits about Bond ranges from "has been played by six different actors prior to Robert Pattinson" to "is a spy, so Jensen Huang is definitely going to try to bang him." Too soon?
