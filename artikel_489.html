Starbucks reveals 2026 holiday cups: How to get new designs and the first-ever holiday pup cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

TL;DR: Starbucks just dropped its 2026 holiday cups and a holiday pup cup. The collection lands in U.S. stores nationwide on Nov. 5.

Starbucks has revealed its official 2026 holiday cup lineup, landing in U.S. stores nationwide on Nov. 5.

The 2026 aesthetic pivots hard into retro nostalgia: layered ribbons, vintage gift bows, holiday tree silhouettes, and a festive palette pairing Starbucks green and red with mint, antique gold, and creamy eggshell white. We're fans.

Starbucks has also made a savvy viral play by creating a dedicated limited-edition holiday Pup Cup.

Available at participating U.S. stores while supplies last, the pint-sized paper cup features miniature evergreen boughs and tiny red bow detailing and is filled with the traditional complimentary dollop of whipped cream. It is a calculated social media play aimed straight at TikTok and Instagram Reels feeds — expect local drive-thru lanes to be completely backed up this weekend with pet owners. I'm tempted to bring my cats.

The cup drop accompanies the official launch of Starbucks' winter menu, headlined by an all-new Tiramisu Velvet beverage platform and a Braised Short Rib Grilled Cheese.

It's important to note that this launch is different from the Red Cup Day giveaway taking place on Nov. 17. These holiday cups are made from single-use paper, unlike the free reusable cups set to launch at participating stores while supplies last.
