US Futures Rally on Anthropic Record IPO; Trump, Xi, Putin Meet

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

US equity futures rise ahead of Anthropic's trading debut. Gray markets point to a jump above 20% when shares start trading. President Trump wraps up his trip to Asia with a meeting with China's Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin. They agree to boost supply of global fuels during their trilateral meeting in Shenzhen. Lizzy Galbraith of Aberdeen discusses the implications of the meeting on "Bloomberg Brief." Keith Lerner of Truist looks ahead to Anthropic's market debut. (Source: Bloomberg)
