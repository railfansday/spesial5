Life Insurers Are Lagging in Private Markets Risk Assessments, ex-Athora CRO Says

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Life insurers haven't built up the same kind of skills to analyze private market investments in the same way as banks have and the lag compared with deployment is "fairly significant" in areas like credit risk assessment, valuation and capital allocation, according to Lukas Ziewer, a researcher and former Chief Risk Officer at Athora. BNP AM's Deborah Shire said investing in the power needed for data centers is a less risky way of playing the artificial intelligence boom as there is less downside peril. Regulators should be much more public on their views on rating agencies, said Nicholas Bamber, global head of private credit at L&G's asset management business. (Source: Bloomberg)
