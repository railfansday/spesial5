Federal Jury Convicts Dominican Republic Man of Illegally Reentering the United States after having been Deported

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

St. Thomas, USVI – U.S. Attorney Adam F. Sleeper announced today that a federal jury convicted Joan Manuel Tavarez Bueno, 34, of the Dominican Republic, for re-entry of a removed alien pursuant to 8 U.S.C. § 1326(a).  After a three-day trial, the defendant was convicted as charged. The defendant faces a statutory sentence of up to 24 months imprisonment. The Chief District Court Judge, Robert A. Molloy, will schedule a sentencing hearing to determine the sentence to be imposed.
