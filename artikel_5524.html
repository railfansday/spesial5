Linkind Brings a New Dimension to Permanent Outdoor Lighting with ColorBlend

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Dual Optics, Double the Impact: Innovative dual-lens technology creates layered colors, dynamic gradients, and stunning outdoor lighting effects.

LOS ANGELES, Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Linkind is highlighting its latest addition to the permanent outdoor lighting category: Linkind Smart Permanent Outdoor Lights ColorBlend. Featuring an innovative dual-lens optical design, ColorBlend transforms ordinary exterior lighting into a vibrant display of layered colors, dynamic patterns, and customizable effects, bringing new creative possibilities to homes throughout the year.

At the heart of ColorBlend is its distinctive dual-lens design, combining 50Â° and 100Â° optics at each light head to create two overlapping zones of illumination. Unlike conventional outdoor lights that project a single uniform color, ColorBlend enables independent color control across its dual lighting zones, producing rich gradients and multidimensional effects.

The result is a more expressive lighting experience, with colors that blend and interact to create effects inspired by natural phenomena such as sunsets and auroras. From vibrant holiday displays to subtle architectural accents, ColorBlend gives homeowners greater freedom to personalize the appearance of their homes.

With 16 million colors, more than 120 preset scenes, and AI-generated effects, ColorBlend offers extensive creative flexibility. Through the AiDot app, users can select ready-made lighting experiences, customize their own effects, or use AI to transform creative ideas into personalized lighting displays.

Beyond decorative effects, ColorBlend also delivers practical everyday illumination. With up to 12,000 lumens of brightness and tunable white light ranging from 1800K to 6500K, the system transitions seamlessly from colorful celebrations to warm, welcoming exterior lighting or crisp functional illumination.

For smart home users, Matter compatibility enables integration with popular ecosystems, including Amazon Alexa, Google Home, Apple Home, Samsung SmartThings, and more.

Designed for Permanent Outdoor Installation

Engineered for year-round outdoor use, ColorBlend features an IP67-rated light string and power adapter, along with UV-resistant materials designed to withstand prolonged outdoor exposure.

The system supports installations of up to 200 feet, with length flexibility to accommodate different rooflines and architectural layouts. Genuine 3M VHB adhesive and screws provide secure mounting, while optional Y-shaped splitter cables and splicing accessories offer additional installation flexibility.

Once installed, homeowners can adapt their lighting to different seasons, celebrations, and everyday needs without repeatedly installing and removing temporary decorations.

ColorBlend joins Linkind's growing range of permanent outdoor lighting solutions, including Linkind Smart Permanent Outdoor Lights PatternChange and Linkind Smart Permanent Outdoor Lights FlexMount, offering consumers additional options for customized outdoor illumination and reflecting Linkind's continued commitment to expanding creative and practical lighting possibilities for the home.
