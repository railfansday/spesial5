Anthropic targets an IPO valuation of up to $1.4 trillion, less than expected

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Investors were expecting roughly $1.5 trillion a week ago, but several large backers pressed for a lower number after AI stocks sold off

Anthropic launched the roadshow for its initial public offering on Tuesday, setting an expected price range of $70 to $78 a share for 825 million shares of Class A common stock.

Anthropic is selling 801,351,354 of those shares, and certain existing stockholders are selling the other 23,648,646. Anthropic will receive no proceeds from the stockholders' sales. The company also expects to give underwriters a 30-day option to buy up to 123,750,000 additional shares.

At the top of the range, Anthropic could carry a fully diluted valuation as high as $1.405 trillion, and the offering could raise as much as $64.35 billion before costs, according to CNBC. Anthropic is targeting a valuation near $1.33 trillion, and an offering priced at the midpoint would bring in upwards of $61 billion, according to The Wall Street Journal. CNN puts the midpoint valuation at about $1.1 trillion once options and unvested stock are counted.

Anthropic has applied to list its Class A stock on the Nasdaq $NDAQ Global Select Market under the symbol "ANT." A registration statement has been filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission and must become effective before any stock can be sold. Copies of the preliminary prospectus are available from Goldman Sachs $GS and Morgan Stanley $MS. Trading could begin as soon as next week, according to CNN.

Anthropic has agreed to sell $26 billion of Class N common stock in a private placement to Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom $AVGO, Google $GOOGL, Micron $MU, Microsoft $MSFT, Nvidia $NVDA and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company at the IPO price, according to CNBC. Those shares will be unregistered and exempt from underwriter lock-up agreements.

In an amended filing, the company said sales through the first three quarters, ending Sept. 30, came to $33.24 billion, a jump of about 1,200% from a year earlier. It posted a net loss of $104 billion over that span, mostly from fair-value adjustments on its derivative liabilities and its convertible notes. Run-rate revenue stood at roughly $81 billion as of late October, an increase of $10 billion over the month before.

The valuation target is well below earlier expectations. Investors were expecting roughly $1.5 trillion a week ago, but several large backers pressed for a lower number after AI stocks sold off sharply last Thursday, according to The Wall Street Journal. A figure near $2 trillion was under discussion several months back.
