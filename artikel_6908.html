Is <i><em>The Rings of Power</em></i> Coming Back for Season 4?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

There’s One Ring to rule them all, but two seasons still to go.

There is One Ring to rule them all, but there are still two more seasons to go. The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power on Prime Video is fast approaching the end of its third season, which means some fans are now asking what the future has in store for the prequel series. While there aren’t definitive answers just yet, Tolkien fans can rest assured in knowing that a fourth season is more likely to happen than not. Here’s what we know so far.

On November 25, The Rings of Power will release the final two episodes of season 3. So, is season 4 a shoo-in? Almost. Per The Hollywood Reporter in a May 2026 story, preproduction was, at the time, “eyeing a schedule that includes preproduction on a fourth season this fall, with shooting to begin in early 2027.” As far as anyone outside the production knows, this work for season 4 has been underway.

In 2017, Amazon secured the rights to produce a Lord of the Rings television series for an eye-watering price tag of $250 million. In 2018, The Hollywood Reporter wrote that negotiations between Amazon and the Tolkien estate included a five-season commitment, while Amazon maintains authority to greenlight or cancel the series at any time. In October 2025, The Ankler reported that Tolkien has a $20 million per season “kill fee” if Amazon ends the show before its fifth season.

As of right now, Amazon has not formally greenlit The Rings of Power for season 4. But some signs point to it as inevitable. Amazon has not released viewership numbers for the current season, so only Amazon knows how the show is performing on Prime Video. Whether the audience is strong enough to keep the show going or Amazon simply wants to avoid kill fees (and a negative reputation in the industry for failing to finish a pricey Lord of the Rings show), will decide the future. For now, fans should enjoy the sunrise in Rivendell while there is still one.
