Hunter Schafer Revisits Her Life in Looks, From ‘Euphoria’ to the Red Carpet

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

When it comes to red carpet dressing, few have as much fun—and take as many risks—as Hunter Schafer. The actor, who has been promoting her new series Blade Runner 2099, brings a sense of drama and experimentation to the Hollywood scene—often thanks to her right-hand stylist, Dara. So what better time than now to have 27-year-old Schafer revisit her already bountiful life in looks?

In her new Vogue video, Schafer takes a look back at some of her most memorable red carpet looks—and there were plenty to choose from. But even before she was shutting down the step and repeat, she was busy serving on the catwalks as a model. The video begins by revisiting some of her earliest shows, like walking for R13 and Marc Jacobs back in 2018. “I remember being very excited to do runway, because I’d grown up watching fashion shows and being obsessed with the models and their street style,” says Schafer, who donned an avant-garde, wide-brimmed hat on the Jacobs runway.

Since then, Schafer has brought the same fierce attitude to her red carpet approaches. In 2019, when she rose to fame thanks to her role as Jules in Euphoria, Schafer wore a gray Rodarte lace and floral-appliquéd dress that instantly cemented her as a style star to watch. “It felt so appropriate for my first carpet,” says Schafer. “It felt youthful.” Schafer has only continued to up the ante—and now totally commits to treating dress up as an artform. Who could forget, for instance, her itty-bitty feathered Ann Demeulemeester bra top and ball skirt she wore to the 2024 Vanity Fair Oscar’s Party? “This was a new era for me,” reflects Schafer. “I remember seeing this look come down the runway. I thought it was so fierce. I still think it’s insane that I wore this to an Oscars after-party.”

More recently, some of Schafer’s most noteworthy style moments have included her gauzy pink 2024 Golden Globes dress by Prada. “The organza was super long—it was essentially a train—and it so happened to be a very windy day,” recalls Schafer. “The wind picked up all the organza, and floated around me. I was really feeling my oats. The photos were awesome!” She also reminisced on her Met Gala after-party look from this year’s festivities, by Steve O Smith. “We had a fun, long night,” says Schafer of the wearing the look. “I met Connor Storrie that night and we danced together to Donna Summer’s ‘I Feel Love.’” Consider us jealous.
