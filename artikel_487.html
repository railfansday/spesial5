Nvidia Should 'Leave the Door Open' for Jensen Huang Return, Says Founders 100 ETF's Cassidy

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Lauren Cassidy, Founder & Chief Investment Officer, at Founders 100 ETF says the Nvidia board should leave the door for Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang to return dependent on the results of the investigation because he has been an 'incredible executive.' Huang was ousted from his post after reports of three affairs, including one with a woman suspected of spying for China. Longtime Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress is taking the job of interim CEO, Nvidia said on Thursday, and Huang will no longer have ties with the chipmaker. The board, which has never had a chair before, appointed director Harvey Jones to that role. (Source: Bloomberg)
