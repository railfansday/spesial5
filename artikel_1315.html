Napoli vs. Lazio: Where to watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Serie A leaders Lazio visit Napoli for Sunday lunchtime, and Max Allegri’s side are under pressure to stop their slide against Gennaro Gattuso’s high-flying Biancocelesti.

It kicks off at 12:30 CET [11:30 GMT] on Sunday at the Stadio Diego Armando Maradona, with Luca Pairetto as referee.

Lazio are top of Serie A on 28 points. Roma’s 3-0 rout of Sassuolo on Saturday cut their lead to a single point, so Gattuso knows anything less than a win could cost him first place. Their opposition today are 14 points behind on 14, and Allegri is under growing pressure after a 2-0 defeat to Juventus and a 4-1 Champions League loss at Porto.

Napoli also have a long injury list. Giovanni Di Lorenzo, Leonardo Spinazzola, Stanislav Lobotka, Benoit Badiashile, David Neres, Alisson Santos, Costantino Favasuli and Alessandro Buongiorno are all out, Mathias Olivera isn’t fully fit, and Luca Marianucci is suspended after his red card in Turin.

Lazio are without captain Mattia Zaccagni and Albert Gudmundsson, while Nuno Tavares is carrying a muscular problem.

In the UK and Ireland, Napoli vs. Lazio is live on DAZN.
