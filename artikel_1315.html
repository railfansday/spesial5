Chiellini: ‘I turned down Arsenal when I was 16, then China in 2018’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Giorgio Chiellini became a legend for Juventus, but reveals it all could’ve been very different. ‘I was supposed to sign for Arsenal at 16, but I said no because I didn’t want to leave my club for free.’

The former defender hung up his boots in the summer of 2024 after representing only four clubs throughout his entire career.

He started out at hometown club Livorno and was sold to Fiorentina in 2004, then on to Juventus a year later, where he remained until the move to LAFC in 2022.

“I was supposed to sign for Arsenal at that time, when I was 16,” revealed Chiellini on the Business of Sport podcast.

“Liam Brady was the head of the academy and I got an offer from Arsenal to sign for them. It was a big amount of money, a year was more or less what now would be €60,000, it was in 1999-00.

“I said no, and I don’t know why, because it makes no sense to reject a possibility like that. I didn’t want to leave my club for free, because if you don’t have a contract before the age of 16, you can leave for a small amount of money.”

The difference in contract laws between Italy and England means quite a few players have since been poached in this fashion, most notably Giuseppe Rossi early in his career.

However, Chiellini felt it was not the right thing to do for a Livorno side that had raised him.

“I signed a contract with Livorno for less than €1,000 a year, because it was my home, I grew up there, and I said it makes no sense to leave now.”

At that time, Arsenal had the likes of Tony Adams in the side, and he admits it is not something he’d advise to anyone else.

“If I had a son or nephew, I’d suggest they go to Arsenal, 100 per cent. I don’t know why I made that decision and my parents allowed me to do it.”

The Italy international also snubbed financial considerations later on in his career, when so many were moving to China to cash in.

“I’ve never signed a contract for money, so that was part of my career, but it was much more about reaching goals. I had an offer from China in 2018, but I didn’t do it because of my family.”
