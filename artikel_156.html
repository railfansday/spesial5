US House Democrats nominate Hakeem Jeffries for speaker

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jeffries is set to be the first Black speaker of the US House of Representatives when Democrats take control of the chamber next year. He will be second in line to the presidency.

Democrats in the US House of Representatives on Wednesday nominated Hakeem Jeffries to be the next speaker.

Jeffries, who represents a district in New York, would be the first Black person to fill the role. He received 251 out of 265 ballots cast in a closed-door, secret vote.

"Thank you House Democrats. Together, we will deliver bold change," Jeffries posted on X after the vote. "And make a big difference for the American people!"

Some progressive Democrats voted against Jeffries' speakership, with the congressman considered by some to be in the moderate wing of the party.

The entire House will do a roll call vote on January 3, 2027, to elect a speaker when the new Congress convenes. As Democrats won control of the House of Representatives in the midterm elections, claiming at least 257 of 435 seats in the lower chamber of US Congress, the 56-year-old Jeffries is all but certain to get the position.

In a rebuke of US President Donald Trump's Republican Party, Democrats also won 56 seats and control of the Senate, the upper house of US Congress.

Jeffries will steer Democratic priorities in the House, such as possible legislation and investigations of the Trump administration. He will also be second in line to the presidency after Vice President JD Vance.

Following the vote, seven of the 11 Democrats who voted against Jeffries issued a joint statement.

"Whether on Medicare for All, stopping ICE terror, getting money out of politics, or ending the genocide in Gaza, our constituents sent us to Congress with a mandate on these issues," Representatives-elect Darializa Avila Chevalier, Adam Hamawy, Frederick Haynes, Melat Kiros, Donavan McKinney, Chris Rabb and Claire Valdez wrote.

"We have worked to secure commitments from Leader Jeffries in good-faith conversations. While we have seen meaningful progress, there is still work to be done to ensure that the direction our party takes this coming Congress reflects what our constituents are demanding of leadership," they added. "Those conversations are ongoing. As such, our vote in caucus today was no."

Under Jeffries, Massachusetts Representative Katherine Clark will serve as female majority leader, and Pete Aguilar of California will serve as majority whip. Clark will be the first woman to serve in that position, with Aguilar the first Latino to fill his elected role in the House.

For the Republican House delegation in the new Congress, Jim Jordan, a representative of Ohio, will be the next House minority leader. Jordan is a staunch Trump supporter.

In the Senate, Chuck Schumer of New York is the frontrunner to lead Democrats in the upper chamber, with Maryland Senator Chris Van Hollen also in the running.

Republican South Dakota Senator John Thune, who currently serves as Senate majority leader, is expected to be minority leader next year.
