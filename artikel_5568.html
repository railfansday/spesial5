TSMC's October revenue rose nearly 60% from a year ago on AI demand

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Revenue reached NT$586.89 billion, up 14.7% from September; January-October sales rose 43.3% from a year earlier

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. reported October revenue of NT$586.89 billion on Tuesday, up 59.7% from October 2025 and 14.7% from September.

Revenue for January through October totaled NT$4.49 trillion, a 43.3% increase from the same period in 2025, the company said. TSMC $TSM also filed the figures with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission in a Form 6-K.

The October total equals $17.88 billion, according to CNBC. The world's biggest contract chipmaker is gaining from appetite for cutting-edge chips as major tech firms pour money into AI.

TSMC stock ended Tuesday’s session up 2.2%.

TSMC reported record net income last month of NT$763.06 billion for the third quarter, up 68.7% from a year earlier. It was the sixth straight quarter of record profit. Third-quarter revenue rose 50.9% from a year earlier to NT$1.49 trillion.

For the fourth quarter, TSMC expects revenue of $52.3 billion to $54.3 billion. The company also expects 2026 revenue to increase by around 44% in U.S. dollar terms.

Chairman and CEO C.C. Wei sees no sign of a slowdown in demand for AI infrastructure. TSMC has said its capital spending this year will reach as much as $64 billion, and it expects outlays across 2026 through 2028 to grow considerably compared with the previous three-year stretch.
