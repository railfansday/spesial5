David Fincher Says He Turned Down ‘The Rings of Power,’ Calls DC Studios “Dysfunctional”

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The director says he was asked to take on 'The Lord of the Rings' TV series, but balked at the "corporate" nature of the job.

Can you imagine David Fincher as the showrunner of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power?

The Social Network and Fight Club filmmaker says he was approached to creatively lead big-budget The Lord of the Rings prequel series — and turned it down.

“I got offered — what is the Lord of the Rings thing at Amazon?” Fincher asked Josh Horowitz on the Happy Sad Confused podcast.

“I got offered that … to be the showrunner. This is before it went to Amazon, right? And I was like, ‘No way.’ It’s an extension of a corporate interest.”

The director compared the idea to Guardians of the Galaxy filmmaker James Gunn taking the DC Studios co-chief job (which Gunn recently departed). “I mean, I look at James Gunn and I think, ‘Those are bigger balls than I have. I would not [want] to take on DC and all of its dysfunction. Like, its innate DNA is dysfunctional.

“I think there’s a horrible constricting of the business right now,” Fincher added. .”You know, with Paramount, Warner Bros … It’s becoming more and more difficult to protect the individual voice in cinema.”

That said, Fincher revealed that he was once “very interested in the idea of Star Wars” and had discussions with Kathleen Kennedy about taking Lucasfilm public before the company was sold to Disney in 2012.

Fincher is currently promoting his upcoming Netflix movie The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, which was written by Quentin Tarantino as a follow-up to Once Upon a Time in Hollywood (read our review).

The Rings of Power is currently airing its third season on Prime Video, with the final two episodes dropping tonight at 9 p.m. ET.
