Kodak Black Has Secret Radio Station in ‘Grand Theft Auto VI’: Here’s How to Unlock It

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Gamers also found another secret station hosted by fictional DJ Pretzel.

Since the release of Grand Theft Auto VI, gamers have been stumbling upon easter eggs that call back to Rockstar Games history and having random encounters like the viral “Terminator NPC.” They’ve also been uncovering secret radio stations.

As people continue to make it through the game’s epic story mode, they’ve unlocked two stations so far. The first one gamers were able to uncover is “The Sniper of La Perle” hosted by Kodak Black and the second is called “The Vault” and that’s hosted by the fictional DJ Pretzel. You won’t be able to unlock these stations in Vice City until you get to Chapter 3, though.

First, Kodak’s playlist gets played at his nightclub and can be unlocked by scanning four posters in North Vice City by using the WAiNK app on your phone. Those locations are as follows: TWA Ponya Tattoo, Drip Drops, Aunt Zola’s Soul Food and Jack of Hearts. “The Vault” station is less of a mission to unlock. All you have to do to be able to listen to that one is find one poster inside The Vault nightclub.

There are 22 radio stations in GTA 6 including these two unlockable ones and they’re hosted by various celebrities like actors, comedians and musicians across several genres.
