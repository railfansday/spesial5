Erling Haaland’s last-minute penalty sends Norway into Nations League quarter-finals

Erling Haaland scored a 96th-minute penalty with the last kick of the game to send Norway into the quarter-finals of the Nations League with victory over Denmark in Copenhagen.

Norway knew then needed to beat Denmark to leapfrog their opponents into second place in Group A4, with Portugal ensuring top spot by beating Wales in Cardiff.

They made a horror start, though, as the visitors found themselves 2-0 down inside 15 minutes as Rasmus Hojlund and Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg put Denmark in control.

Norway started their comeback when Mads Hermansen lost possession under pressure from Martin Odegaard and Haaland tapped into the empty net before half-time.

Captain Odegaard equalised in the 83rd minute to give Norway further hope, before they won a last-gasp penalty as Joachim Andersen was penalised for handball in the box.

Haaland stepped up and kept his cool in front of the home fans in the Parken Stadium, taking off his shirt as Norway wildly celebrated their dramatic victory.

The World Cup quarter-finalists will now advance to the quarter-finals of the Nations League, where they could face either England, France or Germany in the last eight.

Denmark, who had thrashed group winners Portugal 4-1 away from home on Saturday, will instead go into a relegation play-off in March as they bid to keep their top-flight status.
