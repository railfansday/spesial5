QPR release new Adel Taarabt footage and it's getting fans emotional

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Queens Park Rangers have released video footage of modern-day cult hero Adel Taarabt returning to Loftus Road, and given his status in West London, supporters have been reacting in their droves.

The Moroccan is highly-regarded as one of the silkiest players to ever grace the Championship in its modern era, with his influence helping Rangers achieve promotion to the Premier League in 2010/11 under the charismatic Neil Warnock.

Throughout his career, which included spells at some of Europe's most prestigious clubs, the playmaker often made headlines for both good and bad reasons, none of which tainted his legacy in W12.

Rangers are looking to end a long-standing exile from the top-flight in the near future under Julien Stephan, who has worked with some of the game's current leading lights such as Desire Doue and 2025 Ballon d'Or winner, Ousmane Dembélé.

Hoops supporters have certainly got their favourites within Stephan's side right now, but for a brief moment, the current situation in this part of the capital has been forgotten about as Taarabt made an emotional return to his former stomping ground.

Taarabt, 37, announced his retirement from professional football two months ago, with it being well over a decade since his chaotic spell in West London came to an end.

After coming through the RC Lens academy, the 30-time Moroccan international spent time with Tottenham Hotspur before initially joining Rangers on loan in March 2009, before also spending the following season on loan with the club.

Despite being told a permanent signing of Taarabt would see him sacked, Warnock made the playmaker's move permanent in August 2010, with the decision proving to be a stroke of genius as the R's won the league and the cult figure simultaneously enjoyed one of the best individual seasons in Championship history with 19 goals and 21 assists in 44 appearances.

The remainder of Taarabt's time in W12 was certainly less smooth, including public spats with Harry Redknapp, before he departed for Benfica in June 2015 after 164 outings for the club.

Earlier in the week, the former ace teased on Instagram that he had returned to London, but clearly kept the reasoning secret with QPR posting a short clip titled: "The King is Home," with Taarabt emerging from the Loftus Road tunnel onto the pitch.

11 years since his departure, Hoops fans are clearly not in a rush to forget Taarabt's magic which graced the Loftus Road turf on multiple occasions.

Hundreds of them have been reacting on social media to the clip, with one saying: "There is just something about seeing a player back at the stadium, where he gave you some of the best moments supporting this wonderful club."

Another supporter responded: "Why is this emotional! Can tell how much of an impact he had, just seeing him on that pitch again is insane."

"I hope the club is bringing him back as a legend," wrote another Rangers fan.

One of several overjoyed supporters also said: "One of my favourite players of all time and maybe a pain to play with but a bigger pain to play against. One if not the best player to play in the Championship ever,off the pitch a nightmare on it a joy to watch."

"Anyone know why he is here? He’s turned up when we have back to back away games….What does it all mean??," another fan debated.

Given the skillset Taarabt possessed, yet another comment read: Surely he can still play??," whilst another fan jokingly claimed: "Put him in front of Madsen and Kamara and we probably win the league."

As previously mentioned, it is unclear what brought Taarabt back to Rangers' historic home, even though it is no surprise that it has brought back many memories.

Some supporters have attempted to put two and two together, though, after it was recently announced that former Championship goalscoring icon, Charlie Austin, will be present at the club's home clash with Sheffield United next weekend.
