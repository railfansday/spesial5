Best Black Friday Phone Deals: Get Your Next Upgrade For up to 32% Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

These editor-approved are on sale weeks ahead of Black Friday, so get in now before the holiday shopping season starts!

Black Friday is coming, and in a year full of rising tech prices, everyone’s on the hunt for a good deal on a new phone. Even though we’re weeks away from November 27, I’ve already seen retailers like Amazon, tech brands, and even phone carriers roll out early holiday deals. If you’ve been holding out for a cheap upgrade or need a good tech gift for someone in your life, now is a surprisingly good time to shop.

These are the best early Black Friday deals I’ve found so far. I’ve tested every major phone release this year, so I know which phones are worth your time and money, and which ones aren’t worth buying at any price. I have been and will continue to be keeping an eye on every nook and cranny of the internet to find the best smartphone deals this holiday season, so check back here for more deals as we get closer to the shopping world’s biggest day.

Before you dig into the selection of phones on sale ahead of Black Friday, I highly recommend visiting my guide to the best phones of 2026. It includes in-depth reviews of all leading phones that launched this year—from the budget-friendly Google Pixel 10a to Apple’s newest icon, the foldable iPhone Duo.

Speaking of iPhones, keep in mind that Apple generally doesn’t put its latest phones on sale, even on Black Friday. If you’re in the market for an iPhone 18 Pro or the iPhone Duo, your best option is to check with your wireless carrier or, if you are open to switching, one of its direct competitors. In my experience, you're most likely to get a great iPhone deal when switching service providers.

That said, most retailers offer good deals on accessories for those phones, including cases and battery packs. If you know someone who’s getting a new phone, helping them set up is probably a great gift idea.

So the best deals are inevitably on Android phones. And, as luck would have it, there are already some good deals on new unlocked phones from Samsung and Google. These make great gifts because you don’t have to worry about anyone’s carrier situation.

As always, be ready to add the phone you’ve been coveting to your cart if you see it priced well below MSRP, even before Black Friday. Some of the deals on the latest models are available for a limited time. Between this factor and the possibility of a product running out of stock, I suggest acting fast if you are ready to buy.

The entire Google Pixel 11 phone lineup is already on sale. Depending on your preferences and budget, you can get any model, including the shape-shifting Pixel 11 Pro Fold, for between 10 and 30 percent off.

Personally, I think the best offers are on the Pixel 11 Pro and the Pixel 11 Pro Fold, since they’re also my favorite Android phones. You can get either one for almost 30 percent off. With what you save, you’ll have enough cash to cover a new case and maybe an extra tech upgrade, like a new Fitbit.

To my pleasant surprise, the new Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 is also on sale now. It has the same “passport”-style form factor as the iPhone Duo, and unlike Apple’s phone, you can actually get one today. If you’ve been thinking about getting a foldable phone, it’s a no-brainer upgrade at $300 off.
