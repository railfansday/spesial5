You can now buy the Pixel Tag, cases from Bellroy & Spigen available

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Pixel Tag is now available for purchase at the Google Store and Amazon.

A single Pixel Tag costs $29.99, while the 4-pack yields some savings at $99.99. The former is available from both the Google Store (arriving as early as Friday) and Amazon (Thursday delivery). We’re currently only seeing the 4-pack at Google’s storefront.

The Pixel Tag has a pill-shaped design with a silver metal face that features the ‘G’ logo and speaker cutout.

The back is made of polycarbonate in a very light green “Fog.” You’ll find a circular battery door that you press down on and twist counterclockwise to replace the coin cell CR2032, which is rated for “over one year.” Below the door is a physical button that can be used to ring your phone with a double press.

The Pixel Tag is rated IP67 (maximum depth of 1 meter up to 30 minutes) with Bluetooth 6.0, NFC, and Ultra Wideband.

On the Google Store, the Find Hub tracker is available in: Australia, Canada, India, Japan, Singapore, Taiwan, United Kingdom, and United States (except Puerto Rico).
