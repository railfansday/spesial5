McDonald’s has found a new way to sell burgers to people who don’t want to stop playing video games

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

McDonald’s to gamers: We are so hip.

Ahead of the highly anticipated release of Grand Theft Auto VI last week, the fast food giant announced that U.S.-based gamers can now place an order on Discord to be delivered by Uber Eats. And to demonstrate just how hip the septuagenarian company is, the announcement appealed directly to gamers with text speak—it’s littered with derivatives of “u” in lieu of “you.”

“McDonald’s is showing up on Discord to make it a little easier to get ur McDelivery order when breaking from ur servers just isn’t an option,” the company said in a press release. 

Yes, that’s verbatim from the Chicago-based company—the sort of appeal that could well remind some people of the “How do you do, fellow kids?” meme featuring actor Steve Buscemi from an episode of 30 Rock. Surprisingly, the announcement didn’t elicit many jeers from the Reddit crowd of Discord users, where many people drew comparisons to now-defunct collaborations between fast food chains and gaming consoles that similarly let gamers seamlessly order food. 

Hype around the end of a 13-year wait for a new edition of the popular game surely warranted being a little cringey. The company didn’t immediately respond to a request for comment from Fast Company regarding how popular the new ordering option has been thus far, but The New York Times reported that both DoorDash and Uber Eats experienced double-digit spikes in orders in the hours after the game was released. 

MCDONALD’S LATEST MOVES

Even if it’s only a marketing ploy, the Discord ordering optionality illustrates just how much McDonald’s wants to show up for customers. Earlier this month, company executives revealed plans to introduce new value-oriented meal bundles next year to appeal to cash-strapped customers.

And its nearly decade-long collaboration with Uber Eats is nothing to scoff at. Earlier this month, the two companies announced that their nearly decade-long partnership will expand delivery to 14 new markets, adding to its existing 30-country market.

In a more buttoned-up press release intended for investors, Mason Smoot, senior vice president of global franchising and delivery, said its home delivery option—dubbed McDelivery—is a $20 billion-plus global business each year.

Even though the company has been trying for more than a year to overcome a challenging market with its U.S. customers, it’s been making a wide range of attempts to lure them back—and that’s seemingly resonating with investors. 

While McDonald’s shares are still down for the year, they’ve surged more than 14% in the past month. During that same period, the broader S&P 500 Index has slumped nearly 8%.
