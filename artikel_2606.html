Raiders legend eviscerates Aaron Rodgers on FOX NFL Sunday: ‘Quacks like a duck’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Typically on Thanksgiving, all eyes in sports are on the football field. That should have especially been true after the Bears pulled out a thrilling 39-35 win over the Detroit Lions on Thursday morning.

Yet, all everyone can talk about is Aaron Rodgers and his wild rant on the Pat McAfee Show on Wednesday.

Before the Cowboys-Eagles game kicked off on Thursday, the FOX NFL Sunday crew addressed the hot topic in sports.

Each member of the pregame show took turns putting Rodgers on blast. Piggybacking off a point made by Michael Strahan, Howie Long dropped the mic.

Curt Menefee, left, Terry Bradshaw, Howie Long, Michael Strahan, Jimmy Johnson and Jay Glazer. Cigar Aficionado

"If it looks like a duck," Long said in reference to Rodgers being an awful teammate. Getty Images

"If it looks like a duck," Long said in reference to Rodgers being an awful teammate.

"Here's a guy who has won four MVPs. He won a Super Bowl and as you talked about Michael [Strahan], this is the kind of moment that maybe defines who he is.

The Hall of Fame pass rusher then offered a unique opinion that hasn't been talked about much.

"He's a guy that I think picked the wrong sport. He probably should have played tennis. It's an individual sport."

The conversation surrounding the enigmatic QB and whether or not he is a good teammate has raged for years.

He would go on to talk about how every player who has ever suited up in the NFL has had problems. Problems with coaches, teammates, team personnel, etc. But there is a way in which it should be handled.

For most of Rodgers' career, he vilified players who talked about team issues publicly.

all everyone can talk about is Aaron Rodgers and his wild rant on the Pat McAfee Show on Wednesday. AP Photo/Chris Szagola

Then he went scorched earth after learning he was benched by Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy.

Former Steelers quarterback and four-time Super Bowl winner Terry Bradshaw had arguably the strongest words, wishing Rodgers would disappear never to be seen again.

"I'm sorry Aaron Rodgers had to do this. I'm sorry he had to go public with all of this. Am I surprised? Absolutely not.

But one thing from Rodgers' wild rant stood out.

"One thing he said, he said 'when I'm through playing here in Pittsburgh, I'll disappear and you'll never hear or see of me again.'

"I hope this is one time he is telling the truth."
