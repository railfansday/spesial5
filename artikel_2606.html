Injured 49ers receiver reveals surprising update in birthday post

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

49ers receiver Christian Kirk took time out from his birthday celebration on Wednesday to talk about the challenges he's endured over the past year.

Kirk posted a series of photos that included a picture of his knee with stitches sewn into it, with a freshly shaven leg indicating he had surgery to repair his meniscus.

In an Instagram post, Christian Kirk reveals he had surgery on his meniscus. Instagram / @ckirk

"Turned 30 today. Can't lie it came at the perfect time," Kirk wrote in the post. "Grateful for my loved ones and everyone who reached out, means more than you know."

Kirk signed a one-year, $6 million deal with the 49ers over the offseason, and it's not gotten off to the way he hoped.

His tenure in San Francisco has mostly featured him on the team's injured reserve list.

Kirk sustained a calf injury during the first week of training camp and didn't suit up for a game until Week 7 against the Falcons.

While he was in uniform, he was targeted once and failed to catch the pass.

Kirk's afternoon was quiet after the missed catch, spending most of the game on the sideline.

Christian Kirk suited up during team warmups. Getty Images

On Nov. 8, he was placed on the 49ers' injured reserve list due to a knee injury.

"This year has been the hardest of my career.. no other way to put it. But football just like life, can be unfair. I've never cheated the game. And always put in more work than what is required. That's what hurts the most when these things happen."

Kirk added, "God's not done with me yet, and I can't wait to show the next version of myself. 'Pray for the bear.'"

Kirk's absence is just one of many in the 49ers' receivers room.

San Francisco has spent its last three games without Mike Evans but hopes he will be able to return for Sunday's game against the Vikings.

Deebo Samuel pushing quarterback Brock Purdy as his lead blocker. AP Photo/Julio Cortez

In last week's 46-40 win against the Cowboys, Deebo Samuel returned to the field from a hamstring injury and scored two touchdowns. He had three receptions for 31 yards.

The 49ers are also still without Ricky Pearsall (out for the year due to a knee injury) and rookie De'Zhaun Stribling, who's working his way back from an ankle injury.
