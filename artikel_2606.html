Report: Steelers were changing the play-calling process as of Tuesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

More and more information is emerging as to the surprise decision to bench Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers.

Here’s an interesting nugget, from Mike Garafolo of NFL Network.

As Garafolo explains it, the Steelers were changing their play-calling process. Starting Friday, quarterbacks coach Tom Arth, not head coach Mike McCarthy, would have been communicating the plays to Rodgers.

Previously, Arth handled play-calling during the two-minute drill. Moving forward, Arth would have been doing it for the entire game.

They worked on the new approach at practice on Tuesday. It was something Rodgers wanted.

At some point, that changed. McCarthy opted to bench Rodgers instead.

The situation dusts off the drama from nearly eight weeks ago, when Rodgers and McCarthy’s on-field unpleasantries during a loss to the Browns flowed from Rodgers’s frustrations regarding the pace of McCarthy’s play calls.

And it suggests that Tuesday’s move was simply the last domino in a chain that started falling the moment Rodgers shouted, “What the fuck? Call it!” and McCarthy responded by saying, “Fucking asshole.”
