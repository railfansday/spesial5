Giants’ Cam Skattebo makes abrupt backflip declaration: ‘I’m serious’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Giants star running back Cam Skattebo insisted Wednesday that he would no longer perform his signature backflip celebration during games.

His decision came in the wake of Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson tearing his ACL while doing a simple leaping sack celebration on Sunday.

During Thursday night's loss to the Commanders, three Giants players — Skattebo and wide receivers Malachi Fields and Darnell Mooney — all did backflips in the end zone after Skattebo scored a touchdown.

Fields said Wednesday that Giants coach John Harbaugh did not address the team about Hutchinson's injury. So Harbaugh didn't tell Skattebo or anybody else to avoid a backflip celebration in the future.

Skattebo reached the decision on his own.

"I'm done flipping, just for that reason," Skattebo said of Hutchinson's injury. "It's hard to see. Don't want it to happen to me."

"I'm serious," he said when pressed. "There's no reason to flip. Mentally, I'm like: 'F--- it. I'm not doing that s---.'"

He added, regarding Hutchinson's injury, "That s--- ain't going to happen to me."

Skattebo meant he would avoid that sort of unforced season-ending injury by no longer doing his backflip celebration.

Still, at one point during a brief interview on Wednesday, Skattebo left the door slightly ajar to his back flip returning.

"I'm sure I'll do it at some point, if we go to the playoffs or something," he said.

Before Skattebo granted an interview on the matter, he chimed in while Fields was discussing Hutchinson's injury.

"Just bad luck, man," Skattebo said from his nearby locker. "Just bad luck."

Skattebo suffered a gruesome season-ending ankle injury last year, but that didn't happen during a backflip situation.

This past offseason, at a fan event in Manhattan, Skattebo took a spill while attempting a backflip on a stage. He was not injured.

After that incident, Harbaugh essentially told Skattebo to stop doing backflips in off-field or non-game situations.

Skattebo said during training camp that he would avoid doing backflips in any non-game settings, including practices. He was noncommittal about whether the touchdown celebration would return during games in 2026. It has.
