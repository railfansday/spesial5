Mike McCarthy: Aaron Rodgers made an ultimatum outside his job description

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy said today that he decided that Aaron Rodgers would no longer be the starting quarterback because of an ultimatum that Rodgers made.

“There was an ultimatum made, clearly outside of his job description, so it was my decision to bench him,” McCarthy said.

Asked to explain what he meant by that, McCarthy wouldn’t directly answer.

“I’m not going to get into all those specifics. I know you have to ask the question, but those conversations are better served — they’re private conversations,” McCarthy told reporters.

McCarthy said he and every player should take pride in being a Pittsburgh Steeler, and conduct himself like a Steeler.

“We’re all fortunate to work here. This is an incredible opportunity, to work for the Pittsburgh Steelers,” McCarthy said.

The most direct answer McCarthy gave to a question today was when a reporter asked if Rodgers had been released. McCarthy simply answered, “Yes.”
