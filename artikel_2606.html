DK Metcalf should have been ejected earlier than he was, for contact with official

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

As it turns out, Steelers receiver DK Metcalf should have been ejected before he was ejected.

It happened in connection with the first of two personal fouls that prompted his disqualification. After being separated from Eagles defensive back Cooper DeJean following a play that began with 12:52 to play in the third quarter, umpire Mark Pellis pushed Metcalf away from the fray. Pellis had his hand on Metcalf’s chest.

Metcalf can be seen slapping Pellis’s forearm away.

From Rule 12, Section 3, Article 1(e): “Under no circumstance is a player allowed to shove, push, or strike an official in an offensive, disrespectful, or unsportsmanlike manner. The player shall be disqualified from the game, and any such action must be reported to the Commissioner, who may impose further discipline.”

Metcalf should have been disqualified then and there, because “shall” means “must.”

It wasn’t part of a broader fray, with Metcalf not knowing whose arm he was striking. It was Metcalf and Pellis, and no one else.

Later in the quarter, Metcalf drew a second personal foul and was ejected. As the league considers the next step for potential discipline of Metcalf, his contact with Pellis should be a factor in determining whether to suspend Metcalf for Friday’s game against the Broncos — if not longer.
