JJ Redick delivers major Luka Doncic news ahead of Lakers’ Phoenix Suns clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

JJ Redick has delivered encouraging news on Luka Doncic ahead of the Los Angeles Lakers' meeting with the Phoenix Suns, with the Slovenian star expected to be available after an ankle scare.

Doncic left the Lakers' 123-112 victory over the Milwaukee Bucks after appearing to hurt his right ankle in the fourth quarter. However, the latest injury report has provided a major boost, with the 27-year-old not listed among the players dealing with injuries.

Austin Reaves will not be as fortunate. The Lakers guard has been ruled out with a bruised left heel after also exiting the Bucks game.

Redick had already offered reassurance about Doncic when the Lakers returned to practice, suggesting the injury was not a major concern.

"Luka is fine," the Lakers head coach said. "The expectation would be he's available."

The latest report has reinforced that assessment, giving Los Angeles a significant boost as it looks to extend its seven-game winning streak.

Doncic has been outstanding this season, leading the NBA with 38.6 points per game while adding 7.6 assists and 7.5 rebounds. He also recorded 32 points, five rebounds and five assists before leaving against Milwaukee.

However, Reaves' absence will place more responsibility on the Slovenian guard. The pair have combined to average 63.3 points per game, making them central to the Lakers' impressive 11-2 start.

Reaves is averaging 24.7 points and 4.7 assists this season. Although initial X-rays on his heel were negative, the Lakers have opted to give him additional recovery time.

Collin Sexton is also questionable after suffering a right orbital laceration against Milwaukee, while Bronny James is dealing with an illness.

With Doncic expected to feature, the Lakers will have their leading scorer available against a Phoenix side led by Devin Booker. Redick will nevertheless need contributions from the supporting cast if Los Angeles is to cope without one of its most important offensive players.
