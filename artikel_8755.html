Here Are the Results from the 2026 NCAA Cross-Country Championships

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The top collegiate runners in the country competed for team and individual titles in Terre Haute, Indiana.

The 2026 NCAA DI Cross-Country Championships set off on Sunday, November 21. For the first time since 2019, the meet was hosted at the Lavern Gibson Cross-Country Course in Terre Haute, Indiana.

Hannah Gapes of NC State won her first individual title, setting a new 6K course record in 18:44, and Alabama took the women’s team championship. In the men’s race, New Mexico dominated the team competition, winning with just 69 points, and Louisville’s Elsingi Kipruto ran away from the field to win the men’s individual title in 28:27.
