32 Makeup Gift Sets Every Beauty Lover Will Obsess Over

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Somewhere between the first holiday party invite and the moment you realize November is already a quarter of the way gone, your gift list starts growing on its own. The good news? Presents from the beauty category make it a lot more manageable. They’re easy to shop, they’re hard to get wrong, and everyone in your life could use a few more of them, whether they like to admit it or not.

It can be as simple as a lipstick in a case they’ll want to show off, or as big as the LED mask they’ve had in their shopping cart for months now. And there’s plenty to love in between, from skin care they’d never splurge on for themselves and makeup gift sets to a beauty tool or two that turn the bathroom into something close to a visit to the facialist.

Every year, the same people are the hardest to shop for: the mom who insists she doesn’t need anything, the sister with impossibly specific taste, the friend who has genuinely tried it all. This list has them covered, along with a few names you’ll probably add later. Keep scrolling to shop the best beauty gifts and thank yourself in mid-December when you’re crowned best gift giver ever.

The best makeup gifts are the ones most people wouldn’t buy for themselves, like a designer lipstick in a case nice enough to leave out on the vanity, or a set of minis that finally gets someone to try a brand they’ve been curious about. This year’s holiday sets lean right into that, with limited-edition shades and packaging that looks just as good on a dresser as it does wrapped under the tree. Whether the person on your list does a full face every morning or rarely gets past mascara, there’s something here for them.

Tucked inside a limited-edition white pouch with a soft, knitlike texture, it includes the Rouge Coco Baume Satin tinted lip balm, which goes on sheer and builds to lipstick-like color with a few more swipes, plus one of the brand’s hand creams and a bottle of the Le Vernis nail polish in the classic shade Ballerina.

For the person who treats their makeup bag like an accessory, this one comes in a sleek metallic clutch that’s chic enough to carry on its own. Inside, there’s Prada’s Pradascope mascara for lifted, clump-free lashes; the refillable Monochrome lipstick in Tiepolo, a soft, diffused matte finish; plus two lip balm refills in Universal and Astral Pink that keep lips comfortable for up to 24 hours.

For the friend who always has their trusty lip liner handy, you can’t go wrong with this set. Makeup artist (and Vogue Beauty Board member) Fara Homidi’s Smudge & Contour pencils are a cult favorite, designed with a creamy, almost balmy feel, so they glide on without dragging, and can be smudged out with a fingertip or sculpted into perfection with a makeup brush. The brand’s holiday trio comes in three neutrals, a cool beige, a taupe, and a deep brown, covering everything from a barely-there line to a deeper, more defined lip.

If someone on your list always runs late to holiday parties, they’re in luck, because we’ve uncovered the perfect gift for them. Gucci Westman picked the four products she’d reach for with only a few minutes to get ready, and all four come in mini sizes that slip easily into an evening bag. A cherry red blush stick and a rosy-taupe highlighter take care of the cheeks, while a charcoal eye pencil and Westman’s buildable mascara handle the eyes, so the whole look comes together fast.

In our opinion, skin care is one of the easier things to gift, since almost everyone has a routine, and most people are quietly hoping someone will hand them the moisturizer or serum they’ve been too practical to buy for themselves. The best picks this season either upgrade a step someone already loves or introduce them to a brand they’ve been curious about, and a few come in packaging nice enough that the box doesn’t even need wrapping.

Sulwhasoo has built its name on ginseng, and this set is the easiest way to see why. It includes the brand’s full-size Concentrated Ginseng Rejuvenating Cream along with smaller sizes of the matching eye cream, serum, and cleansing foam, so the whole routine is covered from start to finish.

This is the moisturizer to give the person who has tried everything. Dr. Diamond’s HYDR/O is a sheer cream that sinks in quickly but delivers serious hydration, thanks to a long list of hyaluronic acids, ceramides, and squalane that help strengthen the skin barrier over time.

Dieux’s got loyal fans for a reason, and this kit pairs two of its most popular products in one, reusable and decorative tin. The full-size Deliverance Serum helps calm redness and soften the look of wrinkles and dark spots, while a mini Instant Angel Moisturizer locks in moisture and leaves skin dewy enough to wear under makeup. Both are fragrance-free and gentle enough for sensitive skin, so it’s an easy gift even if you’re not sure what someone’s routine looks like.

For the friend who loves a Sunday night face mask, this set is basically a week of spa nights in one box. It includes five of 111Skin’s sheet masks, each one targeting something different, from hydration and brightening to depuffing, plus a lifting mask for the neck. Each takes about 20 minutes, and at a $161 value, it’s a fun way to try the brand’s bestsellers and find out which one is best for you.

Beauty tools tend to be the gifts people secretly want most and are least likely to buy themselves, mostly because the price tags make it hard to justify. That’s exactly what makes them such a good present. This year’s picks bring the kind of treatments you’d normally book at a dermatologist’s office or a facialist into the bathroom at home, from LED masks and laser caps to a hydro-facial that takes about 10 minutes.

Currentbody’s LED masks already have a cult following, and the Series 3 is its most advanced yet. It packs 750 LED bulbs and six different wavelengths, including red and near-infrared light for firmness and fine lines, and blue light for breakouts. The brand’s MyDose technology works out the right treatment for your skin, and there are four preset modes if you’d rather not think about it. A touch-screen controller with a countdown timer makes it easy to stick with, which matters, since LED works best with consistent use.

For anyone dealing with thinning hair, this is one of the most serious tools you can give them. The Capillus Spectrum looks like a regular baseball cap, but inside, it uses red and infrared lasers to stimulate hair follicles, and it’s FDA-cleared to treat hereditary hair loss in both men and women. It only takes six minutes a day, and the cap shuts itself off when the session is done, so it’s easy to wear while you answer email or make coffee.

Think of this as a facialist that fits in your hand. HigherDose’s just-debuted wand pairs a cold laser with red, near-infrared, and blue LED light, so it can help firm and smooth fine lines on one setting and calm breakouts and redness on another. A cooling attachment swaps on to depuff under-eyes and sculpt along the jawline, and most sessions take 10 to 15 minutes. It even comes with a travel case, plus minis of the brand’s peptide serum and facial mist to prep skin first.

For the person on your list who splurges on a biweekly or monthly facial, the Shark Beauty FacialPro is most likely sitting high on their wish list. An easy way to unclog and clean pores plus infuse skin with powerful ingredients like hyaluronic acid, this gift takes its owner into the driver’s seat, giving them the power of visiting their favorite spa right at home.

Hair gifts are a safe bet for almost anyone, since even people who barely think about their routine still have a shampoo they’re loyal to or a styling tool they’ve been eyeing for months. From a splurge-worthy straightener that skips the hot plates entirely to travel-size sets that are perfect for stuffing into a carry-on, there’s something for the person who does their own personal blowout every morning and the one who simply wants their strands to feel soft.

Anyone who’s ever waited for their hair to dry before reaching for a flatiron will understand the appeal here. Dyson’s Airstrait dries and straightens at the same time, using airflow instead of hot plates, so you can go from towel-dried hair to a sleek, finished look with one tool. It has three heat settings and a separate mode for touching up dry hair, and the arms lock together so you can rough-dry your roots first. It’s a splurge, but it cuts out an entire step of the routine.

If holding a round brush and a dryer at the same time has never worked out for you, this is the fix. Wavytalk’s thermal brush works on dry hair, so you get the volume and bend of a blowout from a single tool, with five heat settings up to 420° F and a ceramic barrel that heats evenly. Negative ions help keep frizz down, and since it’s dual-voltage, it’s easy to pack for trips abroad.

As frustrating as it can be, you can’t reach your hair goals if you don’t repair damage first. If your giftee has been trying to assuage breakage, heat damage, or dry and brittle strands, Redken’s Acidic Bonding collection is like the golden ticket. Powered by Bonding Care Complex and citric acid, these products are formulated to reinforce weakened or broken bonds for smoother, stronger hair—and even protect hair color from fading.

For the person in your life who’s always on a plane, this set from RŌZ packs a full routine into carry-on-friendly sizes. Plus, everything is sulfate-free and smells as good as it performs.

We’ve rounded up a short list of both, so whether they’ve been wearing the same scent since college or still haven’t found their signature, there’s something here that’ll end up in their perfume collection.
