Samsung bets that you’ll either buy a Galaxy S27 Pro or Ultra next year

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Samsung is teeing up to kick off 2027 with an all-new lineup of flagship smartphones, and despite rising prices at your local Best Buy, the brand is still hedging that you’re going to pony up for the most premium model available.

As reported by ETNews, Samsung is currently allotting over 70 percent of its total production capabilities to just the Galaxy S27 Ultra and the new Galaxy S27 “Pro” model expected to launch alongside it. That leaves the plain Galaxy S27 and the larger S27+ on the outside looking in. In comparison to this year’s launch, the Galaxy S26 Ultra made up about 50 percent of this series’ production volume, for a grand total of 18 million units.

Historical performance implies Samsung is probably correct here. While big displays are typically what consumers are drawn to, it does appear that the S27 Pro won’t be all that much smaller than the S27+. Considering the Pro should slot in right around the typical price point as the Plus model, it’s not hard to imagine plenty of would-be S27+ buyers moving to the Pro.

For what it’s worth, Samsung betting big on high-end devices usually pays off. We’re on year two of the Fold series seeing big successes — first with the redesigned Fold 7 in 2025, then with the reshaped Fold 8 this year — and the Galaxy S26 Ultra managed to become the best-selling Android phone in Q2 of this year in a list typically populated by iPhones and cheap Galaxy A-series models. Even with prices constantly on the rise, the people yearn for flagship smartphones. You just have to wonder where that ceiling actually is.
