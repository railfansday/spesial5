Pixel 10a drops to $449, the best deal you’ll find after Google’s price hike

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Google may have hiked the price of the Pixel 10a in recent months, but a limited-time deal is the best price you’ll find at $450.

Pixel 10a initially launched for $499 but, like everything else in the chaos of RAMageddon, that price didn’t stick around for long. Google raised the price of Pixel 10a at the start of October, taking the starting price up to $599, leaving most discounts in the past month still higher than the original retail price.

But, right now, you can get a Pixel 10a for $449 from Woot, a rare deal that cuts the price below Google’s original MSRP.

The discount isn’t an all-time low, but it’s still well below any other Pixel 10a deal you’ll find in the near future, and the Pixel 10a is already one of the best deals in mid-range smartphones even at its higher price point. With 7 years of updates, a clean and fast Android build, reliable cameras, solid battery life, and more, it’s just hard to beat.

The one “catch” in this offer is that you’re limited in choices – 128GB in “Obsidian” black is it.
