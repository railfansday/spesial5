Malen d’Or? Forgotten Villa player on Mbappe’s tail in European Golden Boot race

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

It’s just getting a bit silly now. Since Aston Villa sold Donyell Malen to Roma in January, the most any other player in Serie A has scored is 13 goals. Malen has scored 25.

Yep, that’s nearly as many as Inter’s Marcus Thuram and Como’s Anastasios Douvikas – the pair on 13 in the same timeframe – put together.

In fact, no player in Europe’s top five leagues can match him. The leader in that time in the Premier League is Erling Haaland with 16; it’s Raphinha in La Liga with 21; Harry Kane in the Bundesliga with 20; and Esteban Lepaul in Ligue 1 with 19.

It’s the sheer consistency that makes Malen’s impact at Roma so impressive. His drive and determination have helped him showcase what he wanted to all along: he’s an elite goalscorer.

He was in a mean mood on Saturday, scoring four past Parma. His first saw him expertly guide the ball beyond his marker after a cross before slotting home, with the rest all showing his clinical nature in the box.

This is just what Malen does for Roma. He’s found a system where he’s the central figure, playing in his preferred position that he never got a chance to consistently with Villa. He’s backed himself ever since the move and he’s been good for his word.

His efforts against Parma gave him his third hat-trick for Roma, but he went one better and became their first player to score four goals in a Serie A game for almost 25 years.

If you weren’t already, it’s time to start taking him seriously in any debates about the best strikers in Europe. Sure, he doesn’t quite have the same potency for the Netherlands, so you could argue he’s a system player, but when he scores as often as he does in that system, it’s got to count for something.

Sure enough, Malen is becoming one to watch in the race for the European Golden Shoe. Only Raphinha and Kylian Mbappe have scored more than him in Europe’s top five leagues so far, and he’s only one goal behind the latter.

Mbappe won the European Golden Shoe two years ago, in between Harry Kane’s two triumphs. It would take a herculean effort for Kane to retain the accolade due to his current injury, so someone like Malen’s eyes should be lighting up.

He really is hot on the heels of Mbappe right now. You’d imagine the Frenchman will stay the course, while current leader Raphinha has been sensational so far since being converted into a striker himself at Barcelona, but will it last?

Some might ask similar questions about Malen, but he’s been doing this for nearly a year now. There might have been doubts over his ability to carry this form from last season into this one, and he’s made a mockery of them.

Carry on at this rate, and the Serie A golden boot that he came close to capturing last season – despite having half as much time to aim for it as his rivals – could be his. Beyond that, the European Golden Shoe, or even a Ballon d’Or nomination (for the 30-man shortlist, at least) might not be out of reach.

This Roma side have produced the best start to a season in the modern era in terms of goals scored at this stage. Of course, Malen has been front and centre of those efforts.

His latest Instagram post featured music in the background – a song named after him where the singer muses: “Maybe now I only love Bobby Malen.” (Oh, yeah, they call him Bobby over there – it’s a long story).

Just a classic case of a footballer finding a club he fits in at like a glove, and loving life. File that under things you love to see. Unless, perhaps, you’re a Villa fan. Or maybe even a Golden Shoe-hunting Mbappe…
