It’s dark by 5 p.m., the holiday ads have started and I’ve stopped pretending I want to do anything else but rot on the couch. Perfect timing, because TV is stacked. As a streaming editor who watches TV for a living, I’ve rounded up the biggest new and returning shows to watch this week across Netflix, Prime Video, Apple TV and more of the best streaming services.

This week, we're heading back to Middle-earth for more epic fantasy in "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power" season 3, while a new heist thriller, "12 12 12," promises twists and turns. "The Good Daughter" digs into family secrets and dark drama, and the ladies of "The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County" are ready to serve up a helping of reality TV mess. The days are getting shorter, but your watchlist is getting longer. Here’s what to watch this week.
