ESPN Now Has 54 Million Pay-TV Subscribers, Nearly Half Its 2011 Peak Of 100 Million

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The company’s annual report, filed Tuesday with the SEC along with fiscal fourth-quarter earnings results, showed the networks’ linear reach has fallen to nearly half the peak level of 100 million it hit in 2011. The stat was attributed to Nielsen Media Research.

The annual report said 54 million was not just the level of penetration for ESPN, but for “each of our significant domestic branded channels.” It went on to mention ABC, Disney Channel, Freeform, FX and National Geographic. The company’s eight owned local broadcast stations reach about 20% of U.S. television households, according to the report. Watch on Deadline

ESPN is also a tile on the home screen of Disney+, and Disney CEO Josh D’Amaro told Wall Street analysts on Tuesday’s earnings call that more live sports from ESPN are ticketed for Disney+ as part of an ongoing push. The company more than doubled its SVOD operating income in the most recent quarter, reaching $824 million, a hopeful sign for the ongoing streaming pivot. Streaming helped power double-digit revenue gains as the company topped Wall Street forecasts for the quarter.

As it approaches its 50th anniversary in 2029, ESPN is racking up some milestones. It will air its first Super Bowl in February, and it recently swung a major deal with the NFL. In that transaction, which closed last February, the league took a 10% stake in ESPN in exchange for control of the league’s media properties, including the NFL Network.

Longtime investor Hearst Corp. now has an 18% stake in ESPN, putting Disney’s current ownership at 72%.
