Police issue arrest warrants for August burglary at Max Iheanachor's home

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

In August, thieves targeted the home of Steelers rookie first-round tackle Max Iheanachor while he was in Buffalo for a preseason game. Police have now issued arrest warrants in connection with the crime.

Via Mike DeFabo of The Athletic, the warrants target a trio of Los Angeles residents: Winter Brantley, 25, Andre Castle Jr., 23, and Lamar Stewart, 45.

They face charges of burglary, criminal trespassing, theft by unlawful taking, receiving stolen property, and conspiracy. Pittsburgh authorities are working with officials in California to apprehend the suspects.

Winter Brantley is the sister of Iheanachor’s roommate, Ashley Brantley, per police records.

The thieves allegedly absconded with roughly $150,000 to $200,000 in jewelry and designer bags. Per DeFabo, the criminal complaints reveal that police used video evidence from the Pittsburgh International Airport, security camera footage from neighbors, cell phone communications, cell phone location history, rental car billing information, and United Airlines flight information to identify the suspects.

Iheanachor has appeared in all nine regular-season games for the Steelers, with seven starts.
