Giants on right side of controversial call that denied Jaguars touchdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Four of the most-dreaded words in the NFL — is it a touchdown? — helped decide the Giants-Jaguars game.

A third-down pass to Jaguars star Parker Washington early in the fourth quarter was ruled incomplete even though Washington dragged both feet in the end zone before his elbow landed out of bounds. But the officials ruled the ball was loose on the field and the replay booth did not overturn it, determining that Washington did not complete the process of the catch.

"The game's over? That might have been a catch," Giants safety Jevon Holland admitted after a 23-18 win. "That was a hell of a play. When they called it out, I was like, 'Alright.' Shoutout Vegas, man. I don't know."

"He was out — clear as day — and he bobbled the ball too," Phillips said.

CBS rules analyst Gene Steratore, a former referee, thought it should have been called a touchdown.

"I see the ball move a little bit," Steratore said on the TV broadcast, "but I did not think that he lost possession of the football. I have right foot down, tap with the left foot, yes the ball moves a little but the right hand, in my opinion, continues to keep that secure enough. I think it's a touchdown."

"I felt good about it, but it didn't go our way," Washington said. "You can't hang your head on that."

Jaguars head coach Liam Coen said he was "told that the ball switched hands, so that was what it came down to."

Giants K Dominic Zvada converted 3 of 4 field goals, including 2 of 3 from the right hash. But he missed from the shortest distance (32) and made from 43, 53 and 54 yards.

New York Giants kicker Dominic Zvada reacts after missing a field goal against the Jacksonville Jaguars, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, in East Rutherford, N.J. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

Giants C Bryan Hudson was called for an unnecessary roughness penalty in the fourth quarter that turned a third-and-2 into third-and-17 and led to a punt.

"Chasing the ball and the play is not over so we're going to try to push the pile to get a first down," Hudson told The Post. "Can't control what's called. That's all I did. They are trying to hold him up and punch the ball. It's just playing football."

CB Greg Newsome was a game captain against his former team but the Newsome Revenge Game didn't last long. He injured his right ankle on the first defensive series and never returned. He was replaced by Paulson Adebo, who had a timely interception after he was a healthy scratch last game.

WR Jalin Hyatt, the former third-round pick who has been through the wringer over four seasons, made his first catch of the season to convert a third-and-4 midway through the third quarter. The crowd responded with nice applause as Hyatt jogged off.

In his first game since he was claimed off the Eagles practice squad, Xavier Gipson provided a missing spark to the return game.

Replacing Charlie Jones, who was demoted to the practice squad, Gipson had a 27-yard punt return and a 36-yard kickoff return among his five special teams touches. It is his third stint with the Giants.
