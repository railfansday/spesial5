Is Brock Bowers playing today? Latest on Raiders TE's injury status

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza may not have his top target available when the Las Vegas Raiders travel to face the Denver Broncos in Week 11.

Raiders tight end Brock Bowers is questionable for Sunday's game with an ankle injury, and it will likely be a game-time decision as to whether or not he'll play.

Despite sitting out two games with injuries already, Bowers still leads the Raiders in receptions (50), receiving yards (676) and receiving touchdowns (6) on the season.

After getting off to a 3-0 start, the Raiders have lost six of their last seven games to drop 3½ games behind the 7-2 Broncos and Kansas City Chiefs in the AFC West division.

Brock Bowers is officially listed as questionable for Sunday's game in Denver.

He was a limited practice participant on Friday, but was non-committal about his ability to suit up against the Broncos.

"We still have some conversations to have everywhere, so we'll see," he said on Friday, acccording to The Athletic.

If Bowers isn't able to play, tight end Michael Mayer and wide receiver Jack Bech could see the biggest increase in targets from Mendoza.

For fantasy football managers, the Raiders' late afternoon kickoff (4:25 p.m. ET) will make start/sit decisions a bit more complicated.
