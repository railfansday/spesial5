Sean Payton won't use Aidan Hutchinson injury as a teaching moment

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Lions coach Dan Campbell has made his position clear regarding player celebrations, in the aftermath of defensive end Aidan Hutchinson’s season-ending knee injury. Campbell will not tell his players to stop celebrating.

Broncos coach Sean Payton agrees with Campbell’s position.

“I’m kind of with Dan,” Payton told reporters. “I saw the final comments. We’re teachers and we’re constantly… But we’re going to walk out of the doors, and there’ve been a lot of celebrations. I’m kind of with Dan there where it’s unfortunate. Two good players got hurt on the same play, and I think that there are a lot of other things that I would put on tape on a Friday, Saturday that are [teaching moments]. That I wouldn’t put that on.”

For starters, any non-contact ACL tear quite possibly is the result of a partially torn knee ligament that was on the brink of fully tearing. If Hutchinson’s ACL tear hadn’t happened when he landed after jumping to celebrate a sack of quarterback Drake Maye, it could have happened on the next play. Or the next.

And it’s impossible to expect players to not celebrate. A great play is the product of hours of work and effort and discipline, most of which happens away from the public eye. In the arena, with thousands cheering and millions watching on TV, how can the human beings inside the uniforms be expected not to display human emotion?

Injuries happen in football. The vast majority happen during a play. Some happen after a play. It’s unreasonable to reduce it to none; the players shouldn’t be expected to minimize or eliminate the happiness they experience when everything they’ve done privately culminates in a public moment of achievement.
