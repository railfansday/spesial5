Atlanta Hawks (11-6) at Boston Celtics (12-6) Game #19 11/27/26

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Atlanta Hawks (11-6) at Boston Celtics (12-6)Friday, November 27, 20268:00 PM ETRegular Season Game #19, Home Game #8TV: NBCSB, ANF/PSN/Prime Video, NBA-LPRadio: 98.5 Sports Hub, 92.9 The Game, Sirius XMTD Garden

The Celtics return home from their 4 game road trip to take on the Atlanta Hawks. This is the first of 3 games between these teams this season. They will play 2 more games in Atlanta on February 15 and April 9. They split the series 2-2 last season with each team winning one at home and one on the road. The Celtics are 247-153 overall, all time against the Hawks. They are 133-46 in games played at Boston.

This is the final NBA Emirates Cup group game for both teams. The Hawks are 2-1 and the Celtics are the only unbeaten team in the East in group play at 3-0. To make the quarterfinals for the first time since 2023, the Celtics need to beat Atlanta because head to head matchup would be the tie breaker should the Hawks win. They could make it into the quarterfinals as the wild card team if their point differential ends up the largest. Currently they have the best point differential at +68. Detroit +31, Miami +25, Cleveland +24 and Atlanta +21 could challenge that with a Celtics loss and a blow out win from any of those teams.

The Celtics finally got a win after 4 straight losses and are now 2nd in the East, 2.5 games behind 1st place Philadelphia and they are tied with 3rd place New York and half a game ahead of 4th place Atlanta. They are 11-2 against Eastern Conference opponents. They are 6-1 at home and 6-4 in their last 10 games. They won their last game. The Hawks are 4th in the East, 3 games behind 1st place Philadelphia and half a game behind 2nd place Boston. They are 10-2 against Eastern Conference teams. They are 6-3 on the road and 8-2 in their last 10 games. They have won their last 4 games.

The Hawks made some changes since last season. They brought in Lugentz Dort, Aaron Wiggins, Devin Carter and Ryan Nembhard through trades. They waived Carter and Nembhard before the season. They drafted Kingston Flemings with the 8th pick in the draft, Zuby Ejiofor with the 23rd pick and Henri Veesaar with the 52nd pick in the draft. They lost Zacharie Risacher, Gabe Vincent, Christian Koloko, Tony Bradley, and Keaton Wallace.

After this game at home, the Celtics will be back on the road playing at San Antonio and Dallas. Then it's home against Charlotte before the break for the NBA Cup tournament. Then, they play a game at Brooklyn before they have a 7 game home stand against Washington twice, Utah, Detroit, Portland, Miami and Cleveland. Then it's back on the road for a 6 game road trip through Chicago, Utah, Portland, Phoenix, the LA Lakers and the LA Clippers.

The Hawks are playing in the first game of a 3 game road trip that will take them to New York and Miami after this game. They then host Indiana before the break for the NBA Cup Tournament. Then they go on a 3 game road trip through Portland, Phoenix and Denver before they host Orlando, Oklahoma City, Detroit and Charlotte. Then they are back on the road to play one game at San Antonio before hosting Chicago, Milwaukee and Dallas.

The Celtics have been rotating players at the shooting guard spot. They have started Jordan Walsh, Hugo Gonzalez, Baylor Scheierman and most recently, Sam Hauser. The starting lineup of White, Hauser, George, Tatum and Queta are a plus-54 per 100 possessions and they got off to an 18-9 start en route to an 80 point first half. However, after the game, Joe Mazzulla was iffy about sticking with this lineup. I'm guessing that Hauser starts again, but he could go back to Walsh, Scheierman or Gonzalez. I've listed the Hawks starters from their game notes.

Mitchell Robinson is the only player on the Celtics injury report at this time and he is once again out due to an illness. The Hawks have 5 players on the injury report. Mouhamed Gueye is out after surgery to repair a fractured left foot. Jock Landale is out due to a hip injury. CJ McCollum is out with a left calf strain. Asa Newell is out after undergoing surgery to repair a plantar plate tear in his left great toe. Henri Veesaar is out or the season due to a torn ACL.

Keys to the GameDefense – Defense is always a key to winning games. The Celtics have an offensive rating of 115.7 (9th) while the Hawks have an offensive rating of 112.7, which is 20th. The Celtics have a defensive rating of 105.8 which is 2nd while the Hawks have a defensive rating of 109.7, which is 6th. The Celtics have been hot and cold on defense this season. Most of the time they can shut down the other team but sometimes they allow them to score at will like allowing the Magic to score 40 in the 4th quarter or the lowly Kings to score 35 in the 3rd quarter. The Celtics need to defend the perimeter because the Hawks are 4th, shooting 43.3 threes a game. They also have to defend the paint as the Hawks are 6th with 51.2 points in the paint per game. The Celtics must make defense a priority in this game and they have to play that tough defense for 48 minutes and not let up.

Rebound – Next to defense, rebounding is the key to winning. When the Celtics put out the extra effort on the boards, it usually carries through to the rest of their game. The Hawks are averaging 45.7 rebounds per game (5th) while the Celtics are averaging 45.2 rebounds per game (10th). The Celtics need to aggressively go after every rebound. They can't afford to give the Hawks extra possessions and 2nd chance points by allowing them to beat them to rebounds. The Hawks are 5th with 16.9 second chance points per game and so the Celtics must keep them off the offensive glass.

Effort and Focus for 48 Minutes– The Celtics have to play with extra effort overall for all 4 quarters. In most of their losses and even in some of their wins, they have allowed their opponents to play with more energy than them for periods of time during the game. They play well for stretches but let up and allow their opponents to surge ahead, especially in the 3rd quarter. The Celtics offensive rating in the 3rd quarter is 96.9 and their defensive rating in the 3rd quarter is 124.8, giving them a net rating of -27.9, which is the 2nd worst in the league. They have to stay focused for all 48 minutes and be ready for their opponents to play harder in the second half and they need to match that effort. They also have to come out with more effort and energy to start the game and not dig themselves into a hole. The Hawks have been playing very well and so the Celtics will need maximum effort for all 48 minutes to beat them.

Move the Ball Carefully – When the Celtics move the ball and find the open man, they are tough to beat. When they hold the ball and try to do too much individually, they become predictable and struggle. The Hawks move the ball well and average 30.3 assists per game while the Celtics are 16th with 26.7 assists per game. The Celtics need to move the ball, but they have to make careful passes because the Hawks are 2nd in the league with 22.6 points off turnovers per game.

X-FactorsHome Game and Cup Game– They call it home court advantage for a reason and the Celtics need to take advantage of playing on their home court. The Celtics need to get motivation from the home crowd, which should be loud and doing everything in their power to support the Celtics and rattle the Hawks. However, it is the first game at home after a long road trip and those games are tough on the home team. The Hawks have the distractions of travel, an unfamiliar arena and a hostile crowd and hopefully that will give the Celtics an advantage. The Hawks have been off since Sunday and so may also have some rust to deal with. Both teams should have extra motivation to win this game in order to advance to the quarterfinals of the NBA Cup.

Officiating – Officiating is always an x-factor in every game. Every crew officiates differently. Some call it tight, others let them play. The Celtics need to adjust to how the refs are calling the game and not allow bad calls or no calls to take away their focus from playing the game the right way. We have seen how much of an x-factor officiating can be in a few games this season. The Celtics have to play so well all game that the officiating, no matter how bad, can't influence the outcome.
