MNF live chat: Minnesota Vikings vs. Buffalo Bills

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Detroit Lions took care of business in Week 9, taking down the Miami Dolphins, but their chance to gain ground on the rest of the NFC North remains a rough road, as the Packers and Bears also won their respective games on Sunday.

Could the Lions get a break tonight in Minnesota when the Vikings take on the Bills?

Entering tonight's "Monday Night Football" game, the Vikings lead the NFC North with a 6-1 record. If they beat the Bills, they'll stay at the top of the division for another week and improve their record to an impressive 7-1. However, if the Vikings lose to the Bills, they'll fall to 6-2 and drop to third in the North.
