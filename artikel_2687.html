Patrick Mahomes Opens Up on Facing the Bills Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Patrick Mahomes and the Kansas City Chiefs are set to face their rivals, the Buffalo Bills, on Thanksgiving night. The star quarterback sent a strong message ahead of the highly-anticipated matchup.

He delivered another impressive performance last Sunday versus the Arizona Cardinals. Mahomes completed 27-of-39 passes for 324 yards, three touchdowns and zero interceptions in the 34-7 victory. More importantly, he led Kansas City to its third straight win, bouncing back from back-to-back losses.

Mahomes has regained his old form after returning to action in Week 1. He sustained an ACL and LCL injury last December and missed the remainder of the 2025 season. The Chiefs failed to reach the playoffs as they finished with a 6-11 record. It was the first time they failed to qualify for the postseason since 2014.

Oct 4, 2026; Paradise, Nevada, USA; Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes (15) stands on the field after the game against the Las Vegas Raiders at Allegiant Stadium. Mandatory Credit: David Brytus-Imagn Images

The three-time Super Bowl champion addressed the media on Wednesday. He shared that he looks forward to facing the Bills, especially at New Highmark Stadium. Mahomes understands how difficult it is to defeat the AFC East powerhouse team and that he needs to play at his best.

"I mean, you know it's going to take your best football," Mahomes said. Every time we play each other, it always comes down to the very end of the game. And it's about who makes the big plays and the big moments. It's always exciting (and) it's always fun. But you have to take your best football in order to go out there and win. So I'm excited to get to the new stadium, be in a great environment on Thanksgiving and be able to go out there and play our best football."

Kansas City is 8-2 for the season and tied with the Denver Broncos at the top of the AFC West. Buffalo, on the other hand, shares the best record in the NFL at 9-1 with the San Francisco 49ers.
