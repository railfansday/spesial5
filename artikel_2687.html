Browns Week 12 vs. Raiders: Thanksgiving Week practice schedule is different than usual

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

One of the many reasons the NFL is popular is the predictability of almost everything related to the schedule. That will be tested during Thanksgiving Week, with Monday Night Football closing out Week 11, and a Wednesday game opening Week 12, plus Thursday, Friday, Sunday, and Monday games this week as well.

The Cleveland Browns and their fans are excited about being good enough to be worthy of being flexed onto Sunday Night Football.

That move, along with the bye week and Thanksgiving holiday on Thursday, has also led to a change for the Browns' Week 12 practice schedule as they get ready for the Las Vegas Raiders:

After practice – Byron Storer and Mike Rutenberg available

After practice – Todd Monken available and open locker room

It is interesting that Monken has the team practicing a little early on Friday, giving the team a little bit of a long layoff heading into Sunday Night Football, but getting them moving after Thanksgiving Day off.
