Lions DT Skyler Gill-Howard out for season with knee injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Lions have lost another defensive player for the rest of the 2026 season.

Head coach Dan Campbell told reporters on Tuesday that defensive tackle Skyler Gill-Howard is going to miss the rest of the year. Gill-Howard has a knee injury that will require a surgical repair.

The Lions have also lost defensive end Aidan Hutchinson, linebacker Jimmy Rolder, and linebacker Jack Campbell in recent weeks.

Gill-Howard was a sixth-round pick in April and he appeared in all 10 games for the Lions this season. He had 11 tackles, two sacks, a tackle for loss and six quarterback hits.
