Why Aaron Glenn is talking about this Jets game differently: ‘Sticks out’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Jets coach Aaron Glenn believes his team is ready for the rematch.

Glenn, who rarely references past games, brought up the Jets' first meeting with the Dolphins this season when he gave his opening remarks Wednesday about this week's game in Miami.

The Dolphins beat the Jets 28-25 on Oct. 25 at MetLife Stadium. That was the Dolphins' only win this season, and they jumped on the Jets to take a 21-7 lead at halftime. The Jets rallied in the second half but could not overcome their early struggles.

"When it comes to this game, the one thing that sticks out in my mind is this team came down in our building and they punched us in the mouth in the first quarter, and it wasn't good," Glenn said. "I still feel the effects of that. In the second half, though, our guys came out and they played their asses off and we look forward to going down there, starting this game off on the right foot and our guys will be ready to play."

Glenn was asked how he still feels the effects of that game.

"Because I don't like getting punched in the mouth," he said.

The Jets season may have looked much different if they had beaten the Dolphins that day in a game they were favorites. That was the second loss in a five-game losing streak that ended Sunday with their win over the Chargers.

New York Jets quarterback Geno Smith (7) gets sacked during the third quarter against the Miami Dolphins on Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026, at MetLife Stadium. Charles Wenzelberg / New York Post

The Jets are 3-8 and trying to win two consecutive games for the first time in a calendar year. They then have a bye next week followed by a five-game stretch that includes some winnable games.

"That would be awesome. We're trying to set ourselves up," wide receiver Garrett Wilson said of entering the bye off two straight wins. "We've got an opportunity to do that with winning this past week. Honestly, anything else would be disappointing. We want to find a way to start a winning streak. We've got to go about our prep right. We've got a good team coming that beat us the first time. We're excited for the opportunity."

For Glenn, these final six games of the season could determine if he is back for 2027. If the Jets finish strong, owner Woody Johnson may see that as the team moving in the right direction. If the Jets falter, that could be it for Glenn.

Glenn enters this game with an 0-9 record against the AFC East as the Jets coach. Recent history is against the Jets. They have not won in Hard Rock Stadium since the final game of the 2014 season, when Geno Smith was in his first stint with the team and Rex Ryan coached his final game with the franchise.

Even though Glenn said the Dolphins loss earlier this season stayed with him, he does not believe his players need extra motivation.

"I'm always going to say this, and you guys have heard me say this, these guys are motivated to go play," Glenn said. "It doesn't matter who we play, where we play, it's something inside of you that once you cross that white line, you're ready to go compete. They are NFL players, they're playing at the top of their profession for a reason because they're motivated to go play and they're always going to be motivated to go play."

What about their pride after losing in October?

"Man, once you go out there and play, it's always pride," Glenn said. "The thing is, you just don't want to get hit like that, like we did. So, will the guys remember that? I'm sure, absolutely. But the thing is, this is a division game and our guys will be ready to play."

New York Jets head coach Aaron Glenn reacts on the sideline during the second quarter against the Buffalo Bills, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, at MetLife Stadium. Charles Wenzelberg/NY Post

The Jets hope they can build off their 27-24 victory over the Chargers last week, particularly on offense, where they scored on five of their six drives.

"You definitely can [build off it]," Wilson said. "We've got to use that as momentum and take that into the rest of the season. Offensively, I thought we played a good game. There was one drive where I thought we could have gotten a touchdown and it would have really made it a perfect outing. With that said, we did a good job. We've got to find a way to capture that and take it to the rest of the season."
