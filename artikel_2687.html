Raptors' RJ Barrett out with strained right hamstring, Collin Murray-Boyles placed in concussion protocol

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Toronto Raptors forward RJ Barrett has been diagnosed with a mild right hamstring strain and will be re-evaluated in two weeks, the team announced Thursday.

Additionally, forward Collin Murray-Boyles was placed in concussion protocol as a precautionary measure.

Barrett missed Wednesday's 103-99 loss to the Washington Wizards with hamstring tightness. That was the second of back-to-back games for the Raptors, who also lost to the New York Knicks on Tuesday. Barrett played 39 minutes in that game, scoring 22 points with 5 rebounds and 5 assists.

However, hamstring discomfort has been an issue for Barrett in recent weeks. He has been using a stationary bike on the sideline when not on the court, in addition to during timeouts and stoppages in play, according to The Athletic's Esfandiar Baraheni.

Murray-Boyles left Wednesday's contest during the first half after playing four minutes. The Raptors announced that he would not return due to illness, which has apparently been going around the team locker room. When he sustained a possible concussion is not certain.

Barrett, 26, is Toronto's second-leading scorer this season, averaging 20.9 points with 6.4 rebounds and 4.3 assists per game. He's shooting a career-low 28% on 74 3-point attempts. Prior to sitting out Wednesday's contest, Barrett had appeared in all 14 of the Raptors' games.

In his second season, Murray-Boyles is averaging 9.3 points, 4.6 rebounds and 1.8 assists, in addition to 1 steal and 1.1 blocks in 14 games (eight starts). He was the Raptors' 2025 first-round pick (No. 9 overall) out of South Carolina.

Toronto (5-10) has lost its past six games and eight of its past 10. The Raptors currently rank ninth in the Eastern Conference and 16th in the NBA. They visit the Detroit Pistons (10-4) on Friday.
