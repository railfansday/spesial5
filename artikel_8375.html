With limited exception, the mid-size company I work for will not contract with sole proprietors who do not have an LLC. It is part of our audit defense to avoid mis- and re-classification of contractors vs. employees.
Your mid-size company needs to hire better accountants and/or lawyers - the business structure of a contractor will have zero impact on IRS classification and mean nothing in an audit.
The issue is not the IRS, but the state (e.g. California) coming down on the client for not following employment law.
