Airbnb is making a direct play for independent and boutique hotels, and it's selling against Booking.com by name. Lou Zameryka, Airbnb's global head of hotel enterprise and connectivity partnerships and a former Booking.com director of global accounts, told Skift at Phocuswright that "Booking is not hotel-centric" and that Airbnb is "more hotel- and stay-centric as a brand." He also called Airbnb's commissions "competitive," and said on stage that Airbnb would bring hotels millions of incremental customers accustomed to booking directly with hosts. Booking Holdings did not respond to a request for comment. The reception from hoteliers split by segment. Highgate Hotels Chief Revenue Officer Kerry Mack called the push a "huge win" for independent hotels and said Airbnb will create new demand. Hilton Chief Commercial Officer Chris Wilroy was more measured, saying Hilton will keep working with a wide network of distribution partners and pick the ones that deliver guests at the right cost for owners. On AI agents, Wilroy said 70% of Hilton's occupancy comes from Hilton Honors members, and that Hilton is the most "authoritative" source of its own content.
