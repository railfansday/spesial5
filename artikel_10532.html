Which Samsung folding phone is right for you? Galaxy Z Fold 8, Ultra, or Flip 8

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Folding phones are entering a new chapter and, if you’re looking to try one for the first time, you might not know exactly where to start. In this guide, we’ll take a look at some of the most important questions to answer when buying a foldable, and dissect how Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold 8, Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, and Galaxy Z Flip 8 fit the bill to figure out which is the best for you, especially if you’re interested in one this holiday season.

Samsung has three foldables in its lineup this year, two of which are pretty familiar. The Galaxy Z Flip 8 continues the lineup of clamshell-style foldables, offering a form factor similar to that of a classic flip phone. The Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, meanwhile, is designed to be a normal smartphone on the outside, hiding a tablet within. Finally, the Galaxy Z Fold 8 is the new kid on the block, offering a compact outer display that opens up to a media-friendly, wider inner panel.

Each one serves a different need, but they all share similar performance and core specs. Here’s a quick rundown of the key details on each device:

Starting with the displays, the book-style Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Fold 8 Ultra both provide outer displays that are fully usable as standard smartphones, while the inner display is useful for multitasking, media, or just getting more space for reading a website or e-book. Samsung’s top-tier specs deliver performance for everything you’d need, with Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy powering everything from demanding games or media-editing apps to day-to-day use.

With each Samsung foldable, you’re getting a fully capable smartphone, but finding the one that’s best for you is a matter of picking your most important priorities. Cameras, battery life, content consumption, and size are some of the most important points to consider, so let’s take a look one-by-one.

When it comes to cameras, foldables have a tough time. There’s only so much physical space to fit the camera sensors and, with cameras, having more space generally results in a battery overall camera.

That said, the best camera on a Samsung foldable is undoubtedly on the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra.

The Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra has a 200MP main camera that’s similar to the Galaxy S26 Ultra, while it also packs a 50MP ultrawide and something neither of the others offer, a telephoto lens. The 10MP telephoto shooter captures faraway subjects at 3x optical zoom, meaning you’re not giving up on quality when you zoom in. Two 10MP cameras can be found on the displays, with neither sacrificing quality for an under-display look.

Runner-up here is the Galaxy Z Fold 8, which offers a pair of 50MP cameras for wide and ultrawide shots. You can get 2x optical-quality zoom out of the main sensor to still get a good zoom shot, while the main camera still offers good quality and Samsung’s best camera features including 2x optical-quality zoom and the subject-tracking My FanCam.

The Galaxy Z Flip 8 may not have the best camera hardware, but what it lacks in hardware it makes up for in flexibility. This is definitely the best form factor of the bunch for using the foldable design as a tripod for capturing clips. All three devices also use their foldable nature to let you capture full-quality selfies using the rear cameras, which always have better quality than the 10MP dedicated front-facing cameras.

Here’s where specs on paper can tell a different story compared to real life.

The Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra may offer the biggest battery of the bunch at 5,000 mAh, but its battery life is on par with the smaller Galaxy Z Fold 8. Both foldables are designed to last you through a full day, though sticking to the outer display only on the Fold 8 Ultra may bring you slightly better battery life overall.

In either case, you’re also getting 45W fast charging over USB-C, granting you speedy recharging if your device happens to get low through the course of a busy day. These are also “Qi2 Ready,” meaning you can use magnetic wireless chargers if you use a compatible magnetic case.

One of the most important things you’ll want to ask yourself when buying a foldable phone is why you want to opt for a foldable in the first place, and each of these devices gives a different answer.

The Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra is a productivity powerhouse, delivering more space on its inner display for you to get work done through the web, apps, and more. You can run up to three apps at once on the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra’s display, as well as floating apps, and more.

Galaxy Z Fold 8 is great for media consumption. The wider aspect ratio is perfect for watching videos, playing games, reading books, and more, all while still giving you plenty of space for productivity just like the Ultra.

Galaxy Z Flip 8, meanwhile, is all about giving you a more compact device. The outer display lets you quickly reply to messages or keep up with directions, while you can unfold it to do everything you want to on a normal phone. Close it up to more easily stow it away in your pocket or your bag.

If you are moving into a new Galaxy foldable, the good news is that you don’t have to start from scratch. Thanks to Samsung’s Smart Switch app, you can easily transfer supported content including pictures, contacts, messages, and more from an older Galaxy phone, another Android phone, or even an iPhone over to a Galaxy foldable. Apps, messages, photos, and more can all make the move wirelessly, without the need of a cable.

You can get the Galaxy Z Fold 8, Fold 8 Ultra, and Flip 8 from your favorite carriers and retailers, but Best Buy is one of the best places to score a great deal with solid trade-in values and deep discounts around Black Friday and throughout the holiday season.
