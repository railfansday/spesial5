Marvel Boss Officially Reveals Why Avengers Switched from Kang to Doctor Doom

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Avengers: Doomsday is a month away and will find the Avengers, the New Avengers, and X-Men from the Fox films facing off against Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom. Originally, the fifth Avengers film was titled The Kang Dynasty and would have starred Jonathan Majors as Kang, who appeared in Loki and Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania. Marvel fired him in December 2023 after he was convicted on assault and harassment charges, and retooled The Kang Dynasty into the Doctor Doom-centric Doomsday.

However, according to Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige, the Marvel Cinematic Universe always would have pivoted to Doom replacing Kang as the next Avengers-level threat, regardless of Majors' legal issues and firing.

Speaking with Empire Magazine, Feige revealed the plan to pivot away from Kang began around the release of the third Ant-Man film in early 2023. "Quantumania was not received the way we would have liked," Feige said (via IGN). "There are many elements of that that we're proud of, the Kang performance being one of them. But we were changing [The Kang Dynasty] even at that point. It would still have been called The Kang Dynasty, but it would have been a set-up for Doom, midway through that movie."

Majors' Kang the Conqueror variant was the villain of Quantumania after Kang first appeared as He Who Remains in Loki season 1. Much like how Josh Brolin's Thanos appeared throughout the MCU before going toe-to-toe with the Avengers in Avengers: Infinity War and Endgame, Kang was being established as an overarching antagonist before The Kang Dynasty. In contrast to them, Doom has been haphazardly established in the MCU via post-credits scenes in 2025's The Fantastic Four: First Steps and the recent re-release of Endgame. Setting up Doom in a film titled The Kang Dynasty would have been a bold move.

From the public's point of view, it certainly seemed like Marvel retooled the Avengers films in response to Majors' convictions. However, according to Feige, that wasn't exactly the case. "We didn't want to rush into any rash decisions in terms of employment. People think there was a cause-and-effect with Jonathan's issues, and that certainly accelerated some things. But the Doom of it all was well underway before that."

Marvel is known for deception and trickery, so you're well within your rights to doubt Feige's comments here. However, as next year's Avengers: Secret Wars hasn't had a title change, it makes sense that the plan all along was for Doctor Doom to supersede Kang as the main villain. After all, Secret Wars shares its name with Jonathan Hickman and Esad Ribić's 2010s comic series of the same name. That event featured Doom as God Emperor reigning over a realm patched together from fragments of various Marvel universes and continuities. As Doomsday is all about universes colliding and resulting in Incursions, it seems very likely 2027's Secret Wars film will adapt the comic of the same name.

You won't have to wait long to see Doom, the Avengers, and more on the big screen. Avengers: Doomsday hits theaters on Dec. 18.
