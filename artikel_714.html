Where to Watch the NWSL Championship: Washington Spirit vs. Kansas City Current

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Washington Spirit will have the benefit of a hometown crowd at the sold-out game, while the hungry Kansas City Current seek their first title.

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, The Hollywood Reporter may receive an affiliate commission.

The National Women’s Soccer League final is set, with Kansas City Current traveling to Washington Spirit’s Audi Field. It’ll be the Spirit’s third consecutive visit to the NWSL final, and they’ll be seeking their second title. Kansas City Current will be seeking their first title, having visited the finals in 2022.

The Spirit will enjoy home field advantage, a rarity for the NWSL — like the Super Bowl, the venue is set in advance, meaning the field is typically neutral. Because the Washington Spirit are playing in front of a hometown crowd, tickets sold out quickly on primary marketplace Ticketmaster, although resale tickets are still available. Kansas beat Angel City and the top-seeded Gotham FC to reach the championship, while D.C. beat the Portland Thorns and Utah Royals to earn their spot. NWSL playoffs are seeded by standings, and the league is not split into Eastern and Western conferences.

The Spirit’s Trinity Rodman has been a top-five scorer across the league during the regular season, notching 14 goals. The Current’s Croix Bethune, meanwhile, led the league in assists during the regular season.

If you’re watching from home, the game will air in a primetime slot on CBS. Kick-off is at 8 p.m. ET, 5 p.m. PT on Saturday. You can stream CBS with a live-TV service that carries it, such as DirecTV or Fubo, which both offer free trials. The game will also be available to stream on Paramount+ with the Premium plan. Paramount+ does not offer a free trial.

YouTube TV offers a variable free trial that typically starts at five days, making it a solid option for catching sports events. The Sports + News plan starts at $49.99/mo. for the first six months and is $71.99/mo. thereafter. The full YouTube TV plan starts at $59.99/mo. for the first two months and is $82.99/mo. after that. Both packages include CBS, as well as other local channels like NBC, FOX, and ABC.

Hulu + Live TV is available with a three-day free trial, and it includes Disney+, Hulu, and ESPN Select. The service is available with either the ad-supported or ad-free versions of Disney+ and Hulu. Hulu + Live TV includes CBS, getting you live access to the game. You also get other channels that carry regular season games, including ESPN and ESPN2.
