Lincoln Celebrated the Art of Rejuvenation at Billboard Latin Music Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

From wellness retreats to sensory experiences, Lincoln invited attendees to recharge amid the excitement of Miami

Miami doesn’t exactly do quiet—especially during Billboard Latin Music Week, when the city comes alive with music, industry conversations and nonstop events. But amid the excitement, Lincoln offered attendees something unexpected: a moment to slow down and recharge. Explore Billboard See latest videos, charts and news

Throughout the week at Faena Forum, the luxury automotive brand brought its signature approach to comfort, design and rejuvenation to life through immersive experiences that offered a glimpse into the thoughtful details behind Lincoln’s latest innovations. Between back-to-back panels, a little downtime went a long way. Lincoln’s Rejuvenation Pod invited attendees to step away from the bustle and settle into a moment of calm. Inspired by the Lincoln Rejuvenate feature available in the Nautilus and Navigator, the experience offered a taste of relaxation designed to be enjoyed behind the wheel.

Just a few steps away, the Lincoln Navigator turned heads in a specialty exterior color, Frosted Fig. Guests explored the SUV’s luxurious interior, complete with an expansive panoramic sunroof and a sweeping digital display across the dashboard. And because Miami’s sunshine rarely takes a day off, Lincoln offered ice pops to help guests beat the heat.

Inside Faena Forum, the experience continued with opportunities to indulge the senses. A lush floral installation became a picture-perfect backdrop for attendees capturing memories from the week, while a nearby scent discovery station invited guests to explore the fragrance options available through the Digital Scent feature. Together, the installations offered a reminder that luxury isn’t always about what catches the eye and sometimes, it’s about how a space makes you feel.

The Lincoln experience extended beyond Faena Forum on Monday evening, as Billboard’s Latin Power Players celebration brought together some of the industry’s most influential names. Select honorees enjoyed courtesy rides in Lincoln vehicles to the awards ceremony, arriving in style for a night celebrating those shaping Latin music’s global impact.

Across a week defined by music, movement and milestone moments, Lincoln made the spaces in between feel just as memorable. From discovering new scents to exploring luxurious interiors and finding a few minutes to recharge, each experience reflected the pleasure of being present.

And in a city that rarely slows its rhythm, Lincoln made a compelling case for enjoying the pause.
