Russell: Hamilton ruthless like Verstappen but has grace and values

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

George Russell's long-standing feud with Max Verstappen reignited at the Las Vegas GP after the pair collided in final practice; watch Russell's full exclusive interview during Sky Sports F1's live coverage of the Qatar Grand Prix from 2.30pm on Sunday

George Russell says Lewis Hamilton is an equally "ruthless competitor" to Max Verstappen but that his former team-mate has "incredible values, grace and dignity".

Russell and Verstappen's rivalry reignited last weekend at the Las Vegas Grand Prix following a minor collision between the pair in practice, after which the Mercedes driver publicly criticised his Red Bull rival's conduct.

Verstappen won a thrilling race in Las Vegas that saw Russell challenge him for the lead for long periods before crashing and finishing ninth, and afterwards the four-time world champion said the Brit is "so far below my level" before adding that it was "fantastic" seeing him make an error.

Speaking exclusively to Martin Brundle during an interview that will air during Sky Sports F1's live coverage of the Qatar Grand Prix on Sunday, Russell said he found Verstappen's verbal attack on him "amusing", before drawing a comparison between the Dutchman and seven-time world champion Hamilton.

"He [Verstappen] is a ruthless competitor and I massively respect that, but there's also Lewis Hamilton, who I've spent three years team-mates with and watched my whole life," Russell said.

"And obviously Toto [Wolff, Mercedes' team principal] spent 12 years with [Hamilton], who is equally a ruthless competitor and absolute animal behind the wheel, but obviously has incredible values and grace and dignity.

"And Lewis is somebody who I really look up to and who is an incredible role model for young kids coming through.

"But there is no right or wrong, there's just opinions. Everyone has different opinions, and some people like driver A and some people like driver B, and as I've grown older, I don't really let it affect me as much."

Wolff has previously tried to sign Verstappen from Red Bull with Russell telling Sky Sports F1 in June 2025 that his own contract extension was being delayed by Mercedes' attempts to recruit Verstappen.

Wolff was photographed holidaying with Verstappen, has rarely criticised the Dutchman in recent years. and in Las Vegas called the back and forth between the two drivers a "good factor of the entertainment" in Formula 1.

Asked about Wolff's position on his clashes with Verstappen, Russell said: "Well I know where he [Wolff] stands off the record, so I won't probably repeat that.

"Toto and I were flying back after the race in Vegas and we did have quite a laugh about the comments that were said after the race.

"But we just know how certain people go about their business."

Verstappen and Russell have clashed several times since the latter joined Mercedes in 2022, a move that made battles between the pair for top positions a more regular occurrence.

Their first collision came in the Sprint at the 2023 Azerbaijan Grand Prix on the opening lap, before their rivalry spectacularly ignited at the end of 2024 when Verstappen was given a one-place grid penalty for impeding Russell at the 2024 Qatar Grand Prix.

Verstappen said Russell's conduct with the stewards in Qatar meant he had "lost all respect" for the Brit, who then responded with a remarkable explosive verbal attack on the Dutchman the following week at the season-ending Abu Dhabi Grand Prix.

Russell said at the time that Verstappen had threatened to put him "in the wall" in Qatar, and said that "people have been bullied by Max for years". Verstappen questioned the accuracy of Russell's claims and labelled him a "loser" and "backstabber".

Reflecting on his comments during the 2024 meeting with the stewards in Qatar, Russell said: "Well, I wasn't trying to let him off. I mean, I didn't go in there saying, 'that's fine'."

On the alleged threat that the Mercedes driver said followed in Abu Dhabi, Russell added: "That's how he [Verstappen] goes about his business. I've known him long enough that I know, again, that was a bit more of a laugh away comment, I thought."

The pair came collided at the 2025 Spanish Grand Prix after Verstappen felt Russell forced him wide and effectively retaliated by making contact with Russell and was given a 10-second time penalty for the incident.

Russell downplayed suggestions that the feud had begun during the pair's karting days, before insisting he hasn't let Verstappen's latest comments overly affect him.

"We actually didn't have that many comings together in karting," Russell said. "He's a year older than me so we only raced together once in 2011, and then obviously he fast-tracked his career.

"I found it all quite amusing afterwards. But obviously he won the race, I had a c**p race, and he's able to have the last laugh, so I'm sure he quite enjoyed that. A few years ago, it probably would have affected me a lot more, but we're different characters.

"I respect his driving talents immensely and everything he achieves on track. Obviously, maybe off track we do things slightly differently, but that's how it is."
