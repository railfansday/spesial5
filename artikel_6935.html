Cheung also says that “ingredients that help firm and strengthen your skin, including retinol and antioxidants, will help to fight puffy eyes,” too.

Along with containing depuffing ingredients, many popular eye creams for puffy eyes come with a cooling, massage tip applicator (like Caudalie, Ilia, and Tru Alchemy, above) which helps with further depuffing. Plus, they feel incredible.

Everyone's skin is different, and an eye cream that works for one reviewer may not work for you. For example, if you have drier skin, you may prefer a richer formula like the Charlotte Tilbury Magic Eye Rescue Cream, while someone with oilier skin may have better results with a gel-based product like the Dieux Auracle Eye Gel.

Additionally, an eye cream that’s super potent and yields amazing results for one person may be too strong for someone with sensitive skin. We recommend patch-testing a product on your wrist to see if your skin is reactive to it before applying it to more sensitive areas like your eyes.

There are a few different methods for quickly reducing eye puffiness. To depuff your eyes fast, you can dunk your face in an ice bath or apply cold compresses to your eyes, Cheung says. She also says that placing chilled cucumber slices or damp tea bags on your eyes can depuff in a pinch as well.

Damp tea bags are especially useful because you'll get the cooling effects as well as some caffeine for reducing inflammation. Giving yourself a lymphatic massage (with your hands or an ice roller) can help to move any puffiness-inducing fluid around and deflate under-eye bags as well.
