Hailey Bieber, Zoë Kravitz, and Rosé Match in Metallic Gold to Honor Anthony Vaccarello at the CFDA Fashion Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Their looks were pulled from Vaccarello’s final collection at Saint Laurent

Anthony Vaccarello was feeling the love from three of his strongest disciples on Monday evening, as he was honored with the International Designer of the Year prize at the 2026 Council of Fashion Designers of America (CFDA) Fashion Awards in New York City.

The prize served, in part, as a tribute to Vaccarello’s 10-year tenure at Saint Laurent, during which he found quite a few famous devotees. So, it felt fitting that three of them—Hailey Bieber, Zoë Kravitz, and Rosé—bestowed him with the honor, all while matching in metallic-gold looks pulled from his final collection at the French fashion house.

About a week before Vaccarello announced his departure from Saint Laurent, he presented his Spring/Summer 2027 ready-to-wear show at Paris Fashion Week. Held in front of the Eiffel Tower, the swan-song runway show was defined by a stream of nearly 60 glittering gold looks, broken up by only a few outliers in black and bronze.

Pulling from this collection, Bieber wore a sheet metal-like midi dress, which was embroidered with beaded floral motifs, with spiky brown fur decorating the cuffs. A split ran up the center of the skirt, and the Rhode founder paired the design with matching strappy heels.

Kravitz, meanwhile, wore her own midi design in a crinkly material that emulated crocodile skin and featured a teeny belt string tied into a bow along the waist. She matched the dress with a pair of decaying chandelier earrings, but she added a pop of contrast with some grape-colored slingbacks (with gold-dipped toes, of course).

Finally, Rosé switched things up a bit by wearing a two-piece set consisting of a cropped sleeveless top and a split-hem midi skirt, both decorated with intricate beaded designs in a fabric similar to Bieber’s look. The top was trimmed with large golden studs that framed the piece like armor, and everything was matched with a pair of shiny gold sandal heels down low.
