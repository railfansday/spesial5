Macaulay Culkin Is Home Alone Again, This Time in McDonald’s

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Macaulay Culkin has reprised his famous role as Home Alone protagonist Kevin McCallister for McDonald’s global holiday campaign.

In the seasonal ad, a grown-up Kevin finds himself alone again—this time in an empty McDonald’s instead of at home.

Tapping into the mischievous spirit that fueled his childhood adventures, Kevin lets loose in the restaurant and puts his own spin on McDonald’s menu. He mixes a McFlurry and makes a Big Mac while having fun in the kitchen, behind the counter, and in the drive-thru.

To coincide with Home Alone’s 36th anniversary, Wieden+Kennedy Toronto created the campaign, directed by Ramy Youssef and Amir Farhang.

Brittaney is Adweek's creative editor based in London.
