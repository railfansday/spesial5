The Pokémon TCG Ascended Heroes Elite Trainer Box hits its lowest price in months — save $35

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Ascended Heroes Elite Trainer Box is on sale for $141.97 at Amazon, down from its $179.95 list price.

After spending much of the year at eye-watering prices due to the massive market values for particular chase cards, the Pokémon TCG Ascended Heroes Elite Trainer Box is finally becoming easier on your trading card budget — now back under $145 in an early Black Friday deal.

As of Nov. 13, the Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Ascended Heroes Elite Trainer Box costs just $141.97 at Amazon, a $37.98 drop from the latest $179.95 list price. At 21% off, you’ll also get free delivery set for Nov. 24 at the time of writing, and it is only $16.52 off its $125.45 TCGplayer market price.

This new deal from third-party seller Thumbs Up Distribution undercuts the $150.81 Amazon deal we covered just over a week ago and is the lowest price this ETB has seen on Amazon since April — right before its market price skyrocketed.

Each Ascended Heroes Elite Trainer Box includes nine titular booster packs, a full-art foil N's Zekrom promo card, 65 sleeves, Energy cards, and extra accessories.
