Jensen Huang is Reinstated as Nvidia CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Nvidia board of directors announced Monday that it has reinstated Jensen Huang as company president and CEO less than two weeks after his resignation over extramarital affairs.

The board said in a statement that it voted unanimously to bring back Huang effective immediately after holding a series of meetings and discussions with the ousted executive that convinced board members of his "candor and ability to lead Nvidia."

The announcement noted that the Nvidia board received input from "from senior levels of the United States government" that left it confident that Huang’s reinstatement "poses no national security risk".

Huang, 63, voluntarily stepped down on Nov. 12 amid reports of multiple dalliances, including one with foreign business executive and suspected Chinese spy Alice Huang, and another with Nvidia healthcare executive Kimberly Powell, who was his direct subordinate.

"The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," said Nvidia Board Chair Harvey C. Jones. "We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the Company."

This marks a sharp U-turn for Jones, who said on an earnings call last week that the board had no intention of reinstating Huang.

In a statement, Huang said in part that he is honored to return to Nvidia and is thankful to the board for its confidence, and to the employees, shareholders, and his family for thier support.

"I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment," said the billionaire tech titan. "I can’t wait to get back to work."

Nvidia’s stock surged over 30% in the wake of Huang's comeback.

"The NVIDIA Board of Directors has made the correct and proper decision to return Jensen Huang to the position of CEO," President Donald Trump said in a Truth Social post. "The Country needs Jensen’s leadership at NVIDIA in order to ensure the Success and Prosperity of not only the Company, but the American Economy."

Trump, who has been supportive of the Nvidia CEO's reinstatement in his public comments, said Monday that "the Attorney General of the United States has confirmed that the U.S. Government does not possess evidence that he has engaged in any unlawful conduct."

Still, Nvidia's board said a committee formed to investigate whether Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence operation will proceed with its work.

"Mr. Huang fully supports the investigation and is committed to cooperating with it," read the statement.
