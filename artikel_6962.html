Spoilers: Where Erika and Blake Stand After Breaking Things Off During ‘Love Is Blind’

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Reminder: This season was filmed a year ago...things change!

Erika and Blake genuinely seemed to form a connection during Love Is Blind, and honestly...we had hope that they’d make it down the aisle. Sure, they had vastly different lifestyles (he literally lives on a boat for most of the year), but they seemed like soulmate material, and Blake was...kinda willing (?) to settle down and think about starting a family. Buuuut things started getting complicated after Blake’s close friend refused to support the relationship, and ultimately the season ended with Erika ending the relationship ahead of their wedding.

“I think at this point there’s too many concerns, too many questions, that I can’t say yes at the altar,” Erika said, adding, “I think the best thing to do is just to leave the experiment.” Cut to Blake talking to a producer and saying “I’m feeling a little relieved, a little exhausted. Erika cried, I didn’t cry. Who said I cried? I didn’t cry! Erika cries a bunch...you know. It was sad.”

So, where do things stand now? Love Is Blind recently filmed their season 11 reunion (which airs this weekend!) and Reality Receipts podcaster Rikkii Wise (@storytimewithrikkii) has details. According to Rikkii, Erika and Blake are no longer together, but they definitely had a vibe during the reunion. Which makes sense, their breakup was definitely reluctant and more to do with physical distance/lifestyle than anything else.

Sounds like romance isn’t completely dead for these two, so stay tuned.
