OBSBOT Introduces Tail 2 ADV and Tail Air 2: Expanding Creative Freedom for Professional Production Teams and Creators

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Built on OBSBOT's proprietary AI tracking and pioneering PTZR innovation, two new Tail series cameras bring its vision of Autonomous Imaging into distinct production environments.

NEW YORK, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Leading AI imaging brand OBSBOT introduced Tail 2 ADV and Tail Air 2, two new additions to its Tail series designed to address those challenges in different shooting environments on November 19. The products build on OBSBOT's expertise in AI algorithms, precision motion control and optics, enabling technology to take on more of the repeated work of filming while people retain creative control.

For anyone presenting, performing, or creating in front of a camera, constantly wondering if the camera is following can pull attention away from the moment that matters. Behind the camera, production teams face their own demands: keeping a moving subject framed, capturing details from a distance and managing multiple views as the action unfolds.

"True innovation should give creators more freedom, not more complexity. With our latest products, we are expanding an already robust OBSBOT portfolio to take more of the repetitive work out of production, so creators can spend more time on what matters most: their ideas and creativity," said Bo Liu, Founder and CEO of OBSBOT.

Built on Pioneering AI Tracking and PTZR Innovation

For OBSBOT, intelligent camera movement has been a core technology from the start. Its proprietary AI tracking brings together subject recognition and motorized camera control, turning the work of following a subject into an integrated camera capability. That foundation gives people greater freedom to move, perform and present without continuously directing the camera.

Tail 2 ADV and Tail Air 2 extend the foundation in intelligent camera operation built with the first three-axis PTZR 4K camera, Tail 2, to two distinct shooting environments.
