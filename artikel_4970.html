BTCS offloads $44M in ETH to reduce DeFi debt amid market downturn

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

BTCS sold more than half its Ether holdings to reduce leverage following the market sell-off, while BitMine added another 7,819 ETH to its treasury.

Nasdaq-listed blockchain company BTCS sold approximately 27,300 Ether on Friday to repay $43.8 million in loans on decentralized lending protocol Aave as cryptocurrency markets plunged.

According to a Monday filing with the US Securities and Exchange Commission, BTCS sold the Ether (ETH) on Nov. 20 and used the proceeds to reduce its outstanding Aave debt to approximately $10.5 million.

The transaction reduced BTCS’s Aave borrowings by approximately 81%, as the company sought to limit liquidation risk amid heightened market volatility. Its remaining Aave positions had a loan-to-value ratio of approximately 32% following the repayment.

BTCS reported approximately $17.9 million in outstanding convertible notes, in addition to its remaining Aave borrowings, according to the company’s announcement. The company also said it temporarily exited liquidity pool positions in its Imperium DeFi business as part of its efforts to reduce exposure to further market volatility.

Following the sale, BTCS held approximately 24,245 ETH, with cash and digital assets valued at a combined $61 million as of Sunday. The 27,300 ETH sale represented approximately 53% of its combined pre-sale ETH holdings.

The move follows a similar reduction in Ether exposure by digital asset manager CoinShares, which disclosed last week that it sold its entire ETH treasury position and half its Solana (SOL) holdings in October to fund share buybacks.

Meanwhile, Tom Lee’s BitMine Immersion Technologies continued accumulating Ether, purchasing another 7,819 ETH over the past week despite the market downturn.

According to a Monday announcement, BitMine now holds 6,085,564 ETH, representing 4.98% of the cryptocurrency’s total supply. The company said it has purchased Ether every week since adopting its treasury strategy in June 2025.

BitMine also reported $384 million in cash and marketable securities as of Sunday. The company has staked approximately 5.23 million ETH, representing 86% of its total Ether holdings.

BitMine accounts for roughly 75% of the Ether held in corporate and government treasuries tracked by CoinGecko, which listed 35 entities holding a combined 8.16 million ETH at the time of reporting.

The contrasting strategies come as Ether continues to struggle, trading at approximately $1,743 as of 6:35 p.m. UTC on Monday, down 37.7% over the past year and 24.8% over the past 30 days, according to CoinGecko data.
