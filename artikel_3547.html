Chris Briney Would 'Love to Be a F***ing Cowboy' on 'Landman' After 'TSITP'

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Christopher Briney is keeping his options open when it comes to his next roles after The Summer I Turned Pretty — including possibly playing a cowboy on Taylor Sheridan‘s hit series Landman.

During an exclusive interview with Us at the Clashing Through the Snow red carpet on Wednesday, November 19, Briney, 28, weighed in on a potential arc in his costar Michelle Randolph‘s Paramount+ show, saying, “What they’re doing is really great, and I know that they’re still going. I’d audition [for sure].”

Briney shared that he even had a specific role in mind. “I’d love to be a f***ing cowboy, dude,” he added. “With an accent? That’d be fun. I could work with a dialect coach.”

Before pitching himself for a Sheridan series, Briney became a household name playing Conrad in Jenny Han’s TV adaptation of her book series The Summer I Turned Pretty. Briney filmed three seasons of the show and is returning in the upcoming movie, which is meant to help conclude the series.

After Randolph, 29, found out about Briney’s interest in Landman, she told Us, “He’s ready? Oh my God! Let me call Taylor. Christopher Briney is going to be a cowboy!”

Based on the “Boomtown” podcast, Landman is Sheridan’s latest hit series, joining the ranks of Yellowstone, Mayor of Kingstown, Tulsa King, Lawmen: Bass Reeves and Lioness. The Paramount+ series introduced Billy Bob Thornton as a corporate fixer working for an oil titan in a West Texas-set drama with life-and-death stakes.

Randolph plays Thornton’s character Tommy’s daughter, Ainsley, who has faced her own ups and downs as a college student, which will continue to be explored in season 3.

“I feel like I’ve almost grown up with her at a point because I was 24 when I booked the show,” Randolph told Us. “She’s my better half. I wish I looked at life like she does.”

Despite her love for Ainsley, Randolph was excited to branch out with Clashing Through the Snow. The Prime Video movie, which premieres on Monday, November 23, follows two rival book editors stranded in a snowstorm who must share a rental car to New York to land a career-saving author deal.

“When I read the script, I immediately just fell in love with it,” Randolph gushed. “I thought it was funny. I think there’s such value in telling stories that are just meant to bring people joy and don’t ask anything of the audience. And I love rom-coms and I love the holidays.”

Clashing Through the Snow premieres on Prime Video Monday, November 23.
