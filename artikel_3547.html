American Psycho Remake Gets Disappointing Update After Years Of Development

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

A remake of the classic horror film, American Psycho, receives an unfortunate update.

Luca Guadagnino's planned American Psycho remake has received a long-awaited but upsetting update. The director has revealed that his new adaptation of Bret Easton Ellis' bestselling novel is no longer moving forward, bringing an end to years of uncertainty surrounding the project.

During an appearance on Deadline's Crew Call podcast with Anthony D'Alessandro, Guadagnino addressed the status of his American Psycho remake. Although he praised the screenplay written by Scott Z. Burns, the director revealed that the project is no longer happening. However, he did not explain why the film has been canceled or whether there are any plans to revive it in the future.

Scott Z Burns wrote a fantastic script. Unfortunately for me, as we speak, it's not happening.

The project was first announced in October 2024. Lionsgate confirmed that it had been developing a remake of Ellis' 1991 novel, with Burns writing the screenplay and Guadagnino directing. However, instead of just recreating the 2000 Christian Bale-led movie, the new adaptation was going to offer audiences a different take on the original story.

Unfortunately, shortly after the studio announced the project, it faced several obstacles, especially when it came to finding an actor to portray Patrick Bateman. Austin Butler was reportedly linked to the leading role in December 2024, but his involvement was never officially confirmed by Guadagnino.

In early 2025, Ellis explained that the movie had not secured the rights it needed to move forward. Despite the author's comments, Guadagnino was still optimistic about the film during CinemaCon in April 2025, discussing the screenplay's development and his love for the book.

Released in 2000, American Psycho is a psychological horror film directed by Mary Harron, who co-wrote the screenplay with Guinevere Turner. The movie stars Bale as Bateman, a successful New York City investment banker who seems like a normal enough person but is actually a serial killer. The film also featured several other iconic actors, including Willem Dafoe, Jared Leto, Josh Lucas, Chloë Sevigny, Samantha Mathis, Justin Theroux, and Reese Witherspoon.

American Psycho's producer Edward R. Pressman acquired the adaptation rights in 1992, and several directors, such as David Cronenberg and Stuart Gordon, were interested in directing the project. Although Harron chose Bale for the leading role, the studio was also considering casting Leonardo DiCaprio.

American Psycho was based on a novel of the same name. The book was written from Bateman's perspective. Its graphic and intricate descriptions of violence and other disturbing content caused a lot of controversy, resulting in a ton of restrictions being placed on it when it comes to selling the book. However, the book was never outright banned in any country.
