Jason Kelce Trolls Cowboys After Eagles' Stunning Thanksgiving Comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Jason Kelce was in celebratory mood after the Philadelphia Eagles completed a dramatic comeback to defeat the Dallas Cowboys on Thursday, November 26.

“Happy Thanksgiving America!!!” Kelce, 39, wrote via X moments after Philadelphia’s 39-38 road victory over their staunch divisional rivals. The Cowboys are often referred to as America’s Team.

The Eagles (4-7) defeated the Cowboys (5-7) after overcoming a 21-point deficit, the largest Thanksgiving Day comeback in NFL history.

Philadelphia achieved the upset win thanks in large part to the performance of quarterback Joe Milton in just his third-career NFL start. Milton, 26, was 21-of-31 passing for 331 yards with three touchdowns, plus a rushing touchdown.

“Bazooka Joe, my goodness, hell of a performance,” Kelce wrote in a separate post. “Man that was an electric freakin game baby. Just uncle Rico’d us to a win over the Cowboys on Thanksgiving. I’ll remember this game forever, thank you guys!”

Kelce, who played all 13 of his NFL seasons with the Eagles before retiring in 2024, also posted a selfie right after the game ended, writing, “F*** YESSSSSSSS!!!!!”

“A lot of emotions,” Milton said after the game. “But mainly just happy for this team. This team never let up.”

Milton, who appeared in four games for the Cowboys in 2025, added, “It was just great coming back here and playing in this stadium and seeing the guys I worked hard with. But it’s about the Eagles.”

Milton was signed to the Eagles’ practice squad on October 16, replacing quarterback Tanner McKee as the starter against the New York Giants on November 8. McKee, 26, had originally stepped in for Eagles starting quarterback Jalen Hurts, who has been out since multiple blood clots were discovered in his lungs last month.

As for the Cowboys, it was a stunning loss for a team desperately trying to stay relevant in the NFC playoff race.

“The disappointment is in the fact of the fact that all three phases had a chance to go and put this game away, and we didn’t,” head coach Brian Schottenheimer said after the game. “That’s on us as a football team.”

Cowboys quarterback Dak Prescott urged that not all hope is lost, with the Cowboys holding just an 8 percent chance of making the playoffs according to NFL’s Next Gen Stats.

“We’ve got to take some time off this weekend, [start] there and get back to it with more urgency and be better than ever individually and collectively to give it our best chance,” Prescott, 33, said.

The Cowboys return to action against the Seattle Seahawks on Monday, December 7.
