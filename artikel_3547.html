3 Best New Prime Video Series To Binge-Watch This Weekend (November 6–8, 2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

While massive hit Prime Video series such as The Terminal List season 2, Carrie, and Love in Disguise continue to take over global viewership charts, these three new series represent the cutting edge of Prime Video streaming content right now. If you're looking for more great binge-watching options this weekend, check out ScreenRant's updated streaming guides for Netflix, Paramount+, and HBO Max.

Devils' Crest is a brand new anime series that was just added to Prime Video on Friday, November 6. The series was created by Reki Kawahara, best known for Sword Art Online and Accel World, and is based on the book series of the same name. Devils' Crest follows a group of elementary school students who test a new groundbreaking video game called Actual Magic and soon begin to realize that their reality is being morphed by it. The series centers on 6th-grade student Yuuma Ashihara, who explores the MMORPG with his friends.

Devils' Crest blends multiple genres together and is ideal for fans of anime, sci-fi, horror, and fantasy. The first two episodes are available to stream now, with new weekly installments arriving every Friday throughout the remainder of Fall 2026 until its finale drops on December 18. With its short 24-minute episode length, Devils' Crest is one of the easiest new shows to binge-watch this weekend on Prime Video.

Campus Drivers is another exciting new Prime Video series that premiered on Friday, November 6. The coming-of-age romantic drama series takes place along the French Riviera and was filmed in Cannes and Côte d'Azur, transporting viewers to one of the most sought-after destinations on the planet. Based on the bestselling book series of the same name by C.S. Quill, Campus Drivers follows Lois, a heartbroken woman beginning her freshman year at Belrose University who meets Lane, the creator of the school's most popular app.

Campus Drivers consists of just six episodes, all of which are available to stream right now. Each episode of Campus Drivers runs between 28 and 46 minutes long, with a total runtime of just under 4 hours, making it entirely possible to binge-watch in just one sitting. If you're a fan of Prime Video's other school-centered romantic dramas like Off Campus and Maxton Hall, Campus Drivers is one of the best new shows on any streaming platform for you to dive into this weekend.

Prime Video's best new show worth watching this weekend is The Greatest, a new 8-part docudrama about one of the most celebrated sports figures of the 20th century, Cassius Clay, who famously changed his name to Muhammad Ali at the age of 22. Rather than spanning the entire life and career of the prolific boxer, The Greatest narrows its focus on one of the most significant 4-year spans in Clay's history, beginning with his 1960 Olympic gold medal victory at the age of 18 to his monumental defeat of Sonny Liston in 1964, making him a heavyweight boxing champion.

Prime Video's new 8-part biopic traces a legendary boxer's rise, exploring family, faith, and the convictions that shaped him into a cultural icon.

The Greatest was released on Wednesday, November 4, and is quickly becoming one of Prime Video's top new series heading into the weekend. The series also chronicles Clay's personal developments outside the boxing ring, including his spiritual and religious interests and friendship with Malcolm X shortly before the latter's assassination. The Greatest earned an 88% Rotten Tomatoes score and features an exceptional cast led by Jaalen Best, Omari Hardwick, and Giovanni Ribisi. For the best binge-watching experience on Prime Video this weekend, check out The Greatest​​​​​​.
