EA Begs You To Not Delete Madden 27 For GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

We all know what's coming: Grand Theft Auto 6. Whether you're ready for it or not, the most anticipated game of the decade is preparing to launch on November 19, and ahead of its release, EA is begging you to please not ditch footballs for guns.

According to Push Square, EA added a "don't delete" pop-up message to Madden NFL 27. The pop-up says there's an update coming to the football simulator on November 17, just before GTA 6 drops.

"Don't delete," the message reads. "[Encrypted Signal] The target is locked. The crew is assembling for a high-stakes run. Await the signal from our insider on 11/17 ... do not leak this transmission!"

In a November 10 post on Madden NFL 27's official X account, EA said that a throwback Madden 97 update is coming on November 18 with old-school playbooks, 16-bit gameplay, and the All '97 Team. Underneath that post, EA had asked that you stick around a little while longer.

This is in reference to the humongous amount of storage space that Rockstar Games' GTA 6 will take up when it launches. Start clearing out your hard drive now because you need at least 212GB of free space to download GTA 6. In comparison, Madden NFL 27 needs approximately 60GB to install the game.

If you're like me and you still have the base PS5, this means you have approximately 667GB of usable space. Sure, you can fit both GTA 6 and Madden NFL 27 on the same console. However, if you have any other recent releases like The Blood of Dawnwalker (~50GB), Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen (~81GB), Phantom Blade Zero (~135GB), and Valor Mortis (~60GB), then you can see how quickly you'll run out of space--especially since you have to leave a little wiggle room for game updates, too. That's not to mention the games dropping in 2027, such as Final Fantasy VII Revelation, which game director Naoki Hamaguchi said could be around 200GB.

EA has recently gone through a restructuring that saw 400 employees across the company get let go and the legendary studio BioWare get shut down. In the midst of this, the publisher reiterated its commitment to the Madden franchise with a new title currently codenamed Project Gridiron. While filled with potential, we do fear that EA's plan for the series may lean into everything that the company itself does wrong.
