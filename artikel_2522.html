Three takeaways from Royals upset over Wave in NWSL playoff opener

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Goals change games, McGlynn’s best-ever performance and another disappointing ending for San Diego

The opening playoff game of 2026 was considered the least likely of the quarterfinal quartet to produce an upset. But the games are played on the field, and the Royals rode a Kiana Palacios goal and a sterling performance from goalkeeper Mandy McGlynn to a 1-0 win over the Wave in San Diego. The Royals had lost all 6 matches against the Wave since joining the league in 2024, but Friday’s playoff debut was their night.

Here are three takeaways from the first of seven playoff tilts of the season.

Seattle Reign head coach Laura Harvey has said it umpteen times in post match press conferences through the years. And that is exactly what transpired at Snapdragon Stadium on Friday night. The Wave came out the aggressors and may have had a few in the opening 15 minutes if not for McGlynn. And then all of a sudden, a ball sprayed out wide to Cloé Lacasse, a cross into the box for Palacios, a quick turn and shoot, and it was 1-0 Royals. The way the match had been played up to that point on 30 minutes, it stood to reason the Wave would figure out a way to at least create the chances needed to find an equalizer. They really didn’t.
