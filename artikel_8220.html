A great lift system can make or break a ski day. Fast, comfortable lifts in the right places keep skiers moving around the mountain instead of standing in line or wasting time getting from one terrain pod to the next. The payoff? More vertical, more laps, and more time doing what you came for.

Want to spend less time waiting and more time skiing? These 10 Western resorts earned the highest marks for their lift systems in SKI’s annual Resort Survey. Consider them your ticket to a more efficient—and more fun—ski vacation.
