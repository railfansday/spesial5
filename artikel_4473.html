Denisovan remains and an unknown archaic human lineage discovered in southern China

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

An international team of researchers, including first author Song Xing, a researcher affiliated with the Centro Nacional de Investigación sobre la Evolución Humana (CENIEH), has just published a study in the journal Nature that analyzed four fossil teeth recovered from two caves in southern China. The team has identified two possible human lineages dating to the Middle and Late Pleistocene: one with Denisovan affinities and another more primitive lineage whose evolutionary identity remains unclear.
