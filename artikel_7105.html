Why Travis Kelce Didn’t Join Taylor Swift at the 2026 CMA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Taylor Swift returned to the CMA Awards for the first time in 10 years this evening, but her husband, Travis Kelce, wasn’t seen there with her. Though the singer posed on the red carpet solo, she wasn’t alone during the ceremony—she was spotted beside her friend Brittany Mahomes, a fellow Kansas City Chiefs WAG. (She is married to Kelce’s teammate Patrick Mahomes, and they’re expecting their fourth child together.)

Kelce is in the middle of his football season. His next game is at Arrowhead Stadium on Sunday, against the Arizona Cardinals, which could explain his absence this evening.

This past weekend, Kelce left the field for part of the team’s game against the Atlanta Falcons due to an ankle injury. Postgame, he gave USA Today an update. “I’m all good,” he said. “I probably could have went more than I did when I came back, but it was the direction the team wanted to go, so I was right there with them. We had a lot of young guys step up, so it was really nice.”

While he and Swift are supportive of each other’s careers, the pair hasn’t made their red carpet debut at an awards ceremony yet. Last March, they attended the iHeartRadio Music Awards together but skipped the red carpet. Kelce remained by Swift’s side throughout the show.

In an October 2025 episode of their podcast New Heights, Kelce’s brother Jason asked him about being Swift’s creative muse for The Life of a Showgirl. The tight end coyly replied, “I’m just the lucky man that gets to be, you know, the support system for Taylor while she drops one of the coolest albums that I’ve ever listened to, that the world’s seen. I’m excited for her. I’m happy for her. Everything seems to have gone perfectly, all this planning.”

He detailed what it was like watching her make the album, revealing that he got to listen to it before it was released. “You see it from start to finish,” he said. “She started this thing in the middle of her [Eras] Tour in Europe. To see it all unfold and come together and her connect the dots—from the strategic planning to how the album was all going to look—then to listen to the music, and everything comes together and her finally getting that 12th song and being like ‘I think it’s done.’

“From that point on, it’s like her and her label are just working their ass off to make this one of the biggest releases of all time,” he added. “I’m just happy as hell for it because she’s happy with it. She’s proud of this and obviously I’m proud of her for going through the hoops to live out her dreams.”
