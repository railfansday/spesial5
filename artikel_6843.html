House Republicans Couldn’t Have Picked a Worse Minority Speaker

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

So much for cleaning house. Rep. Jim Jordan of Ohio has been a loyal foot soldier of the MAGA regime for years now.

Politico has a very strange story about the battle for Republican leadership in the House of Representatives. Speaker Mike Johnson already has scratched himself from the race to become the next minority leader. And here is Politico’s take, which seems very, very odd.

So far, this is a run-of-the-mill state of the losers’ postelection story. The losing side, particularly a catastrophically losing side, always spends some time in public introspection. It’s almost a kind of penance. But here’s where the story spins out.

As I’m sure Stutzman knows, on any issue that both involves the administration and drove voters in the midterm elections, there’s not a dime’s worth of difference between Scalise and Jordan (R-Van Heusen). They have both been loyal foot soldiers of the MAGA regime. Both of them supported at least one objection to certify the results in 2020. On Tuesday, Jordan thumped Scalise and became the GOP leader, 106–73, and it will be interpreted as another defeat for Johnson’s old leadership team. But the promotion of Jordan doesn’t answer any of the problems that contributed to the loss of Congress by the Republicans. Jordan’s knees haven’t stopped jerking since 2017. He hunted every snipe at which the administration pointed, even the ones that didn’t exist, which was a substantial number of them. He was rude and approximately as committed to bipartisanship as a manhole cover. If the primary issue in the election was corruption, Jordan has made a career out of supplying alibis.

I hope Speaker-in-Waiting Jeffries realizes all of this. This is the legislative equivalent of Stephen Miller running the White House. I’d say the Republicans could have found someone more dedicated to getting things done, but there isn’t anybody like that left.
