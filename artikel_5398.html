After the Outlook outage: Mary Jo Foley on Ignite, Microsoft’s AI pitch, and a new approach to the press

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

by Todd Bishop on Nov 21, 2026 at 10:09 amNovember 21, 2026 at 10:13 am

This week on the GeekWire Podcast, longtime Microsoft beat reporter Mary Jo Foley, a GeekWire contributing writer, sizes up where the company stands after one of its most eventful weeks in years.

Microsoft opened its Ignite conference with an apology for the global Outlook outage, then took direct aim at OpenAI and Anthropic on price, even though it invests in both and sells their models. We discuss the lasting damage from the outage, why enterprise customers have few places to go even when they’re fed up, and Microsoft’s pitch to make Copilot the “OS for work,” as Anthropic goes public.

Mary Jo also explains how Microsoft changed the way it rolls out news at its big conferences, what got lost along the way, and what that says about the company after the shakeup of its communications team. Plus, we weigh who from Microsoft could lead Nvidia, and the GeekWire trivia challenge goes back to 1997.
