EXCLUSIVE: Google Co-Founder Sergey Brin and Ex-Wife Nicole Shanahan List Malibu Home for $33 Million

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The elegant Malibu estate that Google co-founder Sergey Brin purchased with his ex-wife, Nicole Shanahan, has hit the selling block for $33.5 million—months after it was listed as a rental.

Brin, 53, and Shanahan—who went on to join Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s short-lived presidential campaign—bought the "dreamy" Point Dume dwelling for $13.5 million in September 2020, just over a year before it was revealed that they had split after three years of marriage.

The details of their divorce settlement, which was finalized in 2023, were never fully revealed. However, reports suggest that Shanahan walked away with around $1 billion in assets, including shares in Alphabet, Google's parent company.

It has never been revealed how the former couple split their shared properties. Brin purchased another Malibu residence for $35 million just a few months after their separation, a move that many saw as a sign that Shanahan, 41, had kept their former marital home.

Property records viewed by Realtor.com® show that the five-bedroom, 7.5-bathroom dwelling is currently registered to a limited liability company in Nevada that is managed by Shanahan's family office. That LLC lists the same mailing address as her charitable organization, the Bia-Echo Foundation, which is named for the former couple's daughter, Echo.

While it's unclear whether Shanahan ever used the property as her primary residence, she revealed in an October 2025 interview with Frontier Magazine that she was living in a different California property: a mountaintop ranch where she was residing with her partner, Jacob Strumwasser.

Although the home was listed for rent for $175,000 a month in May, the property has now vanished from the rental market and is now available for purchase.

The listing, which is held by Michael Cunningham of Pinnacle Estate Properties, notes that it has undergone an extensive series of overhauls in recent months, describing it as a "newly completed estate."

"A long, private driveway leads to a secluded sanctuary surrounded by mature trees, lush landscaping, and beautiful ocean views, with the rare privilege of keyed access to Little Dume Beach. Meticulously designed with every detail reflects an uncompromising commitment to quality and sophistication," the listing notes.

The property comes with an array of upscale features and amenities, including "keyed access to Little Dume beach," a stunning stretch of sand that is known as one of Malibu's best areas for surfing.

The dwelling itself has plenty to offer, both inside and out. The 1.2-acre lot includes a dazzling swimming pool with a large spa and a pagoda-style area for shade.

The parcel is incredibly private, featuring a gated entrance, a long driveway, and tall trees surrounding the home.

The listing describes the home as the "epitome of 'quiet luxury,'" constructed with top-of-the-line materials and quality craftsmanship.

Upon entering the home, visitors are greeted with a spacious, airy foyer that opens to an elegant dining space and living room, where walls of windows flood the space with natural light. A curved ceiling elevates the room, while a fireplace keeps it cozy.

The dining area separates the living space from the enormous open-plan kitchen, which features a curved island and huge French doors that open to a private deck overlooking the grounds.

For those with a fondness for entertaining, a second meal prep area with its own stove and additional appliances will no doubt prove to be one of the most-used spaces in the dwelling.

Elsewhere on the main floor are a media room—which the listing notes could be turned into an office or library—as well as a generous gym and wellness suite.

"Designed with wellness and longevity in mind, the residence incorporates advanced clean-building technologies and carefully selected materials, including a state-of-the-art VRF climate system, copper and cast-iron plumbing, rigid metal HVAC ducting, sustainable denim insulation, and specialized wiring and shielding intended to reduce EMF exposure," the description adds.

Two more bedrooms can be found on this upper level, each with its own bathroom. Another bedroom is located in a separate apartment space with a private entrance, as well as an additional kitchen and living room.

In addition to Brin, the property has a history of A-list residents, having previously been owned by pop star Pink, who purchased it for $11.9 million in 2010 and used it as her primary residence until 2016, when she sold it for $12.5 million.

The property is located just a mile away from the Malibu home Brin bought for $35 million in April 2022, just a few months after it was revealed that he and Shanahan had split.

He has since expanded his Malibu holdings with another pricier property that he picked up for $49.7 million in July 2025.

However, Brin has recently joined the growing list of billionaires pulling their fortunes out of California amid the threat of Proposition 40, a one-time, 5% wealth tax on residents worth over $1 billion—a proposal that has since been rejected.

The Google co-founder has been an outspoken critic of the tax, likening California to the Soviet Union when asked why he left the state.

"I fled socialism with my family in 1979 and know the devastating, oppressive society it created in the Soviet Union. I don’t want California to end up in the same place," he told The New York Times in April.

California voters rejected Proposition 40, which would have imposed a one-time 5% tax on billionaires’ wealth, with most proceeds earmarked for healthcare. Opponents, including Gov. Gavin Newsom, argued that the measure could drive wealthy residents out of the state and reduce future tax revenue.

Brin has, quite literally, put his money where his mouth is, snapping up several properties outside of California in anticipation of the tax, including a $42 million mansion on the Nevada shore of Lake Tahoe and a $51 million Miami Beach dwelling that he bought from LVMH CEO Michael Burke at the start of the year.

The Miami property is located on the exclusive Allison Island, a 35-acre stretch of land that sits in the Biscayne Bay, between La Gorce and Miami Beach. The 24/7 guard-gated island is home to fewer than 50 single-family homes.

It was custom-built for Burke and his wife, Brigitte Burke, in 2019, five years after they purchased the 1-acre plot of land on which the home now sits for $11.7 million.

The spectacular property spans 10,000 square feet and has seven bedrooms, 8.5 bathrooms, a waterfront pool, and a private dock. The modernist design includes two separate wings of the home connected by a central walk-through area.

It's unclear whether Brin has any plans to offload either of his Malibu homes in the wake of his move from California. Neither dwelling is currently listed on the multiple listing service.
