Fossil posted its first profit in years and raised its full-year outlook

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Fossil Group's third-quarter net income was $11.7 million, compared with a net loss of $39.9 million a year earlier

Fossil Group reported net income of $11.7 million for its fiscal third quarter and raised its full-year 2026 outlook, the company said on Tuesday. That compares with a net loss of $39.9 million a year earlier. Net income was $0.19 per diluted share.

Net sales rose 0.4% to $271.2 million, or 2.0% in constant currency. Operating income was $25.3 million, compared with an operating loss of $21.7 million in the prior-year period. Gross margin widened to 59.8% from 49.0%, which the company attributed to lower licensed brand minimum royalties, accelerated recognition of minimum royalties and better product margins in core categories. Operating expenses came in 11.1% lower, at $137.0 million.

Under its raised guidance, Fossil expects 2026 net sales of $980 million to $990 million and adjusted operating margin of 5.5% to 6.5%, both excluding currency effects. It also expects positive free cash flow. The prior outlook called for net sales to fall 3% to 5%.

"We are pleased that year-to-date results are outpacing our expectations, supporting an increase in our full year financial outlook on both the top and bottom line," CEO Franco Fogliato said in a statement.

In constant currency, net sales in Asia rose 15.1% and sales in the Americas rose 0.1%, while Europe fell 5.6%. Wholesale sales rose 4.4%, and direct-to-consumer sales fell 5.9%. Traditional watch sales rose 6.4%, while leathers fell 20.5% and jewelry fell 10.8%.

Fossil's store count fell to 174 from 204 over the year. The company said store rationalization reduced third-quarter sales by about 190 basis points.

Fogliato told CNBC that Fossil had not foreseen any increase in revenue for another 18 months. The company's previous quarterly net income dated back to the fiscal third quarter of 2022, according to CNBC.
