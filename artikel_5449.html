Spara’s Scout AI assistant acts on sales opportunities before reps know to look

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Artificial intelligence sales agent startup Spara Inc. today introduced Scout, an AI assistant designed to act on sales opportunities before salespeople know to look for them.

Working around the clock, the new service analyzes context from customer conversations, emails and customer relationship management data to identify opportunities, prepare personalized outreach and complete work before reps realize it needs doing.

Scout is aimed at addressing a limitation of most AI assistants, which still require users to tell them what to do, according to Spara. The service instead works within the daily workflows of salespeople, providing an always-on partner that keeps track of customers and helps move work forward without constant direction.

Signals analyzed continuously by Scout include job changes, company news, buying signals and CRM activity across a salesperson’s accounts. From there, the service determines when to engage customers and prospects, as well as who to contact. Instead of requiring another platform to manage, Scout delivers its findings through Salesforce Inc.’s Slack and text messages.

Over time, Scout retains what it learns about each account, along with the salesperson’s preferences and previous outreach. Doing so is said to allow the service to anticipate next steps and deliver more relevant recommendations.

Outreach messages are drafted in the salesperson’s voice, with reps kept in control of what gets sent. Going beyond recommendations, Scout can join customer calls and contribute to conversations in real time, helping salespeople move opportunities forward.

“Sales has always been a game of speed, timing and relationships, but there’s only so much one person can keep track of in a day,” said David Walker, co-founder and chief executive of Spara. “Even the best sales organizations can’t realistically give every rep the support they need to act on every opportunity at exactly the right moment.”

Spara also released early results from users of the new service. Salespeople using Scout were found to have increased the number of personalized emails sent eightfold in their first two weeks of use. Outreach generated by Scout drew a 10.5% reply rate, against 3.5% for traditional sales sequences, according to the company. Early users also booked 29% more meetings.

Chris Brown, a partner at Spara investor Inspired Capital who has worked with the team since the beginning, said Spara has built agents that go-to-market teams trust with their most important customer conversations. Scout is now “putting that trust to work for individual sellers,” he added.

Scout is available for free starting today at spara.com/scout.

Founded by Walker and Zander Pease, Spara offers a platform for building AI agents that carry out sales work across chat, email, text messaging, voice and interactive product demos. The agents handle tasks ranging from engaging and qualifying leads to following up with prospects, booking meetings and managing renewals. Notable customers include Justworks Inc., Rho Technologies Inc. and Mindbody Inc.

Spara launched in September 2025 with $15 million in seed funding. Including additional funding since then, the company has raised $20 million to date, with Radical Ventures and Inspired Capital leading the seed round.
