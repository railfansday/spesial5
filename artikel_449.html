Early Black Friday Best Buy deals include the Apple Watch Ultra 3 and a $270 Dyson vacuum

Best Buy is one to watch as we head into the holiday shopping season. At times, Best Buy's selection and sale prices beat Amazon's and Walmart's during Black Friday. Plus, Best Buy often has daily deals that are well worth peeking at.
