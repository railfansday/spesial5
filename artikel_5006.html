Bloomberg’s James Seyffart: Wall Street Bought $4.2 BILLION BTC in Q3

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Bloomberg Intelligence's James Seyffart breaks down the latest 13F filings for the spot Bitcoin ETFs.

Bloomberg Intelligence’s James Seyffart breaks down the latest 13F filings for the spot Bitcoin ETFs. He says advisors are now by far the largest holders, even though by his firm’s categorization they sold in the third quarter while hedge funds bought. He walks through the three columns wirehouses and brokerage networks use to decide what advisors can recommend. He also says some Wells Fargo and Goldman holdings may be market making rather than long-term investing.
