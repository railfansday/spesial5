Norway Gave Us Skiing—and the Gear to Match

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Here’s something to share during après chatter: The word “ski” comes from Norwegian, and archaeologists trace the prehistoric origins of the sport to the Sami people, who used skis for hunting and travel across what would become Scandinavia. Later, the Norwegian military turned the practice into a sport, complete with races. No wonder Norway dominates so many ski disciplines at the winter Olympics.

It’s also no wonder that Helly Hansen, the legendary weather-protection gear maker, extended their apparel line to ski gear after developing waterproof oilskins almost 150 years ago. With a relentless focus on developing new materials and designs that improve comfort and performance, Helly Hansen’s latest products—like these four pieces—are the season’s secret weapon for getting the most fun out of the snow.

Inventing skiing and the best winter gear should be enough. But to really seal its legacy as the champion of the sport, Helly Hansen also wants you to Ski Free. With the purchase of an eligible pair of pants or jacket, get a complimentary one-day ski pass at one of more than 80 participating ski resorts worldwide.

One of the best jackets for resort skiing gets even better this year. The Men’s Alpha/Women’s Alphelia is lighter, warmer, and even more breathable, thanks to upgraded Lifaloft synthetic insulation that packs down tighter without sacrificing water resistance or performance. Just-right details (detachable helmet-compatible hood and powder skirt, ski pass pocket, underarm vents) now include high-tech safety features like the Life Pocket+, which helps keep your phone alive in the cold, and a RECCO Advanced Rescue System to help others find you in an emergency.

When it comes to gear, you have to earn the name “Legendary”—and these best-selling pants do, with features and flex that adapt to unpredictable conditions. Two-way stretch helps you dig into deep turns without feeling restricted, and the Helly Tech Performance shell fabric locks out precip while shedding moisture from the inside. Primaloft insulation keeps you plenty warm on long lift rides, but you can also dump heat: Lengthy thigh zippers offer venting for strenuous uphill climbs or leg-burning descents on warmer days.

Everyone needs a versatile midlayer. Make yours this one—it transitions perfectly from shoulder-season hiking to winter skiing. In place of conventional pile-and-pill fleece, the unique grid-pattern fleece traps heat and breathes well, keeping you in the comfort zone through changes in temperature and effort without having to drop or swap a layer. A handy chest pocket and slim, stylish profile round out the all-purpose design.

Ever heard a ski patroller complain about cold fingers? Of course not—they know they need gloves that work for both warmth and dexterity, which is why Helly Hansen teamed up with top ski patrol teams to design a glove that does the job all day, even in horrendous conditions. Fully waterproof, breathable, and insulated with Primaloft Black Eco, the Odin glove has reinforced overlays in high-wear areas like the palm and forefingers to increase grip and durability. Fleece lining ensures an oh-so-soft fit, and included carabiner and finger loops keep these gloves out of the snow and on your belt when off.

Founded in Norway in 1877, Helly Hansen has been making professional grade gear to help people stay and feel alive in the harshest environments around the world. Helly Hansen is a leader in performance ski apparel and technical sailing, as well as premium outdoor gear.
