Fragile lighting collection by Marset among 15 new products on Dezeen Showroom

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

A lighting range comprising repeatable spherical and pyramidal forms made from delicate glass is among recent products published on our furniture directory.

Fragile lighting collection by Jaume Ramírez for Marset

A simple table lamp, created by designer Jaume Ramírez for Marset, comprising a round base and pointed shade, has been spun out into a larger lighting range using the same visual language.

Fragile is now a full collection spanning table, wall and pendant lights of varying scales, providing elegant lighting solutions for spaces of all sizes.

Other products featured on Dezeen Showroom recently include playful task chairs with pop-coloured bases and a modular furnishing system designed to zone open-plan offices.

Read on to find out about some of our latest products:

Flora armchair by Andreas Engesvik for Fogia

This dining chair is the result of a three-way collaboration between Swedish brand Fogia, designer Andreas Engesvik and Michelin three-star chef Esben Holmboe Bang, who works out of Oslo restaurant Maaemo.

Flora‘s cantilevered arms, padded backrest, thickly upholstered seat and slender frame aim to make users as comfortable as possible, providing the stamina for hours of seated conversation and dining.

Dining, side and coffee tables comprise this collection by Philippine design brand Reforma and designers Eri and Mikka Mangaran.

Arcos aims to soften the sharp lines and corners commonly found in table design, an effect furthered by solid oak or walnut materiality, which has a natural finish.

Japanese office brand Kokuyo has created an ergonomic chair that features a pioneering integrated self-adjusting mechanism as well as a cocooning backrest made from taut mesh.

Named ingCloud, the chair was designed to facilitate easy movement rather than perfect, rigid posture across the course of a working day.

Lighting brand Neoz has released a compact, statuesque table lamp that’s cordless design facilitates uncluttered tablescapes.

Cooee 1c comprises a dome-shaped shade and all-metal body, which casts atmospheric pools of light on dining tables.

Kili tables by Mario Ferrarini for Lapalma

Designer Mario Ferrarini has released a collection of tables that are informed by the movement of turning.

Kili tables were made for Italian brand Lapalma and come in various finishes and colourway options.

Estonian brand Silen has released a new line of office booths, creating controlled environments for one-to-one meetings as well as calls and focused solo work.

Space Gen 2 office pods boast various performance- and environmental-related credentials, including a frame made from 50 per cent recycled aluminium and a “floating roof” for enhanced airflow capabilities.

HÅG Minu by Andreas Engesvik and Jannicke Grung for Flokk

Designers Andreas Engesvik and Jannicke Grung of eponymous studio Grung Engesvik have created this compact and casual task chair for Flokk.

HÅG Minu comes in six base colour options and a variety of upholstery materials, and was created to be straightforward to disassemble and repair as needed in order to extend its lifespan.

HUB modular system by Francesco Rota for Lapalma

Designer Francesco Rota and Italian furniture brand Lapalma have worked together to release this modular seating system that has a fully upholstered form supported by a slimline frame.

The HUB modular system is made up of various module types that come together to form flowing, curvilinear seating solutions that snake through large-scale interiors.

British furniture brand Boss Design has created a series of office pods named Mews that span small, medium and large scales and can host solo individuals up to large groups.

The pods can be used in clusters to facilitate meetings, focused work or calls in one location, or can be dotted around to spread functionality through spaces.

The Dolce and Vita tile range by Spanish brand Roca is cement-informed and flecked with chip-like inclusions, respectively.

Both styles come in either cool-toned grey or warm-toned beige variations, making them suitable for a range of interior schemes.

German brand Visplay has developed a grid-structured furniture system named Spatio Office that provides structure and zoning in offices that are open-plan or in the process of evolving.

The structures are formed of metal frames fitted with a choice of solid walls, mesh and curtains, and have the capacity to be accessorised with additional planters, storage and pinboards for added functionality and personalisation.

Victoria Aged Brass cordless table lamp by Neoz

A compact, burnished brass body and a choice of fabric shades characterise this small but distinguished table lamp by lighting brand Neoz.

The Victoria Aged Brass cordless table lamp is intended for use on tables in hospitality environments, where its cordless nature prevents excess tabletop clutter.

Turkish brand Parla worked with design studio Goodrich on this fully upholstered armchair bisected at each corner with wedge-like slivers of timber.

The Hew armchair comes in various upholstery options and colourways for the legs, creating further customisation options for an already distinctive seat.

Visplay’s Qubo Office system aims to make use of blank walls or create new partitions that can be accessorised with functional add-ons.

Open metal shelving has a minimalist appeal when left bare, or can become a bank of planters, storage units and lockers.
