This Vizio Soundbar Comes With Wireless Surround Speakers, and It's Nearly 50% Off for Early Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Walmart’s early Black Friday sale just kicked off, offering no shortage of worthy tech deals, and one home audio deal in particular has our attention.  If you’re looking to level up your home theater audio setup for less, the Vizio 4.1 Pro SV410YW-10 Soundbar and wireless surround speakers setup with Dolby Atmos and DTS:X support is on sale for $79 (originally $148), a nearly 50% discount.

The two pre-paired wireless surround speakers can be positioned behind or beside your seating area, while two bass drivers built into the 32-inch soundbar mean you don’t need a separate subwoofer.  The system maxes out at 95 dB, with bass extending to 50 Hz.

The SV410YW-1 connects to your television through HDMI eARC and supports CEC, so you can adjust the volume with your TV remote. While its integrated subwoofers won’t give you the same impressive low end as a dedicated unit, this system still delivers robust, bassy sound that goes far beyond built-in TV speakers. There’s an added perk for Vizio TV owners—it can be attached directly beneath the screen using the QuickFit feature.

Along with the deal are additional promos, like up to two free months of Apple Music and up to a free month of Apple TV. If your current TV sound setup is lacking some oomph and you want an under-$100 upgrade with a subwoofer and soundbar, the Vizio 4.1 Pro SV410YW-10 Soundbar and wireless surround speaker combo is a good choice for the money.
