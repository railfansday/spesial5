Endeavour Silver (EXK) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Silver output rose as Terronera's LNG switch cut costs per tonne.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Endeavour Silver Corp. (EXK -3.24%) reported growth in mine operating cash flow and metal production for the third quarter of 2026, supported by higher realized precious metal prices. Management adjusted its annual production and cost guidance to account for temporary operational suspensions at Terronera and capacity reductions at Guanacev. The company reported an improvement in its consolidated cost profile as the Terronera mine transitioned toward steady-state operations following the completion of its liquefied natural gas power plant. Financial flexibility was increased through the conversion of a project loan into a secured revolving credit facility as the company nears the completion of a feasibility study for the Pitarrilla project.

This revised outlook reflects our results to date and the expectations for the fourth quarter. While these decisions can impact near-term production grades, the longer-term benefit is the opportunity to recover more ounces from our existing mineral resources and potentially extending mine lives. Total revenue was $185 million, up 29% from Q3 last year, with realized average prices of $62 per ounce of silver and $4,209 per ounce of gold. Mine operating earnings reached $69 million compared with the $16 million a year ago, and mine operating cash flow before taxes was $95 million, an increase of 139%. Cash costs were $16.55 per payable ounce of silver, net of byproducts, a decrease of 9% compared to Q3 last year. All-in sustaining costs were $29.54, down 3% from a year ago as well.

The company reported $54 million of operating earnings compared to $2 million a year ago, and earnings before taxes were $40 million compared with a loss of $38 million in Q3 of last year. Looking at direct operating cost per tonne, we've made progress throughout the year. Consolidated costs declined from $187 in Q1 to $162 in Q2 and now stood at $152 in Q3. That was still 5% above Q3 last year, reflecting the different operating mix, with Terronera now included and while Bolañitos is no longer part of our operations.

At Kolpa, grades and recoveries remain consistent while higher throughput helped bring direct operating costs down to $138 in Q3 compared to $153 in the first half of 2026. The economies of scale are offsetting any pressures we have from a labor standpoint. At Terronera, as I mentioned earlier, direct operating costs continued to decline, reaching $156 here in Q3, down from $169 in Q2 and $195 in Q1. Contractor demobilization and workflow improvements helped bring costs down earlier in the year. And in Q3, we completed the LNG plant, allowing us to switch our power generation from diesel to LNG.

Beyond costs, we're encouraged by the steady improvements in Terronera's grades, recoveries and operating efficiencies over the past 3 quarters. We expect further improvements in the coming quarters with the opportunities to strengthen both operating performance and obviously, cash flow. Further drill results are expected in the fourth quarter, and we look forward to sharing those in the coming weeks. At Guanaceví, direct operating costs were $188 per tonne, reflecting lower throughput and the appreciation of the Mexican peso. Similarly, we'll continue to drill at Guanaceví, and we'll be sharing those results in the coming weeks.

Moving over to our balance sheet. We also converted our project loan into a $25 million secured revolving credit facility. It includes an accordion feature for up to an additional $75 million, bringing the maximum potential facility to $100 million. This adds flexibility to fund working capital, operations, permitted acquisitions and investments and the advancement of Pitarrilla. Pitarrilla is a major growth story for Endeavour Silver. Our management team visited the project last week, and the feasibility study is nearing completion. We expect to issue a news release imminently.

Together with the financial flexibility I just outlined, Pitarrilla is central to our long-term growth. We look forward to sharing that next update. To close, I think the third quarter really demonstrated the progress we've made as a company throughout 2026. While we certainly had some challenges along the way, we're seeing the benefits of the work we put into our operations. Terronera continues to improve.
