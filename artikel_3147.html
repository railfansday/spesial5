Monday.com: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Shifting upmarket trades near-term SMB churn for deeper enterprise ties, and Frontier's deploy engineers are the bet that makes it stick.

On its third-quarter fiscal year 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 9, 2026, monday.com (MNDY -9.10%) announced third-quarter revenue of $378 million, up 19% year over year. The enterprise software company is shifting its focus from small accounts to large enterprise clients while pairing artificial intelligence (AI) capabilities with human workflows. This corporate transition creates near-term sales friction but deepens long-term customer relationships. Three things management said stand out.

Management is intentionally directing sales resources toward larger corporate clients while reducing acquisition efforts for smaller buyers. Small and medium business (SMB) customer churn increased as a direct result of this strategy and recent workforce reductions.

The business is migrating clients to a hybrid pricing structure that combines standard seat subscriptions with usage-based credit packages. Annual recurring revenue (ARR), a metric measuring the annualized value of subscription contracts, reached $36 million from these AI capabilities.

Large corporate accounts require hands-on technical support to integrate software into existing operational workflows. Management created monday Frontier, a specialized service organization that embeds technical specialists into client operations to deploy tailored solutions.

Longer sales cycles and workforce cuts create operational friction, yet enterprise software adoption is deepening across large customer accounts. Proprietary software capabilities and dedicated deployment engineers provide structural momentum while management monitors pricing discipline and net dollar retention.

Scaling enterprise relationships and embedding artificial intelligence directly into daily operations remain central to driving long-term value.
