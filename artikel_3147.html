Microsoft says Copilot beats Claude and ChatGPT on price

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The cost claim rests on a study Microsoft paid for, and Anthropic's own figures look far lower.

Microsoft told its Ignite conference that businesses choose Copilot over Anthropic’s Claude and OpenAI’s ChatGPT because it is cheaper to run, citing a study it commissioned from GigaOm. Anthropic says non-coding Claude Enterprise users spend under $75 a month, well below the study’s $205 figure.

Microsoft says companies are choosing its Copilot AI assistant over Anthropic’s Claude and OpenAI’s ChatGPT because it costs less to run, Bloomberg reported. Judson Althoff, who runs Microsoft’s commercial business, made the pitch at the company’s Ignite conference on Tuesday. Microsoft has coached its salespeople to pitch Copilot against both rivals since July.

“Anything you can do in Claude or ChatGPT, you can do better with Copilot, and for less,” Althoff said.

The claim leans on a study Microsoft paid research firm GigaOm to run, which found users would spend much less on Copilot. That study cited $205 a month per user for basic work. Anthropic says its non-coding Claude Enterprise users spend under $75 a month on average.

AI bills have become a big worry for companies this year, with many capping staff AI use. Copilot offers a flat monthly fee for basic tasks, or pay-as-you-go pricing for heavier work. Microsoft itself cut back Claude Code licences for its own engineers in May over token costs.

Copilot had more than 30 million paid users in June, a small share of the 450 million business users of Microsoft’s office software. Its daily users grew fivefold in a year, Microsoft said last month. AT&T pays for tens of thousands of staff to use it, though it also uses OpenAI’s Codex.

The fight is getting personal. Last week Anthropic added Claude Sheets and other office tools aimed at Excel. Microsoft’s Jacob Andreou said at Ignite that rivals’ “simplified viewers and editors force compromise on power and collaboration.”
