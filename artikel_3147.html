Stock Market Today, Nov. 16: Nvidia Sinks as U.S. Tightens AI Chip Restrictions and Analysts Slash Price Targets

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Fresh U.S. restrictions on access to advanced AI chips are adding to Nvidia's mounting challenges, prompting Wall Street analysts to reassess the chipmaker's growth prospects ahead of earnings.

Nvidia (NVDA -4.12%) extended its recent sell-off on Monday, November 16, closing at $156.14. New U.S. restrictions on access to advanced artificial intelligence (AI) chips added to mounting concerns surrounding the company's leadership transition and long-term growth prospects. Several Wall Street analysts also lowered their price targets ahead of Tuesday's highly anticipated earnings report.

Trading volume surged to 326.6 million shares, approximately 119% above its three-month daily average of 148.9 million, reflecting heightened investor uncertainty. With regulatory and leadership risks mounting, Nvidia's upcoming earnings report will be particularly important in determining whether the company's growth outlook can restore investor confidence.

Wall Street suffered broad losses on Monday as investors reduced their exposure to technology and other growth stocks. The S&P 500 (^GSPC -1.92%) fell 1.92% to 7,416, while the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -2.49%) declined 2.49% to 24,510.

Selling pressure was particularly pronounced across the semiconductor industry amid the new restrictions. Advanced Micro Devices (AMD -5.58%) plunged 5.58% to $504.28, while Broadcom (AVGO -3.92%) dropped 3.92% to $333.40. The widespread losses suggest investors are reassessing the broader AI investment landscape, rather than reacting solely to Nvidia's company-specific challenges. Rising Treasury yields and tighter U.S. export restrictions are adding to concerns about semiconductor valuations and future revenue growth.

Nvidia's earnings report on Tuesday comes at a critical juncture for the chipmaker. Wall Street analysts have been lowering their price targets, reflecting growing concerns about leadership stability, regulatory restrictions, and the sustainability of AI infrastructure spending. The latest U.S. restrictions on remote access to advanced AI chips could further limit Nvidia's addressable market, potentially weighing on future revenue growth.

For Nvidia, strong quarterly results alone may not be enough to reverse the recent sell-off. Investors will be looking for evidence that the company can sustain its revenue growth and profit margins, maintain its technological leadership, and navigate an increasingly restrictive regulatory environment. Management's forward guidance will likely carry more weight than its backward-looking financial results in determining whether the stock can establish a sustainable recovery.
