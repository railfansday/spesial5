The Critic as Artist (And Vice Versa): A Fireside Chat with Jerry Saltz

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

During Art Week NYC, a citywide festival organized by ARTnews, Artforum, and Art in America, I had the great pleasure of doing a fireside chat with New York magazine senior art critic Jerry Saltz.

Like many of us, Jerry has been skeptical of AI, so I started the conversation by reading him a description of his approach to criticism produced by an AI that was based on a talk he gave a couple years ago. After chewing on that for a few minutes, we went on to speak about how he became a critic, how he became famous on social media, what he thinks about art being produced today, his strong feelings about auctions (he despises them), and what advice he would give creative people.

We also touched on the recent documentary made about Jerry and his wife, the former New York Times chief art critic Roberta Smith. And of course, it wouldn’t be a conversation with Jerry without plenty of engagement with the audience.

Despite its myriad challenges, Jerry still believes in New York City as a center for artists, and as a place with a vital community. You can watch the full talk, which took place on Saturday, November 14 at the PMC Studio in Midtown Manhattan, via the video here.
