Wolfenstein 3 Announced, Now in the Works at MachineGames

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

MachineGames has officially confirmed it's now working on Wolfenstein 3.

As part of the 25th anniversary celebrations for Xbox, the developer and Asha Sharma posted word on social media.

The Swedish studio went with: "His reputation precedes him. Wolfenstein 3 is in production." Sharma, meanwhile, said: "Wolfenstein 3 is officially in production at MachineGames. One of gaming's most important franchises, and a team that has done remarkable work bringing it forward. Looking forward to sharing more."

In the post below, you can see the opening lines of the script, with chapter one called "Nazitraz". There is also a picture taken from the motion capture studio on the MachineGames tweet:

The team returns to the FPS series, having made the two most recent mainline games: Wolfenstein: The New Order and Wolfenstein II: The New Colossus (let's just forget about Wolfenstein Youngblood). It took a break from the franchise with Indiana Jones and the Great Circle, but now it's back to kill more Nazis.

At this stage (and it's presumably an early one), there's no word on whether Wolfenstein 3 is a multi-platform release or an Xbox exclusive. It's probably still at least a few years away from releasing.
