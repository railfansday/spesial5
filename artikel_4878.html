When shopping for chinos or any other type of pants, the fabric choice is crucial, as it impacts comfort, durability, and overall appearance. Typically, chinos are made with a lightweight cotton blend to create a more comfortable wear for men. While many modern chinos incorporate stretch or performance fibers, traditional 100% cotton options are still widely available. Here’s a rundown of common fabrics and what they’re best for:

A classic choice for chinos due to its softness, breathability, and comfort. Cotton is ideal for casual wear and everyday use. It can be found in various weights and finishes, from lightweight and airy to heavier and more structured.

This is a type of weave rather than a fabric itself. Twill fabrics, often made from cotton, have a diagonal pattern that adds durability and a subtle texture.

Often used in blends, polyester adds durability and wrinkle resistance. It’s less breathable than cotton but can help chinos maintain their shape and resist stains. Polyester blends are ideal for a polished look with low maintenance.

Many chinos use fabric blends to combine the best properties of different materials. Common blends include cotton-polyester (for durability and wrinkle resistance) or cotton-spandex (for stretch and flexibility). Blends can provide a good balance of comfort, durability, and ease of care.

Fabrics with added spandex or elastane offer flexibility and comfort. These are great for chinos that need to accommodate a range of movements and provide a snug fit without losing their shape.

Chinos should fit comfortably around your waist and hips without being too tight or too loose. Look for options with a bit of stretch, as they often offer a better fit and more flexibility. When searching for the best chinos, you'll typically come across these main fits below.

A modern, tailored look that tapers slightly from the thigh down. The leg should taper slightly but not be so slim that it looks like skinny jeans. Great for guys who prefer a more streamlined silhouette without being too tight.

A classic, timeless cut that falls straight from the hips to the ankles. This fit works well for most body types and offers a more relaxed yet polished look.

Designed for guys with more muscular thighs, this fit offers extra room in the seat and thighs while still tapering toward the ankle. It gives a tailored look without feeling restrictive.

A roomier option with more space in the thigh and leg, ideal for casual wear or those who prioritize comfort over a tapered leg.

When it comes to styling chinos for men, it's essential to strike the perfect balance between looking casual and looking polished. For starters, the safest way to style chinos is to opt for versatile colors like classic khaki, navy, or olive. Then, ensure your chinos have the right fit—a tailored fit, preferably, that accentuates your silhouette and is not too loose or tight. Then, for a smart-casual vibe, pair your chinos with crisp button-down shirts or polo shirts for your office days. For something a bit more laidback, dress down your chinos with a well-fitted t-shirt or sweater for your casual outings. For layering, chinos will match well with blazers and shackets for colder seasons. As for footwear, canvas sneakers and loafers in general will give your chinos a more casual feel, while leather sneakers and dress shoes are perfect for dressing up chinos for a more put-together look. We also encourage wearing chinos with a leather belt.

If you're a fit guy looking for chinos that'll match your athletic build, we have some recommendations. Typically, guys who have larger legs will find that off-the-rack chinos are loose around the waist. Bodybuilders tend to run into this problem where they have to size up to get normal chinos to fit their quads, but since they have a slighter waist, it’s often loose. For maximum comfort, look for pants with more room in the quads and glutes while tapering up the waist for an athletic fit.

In short, chinos are a style of trousers, whereas khaki mainly refers to a light brown or tan color. Specifically, chinos are pants typically made of cotton twill fabric, which is durable, light, and breathable, and come in a variety of colors beyond the traditional khaki. Khakis often mean pants made in khaki colors and the term is now often used to describe casual cotton trousers often in a beige or tan color.

While khakis share similarities with chinos, the term is sometimes used more narrowly to refer specifically to beige or tan-colored casual pants, whereas chinos specifically refer to a pants style made in a certain fabric and design.

So all khakis can be chinos, but not all chinos are necessarily khakis since chinos can come in every color imaginable.

Modern chinos are often made with cotton blends that incorporate a little stretch for added flexibility and comfort. Performance-oriented versions may also use fabrics like polyester or nylon to add features such as moisture-wicking, breathability, or quick-drying properties.
