The recent US stock market decline has pushed several stock prices lower. However, some companies have continued to report higher revenue and profits despite the market fall. This has raised interest in stocks that have dropped sharply from their recent highs but continue to show business growth.Here is why each stock has come under pressure and what investors should watch.

Nvidia shares have fallen 26.6% from their October 6 high as investors pulled back from AI stocks following the US midterm elections. Concerns over new data centre rules and reports involving CEO Jensen Huang and a woman suspected of sharing information with China added to the pressure.

However, Nvidia’s business remains strong. Its revenue more than doubled year over year, while profit grew 126%. The company continues to supply AI chips to major technology companies, including Microsoft, Meta, Amazon and Google.

The stock is now trading at its lowest valuation based on past earnings since May 2019. Its November 17 earnings report will be an important event for investors.

Applied Digital builds and operates data centres for AI companies. Its shares have dropped around 74% from their May high of $50.72, recently reaching $13.11. The decline comes amid concerns about AI spending, data centre expansion and opposition to new data centre projects in the US.

However, the company has signed leases for approximately 1.4 gigawatts of capacity, while only 250 megawatts are currently operational. This means much of its planned capacity is yet to generate revenue.

Revenue has grown more than fourfold from a year ago. Management expects 300 megawatts of capacity to be operational in North Dakota by the end of 2026.

Investors will be watching whether the company can complete its projects on time and turn signed leases into revenue.

Microchip Technology makes chips used in cars, home appliances, industrial equipment, defence systems and data centres.

Its shares dropped 22.6% in two days after the company gave a weak outlook for the next quarter. Management said some sales recorded in September were one-time orders and would not repeat.

Despite the decline, the analysis points to a significant improvement in profitability. Daily profit reportedly increased from around $450,000 a year ago to $4.4 million.

Historical data also shows that the stock recovered within a year after eight previous declines of more than 40% from its record highs. However, it often fell further before recovering, so another decline remains possible.

Reddit shares fell 29% on October 30 after its latest earnings report raised concerns about user growth in the US.

Daily active users in the country declined from 53.2 million to 50.7 million. Google’s AI-powered search results are also creating concerns that fewer people will visit Reddit through search.

However, Reddit’s global daily active users increased 12% to 130.3 million. Revenue grew 53% to $895 million, while profit increased 74% to $283 million.

The company expects holiday-quarter revenue of $1 billion to $1.03 billion, compared with $726 million a year earlier.

Despite the recent fall, Reddit shares look attractive because of its revenue growth, profitability and relatively low debt.

SoFi offers banking, personal loans, credit cards, savings accounts and investment services through a single digital platform.

The company added a record 1.2 million members last quarter, taking its total membership to 17.1 million, up 35% from a year ago. Revenue increased 44% to $1.4 billion, while profit rose from $139 million to $241 million.

However, the stock has lost nearly half its value since reaching $32.73 in November 2025. Rising interest rates, pressure on banking stocks and concerns about its technology business have contributed to the decline.

This can be a possible buying opportunity because SoFi’s customer base and profits continue to grow despite the falling share price. A move above $17.74 could also indicate improving momentum.

All five stocks have fallen sharply, but their recovery will depend on different factors. Nvidia’s upcoming earnings, Applied Digital’s data centre expansion, Reddit’s user growth, SoFi’s profitability and Microchip’s next business outlook will be important developments.
