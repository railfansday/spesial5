Get the Capital One Venture X Business if your business will book at least $300 of travel a year through Capital One Business Travel. Its $300 credit and 10,000 anniversary miles return $400 toward travel each year, more than the $395 fee. Otherwise, take the $95 Venture Business, which earns the same 2 miles per dollar.

The Capital One Venture X Business is Capital One’s premium travel card for business owners. Like a charge card, it’s designed to be paid in full each month, so it suits an owner who travels often for work and doesn’t carry a balance.

Owners who book the business’s flights, hotels, and rental cars through Capital One Business Travel use the full credit, so the card pays for itself. Owners who book directly with airlines and hotels earn the same 2 miles per dollar on everyday purchases with the $95 Venture Business.
