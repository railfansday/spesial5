Van Gerwen sneaks into last 16 of Grand Slam and sets up Wade clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Michael van Gerwen edges final group stage clash with Jeffrey de Graaf; Luke Humphries, Nathan Aspinall and Gian van Veen also secure safe passage through to last 16; watch the Grand Slam of Darts in Wolverhampton live on Sky Sports, with the final on Sunday November 22

Michael van Gerwen squeaked into the last 16 of the Grand Slam of Darts to set up a clash with James Wade, who had a comfortable 5-0 win over Ricky Evans.

During Van Gerwen's 5-4 victory over Jeffrey de Graaf it became apparant that the Dutchman had done enough to qualify even if he didn't ultimately win, but his late wobble will still be a concern as he missed seven match darts.

In twice breaking De Graaf's throw - at one stage leading 3-1, and to go 4-3 up - Van Gerwen ensured he would top Group L even if he went on lose 5-4, as he held the tiebreaker in winning the most legs against the throw.

Ultimately, that tiebreaker wasn't neccesary, despite MVG's spurning of several match darts across a couple of legs before he finally landed his eighth.

The three-time Grand Slam champion has booked a meeting with three-time runner-up Wade in the next round, a rematch of the pair's meeting in the 2016 final, which the Dutchman won.

"It wasn't a good game at all," Van Gerwen told Sky Sports after his win. "I messed up! What can I say, I am through to the next round and onto the next one.

"James Wade is playing some fantastic darts. I need to bring my A-game if I am going to beat him, simple as that."

Wade enjoyed a much easier time of things in his winner-takes all shootout with 'Rapid' Ricky Evans in Group K, dispatching of his opponent with such speed as to make a mockery of his nickname.

'The Machine' has dropped only one leg over his two group stage wins, averaging over 100 in both, having beaten Cam Crabtree 5-1 in his opener.

Nathan Aspinall replicated Wade's route through to the last 16 as he too rounded off a couple of superb performances in the group stage with a 5-0 whitewash of Karel Sedlacek.

He also only dropped one leg in his opening match against Maik Kuivenhoven, in which he averaged 111.68, following that up with another ton-plus average against Sedlacek.

"I am sick of being the nearly man," Aspinall told Sky Sports. "People talk about how good I am but I don't win anything. I want that to change that next year. Hopefully this is the start of a good week."

The 11th seed will face Gabriel Clemens in the next round after his 5-3 victory over Rusty-Jake Rodriguez, who had a dart at double to claim a fourth leg in the match, which would have secured his progress at Clemens' expense.

German No 1 Martin Schindler joins his countryman in the last 16 after knocking out Chris Dobey with a 5-3 win in their Group N shootout.

He now meets No 3 seed Gian van Veen in the knockout stage after he got the better of Bradley Brooks 5-2 to round off Group M.

And the last 16 line-up is completed with a meeting of 2023 champion Luke Humphries and Tom Sykes after the former world No 1 beat Joe Cullen 5-2 to ensure his passage through. Sykes, meanwhile, started the night with a 5-1 victory over Krzysztof Ratajski in their winner-takes-all clash.
