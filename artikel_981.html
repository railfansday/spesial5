'My job combines my passion for gaming and football'

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Working on one of the world's biggest football video games is the perfect combination of my two biggest passions, gaming and football.

I'm CJ Ramson. I work on the creation of football video games. I work on Football Manager as an associate QA manager. QA stands for quality assurance.

We're the ones that test the game. We make sure everything works, and we work closely with the developers to try and make the most realistic simulation of football.

Growing up, I loved football, whether that was playing football or playing football video games with my friends. I'd always be doing one or the other. With the video games, the thing that really drew me to them was I've always been into the tactical side of the game, either being able to control the players or create your own tactics.

So a typical day for us, we'll get a new build in from the developers. We'll test it as a QA team, and we'll look for either bugsor ways that we can improve the game. That can be anything from the runs that players make, to decisions they make on the ball, what the team instructions and tactics do. So there's a lot of areas for us to cover. Our final goal is to make it as realistic to real football as possible.

So even though my background was in coaching, there was a lot of transferable skills that I learned and developed through my time coaching that I managed to transfer into this. So whether that's being really analytical and having a good eye for detail and knowingwhen to pick things out and what to look for, or it could be things like timekeeping and making sure things run perfectly on time, and working with other people as well. So social skills where you have to work within a team or even lead a team.

So as much as I loved playing football, I realised quite early on I probably wasn't going to be a professionalfootballer. But I wanted to work in football and find. So at college, I studied sports science, and that's where I found my love of coaching. I did my FA Level 1 and then went on to do my FA Level 2. After I left college, I got a job at Arsenal, which was unpaid, working with them, learning how to develop my coaching skills. Which then led to me having a full-time paid job at Fulham Football Club, working with the youth teams, working with the academy teams. And then that eventually led to me going to an event. I saw an opportunity where you could apply for a job in Football Manager. It was something that naturally I was interested in, had a conversation with them, and realised it was definitely something that I would love to do.

So I went to the networking event at Kick It Out, and that was an opportunity for me to network with other football coaches, other people within the gaming industries, and have a really good insight to different jobs in football gaming. Without doing that, I wouldn't have the opportunity to meet the people at Football Manager and see a new job in gaming, which I wouldn't have beenaware of.

One of the highlights of my career so far is working on the in-and-out-of-possession system on FM 26. This is something we see in real football all the time, so being part of the team that brought that to life and brought it into the game was super cool.

Working within the football industry is a dream come true. Being able to work on something that I'm passionate about, that is played and enjoyed by millions of people, is an amazing feeling for me and an amazing opportunity that I have. And yeah, I love it.
