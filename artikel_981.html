Erling Haaland clinches victory over RB Leipzig to return Manchester City to winning ways

RB Leipzig 2-3 Man City: Haaland scored his 14th goal of the season to win the tight contest and down RB Leipzig in Germany

Erling Haaland struck a late winner as Manchester City edged a thrilling Champions League clash with RB Leipzig 3-2.

City were twice pegged back by the enterprising Germans, who are managed by their former defender Martin Demichelis, but Haaland settled the contest 12 minutes from time.

It was his 14th goal of the season, his 13th in nine career appearances against Leipzig and his 62nd in 61 Champions League games.

City had taken the lead with an own goal from Castello Lukeba but the hosts quickly levelled through Ridle Baku.

Enzo Fernandez restored the lead but City needed Haaland’s late intervention to secure victory after the impressive Antonio Nusa struck a second equaliser.

Despite the win, it was not a night City goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma will care to remember.

Leipzig took the game to City early on and Nusa troubled the Italian with a shot from the edge of the box which he fumbled over the crossbar.

Nusa was then involved in a break down the right which led to an opportunity for the inrushing David Raum but his effort was blocked.

Christoph Baumgartner then went close with a curling effort from distance and Brajan Gruda also fired wide from a dangerous position.

City began to settle and Phil Foden curled an effort narrowly wide but they benefited from a huge stroke of luck to take the lead in the 37th minute.

Nico O’Reilly raced on to a long ball from Rayan Cherki but his cutback appeared wasteful. Lukeba, however, did not realise he was under no pressure and ended up bundling the ball into his own net.

Leipzig regrouped at half-time and returned in determined fashion with Nusa and Baumgartner both forcing saves out of Donnarumma.

But the City goalkeeper was badly caught out when he strayed off his line as Baku broke down the left. He may have been anticipating a cross as Baku reached the byline and rifled in a shot at his near post.

Leipzig could have had another moments later as Nusa played in Ezechiel Banzuzi with a backheel but he missed the target.

City rallied and Haaland got his first chance of the game as a Cherki pass was deflected to him but Maarten Vandevoordt kept out the Norwegian’s snap-shot.

They regained the lead after Leipzig allowed Iliman Ndiaye too much space on the left and he whipped in a cross for Fernandez to head home.

Again City could not hold on to the advantage as Nusa drilled a low shot across goal to equalise from a clever pass by Christopher Nkunku.

But Haaland, despite a largely quiet night, was not to be denied and raced in at the back post to slide home Elliot Anderson’s superb low ball into the box.

Both sides hit the woodwork in the closing stages, with Donnarumma almost embarrassed again as Raum’s free-kick looped over him and struck the bar. Cherki also thumped against the bar at the other end.

The victory secured a vital three points for Enzo Maresca’s team and sent them up to seventh in the Champions League table inside the automatic qualifying spots for the knockout rounds.
