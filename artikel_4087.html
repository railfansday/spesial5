The Video Games You Should Play This Weekend – November 20, 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

This is it. We finally made it. Grand Theft Auto VI is here. I have played the game for a number of hours and read Matt Miller's glowing review, which you can read here, and I still don't know if I personally believe the game is actually out. But that just might the lack of sleep from staying up late to play Grand Theft Auto VI.

Many games chose to avoid the blast radius of Rockstar's latest crime caper, but that doesn't mean it's the only game that came out this week and you can find them below. But as is tradition, here are some of our top stories of the week first.

If you’re not aware that GTAVI launched this week, it’s possible that you’ve been living under a rock somewhere. Nonetheless, we’re here to offer the reminder that after more than a decade of waiting for a new installment, Rockstar has this week rolled out its new game. It’s good. Like, really good, and if you’re an adult who plays video games, it should really be a title you make time for.

In my review, I highlighted some of its many positive traits, but as you prepare to dive into its criminal delights over the weekend, one element I’d especially like to point out is its viability as a game to play with your partner. Like an excellent prestige TV crime show, GTAVI is a game that features a rich, dramatic, funny, and well-written story that I genuinely believe has the potential to be a good time for both a player and a viewer, though, admittedly, the person who gets to enjoy running the sticks is probably having the most fun.

However you plan to enjoy it, here’s hoping Grand Theft Auto VI turns out to be worth the wait you’ve likely had for it. For my part, it certainly has been, and I suspect you’ll find it only gets better the deeper into the game you go.

The running joke publisher Devolver Digital has made with this “spectacular remaster of the original remake of the unreleased action-platformer starring the most famous video game mascot of all time” is that it launched the same day as Grand Theft Auto VI. Volvy’s Adventure has been positioned as a tongue-in-cheek competitor to Rockstar’s behemoth, with Devovler even releasing a trailer that is a shot-for-shot parody of GTA VI’s. While I’ve chuckled at these stunts, Volvy’s Adventure isn’t just a joke game. It’s actually pretty fun.

For one, it’s developed by a respectable studio in Doinksoft, the team behind fun indie games like Gato Roboto, Gunbrella, and this year’s Dark Scrolls. Vovly’s Adventure is a 16-bit 2D platformer where players use Volvy’s oversized fist to punch through objects, which, in turn, propels him in the same direction. Thus, platforming is a fast-paced and explosive exercise in soaring fist-first through barriers and enemies, collecting items, and burrowing through certain platforms. The platforming feels pretty good, and it’s backed by a catchy soundtrack; the destruction is satisfying; and the levels are relatively brief, making it easy to plow through.

I wasn’t sure what to expect from Volvy’s Adventure beyond the marketing jokes, but it’s got some sauce. Sitting at the budget price of $5 (and a launch discount of $3.74), it’s a curiosity that’s easy to check out.

Australia Did It, from Rami Ismail and his team at Aesthetician Labs, hit a little snag with its publicized release date. Valve got backed up approving the release of games on its platform and a handful of games were affected. Australia Did It, despite the unexpected delay, is out... and it's good! Here's what Brian Shea wrote in his review: "Looking back on the hours I poured into Australia Did It, the forgettable train segments will occupy only a small part of my memories, just as they do in each run. Instead, what will stick with me for months to come are the creative combination mechanics, the outstanding turn-based defense system, and the thrill of uncovering the mysteries of this bleak, yet engaging world."

God of War Sons of Sparta is... underwhelming, to be blunt. You can read my review here. The game is a prequel where you play as a young Kratos and go on a 2D Metroid-inspired adventure with your brother, Deimos. If you are interested in getting the full God of War story, there are some interesting narrative and character details in the game, but it has some shortcomings. What's surprising about its re-release this week is that it is now available on PS4. An unexpected "new" platform to say the least.
