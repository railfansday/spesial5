Civilization Developer Firaxis Games Cuts A Third Of Its Staff, Studio Head Departs

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A new report from Bloomberg reveals that Firaxis Games, the developer behind Civilization and XCOM, has cut around one third of its staff. Meanwhile, its studio head Heather Hazen will also depart.

Approximately 60 people have been laid off as work on a new XCOM stalled over the Summer, with the game reportedly missing a huge milestone. Development will continue, but it will likely be met with a pretty drastic shake-up.

A spokesperson for 2K, a subsidiary of Take-Two, shared the following statement:

“Firaxis is restructuring and recentering around the studio’s core strength — designing world class strategy games that have set the industry standard for the genre for the last 30 years.

"As part of these changes, the size of the studio is being reduced to focus on design, technology and the player experience, which means saying goodbye to talented colleagues who helped build Firaxis.

"These decisions are never easy, but they’re essential to build the next chapter of Firaxis.”

Funny how they try to spin these statements to make it sound almost positive, eh?

Firaxis most recently released Civilization VII for the Switch and Switch 2, amongst other major platforms. Reception was fairly mixed, but it received a solid 8/10 from us upon its release on the Switch 1. Since then, it's received numerous updates, but it's arguably failed to achieve the same success as earlier entries.

It's particularly egregious that this should come just a couple of days before the release of GTA 6, another Take-Two title developed by Rockstar Games. It's set to be one of the biggest entertainment launches of all time, but it seems Take-Two felt the need to save even more money elsewhere.
