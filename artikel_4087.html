Doc’s Rainbow Six Siege Remaster Introduces Remote Healing And Revives

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Doc mains, you might want to start celebrating. Rainbow Six Siege is giving one of its most recognizable Defenders a pretty significant upgrade in Year 11 Season 4, and he'll no longer need to put himself in the middle of a firefight to save his teammates.

Revealed during the Osaka Major, Operation Vital Signal introduces Doc's new E-HEAL Monitor system, which lets him remotely heal and revive teammates from anywhere on the map. Rather than having to run over to an injured teammate and risk getting himself taken out in the process, Doc can select them on his Monitor and activate their pre-attached healing patches from a safe distance.

This also means Doc can provide support to teammates who are busy roaming around the map and getting themselves into trouble. Players can trigger patches that were previously attached to teammates, which makes it easier to keep aggressive Operators alive without having to abandon their own positions.

And if Doc finds himself in a bit of trouble, he's not completely out of luck. The remaster also lets him use his own Patches to heal himself and recover when he's Down But Not Out (DBNO). It's a pretty handy addition for an Operator whose entire job revolves around keeping everyone else alive.

Doc isn't the only thing getting attention in Operation Vital Signal, either. The update also introduces the MBZ-19, a new primary weapon with a 64-round magazine, 33 damage per shot, and a 750 RPM fire rate. Jager, Warden, and Thunderbird will have access to the weapon. Other changes include separate Ranked Solo/Duo and Flex playlists, a targeted Bank map update, and modernized versions of Yacht, Favela, and Tower.

Personally, I love playing Doc and have always found him to be a pretty useful Operator. I'm excited about this remaster because there's now less room for human error when it comes to healing teammates. You don't have to worry about missing a Stim Pistol shot or being in the same room as someone who needs healing. Being able to support your team from across the map is a huge improvement, and I think Doc is about to become an even more useful pick.

Operation Vital Signal is the final seasonal update of Year 11, and arrives December 1, 2026.
