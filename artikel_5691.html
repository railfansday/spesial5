Surgeon's Butt-Smacking Charge; Doc Killed in Road Rage Shooting; Nurse Impostor?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

— A weekly roundup of healthcare's encounters with the courts

South Carolina orthopedic surgeon Charles Reitman, MD, was arrested after allegedly smacking a co-worker on the buttocks. (Post and Courier)

Virginia radiologist Adam Gray, MD, was shot and killed in an apparent road rage incident. The man charged with his murder said he shot Gray twice after the doctor banged on his truck's passenger-side window. (WTVR)

A nurse in Maryland is speaking out after being assaulted at work twice. (WMAR)

The British doctor whose evidence helped convict former nurse Lucy Letby in the murder of seven babies had been barred from treating patients during the trial over concerns that he posed a safety risk -- and jurors were never told. (The Guardian)

A driver in Georgia is facing a DUI charge after allegedly hitting a pregnant doctor who was crossing the street at a crosswalk near a hospital. (WSBTV)

Oklahoma internist Nitin Sawheny, MD, and a nurse were arrested after two kids were found abused by their mother; officials say the mother was prescribed drugs illegally, which led to the children getting seriously hurt. (KOCO)

A Wisconsin woman accused of impersonating a registered nurse was promoted twice before she was caught, according to authorities. (WEAU)

A nurse in Texas was sentenced to 20 years in prison for a fatal head-on crash that killed another nurse who was heading to work. (KSAT)

David Parks, MD, of Missouri, and his spouse admitted to illegally prescribing pain pills and other controlled substances, federal prosecutors said.

New York family medicine physician Edgard Salazar, MD, was charged in a $6.8 million Medicare fraud scheme involving unnecessary orthotic braces prescribed via telemedicine, the Justice Department announced.
