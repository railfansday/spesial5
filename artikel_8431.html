10 Years in the Making: My Path to the AT

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Those are great questions, and sometimes I don’t have an answer to either one. If you are thinking of doing the AT, you probably feel, or have felt, similarly. But I will tell you a few things about me and how I got interested in the Appalachian Trail, and we’ll see how things go from there!

Well, I am a 32-year-old trans person and free spirit. I am figuring me out at the moment, so just stick with me for a little bit, please :). In the “system,” I am a mechanical engineer designing building HVAC and plumbing systems. I grew up in rural NC. I am the youngest child in the typical “nuclear family.” However, if you know what it’s like being the queer in a family of straights, I didn’t exactly fit in. I am “the black sheep” of my family.

From age 8 (maybe younger, but I have definitive memories at age 8), I have had some level of depression. At age 12, I found alcohol, and it filled the hole in my heart left by depression. I drowned out most of my life being an alcoholic until age 30. Woot woot to my 3 years of sobriety and living happily!

Well, that’s the thing: I am not really sure. You see, being an alcoholic from 12 to 30 really messed with my brain, and things in life feel weird. Which really leads me to…

Well, I hope to grieve my past and the parents I needed but never had. I want to figure out who I really am, what I am capable of doing, and where I want my life to go in the future. I also want to shift my life to something simpler. Less screen time, more nature. More community, less loneliness. Less consumption and more repurpose.

I first learned about the AT sometime in high school, but hiking wasn’t “cool” in the party crowd; so it wasn’t my thing. Later, in college, I got recommended “Dixie” on YouTube when she started her AT thru-hike. I was immediately drawn to the idea. However, I never had the three things you need to hike the AT (maybe a fourth): money, fitness, time, and support at home for shipping things and safety purposes. Well, things in my life have lined up in an odd way this year such that I have enough money, I have a support system, and I now have the fitness. Unfortunately, like most thru-hikers, I needed to quit my job to get the time to go on this adventure I have been dreaming of for 10 years or more.

Looking forward to being hiker trash and sharing many campfires with y’all for 5-6 months!
