Wales fall short in tight defeat to Australia

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Tries: Lake (41,50), Murray (55), Elias (79) Conversions: Costelow (42,51,56)

Tries: Tupou (21), Paenga-Amosa (33), Jorgensen (47), Ikitau (64) Conversions: Lonergan (34,48), Gordon (65) Penalties: Gordon (74)

Wales vs Australia Nations Championship 8:10pm Saturday 21st November

Wales failed to back up their epic win over New Zealand as Australia edged their Nations Championship match 29-26 in Cardiff.

A first victory over the All Blacks since 1953 had led to week-long celebrations in Wales.

But Australia, having soaked up fierce first-half pressure, produced some fine attacking rugby after the break to prevent Wales from winning three on the spin for the first time since the 2023 World Cup.

Taniela Tupou, Brandon Paenga-Amosa, Max Jorgensen and Len Ikitau secured a try-bonus point for Australia as Ryan Lonergan and Carter Gordon added nine points with the boot.

Wales also scored four tries through Dewi Lake's double, Blair Murray and Ryan Elias, with Sam Costelow landing three conversions, but fell agonisingly short.

Australia fly-half Carter Gordon scored ⁠a penalty in the 74th minute to give the Wallabies a 29-21 lead, which proved enough for victory despite Wales securing a fourth try ⁠through replacement hooker Elias two minutes ​from time.

Wales battled for the winning score in a dramatic close to the match but their attempt to go the length of the field was ended when the ball was ‌knocked on.

"Wales are a tough outfit. What a match," Ikitau said after he was awarded player of the match.

"It's been a tough couple of weeks for us. I know the heart's always there but when you don't get the results it's pretty disheartening. I'm proud of the boys and proud of the effort today."
