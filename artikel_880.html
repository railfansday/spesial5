Val Chmerkovskiy's Son Rome, 3, Shares Advice After DWTS Elimination

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

After Dancing With the Stars' Val Chmerkovskiy and Jenna Dewan were eliminated during the Nov. 10 episode, his and wife Jenna Johnson's son Rome, 3, shared some sweet advice.

Val Chmerkovskiy's biggest fan isn't sidestepping this elimination.

After the season 35 Dancing With the Stars pro and partner Jenna Dewan were knocked out during the Nov. 10 episode, his and wife Jenna Johnson's son Rome, 3, shared a much-needed reminder.

"It's OK to lose, right?" Chmerkovskiy asked the toddler in his Nov. 11 Instagram video. Rome replied, "Yeah, you try your best. If you don't win sometimes, you try to do it so many times."

The dancer—who was eliminated on Grammy Night ahead of the semi-finals despite receiving the second-highest scores from the judges—noted that there will always be ups and downs on the dance floor.

"You gotta learn how to do both," Chmerkovskiy explained. "You gotta learn how to win. You gotta learn how to lose, and it's OK to be sad when you lose for a little bit."

And it helps to have Rome cheering him on. As the 40-year-old put it, "Even though I lost and I'm sad, I feel already better because I have you in my life."

Meanwhile, the toddler emphasized just how many fans Chmerkovskiy—who also faced a one-week suspension earlier in the season after comments made about judge Carrie Ann Inaba—and Dewan had accumulated before their week nine elimination.

"People want you to win because you're a great dancer," Rome said. "You win or you lose, you try again on a different day. If you wanna win, you need to move your body and do it so fast and then you win. You get [to be] the champion."

The dancer captioned the heartwarming interaction alongside a heart emoji and a smiley face, "hard hitting advice from our 3yo."

His comment section was full of love, with guest judge Shirley Ballas writing, "Sending hugs. Great job." Johnson—still in the running alongside partner Harry Shum Jr.—added, "My loves."

While Chmerkovskiy and Dewan won't be holding on to the Mirrorball Trophy this season, the pro had a sweet message for his dance partner after their elimination.

"You've been exactly what I imagined," he captioned his Nov. 11 Instagram post. "The absolute best! Thank you for the opportunity to be by your side through this entire process. I'll never forget it."
