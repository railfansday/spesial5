Buy Virgin Atlantic Flying Club Points With 80% Bonus (1.1 Cents Each): Worth It?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Virgin Atlantic Flying Club is currently offering a best-ever promotion on purchased points. Through December 31, 2026, Flying Club members can purchase points with up to an 80% bonus, which is potentially an opportunity to buy points for under 1.1 cents each. Suffice it to say that this could be a very attractive deal.

Note that the purchase of Virgin Atlantic points is non-refundable, and purchasing points doesn’t count toward Flying Club elite status. Points will be credited to accounts within 24 hours, though they typically post faster than that, and almost instantly.

It’s interesting how much Virgin Atlantic penalizes those in the United States when it comes to buying points. Then again, when it comes to overall points earning opportunities, we can’t really complain in the United States, given the amazing credit card offers we have.

Virgin Atlantic Flying Club ordinarily lets members purchase up to 200,000 points per account per calendar year, before any bonuses. However, during this promotion that cap has been doubled, to 400,000 points.

When Virgin Atlantic Flying Club offers a promotion on purchased points, the best version of the offer is almost always for a 70% bonus. As you can tell, this offer is even better than those, as this is truly a best-ever offer. Personally, I’d recommend buying points based on when you have a short term use in mind, rather than based on the slight difference in the cost per point.

Flying Club points purchases are processed by points.com, meaning they don’t count as an airfare purchase for the purposes of credit card spending. Therefore I’d recommend using a card on which you’re trying to reach minimum spending, or otherwise, a credit card that maximizes your return on everyday spending.

Virgin Atlantic is definitely a frequent flyer program with more niche redemption opportunities. In addition to being able to redeem Flying Club points for travel on Virgin Atlantic, there’s also good value to be had for redemptions on Air New Zealand, All Nippon Airways, and Delta.

Nowadays Virgin Atlantic has dynamic award pricing for its own flights, and for those with flexibility who want the best deal, this can make for some great deals. For example, there are some transatlantic one-way business class awards that cost under 30,000 points, which is amazing.

Beyond that, Virgin Atlantic has several airline partners, some of which are more lucrative than others. Probably the best use of Flying Club points is for travel in ANA first & business class. You can find the redemption rates below.

Paying 52,500-60,000 Flying Club points for a one-way business class ticket between the United States and Japan is a phenomenal value to start with.

All Nippon Airways’ new first & business class are amazing products as well. The major catch is that availability can be really tough to come by.

Everyone will value points currencies differently. Based on my latest miles & points valuations, I consider Flying Club points to be worth 1.1 cents each. However, I tend to value points pretty conservatively, and there are many ways to get way more value from Flying Club points than that.

Virgin Atlantic Flying Club points don’t expire. There’s no requirement to even have any account activity, as the points will never expire regardless.

While buying Virgin Atlantic points could represent a great deal, keep in mind that there are lots of other ways to earn Flying Club points. Most significantly, Virgin Atlantic is partners with major transferable points currencies, including American Express Membership Rewards, Bilt Rewards, Capital One, Chase Ultimate Rewards, and Citi ThankYou, and in all cases, points can be moved at a 1:1 ratio. There are several ways to earn these points aside from outright buying them.
