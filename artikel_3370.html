Taylor Sheridan’s Hit Western Series Officially Dominates Streaming Worldwide

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

From Mayor of Kingstown and The Madison to Lioness and Landman, Taylor Sheridan's impact on modern television through his pantheon of shows cannot be overstated. Currently, the fourth season of Tulsa King is the latest to dominate the streaming charts, as viewers remain gripped by Sylvester Stallone's Dwight "The General" Manfredi and his fight "to legitimize his empire while facing corrupt politicians and new enemies."

As Season 4 continues weekly, Season 5 of Tulsa King remains in production in New York. Erik Palladino, Glenn Fleshler, Anthony Skordi, and Nickola Shreli have already been added to regular series roles, with it confirmed that Girls and Ironheart star Manny Montana has also joined the growing ensemble as "Gilberto Torres, a gangster with a strong presence, though he doesn’t have to advertise it." The good news continues for Sheridan fans, as the most important series in his catalog has returned to the global streaming stage.

At the time of writing, Yellowstone is one of the ten most-watched shows on Paramount+ in the world, a list featuring the aforementioned Tulsa King in second place. Sheridan's flagship series ran for six years, five seasons, and 53 episodes, rarely missing a beat as it helped pioneer a Western renaissance on the small screen. The show's impact cannot be overvalued, as the current landscape of 21st-century streaming simply wouldn't have been the same without its major pop culture impact.
