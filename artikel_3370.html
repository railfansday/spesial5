The entire Hisense U8 lineup is currently discounted ahead of Black Friday, with prices starting at $797.96 for the 55-inch model (down from $1,424.99) and going up to $2,798.99 for the massive 100-inch version (originally $3,999.99). The discounts also extend to the 65-, 75-, and 85-inch models, with savings of up to $1,200 across the lineup. Regardless of which size you choose, Amazon throws in free delivery to the room of your choice, a service that normally costs $19.99.

Brightness is the U8QG’s main advantage, with the manufacturer estimating up to 5,000 nits of peak brightness, while PCMag measured around 3,200 nits when reviewing the 65-inch model. Actual performance will vary with the picture mode and content, but either figure points to strong highlights in Dolby Vision and HDR10 video. Its Mini-LED local dimming also produces deep blacks, although some light bloom can appear around bright objects on dark backgrounds. Audio-wise, its built-in 4.1.2-channel system, which includes surround channels, a subwoofer, and upward-firing speakers, gives movies more scale than typical TV speakers, though a separate sound system can still offer clearer separation and a broader soundstage.

The U8QG also has plenty to offer gamers using a current-generation console or a capable gaming PC. It supports refresh rates up to 165Hz, variable refresh rate, and AMD FreeSync Premium Pro, with a measured 4.3 milliseconds of input lag at 1080p and 120Hz and 10.1 milliseconds at 4K and 60Hz. Those results should make controls feel responsive, even during fast-paced games. There’s also a side-mounted USB-C port that accepts DisplayPort video, while Google TV handles streaming alongside Apple AirPlay and Google Cast. The biggest inconvenience is the limited selection of three HDMI ports, since a console, soundbar, and streaming device would occupy all of them.
