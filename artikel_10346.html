Motorola Signature 27 delivery dates slip ahead of launch as it expands to more regions

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Motorola and Best Buy websites are now showing an early December shipping date, despite at least one customer already receiving tracking info.

Tons of excitement surrounds Motorola’s upcoming release of the Signature 27, and now retail websites are showing that new preorders won’t be arriving until early December. The original announcement in early November proclaimed a November 19 release date, but Motorola tells 9to5Google:

We’re excited by the strong consumer response of the new motorola signature 27, with demand exceeding our initial expectations. As a result, some orders may experience updated shipping timelines. Our teams are working to meet demand and get devices to customers as quickly as possible.

Motorola and Best Buy websites are still showing “Pre-order” buttons, so while you can order one right now, December 8 and December 11 are the current ship dates. Motorola also just announced that the Motorola Signature 27 will be officially available in India starting December 9 (via GSMArena), further complicating the supply chain.

Despite this, one Redditor who preordered says they received a tracking number for the Signature 27 and the free Motorola Watch Ultra preorder, and it’s scheduled to arrive on November 25. The Motorola Signature 27 has been available in Europe since November 16, so it’s clear demand is quite high for Motorola’s biggest premium launch in a long time.

The Motorola Signature 27 is the most premium phone Motorola has possibly ever launched in North America. The company has shied away from releasing flagship phones here for years, although Motorola’s Razr line of foldable phones often blends premium specs with more mid-range ones. The excitement for the Signature 27 is completely understandable in this case, especially now that Motorola is offering the full seven-year software update treatment that Samsung and Google do.
