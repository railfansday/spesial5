JJ Gabriel catches Man United by surprise after confirming exit: Star, 16, posts farewell video as he finalises move to Real Madrid after stunning Old Trafford chiefs with quit bombshell

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Published: 13:58 EST, 17 November 2026 | Updated: 15:07 EST, 17 November 2026

Sixteen-year-old JJ Gabriel has announced his departure from Manchester United, two months on from stunning club officials with his desire to leave.

Gabriel and his representatives made a bombshell request to have his academy contract torn up in September. That request was immediately refused by United and, while there was faint hope that a compromise could have been found to keep him at Old Trafford, he has stayed away ever since.

He has been training in Madrid in recent days ahead of a move to Real Madrid, and has taken to social media to bid farewell to the Red Devils after six years at the club.

Gabriel’s announcement is thought to have caught United by surprise, with the club first learning about it when the video appeared on social media. It’s understood that United haven’t heard from the player’s family since he was last seen at Carrington in September.

Despite his clear intention to sign for Madrid, United’s stance hasn’t changed and they still believe it will require FIFA’s approval for any move to go through.

'Dear Manchester United,' he began. 'I arrived in 2020 as a 10-year-old boy with big dreams.

JJ Gabriel has confirmed his Manchester United exit online ahead of a move to Real Madrid

The 16-year-old penned a speech to the club, which he read out in a post (Instagram/jjnr10)

'Here I grew as a player and as a person. From scoring at Old Trafford to receiving the Jimmy Murphy Award. You gave me moments I’ll always treasure.

'To every coach, member of staff, team-mate, thank you for believing in me and helping me grow. As I begin my next chapter, Manchester United will always be a part of my story. Thank you for everything. JJ.'

Daily Mail Sport exclusively revealed Madrid's growing confidence in securing the free-scoring starlet back in September. Gabriel has now secured a European Union passport through his mother's Cypriot heritage, and sources familiar with the situation believe a switch is imminent.

It is understood Gabriel will play for Madrid's Castilla team, which plays in Spain's third division, as well as in the Youth League.

Gabriel broke a series of records playing for United youth teams, including becoming the youngest player to feature in the Under-18s. Gabriel was 14 and scored twice on his debut.

He has also played – and scored – for the Under-21 side.

Gabriel, who turned 16 last month, is one of the brightest young talents in the world and his exit represents a huge loss to United.

As Daily Mail Sport previously reported, United's decline since the days of Sir Alex Ferguson has been the driving force behind Gabriel's family seeking an exit. United have not won a Premier League title since 2013.

United's decline since the days of Sir Alex Ferguson led to Gabriel's family seeking an exit

A source close to the family explained that United are 'far away' from the likes of Barcelona, Real Madrid, Bayern Munich, as well as domestic rivals Arsenal and Manchester City, and that they want him to 'learn from the best' to maximise his talents.

Gabriel's real name is Joseph Junior Andreou O'Cearuill. His father, Joe O'Cearuill, changed the family name to make it more commercially attractive.

O'Cearuill senior has been described as a fiercely ambitious, pushy parent. He is credited with the decision to get JJ out of United in an attempt to fast-track his career.

The family had previously flirted with other major European clubs, including Barcelona. Gabriel even went on a tour of the Spanish club's ground – and was photographed with superstar forward Lamine Yamal.

Former United head coach Michael Carrick said of Gabriel: 'He's a young boy, he's 15 and the biggest (thing) for me definitely and as a club point of view is looking after our younger players – their welfare, their wellbeing, their exposure.

'And I think all of us we've got to be really conscious of that, really, in this situation.

'As a club, for sure, we're all involved in those conversations. I just think we've got to be very careful of that.'
