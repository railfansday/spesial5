Calafiori honoured in Rome for his achievements at Arsenal and beyond

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Rome Mayor Roberto Gualtieri honoured Italy defender Riccardo Calafiori today for his title victory with Arsenal and for being an ‘ambassador for our city around the world.’

Italy international Calafiori received a special plaque in his hometown of Rome today, not just for his Premier League title win with Arsenal in the 2025-26 season.

“It’s a great pleasure to welcome and greet Riccardo Calafiori,” Rome Mayor Gualtieri said (via Gazzetta) during the award ceremony

“He is an extraordinary sportsman whose achievements make us proud. He is an ambassador for our city around the world, standing out not only for winning the Premier League but also for his style and elegance. He is a wonderful person. That makes us proud: he flies the flag for Rome in the United Kingdom and around the world.”

Calafiori held a press conference after receiving the award, saying that this is the best moment of his career and confirming he’d like to return to Roma one day, in a “more distant future.”

Calafiori was born in Rome and came up through the Roma academy.

The ex-Bologna defender made his senior Roma debut in 2020 under Paulo Fonseca.

He made ten senior appearances with the Giallorossi before joining Genoa on loan in January 2022, then Basel on a permanent deal the following summer.

After two seasons with the Swiss club, he returned to Serie A, signing for Bologna, where he spent one season and helped the Rossoblu qualify for the Champions League.

In the summer of 2024, after featuring for Italy at the Euros, he joined Arsenal for circa €50m.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
