Miami Dolphins’ Most Valuable Player | Week Ten | Youth

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Miami Dolphins have been going through a bit of a youth-movement this season, in case you haven't heard. They're the youngest team in the National Football League by a wide margin — with an average age of just over 25 years at the start of the season. They've also had the most snaps taken by rookie players out of any NFL team this season — leading the Cleveland Browns by a comfortable amount.

That emphasis on developing young players is paying off for the Dolphins in a major way. While the team is currently struggling in the standings — winning just one game on the year so far — their rookies are taking their lumps and learning the ropes at an expedited pace. That should help the team progress much quicker than most anticipate, while keeping their future cap situation in the good (and cheap) place.

One young player who has been excelling in Jeff Halfey's defensive scheme is rookie first-rounder Chris Johnson. Miami's young cornerback has two interceptions on the season after nabbing one against Daniel Jones of the Colts on Sunday. He returned that interception 59-yards for a touchdown — Miami's first pick-6 on the year. In addition to his two interceptions, Johnson also has a forced fumble during his rookie campaign.

Following the game, Johnson spoke about how coaching helped him make his stellar play on Sunday.

"Definitely felt good. Really just reading my keys. Once the quarterback scrambles, he's looking to get the ball out of his hands. That's kind of something we talked about throughout the week. When we get him off-schedule, it's an opportunity. Really just making the most of my opportunity in that situation."
