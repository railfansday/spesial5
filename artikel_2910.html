With the Bills (6-1) sitting in first place in the AFC East and in sole possession of first place in the AFC, today's edition of Buffalo Rumblinks leads off by examining the latest news and trade rumors swirling around Buffalo in the days leading up to the trade deadline.

Check out the news items below and then let us know your thoughts on who Brandon Beane should acquire before the trade deadline in the comments section.

The NFL's trade deadline is at 4 p.m. ET on Tuesday, and while Buffalo's defense is coming off a dominant performance in a win against the Baltimore Ravens second- and third-string quarterbacks, Brandon Beane would be wise to kick the tires on potential solutions to shore up his beleaguered defense.

The problem is, as of now, the Bills only have $4.5 million in available cap space, according to Spotrac, a figure that ranks 27th in the NFL. That just isn't enough cap space to bring in anyone with a base salary, which counts on this year's cap. Sure, the Bills have a few moves they could make to create some cap space, but Beane is limited in how aggressive he can be at the trade deadline.

We discuss possible additions Beane could bring in to improve Buffalo's defense, including linebackers Zack Baun (pictured below), Jordyn Brooks, Daiyon Henley, Cody Barton, and Akeem Davis-Gaither, safeties Budda Baker, Amani Hooker, Elijah Molden, Derwin James, and Cam Bynum, defensive end Khalil Mack, and edge rushers K'Lavon Chaisson and Josh Sweat.

We hear from Beane on how he's being as aggressive as ever heading into trade deadline and more than likely targeting defense; and explain why safety, edge rusher, inside linebacker, boundary cornerback, and interior defensive line are the biggest priorities to address via a trade.
