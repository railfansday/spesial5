NBA Trade Rumors: Is a potential trade target emerging for Utah?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

According to Zach Lowe, teams are calling the Chicago Bulls to trade for Tre Jones. You can listen to the podcast here.

Lowe says Tre Jones is the best backup guard in the NBA, which is exactly what the Utah Jazz need. Considering he is making $8M a year, he would fit into Utah's TPE from the Walker Kessler deal. In that scenario, Utah wouldn't even need to send a player. Although they could send Isaiah Collier in a potential deal, he would be a high-upside play for the Bulls.

Jones is averaging 10.9 points, 3.4 assists, 2.1 rebounds, and 1.1 steals this season. His advanced numbers are pretty solid, and if you put him on the Jazz with the talent surrounding him, he could really flourish. We'll see if Jones ends up being a player who gets moved, but it's something that would make a lot of sense for the Utah Jazz if they're looking to solidify their backup guard position.

Why do I mention the Jazz at all? Well, we know they are having internal conversations about Kyrie Irving. If that's the case, then it's a safe bet they're talking about Jones as well. Maybe they are one of those teams that's already called about Jones? No way to know for sure, but it's time to start paying attention to the rumor mill.
