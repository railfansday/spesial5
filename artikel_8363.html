Congratulations to These 2026 Pacific Crest Trail Finishers (Part 7)

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Pacific Crest Trail is one of North America’s most beautiful and iconic thru-hikes, stretching 2,650 miles from the Mexican border all the way to Canada. Every year, thousands of hikers set out on this unforgettable journey, pushing their limits both physically and mentally as they navigate this incredibly diverse terrain.

This year, wildfires devastated many thru-hikers’ plans. In July, the northern section of the PCT from Harts Pass to the Canadian border was closed due to the Ptarmigan Fire. Because of this, many finish photos look different than in years past.

Thru-hiking teaches lessons in perseverance and resilience, and many hikers were able to pivot from a traditional finish and found creative conclusions to their multi-month journey before the northern terminus officially reopened in mid-September. We’re thrilled to spotlight the incredible hikers who completed this journey in 2026, no matter the end point!

Have you just finished your PCT thru-hike? If so, please celebrate your finish with us by submitting a photo through the form below so we can feature you in a subsequent post. We can’t wait to share your incredible achievement!

“Hopped off trail at White Pass while the fire closures and smoke were still present- I’ll be coming back next year to do the rest!” -Meadowlark

“After fires closed us off from the PCT at Snoqualmie Pass, and with no desire to road walk or inhale smoke any longer, we sent it to the Olympics for a 160-mile traverse, ending along the wilderness coast, making our northern terminus the Strait of Juan de Fuca – the water border with Canada!” -Two Plates

“Found love on the PCT! The trail provides 🫶🏻” -Sunflower & Just Nick

“Despite the difficulties towards the end of the hike, all I can say it was awesome.” -Willem

“PCT was everything I’d ever dreamed of and much more. Even though it once seemed so unachievable, here I am. As a note to my future self, I’d like to keep in mind that even what seems impossible can become reality if you just dare to go for it. You can become anyone. You can do anything. Don’t wait for a better opportunity. Don’t wait for your future self to feel better prepared. Do it now. And while doing it, cherish every moment. Because once lived through, it will be gone forever.” -Freedom

“If you’re going through hell, just keep going.” -Lipstick

Congratulations to all the 2026 PCT finishers — your achievements are remarkable and worth celebrating!
