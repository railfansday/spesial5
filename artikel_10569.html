GTA 6 confirms Xbox Cloud Gaming support but PC players face a catch

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Rockstar Games has confirmed that Grand Theft Auto 6 will support Xbox Cloud Gaming when it launches on November 19, allowing players to experience the highly anticipated title without running it directly on their consoles.

The announcement comes as pre-loading officially begins for players who have digitally pre-ordered GTA 6. Alongside new details about the game’s performance on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, Rockstar has outlined how cloud streaming and Remote Play will work.

While Xbox Cloud Gaming support will extend access to older hardware, Rockstar’s announcement specifically limits the feature to Xbox consoles, leaving PC players without a cloud gaming option at launch.

In its November 10 announcement, Rockstar confirmed that GTA 6 will be available through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox Series X/S and Xbox One consoles.

“The game will also be available via XBOX Cloud Gaming on XBOX Series X|S and XBOX One consoles, utilizing XBOX Series S graphics settings,” the developer explained.

This means Xbox One owners can play GTA 6 through cloud streaming, even though the game won’t receive a native release on Microsoft’s previous-generation console.

Instead of running the game locally, Xbox One users can stream it from Microsoft’s cloud infrastructure, provided they have access to the service and a suitable internet connection.

To access it, owners will need to own the game and then purchase access to Xbox Cloud Gaming either through a Game Pass subscription or by buying hours.

Rockstar also confirmed that the cloud version will use Xbox Series S graphics settings, regardless of which supported Xbox console players use to access it.

“And as with all cloud streaming, connection speeds and other technical factors may influence game performance in ways that would not impact local gameplay,” the company added.

Notably, Rockstar’s announcement makes no mention of Xbox Cloud Gaming support on PC, and Xbox’s Matthew Ball confirmed the game won’t stream to PC. At least not at launch.

Although cloud gaming support is limited to consoles, Rockstar also confirmed that GTA 6 will support Remote Play on both PlayStation and Xbox.

“GTA VI will also fully support Remote Play on PlayStation and XBOX,” the developer stated.

Unlike cloud gaming, Remote Play allows players to stream games directly from their own consoles to another compatible device over an internet connection or local network.

This could potentially allow GTA 6 owners to stream the game to a Windows PC through Xbox Remote Play or to a PC or Mac using Sony’s PS Remote Play application.

It could also open the door to playing GTA 6 on handheld devices such as the Steam Deck and ROG Xbox Ally X20 through compatible Remote Play software.

However, Rockstar has not specifically confirmed PC or handheld compatibility for GTA 6’s Remote Play functionality, meaning those possibilities remain unverified.

Even if Remote Play works on PC, players would still need to own a compatible console running GTA 6, rather than accessing the game directly through a native PC version or Microsoft’s cloud servers.

GTA 6 launches November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, with no native PC release date announced.
