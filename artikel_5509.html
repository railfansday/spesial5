Dow jumps more than 600 points as oil slides and Nvidia stock falls

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The S&P 500 gained 0.9% and the Nasdaq rose 0.8% while Nvidia's board opens a probe into CEO Jensen Huang

The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 619 points, or 1.2%, in Monday trading as oil prices declined.

The S&P 500 gained 0.9% and the Nasdaq $NDAQ Composite added 0.8%. The three indexes began the session in positive territory, following the prior week's losses.

West Texas Intermediate futures, the U.S. oil benchmark, fell close to 3% to near $88 a barrel. Brent, the international benchmark, lost over 3% and was trading close to $95 a barrel.

Nvidia $NVDA stock fell 5% in premarket trading. The chipmaker’s board is launching a probe of CEO Jensen Huang over his ties to a businesswoman whom U.S. intelligence agencies suspect of spying for China, according to CNBC. The probe follows reports that Huang became a spying target after he was romantically involved with an executive suspected of working for Chinese intelligence, according to The Wall Street Journal.

According to The Wall Street Journal, a spokesperson for Nvidia said Huang intends to remain as chief executive.

Chip stocks recovered on Friday. The Nasdaq rose 1.1% and the S&P 500 gained 0.3%, while the Dow lost 237 points, or 0.5%. Thursday’s selloff was driven by AI stocks.

"Retail has not proven to be the dip buyers that they have been in the past," Liz Ann Sonders, chief investment strategist at Schwab Center for Financial Research, told CNBC's "Fast Money" on Friday. Sonders said the Nasdaq and Nasdaq-100 are oversold.

Investors are also weighing worries that stricter rules on how data centers are built, powered and financed could hold back the AI infrastructure buildout after Democrats performed strongly in the midterms, according to CNBC.

President Donald Trump is set to meet Qatar's prime minister on Monday, according to The Wall Street Journal. Qatar is among the nations serving as go-betweens for Washington and Tehran. Trump has said it is "highly unlikely" that he would order a broad new round of military attacks against Iran.

The consumer price index is due Tuesday and the producer price index is due Friday. Earnings from CoreWeave, Cisco $CSCO and Applied Materials $AMAT are due this week, according to CNBC.
