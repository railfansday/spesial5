AI chip stocks extend selloff globally as Democratic sweep clouds data center outlook

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nasdaq futures fell 0.7% on Thursday as Dow futures rose 0.4%, with strategists expecting Democratic wins to slow data center construction

Samsung Electronics stock dropped 7.5% and SK Hynix stock fell 9.5% in South Korea on Thursday as AI stocks extended their selloff after Democrats won both chambers of Congress in the midterm elections, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Nasdaq $NDAQ futures fell 0.7% on Thursday, while S&P 500 futures were flat and Dow Jones Industrial Average futures rose 0.4%. U.S. chip stocks fell before the opening bell. The Kospi and Japan's Nikkei 225 each declined sharply, while AI shares in European markets also retreated.

Strategists expect Democratic victories in Congress and in state governor contests, especially in Texas, to hold back how quickly new data centers get built, according to The Wall Street Journal. A data center slowdown “should impact estimates for AI infrastructure stocks as well as cloud companies,” UBS strategists said in a research note, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Chip stocks had slipped on Wednesday after Democrats won both chambers of Congress and key governorships, raising questions about the pace of data center construction. Nvidia $NVDA stock fell 3% on Wednesday, and CoreWeave stock fell about 10%.

Democrat Gina Hinojosa defeated Republican Gov. Greg Abbott $ABT in Texas, according to The Wall Street Journal, and has called for ending data center development in the state. Wells Fargo $WFC estimates Texas holds 27% of planned U.S. data center gigawatts. Republicans hold the majority in the Texas Legislature, which limits how much Hinojosa can change state policy.
