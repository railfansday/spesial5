IKEA's global sales fell for a third straight year as shoppers cut spending

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Inter IKEA Group's operating profit dropped 15% to €1.46 billion as it cut 850 jobs and committed to more price cuts

Inter IKEA Group reported results for its 2026 financial year on Friday, showing overall revenue, adjusted for currency, down 0.7% year over year at €43.8 billion ($49.1 billion) for the 12-month period that closed in August. The company said operating profit dropped 15% to €1.46 billion.

The decline was the third in a row, according to Bloomberg. According to Sweden Herald, sales fell 1.8%, or almost €1 billion, in the 2025/2026 financial year.

Inter IKEA Group is the owner of the IKEA concept and provides merchandise to the businesses that retail it. It said market conditions were tough and retailer demand was below the prior year. For the most part it held prices steady, though disruptions to supply chains in certain regions drove up the cost of commodities and logistics. The company intensified its drive to trim expenses, which included shedding 850 employees.

"The most important for me is affordability," CEO Jakub Jankowski said in an interview, according to Bloomberg. "We need to continue to lower prices for our customers." Jankowski wrote in the annual report that sales have "stagnated for several years" and that IKEA must get back to growing, according to Bloomberg.

Inter IKEA and its retailers pledged roughly €1.2 billion in September toward another wave of price cuts throughout Europe, according to Bloomberg. Inter IKEA reduced what it charges retailers, who then layered further cuts on top. Sweden Herald said the reductions cover products including the Billy bookcase.

Price cuts in fiscal 2024 pushed wholesale volumes up roughly 6% in the next year, yet they dented revenue and profit margins as tariffs and the cost of sourcing climbed.

Jankowski said sales in Europe have risen over the opening eight weeks of the new financial year, according to Bloomberg. He cautioned that calling that a sustained recovery would be premature. "I would like to see a bit more time to pass with the good result to say that this is a stable trend," he said.

Jankowski said the company restructured operations that had become "complex and fragmented," according to Bloomberg. No further job reductions are scheduled apart from the ones already revealed.

IKEA retailers in both Europe and North America are still rolling out smaller store formats, according to Bloomberg. The company has opted to shut seven of its stores in China, with customers increasingly moving to web orders and delivery to their homes.
