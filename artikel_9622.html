New York City, USA, November 23rd, 2026/Chainwire/--Armitage, Wintermute’s onchain vault curation arm, and Superform today announced a collaboration that gives holders of tokenized NVIDIA, Tesla, Apple and five other stocks an option beyond holding or selling.

Armitage will curate Superform’s Earn Stocks strategies on Base. Built on SuperformOS, these strategies let holders use their tokenized stocks as collateral while keeping their stock exposure.

Holders deposit their tokenized stocks, which are used as collateral to borrow USDC. That USDC is then lent out when expected lending rates are higher than the cost of borrowing. Holders keep their stock exposure, and any additional return comes from the gap between the two rates.

SuperStocks is Superform's family of products built on tokenized equities and Earn Stocks is the strategy layer that puts the assets inside those products to work. Armitage’s role covers strategy design, allocation, risk management and ongoing monitoring.

SuperformOS provides the operating system behind the products, bringing automated strategy execution, rule-based risk controls, accounting, and redemptions onto shared, verifiable infrastructure.

“Putting a stock onchain is the starting point. The opportunity is building the financial products people use around it,” said Vikram Arun, CEO of Superform. “Superform brings together the infrastructure to run those products and the distribution to put them in investors’ hands. With Armitage, we’re bringing Wintermute’s strategy expertise into that system, starting with earning on stocks. Our ambition is to make SuperStocks the building blocks for a new generation of portfolios and investment products.”For us, the opportunity is giving holders more options than just holding or selling their tokenized stocks,” said Igor Igamberdiev, Armitage Lead. “Putting those stocks to use onchain comes with real risks. Stock prices move, borrowing rates change, and positions need to be watched closely. That is the work we do every day, and we’re glad to be doing it with Superform.”

The collaboration covers eight existing SuperStocks products: NVIDIA, SpaceX, Tesla, Alphabet, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft and Apple. Armitage is taking over as curator in stages, starting with the Amazon SuperVault (superAMZN). No new tokenized stocks are being issued.

Additional returns are variable and not guaranteed. Users can lose principal through market, borrowing-rate, liquidation, liquidity, oracle and smart-contract risks. Availability is subject to eligibility and applicable restrictions. This release is informational, not investment advice.

Eligible users can explore Earn Stocks and review SuperStocks product terms at https://app.superform.xyz/#stocks.
