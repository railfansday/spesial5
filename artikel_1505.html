Gasperini praises Wesley: “He changed the game for us.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Roma manager Gian Piero Gasperini had words of high praise for Wesley’s performance as a substitute in tonight’s win over Parma.

“He changed the game for us,” said Gasperini in his post-match media appearance.

“He has excellent acceleration. That’s the advantage of having Champions League squads, where five or six changes can change the game. Or at least players come in who are at the same level as those who leave, but with a freshness that causes problems for the opponent.”

“Everyone came in well, though, and that’s an advantage. In other situations, we didn’t have the squad we have now, and we brought someone on the bench knowing that they could make a significant contribution when needed.”
