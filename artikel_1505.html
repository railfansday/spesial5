GdS: Linear structure and a strong work force – what Krösche is likely to implement at Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

AC Milan are expected to hire Markus Krösche in the next few weeks, and a report has looked a little more at his work today.

Between Eintracht Frankfurt and Milan, there are plenty of differences. From the finances available to how they’re spent, even to the way in which the structure of the club ticks over. So, Krösche will have to learn with the Rossoneri.

However, it won’t be a one way street, with the Diavolo needing to learn plenty too.

As Gazzetta dello Sport writes, Krösche methods are very linear; it’s about arriving and leaving at the right time, having the knowledge to enter and the courage to step away when the time is right.

His belief is that the club will always hold more weight than the manager, which has been shown in the past few months by Gerry Cardinale and there is no shortage of exciting signings on his CV.

What is also worth remembering is that he’s not one to step away from his methods. In 2022, he was given a chance to sign Cristiano Ronaldo. However, it didn’t make sense for him or Frankfurt, so they never made their move.

If it happens his way, Timmo Hardung will arrive alongside the German. The pair met at RB Leipzig and their relationship has only grown since then and Krösche eventually led a team of 70 people at Frankfurt. In other words, he won’t be out of place at the top of the Milan football organisation.

Gazzetta even suggests that it’s ‘no secret’ that his desire is to move to Italy to work under Krösche once again. Given their successes together, it makes sense why all parties are hoping to keep that relationship under the same roof.
