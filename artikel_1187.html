Italy cut gap in race for extra Champions League spot

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Five wins and two draws for the seven Serie A representatives in Europe helped Italy gain ground in the UEFA coefficient rankings, narrowing the gap to the top two nations, England and Portugal.

Italian clubs went unbeaten in midweek European action. Inter and Como won in the Champions League, while Roma and Napoli drew away to PSG and Manchester City, respectively.

Juventus and Milan won in the Europa League on Thursday, while Atalanta also claimed victory, coming from two goals down to secure a 3-2 away win over Borac in the Conference League.

Italy remain sixth in the UEFA coefficient rankings, but the positive results have helped the country close the gap on the top two nations, England and Portugal.

Italy’s average points total is currently just 686 points below that of second-placed Portugal. However, Portugal have only five teams competing in Europe, compared with Italy’s seven.

Only the two countries with the highest average points totals will earn an extra place in the 2027–28 Champions League.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
