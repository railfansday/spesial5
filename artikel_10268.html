Motorola Razr (2027) reportedly gets big upgrades and an early January launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

According to a new report, Motorola’s next Razr flip phone is getting some major upgrades and a big schedule change, with Motorola Razr (2027) launching early next year.

Android Headlines today published a new set of images of the Motorola Razr (2027), as well as key specs. The device is shown in three colors and apparently carries the codename “Raptor.”

There are several key spec changes here, including:

Motorola Razr (2027) is still only set for three Android OS updates and this new model is heavier at 195g (likely due to the bigger battery), but Motorola is very clearly making some big upgrades here. The biggest change is to the displays, both of which now match the Razr+ and Razr Ultra models. Some key specs, such as 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage, are unchanged from the 2026 release.

Motorola has been releasing its Razr series in the Spring, but today’s report claims that Razr (2027) will launch in January 2027, likely at CES and at the same time as the Razr Flex book-style foldable. There’s no word on new Plus or Ultra models yet.
