Zelda Notes Reveals Top 'Game Over Causes' And More Global Play Data In Ocarina Of Time Remake

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Zelda Notes might not appeal to everyone, but in the Ocarina of Time remake on Switch 2, Nintendo is using it to track global player data.

If you haven't already taken a look, roughly a week after the launch of this title, there are now some rather interesting player stats. This includes information about the top "game over causes", the types of special items used, and much more.

As you can see, players (18%) are most commonly "falling victim" to ReDead. Some other stats reveal the Hookshot as the most used special item, and the top damage sources are traps.
