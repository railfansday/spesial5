This Might Be the Best Game You Didn't Know Was Out on PS5

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

How the hell am I only learning about Tanuki: Pon's Summer now? It's out today on PS5 for $19.99 / £15.99 / €19.99.

Somehow, this is a game that we've never covered — despite the fact that it looks bloody brilliant.

You play as a cheeky little tanuki who works a postal service in rural Japan. But in between making deliveries on your sick BMX — which you can learn to master through pulling off crazy tricks — you get to partake in all kinds of cool minigames, like baseball and sumo.

It's ultimately a kind of slice-of-life game, but it just looks so perfectly crafted in the above trailer.

I'll definitely be giving it a shot, so if I can find the time to bring you lot a slightly delayed review, I will.
