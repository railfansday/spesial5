Tiny Bookshop Sells 1 Million Copies, New DLC Expansion Announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Tiny Bookshop, the charming indie title in which you take on the role of bookseller in a quaint seaside resort, has officially sold surpassed 1 million copies sold across PC, Switch, PS5, Xbox, iOS, and Android since its launch in August 2025.

To celebrate, publisher Skystone Games and developer neoludic have announced a new DLC expansion launching on 5th December 2026: Arts & Crafts.

The expansion opens up two new locations – Puffin Primary and the Artist Quarter – while also adding 24 new wagon parts, over 30 new decor items, new quests, and a mystery surrounding a stolen painting. Ooh. It's more of the same, then, albeit with seemingly less emphasis on the act of selling books.

That's all the information we have to go on right now, but more is promised very soon – and we should hope so, given how close the release date is!

We awarded Tiny Bookshop a score of 8/10 in our review, calling it "a fun little experience whether you’re into reading or not". If you enjoyed it, then it might be worth keeping an eye out for the new expansion.
