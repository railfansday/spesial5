Marathon's Fate Hangs in the Balance as Bungie Details Its Game-Saving PvE Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Bungie has reconfirmed that Marathon's major 'Symbiosis' update will launch on the 8th December. You may recall that it was delayed back in September.

Many believe that this patch will be the troubled shooter's make-or-break moment, as Marathon's player numbers remain concerningly low.

The good news is that Symbiosis looks the part. A new trailer highlights its additions, which include a brand new PvE mode titled Expedition.

The official description reads: "Phase 1 of a new PvE-only mode built around a three-run series. Play solo or with a crew, face new combat and environmental challenges without PvP threats, and use Flash Boosts to shape your build during a run."

Basically, Bungie's hoping that the introduction of a non-competitive option draws in new players — and I suppose that's a logical avenue to go down, given that Marathon's struggled to crack the extraction shooter market.

The update also packs a new team-based 'Deathmatch' mode in its beta form, a new social space called Limbo, and a new Runner Shell (character class) named Bishop. A couple of new weapons are up for grabs, too.

And last but certainly not least, Marathon's going free-to-play for a full week when the Symbiosis update drops. Worth a shot?
