Guide: PlayStation Studios: All Sony First-Party Developers and What They're Working On

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Sony's first-party developers are known collectively as PlayStation Studios, and it's these teams that make all the games published by the company.

Whether it's PS5, PC, or beyond, there are always new games in the works. While we don't always know everything about what's in development, we've done our best to collate all the current info in this page.

Below, you'll find a list of every subsidiary studio under Sony's umbrella as well as what they're currently working on. We'll be updating this article every so often, when new information comes to light.

On this page: PlayStation Studios: All Sony First-Party Developers and What They're Working On

Here are all of Sony's teams working out of the company's home country.

Originally an internal development unit named Polys Entertainment, Polyphony Digital is best known for creating Sony’s flagship racing simulation franchise, Gran Turismo.

Current Projects: Polyphony has been supporting Gran Turismo 7 consistently since launch, with lots of free updates adding new events and vehicles. While this looks set to continue, we expect work has begun on the studio's next game, which will presumably be another Gran Turismo.

Team Asobi rose from the ashes of Japan Studio, initially an internal group building tech demos and on-boarding software such as The Playroom. It brought us Astro Bot Rescue Mission for PSVR, PS5 pack-in game Astro's Playroom, and is now best known for its full-fledged 3D platformer, Astro Bot.

Current Projects: Team Asobi's post-launch updates and support for Astro Bot appears to have concluded, so we can presume the whole studio is now working on its next game. It isn't known what that game is, though given Astro's success, we reckon a sequel is likely.

Below you'll find details on all of SIE's European studios.

Firesprite has an interesting history; it was initially formed by developers from Studio Liverpool, an old Sony studio shuttered in 2012. After The Persistence and supporting Sony on The Playroom and The Playroom VR, it made Horizon Call of the Mountain alongside Guerrilla. It's now SIE's largest UK-based studio.

Current Projects: Firesprite is currently working on Until Dawn 2, a narrative horror adventure game coming to PS5 in January 2027.

Creators of the Killzone franchise, and acquired by Sony following the release of Shellshock: ‘Nam 67 in 2005. One of SIE's most technically capable studios, it's now best known for Horizon Zero Dawn, which runs on the studio's own Decima Engine.

Current Projects: Guerrilla is currently making Horizon Hunters Gathering, a co-op action game with roguelike elements and a very different art style. Also in the works is Horizon Steel Frontiers, an MMO being built for PC and mobile; NCSOFT is spearheading production, but Guerrilla is involved as well. It can also be safely assumed that Horizon 3 is in the pipeline.

Finnish developer Housemarque has worked closely with Sony for over a decade. The studio specialises in arcade-like action, bringing us titles such as Super Stardust HD, Resogun, and of course its latest work, Returnal. The success of the latter pushed Sony to acquire the team in 2021.

Current Projects: Housemarque recently released Saros, a rogue-lite third-person action game that builds on the Returnal formula. It has since released a free update adding more content and features. Plans for what comes next are unknown.

The makers of the LittleBigPlanet franchise, and one of Sony’s most imaginative studios. Media Molecule was acquired by Sony in 2010 following the success of Sackboy’s inaugural adventure.

Current Projects: With post-launch support of Dreams now concluded, Media Molecule is working on its next project, which will be a new IP. It's expected this will reduce the focus on creation and will be a more traditional game. Reports suggest it's making an Animal Crossing-esque life sim.

A company held aloft for its uniformly excellent PC ports, Sony acquired Nixxes in 2021 for this exact purpose. It's mostly been focused on porting existing PlayStation Studios titles to PC, but made its first real creative effort with Horizon Zero Dawn Remastered in 2024.

Current Projects: Nixxes collaborated with Housemarque on Saros. It's also likely to be working on unannounced PC ports.

XDEV is a team that mostly concerns itself with providing support to external teams that are making PlayStation-exclusive titles. Over the years it has helped bring many games to PS5 and PS4, such as Detroit: Become Human, Returnal, Death Stranding, Stellar Blade, Rise of the Ronin, and many others. Though it originated in the UK, the company now also operates in the US and Japan.

Current Projects: XDEV is working with Housemarque on Saros, Arc System Works on Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls, and presumably more yet to be announced.

Most of Sony's best-known studios hail from the United States. Here they all are — plus a bonus Canada team!

Infamously created Bubsy 3D for the PS1 before moving on to the Syphon Filter series under the Eidetic name. Acquired by Sony in 2000. Historically focused on portable games, the team broke back into AAA territory with Days Gone on PS4.

Current Projects: Unknown, though reportedly it won't be a Days Gone sequel.

Famed for its expertise in first-person shooters, Bungie has made some of gaming's best-loved franchises in Halo and Destiny. Thanks to its work on the latter, Sony acquired the company in 2022 during its big push into multiplayer and live service games. Bungie has retained some autonomy under this new ownership, though this has softened over time.

Current Projects: Bungie's latest is Marathon, a reinvention of its own sci-fi shooter series from the 90s. This iteration is an intense extraction shooter that launched in March 2026. It'll be supporting this game for the foreseeable future.

A new studio co-founded by Jade Raymond, famed for her work on the Assassin's Creed and Watch Dogs series. The developer has yet to release a game, but has been scooped up by Sony as the team works on its debut project.

Current Projects: Haven was developing the now-cancelled Fairgames, an online multiplayer shooter described as a "competitive heist experience". The studio is currently acting in a support role for other teams.

Famed for PlayStation exclusive series such as Ratchet & Clank and Resistance, the developer has felt like part of the first party family all along. With Marvel's Spider-Man and Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales being such huge hits, it's no wonder Sony scooped the studio up in 2019.

Current Projects: Insomniac recently released Marvel's Wolverine exclusively for PS5 in September 2026. It's extremely likely that Marvel's Spider-Man 3 is in the works, too.

Perhaps the most famous of Sony’s studios, Naughty Dog has a long history creating series such as Crash Bandicoot, Jak & Daxter, Uncharted, and The Last of Us. Acquired in 2001.

Current Projects: Naughty Dog is currently working on sci-fi action adventure game Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet. It's expected this will launch in 2027, though no release information has been announced yet.

Most famous for the critically acclaimed MLB The Show series, San Diego Studio has previously operated as a support studio for external developers, and has also worked on titles like The Mark of Kri and Pain.

Current Projects: San Diego Studio recently launched MLB The Show 26, the latest instalment in its annual series. It's also working on MLB The Show Mobile.

Creators of the God of War franchise. Previously incubated developers like thatgamecompany, while also serving as a support studio for external developers working on exclusive games.

Current Projects: Santa Monica Studio's next game is God of War Laufey, a game following Faye and what happens to her after she dies at the start of God of War (2018). This is due for release in May 2027. Additionally, the team has announced God of War Trilogy Remake, which revisits the Greek mythology games, though this is further out.

One-time Nintendo 64 developer, Sucker Punch is the creator of PlayStation fan favourites like Sly Cooper and inFAMOUS. Acquired by Sony in 2011, the studio is now best known for Ghost of Tsushima.

Current Projects: Sucker Punch most recently released Ghost of Yotei, an indirect sequel to Ghost of Tsushima. Ghost of Yotei Legends, a co-op multiplayer DLC, is now also available. It's unknown what the studio's next game will be.

One of Sony's newest studios, teamLFG is a developer that began as a standalone team within Bungie, but has now spun out to become its own thing. It has yet to release a game.

Current Projects: teamLFG is working on something currently known as Project Gummybears. It's described as a "team-based action game that draws inspiration from fighting games, platformers, MOBAs, life sims, and frog-type games" set within a "lighthearted" and "comedic" sci-fi universe.

Valkyrie is perhaps best known for its GUNS UP! series, which Sony published on various platforms prior to the acquisition. After being a reliable external collaborator on several projects, Valkyrie Entertainment was brought into PlayStation Studios in December 2021. It has since provided support on numerous games including God of War Ragnarok, The Last of Us Part II Remastered, and Uncharted: Legacy of Thieves Collection.

Current Projects: Valkyrie Entertainment holds its cards very close to its chest, so it's difficult to know what it's working on. It can be assumed it's helping out across various projects within PlayStation Studios.
