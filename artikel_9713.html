I Still Love Blogging

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

I know that most people don't read blogs as much as they used to. The internet has changed, and nowadays people seem to prefer short videos, quick posts, and content that takes only a few seconds to consume.

I love the idea of creating a little space on the internet to share thoughts, ideas, experiences, and things I enjoy. I like writing, organizing my thoughts, choosing images, and turning a simple idea into something meaningful.

Maybe blogs don't have the same popularity they once had, but I don't think everything needs to be popular to be worth doing.

Some things are worth creating simply because we enjoy them. And I think that's one of the reasons I keep coming back to blogging.
