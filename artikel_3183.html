My Amazon smart display has been filling with AI garbage. Here's how to clear it out if you're struggling.

With Amazon announcing a new Echo Show 16 to square off with Apple’s upcoming HomeView, the smart displays are having a moment, but I’ve noticed something seems off. In the past week or two, my Echo Show has started showing me a lot of lazy, annoying AI-generated ads under a playback feature called Created For You. So I figured out how to improve my experience with a few settings changes.

Not only is this AI slop vague and accompanied by poorly generated images, but it also doesn’t seem to have anything to do with my recorded interests or personalization, either. The slides range from questions about squirrels finding nuts and how to make cornfield mazes to facts about daylight saving time. Basically, it’s very general content I don’t really care about and don’t want showing on a central display at home.

I suspect Amazon is pushing its AI podcasts and similar chatty content with this latest round of generated ads, but if you’re not interested in viewing any of these canned clips, they can come across as irksome. So I reviewed my settings options and found out how to push my Show display away from these creations and toward more interesting content.

The good news is you can control what your Echo Show, well, shows you — but you have to know what settings to change. This isn’t very easy through the Alexa app, but I found a better way using the Show’s own touchscreen controls. Here’s what to do when you have a minute.

First, when an AI slide like this appears, tap the three-dot menu button and tap the thumbs-down option to indicate your dislike. It’s not a perfect solution, but it should help start weeding out these types of ads.

Next, swipe down on your Echo Show device to access the main menu, and choose Settings. Then choose Display & Appearance, and head to Content Categories.

Here you have two options. You can manually turn off all the categories you don’t want to see, especially those connected to the AI slop that you’ve been seeing. Keep focus on topics that you’re interested in if you still want to see other related content. You can also toggle the Rotate Once setting on, which will show you Amazon’s slides only once, my preferred option for handling this kind of unwanted content.

As an alternative, you can switch your Echo Show to show only a photo album of your choosing. I like getting some Alexa recommendations like recipes and updates, so this option isn’t for me, but if you have a good photo album to share with Alexa, you can limit your slides to only your own content.

With these settings adjusted, you should see far fewer AI-generated images and more slides tailored to your own interests. I’ll keep an eye on my Echo Shows and let you know if anything changes, but this has worked well for me so far.
