Walmart's Black Friday sale has already made heavy cuts to these phone prices

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

Black Friday might not be for another week and a half, but Walmart's Black Friday sale has already begun. The retailer has slashed prices on hundreds of products, including a bunch of different cell phones — some of which aren't that easy to find anymore.

At the moment, a lot of these discounts apply to older phones, rather than the latest and best models. You can save money on recent releases, like the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra, but not to the same extent you can at other retailers. That said, older phones are still just as capable and come with the added benefit of costing considerably less than current flagships. Plus, some of these deals are rare iPhone discounts, which are always worth checking out.

Here are the best phone deals currently on offer in Walmart's Black Friday sale.

The first Apple e-Series model has a lot going for it. While it lacks the A19 chip and MagSafe of the iPhone 17e, the iPhone 16e still offers everything you need to run Apple Intelligence. You also get a full OLED display, a premium design and a surprisingly good camera, considering it's working solo.Price Check: $539 @ Best Buy

Samsung's Fan Edition lineup is a great way to pick up premium features without the extreme price tags that flagships command. Picking up the S25 FE, instead of the S26 FE, means you can save even more money in the process. For your money, you get good camera performance, solid AI support, fast charging, and a premium-looking design you don't see on other cheaper Galaxy phones.Price check: $499 @ Amazon | $649 @ Best Buy

If you want a skinny, lightweight phone, then the Galaxy S25 Edge is the Android device for you. This model is locked to T-Mobile, but you're saving a bunch on the MSRP. For your money, you get a 200MP main camera, 12MP ultrawide, a 6.7-inch display, a 3,900 mAh battery and all the performance power of the Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy chip.Price check: $669 @ Amazon | $699 @ Best Buy

It may be two years old, but the iPhone 16 is still an excellent handset. This one is locked to Walmart's Straight Talk MVNO, but you're still getting one of the first iPhones that doesn't make you wish you'd bought a Pro model, with good cameras, spatial photo support, and powerful A18 performance.

The S25 Plus benefits from a large screen, solid cameras, excellent performance and a whole suite of incredible AI features. It's also a little bit cheaper than the S26 Plus, especially after the recent price hikes, which makes it well worth considering.Price check: $739 @ Amazon

With the iPhone 18 not due until next year, anyone hoping to get a full iPhone experience without buying a Pro will have to stick with the iPhone 17. It's more expensive than it used to be, but if you're willing to lock yourself down to Walmart's own MVNO, Straight Talk, you can save $70 off the MSRP ahead of Black Friday.Price check: $929 @ Best Buy

Foldables are expensive, but picking up last year's Galaxy Z Fold 7 is looking like a great way to save cash. With $700 off the launch price, you can enjoy many of the same benefits as the Z Fold 8 Ultra, but at a discount. It has a smaller battery, but you get the same large foldable display, 200MP main camera, a lightweight design and all the performance you'd want from a flagship device.Price check: $1,899 @ Best Buy
