Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Prairie Operating Co. (PROP +13.93%) reported increased production and revenue for the third quarter, supported by the completion of 37 wells across five pads. Management stated that development activity at the Burnett and Castor pads validated acreage quality and facilitated the conversion of potential locations into proved undeveloped inventory. The company reported a reduction in unit operating costs and highlighted the integration of internal logistics to improve margins. While production reached a peak in early October, the company emphasized disciplined capital allocation and a focus on generating free cash flow to improve its liquidity position by year-end.
