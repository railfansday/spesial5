Have you ever capitalized on Black Friday deals to refresh your kitchen? While this helps with Christmas dinner preparations, Black Friday is unfortunately after Thanksgiving, leaving you unable to utilize your revamped kitchen for Turkey Day. But what if you could upgrade your kitchen appliances before Thanksgiving? With Samsung's early Black Friday deals — available now — you can. From smarter food storage to easier cooking and cleanup, these five Samsung appliances can help make life in the kitchen a little simpler, just in time for holiday hosting.

Every chef knows that a good meal starts with quality ingredients — and proper storage of those ingredients. If you’re hosting Thanksgiving and Christmas, you’re going to need to keep a lot of different ingredients fresh. Samsung’s Bespoke AI Refrigerators can both store a large amount of ingredients and help you stay on top of keeping them fresh.

For larger turkeys, the USDA recommends five to six days of thawing in the refrigerator before Thanksgiving. And turkeys that need to be thawed for this long also require a lot of space. This is where the Bespoke AI 3-Door French Door Refrigerator with Zero Clearance Fit comes in handy. Featuring 24 cu. ft. of storage space, this refrigerator maximizes storage while minimizing the area taken up in the kitchen, so you don’t need to compromise on your turkey to make space in the kitchen. This is because of Zero Clearance Fit, which — combined with sleek counter-depth design — allows the doors to open without additional space along the sides.

Carafes make it much easier to refill glasses of water at the Thanksgiving table, and these can easily be filled up with the Water Dispenser. Additionally, select models include Sphere Ice™, which dispenses ice cubes that keep cocktails colder for longer. In addition to impressing your guests with the elevated experience of spherical ice, their beverages will be optimally chilled whether they are watching the big football game on TV before dinner or you’re all spending quality time together afterwards.

Thanksgiving isn’t only about turkey — it’s also about side dishes and beverages. Mashed potatoes, stuffing, green bean casserole, pumpkin pie, and all the other holiday favorites contain a lot of ingredients that need to be stored in the refrigerator. It can be difficult to accommodate all these ingredients, but the Bespoke AI 4-Door French Door Refrigerator With Beverage Center™ makes it much easier. FlexZone™ Drawer brings flexibility to your storage needs, letting you adjust the amount of space available to you depending on the number of items you need to store. Plus, it has five different temperature settings, allowing you to chill your ingredients accordingly. Meanwhile, the Beverage Center™ keeps refreshing drinks within easy reach and out of sight for a cleaner interior look.
