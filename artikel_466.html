The Roborock Saros 20 is powerful, boasting 36,000 Pa of suction for vacuuming, with a dual anti-tangle system that helps ensure hair doesn't get wrapped up in it while scooting around. The RockDock is helpful for mopping, offering 212°F hot-water washing and 131°F hot-air drying to keep pads fresh and clean for scrubbing. Plus, the robot vacuum has a slim build that can move under furniture as low as 3.13 inches off the ground.

It also features an AdaptiLift Chassis 3.0 system, which can lift the vacuum over thresholds and uneven floors (for example, crossing up to 3.46-inch double-layer steps or cleaning carpets up to 1.18 inches deep), as well as the StarSight Autonomous System 2.0. This maps out your home, but also assists with obstacle avoidance. Mashable's Leah Stodart was impressed by this, writing in her review, "I think this is the first time a robot vacuum in my apartment has had a 100 percent obstacle avoidance rate for multiple days on end."
