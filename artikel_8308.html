Introducing Ski Perks: Exclusive Discounts for Outside+ Members

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Outside+ members can enjoy exclusive Ski Perks discounts on ski gear, apparel, rentals, and more from top outdoor brands. (Photo: Keri Bascetta)

We skiers love a good deal, especially on gear. And if you’ve ever wished you had access to the kinds of discounts usually reserved for ski industry insiders, here’s something worth knowing about.

Outside+ members now have access to Ski Perks, a collection of exclusive discounts and offers from ski and outdoor brands, included with their membership at no additional cost. We’re talking savings on everything from ski apparel and accessories to equipment rentals and recovery tools.

Outside+ already gives you access to an impressive collection of outdoor stories and resources, from gear reviews and adventure films to GPS apps that help you plan your next adventure outdoors. Our goal is to give you best-in-class content and the tools to help you get outside more often and make the most of it.

For skiers, that means unlimited gear reviews, destination guides, and expert advice from SKI, plus original reporting from publications like Outside, Backpacker, RUN, and more. Eligible U.S. members will also receive a print subscription to Outside magazine. Stream Warren Miller films and other adventure programming on Outside TV, explore trails with Trailforks Pro, track your activities with MapMyFitness, and plan your next adventure with Gaia GPS Premium.

And now, you’ve got another way to put that membership to good use.

Check back throughout the ski season for even more perks coming soon.

Already an Outside+ member? Log in to your Outside account and head to your member benefits page. Select the Perks tab to browse available offers, then click Claim for instructions on how to redeem each one.

Not a member yet? Join Outside+ here to unlock Ski Perks and everything else that comes with your membership.

So, the next time you’ve got your eye on a new jacket from our Winter Gear Guide, need to replace your gloves, or are planning a ski trip to one of the Top 50 Resorts in North America, check your Ski Perks before you pull out your credit card. You might find a pretty good deal waiting for you.
