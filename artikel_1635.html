Dortmund a tricky test + Guimaraes on getting to grips with Arteta’s midfield

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Arsenal are in Champions League action this evening, taking on Borussia Dortmund at home. It’s a game that Kepa Arrizabalaga will start in the absence of David Raya, but the news around the Arsenal number 1 was a little more positive than expected. In his press conference yesterday, Mikel Arteta said:

He’s not fit for tomorrow, unfortunately, but it doesn’t look too serious. We need some more tests tomorrow, but it’s quite encouraging. With the pain that he was showing immediately after the action, we were all quite worried, but he seems to be better than what we expected.

We expected something much worse because he could almost not handle the amount of pain that he had at that moment, and the first test that we’ve done showed something that is much more encouraging. There’s no need for surgery, which was the main fear especially because that would rule him out for months, so let’s see.

So, it seems like he’s going to miss a few games, but not anything prolonged, which is obviously good news. Elsewhere, there’s room for rotation, and I think this might be a good chance to give minutes to William Saliba who hasn’t played competitively since the World Cup.  He’s had a couple of behind closed doors appearances, which will be useful in terms of fitness, but nothing compares with actually playing at this level.

I wonder too if players like Ben White, Cristhian Mosquera and Piero Hincapie will fancy their chances, but that seems like a lot of change in the back four – and the goalkeeper – so we’ll have to wait and see there. In midfield, we can bring Myles Lewis-Skelly in for a start, but obviously the manager has plenty to choose from in there, and up front I imagine it will be a chance for Viktor Gyokeres whose minutes have been scant so far this season. Asked if the striker had been knocking on his door about that, Arteta said:

No, we have conversations with every player to make sure that they understand what we are doing, to gather information about the way they are feeling, and so far, it’s been very positive with Viktor and with the rest of the team.

He could really use a goal tonight if he does start, as does Christos Tzolis whose numbers so far this season aren’t where you’d like them to be. Obviously there’s more to assessing a player than simply goals and assists, but for a left winger brought into replace two players who contributed a lot to the team over the last few seasons, just two assists from 11 appearances (Community Shield excluded) is a little below what you’d hope. He has had an injury, of course, but if his season needs a kick-start, tonight would be very timely.

Dortmund sit top of the Bundesliga right now, unbeaten in the league thus far, and with just one defeat all season in all competitions. That did come in the Champions League, away to Bodo Glimt, but this will be a tricky test tonight for Arsenal. Arteta says:

I’m very impressed with them, you see the results so far, where they are in the Bundesliga at the top and where they are in the Champions League and especially the way they are playing, the way they are dominating games, the amount of threat they are able to create. We had them here in pre-season, and they showed the quality and what they are capable of, so we are very much aware of that. But we play at home in front of our crowd, and now we’re going to go and attack the game, that’s for sure.

After a disappointing second half against Newcastle on Saturday, I’m sure the manager will be looking for a response from his side. It’s nice to be at home again, and a long spell of games in London will hopefully less taxing physically without all the travel, so it’d be good to get that off to a winning start tonight.

Elsewhere, Bruno Guimaraes spoke about his first few months at the club, and how he now feels like he’s getting to grips with what Mikel Arteta wants from him, saying:

I’m here, I don’t know, three, four months? It’s still a lot to go, but I now definitely understand my role in the team, the way that Mikel wants me to play in the team. So, yeah, I think I still can play much, much better.

And it’s quite interesting to hear him talk about how at Arsenal he is more ‘free’ than he was under Eddie Howe at Newcastle. Most would feel it was the other way around, but he explained:

It’s very different. When I was at Newcastle, you play more positional, keep more your position. Here it’s a little bit more free.

If you see the game against Newcastle, they were marking us man to man. You see JT [Jurrien Timber] plays a six, an eight and a number nine sometimes, so you have to move a lot. Of course, I was not used to it. So now I understand a little bit more what he wants for the midfield.

I think there’s more to come from him, he’s looked good at times, a little off the pace at others, and I think my main worry is about the physical side of his game. Does he have the running power and athleticism that enables players like Declan Rice and Martin Odegaard to thrive under this manager? At 29, he’s no spring chicken, but I guess there’s always room for improvement.
