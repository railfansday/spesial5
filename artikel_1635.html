Modric hints he could continue beyond 2026-27 season: “As long as I feel this way”

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Luka Modric has hinted that he could continue playing beyond this season, having made Milan his home since arriving in 2025.

In the summer, after some speculation prior to the World Cup, Modric put pen to paper and extended his contract with Milan for another season. It was a huge boost for Ruben Amorim, who pushed for the Croatian to stay and help shape the new Milan.

A few months ago, Modric also announced that he would continue with the Croatian national team, deciding not to retire after the World Cup. It’s a pretty clear signal that he still feels in great shape and perhaps could continue beyond this season.

Speaking to this week’s edition of SportWeek, as transcribed by MilanPress, Modric shared his thoughts on the derby and the project at Milan. He also said that as long as he feels good, he will continue to play football, hinting that the 2027-28 season might not be his last.

“It was a fantastic victory and I think it was our best performance of the season. We deserved these three points, which we really needed, both for our morale and for our confidence.

“We are growing as a team, and if we can limit the dips in form that can occur over a season, we will have a chance to fight for the title, which remains our goal.

“We are putting the coach’s ideas into practice better and better, we are moving in the right direction, and our stadium is driving us: the Milan fans are incredible.”

“I feel good and I still have the passion, ambition, and strength. As long as I feel this way, I want to keep playing. I’m also loved at Milan. I had no doubts this summer, after speaking with Amorim and the club: they wanted me to stay and I just needed to feel wanted.

“Milan is my team, and I want to help the younger players develop,” he stated.
