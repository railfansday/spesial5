Serie A line-ups: Parma vs. Roma

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Roma have Paulo Dybala and Mile Svilar back with Donyell Malen and Rodrigo Mora, as they visit a Parma side beset by injury problems.

It kicks off at the Stadio Tardini at 17.00 GMT (18.00 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from today’s four Serie A games on the Liveblog.

The Giallorossi are one of only two Serie A teams still unbeaten so far this season, and went into the break for international duty just two points off leaders and local rivals Lazio.

Gian Piero Gasperini can breathe a big sigh of relief with the return of both Dybala and Svilar, the goalkeeper who has been out of action for over two months following an accident in training.

Malen and Mora complete the trident attack, with Manu Koné and Gianluca Mancini refreshed after withdrawing from international duty with minor injuries.

The only absentees are Devyne Rensch and Lorenzo Pellegrini.

Parma had shown signs of turning their season around after Alberto Gilardino replaced Carlos Cuesta, but tumbled at home to Bologna with Abdoulaye Ndiaye sent off after just nine minutes.

He is therefore suspended here, with David Romero and Dominik Drobnic out, plus Diego Carlos not at 100 per cent either, but at least Adrian Bernabé is back in the squad.

Giovanni Fabbian and Mandela Keita are in midfield, with Nesta Elphege and El Bilal Touré preferred to Simone Lontani upfront.

Parma: Daffara; Delprato, Troilo, Carboni; Britschgi, Fabbian, Sierro, Keita, Valeri; Elphege, Touré

Roma: Svilar; Mancini, Balerdi, Hermoso; Lulli, Cristante, Koné, Molina; Mora; Dybala, Malen
