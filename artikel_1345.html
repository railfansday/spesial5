Steven Gerrard Names ‘Freak’ Player Who Will Be Talked About Like Messi And Ronaldo

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Former Liverpool and England captain Steven Gerrard has named the one current player he believes will be talked about in the same breath as Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo in 10 years' time.

Gerrard, now 46, is rarely one to dish out praise lightly. Such were his high standards during his playing days, when he was hailed as a Roy of the Rovers figure after dragging his beloved Reds to a memorable Champions League triumph in 2005, he was widely regarded as one of the most demanding players of his generation.

And on the rare occasions he has tipped a younger talent to reach the very top, things haven't always gone to plan. Take Adam Lewis, for example, who Gerrard once described as one of the 'best he's ever seen' but now finds himself plying his trade in League Two.

However, given the reputation this latest player has already built for himself, it seems unlikely Gerrard will be left with egg on his face this time. After all, he's already knocking on the door of Ballon d'Or contention.

There is no shortage of young talents in world football right now and, just as everyone thought nobody could ever compare to Messi and Ronaldo as their reigns at the pinnacle of the sport come to an end, there's someone else that comes along to get the tails wagging.

Rio Ngumoha, Max Dowman, and Desire Doue all rank among the best young players in the world right now. But it is not exactly surprising to learn of Gerrard's love for Barcelona youngster Lamine Yamal. He said during TNT Sports' coverage of Barcelona's most recent Champions League match:

"We're talking about a megastar. He is a freak of a player, the numbers that he's getting at the moment - goals and assists - are beyond belief, scary really.

"Just to be in the stadium tonight to watch him up close and personal, I'm sure it'll be a pleasure. We talk about superstars in England who are 16, 17, 18 and have played handful of games. He's the same age and he's got numbers that are beyond belief, he's already in the conversation for Ballon d'Ors. He's still a teenager, which is a scary thing really."

He continued: "These players come around every so often - Messi, Ronaldo - and I know I'm not saying this too soon, he's going to be spoken about like them in a decade's time."

Barcelona's attacking players have seemed to go up a whole new level so far this season, with a remarkable 46 goals from just 11 La Liga games, and 14 goals in four Champions League matches.

While top scorers Raphinha and Yamal - with the latter having finished third behind Harry Kane and Kylian Mbappe at the 2026 Ballon d'Or ceremony - have earned the majority of the praise, England international Anthony Gordon has also caught the eye following his move from Newcastle United, with five assists and two goals in La Liga.

"Anthony's a learner, he's someone who wants to grow and evolve," Gerrard said. "He wants to become a world-class player himself. The potential's there, we've seen it for club and country now.
