Everton v Fulham Ticket News

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

PREMIER LEAGUEEVERTON V FULHAMHILL DICKINSON STADIUMSUNDAY 6 DECEMBERKICK-OFF 2PM

The ballot application window for Forever Blue+ Members for the Fulham fixture will open on Monday 9 November at 10am.

Ballot applications can be submitted online, over the phone on 0151 556 1878, or in person at the Hill Dickinson South Stand box office. Supporters will have until 4pm on Tuesday 10 November to submit their application. Box office opening times can be found here.

Supporters wishing to enter the ballot during this priority period can gain access by signing up for a Forever Blue+ membership here. Forever Blue Members can also upgrade to a Forever Blue+ membership by calling the Everton Fan Centre on 0151 556 1878 and quoting their membership/supporter number.

Eligible supporters will be able to apply individually or in a group of up to six family and friends (please note, all supporters within a group must meet the eligibility criteria for the ballot). Each membership/supporter number can only be entered into a ballot once. Therefore, if a membership/supporter number is included as part of a group application, it cannot then be used for a separate individual application or an application as part of another group.

Tickets are limited to one per Member. Supporters should note that, in order to accrue an individual booking history, unique supporter numbers must be given for each attending supporter.

Successful applicants will receive confirmation of their ticket by Thursday 12 November.

Should any tickets remain, a second sales window will open at 10am on Friday 13 November for Forever Blue Members. Forever Blue+ Members who did not enter the ballot, or whose ballot entry was unsuccessful, will also be eligible to purchase tickets during this sales window.

Official ticket and hotel packages for all Everton Premier League home fixtures are available via Sportsbreaks.com. Packages include an official match ticket in Block 204 or 206, plus a minimum one-night stay in a handpicked Liverpool hotel, including breakfast.

Official Premium experiences for all Everton Premier League home fixtures are available via seatunique.com

It is an offence under Section 166 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 for any unauthorised person to resell tickets. Further information regarding the Club's stance against Ticket Touting can be seen here.
