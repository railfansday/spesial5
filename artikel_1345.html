Florian Wirtz opens up on chats with ex-Liverpool boss Jurgen Klopp and thanks Andoni Iraola after starring role in Man United thrashing

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Published: 18:12 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 18:24 EST, 24 November 2026

Florian Wirtz has thanked Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola for sparking his resurgence and insists he never stopped believing in himself.

The German arrived with a big reputation when he completed a £116million move from Bayer Leverkusen but has been a constant punchbag for critics after failing to reach the heights that saw him win the Bundesliga title as the league’s star man.

Wirtz, though, has been much improved this year and put in his best performance for the Reds on Sunday in the 4-0 thumping of Manchester United And he insists Iraola – and old Anfield boss Jurgen Klopp – are the reason.

‘I am really enjoying playing under him,’ said Wirtz on Iraola, who replaced Arne Slot in the summer. ‘He is a really good coach. He has shown that his whole career as a manager.

‘He is bringing me back to the place where I was maybe two years ago where I played really good football.

‘I can feel that he gives me confidence and I am really happy about that. I am just trying to give him back what he gives me. I am really happy with that.’

Florian Wirtz has opened up on talking to ex-Liverpool boss Jurgen Klopp after finally hitting form for Liverpool

Wirtz won man of the match in Liverpool's 4-0 thrashing of Manchester United on Sunday

Wirtz has also had the added influence of Klopp, now Germany boss, in recent months. He said: ‘We always speak about Liverpool when we have the time to do so. He had a great time here. He is the coach who stayed here the most of his career.

‘He just tells me all the time that he loved his time here, the people love him here, he tries to give me some advice to get successful over here because he has shown how it is done.

'He knows a lot about this club, a lot of people here, he can give me some good advice.’

Wirtz added: ‘It's not about the people who talk about me. It's the goals I set myself and the performances I want to show for myself.

‘I always believed in myself. I knew my qualities and I know them now. Sometimes there are phases where you have to work even more to get the output, but I'm just enjoying the moment and really happy at the moment that it's going well. I'm happy with myself.’

Meanwhile, Cody Gakpo will miss the Merseyside derby with a calf problem and fellow Dutchman Ryan Gravenberch is also in a race against time to be fit for the trip to Everton due to a fascia issue. Neither have travelled to Belgium for Liverpool’s clash against Club Brugge.
