Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta says he is very happy with Kai Havertz and trusts club chief Andrea Berta to sort out a new contract for him.

The Germany international has been a key player since joining the Gunners from Chelsea back in the summer of 2023, scoring 40 goals in 125 appearances for the club and helping them win the Premier League title.

Still, there has not yet been much of an update on Havertz and a new contract with Arsenal, with the 27-year-old’s current deal set to expire in 2028.

That’s not exactly imminent, but it would still be ideal for Arsenal to get Havertz tied down to a longer contract so as to avoid any uncertainty by the time he has just a year left by next summer, which would lead to his market value tumbling.

“With Kai, again very very happy with him and what he’s doing, what he brings to the team,” Arteta said.

“In the right moment we will discuss these things. We haven’t discussed all the things that are coming now for a while.

“In the background, the club, ownership, Andrea in particular is very much into that and I leave it to him because I fully trust him.”
