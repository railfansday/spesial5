Noni Madueke was Arsenal’s star player against Newcastle United at St James’ Park.

I think it is the best I have seen him play for either England or Arsenal.

Eze gave the visitors a 33rd minute lead and it could have been even worse for the hosts as Arsenal were by far the better side in the first half.

We then saw one of those remarkable turnarounds that make football so special and bizarre, Matthias Jaissle making three subs at the break and when Barnes equalised it then saw Newcastle by a distance the better team in the second 45.

Malick Thiaw putting United ahead from a corner routine before a great run and goal from Noni Madueke saw the game finish 2-2, although Lukas Hornicek should probably have saved it even though the ball went through a defender’s legs.

Noni Madueke very honest when reflecting on the match: “A game of two halves is fair. I thought we were outstanding in the first half and should have gone in with a minimum three-goal lead. Then the second half we weren’t at our standard in the first 20 minutes and when you give this crowd and team belief, it’s difficult. In the end I think 2-2 is a fair result.”

Noni Madueke adding: “You know what to expect from Newcastle at home. The first 20 minutes of the second half is us not sticking by our own principles. I don’t know if we were too relaxed after our first-half performance.”

Noni Madueke on his goal and performance: “The manager has been on me about being ahead of the game, I gambled and tried to stay onside. When I got the ball with that space I knew I had to score. I feel good. There are still things I can improve on but I feel trust from my coach and team-mates.”
