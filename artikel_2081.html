Former Stoke City and Swansea City midfielder completes move to National League promotion hopefuls

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Worthing currently sit 6th in the National League table and are eyeing up promotion to the Football League for the first time in their history. Now, they have strengthened their midfield by signing former Stoke City and Swansea City star Sam Clucas, who brings a huge amount of experience to the team.

The 36-year-old was last contracted to Shrewsbury Town, where he played 34 league fixtures last season, scoring two goals and assisting five. He was an important player in the Shrews’ squad last campaign, but his services were not extended further following the summer.

Across his career, Clucas has played in the Premier League, Championship, League One and League Two, and has featured for a multitude of clubs. Hereford, Mansfield Town, Chesterfield, Hull City, Swansea City, Stoke City, Rotherham United, Oldham Athletic, Lincoln City, Shrewsbury Town and now Worthing are the clubs Clucas has represented.

The experienced EFL star will now prepare for life in the fifth tier, with the hope of bringing an historic promotion to the Reds. Worthing are currently in the play-offs, having lost just one league match in their last 11.

Clucas’ experience will be worth its weight in gold, and he has a clear hunger to deliver success to Worthing. “I can see the vision of the club, and I want to be a part of it,” he said in the club statement confirming his arrival.

The former Premier League footballer has plied his trade in the most competitive division in the world and will bring a calm and level head to the National League side, especially as the pressure ramps up later on in the season.

The ex-Stoke City and Swansea City man has already made his debut too. He came on off the bench on Saturday as the Rebels drew 1-1 with Scunthorpe United.

Clucas has penned a one-year deal with the non-league side, but could be offered longer terms if he impresses at the club. If the move proves to be a success, then Worthing will try to tie him down for longer and prolong his playing career.

But if the former Stoke City and Swansea City man fails to hit the heights expected of him, the Reds will not be hurt by his departure next summer, given his contract length.

He has shown he can still play plenty of games after featuring 40 times across the 2025/26 campaign for Shrewsbury Town. Clucas appears to have plenty left in the tank, and he will help to bring squad depth to Worthing as the busiest time of the footballing calendar nears.
