Car Keys in Apple Wallet coming soon to these popular vehicle brands

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Apple Wallet car keys are about to get a big expansion, with GM launching support for a variety of vehicles across three of its popular brands: Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC.

This fall, GM announced that its long-awaited Apple Wallet car key rollout was finally happening after first being mentioned by Apple in June 2025.

Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC vehicles are all gaining support for storing a digital car key in Apple Wallet.

Per the announcement, a very limited rollout started in late September and into October for select Cadillacs, but it will hit a lot more vehicles in December—including Chevrolet and GMC models.

GM also confirms that the rollout “will continue across additional GM vehicles over time.” It says when a vehicle adds car key support, customers will get notified in the ‘What’s New’ section of their vehicle’s app.
