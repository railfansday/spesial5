Globant: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Outcome-based pricing lifts gross margins about 10 points, so the shift matters even before revenue scales.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 20, 2026 at 3:12PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 19, 2026, Globant (GLOB +4.13%) announced that the annualized run rate of Glob.AI, its artificial intelligence platform, doubled in a single quarter to $106.6 million. The company is shifting away from billing traditional development hours toward outcome-based software delivery, which expands gross margins and locks in enterprise clients. Three things management said stand out.

Management raised its year-end revenue run rate target for Glob.AI to at least $130 million after client adoption accelerated. Software projects delivered through outcome-based AI pods generate gross margins roughly 10 percentage points higher than traditional IT billing.

Globant doubled its share repurchase authorization to $250 million through December 2027 to retire about 13.5% of outstanding shares. Management is funding the buyback using record year-to-date free cash flow of $125.9 million.

Mid-sized accounts using Glob.AI expanded revenue nearly 30% year over year as smaller enterprises adopt outcome-priced AI pods faster than legacy corporate clients. Across all client cohorts, accounts deploying Glob.AI grew 5.2% year over year.

Geopolitical friction in the Middle East continues to press on geographic growth, but client conversion into new software contracts is accelerating. Globant relies on deep enterprise accounts and high cash conversion to fuel its software delivery transformation.

To restore top-line momentum, management is aligning technical teams toward outcome-based contracts.
