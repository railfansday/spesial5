Four-Day Rom-Com Film Festival to Launch in Santa Barbara

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The inaugural event is set for April 22–25, 2027.

The genre is getting its own cinematic celebration with the inaugural Rom-Com Film Festival, launching in Santa Barbara April 22- 25, 2027. The event will bring together filmmakers and audiences for four days of toasting the classics and discovering new stories, after being founded by Carissa Buffel, Kevin Matusow and Kate Bacon of women-owned production company The Traveling Picture Show Company.

“Rom-coms make people smile. It’s really as simple as that. We wanted to create a festival that celebrates this beloved genre and brings people together in a lighthearted and happy way,” Bacon said in a statement. The weekend will feature screening Q&As, filmmaker conversations, talent appearances and panels bringing together directors, producers, screenwriters, actors and bestselling authors, as well as literary programming featuring wine tastings, speed dating, book signings, VIP events and interactive experiences.
