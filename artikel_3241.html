The 25 Best Black Friday Tech Deals — Save Up to 51% on Audio Gear, Gaming, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It’s no secret that in recent years, Black Friday deals have typically kicked off well before the Thanksgiving turkey has even started to thaw. This year is no exception — there are already plenty of early deals on every category imaginable, including clothing, subscription services, and even travel.

Tech is always one of the biggest categories, with rare chances to nab discounts on recently released gadgets. While Black Friday officially kicks off on Nov. 27, we’ve been scouring the web to find the best tech deals that you can already take advantage of.

From headphones and speakers to computers and TVs, these are the best early Black Friday deals you can grab now.

There are a huge number of earbud, speaker, and headphone deals, including savings on top brands like Apple and Sony.

You can save $100 on the latest pro version of Apple’s AirPods, which feature great noise-canceling and a built-in heart rate sensor.

Bose’s QuietComfort headphones are available starting at $200, a massive discount for the newest model.

Sony’s XM6 headphones are among our top picks for noise-canceling, and they got a modest Black Friday discount.

Edifier’s M90 speakers are among our favorite computer speakers thanks to their versatile connectivity options and audio quality.

Sonos is running some pretty serious Black Friday deals. The Era 100 SL is the more affordable version of the 100 speaker — the difference is that the 100 SL does not have a microphone for voice control.

TVs are historically one of the biggest Black Friday categories, and this year is no different. You can save on high-end and budget models alike.

Hisense’s 4K Mini-LED TV is discounted in numerous sizes by nearly half off.

Looking to go big? You can save on Samsung’s 83-inch OLED TV.

You can pick up this 55″ TV from Best Buy’s house brand for half off.

You don’t have to be a biohacker to want to keep tabs on your health — from fitness trackers to recovery tools, there’s plenty of great health gear on sale right now.

The latest-generation Apple Watch is available starting at $300, depending on the size and color.

There are more powerful massagers out there, but make no mistake — the Theragun Mini can deliver quick relief despite its compact size.

The latest Oura ring is $100 off. It’s the smallest smart ring, meaning it won’t get in the way of your workouts. Just bear in mind that to make the most of it, you’ll need a subscription.

The Galaxy Watch8, which is the previous generation, is discounted by $100 to $249.99 for the 40mm version. It features health and fitness tracking, including blood pressure, sleep tracking, and more.

Higher Dose’s red light therapy equipment is discounted during Black Friday, including this starter kit.

You can save on PCs and computer accessories as well.

The MacBook Neo is Apple’s more affordable, entry-level laptop, and it’s even more affordable during Black Friday. The $50 discount may not be a big one, but if you’ve had your eye on the Neo, now is likely the best time to buy.

More partial to PCs? The Zenbook from Asus features a Snapdragon X series processor and is $400 off right now.

Holding out for when Grand Theft Auto VI is available on PC? This gaming monitor is nearly half off on Samsung’s website.

If you want an all-in-one solution for your desktop, Lenovo’s A100 computer is $300 off. It includes a monitor with a built-in PC, plus a wired keyboard and mouse.

Don’t forget the accessories. From chargers to smart plugs, these tools will help you make the most of your gadgets.

A USB hub is a must-have for connecting laptops to monitors, and this one features inputs for USB-C, 4K HDMI, USB-A, and Ethernet cables.

Keep misplacing your keys? You can get one AirTag for less than $20 right now.

A smart plug is one of the best ways to start building a smart home; it can be used to control fans, humidifiers, coffee makers, and more either with a voice assistant or from the app.

Nimble’s power banks are pricier than the average phone charger, but they’re made from recycled plastic and have features like fast-charging and dual charging ports.

Gamers can save on controllers for the PlayStation and XBOX, while PC accessories are discounted too.

Need a new controller for your PS5? The DualSense controller is available in a wide range of colors, and it’s $20 off at multiple retailers.

You can get a small discount on the XBOX controller, which works with the series S or X versions of the console, as well as PCs.

Prefer PC gaming? Razer’s mechanical keyboard features RGB lighting and programmable keys. It’s discounted from multiple retailers.

This gaming headset features a USB-C dongle that makes it easy to switch between multiple devices, including consoles and desktops. It’s currently $50 off.
