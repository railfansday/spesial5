Madewell and The Six Bells Bring Storybook Charm to the Holidays

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Hudson Valley inn lends its old-world touch to the brand.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

There’s nothing quite like the holiday season to boost your sense of nostalgia for cozy moments at home—especially when they involve a touch of cold-weather whimsy. Whether you’re sipping on a hot coffee or tea as you watch the changing leaves or you’re curled up next to a warm fireplace with your latest read, the colder months of the year are primed for ample charm. Of course, these moments are not without their accoutrements, which is right where the new Madewell collaboration with The Six Bells—a country inn located in the Hudson Valley—comes in.

The Six Bells Inn, founded by Audrey Gelman, features interiors inspired by the imagined English village known as Barrow’s Green—complete with its own fictional characters and landmarks—giving the space an old-world touch that feels as if it’s been plucked right out of a storybook. It’s this distinct aesthetic, along with Gelman’s way of storytelling, that led to the inn’s collaboration with Madewell.

“Steven [Cateron, Madewell’s creative director,] and the team at Madewell understood our little world at Six Bells from the very first conversation we had. In the early stages of working together on this collaboration, we wanted this collection to feel like stepping through a doorway into another time where things feel a little more enchanted,” Gelman told ELLE.com in an email. “Every piece in the collection was designed to have that sense of wonder. We hope it brings the spirit of The Six Bells into people’s lives and homes this holiday season.”

With a range of apparel, accessories, and home décor—including vintage-inspired needlepoint ornaments and pillows, stockings, and toiletry bags featuring custom prints based on the inn’s interiors—the collection combines Madewell’s laid-back style with The Six Bells’s country charm. “For the first time, we’re really leaning into home accents, which let us extend [the Madewell] world beyond what you wear and into the spaces you live in,” Cateron said in an email. “The inn’s wallpapers and patterns were a huge source of inspiration, and you’ll find bits of them throughout the collection, right down to the lining of our Denim Trucker Jacket.” There are also pajama sets and even a nightgown for those who want to take one of summer’s biggest trends straight into the holiday season. And with prices ranging from $17 to $248, there are plenty of pieces ready for the gift-giving season (and no doubt, whoever receives these will be tickled to unwrap them).

The limited-edition, 23-piece collection officially launches on November 12 at 10 a.m.; however, ELLE.com readers have exclusive access to the collection now. Shop the collection below.
