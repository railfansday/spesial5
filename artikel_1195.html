Gattuso: Taylor ruled out of Torino clash, but Lazio ‘can’t settle for what we have’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Lazio coach Gennaro Gattuso says Kenneth Taylor will miss tomorrow’s game against Torino, but he has faith in Fisayo Dele-Bashiru: ‘We can’t settle for what we have. Inter have more points than they did at this stage last year, and that tells you a lot.’

Serie A table leaders Lazio visit Torino tomorrow, but the Biancocelesti will be forced to cope without Dutch midfielder Taylor.

“Taylor won’t be available. He had a problem in the second half against Lecce,” Gattuso said at a press conference via TMW.

“He’s had an MRI scan that showed some swelling, and he’ll have another on Monday; Tavares, Sutalo, and Marusic will as well. He won’t be available for the match, while the others, including Noslin and Zaccagni, are doing well.

“The system won’t change; we’ll continue on our pathway. We might pay a price in terms of build-up, but tomorrow’s game fits well with our characteristics,” Gattuso added.

“Nobody can give us what Taylor does, but we’ll do what we know. We’ll put in something more in terms of pace and physicality.”

The Lazio coach seems to have already picked a replacement for Taylor.

“I want to be honest. Dele-Bashiru deserved much more [playing time], and it’s only fair that he sometimes put on an angry face,” he said.

“I make decisions, but he’s improved and has qualities that fit the game. He must play without pressure and show his technical and physical qualities.

“We know the team we’ll face tomorrow. Second balls and transitions will be crucial.”

Tomorrow’s game will be a reunion between Gattuso and his ex-Milan teammate Ignazio Abate.

“I’m happy with what Ignazio is doing; I put him under my protective wing since he was a kid. I’m on really good terms with him. We Milan veterans left something in the young players, and I’m proud of it. He was a teammate, and I coached him too,” Gattuso said.

“I even played him at centre-back, and he’d leave San Siro to applause from the fans. To see where he is now and how he is growing as a coach makes me proud because I have great memories of him.”

Lazio are the Serie A table leaders and the only unbeaten team in the league alongside city rivals Roma.

“I hope to stay for as long as possible, but ultimately, what happens on the pitch will decide. It’s reductive to talk about the standings. I had a coach, Allegri, who is phenomenal with the numbers, whereas I’m not very good with them. I take things day by day, so all I know is that tomorrow we’re going into a difficult match where we need to improve our position in the table,” Gattuso said.

“We win, but the others win too. There’s a gap in the standings, and we can’t afford to make mistakes. I keep an eye on the table because when you’re having a season like this and the others keep getting results, you’re always right there

“It’s great to be up there with Roma; for Lazio and Roma fans, it’s something incredible. But right now, we just have to focus on improving our position—we can’t settle for what we have. Inter have more points than they did at this stage last year, and that tells you a lot.”

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
