13 Pairs of Gloves to Keep Your Digits Toasty All Winter Long

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Every product was carefully curated by an Esquire editor. We may earn a commission from these links. Here’s how we test products and why you should trust us.

Don’t overlook your cold-weather style practicalities.

There are few winter experiences more miserable than stepping outside with bare hands and realizing, too late, that your fingers have stopped working. First they’re freezing, then they’re numb, and before long your skin feels dry and rough and beyond the help of any hand lotion.

But honestly? Gloves can be annoying, too. Some turn a quick phone check into a whole production; others are so bulky you might as well be wearing oven mitts. The best winter gloves keep your hands warm without making your fingers useless—and look good while they’re at it. We’ve rounded up our favorites, from waterproof, windproof pairs built for rough weather to stylish options that still keep your hands nice and toasty. And yes, plenty have touchscreen-compatible fingertips so you can still call and text (and play the Wordle). See our favorite pairs below.

These luxurious leather gloves come from Overland, the brand behind some of our favorite coats. The smooth leather looks very polished, so these gloves can be dressed up if you want, but they go just as well with a casual outfit. They’re also quite durable and are naturally water-repellant, and the cashmere lining adds warmth on the inside.

Commerce Editor Luke Guillory loves these merino wool-lined deerskin gloves from Geier Gloves, a brand that has been making leather products in the U.S. for almost a century. Since the brand specializes in leather, you can bet the deerskin on these gloves is of the highest quality, and the wool lining is very nice, too. Geier also makes a lot of wor, driving, and motorcycle gloves, so you know they’re the real deal. The black and brown colorways look quite polished, but the natural and saddle leather options are also great.

If you want a nice luxurious pair of cashmere gloves, go with these ones from Sunspel. Lately, I’ve been wearing these gloves, which were knitted in Scotland from super soft pure cashmere. The brand has been perfecting the craft for over 150 years, so you can trust that these are the best of the best. It’s basically like enveloping your hands in a cozy blanket. For a full set, get the brand’s cashmere hat and scarf.

These Canada Goose gloves are basically the equivalent of wearing a puffer jacket on your hands. With heavy-duty, long rib-knit cuffs for extra protection and 750-fill-power down on the inside, your hands will stay warm and dry without their mobility being super impacted by something extra bulky. Note that these have a touchscreen-compatible index finger but not thumb.

Wool-blend gloves are as handsome as any of the very best sweaters in your rotation. Even though the outside is made from knit material, the insulated interior will keep your hands warm and dry during your next epic snowball fight. They’re also thin enough to layer under another pair of gloves if it’s really freezing outside.

When it comes to gloves, there’s one brand I’ve trusted for years, and that’s Smartwool. Their gloves are warm without being bulky, and mine have lasted for years. Having a warm pair of gloves that actually does its job well is even more important for my boyfriend, who has Raynaud’s and needs extra protection for his hands, and the gloves he relies on year after year? Also Smartwool. These new fleece gloves from the brand have extra insulation on the back to provide serious warmth, and they’re also functional with touchscreen-compatible pads on the thumbs and index fingers. Sure, they’re a little expensive for fleece gloves at $68—but if you don’t need the extra insulation, you can try the regular Active Fleece Gloves for $48.

If all you need is a solid pair of knit gloves and you’re not looking to spend a fortune, look no further than Uniqlo. The brand’s Heattech knit gloves are soft and warm and have effective touchscreen-compatible fingertips. They also come in 11 fun colors, so you might as well get a couple pairs for yourself—and they make a great holiday gift for literally anyone.

You already know we love Barbour’s waxed canvas jackets, so it’s no surprise we’re also a fan of the brand’s stylish, preppy gloves. These leather-palmed gloves have a cotton tartan face for a cool contrast and wool lining on the inside for extra warmth—and they’re waterproof, so you can break them out no matter what’s going on with the weather. Pair these with the Barbour Beaufort jacket for an iconic look.

Knit gloves are great when it comes to keeping warm, but they’re not the best when it’s raining or snowing. No one wants a pair of soaking gloves (that probably make your hands feel even colder). Enter these waterproof gloves from the brand that brought you the famous Bean Boots. With a waterproof nylon exterior and a down fill, they’ll keep rain and show out while keeping your hands warm. Note that some say the fingers are long, so consider that when choosing a size.

When you’re stuck in a blizzard or your location is prone to unpredictable weather, you’ll need something a little more heavy duty. REI’s weatherproof gore-tex gloves will give your hands the protection they need. They’re also essentially a two-in-one: The removable fleece liners can be used on their own or with the weatherproof outers as an extra layer of warmth.

Naadam’s famous cashmere sweater is one of our favorite pieces of winter clothing and one of our go-to gifts for the holidays, birthdays, Valentine’s Day, etc. The brand makes a matching pair of soft cashmere gloves, which come in 13 colors and are under $100. A genius gift idea? Get them the sweater with a matching pair of gloves, and they’ll wear both all winter long.

If you want that classic Sherlock Holmes leather glove look, you have lots of choices. But if you don’t want to spend a ton on a pair, get these $60 gloves from Quince. Not only do they have a nice leather exterior in either brown or black, they also have a cashmere lining on the inside.

On the high end of the high end, Gucci has a gorgeous luxury pair of leather gloves with the brand’s signature horsebit on the wrist for a fun little detail. Made from leather and silk, these are soft, smooth, and very stylish.
