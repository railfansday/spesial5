Rangers' McInnes wary of Frick's Aberdeen ahead of clash, live on Sky

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Rangers can move to within a point of Scottish Premiership leaders Celtic with a win on Tuesday night, Mario Frick led Aberdeen to a much-needed league win in his first game in charge at Kilmarnock; watch Rangers vs Aberdeen, live on Sky Sports from 7.15pm

Derek McInnes wants to strike the "fine balance" between consistency and using his squad during a hectic spell.

The Ibrox side are at home to Aberdeen on Tuesday night, live on Sky Sports, which is their game in hand over Scottish Premiership leaders Celtic.

A win would move them to within a point of their Old Firm rivals and give them the chance to move back to the top of the table on Saturday, for 24 hours at least.

Rangers have eight games between now and the end of December, including a League Cup final, as they bid to win their first piece of silverware for three years.

"You don't want to make too many changes when you're winning games because you'll be kicking yourself," McInnes told Sky Sports News.

"That's a fine balance. I think that it's important to keep these boys interested, to keep them fit for when they do go in as well, because training is one thing, but everybody wants to play and be available to play and start the weekend.

"Tomorrow night, we might look to freshen it up in one or two areas and then head to the game on Saturday for something similar."

Rangers are unbeaten in their last 11 Premiership games, a run that started with a 1-0 win away to Aberdeen in late August.

"We've not conceded too many goals in that run and we've been quite settled as a team, given to maybe two or three players coming in and out, and I think that's helped us.

"But I do think, getting into this one, we're in a good place, but we all know how quickly that can change. You've just got to be confident with our work, but not complacent.

"Hopefully we'll get our reward for a big performance tomorrow. I actually think we'll really need to play well tomorrow."

McInnes also led his side to a League Cup semi-final win against the Dons last month, but he is expecting a different challenge now Mario Frick is in charge.

"It's the third time we've played Aberdeen this season and it'll be the third different shape we come up against," the former Dons manager added.

"There are added challenges here, but we just need to really make sure we're as good as we can be and hopefully we can get the three points.

"With a new manager, the players have got a lot to prove to him and they'll be able to build on their win on the weekend.

"But for us, it's important that we concentrate on our levels and if we can get our levels, it'll give us a chance."

Frick delivered a much-needed win at Kilmarnock on his Aberdeen debut to lift them up to eighth in the table.

But, the Liechtensteiner is under no illusion about the different challenge that awaits them in Govan.

"It's another opponent with more quality than Kilmarnock, so we have to improve our defence, that's for sure," Frick told Sky Sports News.

"Their quality is also in transitions when they win the ball, they are very, very dangerous. So we have to improve.

"I'm pretty confident that we can show our game.

"We want to try to press them also, not only to wait them, and then without transitions I think it can be dangerous."
