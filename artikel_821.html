Stassi Schroeder Has Shady Reaction to Jax Taylor's Cancer Diagnosis

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Stassi Schroeder shared her reaction to ex Jax Taylor’s cancer diagnosis in an exclusive interview with E! News during People and InStyle’s Drive-By Party in Las Vegas.

Stassi Schroeder makes it a rule not to comment on her ex Jax Taylor.

Days after the The Valley alum—who dated Schroeder from 2010-2013—shared he was diagnosed with kidney cancer, the Vanderpump Villa host revealed her reaction to his health news.

“One of confusion,” Schroeder exclusively told E! News at People and InStyle’s Drive-By Party at the Las Vegas Grand Prix Nov. 20. “I'm not here to doubt somebody's diagnosis. I hope the best for everyone involved.”

As for whether she has a message for Taylor, she simply noted, “no.”

But Schroeder—whose relationship with Taylor was chronicled during their time on Vanderpump Rules—isn’t on the outs with all of her former costars. After all, Katie Maloney accompanied the You Can’t Have It All author to the event.

“Well, Katie Maloney is here with me right now,” Schroeder noted, “so her.”

And while the 38-year-old—who shares kids Hartford, 5, and Messer, 3, with husband Beau Clark—may be tight-lipped about where she stands with her fellow reality stars, she’s also not interested in clearing up any rumors floating around about them.

“You know what, let's just let them all live,” she said. “Let's let them all go and let everyone run with them.”

Taylor, 47, shared he was diagnosed with cancer after a tumor was discovered on his kidney during treatment for kidney stones in July.

“This is something I’ve kept private for the past few months because it’s been one of the scariest times of my life,” he wrote in a Nov. 19 Instagram post, which featured photos from his hospital bed and a video of him walking with the help of a walker. “What I thought was just a kidney stone turned into something I never imagined. My CT scan also showed a tumor on my left kidney. After many tests and appointments I found out it was cancer.”

Taylor—who shares 5-year-old son Cruz with estranged wife Brittany Cartwright—shared that the diagnosis was especially challenging given his father had died of cancer. As he said of the moment he learned of his diagnosis, “It broke me.”

As a part of his treatment, the reality TV star underwent surgery in which a source close to Taylor exclusively told E! News Nov. 19 that he’d experienced "some complications.”

However, the source noted that through it all, Taylor has had his girlfriend Lori Krebs by his side.

"The last few months have really taken a toll on him and she’s always been someone who brings him a sense of peace,” the source said. “They’ve been through a lot together and there’s a very deep love and connection between them."

The insider added, "They’re truly there for one another, and I think going through something like this has only made them realize just how much they mean to each other."

Much of Vanderpump Rules' first season, which debuted in 2013, focused on the drama surrounding Jax Taylor cheating on then-girlfriend Stassi Schroeder in Las Vegas. And after his hook-up with co-star Kristen Doute was revealed during season two, the couple broke up for good.

The VPR alum began dating the casting executive in 2017 while she was still on the show. After she was fired from the series in 2020, Schroeder and Beau Clark legally tied the knot in September of that year during the COVID-19 pandemic before welcoming daughter Hartford in January 2021.

The two later held a formal wedding in Italy in May 2022 and welcomed their second child, son Messer, in 2023.

After surviving a cheating scandal involving Taylor and their former co-star Faith Stowers, he and Cartwright wed in 2019 at a castle in her native Kentucky. Their wedding special aired on Bravo shortly after.

The pair welcomed their son Cruz in April 2021, a year after they walked away from the show.

Three weeks after the baby news, Kennedy and Meyers announced their engagement.

Tom Sandoval & Victoria Lee Robinson: Split

Nearly a year after Scandoval, the bar owner hard-launched his romance with a kiss, posting a February 2024 Instagram Story of model Victoria Lee Robinson laying one on his cheek. "We work very well, we have similar goals and it feels like we could really grow together," he noted to Us Weekly the following year. "I feel so fortunate and lucky to have her in my life. She’s been my rock and stuck by me."

But their relationship shifted in June 2026 when Sandoval was granted a temporary restraining order against Robinson over allegations that she attacked him during an argument that led to her arrest, according to court documents obtained by E! News.

"I have filed my own legal action," Robinson said in her own statement, "because I have my own account of what happened. I cannot allow a false narrative to control my future." Both sides are due to return to court in August 2026.
