Premier League news: Moyes demands more from Grealish, Iraola wary of United threat

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Everton’s playmaker returns after being stood down, while Liverpool’s head coach keeps focus for Anfield showdown

David Moyes has said he wants Jack Grealish to produce more for Everton as he prepares to reintegrate the playmaker into the squad.

Grealish has missed Everton’s past four games mainly for disciplinary reasons having failed to report for training before last month’s Carabao Cup win over Newcastle. His last appearance for the team was in the 2-0 loss to Chelsea on 17 October but he is fit and available for Monday’s visit to Brentford.

“He has served his punishment and we will treat him as we were treating him before,” the Everton manager said. “We will get on with things. The team is doing really well at the moment and what I don’t want to do is turn it into a Jack story because it shouldn’t be.”

The 31-year-old played in a behind-closed-doors fixture against Derby last week andalso apologised on social media for being unable to help the team, who won three of four matches in his absence. Grealish, however, had struggled to replicate last season’s form impact when joining on loan from Manchester City prior to his breach of discipline. And Moyes is demanding more.

He added: “Jack can offer us a lot. I want more from him, I want him to show more than he has done. I want him to give me more assists, more goals. There’s lots of things about him which we need and he’s good at retaining the ball, giving us good possession, carrying. So we hope that he can get back to those levels and give us what we need. I think this time last year he was probably sitting on five or six assists so we’re hoping to get that again.”

The Everton manager has backed Jordan Pickford to respond strongly to the threat from James Trafford to his England place, following after the Leeds goalkeeper’s inclusion and impressive display in the Nations League win over Spain.

“I don’t think it will have a great effect on him,” claimed Moyes. “If you’re playing for England you will always have other players chasing you. I still think Jordan Pickford is out there on his own at the moment. If anything, he’s getting better. It will be good for us now that he’s getting competition [with England].” Andy Hunter

Andoni Iraola has said Liverpool must disregard the latest turmoil at Manchester United and treat Sunday’s visitors like the team that thrived under Michael Carrick last season.

Sunday will be the first time since March 1937 that United have faced Liverpool in a league game at Anfield while languishing in the relegation zone. Mauricio Pochettino could start his United reign bottom of the table should results on Saturday go against his new club.

Iraola never lost in six league meetings with United as Bournemouth head coach. Before his first taste of the fixture with Liverpool, he insisted his north-west rivals are in a false position.

“United finished third in the table last season. You are looking at a squad with exactly the same players. They had very, very good results, especially in the second half of the season,” Iraola said. “Now we have just started, it is just 10 games. For sure, they will be a lot higher in the table [soon]. We have to rate them like this and the truth is the reference we have is that they finished third.

“We have to look at ourselves because we need the points. We need to improve our home form. It is also a big game in terms of relevance. It’s Liverpool v Manchester United, this is the biggest one in English football, so we want to play well and we want to win.”

Iraola was boosted this week by the returns of Conor Bradley and Giovanni Leoni to training after serious knee injuries, although the two defenders are not yet ready for consideration. “We think they will have two weeks on and off with the group,” he said. “Then it is going to be a matter of whether we organise something behind closed doors, 11 v 11, or they straight away play with the under-21s. Before they start playing with us they have to go through that.”

Liverpool’s other long-term absentee, Hugo Ekitiké, remains on course to return in January from an achilles injury. Andy Hunter

Xabi Alonso has admitted Chelsea’s injury list is a concern. The club have a lighter schedule after their failure to qualify for Europe but they are struggling to keep players fit and have a host of selection issues before Saturday’s home game against Leeds.

The Brazil striker João Pedro is yet to return from a thigh injury, Reece James is unlikely to recover from knee surgery until the new year and the France centre-back Maxence Lacroix is out with a long-term hamstring injury. Alonso is having to adjust. Jorrel Hato is unavailable in defence with a back injury and the England Under-21 winger Jamie Gittens, who has made five league starts for Chelsea since joining for £51.5m in the summer of 2025, has been out since September.

“It’s a concern,” Alonso said of the injuries. “Sometimes you can analyse them and find a reason but sometimes it is difficult to find a reason.”

Alonso has made 24 changes to his lineup in his first 10 league games. “It depends on the context and adaptability. I adapt to the circumstances. With the injuries you don’t have a choice. Once you have the whole menu, you can select what you like and I know what I like from the menu.”

Chelsea have failed to score in three of the four league games João Pedro has missed this season. They have also had to monitor Moisés Caicedo closely, with the midfielder minding a muscle issue. But Alonso wants his side to become less reliant on Caicedo and João Pedro.

“We want to have a complete squad,” he said. “That’s the goal of any manager. We have built a good squad. We are a better team with them. But it’s not about making a big drama when someone is out.” Jacob Steinberg

Enzo Maresca has said he may need to tweak the balance of Manchester City’s midfield amid their faltering Premier League form.

City went into the November international break on the back of three consecutive defeats in the league, having begun the campaign with seven wins on the bounce. Maresca’s side are third in the table, six points off leaders Arsenal, and face Fulham at the Etihad Stadium on Saturday.

Maresca defended Enzo Fernández, whose performances have prompted debate over his best position and whether he leaves City’s team unbalanced. Maresca has mostly opted to play the £125m signing from Chelsea deeper in midfield alongside fellow summer recruit Elliot Anderson, with the more defensive-minded Ayyoub Bouaddi, who also arrived in the summer, largely a substitute.

“For sure there are things we need to do better in terms of balance,” Maresca said. “But the same midfield that won seven games in a row is the same midfield that lost three games in a row.

“We won games with Enzo, we lost games with Enzo. I know that when there is a defeat, we are all looking for the reason why. Sometimes we probably need more balance, we need different kinds of players. [It] depends a little bit on the game. The idea is to continue in attack in the same way, creating chances, but finding a balance when we defend.”

Maresca also dismissed the notion that any uncertainty surrounding City’s financial case with the Premier League has negatively affected recent performances. It has been nearly two months since City were found guilty of more than 100 charges related to breaches of the Premier League’s financial rules, and they are waiting for the outcome of their appeal. All three of City’s league defeats have come since the ruling.

“I don’t think the players are thinking too much about the noise,” Maresca said. “I don’t think they are worried about it.” Dominic Booth
