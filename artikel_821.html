Williams to join temporary Swansea coaching team

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Former captain Ashley Williams is set to join a temporary Swansea City coaching team as the Championship club attempt to fill the void left by the departure of Vitor Matos.

Williams, 42, is poised to work alongside fellow ex-Swans Kristian O'Leary and Leon Britton as part of interim coaching staff.

Matos left his role as head coach to take charge at Strasbourg on Wednesday after just under a year in charge at Swansea.

That leaves coaches O'Leary and Britton as the only remaining members of Matos' management team.

Swansea have therefore asked Williams to help out as they prepare for Saturday's home league game against West Bromwich Albion (15:00 GMT).
