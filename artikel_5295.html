Circle Internet Group Inc. (NYSE: CRCL) stock has entered a critical phase after falling nearly 44% from its $159.47 52-week high, but the long-term growth story remains intact. The pullback has brought CRCL stock back into focus as stablecoin adoption, USDC circulation and digital-asset payments continue to expand. With Circle building a broader financial infrastructure around USDC, investors are looking beyond the current correction toward its next growth cycle.

Can CRCL reclaim $150 and deliver stronger long-term returns as the stablecoin market scales? Here’s our Circle stock price prediction for 2026–2040.

Circle Internet Group is positioning itself beyond a single stablecoin product, building a broader digital financial infrastructure around USDC. Its growth strategy centers on expanding stablecoin adoption, payments, blockchain infrastructure and institutional use cases. With these businesses scaling alongside the wider shift toward on-chain finance, CRCL’s long-term valuation will depend on how effectively Circle converts USDC’s growing ecosystem into sustainable revenue and earnings growth.

Circle is increasingly being valued as a stablecoin and digital-financial infrastructure platform, rather than simply a USDC issuer. The core investment thesis rests on the continued expansion of USDC, rising transaction activity and Circle’s ability to capture greater economic value as stablecoins move deeper into payments, settlement and institutional finance. Q2 2026 showed USDC circulation at $73.3 billion, while transaction volume reached $14.8 trillion, highlighting the scale of the network already being built.

The next phase of growth will depend on monetization and ecosystem expansion. Circle Payments Network, Arc, institutional blockchain applications and additional financial products could diversify revenue beyond reserve income and increase the strategic value of Circle’s infrastructure. The company’s expanding institutional footprint also strengthens the case for USDC becoming a more embedded settlement layer across global finance.

For the long-term CRCL stock price prediction, the key metric is therefore not simply USDC circulation but Circle’s ability to convert that scale into sustainable revenue, operating leverage and higher-margin platform economics. If adoption continues accelerating while Circle successfully expands its ecosystem, the current valuation reset could provide the foundation for a stronger multi-year growth cycle

Circle stock is facing renewed selling pressure after failing to sustain its September recovery near $100–$105. The stock has continued to form lower highs and lower lows, falling toward $67.88 and approaching its major demand zone around $55–$65. The recent decline indicates that sellers remain in control, with the stock struggling to establish a sustainable recovery.

The $60–$65 support zone is now critical. A sustained hold above this region could help CRCL stabilize and attempt a rebound toward $75–$80, followed by stronger resistance around $85–$90. However, a breakdown below $60 could expose the stock to further downside toward $50–$55. On the upside, reclaiming $80 would be the first meaningful sign of improving momentum, while the broader supply zone remains around $135–$145

CoinPedia expects that CRCL stock may reach $135-$140 by 2026, while maintaining support above $80 is crucial for the bullish structure to remain intact.

Circle reported better-than-expected third-quarter 2026 results on November 4, with earnings of $0.84 per share, compared with analysts’ expectations of $0.38. Revenue and reserve income reached approximately $865 million, up 17% year over year, while average USDC circulation increased 8% to $73.3 billion.

However, the earnings beat came with some underlying concerns. Reserve income declined 8% to $658 million as lower reserve yields weighed on the business. Circle also disclosed a workforce reduction of approximately 5%, while $182 million in ARC token presale revenue contributed significantly to other revenue. These factors have raised questions about the sustainability of earnings growth.

Circle Internet Group’s long-term valuation is increasingly tied to whether it can turn USDC adoption into a broader payments and financial infrastructure business. The stock remains highly sensitive to stablecoin circulation and reserve economics, but the next phase of growth will depend on transaction activity, subscription and services revenue, institutional adoption and Circle’s ability to expand beyond crypto trading.

CoinPedia’s CRCL stock price prediction combines fundamental valuation, business growth prospects, technical market structure, and broader stablecoin-market dynamics rather than relying solely on historical price movements. The framework evaluates whether Circle’s expanding USDC ecosystem, revenue diversification, institutional adoption and operating performance can support a higher valuation across different investment horizons.

Our analysis focuses on the factors most directly connected to Circle’s future earnings potential, including USDC circulation growth, reserve income, subscription and services revenue, payments activity, institutional adoption, and expansion of stablecoin infrastructure. The pace at which Circle converts growing USDC usage into diversified, recurring revenue is central to the long-term CRCL investment thesis.

Competitive Position & Industry Leadership

CRCL’s valuation is assessed against Circle’s position in the rapidly developing stablecoin and digital-payments industry. We consider USDC adoption, network effects, partnerships, regulatory positioning, product expansion and Circle’s ability to capture a larger share of global on-chain payments and financial infrastructure.

The forecast incorporates price trends, support and resistance zones, trading volume, momentum indicators, moving averages and major chart patterns. Because Circle’s economics are closely linked to USDC reserves and the broader financial system, the model also considers interest-rate expectations, stablecoin regulation, crypto-market liquidity, institutional capital flows and risk sentiment. Changes in these variables can materially affect both Circle’s earnings outlook and the valuation multiple investors assign to CRCL.

The final CRCL stock price prediction weighs these factors together to establish bearish, base-case and bullish valuation paths. The objective is to identify the price range that could be supported if Circle continues expanding USDC adoption, diversifying revenue and strengthening its position in global digital financial infrastructure.

CRCL is moving into a phase where USDC scale, revenue diversification and operating leverage could determine the stock’s next major valuation cycle. Circle’s opportunity extends beyond stablecoin issuance: deeper adoption across payments, financial markets and institutional infrastructure could create multiple growth engines as digital dollars become more integrated into the global financial system.

The near-term path will remain sensitive to interest rates, regulation, crypto-market liquidity and valuation expectations. But if Circle continues expanding USDC circulation while growing higher-value services and transaction-based revenue, the company could build a stronger earnings profile and justify a higher long-term valuation.

For the CRCL stock price prediction through 2030 and beyond, the decisive factor is execution. Sustained USDC adoption combined with improving revenue quality could shift CRCL from a high-growth stablecoin proxy into a broader digital-financial infrastructure play, creating the potential for substantial upside if the company captures a meaningful share of the expanding stablecoin economy.
