Analyst Sees $12 AVAX After Multi-Trillion Deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Home » Altcoins » Analyst Sees $12 AVAX After Multi-Trillion Deal

Ava Labs empowers top financial companies to launch tailored networks through the Ava Cloud service.

The New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) picked Avalanche’s (AVAX) technology for their new, upcoming tokenized securities platform, immediately shuffling the cards with a 10% uptick for AVAX. Whilst the broader crypto market is testifying to massive sell-offs, AVAX became the top gainer among TOP 100 largest crypto currencies by market capitalization.

The new platform combines NYSE’s existing trade-matching technology with Avalanche for instant settlement, supports stablecoin funding, and targets a Q4 launch pending regulatory approvals. NYSE will use Avalanche’s technology stack for core clearing and as a settlement layer. If the tokenized securities field grows into multiple trillions, a huge liquidity boost is coming.

View from the @NYSE floor: pic.twitter.com/9lhHuWSSK0

Now, charterers are floating double-digit AVAX price targets, levels unprecedented since mid-October, 2026. Avalanche’s price is testing the lower support zone around $8 to $8.20, analyst Gopal notes in one of his latest price analyses. Weakening selling pressure hints at a stabilizing structure, but that doesn’t necessarily mean a bounce back rally is immediate.

First, the $9 resistance level comes into focus: a bullish reversal is likely if the altcoin secures that range. Secondly, Avalanche’s price (AVAX) needs to clear the upper band of the Bollinger Band (BOLL) channel. Sustaining above the Red-Label Bollinger Band at $9.29 on the daily time-frame would put Avalanche’s (AVAX) bulls back in the driving seat.

Whilst the Bull Bear Power (BBP) scales haven’t touched the green side since mid-October, the current one-day AVAX price charts present a stabilized setup with the BBP gradually nearing equilibrium. A push below the mathematically-significant $9.29 resistance level would require greater buyer strength & a more consistent trading volume on Spot markets.

Tokenized securities is a swiftly growing niche in blockchain technology. The New York Stock Exchange’s decision could pull in trillions of dollars for the global crypto markets.
