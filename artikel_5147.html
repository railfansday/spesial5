Cardano Faces a Crucial Turning Point Amid Treasury Rejection, Putting $22M ADA Proposal to Rest

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Cardano (ADA) is edging closer to a crucial technical level that could determine its next major move.

According to market analyst Lana Valentis, ADA is nearing the midline of a descending channel on the weekly timeframe, a level seen as a turning point because a successful breakout could open the door to a stronger recovery.

Nevertheless, another rejection could propel the cryptocurrency’s downward trajectory. Per CoinGecko data, ADA is trading at $0.1523, with the descending channel’s midline presenting a notable test.

Why are network fundamentals offering an optimistic foundation? Well, Valentis acknowledges that more than half of all ADA is staked, with the blockchain recording over 10,000 transactions daily and generating approximately $58,000 in daily network revenue.

Therefore, these figures point to continued ecosystem participation despite challenging market conditions.

Cardano’s governance community recently rejected a proposal to spend 22 million ADA from the treasury to purchase and tokenize copper.

Therefore, this overwhelming opposition shows the community’s willingness to scrutinize major spending decisions and maintain control over treasury allocations.

Cardano has also attracted attention after founder Charles Hoskinson credited the network’s staking model since the Binance hack left its infrastructure unaffected.

Hoskinson pointed to Cardano’s early focus on emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence, liquid non-custodial staking and Layer-2 solutions built around its Extended UTXO (EUTXO) architecture.

As reported by ZyCrypto, the hacking incident highlighted the potential advantages of Cardano’s staking design, which lets users delegate ADA without transferring custody of their holdings.
