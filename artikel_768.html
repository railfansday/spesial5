SLOMW's Whitney Leavitt Is Starring Her Own Reality Show on Hulu

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives alum Whitney Leavitt is set to star in her own reality series six months following her exit from the Hulu show after five seasons, according to her rep.

Whitney Leavitt’s next move isn’t a secret anymore.

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives alum is set to return to the small screen in a new Hulu reality series that follows her life with husband Conner Leavitt and their kids Sedona, 7, Liam, 4, and Billy, 2.

Whitney’s rep confirmed the upcoming series to E! News, sharing that the 33-year-old and her husband will be producing the series through their Leavitt Media House.

E! News has also reached out to Hulu for comment but has not heard back.

Whitney had previously teased the possibility of a new reality series, telling E! News in June, "We're going to be working on a lot of unscripted and scripted.”

"We're definitely in the development of an unscripted show right now,” she hinted, “so I'm really excited to be able to share more about it, so we'll see. You'll find out soon."

And the Broadway star was open about wanting to create her own content, especially after launching her production company earlier this year.

"SECRET’S OUT!!!!!!" she wrote on Instagram in May. "‘Leavitt Media House’ Production has officially launched and we’re working on something big!!!!!!!!!"

She also knew fans were interested in continuing to follow every aspect of her life, captioning a follow-up Instagram post, “Y’all keep asking for a Leavitt reality show. Careful what you wish for."

Whitney announced her decision to leave SLOMW after five seasons in May, revealing the news during a performance of Chicago on Broadway.

"What is that?" she said, pointing to an open newspaper. “Let me see. 'Whitney Leavitt Announces She's Leaving The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives.'"

As for why she was stepping away from the series?

“I wouldn’t be where I am without it,” she told The Hollywood Reporter in March, “but it feels like it’s time to challenge myself in other ways and fulfill these dreams and passions that I’ve been trying to get even before the show.”

Though Whitney said she “wouldn’t be” where she is with the MomTok group she created with costars Mayci Neeley, Taylor Frankie Paul, Layla Taylor, Demi Engemann, Jessi Draper, Jen Affleck and Mikayla Matthews, she reiterated, “Maybe it’s time for me to branch out in other areas."
