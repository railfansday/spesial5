Metcalf denies poking DeJean in the eyes as NFL rules out suspension

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

DK Metcalf was ejected from the Pittsburgh Steelers' 19-16 loss to the Philadelphia Eagles after appearing to reach into Cooper DeJean's facemask and poke him in the eyes; the NFL will not suspend the receiver but he could be fined, according to sources

Pittsburgh Steelers wide receiver DK Metcalf will not be suspended by the NFL for appearing to poke Philadelphia Eagles defensive back Cooper DeJean in the eyes but he could still be fined, according to sources.

Metcalf denied that he intentionally stuck his fingers in the Eagles defender's facemask when addressing the media on Monday.

Having been ejected from a game for the third time in his career during the second half of the Steelers' 19-16 loss on Sunday, Metcalf initially said that he "couldn't remember" the incident, before backtracking and saying that he was trying to send a message to DeJean to "stop pushing me".

Metcalf had caught only one pass for two yards before being sent to the locker room after being flagged twice for personal fouls following a couple of confrontations with DeJean.

The NFL will review Metcalf's actions during the game for potential fines as part of its standard disciplinary review process, ESPN and NFL Network reported, but he escapes a suspension.

The 28-year-old was banned for two games last season for swiping at a fan in Detroit and was shown the door twice earlier in his career while playing for the Seattle Seahawks.

Asked why this keeps happening, Metcalf told reporters: "I think you got to ask the people that seem to do that to me".

Metcalf noted that opponents trying to get under his skin is nothing new, saying that San Francisco 49ers players attempted to do that regularly during the Seahawks' frequent meetings with their NFC West rivals.

The two-time Pro Bowler, who is on pace for career lows in receptions and yards receiving this season, declined to blame the heated interaction on frustration, pointing out that the Steelers are "winning", though Pittsburgh (6-4) has dropped two straight after losing in Philadelphia.

Steelers coach Mike McCarthy called Metcalf's actions "unacceptable" but said he needed more information before deciding whether to take any action.

Metcalf did not appear to be worried about being disciplined by the Steelers or the NFL. "You've got to ask [Pittsburgh general manager] Omar [Khan] that, or anybody else other than DeKaylin," Metcalf said, using his given name.

The highest-paid wide receiver in team history also added that the Steelers need to "score points when we get the ball and not stall on third down or get a turnover on downs, stay on the field so our defense can stay off the field".

Pittsburgh remain firmly in playoff position as Thanksgiving looms but face a daunting stretch over the next three weeks that includes a pair of meeting with first-placed teams in their division - the Denver Broncos (8-2) and Jacksonville Jaguars (7-3), as well as a key clash with fellow AFC wildcard contenders the Houston Texans (4-6).
