Manchester won't always be blue - Pochettino

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Mauricio Pochettino's first Premier League game as Chelsea boss was against Liverpool - as will be the case in his new job

Mauricio Pochettino says there is no time to waste as he sets out to restore Manchester United to being the city's most successful club.

The Argentine arrived to begin his new role as United manager on Wednesday after agreeing a two-and-a-half-year contract.

The USA beat Haiti 2-0 in Cincinnati in his final game in charge on Tuesday, after which he spoke to TNT Sports., external

Analyst DaMarcus Beasley, who spent the 2006-07 season on loan with Manchester City, joked that "Manchester is blue, it will always be blue".

Pochettino replied: "No way, you will see. Give me time and that is going to change."

The former Chelsea, Tottenham and Southampton boss flew directly to Manchester on a private jet after the game and landed at about 11:30 GMT before heading to the club's Carrington training ground.

Pochettino's first game in charge is on Sunday, when United travel to face rivals Liverpool at Anfield (16:30 GMT).

"I have plenty of energy. It's a privilege to be head coach of Manchester United. And we are not wasting time. We need to be ready to compete on Sunday," he said in his first interview to the club's in-house media team.

"Now it's not time to think about rest, it's not time to think about sleep.

"It's about being ready and to prepare everything to be sure that we are going to compete in the best condition at Anfield."

United are 18th in the Premier League having won just once this term, with Liverpool 14 places and 10 points above them.

"We know that it is not a situation that deserves to be at a club like Manchester United and for the fanbase," Pochettino added.

"We're excited because the new challenge is big, but it's the adrenaline that you want to feel," he said.

"We are going to have a different job but we have the experience to deal with that. We need to add our common sense in the way that we are going to approach all the things."

Tuesday's win ensured the US advanced to the Concacaf Nations League semi-finals and that Pochettino finished with a 62.16% win ratio from 37 games in charge.

Mauricio Pochettino became head coach of the USA men's national team in September 2024

The 54-year-old led the team to the 2025 Gold Cup final and the last 16 of the 2026 World Cup, when the US were co-hosts.

"I am proud of what we achieved," Pochettino said in a statement on Instagram., external

"When we arrived, I wanted us to believe that we could compete with anyone. I hope today that belief is stronger, not only with the players but with everyone who supports this team.

"This experience has re-energised me and given me so much, both in football and personally.

"Leaving is tough at this moment, but sometimes in football an opportunity comes that is impossible to say no to. I have always believed in following my heart, even when it means leaving a place where I have been very happy.

"I hope this team continues to grow and achieves all the success it deserves."
