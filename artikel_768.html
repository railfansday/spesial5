Khloe Kardashian Says Lamar Odom Is "Dead” to Her After Documentary

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Khloe Kardashian said during the Nov. 12 episode of The Kardashians that Lamar Odom has burned his final bridge with her after his comments in his Untold documentary.

Some things, you just don’t come back from.

And for Khloe Kardashian, her ex-husband Lamar Odom’s characterization of their relationship in his Untold Netflix documentary was a bridge too far.

The topic is brought up in the Nov. 12 epsiode of The Kardashians by Scott Disick, who asked, “Hey, did you hear what Lamar said about this new documentary?”

The question prompted Kardashian’s BFF Malika Haqq to note, “I wanted to get his ass.”

ICYMI, in Untold: The Death & Life of Lamar Odom, Odom alleges he only married Kardashian to further his own fame and downplays his ex-wife’s role in ensuring his health and safety in the wake of his near-fatal overdose in 2015—claims that struck deep for Kardashian.

“I don’t need you to thank me, just don’t talk about me,” she said during a confessional during the Kardashians episode. “Don’t discredit me.”

As Kardashian—mom to kids True, 8, and Tatum, 4, with ex Tristan Thompson—put it, “The bridge is burned and there’s no more rope to throw over, so he is dead to me.”

More than anything, the fact Kardashian willingly participated in the documentary, only to have her ex make disparaging comments was the bitter-tasting icing on the cake.

“I’m watching the documentary and I’m like, ‘Wait, I took time out of my life to sit here and do a favor for you,’” the True American founder said in the confessional. “I’m not being paid. I’m doing this ‘cause I’m trying to be nice. And there’s so many things that I just found really distasteful.”

Among them, “saying that you weren’t really in love with me,” Kardashian explained. “You married me just so you could be more popular in pop culture, all these things, which is so weird. Even if that is your truth, why don’t you say it without me being on the other side of these interviews? Like, I just thought that was really, really lame.”

For his part, Odom—dad to three with ex Liza Morales—has since pointed out it was one of his children who specifically alleged he only married Kardashian for the fame, though in Untold he admits to wanting to marry her after seeing the reality star’s family “lifestyle.”

Nevertheless, Kardashian said she has no more patience for such energy.

“This just solidifies what liars, con artists, cheats, thieves, trolls that men are,” she said. “And I just don’t want to be involved. Play with yourself, don’t play with me.”
