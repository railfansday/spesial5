Bears stun Lions with TD in final seconds of NFL Thanksgiving epic

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Detroit Lions lead for 53 minutes and 31 seconds, stretching their advantage to 11 points midway through final quarter after Jahmyr Gibbs' third TD from 70 yards out; Rome Odunze scores twice, including game-winner, to clinch Chicago Bears comeback that secures top spot in NFC North

The Chicago Bears scored the game-winning touchdown with just three seconds remaining to secure a stunning 39-35 comeback win over the Detroit Lions on Thanksgiving and claim first place in the NFC North.

Rome Odunze's second TD - a sensational toe-tap grab in the back of the end zone - clinched the victory and deny Detroit, who had led by 11 earlier in the fourth quarter after Jahmyr Gibbs ran in his third score of the game from 70 yards out.

Chicago's lead secured right at the death was the first lead change in a game they've been involved with since they trailed the Carolina Panthers 7-0 in the first quarter of their opening game of the season. They had trailed the Lions for 53 minutes and 31 seconds of this match.

The Bears' dramatic win nudges them into solo first in the fiercely-fought NFC North, at 9-2 on the season, while the Lions drop to 8-3 with a first loss in seven.

It also improves Chicago's record in the division to 1-2, with Detroit now 2-1, which could prove crucial should any potential playoff tiebreakers be necessary to separate the two teams - who meet again on the penultimate week of the season - or the Minnesota Vikings (7-3) and Green Bay Packers (6-5).

The game was initially slow to ignite, with only a Lions field goal to show from the first quarter, before Gibbs' first score to start the second lit the blue touchpaper.

The Lions continued to lead thereafter, until the Bears came roaring back in the final quarter. Caleb Williams firstly located Odunze deep down the field for a 41-yard TD, the QB ran in the resulting two-point play to cut the score to three, before Jared Goff threw an interception on the next series.

Chicago turned the ball over on downs, failing to convert on 4th and 3 just inside Detroit territory, but they got the ball back with just over two minutes to go, and this time marched down the field before Williams linked up with Odunze again for the game winner with seconds remaining.

Williams finished with 345 yards passing and those two scoring strikes to Odunze, while Bears running back D'Andre Swift had 162 yards from scrimmage and a touchdown. Gibbs had 20 carries for a massive 205 yards and three TDs for the Lions but it didn't prove to be enough.

After the Lions' first quarter field goal, they failed to add further points on the back of a Colston Loveland fumble, but they would find the end zone early in the second - Gibbs from down at the goal line - as the Bears offense was slow to fire.

Chicago finally sparked into life in the second and tied the game up at 10-10 with the first of two Kyle Monangai scores.

Detroit were back in front by the half, up 19-10, after a bizarre sequence saw them first awarded a safety as a Bears player deliberately batted a fumbled football out of the end zone, before Goff found Sam LaPorta for an 11-yard TD.

A second Gibbs touchdown was sandwiched in between scores for Monangai and Swift in the third quarter, while Jake Bates' second field goal put the Lions 29-24 up early in the fourth.

Gibbs only added to that advantage as he completed his hat-trick of TDs with the best of the bunch, breaking loose from 70 yards out but the game was far from over, with Willams and Odunze answering back almost immediately.

After Chicago failed to capitalise on Goff's interception, Detroit missed the chance to ice the game when opting against a 50-yard field goal try as they went for it on 4th and 4 from the Bears 33-yard-line.

Stopped short, Chicago got the ball back to pave the way for Williams and Odunze's dramatic, game-winning connection in the closing seconds.
