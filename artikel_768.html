Could Jason & Travis Kelce Judge ‘DWTS’? Taylor Swift’s Backup Dancer Thinks It’d Be ‘Incredible’

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Jan Ravnik wants to make it happen, even though the brothers know "nothing about dance."

Taylor Swift‘s Eras Tour backup dancer Jan Ravnik, who’s currently competing as a pro on Dancing With the Stars, has an idea for a celebrity guest judge that would bring the show to new heights.

While speaking to Entertainment Weekly in a clip posted Wednesday (Nov. 11), Ravnik gave it some thought when asked who his “dream” guest judge would be before bursting out laughing when the perfect idea struck him. “You know what would be cool?” he said. “If there would be the Kelce brothers here, one of them.”

“They would be incredible,” he added of his famous former employer’s husband, Travis Kelce, and her brother-in-law, Jason Kelce. “They would know nothing about dance, but somehow judge great.”

The ball is in the New Heights co-hosts’ court now, but for the record, the Kelce brothers got moves. Travis has been known to bust a groove while celebrating touchdowns at Kansas City Chiefs games, and Jason proved he’s a one-of-a-kind dancer in 2024 while getting down before a Monday Night Football broadcast.

Swift herself has appeared on DWTS a number of times, though not as a judge. In addition to performing a few different times on earlier seasons, she sent a video message cheering on the contestants in 2023 as they took part in a special Taylor Swift Night featuring Eras choreographer Mandy Moore as a guest judge — not to mention the countless other times competitors have danced to the 14-time Grammy winner’s music over the course of the show’s 35 seasons.
