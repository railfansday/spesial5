PSG's Doue to have surgery on ruptured ACL

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Desire Doue has scored six goals in all competitions for PSG this season

Paris St-Germain forward Desire Doue will have surgery later this month after rupturing the anterior cruciate ligament in his right knee.

The 21-year-old was in tears as he was taken off on a stretcher towards the end of the first half in the Ligue 1 champions' 3-2 victory over Nice on Saturday night.

Doue, who has arguably been PSG's best player this season, twisted awkwardly and went down under no contact.

The match was stopped for five minutes as Doue - who was visibly in pain - received treatment before he was applauded off by the crowd at the Allianz Riviera Stadium.

It is a huge set-back for the France attacker, who missed 13 games in 2025-26 with calf and hamstring injuries.

The European champions have been plagued with injury issues so far this season and are already without Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, who sustained an ACL injury of his own against Barcelona in October.

PSG return to action on Wednesday, when they host Roma in the Champions League.
