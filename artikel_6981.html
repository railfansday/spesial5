Escalate complex technical issues to appropriate IT teams.

Maintain accurate records of incidents, resolutions, and troubleshooting procedures.

Provide remote assistance using remote desktop support tools.

Follow IT security policies and maintain data confidentiality.

2–6 years of experience as a Help Desk Analyst, Service Desk Analyst, or IT Support Specialist.

Strong knowledge of Windows operating systems and basic macOS troubleshooting.

Experience supporting Microsoft 365, Outlook, Teams, and Office applications.

Understanding of networking fundamentals, TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, and VPN.

Knowledge of Active Directory and user account administration.

Experience with ticketing systems such as ServiceNow, Jira, Zendesk, or Freshservice.

Familiarity with remote desktop and technical troubleshooting tools.

Strong verbal and written communication skills.

Excellent customer service and problem-solving abilities.

Ability to manage multiple support requests in a remote work environment.

Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field.

Experience with ITIL-based service management processes.

Familiarity with Microsoft Azure and Microsoft Entra ID.

Basic knowledge of endpoint security and IT asset management.

Experience supporting geographically distributed teams.

Professional development and certification support.
