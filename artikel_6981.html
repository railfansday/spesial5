DOL flips independent contractor switch back with new final rule

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Secretary of Labor Keith Sonderling said the agency’s renewed “economic reality” test would provide greater clarity to both workers and employers.

The rule marks a return, of sorts, to the first Trump administration’s 2021 independent contractor final rule. But DOL Wage and Hour Administrator Andrew Rogers said Thursday that the new regulations add “modifications and enhancements” in an effort to create a “streamlined” worker classification analysis.

Those changes include a clarification that the agency’s test applies to the FLSA as well as the Family and Medical Leave Act and the Migrant and Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection Act, since both use the same definition of “employ” that the FLSA does, per DOL. But the test doesn’t alter assessments under other federal laws, like the Internal Revenue Code or the National Labor Relations Act, Rogers said.

DOL also included 14 separate “fact-specific” examples that illustrate how the rule would be applied to different occupations, such as delivery workers and truck drivers.

“While courts will have the ultimate say, the final rule and its illustrative examples should aid employers — particularly small employers — in making decisions regarding the proper classification of workers,” James Plunkett, shareholder at Ogletree Deakins, said in an email to HR Dive.

The final rule is effective 60 days from the date of publication in the Federal Register.
