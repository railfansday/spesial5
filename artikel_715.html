Awards Season Calendar (Updated Nov. 18, 2026): The road to the Oscars is a marathon, not a sprint.

Variety’s Awards Season Calendar is your definitive guide to the 2026–2027 race, charting the key moments, milestones and must-know dates that shape the most competitive season in Hollywood. From eligibility deadlines and nominations to final voting windows, this page tracks every event building toward the 99th Academy Awards on March 14, 2027.

As the industry leader in awards coverage, predictions and analysis, Variety delivers the most comprehensive breakdown of the season, spanning the Oscars, Emmys, Grammys and Tonys. You’ll also find essential dates and ceremonies from the Golden Globes, the Actor Awards (formerly the SAG Awards), the Producers Guild of America (PGA), the Directors Guild of America (DGA) and more.

All major dates and events are highlighted in bold for easy reference. Bookmark this page and check back often, as new entries and updates will be added throughout the season. All dates are subject to change.
