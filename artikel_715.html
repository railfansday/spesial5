Will Packer Launches Microdrama Studio With Backing From NBCUniversal, HarbourView

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The venture, called June, will produce vertical video series aimed at Black viewers.

Producer Will Packer is getting into the microdrama business.

Packer and his son, Dom Packer, are launching a venture called June that will develop, produce, finance and acquire original vertical video series aimed at Black audiences. June also has backing from NBCUniversal and venture capital firm HarbourView Equity Partners.

Will Packer will be CEO of the new company, and Dom Packer is president, with a focus on strategy and operations.

“I’ve always believed in telling authentic stories that meet audiences where they are, and with June, we’re building a home for those stories alongside Sherrese Clarke, her expert team at HarbourView and my longtime trusted partners at NBCUniversal,” said Will Packer. “The name captures the warmth and romance of summer and celebrates Black freedom and culture through Juneteenth and Black Music Month. That spirit of joy, connection and pride is at the heart of what we’re creating.”

Packer has a standing relationship with NBCUniversal. Under first-look deals with both Universal Pictures and Universal Television, he has produced films and series including Girls Trip, You, Me & Tuscany, Night School and Fight Night: The Million Dollar Heist.

HarbourView specializes in investments in media and entertainment. It has stakes in Fifth Season and Lion Forge Entertainment and owns portions of the music catalogs of artists including Kelly Clarkson, Brad Paisley, Chaka Khan and the estate of Quincy Jones, among others.

“Vertical entertainment offers an intimacy and immediacy that can make culturally resonant storytelling especially powerful,” said Sherrese Clarke, founder and CEO of HarbourView Equity Partners. “Will and Dom’s vision for June puts the experiences and perspectives of an audience that has helped shape music, entertainment, and culture around the world at the heart of this category, and HarbourView is proud to help bring it to life, empowering a studio that can create stories and connect them to the brands that want to reach this audience.”

Added NBCU Entertainment chief business officer Liz Jenkins, “Will has a seasoned instinct for stories that resonate with audiences and June is an opportunity to pair that creative vision with a format built for how viewers are engaging today. Our strategy isn’t about chasing a format. We always start in the same place: with the story, the storyteller and the audience. This is exactly the kind of creator-first partnership we want to build, working with storytellers to evolve thoughtfully, learn quickly, and aim to define the future of mobile-native storytelling.”
