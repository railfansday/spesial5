11 IKEA Kitchen Finds Designers Swear By in Their Own Homes

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

These finds deliver big style without breaking the budget.

Every item on this page was hand-picked by a House Beautiful editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Interior designers always know where to source the “good stuff,” and over the years we’ve gotten them to divulge their favorite secret spots for furniture and decor. The selections run the gamut from luxurious to affordable, showing that as long as you have an eye for design, even a budget-friendly pick can feel like a million bucks.

One affordable spot all designers keep coming back to is, unsurprisingly, IKEA. You can find anything you need at the Swedish retailer, often at unbeatable prices. We’ve already spoken with designers about what they generally like to buy from the brand, so this time around we’re getting more granular: we asked several pros what they like to buy for their kitchen, specifically.

Why this space? Well, our team is personally obsessed with this area of the home, and if you didn’t know, IKEA has an expansive array of kitchen products. From glassware and tableware to built-ins that make your cookspace look custom, IKEA has just about everything you need for your kitchen. Whether you’re looking for shopping inspiration or want to pick up designer-approved picks, check out what our favorite experts are buying from IKEA ahead.

“I use simple white dinnerware from IKEA at my own vacation home. It’s basic enough for everyday meals and can be paired with beautiful linens, chargers, and glassware for entertaining.” —Diane Hemmersbaugh, founder of Theo & Co. Design

“Stainless steel is a beautiful foil for warm wood and richly veined stone—it has such a distinct way of bringing that contemporary feeling into a warm, cozy space. I love this bowl’s clean curve and the way its surface catches the light. I’d leave it on the counter filled with pears, alongside a vintage ceramic vessel. Mixing reflective and matte surfaces gives even a small grouping depth.” —Anu Jain, founder of Atelier Oleana

“I'm a big carafe girlie and love using them as a vase, for drinks, or pure decoration. The cork stopper gives this simple glass carafe a lovely tactile detail. I’d use it for water at the dining table, paired with colored glasses and patterned linens. I like combining an understated everyday object with pieces that have more pattern or history—that mix makes a table feel personal.” —Anu Jain of Atelier Oleana

“It can be tricky to add personality to a kitchen with all of the necessary hardscape in place (cabinetry, appliances, countertops). A rug is a great way to bring in some color and personality. Since most people have kitchen rugs, why not choose something stylish? I prefer flatwoven rugs that have texture and color variations in kitchen spaces so that they can be easily cleaned and can hide small spills or crumbs.” —Vicki Taylor of Tailored Spaces

“We make a lot of skirted tables with a Kallax bookshelf raised a few inches with legs, and the divided storage is sort of a secret bonus.” —Lilse McKenna, founder of Lilse McKenna Inc.

“A multi-purpose stool has a place in every kitchen. This unique and beautifully designed stool can serve as a boost to reach high-up items. It can function as a plant stand in a kitchen that gets a good amount of light. And it can be pulled into a living space if extra seating is needed.”—Vicki Taylor of Tailored Spaces

“For the perfect countertop hutch, I’d start with the SEKTION high cabinet. Paired with the EKOPING glass doors, it looks just like a custom built-in on a budget, and ENKOPING also comes in a matching drawer front. I’d choose the brown walnut finish for all of them, since I love incorporating darker wood tones into any kitchen. To finish it off, I’d always add a custom crown piece above so it looks truly built-in.” —Brooke Mennen-Talsma, founder of Brooke MT Interiors

“I really like IKEA’s dishtowels. The blue and white patterned Valvardad collection, in a linen-cotton blend, is classic-looking. It's pretty enough to hang in the kitchen when not in use!” —Diane Hemmersbaugh, founder of Theo & Co. Design

“The Gladelig dinnerware set has a handmade pottery feel to it with the contrasting edges. This collection feels expensive and adds a little organic touch to each meal.” —Sarah Fontanez, founder of Pomme House

“We are ordering these to use as wastebaskets because I love their jute products and, bonus, these come with a liner.” —Lilse McKenna, founder of Lilse McKenna Inc.

“The Lohals jute rugs are a great neutral option, and you cannot beat the price for anyone with dogs or little kids who is not ready to commit to a pricier rug option.” —Lilse McKenna, founder of Lilse McKenna Inc.
