Zac Affleck and Jen Affleck are divorcing after seven years of marriage, and Zac just hopped on TikTok to confirm that he learned about Jen filing via a TMZ report. The video features Jen filming content while postpartum, and Zac can be heard in the background criticizing her appearance and saying “your tits are sagging all over.”

Meanwhile, Zac said in his initial video, "I found out from a TMZ reporter about the filing. But regardless of that, this is something we've been talking about for a long time. Jen came to me about a year ago, after Dancing With the Stars, and told me she was unhappy and wanted a divorce. We got married so young, and it sucks because I really did feel like we had something pretty special when we got married.”

Zac ended his comments by saying he hopes to coparent his kids, explaining “I just hope that we can still give them a normal childhood, but as far as that goes, Jen and I are definitely on the same page of wanting to give them the best the best life we can.”
