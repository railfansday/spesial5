Applied Optoelectronics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

SUGAR LAND, Texas, Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Applied Optoelectronics, Inc. (NASDAQ: AAOI) ("AOI"), a leading provider of advanced optical and HFC networking products that power AI, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

"We are pleased to have generated record revenue for our sixth consecutive quarter and to have returned to GAAP profitability in Q3," said Dr. Thompson Lin, AOI's Founder, President and Chief Executive Officer. "We have made meaningful progress in scaling our manufacturing capabilities, particularly for our 800G and 1.6T products, to address demand for AI-related optical technologies that we believe is accelerating. We have recently secured a multi-year strategic agreement with a Tier 1 hyperscale datacenter customer, which we think provides us with improved long-term visibility and reinforces our confidence in the opportunity ahead as demand for high-bandwidth, energy-efficient optical connectivity products continues to grow."

"We delivered another solid quarter, including accelerating revenue growth and improving profitability, driven by robust demand and manufacturing expansion in both our datacenter and CATV businesses," said Dr. Stefan Murry, AOI's Chief Financial Officer and Chief Strategy Officer. "We currently have a total manufacturing equipment capacity approaching 330,000 units per month. We expect our 2027 capacity expansion plan in the US to take longer to complete due to longer than expected time to obtain necessary electrical power among other construction related issues. As we work through these construction issues, we now expect by the end of 2026 that we will have sufficient manufacturing equipment installed to produce over 500,000 pieces of 800G and 1.6 Tb products per month and we expect to grow our manufacturing capacity to over 1 million pieces of 800G and 1.6 Tb transceivers per month by the end of 2027. We remain focused on executing on our capacity expansion plans and believe we are well positioned to support the next phase of AI datacenter deployment."

Applied Optoelectronics, Inc. (AOI) is a leading developer and manufacturer of advanced optical and HFC networking products that serve as the critical building blocks for AI datacenters, CATV and broadband fiber access networks worldwide. AOI supplies these critical infrastructure products to tier-one customers across cloud computing, CATV broadband, telecommunications, and FTTH markets. The company has R&D facilities in Atlanta, GA, and engineering and manufacturing facilities at its corporate headquarters in Sugar Land, TX, as well as in Taipei, Taiwan and Ningbo, China. For additional information, visit www.ao-inc.com.
