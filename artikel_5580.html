BMW is adding South Korean battery makers to cut its reliance on China

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Samsung SDI will supply cells in Europe and LG Energy Solution in the U.S. for BMW's sixth-generation batteries from the late 2020s

BMW has contracted South Korean battery makers Samsung SDI and LG Energy Solution as additional cell suppliers for its sixth-generation high-voltage batteries, the company said on Monday.

Samsung SDI will supply BMW from its established plants in Europe, and LG Energy Solution will do so from its established plants in the U.S. Deliveries start in the late 2020s, BMW said. In Europe, the company will now source cells from Chinese suppliers CATL and EVE Energy, as well as Samsung SDI.

BMW said it applies a "local for local" principle, buying components close to where vehicles are built. The company said this shortens transport distances and increases supply security, and that a globally diversified supplier network supports resilience and flexibility.

“Our global partner network with leading cell manufacturers ensures that we have a resilient, flexible and competitive battery cell supply,” said Hendrik Lang, head of purchasing and quality of high-voltage batteries at BMW, in a statement.

BMW assembles its sixth-generation batteries at five new facilities built near its vehicle plants. They are in Irlbach-Straßkirchen in Lower Bavaria, Debrecen in Hungary, Shenyang in China, Woodruff in the U.S. and San Luis Potosí in Mexico, according to the company.

BMW is ramping up worldwide output of the batteries, which run the carmaker's latest lineup of electric vehicles, including the iX3 SUV, according to Bloomberg.

The move fits a wider effort among automakers in Europe and the U.S. to become less dependent on parts from Chinese suppliers, according to Bloomberg. China leads the world in making batteries and in refining the key materials that go into them, Bloomberg said, a position that leaves automakers in Europe and North America vulnerable when it comes to capacity near their factories.

In September, analysts and executives said U.S. government funding falls short of what is needed to reduce China's hold on the global battery supply chain. China accounts for roughly four-fifths of all battery cells made worldwide, according to the International Energy Agency.

BMW also completed a $1.7 billion investment in its South Carolina operations in June, including the Woodruff plant. Woodruff is to supply high-voltage batteries for at least six fully electric models that BMW plans to assemble in the U.S. by 2030.
