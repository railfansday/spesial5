Adria Arjona Toasts Her Women in Film Honor With Max Mara at the Chateau Marmont

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Onslaught actress brought ELLE along as she got ready for the meaningful evening.

On Wednesday evening, the Chateau Marmont was in full Hollywood mode as Max Mara gathered friends for a cocktail reception to toast Adria Arjona. The Onslaught actress was named this year’s Women in Film Max Mara Face of the Future, an award recognizing an actress’s achievements in film and television, community contributions, and embodiment of style. (Past honorees include Emily Blunt, Hailee Steinfeld, and Gemma Chan.) With the Women in Film Honors taking place the following night, the gathering offered an intimate start to a larger celebration of women shaping the screen industries.

For what Arjona called an “amazing honor,” she channeled minimal elegance in a sleek black Max Mara gown, its halter neckline bringing a touch of drama to the otherwise understated look. Her longtime partner, Jason Momoa, joined her for the evening, alongside guests including Jessica Alba, Shay Mitchell, Brittany Snow, Zoey Deutch, and Victoria Justice.

The timing feels fitting. Arjona’s recent work in Onslaught, where she plays an army veteran and single mother with PTSD, has expanded her range, and she’ll next appear in The Thomas Crown Affair in March. Away from the set, the Puerto Rican actress has made community work a long-standing commitment, supporting underserved women in downtown Los Angeles for more than a decade and children in Guatemala for 15 years.

From her beauty routine to the final look, plus a car ride filled with friends and family, Arjona brought ELLE behind the scenes as she prepared for the Chateau Marmont celebration.
