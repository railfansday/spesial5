In a world where we’re hunched over our computers from nine to five, and then slouched and scrolling from five to nine, good posture is a rarity. But that doesn’t mean we should just accept a fate of poor spinal alignment. Especially when there’s actually a really easy solution (that doesn’t include deleting TikTok from your iPhone).

Allow me to introduce you to the posture-correcting bra. Along with delivering superior support, the shapewear category uses stretchy fabrics with varying levels of tension to reposition your shoulders and help you breathe better. Taylor Swift even wore one while training for the Eras Tour—and hers alone is low-key life-changing.

Now, even the best posture bras aren't intended to fix serious medical issues on their own, so it's not a substitute for, say, a scoliosis brace. But if you're looking for a little extra help retraining your body to sit up straight with your head held high, these types of bras encourage small corrections in the right direction!

A posture-correcting bra works best alongside an ergonomic chair, Pilates classes, or a new mattress, rather than as a replacement for any of them. It also happens to be the smallest investment of the bunch, and editors like me are big fans. Keep reading for my top picks—including the one and only Taylor Swift-approved option.
