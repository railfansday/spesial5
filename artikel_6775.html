Discover the Best Dresses for Every November Occasion

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

All products featured on Vogue are independently selected by our editors. However, we may earn affiliate revenue on this article and commission when you buy something.

No matter the occasion or season, there are always plenty of opportunities to wear a dress and, of course, an overwhelming number of options from which to pick. We’ve put together an edit of the best dresses of the month; check back every two weeks for a new selection of styles, handpicked by Vogue.

November is here, calling for wardrobe reshuffles and a fresh look at the fall trends defining the season. Based on this month’s standout finds, the key elements are texture, color, and fabric. Art Deco influence appeared throughout the runway—there’s no better time than now to embrace the trend with drop-waist styles that fit the bill, as seen at Kallmeyer and Fforme. Cummerbunds, usually known for tuxedo dressing, have now found a feminine place, notably at Róhe. It’s also the season for autumnal textures in wool, velvet, jacquard, and knits—try a jacquard number from Tory Burch for an evening occasion, or pick up a sweater dress from Laura Manoogian to wear throughout the winter. And for all fall weddings and holiday festivities on the horizon, there are simply too many good options—from Maria McManus to Khaite—that will make you the best-dressed guest.

Whether you’re shopping for new essentials, vacation, or a destination wedding, these are November’s best dresses.

Cooler temperatures are no reason to retire color. Consider this your invitation to embrace it. Fforme’s elegant take on the evening dress features a dropped waist, mock neckline, and sleeveless silhouette—all in a rich berry hue.

What’s a fall wardrobe without a knit dress? This under-$300 find from Banana Republic makes for a go-to in any wardrobe. Style it with knee-high boots for effortless cold-weather dressing.

Tory Burch sent this printed dress—in a trompe l’oeil pattern resembling tiger stripes—down the runway in two iterations: a delicate pastel jacquard and a rich purple version. With a fitted bodice that cascades into a handkerchief hemline, it’s a romantic silhouette that needs little extra to feel styled. A close-cut jacket and patent pumps should do the trick.

A knit dress is always a good idea. Simkhai’s ribbed iteration, accented with a delicate white trim, makes a subtle yet striking statement. Add a knee-high boot, a sweeping opera coat, and a chic clutch.

A dress that’ll get you multiple double-takes. This red maxi with a built-in cummerbund reimagines traditional black tie dress codes for a feminine-meets-masculine twist.

ICYMI: Jacquard is having a moment. This black V-neck dress, accented with a cummerbund sash, brings a polished edge to evening dressing. Even better? It’s under $300.

Conner Ives can do no wrong. Spaghetti straps, delicate pleats, and a vibrant color story—the dress is composed of four different printed vintage scarves—make this a vacation must-have, whether you’re spending Thanksgiving in Nayarita or St. Barths.

The shift is a forever classic and Posse’s silk-satin take is minimalist in the best way. Bring that less is more ethos to accessorizing with a simple pouch and drop earrings.

Emily Dawn Long—a CFDA 2026 runner-up—has a knack for intuiting what we want to wear before we do. This bright color-blocked midi is just one recent example.

Consider this sleeveless, funnel-neck knit dress a blank canvas for styling. Layer it over or under a long-sleeve knit (for extra warmth), throw on a sweeping shearling coat, then finish with flat boots. The possibilities are endless for cold-weather dressing.

You know the LBD, you (we hope) love the LBD. Play with proportions to give it a fresh look: a long duster coat, statement brooch, and polished loafers toughen up its feminine spirit.

Why yes, it’s Kallmeyer’s fall collection opening look: a creamy drop-waist style made from a silk charmeuse with caped armholes and a high funnel-neck. Follow the fall show’s styling notes and pair it with patent leather knee-highs–a pulled-back bun and minimal jewelry give the dress its well-deserved moment.

Maria McManus first introduced this dress in an off-white shade; this rich eggplant hue makes for a wonderful autumnal option.

Khaite’s chiffon maxi strikes the perfect balance between delicate and wearable. Give it heft by styling with a leather coat, tights, and strappy sandals.

Weddings or galas on the horizon? This strapless satin gown—as seen on supermodel Karlie Kloss, who wore it earlier this year for Taylor Swift’s wedding—works for either occasion.

Minimal, elegant, and endlessly versatile, this long-sleeve satin number is the kind of wardrobe staple that moves easily from desk job to after-hours plans. Dress it up with pumps or keep things understated with a flat—it looks sophisticated however your style it.

Kallmeyer’s Ivette dress is easily one of the season’s most talked-about dresses. With Art Deco influences leading the trend conversation, its embellished design feels especially on-trend.

Feathers, feathers, and more feathers! Whether you’re dressing for a special occasion or are a bride-to-be, this Loulou de Saison dress is designed to make an entrance. The drop-waist silhouette, adorned with feather embellishments around the hips, delivers just the right amount of drama. More is more!

Print enthusiasts, look no further than this floral maxidress from Banana Republic. Rendered in a rich persimmon print, the silhouette skims the body before flaring into a sweeping skirt. A romantic take on fall florals.

J.Crew’s cult-favorite rollneck sweater has officially entered its dress era. Just in time for sweater weather, its simple silhouette makes a strong case for becoming your new go-to. Style it with hosiery and loafers, then tie a colorful knit around your for an extra layer.

ICYMI: Purple is the color to own this season, and this violet maxi from Zara features an alluring open-back silhouette. Not quite ready to bare it all? Layer a lightweight top underneath, either in a neutral hue or a contrasting pop of color.

Caftans can be worn year-round—you just have to pick one in a more fall-friendly fabric. Taller Marmo’s long-sleeve is cut from double satin, with adjustable buttons that allows you to wear it in more ways than one—off-the-shoulder, single-shoulder, or or with a boat neckline.

Velvet chartreuse has never looked better, courtesy of Reformation x Alexa Chung, whose recent collaboration delivered one of the finest evening dresses of the month. Pair it with a satin bag and kitten heels.

This Staud dress creates the illusion of separates: A fitted black silhouette contrasts with a white draped skirt. A study that proves that opposites really do attract.

Meryll Rogge’s debut collection for Marni has officially dropped. The color-block mini is cut from one of the collection’s most memorable prints: a playful floral print that captures the house’s signature eccentricity. Pair with loafers and a long necklace.

For a quiet statement, consider this tea-length dress from Merlette, crafted from a rich butter yellow satin with ruched sleeves. On chillier days, add a faux-fur coat on top.

Altuzarra’s flutter-sleeve dress balances romance and structure, with delicate draping that creates soft movement. Save this for a special occasion.

Straight from the runway, this sleeveless knit turtleneck is a master class in sculptural dressing, crafted from a stretch rib that contours the body and falls to a handkerchief hem.

Who needs a necklace when the dress is the jewelry? Brandon Maxwell turns a V-neck dress into a work of art, with a beaded neckline that moves like fringe. An elegant, embellished option for an evening affair.

No dress roundup is complete without a little black dress. This off-the-shoulder option from Fforme is cut from a thick scuba fabric for a sculpted look. Complete the idea with strappy sandals and an embellished evening bag.

This Tory Burch runway dress was an editor favorite. Cut from jacquard in a slate blue and cherry-check motif, the color combination creates an optical illusion of a rich purple hue (one of this season’s trending hues). Two versatile details make it especially wearable: Both the necktie and belt are removable. Complete the look with fall boots.

To celebrate Vogue’s book club selection Sense and Sensibility, Vogue collaborated on a limited-edition nightgown inspired by Jane Austen's heroines. A nightgown that isn't exclusive to home can be styled with a trench and equestrian-inspired boots for an effortlessly romantic look.

Simon Miller's take on the slip dress is both elegant and flattering. This dress works season after season; it's the kind of piece that always makes an entrance, whether paired with boots or sleek mules.

CJR is known for its bold use of color, and this dress is no exception. Inspired by the work of Ettore Sottsass, the graphic polo maxi makes for a statement for any occasion. Dress it up with pumps or style it with flat boots for the weekend.

For upcoming weddings or formal events, look to this artful Tove dress. Playful yet sophisticated, the halter-neck silhouette is elevated by sculptural detailing. Finish off with strappy sandals for a polished finish.

For a minidress to wear this fall, opt for Khaite's Scarla dress. The modern skater silhouette, cut from delicate silk chiffon, feels romantic yet contemporary, making it an especially chic option for a bride-to-be.

Call it the ultimate party dress. The holiday season may still be a little off, but it’s never too early to plan your party wardrobe. This embellished Posse mini will make you the best-dressed guest.

Zara has collaborated with filmmaker Luca Guadagnino and production designer Stefano Baisi on a collection spanning from ready-to-wear to homeware. This scarf-draped dress strikes the perfect balance of elegance and ease.

Minimal yet statement-making, this Magda Butrym gray dress has an off-the-shoulder neckline and a fitted silhouette. A tonal floral appliqué adds a feminine, sculptural finish. Pair it with kitten heels and a clutch for an elegant evening look.

When your wardrobe is craving a dose of color, look to yellow. This H&M Studio dress is cut from a lightweight fabric with romantic ruffle detailing, making it an effortless choice for a warm-weather holiday or a destination wedding.

For a fall dress, look to this minidress inspired by the ’60s. Cut from a wool blend, this double-breasted dress is an ideal layering piece. Style it with a colorful turtleneck or button-down and add tights and boots for a milder fall day, or pair it with loafers.

Alaïa's approach to construction is like no other, creating some of the most flattering silhouettes around. Cut from velvet, this tank dress makes for an understated yet impactful evening option—a minimalist statement.

Maygel Coronel’s dress proves that simplicity can still make an impact. The elongated drop waist, fluid draping, and bubble hem give the silhouette a fresh modern feel.

A fall wardrobe essential, this long-sleeve satin dress is a versatile piece that works from nine to nine. Polished enough for the office and elegant enough for an evening, it transitions seamlessly from day to night.

A black dress is a timeless choice, and this Altuzarra option interprets the style for modern wardrobes with draping and an asymmetric knotted waist. A chic choice for fall weddings: Just add a kitten heel and a clutch.

A little lace, or in this case a lot, goes a long way. This runway favorite from Khaite strikes the perfect balance between romantic and edgy. Ground it with sleek black shoes.

Violet! Purple! Amethyst! However you choose to call them, deep shades of purple are having a serious moment. Style with something red—a clutch or heels, or even a bold lip—for contrast.

Toteme makes the kind of dresses you’ll want to wear on repeat—and this draped maxi is one of them. New in for the season (we’ve written about it in cream, red, and butter yellow), this rich olive hue feels just right for fall.

Casual meets sophisticated in this fresh-off-the-runway Prada mini, featuring a drawstring waist and cultural motifs at the hem. Wear it over pants for an unexpected look.

Cut from a thick satin, Róhe’s sculptural one-shoulder dress contours the body just so, and needs little in the way of styling to feel complete; simply finish with a tassel clutch and kitten heels. Also available as a top, it’s equally chic styled as a full ensemble for an important event.

Color is all everyone can talk about this season—and chartreuse is one of the It hues. This Taller Marmo dress with an integrated cape makes for a striking wedding guest option.

The kind of dress Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy might have worn. This simple boatneck style from Zara is minimal yet endlessly versatile.

Tory Burch knows her way around a shirtdress, and this style–straight off the fall runway–comes with a removable necktie, allowing you to switch up the look depending on the occasion.

From weddings to galas, look to this Fforme drop waist dress in blue sapphire hue. Pair with strappy sandals and a sleek pouch bag.

&Other Stories’s pleated mock neck dress is an effortless style that can easily be transformed for any occasion. Pair it with blazers and loafers for the office or dress it up with pumps for evening events.

For a lace dress that’s feminine but not overly sweet, look no further than Maria McManus’s black satin midi. Style it with a button-down or cardigan knotted at the waist, then add an unexpected pop of color with either your shoes or your bag.

A rich blue hue and draped silhouette give this dress a modern take on the 1920s drop-waist silhouette. Dress it up with heels for evening or ground it with loafers for an uptown-meets-downtown approach.
