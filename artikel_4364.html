GTA 6: How to Get into Bank Vaults

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Adam Beck is a Canadian who has covered the gaming industry for over twenty years. He has a passion for RPGs, action, stealth and horror games.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is one of the most complex and grandiose open worlds there has ever been, and because of its name, there will be a bit of stealth. But it’s not cars you’re stealing, or at least not in this guide. Here, we are talking about Lucia and Jason finding their way into a higher payday, and to do that, they will need to rob some more advanced locations, specifically banks. With this guide, you’ll learn how to get into the vault, where all the money is kept.

So, you’re fed up with the cash from the drawers in banks and the small safes in the back rooms and want some of the bigger dough. Well, there’s only one way to do that: through the bank vaults.

Players already have a list of 61 cheats to look forward to.

Getting into a bank vault isn’t easy as it requires a lot of planning and work. Well, a little bit. Unfortunately, the first requirement in order to get into a bank vault will likely be disappointing to most people: you need to be at the end of Chapter 4. This means that you have done jobs for Yasmani in Vice City and have completed the VCIA Takedown mission, one of the biggest heists in the game.

After completing this, you will be given some time to cooldown and lie low. But for Jason and Lucia, that means finding more creative ways of robbing. Yasmani will contact you about a money truck coming around Little Cuba. Head to the Cuban Restaurant, Café Orquidea and wait. This will unlock the That’s Where the Money Is … quest.

Here you will have to tail a money truck, don't get too close until it reaches its destination: Lombank – Ocean Beach. This is one of the smaller banks in the area, having only medium security but a decent amount of take if you’re up to the challenge. Once both you and the money truck have parked, head inside and stick the place up. Head through the back door and rob the vault as it’s open thanks to the money truck making a pickup (or dropoff).

The challenging part is, because you robbed the vault, you now have four stars, which can be trouble to get away from. Thankfully, there is a dock nearby, and taking a high-speed boat out into the open waters has a greater chance of escape. Once you do, unfortunately, the island is now looking for you.

Thankfully, now that you have gotten your first taste of the vault, you’ll want more. Thankfully, Yasmani will now offer tips for when a security van is making a pickup or drop off, allowing you to time when to go shopping.

After that, all you need to do is call or visit Yasmani and you can take one of the money vaults in Vice City. Mind you, it will cost you $10,000 for the tip, but the take will be much larger. Just be prepared for some heavy gunfire.
