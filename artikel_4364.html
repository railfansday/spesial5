Bandai Namco Shuts Down Reflector Entertainment, Expects €21 Million in Losses

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Before joining the team at DualShockers, Lauren was a Features Writer at Adventure Gamers, a Guides Writer at Gameranx, as well as contributing as a News Writer at PCInvasion and Daily

Bandai Namco has announced that it is shutting down one of its studios, Reflector Entertainment. Perhaps one of the lesser-known studios under Bandai Namco’s wing, Reflector Entertainment has yet to show its potential.

The latest and only project by Reflector Entertainment is Unknown 9: Awakening, which rolled out in October 2024. It’s an action-adventure where you step into the shoes of Haroona, who is able to enter a mysterious dimension that overlaps our own, and she can channel numerous powers on her quest.

Tripwire Interactive has been hit with layoffs for a second time this year, but it has been confirmed that work continues on Killing Floor 3 and Maneater 2.

The game is just one of many parts of the IP’s narrative universe, as there are also stories told across podcasts, comics, novels, and web series.

Bandai Namco acquired Reflector Entertainment in 2020, and even though the studio is based in Canada, Reflector joined the family over at Bandai Namco Europe. At the time of the acquisition, the studio was already working on Unknown 9: Awakening. After Unknown 9: Awakening’s weak reception among fans and critics, Reflector Entertainment announced that 18% of its staff were being laid off, just one month after the game’s launch.

Unknown 9: Awakening sits at a Top Critic Average of 62 on OpenCritic, along with just 17% of critics recommending the game. Of course, that’s less than ideal, especially with the backing of Bandai Namco, who are known for delivering some of the biggest franchises, including Dark Souls, Code Vein, Dragon Ball video games, and so much more.

The reason given for the liquidation of Reflector Entertainment was that it came as part of a continuous review into The Group’s business portfolio, and “after comprehensively considering future business plans, necessary additional investment, and the possibility of profitability and business continuation, the Company has decided to discontinue Reflector’s business, dissolve and liquidate it.”

The statement clarifies that a concrete liquidation schedule “is undecided as of now. The Company will advance the necessary procedures according to local laws and regulations, and once said procedures are completed, the liquidation will be complete.”

The matter comes with huge losses for Bandai Namco, as they expect to “post an extraordinary loss of approximately 21 million euros (approx. 3.5 billion yen) on its consolidated financial statements for the third quarter (nine months) of the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027, and thereafter.” The final figure and timing remain under review, but the impact will be eye-watering, nevertheless.

It’s unknown how many employees are impacted by the closure of Reflector Entertainment, and at the time of writing, Reflector Entertainment has yet to comment on its liquidation.

Sadly, Reflector Entertainment joins a long list of studios to be shut down or hit with layoffs this year. As a result of the XBOX layoffs, for example, two separate agreements to sell off the creators behind Hellblade, Ninja Theory, fell through. As a result, the studio is facing a proposed closure.

Consultation with Arkane Lyon, the team behind Dishonored and the upcoming Marvel’s Blade game, remains underway and is expected to continue through the end of the year.
