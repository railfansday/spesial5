Remember when survival horror games were all about fixed camera angles, chunky polygonal characters, limited ammunition, and doors that took an unnecessarily long time to open?

Well, someone decided to bring all of that back with an unexpected project inspired by Zach Cregger's Resident Evil movie – and fans (including myself) are absolutely loving it!

Called Zach Cregger's RESIDENT EVIL: The Video Game, this unofficial fan project was created by the developer and game producer Stefano Cagnani, who already created similar projects in the past for classic films such as Alien, Dawn of the Dead, The Crow, and more. And you can play it for free on itch.io right now by checking out the project on this page!

It is currently available for PC and reimagines the director's movie as a classic PlayStation 1 survival horror experience. And honestly, if you're anything like me and have a soft spot for the franchise's early days, this might be right up your alley.

There are several Resident Evil titles that deserve to be remade after Resident Evil Veronica.

As its name suggests, the project takes the movie's premise and gives it a nostalgic makeover, complete with everything you'd expect from a late-'90s Resident Evil game. So yes, we are talking fixed camera angles, tank controls, low-poly graphics, dark environments, scarce ammunition, and even those iconic close-ups of doors opening as you move between areas.

However, don't expect to experience the entire movie just yet. The current version only covers its opening sequence, following the snowy road until the protagonist reaches the farm – but it already features the appearance of one of the movie's most terrifying creatures.

What makes this project so interesting is how lovingly it recreates the atmosphere of classic survival horror. It's a wonderful love letter not only to Cregger's movie (which finally broke the franchise's curse of not having a genuinely good live-action film – sorry, Milla Jovovich!), but also to the early Resident Evil games that made so many of us fall in love with the series.

If you are a fan of horror titles, there are plenty of old games that should be brought back, as they were something great.

Although the project was released a few days ago, it's only now starting to gain more attention, with fans sharing gameplay footage and streaming their experiences online. And judging by the reactions, Stefano might have something pretty special on his hands.

One player was already asking for more, commenting, "Full game needs to be released as soon as possible." Another praised its nostalgic atmosphere, saying, "This looks actually pretty sick. [It] takes me back to the 90s style of Resident Evil." Not everyone was simply celebrating the throwback, though. One particularly amusing comment praised the project while taking a little jab at the movie's humor: "did they remove the constant jokes and constant humor? If so, I'm sold!"

Well, I guess you can't please everyone! Regardless, it's lovely to see a fan project capturing the attention of Resident Evil fans, especially one that understands just how much atmosphere and a few well-placed camera angles can do for a survival horror experience. So, if you've been missing the days of wandering through dark corridors with barely enough ammunition to defend yourself, this free little project might be exactly what you need.
