Mile Svilar one step closer to return from injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Today, Mile Svilar will undergo a crucial check-up to permanently remove the brace on his right foot.

As reported by Il Messaggero, the nagging bone problem in his fifth toe can now be considered completely behind him, and the starting goalkeeper is ready to return to the Giallorossi goalposts to defend the club’s colors in the match at the Stadio Tardini against Parma.

The return of the number one and the possible recovery of Dybala are two key elements in the coach’s weekly work, as he intends to use every training session at Trigoria to restore the team’s peak performance and tactical fitness.
