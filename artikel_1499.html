Naples: new clash for Kevin De Bruyne, a departure under study

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

After two consecutive defeats and increasing pressure on the shoulders of Max Allegri, Naples saved the furniture against Serie A leader, Lazio Rome, by snatching a 1-1 draw at home. Enough for the Italian technician to be kept on the job, but the problem could come from elsewhere.

Indeed, according to Il MattinoNaples is considering a sensational departure from Kevin De Bruyne from the winter transfer window. Starting yesterday, the Belgian midfielder is still struggling to integrate, culminating in the argument with his teammate Lorenzo Lucca in the middle of the Champions League match against Porto. According to the Italian newspaper, this is not the only one with which De Bruyne has clashed.

Indeed, De Bruyne would also have encountered problems with striker Rasmus Hojlund and captain Di Lorenzo. Clearly, the Belgian player would not really be appreciated within the Neapolitan locker room. And on the ground, he continues to disappoint, despite some flashes. The former Manchester City player admitted his difficulties with Antonio Conte last season, and hoped for a better relationship with Allegri.

A little more convincing on a statistical level, with 4 goals on the clock and 4 starts in the Champions League, the 35-year-old player does not entirely satisfy his management, who is considering an amicable, and especially early, separation. Under contract until next June, De Bruyne could be encouraged to leave, between the desire to rejuvenate the workforce and save a salary of €6 million per year.
