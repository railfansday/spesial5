Plains All American Pipeline, L.P. (PAA -2.49%) reported third-quarter adjusted EBITDA attributable to PAA of $711 million, reflecting the company's strategic focus as a premier crude oil midstream provider following the divestiture of its Canadian natural gas liquids business. Management indicated that production growth in the Permian Basin is trending toward the high end of previous estimates, while the Silver Creek acquisition provides immediate scale in the Rockies platform. The company stated it is on track to deliver $50 million in organizational efficiencies and has captured $50 million in synergies from its Cactus III acquisition. The company reported that recent capital structure optimization, including the redemption of preferred securities, will provide meaningful accretion to distributable cash flow moving forward.
