Why Shares in SpaceX Popped Higher Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Orbital data centers just moved a step closer to reality.

By Lee Samaha – Nov 27, 2026 at 3:27PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Space Exploration Technologies ›

Shares in Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +4.83%) rose by 5% at 1 p.m. today as the market continued to digest a couple of positive news developments for the company. The first is CEO Elon Musk's pledge to give every child in the U.S. two SpaceX shares, one from the company and one from Musk personally. The second is the news that the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) granted SpaceX the authority to deploy 1 million data centers in space.

The plan to gift shares is not only a huge display of generosity by Musk, but also creates significant goodwill around the company and strengthens public awareness of what SpaceX is doing in the space and AI industries.

Those aims are also strengthened by the FCC announcement, which clears a path for orbital data centers that SpaceX's management believes it could deploy as early as 2028. The idea is that the cost of orbital data centers will inevitably fall as rocket technology becomes more productive. Furthermore, orbital data centers don't face the power bottlenecks that many data centers do right now.

Orbital data centers address rising real estate, construction, and power costs and avoid the permitting issues that terrestrial data centers are increasingly facing. In addition, since Musk believes the price of compute will rise, revenue from orbital data centers may increase while costs decrease.

It's a key part of SpaceX's long-term growth program, and the FCC decision is a positive result. Still, there are many question marks around their ultimate deployment, not least the key question of how many years orbital data centers will actually be productive, given the likelihood that they won't be serviceable. Something to think about.
