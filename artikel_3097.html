Musk-Free ETFs Arrive: Investing Without the Tesla Factor

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

XXSP is a new investment opportunity, but you'll want to factor in the potential cost.

Love him or loathe him, Elon Musk knows how to make a splash. By turning ambitious technical ideas into high-profile ventures, Musk attracts capital, talent, and public attention.

Given Musk's fame, it's natural for many investors to want in on the action. Still, others hesitate to invest in an exchange-traded fund (ETF) heavily exposed to Musk-linked companies due to fear of concentrated risk.

For example, while one investor may be drawn to the ARK Innovation ETF (ARKK -8.98%) because of its 11.2% holding in Tesla (TSLA -10.77%) and 6.05% holding in SpaceX (SPCX -9.05%) -- as of Nov. 2, 2026 -- another may worry that the fund leans too heavily on Musk companies.

If you worry about the volatility, valuations, and governance risks associated with these high-profile businesses, you're not alone. That's where the Subversive S&P 500 Redacted ETF (XXSP -4.14%) is finding its niche. XXSP seeks to provide broad large-cap U.S. equity exposure while excluding Musk-led companies. At least 80% of assets provide exposure to companies in the S&P 500.
