How to make a great video with an AI agent

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Today, anyone can describe a video in a sentence and get a finished one back from an AI video agent. Getting a good one still comes down to two things: knowing how to ask for it, and having a creative idea worth asking for.

If you run a small business or a small team, AI video agents are good news. You don’t need an editor, a designer or a big budget. You just need a clear message and some principles on how to make it work. Five principles make the biggest difference to what comes back after the prompt: set the goal and audience, bring your source material, say what viewers should do next, describe the look and feel in plain words, and treat the first version as a draft.

Start by setting the dials before you type anything, including the goal, audience and scope. The goal is what the video should achieve. The audience is who watches and what they already know. The scope is how long the video runs, how many topics it covers and how long each scene lasts. Favor a story arc over an infodump, with a problem, a tension and a resolution. When you write the prompt, describe it conversationally, like a mood board in words.

Most people have more raw material than they think. Give the agent the material you already have instead of asking it to guess. Good sources include a help-center article, a PDF manual, a slide deck, a product page or a launch blog post. The agent applies your brand guidelines, terminology and product details to the video, so you spend less time correcting facts afterwards.

Name the viewer and the action you want them to take. A video for a new hire and a video for a prospective customer should look and sound different, even when the facts are identical. State the next step, whether that is a sign-up, a booked call, a set of steps to follow or one thing to remember. The type of video also shapes the brief:

Use plain words, not design vocabulary. “Clean and calm, with lots of white space” or “upbeat and fast-paced, like a product launch” is enough to steer the result. A good place to add reassurance: if you can’t describe it in words, upload images that match the look and feel of what you’re going for. Share your brand colors, logo or past videos you like if you have them. Before you had a mood board in words but you can actually attach a mood board in images too. Business video has to look like your business, not like a generic template, and an AI video agent can only match a style it has seen.

Treat the first version as a draft and keep talking to the agent. The real advantage of working with an agent is that you can revise by conversation: “Make the opening shorter.” “Swap the second scene for a screenshot of the dashboard.” “Make the tone warmer.” Small, specific requests work better than “make it better.” Change one or two things at a time so you can see what each change did, and stop when the video says what you wanted to say.
