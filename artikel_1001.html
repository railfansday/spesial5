Rybakina and Sabalenka lead field at WTA Finals - live on Sky Sports

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The season-ending championship heads to Indian Wells for the first time, with new world No 1 Elena Rybakina leading the field along with Aryna Sabalenka and Coco Gauff; You can watch the WTA Finals live on Sky Sports from 8-15 November

Defending champion Elena Rybakina leads the field at Indian Wells for the WTA Finals - live on Sky Sports from 8-15 November.

The crown jewel of women's tennis takes place in Indian Wells, California with eight singles players and doubles teams having qualified in the Race to the Finals competing for tennis' ultimate prize.

Matches are played in a round-robin format between two groups of four, with the top two players or teams from each group moving on to the knockout stage.
