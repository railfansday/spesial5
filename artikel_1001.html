Children in South Asia and sub-Saharan Africa are less likely to receive two doses of measles vaccine than are children in other parts of the world. Credit: Yuri Cortez/AFP/Getty

Measles cases in the world’s children have been climbing since 2020, reversing a decades-long decline, according to a global study of measles infections, deaths and vaccine rates1. The study found that nearly 16 million children under the age of five caught measles in 2023, of whom 118,000 died.
