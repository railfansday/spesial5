Though a creator based in Turkey is dominating the Global Sub Top 50, India is still the most ubiquitous country in the ranking. 14 of the top 50 channels hail from that nation, and a few of them have discovered a science experiment that reliably delivers millions of views each day.

Want to feel old? This video arrived on YouTube more than two decades ago.

Once upon a time, the combination of Diet Coke and Mentos produced a viral online video trend. Millions of viewers tuned in as amateur scientists performed backyard experiments, turning ordinary soda bottles into towering fountains of bubbling spray.

The Diet Coke and Mentos trend has survived into the era of YouTube Shorts, but it’s not nearly the fad it once was. Instead, creators have found another combination that produces a terrific reaction: ENO antacids and water.

ENO tablets are similar to Alka-Seltzer, in that they are effervescent antacids capable of curing tummy troubles. The products differ in their active ingredients and the regions where they are most commonly consumed. ENO is a common drugstore find across South Asia.

Some Indian creators have figured out a trick that turns a humble ENO tablet into an improvised firecracker. By injecting water directly into ENO packages, those creators produce localized reactions that cause the packets to explode after a few seconds.

Here’s what makes the ENO videos extra intriguing: The channels uploading these clips are including shoppable links that viewers can use to pick up the tablets for themselves. It’s as if the Diet Coke and Mentos channels from back in the day had sold those products right there on the screen (which, let’s be honest, if YouTube had its shopping tools back then, there definitely would have been some in-app Mentos purchases going on).

The ENO trend’s commercial ties give it legs. Creators aren’t just encouraged to try out this experiment because it gets them views. They can also profit from it directly, and the homemade firecrackers only take a few seconds to set up.

And if you don’t live in a region where ENO tablets are available, well, maybe it’s time to bring back Diet Coke and Mentos. Everything old on the internet eventually becomes new again, and as one of YouTube’s oldest trends comes back around, the creators peddling it can even work in some sales, too.
