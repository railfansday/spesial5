Man calls police on his ex for taking back her Xbox account to play GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A woman said her ex-boyfriend called the police on her after he bought Grand Theft Auto 6 and she removed his access to her Xbox account, all so she could play.

Grand Theft Auto 6 was finally released on November 19, with an estimated 1.5 million people taking the day off in the United States so that they could experience Jason and Lucia’s story, as well as the wider state of Leonida.

However, shortly after release, a woman in Washington State revealed that her ex had called the police on her because she took her Xbox account back. In her TikTok, Ciara Benson noted that her ex, John, had access to her account for six years before she decided to lock him out.

“Went in my account, removed all devices, signed out of everywhere, changed my password, added a restriction, it’s mine,” she said. “You don’t want to pay for your son? That’s fine. I got a new game, bitch.”

Benson’s original TikTok has racked up close to 1 million views, but she also provided a follow-up too, revealing her run-in with police over it.

The TikToker said that her ex reported her for “credit card fraud,” which prompted officers to give her a call about it. “They did let me know that they did see the video I posted, so they were privy to the things going on,” she added.

Ciara also noted that she had removed all payment options from the account, seeing as they were not linked to her.

“He let me know that there was no evidence of a crime being committed that he could see, so they just gave me the incident report in case I need it. That was nice of them, very cool. The officier asked me how GTA was and if my son had played it.”

Plenty of viewers have seen the funny side of her story, and even shared similar situations of their own. “My ex-husband tried to add a World of Warcraft character in our divorce stipulations,” one said.

“911 is wild,” another added. “You should find someone that’s good at it. Let them play and send him updated screenshots of the game,” another joked.

Ciara said she hasn’t gotten “very far” with the missions just yet, but there is plenty of help out there for anyone struggling with GTA 6. If you need a fast car, there is a list of those, as well as the best weapons to use when you’re busting up some banks and stores for quick cash.
