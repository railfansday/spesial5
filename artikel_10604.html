Subway reveals Vice City sub for GTA 6, but there’s only one way to get it

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Subway is celebrating the launch of GTA 6 with a new Vice City Cubano sandwich, but fans hoping to try it will need to be in Miami to get their hands on one.

With Grand Theft Auto 6 finally launching on November 19, 2026, fast food chains are getting in on the excitement with special promotions inspired by Rockstar’s highly anticipated game.

Popeyes has already announced its Spice City Sandwich, featuring two crispy chicken fillets, while KFC in Australia is hosting a fake funeral to give fans an excuse to take the day off work.

Now, Subway is joining the celebrations with a sandwich inspired by Vice City, although there’s a catch.

According to an announcement shared with Dexerto, Subway has given its existing Cubano Pulled Pork Melt a GTA 6-inspired makeover, renaming it the Vice City Cubano.

The sandwich features hardwood-smoked pulled pork, Black Forest ham, melted provolone cheese, pickles, and mustard, all served on freshly baked artisan Italian bread.

It’s available in both six-inch and footlong sizes, but there’s one major restriction: you can only get it in Miami.

The Vice City Cubano is exclusive to the Florida city that inspired GTA 6‘s fictional setting, meaning fans elsewhere in the US won’t be able to order one under the promotional branding.

Those lucky enough to be in Miami can purchase the sandwich in-store, online, or through the Subway app, with delivery available for players who don’t want to put their controllers down.

Subway also showed off the sandwich in an Instagram post, adding to the growing number of food promotions surrounding the game’s release.

This isn’t the first time GTA 6 has taken over Miami’s food scene, either.

In October, Rockstar brought GTA’s iconic Burger Shot and Cluckin’ Bell restaurants to life at the Kaseya Center, complete with themed packaging and actual food.

The limited-edition merchandise proved so popular that fans began reselling items for hundreds of dollars.

Unlike Popeyes’ nationwide sandwich promotion, Subway’s Vice City Cubano is keeping things local, giving Miami residents another way to celebrate GTA 6’s long-awaited arrival.
