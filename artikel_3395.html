Scarlett Johansson’s New Crime Thriller Sets First Box Office Record Ahead Of Wide Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Paper Tiger is already a winner at the box office.

2026's Paper Tiger is a crime drama written and directed by James Gray that opened in theaters on November 13 following its world premiere at the 79th annual Cannes Film Festival. The movie is set in 1986 and follows two brothers played by two-time Oscar nominee Adam Driver (Star Wars: The Force Awakens) and Emmy nominee Miles Teller (Whiplash) getting involved with the Russian mob. Driver's fellow two-time Oscar nominee and Marriage Story co-star Scarlett Johansson (Avengers: Endgame) also stars as Hester Pearl, the wife of Teller's character, Irwin.

Per Box Office Mojo, Paper Tiger earned a 3-day gross of $215,009 at the domestic box office in its opening weekend, during which it was playing in just six theaters. This gives the movie a debut per-screen average of $35,834, which breaks James Gray's record by a wide margin. The writer-director's previous highest debut per-screen average at the domestic box office was his 2016 movie The Lost City of Z, which debuted with a per-screen average of $27,543.

The Lost City of Z was previously the only James Grey movie to have a per-screen average of more than $15,000 during its debut. Because the scale of the releases of James Gray movies has been widely variable, this record is not necessarily a strong predictor of how Paper Tiger will perform in the long term. For instance, while The Lost City of Z is the previous record-holder, its overall worldwide haul is limited because it never played in more than 900 theaters at the domestic box office in a single weekend. Meanwhile, Ad Astra, which is the director's highest-grossing overall, opened in wide release on more than 3,000 screens.

However, this early record could indicate that Paper Tiger may be James Gray's second movie since We Own the Night to pass the $50 million milestone worldwide. Should that be the case, it will be a solid return on investment, as the movie's reported budget is just $15 million, which most likely places its estimated break-even point somewhere around $37.5 million.

It could potentially perform even better than We Own the Night. Both titles are star-studded crime dramas (the 2007 title starred Joaquin Phoenix, Mark Wahlberg, and Eva Mendes), but We Own the Night had mixed-to-negative reviews, earning a Rotten Tomatoes splat thanks to a 57% score. Meanwhile, Paper Tiger reviews have been glowing, earning the new movie a Certified Fresh score of 85%.

Another factor that could help Paper Tiger is its Oscar buzz. Should it become a strong contender in the 2026-2027 awards season (potentially earning third nominations for Adam Driver or Scarlett Johansson, a first nomination for Miles Teller, or a first nomination for James Gray), its theatrical release could have strong play in early 2027, adding to its revenue long after its initial wide expansion.
