This GTA 6 Moment Is A Trolling Master Class From Rockstar

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

GTA 6 fans are keeping the spirit of Looney Tunes alive via accidental car crashes

Have you ever seen those classic Looney Tunes cartoons where the murderous Wile E. Coyote dashes after the infuriating Road Runner? Usually, a high-speed chase ends with an unexpected comic twist, such as a bundle of dynamite exploding at the wrong time. Perhaps the most famous gag from these iconic animated shorts​​​​​​​ is the one where the plotting Coyote is tricked into running headfirst into a canyon wall painted to look like the entry to a tunnel.

Some people are currently learning that the silly gag can be more convincing than they thought, especially in a video game as hyperrealistic as Grand Theft Auto 6.

Since the release of Rockstar's open-world game, social media has filled up with clips​​​​​​​ of players running down a street at high speed. Maybe they're in the middle of a police chase, or maybe they're just not paying much attention. The high-speed drive ends in misfortune when the player realizes that the area in front of them isn't a street at all — it's a mural​​​​​​​ that looks like the natural continuation of the street.

Street art like this is common in GTA 6, but the placement of this particular mural and its muted color palette make it seem like Rockstar fully intended​​​​​​​ for it to mislead people. As such, viral videos showing untimely car crashes have helped popularize the name "Looney Tunes wall" for this part of the game.​​​​​​​ If fans don't call it that, they usually still compare the encounter to Looney Tunes anyway.​​​​​​​

Though it can be infuriating in the heat of the moment,​​​​​​​ fans are delighted by the jest as well. "I really hope they release some stats on how many people hit this wall," one YouTube caption reads.

Other GTA 6 fans can't believe something so simple got them. To be fair, the area is full of shops — including some spots Jason and Lucia can rob. It's easy to stop looking closely when you're actively trying to run away with a stolen stash as the police chase you. It's also positioned so the surrounding stoplights frame the fake tunnel, adding a physical layer of legitimacy to the optical illusion.

If you still think you'd never fall for it, don't be too sure.​​​​​​​ Earlier in 2026, a man in Scotland crashed his car into a wall after mistaking a mural featuring two giant eyes for an actual tunnel.​​​​​​​

"Bro fell for literally the oldest trick in a book," reads one comment on Reddit in a thread where a player expresses disbelief over the joke.
