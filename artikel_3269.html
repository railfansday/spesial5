Bowen Yang Returns To ‘Saturday Night Live’ As Jensen Huang In Cold Open That Skewers Tech Billionaires’ Susceptibility To Honey Pot Plots

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Bowen Yang returned to Saturday Night Live to play Jensen Huang in the show’s cold open, a skewering of tech billionaires susceptibility to honey pots, or being wooed by attractive women and thinking that they genuinely are in it for the romance.

The skit follows Huang’s resignation as CEO of Nvidia following revelations that he had an affair with a woman who may have been a Chinese spy, and another affair with a subordinate. That sent shares of the company tumbling.

“When you invite Jensen over, you know what is about to go down — the S&P 500,” Yang’s Huang said. “That’s right — I’m into BDSM — Breaking Da Stock Market.” Watch on Deadline

The premise of the skit had John Mulaney, this week’s host, playing a CIA official trying to instruct the gathered tech billionaires to be on the lookout for women who show interest in them.

“America needs you to learn the difference between a woman who would actually want to have sex with you and a woman who is clearly a spy,” the CIA official told them. “Now you all saw former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resined this week after he was acught having an affair with an alleged Chinese spy. What do you think the lesson is here?”

“Yeah, never date a Chinese woman,” Anthropic’s Dario Amodei (Jane Wickline) answered.

“Never date a human,” said Mark Zuckerberg (Andrew Dismukes).

“Never date anyone lest they become the Antichrist,” responded Peter Thiel (James Austin Johnson).

“No, and Peter, you got to chill with the Antichrist stuff,” the CIA official told him.

The recent midterms in many ways were a backlash against the AI overlords, with opposition to data centers reflecting fears of AI’s impact on jobs and humanity.

“Show us some respect. We do a lot for this country,” Thiel told the CIA official. “Name one time technology has let us down.”

“Yesterday, when every plane in America was grounded,” the CIA official answered.

“The day before that, when Microsoft went dark,” the CIA official reminded.

Mulaney’s character went on, “Look, I get it, I can’t imagine how disorienting this has been for you to go from these bug-eyed little weirdos to being gods. I mean, never in the history of our species have people as pale and strange as you, a) survived, and b) been given all the world’s wealth and power.”

The skit also featured Ben Marhsall as OpenAI’s Sam Altman and Jeremy Culhane’s return as an Elon Musk, incomprehensible to the point where the CIA official has to spray him with a water bottle to shut him up.

The CIA official tried to get across the dangers they were facing in getting hooked in to a spy plot.

“Let’s start an exercise,” the CIA official told them. “Let’s say you’re at a bar and an attractive woman approaches and asks you, ‘What are you working on?’ Dario, what do you say?”

Amodei responded, “I would say, ‘Madam, what a pleasure to see you this evening. May I interest you in an industry secret?'”

Zuckerberg then said, “I would just assume that she wanted to sleep with the man who created the metaverse, as all woman do.”

“But they don’t, right?” the CIA official said, before showing him a Venn diagram. Next to a large circle of beautiful women was a tiny dot representing “people who like the metaverse.”
