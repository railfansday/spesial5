Management stated that the update to the federal carbon intensity model significantly increased the value of credits generated by dairy renewable natural gas production. The company is advancing infrastructure projects intended to reduce fossil fuel consumption at its ethanol facility while expanding feedstock production for the renewable diesel market. Additionally, the company is pursuing international growth through biodiesel exports and a potential public offering of its subsidiary in India.
