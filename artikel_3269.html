Skydance confirms HBO Max and Paramount+ ‘value’ bundle plan

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

HBO Max and Paramount+ customers will soon be able to pay for one subscription that unlocks both services. That’s according to Skydance, the company that came out of Paramount’s Warner Bros. Discovery acquisition.

Skydance announced plans for the HBO Max plus Paramount+ bundle today, promising to deliver the package deal early next year.

Skydance doesn’t say how much the bundle will cost or if it will have ad-supported options. Both HBO Max and Paramount+ currently offer cheaper subscriptions with ads as well as premium ad-free options.

HBO Max can also be bundled with Disney+ and Hulu today, though this is a package offering that we can probably expect to go away in the future.

Ultimately, it would make sense for Skydance to package a unified service that includes all of the content from both services. For now, at least, it sounds like the short-term plan is to sell the two services separately with an option to bundle and save.
