Signal now blocks screenshots on iPhone to stop Siri AI from seeing chats

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Popular secure messaging app Signal has rolled out a new update that automatically disables screenshots for the sake of blocking Siri AI from reading your chats. Here are the details.

Signal’s latest app update on iPhone disables a key system feature: taking screenshots.

As Signal told Reuters, that’s because Apple doesn’t currently provide better tools for iOS developers to safeguard app content from Siri AI and third-party AI agents.

By default, users who update and try to take a screenshot will see a message that says: “Screenshot prevented by ‘Block Siri AI’ setting.”

Prevent Siri AI from scanning Signal content. This will also block you from taking screenshots in the app and App Switcher.
