$100 Off the AirPods Pro 3 Might Just Be the Best Black Friday Deal This Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

This year’s Black Friday has brought some steep savings on headphones, tech, and more. And while we’ve done our best to comb through the thousands of deals to find the ones that are actually worth it, if you’re looking for just one deal, you could do a lot worse than a $100 discount on the latest AirPods Pro.

Currently, the third-generation AirPods Pro are available for $149, a 40% discount on the regular $249 price. This deal is available on Amazon and other retailers, including Target and Walmart. Apple, meanwhile, is offering a $50 gift card with a purchase of the AirPods Pro 3.

→ Built-in heart rate sensor→ Live-translation→ Hearing protection

When we tested Apple’s AirPods, we were impressed by the wide variety of features beyond listening to music. They feature a built-in heart rate monitor, which is unobtrusive and impressively accurate. They can also be used for hearing protection in loud settings or as a hearing aid for mild to moderate hearing loss.

The AirPods Pro 3 have among the best noise cancellation on the market, if one or two steps behind Bose’s QuietComfort Ultra earbuds. The audio quality itself is impressive, with a clear, neutral sound profile across a range of genres.

The earbuds feature pinch and swipe controls on the stems — they’re easy to use, and the sensors’ discreet placement makes accidentally touching them less likely while adjusting the fit. Apple slightly redesigned the shape of the earbuds so they fit more securely, and the shorter stems don’t stick out as much. I found it easy to get a good fit out of the box, and Apple includes a total of five eartips for a custom fit.

Unsurprisingly, the AirPods work very well with Apple devices, but their features are limited if you’re using an Android device. Fortunately, there are other deals as well, including discounts on the Pixel Buds Pro 2 and Samsung’s Galaxy Buds4 Pro.
