Gabriel Iglesias' Tour Will Continue After Onstage Medical Incident

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Gabriel Iglesias is making sure the show goes on ... because he's not canceling any of his upcoming tour dates despite the recent medical incident he endured.

Sources close to Gabriel tell TMZ ... all of the comedian's "The 1976 Tour" dates are still on schedule and will continue with no further interruptions expected.

We're told Gabriel will perform at his scheduled shows in Memphis, Chattanooga, Dallas and even overseas in Australia. A source says Gabriel is on the mend and taking things day by day.
