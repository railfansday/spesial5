Jennifer Lopez Addresses "Disappointing" Divorce From Ben Affleck

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Nearly two years after Jennifer Lopez and Ben Affleck finalized their divorce, the "Let's Get Loud" singer looked back at the aftermath of their "disappointing" breakup.

Jennifer Lopez knows that sometimes, love does cost a thing.

Nearly two years after the "On The Floor" singer and Ben Affleck finalized their divorce, she reflected on the weight of their breakup—and whether she's ready to dance again with someone new.

"I'm done with that part of my life," Lopez said on the Nov. 17 episode of Benny Blanco and Lil Dicky's Friends Keep Secrets podcast. "It was such a hard, disappointing last situation on every level. On every level, it was so disappointing. I don't ever want to delve into that."

Though she is focusing on living life single, that doesn't mean she'll never be ready for another great love story, especially after taking a deeper look at her split from the Oscar winner.

"I'm OK with myself," she explained. "I was a serial monogamist, one after the other, trying to find the thing, hoping that this was it."

Now, the 57-year-old—mom to 18-year-old twins Max and Emme with ex-husband Marc Anthony—is getting loud with advice for others navigating divorce.

"Always operate in a way that you'll feel proud of when you look back," she shared. Looking back at her experiences, she added, "Even as painful as some of them were so bad—life-shifting pain and hurt—I can look back and be like, 'I loved completely. I acted in a way that I was proud of as a human. I didn't treat anybody in any way that I would be ashamed of.'"

"I did the best that I could in the moment with what I knew," she continued. "That's my advice for anything because when you go through hard things like that, it's easy to be like, 'F--k this.' You're in so much pain you want to hurt somebody else, or somebody has hurt you so much, but you can't. I've always held myself to that kind of standard."

Since then, Lopez—who filed for divorce from Affleck in August 2024, exactly two years after they got married—has continued to carry what she learned from their breakup and its aftermath.

"Be the best person that you can be right now, every single time, especially when it's hard," she emphasized. "When you say you're strong, I think that's part of it. I can look at these things and be like, 'It's OK. These are experiences in my journey.'"

"I don't know why it was my journey sometimes," she admitted. "Then I realized, no, I'm here to learn something. I finally got to a place where I feel like I've learned it, but it took the f--king house to fall down on top of me to do that."

Looking ahead, the "Let's Get Loud" singer—who was first engaged to Affleck in 2002 before splitting two years later—will be dancing for her papi when the time is right.

"I'm different," she said. "Because it changed me. Because I allowed it to change me. Because I wanted to learn the thing. I was like, 'It's done. I'm not doing this anymore. What are you going to do different?'"

"You can intellectually be a smart person and know things," she explained. "But the knowing is not the thing. It's changing the behavior and the patterns. That's the thing. And that is a conscious action that you have to do. You have to change."
