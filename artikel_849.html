Littler faces potential nightmare route at World Championship

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

World No 1 Luke Littler faces a potential nightmare route to World Championship glory with Gary Anderson, Michael van Gerwen, and Luke Humphries all in line to face him.

Littler crashed out of the Grand Slam of Darts after a shock last-16 defeat to Jermaine Wattimena in Wolverhampton last week.

His exit follows quarter-final losses at the World Series of Darts Finals and the European Championship and a first-round defeat at the World Grand Prix.

Littler is now not due to play until the opening night of the World Championship on December 11, having failed to qualify for the Players Championships Finals in Minehead at the weekend.

'The Nuke' is projected to be the top seed for the 2027 PDC World Darts Championship, putting him on a potential semi-final collision course with Luke Humphries while keeping him apart from Gian van Veen and Grand Slam winner Gerwyn Price until the final.

Before that, the 19-year-old from Warrington could face a tricky third-round showdown against former semi-finalist Dave Chisnall, with two-time champion Gary Anderson a potential fourth-round opponent.

They famously met in the semi-finals of the Grand Slam in 2024, which Littler memorably won in a deciding leg thriller.

If Littler gets through that, he is likely to face three-time winner Michael van Gerwen or two-time semi-finalist, Nathan Aspinall.

Then it could be a repeat of the 2024 final against Humphries for a spot in the January 3 showpiece where Van Veen or Price may await.

Price, who took home the £200,000 jackpot in Wolverhampton, has overtaken Littler with the most ranking money earned this season and means he is provisionally up to world No 3, with Littler still No 1, Van Veen at No 2, and Humphries down to No 4 - potentially shaking up the draw heading to Alexandra Palace in December.

Commentator Dan Dawson says Price's victory over Van Veen to claim his fourth Grand Slam title has "done him a favour".

He explained on Sky Sports: "Price has had to battle to win this Grand Slam more than he has in any other since his first, because we've seen him play unbelievable stuff to win Grand Slam titles.

"But I think that's what he's had to do to become a winner again, and seeing him smile and seeing that pressure lift, just hearing him talk, I think that is an enormous sign because he's already looking ahead. What can I win next?

"So that has done him a favour if he is looking to become a world champion again."
