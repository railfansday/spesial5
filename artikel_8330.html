Getting Lost on the Camino Led to Clifftop Oasis

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Today was an easy, relaxed stage mostly spent walking along the waterfront with very little elevation change. We took our time getting started at 8:30 AM and immediately stopped at the first café we could find for some morning caffeine.

It turned out to be a gorgeous, sunny day with temperatures in the low 60°Fs. While the sun felt warm on our backs, the brisk breeze blowing off the water felt like a blast of cold air conditioning.

At the edge of town, an ancient fort and castle stood guard out on a rocky point. Just past the fortress, the road narrowed and hugged a dramatic path carved directly into a cliffside above the pounding ocean. Both of us were completely in awe of the scenery.

Fortunately, a wide pedestrian walkway separated us from the rush of passing cars. We followed this coastal stretch until reaching the next town, where the trail finally turned inland.

Once we headed inland, the path climbed into the hills—and we quickly got lost once again. After some backtracking, we found our way back onto the official Camino route and continued our ascent.

We followed narrow residential streets steadily uphill until the pavement gave way to a rocky forest trail. Climbing higher through the woods, we eventually reached the crest of the hill. From the top, sweeping views of the rugged coastline stretched out both ahead and behind us, complete with a picturesque lighthouse perched on a distant point.

From the ridge, we descended the forest trail until it reconnected with paved roads winding through small coastal farms. Before long, we arrived at our hotel in Mougás, perched right on a flat shelf directly above surf crashing onto the rocks below.

This stretch of the coast is very rural, featuring just one small grocery store and a handful of cafés. After dropping off our packs, we grabbed a meal at a local bar, picked up a bottle of wine from the grocery store, and headed back to relax.

Our hotel featured a pool area with a large grassy lawn right on the edge of the cliffs overlooking the ocean. The sun was shining brightly enough for us to shed our extra layers and soak up the afternoon warmth—a truly perfect ending to a very chill day on the Camino.
