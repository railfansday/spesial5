17 Best Gifts for Women in the Enshittocene (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Considering the current state of affairs, the best gifts for the women in our lives are the ones that let them mentally clock out for a bit. The world is on fire—literally and figuratively. Plastic is melting onto corals, the measles are back, and just to kick us while we’re down, the girlies can’t even enjoy a Caesar salad or some chips and guac in peace. Some days, I'll catch myself staring at the sky and think, I wish I could hitch a ride to space, but of course, Elon Musk has ruined that fantasy for me, too.

If the woman in your life is exhausted by the headlines, by the slow-motion collapse of democracy, by the sheer audacity of men in power, or perhaps just the overall state of humanity, she deserves a treat. She frankly needs a portal to another reality, but since that's not possible just yet, a temporary escape will have to do.

With three heat levels (up to 113 degrees Fahrenheit) and three vibration patterns (low, high, and wave), it stimulates circulation, loosens tight muscles in the lower back, and offers relief when she’s sitting at her desk for eight hours trying not to scream.
