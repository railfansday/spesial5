GTA 6 release time arrives: How players are reacting

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Grand Theft Auto VI is officially out in the wild.

After years of waiting and years of memes about the wait for GTA 6, players are exploring the fictional state of Leonida as we speak.

The launch follows pre-release hype that involved high-profile leaks of game footage, a Netflix special, advertising campaigns in Miami, an expensive merchandise release in London, and more than a decade of speculation.

As players dive into the game on PS5 and Xbox, they are turning to social media to share their feelings on the game and the moment they’d been waiting for all this time.

GTA 6 is rolling out at midnight local time on Nov. 19 worldwide. Players in New Zealand, as well as those who changed their console settings to appear to be in New Zealand, began playing at 6 a.m. ET today.
