Villarreal deny claims former Arsenal player needed eye surgery after watching solar eclipse

The club released a statement saying his operation was ‘entirely unrelated’ to the eclipse

Villarreal issued a statement denying reports that striker Nicolas Pepe underwent eye surgery after damaging his eyes staring at this summer’s solar eclipse, calling the claims “completely false”.

The La Liga club said that the surgery the former Arsenal forward underwent in October was “scheduled in advance” and “entirely unrelated” to the eclipse.

The eclipse, which took place on 12 August, was the most dramatic for nearly three decades. Experts warned in advance that it was only safe to view the event through specialised solar eclipse glasses, with ordinary sunglasses not offering adequate protection for the retina.

A video purportedly from one of Pepe’s social media accounts showing the eclipse recently began circulating online, alongside claims that the surgery was due to looking directly at the sun, risking serious eye damage.

Pepe missed five matches due to the operation and returned to action wearing an eye mask. He was a 75th-minute substitute during Villarreal’s most recent outing, a 3-2 defeat to Barcelona on Sunday.

Villarreal posted on X on Sunday: “Villarreal CF, in response to reports published in various media outlets recently, clarifies that it is completely false that Nicolas Pepe's eye surgery has any connection to viewing the solar eclipse

“The surgery had been scheduled in advance for reasons entirely unrelated to this event.

“The club urges accuracy and responsibility when reporting on matters related to its players' health.”

Pepe has scored 14 goals in 86 appearances for the Spanish club since a move from Trabzonspor in 2024.

The 31-year-old had been Arsenal’s record signing when he joined for £72m from Lille in 2019, but only scored 27 goals in an underwhelming spell at the club, and ultimately joined the Turkish side on a free transfer.

He has scored three goals in 12 games this season.
