Barcelona will be determined to show they are not just flat-track bullies when they host Aston Villa on matchweek four of the Champions League 2026/27 league phase.

After starting the season with 11 successive wins in all competitions, Hansi Flick's side came up short in their first big week of the campaign, losing 4-2 to Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League a fortnight ago before falling to a 2-1 home defeat against Real Madrid in La Liga.

They responded to their blip in the best way possible on Saturday night, beating Alaves 6-1 to regain their two-point lead at the top of La Liga. However, the visit of Villa marks the start of another tough week, with this game followed by a trip to second-place Atletico Madrid in the league on Sunday.

Barca are missing several players through injury, most notably Dani Olmo, Xavi Espart, Eric Garcia and Jules Kounde.
