We'll reveal the answer this time next week when we reach the next edition of The Pre-Match Poser – for now, here's the answer to last week's poser…Q. Only 10 managers have ever had it. Brian Clough was the first, Sir Alex Ferguson was the longest, Sean Dyche and Eddie Howe were the last Englishmen, and Mikel Arteta is one of three Arsenal managers. What are we talking about?A. The title of being the longest-serving manager in the Premier League.

Clough went into the first Prem season having been Nottingham Forest boss since the 1970s; Fergie took the title in February 1995 and held onto it until his retirement in May 2013; Eddie Howe had the title at Bournemouth before leaving, when Sean Dyche took it over; and Arteta has followed Arsene Wenger and George Graham to become the longest-serving in the Premier League today.
