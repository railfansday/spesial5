Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Text description provided by the architects. CONTEXT: VILLA AND LANDSCAPE: The project reads Medveja as part of a broader coastal territory shaped by a particular culture of residential and tourist inhabitation, recognising the continued relevance of temporary dwelling model rooted in the tradition of Opatija's 19th-century summer villas. Within this continuity, temporary inhabitation does not imply a lack of belonging; rather, it establishes a more enduring relationship between dwelling, climate and landscape.
