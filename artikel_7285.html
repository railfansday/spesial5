The Role

As the new Director, Revenue Accounting, you will lead and own the end-to-end Revenue Accounting function, ensuring accurate and timely revenue recognition, financial reporting, and compliance with ASC 606. You serve as the primary Accounting partner to Sales, Deal Desk, Revenue Operations, and Product on complex customer arrangements, contract structuring, and revenue-related business decisions. If you dream of driving operational excellence, creating scalable processes, strong internal controls, and systems automation to support business growth, this is the opportunity for you!

Responsibilities & Accountabilities 

The Director, Revenue Accounting will oversee all aspects of revenue accounting, including monthly and quarterly close, technical revenue accounting, contract reviews, revenue reporting, audit support, and related internal controls. 

The role requires a hands-on leader capable of managing day-to-day execution while driving strategic initiatives across revenue systems, deal structuring, automation, and process improvements. 

You will work cross-functionally with Sales, Deal Desk, Revenue Operations, FP&A, Product, and IT to ensure revenue arrangements are appropriately structured, recorded, and reported. 

Revenue Accounting & Financial Close

  • Own the monthly, quarterly, and annual revenue close, including revenue journal entries, reconciliations, and financial statement accuracy. 
  • Oversee revenue recognition, deferred revenue, RPO, contract modifications, and revenue allocation under ASC 606. 
  • Review revenue flux analyses, account reconciliations, and key revenue-related accounting judgments. 
  • Ensure timely and accurate revenue reporting to Finance leadership and support management and Board reporting. 
  • Oversee revenue-related reserves, adjustments, and other nonstandard accounting matters. 

Contract Review & Technical Accounting

  • Serve as the primary accounting reviewer for customer contracts, renewals, upsells, and nonstandard commercial arrangements. 
  • Evaluate complex contract provisions, assess ASC 606 implications and document technical accounting conclusions for significant or unusual transactions. 
  • Partner with Sales, Deal Desk, and Legal to proactively identify and resolve accounting and commercial risks. 
  • Maintain revenue recognition policies, contract review guidelines, and approval controls. 

Revenue Systems, Automation & Process Improvement

  • Partner with Revenue Operations, Product, and IT on CPQ, SKU architecture, pricing and packaging, and revenue systems enhancements. 
  • Oversee revenue-related system requirements, including standalone selling price (SSP) allocation, contract data, entitlements, and integration with Sage Intacct. 
  • Drive automation of revenue close, reconciliations, reporting, and contract review processes. 
  • Establish scalable processes and controls supporting new products, usage-based pricing, and evolving business models. 
  • Lead Finance participation in user acceptance testing and implementation of revenue-related system changes. 

Audit, Compliance & Internal Controls

  • Serve as the primary Revenue Accounting contact for external auditors. 
  • Prepare and review revenue accounting schedules, technical memos, and supporting audit documentation. 
  • Maintain effective revenue-related internal controls, including deal approval, revenue recognition, and reporting processes. 
  • Identify control gaps and implement improvements to strengthen financial reporting accuracy and operational efficiency. 

Cross-Functional Partnership & Team Leadership

  • Lead, mentor, and develop the Revenue Accounting team, ensuring appropriate coverage, accountability, and knowledge transfer. 
  • Partner closely with FP&A on revenue forecasts, bookings, RPO, and management reporting. 
  • Collaborate with Sales, Deal Desk, and Revenue Operations to balance commercial flexibility with sound accounting and financial controls. 
  • Provide technical guidance and training to business partners on revenue recognition requirements and deal structuring. 

Competencies and Behaviors 

  • Technical expertise: Strong command of US GAAP and ASC 606, with sound judgment on complex accounting matters. 
  • Ownership and accountability: Takes end-to-end responsibility for deliverables, decisions, and issue resolution. 
  • Hands-on leadership: Comfortable operating at both strategic and detailed execution levels. 
  • Business partnership: Commercially minded and able to work effectively with Sales and other functions while maintaining appropriate financial controls. 
  • Analytical and problem-solving skills: Able to identify risks, challenge assumptions, and develop practical solutions. 
  • Systems and process orientation: Proactive in driving automation, operational scalability, and continuous improvement. 
  • Communication and influence: Able to clearly communicate complex accounting matters to technical and nontechnical stakeholders. 
  • People leadership: Develops talent, builds team capability, and fosters accountability and collaboration. 
  • Adaptability: Thrives in a fast-paced environment with evolving products, systems, and business requirements. 

Educational and Experience Requirements 

  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting or Finance; CPA required or strongly preferred. 
  • 12+ years of progressive accounting experience, including significant revenue recognition and technical accounting responsibilities. 
  • 5+ years of experience in SaaS, enterprise software, or technology companies. 
  • Strong working knowledge of ASC 606, including multiple performance obligations, SSP allocation, contract modifications, and usage-based arrangements. 
  • Experience leading revenue close processes, managing teams, and supporting external audits. 
  • Experience with Salesforce, CPQ systems, and ERP/revenue accounting systems; Sage Intacct experience preferred. 
  • Demonstrated success in revenue process automation, systems implementations, and cross-functional initiatives. 
  • Public accounting or Big Four experience preferred.
