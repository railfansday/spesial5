Mora praises Wesley: “He’s a great player and teammate.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

After the 3-2 defeat to Manchester United, Rodrigo Mora, who played a strong first half and was substituted at halftime, spoke to TNT Sports.

The Portuguese praised Wesley, also discussing their relationship in the dressing room.

“I think Wesley played another great match today. He’s a great player and will reach the highest levels of football. We mostly talk about Brazil and Brazilian football, which we both follow.”

“The language, of course, helps us a lot. He helped me a lot in this welcome to Roma, and I’m truly grateful to him for that.”
