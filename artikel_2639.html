Sophie Cunningham Hits Luka Doncic With $50 Million Reality Check

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sophie Cunningham did not just clap back at a silly comparison; she exposed a massive financial gap that fans often ignore. When her podcast co-host Drew Hanlen tried to compare her shot attempts to Los Angeles Lakers star Luka Doncic, Cunningham set the record straight.

The Indiana Fever standout made it clear that the two players operate in completely different worlds, not just in skill but in pay. According to Sports Illustrated, she pointed out that Doncic earns $50 million a year while she takes home $700,000 on her WNBA check.

The numbers Hanlen used to make his point were wild, but they missed the real story. The NBA star had already attempted 221 shots through just nine games, while Cunningham took 226 attempts over the entire 43-game 2026 WNBA season.

Hanlen noted that Doncic had taken 102 free throws in that small sample, whereas Cunningham managed only 74 free throws all of last year. Hanlen explained the roles differ significantly, yet Cunningham insisted the stats are the sole factor.

Cunningham's response highlighted how the new collective bargaining agreement has changed the game for women in basketball, even if the gap remains huge. Her 2026 WNBA earnings greatly exceeded previous amounts due to a new CBA that drove historic player pay hikes.

Sports Illustrated reported she signed a one-year Fever contract and earned above the league average of just over $500,000. The prior year's average sat near $120,000, demonstrating genuine advancement for the league.

Yet the difference between the top NBA earners and top WNBA stars is still staggering. Cunningham made her feelings known when she said, "He also makes $50 million a year. I make $700,000 on my WNBA check." It is hard to argue with that math when looking at the sheer volume of shots taken by a player with that kind of financial security versus a player fighting for every dollar.

Instead of comparing herself to a global superstar with unlimited shot attempts, Cunningham wants people to look at players with similar roles and stats. She told Hanlen, "You should compare me more to Quentin Grimes."

She pointed out that they average around 8-10 points and claimed, "My 3-point shooting is actually better than the both of them." Her final word on the subject was simple: "If we're gonna compare, let's compare."

Now that her time with Indiana could be done, the market will decide her next big move.
