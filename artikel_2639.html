David Bailey applying pressure

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

New York Jets EDGE David Bailey has recorded 45 pressures this season, third-most by rookie defender through Week 11 since 2018, trailing only Jared Verse (50 in 2024) and Nick Bosa (49 in 2019), per NextGenStats.

Why it Matters: Bailey has 50 tackles and 9.5 sacks (third most in NFL) on the season. If he qualifies as a DE in your IDP league, he accounts for the most fantasy points among defensive linemen. He'll have a great matchup against the Miami Dolphins next week.
