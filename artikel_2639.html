Report: Aidan Hutchinson has torn ACL but no additional damage

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson received a bit of good news after additional testing on his right knee.

Tom Pelissero of The Ringer reports that, while Hutchinson will miss the rest of the season with a torn ACL, he has no other significant damage.

Initial reports indicated the Lions feared Hutchinson “probably” had additional damage in his knee, specifically his meniscus.

Hutchinson and Patriots quarterback Drake Maye were both injured on Hutchinson’s third-down sack of Maye with 9:28 left in the first quarter. Maye injured his ankle on the play, and the injury could be season-ending. Hutchinson injured his knee on the celebration afterward.

Walt Anderson, the NFL’s officiating rules analyst, said through a league spokesman that Hutchinson’s tackle was not an illegal hip drop.

This marks the second time in three seasons that Hutchinson’s season will end early. He finishes 2026 with six sacks.
