Austin Reaves remains out vs. Jazz, Walker Kessler returns

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Following a dramatic overtime win against the Hornets on Tuesday, the Lakers will still be at nearly full strength against the Jazz on the second night of a back-to-back, with one major exception.

Austin Reaves will miss his third game since suffering a heel bruise against the Bucks last Wednesday. However, he is the only player on the injury report for the purple and gold with Walker Kessler, who also missed Tuesday's game, set to return.

There are multiple players notable in their exclusion from this injury report. First, as noted, Kessler will return after being out with leg soreness on Tuesday.

"Walker, some lower leg soreness, got bumped," head coach JJ Redick said before Tuesday's game. "Just thinking big picture. We don't think it's serious, but it makes sense for him to, as a precautionary, be out tonight."

Luka Dončić playing both legs of the back-to-back is notable, too. Luka's two missed games this season have both been during a back-to-back, but he did play in the team's previous back-to-back at home.

As for Austin, Redick said Tuesday that he would do on-court work Wednesday morning to see where he's at. The injury and return remain a pain tolerance matter with Redick having previously said that there is no structural damage with the injury.

It is easier to be patient with Reaves' return with the Lakers winning. But with a match-up with the Spurs looming, it would also be nice to see him return soon so he can take part in that contest.

As for the Jazz, who are also on the second night of a back-to-back, their injury report is nearly the same as it was against the Clippers. Jaxson Hayes, who was out on Tuesday, has been upgraded to questionable.

Jaren Jackson, Lauri Markkanen and Jusuf Nurkic are all out. Kobe Bufkin also will not be making his return to LA as he is with Utah's two-way team.
