7 Ways Rockstar Is Bringing GTA 6’s Vice City to Life in the Real World

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto 6, which launches Nov. 19, is set to be among this year’s biggest cultural moments and one of the most anticipated game releases in history.

Thirteen years after its predecessor GTA 5 came out and sold nearly 235 million copies, the new version returns to Vice City, inspired by Miami and set in the fictional state of Leonida.

To build hype for the game, developer Rockstar Games has brought Vice City to life in the real world. Over the past several weeks, iconic spots in Miami have been transformed into landmarks from the game, laying easter eggs for fans and immersing them in its neon-soaked world.

Brittaney is Adweek's creative editor based in London.
