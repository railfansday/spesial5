Miami Chases First ACC Sweep With Boston College Showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The No. 7 Miami Hurricanes stand on the brink of history this Saturday. A win against Boston College would mark the program's first-ever sweep of the Atlantic Coast Conference regular season. The Hurricanes hold a perfect 8-0 record inside the league, while their 10-1 overall record keeps them firmly in the hunt for the College Football Playoff. They face a Boston College team that sits at 5-6 and desperately needs a victory to reach bowl eligibility. The matchup against Boston College does not carry the heavy weight on Miami, already securing a spot in the ACC championship game in Charlotte on Dec. 5.

Miami has been nearly flawless all season, but one loss to Notre Dame on Nov. 7 still stings. The offense has found ways to score without its star receiver, Malachi Toney, who has missed the last three games with an ankle injury. Quarterback Darian Mensah has stepped up with 3,317 passing yards and 29 touchdowns this year. His performance has earned him national attention, with some analysts projecting him as a first-round NFL Draft pick. The team is ready to close out the regular season strong, but history warns against taking any opponent lightly.

History offers a stark reminder for Miami fans about the dangers of complacency. In 2017, the Hurricanes entered their final regular-season game with a 10-0 record and a 15-game winning streak. They were ranked No. 2 in the nation and had already clinched a spot in the conference title game. Facing a 4-7 Pittsburgh team with nothing to play for, Miami suffered a shocking 24-14 defeat. The loss ended their undefeated season and derailed their playoff hopes. That season ended with a 38-3 loss to Clemson in the championship game. Miami must avoid a repeat of that nightmare scenario this Saturday.

The current Boston College team shares some DNA with that 2017 Pittsburgh squad. The Eagles have lost three straight games before their recent 27-15 win over Syracuse. They enter this game with a losing record and a lot of pressure to get back to a bowl game. Coach Bill O'Brien knows the stakes are high for his players. He told reporters that his team will travel to Miami and play their game. "We got one more game. And we'll show up in Miami," O'Brien said, according to CBS Sports. "We'll get on the plane, we'll go down there, and we'll make sure we're there, to play Miami. And then we'll see what happens."

The health of Malachi Toney remains a key question for the Hurricanes. Toney leads the ACC with 13 touchdown catches and has over 1,000 receiving yards. He is considered a top NFL Draft prospect, but his recovery from ankle surgery is not complete. Miami head coach Mario Cristobal offered a positive update on Toney's progress during Wednesday's press conference. "He's looking great," Cristobal said. "I mean, you saw him run, you know, and with all the cutting and running routes. So I mean he's doing really, really well. We'll see."

Even if Toney cannot play, the Hurricanes have other weapons to rely on. The offense has diversified its attack in recent weeks. Tight end Elija Lofton is expected to return after missing time last week. Running back Mark Fletcher Jr. scored three touchdowns in the win over Virginia Tech. Boston College linebacker Anthony Palano knows the threat Toney poses when healthy. "You got to try and limit it. That guy, he's explosive, definitely," Palano said, according to Sports Illustrated. "Just watching him and some of the tape that he's got, [we're] just trying to limit what he can do, which is going to be tough."

A victory on Saturday keeps Miami's playoff hopes alive as they get ready for the conference title game against Wake Forest. The Hurricanes are currently ranked No. 7 in the College Football Playoff rankings. They need to maintain their momentum to lock in a high seed in the 12-team field. Boston College, on the other hand, is fighting for its season. A win would guarantee them a bowl game just one year after finishing 2-10. The Eagles have shown they can run the ball effectively, but they face a Miami defense that has been solid all year.

The all-time series between these two teams favors Miami heavily, with a 25-6 record. However, Boston College won the last meeting in Miami back in 2011. The game is scheduled for Saturday at noon in Coral Gables, Florida.
