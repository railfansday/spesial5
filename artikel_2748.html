AFC North Week 11 Recap: Aaron Rodgers released by the Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Drama is afoot in the AFC North following a week in which all three teams that played suffered losses.

The Bengals' hot streak ended in Week 11 with a 22-20 loss to the Washington Commanders on Monday Night Football.

Joe Burrow came back down to Earth following multiple games with at least four touchdowns over the past six games, completing 28 of 40 pass attempts for 300 yards and two touchdowns against the Commanders. Both Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins were held in check as the superstar duo combined for 74 receiving yards and one touchdown on seven catches, while Mike Gesicki led the team with five catches for 77 yards. The ground game was quiet for the Bengals on Monday, as Samaje Perine led the way with just 26 rushing yards on six carries.

Boye Mafe continues to look like a free agent steal for Cincinnati as he racked up three sacks against Washington, bringing his total to 9.5 for the season — a career best for the fifth-year edge rusher.

The Browns enjoyed a bye week following their win over the Houston Texans in Week 10. They will face the Las Vegas Raiders upon their return next Sunday as they fight to remain in playoff contention.

The Steelers fell to 6-4 with a 19-16 loss to the Philadelphia Eagles in Week 11.

Sunday's game feels like an afterthought for Pittsburgh following the recent shocking news that the team first benched and then later cut quarterback Aaron Rodgers. Following his reported benching, the future Hall of Famer aired his frustrations regarding head coach Mike McCarthy during a Wednesday appearance on The Pat McAfee show. It was later announced that Rodgers had been released. Mason Rudolph will take over as the team's starter now. This drama comes after DK Metcalf, the team's highly-priced wide receiver, was ejected from Sunday's game against the Eagles. Metcalf had just one catch for two yards before being thrown out. It was Max Hurleman who led the team with six catches for 61 yards and a touchdown. Rodgers led the team in rushing with just 29 yards on five carries.

Alex Highsmith finished with two of Pittsburgh's three sacks on backup quarterback Joe Milton.

The Ravens' sixth consecutive loss came in a 35-33 shootout with the Carolina Panthers.

Jacoby Brissett once again gave Baltimore a fighting chance, completing 24 of 37 pass attempts for 276 yards, one touchdown, and an interception on the final drive of the game. The rushing attack showed life after picking up just 16 yards on the ground a week ago against the Los Angeles Chargers. Derrick Henry had his best game in over a month with 94 rushing yards and a touchdown on 13 carries, though he did lose a fumble on a catch at the start of the fourth quarter. Justice Hill's strong day was also marred by a costly fumble that went through the back of the end zone for a touchback. It did not stop there, though, as LaJohntay Wester put the ball on the ground two more times on punt returns, losing one that led to immediate points for the Panthers. Wester has since been released. Zay Flowers led the team in catches and receiving yards with eight for 97, while Rashod Bateman caught a 52-yard touchdown on a trick play.

Mike Green was a standout for Baltimore's defense once again this week, notching two sacks for the second game in a row. T.J. Tampa was another bright spot during an otherwise disappointing defensive effort, as the third-year cornerback intercepted Bryce Young in the end zone to end Carolina's drive.
