Celtics get good injury news ahead of game vs. Nuggets

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

DENVER — The Celtics will be at full strength when they face the Denver Nuggets on Wednesday night. Neemias Queta, who missed Monday's game to rest, is fully available.

Boston is coming off a 115-105 loss to the Orlando Magic, just its second loss in 13 games. The Celtics recovered from a sloppy first half to tie the game at 80, but Orlando scored 40 points in the fourth quarter to pull away.

"We fought our asses off to get it to a one-possession game," Joe Mazzulla said after the loss. "We take the lead, I think it's 80 to 80. There are three or four plays that we don't come up with that turn into catch-and-shoot threes or a layup for them. Those are the ones that you focus on."

Orlando did most of its damage inside, outscoring Boston 54-32 in the paint. Queta's presence was sorely missed, and Paul George said that the team did not defend as well as they should have.

"If anything, maybe we overrespected them behind the three-point line," George said. "We knew Orlando was a paint team. They like to drive and get to the rim. They got a lot of guys that kind of put their head down and get to the rim. So we kind of played into their strengths. So that's on us."

The loss came on the second night of a back-to-back, after the Celtics jumped out to a 42-9 first-quarter lead against the Dallas Mavericks on Sunday and ultimately won by 37.

Mazzulla said the team didn't overreact to the Mavericks win nor the Magic loss.

"We weren't jumping around for joy last night," he said. "I mean, again, you can't coach to the scoreboard."

"There's a lot of things we obviously can learn from, and going on the road tomorrow," Tatum said. "So obviously, probably watching film and regroup and just get ready for Denver."

The Nuggets are 7-5 and coming off a Monday night loss to the Phoenix Suns. They're been better on the road (5-2) than at home (2-3).

Jamal Murray leads the way with 26.1 points and 6 assists per game, while Nikola Jokic averages 24.1 points, 10.9 rebounds, and 8.9 assists.
