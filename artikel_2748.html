Boxing world champion taken into ICE custody at El Paso airport

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Venezuelan boxing champion Jorge Linares was taken into custody by U.S. immigration agents at an airport in Texas over the weekend as his attorneys work to resolve his immigration case.

Linares, a former three-division world champion, was arrested Sunday at the El Paso International Airport. He first announced his detention in a post on X.

"ICE has me at the Texas border crossing," he said in a post on X. "This is crazy."

His legal team confirmed Linares' arrest in a news release Tuesday, adding that he is authorized to be in the U.S. and has been fully cooperative with immigration authorities.

"We are in communication with ICE and are working to resolve this matter as quickly as possible," the statement read. "Immigration matters follow established procedures, and those procedures take time. We are confident in Mr. Linares's position and ask his fans, the press and the public for patience in the meantime.

HOUSTON, TEXAS - DECEMBER 02: Jorge Linares poses for a picture backstage at The Toyota Center for the Garcia Duarte welterweight fight on December 2, 2023 in Houston, Texas. (Photo by Thaddaeus McAdams/Getty Images) (Thaddaeus McAdams/Getty Images)

An ICE spokesperson told Chron that Linares, based in Miami, had permission to enter the U.S. temporarily on October 23, but stayed longer than the term permitted.

"Being in detention is a choice," the ICE statement said. "He entered the country with permission to stay for a temporary period, but in defiance of our nation's laws, he overstayed his welcome."

"To be clear, a pending application or work authorization does not confer legal status in the United States," the spokesperson added.

The agency did not specify how long Linares was authorized to stay in the country, what type of authorization he received or why he was taken into custody when he was.
