Latest: Top reporter claims PSG line up new deals for 3 senior starters and one academy talent

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

PSG are preparing to hand out some new contracts to keep the core of their superteam together according to the latest reports.

If you enjoy PSG Talk coverage and want to see more of it, add us as a preferred source on Google to make us a favourite and see more of our content.

Top PSG Journalist Benjamin Quarez has been writing for Le Parisien about some important contract talks at the Parc des Princes.

Last night he reported that PSG are "considering an extension" for veteran captain Marquinhos, and that talks have already taken place with the club over a potential new deal.

"He remains an important figure both in the locker room and on the pitch," the piece claims.

There seems no reason to not keep a good thing going and offer him a new deal.

Quentin Ndjantou. (Photo by David Lidstrom/Getty Images)

The Brazilian isn't the only one who will be getting a new deal. There continues to be whispers (which Quarez mentions here) of new deals for Ousmane Dembele and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia.

On top of securing those first team stars long term, the club are also trying to reach an agreement with academy youngster Quentin Ndjantou, who gets minutes as a sub here and there for Luis Enrique. His contract expires in the summer of 2028, and the club want to extend that to 2031 to protect his value.

France are back in action on Sunday, and ahead of the game Zinezine Zidane has been praising Desire Doue.

Luis Enrique has defended the form of Ferran Torres for PSG after his very hot start went cold.

Senny Mayulu has just got a call up to the French senior team for the first time, a big honour and a special moment.
