Ole Miss football game vs Oklahoma switched to Sunday over travel issues, live updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Ole Miss football game against Oklahoma has been moved to Sunday, Nov. 15 and won't be played today as originally scheduled.

No. 17 Ole Miss (6-3, 4-3 SEC) plays at Oklahoma (5-4, 3-3) at noon on Nov. 15. The SEC announced the news shortly after midnight Nov. 14. The TV network for the game has not yet been determined.

The Rebels and Sooners were originally scheduled to play at 3:15 p.m. on Saturday, Nov. 14 on SEC Network.

The Rebels' flight to Oklahoma on Nov. 13 was postponed by the FAA's nationwide ground stop order, which began around 3 p.m. and was lifted around 7:30 p.m. Despite the order being lifted, Ole Miss said it would not make the trip on Nov. 13.

07:42 AM ET, November 14 2026, Kevin Procter

Ole Miss released a statement explaining whey the team was not able to travel to Oklahoma late on Nov. 13 after the FAA ground stop was lifted.

"The Ole Miss football team was unable to depart Oxford Friday night due to the FAA ground stoppage," Ole Miss football said in a statement. "After the stoppage was lifted, a late-night departure could not be accommodated by the charter company with which Ole Miss was contracted and the team's departure has been delayed until Saturday afternoon."

Ole Miss flies out of Tupelo, which is about 50 miles from Oxford. The flight time from Tupelo to Oklahoma City is about 90 minutes. Ole Miss typically arrives at the stadium about three hours before the game.

It is a good time in the schedule for Ole Miss to be coming off a short week. The Rebels face Wofford on Nov. 21.

The Rebels have already played on Sunday once this season. That was in Week 1 against Louisville to open the season. Ole Miss won that game Sept. 6.
