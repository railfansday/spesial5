Buccaneers at Lions: Initial injury report for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers returned from their bye week and began preparations for their Week 11 matchup against the Detroit Lions on Wednesday, with much of the attention surrounding the health of quarterback Baker Mayfield.

Tampa Bay listed six players on its initial injury report, including two who did not participate in practice.

Mayfield (foot/right thumb) was held out of Wednesday's session after suffering an injury to his foot and ankle area during the Buccaneers' Week 9 loss to the Chicago Bears. The veteran quarterback had reportedly been wearing a walking boot periodically throughout the bye week but was seen without one Wednesday while observing practice in street clothes.

Head coach Todd Bowles offered somewhat encouraging news regarding Mayfield's recovery, saying the quarterback has made progress and is doing considerably better than he was a week ago. However, as reported by Buccaneers senior writer Scott Smith, the team will wait until Friday to make a determination on his availability against Detroit.

Rookie quarterback Jalon Daniels took first-team reps Wednesday and would once again be called upon should Mayfield be unable to play. The Buccaneers also promoted quarterback Easton Stick to the active roster earlier this week and signed veteran Cooper Rush to their practice squad.

Aside from Mayfield, safety Antoine Winfield Jr. (ankle) was the only other Buccaneer held out of practice Wednesday.

Outside linebacker Yaya Diaby (knee), who missed Tampa Bay's game against Chicago, was a limited participant. Cornerback Josh Hayes (knee), tight end Cade Otton (thumb) and left tackle Tristan Wirfs (toe) participated fully. Hayes returned to practice Wednesday, opening his 21-day activation window from injured reserve.

For Detroit, center Cade Mays (ankle) was the only player listed as a non-participant. Cornerbacks D.J. Reed (hip) and Ennis Rakestraw Jr. (shoulder) were among six players listed as limited, while safety Kerby Joseph (knee) was listed as a full participant. The Lions conducted a walkthrough Wednesday, meaning their participation designations were estimates.

All eyes will remain on Mayfield throughout the remainder of the week as the Buccaneers look to avoid playing without their starting quarterback for the fourth time this season.

The Buccaneers and Lions are scheduled to kick off Sunday at 1:00 p.m. ET on CBS.
