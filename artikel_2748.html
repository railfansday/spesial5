Aqib Talib gets 100% real on Giants’ TNF loss to Commanders

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Aqib Talib gets 100% real on Giants' TNF loss to Commanders appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The New York Giants let one slip away on Thursday Night Football, and Aqib Talib believes they have nobody to blame but themselves. Breaking down the Giants' 24-21 loss to the Washington Commanders on the "Gridiron" podcast, the former All-Pro cornerback did not mince words.

"I think the Giants lost this game more than the Commanders won this game. But I love how the Commanders finished the game," Talib said.

Talib pointed to Washington's aggressive defensive approach as a key factor, particularly the way the Commanders went after Giants quarterback J.J. McCarthy.

"They didn't come out conservative on defense. They blitzed the s**t out of J.J. Every play. Made him get the ball out his hand, made him make quick decisions. That's a non-conservative defense right there," Talib said.

The numbers back him up. Washington blitzed 30 times on the night, according to Sportradar, keeping constant pressure on McCarthy. To their credit, the Giants still moved the ball, racking up 399 total yards, but they also turned it over twice and missed a field goal attempt.

The Giants appeared to have the game in hand late. McCarthy connected with Isaiah Likely for a 66-yard touchdown with 8:24 left in the fourth quarter, giving New York a 21-17 lead. However, Jayden Daniels answered with a 32-yard scoring strike to Stefon Diggs with just 1:05 remaining to seal the win for Washington.

Daniels finished 23-of-33 for 249 yards with three touchdowns and two interceptions, while Cam Skattebo found the end zone twice on the ground for the Giants.

Talib also lamented the state of the NFC East as a whole, a division that has long been one of the most storied in football.

"This division has won more Super Bowls than any division. And this division has been historically great. And it has hurt my heart all year to watch how this division has turned into the NFC Least," he said.

The loss dropped the Giants to 5-3-1 on the season. The two NFC East rivals had played to a 27-27 tie in their first meeting back in October.

New York will look to bounce back when it hosts the Jacksonville Jaguars at MetLife Stadium next Sunday afternoon.
