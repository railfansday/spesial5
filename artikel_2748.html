Pennridge-Quakertown game postponed; Bristol-Morrisville cancelled

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The 97th annual Pennridge-Quakertown Thanksgiving football game was postponed to Saturday morning due to the expected heavy rain on Thursday.

The game between the 9-2 Rams and 6-5 Panthers will be played Saturday at 10:15 a.m. at Quakertown.

Both teams qualified for the District One Class 6A playoffs and were ousted in the first round, with Pennridge falling to North Penn 38-21 and Quakertown losing to district finalist Spring-Ford 42-28.

As for the scheduled Bristol at Morrisville Thanksgiving matchup (10:30 a.m.), it has been cancelled "due to (the two schools) not being able to agree on a (makeup) date," according to Morrisville head coach Corey Keen.

Bristol will finish the season 7-5 while Morrisville ends up 7-3.
