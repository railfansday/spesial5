Pochettino's first Man Utd training session filmed by drone operator

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

New Manchester United head coach Mauricio Pochettino (right) worked with his full squad for the first time on Thursday

Manchester United fans have been given a unique and unexpected insight into Mauricio Pochettino's first training session after footage was captured by a drone flown next to the club's Carrington training ground.

Twelve minutes of the session was filmed by a drone operator and uploaded onto YouTube.

Pochettino and his players, who were split into three groups, are clearly visible, with the session filmed from a variety of angles and distances.

"For the people asking about legality. It is unrestricted airspace, meaning anyone can fly over it," said the drone operator.

"The drone remained in line of sight at all times, it didn't fly over anyone or even near anyone so was a perfectly legal flight going by the CAA rules that regulates drone usage.

"The drone is correctly labelled, and I hold all the relevant licences, public liability and insurance required. As for leaking team news/tactics, it was half an hour into the managers first training session, there are many media there so certainly won't be the only footage of training released."

The session was also filmed by United's own media channel, who regularly film club training sessions.

Manchester United have declined to comment.

Manchester United head coach Mauricio Pochettino (second right) hugs forward Bryan Mbeumo

There were 10 outfield players working with Pochettino, although it seems unlikely it will be the starting line-up for Sunday's trip to Liverpool.

While Harry Maguire, Kobbie Mainoo, Amad Diallo, Matheus Cunha and Bryan Mbeumo are regular starters, Ayden Heaven and Andrey Santos have had less involvement, and neither Joshua Zirkzee nor Harry Amass have started a Premier League game this season. Jaume Camacho 17, who is yet to feature at senior level, was also with the group.

On an adjoining pitch, skipper Bruno Fernandes is shown in one group playing the 'rondos' game, where two players are in the middle trying to intercept the passes of the five players in a circle on the outside. Youngster Shea Lacey and last season's FA Youth Cup-winning skipper Dan Armer are also involved, along with England youth international Bendito Mantato.

Next to that is another group which includes more experienced players in Lisandro Martinez, Matthijs de Ligt, Youri Tielemans, Mason Mount, Leny Yoro, Noussair Mazraoui and Diogo Dalot.

The identical twins Jack and Tyler Fletcher are split between the two groups, with Pochettino's assistant Jesus Perez across both.

Not all the players were identifiable and United confirmed defender Patrick Dorgu was involved.
