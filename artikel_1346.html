Newcastle Add Kieran McKenna To Wanted List

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Newcastle could be in the market for a new manager in the near future, with Matthias Jaissle continuing to struggle.

The Magpies were held to a 1-1 draw last time out against Fulham, meaning they've now failed to win in their last three games in all competitions.

David Moyes had Jaissle's number in recent weeks, beating Newcastle in both the Carabao Cup and the Premier League in the space of four days.

There's been a lot of criticism for the Newcastle manager as they struggle to string together a run of successful results.

The Magpies do sit in 11th place at the moment, but some of their performances and results have been unacceptable.

Jaissle doesn't appear to be under immediate pressure to be sacked, but Newcastle are now doing succession planning in case they do decide to sack him.

According to journalist Alan Nixon, Newcastle have now put former Ipswich Town manager Kieran McKenna on their wanted list.

Newcastle did consider McKenna as an alternative to Jaissle before they made the appointment in the summer, and the former Ipswich man is still available.

Ipswich are still owed compensation if a club like Newcastle does come in for him, but Nixon claims that Jaissle would be relatively cheap to sack.

With Newcastle already considering other coaches, the pressure is seriously mounting on current manager Jaissle. Whether McKenna is the man to turn it around remains to be seen, but there's no doubt he completely overachieved with Ipswich.

Jaissle hasn't shown enough from a tactical perspective so far to prove that he is the right man to take Newcastle forward.

Of course, it's still early days, and he probably deserves a bit more time to implement his ideas. Still, his unwillingness to play some of their young talents will frustrate supporters immensely.

Newcastle fans would be able to accept some of the young players not getting minutes if they were winning games regularly, but that's simply not the case.

Newcastle's only victories this season in the Premier League have come against struggling Crystal Palace and Hull City, while they also beat Tottenham, who were going through a difficult period.

Some of the blame has to be on recruitment, but there's no doubt another manager could get more out of this squad.

McKenna, described as an 'elite' manager. has proven in the past to be able to work with what he's got, doing a phenomenal job to get Ipswich promoted twice on the bounce.
