The 2026/27 Nations League knockout stage draw has been completed - with the eight remaining teams finding out their path to the final.

England were mightily impressive in the group stage. They beat world champions Spain 3-2 in Madrid to top Group C. They will now face Norway in the quarter-finals of the competition.

Spain have been handed a very tough draw against France as a result of finishing below England. La Roja's 43-match unbeaten run may have come to an end, but they'll fancy their chances against a side that they defeated in the semi-finals of the 2026 World Cup.

Portugal, who are now without Cristiano Ronaldo following his retirement from international football, face Netherlands, while Belgium take on Jurgen Klopp's Germany.

The quarter-finals will be two-leg ties, with both games taking place at the end of March. The four victors will progress to the Nations League Finals, which will be held between June 9 and June 13, 2027.
