Arsenal wonderkid Max Dowman has added another record to his long list of achievements already in his career.

The 16-year-old attacking midfielder looks like a phenomenal talent, and Sam Dean of the Telegraph has pointed out that he’s just made history yet again.

Dowman had already broken similar records at youth level for England, becoming their youngest ever player and scorer at Under-17 and Under-19 level.

Despite still only being 16, it seems clear that Dowman is already too good for that level, hence his early step up to the Under-21s. Given his fairly regular appearances already for the Arsenal first-team, it’s also surely not too long before he earns his first senior England cap.

Dowman has looked like a huge prospect for some time now, and in 2026 we’ve started to see a lot more of him showing what he can do at the highest level.

Arsenal clearly already trust him, with Dowman notably brought on as a substitute in a tight game against Everton last season and immediately changing the game with his quality.

The youngster put in a dangerous cross that led to the opening goal for Viktor Gyokeres, and he then scored a memorable second goal himself to secure an important 2-0 win.

Dowman also scored a brace in a Carabao Cup game against Ipswich Town earlier this season, making it three goals in 18 senior appearances for the Gunners before his 17th birthday.

After such a superb start to his career, the world is surely Dowman’s oyster, though we’ve also seen plenty of wonderkids who started brightly but failed to live up to their potential.
