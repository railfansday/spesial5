Maguire Axed as Rashford Decision Made - Pochettino's First Man Utd XI v Liverpool Predicted

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Mauricio Pochettino will lead Manchester United out for the first time on Sunday afternoon when they take on arch-rivals Liverpool at Anfield.

The Red Devils opted to make a change in the dugout during the international break, with Michael Carrick sacked after just one win in the opening ten games of the season, leaving the team 18th in the table, just one point off the bottom.

Pochettino has been appointed on a two-and-a-half-year deal at Old Trafford, giving him time to revolutionise Man Utd, and has now officially taken over his duties after helping the USMNT to an 8-0 aggregate win over Haiti in the Nations League during his final two games as their boss.

United now face arguably their toughest game of the season in his first outing, and the Argentine will have just three days of training with his new squad of players to try and prepare them for the clash.

So what will his first starting lineup look like, based on the players he is expected to have available? GIVEMESPORT has had a go at predicting it...

The goalkeeper position is one that is not up for debate at this stage, and Senne Lammens will be the starting goalkeeper for now. The defence, however, is not as clear-cut.

£111m duo Luke Shaw and Harry Maguire are listed as doubts after recent injury issues, and the likelihood of Pochettino taking risks immediately is slim to none. That means Patrick Dorgu should get the nod at left-back, having got a goal and assist as a substitute in the 4-4 draw against Aston Villa before the international break.

In central defence, Leny Yoro has been impressive this season and should start in his preferred centre-back role. At the same time, Lisandro Martinez is available again after his suspension and Pochettino is likely to lean on his fellow countryman to provide some experience and leadership. At the same time, Diogo Dalot should shift back over to his preferred right-back position.

Mauricio Pochettino's pending arrival at Man Utd could be about to save one star's Old Trafford career.

The midfield underwent a major rebuild in the summer, and despite the team's struggles this season, the midfield has been far from the biggest issues at the club.

Kobbie Mainoo has continued to prove himself as a leader on the pitch and will retain his place in the heart of the midfield, but with Carlos Baleba injured, Pochettino will have a decision to make over who partners him.

£50m summer signing Andrey Santos is the more defensive-minded option, but Youri Tielemans has the experience and quality on the ball to provide what Pochettino likes, and the Belgian is more likely to get the nod in this game as a result.

In the number ten role, Bruno Fernandes has started to rediscover some form after scoring against Roma and Aston Villa, while he also found the net for Portugal in their 3-2 win over Wales this week, and so the skipper will keep his place in the team as a key player.

Man Utd have found their goalscoring boots again in recent games, and Pochettino will be keen to maintain that momentum in this game.

Benjamin Sesko remains sidelined until the new year with a shin problem, and while Joshua Zirkzee netted in the draw with Aston Villa he is likely to continue on the bench with Bryan Mbeumo getting the nod as the starting striker, much like how Heung-Min Son did at Spurs at times under Pochettino. Amad Diallo's work rate and dribbling is likely to be something the new boss likes, and should keep him in on the right wing ahead of Shea Lacey for this game.

On the left, Marcus Rashford is expected to be a key player under the Argentine, who is understood to be 'obsessed' with the Carrington graduate. But the England international has missed the last month with hip and groin problems, and he is unlikely to be rushed back, meaning Matheus Cunha will get a chance to stake his claim for a starting spot at Anfield.

Predicted Man Utd XI vs Liverpool: Lammens; Dalot, Yoro, Martinez, Dorgu; Tielemans, Mainoo; Amad, Fernandes, Cunha; Mbeumo.
