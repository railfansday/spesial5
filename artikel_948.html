Jack Grealish returns to Everton squad for first time since disciplinary breach

Jack Grealish has returned to the Everton squad for the first time since being dropped for a breach of discipline.

Grealish last played in a 2-0 home defeat to Chelsea on 17 October, with the former England international punished for skipping training when he was not authorised to do so before the Carabao Cup tie with Newcastle.

He has not featured in four games - the first of which, against Arsenal, due to illness - with manager David Moyes not including him in previous matchday squads due to a lack of match fitness.

But after featuring in a behind-closed-doors friendly against Derby during the international break, Grealish has returned to the first-team fold and starts on the bench for Everton’s trip to Brentford on Monday night.

Moyes has preferred Merlin Rohl, Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall and Tyrique George in the attacking midfield positions behind Thierno Barry, but Grealish could be in line for a substitute cameo.

Grealish apologised to fans after his friendly return to action, writing on his Instagram story: "Nice to be back out there with the boys today after a few good days training.

"I want to apologise to the fans and everyone at the club for not being able to help the team the last few weeks.

"I love playing for this club and can’t wait to be back playing in front of you guys in an Everton shirt. See you all soon."

Grealish spent the 2025-26 season on loan at Everton before injury disrupted his spell, but he secured another temporary deal on transfer deadline day.

In his absence, Everton have won three of their last four matches to move up to sixth in the Premier League table, while also earning their spot in the quarter-finals of the Carabao Cup.
