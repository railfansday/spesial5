What to Wear on a Winter Date Night, According to Celebs

Dressing for date night is pretty simple during the spring and summer: grab a slinky tank top, slip on a mini skirt, and throw on a pair of heeled flip-flops. But once the weather cools, things get a bit trickier.

Oversized fisherman sweaters, corduroy pants, and snuggly slipper-like boots are all great for cozy, laid-back days. But sometimes you want to look a bit more elevated, whether you're heading out on a hot date or meeting your best friends for dinner. You want an outfit that feels special and isn't going to leave you shivering in the frigid cold—and Hollywood's biggest stars have it figured out.

Lola Tung, Kristen Bell, and Olivia Munn are a few recent celebrities spotted in cold-weather pieces, including funnel-neck band jackets, slick pants, and turtleneck dresses. Each celebrity-approved ensemble additionally includes rich winter fabrics, like suede, velvet, and leather, creating both a bold and warm fashion option.

If your calendar is stacked with dinner reservations and holiday plans, you're going to want to keep reading to discover a slew of style-forward early winter outfits, straight from your favorite celebrities to your closet.

Lola Tung knows the power of an all-black ensemble. To keep the ensemble from looking flat, she slipped on pieces featuring different finishes, like shiny leather and sheer nylon, to add dimension. This combination made each item—from her knee-high boots to her edgy band jacket—stand out. For added visual interest, Tung threw on a patchwork Coach bag in warm fall hues.

Free People Ryland Vegan Leather Band Jacket

Follow Coco Rocha’s lead on the nights you’re eager to wear pants but aren’t feeling casual denim. Her rich leather trousers elevate the twisted, off-the-shoulder long-sleeve, making the outfit look thoughtfully curated (while remaining secretly comfortable). The monochromatic pieces, including Rocha’s snip-toe boots and suede coat, offer a streamlined finish.

Frame The Modernist Leather Slim-Fit Palazzo Pants

Whether you’re hitting up your favorite dance bar or going to a swanky cocktail lounge, Meghann Fahy’s sophisticated going-out look fits the bill. While her mini dress makes for a leggy moment, the turtleneck adds warmth, with the leather bomber jacket serving as an extra layer of coziness. Pointed-toe heels are a dressy finishing touch, but you could easily swap for boots instead.

For those ultra-luxe evenings, reach for a plush velvet coat like Olivia Munn. The lustrous fabric looks elevated and expensive, doing the heavy lifting for you. Simply throw on trousers and patent leather heels to complete the look. It’s an ideal option for glamorous dinners and upcoming holiday gatherings.
