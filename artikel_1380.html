Leeds Open Talks to Sign Bilal El Khannouss for Daniel Farke

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Despite an impressive start to their Premier League campaign, Leeds United are lacking something in the final third and, according to TEAMtalk, they are looking to put that right in the January transfer window.

Only the top two clubs in the table - Arsenal and Brighton and Hove Albion - have conceded fewer goals than Leeds' eight, but the Yorkshire outfit are also averaging scoring just one goal per game after the first 10 matches of the season.

If the Whites do harbour serious ambitions of pushing for a top-half finish this term, then that number obviously needs to improve.

Daniel Farke would have been hoping that the summer additions of Harry Wilson and Jean-Matteo Bahoya would help boost their goal tally, but neither player has found the net and both are actually struggling to find a spot in the starting XI at Elland Road.

It was no secret that Farke wanted to sign a new centre-forward in the summer to provide extra competition for Dominic Calvert-Lewin, who has netted three of their 10 Premier League goals in 2026-27.

Leeds were interested in a move for Liam Delap during the summer transfer window, according to GIVEMESPORT sources, but the striker ended up making a big-money move to Nottingham Forest instead.

While club chiefs could be looking at the striker market again in the New Year, they are actually said to be holding talks with Bundesliga side Stuttgart to see if a January move for former Leicester City forward Bilal El Khannouss is possible, according to TEAMtalk.

Farke is said to love the player, having followed his progress ever since he joined Leicester City in 2024, and wants him in January, although a move could be unlikely for two big reasons.

Farke is said to be particularly impressed with El Khannouss' ability to operate across the frontline, as either a winger or as a No.10, believing he could add something different to the frontline.

The report adds that Stuttgart aren't keen to sell the Morocco international mid-season, following a positive start to their Champions League campaign.

The Bundesliga outfit have taken seven points from a possible 12 in Europe's elite club competition, and are currently on course to be in with a chance of qualifying for the knockout stages of the competition.

That will be frustrating for Leeds, who are able to afford the 22-year-old's price tag of around £33m, although they could reignite their interest again next summer, given Farke is such a long-term admirer of the African genius.

Hoeness is too, having lavished his forward with big praise as recently as September.

He definitely deserves special praise. His [opening] goal was very easy on the eyes. One also needs to mention the second goal. His ball control on the flank and then that through ball. He played a critical role in us winning the game today and, accordingly, he definitely deserves the recognition.

“To that, I’d like to add that he contributed a very well-rounded game. He defended very responsibly, played very intensely, initiated plenty of duels, and won plenty of them as well. It was a really great performance today from Billy [El Khannouss]."

The player himself is also said to be uncertain about leaving Stuttgart, where he is seen as a key player and well liked by manager Sebastian Hoeness, while a former coach at Genk claimed the attacking genius had 'eyes in the back of his head'.

Those doubts have surfaced over concerns about playing time, and the player is said to want guarantees over minutes before considering a move to another club like Leeds.

That could be a potential red flag given every player needs to earn his spot, but Leeds will need to look elsewhere anyway given the stance on a potential January deal for El Khannouss.

Leeds could pull off a huge signing in January - a deal which would be on par with their signing of Will Lankshear.
