Rio Ferdinand labels Man United's Michael Carrick sacking 'like ChatGPT' - with 142-word statement sent by communications department and no message from club chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Published: 07:20 EST, 12 November 2026 | Updated: 07:22 EST, 12 November 2026

Man United legend Rio Ferdinand has called for 'a face' to explain the decision to sack Michael Carrick after comparing the club's statement announcing his dismissal to ChatGPT.

Carrick's dismissal was confirmed in a 142-word statement released at 10.37am, which was sent out by Man United's communications department.

Man United have moved closer to appointing former Tottenham and Chelsea boss Mauricio Pochettino after reaching a verbal agreement with the US national team boss on Wednesday. The club then made a formal approach to US Soccer.

Man United's key decision makers, including chief executive Omar Berrada and technical director Jason Wilcox, have not issued a message following the decision to part ways with Carrick.

Ferdinand, who played alongside Carrick at Man United, has called for officials to front up to the decision in a post on social media.

'Man United statement like Chat GPT… I wanna hear a voice!' Ferdinand wrote. 'I want to see a face to this…'

Michael Carrick - pictured arriving at Carrington on Thursday morning - has been sacked after Manchester United's disastrous start to the season

Man United's leadership, including minority owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe, chief executive Omar Berrada and technical director Jason Wilcox, were not named in the club's statement

Man United's club statement confirming the decision read: 'Manchester United can confirm that Michael Carrick has departed his role as men’s first-team head coach.

'Michael rejoined the club in January and guided the team to a third-placed Premier League finish by winning 12 out of the remaining 17 games of the 2025/26 season, accumulating the highest points tally in the league during that period.

'After a disappointing start to the season, the club has come to the extremely difficult conclusion that a change is necessary in order to achieve our ambitions.

'Michael is, and always will be, a Manchester United legend. Just as he did as a player, he has conducted himself with the highest standards of dedication and professionalism throughout his time back with the club. His leadership and achievements last season will always be fondly remembered.

'The club intends to appoint a permanent successor as quickly as possible.'

Minority owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe, along with the Glazer family, who dialled in virtually from the United States, Berrada and Wilcox reached a verdict in the south of France yesterday, all while Carrick remained in limbo back in Manchester.

Carrick arrived at United's training ground at 7.50am this morning and was dismissed. He left at 10.18am, less than two and a half hours later.

United States boss Mauricio Pochettino is ready to take over as United's new manager

Ferdinand, speaking on Rio Presents, claimed Carrick had been disrespected in the way his dismissal had been handled.

'You do everything you can to make this end in the most amicable and respectful and pleasant way possible,' Ferdinand said.

'I think the way it has been done, it could have been done better. I think the leaks coming out of the club are terrible, they don't reflect well on anybody at all.

'It adds to the disrespect to the person in charge at the time. It is terrible to play out in the public like this. The minimum you should get is a bit of respect.'

Carrick leaves with a record of 14 wins – 12 of which came last season, prior to being made permanent manager – six draws and eight defeats, including exiting this season's Carabao Cup at the first hurdle against Brighton at Old Trafford.
