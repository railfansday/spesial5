Report Barcelona still eager for Bastoni even with €70m asking price

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

There are new reports that Barcelona will schedule meetings for Inter’s Alessandro Bastoni, despite Hansi Flick doubts and the interest growing from Chelsea in the Italy defender.

The centre-back has been on the Blaugrana wish list for several years, and they made a strong push for him over the summer, only to be rejected outright.

His contract only runs to June 2028, and although there are negotiations to extend, they have not reached a conclusion yet and are moving very slowly indeed.

There had been suggestions in recent days that Barcelona had cooled off on Bastoni, because coach Flick was not sure that he would fit well into a four-man defence.

However, Sky Sports Switzerland reporter Sacha Tavolieri and Matteo Moretto on Fabrizio Romano’s Italian language YouTube Channel claim that the Catalans are still eager, planning some new meetings.

The asking price remains €70m, even with the contract running down, and they will discuss the situation between director Deco and coach Flick before deciding whether to dive back in next season.

The most likely time for Bastoni to walk away from San Siro was last summer, when he was still stinging from a series of controversies including his red card in the World Cup play-off against Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Since then, he has won the Scudetto and Coppa Italia Double at Inter, and restored some of his reputation for Italy.
