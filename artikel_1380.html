Rail Issues Ongoing For Coventry Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Everton supporters attending Friday night’s Premier League fixture against Coventry City at Hill Dickinson Stadium are strongly encouraged to allow extra time for their journey and arrive early ahead of the 8pm kick-off.

Supporters travelling from South Liverpool are reminded of the ongoing planned engineering works on the Merseyrail network, resulting in disruption to rail services from South Liverpool.

The engineering works, due to be completed this weekend, will see replacement bus services operating between Hunts Cross and Moorfields, with no rail replacement bus stop at Liverpool Central.
