China’s Foreign Ministry confirmed on Monday that President Xi Jinping will host Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen on Thursday, the first time the three leaders will sit down together. The meeting will take place alongside the APEC summit, after both the White House and the Kremlin announced it. The Wall Street Journal reported last month that Putin first floated the idea as a way to negotiate an end to the war in Ukraine. However, the three sides appear to want different things from the talks. The White House said Trump plans to focus on reducing nuclear weapons and the US-Israeli war with Iran, and does not expect to raise Ukraine. Evercore ISI’s Neo Wang said disagreement over the agenda is now the biggest obstacle. CNBC reported that Quantum Strategy’s David Roche said China is unlikely to accept nuclear arms talks until it reaches parity with the US, possibly a decade away. Peter Alexander of Z-Ben Advisors said simply hosting both leaders gives Xi a major political win. Trump left Washington for a four-day Asia trip that also includes the Philippines.
