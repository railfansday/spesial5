Hedera’s Banking Push Leaves HBAR Waiting For Demand

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Home » Trendsetter News » Hedera’s Banking Push Leaves HBAR Waiting For Demand

Hedera’s banking integrations are expanding, but a rapid demand rise for HBAR remains unproven.

Hedera collects only a few hundred dollars in network fees on a typical day despite HBAR’s multibillion-dollar valuation, according to an analyst examining the gap between institutional adoption and token demand.

Fire Hustle’s central question is whether banks using Hedera will need meaningful HBAR holdings – or simply benefit from its inexpensive infrastructure.

The analyst points to Taurus integrating Hedera into a platform serving more than 40 banks and regulated institutions. That gives those clients access to HBAR custody, staking and token issuance, but access is not evidence that banks have launched products or accumulated tokens.

Hedera denominates transaction fees in dollars and collects payment in HBAR. When the token’s price rises, fewer tokens are needed to cover the same dollar-denominated charge.

That makes costs predictable for businesses. It also weakens the assumption that rising transaction counts must produce proportionately stronger token demand, particularly when fees remain small.

“Who has to hold HBAR and how much,” the analyst asks. The video identifies staking and operational token inventories as possible demand sources, although it does not establish how much HBAR institutions would need to retain.

The infrastructure argument centers on CLPR, which the video describes as technology allowing networks to verify one another’s records cryptographically without an intermediary holding funds.

According to Fire Hustle, Hashgraph contributed it to the Linux Foundation and opened an early-adopter program targeting payments, foreign exchange and collateral movement.

She highlights Swift and Wells Fargo joining the foundation on the same day as the contribution. But the qualification matters: “There’s no official announcement that Swift is using Clipper.” Shared foundation membership does not establish a deployment.

The video also places Hedera among participants in a UK tokenization initiative, citing a live on-chain repo trial targeted for April 2027 and led by BlackRock and NatWest. It does not establish that Hedera or CLPR will power that trial; other named participants include Ripple, Coinbase and Circle.
