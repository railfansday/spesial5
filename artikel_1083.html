By HARRY BAMFORTH, LIVE SPORTS COVERAGE REPORTER

Let's take a look at who the big inclusions and omissions are in this squad because there are a few.

Thomas Tuchel on Ben White being booed back in March:

It's going to be interesting to see the reaction to Ben White's first touch as an England player again.

Back in March, when he first returned from his exile, there were huge boos from the Wembley crowd. They were clearly on Gareth Southgate's side and not White's.

But they can't boo their own forever. White may have missed the World Cup through injury, but I expect him to become England's No.1 right-back under Thomas Tuchel; the German clearly likes him.

Tuchel has made it clear he wants White well supported, not booed.

Alvaro Arbeloa, Fulham boss, on Josh King's selection:

I think there were many people who were surprised when the Fulham youngster was called up to the World Cup pre-camp extended squad in the summer, myself included. But we are not surprised by his inclusion this time.

The 19-year-old has been a shining light for Fulham in what has been a topsy-turvy beginning to the season. His performance against Chelsea on the opening weekend still sticks out in my mind; the boy is a baller.

Many Fulham fans have been making their cases to friends around the country about why King should play for Fulham and now they may feel vindicated.

What a moment for the youngster, and the first call-up of many.
