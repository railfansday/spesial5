Thomas Tuchel disappointed with England fans booing Ben White: ‘I thought we were past that’

Thomas Tuchel is confident a resilient Ben White can cope with the boo boys but hopes England fans will allow the defender to start a new chapter.

The 29-year-old made himself unavailable for the national team under Sir Gareth Southgate having left Qatar during the knockout phase of the 2022 World Cup for “personal reasons”.

White reportedly fell out with former assistant Steve Holland and only returned to the England set-up under Tuchel in March, when he was booed by fans before missing the World Cup through injury.

The Arsenal defender this week said supporters would not react like that if they knew the full story, but he refused to “throw anyone under the bus” and was jeered having been brought on in Thursday’s 2-1 Nations League win against Croatia.

Put to Tuchel that even Croatian substitute Luka Modric got a warmer reception than England introduction White, he said with a laugh: “I didn’t hear the boos, to be honest.

“I heard the claps for Luka Modric, which is nice because he deserves it, he’s a legend of the game, but I honestly didn’t hear the boos for Ben. I thought we were past that chapter.

“I hope it doesn’t happen again. He’s a player of ours. Since I’m in charge, he’s desperate to come and to be with the group. He doesn’t put a foot wrong since I’m in charge.

“I can only judge him on what I see and what I experienced, and it is on the highest level, and he can really help us to win football matches.

“Like I said, he’s part of our group, and we will protect our group, and are protective of our group. He deserves to be with us and, yeah, I hope we can open a new chapter for him.”

There were fewer boos than in March’s friendlies and Tuchel is confident such a reaction will not see White walk away again should negativity continue.

“I don’t think so,” he said. “I don’t want to make it bigger than it is. I don’t think so, I don’t hope so.

“He seemed to me always committed and always dedicated and excited to come into camp and get the call-up.

“He had too many injuries. He’s a high-quality player, and I honestly don’t even know what happened and what were the deep reasons in the situation that he left camp.

“So, like I said, (since) I’m in charge, there is no reason to doubt his commitment or passion. I don’t want to think in these extreme terms at the moment.

“He overcame the boos the last time. I didn’t hear them this time and he can play on a high level. He proves it week in, week out for Arsenal.”

The White situation was a sideshow on a night that saw Harry Kane cap his record-breaking 126th England appearance with a match-winning penalty after Morgan Rogers’ goal was cancelled out by Petar Sucic.

The 2-1 win rubber-stamped their place in March’s Nations League quarter-finals and keeps hopes alive of topping Group A3 ahead of Sunday’s trip to leaders Spain.

Tuchel said: “It cannot always be glamorous and super exciting. Sometimes just to be serious and determined, and we got the job done.

“We wanted the win, we wanted a ‘final’ on Sunday in Spain, and we have it.”
