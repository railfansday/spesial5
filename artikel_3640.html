It's weird to type this out, but the GTA 6 release is finally upon us after a 13-year wait.

Red Dead Redemption 2 launched to acclaim at the time to ease the wait, though few things in gaming feel as big an event as a Grand Theft Auto release from Rockstar.

And thankfully, the wait looks to be worth it. The GamesRadar+ GTA 6 review lands on a perfect score, and says "Rockstar hasn't just set a new benchmark, it has delivered the highest-fidelity sandbox of the generation." It's not just us, either. The GTA 6 Metacritic score is a good 'un. In fact, only the OG Zelda: Ocarina of Time can match it.

So, we're probably on the same page. It's time to get the download going, and thoughts to our gaming brethren on PC who don't own a console.

To celebrate the moment, we're firing up the live blog to put everything you need to know in one, evolving space – from review buzz to launch requirements to exactly how the GTA 6 release time rollout is working. On that note, let's crack on.

So! The game is good, though I have an inkling that plenty of you have already pre-ordered it. According to Sensor Tower, Rockstar has already made $1.45 billion from GTA 6, aided by nearly 90% of us buying the controversial $100 Ultimate Edition. Oof!

I imagine a few more people will be tossing some more money Rockstar's way.

We're not the only ones who dig this one either. The GTA 6 Metacritic score ranks it among the most popular games of all time. The only competition is The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. The original one, that is. Mind you, the remake is pretty nifty, too.

First things first. The main talking point today, as we count down the hours to launch, is critics' verdicts on GTA 6. As per our own GTA 6 review, we've landed on a perfect score.

"GTA 6 is the highest-fidelity sandbox of its generation," he says. "Rockstar has built an open world of truly astonishing scale, scope, and quality.
