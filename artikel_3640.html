How to get the Zee Sprite in Fortnite

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Zee Sprite is one of several new options we have in Fortnite Treasure Hunters, and it’s a pretty unique one to collect. I really love the in-game ability of this Sprite, but the harder task is to add it to your collection.

The Zee Sprite is of Mythical rarity, making it one of the hardest ones to collect. If you’re relying on luck, you’ll need to open every Rare and Sprite chest possible to stand a chance. Mythic rarity has very low drop rates to begin with. Additionally, there’s no guaranteed for the Mythic Drop to be a Zee Sprite.

What you can do instead is to collect as many other Sprites as possible. Once you have your collection almost filled up (ideally, you should have all but the Zee Sprite), use the Lucky Locator. When you do so, it will help you to obtain a Zee Sprite. Then, it’s all about surviving and extracting the Sprite to add it permanently to your collection.

Finally, you can kill as many players as possible and hope to get lucky. This is precisely how I managed to get it on my end. Once again, it’s all a matter of luck.

If you equip the Zee Sprite, you’ll be able to use Zero Point Dash charges. This ability allows you to dash forward when you press the jump button while you’re in the air. As you level up the Sprite, you’ll increase the number of charges you get.

The boost you get to your movement makes this a great choice if you like to engage in player fights.
