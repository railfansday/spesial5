Which 'Love Is Blind' Season 11 Couples Got Married and Who Broke Up?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Love Is Blind season 11 delivered its usual mix of altar shockers, cold-feet confessionals and post-pod reality checks.

Despite the Netflix experiment chronicling six pod engagements tackling the next steps, only one couple actually made it down the aisle, while the rest unraveled at various stages. Scroll below to see where every pairing stands heading into the reunion:

Jax broke up with Stew moments after meeting in person.

“[I was like], ‘My God, this is so intense,'” she told Us in October 2026 of the experiment. “And then when we sat down, I think it really kind of, I was like, ‘OK, this doesn’t feel right.'”

They cleared the air after leaving pods, becoming friends.

As for Jax’s engagement ring, Stew told Us, “Word on the street is, if you catch her walking around Boston, she still has the ring. You have to stay tuned to the reunion.”

In a teaser for the Love Is Blind reunion, Jax revealed that she wears the diamond “every day.”

Michaella ended her engagement to Rob days before their wedding day following his outburst at their Pod Squad meetup.

“I just, like, don’t deserve any of what I’m getting,” she admitted to costar Erika Ellis in a bathroom confessional. “I feel like he hates me. I love him, and it’s just a lot. We’re not getting married. I cannot marry this man. Under any circumstances, I cannot marry him at this point. … I’ll be OK.”

Erika and Blake broke up before the respective bachelor and bachelorette parties. Erika, for her part, had lingering “questions” about the yacht captain’s international boating schedule and when they would actually have time together.

The couple broke up right after the cast’s bachelor and bachelorette parties when Wilson didn’t think Wilnie was “nurturing” enough to be a wife. Wilnie, meanwhile, said in a confessional that she was “heartbroken” but doesn’t regret the journey since she gave the relationship and experiment “[her] all.” Wilson noted in a separate interview that it was “fair to say” their relationship and engagement were over.

Despite Aneha expressing that she’ll love Cam “for the rest of [her] life,” it was admittedly “weird” how her parents were absent from the ceremony over their disapproval of the experiment. In the end, Aneha tearfully said at the altar that she was “freaking out” over whether to say “I do.” She ultimately shared she wasn’t ready to get married because they needed “more time.” Cam understood Aneha’s hesitation because it was a “big decision,” stressing that he loves her.

“Cam at 28, just not husband material,” Aneha said in a confessional of his communication struggles. “He needs more time. He’s not fully cooked yet, right? Cam has the makings of a great husband. I’ve never loved someone like I’ve loved Cam; now that he’s in my life, I can’t imagine my life without him. It’s like, we should be married, right? It’s just not the right time though.”

While Aneha wanted to leave their wedding still engaged, Cam told his parents he doesn’t “know what happens now” with regard to their relationship.

Cam and Aneha spoke after the ceremony, with the Netflix groom saying he needs “some time to process everything” about whether their relationship will progress.

“I don’t want to step away,” he acknowledged. “I love you and care about you, like, why couldn’t we say yes, you know? I think I just need a second to process the experience.”

After navigating “long conversations” about differing healthcare and political beliefs, Sarah and John were the only season 11 couple to get married in the finale.

“We did it, we’re married! Love is blind,” John said in a joint confessional with his bride. “Sure, I might have been happyish by myself, but not even close to how happy I could be with the right person.”

He also told Sarah that he makes him a “better person” and that he loves her, gushing, “I’m excited for a life with you.”

Love Is Blind is currently streaming on Netflix. The season 11 reunion drops Sunday, November 8, at 9 p.m. ET.
