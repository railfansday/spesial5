Rachael Leigh Cook Wants This 'She's All That' Costar as Hallmark Leading Man

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Rachael Leigh Cook’s Laney Boggs got her happily ever after in She’s All That — but which of her former castmates does she want to cross over to Hallmark as her next leading man?

“Dulé Hill is an incredible romantic lead,” Cook, 47, exclusively told Us while promoting her latest Hallmark rom-com, Double Booked for the Holidays. “Oh my God! Him in the movie Holes, I cry every time.”

She gushed over Hill, 51, and his leading man energy in 2003’s Holes, adding, “I cannot. So handsome,” telling Us she’s “probably” pick him to be her opposite on a Hallmark project.

While Hill portrayed Sam in Holes — who is not the lead, but does have a starcrossed romance — it was his role as cool kid Preston in 1999’s She’s All That where he first crossed paths with Cook, who played leading lady Laney.

Hill and Cook later shared the small screen on Psych when she guest starred as Abigail Lytar in several episodes from 2008 to 2010. Abigail was one of psychic detective Shawn Spencer’s (James Roday Rodriguez) girlfriends on the hit USA Network series, while Hill played Shawn’s BFF and sidekick Burton “Gus” Guster.

In She’s All That, meanwhile, Laney falls for the boy next door, Zack Siler (Freddie Prinze Jr.). While trying to turn the quiet, art nerd into the next prom queen to win a bet, Zack catches feelings for his neighbor, and the pair spark a real romance.

In addition to working with Hill again, Cook said she’d tap Tamara Mello, who played one of her “tormentors” Chandler in the rom-com, to play opposite her in a Hallmark film.

The actress explained that Mello, 50, “Is actually a really good friend of mine, and she’s one of the funniest people I’ve ever met.”

While fans might’ve guessed Cook would pick Prinze Jr., 50, to join the Hallmark family since they had such great chemistry in the ‘90s film, she told Us they already have something else on the books.

“Freddie and I are definitely gonna, we’ve already talked about doing something together again,” she teased. “So of course I want to work with him, but we already have something planned that’s not quite for Hallmark, so I don’t think I would drag him in quite on that plan, just to not distract from what we’re trying to do.”

Fox Entertainment Studio announced in May 2025 that Cook and Prinze will reunite for The Christmas Affair, which has yet to receive a release date.

The pair will play “two famous sports broadcasters [who] bond over their cheating spouses during the holiday season,” according to the logline. “As they navigate the media frenzy and Christmas festivities together, unexpected feelings emerge — but timing isn’t always everything.”

As fans wait for more on Cook and Prinze’s next collaboration, they can watch the actress in Hallmark’s Double Booked for the Holidays.

In the movie, Cook plays twins Abigail and Charlie, who agree to run a bookstore in Hay-on-Wye in Wales during Christmastime. Along the way, the pair dive into a decades-long mystery about the town’s most famous author and stumble upon not one, but two romances of their own.

“I had a great time. I’m not gonna sit here and tell you that the world needed two more of me in a movie. Let’s be realistic,” Cook told Us with a laugh. “But I do know that no one was ever gonna call me up and say, ‘Do you want to play your own twin again?’”

She added, “Sometimes, when an opportunity presents itself, that’s that unique and special. You just have to say yes. You don’t ask questions. You just go, ‘I hope I can pull this off.’”

Double Booked for the Holidays premieres on Hallmark Channel Sunday, November 22, at 8 p.m. ET and streams the next day on Hallmark+.
