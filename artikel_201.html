The US national cyber director is stepping down

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

White House national cyber director Sean Cairncross will be stepping down from his role, Axios reported. The news comes as Democrats prepare to take control of Congress and as businesses across the country report a flurry of technical outages whose cause remains unknown.

Cairncross, the president’s principal adviser on national cybersecurity policy and strategy, took office in August 2025. Since then, he has met with top AI leaders and helped the Trump administration make key AI decisions, according to Axios. The outlet also reports that the split between Cairncross and the White House was amicable, and his departure was timed so that the White House could nab a Senate confirmation for his role before Democrats take control of Congress in January.

Cairncross, a lawyer and longtime Republican operative, had no cybersecurity background when the Senate confirmed him 59–35. He previously served as the Republican National Committee’s chief operating officer and, during Trump’s first term, as CEO of the Millennium Challenge Corporation, a federal foreign aid agency. The office hasn’t been around long; Congress created it in early 2021, and Cairncross was only its third Senate-confirmed director. The first director, Chris Inglis, left after about 19 months, and the job then went about 10 months without a permanent director.

We’ve reached out to the White House for comment.
