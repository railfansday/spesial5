Elon Musk joins 165,000 others calling for Nvidia to bring back Jensen Huang in open letter

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Silicon Valley figures back CEO ousted after revelations of affair with suspected Chinese spy, as US treasury secretary urges Nvidia to carry out ‘dramatic shake-up’

Elon Musk and a host of other tech executives are among more than 165,000 signatories of an open letter calling for Jensen Huang’s reinstatement as Nvidia chief, days after he resigned from the multi-trillion dollar chip company.

The letter demands that the company’s board members “bring Jensen back while an independent investigation establishes the facts”.

Huang’s resignation on Thursday ended a 33-year run at the helm of the AI giant he co-founded, after revelations he had at least two extramarital affairs, one with a woman accused of spying for China and another with a subordinate.

US lawmakers from both sides of the aisle have demanded Huang and Nvidia appear before Congress to explain whether US national security had been put at risk – with some in the president’s own party suggesting that Donald Trump’s administration must also must answer questions on what it knew about Huang’s relationship with the suspected Chinese spy.

According to the Financial Times, Huang has discussed a possible return to the company with people close to him. One Huang ally told the paper they were putting together ⁠a “war room” of Silicon Valley executives to lobby ​for the former Nvidia ‌chief’s return ‌and would try to garner support from ‌the White House.

“Everyone that matters in tech outside the frontier labs loves Jensen,” the person said, adding that the efforts had Huang’s support.

On Friday, two major shareholders, investors Brad Gerstner and Gavin Baker, urged the board to keep “all options” open.

In Washington, however, US treasury secretary Scott Bessent argued on Sunday that Nvidia should look “outside” the company for its next chief. “They need a dramatic shake-up,” Bessent said on Fox News.

Bessent also called on Nvidia to explain whether it knowingly allowed its most advanced AI chips to reach China, saying this was more important than Huang’s affair with the alleged spy for Beijing.

The US forbids sales to China of the most cutting-edge AI chips, as parts of its strategy to stay ahead of Beijing in the race to dominate artificial intelligence.

In late 2025, Trump authorised sales of Nvidia chips called H200s to the Chinese – but not the most advanced chips used to run AI models reshaping modern society.

“Nothing above the H200s can be sold to the Chinese,” Bessent said, adding that it was important to examine “whether Nvidia had a culture of compliance around the US export restrictions”.

Bessent said he wanted answers from Nvidia and its internal investigation of Jensen Huang’s behaviour within the next 10 days.

Alongside Musk, the tech heavyweight signatories on the letter calling for Huang’s return include Tim Sweeney, founder and CEO of video game publisher Epic Games, and Garry Tan, president of Y Combinator, San Francisco’s most renowned business incubator.

Another signatory, Uber co-founder Travis Kalanick, wrote that Huang had “erred, yes – to err is to be human – but I do not believe anything he has done rises to the bar of separating him from his company”.

More than 7,500 signatories identified themselves as Nvidia employees, equivalent to 18% of the company’s workforce.

The support illustrates the unique standing of Huang, who is seen as a godfather of AI within the industry. Under his leadership, Nvidia became a pivotal Silicon Valley investor, financing datacentres and startups, earning it the nickname “the central bank of AI”.

But the company has also faced criticism that it has fuelled a speculative investment bubble. Huang fought any constraints on the AI race, clashing on these issues with Claude creator Anthropic and its chief Dario Amodei.

Jensen Huang and other Nvidia officials have agreed to testify before Congress, according to officials, but no date has been set yet.
