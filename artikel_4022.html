Zelda: Ocarina Of Time Remake Crowned 2026's "Best-Selling Physical Video Game In The US"

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake for Switch 2 has got off to an outstanding start, selling over five million copies (approx 5.2 million units) in just four days. Sales in the UK and Japan have contributed to this, and now, Circana Retail Tracking Service has shared data about the title's US launch.

As highlighted by Circana's senior director and video game industry advisor Mat Piscatella (via social media), "after just one week", Ocarina of Time for Switch 2 is this year's "best-selling physical video game in the US". Interestingly, it's the first title to surpass one million physical units in a US launch week since the 2023 Switch title, The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom.

Considering the year is almost over, and Grand Theft Auto 6's retail release is actually a "code-in-box", we imagine Ocarina's remake will hold onto the title of 2026's best-selling physical game (that's actually a proper physical game). However, there is one technicality - Circana tracks and reports "code-in-box" sales as "new physical video game sales", so Zelda may not actually keep the crown. And GTA 6 has already smashed pre-order records.

According to Nintendo's official data, Ocarina of Time is currently the second-highest selling Switch 2 game globally. As we previously noted, this puts it ahead of Donkey Kong Bananza's lifetime sales of 5.05 million, and the Switch 2 launch title Mario Kart World leads with 16.48 million sales. So it's still a tremendous effort for the remake of the legendary N64 title.

In our review here on Nintendo Life, we awarded Ocarina of Time's Switch 2 remake 9/10 stars, calling it the "best way to play one of the most seminal games of all time". After a brief dip to 95, it's once again sitting on a Metacritic score of 96/100.
