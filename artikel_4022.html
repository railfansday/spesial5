My greatest regret about Grand Theft Auto 6's release is that I didn't make an 'angry players' bingo card beforehand. Seriously, with how predictable all the outrage is, I could've had the winning card by now, and the game's not even been out for 24 hours yet.

What would I have on my bingo card? Well, a couple of things that'd be on there are: angry at 30 fps, angry at shooting police (a classic), and slap-bang in the middle I'd have angry that you can't cheat (the other kind of cheat) on your partner.

It seems that there's a group of players who are taking issue with the fact that GTA 6's story revolves around Jason and Lucia's relationship and therefore you can't pick up other women like strippers or Mandy, an early side character who players seem to be quite preoccupied with right now. Although, I haven't heard anyone complain that Lucia can't get with other guys.

You can still go to strip clubs, though. You can even go as a couple; how modern. But Rockstar hasn't added the ability to do much more simply because it doesn't fit with the story. In a statement to Shacknews Rockstar explains:

"GTA 6 is a game about a couple in a bind, lovers on the run. While we have taken a lot forward from previous GTA titles, everything we have implemented in [GTA 6] has been done to support the spirit of the story we wanted to tell, and the gameplay systems we wanted to expand upon—things like the wanted system, the radio, robberies and car theft. Many aspects of previous titles are not present in GTA 6, but the elements we have we believe are all necessary and complementary to the feeling and motivations we wanted to emphasise as you play through Jason and Lucia's story."

Honestly, it makes sense. Given the fact that this story clearly revolves around the two of them you'd probably have a considerably worse time towards the end if there was an option to break the couple up. It's bad enough running into your ex in public, but imagine having to do a bank heist with them? Yeah, no thanks.

I'm sure once (or whenever) GTA 6 online comes out players will be able to do whatever they please. But for now you'll have to stay within the confine of GTA 6's storylines, which means being in a loving relationship, how sad.
