PS5 Has Officially Crossed 100 Million Sales, Sony Confirms

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The PS5 has now officially surpassed the 100 million sales milestone.

This news comes via Sony itself, which has just released newly updated figures for the current-gen console.

On the Sony Interactive Entertainment website, it states PS5 has sold "more than 100 million" units as of 10th November, 2026.

This means that, since the end of the last financial quarter (30th September 2026), Sony has shifted another 2.1 million machines.

It's taken slightly longer for PS5 to reach 100 million than its predecessor, the PS4, which was reportedly the fastest ever console to hit the milestone.

Given the many hurdles PS5 has contended with during its lifespan — launching amid the COVID-19 pandemic, shortages, hardware prices skyrocketing, etc. — it's quite remarkable it's managed to maintain such a healthy momentum.

It's possible this recent surge in PS5 sales is related to the imminent arrival of GTA 6, as well as the usual bump due to the approaching holiday season.

In any case, PS5 joins the list of consoles that have surpassed the milestone, and it's the fourth Sony home console to achieve it.

Sony also reports that PS5 is its most profitable generation ever, so all-in-all, it's been a very good generation for business.

The console still has a bit of time left before the inevitable PS6, which is expected to arrive within the next couple of years; whether Sony can keep the momentum going into the new generation remains to be seen.
