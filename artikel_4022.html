Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble canceled

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free-to-play multiplayer arena shooter Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble has been canceled, publisher Sony Interactive Entertainment and developer Oh BiBi announced.

The game soft-launched for iOS and Android in the Philippines and Indonesia alongside its announcement in November 2025, followed by Norway, Sweden, Finland, and Denmark in December 2025, Canada in May 2026, and the United Kingdom in August 2026.

In a message posted to the game’s official website, Oh BiBi said a final update is being released today, and the game will be removed from storefronts on November 21. Servers will officially close on May 1, 2027.

■ Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble Update (November 6, 2026)

We’ve made the difficult decision to cease development of Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble.

The final planned update will be rolling out November 6, 2026, and the app will be removed from stores on November 21, 2026.

Players who already have the game will be able to continue playing until May 1, 2027, when the game’s servers will permanently shut down.

On behalf of the dev team and everyone helping to make the game, thanks to our amazing community for all your support, especially to those who have spent time playing, sharing feedback, and helping shape Ranger Rumble throughout its development!

Game servers will permanently shut down on May 1, 2027.

Can I continue playing after the game is removed from stores?

If you already installed the game, you will be able to continue playing the game until End of Service on May 1, 2027.

Will I still be able to download and reinstall the game?

If you’re an existing player, Yes, until November 21, 2026, after that the game will be delisted from Apple’s App Store and Google Play.

Will the game still work after the servers have been shut down?

No, once the game servers are shutdown on May 1, 2027, the game will no longer load.

Will there be any additional game updates?

No, there are no planned updates after November 7, 2026.

Will existing game modes, multiplayer matchmaking and competitive features remain available?

All current game modes, matchmaking and competitive features will remain active until End of Service on May 1, 2027.

Will there be any final events, rewards or opportunities to unlock content?

The Qwark Pass seasons will continue to run until May 1, 2027, and the Super Pass is now free, and all content can be earned throughout each season.

What happens to my Quark Pass and any unclaimed rewards?

You have until End of Service on May 1, 2027, to enter, claim and utilize any rewards earned through play.

No, in-app purchases have been removed. You can continue to earn rewards through play and spending that currency on upgrades, cosmetics, characters and currencies.

What happens to my unused in-game currency?

You have until End of Service on May 1, 2027, to use any currency you’ve earned or purchased.

Can I request a refund for previous purchases?

Your eligibility for a refund for any purchases made will be through Apple’s App Store or Google Play.

What happens to my account and game progress after shut-down?

All player data will be wiped from the servers at the End of Service, May 1, 2027. If you want to delete your data ahead of May 1, 2027, you can contact support here.

Can I transfer my progress, purchases or unlocked items to another game?

No, your progress and items are not transferable to any other game.

What happens if I registered for the game or earned pre-registration rewards?

Pre-registration rewards will be available to all players logging into the November 7, 2026 update.

What happens to my personal data after the game closes?

All player data will be deleted at End of Service, on May 1, 2027.

Does this affect other Ratchet & Clank games or PlayStation services?

No, Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble‘s ceasing development has no impact on other Ratchet & Clank games or PlayStation services.
