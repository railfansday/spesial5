Xbox CEO Announces Major New Bethesda Games In The Works, Including Wolfenstein 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

"Wolfenstein 3 is officially in production at MachineGames"

Xbox boss Asha Sharma has been taking the opportunity today to announce multiple new Bethesda games that are in the works as Xbox first-party titles, including the long-awaited Wolfenstein 3.

Sharma made the announcement on social media while highlighting that Wolfenstein is "one of gaming's most important franchises", and the third entry is once again being helmed by Bethesda studio MachineGames.

Here's a look at her post with an accompanying teaser image:

Prior to this, the Xbox CEO also revealed that Bethesda studio id Software has begun work on its next game, while also hyping up a new "Slaughter Mode" update coming to DOOM: The Dark Ages Revelations next week.

The Verge editor Tom Warren reckons id Software's next title will be another entry in the DOOM series:

It sounds like both of these projects are still very much in their infancy as things stand, although it was rumoured back at the start of 2026 that performance capture was set to begin on the next Wolfenstein title.
