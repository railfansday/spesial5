We got confirmation a couple of months ago that the US Army's 3rd Infantry Division was offering a four-day pass to play Grand Theft Auto 6 for soldiers who re-enlist for at least two years. And now the time has come to collect.

Talking to Task & Purpose one soldier who took advantage of the program, Sgt. Jamire Smith, described how he's planning to spend the long weekend: "I'm all campaign. I’m all about the heist. I think I have a good 24 hours to waste on it.

"I was actually in the process of getting out, I was towards my final stages of getting out … but my company and the battalion helped me navigate through that and they showed me that there’s still a need for me, and that I am still a clear and value asset to the team, and so that’s really what drove me to say 'let’s keep going, let’s keep driving'. Maybe be a lifer in this, since I already made it my career."

In total 43 soldiers re-enlisted through the GTA 6 incentive, an idea that was born from Staff Sgt. Brittany Wolfer.

"It's not supposed to be the deciding factor," Wolfer says. "There is always going to be a disconnect between your senior command and your lowest soldier. So how do you bridge that gap to where that lower soldier still knows that their battalion commander or their senior leadership cares about them and sees what they're interested in. And that’s kind of what this was for."

Wolfer first brought the idea to her battalion commander who actually played GTA 2 back in the day and so was up for trying the incentive out. "I came into [the unit], there hadn’t been a counsellor for about eight months, so there was a lot of gaps and things that were falling through because the soldiers didn’t have that presence within the formation," Wolfer adds. "This kind of was just a way to grab people's attention and kind of be like, 'Hey, here I am. I’m the person you’re gonna come talk to,' and [it] kind of snowballed from there."
