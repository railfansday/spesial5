The Gulf's oil-eating microbes lay in wait before Deepwater Horizon spill, frozen samples suggest

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A year before the Deepwater Horizon spill, a NOAA crew scooped up seawater from the Gulf and froze it. Sixteen years later, FIU biologist Shen Jean Lim tested it only to discover the microbes that devoured much of the oil in 2010 were nowhere to be found in 2009. So where did they come from?
