December 1 Is Officially A Big Day For Xbox Game Pass

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Big things are coming to the Xbox Game Pass on December 1, when two great games join the games catalog for members. If that's not enough, several more exciting titles are scheduled for addition to the Game Pass.

These games join the growing list of 900 titles that are included in the members' library. New games are added frequently, including day-one releases. That's the case on December 1, when one tiny mouse goes on a big adventure with a day-one release joining the catalog.

Also arriving on December 1 is The Outlast Trials, Red Barrels' highly rated 2023 co-op survival horror game. You and a group of friends can play through Cold War-era therapy that consists of good-old-fashioned brainwashing and mind control. Survive through a series of challenges and horrific scenarios designed to test your strength, both physical and mental.

Finally, World of Tanks fans will receive some in-game benefits on December 1. Blitz players will receive their monthly bonus on the PC, while Modern Armor will get an XP Stock bundle on console, which is full of goodies to level up and progress faster in the game.

A few more games are coming to the service soon: Motorslice and Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts 2 join the catalog on November 24. The 3-person PvP tower-climbing new title Arkheron hits the Game Pass on November 27 as a day-one Game Preview (aka early access).
