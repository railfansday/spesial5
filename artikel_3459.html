Avengers: Doomsday Will Officially Bring Back One of the MCU's Best Heroes

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Maybe that Young Avengers dream isn't dead after all

The Avengers are set to get a little more help in December's Avengers: Doomsday. In an Instagram post on Sunday, Hailee Steinfeld confirmed she would return to the Marvel Cinematic Universe as Kate Bishop/Hawkeye while promoting her new movie Hexed. In the video, she pans to a director's chair with her name on it, like how the Doomsday cast was initially revealed in an hours-long stream last year.

On Monday, Nov. 23, Marvel released the final trailer for Avengers: Doomsday. It includes a brief shot of Steinfeld's Hawkeye amid the fray.

Steinfeld joined the MCU in 2021's Hawkeye, a Disney Plus show heavily inspired by Matt Fraction and David Aja's mid-2010s Hawkeye comics. As a kid, Kate Bishop watched Hawkeye defend New York City during the events of The Avengers, inspiring her to pick up the bow and arrow herself. She later meets Jeremy Renner's Hawkeye in a post-Endgame world, and the pair get up to various shenanigans around Christmas time.

She makes an end-credits cameo appearance in 2023's The Marvels as Kamala Khan/Ms. Marvel (Iman Vellani) does her best Nick Fury impression; Khan wants to put together a group of heroes herself. This led to a lot of fan speculation that Marvel might have a Young Avengers project in the works, but Marvel hasn't announced anything like that. However, with Cassie Lang also appearing in Doomsday and Billy and Tommy Maximoff firmly in the MCU, who knows what Marvel is planning for its young heroes next.

You'll see Steinfeld as Hawkeye (but not Renner... that we know of), Chris Evans as Steve Rogers, Florence Pugh as Black Widow, and more heroes in Avengers: Doomsday when it hits theaters Dec. 18.
