'Game Of Thrones Meets Outlander' Fantasy Series Reveals New Title & 2027 Release Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Game of Thrones fans looking for a new fantasy series with the same political intrigue may have found the perfect replacement, with an exciting new update on the next entry in the saga.

It's been over 15 years since the most recent entry in the A Song of Ice and Fire series, with A Dance with Dragons arriving back in 2011. While George R.R. Martin continues working on the long-awaited The Winds of Winter, there's still no official release date in sight. HBO has kept Westeros alive through its expanding slate of television adaptations, but readers hoping for another sprawling fantasy epic that scratches the same itch have had to look elsewhere.

One such alternative has been a major hit among fantasy readers, and the New York Times #1 best-selling adventure features "the page-turning politics of Game of Thrones [and] the worlds-spanning romance of Outlander," according to the author's official website, with a brand-new release just announced for 2027.

Ilona Andrews' Maggie the Undying series is officially returning in 2027, with the bestselling author revealing some exciting new details about the next installment in the fantasy saga.

On November 10, 2026, Ilona Andrews officially announced that the second book will be titled This Crown Will Not Rule Me, with a confirmed release date of September 21, 2027. The announcement also included a first look at the novel's cover artwork by Helena Elias, alongside a new story synopsis, which you can check out below.

For those unfamiliar, Ilona Andrews is actually the pen name of husband-and-wife writing duo Ilona and Gordon Andrews, who are best known for their bestselling Kate Daniels, Hidden Legacy, and Innkeeper Chronicles fantasy series.

Their latest series, Maggie the Undying, is a planned trilogy that began with This Kingdom Will Not Kill Me in March 2026. It follows Maggie, a devoted fantasy reader who unexpectedly finds herself transported into the world of her favorite unfinished book series.

The newly revealed description for This Crown Will Not Rule Me teases a dangerous new adventure:

The kingdom of Rellas is safe—but for how long? Having averted a fateful attack on the capital, Maggie must now keep its people safe from an even deadlier struggle for the crown. Unfortunately, her allies keep getting in her way: Everard, who is ever the more insistent on making Maggie his duchess; the knight Arvel, who is determined to uncover her secrets; and a mysterious religious sect that continues to pester her for unknown reasons. If that’s not enough, the plot is no longer as Maggie remembers.

Beyond the political intrigue, the sequel introduces a dangerous "monster extermination" known as the Culling, which is arriving a year earlier than expected with "thrice as many nests." With Maggie's knowledge of the original story becoming increasingly unreliable, her apparent immortality may not be enough to save everyone she cares about.

You can pre-order This Crown Will Not Rule Me via Amazon here.

The first book in the series has already proven enormously successful, becoming a New York Times and USA Today bestseller and earning a nomination for Readers' Favorite Fantasy in the 2026 Goodreads Choice Awards. With that kind of momentum behind it, Maggie the Undying is quickly establishing itself as an exciting alternative for fantasy readers looking for their next Game of Thrones fix.
