All the Ways Princess Kate Paid Tribute to Queen Elizabeth II and Princess Diana on Remembrance Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

As the future queen of England, Princess Catherine will always bring a little piece of history with her.

Over the weekend, the British royal family celebrated Remembrance Day—a national day of memorial to honor service members and civilians who died in World War I, World War II, and subsequent military conflicts. For the two-day occasion, Kate dressed in elegant black looks, which she accessorized with layers of meaningful pieces.

On Saturday, to attend the Royal British Legion Festival of Remembrance event at London’s Royal Albert Hall, Kate slipped on a black-and-white polka-dot dress by Alessandra Rich, and added some sparkle with Queen Elizabeth II’s Orbital pendant earrings. The late monarch first publicly wore the two-tier diamond earrings to the state opening of parliament in May 2012, and later loaned them to Kate in 2016. The princess inherited the jewelry after the queen’s passing in 2022.

Top top off her dotted look, Kate pinned on a red poppy brooch from Izzy Ager of Lock & Co. (The poppy is a symbol of Remembrance Day.), and her Irish Guards Regimental Brooch.

On Sunday, the Princess of Wales stepped out in a sophisticated black Catherine Walker coat (one of her favorites), that she adorned with her HMS Glasgow brooch and three red poppies. She also brought a little of the late Princess Diana with her for the special day by accessorizing with Lady Di’s famous Collingwood pearl drop earrings. Each earring features a diamond stud and a diamond bell cap atop a shiny white pearl. The pieces were gifted to Diana in 1981, for her wedding to then-Prince Charles, by London jeweler Collingwood (a favorite of the Spencer family).
