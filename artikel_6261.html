Setting Sail With Kids? These Are the Best Cruise Lines for Families

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The best cruise lines for families with kids and teens are likely more diverse than you realize. Many are the big-ship brands you see in ads, tricked out with waterslides, dazzling shows, and an abundance of onboard restaurants to satisfy picky toddlers and ravenous teens alike. Yet other family-friendly cruise lines let mom and dad get the taste of luxury they crave or provide the entire crew with adventure and active pursuits around the globe.

I’ve sailed on more than 80 cruises, sometimes with my kids, often surrounded by everyone else’s. I can tell you that the best family cruises won’t make bedtime any easier, but they will do their best to make sure no one in your crew ever feels the need to cry, “I’m so bored!”

A cruise line that devotes a day to all things LEGO and offers a special baby laundry service gets top marks in my list of the best cruises for kids and teens. MSC Cruises is a European big-ship cruise line that’s making waves in the US with its family-friendly mega-ships.

Five kids clubs for babies, preschoolers, kids, tweens, and teens target activities to different age groups. MSC is constantly developing new family programs, including family discos, original game shows, drone races for teens, MasterChef at Sea cooking challenges, and obstacle course competitions.

World Class ships feature the top-deck Family Aventura District with an outdoor adventure area (including an overwater swing ride), water park, and an indoor space for rollerskating and bumper cars. Select ships offer arcades with a Formula 1 simulator and virtual reality attractions or the Robotron robotic-arm thrill ride. The line’s US–based ships also feature connecting cabins and family-friendly dining (the pizza and gelato are superb).

Families looking for an exclusive hideaway can book rooms in The Yacht Club, with access to a private restaurant, lounge, sun deck, and pool—all ages welcome.

Standout itinerary: 7-night Caribbean and Bahamas cruise on MSC World America sailing round-trip from Miami

Royal Caribbean operates the world’s largest, most activity-laden ships, and its calling card is high-energy fun for all ages.

Families can rock climb, zip-line, ice skate, splash down waterslides, solve escape room challenges, and enjoy entertainment ranging from musical performances to acrobatic high-dive dance shows without leaving the ship. The enormous Icon Class ships even have a dedicated family deck called Surfside, where kid-friendly restaurants, splash playgrounds, and a carousel are surrounded by cabins and suites designed to accommodate families.

On all ships, parents can steal away for adults-only fun when they drop the littlest cruisers at onboard nurseries run by professional caregivers. Kids can make crafts, play games, and do science experiments at onboard camps operating morning until late in spacious, tech-enhanced multipurpose spaces. Teens have their own hip hangouts for foosball, video games, and evening parties with new friends. The whole family can later reunite to share the day’s fun over Johnny Rockets burgers and shakes or perhaps a teppanyaki dinner with showman chefs.

Standout itinerary: 7-night Perfect Day CocoCay and Caribbean cruise on Icon of the Seas sailing round-trip from Miami

Disney’s rapidly expanding cruise ship segment isn’t just for preschoolers reveling in their Frozen era. Its itineraries across the Caribbean, Alaska, and Europe offer family fun for a wide range of ages, including adults. Plus, its short itineraries pair nicely with a theme park visit.

Entertainment is a highlight, from live on-stage productions of classics like Moana and Beauty and the Beast to mealtime shows themed around the Marvel universe and Coco. The ships’ movie theaters screen first-run flicks across the Disney-owned studios (not just animated classics), and the once-a-cruise Pirate Night is the only time you’ll ever see fireworks launched from a cruise ship.

Disney Cruise Line’s enormous youth facilities, also with nurseries and teen clubs, offer activities themed to Disney IP. Kids can design a superhero suit or theme park ride, pilot the Millennium Falcon, and join Olaf for virtual snowball fights. The line also treats adults very well with pools, sun decks, fine-dining restaurants (including ones by Michelin-starred chefs), bars themed around The Haunted Mansion and Star Wars, and cafés exclusive to travelers ages 18+.

Disney supports traveling families in less flashy ways, as well. Cabins feature extra berths for kids, split bathrooms with a toilet in one room and a sink in the other, and room-dividing curtains so grownups won’t distract would-be nappers. Waiters will purée baby food and cut up your kid’s dinner all while cracking silly jokes and performing magic tricks. When the travel logistics are handled, families can focus more on having fun.

Standout itinerary: 4-night Bahamas cruise on Disney Wish sailing round-trip from Port Canaveral

Retirees rule the world of river cruising, but AmaWaterways has teamed up with Adventures by Disney (Disney’s escorted travel arm) to offer select Rhine and Danube cruises to families.

Disney’s onboard Adventure Guides handle all the logistics in port, from escorting tours in every destination to managing dietary restrictions and directing guests to the best restaurants and local treats. On board, they host movie evenings and kids-only dinners so parents can enjoy a leisurely meal with adult conversation.

Adventures by Disney swaps the history- and culture-focused walking tours typical of river cruise excursions for alpine coasters, amusement parks, river kayaking, and visits to chocolate factories. A selection of winery visits and slower-paced tours appeal to multigenerational families willing to split up childcare responsibilities ashore.

AmaWaterways, in turn, has specially built ships with more third-berth and connecting cabins than the typical river ship. It supplements its regional cuisine with kid favorites, while introducing the whole family to specialties like French macarons and German sausages.

Standout itinerary: 7-night Rhine River cruise from Basel, Switzerland, to Amsterdam, Netherlands

A family cruise can be a nature-filled adventure to places like Alaska, the Galápagos, Baja California, Iceland, Greenland, and even Antarctica. For active families with curious kids and teens (and parents!), National Geographic-Lindblad Expeditions is a top choice.

The cruise line’s ships carry under 200 guests, so families can focus on each other without those big-ship distractions. Though small, the ships do offer connecting cabins to accommodate groups and casual dining with plentiful options to suit all tastes.

On a National Geographic cruise, the destination is the draw, and family-friendly activities range from snorkeling and kayaking to hiking and biking. On select voyages, the cruise line offers its National Geographic Explorers-in-Training program for kids and teenagers, led by certified field instructors. The program focuses on nature, science, and storytelling, with activities like practicing smartphone photography in Alaska with a photo instructor, collecting and then observing plankton under a microscope in Baja California, and learning to drive a Zodiac.

Standout itinerary: 7-night Galápagos Escape cruise from Quito, Ecuador

Luxury cruise ships aren’t built for kids, but select cruise lines make an effort to entertain junior cruisers during holiday and summer sailings in places like Alaska and the Caribbean. Regent Seven Seas Cruises’ ships might not offer a dedicated kids club on board, but the line’s longstanding Club Mariner Youth Program welcomes children ages 5 to 17 with a schedule of onboard activities.

Trained counselors make use of the ships’ public spaces to keep kids busy with mini-golf games, dessert decorating, karaoke parties, and movie nights. In Alaska, programming is destination-themed, and a youth-oriented enrichment speaker engages kids in learning about Alaska’s nature and wildlife.

Don’t underestimate your family’s ability to find fun on an adult-oriented ship. On Regent, kids can enjoy swimming in the onboard pool, joining a team for trivia, and playing board or card games in the library. Afternoon tea and evening shows may also become family attractions. You might find that complimentary room service from the main dining room menu coupled with movies on-demand in your suite is the perfect family activity to end your day.

Standout itinerary: 7-night Alaska cruise on Seven Seas Explorer from Vancouver, Canada, to Whittier, Alaska
