American Airlines Cancels & Launches 6 Routes: See All Changes Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

In the past week, American Airlines revealed more changes to its huge network. They all involve Los Angeles International Airport, at which the oneworld member is the third-largest operator for passengers, flights, seats for sale, and available seat miles.

American's schedule submission to OAG shows that Los Angeles is the carrier's ninth-busiest hub for departures this year. It rises to eighth for available seats and seventh for ASMs. And for passenger traffic, which is obviously retrospective, it was eighth through August. While Los Angeles is not a huge hub for American, the route changes are notable.

In the latest schedule update, two of the following three routes will now be removed in early 2027. The plan was for them to be served daily through 2027, although Cleveland was to have only a handful of services in April and May before being ramped up to daily again for the peak summer.

The link to Eugene, Oregon, is notable. This route last had regular flights with American — or, strictly speaking, SkyWest operating for American Eagle on behalf of American — six years ago. AeroRoutes shows that the northbound market was due to return in 2027, but now it won't. American's network to Eugene has halved, with only Phoenix still served.

The carrier removed various other routes from Los Angeles in 2025 or 2026, but they don't really count as they weren't 'normal' operations. They included the intra-Californian links to San José (for the Super Bowl) and Palm Springs (for Coachella), along with the transborder Toronto (for the Toronto International Film Festival). Some may return in 2027. The main exception was Flagstaff, which had a weekly service through May 2025.

Focusing on December 2026 through next summer, all the following links are due to see American or its regional unit. As is usually the case with the US Big Three carriers, all have previously been served, albeit not necessarily always regularly.

For example, having previously been served for years, the last regular operations to Reno were five years ago. However, the route has had irregular flights each year, most recently in September 2026, seemingly for the Burning Man festival. Regular flights are coming back.

Excluding the now-ending service to Cleveland, which began in April 2026, American has only inaugurated one other route from Los Angeles this year. It was to Washington Dulles, which was previously on the carrier's map for many years until 2020.

American returned to Dulles in April 2026 and runs daily on the 737 MAX 8, although the A321ceo and A321neo will appear briefly in 2027. Two daily flights existed until early 2020 while three were last available in 2017.

Let's look at March to October 2027, after the routes that are ending will have been removed. American plans an average of 143 daily departures, up from 133 daily in the same period in 2026. As of November 20 and subject to change, 56 routes will be available, which includes the time-limited operation to Louisville for the Kentucky Derby.

With 143 daily departures, American will have a fifth of all services from Los Angeles International. While activity has jumped by 7% in a year, which is very strong in itself, it is the slowest expansion of the top three operators. Delta is up by 16% while United has added 10% more services.
