Nintendo Store App Receives A Small But Worthwhile Update, Here Are The Patch Notes

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Remember the dedicated Nintendo Store mobile app? Honestly, we sometimes forget about it too. But hey, Nintendo is still updating it, and the latest patch should make it a little easier to see what you have been up to on your Switch this year.

The ver. 3.4.0 update launched on iOS and Android earlier today, and it seems to be all about making the user experience that bit better.

The big change this time around is that you can now find information about your DLC and other software habits on the Play Activity screen, but there have also been some tweaks to the User Page and more.

Far from anything groundbreaking, then, but a worthwhile little addition to anyone wanting some more info from their Play Activity rundown.

You'll find the (admittedly, very short) patch notes from the App Store below:

・ Information about downloadable content and other types of software can now be found on the play activity screen. ・ Design changes have been made to improve the readability of the user page. ・ Various improvements and fixes have been made.

Do we wish that all of these disparate Nintendo apps were combined into one? Sure! Are we pleased to see Nintendo is still working to improve them behind the scenes? Absolutely.

I think Nintendo Life misread the patch notes and what “Infortmation” Nintendo has added to Activity Log pages.

The new patch doesn’t display play-logged data on DLC. It simply lists DLC and other products you can find on the Nintendo Store.

For example, if you have The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild on your Play Activity tracker, it will show a section for DLC you can purchase (the only one being its Expansion Pass), a separate area for its Nintendo Switch 2 Edition Upgrade Pack (which is likely what it means by “Other Software” and related products you can get from the Nintendo Store app, like amiibo and My Nintendo rewards.

So the activity tracker won’t track time you spent playing the Champions DLC, or specifically playing the Nintendo Switch 2 Edition.
