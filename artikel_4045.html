@Chocobo_Shepherd The industry standard for refresh rate is 30fps, 60fps is what native games are suppose to run at but 30fps is standard for everything including music, movies, slides show, cameras, etc., with gaming taking that into account too, etc. It's similar to how resolution works, 480p is standard, anything below that is low quality or low-res, 720p is high quality and anything over that count as high def a.k.a. HD whereas 1440p or over count as 4K.

Also the higher the refresh rate, the lower they had to tone down the resolution otherwise the games won't be able to perform smoothly meaning you can't have 4K and still expect 120fps. It's just not gonna work, not every clear details will fly in 120fps or even 60fps. This is why to keep the details clear and the game smooth, some bigger games target for 60fps only in either 720p or 1080p. Smaller scale games like indies may target 1080p 60fps but that's only cause there's not many details to interfere with the refresh rate.
