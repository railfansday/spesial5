A Complete Guide To The Nauctis Commander Precons In MTG

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The first set of 2027 brings with it four exciting new precons, and here's what we know about them so far.

We already know that 2027 will be the year of the Magic: The Gathering Commander precon, with at least 24 new decks arriving across six total sets. The first set of the year is Nauctis: The Sunken Realm, and we've already got a look at the four precons arriving with this underwater-themed set, and there's a lot to like.

We have a good idea of the theme for each deck, and another leak suggests a very powerful Commander ability is coming to an all-new Shark card.

The Nauctis: The Sunken Realm Commander precons are officially launching on Friday, February 5, 2027. If you are attending a prerelease event or have a good relationship with your local game store, you may be able to purchase your precons starting Friday, January 29, 2027.

The base editions of the Commander precons will cost $49.99, but no longer come with any foil borderless Commander cards. These have been moved to the premium editions of the decks, a new version exclusive to in-universe sets (Universes Beyond still has collector's editions). Premium edition decks not only include the foil borderless Commander cards, but are also printed entirely in traditional foil, and they cost $79.99 each.

We're at the end of 2026, and with all seven MTG sets now out, we're going to rank all the Commander precons we've seen so far.

Blood in the Water is the Shark deck that focuses on swinging wide with creatures and creating blood tokens, a returning mechanic that will play a big role for the creature in Nauctis.

A leak has suggested that the face Commander of this deck will have Eminence, a powerful ability that triggers both when your Commander is in play and in the Command Zone.

Based on what we've seen so far, this is the fastest, most aggressive deck of the bunch, and it looks like it will be a ton of fun to play, especially if that face Commander is packing such a powerful ability.

Group hug returns! Seal the Deal will have you offering your opponents something to stop them from attacking you. In this case, it appears to be treasure tokens, but not nearly enough has leaked about this deck for us to say anything for sure.

Seals will play a role for sure, but we also anticipate Octopuses and Otters could feature, too.

We haven't had a group hug precon since Ms. Bumbleflower in Bloomburrow, and that card went on to be quite the popular card in the format, so we're extremely excited to see what happens with the new Seal on the block.

We're expecting Terrors of the Abyss to be the huge, scary sea-creature deck, and this has been backed up by an early leak showcasing the face and backup Commander for the deck.

While far from official, the cards were revealed in Japanese marketing material that was quickly pulled, and it looks like Terrors of the Abyss will be led by a giant sea serpent called The Noble Serpent. It's a huge eight-mana card that has you mill four cards right at the beginning of the game while it is in your Command Zone. Then, whenever it is in play, at the end of your turn, it lets you bring a creature back from your graveyard to the battlefield for free (provided it's classed as a Sea Monster).

Much like with Seal the Deal, we don't know an awful lot about the Merfolk Regiment deck, other than what's printed on the box.

It appears to focus on going wide, meaning you'll swing with a lot of creatures to outnumber your opponent's defenders and get damage through.

Tap and convoke is also mentioned, meaning we may be able to tap creatures on the battlefield to cover part of the cost of casting additional, more powerful creatures, a mechanic that Merfolk are more than familiar with.
