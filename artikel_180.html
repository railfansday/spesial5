First Thing: Trump, Xi and Putin hold historic trilateral meeting in China

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The three leaders spoke about international peace and nuclear weapons as Trump called Putin a ‘friend’. Plus, yes, GTA VI is really finally here, and the critics have their verdict: masterpiece

Good morning. Donald Trump has met Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping at the Apec summit in Shenzhen, and said the three leaders were “really making history”. The trilateral meeting lasted roughly an hour and a half, according to Chinese state media. On arrival the US president described his Russian counterpart as “my friend”.

Putin told Xi and Trump that their countries shared “many converging interests” when it came to resolving conflicts, including the full-scale war in Ukraine, which the Russian president started in 2022.

Trump said the three countries were “uniquely positioned” to discuss nuclear weapons, while Xi said “every effort must be made to prevent nuclear war” and that nuclear weapons ⁠should be comprehensively prohibited and completely ⁠destroyed.

China released a readout of an earlier Trump-Xi meeting, with the tone being largely positive, but containing a warning on Taiwan and a call to strengthen communication on trade and AI. Trump has now departed from China.

What was unusual about China’s welcome to the Japanese delegation? Apec’s Chinese hosts went out of their way to give world leaders a warm welcome as they arrived in Shenzhen, with one exception. Japan’s prime minister, Sanae Takaichi, had to make do with a modest guard of honour and a welcome from a relatively junior Chinese government official.
