Norris grabs pole in rainswept qualifying for Sao Paulo Grand Prix

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

McLaren's outgoing world champion Lando Norris grabbed pole position for the Sao Paulo Grand Prix with a brave and well-judged drive in Saturday's rainswept qualifying at Interlagos.

The 26-year-old Briton clocked a best lap in one minute and 27.470 seconds to outpace George Russell of Mercedes by 0.204 seconds as heavy rain curtailed Q3 at the end of a session that lasted almost two and a half hours.

New world champion Kimi Antonelli was third for Mercedes, but he will start Sunday's race from the front row as Russell faces a five-place grid penalty for taking new power-unit components.

It is the 20th pole position of Norris's career and his third in succession in Brazil.

"It's been a great qualifying, to be on pole again here, two years in a row! Different conditions to last year, but I am very happy.

"It was about getting two laps in and not making a mistake and that is exactly what we did so it has been a very good day."

Russell was disappointed not to take pole after being told by Mercedes to ease off on his final push lap.

"Congrats to Lando, but it was definitely my pole lap. We should have finished the lap."

Antonelli said: "It was very tricky out there and, to be fair, I didn't know what to expect as I have never driven here before in the wet. I made a mistake and I nearly went off!"

Charles Leclerc was fourth for Ferrari but also faces a penalty of 10 places for taking a new engine.

Racing Bulls' Liam Lawson was fifth ahead of Audi's Nico Hulkenberg, Isack Hadjar of Red Bull and Oliver Bearman's Haas.

Two-time champion Fernando Alonso was ninth for Aston Martin and Alex Albon 10th for Williams.

Seven-time champion Lewis Hamilton of Ferrari qualified 11th, but also faces a penalty for taking two new engines on Saturday and will start from the back of the grid while four-time champion Max Verstappen, who also has a 20-place penalty, was 16th for Red Bull.

The session began after a 90-minute delay in treacherous conditions with high plumes of spray rising from the cars.

Valtteri Bottas led out in his Cadillac with a 1:42.314 to set the bar with team-mate Sergio Perez soon improving on that before Haas's new boy Brazilian Rafael Camara hit the barriers, but continued.

Verstappen then went top ahead of Hamilton as conditions improved. Aware that there was more rain on the way, the teams warned their drivers that these were the best conditions.

Remarkably, they completed Q1 with Norris on top in 1:30.939, Camara through in 13th and both Alpines eliminated along with Lance Stroll of Aston Martin, both Cadillacs and local hero Gabriel Bortoleto who damaged his Audi's rear wing.

For Pierre Gasly it was his first Q1 elimination this year.

Verstappen, set to start from the back of the grid after taking a new power unit, decided not to go out again and instead saved his tyres.

In the end, a red flag halted Q2 when Camara smacked the wall at Juncao, leaving Russell on top ahead of Lawson while Hamilton, set for his 400th Grand Prix, went out in 11th with McLaren's Oscar Piastri, Camara, Racing Bulls' Arvid Lindblad and Carlos Sainz of Williams.

Antonelli survived in 10th while Alonso made Q3 for the first time this season.

Alonso led the queue, but he did not have the pace as Norris and then Russell went top as the rain arrived and Norris regained the initiative.
