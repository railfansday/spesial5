Sam Hartman Roasted by Former Football Pros for ‘Bachelor’ Casting: ‘You Deserve a Slap in the Face’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It's safe to say that Sam Hartman won't be getting any roses on the football field.

The Washington Commanders quarterback, 27, was a hot topic of discussion on the Monday, November 16, episode of the Gronk & Jules Show following his casting on season 30 of The Bachelor, which will premiere in March 2027.

"Did I see him on Good Morning America promoting this?" asked former New England Patriots wide receiver Julian Edelman. "We're promoting in the middle of a season? What are we doing? He better be getting a lot of money."

Though Hartman, 27, is on the team, he is currently part of the practice squad.

Former tight end Rob Gronkowski, who also played with the Patriots as well as the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, said that if the QB was on the current roster and "accepted" ABC's invitation to join the reality dating show, "You deserve a slap in the face."

"Your priorities aren't straight, obviously," added Gronkowski, 37.

If a player on the current squad was part of The Bachelor, Gronkowski said, "You're not showing that you're all in because in the NFL, you got to be all in once the season hits. This would have been a big distraction. No doubt about it."

Edelman, 40, added that Hartman wouldn't fare well in the locker room while also being on the TV show.

"I don't know how it is now," he said, "but with us… Oh my god! This guy wouldn't live."

"We wouldn't have been able to eat him alive because [Patriots coach] Bill [Belichick] would have released him by the time he even got back in the locker room," said Gronkowski.

Following the announcement that Hartman is joining Bachelor Nation, he addressed fans in a TikTok video posted Tuesday, November 17, and said that football remains his top priority.

"Really excited to get back to work," he said. "I gotta get back to the day job running around throwing the football. All the focus is there. But once the season wraps, can't wait to join. Can't wait to meet all the beautiful women. Can't wait to go on this journey with you guys. I'm super excited."
