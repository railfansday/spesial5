Yaxel Lendeborg barks at girlfriend Tacarra’s thirst trap before Warriors game

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Golden State forward Yaxel Lendeborg looks to be the Warriors' future.

And Lendeborg's future looks to be bright as well, given the rookie's relationship with his girlfriend, Tacarra Foreman.

Tacarra Foreman posing for a photo before the 2026 NBA Draft.

These two got together before Lendeborg's final college season at Michigan and are still going strong.

While Lendeborg and Foreman aren't always with each other when the Warriors are on the road, they are still finding ways to show each other love.

Foreman made a TikTok post on Thursday that showed her sitting at her vanity while on FaceTime with Lendeborg.

She was showing off her look, and Lendeborg approved — which was shown by him barking at her through the phone screen.

Tacarra Foreman sitting at her vanity while FaceTiming Yaxel Lendeborg. TikTok/@tacarra

Barking has become a popular internet expression of a man showing attraction to a woman.

It is usually done in a playful and exaggerated way, which is what Lendeborg looked to be doing in the video.

Golden State Warriors forward Yaxel Lendeborg (1) smiles during the first half of an NBA basketball game against the Sacramento Kings, Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026, in San Francisco. (AP Photo/Justine Willard) AP Photo/Justine Willard

Foreman's video has been reposted on X and went viral, amassing over 200,000 views in about 15 hours.

Fans are finding Yaxel's reaction to Foreman's thirst trap hilarious.

The Warriors are facing the Portland Trail Blazers on Friday. They're going to need a great performance from Yaxel not just tonight, but for the foreseeable future until Steph Curry returns from his injury.
