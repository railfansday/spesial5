Pistons vs. Pelicans Discussion: Game Time, TV, Odds, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

In an ultimate bit of disrespect, the Detroit Pistons are resting star Cade Cunningham tonight against the New Orleans Pelicans. The reasoning appears to be twofold — 1. Cunningham played last night. 2. The Pelicans. The poor, beleaguered Pelicans, home to former Pistons executives Joe Dumars and Troy Weaver, are not having a smooth go of it early in the season. They are 5-10, the coach is fighting with players, and a starter has already asked for a trade. That doesn't mean there isn't talent on the team. In fact, Dumars is being ridiculed because he's asking for the moon and the stars from other GMs interested in some of his very useful players. That means the Pistons could very well drop a game tonight coming off the high of an absolutely gutsy performance against the San Antonio Spurs. But it also means that Detroit thinks it also has a pretty decent shot to win even without their superstar player. The good news is that while Cunningham, Jalen Duren, Duncan Robinson and Ron Holland all remain out, we might see Isaiah Joe back in a Pistons uniform.
