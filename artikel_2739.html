Antonelli fastest but penalty leaves Verstappen on pole

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli was fastest in qualifying for the Las Vegas Grand Prix but a grid penalty will hand pole position to Red Bull's Max Verstappen.

Antonelli, running at the back of the pack of cars in qualifying and maximising his tow, beat Verstappen by 0.258 seconds.

But the new world champion has a five-place grid penalty for using more than permitted number of engine components and will start sixth.

The Italian's team-mate George Russell was just 0.073secs behind Verstappen and will start alongside the Dutchman on the front row.

McLaren's Lando Norris qualified fourth, and will start third, ahead of Red Bull's Isack Hadjar.

Both Ferraris were eliminated in the second session and qualified 11th and 12th with Charles Leclerc ahead of Lewis Hamilton.

Each will be promoted a place by a 10-place penalty for McLaren's Oscar Piastri, who qualified seventh.

Racing Bulls' rookie Arvid Lindblad, sixth fastest, is the last driver to be promoted as a result of Antonelli's penalty.

Racing Bulls' Liam Lawson will start seventh with Alpine's Pierre Gasly eighth and Franco Colapinto ninth.

Antonelli's final run did not start auspiciously as he and Russell became tangled up with the Alpines and Racing Bulls as they sought the best track position to optimise the slipstream effect.

Antonelli ended up the last driver to run and made it to the line with only one second to spare before the chequered flag, one second behind Lindblad.

Normally, being so close to the car in front would be a compromise but in Las Vegas it magnified the tow effect and Antonelli leapt to the front. "That was all the tow," he said over the radio after being told he was on pole.

"Starting the lap, I was only one second behind the car in front and I thought I was screwed," he said. "But the tow is so powerful here."
