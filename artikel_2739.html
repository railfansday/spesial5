Mike Florio gives credit to Steelers’ Aaron Rodgers for ‘valid point’ after shot at decision-makers about breakout star

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Pittsburgh Steelers wide receiver Max Hurleman unexpectedly stole the spotlight during Sunday's 19-16 road loss to the Philadelphia Eagles. In his NFL regular-season debut, he caught six passes for 61 yards and scored Pittsburgh's only touchdown. The 25-year-old undrafted receiver admitted to Ashley Wenskoski of Pittsburgh Sports Tonight that he was still adjusting to the sudden attention while wearing Antonio Brown's famous No. 84 jersey.

Despite being a bright spot in an otherwise sluggish offensive performance, Hurleman reverted to the practice squad after the game. He also contributed 10 special teams snaps, 36 punt-return yards, and 32 kickoff-return yards. NBC Sports analyst Mike Florio addressed the situation, giving credit to quarterback Aaron Rodgers for making a "valid point" about Pittsburgh's decision-makers keeping Hurleman off the 53-man roster all season.

According to Florio, Rodgers's public comment highlighted a significant vulnerability for the organization: any of the NFL's 31 other teams can now easily poach a proven, cap-friendly playmaker and special teams contributor from Pittsburgh's practice squad without having to give up any compensation. Given Hurleman's immediate production following DK Metcalf's ejection, Florio noted it would be surprising if the Notre Dame product remained unprotected for long. This means the Steelers must either promote him permanently or risk losing him to a rival team.

Ironically, Rodgers' pointed reminder to the decision-makers, in which he told reporters, as reported by Ari Meirov, that teammates felt Hurleman deserved a 53-man spot even before his training camp injury, came just hours before a stunning shakeup. On Tuesday night, Pittsburgh benched Rodgers in favor of Mason Rudolph ahead of Friday's Black Friday clash with the Denver Broncos.
