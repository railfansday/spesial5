Full practice for Jaxon Smith-Njigba

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Seattle Seahawks WR Jaxon Smith Njigba (non-injury) was a full participant in practice Thursday, Nov. 12.

Why it Matters: After a day off to start the week, Smith-Njigba was back in action on Thursday. He leads the NFL in both receiving yards and receiving touchdowns heading into Week 10, and he should continue his strong season against the vulnerable defense of the Raiders.
