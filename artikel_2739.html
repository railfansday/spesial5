The Falcoholic Reacts: The pressure is heating up on A.J. Terrell

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

After a tremendous defensive start to the season, Jeff Ulbrich's unit has endured some tough losses in recent weeks. Some of it can be attributed to facing two elite quarterbacks in Joe Burrow and Patrick Mahomes. Much of it comes down to the team's personnel limitations. As hard as they compete and cause chaos, it's tough to succeed down to down against the best teams without many top-tier players. It also doesn't help when your best players aren't performing anywhere near where they should.

A.J. Terrell, Xavier Watts, and James Pearce Jr. are three foundational pieces on the defense. While inserting Pearce Jr. into this debate could be harsh given he's only played two games since returning from suspension, the defense is counting on him to make an impact.

He hasn't done that, although there are far bigger concerns. The Falcons' top cornerback isn't playing to the high standards he's set over the last five years. That's what made him an easy choice for fans when determining which highly regarded defensive player needs to step up the most.

Wide receivers are beating Terrell vertically far too often. The physical, disruptive style Terrell normally plays with has been non-existent. He isn't jamming pass catchers at the line of scrimmage. The route recognition to close on passes when in zone has been missing.

There are also instances of communication breakdowns in his area. Without Jessie Bates, Terrell is going to be counted on more than ever to be that dependable veteran presence. He needs to start playing at that level for fans to not only regain confidence in him, but also in the team's overall outlook.
