Monday Night Football: Chargers run past Ravens 23-20 for first win of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Jacoby Brissett had a chance to play the hero in his first start for the Ravens. He did his part, but Zay Flowers fumbled when Tarheeb Still stripped him and Eric Molden recovered for the Chargers at the Los Angeles 46 with 2:03 remaining.

By the time Brissett got the ball back, the Ravens were in need of a miracle, and his Hail Mary on the final play fell incomplete.

The Chargers won for the first time, holding off the Ravens 23-20. Los Angeles is 1-8, while Baltimore lost its fifth in a row to fall to 4-6.

The Chargers led from start to finish, winning for the first time since Week 16 of 2025 when they beat the Cowboys. They twice had a double-digit lead in the second half.

Brissett, acquired in a trade from the Cardinals last week, went 33-of-49 for 340 yards and an interception. It was his most passing yards since Week 11 of last season when he threw for 452 yards for the Cardinals against the 49ers.

He had no help from the running game, which contributed 16 yards on 15 carries. Derrick Henry had 10 yards and two touchdowns on 10 carries.

Mark Andrews caught eight passes for 82 yards and Flowers had six catches for 74 yards.

The Ravens outgained the Chargers 347 to 287, but Los Angeles gained 160 yards on the ground.

With 1:52 left, Justin Herbert completed an 11-yard pass to Charlie Kolar for a first down. The Chargers ended up punting, but the Ravens had only 11 seconds from their own 20. Brissett threw a 21-yard pass to Ja’Kobi Lane before R.J. Mickens knocked down a Hail Mary attempt intended for Lane to end the game.

Herbert was 12-of-18 for 129 yards, with Tre’ Harris catching five passes for 86 yards. Herbert ran for 51 yards and a touchdown on seven carries. Omarion Hampton had 22 carries for 105 yards and a touchdown.
