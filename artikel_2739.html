Appalachian State wins 108-43 over Christendom

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

BOONE, N.C. (AP) — Mason Costello's 15 points helped Appalachian State defeat Christendom 108-43 on Wednesday night.

Costello shot 5 for 9 (2 for 5 from 3-point range) and 3 of 3 from the free-throw line for the Mountaineers (2-0). Da'Mori Lytch scored 13 points, going 5 of 5 from the field. Corbin Allen went 5 of 7 from the field (1 for 3 from 3-point range) to finish with 11 points.

Daniel Schultz led the Crusaders in scoring, finishing with 16 points. Charlie Evich added eight points for Christendom. Mark Kelly finished with six points.

Appalachian State plays Auburn on the road on Monday, and Christendom visits Liberty on Saturday.
