Jaguars' Parker Washington injures shoulder, OUT vs. Titans

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Jacksonville Jaguars wide receiver Parker Washington suffered a shoulder injury during practice on Thursday and will not play against the Tennessee Titans this Sunday.

Washington showed up on the injury report as working limited Thursday with a shoulder injury, and Jaguars head coach Liam Coen confirmed he will not play and will be "week to week" moving forward.

Washington is in his contract year and slated to be a free agent after the season, after being drafted in the sixth round of the 2023 NFL Draft.

Jacksonville Jaguars wide receiver Parker Washington (11) was born March 21, 2002, in Sugar Land, Texas. He is 24 years old.

Washington attended Pennsylvania State University and was drafted as the 185th pick in the sixth round of the 2023 NFL Draft by the Jaguars.

At 5'10 and 204 pounds, Washington is in his fourth season with the Jaguars.

His 2025 regular season stats feature 58 receptions, 847 yards and five touchdowns. See some of the best photos from his career.
