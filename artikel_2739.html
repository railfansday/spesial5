Steelers' offense is awful in loss to Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Steelers entered today’s game looking like playoff contenders. The Eagles entered today’s game looking like they had given up on the season. You’d have to be an idiot to think the Eagles could win today.

But as Chris Berman used to say to the late, great Tom Jackson on NFL Primetime, That’s why they play the games.

It was the Eagles who came out looking like a team in contention, and the Steelers who came out flat in a 19-16 Eagles win in Philadelphia.

The Steelers’ offense was awful, particularly running the ball. Aaron Rodgers was the leading rusher with 29 yards on five carries. Jaylen Warren, who has played so well for most of this season, managed just 28 yards on nine carries, and Rico Dowdle had 15 yards on five carries.

Pittsburgh didn’t score a touchdown until 1:21 remained in the fourth quarter, Aaron Rodgers hit Max Hurleman in the end zone for 20 yards.

Steelers receiver DK Metcalf was ejected after two personal fouls, the second of which was called as taunting but appeared to involve Metcalf trying to poke Cooper DeJean in the eye. Metcalf finished the game with one catch for two yards and had the worst of his many bad games this season.

Eagles kicker Jake Elliott made four field goals, including a 53-yarder in the third quarter and a 55-yarder in the fourth quarter that kept Philly on top even as its offense stagnated.

Eagles quarterback Joe Milton wasn’t great, completing 11 of 21 passes for 141 yards, but he showed a little bit of promise with both his arm and his legs, and played well enough that there’s no reason the Eagles shouldn’t stick with him and see what he can do. Saquon Barkley also wasn’t great, but he did manage a 38-yard touchdown run.

The Steelers, who still haven’t won in Philadelphia since 1965, fall to 6-4 on the season. The Eagles improve to 3-7.
