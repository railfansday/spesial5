Diamondbacks trading for Red Sox right-hander Johan Oviedo

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Arizona Diamondbacks executed a trade that expands the team's pitching depth on Friday, Nov. 20, acquiring right-hander Johan Oviedo from the Red Sox in exchange for High-A left-handed pitcher Luke Dotson and a player to be named later, according to a source.

Oviedo, a 28-year-old from Cuba, is 15-26 with a 4.29 ERA over six seasons, allowing 324 hits and striking out 327 hitters in 364 2/3 innings spent with the St. Louis Cardinals, Pittsburgh Pirates and Boston Red Sox.

He has started 67 of the 82 games in which he has appeared.
