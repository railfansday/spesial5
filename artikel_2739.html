Austin Reaves progressing, full participant in Lakers practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

While Austin Reaves will not play in Monday's game against the Jazz, he is continuing to make progress.

The Lakers had practice on Sunday, which featured Austin Reaves as a full participant. It was not, however, a live practice as the Lakers did some defensive drills among other things.

Right now, Reaves is focused on finding the right shoe and insert combo to help alleviate the pain in his heel. Redick said the pain is not in the bottom of his heel, but on the medial — or inside — side of his foot.

By taking one more game off, Reaves effectively gets an extra week of rest. The Lakers won't play again until Friday, so that may have gone into the calculus.

However, if he does return on Friday — and Redick recently expressed hope he would be back this week — it would mean Reaves missed nearly two-and-a-half weeks after leaving in the second half against the Bucks. Getting him back, obviously, is great, especially for a measuring-stick game like the one against the Spurs.

At this point, if Reaves doesn't return on Friday, it raises a lot of questions, both about the severity of his injury and the Lakers' process of reporting them. The latter point is one that is not a new issue. Even with this injury, Reaves has been day-to-day for two weeks.

Fortunately and hopefully, that day is soon coming now.
