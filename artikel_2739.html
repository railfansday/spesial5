Bo Nix doesn't do much in Week 11 victory vs. Raiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Denver Broncos quarterback Bo Nix completed 19 of 35 passes for 225 yards, one touchdown and one interception in Week 11 against the Las Vegas Raiders, while adding six runs for just five yards.

Why it Matters: Nix remains more of a QB2 in most fantasy leagues heading into the Black Friday in Week 12 against the Pittsburgh Steelers.
