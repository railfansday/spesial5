Savio scores on debut as Spurs cruise past Charlton in Carabao Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

It looked like this was going to be a night for “one of their own”, but Tottenham Hotspur’s latest big-money arrival, Sávio, stole the headlines to crank up the excitement at his new club.

Mikey Moore had set Spurs on their way to what proved a comfortable Carabao Cup success over Championship club Charlton Athletic and heard his name sung by the home fans.

But Sávio stepped off the substitutes’ bench to take the limelight with a late goal just a few days after completing his expensive switch from Manchester City, and head coach Roberto De Zerbi is demanding many more to come.

“A perfect day for him,” said De Zerbi. “His biggest improvement is to increase the number of goals. He is one of the best wingers, but the best wingers score many goals and I spoke with him two days ago, and we put the goal at 10-12 goals.

“We need 10-12 goals from the wingers. Last season, we suffered too much because the number of goals were not enough.”

"What an introduction!"Sávio scores a brilliant goal on his Spurs debut! 👏 pic.twitter.com/WgmcfgeNpX

Having watched some of Tottenham’s signings fall to a 3-0 Premier League defeat by Brentford a few days earlier, Sávio’s late impact was the perfect lift for the Spurs supporters.

The club have committed to spending almost £400m this summer, leading to debate by outsiders over whether it will prove to be money well spent and huge waves of excitement within the fanbase.

A deal that could be worth as much as £85m, if add-ons are achieved, for Sávio certainly raised some eyebrows, but the Brazilian provided the perfect response by scoring just 11 minutes into his debut as a late substitute.

In the 82nd minute, Sávio cut in from the flank and scored with a shot past Charlton goalkeeper Tiernan Brooks. He and the Spurs crowd thought he had a second a few minutes later, but his shot hit Ben Davies on its way into the net and the defender was credited with the goal.

Having sung for Moore earlier in the game, the Spurs fans wasted no time in trying out their new song for Sávio, inspired by the Spandau Ballet hit Gold.

Moore has been expected to move to Cologne on loan, but provided a reminder of his talent by opening the scoring in the first half with only his second goal for the club.

What a hit from Mikey Moore! ⚪ pic.twitter.com/OgSM4qhiqs

“We have to understand the best solution,” De Zerbi said. “I would like to keep him. He’s young but a good player, but we can’t make the wrong decision. He needs to play, he needs to get games inside him.”

Cutting in from the right, where Sávio is primed to play, Moore surged past his marker and shot from just inside the box. Brooks should have stopped it, but the ball flew past him and into the net.

Three minutes after Moore’s opener, Dominic Solanke scored his first goal since March to offer a reminder of what he can still give De Zerbi, who is poised to welcome Omar Marmoush to Tottenham.

Buoyed by his first-half goal, Moore skipped his way around a couple of tackles at the start of the second period but his low cross was cut out. The Spurs fans responded by singing “he’s one of our own.”

Substitute Kevin Danso netted with his first touch from a header, before Sávio was introduced to steal the show, despite a late consolation goal from Charlton’s Lloyd Jones.

Kevin Danso scores with his first touch since coming on! ⚪ pic.twitter.com/oqUU7wjoJW

The victory set up a tough third-round trip to Anfield to face Liverpool for Tottenham, but De Zerbi said: “It’s a great game. If we want to win the competition we have to win the games.

“Sometimes in this competition, it can happen that you play against big teams. It’s a great challenge for the players, for me, for our clubs. We have to arrive ready to play in our style, with personality, ready to play a game following the work we are doing on the training ground.”

A place in the League Cup third round is at stake as Tottenham host Charlton in a London derby at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium. The last competitive meeting between these sides came in the FA Cup back in January 2011, with Tottenham winning that game 3-0 at White Hart Lane. A then 19-year-old Andros Townsend made his debut that day and scored, with Jermain Defoe scoring a brace.

Tottenham’s Premier League campaign got off to the worst possible start as they lost 3-0 at Brentford last weekend. Tottenham have been very busy during this summer transfer window and that has continued this week. Savio (formerly Savinho) has arrived from Manchester City in a deal that could be worth £85m and the Brazilian could make his Tottenham debut tonight, having been registered in time.

“I think in attacking positions we need players capable one-on one, to create something, to create a chance to score,” Roberto De Zerbi said. “In terms of one-on-one, he [Savio] is one of the best players, especially since 2024. He can play on the right side, on the left side. I have known him from a long time, I spoke with him when he went to Manchester City. He is a big player for us.

“I do not know if I am the right man [to get the most out of Savio], but for sure he has to achieve. I think a player who is so good in one-on-one, so good at creating chances to score, to be complete, he has to score. If you analyse the other best wingers in the world, many of them, they score 10 to 15 goals per season. He has the qualities. Maybe his habits, he has to change habits, because he has to be focused on the assists, the one-on-one dribbling and also to score.”

Omar Marmoush is closing in on an initial loan deal from Manchester City but that move is not complete and thus he is not available for Tottenham tonight. Micky van der Ven and Pedro Porro are both available having missed Tottenham’s loss at Brentford but the hosts are still without Xavi Simons, Pape Sarr, Dejan Kulusevski and Wilson Odobert.

Charlton have made the perfect start to their Championship campaign; a 2-1 home win over Derby was followed up by a 2-1 victory at recently-relegated West Ham. Nathan Jones’ side earned their place in the League Cup second round with a 3-1 win at Cheltenham in the first round.

“We have to be very organised and very professional, they [Tottenham] are a top team,” Jones said. “They have a great manager and a brilliant group of players. They already had a Premier League squad and they have spent, what is reaching about, I do not know, £300 million or something like that. It is a wonderful test, we have to be right near our best and do both sides of the game very well. Of course, you want to test yourself against the best managers and you want to test yourself against the best teams. For us, this is not our priority, the Championship is our priority, but this is the next game and therefore it is the most important.”
