Blues Trio Called Up For England's Nations League Fixtures

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Everton trio Jordan Pickford, Jarrad Branthwaite and James Garner have been called up to represent England in the Three Lions’ upcoming Nations League clashes with Croatia and Spain.

Thomas Tuchel’s side sit second in Group C following home and away victories against Czech Republic, with a 7-0 thrashing of Croatia sandwiched in between.

England host Croatia at Wembley on Thursday 12 November (7.45pm GMT) before travelling to face world champions Spain on Sunday 15 November (7.45pm GMT).

Harrison Armstrong and Tyrique George have been called up to represent England's Under-21s as the Young Lions prepare for upcoming games against Portugal and France.

The pair of fixtures will be the group’s first preparation matches ahead of next summer’s UEFA U21 EURO Finals – the young side managed by former-Blue Lee Carsley.

The youngsters secured their place in the Finals last month, and will head to Albania and Serbia in June 2027 with hopes of winning a third successive U21 EURO title.

They will face two nations who will also be heading to the Finals, with Portugal first up at Ashton Gate Stadium on Friday 13 November (7.30pm GMT).

Then, France are the visitors for the second game on Monday 16 November (7.30pm GMT).
