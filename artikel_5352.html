CME Group Announces Strong European Dairy Volumes on First Day of Trading

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

CHICAGO, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- CME Group, the world's leading derivatives marketplace, today announced that more than 100 dairy contracts of its new European dairy contracts exchanged hands in the first day of trading.

CME Group launched contracts for European Butter, European Skimmed Milk Powder, European Whey Powder and European Liquid Milk on November 23, signifying the first step in regional expansion following its agreement with the European Energy Exchange from earlier this year. A total of 126 contracts traded of European Butter and European Skimmed Milk Powder futures and open interest reached 60 across the two contracts.

"The launch of our European Dairy contracts is a significant milestone in bringing our U.S. dairy franchise to a global audience," said John Ricci, Managing Director and Global Head of Agricultural Products. "The first trades of our European Butter and European Skimmed Milk Powder futures highlight the need for participants across Europe to have the tools to manage regional price risk on a single platform."

CME Group's dairy complex reached a monthly volume record for futures and options with 11,611 contracts in October. In addition, dairy futures and options reached a record open interest on November 3, 2026, with 511,090 contracts.

European Dairy futures and options are tracking EEX indices before transitioning to CME Group benchmarks. They are listed on and subject to the rules of CME. For more information on these products, please visit here.
