Arion Kurtaj, the now 21-year-old hacker behind the massive GTA 6 leak of 2022, has been released from prison, as a judge warns that he shouldn't try this kind of hack again.

Kurtaj was 16 years old when his hacks against Rockstar began alongside other members of hacking group Lapsus$. Following the release of at least 90 videos featuring early GTA 6 gameplay, Kurtaj was deemed psychiatrically unfit for trial and was eventually sentenced to a lifetime stay in a secure hospital because of his severe autism. Rockstar said that the hack had cost it $5 million.

But Kurtaj's status changed in January, when he was transferred to a standard prison ahead of a planned retrial. Last week, he pleaded guilty to hacks against Rockstar and others.

Kurtaj has been given a seven-year sentence and released on the same day, as the BBC reports. He had already served the majority of that sentence during his time in the secure hospital and prison. The judge, Mr. Justice Turner, told Kurtaj "you are a very talented young man," but offered a warning: "Don't let this happen again."

The hacker has been released on license which, for my fellow Americans, is essentially a form of parole. He's been given a five-year Serious Crime Prevention Order restricting him from using online services including Telegram and Discord.

Of course, this isn't the only hacking incident Rockstar is dealing with. Ahead of launch, a hacking group or individual known as Cyberleek released numerous gameplay videos from a seemingly near-final build of GTA 6. Now, Cyberleek has released an Xbox build of the game which modders are working to turn into a full-blown PC port. At this point, it's possible that an unofficial GTA 6 PC port could land before Rockstar can deliver an official version.
