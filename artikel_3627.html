Zara Tindall Shows Cheltenham Why She's The Queen of Race Day Dressing

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Zara Tindall didn’t let the cold snap stop her from enjoying a day at the races on Friday. Accompanied by her close friend Dolly Maude and country-set favorite fashion designer Jade Holland Cooper, the daughter of Princess Anne and granddaughter of Queen Elizabeth II brought a dash of regal glamour to the proceedings following a glitzy night out in support of Prince William.

And while the sun may have been out, the Olympic equestrian still needed to wrap up against the chill. Tindall opted for a long plaid coat from L'Agence, a Purdey roll-neck jumper in brown, and a fascinator from Sally-Ann Provan. She accessorized with a new pair of Hector Lion earrings, a new Fairfax & Favor handbag, and completed the look with leather boots, also from Fairfax & Favor.

Zara Tindall joined in the action at Cheltenham Racecourse on Friday

Dolly, on the other hand, wore an avant-garde feather coat with knee-high boots and what looked to be a pair of emerald tights.

The pair were last spotted together in October, when they attended the William Hill Showcase Meeting at Cheltenham, but their friendship goes way back. Long considered Zara’s best friend, Dolly became Princess Anne’s lady-in-waiting in 2024. A former NHS midwife, she helped to deliver Zara’s third child, Lucas Tindall, on the floor of Zara’s bathroom at Gatcombe Park after the plan for the birth went awry in 2021.

Jade Holland Cooper and Zara Tindall on day one of The November Meeting at Cheltenham racecourse

But Dolly’s bond with the niece of King Charles III goes back long before that. (Not for nothing did Zara’s husband, Mike Tindall, joke that the 53-year-old is “more important than I am.”) Dolly was Zara’s maid of honor when the pair tied the knot, and when Zara split from her then-fiancé, jockey Richard Johnson, in 2003, the pair moved in together—they still live close by in the Cotswolds.

Having worked as a maternity practitioner in the NHS in Gloucestershire for 13 years, Dolly also did some philanthropic work using her maternity expertise, working in the unit of a hospital in Kambia, Sierra Leone for a month in 2013. At Kambia, she taught staff basic nursing skills. Before qualifying as a nurse, Dolly studied fashion and Cordon Bleu cookery at Eastbourne College of Food and Fashion, graduating in 1989.

Zara Tindall also braved the stormy weather for a day at Cheltenham with Dolly in October

Dolly Maude is known for her love of horses—a passion she shares with Zara and Princess Anne—and is often seen in Ascot’s royal box alongside the Princess Royal. In March 2024, she made her first official appearance as the Princess Royal’s lady-in-waiting, a momentous occasion that marked a new phase of her enduring bond with the royal family.

The royal confidant has four children with her husband Chris: Billy, who studied at Swansea University; Nell, who trained at the Central School of Ballet; a keen skier, Ted; and Mary.

Princess Anne's daughter wrapped up in a warm plaid coat and roll-neck jumper

Concerning Dolly’s appointment, another friend of Mrs. Maude's told The Telegraph, “She’s such a great girl. For the Princess Royal, it’s a perfect fit because of their shared love of dogs and horses. She’s not only extremely close to Zara, she also gets on brilliantly with the Princess. She’s fiercely loyal and discreet. It’s a great match.”

While much of Zara Tindall’s time this season has been spent touring the country’s most prestigious racing meets, she still found time to support her cousin, Prince William, at one of his treasured annual traditions. The Prince of Wales helped to put together the Tusk Conservation Awards at Raffles on Wednesday night and invited Zara to the celebration of conservation, alongside Lady Amelia and Lady Eliza Spencer and the Marchioness of Bath.

Dolly Maud made her debut as lady-in-waiting for the Princess Royal at the 2024 Commonwealth Day Service in March

Zara showcased her characteristically elegant style, donning a sapphire-blue Rebecca Vallance short-sleeved midi number, aptly named the “Crown Jewel” dress, which she paired with Kiki McDonough earrings. It is not the first time that the royal has lent her support to Prince William’s second-longest patronage: she also joined the crowd at the Tusk Awards in 2025, donning Rebecca Vallance again, and tearing up at the Prince of Wales’ speech in honor of those who gave their lives as rangers.
