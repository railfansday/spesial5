“He will be in charge” – Di Marzio clarifies positions of Krösche and Ibrahimovic

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Gianluca Di Marzio has confirmed Markus Krösche’s imminent arrival, and that he will be given the reins to take AC Milan forward.

It is not exactly a well-kept secret that Milan have been pursuing Krösche. He was the main target in the summer to come in and lead the management, but Eintracht Frankfurt blocked his exit. Now he has stepped down from his role there, a move to Italy has been in the headlines again.

Several sources have reported that the Rossoneri have an agreement in place with the German director to come in and be the new Head of Football. In other words, he will be the main man on the sporting side, and will offer some support to head coach Ruben Amorim.

What kind of relationship will the former Eintracht executive have with Zlatan Ibrahimovic? Gianluca Di Marzio spoke about it live on Sky, and his comments were relayed by MilanNews.

“Ibrahimovic has never been in charge of AC Milan, not when he left, nor when Tare left, nor during the interregnum. Cardinale is in charge now because he is Amorim’s true point of reference,” he began.

“The coach is there, and there will be the new Head of Football, Marcus Krosche. He will arrive from Eintracht, and now all that’s waiting for this agreement to be formally finalised. He will be in charge, and Ibrahimovic will stay on as he is now, that is, as a consultant to RedBird and Cardinale.

“But as you’ve seen, since the start of the season he’s been to Milanello once and to San Siro once or twice. He’s not the one in charge of AC Milan operations right now.”
