Choose Visible if Verizon’s signal is strong where you live and work and chat-only help suits you. Its base plan costs $25 a month, taxes and fees included, but its data can be slowed behind other traffic when a cell site is busy. Pay $35 for Visible+, whose data isn’t, if you stream or work online in crowded places.

Visible is an online-only prepaid carrier on Verizon’s network, and its base plan costs $300 a year for one line and $600 for two. Visible+, whose premium data isn’t slowed behind other traffic, costs $420 a year for one line and $720 for two with Visible’s group discount, against $540 and $1,080 plus taxes and fees for Verizon’s own Simplicity plan with AutoPay.

Visible’s base plan suits budget-minded singles and couples with strong Verizon signal who don’t need a store or premium data. Shoppers who want a store can get Verizon’s network at Total Wireless, which says its $25 bring-your-own-phone plan with AutoPay isn’t slowed behind other traffic, and anyone with weak Verizon signal can try Boost Mobile on other carriers’ towers.
