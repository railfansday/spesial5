GTA 6 In-Game TV Show Macca & Friends Looks Ridiculous In The Best Way

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Rockstar Games has released the first footage of an in-game GTA 6 TV show called "Macca & Friends," which is about a sunglass-wearing pink alligator and his wetland friends in Leonida (the stand-in for Florida).

One of Macca's friends is Pinchie the crab, who is Macca's landlord. Macca is also friends with a hat-wearing manatee and a flamingo. Check out the trailer below, which sets up a story where Macca needs to get money to pay his landlord. The story involves the group seemingly posing as cops and sneaking into a police station, maybe to grab some cash from an evidence locker?

Macca & Friends will be available to watch on Weazel, which is GTA's version of Fox. For now, all we have is this teaser. Given Rockstar's extreme push toward depth and immersion, we wouldn't be surprised if Macca & Friends is an actual multi-episode TV show in GTA 6 that people can watch on their in-game couch.

GTA 6 has an impressive attention to detail, so much so that a lot of what Rockstar created, fans may never even see.

GTA 6 launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Preloading is underway now, and that's good news because the game is 212GB.
