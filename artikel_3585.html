Why Did ABC Bleep Part of Miranda Lambert and Kacey Musgraves' CMAs Duet?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Fans were thrilled to see Kacey Musgraves and Miranda Lambert take the 2026 CMA Awards stage together — but their performance left a lot to be desired.

When the country singers performed their duet “Horses and Divorces” off Musgraves’ Middle of Nowhere album on Wednesday, November 18, those watching at home missed multiple parts of the song due to sporadic spats of dead air.

ABC had to bleep out several lines due to the pair’s NSFW lyrics, including the second line of the song, which led to a muffled and disjointed performance.

After Musgraves, 38, began by singing, “You know that I’ve said some things about you,” referring to her and Lambert’s past feud, Lambert’s line, “And I’ve done my fair share of s*** talkin’ too” was muted.

The broadcast was forced to bleep over the “s*** talkin’” portion of the lyrics — but it wasn’t the only line butchered by on-air censoring.

Later in the song, Texas natives Musgraves and Lambert bonded over their fandom of country legend Willie Nelson, singing, “We both love Willie, but I mean really, what a**hole doesn’t like Willie?”

The “a**hole” section was bleeped and aired as dead air, too, once again messing with the natural rhythm of the song.

Fans, however, were still able to see two of country music’s biggest stars put on one heck of a show while paying homage to their Texas roots.

Musgraves wore booty shorts — or hot pants — and a skintight tank with the word “Texas” written on it, coupled with a pair of sexy high heels as she walked around the beauty parlor-inspired set.

Lambert, 43, meanwhile, wore a jean skirt, crop top and knee-high boots as she strutted her stuff around the stage.

The pair’s CMAs performance comes after they buried the hatchet earlier this year when recording their hit duet, “Horses and Divorces.”

Lambert and Musgraves previously feuded over Lambert’s 2011 song “Mama’s Broken Heart,” a song that Musgraves cowrote and planned to record herself before Lambert allegedly persuaded Musgraves to allow her to record it instead.

The song became a top 10 Billboard Country Airplay chart hit and led to several award wins for Lambert. It wasn’t until three years later that Musgraves earned own breakthrough hit with 2013’s “Merry Go ‘Round” off her debut album.

In April, Musgraves confirmed that she did have “grass-fed, grade A” beef with Lambert more than a decade before they chose to team up for “Horses and Divorces.”

“I mean, it’s real and that’s why I love this song, because it’s not coming from some contrived place in a writing room,” she told Variety. “We’ve come together after years of really, honestly, not being friends. I had this idea one day when I saw her on Instagram, riding one of her horses, and I thought, ‘Well, I guess we have two things in common: horses and divorces.’ And I was like, ‘Wait, that’s a song.’”

After writing the song, Musgraves recalled reaching out to Lambert for the first time in “years” and told her, “‘Hey, I have this idea. If anybody would get it, it would be you. We’ve had our s*** over the years, but this would be really funny.’ And she was like, ‘I’m down.’”
