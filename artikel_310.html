Get the Apple iPhone 17e from $5.99 per month at AT&T — no trade-in required

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

TL;DR: AT&T is offering the Apple iPhone 17e starting at $5.99 per month over 36 months (down from $699.99 retail) with a new unlimited line — or $0.43 per month online.

Most carrier phone deals come with a catch — you usually have to hand over a relatively recent smartphone just to unlock the advertised monthly rate. This AT&T iPhone 17e deal skips that requirement entirely, letting new customers get the 256GB Apple iPhone 17e starting at $5.99 per month over 36 months when they activate a new line on an eligible unlimited plan, with no trade-in required.

Here's how the pricing math actually breaks down, because there are two different numbers floating around on AT&T's promo page. Normally, the 256GB iPhone 17e carries a $699.99 retail price at AT&T, or $19.45 per month on a 36-month, 0% APR installment plan. With this offer, AT&T applies up to $484.36 in monthly bill credits over three years, reducing the base promo rate to $5.99 per month (the rate business customers and shoppers buying through retail partners will pay).

However, if you are a new consumer customer ordering directly online at AT&T, the carrier adds an extra $200 in online bill credits ($5.56 per month for 36 months) on top — bringing your effective payment all the way down to $0.43 per month, or about $15.48 total over three years. Even as Apple's entry-level model, the iPhone 17e covers all the everyday essentials without feeling stripped down. You get a 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display protected by Ceramic Shield 2 (rated for three times better scratch resistance than the iPhone 16e), Apple's fast A19 chip, up to 26 hours of video playback on a charge, and 256GB of starting storage.

Apple also added 15W MagSafe wireless charging — double the 7.5W speed of the 16e — along with the customizable Action Button for quick shortcuts or visual intelligence, and a 48MP Fusion camera with optical-quality 2x Telephoto zoom and adjustable portrait background blur after you take a photo.

Before checking out, it helps to know how the carrier fine print works so there are no surprises on your bill:

You need an eligible unlimited plan — This offer requires activating a new line on a qualifying postpaid unlimited voice and data plan. For a single consumer line on AT&T Value 2.0, plans start at $60 per month before AutoPay and paperless billing discounts ($50 per month once those discounts kick in within two bills), plus taxes and monthly carrier fees.

Expect upfront taxes, a $35 fee, and a short wait for credits — Well-qualified buyers get $0 down, though you still pay sales tax on the full $699.99 retail price upfront plus a one-time $35 activation fee. Promotional bill credits can take up to 3 billing cycles to appear on your statement, and you cannot cancel service on any other lines on your account within 90 days, or those credits could stop.

It is a three-year commitment unless you pay for early upgrades — Because the discount is spread across 36 monthly bill credits rather than taken off the price upfront, canceling your wireless service, paying off the phone early, or upgrading early will stop your remaining credits and leave you owing the rest of the phone balance.

As long as you are already looking to switch to AT&T (or add a new line) and plan to stick with your plan for three years, getting a 256GB iPhone 17e for $0.43 a month online — while keeping your old phone as a backup or handing it down — is about as painless as a carrier upgrade gets.
