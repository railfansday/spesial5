Gasperini dreams of bringing Lookman to Roma

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Roma continues to evaluate new attacking options ahead of the January transfer window, and Ademola Lookman has emerged as one of the names being pursued by the Giallorossi management.

According to La Repubblica, the Roman club is monitoring the situation of the Nigerian striker, who is finding little playing time at Atletico Madrid.

The player has never started in the Champions League and was also left out of Sunday’s big match against Barcelona. This situation could open up new possibilities in the coming weeks.

A potential arrival in the capital would represent a return to Gian Piero Gasperini, with whom the striker enjoyed one of the most important moments of his career.

The two won the Europa League in 2024 with Atalanta, thanks in large part to the Nigerian’s historic hat-trick in the final against Bayer Leverkusen.
