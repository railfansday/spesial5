Nations League team of the international break: Bellingham, Hojbjerg, Oblak…

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

An entertaining international break has come to an end, marking the return of domestic football this weekend.

Before the dust settles on the UEFA Nations League group stage, we have compiled an XI of the best performers from the November international break.

Including two Liverpool players, here we go…

It was a pair of clean sheets for Slovenian legend Oblak as his nation earned a second-place finish in Group E ahead of Scotland and North Macedonia.

Across both games, a 0-0 draw against Switzerland and a 3-0 win in North Macedonia, Oblak made seven saves, four of which came from shots inside the box, and five successful high claims.

FIFA Ultimate Team cult hero Hysaj was superb this international break, helping Albania earn 2-0 victories over Finland and Belarus.

Hysaj assisted against Finland, creating two big chances and making four effective clearances. Against Belarus, he bagged himself a goal.

Tougjas was colossal in Estonia’s wins over Bulgaria and Luxembourg, making a last-man tackle against the former and a clearance off the line against the latter.

His international teammate, Karol Mets, deserves an honourable mention after his last-man tackle in the 1-0 win over Luxembourg and four effective clearances against Bulgaria.

One of two Liverpool players in this XI, Kerkez was on form for Hungary this international break.

Against Northern Ireland, Kerkez created one big chance, had four attempted assists and completed two dribbles. Defensively, he also recorded four effective clearances, two won tackles and two blocked crosses.

He was just as impressive against Georgia, doing more defensive work than attacking and contributing to a clean sheet in his side’s 2-0 away win.

Probably the best-performing player in the Nations League over the two November games, Hojbjerg was absolutely phenomenal for Denmark in their huge 4-1 win over Portugal and late defeat to Norway.

Hojbjerg assisted after scoring a penalty to put the Danes ahead in Portugal and was also on target as his side raced into a 2-0 lead against Norway on Tuesday night, only for Erling Haaland to score twice either side of Martin Odegaard’s equaliser, including a 96th-minute penalty to pip second place ahead of their crestfallen opponents.

Despite Denmark’s ultimate shortcomings, Hojbjerg was a man possessed. He also recorded a clearance off the line against Norway and won six tackles across both games.

One of the most underrated players in Europe, Guler is in his element when representing his country as the top boy.

It was another fruitful break for the Real Madrid star, even if Turkey were unable to get their first point on the board in a horrible group consisting of themselves, France, Belgium and Italy.

Guler ran the show against Belgium but was unable to inspire even a draw in Izmir. He also impressed against Zinedine Zidane’s France, assisting Abdulkerim Bardakci’s consolation goal.

Things are beginning to click for Germany under Jurgen Klopp.

Not only is that good news for every Germany supporter, but also for Liverpool supporters, who will be thrilled to see Florian Wirtz starring for his country. Well, thrilled because he could replicate this form at club level, or frustrated because Anfield are yet to see this version of a player who cost the club over £100million.

Wirtz scored three goals in Germany’s two wins, starting with one in the 3-1 triumph over nine-man Serbia before an 11-minute brace in the 5-0 statement victory over old rivals the Netherlands.

The big question is: can Klopp’s influence transform Wirtz’s fortunes at Liverpool?

England deserve at least one representative in this XI after topping their Nations League group with wins over Croatia at Wembley and, more notably, in Madrid against reigning world champions Spain, ending their incredible 43-game unbeaten run.

Bellingham was a man possessed at the Wanda Metropolitano, thriving in the hostile atmosphere as a Real Madrid player representing England. Jeering this man only makes him play better, you know?

Bellingham could be the best player in world football right now.

Another beneficiary of Klopp’s style is RB Leipzig winger Gruda, who is back in his home country on loan from high-flying Brighton & Hove Albion.

He was on fire in Germany’s two victories, scoring against Serbia and enjoying the best night of his career against the Netherlands on Monday, scoring twice and providing one assist.

What makes it even better is that the Serbia game was his senior debut for Germany.

The Red Devils earned a second-place finish ahead of Turkiye and Italy after beating both teams, finishing one point behind France in Group A, and Jeremy Doku’s performances were a massive reason why.

Kevin De Bruyne and Maxim De Cuyper were also brilliant, but Doku’s assist against Turkiye earned Belgium a huge away win, and his goal put his side 3-1 up against the Italians after only 26 minutes last Sunday.

Krstovic has seven goals in 11 Serie A matches for Atalanta this season, and he carried that excellent form into the international break with Montenegro.

Montenegro finished the Nations League group stage with five wins and one draw and, in the most recent break, Krstovic scored in a one-goal victory against Latvia and was involved in all four goals, scoring and assisting two each in a 4-1 win against Cyprus.

As far as international breaks go on an individual level, this is the cream of the crop.
