Influencer accused of faking cancer in $37K romance scam becomes international fugitive

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

A Brazilian influencer accused of pretending to be a young woman with cancer to scam an online boyfriend out of $37,000 has become an international fugitive after fleeing the country.

Luís Felipe de Oliveira, 23, better known online as Felipe Heystee, is wanted by Brazilian authorities after allegedly spending more than two years pretending to be a 19-year-old woman named Luana.

According to Terra, Heystee allegedly developed an online relationship with a 46-year-old geographer, convincing him to send money for supposed cancer treatments, rent, and other expenses.

Between May 2022 and August 2024, the victim transferred approximately 207,600 Brazilian reais ($37,000 USD), despite never meeting his supposed girlfriend or speaking to her over video call.

In one conversation, the fake girl requested money for “an ultrasound and a pap smear to check for cancer” and then added, “I just remembered I didn’t pay my health insurance this month.”

The alleged scam began falling apart when the victim noticed several bank transfers were going into accounts bearing Heystee’s name.

Suspicious, he conducted a reverse-image search on a photograph sent by Luana and discovered it belonged to a completely different person, a 16-year-old influencer who had no connection to Heystee.

After contacting the girl’s family, the man reported the suspected fraud to police.

“They said their daughter didn’t know Felipe and suggested he’d taken the girl’s photo without permission to trick me,” he said.

According to Need To Know, Heystee fled Brazil for the Dominican Republic on March 4, 2026, approximately 24 hours after prosecutors requested his arrest.

His name was subsequently added to Interpol’s Red Diffusion system on July 27, alerting international law enforcement agencies that he was wanted by Brazilian authorities.

Despite being a fugitive, Heystee’s social media, where he has amassed 2.3 million TikTok followers and more than 1.5 million Instagram followers, has remained active.

A Brazilian court has ordered his social media accounts blocked and frozen more than R$200,000 in assets that could help compensate the victim.

Heystee faces electronic fraud charges, with a criminal hearing scheduled for November 18.
