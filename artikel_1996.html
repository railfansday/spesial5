Sources: Romeo Lavia's stance on Aston Villa January move revealed

Chelsea midfielder Romeo Lavia is resistant to potentially moving to Aston Villa in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The Villans have been linked with a surprise move for the Belgian in recent days, but are set to find it difficult to tempt him into making the switch.

Chelsea are not keen on the idea of selling the 22-year-old mid-season, and the player himself is also not fully on board with the idea of an exit.

Sources say that Lavia is happy at Stamford Bridge, and is still keen to stay put and fight for his place in the squad.

Injuries have hampered the starlet since his £53million move from Southampton back in 2023, but he has been a key part of Xabi Alonso's plans so far.

Lavia has already started six Premier League games this season and has chipped in with a goal and an assist already.

The centre of midfield have been a problem position for Chelsea in recent weeks, after they ran out of time to replace Enzo Fernandez in the summer.

That has seen Lavia get significant minutes as a result, and he has turned in some promising performances of late.

That has caught Villa's attention in recent weeks, but their chances of striking a deal for him this winter are very unlikely.

Football Insider revealed on Tuesday that the "only way" Chelsea would allow Lavia to leave is if they brought several new midfielders into their squad this winter.

That could leave Villa waiting on business being completed elsewhere for them to land their target, which is not a good situation to be in.

Former Villa chief executive Keith Wyness told Football Insider last week that his former club could potentially accept an "£80m" offer for Boubacar Kamara this winter.

The Frenchman is being tracked by Chelsea ahead of the January window, and Villa hold all the cards over his long-term future.

Wyness believes that Unai Emery's side are under no pressure to sell, but are well aware that Chelsea are capable of paying "silly money" for their targets.

He believes that Villa could yet be tempted into a sale if an £80m offer were to be put on the table, given they feel it is above Kamara's true valuation.
