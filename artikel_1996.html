Can Milan extend their unbeaten run? Previewing the Derby della Madonnina

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Tomorrow night, AC Milan host Inter in the Derby della Madonnina and it’s a huge game for both teams, as always.

A game that needs little introduction, the Milanese derby is one of the biggest and best games in the footballing calendar for neutrals. For fans, well, that decides which side of the city you fall under. Recently, it’s been the Rossoneri that have celebrated more often.
