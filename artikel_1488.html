The Madrid press already puts Yan Diomandé at the level of Lamine Yamal

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Madrid press continues to be under the spell of Yan Diomande. The Ivorian particularly impressed during the trip to Mestalla, where he completed six dribbles, his third best total of the season and his best away from home in the league. After a start to the season where he had to adapt, the former Lepizig player became a real weapon for José Mourinho to strike and gain depth. All competitions combined, only Kylian Mbappé did slightly better than him at Real Madrid with 53 successful dribbles, compared to 52 for Diomande, while the Frenchman played 541 additional minutes.

In an article devoted to the Ivorian winger, A.S. already places him in the same discussion as Lamine Yamal, the current world reference in dribbling in Spain. With 43 successful dribbles in La Liga, the Real Madrid player occupies second place in the ranking, behind the Barcelona player and his 57 achievements. But Diomande has a better average, with a successful dribble every 20 minutes, compared to 23 for his Catalan rival. Enough to fuel a comparison which is gaining more and more momentum in Spain.

This spectacular progression did not go unnoticed in the Madrid locker room. His speed, his technical quality and his ability to play on both wings are attractive, as is his understanding with Kylian Mbappé. Jude Bellingham was also impressed with his shot quality after his stunning goal at Santander. The former Leipzig player is gradually establishing himself as an important element of the Madrid collective, to the point of having already played more minutes than Vinicius this season (892 against 882).

The comparison with the Brazilian is another lesson highlighted by the local press. Diomande has already played eight times on each wing and displays interesting statistics in both roles. On the left side, usually occupied by Vinicius, he has three goals and an assist, compared to one goal and an assist on the right. For the Madrid daily, the Ivorian is changing dimension and is now invited into the discussion with the best dribblers in the championship.
