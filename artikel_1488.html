Will Ronaldinho play for Ravenna again? Everything we know after his comeback game

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Yes, it really happened. Ronaldinho back on the pitch at the age of 46.

It’s safe to assume there were more eyes on a Ravenna fixture than ever before on Sunday, with the promise of Ronaldinho returning to action more than a decade after retiring.

One of the most joyful footballers to watch in his prime, Ronaldinho playing in Serie C would have been unthinkable back in the day when he was enjoying his peak years with the likes of Barcelona and AC Milan.

But after becoming an investor in Ravenna earlier this year and being signed up as a player, the Brazilian made his debut in their clash with Forli.

He was initially expected to play for half an hour, but ended up lasting the whole first half. Ravenna won 2-0, with Ronaldinho involved – the extent of which has been exaggerated by many accounts – in the build up for the first goal.

His highlights reel from the game didn’t look too shabby for a player out of the game for so long.

So, was it a one-off or could we see Ronaldinho’s revival continue with further appearances for Ravenna?

The great man himself isn’t ruling it out.

“Those 45 minutes went by too fast; for me, football has always been like that,” he told Sky Sport Italia.

“I don’t know yet. I wouldn’t rule out a return; it depends on the president. If he calls me, I’ll come. Playing makes me happy; experiencing moments in the locker room, all of that is wonderful. Football is the most beautiful thing I’ve ever had in my life.”

What does the man it depends on reckon, then?

“We’ll see how things go; he was a phenomenon,” replied president Ignazio Cipriani. “He didn’t want to come off; he was having fun.”

Vice-president Ariedo Braida, who was AC Milan’s sporting director during Ronaldinho’s time there, was more open, insisting: “I hope he returns to play again. I hope to see him here once more.”

Ravenna’s coach Andrea Mandorlini isn’t ruling it out either.

“Ronaldinho is one of the greatest champions of the modern era,” he said. “He made his return wearing our colours, and we are proud of that. It was a great day, with a stadium full of energy. Our thanks go to the ownership for even conceiving the idea.

“Another appearance? We’re here – we’ve shown that. We’re happy and proud. If he wants to do it again, we’d be more than happy. There are no issues at all. He was with us, and he even treated us to a few flashes of his world-class skill.”

Ravenna’s next game is away at Livorno on Sunday. They remain in a promotion chase and probably can’t afford a publicity stunt every week if they want to keep it up, but who knows? Ronaldinho looked tidy enough on his club debut and could have more to give.
