Bruno Fernandes’ next club: Six options after Man Utd contract comments

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Bruno Fernandes has once again cast his Manchester United future into doubt with his latest comments on his contract, but where might he go next if he does leave in 2027?

Fernandes was outstanding last season for United, but it’s been a sorry season for them so far. That isn’t entirely their captain’s fault, but naturally, with his contract in its final year, doubts have been raised over his future.

Now, Fernandes has confirmed there are ‘no negotiations’ about his contract after already reportedly rejecting two offers to extend his stay at Old Trafford.

He told Sky Sports: “No, there’s no negotiations ongoing, but I’ve seen people saying that he’s asked for more money, he’s asked for more years. If I wanted more money, I wouldn’t be here. I had somewhere to go to get much more money than being at Manchester United.

“I could have been in a place that is much warmer than Manchester.

“Two summers ago, three summers ago, last summer, I always had the chance to leave and I always chose to be here. The thing is, I’ve always showed how much I care about the club and how much I want to win at this club. And I always said, in the moment the club thinks it’s time for me to move on, I will move on.

“I’m not going to be the player that I’ve seen many in this time at Manchester United. They’re going to stay here just by staying, just finish my contract and then we will see. In the moment the club says, ‘Bruno, it’s time to part ways’, I’m going to be the first one walking out the door.

“Yeah, the club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay. And as you said, there are no negotiations going on.

“We spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side because I don’t think it’s the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most important thing now is that the club gets back to win games.”

He’s got a point, to be fair, but there’s now a big question mark over his future at the club.

If Fernandes does leave, here’s where he could end up.

It’s the go-to solution now. Ageing player on the decline, but still extremely talented and well-paid? Saudi Arabia might be your only option.

Dealmakers in the Saudi Pro League have tried to tempt Fernandes before, but he’s held firm in Manchester – committing, as he inferred, to less money and less degrees on the thermometer.

But the interest probably hasn’t gone away and it might be a different answer this time around.

Of the major Saudi clubs, Al Hilal have been the most strongly linked with Fernandes. It could give him the chance to team up with his compatriot Ruben Neves, as well as other former Premier League players like Ollie Watkins and Gabriel Martinelli.

Even at 32, it probably feels too soon for Fernandes to move to the Middle East, but it might be his most realistic option.

Mohamed Salah showed in the summer that just because you expect an ageing, declining, extremely talented and well-paid player to go to Saudi Arabia, it doesn’t have to be the case.

The Turkish Super Lig can also be a suitable landing spot for someone not quite ready to let go of European football yet.

Salah signing for Trabzonspor instead of one of the country’s powerhouses was surprising, and we’d assume Fernandes wouldn’t follow suit.

Galatasaray would probably stand out as the most competitive team for him to join. Just imagine him teeing up Victor Osimhen. There might be another assists record for him to break.

Fernandes had a fantastic spell with Sporting before he joined United, scoring 64 goals from 137 games for them.

He earns more than six times more in wages these days than he did when he was playing his football in Lisbon, so he’d have to make some sacrifices to go back.

It’d be a nostalgic choice, but it’s hard to see it happening.

Is Fernandes good enough for Barcelona or Real Madrid? On his day, yes. Do either of them have a burning need for him right now? Probably not.

But we can definitely picture him playing in La Liga. And he has the right balance of flair and grittiness to succeed in an Atletico Madrid side.

It’s the kind of move that would allow him to keep pushing for honours at a high level in Europe.

Atleti aren’t afraid of picking up a big name from the Premier League if their budget allows, so could Fernandes be next? It’s not out of the question.

Before he became the household name he is today, Fernandes actually started his career in Italy. He played for Novara in Serie B before spells in Serie A with Udinese and Sampdoria.

It was only after five years in Italy that he headed back to his native Portugal with Sporting, directly prior to his United move.

Obviously, Fernandes would be a far bigger draw now if he was to return to Serie A. He wouldn’t be re-joining basement dwellers Udinese, let alone Sampdoria in Serie B or Novara in Serie C.

Instead, it’d be the big boys setting their sights on Fernandes. AC Milan, Inter, Juventus…

Inter especially have a penchant for picking up veterans on freebies, but it’s their two rivals who have been linked with Fernandes more recently.

A move to AC Milan, of course, would see him reunite with Ruben Amorim. Now that’d be interesting.

Fernandes actually had a decent record under his compatriot with 20 goals from 58 games together. Not too shabby.

Milan haven’t blown anyone away yet under Amorim but have made a better start to his reign than expected. A sprinkling of star quality with a signing like Fernandes might help them get back on their perch.
