What Will Anthropic’s Price Be in 3 Months? History Offers an Answer That’s Strikingly Clear.

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The stock climbed 17% on its first day of trading.

By Adria Cimino – Nov 21, 2026 at 4:02AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Anthropic ›

Anthropic (ANT -8.50%) soared onto the scene this week with the biggest initial public offering on record, raising more than $81 billion. This figure climbed to $93.5 billion as underwriters exercised an overallotment option. Anthropic opened at $111 on its first day of trading, representing a 33% gain from its IPO price of $83. The stock finished the day off its peak, however, with a 17% increase.

Investors kept a close eye on the Anthropic IPO as it represented the first pure-play artificial intelligence (AI) company to debut on the market -- and could offer insight into general sentiment regarding the AI space. AI stocks led major indexes higher in recent years as investors cheered the technology's game-changing potential. This year, however, concern has grown about the depth of tech companies' spending on the AI build-out. Investors have questioned whether the revenue opportunity will support the hundreds of billions of dollars invested and whether this pace of investment is sustainable.

After Anthropic's explosive debut, you may be wondering what the stock price will be in three months. History offers an answer that's strikingly clear.

First, though, let's take a quick look at the Anthropic story so far. Anthropic, founded by Dario Amodei and a team of former OpenAI researchers in 2021, is the maker of popular AI model Claude. The company has seen revenue roar higher ever since Claude's initial launch just three years ago. Over that time period, it's gone from about zero to more than $17 billion in the latest quarter.

Anthropic fits into the AI ecosystem in the following way: It partners with major cloud providers for the enormous compute it needs to train its Claude models, then monetizes Claude through serving companies and consumers. Enterprise, however, is a key focus for Anthropic, and more than 50% of the Fortune 500 brought in at least $100,000 in revenue on a trailing 12-month basis as of the end of September.

Now, let's consider our question: What will the Anthropic stock price be three months after this historic IPO? For an idea, I looked at 10 of the biggest IPOs from 1999 through 2023, including names such as Meta Platforms and Uber Technologies. In their first three months of trading, eight delivered a negative performance. And five of those declines were in the double digits. In fact, the average three-month return was a 13% decline.

Two recent IPO stocks, Space Exploration Technologies and Cerebras Systems, confirm that trend. They fell 6% and 29%, respectively, in their first three months of trading.

So, history offers us a strikingly clear answer: If Anthropic follows the trend, the stock may drop over the coming three months, potentially even in the double digits.

What does this mean for you as an investor? If you're already an Anthropic shareholder, this isn't a reason to sell. Short-term performance isn't important; what's more important is a company's earnings and stock performance over a number of years.

As for whether you should buy Anthropic, my answer is the following: I wouldn't rush to get in on the stock. The AI lab offers an impressive growth story, but it still comes with a hefty amount of risk. This includes the investment required to continue building and training models and other potential problems such as any slowdown in demand or competition from rivals like OpenAI. Questions about possible regulation of the industry could also weigh on stock performance.

All of this means you should take your time evaluating Anthropic, and if you feel the stock suits your investment style, consider buying on an eventual dip.
