Rob Edwards backed to replace John Eustace at Derby County - but Middlesbrough 'concern' raised

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

It has been a nightmare start to the season for Derby County in the Championship.

After missing out on the play-offs on the final day of last season, many expected Derby to mount another promotion push this time around, but it has certainly not worked out that way.

It was another disastrous night for Derby as they were beaten 4-0 by Norwich City at Pride Park on Tuesday night, with goals from Anthony Musaba, Paris Maghoma, Darlin Yongwa and Jovon Makama sealing an emphatic victory for the Canaries.

The Rams' latest defeat extended their winless run to 10 games, and while they remain 23rd in the Championship table, they are now seven points from safety after winning just one of their opening 14 matches.

Despite increasing numbers of supporters calling for head coach John Eustace to be sacked, the 47-year-old will be in charge for the visit of Bolton Wanderers on Friday night, but with the November international break looming, he will be desperate for a result against the 17th-placed Trotters.

Should Derby part company with Eustace, one name that has been suggested as a potential replacement is Rob Edwards, who has been out of work since leaving Wolverhampton Wanderers in June following their relegation from the Premier League.

Edwards' most notable achievement came when he led Luton Town to promotion to the top flight via the play-offs in 2023, while he has also had spells with the likes of Forest Green Rovers, Watford and Middlesbrough, and he has been linked with a number of Championship clubs this season, including Southampton, Burnley and Wrexham.

When asked for his thoughts on the possible appointment of Edwards, FLW's Derby County fan pundit Shaun Woodward said that he would be happy to see the 43-year-old replace Eustace, but he admitted that he has concerns about his lack of loyalty after he walked away from Middlesbrough after just 16 games in charge to join Wolves last November.

"Forget Rob Edwards, I'd probably take Rob Earnshaw right now with the way things are going at Derby," Shaun told FLW.

"John Eustace has lost the dressing room, he sounds like a defeated man when he's doing his interviews post-match.

"We're shipping goals at an alarming rate, we're not looking like we're going to score, there's no fight in the team.

"They all look like they're out for themselves, they're hiding, it's a horrid, horrid time to be a Derby fan right now.

"We desperately need a change of manager.

"Rob Edwards has got experience of getting promotion out of this league at Luton, he did well in the Premier League as well with limited resources.

"My only problem with him is when he was at Middlesbrough and seemingly had a good thing going there, his head was turned, and he left to go to Wolves, which worries me.

"We've had quite a few managers over the years that have done well at Derby, and then a bigger club has come looking and they've left.

"That worries me with Rob Edwards, would he do the same again?

"Let's say he got us up the table this season, next season he gets us promoted or in and around it, and then another club comes looking and he leaves us.

"We want stability at the club, so that's my only concern with Edwards, but I would take him right now because something has to change.

While Edwards had a strong connection to Wolves, his decision to leave Middlesbrough while they were second in the Championship to take over at a club that looked doomed to relegation from the Premier League was questionable, and it will be a red flag in the minds of prospective employers.

With the club currently sitting seven points from safety, it seems Derby could be involved in a battle for survival this season, and it would be a risk for them to appoint a manager who has two relegations on his CV with Luton and Wolves, even if there are mitigating circumstances for those failures.
