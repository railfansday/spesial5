Sheffield United vs Watford predictions as ‘clear issue’ brings unanimous scoreline verdict

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Sheffield United lost 2-1 away at Charlton Athletic at The Valley last time out before the international break, with Jordan Ayew getting their goal. SUFC are positioned in 10th place in the Championship table after their first 15 fixtures of the season.

The Blades are only two points off the play-offs behind both QPR and Southampton at the moment. Prior to their defeat against the Addicks, they went four games unbeaten under Chris Wilder.

As for Watford, it has been a tough campaign for them to date and they are 20th in the division. They are five points above the relegation zone ahead of Derby County and Burnley.

The Hornets drew 0-0 at Vicarage Road with promotion chasing Middlesbrough in their last outing which wasn’t a bad result. However, pressure continues to grow on their head coach Alessio Dionisi amid their poor run of form.

“Sheffield United will be desperate to bounce back from their defeat to Charlton Athletic last time out. SUFC have lacked consistency overall this season but remain in a strong position as they eye a place in the play-offs.

“The Blades are unbeaten in their last three outings at Bramall Lane so they are starting to make their home ground a bit of a fortress. They should fancy their chances against a team struggling for form this weekend.

“Watford will be nervously looking over their shoulder towards the bottom three after their poor start to the season. The Hornets continue to stick by manager Alessio Dionisi at the moment which is very unlike their hierarchy.

“They haven’t won away in this campaign and are struggling away from Vicarage Road. For that reason, I think they’ll lose in South Yorkshire.”

“Sheffield United saw their momentum stunted by that loss to Charlton Athletic, but they’re still in a decent position and should be confident of overcoming Watford this weekend.

“Chris Wilder hasn’t found it easy since his return to Bramall Lane amid off-pitch battles between past and present owners, but SUFC have built a strong foundation at the back with top Championship defenders like Liam Kitching and Japhet Tanganga. Firepower can go missing at times, but that becomes less of an issue if you’re good at the back.

“Watford got some good results of their own before the break though, beating Lincoln City and drawing to Middlesbrough without conceding. Another battling defensive performance could see them keep the likes of Romelle Donovan and Jordan Ayew at bay.

“The clear issue for Watford is that neither of those results came at home. In fact, they’ve got just one point in seven away games this season.

“For that reason, I’m predicting a Blades win here.”
