West Brom set for major boost - it will impact Celtic

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

West Bromwich Albion are plotting fresh contract action to secure the long-term future and reward the performances of a star performer at the Hawthorns in a new development which could have a detrimental transfer impact for Scottish giants Celtic.

The Baggies have undertaken serious strides in what represents James Morrison's first full campaign in charge, with the ex-Albion midfield star having guided his old side to safety in the dying embers of the previous campaign before directing a play-off charge this time around.

West Brom find themselves positioned fourth in the Championship ahead of this evening's crunch home clash against promotion rivals Southampton following three victories in their last four matches over Wrexham, Blackburn Rovers and title favourites West Ham.

An uplifting summer transfer window enabled supporters to dream of promotion pushes once again, and the Baggies did well to bolster Morrison's squad with influential signings such as Felix Horn Myhre, Jimmy-Jay Morgan and Rabby Nzingoula while retaining star performers in the Black Country.

It's no secret that a number of West Brom's players did attract interest, and one of those players to have earned admirers elsewhere is now edging closer to extending his stay in blue-and-white in what will come as a blow as far as Celtic are concerned.

The player in question is skipper George Campbell, who, as per a new update via X from Mike McGrath of the Telegraph, has been sought out for a contract extension by Albion.

Just this afternoon, McGrath has reported that West Brom have entered negotiations over a new contract for Campbell in an effort to reflect the three-cap USMNT international's ever-growing importance and authority at the Hawthorns after initially arriving in the summer of 2025 in a £1.5 million deal from MLS outfit CF Montreal.

Campbell enjoyed a positive individual debut campaign last time out in spite of West Brom's collective struggles, which would see the defender earn wide plaudits for his steely and composed defensive displays while racking up 39 Championship appearances and finding the back of the net on four occasions.

After being named as West Brom's club captain over the summer following Jed Wallace's departure, Campbell has taken his performances up a notch and has relished the added responsibility as the heartbeat of a much-sturdier backline under Morrison.

That's exactly why the Black Country outfit are now pushing to secure a new contract for Campbell, who has appeared in all but one of his side's second-tier fixtures to date, and still has just under three years remaining on his current terms.

West Brom's ongoing contract negotiations with Campbell will represent a considerable boost to all at the Hawthorns if such discussions come to fruition, providing the Baggies with fresh security in the transfer market and protecting one of their prised assets from being poached while rewarding the 25-year-old with the sort of deal that his ever-increasing importance has only warranted.

It's not quite so much a boost for Celtic, however, with Martin O'Neill's side having reportedly expressed an interest in the ex-Atlanta United ace's signature at the back-end of the 2025/26 term.

Reports had suggested that Celtic were interested in Campbell alongside unnamed Bundesliga and Championship clubs, even indicating that the then-£2.5 million-rated Albion defender was open to the possibility of a switch to Parkhead.

It never materialised, though, and Celtic would actually last the summer transfer window without acquiring a central defender as O'Neill only made minor adjustments to his backline.

Indeed, the Hoops boss offloaded three central defenders while only bringing in full-backs Landon Emenalo and Jordan Lotomba from Chelsea and Feyenoord respectively right at the end of the window, and with the Glaswegian side's backline having been shakier-than-usual in league action with some notable gaping shortcomings in European competition, it's an area of the pitch that they will surely intend on bolstering once January rolls around.

However, should they seek to set their sights on a deal for Campbell once again, it's likely that any such efforts would go nowhere if he ends up putting pen to paper on fresh terms with Albion, whose ongoing efforts for a play-off finish could also go some way to convincing the popular captain of committing to a long-term future with the Baggies.

Naturally, West Brom supporters are excited by the news that a new contract for Campbell could be nearing.

Always keen for the club to keep hold of their star performers, it would be a statement of intent if the Baggies end up successful in their bid to extend the highly-rated defender's future and fans are lapping it up.
