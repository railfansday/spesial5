Middlesbrough boss Kim Hellberg breaks silence on Crystal Palace links

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Kim Hellberg is continuing to earn admirers as Middlesbrough's pursuit of promotion to the Premier League toils on, and with new interest emerging from Crystal Palace ahead of Pierre Sage's increasingly-inevitable sacking, the Swede has now made his thoughts clear on a potential switch to Selhurst Park.

Middlesbrough will fancy their chances of successfully avenging last season's heartbreaking last-grasp play-off final defeat to Hull City after enjoying a strong start to the opening months of the 2026/27 Championship campaign, which has seen Hellberg's side lock down second-place heading into the November international break with eight victories, five draws and just two defeats from 15 fixtures to date.

Level on points with both league leaders Birmingham City and third-placed Stoke, Middlesbrough have often appeared the team to beat in the second-tier thus far and will intend on picking up where they left off once they return to action in two weeks' time with a double-header against Bristol City and a fellow promotion rival in Swansea - having endured a slight dip in form during Will Lankshear's injury-enforced absence.

The Teessiders have shown that, under Hellberg's tuition, they are capable of finally ending their top-flight exile and getting over the finish line once and for all, which is exactly why the Riverside Stadium faithful will want nothing more than recent links to Crystal Palace to go away.

Interestingly, though, Hellberg himself has now broken his silence on the speculation in a crucial new development.

Hellberg's exploits on Teesside appear to be gaining wider attention, and a report from Sky Sports on Sunday evening named the hugely-popular 38-year-old as one of seven candidates on Crystal Palace's managerial radar alongside the likes of Sean Dyche, Frank Lampard, Vitor Matos of Swansea and leading contender Thomas Frank.

This update, of course, comes amid the impending sacking of Pierre Sage, which is yet to be formally finalised but seemingly represents an inevitability after the Frenchman left the Eagles rooted to the foot of the top-flight table with just two wins from his opening ten encounters heading into the latest two-week window.

Hellberg has publicly spoken of his dream to manage in the Premier League, but that's precisely where Middlesbrough could be headed on his watch and the Swedish boss has now reacted to links with the likely-soon-to-be-vacancy at Selhurst Park.

Having only been appointed at Boro just under twelve months ago, Hellberg discussed the reported interest with Swedish outlet Fotbollskanalen and reiterated his desire to focus on the job at hand with the Championship promotion hopefuls.

When asked how he feels about speculation linking his services with Palace, he explained: "Absolutely nothing, absolutely nothing.

"It's always nice to be on lists like that, if that's the case - it shows you've done something right. But I think it's mostly just talk, and for me it's all about doing a good job here.

"We actually have a chance to get promoted this year. We feel that there is a chance, but then it's clear that it will be difficult.

"We are one of five, six teams, maybe not the biggest favourites, but behind them I think we have a good chance.

"My focus is here and hopefully we can get up, that would have been fantastic of course."

It's abundantly clear that the entirety of Hellberg's focus is on the task at hand, which is getting Boro back to the promised land at the second time of asking - and they've got a real strong shot of doing just that on his watch.

For a head coach of Hellberg's quality - and we scarcely see that in the Championship, truth be told - it could be easy to be swayed by the opportunity to manage at the highest level.

However, Middlesbrough's own project is attractive, with Steve Gibson rewarding Hellberg with no shortage of financial backing to bolster Boro's squad, and they've only strengthened after sanctioning the big-money departure of Hayden Hackney to Everton over the summer.

Indeed, Boro have one of the Championship's strongest and deepest sides, and from defence to attack, stars such as Adilson Malanda, Aidan Morris and most notably the aforementioned Lankshear are all long-term assets with serious future resale value.

Hellberg has a gifted and high-ceiling squad at his disposal, and missing out on promotion with what he has would undoubtedly represent a failure.

That said, there's no better coach to finally return Boro to the big time and, having only appointed Hellberg after Rob Edwards' contentious and ultimately ill-fated decision to jump ship for Wolves merely months after arriving in the North East, they'll only be pleased and relieved in equal measure to see much more loyalty, dedication and focus from the sought-after Swede.

Make no mistake about it, Middlesbrough won't find a finer opportunity to end their promotion hoodoo as Hellberg continues to kick on with one of the strongest Boro squads we've seen in recent history.

Albeit expensively-assembled, there's no denying the vast quality across Boro's squad. Radek Vitek between the sticks and the likes of Callum Brittain, Neto Borges, Malanda and Ash Phillips provides a second-tier backline stiffer than most, even if Hellberg's emphasis on committing bodies forward and dominating possession in attacking thirds can see the Teessiders occasionally exploited in quick counter-transitions.

You won't find many stronger and more multi-functional midfielders at this level than Middlesbrough's, though, which features Sebastian Berhalter, Riley McGree and Morris - and even versatile French defender Malanda.

The attacking line is just as impressive, and when Hellberg can call upon the joint double-act brilliance of Amario Cozier-Duberry out wide and top-scorer Lankshear alongside Tommy Conway and Jeremy Sarmiento, it's no surprise that only two sides are averaging more goals a game.

Middlesbrough have serious, well-stocked quality in almost every area of the pitch and are a joy to watch at full throttle, and Hellberg himself only knows that all-too-well and won't be willing to part with this talented and exciting squad easily.

Boro, in truth, really should have held their nerve to seal a top-two finish last term, but the opportunity for promotion is even greater now with an improved squad and a route back to the top-flight by the time May rolls around may be exactly what's required to ensure Hellberg's long-term future remains at the Riverside.
