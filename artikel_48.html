DR Congo: Copper reserves put Africa at the centre of the global race for critical minerals

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Africa is emerging as a key player in the global race for copper, accounting for around 10 per cent of the world's estimated reserves. The Democratic Republic of Congo is at the centre of this growing interest, having mined 3.4 million metric tons of copper in 2025. Its vast mineral resources are attracting growing attention from countries and companies around the world, as demand for the metal rises due to its importance in energy infrastructure, electric vehicles and the global transition to cleaner energy.
