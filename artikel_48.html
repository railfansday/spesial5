5 European countries aim to send rejected asylum seekers to Rwanda

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A group of European countries announced Monday that they aim to set up a “return hub” in Rwanda where rejected asylum seekers could be sent from next year, a move that they hope will also deter more people from coming. The plan by Austria, Denmark, Germany, Greece and the Netherlands, which have spearheaded negotiations with non-EU countries, drew immediate criticism from human rights groups.
