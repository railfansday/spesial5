Report: It's done, Liverpool agree terms with African superstar

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

An exciting midfielder could soon be on his way to Liverpool, an agreement has been struck.

Liverpool have been crying out for a proper holding midfielder for years now, and under Andoni Iraola, that need is becoming increasingly difficult to ignore.

Ever since Fabinho left Anfield in 2023, the Reds have struggled to find a natural successor to the Brazilian.

They've tried different solutions. Alexis Mac Allister has played there, Ryan Gravenberch has been deployed in the role, and even Dominik Szoboszlai has been asked to operate deeper.

But none of them are natural defensive midfielders.

And that's a problem, especially when you consider how Iraola wants his Liverpool team to play.

The Spaniard demands intensity, aggression and relentless pressing. His system is built around winning possession high up the pitch and transitioning quickly into attacking situations.

The midfielder Liverpool need is obviousLiverpool also need a midfielder who is comfortable receiving possession under pressure, capable of progressing the ball and intelligent enough to dictate the tempo. Essentially, they need someone who can combine Fabinho's defensive awareness with the technical qualities of a modern deep-lying playmaker. The emergence of Trey Nyoni has certainly been encouraging. His composure and passing ability offer Liverpool something different, but expecting a teenager to shoulder that responsibility every week would be unfair. Ultimately, Liverpool need to invest in a specialist. If Iraola is going to implement his philosophy successfully and turn the Reds into genuine title contenders again, having the right player anchoring his midfield could make all the difference.
