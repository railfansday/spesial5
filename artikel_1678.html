Senegal reinstated as 2025 AFCON champions

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Senegal have been reinstated as the winners of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations after the Court of Arbitration for Sport's (CAS) ruling.

The decision overturns a previous decision to strip Senegal of the title they won against hosts Morocco during a chaotic showpiece match in January.

The Teranga Lions triumphed 1-0 in the final after extra time, with Pape Gueye's 94th-minute goal; however, the Confederation of African Football (CAF) decreed Senegal had breached competition regulations during the match.

Senegal left the field of play in second-half stoppage time in protest of a penalty awarded against them before then returning to the field – Brahim Diaz went on to miss that spot-kick, forcing extra time.

The decision to give Morocco the title came two months after the final, but Senegal's football federation (FSF) subsequently appealed to CAS, which backed the decision of referee Jean-Jacques Ndala.

Ndala opted to restart the game after Senegal returned to the pitch, and the match was played to its conclusion, meaning the result of the final stands, and they have been confirmed as the AFCON 2025 winners.

"The Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) has upheld the appeal by the Senegalese Football Federation (FSF) against the Confederation of African Football (CAF) and the Royal Moroccan Football Federation (FRMF) concerning the final of the Africa Cup of Nations Morocco 2025 (AFCON 2025)," a CAS statement read.

"The decision rendered by the CAF appeal board is set aside, and the decision by the CAF disciplinary board is reinstated.

"Consequently, the result obtained at the end of the AFCON 2025 final is confirmed (1-0 for Senegal)."

It means that Senegal have won their last two AFCON finals (2021 and 2025), after losing their first two (2002 and 2019).

That match also marked the first time Senegal had scored a goal in an AFCON final (fourth appearance), with Gueye's goal also the nation's 100th at the tournament.
