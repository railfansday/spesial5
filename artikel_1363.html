Tottenham enquire about Serie A top-scorer Varela as Liverpool send scouts

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Il Corriere dello Sport reports that Juventus aren’t the only club interested in Monza sensation Gustavo Varela, as Tottenham have enquired about the Portuguese striker, while Liverpool regularly send scouts to watch him in action.

Monza star Varela has already attracted interest from several clubs around Europe. The latest report from Corriere dello Sport claims that Premier League sides Tottenham, Brentford and Sunderland have already sounded out the striker’s availability, while Liverpool regularly send scouts to watch him.

Varela is a new Monza signing, joining the newly promoted Serie A side over the summer on a €2.5m deal from Benfica.

The 21-year-old has scored nine goals in ten Serie A games so far this season, resulting in the best scorer in Italy’s top flight.

Other European clubs, including Juventus, Napoli and Arsenal, have recently been linked with a January move for the Portuguese striker, who has recently received his first senior call-up with his national team.

His former club Benfica do not have an option to re-sign him, but only retain a 20% sell-on clause and can match any offer Monza receive in the coming years.

According to multilpe sources, Monza’s asking price for Varela is €40m.
