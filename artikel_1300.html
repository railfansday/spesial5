Serie A Liveblog: Napoli-Lazio, Inter-Como, Genoa-Milan, Fiorentina-Juventus and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from today’s five Serie A games, as Napoli in crisis host leaders Lazio, Milan visit Genoa, there’s Monza-Atalanta, a massive Inter vs. Como clash, then Juventus away to Fiorentina.

We begin at 11.30 GMT (12.30 CET) at the Stadio Maradona, where Napoli desperately need to turn things around, but are against a Lazio side flying high in top spot.

At 14.00 GMT (15.00 CET), Milan and Atalanta also try to shake off European setbacks with their trips to Genoa and Monza respectively.

All eyes on San Siro at 17.00 GMT (18.00 CET) for a huge Inter vs. Como Scudetto battle to fuel Cesc Fabregas ambition.

Finally, the bitter grudge match between Fiorentina and Juventus at 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET).

Milan: Maignan; Gila, De Winter, Diawara, Musah; Rabiot, Modric, Moreira; Pulisic, Cisse; Ramos

Nightmare for Alfonso Pedraza, who turns the ball into his own net just minutes after coming on. Good play from McTominay to win the ball back, before the Partneopei fired a cross over from the left-hand side. Both Hojlund and McTominay went for the ball but it was the Spaniard who got the last touch to knock the ball past Mandas.

Tavares did make way at half-time, with Alfonso Pedraza on in his place.

Second half is underway in Naples as Lazio resume play.

The visitors lead heading into the break, thanks to Frattesi’s quality finish. A few injury troubles for Lazio, though, who lost Sutalo to an apparent hamstring in the first half, with Nuno Tavares also now struggling too.

Napoli have shown a couple of promising glimpses and if it was not for Mandas, could have equalised just before the end of the first 45.

One additional minute to be played at the end of the first half.

First yellow card of the game goes to Noa Lang after cutting down a promising counter-attack.

DAZN Italia claim Noa Lang gestured towards the fans telling them to make more noise. They did not appreciate that.

Lang picks the ball up and gives it to Oliveira who puts the ball into a dangerous space. McTominay and Hojlund seem to almost get in the way of one another and the Dane’s knock-on is saved by Mandas on the goal line.

Napoli win a corner kick and send it into the box but it is dealt with well by the Lazio defence and the hosts must reset once again from the halfway line.

A bit of sustained pressure from the hosts now as they manage to work a few half chances into the Lazio box. The visitors do well to defend the opportunities but Napoli just showing a bit more intent with the ball.

Strong tackle gives Napoli the chance to break, they get the ball into the box but Lang sends a searching cross over Hojlund’s head and into empty space from the byline.

Napoli not committing the numbers into the box and it’s making it hard for them to fashion a genuine attempt on goal .

Napoli rushing things far too much when they attack, giving away possession cheaply too often and too easily. Lazio being given a lot of time on the ball whilst Napoli repeatedly turning it over.

Lazio get another good counter-attacking chance as Isaksen drives the ball forward from his own half but gets it mixed up and gives away possession.

Napoli then have a counter attack of their own before Hojlund finds himself with plenty of space and time, about 10 yards from goal, and cannot even get his attempt on target, fluffing it completely. Bit of a let off for Lazio.

Sutalo down injured after appearing to have pulled a muscle, he is receiving treatment as the Biancocelesti hurriedly prepare a substitution by the looks of things…

Lazio launch an electric counter attack this time, Noslin driving forward, before a lovely ball through for Frattesi who fires it past Milinkovic-Savic!

De Bruyne whips in the corner but Lazio get the ball clear fairly comfortably.

Napoli get a decent chance to counter-attack but they waste a lot of time in the middle of the park allowing Lazio to regroup. Lang manages to get the ball into a crossing position and fires a low one into the box but Tavares is back and deflects it out for a corner to the hosts.

There aren’t any Biancocelesti supporters in the arena, as a history of violence between the sets of fans has made this a high-risk fixture, read more here.

Noslin goes down in the box after some tricky footwork but he tripped himself up really over the ball rather than any real contact from the Napoli defence and there are not any appeals for a penalty.

Lazio, however, continue to get the ball in dangerous positions and Napoli are still waiting for their first real chance of the game.

Lazio are not letting up here. Hojlund feels he should have won a foul after losing a header to Sutalo before the visitors once again break into space. This time Tavares’ cross is deflected out for yet another corner.

The Portuguese wing-back swings another one in and Sutalo rises to head the ball at goal but Milinkovic-Savic gets across and down well to deny the visitors once again.

Isaksen looking really dangerous once again, with a quick turn of pace he surges into the box from the resulting throw and puts a dangerous ball into the six-yard box, however Milinkovic-Savic and the Napoli defence do just enough to keep the ball out of the net.

Tavares sends the corner over after the referee breaks up some jostling in the box. It’s a good delivery but it is headed clear and Lazio win a throw-in on the other side.
