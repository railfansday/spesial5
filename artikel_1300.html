Man United's January transfer budget will be less than £50m with Mauricio Pochettino expected to make only TWO signings - despite needing to bolster their attack and defence

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Published: 16:30 EST, 23 November 2026 | Updated: 17:03 EST, 23 November 2026

Mauricio Pochettino will have less than £50million to buy Manchester United out of trouble in the January transfer window.

Pochettino admitted that United are in a relegation battle after a painful 4-0 defeat to Liverpool in his first game in charge left the club second from bottom of the Premier League.

It’s understood the Argentine will have a limited budget well under £50million with United’s hands tied by the league’s new financial rules. Pochettino is only expected to make two new signings in January, even if one of them is a loan deal.

Sources have not ruled out the possibility of United gambling if there is a genuine threat of the club being relegated for the first time in 53 years, but they would still have to spend responsibly.

The defeat to Liverpool underlined the need for Pochettino to strengthen in central defence, with Brentford’s Nathan Collins emerging as a strong candidate ahead of Calvin Bassey, who is expected to sign a new contract with Fulham, and Everton’s Jarrad Branthwaite who is valued at over £70million.

United also want a left-back after failing to sign cover for Luke Shaw in the summer when they were linked with Barcelona’s Alejandro Balde and Rayan Ait-Nouri of Manchester City. Both those players could be potential targets again.

Pochettino would also like a new striker after Benjamin Sesko’s shin injury left him short of firepower until the New Year.

Monaco’s Folarin Balogun, who played under Pochettino for the US national team, Leeds striker Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Ivan Toney, the former Brentford centre-forward now playing for Al-Ahli, have all been linked with a move to Old Trafford.

However, Toney’s £500,000-a-week wages put him out of United’s reach, and Leeds are keen to extend Calvert-Lewin’s contract which is worth £180,000-a-week.

Meanwhile, United are appealing to the FA over Leny Yoro’s controversial red card against Liverpool.

Yoro was sent off in the eighth minute at Anfield for a challenge on Alexander Isak on the edge of the penalty box. The Frenchman was dismissed by referee Sam Barrott, and Dominik Szoboszlai scored Liverpool’s first goal from the free-kick.

However, United insist that Yoro did not deny Isak a clear goalscoring opportunity because goalkeeper Senne Lammens would have reached the ball first.

The club have notified the FA of their intention to appeal in the hope of having Yoro’s one-match ban overturned so he can play against Brentford at Old Trafford on Saturday.
