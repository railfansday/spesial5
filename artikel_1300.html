The United States Men's National Team are looking for a new manager after Mauricio Pochettino made the decision to leave his role and join Manchester United.

The Argentine was appointed USA manager in 2024. He has won 22 of his 36 matches in charge, leading his side to the last 16 of the 2026 World Cup on home soil. He will manage the Stars and Stripes one final time against Haiti on Tuesday evening.

USA are aiming high as they search for a replacement for Pochettino. According to GiveMeSport sources, Pep Guardiola is on their radar, and they want him to be their manager.

Guardiola, considered one of the greatest managers in football history, is currently a free agent after leaving Manchester City in the summer.

It remains to be seen whether USA are able to land Guardiola, but many think that he would be an incredible coup should he put pen-to-paper.

USMNT legends Tim Howard and Landon Donovan don't share that opinion, though. Speaking on 'Unfiltered Soccer with Landon Donovan & Tim Howard', the pair explained why they thought the USA shouldn't go for Guardiola. Howard said:

"Pep Guardiola. And by the way, I think the next time I see Pep Guardiola, he might smack me and I'm okay with that. He's absolutely not the right manager for this team, for the length of time that it's going to take to get through to the next World Cup.

"Here's why. Pep Guardiola has only ever succeeded with tier-one players. People might get upset with this. We have probably tier-three players globally. Probably. I might have been a tier-three player myself, whatever.

"But Pep Guardiola, Barcelona, Bayern Munich and Man City with more money than probably should have, has succeeded. We actually, me, you and every other football fan in the world, we actually don't know if Pep Guardiola is a good manager. Coaching level two or three players, we do not know. We do not know. And nobody out there can tell me they know."

Donovan agreed with his compatriot, adding: "Pep is a phenomenal manager, but this would not be right for the U.S. team. Would he be good for our 18-year-old players? I don't know. We have good, elite players, but he's been with the best.

"Pep is a genius who changed the way soccer is played in the world, but that is way different from having two training sessions to prepare for Haiti this week.

"As a national team coach, being a manager of people is way more important than getting the team to play a certain way. I don't think he fits for us."

Their comments haven't gone down well among USMNT fans.

One said: ‘I cannot believe what I just heard.' A second commented: ‘What the heck did Howard actually say that?’ while a third stated: 'This is horrendous from both of them.'

A fourth commented: 'The dumbest thing I’ve ever heard and another reason USMNT will always be trash. If Pep even picks up the phone you’d be lucky.'

'Bonkers take. He is too good for us,' a fifth said, while a sixth wrote: 'They are so scared a USMNT might some day better than they were they actively are moving against the idea of hiring this generation's best manager.'

At the time of writing, Guardiola is the third favourite to replace Pochettino. Nashville SC boss BJ Callaghan is the leading contender.
