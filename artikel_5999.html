Dozens of FBI and DOJ personnel are investigating Peltola's Alaska victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

‘Fox News @ Night’ anchor Trace Gallagher reports on Democrat Mary Peltola defeating GOP nominee Dan Sullivan in the Alaska U.S. Senate race, flipping the ninth GOP-held seat.

At least 50 FBI agents and more than a dozen lawyers from the U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) are working on an investigation into Sen.-elect Mary Peltola, D-Alaska, and her alleged involvement in recruiting a candidate with the same name as her opponent, Fox News has learned.

The Associated Press projected Peltola's victory on Tuesday, forecasting that she'd defeat incumbent Sen. Dan Sullivan, R-Alaska, with 50.1% of the electorate's votes.

However, senior DOJ officials have opened an investigation into Peltola's campaign and Democratic operatives to determine if any laws were broken in an effort to recruit Daniel J. Sullivan, a third candidate with almost the exact same name as the incumbent senator, to run.

Attorney General Todd Blanche "sees the Peltola fraud as so egregious, he is putting a lot of resources into the criminal investigation," an ally close to Blanche told Fox News Digital.

The DOJ has sent over 200 subpoenas as part of the probe, which sources told Fox News is focused on the Peltola campaign and the Democratic Senatorial Campaign Committee (DSCC).

Alaska Democratic Senate candidate Mary Peltola speaks at an election night event Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026, in Anchorage, Alaska.  (AP Photo/Lindsey Wasson)

At least five field offices are involved in the effort and nearly 100 letters have been sent out informing involved parties to preserve their records.

High-profile elections attorney Marc Elias, whose law firm represents the Peltola campaign, was one of the recipients of a preservation letter.

A federal grand jury is meeting in Alaska once a month to review evidence, Fox News has learned. The Justice Department declined to comment.

Sen. Dan Sullivan, R-Alaska, and former Rep. Mary Peltola, D-Alaska.  (Andrew Harnik/Getty Images; Nathaniel Wilder/Bloomberg via Getty Images)

Sen. Sullivan has previously slammed Peltola for allegedly scheming to get the alternate Dan Sullivan on the ballot.

"This was a secret project that they had. And then they worked on it for over a year. And then she consistently lied about it," Sen. Sullivan said.

SEN DAN SULLIVAN BRANDS PELTOLA’S ALLEGED BALLOT SCHEME A ‘SCANDAL AT THE HIGHEST ORDER’

Peltola, for her part, denies ever knowing anything about recruiting efforts to get Daniel J. Sullivan, aka "Decoy Dan," on the ballot.

"I just want to clear the air," Peltola told supporters during an October speech in Juneau.

PELTOLA INSISTS 'I KNEW NOTHING' ABOUT ALLEGED 'DECOY DAN' SCHEME AS SULLIVAN ALLEGES SHE IS 'STILL LYING'

"I knew nothing about any other candidates on the ballot except for me and the incumbent. That is all I have ever known about. I am not interested in tricking voters. I know I can beat Dan Sullivan," she said.

Mary Peltola launches her campaign as a Democratic candidate for Senate in Anchorage, Alaska, on Jan. 17, 2026. (Reuters)

A New York Times bombshell report released in October claimed that Peltola campaign staffers and senior aides at the DSCC jointly plotted to get the "Decoy Dan" on the ballot in an effort to confuse voters.

MARY PELTOLA'S CAMPAIGN, DEMS BACKED COVERT SCHEME TO FIELD SPOILER CANDIDATES IN ALASKA SENATE RACE: REPORT

The DSCC has also denied involvement in the alleged scheme.

Peltola, according to the AP, garnered 144,486 votes to Sen. Sullivan's 131,965 votes. A third candidate, Republican Gerald Heikes, received 6,105 votes. All other candidates, including Daniel J. Sullivan, received 5,561 combined votes.

Alaskan officials have yet to certify the state's election totals, having previously targeted Nov. 25 as the date to certify.

The DOJ had initially filed a civil lawsuit to try and delay certification, but the department now views that lawsuit as moot, as all votes have been counted.

The Peltola campaign, Sen. Sullivan, and the DSCC did not immediately respond to Fox News Digital's request for comment.
