Airbnb Rates Rose After California Required Upfront Pricing—Research Shows Guests Still Benefit

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

California required Airbnb to put mandatory fees into the price travelers see up front. A new study of San Francisco and San Diego listings finds rates rose 4.8% relative to cities outside the mandate. The researchers still conclude guests came out ahead. Being able to figure out what you’re buying has value. A cheaper-looking listing isn’t necessarily a cheaper stay. The rental with the lowest headline price isn’t necessarily the one you’d choose if you could compare them properly. Back in 2022, when Airbnb introduced a toggle to show the price including fees, I wrote that guests shouldn’t have to turn it on. Showing what a stay costs ought to be the default. (And why would you toggle it off except for research purposes?) Ultimately hiding charges punishes honest businesses. The property showing its full price looks more expensive than the competitor holding something back until later. You make the customer click through every listing and do the math just to find out what a property costs, which makes it costly to compare. I’ve even covered a rental on Booking.com with a $1,000 destination fee, on top of a resort fee and cleaning fee – listed in the fine print. The Fees Became Easier To See. The Stays Didn’t Get Cheaper. The November working paper compares entire-home listings in the two California cities with matched properties in Seattle and Chicago, using July 2023–July 2025 data. Its price measure includes cleaning charges, but excludes Airbnb’s service fee and taxes. Listings more dependent on cleaning fees as a portion of total price were more likely to exit the platform. Higher quality listings were more likely to remain, while new listings offered lower fees and prices than incumbents. That creates an opportunity for hosts who were already offering a good deal. If you build cleaning into your rate while the place next door breaks it out, your property can look overpriced. Put both totals on the screen and you may be able to charge a higher rate while still offering a lower total price than competitors. There can also be fewer rentals competing for the same guests if enough hosts leave. That’s a reason not to assume that forcing everyone to advertise total price will lower prices. Limits To The Model Holding the available properties fixed, the paper’s model finds higher prices more than wipe out the direct gains from disclosure. Changes in the rental pool flip the result positive. Hotels aren’t included. Excluding storm-affected comparison months also reduces the estimated rate increase to 2.9%. Someone returning to the same property at a higher price might be worse off. Someone who avoids a bad value rental, or finds a better one once the prices are comparable, can benefit even while spending more. If an Airbnb becomes too expensive, the alternative isn’t necessarily another vacation rental. It might be a hotel, a different neighborhood, or deciding not to take the trip. The model also leaves out time saved searching. Anyone who’s clicked through 20 listings just to assemble a usable set of prices knows that’s worth something, too. The estimate of how much better off people are takes more assumptions than observing what happened to rates. A Cleaning Cost Can Be Real Without Being A Surprise Cleaning a house after a two night stay can cost about as much as cleaning it after a week. There’s a reason longer stays can be cheaper per night. Showing the full price doesn’t stop a host from accounting for that. Say one property charges $150 a night plus $150 for cleaning. Another charges $210 a night with cleaning included. For two nights, the first costs $450 and the second $420, before other charges. For a week, the first is cheaper. A search where you’ve already entered your dates can show the right comparison without making you calculate it yourself. Airbnb finally made total price display standard worldwide in April 2025, generally before taxes. The study looks back at California moving earlier to help understand the effect of the change. I’ve rented homes on Martha’s Vineyard and when visiting family outside Australia’s Gold Coast. A whole house can make more sense than hotel rooms for a family or group. Airbnb also now even says that the cleaning fee actually buys the cleaning, limiting the chores hosts can require.
