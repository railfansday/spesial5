Giving Howard Lutnick His Due: China Is a Hard Problem

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Why was Xi blocking the exported chips the Trump administration was willing to give him?

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, Chinese President Xi Jinping, and an Nvidia AI chip

Why wasn’t China taking the chips? The ones President Trump was willing to sell them — for a piece of the action, naturally. The ones Xi Jinping didn’t need to steal.

The question presents, for me, another valuable lesson in humility — in what Rich Lowry and I discussed on our podcast this week as the imperative of checking your premises when real-world events pose unexpected complications.

Andrew C. McCarthy is a senior fellow at National Review Institute, an NR contributing editor, and author of Ball of Collusion: The Plot to Rig an Election and Destroy a Presidency.
