Ten products that upgrade office interiors

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The following products for offices published on our Showroom section allow for streamlined workplaces that don’t compromise on aesthetics.

These include desks, sit-stand tables as well as those with integrated outlets, modular seating systems and ergonomic task chairs.

Global designers and brands aimed to boost productivity through these items, whether that be by reducing desktop clutter, providing spontaneous opportunities for meetings and light work, or correcting posture to make long periods of sitting less taxing on the body.

Read on to find out more about this selection of workplace furniture.

TOT table by Isern Serra for Omelette Editions

A bisected tabletop with electrical outlets tucked neatly inside characterises this co-working desk by Spanish designer Isern Serra for furniture brand Omelette Editions

Supported by cylindrical legs, the TOT table provides a 12-socket power board, allowing wires to be neatly concealed to eliminate excess visual and physical tabletop clutter.

Muku task chair by Foster + Partners for Okamura

Japanese furniture brand Okamura has released a sleek office chair designed by eminent British studio Foster + Partners.

Muku comes in a sober trio of colourways – light grey, dark grey, red and black – all partly made from recycled materials: recycled aluminium for the frames, and recycled fishing nets for the mesh seat upholstery.

Pullman Modular seating system by NaughtOne

All three segments in the Pullman Modular system feature high backs, creating visual seclusion, sound-dampening and room division in open plan interiors.

Dynamism is given to steel and plywood in this task chair by designer Alina Schnizler for German brand Wilkhahn.

WiChair features a thick, strong spring beneath its seat, which gives bounce to the structure and encourages active sitting.

Indoor landscapes are created via this undulating modular sofa system created by designer Francesco Rota and Italian brand Lapalma.

HUB aims to create islands of activity in open plan spaces, creating space for working, meeting, conversing and waiting.

TNK500 Aurea task chair by Alegre Design for Actiu

Spanish brand Actiu worked with design firm Alegre Design on this task chair, which has a distinctively metallically-finished frame.

TNK500 Aurea supports healthy posture when working, and is made from almost 60 per cent recycled materials.

Konzert office furniture system by Paolo Dell’Elce for Knoll

Complete private office fit-outs are made cohesive with US furniture brand Knoll and Italian designer Paolo Dell’Elce’s new furnishing system.

Konzert comprises configurable wall panels, tables, seating and storage that come in various curated colourways and finishes.

Seated and standing work and meetings alike are made possible by this series of tables by office furniture brand Actiu.

Menssula sit-stand work tables come in various sizes and formats, all containing an electrically-powered height raising mechanism.

Norwegian designers Andreas Engesvik and Jannicke Grung have created a compact and lighthearted task chair for Flokk-owned furniture brand HÅG

HÅG Minu comes in seven designer-curated curated colourways and aims to bring the comfort and casualness of residential furniture to workplaces.

British furniture brand Boss Design aimed to bring adaptability and personalisation to this range of office booths.

Eden pods can be fitted out with lounge furniture, tactile cladding and finishes, and planters on their roofs to bring the health and wellbeing of plants into workplaces.
