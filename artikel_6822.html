Novelty Tights Are the Easiest Way to Make Your Fall Outfits More Interesting

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

From polka dots to leopard print, these outfit ideas make simple fall looks feel fresh.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

If you’re wondering how to get a little more creative with your outfits as the weather cools, look no further than novelty tights. From shimmery to polka dot, leopard print to lace, patterned tights instantly elevate even the simplest look, turning a basic skirt or dress into something with real personality.

The best part is how little effort it takes. A single pair can transform pieces you already own, giving them new life with no shopping spree required. Pair bold prints with understated layers, like a neutral sweater dress or a tailored blazer, so the tights take center stage. Or instead, give your look a more-is-more persona with extra sparkle or shimmer.

If you prefer to ease into the trend, sheer and lace styles offer a subtler way in, adding texture and a hint of drama without overwhelming an outfit. Whether you’re heading to the office, a dinner out, or a casual weekend errand, there’s a pair that fits the mood.

Ahead, we’ve rounded up some inspiration for how to style novelty tights this fall.

This holiday season, give your trusty LBD a fresh perspective with a pair of polka dot tights. We love a sheer burgundy style, which adds a rich pop of color that stands out against black without overpowering it.
