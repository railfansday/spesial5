This time of year, my shopping cart is primarily filled with gifts for others, but when I saw that lululemon was starting its Black Friday sale over a week early, I had to treat myself. What can I say? It’s an amazing time to stock up on wardrobe staples like this Evolve Half-Zip Hoodie while it’s only $84. Lululemon doesn’t host a lot of official sales, so I’m excited to see rare savings on several bestselling items. Merry early Christmas to me!

Of course, this is also a fantastic opportunity to buy gifts for your fellow Lululemon-loving friends and family. I love to get the Everyday Belt Bag for my daughter who uses it as a crossbody, and they are an impossibly low $19 (depending on the color). I’ve already got one sitting in my cart. I’ve rounded up the most notable deals below (including plenty of winter-friendly workout gear), which are slated to last through Cyber Monday. I wouldn’t wait, though—popular pieces like the Belt Bag tend to sell fast.
