Billy Ray Cyrus Reacts to Daughter Miley and Maxx Morando's Intimate Wedding

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Billy Ray Cyrus is one proud dad following his daughter Miley Cyrus and her husband Maxx Morando‘s intimate Palm Springs wedding.

“My little girl married the love of her life! I can’t describe how beautiful it felt to have all my family together in one room, united by love,” the “Achy Breaky Heart” musician, 65, wrote to Miley via a Monday, November 23, Instagram post. “@miley words can’t begin to express how proud you make me every day… I’m the proudest dad alive!!”

Billy Ray and his girlfriend, Elizabeth Hurley — who have recently sparked their own engagement rumors — were in attendance when Miley, 34, and Morando, 28, tied the knot at the late Frank Sinatra‘s former Twin Palms estate in California on Saturday, November 21.

The singer paid tribute to his new son-in-law as well on Monday, writing to Morando, “@mmaxxm I’m so honored to welcome you to the family! The two of you kids are a match made in heaven.”

Miley has a double milestone to celebrate this week, as she also turned 34 on Monday, a mere two days after she exchanged vows with Morando. (The “Let’s Get Married” singer was previously wed to Liam Hemsworth from 2018 to 2020.)

“Happy, happy birthday Miley – I can’t wait to keep celebrating you both today and forever,” Billy Ray added.

Earlier, he told TMZ that the best part of attending Miley’s wedding was “knowing that the love is going to last forever.”

Asked whether he walked his daughter down the aisle, Billy Ray simply said, “It was a beautiful ceremony.”

Us previously reported that Miley was walked down the aisle by her mother, Tish Cyrus, who attended the nuptials with her husband, Dominic Purcell. (Miley’s sisters, Noah Cyrus and Brandi Cyrus, were both in attendance, as was Liz’s son Damian Hurley.)

“It was a really intimate wedding, and there weren’t any big celebrity guests outside of her family,” a source exclusively told Us, adding, “It wasn’t a typical Hollywood or traditional wedding, but was everything Miley wanted.”

The Hannah Montana actress broke her silence on Sunday, November 23, by sharing a carousel of photos from her special day. She subsequently thanked her team for pulling off the glamorous ceremony while acknowledging that her wedding coincided with another major milestone.

“Waking up on my 34th birthday feeling so grateful,” she wrote via Instagram on Monday. “Thank you for all the wishes & to everyone who made this weekend a memory we’ll cherish for a lifetime and beyond.”

Miley and Morando started seeing each other after being set up on a blind date in 2021. In addition to their romance, the former Regrettes drummer was credited as a producer on Miley’s 2023 album Endless Summer Vacation.

Us confirmed in December 2025 that Cyrus and Morando were engaged when she was spotted at the Avatar: Fire and Ash premiere in Los Angeles wearing a cushion cut diamond ring set in a 14K yellow gold band, designed by Jacquie Aiche.
