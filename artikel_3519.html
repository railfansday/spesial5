Rumer Willis Shares Rare Photo With Dad Bruce Amid His Dementia Battle

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Rumer Willis is grateful for the little things this Thanksgiving, including time with father Bruce Willis.

“Thankful ❤️,” Rumer, 38, captioned a Friday, November 27, Instagram carousel, leading with a photo of herself with Bruce, 71.

In the snap, Rumer held her hand up to Bruce’s cheek as the retired action star looked into her eyes.

Rumer is the eldest daughter of Bruce and his ex-wife, Demi Moore, who also share daughters Scout, 35, and Tallulah, 32. (Bruce and wife Emma Heming Willis are parents of daughters Mabel, 14, and Evelyn, 12.)

In addition to the father-daughter image, Rumer also shared pics of her daughter Louetta, 3, as well sister Tallulah smiling with husband Justin Acee. (Rumer shares her only child with ex Derek Richard Thomas.)

“The first picture speaks a thousand words ❤️❤️❤️❤️,” one social media follower wrote in the comments section, with another adding, “Your Dad and you look fabulous! Thank you for sharing! I am a big fan of his work.”

The Die Hard star’s family announced in 2022 that Bruce was diagnosed with aphasia, which later progressed to frontotemporal dementia. Amid his health battle, Heming Willis, 50, moved her husband into a separate residence with a full-time care team.

“It was one of the hardest decisions that I’ve had to make so far,” Heming Willis said in Emma and Bruce Willis: The Unexpected Journey – A Diane Sawyer Special, which aired in August 2025. “I knew, first and foremost, Bruce would want that for our daughters. You know, he would want them to be in a home that was more tailored to their needs, not his needs.”

Heming Willis and Bruce’s daughters now all frequently visit the now-retired actor, occasionally sharing glimpses via social media. After Tallulah posted footage from a recent moment with Bruce earlier this month, she deleted the image and issued a public apology.

“Hi, I posted a photo of my dad yesterday because we were having a BEAUTIFUL visit,” Tallulah wrote via Instagram on Sunday, November 22. “I had someone take a super sweet photo of a shared moment between us watching a video of Frankie Valli singing.”

She continued at the time, “I was very excited by the sweetness of the moment that I didn’t think farther ahead that this was possibly too intimate a photo to show. I can acknowledge that. [I] vow to do better. I know you all love him. I love him, and he’s still always the absolute hero we know him to be.”
