'GTA 6' Pre-orders Come With An Important Asterisk

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A PSA for anyone who pre-ordered the standard or Ultimate Edition of GTA 6

Millions of Grand Theft Auto 6 fans are prepping for Nov. 19 by clearing their calendars, taking paid time off, and of course, pre-ordering the game. Normally, pre-ordering a game could be risky — what if it's a bad experience? Rockstar Games' sterling track record combined with GTA 6's solid pre-order bonuses have nonetheless tempted many gamers to throw caution to the wind. Yet as launch day approaches, now is a good time to start reading the fine print attached to those pre-order bonuses.

So, yes, Nov. 19 is important — but so is the date approximately 30 days from when you sign up for the free month. It may not be a concern now, but Rockstar also hasn't revealed the full extent of what's next for GTA Online or what that looks like alongside GTA 6. We know that the multiplayer version of GTA is being developed in tandem with leading modders who helped roleplaying explode in popularity on platforms like Twitch. The quasi-MMO also launched a mode in late 2025 that equips fans to create missions with their own objectives and NPCs.

Once Rockstar reveals the future of the platform and fans finish the base game, a bunch of people might suddenly remember they have the option of a free GTA Plus month — the pre-order offer is good until March 31, 2027.
