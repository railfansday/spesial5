Tanggal: 2026-11-20

As we've reported, the former couple only communicates through a parenting app as part of their restraining order ... meaning their contact is already limited.

Those close to Jax say he's matured significantly over the past year ... becoming more guarded about his relationship and private life.

Jax credited Lori for standing by him throughout the ordeal ... and said he wants to be around to watch Cruz grow up.
