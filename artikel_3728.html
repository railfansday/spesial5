Clicky Islands came out on the heels of a certain game from Rockstar, too

May I remind you, GTA 6 wasn't the only game to come out in the last two weeks. Clicky Islands, a fun throwback island builder published by Palworld company Pocketpair, arrived earlier in the month, and according to the publisher's estimates, it's doing very well indeed.

On Twitter, John 'Bucky' Buckley, the head of publishing and communications at Pocketpair, takes a second to highlight just how successful the game's been. "Clicky Islands launched ten days ago, and it's smashed every target!" he writes. "Really proud to be supporting this game and the two Polish wizards who cooked it up!"

It is a delightful little gem that merges citybuilding and idle game ideas. Essentially, you land on a remote island, and start making it more habitable for your people, but how you do that is distinctly streamlined – you mostly just click around and let things be built, gradually accruing more resources.

As Pocketpair's monster-collecting survival adventure continues to grow, more opportunities for games like this can and will emerge, and it's good to see that money and energy being put to good use. If Clicky Islands is anything to go by, I'd keep an eye on what else the publisher may have in store.

Meanwhile, Buckley's been teasing that we'll hear more about Palfarm and the coveted Palworld 1.1 soon. No rest for the wicked – unless Rockstar's about to put something out, that is.
