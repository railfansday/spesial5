Some Players Are Crashing Out About GTA 6 Only Being 30 FPS: ‘Starfield Got Assaulted For Being Locked To 30 FPS’

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S ushered in the era of 60 fps gaming on consoles. Players have come to expect, at the very least, a performance mode that scales the rest of the visuals down in order to accommodate a higher image refresh rate and smoother-looking animations. But Grand Theft Auto 6, the sequel some have waited over a decade for, will be locked at 30 fps across all platforms, including PS5 Pro. How are some people taking this? Not well.

Rockstar Games confirmed the news yesterday, though it shouldn’t have necessarily come as a big surprise. Studio development head Rob Nelson was reportedly telling content creators as much back during the August extended gameplay reveal. GTA 6 was running at 30 fps at the time, and while he wouldn’t rule out a 60 fps mode down the line, nothing was being promised for launch.

So what’s the big deal? If you’re tuning in to gaming for the first time in a while just for GTA 6, the framerate affects how crisp and natural a game looks in motion. 30 fps has more of a movie quality to it since there are fewer frames showing each precise change in the picture. 60 fps is often described as “buttery smooth” because all of the animations flow together more seamlessly. Some people still struggle to tell the difference between the two modes. For others, the difference is immediately jarring.

There’s been a lot of people debating the impact of 30 fps in the wake of the news. It’s worth pointing out that no modern Rockstar game has run at 60 fps at launch. But the last game it released was Red Dead Redemption 2 in 2018, before the modern era of 60 fps gaming on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. Updated ports of its back catalog to those platforms have supported higher frame rates as a result. Others have also pointed out how quickly gamers have been to savage games from other developers that didn’t support 60 fps.

“No offense towards GTA 6 it will be good, but Starfield got assaulted for being locked to 30 fps and runs at 60 fps on Xbox and PS5 now, are we gonna have the same energy for GTA 6 being locked to 30 fps?” one person wrote on X (via IGN). “We do lock it at 30, because we want that fidelity, we want all that stuff,” Starfield director Todd Howard said prior to the game’s release. “We donât want to sacrifice any of it.â It did not go over well. Driving, shooting, and minigames are all a big part of that, but not necessarily in the same way that perfectly timed parries and dodges matter in a FromSoftware boss fight.
