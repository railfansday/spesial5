EA really doesn't want you to delete Madden NFL 27 to make room for GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

"Don't let 210GB get between you and your league."

Assuming you didn't just buy a PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X/S to play Grand Theft Auto 6, your console's limited storage space is likely already strained, meaning tough choices may have to be made to make room for Rockstar's chonky 212GB crime game.

In a move I don't remember seeing before, EA is asking players to spare Madden NFL 27 from the purge to free up console storage space. As spotted by Push Square, a desperate in-game message pops up when you launch the American Football game, pleading with you not to delete it.

The message highlights 17th November as the date of a Madden NFL 27 update, though its styling, wording and choice of background seem to hint at GTA 6.

"Don't delete!" it reads, as shared by Zirksee on X. "[Encrypted Signal] The target is locked. The crew is assembling for a high-stakes run. Await the signal from our insider on 11/17… do not leak this transmission."

EA's language and intention, however, are much clearer in this post on X, which specifically references GTA 6's large file size in a cheeky way, seemingly promoting the same update.

"Don't let 210GB get between you and your league," the post reads, clearly quoting GTA 6's file size.

The update in question is fairly notable, adding a Madden 97 throwback with era-appropriate players, playbooks and what looks to be 16-bit visuals. Though it's obviously not related to GTA 6 in any way, it's easy to see why EA would fear a significant drop in players once Rockstar's game arrives, something other publishers are undoubtedly worried about.

As part of a recent restructuring, EA laid off 400 employees, closed down BioWare, cancelled unannounced projects and revealed a refocusing on its core franchises and biggest monkey-makers. That includes EA Sports, of course, where a team is currently developing a game codenamed Project Gridiron, described as "a social-first open-world experience for our American Football team".
