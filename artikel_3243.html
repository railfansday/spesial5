Watch Wu-Tang Clan Perform ‘Triumph,’ ‘Bring Da Ruckus’ at NFL Thanksgiving Halftime Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Wu-Tang Clan showcased a slew of classic hits during the NFL Thanksgiving Day Game Halftime Show.

The annual match-up pitted the Chicago Bears against the Detroit Lions at at Ford Field in Detroit, with the hip-hop group taking the field midway through. The game, which aired on CBS, also saw Broadway singer Antonio Cipriano performing the National Anthem.

The group, joined by Red Hot Chili Peppers’ Chad Smith on drums, kicked it off with “Triumph” from their 1997 album Wu-Tang Forever. The rappers also showcased a medley that included “C.R.E.A.M,” “Da Mystery of Chessboxin,” and “Bring Da Ruckus.” Notably, Ol’ Dirty Bastard’s son Young Dirty Bastard performed his father’s lines off 1995 single “Shimmy Shimmy Ya.”

Wu-Tang Clan were recently inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame during a ceremony in Los Angeles. During the ceremony, all living members of the Staten Island group hit the stage to perform a medley of some of their biggest hits, including “C.R.E.A.M.” and “Wu-Tang Clan Ain’t Nuthing ta F’ Wit.” They got a spirited introduction from Chris Rock. Earlier this year, the group celebrated the Knicks’ championship victory with a performance on The Tonight Show. The musicians showcased a medley of performed a medley of two songs, “Bring Da Ruckus” and “C.R.E.A.M.,” on the late-night show, with the help of the NBA team themselves.

Wu-Tang Clan played a small (but apocryphally pivotal) role in the team’s championship run. During Game Four, the Wu-Tang Clan provided the halftime entertainment, taking the stage when the Knicks were down a seemingly insurmountable 29 points. Had the Knicks lost, the Spurs would’ve tied the series at two games apiece. Despite the heavy deficit, Method Man ended the performance by declaring, “Knicks in five.” The team then rallied to win Game Four 107 – 106, then dispatched the Spurs in Game Five a couple of days later.
