Essent Group Ltd. (ESNT -7.57%) reported third-quarter results characterized by stable mortgage insurance performance and continued expansion of its reinsurance operations. Management reported that elevated persistency, driven by low-rate mortgage portfolios, continues to support the insurance in-force base while purchase volumes remain constrained by housing affordability challenges. The company reported a significant increase in reinsurance premiums as it expands into property and casualty lines, seeking to diversify earnings and optimize capital through synergy credits. Management stated it remains focused on a balanced capital strategy, as evidenced by the authorization of a new $500 million share repurchase program and the maintenance of a quarterly dividend.
