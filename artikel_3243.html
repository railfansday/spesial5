Is Coca-Cola (KO) the Best Stock to Own During a Market Sell-Off?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The world’s top beverage maker is a reliable safe-haven stock.

10 stocks we like better than Coca-Cola ›

Coca-Cola (KO +1.32%), the world's largest beverage maker, is usually a reliable long-term investment. But is it the best blue chip stock to own during a market sell-off?

Coca-Cola regularly updates its flagship sodas with new flavors, smaller serving sizes, and healthier versions to reach new customers. To offset declining soda consumption, it's launched and acquired a wide range of non-carbonated drinks over the past several decades.

Coca-Cola maintains high gross and operating margins by only selling its concentrates and syrups to its bottling partners, which produce and distribute the finished beverages.

That's how Coca-Cola generates plenty of cash to cover its dividend, which it has raised annually for 64 consecutive years. Maintaining that streak for more than 50 years makes it a Dividend King. Its forward dividend yield of 2.2% might seem unimpressive amid rising interest rates, but its low payout ratio of 62% leaves plenty of room for future hikes.

Coca-Cola isn't an exciting investment, but its scale, diversification, wide moat, and robust cash flow growth make it an easy place to park your cash for a few decades. Over the past 40 years, it delivered a total return of 10,838% with reinvested dividends -- even as the U.S. economy endured four recessions and the S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.45%) experienced five bear markets. Therefore, Coca-Cola should remain a safe stock to own during the next market downturn.
