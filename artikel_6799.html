All of Princess Kate’s Remembrance Sunday Looks Over the Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Princess of Wales first attended the event in 2011

Princess Catherine has joined the royal family for the National Service of Remembrance every year since 2011, the same year that she got married to Prince William. The somber annual ceremony, held on Remembrance Sunday at the Cenotaph, a war memorial in London, honors service members who have lost their lives at war. It takes place on the second Sunday of November, and it involves a church service, a wreath-laying ceremony, and a formal procession, all of which are attended by various members of the British royal family.

When it comes to her royal style, the Princess of Wales has long embraced a more sustainable approach, frequently rewearing dresses, jewelry, and accessories. Over the years, that style philosophy has been on full display at Remembrance Sunday, where Kate has made a tradition of recycling looks and adding sentimental touches through meaningful brooches and heirloom jewelry.

One of her signature accessories is the red poppy pin, a symbol that has come to be widely associated with the holiday. Kate is often seen wearing three poppies at once, and while the royal family has never confirmed the significance behind the number, one theory suggests it may be a personal nod to her great-grandmother’s three brothers, all of whom lost their lives during World War I.

Throughout the years, Kate has also worn pieces that once belonged to late royal family members like Queen Elizabeth II and Princess Diana, which she has styled with outfits from Catherine Walker, Alexander McQueen, Temperley London, Diane von Furstenberg, and more.

Ahead, take a look back at Princess Kate’s Remembrance Sunday style through the years, from her first appearance in 2011 to now. And be sure to check back for more updates once the Princess of Wales steps out for the 2026 ceremony, too.

For her first appearance with the royal family at the Cenotaph memorial, Kate wore a black Diane von Furstenberg coat adorned with a double poppy pin along with a Jane Corbett hat and Kiki McDonough earrings.

At the 2012 Remembrance Sunday ceremony, the then-Duchess of Cambridge rewore her Diane von Furstenberg coat from the previous year, this time paired with a Philip Treacy hat, Annoushka earrings, and a single sparkling poppy brooch.

Opting for a different outerwear look for her third appearance, Kate wore a single-breasted Temperley London coat, which she accessorized with a Sylvia Fletcher for Lock & Co. hat and Kiki McDonough hoop earrings with Annoushka pearl drops.

In 2014, Kate wore a black Alexander McQueen jacket over a black ruffled BCBG dress. She rewore the Jane Corbett hat she donned in 2011, and she added a floral brooch, a Mappin & Webb diamond necklace, and Princess Diana’s sapphire earrings.

Kate stepped out in another Alexander McQueen coat for the 2015 Remembrance Day service, which featured velvet trim along the collar, sleeves, and waist. The then-duchess topped it off with a Lock & Co. hat adorned with a sculptural butterfly and black veil, the Kiki McDonough hoop earrings and Annoushka pearl drops she’d worn in 2013, and a triple-poppy pin on her lapel.

In another rewear, Kate donned the same Diane von Furstenberg coat she wore to her first and second Remembrance Sunday services in 2011 and 2012. This time, she styled it with a John Boyd hat, diamond-and-pearl earrings, and three poppies on her lapel.

Kate switched up her look for the 2017 National Service of Remembrance, opting for a double-breasted, collarless Dolce & Gabbana coat with oversized gold buttons, statement Oscar de la Renta earrings in gold, and a Philip Treacy hat she wore in 2006, during her early days of dating Prince William. She also debuted a new hair look, pinning it up to create the illusion of a short bob.

The 2018 Remembrance Day Service marked a significant milestone: the 100th anniversary of the end of World War I. For the occasion, Kate wore a custom Alexander McQueen jacket with military-inspired details, including a high white collar, red shoulder embellishments, and crest-style buttons. She completed the look with a Lock & Co. hat, a triple red poppy pin, and a brooch honoring the women who served during World War I.

Opting for another military-inspired jacket, Kate wore a Catherine Walker coat and a Philip Treacy hat featuring a black veil. Forgoing her usual poppy pin, she instead wore a special pin known as the “Codebreaker’s Brooch” in honor of her grandmother who worked at Bletchley Park during World War II.

At the socially distanced 2020 Remembrance Day ceremony, held amid the COVID-19 pandemic, Kate once again opted for a military-inspired look. She wore a Catherine Walker jacket with fringe detailing at the shoulders, complete with a Philip Treacy fascinator, pearl drop earrings, and her signature triple-poppy pin.

Kate brought back her Alexander McQueen coat from the 2018 service for the 2021 Remembrance Day ceremony. She paired the repeat look with Princess Diana’s pearl earrings, a wide-brimmed Lock & Co. hat, and her signature triple-poppy pin, along with a crystal flower brooch from the Royal British Legion.

For her first Remembrance Day Service following Queen Elizabeth II’s passing, the Princess of Wales wore a black Catherine Walker coat, Princess Diana’s pearl-and-diamond earrings, and a wide-brimmed Philip Treacy hat. She completed the look with her signature trio of poppies, paired with an Art Deco-inspired diamond brooch.

The Princess of Wales opted for multiple rewears at the 2023 Remembrance Sunday service. She repeated the custom Catherine Walker coat she first wore in 2019 and the Philip Treacy hat she previously wore at the 2012 and 2017 services. Then, for some added sparkle, she wore Queen Elizabeth’s diamond-and-pearl earrings, and her signature three-poppy pin.

Kate didn’t make many public appearances in 2024 due to her cancer diagnosis, but she still attended the Remembrance Sunday service that year. She wore a Catherine Walker coat dress, featuring a velvet bow collar, and she accessorized with a Juliette Botterill hat, Queen Elizabeth’s earrings, and her triple-poppy pin.

Kate’s look at the 2025 Remembrance Sunday service featured a rewear with a new twist. She opted for the same Catherine Walker coat from 2022, but this time, she styled it with a lace navy-blue pie-crust collar blouse draping out of the top. She accessorized with a sculptural Lock & Co. fascinator, Princess Diana’s pearl-drop earrings, a single poppy brooch, and a Royal Air Force brooch.
