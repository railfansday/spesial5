All the Stars at Tiffany &amp; Co.’s Glistening Holiday Kickoff

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

From Amanda Seyfried to Mikey Madison to Jordyn Woods-Towns

The holiday season is almost upon us, and if there’s one label that knows how to do the festive months right, it’s Tiffany & Co. From its shimmering diamonds to its signature blue boxes, the jewelry house symbolizes all the excitement, elegance, and indulgence of winter’s busiest days. And on Tuesday night, its newest ambassador, Amanda Seyfried, kicked things off with a glistening snowflake-covered event at the Landmark, the house’s Fifth Avenue flagship store.

Seyfried, who christened the season by placing a Tiffany ornament on the house’s blue box tree and illuminating the Bird on a Rock installation, went full New Year’s Eve glamour for the occasion—wearing a black velvet maxi dress with a one-shoulder silhouette, side cutout, and high leg slit. She paired the piece with red lips and links of diamonds around her neck (Tiffany’s, of course). To match, she added winged diamond earrings, a watch, and a small link ring from the label.

The actor was joined on the blue carpet by Mikey Madison, who wore a black satin two-piece with diamonds from Tiffany’s Bird on a Rock collection, and Jordyn Woods-Towns, dressed in a custom off-the-shoulder Markarian gown with a set of the house’s Sixteen Stone pieces. Louisa Jacobson went for something more colorful, pairing a white peplum jacket with a skirt in Christmas-tree green. She added a Bird on a Rock brooch to her top, and slipped her feet into brown heels.
