ELLE’s Guide to the Best Black Friday Fashion Sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Save on designer bags, holiday party looks, jewelry gifts, and cold-weather staples.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

It’s a great time to love fashion. Hundreds of Black Friday sales are live throughout the holiday weekend—but with so many places to shop, it can be hard to know where to start (and when to stop). To help you maximize your budget, we’ve outlined where ELLE editors are shopping this year. From major department stores to designer labels, these are the 28 best sites to browse for Black Friday–Cyber Monday fashion deals, organized alphabetically for your convenience.

Aligne is one of ELLE editors’ go-to fashion labels, so we’ll be jumping on sweaters, dresses, and work staples included in its Winter Sale. We’re equally impressed by the Aligne deals we’re seeing at Nordstrom.

Alo is offering a rare sitewide discount, excluding cashmere and Atelier items. Take 30 percent off the brand’s popular workout leggings, sports bras, and jackets—but hurry, many items will sell out before Cyber Monday.

Amazon’s massive Black Friday sale includes thousands of fashion items, including modern cult classics like Ugg boots and Ray-Ban sunglasses. The retailer typically matches prices with department stores and DTC websites, so you can do all your sale shopping in one fell swoop.

Through December 2, Banana Republic shoppers can take 40 percent off their entire purchase. Even cashmere is included in this year’s promotion.

Coach has markdowns on all kinds of handbags to gift—and boots, loafers, and leather jackets worth keeping for yourself. Beyond the label’s website, expect Nordstrom, Revolve, and Amazon to have Coach deals, too.

The celebrity-loved label Cuyana is offering 30 percent off almost all leather goods. Its high-quality leather totes make perfect everyday work bags—and the ultimate holiday gifts for busy women in your life.

ELLE readers can use the promo code ELLE20 to score 20 percent off all DeMellier bags through November 30. Before you shop, check out our guide to celebrity-worn styles included in the sale.

Dôen’s Giving Thanks sale includes dozens of deals on dresses and tops—but we’re also seeing discounts on the brand at Net-a-Porter, Nordstrom, and Saks Fifth Avenue.

Éterne makes some of ELLE editors’ favorite basics, so it’s safe to say we’ll be shopping for tees, tanks, and other staples while they’re an impressive 70 percent off. With discounts this deep, we don’t expect stock to last long.

The beloved denim label Frame is offering 30 percent off dozens of jeans, jackets, sweaters, and coats—plus an extra 50 percent off existing markdowns. We’ve detailed celebrity-worn items included in the brand’s sale.

Revolve’s sister site Fwrd has thousands of Black Friday deals, including some of creative director Kendall Jenner’s picks. Shop wisely, and you can save hundreds, if not thousands, on designer finds from The Row, Chloé, and more.

There’s no time like the present for J.Crew fans. The beloved brand is offering 40 percent off almost everything sitewide. Typically, cashmere is excluded from the brand’s sales—but right now, it’s 30 percent off.

Leset is a staple in editors’ wardrobes, so we’ll be taking advantage of the label’s rare sitewide sale. All tees and matching sets are 25 percent off, including the Margo tee loved by Jennifer Lawrence, Katie Holmes, and numerous ELLE staffers.

Lululemon rarely hosts sales, so the brand’s Black Friday deals will sell out fast (especially on neutral colors like black). Kick-start your fitness resolutions with deals on leggings, sports bras, sneakers, and more.

Madewell fans should go straight to the source this Black Friday. The label is offering 40 percent off almost everything on its site using the code LETSGO—and that includes an extra 40 percent off existing markdowns.

The editor-loved, celebrity-approved footwear brand Margaux is hosting its only sitewide sale of the year. We’ll be shopping boots and flats while they’re 20 percent off through November 30.

Those looking for jewelry gifts should take advantage of Mejuri’s sitewide Black Friday sale, which includes natural and lab-grown white sapphires and diamonds for up to 30 percent off. Items return to full price on November 30.
