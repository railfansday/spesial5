The Best Black Nail Polishes for a Deep, Moody Manicure

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

From glossy jet black to shimmering charcoal, these shades deliver instant drama.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Black nail polish has never really been a trend—it’s a constant. It shows up on runways in Paris, backstage at rock shows, and at the desk of the most polished person in the office. It’s equal parts rebellion and refinement, and the right shade can make a manicure look like a chic, sophisticated statement or a mood.

The catch is that black polish is not one color. If you hold three bottles side by side, you’ll notice: one is a flat, inky void, another leans toward blue or red-black, and a third glitters with a faint shimmer. So you see, undertones matter. And formula changes everything. While a glossy cream gives you high-shine drama, a matte finish reads modern and architectural.

So whether you want the classic jet black that goes with everything, a vampy near-black with a hint of purple or blue, or a metallic charcoal for people who want a little sparkle with their shadow, there’s a perfect shade out there.

Ahead, we break down the best black polishes, from the one-coat wonders to the formulas that last through a full week without chipping.
