Jan Ravnik Summoned to “Closed-Door Talks” With “Furious” ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Producers

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Here’s what happened after Jan was censored on the live DWTS finale….

Jan Ravnik went slightly off-script during Tuesday night’s Dancing With the Stars finale, by which I mean he gushed “she fucking killed it” about Tatyana Ali. On live TV. On Disney+.

Mickey Mouse has been in a state of shock ever since, but the moment was honestly very sweet and endearing…though, apparently Dancing With the Stars producers didn’t think so. The Sun reports that Jan was pulled into “closed door talks” after swearing, and everyone was “furious.”

A source tells the outlet that “There was a gasp from the makeup and costuming teams when Jan let out the bleep and then there was a loud groan from the production room with all the producers. They seemed pretty furious Jan did that when he knows better. He did get a talking to after, like ‘hey don’t do that again’ but I don’t think it will impact his job long-term.”

Fortunately, the incident will not impact Jan’s role on the show and he’s expected to return next season. As for Jan’s reaction, he told Extra that “It was just a perfect way to describe the last dance,” while Tatyana said “I wasn’t that shocked…he was just being real, it’s live TV though.”
