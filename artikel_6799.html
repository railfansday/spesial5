What to Pack for a Cute and Cozy Winter Getaway

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Target's closet staples elevate your cold-weather look without overstuffing your suitcase.

A winter getaway can feel magical, whether it's a weekend in a mountain cabin with friends, a bucket-list trip to the holiday markets of Europe, or a simple, restorative vacation week with family. Packing for these trips, however, can be a puzzle. You'll want lots of base layers to keep warm, outerwear that's both functional and stylish, cozy loungewear for game nights and hanging by the fire, and festive looks for photo-op-worthy nights out. Oh, and you need it to all fit in your suitcase or weekend bag.

No worries! Right now, Target is stocked with versatile pieces that can help you check all those boxes and make planning—and packing—your fits easy. Think affordable, 100 percent cashmere sweaters, faux fur coats, and cold-weather accessories in this season's hottest hues. Here, we outline a holiday travel packing list filled with our top cozy, stylish finds from Target.

Cashmere is warm, but not bulky, and a natural, breathable fiber that doesn't require frequent washing, making it ideal for your winter travel wardrobe. A crewneck cardigan in a poppy red can look festive at holiday gatherings or serve as a personality piece when paired with royal purple or prints. A striped blue crewneck pullover feels casual yet cool with jeans or trousers, while a simple black option can be dressed up for a dinner out.

If you're sightseeing, hitting a holiday craft market, or shopping through a city's downtown for last-minute gifts, you'll likely be outdoors, meaning that your coat will be the focal point of your outfit. Rather than treating your jacket like an afterthought, invest in a statement piece: A faux suede and shearling coat in a deep, espresso brown can feel like a vintage '60s find, while an ivory faux fur option will command attention while keeping you warm. A puffer with a fun and festive print can be a fashion-forward choice for ski trips.

When faced with frigid temps, you'll want to make sure you pack the works: scarves, hats, and gloves. These accessories don't need to be purely utilitarian—they can add a pop of personality to your outfit while keeping you cozy. A knit cashmere triangle scarf in an on-trend powder blue will pop against a brown coat or sweater, while a purple cashmere beanie adds a playful touch to all-black outerwear. If you prefer vintage looks, an aviator-style hat with a faux shearling brim is an unexpected choice, while black leather mittens with a faux fur cuff have just the right amount of edge.

For dinners out, a simple top like a black satin halter can elevate a pair of jeans and will take up minimal space in your bag. If you're attending a big celebration, like New Year's Eve, you can never go wrong with velvet and lace. If your style is more edgy, skip the frill in favor of a corset top, mini skirt, and tall boots.

Flare leggings and a striped fleece are comfortable enough to wear while relaxing on the couch, but will look put together if you bump into someone from high school when you're out running last-minute holiday errands. If you prefer snuggling up in sweats, pack at least one hoodie with personality in the form of a graphic print. Layer a cute cami with lace trim underneath for a cozy yet styled look.

Look for clothes that add warmth without adding bulk, such as a cashmere crewneck sweater. If you're flying this holiday season, wear your thickest items, like your coat and boots, on the plane.

Bold outerwear is on trend for 2026. Look for coats that make a statement with materials such as faux fur and silhouettes including cinched waists.

A simple black sweater can be a blank canvas for your trip. Wear it with jeans and comfortable shoes while you sightsee, then add wide-leg trousers and gold jewelry for a dinner out. If you're heading to a party, you can also pair a simple black sweater with a mini skirt, tights, and boots for a festive look.
