Humanoid robots fail supermarket jobs after “slacking off” and having ‘bad attitude’

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Robots were put to the test in a supermarket in China, but two actually failed to complete their tasks, and one needed to have more “enthusiasm.”

If you believed sci-fi movies back in the day, humans should have been replaced by robots at this point. As it is, though, engineers are still playing catch-up and trying to develop increasingly life-like humanoids.

Some have already rolled out across the United States and China, mainly focused on companionship and doing some menial at-home tasks. There are some, though, that are working in stores too.

In China, those supermarket working bots have been put to the test: trying to complete a 24-hour shift. However, as TechSpot pointed out, two of them actually failed to complete their tasks.

The test, which was focused on Agibot’s G2 robots, was run by the company itself and advertised as ‘Robots Never Clock Out.’ The bots worked around the clock on tasks across four different periods of the day.

While four of the six robots were able to complete almost 2000 tasks that were required of them, two didn’t and were not promoted to “official employees” as a result. One of these was a cleaning bot that had to deviate its path due to a trash can.

The other bot was assigned to checking stock levels on shelves, but “lacked initiative” and couldn’t keep up with the pace of the work. As per ITHome, this bot was seen to be “slacking off,” and the robot’s “on-duty enthusiasm” was found lacking too.

These two bots will remain in an “observation” group with the hopes that they will follow the other four in becoming full-time employees in the future.

As noted, robots have been popping up in stores anyway, with Japanese convenience stores blending AI tech and robotics to tackle labor shortages and streamline shopping.
