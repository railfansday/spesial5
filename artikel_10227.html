Google Wallet rolling out digital Google Home keys with supported locks on Android

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Google Wallet for Android is rolling out support for digital home keys if you have a compatible smart lock in Google Home.

After adding a home key to Google Wallet, you can Tap to Unlock with your phone. This leverages NFC and “works even when your phone screen is locked” by default. However, you can adjust verification settings in the Google Wallet app to require screen lock or biometric authentication. This requires Android 9+.

There’s also support for Unlock on Approach with an Aliro Ultra Wideband (UWB)-compatible Android phone (see below) and an Aliro UWB-compatible lock. Wear OS watches are not supported at launch.

Behind the scenes, the digital key is linked to the Google Home app rather than a single lock. As such, “each of your compatible Aliro locks will work with a single key in your Google Wallet.” Keys are added to Google Wallet using the Home app, which will prompt you to save after setup. You can also go to Home settings > Google Wallet key.

Afterward, your key “automatically syncs across compatible Android phones signed into your Google Wallet account.”

The card in Google Wallet features a simple Google Home logo with your home’s name in the top-right corner.

The following locks are supported at launch: Aqara u400 (UWB and NFC), Schlage Sense Pro (NFC at launch and UWB to follow), and Nuki Keypad 2 NFC. Support for more models is coming.

Additionally, support is rolling out first to the Google Home Public Preview before coming to all users “over the coming weeks.”
