Final Fantasy 14 is set to make some big changes when the upcoming expansion Evercold releases in February. An overhaul to combat, dailies, and new raid difficulties are set to shake things up and, hopefully, keep people sticking around for longer.

And judging by a recent Letter from the Producer, Naoki Yoshida really wants folks coming back to see what Evercold is all about. So much so that the game's offering 30 free days for long-gone adventurers… or a quick-and-dirty paid skip if you're so inclined.

"Encouraging players to return is no mean feat," Yoshida wrote. "People pause their adventures for all kinds of reasons, including dissatisfaction and loss of interest. Although we continually update the game to address such issues, the fact remains that getting players to resume their journey is a difficult task indeed."

I can only assume that for that reason, the decision's been made to offer up a $9 cash shop item that'll skip you through Endwalker's patch story, as well as all of Dawntrail up until the final story patch. Now Final Fantasy 14 has offered paid story skips in the past—it still does—but this is the first time it's explicitly offered one for the current expansion. Usually the skips'll stop dead before the current expansion, meaning you still need to play through the newest story.

It's a pretty niche paid skip, too. You'll need to have at least completed Endwalker's main story and have a level 90 job on your chosen character, and even then you'll still have to level up to 100 in your own time in order to tackle the final patch quest of Dawntrail. But worry not, if you haven't played the game in six months, you'll be able to snap up 30 days of free game time until January 14. Plenty of time to get a job to max level and prepare for Evercold.

I'm mostly surprised by the introduction of this paid item because Yoshida has long been rather protective of the game's story. Something he admits in this very same letter: "I strongly believe that FF14 is a story-driven MMORPG," he wrote. "On top of that, skipping it all means you likely won't be familiar with the battle system and therefore will become a burden for other players in multiplayer content."

I do find it a little funny that Yoshida is worried that story skippers will weigh others down in dungeons and the like—I've played many a level 100 dungeon with folks who have clearly played enough to understand how to play yet still can't press their buttons properly.

But anyway, Yoshida does admit that there is a lot of story to get through. "The Hydaelyn and Zodiark saga alone encompasses five expansions' worth of content," he wrote. "And with Dawntrail marking the start of the second saga, plus the coming addition of Evercold, the story continues to unfold into something even larger."

It really does feel like Square Enix wants Evercold to mark a big resurgence for the game, one I really do hope pays off for the developer. I've been playing off-and-on since A Realm Reborn and have seen just about every evolution the game has taken, and also the ways it has stagnated over time. Its confidence in trying to draw people back has me excited for the changes that are coming.
