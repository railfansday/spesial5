Brooklyn Building Collapses, Firefighters Searching for Anyone Trapped

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A building collapsed in Brooklyn, and firefighters are searching for anyone who may be trapped ... according to officials.

The building collapsed around 4 PM in Flatbush ... and the FDNY says there were reports of people trapped, and multiple people have been injured.

The fire department says 2 people are being evaluated, but responders are still searching for anyone else trapped inside.

According to ABC7 ... the building collapsed into an open construction site, and FDNY officials say one person is dead and several 4 construction workers were injured.

You can see in the video ... it looks like the entire side of the building has fallen away, exposing bedrooms, closets, and the stairwell.

It's unclear if the construction work going on had anything to do with the building's collapse.
