‘Christina Aguilera: Christmas In Paris’ Producer Vertigo Live Faces Closure Over Unpaid Tax Bill

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

EXCLUSIVE: Vertigo Live Productions, the British company behind music specials like CBS‘s Christina Aguilera: Christmas in Paris, is facing the threat of liquidation over an unpaid tax bill.

HM Revenue & Customs is pursuing a “winding-up petition” against Vertigo Live Productions Limited, which in the UK is often the last resort taken by a creditor when attempting to recover debt.

HMRC is essentially seeking a court order to put Vertigo Live into liquidation. The petition alone could trigger the freezing of Vertigo’s bank accounts. The precise nature of the dispute and the amount allegedly owed are unclear. Vertigo Live has not responded to requests for comments. The company’s website was returning an error message on Friday. HMRC does not comment on individual cases. Watch on Deadline

HMRC’s winding-up petition is the third such petition Vertigo Live has faced this year. Cafe Croissant, a French firm that provided production services on CBS special Christina Aguilera: Christmas in Paris, issued proceedings in June.

In January, Luna Remote Systems, a remote camera company, also sought a winding-up petition against Vertigo, which has been involved in other specials including Duran Duran: A Hollywood High and Billy Idol: State Line – Live at Hoover Dam.

The Cafe Croissant and Luna petitions were respectively resolved and closed, according to court notices.

Furthermore, The TV Watercooler, a UK television industry forum, published a notice alleging that freelancers who worked on Christina Aguilera: Christmas in Paris went unpaid for months.
