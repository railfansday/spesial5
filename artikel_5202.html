Most of the Magnificent 7 stocks traded lower, with Nvidia (NVDA) gaining 1%, Amazon (AMZN) rising 0.5% and Tesla (TSLA) adding 0.4%. Alphabet slipped 0.1%, while Apple and Microsoft each fell 0.3%. Meta Platforms declined 0.4%. Health insurance stocks also moved lower after the US government proposed a Medicare Advantage audit framework under its CRUSH proposal, raising concerns across the sector. Among individual stocks, Aecom surged 8% after issuing a stronger fiscal 2027 outlook. Home Depot fell 2% after reducing the upper end of its profit guidance. Omeros dropped 7% on weaker-than-expected quarterly revenue, while Red Cat plunged 30% after withdrawing its full-year revenue target.
