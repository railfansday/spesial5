Delightful Delap and outfought Anderson - Forest v Man City player ratings

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Delap brace inspires Forest to stunning win over Man City

Liam Delap scored twice against his old club to help Nottingham Forest to victory and inflict a third straight Premier League loss on Manchester City.

Here's how I rated the players and and you can see how you scored them below.

Matz Sels: Could do nothing about Rayan Cherki's goal. 6

Daniel Munoz: Looked pumped and did superbly to keep the dangerous Jeremy Doku quiet. 7

Jair Cunha: The centre-back found himself in attack in the sixth minute, when he pounced before coolly rolling in the opener. Made a brilliant goalline clearance at the other end. 8

Nikola Milenkovic: Stood tall to block a Jeremy Doku strike early on which epitomised his overall showing. 7

Murillo: The Brazilian was solid and did what was asked of him, to help stop Erling Haaland. 7

Neco Williams: Got stuck in when required. A solid performance. 7

Ryan Yates: The captain's poor clearance allowed Cherki to score in the second half. 6

Xaver Schlager: Provided the assist for the second goal in a decent showing. 7

James McAtee: Had a very good game against his former club. Could have enjoyed a goal but had a weak shot saved, before blazing another over. 7

Morgan Gibbs-White: Hard-working and caused City a few problems in the final third. 7

Liam Delap: A double for the no-nonsense ex-City striker. Two thunderous finishes brought the stadium alive. 9

Subs not used: John Victor, Ousmane Diomande, Callum Hudson-Odoi, Chris Wood, Arnaud Kalimuendo.

Gianluigi Donnarumma: Another game without a clean sheet, another three conceded. Aerially suspect too. Not having a good season. 4

Abdukodir Khusanov: Seems awkward when shunted out to the right, before being moved back inside. 4

Ruben Dias: A desperate lunge to try to block Delap's goal summed up his - and his side's - poor display. 4

Marc Guehi: Part of a City defence that were all over the place in the first half before being hooked at half-time. 4

Josko Gvardiol: Not in great form at the moment. Soft on the ball early on when he had his pocket picked by Cunha for Forest's opener and was also substituted. 4

Elliot Anderson: Made an unhappy return to the City Ground where he lost the midfield battle. 5

Enzo Fernandez: A really disappointing performance. Should have pulled a goal back but sent his header from close range over. Midfield seems to lack in balance with him and Anderson in the side. 5

Jeremy Doku: Got no change out of Munoz and produced very little. 5

Iliman Ndiaye: Missed interception led to Forest's second goal and did not contribute much in attack. Not his best game. 5

Rayan Cherki: Pulled a goal back for City with a stunning finish in the second half. 6

Erling Haaland: Had some chances but not his best showing in front of goal. 5

Matheus Nunes (on 45, Guehi) Failed to put a foot in on Delap for Forest's third. 5

Nico O'Reilly (on 45, Gvardiol) With Gvardiol shaky, will the Englishman be given a run of games in the side? 6

Subs not used: Geronimo Rulli, Mateo Kovacic, Vitor Reis, Ayyoub Bouaddi, Allan, Rico Lewis.

After the opportunity to rate players has closed, the score displayed represents the average from all the submissions by BBC Sport users.
