Delap's chance at redemption against toothless Chelsea

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Liam Delap has a chance to show former club Chelsea what they are missing as Xabi Alonso's side continue to struggle in attack without Joao Pedro; watch Nottingham Forest vs Chelsea live on Sky Sports Premier League and Main Event on Friday Night Football; kick-off 8pm

When Liam Delap joined Chelsea in the summer of 2025, he probably had ambitions of cementing himself as an elite Premier League No 9. Perhaps even positioning himself to be the heir to Harry Kane's throne for England.

Less than a month later, Joao Pedro made the move to Stamford Bridge and suddenly, the landscape changed. He was not the first-choice striker at the club.

What followed was an arduous campaign that brought three goals in 47 games and saw Delap succumb to what is now the fabled curse of the Chelsea No 9 shirt.

A club-record move to Nottingham Forest in August has yet to truly revitalise his career either. But Oliver Glasner does have faith that there's a 20-goal-a-season striker hiding in the 23-year-old. What better time to showcase it than against his former employer?

This isn't just an opportunity for Delap to prove a point, mind. If anything, this game comes at the most opportune time to show Xabi Alonso exactly what he is missing at this very moment.

Since Joao Pedro has been on the sidelines with a thigh injury, Chelsea's devastating attack has often looked tame. Without the Brazilian as a focal point, Morgan Rogers and Cole Palmer have struggled to hit the same heights. As Paul Merson playfully put it: "if you had The Three Musketeers but took one of them out, you'd struggle to sell a film."

Joao Pedro has not played since the 2-0 win on October 17 against Everton. In the four Premier League matches since, Chelsea have lost three and won once against 10-man Manchester United. In those games, they scored three goals. They managed more than that in wins over Bournemouth and Brighton prior alone.

Alonso has tried all sorts to combat the loss. They played without a recognised striker against Tottenham and got ripped apart. The same approach was used in the defeat to Sunderland. Against United, Willian Estevao tended to operate centrally while Emmanuel Emegha was finally given the chance against Leeds. He scored but also had the fewest touches of any starter that day, being the only one to remain in single digits.

Danny Welbeck would appear to be the most obvious option, but Alonso appears reluctant to use his very own Benjamin Button from the off. That makes Emegha the only natural choice.

It's difficult to compare Delap and Emegha from last term for different reasons. The former was often watching from the bench while the latter was out from December to April with injury at Strasbourg. The season before, though, indicates that perhaps Delap could offer a little more with his all-round game.

What makes Joao Pedro so unique to Chelsea is not purely his scoring numbers. It is the fact that he is a consistent top-10 performer among all Premier League strikers with his link-up play.

While Emegha's data shows him to be more prolific in front of goal, the Englishman is far closer to Joao Pedro in build-up play than his Dutch counterpart. There is still a gulf in quality as you'd expect, but it certainly shows that the former Ipswich man was probably more suited to filling in when the Brazilian is unavailable.

That was the case two seasons ago and is even more so since he has joined Forest. The indication being that even in a period that hasn't been so fruitful, he has still been able to develop.

Delap has shown he can thrive when he has a chip on his shoulder. His brace against Manchester City was as great an example as you could find to show that. Against another former club, with his team struggling to get results and fans fans beginning to grow frustrated.

That was the night he announced himself to the City Ground faithful. On Friday, it will be an opportunity to announce himself to the travelling supporters, something he'd have wished to have done by this time last year.

He may even be further buoyed by the fact that his former side are struggling just as much at the back as they are in attack. Chelsea have the fifth-worst defensive record in the Premier League from the first 11 games.

That is a mouth-watering fact for any striker, let alone one who has no excuse but to have the bit between his teeth. It feels as though the cards have fallen as perfectly as they possibly could for Delap to have his say.

No one will ever know if there's a scenario where Delap staying could have been beneficial for his career. But Friday Night Football is his platform show it would have been beneficial for the Blues.
