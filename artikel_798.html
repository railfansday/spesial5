John Summit, David Guetta, Alison Wonderland & More to Play College Football Playoff National Championship Weekend

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The free AT&T Playoff Playlist Live festival happens Jan. 23-24 in Las Vegas.

The 2027 College Football Playoff National Championship will see two top teams competing on Jan. 25 at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas. Before that, a heavy-hitting lineup of DJs will provide pump-up music before the big game. Explore Alison Wonderland David Guetta John Summit See latest videos, charts and news

This year’s edition of AT&T Playoff Playlist Live – The CFP Music Festival will happen Jan. 23-24 at Las Vegas’ Grand Prix Plaza, located just off the Strip. The night 1 lineup will feature David Guetta, Marshmello, Alison Wonderland, Honeyluv and Girl Math, the duo made up of Nala and VNNSA. On day 2, the lineup will feature John Summit, Fisher, Sofi Tukker, Korolova and DJ Skilah. Doors open at 3 p.m. both days. Admission to this event is free, with advance registration required. Registration details will be announced via the CFP’s official mobile app at a later date.

“No city embraces the variety of music genres like Las Vegas,” Ryan Allen Hall, the CFP senior director of public events and sponsorships, says in a statement. “But it’s clear, electronic music is the pulse. From the residencies, nightclubs, dayclubs, lounges, pools and festivals, the city simply does EDM like only a select few in the entire world.

“But the magic for us is to bring together this stacked lineup in Las Vegas and to charge the fans absolutely nothing,” Hall continues. “It is all part of the CFP’s commitment to providing a world class championship experience to our fans and guests, especially during National Championship week. We curated a lineup specifically for the city of Miami last year and it was the largest concert ever seen on South Beach. A true spectacle. We’re here to do the same for the city of Las Vegas this January.”

The 2027 edition of the event follows this year’s edition, which happened in Miami Beach with artists including Summit, Calvin Harris, Peso Pluma and Latin Mafia performing for a crowd of approximately 50,000.
