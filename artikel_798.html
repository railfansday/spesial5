Hailey Bieber Reveals Son Jack’s Favorite Food in Sweet New Video

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Hailey Bieber shared that her and Justin Bieber’s son Jack Blues, 2, has inherited her love of a tasty treat in a new Rhode behind-the-scenes video.

Hailey Bieber famously created the glazed donut makeup trend—but her son takes the cake with the sweet treat itself.

In fact, the Rhode Beauty founder shared her and Justin Bieber’s 2-year-old son Jack Blues recently woke her up to reveal his preference for the snack.

“The other day, I vaguely woke up when he was talking in the middle of the morning,” Hailey shared while getting her hair styled in a Rhode behind-the-scenes clip posted to the brand’s Instagram Nov. 18. “He’s like, ‘I like donuts! I like them.’”

The 29-year-old’s hair stylist pointed out that Jack has inherited his mom’s love of the pastry.

“That’s your kid,” she joked to Hailey, who agreed with a smile, “Yeah.”

And as the entrepreneur prepares to mark a new milestone when she turns 30 on Nov. 22, she’s looking back at the biggest experience of the previous decade.

“Having a baby, becoming a mom definitely changed me the most in my 20s,” Hailey replied with a smile when asked what part of the decade transformed her the most. “I always knew I wanted to be a mom. I always knew I wanted kids, and it just totally transforms you as a person for the better.”

Though it’s been over two years since she entered that chapter, motherhood continues to bring her teachable moments.

“I ask every parent this,” Hailey shared. “I think that kids are just in a lot of ways born who they are. That's my biggest learning since having a child.”

As she put it, “I know that that's my kid, but I'm also like, ‘How are you, my child?’”

But there’s no doubt that Jack is Justin’s child, too. After all, the tiny tot may already be following in the Grammy winner’s footsteps.

Hailey shared an adorable video on her Instagram Stories Nov. 12, showing Jack playing some notes on a keyboard, writing over the footage, “blues loves the keys.”

And while time will tell if Jack will one day be hitting the stage like his dad, Hailey previously teased that she and Justin plan to add at least one member to their band.

“I definitely want more than one, but I’m taking it one at a time,” she told Interview magazine in April. “Some days I want two. Some days I want five.”
