Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Defined benefit (pension) versus defined contribution

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

  • Inflation adjustment maxes at 1.5%/year
  • If she leaves service early she can cash out her contributions + a modest (~2%) appreciation
For me these two are reasons against the pension plan. The inflation protection is weak. Some plans may have similar annual increases but in parallel provide an inflation-adjusted floor, for example the LA Schools which promises that after inflation the pension will be at least 85% of the starting value. Since this one does not, in a 20-30+ years retirement even moderate inflation would erode the pension significantly.

Second, the 2% inflation if she leaves and cashes out may be at most break-even after inflation. While markets are not guaranteed to have positive returns over any length of time, it is likely the index-fund alternative in the Defined Contribution choice would have much better returns if she leaves early.
