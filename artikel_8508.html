Use Upside if participating gas stations sit on the routes you already drive. Claim an offer before you pump and pay with a card linked to the app, and the cash back comes on top of your card’s rewards. Where few businesses near you take part, it isn’t worth the extra step.

Upside is a free app that pays drivers and diners cash back at more than 100,000 gas stations, convenience stores, grocery stores, and restaurants, by its count. Each offer is for one location, and the business can change it often, based on its prices, its traffic, and your spending habits.

Commuters who buy 12 gallons a week at Upside’s average offer of 11¢ a gallon earn about $69 a year, on top of their card’s rewards. Drivers with no participating stations on their routes get more from the card-linked offers already in their bank’s app.
