Tanggal: 2026-11-17

So I spent way too much time in Claude code. I'd say that I like Wealthfolio the best, but it was the hardest to setup by far if you want historical transactions. You basically need not just snapshots, but all your old credit card and brokerage statements to categorize everything via AI and put into its database.

Sure was a lot easier to just import csvs and simple stuff to and have it keep updated. If you just want budgets and spending tracking, Sure is probably the best.

Where I like Wealthfolio more is on the investment tracking, but only after setting it up. And it's a giant PITA for bogleheads. The best way I found to categorize things by how most of us want with US, International, etc is through custom categories.

I also really like how Wealthfolio let me create events like Travel and Business trips. That way you could have those filtered out or in depending on what you want to look at, while your rules for food and hotels etc on those vacations all get categorized properly too. In Sure, that capability is more limited and you really just have to add tags with no timeframe of your vacation.

Once wealthfolio is setup and has everything, and i mean everything, it's Fire calculator is actually pretty cool. It has a ton of inputs you can put in and change for different assumptions. I was slightly disappointed that it didn't come up with spending estimates since it had all my spending data in it, but oh well. But I'd say it's Fire or retirement calculator found in the goals menu is more fully featured than Fidelity Full View ever was.

If you're a sadist, and you can take the time to use AI to gather all your brokerage and bank statements, input everything into wealthfolio, then I'd say it can pretty much replace your investment tracking spreadsheet. But it's a lot of work to get to that point.

Most credit cards and brokerages only have data back about 7 years. A couple of my credit cards were only 2 years.

If you're a Fidelity Full View convert, I'd say to just abandon the Full View csv export unless it's all you have. I wasted way too much time trying to get my FullView csv to work, and the more I looked at it the more I realized Full View was just wrong in so many places. Hence my journey to download a million brokerage and bank statements.
