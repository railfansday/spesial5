"No One Gets Left Behind" - CDPR On The Witcher 4 Releasing For All Platforms At The Same Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

"Switch 2 is clearly getting left behind"

This week, CD Projekt Red and Sony announced an exclusive marketing deal for Ciri's upcoming title, The Witcher 4. It's raised some concerns online about the fourth outing potentially being a "timed-exclusive" for the PS5, but thankfully, this won't be the case.

CD Projekt Red's co-CEO Michal Nowakowski confirmed this to a fan via social media, but his response about how "no one gets left behind" (in reference to previously announced versions of the game) appears to have upset some Nintendo fans, as there's currently no confirmation of a Switch 2 version.

"All the platforms release at the same time - PS5, XB, and PC. No one gets left behind."

The Witcher 4 was unveiled for PS5, Xbox and PC in 2024, and there's been no mention of a Nintendo release since then. While this doesn't completely rule out a Switch 2 port in the future, Nintendo fans have noted how the "Switch 2 is clearly getting left behind" in this case.

Of course, we'll remain hopeful given CDPR's strong support of Nintendo's platforms in the past and more recently during the Switch 2's launch window. The fourth game, powered by Unreal Engine 5, also isn't expected until 2028, so anything could happen between now and then.

The Polish studio previously worked its magic (with the help of Saber Interactive) during the Switch generation, with an "impossible" port of Witcher 3. Most recently, Switch 2 players got Witcher 3 Remastered, and this is set to be followed by the new expansion 'Songs of the Past' in 2027. CDPR also did a "preem" job on the Switch 2 version of Cyberpunk 2077.
