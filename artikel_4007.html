Pikmin's Mobile Soundtrack Has Been Added To Nintendo Music, Here's Every Song Included

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Celebrating five years of "blooming together"

Alongside the release of Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy this week, Nintendo added the mobile soundtrack Pikmin Bloom to its music app. This also happens to line up with the mobile title's fifth anniversary.

This update adds 77 songs to Nintendo Music (with a run time of 1 hour and 50 minutes). Masato Kouda was in charge of the music for this particular entry. Here's the full song list for this latest update:

In case you missed it, during its new Pikmin Day celebration this year, Nintendo announced that Pikmin Bloom would be getting a new update called 'Secret Caves' in Winter 2026. In this update, players will collect treasure in caves by defeating creatures.
