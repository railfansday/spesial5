Feature: Five Reasons Why 2026 Is The Best Time Ever To Be An Xbox Series X|S Owner

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The two consoles are celebrating their sixth anniversary on November 10th

We've got a lot of anniversaries taking place over the next few weeks, including the big 25th anniversary of Xbox on November 15th, but we thought we'd first take a moment to celebrate the Xbox Series X|S anniversary as well.

Both of these consoles launched six years ago on November 10th of 2020, and Pure Xbox was there at the time with a big celebration of the launch along with an unboxing preview, a full review, and various bits of coverage.

Hardware Review Xbox Series X - The Fastest, Most Powerful Xbox Ever

A lot has changed over this past six years for Xbox (and indeed, the gaming industry at large), and not all of it has been for the better by any means. As someone who picked up an Xbox Series X at launch though, I reckon 2026 has been the best time ever to own the console, so this seems like a good chance to highlight some reasons why!

Here are those five reasons — in my personal opinion — on why it's so good to own an Xbox Series console right now:

Prior to this year, there had been a lot of disgruntlement amongst the fanbase over the "This is an Xbox" marketing campaign that essentially told us the Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S weren't all that important anymore. That might not have been the intention, but it's certainly the impression that many fans came away with.

We've seen a complete change in 2026 though, with Asha Sharma's new leadership team insisting that console is a major focus for them, and implementing an absolute ton of new console-specific features throughout the year. From Mythic Achievements to new startup animations, it's been an amazing year for console updates without a doubt.

The obvious benefit to being an Xbox Series X|S owner this late in the lifecycle is that you get access to a tonne of great games from over the years. 2026 has been an incredible year for new releases too — both first-party and third-party — and I think Xbox Game Pass also enjoyed another really fruitful year complete with many "day one" releases.

My personal backlog is extensive at this point, and it doesn't help that ID@Xbox keeps knocking it out of the park with so many great indie releases on a monthly basis. Xbox Play Anywhere is growing on an extremely impressive scale as well, meaning I can play loads of my purchased Series X|S games on PC whenever I want to.

Despite what some might think, we've had exclusives on Xbox consoles over the past six years, but most of them have been of the third-party variety. Gears of War: E-Day changed all that in October when it become the first permanent console exclusive that Xbox has published in a long time, and it'll be followed up by Clockwork Revolution in 2027.

In other words, you're going to need an Xbox Series X, an Xbox Series S or a powerful enough PC to play these games going forward, which certainly makes these consoles more attractive for fans of certain titles and franchises. I'm certainly comforted knowing that I already own a Series X for when Xbox decides to make even more games exclusive.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is going to have the biggest video game launch of all time, and that's only going to happen across PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There's no PC version whatsoever right now, and fans could be waiting until well into next year until they get a native Windows release of the game.

That automatically makes it a fantastic time to be an Xbox Series X|S owner if you're interested in GTA 6! All of the pre-release footage has been on base PS5, which means it should look basically the same on the Xbox Series X. Everyone's a little bit more concerned about the Series S version, but this is Rockstar we're talking about - they're surely going to pull out all the stops to optimise the biggest game ever for Microsoft's lower-powered console.

It's not usually the case that we're talking about a strong future for six-year-old consoles, but the video game industry isn't in a normal place right now. Everyone knows about the component crisis that's causing prices to vastly increase across the board - something that's affected new purchases of the Series X and S as well.

However, with rumours that Xbox's Project Helix console (potentially set for 2028) could cost well over $1,000, it seems more likely than ever that the Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S have still got long futures ahead of them. Combine that with all the new console features that have been added this year, plus the arrival of new technology such as AMD FSR Upscaling 4.1, and it looks like there's a lot more life left in these consoles going forward.

Again, I appreciate that it's not a great time to buy an Xbox Series X or Series S right now due to the aforementioned component crisis, and here's hoping we might at least get some Black Friday deals in a few weeks' time.

For the rest of us who already own one though, this has got to be the best time ever, right? Amazing games, loads of new features, exclusives, GTA 6... I'm very glad to own an Xbox Series X sitting here in November of 2026.

My only fear? That it'll suddenly break and I'll have spend an absolute fortune on a new one.
