Rockets get back to winning with 119-104 victory over Pacers

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Kevin Durant scored 21 points and Reed Sheppard knocked down six triples off the bench on his way to 20 points to lead the Houston Rockets past the Indiana Pacers 119-104 despite Houston losing star center Alperen Sengun to injury. Sengun was poked in the eye and hit in the face and did not return, and Houston head coach Ime Udoka said he's not sure how much time Sengun will miss. In addition, Rockets guard Marcus Smart exchanged words with a fan, and Pacers coach Rick Carlisle was ejected in what was quite the eventful matchup for a Wednesday night.

KD shot 7-for-9 from the field, including 4-for-5 deep, while Sheppard was 7-for-10 from the floor and 6-for-7 from deep on a night in which the Rockets shot 19-for-35 from three overall. Houston also got 17 from Jabari Smith Jr., who shot 3-for-6 from deep, 15 each from Fred VanVleet and Amen Thompson, with VanVleet also adding 6 assists and Thopmson adding 9 boards, 3 assists, 1 steal and 1 block. And finally, to round out the double-digit scorers, Tari Eason had 11. Sengun had 7 points and 7 boards in just 14 minutes of action.

Pascal Siakam led the way for Indiana with 24 points, and Kelly Oubre Jr. had 17, but the Rockets led for most of the way in this one, despite both teams shooting 49 percent on the night. The difference was Houston won the rebound battle 46-35 and held the Pacers to 10-for-35 from deep. That's not enough to overcome Houston on a night they are shooting well. The Rockets just have too many weapons.

Steven Adams and Oscar Tshiebwe mostly filled in for Sengun, playing 27 minutes between them, but combined for just 8 points and 9 boards. You'll likley see more of the same if Sengun misses time, and if he misses extended time, you could see some Jabari Smith Jr. at a small-ball five on the nights there's no Steve-O.

The Rockets now move to 12-4 on the season and sit squarely in third right now in the Western Conference. They will return to action on Friday with a road contest against the Denver Nuggets. Houston will hopefully have Sengun ready for his match with Nikola Jokic.
