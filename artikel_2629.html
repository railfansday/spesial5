Eagles at Cowboys Week 12 prediction

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The 2026 Philadelphia Eagles season has not been very fun.

That being said, the Birds have a chance to give their fans a really fun moment in time by beating the Dallas Cowboys on Thanksgiving.

The Eagles won in Week 11, yeah, but it's hard to feel like that victory could've aged any worse. The Pittsburgh Steelers are clearly an absolute mess. The Eagles beating them probably said more about Pittsburgh's dysfunction than it did Philly turning a corner.

Case in point: the Eagles' offense was still pretty awful despite undergoing some stylistic changes. It's difficult to envision this unit keeping up with the Cowboys' scoring pace.

That said, the Dallas defense is bad. They might be less bad now than Mike Pettine is their play-caller … but last week's win over the Tennessee Titans hardly proves as much.

The path to an Eagles victory involves the offense capitalizing on the Cowboys' defensive struggles … and Vic Fangio's defense really rising to the challenge. With 11 sacks and 21 tackles for loss over their last two games, the Eagles' defensive line has shown signs of life. It's time for Jalen Carter to step up and deliver in a big way, especially given that the Cowboys will be without starting right guard Tyler Booker.

If the Eagles win this game, it'll be the most satisfying possible victory in a lost season.

If they lose, well, they'll be 3-8 and much closer to being officially eliminated from playoff contention.

Unfortunately, I see the latter scenario more likely.

Bold prediction: Joe Milton throws a touchdown pass where the ball deflects off one player's hands, bounces high into the air, and gets caught by Darius Cooper.
