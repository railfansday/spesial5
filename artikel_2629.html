Grizzlies' GG Jackson suffers 'serious foot injury' in 129-110 win over Nets

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Memphis Grizzlies coach Tuomas Iisalo confirmed GG Jackson sustained a "relatively serious foot injury" in the Grizzlies' 129-110 win over the Brooklyn Nets on Nov. 14.

Jackson played just 2 minutes, 52 seconds before leaving the game with 5:01 to play in the first quarter. The Grizzlies later confirmed during the game that Jackson would not return because of a left foot injury.

"That's very sad for him. He was catching a good stride," Iisalo said after the game. "There will be some further testing, but so far it doesn't look very good."

Jackson knocked down a 3-pointer before exiting. He scored 14 points on 4-of-11 shooting against the New Orleans Pelicans on the first night of the Grizzlies' back-to-back.

Jackson is averaging 7.0 points per game this season, shooting 37.1% from the field and 40% on 3-pointers.
