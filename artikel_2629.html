The Chiefs are back atop AFC West thanks to tiebreakers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Denver Broncos are on their bye in Week 10 and that allowed the Kansas City Chiefs to pass them thanks to their win and some tiebreakers.

The Broncos and Chiefs each have a head-to-head win so it moves to the divisional record tiebreaker. Kansas City is 3-1 in the AFC West while the Broncos are only 2-1 at this point. The Chiefs are atop the AFC West!

The Cleveland Browns won and the New England Patriots lost to swap spots in the wild card. Cleveland is in and New England is out.

The Chiefs and Broncos first go to a divisional tiebreaker at 7-2, where Kansas City wins with a 3-1 record in the AFC West over the Broncos at 2-1. Kansas City is the division leader.

The Chiefs, Jaguars, and Bengals go into a three-way tiebreaker and one team hasn't beat all the others, so it heads to AFC record. Kansas City wins with a 6-1 AFC record to secure the two seed.

Then the Jaguars have a head-to-head win over Cincinnati to nab the three seed.
