Report: Aaron Rodgers was "excused" from work on Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The sudden, late-night benching of quarterback Aaron Rodgers has created a slew of questions that are being answered in real time.

One of the obvious questions was answered on Wednesday morning, when Gerry Dulac of the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette reported that Rodgers didn’t show up for work on Wednesday. Implicit in that report was the idea that he chose to no-show.

Now, Adam Schefter of ESPN reports that Rodgers’s absence was “excused” as he processes the fact that coach Mike McCarthy has decided to play Mason Rudolph on Friday against the Broncos.

At some point, Rodgers will have something to say. Maybe he’ll make a surprise appearance on McAfee’s show today, even if that development would surprise no one.

Rodgers will surely have an opinion on the situation. Chances are that his opinion will be something other than, “You know, they were right to bench me.”

We’ve seen this movie before. Rodgers has no qualms about airing grievances. Now, the Steelers have given him a specific reason to feel aggrieved.

The surprise at this point wouldn’t be Rodgers showing up on McAfee’s show and flaming the Steelers. The surprise would be Rodgers saying nothing.

Silence could easily be interpreted as acceptance of, and possibly agreement with, the move. Beyond that, Rodgers has shown (specifically since his 2021 heel turn) that he’s never silent on topics about which he has strong feelings.
