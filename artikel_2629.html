Instant thoughts after Notre Dame basketball falls to No. 1 Florida

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Twenty-one random thoughts from the home office back in Indiana while watching Notre Dame basketball lose its first game this season, 86-78, to top-ranked Florida in the quarterfinals of the Players Festival 8 in Las Vegas ...

∎ A ton of good stuff there in the second half from Notre Dame, be it Brady Koehler (17 points, five rebounds) or Braeden Shrewsberry (20 points, five rebounds) or Logan Imes or Ethan Roberts. There was a time when it looked like this one would get away from Notre Dame, which was back within three with 11:20 left and six with 4:28. Against that team? Take it.

More: A lot more to like in this one for Notre Dame basketball against SC State

∎ The Irish (4-1) have lost their last three games against No. 1 ranked teams since last beating top-ranked (USA Today poll) North Carolina at home on Feb. 6, 2016. Notre Dame has eight victories over No. 1 teams in program history.

∎ Down by 16 in the opening minute of the second half? Just where Notre Dame wanted it. The Irish kept hanging around, kept chipping away, kept getting some stops. Like that, a 20-9 run helped the Irish get back within five with 11:36 left. Put some game pressure on those other guys.

∎ The Irish got back to within three and had a chance to tie, but Shrewsberry couldn't get a 3 to fall. Next time down, Koehler's 3 rattled in and out and halfway down and jumped out. Denzel Aberdeen banged in a 3 at the other end and like that, the deficit was back to nine.

∎ Florida likely wondered at points in the second half, don't those guys ever go away? Other Irish teams? Maybe. This one? Different.

∎ Chalk this one up to not enough made shots, not enough stops and too many second and third chances for the other guys. The Irish can weather too many missed opportunities on one end, but not both.

∎ The last time Notre Dame played a No. 1 team – back in February back at Purcell Pavilion – it trailed No. 1 Duke at half 54-22. On Tuesday, it was 47-32 at the break, so there's that.

∎ Could Notre Dame make a trade for one of Florida's bigs at halftime? Maybe for a guard or a swingman to be named later? Would have liked the Irish chance if that could have happened.

∎ Outrebounded 20-7 in the first half and allowing 22 points in the paint is too much for any team to overcome.

∎ A 17-4 Florida run dumped Notre Dame into its largest deficit of the season – 14 points – with 2:54 left in the first half. Notre Dame never led the first 20 minutes and trailed by as many as 16.

∎ Playing in Las Vegas each of the last three seasons often has seen Notre Dame bring its "A" compete game. The five Irish losses prior to Tuesday, three of which were to ranked teams, were by an average of 7.2 points.

∎ Lost in the loss was the steps taken by guard Jonathan Sanderson, who often looked like a freshman the first four games. He then went for 14 points and two assists in 21 minutes against the Gators and looked/played like he belonged.

∎ Think fifth-year wing guard Ethan Roberts was high on the priority list on Florida's scout? After going for 25 points on nine-of-10 from the field and seven-of-eight from 3 in the first half in last week's win over South Carolina State to earn Atlantic Coast Conference player of the week honors, Roberts was 0-for-2 from 3 for two points in the first 20. Roberts didn't get his first field goal until 14:50 remained. It came on a drive. Roberts labored to 10 points and finished 0-for-3 from 3.

∎ The Irish tumbled into a double-digit deficit (15-5) the first 8:20, but climbed back to get within two, 15-13, thanks to an 8-0 run buoyed by 3-pointers from two bench guys – Logan Imes and Sanderson. When the Irish could get stops and get out in transition, they got some good stuff. Playing against a halfcourt defense? Not so much.

∎ A window of opportunity opened for the Irish just over halfway through the first half when Boogie Fland left to have an ailing right shoulder tended to and Rueben Chinyelu left with his second foul. Florida still had more than enough options to counter a 13-4 Irish run. This one got away from Notre Dame AFTER those two Florida starters departed.

∎ This was a game that Notre Dame really could have used sophomore center Tommy Ahneman for his size, rim protection and option to get something done around the bucket. The 6-foot-10, 255-pound Ahneman is out for six to eight weeks with a right arm (wrist) injury suffered in the Nov. 9 win over Eastern Illinois. Ahneman did not have surgery, but has since had his right wrist area in a cast, where it will remain for about seven weeks.

∎ Why was Ahneman missed? That Florida front line of Thomas Haugh, Rueben Chinleyu and Alex Condon came into Tuesday averaging a combined 41.1 points, 25.6 rebounds and 8.3 assists. The three then went for 39 points, 22 rebounds and nine assists. Florida's first four baskets were all point-blank looks. An early challenge with no Irish answer, really all night.

∎ Notre Dame basketball had started to make a little noise nationally. On Monday, Notre Dame received three votes in the weekly Associated Press (media) national poll – two more votes than ACC colleague North Carolina. Regardless of the game against Florida, Notre Dame has a chance in November and December to make a little more racket – and garner a few more votes – in the Associated Press and USA Today coaches' polls. Notre Dame has not been ranked in the USA Today poll since January 13, 2018, before a 69-68 home loss to North Carolina bumped it from No. 25.

∎ Earlier Tuesday, Notre Dame basketball took to social media to unveil the green uniforms it will wear this year, which begs the question – why green? The color doesn't hold anywhere near the historic significance as it does with football (Green Machine, right Digger?). Notre Dame won't make a uniform splash nationally until it decides to return to the black and white and green unis it debuted in February 2006 in a win at Seton Hall (the "Urban Irish," as former head coach Mike Brey said) and the "mustard" golds it rode in 2015 all the way to coming this close to beating Kentucky in an NCAA regional final. Want a look that pops? Go with those two, starting with the mustard golds. Back in black, too. Green? Meh.

∎ At tip-off Tuesday, the temperature in Las Vegas was 60 degrees and sunny. Back in South Bend, it was 54 degrees and sunny. Vegas sharps would call that a push, no?

∎ Death, taxes and games against Rutgers in Las Vegas in November. For the third straight season and the third straight Players Era, Notre Dame and Rutgers will meet in Vegas, this time, a second-round game Wednesday, Nov. 18 at 2 p.m. In a quarterfinal contest that preceded Notre Dame-Florida, Rutgers (3-1) lost 85-73 to No. 7 Houston, which Notre Dame has also played the previous two years at Players Era.
