Bills vs. Chiefs prediction: Which team wins latest chapter of Josh Allen-Patrick Mahomes saga?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

The NFL's three-game Thanksgiving slate concludes with a blockbuster battle between two perennial playoff teams in the Buffalo Bills and Kansas City Chiefs. A pair of franchises that have regularly butted heads during the Josh Allen and Patrick Mahomes eras clash once again in one of the season's biggest showdowns to date.

As is often the case when they square off in the regular season, there are significant postseason seeding implications at play in this showdown between the Bills and Chiefs. Buffalo stands in control of the AFC's No. 1 seed, while Kansas City is in a tightly contested race with the Denver Broncos for the AFC West crown and the top-four seed that comes with it. Should the Chiefs beat the Bills, not only would they solidify their standing in the division hunt, but they would also then give themselves a clear path to the top overall seed and a first-round bye.

This game stands among the greatest Thanksgiving matchups in NFL history. The combined 17-3 record between the two teams is the second-best mark by win percentage entering a Thanksgiving game in the last 50 seasons.

Both sides are also playing their best football of the season heading into the holiday duel. This is just the second game in league history to feature two teams riding three-game win streaks in which all of those victories came by 17 or more points. The only other such instance came in 2007 between Tom Brady's New England Patriots and Peyton Manning's Indianapolis Colts. The quarterback face-off in this contest is about as legendary as that one.

It is difficult to knock Kansas City, because when it wins, it does so in resounding fashion and shows all the makings of a future Super Bowl winner. But the Chiefs' strength of schedule and results against projected playoff teams do raise questions about whether they can take down the Bills on the road in primetime. The Chiefs have only played three games against opponents with winning records all season and are just 1-2 in those contests. Both losses came on the road. Simply put, they have not shown an ability to win games like the one they are about to walk into.

At the same time, the Bills defeated each of the three best teams on their schedule thus far. Plus, for whatever reason, Allen has Mahomes' number when they meet in the regular season. Add the defensive revival into the equation, and everything comes up Bills in this matchup. Pick: Bills 38, Chiefs 33
