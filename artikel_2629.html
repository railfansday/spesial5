What Eagles’ Nick Sirianni said after snapping 7-game losing streak with Steelers win

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

What Eagles' Nick Sirianni said after snapping 7-game losing streak with Steelers win appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Nick Sirianni expressed huge relief to have the Philadelphia Eagles back on the win column after leading them past the Pittsburgh Steelers 19-16 at home on Sunday afternoon.

Sirianni has been the Eagles' head coach since 2021, going through his sixth season with the franchise. He led them to a Super Bowl title in 2024, having succeeded at getting Philadelphia back to the mountaintop.

However, the 2026 campaign has been brutal for Sirianni and the Eagles. They are shorthanded with Jalen Hurts out for the season with blood clots, resulting in Philadelphia quickly falling down the standings. They were going through seven consecutive losses before finally ending the skid with the win over the Steelers, resulting in positive remarks from Sirianni after the game.

"We got in (the locker room) like, 'Man, we missed this.' …We've had a lot of games like that. We've been used to winning those games in the past; haven't [gone] our way this year. So, it was good to be able to do that today. That's a good football team. A lot of respect to that football team," Sirianni said, per reporter Zach Berman.

Nick Sirianni and the Eagles hope to have a better end to the 2026 campaign. Their playoff hopes are quite low at the moment, but they can get a few more wins with some scrappy efforts down the stretch.

Philadelphia set the tone early on, leading 13-0 after the first quarter. Pittsburgh attempted to rally back for the remainder of the game, but a pair of field goals was enough for the Eagles to secure the home win.

Joe Milton started as he completed 11 out of 21 passes for 141 yards. Meanwhile, Saquon Barkley had a solid outing on the ground with 17 carries for 75 yards and a touchdown.

The Eagles will look forward to their next matchup as they remain active for Thanksgiving week, being on the road. They take on the Dallas Cowboys in Week 12 as kickoff commences on Nov. 26 at 4:30 p.m. ET.
