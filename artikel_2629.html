‘All About Your Team’: Jeff Gordon on Chase Elliott, Crew Chief Dynamic

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Hendrick Motorsports Vice Chairman Jeff Gordon provided insight on the working relationship between Chase Elliott and crew chief Alan Gustafson. The four-time NASCAR Cup Series champion said the two have mutual respect for each other and want to continue working together.

Gustafson will remain the crew chief for Elliott for the 2027 season. However, HMS recently announced crew chief changes for next year. Rudy Fugle will be crew chief for Alex Bowman's No. 48 in 2027 after six seasons with William Byron.

Byron, meanwhile, will have Adam Wall atop the pit box next year. Wall was previously the crew chief for HMS driver Corey Day in the O'Reilly Auto Parts Series.

During a recent interview with SiriusXM NASCAR Radio, Gordon noted that Elliott and Gustafson have been together a long time. In fact, Gustafson has served as Elliott's crew chief since the driver of the No. 9 moved up to full-time Cup Series racing in 2016.

"It's all about your working relationship; it's all about your team and, listen, those guys have a bond. They've been together for a long time and they have a tremendous amount of respect for one another and I think Chase knows that Alan and that team are strong," Gordon said.

Elliott won two races in 2026 with victories at Martinsville and Texas. However, the 2020 Cup champion wound up finishing 12th in the standings.

Although the 2026 season did not end how the No. 9 team hoped, Gordon reminded everyone of their history.

"When they're clicking, they go and just like they did this past year, they won two races. I know they would've liked to have had more in the Chase and a better end of the season and let's not forget they've won 23 races together and a championship," Gordon said.

The bond between Elliott and Gustafson is something HMS appreciates, Gordon said, because the two are always dialed in on how to become a better team.

"Anytime we're sitting down with those guys and evaluating it, they just want to get better and stronger and continue to work together and we love that," Gordon said.

While Elliott and Gustafson have been proven to be a championship-winning combination, the driver's 2026 stats are alarming.

Elliott kicked off the year strong with a win at Martinsville, which was the seventh race of the campaign. It was the earliest the 30-year-old out of Dawsonville, Georgia had ever won in a given season. Four races later, Elliott notched his second win of the season at Texas.

Unfortunately for the No. 9 group, the season took a negative turn. Elliott wound up finishing 12th in the standings, the second-lowest of his career. His average finish ended at 15.1, tied for the worst of his career. Elliott also tallied 13 top-10 finishes, which is a career-low.

Elliott enters a contract year in 2027 with HMS. Given the dismal latter half of the 2026 season, it will be interesting to see what the future holds for the eight-time Most Popular Driver Award winner.
