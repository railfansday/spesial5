What NFL game is on TV in your area today? Week 10 coverage map

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The first NFL Sunday after the league's trade deadline could influence playoff and division races for weeks to come. And the stakes will be raised early.

The Detroit Lions and New England Patriots get the Week 10 schedule started in the morning with an international game in Munich, Germany. As usual, FOX and CBS will then split the rights to the 11-game Sunday afternoon slate from there.

FOX will get to broadcast six games, with a game in both the early- and late-afternoon windows in each region. The network will air the San Francisco 49ers' game against the Dallas Cowboys as a national broadcast for its late afternoon time slot. CBS has the rights to five games and will broadcast just one regionally.

Here's a look at which games will be broadcast in each area of the country during Week 10 of the 2026 NFL season.
