GTA 6 actor has no idea who he plays & asks fans to find his character

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The actor behind an NPC in GTA 6 is sending players on a manhunt to find him, as he has no idea who his character actually is.

GTA 6 had been in development for a long time before it arrived on November 19. This means that many actors who appear in the game may have had to wait years to see their work fully realized in Vice City.

That’s exactly what happened to Guy A. Fortt, who is named in the GTA 6 credits. In fact, it’s been so long since he actually gave Rockstar his performance, he no longer remembers who he’s even playing.

“So, I was a motion capture actor on GTA 6, but I don’t know who the hell I am,” Fortt explained in a video. “I auditioned about four or five years ago, they put the balls all over my body and face I was [gesturing], and then I forgot all about it.”

“Then I get a random email telling me that I’m in GTA 6. This is where I need your help.”

He goes on to explain that he doesn’t play games and so doesn’t own a console, so he wants players to help him track down his character. Thankfully, he has a couple of clues to help the search.

“All I know is I was behind the counter, I was standing there. I know the leads came in, they were talking to me [and] I had balls and stuff all over my face, and I was talking to them about something,” he continued.

Based on this description, it sounds like he is some kind of store clerk. Perhaps he’s the owner of one of the many stores that Jason and Lucia can rob across Leonida.

The following day, he posted an update saying that three million players are currently on the hunt for his character, admitting he had “no clue” what Grand Theft Auto was or how big it was.

However, despite the community’s best efforts, Fortt’s character remains a mystery at the time of writing.
