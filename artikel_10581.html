Hero kitty saves mom from robber after daughter uses hissing cat as weapon

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A cat has become an unlikely hero after helping stop a robbery in Mexico when its owner’s daughter picked it up and used the hissing feline to scare off an armed suspect.

The incident took place on November 7 at a family-owned grocery store in Puebla, Mexico, where a man threatened a woman working behind the counter.

However, the would-be thief hadn’t counted on the family’s cat, Misifú, who quickly turned the tables on him.

According to Mexico News Daily, the suspect initially entered the store in the San Ramón neighborhood and asked a woman named Lidia about medication.

He returned minutes later wearing a mask and hoodie, demanding money from the register while threatening to use a weapon allegedly concealed in his backpack.

That’s when Lidia’s daughter rushed to help, grabbing the family’s cat and holding it directly toward the robber’s face.

Rather than staying calm, Misifú began hissing and swiping at the suspect with its claws extended, scratching at his hands as he tried to protect himself.

The unusual defense worked, with the man backing away before eventually fleeing the store empty-handed.

Security camera footage of the incident went viral after being shared online on November 18, showing the suspect retreating as the cat repeatedly lashed out.

According to local outlet Tiempo, the family released the footage in hopes of identifying the would-be robber, who reportedly remains at large.

The family has also called for increased police patrols in the area following the attempted robbery.
