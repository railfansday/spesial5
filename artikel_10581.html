24lb cat Sloppy Joe gets adopted and the internet is delighted

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

A chunky cat has gone viral after finally being adopted from a shelter in the United States, with the internet celebrating Sloppy Joe’s chance to enjoy life.

The internet loves animals of all shapes and sizes, but if you’re a fluffy sidekick that’s carrying a few extra pounds, well, you’re probably going to get a little bit of extra love too.

That has been the case with a 12-year-old cat known as Sloppy Joe. The cat, who weighs 24lbs, was taken in by the Humane Society of Chittenden County back in October and had been on the lookout for a new owner.

Well, on November 22, the animal shelter revealed that one of their own staff members had decided to adopt the big cat, allowing him to enjoy his “golden years” in a loving home.

“Congratulations to staff favorite Sloppy Joe! He stole all our hearts when he was surrendered to HSCC earlier this month, but none more than his office mate Sydnie!” they posted on Instagram.

“While living in the offices of our clinic scheduler, Sloppy Joe found himself a new best friend – and Sydnie brought him home this week. We’re so glad to know this special guy is in great hands as he continues to work on his weight loss journey in his golden years.”

A post shared by Humane Society of Chittenden County (@hsccvt)

Naturally, Instagram users were delighted by the news, with the post attracting more than 41,000 likes.

“This guy looks like he broke into a bakery and ate all the pies,” one joked. “I love sloppy Joe so much, so happy for him and his new owner!!” another added. “He’s already sooo loved! What a cutie pie,” another said.
