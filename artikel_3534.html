'DWTS' Season 35 Finalists Revealed After Jaw-Dropping Elimination

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The countdown to Dancing With the Stars finale night is officially on.

During the Tuesday, November 17, episode of ABC’s reality competition show, Ezra Frech, Maura Higgins, Connor Wood, Tatyana Ali, Harry Shum Jr. and Amber Glenn competed with their professional partners for a spot in the season 35 finale.

Each pairing had two full performances, with one ballroom and one Latin-inspired dance.

“Maura just exudes charisma,” Derek Hough shared at the top of the show. “Connor has been a huge surprise in this show.”

Bruno Tonioli added, “Ezra is a natural leading man. That guy is turned on to another level. Tatyana is America’s sweetheart. Her strength is becoming a storyteller through performance.”

As for Carrie Ann Inaba, she gave a shout-out to “ultimate showman” Harry and “powerhouse” Amber.

“You have to leave it all on the dance floor,” she proclaimed.

Despite plenty of perfect 10s, one pair had to say goodbye. Cohosts Julianne Hough and Alfonso Ribeiro revealed at the end of the episode that Jenna Johnson and Harry, 44, were eliminated just one week before the finale.

Keep reading to see all the scores from the semifinals and don’t forget to watch the DWTS season 35 finale — airing live coast-to-coast on ABC — on Tuesday, November 24, at 8 p.m. ET and 5 p.m. PT.
