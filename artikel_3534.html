How To Watch Avengers Endgame: Encore At Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

What if you could take the same movie that swept up the entire globe in hype and watch it all over again ... but, this time, with a little extra #brandsynergy? That was the (admittedly cynical) gimmick behind "Avengers Endgame: Encore," the buzzy re-release that ruled the box office again earlier this year and will no doubt do so on the streaming charts in the days and weeks ahead. The blockbuster, at one time considered the capstone to the grand Marvel Cinematic Universe experiment, now comes with a whopping four minutes of new footage meant to tie things together with the upcoming "Avengers: Doomsday." That includes a noticeably altered ending and new post-credits scenes, all of which will serve as a bridge to the multiverse-focused chaos to come.

That means you can expect to get your Infinity Vision on (no, we're still not quite sure what that means) with just a snap of your fingers, now with added scenes and new color grading.

Party like it's 2019, folks, because everything old is (slightly) new again. Back then, we had no inkling of a certain novel virus making its way across the globe, we had not yet had the privilege of seeing Palpatine somehow return in "Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker," and we certainly didn't think the MCU could ever top its grand finale of Earth's Mightiest Heroes assembling against the Mad Titan Thanos. Less than a decade later, Marvel Studios is hoping to keep the hype train going on the road to "Doomsday" with "Avengers Endgame: Encore."

In short, it's a good time to be a Disney fan ... and a Disney stockholder, for that matter. For those looking for an excuse to revel in some nostalgia, "Endgame: Encore" fits the bill. "Avengers: Doomsday" will finally arrive in theaters on December 18, 2026.
