Benson’s Next Major Law & Order: SVU Reunion Can Be A Lot More Satisfying Than Stabler

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Laura Hurley has been writing about the entertainment industry for more than twelve years, after a decade as a content producer and editor at CinemaBlend after writing for WhatCulture and Examiner. Attending events like SCAD TVfest and San Diego Comic-Con over the course of her career, she has been immersed in the world of network, streaming, and cable TV.

Law & Order: Special Victims Unit generated more hype than usual ahead of season 28 thanks to a major milestone: the 600th episode. The long-running drama brought Christopher Meloni back to reprise his role as Elliot Stabler for the first time since Law & Order: Organized Crime was canceled, and longtime fans could look forward to the highly anticipated reunion between Stabler and Mariska Hargitay's Olivia Benson. Unfortunately, their reunion was unsatisfying. The 600th episode rewrote elements of their past. Now, as season 28 continues, another reunion could prove to be much more meaningful.

Several reunions from Benson's earliest days with Special Victims will, sadly, be impossible. SVU handled Richard Belzer's real-life passing by giving Munch a moving tribute. Then, the show killed off Dann Florek's Don Cragen in season 27 (though Florek is still displeased about Cragen's death). After the 600th episode, it seems Stabler won't be back for a long time, if at all. Fortunately, SVU is bringing back somebody else from the old days to work with Benson again.

Alexandra Cabot, the first full-time assistant district attorney for Manhattan's Special Victims Unit, will return in an upcoming season 28 episode. While details about actress Stephanie March's return as Cabot are scarce, the confirmation that Mariska Hargitay will work with another veteran of SVU's golden years provides something to look forward to. There's no indication she'll appear in SVU's upcoming fall finale. It's safe to assume NBC will heavily promote Alex Cabot's return to Law & Order: Special Victims Unit when it arrives in the new year.

Even though Cabot wasn't Benson's longest-running ADA during her 28 years (and counting) in the Special Victims Unit, the two formed such a strong bond that many consider Cabot one of Benson's top love interests. They never canonically coupled up, so they can't claim a spot on any official ranking of Olivia Benson's love interests, but their relationship makes Cabot the perfect person to bring back in such a milestone season.

Plenty of time has passed to build anticipation for a reunion between Benson and Cabot. Nine seasons have gone by since Stephanie March last appeared as a guest star on SVU, so there's a lot to catch up on. Though fans can always revisit her years as part of the cast on Peacock, Cabot's departure left lingering questions, even now, nearly a decade after her last appearance on the show.

Olivia Benson has had seven love interests in Law & Order: Special Victims Unit, but only one of them can be considered the best of the bunch.

The stakes are also lower for their reunion than they were for Christopher Meloni's arrival as Stabler. Viewers are less likely to be unsatisfied with her return, no matter how it happens. If anything, a reunion with an old, less fraught friend could be just what Benson needs to realize that she should focus on her personal life instead of dedicating herself wholly to her job.

Benson's relationship with Stabler has been messy since season 1 in 1999; her relationship with Cabot was much healthier despite their occasional differences, and it's due for a revisit in 2028. The franchise hasn't spent years building up expectations for their relationship, not the way it did with Stabler. The first story to feature Stephanie March since season 19 could be exactly what season 28 needs to shake off the issues with the 600th episode.

When the news broke that Christopher Meloni was returning for the 600th episode, the odds seemed good that the installment would celebrate Law & Order: SVU's origins much more effectively than the 500th episode did. Whether viewers were rooting for a romance or just wanted to see the old partners investigating a case again, Stabler's return could have been perfect for Benson's 600th episode. Instead, the episode rewrote some of their foundational history.

The issues that made Stabler such a questionable cop by the end of his time in the franchise didn't cause problems at first, and their partnership was more solid than it was dysfunctional. The 600th episode presented his behavior as more problematic from the start than season 1 ever did, all to motivate Benson to reevaluate herself. Plus, the episode's ending felt much more like a goodbye to Stabler (and Meloni) than a fitting acknowledgment of a historic 600-episode run.

As much as Meloni deserved a better exit than the abrupt cancellation of Law & Order: Organized Crime, his big exit shouldn't have come at the expense of Benson's character in such a huge milestone. After all, Stabler hadn't been a series regular on SVU since 2011. It didn't help that the episode opened by revealing the two old partners hadn't spoken in a year and a half. Stabler even denied they were friends.

If it had been a setup for them to resolve their issues later in the season, the twist wouldn't have been a problem. As Stabler's final appearance in season 28 (and possibly his final appearance ever on SVU), it just wasn't satisfying. Reuniting with Cabot likely won't end in tears for Benson, and the two women will hopefully capitalize on their chemistry in their season 28 episode.

Stephanie March's episode of Law & Order: Special Victims Unit will be the ninth episode of the season. The fall finale will be the seventh, so Cabot will be back on our screens early in the new year.

Law & Order: Special Victims Unit season 28's fall finale airs Thursday, December 3, at 9 PM ET on NBC.
