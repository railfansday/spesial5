Roblox cracking down on fart games as viral hit disappears

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

A popular Roblox game about farting has been taken offline after attracting more than 13 million visits, with other similar games also reportedly disappearing from the platform.

The game, called Fart Together, allowed players to take part in multiplayer gameplay built around fart noises and toilet humor.

Despite its unusual premise, it had built up a substantial player base, reaching a peak of more than 14,000 concurrent players on November 7, 2026.

However, just days later, the game became unavailable, prompting speculation that Roblox has started cracking down on fart-themed experiences.

Roblox news account 4Lulzy highlighted the disappearance of Fart Together on X, claiming that other games featuring similar content had also been removed.

Roblox appears to be moderating games with gameplay revolving around farts.One game, Fart Together, had over 12 million visits before being deleted. Similar games were also wiped. pic.twitter.com/IXb3qgMp2k

The original listing for Fart Together, also known as Fart Together – Loud Farts, now displays a “Title Unavailable” message, preventing players from accessing the experience.

According to third-party tracking data, the game had accumulated more than 13 million visits before becoming unavailable.

Popular Roblox YouTuber KreekCraft also drew attention to the situation in a video, raising further awareness of the apparent removals.

However, it remains unclear exactly how many games have been affected or whether Roblox is specifically targeting experiences built around farting.

While several similar games have reportedly disappeared, Roblox has not publicly confirmed any new rules banning fart-related content.

The platform uses automated moderation systems and human reviewers to enforce its Community Standards, which govern the types of experiences developers can publish.

Games can be removed for violating these rules, although crude humor and fart-related gameplay are not explicitly prohibited across the platform.
