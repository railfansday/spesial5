Can BNB defend its crucial $500 weekly swing low before 2026 ends?

Binance Coin [BNB] presented an attractive trading opportunity. The crypto market, for the most part, has a strongly bearish bias.

The selling pressure that drove the two giants, Bitcoin [BTC] and Ethereum [ETH], below key psychological levels such as $60k and $2,000, had a considerable impact across the altcoin market as well.

Monday, the 23rd of November, saw heightened volatility and triggered $520.89 million in short liquidations alone across the crypto sphere, according to CoinGlass data.

It will come as no surprise, then, that the Binance Coin trade setup was also a bearish one.

The bearish price action that began in October-November 2025 eventually drove BNB back to the $500 lows. The last time BNB had been so close to this key support was in early 2025.

Yet, it could be revisited before 2026 comes to an end.

A bullish internal structure break (green) was seen, but the bearish imbalances put a halt to this rally. The imbalances BNB left behind during its descent from $933 to $570 in January-February 2026 acted as a supply zone.

Combined with Bitcoin and Ethereum weakness, the crypto markets stalled, helping establish a BNB correction back to the $537 swing low and potentially toward $500.

Technically, the swing structure was bullish and will remain so until a weekly BNB close below $500.

Zooming in, we find a bearish BNB structure. The fair value gap (red box) from $551.45-$576.39 was swept, helped by Monday’s volatility. Failure to break beyond $600 cemented the bears’ dominance.

It also established the next target- $503.75. On the way, the $530 zone, which had served as a local support in recent days, could temporarily stall the sellers.

The 1-hour chart aligned with the higher timeframes. A session close below $550.82 on this timeframe confirmed a bearish structure shift, and a retest of the $555 supply zone, another imbalance on this timeframe, and the subsequent rejection strongly signaled further downside.

An hourly session close above $562.19 would invalidate this sell setup, while the $503 and $500 remain feasible targets.
