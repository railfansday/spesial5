DP World Tour: Every result from the 2026 Race to Dubai season

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Five Rolex Series events remain the same - Hero Dubai Desert Classic, Genesis Scottish Open, BMW PGA Championship, Abu Dhabi Championship, DP World Tour Championship; New Zealand's Ryan Fox won the 154th Open Championship at Royal Birkdale in July for his first major title

Week-by-week results from the 2026 DP World Tour season, where Rory McIlroy claimed a record-equalling eighth Race to Dubai title.

The five Rolex Series events were the Hero Dubai Desert Classic (January), Genesis Scottish Open (July), BMW PGA Championship (September), Abu Dhabi Championship (November) and DP World Tour Championship (November).

New Zealand's Ryan Fox won The Open at Royal Birkdale in July, following on from major wins for Rory McIlroy (The Masters), Aaron Rai (PGA Championship) and Wyndham Clark (US Open).
