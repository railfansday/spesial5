As Advent calendars become increasingly popular, the team at Taste of Home continues to hunt for the best of the best. Nothing screams “the holidays” quite like decorating a Christmas tree. And while everyone likes to tackle the job in their own way, mixing it up with a new tradition is such a fun way to make lasting memories. That’s why we love the idea of decorating throughout the season with a new surprise ornament each day.

To help shoppers find the right fit, we’ve listed our favorite ornament Advent calendars below, spanning a range of interests. Happy countdown!
