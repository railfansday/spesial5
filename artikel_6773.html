Mark Ballas Reveals Dream Celeb Partner for Next Season of ‘Dancing With the Stars’

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Dancing With the Stars season 35 is just a few weeks away from wrapping up (Tuesdays are about to get a lot more boring), which naturally means we’re already fan casting season 36. And apparently we’re not alone. Mark Ballas just revealed the celeb partner he wants to be paired with next season, and it’s none other than his wife, BC Jean.

When asked about his dream partner by The Hollywood Reporter, Mark said, “I don’t know; my wife and I always joke, I’d love to do it with her. That’d be so fun. I mean, I don’t know. Maybe the record blows up enough at some point. We could do that together. That would be a riot. That would be that fun.”

BC Jean has performed songs for several of Mark’s performances with Maura Higgins this season, and Mark says having her in the ballroom has “been great,” adding, “She sang on the show a couple times in the past, which has been cool. When she came on to sing ‘Black Velvet,’ that was kind of a happy accident, because our original singer, Travis Garland, made it pretty far on America’s Got Talent, and the show overlaps. So last minute, BC [Jean] stepped in to save the day. It’s kind of a cool out-of-body experience to be hearing her voice as I’m performing, and there’s a sense of love and home and comfort there. We had her sing on the Lost Boys song as well for Halloween Night, which is really good. She’s my favorite-sounding voice ever. It’s a really, really, really cool moment.”
