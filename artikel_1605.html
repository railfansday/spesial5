CF: Instalments and repeatable bonuses – the exact figure Milan paid for Goncalo Ramos

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Today, more information about the structure and cost of Goncalo Ramos’ move to AC Milan has been revealed.

It’s fair to say that Ramos’ start to life with Milan has been poor. The Portuguese striker has not scored (or assisted) as many as had been hoped from a player worth a substantial amount and it’s fair to suggest that some have been disappointed with his time at San Siro so far.

However, the club will not be giving up on him anytime soon, considering he is their record arrival.

This evening, Calcio e Finanza have looked at the fee paid for Ramos – as they did for Diego Moreira recently – and picked apart both the fee, the bonuses and the structure of the payment, as MilanNews relays.

In terms of the fixed fee, the Rossoneri agreed on a deal worth €70 million, with an extra €3.1m in additional costs. This figure will be paid over the course of four years, with yearly payments scheduled on July 15 on each year – ending in July 2029 – and each payment will be worth €17.5m.

Then, there is €16m in bonuses that can be achieved, taking the total fee to almost €90m if all bonuses are achieved.

If Milan win the Scudetto, €2m will be owed; then there are several Champions League bonuses, which could be worth up to €5.2m if the Diavolo win the competition. Finally, if he is the top goalscorer in Serie A, another €2m will be owed to PSG.

These bonuses are capped at €16m and are repeatable, but they’ll only be redeemable if the Portuguese plays 60% of games in that season, unless he is the top scorer, where only the €2m will be owed.
