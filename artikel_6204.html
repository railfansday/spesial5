American Airlines Launches 9 New International Routes: See Full List Now

Fort Worth-based American Airlines is set to add nine new international routes in 2027, as the carrier expands its reach to Europe, Asia, and South America. The airline's first new service will be between Miami and Venezuela, while Chicago to Tokyo will be the first long-haul route added in March.

Using data from Cirium, an online aviation analytics company, the oneworld carrier will gradually add services in 2027 between January and late May, with a mix of the Embraer E175, Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner, 777-200ER, Airbus A321XLR and A321neo.

Nonstop flights from Miami to Barcelona, Venezuela will launch on January 15. The services to General Jose Antonio Anzoategui International Airport will begin three times weekly on the E175. This is the shortest flight in this analysis, and the only service with American Eagle-operated aircraft.

Tokyo Narita will be connected nonstop from Chicago this March. The airline will begin the daily 787-9 service on March 19; American will become the fourth airline on the route after All Nippon Airways (ANA), Japan Airlines (ending in late March), and United Airlines. American already serves Tokyo Narita from Dallas/Fort Worth.

Charlotte to Barcelona is up next, with March 27 scheduled for the airline's first flight between the North Carolina city and the Catalan capital. These flights will run daily on the airline's 777-200ER. These flights will join year-round services to Barcelona from Miami and Philadelphia, with seasonal additions from Chicago, Dallas/Fort Worth, and New York-JFK.

In 2027, American will leverage the long-range capabilities of the Airbus A321XLR to add European destinations to its network. This begins in March, with the airline adding nonstop flights from New York-JFK to Amsterdam. These flights are scheduled to run daily.

Porto is the airline's latest Portuguese connection, with the narrowbody aircraft able to fly nonstop from Philadelphia to Porto, a city famed for its downtown core UNESCO World Heritage Site. These routes are scheduled to operate once daily in each direction.

From New York-JFK, nonstop flights are set to begin to Nice, connecting the French Riviera to the Big Apple. These services will begin on May 6 and operate daily. The final A321XLR route to be added will fly between Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, and Vienna, Austria (VIE). This service will also begin on May 6 and operate daily.

American Airlines launched six new nonstop transatlantic routes in 2026, connecting US hubs with Edinburgh, Budapest, Prague, Athens, Zurich and Milan

American is set to return to the Levant, with the oneworld carrier scheduling nonstop flights from New York to Tel Aviv from March 27. These services are set to operate daily on the 777-200ER and will be the first time the airline has operated to the region since tensions saw major US carriers withdraw service. United and Delta have already resumed operations to Tel Aviv, with American the last of the US legacy carriers to return.

Nonstop flights to Iceland are the last of the nine international routes American plans to add in the first half of next year. Direct flights from Philadelphia to Keflavik, Iceland's primary international gateway, will take off on March 27 and operate daily on the A321neo.
