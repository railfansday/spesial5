20 Hours: Emirates' 11 Ultra-Long Airbus A380 Routes Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

His background spans airport operations, aviation infrastructure, and travel consulting, having worked with global organizations across New Zealand, Europe, and North America. Emirates, the world's largest operator of the Airbus A380 and Boeing 777-300ER, has a wide range of international routes. Its operations from one of the world's busiest international airports, Dubai International Airport (DXB), stretch to all six continents. The airline operates several ultra-long-haul routes, including one that exceeds 20 hours.

Emirates leverages its A380 to transport large numbers of passengers connecting through its Dubai hub, working within tight airport slot limits. The double-decker's capacity has become a flagship brand for the airline, as it operates more than 100 of the iconic superjumbos. Data for this analysis has been sourced from OAG, with flight times based on the winter 2026 timetable.

The longest route in the Emirates A380 network is the airline's daily one-stop service between Christchurch Airport (CHC) and Dubai. This service from New Zealand's second-largest city operates with a stopover in Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport (SYD). Being the only regular A380 service from Christchurch, the flight takes a total of 20 hours and 10 minutes block time, gate to gate.

This service remains popular as it allows passengers to experience the A380 on a trans-Tasman flight. Tickets are sold under fifth freedom privileges, allowing passengers to fly Emirates solely between Christchurch and Sydney, or, if necessary, on to Dubai.

Christchurch is the second New Zealand gateway for Emirates, with the carrier also maintaining year-round services from Auckland Airport (AKL). Nonstop flights from Auckland to Dubai operate daily with a flight time of 17 hours and 25 minutes. This route is Emirates' longest nonstop A380 service.

Emirates operates a one-stop service between Dubai and John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK), with a flight duration of 18 hours and 55 minutes, including a stopover in Milan, Italy. This marks the airline's second-longest A380 flight. The airline again takes advantage of the fifth-freedom privileges that allow the carrier to sell tickets solely on the Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP) to New York service.

Dubai to Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) comes in at fourth place, with its nonstop service to California having a total flight duration of 17 hours and 20 minutes. This is Emirates' second gateway in California, with the Dubai to San Francisco (SFO) flight time reaching 16 hours and 45 minutes. Return flights from San Francisco also feature in this analysis, with services back to Dubai reaching 16 hours and five minutes gate-to-gate.

Just ahead of San Francisco is Emirates' return to George Bush Intercontinental Airport (IAH). Its A380 flight to the Texan city has an estimated flight time of 17 hours.

Emirates is investing billions to keep its A380s flying, while Qantas accelerates its Superjumbos for retirement.

The 8th-longest A380 flight operated by Emirates connects Dubai and São Paulo/Guarulhos–Governor André Franco Montoro International Airport (GRU). Its connection to Brazil sees the double-decked aircraft fly for more than 15 hours and 30 minutes. This is Emirates' only A380 destination in South America.

Nonstop flights from Dubai to New York-JFK remain one of the longest flights in the airline's network. Flights to the Big Apple take a total of 14 hours and 35 minutes gate to gate. Nonstop services to Australia's largest city, Sydney, have the same flight duration, with the airline operating this route at the same time as its onward leg to Christchurch.

Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ) is the final service noted, with its nonstop A380 service taking a total of 14 hours and 35 minutes gate-to-gate. Emirates service to Toronto has operated since 2007, and the airline services the city pair with a daily rotation.
