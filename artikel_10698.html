Florida woman arrested after targeting ex’s Pokemon cards because he wasn’t giving her attention

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A Florida woman has been arrested after allegedly targeting her ex-boyfriend’s Pokemon card collection during an argument, before returning to his apartment for a bong and ending up facing felony charges.

Nadia Hussain, 23, was arrested in Orlando on November 7 following a bizarre confrontation involving Pokemon cards, a cellphone, and an interrupted 911 call.

According to an Orlando Police Department arrest affidavit obtained by Guessing Headlights, Hussain had spent the night at her ex-boyfriend’s apartment before the pair got into an argument.

Her ex, Jordan Bonilla, asked her to leave, but she allegedly refused, prompting him to begin recording the confrontation.

Police say Hussain knocked the phone from his hand, struck him several times, and repeatedly demanded that he “delete the video!”

She then turned her attention to his Pokemon card collection, which was sitting on the living room table.

According to WFTV, Hussain later admitted to police that she deliberately grabbed the cards after realizing how much it upset her ex-boyfriend.

She also told investigators she had been purposefully annoying him because she felt he wasn’t showing her enough affection.

Bonilla eventually escorted Hussain to her car before rushing back upstairs and locking his apartment door.

However, the encounter wasn’t over. Hussain returned, claiming she had left her bong inside.

Despite promising not to enter, she allegedly forced her way into the apartment when Bonilla opened the door to hand it over.

Police say she headed straight toward the living room before Bonilla blocked her path. She then entered a bathroom with a second entrance to the living room, leading him to believe she was attempting to reach his Pokemon cards again.

When Bonilla tried calling 911, Hussain allegedly struck his phone several times to prevent the call. Police confirmed dispatchers received a 911 hang-up from his number.

Neighbors eventually intervened and helped remove her from the apartment.

Hussain was arrested and charged with domestic battery and witness tampering to hinder communication with law enforcement, the latter being a third-degree felony.

Despite all the chaos surrounding the Pokemon collection, no theft charges involving the cards were filed.

It’s hardly the first bizarre incident involving Pokemon cards this year. In May, a Florida man was arrested after allegedly using a chainsaw to steal $12,000 worth of cards.

More recently, the FBI began investigating thieves using stolen credit cards to purchase rare Pokemon cards from trading card stores.

In Hussain’s case, there is no indication that any Pokemon cards were stolen or damaged, although the confrontation has left her facing criminal charges. The allegations have not been proven in court.
