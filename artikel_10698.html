Man fined hundreds of dollars after giving vape to quokka for photo

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

A man has been fined after a photo showing a vape being held to a quokka’s mouth on an Australian island sparked a police investigation.

The image, which, according to police, was taken on Rottnest Island off the coast of Perth on November 2, circulated on social media and prompted authorities to investigate the incident.

According to 7news, Western Australia Police traced the image to a 29-year-old man, who was subsequently issued a $200 (approximately $130 USD) infringement notice.

“Investigators tracked the image to a 29-year-old man, who was subsequently issued with a Rottnest Island Authority Infringement by police,” authorities said.

The photo appears to show an electronic cigarette being placed against the animal’s mouth, although it remains unclear whether the quokka inhaled any vapor or was simply biting the device.

Quokkas are native to Western Australia, and touching or feeding the animals is prohibited. Visitors who interfere with quokkas can receive an on-the-spot fine of $200, while prosecutions under biodiversity conservation laws can carry penalties of up to $10,000.

Western Australia Premier Roger Cook condemned the image, describing it as “disgusting.”

“Our quokkas are international drawcards for Western Australia and Rottnest Island because of their warm, welcoming nature,” he said.

“Any attempt to put quokkas at risk or harm them should be met with consequences.”
