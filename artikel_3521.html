RHOSLC’s Jen Shah Reacts to Seeing Elizabeth Holmes' New Documentary

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City alum Jen Shah is ready to see even more about her former prison pal Elizabeth Holmes.

“Thank you, @A24, for hosting a private screening of You Can See Everything for me and my guests on Friday,” Shah, 53, wrote via Instagram on Monday, November 16. “I watched it through the lens of my own friendship with Elizabeth. For everyone who’s been asking what I thought, consider this a little preview. You’ll hear from me soon. 🎬#YouCanSeeEverything.”

Holmes, 42, and her partner, Billy Evans, were the subjects of a nearly three-hour film, which followed their family in the 34 days leading up to the start of her federal prison sentence in May 2023.

In January 2022, Holmes was found guilty of three counts of wire fraud and one count of conspiracy to commit wire fraud against investors.

She is currently serving time at the Federal Prison Camp in Bryan, Texas, which is the same facility where Shah served nearly three years for her role in a nationwide telemarketing scheme. (The former Bravo star pleaded guilty in 2022 and was released in December 2025. Shah finished her home confinement in August.)

Previously, Shah’s rep shared a glimpse into how his client built a relationship with Holmes behind bars.

“They’re friends. They’re both rehabilitating and have bonded over being on this journey of positive change,” Chris Giovanni told People in September 2023. “Their situations brought them together, and they have a good understanding of one another. They’re getting through it together.”

Giovanni continued, “Jen’s given her a lot of advice. Jen gets all the ladies together, and they rally behind her while she teaches them fitness moves, and Elizabeth has been there right along with them.”

While watching You Can See Everything, Shah sported a yellow dress with a faux fur coat. She completed the look with knee-high leopard-print boots and a matching clutch.

“Movie night 🎬🖤 Thank you, @A24,” she wrote via Instagram while posing with her friends at the VIP screening. “#YouCanSeeEverything.”

After Shah served her time, some fans may be wondering if the reality star could ever return to RHOSLC.

According to original cast member Lisa Barlow, it may not make the most sense because Shah isn’t the closest with the current group of ladies.

​​“I want her to be happy. I want her to be thriving. I want her to be with her children and her husband and her mother and everybody else,” Barlow, 51, said during a July episode of the “Therapuss” podcast. “I think that’s important. I think she’s really sorry. She did restitution, and she’s paying for her mistake.”

“I think she talks to Meredith [Marks] every now and then,” Barlow continued. “She’ll call Meredith randomly, or Meredith will check in with her. But I don’t think anyone has a deep friendship with her.”
