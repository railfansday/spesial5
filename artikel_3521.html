Taylor Sheridan’s ‘Landman’ Is Officially Changing Its Winning Formula

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

When it comes to television, Taylor Sheridan has developed a rather impressive habit of keeping audiences entertained throughout the year. Between Yellowstone, Tulsa King, Lioness, and his ever-expanding collection of Paramount+ dramas, there's rarely a month when the filmmaker doesn't have something new for fans to watch. However, one particular series has made November its own over the past two years. Just one small issue. This year, we're going without.

Landman, Sheridan's enormously successful Paramount+ drama, will break its November premiere tradition for the first time in 2026, with Season 3 not arriving this month. The series debuted on November 17, 2024, before returning for its second season on November 16, 2025, establishing an annual release pattern that audiences might reasonably have expected to continue, but with production on Season 3 only beginning in Fort Worth, Texas, on October 1, time has run out.

In Landman Season 1, we’re dropped into the oil fields of West Texas, and we meet the players of M-Tex Oil, including boots-on-the-ground exec Tommy Norris (Billy Bob Thornton), CEO Monty Miller, Monty’s wife Cami, and Tommy’s earnest, entrepreneurial son Cooper. By the season finale, with an ailing Monty on the verge of death, Tommy assumes the role of M-Tex president – and is terrorized by heavies from a Mexican drug cartel.

In Landman Season 2, Cami steps out of her late husband’s shadow to assume control of M-Tex. Meanwhile, Tommy (who was spared a grisly fate by the cartel boss Gallino) juggles an ever-complicated work and home life, the latter of which now includes his father, T.L. Cooper sees his oil fortunes rise – and his love life with Ariana move toward marriage.
