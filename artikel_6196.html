The Best Hotels Near Disneyland Paris With Cinderella Suites, 'Toy Story'–Themed Rooms, and Quaint Cottages

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Located just 40 minutes from Paris Charles de Gaulle Airport, Disneyland Paris is the ideal place to begin a Parisian vacation or say au revoir before returning home. Disneyland Paris's two theme parks, Disneyland Park and Disney Adventure World, pair timeless charm with an ease you'll never find stateside. Here, you can sip Champagne in front of Sleeping Beauty's castle—or visit a dragon beneath it—while filling your days with delightful architecture, unprecedented thrills, and the wildest wilderness ride Disney's ever built (Big Thunder Mountain!).

There's also never been a better time to visit. Walt Disney Studios Park's March 2026 transformation into the new Disney Adventure World brought a drone-filled nighttime show, Avengers coaster, and even a Frozen-themed land featuring a lifelike Olaf robot to its once-faltering second gate. Now, between Fantasyland classics and futuristic favorites, there's something new and thrilling for the entire family to experience.

Of course, you'll need somewhere to rest your head at night, and that's where the best hotels near Disneyland Paris come into play. Staying at a Disney-branded property typically affords guests special amenities including ticket packages, exclusive character meet-and-greets, and Extra Magic Time, which provides bonus time in the parks prior to opening. These hotels are great options, but can also be pricey—luckily, there are also a few non-branded properties in the area that make for great alternatives. There's something here for everyone and every budget.

Unsure of whether you want to add something so Américain to your Paris vacation? As a theme park journalist who has visited all Disney parks worldwide, trust me: It's worth the visit. Even with so many cultural monuments in Paris, nothing is more delightfully uncanny than witnessing the United States section of It's a Small World. So, whether you're looking for glamour or simply a good location, read on for the very best hotels near Disneyland Paris.

Surroundings like these may sound quotidien to someone like Princess Belle, but the resort is as sweet as the rooms are spacious, making it an ideal choice for a hotel near Disneyland Paris as well as extended trips to nearby towns.

What area is the best to stay in when visiting Disneyland Paris?

Any hotel near Disney Village, the entertainment and dining district at the entrance to Disneyland Paris's two theme parks, is going to be the most convenient choice. This not only offers more ease and access, but affords you the option to shop and dine after the parks close each night. Still, booking nearly any Disney-operated hotel will give you benefits like Extra Magic Time and exclusive character encounters, which help get you even more out of your Disneyland Paris vacation.

Are there any hotels that are walking distance to Disneyland Paris?

Yes—but you might want to bring your sneakers. Disneyland Hotel is located directly at the entrance to Disneyland Paris, while Disney Hotel New York - The Art of Marvel, Disney Newport Bay Club, and Disney Sequoia Lodge are all a 10- to 15-minute walk through Disney Village to reach both parks. Disney Hotel Cheyenne is a further trek, about 15 to 20 minutes away, but absolutely doable. Shuttle buses are available as well.

What is the 3-2-1 rule at Disneyland Paris?

The 3-2-1 rule is essentially shorthand for how to streamline your Disneyland Paris experience. Experts recommend selecting three rides, two entertainment offerings, and one dining option for an easy, straightforward approach to planning a day at any Disney theme park. It works as well at Walt Disney World and Disneyland as it does here at Disneyland Paris, but I recommend you still do a little research before you go. Be sure you don't skip Phantom Manor, which can only be found in Paris, secure your dining reservations early, and don't miss the nighttime shows offered at both parks—they're among the best in the world.

It is best to stay within the park at Disneyland Paris, or outside of it?

It goes without saying that the closer you are to the castle, the “better” your hotel will be, but it shouldn't be a deal-breaker. Convenience is always a factor, but Disney-operated hotels at Disneyland Paris tend to be much pricier than their American counterparts, so staying a bus ride away might be necessary. Still, the perks are plenty across most of Disneyland's own hotels, including extra time in the parks, which is why I recommended so many of them.
