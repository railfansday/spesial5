As soon as you apply for a credit card, your application is processed by a computer. You’ll typically receive one of three responses: immediate approval, some form of “pending” status or denial.

Receiving instant approval doesn’t guarantee that you can begin using your card immediately. If you receive instant approval, you may be able to view your card number right away; otherwise, you’ll usually need to wait until your physical card arrives in the mail before you can begin using it.

It can take five to 10 business days to get a new credit card after approval. However, that timeframe varies by card and issuer. For instance, American Express offers overnight shipping for its top-tier cards. Some issuers offer expedited shipping on request, but there’s no guarantee. You may need to pay when requesting expedited shipping from some banks (including Barclays).

Getting approved for a new credit card is exciting, but waiting a week or more for it to arrive in the mail isn't. Fortunately, some issuers let you start using your new card immediately after approval, either by providing an instant card number or by allowing you to add your card to a digital wallet.

That instant access can give you a head start on meeting the welcome bonus spending requirement, since the clock starts ticking as soon as your account is approved — not when your physical card arrives. It can also come in handy if you need to make a purchase right away, whether you're booking travel or taking advantage of a 0% introductory APR offer. For cobranded store credit cards, you may also receive a discount on your purchases, starting as soon as you use the card.

Here's a look at which major credit card issuers offer instant card numbers, digital wallet access or both.

Some issuers provide an instant card number, while others, like Chase, instead let eligible cardholders add their new card to a digital wallet immediately after approval. Both options let you start using your card before the physical version arrives.

While there are too many cards to list that offer instant card numbers or access, here are six of our top picks.

American Express provides your new account number immediately after your online application is approved, provided your identity can be verified.

The instant card number will be the same as the physical card you’ll get in the mail, but the four-digit card identification number on the front of the card will be different. Because of that, you may want to wait to set up recurring payments.

Note that instant card numbers for some cobranded cards may only be used with the cobranded company (like Delta, Hilton or Marriott).

In addition to instant numbers for new cardmembers, Amex gives existing cardmembers access to their card information when ordering replacement cards.

Most Amex personal and business cards offer instant card numbers; notable exclusions include the Amazon Business Card and the Amazon Business Prime Card.
