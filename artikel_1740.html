Jamie Carragher makes surprise Liverpool claim after Manchester United rout as key concern revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Jamie Carragher believes Liverpool put in their best attacking performance of the season in Sunday’s 4-0 defeat of Manchester United - but he remains worried about Bradley Barcola’s output.

Mauricio Pochettino endured a nightmare return to the Premier League at Anfield. Leny Yoro was sent off in the eighth minute and United never recovered, with Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz all getting their names on the scoresheet.

Despite netting four against their great rivals, though, the Reds' attacking performance was far from perfect. They enjoyed the vast majority of possession on the day and created a huge number of chances, but slack finishing meant an embarrassing defeat for United did not become a more humiliating one.

Carragher, though, was brimming with enthusiasm as he spoke post-match, lauding what he considered to be the club’s finest attacking performance of the campaign.

“I think this season for Liverpool, the attack has been a struggle,” he began, speaking on the Gary Neville Podcast. “They haven’t created a lot of big chances when you look at the stats, or scored a lot of goals, especially at home.

“That’s the first time I’ve come away and looked at Liverpool as an attack and thought ‘ooh, I like that’. Not just one of them playing well, all four of them playing well.

“Liverpool have spent top dollar on their attacking play, so we should be pushing them to be a top team. They haven’t really shown that yet. Today I hope that is the first sign of a front four clicking together.”

Alexander Isak led the line at Anfield but was guilty of a dreadful miss as he shanked a shot well wide when one-on-one with Senne Lammens after the break.

While Carragher admitted the Swede should be disappointed to have come away without a goal, he insisted he was impressed with a performance reminiscent of his Newcastle pomp.

“I was a bit gutted for Alexander Isak not scoring, because he’s scored goals for Liverpool this season without really putting in a great performance, but his job is to score goals, no problem.

“But when I think of Isak at Newcastle, he scored goals, he was playing well, he was heavily involved. And we’ve almost got the flip of what we’re seeing this season.

“He missed some big chances but I’m not worried about that, because I know nine times out of ten, when he gets the chance he will score. I love the look of how sharp he was, how involved he was.”

Carragher was also impressed with Bradley Barcola, despite echoing the criticisms he often faced at Paris Saint-Germain that his game lacked goals. The winger only managed 39 goals in 152 appearances for the French heavyweights before moving to Liverpool in a deal worth up to £123m.

“Barcola is just absolutely electric. I don’t think he’s ever going to be a goalscorer.

“Will he be able to improve in front of goal? Maybe slightly. But we can see already some of the criticisms he had at Paris Saint-Germain in the past, about not scoring. You know, you spend that type of money, you expect someone to be able to finish when they have decent chances.”

Liverpool return to Action on Wednesday evening, visiting Club Brugge in the Champions League.
