Alan Shearer’s Premier League predictions including Arsenal vs Man City

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Alan Shearer is back with his latest round of Premier League predictions ahead of another huge weekend of action.

Arsenal host Manchester City on Sunday afternoon with Enzo Maresca’s side title hopes fading after a run of four games without a win.

Manchester United are sat in 19th place and host Brentford at Old Trafford in a huge game for Mauricio Pochettino after his debut in the dugout ended in a 4-0 humiliation at Anfield last Sunday.

Liverpool meanwhile make the short trip over to the Hill Dickinson Stadium for the first Merseyside derby of the season with Everton picking up just one win in their last 10 meetings with their neighbours.

As ever, England legend and Betfair ambassador Alan Shearer has revealed his Premier League predictions to Metro.

What a tough one to call. Big, big game for both clubs. Big game for both managers. We saw Forest when they went up against City, didn’t we? We thought, ‘Okay, maybe this is what we’re about to see in Liam Delap and that performance’. But then it went against them again last weekend.

Chelsea, I don’t know. I haven’t got a clue what’s going to happen because they just got battered into submission by Leeds and that would be the worry. I think this is such a tough one to call. I’m fascinated to see what Chelsea offer, particularly in forward positions again. I’m going to go for a draw. I don’t have any confidence in either. I think that would be a better result for Forest than it would be for Chelsea, so I’m going to go for a draw.

Chelsea are really missing Joao Pedro. He’s a top player. But they were still conceding goals when he was here. I know they were outscoring teams, but they were still conceding. They need him as soon as possible

I’m going to have to go for a Leeds win. However, it was a big win for Frank Lampard last week, but I think Leeds have got something going there and particularly at home with the crowd back with them. I think Calvert-Lewin’s playing really well. He’s having a really good season. He’s been a big player for them, and he looks very happy there and they’re getting the best out of him. We didn’t see that enough previously in his career. I think that would be a good piece of business for Leeds. I’m going to go home win.

I don’t see Leeds getting into the top four, but I do see them having a chance of European football, such as how they’re doing and the way they’re playing. Elland Road is a brilliant place.

Another tough one to call. There’s no way I could say a home win because there’s no evidence to back that up. So again, I’m going to go for a draw.

Man United need wins, they need to get out the bottom three. I guess what he’s looking for is some improvement. They had a bit against Sporting Lisbon this week, so he’ll want the improvement again and you can tell he’s trying everything, trying all the old managerial tricks in terms of trying to praise his players to get a bit belief in them because you can tell there’s none of that at the minute and all his words after the Lisbon game were how well they did.

All that is for a reason that’s trying to get some belief and confidence into his players, but let’s see how they react in a league game at home. The crowd will be with Poch and will be with the team to begin with. They then have to cut out the mistakes and give the crowd something to roar on, but I think Brentford will be tricky opposition.

I think Aston Villa will look at this and hope that they can get back to winning ways and I’m going to go with an away win just because of that. I think they’ve slipped up recently, but in terms of possession and shots and all of those things against Sunderland, it was one of those where they should have won the game. I think they’ll go to Ipswich and get the win.

I’m going to sit on the fence and think Everton will just try and make it a bit of a scrap in the hope they can nick something out of it. I’m a big fan of David Moyes, as you know, but I always tend to sit on the fence in derby games just because of the atmosphere and all of those things. I think if it was at Anfield, I would have gone for a home win, but because it’s at Everton, I’m going to go for a draw.

James Tarkowski is a big loss for Everton. I didn’t agree with the red card, to be honest, but I know what the game is now. He’s a big player for them, so it’ll be a big miss.

Honestly, I was sitting there thinking to myself, ‘My God, what is the game coming to when that’s a red card?’ It’s like, come on but it is what it is. We have to accept it.

Brighton absolutely battered Hull. The keeper was brilliant for Hull, got man of the match. He made some incredible saves. I just think if Brighton had a top-class centre forward, they could be one of the favourites for Champions League football this year. They’re playing some really, really good football.

I think I’m correct in saying I don’t think Newcastle have got a good record down there. I’m going let my head rule my heart and unfortunately I’m going to go for a home win. I’d love to see Newcastle go in with the same attitude as they did against Arsenal, try and press Brighton because you can’t let Brighton get going, particularly at home where they do play some good stuff all over the park.

I just hope Newcastle are brave and we see the second half performance that we saw against Arsenal but I am going to go for Brighton Because of the form they’re in, unfortunately.

Palace were a little really poor defensively last week against Coventry, and it doesn’t take a genius to work out what Frank has to do with them. Defensively they’re a mess and they’ve got some of the worst stats in the league. So, he has to stop that.

He has to somehow defensively make them harder to score against. I don’t know how he’s going to do it because he’s going to have to, again, very much like other managers or new managers, you’re going to have to try and get through to January and try and get reinforcements in there. I’ve got no belief in either team going there, to be honest, so again, I’m going to say a draw.

Marco Rose mixed the team up again in Europe and he is doing a wonderful job. Fulham, I thought were brilliant, defensively brilliant last week against Manchester City and thoroughly deserved their point, but just because of the form Bournemouth are in, and resting players on Thursday, I’m going go for a Bournemouth away win because of the form they’re in.

Sunderland were in Europe on Thursday and it will be interesting to see how much that will impact them at the weekend. It doesn’t look as if Brobbey’s going to be fit again. I guess if Brobbey’s fit, then I would go for a home win because I think defensively, he would cause Spurs issues, but if he’s not, then I don’t see Sunderland having enough to win. I’m going to say a draw again as well. Again, I’ve got no confidence to say that Spurs could go there and win. So, I’ll go for a draw.

Obviously, I watched Arsenal last weekend against Newcastle. Newcastle rattled them in the second half after they went one down under huge pressure, but credit to them, they came back and got the point, albeit from a terrible goalkeeping mistake. City are all over the place defensively and so easy to play through.

Napoli, who are struggling in Serie A and in Europe, scraped a draw. I’m going to go for an Arsenal home win. A really, really tight game, but I think Arsenal win.

Maresca is searching for ideas on how to stop them conceding and how to beef them up in midfield and protection. Mark Guehi, he’s having a terrible time defensively. He’s searching for ideas to try and stop that. He left out Fernandez last week to play Anderson, Bouaddi and Foden and he’s searching for ideas to try and halt what’s happening defensively in their team without any look at the moment.
