Aya Gold & Silver Inc. (AYA -6.66%) reported record quarterly results for the third quarter of 2026, characterized by significant increases in silver equivalent production and revenue. Management reported that the Zgounder Silver Mine has reached a stable production rate of approximately 6 million ounces per year, supported by record milling and mining throughput enabled by supplemental crushing capacity. The company has prioritized the development of the Boumadine project, where a preliminary economic assessment outlined substantial net present value and a 14-year mine life. Management stated that the company remains in a strong financial position with $183.8 million in cash, which will support the funding of future capital projects at Boumadine without immediate external financing needs. Furthermore, the company expanded its exploration footprint in Morocco by 50% through the acquisition of the Anti-Atlas and Tizi portfolios, broadening its exposure to precious and critical metals.
