Digi Power: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Its 2027 build-out is funded by customer prepayments and project debt rather than new shares, so expansion is tied to contracts already signed.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 13, 2026, Digi Power (DGXX +3.17%) announced that its transformation into an artificial intelligence infrastructure provider is taking hold as revenue grew 85% sequentially. The pivot from legacy Bitcoin mining (running specialized computers to validate cryptocurrency transactions) toward high-margin computing infrastructure and direct token sales fundamentally changes the company's long-term earning power. Three things management said stand out.

The company signed a 10-year take-or-pay contract (agreements requiring customer payment regardless of service usage) for colocation, or renting data center space and power, with artificial intelligence tenant Cerebras at its Columbiana, Alabama facility. The agreement provides long-term revenue visibility across its multi-phase build-out.

Through NeoCloudz, its cloud services brand, management launched the Token Factory platform to sell artificial intelligence inference (running completed models to process data) directly to customers. Operating on owned infrastructure avoids wholesale price discounting and yields high margins.

Management plans to fund 2027 capital expenditures (CapEx), or investments in physical infrastructure, through project debt (loans secured by specific facilities) and increased customer prepayments to protect equity value. This disciplined strategy ties expansion spending directly to contracted customer commitments.

Operational momentum is accelerating across data center capacity, and artificial intelligence revenue now accounts for nearly half of total sales. Owned power assets and colocation contracts anchor future value while management maintains strict pricing discipline.

Execution on upcoming delivery milestones remains central to strategy as industry power constraints intensify. Upfront customer prepayments provide the capital to expand recurring cash generation without expanding equity.
