Highlights: Area 51 helps San Antonio bounce back with a win over the Clippers

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The San Antonio Spurs needed to bounce back after a disappointing loss to the Detroit Pistons on Tuesday night. A back-and-forth contest with the struggling Los Angeles Clippers wasn't exactly the dominant response some might have wanted, but the Spurs pulled out a 124-112 win.

The Spurs needed leading performances from their stars to outlast the Clippers. As they have so many times before, San Antonio relied on Area 51, the duo of Stephon Castle and Victor Wembanyama.

Castle stuffed the stat sheet with 21 points, 12 assists, 6 rebounds, and 4 steals (and 8 turnovers, but that's another story). He was slinging the ball all over the floor, including on this full-court pass that led to a transition "shunk" by Wembanyama.

Yes, you read that right. Shunk. A shot and a dunk.

Wembanyama told reporters after the game that he has a blister that has been hurting his hand on dunks. So when he had the chance, he dunked the ball without touching the rim. So the shunk was born.

Castle's ability to draw two defenders is one of his best skills. Here, he jumps Kris Dunn's pass to Brook Lopez and streaks to the other end, drawing two defenders and kicks it to Julian Champagnie for a wide-open three.

Defense to offense was the name of the game for both teams on Thursday. The Spurs scored 19 points in transition and 25 points off turnovers.

Castle didn't just get it done on the break. He had a balanced game in the half-court, orchestrating the offense for the Spurs with Dylan Harper sitting out. His connection with Wembanyama was on full display on this lob, as he once again drew the attention of multiple players, freeing up the big man for an easy lob.

Wembanyama got to the rim more often than he did against Detroit. The big man had 31 points and 11 rebounds, scoring 22 of those points inside the arc.

Wembanyama added 3 assists, including this sweet wraparound pass to Keldon Johnson. KJ was a key contributor off the bench, adding 10 points, 5 rebounds, 3 assists, and 2 steals.

Perhaps the best play of the night came from another bench player, Harrison Barnes. The veteran got out in transition and rose, using all 6 feet 8 inches of his frame to slam the ball home over the springy shot-blocking Yanic Konan Niederhauser. The "unc" has turned back the clock a few times this season. San Antonio will need him as they wait for Harper and Tobias Harris to heal up and rejoin the lineup.

The defense, particularly down the stretch, helped the Spurs get back into the game. Devin Vassell was a key part of their defensive effort, racking up 3 steals in the win. This is just an all-out effort play. Vassell gets a difficult strip on Darius Garland, grabs the 50/50 ball, and dunks it home in transition.
