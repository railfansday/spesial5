AMD vs. Intel: Which Is the Better Artificial Intelligence (AI) Chip Stock to Buy Right Now?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Both companies have been experiencing solid growth in their data center businesses, but one of them seems like the better bet.

Advanced Micro Devices (AMD +13.26%) and Intel (INTC +5.49%) have benefited from healthy demand for their artificial intelligence (AI)-oriented chips, which are being deployed by major hyperscalers in data centers to handle both training and inference workloads.

Not surprisingly, both semiconductor stocks have delivered healthy gains to investors this year, despite the brutal sell-off they have experienced lately. While AMD shares have jumped 137% in 2026, Intel has clocked respectable gains of 60% as of this writing. However, their latest results clearly indicate that one of them is stealing a march over the other in the AI chip market.

We will examine AMD's and Intel's latest results and determine which of these two chipmakers is the better buy right now.

Intel's revenue from its data center and AI (DCAI) segment increased by 78% year over year in Q3 to $7.3 billion. What's worth noting is that the company's DCAI revenue growth has been accelerating in recent quarters.

However, AMD trumped Intel with a much stronger year-over-year increase of 97% in its data center segment to $8.5 billion in the previous quarter. So, AMD is now outselling Intel in the data center segment, and that's not surprising. After all, AMD sells both server central processing units (CPUs) and graphics processing units (GPUs). At the same time, Intel seems more inclined toward server CPUs after its Gaudi series of data center GPUs failed to find much favor among customers.

Meanwhile, Intel management noted on the latest earnings call that it expects server CPU industry shipments to increase by more than 30% in 2026 and 2027. AMD, therefore, is growing significantly faster than the end market, indicating it is taking market share from Intel.

Moreover, AMD's guidance clearly suggests that it is a better investment compared to Intel stock. The company anticipates a 66% year-over-year increase in revenue in the current quarter to $17 billion, at the midpoint of its guidance range. On the other hand, Intel's $15.5 billion revenue guidance points to a potential jump of just 13% from the year-ago period.

Even better, AMD is projecting revenue growth of more than 80% in 2027, which is more than 2x the company's long-term growth forecast. As such, it is evident that AMD is the better semiconductor stock to buy right now, especially given that it is cheaper than Intel on a valuation basis.

AMD stock trades at 30 times forward earnings, according to Morningstar. Intel is slightly more expensive at 35 times forward earnings. However, AMD's earnings per share (EPS) are projected to increase by 118% in 2027, well above Intel's estimated EPS growth rate of 20%.

Also, analysts have become significantly bullish about AMD's long-term EPS growth potential following its latest quarterly report.
