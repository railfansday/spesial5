The deals include compact inverter generators and larger backup models from Bilt Hard, DuroStar, DuroMax, Westinghouse, Champion, and Generac. Dual-fuel generators run on gasoline or propane, and tri-fuel models add natural gas.

Run this Bilt Hard dual-fuel generator on gasoline or propane, whichever is easier to get during an outage. It’s rated for 3,500 running watts on gasoline and 3,200 on propane, so plan your load around the fuel you’ll use.

Amazon’s other generator deals are below. Start with the appliances you need during an outage, then compare their running and startup watts with each generator’s output on your chosen fuel. The wattage in a product name is usually the peak rating, not continuous output.

Generator safety: Operate fuel-powered generators outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with exhaust directed away from buildings and openings. Never run one in a garage, even with the door open. Use working carbon monoxide alarms and follow the manufacturer’s instructions for protection from rain and snow. Have a qualified electrician install the appropriate transfer equipment before connecting to house wiring; never plug a generator into a wall outlet.
