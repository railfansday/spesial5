Introducing M-suite: Fast Company’s exclusive community for moms in senior leadership

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

What is M-suite? 

M-suite is a community and content series focused on women navigating senior-level careers and motherhood. We launched in late September of 2026.  

The community’s home base is our WhatsApp chat, designed to fit into the busy lives of our members. Membership also includes a members-only newsletter, a private LinkedIn group, and virtual events where members can connect and learn from one another.  

M-suite hosts regular virtual events, plus pop-up in-person gatherings in M-suite hubs and at events that are already frequented by our members. Our events are limited to M-suite members only.  

M-suite is also a content series within Fast Company. We cover news, ideas, and workplace issues shaping the lives and careers of working mothers. Our M-suite section is not limited to M-suite members because we think these issues are bigger than motherhood alone. Non-moms, colleagues, managers, and leaders need to be part of the conversation, too. 

What M-suite isn’t 

M-suite isn’t a forum for debating birth methods, breastfeeding, or parenting styles. We support all approaches to motherhood and want to keep the conversation focused on the intersection of motherhood and ambitious careers. 

How do I apply?

Visit our application page here.  

We take up to two weeks to reply. Be sure to whitelist our email addresses.  
 
Updates on the status of your application and community updates: community@fastcompany.com  

Newsletters from the team: newsletters@mv-news.fastcompany.com 

What are the requirements for membership?

We prioritize moms who are in senior leadership positions. M-suite requires an active Fast Company subscription. At this time, our memberships are limited to U.S.-based moms only.
