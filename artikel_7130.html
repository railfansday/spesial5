Technical Account Manager (APJ) at Supabase

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Supabase is the Postgres development platform, built by developers for developers. We provide a complete backend solution including Database, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime, and Vector Search. All services are deeply integrated and designed for growth.

We’re seeking a Technical Account Manager (TAM) to be the primary technical partner for our Team & customers after the sale, helping them adopt, operate, and get long-term value from Supabase.

At Supabase, TAMs are the technical owners of their assigned accounts, working closely with our most valuable customers to:

Understand each customer’s architecture, use cases, constraints, and goals.

Validate deployments and guide customers through onboarding and early success.

Proactively surface risks and optimization opportunities before they become incidents.

Coordinate internal teams on complex escalations and drive root cause analysis.

Align Supabase capabilities with the customer’s roadmap.

Successful TAMs are proactive, technically credible, and customer-first. TAM is a relationship, not a response channel: you’ll act as an extension of your customers’ teams and advocate on their behalf within Supabase.

You’ll be joining our Success team, where you will be expected to lead and innovate around the following areas of responsibility:

Act as the technical owner for your assigned accounts, documenting environments, mapping dependencies, and tracking technical risks.

Validate architecture and deployment plans after the sale, guide initial configuration and best practices, and make sure customers understand platform capabilities and limitations.

Help customers reach early success milestones to shorten time to value and reduce early friction.

Regularly review customer environments and usage patterns to identify scalability, performance, reliability, security, and compliance risks and opportunities.

Plan upgrades and lifecycle events, and run architecture reviews for upcoming initiatives, including capacity and growth planning, feature readiness, and early identification of technical blockers.

Coordinate across Support and Engineering on complex or high-impact issues. TAMs are not frontline support, but provide the context, prioritization, and follow-through that drive resolution, root cause analysis, and preventative action.

Serve as the voice of the customer to Engineering and Product, sharing specific, reproducible feedback on recurring pain points and taking part in beta and early access programs.

Partner with Sales and Customer Success on account health and retention, with technical input on renewals, expansions, and evaluations, and early flags on risks to satisfaction.

6+ years of relevant work experience in engineering, SRE, technical consulting, or similar, including operating production systems under real constraints.

Strong technical depth across database internals, scalability, security posture, and infrastructure tradeoffs. You understand a customer’s architecture as well as they do, without escalating every question.

A proactive mindset: you spot risks and optimization opportunities before the customer does, and you treat your accounts like a personal portfolio.

Sound escalation judgment: you know when to escalate, when to resolve internally, and how to communicate clearly under pressure without creating noise.

Very strong communication skills. TAMs explain complex issues to non-technical executives and root causes to senior engineers, often in the same day.

Awareness of how TAM performance shows up in retention and NRR, including surfacing expansion signals and renewal risks early.

Experience working in async or globally distributed teams.

We hire globally. We believe you can do your best work from anywhere. There are no Supabase offices, but we provide a WeWork membership or co-working allowance you can use anywhere in the world.

Every team member receives ESOP (equity ownership) in the company. We want everyone to share in the upside of what we’re building together.

Use this budget to set up your ideal work environment—laptop, monitor, headphones, or whatever helps you do your best work.

Supabase covers 100% of health insurance for employees and 80% for dependents, wherever you are. Your wellbeing and your family’s health are important to us.

Once a year, the entire company gathers in a new city for a week of connection, collaboration, and fun. It’s a highlight of our year.

We operate asynchronously and trust you to manage your own time. You know what needs to be done and when.

Every team member receives an annual education allowance to spend on learning—courses, books, conferences, or anything that supports your growth.

Supabase was born-remote and open-source-first. We believe our globally distributed team is our secret weapon in building tools developers love.

Over $1B raised (including our $500M Series F)

We move fast, build in public, and use what we ship. If it’s in your project, we probably use it in ours too. We believe deeply in the open-source ecosystem and strive to support—not replace—existing tools and communities.

New remote jobs delivered to your inbox, every week.
