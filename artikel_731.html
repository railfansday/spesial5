Signed: Jackson Wang Inks With WME; Beach Bunny Hops to Atlantic’s Shores

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Other recent signings include Silent Planet, Seb Lowe, KARLEE GIRL, Season 25's "Shotta's" and Dirty Projectors.

Singer, songwriter and entrepreneur Jackson Wang signed with WME. Among his other projects, the Chinese artist is currently starring in an Apple campaign tied to his new single and video “Thank You,” released in September. He continues to be represented by Team Wang Records and Roc Nation. Wang released his debut album, MIRRORS, in 2019 and has since collaborated with artists including Ciara, Swae Lee, Major Lazer, Galantis, Gucci Mane, Joji and GoldLink. He has also played U.S. arenas, including Los Angeles’ Kia Forum and Brooklyn’s Barclays Center. — Chris Eggertsen

Beach Bunny signed to Atlantic Records. The band’s debut single for the label is “Vinegar.” The group is embarking on a 31-date North American headline tour beginning next March, with a 13-date U.K./EU run opening for Weezer to follow starting in May. The name Beach Bunny refers interchangeably to lead singer Lili Trifilio and the band also composed of bassist/lead guitarist Anthony Vaccaro and drummer Jon Alvarado. Its most recent album, Tunnel Vision, was released last year.

“‘Vinegar’ is less a discussion and more a bubbling resentment directed at the 21st century mass commodification of everything,” said Trifilio in a statement on the new track. “The song was inspired by conversations shared with friends in soul-sucking jobs trying to make ends meet, fears around ai, and an ongoing frustration with mass consumerism, commercialization, and greed. It’s impossible to escape being observed, marketed to, and influenced, but it’s important to be aware and question if what you’re being sold is in your best interest.”

Beach Bunny released its first two albums, 2020’s Honeymoon and 2022’s Emotional Creature, on Mom+Pop before putting out Tunnel Vision via AWAL. — Chris Eggertsen

Check out more recent artist signings below.

Progressive metalcore band Silent Planet signed with Pure Noise Records, which will release the group’s upcoming album Infinite Sleep on Jan. 29. The first single from the album is “fangs!!” Featuring Jacob Charlton of Thornhill. It’s the band’s first original new music to be released since its 2023 album SUPERBLOOM. Silent Planet is currently touring the U.S. alongside Saosin, Like Moths to Flames and Save Us. It will hit Europe alongside Resolve in January, and Australia alongside Dayseeker, Jutes and sabe6 in February. — Chris Eggertsen

U.K. artist Seb Lowe signed to Nettwerk and released his new single “The Button.” Lowe is touring the U.K., EU and North America later this year and early next. — Chris Eggertsen

Pop singer-songwriter KARLEE GIRL signed with Interscope Records. She has released tracks including “Little Things,” “Right Back” and “What Now?” — Chris Eggertsen

London-based indie label Isekai Records and New York-based broke Records signed “Shotta’s,” the breakout rap/R&B single from Season 25. The seven-figure deal is believed to be one of the biggest single-song agreements in U.K. music history, according to a press release. Isekai and broke have an existing partnership to sign and develop emerging artists.

“Shotta’s” was released through TuneCore on Sept. 25 and gained traction via a snippet posted to Season 25’s TikTok account. The track has been on the U.K.’s Official Singles Chart for five weeks, so far peaking at No. 23. — Chris Eggertsen

Dirty Projectors signed to Joyful Noise Recordings and the band launched its own imprint, Well-Tempered Zealot. Its new single, “Jumbotron,” is the band’s first release since early 2025. The group currently features David Longstreth on piano, Chris Bear on drums and Karl McComas-Reichl on upright bass. Longstreth will embark on a solo headlining tour in the spring. — Chris Eggertsen

Xana, a Vancouver-based artist whose music is described in a press release as “alt-dark-pop,” signed to Nettwerk. The label will release her third studio album, Where Else Would I Go?, on March 19. Her new single, “Ii It Everything You Thought?”, releases on Friday (Nov. 20). She embarks on a tour of the U.K. and EU on April 16, and a tour of the U.S. on May 19. Her most recent album, The Sex Was Good Until It Wasn’t, was released in 2024. — Chris Eggertsen

Indie folk artist Laura Gibson signed with Fluff & Gravy Records in the U.S. and Loose Music in the U.K. Gibson released the single “Sylvia,” her first solo music since 2018, last week. She is currently at work on her next album. — Chris Eggertsen

L.A.-based guitarist and songwriter Kora Puckett signed with TODO Records. His first release on the label is the single “In My Veins,” while his debut solo album is slated for release next year. Puckett is a member of the band Narrow Head and currently plays in The Berries and Seko. As a songwriter and producer, he’s worked with Ethel Cain, Y2K, Lil Aaron, YUNGBLUD and more. — Chris Eggertsen

U.K. singer-songwriter Danny Griffiths signed to Glassnote Records, which released his debut single, “Lightweight Champion,” on Oct. 23. According to a press release, a demo of the song has been widely circulated. Griffiths has supported The Kooks, Jamie Webster and The K’s on tour, with headline dates slated through November and December and more to come next year. Griffiths is represented by Colin Schaverien and Stefano Anselmetti at Prolifica Management and by Charly Beedell-Tuck at Solo Agency Limited. — Chris Eggertsen

Singer-songwriter Joe Noto signed with David Fanning’s Nashville-based label Off Road Records. The New York-born and Florida-raised Noto moved to Nashville in 2021. He just released his debut song for Off Road Records, “Gotta Know About That.” — Jessica Nicholson

Nettwerk signed Southern California dance trio No Suits, composed of Saj Sanghvi, Max Carstens-Pierro and Reed Hallums. The latest release under the deal is the single “I Still Want You” featuring MAE. It was preceded by the release of tracks “Do or Don’t” and “Anyways.” — Chris Eggertsen

Singer-songwriter Naima Bock signed to Fire Talk/Breakfast Records. The first release under the deal is the single “Rocks.” She’s set to embark on an EU/UK tour next spring. — Chris Eggertsen

Alt singer, songwriter, guitarist and producer JT LOUX signed to Big Loud Rock, which released his new single “Killing Me Slow” on Friday (Nov. 13). The record was co-written with and produced by Stevie Aiello of Thirty Seconds to Mars. Originally from Sacramento, Calif., and now based in Nashville, LOUX will open for both Girl Tones and Low Cut Connie on tour dates this month in Philadelphia, New York, Washington, D.C., and more. — Chris Eggertsen

Loop Group signed Nashville-born singer-songwriter Connor McCutcheon for booking, label and artist services. Loop is composed of Loop Artist Services and North Loop Agency. The latter has already booked McCutcheon at two Country Thunder festivals taking place next year, along with Summer Sounds. McCutcheon released his latest single, “Dopamine,” on Nov. 6. — Chris Eggertsen

In De Goot Entertainment signed Nashville-based rock band Eddie and the Getaway (“Love Myself, “superNOVOCAINE”) to its management roster. The group’s new single, “Little White Lies,” comes out Friday (Nov. 20). The band is composed of lead singer Eddie Eberle, guitarist Mark “Bootsy” Stults and drummer Jack Silverman. — Chris Eggertsen

Position Music signed L.A.-based singer-songwriter Connor Kaufman (“Bleed”) to a joint venture label deal with Sounds Good and a solo publishing deal. According to a press release, Kaufman has generated more than 300 million global streams. — Chris Eggertsen

Equal Vision Records and kill iconic records signed Las Vegas post-hardcore band Amarionette, which will release a new studio album next year. The group was recently rejoined by original vocalist Quin White, who had been replaced by Issy Berry after his departure in 2018. The first release under the deal is the track “Panic!”. — Chris Eggertsen

L.A.-based artist and producer Dalhaus (Zeke Chabon) signed to American Death Records. The first release under the deal is his new single “Charlie’s Song.” Dalhaus is playing a run of L.A. dates this month. — Chris Eggertsen

L.A.-based singer-songwriter Hailey Picardi signed with WME for global representation in all areas. Her single “double edged sword” has racked up more than 29 million streams globally since its release earlier this year, according to a press release. In September, she made her touring debut as an opener on Bella Kay‘s The Reckless Tour. — Chris Eggertsen

Dominican artist La Greña, who has collaborated with Anuel AA and others, signed with Vydia in partnership with ShowOff Gang Music for the release of his upcoming album DIFERENTE, which drops Nov. 27. The lead single, “Todos Loco,” is out now. — Chris Eggertsen

Guitarist, singer and songwriter Mary Cutrufello signed to Hygh Tension Records. Her upcoming six-song EP, No One to Lie To, is preceded by a free download of “(Don’t Think of Me On) Sunday,” which fans can access by joining her mailing list. This marks Cutrufello’s first label release since 1998’s When the Night Is Through, released on Mercury Records. — Chris Eggertsen

New York multi-instrumentalist Kierst signed to Brooklyn-based indie Take Care Records. The first release under the deal is the single “Two Steps Back.” Kierst’s first release was the 2022 EP Thud, which came out via London’s Sad Club Records. She’s slated to perform at New Colossus Festival in New York City as well as SXSW next year. — Chris Eggertsen

Siberia-born saxophonist Valentin signed to Sony Music Masterworks. His debut album with the label is slated for next spring. Its first single, a recording of French composer Jean-Philippe Rameau’s “The Arts and the Hours,” is out now. — Chris Eggertsen

Reno, Nev.-based punk quartet Donkey Jaw signed with Hey!Fever Records, which released the band’s new single “No Vacancy.” The label will release a vinyl collection of the band’s previous singles alongside new tracks next year. Donkey Jaw is composed of Zack Ryan on lead vocals and guitar, Sean Brenker on guitar and vocals, Jared Obregon on bass and Koedus McKinley on drums. — Chris Eggertsen
