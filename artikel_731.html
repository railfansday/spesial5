UK confirmed as 2035 Women's World Cup hosts - which stadiums will be used?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The tournament will be the first football World Cup to be held in the UK since 1966; 48 teams will be involved, with matches played at19 venues across England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland

The UK has been confirmed as the host of the 2035 Women's World Cup.

The bid was unopposed after FIFA declared it the sole valid bid in April 2025 and ratified it at an Extraordinary FIFA Congress meeting on Monday.

It will be the first time the nation has hosted a football World Cup since the men's World Cup in 1966.

The 2035 edition will be the second tournament featuring 48 nations, following 2031, which will be hosted by Costa Rica, Jamaica, Mexico and the United States, whose bid - which also ran unopposed - was also confirmed to be successful.

The dates for the tournament are yet to be confirmed.

It will take place across 19 stadiums across 15 cities, with one additional host city and three additional stadiums for future consideration.

Presenting the bid at the FIFA meeting, Sky Sports News presenter - and non-executive FA director - Jess Creighton said: "Welcome to your Women's World Cup 2035. A tournament where we want every team, every fan and every nation to feel at home.

"Across the UK, football has always been about bringing people together - and that's why, in 2035, we'll welcome it the way football always has: by helping everyone feel at home.

"Our ambition is to deliver the most-watched FIFA Women's World Cup ever.

"We're predicting in 2035, there will be more than 3.5bn viewers, and over 4.5m tickets sold."

English Football Association chair Debbie Hewitt addressed the Congress after the bid's success had been confirmed.

"Thank you for your trust and confidence in awarding us the FIFA Women's World Cup 2035," Hewitt said.

"We're deeply honoured by your support and grateful for the opportunity to host the pinnacle of women's football on behalf of the global football family.

"As we said throughout our bid, we want this to be your FIFA Women's World Cup, a World Cup for everyone.

"Together, we will celebrate the power of women's football, inspire the next generation, and unite fans from every corner of the world. We look forward to welcoming you to the United Kingdom in 2035 and just as importantly, to working closely with you all in the years ahead to deliver an exceptional FIFA Women's World Cup that the entire football community can be proud of."

Prime Minister Andy Burnham said: "The world is coming to the United Kingdom. In 2035 the biggest tournament in women's sport will be played in cities across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, bringing visitors, investment and jobs to every part of the country.

"Our players have inspired a generation of girls. In 2035 that generation will watch the best in the world play on their doorstep - and some of them will be on the pitch. But the impact of this World Cup will go much further. It will reach into communities across the country, bring more people into the game and leave a legacy that lasts long after the final whistle."
