Fate of Mormon Wives: Orange County Revealed After Season One

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County—which stars Jen Affleck, Aspyn Ovard and more—will get a 20-episode second season after its successful debut.

Raise a glass of dirty soda for The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County ladies—because they’re not going anywhere.

After all, the Hulu series starring Bobbi Althoff, Aspyn Ovard, Avery Woods, Salomé Andrea, Jordan McClanahan, Ashleigh Pease, McCall DaPron, Madison Bontempo and Jen Affleck was just renewed for 20 more episodes.

“The OC Wives aren’t done making waves,” the show announced on Instagram Nov. 23. “Season 2 is coming soon to @hulu!”

And it didn’t take long for the cast to react to the news, with Affleck sharing a video on Instagram of herself lip synching to Kanye “Ye” West’s viral line, “Everyone wanted to know what I would if I didn’t win, I guess we’ll never know,” as she zoomed into a billboard with her castmates.

“Guess we’ll never know,” Affleck captioned the video with a winking emoji. “Season 2 coming 2027 @secretlivesonhulu.”

Ovard, for her part, shared a video to social media with Woods set to Fetty Wap’s 2015 hit “Trap Queen.” As she wrote on the screen in reference to the lyrics, “Everybody hatin we just call them fans tho.”

“Renewed for 20 episodes!!!” she added in the caption, “season 2 here we come @secretlivesonhulu.”

This season, Ovard shared an especially vulnerable glimpse into her personal life, opening up for the first time about her and ex-husband Parker Ferris’ eldest daughter Cove, 7, being diagnosed with terminal cancer.

"Everything happened so, just out of nowhere," the YouTuber said through tears during a confessional on the show’s Nov. 12 premiere. "One day she was OK, and then the next day she wasn't."

"At the hospital, you're like, 'It has to be fine,'" the 30-year-old—also mom to Lola, 5, and Elle, 2, with Ferris—continued. "You can't fathom that they're actually going to tell you that your baby has cancer, and that it's terminal. She could be gone in a month, we don't know. I've been living like that for over three years, just having no idea."

Affleck also had her fair share of difficult moments on the show’s first season, detailing how her estranged husband Zac Affleck gambled away their savings.

“He was being really weird,” Affleck, who is currently pregnant with their fourth baby and filed for divorce in early November, shared during a conversation with Althoff in the fourth episode of the Hulu series. “It seemed like he just wasn't being honest. So, I went to check our bank. The money's gone, and there's over $100,000 in there. So, I called him and I'm like, ‘Why is the money gone?’”

“He’s done this before multiple times,” she continued, “whether it was through online poker or it was through investing in crypto, I said, ‘You're not allowed to do that anymore.“

And that was her breaking point. As she recalled, “I said, ‘Yeah, I'm done. I'm divorcing you. F--k you.”

Until season two of Mormon Wives: OC gets an official release date, keep reading for more insight into their love lives…

After many ups and downs, Jen Affleck—who is pregnant with her and Zac Affleck's fourth baby—officially filed for divorce from her husband of seven years, E! News confirmed Nov. 4.

Aspyn Ovard, who filed for divorce from Parker Ferris in 2024 after eight years of marriage, is currently in a throuple relationship with married couple Billy Davis and Bri Davis.

Following her divorce from Cory Althoff, podcaster Bobbi Althoff started dating Tyler Hawkins, who plays a special role in Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County.

"He styles me, that’s how he supports me,” she told E! News in May 2026, adding that the basketball player, "picks out my outfits every day. He styles me every day for the show. It’s great."

However, Hawkins confirmed in a November 2026 TikTok that the duo had split two months prior.

Despite ongoing relationship speculation, Salomé Andrea and Aaron Brimhall are still going strong as they prepare to welcome their third baby together. The duo, who married in 2012, welcomed twins Luca and Rue in 2018 and daughter named Shiloh in 2026.

McCall DaPron—the sister of Mormon Wives' Mayci Neeley—shares four kids with husband Kirk DaPron, who she wed in 2011.

Ashleigh Pease and Nate Pease are rock solid after over a decade of marriage. "This 13th year together is one that has felt full circle!" she wrote on Instagram in September. "A season of fruits. Comfort. The happiest milestones."

Madison Bontempo and Kyler Steven Fisher, who wed in 2013, have been open about the highs and lows of their relationship. "Kyler and I aren’t perfect," she wrote on Instagram in October. "We’ve had arguments. We’ve had hard seasons. We’ve had moments we wish we could take back. And we’re not afraid to admit that."

But, she added, "Those moments aren’t our whole story. There’s also laughter, friendship, forgiveness, growth, five beautiful children, a million memories, and a love that has continued to evolve through every season of our lives."

Avery Woods and David Woods, parents to Ziggy and Stevie, are still feeling the love after a decade of marriage. As she simply put it, "my forever + always."
