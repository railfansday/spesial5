Longtime Exec Howard Lee Departs as Skydance Confirms New Cable Structure and Division Name Under Channing Dungey

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Skydance’s merged cable networks has a new division name: Skydance Global Cable Media. Warner Bros. TV Group and U.S. Networks head Channing Dungey also revealed the structure under the new entity, which combines the Warner Bros. Discovery and Paramount Global cable networks under one roof.

As part of that new organization, longtime exec Howard Lee, who had most recently served as chief creative officer of US Networks, is exiting after 28 years at Discovery. “One of the hardest parts of any transition is saying goodbye to talented leaders who have contributed so much and, just as importantly, have been trusted colleagues, friends, and mentors,” Dungey said in a memo to staff. “I want to express my sincere thanks to Howard Lee for his excellent leadership. I’m deeply grateful for the immense talent, commitment, and passion he brought to Warner Bros. Discovery’s US Networks over the years and wish him the best in his future endeavors.”

As part of merging a huge portfolio of more than 50 cable networks, Dungey divided them into four buckets: unscripted lifestyle, broad-based entertainment, talent/music/live and comedy/animation.

In unscripted lifestyle, Food Network prexy Betsy Sanner Ayala will add Discovery, HGTV and Magnolia oversight, with Discovery head of content Joe Boyle reporting to her.

In broad-based entertainment, TNT/TBS/ID president Jason Sarlanis will also now oversee TLC, with TLC head of content Alon Orstein reporting to him.

In the talent/music/live division, OWN president Tina Perry will add BET and MTV/VH1 to her oversight. Sitarah Pendelton will continue as head of content for MTV/VH1, Brian Rikuda will serve as head of BET, and Aisha Summers-Burke will continue as head of BET content. All three execs report to Perry. (BET Media Group head Louis Carr announced his exit on Monday.)

Nickelodeon and Nickelodeon Animation Studios, Adult Swim, Cartoon Network, Warner Bros. Animation, Cartoon Network Studios and Hanna-Barbera Studios Europe will now be part of Skydance Television Animation, overseen by president Michael Ouweleen. Ouweleen will also continue oversight of Cartoon Network and Adult Swim. Overseeing the studios assets (Nickelodeon Animation Studios, Warner Bros. Animation, Cartoon Network Studios, and Hanna-Barbera Studios Europe) will be Peter Girardi and Alec Botnick, both reporting to Ouweleen. Botnick will continue to oversee Nickelodeon programming with Laurel Weir. Meanwhile, Comedy Central oversight will remain with Ari Pearce, reporting to Ouweleen.

As for Lee, he first joined Discovery Inc. in 1998 as VP of Travel Channel Development; he later helped launch Planet Green, then moved to TLC, where he was first exec VP of production and development and later rose to president of TLC originals — before taking over as preident of Discovery Channel Networks and TLC in 2022. He was named chief creative officer of US Networks at Warner Bros. Discovery in Dec. 2024.

Lee developed and launched pop cultural institutions like “90 Day Fiancé,” “Toddlers & Tiaras,” “Sister Wives,” “Dr. Pimple Popper” and “Baylen Out Loud.” Under his watch, Puppy Bowl delivered its biggest audience in eight years and Shark Week posted its largest year-over-year ratings growth in over a decade.

As we bring together our renowned cable brands across Warner Bros. Discovery and Paramount, I’m pleased to share an organizational update that will help us leverage the full strength of our combined portfolio and position us for future success.

Our newly formed group will be called Skydance Global Cable Media and will encompass a premier roster of 50+ cable networks, including such iconic brands as Adult Swim, BET, Comedy Central, Discovery, Food Network, HGTV, ID, MTV, TLC, and Nickelodeon, among many others. Together, these brands deliver culture-defining entertainment, compelling kids and family programming, and leading lifestyle content to audiences around the world.

The breadth and quality of programming this team delivers is truly unmatched, from Adult Swim’s Rick & Morty to TLC’s “90 Day Fiancé” franchise, from Discovery’s “Puppy Bowl” and “Shark Week” to the Soul Train Awards on BET and the VMAs on MTV, our brands are bigger than television. They are part of our audiences’ daily lives in meaningful and unique ways, and I look forward to stewarding their legacies into the future alongside you.

As we look ahead, our focus is clear: create exceptional stories, unlock the full potential of our programming, franchises and brands, and deepen our relationship with audiences. The quality and reach of our programming, together with the extraordinary talent across our organization, give us a strong foundation for what comes next.

To help achieve these strategic goals, we have created four distinct segments within the group. I’m pleased to announce our new structure and creative leadership for Skydance Global Cable Media:

Unscripted Lifestyle: Betsy Sanner Ayala, currently President of Food Network, will continue in that role and also oversee Discovery, HGTV, and Magnolia. Joe Boyle will continue as Head of Content for Discovery, reporting into Betsy.

Broad-Based Entertainment: Jason Sarlanis, currently President of TNT, TBS, and ID, will continue in that role, and will also oversee TLC. Alon Orstein will continue as Head of Content for TLC, reporting into Jason.

Talent, Music, and Live: Tina Perry, currently President of OWN, will continue in that role and add BET and MTV/VH1 to her purview. Sitarah Pendelton will continue as Head of Content for MTV/VH1, Brian Rikuda will serve as Head of BET, and Aisha Summers-Burke will continue as Head of BET Content, all reporting into Tina.

Our fourth network group will be comprised of Comedy and Animation. Skydance Television is now the home of some of the most incredible animation networks and studios in the industry, including Nickelodeon and Nickelodeon Animation Studios, Adult Swim, Cartoon Network, Warner Bros. Animation, Cartoon Network Studios, and Hanna-Barbera Studios Europe. I’m pleased to say that all these beloved assets will now be together as part of Skydance Television Animation, and this group will be overseen by Michael Ouweleen, President. Michael will also continue with day-to-day oversight of Cartoon Network and Adult Swim. Reporting to Michael and leading the work overseeing the studios assets (Nickelodeon Animation Studios, Warner Bros. Animation, Cartoon Network Studios, and Hanna-Barbera Studios Europe) will be Peter Girardi and Alec Botnick. Alec will also continue to oversee programming on Nickelodeon along with Laurel Weir. In addition, Comedy Central will roll up to Michael, with Ari Pearce continuing day-to-day oversight of the network.

I’m excited about this new structure and feel confident that this incredible team of leaders will help defend our core business while building out a multi-platform strategy for these brands that will allow us to reach audiences wherever they may be. We are continuing to work through the process and will share more about the international business in the coming weeks.

One of the hardest parts of any transition is saying goodbye to talented leaders who have contributed so much and, just as importantly, have been trusted colleagues, friends, and mentors. I want to express my sincere thanks to Howard Lee for his excellent leadership. I’m deeply grateful for the immense talent, commitment, and passion he brought to Warner Bros. Discovery’s US Networks over the years and wish him the best in his future endeavors.

I know we have a lot of work ahead and I am excited to get started. With our unrivaled portfolio of brands, incredibly talented teams, and shared passion for making great TV, there is nothing we can’t achieve together.
