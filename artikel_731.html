Khloe Kardashian Says Travis Barker "Never" Sees Kourtney Kardashian

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Khloe Kardashian shared insight into a conversation she had with Travis Barker about how his wife Kourtney Kardashian spends her time: “He’s like, ‘I don’t see her ever.’”

Khloe Kardashian is shedding light on family dynamics.

The Khloe in Wonderland host shared a revelation she learned about her sister Kourtney Kardashian’s marriage to Travis Barker, claiming the Blink-182 drummer told her he doesn’t spend much time with his wife.

“Yesterday, I’m at the Get Real event. Travis is there [too] because he is announcing his documentary,” Khloe told sister Kim Kardashian on the Nov. 26 episode of The Kardashians. “So he’s like, ‘Just so you know, I am not the reason why you don’t hang out with Kourtney.”

“He’s like, ‘I don’t see her ever. I never see her. I don’t hang out [with her],’” the 42-year-old continued. “He’s like, ‘So I’m in your boat. You want to hang out with her? I want to hang out with her. So I’m on Khloe’s side.'”

Because of this, Khloe quipped that she’s curious about what Kourtney—mom to son Rocky Barker, 3, with Travis—does “all day long.”

But Travis—also dad to son Landon Barker, 23, as well as daughter Alabama Barker, 22, and stepdaughter Atiana de la Hoya, 27, with ex Shanna Moakler—expressed that his wife spends the bulk of her time with her kids, noting that she’s a “great mom.”

Following her conversation with the rocker, which Khloe described as “awkward,” the Revenge Body alum received a call from her sister.

And after Travis spilled the beans on their conversation, Kourtney made sure to set the record straight with her sister, emphasizing that while the public may think it’s Travis who fills up her time, that’s not exactly the case.

“’And I had to tell him,’” she told Khloe. “It’s what the public thinks. ‘It’s because of Travis.’”

Though Khloe didn’t disagree, she once again wondered what her older sister is filling her days with if she’s not with her husband.

“And I go, ‘But Kourt. We really need to talk. What do you do all day long?’” she quipped, recalling her exchange with the Lemme founder. “She just started laughing.”

Yet, ultimately, Khloe wants Kourtney—also mom to kids Mason Disick, 16, Penelope Disick, 15, and Reign Disick, 11, with ex Scott Disick—to find a way to juggle the many relationships in her life.

“I wish Kourtney was better at balancing multiple relationships and that’s fine,” she explained in a confessional. “Some people are, some people aren’t.”

“But it was never me singling out one person,” Khloe added. “She’s married—and first of all—she should be spending time with her husband. And I’ve told her that. It’s just about learning how to balance more than just one relationship.”
