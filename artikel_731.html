Dua Lipa, Tame Impala & SZA to Headline Coachella 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Daddy Yankee, Madonna, Phoebe Bridgers, Don Toliver, Zara Larsson, Weezer, Dom Dolla and many others are confirmed for next year's event.

Dua Lipa, SZA and Tame Impala will headline Coachella 2027, the festival’s reps announced on Wednesday, Nov. 11.

Also joining the lineup over its two weekends are Daddy Yankee, Madonna, Phoebe Bridgers, Don Toliver, Zara Larsson, Weezer, Dom Dolla and many more.

“Kill Bill” hitmaker SZA has previously performed at the festival, playing sets in 2016 and 2018, and making a cameo during Justin Bieber’s headlining set in 2026. Tame Impala spearhead Kevin Parker is also a Coachella veteran, having headlined the festival in 2019 after playing sets in previous years.

However, Lipa — whose 2024 Billboard 200 No. 2 album Radical Optimism featured Impala’s production on many of the songs — has never been on the lineup, having only ever taken a stage in the Valley as a guest performer during Martin Garrix’s set in 2017.

The 2027 iteration of Coachella will take place across two weekends on April 9-11 and April 16-18. Information on ticket packages is already available on the festival’s website.

Fans who miss out on tickets can once again watch from their devices, as YouTube returns for its 15th year as the exclusive livestream partner for both weekends.

Leading up to the 2027 headliner announcement, there’d been much speculation that Lipa and Impala would be leading the bill. Their desert takeover will come after Bieber, Sabrina Carpenter and Karol G headlined the festival in Indio, Calif., in 2026, a year that also saw sets from KATSEYE, sombr, The XX, Disclosure, Teddy Swims, Devo, The Strokes, GIVĒON, Addison Rae, Labrinth, David Byrne, PinkPanteress, Laufey and more.

The year before that, 2025 headliners Lady Gaga, Green Day, Post Malone and Travis Scott headed a lineup that also featured Missy Elliott, Benson Boone, LISA, FKA Twigs, GloRilla, Tyla, Artemas, Charli xcx, Anitta, Clairo, ENHYPEN, Jimmy Eat World, T-Pain, Megan Thee Stallion, Zedd, JENNIE, Beabadoobee, Ty Dolla $ign, Rema, Shaboozey and more.

Passes go on sale from Friday, Nov. 13 at noon PT. For more visit coachella.com and check out the full bill below.
