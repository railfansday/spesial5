Here’s how to watch Bills at Jets in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Buffalo Bills enter Week 10 with a record of 7-1, and have the current lead in both the AFC East and AFC overall. This afternoon, Buffalo will play just their second AFC East divisional matchup of the season, taking on the 2-7 New York Jets.

On paper, it's a matchup that heavily favors the Bills and their top-ranked scoring offense (32.0 ppg) that's also number-one in total yards per game (386.4 ypg). The Jets are much further down the ladder in most offensive metrics compared to Buffalo, but every NFL fan knows that divisional games are always a wild card.

This will be the 132nd matchup between the Bills and Jets in a series that dates back to September 11, 1960. Buffalo leads the all-time series 73-58. The series has recently belonged to the Bills, with them winning the last five games, dating back to November 2023.

The last game between Buffalo and the Jets was Week 18 of the 2025 NFL season, played on January 4, 2026 — a game the team from Western New York won 35-8, facing little resistance despite resting most of the team's starters. The Bills are 12-4 against the Jets since quarterback Josh Allen took over as Buffalo's starting quarterback in 2018.
