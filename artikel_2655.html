How to watch Dallas Mavericks vs Denver Nuggets: TV, live stream, info for tonight's game

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In their first of three meetings this season, the Dallas Mavericks and Denver Nuggets will face off Tuesday night at Ball Arena on NBC and Peacock.

Dallas (10-7) has won three consecutive after snapping a three-game losing streak.

Denver (10-5) also is on a three-game winning streak

The teams are tied atop the NBA Cup West Group A standings at 2-0. The winner of Group A could be decided by Tuesday's game depending on Friday's results.

Dallas relies on veteran point guard Kyrie Irving and emerging star forward Cooper Flagg, who has followed up last year's Rookie of the Year award by improving his scoring (24.2 points per game) and assists (6.4 per game) to lead the Mavericks in both categories.

Flagg, who turns 20 on Dec. 21, has the second-most points (1,812) and assists (406) by an NBA teenager (behind only LeBron James in both)

Irving, who missed last season while recovering from a torn ACL suffered in March 2025, has the most 40-point games (five) of any player under 20. After being sidelined for 19 months, Irving is second in the team in scoring (22.3 ppg) and assists (4.4 per game), and Dallas is 8-4 with him but 0-3 in games he's missed (losing each by at least 15 points.

Denver's dynamic combination of center Nikola Jokic and guard Jamal Murray has been one of the NBA's best pairs for the past decade — winning 416 games together since 2016. They have assisted each other more than 60 times, the most of any teammates in the league.

After becoming the first player to lead the NBA in assists and rebounds last year, Jokic's numbers have dipped, but he still leads the Nuggets rebounding (10.7 per game, down from 12.9) and assists (8.9, down from 10.7) while averaging 24.1 points per game.

Murray leads the Nuggets in scoring at 25.7 points per game after a career-high 25.4 average last year. He also is up in rebounds per game (5.2), field goal percentage (51%) and 3-point percentage (46%) after setting career highs in all but 3-point shooting last year.
