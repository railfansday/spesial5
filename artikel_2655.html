NFLSU Week 11 Report Card

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Week 11 of the NFL season is in the books. Here's how it looked from an NFLSU perspective.

For the Colts, S A.J. had nine tackles (four solo) and a pick returned for two yards. DE Arden Key had a solo tackle. For the Texans, G Ed Ingram played a clean game and played every offensive snap. WR Kayshon Boutte caught both of his targets for 40 yards and a TD. TE Foster Moreau caught his only target for eight yards. CB Derek Stingley Jr. had three tackles (two solo).

Chiefs CB Kristian Fulton had a pass defense and two solo tackles. CB Mansoor Delane picked up a solo tackle of his own.

Bears WR Zavion Thomas had one rush for thirty yards, two catches on three targets for 56 yards, and one kickoff return for 29 yards. Saints WR Barion Brown had two rushes for 0 yards and three kickoff returns for 79 yards.

The only former Tiger to play in this game was Panthers G Damien Lewis. He played a clean game and didn't miss an offensive snap.

Giants WR Malik Nabers caught seven of his 12 targets for 55 yards and a TD. Jaguars WR Brian Thomas Jr. caught one of his three targets for an 18-yard TD. DT Davon Godchaux had three assisted tackles.

Bills C Lloyd Cushenberry III played one offensive snap and seven special teams snaps. LS Reid Ferguson had a clean game. CB Tre'Davious White made his return from injury in this game and recorded a pass defense.

The only former Tiger to play in this game was Bucs TE Bauer Sharp. He failed to catch his only target.

The only former Tiger to play in this game was Titans CB Cor'Dale Flott. He recorded two solo tackles.

Jets TE Mason Taylor caught both of his targets for 25 yards. Chargers CB Donte Jackson added three solo tackles.

The only former Tiger to play in this game was Steelers LB Patrick Queen. He had four tackles (three solo) including a TFL.

The only former Tiger to play in this game was Broncos DE Sai'vion Jones. Despite playing 25 defensive snaps and six special teams snaps, Jones didn't record a stat.

The only former Tiger to play in this game was Vikings WR Justin Jefferson. He caught seven of his nine targets for 86 yards, added a one-yard rush and lost a fumble.

Heisman Bowl II was worth the hype. Before talking about the offenses, I can't ignore Commanders LB K'Lavon Chaisson's night in which he had two tackles (one solo), two QB hits and 0.5 sacks. Bengals QB Joe Burrow had another exceptional night through the air, going 28/40 for 300 yards and 2 TDs for a QBR of 108.3. He added two rushes for -1 yards, the first rush going for 0 yards on a failed fourth down conversion and the second a kneel for -1 to end the first half. WR Ja'Marr Chase caught five of his nine targets for 31 yards. However, it was Jayden Daniels who got the W once again. He went 23/35 for 289 yards and no TDs for a QBR of 91.2 while adding six rushes for 55 yards. This was a night of redemption after a disaster several weeks ago against the Giants. With 23 seconds to go and the ball at midfield, Daniels found Antonio Williams streaking across the field to get into field goal range. He got the team lined up and set, spiked the ball with five seconds left, and gave way to Drew Stevens' 26-yard walk-off winner. Both star QBs looked the part on a national stage, and it forces me to give this game a grade higher than A+.
