Cavs lose 124-121 to Grizzlies despite Donovan Mitchell’s 34 points

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Cleveland Cavaliers fell to the Memphis Grizzlies 124-121. Sam Merrill had an opportunity to tie or take the lead with a pair of free throws in the final seconds, and missed them both.

It was a high-scoring first quarter despite neither team shooting the ball well from behind the three-point line. Both the Cavs and Grizzlies shot below 30% from downtown in the opening quarter, but scored over 30 points each.

Donovan Mitchell led the way in the first quarter with 10 points on 5-for-8 shooting (5-of-6 inside two-point range). He's done a great job of getting downhill and applying pressure on the rim this week, such as in his 39-point performance over the Chicago Bulls on Monday.

Mitchell finished with 34 points tonight on 14-for-27 shooting.

The score was 72-71 at halftime. Taylor Hendricks, Cam Spencer and Cedric Coward combined for 31 points on a perfect 10-for-10 shooting in the first half. It was an offense-first type of game.

Points in the paint were key. Each team scored 34 points in the paint during the first half, with the Cavs shooting 17-for-21 from that zone and the Grizzlies converting on 17-of-19 attempts. Those aggressive attacks on the basket also led to 33 free throws between both teams in the first two quarters. So, even though it was a high-scoring game, it wasn't exactly high-octane.

The Cavs were without Evan Mobley, Peyton Watson, and Jaylon Tyson tonight.

The Grizzlies did not have Ty Jerome, who missed tonight's game with a right hip impingement.

Rookie Meleek Thomas contributed 14 points on 4-of-6 three-point shooting. His on-ball juice off the bench has helped Cleveland survive this stretch of injuries.

Mario Hezonja filled in for his first start of the season, posting 16 points, 7 rebounds and 5 assists on 7-for-12 shooting. Hezonja's been a nice pickup for the Cavs this season and has made it past the point where his contract becomes guaranteed this season — meaning Mario is here to stay.

Norchad Omier had another strong night on the glass, grabbing three offensive rebounds in the same possession as he continues to work his way into Kenny Atkinson's rotation.

The Cavs ultimately weren't able to get stops when they needed them. It was too easy in the paint for Memphis throughout the night, and that resulted in a loss for Cleveland.
