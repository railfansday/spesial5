Lions move up in NFC playoff picture following win over Patriots

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Detroit Lions are back in first place in the NFC North! The Lions won in Germany to move to 7-2 on the season. The Chicago Bears are on a bye, so they will remain 7-2. The Minnesota Vikings won to match them at 7-2. The three-way tie atop the division goes to the Detroit Lions because of their win over the Minnesota Vikings, making them 1-0 in the three-way head-to-head. The Vikings are next because they have a win over Chicago.

For the top spot in the NFC, the San Francisco 49ers are 7-1 and face the Dallas Cowboys Sunday afternoon. If the 49ers lose, they fall into a tie with the Lions at 7-2. Detroit will move into first place with a 4-1 record in the NFC while San Francisco will fall to 4-2 in the conference.
