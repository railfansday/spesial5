Brandon Aiyuk is a free agent after clearing waivers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Wide receiver Brandon Aiyuk is officially a free agent.

Aiyuk went unclaimed around the league after the 49ers placed him on waivers Monday, which means that he’s now free to sign a contract with any club. It was the expected result as any team claiming Aiyuk would also inherit his contract.

Aiyuk expressed interest in signing with the Commanders during his extended impasse with the 49ers and the path is now clear for that to happen. Given how long Aiyuk has been off of the field, it would seem likely that his stint with his next team will begin on the practice squad.

There’s no word about a deal at this point, but things can develop quickly now that Aiyuk is free to sign.
