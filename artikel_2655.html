David Bailey is first player with 9.5 sacks, six forced fumbles in first 10 NFL games

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Jets haven’t won a lot of games this season, but they picked a winner when they drafted linebacker David Bailey with the No. 2 pick in this year’s draft.

Bailey had two sacks of Justin Herbert in Sunday’s 27-24 win over the Chargers and he forced a fumble on one of those takedowns. That moved Bailey up to 9.5 sacks and six forced fumbles on the season, which makes him the first player to notch those numbers in the first 10 games of his career — Bailey missed one game due to injury — since sacks became an official stat in 1982.

“I’m not surprised and you guys ask me that all the time and I’m going to keep telling you the same thing. We expect that from him,” head coach Aaron Glenn said, via a transcript from the team. “I think he’s close to 10 sacks right now. The thing that’s really sticking out to me is the fact that he’s doing everything he can to get the ball back to the offense. He has maybe six or seven forced fumbles this year. I don’t know where that is as far as record, but it has to be somewhere up there. Listen, any time he’s on the field he gives us a chance for something big to happen. He’s a big-time playmaker. I call guys like him ‘pressure players.’ He’s able to make big plays at a time of need and he does a good job at that.”

Bailey is now the betting favorite to be the league’s defensive rookie of the year and he provides reason for optimism about the future amid another rough season for the Jets.
