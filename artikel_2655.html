Dak Prescott tosses five touchdowns in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dallas Cowboys quarterback Dak Prescott completed 30 of 51 passes for 343 yards, five touchdowns and one interception in Week 10 against the San Francisco 49ers, while running four times for seven yards and he was sacked once.

Why it Matters: Prescott did a great job against a stout San Francisco defense, as the Cowboys just kept scratching and clawing to stay in a wild 46-40 game. He has rolled up back-to-back 300-yard games, while posting 11 touchdown passes in the past four outings. He is a solid QB1 in all fantasy leagues, and he'll look to keep it going against the visiting Tennessee Titans in Week 11.
