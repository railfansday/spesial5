Senior Software Engineer II, Agentic Apps at Kit

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Kit is the email marketing platform experts choose to grow their audience, deepen their relationships, and build a business around the work they love. For coaches, YouTubers, authors, podcasters, and other creatives, there isn't a better marketing hub to rely on to grow audiences, automate email marketing, and sell digital products — all within one platform.More importantly, there isn't a team more committed to helping creators earn a living. We're on a mission to help creators earn $1 billion using our creator marketing platform. We have always been 100% independent and 100% remote.We are proud to have built a product that our customers love, and we look for people who have enthusiasm and belief in our mission, vision, and values to join our team. We're also embracing AI thoughtfully — both in how we build and how we hire to ensure our team is adaptable, innovative, and ready for what's next.

Creators use Kit's first-party apps and integrations to connect Kit to the rest of their business. Today we build and maintain these apps by hand, with help from an agency partner. That doesn't scale. We're looking for a Senior Software Engineer II to own app delivery and maintenance end to end, so a small team can support creators of every size.

You'll be the first engineer on the Apps squad. Your focus is shipping high quality apps at a high pace, and maintaining them to an industry-best standard. To get there, you'll take a systems-level view and build on Kit's coding agent. You'll work closely with our AI Developer Experience squad, which builds the coding agent. You'll own the creator experience of every app you build, and you're free to improve Kit's platform when that makes those apps better.

This is a full-time IC role for an AI-first, product-minded, full-stack engineer who has shipped agent workflows to production and judges them by reliable outcomes, not lines of code. You combine technical depth with leadership and extreme ownership. You take input from Partnerships, Product, and Design to help shape the "what," and you own the "how." You thrive in ambiguity, take initiative without waiting for permission, and elevate everyone around you through your generosity and optimism.

We're excited about first-party apps because they let creators do more with Kit than Kit does on its own. Our own apps should just work, and set the bar for every app built on Kit.

You'll report to John Bohannon, Senior Engineering Manager for the Ecosystem and Apps squads. You'll collaborate closely with a Partnerships Manager, Product Manager, Product Designer, and engineers on the Ecosystem squad. You'll also work closely with our AI Developer Experience squad on Kit's coding agent, and with a contractor who helps maintain our apps.

The Apps squad works as a sibling to Ecosystem. You'll set its rhythms, collaborate with our Partnerships Manager on which apps to build next, and join Ecosystem's cycle planning.

A peer onboarding buddy will help you learn our systems and codebase, and you'll have engineers across Kit for support and mentorship.

First Week: Set up your local environment and merge your first production code. Meet the team through Get To Know You's. Start the app handover with the contractor who maintains them today. Watch the recorded app walkthroughs and read the runbooks for our most-used apps.

First Month: Audit our first-party apps and integrations, starting with the apps that need the most maintenance. Measure what each app costs to maintain, including partner app reviews like those at Google, Meta, and Shopify. Learn how Kit's API, OAuth, and App Store support creators from small to large. Fix bugs and support escalations in the apps you now own. Learn how Kit's coding agent works with the AI Developer Experience squad.

First Three Months: Adapt Kit's coding agent to build your first apps and maintain existing ones. Give it the context, guardrails, and evals it needs. Turn what you learn into shared pieces every app can use, like a reusable load test, infrastructure automation, and app manifests. Write runbooks that let others work on any app. Drive each app's maintenance toward zero human time with the coding agent.

First Six Months: Ship new apps at a steady pace. Take over the rest of the app catalog and apply the coding agent to its maintenance. Show that one engineer with agents can maintain many apps at high quality. Define the metrics and dashboards for agent-built apps: launches, reliability, time to fix, and revenue impact. Share how agents build and maintain our apps, for example in a Lunch & Learn.

First Year: Kit ships more high-quality apps, faster, and the coding agent handles more of the routine builds and fixes, with you steering the system. The legacy integration model is retired. You use catalog metrics to recommend where the Apps squad invests next. You and the AI Developer Experience squad improve the coding agent together, so other Kit engineers benefit too.

Expert in agentic AI development. You've built agent workflows that ship and maintain production code, with evals, guardrails, and human review.

Strong in APIs, including OAuth, webhooks, and integrations with third-party platforms. Fluent in Ruby on Rails, or able to ramp into it fast. Full-stack, with React where an app needs it.
