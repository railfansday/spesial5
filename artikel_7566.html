Global Dimensions, LLC: Part-Time HUBZone Business Operations & Support Specialist

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Location: Remote – United States (Must reside in an SBA-designated HUBZone)

Schedule: Part-Time | 10 Hours per Week | Flexible Monday–Friday

Anticipated Assignment: January 18, 2027 – March 31, 2027

Compensation: Hourly compensation will be based on the applicable federal, state, or local minimum wage at the employee's primary work location. Where only the federal minimum wage applies, the hourly rate is $7.25.

Global Dimensions, LLC is a HUBZone-certified, Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) headquartered in Fredericksburg, Virginia. We provide mission-critical professional and technical services supporting federal government and defense operations.

Global Dimensions is seeking motivated, reliable, and detail-oriented individuals residing in SBA-designated HUBZone communities to join our remote business support team.

This temporary, part-time opportunity offers flexible scheduling, hands-on professional experience, and the opportunity to contribute to meaningful business operations supporting a federal government contractor.

As a Business Operations & Support Specialist, you will assist with administrative functions, business research, project coordination, and other corporate support activities.

Assignments will be based on individual qualifications, experience, interests, and organizational needs. Opportunities may include supporting Business Development, Human Resources, Recruiting, Program Management, Contracts, and Corporate Operations.

This position is ideal for individuals seeking flexible, part-time remote employment while developing valuable administrative, organizational, and business support skills.

Conduct online research related to federal contracting opportunities, government agencies, industry trends, prospective business partners, and professional contacts.

Organize and maintain business development information, research findings, spreadsheets, and internal databases.

Prepare, update, and maintain reports, presentations, business documents, tracking spreadsheets, and administrative records.

Assist with data entry, document management, records organization, and approved information-gathering assignments.

Support recruiting and human resources activities, including researching candidate sources, tracking job advertisements, coordinating candidate communications, and assisting with interview scheduling.

Assist with onboarding coordination and administrative tracking in accordance with company procedures.

Provide project and program support, including monitoring schedules, tracking deadlines, organizing deliverables, and preparing status updates.

Participate in virtual meetings, team communications, and special projects as assigned.

Prepare research summaries, checklists, progress reports, and other business support materials.

Perform additional administrative and operational support duties based on qualifications and company needs.

Must currently reside in an SBA-designated HUBZone and meet applicable residency verification requirements.

Previous experience in administrative support, office operations, research, customer service, or business coordination.

Exposure to government contracting, recruiting, human resources, business development, or project management.

Ability to manage multiple assignments, prioritize responsibilities, and adapt to changing business needs.
