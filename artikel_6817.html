Pamela Anderson, 59, Pairs a Classic Red Manicure With a “Bombshell Bob”

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The actress makes a case for minimal makeup and statement curls on the red carpet

In 2023, Pamela Anderson famously vowed to wear less makeup on red carpets. Since then, the actress has attended countless events makeup-free—but she has never left her hair behind. In fact, Anderson, known for her iconic long blond hair, makes her hairstyle the centerpiece, as she did last night at the 2026 Council of Fashion Designers of America (CFDA) Fashion Awards.

Anderson walked the CFDA Fashion Awards red carpet in her typical minimalist makeup look, but with “bombshell curls” inspired by old Hollywood. In a black halter dress with feather detailing, accessorized with a just-bitten berry lip, Anderson wore a coiffed bob with old Hollywood pin curls. Because the curls fell away from her face, they showcased her glowing, fresh skin.

Anderson’s retro hair wasn’t the only statement look last night. She wore fall’s boldest nail trend—a bright, cherry-red almond manicure—contrasting with her satin black-and-white polka-dot clutch. With no shortage of buzzy new nail trends swirling around the internet, like “chili pepper nails” and “vanilla nails,” Anderson proves that sometimes the best shade for your manicure is classic red.

Lately, Anderson has given a master class in the many ways to style bobs. Just a few weeks ago, while at the 2026 Academy Museum Gala in Los Angeles, Anderson wore a head full of curls with subtle bangs and lots of volume.

In an interview with Anderson last year, she shared her hair philosophy—why she never sticks to one style. “I think people associate me with big blond hair, but if I look at my whole life, I’ve always been one to change up the way that I look,” Anderson told Bazaar. “Your hair is another way to express yourself, and I’ve never been shy.”

She continued: “I guess I’m genetically blessed, but I’ve always had very thick, healthy hair. I do like to cut it and to style it differently. It’s freeing. I am a big lover of change; that’s what keeps us young and excited.”
