Ballet Flats Aren’t Going Anywhere—Here’s How to Make Them Winter-Ready

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The cold-weather styling trick that keeps your favorite flats in rotation.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Here at ELLE, we love our ballet flats. They’re cute, polished, chic, and of course comfortable. And even though the temperatures are quickly dropping, we’re not ready to give them up just yet. Enter tights, the unsung hero of the cold-weather wardrobe.

Paired with a ballet flat, you get a look that feels slightly Parisian, a little bit dancer-off-duty, and entirely wearable. This pairing is an easy way to refresh your cold-weather wardrobe, whether you’re heading to the office on a Tuesday or meeting friends on the weekend. And with hosiery getting a playful update this season, patterns, prints, and bold color are giving classic flats a whole new personality.

From the office to dinner to a lazy weekend stroll, these are the ways stylish women are pairing tights with ballet flats right now.

For a look that feels polished but playful, pair delicate lace tights with your favorite everyday ballet pumps. The sheer pattern adds subtle texture to the leg, while the flat silhouette keeps the whole outfit relaxed and easy to wear—even with a dressy tweed set.
