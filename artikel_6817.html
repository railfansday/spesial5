Don’t Skip Hoka’s Black Friday Sale on Its Best Sneakers

Including the Clifton 10’s and Bondi 9’s you’ve been eyeing.

No matter if you are just jumping on the Hoka bandwagon or you’ve been wearing them for years, you know that finding these shoes on sale in your size is akin to a holiday miracle. The brand’s lightweight and supportive shoes have won over runners, walkers, and anyone who stands on their feet for long periods of time. Our Esquire editors have a lot of strong feelings about which shoe is the best, but overall, if you want cushioned footwear, Hoka is the way to go.

The brand doesn’t do a ton of big sales, so the 20-percent-off deals that are happening now on Hoka.com are indeed noteworthy. There’s plenty of variety in colors and sizes available on most sneaker types, which isn’t usually the case. You’re lucky if one bright, garish colorway is discounted.

The Clifton 10 is one of the most popular models, and stock is already running low. But if you can get them while they are on sale, they are a favorite of deputy commerce editor Heath Owens. “As a multi-marathon runner, these are the shoes I reach for on my easy days,” says Owens. “The plush cushioning is undeniable, but it also has a pretty significant heel-toe drop—8mm—that gives them a rocker stride. It makes me feel a little taller too, which never hurts.”

For an all-around shoe, shop the popular Bondi 9, as it is great for running as well. If you’ve got flat feet or tend to have issues with overpronation, the Gaviota 6 might be a better go-to. Since the weather can be dicey for running this time of year, if you’re looking for a sneaker with better traction or that can handle some off-road trail runs, opt for the Challenger 8 while it’s on sale.

Again, this sale just started, and I’ve already seen select sizes and colors sell out, so don’t wait to upgrade your sneakers. And if you stumble upon flashier deals on Amazon, don’t trust them.
