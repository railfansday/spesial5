Unleash Your Kid's Fall Wardrobe With The Looney Tunes x SHEIN Collab

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Featuring Bugs Bunny, Tweety Bird, Taz the Tasmanian Devil, and more.

Looking to add a little nostalgia to your kiddo’s fall wardrobe? The Looney Tunes x SHEIN collection brings classic characters (like Bugs Bunny, Tweety Bird, and Taz) to a colorful lineup of playful pieces for babies, kids, and tweens.

Think graphic tees, coordinated sets, comfortable layers, and everyday staples that will seamlessly take your youngsters from the classroom to afterschool playtime, weekend outings, and cooler-weather adventures. (There are even some accessories, such as oversized scrunchies, you’ll want to steal for yourself!)

The best part? The Looney Tunes x SHEIN collab combines character-driven style with a budget-friendly price point. Plus, SHEIN continues to add new pieces to the collection regularly, so you can keep things fresh for your family each season.

Keep scrolling for our favorite fall picks from the collection.

This two-piece set includes a soft crewneck sweatshirt and sweatpants to keep your tot comfy and cozy this fall and beyond. The bright red color and graphic print are oh-so-sporty and stylish.

Three T-shirts are better than one. This set comes with three colorful graphic tees your kiddo will love. They can wear Taz, Tweety Bird, or Bugs Bunny shirts with jeans and sneakers to take them from school to a playdate, no change of clothes required.

Make bedtime fun with this bright Bugs Bunny green and blue striped pajamas set. The stretchy, lightweight material is great for fall nights when the temps change suddenly from warm to cool.

Your tween will look pretty in pink in this sweet pajama set with a girly print. The fabric is so soft, they won’t want to change out of it in the morning (and we don’t blame them!).

This sweatshirt is the ideal layering piece for fall, and the black and white colorblock is the ultimate in street style. Your child can throw it on over a tee and pair it with jeans and sneakers for a hip, sporty look.

Street style never looked so cute. This breezy pink graphic dress with a Bugs Bunny print is an excellent pick for mild fall days. A baseball cap and colorful sneakers complete the sporty vibe.

Dress your baby up in this nostalgic baby blue onesie. The long sleeves and loose, stretchy fabric make this an A-plus choice for unpredictable fall weather.

Oversized scrunchies are all the rage these days. Not only do they make a fun, eye-catching accessory for kids and adults, but scrunchies are gentle on your hair to boot. We won’t judge if you want to keep this scrunchie all to yourself!

Slip your feet into these fuzzy lounge slippers at the end of a long day. These bright yellow Tweety Bird slippers are incredibly plush. Wear them with or without socks and your favorite colorful PJs for an evening fashion moment.
