Atmos For Business: Earn Up To 23.5x Points On Alaska & Hawaiian Flights

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Atmos for Business is the small business rewards program of Alaska Airlines and Hawaiian Airlines, and it runs parallel to the Atmos Rewards program (Atmos for Business used to be known as the EasyBiz program).

Most major US airlines have a small business rewards program, and the intent is to offer extra rewards when businesses book travel. The good news is that even small businesses qualify for this, and it can really boost the rewards you earn.

Along those lines, in light of the recent refresh of the Atmos™ Rewards Visa Signature® Business Card (learn more), I think this program is worth a close look. Long story short, Atmos Rewards members could be earning up to 23.5x points per dollar spent on airfare. Based on my valuation of 1.5 cents per Atmos Rewards point, that’s the equivalent of a jaw dropping 35.25% return on eligible spending. That’s simply unrivaled.

Atmos for Business is the self-managed business travel program by Alaska and Hawaiian, intended for small and medium sized businesses.

The program is open to companies and organizations with at least $5,000 in annual travel spending, so if you’re a frequent flyer with the company and have a small business, that’s not much of a hurdle. You can add an unlimited number of employees to your account, and all of their spending counts toward that requirement.

Now, the terms suggest that you must have spent at least $5,000 or more on eligible Alaska and Hawaiian travel in the last 15 months, though in practice, that’s not directly enforced, best I can tell. After all, this is even intended to attract new businesses that may be looking to switch travel to Alaska or Hawaiian.

The total number of points that you can earn per dollar spent with Atmos for Business is pretty incredible, assuming you have the Atmos Rewards Business Card. As of 2027, Atmos Rewards members can choose how they get rewarded for flying, and they can choose to earn rewards based on distance flown, dollars spent, or segments flown.

As you can see, a non-elite member booking through the Atmos for Business portal and paying with the Atmos Rewards Business Card would earn 15x points per dollar spent, while a Titanium member booking through the Atmos for Business portal and paying with the Atmos Rewards Business Card would earn 22.5x points.

This doesn’t factor in the extra 1x points that the company earns, for a total of up to 23.5x points per dollar spent. Furthermore, it doesn’t factor in the 10% status points bonus, but that’s a different story.

While I’m going for top tier Atmos Rewards Titanium status, I still don’t fly with Alaska or Hawaiian often, given that I live in Miami, so it’s not the most practical carrier. Still, my goodness, the return on airfare spending is absolutely unreal here, if you are a loyalist.

To make sure there wasn’t any catch to all of this, I signed up for the Atmos for Business program. This was super straightforward — I just had to log into my Atmos Rewards account, and then fill out some basic company details.

This included the country my business is registered in, my business name, my tax ID, my phone number, and my address.

It was that easy, and I was registered instantly. The Atmos for Business program account management page looked mighty familiar, as it’s basically the same concept as the American AAdvantage Business program. There you can add travelers, manage payments, etc.

To actually book travel, you just select the “Book Travel” heading. That will bring you to a search that looks identical to what you’ll find on Alaska’s website, but it’s customized to Atmos for Business.

Pricing is identical to what you’d find directly on the websites of Alaska or Hawaiian, so that’s pretty tough to beat.

Atmos for Business is the small business rewards program of Alaska Airlines and Hawaiian Airlines. Even small businesses qualify, though the idea is that it’s for those who spend at least $5,000 per year on flights with the company across employees, which isn’t a very high bar.

The main advantage of Atmos for Business is that you earn one extra point per dollar spent, plus 10% bonus status points. But the area where the program gets really rewarding is if you have the Atmos Rewards Business Card, as that offers 10x points per dollar spent through Atmos for Business.

If you were an Atmos Rewards Titanium member booking through Atmos for Business and paying with the Atmos Rewards Business Card, you’d potentially earn a total of 23.5x points per dollar spent on airfare, with no caps. This is simply an amazing rate of return. Man, I’m really bummed that I can’t fly with Alaska or Hawaiian more, as that return on spending is something to get excited about.
