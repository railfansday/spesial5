Florida Passenger Barred By American Airlines—She Kicks Police, Spits In An Officer’s Face

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

An American Airlines passenger was arrested in Florida on nine charges after being denied boarding. Police say she refused to leave the Tampa airport terminal, chest bumped one officer, kicked another in the chest, and spat in a third officer’s face. Ana L. Gianaras, 34, of Grayslake, Illinois, was arrested around 3 p.m. November 8 in Airside F according to the affidavit. Guessing Headlights reported the arrest and obtained the police and court records. The booking photo comes from the Hillsborough County Sheriff’s Office: Police say Gianaras yelled, cursing and screaming and that her speech was slurred and her breath smelled of alcohol. She was sitting at a bar when, according to the affidavit, she got off her barstool and struck one of the officer’s chest with her own. That’s when officers say they tried to arrest her. She tensed up and tried to pull away as they struggled to handcuff her. During a search, she allegedly kicked another officer in the chest with a “reverse single leg kick.” Then, as another officer attempted to place her in the rear seat of a patrol car, police say she turned and spat in his face. Police say they found: Five cannabis cigarettes. Two plastic tubes containing suspected THC oil. 6.9 grams of orange pills identified as 30 milligram Adderall tablets. The affidavit reports preliminary positive field tests for the cannabis and oil and that she couldn’t produce a prescription for the Adderall. And once she made it to jail, sheriff’s deputies used a Taser but it’s not clear why. Her charges break down as: Six felonies: three counts of battery on a law enforcement officer, one count of resisting an officer with violence, and two counts of controlled substance possession involving the Adderall and THC oil. Three misdemeanors: disorderly intoxication, resisting an officer without violence, and possession of less than 20 grams of cannabis. She was booked at 5:21 p.m. on November 8. At her first appearance the next day, the court set bonds totaling $26,150. After two nights in jail, she was released November 10 on bond.
