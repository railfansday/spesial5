Every startup has expenses, whether you’re running a business from home or opening a brick-and-mortar store. For points and miles enthusiasts, the good news is that many of these expenses can earn valuable credit card rewards, from equipment purchases to eligible tax payments.

By putting eligible business expenses on the right credit cards, you can earn rewards on purchases you're already making while working toward welcome bonuses and annual spending thresholds. Just be sure you can pay off your balances in full and that any processing fees don't outweigh the rewards you earn.

Here’s a list of tips for maximizing your rewards and choosing the right credit cards for your business.

The right business credit card can help you earn rewards on everyday expenses while offering valuable perks like travel benefits and purchase protections. Whether you prefer cash back or points and miles, here are some of our top picks for businesses of all sizes:

These cards have different spending requirements and earning structures, so the best fit will depend on how much your business spends and whether you prefer cash back or transferable points and miles.

If you're starting a business from home, you can earn rewards on your personal housing expenses through Bilt. The loyalty program lets you earn Bilt Points by paying rent or your mortgage.

You can maximize your rewards with one of three Bilt credit cards:

You can transfer your points to Bilt's loyalty partners or book travel through Bilt's travel portal.

All three Bilt cards allow you to earn up to 1.25 points per dollar spent on eligible rent and mortgage payments without transaction fees. Under Bilt's Housing-only rewards option, your earning rate depends on how much you spend on everyday purchases with your card each billing cycle. If you spend the amount of your monthly housing payment(s) or more in everyday purchases on the Bilt Card, you'll earn 1.25 points per dollar on your rent or mortgage payment that month.

Bilt's housing rewards apply only to eligible residential properties, not to commercial office space.

Business expenses can add up quickly, especially when you're getting a company off the ground. The right credit cards can help you earn valuable rewards on purchases you'd make anyway, from office supplies and advertising to travel expenses.

Before choosing a card, consider which expenses account for most of your budget, whether you can meet a welcome offer's spending requirement and how much value you'll get from the card's ongoing benefits.

With the right strategy, you can turn everyday business expenses into valuable points, miles or cash back to put toward your next trip or reinvest in your business.
