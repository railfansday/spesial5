Which 'OMITB' Stars Are, Aren't Returning for Season 6 After Major Changes?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Only Murders in the Building is returning for season 6 — but not every star will be making a comeback after surprise changes.

The hit Hulu series, which premiered in 2021, follows Mabel (Selena Gomez), Oliver (Martin Short) and Charles (Steve Martin) as they solve mysterious deaths that take place in their New York City apartment building. Season 1 explored the death of Tim Kono (Julian Cihi), the sophomore season uncovered Bunny Folger’s (Jayne Houdyshell) murderer and season 3 featured Ben Glenroy’s death as the main mystery (Paul Rudd). Season 4 then picked up with Sazz’s (Jane Lynch) death, with season 5 focusing on Lester (Teddy Coluca).

When the show returns in December 2026, Cinda Canning’s (Tina Fey) death will be investigated after her podcast covered a woman who is the main suspect in the murder of a royal descendant.

News previously broke that the show would move to London for season 6. The series was even retitled Only Murders in London before it was confirmed that the series would then return to New York City.

Deadline reported in September 2026 that writers will relocate from Los Angeles to the East Coast for a potential seventh season. While the show hasn’t been officially renewed yet, it is reportedly expected to return amid ongoing success.
