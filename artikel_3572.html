Dane Cook, 54, and Wife Kelsi, 28, Expecting Baby No. 1

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Comedian Dane Cook and his wife, Kelsi Cook, are becoming parents in 2027.

“We’ve been bursting with excitement to share this news! 🤍,” the couple wrote via Instagram on Tuesday, November 24. “1 Samuel 1:27.”

In the social media post, Kelsi, 28, confirmed she is pregnant while holding sonogram pictures. In another photo, Dane, 54, can be seen cradling his wife’s baby bump.

“I’m going to need some To the Future baby sizes,” Dane wrote via his Instagram Story while referencing his merch line. “Feeling so incredible.”

The exciting news comes weeks after the Netflix release of his new documentary, Funny Money: The Dane Cook Story, and two months after the couple celebrated their third wedding anniversary. In honor of the milestone, Dane shared a heartfelt post to his wife on social media.

“It’s been three years ago today since we got married on that beautiful evening in Hawaii and they have been nothing short of an adventure. The best kind of journey,” he wrote via Instagram on September 23. “From our always hopeful good mornings to our peaceful good nights (well unless the dogs are on one) — you’re the best. Thank you for the simple reminders that we are blessed.”

Dane continued, “Everything this year was because of your inspiration. Especially the documentary — it would not be here without you! Happy Anniversary to us ❤️.”

Dane and Kelsi’s love story began in 2017 when the pair started dating after a surprise meeting during game night.

“We met at a game night I host at my place,” Dane told an inquiring fan during an Instagram Q&A. “We were friends for a while & soon after, fell in like with each [other] and then upgraded to love.”

Although the couple’s  26-year age difference has made some headlines, Dane assured fans that he and his wife remain unbothered by any chatter.

“Sometimes I actually find myself saying, ‘Where has she been all my life?’” Dane said during a 2018 appearance on Jimmy Kimmel Live! “And then I realize she wasn’t alive for the first 26 years of it. People are like, ‘You’re robbing the cradle.’ I was like, ‘She hasn’t slept in the cradle for, like, nine years. Relax.’”

All jokes aside, Dane told host Jimmy Kimmel that Kelsi is “the kindest, sweetest, just my favorite — my best friend.”

That sentiment can be seen on social media, where Dane frequently gushes over his wife.

“None of what’s in my life today exists without this lovely, loving woman standing right here, firmly by my side,” he shared via Instagram on September 19. “We champion each other, fight for each other, and we laugh a lot. I’ve said this before: Kelsi has a savage / silly sense of humor. We’ve had the greatest bunch of years.”
