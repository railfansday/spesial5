WoW: Forever Weekly Player Numbers Are The Highest For Blizzard’s MMO In 15 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

World of Warcraft: Forever continues to break ground for developer Blizzard, which revealed new stats related to Forever's already record-breaking launch.

Forever launched on November 4, and it didn't take long for Blizzard to announce that the game, the third major version of Blizzard's MMORPG, was the biggest launch in WoW history in terms of concurrent and returning players.

Now, Blizzard is revealing more launch stats. Holly Longdale, executive producer and vice president for WoW, announced on social media that Forever has "more people playing weekly than we've seen in World of Warcraft in 15 years."

The tidbit translates to Forever having more weekly active players than any WoW release since the game's 2010 Cataclysm expansion. That's quite the accomplishment considering that time period also includes massively popular expansions like 2016's Legion and the release of WoW Classic in 2019.

That wasn't the only statistic Blizzard had to share. It revealed that more than 15 million characters have been created in Forever, with nearly 1.5 billion quests completed so far. It also shared the game's deadliest boss, Archmage Arugal from the Shadowfang Keep dungeon, as well as nearly 29 million PvP kills and almost 197 million player deaths. While fun, Blizzard's stats don't reveal the numbers that more than a few players are likely curious about, like Forever's most popular races and classes. For that, players have to rely on third-party fan sites that have tracked Forever statistics since launch.

Forever's launch may be breaking records, but that wasn't enough to score the MMO a nod at The Game Awards 2026. In other WoW Forever news, Blizzard recently made yet another big change to dungeons.
