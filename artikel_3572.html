Xbox Project Helix Is A Console That Plays PC Games, Not The Other Way Around

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Xbox has revealed more details about its next-gen device, Project Helix, describing it as a "console capable of playing games from your PC."

Prominently featured in the Xbox 25th Anniversary Magazine, VP of next-gen Jason Ronald said the Xbox Series X|S successor is designed to build on the successes — and hard lessons — of previous Xbox generations, with fewer barriers to play.  "When we think about Project Helix, it's really designed to be the ultimate gaming machine--one that removes all the barriers of play that have historically existed," Ronald said. "My entire library is accessible to me. I'm able to bring four generations of my Xbox library and progression with me and have it available instantly. And we remove the arbitrary constraints between console and PC games."

Ronald added that the Project Helix family of devices isn't purely focused on traditional next-gen themes like better graphics, but on thinking ahead to create "innovative" experiences. "We don't think the next generation is defined by 8K, or 240 hertz, or some of the traditional metrics--it's really about gameplay innovation," Ronald explained. "We're very clear on the design goal: Helix is designed to be a console that is also capable of playing games from your PC. It is not a PC that can also play console games. The goal is making it easy, intuitive, and instant to access my games. But if you're a hardcore gamer or a power user who wants to have that deeper level of customizability across settings and performance options, you can absolutely go do it."

Project Helix will also let "hardcore" gamers customize their gaming experience, but how much will all of this cost? Xbox isn't revealing a ballpark figure for Project Helix yet, but Ronald did mention the company is aiming to balance state-of-the-art gaming with an "affordable" price tag. Game preservation will also play a key role, letting users bring their Xbox library with them, and Ronald teased Project Helix as a brand that spans multiple facets of gaming, from living room experiences to handheld sessions and cloud streaming. As a sign of things to come, the Xbox mode on the recently released ROG Xbox Ally was mentioned as the direction the gaming division is headed.

The news on Project Helix comes at a time when Xbox has had several highs and lows throughout 2026. This year saw several first-party games release and earn plenty of acclaim, while the anniversary celebrations included a special-edition Xbox Series X that few people could get their hands on — for retail price, that is. At the same time, the rise of Asha Sharma as Xbox CEO has seen scores of jobs and studios slashed from the Xbox family as the company undergoes a "reset" aimed at making it more profitable.
