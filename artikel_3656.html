Shannon Beador Reflects on Her Dog Archie’s Death in Heartbreaking Post

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Shannon Beador shared a poignant post as she remembered her beloved late dog Archie ahead of the holiday season.

Posting via her Instagram on Friday, November 20, the Real Housewives of Orange County star, 62, shared a carousel of photos featuring Archie, who died in May.

“I’ve been wanting to post about Archie but it has been very difficult for me to put anything together. And as Thanksgiving approaches, it’s important for me to acknowledge how grateful my girls and I are for Archie’s presence in our lives,” Shannon wrote in the post’s lengthy caption..

She continued, “Archie passed away five days after our finale party was filmed. It happened so quickly. I was grateful that Stella was able to come home from Paris to say her goodbyes.”

Shannon went on to recall Archie’s final moments before he passed away shortly after his brain cancer diagnosis.

“Archie had been in the hospital and was so drugged, he couldn’t even stand up. We took him off all the medication and he spent his last day doing all of his favorite things,” she divulged. “Archie went to the beach, ate every type of food that he loved, and spent time sitting in the park.”

In May, Shannon shared the sad news of Archie’s death, which came nine days after the Bravo star announced her pet had been diagnosed with brain cancer.

“Heaven has a new special angel. Archie passed away yesterday and the girls and I are heartbroken. For the last 10 years, Archie wasn’t just my dog — he was my constant comfort, protector, best friend and truly the real “man” in my life,” she wrote.

Shannon continued: “I have been through so many highs and lows, and through every good time and every heartache, Archie was always by my side and loved me the same way every single day. Never judging me and always giving pure, loyal, and unconditional love.”

The reality star thanked her followers for their well wishes and added, “There is such a void in our household right now.”

In her earlier posts, Shannon, who adopted Archie in 2016, explained she was looking into treatments for Archie.

“I know my posts about my dogs are usually fun and silly, but today I wanted to share something more serious,” Shannon shared on May 5. “After experiencing multiple seizures, Archie has been diagnosed with brain cancer.”

She continued, “Our heart aches for our sweet Archie. He means the world to the girls and I. We are going to do everything we can to help him fight this.”
