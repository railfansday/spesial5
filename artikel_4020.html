EA Sports FC 27 Is Using AI To Add The Voice Of Late Commentator John Motson

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

EA Sports FC 27 will today add the voice of the iconic commentator John Motson, who sadly passed away in 2023.

This marks the first instance a dead commentator will be added to the game, and EA has used a combination of old recordings and AI to make it happen. In fact, senior game design director Mat Prior states that this is "the only way" that Motson could be added, since there are no recordings of him talking about modern players like Chloe Kelly and Bradley Barcola.

Motson's widow and son have given their blessing to include the new commentary, and Prior insists that the team wouldn't have proceeded without permission:

"There was no way we would have moved forward with this in any way, shape or form if they weren't fully on board. At the outset we wanted to pay tribute to to Motty as the commentator and as this emotional connection - he was the very first voice of our game - so we really wanted to do him justice and bring him back."

Motson's son, Fred, firmly believes that his father would have wanted to be involved in the game after his death, stating "My dad was the least technological person in the world. Till his dying day, he had a typewriter on his desk. So I don't think he would have quite understood what the AI was doing technologically - but I think, on the other hand, he was always really keen to get involved in these kinds of projects."

John Motson was the first commentator for the series, appearing in every entry from Fifa 96 to Fifa 2005. He also make a brief appearance in FIFA 19. His family were paid for his voice and likeness, but specific financial terms have not been disclosed.
