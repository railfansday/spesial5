Three Games Are Confirmed For Xbox Game Pass To End The Month (November 24-27)

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Here's what's on the way to close out the month

With only a handful of days left to go, November is well and truly coming to a close. Before we whip out the Xmas deccies, we've still got another three Xbox Game Pass titles to come over the next few days.

Starting off with XGP Premium members being treated to not just one, but two new additions to the mid-tier subscription. MOTORSLICE is making the move from Ultimate, whilst Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts 2 is joining all but Essential on November 24.

The only "day one" game is coming just three days later, when Arkheron, a "fast-paced, clip-worthy team PvP game" joins Ultimate and PC Game Pass. It looks like a vibrant one if MOBAs are your cuppa.

Admittedly, it's a bit of a quiet end to November, but we'll leave you with a closer look at each of these games below.

Parkour through the ruins of a megastructure, climb massive bosses, and hunt down every construction equipment in this slice of life action-adventure with immaculate vibes.

Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts 2 now includes ‘Butcher’s Banquet’: a major expansion which introduces an entirely new and expansive region - The Temple - complete with all-new objectives and some of your most deadly contracts, yet.

Arkheron is a fast-paced, clip-worthy team PvP game where the items you equip dynamically change how you play. Grab your friends, loot Relics that change your build mid-fight, and climb a haunted Tower floor by floor. If you've played a shooter or a MOBA, your aim and your instincts already work here. The builds, the counters, the hundred ways a fight can turn all boils down to your adaptability and skill.
