Xbox Anniversary Weapon Skins For Gears Of War: E-Day Are Now Free To Claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Celebrate Xbox's 25 years by chainsawing Locust grubs

Xbox's 25th anniversary is just around the corner, and to celebrate the massive milestone, The Coalition is giving away a free translucent green weapon skin bundle in Gears of War: E-Day.

If I'm being honest, it's not massive news, but it's a nice freebie for E-Day players. We're feeling a little merry for Xbox's anniversary on Sunday, so why not? Here's a look at all 12 weapons with the 'Xbox Anniversary' skin pack equipped.

The skin looks lovely across all weapons, but I'm particularly fond of how it looks on the Ripper and Longshot. If you'd like to claim the bundle for yourself, simply boot Gears: E-Day, head to the Store and claim it for free.

Gears joins Halo: Campaign Evolved and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 in celebrating the quarter-century milestone with a translucent green skin. My favourite has gotta be that Xbox M4 skin in MW4, but seeing as this is an E-Day freebie, you best believe I'll be rocking the Xbox Anniversary bundle in Versus.

In fact, Xbox recently announced a last-minute restock of Xbox Series X25 consoles on the Microsoft Store. Meanwhile, Gears of War: E-Day Season 2: Legacy begins December 1st, bringing with it a whole host of fan-favourite maps, character skins, and more.
