SBI Solana [SOL] Global has officially kicked off full-scale operations! What are they building, and why now?

SBI Holdings and Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group (SMFG) are now officially in business with the Solana Foundation. On Wednesday, the three confirmed that SBI R3 Japan has been turned into a joint venture. Its new name is SBI Solana Global, and it’s fully up and running.

The company will handle financial assets, RWAs, stablecoin payments, and on-chain assets. SBI Solana Global wants to help issue and distribute tokenized bonds, funds, and loan receivables.

It will build payment systems for transfers inside Japan and overseas, and explore agentic micropayments.

Here’s a bit of background though. SBI’s crypto exchange, SBI VC Trade, listed USDC in March 2025. This June, SBI and Startale launched JPYSC, a stablecoin backed by the yen.

The Solana team-up itself started in July. What’s changed now is SMFG. Having one of Japan’s largest banking groups in the venture gives it a fair amount of weight.

The news is big for Solana. For the price, however, not so much.

SOL was valued at $68.71 at press time. In mid-October, it was above $100. In fact, on 23rd November, it went up to $78 for a short while before sellers dragged it right back down.

The RSI had a reading of 27.9, still in oversold territory. It has stayed above its average for most of the past week, with the same better than mid-November… when it sank to close to 19.

The DMI also seemed to be similar. The ADX had a reading of 47.9 and fell from its recent high, indicating that the downtrend may be losing some strength. Sellers still seemed to be at the front though, with the -DI at 32.7 against the +DI at 14.9.

This implied that the gap has shrunk in cpmparison to a week ago.
