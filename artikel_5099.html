Analyst: XRP Below $1 Revives a 70% Bounce Bet

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Home » Trendsetter News » Analyst: XRP Below $1 Revives a 70% Bounce Bet

XRP’s dip below $1 has revived hopes of a roughly 70% rebound, with one analyst citing a similar August rally.

XRP could repeat a roughly 70% rebound from below $1, according to a crypto commentator who points to an August rally as the template. In a new YouTube episode, Zach Rector argues that another sharp recovery is possible, though the case rests largely on a historical chart comparison rather than a confirmed catalyst.

The chart discussion puts XRP’s latest low near $0.89. The analyst says an earlier slide to $0.98 in August was followed by a rise toward $1.70 within four or five days.

“I’m not here to guarantee that that’s coming again,” Zach Rector says, before overlaying the earlier price pattern onto the latest decline. The analyst also suggests XRP could revisit $2 quickly, without committing to a date.

The explanation centers on leverage: the host argues that downside liquidations have largely cleared, leaving market makers an incentive to drive prices higher. The transcript provides no liquidation figures, order-book data or positioning metrics to substantiate that view.

That distinction matters. A previous bounce illustrates how sharply XRP can recover, but it does not establish that selling pressure is exhausted or rule out another decline.

The more consequential adoption discussion concerns the analyst’s claim that 1,700 institutions, corporations and enterprises use Ripple solutions. Rector explicitly acknowledges that there is no breakdown showing how many use products involving XRP.

Zach Rector also defends Ripple’s emphasis on its RLUSD stablecoin, arguing that greater stablecoin liquidity could attract activity to the XRP Ledger.

A statement attributed to Old Glory Bank supports offering both XRP and stablecoins, with regulated stablecoins potentially better suited to payments where businesses require certainty of value.

Still, stablecoin issuance on the ledger should not be confused with evidence that every payment requires XRP as an intermediary. The YouTube video does not establish that connection, despite the host’s bullish interpretation.

Zach Rector also identifies a proposed lending protocol as a potential source of collateral demand.

For crypto enthusiasts, these are separate propositions to track: a short-term rebound based on chart resemblance, and a longer-term investment case that depends on measurable network activity, liquidity and actual XRP usage.
