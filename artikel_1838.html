Where to watch Italy vs France: TV channel and live stream for Nations League today

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

France can secure top spot in Nations League Group A1 by winning away in Italy tonight.

Zinedine Zidane’s first games in charge of France have produced three wins and one draw - the only game where they dropped points coming at home to Italy in the reverse fixture last month when Michael Olise’s second half goal was cancelled out by Alessandro Bastoni.

Thanks to UEFA tiebreaker rules, France have not yet secured their spot in the next round, but a draw would be enough for them to advance to final eight.

Conversely, hosts Italy could guarantee a top-two spot with victory, alleviating some pressure on their last game - a trip to Brussels to face Belgium, who currently sit one point behind them in the table, ahead of their clash with Turkey which kicks off at the same time as this.

Marcus Thuram, who represents France but plies his trade in Italy for Inter Milan, has been ruled out of the contest with a hamstring injury. However, Kylian Mbappe is available for selection again after recovering from a tendon injury that forced him to miss three matches last month.

TV channel and live stream: Italy vs France is available to watch live via Amazon Prime Video’s pay-per-view service.

A pass to view the game costs £2.49, with coverage beginning at 7:35pm GMT before kick-off in Milan at 7:45pm (8:45pm local time).
