Manchester United make January transfer decision as Mauricio Pochettino ‘identifies targets’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Manchester United are set to back Mauricio Pochettino the January transfer window.

Sky Sports reports that the Red Devils are prepared to sanction some signings for their new manager in the winter, with the club currently sat in the Premier League relegation zone.

Pochettino, who arrived at Carrington to begin work on Wednesday, will be tasked with guiding United away from the bottom three and as high up the top-flight table as he can, while also seeing how far he can get his new side in the Champions League.

The 54-year-old will be keen to stamp his authority on the playing squad at Old Trafford, particularly with a number of key players’ contracts expiring at the end of the current campaign.

Bruno Fernandes, Lisandro Martinez, Harry Maguire and Luke Shaw will also see their deals come to an end in the north west in the summer, and there has not yet been much movement in regards to extensions for any of the quartet.

Captain Fernandes turned down a second new contract offer last week to throw his future further into doubt, while question marks remain over the other three.

It is believed that United are eyeing reinforcements at centre-back, left-back and up front in the upcoming transfer window, with injuries hampering them across all three positions.

At left-back and centre-forward, they are particularly light after issues for Shaw, Patrick Dorgu and Benjamin Sesko.

Pochettino is expected to be consulted on any potential incomings at Old Trafford, and it is thought that the former Tottenham and Chelsea boss will have significant input into who comes through the door.
