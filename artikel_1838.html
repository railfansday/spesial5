De Winter insists Milan are ‘confident’ in their foundation ahead of Inter clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Koni De Winter has shared thoughts on tonight’s big game between AC Milan and Inter, insisting that the Rossoneri are confident in their abilities.

Wednesday’s clash was a misstep for Milan, to say the least, as they suffered a 4-2 defeat in front of their home fans. Instead of boosting their confidence and improving the situation in the standings ahead of the derby, they were faced with a lot of criticism.

De Winter was one of the players who didn’t have the best of games against the Rossoblu, committing a silly mistake just before Genoa got back into the game. As such, he will surely look to prove the doubters wrong tonight.

Speaking to DAZN just a few minutes ago, as transcribed by MilanNews, De Winter insisted that Milan are still confident in their foundation. There have been no changes to how they approach the game, in other words, and now it’s time to show what they have got on the pitch.

Tonight’s mission is close the gap in the standings. How do you approach the match?

“We have a clear foundation and we are confident in that. We are approaching this match like any other. We feel ready and want to find the energy we lacked a bit in the last match.”

Inter seem to have a different lineup this evening…

“We have a foundation, we have principles, and we rely on that. If something goes wrong during the game, the coach is always ready to see what we are missing.”

It’s Amorim’s first derby. What did he say to you?

“There’s no need to motivate the team for this match. Everyone knows how important it is for the city, the players, and everyone else. He didn’t have to say anything. We are already motivated.”
