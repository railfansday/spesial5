Spain boss Luis de la Fuente issues warning after Nations League defeat to England

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Luis de la Fuente has stated the “best is yet to come” from Spain following their loss to England.

England’s 3-2 victory away in Madrid on Sunday evening ended Spain’s run of 43 straight games without defeat, handing the World Cup winners their first loss since March 2024.

Under De La Fuente, Spain have won a European Championship, a World Cup and a Nations League title in 2023 - as well as reaching the final of the latter in 2025.

However, defeat to England means that Spain will enter the quarter-finals stage of the 2026-27 Nations League as an unseeded runner-up, potentially setting up a showdown with France in March next year.

Reflecting on the game, De La Fuente said: “In the first 30 minutes, they knocked us out of our rhythm and were better than us. After that, we fought back, and I thought we were going to get the draw.

“But I’ll use a phrase that I’ve used before when we were unbeaten: ‘The best is yet to come’.”

De La Fuente also shared his belief that England approached the game in Madrid as a ‘final’, with Spain not as motivated for the game - which allowed the Three Lions to ‘trample’ over La Roja.

"The fact that the whole world wants to beat Spain is a great feeling,” he added. “This was a 'final', and in this type of game, you have to be spot on, in great shape, and if not, then an opponent like England, France, Portugal or Italy can trample all over you.”

The Spanish head coach went on to state that his side’s ambitions of winning the Nations League remained in place, with his side ‘determined’ to make amends.

He continued: "We'll try to be exactly the same in defeat as we were in victory. Okay, we haven't often tasted defeat, but we stay proud; we stay determined - in March, we're in the quarter-finals, and we'll go to those knockouts with the intention of winning, and winning the tournament."
