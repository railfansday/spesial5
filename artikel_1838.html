Manchester United co-owners INEOS sack manager as official statement released

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

INEOS have sacked Lausanne-Sport manager Luka Elsner after just five months in charge, amid ongoing pressure on Manchester United head coach Michael Carrick.

The Swiss club, which is part-owned by Sir Jim Ratcliffe's INEOS group, confirmed on Tuesday that they had mutually agreed to end their relationship with the 44-year-old.

Elsner was appointed on 1 June but struggled to make an impact, winning eight of his 12 Swiss Super League matches. Lausanne's poor start to the campaign left them bottom of the table, prompting the club to make a change.

A statement read: “After lengthy discussions about the current sporting situation, FC Lausanne-Sport and Luka Elsner have mutually agreed to end their collaboration.”

His departure comes at a difficult time for INEOS at Manchester United, where Carrick is facing scrutiny following a disappointing run of results.

United's supporters have made their frustration with the club's ownership clear in recent weeks, directing criticism towards Ratcliffe and the Glazer family.

Protests continued during Tuesday's Champions League meeting with Roma, when fans threw green and gold balloons on to the pitch in the 21st minute while chanting against the owners.

The atmosphere improved after United produced a second-half comeback to secure a 3-2 victory. Bruno Fernandes scored from the penalty spot before Matheus Cunha struck twice to complete the turnaround.

However, the result is unlikely to remove the pressure on Carrick, who remains under scrutiny as United attempt to improve their domestic form.

The decision to part company with Elsner after such a short period is also likely to attract attention given INEOS' involvement in both clubs.
