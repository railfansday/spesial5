Man Utd told they made ‘criminal’ mistake not signing Premier League winner

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Ex-England striker Carlton Cole has slammed Manchester United’s recruitment under INEOS and claims it was ‘criminal’ the club didn’t explore a move for Danny Welbeck in the summer.

There had been a sense of optimism amongst United’s fanbase heading into the season after Michael Carrick picked up the pieces from Ruben Amorim’s turbulent reign to lead the club to a third-place finish last term.

But the Red Devils once again find themselves in a state of disarray after a wretched start to the 2026/27 campaign, which ultimately cost Carrick his job earlier this month.

With genuine fears growing that United could become embroiled in a relegation scrap, Mauricio Pochettino has been tasked with steadying the ship and steering a dispirited squad away from the bottom of the Premier League table.

The Argentine’s tenure got off to a nightmare start at the weekend, though, as his side were put to the sword at the hands of arch-rivals Liverpool in a thumping 4-0 defeat at Anfield.

Sunday’s result ensured United remain firmly in the drop zone after 11 matches, only one point better off than rock-bottom Crystal Palace with the worst defensive record of any team in Europe’s top five leagues.

If Pochettino was expecting to be supported with significant financial backing in his efforts to turn United’s fortunes around this winter, he will likely be disappointed.

After all, Reports this week suggest the 54-year-old will have a budget of ‘well under £50million’ to work with when the January transfer window opens for business.

Asked what Pochettino must address with his meagre funds while tackling United’s woes on The Dressing Room Podcast, Cole replied: ‘The spine. They haven’t got a striker, a worthy striker.

‘Midfield, I think they’re alright there for now because they’ve got Carlos Baleba and I think he’s a fantastic player by the way, they’ve got Kobbie Mainoo, Bruno Fernandes… I think they’re alright in that position.

‘I know the boy from Chelsea, Andrey Santos, he’s there, but I know he’s not tuned in yet because it’s high pressure right now and he’s a little bit off it. They’ve got Youri Tielemans as well. Then you’ve got the centre-backs…’

According to Cole, United missed a trick when they gave Chelsea a free run at signing Welbeck from Brighton in the summer.

‘I think it’s criminal they didn’t sign Welbeck,’ he added.

‘I think he’s wasted at Chelsea at the moment, they should have signed Welbeck. He’d have done alright!’

Eyebrows were raised when Pochettino named his team to face Liverpool on Sunday, with Matthijs de Ligt thrown in for his first Premier League start of the campaign.

There were signs of rustiness in De Ligt’s game from minute one and it was the Dutchman’s calamitous defending that led to Dominik Szoboszlai’s breakthrough – and Leny Yoro’s sending off – after only ten minutes.

‘I thought that was him [Pochettino] protesting already,’ Cole said of De Ligt’s surprise selection.

‘I think it was on the first or second day that he was in, he was already saying he wanted more control in the transfer market. I thought you negotiated that in your deal!

‘He’s chosen the right time to do it [make a protest], in a game like that.

‘This was a free hit for him and yes, he needs defenders. But I’ve said it before, INEOS have gone with signing forward first and then the next window they get a midfielder… who does that? So that’s what has happened.’

Cole went a step further on Pochettino’s decision to field De Ligt, adding: ‘I think it’s bang out of order, he’s been stitched up.

‘He’s obviously not fit, he can’t read it. When you’ve been out so long, you get your bearings wrong, you don’t know where you are on the pitch. It happens, right? So I give him grace for that.

‘The problem is that Yoro is not experienced. So if you’re going to bring in De Ligt, you’ve got to bring another experienced defender in with him.

‘If De Ligt is going to be there and Harry Maguire is there then it’s alright and if Maguire makes that mistake that Yoro made, I get it.

‘But Yoro is still young, he’s still learning the game. They’re both, to some extent, inexperienced at this moment in time so it’s the blind leading the blind.’

United will look to stop the rot when they switch their focus to Champions League matters away to Sporting on Wednesday evening.

Three days later, Pochettino’s men return to Premier League action at home to Brentford.
