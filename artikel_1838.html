Celtic boss Martin O’Neill issues big Kieran Tierney update as fitness levels and position assessed

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Martin O’Neill believes Kieran Tierney is back to his very best and harbours no fears about the defender’s fitness levels ahead of a frantic festive schedule.

Although the Celtic manager substituted Tierney towards the end of Saturday’s 4-1 win over St Mirren, the player started – and finished – both of Scotland’s games in the recent international window, when he was deployed as left centre-half in a back-three formation favoured by new international manager Sebastien Pocognoli.

O’Neill admits this could be an option for Celtic if he chooses to play a system that he once was synonymous with during his first spell in charge at the club.

“Kieran can play as a centre-half, there’s no doubt about it,” said O’Neill. “He competes better in the air than I ever thought he did.

“It was only when I came back to Celtic that I realised that. He will battle in the air and he’s a reliable defender. If we are ever dropping into a back three, then it wouldn’t be a problem for him.”

Celtic face a tough upcoming run of games, including travelling to take on second-tier Portuguese club Torreense in the Europa League on Thursday night. Two more away games against Hearts and Dundee quickly follow. The Parkhead side have just one more home match – against St Johnstone – before Christmas. Two other assignments, against Aberdeen and Omonia Nicosia, are away.

O’Neill has already spoken of “manoeuvring” players in the first-team squad to ensure no one is pushed beyond their limits.

Tierney clearly merits the most concern given his injury history. However, he has been quietly putting in the minutes for his club as well as his country in recent weeks. He has played the full 90 minutes of two of Celtic’s last four matches and was only subbed off on Saturday with 20 minutes left against St Mirren with the game already won. O’Neill revealed that he has a crafty tactic for dealing with the player if he begins to look like he might want to come off; he pretends not to notice him. There is no point in the player looking forlornly towards the dugout.

“Tierney played two 90 minutes for Scotland and that made it difficult for him to last the full game against St Mirren,” said O'Neill.

“Internationals take it out of you and he played every second. He played left centre back and that maybe means less running, but those games are tough.

“Previous to his injury, he was feeling it. But since he’s been back, he’s played a lot of games. Sometimes with Kieran, when you feel as if he’s flagging, you just ignore him. If he’s coming near you, you turn away because you want him to play the games!

“Kieran has done great for me,” he added. "He’s been brilliant, he really has, and he’s getting fitter too. Kieran will be big for us in Europe again. You look at the Besiktas game. Our winning goal late on came from his shot when he was still getting high up the pitch.

“Kieran has done so well for us and there are signs that he’s back to the Kieran Tierney who got a move to Arsenal. He defends well, he creates goals and he comes up with some too.

“I genuinely couldn’t speak highly enough about Kieran. He’s a great lad too - very popular in the dressing room.”
