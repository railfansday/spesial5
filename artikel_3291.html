Watch Taylor Swift Surprise Brenda Lee With ‘Rockin’ Around the Christmas Tree’ Honor

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Last week, Brenda Lee received the latest accolade in her storied career in the form of a visit from Taylor Swift. Swift, who once wrote an essay outlining her super fandom of Lee, surprised the 81-year-old legend with a Diamond certification from the R.I.A.A. for “Rockin’ Around the Christmas Tree.” Lee recorded the song as a 13-year-old child-star in 1958, and it now resurfaces at or near the top of the charts every holiday season.

“I’m so happy to have this moment with you,” Swift tells the singer in a video released by Brenda Lee’s team. “I’ve been such a fan for so long.”

“And I’m a fan,” Lee responded. “I wish you wasn’t so tall.”

Indeed, the two artists’ mutual respect for each other goes back years. Lee proudly displays an autographed guitar from Swift in her own home, while Swift has performed Lee’s “I’m Sorry” onstage. In her 2017 essay on Lee, published in Woman Walk the Line, Swift described her as “the singer who mastered the sound of heartbreak.”

As Swift explained to Lee in the video published on Monday, “Rockin’ Around the Christmas Tree” is one of just two Christmas songs to ever be certified Diamond. The other is Mariah Carey’s “All I Want for Christmas Is You.”

Lee, for her part, becomes emotional in the video, mentioning the songwriter, Johnny Marks, the producer Owen Bradley, and the studio musicians who played on the recording.

“Sometimes I do wonder, what are the medals I have to show to get elected to the club?” she told Rolling Stone in 2018. “You wonder, ‘OK, is that because I’m a girl? What is that?’ I’ve never been able to name it or understand it. I mean, my record sales are certified, they’re not a lie. They’re not up for debate. I didn’t do it, the Recording Industry Association of America did it, so blame them.”

Swift has been spending more time in Nashville of late. In October, she attended her induction into the Nashville Songwriters of Hall of Fame, and last week she performed at the 60th CMA Awards, mixing her nominated single “I Knew It, I Knew You” with a portion of her 2006 hit “Our Song.” Country artists like Eric Church, Sugarland, Keith Urban, and Brad Paisley all appeared in the segment.
