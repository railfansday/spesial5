Pipe Bueno Inks Global Booking Deal With CMN: ‘They Share My Vision for Growth’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Colombian artist joins a star-studded roster that includes Marc Anthony, Christian Nodal and Greeicy.

Pipe Bueno has signed a global booking deal with Cárdenas Marketing Network (CMN), Billboard can exclusively announce today (Nov. 24). CMN, led by Henry Cárdenas, will oversee the booking of the Colombian artist’s performances worldwide.

He now joins a star-studded roster that includes Marc Anthony, Christian Nodal, Greeicy, Ana Gabriel and Grupo Niche, to name a few.

“Working with CMN is a dream come true,” Bueno, who’s a pioneering force in the música popular or regional Colombian music scene, said in a press statement. “I’ve known Henry for many years and have always admired what he has accomplished as a Colombian in the U.S. entertainment industry, working with major artists like Marc Anthony. Today, I have the opportunity to join forces with him and a team that shares my vision for growth. This partnership represents a major step toward taking my career to the next level, transcending the boundaries of Colombian popular music, and establishing myself as an increasingly global artist.”

Cárdenas, CEO of CMN, added: “Pipe Bueno is an artist with an established career and tremendous international potential. We’re excited to join forces to bring his music and live performances to new stages and continue opening doors for Colombian popular music around the world.”

Bueno — one of the leading voices of Colombia’s música popular that fuses ranchera and the string music known as carrilera in Colombia — has become a household name by also experimenting with pop, ballads and urban music. His first Billboard chart entry arrived at the Latin Digital Song Sales and Latin Rhythm Airplay charts with his 2014 song “La Invitación” (featuring Maluma), and in 2024, he signed a deal with Warner Music Latina.

Currently, he’s making the rounds with his album Aquí Estoy Yo, home to the viral love song “Señora,” which debuted at No. 7 on the Billboard Colombia Hot 100, earning the week’s Hot Shot Debut distinction and generating more than 28 million radio impressions in the South American country.

“Pipe has built a solid career in Colombia, and this partnership with CMN reflects a vision we’ve been working toward for years: expanding his international presence alongside a team with the experience and reach to make it possible,” shared Juan Guillermo Ballesteros, Bueno’s manager. “We want to continue growing without losing the essence that has connected Pipe with his audience from day one.”

The new partnership with CMN will aim to expand Bueno’s presence in new markets and introduce música popular to new audiences around the world.
