Larry David Sets Six-City Live Conversation Tour For February 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

EXCLUSIVE: Larry David, co-creator of Seinfeld and creator and star of HBO’s Curb Your Enthusiasm, has announced a six-city live conversation tour to take place in February 2027.

Titled A Conversation with Larry David, the tour will feature his daughter, writer and filmmaker Cazzie David, as special guest moderator.

The announcement follows the pair’s sold-out September 24 event at the Arlington Theatre in Santa Barbara, California. The upcoming tour will bring their onstage conversation to audiences in Toronto, Brooklyn, Jersey City, Durham, Charlotte and Nashville. Moderated by Cazzie, each evening will feature a retrospective discussion spanning Larry’s early stand-up years, his time on Seinfeld and Curb Your Enthusiasm, and everything in between.

Presale tickets will be available beginning Wednesday, November 18 at 10 a.m. local time through Friday, November 20 at 9 a.m. local time. Tickets go on sale to the general public Friday, November 20 at 10 a.m. local. Watch on Deadline

Larry David is repped by WME; Cazzie David by CAA, manager Liana Weston, and Sloane, Offer, Weber & Dern.
