Thom Velde Dies: Longtime Head Film Buyer For AMC Theatres Was 83

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Thomas “Thom” Velde, a longtime head film buyer for AMC Theatres who also oversaw its Canadian operations, has died. He was 83. His family said he died October 31 of cancer in Rancho Mirage, near Palm Springs.

Velde spent more than a quarter-century at AMC Theatres as head film buyer, negotiating film agreements with domestic and international studios. He also oversaw AMC’s Canadian ops and real estate ventures.

He joined AMC in 1978, rising to become SVP Film Buyer of its Central Division before being promoted to SVP and managing director of AMC international film marketing in 1998. Tasked in that post with handling film buying policies in Canada, Asia, Europe and other regions, he forged relationships with film companies, distributors and government agencies around the world. He also supervised and trained film buyers for the exhibition chain.

After leaving AMC, he launched T Velde Consulting, working independently in motion picture distribution and consulting for clients including Microspace Communications and CJ America.

Velde’s family said was an avid golfer and a devoted college football fan who will remembered for his strength of character, his generosity and the way he made time for the people in his life.

He is survived by his wife, Deborah; daughters Andrea Ter-Velde (John McLoughlin) and Courtney Ter-Velde (Greg Horton); son, Sam Velde; stepsons Matthew Castillo (Crissy Castillo) and Spencer Castillo (Claire Castillo), two grandchildren and five step-grandchildren.

A celebration of life is set for 3 p.m. December 8 at Woodland Hills Community Church, with a reception to follow there. Velde’s family said donations in his memory may be made to to the Will Rogers Motion Picture Pioneers Foundation or the Motion Picture & Television Fund.
