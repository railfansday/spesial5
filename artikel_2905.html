Steelers OLB Alex Highsmith seen in boot after loss vs. Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Steelers outside linebacker Alex Highsmith was seen in a walking boot after suffering an ankle injury in the Steelers' 19-16 loss to the Philadelphia Eagles Sunday afternoon, multiple reporters posted after the game.

Highsmith suffered the injury on his second sack of the game, when his left ankle appeared to get rolled up on while bringing down Eagles quarterback Joe Milton. Highsmith was initially ruled questionable to return in the first quarter, and later ruled out.

Highsmith's injury left an already shorthanded Steelers outside linebacker down another top player. Nick Herbig (hip/ribs) was ruled out ahead of Pittsburgh's game in Philadelphia, leaving the Steelers with T.J. Watt, Jack Sawyer, and the recently signed K.J. Henry at the position for the rest of the game.

With new Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy rarely providing injury updates to the media, Highsmith's status will primarily be monitored in the team's injury reports ahead of a Black Friday game in Week 12 against the Denver Broncos.
