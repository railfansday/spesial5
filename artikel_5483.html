Luma Health Brings 59 Actions to ChatGPT with New Integration, Helping Health Systems Run Full Workflows With One Prompt

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Health system leaders can ask ChatGPT to move patients to a new provider, enroll patients in procedure prep, run end of day close, or flag unpaid balances, and Luma takes action

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Healthcare leaders already use ChatGPT to answer questions. Now, they can use it to get more work done using Luma's Operational AI platform. Luma Health is introducing a new plugin for ChatGPT that includes 59 actions to manage clinic tasks like appointment prep, rescheduling and waitlist, outreach campaigns, colonoscopy prep, or balance follow-up from a conversation.

For health systems, work that used to take a report request, a spreadsheet, and hours of phone calls can now start with one prompt. Every action follows the rules and permissions the health system already has in place. Luma reads from and writes back to the EHR, so the EHR stays the system of record.

"Healthcare leaders have long had to click through screen after screen to find the data they need and act on it," said David Beam, VP of Technical Delivery at Luma. "Bringing Luma to ChatGPT empowers leaders with 'headless' AI workflows, allowing them to get their data and the power to act on it right at their fingertips while the EHR stays the source of truth."
