RealPlus brings HouseCanary Google Listings to NYC clients

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

RealPlus is partnering with My State MLS to offer HouseCanary’s Google Listings Program to NYC residential brokerages. Active listings can appear in Google mobile search with photos, pricing, and click-to-contact links to the listing agent.   AI Summary

RealPlus is partnering with My State MLS to make HouseCanary’s Google Listings Program available to its New York City residential brokerage clients, giving brokers and agents a new channel to surface listings directly in Google mobile search results.

The integration allows active listings to appear in mobile search with property photos, pricing and agent contact information, according to the company announcement. The placements appear above traditional sponsored real estate ads and include a click-to-contact feature that connects prospective buyers directly with the listing agent.

For brokerages, the offering effectively functions as an additional digital listing distribution channel, alongside portals and IDX, but inside Google’s search environment where most buyers begin home searches. In a tight lead-conversion market with rising online advertising costs, direct search visibility and agent-connected lead flows can help firms diversify away from solely portal-driven leads.

“We are always looking for ways to expand the value and capabilities we bring to our clients,” Jennifer Boucher, the chief product officer at RealPlus and a HousingWire Insider, said in a statement. “This partnership is a great addition to our offerings, giving brokers and agents another way to put their listings in front of prospective buyers and generate direct connections.”

The HouseCanary Google Listings Program will be available to RealPlus clients later in November and will be offered at no cost to participating listing brokers and agents, the company said.

RealPlus said the move aligns with its broader strategy of building listing and data infrastructure tailored to New York City residential brokerages at a time when firms are reassessing tech stacks, lead sources and marketing spend. For listing agents, a no-cost path to higher placement in search results could support listing presentations and seller expectations around online exposure.

The Google Listings Program is powered by HouseCanary, which negotiates agreements with multiple listing services to send their brokerage members’ listings to Google. Participating MLSs include  Arizona Regional MLS, Bright MLS, California Regional MLS, IRES MLS, Permian Basin Board of Realtors, REcolorado and San Diego MLS.

HouseCanary said it also works directly with brokerages, like the three announced Wednesday, to ensure their listings flow into the program via a participating MLS. Brokerages that want their listings included need either to belong to an MLS that is in the program or to encourage their local MLS to opt in.

Over the past few weeks, HouseCanary has announced that real estate brokerages REMAX Advantage, Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Florida Properties Group, ENRG Realty, Side and The Keyes Company and Illustrated Properties, all RealTrends Verified firms, have signed on to the program, sharing its listings with HouseCanary via My State MLS.

These announcements also comes as HouseCanary continues to work through its Chapter 11 restructuring. Under the firm’s proposed plan, which remains subject to court approval, HouseCanary is targeting emergence from bankruptcy before year-end, with full recovery for all approved creditors. Management has positioned initiatives such as the Google Listings Program and the AI platform as core to its go-forward strategy while it continues to serve existing customers.

Launched in early 2026 and slated to expand to all eligible U.S. markets this fall, the program is one of several industry experiments that put MLS and brokerage listings in front of consumers in new contexts, potentially changing how buyers first encounter a property and which party — portal, brokerage or platform — captures the initial inquiry.
