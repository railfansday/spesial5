Allstate's multiday outage is blocking customers from claims and roadside help

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The insurer says customers can still reach it by phone and local offices and will not lose coverage or face late fees

Allstate $ALL is working through a multiday outage of its website and mobile app that has made it difficult for customers to file claims or call for a tow during Thanksgiving week, according to The Wall Street Journal.

The insurer issued a statement Wednesday acknowledging that, for the past two days, access has been limited for some customers. Allstate said it is still fielding and resolving claims. "Allstate customers can reach us in many ways, including online, by phone and through local offices," the company said. Customers whose payments are held up by the interruptions will keep their coverage and won't face late penalties, Allstate said.

Allstate has not explained the cause of the outage or given a restoration time, according to The Wall Street Journal. Reports on Downdetector, a crowdsourced outage tracker, began Monday morning, according to TechCrunch. Users have been locked out of their accounts and cannot check what their policies cover or settle what they owe.

No publicly available information suggests the outage was the work of hackers or that customer data was exposed, according to Newsweek.

Customers posting on social media said they could not pay premiums, close on a mortgage, register a newly bought car or arrange a tow, according to The Wall Street Journal. Some Allstate agents also said in online posts that they were powerless to assist customers or write new policies, the newspaper's account said.

Allstate's roadside-assistance page carried a message saying "Our site is currently down for maintenance," according to The Wall Street Journal. Customers who try to log in see a notice saying the company is trying to bring access back "as soon as possible." A notice on Allstate's website tells people who cannot submit or oversee a claim through the internet to phone (800) 255-7828, according to Newsweek.

The outage coincides with heavy Thanksgiving travel and a nor'easter on the East Coast, according to The Wall Street Journal. Allstate provides coverage to 8 million homeowners as well as 26 million drivers.

Leaders of the Oklahoma House Insurance Committee urged Allstate to give policyholders clear information on the choices available to them. Policyholders "should not be penalized for something completely outside their control," they said in a statement, according to The Wall Street Journal.

The disruption follows outages that hit air travel, Microsoft $MSFT Outlook and bank transactions in recent weeks. Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton and Secretary of State Marco Rubio said on Nov. 18 that companies patching vulnerabilities and upgrading outdated systems caused those incidents. Rubio said, "We are not under systemic attack."
