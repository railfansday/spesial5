Fischer Homes Surpasses $39M Raised for St. Jude

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The St. Louis Dream Home built by Fischer Homes.

Fischer Homes helped raise $4.06 million for St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital through its 2026 St. Jude Dream Home Giveaway campaigns in Cincinnati, St. Louis, and Louisville.

The campaigns coincided with Fischer Homes marking 20 years of partnership with St. Jude and brought the builder’s cumulative contributions to more than $39.36 million. The 2026 Cincinnati campaign generated $1.48 million, St. Louis raised $1.68 million, and Louisville contributed $900,000.

Fischer Homes designed and built a Dream Home for each market, making it the only builder to construct three St. Jude Dream Homes in a single year, they noted. The homes included a Linden plan in Cincinnati valued at $540,000, an Emmett plan in St. Louis valued at $735,000, and a Grandin plan in Louisville valued at $533,000.

Trade partners, vendors, sponsors, employees, and community members contributed materials, labor, and other resources to complete the homes and support the giveaways.

“When we look back on 20 years with St. Jude, we’re reminded that the impact of this partnership goes far beyond the homes we’ve built or the dollars we’ve raised,” Fischer Homes said in announcing the milestone. “It’s about the lasting difference made possible by our associates, trade partners, vendors, and local supporters, who have contributed their time, talent, and resources to a mission that matters.”

In recognition of its support, Fischer Homes received a St. Jude 20-Year Partnership Award at the 2026 St. Jude Dream Home Builder Summit in Memphis. The builder constructed its first St. Jude Dream Home in Cincinnati in 2003.

The St. Jude Dream Home Giveaway raises money through giveaways of newly constructed homes, with builders and their trade partners donating time, materials, and resources to support St. Jude’s childhood cancer and other disease research and treatment efforts.
