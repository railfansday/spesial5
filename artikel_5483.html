Lemonade Announces Online Viewing Details for 2026 Investor Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

NEW YORK, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Lemonade, Inc. (NYSE: LMND) will hold its third Investor Day on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, in New York City. The program begins at 9:00 a.m. ET.

Attendees can join in person or virtually through a live webcast. In-person attendance is by invitation only, and advance registration is required.

The live webcast of the event will be available on the Lemonade Investor Relations website, investor.lemonade.com, and on YouTube. A replay will be posted after the event.

To register for the online webcast, click here.

About LemonadeLemonade offers renters, homeowners, car, pet, and life insurance. Powered by artificial intelligence and social impact, Lemonade's full stack insurance carriers in the US and the EU replace brokers and bureaucracy with AI, aiming for zero paperwork and instant everything. A Certified B-Corp, Lemonade gives unused premiums to nonprofits selected by its community, during its annual Giveback. Lemonade is currently available in the United States, Germany, the Netherlands, France, and the UK, and continues to expand globally.

Follow Lemonade on X and Instagram for updates.
