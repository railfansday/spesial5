RS offers ebm-papst solutions for rack-level thermal challenges in high-density data centers

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

ebm-papst DiaForce, AxiForce, and RadiCal series fans help mitigate rack-level hot spots, minimize harmonic vibration, reduce parasitic power draw, and improve energy efficiency in critical data center infrastructure.

FORT WORTH, Texas, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- RS, a high-service global product and service solutions provider for industrial customers, offers high-performance ebm-papst air-moving products engineered to solve rack-level thermal management challenges in high-density data centers, including several series of robust, compact, and versatile fans. As processing demands soar, these proven fan technologies allow data center operators, system builders, and thermal engineers to optimize airflow, overcome high static pressure resistance, minimize harmonic vibration, reduce parasitic power draw, and reliably protect critical assets from thermal damage and mechanical stress.

Rapid growth in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and high-performance computing has pushed server rack power densities from 3â5kW a decade ago to 40â100kW or more per rack today. Because server chips convert nearly all electrical energy into heat, unmitigated thermal energy creates localized hot spots that facility-level air conditioning cannot effectively dissipate, risking component degradation, silicon melting, and unplanned downtime that averages around $9,000 per minute. While liquid cooling adoption is growing, fan-based forced-air cooling remains the global standard for roughly 90% of data centers due to lower capital costs, easier maintenance, legacy facility compatibility, and zero risk of fluid leaks near high-voltage electronics.
