Six Flags warned that a roller coaster death will drag on fourth quarter earnings

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The company forecast fourth-quarter same-park adjusted EBITDA of $140 million to $150 million, compared with $167 million a year earlier

Six Flags Entertainment forecast fourth-quarter same-park adjusted EBITDA of $140 million to $150 million, versus $167 million in the year-ago period, after a fatal accident at Six Flags Over Georgia and bad weather left October softer than planned. EBITDA stands for earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization.

The company said on Thursday that October fell short of its plan because of unfavorable weather on several of the biggest weekends early in the month, along with the effect of the accident and related ride closures. Six Flags expects about 60 additional operating days in the fourth quarter versus last year, mainly from the return of Holiday in the Park winter events.

“Following the incident at Six Flags Over Georgia last month, we are cooperating fully with the ongoing investigation,” President and Chief Executive Officer John Reilly said in a statement, according to The Wall Street Journal. “We have launched a comprehensive review of ride safety across our entire portfolio, supported by independent third-party engineering and inspection specialists.”

For the third quarter ended Sept. 27, Six Flags recorded a net loss totaling $366.1 million, equivalent to $3.59 for each share. That compares with a $1.19 billion loss, equal to $11.77 per share, in the comparable quarter last year, when the company booked a $1.52 billion non-cash impairment charge. The latest quarter included $687.7 million of non-cash impairment charges.

Net revenue dropped to $1.17 billion, an 11.0% decline, because the year-ago quarter counted eight parks the company no longer operates. On a same-park basis, which excludes those parks, revenue rose 1.4%. Adjusted EBITDA was $493.2 million, down 11.1% as reported and up 1.1% on a same-park basis.

Hassan Jihad Ibn Kariem, 42, was killed in October in a mishap aboard Goliath, a roller coaster at the Georgia park, and several others were hurt, according to The Wall Street Journal. Six Flags has said it will install extra restraints on Goliath and on comparable rides elsewhere in its chain, most of which are fitted with clamshell lap bars.

Six Flags stock fell more than 10% in after-hours trading after the Goliath accident on Oct. 12. The company temporarily closed similar attractions at other parks, including Carowinds and Kings Island.
