What Airbus Says About a New North American Factory and Demand

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Robin Hayes, Chairman and CEO, Airbus North America

Moderated by Gordon Smith, Airlines Editor, Skift

Aircraft manufacturers face growing pressure to deliver the next generation of more efficient airplanes while meeting airlines’ expectations for operational reliability. Hayes said durability has become a more prominent factor in aircraft development, adding another requirement to an already complex decision about when to introduce new technology.
