Avelo Airlines Launches 16 New Routes In 4 Weeks: See Full List Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Newish airlines tend to grow strongly, benefiting from the revenue, unit cost, market share, and competitive advantages of doing so. For example, five-year-old Breeze Airways' passenger traffic grew by 47% in the 12 months to August. In distinct contrast, Avelo Airlines' passenger numbers fell by 15%.

Avelo has been struggling recently as it seeks to find what works. As such, it has cut many routes, including its entire international operation, and removed multiple served airports from its map. However, the airline, whose fleet consists of 17 Boeing 737-700s and 737-800s, will launch 16 new routes in November and December. Still, compared to the same two months in 2025, 30+ routes have been removed.

Analysis of the airline's schedule submission to Cirium Diio has been explored. This compared the budget operator's network between January 2025 and October 2026 with what is set to operate in November 2026. The process found that the following ten routes will launch. This includes a return to the Caribbean. However, as the two routes will be to Aguadilla, Puerto Rico, they'll be domestic. Aguadilla is a brand-new destination for Avelo.

Half of the entries will be from McKinney (DTX), where Avelo's latest base just opened. Located around 34 miles (54 km) north of downtown Dallas, McKinney's passenger terminal opened on November 11. Not surprisingly, that was exactly when the ultra-low-cost carrier Avelo began flying from the airport. In doing so, the carrier now serves three airports in the Lone Star State: Dallas/Fort Worth, McKinney, and Houston Intercontinental.

McKinney is near Dallas, Concord is near Charlotte, and Lakeland is between Orlando and Tampa

Initially, Avelo had one aircraft based at McKinney. However, that'll jump to two this month and, eventually, three. When a trio of aircraft are stationed there, nearly a third of the budget carrier's fleet will be there, which perhaps illustrates the challenges that it has faced.

Simple Flying's Live Flight Tracker shows that the N801XT and N803XT, both of which are 737-800s, will be there this week, although the specific airframes may change in the future. Generally, each aircraft has four legs per day. For example, on November 19, the 20.8-year-old N803XT, which is ex-Turkish Airlines, will fly McKinney-Orlando-McKinney-Las Vegas-McKinney.

They will all be from McKinney and, like the five mentioned earlier, will be brand-new markets. Significantly, it'll be the first time that Avelo has flown to Denver, albeit only with one route and four weekly departures. It'll mark Avelo's return to Colorado after last flying there in September 2024 from Burbank to Colorado Springs.

Previously, the airline also flew from Burbank to Grand Junction and from Burbank and Las Vegas to Fort Collins. The new route contributes to Avelo having a record number of flights to Atlanta, the world's busiest airport for passengers but not for flights

However, context is needed. Only three markets will exist: Lakeland, McKinney, and Tweed New Haven. Previously, Avelo flew there from Wilmington, Delaware. Things are not this simple, as it appears that Lakeland-Atlanta service will end in early January 2027. No flights are scheduled beyond there, but that might change.

US Department of Transportation (DOT) information currently extends to August 2026. In the 12 months to that summer month, Avelo transported nearly 2.3 million round-trip passengers. Compared to the prior 12 months, traffic fell by 15%. While not a like-for-like comparison, Breeze grew by 47%.

Avelo's average load factor is 77.7%. While that is low compared to many budget operators around the world, it is relatively normal for US airlines. As the number of seats for sale fell by 16%, the smaller reduction in traffic (-15%) means that the load factor improved to the highest level since the 12 months to August 2023. What is not known is how fares changed.
