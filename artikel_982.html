Republic of Ireland have had to dust themselves off quickly following Saturday's late 1-0 defeat by Austria, with tonight's clash with Kosovo the very definition of a must-win game.

The Irish start the night a point and a place behind their opponents in Group B3, so a victory would take them into the League A/B promotion play-offs and ensure they are in pot two for next month's Euro 2028 qualifying draw.

They will be without captain Nathan Collins after the defender sustained a knee injury in the closing stages of the defeat by Austria, with Jimmy Dunne replacing him in the matchday squad.

Heimir Hallgrimsson's side were also on the wrong end of a late winner in their previous meeting with Kosovo, as Vedat Muriqi scored seven minutes from time to earn a 1-0 victory for the Balkan nation.

Tight games have been a theme for tonight's visitors during their Nations League campaign, having scored just three goals and conceded four, while they drew 0-0 at home to Israel on Saturday.

Veldin Hodza was sent off in that game and is suspended for this one as a result, while Juventus' Edon Zhegrova has withdrawn from the squad with an injury.
