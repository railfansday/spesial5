Micah Parsons cleared to play Wednesday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Micah Parsons is set to make his long-awaited return to the Packers lineup against the Rams on Wednesday night.

Parsons said on Monday that he was waiting to speak with Dr. Neal ElAttrache, who performed his ACL surgery, later in the day about whether he’d get the green light. Ian Rapoport of NFL Media reports that call has taken place and that Parsons has received final medical clearance to make his first appearance of the 2026 season.

The defensive end has been practicing for the last few weeks and has now moved through every step of his return from the torn ACL he suffered last December.

Parsons joined the Packers in a trade ahead of the 2025 season and had 12.5 sacks in 14 games before that injury. It remains to be seen how much Parsons will play in his first game in nearly a year, but the Packers will be hoping for him to have an impact in whatever playing time he gets Wednesday while looking for that to ramp up as he regains his form in the coming weeks.
