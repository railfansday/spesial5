Quinnen Williams: We executed better on defense, but we still gave up 30 points

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Cowboys defense was under the microscope all week due to the decision to strip defensive coordinator Christian Parker of play-calling duties and hand them to assistant Mike Pettine, but defensive tackle Quinnen Williams pointed to something else playing a leading role in the unit’s performance on Sunday.

Dallas did not allow a touchdown in the first half while building a 27-9 lead over the Titans and ended the day as 51-30 winners. Williams, who had two sacks and a forced fumble in the win, “no matter who called the call, no matter what call, the players on the field had to execute” and that he was “super proud” of the way that the group responded on Sunday.

Williams’s pride in the performance did not obscure the fact that it was far from perfect.

“We still gave up 30 points,” Williams said, via the team’s website. “So we definitely got to go watch the film and learn from it and get better, because we know the defense we can be and we have a long way to go, and we have to take steps forward to get there.”

The Titans are a one-win team, which makes them exactly the kind of opponent you want to see when trying to right the ship. The Cowboys have another losing team on deck with the Eagles coming to town on Thanksgiving and they’ll have to keep pushing to improve to make Sunday’s win a sign of things to come rather than a momentary blip for an underwhelming unit.
