Jaguars can’t move up in playoff picture despite win

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Jacksonville Jaguars keep winning but they can't move up in the AFC standings. The Kansas City Chiefs won on Sunday to leap from fifth into second, bypassing the Denver Broncos in the AFC West. Kansas City's record in the AFC is 6-1, slightly better than the Jaguars at 6-2, so the Chiefs are the two seed right now.

In the AFC South, the Jaguars got help from a Houston Texans loss to the Cleveland Browns. The Jags have a stranglehold on the division now up four games.

The Chiefs and Broncos first go to a divisional tiebreaker at 7-2, where Kansas City wins with a 3-1 record in the AFC West over the Broncos at 2-1. Kansas City is the division leader.

The Chiefs, Jaguars, and Bengals go into a three-way tiebreaker and one team hasn't beat all the others, so it heads to AFC record. Kansas City wins with a 6-1 AFC record to secure the two seed.

Then the Jaguars have a head-to-head win over Cincinnati to nab the three seed.

The Broncos slot in as the top Wild Card.

2. Kansas City Chiefs (7-2, 3-1 AFCW, 6-1 AFC)

3. Jacksonville Jaguars (7-2, 6-2 AFC, win over CIN)

4. Cincinnati Bengals (7-2, 5-2 AFC, loss to JAX)
