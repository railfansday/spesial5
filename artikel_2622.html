Eagles vs. Cowboys, live updates: Joe Milton going nuclear in upset bid

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Happy Thanksgiving, Philadelphia Eagles fans.

Let's see if the Birds can score an upset win against the Dallas Cowboys at AT&T Stadium in Week 12 NFL action.

The Eagles (3-7) are coming off their first win in two months, beating the Pittsburgh Steelers last Sunday — a victory that played a factor in the Aaron Rodgers-Mike McCarthy drama that has since unfolded.

More importantly for the Eagles, their 19-16 victory over the Steelers ended a brutal seven-game losing streak.

But now, the Cowboys (5-6) and their high-powered offense awaits Nick Sirianni's team on Turkey Day.

The Cowboys are in the hunt for first place in the NFC East, trailing the division-leading New York Giants (6-3-1).

Understandably, Dallas is a heavy 8.5-point favorite vs. Joe Milton and the Eagles, per FanDuel Sportsbook.

But can the former Cowboys QB spring an upset against his old team? Milton will need plenty of help from Saquon Barkley, DeVonta Smith and Philly's defense — which will have its hands full with Ceedee Lamb, George Pickens and Co.

The last time these two teams met was in Week 7, when Lamb had two touchdowns, Pickens scored, and the Cowboys won going away, 38-21, at Lincoln Financial Field.

We'll see if the Eagles can keep this one closer — or if it's more of the same on Thanksgiving in Texas.

Stay tuned for updates here throughout the game.

After a huge defensive stop on third-and-short, defensive tackle Ty Robinson blocked the Cowboys' punt, and Kenny Moore returned it for a touchdown.

Just like that, the Eagles are back in it.
