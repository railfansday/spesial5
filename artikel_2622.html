Bears Week 12 injury report vs. Lions: 3 players ruled out, 2 players questionable for Thanksgiving clash

The Chicago Bears take on the Detroit Lions in a Thanksgiving tilt featuring two potential Super Bowl contenders. The Bears are riding a five-game win streak after a 21-13 victory over the New Orleans Saints.

But they will be without a trio of players for their Thanksgiving game, and could potentially be without two more. Here's all we know about the Bears' injury concerns heading into Week 12.

The Bears have three players listed as out on the injury report. Those are defensive backs Kyler Gordon and Elijah Hicks, as well as Roschon Johnson.

Meanwhile, cornerback Tyrique Stevenson and linebacker D'Marco Jackson are listed as questionable.

Gordon missed the win over the Saints after picking up a shoulder injury in their win over the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. The corner has played just four games this season, making 13 total tackles. But despite playing just four games, he's got four sacks (tied for third on the team) and two forced fumbles (tied for the team lead).

Hicks had played in all 10 games this season, making 12 total tackles, but he has been sidelined with a concussion. Meanwhile, Johnson has 189 total yards from scrimmage this season, with one touchdown.

With all the injuries to the secondary, Tyrique Stevenson's status will be crucial for Chicago. Stevenson has played in nine of the team's 10 games, making seven starts.
