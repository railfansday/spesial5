Nintendo Reveals Switch 2 Black Friday Deals (US)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Nintendo has revealed a bunch of offers in the US for this year's Black Friday in a hope to boost its sales ahead of the holiday season.

The headline here is a Switch 2 hardware bundle, which contains the console, a digital copy of Mario Kart World, and a 3-month individual membership to Nintendo Switch Online. Priced at $499.99, this effectively wipes out the $50 price hike we saw earlier this year. This discount will be applicable from 22nd November to 5th December.

From 22nd November to 29th November, you can also bag $20 off the suggested retail price of select first-party games, including Donkey Kong Bananza and Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2.

All of these offers will be available at Nintendo New York and Nintendo San Fransisco alongside select retailers. A Black Friday eShop sale will also kick off from 19th November, so keep an eye on Nintendo Life for our recommendations when the time comes.

Nintendo recently confirmed that the Switch 2 has surpassed 26 million console sales, despite a drop of around 37% year-on-year. The recent launch of Ocarina of Time will no doubt boost console sales nicely, as the game itself has already sold over 5 million copies.
