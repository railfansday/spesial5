Fernando Alonso Chases Dakar Glory While F1 Reigns Supreme

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The two-time Formula 1 champion confirmed on Tuesday that he will race in the Dakar Rally in 2027. He joins Aston Martin as a factory driver for the first time in the team's history. The move occurs while he is still racing in Formula 1. The 45-year-old Spaniard will drive a Prodrive-built Aston Martin Dreadnought. The race takes place from January 9 to January 23, 2027. It moves to Portugal and Morocco after leaving Saudi Arabia. This marks his first Dakar appearance since his 2020 debut with Toyota. That year, he finished 13th overall and took second on Stage 8.

Alonso brings his navigator, Marc Coma, back to the seat. Coma is a five-time Dakar winner on motorcycles. They are paired for the two-week marathon. The squad also includes Sebastien Loeb and Lucas Moraes. Loeb is a nine-time world rally champion. The car itself, the Dreadnought, was first a digital military vehicle in a video game. Aston Martin CEO Adrian Hallmark said, according to Motorsport.com, "Aston Martin Dreadnought was conceived to dominate any terrain, under the most extreme circumstances, and come through it all to deliver on its mission." The project is partnered with Prodrive.

Alonso knows the risk of doing both. He admitted that Formula 1 remains his priority. "After deciding to continue racing in Formula 1 with Aston Martin, I thought carefully about Dakar," said Alonso. He called the decision simple because he wanted to embrace the full challenge. He has enormous respect for the race and the specialists who master it. Testing a prototype in Morocco in October helped prepare him. The African nation is an ideal testing ground with its vast deserts. Speculation grew after he extended his F1 contract in September. He wants to see what this exceptional team can achieve.
