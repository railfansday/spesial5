The playoff projections are back! We first unveiled them after Week 1, but early in the season, it's hard to tell who's actually good. However, with half the season now over, there's much more data available, and our projection model has taken it into account.

If you're wondering how these projections work, we brought in our resident analytics expert, Stephen Oh. Oh simulated the rest of the season to see how things will end up playing out.

With that in mind, here's what the playoff field looks like according to Oh's projections:

AFC (Current record)1. Bills (7-1) -- AFC East champion 2. Jaguars (6-2) -- AFC South champion 3. Broncos (7-2) -- AFC West champion 4. Bengals (6-2) -- AFC North champion 5. Chiefs (6-2) 6. Steelers (6-2) 7. Patriots (5-4)JUST MISSED: Texans (3-5), Browns (5-4)

AFC breakdown: According to our projection, the only real battle will be for the seventh seed. We have the Patriots projected to get the spot right now, but the Texans are nipping at their heels. We have both teams projected to finish the season at 9-8, but the Patriots are sneaking in thanks to a better conference record.

NFC (Current record)1. 49ers (7-1) -- NFC West champion 2. Lions (6-2) -- NFC North champion 3. Giants (5-2-1) -- NFC East champion 4. Buccaneers (3-6) -- NFC South champion 5. Bears (7-2) 6. Vikings (6-2) 7. Packers (6-3) JUST MISSED: Seahawks (5-4), Rams (5-4)

NFC breakdown: For the first time this season, our model projects ALL FOUR NFC North teams to make the playoffs. At this point, it feels like the only way it won't happen is if the Rams or Seahawks reach 11 wins, but our computer doesn't see that happening. Having all four teams from a single division make the postseason in the same year wasn't possible until 2020, when the NFL expanded the playoffs to 14 teams. Since then, there have been several instances where a division has put three teams in, but we still haven't seen a division put all four of its teams in, so this could be a history-making season.

Not only did the computer project the entire playoff field, but it also picked a surprising Super Bowl winner. Spoiler alert: It was the BILLS. The Bills have never won a Super Bowl, but our computer thinks that's going to change this year.
