Mavericks vs. Nuggets predictions: Odds, recent stats, trends and best bets for November 24

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Dallas Mavericks (10-7) and the Denver Nuggets (10-5) each bring a three-game winning streak into tonight's matchup at Ball Arena. Dallas enters at 10-7 overall and 3-5 on the road, while Denver is 10-5 overall and 4-3 at home. The Nuggets continue to rely on their offense. They average 117.0 points per game on 49 percent shooting, compared with 112.4 points for the Mavericks. Defensively, the Mavs allow 112.5 points per game, while Denver's potent attack is less than two points better than their generous defense which is surrendering 115.2.

Dallas earned their third straight win Sunday, surviving a remarkable late rally to beat the Utah Jazz 116-114. Dallas used a 20-5 third quarter run to build an 18-point advantage and still led 114-98 with 2:14 remaining, only for Utah to score 16 unanswered points and tie the game with 4.7 seconds left. Cooper Flagg answered by making two decisive free throws with 3.7 seconds remaining, completing a 28-point performance, before Utah missed a three-pointer at the buzzer. Kyrie Irving scored all 16 of his points in the first half, while Dallas converted Utah's 23 turnovers into 32 points. Flagg is averaging 24.3 points, 6.3 rebounds and 6.4 assists, with P.J. Washington adding 14.0 points and a team-high 6.7 rebounds per game.

The Nuggets also required some late-game resilience Sunday, outlasting the Chicago Bulls 151-145 in overtime. A seven-point lead for Denver disappeared in the final 40 seconds of regulation, with Chicago tying the score at 131 on Josh Giddey's layup with 2.1 seconds left. The Nuggets recovered by scoring the first eight points of overtime, led by Jamal Murray, who finished with a season-high 45 points and scored eight in the extra period. Nikola Jokic delivered his 202nd career triple-double with 37 points, 18 rebounds and 11 assists, while Denver scored 66 points in the paint and shot 53 percent from the floor. Murray is averaging a team-high 26.2 points, and Jokic remains a consistent triple-double averaging 25.1 points, 10.9 rebounds and 9.2 assists per game.

This is the first time this season these teams take the court against each other. Their most recent meeting was on March 25 last season with the Nuggets winning 142-135 at Ball Arena. That win gave Denver a split of the four-game season series against the Mavericks.
