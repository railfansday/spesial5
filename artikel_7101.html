Socure: Technical Content Marketing Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Socure is building the identity trust infrastructure for the digital economy — verifying 100% of good identities in real time and stopping fraud before it starts. The mission is big, the problems are complex, and the impact is felt by businesses, governments, and millions of people every day.

We hire people who want that level of responsibility. People who move fast, think critically, act like owners, and care deeply about solving customer problems with precision. If you want predictability or narrow scope, this won’t be your place. If you want to help build the future of identity with a team that holds a high bar for itself — keep reading.

This is an employment marketing role for someone who can make exceptional engineers want to work at Socure. Socure is building AI-native infrastructure for identity, fraud prevention, and trust, and we need your help getting that engineering story out to the places where great technical talent discovers ideas, follows builders, and finds its next challenge.

You will work closely with engineers, technical leaders, recruiting, and marketing to turn APIs, machine learning models, identity graphs, and decisioning workflows into a compelling case for joining the team. Your work will show software engineers the problems they can solve here, the systems they can help build, and the ambition behind Socure's mission.

The best engineers are not looking for generic employer branding. They are looking for hard technical problems, strong peers, meaningful architecture, and a mission worth their time. You will make those things visible - in the communities, publications, developer platforms, social channels, and other talent-rich spaces where engineers actually pay attention.

Interview engineers and technical leaders, learn how the platform works, and turn real technical work into stories that make top engineers lean in.

Write technical content - including engineering blogs, deep dives, architecture stories, technical whitepapers, and recruiting-facing website copy - that makes the work at Socure tangible.

Own an employment-marketing content calendar built around the channels and communities where software engineers discover work, exchange ideas, and decide which companies deserve their attention.

Adapt and distribute stories across developer platforms, technical publications, engineering communities, social channels, and other high-signal places where the best talent gathers.

Partner with recruiting and employer brand to connect content to talent goals, strengthen candidate interest, and support hard-to-fill engineering hiring.

Translate APIs, machine learning models, identity graphs, decisioning workflows, and cloud-based architectures into accurate, engaging stories for technical audiences.

Show why the problems matter, what makes Socure's approach distinctive, and what an engineer could help build next.

Develop enough understanding of Socure's products and platform to write with authority, ask sharp questions, and earn the trust of technical experts.

Stay current on AI, fraud, identity, security, and infrastructure trends, and turn them into timely points of view that attract the right people.

Work with engineering, recruiting, employer brand, product marketing, and technical subject matter experts to find the strongest stories and get them ready for the right audiences.

Partner with design, social, and distribution teams to package and place content for reach, credibility, and candidate action.

Build a repeatable feedback loop with engineers so the content stays technically honest and genuinely representative of the work.

Protect a high editorial standard for clarity, accuracy, voice, and consistency across every channel.

Edit and proofread content from other contributors, and help maintain Socure's content style guide.

Use AI tools such as ChatGPT's Deep Research to accelerate research, drafting, and technical enablement while keeping human judgment, verification, and editorial quality at the center.

Demonstrable experience in content writing, technical writing, employer brand, recruiting marketing, or content marketing, ideally in identity verification, fraud, cybersecurity, fintech, infrastructure, or another technical B2B SaaS environment.

Strong technical curiosity and the ability to learn and write accurately about APIs, machine learning, and cloud-based architectures.

Exceptional writing and storytelling skills, with a talent for making complex technical work clear, credible, and exciting to software engineers.

A portfolio of published technical, engineering, employer-brand, or recruiting content, such as blogs, deep dives, whitepapers, case studies, or similar work.

Experience partnering cross-functionally with engineering, recruiting, employer brand, product marketing, and subject matter experts.

Strong editorial and distribution instincts, with comfort owning a content calendar, finding the right audience and channel, giving and receiving feedback, and maintaining a consistent voice.

Experience using AI tools for research, drafting, and content production is a plus.

If you can understand ambitious engineering work, find the audience that will appreciate it, and make great engineers curious enough to start a conversation, we want to hear from you.
