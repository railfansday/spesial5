Charlie Austin reacts to major Ian Holloway update at Swindon Town

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Ex- Swindon Town, Burnley and QPR striker Charlie Austin has offered his services to the Robins after his former side dismissed Ian Holloway off the back of four straight defeats in all competitions.

Holloway has been under the magnifying glass ever since the start of the season, following a total collapse last year, which saw his side fall from second to completely missing the play-offs in the final 15 games.

Despite an okay start to the current League Two campaign, especially at home, just two wins in 15 in all competitions have put the self-proclaimed 'dead man walking' to rest, with a 2-0 away defeat at Newport County proving the fatal blow.

Now, Swindon are searching for a new boss, and a fan favourite on the pitch has stated that he is "free and available to help" turn things around in any sense.

Charlie Austin's affiliation with Swindon needs no introduction. The 37-year-old's EFL career began at the County Ground, with the Robins plucking him from non-league side Poole Town in 2009, aged 20.

From there, Austin spent 18 months before securing a move to Burnley, where he'd become an established Championship and Premier League frontman, before returning to Swindon midway through the 2022/23 campaign to end his professional career.

His final EFL season actually saw him play in every game for the Robins three years ago, and following the departure of Ian Holloway, the former striker took to X to offer his services to his former side.

"I am free and available to help," Austin tweeted, with the proximity to Holloway's dismissal implying that this is regarding the vacancy at the County Ground.

Austin hasn't shied away from his desire to get into coaching after his playing career came to an end. He publicly spoke about how he didn't get accepted into the QPR academy coaching team back in August.

This is a lot different from an academy role, but it reaffirms that aspiration to get into the dugout in some capacity in the near future.

The general vagueness of the tweet has sparked mixed reactions across X. However, when you sieve through fans questioning whether the 37-year-old can put his boots on and play for their team, or paint their living room, some Swindon supporters have reacted.

Most have taken the "free" part of Austin's tweet and used it to mock their current owner, Clem Morfuni, who they're currently not too happy with.

"Being free is great because that fits morfuni's budget! Happy with Gav [Gunning] as your assistant?" one Swindon fan questioned. "Free !! That’s music to our owners ears," another said.

Others wondered what a Charlie Austin coaching staff would look like, and questioned if other players from their 2009/10 squad, which made the League One play-offs, could join him.

"Get the band back together? Austin, [Billy] Paynter, [Simon] Ferry, [Jon-Paul] McGovern and [Phil] smithy as GK coach 🤣🤣," one fan tweeted.

Austin may not be the man to succeed Ian Holloway in the Swindon dugout. Every manager has to start somewhere, but hiring the former striker to his first role would be a massive risk.

But bringing him in as part of a new-look coaching staff may not be the craziest thing that the club has done. The 37-year-old is a popular figure among Swindon fans, and having him back at the club in a coaching capacity could help restore some good feelings at the County Ground.
