West Ham 'working on' Scott Parker's 'priority' signing - ex-Employee

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Scott Parker is set to prioritise the addition of a new goalkeeper as he hopes to strengthen his West Ham United squad in January.

The new boss has run the rule over the squad available to him upon his arrival at the London Stadium, and has already identified key areas for improvement ahead of the window.

Mads Hermansen and Alphonse Areola represent the current goalkeeping options in East London, with the former the current first choice, but he has struggled this season.

As a result, former West Ham scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that work is ongoing behind the scenes to identify a new goalkeeper to strengthen Parker's side.

So far this season, Hermansen has kept just four clean sheets in 15 Championship games, and his struggles have seen the Hammers slide down the league table.

The addition of a new goalkeeper could be the difference between securing promotion or remaining in the Championship, and Parker is determined to stop leaking goals.

Football Insider reported on 5 November that Hermansen has been torn into by West Ham fans, who are growing increasingly concerned by his perofrmances of late.

The fans are not the only ones, however, with club chiefs and the new manager also having identified the goalkeeping situation as a potential problem ahead of the window.

Parker is aiming to strengthen his squad in multiple positions, and hopes he will receive the club's backing, with a new goalkeeper being tipped as his first priority.

"Scott Parker will want West Ham to support his ideas when the transfer window opens," Brown told Football Insider.

"He will have assessed the squad before he took the job, so he'll know which areas they need to improve in and which players are not good enough to keep around.

"Hermansen is one of those who falls into that second category, he has not been good enough this season, and that has become a major concern.

"Whenever your goalkeeper is making mistakes, costing you goals, or performing below his usual level, it makes it difficult to win games and to hold on to results.

"So signing a new goalkeeper, one who can be consistent and help improve that defensive record, is going to be a priority for Parker and I'm sure it's something they're working on.

"Scouts will be having a look at a number of goalkeepers who might be available in January, and if they find the right option who is available, it's something they will want to do."

Parker endured a difficult start to life in the dugout at West Ham after losing his first game in charge away from home against Blackburn Rovers at Ewood Park.

That may come as a reality check for the manager, who has been tasked with leading West Ham to promotion, which will not be easy if their recent on-pitch issues continue.

However, former Everton CEO Keith Wyness told Football Insider that Parker is the perfect man for the job and West Ham have made the right call with his appointment.

His previous record in the Championship, where he has previously won promotion with three different clubs, was key to West Ham's decision to target him.

Meanwhile, Parker has been backed to light a fire under his current crop of struggling players and to get the best out of them, with calls for those players to finally step up.
