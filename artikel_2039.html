Lazio 0-1 Milan Primavera: Rossoneri into play-off spots with fourth straight win

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Milan Primavera have begun to build some real momentum, as they recorded another win over Lazio in Sunday’s early kick-off.

Before the November international break, the Primavera managed to build on their mid-week cup win with a strong performance against Monza, winning 5-0 away from home. Max Anastasio, Mattia Zaramella (x2), Riccardo Grassini and Yanis Messaoudi scored to move the Rossoneri up to 11th in the standings.

That made it three wins in a row, and today it became four in the capital against Lazio. Giovanni Renna’s side scored the only goal of the game in a 1-0 victory that they were made to fight hard for.

The Rossoneri took the lead after just eight minutes when right-back Luca Nolli registered his fifth assist of the season with a perfect cross that found Mattia Angelicchio in the box, who deceived the Biancocelesti defence and headed past D’Alessio.

The home side had a great chance to equalise in the 21st minute, but Gatto squandered it, failing to tap in in front of goal. In the second half, Milan managed to contain the Biancocelesti as they pushed to find an equaliser. They had some chances, but the Diavolo held firm.

With another three points on the board, Renna’s boys moved up to fourth place in the table, while awaiting the result of Bologna’s game against Torino tomorrow.
