Former winger wants selfish Bruno Fernandes to be sold and identifies replacement

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Despite a slow start to last season, Bruno Fernandes went on to finish the last season with 21 Premier League assists, breaking the competition’s single-season record.

This season has been a different situation. He isn’t at his usual best, contract talks have reported stalled and he publicy questioned Carrick’s tactics.

Bruno Fernandes has come under criticism for his performances and Ashley Young joins in as the latest to call for Bruno’s sale in January.

Manchester United have been told to cash in on Bruno Fernandes in January rather than offer the midfielder another contract. Meanwhile, Young identifies Morgan Gibbs-White as an upgrade over the Portuguese star.

The criticism of Fernandes has been particularly strong, with questions raised over his influence in central midfield.

“If Manchester United can cash in on him in January, I’d do that. I wouldn’t be looking to extend his contract at all. Keep him there, he’d be unhappy,” Ashley Young said while talking to BBC.

Young also questioned Fernandes’ attitude towards individual recognition, suggesting that winning personal awards appears to be an important part to his United career.

“He seems to me as a player that personal accolades are more important than the team. And that’s what he’s about. He’ll be looking at that, yeah, I want player of the year. I won player of the year. It was, it was me. You know, it weren’t my other teammates.”

The concerns were not limited to Fernandes’ attitude. His defensive positioning and the spaces he leaves behind when playing in central midfield were also highlighted.

The criticism comes from a view that opponents have regularly found opportunities to play through United’s midfield, particularly when Fernandes steps forward.

“I was at Everton teams, playing against Manchester United, and if he’s playing in central midfield, the tactic was, play through the midfield when you see them step out because there’s so much space in there. And it’s been the theme. It’s been the theme for years that Manchester United, if they play him in central midfield, there’s spaces all behind him.”

Ashley Young’s suggested alternative is Nottingham Forest’s Morgan Gibbs-White, who is believed to offer a different balance to the United midfield.

“I would go and get Morgan Gibbs-White if you can. And I’d go and do it in January. For me, I think Morgan Gibbs-White would give more to that team than what Bruno Fernandes is now.”

It would be a major decision for United if they let him go. There is no doubt on his quality as he has scored 113 goals in 341 appearances since arriving from Sporting in 2020.

However, his future has become a talking point after United reportedly saw two contract offers rejected. Fernandes is understood to want a four-year agreement, while the club remain cautious about committing to such a deal with the midfielder already 32.

With Mauricio Pochettino in the dugout now and reportedly involved in his contract negotiations, the upcoming months can be very different for Manchester United and Bruno Fernandes.
