West Ham & Middlesbrough sent warning over what Wolves are doing right now

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

West Ham and Middlesbrough have been warned that Wolves have the potential to win the Championship if they ‘click into gear’.

It has been a positive start to life at Molineux for Cesar Peixoto, with his side sitting third and just one point away from league leaders Boro going into the midweek set of fixtures, where Wanderers host lowly Derby.

Yet, despite the good position, Wolves fans haven’t been totally convinced by Peixoto or the team, with chants directed against the manager as recently as last month.

Even if Wolves haven’t always impressed with their performances, they have found a way of getting results, and they head into the Derby game unbeaten in seven, picking up 17 points in that period.

The latest victory was a 2-1 success over Charlton, which was much more comfortable than the scoreline suggests, as Peixoto’s side were rarely troubled all afternoon.

So, they are building momentum, and speaking on The Pyramid Podcast, Jimmy from the Blades Ramble explained how Wolves have gone under the radar somewhat, as he stated they could still have more to offer, which makes them a huge threat in the title race.

“Wolves are just getting it done. Even when they haven’t been turning up (performances), they’ve been doing enough. So, it worries me that if they turn it on, and click into gear, and reach another level, they could be the ones.

“This is the opportunity for Wolves though, because everyone said West Ham, but Wolves aren’t that far behind West Ham (in terms of quality), but they’ve been overlooked because of West Ham being in the league. It shouldn’t surprise anyone if Wolves go on and win this league (even if) I still think Middlesbrough are the team to beat.”

There’s no denying that Wolves haven’t been a free-flowing, exciting team to watch for large parts this season, but it should be said that they are very solid defensively, which gives you a good foundation to pick up points.

A look at the amount of pubs within a two-mile radius of every Championship ground this season.

Peixoto walked into a club in a very difficult situation, as the fans are understandably furious with the owners for their rapid decline in recent years, and that anger is still there.

Plus, there is a feeling that more promises were broken in the summer because of the way the window played out, which has made it difficult for the supporters to fully embrace Peixoto and the team.

But, to his credit, Peixoto has navigated through that very well in terms of results, and if Wolves can beat Derby, they will be on two points a game after 17, which is a brilliant return.

Many would agree that there should still be more to come from this Wolves side, as whilst they are good defensively, the attacking players haven’t hit the heights many expected.

Mateus Mane and Rodrigo Gomes aren’t showing their best levels consistently, whilst Adam Armstrong is only just scoring regularly after a slow start. Meanwhile, Fer Lopez’s influence on the team has reduced, even if he remains a superb talent, and Jordan James hasn’t really got going in Old Gold.

When you add in that Raul Jimenez has missed most of the season through injury and won’t return until January, it just highlights why people think Wolves are capable of doing so much more.

So, the challenge for Peixoto and the players is to make that happen, as Wolves do have the talent and squad depth to go all the way this season.
