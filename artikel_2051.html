Wrexham team news and predicted XI to face Bristol City with Phil Parkinson backed to field an unchanged side

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A professional 1-0 victory at Lincoln City extended Wrexham’s unbeaten run to three games, having drawn with Wolves and secured a statement win over table-topping Middlesbrough.

Inconsistency has plagued the Red Dragons’ campaign so far though, struggling to rekindle the fine form which saw them go within touching distance of the Championship play-off places last season. But, Phil Parkinson’s side now appear to have found their feet.

After bringing an end to their five-game winless away run with a victory at Lincoln City, Wrexham are slowly but surely climbing the table and sit in 14th ahead of a trip to face a troubled Bristol City side.

The Robins have lost three on the bounce with pressure mounting on under-fire boss Michael Skubala after another underwhelming showing against Middlesbrough, although the Bristol City hierarchy have publicly backed him to turn them around.

Injuries have played a significant role in Wrexham’s slow start to the campaign, with influential forwards Josh Windass and Nathan Broadhead among those sidelined. Both are expected to return to action next month.

Question marks surround whether Oliver Rathbone will feature at Ashton Gate after being withdrawn on Saturday with a dead leg. Thankfully, Parkinson confirmed after the game that it isn’t a serious issue.

Callum O’Hare hasn’t featured since the defeat to Blackburn Rovers last month and remains a doubt with an ankle problem, while Sam Smith is a long-term absentee with an ACL injury.

In better news, New Zealand international Liberato Cacace, who is yet to make an appearance this term due to recurring calf injuries, is back in training.

Sebastian Revan is also nearing a return having been sidelined with a serious hamstring injury suffered on loan at Burton Albion in February. The 23-year-old is expected to be fit in the New Year.

Patterson; Cleworth, Worrall, Doyle; Kabore, Whiteman, Dobson, Thomason; O’Brien, Rathbone; Moore

The Wrexham side has remained largely unchanged since their much-needed victory over Middlesbrough, with Parkinson seemingly settled on his preferred starting team.

Anthony Patterson will be in goal after two clean sheets from his last three games. Max Cleworth has impressed recently with the experienced Joe Worrall alongside him and both will keep their places alongside standout performer Callum Doyle.

The Red Dragons’ positive form has coincided with Issa Kabore finding his stride in a right-wing back position, which saw him register an assist against Wolves. George Thomason has been equally important on the opposite flank and netted the winner at Lincoln, so both start again.

The midfield pivot of Ben Whiteman and George Dobson remains too, despite Ben Sheaf pushing for a place. Lewis O’Brien and Oliver Rathbone will be keen to impress once again in more advanced positions with Wrexham’s wealth of attacking talent waiting to return from injury.

They were sweating on the fitness of Kieffer Moore ahead of the trip to Sincil Bank, but he played the full 90 minutes at Sincil Bank and will likely lead the line.
