Man arrested in Nashville after DNA links him to unsolved 1990s rape cases

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Archibald Amory, 58, reportedly being held in Tennessee pending return to New York, where he was tied to two cases

A man has been arrested in Nashville after DNA linked him to a series of unsolved New York City rapes from the 1990s, according to a news report.

Archibald Amory, 58, was detained on Thursday in Hermitage, a neighborhood of Nashville, and is being held there pending his return to New York City, according to the Daily News, citing sources and Nashville authorities.

Amory has been linked via DNA to two rapes that took place in Queens, and connected to several other uncharged sex assaults. Sources said recent advances in DNA technology and investigative methods helped identify Amory.

Court papers filed in Tennessee said special victims investigators in Nashville and with the NYPD “identified Archibald Amory as a wanted fugitive in connection to a series of rape investigations originating in the jurisdiction of New York City”.

Armory appeared in court in Nashville on Monday, according to a spokesperson for the district attorney of Davidson county. He is currently being held on $800,000 bond.

Another man has also been arrested, in Miami, for allegedly raping a woman in New York’s Central Park last month.

Dereck Rivera-Deleon, 30, was arrested on Tuesday at a Miami Burger King where he had been working. Police said he will also be charged in two other Central Park rapes.

Rivera-Deleon will be charged with the sexual assault of a 39-year-old woman who was walking just outside Central Park on 24 October when he pulled a knife on her near E 66th Street and Fifth Avenue.

The suspect allegedly dragged the victim into the park, where he raped her, before a bystander, who witnessed the attack, called 911. Rivera-Deleon was identified as the attacker via surveillance film, and investigators have linked him to a Central Park rape in 2023 and another in 2021.
