Vikes Views: Nincompoop of the Week: Week 10 – Vikings at Packers

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Minnesota Vikings dismantled the Green Bay Packers, 30-10. Even in a 20-point blowout at Lambeau Field, somebody still earns the crown. Let's take a look at who was a Nincompoop this week.

The Vikings defense is going to make a lot of offensive lines look bad. The Packers offensive line was also missing a few key players. However, there are no excuses for what happened on Sunday. There were plays where four or five Vikings were in the backfield almost immediately. Jordan Love had no chance on seemingly half of his dropbacks. The eight sacks allowed were the most surrendered by any team in the NFL this season.

One of the reasons the Vikings were able to generate so much pressure was the Packers' game plan. The Vikings defense is susceptible to the run game, yet Green Bay never seriously tested them. Maybe that's because the Packers have one of the worst rushing attacks in football. They had some success when they ran the ball, but they abandoned it way too early. When your quarterback is getting battered and you keep asking him to throw deep, the results shouldn't be surprising.

If it wasn't for his 24-yard reception, Felton would have contributed negative yards to the offense. Two pre-snap penalties from a receiver already struggling to find his rhythm didn't help matters. Tai has just 99 yards in 26 career games. Felton didn't do anything obviously wrong, he'll just need to do a lot more if he wants to remain a regular part of the offense.

An early missed field goal, numerous penalties, and a late fumble on special teams didn't help the Packers' chances on Sunday. They were sloppy from start to finish. On a day when the Vikings' special teams unit was nearly perfect, Green Bay's special teams played a major role in the team's downfall.

Not a name I ever expected to put on this list. Darrisaw allowed another two sacks on Sunday, bringing his total to a career-high eight sacks allowed this season. The Vikings finally have competent quarterback play again, and the last thing they need is their franchise left tackle allowing defenders free shots at Kirk Cousins. Darrisaw is struggling to regain his form after a few injury-plagued seasons.

My vote goes to Matt LaFleur. I'd probably go LaFleur, Pack OL, Darrisaw, Pack Special Teams, and Tai Felton if I had to rank them.
