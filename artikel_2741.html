Texas A&M Basketball Suffers First Loss of the Season After Falling in Battle 4 Atlantis

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Texas A&M basketball saw its undefeated start come to an end Wednesday afternoon, falling 88-83 to Xavier in the opening round of the Battle 4 Atlantis in the Bahamas.

The Aggies entered the matchup at 6-0 and were looking to continue building momentum after an impressive start to the season. However, Xavier proved to be a difficult challenge, using strong rebounding and timely offensive runs to take control of the game.

Texas A&M battled back from a significant deficit and made things interesting late, but the Musketeers delivered when it mattered most to secure the five-point victory.

With the loss, the Aggies dropped to 6-1, while Xavier improved to 6-1 and advanced in the tournament.

PJ Haggerty led Texas A&M with 18 points, continuing his strong start to the season. Jalen Shelley also provided an important contribution with 16 points.

The Musketeers came out aggressively and took advantage of Texas A&M's early mistakes. Turnovers and fouls created problems for the Aggies, allowing Xavier to establish control during the opening minutes.

Xavier eventually built a double-digit advantage, leading 34-22 with eight minutes remaining in the first half.

Texas A&M responded with a strong finish to the period. Haggerty helped get the offense moving, and the Aggies closed the half on an 8-0 run. A three-pointer from Josh Irving capped the stretch and brought Texas A&M within four points.

Despite the comeback, Xavier still held a 45-41 advantage at halftime.

The second half started poorly for Texas A&M, as Xavier quickly extended its lead. The Musketeers pushed the advantage to 18 points at one stage, putting the Aggies in a difficult position.

The Aggies turned up their defensive pressure and began forcing turnovers with their high-pressure defense. Those mistakes created opportunities in transition and helped Texas A&M work its way back into the contest.

Haggerty continued leading the offensive attack, while Shelley provided a major spark. Shelley delivered two dunks in a 54 second stretch, helping fuel an 11 point swing that brought the Aggies within one at 75-74.

Suddenly, Texas A&M had a legitimate opportunity to complete the comeback.

With the game coming down to the final minutes, both teams needed to execute offensively.

Texas A&M continued applying pressure, but Xavier managed to keep its composure and make important shots. The Musketeers also benefited from their advantage on the boards, finishing with 50 rebounds compared to 32 for the Aggies.

The difference became especially important late, as Xavier secured additional possessions and limited Texas A&M's opportunities to take control.

The Aggies had one final chance to make things interesting, but Xavier converted its late opportunities at the free-throw line and held on for the 88-83 victory.

Mike Nwoko led the Musketeers with 20 points and 10 rebounds, while Tru Washington added 15 points. Gabriel Pozzato contributed 14 points off the bench.

Although the loss was disappointing, Texas A&M showed plenty of fight by battling back from an 18 point deficit.

The Aggies will now turn their attention toward Marquette as they continue their Battle 4 Atlantis schedule. The matchup provides an opportunity to respond and avoid allowing one defeat to derail an otherwise promising start.

Texas A&M's undefeated record is gone, but the season is still young. If the Aggies can improve their rebounding and limit early mistakes, they have the opportunity to get back on track.
