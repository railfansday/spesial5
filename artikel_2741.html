Aidan Hutchinson has torn ACL, out for season

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

On Sunday, reports emerged that Detroit Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson was feared to have suffered a season-ending knee injury, but the team would continue to do more tests when they arrived back in the United States. Unfortunately, those fears were confirmed back in Michigan, as Hutchinson tore his ACL and will miss the rest of the Lions' 2026 season, according to NFL Insider Tom Pelissero.

If there is any positive news to glean from this situation, it is that additional testing revealed no further knee damage, which was also a major concern immediately after the injury.

Still, it's a huge blow to the Lions' 2026 season, considering Hutchinson was the most disruptive force on the team's defense. Through 11 games, Hutchinson had amassed 6.0 sacks, one forced fumble, and 52 pressures (trailing only Buffalo EDGE Greg Rousseau). More than that, he was a force multiplier, making his teammates around him better by drawing more attention and bodies away from them.

On Sunday night after the Lions' win over the New England Patriots, coach Dan Campbell noted they'll need several players to step up if Hutchinson's absence lasts.

"We gotta go. We'll see where Hutch is at, but we've gotta step up now," Campbell said. "We need some guys to really step up—and if that is the case (with a serious Hutchinson injury), fill that role."

Detroit has been able to put together more of a diverse pass rush this year. Hutchinson has only been responsible for 6.0 of the team's 32 sacks (he had 14.5 of the team's 49 last year). The Lions have gotten a fair amount of production from Alim McNeill (6.5 sacks), DJ Wonnum (6.0), Derrick Moore (4.0), and Skyler Gill-Howard (2.0) along their defensive front.

"We like 'em. That's why we acquired them when we did," Campbell said of their remaining defensive linemen. "And I know this: Derrick Moore gets better every game. The more reps he gets, he gets better. It was good to get Wonnum back today. We know what Mac [McNeill] is, Sky. So can Tyleik (Williams). And all those guys, even Levi (Onwuzurike), (Tyler) Lacy, they play a different flavor, but they push the pocket; they got movement. But I think the important thing is, man, we knew we had to do to get out of this game with a win. We had to play really good defense."

For Hutchinson, it's a devastating blow to his career. Just two years ago, he suffered a gruesome broken leg that ended his 2024 season. And while he returned to full health and form in both 2025 and 2026, recovering from a torn ACL brings a whole new set of challenges for Hutchinson.
