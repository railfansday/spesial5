Jeffery Simmons: I didn't simulate snap count on false starts, Lions are "crying" about a them problem

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Long before the Bears beat the Lions on a last-second touchdown catch by wide receiver Rome Odunze, one of the storylines of the game was a run of false start penalties by the Lions.

Center Juice Scruggs was called for back-to-back false starts and then picked up a third later in the half. Left guard Christian Mahogany was flagged for another one before the next snap and there were indications from the Lions that Bears defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons was simulating the snap count to get them to jump.

Mahogany made that accusation explicit after the game that the Bears “find out the snap count of the quarterback and they use it” while adding that Lions linemen were wearing microphones that he believes will back him up. Simmons was not penalized, but was seen speaking to the referee during a break in play. He denied Mahogany’s allegation after the 39-35 win.

“We don’t have to. We don’t have to,” Simmons said, via Dave Birkett of the Detroit Free Press “We line up and play football. They quarterback check. I said, ‘Run.’ I didn’t say, ‘Hut.’ I mean, their cadence, they say, ‘White 80, white 80.’ I mean, when you lose football games, it’s a lot of complaining and a lot of crying. I mean, there was a couple times that they false start, we didn’t say a word. So that’s their problem, not ours. . . . If I say, ‘Run,’ I’m telling my defense, run. Think I care to tell them what they about to do? I’m talking to my guys. So all the crying that they’re doing, that’s a them problem, not me.”

Mahogany was flagged for taunting Simmons during a 70-yard Jahmyr Gibbs touchdown run in the fourth quarter and kicker Jake Bates missed the ensuing extra point to leave the score 35-24. The Lions would not score again and the Bears headed back to Chicago with an improbable victory.
