Lakers’ Luka Doncic leads NBA in defensive stat

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Entering Wednesday's game against the Kings, Luka Doncic was leading the NBA in defensive two-point field-goal percentage as the primary defender (21.9%) with a minimum 30 attempts defended.

It's an eye-popping stat for Doncic, who is known for his wizardry on offense but who has been a lighting rod for criticism on defense over his eight-season career.

Los Angeles Lakers guard Luka Doncic, right, looks on while guarded by Sacramento Kings forward Leaky Black during an NBA basketball game Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026. AP Photo/Scott Marshall

Doncic is having an early MVP campaign, averaging a league-leading 38.5 points, 8.1 rebounds and eight assists for the Lakers (7-2).

He has been making notable contributions on defense, too.

Doncic recently had five steals and three drawn charges in a 131-125 win over the Raptors on Nov. 1, becoming the first player to record those defensive marks in the same game since player tracking began in 2013-14, per ESPN Research.

Los Angeles Lakers guard Luka Doncic reacts during the first half of an NBA basketball game against the Sacramento Kings in Sacramento, Calif., Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026. AP Photo/Scott Marshall

Luka Doncic (77) of the Los Angeles Lakers shoots against the Sacramento Kings on Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026, at Golden 1 Center in Sacramento, Calif. NBAE via Getty Images

"Obviously, I'm not the best defender in the NBA, but I think this year I really took a step forward," Doncic said Nov. 1. "Obviously people are going to talk what they want to talk [about] me. But I know, and my team knows, that I'm better this year."

Doncic has scored 40 or more points in five of his last six games, including leading the Lakers to a 116-103 win over the Kings on Wednesday.

Doncic was recently asked in a tongue-and-cheek manner if he'd rather win the MVP award or Defensive Player of the Year.
