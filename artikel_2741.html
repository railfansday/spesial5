Which Rams’ players came out win hurt in Week 12?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Los Angeles Rams saw tight ends Tyler Higbee and Davis Allen leave their win over the Green Bay Packers early due to injury. The Rams, like many NFL teams, have had a hard time staying healthy, but somehow the Rams are finding ways to win. L.A. has won five games in a row to rise toa 7-4 record. The Rams are in the playoff race, but they'll need to keep winning to stay there.

Will the Rams be able to keep winning with all their injuries? Fair question. The Rams get some time before their next game against the Kansas City Chiefs to get healthy.

"Parkinson somehow lost a yard on two reception despite the team losing both Tyler Higbee and Davis Allen to injury, the latter of whom departed in the first half and the former of whom left at the beginning of the fourth quarter. The Rams completely controlled the flow of the game after Packers receiver Christian Watson departed with a concussion following a vicious targeting hit down the sideline just before the half, eventually leading to 47 total team carries to just 23 Matthew Stafford pass attempts. The Rams have some things to figure out given the sheer number of bodies missing after also losing two offensive linemen in the game. The long week hopefully affords them enough time to return to health ahead of a Week 13 meeting with the Chiefs.

"A pair of highlight-reel plays thrust Green Bay right back into the thick of things. LaFleur turned to trickery initially, with wideout Savion Williams taking a handoff and throwing the ball back to Love, who went on to fling a pass downfield to Watson. His toe-tapping 21-yard reception was followed by a 39-yard catch and run from another receiver, Matthew Golden.

Watson was the one who found pay dirt, reeling in a 6-yard touchdown pass from Love midway through the second quarter. That score cut the Packers' deficit to 13-10.

Watson's night came to a premature end on Green Bay's next drive. LaFleur rolled the dice on fourth-and-1 from the L.A. 30-yard line, and it originally looked like that decision was going to be rewarded. Love located Watson for what would have been a chains-moving pickup, except the pass was broken up by a brutal blow from Kinchens near the sideline that leveled Watson."

While the hit was violent, it seems excessive for him to have been ejected. While players will get ejected for targeting at the college level, that's not something that's seen very often in the NFL. Players can be ejected for two qualifying unsportsmanlike conduct penalties. However, this was Kinchens' first penalty. Officials also have the option of disqualifying a player for a flagrant act. Kinchens' hit was deemed to be flagrant"

"The Los Angeles Rams handled their business to start Thanksgiving week, taking down the Green Bay Packers in a 26-13 win on Wednesday night. Now, they wait and see if they get any help from other teams around the league on Thursday and Sunday afternoon.

According to The Athletic, the Rams have a 47% chance to make the playoffs after Wednesday's win. There are three games that can swing their odds a few percentage points if the results go Los Angeles' way. Here's a rooting guide for the rest of Week 12."

"Rams make a statement. Entering this game, it was fair to expect the Packers could prevail as part of their playoff push. The Rams were playing with an offense held together by duct tape and chewing gum and had defeated a quartet of opponents (the Raiders, Chargers, Commanders, Cardinals) that weren't exactly the NFL's version of The Expendables. In terms of reputation, the Packers entered Wednesday night with more respect for their potential than the opposition could boast. The Rams decided to shove that skepticism right back in the direction from which it came Wednesday night by leaning deeper into their new identity, riding the running game to dominate time of possession and grind the Packers down to a pulp. Matthew Stafford only needed to complete 16 of 23 passes for 140 yards, threw an interception that could have been pivotal, and ended up not worrying about it for more than roughly 55 seconds. The Rams won the physical battle in the trenches against the Packers' beleaguered offensive line, shut down Green Bay's offense entirely in the second half and walked away with a resounding win that not only moved them to 7-4, but will likely make the rest of the NFL take them seriously again."

"Los Angeles Rams press conference with head coach Sean McVay, quarterback Matthew Stafford, outside linebacker Myles Garrett, and running back Blake Corum following Wednesday's 26-13 Week 12 win over the Green Bay Packers at SoFi Stadium."

"INGLEWOOD, Calif. – Behind a career performance by running back Blake Corum and timely playmaking by their defense, the Rams defeated the Packers 23-13 Wednesday night at SoFi Stadium to extend their win streak to five games.

Corum led Los Angeles in rushing with 32 carries for 184 yards and one touchdown, the rushing yards a new single-game career high. Meanwhile, running back Kyren Williams added 12 carries for 46 yards and one touchdown.

Including the two carries by wide receiver Xavier Smith, and excluding Stafford's one scramble, the Rams finished with twice as many rush attempts (46) as a team as pass attempts (23)."
