Eagles-Giants final score: Philadelphia loses at home to New York for first time since 2013

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Philadelphia Eagles are 2-7 after losing to the New York Giants on Sunday afternoon. Final score: 19 to 11.

This marks the Eagles' seventh straight loss. The Birds are 5-12 in their last 17 games, which is the equivalent of a full regular season.

This result also marks the Eagles' first loss to the Giants in Philly since the 2013 season. A historic loss in a historically bad season.

The Eagles turned to Joe Milton at quarterback for a spark but they did not get it. The offense was pretty ineffective all day long. Vic Fangio's defense mostly battled … but their effort wasn't enough.

Next up for the Eagles: the NFL trade deadline is at 4:00 PM Eastern on Tuesday, November 10 before the Eagles' bye in Week 10. They'll return to host the Pittsburgh Steelers in Week 11.

Read on for a recap and stay tuned for BGN's postgame coverage. Also, check me out talking about this game on the PHLY Eagles postgame show.

The Giants brought in Dominic Zvada for a 53-yard field goal make. EAGLES 0, GIANTS 3.
