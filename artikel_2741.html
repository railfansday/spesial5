Titans WR had 'incident' involving law enforcement at Nashville airport

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The bye week was not all quiet for the Tennessee Titans.

Wide receiver Calvin Ridley was involved in an incident at Nashville International Airport over the weekend, with details remaining scant other than the Titans acknowledging they are aware of the "incident" and are communicating with the Metro Nashville Airport Authority.

"With regards to Calvin, we're aware of everything that's happening. From my understanding, it was a very minor miscommunication at the airport," Titans head coach Robert Saleh said Monday, Nov. 9. "Unfortunately, the law enforcement (agencies) have it, and that's about as far as I can go with commenting."

Ridley has six catches for 91 yards in seven games this season and could be a candidate to be moved to another team prior to Tuesday's trade deadline.

The Titans are coming off their extended rest with a 1-7 record and heading into a Nov. 15 matchup against the AFC South's first-place Jacksonville Jaguars.

It has not been a smooth season for Tennessee. The Titans released defensive lineman Jordan Elliott last month after he pulled a gun and threatened a teammate in the parking lot of the team's facility.

Saleh was asked if the latest Ridley development was "frustrating," but the former head coach of the New York Jets would not go that far.

"I think when you're losing everything's exacerbated, but all thirty-two teams deal with things throughout the season," Saleh said. "But yeah, it's unfortunate with what happened."

A first-round pick of the Atlanta Falcons in 2018, Ridley has also played for the Jacksonville Jaguars and Tennessee Titans. He served a suspension during the 2022 season for betting on NFL games while he was injured the previous season. This is his third season of a four-year, $92 million contract he signed with Tennessee.
