New 9-hole Florida golf course opens, will soon welcome public

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It isn't open to the public just yet, but golfers wiil soon have a chance to play the newest layout in Marion County, Florida: Irons Nine Pitch & Putt at On Top of the World in Ocala.

The short course, which debuted on Oct. 23, is currently open only to OTOW and Stone Creek residents. It will be open to all at some future, unannounced date.

Irons Nine is, as the name suggests, a nine-hole course where you only need your irons (and a putter.) It is a par-27 short course designed for beginners and experts alike.

"With rounds taking approximately 45 minutes, Irons Nine Pitch & Putt provides a convenient opportunity to enjoy golf with family and friends," the course says in media materials.

It also has its own pro shop at 9265 SW 92nd Court Road. Learn more at Irons9.com

In October, Golfweek released its annual list of the Top 25 public-access par-3, short, and non-traditional courses in the United States. The list includes several Florida courses. Golfweek said it started publishing this list in 2023 "to acknowledge a recent and rapid rise in short courses."
