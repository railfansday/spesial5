Bears claim DB Isaac Yiadom off waivers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Chicago Bears have claimed defensive back Isaac Yiadom off waivers, the team announced on Tuesday.

Yiadom, 30, is listed at 6'1" and 188 pounds, and he's primarily played as an outside cornerback during his time in the NFL. Originally a third-round pick by the Broncos in 2018, he has played for six different teams over his nine-year career, most recently playing in three games for the Commanders this season. He's played under Bears defensive coordinator Dennis Allen before, as he played in New Orleans under Allen in 2022 and 2023.

In his time playing in the NFL, Yiadom has played enough to qualify for a PFN CB Impact grade in three of his nine seasons thus far. Most recently, he graded as a middle-of-the-road starter for the 49ers in 2024, finishing with a 75.9 grade. In that season, he had 46 tackles, two forced fumbles, an interception, and six pass deflections in 17 games.

With Yiadom being an outside cornerback by trade, one figures that he'll project as depth behind the likes of Jaylon Johnson and Tyrique Stevenson, more so than a backup nickel behind Kyler Gordon. However, since Gordon is currently injured, one could wonder if they feel good about either Clark Phillips III or Malik Muhammad in the nickel in the veteran's absence. Muhammad has also taken plenty of snaps outside, though Chicago would need more depth like Yiadom outside if they plan on playing the rookie in the slot.
