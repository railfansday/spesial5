Loss to Cincinnati eliminates Deion Sanders, Colorado from bowl eligibility

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Deion Sanders’s fourth season at Colorado will end without a bowl game.

The Buffaloes lost at Cincinnati on Saturday, 35-28. The outcome drops Colorado to 4-7 with one game to play.

Sanders became the head coach of the program in 2023. The Buffaloes qualified for the Alamo Bowl in 2024. In Sanders’s three other seasons, the Buffaloes didn’t become bowl eligible.

The loss to Cincinnati leaves Sanders with an overall record of 20-28. His status for 2027 is uncertain, at best.

“This is a good — solid to me — football team,” Sanders told reporters after the game. “I know the record is not indicative of that and it’s disappointing and I don’t even understand. . . . [Y]ou’ve got to give Cincinnati a tremendous amount of credit, but it don’t seem like, to me, the better team was victorious.”

He has a $25.5 million buyout, which could buy him a fifth year with the program.
