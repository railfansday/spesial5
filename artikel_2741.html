James Harden erupts for 51 to pass Kobe Bryant on 50-point games list

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

James Harden turned back the clock Friday, Nov. 20, reminding NBA opponents and fans why he is one of the best scorers in league history.

The 37-year-old scored 51 points Friday to lead the Cavaliers to a 128-119 win over the Indiana Pacers in an NBA Cup East Group B game at Rocket Arena in Cleveland.

Harden's 51 points were the most he's scored in a game this year and came two days shy of the one-year anniversary of his last 50-point game, when, as a member of the Los Angeles Clippers, he shelled out 55 in a win against the Charlotte Hornets on Nov. 22, 2025.

But his scoring output Friday night represented a more meaningful milestone: Harden notched his 26th 50-point game of his career, passing Kobe Bryant for third all time in NBA history. Michael Jordan ranks second with 31 such games, while the all-time leader, Wilt Chamberlain, seems likely to retain that record for a very long time having crossed the 50-point threshold an astounding 118 times.

Only three other players have scored at least 50 points this season, and all three are MVP candidates. Los Angeles Lakers star Luka Dončić has passed the 50-point mark twice, tallying 53 against the Portland Trail Blazers on Nov. 5 and 52 against the Utah Jazz on Oct. 25. San Antonio Spurs star Victor Wembanyama dropped 52 against the Dallas Mavericks on Nov. 4, while Detroit Pistons guard Cade Cunningham scored 52 in a double overtime win against the Washington Wizards on Nov. 12.

Harden ranks ninth in NBA history and 10th in NBA-ABA history for most career points with 29,633 in 1,237 games played. Next on the career list is Hall of Famer Julius Erving with 30,026 career points in the NBA and ABA. Philadelphia 76ers star LeBron James holds the record for most points in league history with 43,628 (through Nov. 20).
