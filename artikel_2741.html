Arizona Cardinals sign wide receiver Bub Means to practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Arizona Cardinals wide receiver room is depleted, so they have two choices.

Sign a veteran who may or may not be effective… Zay Jones like.

Throw as many young guys at it and see if any of them make it.

The Cardinals have decided to try both, and today they added another potential option in Bub Means.

The Arizona Cardinals Football Club today announced that the team has signed wide receiver Bub Means to the practice squad.

The signing took place today at the Dignity Health Arizona Cardinals Training Center in Tempe, AZ.

Means (6-2, 215) appeared in seven games (one start) as a rookie with New Orleans in 2024 and had nine receptions for 118 yards and one touchdown. He entered the league with the Saints as a fifth-round selection (170th overall) in the 2024 NFL Draft out of Pittsburgh. The 25-year old Means missed the 2025 season on injured reserve with New Orleans and was most recently on the Chiefs practice squad in addition to spending time on the Browns practice squad earlier this season.

Welcome to the desert, Bub and good luck.
