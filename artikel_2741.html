Lakers honor Lawrence Tanter with video tribute, commemorative banner

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Lakers honored Lawrence Tanter with a video tribute on Monday as the Lakers played the Jazz.

The video included footage of Tanter introducing Magic Johnson, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Shaquille O'Neal, Kobe Bryant, LeBron James and Luka Doncic, as well as his signature "Laker girl" call and a banner drop over his 43 years as the team's P.A. announcer.

Former Lakers Mychal Thompson and Michael Cooper then took the court to say a few words about Tanter, who died at age 76 in September.

Mychal Thompson, left, and Michael Cooper honor the late Lawrence Tanter during a game between the Lakers and Jazz on Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, in Los Angeles. Getty Images

"As much as you guys love him, we love him even more," said Thompson, who won two championships with the Lakers in 1987 and 1988 as Tanter served as the team's P.A. announcer.

Tanter was the voice of the Lakers during 10 NBA championships after being hired during the 1982-83 season.

The Los Angeles Lakers honor Lawrence Tanter outside Crypto.Com Arena on Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, in Los Angeles, before a game against the Utah Jazz. NBAE via Getty Images

The Lakers also honored Tanter with a commemorative banner on Star Plaza outside of Crypto.com Arena before Monday's game. Fans were able to share personal messages and memories from 4 p.m. to 8 p.m.

Tanter suffered a a stroke in March before moving into an advisory role in June.

The Los Angeles Lakers honor Lawrence Tanter outside of Crypto.Com Arena before a game against the Utah Jazz in Los Angeles on Monday, Nov. 23, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

"We are deeply saddened to share the news of the passing of legendary Lakers public address announcer Lawrence Tanter," the Lakers said in a statement in September.

"For 43 years, LT's voice served as the soundtrack to Lakers games at the arena. He will be remembered as a cherished member of the Lakers family."
