Buy Big Appliances on Holiday Weekends – Ideally When Last Year’s Models Go on Sale

If your old refrigerator, washer, dryer, dishwasher, or range can hold out a few more weeks, buy its replacement on a holiday sale weekend, ideally one when stores are clearing out last year’s models. Before you pay, confirm the warranty and what delivery, haul-away, and installation will cost.

Like most big-ticket items, large appliances go on sale on a yearly schedule. The main sales fall on five holiday weekends: Presidents Day, Memorial Day, the Fourth of July, Labor Day, and Black Friday.

Not every appliance gets its new models in the same season. New refrigerator models arrive in May, while new washers, dryers, and dishwashers usually come in September and October, says Jason Hallam, a vice president of appliance merchandising at Lowe’s, in a Fidelity guide.

If yours can’t hold out for its weekend, shop the Presidents Day or Fourth of July sales instead.

Say a refrigerator lists at $1,400 and a Memorial Day sale takes 25% off. You’d pay $1,050 instead of $1,400, a $350 saving for waiting a few weeks. Compare last year’s model with the new one, spec by spec, so you know which new features you’d give up for the lower price.

Ask the store whether the sale price includes delivery, haul-away of the old appliance, and installation. Best Buy, for one, charges $49.99 to haul away the old one on delivery, and installation may cost extra. Then confirm the model comes new in the box with the full manufacturer’s warranty.
