Personal Consumer Issues • Crawlspace Dehumidifier

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Four years ago, I purchased a SantaFe Ultra Air 120 dehumidifier for the crawlspace. It was recommended by one of our contractors because many of the floor joists were moist. I spent $13,800 to install this unit, and now it is freezing up. The 2-year labor warranty has expired, but I still have the 6-year parts warranty. After some research, I noticed that many buyers report these units failing frequently. It seems like a lot to spend $13,800 for a unit that lasts only about four years. Has anyone else had experience with these units?
