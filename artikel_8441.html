Book a Hostel’s Private Room for About 40% Less Than a Hotel – Bunks Optional

Many hostels rent private rooms, not just dorm bunks, for well under a hotel room’s price, though you may share a bathroom. Book one that recent guests rate highly for security and location, and cook in the shared kitchen to cut your food bill too.

Hostelz, a hostel price-comparison site, put the median price of a hostel’s private room at $58 a night in the 12 months through October 2026, against $100 for a hotel room. At those prices, 10 nights cost $580 in the private room and $1,000 in a hotel, and you usually give up daily cleaning, not the lock on your door.

A dorm bed, often with a locker, runs a median 26% of what a hotel room costs in the same city. Dorm beds ran a median $10 a night in Hanoi, $24 in Mexico City, and $43 in London. A private room in the same hostel runs about three times the dorm price, and you get the room to yourself. A couple pays about $39 for two dorm beds, so the $58 room costs them just $19 more.

Hostelworld takes reviews only from guests who booked through it and stayed, and they score each hostel in seven categories, security and location among them. Read the newest reviews for noise and how clean the shared bathrooms are, and check the house rules for quiet hours and an age limit, which some party hostels set at about 35 or 40.

Private-room guests still share the guest kitchen and common rooms, so you can cook some meals and meet other travelers. Expect more noise than in a hotel, and book a room with its own bathroom if you’d rather not share one. If you’re traveling as a family or a group, compare other cheaper places to stay than a hotel too, such as a vacation rental.

Hostelling International USA, the nonprofit that runs HI hostels in New York, Boston, Chicago, and other cities, charges $20 a year for a membership. Members get 10% off every stay at those hostels and member prices at participating HI hostels abroad. The 10% discount covers the $20 fee on $200 worth of stays.

Before you book, price the private room against hotels in the same neighborhood for the same nights, so you know what it saves you.
