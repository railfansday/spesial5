Week in review: Boston, Baylor and Penn State universities in budget holes

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Let Higher Ed Dive's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

We’re rounding up last week’s most interesting stories, from financial woes at major institutions to college campus takeovers in Pennsylvania and Florida.

Boston University has so far missed its revenue estimates for the fiscal 2027 year by $42 million, which officials attributed to tuition and enrollment pressures. The news comes after the private institution last year laid off 120 staff members and eliminated another 120 vacant jobs because of budget woes.

That’s how many U.S. colleges reported drops in international graduate student enrollment this fall, with an average decline of 13%, according to a new survey released by NAFSA: Association of International Educators and others. Meanwhile, 45% of colleges said they had fewer foreign undergraduates enroll. The vast majority said federal policies were creating barriers to international recruitment.
