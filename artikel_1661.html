Liverpool are lining up a move for an exciting Portuguese sensation. He is an unreal talent.

Even though the football we are witnessing on the pitch at the moment isn't always pretty, it's an exciting time to be a Liverpool fan right now.

Seeing the beginning of a project blossom into something special is the best part of being an avid fan.

The glory and the sheer euphoria of lifting trophies under Jurgen Klopp was incredible. But it was even sweeter having endured the journey along the way.

It's the journey that matters the most. Overcoming those hard times. Just like the club's anthem goes: through the storm, there's a golden sky.

And there is lots of optimism to believe that the golden sky isn't too far away in Andoni Iraola's reign either.
