Maresca insists Haaland mood is ‘normal’ after poor Man City form

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Enzo Maresca insisted Erling Haaland's mood is "normal" after the striker's recent interview about Manchester City's poor form.

City came from behind twice to salvage a 2-2 draw against Napoli in the Champions League on Tuesday, with Rayan Cherki scoring a late equaliser at Etihad Stadium.

And since MD8 in the Premier League, only Crystal Palace (0) have picked up fewer points than City (one).

City are just the second side in Football League history to win their first seven league games in a season and then none of their next four, along with Sheffield Wednesday in the 1904-05 top-flight.

Haaland has scored just once in his last five games in all competitions for City, and after their draw with Napoli, he said he had "no idea what the team is missing" and accepted "something was not clicking fully".

While Maresca conceded Haaland is "upset", he believes it is completely normal for the striker to feel that way amid their recent run of results.

"I think Erling is a winner. It's normal that he's not happy when we don't win games, but not just Erling, everyone," said the City boss.

"Remember, just a few weeks ago he was dancing on the pitch. You can write and think many different things.

"Erling is upset. I'm upset because we're not winning games. But this is our job; this is football. That's why I need to maintain the balance.

"He's a nine. They live day by day for goals and winning games. If they don't score or win games, they're upset.

"So, I don't think that affects his game, but for sure he's not happy not winning games, but everyone is on the same page.

"I don't see any problem at all with Erling."

City face league leaders Arsenal on Sunday, and Haaland has enjoyed recent outings against the Gunners.

He has scored in each of his last four Premier League games against Arsenal and could become the fourth player to score in five in a row in the competition against the Gunners, after Charlie Austin (December 2018), Harry Kane (April 2017), and Jimmy Floyd Hasselbaink (September 2000).
