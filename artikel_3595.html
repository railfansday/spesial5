Diablo 4 Is Making Helltides A Hell Of A Lot Easier

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

One of Diablo 4's oldest endgame activities, Helltides, is getting a major refresh for Season 16.

As detailed by developer Blizzard in their full breakdown of Season of Tyrael's Gambit, Helltides will soon be far more efficient to run than before. The whole point of the activity is to kill as many demons as fast as possible, acquire the Aberrant Cinders they drop, and then use those cinders to open chests for items.

Dying means losing half of the cinders you've collected, but they are also lost when the Helltide ends. Oftentimes that means having left over amounts of cinders you can't spend, only to then go to the next Helltide five minutes later and repeat the process from zero. It's been this way since Diablo 4 launched back in 2023.

In Season 16, that will finally change. Blizzard is getting rid of the downtime between Helltides spawning and making it so there will always be a Helltide up in one of the game's original zones, as well as one in expansion areas like Hahantu or Skovos. Even better, Aberrant Cinders won't be lost once a Helltide ends. Instead, players can collect up to 5,000 cinders at a time to be spent on opening chests within the activity. Additionally, the overall loot quality from opening chests in the Helltide will be improved and chests can be opened instantly.

Those changes should go a long way towards making Helltides feel more valuable and efficient when trying to farm gear, as you can now blast demons to your heart's content until reaching the Aberrant Cinder cap. Just don't die while you have a full 5,000 stored up. 

Diablo 4 Season 16 launches December 1. Alongside its Helltide changes, the season will bring big updates to War Plans as well. A new system, War Plans Tactics, will give players more control over what activities appear in their War Plans, as well as more options when it comes to rerolling them and the rewards they grant. Players will also have new Horadric items to chase that will modify specific Talisman sets, greatly increasing their power. 

Blizzard additionally announced many of the features that were added as part of Diablo 4 Season 15, Hell's Legacy, will be made permanent. Those additions include the season's capstone dungeons, optional bosses like Moophisto, the Annihilus Seal, Hellfire Torch Charm, and the season's legacy Unique items like Stone of Jordan.
