Taylor Swift Dazzles at the 2026 CMA Awards With Multiple Wardrobe Changes

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Taylor Swift made her triumphant return to the 60th annual CMA Awards —and best believe her wardrobe met the monumental moment.

Swift, 36, waltzed down the Wednesday, November 18, red carpet in a Toy Story-yellow strapless gown, which she later traded for two separate frocks during the show.

The pop star, who famously got her start in the country music scene before making the often-difficult transition to pop music, received a Single of the Year nomination for her Toy Story 5 song “I Knew It, I Knew You.” Swift, however, ultimately lost to Ella Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas,” though the loss didn’t let that slow down her show-stopping, star-studded live performance.
