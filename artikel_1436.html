MU: Mauricio Pochettino could delay his arrival

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Manchester United will have to wait before being able to count on Mauricio Pochettino. While an agreement has reportedly been reached to make the United States coach the successor to Michael Carrick, the Argentine coach is not expected to immediately join the Red Devils. According to information from The AthleticPochettino would have decided to finish the international gathering with the American selection before taking up his new role at Old Trafford. A situation which could complicate the debut of the 54-year-old technician, since Manchester United hoped to take advantage of this truce to prepare for his arrival and allow him to work with his new squad. Mancunian leaders would nevertheless have accepted this schedule, while a financial agreement estimated at 9 million euros would have been found with the American Federation to release the former coach of Tottenham and PSG.

Despite the numerous information regarding his future, Mauricio Pochettino continues to behave normally with his players. When training resumed with the United States, the Argentinian only mentioned the session program and the integration of three young players from the under-20s, without referring to Manchester United. “ Everything here is as usual. We practice and play like we don’t hear anything “, explained American defender Chris Richards, recognizing however that the group had become aware of the rumors. “ We train like he’s going to be our coach again tomorrow, because we don’t know what’s going to happen “, he added. The United States is scheduled to face Haiti twice in the CONCACAF Nations League, Saturday in Miami and Tuesday in Cincinnati. An immediate departure of Pochettino and his deputy Jesús Pérez would therefore place the American Federation in a delicate situation. For Manchester United, this setback could have sporting consequences: if his appointment is confirmed, the Argentinian would only have a few days to prepare for his first match, scheduled for Liverpool.
