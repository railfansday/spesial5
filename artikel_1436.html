Atlético: Julian Alvarez’s catastrophic evening

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The series lasted all summer and did not have the epilogue desired by the player, who was very touched or even dejected by the situation. The weeks have passed and Julian Alvarez has digested his immense disappointment at not having joined FC Barcelona during the transfer window. For openly declaring his desire to leave, he was punished by his own supporters, who did not fail to boo him at the start of the season. Until things gradually return to normal.

But inevitably, the reception of the Blaugrana on Sunday evening had symbolic significance for the 26-year-old Argentine striker, established by his coach Diego Simeone. And the match could ideally have started if he had been more inspired from the 5th minute. Thrown against Joan Garcia, he chose to go around him and finally found himself without a shooting angle, provoking an initial reaction of spite from his supporters. The first period, in favor of Atlético, gave him two shooting opportunities but it was in the second half that he missed a second face-to-face, with a lob off target. If he was decisive on Giuliano Simeone’s goal, he could blame himself for his two missed opportunities, which could have offered victory to his team, now relegated to 3rd place in Liga.

Obviously, the Spanish press had its eyes on him on Sunday evening, and it is not kind to the Spider. “Julian returns to hell”written as follows Marca. “Julián was completely dejected by this evening, when everything seemed to work in his favor so that he was almost completely forgiven for his summer dream with this same team”note A.S. on his side. His coach defended him after the match.

“He played an important match, he was involved in three other actions in addition to his assist for Giuliano. And the center for Baena was also his. We all wanted this goal, but we will wait because he is a great player and to achieve our goals, we need the best Julián, otherwise we will not succeed.explained Diego Simeone, as confirmation that everything is not yet back to normal for his player, whistled by part of the public after his second miss.
