FIFA: the surprise candidacy of Philippe Diallo gains weight against Gianni Infantino

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The race for the FIFA presidency is entering its home stretch. While Gianni Infantino intends to run for a new mandate during the election scheduled for March 18, 2027 in Rabat, Morocco, the opponents of the outgoing president still have a few days to find a candidate capable of bringing their challenge. The deadline for submitting applications is set for November 18, i.e. Wednesday, and UEFA is working to present a profile likely to unite its 55 member associations. A delicate mission for the European body, which has been looking for several weeks for a sufficiently influential personality to embody a credible alternative. Meanwhile, the Italian-Swiss leader is increasing diplomatic trips and seduction operations with the various confederations to consolidate his lead, at a time when Kozo Tashima, honorary president of the Japanese Football Federation, is also a candidate.

However, the avenues have multiplied in recent weeks without really coming to fruition. As we revealed to you exclusively this morning, UEFA once considered supporting Luis Figo, with the idea of ​​banking on the notoriety of the former Portuguese international to lead the battle against the FIFA president. An option which was not ultimately unanimous within the European body. Other names have also been circulating, starting with that of Arsène Wenger, director of world football development at FIFA, whose possible candidacy would have required a spectacular change of position. The former Arsenal coach would have ultimately declined this possibility. Didier Drogba and Mikaël Silvestre were also mentioned, without these avenues materializing. Faced with these successive refusals and the urgency of the timetable, European leaders must now make their choice.

According to information from Guardianconfirmed by RMC SportPhilippe Diallo could finally be the surprise candidate chosen by UEFA to challenge Gianni Infantino. The president of the French Football Federation, in office since 2023, would have taken a step ahead of the other contenders and his candidacy could be made official on Tuesday. The UEFA executive committee must meet to examine this possibility and seek the approval of the 55 member federations. The choice of a French leader would mark a change in strategy for Europe, which initially hoped to rely on a personality with greater international notoriety, from the field or the sidelines. Philippe Diallo, however, presents the profile of an experienced administrator and has gradually established himself as one of the most assertive opponents of Infantino. Last September, he considered that the initiatives of the FIFA president during and after the 2026 World Cup had seriously damaged the image and unity of football, considering that trust was broken.

It now remains to be seen whether this candidacy can really shake up Gianni Infantino who maintains a very favorable position. Philippe Diallo could count on the support of the vast majority of European associations, but also try to convince several French-speaking federations of the African Football Confederation. A job of persuasion which promises to be particularly complicated, since the outgoing president benefits from numerous relays on the African continent and in other confederations. According to RMC SportInfantino would already have 80 to 90 votes acquired from the votes expected in March 2027. Philippe Diallo’s lack of international notoriety also constitutes a serious handicap, despite his weight within French and European football. THE Guardian also underlines that the multiplication of opposing candidates could ultimately serve the interests of the outgoing president, who would have an easy time presenting himself as the candidate of unity in the face of a divided opposition. The possible appointment of the boss of the FFF would open a new chapter in this political battle, without guaranteeing UEFA the ability to shake Gianni Infantino.
