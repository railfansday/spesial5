The Mercedes-Benz GLA enters its third generation with the 2028 model, which for the first time will also offer an EV variant.

The GLA EV is coming as the rear-wheel-drive GLA250+ and the all-wheel-drive GLA350 4Matic.

The GLA250+ has 268 horsepower, while the GLA350 gets 349 ponies and delivers brisker acceleration.

You could think of the GLA EV as the hatchback, SUV-ish version of the CLA EV sedan. The two are mechanically identical.

Compared to the current GLA, the 2028 model is longer and wider, and sits on a longer wheelbase.

A three-pointed-star motif appears in the headlights.
