Prehistoric 'giant rat' found in Thailand from fossil teeth

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

A giant rodent weighing around 30 kilograms (66 pounds) roamed what is now southern Thailand 33 to 35 million years ago, scientists said, after identifying a previously unknown species from two fossilized teeth.
