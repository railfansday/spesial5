From Failed Businessman to Accused Plotter in the Killing of Haiti’s Leader

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Prosecutors say Christian Sanon was a con man who oversaw a plan to assassinate the country’s president. His defense says he played no part in the plot’s fatal outcome.

He claimed to be a Florida doctor and pastor, but there is no record of him having a U.S. medical degree or leading a church.

He filed for bankruptcy, but his problems really began when he started promoting and broadcasting plans to become Haiti’s president, calling himself the country’s savior and boasting that he had the backing of the U.S. government.

Now, Christian Emmanuel Sanon is on trial in Miami, accused by federal prosecutors of conspiring to kidnap or kill the last elected president of Haiti, Jovenel Moïse, who was assassinated in his bedroom in July 2021.

In court, Mr. Sanon, dressed in a suit and tie, has the bearing of a statesman. But prosecutors say the 67-year-old Haitian American was nothing more than a con man.

The well-spoken Florida resident is accused of teaming up with a South Florida security firm and a hit squad of Colombian former military commandos, who stormed Mr. Moïse’s presidential residence at night and shot him at point-blank range.

The killing plunged Haiti into chaos that continues to grip the country with gangs controlling much of the capital, Port-au-Prince, preventing an election to replace Mr. Moïse. (His widow, Martine Moïse, has registered to run for president in an election that, for now, is scheduled next year.)
