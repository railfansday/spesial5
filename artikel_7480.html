Snapchat will show more info on mutual friends

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The app will allow users who opt in to see a list of friends they have in common with someone, directly on their profile.

Snapchat wants to get more people talking in the app, so it’s expanding its info display on mutual friends. The updated format will enable users to see expanded profile info for any friends that they have in common with connections.

As shown in these example screens, Snapchat’s mutual friends update will give users more info about friends they have in common directly on their profile display.

To be clear, Snapchat already displays a count of how many friends users have in common. The mutual friends feature builds on that by showing who those friends are.

That could get more people interacting, and given the slowdown of Snapchat’s audience growth, that could be a critical focus.

Snapchat’s user growth has been stagnating, especially in the U.S. and Europe, where its daily active user count has been flat through the third quarter. As such, Snapchat needs to capitalize on its existing audience in order to maximize opportunities and drive advertiser interest.

Maybe offering more specific connection tools could help boost audience interest.

The question then is whether Snapchat users will actually want to display their expanded information to friends of friends, or whether they’d prefer to keep their Snapchat friend circles exclusive.

Given the more private focus of the app, the latter seems more likely, but again, Snapchat needs to try new things in order to drive user activity.

Snapchat also said that both the user and their connections will have to opt-in to the feature before their expanded information is displayed to others.

Mutual friends is now available to Snapchat users in the U.S., Australia, France and Sweden on iOS and Android.
