Man Utd target Fulham's Bassey - Monday's gossip

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Manchester United target Fulham's Calvin Bassey and Brentford's Nathan Collins, AC Milan want Endrick on loan, and Premier League clubs chase Nico Paz.

Fulham and Nigeria defender Calvin Bassey, 26, has been identified as one of Mauricio Pochettino's key targets for Manchester United in the January transfer window. (Telegraph - subscription), external

Brentford's Republic of Ireland defender Nathan Collins, 25, is on Manchester United's radar but is also wanted by Liverpool and Tottenham. (Football Insider), external

Manchester United are keen to keep 29-year-old defender Noussair Mazraoui, who is attracting interest from AC Milan, Juventus and clubs in Saudi Arabia. (Caught Offside), external

Juventus midfielder Weston McKennie, 28, is on the radar of Pochettino, who has coached him with the USA side. (Gazzetta dello Sport - in Italian), external

AC Milan are hopeful that their relationship with Brazil legend Kaka can help them sign compatriot Endrick, 20, on loan from Real Madrid. (Gazzetta dello Sport - in Italian), external

Paris St-Germain, Arsenal, Chelsea and Manchester United retain an interest in Como and Argentina midfielder Nico Paz, 22, but Real Madrid hold a buy-back clause. (Fichajes - in Spanish), external

Everton are eyeing a move for 26-year-old Brazilian winger Samuel Lino, who has 21 goal involvements in 33 games for Flamengo this season. (Liverpool Echo), external

Stuttgart have no plans to sell Morocco midfielder Bilal El Khannouss, 22, despite interest from Leeds United. (Football Insider), external

Manchester United's Netherlands striker Joshua Zirkzee, 25, and Chelsea's English winger Jamie Gittens, 22, are wanted by Napoli in January. (Tuttomercato), external
