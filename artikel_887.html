For just the second time in 20 seasons, Novak Djokovic has failed to qualify for the season-ending ATP Finals after a shock defeat in the second round of the Rolex Paris Masters.

The 39-year-old, who also failed to make the 2017 edition due to a elbow injury, needed a run to the semi-finals to keep himself in with a chance of clinching a spot at the season-ending showpiece in Turin.

Djokovic, a seven-time champion in Paris, started well against 25-year-old Australian Rinky Hijikata, the world No 83, but faded badly in a 2-6 6-3 6-3 defeat.

With two places still available for the season-ending championship, Djokovic needed a run to the semi-finals in Paris to stay alive in his hopes, but his exit leaves the door open for the remaining contenders, including 12th-placed Taylor Fritz and 13th-placed Tommy Paul.

Hijikata told Sky Sports: "It took a lot. I was getting outplayed in the first set and had to hang tough.

"In the end I was glad I maintained my level and trusted myself in the big moments. I felt I had to take it to him and play aggressive."

Alexander Zverev became the first male player in the history of tennis to lose in straight sets on his debut match as world No 1.

The 29-year-old German, who won the French and US Opens this year, was elevated to top spot in the ATP rankings for the first time on Monday.

However, he blew a 5-2 lead in a first-set tie-break on his way to a surprise 7-6 (7-5) 6-2 defeat by Italy's Lorenzo Musetti at the Paris Masters.

Speaking in his press conference, Zverev said: "To put it simply, he played really well and I played really badly. It was perhaps one of my worst matches in the last few months.

"I was a bit annoyed the last couple of days because achieving the world No 1 status is something that every player dreams of. But then I feel like I'm the only world No 1 in history who has to explain why it's normal for me to be No 1, which I find quite difficult because I deserve to be where I am, even though I lost today.

"I don't think anyone has had to explain themselves as much as I have in the last couple of days, which is annoying."
