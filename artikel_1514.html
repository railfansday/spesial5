Gasperini pushing to sign Kvernadze in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Gasperini has chosen Giorgi Kvernadze. And Roma could try to accommodate him as early as January.

The Frosinone winger is one of the first names the coach mentioned to Tony D’Amico after the summer transfer window closed.

This interest has been ongoing, as Gasperini had already been following the Georgian last season, when he was playing in Serie B.

The move up a division hasn’t intimidated him; on the contrary, Kvernadze has continued to demonstrate the qualities that had attracted the attention of the Giallorossi coach, convincing him to ask the club to explore the matter further.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, his technique, acceleration, and unpredictability are the characteristics that make him particularly attractive to Gasperini’s project.

The Georgian responds above all to a specific need: finding a right-footed attacking midfielder capable of starting on the left. This is the profile Gasperini has sought since his arrival in the capital, and Kvernadze could finally provide it. It’s no surprise, then, that his name has been persistently recurring in transfer discussions at Trigoria.

Roma, however, doesn’t want to wait too long. Signing him during the winter transfer window could prove more advantageous than in June, when further positive performances and new goals could further increase his value. For this reason, D’Amico is considering making an early move, before the race for Frosinone’s talent becomes more complicated.
