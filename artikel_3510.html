Apple now lets developers offer discounts when users move to cancel subscriptions

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Apple today rolled out a new subscription retention tool for developers looking to reduce cancellations. Here are the details.

During WWDC earlier this year, Apple announced Retention Messaging, a new feature that lets developers display personalized messages and special offers to users as they attempt to cancel their subscriptions.

Now, as the company explained in a new post on its developer blog, the feature is available, allowing developers to configure messages that “appear in the cancellation flow and can include text, images, or a special offer.”

If this sounds familiar, that’s because this is not Apple’s first attempt to help developers reduce cancellations.

Apple’s documentation notes that developers can create up to 10 retention messages per app, each associated with one or more subscriptions.
