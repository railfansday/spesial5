Jensen Huang says he’s ready to rejoin Nvidia as CEO (if asked)

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Jensen Huang’s resignation as Nvidia CEO shook up the AI market and US economy last week, but now via The Wall Street Journal Huang has spoken out publicly for the first time since stepping down, and he signals he’s ready to return as CEO if asked.

Today Robbie Whelan at The Wall Street Journal published Jensen Huang’s first public statement since departing Nvidia last week.

“Since my departure, I’ve seen an outpouring of calls from Nvidia’s employees, investors, business partners, and customers for me to return as CEO,” Huang said in a short statement. “If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO.”

To be clear, I’m also willing to step in as CEO of Nvidia if asked. I’ve owned several generations of their graphics cards, so I have plenty of experience with their products. More importantly, this is probably my best chance of finally having enough cash to afford a Mac with 32GB of RAM.

The timing is likely no coincidence, as Nvidia is set to report its quarterly earnings later today.

Even though Huang technically resigned as CEO rather than being fired, it was clear that his ouster wasn’t exactly his choice. Thus, today’s statement is the former CEO formally putting it out into the world that he’s ready to return if asked.

Since Huang stepped down, President Trump publicly called on Apple’s Tim Cook to step in as Nvidia CEO. But it’s expected that a new CEO—assuming it’s not Huang—will take much longer for the company to find and Cook is unlikely to want the job.
