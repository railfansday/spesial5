Alec and Hilaria Baldwin Joke 'Gaslighting' Is the Secret to Their Marriage

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Alec Baldwin and wife Hilaria Baldwin revealed that a good sense of humor — and “a lot of gaslighting” — is the secret to their nearly 15-year marriage.

“I think it’s our banter,” Hilaria, 42, exclusively told Us of the pair’s relationship on Tuesday, November 17, while attending Tony Bennett’s 100th Birthday Benefit in New York City. “It keeps us both getting old very fast and young, both the things at the same time.”

Alec, 68, revealed that some slight decorating changes, courtesy of his wife, keeps him on his toes, too.

“I come home and the house is completely redecorated,” he told Us, which Hilaria confirmed as true.

“Yes, every day when he leaves, if I don’t have to work, I come home [and decorate],” she admitted.

Alec, who married Hilaria in 2012, joked, “I come home and I’m like, ‘Whose idea was this?’ And she says, ‘Don’t worry about it.’”

Hilaria confessed, “I tell him it was his idea, and he’s like, ‘Wait a minute.”

Alec confirmed his wife’s shenanigans, saying she’ll ask him, “Don’t you remember, this was your idea? We talked about this.”

Hilaria, who shares seven children with her husband, teased, “See we can both gaslight each other.” (Alec is also the father of daughter Ireland Baldwin, whom he shares with ex-wife Kim Basinger.)

Alec, while grinning, added, “A lot of gaslighting. The key to a happy marriage is a lot of gaslighting.”

“Yes, gaslighting, but banter,” Hilaria chimed in when talking about their success as a couple. “And then we’re both not sure what’s real and not real anymore, but we both do it to each other.”

The keys to Alec and Hilaria’s marriage aren’t the only things that the pair see eye to eye on.

The Baldwins are both big fans of the late Tony Bennett, who died in 2023 at the age of 96, and his legacy, telling Us that they came out to support Tony Bennett’s 100th Birthday Benefit in honor of their personal connection to the Grammy winner.

“We have known Tony and his family for years, and supporting the foundation, exploring the arts, the school they built, all those things, that was a big thing for us,” Alec told Us on the red carpet. “Because I had a very surprising and unexpected friendship with Tony, and he was great. I loved him.”

The comedian recalled working with Bennett on Saturday Night Live when the famed musician played a Tony Bennett impersonator for a sketch.

“That was the pinnacle of my career,” Alec shared. “Literally, that was a high point.”

He added that he and Hilaria know Tony’s son Danny Bennett and “his whole family” and they’ve been “working whenever we can to come to these events and support Tony.”

Hilaria noted that she “always” thinks of Tony as “the beginning” of her relationship with Alec, because the singer was “such an important figure” to them.

“I realized how big of a heart Tony [had], but Alec as well of how much giving and the two of them together,” she recalled. “One of the things I really loved about Tony and I think about regularly is gratitude. He’s like one of the most grateful people in the world. He just loves being in. He’s a good guy.”

During the event, Tony’s legacy of love and gratitude was given an even bigger platform when Exploring the Arts (ETA) and Third Street Music School announced their plan to open the Tony Bennett Institute for Arts Education at Third Street (TBIAE), describing it as a “new initiative bringing together two established organizations to expand access to high-quality arts education and create more opportunities for young people to discover and develop their creative potential,” according to a press release.

TBIAE represents “a new chapter in Tony’s legacy during the year he would have celebrated his 100th birthday, which also coincides with the 25th anniversary of Frank Sinatra School of the Arts, the public high school [Tony] and his wife, Susan Benedetto, founded in his hometown of Astoria, Queens,” per the foundation’s statement.

Alec, for his part, joined Danny, 72, on stage during the event to help unveil the plans for the institute.

“These kids are focused, joyful, and proud. You can feel that someone believed in them. That was thanks to Tony and Susan,” he said of Tony and Susan’s commitment to arts education. “Tony used to say he wanted to leave the world a little more beautiful than he found it. Mission accomplished.”

For more details on the Tony Bennett Institute for Arts Education visit www.exploringthearts.org.
