LEGO Star Wars 50th Anniversary Guide: Every Set Confirmed/Explained & Ranked By Excitement

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Next year, Star Wars will celebrate 50 years of the franchise, and The LEGO Group is going all out with some of the most exciting models and sets ever released. The start of an entire wave of new sets and models releasing in 2027, the first LEGO Star Wars 50th anniversary models have been officially revealed.

Complete with brand-new golden box art and the newly revealed official Star Wars 50th anniversary logo, LEGO will release an impressive mix of different sets beginning January 1st. From upscaled minifigure models and dioramas to epic artwork pieces every Star Wars collector will be dying to display on their walls, there's something for everyone, and each set will come with some of the coolest exclusives we've ever seen.

Keeping that in mind, here's a full breakdown for every confirmed LEGO Star Wars 50th anniversary set (so far), ranked from least to most exciting.

Stormtrooper (Concept Art) Helmet (75495)

The LEGO Star Wars Stormtrooper (Concept Art) Helmet is a very cool tribute to Ralph McQuarrie's earliest pieces of Star Wars concept art, with so much of his work acting as the key foundation for the Star Wars galaxy and its visual identity. As such, the new helmet model, coming in at 832 pieces, is a more unique helmet design compared to what the standard Imperial Stormtrooper helmets ended up looking like on screen, which is what makes this upcoming model so unique.

Additionally, the set will also come with a small 6x8 Rogue One poster, one of several different collectible Star Wars movie posters set to be released with various sets throughout the year. That said, the rest of the models on this list are arguably just a little bit cooler, with other sets also having exciting tributes to McQuarrie's early concept art as well.

Up-Scaled Luke Skywalker Minifigure (75482)

Recent years have seen The LEGO Group experimenting with oversized minifigure models, though the latest Up-Scaled Luke Skywalker model may be its best yet. Featured in his classic Rebel pilot outfit, the brick-built minifigure is over 10 inches tall and is made up of nearly 1,000 pieces. As such, it's quite the cool display model, and would no doubt go great with the Up-Scaled Darth Vader model LEGO released earlier this year during the summer.

Additionally, the set's big 50th-anniversary draw is the Princess Leia (Concept Art) minifigure, also inspired by McQuarrie's iconic early designs, one of several collectible Concept Art minifigures that will be released in 2027. While the price-per-piece count isn't terrible, the jury's still out personally as to whether LEGO's latest Up-Scaled Minifigure is worth getting over some of these other sets.

The Rebel Y-Wing Starfighter is one of the more traditional playsets in the anniversary collection, recreating a classic Star Wars ship from the Rebel Alliance's first fight against the Empire as seen in 1977's A New Hope. Coming in at 676 pieces, this new Y-Wing may just be one of the best versions of the classic Rebel bomber yet, and I'm very excited about the Mon Mothma, Dutch Vader, and R2-BHD minifigures set to come with it.

Additionally, the exclusive 50th-anniversary offering is none other than the Luke Skywalker (Concept Art) minifigure, complete with the respirator and goggles the character was originally envisioned to have back when he was set to be named Luke Starkiller.

Click Here To View Rebel Y-Wing Starfighter (75478) on LEGO.com

LEGO's new 628-piece Darth Vader 2-in-1 Wall Art set is a very exciting display piece, as collectors will be able to create one of two exciting builds. The first is a unique wall display of Darth Vader's iconic helmet using a mix of black, red, and white pieces, while the other build is the classic "I am your father" phrase with the Dark Lord of the Sith's signature red-bladed lightsaber serving as a unique backdrop.

The set will also include a 6x8 poster of The Empire Strikes Back, an incredibly fitting 50th-anniversary exclusive. Overall, the flexibility makes this new Darth Vader set stand out, and I wouldn't be surprised if some collectors end up being two in order to display both builds simultaneously. It's also one of the more affordable adult-oriented sets in the wave, being priced at $69.99.

Click Here To View Darth Vader 2-in-1 Wall Art (75466) on LEGO.com

One of the most iconic (and longest) lightsaber battles in the entire Star Wars saga finally gets an impressive LEGO diorama set with the upcoming Mustafar Duel set. Featuring dual-printed minifigures of Obi-Wan Kenobi and a Sith yellow-eyed Anakin Skywalker, the model depicts their culminating Revenge of the Sith battle over Mustafar's lava flows with the duo standing on rotating platforms. There's also a great tile on the front of the build with Obi-Wan's classic line: "You were the Chosen One!"

The set also comes with an exclusive Han Solo (Concept Art) minifigure depicting the iconic smuggler as the lightsaber-wielding space pirate he was originally envisioned as. For just $39.99, Mustafar Duel is easily one of the most exciting and appealing sets in this upcoming wave.

Click Here To View Mustafar Duel (76470) on LEGO.com

If the Mustafar Duel is one of the prequel trilogy's defining moments, Duel on Bespin recreates the original trilogy's. The 718-piece set features the bridge where Darth Vader and Luke Skywalker fought in their momentous Empire Strikes Back duel that ended with Vader cutting off Luke's hand before revealing himself as the young Jedi's father. Naturally, the build features Darth Vader's most iconic line on a quote plaque tile at the front of the set.

LEGO's previous Bespin Duel set was meant for Star Wars Celebration 2020, though the global pandemic canceled the event, resulting in the limited stock pivoting and becoming an exclusive Target release. As such, it's very exciting to see a new version seven years later that should be much more accessible for collectors. Additionally, the included 50th-anniversary minifigure is arguably the biggest draw, considering it's based on McQuarrie's first designs and concept art for Darth Vader himself.

Click Here To View Duel on Bespin (75480) on LEGO.com

Currently, the most exciting LEGO Star Wars 50th anniversary set is also its biggest with the upcoming New Hope Movie Poster, a brand-new LEGO Artwork set recreating the original theatrical poster used to promote the 1977 film. Coming in at 3,209 pieces, the $300 set is 23 inches tall by 16 inches wide, using a variety of layered bricks to give the new LEGO Artwork model a 3D appearance. The set will also include a 6x8 collectible poster build for A New Hope.

All things considered, the scale, piece count, and nostalgic appeal of the New Hope Movie Poster models easily make it an incredible centerpiece for Star Wars' 50th anniversary. Beyond all the others, this one has definitely got me the most hyped as a longtime LEGO Star Wars fan.
