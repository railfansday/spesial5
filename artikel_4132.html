Magic: The Gathering Gets Official Burger King Deck Box And Sleeves

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

For years now, Pokemon Trading Card Game collectors have become accustomed to collaborations with McDonald's. Every year, the fast-food chain releases a series of cards with a special holographic pattern, limited to Happy Meals from the Golden Arches. In typical Pokemon fashion, recent promotions have caused carnage.

Magic: The Gathering has decided it has a little more class than collaborating with McDonald's; instead, it's teaming up with Burger King, where in one country, players can buy a special meal to get an exclusive Burger King-themed MTG Deck Box and Card Sleeves.

Burger King Italia has just released the Great Gathering Bundle. For €19.90 ($22.25), the bundle includes either a Krispper, Double Cheese King, or Whopper Plant-Based, plus 6 chicken bites, a box of fries, and a snazzy, slightly hilarious Burger King Deck Box and Card Sleeves.

The Deck Box and Sleeves bear the traditional Burger King orange and are designed to look like one of the chain's burgers. The box has sesame seeds on the front, with typical burger ingredients, like cheese, lettuce, and tomato, on the side. The promotion is available until November 30, if you fancy booking a quick trip to Italy.

Burger King joins an interesting couple of years of Magic: The Gathering collaborations. Although this is one-of-a-kind, the TCG has delved into a range of different franchises as part of its Secret Lair range, including Star Trek, Lo-Fi Girl, Hatsune Miku, and potentially soon KPop Demon Hunters.

With this promotion limited to a single country, calls have begun online, with people asking the Italian population to grab one for them. "Which of my great Italian friends is getting this beautiful BK deckbox for me?!?" said Matheus Vizos on Twitter. It's unlikely they'll have many takers, as, given the current state of TCGs, this sadly feels like a prime scalper opportunity.
