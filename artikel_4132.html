We've only just had the release of the Pokemon Trading Card Game's most recent expansion, Delta Reign, but it seems like attention is already moving on from that set to the next, Flowing Fists.

Although Flowing Fists doesn't launch until February next year, some people on social media have begun getting their hands on products early, giving us our first look at some of the cards in the set, including a stunning Blastoise Illustration Rare.

The upcoming Flowing Fists set will pull cards from the Japanese-language expansion, Aura Seeker. As the name might suggest, the expansion will be centered around Mega Lucario Z, which was first introduced in Pokemon Legends: Z-A last year. We've had early glimpses of the Japanese Mega Lucario Z ex and Mega Garchomp ex Special Illustration Rares, and now the English versions, along with a handful of other unrevealed cards, have come to light.

It's unclear how, but some people have managed to get their hands on a bunch of single Flowing Fists blisters and are pulling what will undoubtedly be the set's most desirable cards.

As is so often the case, the images look like they've been taken on a potato, but the quality of the cards shown mean the set is going to be an exciting one.

The Mega Garchomp Z Special Illustration Rare features the Pokemon rocketing toward the camera, its two blades ready to attack. It can be seen below:

The Mega Lucario Special Illustration Rare was also pulled early, and features the new form of the Pokemon looking rather majestic. It's ability, Aura Seeker, matches the Japanese set's name.

The star of the show, though, is the Blastoise Illustration Rare. While it may have been "demoted" from Special Illustration Rare status, with the last one it received coming in the 151 set, this card is spectacular. It features one of the coolest depictions of the Pokemon standing in a rocky cavern, surrounded by water. I may be a little bit of a Gen 1 simp, but it's a great card.

Should more photos emerge throughout the day, we'll update this article, but I have a feeling Flowing Fists will be better than any standard set the Pokemon Trading Card Game received this year.
