Guide: How To Unlock The Hall Of Fallen Foes In Zelda: Ocarina Of Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Where to find the Switch 2 remake's big new feature

The Hall of Fallen Foes is a new addition to Zelda: Ocarina of Time's Switch 2 remake.

The feature is both a museum cataloguing all the enemies you've encountered so far, and a way to rematch bosses for an extra challenge.

Here's how to unlock the Hall of Fallen Foes in Zelda: Ocarina of Time, and how to leave when you're done wiping the floor with the returning adversaries within.

On this page: How To Unlock The Hall Of Fallen Foes In Zelda: Ocarina Of Time

The Hall of Fallen Foes unlocks when you become Adult Link, and have the ability to turn back into Child Link via the Temple of Time once you have completing the Forest Temple dungeon.

From there, approach Kakariko Village from Hyrule Field as Child Link, where Kaepora Gaebora will halt you just before you enter and explain how accessing the Hall of Fallen Foes works.

Kaepora Gaebora explains that you can access the Hall of Fallen Foes by sleeping in a bed. There are multiple spots you can do this across the game, but the closest location from here (which Kaepora Gaebora will point you toward) is the Grand Lodge in the south of Kakariko Village.

Whether it's here or via resting place, you know you can access the Hall of Fallen Foes when a bed is glowing green.

Approach, choose to sleep and dream, and you'll awaken in the Hall of Fallen Foes. Here you can find out details of all the enemies you have defeated on your quest so far, with bosses on the outer edges for you to battle once more in a boss rush-style mode.

Once done, talk to Kaepora Gaebora to leave. A useful note: any items or resources you use in the Hall of Fallen Foes will be replenished when you return to the main adventure, so feel free to go wild with your tactics during a boss rematch.
