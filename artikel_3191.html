The Marzetti Company (MZTI -0.95%) reported a decline in fiscal first quarter earnings as a regional health outbreak and elevated logistics expenses offset gains from its recent acquisition of the Bachan's brand. Management stated that a cyclospora outbreak primarily affected results in the Great Lakes region, where the company maintains its highest market development, accounting for 30% of its overall business. Despite these headwinds, the company integrated the Bachan's business and reported market share gains in its frozen dinner roll and garlic bread categories. The company is implementing new pricing actions in its frozen segment to mitigate ongoing transportation inflation and expects financial performance to improve as the health outbreak impact moderates.
