Everything at Parke Is 20% Off for a Limited Time (Even the New Stony Clover Lane Collab)

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Forget concert tickets. One of the hottest drops of the season might just be a sweatshirt.

Parke has built a devoted following around its vintage-inspired varsity styles, with fans eagerly awaiting each new collection and popular pieces disappearing from (virtual) shelves. But if you’ve been holding off on joining the Parke craze, the brand’s upcoming Black Friday sale might be all the convincing you need.

Starting Tuesday, Nov. 24, at 10 a.m. ET, Parke is taking 20% off sitewide, including its bestselling sweatshirts, denim, tees, and accessories. The sale runs through Cyber Monday, Nov. 30, and coincides with the release of a new collection that brings back some familiar favorites.

Dubbed the Keepsake Collection, the new drop takes inspiration from some of Parke’s most popular designs and collaborations of 2026, giving shoppers another shot at the styles that helped make the brand a social media sensation.

It’s the latest move from a label that has managed to make vintage-inspired basics feel like collector’s items. Founded by Chelsea Kramer in 2022, Parke has become known for its oversized Varsity Mocknecks, relaxed denim, and limited-edition releases, including recent collections celebrating New York City and a collaboration with Stoney Clover Lane.

Parke isn’t reinventing the sweatshirt, but it has figured out how to make people want another one. Its oversized fits, throwback graphics, and rotating colorways have turned a familiar wardrobe staple into something fans keep coming back for.

And while Parke’s drops have become an event in their own right, the Black Friday sale makes shopping the brand a little easier on your wallet. With 20% off, a $130 Varsity Mockneck comes down to $104.

Just don’t spend too long debating which color to get. If Parke’s previous releases are any indication, the most popular styles could be gone before you make up your mind.
