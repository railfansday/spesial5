‘Twilight’ Director On Robert Pattinson Landing James Bond Role: “I Almost Fell Out Of My Bed”

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Robert Pattinson is the next James Bond, and his Twilight director had an interesting reaction when she learned about his casting.

Catherine Hardwicke cast Pattinson in 2008’s Twilight, which catapulted the actor into worldwide fame.

“I almost fell out of my bed,” Hardwicke said on The Kyle Buchanan Show. “I just didn’t think they were going to choose him because of The Batman and he’s so famous and they like to get the unknown [actor].”

She continued, “I did almost fall out of the bed, and that is the same bed that Rob and Kristen auditioned for the first time and did their kissing scene. It was kind of bizarre. The filmmaker said that Pattinson is “intense and interesting and has got a lot of stuff going on in there. He’s weird and he’s original. He does not fit the mold. He’s not afraid to say crazy shit, that’s what I love about it.” Watch on Deadline

Twilight fans recently resurfaced a clip from the movie saying they predicted Pattinson would become Bond one day. A scene from the saga shows Justin Chon’s Eric Yorkie passing out flyers mentioning the theme for the prom, which was Monte Carlo, adding, “Gambling, tuxedos and Bond. James Bond.” The camera then cuts to Pattinson’s Edward Cullen.

“You probably saw that scene online where we say, ‘Bond, James Bond,’ and then we cut to Rob,” Hardwicke said. “That’s the one scene where I was like, ‘Let’s put the sunglasses on.’ He got out of the car, and he looked so suave that he inspired the line, ‘Bond, James Bond.’ It was cool. We did think he looked like James Bond. I guess we were seeing the future.”
