D-Wave Quantum: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Its dual hardware strategy, serving customers' optimization needs while pursuing a longer-term fault-tolerant roadmap, is the company's most strategic claim to evaluate.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 10, 2026, D-Wave Quantum (QBTS -8.63%) announced that nine-month bookings jumped 611% year over year to $37.4 million. Commercial contract momentum gives the business predictable multiyear revenue as hardware deployments scale across corporate clients. Three things management said on the call stand out.

Contract commitments surged as enterprise customers expanded system purchases and cloud services. D-Wave Quantum saw its remaining performance obligations (RPO) -- contracted revenue that hasn't yet been recognized on the income statement -- expand significantly due to major enterprise agreements.

Non-dilutive federal funding helps de-risk long-term technology road maps without diluting equity holders. D-Wave Quantum finalized a major government agreement under the CHIPS and Science Act, a federal program designed to strengthen domestic technology manufacturing and research.

Commercial viability relies on serving immediate optimization needs while building next-generation architecture. D-Wave Quantum develops both annealing quantum computers, which solve optimization problems, and gate model systems, which strive to achieve fault-tolerant calculations over time, meaning the computers can operate correctly despite the presence of computational errors.

Revenue grew 21% year over year to $4.5 million in the third quarter of 2026, and management isn't hiding its enthusiasm about commercial progress. D-Wave Quantum holds structural advantages through established commercial customer relationships and a dual-platform product strategy, while management focuses on system sales execution and partner-led deployments.

As customer applications transition from pilots to full production, expanding partnership channels and scaling hardware fabrication remain essential priorities for long-term value creation.
