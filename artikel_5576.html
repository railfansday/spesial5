Data Security Market - Investment and Funding Scenario

Investment in the Data Security Market is increasingly focused on scaling AI-driven data protection platforms, strengthening data security posture management (DSPM), and integrating sensitive data discovery, access governance, and automated risk remediation. In April 2025, Forcepoint completed its acquisition of Getvisibility, expanding its capabilities in AI-powered DSPM and data detection and response across hybrid cloud and generative AI environments. The Data Security Market is shifting from standalone protection tools toward integrated, AI-powered platforms that combine sensitive data discovery, classification, data loss prevention, and automated risk remediation. Revenue opportunities are expanding as enterprises seek continuous protection across cloud, SaaS, on-premises, and AI environments. In February 2026, Cyberhaven launched its unified AI and data security platform, integrating DSPM, DLP, insider risk management, and AI security. In September 2026, Palo Alto Networks made Cortex Data Security generally available, unifying data discovery, posture management, protection, and response. These developments highlight growing opportunities for vendors to expand platform subscriptions and monetize advanced AI-aware data protection capabilities.
