Warmth and Whimsy: The Blanket Coat Does It All

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Is this fashion’s most comforting coat of all time?

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Fashion is all wrapped up in wraps, shawls, and capes. Call them what you will, but at the end of the day, they’re truly elevated blanket coats. For further proof, look at the fall/winter 2026 runways, where Colleen Allen and TWP led the charge with models dripping in comforting supersized scarf shawls turned coats. Elsewhere, at Fforme, models were decked out in huge swaths of furry fabrics that completely enveloped the wearers, as if swaddled. At Khaite, dresses were styled with large cocooning blanket-like throws trailing off the backs. Blanket-like pieces also emerged from stiff leather coats at Altuzarra and cascaded off the shoulders at Proenza Schouler.

Aside from being easy to wear and warm, the blanket coat offers a different kind of feel than your average black puffer might. In a tumultuous world, the blanket coat serves as a literal, wearable wrap of warmth and a representation of physical and metaphorical well-being. Not unlike the puffy statement scarf, a blanket coat is deeply practical but also inherently whimsical in spirit, even if not aesthetically so. It’s almost a little bit like taking your bed out the door with you. You already have the pillow by way of the puffed scarf. Why not bring the blanket too? And while you’re at it, don’t forget grandma’s couch in the form of the trending tapestry bag. It’s all about tactile touches.

Designers are clearly and intentionally leaning into the feeling of comfort: For TWP’s aforementioned fall/winter 2026 options, founder Trish Wescoat Pound referenced hot toddies, jigsaw puzzles, and fireplaces, in addition to other warm and cozy-feeling fabrics like Scottish cashmere in her show notes. In a more simplified vein, for Rachel Scott’s Proenza Schouler debut, where blanket-like shawls cascaded off the shoulders, the show notes described the pieces as clothes that “move easily between contexts. They are for a new New York woman, a global woman, who is aware of what the world asks of her but is unwilling to acquiesce to expectations.” Phoebe Philo’s recent take on the trend comes in the form of a brown and bronze houndstooth wool blanket coat with a hand-frayed hem.

Smaller brands like LilyEve also make a good business out of the blanket coat; the brand, in particular, upcycles hand-sourced vintage Hermès blankets and re-creates them into one-of-a-kind coats. “Seeing those little pieces and trying to think of what other product could come out of that, whether it’s tiny pieces of toweling or silk left from the scarves, that’s always a really fun inspiration,” she told ELLE earlier this year.

Certainly, there’s no arguing that we’re all craving comfort at the moment. Fashion’s obsession with blankets as clothing on the runway just further proves it.
