Social Media & UGC Marketing Specialist (German-Speaking)Full-time (40 hours per week), Remote (CET working hours)

About the CompanyYou will be joining a fast-growing, clinician-led digital healthcare company that helps people manage long-term health conditions with evidence-backed treatment plans. The business has grown rapidly year on year across several international markets, and its German weight-loss programme, which includes GLP-1 treatment, is a key growth area. The team is now building a steady pipeline of authentic creator content to power its paid social growth.

The RoleAs a Social Media & UGC Marketing Specialist, you will keep a steady flow of fresh, authentic creator content coming into the German market’s paid and organic social channels. You’ll be hands-on with creators from the first message to live content. That means finding the right people on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube (ideally those already on a GLP-1 treatment journey), briefing them, and making sure strong content lands on time.

You’ll work closely with the Performance Marketing and Creative teams, reporting to the growth or creative strategy lead. This is a hands-on, executional role, ideal for someone who is highly organised, commercially minded and energised by a fast-paced environment. Because the role sits in the weight-loss space, you should be comfortable working with health-related content and GLP-1 treatment.

Source, vet and onboard UGC creators on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and UGC platforms who fit the brand and audience

Prioritise creators with real experience of the treatment to keep content authentic and credible

Write clear creative briefs and guide creators through concepts, revisions and delivery

Answer creator questions on briefs and compensation, and negotiate partnership terms

Own the workflow from brief to launch, working with Performance Marketing and Creative to get content into Meta ads (mainly partnership ads) and organic channels

Make sure all content is accurate, compliant and on-brand for a healthcare setting

Manage creator DMs, the shared inbox, contracts, content tracking and budgets within agreed limits

Spot new formats and creator trends and bring test ideas to the team

1–3 years of experience in social media, influencer, creator or UGC marketing (agency or in-house)

Hands-on experience sourcing creators and managing them day to day

Fluent German (most creator conversations are in German) and confident English

A strong eye for social content that feels authentic and performs on Meta and TikTok, plus the writing skills to produce briefs creators actually understand

Comfortable with basic performance metrics (CTR, hook rate, CPA) and keen to learn more

Highly organised: juggles many creators, conversations and deadlines without dropping the ball

Proactive and driven, with a sense of urgency, a test-and-learn mindset, and real enjoyment of hands-on execution

Experience at a direct-to-consumer (D2C) brand

Previous experience in a startup or fast-growing company

Tools & StackInstagram, TikTok, YouTube, UGC platforms, Meta Ads
