Ezra Sosa Called Out These ‘DWTS’ Pros for “Betting Against Him” in a Since-Deleted TikTok

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Though Ezra Sosa and his celeb partner, Julia Stiles, were eliminated after performing a paso doble to “Satisfied” from Hamilton on Dancing With the Stars’ Disney Night, the pro is still keeping up with the competition—and he seemingly called out some shady behind-the-scenes behavior from former DWTS pros.

After Tuesday night’s shocking results, Ezra posted—and later deleted—a TikTok where he wrote, “I wonder how much money some former pros have lost on Kalshi…”

Ezra seemingly slammed Maks Chmerkovskiy and Gleb Savchenko for placing bets against their fellow pros on Kalshi.

“get them ezra, that behavior is so weird from former pros, they are such losers,” one fan wrote after a screenshot of his TikTok circulated on Reddit.

Another pointed out that other former pros, including Sasha Farber and Peta Murgatroyd, have also placed online bets throughout season 35.

“I hope that all the former pros betting on the show never get asked back in any capacity, even in the live audience,” they wrote. “It’s a massive conflict of interest and can actually sway voting patterns if Kalshi bets on dwts become more widespread and normalized. Maks, Gleb, Sasha, Peta I’m looking at you losers!”

However, someone chimed in with a valid point: “Unfortunately they’re not losing their own money (in fact they’re actually getting paid), they’re just trying to influence their followers to lose money on what should be an illegal app.”

Ezra isn’t the first person to call former pros out on their bets, either.

During a November 18 interview with USA Today, Jan Ravnik made a pointed remark when asked to leave a message for his haters: “I hope you lose all your money.”

The day before that, Jan made a dig at Maks during a DWTS livestream when Brandon Armstrong and Alan Bersten commended him for his “beautiful” choreography. “Say that to Maks,” he said.

This all comes after Maks and Gleb publicly shared their DWTS season 35 bets online, but Maks has long criticized Jan’s involvement on the show.

During an October 2025 episode of The Penthouse with Peta podcast, Maks claimed, “I’m sorry, Jan has absolutely no business being a pro on Dancing with the Stars. There’s zero foundation, technique, quality, understanding of the partnership.”

He has since apologized to Jan, but still placed bets against him so…who even knows what’s going on.
