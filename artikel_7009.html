Hopper: Growth Product Manager, HTS Media

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

About the RoleAs a Growth Product Manager, HTS Media, you will own the roadmap for growing advertiser demand across brands: more qualified pipeline, faster campaign launches and higher advertiser spend within Hopper's ecosystem, working closely with engineering leadership, the sales team, Ad Operations and Account Management. This is a builder role: you will start by owning the roadmap for sales pipeline and sales efficiency, using AI-assisted systems to create demand and make every seller faster, then grow into owning the platform roadmap, including the campaign buying interfaces, in partnership with engineering leadership. In your first year, you'll be judged on the pipeline your systems create, how much faster campaigns go from signed to live, and growth in spend per advertiser.

Own the roadmap for sales pipeline and sales efficiency, with goals and success metrics tied to a revenue target

Build AI-powered systems that generate demand, from account research and signal-based targeting to personalized outreach and follow-up, and measure the pipeline and meetings they produce

Grow into owning the platform roadmap for the campaign buying interfaces with engineering leadership: write the specs and decide what gets built, and in what order

Work with Ad Operations to remove friction between a signed campaign and a live one

Put insights into the buying interface, such as forecasts, benchmarks and media plan recommendations

Use SQL on booking, search and campaign data to size opportunities, find advertiser results and decide what to build next

Run demand experiments across channels such as LinkedIn, email and events, and track results in internal dashboards

Support HTS Media positioning and reusable sales materials (deck templates, one-pagers and case studies), and the HTS Media website and offers site

4+ years of experience in product management or growth at a technology company, ideally in B2B SaaS, marketplaces or ad tech; experience in online advertising or travel is a plus

A track record of owning a roadmap and shipping product with engineering that moved a measurable growth metric, such as pipeline, conversion, time to launch or spend per account

Hands-on experience building AI-assisted or automated workflows that a real sales team used, with tools such as Clay, n8n, Zapier or Make, and LLM APIs (equivalents are fine), and the ability to describe what you built and what it produced

Strong SQL on a cloud data warehouse such as BigQuery, and comfort turning booking, search and ad-serving data into decisions, forecasts and in-product insights

Excellent writing and specification skills, and the ability to work with engineering leadership and communicate clearly with advertisers and internal teams

Self-driven and organized, and comfortable building without a playbook in a fast-growing business

Well-funded and proven startup with large ambitions, competitive salary and upsides of pre-IPO equity packages
