TransPerfect: Plumeria - Voice Message Collection Project - India

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

DataForce by TransPerfect is looking for people living in India to share existing WhatsApp voice messages for a short, remote project. If you use voice messages to chat in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, or Urdu, this could be a good fit for you. You don’t need to record anything new!

You may qualify if you’re a native speaker of one of these languages and have 12–15 minutes of voice messages you personally recorded and sent in a single WhatsApp conversation before November 12, 2026.

You will select one WhatsApp chat containing approximately 12–15 minutes of voice messages in your selected project language(s) that you personally recorded and sent, export it, and submit it through our DataForce Contribute app following the guidelines shared by our team.

Only existing voice messages sent before November 12, 2026 are eligible. Newly recorded or scripted messages will not be accepted.

Only your own voice messages count toward the required audio. Messages containing personal information, unintelligible speech, or explicit content will not be accepted.

Be a native speaker of Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, or Urdu.

Have approximately 12–15 minutes of your own existing WhatsApp voice messages in one conversation in your selected project language.

Sign the participation consent form before submitting your messages.

If you are a native speaker of more than one project language and have suitable voice messages in each, you can indicate this in a single application.

This is a fully remote project. You can participate from the comfort of your home.

The task is estimated to take approximately 30 minutes per language submission, including reviewing the guidelines, exporting the conversation, and uploading your submission.

All submissions will undergo quality review to confirm the speaker, language, recording dates, audio clarity, and absence of personal information in the accepted audio.

Compensation is $12 USD for each accepted submission containing at least 10 minutes of approved audio per language.

You will also receive a $3 USD completion bonus per accepted submission if you submit by the deadline specified in your project instructions email.

As payment method, we offer PayPal, Gift Card, and Wire Transfer, according to your preference.

Please note that our Quality Assurance team will review all submissions to ensure they meet the project specific requirements. The project team will provide you with feedback once the Quality Check process is completed.
