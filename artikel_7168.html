Chief Customer Officer at Xapo Bank

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Diversity is at the heart of who we are at Xapo Bank. We’re a fully distributed team of over 160 Xapiens that work remotely from 50+ countries around the world.

Our beginning: A world that enjoys economic freedom and wealth protection,no matter where you live or who is running your country.To achieve that, we search the world for the best people for the job. We work hard, think globally, and inspire each other to learn and grow. We are committed to changing the way things are done.

Although we are headquartered in Gibraltar, this is a full time, 100% remote position Work from anywhere!

Xapo's product suite has grown from a Bitcoin vault into a full member proposition: BTC and USD accounts that pay interest, a global card with Bitcoin cashback, Bitcoin-backed loans, a growing suite of BTC wealth and credit products, and access to stocks, ETFs, and stablecoins, all inside one regulated relationship.

Xapo is a bank, and a bank earns on the money its members choose to hold with it. Each of those products shipped well. What hasn't yet existed is a single owner of the economics of the customer relationship: one person accountable for winning the right members, growing what they hold with us, and keeping them, and for the balance-sheet products that make that possible.

The Chief Customer Officer is a new role at the top of the house, reporting to the CEO. You will run the stream that grows what members hold with Xapo, and you will own the Customer P&L, retail and institutional. It brings growth, client coverage, product management, and product design under one leader who is judged on customer contribution, the assets attracted and kept, and the members who stay. This is a customer-focused role in the most commercial sense: what a member earns from us, not the feature we shipped, is the centre of every decision.

The measure of success is a Customer P&L that grows on what members hold and keep: LTV:CAC improving, approved-to-paid rising, churn falling, and customer contribution the business can plan against.

This is a banking role, not a requirements-gathering role, a marketing role, or a role you can run from a dashboard. Products are priced on the balance sheet and judged on the money they attract and keep.

This part matters as much as the mandate. The known failure mode for this role, in any company, is a CCO with a polished customer-insight deck and no ownership of the number. That is disqualifying here.

Day 30: The Customer P&L baseline is documented across retail and institutional: customer contribution, LTV:CAC, approved-to-paid, churn, net paid-member growth, and assets attracted and kept. You have formed your own independent view of where members are won, lost, and under-served, and shared it with the CEO and Board.Day 60: The unified Customer operating model is live: one set of key results across Growth, Client Coverage, Mission PMs, and Product Design, read weekly from one source. Every mission PM owns a product's contribution and its price, and the roadmap is ranked on what it earns, built with Finance and the line leads.Day 90: At least one measurable improvement in LTV:CAC, approved-to-paid, churn, or net new AUM flow is live and attributable to a change you made, not a feature ship, an outcome moved. Every cell in your line has defined key results and an owner who explains the number every week.
