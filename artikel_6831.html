27 Thoughtful Family Christmas Gifts Everyone Will Be Excited to Unwrap

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Every item on this page was hand-picked by a House Beautiful editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

You’re sure to find a winner or two from our carefully curated list.

Buying family Christmas gifts can be more difficult than you think, especially if you’re shopping for an uncle you only see occasionally or the picky cousin who seems to dislike everything. The best place to start is usually to ask those closest to you for ideas, but if the suggestions from others are slow going, you can check out our curated picks ahead.

How did we select them? Well, we asked our editors for the best items they’ve ever received (or given) from their families and kept in mind all the different interests and budgets one might have while looking for gifts.

We found items that appeal to the bird watcher in the family, the sentimentalist, the design lover, and more. The 20+ items range from crowd-pleasers like a heated blanket to hyper-specific items like a stone bathmat for the germaphobes in the family. You can find products across all price points, too, because we know you may want to spend as little as $50 on someone—just know that gifts at this price point still feel thoughtful and look nice—or a couple of hundred on your favorite sibling.

Ahead, shop our top picks for the whole family.

This portable marble lamp only weighs two pounds and adds the most beautiful ambient light to their space. It has three adjustable brightness levels, and the light will gently flash to warn you when the battery is running low. Family members of all ages will appreciate this beauty, from the aunt who wants to display it on her bookshelf to the teenager going off to college who needs to decorate their dorm room to the parent who wants a chic nightlight in their nursery.

If 2027 is their year to get in touch with nature more or pick up a hobby, gift them this smart solar-powered bird feeder to encourage their newfound passion. The bird feeder has both day and nighttime vision, and when paired with a Featherpal subscription, it can identify over 10,000 bird species. However, a subscription is not needed just to see what birds visit the property.

The best games allow you to team up and tackle a challenge together. See who in your family thinks alike with Wavelength and add a prize at the end to make the game more competitive. “This is the group game I always bring on vacations or during the holidays,” Senior Commerce Editor Marina Liao says. “The rules are easy to learn, though coming up with the clue can be difficult depending on who you’re playing with and how they think! I like that everyone gets a turn, and if you happen to have very opinionated friends and family, Wavelength will get heated!”

Before you worry that this only comes as a family of three, the bear family can be personalized with up to seven pieces! The writing is laser-engraved, so the text will never fade. Display this adorable decor on a mantel, console table, bookshelf, or even on the windowsill.

Everyone could use an eye massager, especially with how much we spend looking at screens. “There was a period last year where I kept getting dry eyes, so my optometrist recommended heating pads,” Liao says. “I bought this after seeing how many reviews it had on Amazon, and it wasn’t super expensive. I used it two to three times a week for only a month and saw a dramatic improvement in my eye moisture! This massager also has tiny speakers that connect to your Bluetooth so you can listen to a podcast or music while your eyes are closed.”

Tonies were designed for the kids in the family, but everyone can enjoy the audio and storytelling. This is for the parents who don’t want to introduce tablet time too soon for their kids. Unwind at night or in the middle of a day with stories and music from the different collectibles that range from Bluey to Disney characters.

Are you still looking for a stocking stuffer? Allow us to introduce you to the Rabbit, which will make opening a bottle of wine the easiest thing you've ever done. "I've tried your traditional corkscrew wine opener, the automatic kind, and the Rabbit, and nothing compares to the Rabbit," Liao says. "All you have to do is put this over the bottle's neck and then lift the gray lever to draw the cork out of the bottle. I don't have to worry about the cork splintering, which happens whenever I use any other method, and I no longer look like a fool struggling to open wine at parties. I got the Rabbit as a gift a few years ago, and it's one of my favorite items I have received."

Does your family visit Aspen every winter? If yes, this candle set is perfect for mom, dad, sister, or even brother, as it'll help conjure up all those warm and fuzzy family memories on the mountain. Let the notes of fir needles, blue spruce, and fireplace ignite their olfactory senses.

Getting a weekly massage may not be in the cards for their schedule or budget, which is why we love an at-home massager. This device has eight different massage nodes and three adjustable speeds (slow, medium, fast). It even heats up to ease muscle tension and stress while improving blood circulation. Best of all, they can use this while watching their favorite show or on the couch while watching the grandkids play.

They don't need a spacious backyard to start growing their own herbs and veggies: just gift them the popular Click & Grow, which can be left out on their kitchen island or countertop. The smart garden uses an LED grow light and pre-seeded plant pods to give you a bountiful harvest of basil, peppers, rosemary, and more.

We'll never say no to another throw or blanket, especially if it's heated. Don't knock it until you've tried it, because the feeling of being under one after you just walked the dog in 10-degree weather, or after you just got back from a long day outside in the snow, is the best feeling in the world. This one comes in so many colors and sizes and has six different heat levels!

Do you have a creative family member or someone who likes to doodle as a hobby? If so, they will appreciate this blank canvas they can draw on using just water. Here's how it works: dip the brush in water, draw whatever you want on the surface of this board, then watch it disappear over time as the water evaporates. We love a no-mess art activity.

You may not want your little niece or baby cousin playing with your expensive camera or iPhone, but if they show interest in documenting the sweet moments at family gatherings, get them their very own kid-friendly camera. This one takes photos up to 8 megapixels and includes a 32GB memory card that can hold up to 4,000 pictures. With a fully charged battery, the camera can take 1,000 pictures or record two hours of video, giving the little ones in the family plenty of time to capture every detail.

Who doesn't love getting out of the shower or tub and being wrapped up in a toasty towel? This smart bathroom gadget will keep your towels heated for about an hour and can hold approximately two towels depending on how big they are. You can even stick your robe or clothes in there, should you want to toast those items up.

Cozy Earth consistently tops our editors' lists for the best bamboo sheets (the brand's pajamas are so comfy, too), so if you know your family members would appreciate new bedding, this set makes the perfect gift. "Every season, my husband's family does a white elephant gift exchange,” Liao says. "I've learned, over the years, they all love functional or edible gifts versus wacky ones—everyone fought over a trash can last Christmas—so I'm showing up with a set of crisp, cooling sheets in a size queen! Whether they want this for themselves or their guest rooms, I know the bougie sheets will be a hit with the practical aunts and uncles."

When there are four or more schedules to juggle in a household, it can feel overwhelming, which is why we're big proponents of a digital calendar that keeps things organized. This one is extremely popular and one of the more stylish options available.

We love Framebridge's selection of photo ornaments, and this is the type of gift that works for any family member who's putting up a tree. Whether you gift this to your aunt featuring a photo of you two or have it be a portrait of the family dog for your parents, this sentimental family gift will be precious and memorable.

Buying smart home gadgets can add up, so if you want that peace of mind without racking up your credit card bill, get a Ring Doorbell.

"This was one of the first things I bought for my front door because I live on a busy street and sometimes have random people sit on my stoop," Liao says. "The video quality is clear, even at night, and I like that you can see what else is happening around your neighborhood through the community board on the app. I feel much safer having this and being able to see who is outside my door before I open it. I've gifted a Ring Doorbell to my parents and will also gift it to my sister when she moves into a new apartment next year! I love this thing."

This kettle may be the smartest, prettiest kettle we have ever seen. It has English, French, and Spanish built in, along with a host of features you'll find helpful. You can schedule your kettle to heat to a specific temperature when you need it, choose from different preset temperatures, and customize the altitude setting depending on where you live. Additionally, there is a hold mode to keep the water hot from anywhere between 15 to 60 minutes, plus a chime to alert you that the water is done boiling. This kettle comes in four other colors if someone in your family is not so fond of blue.

Get a stone bath mat as a gift that the recipient and their whole family can appreciate. They are in for a real treat if they're not familiar with this water-wicking bath mat. "I got one years ago, and it is amazing," Liao says. "The mat dries so quickly that when it's my husband's turn to shower five minutes later, he doesn't feel any leftover wetness that lingers on a more traditional fabric bath mat. I bought one for my mother-in-law as a Christmas gift; she loved it so much that she eventually got two more. I admit this is a seemingly random gift, but once they try it out, they will never return to a plush bathmat ever again."
