There was gore in the original Resident Evil?

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

Most people don't associate the original Resident Evil with being overly grisly, but a lot's changed in the 30 years since it released. These days, we've become desensitized by dismemberment as a technical showcase rather than something to be horrified by, making 1996's Resident Evil look downright cozy next to today's horror pantheon. And yet, it was apparently shocking enough 30 years ago that series creator Shinji Mikami worried it wouldn't be fit for sale until Sony stepped in with some clutch packaging additions.

Mikami appeared on a Japanese podcast (timestamp) last month to talk about Resident Evil's legacy amid its 30th anniversary, but his comments haven't been widely reported until this week when Twitter user Genki started spotlighting the highlights. In one such example, Mikami tells the interviewers that OG Resident Evil was considered so "grotesque" that Capcom feared it wouldn't be fit for sale. We can only go off machine translation here as there's no English transcription, but Mikami says it was Sony that stepped in and saved the day by adding stickers to Resident Evil's packaging warning potential buyers of the game's gory nature.

Resident Evil creator Shinji Mikami says they were worried that the original Resident Evil game would not be allowed to go on sale because it was too grotesque but Sony had a solution that saved them. He said they didn't know what to do but Sony said that if they put a sticker on the game warning about the grotesque scenes then it would be okay to be released. Shinji Mikami says that really saved them. https://t.co/zgXIPPCIGQNovember 16, 2026

Mikami would continue to make excessively bloody games even beyond his time at Capcom with The Evil Within 1 and 2 at Tango Gameworks, but it's unclear if his next game has anything to do with the survival horror genre. He said in the same interview that his new studio Unbound is on track to release its debut title in 2027, but previous comments indicate he might be wanting to explore different genres after spending three decades scaring the bejesus out of players.
