The Sunday Papers

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Sunday Papers is our weekly roundup of great writing about (mostly) videogames from across the web.

Sundays are for googling local purveyors of live Christmas trees, the sole upside of no longer having a cat being that you can once again enjoy the smell of chopped woodland without the risk of it poisoning a family member. Well, then again, I mean... empirically speaking, you're probably just assuming that your human loved ones haven't eaten spruce needles. Can you say for an absolute fact that they wouldn't?

Fortunately, while your nan is off having her stomach pumped, you can pass the time by reading some good words from this week.

First up is, if I'm honest, nepotism. But there's also no rule against the Sunday Papers including RPS staff when they're not writing for RPS, so in the interests of spreading understanding about games media's most underappreciated discipline, here's our own guides writer Jeremy Blum on the making of his (excellent) Castlevania: Belmont's Curse walkthrough.

Ah, sod it, here's more Inside Baseball, as Reuters' Matthew Leake looks out at the shining sea to behold the creaking, precarious flotsam-raft we call games journalism. Fun fact: prior to editing, this quoted our good friend and former colleague, Brian Caldwell.

I continue to eagerly read about Marathon, I game I have zero intention of playing myself. This time it's Artemis Octavio for Skybox, on inhabiting the metal skin of the Rook.

Over on Unwinnable, Justin Reeve considers the distinctly American flavour of violence running through the Red Dead Redemption series.

Professor and author Dave Karpf checks in on the tech CEO class, who are definitely having a normal one now that a comrade is under the microscope.

Finally, five words: Neil the Seal Management Plan.

Music this week is, oh I don't know, let's say Beach Bunny. See you tomorrow.
