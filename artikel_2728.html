Celtics survive late scare in NBA Cup, beat Wizards 131-116

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Boston Celtics traveled to Washington to face the Wizards in their third game of the NBA Cup Group C on Tuesday night. After a close first quarter, Boston exploded in the second quarter with 46 points as they cruised to large lead at halftime, but Washington completely outplayed Boston in the second half as they raced back into the contest. Boston managed to hang on to the win late with a 131-116 victory with Jayson Tatum scoring 41 points on the night.

For Boston, they had a new-look starting five with Derrick White, Paul George, Jayson Tatum, Sam Hauser, and Neemias Queta. Head coach Joe Mazzulla opted to move away from Jordan Walsh in the starting back court and Mitchell Robinson (illness) remained out for Boston.

The Wizards started Trae Young, Kyshawn George, AJ Dybantsa, Anthony Davis, and Alexandre Sarr. The 2026 NBA number 1 pick, AJ Dybantsa, hit the first points of the game with a two-point jumper. Boston hit their first 4 shots of the game as they got out to an early 9-2 lead. AJ Dybantsa scored the first 4 points for Washington against his favorite team growing up.

Neemias Queta had two early lob dunks and 8 early points for Boston against the double-big lineup of Washington. Tatum and George had one bucket each, but it was all Neemias Queta to start the game for the visitors.

Baylor Scheierman and Payton Pritchard were the first Celtics off the bench for Boston. Ron Harper Jr. also checked into the game for first-quarter minutes for Boston as the coaching staff seemingly searching for a spark after 3 straight road losses.

Chris Cenac Jr. checked into the game late in the quarter and provided some defensive hustle for Boston. Payton Pritchard nailed a deep logo triple for his first score of the game. Both teams traded buckets in a four-minute stretch to finish off the quarter well. After a high-octane quarter of basketball, Boston took a 34-28 lead into the second quarter.

Paul George came to play in the second quarter; he hit back-to-back triples and dished out a dime to Luka Garza as Jayson Tatum watched on from the bench. Boston got out to a 17-point lead with Tatum on the bench as the C's racked up the points to start the second quarter.

Washington had just 34 points at the midway point of the second quarter. Tatum, George, and Queta had outscored the entire team, as the Celtics were in cruise control up by 23 points, 57-34.

Jayson Tatum returned to the game with a big lead, and he scored 8 straight points as Boston was dominating the Wiz. Tatum had 28 points and 5 assists in the first half, shooting 6 of 9 from downtown, as the team was primed for a third straight NBA Cup win.

Derrick White finally hit the scoreboard late in the quarter; he hit a pair of 3-pointers as Boston was in complete control, scoring a whopping 46 second-quarter points. The Celtics ended the half with 80 points in the first half alone, the Washington Wizards in a big hole, down by 31 points after 24 minutes of play.

Jayson Tatum, Paul George and Neemias Queta were outstanding for Boston combining for 61 points in the first half.

Washington outscored Boston 16-8 to start the second half; Joe Mazzulla called a timeout after AJ Dybantsa showed his hops, driving into the key and throwing down a Vince Carter-esque dunk. Paul George broke Kyshawn George's ankles straight after the timeout, as he had a nasty two-pointer pull up which the crowd appreciated.

Neemias Queta registered his fourth fouls and had to take a seat six minutes into the third. Washington cut Boston's lead back to 15 points, the Celtics struggling once more in the third quarter. Hugo Gonzalez had a pair of hustle plays late in the third for Boston, highlighted by a come from behind block on AJ Dybantsa.

Paul George and Payton Pritchard hit a pair of timely 3-pointers to give Boston some breathing room with 2 minutes to go in the quarter. The Celtics went on a late 8-0 run but the quarter ended, 104-89.

Boston struggled to shoot the ball in the fourth, they started out shooting just 1 of 7 from the field. Washington cut the Boston lead to just 11 points, 110-99 with seven minutes to go. A Sam Hauser corner 3-pointer broke a 4-minute field goal drought for Boston. Payton Pritchard's 14th point of the night gave Boston a 15-point lead once again.

Anthony Davis scored a two-pointer floater, Kyshawn George converted on a layup, and Washington was back in the game with 4 minutes to go, 117-110. Boston was blowing a massive lead as the Wizards home crowd finally woke up. The home team cutting it to just five points with plenty of time remaining.

Baylor Scheierman bailed out the C's down the stretch — he had a pair of 3-pointers and 8 points in the last 2 and a half minutes of the game. Boston hung on despite Washington pushing them all the way.

The Boston Celtics will now travel home after 4 straight road games to host the Atlanta Hawks on Friday 27th of November at 8 pm EST. A win would clinch Group C and a close loss would most likely give them a wild card berth.
