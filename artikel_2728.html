Week 11 odds: Steelers open as favorites vs. Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The PIAA football state championships may be a few weeks away, but the Pittsburgh Steelers and Philadelphia Eagles are renewing the cross-state rivalry on Sunday afternoon.

Pittsburgh is coming off an embarrassing primetime defeat to the Cincinnati Bengals, losing 38-18 in a game that wasn't nearly as close as the score indicates. The Steelers managed only 40 yards of offense in the first half and lost possession of first place in the AFC North. The Eagles are in a free fall, losing seven games in a row. Only the winless Los Angeles Chargers (0-8) have a worse losing streak. They're coming off their bye week after a 19-11 home loss to the New York Giants on November 8.

Both teams are looking for positivity and momentum following a terrible week for the Steelers and an unimaginably poor season for the Eagles in 2026. The Steelers are 4.5-point road favorites on FanDuel Sportsbook entering Week 11.

The Steelers are -200 on the moneyline for the 1 p.m. game on Sunday. Pittsburgh is 6-3 on the season, but 2-3 away from home. Mike McCarthy's team is averaging 17.4 points per game in those matches. The over/under total is set at 38.5 points.

Philly has historically dominated the matchup at home, but is without former Super Bowl MVP quarterback Jalen Hurts.

Philadelphia has won the last 11 games played on the eastern side of the state. Pittsburgh defeated the Eagles 38-29 at Heinz Field on October 11, 2020.

The Steelers have not won in Philadelphia since October 24, 1965. Their last victory came before the Super Bowl era, the Penguins were founded, the Pirates won their last two World Series titles, and NFL and AFL merged.

Pittsburgh needs a win to keep pace with the Bengals and hold off the Cleveland Browns, who are making a charge in the AFC Wild Card race. The Steelers look to prevent a snowball effect after a crushing Cincinnati loss that could be the turning point of the 2026 season.
