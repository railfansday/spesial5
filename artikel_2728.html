LSU flips linebacker Davon Smith away from Arizona: 'The culture here is just different'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Lane Kiffin and the Tigers are red-hot on the recruiting trail, and have added another piece as National Signing Day closes in.

The Tigers flipped three-star linebacker Davon Smith away from Arizona days before the Early Signing Period, Rivals has learned.

Smith declared for the Wildcats after an exciting official visit to Tucson over the summer.

LSU kept its foot on the gas with the Spring (Texas) Westfield defender and vaulted to the top with Smith during his official visit to Baton Rouge. The Tigers kept their foot on the gas with the talented LB prospect throughout his seniror season.

"The culture here is just different," Smith told Rivals. "They have advanced technology, top-tier coaching. Just overall a great place to go play ball. Coach (Blake) Baker is one of the best defensive coordinators I've been around."

The Tigers offered late in the spring, but came on exceedingly strong since.

Defensive coordinators Blake Baker and Chris Kiffin led the way in Smith's recruitment.

"This defense fits my play style very well. I see myself having a similar game to Davhon Keys, and they took a big step in my recruitment once I actually got to see it for sure," he explained. "It's a huge opportunity to play in the SEC, especially under Coach Baker and Coach Kiffin. My fit with the Tigers excites me because the defense is built on physicality."
