Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The State Open begins with warm-ups at 11 a.m.; the first event is scheduled for 1 p.m.

$10 for adults and $5 for students and seniors; children 5 and under and military members in uniform or with ID are admitted free. All tickets are digital through GoFan; no cash sales will be available at the event. Buy tickets in advance. Screenshots will not be accepted.

Greenwich sophomore Alden Charette defended her State Open diving title Thursday, scoring 546.75 points to break the Open record of 534.75 set by Westhill/Stamford's Rachel Burston in 2015. Charette now holds the FCIAC, Class LL and State Open records for 11 dives. She also holds Greenwich High's records for six and 11 dives. It was an amazing season for the sophomore.

Darien's Victoria Brupbacher was second with 457.40 points, Ridgefield's London Lewis was third with 438.30, Staples' Dylan Mogelof was fourth with 435.70, and New Canaan's Julie Frangione was fifth with 413.15.
