Dawn Staley, Geno Auriemma share uneventful handshake in reunion

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After their Final Four spat this past April, South Carolina women's basketball coach Dawn Staley and UConn women's basketball coach Geno Auriemma kept things admirably uneventful with their Tuesday pregame handshake.

As the two ranked teams faced off at Mohegan Sun Arena, Staley and Auriemma very briefly shook hands and kept about their business after the duo's very public argument during the spring.

The irony was that that heated exchange apparently happened over Staley missing a pregame handshake with Auriemma, one that's no longer required before women's NCAA tournament games. Time heals all disputes?

It's good to see these two pros being pros. It's only fair to the athletes to keep the focus on basketball.

South Carolina! They're the top team in the nation.
