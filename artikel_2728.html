CeeDee Lamb fined $23,882 for block the league used as a highlight

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

When Cowboys wide receiver CeeDee Lamb made a downfield block on a George Pickens touchdown catch Sunday against the 49ers, the league’s website was so impressed that the highlight video on NFL.com emphasized Lamb’s block over Pickens’ touchdown.

“Can’t-Miss Play: CeeDee Lamb’s HUGE block springs Pickens free for 39-yard TD,” the caption on the highlight video says.

Calling the play on Fox, Tom Brady praised Lamb, immediately recognizing it as a great downfield block.

But the league’s disciplinary process didn’t think it was so great. Lamb has been fined $23,882 for what the NFL deemed “Unnecessary Roughness: Use of the helmet.”

Lamb confirmed to reporters on Thursday that he had been informed he was getting fined, but he insisted he didn’t make contact with his helmet and found it frustrating that receivers take hits routinely but get fined when they make one.

“I can block, and I’m real good at doing things like that, but I get fined,” Lamb said. “I never touched him with my helmet. . . . I guess as a receiver, we can get hit, but can’t deliver the punishment.”

Lamb will surely appeal the fine for what looked, both to the league’s website and the league’s all-time greatest player, like a good block.
