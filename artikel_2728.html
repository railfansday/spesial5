Raiders TE Brock Bowers leaves loss to Seahawks early with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Brock Bowers didn't last long on Sunday afternoon.

The Las Vegas Raiders tight end was ruled out of their 21-13 loss to the Seattle Seahawks due to an ankle injury he suffered early in the first quarter. That knocked him out for the majority of Fernando Mendoza's starting debut at Allegiant Stadium.

Bowers, in the first quarter of the contest, went up to block Seahawks safety Nick Emmanwori for running back Ashton Jeanty. But as Jeanty cut the other way, Bowers had his legs rolled up on in a pile and he went down awkwardly.

Bowers remained down for a few moments while the team's training staff helped him, and he then limped to the sideline.

Bowers did return to the game briefly after that, which helped lead the way for Mendoza's rushing touchdown to get them on the board. But he left the game again late in the second quarter, and the team ruled him out for good just before the start of the second half with the ankle injury. Clearly, it was just too much for him to overcome.

Bowers may have escaped the contest without a major injury, per NFL Network. Early reports suggest the tight end is more day-to-day with his ankle issue. He'll undergo tests Monday to determine the severity of the injury.

Bowers finished with two catches for 18 yards. He entered Sunday with 658 yards and six touchdowns this season, his third in the league after the Raiders took him with the No. 13 overall pick out of Georgia in 2024.

Though the Raiders entered the locker room up by a field goal, the Seahawks scored a pair of touchdowns in the third quarter to break the game open. Jadarian Price ran it in from 12 yards out first, and then Jaxon Smith-Njigba hauled in a 71-yard pass from Drew Lock to give Seattle an 11-point lead at the time.

That was enough to secure the eight-point win.

Lock ended up going 14-of-17 for 240 yards with two touchdowns in the win for Seattle, which improved to 6-4 on the season. Zach Charbonnet had 94 rushing yards on two carries, and Smith-Njigba had 171 yards and a score on eight catches.

The Raiders dealt away quarterback Kirk Cousins at the deadline, which paved the way for Mendoza, the former Indiana star and reigning Heisman Trophy winner, to get the first start of his career. Mendoza went 17-of-29 for 143 yards in the loss. Mike Washington led them on the ground with 23 yards, and Jeanty had 46 receiving yards on four catches. The Raiders now sit at 5-5 on the year.

Though Mendoza appears to be holding his own so far, losing a target like Bowers for any substantial amount of time will make things much more difficult for him as his NFL career gets going.
