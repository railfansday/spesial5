Liam Coen: I was told ball switched hands on Parker Washington catch

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Giants held off the Jaguars on Sunday, but the outcome might have been different if referees had made a different ruling on a pass to Jaguars wideout Parker Washington early in the fourth quarter.

Washington appeared to make a diving catch in the back corner of the end zone, but officials on the field ruled that the pass was incomplete. Jaguars head coach Liam Coen challenged that Washington had control of the ball while inbounds, but the call was upheld on review.

After the game, Coen was asked if he received an explanation for the ruling by officials.

“I was told that the ball switched hands,” Coen said.

During the CBS broadcast of the game, officiating analyst Gene Steratore said that he spoke to the league office and that “they felt like there was not enough clear and obvious evidence to change the ruling on the field.”

Washington bobbled the ball briefly at first contact, but appeared to have established control by the time he hit the ground and maintained it after landing. The call went the other way in a consequential moment for the Jaguars, but an ill-timed turnover and Coen’s decisions later in the game also had a heavy hand in the final result at MetLife Stadium.
