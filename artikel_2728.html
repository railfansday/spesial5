Steelers LG Mason McCormick carted off with leg in air cast late in second quarter

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Pittsburgh has a significant injury concern along its offensive line.

Steelers left guard Mason McCormick was carted off the field in an air cast late in the first half after suffering an apparent lower-leg injury in Sunday night’s matchup against the Bengals.

McCormick went down on a third-and-8 play that ended with a sack of Aaron Rodgers.

McCormick’s right leg was placed in an air cast as he was carted off the field for further examination. McCormick had played every offensive snap at left guard for the Steelers so far in 2026.

Chris Boswell hit a 45-yard field goal after the injury to give Pittsburgh its first points of the game.

Cincinnati leads 17-3 late in the second quarter.

UPDATE 9:51 P.M. ET: The Steelers quickly ruled McCormick out with an ankle injury.
