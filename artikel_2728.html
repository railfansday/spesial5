Skip Bayless accuses Giants QB Jameis Winston of trying to go viral during loss to Commanders

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Skip Bayless wasn't impressed by Jameis Winston's unusual snap count during the New York Giants' 24-21 loss to the Washington Commanders. The outspoken commentator accused the quarterback of prioritizing attention over winning during Thursday night's matchup.

Winston attempted to draw Washington offside by incorporating a familiar song into his cadence. The moment quickly attracted attention, but Bayless questioned whether Winston was taking the situation seriously.

In a video shared on social media, Bayless criticized the quarterback's approach and suggested he wanted the moment to go viral.

"His cadence is just clownish to me," Bayless said, questioning Winston's decision to break into the song.

Bayless acknowledged laughing at the moment himself. However, he argued that Winston's behavior reflected a lack of seriousness during a critical stage of the game.

"That's how you lose. It's not serious," Bayless said.

The unusual sequence occurred late in the fourth quarter. New York held a 21-17 lead while facing fourth-and-1 deep in its own territory.

Winston attempted to fool Washington's defense with the unconventional hard count. However, the Commanders remained disciplined, and the Giants eventually punted after a delay-of-game penalty.

Washington later completed its comeback, winning 24-21 after a late touchdown.

Giants coach John Harbaugh subsequently explained that the unusual cadence was intentional. The team hoped to draw Washington offside rather than run an actual play.

The explanation provides important context for Bayless' criticism. Winston wasn't improvising without the coaching staff's knowledge, although the tactic ultimately failed.

The Giants now face questions about their late-game execution following another disappointing finish. Bayless' comments have added another layer of scrutiny to a moment that quickly became a viral talking point.
