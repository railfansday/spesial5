Buck Mason Just Released a Very Cozy Collab with Sierra Designs

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It’s the first team-up between the much-loved menswear brand and iconic outdoor supplier.

Every product was carefully curated by an Esquire editor. We may earn a commission from these links. Here’s how we test products and why you should trust us.

El Niño be damned. Winter is just around the corner, and even in a very weird year for weather, I can guarantee you this: There are cold days ahead. It pays to be prepared for them. And you could do a whole lot worse, preparation-wise, than the new collaboration between Buck Mason and Sierra Designs.

You probably know both brands already, but just in case you don’t, here’s a brief primer. Buck Mason, founded in 2013, makes the kind of cool-but-accessible menswear staples that you can build an entire wardrobe around. Sierra Designs is one of the OG American outdoors brands, founded in 1965 and responsible for iconic designs like the 60/40 mountain parka and all sorts of other equipment and clothing built for exploring nature. Both, handily enough, were born in California—and today they released their first-ever collaboration.

The new collection draws heavily from Sierra Designs’ ‘70s heyday, with reworked versions of an archival down jacket and vest serving as the stars of the show. Both are made from lightweight Japanese nylon and utilize a 600-fill-power, 80/20 blend of down and feathers for insulation. They’re complemented by a range of vintage-y tees, thermals, totes, and caps.

The overall vibe is easygoing and not too techy—exactly the sort of stuff you’ll want to wear in everyday life, not just on some windswept summit. Which is what makes it so damn good. There’s no shortage of high-performance winter gear out there, but much of it falls short on the style front. This is the sort of stuff that’ll do the job but won’t have you looking like you just finished a black-diamond run. Wear it with chinos and hiking boots or jeans and sneakers. To the coffee shop on a blustery morning or the park for a day out with the family. Or, yeah, even in the great outdoors. Just make sure to snag whatever you like soon, as Buck Mason’s collaborations have a nasty habit of selling out.
