A Behind-the-Scenes Look at Esquire’s Party for the 20th Anniversary of the Big Black Book

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

At the Ludlow House on the Lower East Side, a bunch of New York’s coolest people gathered to raise a glass to the essential guide to style and luxury.

It’s been two decades since the first edition of the Big Black Book debuted in 2006. Back then, a style guide devoted to the ins and outs of the luxury market wasn’t exactly common, but we believed it was necessary. The idea wasn’t to spam readers with a bunch of stuff they couldn’t afford but to give them a glimpse into the world of well-made, time-tested pieces that can create the backbone of a considered wardrobe.

Turns out, we were right. Men want to dress well, and they want to invest in the items—bench-made boots, bespoke tailoring, designer apparel—that look great on day one but even better a few years down the line. The fact that the Big Black Book is still going strong in 2026 is a testament to that, so we decided to celebrate. At the Ludlow House on the Lower East Side, we gathered a group of people who represent the BBB ethos. Sharp dressers, sure, but also sharp minds—the folks who don’t just define luxury in the moment but also lay the foundation for its future.

It would’ve been even more fun to invite every Big Black Book fan along for the ride, but we quickly realized that logistics—and fire marshals—might not allow it. So, in lieu of bringing everyone inside, we’re sharing a gallery of photos from the party. Keep scrolling for an inside look at the festivities.
