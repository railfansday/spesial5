All About Madelaine Petsch and Tyga’s Mysterious 2-Year Relationship Amid Those Breakup Rumors

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

One of the most unexpected couples to come out of 2025? That’s gotta be Madelaine Petsch and Tyga, who shocked everyone when they sparked dating rumors just about a year and a half earlier. But the two kept on dating (and keeping everyone guessing) up until November 2026, when multiple sources confirmed their split to People. I still don’t know whether that rock on her left ring finger was actually an engagement ring, but here is what I do know about Madelaine and Tyga’s low-key relationship leading up to their rumored breakup.

For starters: Prior to Tyga, Madelaine was most recently linked to music manager Anthony Li, and before that, she had a three-year relationship with musician and actor Travis Mills. Tyga, meanwhile, famously dated Kylie Jenner for three years and, before that, he had a relationship with Blac Chyna, with whom he shares a 13-year-old son, King Cairo Stevenson. Wondering how he and Madelaine came to be? Let’s get into it.

During a recent episode of Cosmo’s Blind Date, Madelaine reveals that she and Tyga first crossed paths at Vas J. Morgan and Michael Braun’s Halloween party in 2024. “I was Kim Possible. Camila Mendes was Shego,” she says. Iconic.

Tyga, meanwhile, appears to have been dressed like a…skeleton?

Madelaine attends Tyga’s 35th birthday party at Six Flags Magic Mountain (which he casually rents out for the occasion).

The two are photographed together for the first time at a Lakers game, sparking dating rumors.

Later in the month, the maybe-couple is spotted together at Vanity Fair’s Instagram Young Hollywood party at Chateau Marmont, where they’re photographed together inside and spotted leaving arm in arm.

A source confirms to Us Weekly that the couple is “definitely” dating…and has been “for about four months.”

Meanwhile, another insider tells TMZ that the two started out as friends but “around a month ago the friendship turned romantic.” The insider adds, “They’re telling people who know them that the relationship is new, and they’re enjoying each other’s company and their time together.”

The two hit up Coachella together, and in a since-deleted photo dump following the weekend, Madelaine makes the relationship Instagram official.

Later in the month, they make another appearance together at Disneyland.

Madelaine and Tyga are spotted at The Bird Streets Club together, seemingly confirming that they’re still going strong.

A month later, they’re photographed in L.A. during another cute date night.

Madelaine posts a photo dump on Instagram after attending a wedding in Italy…which happens to include a photo of herself wearing a massive diamond on her left ring finger…which of course sparks engagement rumors.

The two are photographed together amid engagement rumors at the FIFA World Cup quarterfinals.

Camila Mendes shares a video of Tyga serenading Madelaine at her 32nd birthday party to her Instagram Story.

A source for People claims Madelaine and Tyga are “going strong,” adding, “They’re really happy and she’s been spending time with his son, too.”

They’re also spotted enjoying a spooky date night for Scary Farm at Knott’s Berry Farm.

Madelaine appears on Cosmo’s Blind Date, where she confirms that she’s “in a relationship.” Oh, and her mysterious diamond ring makes an appearance, too…

People reports that the couple has split after more than a year of dating, though neither Madelaine nor Tyga has confirmed the rumors. Siiigh.
