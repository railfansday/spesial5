Prediction: Monza vs Fiorentina

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Marking their return from November’s international break on Monday night, Fiorentina will travel to Lombardy when they meet Monza.

With action from across Europe’s elite domestic leagues pencilled in on Monday, there are plenty of attractive betting promotions available.

In a showdown between two camps who find themselves scrapping it out at the foot of the Serie A standings, you can find all of the key Monza vs Fiorentina H2H stats here.

Verdict: Gustavo Varela over 0.5. shots on target

Starting with the hosts, while Monza might have only made their iconic Serie A return this season, it is no secret that the Biancorossi have an almighty job on their hands in the race to keep hold of their top-flight status. Last seen heading into November’s international break with a toothless 1-0 loss at home against Atalanta, Ivan Juric’s side find themselves sat in the drop zone and widely regarded as the standout relegation candidate. Signing off October with a 2-1 defeat against Bologna, the Lombardy-based outfit have lost each of their previous four consecutive Serie A appearances by an aggregate score of 10-4. With their nightmare rut underlined by a 4-1 drumming against Napoli last month, Monza have also gained an unwanted reputation for their defensive struggles. In fact, Monday’s hosts have registered just a sole Serie A clean sheet from any of their first 11 attempts.

While French youngster Yvan Maye might have been suffering from a muscle issue prior to November’s international break, Monza are hopeful that the 20-year-old will return to Juric’s squad here. Likewise, former Pisa defender Samuele Birindelli could be handed a full recall after recovering from a recent knock.

Enjoying what has been a sensational Seire A flurry this season and already registering nine goals in his first 10 top-flight appearances, the red-hot Gustavo Varela will lead Monza’s attack. Completing a shock loan switch to Lombardy over the summer, Southampton youngster Jay Robinson should partner Varela in the final third.

As for the visitors, only just dragging themselves over the finishing line last season as they clung to their Serie A status, Fiorentina were simply hoping for smoother sailing this time around. However, enduring another public struggle and with fans around Tuscany making their frustrations abundantly clear, Palo Vanoli’s men are searching for an essential boost when they mark their return on Monday night. However, despite opening November with a rollercoaster 4-2 victory away at Sassuolo, that is the only win that the Viola have registered from their previous seven consecutive Serie A appearances. Despite heading into the fortnight interval with an action-packed 2-2 draw at home against Italian icons Juventus, Fiorentina have shown their own glaring defensive struggles this season. Along with shipping at least a pair of goals in all of their last six straight outings, Vanoli’s side have failed to record a single top-flight clean sheet since opening up the new campaign.

Yet to make a maiden Serie A appearance this season and still sidelined with a long-term knee injury, Fiorentina will once again be without Italian defender Fabiano Parisi on Monday night. Completing a summer from Premier League outfit Everton, Portuguese striker Beto will be hoping to make a first start since October 10th.

Now in his third campaign in Tuscany, former Manchester United goalkeeper David de Gea will start between the sticks here. Notching a quartet of Serie A strikes since arriving from Spanish icons Real Madrid, 19-year-old Franco Mastantuono will play a leading role in the final third.

Two camps who have shown their own glaring defensive issues this season, Monza and Fiorentina also have a habit of producing plenty of fireworks when they meet. Watching Monday’s hosts claim a 2-1 victory when they last welcomed the Viola to Lombardy for a Serie A showdown back in January 2025, chances could come thick and fast. Likewise, with six of the seven historic battles between Monday’s opponents finish with both teams finding the net, we could be treated to a potential rollercoaster Serie A showdown.
