Sources: Rangers doing everything to block Ryan Naderi move

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

FOCUS Exclusive reporting, domestic and international transfer markets, and elite-level insights. His “Deep-Lens” analysis connects the dots between boardroom strategy and pitch-level recruitment, providing a level of transfer clarity that goes beyond surface-level rumours.

Rangers are working hard to keep hold of Ryan Naderi ahead of the January transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.

The 23-year-old has become a crucial part of Derek McInnes' ambitious plans and is a fans' favourite at Ibrox, and his departure would come as a significant blow.

A number of clubs across Europe are believed to have been placed on high alert as a result of his performances this season, where he has scored nine goals in 17 appearances.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Rangers have no plans to entertain Naderi's exit regardless of the interest in his situation.

The Czechia international is seen as a crucial asset in their attempts to win silverware this season, having recently scored in the League Cup semi-final to send Gers through.

McInnes is counting on Naderi to play an important role for the rest of the season, and any January exit has now been ruled out, with Rangers determined to block any move.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider on 8 November that Rangers are bracing for interest in Naderi and are expecting to receive offers.

However, those offers will not be entertained, with the forward still on a long-term contract at Ibrox and plans in place to keep him at the club for the foreseeable future.

Regardless of the interest from elsewhere, Rangers will do everything in their power to ensure Naderi remains part of their squad for the second half of the season.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I don’t see how he could be prised away in January, he's really stepped up since the injuries to Chermiti and Shankland.

"Naderi has formed a good partnership up front with Kelsy, and Rangers have been on a good run of form domestically with those two leading the line.

"Naderi has nine goals and six assists so far this season, that shows how important he is for Derek McInnes' side, and he's under contract until 2029 as well.

"Derek McInnes won't be looking to weaken his squad in January, he'll be looking to keep hold of Naderi and as you said he's got a bit of a knack of popping up with important goals.

"So Rangers will be doing everything in their power not to lose him and definitely see him as an important part of their plans."

Naderi's performances for Rangers so far this season have attracted interest from a number of clubs across European football, many of whom are tracking his situation.

English Championship outfit West Ham are believed to be among them, with Scott Parker aiming to strengthen his attacking options as they continue their bid for promotion.

However, Football Insider revealed on 13 November that Naderi is prepared to reject any advances from West Ham in the January transfer window.

The forward has no plans to drop into the Championship and is happy with life at Rangers where he will help the side compete for silverware and European qualification.

However, there are fears that offers from the Premier League could turn his head if any top-flight English clubs were prepared to make a move in the near future.
