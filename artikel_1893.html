Liverpool ‘regret’ Ryan Gravenberch deal as sale lined up – Club adviser

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Liverpool may make a surprise move to sell out-of-favour midfielder Ryan Gravenberch in the upcoming January transfer window.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Dutchman could potentially make a return to the Bundesliga.

Gravenberch only signed a new deal at Anfield back in March, but has fallen to the fringes of the squad under Andoni Iraola in recent months.

Wyness believes that Liverpool may already “regret” their move to tie Gravenberch down long-term, and are likely to consider selling him within the next 12 months.

The 24-year-old has started just four Premier League games so far this term, and is now out injured having been left as an unused substitute for the defeat to Arsenal on 1 November.

Trey Nyoni’s emergence in the first-team squad hasn’t helped Gravenberch, who now has an extra body to compete with for game time in the midfield.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy business advising elite clubs – believes that Liverpool are likely to look favourably at the chance to offload Gravenberch’s “expensive wages” as soon as possible.

The former Ajax starlet made 50 appearances in all competitions last season, but has found himself behind both Dominik Szoboszlai and Alexis Mac Allister in the pecking order this term, as well as Nyoni at times.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained that he expects movement in terms of Gravenberch’s future either in January or next summer.

“I don't think it's the player having regret, I think it's Liverpool possibly having regret as to whether they signed him. That's the problem. It's a bet on the system and when you change manager you change systems,” he said.

“And when he was given that renewal, that's what they were thinking about at the time. I think it’s probably the club that are thinking maybe that was the wrong decision at that time.

“He's on expensive wages. Unless there's a squad depth issue that means they don't want to move him on, I think they'll be looking to move him on to the Bundesliga as soon as they can do. That’s where I think the interest might be.

“I'd expect to see possible movement even as soon as January, or if not next summer.”

Football Insider revealed on Thursday that Liverpool will explore a “very expensive” move for Bayern Munich’s Jamal Musiala next summer.

Recent reports have suggested that the attacking midfielder has a release clause that can be triggered from next summer, with Bayern understood to be worried about his future.

However, Musiala is continuing to suffer from a neurological issue that is causing him to have seizures during games and training sessions, and he has started just once in the Bundesliga so far this term.

Liverpool are prepared to launch a move for the 23-year-old starlet ahead of next season, but only if he can prove his fitness at the top level between now and then.
