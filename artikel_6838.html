Cobalt Is Winter’s Coolest Shade: Here’s How to Wear It

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The color of the season is ultra-easy to style.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Every season, there’s a new breakthrough color. Last year, we saw butter yellow and tomato red take over, and this fall we’ve seen burgundy star as the hue of the season. Now, cobalt blue is taking center stage, and we’re here for it.

Striking and statement-making, cobalt blue is one of our favorite standout color trends yet. It’s bold, yes, but somehow it can seamlessly transition from a star-of-the-show satin top or sparkling pump to a foundational cardigan or cashmere sweater. The color gained traction on last season’s runways, but it’s since permeated street-style culture around the world. One of our favorite ways to wear cobalt is with an eye-catching accessory, and Dior’s Mini Book Tote is currently topping our holiday wish lists.

But even if you’re not in the market for a new handbag, there are plenty of ways to wear the shade. Whether you’re headed on a Sunday stroll through your local holiday market or hitting a fancy festive soirée, we’ve found a cobalt blue-inspired outfit that’s bringing a little colorful cheer to your winter ensemble.

Make cobalt the main event with a brazen blue overcoat. Paired with patent heeled boots and sporty styling underneath, this is the perfect off-duty outfit—taking you from a workout to brunch. We’re also loving @designedbychep’s custom embroidered sweaters (which can come in cobalt threading!), and a lighter blue-hued bag for contrast.

Flip the script and wear your cobalt as a layer underneath an oversize trench. ATM and Aligne both make great colorful basics, while Jenni Kayne’s quiet luxury takes form in a structured overcoat. The cobalt strap on Moon Boot’s fluffy ballerinas is a nice touch.

Take the trend to your holiday party, pairing sleek satin with a luxe leather to elevate your look. A sparkly cobalt heel ties in the top, and Larroudé makes the perfect pair.

David Yurman Crossover Three Row Huggie Hoop Earrings

If you’re looking to test the cobalt blue waters, start with a radiant accessory. White & Warren’s vibrant travel wrap and Clare V’s more muted bags offer different levels of brazenness, so you can choose which feels more suited to you.

A casual, off-duty look takes a trend-forward turn with Guest in Residence’s cardigan. Paired with AG’s beloved lightly baggy jeans and polished with Scarosso’s easy loafers, running errands has never looked so chic.
