Pokémon Champions X Pokémon GO Collab Taking Place Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

As you've likely already seen, Pokémon Champions is getting a balance update, more Pokémon and more Mega Evolutions next month on 2nd December 2026.

A week after this, there'll also be an in-game collab taking place with the mobile hit Pokémon GO. This campaign takes place between 8th December 2026 and 18th December 2026, and during this time, you'll be able to unlock a Professor Willow Outfit Set and 10 Training Tickets.

"You can obtain the Professor Willow Outfit Set one time only by bringing Pokémon you befriended in Pokémon GO to Pokémon Champions as visitors.

"You can obtain the Training Tickets by bringing Pokémon you befriended in Pokémon GO to Pokémon Champions as visitors. You will get one Training Ticket the first time you bring a Pokémon first discovered in each of the regions"

These rewards can be obtained in the Switch and mobile versions of Champions. These rewards may also be made available in the shop (or other areas of the game) at a later date.

During the Pokémon Champions Celebration Event in Pokémon GO (between 8th December 2026 and 14th December 2026), you can expect encounters with Charmander, Starly, Grookey "and more" in the wild. You'll also have the chance to encounter Absol, Gible, and others. You can find out more details about the Raids, Event Bonuses, Field Research (and more) on the Pokémon GO website.

"Each of these Pokémon is especially valuable on Pokémon Champions teams once they’ve been fully evolved (except Absol, of course, who’s ready for action right away). There will also be an increased chance to encounter Kecleon at PokéStops!"
