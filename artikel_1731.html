NFL legend Tom Brady in talks to buy minority Chelsea stake

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

NFL legend Tom Brady is in talks to buy a minority stake in Chelsea.

The 49-year-old recently sold a minority stake in Birmingham City three years after buying in and is looking to leverage his positive relationship with Behdad Eghbali to take a similar stake in Chelsea.

It is said that Brady, who attended the Club World Cup as a guest of Chelsea, is looking to buy only a small stake in the club.

His interest comes barely two months after the Eghabli-controlled Clearlake Capital bought Todd Boehly and Mark Walter out of their co-controlling stake in the club, increasing their own stake to 86.5 per cent. That deal, completed in September, was worth nearly £1bn.

Brady works as an NFL analyst for Fox Sports as his day job, but also holds a stake in the Las Vegas Raiders.

Birmingham confirmed his move to sell his shares in a club statement earlier this month, which read: “It was truly amazing to have someone with Tom’s experience and passion associated with the Club, and the Club looks forward to continuing to build on that foundation from the training ground to the Birmingham community.”

Brady is one of the NFL’s most decorated players, having won the Super Bowl a record seven times, six of which came with the New England Patriots.

While working with Birmingham City, he appeared in an Amazon Prime documentary about the club’s fortunes, in which he publicly questioned then-manager Wayne Rooney’s work ethic.

Boehly’s departure from Chelsea left Clearlake Capital as the club’s majority owners, with Swiss businessman Hansjorg Wyss also owning a 13.5 per cent stake.
