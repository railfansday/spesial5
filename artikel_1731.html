Massive surprise: Liverpool heads turned by striker in electric form, they want to sign him

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Liverpool have reportedly had their heads turned. They're aiming for a new striker and he's in ridiculously good form.

Liverpool's attack is a strange one. It's wildly imbalanced and lacks centre-forwards, especially with Hugo Ektike out injured. The Reds are heavily reliant on Alexander Isak when he's available.

Of course, that's been relatively okay so far. Isak has taken his chances and is performing as one of the best finishers in world football right now - long may that continue.

But with it completely unknown if Ekitike will be able to play a significant role this season, it's entirely possible that Liverpool look for another striker. Someone to ease the pressure on Isak and maybe even rival him for a place.

That appears to be the logic, if reports coming out of Italy are to be believed.

The Portuguese star only signed for them in the summer after their promotion to Serie A. And that's gone well for him. Very well. Varela has scored nine times in nine games, leading the scoring charts in Italy. Monza's attack isn't poor with him at the forefront, either. Calciomercato.it reports that Liverpool are now in the running to sign Monza striker Gustavo Varela. The Portuguese star only signed for them in the summer after their promotion to Serie A.

And that's gone well for him. Very well. Varela has scored nine times in nine games, leading the scoring charts in Italy.

Monza's attack isn't poor with him at the forefront, either. They've scored 17 times over their 11 fixtures, so this isn't just a case of a player scoring all on his own to the expense of the team overall.

But we would still categorise this one as almost certainly not true. Varela has started the season wonderfully but the idea that Liverpool are suddenly on board with signing him after around 11 matches is ridiculous.

The 21-year-old scored six times in Portugal last season and his efforts this campaign are highly likely to be a purple patch. Maybe that's not the case, of course, but elite teams would want to see a full season before deciding that.

Serie A is historically littered with players who enjoyed remarkable breakout campaigns, got a big move, and then fell away quickly. It's also worth noting that Varela has scored two penalties - he'd actually be joint-third in the charts for non-penalty goals.

So we're not buying this one. Maybe Varela keeps up his form all season and then yes, absolutely Liverpool would be looking at him. 11 games in, though? That's just not enough.
