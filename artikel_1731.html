Rasmus Hojlund reveals the Manchester United stars left ‘devastated’ by INEOS decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Rasmus Hojlund has revealed that Bruno Fernandes was among the Manchester United players to be left “devastated” by the recent sacking of Michael Carrick.

Carrick was relieved of his duties as Man Utd boss earlier in November after weeks of intense speculation and less than six months in permanent charge on a two-year contract, with just one win from their opening 10 games having seen his side languish in the Premier League relegation zone.

It is United’s worst start to a top-flight season since 1972, with former Chelsea and Tottenham manager Mauricio Pochettino leaving his role as head coach of the United States national team to quickly succeed Carrick, only to suffer a chastening 4-0 defeat by arch-rivals Liverpool with 10 men in his first match in charge.

Carrick’s final weeks at United saw senior players air their grievances in public interviews following listless defeats, with Lisandro Martinez taking aim at the club’s recruitment after the loss to Atletico Madrid and Fernandes then questioning Carrick’s tactics in the 4-1 thrashing away at Como.

However, captain and coach were said to have held “clear-the-air talks” and been on good terms before the latter’s dismissal, with Hojlund revealing that Fernandes - along with United and Portugal team-mate Diogo Dalot - had been “devastated” by Carrick’s departure but hopeful of bouncing back quickly under his replacement.

The Danish striker was back in Manchester on Tuesday night in the Champions League, scoring for Napoli against City and making headlines with his pro-United celebration in a lively 2-2 draw at the Etihad Stadium.

Hojlund previously spent three years at Old Trafford between 2023-26 following a high-profile £72million transfer from Atalanta aged 20, netting 26 times in 95 total appearances across all competitions before returning to Serie A for £38m in the summer as Napoli exercised their option for a permanent deal following a successful season-long loan spell last term.

“In the international break when I spoke with Bruno and Diogo, they were all obviously devastated that Michael was going but also looking forward to some change,” said Hojlund, who recently scored twice for Denmark in their 4-1 thrashing of a Cristiano Ronaldo-less Portugal in the Nations League.

“I am still speaking with [Denmark team-mate and United player] Patrick [Dorgu] and I just wish the guys all the best and I am sure they will turn it around this season.”
