Chelsea injury update: Moises Caicedo, Joao Pedro, Reece James latest news and return dates

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Moises Caicedo has retaken his place on Chelsea’s treatment table, compounding a miserable afternoon for the Blues.

Xabi Alonso’s side were handed a 4-1 hiding by Leeds on Saturday afternoon, leaving them with one win in their last five and under serious pressure to get back on track.

Their efforts to sink the Yorkshiremen were not helped when Caicedo was forced off with 20 minutes played, shortly after a crunching James Justin tackle.

With a Friday night trip to Nottingham on the cards, Alonso will hope to have the Ecuadorian at his disposal as he bids to get his struggling side back on track.

With that said, here is all the latest Chelsea injury news and potential return dates...

Moises Caicedo overcame an international break knock to start as Chelsea faced Leeds at the weekend, but he lasted just 20 minutes before being forced off.

He came off worse from a heavy tackle by James Justin and attempted to play on, but eventually signalled to the bench that he could not continue.

Speaking at full-time, Alonso explained: “It’s a knock and we need to wait until tomorrow to see how he is. He was in pain and let's see how bad it is.”

Chelsea’s talisman striker remains sidelined with a thigh injury suffered ahead of last month’s shock defeat by Tottenham.

He has now been sidelined for a full four weeks, and did not represent Brazil during the international break.

Speaking ahead of hosting Leeds, Alonso confirmed he was still facing a number of weeks out and that he has yet to return to training.

“Not yet,” the Spaniard replied when asked about his No9. “He’s not ready to train yet for the team. Not for Leeds, no. We need to see day by day how he is feeling.”

Behind the scenes at Chelsea, some are concerned he will not be able to feature for the Blues until the new year.

Reece James suffered a knee injury in training last month, and was forced to undergo what the club described as a minor surgery.

Standard Sport reported at the time that he was facing three months out and is not expected to return until January.

Maxence Lacroix was forced off with a hamstring injury as Chelsea were knocked out of the Carabao Cup by Liverpool.

He was attempting to track Cody Gakpo’s run shortly before half-time when he pulled up and was withdrawn.

The Frenchman is not expected to return to the fold until January.

Jorrel Hato has been out with a back injury since before the November international break, which is continuing to plague him.

There was optimism he could make a comeback to face Leeds at the weekend, but speaking ahead of the match, Alonso said that would not be possible: “Jorrel, no. Jorrel has a back problem and we need to be patient because it’s going to take a while.”

Jamie Gittens is slowly closing in on a return to competitive action for Chelsea, having recently returned to training.

He has been sidelined since October with an ankle injury sustained on international duty.

Alonso said: “Jamie Gittens, he’s better. He’s already training with the team but we want to give him more time to come back in the squad.”
