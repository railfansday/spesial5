Arsenal make approach to sign Spain international to replace £50m star

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Arsenal have made contact with the representatives of Sporting CP right-back Ivan Fresneda, according to reports.

Fresneda, who spent time in Real Madrid’s youth set-up, has made 94 appearances in all competitions for Sporting, having signed from Real Valladolid in August 2023.

The right-back made his first appearance for the Spain national team over the October international break, but has just 18 months left on his deal with Sporting.

Arsenal have been long-time admirers of Fresneda, and The Athletic now reports that the Gunners have made contact with the 22-year-old’s representatives ahead of the January transfer window.

Mikel Arteta’s side are well-stocked with options at right-back, with Jurrien Timber and Ben White both impressing so far this season.

However, while Timber is reportedly close to signing an extension on his current contract, Fresneda is seen as a potential successor to 29-year-old White, whose contract runs out in June 2028.

Arsenal are also said to be interested in young Paris Saint-Germain full-back David Boly, although a move for the talented 17-year-old could be difficult to pull off.

Elsewhere, the Gunners could look to strengthen at left wing after letting Gabriel Martinelli and Leandro Trossard leave over the summer.

Christos Tzolis made a bright start after arriving from Club Brugge, but the Greek winger has not scored or assisted since returning from injury at the start of November.

RB Leipzig winger Antonio Nusa is a player that Arsenal have kept an eye on and could target in January.

The 21-year-old has notched up five goals and seven assists in 10 Bundesliga appearances so far this season and scored for Norway at last summer’s World Cup.
