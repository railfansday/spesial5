Tricoci University of Beauty Culture Partners with L3VEL3 to Expand Professional Barbering Education Across All 15 Campuses

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

CHICAGO, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Tricoci University of Beauty Culture is excited to announce a new partnership with L3VEL3, a nationally recognized professional grooming and barbering brand. The partnership will bring L3VEL3's professional grooming, styling, and finishing products into Tricoci University's student clinics across all 15 campuses in Illinois, Indiana, and Wisconsin, giving students hands-on experience with products used throughout today's professional barbering and beauty industries.

The partnership reflects Tricoci University's continued commitment to providing students with career-ready education that extends beyond the classroom. By collaborating with brands that are helping shape the professional beauty industry, Tricoci aims to connect students with the products, tools, techniques, and industry professionals that can help prepare them for successful careers.

Tricoci University and L3VEL3 are currently finalizing the initial product assortment, ordering process, educator training, and integration into student clinics. A coordinated rollout across all 15 campuses is expected by early 2027, with top L3VEL artists beginning staff and student training in January. Students will have opportunities to work with L3VEL3 products while developing their knowledge of professional grooming and styling techniques, product recommendations, retail practices, and the importance of delivering an exceptional guest experience.

In addition to introducing professional products into its student clinics, Tricoci University is exploring opportunities to incorporate L3VEL3's expanding collection of professional barbering tools into its student kits. The potential assortment includes clippers, trimmers, shavers, shears, razors, and the brand's new professional blow dryer, alongside a selection of grooming and styling products. While the final kit assortment is still being determined, the goal is to provide students with access to professional-quality tools and products they can use throughout their education and carry into their careers after graduation.

The partnership will also create opportunities for students and educators to engage directly with established professionals in the barbering industry. Tricoci University looks forward to bringing L3VEL3 artists to its campuses for educational demonstrations, technical training, and professional development experiences. The brand's artists may also participate in Tricoci University's annual Beauty Blitz, which brings students and educators together for industry education, inspiration, and hands-on learning.

Through these experiences, students will have opportunities to learn new techniques, gain insight from nationally recognized artists, and build connections within the professional barbering community. The partnership also supports a broader approach to career education that emphasizes not only technical proficiency, but also product knowledge, guest relationships, retail skills, personal branding, and the business acumen needed to succeed in today's competitive beauty industry.

"We want students to graduate with more than the technical skills needed to pass their state board exams. We want them to be confident, competitive professionals who understand the industry and are prepared to take advantage of the opportunities available to them," said Larry Foster, Chief Operating Officer at Tricoci University of Beauty Culture. "L3VEL3 is an exciting brand that has made a tremendous impact on professional barbering, particularly among the next generation of beauty professionals. This partnership gives our students access to products, tools, and potentially some of the artists they already know and admire. We are constantly looking for ways to bring the professional industry directly into our classrooms, and this is another important step in creating an educational experience that prepares students for success beyond graduation."

Looking ahead, Tricoci University sees opportunities to expand the partnership through ongoing artist education, advanced technical workshops, student competitions, demonstrations, and other experiences that connect students with the professional barbering community. By introducing students to recognized industry brands and professionals during their education, Tricoci University continues to strengthen the connection between career training and the evolving expectations of today's beauty industry.
