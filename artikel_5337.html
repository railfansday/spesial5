Zcash’s Game-Changing NU7 Upgrade Goes Live, Slashing Block Times 3X as $5,000 ZEC Rally Comes Into Focus

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

NU7 has successfully activated on mainnet. A new era of Zcash is here. $ZEC pic.twitter.com/rPaSFe9h7j

The Network Sustainability Mechanism addresses a long-term challenge that Zcash shares with Bitcoin: how to keep its network secure as mining rewards decline with each halving. As block subsidies become smaller, both networks face questions about whether transaction fees alone can provide enough incentive for miners to continue securing the blockchain.

Under the new mechanism, 60% of transaction fees from each block goes into a publicly visible reserve, while miners receive the remaining 40%. The approach creates a dedicated funding pool to support Zcash’s long-term security and sustainability while preserving its hard-capped supply of 21 million ZEC.

The reserved fees are scheduled to be gradually reissued through block rewards starting at block 8,939,664, expected in 2031, with the recycled funds distributed along a smooth curve on top of the existing halving schedule.

NU7 also introduces a cap on the amount of shielded transaction activity that can be included in each block, reducing the volume of data wallets need to scan when searching for incoming payments. This is expected to make wallet synchronization more efficient and improve the overall user experience.

Despite the major network upgrade, ZEC has yet to register a strong bullish market reaction. The cryptocurrency was down 0.8% over the past 24 hours, trading at $1,313 at press time, as broader weakness across the crypto market weighed on prices.

Despite the recent price pullback, some prominent crypto investors remain bullish on Zcash’s longer-term prospects. Chris Burniske, a renowned investor known for his early interest in Solana (SOL), pointed to the ZEC/BTC chart as the key indicator to watch, describing it as “still the only chart that matters as it shows what comes next.”
