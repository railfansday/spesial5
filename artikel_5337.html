Mark Zuckerberg’s Meta AI predicts a major catalyst could spark a mouthwatering gold rally that, if it happens, would leave investors euphoric.

The proposal calls for a coordinated announcement by a coalition of major emerging-market central banks and sovereign wealth funds, stating their intention to launch a multi-year strategic program to accumulate gold.

The aim is to significantly increase gold’s share of their official reserves as a deliberate move to diversify away from traditional fiat currencies.

This initiative would accelerate a structural shift in the global monetary system and generate sustained official-sector demand that surpasses current annual gold mine supply.

On the higher timeframes, gold has been consolidating in the $3,990–$4,050 range after retreating from recent highs above $4,200 earlier in October 2026.

The price remains above key intermediate support levels while facing resistance in the $4,150–$4,250 range. In a typical bull market, a sustained breakout above $4,300–$4,500 would confirm intermediate strength and pave the way for new all-time highs.

In an extreme scenario of strategic accumulation by multiple central banks, surpassing previous highs could trigger significant measured-move extensions and multi-year Fibonacci projections from the long-term base established earlier in the decade.

Currently, momentum indicators are neutral to corrective, indicating substantial room to move into the extreme overbought levels typically seen in major precious-metals advances.

Key near-term support levels are located around $3,900–$3,950 and $3,750–$3,800. Maintaining those levels would keep the broader uptrend intact.

Beyond official gold purchases, several major global developments could strengthen the broader precious metals market. A lasting resolution to the Russia-Ukraine conflict could free up capital and reduce certain risk premiums, while still allowing for ongoing monetary and geopolitical uncertainties that favor hard assets.

A permanent ceasefire between Iran and the United States, along with normalized shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, could help lower energy-driven inflation spikes. This situation might also encourage central banks to maintain accommodative policies longer.

However, things aren’t looking too great in either Ukraine or the Middle East, with Russia bombing civilians as recently as yesterday, while Iran and the US continue to attack one another, making any hopes of a ceasefire seem a long way off.

Other supporting factors include growing concerns about the stability of fiat currencies, ongoing de-dollarization trends among major economies, high fiscal deficits in key nations, and a clear shift by major central banks towards more aggressive monetary easing.

Historically, these factors have led to increased demand for gold and silver as safe-haven assets.

In this speculative scenario, if favorable global conditions for hard assets continue, gold could potentially reach a price range of $8,000 to $12,000 per troy ounce, with a stretch target exceeding $15,000 by January 1, 2027.

However, it’s important to emphasize that this is purely speculation and should not be considered a realistic base-case outlook.

Achieving this price would require extraordinary coordination among various central banks and sovereign entities, which is currently highly unlikely.
