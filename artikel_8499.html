If your Medicare Advantage plan is ending, you have a limited-time right to buy a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policy without being turned down or charged more for your health. Use it by returning to Original Medicare instead of joining another Advantage plan, and apply before your old plan ends so your coverage has no gap.

Your plan has to mail you a non-renewal notice at least 90 days before it ends, so by early October. In July 2026, one of the largest Medicare Advantage insurers told investors its 2027 plan exits would affect about 600,000 members.

Outside your one-time Medigap open enrollment window, federal rules let insurers ask about your health, then turn you down or charge more. A plan exit flips that. If your Advantage plan leaves Medicare or your area, or you move out of its service area, insurers must sell you a policy, cover pre-existing conditions, and can’t charge more because of your health.

The right lasts until 63 days after your old plan ends, but applying late leaves a gap. Leaving Advantage by choice? Get approved for Medigap before you drop your plan.

Mutual of Omaha is a strong place to start. AM Best rates Mutual of Omaha Insurance Company A+ (Superior). Its online tool prices Plan G from your ZIP code, birth date, and sex, with no contact details required, and you can apply online. Couples leaving the same plan can each save with its household discount where it’s available, though the amount and rules vary by state.
