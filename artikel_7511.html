Maker’s Mark spotlights signature red wax top in visual refresh

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The brand evokes its heritage and craftsmanship with a new color palette, website and typography.

Maker’s Mark, a Suntory Global Spirits brand, pulled back the curtain on a new global brand identity this week that updates the brand’s color palette, photography and graphic elements with a more minimal, modern aesthetic that evokes the brand’s heritage. It is live in the U.S., China, Australia and the U.K., with other markets planned throughout 2027, according to a press release.

The new visual identity spotlights the bourbon’s iconic red wax. Custom typography is inspired by the handwriting of Margie Samuels, one of the co-founders of Maker’s Mark, who died in 1985 and was responsible for creating the brand name as well as its original bottle design. The bottle design remains unchanged, per press materials. Tactile elements included in the wax seal, such as wheat stalks and oak rings, were created using materials found at Star Hill Farm, one of the properties owned by the brand and the namesake of its Star Hill Farm Whisky.

"In a world increasingly driven by speed and automation, we still believe some things are worth making slowly, by hand," said Chloe Lloyd-Jones, vice president of global brand marketing at Maker's Mark, in press materials.

The brand last made changes to its visual identity in 2021, but they were not as extensive. The latest refresh was developed in partnership with Turner Duckworth London and is part of the “Perfectly Unreasonable” campaign.

The new design will appear across digital, social and retail channels, along with a new website. The overhaul is supported by in-person experiences in the U.S. and China, along with out-of-home activations in Australia and the U.K.

The refresh comes as the bourbon industry faces significant challenges, including slowing demand and a bloated inventory, which are driving down prices. Tariffs have also taken a toll on the industry, with exports of American whiskey, which includes bourbon, falling 19% in 2025. The industry’s issues are exacerbated by the general trend of Americans drinking less overall. A little over half of Americans said they drink, according to a recent Gallup poll, the lowest it’s been since the organization started measuring the trend in 1939.

Suntory Global Spirits ceased production at its flagship Jim Beam distillery in Clermont, Kentucky at the beginning of 2026. It recently announced it would continue to pause distilling operations until at least the end of next year. Maker’s Mark is distilled at a different location, which remains operational.

“Perfectly Unreasonable” launched in 2025 and is the first campaign for the brand by Carmichael Lynch. In December, Maker’s Mark entered into its first sports league sponsorship and became the first official spirits partner of the women’s basketball league Unrivaled.

Suntory Beverage & Food Limited, which is traded on the Tokyo Stock Exchange, saw its revenue increase 9.8% year-over-year for the nine months ending Sept. 30, 2026, according to financial results.
