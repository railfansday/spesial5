How to get the Craquerlure Hunter Mask in The Division 2: Echoes of Central Park

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Craquerlure Hunter Mask is available in The Division 2 as a secret item you can unearth from the Echoes of Central Park DLC. There are several steps to complete before you can get this mask.

Let’s find out how to add it to your collection.

This quest works around finding numerous candles spread all around the Central Park map.

The first candle is located just west of College Criminal Justice mission waypoint. You’ll want to do this quest at night; otherwise, you won’t find the lit candles. Once you’re at the spot, you’ll see several candles on top of a car.

Use the Wave emote to extinguish the candles.

Now, head south from the East Entrance. You’ll find the candles in the bushes, and you’ll have to use the Salute emote. This will put out the flames.

The third spot will be at the Bird’s Nest control point. If it’s already under your control, you can fast travel to the spot. Drop to the bottom floor, turn around, and you’ll find the candles in a corner.

Use the Exercise emote to extinguish the flame.

The fourth one is north of the Handle control point. Fast travel to the location and head to the marked spot to find the candles. You can snuff out the candles with the Request Help emote.

To find the next one, head to the marked spot in Dakota District. Look for a building with a giant Santa Claus on top of it. You can trace it using the festive lights. Go through the passageway below Santa, and look left to find the candles.

Use the Clap emote to extinguish the flames.

Go to the marked spot on the image above, and you’ll find a lit entrance. The easiest way to travel here is from the Dojo safehouse. Go in through the lit entrance, and you’ll find the final candle.

Use the Surrender emote to put out the candles. This will summon a few enemies that you need to kill to get the mask as a guaranteed loot drop.
