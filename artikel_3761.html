Complete Fortnite Kasane Teto Twindrill Diva music pass – All skins, songs, and other rewards

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A fresh music pass has landed in Fortnite, and like always, it features a new artist and an array of goodies to go with them. This one is called the Twindrill Diva music pass, and it’s all about Kasane Teto.

There are quite a few different passes you can acquire in Fortnite, and each one sets you back an additional fee. To help you figure out whether this one is worth adding to your collection, here’s a complete breakdown of the Twindrill Diva Kasane Teto music pass in Fortnite.

There are three pages worth of rewards you can unlock throughout the Kasane Teto-themed Twindrill Diva music pass in Fortnite. This version of the music pass is much shorter than the average one, but there’s still a decent amount to unlock.

Here’s a full breakdown of all rewards featured in the Kasane Teto Twindrill Diva pass.

As of the time of writing, the only way to get any Kasane Teto Outfit is through the music pass. You can purchase this pass to unlock her base Kasane Teto Outfit style, then fully work through it to earn the Kasane Teto (Reactive On) toggle for this look.

There will also likely be a Gold Kasane Teto Outfit available to unlock through Treasure Chests at some point during this season, as has been the case with all Captains who have arrived on the island. This will be the Treasure Chest item with the overall lowest loot odds and drop rates, though, so finding this skin will be incredibly tough.

There’s also a chance that some other Kasane Teto Outfit may be added to the item shop in the future. This is usually the case for most artists, so keep an eye out on the shop for an additional style that can be bought using V-Bucks.

The Kasane Teto Twindrill Diva music pass runs from Nov. 12, 2026 to Jan. 20, 2027. This means you have about two and a half months to decide whether you want to purchase and work through it.
