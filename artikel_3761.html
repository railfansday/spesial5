Ao Oni: The Horror of Blueberry Onsen Plus and Ao Oni 2 coming to PS5 on December 17

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Game Studio will release horror RPGs Ao Oni: The Horror of Blueberry Onsen Plus and Ao Oni 2 for PlayStation 5 on December 17, the developer announced.

Ao Oni: The Horror of Blueberry Onsen first launched for Switch on April 25, 2025, followed by PC via Steam on May 1, 2025. Ao Oni 2 launched for Switch and PC via Steam on August 6, 2026.

Here is an overview of each game, via Game Studio:

Challenge yourself while being chased by fear! A horror puzzle action adventure!

A Completely New Version of the Legendary Horror Game Ao Oni!

[What is the new “blue tragedy” unfolding at the abandoned hot spring?]

The once-popular hot spring inn known for its “beauty-enhancing hot spring” is now a ruin.

Hiroshi, Takeshi, Mika, and Takuro visit the abandoned hot spring inn to record “Mika Channel” and encounter a “suspicious man” and “Ai” there…

What is the Blueberry Hot Spring found in the abandoned inn, and what awaits them in its deepest depths…?

Can Hiroshi and the others escape safely?

There are many new elements such as conversations with characters, cooperative play with friends, and a chat function.

In addition, many new species of blue demons appear! There is even a monster in the form of a humanoid dog…?

The Fastest Game Ever! “16x Speed Mode” Has Been Added!

In “High Speed Mode” you can freely choose between 2x and 16x speed!

Can you escape from the Ao Oni chasing you at high speed?

Participate in online ranking by your score when you clear the game!

Play quickly and without mistakes, and aim for a high score!

Includes the Additional Scenario “Episode: The Untold” Where You Can Experience the Other Side of the “Blue Tragedy”!

Don’t miss the story behind the incident and a brand-new Ao Oni yet to be seen!

A double dose of blue terror in an abandoned, rain-slicked school.

The latest installment in the legendary horror franchise, Ao Oni!

Uncover the mystery while fleeing for your life! A pulse-pounding puzzle-horror action-adventure!

This time, the stage is set in a school, featuring two massive, star-studded scenarios!

Years ago, a school was abruptly shuttered, left to rot.

Looking for a thrill, Hiroshi, Takuro, Takeshi, and Mika break in for a test of courage—

only to blackout and wake up trapped inside a makeshift prison cell…

To their surprise, they are joined by their teacher, who had spotted them sneaking in and followed them inside…

Will they find a way to escape this desolate school alive?!

The Classic Speed Mode Returns With a Maddening New Option: “17x Speed”!

Customize your challenge by choosing any multiplier from 2x all the way up to 17x!

Push your reflexes to the absolute limit!

Brand-New Scenario: Ao Oni: Seaside School

Hiroshi and the gang head to the coast for their summer seaside school trip.

Driven by the classic summer urge for adventure after hearing rumors of a monster lurking in an abandoned school near their lodge, they decide to sneak inside the school after dark…

Mysterious silhouettes, school chimes echoing through empty hallways…

And a terrifying horde of never-before-seen, mutated monsters…

What is the dark truth behind the incident that once shook this school?

Separated from the group, Hiroshi begins his desperate search to find his friends.

Conquer four distinct stages in any order you choose!

The difficulty scales depending on the path you take.

Find your optimal route and turn the odds in your favor!

A Diverse Lineup of Unique Ao Oni Awaits!

Encounter a multitude of brand-new Ao Oni species!

Could some of these monstrosities even take the form of twisted sea creatures…?!
