Guide: Zelda: Ocarina Of Time Walkthrough (Switch 2 Remake): Guide To The Main Quest, Side Quests & Collectables

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Zelda: Ocarina of Time is a dense adventure with many secrets to find, and while the critical path is fairly linear, it's rarely straightforward - asking you to pick up select items and upgrades before you can start exploring its many dungeons.

That's why a Zelda: Ocarina of Time walkthrough comes in handy. Though the Switch 2 version follows the same beats as the N64 original, we'd estimate around 10% of the game's puzzles and locations have been changed or updated - just enough to throw off those very familiar with the 1998 version.

This page features both an abridged and thorough walkthrough covering the critical path, plus a repository of our other Zelda: Ocarina of Time guides, from optional side-quests to items and collectables.

If you want brief directions and as few spoilers as possible in terms of item unlocks and progression, then use the following abridged walkthrough to take you through the game. If you want extra specifics - including useful optional extras as you go - then check the following section.

For a more detailed walkthrough of the critical path, this section can explain specific steps on how to progress. If you want even more detailed step-by-step guides to the game's many dungeons, then follow the links to dedicated pages on each.

Before you can visit the Great Deku Tree, you must find a Sword and Shield, otherwise Mido won’t let you through. You can find the Kokori Sword in the Forest Training Center to the southwest (crawl through the vent to get there, then find it in the chest above), and the Deku Shield from the shop in the northeast by collecting Rupees found scattered around the village.

Visit the Deku Tree, complete the Great Deku Tree dungeon, and get the Kokiri’s Emerald key item and an instruction to head to Hyrule Castle. Now leave Kokiri Forest to the west to receive an Ocarina in a cutscene before leaving to Hyrule Field.

Head directly north along the path until you reach the castle drawbridge. The area inside is the Hyrule Castle Market - there’s lots of optional activities here, including the chance to get your first Bottle from the Potion Shop - and when ready, proceed to Hyrule Castle, where after getting the Weird Egg from Maron, you must make your way through the grounds to meet Zelda.

You'll now have Zelda’s Letter and learnt Zelda’s Lullaby on the Ocarina. The critical path is to Death Mountain via Kakariko Village, though you should visit the Lost Woods first (for Saria's Song, which you'll need shortly) and ideally, an optional visit to Lon Lon Ranch (for a Bottle and the first step in how to get Epona).

When ready, go east from Hyrule Field’s drawbridge and up the steps to Kakariko Village.

After finding all Cucco locations for a Bottle and visiting the Graveyard for the Sun’s Song (both optional), show Zelda’s Letter to the guard at the north exit. He gives you an optional errand - returning to the Hyrule Castle Market for a "proper shield" (the Hylian Shield) and also to start the Happy Mask Shop quest.

The aim is to reach Goron City. Hug the rightmost wall: this will take you up and round the corner, then back on yourself up the cliff. The path will now split: take the left branch to Goron City.

Inside Goron City, head to the lowest level, where on the north side is a closed door. Play Zelda’s Lullaby here to open it. Inside is Darunia. Talk to him, then play Saria’s Song (which you can get from the Lost Woods). This will see you unlock the Goron’s Bracelet, allowing you to use Bomb Flowers.

Now leave Goron City the way you came (the top entrance), then go directly right to see a Bomb Flower near a fence. Now you have the Goron's Bracelet, throw it over and you’ll bomb the entrance to Dodongo Cavern, allowing you to enter.

Once you have completed the Dodongo Cavern dungeon and picked Bombs and the Goron’s Ruby (the Spiritual Stone of Fire), feel free to scale Death Mountain for a Great Fairy Fountain location (unlocking Din's Fire) before heading off to Zora Domain.

Zora River - which leads to Zora Domain - is just east of Kakariko Village and Death Mountain on Hyrule Field.

Reach the end - the way there is fairly linear - and access Zora Domain by playing Zelda's Lullaby at the waterfall.

Inside, you must find the whereabouts of Princess Ruto by getting the Silver Scale in a mini-game at the top of the waterfall, then by visiting Lake Hylia via the underwater shortcut for a Bottle with a letter inside. (Our dedicated Zora Domain walkthrough features more specific directions for all these, by the way!)

Speak to King Zora, Bottle up a fish found in Zora Domain, then give it to the giant fish in Zora Fountain to access Lord Jabu-Jabu's Belly dungeon.

Once the dungeon is complete and the Spiritual Stone of Water is in your possession, head to Hyrule Castle. After the cutscene, dive in the moat and collect the Ocarina of Time, where you’ll then learn the Song of Time.

Head to the Temple of Time which branches off from Hyrule Castle Market. Inside, stand in front of the inscription at the far end, and play the Song of Time. Now go into the next room and retrieve the Master Sword.

After the cutscene, you’ll become Adult Link, and are told to head to Kakariko Village. Do so, then head to the Graveyard. On the left row of graves is a tombstone with flowers in front; push the stone and head down the hole. Now follow Dampe through the maze and at the end, you’ll receive the Hookshot.

To leave, play the Song of Time on the blocks, to emerge in the Windmill. Use this opportunity to get the Heart Piece opposite, then speak to the man to learn the Song of Storms - you’ll need this later.

With the Hookshot in hand, you can explore more of Hyrule, but when ready, head to Forest Temple: it can be found at the end of the Lost Woods, and inside the dungeon, you'll unlock the Bow and be rewarded with the Forest Medallion story item.

Complete the Forest Temple dungeon to unlock a Sage. At this point onward, upon returning to the Temple of Time, you can time travel back into Young Link by putting the Master Sword the pedestal. Also, to help, Sheik will teach you The Prelude of Light, which allows you to teleport back to the Temple of Time - a pretty handy shortcut.

As Adult Link, make your way to Death Mountain (it’s worth going into the Shooting Gallery in Kakariko Village now you have the Bow, which rewards you the Big Quiver to hold 40 Rupees as you go), then head into Goron City.

On the next level down is a Goron rolling around, and your aim is to detonate a Bomb near him so he stops. Once successful, the Goron will reveal their name is Link, son of Darunia, and will explain the Goron's plight.

Exhaust the dialogue options to receive the Goron Tunic - a red garb which allows you to withstand high temperatures - and directions to the Fire Temple, which is by heading to Darunia’s room (located on the bottom level at the back) and then by pulling the statue to reveal a route to the Death Mountain Crater.

Once in, go over the drawbridge using the Hookshot to meet Sheik, who will teach you Bolero of Fire. Now head through the left doorway, drop down and follow the passage to reach the dungeon, unlocking the Megaton Hammer and the Fire Medallion along the way.

Before you leave, it's worth going south across the bridge from the exit to Goron City in the Death Mountain Crater and using your Megaton Hammer on the cave entrance to get the Magic Meter upgrade from the Great Fairy Fountain within.

Head to the Ice Cavern, which is located in Zora Domain (or specifically, in Zora Fountain where you encountered Lord Jabu-Jabu as a child). Doing so will see you unlock the Iron Boots; make sure you grab a Blue Fire before leaving, and return to Zora Domain and use the Blue Fire on King Zora to free him and receive a Zora Tunic, which allows you to breathe underwater for an unlimited amount of time.

With both the Iron Boots and Zora Tunic in hand, we have everything we need to take on the Water Temple. Play the Serenade of Water to be teleported there when ready. Completing this will see you receive the Longshot - a Hookshot upgrade - and the Water Medallion.

Before we move onto the next temple, on this island is a plaque in front of you which hints at a puzzle involving the morning sun. If you are reading this as soon as the Water Temple has wrapped, the sun should still be rising: aim for the halo with your Bow and fire an Arrow, which will see it transform into a Fire Arrow on the platform ahead.

When you’re ready, head to Kakariko Village as Adult Link. When you arrive, a cutscene plays showing the place on fire, Sheik appearing, and a spirit emerges from the well. You’re then taught the Nocturne of Shadow, allowing you to travel to the Shadow Temple - though there’s a place to visit you need to visit first - the Kakariko well.

As it's currently blocked off as an adult, so you need to return as a child. If you haven’t already, learn Song of Storms from the Guru-Guru inside the windmill, then return to the windmill as Young Link and play Song of Storms back to him (now you have the Nocturne of Shadow, the return journey will be a lot quicker).

Doing so will cause the windmill to spin out of control and lower the water in the well. Drop down and complete the Bottom of the Well mini-dungeon to get the Lenses of Truth.

If you haven’t already, you also need Din’s Fire. This is acquired by visiting the Great Fairy Fountain location at the top of Death Mountain. Now you have Din’s Fire and the Lenses of Truth, play Nocturne of Shadow, then use Din’s Fire in the centre of the torches to gain access to the Shadow Temple, where you’ll get the Hover Boots along the way, and another Medallion.

With that done, it’s time to head to Gerudo Valley. It’s found in the west of Hyrule Field, and you need either the Longshot or Epona to make it over the bridge at the start.

Complete the Gerudo’s Fortress stealth section - essentially let yourself get captured, then find the four carpenters so you can explore freely - then make your way across the Mirage Desert's trials (you need the Longshot and the Lenses of Truth) to reach the Evil Desert Colossus area and the Spirit Temple. This unlocks two new items — the Silver Gauntlets and the Mirror Shield — and another Medallion.

The Gerudo Valley area also features two optional extras - a Great Fairy Fountain (Nayru's Love; Bomb the wall on the north wall of the Evil Desert Colossus) and within Gerudo’s Fortress, the Gerudo Training Ground (a mini-dungeon which grants the Ice Arrow.

Assuming you have now awakened every Sage and received every Medallion, it’s now time to head to the Temple of Time and start the final stretch of the game.

A cutscene will play and you’ll receive the Light Arrow, and an instruction to head to Ganon’s Castle.

Approach the lava moat around the castle to form a bridge with the help of the Sages. The dungeon within is composed of six ‘routes’ from a central hub; though you can tackle them in broadly any order, go for the Shadow Barrier to unlock the Golden Gauntlets first, which is required for completing every puzzle.

Once that - and a brief trip to the Great Fairy Fountain just outside the castle beyond the bridge for a useful defensive upgrade - it’s time to scale the tower, or to halt your progress so you can mop up any remaining side-quests and collectables (see the end of this guide for a list) before the finale.

The tower within Ganondorf’s Castle is essentially a short gauntlet of battles until you reach Ganon himself at the top, and from there, it’s a linear stretch until the end credits. Good luck!
