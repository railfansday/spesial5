Metaphor: ReFantazio Announcement Officially Coming Tomorrow

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Studio Zero, the team behind the wonderful Metaphor: ReFantazio, is teasing an announcement for the game tomorrow, although fans are very wary.

It’s a little hard to believe, but it’s been more than two years since we were blessed with Metaphor: ReFantazio, which was easily one of the frontrunners for 2024’s game of the year. In a surprise move, considering it comes from Atlus (well, from an internal team at the studio, but you know what I mean), Metaphor hasn’t had any kind of major post-launch content.

Sure, we’ve had plenty of smaller DLC packs and bonuses, but Metaphor has somehow avoided an improved re-release or even just a big story expansion. Fans have been hopeful that Atlus might finally confirm something is in the works for the game soon, which has made a recent tease very enticing, even if most are sticking to being sceptical.

Earlier today, the official Studio Zero Twitter account suddenly shared a rather interesting post, which started with laughter before saying, “Tomorrow, I have an announcement for you all”. The tooth emoji at the end of the post and the image of a Homo Tenta make it clear the post is coming from their perspective.

Don't mind me, I'll just be over here starting the fan club for the Magnus brothers.

While that fact alone should probably tell you that this isn’t going to be a major announcement, fans are going wild in the comments and praying that we’re finally getting a story DLC. Others are expecting a Persona 5 Royal style re-release, which would make some sense considering it’s been two years since the original launch. I don't think that would be dropped on a random Sunday, though.

Others are hopeful that the rocky Switch 2 port could finally be getting fixed, although teasing that as an announcement is a bit tone deaf.

Of course, this is Atlus we’re talking about, and as the Persona 30th anniversary has taught us, there’s a very good chance that whatever’s being teased here is actually just merchandise. Metaphor already has a ton of merch, but the Homo Tenta getting its own statue or diorama would make some sense considering how the post is worded.

With all of that in mind, you should probably be careful not to get too excited about whatever announcement is being hinted at here. What’s coming could be grand and incredible like an expansion or re-release, but it could also be a literal toothbrush based on Homo Tenta, so take it all with a pinch of salt.
