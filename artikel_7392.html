Bare Funeral Group Pty Ltd: Customer Care <> Pet End of Life Arranger

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

At Goodbye Good Boy, we provide compassionate end of life services, supporting veterinary clinics and the families they care for during one of life’s most difficult moments.

Having experienced firsthand how overwhelming pet loss can be, we built a service focused on support, simplicity, and genuine care. Backed by more than 1,000 five-star reviews we are making a real difference and we want like minded people to join us on the journey.

We’re looking for an empathetic, tech-savvy communicator with exceptional attention to detail to join our customer care team. You’ll guide and support families through one of their most vulnerable times. It’s no stretch to say this is one of the most important roles in the company.

This is a phone-based role, helping families arrange in-home euthanasia and pet cremation. You’ll take the time to listen, show them you care, and coordinate and communicate every step of the process.

That makes sense, but what would my day to day look like?

Families come to us looking for guidance, empathy and support as they arrange their pet’s end-of-life care. Each day, you’ll be:

Answering a high volume of inbound calls.

Offering thoughtful guidance as families consider the right time to say goodbye.

Being a trusted guide throughout the arrangement process.

Navigating multiple online systems with speed and accuracy.

Liaising with our veterinary team to find available appointment times.

Coordinating with our crematorium team and external partners to arrange services.

Supporting multiple families at once, following a structured workflow so every family receives the same outstanding care. The ability to multitask is a must!

You’ll report to our Customer Support Team Leader and work alongside a team of amazing, like-minded people.

OK, sounds great. Who are you looking for?

This is a hard but incredibly impactful role. Families come to us in all kinds of circumstances, trusting us to guide them through a difficult part of their lives.

A genuine desire to offer empathy and support to pet parents from all walks of life.

Confidence guiding people who need thoughtful, non-judgemental support.

The ability to work in a fast-paced environment.

Comfort using technology and following processes.

The ability to take the lead in a conversation and build trust.

Most importantly, we want someone who believes in our mission. Everyone at Goodbye Good Boy is driven by the impact of our work, and we want that to continue with every person who joins our team.

We operate seven days a week, supporting families from 7am–8:30pm Monday to Saturday and 8am–6pm Sunday (AEST).

You must be available for at least four eight-hour shifts per week, including Saturdays, plus a rotating Sunday shift at least one in every three weekends. Ideally available for 30 hours per week.

We’re a passionate team on a mission to bring positive change to the pet end-of-life industry. We’re incredibly proud of our work, celebrate our many milestones, and take immense pride in the difference we make for the families we have the privilege to serve.
