Ads of the Week: 11 Campaigns That Caught Our Eye, From Calvin Klein to Dunkin’

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The holiday ads are falling like snowflakes, and brands are wheeling out the big celebrities. Selena Gomez and Tate McRae star in a spot for Ulta Beauty; Channing Tatum reprises his role as Marvel’s Gambit for Dunkin’; Alan Cumming narrates an animated spot for e.l.f.; and Jude Bellingham strips down for Calvin Klein.

And Apple is celebrating a different holiday, with a beautifully choreographed Diwali film, shot on iPhone.

Elsewhere, Levi’s introduces us to Gus the yeti, Canva shows how its graphic design tools can be used in everyday life, and Visit Iceland sits in on a meeting so you don’t have to.

Matt Barker is a contributing writer for ADWEEK. He has previously written for Campaign, Marketing Week, and Condé Nast.
