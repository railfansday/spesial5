Few Apple releases in recent years have generated the kind of buzz that bestowed the iPhone Duo when it was first announced in September, during the tech giant’s annual “Surprise and Shine” product launch event.

The first-ever foldable iPhone, the Duo became an instant must-have for tech enthusiasts and everyday consumers alike, driving pre-order demand akin to a new Taylor Swift album release or BTS tickets. The iPhone Duo officially dropped on Oct. 23 and quickly sold out across the country, even at its $2,000 price point. But there’s a way to lock in an iPhone Duo for yourself right now and at a discount, thanks to a new T-Mobile offer online. Right now, T-Mobile members can get up to $1,200 off a brand new iPhone Duo when you activate a new line or trade-in an eligible device.

Even better: you can get the Phone Duo for $1,200 off with $0 out of pocket and nothing due today: no money down, no taxes and no fees.

The deal is open to both new and existing T-Mobile members who purchase an iPhone Duo and sign up for one of T-Mobile’s new “T-Mix” plans. Just unveiled this month, the plans let you choose the features and benefits you want, so you don’t overpay. Every plan comes with unlimited talk and text, with a different number of “picks” that you can redeem for more data, streaming subscriptions (I.e. ESPN Unlimited, YouTube Premium or Netflix) or device deals.

T-Mix plans start at just $55/month for the “Standard” plan with 1-4 picks included. The “Loaded” plan at $95 adds on 250GB of high-speed hotspotting data plus up to 21 picks.

Don’t want to sign up for a new plan? You can also claim the $1,200 iPhone Duo discount by trading in a phone that’s part of T-Mobile’s list of eligible devices, which includes iPhone 15, 16 and 17 models, along with select Samsung Galaxy Ultra, Edge, Flip and Fold models (the $1,200 will be applied as monthly bill credits up to 36 months).

T-Mobile has the iPhone Duo available in “night sky” and “star white” with storage options ranging from 256GB to 2TB. Both phones are available to reserve now at T-Mobile.com.

The iPhone Duo is Apple’s introduction into the foldables category, with a 7.6-inch screen when opened, offering a larger display for viewing content, gaming and working across apps. The smartphone measures just 5.4 inches when closed, making it a true, pocket-sized device.
