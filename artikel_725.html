Teddy Swims’ ‘Mr. Know It All’ Hits No. 1 on Billboard Pop Airplay Chart

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The singer-songwriter scores his second leader at the format, after "Lose Control" in 2024.

Teddy Swims earns his second No. 1 on Billboard’s Pop Airplay chart as “Mr. Know It All” ascends two spots to the top of the Nov. 14-dated ranking.

Released on SWIMS Int./Warner Records, the song becomes the singer-songwriter’s second leader at the format, after “Lose Control” reigned for two weeks in April 2024.

The Pop Airplay chart measures songs’ weekly plays, as tabulated by Mediabase and provided to Billboard by Luminate, on nearly 150 U.S. mainstream top 40 radio stations.

Swims has also hit the Pop Airplay top 10 with “Bad Dreams,” which reached No. 8 in April 2025, and “The Door” (No. 9, November 2024).

On the all-genre, multimetric Billboard Hot 100, “Mr. Know It All,” which Swims cowrote, has hit a No. 26 high, likewise becoming Swims’ fourth top 40 hit, after “Bad Dreams” (No. 30), “The Door” (No. 24) and “Lose Control” (No. 1 for one week). “Lose Control” went on to rule as the No. 1 song on the 2024 year-end Hot 100.

“I feel like every time I meet one, I’m grateful, but I’m never satisfied,” Swims told Billboard News’ Tetris Kelly earlier this year of how he assesses goals. “The human eye sees, like, 20 miles ahead, right? So soon as you get that 20 miles, you’re seeing 20 more miles and you’re, like, there’s still much further to go. So I always try to celebrate the wins, but I want to be the best. I try to just be where my feet are and know that this is where I’m supposed to be.”

Added the father of a now-16-month-old, “I just also want my son to think I’m cool.”

All charts dated Nov. 14 will update Tuesday, Nov. 10, on Billboard.com.
