Robert Pattinson Manifested His James Bond Casting 14 Years Ago

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The actor will be the next 007, taking over the iconic role from Daniel Craig, who concluded his run with 2021’s 'No Time to Die.'

Even if it wasn’t intentional, it seems Robert Pattinson manifested his James Bond casting 14 years ago.

In 2012, during the height of his Twilight fame, the actor was asked in an interview with the U.K. online publication Shortlist if he could be playing 007 in 10 years’ time.

“Yeah definitely,” he responded, “but more like 20 years.”

Pattinson added, “I think there’d be nothing worse than, like, ‘Let’s get a fresh-faced Bond!’ That would be the worst idea in the world. After Daniel Craig, you have to have some baggage. It would be ridiculous to reinvent [him] as some young posh kid. Or, if [he] was, I’d have to get so tortured in the first few scenes. I’d have to do the first film with one arm or something.” Related Stories Movies James Bond Found: Robert Pattinson to Play 007 Movies Matt Damon Reveals Ben Affleck's Review of 'The Odyssey' -- and the One Performance He Couldn't Stop Talking About

Around the time of the interview, the Primetime actor was preparing for the release of The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 2 later that year, and Craig was gearing up for the release of his third 007 movie, Skyfall.

In an earlier interview with BBC News in 2010, Pattinson also acknowledged that taking on cinema’s most famous secret agent one day would be “quite big shoes to fill.” He also quipped that if he took over the role right then, he’d be “probably pretty bad.”

After 16 more years of acting experience and becoming one of Hollywood’s most in-demand actors (Pattinson stars in four films just this year alone), he clearly wasn’t “bad” in his Bond audition. The Hollywood Reporter previously reported that Pattinson was among the finalists being considered for the role, in addition to Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, Josh O’Connor and Nicholas Hoult.

Director Denis Villeneuve and producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman ultimately decided Pattinson was perfect as the next 007, taking over the iconic role from Craig, who concluded his run with 2021’s No Time to Die.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime,” Pattinson said in a statement about his casting. “I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”
