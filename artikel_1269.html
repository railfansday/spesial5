Report Fabregas was targeted by Manchester United before Pochettino decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Como coach Cesc Fabregas was discussed by Manchester United before they reached an agreement with Mauricio Pochettino, claim The Athletic, after crushing the team 4-1 in the Champions League.

The Premier League side began making genuine plans to sack Michael Carrick after that humiliating result at the Stadio Sinigaglia last month, although they waited until the break for international duty to pull the plug.

They have paid circa £9m to buy Pochettino and his staff out of their contracts with the USMNT, announcing their new coach.

According to The Athletic, Pochettino was by no means top of the wish list when Manchester United directors began parsing the options to replace Carrick.

It is no secret that Unai Emery of Aston Villa was one of their first targets, but the 55-year-old was not interested in leaving the current project when signed on until 2029.

Another potential candidate was current Brighton and Hove Albion boss Fabian Hurzeler, but the price quoted to them for breaking that contract would’ve been £15m.

It is reported that Fabregas was discussed, but that it never even got so far as a formal approach to the Spaniard or Como.

This would’ve been ill-fated, because Inter had already tried to poach Fabregas last summer and were rejected outright by the club’s rich Indonesian owner Mirwan Suwarso.

The idea of letting the coach leave mid-season would’ve been even more improbable, especially just weeks after beating this Manchester United team in the Champions League, then crushing RC Lens 9-1.
