GTA 6 Has A Surprising Late Game Cameo That Knocked Me On My Ass

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Across social media and YouTube, a massive spoiler from the final chapter of Grand Theft Auto 6 has been spreading online. It is a cameo by a past character that has people going wild. Some fans who have sped through the game’s story are rushing to be the first to share it online. Others don’t believe it’s even real. I’m here to confirm that, yes, the wild cameo you saw is real, and it made me scream.

This is your last warning. The next paragraphs are going to completely reveal the spoiler. If you are a massive GTA nerd and/or love surprises, stop reading now and close this article. Seriously, you’ll thank me later. For those who don’t care about spoilers, GTA lore, or who were never planning to play the game, let’s dive into one of the biggest surprises hidden within the massive world of GTA 6. Last chance to turn away. See you on the other side.

Near the tail end of GTA 6‘s big, epic crime story, Jason and Lucia begin working with Giorgio, a wealthy shipping magnate. He’s been working with some cartels and Eastern European criminals and has been letting them use his ships to smuggle cocaine across the ocean. It’s been working out pretty well for a while, but by the time Jason and Lucia show up, the Coast Guard has seized all the drugs from a recent shipment. He has a plan to rob the Coast Guard, get his coke back, and make everyone happy before they come to his big penthouse and kill him. But then, just as his big heist is coming together, a higher-up from the Balkan criminal family he’s been working with shows up to put a bullet in his brain.

And the person sent to Vice City is none other than Grand Theft Auto IV protagonist Niko Bellic. Yup. As shared online by many players already, a much older Niko shows up in two cutscenes to threaten Giorgio and then to collect the drugs once the heist works out. Here are those cutscenes:

I will admit that before he showed up, when I heard Giorgio yell out, “Daddy’s back bitches!” I chuckled to myself and thought, “Oh nice, a reference to the opening line of GTA IV. Cute.” I had no idea that would be followed up by the reveal of fucking Niko Bellic. I might have let out a scream and paused the game in pure shock and amazement.

We’ve not heard much about Niko since the ending of GTA IV. So seeing him back in the flesh, with grey hair, was great. And I love that Rockstar got back the original voice actor, Michael Hollick. And yes, that’s why in the credits of GTA 6 posted online by Rockstar, he is simply labeled “The Hitman.”

I can’t wait for more GTA fans and nerds to discover this moment and share their reactions. I’ll have more to share about the cameo and how it came about soon from my interview with Rockstar Games head of production Rob Nelson. But for now, let’s just enjoy the fact that Niko is back, baby.
