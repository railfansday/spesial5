I spend a lot of time looking at fashion trends for my job, but there’s one thing I always find myself coming back to: what cool NYC girls are wearing. Their outfits have a way of making even the most unexpected shoe styles look ridiculously chic, and this fall-to-winter season is no exception. In fact, the latest footwear trends have me seriously reconsidering some of the shoes I’ve been reaching for on repeat.

Some styles I’m noticing include low-profile loafers that feel sophisticated, extra-tall boots that make legs look longer and statement ballet flats with enough personality to steal the show. I rounded up 13 of my favorite Amazon finds that capture the cool-girl aesthetic without sacrificing everyday wearability. Whether you’re dressing for work, heading out to dinner or refreshing your weekend wardrobe, these shoes make getting dressed a whole lot more exciting.

1. My Favorite: Chunky loafers can sometimes make an outfit feel bottom-heavy, which is why I’m loving the shift toward slimmer, more streamlined styles. These sleek suede loafer mules offer a more refined alternative, thanks to the expensive-looking suede material and a backless design for easy wear. The cushioned footbed is another bonus, giving you a little extra support for everything from coffee runs to casual office days.

2. A Little Lift: Who says you have to choose between the polish of loafers and the extra height of heels? These suede penny loafers give you the best of both worlds with a sleek, streamlined silhouette and a low-block heel that creates the illusion of longer, slimmer legs. Plus, the neutral taupe shade makes them easy to style with everything already hanging in your closet.

3. Designer Details: I’m a firm believer that the right details can make an affordable pair of shoes look significantly more expensive. These chic backless loafer mules prove my point with their gleaming gold chain, streamlined silhouette and creamy bone finish that feels especially sophisticated for fall and winter. Better yet, the low heel and slip-on design make them a practical alternative to dressy flats when you want something polished but easy to wear.

4. Everyday Essential: Traditional penny loafers can sometimes feel a little too preppy, but this slim, ruched-toe version has a much cooler, contemporary look. These sleek shoes feature a low-profile sole that won’t add unnecessary bulk, while the cushioned insole makes them an easy choice for long workdays or busy weekends. With colors ranging from classic black and chocolate brown to navy blue and statement red, there’s a shade for practically every personal style.

5. My Favorite: As someone who’s 5’3″, I’m especially excited about the extra-tall boot trend because the right pair can make my legs look so much longer! This chocolate brown style has a sleek, fitted shaft that creates a streamlined silhouette, while the pointed toe and block heel add even more leg-lengthening appeal. Plus, the rich finish makes them look incredibly expensive.

6. Kitten-Heel Cuties: I love tall boots that make my legs look longer, but I’m not willing to sacrifice comfort for sky-high heels! These suede knee-high boots offer the perfect compromise with a sleek shaft and dainty kitten heel that’s easier to walk in throughout the day. The pointed toe creates a flattering appearance, while the neutral shade pairs beautifully with everything from sweater dresses to dark-wash denim.

7. The Power Boot: Why settle for ordinary ankle boots when a tall silhouette can make your entire outfit look more polished? These black heeled boots feature a sleek leather-looking finish, modern square toe and sturdy block heel that offers a more stable base than a skinny stiletto.

8. My Favorite: I’ve never been much of a ballet flats person, but the edgy, statement-making styles taking over NYC have me reconsidering. These studded ballet flats put a cool-girl twist on the traditionally feminine shoe, featuring shiny metallic embellishments and a rich suede-like finish that looks expensive. Plus, they’re an easy way to make a simple jeans-and-sweater outfit feel more fashionable.

9. Buckle Up: Sometimes all it takes is one unexpected detail to turn an everyday pair of flats into something worth talking about. These pointed-toe slingbacks feature an oversized buckle that gives them a luxe-looking finish, while the adjustable strap helps prevent annoying slipping as you walk. I especially love how the pointed toe looks with wide-leg trousers, giving you a chic alternative to pumps for work or dinner plans.

10. Texture Talk: Every time I look at NYC street style lately, I swear another pair of woven ballet flats catches my eye. These woven ballet flats nail the trend with a rich basketweave texture, delicate bow and Mary Jane strap that feels more interesting than traditional flats. The elastic strap also helps keep them in place, so you can wear them all day.

11. My Favorite: Chunky sneakers have had their moment, but I’m much more excited about the slim, ballet-inspired styles that are taking over. These Adidas Mary Jane sneakers give the best of all worlds with a sporty rubber outsole, elastic straps and a sleek shape. I especially love the red-and-white color combination, which adds personality to outfits without sacrificing the practicality of everyday sneakers.

12. Balletcore Beauty: If you love the feminine look of ballet flats but prefer the coverage and practicality of sneakers, this trend might be your new favorite. These ivory ribbon-lace sneakers have a sleek, low-profile shape that won’t overwhelm your outfit, while the delicate lace trim makes them feel special enough to wear with skirts and dresses. The cushioned insoles and wide-width options are another bonus, giving you more ways to find a comfortable fit.

13. New and Notable: I probably don’t need another pair of sneakers, but this new Amazon arrival is making me think otherwise!
