Super League reveals new format, seven-team play-offs, international games

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Super League clubs voted to expand competition to 15 teams for 2027 season; London Broncos confirmed as club to take that place; New format revealed, with play-offs now involving seven clubs; International games in Las Vegas, Dublin; dates for Magic Weekend, Rivals Round revealed

Super League has revealed a new format, a seven-team play-off system and Magic Weekend and Rivals Round dates, on the same day London Broncos were confirmed as the competition's 15th team for 2027.

Earlier this month, Super League announced its 14 clubs had voted in favour of a motion to expand to 15 teams for the 2027 season.

Clubs were then invited to apply, with London Broncos - who had made no secret of their desire to be in the top flight - one of those to do so having completed the treble in 2026 during a brilliant season for them in the Championship.

With 15 teams competing, the 2027 Super League fixture schedule will take on a new format with the season starting on Thursday January 28.

Each club will have 13 opportunities to play in front of their home supporters, alongside 13 away fixtures, with 14 clubs competing in the Magic Weekend.

The structure means each team will face every other club at least once, while there will be one different opponent that each club does not host at home and travel to during the regular season.

The expanded competition will see every club receive two bye rounds during the regular season, while London Broncos will receive an additional bye during Magic Weekend.

Rivals Round will return over the Easter weekend (March 25), bringing some of the biggest rivalries in sport to the forefront of the Super League calendar.

Magic Weekend will remain a key feature of the Super League calendar in 2027, returning to Everton FC's home, Hill Dickinson Stadium, in Liverpool on Saturday May 1 and Sunday May 2 (Bank Holiday weekend).

Under the new play-off format, the League Leaders will earn an automatic home place in the semi-finals, while the remaining six teams will compete in the Eliminators for the opportunity to move one step closer to Old Trafford.

As a result, the runners-up will no longer progress automatically to the semi-finals.

The revised system will create a new dimension to the race for the Super League Grand Final, with seven clubs remaining in contention for the ultimate prize.

The 2027 season will see Super League continue to take some of its biggest games to international audiences, with major events confirmed in Las Vegas and Dublin.

Bradford Bulls and Leigh Leopards will meet at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas on Saturday February 27, as Super League returns to the United States.

The competition will then make history in April, with the first-ever Super League fixtures played in Ireland.

Warrington Wolves and Wigan Warriors face-off at Laya Arena in Dublin on Saturday, April 24, before St Helens and Hull FC meet at the same venue on Sunday, April 25.

The Dublin double-header brings four Super League clubs to the Irish capital for a landmark weekend, providing supporters with a unique opportunity to experience the competition in a new setting.-

Magic Weekend fixtures will be announced on Wednesday December 2, while the full 2027 Super League fixtures will be revealed on Thursday December 3.
