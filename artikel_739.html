No Rodgers, no problem! Steelers shock Broncos after dramatic week

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Pittsburgh Steelers stun the Denver Broncos 27-5 in their first game since releasing four-time MVP quarterback Aaron Rodgers following his remarkable comments regarding head coach Mike McCarthy; Rodgers had accused McCarthy of falling asleep in meetings

It had been a week clouded by Aaron Rodgers and his explosive claims regarding head coach Mike McCarthy, but the Pittsburgh Steelers swatted at the storm to claim a shock 27-5 win over the Super Bowl-contending Denver Broncos on Black Friday.

Broncos quarterback Bo Nix endured a miserable afternoon as he surrendered two interceptions and a lost fumble that resulted in two defensive touchdowns for the Steelers.

Denver trailed 24-5 after a dire first half in which they were limited to just 58 yards of offense, becoming just the third team since 1991 to post fewer than 60 yards while conceding two defensive touchdowns over the first two quarters of a game.

TJ Watt scored with an end zone fumble recovery, Jalen Ramsey returned a Nix interception to the house and D.K. Metcalf recorded a touchdown catch before appearing to celebrate by holding up eight fingers in an apparent support of Rodgers.

The Broncos looked anything but a contender as they slumped to 8-3, while the Steelers distanced themselves from a reflection of turmoil to improve to 7-4 in their push for the playoffs.

The Rodgers saga had lingered as an unwelcome backdrop heading into the game after the four-time MVP quarterback was released this week in the wake of his sensational appearance on the Pat McAfee Show.

Rodgers had claimed that head coach McCarthy fell asleep in team meetings, rebuffed suggestions to connect better with Pittsburgh's defensive players and accidentally shouted "one, two, three, Packers!" at the end of a team talk against the Philadelphia Eagles, the latter of which McCarthy would admit as an embarrassing moment.

Rodgers was subsequently cut amid what is set to mark the final season of his NFL career, though the storyline resurfaced during the game as broadcaster Al Michaels read a supposed text from the 42-year-old in which he is said to have claimed the Steelers had "barred" him from the team facility after he was benched for Mason Rudolph.

Rudolph started in place of Rodgers for the first time since the incident as he went nine of 15 passing for 142 yards and a touchdown, while Rico Dowdle and Jaylen Warren combined for 117 yards on the ground.

Reflecting on their win after such an eventful week, Rudolph said: "We just sort of rallied around Coach McCarthy, and I think in a weird way, the adversity brought us brought us closer together.

"It was a lot. Sort of unprecedented - certainly like nothing I've ever been through, and probably the same for all of my team-mates.

"I'm just so proud of the way we turned the page from [Aaron Rodgers' release on] Wednesday, and the veteran leadership - the Cam Haywards, the Jalen Ramseys, the TJ Watts - they really led us forward."

It took until 9.23 remaining in the third quarter for the first first down of the second half to arrive when the struggling Nix connected with Jaylen Waddle for 22 yards.

Still, it would come up empty amid a sequence of five successive punts between the teams, until Denver turned the ball over when Zach Allen forced a fumble from Dowdle that was recovered by Riley Moss with 3.31 on the clock.

Such was the story of their night that Denver failed to capitalise as their resulting drive ended in Wil Lutz missing a 53-yard field goal at the beginning of the fourth quarter.

Rudolph followed up by launching a downfield strike to Roman Wilson for 37 yards to set up Chris Boswell's 33-yard field goal for a 27-5 lead with 11.16 to play.

Nix's turnover issues resumed when he was intercepted by Daylen Everette at Pittsburgh's seven-yard line to ultimately end the game.

For so long defense has been the heartbeat of the Steelers operation, and it leapt to the occasion in the opening exchanges by way of TJ Watt as their stalwart edge rusher downed Nix's finger-slip fumble in the end zone for a deadlock-breaking touchdown in the first quarter.

Pittsburgh then forced a swift three-and-out to pave the way for Rudolph's 22-yard touchdown strike to Metcalf, who did outstandingly to haul in a back-pedalling catch while facing man coverage from star corner Pat Surtain II.

Denver turned to their own defense as a potential route back into the game when Alex Singleton stuffed Warren in the end zone for a safety, before Lutz and Boswell exchanged a pair of 44 and 47-yard field goals.

The Steelers then stretched their lead with a second defensive first-half touchdown when Ramsey turned a Nix interception into a 36-yard slaloming pick-six with 1.33 to play.
