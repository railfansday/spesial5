Kids’ Choice Awards: ‘Spider-Man: Brand New Day,’ Zendaya, Tom Holland, BTS Among 2026 Winners

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The show, hosted by Alex Warren, aired simultaneously on CBS and Nickelodeon for the first time.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day and its stars Tom Holland and Zendaya, along with BTS, were among the big winners Saturday night at the Kids’ Choice Awards.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day was named favorite movie, while Holland and Zendaya were named favorite movie actor and actress, respectively.

BTS scooped up three orange blimp statuettes: favorite music group or duo, favorite song for “Swim” and favorite album for Arirang.
