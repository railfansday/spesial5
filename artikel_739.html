Jodie Sweetin's Daughter Zoie, 18, Is Her Twin in Rare Photo

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Jodie Sweetin shared a rare photo posing alongside her and ex Cody Herpin’s lookalike daughter as they enjoyed a girls’ night in Los Angeles: “Had the best time out with Zoie!”

A closer look inside Jodie Sweetin’s bond with her kiddos? You got it, dude.

The Full House alum shared a rare photo of her and ex Cody Herpin’s 18-year-old daughter Zoie enjoying a festive girls’ night out together—and they couldn’t cut it out with their uncanny resemblance if they tried.

“Had the best time out with Zoie!” Jodie captioned a Nov. 23 Instagram post featuring a selfie of the mother-daughter duo. “Nothing better than getting dressed up and spending a fun night together.”

In the photo, Jodie, 44, gave the camera a wide smile as her daughter leaned in close by her side with her tongue sticking out during the pair’s outing at the Westfield Century City mall’s holiday village in Los Angeles.

And while the adorable snap will certainly fill the hearts of Full House fans everywhere, it’ll also have them saying “Oh, Mylanta!” After all, Zoie was practically her mom’s twin as she rocked a soft glam look for the occasion.

And it’s safe to say the teen had just as much fun as the Fuller House star. As she commented under the post, “Love u mama.”

Recently, Jodie—also mom to daughter Beatrix, 16, with ex Morty Coyle—shared insight into Zoie’s future plans.

“She wasn’t quite sure what she wanted to do, and she had an opportunity to play soccer somewhere at community college and get a scholarship, so she was like, ‘Yeah, let me like save some student debt and all of that while I’m figuring out what I want to do,’” Jodie said during her July 15 appearance for The Dory Jackson Interview. “I’m so proud of her because this was absolutely, I think, the right next step for her.”

Sweetin continued, “I just wanted her to feel like she was in control of what comes next. She’s like putting the pieces together. She can’t put the dishes in the dishwasher, but she’s putting the pieces of her life together, so I’ll take the small wins where I can get them.”

As for whether she’s ready to be an empty nester with Beatrix not too far from adulthood?

“I don't think it'll ever be empty,” Jodie—who married husband Mescal Wasilewski in 2022—explained. “Do we ever empty the nest these days? You know, hardly. At some point, my husband and I would like to move out of LA and that will obviously come once they're done with school and all that kind of stuff. I'd still want a place here so they can fight over who gets to stay in mom's condo or something.”

Even so, how rude it would be to assume she’d stifle her daughters’ independence.

“I'm not one of those parents who's been really afraid of their kids growing up,” she said. “If anything, I have enjoyed watching who they have become.”
