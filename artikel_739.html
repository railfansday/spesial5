Taylor Swift Returns to CMA Awards Stage for Star-Studded ‘I Knew It, I Knew You’ Performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Brad Paisley, Lainey Wilson and more appeared in the sweet set featuring a hint of "Our Song" and nods to Toy Story 5.

She came back when it mattered. Taylor Swift took the CMA Awards stage again for the first time in 13 years — her favorite number, natch — when she performed “I Knew It, I Knew You” at the 60th annual ceremony held at the Bridgestone Arena in Nashville on Wednesday (Nov. 18).

The star-studded performance kicked off with Swift crooning the opening lines of the Toy Story 5 hit as she made her way around backstage, with Brad Paisley dusting off her numerous trophies, Brooks & Dunn gambling, host Lainey Wilson handing the singer a pair of sunglasses, with additional cameos by Eric Church and Little Big Town.

Eventually, as Swift — sparkling in her Jessie-inspired cowgirl outfit — stepped out onto the stage inspired by Toy Story 5, for which she penned the Billboard Hot 100 hit, she strolled by a banjo-strumming Keith Urban as Bill Anderson handed her her guitar, which was sparkling silver along the edges with a baby blue front featuring glittery clouds. During the performance, Swift also mixed in a bit of “Our Song,” the Hot 100 No. 16 hit off her 2006 debut album, before going back to the Toy Story track.

As the performance wound down, Swift walked to the tree behind her, which featured the words “Jessie was here” carved into the trunk, and sat down for the post chorus.

Going into the evening, the country turned pop superstar had nine previous CMA Awards. Her first win was 2007’s horizon award, which was later renamed to new artist of the year. The next year, she was victorious in the entertainer of the year category, becoming the youngest artist to win the honor. At this year’s ceremony, “I Knew It, I Knew You” was nominated for single of the year, marking Swift’s 26th career CMA nod. (The trophy went to Ella Langley’s megahit, “Choosin’ Texas.”)

This year’s ceremony was hosted by Wilson, the third year helming the show for the star. Ella Langley, who exploded this year with the popularity of her Hot 100 record-setting hit “Choosin’ Texas,” led in nominations with nine nominations. Just behind her was Miranda Lambert, who had seven. Kacey Musgraves and Ben West followed in third with five each.

The 2026 CMA Awards aired live on ABC and Disney+, and streams on Hulu the following day.
