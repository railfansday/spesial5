Named 'Maria,' a new species of amphibian pays tribute to the world's most popular name

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Maria, the most popular female name in Brazil and around the world, was chosen for a new species of caecilian, a type of amphibian commonly known as a "blind snake," found in this South American country. The researchers chose the name Siphonops maria to raise awareness of this little-known animal, even among researchers in the field.
