Mynd.ai Announces Change to American Depositary Share Ratio

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

ALPHARETTA, Ga., Nov. 6, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Mynd.ai, Inc. (NYSE: MYND) ("Mynd" or the "Company"), a global provider of interactive technology and AI solutions for education and commercial customers, today announced that it will change the ratio of its American Depositary Shares ("ADSs") to its ordinary shares from one ADS representing ten (10) ordinary shares to a new ratio of one ADS representing seventy-five (75) ordinary shares. For the Company's ADS holders, the change in the ADS ratio will have the same effect as a one-for-7.5 reverse ADS split.

The ADS ratio change is expected to become effective on or about December 7, 2027 (U.S. Eastern Time), subject to completion of the applicable procedures with the Company's depositary, Citibank, N.A. (the "Depositary"), and other relevant parties. The ADSs will continue to trade on the NYSE American under the symbol "MYND," with a new CUSIP number of 628988206.

Following the effectiveness of the change, each ADS will represent seventy-five (75) ordinary shares of the Company, compared with ten (10) ordinary shares previously. The ordinary shares of the Company will not be affected by the change in the ADS ratio, and no ordinary shares will be issued or cancelled in connection with it.

The exchange of every seven and one-half (7.5) then-held ADSs for one new ADS is expected to occur automatically on the effective date, with the then-held ADSs being cancelled and new ADSs issued by the Depositary. Fractional new ADSs will not be issued in connection with the change in the ADS ratio. Instead, fractional entitlements to new ADSs will be aggregated and sold by the Depositary, and the net cash proceeds from the sale, after deduction of applicable fees, taxes and expenses, will be distributed to the applicable ADS holders in accordance with the terms of the deposit agreement.

ADS holders are not required to take any action. ADSs held in brokerage accounts will be adjusted automatically, and holders of certificated ADSs will receive instructions from the Depositary. The Company will pay the Depositary's fees and charges in connection with the surrender, cancellation, exchange, and issuance of ADSs effected as part of the ADS ratio change. ADS holders should consult their brokers, banks, or other financial intermediaries for information regarding the treatment of their holdings in connection with the ratio change.
