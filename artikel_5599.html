BYD unveiled a $310,000 electric sedan to rival Rolls-Royce

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Yangwang opened China reservations for a limited edition at a preliminary 2.08 million yuan; sales elsewhere are not announced

BYD’s luxury brand Yangwang unveiled the Altior, an all-electric four-seat sedan, at a global premiere in Abu Dhabi, the company said. The car uses four motors and has a range of more than 1,000 kilometers (621 miles) on the CLTC cycle.

The Nov. 7 event marked the first time the brand had staged a worldwide premiere beyond China, according to TechNode. Yangwang opened reservations for a limited edition with a preliminary price of 2.08 million yuan, about $311,000, and a deposit of 300,000 yuan.

The Altior measures 5.6 meters (18.4 feet) long, 2.03 meters (6.7 feet) wide and 1.59 meters (5.2 feet) tall, with a 3.5-meter (11.5-foot) wheelbase, BYD said. Its four motors are each controlled separately, which lets the car fine-tune the torque sent to every wheel over 100 times every second. The car can perform tank turns and has an emergency flotation function for accidental entry into water.

BYD said the car uses its Second-Generation Blade Battery and supports FLASH Charging. Additional technical details and charging specifications will be announced at a later date, the company said.

Inside, the rear seats are aviation-style zero-gravity seats with massage systems. The cabin has a 39-speaker Dynaudio sound system, a powered 21.4-inch overhead screen, and front and rear refrigerators. Yellow Hetian jade finishes the air vents, and the upholstery features both Suzhou embroidery and perforated sections. Front doors open to 80 degrees and rear doors to 90 degrees.

The Altior will come in Executive and Bespoke editions, BYD said. The first themed Bespoke Edition, Shanhe Dingyun, has a brown and gold exterior and a jade-inspired interior. BYD said it will be a limited run.

Design Director Wolfgang Egger said in a statement that the design draws on the ding, an ancient Chinese ceremonial bronze vessel, and took three years to develop. "ALTIOR represents our vision for the future of Chinese luxury," BYD Executive Vice President Stella Li said in a statement.

The car targets sedans from Rolls-Royce, Bentley and Mercedes-Maybach, according to Carscoops. A Rolls-Royce Phantom starts at roughly 8.5 million yuan in China. BYD has not announced availability outside China.

The launch follows BYD's push into overseas markets. BYD reported second-quarter net profit of 8.2 billion yuan, up 30% from a year earlier. First-half exports rose 71% to more than 790,000 vehicles, or 44% of total sales.
