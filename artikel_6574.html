Through the Meta Lens: A Day on Set With Vogue Shopping’s Market Editor

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

From documenting fittings to capturing behind-the-scenes styling, my Ray-Ban Meta glasses integrated seamlessly into a day on set. Using them for the first time was remarkably intuitive: they were easy to set up and comfortable to wear, and I was able to put them to use right away. Recording everything as I saw it made the day feel easy (I just had to keep my hair tucked to stop flyaways from brushing the lenses!), letting me upload imagery and share updates with the team in real time.

Set days usually start by me recording fittings and documenting styling moments, which I usually capture pre-shoot—this helps set the tone for the day. I typically capture everything on my phone; this time around I just used my Meta glasses. The day goes by quickly when we’re shooting, and I sometimes forget to capture those fun BTS snippets. After each look has been photographed, I usually take final sitting photos to track the pieces we shot. With my Meta glasses on, I was able to record everything without interrupting the creative flow.

For our most recent dress shoot, we called in a mix of holiday and resort pieces that felt right to wear right now. We went for bold colors (inspired by all the saturated hues seen on the spring runways) and rich textures that were fitting for both everyday wear and end-of-year festivities.

The Meta glasses were an actual game-changer in my process, and I plan to keep using them to capture special everyday moments, at work and at home—particularly those fleeting precious ones of my young daughter.

Inspired by several of Celine’s tonal spring looks, I wanted to bring this liveliness into the holidays with a similar bold color palette.

To play up the drama on this Dries Van Noten gown, I wanted to go all out with accessories. Enter: a shearling cape, opera-length leather gloves, and a pair of statement earrings. Together, they lean into its main character energy.

Knit dresses are a given in the winter. Instead of going with a simple rib, I wanted a strong textural moment. This bouclé style was a natural choice. To zhuzh it up, I pinned this Lié Studio brooch on it, adding knee-high boots and a patterned clutch as finishing touches.

The cummerbund isn’t going anywhere any time soon! Here, it wraps around a red Róhe maxi dress, which I paired with Khaite’s mini zebra bag and a pair of black kitten heels for a graphic pop.

The idea behind this ensemble was all about contrast—achieved here by pairing a deep berry Fforme dress in hammered satin with a yellow velvet Ossou jacket. I kept accessories to a minimum with a simple duo of pendant necklaces.
