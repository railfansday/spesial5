Nuix Appoints Lori Cornmesser as Global Vice President, Partner Ecosystems, to Accelerate Growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

SYDNEY, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Nuix (ASX: NXL), a leading provider of investigative analytics and intelligence software, today announced the appointment of Lori Cornmesser as Global Vice President, Partner Ecosystems. Cornmesser will lead the strategic evolution of Nuix's partner network, scaling strategic alliances across global systems integrators (GSIs), service providers, cloud marketplaces, channel partners, resellers, and technology ecosystems.

Cornmesser brings over 25 years' experience building and leading partner ecosystems globally. Reporting directly to Oliver Harvey, Chief Customer Officer, Cornmesser will oversee partner engagement, enablement, and joint go-to-market execution. The appointment underscores Nuix's expanded commitment to its partner strategy, prioritizing co-innovation and services opportunities that deliver transformative solutions to mutual customers.

"Lori has a rare track record of turning partner programs into primary growth engines," said Oliver Harvey, Chief Customer Officer, Nuix. "Organizations facing the highest scrutiny increasingly rely on trusted advisors to help them turn complex data into defensible insight, and our partners are central to how we reach and serve them. Lori's experience building repeatable, partner-led motions will accelerate that strategy globally."

Nuix's partner ecosystem offers an opportunity to integrate its unstructured data intelligence platform, Nuix Neo, more deeply into the technology stacks of large enterprises, government agencies, and law enforcement organizations.

"Nuix sits at the intersection of two things partners value most: hard problems and high-stakes customers," said Cornmesser. "Investigations, compliance, and legal review demand forensic-grade data intelligence, and there is a significant opportunity to put that capability in the hands of partners who already advise the world's most scrutinized organizations. I look forward to building an ecosystem that grows alongside them."

Prior to joining Nuix, Cornmesser was at Juniper, managing more than 5,000 global alliance partners and developing go-to-market strategies with major service providers including AT&T, Verizon, BT, Orange Business, TelefÃ³nica, Optus, and Telstra, before leading channel growth for Juniper's newly formed Enterprise business. More recently, Cornmesser expanded 1Password's channel, distribution, MSP, marketplace, and strategic alliances footprint across Europe and Asia and built global partner ecosystems at Infoblox, Ixia/Agilent, Deepwatch, and CyCognito.

Cornmesser holds an MBA and a Bachelor of Business Administration from Columbus University, and is based in San Jose, California, USA.
