These Macro Events Could Spearhead a Massive Bullish Run for Crypto

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

A series of major macroeconomic events scheduled for November 2026 could catalyze a strong upward move in cryptocurrency markets, according to analysis from data-driven researcher Stacy Muur. After the volatile price swings that marked the end of last week, the analyst noted that markets are still turbulent, but upcoming data releases and policy signals may ultimately clear the path for renewed bullish momentum.

Muur identified 8 key events that stand to influence crypto prices in the near term. These include $125B in U.S. Treasury auctions on November 9, 10 and 12.

The October Consumer Price Index report follows on November 10, with the Producer Price Index due on November 13. October retail sales and NVIDIA’s third-quarter earnings both landed on November 17.

The Federal Open Market Committee minutes are set for November 18, while the October Personal Consumption Expenditures inflation reading arrives on November 25. Additionally, weekly initial jobless claims are scheduled for November 12, 19, and 25. The next FOMC rate decision follows on December 9.

A supporting chart shared by the analyst shows the relative impact these types of releases typically have on crypto volatility. Federal Reserve rate decisions lead with a 2.7x multiplier, while Consumer Price Index and Non-Farm Payrolls reports each register 2.1x. Personal Consumption Expenditures data carries a 1.6x effect, Producer Price Index prints 1.5x, Gross Domestic Product reports 1.1x, and jobless claims 1.0x.

If the incoming inflation and employment data continue to show cooling pressure, markets could interpret the results as supportive of a more accommodative Federal Reserve stance. Soft readings on CPI, PPI, and PCE, combined with stable jobless claims, have helped fuel sustained rallies in Bitcoin and the wider crypto sector in past cycles.

NVIDIA’s earnings may add further upside if the results reinforce strength in the AI and technology themes that have increasingly correlated with digital asset performance.

As such, traders are watching the calendar closely as a string of favorable macro surprises could shift sentiment decisively, turning the current period of uncertainty into the foundation for a broader bullish run across crypto markets in the weeks ahead.
