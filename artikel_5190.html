XRP News: Thanksgiving Rally or December 1 Supply Shock?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

XRP has a history of making Thanksgiving interesting, old news for some. In 2024, Ripple surged nearly 49% in the three days following the holiday. Last year, it went nowhere. Now, XRP is heading into another Thanksgiving with $1 in sight and Ripple’s 1 billion-token escrow unlock looming on December 1.

The timing puts bulls in a tight spot. XRP is recovering from a November sell-off, but the rebound has yet to clear major resistance. A holiday rally could give buyers some breathing room before next week’s token release puts supply back in the spotlight.

The 2024 Thanksgiving weekend was anything but quiet for XRP. The token closed November 28 at $1.54, climbed to $1.80 the following day, and reached $2.29 by December 1. That was a gain of nearly 49% from Thanksgiving’s close.

The rally had fuel beyond holiday trading as Donald Trump’s election victory revived hopes for a friendlier crypto regulatory environment, while XRP was breaking out after years of being held back by its SEC lawsuit. Buyers were chasing momentum, and the long weekend did little to slow them down.

Last year’s price action was considerably less exciting. XRP closed Thanksgiving on November 27, 2025, near $2.20, then drifted around $2.16 over the following days. The market offered no repeat of the explosive move seen a year earlier.

That history makes this year’s comparison interesting, but the conditions are different. XRP is entering Thanksgiving after a painful November decline.

XRP’s recovery toward $0.95 has brought the psychological $1 level back into play. Buyers have been defending the area below $0.90, preventing another immediate breakdown and giving the short-term chart some breathing room.

But the problem is overhead resistance, as XRP must first push through $0.96–$0.98 before $1 becomes a realistic breakout target. That zone has become the immediate test for buyers. A clean move through it could attract momentum traders and open the path toward $1.02 and $1.08.

The $1 mark matters because XRP has spent November losing ground. Reclaiming it would put the token back above an important psychological threshold and give bulls something concrete to work with.

Failure to break resistance could leave the rebound looking like another dead-cat bounce. XRP’s recent low around $0.89 remains the level to watch. Losing it would put $0.86 back on the radar and risk undoing the recovery.

For now, the chart is caught between an improving short-term bounce and a damaged larger trend. Bulls have a chance, but they need to turn that chance into a breakout.

The next complication arrives Monday, with Ripple’s scheduled escrow contracts. It will release 1 billion XRP, renewing concerns about supply just as the token tries to recover from bad geopolitical news.

The billion-token headline sounds worse than Ripple’s typical net release. Over the past year, the company has re-escrowed around 70% of monthly unlocks, leaving around 300 million XRP outside escrow on average. The market will be watching whether December follows the same pattern.

The full 1 billion XRP unlock is not equivalent to 1 billion tokens hitting exchanges for sale. What Ripple re-locks, retains, or moves afterward will determine how much circulating supply actually changes.

What does it need? XRP cannot afford to enter Monday with a failed breakout and weakening support. If buyers reclaim $1 before the unlock, sentiment could shift in their favor. If XRP remains pinned below resistance, the escrow event gives nervous traders another reason to stay cautious.

Thanksgiving delivered XRP a spectacular rally in 2024. This year, bulls need more than a holiday repeat; they need the $1 back.
