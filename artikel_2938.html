AI Pullback Raises the Cost of Data Center Financing

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The recent pullback in AI stocks is spilling into credit markets, making some data center financing more expensive even as demand for computing power remains strong. MUFG Americas CFO Mark Thumser says lenders are becoming more selective and investors are demanding wider spreads and better terms. He also argues the massive AI infrastructure buildout remains a long-term, transformational trend. He joins Bloomberg Businessweek Daily to discuss financing demand following Anthropic’s IPO, the outlook for global credit markets, and growing public resistance to data center development. (Source: Bloomberg)
