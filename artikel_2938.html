Mosaic downgraded by Freedom Broker as sulfur costs squeeze profits

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Freedom Broker downgraded Mosaic (MOS) to Sell from Hold on Wednesday, warning that rising sulfur costs will continue to pressure the fertilizer producer's earnings through the end of 2026, with meaningful relief unlikely until next year.
