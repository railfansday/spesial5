Report: Suns prefer size over a point guard

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

We have the latest update from the Phoenix Suns point guard saga, and it's taken an unexpected turn: to the frontcourt.

The Phoenix Suns reportedly have "greater concern" over their lack of size than their current point guard situation, according to NBA insider Jake Fischer.

With Khaman Maluach still very raw and Mark Williams expected to be sidelined for the foreseeable future, the team is thin at center. Now starting Oso Ighodaro at center after Maluach began the year as the starter, the Suns have opted to play small at times, which has hurt them on the boards. Before the games on November 19, Phoenix ranks 30th in the NBA in defensive rebounding percentage, according to HoopsJunkie.

While Fischer didn't report any bigs Phoenix could be targeting, Arizona Sports insider John Gambadoro reported last night on X that the Suns are unlikely to make a deal this early in the season and are trying to build off their winning play of late. They've won six of their last eight and are now 7-7 after starting 1-5.

Over the last few games, Phoenix's starting backcourt has played some of its best basketball of the season. Jalen Green and Devin Booker have shot a combined 52% from the field and 41% from three over the past two games, and the Suns are 2-0 to start their five-game road trip.
