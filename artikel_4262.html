XBOX Celebrates 25 Years With New Update Resurrecting Classic Boot-Up Sequence

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Greg has more than than 10 years of experience in video game journalism, covering some of the biggest games on the 8th and 9th generations of console.

Big anniversaries often come with a healthy dose of nostalgia, and XBOX is certainly not letting fans down with their latest announcement, which is sure to stir a wistful longing for simpler times in gaming.

Most players remember their first consoles, or the excitement of tearing open a new one on birthdays or Christmases–the enduring excitement of plugging it in for the first time and hearing the techy tones of the numerous iconic boot-up sequences.

PlayStation nostalgiacore content often goes viral, harping on about the ethereal start-up sounds of the PlayStation 2, while many fans rave about the perfection of the Gamecube’s legendary unfolding animation.

However, Microsoft is reminding gamers that they had a few iconic boot-up animations too, with a new update bringing them back to modern consoles and giving fans the feels.

Microsoft just announced on X that it is resurrecting its classic boot-up sequences from XBOX eras gone by in a new update, set to celebrate 25 years of gaming on the platform. Returning are all original sequences from XBOX’s first days as a console juggernaut, with the dramatic green X from 2001 making its comeback, as well as the iconic XBOX 360 intro.

Fans will remember these fondly from the days when Halo was the biggest game in the world, or when they first dived into Call of Duty Zombies for the first time. For younger fans, the XBOX One sequence will also be available, allowing them to relive their first XBOX era.

Could we soon be playing OG Xbox and 360 titles on Game Pass with quality of life updates?

Microsoft is also rolling out two additional XBOX 360 boot-up animations as part of the update, allowing players to customize their start-up experience to whichever design suits them best.

The update is rolling out imminently to all Xbox Series X/S owners as a thanks for their support. Players can change their desired boot-up sequences in their console settings, with quite a variety to now choose from.

While Microsoft may be trying to remind fans of brighter days when it was winning the console wars, XBOX CEO Asha Sharma hasn’t had an easy ride thus far after taking the helm earlier this year. As a complete newcomer to the games industry, Sharma has worked hard to immerse herself in the complex culture of video games in a somewhat trial by fire experience.

She certainly entered the role with high hopes for XBOX, notably saying she hoped the platform could “reach 1 billion people” daily, which raised a few eyebrows in the industry.

The company has been working hard (and secretively) on its XBOX Series X/S successor, titled Project Helix, which they hope will further bridge the gap between console and PC—something which Microsoft has been striving to achieve for years now. Layoffs have plagued the company and its studios, however, with thousands of roles being cut under its vast umbrella, leading to resentment from fans and industry professionals.

However, some have rallied behind the company due to its recent partnerships with studios like Kojima Productions, best known for its auteur leader and brain behind Metal Gear Solid, Hideo Kojima.

XBOX recently announced a years-long collaboration with Kojima and will see exclusive titles come to the platform, including the horror title OD and the proposed gaming-TV crossed Physint. The partnership comes after Kojima’s deal with XBOX rival, PlayStation, fell apart due to funding constraints.
