Aurora Gaming sweep Pixel Lumina to reach Europe Qualifier 1 final after BONK disqualification

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Aurora Gaming beat Pixel Lumina 2-0 to reach the Europe Qualifier 1 final, days after the UK side that had knocked them out was disqualified for cheating. The final decides seeding for the Europe Qualifier Playoffs, the next step towards VCT EMEA Kickoff 2027 in Berlin.

Aurora Gaming will play in the Europe Qualifier 1 final. Their best-of-three semi-final against Pixel Lumina began at 16:00 on Tuesday. Aurora took the opening map in 41 minutes and closed out the second in 35 minutes.

The win completes a turnaround for a side that had already been eliminated from the qualifier. Their earlier exit had come against BONK.

The fallout reached beyond the bracket. Every BONK player and registered participant was also provisionally suspended, GINX TV reported.

The disqualification forced a rework of the schedule. Riot had set Qualifier 1 for 5 to 8 November as a single-elimination, best-of-three bracket. The Pixel Lumina semi-final and the final were pushed back to make room for the rebuilt bracket. All three affected organisations agreed to the new timetable.

Pixel Lumina, a team based in Hungary, came into the match at rank 120 in the Elo power rankings. They hold a rating of 1559 and a 30-28 record across 58 rated matches. Aurora are unranked. Before this semi-final, Pixel Lumina lost to CGN Esports in the VCL EMEA Last Chance Qualifier 2026. That defeat followed wins over FOKUS and Twisted Saints Esports in the same event.

Aurora’s lineup features Boo, chiwa, Derke, B1SK and trexx. Derke joined straight after Valorant Champions 2026, where his Vitality side lost the grand final 3-1 to LOUD.

Aurora now face the winner of xXxMasterProMegaTeamxXx versus F9 EICAR. That final decides seeding for the Kickoff Qualifier Playoffs. Aurora are already listed as qualified for the playoffs through Qualifier 1, so the final is about position rather than survival.

The wider prize is a place at VCT EMEA Kickoff 2027. Europe will supply half of the four non-partner sides heading to the event, Riot Games said, while Türkiye and MENA each send a single team. Kickoff itself will be played offline at the Riot Games Arena in Berlin with 12 teams.

The rest of the European field is still taking shape. Europe Qualifier 2 is scheduled for 12 to 15 November and is also played as single-elimination best-of-three brackets.
