Anthony Gordon Broke Cristiano Ronaldo Record During Sabah 1-4 Barcelona

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Anthony Gordon scored a brace for Barcelona in the Champions League against Sabah FK on Wednesday night, breaking a record set by Cristiano Ronaldo in the process.

The England international, who signed for the Spanish giants in the summer for £69.3 million, scored Hansi Flick's outfit's first two goals in Azerbaijan, setting them on their way to a 4-1 win. Fellow summer signings Karim Adeyemi and Gabriel Jesus netted the other two goals as La Blaugrana moved up to fourth in the Champions League table.

Eyebrows were raised when Barcelona opted to splash such a significant sum on Gordon in May, but he has largely silenced the sceptics so far with an encouraging start to life at the Camp Nou.

The wide man now has five goals and four assists to his name at his new club, and has subsequently established himself as a pivotal figure in Flick's team.

Not only is he producing fruitful numbers for the La Liga Champions, but he's also making history, eclipsing some of the best players of all time in some very specific metrics.

Gordon was renowned for his pace in England, but he's arguably developed a different side to his game since signing for Barcelona, and this was on show against Sabah.

Both of his goals showed intelligence and awareness to be in the right place at the right time to tap home from close range. It's this smart movement that has seen him develop as a goal threat, and this has helped him set two new records.

Scoring four for Newcastle in Baku against Azerbaijani side Qarabag last season, Gordon's tally of six in the Eastern Asian nation set the record for the most goals scored by any English player in away games in one country in the Champions League.

These goals also made him the first player in history to score six away goals in a single country in the same calendar year in Europe's elite club competition.

Barcelona supporters appear to have made up their minds about the £69 million England international following his 15th game for the club

He surpassed Cristiano Ronaldo, who netted five for Real Madrid in Turkey in 2013. Karim Benzema is currently joint second in this extremely niche statistic, also scoring five for Los Blancos, but in England in 2022.

Speaking after the match, Gordon stated that Azerbaijan must be his 'special place'.

"Even when we were 3-0 up, we didn't stop attacking," he said.

"We kept trying to score. That's our style, and I think it's very important. Baku must be a very special place for me... I should have scored another hat-trick tonight, but I wasted that chance with my left foot."

The former Everton man even joked that he might need to move to Baku at some point in his life.

"Maybe at some point in my life I need to move here! I've been very successful here, and yeah, great game today. Obviously two goals, so I'm happy, but potentially could have had a hat-trick, so still a lot of work to do," he told Stan (via Live Score).
