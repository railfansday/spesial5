What Sir Alex Ferguson Thought of Alejandro Garnacho at Man Utd

Sir Alex Ferguson’s previous comments on Alejandro Garnacho have resurfaced amid the Argentine’s current struggles at Aston Villa.

Garnacho, 22, spent his early youth career at Getafe and Atletico Madrid before moving to United in 2020, where he impressed in the academy and was handed his senior debut in 2022.

However, the Argentine’s early form failed to continue as he endured a couple of tough seasons before leaving Old Trafford for Chelsea in 2025, for a reported fee of £40 million.

Since moving to Chelsea, Garnacho has so far failed to rediscover his best form and was loaned out to Villa this summer, where he is yet to make a mark under Unai Emery.

This week, Garnacho was left out of Villa’s squad for their 1-0 defeat against Galatasaray, with Emery opting to pick only 11 substitutes out of a possible 12, which included two goalkeepers.

Back in 2024, as reported by Goal, legendary United boss Ferguson heaped praise on both Garnacho and Kobbie Mainoo, highlighting the pair’s ‘composure and confidence’.

While attending a golf event, Ferguson was asked by TikTok star Jamal Niaz about the impact Garnacho and Mainoo have had on United, with the Scotsman naming the pair as the ‘foundation’ of what the club stands for.

Ferguson said: “It's the foundation isn’t it?

“It's the heart and soul of what Manchester United has been since the days of Sir Matt Busby, who started the whole process of trusting young players playing.

“They're young. What they’re showing at the moment, the composure and confidence to play in the first team and not be daunted by it. In a way, it shows you the kind of courage they have.”

Garnacho left Man Utd in 2025, joining Chelsea for £40 million. He struggled to impress at Stamford Bridge and was sent on loan to Aston Villa this summer.

So far this campaign, Garnacho has only managed 95 minutes across seven matches in all competitions for Villa, with the 22-year-old failing to impress Emery and his coaching staff.

The Argentine’s Villa stint is quickly becoming similar to what Harvey Elliott endured last season under Emery during his loan spell from Liverpool.

When asked why he opted to snub Garnacho even though he left one space in the squad empty, Emery simply said: “Today the players were here who I think are the best players to be here with us.”

Emery's comments about Garnacho after he failed to make Aston Villa's squad for their clash against Galatasaray haven't gone down well.

In truth, Garnacho has simply failed to live up to the expectations since his brilliant breakthrough season at Old Trafford.

Currently behind the likes of Alysson, Ibrahim Mbaye, John McGinn, Johan Manzambi and Emi Buendia in the Villa pecking order, it remains to be seen whether Garnacho will try to engineer a move away when the January transfer window opens.
