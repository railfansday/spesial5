Dua Lipa, SZA and Tame Impala Set to Headline Coachella 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Ahead of Coachella 2027, Dua Lipa, SZA and Tame Impala have been confirmed as headliners for the annual two-weekend music festival in Southern California.

You should know that Coachella 2027 is already shaping up to be the best yet.

After all, Dua Lipa, Tame Impala and SZA are officially headlining the iconic California desert the upcoming music festival, set to take place the second and third weekends of April 2027.

Dua will own Friday at the festival, where the "Levitating" artist will be joined by the likes of Madonna, Zara Larsson, Steve Lacy and Jisoo.

Tame Impala will command the stage on Saturday—the lineup also including artists such as Phoebe Bridgers, Weezer and Robyn—while SZA is the top billing on Sunday, with Kygo, Becky G and Don Toliver among the other performers.

For her part, Dua will take the stage for her first solo set at the annual festival, exactly 10 years after making a guest appearance in 2017 during Martin Garrix’s set.

And while training season for the headliner stage has just started, the forthcoming performance may mark the first new musical release since her 2024 album Radical Optimism. (Not to mention, Dua’s frequent appearances outside of Electric Lady Studios in New York the last few months have gotten fans levitating.)

Following her wedding to Callum Turner in May, the “Training Season” singer hinted at what was to come for her fourth studio album—noting her source of inspiration has shifted.

"Talking about being heartbroken is a very common thing,” the 31-year-old told Vogue Italia in an August interview. “When I was younger, I found much more inspiration in the search and desire. And that’s because feeling broken is a very strong and loud emotion.”

As she has hit the studio again, Dua has set out some new rules for herself.

“I want to discover what happens when you truly find love,” she explained. “I’m interested in the complexities of a relationship, its quieter aspects, the little thing.”

SZA, meanwhile, is no stranger to the Coachella stage, making her Coachella debut in 2018 and even making an appearance last year with Justin Bieber to perform their hit duet “Snooze.”

But while the performance during weekend two of the festival was special for fans, the 36-year-old shut down rumors she was tapped by the festival to act as Justin’s understudy if he failed to make a performance.

“Lmao who made this up?” SZA wrote on Instagram last year. “I’ve seen this 4 times now. I’m in New York no one paid me a dime. Wishing everyone the best.”

During her upcoming Coachella performance, though, SZA might just kill fans with some new tracks. After all, she confirmed in a recent Instagram comment she would “indeed” be returning with new music after releasing SOS Deluxe: Lana last year.

And as for Tame Impala? The musician (real name: Kevin Parker) last hit the Coachella stage in 2019. And considering he’s put out The Slow Rush and Deadbeats in the interim, fans will want to be right there where he’s performing next year—especially if Jennie makes an appearance to run from sunlight, Dracula.
