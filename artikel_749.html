How to Watch the 2026 Latin Grammys Live Online

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Tonight's ceremony will features performances from Karol G, Rosalía, Daddy Yankee and more.

The biggest names in Latin music are gathering in Las Vegas on Thursday night for the 2026 Latin Grammy Awards, with Karol G, Rosalía, Anitta and Daddy Yankee among the artists set to take the stage.

The 27th annual ceremony will broadcast live from the MGM Grand Garden Arena on Nov. 12 at 8 p.m. ET/PT on Univision, UNIMÁS and Galavisión. Viewers can also stream the three-hour show live on ViX, TelevisaUnivision’s Spanish-language streaming service. Other options include Fubo, which offers a dedicated Latino package and Hulu + Live TV which offers access to Univision in participating markets alongside a free trial.

Coverage begins ahead of the main ceremony with the “Noche de Estrellas” red-carpet pre-show, airing on UNIMÁS, Galavisión and ViX beginning at 6 p.m. ET. Univision will join the coverage at 7 p.m. ET, one hour before the awards begin.

Producer and songwriter Edgar Barrera leads this year’s Latin Grammy nominations with 10 nods, including for Producer of the Year and Songwriter of the Year. He’s followed by Karol G, Rosalía, Jorge Drexler and Argentine duo Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso, who each earned seven nominations. Silvana Estrada and Mon Laferte trail closely behind with six apiece.

Among the most closely watched races is Album of the Year, where Karol G’s “Tropicoqueta” and Rosalía’s “Lux (Complete Works)” will compete against Anitta’s “Equilibrivm,” Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso’s “Free Spirits,” Jorge Drexler’s “Taracá” and Mon Laferte’s “Femme Fatale,” among others.

Karol G and Rosalía are also competing in the Record of the Year category, with “Coleccionando Heridas,” Karol G’s collaboration with Marco Antonio Solís, facing off against Rosalía and Yahritza y Su Esencia’s “La Perla.” Other contenders include Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso’s “Ha Ha,” Drexler’s “¿Cómo Se Ama?” and Estrada’s “Dime.”

This year’s awards recognize recordings released between June 1, 2025, and May 31, 2026, with nominees competing across 60 categories. The ceremony will also honor Daddy Yankee as the Latin Recording Academy’s Person of the Year, making him the first Latin urbano artist to receive the distinction.
