Feid, Anthony Ramos & More to Join 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade & More Uplifting Moments in Latin Music

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Plus, Eladio Carrión's charity golf tournament returns to Puerto Rico, Erika Ender celebras 35-year career in Panama.

From career milestones to new music releases to major announcements and those little important moments, Billboard editors highlight uplifting moments in Latin music. Feid and Anthony Ramos are among the performers set to join the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade on Thursday (Nov. 26), bringing Latin music representation to the centennial celebration. The parade begins at 8:30 a.m. ET and will air on NBC and Telemundo, with streaming on Peacock. The lineup also includes Baha Men, Natasha Bedingfield, Blues Traveler, DEVO, Wyclef Jean, Queen Latifah, Keke Palmer, RIIZE and Smash Mouth, with a special Dolly Parton tribute led by Megan Hilty and a featured performance by EJAE. At Herald Square, Megan Moroney and Teddy Swims will perform, while Broadway shows will join in a special celebration. The parade will feature a record 100 inflatables, six new floats and more than 11,000 volunteers and participants for its milestone march.

Panamanian singer-songwriter Erika Ender celebrated her 35-year musical career in Panama with the premiere of a musical based on her life and the presentation of her book, AprEnder El Éxito, which is currently available for pre-order.

The musical Erika Ender — written and directed by Venezuelan playwright Benjamín Cohen and featuring a cast of approximately 80 performers — held its grand international premiere on Thursday (Nov. 19) at the National Theater in Panama City. In addition to Ender and her family, the event was attended by prominent figures from the music and entertainment industries, including Desmond Child, Elena Rose, Elvis Crespo, Aymée Nuviola and Diana Reyes, as well as Panamanian boxing legend Roberto Durán. Also in attendance was Randy Cuevas, of the band Rabanes, who was responsible for the musical arrangements for the production.

Before the show began, Ender was presented with a medal in “recognition of merit and an exceptional artistic career” by Panama’s Minister of Culture, Maruja Herrera. The award is a new annual honor designed to celebrate individuals who, through their personal example and artistic work, make an extraordinary contribution to Panama’s cultural enrichment and international profile. The following morning, at the same venue, the book was presented during an event moderated by media personality Ismael Cala. — SIGAL RATNER-ARIAS

Building on last year’s fundraiser, Eladio Carrión’s Sauce Boyz Country Club Golf Tournament returns to Puerto Rico on Dec. 2, bringing artists, athletes and community supporters together at Coco Beach Golf & Country Club in Río Grande. The tournament’s first edition raised $179,300 for social initiatives across the island; this year’s event aims to build on that impact and support opportunities for children and young people.

“What motivates me the most is knowing that all of this serves a purpose that goes beyond us. There are so many young people in Puerto Rico with big dreams and tremendous potential, said Carrión in a statement. “If we can do our part to help them have better opportunities, access to resources, and people who believe in them, then we are doing something meaningful. I want this tournament to continue growing, but above all, I want it to continue making a real impact on the lives of our people”.

Red Bull Batalla has announced the 16 MCs who will compete in its 2026-27 U.S. National Final, returning to Los Angeles for the first time in five years. The event is set for Feb. 20, 2027, at The Bellwether, where the winner will earn the chance to represent the U.S. at the international final in Peru in April. The field includes three-time national champion Reverse, two-time champion Yartzi and rising competitor Dajamii Alien, who could become the first woman to win the U.S. national title.
