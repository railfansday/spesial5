For the week 11 matchup, two prime NFL teams, the Vikings and 49ers, play against each other for domination on the field. Meanwhile, there might be one or two celebrity sightings during the game too.

The NFL Mexico City Game happens on Sunday (Nov. 22) with a matchup featuring the Minnesota Vikings (7-2) taking on the San Francisco 49ers (8-1) at Estadio Banorte in Mexico City, Mexico, with a start time of 8:20 p.m. ET/5:20 p.m. PT (4:20 p.m. CST).

The Vikings-49ers game broadcasts on NBC, while the NFL game is available to livestream on Peacock.

NFL games are known for celebrity sightings, so it’s likely there may be a number of famous actors, actresses and recording artists in attendance. Artists have been known to appear at games in Mexico in the past, include NFL fans Eiza González, Guillermo Ochoa and others.

Latin Grammy Award-winning rock band The Warning performs at halftime, which starts around 9:45 p.m. ET/6:45 p.m. PT (8:45 p.m. CEST).

Plus, Mexican singer-songwriter Majo Aguilar is scheduled to perform the national anthem of Mexico, “Himno Nacional Mexicano,” while singer MXKA is set to perform the American national anthem, “The Star-Spangled Banner.”
