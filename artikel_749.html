At a Glance: How to Watch Billy Evans on CBS Sunday Morning

The episode airs Sunday, Nov. 15 at 6 a.m. PT/9 a.m. ET on CBS.

CBS Sunday Morning is available to watch live on any streaming service that carries CBS network, namely DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), Fubo (also with a five-day free trial) and Hulu + Live TV.

The episode will also be available to stream live with a Paramount+ Premium subscription or on-demand following the broadcast with Paramount+ Essential. While the service no longer offers a free trial of its own, customers can claim three complimentary months of Paramount+ Premium (and a host of other add-ons) with DirecTV’s Entertainment, Choice or Ultimate packages, which also offer the aforementioned five-day free trial period. Alternatively, the Premier package, DirecTV’s top-tier package, includes Paramount+ indefinitely as part of its standard streaming lineup.

To claim the free trial, select your preferred Signature package. Once it prompts you to choose how many Gemini devices you’d like to connect, you’ll select “0 – Stream without the Gemini Air” as the five-day free trial is only available to customers without a leased Gemini device.

Learn more about each plan option, including how to build your own channel lineup (starting at just $20.99 per month), at directv.com.

Paramount+ Essential is the streamer’s ad-supported base package, costing $8.99 per month or $89.99 annually.

Paramount+ Premium (formerly known as Paramount+ with Showtime) is $13.99 per month or $139.99 per year and offers everything in the essential plan without ads (except for live TV), plus access to all of Showtime, the ability to stream CBS live with more sports and events, the option to download movies and shows and the ability to watch in 4K Ultra HD, Dolby Vision or HDR10.

Watch CBS with Hulu + Live TV. Subscriptions come bundled with Disney+ and ESPN+, and start at $89.99 per month.
