Man Utd expected to sack head coach Carrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Michael Carrick signed a two-year deal with Manchester United last summer

Manchester United look increasingly likely to sack head coach Michael Carrick.

The club opted to stick with Carrick after the side's 3-0 home defeat by Bournemouth on 25 October, with the intention of reassessing the situation during this month's international break.

Multiple sources have told BBC Sport that the board remain committed to that process, and that no final decision on Carrick's future has been made.

However, there is a growing expectation that chief executive Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox will recommend to minority owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe and the Glazer family for Carrick to be sacked.

The 4-4 draw with Aston Villa on Sunday means United, who are 18th in the table, have made their worst start to a domestic campaign in 40 years.

BBC Sport reported in October that the club had sounded out former Newcastle boss Eddie Howe and ex-Germany coach Julian Nagelsmann over replacing Carrick.

Former Chelsea coach Antonio Conte is known to be keen on the role, while multiple sources have told BBC Sport that former Spurs boss Mauricio Pochettino is open to a move to Old Trafford.

Pochettino signed an extension to his contract as USA coach in August.

However, the deal is thought to contain clauses that would allow him to leave for specific high-profile jobs. The US have a two-legged Concacaf Nations League play-off with Haiti on 14 and 18 November.

The Villa draw was Carrick's third match in charge since the club opted to give the 45-year-old more time at Old Trafford.

Results during that time - a win, a draw and a defeat - were mixed, and displayed the best and worst of Carrick's reign with United shipping goals but displaying good character to come back from two goals down on three occasions.

United's board toyed with sacking Carrick after the loss to Bournemouth, but decided the Englishman - and his players - had earned the opportunity to show that they could turn the club's fortunes around.

It was also felt that the two-week international break in November represented a better chance for the club to properly assess the club's start to the campaign.

Unfortunately for Carrick, it makes for poor reading.

With one victory in 10 Premier League matches, United have made their worst start to a top-flight campaign since the 1986/87 season.

They sit 18th in the table, one point above bottom-placed Crystal Palace.

Only the Eagles and Ipswich have conceded more than the 19 goals United have shipped in the Premier League this season.

It is hard to believe given the club's current predicament just how quickly Carrick's tenure has soured.

Looking back, the 48 hours before United's season-opening match at Hull were pivotal.

With Benjamin Sesko still trying to ease his way back from the shin issue that ended his previous campaign early, Amad Diallo suffered an ankle injury that would rule him out for seven weeks. It meant Carrick was deprived of his first-choice striking alternative, with Bryan Mbeumo having to play on the right of the attack.

Carrick instead turned to Matheus Cunha, whose preference for dropping deep took him into the spaces captain Bruno Fernandes likes to occupy.

It neutralised United's attack on a day they seemed ill-prepared to meet the physical challenge of an opponent motivated by their long-awaited Premier League return.

In his role as interviewer for Nordic broadcaster Viaplay, former Norway and Middlesbrough striker Jan Aage Fjortoft later claimed United had lacked passion - something Carrick dismissed as "ridiculous".

"It's one game," Carrick told the media afterwards.

The following week, United were losing to Ipswich - another newly promoted side. Only a slip from Marcelino Nunez inside his own half allowed Cunha to tee up Fernandes for an undeserved equaliser.

The Portugal playmaker, who had just been crowned PFA Player of the Year for 2025-26, went on to complete a hat-trick in a 5-2 win.

He has scored just once - a penalty against Villa on Sunday - in the league since.

After the Ipswich win came a trip to Everton.

Twice United led, the second occasion when Sesko struck two minutes from full-time after coming on as a substitute. Deep into stoppage time, Ainsley Maitland-Niles drove home from long range to deny the visitors.

Asked how confident he was in his side's ability to react to a disappointing start to the season, Carrick snapped back: "It's not been that bad."

Time and again, United's fitness was called into question. They struggled badly against a Manchester City side reduced to 10 men in the first half before their rivals benefited from an Erling Haaland goal that referees' chief Howard Webb later admitted should not have stood.

Carrick's complaints were justified - but they masked a poor performance from his team.

After taking a 2-0 lead inside 10 minutes against Brighton in the EFL Cup, United were outplayed and beaten, thanks to two goals in four second-half minutes.

The fine margins United benefited from during Carrick's interim spell were now going against them, and his coaching staff - lauded months earlier - could not find an answer.

Carrick called Cunha's injury-time equaliser against a Fulham side who started the game bottom of the table a positive to build on. But immediately after the international break, United were held to another draw - conceding in injury time to an out-of-form and 10-man Tottenham.

The whirlwind was starting to pick up strength.

Lisandro Martinez, who scored an own goal against Fulham, was at fault for Spurs' leveller, which made his post-match outburst after the 2-0 Champions League defeat by Atletico Madrid more amazing.

The Argentina defender criticised United's summer recruitment and placed the blame at the door of "the technical director" - Wilcox.

By the time of Martinez's outburst, Sesko and left-back Luke Shaw were injured. Marcus Rashford has not played since. The rotation at left-back that eventually led to Carrick starting five different players in that role was in full swing.

After conceding in the opening minute against Atletico, Como scored inside the first two in a 4-1 Champions League defeat for United. Fernandes came out afterwards and criticised Carrick's tactics.

He was dropped for the Bournemouth game, but returned as captain at Chelsea. United were beaten in both matches.

With just three wins in 15 matches in all competitions this season and United stuck in 18th in the Premier League, Carrick is set to pay the price.
