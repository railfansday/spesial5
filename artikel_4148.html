Eight Xbox Games Are Out Today, Including The Next Great Espionage RPG (November 3)

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Welcome back to your daily update of all the new releases on Xbox today!

There's a couple of interesting games up for grabs, namely Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered and ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut. The former is a rejuvenated OG Xbox title which has you tombstoning big beasties as the King of Monsters, whereas the latter is the next game from ZA/UM, the creators of Disco Elysium.

As for Xbox Game Pass, there's two titles to get stuck into today - if you have a PC/handheld, that is. The first game, Pit of Goblin, is available across console and PC, whereas Football Manager 27's launch preview is only playable on PC. The console version is on the way November 10th, alongside its full release on XGP.
