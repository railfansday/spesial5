In recent years, there have been some stunning (and not-so-stunning) limited-edition custom consoles. These are often created to promote a game and are usually available in limited quantities via competitions and giveaways. In 2019, Xbox commissioned 12 custom Xbox One consoles to celebrate the launch of Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice, and more recently, it shoved a Series X down an alligator's throat.

This new one is a real head-turner, though, and it's one that you'll almost certainly never see in person, as Rockstar just gifted players of Miami Heat custom Grand Theft Auto 6 PlayStation 5 Pro consoles.

Grand Theft Auto's Vice City is heavily based on Miami. Grand Theft Auto 6 takes that a step further, with Leonida looking very much like Rockstar's version of Florida. The parallels are so great, in fact, that Rockstar turned the city of Miami into Vice City.

It's painted electrical boxes, taken over the airport, and a nightclub has temporarily changed its name to match the one in GTA 6. It's also opened up Burger Shot shops for people to get GTA's burgers in real life, and perhaps most notably, will take over the Miami Heat basketball team for one night. It's set to wear limited-edition jerseys, and the court has been redecorated with the infamous blue and pink of Vice City.

The collaboration seemingly didn't stop there, either. Over the last few hours, Miami Heat players, including Myron Gardner, Davion Mitchell, and Tim Hardaway Jr., have posted stunning PS5 Pro care packages they've received from Rockstar.

Delivered in a custom suitcase, the players received two limited-edition Grand Theft Auto 6 PS5 DualSense controllers, a copy of Grand Theft Auto 6, and a custom PS5 Pro console. The console is a slick silver color, etched with palm trees and the VI from the GTA logo. The vent running across the middle is metallic pink and blue.

These are some of the coolest consoles we've ever seen, and for what will likely be the biggest media event for many, many years. It's just a shame that we'll probably never get to lay our eyes on them in person.
