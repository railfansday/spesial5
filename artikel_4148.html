Marvel 1943: Rise Of Hydra Reportedly Still In Development Amid Rumors Of Its Cancellation

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Yesterday, a rumor emerged on X claiming that Marvel 1943: Rise of Hydra, the upcoming World War II-era superhero game led by Uncharted creator Amy Hennig, was being cancelled. After making the rounds on social media, new reports claim this is not the case, at least for now.

The rumor stemmed from a now-deleted X post from a fan account called Marvel 1943 Rise Of Hydra Countdown claiming, through supposed inside sources, that Skydance was preparing to announce the game’s cancellation. This rumor was quickly picked up and circulated by online gaming circles, gaining steam due to growing uncertainty around the game’s status.

Marvel 1943: Rise of Hydra was revealed in 2022 and stars Captain America and Black Panther (Azurri, T'Challa's grandfather), alongside Howling Commandos member Gabriel Jones and Wakandan Spy Network leader Nanali, as they battle Hydra during World War II. Developed by Skydance New Media, led by Amy Hennig, it’s been repeatedly delayed, and only cinematic footage has been released. This has understandably caused many to question the game’s fate.

However, a few hours after the cancellation rumor made the rounds, Bloomberg’s Jason Schreier reported on Bluesky that Skydance’s leadership addressed the rumor with the development team on Slack and assured them Marvel 1943 was not canceled. However, Schreier adds this could change due to the uncertainty caused by the recent merger between Warner Bros. Discovery and Paramount Skydance (Skydance New Media is a game development branch of the now-merged Skydance Corporation).

Skydance Games told the development team behind Marvel 1943: Rise of Hydra on Slack this afternoon that the game is not canceled, despite a widely circulated rumor on Twitter today claiming that it was.

Furthermore, Kotaku’s Rebekah Valentine clarified in her report that Marvel 1943 executive producer Lu Giglotti told staff in a Skydance Slack message he’d spoken directly with the executive leadership team to confirm Marvel 1943 is still alive, saying, "There is no truth to these claims." According to Kotaku’s report, Giglotti informed the team that Marvel 1943 was approaching an Alpha milestone later this month. Giglotti also told staff not to let the rumor distract them and that Skydance was "monitoring the situation" to address in the future.

Kotaku’s report also sheds light on the game’s tumultuous development cycle. Marvel 1943 was originally pitched as a Netflix game before it was eventually turned into a console and PC title. This transition means the current iteration is “light on gameplay” and lacks substantial interactions, with players primarily using a single button to play it.

This isn't a completely surprising revelation, as Marvel 1943 was billed as a narrative-driven, linear single-player experience when it was revealed, but the style of game has never been clear. The same reveal also saw Skydance promise the game boasted "intuitive controls and exhilarating second-to-second gameplay that captures the action and excitement of Marvel, inspired by landmark comics, television, and films, as they navigate this all-new globe-trotting adventure." This disconnect may be attributed to Kotaku also reporting that Marvel 1943 is “well over budget,” and its overall scope has been heavily scaled back. Regardless, the outlet’s source claims the game is “close to done.”

Marvel 1943: Rise of Hydra was initially slated to launch in 2025. It was then delayed to early 2026 "to add more polish", according to Skydance, before being pushed again to an undetermined launch window. The game has no announced platforms.
