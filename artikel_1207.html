Crystal Palace launch appeal against Jean-Philippe Mateta's red card in Tottenham defeat - with France star facing three-match ban

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Published: 11:02 EDT, 3 November 2026 | Updated: 11:06 EDT, 3 November 2026

Crystal Palace have appealed Jean-Philippe Mateta's red card after he was dismissed against Tottenham Hotspur on Saturday.

Mateta was shown a straight red card for a late challenge on Micky van de Ven in the 13th minute of their 2-0 defeat at Spurs, catching the centre-back's achilles with his studs up.

Referee Michael Oliver initially issued a yellow card but was called to the monitor by VAR Jarred Gillett to upgrade it.

When advising on the decision, Gillett said: 'Point of contact is on the achilles, nowhere near the ball. He’s sprinting at speed and with intensity, the studs catch him flush on the achilles. I think it ticks all the boxes for serious foul play.'

Oliver then said over the tannoy: 'After review, Crystal Palace No.14 makes full, forceful contact with the studs on his opponent's achilles. The final decision is red card.’

The 29-year-old is facing a three-match ban but could see the suspension overturned or reduced if successful on appeal.

However, Sky Sports’ ref watch panel, former referee Dermot Gallagher, Tim Sherwood and Jay Boothroyd, unanimously agreed on Monday that a red card was the correct call.

Without Mateta, Eddie Nketiah is Palace’s only available striker with Jorgen Strand Larsen sidelined with an ankle injury. It comes as a blow to Eagles boss Pierre Sage, who is fighting to save his job heading into the international break.

Palace host Liverpool on Sunday, having won just one of their last six Premier League matches and languishing in the relegation zone.
