David Raya to miss Arsenal's Champions League tie against Borussia Dortmund after worrying injury against Newcastle - but Mikel Arteta insists No 1 WILL be fit for Man City clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Published: 09:10 EST, 23 November 2026 | Updated: 10:49 EST, 23 November 2026

Mikel Arteta has said the shoulder injury David Raya sustained against Newcastle on Saturday ‘doesn’t look too serious’, sparking hope that the goalkeeper could return in weeks.

Raya was forced off in the 70th minute of Saturday’s thrilling 2-2 draw at St James’ Park, hurting his left shoulder in a collision with William Osula.

He looked in considerable pain as he was helped off the pitch by Arsenal‘s medical staff.

Arteta provided a promising update ahead of the Gunners’ Champions League match against Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday.

‘He’s not fit for tomorrow, but it doesn’t look too serious,’ said Arteta. ‘We need some more tests tomorrow, but it’s quite encouraging. It seems to be better than expected.

‘We expected something much worse because he could almost not handle the amount of pain he had. The first test shows something much more encouraging.

Arsenal's No 1 David Raya underwent an MRI scan the day after going off against Newcastle

Raya hit the turf in pain and went off the pitch clutching his arm to indicate that something serious had happened

‘There is no need for surgery, which was the main fear because that would have ruled him out for months.’

Raya did not turn out with his team-mates at Arsenal's training ground in the pre-match session open to the media ahead of the Gunners' clash with Borussia Dortmund.

Bukayo Saka and Martin Zubimendi were also not available for the open session with less than 24 hours until their meeting with the Germans.

Raya has been sensational for the Premier League champions and his absence is likely to hurt the best defensive unit.

'That's a massive worry,' Ian Wright said on Sky Sports when the stopper went down. 'I don't want to say I'm petrified simply because Kepa is in goal, he's the guy who has got to try and keep the goals out for us now.

'But yeah, that is a massive blow. Someone like him, Declan Rice, Gabriel, it is very tough to replace those guys.'

After their Champions League meeting on Tuesday, Arsenal face a crunch clash with Manchester City in the Premier League next Sunday.

The Gunners will play nine more games before the end of the year as they look to push forward in both the league and Champions League.
