Chelsea Owner Slammed For Response When Asked Why Blues Signed Alejandro Garnacho

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

It's been a terrible few days for Alejandro Garnacho's career and, after Unai Emery was criticised for his comments explaining why he snubbed the winger from Aston Villa's Champions League squad this week, Chelsea owner Behdad Eghbali has twisted the knife further by revealing the baffling reason the club signed the Argentine ahead of last season.

The 22-year-old made just 24 appearances during the 2025/26 season after forcing a move away from Manchester United, having overzealously felt he had outgrown the Red Devils.

He was subsequently sent out on loan to Villa for the current campaign - a decision by the Midlands club's hierarchy that made little sense given their previous issues with the Jadon Sancho and Harvey Elliott loan deals.

Emery effectively dismissed Garnacho by suggesting he wasn't good enough. The treatment feels all too familiar, but even more damning comments have since emerged from his parent club, prompting further sympathy for the winger while raising serious questions about Chelsea's transfer strategy - or complete lack thereof.

Towards the end of Garnacho's stay at Old Trafford, it would seem the whole world could see his attitude and performance issues meant that any potential suitors would be taking a gamble on the eight-cap Argentina international.

Simon Jordan, a pundit for TalkSport, revealed that he asked Behdad Eghbali why Chelsea signed Garnacho in the first place, despite the ominous red flags. His response was 'It was only £50 million', while he later revealed: "We interviewed him on Zoom."

His comments seemed to prove that the Blues' recruitment team didn't do much research before signing him, and it has left plenty of the Stamford Bridge faithful rightfully annoyed. One X user wrote: "‘[Roman] Abramovich sold the club to pure morons," as a second added: "Oh my gosh this is embarrassing." Meanwhile, a third remarked:

"These are the people running our football club.."

Emery's comments about Garnacho after he failed to make Aston Villa's squad for their clash against Galatasaray haven't gone down well.

"That's a disgrace to put it in this way," a fourth concluded, as the Blues also signed Jamie Gittens - who has failed to make a Premier League appearance under Xabi Alonso - as well as Jorrel Hato, Mamadou Sarr, Liam Delap, and Dario Essugo in the same summer.

Joao Pedro and Estevao are arguably the only two signings in the same period that worked out. But even then, the latter has struggled for fitness and currently doesn't look poised to reach his full potential.

The sudden increase in attention on Garnacho this week has since made him remove all mention of Aston Villa on his Instagram page - which some outlets are reporting as a 'subtle dig' at the club.

But his social media actions should paint a better picture of just how he is feeling right now as his career takes a massive pause. It should also shine a harsh light on both Villa and Chelsea's poor transfer business, as they continue to sign players their managers have no intention of giving a proper chance.
