Conference League line-ups: Borac vs. Atalanta

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Atalanta hope Gianluca Scamacca, Jonathan Rowe and Giacomo Raspadori can give them a much-needed Conference League boost away to Borac.

It kicks off at the Gradski Stadion Banja Luka in Banjaluka at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game, Olympiacos-Milan and Juventus-Omonia Nicosia on the Liveblog.

It has been a tough season for Atalanta, who on top of adapting to Maurizio Sarri’s radical different style of football, have also been constantly dealing with an injury crisis.

They are fresh off a remarkable comeback from 4-2 down to hold Inter 4-4 in Serie A at the weekend.

However, they also lost goalkeeper Marco Carnesecchi to a serious knee injury that will require surgery.

He joins Isak Hien, Odilon Kossounou and third-choice goalkeeper Thomas Pompei on the treatment table, so Marco Sportiello goes in goal.

There are several other changes, as Nicola Zalewski, Raspadori and Rowe fan out behind striker Scamacca, resting top scorer Nikola Krstovic.

The Bergamo boys need points after beating Pafos, then losing 2-0 away to Ajax and held 1-1 by Kairat Almaty.

FK Borac Banja Luka have so far secured one win and two defeats in the Conference League.

They have Srdan Grahovac not fully fit, and Stefan Savic is a long-term absentee with a torn Achilles tendon.

Borac: Jurkas; Cipetic, Meijers, Carolina, Herrera;  Joijc, Deket, Hiros; Sijoric, Juricic, Vukovic

Atalanta: Sportiello; Zappacosta, Scalvini, Kolasinac, Bernasconi; Ederson, Pasalic; Zalewski, Raspadori, Rowe; Scamacca
