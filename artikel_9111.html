CyberLink's Director Suite is a more affordable alternative to Adobe's Creative Cloud package. It doesn't include as many programs and services, but it covers everything that most content creators need: namely video editing, photo editing and audio mixing through three integrated apps: PowerDirector, PhotoDirector and AudioDirector.

If you only want video editing, there's no need to pay for the whole Director package. A subscription to PowerDirector 365 alone now costs just $54.99 for the first year in Cyberlink's Black Friday sale. This includes all the same access to generative AI video editing tools.

CyberLink's Director Suite includes PowerDirector 365, which has long been our top pick for beginners in our guide to the best video editing software. Its handy templates, ready-to-go effects and AI agent make it easy to get started editing your own video footage, and there are also generative AI tools that let you generate new video footage from text prompts or image references (also see our pick of the best AI video generators).

The software features AI object and background removal, motion tracking and reframing. AI tools also allow you to edit videos by simply describing the look you want and uploading a reference photo for the AI to recreate the style.

You also get all the standard video editing tools like keyframe editing, speed controls, stabilisation, lens correction, blending models and green screen for effects and compositing.
