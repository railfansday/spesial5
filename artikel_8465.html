Open Relay if your business budgets by setting money aside, since its free plan gives you separate checking accounts for taxes, owner’s pay, and profit, with rules that split incoming money among them. Upgrade to a paid plan only if you keep enough in savings for its higher rate to cover the monthly fee.

Relay is an online business banking platform whose free Starter plan gives a small business up to 20 checking accounts (10 for a sole proprietorship) to keep money for taxes, owner’s pay, and profit apart. By our math at the rates Relay posted in September 2026, the extra interest that savings earn on the paid Grow and Scale plans covers their $30 and $90 monthly fees only once the balance reaches about $53,000.

The free Starter plan covers small businesses that budget by account or give employees cards with spending limits. A business that deposits cash every day is better off at a branch bank, and a freelancer who needs just one account can compare the best bank accounts for freelancers.
