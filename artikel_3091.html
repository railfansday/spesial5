Reservoir Media (RSVR) Q2 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Streaming growth and catalog deals drove results, and full-year guidance moved up.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Reservoir Media, Inc. (RSVR +5.18%) reported financial results for the second fiscal quarter of 2027, showing growth in both its music publishing and recorded music segments. Management raised and narrowed its full-year guidance for revenue and Adjusted EBITDA following performance in the first half of the fiscal year. The company's recent strategic activity included the acquisition of music catalogs from José Feliciano and Billy Strayhorn and several new talent signings. An independent special committee formed in March 2026 continues to evaluate previously received unsolicited and non-binding acquisition proposals.

In the second fiscal quarter, we extended our run of top-line growth with revenue up 10% year-over-year and organic growth of 4%. In September, we announced a futures publishing deal with London-based songwriter and producer Fred Ball. Fred co-wrote and co-produced Rihanna's diamond-certified "Love on the Brain," and his work spans records with Beyoncé, Jay Z, Eminem, Alicia Keys, Mariah Carey, and Olivia Dean, among many others.

We also signed Grammy-nominated songwriter and producer Jake Sinclair to a publishing deal for his future works. Jake has written and produced hits for Fall Out Boy, Weezer, Five Seconds of Summer, and Panic! At the Disco, including their diamond-certified track "High Hopes." Alongside the publishing deal, we formed a joint venture with Jake to identify and develop emerging songwriters and artists, combining Jake's creative instincts with Reservoir's global platform and publishing expertise. We believe this approach can generate attractive opportunities to build incremental value for both Reservoir and Jake by further aligning our interests.

We also expanded our frontline roster across key genres and markets. In the U.K., we welcomed Scottish indie rock band The Snuts and Ivor Novello-winning singer-songwriter Orla Gartland. Across country and folk music, we announced deals with songwriter Justin Dukes, singer-songwriters Ava Liv Mabry and Riley Biederer, and singer-songwriter duo The Reklaws. Together, these signings demonstrate our continued commitment to developing a globally diverse roster of talent. By investing in creators across genres, geography, and career stages, we strengthen the range of opportunities available across our publishing business. Beyond our active roster, we continue to build on longstanding relationships with established catalogs and their representatives.

During the quarter, we announced the acquisition of Billy Strayhorn's publishing catalog, in addition to participating in rights to his name, image, and likeness. Widely regarded as one of the great composers in jazz history, Strayhorn was Duke Ellington's longtime creative partner and wrote enduring standards, including "Take the A Train" and "Lush Life." Reservoir has published his catalog for more than a decade, working closely with his estate to champion his music, and we are honored to continue to steward his legacy in the years to come.

In recorded music, We expanded our portfolio through a deal with legendary Puerto Rican singer-songwriter José Feliciano to acquire master recording rights to his catalog, as well as shared rights to his name, image, and likeness. Over a career spanning six decades, José has released more than 60 albums, crossing over into Anglo-American pop and earning multiple Grammy Awards, a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame, and the National Medal of Arts, among numerous other honors. He is also the writer and original recording artist behind "Feliz Navidad," one of the world's most enduring and widely played holiday songs to this day.

The recording, which returns to the charts each holiday season and recently reached number six on the Hot 100, was inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame in 2010, and just this year, was selected by the Library of Congress for preservation in the National Recording Registry. This deal builds upon our existing investments in Latin music, which remains one of the fastest-growing areas of global music demand. José's remarkable catalog combines lasting cultural relevance with continued audience engagement, reflecting our strategy of partnering with influential artists and investing in music with the potential to resonate across generations and markets.

Notably, through the deals with Billy Strayhorn and José Feliciano, Reservoir is taking part in the NIL value chain, a facet of transactions we have leveraged throughout the last 12 months during the Miles Davis centennial. Introducing Miles and his music to new fans through key brand partnerships has been an important way of continuing to bring value to his portfolio, both culturally and financially. Structuring deals with NIL components is a thoughtful way we continue to seek opportunities to enhance the value of Reservoir's assets and share them to a wider audience. In looking at the broader music landscape, several trends are particularly relevant to our business.

Subscription pricing continues to present opportunities for growth as streaming services implement price increases and consumers consistently demonstrate the value they place on access to music. Additionally, sentiment around catalog transactions remains positive, reinforcing our view that well-managed catalogs are durable, long-life assets. Third, for many established artists, touring and brand partnerships now drive more of their economics than a traditional record contract. We remain focused on positioning Reservoir to participate in this growth while maintaining the financial discipline that has guided our investment decisions.

Reservoir's business is rooted in our responsibility to protect the rights and creative legacies of our songwriters, artists, estates, and partners. Jim Heindlmeyer: Thank you, Golnar, and good morning, everyone. Our strong second fiscal quarter results reflect our impressive catalog of in-demand assets as we continue acquiring more catalogs, deepen existing relationships, and identify opportunities. Revenue for the second fiscal quarter was $49.9 million, a 4% year-over-year improvement on an organic basis and a 10% increase when including acquisitions. This was led by the 18% growth in our Recorded Music segment and the 8% increase we had in Music Publishing. Turning to our operating expenses, the total cost of revenue increased 7% compared to the prior year quarter, while our administration expenses and amortization and depreciation costs grew 7% and 12% respectively versus the prior year.

The increase in administration expenses was driven by higher expenses within Music Publishing and Recorded Music segments. Amortization costs grew due to the acquisition of additional music catalogs. Looking at operating performance for the second quarter, OIBDA was $20.7 million, an increase of 14% year-over-year, and Adjusted EBITDA was also up 14% to $22.1 million compared to our Q2 in fiscal 2026. The increases in OIBDA and Adjusted EBITDA were due to higher revenues, partially offset by increases in cost of revenue and administration expenses, as I just mentioned. Interest expense was $7 million for the quarter versus $6.7 million in the prior year, driven primarily by increased debt balances used to fund acquisitions of music catalogs and writer signings.

Net income for the second quarter was approximately $4.6 million compared to net income of $2.2 million in the second quarter of fiscal 2026. The increase in net income was primarily driven by the increase in operating income and the higher gain on fair value of swaps and gain on foreign exchange, partially offset by increases in interest expense and income tax expense. This resulted in $0.07 diluted earnings per share for the quarter, up from a diluted earnings per share of $0.03 in the prior year quarter. Our weighted average diluted outstanding share count during the quarter was approximately 66.8 million. Now let's dive into our segment review for the quarter.

Music Publishing had an 8% increase in revenue versus the prior year quarter at $33.2 million, largely due to a 10% increase in digital revenue, which was driven by the acquisition of additional music catalogs and continued growth at music streaming services. Additionally, synchronization revenue expanded by 13% within Music Publishing, driven by the timing of licenses. Moving to our Recorded Music segment, we had an 18% increase to $15.4 million in revenue compared to our Q2 last year. This was driven by a 33% increase in digital revenue, which was also supported by the acquisition of additional music catalogs and continued growth at music streaming services.

Robust neighboring rights revenue, driven by the acquisition of additional music catalogs and new affiliations, also supported our strong growth in Recorded Music. Turning to our balance sheet. For the six months ended September 30th, 2026, cash provided by operating activities was $21.6 million, Which was a decrease of $3.7 million compared to the same period last year, primarily due to a change in cash used for working capital resulting from the timing of royalty payments to creators and payments of accounts payable, partially offset by the timing of royalty advance recoupments. We had total available liquidity of $113.9 million, consisting of $24.8 million of cash on hand and $89.2 million available under our revolver.

We will continue to review our outlook as the year progresses and look forward to providing an update when appropriate.
