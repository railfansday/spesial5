Grupo Financiero Galicia (GGAL +3.90%) reported third-quarter results marked by a sequential compression in margins as the Argentine economy transitions toward lower inflation and reduced nominal interest rates. Management reported that while headline profitability fell 16% from the second quarter, underlying operational metrics improved through reduced loan loss provisions and lower administrative expenses. The company stated that the integration of HSBC Argentina is contributing to significant efficiency gains, with group expenses falling 36% year over year. The group maintained high liquidity and a strong capital position, which management stated provides strategic optionality to accelerate growth as credit demand recovers.
