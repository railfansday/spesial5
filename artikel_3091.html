Greg Abel's Berkshire Hathaway Owns 37% of This Company. Here's Why the Stock Is Also a Buy for Retail Investors.

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Sirius XM has performed well in 2026, and it has a deal in place that could generate even more meaningful revenue in the years ahead.

Berkshire Hathaway (BRKA +2.60%) (BRKB +1.41%) CEO Greg Abel's tenure is being defined by aggressive moves, with one of the most recent being the addition of more shares of Sirius XM Holdings (SIRI -1.32%) to the company's portfolio.

As revealed in a Form 4 filing, from Oct. 30 to Nov. 3, Berkshire purchased approximately $27 million worth of Sirius XM shares. By Berkshire's standards, that may sound small, with Sirius XM's weight in its portfolio at only 1.1%.

That said, the bigger vote of confidence from Abel's company is that it now owns 37.4% of the satellite radio provider. And as we head into 2027, investors may also want to consider Berkshire's moves and add the stock, or more of it, to their own portfolios.

Berkshire Hathaway CEO Greg Abel. Image source: Getty Images.

Over the last five years, Sirius XM has faced increased competition, a declining customer base, rising content costs, and a difficult market for acquiring new customers.

Heading into 2027 and beyond, while the stock price might not climb more than 60% each year, there's a reason Sirius XM could continue to be a long-term winner.

In April, Sirius XM struck a deal with Alphabet to become the exclusive advertising representative of YouTube's audio ad inventory in the United States. While YouTube was essentially just a platform to upload videos in its early days, it's become a more comprehensive destination for entertainment, ranging from live sports to podcasts to music. And it's a huge market for advertisers to reach. "According to a study from SiriusXM Media and Edison Research, there are more than 212 million monthly listeners in the U.S. engaging in audio-first content or environments on YouTube," Sirius said in a press release.

This deal won't be massively transformative right away, but by 2029, Deutsche Bank analysts estimate the partnership could generate $2 billion in incremental annual revenue for Sirius. For a company that generated $8.5 billion in 2025, that's meaningful.

If Sirius XM can set up other deals like this, it could ultimately become a profitable toll collector in the audio advertising space rather than competing in the crowded audio entertainment sector.
