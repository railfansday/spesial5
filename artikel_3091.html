ESPN’s ‘Monday Night Football’ Interrupted As Lights Go Out During Bengals-Commanders Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

ESPN‘s Monday Night Football went dark as the Cincinnati Bengals played the Washington Commanders.

The NFL game was paused shortly after the second half began due to a blackout at Northwest Stadium in Maryland.

As the stadium went dark, images of players like Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow appeared on screen as he continued to practice his throw.

“Back in the day, when the lights would go out, it would take forever to get the lights going,” the ESPN broadcaster said during the live game. Watch on Deadline

He continued, “We’re being told that it is power-related and not the stadium.

The only lights in the stadium that were on were the Jumbotron and the screens around the stadium with advertising. Fans tried to help by turning on their phones’ flashlights, but that wouldn’t have been enough to light up the stadium.

ESPN took a commercial break while stadium officials worked to fix the lights, and when they returned, the stadium was lit up again to continue the third quarter, which was 10-7 with the Bengals leading.

“The lights in the stadium went out at the beginning of the third quarter of Bengals-Commanders,” read the ESPN social media post shared on X, the microblogging platform formerly known as Twitter.
