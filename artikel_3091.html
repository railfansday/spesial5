NorthWestern Energy Group, Inc. (NWE +0.02%) focused the third quarter on finalizing its merger with Black Hills Corporation while managing the financial integration of expanded ownership in the Colstrip generation facility. Management affirmed its standalone 2026 earnings guidance but cautioned that results are trending toward the lower end of the range due to unfavorable market power prices and mild weather patterns in the Pacific Northwest. The company is advancing a multiyear capital plan centered on regional transmission development and new natural gas generation in South Dakota to meet updated resource adequacy requirements. Strategic efforts also include the development of a large new load tariff in Montana to facilitate the integration of energy-intensive data center customers into the regulated rate base.
