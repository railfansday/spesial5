Marzetti: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Chick-fil-A's permanent spicy sauce extends a 43-year partnership, potentially giving Marzetti a foodservice anchor that retail volatility cannot easily shake.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 22, 2026 at 5:15PM EST

On its fiscal year 2027 first-quarter conference call, held on Nov. 3, 2026, Marzetti (MZTI -0.81%) announced a net sales decline of 2.4% to $481.5 million driven by lower organic volume. Long-term shareholders must weigh temporary supply disruptions against the company's growing retail distribution and licensing momentum.

Retail segment sales grew 2% to $253 million, supported by $17.8 million from Bachan's, the company's newly acquired Japanese barbecue sauce brand. Demand for the brand is accelerating on store shelves as sales velocity outpaces total distribution points (TDPs), a measure of store availability.

National restaurant chain accounts represent a foundational growth channel for the Foodservice segment. Management expanded its 43-plus-year partnership with Chick-fil-A by manufacturing a new menu item distributed to locations nationwide.

Higher diesel fuel costs and linehaul transportation rates created a 1.2-percentage-point margin headwind in the first quarter. Management negotiated price increases across grocery aisles to protect gross margin, which measures profit after direct production costs.

Higher transport costs continue to pressure near-term operations, but strong retail distribution and pricing discipline protect fundamental value.

Management expects operational efficiency programs and new product launches to restore margin expansion later in the fiscal year.
