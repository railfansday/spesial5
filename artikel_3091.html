Amazon Adds Uber Eats to Prime Member Perks

The new delivery perk is available to Prime members through Amazon's app and website.

Amazon has a new meal delivery partner to offer Prime members free delivery: Uber Eats. If you’re always overpaying because of Uber Eats’ or Amazon’s delivery fees, an Amazon Prime membership may be worth it — especially before Black Friday deals sales start.

The new perk also comes with $20 off the first two Uber Eats eligible orders of $25 or more for the first 14 days, plus Amazon-exclusive offers. And if you have a Prime Visa credit card, you’ll earn 5% back on all Uber Eats orders until Dec. 5 with a Prime membership. You’ll need to order your meals through Amazon’s app or website to get the savings.

In order to get free delivery and $20 off your order, go to the Uber Eats tab at the top of your Amazon app or Uber Eats on Amazon’s website. Place your order as usual with a participating restaurant but make sure the $20 discount applies at checkout.

I tried the new perk and promo out for Saturday’s dinner. However, I will say the tip, a nearly $10 service fee, tax and the delivery fee all added up to the cost of another meal — which is worth paying attention to when you order.

But you may wonder what that means for the GrubHub partnership that both Amazon and GrubHub touted for years — dating back to 2022. Before now, your Prime subscription included a GrubHub Plus membership at no additional cost. GrubHub Plus included no delivery fees, lower service fees and 5% back on pickup orders.

The new partnership launched the same day that Lyft and DoorDash announced that they’re teaming up for a new subscription, Lyft Go. An Amazon spokesperson said in an email to CNET that the GrubHub and Amazon partnership ended on Thursday. There’s no telling how long Amazon will partner with Uber Eats.

“We continuously evaluate our third-party offers to ensure they provide the best possible value for Prime members,” the spokesperson said. “As part of that ongoing evaluation, we identified an opportunity to deliver an even better restaurant delivery experience.”

However, a GrubHub spokesperson said in an email to CNET that if you have a Prime membership and activated GrubHub Plus, you’ll have the membership until Dec. 31, 2027.

GrubHub shared a joint statement from Amazon and GrubHub in an email to CNET:

On Nov. 5, Grubhub and Amazon have decided to end the Prime-exclusive Grubhub Plus offer, and Amazon customers will no longer be able to place Grubhub orders through Amazon.com or the Amazon Shopping app. Amazon and Grubhub are proud of the partnership and the value provided to Prime members over the years. As the agreement concludes, both companies remain committed to serving our customers and delivering a great customer experience.

The new Uber Eats perk is now available to all Prime members, including those with Prime Access and Prime for Young Adults. And if you’re looking for another dining option, your Amazon Prime membership still offers free same-day delivery on perishable grocery orders over $25 when you order from Amazon Grocery.
