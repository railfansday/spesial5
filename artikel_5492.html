CSMI Selected by the State of Connecticut to Support Defense CyberSecure Program CMMC Initiatives

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

SALEM, N.H., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- CSMI has been selected by the State of Connecticut Office of Manufacturing as an advisory council member for the Defense CyberSecure Program (CT-DCP) to support the development and execution of cybersecurity strategies for Connecticut's defense and aerospace manufacturing sector.

"CSMI brings an exceptional depth of experience, and a demonstrated understanding of what manufacturers need to navigate the CMMC process, with a particular sensitivity to the needs of small to medium-sized manufacturers," said Michelle Hall, Director, Office of Manufacturing, Connecticut Economic and Community Development. "Their technical expertise, industry relationships, and commitment to hands-on support makes them an exceptional choice as an advisory council member and trusted service provider. They will be invaluable in helping Connecticut manufacturers strengthen their cybersecurity capabilities, protect sensitive information, and remain competitive in the defense supply chain."

Through the CT-DCP initiative, CSMI will help manufacturers access funding, training, technical assistance, and qualified cybersecurity providers as they work toward Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) Level 2. Achieving certification is essential for organizations seeking to maintain eligibility for current and future Department of War contracts.

"Our goal is to ensure that Connecticut suppliers have access to a full complement of tools, guidance, and support, necessary to continue their crucial roles in support of the U.S. Warfighter," said Christopher Knox, CSMI Senior Program Manager and CT-DCP Board Member.

CSMI currently conducts cybersecurity uplift programs for suppliers throughout the U.S. Defense Industrial Base (DIB), as well as international partners by performing initial gap analysis and all aspects of supplier preparation for final CMMC accreditation. This skillset will help Connecticut manufacturers strengthen cybersecurity practices, develop policies and procedures, safeguard sensitive data, and meet applicable defense contracting requirements. CSMI, as a Managed Services Provider (MSP), provides CMMC compliant network support services which covers a significant number of CMMC Level 2 controls, enhances network security and greatly impacts achievement of CMMC certification.

About CSMI - Founded in 2002, CSMI provides cybersecurity consultancy, training, mission support, and integrated logistics services to the U.S. Government, defense industry, and allied nations. With experience in over 30 countries, 5 continents, afloat, & domestically, CSMI leverages its vast expertise to advance the defense industrial base and U.S. national security interests worldwide.
