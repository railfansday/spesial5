LateralCare Announces Six New Strategic Partnerships to Expand Access to AI-Native Revenue Cycle Management Nationwide

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Healthcare associations across five states recognize the potential of revenue alai by LateralCare to transform revenue cycle operations, strengthen financial performance, and empower their members with purpose-built AI.

DALLAS, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- LateralCare, a healthcare technology company advancing AI-native revenue cycle management (RCM), is proud to announce six new strategic partnerships with leading healthcare associations across the United States. These partnerships mark a significant milestone in LateralCare's continued growth and reflect a shared commitment to bringing meaningful innovation to healthcare organizations through its flagship platform, revenue alai by LateralCare.

The newest strategic partners include the Dallas-Fort Worth Hospital Council, Hospital Council of Northern & Central California, Michigan State Medical Society, Nassau-Suffolk Hospital Council, Northern Metropolitan Hospital Association, and South Florida Hospital & Healthcare Association.

Together, these organizations serve healthcare providers across Texas, California, Michigan, New York, and Florida, representing diverse healthcare environments and a common priority: helping their members navigate growing operational complexity while building stronger, more sustainable financial futures.

At the center of these partnerships is a shared belief that the future of revenue cycle management requires more than incremental improvements. It requires a fundamentally more intelligent approach.

As healthcare organizations face mounting administrative demands, evolving payer requirements, staffing challenges, and increasing pressure to protect revenue, the need for practical, scalable AI solutions has never been greater. Each association recognizes the potential of revenue alai by LateralCare to help its members address these challenges, improve operational performance, and capture more of the revenue they have earned.

Unlike traditional RCM technologies that address individual problems in isolation, revenue alai by LateralCare brings AI-native intelligence across the revenue cycle, connecting contracts, claims, billing, denials, audits, and financial insights through a unified approach.

Powered by Agentic AI, the platform helps healthcare organizations identify reimbursement risks earlier, prevent avoidable denials, strengthen billing accuracy, reduce administrative workloads, and uncover opportunities to improve revenue integrity. Its human-in-the-loop architecture combines intelligent automation with explainable decision-making and appropriate human oversight, helping teams operate with greater efficiency, visibility, and control.

Designed to meet organizations where they are, revenue alai offers the flexibility to address specific revenue cycle challenges through individual capabilities or support broader, end-to-end transformation as organizational needs evolve.

"Healthcare organizations don't need another technology promising transformation. They need solutions that can make a meaningful difference in how their revenue cycles operate today," said William Reau, Chief Executive Officer of LateralCare. "These six partnerships represent something incredibly important: healthcare associations recognizing that their members deserve access to more intelligent, proactive approaches to revenue cycle management. We're excited to work alongside these organizations to demonstrate what's possible when purpose-built AI helps providers protect revenue, empower their teams, and strengthen their financial futures."

Through these collaborations, LateralCare will engage with association members to share practical insights into AI-driven RCM, explore the operational challenges facing their organizations, and demonstrate how revenue alai can support their financial and operational priorities.

The partnerships also reflect a broader shift in healthcare's approach to artificial intelligence. As AI moves from discussion to practical implementation, healthcare leaders are increasingly looking for technologies that do more than automate existing processes. They want solutions that help their organizations anticipate problems, make better-informed decisions, and achieve measurable improvements.

For healthcare associations focused on connecting members with valuable resources and innovative solutions, revenue alai by LateralCare represents an opportunity to introduce technology designed specifically for the financial and operational realities of healthcare.

For LateralCare, the addition of six strategic partners reinforces growing momentum behind its vision for a more intelligent, connected, and sustainable revenue cycle.

"Our vision has always been to make advanced AI practical, accessible, and impactful for the healthcare organizations that need it," added Reau. "By collaborating with associations that understand the challenges their members face, we can bring that vision to more providers and help accelerate the adoption of technology that supports both financial resilience and the people delivering care."

As LateralCare continues to expand its network of strategic relationships, the company remains focused on a central objective: helping healthcare organizations move beyond reactive revenue cycle management toward a more proactive, AI-native future.

The next era of revenue cycle management is already here. Through these partnerships, more healthcare organizations will have the opportunity to experience it firsthand.
