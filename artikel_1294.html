Report Napoli reject PSV bid for De Bruyne

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Dutch newspaper De Telegraaf reports that Napoli have rejected an opening bid from PSV Eindhoven for Kevin De Bruyne worth €2m.

Napoli have reportedly rejected an opening offer for their midfielder De Bruyne.

According to De Telegraaf, the Partenopei have turned down a €2m bid from Dutch side PSV, who have already opened talks with the ex-Manchester City star.

De Bruyne’s contract at the Stadio Maradona expires at the end of the season, and the midfielder has recently refused to rule out a possible January switch to PSV.

According to the report, De Bruyne is open to moving to PSV in the January window, which would allow him and his family to move into the home they own in the Belgian Limburg.

It is still unclear whether Napoli will be open to selling the experienced midfielder in January.

According to De Telegraaf, the Partenopei will ask for a higher transfer fee, while Corriere dello Sport claims the Serie A side wants to retain De Bruyne until the end of the season.

Napoli hold an option to extend De Bruyne’s contract until 2028, while Gazzetta reports that Frank Zambo-Anguissa, rather than the Belgian, could leave in January, with Marseille interested in the Cameroonian midfielder.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
