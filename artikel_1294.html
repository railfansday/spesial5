Official: Chivu wins Best Serie A Coach Award for 2025-26

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Inter coach Cristian Chivu won the Panchina d’Oro award in Coverciano for the best Serie A coach in the 2025-26 season.

The prizes for the best coaches in Italian football for the 2025-26 campaign were awarded today in Coverciano, with Football Italia in attendance.

Chivu beat Roma’s Gian Piero Gasperini (third) and Cesc Fabregas (second) to win the “Panchina d’Oro” for the first time.

Chivu was awarded after winning the domestic double—the Scudetto and Coppa Italia—in his first full season as a Serie A coach. Inter hired him in the summer of 2025 to replace Simone Inzaghi.

Coaches in each category determined the winners with their votes. The votes for Serie C, B and A took place in the morning in Coverciano.

“Thanks to you all for this award,” Chivu said.

“It’s always special to receive an award given by colleagues. I thank the technical sector, the coaches, my club, the players, and the staff. The players are those who turn our ideas into results. Last but not least, I want to thank my family, who always support me when I win, but mostly when I lose.”

Among the winners were Massimiliano Alvini of Frosinone as Best Serie B coach and Francesco Tomei of Ascoli as Best Serie C coach.

Below are all the winners at the Panchina d’Oro in Coverciano

Futsal Women Serie A: Cataldo Guarino (Bitonto C5)

Futsal Men Serie A: Diego Rios (L84 Torino)

Serie B Women: Selena Mazzantini (Como 1907)

Special Panchina d’Oro: Francesco Farioli (Porto FC) and Daniele Franceschini (Italy U17)

Best Youth Sector Chief: Matteo Silvestri (Empoli, now Sassuolo)

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
