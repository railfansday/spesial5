Kendall Jenner Isn&#8217;t as Naked as Her Tequila in 818&#8217;s Biggest Campaign Yet

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Kendall Jenner’s 818 Tequila is stripping it down to the basics in its first-ever product spot and biggest brand push yet.

Created by Nice & Frank and produced by OBB Bolded, “Nothing Extra.” highlights 818’s three all-natural ingredients: agave, water, and yeast.

Shot at Flamingo Estate in Southern California, the spot opens with Kendall Jenner walking through a beautiful and lush garden, hidden by flora with only her bare shoulders and legs suggestively revealed. “Some things don’t need dressing up,” a voiceover says. “They’re better stripped down.”

Finally, Jenner steps out from the brush to reveal she’s “not naked at all,” fully clothed in an off-the-shoulder dress.
