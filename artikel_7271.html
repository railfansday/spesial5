Stripe: Account Executive, Product Sales (Terminal), Enterprise Retail

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

Product Sales is a team of deep functional and technical specialists who both lead and advise on deals for the most strategically important products in Stripe’s portfolio.  The Terminal Product Sales is tasked with attacking a $15T market opportunity for unified online and offline commerce. The team is lean, high visibility and highly cross-functional!

As an Account Executive, Product (Terminal) focused on Enterprise Retail, you will use your sales and product expertise to drive growth of Stripe's Unified Commerce suite of hardware, software and payments solutions among North America's largest retailers. You will partner with product, engineering and marketing teams to build deep product and industry knowledge, and you will lead strategic, multi-stakeholder deals with Fortune 100-500 retail brands. You will lead in-depth product discussions with existing and prospective customers and help build and execute our go-to-market strategy for the retail vertical.

You are an adept salesperson, able to hold business-level and technical conversations at every level of a large organization, up to the C-suite (CEO, CFO, CIO and CTO). You are used to building value in competitive, long-cycle enterprise situations and enjoy working on products that require deep product understanding combined with technical knowledge. You are as comfortable working with clients as with internal stakeholders, including executives. You are excited to become an expert and share your knowledge. You are an organized self-starter and an excellent communicator who is highly analytical and has a track record of improving the processes and organizations around you.

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.
