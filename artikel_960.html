England to face Norway in Nations League last eight

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

England have been drawn to face Norway in the UEFA Nations League quarter-finals; the quarter-finals will be two-leg ties and will take place in the March international break; Wales will face Slovenia and Republic of Ireland will take on Croatia in the A/B play-offs

England will take on Erling Haaland and Norway in the Nations League quarter-finals.

Thomas Tuchel's side finished top of their group thanks to their dramatic 3-2 win over Spain at the Metropolitano Stadium, while Norway finished second behind Portugal in Group D.

The two sides met in the 2026 World Cup quarter-finals, with a Jude Bellingham goal in extra-time sending the Three Lions through to the last four.

Should England progress, they will meet Belgium or Germany in the semi-finals. The other two quarter-final ties have seen world champions Spain drawn with France and Netherlands take on Portugal.

The quarter-finals will be two-leg ties and will take place in the March international break, with the first legs between March 25-27 and the second legs between March 28-30.

This will then be followed by the Nations League Finals, which will be held between June 9 and June 13, 2027.

The tournament will feature two semi-finals, a third-place play-off and the final. The four semi-finalists will be invited to make bids to host the semi-finals and final.

Wales and the Republic of Ireland have also learned who they will face in the Nations League A/B play-offs.

Wales avoided automatic relegation to League B after securing one of the two better last place finishers in League A, while Republic of Ireland finished runners-up in their League B group behind Austria.

Craig Bellamy's side will take on Slovenia, while Heimir Hallgrimsson's men will face Croatia.

The play-off ties will also take place during the March international break.
