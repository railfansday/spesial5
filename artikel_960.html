'Monaco unrest as they prepare to face Hearts' - gossip

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Mamadou Coulibaly and Folarin Balogun are being linked with January transfers

Speculation surrounds the future of two top Monaco players as they prepare to face Heart of Midlothian, while the Scottish Premiership's top summer free agents are highlighted.

Hearts' Conference League opponents, Monaco, have been plunged into uncertainty with striker Folarin Balogun saying he wants to leave in January amid links to Manchester United, while midfielder Mamadou Coulibaly is attracting interest from Napoli. (Edinburgh Evening News, external)

Monaco's 25-year-old American striker, Folarin Balogun, whose deadline-day move to Everton collapsed, is in talks with AC Milan and Juventus over a January deal. (Calciomercato, external)

Celtic winger James Forrest and right-back Tony Ralston, Rangers defender Dujon Sterling, Motherwell forward Tawanda Maswanhise and left-back Emmanuel Longelo, Hearts striker James Wilson, Dundee United wing-back Will Ferry, St Mirren midfielder Killian Phillips, Hibernian wing-back Nicky Cadden, Falkirk full-back Keelan Adams and Kilmarnock striker Tyreece John-Jules are among Scottish Premiership players out of contract next summer who could attract January transfer interest. (The Herald, external)

Former Celtic centre-half Ramon Vega has revealed he had agreed a transfer switch to Real Madrid only for the deal to be ripped up while on a flight to Spain because manager Fabio Capello was sacked. (Glasgow Times, external)

Dunfermline Athletic head coach Neil Lennon is happy to entrust Sam Cleall-Harding's rehabilitation to parent club Dundee United as the 20-year-old on-loan defender recovers from a serious hamstring injury. (The Courier, external)

Stefan Wolf, who was president when new Aberdeen head coach Mario Frick was appointed by Luzern, has revealed the former Liechtenstein player's trust in youth proved to be of great financial importance to the Swiss club. (Press & Journal, external)

Former Rangers manager Graeme Souness, who was part of the executive team responsible for finding a replacement for Michael Beale, has revealed he backed Frank Lampard, now with Coventry City, to become team boss before Philippe Clement was appointed because of the ex-England midfielder's experience and Chelsea and Manchester City connections. (FourFourTwo via Glasgow Times, external)

Ally McCoist does not harbour a feeling of "what if" about becoming Rangers manager at a time of financial turmoil despite believing he was "in the right place at the wrong time". (Daily Record, external)

St Johnstone centre-half Bozo Mikulic has made himself available to face Falkirk despite breaking his nose against Dundee and the injury plagued 29-year-old has declined the offer of a custom-fitted mask. (The Courier, external)

Celtic midfielder Benjamin Nygren is an injury doubt for Sweden's Nations League match in Poland on Tuesday after he missed their final training session. (The National, external)

St Johnstone chairman Adam Webb is sticking with his decision to hand three stands to visiting Celtic and Rangers fans as it has already proved lucrative but is open to tweaking some other controversial ticketing changes. (The Courier, external)
