Grading quarterback Jared Curtis after Vanderbilt football loss to Alabama

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Vanderbilt football lost to Alabama 21-6 at FirstBank Stadium on Nov. 14, failing to score a touchdown for the first time this season.

The Commodores (2-6, 1-6 SEC) played well defensively, but the offense had only 39 rushing yards (not including sacks) and the team committed 14 penalties for 92 yards. Freshman quarterback Jared Curtis had to produce most of the offensive yards himself.

All six points came on a pair of 50-plus yard field goals from Brock Taylor.

Curtis was 25-for-40 passing for 223 yards. He didn't throw any touchdowns or commit any turnovers.

He was sacked five times for 27 yards. He ran four times for 9 yards.

Curtis had an uneven performance against Alabama. On one hand, he didn't commit any turnovers, something that hurt him against Kentucky and Arkansas. However, his efficiency wasn't what it was in some of the earlier games.

He made only one throw of more than 20 yards, missed some open receivers and sometimes completed throws that didn't gain much yardage. He also threw out of the end zone on fourth-and-4, down two touchdowns, when any attempt to make a play would've been better than throwing it away.

Curtis didn't lose Vanderbilt the game − the bigger issues were penalties and an inability to run the ball − but he didn't do anything to win it, either.
