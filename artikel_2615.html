Cowboys' offense rolling over Eagles' defense in Dallas

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Cowboys are stuffing the ball down the Eagles’ throats on Thanksgiving.

Dallas has a 21-3 lead late in the second quarter, and Philadelphia has had no answer for running back Javonte Williams and wide receivers CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens.

It hasn’t helped that after Eagles defensive back Cooper DeJean was ruled out with a concussion, Eagles running back Saquon Barkley (stinger) and tight end Dallas Goedert (shoulder) both exited the game and were listed as questionable to return. Goedert was later ruled out.

Eagles quarterback Joe Milton is doing his best to move the ball without some of his top weapons, but the injury-plagued Eagles are struggling.
