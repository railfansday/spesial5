Kyle Shanahan has bold response to this 49ers team being his best ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Is the 2026 version of the 49ers the best team Kyle Shanahan has coached?

It's a fair question given the way San Francisco played Sunday night when they thumped the 7-2 Minnesota Vikings, 35-10, and firmly planted their flag for the No. 1 seed in the NFC.

But Shanahan has coached two teams that have earned the conference's top seed previously, in 2019 and 2023, and both of those teams reached the Super Bowl.

San Francisco 49ers running back Christian McCaffrey (23) celebrates after scoring a touchdown against the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Fernando Llano

This team is among the Super Bowl favorites coming out of the week at 9-1. They have steamrolled through the NFL this season, including against its stiffest competition. But Shanahan says there's a long way to go before determining if this is the best team he's had since becoming the 49ers coach in 2017.

"No, but it's still early. We got to earn that," Shanahan told reporters. "It's halfway through the year, we've done some good things, but it's still to be determined.

"A team's (season) is seven months long. We know how we've been so far. But we're halfway there and we have a long way to go."

San Francisco 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan answers a question after an NFL football game against the Dallas Cowboys in Arlington, Texas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/Julio Cortez

Shanahan is revealing but also cagey when he speaks to the media.

He likely has a strong opinion of how this team would stack up against his previous Super Bowl teams, but it wouldn't be wise to speak in those terms with so many games remaining. Shanahan is the type to make those proclamations after a season rather than during it.

But with wins over the Rams, Seahawks, Broncos and Vikings, the 49ers' resume speaks for itself as they head down the stretch of the regular season. And given Shanahan has a 9-5 playoff record, there's no doubt San Francisco will be formidable in the playoffs if they can remain healthy.

San Francisco 49ers' Mike Evans in action during an NFL game against the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. JOSE MENDEZ/EPA/Shutterstock

The talent on both sides of the ball is staggering. They have Pro Bowlers and All-Pro players all over the field, which has been evident through the first 10 games.

The 49ers are the overwhelming favorites to earn the top seed following Sunday's win. But there are seven games left in the regular season and a lot can happen that could change the team's fortunes dramatically.
