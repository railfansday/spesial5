The Colts should “evaluate” Shane Steichen

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

"We'll evaluate everything." Have you heard that before? It's becoming a canned phrase and mantra for Indianapolis Colts head coach Shane Steichen. The most recent example comes after the Colts were ripped apart by the Houston Texans on Thursday Night Football. That's a smart move, Shane. Maybe the Colts organization needs to do the same for you too.

The Colts sit at 3-8 on the year, and Steichen's overall record is 28-34. The man who was brought in due to his success with Jalen Hurts and the Philadelphia offense was unable to mold Anthony Richardson in the same fashion or create results. Zero postseason appearances and a 7-25 record when the opponent is over .500 make the case why he needs to be heavily scrutinized.

It is wise to consider benching Daniel Jones. Does it get better? Maybe not. That's on Chris Ballard and Steichen, however. Steichen had a choice between the two men at quarterback. Not that his options were necessarily glowing, but he picked Jones to be his guy. From the onset, it looked like a stroke of genius. Now, it looks disastrous. He can evaluate all he wants. The Colts organization should do the same. It isn't just Steichen, but it starts there. Based on the last four years of data, the case can be made that a change in leadership is needed.

Evaluating is normally a good thing. What can trip one up is the timing. Shane Steichen's "evaluation" of Daniel Jones and the quarterback position might be too little, too late to save his job. Steichen's time with the franchise might be drawing to a close, but the Colts organization will live on. Their time for evaluation is now. It starts with the head coach.
