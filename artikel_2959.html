Management at Noah Holdings (NOAH -10.59%) reported a period of transition as the company adjusted to new cross-border regulatory frameworks in China while transitioning toward an AI-integrated service model. The company reported a decline in total revenue and operating profit, largely attributed to volatility in performance-based income and one-time costs associated with organizational restructuring. Management noted a strategic shift toward international markets, with the Singapore and Hong Kong offices serving as primary booking centers and pilots for new technology-driven client acquisition channels. The company maintained a debt-free balance sheet while continuing to distribute dividends and invest in expansion across Japan, Australia, and Canada.
