Celtics legend Kevin Garnett warns Stephen A. Smith about Lakers rant

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Polarizing media personality Stephen A. Smith raised eyebrows when he essentially called out the NBA for potentially letting the Los Angeles Lakers win a championship, given their roster's prevalence of white players.

Boston Celtics legend Kevin Garnett checked Smith for his sentiment — albeit not for the reason some might assume — during Thursday's episode of the "KG and Stephen A." show.

Luka Doncic (77) and Austin Reaves (15) of the Los Angeles Lakers look on during a game against the Toronto Raptors on Nov. 1, 2026, in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

"They making you look crazy. You talking all that s—t about them white boys, saying they can't win, and s—t," Garnett said.

"All I said was the three top dudes on a basketball team being white has never led an NBA team to a championship since the days of George Mikan. That is a fact! Nobody can dispute that s—t! I said it and I meant it. I ain't apologizing for it," Smith responded.

Stephen A. Smith speaking to Kevin Garnett during the "KG and Stephen A." show. (YouTube/@KG and Stephen A.)

Boston Celtics legend Kevin Garnett speaking to Stephen A. Smith on the "KG & Stephen A." show. (YouTube/@KG and Stephen A.)

"They finna change that. They are finna change that. You ain't got to apologize. They are finna change that though.

"I'm not asking you to back up on none of that. I'm saying check what you said, because this dude is averaging 40, Stephen A. 40!" Garnett responded.

"White boys" sentiment aside, it's fascinating to hear Garnett — whose Celtics beat the Lakers to win an NBA championship in 2008 before losing to them in the 2010 NBA Finals — say that Los Angeles is going to prove Smith wrong and win a title.
