Exiled Jack Grealish RETURNS to Everton training after being dropped following boozy nights at pubs and karaoke: Ex-England star could be back in team after international break

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Published: 12:34 EST, 6 November 2026 | Updated: 14:16 EST, 6 November 2026

Jack Grealish will return to Everton training after being dropped and exiled by David Moyes.

The 31-year-old was axed for back-to-back matches against Newcastle following a 'breach of club discipline' and is not part of the Toffees' matchday squad for the third game on the spin when they host Coventry tonight.

Grealish, who has made seven appearances since re-joining Everton for a second season-long loan spell from Man City in the summer, had been banished since failing to show up for a training session on Monday last week. It is understood staff struggled to make contact with the winger.

Grealish will now be reintegrated on a tailored training programme to regain match fitness and could be included in first-team sessions during the international break from next week.

Grealish had made four successive starts before he missed Everton's 2-0 defeat by Arsenal on October 24 due to illness.

Grealish was reported to have had back-to-back nights out prior to the Toffees' defeat in north London.

Jack Grealish, previously pictured out with a friend, was exiled by Everton but is now set to return to training with the team

David Moyes could start Grealish against Brentford after the international break

The three-time Premier League winner attended the Pride of Britain awards at the Grosvenor House hotel in London on October 19 where he presented the Child of Courage award to 12-year-old Zac Howells.

He is then reported to have finished the night of celebration off at Bam Karaoke Box, a karaoke hotspot in Victoria, central London.

According to the Sun, he stayed up until the early hours of the morning singing with his friends and enjoying the drinks on offer.

The next day, Grealish is believed to have returned to Cheshire, where he and many of his footballer friends live, to meet team-mates Jordan Pickford and Tyler Dibling at The Mauldeth pub in Wilmslow.

The trio, joined by Grealish's former City team-mate and captain Kyle Walker, where they watched Paris Saint-Germain's thrilling Champions League victory over Barcelona. A witness claimed to the Sun that they enjoyed a few drinks.

Moyes had been coy on the situation when asked about it on Thursday. He said: 'You probably know I'm not going to give any details, it is a club decision and I support that decision (for him to be punished). It is something I don't want to speak too much about.

'The one thing about Everton is that we have got really good values behind it. All the players stand up to the values and accept the values, on the pitch and off the pitch. From that point of view, I think the players did a brilliant job.'

Without Grealish once again, Everton are seeking to secure a third win in a row when they come up against former Toffees boss Frank Lampard and his Coventry team.

Grealish is reported to have gone on consecutive nights out after attending the Pride of Britain Awards on October 19

After the Coventry clash, Everton are next in Premier League action at Gtech Community Stadium against Brentford on November 23.

Grealish will be targeting a return in that fixture and hope he can begin to rebuild his relationship with Moyes over the next three weeks.

He has played for England 39 times and turned out for Everton on 22 occasions, scoring twice and providing six assists, with a nasty foot injury cut short his campaign last term.
