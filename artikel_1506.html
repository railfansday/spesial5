Bologna expected to interrupt Dovbyk’s loan

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Artem Dovbyk’s future could change as early as the January transfer window.

The Ukrainian striker, who joined Bologna on loan from Roma, has barely seen any playing time in the first half of the season, and the Rossoblu is considering ending his contract early.

As reported by Matteo Moretto on Fabrizio Romano’s YouTube channel, talks are expected between Roma and Bologna in the coming weeks to assess the striker’s situation.

The Emilian club is reportedly considering his departure, having developed concerns that Dovbyk isn’t the right striker for the team at this time, especially given the difficulties he’s encountered so far.

On the other hand, Roma hasn’t planned Dovbyk’s return in January.

The Giallorossi have in fact structured their annual sports program without considering the Ukrainian’s reinstatement mid-season.

Therefore, future discussions between the two clubs will clarify which solution to adopt ahead of the winter transfer window.
