“I always wanted more”, Jonathan Clauss opens up about his relationship and his infidelities

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Jonathan Clauss has made very personal confidences about his love life. In the documentary “Football and Sex: Impossible Love? », broadcast by L’Équipe, the OGC Nice player notably returned to his former infidelities and the difficulties encountered with Pauline, his wife.

Jonathan Clauss does not seek to avoid his responsibilities. “I couldn’t be completely satisfied with what I had. I always wanted more. As soon as I ended a relationship, I started a new one. Afterwards, there were lies, deceptions”he admits.

The footballer also describes a period during which he wanted to enjoy all aspects of his life without really measuring the consequences of his actions. “I wanted to go out, enjoy, be in a relationship, I wanted everything. I was a little idiot”he assumes.

He even recounts a particularly embarrassing anecdote, when a woman with whom he was in a relationship exposed him to his partner at the time. “Leaving the club, there was a girl I often slept with who was on FaceTime with my current girlfriend saying: “Look, Jo is with her, he’s chatting with her.” Pauline said to me: “What is this story? » »

Now married to Pauline for more than a year, Jonathan Clauss seems to have realized how far he has come. The 34-year-old French international notably measures the efforts made by his wife to preserve their relationship. “If we are here, it’s because she stayed. I’m grateful for the decisions she made for us because deep down, not everything is healed.”he confides.

A particularly personal speech, in which Jonathan Clauss recognizes his errors and underlines the decisive role of his wife in the reconstruction of their relationship.
