Svilar celebrates 100 appearances in Serie A

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Mile Svilar has reached 100 Serie A appearances for Roma.

He achieved this milestone at the Tardini with a fantastic performance.

The Giallorossi goalkeeper returned to action after an injury that forced him to miss nine games.

A long absence, he handled as if it hadn’t happened. Against Parma, he was decisive in the first half with a couple of miracles that crowned the milestone.

Svilar made his Serie A debut against Bologna on May 14, 2023.

He finished his first season with 3 appearances, rising to 15 in 2023/24. The turning point in his numbers came in the following two seasons, with 38 games in 2024/25 and the same number in 2025/26.

The 5 games this season, before the injury, had brought him to 99 in Serie A on the eve of the match at the Tardini.

In Svilar’s six Serie A matches in 2026/27, Roma have won five, drawn one, and lost zero. They have conceded four goals, and the goalkeeper has kept three clean sheets: Roma-Fiorentina 4-0, Lecce-Roma 0-4, Torino-Roma 0-2.
