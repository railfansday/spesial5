The clubs who’ve won the most league titles in Europe since GTA V was released

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A lot of time has passed between the releases of GTA V and GTA VI. Enough for some dominant clubs to claim double figures of league titles…

The latest Grand Theft Auto game is finally getting released this week after a 13-year wait. When its predecessor came out in September 2013, David Moyes was the Manchester United manager, and Steven Gerrard and John Terry were still captaining Liverpool and Chelsea respectively.

There’s been a lot of silverware to play for since.

Here are the clubs from Europe’s top 10 leagues with the most league titles won in the time between GTA5 and GTA6.

The Bundesliga is Bayern’s world and everyone else is just living in it.

They’ve lifted all but one of the past 13 league titles in the German top flight. The only one they didn’t, of course, was when Bayer Leverkusen went invincible.

It’s a similar story in France, where PSG are streets ahead of the competition – both financially and in terms of results.

Monaco and Lille are the only teams to stop them winning Ligue 1 in the past 13 years.

Olympiacos have never failed to finish in the top three in Greece in the corresponding timeframe.

They were already on three league titles in a row when GTA5 came out and went on to add another four to their consecutive total. AEK Athens and PAOK are the only sides to have broken their dominance since, too.

It was Atletico Madrid that won the first La Liga title post-GTA5, but Barcelona were back on their perch soon enough, claiming four of the next five.

Lionel Messi was like a cheat code, winning the golden boot in five consecutive seasons – even ones where either Madrid side won the league itself.

Barcelona have won three of the first five titles in the post-Messi era as well. Rivals Real Madrid only have four to their name from the past 13 years.

Club Brugge have cemented their status as the second most successful Belgian club for league titles won over the past decade or so, even if Anderlecht are still some way out in front.

Since ending a 10-year wait for the title in 2015-16, Club Brugge have never gone more than one season without winning the league again.

Fenerbahce won the first Turkish title after the release of GTA5. We’re slightly shocked they haven’t won it again since.

It’s no surprise, though, that their arch-rivals Galatasaray have been victorious plenty of times instead, while Besiktas have got their hands on a few titles too and there’s been one each for Trabzonspor and Istanbul Basaksehir.

Juventus had Serie A in a stranglehold in the 2010s, winning nine league titles in a row (seven of which were this side of the GTA5 release).

Funnily enough, they’ve not won it again since that streak ended, with Inter picking up three Scudetti, Napoli two and AC Milan one.

Man City won only their second Premier League title in 2013-14. They’ve since added another six, which kind of shows just how far back we’re talking.

Even Leicester have won the Premier League more recently than GTA5’s release and they’re in League One now.

There is currently something of an asterisk over Man City’s success after their guilty verdicts on 114 charges, but they were pretty much unstoppable on the pitch in their heyday under Pep Guardiola.

Chelsea, Liverpool and Arsenal have also been title winners in the same timeframe, but none more than twice.

No Portuguese club can rival Benfica’s 38 league titles. They began collecting them in the 1930s and haven’t lost their appetite.

They’ll be a bit miffed at only winning one of the past seven available, even though they’ve never dropped out of the top three. A four-in-a-row streak from 2013-14 to 2016-17 was more like it.

The past 15 Eredivisie titles have been shared between the three most successful clubs in the Netherlands: PSV, Ajax and Feyenoord.

Currently on a streak of three in a row, PSV have been playing catch-up with Ajax’s all-time tally in recent years. But Ajax themselves have won four titles in the same timeframe.
