Report: Liverpool transfer blow could lead to surprise January deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Liverpool's recruitment drive looks set to continue this winter.

The Reds have spent over £650 million since winning the Premier League in 2025, but the rebuild is far from complete.

Julian Ward has taken the reigns within the Anfield recruitment team and work is now being done to action some of the club's winter plans.

Andoni Iraola's side aren't the only team looking to do business in January though, and a European giant could spoil the plans of the Merseyside club.

The potential transfer blow would frustrate Liverpool but could open the door for another deal to be completed sooner than planned.

The Reds are in the market for defensive reinforcements, and VfB Stuttgart standout Finn Jeltsch has been one of the most exciting names linked with a Merseyside move.

Liverpool are one of many top European clubs monitoring the 20-year-old German defender, who has played in every Bundesliga game for Stuttgart so far this season.

Several top sides are already in the race for Jeltsch's signature, but La Liga leaders Barcelona have now identified the wonderkid as a target.

That's according to an update from Ekrem Konur, who claims that Barca now consider Jeltsch a potential defensive option, with Hansi Flick an admirer of the youngster's profile.

The report does state that there are some reservations regarding the German's age and readiness to be an instant starter, meaning that he may be considered a long-term option rather than an immediate priority.

Arsenal, Inter Milan and Manchester United are all named alongside Liverpool as potential rivals for Jeltsch's signature, but Barcelona winning the race could actually do the Reds a favour.

Ronald Araujo has quickly become a key figure in Iraola's backline, but the Uruguayan is only on loan from Barcelona and a permanent move to Merseyside isn't guaranteed.

The Reds have a buy option, but there is uncertainty around whether they will be willing to pay the full amount.

If Barcelona were to make a winter move for Jeltsch then the door could open for Liverpool to negotiate a cheaper option for Araujo in January.

Ward will hope that he can be the man to win the race for Jeltsch, but if Liverpool don't sign the 20-year-old then a move to Barcelona could be a boost for the Reds too.
