Tony Romo, After Parting Ways With CBS Sports, Named ‘Chief Predictions Officer’ of Jake Paul’s Betr Betting App

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Former NFL quarterback and football analyst Tony Romo has landed his next gig: He’s joined Betr, the sports gambling app co-founded by Jake Paul, as its first-ever “chief predictions officer.”

In the new role, Romo will serve as “a prominent face of the Betr brand,” appearing in original content, delivering sports predictions and engaging with fans across the company’s gaming products, including Picks, Predictions, Social Sportsbook, Social Casino, Arcade and Rips. Romo, former QB for the Dallas Cowboys, had been an on-air analyst with CBS Sports since 2017. Last month, the network announced it was parting ways with Romo. That came after Romo had pleaded no contest to a charge of operating while intoxicated stemming from an incident that took place in Milwaukee in July.

Romo said: “I’ve spent my entire career trying to anticipate what’s going to happen next, whether that was on the field or in the broadcast booth. Now I get to put those instincts to work in a completely new way with Betr.” He added, “I’m looking forward to having some fun, making predictions and being part of where this company is headed. And I’ve got a pretty good feeling about what’s coming next.”

“Tony Romo has built an extraordinary reputation around something that sits at the very heart of our business: predictions,” Joey Levy, founder and CEO of Betr, said in a statement. “His ability to see what’s coming before anyone else has made him one of the most compelling personalities in sports, and we see a tremendous opportunity to bring that talent and personality to our growing ecosystem.”

Romo joins Betr’s roster of sports and entertainment talent partners, including NBA veteran Patrick Beverley, former Heisman Trophy winner Johnny Manziel and actors Jeremy Piven and Adrian Grenier.
