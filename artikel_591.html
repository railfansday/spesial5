Huw Edwards Speaks Publicly About Conviction and Downfall for First Time in Channel 4 Doc: ‘I’d Lost the Plot’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Huw Edwards, the former BBC news anchor who in 2024 was convicted of three counts of making indecent images of children, will speak publicly about his criminal actions and downfall for the first time in a new Channel 4 doc.

Filmed over nearly a year and capturing Edwards at home in rural Wales, the doc follows a man who for years was one of the most respected voices on British TV as he’s forced to confront his loss of fame, declining health, fractured relationships and his crimes.

“From trusted national figure to registered sex offender,” says the voiceover in a short trailer for the documentary, in which an agitated Edwards says that he’d “lost the plot” when asked what he thought he was doing.

Edwards served as lead presenter of the “BBC News at 10” from 2003 and 2023 as well as the BBC News’ chief presenter, and would anchor coverage of major events such as the death and state funeral of Queen Elizabeth II and the coronation of Charles III and Camilla. However, this came crashing down in 2024 following allegations of workplace misconduct and a police investigation into child pornography. He would plead guilty to making indecent images of children and be given a six-month suspended sentence.

“Channel 4 has an established reputation for tackling controversial subjects through exemplary filmmaking,” said Sacha Mirzoeff, senior commissioning editor at Channel 4. “‘Huw Edwards – Guilty’ is no exception in its quest for answers from a man who is trying to come to terms with his abuse of power.”

Added director Patrick Forbes: “This was an important film to make. It’s rare to find a subject that touches on so many significant contemporary issues – whether it’s celebrity culture or the power of the media – as well as enduring questions about crime and punishment.”

“Huw Edwards – Guilty” was commissioned by Mirzoeff and produced by Oxford Films. It was directed by Forbes. The producer is Stephanie Collins, with Vanessa Tuson and Nick Kent as executive producers.
