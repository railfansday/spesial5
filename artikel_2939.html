Embiid available, Brown out as Sixers visit Pacers in critical NBA Cup matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Philadelphia 76ers are back in action Friday night after having the Thanksgiving holiday off, hitting the road to visit the Indiana Pacers in NBA Cup play.

Friday's contest will be the first leg of the team's fourth back-to-back of the season, with Philly set to visit the Houston Rockets Saturday night.

But first, the Friday matchup. Joel Embiid is questionable to face the Pacers due to illness. If he were downgraded to out, it would be his third game in a row missed due to being sick. Jaylen Brown is out due to left hamstring injury management. Brown exited the Sixers' Thursday win over the Miami Heat after limping to the locker room just before halftime due to hamstring tightness.

For Indiana, Tyrese Haliburton remains out as well as Ben Sheppard and Johnny Furphy. T.J. McConnell (hamstring), Kelly Oubre Jr. (hand) and Larry Nance Jr. (ankle) are all questionable.

The 6-12 Pacers have had a bumpy ride so far this season as they continue to deal with injuries up and down the lineup, most notably Haliburton being sidelined for all but the first four games of the year due to that hamstring injury.

Indiana most recently played all the way back on Monday evening, snagging a victory over Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and the Oklahoma City Thunder. Prior to that, however, they had lost four in a row. That's been the story of the season thus far for the Pacers: pick up a win, but lose the next bunch. They just haven't found a way to string victories together just yet.

This is the third meeting of the season already between the Sixers and the Pacers, with Philadelphia taking the wins in both previous contests, both by relatively narrow margins. Pascal Siakam led the way for Indiana in both of those games, posting a 26-point, 11-rebound double-double in one and a 31 points in the other. He's been leading the squad in general this campaign, posting 22.9 points per night on 50.7% field goal shooting and 37.0% shooting from long range.

Of the two games ahead of them in as many days, this game against the Pacers appears, on paper, to be the easier battle. I always add the caveat of that all just being "on paper" because, well, there are just no guarantees in the NBA. But that being said, Friday night is a good opportunity to add another game to the win column before a tougher matchup on Saturday night with some probably-weary legs.

It feels like a number of times the Sixers have trailed or lost this season has come down to their opponent being much hotter from long range. The Pacers, however, are second to worst in the association in three-point percentage (33.3%) so hopefully that means they won't bombard the Sixers with triples the way other teams have been able to at times. Hopefully.

If the Sixers can beat the Pacers Friday, they'll win the East's Group B and advance to the knockout stage for the first time since the NBA Cup was introduced in 2023-24.

The Sixers and Pacers tip off at 7:30 p.m. ET.
