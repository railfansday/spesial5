Who should be the El Paso Girls Athlete of the Week? Cast your vote

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

With the 2026-27 high school sports season underway, the El Paso Times will give readers a chance to vote to select El Paso's top athletes and teams.

This week, readers voted for the Girls Athlete of the Week in volleyball, cross country and basketball for the week of Nov. 2-7.

Kylie Vega, Eastwood cross country. Vega finished top five at the Class 6A state cross country meet.

Zoeh Cereceres, Chapin volleyball. In two matches last week, she had 42 kills and 40 digs as the team reached the Class 5A regional semifinals.

Abigail Garduno, Burges basketball. In a win vs. Eastwood, she scored 18 points and had 13 rebounds.

Jazlyn Hernandez, San Elizario basketball. In a matchup vs. Loretto Academy, she scored 40 points.

Nathaly Ortiz, Horizon basketball. In a win vs. Mountain View, she scored 19 points.

Emery Puga, Hanks basketball. In the season-opening win vs. Socorro, she scored 17 points.

Kimberly Charles, Ysleta basketball. In a win vs. YWA, she scored a team-best 22 points. 22 points.

Anna Rios, Riverside basketball. In a win vs. Austin, she scored 22 points.

Sarai Duncan, Andress basketball. In a win vs. Eastlake, she scored 15 points.

Charlize Hanany, El Paso High basketball. In a win over Coronado, she scored 16 points and added 6 steals, 6 rebounds, and 2 assists.
