Glaukos: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A $1 billion revenue target for 2027 assumes potential Medicare restrictions on a key drug, yet management still sees a path to scale.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 23, 2026 at 10:45AM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, Glaukos (GKOS +5.47%) announced record consolidated net sales of $211 million, up 58% year over year. The ophthalmic medical technology company expanded its profit margins while raising full-year financial targets.

Management introduced a preliminary 2027 revenue outlook of $1 billion. This target accounts for potential Medicare coverage restrictions on iDose TR, an injectable intraocular drug delivery implant for glaucoma, while reflecting momentum behind Epioxa, a drug treatment for keratoconus, a rare corneal eye disease.

Epioxa generated $53 million in net sales during its first full quarter after receiving its product-specific J-code, a standardized Medicare reimbursement billing identifier. The company established coverage pathways across 90% of commercial insurance lives to expand patient access.

Adoption of higher-margin therapies pushed non-GAAP gross margin, a metric measuring profitability after direct production costs, up nearly four percentage points year over year to 88%. Management expects this margin profile to persist as key growth drivers scale.

Net sales are climbing rapidly across key product lines, and management is not hiding its satisfaction with the operating trajectory. Glaukos holds broad insurance coverage, a network of 300 treatment centers, and a pipeline of 14 clinical programs. Management is watching reimbursement workflows and regional Medicare decisions.

The company continues balancing near-term profit execution with commercial investments across retinal and glaucoma therapies to drive top-line expansion.
