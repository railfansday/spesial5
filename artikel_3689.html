Original Director Behind Marvel’s Failed Mahershala Ali Blade Movie Says ‘Life’s Too Short To Make Sh*t Films’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Bassam Tariq directed 2026’s critically-lauded action-thriller Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother starring Mahershala Ali. He was also supposed to direct Ali in Marvel’s period-piece Blade reboot, but getting that project off the ground ended up being a nightmare for everyone involved. Tariq said he’ll never put himself in that situation again.

“It was funny and strange to start casting and building without a proper script. You’re sitting there and explaining to actors what their role will be, and they just have to believe you,” he said in a new interview with New Arab (via IGN). “That’s also very dangerous because if the text comes and it doesn’t add up to what you were saying, your credibility gets completely shot. All I have is my integrity, and I’ve been thinking about that a lot.”
