California Bans Waymo from Highways, Citing Recent Safety Issues

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

California has banned Waymo self-driving vehicles from state highways until the company fixes safety concerns related to construction zones and police activity.

California is tightening its restrictions on Waymo’s services. The DMV modified Waymo’s permit last week to ban the driverless vehicles from highways following several examples of the vehicles trying to enter hazardous situations.

The DMV specifically cited two incidents that happened earlier this month. On November 5, three Waymos reportedly drove into a construction zone. The next day, a Waymo vehicle tried to pass California Highway Patrol vehicles that were performing a traffic break. California officials called these vehicles an “imminent hazard to public safety” as long as they continue to ignore road hazards.

This isn’t the first time Waymo has had to pause its highway driving program. In May 2026, the company paused freeway operations to improve vehicle responses in construction zones after similar safety concerns arose.

However, it seems it’s back to the drawing board on construction zone safety. As a result, Waymo is proactively taking all of its vehicles off the freeway until it can develop a solution. The company currently operates in 15 cities, but highway driving was only available in Phoenix, Miami, Austin, Los Angeles County, and the San Francisco Bay Area.

It’s unclear how long the driverless vehicles will stay off the highway. California’s DMV restriction only applies within the state, meaning Waymo could put its vehicles back on the highway in other states before the California ban, which has no distinct end date, is lifted.

State officials say they’ll need solid proof that Waymo vehicles can effectively detect and avoid highway hazards, including construction zones and police activity, before reinstating Waymo’s original license. A Waymo spokesperson told the Associated Press that the company will test highway driving with trained specialists behind the wheel to develop a fix for the safety issues as soon as possible. The autonomous vehicles will continue to operate as usual on non-highway streets.
