Prince Harry and Meghan Are Giving Their Kids Their First British Christmas

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

There’s nothing like London during the holidays

The Sussexes are in for a very British Christmas. After moving back in late August—following more than six years in California—Prince Harry and Duchess Meghan are ready to spend the holidays in England.

Per a new People report, the Duke and Duchess of Sussex are planning to celebrate both Thanksgiving and Christmas at their new home in the Cotswolds with their children, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet. Meghan’s mother, Doria Ragland, is also expected to join.

A source told People that after her estranged father’s recent arrest, Meghan “wants the holidays to be as quiet and enjoyable as possible for the family.”

“Traveling back and forth would be too much for everyone,” the source added. “She doesn’t want to add any more pressure right now.”

It’s been a while since Harry and Meghan spent the holidays in the United Kingdom. In 2017, the engaged couple joined Harry’s royal family for Christmas at Sandringham. And in 2018, as a newly married couple, they attended the traditional Christmas Day church service at St. Mary Magdalene Church at Sandringham.

In the years that followed, as the Sussexes stepped back from their senior royal posts, they spent their holidays in either Canada or the United States, which means this will be their little children’s first true British Christmas. We know they might not make it to town for the festivities (as they are trying to keep a low profile), but there really is nothing quite like a London Christmas.

We already know Meghan’s Christmas table spread will include plenty of jam, flower sprinkles, and party bubbles. And we can’t wait to learn more about the family’s end-of-year celebrations.
