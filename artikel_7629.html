‘ER’ and ‘The Pitt’ are similar enough for lawsuit to move forward, appeals court says

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Actor Noah Wyle attends an event for HBO’s “The Pitt” at the David Geffen Theater on June 9, 2026, in Los Angeles. (Photo by Phillip Faraone/Getty Images for HBO)

A unanimous California appeals court on Wednesday found that medical TV shows ER and The Pitt have enough similarities that a lawsuit alleging that The Pitt is an unauthorized rebranded version of ER can move forward.

A three-judge panel from the Second District Court of Appeal in California ruled that the trial court judge correctly rejected a motion to dismiss the case brought by the widow and estate of ER creator Michael Crichton.

However, the panel dismissed actor Noah Wyle, who starred in both shows and produces The Pitt, as a defendant, along with two other producers who worked on both series.

The Pitt is an HBO Max series depicting a real-time day in the life of a Pittsburgh emergency department. It has won two Emmy Awards for best drama for its first two seasons and two Emmy Awards for Wyle for best actor, according to a story by the Associated Press.
