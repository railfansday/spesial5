Rice verbally agrees new Arsenal contract

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Declan Rice has just over 18 months left on his existing contract, with a further year's option in Arsenal's favour; the Premier League champions want to bring him in line with the club's top earner, Bukayo Saka; Rice admitted on England duty that he is close to a new agreement

Football Correspondent @RobDorsettSky

Declan Rice has verbally agreed terms on a new long-term contract at Arsenal, Sky Sports News can reveal.

Sky Sports News has been told his new deal will bring him in line with Bukayo Saka, who is the highest-paid player at the Emirates.

Rice and his representatives have been in negotiations for several months, with the club keen to tie down one of their most important players, even though he still has 18 months left on his existing contract, with a further year's option in the club's favour.

Rice was named Arsenal's Player of the Year last season when they won the Premier League title and this new deal will take him into his early 30s.

The 27-year-old joined Arsenal from West Ham for a club record £105m in 2023. He's gone on to be a key member of Mikel Arteta's leadership group, and someone who the Spanish coach has built his team around.

Speaking on Saturday ahead of England's final Nations League group game in Spain, Rice confirmed that he was close to an agreement.

"I'm a player that always sets myself targets, always pushes for more. I've always been stable in the clubs I'm at. The new contract is hopefully on the horizon soon.

"I'm extremely lucky I'm at a club like Arsenal where the manager shows me so much trust. I've so many team-mates who put so much energy and effort into me. I'm extremely lucky that I don't have to worry about that stuff. It makes playing football a lot easier."

Arsenal are not just tying down Declan Rice - one of their most important players - to a bumper new deal, they are tying down arguably the Premier League's best player into his remaining peak years.

And this season is the best version we've ever seen from Rice.

The England midfielder has started the campaign on fire and tops the Premier League assist charts at the moment with five - all of them from open play.

But this creative spark from Rice has come despite the Arsenal man playing a deeper role in Mikel Arteta's set-up. A sitting midfielder, yes - but still as attacking as ever.

No midfielder in the Premier League has made more final third passes or passes through the lines than Rice this season.

His number of final third entries has nearly doubled compared to two seasons ago. And his recent goal for Arsenal against Bayern Munich in the Champions League summed up how he is influencing the top end of the pitch a lot more.

That goal cancelled out Harry Kane's strike for Bayern. Rice is Arsenal's superstar equivalent.

Rice can do everything in midfield for Arsenal. He can hurt teams by running through them, or passing through the lines. He can score, as well as create. He can damage from open play, or from his deadly delivery from corners and free-kicks. He is the complete midfielder.

Meanwhile, another huge figure in the Arsenal starting XI - captain Martin Odegaard - has said he has not yet begun negotiations over a new contract, even though his current deal expires at the same time as Rice's.

Speaking while on international duty with Norway, the Arsenal captain said: "I'm happy and hope I can be there for a long time to come, but so far we haven't started on that.

"There is no stress about it. I am where I am, and I am super happy with it. Things are going well, and it is not something I am stressed about. As I said, I want to be there for a long time to come. I am enjoying myself a lot."
