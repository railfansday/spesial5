Review Program Managers are attorneys with extensive document review project management experience who serve as a central point of contact with counsel, client and internal Lighthouse stakeholders on projects requiring managed review. Review Program Managers consult on review project planning and workflow strategy – focusing first on how best to leverage technology, analytics and other Lighthouse consultative services to reduce data from linear review, and then on the optimization of workflows and incorporation of appropriate technology to improve the speed and accuracy of any eyes-on review. The Review Program Manager provides oversight and management of the work conducted by our review staffing partners to ensure quality and timeliness of all deliverables.

This is a dynamic consultative role focused on promoting a tech-forward approach to drive down review volumes and increase the efficiency and accuracy of review team work product to help our clients drastically reduce spend on linear document review. The position centers on a consultative approach as opposed to a limited focus on execution of tactical items.
