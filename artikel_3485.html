GTA 6 Modders Are Trying To Vibe-Code A PC Port, And It’s Not Going Well

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

PC modders are racing to get GTA 6 playable on PC after the leak of the game's complete Xbox build, but so far it doesn't look too promising.

GTA 6 publisher Take-Two Interactive and developer Rockstar Games haven't been sitting idle either, as one of the leads behind the Project Leonida Discord group claimed in now-deleted social media posts that they were stepping away from the mammoth task of getting GTA 6 to run on PC. Project Leonida has also been taken down, but it's not the only group trying to get GTA 6 running outside of PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

None of these groups have succeeded yet, and as Twitter user Chasm detailed online, maybe relying heavily on AI tools isn't the way to go. Dev streams have been full of users leveraging generative AI tools to do something, with several prompts being absolute gems of wishful thinking. Here's a snippet of what people have been throwing at Google's Antigravity, while butchering the English language:

  • Continue working and do apply ever part of the code
  • make no mistakes
  • You need todo one thing at a time not a little bit of everything
  • we need all the game start like the rockstar launch so on that stuff, not in game stuff yet
  • work faster
  • there has to be a faster way to iterate than this

Even with an army of enthusiasts throwing prompts and cash at generative AI, a copied version of GTA 6 still seems far out of reach. Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick commented in March that the idea of using AI and simply pushing a button to make a hit game was "laughable," and he referenced the music industry, which is grappling with a flood of AI slop to top streaming platforms. "Right now [in music] there are programs that allow you to put out a prompt and get a professionally recorded song spit back out at you. It sounds like a song, but I defy you to listen to it more than once. It's great to send as a greeting card to your partner on their birthday, but that's about it," Zelnick said.

Whatever progress is made in the GTA 6 PC port scene, don't expect it to last long before Take-Two's lawyers pounce. The publisher hasn't shied away from protecting its IP in the past, and takedown notices for any future GTA 6-related projects seem likely.
