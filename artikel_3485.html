PSN Went Down Sorta On GTA 6 Eve

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

On the eve of GTA 6's official launch on November 19, Sony's PlayStation Network appears to be facing some issues, at least for some people, at some point, today.

Reports have emerged that the PSN is borked, and although the PlayStation Status website says everything is fine, that might not be true for everyone.

As with all outages, your mileage may vary. It was reported that the PSN had a temporary outage for launching games specifically, and this has since been fixed.

GTA 6 is expected to put enormous strain on PSN and the Xbox Network as millions of people log in to play. GTA 6 is already live and playable in New Zealand, Australia, and other parts of the world, but those places are not where the majority of gamers are.

Keep checking back with GameSpot for more on how online networks are holding up as GTA 6 approaches.
