GTA 6 Is Approaching An Absurd New Sales Milestone, Report Says

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Grand Theft Auto 6's success continues to come into focus, as a new report from Alinea Analytics says the game sold 44.9 million copies through its first weekend. This would be up from the 32.5 million copies that Rockstar owner Take-Two announced last week, covering the game's pre-purchases alone.

According to Rhys Elliott of Alinea Analytics, GTA 6 has reached 33.7 million copies sold on PS5 and 11.2 million on Xbox Series X|S, for a split of 75.1% to 24.9%.

Take-Two announced $3.79 billion in sales for GTA 6 on day one, and Alinea estimated that the figure has grown to $4 billion through its first four days.

Sony and Microsoft keep 30% of the revenue for digital copies via platform fees, so those companies are winners here as well.

Elliott went on to point out that GTA 6's $4 billion in revenue thus far is roughly equal to the combined box office results for this year's two biggest movies, Spider-Man: Brand New Day ($2.5 billion) and The Odyssey ($1.8 billion). Of course, GTA 6 is an $80-$100 game, while (hopefully) no one is spending that much to see a movie, at least not at one time.

Elliott also shared data that shows around 40.8 million people logged in to play GTA 6 on Sunday, November 22, with PS5 making up 76% of those players. Additionally, Elliott said the data shows that about 20% of players--or about 9 million people--have spent more than 20 hours in the game already.

GTA 6 is only going to get bigger when it comes to sales as more and more people buy the game. Additionally, GTA 6 is expected to come to PC in 2027, which will be another selling cycle for the game. Beyond this, GTA 6 is expected to add an online mode in 2027 that will no doubt feature microtransactions and other ways for Rockstar to make money off the fanbase.
