Fans report Umamusume: Pretty Derby AI clone brimming with ads, Cygames gets it taken offline

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

An AI clone of Umamusume: Pretty Derby has been stuck off the Google Play Store after it drew the ire of fans, and the community organised to report the offending title to Cygames.

Anime Racing Academy, developed by HIFI Publishers, was described as an "action-packed anime racing game" on its official store description. Like Umamusume, it allows players to manage and race humanoid horse girls, with some of its designs either very close replications of Umamusume characters while others appear to be outright copies. Unlike Umamusume, it was packed full of ads, as highlighted by curious fans like the one below.

Anime Racing Academy気になったのでチラ見😀これはひどい・・・🫪ワンアクションごとにAD、PRバナーも常に表示、この内容でもGooglePlayは審査が通ってしまう驚き。そっと削除。 pic.twitter.com/E5h3SABLcS

Once Umamusume fans discovered the game, many pushed to have the game reported and taken off the Google Play Store. As of writing this appears to have happened, as the game is no longer discoverable on the mobile platform. Other games from HIFI Publishers remain up on stores, which indicates this specific game has been reported and subsequently taken down.
