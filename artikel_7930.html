Should I use a premium or budget diaper if my baby’s in daycare full-time?

Budget diapers can handle daytime. In Consumer Reports’ 2026 testing, its budget Value Pick scored as high on absorption and dryness as its top performer. Many parents opt for budget during the day and premium for overnight.

Is a thinner or thicker diaper better for an active, cruising toddler?

A slimmer core with a flexible waistband (like Pampers Cruisers 360 Fit) tends to give busy babies more freedom of movement without losing all-important absorbency.

How often should I change a newborn’s diaper to prevent rash?

Changing diapers every 2 to 3 hours and using barrier ointment is the main way to prevent diaper rash, per AAP. If a rash won’t clear up, bleeds, or looks like an allergic reaction, call your pediatrician or dermatologist.

Do I really need a different diaper for day versus night?

Not always, but heavy wetters often do. Separate day and night diapers are an increasingly common fix for overnight soakers. Pampers AMORE, launched in March 2026, is billed by Pampers as its most absorbent diaper, in sizes Newborn-7.
