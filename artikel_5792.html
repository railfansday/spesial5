Nurse Shot Dead; Fertility Doctor's Sex Charges; Botox Doc Gets 15 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

— A weekly roundup of healthcare's encounters with the courts

A 37-year-old New York City nurse was shot dead in her apartment in an apparent murder-suicide. (New York Post)

Justin Neisler, MD, of Colorado, is facing new charges after authorities allegedly found child sexual abuse material on a hidden cellphone in the Atlanta prison where he is serving 23 years for producing child sexual abuse material. (WSB-TV)

Jerome Check, MD, PhD, a fertility specialist in New Jersey, was charged with sexually assaulting three patients. (ABC 6)

California chiropractor Robert Kovacs has been accused by patients of inappropriate touching and sexual battery. (KRON 4)

A hospital employee in Texas was arrested after a patient reported sexual abuse. (WFAA)

A New Jersey family is accusing an organ procurement organization of pressuring them to donate their loved one's tissues and organs without letting them know that he would be unrecognizable at his funeral. (ABC 6)

The Equal Employment Opportunity Commission's lawsuit against Yale New Haven Hospital in Connecticut over cognitive testing of physicians 70 and older is heading to trial. (Becker's Physician Leadership)

California cosmetic dermatologist Violetta Mailyan, DO, was sentenced to 15 years in prison for fraudulently billing Medicare more than $45 million for Botox injections that weren't necessary or were never done, the Justice Department announced.

A New York radiology practice and one of its physicians has ended a court dispute over a $20 million private equity deal. Gregory Ball, MD, initially raised concerns that the deal was rushed and then shortchanged. (Radiology Business)

A Florida pharmacist was sentenced to over 7 years in prison for illegally distributing more than 300,000 oxycodone pills to patients who didn't need them, the Justice Department said.
