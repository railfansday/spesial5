Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Position Title: Online Adjunct Instructor for EDUC 829CR: Therapeutic Play Techniques for Child Life Specialists

Course: EDUC 829CR: Therapeutic Play Techniques for Child Life Specialists (3 credits)

Students will explore the meanings and purposes of play and how play develops as a child develops. Various theories of play therapy will be introduced and the roles of child life specialist and play specialist will be delineated. Students will learn how child life specialists can create the optimal environment to encourage learning, development and healing through play in hospitals and other healthcare settings.  The course also covers directive and nondirective therapeutic play techniques for use in playrooms, clinical settings, and at the bedside, both with the individual child and with groups. This course is for Child Life students only.
