Confirmed UEFA Women's Champions League Lineups: PSG Féminines vs. FC Bayern Frauen

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

FC Bayern looked to return to winning ways in the UEFA Women's Champions League before their top-of-the-table Frauen-Bundesliga clash with VfL Wolfsburg on Saturday. Last season's semi-finalists face French side PSG at Parc des Princess, where they recorded a 3-1 victory at the same stage last season.

Here are the official lineups for the matchday 4 fixture in Europe's premium competition.

PSG: Kiedrzynek; Isabela, Mbock, Dudek ©, Carmona; Groenen, Osteras, Echegini; Kangjinga, Feller, Lafontaine

Bayern: Mahmutović; Gwinn, Eriksson ©, Ballisager, Kett; Amani, Tanikawa; Dallman, Harder, Bühl; Demnjanović
