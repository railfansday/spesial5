Liverpool's Ngumoha still a "very interesting option" for Bayern Munich

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Rio Ngumoha remains a “very interesting option” for Bayern Munich regarding the left wing position, as Sky Germany reports.

The Bundesliga champions made a very concrete attempt to sign Ngumoha last summer, but the move fell through.

As things stand, Ngumoha's current employers Liverpool are doing everything to extend the player's contract which runs until 2028.

However, the pay-TV channel highlights that an agreement is far from being reached and therefore the outcome of his future is open.

So far this campaign, Ngumoha has featured 14 times in all competitions totally just 642 minutes while providing 2 assists.

The Englishman is already valued at €50million by Transfermarkt.com, adding speculation to whether Bayern would pay such an amount.
