Drones offer a new view of wildlife health. But what are they missing?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Drones are transforming how scientists study wildlife, allowing researchers to photograph and measure animals in their natural environments, monitor larger populations and collect observations with less disturbance. While a photograph can reveal changes in an animal's size and shape, it cannot confidently tell researchers what those changes mean for its health.
