‘Dark’ Creators Revisit Eerie German Forests in First ‘Sonnental’ Teaser

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Lisa Vicari stars as a profiler investigating a gruesome murder in her old hometown, inspired by 'Struwwelpeter,' a real and really sinister German children's book.

Jantje Friese and Baran bo Odar, creators of Netflix’ cult hit series Dark, are going back into those eerie German woods.

HBO Max has released the first teaser trailer for the pair’s new mystery thriller, Sonnental, and it looks as Grimm as they come. Check it out the below.

The six-part series stars Lisa Vicari as Anja Vogler, a profiler sent in to investigate a gruesome murder in her old hometown. As more bodies pile up, Vogler begins to see a connection between the killings and motifs from Struwwelpeter, a sinister (and very real) German children’s book meant to scare kids straight with nasty stories of what can happen if they misbehave.

Peter Kurth (Babylon Berlin) co-stars as investigator Norbert Troschitz. The regular cast includes Jan Friedrichsen as Anja’s son Max, Iris Böhm as Anja’s mother Kerstin, Merlin Rose, Maria Simon, Mišel Matičević and Stipe Erceg.

Created by Matija Dragojević, and co-written by Dragojević and Bob Konrad (Kleo, 4 Blocks), Sonnental is an HBO Max German original. All six episodes drop on HBO Max in March 2027.

Dark ran for three seasons on Netflix, from 2017 to 2020. Jantje Friese and Baran bo Odar’s follow-up Netflix series, the ambitious period drama 1899, was canceled after a single season.
