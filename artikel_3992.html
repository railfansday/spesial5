MAGES. to announce Girls und Panzer game for console on November 14

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

MAGES. will announce a new Girls und Panzer game for console at “Oarai Ankou Matsuri 2026 Girls und Panzer Mini Mini Hobby Show” on November 14.

The information is tucked away in the list of contents for the event, which will be held from November 14 to 15 at Oarai Seaside Station.

Further details, such as whether the game will be a visual novel in the style of MAGES.’s other anime adaptations, are currently unknown.

D3 Publisher‘s newly announced Girls und Panzer: All-Star Tank Carnival will also exhibit the same event.
