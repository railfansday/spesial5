Switch 2 worldwide sales top 26.35 million, Switch tops 157.20 million

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Nintendo Switch 2 has sold 26.35 million units and Switch has sold 157.20 million units worldwide as of September 30, 2026, Nintendo announced in its latest earnings release.

A total of 2.67 million Switch 2 hardware and 15.04 million software were sold during the three months ended September 30, 2026, while a total of 0.61 million Switch hardware and 31.92 million software were sold.
