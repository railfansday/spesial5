Spyro's New PS5 Game Teases Appearance at This Year's Game Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Publisher Activision and developer Toys for Bob are teasing Spyro: A Realm Beyond will be part of The Game Awards 2026.

In just a few weeks, the upcoming platformer starring everyone's favourite purple dragon will get a public showing for the first time since its announcement back in June.

As reported by Gematsu, an email from Activision makes it pretty clear we'll be seeing more of the game during the event.

"[The Game Awards 2026] are around the corner, and Spyro is getting fired up," it says. "Before we get there, here's a look at some of the Spyro: A Realm Beyond assets [of the Painted Plains area] you might have sneaked a peek at in [Xbox’s 25th anniversary] commemorative magazine. Who is ready to see more of the first all-new Spyro adventure in nearly 20 years? Sheep beware."
