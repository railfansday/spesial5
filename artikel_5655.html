Joint Letter Calling for International Rail to Be Central to the UK-EU Reset

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The UK-EU summit is the moment for the UK to establish a programme of cooperation with its European partners on international rail.

A coalition of transport, environmental and youth organisations, as well as operators, asks the UK to use next week’s summit as an opportunity for closer cooperation on international rail. The signatories also call on the UK to commit to establishing an international rail strategy incorporating passenger and freight services, with ambitious targets for modal shift from air to rail, developed in close partnership with European counterparts.

Transport & Environment’s (T&E) vision is a zero-emission mobility system that is affordable and has minimal impacts on our health, climate and environment. Created over 30 years ago, we have shaped some of Europe’s most important environmental laws.
