Casa Nostra Kindergarten / Sarquella Torres

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Text description provided by the architects. Set on the southern edge of Banyoles Lake, La finca de Casa Nostra occupies a site of exceptional environmental sensitivity. Acquired in 1926 by Magdalena Aulina's Cultural Institution, the property has supported educational activities for nearly a century. Over time, these have accumulated in the form of a small campus, a constellation of buildings constructed incrementally and shaped by the landscape as much as by institutional need.
