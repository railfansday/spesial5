This 1927 Silent Film Won an Oscar That Was Never Awarded Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Released in U.S. theaters on November 4, 1927, F.W. Murnau's silent masterpiece Sunrise: A Song of Two Humans remains one of cinema's greatest achievements—celebrated for its groundbreaking cinematography, its inaugural Academy Award win, and its enduring emotional power nearly a century later.
