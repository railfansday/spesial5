3 Altcoins Hit It Big as Market Trades Sideways

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Home » Altcoins » 3 Altcoins Hit It Big as Market Trades Sideways

Technical analyst Ali Martinez named three mid-cap altcoins that stand out from the bleeding crowd.

While major cryptocurrencies have faced selling pressure in recent weeks, three smaller altcoins have posted sharp gains. Analyst Ali Charts highlighted Midnight (NIGHT), Venice Token (VVV), and Pieverse (PIEVERSE) as examples of mid alts moving way higher against the wider trend.

NIGHT has climbed roughly 33% over five days. The altcoin has traded inside a channel since early October and just recently tested the upper boundary near $0.052 before being pushed below the trend-line once again.

A sustained close above that level is being watched as a possible trigger toward $0.070, while the mid-range near $0.044 remains the nearer support while price stays inside the channel. NIGHT currently trades around $0.047.

VVV has risen about 84% since its November 14 low. Despite the altcoin’s recovery, the new token remains inside a descending channel.

The chart structure is being watched for a potential extension toward $32 before any larger pullback. VVV is trading near $28, still below its recent high near $34.5.

PIEVERSE has recorded the strongest move of the three. After a TD Sequential buy signal on October 22, 2026, the altcoin rose more than 400% and reached a high near $5.35. The same indicator has now flipped to a sell signal.

Crypto market connoisseurs are monitoring possible corrective levels near $2.60 or $1.85 if profit-taking intensifies. PIEVERSE currently trades around $4.00, below its recent peak.

The three altcoins have outperformed the broader market, but their setups differ.
