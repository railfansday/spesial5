40 AD-Approved Interior Photographers on the West Coast to Book for Your Next Project

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Whether the view outside the window is of the Santa Monica pier or the peak of Mount Rainier, the homes AD’s most trusted interior photographers on the West Coast shoot always end up looking as impressive as the scenery that surrounds them. While many of them are bicoastal or even work internationally, these pros are experts on shooting West Coast interiors. And they’re not just leading lights in the field of interior photography; many of these artists excel in travel, landscape, or portrait photography as well—but each one of these photographers is guaranteed to make a home look like a worthy setting for a Hollywood blockbuster.

Step inside a Beaux Arts San Francisco home designed by Jonathan Adler.

Looking for a pro who can capture architecture as well as your latest interior? Maybe even a portrait too? You’ll want Jose Manuel Alorda on your speed dial. Raised in Mallorca and shooting since the age of 14, Alorda is now based in San Francisco.

A midcentury Bay Area home gets a Japandi-inspired renovation courtesy designer Cathie Hong.

If your design style leans minimalist, find an interior photographer like Margaret Austin. This talent is able to capture clean lines and balanced proportions just as intended while still managing to inject life and personality—even if the space is intentionally sparse.

A little guest enjoys a big room at Kisawa Sanctuary in Mozambique.

Warning: Tanveer Badal’s portfolio may prompt a hotel booking. The Los Angeles photographer and director has captured Amangiri and One&Only Mandarina, bringing hospitality into focus alongside projects involving AvroKO, Nickey Kehoe, and Fettle.

Peter Baker’s interest in the built world continues long after a commission wraps. The Los Angeles photographer pairs interiors work for talents including Martha Mulholland and Alexander M. Liberman with an extensive fine art practice exploring his surroundings.

A subdued palette allows scenic views to remain the focus in this West Coast condo.

This sharpshooter may have been born in the UK, but Bartlam is now firmly rooted in LA, where she deftly captures interiors as well as striking landscapes.

Playful, comfortably lived-in interiors have met their picture-perfect match with Bengochea, a Domino magazine photo editor turned full-time photographer. Bengochea’s creatively experiential point of view makes an interior all the more inviting, whether he’s shooting a chandelier dramatically reflected in a wall-hung mirror or a styled centerpiece seen from above.

A media room layered in Orior furnishings

Known for her artistic commercial work, which centers on architecture and interiors, Elizabeth Carababas produces shots with a strong scenographic sense—one gets the feeling that the inhabitant is just outside the frame.

AD100 design firm Pierce & Ward crafted a California cottage with Scottish charm for actor Karen Gillan.

Justin Chung’s photos are as sunny as California itself. He captures the state’s famous light masterfully as it illuminates residences with its warm glow, showcasing the best of LA living.

The bathroom in a 1927 home in Los Feliz designed by Daphne Nauleau

Michael P.H. Clifford has landed multiple cover stories in top design publications, including AD’s May 2024 issue with Emma Roberts, and has a knack for capturing people naturally yet strikingly within their homes.

Step inside Louisa Pierce’s century-old Tudor in Birmingham, Alabama.

Ty Cole adroitly navigates a wide array of clients, from architecture firms to corporate behemoths including PNC Bank and Morgan Stanley. He’s also a treasured collaborator here at AD and has contributed to Domino, Fortune, and Departures, among others.

Designer Melissa Benham revitalized this home in the Hollywood Hills.

Check your design bookshelf: Roger Davies’s photography may already be on it. The Los Angeles talent has shot volumes devoted to Oliver M. Furth, Suzanne Tucker, and Nickey Kehoe, and also works with David Netto and Marmol Radziner.

Natural light floods this living room in Rancho Mirage.

Jason Statham’s Los Angeles home and an Amber Lewis–designed family retreat are among Shade Degges’s AD assignments. Based in Los Angeles, he photographs interiors, architecture, travel, and portraits, following the design story beyond the front door.

A grungy San Francisco basement got contemporary English cottage vibes courtesy Innen Studio.

The quiet, contemplative interiors that Bess Friday has captured while working all over the world have appeared in AD, as well as in House Beautiful and Elle Decor. From beachside bungalows to Parisian apartments and French chateaux, her architectural subjects are always shown in their best light.

The theatricality of shadows created by drapery, detailed carvings and other textured surfaces is heightened through Sam Frost’s lens. His photos of interiors tell stories that are intentionally open to interpretation by the viewer.

The bedroom of a mid-century home in Palm Springs designed by Proem

With a particular knack for capturing the beauty of Southern California deserts, Gerber is known for his work in the fields of fine art, interiors, and architecture. Shooting in quintessential Californian locales such as Palm Springs and La Jolla, he plays up the connection between homes and their natural surroundings.

Inside the historic California house of architects Vishaan Chakrabarti and Maria Alataris

Alanna Hale describes her approach as unfussy and collaborative, two words we love to hear from the client side. Her portfolio is varied, but a thread of sunny, escapist sensibilities weave throughout.

Charli XCX, Bryce Dallas Howard, and Chelsea Handler—and their homes, of course—have all appeared in Tim Hirschmann’s shoots for AD. The Englishman has also contributed photography to three books including one dedicated to Los Angeles Interiors.

A wood-paneled kitchen by Lafayette Studio

For Jess Isaac, a room’s daily rhythms matter as much as its furnishings. Guided by atmosphere and changing natural light, the Los Angeles photographer has captured interiors by Amber Lewis, Kristen Panitch, and Lafayette Studio.

This home in West Marin was redone by Commune Design.

An art-school background and a life between Los Angeles and southern France inform Laure Joliet’s eye. Her interiors collaborators include Commune Design, Reath Design, and Geremia Design; she’s also known for shooting portraits of artists in their studios, a series she calls “Studio Visits.”

Designer Heidi Caillier’s home in San Francisco

Distinctive among the work of interior photographers on the West Coast, Haris Kenjar’s photography style lends itself perfectly to rustic and traditional interiors, where the rich tones of wood and the details of intricate moldings become the stars of the show. His clients include leading American designers and top publications including AD.

A light-filled lounge captured by Natasha Lee

Lee rambles around the West Coast and beyond, photographing hospitality destinations and other enticing sites. Snapping vibrant pictures of settings, food, and the people who make a place tick, she deftly conveys the essence of a place. Growing up, she split her time between Malaysia and California, which she now calls home.

The sunlit bathroom in Lucy Hale’s Los Angeles home, designed by Proem

Grant Legan brings an art director’s perspective to the places he photographs. With Beni Rugs, Four Seasons, and The Well among his clients, the Los Angeles talent moves between lifestyle and hospitality. Light and emotion guide his approach.

Inside a ’70s-style river island home outside of Portland, Oregon, designed by Jessica Helgerson

The golden hues of fall foliage and the colors of an evening sky are frequent backdrops for Aaron Leitz’s architecture and interiors photos, in which he emphasizes the buildings and spaces within their natural surroundings. In his own words, Leitz “combines abstraction and realism in ways that only a camera can.”

Frank Lloyd Wright’s Summit House in LaVerne, California, with a living room designed by Zeb and Crunk

Born in Tokyo and now based in Los Angeles, Makino grew up inspired by visionaries including Le Corbusier and Frank Lloyd Wright. Those creative connections clear even after a cursory glance at his work, which beautifully captures the natural materials so often used in architecture and interiors projects.

A Seattle scullery or “messy kitchen” as the clients call it, designed by Allison Lind in Washington state

Kara Mercer lets natural light do some of the talking. The Seattle photographer and creative director pairs thoughtful composition with work for collaborators including Allison Lind, Kat Lawton, and Casework, moving between interiors, hospitality, and lifestyle.

A view to the pool from inside the Playa Grande Beach Club in the Dominican Republic, designed by Kemble Interiors

On her website, Karyn Millet files her residential work under Domestic Bliss—and the images live up to that name. The Los Angeles photographer also turns her lens on hotels and historic spaces, with collaborators including Joe Lucas, Joan Behnke, and Celerie Kemble.

A Beverly Hills home designed by Suchi Reddy

From the pages of Apartamento to Teen Vogue, Ye Rin Mok has published work widely. We’re also big fans at AD and have featured her work in a number of home tours. (See her walkthrough of John Stamos’s Beverly Hills home here.)

A crisp white dining room captured by Tessa Neustadt

This Angeleno has years of experience as a photographer under her belt. From interiors, food and commercial, her subjects are captured in bright and crisp stills.

Designer Gille Mills created this living room in Hollywood.

In each of her shoots for AD, Jenna Peffley has been able to convey the personality of the home and its occupant to a T—be it Miley Cyrus’s bold and boisterous playground, Colman Domingo’s sunny modernist digs, or Orville Peck’s rustic tree house.

A former model who worked with the likes of Richard Avedon and Mario Testino, Resen moved behind the camera shooting fashion and travel as well as interiors. Having been an art director and magazines including Vogue, and publishers such as Rizzoli, Resen brings an editorial eye to every project.

A bedroom in Beverly Hills by Simo Design

Jessica Sample’s portfolio could keep an interiors lover and a travel obsessive equally occupied. The Los Angeles photographer shoots homes and hotels alongside far-flung destinations, with collaborators including House of Honey, Simo Design, and Hammer and Spear.

Interiors by Lauren Nelson and Meredith Dunn, of Lauren Nelson Design

A resident of the Bay Area who also works out of the Big Apple, Seth Smoot is the eye behind these light-drenched photos of Sweet July, Ayesha Curry’s store and event space. His partner Kendra, a stylist, is a frequent collaborator.

Inside the brick-walled home of a collector

Photographer Jake Stangel has taken on a range of commercial and editorial clients, from The California Sunday Magazine to Heath Ceramics. A dual citizen of the US and Canada who also holds visas to work in China and Brazil, Stangel is a colorful collaborator who might even bring a Doja Cat–and–Four Tet–studded Spotify playlist to your next shoot.

Inside stylist extraordinaire Colin King’s light-filled Manhattan loft

Rich Stapleton’s photographs offer a moment of calm and reflection. His focus on discovering and highlighting details reveals a process that involves truly understanding what makes a space unique and then tracing a personal narrative through a series of images.

This spa-like bathroom in San Francisco was designed by Noz Nozawa.

For anyone keeping tabs on the Bay Area’s expressive interiors, Christopher Stark’s collaborator list should ring a few bells. Ken Fulk, Jay Jeffers, Noz Nozawa, and Jon de la Cruz have all worked with the San Francisco photographer.

A full-floor unit in the Sherry-Netherland apartments in New York, designed by Martin Brûle

From Frank Gehry to Giancarlo Valle, Christopher Sturman’s subjects cover quite the architectural spectrum. Working between Los Angeles and New York, he has also photographed projects involving Renzo Piano and Clements Design.

A globally inspired interior captured by Teal Thomsen

Texas-born and California-based, Teal Thomsen possesses a deep appreciation for the American West. That’s clear in her photography, which features plenty of natural light and warm earth tones.

Delivering inviting shots for editorial and commercial clients alike, Madeline Tolle has produced a number of shoots for AD, including the LA pad of actor Jesse Tyler Ferguson.

A photo showcasing the Pira G2 system bookshelf by String Furniture

Pia Ulin knows her way around a camera—and clay. The photographer and ceramicist works between Los Angeles and Stockholm, with Halleroed and Lotta Agaton Interiors among her collaborators and IKEA and String Furniture in her campaign portfolio.

What happens when a documentary photographer spends time with a landscape studio? Will Warasila’s 10 Days with Terremoto photo series offers one answer. Based between Montana and Los Angeles, he looks beyond buildings to the people shaping their surroundings.
