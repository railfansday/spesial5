What did Giannis Antetokounmpo have to say after playing the Bucks?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

MIAMI – Giannis Antetokounmpo said in the immediate run-up to his first game playing against the Milwaukee Bucks, the organization that drafted him in 2013 and traded him to Miami in 2026, that it would just be another game.

In the Heat locker room after they toppled the Bucks 137-130 on Nov. 18 at the Kaseya Center, he reiterated as much because, "Basketball is basketball. I mean, I try to go out there and try to do whatever I could to help my team."

To do that in this game, Antetokounmpo had an efficient night, scoring 27 points on 7 of 11 shooting to go with 14 rebounds and nine assists. He was also 13-for-16 from the free throw line.

It was a cool performance on a rainy, and relatively chilled night by Miami standards, and Antetokounmpo kept that distant demeanor throughout the evening. When asked if the massive turnover in Milwaukee's roster and coaching staff perhaps added to that, Antetokounmpo demurred.

"Look, I think since I was a kid, when I play basketball I try to lock in as much as I can," he said. "People that have been around me know how I operate on game days. I try not to say much. I try not to interact as much. The only thing in my mind is 'how can I help my team win and how can I do my job?'

"It doesn't matter who I play. I could have been playing against my four brothers and my two sons. It doesn't matter. I try to do my best and try to do things that I've worked on. And try to have fun. It doesn't matter who; what uniform I go up against."

Five years ago, Giannis Antetokounmpo led the Milwaukee Bucks to their first NBA championship in 50 years after he scored 50 points in the clinching Game 6 over the Phoenix Suns. It capped an incredible playoff run that also saw him return faster than anyone thought from a hyperextended left knee he suffered during the Eastern Conference finals.
