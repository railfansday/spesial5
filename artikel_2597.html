Luka Dončić points, reaction as Lakers take on Hornets

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Los Angeles Lakers (13-2) got their ninth straight win, but it took everything to get past the Charlotte Hornets (4-10) in a 118-114 overtime thriller on Tuesday, Nov. 17. With both Austin Reaves and Walker Kessler ruled out ahead of tipoff, it looked like the onus would fall on Luka Dončić's ability to continue his historic start to the season, but it was Quentin Grimes who emerged as the hero of the night while Dončić had a pedestrian (by his standards) 36 points.

Dončić found himself in foul trouble early when he got whistled for his fourth in the final seconds before halftime, and the Hornets led by as many as 18 points in the third before a 7-2 run to end the quarter put the Lakers back within striking distance of a 13-point deficit.

With Dončić on the bench for the first six minutes of the final frame, Grimes went nuclear for 21 points, capping off the Lakers' rally with a last-second 3 – on an assist from Dončić – to force OT. The 26-year-old wing finished the night with a career-high 47 points on another career-high 10 threes made, making him the only Laker aside from Kobe Bryant to score at least 10 shots from long range.

Dončić, meanwhile, got his 11th consecutive 30-point game to continue his historic early season tear. Despite the fouls and some brutal turnovers in the first half, he finished the night with seven assists and six rebounds, including some key defensive boards and takeaways down the stretch.

Grimes and Dončić combined for 83 points and 15 threes as the Lakers outscored the Hornets 37-20 over the fourth quarter and overtime; within that stretch, Charlotte was held to just three points in OT.

Through 12 games played entering Tuesday, Dončić was leading the NBA with 39.0 points per game, 7.8 assists and 7.2 rebounds while shooting 48.6% from the field and a career-best 40.6% from 3-point range. He's coming off a 43-point performance in Sunday's win over the Phoenix Suns, his seventh game scoring at least 40. With Dončić on the floor, the Lakers have outscored their opponents by a total of 115 points.

His defense has also been spectacular, averaging 2.4 steals in LA's active eight-game win streak. The 27-year-old's four charges drawn are second-most in the league; he's also tied for third in defensive loose balls recovered (eight) this season.

USA TODAY Sports provided updates and highlights of the Lakers vs. Hornets game:

Quentin Grimes put his fingerprints all over this one. Aside from his career-high 47-point game and forcing overtime, the wing disrupted Charlotte's inbound pass to Royce O'Neale to force what ended up being the turnover that decided the game. The Lakers got the ball back up one with 17.5 seconds left, and the Hornets quickly fouled Grimes to send him to the line, where he hit both free throws to put LA up by three.

Matisse Thybulle forced another turnover on the ensuing possession and made a free throw to give the Lakers a two-possession lead -- and the win.

The Lakers have their first lead since the second quarter on a fadeaway from Dončić at the 3:15 mark of the overtime period.

It was only fitting for the hot hand to save LA's win-streak at the last second. Matisse Thybulle grabbed a missed Coby White free throw and fed it to Luka Dončić, who made his way to the three-point line but ultimately found Grimes on the opposite wing.

It was Grimes' 10th made three of the night, and at 45 points he's now one shy of his career-high.

Dončić reaps the rewards of Grimes' hot hand in the fourth, completing the rally with a three of his own to tie the game at 104-all with just over three minutes to go. The shot puts Dončić at 32 points on the night, his 11th consecutive 30-point game.

Elgin Baylor is the only other Lakers with multiple streaks of at least 11 consecutive 30-point games.

This is supposed to be a Luka Dončić live blog, but it's Grimes who has taken over with the Slovenian superstar on the bench halfway through the fourth quarter. Grimes continues his hot streak from Sunday, and has now hit 8-of-10 threes -- tying his career-high -- and has scored 13 points in the quarter to reach 37 points. It's Grimes' ninth career 30-point game.

The Lakers now trail by just four, and Dončić has checked back into the game.

It's been a rough night for LA. Luka Dončić is up to 29 points but went cold shooting 1-for-5 over the final six minutes of the frame, the lone bucket a floater at the 46-second mark. The Hornets have led by as many as 18 but a 7-2 run to close the quarter has put the Lakers back within striking distance.

Dončić returned to the floor at his usual time despite the early foul trouble, and that risk worked out early on as he went off for 13 points in the second quarter... until he got whistled for shoving Royce O'Neale coming up the court with eight seconds to go before halftime for his fourth foul of the game. JJ Redick voiced his displeasure at the call and picked up a technical in the process.

The Lakers trail the Hornets 66-59 at the half after an impressive showing from Brandon Miller and Naz Reid, who have 19 points apiece and have led Charlotte -- the worst shooting team in the league at 40% entering Tuesday -- to a 54.8% field goal percentage through the first two frames.

Dončić has 22 points, four assists and two rebounds. Quentin Grimes has 16 points and has made four of his five three-pointers, but they're the only two Lakers in double figures at halftime. Matisse Thybulle (six points) is the only other Laker with more than four points.

It's been a frustrating first quarter for the Lakers superstar, who scored just four points through the first 10 minutes of the opening frame as Sion James has been playing suffocating defense. Dončić managed to get five points in the final two minutes before he was called for a charge on James on the Lakers' second-to-last possession. Head coach JJ Redick challenged the call, but the ruling was upheld due to an off-arm shove from Dončić.

Dončić finished the quarter with nine points shooting just 33.3% and is one of six from deep as the Lakers trail Charlotte 34-30. He usually sits the first six minutes of the second quarter, but Redick has a decision to make as he now has to manage his best player's foul troubles with his second and third best players both out.
