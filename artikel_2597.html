MVP race is currently down to Joe Burrow and Brock Purdy

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

With 11 weeks down and seven to go, the MVP race is currently down to two players.

The favorites for the top individual honor are Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow and 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy.

Burrow has the lowest odds, at -110. Purdy stands at +120.

The next guy on the list is Bills quarterback Josh Allen, at +850. Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes is +1600.

It can, and will, change. A huge game from Allen or Mahomes (or both) on Thanksgiving night could close the gap. For now, though, it’s Burrow and Purdy.

Purdy strengthened his case on Sunday night, by cracking the code on the Minnesota defense. If the 49ers ultimately secure the No. 1 seed in the NFC, that’ll help Purdy’s case, too.

Regardless, Purdy is finally getting the appreciation he deserves. And the ultimate acknowledgement will come if he ultimately takes home the MVP award — making him the first 49ers player to win it since Steve Young in 1994.
