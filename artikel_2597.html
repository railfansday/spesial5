Broncos suspend RB ahead of Steelers matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Broncos are suspending running back J.K. Dobbins for one game due to "conduct detrimental" to the team, Denver head coach Sean Payton said Tuesday.

Payton said it was "head coach's decision" and declined to provide details. Dobbins will miss all team activities leading up to and including Denver's Black Friday game against the Pittsburgh Steelers, per ESPN.

Dobbins opened the Broncos' season as the team's starting running back, but he's only started one of the team's last four games entering the Steelers matchup. Broncos rookie running back Jonah Coleman broke out with a 24-carry, 111-yard, three touchdown on Sunday against the Raiders.

However, Dobbins' absence will still affect the Broncos' depth, with top receiving back RJ Harvey still on injured reserve. Denver could turn to Tyler Badie, who is in his 21-day return from injured reserve this week with a thumb issue, as RB2 against the Steelers.

Dobbins and Coleman are currently the only running backs on Denver's 53-man roster.
