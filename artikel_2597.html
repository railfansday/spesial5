Bills Mafia’s confidence reaches season high ahead of Week 10 at Jets

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

If you've been a part of Buffalo Rumblings for a bit now, you're likely familiar with our weekly Reacts polls. Well, once again, we asked your thoughts about the Buffalo Bills and you reacted!

Things are pretty darn good in Bills Country these days, thanks to Buffalo's 7-1 record and current place atop the list in the AFC playoff list. There are a lot of games left to play, but if the season ended today, the Bills would have earned the number-one seed and a bye week to begin the playoffs. Remember, all this is happening while the team navigates a first-time head coach in Joe Brady, and a first-time NFL defensive coordinator Jim Leonhard.

Buffalo's offense is legit, ranking first in the NFL in points per game (32.0) and also No. 1 in total yards per game (386.4). As for the defense, it's appears to be turning the right corners, improving to a middle-of-the-pack unit overall since the bye week. That's after spending a bulk of the early season among the worst defenses in the league.

Quarterback Josh Allen and running back James Cook III have once again put the NFL on notice, which is of course due in no small measure to the team's offensive line and a bevy of skill players who are equally interested in blocking defenders into tomorrow on any snap.

Is 7-1 a surprise? Likely not given Allen. Yet it's still worth wondering how much leeway Brady and the new coaching staff should get in their first season, even considering Allen. It's worth noting Buffalo's recent favorable stretch of games against backup and emergency quarterbacks. That shouldn't excuse away their legitimacy as a Super Bowl contender. Good teams defeat bad teams, and the Bills have done that without little problem.

Impressively, Buffalo is the only undefeated road team (4-0) left in the NFL this season. Winning is tough in general, but doing so on the road presents teams with the final boss of football challenges.

If our latest poll results are any indication, confidence is contagious. Bills Mafia has bought in — no strings attached. Heading into Buffalo's Week 10, 95% of Rumblings voters are confident in the current direction of the team under Brady, Leonhard, and president of football operations/general manager.

You may be curious to see what fan confidence numbers look like up to this point in the season, which, if true, means you're in luck. So, what can we take away here from that which has begun taking from humans? It's hardly Earth-shattering, but like almost every team's fan base, the beginning of the season is filled with massive (where are my Rumblers playing drinking games to this word?) expectations. That usually sets an often-difficult-to-maintain high bar.

Yet lo and behold, we have a new season high this week. Winning, so contagious.

That low mark? You know what that's all about… those pesky New England Patriots, and Buffalo's only loss so far this season.

Can the Bills maintain their current level of play? Honestly, it still feels like they have quite a bit to go before reaching their full potential. However, big games await within the pressure cooker that is winter football. That's precisely why Beane went all out as able at the trade deadline. Buffalo can leave nothing to chance, regardless of encouraging signs on defense.

Speaking of Beane and the team's front office, let's tackle the results for our other poll question…

The ever-scrutinized Beane once again made a trade-deadline move to bring in a talented veteran player. While many thought the Bills would struggle to swing too big due to salary cap concerns. Some were resigned to the idea of Beane doing nothing before the deadline came and went, only to be pleasantly surprised.

I'm of course talking about safety Amani Hooker, who joins the Bills as the current interception co-leader. Buffalo prioritized a need at safety following C.J. Gardner-Johnson's release, and Damar Hamlin's subsequent promotion to starting safety. Where CJGJ looked out of sorts, Hamlin settled things and played competent football.

But to potentially add someone of Hooker's talent was a situation Beane refused to ignore. It eventually worked out in his favor after lunch the day of the trade deadline, with Buffalo sending a 2027 second-round pick to the Tennessee Titans to land Hooker and a 2027 fifth-round pick in return.

We asked Rumblers what they thought of the trade — a move that provides the Bills with a new starting safety under contract for the next two seasons. As you can imagine, the trade was met with rave reviews.

Leading the way were 71% of voters who gave Beane and Buffalo an "A" for the trade. That was followed by 28% of voters more comfortable stamping a "B" on the trade in the early hours. You can likely ignore the 1% of voters who gave the trade an "F".

The great news is that Hooker is expected to make his Bills debut this afternoon in New Jersey against the Jets.

Are you curious to see how Jets fans over at Gang Green Nation feel about their team heading into Week 10? Wonder no more: Just 29% of voters at GGN are confident right now in this year's Jets team.

This week's national poll results offer a couple interesting talking points. First up we have voters' thoughts on the NFL's best head coach to this point of the season. Bills Mafia is most interested to hear how Joe Brady did, or if he even made the cut. He did, but comes up in last place in this poll with just 2% of the vote.

Brady trails Kyle Shanahan (leading with 32%), Ben Johnson (20%), John Harbaugh (18%), Liam Coen (17%) and a catch-all of "other" options (11%). Why wouldn't the head coach of the team with the NFL's current best record fare better in this poll?

It's very likely the Josh Allen effect. With Allen, excellence is expected (to borrow from Porsche), as is success.

Next voters were asked to weigh in about players who were traded, and the impact they'll bring to their new teams. Are you surprised to learn that Amani Hooker didn't register in the top five of the final results? Yes, quarterback Kirk Cousins (leading here with 54%) should provide a massive boost to the Minnesota Vikings. His return the NFC North should pay huge dividends for the Vikings' offense, especially wide receiver Justin Jefferson. By nature, the quarterback position has the biggest individual impact.

Not all that far behind is Cousins' new North frenemy, defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons (35%) of the Chicago Bears. He's already played lights-out in two games for Chicago.

After Cousins and Simmons, the numbers fall off a cliff. Thirty-two-year-old defensive tackle DeForest Buckner (4%) returns to the San Francisco 49ers by way of the Indianapolis Colts.

Safety Jessie Bates III, someone plenty of Bills fans hoped to see land in Buffalo also earned just 4% of the vote after being traded to the Jacksonville Jaguars. Defensive tackle Jordan Davis also heads to Duval County, and rounds out the top five, but won just 3% of votes.
