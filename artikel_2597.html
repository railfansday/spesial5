Watch Nolan James Jr run over a defender for a Notre Dame touchdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

No. 3 Notre Dame has been moving the ball well against Boston College so far, and the second Fighting Irish touchdown came at the end of another drive in which the Irish have been working to establish the run.

It also showed that Nolan James Jr. can be a punishing runner.

James lowered his shoulder into a defender on a second-down-and-8 run against the Eagles. He didn't need the goal line, but he got there.

The run finished off a 12-play, 80-yard drive that took 6:19 off the clock.
