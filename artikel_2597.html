Ryan Flournoy records 71 yards, touchdown in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dallas Cowboys wide receiver Ryan Flournoy posted six receptions for 71 yards and a touchdown on seven targets in Week 10 against the San Francisco 49ers.

Why it Matters: There is plenty of football to go around in Big D. George Pickens had 11 receptions for 145 yards and three touchdowns, while CeeDee Lamb went for seven grabs, 82 yards and a score, and Flournoy still had a nice outing. He has emerged as a low-end WR4 or high-end WR5 on the rise, and he'll be a fantasy must-start if anything should happen to Lamb or Pickens.
