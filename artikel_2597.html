Expert now claims it wasn’t actually Mike McCarthy’s decision to release Aaron Rodgers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Isaiah Stanback doubts that Mike McCarthy was the decision-maker in ending Aaron Rodgers' time with the Steelers.

Some may have jumped to this conclusion due to the news of Rodgers being released coming around an hour after he publicly criticised the head coach.

However, when looking at the big picture, Stanback believes that there is more to this breakdown between quarterback and franchise.

Analyst Isaiah Stanback shared his thoughts on Aaron Rodgers being released from the Steelers during Good Morning Football.

When commenting on the fractured relationship between the legendary quarterback and his coach, Stanback raised a larger point.

He believes that feelings being hurt and disrespect being shown would have impacted the stance of the people above Mike McCarthy even if the comments were directed at him.

"I don't believe this was a McCarthy decision," Stanback theorised. "I don't know. I can speculate like the rest of us are speculating, but I do not believe this was McCarthy's decision."

He admitted that while Rodgers and his former coach have had their "tiffs", their relationship has previously been compared to an "old married couple", suggesting that they butted heads but made it work.

"If that was the case and they'd had some kind of tiff, he [Rodgers] would have been bumped down to number two. Go sit down. If something happens to Mason Rudolph, cool, you come in and save the day because we're in playoff contention."

Stanback went on to add that "coaches and organisations don't get as emotional with decisions like this", suggesting that he thinks it is possible the call was not made by someone from the Steelers either.

The former Super Bowl champion didn't go on to elaborate or point any fingers following his comments.
