Fins Up, Fins Down | Winners and losers from Miami Dolphins Week 11 defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Miami Dolphins have played ten games during the 2026 NFL season, losing all but one of them. In Week 11, they fell victim to their AFC East division rivals — the Buffalo Bills. It wasn't quite the mollywhopping most expected, but Miami still lost by 17 points to Josh Allen, James Cook and company.

The Dolphins defense forced multiple turnovers — causing Allen to throw two (nearly three) interceptions. However, Miami's offense — led by Kyle McCord in his second-ever start — couldn't hold up their end of the bargain. They settled for just three field goals on the day, but at least new kicker Daniel Carlson nailed all three — including two 52-yarders.

After a couple of games where he didn't live up to the standard he had set earlier in the year, Dolphins rookie linebacker Jacob Rodriguez finds himself once again trending in the right direction as our Fins Up player of the week. Against the Bills, Rodriguez led the team in tackles (13 combined) and also had one of those interceptions of Josh Allen.

Miami's high second-round investment in Rodriguez is quickly becoming one of the major steals of the 2026 NFL Draft. Rodriguez ranks 7th in the National Football League in tackles with 93 — sitting just one ahead of his counterpart in South Florida, Jordyn Brooks. He also has a sack, a forced fumble and two interceptions.

After a decent NFL debut last week against the Indianapolis Colts, quarterback Kyle McCord came crashing back down to earth in Week 11 against the Bills. For his poor performance, he earns our Fins Down designation this week. Now, McCord certainly wasn't helped by his receivers, as Chris Bell and Caleb Douglas combined for five drops on Sunday. However, McCord wasn't playing to his capabilities — neither throwing with strong anticipation or accuracy against Buffalo.

He finished the game with 12 completions on 33 attempts (36.4%) for just 102 yards passing, zero touchdowns and an interception. His passer rating of 32.6 was the lowest of any quarterback in the NFL through Sunday's contests. Daniel Jones' 45.9 passer rating on Thursday night earned him his walking papers the following day.
