The Wait Is Over: Black Friday’s Best Apple Watch Deals Are Finally Here

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Just as I predicted, the Apple Watch SE 3 and Series 12 are at record-low prices.

They say good things come to those who wait—and when it comes to Apple Watch deals, that couldn’t be more true. Amazon’s 12-day Black Friday sale is here, and just as I predicted, the Series 12 and SE 3 are both on sale for the lowest prices we’ve ever seen. However, steals I saw on the Ultra 3 and Ultra 4 earlier this month have sold out—but I’m expecting a restock.

So, should you shop these record-low deals now or wait until Black Friday (November 27)? As a tech-obsessed editor who tracks these prices all year long, I’m sharing everything you need to know below.

The best Apple Watch deal I’m seeing for Black Friday? The Apple Watch SE 3, which just returned to a record-low price of $199. It’s the most budget-friendly smartwatch in Apple’s lineup, but the S10 chip-powered device doesn’t skimp on premium features. There are tons of handy health insights to help you reach your fitness goals, from workout monitors that’ll count those 10,000 steps to sleep scores and even period cycle tracking.

This watch is available in 40mm or 44mm sizes, making it a great option if you prefer a sleeker look that won’t compete with your outfits. It also boasts up to 18 hours of battery life, with a fast-charging mode that’ll get you from 0 percent to 80 percent battery in just 45 minutes. Basically, this is your best option for a no-frills, entry-level smartwatch that won’t break the bank.

If you’re looking for more in-depth fitness insights and faster processing, the brand-new Apple Watch Series 12 is also worth adding to cart while it’s on sale for its lowest price ever. This S11 chip-powered model boasts a longer battery life than the SE 3 at up to 24 hours, and a slightly larger screen size at 42mm or 46mm. It boasts the same safety features and fitness trackers as the SE 3, plus upgraded insights like a Blood Oxygen app and hypertension notifications.

The Apple Watch Ultra 4 is the largest and most premium model in Apple’s lineup, with a 49mm case size and S11 chip. Most impressive of all, it has the longest battery life of any Apple Watch—50 hours, to be exact—which is more than double the life of the SE 3 or Series 12. It’s currently out of stock, but it was on sale for a record-low price of $700 earlier this month.

Similarly, the Apple Watch Ultra 3 (2025) is currently sold out, but it was on sale for $549 a few weeks ago. This S10 chip-powered model offers health insights like heart rhythm reports, blood oxygen scoring, and sleep score tracking, as well as safety features like crash detection and emergency SOS. Plus, it boasts an impressive 42-hour battery life to boot.

My two cents? The Apple Watch SE 3 and Series 12 are the best models for most shoppers, and they’re finally at record-low prices. I recommend scooping them up now before they inevitably sell out—no need to wait until next week. However, if you prefer a high-tech model like the Ultra 3 or 4, I predict at least one will be back in stock soon.
