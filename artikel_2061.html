FA Trophy third round draw in full: National League's Boreham Wood, Southend United learn opponents

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

By Darren Plant | 16 Nov 2026 15:42

Holders Southend United have been handed a home tie against Dartford in the FA Trophy third round.

Southend, who are managed by former England international Kieron Dyer, will square off versus the National League South side on December 12.

Meanwhile, National League leaders Boreham Wood will also face National League South opposition in the form of Salisbury.

In the Northern section of the draw, Harrogate Town were handed a trip to National League North outfit Darlington.

AFC Fylde will go up against fellow National League club Halifax FC, while Yeovil Town will travel to Tonbridge Angels.

Carlisle United have been handed a home tie against Southport of the National League North.
