Arsenal youngster suffers international injury setback

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Ceadach O’Neill has suffered a setback in his development after being ruled out of the Northern Ireland squad due to injury.

O’Neill had been named by Michael O’Neill in the squad for the last two games of the Nations League against Ukraine and Hungary. The 18-year-old was hoping to make his first senior start after appearing in four games for Northern Ireland, but all as a substitute.

However, he will miss the international break after suffering an injury. He was also unable to play in Arsenal’s Under-21s 5-2 win over Aston Villa on Sunday.

The news comes at a good time for the winger who has recently been getting more attention in Arsenal’s first team.

O’Neill has impressed Mikel Arteta in training with the senior players and has also made his first team debut in the Carabao Cup.

His performances have indicated he may be another academy player who will fight for a spot under Arteta. He also impressed during Arsenal’s loss to Fleetwood in the Carabao Cup.

The teenager was one of only a few players to leave the game with their head held high after providing Gyokeres with a good cross to bring the score back level.

He was also included in the Arsenal squad that travelled to Liverpool in the Premier League. Although he did not play, it was still a positive result.

The inclusion of the Northern Ireland call-up means he could continue to develop. Getting his first senior start would help him show he can perform outside of the academy.

O’Neill’s departure is a key moment for Northern Ireland. They still have much to play for in their final Nations League fixtures.

The squad sit one point above Hungary in Group B2 with Ukraine four points behind. They must now face Ukraine before they take on Hungary.

A first-place finish would guarantee promotion to League A. Finishing second would see them enter the promotion play-offs. With a promising young player leaving after the last international break, Michael O’Neill will be disappointed to lose him.

The manager praised O’Neill for his contribution to the squad and wished him a speedy recovery. The winger will now work on getting back to full fitness.

His recent inclusion in the first team, shows how far he has come in recent weeks. He will be hoping to get another chance when he is fully fit.
