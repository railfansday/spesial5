Di Marzio: Sporting CP striker emerges as idea for Milan amid Amorim’s admiration

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

AC Milan want to sign a striker in the winter transfer window, and Gianluca Di Marzio has put forward a new target.

Di Marzio has published an update on his website regarding Serie A clubs and the search for a centre-forward that many of them have commenced. He confirms that Milan are among the teams seeking attacking reinforcements, salso o they can loan Francesco Camarda out.

A name that’s been floating around for a few days – in addition to Endrick of Real Madrid and Nicolo Tresoldi of Club Brugge – is Sporting CP’s Fotis Ioannidis. He’s a player that Ruben Amorim really likes, having kept a close eye on things in Portugal, but Roma are keen too.

Ioannidis is a name worth considering even if he may not be top of the list, given Milan are the ‘only team without spending constraints’ in Serie A. Cardinale has already shown that if he wants a player and Milan truly needs him, he’s ready to invest.

The 26-year-old has racked up two goals and two assists in 10 league games for the Lisbon-based club, who just welcomed Paulo Fonseca as their new head coach. Overall he has eight goals and six assists in 37 games (all competitions), plus seven in 26 for the Greece national team.
