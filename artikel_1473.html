Manchester United have found Benjamin Šeško’s replacement

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

With the absence of Benjamin Šeško until the start of 2027 and the poor form of Matheus Cunha, Manchester United does not really have solutions at the forefront of its attack. To fill this gap, the Mancunian club plans to take action this winter to attract Dominic Calvert-Lewin, according to The Athletic. Indeed, the Leeds United striker has been a long-time target of the Red Devils’ sporting management and the recruitment unit is working to assess the possibility of a transfer during the winter transfer window.

Arriving free in August 2025, the former Everton center forward has found the net 20 times in 49 appearances with the Peacocks. This season, the 29-year-old England international (14 caps) has already scored five goals in ten matches in all competitions (717 minutes played), including four in eight Premier League matches (686 minutes played).
