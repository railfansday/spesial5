Gollini remains sidelined, Vasquez to replace him

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Roma lost at Old Trafford, but Devis Vasquez put in a solid performance and did his best to counter Man United’s late push.

The Colombian, who returned to the national team after several years, will have to repeat his performance at the Olimpico.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, Pierluigi Gollini is not fit for tonight’s match, November 7th, against Sassuolo.

Gollini was forced to miss the match against the Red Devils due to back pain, which he suffered a few hours before the Champions League match, and the problem hasn’t subsided yet.

The 1998-born player, who until a few months ago was out of the squad, will return to Serie A 17 months and 13 days after his last appearance (Empoli-Verona, May 25, 2025).
