A new mole among the Blues? Cyril Hanouna drops a clue to Zinédine Zidane

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sixteen years after the Blues mole in Knysna, is the French team harboring a new secret informant? This is what Cyril Hanouna has been clearly implying for several days, by making shattering revelations behind the scenes of the French selection in his show All Beautiful All N9ufon W9.

After ensuring that Kylian Mbappé and Zinédine Zidane were at odds, which the coach denied at a press conference before France-Turkey, and that the Real Madrid striker had alienated Ousmane Dembélé and a large part of the locker room, the host affirms that another conflict exists within the group.

“There is a war between the captains and those who have just arrived and who are starting to piss off”he says, while specifying that “Zizou” is “very close” new players he recently called up. Information that he describes as “safe”. Where does he get it from? “Since Thursday, they have been looking for the mole”he laughs.

“Everyone is stressed. In the staff, they all look at each other: who is the mole? Who gives information to Hanouna? “, rejoices the presenter, before addressing the 1998 world champion: “Zinédine, you know, it’s not on your staff. And unfortunately, Zinédine, you won’t be able to fire the person who is the mole. »

And Cyril Hanouna continued: “He’s someone who’s invincible. That’s all I have to say. I kiss you, Zinédine, and you know that I love you. » These so-called internal tensions will perhaps ease themselves since the Blues will not see each other again before March 2027, for the quarter-finals of the League of Nations.
