Romano: Man Utd Set to be Offered Chance to Sign Balogun for Pochettino

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Manchester United are already considering the moves they want to make in the January transfer window, according to Fabrizio Romano.

Having transformed their attack and midfield during the previous two summer transfer windows, it was expected that the focus would be on doing the same for the defence in 2027.

However, it appears as though that plan is too late, given what we have seen from the Man United defence this season.

Ahead of Mauricio Pochettino's first match in charge against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday, the Red Devils lie in the Premier League relegation zone having won just one of their opening 10 top flight matches this term.

United have conceded at least two goals in matches against Aston Villa, AS Roma, Bournemouth, Chelsea, Como, Atletico Madrid, Brighton and Hove Albion, Everton, Ipswich Town and Hull City, and that clearly is nowhere near good enough for a club with ambitions to finish in the top four again and make progress in the Champions League.

Romano has already confirmed that the club is looking at the centre-back position ahead of January, with doubts over the future of Lisandro Martinez following his public outburst against Jason Wilcox following defeat to Atletico, which saw him given a dressing down by United's director of football, and a fine.

Martinez's contract runs out at the end of the season and while United have an option to extend it by a further 12 months, it appears as though a 2027 exit is currently looking like the most likely outcome.

United have been keen to sign a new left-back too in recent months but, according to Romano, the centre-forward position is seen as a bigger priority in the New Year.

That is hardly surprising, given it is now November and the club has spent most of the campaign playing without a recognised striker following an injury issue suffered by Benjamin Sesko.

AS Monaco striker Folarin Balogun came close to joining Everton on deadline day in the summer, and after talk he could reunite with Pochettino at Old Trafford, Romano has confirmed that he expects United to be offered the 25-year-old.

Speaking on one of his latest YouTube videos, the Italian journalist said:

"Some of you are asking me now about Folarin Balogun and Manchester United. Why these links? Because Balogon is not playing that much at Monaco at the start of the season after that crazy deadline day story with Everton.

"And because Mauricio Pochettino is now the Man United boss, and so obviously Pochettino and Balagun had a fantastic connection with the U.S. men's national team. Balaguna had a very good World Cup, so obviously the connection between Balagun and Man United, with United looking at a striker and Mauricio Pochettino as a coach, is quite normal and natural.

"But my understanding is that at the moment there is no active conversation taking place, so there are no discussions about Balogun to Man United.

I will not be surprised if Folarin Balogun gets offered to Manchester United because the relationship with his agents is a good relationship, and because Man United will look at strikers.

"So obviously, the agents of strikers potentially available in January will be ready to offer the boy to clubs, including Manchester United. Now even more [likely] with Pochettino."

United chiefs need to have a serious think about exactly what they want from a new striker in January.

The January window is widely regarded as a seller's market rather than a buyer's market, and United are unlikely to be able to land their first-choice target in the New Year.

Then, the Red Devils must decide if they are going to use the loan market, or sign a player on a permanent basis if they can negotiate a sensible price.

GIVEMESPORT sources revealed previously that the club do hold an interest in Leeds United striker Dominic Calvert-Lewin, but the former Everton star is more likely to extend his contract at Elland Road.

While Balogun would only arrive at Old Trafford with two Premier League appearances under his belt, the fact that he immediately knows Pochettino and what he wants should help him to settle quickly.

Balogun has produced consistently under the Argentine at international level, while he has been a prolific performer for Monaco too, prior to this season.

Balogun's record under Pochettino for the USA

It was going to cost Everton £40m to prise the striker away from Ligue 1 in September, and it remains to be seen whether United would be willing to reach that fee for the former Arsenal attacker.

What is clear though, is that he does know how to finish, having been labelled 'world-class' by a beIN Sports commentator previously following a stunning goal against Marseille.

If he could translate his form to the Premier League, which is no easy feat, then he could be a no-brainer addition in January.

A loan with an option to buy would probably be the most sensible deal from a United point of view, but that may not be to Monaco's liking.
