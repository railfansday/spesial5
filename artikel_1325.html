Champions League: Lens vs. Como – probable line-ups, where to watch on TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Cesc Fabregas’ Como visit Lens in the Champions League Round 4 with Nico Paz and Anastasios Douvikas expected to start up front.

It kicks off at 21:00 CET [20:00 GMT], the same time as Porto-Napoli.

Tuesday’s Champions League action brought mixed results for Serie A clubs, with Inter beating Feyenoord 3-0 away and Roma losing 3-2 at Old Trafford to Manchester United after wasting a two-goal lead.

Como have three unavailable players: Moise Kean, Trevoh Chalobah and Assane Diao.

According to Sky and Gazzetta, Martin Baturina is expected to return to the starting XI after being benched against Venezia in the previous Serie A round.

Paz and Douvikas, Como’s main attacking men, are expected to get no rest tonight, while Lucas Da Cunha will start again at right wing. Mattia Liberali is available on the bench after recovering from an injury.

Former Udinese star Florian Thauvin will get the nod up front for Lens, and Saud Abdulhamid is another familiar face for Serie A fans. The ex-Roma defender is expected to start as a right wing-back tonight.

TNT Sports 6 and HBO Max will broadcast the match live in the UK and Ireland, and Paramount+ in the USA. Football Italia will provide live updates via a live blog.

Lens (3-4-2-1): Risser; Todibo, Baidoo, Antonio; Aguilar, Haidara, Cuisance, Udol; Thauvin, Kadile; Ivanovic.

Como (4-2-3-1): Butez; Yan Couto, Ramon, Kempf, Valle; Milla, Perrone; Da Cunha, Nico Paz, Baturina; Douvikas.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
