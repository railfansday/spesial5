Ten-Man Blues Undone At Brentford

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Everton’s return to Premier League action proved a forgettable one as a 17-minute first-half spell ultimately condemned them to a 4-0 defeat at Brentford.

Looking to extend their three-match unbeaten run built before the recent international break, the Blues struggled to get going at the Gtech Stadium – before rotten luck left them with a mountain to climb.

On 23 minutes, Keane Lewis-Potter’s run into the area was picked out by Jaidon Anthony as his finish took a horrible deflection off Jarrad Branthwaite to beat Jordan Pickford.

Worse misfortune struck Everton two minutes later as Lewis-Potter’s shot was blocked by James Tarkowski, fell straight back into the path of the same Brentford player whose rebound was destined to be kept out again, only to hit Thierno Barry and wrong-foot Pickford.

The Toffees’ hopes of mounting a comeback were then all but ended five minutes before the interval when Tarkowski was penalised for a sliding tackle on Yehor Yarmoliuk.

Initially slow to award the free-kick, referee Craig Pawson eventually showed the Blues captain a yellow card – only to be called over by VAR and soon change his decision to red for violent conduct.

Left to battle on with 10 men, Everton had a sniff of a comeback with seconds left of the first half when fine work from Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall ended with the No.8 chipping in a cross that Barry did well to nod into the bottom right-hand corner, only for keeper Caoimhin Kelleher to somehow claw the ball away.

Jack Grealish came on for the start of the second half and soon made an impact with a teasing centre across the six-yard box that just evaded Barry.

But any hopes of an unlikely fightback were ended on 67 minutes as Vitaly Janelt fired a 25-yard strike into the top right-hand corner.

And a miserable night was summed up in the dying moments when Damsgaard's corner found its way to substitute Fabio Carvalho to tap over the line from a yard out.
