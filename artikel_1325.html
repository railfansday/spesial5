Football Italia Show: Azzurri beat arrogant France, we told you about Dimarco!

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The latest episode of the Football Italia Show, filmed at San Siro, examines Italy’s 3-1 win over France in the Nations League.

The victory allowed the Azzurri to climb to the top of group A1, level on ten points with Les Blues, while Belgium follow with nine.

France took an early lead through Michael Olise after just 10 minutes, capitalising on a misplaced pass from Gianluigi Donnarumma. However, Italy responded emphatically, with Pio Esposito restoring parity before half-time.

The second half saw further drama, as Rayan Cherki struck the post before Sebastiano Esposito missed a penalty. Davide Frattesi then put the Azzurri ahead, finishing off a move created by Federico Dimarco, before Pio Esposito converted from the spot to complete the 3-1 triumph.

Football Italia was among the accredited media at San Siro, with our editor offering an exclusive pitchside assessment of the encounter at full-time.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.

Thanks for this Lorenzo. Very good performance and a great result. Fair play to the players I doubted.

DiMarco is still a wait and see. He can’t do it for 90 for Italy. As a sub in the right moment and against tired legs or maybe a free kick needed to unlock a game. But in the wrong one he could get bullied and it be detrimental. If it was like basketball rolling on and off and extra large squads he may be ideal.

Peak DiMarco got bullied by Spain (amongst other games Norway / Bosnia) & PSG when it counted as a starter for club and country, albeit different formation. When all the right wingers are in and ready, it’s whether he is justified to take someone else’s place. As an extra player sure but if it’s 2028 and there’s one spot left and Inacio is starting for Dortmund as a winger doing well, or Vavassori in Serie A or Croci breakout season at 17 or Cacciamani only one of these guys or DiMarco, no thanks.

Great result but we’re getting excited about an arrogant France in transition with many holes in their team (aside from the hype from the British media).

For years I’ve believed in Italy and the players without bias and honesty (often not picked i.e Kayode since Spalletti, often too many undeserved picks Di Lorenzo / Mancini / Cristante etc).

Cambiaghi & DiMarco vs Spain or England (who I don’t rate as highly as everyone seems too – since despite some good players, they always struggle when they play a top team & have had easy draws for years). Then again I wouldn’t want to risk testing these guys capabilities in games that matter more – when we’ve seen their ceiling.

In Euro 2024 Cambiaghi would be great instead of OR alternating with Zaccagni, I liked Pepe in 2010 but those were desperate times for Italy. Not the same today with players at our disposal.
