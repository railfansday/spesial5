Hoskinson Weighs In on Cardano (ADA) Price Collapse

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Charles Hoskinson, founder of Cardano (ADA), has tried to clear the air regarding the utility of the cryptocurrency. This comes as ADA has fallen more than 95% from its all-time high.

Cardano founder Charles Hoskinson responded to frustrated Cardano community members who are disappointed by the blockchain’s pace of development and inability to rebound from the current slump.

On Nov. 21, Cardano founder Charles Hoskinson took to X to counter criticism about the current state of Cardano.

The exchange was triggered by a joke made by an X user stating that Grand Theft Auto 6 came out before a working Cardano blockchain.

Hoskinson replied to the comment, saying: “Cardano works.”

Later, Hoskinson addressed the poor price performance of the cryptocurrency and explained that price doesn’t always reflect the capabilities of blockchain networks.

“Yeah. Price doesn't equal utility. Is Solana 75 percent less capable than it's all time high? It's an infinitely better product today for many more people. And so it is for Cardano,” he said.

What this means is that even if the value of a blockchain network's native cryptocurrency is dropping, the technology can still advance.

An X user, however, contested his comparison with Solana, stating that Cardano hasn’t been able to retake its previous all-time high even after years of development.

This drew an increasingly irritated response from Hoskinson.

“So every single thing a f****** scam? Everything they do failed? I’m so tired of you people,” said the Cardano founder.

This recent statement by Hoskinson comes as Cardano (ADA) remains one of the worst-performing major cryptocurrencies in the current market downturn.

As per CoinGlass data, ADA is currently trading at around $0.14. The cryptocurrency is down by about 57% since the beginning of 2026.

It has lost a whopping 95.4% of its value from its all-time high of $3.09, set in September 2021.

On Nov. 21, ADA briefly dipped toward $0.13. This came as another sell-off hit the broader crypto market.

Cardano has been in a steady decline, leading to criticism regarding its adoption, activity in its ecosystem and ability to compete with the likes of Ethereum and Solana.

Nonetheless, Charles Hoskinson has maintained that short-term market performance should not be conflated with the progress of blockchain technology over the long run.

Earlier this month, the Cardano founder tried to reassure frustrated ADA holders and tell them that things would eventually get better.

“It's all going to be ok. Everyone will be happy next year after all this madness, hate and idiocy,” said Hoskinson in a previous X post.
