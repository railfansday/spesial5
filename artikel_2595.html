Packers activate RB Chris Brooks, WR Skyy Moore

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Packers will have two offensive players back in the lineup for Sunday's matchup with the Vikings.

Running back Chris Brooks and wide receiver Skyy Moore were both activated from injured reserve on Saturday. Brooks ran 15 times for 70 yards in the first four weeks of the season and has missed five games with an ankle injury. Moore has also been out with an ankle injury and had seven catches for 35 yards in five appearances.

Brooks provides depth in the backfield with Josh Jacobs out this week. Kaleb Johnson, MarShawn Lloyd, and Pierre Strong are also on hand.

The Packers made space for Brooks and Moore by placing tight end Josh Whyle on injured reserve and waiving wide receiver Coleman Owen.
