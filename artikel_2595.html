Kyler Murray listed as limited in Vikings practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Vikings quarterback Kyler Murray’s practice status changed on Wednesday.

Murray has missed the last three weeks with a hamstring injury, but the team listed him as a limited participant in practice on this week’s first injury report.

The Vikings traded for Kirk Cousins after Murray’s injury and he is now the team’s starter. Carson Wentz has been backing him up and it is unclear if that will change should Murray be cleared ahead of Sunday’s game against the Falcons.

Wide receiver Jordan Addison (thumb), left tackle Christian Darrisaw (shoulder), defensive lineman Jalen Redmond (ankle), cornerback Isaiah Rodgers (thigh), and safety Harrison Smith (thigh) were listed as out of practice. Wide receivers Tai Felton (hip) and Myles Price (knee, groin) joined Murray as limited participants.
