‘Doing all that crying’ – Kendrick Perkins snaps at ‘soft’ Donovan Mitchell’s request for NBA

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Kendrick Perkins called out Donovan Mitchell after his request to the NBA to possibly cancel the Cleveland Cavaliers' game against the Dallas Mavericks on Saturday.

The seven-time All-Star's request was led by the fact that there was a nationwide grounding of the flights by the FAA, which resulted in the Cavaliers having to fly on Saturday from Miami, after playing the Heat on Friday.

Eventually, the game went on, but the team decided to rest all their starters against the Mavs. However, Perkins wanted to make his displeasure towards Mitchell clear after his comments to the media.

After facing a tough battle against the Miami Heat, which they lost 133-121, the Cavs had to fly back home to play the Dallas Mavericks.

The unprecedented situation led to more than 4,700 flights being cancelled on Friday night, which meant the Cavs had to stay in South Beach and fly back to Cleveland on game day.

"This is unprecedented. Back-to-back. Three-hour flight. It was already going to be a tough one," told Mitchell as per Chris Fedor.

Perkins had a straightforward response to these comments. He said: "Soft as h***!!! Doing all that crying… leave some room for the babies."

The Cavs ended up winning the game without Mitchell and James Harden, as Tyrese Proctor and Mario Hezonja combined to score 46 points, while Meleek Thomas and Thomas Bryant added 20 and 19 points, respectively, in a 127-117 win for the Cavaliers.

Donovan Mitchell hasn't responded to Perkins' comments yet. He will be hoping to return to the lineup for the next game against the Chicago Bulls on Monday and deliver a strong performance to prove himself to the fans.
