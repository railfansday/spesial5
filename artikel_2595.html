The crew at Xfinity Mobile Arena had changeovers — or, …

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The crew at Xfinity Mobile Arena had changeovers — or, on days like this, what they call a "doubleheader" — down to a science. In November alone, the arena was scheduled to host 21 events requiring 19 changeovers. So while the 76ers and Pacers battled it out on the court during the third quarter, a team led by senior vice president of operations Tim Allen huddled behind closed doors, poring over a detailed map of the arena upstairs. Every minute had to be accounted for: which entrances and exits carpenters would use to remove the basketball court, when ice technicians could access the rink for maintenance and how cleaning crews would clear thousands of pounds of trash before a new wave of fans poured through the doors. The moment the final buzzer sounded in the 76ers' 131-124 victory, the clock started ticking. It was 3:27 p.m.
