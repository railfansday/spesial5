Fantasy Basketball Waiver Wire Week 6: Charles Bassey, Julian Champagnie filling in for injured stars

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Injuries, injuries, and … more injuries. Key injuries have unfortunately been a main theme in the Association this season, and fantasy basketball managers have surely taken notice.

Dylan Harper, Norman Powell, RJ Barrett, Collin Murray-Boyles and Jaxson Hayes all suffered injuries last week, while Austin Reaves and Evan Mobley remained sidelined.

Fortunately, I've got 12 priority adds to help you navigate those injuries and find replacement options for your rosters.

Here are my top Waiver Wire adds for Week 6.

Others to consider: Ja'Kobe Walter (26%), Pelle Larsson (20%), Mikel Brown Jr. (12%), Isaac Okoro (10%), Svi Mykhailiuk (9%), Danny Wolf (9%), Yanic Konan Niederhauser (7%), Bennett Stirtz (5%), Jamison Battle (2%)

Harper is out 2-3 weeks with a hip strain, and Champagnie's role should grow with the second-year stud sidelined.

Champagnie has started each of the last two games without Harper, averaging 14.5 points, 3.5 rebounds, 2.0 assists and 3.5 triples across 27 minutes.

Keldon Johnson has fared well with Harper out, averaging 10/4.5/3.5 with a steal and a triple across 25 minutes. He's worth a look if you can't get Champagnie, but the latter is the preferred add.

An injury in the season opener and three early absences have left Johnson largely under-rostered, and he's still available in nearly 70% of Yahoo! leagues.

Over his last six games, Johnson has averaged 14.5 points, 3.2 rebounds, 3.0 assists, 1.3 steals and 1.8 triples across 29.7 minutes. He's locked into the starting five, and Johnson is worth a look.

Hayes injured his knee on Sunday and had an early exit against the Mavericks. Bamba stepped in and finished the game with six points, 10 boards, an assist and a block across 23 minutes.

With Jaren Jackson Jr. (knee), Jusuf Nurkic (foot) and Lauri Markkanen (hand) sidelined, Bamba could be inserted back into the starting five if Hayes is forced to miss time. In three starts this season, Bamba has averaged 15.7 points, 10.3 rebounds, 2.3 assists, 1.0 steals, 1.3 blocked shots and 2.3 triples across 30 minutes.

After 10 straight starts, Burries shifted back to the bench in Saturday's loss to Philly, but he still logged 26 minutes and delivered a career-best 26 points.

Over his last four, the rookie has averaged 18.3 points, 6.5 rebounds, 3.0 assists, 1.5 steals and 2.3 triples. He's a strong rebounder at the guard position, and he can rack up useful assists, steals and triples along the way.

Thybulle is the only Laker to start every game this season. In addition to his three-point shooting abilities, he's a menace on the defensive end.

Among players who have played at least 10 games, Thybulle is tied with Walker Kessler for third in "stocks" at 3.2. Thybulle is a Top-75 player on the season, yet he's available in nearly three fourths of Yahoo! leagues, making him a worthwhile add ahead of Week 6.

Over his last two games, Hunter has totaled 50 points, 12 rebounds, four assists, two blocks and seven triples across 78 minutes.

The score-first forward is a better play in points leagues due to his lack of consistent peripheral contributions, but he's playing monster minutes and doing enough in the box score to be viable in 12-team leagues.

Dejounte Murray is ready to leave the Big Easy, and the Pels' roster can't seem to stay healthy. Poole has seen increased run as of late, and he figures to be a significant rotation player for the foreseeable future.

Across his last four, Poole has averaged 16.0 points, 1.5 rebounds, 3.3 assists, 0.8 steals and 2.3 triples across 26.3 minutes. Like Hunter, Poole is a better add in points leagues, but he's still worth a look in category formats thanks to his increased role and ability to score, swipe and hit triples.

Over the last week, Shannon Jr. has averaged 17.8 points, 3.3 rebounds, 3.8 assists, 0.8 steals and 1.8 triples across 26.5 minutes over four games.

The third-year man is making the most of his opportunities. He started the season as a key rotation player, but his role and production have increased since Anthony Edwards was sidelined.

The sixth-year journeyman is enjoying the best season of his career, and he could be a permanent fixture in the starting five moving forward.

Across his last three games (all starts), Bassey has averaged 13 points, 10 rebounds, 1.3 assists, 1.7 steals and 1.0 blocked shots across 24.3 minutes.

Norman Powell is dealing with a right hamstring strain that will likely keep him sidelined for Monday's and Wednesday's games.

Hield drew his first start of the season in Sunday's high-scoring loss to the Nuggets, and he finished with a monster 33/7/6 line with a steal and seven triples. Hield was trending in the right direction prior to his elevated role, and Powell's injury opens the door for at least a couple more strong performances.

On Nov. 18, Mobley was given a 7-10 day re-evaluation window due to a left great toe strain, which means he will miss at least one more game this week and potentially more.

Mobley has missed five straight games, and Hezonja has picked up the slack. Over his last four outings, the veteran has averaged 14.3 points, 7.0 rebounds, 3.5 assists and 1.8 triples across 24.3 minutes. He should continue to see meaningful run until Mobley returns.

Graves has logged 30+ minutes in two straight games, logging extra playing time due to the absences of Kawhi Leonard (knee), Murray-Boyles (concussion) and Barrett (hamstring).

He drew his first start in Friday's win over Detroit, posting a 20/10 double-double with an assist, three steals, a block and a pair of triples. Toronto plays Monday, Friday and Sunday this week, making Graves a solid streamer to start the week and a potential weekend play.
