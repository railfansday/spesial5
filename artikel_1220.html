Napoli visit Man City without 11 players as Vergara faces lengthy layoff

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Napoli will travel to Manchester City without 11 players, including four centre-backs, while Antonio Vergara is expected to be sidelined for more than two months with a high-grade tear in his left thigh.

Napoli face several selection problems ahead of Tuesday’s Champions League away match against Manchester City.

The Partenopei, who sit eighth in Serie A, will travel to England without 11 first-team players.

Alisson Santos, Benoit Badiashile, Alessandro Buongiorno, captain Giovanni Di Lorenzo, Stanislav Lobotka, Alex Meret and David Neres are injured; Rafa Marin is suspended, while Luca Marianucci and Costantino Favasuli are not part of their UEFA squad.

Vergara was the latest player to suffer an injury, coming off in the first half of Saturday’s 1-0 win over Torino at the Stadio Maradona.

Napoli issued a statement on Monday after the Italy international underwent medical tests. The club said Vergara had suffered a high-grade tear of the right biceps femoris muscle in his left thigh. According to Sky Sport, the 23-year-old is expected to be out for more than two months.

Napoli’s injury crisis has hit hardest in attack and defence. In Manchester, coach Massimiliano Allegri will be without four of his six centre-backs, predictably prompting Mathias Olivera to join Amir Rrahmani and Sam Beukema in a three-man back line.

Allegri will hold a pre-match press conference in Manchester later today, alongside former Manchester City star Kevin De Bruyne.

Allegri has already suggested Napoli may not travel to Manchester with their highest motivation. “It’s a very important match and a great one to play in,” the coach said after Saturday’s win over Torino.

“We have to be realistic; our Champions League campaign will be decided against Club Brugge, Sabah and Viking. We had to beat Bodo, and we did. Tuesday will be very important and difficult, but we need to sort out the league, and the home match against Club Brugge will be crucial.”

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
