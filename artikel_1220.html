Wesley Fofana’s Chelsea nightmare continues as Liverpool win shoot-out

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Unfortunately for Chelsea, Wesley Fofana put his hand up twice. Firstly to concede the first-half penalty for handball that allowed Liverpool to equalise quickly after Chelsea themselves had scored from the spot.

And secondly to volunteer to take one of the penalties in the shoot-out. The defender had to score to keep Chelsea in it, but he woefully, nervously skied his effort high into a gleeful Kop and it was Liverpool who went through to the quarter-finals of the Carabao Cup.

LIVERPOOL ARE THROUGH TO THE QUARTER-FINALS OF THE CARABAO CUP! 🔴 Wesley Fofana blazes over to hand Liverpool their FIFTH consecutive victory ✅ https://t.co/M4n01R8DSk

There the reward for Andoni Iraola, the head coach, is a home tie against the club he left in the summer: Bournemouth. The draw has opened up for Liverpool, and they know it, with only Manchester City left of the other big guns. But despite again playing at Anfield, they would have preferred not to face such a dangerous and motivated opponent, who have just reeled off five wins and five clean sheets in a row under Iraola’s replacement, Marco Rose.

This was also a big win. A meaningful win. One that was celebrated as such with Dua Lipa’s One Kiss – an anthem on Jürgen Klopp’s run to Liverpool’s first Champions League final under him in 2018 – played over the loudspeakers at the end as the players joyfully milled around the pitch.

One kiss is all it takes 🎶 https://t.co/0jfujy4V94

“I am happy to feel it. We feel the support,” Iraola said when asked whether he sensed the momentum and togetherness building in his reshaped Liverpool side. It looks as though they are.

Fofana was Chelsea’s eighth taker, so they were running out of options, but it was a truly terrible effort. The 25-year-old turned and scratched his head – just as many Chelsea fans have done over his underwhelming, albeit injury-hit, contributions since his eyebrow-raising £75m move from Leicester City in 2022.

Both sides had the chance to win it earlier in the shoot-out. For Liverpool it was Kostas Tsimikas, who had taken the decisive kick to beat Chelsea in a shoot-out in the 2022 FA Cup final, but the Greek full-back also completely missed the target.

Tsimikas has the chance to put Liverpool through... And fires over the bar! 😱 https://t.co/eCKBT6njMB

Then Chelsea striker Emmanuel Emegha – on his debut – saw his kick saved, after an effort by Ryan Gravenberch had also been stopped. In the end, Liverpool’s stand-in captain Joe Gomez – still to score an actual goal – crashed in his kick before Fofana’s blunder. The 29-year-old Gomez finally “scored” and it was an assured strike.

This was the fifth penalty shoot-out between these two clubs, in all competitions, with Liverpool having now triumphed in all five. They won the toss for this one and elected to take the kicks in front of the Kop, which roared its satisfaction and did its best to unsettle the Chelsea takers.

Both stand-in goalkeepers – Giorgi Mamardashvili and Mike Penders – made one outstanding save each in the shoot-out. Chelsea substitute Valentín Barco was the other player to miss, hitting a post.

Chelsea blink first in the shootout! 🔵 Valentín Barco's penalty bounces off the post... https://t.co/rSXGfQctbI

The other efforts, in truth, were outstanding and assured with Liverpool, undoubtedly, benefitting from Iraola turning to his bench and bringing on Alexander Isak, Alexis Mac Allister, Florian Wirtz and Bradley Barcola. All scored their penalties.

Among Chelsea’s scorers was Jordan Henderson and the former Liverpool captain received a strange homecoming in his first appearance at Anfield for three years. He was booed, in the first half at least, and especially when he tucked the ball under his arm before Chelsea took their penalty – in normal time – which gave them the lead. Henderson turned to the stands, grinning.

In truth it was more pantomime than meaningful and Henderson, like another former, Champions League-winning Liverpool midfielder Xabi Alonso – now Chelsea’s head coach – did not celebrate as Morgan Rogers scored. The penalty came after a rash challenge by Gravenberch, who crashed into Moisés Caicedo as the Chelsea captain ran on to Malo Gusto’s shrewd reverse pass inside the area.

Afterwards, Alonso faced more questions about his decision not to use Estêvão Willian. Despite injury to João Pedro and Cole Palmer’s suspension, there was only a place on the Chelsea bench for the 19-year-old Brazilian. And he stayed there even though Alonso used all five substitutions. Estêvão has made just two starts under Alonso, both in this competition. “The season is long and there will be moments for everyone,” Alonso said.

It was hardly a ringing endorsement especially as Alonso brought on Emegha instead. The 23-year-old striker’s contributions amounted to having his shoot-out penalty saved and then bizarrely and violently charging at Virgil van Dijk – his fellow Dutchman, is there an issue there? – long after the ball had gone, pushing the Liverpool captain over.

Alonso said the exit was a “small set-back,” and maybe it was, even if he started with a stronger side than Iraola. But with Chelsea not in European competition and unlikely to win the Premier League, it means he is already down to the FA Cup should silverware be won this season.

There is no VAR at this stage of the competition but referee Darren England appeared to get all the big calls right. Rogers claimed a penalty before Chelsea’s one was awarded, but Ronald Araújo was probably just too strong.

It is an all-Premier League tie at Anfield as Liverpool host Chelsea in the last 16 of the League Cup, with a place in the quarter-finals at stake. This will be the first managerial meeting between childhood friends Andoni Iraola and Xabi Alonso.

Liverpool beat Tottenham 3-1 at Anfield in the last round of this competition, which included a stunning goal from Dominik Szoboszlai to seal progression to the last 16. They earned their first home Premier League win of the season on the weekend as they beat Brighton 2-0. Iraola’s men come into this game off the back of four straight wins in all competitions.

“There will definitely be changes, but we want to win the game and we want to be competitive,” Iraola said ahead of this tie. “We have to see how the legs; we are not just in the Carabao Cup, but also in the Champions League and we have been trying to make changes from game to game. We want to be in the next round. We have been unlucky in the draw, playing Chelsea, and they have been unlucky also. But we want to be in the draw again and that only happens if you win. We need to rotate, all the top teams need to rotate when you are playing every three days. Sometimes you are forced to rotate due to injury or sometimes different demands in different games tactically. I am happy that everyone is involved. When we have had to use someone who has not played a lot of games, they have helped us. The more players we have in a good place, the easier my selection will be, even if it is hard to leave players out.”

Giovanni Leoni, Conor Bradley and Hugo Ekitike remain long-term absentees for the hosts with Jeremie Frimpong also missing. Florian Wirtz could feature despite going off with an ankle knock during the win over Brighton.
