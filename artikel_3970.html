It's official: Spider-Man: Brand New Day is getting a re-release, complete with new scenes.

Brand New Day – as the initial rumors suggested earlier in November – will return to cinemas on December 4, just two weeks before Avengers: Doomsday.

"Welcome to a brand new Brand New Day," Sony Pictures announced on Twitter. "The record-breaking movie is coming back to the big screen on December 4 with an extended cut featuring new scenes and a couple more surprises."

Those new scenes and surprises are under wraps for the time being, but we would be shocked if Rosario Dawson's Claire Temple wasn't part of the extended cut – especially given how transparent the Ahsoka actor was about being left out of the movie.

The surprises, too, could be Avengers: Doomsday-shaped. The Brand New Day post-credits scene suggests Doom is hunting Spidey, while we already know Hulk was on the supervillain's radar courtesy of Avengers: Endgame Encore's bonus footage.

The re-release is also a chance for Brand New Day to cement its standing as the third highest-grossing movie of all time with $2.5 billion dollars (for a few weeks, at least).

Starring Tom Holland, Zendaya, Jacob Batalon, Sadie Sink, and Jon Bernthal, Spider-Man: Brand New Day sees Peter Parker coming to terms with a world that no longer knows he is Spider-Man – all while battling a terrifying threat he can't see. That, of course, would be revealed as Jean Grey, with Sadie Sink later officially named as part of 2028's X-Men movie.
