Black Friday Amazon device deals: Save up to 58% on Echo devices and get $40 free credit

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Well, it's finally here: Black Friday has begun. And no one does Black Friday harder than Amazon, which kicked off its savings a full week early. Now that the big day has arrived, you can save 50% and up on Amazon Echo devices. On top of that, Prime members who buy select Echo devices will also get a $40 free credit to spend on additional Amazon smart home products. And on top of that, we found tons more deals on Kindle e-readers, Blink cameras, and the brand-new Alexa tablets.

To claim the $40 credit on Echo purchases, make sure you're signed into your Amazon Prime account. Then, look for an on-page coupon on Echo product pages. If you're not a Prime member, be sure to sign up for a Prime 30-Day Free Trial.

Sure, Black Friday technically falls on Nov. 27, but Amazon's official Black Friday Week started with a bang and is staying strong into Black Friday proper.

If there's an Amazon device on your list, there are some massive discounts live on Fire TV Sticks, Kindles, Echo devices, and Alexa tablets. Since many of these devices saw price increases in 2026 (thanks, RAMageddon), this is your chance to cancel them out. But we're pleased to say that so far, many of these deals are surpassing expectations — particularly on the brand-new Echo devices.

We'll be tracking all of the best Amazon device deals throughout Black Friday and Cyber Monday. For deals beyond Amazon devices, be sure to follow along with all of Mashable's coverage in our Black Friday hub.

Amazon's Black Friday sale is a 12-day event, lasting from Nov. 19 to Nov. 30. Cyber Monday falls on Nov. 30, so the end of Amazon's sale marks the end of the Black Friday/Cyber Monday programming from the mega-retailer. That said, historically we've seen some deals last into the days following, and even return ahead of Christmas in rarer cases.

Most items from Amazon from Nov. 1 to Dec. 31 can be returned until Jan. 31, 2027, with some exceptions. Apple products, for instance, must be returned by Jan. 15, 2027.
