How to Make the Ink Used to Write Hamlet, the Declaration of Independence, and…

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Simon Garfield traces the history of Henry Stephens’ indelible writing fluid and gives a historical recipe for iron gall ink, used in documents and works ranging from Magna Carta to the Declaration of Independence. He also explores how Stephens built a successful ink business and why its blue-black formula became the standard for official writing. [lithub.com]
