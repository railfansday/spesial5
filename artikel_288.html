Lyft's new subscription bundles in DoorDash's DashPass for $10 per month

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

DashPass members can get Lyft Go at no extra cost.

Lyft is replacing its Lyft Pink subscription with a new offering that bundles in DoorDash's DashPass. As with the previous offering, Lyft Go costs $10 per month, but the annual plan is $3 cheaper than Lyft Pink's at $96. If you're already a DashPass member, you can link your DoorDash account and get Lyft Go at no extra cost.

Along with DashPass (which includes discounts and lower fees for DoorDash orders), Lyft Go members get eight percent from every ride back as credits, up to $40 per month. Credits can be put toward future rides or DoorDash orders.

Lyft and DoorDash (which is set to buy Instacart for $17 billion) are expanding their partnership in other ways. On certain rides, you'll be able to browse DoorDash restaurants in the Lyft app and place an order that'll start making its way to your destination before you get there.
