Mauricio Pochettino's first Man United training session is LEAKED ahead of Liverpool clash: Drone over Carrington captures 12 MINUTES of footage in major security breach

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Published: 14:07 EST, 19 November 2026 | Updated: 14:15 EST, 20 November 2026

Manchester United have fallen victim to a security breach after footage of Mauricio Pochettino’s first training session as head coach was leaked online.

The 12-minute video taken by a drone hovering over the club’s Carrington training complex on Thursday clearly shows Pochettino and his coaching staff working with the players.

The incident is a major embarrassment for United on Pochettino’s first full day in charge after the club paid £10million to buy the Argentine and his staff out of their contracts with the US Soccer Federation.

His first game is against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday, although it’s unclear how much United’s bitter rivals would be able to benefit from the video.

United regularly use their own drones to film training sessions, but put up signs six years ago banning the use of them near the training ground in case they could reveal top-secret information to their opponents.

Mauricio Pochettino's first Man United training session has been leaked on YouTube

Footage of the session was captured from a drone close to Carrington and uploaded online

Man United players were captured during a drill under the gaze of Pochettino's coaching staff

The footage was posted on YouTube by an account called CC Overview which insists that the airspace over Carrington is unrestricted and the drone was operating legally within Civil Aviation Authority rules.

It said that the video was taken half an hour into Pochettino’s training session and clearly shows the 54-year-old instructing the United squad along with his coaching team of No.2 Jesus Perez, first-team coach Miguel D’Agostino, goalkeeper coach Toni Jimenez, his son and fitness coach Sebastiano Pochettino and conditioning coach Silvia Tuya.

Pochettino only flew into Manchester on Wednesday with D’Agostino, Tuya and analyst Alec Scott after taking charge of his last game as US coach against Haiti in Cincinnati.

They caught a private jet provided by United after the game and he went straight to Carrington after landing in the UK shortly before noon.

Perez, Jimenez and Sebastiano Pochettino have been at the training ground since the weekend after being given permission by the US Soccer Federation to leave after the first-leg against Haiti in Miami.

Pochettino addressed the Man United's internal media team for the first time on Thursday, outlining how he plans to drag this underperforming squad up the table.

'The culture is the most important,' Pochettino explained.

'Create the habits, the principles, and I think that is what we want to create.

'We need to [be] looking forward and to find a way to create the best habits and the culture that makes us to be close to winning every single game.'

'The players need to express their talent. We need to create the environment and the platform for the players to perform. That is the most important thing and that is our priority now – it's to work with them and try to take the pressure.'

Pochettino has stressed he is focused on the potential of his squad, rather than their present position in the Premier League.

'It's a mistake to see the [current] situation. We need to see the potential of this club,' a defiant Pochettino added.

'Of course, the circumstances are the circumstances, and we need to be responsible for the circumstances. But at the same time, we need to understand that it's a game and, to connect with the game, we need to feel free.

'The players need to express their talent. We need to create the environment and the platform for the players to perform. That is the most important thing and that is our priority now – it's to work with them and try to take the pressure [off].

'Pressure needs to bring energy, responsibility needs to bring energy. Because if not, it's too heavy to put all that pressure and responsibility on your back.

'When you connect with the game, you connect through your talent and when you feel freedom, when you feel happy, when you want to play for your team-mates, when you want to play for your family, when you want to play for your club, your fans.

'That is what we need to create all together and I think we have an amazing staff - we were talking a lot today. Then [we need to] settle all the principles and the ideas and to create our own philosophy and start from zero.

'I think with the power of this club and the fans supporting us, I think we will get in the place that we deserve to be.'
