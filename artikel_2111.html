Chris Sutton delivers brutal ‘all-inclusive holiday’ jibe at new Celtic star

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Chris Sutton has jumped to the defence of Jordan Lotomba after the Celtic full-back endured a difficult first start against Besiktas.

Lotomba was handed his opportunity by Martin O’Neill in the 3-2 Europa League victory at Celtic Park, having struggled for game time since arriving late in the summer transfer window.

The defender was given a demanding assignment up against Leandro Trossard and found himself tested throughout the evening.

But former Celtic striker Sutton believes it would be wrong to single out Lotomba for criticism given the circumstances surrounding his arrival at the club. Writing in his Daily Record column, Sutton said: “I actually had some sympathy for the full-back.
