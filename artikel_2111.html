‘Needs a big result’: West Brom vs Bristol City predictions

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

West Brom are unbeaten in their last eight Championship games as they eye promotion to the Premier League. WBA are in the play-offs after 17 fixtures along with Wolves, Birmingham City, Southampton, QPR and Swansea City.

The Baggies won 3-1 away at Bolton Wanderers on Wednesday night. Aune Heggebo bagged a brace for James Morrison‘s side in the North West, whilst Felix Horn Myhre also managed to find the net.

Bristol City find themselves positioned down in 16th position in the table after an inconsistent campaign to date. They lured head coach Michael Skubala away from Lincoln City over the summer.

The Robins drew 1-1 against Wrexham at Ashton Gate last time out on Tuesday evening. Cameron Pring scored their goal in the second-half as they rescued a point.

“West Brom have made The Hawthorns a fortress this season and are very solid on their own patch. WBA have lost only once there in this campaign.

“The Baggies are on a roll and will be confident of getting another three points on the board this weekend. They haven’t been beaten since 10th October against Birmingham City and will be in good spirits.

“As for Bristol City, pressure continues to grow on their head coach Michael Skubala. It has been a tough spell for him at Ashton Gate so far and he needs a big result on Saturday or the heat will be further intensified on his position.

“The Robins have won only one in their last nine matches and are struggling for points. For that reason, I think they will slump to another disappointing loss in the Midlands.”

“West Brom’s impressive season just keeps going, with the Wednesday night win over Bolton Wanderers making it eight league games without defeat. James Morrison will have been pleased to see Aune Heggebo back on the scoresheet with a brace, and Felix Horn Myhre now has four goals in his last seven games as well.

“That was away from home as well. Now, WBA return to The Hawthorns, where they have lost just once and conceded only seven times in eight games this season.

“Bristol City aren’t in a great place down in 16th and with questions being asked of manager Michael Skubala, but when they have played better this campaign, it has been on the road. The Robins have only lost twice away from home, so it isn’t out of the question that these two play out a score draw here.

“However, on overall form, the strength of the teams and cohesion of the squads, you have to think the Baggies win here. I’ll go for a home victory this weekend.”
