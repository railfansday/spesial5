7 Best Faux Garlands for Long-Lasting Holiday Cheer (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

With Halloween in the rearview, it’s time to take down the cobwebs and inflatable skulls and go full throttle into the holiday season. While seasonal garlands are fittingly festive, the natural editions tend to make a mess. For anyone who’d rather not be tasked with vacuuming the dead pine needles that seem to accumulate by the minute, faux garlands are a worthy solution. They offer the look (and often the feel) of the real thing without the sticky sap or endless cleanup.

Fake garlands have come a long way since their humble, low-quality beginnings. Now made with premium artificial foliage and durable wire frames, some of our favorites are nearly indistinguishable from their natural counterparts. Better yet, you can use them year after year to deck the halls (and mantels, bannisters, front porches… you get it), sometimes both indoors and out. No need to strip a tree of its limbs; simply light a pine-scented candle and let everyone think you did.

There’s not much more you could want in a garland than what this single, super-realistic-looking strand can accomplish. Afloral's bestselling Norfolk pine garland is eight feet long and more voluminous than many faux alternatives. Its branches and needles are intentionally spaced irregularly to give it a natural appearance, and the wired base makes it easy to shape, drape, and arrange just about anywhere. Wind it around a staircase or use it to dress up your front door; the possibilities are pretty much endless.

For those looking for a pre-arranged garland, we recommend Crate & Barrel’s Kew Garden garland by florist Abigail Ahern. A curated selection of faux evergreen fronds, berries, and twigs combines with velvet ribbons for an English-cottage-inspired centerpiece. The decoration work has been done for you; all you need to do now is hang it.

Simple but effective, the Fraser Fir is the kind of garland that practically begs to be draped over your mantel. Constructed with Balsam Hill’s proprietary True Needle foliage, the Fraser’s natural texture and full volume (read: no bare spots) make it look so convincingly real that your friends will wonder how you keep your home so tidy all season long. It’s available in multiple lengths (including the 10-foot strand for $159), with the option to add LED lights if you're after a little extra twinkle.

If traditional greenery isn't quite your thing, consider this garnet-hued strand as a fun alternative. Made of clusters of velvety, flocked bobbles, it adds rich color and plenty of texture to your seasonal setup. Weave it through a wreath or let it take center stage on its own. It's a little unexpected, which is sometimes exactly what a room needs.

If your priority in a faux garland is a natural-looking arrangement, consider this six-foot garland from McGee & Co., which has all the makings of a woodland find. Natural-looking pinecones and delicate, handcrafted white berries dot its lush, layered swaths of greenery, giving it plenty of intrigue without rendering it overly adorned. It's a lovely option for anyone who prefers their holiday decorations a little more rustic than glitzy.

This faux garland adds bright red berries and natural-looking pinecones to its lifelike pine greenery for an overall look that feels like it was gathered on a winter walk. At six feet long, it’s quite versatile, able to dress up a mantel or wind around a staircase. A plaid ribbon makes a nice finishing touch, if you're so inclined.

Who says Christmas decorations have to be red and green? This playful ball garland from CB2 takes a more eye-catching approach, combining warm metallic tones in a mix of sizes for plenty of visual interest. It's an apt design choice if you’re planning to go full tinsel mode this season, or simply looking to add a little shine to an otherwise understated setup.
