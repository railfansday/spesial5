Canton’s CC rises 4% as SEC staff view it as ‘digital commodity’

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

SEC staff’s no-action letter covers CC earned by running infrastructure and applications, but is not a binding ruling on its legal status.

Canton Network’s native cryptocurrency rose 4% on Wednesday after the US Securities and Exchange Commission’s (SEC) Division of Corporation Finance issued a no-action letter stating it views the token as a “digital commodity.”

Canton Coin (CC) traded at $0.098 at 10:27 am UTC, but was still down roughly 4.2% over the past week, according to CoinMarketCap.

In its Tuesday letter, SEC staff accepted the explanation that CC’s value comes from how the network operates and demand for the token, rather than a central team’s efforts to generate profits for holders.

The division also said it would not recommend enforcement action over CC’s issuance without securities registration, provided the tokens are created under the conditions described by Canton.

In Friday’s letter to the SEC, Canton developer Digital Asset said network participants earn CC by operating infrastructure and applications, with the protocol determining how many tokens they can mint based on their activity.

The SEC issued guidance in March distinguishing digital commodities, such as Bitcoin and Ether, from securities. It said these tokens are not themselves securities, although the way they are offered and sold can still be subject to securities laws.

The SEC’s response reflects its staff’s position and is not a binding ruling on CC’s legal status.

SEC staff previously issued token-related no-action letters to crypto projects, including DoubleZero (2Z) in September 2025 and Solana-native decentralized physical infrastructure network (DePIN) project Fuse in November 2025.
