GTA 6 Ultimate Edition DLC Won’t Be Permanent Or Immediately Accessible

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Despite all the controversy surrounding its expensive DLC edition and discless release, Grand Theft Auto 6 has been absolutely smashing just about every review record out there, with countless new gameplay details and footage going live now that its staggered regional release has started. While most of the responses have been glowing so far, a few details still haven't gone over well with fans.

Although the high price didn't deter many players, at least if its pre-release sales figures are anything to go by, early player reports have been noting that several of the in-game content offered by GTA 6's deluxe edition aren't as permanent as they first sounded.

On top of the $100 price tag, early sources like Pirat_Nation have claimed that much of the content included in GTA 6's deluxe edition won't be available from the start. Requiring you to progress past certain points in the story to access them, certain mechanics like limited vehicles, stores, and customization options will take a bit of effort to unlock.

While progression locking the DLC already has some fans disgruntled, the part that has fans most frustrated is that several inclusions in the DLC will supposedly only be available for certain chapters, like Jason's Dinka Enduro Motorcycle, Low Riding Pickup, and Crest Kayak that can only be used in the very first chapter of the game before seemingly losing access to them again.

While these changes likely won't detract much from Grand Theft Auto 6's glowing review scores, another concern fans have been raising comes from the GTA+ subscription service benefits that New Zealand players have been sharing online, which seemingly grant other in-game rewards and exclusive content in exchange for a paid monthly fee.

While the content offered by GTA+ isn't exactly game-changing and mostly offers bonuses like passive discounts or sale increases, it does also provide a free racecar and garage for players to use, offering a significant advantage to start off with compared to players who simply purchase the base game, or even its deluxe edition.
