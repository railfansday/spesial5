Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Colombian oncology costs weighed on profits, but cash flow surged.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 18, 2026 at 4:08PM EST

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Auna (AUNA -12.37%) reported top-line growth driven by higher surgical volumes and oncology sessions across its regional platform, though consolidated profitability was impacted by extraordinary costs in Colombia and Peru. Management reported that the company terminated an unprofitable oncology risk-sharing contract in Colombia that represented 4% of segment revenue after treatment costs significantly exceeded assumptions. In Mexico, the Monterrey healthcare network achieved its first quarter of Adjusted EBITDA growth in 2026, offsetting regulatory changes in VAT deductibility that affected the insurance sector. The company reported substantial year-to-date free cash flow growth of 95%, which supported a reduction in the net leverage ratio to 3.5x. Management reaffirmed its full-year revenue guidance of 10% to 14% growth while revising Adjusted EBITDA expectations to remain broadly stable or slightly down for the year.
