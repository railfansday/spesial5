Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Production climbs as the company nears positive free cash flow in 2027.

Granite Ridge Resources, Inc. (GRNT +13.28%) reported a sequential increase in production and earnings, noting an inflection point as the company transitions toward generating positive free cash flow in 2027. Management indicated that 2026 represents the final year of investing ahead of cash flow, with future growth supported by a higher oil weighting and the expiration of lower-priced hedges. The company is expanding its operator partnership model through a new agreement in the Midland Basin and initiated a capital return program that includes both a recurring dividend and a share repurchase authorization. Management reported that the traditional non-operated business continues to generate steady cash flow across six basins, while the partnership platform compounds inventory and growth.
