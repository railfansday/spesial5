GrowGeneration (GRWG) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Store cuts and tariff refunds drove a profit swing as margins climbed sharply.

GrowGeneration Corp. (GRWG -3.82%) reported a transition from a retail-centric hydroponic provider to a national commercial cultivation supply and proprietary brand platform. Management reduced its retail footprint to 19 stores during the third quarter and expects to operate 14 locations by the end of 2026, down from 65 locations in 2022. The company achieved its highest quarterly net income since 2021, supported by $2.6 million in tariff refunds and increased sales of internal brands. While total revenue declined slightly compared to the prior year, the company reported positive adjusted EBITDA for the second consecutive quarter and maintained a debt-free balance sheet with $41.8 million in liquidity.
