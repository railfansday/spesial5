Star Bulk Carriers (SBLK -3.56%) reported a significant increase in profitability for the third quarter of 2026, driven by a 48% year-over-year rise in its time charter equivalent rate. Management reported the completion of the company's eight-vessel Kamsarmax newbuilding program and the successful parallel listing on Euronext Athens, which raised $124.4 million in gross proceeds. The company stated that its balance sheet strength, characterized by a 74% reduction in net debt since 2021, supported a $1 per share dividend. Management indicated that its cost structure remains an industry advantage, with daily vessel operating expenses significantly below the peer average while maintaining a fleet of 138 vessels.
