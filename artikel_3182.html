KT Corp (KT) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Management outlines a core business rebound and bold AI infrastructure plans.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

KT Corporation (KT -2.03%) reported third quarter results characterized by a recovery in core telecommunications and a significant expansion in the business-to-business order pipeline. Management reported that wireless revenue is recovering following the conclusion of a customer promotion program, while 5G penetration reached 83.5%. The company is executing an AI transformation strategy, targeting a twofold growth in related revenue by 2028 compared to a 2025 baseline. Strategic focus remains on infrastructure, with a long-term goal to reach 1 gigawatt of AI data center capacity by 2031. Shareholder returns continue with a minimum annual dividend target and the completion of a substantial share buyback program during the quarter.

KT based on trust, efficiency and execution will be at the forefront of making Korea a nation that best utilizes AI. In 2027, we will enhance customer value translated into growth and earnings and make the leap together with customers.

Hye-Byung Min: Let me now move on to the financial results for the third quarter of 2026. Similar to last year, the outcome of the collective bargaining is also reflected in the third quarter figures. Net income declined 17.5% year-on-year to KRW 367.3 billion and EBITDA came down 5% year-on-year, reporting KRW 1.428,3 trillion.

Underpinned by dedicated line services, enterprise internet and data revenue expanded, but due to the end of the system infrastructure project last year, B2B service revenue dipped marginally by 0.7% year-over-year. AX revenue expanded 9.8% year-on-year due to continuation of steep up trends in data center business and rise in large-scale AI and cloud projects for the financial sector. Next is on the performances of our major subsidiaries. KT Estate reported 39.1% year-over-year growth, driven by the growth of hotel business and expanded revenue from development projects in Dunsan, Daejeon.

Content business reported 12.1% year-over-year growth, powered by growth from KT Studio Genie and KT Millie's Library.
