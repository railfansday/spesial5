Investors who miss out on Anthropic’s IPO, set for Thursday, may still end up owning a small piece of the AI company through funds they already hold. Many actively managed mutual funds and ETFs bought Anthropic shares before the listing, so investors in those funds will gain exposure automatically once trading begins. Index funds will add the stock over the following weeks, though timing varies. Inclusion in the Nasdaq-100 and Russell 3000 is expected to take longer than it did for SpaceX. In most funds, Anthropic will make up well under 1% of holdings, which limits the impact of any sharp price swings after the debut. In an interview with CNBC, Jay Ritter, director of the IPO Initiative at the University of Florida, cautioned against putting too much money into any single stock. Anthropic could raise up to about $90 billion including its private placement, which would top SpaceX’s $86 billion record from June. The listing comes amid wider doubts about the AI trade after Jensen Huang’s exit from Nvidia.
