Anthropic shares open at $111.50, 34.3% above $83 IPO price

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Claude chatbot maker began trading under the ticker ANT, with the company’s valuation at around $2 trillion at the opening price.

Anthropic shares opened trading under the ticker ANT on the Nasdaq at $111.50 Thursday, about 34.3% above their initial public offering (IPO) price.

Anthropic shares opened trading under the ticker ANT on the Nasdaq at $111.50 Thursday, about 34.3% above their initial public offering (IPO) price.

The Wall Street Journal had reported an expected opening of $110 minutes before trading began as fresh headlines poured in by the minute with ever-rising opening price predictions. The opening price values Anthropic at around $2 trillion on a fully diluted basis.

Anthropic had priced 980 million shares at $83 on Wednesday, above its initial $70-$78 range. The $81.3 billion IPO includes stock from existing shareholders. A separate $26 billion private placement brings the combined deal to $107.3 billion.

Anthropic’s IPO has blasted past SpaceX’s record $75 billion IPO in June, showing that the scale of investor demand for AI companies appears undeterred. SpaceX shares opened at $150, above their $135 offer price.

Crypto exchange OKX said in a note shared with Cointelegraph that its Anthropic pre-IPO perpetual contract traded at about 103 USDC (USDC) earlier Thursday, roughly 24% above the offer price. The contract provides derivative exposure without ownership of Anthropic shares. Binance Wallet and Kraken also offered eligible users tokenized IPO exposure through xStocks. However, both warned that actual allocations could be partial or even zero, with unused funds returned.

Crypto platforms also offered exposure ahead of SpaceX’s listing, although Binance, Bybit, Bitget Wallet and MEXC later canceled their tokenized IPO campaigns after failing to secure share allocations.

Anthropic faces stiff competition from OpenAI, DeepSeek and Google while committing billions to computing infrastructure, including a $28 billion, 15-year data center lease with Bitcoin miner Riot Platforms. The company has committed around $901 billion in total to purchases and leases, mostly for computing power and infrastructure, according to its IPO filing, with spending stretching out as far as 2045.
