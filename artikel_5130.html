As Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (NASDAQ: SPCX) celebrated its first operational deployment of Starlink V3 satellites aboard Starship Flight 15, Wall Street analysts expect SpaceX stock to rally to a new all-time high (ATH) over the next 12 months.

On Wednesday, November 18, SpaceX stock had received an average 12-month price forecast of $267.76 from 34 Wall Street analysts sampled by TipRanks over the last 3 months.

With SPCX trading at $144.40 at the time of writing, these analysts are predicting a potential 85.43% upside move by November 18, 2027.

On Tuesday, Colin Canfield, an expert at Cantor Fitzgerald, reiterated an ‘Overweight’ rating for SpaceX stock. Canfield set the firm’s 12-month price target at $345 driven by the company’s successful execution of Flight 15 on November 13, 2026.

Most importantly, the firm believes that markets severely underappreciate the company’s potential in the Artificial Intelligence (AI) space.

As a result, SpaceX stock has received an average rating of ‘Strong Buy’ bolstered by rising capital expenditure on AI buildout. Furthermore, SpaceX is currently likely to merge with Tesla Inc. (NASDAQ: TSLA) to strengthen its AI capacity.

At press time, the company had a market capitalization of roughly $1.96 trillion, which has been criticized as high with regard to its projected 2026 revenue of $51.6 billion.

From a technical analysis standpoint, SPCX’s price must consistently close above a major resistance level around $172.06 to confirm a rally towards its ATH. After hitting an all-time low of about $105.17, Wall Street analysts are confident SpaceX stock could continue with a bullish outlook in 2027.
