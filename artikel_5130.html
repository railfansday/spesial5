BlackRock’s SBI Deal Opens XRP Tokenization Question

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Home » Trendsetter News » BlackRock’s SBI Deal Opens XRP Tokenization Question

BlackRock’s reported collaboration with SBI Holdings and Startale Group explores linking tokenized funds with a yen stablecoin.

BlackRock’s reported agreement with SBI Holdings and Startale Group concerns tokenized funds and a yen stablecoin—not a confirmed role for XRP.

In her video, wealth commentator Kamilah Stevenson examines how the proposed collaboration could connect Japanese savings with on-chain investments, while acknowledging that the announcement names neither XRP nor a blockchain.

That distinction matters. SBI’s close relationship with Ripple gives XRP investors a reason to follow the project, but it does not establish that BlackRock intends to use the token.

According to Stevenson, the companies signed a memorandum of understanding to explore connecting tokenized money market funds with JPYSC, a yen stablecoin. No investment amount was announced, and the memorandum is exploratory rather than a final commercial contract.

The proposed work covers technology, distribution and regulatory requirements. Stevenson says other financial institutions could also participate.

She identifies BlackRock’s BUIDL fund as an existing example of the model. Launched in 2024, it held roughly $2.17 billion in Treasuries, repurchase agreements and cash at the time cited in the video; that figure should not be read as a current valuation or a commitment to this project.

The commercial attraction is Japan’s large pool of household savings, much of it held in cash and deposits. Stevenson describes JPYSC as issued by SBI Shinsei Trust Bank and distributed through SBI VC Trade, potentially giving the partnership an established domestic distribution route.

Stevenson points to SBI Remit’s use of XRP for remittances and SBI’s broader XRP-related activities as reasons to watch the partnership. Her central caveat is explicit: “Nobody can tell you that this deal uses XRP.”

A connection between yen-denominated money and dollar-denominated fund assets raises foreign-exchange and settlement questions. XRP could theoretically serve as a bridge, but there’s no evidence that the partners have selected it.

In a nutshell, the meaningful milestones are a named blockchain, disclosed settlement arrangements and an actual product launch.

Tokenized fund distribution could expand without generating demand for XRP; SBI’s involvement alone is not evidence of token adoption.
