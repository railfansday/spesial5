BTC briefly slipped below $60,730 in late June and early July before recovering sharply. That rebound carried prices toward $86,850 by September, but the move failed to change the bigger picture.

CryptoQuant’s analysis of Binance inflows by spent UTXO size suggests selling pressure isn’t limited to large holders.

The data also indicates Binance inflows haven’t declined significantly, while spot demand remains too weak to absorb incoming supply. That’s an uncomfortable combination for buyers.
