The first season of Netflix’s 3 Body Problem series ended with the remaining members of the Oxford Five disillusioned at the idea of humanity being utterly unprepared to deal with the San-Ti’s arrival on Earth. But in the first trailer for the show’s upcoming second season, it looks like the world’s greatest minds have been dreaming up inventive ways to fight back against the oncoming alien threat.

Though Sophon (Sea Shimooka) insists that the San-Ti wish humans no ill will and only want to speak in the new trailer, MI6 chief Thomas Wade (Liam Cunningham) is certain that the aliens’ claims of sending an “emissary” is a ruse. Wade remembers vividly how the San-Ti likened humanity to bugs, and he knows that extermination is central to their plan to take the planet for themselves.

With the Staircase Project having been an abject failure and the San-Ti beaming ominous messages into the sky, people have reason to believe that the aliens might really be able to conquer the world when they arrive from another galaxy in a few hundred years. But for all the doom and gloom, Dr. Saul Durand (Jovan Adepo) can’t shake the feeling that the San-Ti’s attempt at assassinating him could mean that he and the other Wallfacers might actually have a chance at turning the tides.

In addition to some new glimpses of the San-Ti’s virtual reality (and that nifty headset), the season 2 trailer features a handful of what appears to be people working on a weapons system based in space. The shots make it look like 3 Body Problem’s next chapter will be bigger and more epic as its heroes get even more serious about coming up with novel ways to handle their invaders. The trailer doesn’t really make clear how much of Cixin Liu’s novel this new season will tackle in its six episodes. But when the show returns on January 14th, it’s probably going to be another wild ride.
