Google AI Ultra upgrade adds YouTube Premium Family

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

This perk is available for both the Google AI Ultra 5x plan at $99.99/month and AI Ultra 20x at $199.99/month.

YouTube Premium Family costs $26.99⁠/month and lets you add up to five family members in your household. It offers ad-free YouTube and YouTube Music with background playback and offline downloads, as well as other benefits like Ask Music.

AI Pro at $19.99/month only offers Premium Lite, which excludes YouTube Music benefits.

Google says this has been a highly requested feature. It makes the upgrade from Pro to Ultra a more compelling offering if you need the storage or are a big Gemini user with several family members. AI Pro with 10 TB is $49.99/month, with AI Ultra 5x offering 20 TB and AI Ultra 20x boosting that to 30 TB.
