Dad Kills Wife, 3 Kids as Young Child Begs for Their Life in Murder-Suicide

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Authorities in Florida said a father fatally shot his wife and their three young children before taking his own life in a horrific murder-suicide at their home.

Hillsborough County Sheriff Chad Chronister said during a news conference on Thursday, November 19, that authorities learned about the tragedy earlier that day around 3:30 p.m. when Hillsborough County dispatchers received a 911 call with no one responding on the line.

Dispatchers said they heard a man say, “Sorry, buddy,” before a gunshot was heard. A small child was also heard begging for their life in the background of the call.

Deputies arrived at the home in Plant City, Florida, 10 minutes later and spotted a lifeless child on the floor inside, so they kicked down the door to enter the home. The officers then discovered the man had “executed” his wife and their three children before dying by suicide.

Investigators have not shared who made the initial 911 call from inside the home, and deputies are still working to determine what events led up to the killings. Chronister added that there is no clear motive as of time of publication.

While the names of the deceased have not yet been publicly revealed, Chronister said that the children were 1, 3 and 5 years old.

Chronister said that the family had moved into the home about one year before the murder-sucide. The family had no history of reported domestic disputes, and deputies had never responded to any calls for service at the home.

The sheriff also took a moment to urge anyone struggling with their mental health to get help, and he encouraged them to visit the Crisis Center of Tampa Bay.

“As we approach the holidays, we know that some of this community will struggle, will be overwhelmed with emotions. I’m making the plea to all of you: If you need help, simply have the courage and strength to ask for it,” Chronister said.

The Hillsborough County Sheriff’s Office maintained a heavy presence at the home as they processed the scene late into Thursday night.

Chronister added that the responding deputies and the dispatcher who heard the 911 call were able to take off, and they were provided with peer support and resources to cope with emotional trauma of covering the case.
