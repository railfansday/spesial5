10 Legendary Weapons You Should Have in Borderlands 4

Borderlands 4 has a lot of legendary weapons. With the first entire year of content for the game being done, there are now over 160 different legendary weapons that you can choose from.

This only covers the actual weapons, like legendary assault rifles and shotguns, and not the grenades, shields, and class mods you can get. When choosing a weapon, there is a lot to consider.

At this point, there are several weapons that you should have in your bank or on your character that have stayed consistently strong. If you don’t have these weapons, you should probably start farming for them in case there are future DLC plans.

Crow-Sourced is not a legendary weapon, but I am counting it anyone because it is a Pearlescent and they are more rare than legendary items in Borderlands 4. This weapon is one of the Pearlescent weapons added with the first DLC titled Mad Ellie and the Vault of the Damned.

To obtain this assault rifle, you need to make your way over to the Captain Willis boss fight in The Maelstrom. In other words, you won’t get it until the end of the DLC, but it is well worth the wait.

This weapon has the Left of the Murder ability, which makes it fire out random projectiles as you shoot it. Charging the weapon increases the number of projectiles, making it more lethal the more it is charged.

Captain Willis will give you a run for your money in the Mad Ellie and the Vault of the Damned DLC for Borderlands 4.

The Swan Song is one of those weapons that you can use to clear a room very quickly, which means it is a guarantee that you should have it on your characters. This weapon fires fast, hits hard, and even shoots cannonballs.

That’s right, you read that correctly, it first cannonballs. Thanks to its Cannonball ability, you can shoot out cannonballs from the underbarrel of this weapon. Each kill with this weapon grants additional cannonballs, with four being the maximum.

As a weapon, the Swan Song is very powerful, but the underbarrel packs a massive punch, so it is important to continuously use it as you take down enemies. Speaking of takedowns, you get this weapon from the Takedown at Hadron Abyss.

The Darkbeast is one of my go-to weapons when I create a new character. I have a level 30 version of this weapon for new characters, and I always make sure to get one at max level as well for each character.

This SMG packs a serious punch, with it often outperforming other weapons that I use on my characters. Its Parley ability makes it so bullets pierce enemies, and have a 5% chance to cause an explosion when they hit an enemy.

Thanks to this weapon's amazing fire rate, that 5% seems to proc very often, causing major damage. You will never be let down by the Darkbeast, and you can get it from Voraxis in the Coastal Bonescape.

With how I mentioned that the Darkbeast tends to outperform other weapons, there are some that still outperform it, and the Ohm I got is one of them. This is an electric legendary SMG that fires a laser at opponents, and it will absolutely destroy shields.

It has the ability Energy Transfer, which boosts its damage by 50% whenever your shield is active. However, it consumes your shield in the process. This can be prevented if you have an overshield, so any character can get massive damage with this weapon if they just have an overshield.

The best part about this ability, besides the damage, is that it doesn’t consume ammo when you have a shield either, allowing it to fire infinitely until your shield is gone. You can get this weapon from the second Ripper Queen fight.

If you want a weapon that can delete a boss’s health bar, look no further than the Hot Slugger. A few shots from this can annihilate a health bar if you have the right build.

This legendary shotgun has the Blazing Barrel ability, which gives it a 30% damage boost for 10 seconds after you get a kill. This can stack up to three times for a massive 90% damage boost if you keep getting kills.

You can stack this damage during a boss fight by killing minions and then destroy the boss’s health bar. There are multiple builds that can take advantage of this weapon, and it is amazing.

The Convergence has been around since the start of Borderlands 4 and it still stands as one of the best legendary shotguns in the game, and it is one that you can get relatively easily. It is also a gun that can carry you through many hardships.

This weapon has the Asymptotic ability, which it doesn’t give an explanation for, but you can easily tell what it does as soon as you fire the weapon. Every time you hit an enemy, additional shots converge onto that enemy dealing additional damage.

This causes the weapon to deal some massive damage to enemies. Combined with its fast fire rate, you can easily wipe out health bars in no time. This weapon is dropped from Bramblesong in the Bittervein Augur Mine.

There are many bosses in Borderlands 4, but only Bramblesong has the Convergence as a dedicated drop.

Don’t you just wish that your guns had triple the bullets that you could embed into the bodies of your enemies? Well, the Triplicate, one of the best DLC weapons, aims to deliver, as it will indeed fill your enemies full of lead.

This weapon has the Tripled ability, which makes it so additional explosive projectiles get lobbed whenever you hit an enemy. This makes the weapon overpowered, and capable of taking down groups of enemies in record time.

If you manage to get a shotgun alternate fire mode on this weapon, it will score multiple hits, allowing multiple projectiles to spawn out of the enemy, and when used against larger enemies, it is a death sentence. You can get this weapon from The Reject Prototype in the Amara and the Vile Shadow DLC.

The Golden God is amazing as long as you don’t mind continuously buying ammo for it, as it will use a ton of ammo. However, that ammo loss is okay since you will annihilate every enemy you look at.

The ability Rage make it so that the Golden Gun does not break when you hit the heat threshold. Instead, it continuously consumes greater and greater amounts of ammo until you eventually run out.

This allows you to continuously output damage at an impressive rate, but you will just use a ton of ammo in the process. This weapon can be obtained by farming Idolator Sol, which is one of the earlier bosses in the game.

Stray is a legendary sniper rifle that was available from the get-go when Borderlands 4 launched. All you needed to do was make your way over to the Tendercage Ripper Drill Site and kill the boss called Pango Bango.

Pango Bango drops three weapons, but Stray is by far the best of them. This legendary sniper rifle has the ability stray, which makes it fire two secondary bullets that home in on nearby targets.

This makes it great for clearing out crowds, and one of the best sniper rifles in the game. It is a weapon, like the others on this list, that works very well with most builds, and can be utilized by any of the characters effortlessly.

At this point, everyone should have the Bod in either their bank or on their character, as this weapon is fantastic, and is amazing for testing out builds. The reason is that is counts as every type of weapon.

The All-Arounder ability on it makes it so that it counts as a shotgun, assault rifle, sniper rifle, pistol, and SMG. This also means that it is affected by every type of damage increase, even for specific weapon types.

Even more, the damage bonuses can stack, meaning you can get the benefit of skills that increase damage for SMGs and shotguns on this weapon. To obtain the Bod, you will want to farm Axemaul in Carcadia Burn district of Dominion.
