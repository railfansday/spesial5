2027 Chrysler Pacifica Adds a New Copper Styling Package to the Menu

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Chrysler’s refreshed minivan can now be dressed up with a new Copperline theme, and Ski Grey is a new cabin color.

The Chrysler Pacifica has gone largely unchanged during its decade-long life. After its 2017 introduction, the minivan has seen just two mild refreshes—one in 2021 and one for 2027. Hey, if it ain’t broke, don’t fix it.

The Pacifica’s latest refresh comes with a rhinoplasty, trading its aging face for a more futuristic front end; its backside has been restyled too. That’s about where the list of upgrades starts and ends, but Chrysler has now revealed a few new styling options that add a bit more flavor to this year’s Pacifica family.

The new Copperline appearance package adds copper-colored exterior and interior pieces to the Limited and Pinnacle trim levels. Copperline Pacificas will wear copper-tinted 20-inch wheels and unique badging, along with copper-accented fascias and mirror caps.

Inside, Copperline models will feature Sol Stopper copper stitching on either black or Blue Agave nappa leather seats, depending on the trim. Pinnacle models use Copper Alloy accents on the instrument panel and air vents, and the Limited trim adds copper bits to the center console as well. Both trims will use black seatbelts with copper borders.

Also new for 2027 is a Ski Grey interior colorway, which is offered on Select and Limited trims without the S appearance package, as well as the Pinnacle. The airy, light grey interior adds a brighter option to the Pacifica’s lineup, which now features eight color and material combinations to choose from.

The Copperline package adds $1995 to Limited models and $995 to Pinnacle models. The Ski Grey interior is a no-cost option on the Pinnacle, but it will be a $295 upcharge on Select and Limited trims. Orders for 2027 Pacificas open later this year, and the vehicles will begin arriving at dealers in early next year.
