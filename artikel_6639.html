4 Winter Events That Are Inspiring Our Holiday Gifting

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The temperatures may be getting colder, but our social calendar is just heating up.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

The winter season is officially upon us, and while the frosty weather can often tempt us to hibernate indoors, there is quite a stacked agenda of events happening outside our front doors.

From the beachy scene of Miami Art Basel to the timeless elegance of the Vienna Opera Ball, winter’s main events offer a wide variety of gifting inspiration, no invitation required. So whether you’re gifting a tennis devotee or a champagne connoisseur this year, there’s a little something for everyone here. Ahead, find gifts inspired by the standout events of the winter season that bring the spirit of them a little closer to home.

Starting off our lineup on a warmer note, we’re heading to Miami Beach for Art Basel. Opening on December 4, the event brings together creatives, artists, and curators from around the world to take in the latest exhibitions. Dive into the art scene with bold gifts like Fieldbar’s mini bar cooler, Jonathan Adler’s colorful beach towel, or Gather’s abstract hand-blown wine glasses. Perfect for the artistic visionary in your life or just for the avid beach-goer planning their next getaway.

Tie up those sneakers and stretch out those legs because we’re taking a sportier approach for the next event on our agenda. The Australian Open starts off the tennis season for the new year from January 11 to 31 in Melbourne. Even if you can’t make it to Australia this season, tennis is a year-round sport, so save any of these gifts for your next match. Get ready to take on the courts with a sleek pair of sneakers from Celine, Sony’s noise-canceling headphones, and sporty socks from Comme Si.

Now not all of the events on this agenda are warm escapes for the winter season. Our next event takes us to Aspen for a bit of snow and sport. The X Games brings out some of the biggest competitors in snowboarding and skiing, from January 29 to 31. Even if you’re not in line to compete this winter, you can still look chic while staying warm. Start with a creamy cashmere turtleneck from Polo Ralph Lauren and a color-blocked wool Loewe scarf wrapped around your neck. Pair it with a set of Yves Salomon mittens, and you’re ready to hit the slopes in style.

Last, but certainly not least, we end the season on a glamorous note with the Vienna Opera Ball. Taking place on February 4 next year, the iconic ceremony will bring together an evening of dancing and champagne toasts. Set the scene at home by popping open a bottle of Laurent Perrier champagne and pouring it into a Lobmeyr crystal coupe, maybe a Cartier Baignoire watch on your wrist, and you’re practically ready for the next waltz in the ballroom.
