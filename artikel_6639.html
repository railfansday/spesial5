Sweet Potato Casserole

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Move aside, other casseroles—sweet potato casserole ranks at the very top of my list when it comes to Thanksgiving sides. I'll be coming back to this recipe time and time again, thanks to its buttery pecan topping. It’s still plenty sweet (thanks to the brown sugar), but not enough to make the sweet potatoes taste like straight-up dessert.

After preheating the oven and greasing a baking dish with cooking spray, place your sweet potatoes in a large pot and cover with water. Bring them to a boil, then reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer until the potatoes are tender and you can poke them with a fork. Drain the water, then let them cool slightly before transferring to a large bowl. Add the butter to the potatoes, then mash with a potato masher or 2 forks.

Mash until you reach your desired consistency (I prefer it smooth with a few chunks). Once they’ve reached the consistency you’re looking for, add eggs, milk, brown sugar, salt, nutmeg, and vanilla and stir until smooth. Once mixed, transfer to the prepared baking dish.

In a medium bowl, mix all of the topping ingredients together until combined.

Once combined, sprinkle the topping over the sweet potato mixture. Bake the casserole until it is warmed through and the topping is golden brown, then serve immediately while warm.

This is a great make-ahead Thanksgiving recipe to save yourself time (and energy) on the big day. Prep the casserole up to step 2, then refrigerate until you're ready to bake (we recommend no more than a few days ahead of time). When you're ready to bake, you can prepare and top with the crumbly topping.

If you have any leftovers (which I can't imagine you would), put them in an airtight container—or cover your casserole dish—and refrigerate for three to five days. This casserole also freezes wonderfully. Similar to prepping it ahead, simply prepare your casserole and bake it without topping it with the pecan topping. Allow it to cool before covering and freezing. When you're ready to serve, simply thaw it in the fridge overnight, top it with the crumbly topping, and bake.
