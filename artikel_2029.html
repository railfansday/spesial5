Sources: Leeds set sights on Bilal El Khannouss after transfer mission

Leeds United are plotting a move to sign Stuttgart star Bilal El Khannouss in the January transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.

The 22-year-old has been a long-term target for Daniel Farke's side, who have been keeping a close eye on his performances since his move to the Bundesliga outfit in 2025.

He has been on the radar at Elland Road since his spell at Leicester City, and despite having missed out on a deal previously, they could be tempted to return in January.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Leeds remain very keen on El Khannouss and have been keeping tabs on his availability.

The Morocco international would add quality to Farke's squad, and Leeds could step up their efforts to land him in the January window amid new speculation about his future.

However, there are doubts about whether Stuttgart would be willing to entertain offers despite suggestions elsewhere the El Khannouss could be allowed to move on.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider during the summer that Leeds were keeping tabs on El Khannouss and had considered reigniting their interest.

Despite opting against a move in the summer, they have continued to keep tabs on the midfielder's progress, and have been impressed by what they've seen at Stuttgart.

As Farke plans to strengthen his squad in the January transfer window, there is a feeling Leeds could reignite their intrest, despite doubts about his availability.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Well if there was an opportunity there to sign someone like El Khannouss, I think Leeds would definitely be looking at him.

"They have held a long-term interest in the Morocco international, they were hoping to sign him from Leicester before he made the move to Stuttgart and he was a big miss.

"They've been keeping an eye on him since his move to the Bundesliga outfit, so if he becomes available, they might be tempted to make a move.

"It's a long-term interest that Leeds have had in him but just think with Stuttgart competing well in the Bundesliga and the Champions League it'll be a very hard deal to do."

Leeds are not the only club interested in signing El Khannouss, and his potential availability in the January transfer window is likely to place a number of suitors on alert.

Before his move to Germany from Leicester City in 2025, the likes of Tottenham and Crystal Palace were also believed to be keen to prise him away from the King Power.

Meanwhile, Football Insider reported back in August that Newcastle could be tempted to reignite their long-term interest in El Khannouss if he is allowed to leave Stuttgart.

The Magpies have been keen to strengthen their squad following a busy summer transfer window, and could look at some of their previous targets if their status changes.

Much will depend on whether Stuttgart are prepared to let El Khannouss go mid-way through the season, but he will have no shortage of suitors if he does move on.
