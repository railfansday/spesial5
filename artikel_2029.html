Why journalists’s claim of imminent departure will go down just fine with fans | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

When relegation reality kicked in and the dust settled, fans waited for players to confirm their intentions as far as playing in the second tier were concerned. The first two to break cover were inevitably Jarrod Bowen and Tomas Soucek who made their loyalties pretty clear.

Others followed including Mavropanos and Castellanos – and fans thought there was the spine of a team that would flourish in the Championship. Supporters renewed their season tickets in their tens of thousands, hoping that West Ham would dominate the division after two terrible years of being rooted at the foot of the Premier League.

West Ham sit in ninth having fired Nuno, (the Championship Manager of The Month for September, remember) and the ‘match winner’ we all expected Taty Castellanos to become has simply not materialised.

Castellanos: All ‘attitude’ and no ‘action’.

So much so that now there’s a realisation from Scott Parker that West Ham need a new striker and news just today from Taty’s homeland suggests he’s made his own mind up that it is time to walk away: Argentine journalist Renzo Pantich (with some 250k followers) confirms it:

“Referring to a possible departure from West Ham, Pantich had said: “He wants to leave West Ham, many have already begun to give it as a possibility or as if River laid his eyes.”

Pantich separated the possibility that Castellanos wanted to leave the English club from his intention to return to South America. About his future, he said: “Well, the footballer would not plan to return to South America, in fact he rejected an offer to Flamengo and also plans to stay a little longer in Europe.” (Elintransigente.com)

Which certainly suits the club and its own fans: Europe, specifically the Premier League, is where the money is that would allow West Ham to recoup most if not all of their outlay.

Unlikely, you’d think, that Taty would find a Premier League side willing to take him after failing so spectacularly in the Championship?

Knowing the craziness of football, it is probable there’s already a manager out there who thinks they can unlock his talent and his goals. They might need to work on his attitude too.

Castellanos has been one massive disappointment for supporters this season, with his goals, assists and build-up play having deserted him, the only thing remaining is the scowl and the attitude.
