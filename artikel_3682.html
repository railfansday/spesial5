How to get the third map fragment in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The third map fragment in Fortnite Treasure Hunters is now live, and this one takes us all the way to the north. With the latest launch, we can find three different fragments and complete a significant portion of the quest.

However, the location of the map fragment won’t be revealed on the in-game map, which makes it tricky to find.

As I said, we have to travel all the way to the top to find the map fragment. To be precise, it can be found under a tree at the base of the building that’s exactly opposite the Crusty Cod. I have marked its location in the image below.

In simpler words, you need to reach the base of the tree shown in the image below. You can spot the tree easily when dropping from the top since there aren’t many trees in the middle area of the POI.

When you reach the base, you’ll find the map fragment resting on the ground. Interact with it to complete the quest and add the fragment to your collection. As I mentioned above, we can now complete half of the map fragment quest, but it’s still going to require a few more weeks for us to collect the remaining ones.

Friendship Villa is a hotspot for players as there are NPCs to recruit and valuable loot to find. Hence, drop in and find the fragment as quickly as possible. Relocate to other locations unless you want to participate in firefights and take down opposing players.
