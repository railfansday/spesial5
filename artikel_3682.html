"We’ve seen a lot of discussion about the (DPS) role of the season 6 hero. We understand that you were looking forward to another support hero next season and I hope that giving context for the decision will help players understand it, even as some of them are disappointed."

"When we started work on the detective, he was a support. But as time went on and the kit continued development we realized that it wasn’t working. Once we tested Hudson as a DPS, the turn around in our feelings for him was instantaneous and dramatic. This decision ultimately wasn't whether we release a DPS or a support hero, it was whether we release a great character or one we didn't believe in. Building heroes for OW is as much of an art form as a technical challenge." via a social media post.

Last week we found out a few new things about the next hero coming to Overwatch in season 6. He's an omnic detective, his name's Hudson, he has a fun accent, and he will be a DPS. Hudson is the last hero to be released from the batch teased earlier this year during Overwatch's Spotlight, and after all this time I'm very excited to see what he has to offer. But not everyone shares this sentiment.

There's a good chunk of players who are upset about Hudson, before they've even tried him out, simply because he's not a support hero, which was what Team 4 initially promised him to be. I've seen this discontent online and honestly ignored it, thinking it was either bots or people farming for attention because I cannot fathom why anyone is honestly upset about this.

I've been maining support in Overwatch for almost 10 years now, ever since open queue grinded me down and I accepted that no matter how much I liked DPS, I had to role fill if we wanted to win games. So, after all these years I love getting my hands on a new support hero, but after four new supports this year (including Sombra's transition to the role), I'm more than satisfied with the role's current diversity of heroes.

But Team 4 is taking the criticism seriously, as Keller has addressed it directly. Aside from trying to convince players that shoehorning a hero into a role just because that was the plan 10 months ago isn't a great idea, he also explains how "game dev isn't always straightforward" and how the team needs "to adapt to create the best version of something".

I completely agree. I'd much rather have a hero that was crafted for a role holistically than one forced into it to fill a quota. Letting the people who know Overwatch the best, aka the devs, have the freedom to craft heroes and change them when needs be is what will ensure the best possible outcome.

This is such a non-issue, especially considering how strong the support roster currently is. Look at our poor tank players for example, forced to switch between Orisa and Mauga every game. Go give them some love and attention, lord knows they need the energy much more than us over in support.
