NEAR Protocol, Chainlink and Litecoin remain under selling pressure as the broader cryptocurrency market continues to struggle. NEAR is testing support near $3 after losing much of its October gains, while LINK has fallen below the key $9 level as sellers maintain control.

NEAR Protocol ($NEAR) is trading near $2.98 after giving up some of its momentum from its October rally. Currently trading at $2.976 on Binance at 14:16 UTC on November 20, the asset has a day's high of $3.226 and a low of $2.962.

On its daily chart, NEAR has been on a steady decline since its October high near $6. Having given up much of its momentum from the September rally, NEAR has also been unable to sustain any of its rebound attempts. As such, the next price move will be critical for any potential recovery.

Currently trading below its short-term moving averages and testing its long-term moving average support near the psychologically significant $3 level, NEAR faces a key test. It remains to be seen whether this will lead to a potential rebound or further declines.

The market structure also remains bearish. However, lower trading volumes compared to the September rally point to weakening buying interest. Nonetheless, this does not necessarily confirm that selling pressure has eased.

Chainlink (LINK) has been on a steady decline, with each rebound attempt forming lower highs. Currently trading near $8.79 and below all of its major moving averages on the daily chart, LINK has also lost the important psychological support level at $9.

With its RSI approaching oversold territory, LINK faces another test as traders watch for signs of renewed buying interest.

For now, LINK/USD is trading at $8.786 at 14:16 UTC on November 20, after opening at $8.993, touching a high of $9.062, and hitting a low of $8.667.

Chainlink (LINK) is trading below all of its moving averages. In addition, it has dropped below the previous support at $9, indicating that selling pressure continues to control the market.

For LINK/USD to rise substantially from its current levels, LINK needs to reclaim the $11 area. Until then, the broader market outlook remains bearish.

Litecoin has reversed most of the bullish rally it experienced in September and is now facing renewed selling pressure. The cryptocurrency is currently hovering around $41.68.

Litecoin continues to form lower lows and lower highs since peaking near $75 in October.

Litecoin's volume has also failed to show any signs of increased buying pressure after the recent sell-off. Although green candles appear occasionally, they have not been strong enough to indicate a change in trend. For LTC/USD to reverse its current direction, it will need to show sustained buying pressure.

Selling pressure has remained strong throughout November, pushing the price below several support levels. At the time of writing (14:22 UTC on November 20), LTC is trading at $41.679, with a day's high and low of $42.454 and $41.047, respectively.

Overall, the daily chart remains bearish.

Currently, the price is trading below all the moving averages on the chart, while the short-term averages are also moving lower. This suggests that sellers remain in control.

Trading volumes have also failed to increase significantly, indicating limited buying interest in LTC. The RSI is close to the oversold region, meaning a short-term rebound cannot be ruled out.

That said, there is no confirmation of a reversal. Immediate support is at the $41 level, followed by the psychological $40 level.

A breakdown below $40 could result in further declines toward $38. For a turnaround, the price first needs to move above the 44-45 region. Therefore, the overall market bias remains bearish.
