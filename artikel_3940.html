The Witch Life-Sim Witchbrook Has Officially "Gone Gold"

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

During the Indie World Showcase in October, it was confirmed the witch-themed life-sim and social RPG Witchbrook would be coming to the Switch 1 & 2 on 28th January 2027.

In an update from the developer this week, the team has now reassured Nintendo fans it's locked in for this date, as the game has officially "gone gold" on all platforms. Here's the message, along with a fitting in-game screenshot:

"We're delighted to announce that Witchbrook has GONE GOLD ALL PLATFORMS! We're can't wait for you all to join us on the journey on Jan 28th!"

Again, this basically means everything is currently on schedule, and the master copy is ready to go. Of course, "going gold" in the digital age means teams can still spend time polishing their title before the launch.

In this particular case, Witchbrook is getting an eShop release (with a physical version to follow), and we can probably expect some patches and updates as well. It was recently confirmed Witchbrook would also support crossplay between Switch 1 & 2 on release.
