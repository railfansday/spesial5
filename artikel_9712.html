Measuring the Impact of Expo Router Deep Links

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Posted on Nov 13 • Originally published at amitchakraborty.dev

Say you are a mobile app developer working on a React Native project using Expo Router. You've heard about the benefits of deep links, but you're not sure if implementing them will have a significant impact on your app's performance. You've tried to measure the effect of deep links on your app's routing, but you're not sure what metrics to track or how to instrument your app to get meaningful data.

You were trying to optimize your app's routing to improve user experience, but you're not sure if the effort will be worth it. You've seen a symptom - a noticeable delay when navigating between screens - but you don't know if it's related to deep links or something else. The delay is costing you users, who are abandoning your app due to frustration with its performance.

Expo Router deep links can improve app performance by allowing the app to navigate directly to a specific screen, rather than loading the entire app and then navigating to the desired screen. However, implementing deep links requires changes to your app's routing configuration, which can be complex and time-consuming. To determine whether the effort is worth it, you need to measure the impact of deep links on your app's performance.

One key metric to track is the time it takes for the app to navigate between screens. This can be measured using the navigation module in Expo Router, which provides a navigate function that returns a promise that resolves when the navigation is complete. You can use this promise to measure the time it takes for the navigation to complete, and compare it to the time it takes without deep links.
