Where to Stream ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Season 35 Finale Live Online

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The special three-hour event — airing live across all U.S. time zones for the first time in show history — will see the final five couples perform three routines each.

The season 35 finale of Dancing With the Stars marks the show’s very first time being broadcast live coast-to-coast — zero tape delay in any U.S. time zones.

The three-hour episode airs on Nov. 24 at 5 p.m. PT/8 p.m. ET on ABC, available to stream live via DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), Fubo (with a five-day free trial), Sling and Hulu + Live TV.

The finale will also stream live on Disney+, which doesn’t offer a free trial of its own, but is included in any of DirecTV’s Signature packages, which do offer the aforementioned free trial. At a Glance: How to Watch Dancing With the Stars Season 35 Finale

The season 35 finale airs live on Tuesday, Nov. 24 at 5 p.m. PT/8 p.m. ET on ABC (carried on live TV streamers like DirecTV, Fubo, Sling and Hulu + Live TV) and will be simulcast on Disney+.

Since the competition show airs on ABC, it’s also available to watch live on any streaming service that carries said network, including DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), Fubo (also with a five-day free trial), Sling and Hulu + Live TV.

The finale will also stream live on Disney+, and while the service doesn’t offer a free trial directly, fans can get free access through any of DirecTV’s Signature packages, which include the Disney+ and Hulu bundle and offer a five-day free trial.

To claim the free trial, select your preferred Signature package. Once it prompts you to choose how many Gemini devices you’d like to connect, you’ll select “0 – Stream without the Gemini Air” as the five-day free trial is only available to customers without a leased Gemini device.

Learn more about each plan option, including how to build your own channel lineup (starting at just $20.99 per month), at directv.com.

After the trial, plans start at $54.99 for the first month and $64.99 monthly thereafter.

In select markets, ABC is included in Sling’s budget-friendly Select plan, from $19.99 per month; Sling’s Blue plan, from $45.99 per month; and Sling’s Orange & Blue plan, from $60.99 per month.

Watch ABC with Hulu + Live TV. Subscriptions come bundled with Disney+ and ESPN+, and start at $89.99 per month.

Season 34 champions Robert Irwin and Witney Carson return to the DWTS dance floor for the 2026 finale.
