Was The Rolling Stones’ 1973 Still Part of the Band’s Classic Period, Or the Beginning of the End? (Greatest Pop Stars Podcast)

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

This Vintage Pop Stardom episode of Greatest Pop Stars looks at a year where the World's Greatest Rock and Roll Band was at full power, but maybe moving into a different phase of its career.

In 1973, The Rolling Stones were coming off one of the greatest album runs in rock and roll history, a four-album stretch that cemented them as the World’s Greatest Rock and Roll Band, culminating in 1972’s Exile on Main Street and its legendarily hedonistic accompanying tour. Rather than take time off to victory lap following this iconic period, they headed to Jamaica to record their third album of the ’70s, released in 1973 as Goats Head Soup — which topped the Billboard 200 and spawned a Billboard Hot 100 No. 1 single with breakup ballad “Angie,” but drew middling lukewarm and is now viewed by many fans as the beginning of the end of the band’s glory days. On this Vintage Pop Stardom episode of the Greatest Pop Stars podcast, host Andrew Unterberger is joined by Billboard executive digital director, east coast Joe Lynch — who wrote an excellent Stones cover story in July — to discuss a fascinating year in the Stones’ trajectory, where they were still one of the biggest bands in the world, but moving into their “holiday period” as rock standard-bearers.

Along the way, we answer all the most pressing questions about the 1973 Rolling Stones: Was Goats Head Soup really that much of a step down for the Stones, or is it a bit misunderstood historically? Is it surprising we didn’t get more of a reggae album out of it? What was the deal with that album title and album cover? Is “Angie” a little too MOR for our tastes? How much did the band go on to miss Mick Taylor (and/or Jimmy Miller) after their departures? Was Truman Capote really unable to find a story following the Stones on their single most storied tour? And perhaps most importantly: Why the hell are we doing the 1973 Rolling Stones for this podcast right now anyway?
