Daniel Craig Stepped Away From James Bond Role To Spend More Time at Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Daniel Craig first stepped into the role of James Bond 20 years ago, and now, he has officially passed the duties along to someone new: Robert Pattinson.

Craig made his 007 debut in the 2006 film “Casino Royale” and held the legendary gig for 15 years. He had originally planned to play the British spy for four films, but ended up having a successful five-movie run with “Quantum Solace” (2008), “Skyfall” (2012), “Spectre” (2015), and “No Time To Die” (2021).

In addition to filming one more motion picture than he had planned, Craig, 58, has spoken out about how physically demanding it is to be James Bond. In the time between the last two films, he told The Sunday Times he was “getting old.”

“I’m creaky,” he admitted in the 2019 interview. In 2024, he told The New York Times, “I find it really hard, this job, and it gets harder as I get older.”

Beyond the grueling stunts and action required for each installment, the role demanded a huge time commitment that pulled Craig away from his family.

“The Girl With the Dragon Tattoo” star married actress Rachel Weisz in June 2011 in an intimate New York ceremony. Together, they welcomed a daughter named Grace in September 2018.

Craig also has an older daughter, Ella Loudon, 34, whom he shares with his first wife, Fiona Loudon. He’s also a stepfather to Weisz’s son, Henry Aronfsky, 20, from her former relationship with filmmaker Darren Aronofsky.

Although Craig has said that he’s “not a method actor,” he described himself as a “nightmare to be with” while he’s working.

“I want to go home and forget it all and just be normal and be like, 'Hey, family,' but half my brain’s at work,” he said. But having a young daughter at home has changed his approach to his career.

“I don’t want to be away from home as much as I have in the past,” he explained.

Craig and Weisz, who both hail from England, have called New York home for quite some time.

In April of this year, they sold their Brooklyn brownstone for $11.8 million. They had picked it up for $6.75 million in 2018.

Built in 1901, the 6,600-square-foot abode spans five levels, which are accessible by elevator. It included a roof deck, a patio, and a backyard garden.

The six-bedroom, 5.5-bathroom residence was damaged by a fire in 2016, before the couple snapped it up. According to Architectural Digest, the previous owner, novelist Martin Amis, had transformed the home into a “clean, blank slate” in light of the damage, and Craig and Weisz put their own spin on it in the nearly eight years they lived there.

The off-market transaction came just a year after the couple sold an adjacent townhouse for $4.05 million.

They still hold ties to the Empire State, with an upstate retreat in Marbletown, NY, that Weisz purchased for $2.1 million in 2008. Nestled on an idyllic 124-acre parcel, the 2,500-square-foot stone home dates to 1785 and includes four bedrooms and three bathrooms.

Overseas, Craig and Weisz have a $4.2 million Victorian mansion in a conservation area in North London. It was a home the “Vladimir” actress had purchased before they were married.

According to the Daily Mail, they sought to heavily renovate the 1840 home in 2023, but had to alter their plans when an objection claimed the proposal would clash with the home’s original style. The renovation was ultimately approved in 2024.
