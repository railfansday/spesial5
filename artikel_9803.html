Apia, Samoa, November 13th, 2026/Chainwire/--BTSE Enterprise Solutions has been named Best White-Label Digital Asset Exchange Platform by a Vendor at the Digital Banker Awards 2026. The awards are presented by The Digital Banker, a global financial media and research platform known for its banking awards programs, which recognize leading banks, financial institutions and technology providers worldwide.

The Digital Banker’s audience is senior decision-makers across banking and financial technology, including executives at banks, payment providers and digital asset businesses. Its awards are designed to serve as unbiased benchmarks for the industry, giving institutions an independent signal when choosing partners. For brokers and trading platforms weighing a digital asset launch, that matters: the choice of vendor is often what determines whether a project clears licensing, due diligence and board approval.

BTSE Enterprise Solutions offers a white-label platform that lets partners launch a branded exchange in weeks rather than years. Each deployment can be configured to support jurisdiction-specific requirements, helping operators address local requirements without rebuilding the core platform. More than 100 corporate clients now run exchange and payment platforms with BTSE Enterprise Solutions.

“Companies entering digital assets aren’t just looking for a vendor. They’re looking for an operator they can trust from day one,” said Jeff Mei, COO of BTSE Enterprise Solutions. “And most of our clients don’t come to us for just software. They come because running an exchange safely at scale is hard. Not only do they need around-the-clock technical and operations support, but they also need a partner that can provide a strategic view of their business and how it fits within the industry as a whole.” Mei said the award belongs to the company’s engineers and operations personnel, and to every client who trusted BTSE with their brand.

Looking ahead, BTSE is focused on helping traditional financial institutions in emerging markets enter digital assets with confidence, and on extending its payments and card products. Regulatory readiness is also becoming a larger part of the business as more countries introduce crypto exchange and payment regulations.

BTSE Enterprise Solutions offers technology solutions under BTSE Group, providing white-label exchange, payments and digital asset infrastructure to banks, fintech companies, brokers and entrepreneurs worldwide. Built on the trading systems expertise behind BTSE’s exchange, the company has offered its enterprise platform since 2022. For more information, visit www.btsesolutions.com.
