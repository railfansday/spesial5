Watch: Milan players dominate as Inzaghi picks best XI of team-mates

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Filippo Inzaghi has picked the best team that he played with, and it is unsurprisingly dominated by AC Milan players.

Inzaghi joined Milan at the age of 27 and spent the best years of his career at San Siro, becoming a fan favourite. He bagged a decisive brace in the 2007 Champions League Final against Liverpool in Athens and in the Club World Cup Final against Boca Juniors in Yokohama in the same year.

His record of 126 goals in 300 official appearances for the Rossoneri was incredible, as was his trophy haul: 2x Champions League titles, 1x FIFA Club World Cup, 2x Serie A titles, 2x UEFA Super Cups, 1x Coppa Italia, and 1x Supercoppa Italiana. He would return as head coach for 2014-15 too.

Inzaghi was interviewed by Salotto del Pallone for a video podcast, and as part of it he was asked to pick the best XI that he played with based on three options presented for each positions, from his spells at Juventus, Milan and with the Italian national team.

Defence: Maldini, Serginho, Thiago Silva, Zambrotta.
