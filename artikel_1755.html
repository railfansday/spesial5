Opinion - €70m steal: Arsenal name their price, now it's Liverpool's turn

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Liverpool have their opportunity. Arsenal named their price and it looks like it could be a €70m steal for the Reds.

We're hoping Liverpool make a major move in midfield next year - possibly even in January. It's clear to all that there's an issue in the middle of the pitch, with the Reds lacking proper control in there.

It's not an easy one to fix. Liverpool have become incredibly reliant on 19-year-old Trey Nyoni, a player who's still learning the game at the highest level. But that's what happens when you lack proper controlling midfielders - introduce one of any quality and the team looks better.

And the team certainly looks better with Nyoni in there. He, of course, happens to be an incredibly talented player, which helps, but we're not sure Liverpool will want to put all their eggs in that basket. He's only got limited experience, after all, in a position that typically demands a lot of it.

So we do expect Liverpool to make some sort of move. Potentially for an Arsenal player, in fact.

His last-minute change of heart prevented the deal, with Arsenal swooping in to complete it a year later. We're big, big fans of Martin Zubimendi, a player who very nearly joined Liverpool back in 2024. His last-minute change of heart prevented the deal, with Arsenal swooping in to complete it a year later.

But despite winning the Premier League with Zubimendi at the heart of their midfield, Arsenal are ready to move on already. The Spaniard has barely featured in the league this season and appears primed to depart.

We believe that's a massive mistake on Arsenal's part. Our Value Adder model holds Zubimendi in high regard and he's exactly the kind of player who Liverpool need right now.

AS in Spain report that the likes of Real Madrid and Chelsea are keen to sign him and that Arsenal are willing to sell. The price is high at €70m but for such an experienced player with his skill set? It could be a steal, especially as we expect that Zubimendi would move Liverpool on a level.

So we're advocating for this one and strongly. Arsenal are willing to part with one of the best controlling midfielders in the world at a time when Liverpool are crying out for one.

Make the move and let them regret it, we say.
