Huddersfield Town chief's praise for 'excellent' and 'humble' Ashley Fletcher + what pleased him most in win at Doncaster Rovers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The recalled forward, who started ahead of Bojan Radulovic, was excellent in the first half-hour at the Club Doncaster Sports Village and provided assists for the visitors' two goals from captain Cameron Humphreys and top-scorer Joe Taylor.

After the game changed following Bali Mumba's 34th-minute dismissal, Fletcher dropped to the left to help the team cope with playing with a man light.

The frontman continued to perform responsibly for the team before being sacrificed on 55 minutes when another midfielder in Marcus Harness entered the fray.

But Fletcher's impact was not lost upon Drury, whose side draw level on points with leaders Stockport County and maintained their fine away record this term - while recording their first-ever league victory, at the fifth attempt, at this South Yorkshire venue.

Drury, whose side return to action at home to Stevenage on Tuesday, said: "I thought he started the game so well.

"At half-time, we could have taken somebody off and changed the shape and everything, but we said we wanted to leave it for five minutes to see where it is.

"People understood the roles and Ashley Fletcher within that, I thought he was excellent.

"I was gutted for him that he had to come off, but he's a humble guy and he recognises the necessity of it. Hopefully, next time we have a game, he'll be more involved in that."

Despite playing with 10 men for around an hour, Town's organisation reminded spot on, allied to their sense of control and maintenance of an attacking threat.

Drury added: "That was probably the most pleasing aspect of it. You can't stop them putting you low, you can't stop them getting to your box because you've got one less player.

"But you can hold the line as high as you can and I felt we did that. We were aggressive with it.

"When we were put low, we were fighting to stop crosses. We were picking up in the box. Listen, we had some hairy moments in there, of course, but I think sometimes you get what you deserve and I felt the players deserved that result.

"I really did, particularly going down to 10 men for the amount of time that we did."
