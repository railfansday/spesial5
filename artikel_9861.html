I never knew him personally but I followed his work on bufferbloat since the very early days, when Jim Getty (of X11 fame) had a series of rants on his overly priced FiOS (FTTH) connection that behaved miserably whenever one of his kids (IIRC) downloaded anything. I'm not sure which one of the two coined the term "bufferbloat". But I remember Dave carrying the bufferbloat torch from then on. His writings were always fascinating to read even though, as time went on, they got more and more complex and I had a hard time keeping up. It felt like Dave was being an unofficial coordinator of the tiny gang of people trying to fix the internet: talking to cable operators and hw manufacturers, fixing bad drivers, fixes in existing TCP implementations and analyzing new ones, fixing network scheduling in the kernel (CoDel!!!), giving talks with water props!, CeroWrt, the list goes on… It really feels like this award, much deserved, is not enough for all the work Dave did, and all the work Dave helped others bring to fruition. May others be inspired by Dave and his work. I know I am.
