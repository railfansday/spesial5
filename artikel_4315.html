10 Legendary Shields You Should Have in Borderlands 4

Legendary items are the prize you are searching for in Borderlands 4. They will help you complete your build, and carry you into the different levels of Mayhem Mode.

With over a year’s worth of content added to the game, there are plenty of legendary items, which means you need to start refining which ones are best for your characters. Once you have all your legendary weapons, including legendary assault rifles, you need to move on to legendary shields.

Shields will protect you, give you some additional buffs, and help you fight back against the swarms of psychos and Order Troops across Kairos. If you haven’t obtained them already, these shields are ones that you should definitely get.

One of the best shields from the base game of Borderlands 4 is the Super Soldier, as it literally enhances your abilities, turning you into someone who can run and shoot fast. It is almost like you took the super serum given to Captain America. There are plenty of references and easter eggs to find across Kairos, too.

The shield has the ability called Power Play, which gives you an overshield whenever the shield refills. This is great for procing abilities on weapons like the Ohm I got that get benefits from overshields.

Additionally, while your shield is full, it grants you 25% increased fire rate and 25% increased movement speed, which is amazing on characters like Rafa and Loveless who can amp up their movement speed. If you want to get this shield, you will need to farm Vile Lictor in Terminus Range.

There are some shields that will carry you far into the game, and then there are shields that are perfect starting shields that pack a punch, and are great for new characters. The Firewerks shield is perfect for any new character that you create.

This shield comes with the Bundled ability, which fires rockets at enemies each time you get a kill. These missiles deal fairly decent damage, making it easier to clear crowds of enemies quickly.

Of course, being a shield you want to get early, you would expect to be able to farm this weapon early in the game, which you can. You can get this shield from Spashzone in Riptide Grotto, which is near where you start the game. Some legendary farming locations are trickier to find than you may think.

A shield is meant to keep you safe from damage, and the Onion is all about that by offering you a means of negating damage for brief periods of time. Instead of a normal shield, this is an armor shield, meaning it has multiple segments.

The ability Shallot Shell makes it so you are immune to damage for a couple seconds after an armor segment breaks. Since this shield often has four segments, you have four times where you will be immune to damage before the shield is gone.

It is great for increasing your survivability, and is perfect for characters who are a bit squishy. You can obtain this shield by farming Sludgemaw at Watershed Gate.

Do you want a shield that will cause some devastation each time it breaks and refills? Well, you might want to get your hands on the Extra Medium then, as it will cause some serious devastation.

This shield isn’t the most protective, with it sitting near the average when it comes to shield health. However, this is made up for by the ability Nucleosynthesis, which causes it to trigger a nova that deals major damage in the surrounding area each time it breaks or refills.

Of course, you need to let the shield completely refill before you can trigger the effect again, but it is great for getting out of sticky situations, and you can obtain this shield by farming the Vault Boss named Inceptus.

One of the biggest things that kills me in Borderlands 4 is self-damage. I blow myself up all the time with grenades and other types of splash damage. It is a serious issue. Luckily, the Honey Badger fixes this issue.

The Honey Badger shield has the Ferocity ability, which reduces self-damage dealt by 90%, meaning you can take a hit from a grenade and not get thrown into a fight for your life. It is the perfect shield for those who deal a lot of splash damage all around them with Torgue weapons.

Of course, if you don’t have this issue, you can skip over this shield, but it is worth it if you do. You can get the Honey Badger from Big Oil in the Bloodstained Hollow as part of the Vault of the Damned DLC. For more gear, check out the best DLC weapons.

There are some shields that have amazing abilities that boost your damage, but this damage buff comes at a cost. For the Protean Cell, this cost is the shield’s health. Luckily, it has a lot of health.

This shield has the Direct Current ability, which drains the shield over time, but grants you a bonus 40% shock damage. However, while this ability is active, you take direct damage, meaning the shield won’t protect you, so you need to be selective about when you use it.

Use this shield correctly, and you will rip through enemy health bars in seconds. Just don’t get carried away or it could mean the end of your character. You can get this shield from Fractis in Grindstone of the Worthy.

There are plenty of weapons in Borderlands 4 that have been added in DLC. Which ones of them are the best?

Every character can have some sort of status effect build, which makes the Exfoliatrix a great shield because it can be used efficiently by every character. You just need to be running a status effect build.

The ability of this shield is Exfoliatrix, which increases your status effect chance for six seconds each time an armor segment breaks. Additionally, whenever an enemy receives status effect damage, you recover a portion of the shield.

If you are constantly dealing status effect damage, you will be well protected by this shield, and you won’t need to worry about taking damage. You can get this shield by farming the Digi-Rifts on Providence Island in the FL4K and the Last Resort DLC.

An interesting ability that some shields have is that they can spawn kill drones that help you damage enemies. The Little Sister is one of these shields, and it does a great job taking down enemies.

It has the ability Sister, which grants you a kill drone whenever an armor segment breaks. This kill drone fights alongside you, mimicking the element of the gun you are using, and dealing 25% of the gun’s damage.

It is a perfect little partner to help you on the battlefield, and one that will continuously deal damage for you, helping you to wipe out groups of enemies. You can get this shield from Throne’s Experiments in Research Center B-0451. It can also be dropped from the chests at the end of the Takedown at the Hadron Abyss.

The Watts 4 Dinner shield is pretty similar to the Protean Cell shield, as they both grant you bonus shock damage. While the damage buff is less for this shield, it is a far better shield overall.

The Watts 4 Dinner shield has the ability Overshield Eater, which makes it so enemies have an 8% chance to drop an overshield when hit. Whenever you have an overshield active, you get a bonus 25% shock damage.

This is very easy to obtain, especially if you have skills on your character that grant overshield. Giving you a consistent 25% damage bonus is amazing, making this shield one of the best in the game. You can get this shield by farming Origo in the Vault of Origo.

Nova shields can be really strong, especially when they give you a damage buff and also release a nova. The Cindershelly is great because it does both of these things, making it easy to take down enemies.

This shield has the Glass ability, which makes it so you get a stack of resolve whenever the shield is damaged. Each stack of resolve grants you 2% damage reduction, and when full of stacks, this means you have a 50% damage reduction.

To top it off, when this shield breaks, it releases a nova that deals a set amount of damage, but this damage is increased by 25% for each stack of resolve you have, meaning you can do an insane amount of damage with the blast. You can get this shield from The Backhive in Dissected Plateau.
