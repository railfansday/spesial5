Key Birmingham City player attracts new transfer interest with Premier League club keen

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Birmingham City sit top of the Championship table after 15 games, and one man who has been ever-present is centre-back Phil Neumann. The BCFC star has played all 1,350 minutes of league football this season, and according to reports in Germany, his performances are drawing attention from a higher level.

Sky Sports Germany states that Neumann is of interest to Premier League side Nottingham Forest and Fiorentina of Serie A. There is a chance the Blues defender could jump to a higher level as soon as the winter transfer window.

Chris Davies and co will surely be adamant that a player as key as Neumann does not leave during a push for promotion from the Championship. The 29-year-old signed a three-year deal upon arrival in 2025, so his current contract runs until 2028.

Neumann joined Birmingham City for their step up to Championship football after signing a pre-contract deal in January 2025. He took a bit of time to settle into the Blues side as Davies worked out his preferred centre-back pairing, but a strong end to the 2025/26 season set the German up well for the new campaign.

The City defender has been nigh-on undroppable this term, hence why he has played every minute of all 15 Championship games so far. Across all competitions, the only game Neumann didn’t start ended in a 6-1 loss to Brentford in the Carabao Cup.

Neumann has been one of the Championship’s best centre-backs this season, as displayed by FotMob‘s dataset. Compared to his divisional counterparts, the BCFC ranks in the top 5% for duels won (98), touches in the opposition box (32), defensive contributions (181), tackles (25), interceptions (26) and clearances (115).

Neumann is a powerful and commanding aerial presence and dependable in possession. His partnership with Christoph Klarer is providing the foundation for the Blues to be bolder going forward.

Birmingham City’s trajectory and their ambitions should be enough to fend off interest in Neumann in January. If his head is turned and a big enough bid comes in, that could change things, but the Blues are certainly under no pressure to sell and he appears to be happy on and off the pitch.

Beyond then, however, keeping Neumann will be down to winning promotion. The German has proven himself at Championship level and if the opportunity is there, he will be determined to show he can cut it in top-flight football.

Neumann has previously expressed an interest in returning to the Bundesliga. Nottingham Forest and Fiorentina make for attractive destinations too though, so time will tell just what 2027 has in store for the BCFC star.
