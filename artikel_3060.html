Planet Fitness: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Its share buyback plan rests on cash from refranchising clubs, a tension the 8% revenue outlook alone does not resolve.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 5, 2026, Planet Fitness (PLNT +4.76%) announced a shift toward a regional pricing strategy and plans to sell corporate-owned gyms to franchisees. The fitness chain is balancing lower-priced memberships with profitability to expand franchisee cash flow and buy back stock. Three things management said stand out.

Testing showed that a blanket price drop did not boost total revenue across every market, so management is shifting to local pricing power. Customizing prices by region protects value while giving franchisees room to grow revenue.

Planet Fitness is evaluating whether to sell a portion of its 280 corporate-owned clubs back to franchise operators. Refranchising these locations turns physical assets into liquid capital while keeping corporate locations lean enough to test new ideas.

Buying Be-Fit in Portugal gives the company 25 existing locations and 80,000 members across the Iberian Peninsula. Management intends to scale European operations through acquisitions, convert the locations to its brand, and eventually refranchise them to local operators.

Systemwide same-club sales grew 1.5% in the third quarter, prompting management to raise its full-year revenue growth outlook to 8%. High membership retention and stable royalty streams underpin cash generation while management monitors domestic real estate timelines.

A new $500 million share buyback authorization reinforces capital return priorities as refranchising accelerates.
