WWE Star Thea Hail Shaves Head on Live TV Amid Alopecia Diagnosis at Age 23

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

WWE NXT star Thea Hail bravely shaved her head on live TV after being diagnosed with alopecia at age 23.

Back in August, Hail, real name Madison Knisley, shared via Instagram that she had alopecia areata, which, according to the Mayo Clinic, causes hair to fall out in patches. “Alopecia areata is a common autoimmune disorder affecting about 1 in every 1,000 people,” per the Mayo Clinic, with over 80% of people diagnosed with the condition showing signs of alopecia areata before the age of 40.

On the Tuesday, November 24, episode of WWE NXT on The CW, Hail got emotional as she addressed her progressive hair loss in front of the live audience at the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida.

“You’re expected to walk through that curtain and look perfect,” Hail said through tears, acknowledging the beauty standards placed on women in professional wrestling.

“I’m sure you can all imagine how afraid I was to share with the world that at 23 years old, I was diagnosed with alopecia,” she continued. “In less than four months, I lost about 40% of my hair. Since July, I’ve been asking myself the same questions you’re all probably wondering: will it grow back? How will I look? What does this mean? But most importantly, how will other people look at me if I have no hair?”

The former NXT Women’s North American Champion vowed to “take this moment of weakness and turn it into a strength” because “alopecia has been a bit of a blessing in disguise for me.”

Hail said, “People are so quick to jump to spread hate about something they don’t understand. And maybe this world would be a better place if we spent less time judging people based upon our differences and spent more time appreciating what makes somebody who they are.”

“We should not be apologizing for being different. We should be celebrating it. We should be kind to the people that don’t look like us, and we should be compassionate towards the people whose struggles we maybe don’t understand,” she continued. “And most importantly, we should stop living our lives trying to meet somebody else’s definition of perfection because nobody is a better you than you are.”

Hail went on to address her current storyline rival, Chantel Monroe, who has mocked her alopecia on WWE NXT in recent weeks.

“I refuse to let your cruelty make me ashamed of who I am,” she told her nemesis. “I’ve spent my entire career proving that I don’t stay down and this is no different. So with or without hair, I’m Thea Hail.”

“And in a world where nobody knows what’s real or not, this is,” Hail concluded, proceeding to shave her head in the middle of the wrestling ring.

In a video shared via the WWE NXT Instagram account, Hail was seen addressing the camera as she left the ring, describing herself as “bald and beautiful.”

In the clip, she was then embraced by WWE Hall of Famer Shawn Michaels, who oversees NXT through his role as Senior Vice President of Talent Development, Creative at WWE.

Michaels, 61, heaped praise on Hail for her act of courage in his own Instagram tribute, too.

“There are moments in this business that go far beyond what happens inside the ring,” he captioned a video of the emotional moment. “The courage and strength Thea showed tonight is something truly special. Sometimes, the bravest thing you can do is embrace change and face it head-on.”

He concluded, “Couldn’t be prouder of you, Thea. ❤️”
