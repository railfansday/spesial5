New GTA 6 gameplay is so realistic fans can’t believe it’s not real life

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

A new GTA 6 gameplay clip has left fans stunned by how realistic it looks, with some struggling to believe they’re actually watching a game.

With Rockstar’s highly anticipated sequel finally upon us, players have been eagerly awaiting a closer look at the game in action.

While previous trailers have showcased the stunning visuals of Vice City and the surrounding state of Leonida, a new 15-second clip has taken things to another level. In fact, the footage is so convincing that some viewers initially mistook it for a real video filmed on the streets of Miami.

On November 11, popular Miami-based social media account Only in Dade shared a short first-person video showing several customized cars parked along a busy street, with pedestrians walking past. It recreates an actual video from West Coast Customs’ crossover with GTA 6.

At first glance, it looks like any other video filmed in Miami, but the entire scene was actually recreated inside GTA 6. Rockstar subsequently shared the footage on its official Instagram story, while Only in Dade also confirmed that it was genuine gameplay.

Miami is officially a simulation. #OnlyinViceCity pic.twitter.com/wfJhm0frs3

Since the clip was shared, fans have been responding in disbelief, saying they thought the footage was either real life or AI-generated.

“OMG I looked at this 100x and I still can’t believe this is in-game. HOW ON EARTH is this possible,” said one reply.

“I genuinely thought this was real life dawg wtf,” said another, while a third added: “BRO! I legitimately thought it was AI or IRL as well!”

Some also joked that concerns about the game being limited to 30 FPS on console have been laid to rest.
