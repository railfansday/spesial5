Ateez Debuts at No. 1 on Billboard 200 for the Third Time This Year; U2’s ‘Carnaval de Luz’ Bows at No. 4

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Senior Music Writer and Chief Music Critic

The K-pop group Ateez continued its string of chart successes with the No. 1 bow on the Billboard 200 album chart of “Golden Hour: Part 6.” It’s the third time in 2026 that the group has had an EP debut in the top spot; the last such occasion came in July, when they premiered at No. 1 with (you guessed it) “Golden Hour: Part 5,” and they did it with “Part 4” before that in February.

U2 had the second-biggest premiere of the week, with the band’s first full album of original material in nine years, “Carnaval de Lux,” bowing at No. 4. The previous Ateez EP debuted with 228,000 equivalent album units, and this new “Part 6” followup didn’t follow terribly far behind, with 195,000 units. Billboard reported that the tally was almost entirely sales-driven, with individual copies that were sold amounting to 191,000 of the 195,000 total. A big factor in the massive physical sales, as is typical for K-pop, was the availability of substantial variants designed to encourage multiple sales among fans, with different collectible items in each package — 32 such vinyl or CD variations altogether, Billboard said.

With this latest chart-topper, Ateez became the first group to have 10 albums or EPs in the top 10 in the 2020s. Only two very prolific solo artists have managed more this decade: YoungBoy Never Broke Again with 15 and Taylor Swift with 14.

U2’s bow, like Ateez’s, was also far more dependent on sales than streaming, although there were likely not as many of their fans buying every version they could.. “Carnaval de Luz” debuted with 49,000 equivalent album units; full album sales accounted for 42,500 of those units. There were 18 different physical variants for the U2 album, per Billboard.

Among the eight holdovers in the top 10, Olivia Rodrigo’s “You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love” was tops, falling one rung to No. 2. With 62,000 units for the week, the blockbuster album was down by only 1%.

Rounding out the top 10 were Morgan Wallen’s “I’m the Problem” at No. 3; Noah Kahan’s “The Great Divide” and “Stick Season” at Nos. 5 and 7, respectively; Ella Langley’s CMA-winning “Dandelion” at No. 6; SZA’s “SOS” at No. 8; Olivia Dean’s “The Art of Loving” at No. 9; and Lil Baby’s “Fresh,” dropping eight spots to No. 10 in its second week.
