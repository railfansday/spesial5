Searchlight Pictures Casting Head Yesi Ramirez Leaving Division Amid Restructuring

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The ‘A Complete Unknown’ and ‘Moonlight’ casting director said in a statement that she will return to casting independently.

Yesi Ramirez, the head of casting for Searchlight Pictures, is departing the indie and specialty division after roughly six years.

The move comes amid a restructuring in the casting department at the Disney-owned division, which this year released films including Wild Horse Nine and Ready or Not 2: Here I Come, with Behemoth! and The Families Stone on the way.

In a statement, Ramirez said she would return to the world of independent casting after leaving the company. “I’ll genuinely miss working alongside my Searchlight talented colleagues, many of whom have become dear friends. At the same time, I’m excited to return to independent casting, get back in the trenches with filmmakers, and be hands-on in discovering more talent and being creative,” she said. “It’s a part of the work I’ve always loved, and I’m excited to get back to it.” Related Stories Movies 'The Odyssey' to Receive Ensemble Performance Award at Palm Springs International Film Festival (Exclusive) Music Searchlight Pictures Enters Merch Partnership With Universal Music's Bravado (Exclusive)

At Searchlight, Ramirez oversaw the selection of actors on projects spanning the Oscar winner The Eyes of Tammy Faye as well as The Menu, Fire Island, Ready or Not and Ready or Not 2, Rental Family and Flamin’ Hot. She personally cast the Bob Dylan biopic A Complete Unknown, advocating in particular for Monica Barbaro to play Joan Baez — a role for which Barbaro later earned an Oscar nomination.

Ramirez also personally cast the titles Chevalier, See How They Run and The Families Stone.

Prior to Disney purchasing 20th Century Fox, Ramirez led casting for three different film banners at the company and served as the casting director on The Hate U Give and The Art of Racing in the Rain.

Across her 20-year career in casting, Ramirez has worked on projects as varied as Blood Diamond, 21 Jump Street and Magic Mike XXL. She notably cast Barry Jenkins’ Oscar winner Moonlight, which earned her a 2017 Artios Award from Casting Society.
