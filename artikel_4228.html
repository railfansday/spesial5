The Game Awards Reveals Its Six Nominees For Game Of The Year, Including GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

With The Game Awards fast approaching on December 10th, the official nominees have been announced today for every single category, including for the big Game of the Year award that takes the main spotlight.

The headline news is that GTA 6 is in the running for that award, even though it hasn't released yet. Nominees for The Game Awards are selected by "a voting jury of over 100 leading media and influencer outlets across the globe", so clearly they think it's worth being in the discussion for GOTY. To be fair, it's a probably a no-brainer.

Here's the full list of nominees for Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2026:

Elsewhere, GTA 6 is in the running for multiple other awards on December 10th, including Best Performance, Best Narrative, Best Action Adventure Game, Best Score & Music and Best Audio Design. It was even revealed in the nominees that Manni L Perez is part of the cast for GTA 6, which hadn't been confirmed until today.

As for Xbox's first-party studios, Fable is up for Most Anticipated Game, Fallout is up for Best Adaptation, Minecraft is up for Best User Creation, Forza Horizon 6 is up for Innovation in Accessibility and Best Sports/Racing Game, Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 and Gears of War: E-Day are up for Best Multiplayer Game, and Gears of War: E-Day is also up for Best Action Game and Innovation in Accessibility.

You can check out the full video where Geoff Keighley runs through each nominee for all the awards down below, and rest assured we'll have complete coverage of the event on December 10th, with plenty of surprises expected for it!
