Poppy Playtime Chapter Five takes you into the depths of cute terror as it launches today

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Like it or loathe it, for many younger horror fans, Poppy Playtime trumps the likes of Resident Evil, Alien Isolation and even Five Nights at Freddy's in their estimation. Case in point: the seismic reaction to Poppy Playtime Chapter Five, which sold 110,000 copies in only three hours on PC, and which has now finally landed on iOS and Android!

If you're not familiar, Poppy Playtime sees you delving into the ruined toy factory of Playtime Co., and unsurprisingly the enormous, spooky abandoned industrial relic turns out to be hosting a lot more than just asbestos and still water, with the vicious Huggy Wuggy (yes, really) dogging your steps as you delve further, and further, into the depths of the factory.

And as you might expect, your trusty GrabPack is also getting new tools to help you out in navigating the tricky new environmental puzzles. All of which you'll need to use to their utmost to avoid an untimely end.

While I may not necessarily get the appeal of Poppy Playtime, I can certainly respect how successful it's been for developer Mob Entertainment. And I'm sure that a few of you are eager to jump into Poppy Playtime Chapter Five on iOS and Android!
