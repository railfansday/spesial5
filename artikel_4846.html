The Best Black Friday Activewear Deals to Shop Right Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Score up to 75% off workout clothes and everyday athleisure from lululemon, Alo, Gymshark, and more.

Women's Health may earn commission from the links on this page, but we only feature products we believe in. Why Trust Us?

If there's one category I can never resist shopping, it's athleisure and activewear. I'm always on the hunt for a new pair of leggings, a supportive sports bra, or a matching workout set that makes me feel just a little more motivated to hit the gym (even on the days I'd rather stay on the couch). As a style writer who's spent years testing workout clothes and covering Black Friday sales, I've learned which brands are worth the splurge and, more importantly, when you can actually score a good deal on them. And with Black Friday here, some of my favorite workout brands are offering savings worth checking out.

Whether you're loyal to lululemon leggings, love Gymshark's gym-ready sets, or prefer Alo's studio-to-street staples, there are plenty of opportunities to refresh your workout wardrobe without paying full price. I'm rounding up the best Black Friday activewear sales from brands like Popflex, Rhone, and more, along with the pieces I'd actually add to my own cart. Happy shopping!

lululemon’s “We Made Too Much” section is stacked with deals on some of my favorite styles for Black Friday. Lots of pieces range from 30 to 40% off in select colorways, but you can even find options for over half off—like the Align Dress (which is one of our favorite travel dresses), which is nearly 60% off before it sells out.

lululemon Hotty Hot High-Rise Lined Short

Rhone is one of my favorite spots for activewear (I love the Revive legging!) and athleisure. The label’s Black Friday sale started today, November 23, and you can take 25% off virtually the entire site. I suggest snagging your faves now before they sell out, but if you wait for the Cyber Monday sale (which begins Sunday, November 29), you can take up to 50% off select styles.

I only tried our Popflex workout clothes recently, and apparently I’ve been living under a rock all these years. The brand has the cutest styles for not only activewear but athleisure too. Good news: Popflex’s Black Friday sale runs November 23 through November 30 at midnight, with sitewide discounts of up to 75%—and no code required! Plus, my fave matching workout set: the Crisscross Hourglass Booty Short and It Girl Bra are included in the sale.

Through December 30, Gymshark is offering up to 50% off their workout clothes and loungewear, including editor-favorite sports bras, leggings, sweatpants, and hoodies. The best part? There’s no code required, and as of right now, plenty of popular colors and sizes are still in stock—but I bet that won’t last long.

Gymshark Cosy Luxe Straight Leg Regular Sweatpants

Alo’s Black Friday sale this year includes 30% off sitewide—starting now. Yep, you read that right: sitewide. That includes our favorite workout leggings from the brand (the 7/8 High-Waist Airbrush Legging) as well as our go-to stylish sports bra (the Airlift Suit Up Bra).

Spanx’s Black Friday sale kicked off on November 18 and will run through December 1 with discounts between 30% and 50% off sitewide. Plus, a gift with purchase (AirEssentials Blanket) with purchases of $350 or more, and an extra 30% off markdowns from November 29 through December 1, automatically applied at checkout with code EXTRA30.
