Ripple Veteran David Schwartz Draws the Line With XRP Spammers

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Ripple CTO Emeritus and renowned XRP legend, David Schwartz has expressed displeasure while issuing a strict warning to members of the XRP community over growing conduct he finds unacceptable.

In a post shared earlier today, Schwartz revealed plans to block most XRP users over the growing number of irrelevant replies and unsolicited promotions recently flooding his posts on X.

Schwartz's warning came after he explained that he has recently noticed a growing pattern of irrelevant crypto-related comments on his posts on X.

He mentioned that there has been a substantial increase in users, especially from the XRP community, responding to his comments on unrelated posts with insults, accusations, and crypto-related messages.

According to him, the behavior has become disruptive enough that he now has to block some of those accounts, as he explained that the replies not only affect his interactions but also inconvenience other X users whose posts he engages with.

Schwartz considers this conduct very concerning, as flooding unrelated conversations with XRP content or personal attacks can force the people he is responding to to block him to prevent their followers from receiving irrelevant notifications.

In his post, Schwartz further spotlighted the increasing fraud allegations he has continued to receive on his unrelated posts, revealing plans to take action on that as well.

He mentioned that he will block the accounts of users who follow him across X to accuse him of fraud or scamming, whether their comments are positive or negative.

While expressing his displeasure, Schwartz warned that he would not tolerate people using his participation in other users' discussions as an opportunity to promote XRP or attack him.
