XRP Price Prediction: $1 Wall Breaks, What’s Next?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The CryptoNews editorial team is composed of seasoned writers specializing in cryptocurrency and blockchain technology. Their expertise ensures comprehensive, accurate, and insightful content for...

XRP price has lost the $1 mark, and buyers are running out of powder to defend the breakdown. The token floats at $0.95, extending a painful decline that has erased 28% of its value over the past month. XRP has now closed below $1 for six consecutive sessions, turning a psychological support level into an immediate resistance.

The next important test sits around $0.89, where buyers previously stepped in during November’s sell-off. A break below that level could expose $0.80, leaving XRP vulnerable to another leg lower.

Institutional demand has offered little immediate relief. U.S. spot XRP ETFs recorded $19.13 million in weekly net outflows through November 13, their largest weekly withdrawal in eight months. The selling has since cooled, with ETF flows showing no net movement on November 18 and 19. Cumulative inflows remain around $1.79 billion.

CryptoQuant data shows Binance’s XRP Supply Shortage Index climbing to 2.4 on November 19, its highest reading since July. Whale withdrawals reportedly exceeded $127 million since November 10.

Exchange supply is tightening, but that hasn’t translated into buying pressure; sellers are continuing to control price action.

XRP isn’t the only project betting on blockchain utility to attract the next wave of capital. Bitcoin Hyper ($HYPER) is taking a different route, building an SVM-powered Layer 2 designed to bring faster transactions, lower fees, and smart contracts to Bitcoin.

The idea is to unlock Bitcoin-linked liquidity for DeFi, decentralized applications, and potentially tokenized assets without relying on Bitcoin’s slower base-layer execution. Its testnet wallet, bridge, and explorer are already available for community testing.

The presale has raised more than $33.2 million, with HYPER priced at just $0.0136877 and advertised staking rewards of 35% APY. November 26 is the planned token launch and presale deadline, leaving little time before the current entry window closes.

XRP’s daily Relative Strength Index has fallen below 30, placing the token firmly in oversold territory. However, that alone won’t stop the downtrend.

The immediate support zone sits between $0.94 and $0.95. Losing this area would put the November 14 low near $0.89 back in focus.

A decisive break below $0.89 could open the door toward $0.80.

On the upside, XRP needs to reclaim $0.985 before challenging the psychological $1 barrier again. A sustained move above $1 would give buyers their first meaningful opportunity to reverse the breakdown.

For now, the bearish structure remains intact. XRP needs more than an oversold bounce to convince buyers that the worst is over.
