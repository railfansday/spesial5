XRP Price at the $1 Line: Should You Sell or Add More to Your Bag?

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

XRP is dangerously close to losing the $1 price mark, putting holders in an uncomfortable position. Some holders may see another buying opportunity, while others are wondering if it is time to cut their losses.

The token briefly slipped below $1 this morning, hitting an intraday low close to $0.99 before recovering. XRP subsequently moved to $1.02, leaving its most important psychological support level under pressure.

The selloff has become harder to ignore. XRP has fallen by 37% from its October highs of $1.60, while Bitcoin’s decline has dragged the wider crypto market lower.

For those who bought XRP above $1.30, the current price is making for a difficult decision. Selling now locks in losses, but holding through another breakdown could mean enduring an even deeper correction.

Is XRP approaching an attractive accumulation zone or simply preparing for another leg down?

The $1 level has always been a psychological support that can influence how investors behave. A clean breakdown could trigger stop-loss orders, liquidations, and additional selling from nervous holders. There is also a technical reason to watch this area closely.

XRP previously found support around $0.985 in August. That level is now an important line for buyers attempting to prevent a new 2026 low. Meanwhile, the technical indicators are showing considerable weakness. XRP’s daily RSI recently dropped to 21.5, placing it deep inside oversold territory. Its MACD also remains bearish, suggesting sellers still control short-term momentum.

Retail investors may be increasingly uncomfortable holding XRP through another breakdown, particularly those who entered during the summer rally. Larger holders, meanwhile, could view the correction as an opportunity to accumulate at substantially lower prices.

Exchange balances, whale wallet activity, and large transaction volumes are important indicators to monitor. If large holders begin accumulating while retail investors distribute their tokens, XRP could be approaching a meaningful change in market positioning.

But if selling pressure spreads across both groups, the $1 level could become difficult to defend. But price action is not the only factor XRP investors should consider.

Ripple continues positioning its ecosystem around institutional payments, financial infrastructure, and stablecoin adoption. Its ambitions could eventually strengthen the long-term investment case surrounding XRP.

While Ripple continues pursuing institutional adoption, another project is betting on a different combination: Bitcoin’s liquidity and Solana-style execution.

Bitcoin Hyper is developing a Bitcoin Layer 2 network that integrates the Solana Virtual Machine (SVM) for faster transactions and smart contract execution. Its architecture aims to combine Bitcoin settlement with high-speed execution, potentially opening the door to DeFi applications and institutional partnerships.

The proposition is interesting: Bitcoin provides the underlying liquidity narrative, while SVM technology aims to deliver the speed needed for more sophisticated financial applications.

The project has raised more than $33 million, showing considerable early fundraising momentum. It also offers 35% APY staking rewards, another incentive for early participants. Holders can stake their tokens and accumulate additional HYPER rather than simply leaving their allocation idle.

The project’s November 26, 2026 presale conclusion and token launch deadline is approaching quickly, potentially creating additional FOMO among early participants. Bitcoin Hyper is another project entering the conversation as the market searches for faster and more scalable blockchain networks.

The next few trading sessions could determine whether XRP stages a recovery or extends its correction. A bullish scenario requires buyers to reclaim $1.10, followed by a stronger move above $1.17. That would suggest selling pressure is finally weakening.

The neutral scenario would involve XRP holding between $1 and $1.10 while investors wait for a clearer market direction. The bearish scenario is more concerning. A sustained daily close below $0.985 could expose XRP to another decline toward $0.90, potentially creating fresh selling pressure.

For now, the XRP price outlook remains fragile. Oversold conditions offer some hope for a relief rally, but buyers need to demonstrate conviction.

Those betting on Ripple’s long-term institutional ambitions may see the current correction as an opportunity. Short-term traders, however, have good reason to wait for confirmation that $1 support can survive.
