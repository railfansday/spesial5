XRP traded level with Ripple's RLUSD stablecoin on Wednesday, Nov. 18, for the first time in five days. On Binance, XRP touched $1.0078 at one point before it pulled back to around $0.9878.

Former Ripple adviser Antony Welfare joked about the "dollar peg" in a post on X. "Wohooo! We did it, XRP reclaimed the $1 level. Welcome $XRP to the One dollar peg world. Now feel free to move on past the peg and onto the 10x peg," he wrote.

XRP gained 4% to 4.5% over 24 hours and recovered about 12% from its local bottom. On Nov. 13 and Nov. 14, a wave of liquidations hit the crypto market. XRP broke below the $1 support level and fell to between $0.893 and $0.91 by the weekend. It was the lowest level for the token since November 2024.

XRP still trades more than 70% below its 2025 peaks, and Ripple's RLUSD stablecoin held at $1 during the whole drop. In late October, RLUSD received an investment-grade credit rating from Moody's, and it was listed on Coinbase and Robinhood.

At the Swell conference in late October, Ripple reported more than 1,700 institutional clients, and the company said that it expected to reach an annual product revenue run rate of about $900 million by the end of 2026. This fall, Ripple also received final approval from the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) to launch Ripple National Trust Bank. Besides, the company plans to start institutional lending against XRP collateral in 2027.

Whales have pulled more than $127 million worth of XRP off Binance since Nov. 10, at a pace of $12 million to $33 million a day, according to CryptoQuant data.

U.S. spot XRP ETFs saw $19.13 million in outflows last week and no flows on Monday, according to SoSoValue. On Tuesday, Nov. 17, the funds lost another $6.14 million. Canary's XRPC and Grayscale's GXRP accounted for $5.01 million and $1.12 million of that amount, respectively. Total assets under management dropped to $1.08 billion.
