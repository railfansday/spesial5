While the broader crypto market struggles to find direction, the Pieverse price has taken a different path, extending its rally since October 30. The token has climbed from around $1.00 to $1.66, with its latest daily session up more than 15%. The sustained advance has drawn attention to a relatively lesser-known project operating at the intersection of Web3 payments and the emerging AI-agent economy.

However, the rally raises an important question: what is driving PIEVERSE higher when much of the market remains under pressure?

PIEVERSE is showing strong bullish momentum on the daily chart, with the token trading at $1.6666 after gaining 15.49% in the latest session. The recovery has pushed the price above the 0.618 Fibonacci level at $1.4303, bringing the 0.786 level at $1.7068 into immediate focus. A decisive move above this resistance could open the way towards the $2.0589 level, followed by the extended upside target at $2.5065.

However, the RSI climbed to 82.15, moving well above the 70 threshold and indicating overbought conditions. Although this reflects strong buying pressure, it also increases the possibility of profit-taking or a short-term correction. On the downside, $1.4303 is the first important support to watch, followed by the 0.5 Fibonacci level at $1.2362. Holding above these levels would help preserve the recent recovery structure. For now, bulls need to overcome $1.7068 to sustain the rally, while the elevated RSI suggests that further upside may not come without periods of consolidation.

PIEVERSE’s latest rally is accompanied by a sharp increase in derivatives exposure, with aggregated open interest climbing to $37.49 million. The surge suggests that traders are adding positions as the token advances, potentially amplifying price volatility. Funding rates have also turned positive at 0.0198, indicating that bullish positioning is gaining traction in the perpetual futures market.

However, the CVD readings reveal a more complicated picture. Futures CVD remains deeply negative despite a slight recovery in its latest reading. In contrast, spot CVD has steadily climbed towards ~$3.66 million, suggesting that spot buying pressure has been improving. This divergence indicates that the recent rally is not being supported equally across spot and derivatives markets.

Liquidation data also shows recent activity on both sides, with the latest displayed readings at approximately $123,608 and $26,825. While this reflects increased volatility around the recent advance, it does not yet establish a sustained liquidation-driven short squeeze.

For now, the combination of rising open interest, positive funding and improving spot CVD supports the bullish price action. However, if open interest continues expanding while funding remains elevated, the market could become vulnerable to a leveraged long unwind if PIEVERSE fails to sustain its upward momentum.

Another factor that could influence PIEVERSE’s rally is its scheduled token unlock on November 14. Approximately 33.4 million tokens are expected to enter circulation, representing around 3.3% of the total supply. At the prevailing prices used in earlier estimates, the allocation was valued at roughly $32.5 million.

The additional supply could create selling pressure, particularly after the token’s sharp advance since October 30. Investors and early participants may look to realise profits, potentially increasing volatility around the unlock date. However, the impact will depend on the allocation recipients and whether they decide to sell or retain their tokens.

For now, the upcoming unlock adds another uncertainty to PIEVERSE’s bullish momentum. With the RSI already in overbought territory and derivatives exposure expanding, the market’s ability to absorb additional supply could become an important test of the rally’s strength.

PIEVERSE price has demonstrated considerable strength despite uncertainty across the broader crypto market, supported by rising open interest, positive funding rates and improving spot buying pressure. However, the rally is also showing signs of overheating, with the daily RSI above 80 and leveraged exposure increasing.

The $1.7068 Fibonacci level is the immediate hurdle for buyers. A decisive breakout could extend the recovery towards $2.0589, while a rejection may bring $1.4303 and $1.2362 back into focus. The November 14 token unlock adds another factor that could influence market sentiment.
