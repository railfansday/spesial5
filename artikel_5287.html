Peter Brandt Predicts More Pain for Bitcoin

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Veteran commodity trader Peter Brandt has provided yet another bearish outlook for the world's largest cryptocurrency, arguing that the crypto asset could see much more misery if its historical market trends play out.

Brandt's latest doomsday prediction comes at a time when Bitcoin is still trying to recover from its biggest sell-off to date, which has wiped out close to half of its market value from its October 2025 peak.

The trader's latest post on X highlights a pattern he has found throughout Bitcoin's price history: previous bull runs have always ended with devastating market corrections.

"Every bull market phase in the history of BTC has ended with at least a 75% correction of the prior parabolic bull trend," Brandt's X post reads.

Continuing his warning, Brandt raised an alarming question: "Is this what is happening with Bitcoin?"

The trading veteran has not put a number on the possible crash. But his words point toward the possibility that the current drop in Bitcoin might be part of a larger correction and not just a temporary dip.

A look at Bitcoin's historical crashes could paint a scary picture.

Bitcoin has a history of sharp corrections following major bull runs.

For example, the crypto asset dropped by around 84% from its December 2017 high to its December 2018 low. Another example of such a deep price correction can be found in the 2022 bear market, when BTC saw a drop of around 77% from its November 2021 all-time high.

These historic crashes have shown the extreme losses that Bitcoin investors have faced during such market corrections.

At the time of writing on Nov. 20, Bitcoin is currently trading at around $62,000, which is almost 51% below its October 2025 all-time high.

If it were to undergo a 75% price correction from its all-time high, then it would drop to around $31,550.

But it's worth mentioning that Brandt referred to a correction of the prior parabolic bull trend, which may not be the same as a 75% correction from the peak.

Interestingly enough, this is not the first time the trader has warned about an even darker future for Bitcoin's price.

Earlier, on Nov. 15, Brandt shared a weekly BTC chart showing a potential head-and-shoulders formation, a technical pattern that usually signals a bearish reversal.

According to Brandt, if the pattern is confirmed, there's a chance that Bitcoin could never revisit its previous all-time high in his lifetime.

The chart he shared also indicates an important zone between $60,000 and $65,000. A confirmed breakdown below this zone could further strengthen the bearish outlook.

Moreover, there is still plenty of selling pressure in the crypto market. The latest update from Lookonchain on Nov. 19 revealed that U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs had seen net outflows of around $975 million over the past seven days. The biggest net outflows came from BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT).
