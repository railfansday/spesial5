Hollow Knight: Silksong DLC News Is Coming This Week In A Team Cherry 'Special Presentation'

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

You're damn right it is! Team Cherry has announced that it'll be hosting a 'Cherry Time!' special presentation this week on 27th November at 3pm GMT / 7am PT / 10am ET, where we can expect an update on the game's upcoming Sea of Sorrow DLC and a bunch of other "exciting announcements".

The event will be streamed on the Team Cherry YouTube channel. You can find the precise time it will get underway in your region below:

Join us in 48 hours for Cherry Time! Get ready for a bunch of exciting announcements, plus an update on Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow! Premiering here: youtu.be/M-QUgdcBpZU

The free expansion was teased late last year with a 2026 release window attached to it. We still don't have a firm release date attached to it at the time of writing, so we'd expect to either hear about one (or a delay) in this week's presentation.
