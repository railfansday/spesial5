The 3 summer targets Blackburn Rovers should pursue again in January, including Balazs Toth replacement

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Blackburn Rovers have exceeded the expectations of many in the early stages of the 2026/27 campaign. Tony Mowbray‘s side sit 13th in the Championship after 15 games, when many expected them to be in the thick of a relegation battle.

That fate could yet await with a lot of the season left to play, but spirits are fairly high at Ewood Park. There is no hiding that the Rovers squad needs work though, with only three new summer signings failing to suitably reinforce the ranks.

New targets will be on the radar, but Blackburn Rovers identified some smart options in the summer who could be available this winter. Here, we put forward three summer targets who should be back on the radar ahead of January…

Balazs Toth has continued to resist Blackburn Rovers’ new contract efforts. It remains to be seen exactly when he leaves, but a departure seemingly awaits at some point in 2027, and the club could do with sourcing a replacement sooner rather than later.

Alex Palmer was linked with a move to Ewood Park in the last transfer window, and he could be a fine option to consider to replace Toth. The 30-year-old is out of favour at Ipswich Town under Gary O’Neil, but he is a proven Championship goalkeeper who could quickly become a dependable No. 1 for Mowbray.

Palmer is under contract until 2028 at Portman Road but given that he has not made a Premier League matchday squad this season, the Tractor Boys should not have an issue letting him go for a fairly cheap fee.

Blackburn Rovers have one of Europe’s most clinical strikers in Andri Gudjohnsen at the moment, but competition beyond him is limited. Yuki Ohashi is the only other out-and-out centre-forward, so more depth would not go amiss.

Daniel Jebbison was targeted by Rovers in the summer and a loan-to-buy deal was even agreed, but he ended up staying at Bournemouth. Given the fact that he has played only four times so far this season, the 23-year-old would benefit from a winter move elsewhere to find more game time.

The Canadian striker has Championship experience and has strong physical traits like Gudjohnsen. Jebbison is a strong finisher on both feet and Mowbray’s style of play and ability to develop players could help him hit prolific form.

Last but not least is a much-needed midfield reinforcement. Mowbray has expressed a desire to sign a new central midfielder in the winter, and someone who offers experience but has more years ahead of Sam Morsy and Adam Forshaw would be ideal.

Alan Browne fits this profile perfectly. The 31-year-old has played over 400 Championship games but showed on loan at Middlesbrough last season that he still has plenty in the tank, offering a well-rounded skillset, versatility and energy in the middle of the park.

Browne is way down the pecking order at Sunderland and out of contract next summer. It should be an easy deal for Blackburn Rovers to strike and he would instantly improve their options in midfield.
