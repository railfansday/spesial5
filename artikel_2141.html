Exclusive: Birmingham City & Middlesbrough plot transfer that will interest Leeds United

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Birmingham City and Middlesbrough are both firmly in the hunt for automatic promotion to the Premier League and the Championship pair could be poised to enter battle away from the pitch, too, as Football League World can exclusively reveal that they are planning to rival each other for a Ligue 1 star with Lorient in a development that will interest Leeds United.

Both Birmingham and Middlesbrough have emerged among the early pace-setters in the opening months of the 2026/27 Championship campaign, which is precisely why the duo occupy the prised two automatic promotion places heading into the November international break.

Birmingham have taken significant strides following a mixed return to life in the second-tier last term, and Chris Davies' side are enjoying full-throttle form with six victories from their last seven outings to soar to the summit of the table.

Middlesbrough, meanwhile, are back with a vengeance after losing last season's play-off final in heartbreaking last-grasp fashion to Hull City, and the Teessiders appear even stronger from offsetting Hayden Hackney's big-money departure for Everton with a host of exciting additions that have helped the club to second-place at this moment in time despite mixed recent form in the injury-enforced absence of 13-goal hitman Will Lankshear.

The two Championship promotion-chasers are preparing to attack the January transfer window with extra injections of quality to help their respective efforts at getting over the line back to the Premier League once May rolls around and, in a new twist that may just interest Leeds United in the top-flight already, a fascinating battle could soon take place for a starlet out of Ligue 1.

Plans for the winter window are already heading underway in the boardrooms of Birmingham and Middlesbrough, and Football League World has learned of an exciting and highly-rated target on the radar of the second-tier's two strongest sides so far this season.

FLW can exclusively disclose that Blues and Boro are plotting moves for Lorient sensation Arthur Avom, who has emerged as one of Ligue 1's most promising young midfield talents in recent years.

Sources close to FLW have revealed that the two outfits are poised to rival each other in a bid to land Avom's sought-after signature, with the midfielder's current contract in Brittany due to expire at the end of the current campaign.

Still aged just 21, Avom would provide esteemed pedigree after already racking up close to 80 appearances for Lorient since debuting back in the 2024/25 campaign when he helped Les Merlus achieve promotion back to the top-flight.

He's now in his third straight season as a first-team regular and has started seven of his 10 league appearances under Alexandre Dujeux this term.

Avom has also made an impression on the international stage with his native Cameroon, earning 17 caps to date and representing the Indomitable Lions at the African Cup of Nations last year.

Ligue 1 is a hotbed for elite-level talent across the European footballing landscape and, with Avom having cemented his prestigious reputation among the division's leading midfielders within his age bracket, it's no surprise that the Cameroon international is earning an ever-increasing litany of suitors ahead of the expiration of his contract.

A previous report published by Africa Foot at the end of the summer window claimed interest from numerous clubs in England and beyond, as the likes of Leeds United, Everton, Crystal Palace and Sunderland were all linked with his services alongside Club Brugge, Fenerache and fellow Ligue 1 outfit Strasbourg.

Indeed, that report indicated an asking price worth £17.1 million, which would likely prevent either Birmingham or Middlesbrough from completing a move.

However, the important detail is that months have passed without any resolution to Avom's contractual situation after Lorient exercised a one-year extension option in order to prevent losing the midfielder on a free transfer, and as he edges ever closer to the conclusion of his terms, the French outfit will need to reluctantly lower their demands.

Should they fail to, of course, there is every chance that Avom could end up leaving for nothing.

Lorient, with an esteemed history of developing and selling on talent, are only sure to know better, and it may just turn out that Birmingham and Middlesbrough are holding out for a valuation reduction that could enable either of the ambitious second-tier pair to steal a march on even bigger clubs and land Avom under the nose of various Premier League admirers.

Make no mistake about it, Avom's existing body of work and pedigree, coupled with the sort of footballing ceiling and future potential to earn the eye of some of Europe's premiere talent-developers, means that he would represent an unprecedented coup to land in the second-tier.

The list of Premier League admirers in the defensive midfielder's wake appears to be little secret, and while that's a factor capable of working against Blues or Middlesbrough, they would have the opportunity to nurture and afford instant regular minutes to the Cameroonian before potentially taking him to the top-flight once the season wraps up.

Of course, whether Avom himself would be willing to drop to second-tier football after earning his stripes at such a young age in Ligue 1 remains open to debate and will likely be determined in due course, but either way, it's a telling reflection of the immediate and longer-term ambitions in equal measure held within these two particular clubs and that should serve to excite both sets of supporters.

A dynamic ball-winning midfielder with relentless energy and the ability to both break up and initiate play forward with powerful and progressive upfield carries, Avom's skillset would seamlessly translate to any Championship engine room and has unsurprisingly caught the attention of clubs throughout the continent.
