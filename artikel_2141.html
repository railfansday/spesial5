Four goals in four games: £8m ex-Middlesbrough man’s fine form fails to save new club from dramatic collapse

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Middlesbrough had a busy summer transfer window as they prepared for the 2026/27 campaign. Boro struck big-money deals for the likes of Will Lankshear, Radek Vitek and Ashley Phillips in their hunt for redemption after Championship play-off final heartbreak in May.

However, there were departures from the Riverside Stadium too; some more surprising than others. Among the moves to raise eyebrows across the EFL was Morgan Whittaker’s sale to Colorado Rapids for a reported fee of around £8m.

The 25-year-old scored 14 goals and provided seven assists in 40 Championship games last season. As such, it was a little surprising to see him move for a fee barely over the £7.5m package sum paid to sign him from Plymouth Argyle in January 2025.

Whittaker quickly caught the eye in the United States. In his first MLS start back in September, the former Middlesbrough man netted a brace in Colorado Rapids’ 3-3 draw against Seattle Sounders.

He would then go four games without a goal before really hitting his stride in October. Whittaker would score in four consecutive games against Vancouver Whitecaps, San Jose Earthquakes, Chicago Fire and FC Dallas to take him to six goals in 10 league games for the Rapids.

Unfortunately, despite Whittaker’s form, the side desperately struggled for results. Of those 10 appearances, Colorado only won twice, and it ultimately cost them a place in the MLS play-offs.

They sat in 8th on the final day, which would have been enough to qualify. However, Whittaker’s goal against Dallas was his side’s only one in a 3-1 defeat, seeing Colorado slip to 13th and completely out of play-off contention.

It marked the second time in consecutive years that the Rapids have missed out on the play-offs on the final day.

It was a surprise to see Whittaker swap Middlesbrough for Colorado Rapids, especially given the finances of the deal weren’t too eye-catching. However, it marked an exciting opportunity for the forward to go and play in the MLS, and his goalscoring qualities have certainly been on show.

At 25, he still has plenty of time to develop and reach higher levels too. Impressive form in America combined with his strong Championship track record means Whittaker could certainly earn a switch to a bigger league in the years to come.

It would not be a surprise to see Whittaker plying his trade on English shores again. For now though, he’ll take time to rest up in the MLS off-season before attacking the 2027 campaign with a vengeance.
