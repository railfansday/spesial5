Neil Warnock reacts to James Bond announcement in hilarious way

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Former Queens Park Rangers and Sheffield United manager Neil Warnock has reacted to Robert Pattinson being cast as the new James Bond in a hilarious social media post.

Neil Warnock and Steve Bruce have the most promotions from the second tier to the top flight, while Warnock has eight promotions across all divisions. That places the 77-year-old with the outright record in English football history.

However, he has not managed in the EFL since Huddersfield Town in 2023, with short stints at Aberdeen and Torquay United recently. He instead was eyeing a different role away from football following a tongue-in-cheek social media post.

Daniel Craig played James Bond for 15 years between his first film appearance in 2006 and his final film in 2021, starring in five movies. However, for years now, there has been speculation over who would replace him.

That has been filled by Robert Pattinson, but former Queens Park Rangers and Sheffield United boss Warnock has reacted to the decision. Firstly, he wrote: "You’ve got to live and let die for 3 points."

Another response was to promote his new range of socks, with Warnock also stating: "The names Warnock…Neil Warnock: Chief of Socks 🧦"

The 25 top scorers in the Championship since the second tier was rebranded back in 2004/05, including Billy Sharp and Aleksandar Mitrovic.

However, the most hilarious response from Warnock included an edited version of Pattinson's announcement post, which also said: "I told them at the audition that I was only available until the end of the season. Never heard back 🤷‍♂️"

Much like 007, Warnock spent a career taking on impossible missions and invariably walking away with the job done. Armed not with a Walther PPK, but a sharp tongue and a well-worn clipboard, English football’s ultimate troubleshooter carved out a record-breaking legacy across four decades in the dugout.

With a staggering eight career promotions, Warnock repeatedly turned relegation-threatened strugglers into overachievers. From Notts County and Sheffield United, to QPR and Cardiff City, he possessed a unique licence to thrill, baffling rivals, and delighting supporters with his unmistakable brand of grit and charisma.
