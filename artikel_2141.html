Seriously let down by the players in front of him as he was helpless to avoid picking the ball out of his net on three occasions. Had made a handful of simple stops before being stumped by Jamie McCart's inexplicable own goal, and given no chance after Stephen Kingley teed up Thun for a second. The third was by him in a flash. Scoreline could have been worse if not for the keeper.

Perhaps a natural right-footed player would have cleared easily, but no excuse for this mistake by an experienced player. Night only worsened by a suspected hamstring injury which forced him off.

Took a forearm to the jaw from Furkan Dursun in the first-half, for which the forward was only booked. Was then badly outmuscled by the forward who poked home a third for the hosts in Switzerland after getting in front of the defender. Centre-back did give travelling 2,000 Hearts fans something to cheer with terrific header into the bottom corner after rising highest at the backpost to net a consolation.

Was so close to scoring for Hearts in Trabzon last time out in this competition and was denied a headed goal again here, with Niklas Steffen producing a terrific low save to his left. Wasn't dominant or convincing enough in the rearguard for Hearts but that is criticism which can be levelled at the whole defence.

Another player forced into an uncomfortable position owing to the injury situation as he slotted into left back. He was industrious but not always the most effective in duels in midfield with Hearts overrun by the hosts.

One of few Hearts players who looked like doing something going forward. Hearts are a better team when Mendy is heavily involved in his all-action style, but that was missing against Thun. He cut a tired figure in the midfield and wasn't able to really dictate any play or take charge of the midfield battle as he was bypassed regularly by Thun.
