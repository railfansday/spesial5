Trump Asked to Preserve Nvidia Documents by Senators

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Trump administration was asked on Thursday by Senators Elizabeth Warren of Massachusetts and Chris Coons of Delaware to preserve all records regarding the export of Nvidia Corp.’s advanced semiconductors to China or to “entities that may divert such chips.” The request comes as Nvidia’s board is meeting to discuss the fate of Chief Executive Officer Jensen Huang following reports he had multiple extramarital affairs. Tyler Kendall reports on Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
