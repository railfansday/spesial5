Prediction: This Memory Stock Will Be 2027's Best-Performing AI Stock

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Micron's AI memory demand, supply constraints, and low forward valuation could make it a standout AI stock in 2027, but cyclical risks remain.

While much of the attention has gone to chipmakers in 2026, the artificial intelligence (AI) boom is creating some unexpected winners, and Micron Technology (MU -3.21%) just might be the next. As models grow more demanding and data centers continue to expand, the need for advanced memory will only accelerate.

Plus, with several major trends working in its favor, this memory stock could turn out to be one of 2027's biggest AI beneficiaries.

Micron is emerging as one of the most important suppliers to the AI build-out, providing the dynamic random access memory (DRAM), NAND, and high-bandwidth memory (HBM) needed to power AI infrastructure. In fact, its fiscal 2026 performance showed how dramatically that position has driven he company's growth, with its cloud and data center products benefiting from strong demand and improved pricing.

At the same time, the company says AI demand for memory is growing faster than the industry can expand supply. To that end, Micron has increasingly focused on higher-value data center products, where demand for HBM and advanced server memory is being driven by hyperscaler spending. Indeed, advanced AI chips require enormous amounts of HBM memory to move data efficiently.

Meanwhile, the company has been expanding its portfolio as AI systems become more complex and demanding. For example, its recent product developments include advanced server memory, high-performance solid-state drives (SSDs) designed for AI workloads, and new memory products for physical AI applications.

Looking ahead, Micron is heading into fiscal 2027 with management pointing to continued AI-driven demand, while its latest guidance and product roadmap suggest that advanced data center memory remains central to its growth strategy.

In fact, memory demand continues to accelerate even as bringing new supply online is neither quick nor easy. That very bottleneck is supporting stronger pricing and profitability across its business.

After all, building leading-edge manufacturing capacity takes years and requires enormous investment. Micron's own expansion plans are proof of that.

Specifically, its new Idaho manufacturing capacity is expected to begin producing DRAM wafers in calendar 2027, while its larger New York expansion will help expand supply over the next 20-plus years.

That combination could make Micron one of the more interesting AI stocks to watch in 2027. That's because, unlike companies whose valuations depend on future promises, Micron is already embedded in the AI infrastructure stack where supply constraints and customer demand are tangible.

On top of that, the company is benefiting from its position as the only major U.S.-based memory manufacturer. Indeed, that's an important strategic advantage as governments and tech companies prioritize supply chain resilience and domestic manufacturing.

But the company still faces meaningful risks.

For one, Micron remains exposed to the cyclicality of the memory industry. In other words, if AI spending slows or manufacturers bring too much new capacity online, memory prices could come under pressure.

Plus, there's the risk that hyperscalers may not earn attractive enough returns on their AI investments. That means any slowdown in data center construction would likely affect Micron's revenue outlook.

Right now, 45 of 49 Wall Street analysts rate Micron stock a buy or strong buy, with an average target price of $1,561.91, suggesting the stock could more than double by this time next year. On top of that, its forward price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio of 4.5 offers an attractive entry point for investors who think the AI memory boom still has room to run. The combination of upside and low valuation could help Micron outperform AI peers in 2027.

Looking ahead, with memory becoming increasingly vital to the AI build-out and Micron strengthening its market position, the company could surprise investors as one of 2027's biggest AI stock winners.
