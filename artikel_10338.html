YouTube TV sale drops the price to $49.99/month for a limited time

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

YouTube TV isn’t cheap, but a sale around Black Friday cuts the cost of the service down to just $49.99/month for a limited time, with a couple of strings attached.

The cost of YouTube TV generally lands at $83/month following the most recent price hikes, but there are ways to cut that down. New packages such as “Sports + News” drop the price by a fair bit, with the “Entertainment” package being the most affordable at $55/month.

But a Black Friday deal undercuts even that.

YouTube TV is currently offering the “Sports + News” plan for $49.99/month for 6 months, savings of $132 before the normal $71.99/month price kicks in. It’s a great deal, though one that only “new YouTube TV users” are eligible for. If your Google account has ever signed up for YouTube TV, even a trial, this offer isn’t available – do with that information what you wish.

New YouTube TV users pay $49.99/mo for 6 months of Sports + News Plan (save $132), then $71.99/mo thereafter. Ends 12/2/2026.
