Deborah Ann Woll Will Reprise Her Role As Laufey In Amazon's God Of War Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Deborah Ann Woll, likely best known as Karen Page in Daredevil and Daredevil: Born Again, has just been cast as Laufey in the upcoming live-action God of War series. It's a perfect fit, not just because she's a great actress – Woll already portrays the character in the video games.

The live-action God of War series is currently in production, and after a series of headlines about Kratos, it's refreshing to get some news about his late wife, Faye. Also known as Laufey, she dies right before 2018's God of War (which the show is based on) begins. The game centers on a journey between Kratos and his son to spread Faye's ashes from the highest peak in the realms.

Faye is not present in 2018's God of War, but she appears in God of War Ragnarök in flashback and dream sequences. In this game, she's played by Deborah Ann Woll, an oddly high-profile casting for a relatively minor character. It all made sense earlier this year, however, when we learned Woll will reprise her role as the star of God of War Laufey, which tells Faye's story as she fights through a mythological underworld.

It's a cool piece of news, and it implies that Faye will have more of a presence in the series than in the first game, where she never appears at all. As for what shape that presence takes, we have no idea.

Woll joins the likes of Alastair Duncan, who plays Mimir and will also reprise his role. The two don't really interact in the games, however, so it's unlikely to see them share the screen in the series.

The main news around Prime Video's God of War is about its protagonist. Originally announced as Ryan Hurst, the actor suffered a serious injury on the set of the show and was replaced by Dave Bautista (Guardians of the Galaxy, Glass Onion) in August. Other key cast members include Callum Vinson as Atreus, Sonya Walger as Freya, Mandy Patinkin as Odin, and Ed Skrein as Baldur.

For more God of War, you can look forward to Woll's portrayal of Faye in God of War Laufey, though it recently got delayed.
