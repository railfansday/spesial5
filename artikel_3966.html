Sony's 10 Most Profitable PS5 Games Reported in New Estimates

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Sony's received a lot of criticism for its first party output this console generation. It's no secret that the company has chased live service trends at the cost of single player experiences — the kind of games that solidified the PS4 as a fan favourite machine.

And so it's interesting to see which Sony-published titles have actually made the most money across the last six years or thereabouts.

Now, I should stress that the numbers I'm about to relay are not official. They're estimates based on Alinea Analytics' data-tracking methods — but by and large, the outfit does end up providing fairly accurate numbers.

The team's put together a list it claims are the top ten Sony-published games of the PS5 generation, ranked by premium gross revenue.

Revenue is primarily driven by sales — and so it makes sense for Ragnarok and Spider-Man 2 to be up there at the top, given that they're Sony's two best-selling releases of the generation.

The former has sold roughly 21 million copies, while the latter is closing in on 20 million. The report states that both are evergreen titles, meaning that they're continually selling a decent number of copies despite their age.

Meanwhile, Helldivers 2 is the real outlier on this list. The live service shooter has made Sony a fortune, but most of that money's come from the game's success on PC platform Steam.

The report estimates that just over 66% of copies were sold via Steam, while PS5 accounts for just 26%. The other 8% belongs to Xbox.

Stellar Blade is interesting, too. It's a roughly 50-50 split between PS5 and Steam, with PS5 just edging it.
