OpenAI starts testing its dots agents with some ChatGPT Plus users

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

“Heads up, we’re starting to test dots with a small number of ChatGPT Plus users,” Thibault Sottiaux, who leads OpenAI’s core products, wrote on X on Thursday.

OpenAI has not said how many Plus users are in the test, and its pricing page has not changed. Access is decided by OpenAI account by account. It is not available in Europe, the UK or Switzerland.

Sottiaux said all ChatGPT Business Standard users are also getting dots. OpenAI’s release notes say the rollout covers eligible members in the US, who can each create one dot.

OpenAI launched dots in September for Pro and Business Premium users. Each dot runs on its own cloud computer, works in the background and can connect to thousands of apps. Last week, OpenAI added a wallet that lets them shop for users.
