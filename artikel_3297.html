‘Hunger Games: Sunrise On The Reaping’ $225M WW Opening Bests ‘Wicked: For Good’ & ‘Michael’ – Sunday Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

SUNDAY AM UPDATE: It’s the best opening for Lionsgate in quite some time as The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping minted $225M around the globe, $105M from 40,000 screens in 84 territories.

That figure bests Lionsgate’s previous big opening of 2026, Michael which boogeyed to $218.8M in addition to last November’s pre-Thanksgiving blockbuster Wicked: For Good which debuted to $223M, the highest in that Broadway musical franchise. Those numbers are reported, not like-for-likes.

Among all Hunger Games movies in the box office, the sixth title here (the fifth directed by Francis Lawrence) is the fourth biggest worldwide debut after 2013’s Catching Fire ($304M WW), Mockingjay Part 1 ($276.2M) and Mockingjay Part 2 ($241M) and ahead of the original Hunger Games ($214.2M). Sunrise on the Reaping is light years ahead of the global start of the previous Suzanne Collins novel, 2023’s Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes which debuted at $100.8M. Internationally, 81 out of the 84 territories ranked No. 1. The top No. 1 territory debuts we have are: UK with $17.4M, the highest opening of the Franchise and in Lionsgate history. Australia counted $9.1M, Germany $8.4M, France was $7.1M (750,000 admissions), Mexico opened to $7M while China posted $5.9M.

For Imax, Sunrise on the Reaping posted $26.4M around the global, the biggest opener for the large format exhibitor for the franchise. The pic was a Filmed for Imax title and was also Imax’s biggest global opening for a Lionsgate movie and Imax second best November global opening weekend of all-time.

In North America, Sunrise on the Reaping took in $16.3M for Imax, repping 14% of the weekend and also the exhibits biggest November domestic opening weekend of all-time. Abroad, the Lawrence directed title counted $10.1M, repping 10% of the offshore debut. A total of 15 markets had IMAX top ten all-time opening weekends including Argentina, Australia, Belgium, Germany, Italy, and Netherlands.

The pic is also the No. 1 Indian feature in several markets including Thailand ($1.9M), Nepal ($1.5M), Indonesia($1.2M), the Netherlands ($610K), and Cambodia ($355K). Upcoming releases include Argentina (Nov 26), Central America (Nov 26), Colombia (Nov 26), Ecuador (Nov 26), Peru (Nov 26) and Czechia (Nov 26).

PREVIOUS FRIDAY EXCLUSIVE: Lionsgate’s sixth Hunger Games move, The Hunger Games: The Sunrise on the Reaping, is off to a strong start with $24.6M through Thursday night. Add that to the expected $51M Friday, which includes $21M in previews, and the global running cume is around $76M. Already, that’s close to toppling the previous $100.1M worldwide start of 2023’s The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

The fifth Francis Lawrence directed movie in the Suzanne Collins franchise has opened to No. 1 in 66 out of 67 markets to date with another 17 territories set to bow this weekend. It would not be shocking if the movie files a global opening plus-or-minus $200M global.

On Wednesday, the Joseph Zada, McKenna Grace, Elle Fanning, Jesse Plemons, Glenn Close, Kieran Culkin ensemble pic bowed in France to No. 1 with 154,000 admissions or $1.4M, which was +157% ahead of Songbirds & Snakes.

In Scandinavia, Norway on Wednesday, Sunrise opened to No. 1 with $494,000, marking the largest opening day in the Hunger Games franchise locally. Sweden opened to No. 1 with $459,000, coming in +31% ahead of Songbirds & Snakes.

Belgium bowed to No. 1 as well with $343,000, the largest opening day locally for the feature take of the Collins’ novels.

On Thursday, Haymitch set down in Australia with a No. 1 $1.5M opening for the day, +137% ahead of Songbirds and Snakes. Germany was also No. 1 with $1M, +83% ahead of Songbirds & Snakes.

Lionsgate distributes their movies in Latin America with partner IDC. The series has always called at the B.O. there and combined all markets did close to $3M at No. 1. Of that, Mexico minted $1M, +107% ahead of Songbirds and Snakes, while Brazil grossed $679,000.

Italy’s first day was also molto bene, +79% ahead of Songbirds & Snakes, for a No. 1 of $680K.

In Scandinavia, Denmark bowed to No. 1 with $461,000, +65% ahead of the previous 2023 chapter. Finland was also No. 1 with $205,000 +36% higher than the Tom Blyth and Rachel Zegler starring installment.

Netherlands charted No. 1 yesterday with $412,000, +66% over Songbirds & Snakes.

In the Middle East, Sunrise on the Reaping bowed to first place in all markets with a combined $390,000. The UAE opened to $129,000, +135% ahead of Songbirds & Snakes. Saudi Arabia opened to $126,000 was also +155% ahead of the last Hunger Games movie.

The net production cost of Sunrise on the Reaping is $150M, with a majority of that financed via foreign sales. The U.S. marketing spend is $60M, sources say. Lionsgate also directly distributes the movie in the United Kingdom.
