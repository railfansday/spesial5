The Intellectual Property Corporation Unveils Executive Team Following Sony Split

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

EXCLUSIVE: Earlier this year, The Intellectual Property Corporation (IPC), which was founded by Eli Holzman and Aaron Saidman, became independent after exiting from Sony Pictures Television.

The company has now unveiled its leadership team as a fully independent company, at least for now.

The group includes Alecia Ashby as EVP, Head of Production, Sam Marshman as SVP of Development, Todd Frangella as SVP, Head of Finance and Tina Tozzi as VP of Development.

Ashby has been with the company for around ten years and has worked on series including HBO’s We’re Here and Netflix’s Indian Matchmaking as well as co-producing With Love, Meghan. Watch on Deadline

Marshman was most recently a development consultant at Sony and before that spent 14 years at Renegade 83. His credits including Amazon’s The Pack, USA Network’s Snake in the Grass and Scrabble and Trivial Pursuit for The CW as well as working on Discovery’s franchise Naked and Afraid.

Frangella has joined from The Great British Bake Off producer Love Productions, where he was SVP of Production, Finance & Operations at its U.S. operation. He has worked on series including A&E’s Intervention and Bravo’s The Real Housewives of New York City.

Tozzi joined in November 2025 and was previously Head of Development at Togethxr as well as worked at Westbrook Media and Is It Cake? producer Alfred Street Industries. She worked on shows including Netflix’s New York Homicide and Sparking Joy with Marie Kondo.

Elsewhere, the company has named Megan Shen as Development Coordinator and Macy Bernstein as Executive Assistant to Holzman and Saidman.

Over the summer, the company landed its first greenlight since becoming independent. It is making Weight to Date, a dating docu-format for Netflix, as first revealed by Netflix. The company is also producing a three-part documentary about 50 Cent for Hulu.

“For proven, creatively ambitious producers, there are significant opportunities in the unscripted space. And our independent structure affords us the agility to seize that moment. After an exhilarating first decade in business, IPC is redoubling our focus and intensity as we continue to strive to launch hits in partnership with nonfiction’s most discerning buyers,” said IPC Co-Founders and Co-CEOs Saidman and Holzman. “IPC has assembled a team of exceptional executives, who bring with them decades of experience creating hundreds of hours of premium programming. Their taste, ideas and tenacity are exactly what this moment demands. And we are truly excited about what we are going to accomplish together in this next chapter for IPC.”
