Star Wars: Galactic Racer Officially Getting the Feature Every Racing Game Needs

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The year's best Star Wars game gets even better

Scrib doon! Star Wars: Galactic Racer developer Fuse Games announced the racing sim is getting a key ingredient next year: split-screen multiplayer. Up to four players will be able to square off on the same screen at once after the update.

"We’ve heard you!" Fuse's social media announcement begins, as the developer acknowledges this is a feature players have wanted since the game launched in early October. "A dedicated team at Fuse Games has been working hard to develop a new mode: Free Play. Choose your vehicle, character, planet, and track to race alone or with up to 3 other players in local splitscreen action."

Galactic Racer launched with multiplayer modes, but they're only playable online. It's not clear if local split-screen multiplayer will also be playable in those other modes, like Podracing Tour, or if you'll only be able to enjoy split-screen gameplay in the new Free Play mode.

Star Wars fans have eaten well this year. Bit Reactor's Star Wars Zero Company brought turn-based tactical gameplay to the Clone Wars a month and a half before Galactic Racer let everyone join the Outer Rim's Galactic League.

It's one of the year's highest-rated games, and don't be surprised if Galactic Racer takes home some hardware come awards season. "The game's learning curve is steep, and there's a lot to manage and think about in a race. But boy, when you get there, the rush is unmatched. This is a game of both blistering speed and tactical nuance, where smart use of your abilities is just as important as racing reflexes. It's a deep game system, and the further you get into a run, the better and more rewarding it gets," Polygon's Oli Welsh wrote in his Galactic Racer review, where he called it "the best arcade racing game in a generation."

Star Wars: Galactic Racer is available on PlayStation 5, Windows PC, and Xbox Series X. The Free Play mode will be added "in early 2027," according to Fuse Games' announcement.
