Game Of Thrones: A Song Of Ice & Fire Officially Returns In 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

There is an abundance of media based on Game of Thrones, the TV show, as opposed to the original source material. That's understandable, as while the original novels helped give life to the show, it was HBO's adaptation that truly popularized it. For the vast majority of people, it is the faces of Kit Harington, Peter Dinklage, Nikolaj Coster-Waldau, and Lena Headey that they remember. So, in the many Game of Thrones video games, for example, their faces are the ones modeled, rather than whole new interpretations of the characters.

However, while the abundance of upcoming Game of Thrones spin-offs and adaptations may more closely resemble the show, a brand-new release goes back to those original novels to offer a unique experience that fans of the books will enjoy. It is absolutely aimed at dedicated fans who love all things A Song of Ice and Fire, as well as tabletop enthusiasts looking for a new wargaming experience they can truly sink their teeth into.

There have been Game of Thrones board games in the past, but, as aforementioned, these have more often than not adapted the show and not the books. Well, A Song of Ice and Fire: Tactics aims to rectify that with a brand-new tabletop skirmish game that offers a completely fresh take on the many factions and characters featured within the original novels. This new tabletop game is developed by CMON, a studio that has worked with a number of major IPs to great success in the past, including Marvel, DC, and Dune.

A Song of Ice and Fire: Tactics, like the many skirmish tabletop games before it (Halo: Flashpoint is a good comparison), is a wargame for two players that sees each one control a small force of miniatures in a turn-based battle. Utilizing action points, abilities, different unit types, dice, and more, players will attempt to overwhelm the other player and kill their way to victory. It is a combat-focused experience that veers away from the diplomatic politicking of the books and more into the bloody battles.

There are a number of products within the A Song of Ice and Fire: Tactics game that are set to be made available to the public through retail. The best place to start is the A Song of Ice and Fire: Tactics: Battle of the Trident Starter Set, which gives you two warbands, dice, stat sheets, rulebooks, and everything else you need to get started. There are also expansions with other warbands based on iconic factions such as the Brotherhood Without Banners, as well as major sets.

A Song of Ice and Fire: Tactics was crowdfunded successfully all the way back in February 2024 via Gamefound, but it has yet to be distributed to backers. It is still in production, although a release date has yet to be confirmed by the studio. CMON has posted a few new updates via the Gamefound page and will undoubtedly post the official release date there as well. It is likely that it'll finally be released in 2027, at which point A Song of Ice and Fire (as well as Game of Thrones) fans can finally get their hands on it.
