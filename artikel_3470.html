Fair Isle sweaters have been around since the 18th century, being crafted with the warmest knits and stitched with homely geometric patterns. And while the style was more of a functional piece for Scottish fishermen, it has since been turned into a winter sensation that fashion people don year after year. Here’s the thing: Lately, the classic crewneck is taking a backseat to make way for a fresh take on the pullover. We’re talking about Fair Isle cardigans, which are destined for every cool girl’s closet in 2026.

Delivering peak ‘grandmacore’ style, Fair Isle cardigans have a vintage appearance with front buttons, simple motifs and cozy fabrics. And while they’re the newer version of the familiar crewneck, everyone from shoppers to celebrities like Hailey Bieber is breaking them out for the season. We’re following suit with equally stylish picks from the Gap, Lands’ End, Vineyard Vines and more. And because they’re all right on Amazon, Prime members will get their loot in only two days’ time.
