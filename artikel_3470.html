Chris Pratt Officially Addresses Star-Lord Return Amid James Gunn's DC Departure

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Chris Pratt has been busy as ever in 2026 despite being years removed from his big-budget franchise roles, but that hasn’t stopped him from finding success at the box office. Pratt stumbled with the release of Mercy (co-starring Rebecca Ferguson) earlier this year, but despite an underwhelming performance in theaters, the movie went on to find success on Prime Video. Pratt swiftly returned to box office greatness thanks to his titular role in The Super Mario Galaxy Movie, the sequel that grossed over $1 billion at the global box office. In the latter half of the year, Pratt has focused more on streaming projects like the second season of The Terminal List, which is now streaming on Prime Video. He’s soon to debut a new movie on Apple TV with Way of the Warrior Kid, which co-stars Linda Cardellini.

Most fans would agree that, despite all the new roles he’s taken on in the last few years, Pratt is still best known for his role as Star-Lord in the Guardians of the Galaxy movies. All three films were written and directed by James Gunn, who was the co-head of DC Studios along with Peter Safran for several years before stepping down from his duties earlier in the week. Fans around the world are already asking about what James Gunn is going to do next, with some speculating that he could return to Marvel for another Guardians movie. During a recent interview to promote the second season of The Terminal List, Pratt was asked about potentially returning to the Marvel universe for a new Star-Lord project, possibly one even under James Gunn. His answer might shock you:

“That would be awesome. I love working with James [Gunn]. He’s the man. I haven’t had the chance to speak with him since that news came out… it's always tough because anytime something like this comes up it could always become a headline. All I’ll say is I love James. He directed all three [Guardians of the Galaxy movies], and that’s pretty rare… so if he wanted to come back and bring it back, I would love that.”

At the end of Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3, the card that played across the screen promised fans that “the legendary Star-Lord will return.” There’s been no official confirmation yet on which project will deliver on this promise, but most fans assume that Pratt will reprise the role in Avengers: Doomsday later this year. Still, Pratt told Collider last year that Marvel would follow through on the promise, so he certainly knows something that we don’t.
