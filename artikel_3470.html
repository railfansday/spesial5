‘The Rookie’ Meets ‘Criminal Minds’ in Netflix’s New Near-Perfect Crime Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

When it comes to network TV, ABC has been dominating with consistent, chart-topping shows for many years now. Between High Potential, starring It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia's Kaitlin Olson as the hyper-intelligent single mother Morgan Gillory, and the newcomer R.J. Decker, led by Scott Speedman as an ex-con photographer turned P.I., the network has found critical acclaim and strong viewership alike.

But while High Potential and R.J. Decker are certainly a few of the latest successes from the network, Will Trent, which was first released in January 2023, has also been going strong since its release. With Season 5 of the series already confirmed, the crime series has proven to have a killer procedural formula, one that draws in viewers with episodic cases while still developing major storylines for the main characters. And while it might not be getting the same attention as other ABC shows right now, it's the perfect binge for crime fans and ABC shows alike.

Will Trent, based on Karin Slaughter's bestselling series of crime novels, follows the titular special agent (Ramón Rodríguez) at the Georgia Bureau of Investigation. Abandoned at birth and growing up in the harsh environment of Atlanta's overburdened foster care system, Trent parlayed his experience into a unique perspective on crime that has helped him post a remarkable clearance rate and become renowned for his dedication. Along the way, he's still had to wrestle with his past and other personal challenges in the pursuit of justice.

Throughout its run, Will Trent has seen massive, and growing, success. Season 4 was the most-viewed installment of Will Trent to date, becoming the first series on the network since Scandal to grow in ratings every single year it's been on the air. In fact, it's the first broadcast show across all networks to accomplish the feat in nearly 14 years, according to Nielsen's measure. On Rotten Tomatoes, the series also received critical acclaim, reaching an impressive 89% from critics in Season 1. While the following seasons have yet to earn a critics score, reviews say it all. "Will Trent continues to go deeper into its characters’ lives and psyches, transforming the show from an average crime procedural to one of the best character-driven dramas on any broadcast network," writes one critic review from Season 4, which premiered on January 6, 2026.

Season 5 of Will Trent, which will be premiering on January 6, 2027, already revealed some major plans for the new season. Among them is the addition of The Pitt actress Alexandra Metz, who has been tapped to play Birdie Palmer, the newly appointed Deputy Director of the GBI. Reports say that Birdie is confident and ambitious, but she is "deeply guarded after a traumatic event in her past."

The Ramón Rodríguez-led series just got a major update.

Metz's arrival comes at a pivotal point after the unexpected and controversial death of Amanda (Sonja Sohn) in the Season 4 finale. Speaking with Variety, showrunner Karine Rosenthal explained why Amanda’s death creates such a major reset for the show. “Amanda has been the character who has protected Will for as long as she has known him. So this gives us the opportunity for a completely new dynamic in the GBI, because her replacement will not have that same relationship with Will," she said. "Her death had the most impact on the most amount of characters. It really sets everybody reeling from this enormous loss. So it’s such a reset for our characters in a way that is painful but exciting for Season 5.”

With all that said, while that cliffhanger from Season 4 had some fans reeling, the fans' dedication to the show continues to prove how much of a hit Will Trent is on ABC. With a hardened yet endearing lead who audiences can't help but to root for, and episodic cases that keep viewers guessing, the series is another home run for ABC, as well as an easy, satisfying binge for anyone who hasn't been tuning in.
