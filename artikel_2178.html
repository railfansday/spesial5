‘Definitely’: Birmingham City reporter delivers Chris Davies contract verdict amid impressive turnaround

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Birmingham City have won six of their last seven Championship games to surge to the top of the table after 15 matches. BCFC are in a fine position as the November international break continues, and the work of Chris Davies has drawn significant praise.

There have been doubts over his Blues future at times, especially during a disappointing 2025/26 season, but Davies has completed an impressive turnaround to secure his position. And, one man who believes the Welshman’s work should be rewarded with a new deal is Birmingham Live’s Alex Dicken.

When quizzed on Davies’ contract situation in a fan Q&A, Blues reporter Dicken wrote: “I think they should give it a few months to allow the relationship between John Murtough (Birmingham City’s new director of football) and Davies to develop – and get the transfer window done and dusted – but it’s definitely something that should be looked at before the end of the season.

“I think Chris Davies is the sort of manager who will want security and to know where he stands. It is remarkable what he’s done in the last six months when you consider he faced questions over his future at the club in March/April last year. He has shown huge development as a young manager.”

Davies started strongly as he led a strong Birmingham City side to a rampant League One title win. That was expected though, so the boss and his side rose to the Championship with a point to prove.

And, they failed to prove it. Last season, BCFC struggled for consistency and frustrated their supporters with football that was often drab and unimaginative. It led to calls for Davies to be replaced by a bigger name, but the hierarchy stuck by their boss.

There were some lingering doubts early this season, but the doubters have been silenced with a stunning surge to the summit of the Championship. Birmingham City play with much more speed and impetus in possession, as shown by the fact that they have scored 21 goals in the last 10 games. They’ve maintained defensive solidity too though, with Christoph Klarer and Phil Neumann starring.

Davies was criticised for not adapting or getting the best out of City’s attacking talents last season. He has done both of those things this term though, highlighting his own development and growth as a boss.

Davies dealt with the pressure of turning around Birmingham City’s fortunes. But, such is the ambition of the club that he must now take on the task of maintaining their impressive form.

BCFC have eyes on a rise to the Premier League with a new stadium on the horizon and dreams of a Jude Bellingham return remaining for Tom Wagner.

Davies’ importance in this cannot be overstated, and further security over his future would be richly deserved. His current contract runs until 2028, so entrusting him with the next stage of their project means a new contract will be required.

As Dicken argues though, perhaps it is best to wait until new director of football Murtough is settled. If a relationship can be built between the two, an agreement becomes even more logical.
