34-year-old must be added to Millwall’s 25-man squad after initial omission: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Millwall will be thankful to head into the international break with not too much damage done to their play-off chances. The Lions have seven fewer points than they did this time last year. Their latest performances do not indicate a side that will challenge for promotion once again.

Aside from injuries, a tactical problem Alex Neil is facing is in midfield. With a young duo of Jenson Metcalfe and Derek Mazou-Sacko, the South London outfit are being exploited in the middle of the pitch and experience is needed desperately

Casper De Norre is currently sidelined, and Millwall don’t have many senior figures in that position. With Massimo Luongo failing to make the Lions’ 25-man squad, his absence is hurting the Lions in the opening half of the season.

Millwall signed Luongo in the 2025 summer transfer window after his exit from Ipswich Town. The Australian was initially recruited to be a secondary option for the Lions. However, he quickly became a regular in the starting XI until an ACL injury against QPR ruled him out for the rest of the term.

His experience was invaluable to Neil’s side in the opening 10 games of the 2025/26 campaign. When the 34-year-old was involved, the South London outfit lost just twice. They could use his Championship expertise during their horrid run of form.

Metcalfe and Mazou-Sacko have a combined 44 Championship appearances, which is a fraction of the 245 Luongo has. While the duo haven’t been poor on a personal level, their positional awareness can be lacking, which is why Millwall have been caught out four times on the counter-attack.

In just nine second-tier games last term, Luongo made 10 interceptions and conceded just six goals whilst he was on the pitch. The Lions need to improve their defensive record, and the Australian would certainly help.

Luongo has featured several times for Millwall’s development squad to rebuild his fitness. However, there will undoubtedly be questions about whether he will be the same player after his injury. Regardless, given the Lions’ frustrations in midfield, Neil should want the 34-year-old in his squad for the rest of the season.

Obviously, the Australian can’t play until January, when the transfer window opens, with the next squad able to be submitted at the beginning of February. However, the Lions will improve when he is available for selection.

Luongo was dispossessed just five times last campaign and won 52% of his duels. His 47 recoveries in nine games were crucial to Millwall’s early fortunes last year. His involvement this term could help Neil’s side become a side that is contending again.
