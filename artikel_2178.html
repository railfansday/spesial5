Ex-Celtic star suffers major injury scare just hours after Scotland call

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Former Celtic defender Stephen Welsh has handed Scotland boss Sebastien Pocognoli an injury scare just hours after being named in his latest squad.

The Swansea City centre-back was forced off after only 24 minutes of his side’s clash away to Portsmouth following a late challenge from Ebou Adams.

Welsh needed assistance from two members of the Swansea medical team as he made his way off the pitch, raising concerns over his availability for Scotland’s upcoming Nations League double-header against North Macedonia and Switzerland.

The 26-year-old was included in Pocognoli’s squad earlier in the day, with the Scotland boss also welcoming several established names back into his group, including Scott McTominay.

However, Welsh’s injury could now force Pocognoli into making a change before his squad even meets up.

Swansea boss Vitor Matos admitted after the game that there is a possibility the injury could prove serious, with the defender set to undergo a scan.

Asked about Welsh’s condition, Matos said: “Of course there is that possibility, but let’s first have the scan and then speak about it.”

Welsh has been an ever-present for Swansea this season and has also established himself in the Scotland set-up.

The former Celtic man earned his first senior cap against Slovenia during the previous international camp before featuring in all of Scotland’s matches during that break.

He now has four appearances for his country, but Scotland will face an anxious wait over his fitness ahead of the meetings with North Macedonia and Switzerland.
