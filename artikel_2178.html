Sheffield Wednesday transfer update emerges as Dave Challinor faces tricky situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Sheffield Wednesday remain in discussions with the EFL over what they can do in the January window, although it seems unlikely Dave Challinor will be in a position to make major changes to the squad.

It was a remarkably hectic summer for the Owls after Arise Capital Partners completed their takeover of the club at the end of the previous campaign.

A major squad overhaul followed, but Wednesday were under EFL restrictions in terms of what they could do, so the squad was packed out with loan signings and free transfers.

Nevertheless, they still boast a lot of quality by League One standards, but an inconsistent start to the season meant Henrik Pedersen paid the price as he lost his job.

The Yorkshire outfit moved quickly to bring in Dave Challinor as his successor, and the former Stockport boss is preparing for his first game in charge at Oldham on Sunday in the FA Cup.

Whilst he will be keen on a cup run, the league will be the priority, and Challinor will hope to take Wednesday back to the Championship at the first attempt.

In normal circumstances, the new boss would also have one eye on the January window, where he may want to make a few tweaks to the group.

Yet, as outlined above, the situation at Wednesday is different due to the restrictions, and the Sheffield Star has stated that the club are ‘close to the limit’ in terms of what they can spend.

And, speaking to the same outlet, sporting director Simon Wilson admitted they still don’t know exactly how they will be able to back Challinor in the New Year.

“We’re not 100 per cent sure exactly what it is. We’re constantly in dialogue with the league around the rules and the limitations. We obviously have the ability to change the squad, as anyone does. The window is open and business can be done.

“At this moment in time, Dave has just come into the building, he needs a bit of time to assess the squad and get a feel for which players are going to be the most useful and where we have areas of need. Then we’ll work into the window from that point.”

Obviously, this is not ideal for Wednesday, as most clubs will already be drawing up plans for January, and whilst the Owls may have players in mind, they still don’t know who they can bring in.
