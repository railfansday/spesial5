After taking seven years to make Persona 6 and four years to make Persona 5, Atlus promises future games will come quicker

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

"People won't have to wait as long for the next game as they have previously."

Persona 6 is still slowly chugging in the background, but developer Atlus is promising that future games in its beloved RPG series won't take quite as long as they used to.

There was a four year gap between 2012's Persona 4 Golden and the original Persona 5, and it's already been seven years since the expanded Persona 5 Royal first dropped. Despite the sometimes agonising wait filled with countless spin-offs and collaborations with live service games, we've only seen a vague teaser trailer for the next proper Persona.

P-Studio general producer Kazuhisa Wada recently assured folks that the pattern wouldn't repeat again. "Persona 6 is not the end," he said in a Sega Samania interview translated by GamesRadar. "We have been working to make sure that people won't have to wait as long for the next game as they have previously. In order for Persona to grow as an IP, we consider how to live up to people's expectations in terms of creativity and development every day."

Wada doesn't go into any more detail about how Atlus plans to release Persona games quicker. Is the next mainline game already in development alongside Persona 6, or is Atlus just streamlining how it makes games? Either way, the studio seems to have abandoned its divisive habit of returning to and improving its games after a few years - P3 Portable, P4 Golden, P5 Royal. I imagine that'll cut down on wait times, at least somewhat.

"Large-scale RPGs like the Persona series take five years or more to develop," he added. "So there can be a long time between numbered entries."

As for Persona 6 itself, Wada unsurprisingly "can't say much" about the much-hyped, high-schooling, demon-punching game. "But we want our games to demonstrate all of the hard work we've put in. With that in mind, we are creating something better than anything we've made before, and that is hugely satisfying."

We don't know much about Atlus' occult sequel, officially, but a massive information leak earlier in the year revealed a full achievement list for Persona 6 and a bunch of other unreleased games, including Kingdom Hearts 4 and an unannounced Sea of Thieves project. Be wary of online spoilers, then.
