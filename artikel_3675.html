GTA 6’s Stars Release A Very Nice Statement As Game Goes Live

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here, and along with that, the actors playing the main characters Jason (Dylan Rourke) and Lucia (Manni L. Perez) have released a heartfelt joint statement.

"Years witnessing the hardest working and most skilled artisans game making has to offer. We are truly honored to collaborate on this worldwide testament to human creativity, ingenuity, and sheer willpower," they said. "So much learned and so much to learn, keep fighting for what you love, stay humble. See y’all in Leonida."

Rockstar kept the actors' identities a secret throughout development, though fans worked things out for themselves a long time ago.

Rockstar has now published the full GTA 6 credits confirming all the cast and everyone else who contributed to the game. In addition to Rourke and Perez, the main voice cast for GTA 6 includes Brad Carter as Donnie, Bobby Moynihan as Cal, and Stephen Root as Brian.

Numerous other famous people have credits in the game, including "Weird Al" Yankovic, Doug Stanhope, Stavros Halkias, and JB Smoove, just to name a few.

GameSpot’s GTA 6 review scored the game a rare 10/10. “GTA 6 is an astonishing open-world action game that I’d wager won’t be outdone until Grand Theft Auto 7,” reviewer Tamoor Hussain said.

GTA 6 has now unseated the Ocarina of Time remake for Switch 2 to become the highest-rated game of 2026 on GameSpot sister site, Metacritic, and is tied with the original Ocarina of Time for the highest aggregate review score ever.

After GTA 6 reviews came online, shares of Rockstar owner Take-Two ripped higher, but the company is still down massively this year and over the past 12 months.
