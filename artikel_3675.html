Is Pete Davidson Dating Sarah Jane Nader? Their Relationship Status Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Pete Davidson and Sarah Jane Nader are not dating despite recent romance rumors.

A source tells Us that the Saturday Night Live alum, 33, and the Love Thy Nader star, 24, are just friends.

Fans began to speculate about the pair after they were spotted on a night out in New York City in August. Davidson and Nader were later seen together again at an AMC movie theater in NYC’s East Village earlier this month.

Davidson was most recently linked to model Elsie Hewitt, with whom he shares 11-month-old daughter Scottie. Us confirmed in March 2025 that the pair had begun dating. Four months later, Hewitt, 30, and Davidson announced that they were expecting their first baby. The exes welcomed Scottie in December 2025.

Us exclusively reported in May that Davidson and Hewitt had called it quits after just over a year of dating.

“They are still sorting things out as it pertains to their split,” a source close to the former couple exclusively told Us at the time. “They tried to make it work but there were different stressors, including having a baby together as two people who didn’t yet know each other that well. They are both trying to make it work and have made accommodations in their schedules for the baby.”

The source added, “They are still rooting for each other but are each going their own ways, outside of working to coparent and focusing on their baby.”

An insider later told Us that Hewitt and Davidson were “moving forward” after their split.

“Their communication has improved,” the source shared. “They’ve been working together on parenting. Pete has adjusted his schedule to be there for their daughter. Pete and Elsie both wanted [the relationship] to work and it didn’t.”

A source later confirmed to Us in July that Hewitt and Davidson had reached “a joint agreement pertaining to their child.”

“We are cooperatively coparenting our daughter whose well-being remains our top priority, and we are fully committed to working together to give her love from both of us,” they told Us in a joint statement via their reps.

Davidson and Hewitt reunited on Halloween to celebrate the holiday with their daughter.

“Smells like Halloween,” Hewitt captioned a photo of the pair crouching beside a red wagon holding Scottie, who was dressed like Tony Soprano from HBO’s The Sopranos.

Nader, meanwhile, was previously romantically linked to Eliana Orly and Alexis Williams.
