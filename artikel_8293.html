Get Out and Stay Out with These All-Weather Layers

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Some of the best holiday gifts have a story behind them. With Berghaus, that story stretches back six decades, to 1966. That’s when mountaineers Gordon Davison and Peter Lockey founded the brand and began creating innovative, stylish outdoor apparel from their headquarters in northeast England. Since then, Berghaus has built a reputation for designing gear that withstands the gnarliest conditions. And now the story continues in the United States, where just this year, Berghaus became available to consumers.

Whoever you’re shopping for—friend, family, or yourself—these seven picks from the Berghaus line will upgrade anyone’s holiday season. They’re functional and fashionable and do more than fend off snow, cold, and rain. They make it easier to spend more time outdoors, which is the greatest gift of all.

If your family goes for a holiday hike no matter the weather, this is the jacket for the job. The Kara-Su 7000 GTX is constructed with premium three-layer Gore-Tex Pro, which provides an impenetrable—and superdurable—barrier against snow, sleet, and rain. That superior waterproofing is combined with an adjustable hem and cuffs that further seal out the elements, along with a highly customizable hood.

Six well-placed pockets make it easy to store extras like gloves, candy canes, and other stocking stuffers. The jacket also comes in three bold colorways—ideal for staying seen in a blizzard and always looking good.

Straight-leg pants are having a moment, and the Kara-Su Pant harnesses that style trend and updates it with trail-ready design. Its accommodating cut, articulated knees, and stretch fabric enhance mobility for all outdoor pursuits.

Adjustable ankle hems ensure an always-right fit, and three pockets keep the essentials close at hand. The Kara-Su Pant is built with durable synthetic material that will last for years, which is a good thing because you’ll want to wear them everywhere.

Across the decades, Berghaus apparel has been lauded for matching cutting-edge style with top-notch outdoor performance. For proof, look no further than this retro-chic top: The Kara-Su Sweater turns heads with bright colorblocking, an oversized cut, and the brand’s classic Extrem 7000 logo that ties in Berghaus’s rich history.

Looks aren’t the only appeal, of course. The Kara-Su Sweater’s jersey fabric is snuggle-next-to-the-fire soft, and the ribbed cuffs and hem help it retain its shape over time. Put that all together, and you have a cozy gift that’s guaranteed to be an instant favorite.

This shell blends a sleek, trail-to-town aesthetic with stay-dry-anywhere protection. The Meru Mountain Jacket features two-layer Gore-Tex waterproofing for keeping out every drop of precipitation while remaining breathable enough for hiking, urban exploration, and more. If the weather really starts to kick up, adjustable everything—waist, cuffs, hood, you name it—provides additional protection, complemented by a double storm flap.

Heritage Berghaus branding on the chest and shoulder gives the Meru Mountain Jacket a technical vibe that would look good in any era, and it comes in a variety of eye-catching colorways. Two low-profile zippered pockets near the waist complete the feature set.

This Trango Jacket’s design and performance are just as striking as its looks. Most folks probably notice the latter first: Featuring throwback colors and prominent cargo pockets, the Trango looks like it just stepped out of a time machine from the ’90s. But its performance is all modern: Three-layer Gore-Tex easily sheds moisture, and a hip-length cut provides additional coverage.

Typical of Berghaus jackets, the Trango is packed with smart details: The toggles for adjusting the waist, hood, collar, and hem can be easily operated with gloves on, and the hood’s stiffened brim will keep water from dripping in your eyes. This is the jacket to wear if building a snowman—in a snowstorm—is on the agenda.

For days when you need a little protection and a lot of breathability, the Trango Mountain Windbreaker is the clear choice. It blocks the wind and cuts the chill, and its DWR-treated fabric can withstand light precipitation.

This jacket is wonderfully versatile. The hood offers protection, but on clear days, it stows away to preserve the Trango’s clean lines. The cordlock hem seals out drafts, and two pockets add convenient storage.

When staying warm is priority one, the Trango Mountain Jacket offers a cozy refuge. Synthetic Hydroloft insulation traps heat, and the elasticated, ribbed hem and cuffs ensure warmth stays where it’s needed.

The cropped design works with any outfit, and two large zippered hand pockets can hold a hat, gloves, phone, and more. As always, heritage Berghaus branding on the chest completes the package with a timeless look.

Founded in 1966 in Newcastle upon Tyne, UK, Berghaus is a global leader in premium outdoor outerwear, known for innovative designs and lasting durability. From pioneering lightweight rucksacks to iconic Gore-Tex jackets, Berghaus equips adventurers to tackle the elements.
