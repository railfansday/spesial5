Top 10 Resorts in the East for Terrain Variety (2027)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Terrain variety may not be the first thing you think of when planning a ski trip, but it matters, especially when your group includes different ability levels. A resort’s range of runs can make or break a group ski day. It can be the difference between beginners and intermediates riding lifts together or only meeting up at lunch.

Below are SKI readers’ 10 top-ranked resorts for terrain variety. Each has something for everyone, from hard-charging experts to those who still need help buckling their boots.
