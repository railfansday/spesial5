West Ham keeping tabs on upcoming Bristol City prospect

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

West Ham are on the lookout for potential transfer targets as the January window nears, and it appears they’ve got eyes on the next generation. The Sun states that WHUFC are interested in Joe Duncan, who is currently on loan at Southend United from Bristol City.

The 20-year-old, who was a surprise call-up to Craig Bellamy’s Wales squad for their UEFA Nations League fixtures against Norway and Portugal, has caught the eye in the National League. Duncan has earned rave reviews since moving to Roots Hall, playing a key role in them sitting in 3rd place.

Ipswich Town are also said to be monitoring Duncan’s situation, with the shot-stopper’s contract set to expire at the end of the season. The Bristol City academy graduate is yet to make a senior first-team appearance at Ashton Gate, having joined aged 11.

It is common for Football League sides to loan out their academy prospects to non-league outfits to give them a taste of regular first-team action. Duncan is following that very path, and interest from West Ham and Ipswich Town shows his success is not going unnoticed.

Penning his first professional contract at Bristol City in the summer of 2023, the two-time Wales U21 international flourished at Tiverton Town and Farborough.

With Tiverton, he was nominated for their Player of the Month award in each month he was there which speaks volumes about the impact he had in Devon. A stint with Farnborough last season brought similar success, helping to retain their National League South status after arriving in March.

Making the step-up to the National League with Southend United felt like the perfect stepping stone ahead of the new season and he has impressed at Roots Hall, despite only keeping one clean sheet.

A first senior call-up to the Wales squad indicates the potential Duncan has though, featuring on the bench in their defeat to Norway on Saturday.

Discussion is rife around what business West Ham may do in the January transfer window, but adding another young ‘keeper to their ranks shouldn’t be top of the to-do list.

Although the goalkeeping department is an area of concern due to the uncertain future of Alphonse Areola and poor performances of Mads Hermansen, the Hammers have plenty of talent waiting in the wings.

Finlay Herrick has shown his worth in the limited minutes he has been given, while Mason Terry continues to aid his development with loan moves in non-league, signing for Yeovil Town last week.

A January addition between the sticks will be paramount for Scott Parker to help shore up a leaky defence, but it is much more likely to be a senior figure.

Duncan is undoubtedly a fine talent who could become a big name in the years to come. However, a move to WHUFC may not provide a clear pathway to first-team football, given the young shot-stoppers already in their ranks.
