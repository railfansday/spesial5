Titans' Jaylen Harrell, 24, Hospitalized After 'Serious Car Accident'

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Tennessee Titans defensive end Jaylen Harrell has been hospitalized after a car crash.

“Yesterday, Jaylen Harrell was involved in a serious car accident that resulted in him being taken to the hospital in his hometown of Tampa, Florida,” a rep for the Titans told ESPN in a Saturday, November 21, statement. “We have been in constant communication with Jaylen’s family and are still working to gather more information.

Harrell, 24, had been a passenger in an Uber rideshare at the time of the accident, ESPN reported. No further details have been publicly shared.

The linebacker, who was selected by the Titans in the seventh round of the 2024 draft, has been on injured reserve at the start of the 2026-2027 NFL season after tearing his ACL in the preseason.

“I’m praying for Jaylen,” his teammate Kevin Winston Jr. told reporters during an August press conference. “I talked to him about it, having went through the same thing. I told him he’s gonna be just fine. I know Jaylen as a person, I told him I’m praying for him and best of luck with everything. I talked to him even before anything happened, just wishing the best and make sure he’s keeping his head high.”

The safety, 22, added, “He’s gonna come back better than ever. You know, a lot of the time, these ACL injuries when you’re dealing with guys that really care about their bodies and take care of themselves, they always come back stronger. I’m feeling that myself, so I’m excited for Jaylen and I’m praying for him, as well.”

Long before his injury, Harrell was focused on consistently improving his football skills.

“I feel like I’ve always had that prove-it mindset,” he told reporters in 2024. “Coming off a national championship team [in college], you get to the league, and nobody cares about that — you have to show you can do it on the biggest stage.”

Harrell continued at the time, “I always have belief in myself. I am always a hard worker, a go-get-it type dude. So, I feel like that is my mindset. I look at everything like: I’m a seventh-round rookie and I have to go get everything, ain’t nothing I’m going to get, I have to go earn everything, you feel me? I have to go attack each day, day by day.”
