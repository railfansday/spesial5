Candace Cameron Bure Says She Gets “Substantial” Residuals From ‘Full House’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The actress played D.J. Tanner throughout the show's run from 1987 to 1995, and later reprised her role in the sitcom’s spin-off 'Fuller House.'

Candace Cameron Bure is getting candid about the residuals she still earns from Full House.

The actress appeared on a recent episode of The McBride Rewind podcast, where she revealed that she gets “substantial” payouts from the sitcom, which ran for eight seasons from 1987 to 1995.

“I’m always happy when I open that check and deposit it,” Bure added.

The actress played D.J. Tanner, the eldest sister to Stephanie (Jodie Sweetin) and Michelle (Mary-Kate/Ashley Olsen), throughout the show’s run. She also reprised her role in the sitcom’s spin-off Fuller House, which ran for five seasons from 2016 to 2020.

Host Josh McBride also brought up Sweetin’s appearance on the podcast in April 2026, where she shared that her residuals aren’t as great as they used to be.

“I got a one-cent check the other day. No, there’s no syndication anymore because it’s all in streaming. Who gets paid for that? Nobody gets paid for that,” Sweetin said at the time. “Sure, in my 20s, there would be money, but not reliable. You don’t know how much it’s going to be or how often they’re going to run the show. So, sometimes you’re like, ‘Oh, cool. That was nice.’ And then sometimes you’re like, ‘All right, well, there’s a nice dinner out.'”

In response, Bure told McBride, “I actually saw that and she said she doesn’t get much now. And I was like, ‘Jodie, you gotta check with your agent or you gotta call your bank,’ ’cause I still get residuals.”

Elsewhere on the podcast, Bure also addressed the Olsen twins’ decision not to appear on the spin-off. “It was a bummer because it would have been fun to have everybody back,” she said of Fuller House. “But at the same time, it wasn’t surprising just because none of us really have stayed close with the girls.”

“Not because anything went wrong or there was any bad blood. I just think they were kids. They were [filming from] 9 months old until they were 8 years old. They have very different memories than we do. Even me being 10 years older than them, I have significant, great, wonderful memories,” she continued. “They were babies. They probably don’t even remember half the stuff [we do]. They didn’t have the attachment to the crew and the people like we did being older. And they had such a successful fashion business. They haven’t been actors for a long time. They just haven’t been in that part of entertainment.”
