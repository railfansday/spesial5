‘The Hawk’ Starring Will Ferrell Canceled After One Season at Netflix

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

“The Hawk” has hit the rough. Netflix has canceled the golf comedy series after just one season.

The series, which starred Will Ferrell in his first regular TV role since “SNL,” originally debuted on the streamer back in July. While the show did make it into the Netflix global top 10 chart for three weeks upon its release, the cost of the series, which sources say was increased by Ferrell’s salary as star and co-creator, raised the bar for success that would have led to a second season. The official logline for “The Hawk” states “Lonnie Hawkins (Ferrell) ,2004’s number one golfer, struggles on the back nine of his career to recapture his magic. His body says retire, but his heart says he’s not done yet. His ex-wife and his son Lance, golf’s new golden boy, know he’s through. But with one more major to win to complete golf’s Grand Slam, Lonnie refuses to believe he’s anything other than one stroke away from the greatest comeback in golf history.”

Along with Ferrell, the cast included Molly Shannon, Jimmy Tatro, Fortune Feimster, Luke Wilson, Chris Parnell, Katelyn Tarver and David Hornsby.

Ferrell co-created the series with Harper Steele and Chris Hency. All three were executive producers along with Jessica Elbaum and Alix Taylor for Ferrell’s Gloria Sanchez Productions; Rian Johnson, Ram Bergman and Nena Rodrigue for T-Street; David Gordon Green; and Andrew Guest.

While this was Ferrell’s first starring role in a Netflix show, he has produced a number of series at the streamer over the past several years and has multiple projects in the pipeline. He is executive producing new series “All the Rage,” “Prep,” and Nikki Glaser’s first scripted series as part of her Netflix Is a Joke Studios deal, all via Gloria Sanchez Productions.

Deadline first reported the cancellation news.
