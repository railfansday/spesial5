Russell Brand Transferred to Lower Security Prison That Has Yoga

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Russell Brand has been transferred to a lower security prison that has yoga, an astroturf football field and origami ... days after he was sentenced to 10 years and 6 months for rape and sexual assault.

The comedian was moved from London's HMP Wandsworth to HMP Littlehey in Cambridgeshire Friday ... The Sun reported.

Brand's new digs have a lot of amenities ... from two sports halls and single-person cells, to a football league and a choir.

They even have indoor lawn bowling, apparently!

We reported earlier ... Brand's old prison did not allow conjugal visits, and HMP Littlehey -- which allows weekly visitation -- does not permit the privilege either.

As you know ... Brand -- who has maintained that he didn't commit any criminal wrongdoing -- was convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault.
