Taylor Swift Is Getting a Museum of Fine Arts Exhibit in Boston

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

There’s a good chance Taylor Swift fans might be planning their next vacations.

The Museum of Fine Arts, Boston announced Wednesday that they’ll be showcasing a Taylor Swift-themed exhibit, based on the singer’s 12 albums and Eras. “Taylor Swift: The Eras Collection” will only be shown at the Museum of Fine Arts and will run from April 10 to Aug. 15.

“Every era of Taylor Swift’s career introduces a new sound, a new look, a new glimpse into the mind of an artist who has mastered the craft of reinvention,” the announcement read. “Presented at the MFA, a museum renowned for its collections of fashion and musical instruments, “Taylor Swift: The Eras Collection” takes visitors on a journey through Swift’s genre-defying career—exploring how she combines songwriting, performance, and visual storytelling to continually surprise and connect with audiences around the world.”

Opening April 10, the exhibit will include over 50 outfits, sketches, instruments, and more; all themed around performances, music videos, and tours throughout Swift’s discography. This will include her custom red Alberta Ferretti gown from folklore and evermore, the 2022 Midnights bedazzled bodysuit from Zuhair Murad, and the tinsel fringe jacket Swift wore during the “Karma” finale of the Eras Tour. (Swift was wearing the brightly colored jacket when she famously changed the live “Karma” lyrics in 2023 to a Travis Kelce nod: “Karma is the guy on the Chiefs / Coming straight home to me.”)

“This is the first exhibition in the MFA’s history devoted to a musician, and we’re thrilled to invite visitors for an up-close look at the fashion and instruments of one of the most influential cultural figures of our time,” said MFA director of public programs Kristen Hoskins in a statement. “I’m excited to welcome audiences across generations to this unique experience at the Museum.”

Tickets for Taylor Swift: The Eras Collection are $40 and will go on sale Dec. 13 — the singer’s 37th birthday.
