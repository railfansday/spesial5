Leak reveals details for more iPhone 18 models launching early 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Apple is rumored to have three new iPhone models launching in early 2027: iPhone 18, iPhone 18e, and iPhone Air 2. Here’s what to expect from the iPhone 18 models per the latest leak.

Last year when the AI-driven memory crisis started escalating, reports indicated that Apple planned to cut costs with its more budget-friendly iPhone 18 models by using the same manufacturing process for both.

Subsequent rumors indicated iPhone 18 and iPhone 18e might be more similar than usual as a result. And the latest leak offers more evidence of that.

In a post on Weibo, Fixed Focus Digital shares new details about both the iPhone 18 and iPhone 18e based on a leaked image he says has been verified by several sources.

Got to hold and use the Duo over the weekend, want to get it but hesitant due to first gen device status mixed with current pricing. I am still using a 13 Pro since launch, and at the end of the day realize I don’t leverage half of the features so I think there is opportunity to go to a basic iPhone. I wanted the refresh rate primarily but the bar has been raised so much I think I no longer need the pro. On board storage however may be the deciding factor.

Offering the same battery in both devices is likely a cost-cutting measure, but it’s great news for users. Bringing larger batteries alongside the efficiency gains of A20 sounds like a big upgrade.

The move to a 6.3-inch display on iPhone 18e likely means the device will move away from the notch to a Dynamic Island instead. Apple hasn’t made 6.3-inch displays with a notch before, so here’s hoping it doesn’t start now.
