Metro Offers Samsung Galaxy A37 for Free With $40-a-Month Plan

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

While flagship phones tend to get most of the buzz, there are plenty of affordable smartphones that offer many of the features of a top-of-the-line device. Samsung’s latest Galaxy A37 offers dependable everyday performance in an affordable package, and it’s currently available for free with an affordable phone plan from Metro.

The Galaxy A37 features a three-lens rear camera, including an 8 MP ultra-wide, a 50 MP wide-angle, and a 5 MP macro camera. The A37 is also designed for smooth performance. According to Samsung, the phone has CPU and GPU improvements over the previous generation of 12% and 19%, respectively. Battery life is also competitive with flagship phones; the 5,000 mAh battery means you won’t have to reach for the charger, and when you do, the phone can charge from up to 60% in 30 minutes. The offer features the A37 with 128 GB of storage. The phone borrows the sleek aesthetic of the flagship S26 model, and it’s available in lavender or charcoal.

If you need a new phone or a new plan, Metro is offering the Samsung Galaxy A37 for free when you bring your phone number. The activation fee is also waived when you sign up. The affordable plan costs $40/mo. and includes unlimited 5G data, unlimited talk and text, and Scam Shield to block spam calls and spam texts. Metro also offers a five-year price guarantee, so your monthly bill won’t jump up as soon as you sign up. Metro runs on T-Mobile’s network, which is the best mobile network according to Ookla Speedtest and the most 5G coverage nationwide. If you’re in the market for a new phone and carrier but want to keep your number, Metro’s offer is a compelling one.
