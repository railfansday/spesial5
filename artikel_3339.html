‘Real Time’: Bill Maher Jokes ‘GTA VI’ Has Caused “More Workplace Absences Than ICE,” Notes He’s Glad James Bond Cast “Very Heterosexual Male”

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

After Grand Theft Auto VI and James Bond took over social media this week, Bill Maher has added his two cents to the discourse before going on hiatus.

On Friday’s Season 24 finale of Real Time, the comedian expressed his fascination with the “giant cultural phenomenon” following GTA VI‘s multi-billion dollar opening. “No movie ever did that,” he noted.

“Grand Theft Auto came out yesterday, and about a fifth of the young men in America stayed home and didn’t go to work, to play a game,” said Maher in his opening monologue. “This thing has caused more workplace absences than ICE.”

Thursday marked the release of Grand Theft Auto VI from Rockstar Games, the first GTA installment since 2013. Following a record-breaking launch, the highly-anticipated video game has already amassed $3.79B in its first 48 hours. Watch on Deadline

Later in the episode, Maher said, “I am intrigued by it. But again, you are criminals, and you’re doing criminal activities—not all criminal activities, and not unmotivated criminal activities. But it is something disturbing about so many people wanting to do this.

“I heard … you can either choose to be a good boyfriend or a bad boyfriend,” he added. “And the guy said, ‘Why would you choose in a video game to be a good boyfriend?’ You know, people wanna smack the hooker with the car antenna. There’s just something a little disturbing about it.”

Pivoting to Robert Pattinson’s casting as James Bond in Denis Villeneuve’s 007, Maher praised Amazon MGM Studios for the casting choice.

“I think he’s great. I don’t understand what all this hoopla is about it,” he said. “What, is he too millennial, he’s too gay, he’s too fake? He’s great, he’s perfect … He’s 40. Perfect! You need a grown ass man to be James Bond. James Bond’s not a boy.”

Maher added, “And also, I would have been happy with Black James Bond, because you can be James Bond and be Black. I wouldn’t have been happy with a woman or a lesbian James Bond, that’s a different character. James Bond is a very heterosexual male, that’s too essential to not have that be a part of James Bond.”

Real Time‘s Season 24 finale comes after HBO gave the show another two-season renewal earlier this year, with its Season 25 return set for Jan. 15.
