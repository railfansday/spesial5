Talking Point: What Are You Playing This Weekend? - Issue 658

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

This is it, folks: the last WAYP before GTA 6. Can you believe it? It’s been a long road to this point, but we finally made it. Congrats!

I'll be finishing The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time on Switch 2 this weekend. I don't have any nostalgia for the original, so this remake hasn't hit me quite as much as it would a fan, but I can still tell this is something special. I can't even imagine how amazing it must have been to experience at release on the N64.

It's still handheld PlayStation gaming all the way for me, with 2007's PSP game, Castlevania: The Dracula X Chronicles, being one of many reasons why I love my PS Vita.

I made the terrible mistake of purchasing Raccoin and Warhammer Survivors, so you know where this is going...

I've finished Onimusha: Way of the Sword now, and really liked it, so now I'm between big games. I'd quite like to start playing Control Resonant, but there's a QSSR update coming in December, so maybe I'll hold off for now. In the meantime, I'll probably just chill out with some Lumines Arise or Vampire Crawlers.

That's what we're playing this weekend, then, but what are you getting to grips with in the weekend before GTA 6? Just a few more days, folks.

As the Editor of Push Square, Sammy has over 15 years of experience analysing the world of PlayStation, from PS3 through PS5 and everything in between. He’s an expert on PS Studios and industry matters, as well as sports games and simulators. He also enjoys RPGs when he has the time to dedicate to them, and is a bit of a gacha whale.

Donkey Kong Bonanza and look outside before GTA6

God of War (2018) Now I understand why people talk about it so much and why it's held in the highest regards. Everything blew my expectations out of the water. The voice acting was incredible and some of the best I've heard in any game, the visuals impeccable, and the score sounded and felt so powerful. The combat had such perfect flow and I felt like a real warrior, no other game has made me feel that way before. Then there was the story, so phenomenal. Today I listened to the novel of the game read by Mimir himself, all nine-hours of it (Alistair Duncan is the perfect narrator) and I wasn't playing Barbies Rewind as I listened oh no. I was pumping up my hours in what is now one of my favourite digital dopamine delivery systems.

I jumped on the game the second it went live, then after a while (eleven-hours) had to stop so I could give God of War the attention it deserved (and because I was told to go to bed) Now I can once again heed the Emperor's call to purge the Tyranid filth from the universe. It might look like a Vampyre Survivors reskin, it's not. There's so much more to the game, multiple troops onscreen, chainswords, boltguns, bit-boxes, paint pots, power-ups and more dangerous enemies. There are all kinds of lore accurate characters/classes and the tools needed to get the job done. It feels so good to stomp around the battlefield in a suit of armour reducing hordes of monsters to ash while additional troops shoot and cleave their way through with me.

My favourite characters so far are Brother Luca Amasius (boltgun) and Captain Tor Garadon (he rains down rockets everywhere) I may have also bought a couple of Warhammer sets too, and some books but that's a hobby to try for another day. I have some Warhammer audiobooks ready to go for future runs too, the Nightlord books were recommended. Another game launched on the same day, it too is of the dopamine delivery genre.

My first coin pusher game feels more like a drug pusher because once again there's all of that dopamine. Addiction is a strong word, but I can say that yes this game is incredibly addictive and I've never played anything like it. You take control of a greedy little panda who has a thing for shinies and uses a coin pushing machine to get that fix. It's chaotic, there are literal money trees, all kinds of passive perks, flashy quirks. It's all in the name of good fun and in getting those higher numbers. It's the perfect little 'one more run' game.

Continuing to play the GOTY Phantom Blade. I’m about 25 hours in and adoring it. Story is there and very good if you don’t mind reading.

To break it up I may dip back into Valor Mortis or Control Resonant, but my time is set for the next few months. I’ll be skipping GTA6 at launch as I remember 5 being good, but not generational for me.
