Splatoon 3 Version 12.0.1 Update Out Now, Here Are The Patch Notes

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Update [Sat 28th Nov 2026, 12:30am]: Nintendo has now shared the English patch notes for Version 12.0.1 of Splatoon 3. As previously noted, it resolves an issue with the Anarchy Battle. Here's the rundown courtesy of Nintendo's official support page:

Fixes to Issues with Anarchy Battle (Series)

Note: Compatible with Ver. 12.0.0 battle replays. Not compatible with Ver. 11.3.0 or earlier battle replays.

Original Story: Last week, Nintendo released a major update for its team-based squid-shooter Splatoon 3, bumping the game up to Version 12.0.0.

It included changes to multiplayer, weapons adjustments, changes to Anarchy Battle, and even a change to the GameChat feature on Switch 2. Now, following this, Nintendo has released Version 12.0.1. The English patch notes aren't live just yet, but it is only a small update compared to the previous one.

According to a rough machine translation, it includes a fix for Anarchy Battle. As usual, certain features such as local wireless and battle replays may not be compatible with earlier versions of the game. We'll update this post with the English patch notes when they go live.

You can find out more about what Splatoon 3 Version 12.0.0 included in our previous post here on Nintendo Life. In case you missed it, Splatoon Raiders recently got an eShop demo.
