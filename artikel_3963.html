'We Want to Create Franchises That Withstand Decades': Sony Reveals Ambition to Dominate Anime

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Sony’s gradual transition from consumer electronics to entertainment giant is paying dividends, as the firm’s value continues to rise on the back of hits like Spider-Man: Brand New Day and the overall PlayStation business.

But the manufacturer has one big ambition that will bring all its divisions together: anime.

A big Bloomberg report, focusing on subsidiary Aniplex’s boss Atsuhiro Iwakami, shed some light on the firm’s strategy. It’s moving aggressively to dominate an industry that’s booming in popularity worldwide.

Iwakami points to Demon’s Slayer as justification for its approach.

Its first movie, 2020’s Mugen Soul, dethroned Spirited Away to become Japan’s highest-grossing film. Its successor, 2025’s Infinity Castle, went on to gross almost $1 billion worldwide.

Iwakami’s goal is simple: to find or create the next One Piece, a franchise that could become the centrepiece of its entertainment division for potentially decades to come.

As the article points out, anime unifies all of Sony’s divisions in an unprecedented way: content can be created by Aniplex, distributed by Crunchyroll and Sony Pictures, promoted by PlayStation (where there’s an inherent overlap in audiences), and even supported by Sony Music (anime theme songs et al are wildly popular).

To support its ambitions, the company has become one of the biggest investors in light novel powerhouse Kadokawa, and Iwakami admits it would invest in sector stalwart Shueisha as well should the opportunity arise.

But original content will be foundational to Sony moving forwards, as it faces aggressive competition from Netflix and Disney, both of which are competing for content in the wake of the anime boom.

Sony ultimately wants a franchise it owns outright, where it can control all merchandise sales and more.

It recently founded a new studio, Hayate, as part of a joint collaboration between Aniplex and Crunchyroll. Its remit is to create original anime for a global audience.

Next year, Sony will add an anime tab to the PS5’s homepage, giving it another opportunity to promote its content to players around the world.

I’m particularly interested to see if PS Studios is eventually recruited to create original games based on some of these properties, and how it approaches those.

Obviously, if the goal is to create franchises that reach as many people worldwide as possible, then exclusivity may not necessarily be the play here.

But while Sony already has a strong foot in the door, it admits it’s still at the start of its strategy right now. It’s going to be interesting to see how things evolve over the coming years.
