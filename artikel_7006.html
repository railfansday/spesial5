Job Title:License-Eligible Therapist/Counselor Employment Type: W-2 Part-TimeLocation: Remote (U.S.)

Supervision & Start Date: This is a license-eligible position. You will practice under the supervision of a VPM licensed clinician as you work toward full licensure. Because no insurance paneling is required before you begin, you can start right away once background checks and onboarding are complete.

Scheduling & Availability (ET): This is an after-school role. Weekday sessions are scheduled Monday–Friday, 4:00–8:00 PM. Weekend schedules are dependent on clinician availability.

Clinician Availability: You provide your weekly availability upfront, and VPM assigns cases to fill it. Weekday sessions are only scheduled 4:00–8:00 PM. Weekend sessions are scheduled based on clinician availability. You will not work all of those hours.Clinicians will onboard in a ramp format up to a minimum of 10 client sessions per week within 30 days.

Applicant Note: Please do not apply for this position if you are unavailable to attend onboarding on your date of hire. Onboarding is required on day one — four hours starting at 9:00 AM (2 hours organizational onboarding + 2 hours documentation onboarding) — and will most likely fall during your full-time job's business hours. Onboarding hours are paid. If you are unable to take off during business hours, this is not the job for you.

Compensation: $45-50/hr for scheduled client sessions.

Sign-on bonus: $1,500. Payment schedule and terms are provided in the offer letter.

Virtual Peace of Mind (VPM) is a fast-growing, fully remote mental health practice dedicated to providing evidence-based mental health services to children, adults, and families across multiple states. This position is for our Student Assistance Program (SAP), our children's program (Virginia). Our mission is to expand equitable access to mental health services through high-quality virtual care — and to support the clinicians and professionals who make that care possible.

We're building a culture that is mission-driven, accountable, supportive, and built for sustainable remote work.

At VPM, you'll be part of a team transforming access to youth mental health through virtual care — with a remote model designed to support excellent work and real life.

VPM is seeking bilingual Licensed-Eligible Therapists to provide short-term, school-based tele-therapy to K–12 students across Virginia through a state-funded initiative in partnership with the Virginia Department of Education (VDOE).

This position focuses on delivering culturally responsive, evidence-based therapeutic services in English and a second language through a secure, HIPAA- and FERPA-compliant virtual platform. Therapists will play an essential role in expanding access to care for students regardless of insurance coverage, immigration status, or language barriers — including students and families who prefer to receive services in a language other than English.

Residents will receive supervision toward licensure, with opportunities for leadership, school consultation, and program development as they grow within our expanding clinical framework.
