Week In Review: A reversal on Head Start pay and benefits

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

We’re rounding up last week’s news, from a Senate FY27 budget proposal to the latest on kindergarten readiness.

An effort to boost pay and benefits for Head Start employees is being sunset by a U.S. Department of Health and Human Services final rule published Nov. 16. The final rule from the Trump administration reverses a 2024 Biden-era rule that would have raised most Head Start teachers’ salaries by $10,000 in alignment with pay for other public preschool teachers. Supporters expected the Biden-era rule, which would have been fully implemented by 2031, to help Head Start early childhood education programs recruit and retain qualified staff and provide consistent quality programming for children and families.
