The 11 Man Utd players out on loan in 2026-27 & how they’ve been faring

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Manchester United have endured a disastrous start to the 2026-27 season, but how have their loanees fared away from the club?

While new United head coach Mauricio Pochettino is currently focused on getting United out of the relegation zone, he’ll also want to keep an eye on their loanees.

We’ve taken a look at every player that United sent out on loan in the summer and how they’ve performed this season.

The most high-profile player on this list, Onana spent two error-strewn years at Old Trafford before joining Trabzonspor on loan in 2025.

He helped them win the Turkish Cup last season, and they paid a fee in the region of £1million to bring him back to the club for a second loan spell.

The Cameroon international has kept seven clean sheets in 12 appearances in the Turkish Super League this season, and they currently have the best defensive record in the top flight.

He did concede from a corner in a Conference League group stage clash against Finnish side Kuopion Palloseura, but made amends by saving a penalty later in the game.

Karl Darlow’s arrival saw Bayindir deemed surplus to requirements at United, and he joined Celta Vigo on a season-long loan deal.

But the goalkeeper is yet to make a single appearance in LaLiga, as Romanian shot-stopper Ionut Radu is Celta’s clear No. 1.

He has also remained an unused substitute in the Europa League, and his only appearance to date came in a 5-0 win over fourth-tier side Salamanca CF in the Copa del Rey.

Reports in Spain suggest that Celta could look to terminate the loan deal in the January transfer window.

Harrison won the Denzil Haroun Reserve-Team Player of the Year award in the 2023-24 season, before going on loan to Chester and Shrewsbury Town.

The goalkeeper is now on his third temporary spell away from Old Trafford, having joined Scottish Championship club Greenock Morton in the summer.

He endured a difficult start to the season and lost six of his first seven games for the club, but has helped turn their form around in recent weeks.

The fourth goalkeeper on this list, Honor joined United from Blackburn Rovers earlier this year and initially linked up with their Under-21 squad.

He completed a loan move to National League North side Southport in the summer and started the first seven games of the season.

But the 20-year-old was dropped from the team after they signed fellow goalkeeper Douglass Lukjanciks on loan from Everton at the start of September.

He has been training at Carrington in recent weeks, and United could look to send him to another club once the January transfer window opens.

Kone spent the second half of last season on loan at Swiss Super League side Lausanne-Sport, who are also owned by Sir Jim Ratcliffe’s Ineos Group.

The midfielder only made five appearances in an injury-hit spell, but returned to the club in the summer on a season-long loan deal.

He marked his first start of the season with an assist against Young Boys, and followed that up with a goal and an assist in a 6-0 win in the Swiss Cup.

But the 20-year-old was shown a straight red card in a 4-0 defeat against Lugano in September and has since made just two substitute appearances.

After winning the Rotherham United Player of the Season award in 2025-26, Gore returned to League One and joined Luton Town on a season-long loan deal.

An injury disrupted his bright start to the season at Kenilworth Road, but he has found his way back into the team in recent matches.

“He’s done really well Dan, and it was a shame he picked up a slight injury which was a strange one,” Luton manager Jack Wilshere said.

“He just slipped in training and then he was out for two weeks. It kind of killed his rhythm a little bit. He came on the pitch against Colchester and he was one of the real positives from that game as he was slowly, slowly building, so we have to get him back to that place.

“I thought he was good, he’s aggressive, he’s tenacious in midfield. He uses the ball well, he can drive the game forward and someone we’re going to need.”

The 22-year-old midfielder helped Luton book a place in the FA Cup second round by registering an assist against Forest Green Rovers.

Following their success with Gore, Rotherham United went shopping at Old Trafford again and signed Fitzgerald on a season-long loan deal.

The midfielder is being managed by Alex Bruce, who also came through the United academy and is the son of three-time Premier League winner Steve Bruce.

He was initially eased into the team at the start of the season, but has now established himself as one of the first names on the teamsheet.

The 19-year-old has picked up multiple Man of the Match awards and was named their Player of the Month for October after registering two assists.

“I felt Jayce (Fitzgerald) was outstanding again and what a prospect he is,” Bruce said after a 0-0 draw with Grimsby Town.

Moorhouse had interest from Plymouth, Huddersfield, Wigan and Stockport in the summer before joining Dundee United on a season-long loan deal.

The midfielder made just two appearances in the Scottish Premiership before sustaining an anterior cruciate ligament injury in a friendly.

He has returned to United and is now set for a lengthy spell on the sidelines.

After spending the second half of last season on loan at St Mirren, Devaney returned to Scotland and joined Hibernian.

The loan deal also includes an option to make the move permanent at the end of the season, although he is still hoping for a future at Old Trafford.

“I’ve experienced that a lot in the short career that I’ve had so far,” he said. “But, as I said, I’ll back myself to go in at United at the start of next season and impress.”

He started their first four games of the season but came off the bench against Dundee United and was sent off just six minutes later for denying a clear goalscoring opportunity.

In the process, the 19-year-old suffered an ankle injury that kept him on the sidelines for a month, and he has only just returned.

He will hope to be back in the starting line-up by the time Hibernian face Rangers in the Scottish League Cup final next month.

Wheatley was linked with a permanent move to Everton in the summer, but United ultimately decided to send him on loan to Championship side Lincoln City.

The striker made his debut off the bench in a Carabao Cup tie against Blackpool, but then picked up an injury that put him on the sidelines.

He recently returned to full fitness and made his Championship debut, playing 59 minutes in a 1-0 defeat at home to Wrexham.

“There were some flashes of what he’s capable of,” head coach Tom Shaw said. “Ethan, obviously that’s his first start, he’s not had loads of football, but he’s a high-potential young player. He’s got good athleticism, good quality.

“Perhaps he didn’t have enough of those moments and situations to show that today, but we know that the threat is there. That’ll be really good for him, the 60 minutes and the experience.”

Having starred for Arsenal’s academy, Chido Obi arrived at United in 2024 with a reputation as one of football’s most exciting young talents.

The striker made eight first-team appearances in his debut season, but remained with United’s youth sides throughout the 2025-26 campaign.

He joined newly-promoted Eredivisie side Willem II in the summer in search of regular first-team football, but the loan move has not gone to plan.

The 18-year-old has no goals to his name and is yet to start a single game for the club, with all seven of his appearances to date coming off the substitutes’ bench.

He also faced criticism for his lack of defensive work after Willem II went down to 10 men in a domestic cup win over Helmond Sport.

“It is partly a matter of mindset, but also of youthful exuberance,” then-caretaker manager Adrie Koster said. “That boy has never experienced anything like this before.

“He doesn’t do it on purpose either, so we shouldn’t focus everything on him. This is simply part of his learning process. But we are going to talk to him about it.”

United are set to consider recalling the Denmark Under-21 international in the January transfer window.
