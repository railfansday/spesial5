The records that Max Dowman has already broken – & the next he could smash with Arsenal and England

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Max Dowman is a little over a month away from his 17th birthday but he has already racked up an impressive number of records in football.

The Arsenal winger scored a late winner to give England U21s a 3-2 victory over France’s equivalent age group and in doing so, became the youngest scorer for England’s U21 age group.

That adds to a growing list of records the Arsenal man has already achieved.

Youngest goalscorer in Premier League history

While he missed out on the youngest player to feature in the Premier League by a couple of months to his Arsenal teammate Ethan Nwaneri, Dowman is currently the youngest goalscorer in Premier League history.

Dowman netted against Everton in March at the age of 16 years and 73 days, beating Everton’s James Vaughan to the record.

Vaughan scored back in April 2005 when he was 16 years and 270 days old. Other 16-year-olds to score in the top flight include James Milner, Wayne Rooney and Rio Ngumoha.

Youngest player to win the Premier League

Dowman already has a Premier League winners medal and is the youngest to have ever lifted the trophy.

Dowman was 16 years and 144 days when Arsenal lifted the trophy away at Crystal Palace on the final day of last season and took the record off Manchester City’s Phil Foden.

The first title Foden was involved in was the 2017/18 season when Foden was 17 years and 350 days old.

Dowman also had a bigger role than Foden as he played six, scored one and assisted one compared to Foden’s one assist in five games.

Manchester City’s Rico Lewis is third on the list having been 18 years and 188 days when City lifted the title in 2023.

Youngest player to appear in the UEFA Champions League

Dowman also has history in Europe too after he became the youngest player to ever feature in the Champions League.

He was 15 years, 10 months and four days when he appeared in Arsenal’s 3-0 win over Slavia Prague last year, beating the former record set by Dortmund’s Youssoufa Moukoko in 2020.

England Under-21s youngest-ever player and goalscorer

Dowman’s appearance in the 1-0 loss to Portugal earlier this month saw him become the youngest player to ever represent England at the U21 level, beating the previous record set by Jude Bellingham.

His goal against France added yet another record to his tally as he took another record away from Bellingham.

England’s next Ballon d’Or winner: Ranking the 5 favourites after Harry Kane’s historic triumph

With hopefully a long career ahead of him, there are plenty of records Dowman could break.

Dowman has two more chances to become the youngest Champions League winner in history. That record currently belongs to Nwankwo Kanu who was 18 years and 296 days old when Ajax won it in 1995.

Should Arsenal reach the final this season in Madrid, Dowman will be 17 years and 149 days old. Should Arsenal fail this year, Dowman could win it at the age of 18 years and 148 days the following season.

One record Dowman will not be able to achieve is becoming the youngest appearance maker in England history. This international break window was the final chance for Dowman to do it for he will be 17 years and 81 days by the time of the next one in March. Theo Walcott was 17 years and 75 days when he set the record back in 2006.

Given he started so young, Dowman has a good chance to break a lot of Arsenal records too.

The obvious one would be going after the record appearance holder. That currently belongs to David O’Leary who played 722 games from 1975 to 1993.

Dowman is just a tad short of that currently on 19 but say Arsenal play around 60 games a season and Dowman plays in every one of those, he would reach O’Leary in 12 seasons.

Obviously he is not going to play in every single match and so if we are a little more conservative, you could say he would reach that mark around 15/16 seasons from now, should he develop into a starter. 16 seasons from now would put him at 33 which is not over the hill for a footballer by any means.

If someone plays that amount of games, you would also fancy him to have a good chance of becoming Arsenal’s top scorer of all time. That accolade currently belongs to Thierry Henry who scored 228 in 377 games. Dowman is currently on three so plenty of ground to catch up.

Playing that many games will also likely see him come close to the top of the appearances makers in the Premier League. James Milner currently has that title, having played 658 games in the top division. Dowman is currently on eight.
