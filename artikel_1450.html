Confirmed: Devyne Rensch sidelined for 20 days

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Bad news for Gian Piero Gasperini’s Roma, who will be without Devyne Rensch for the next three weeks.

The Dutch full-back, forced off after just a few minutes of the Champions League match against Manchester United, underwent tests to assess the extent of the injury.

The tests confirmed a grade I tear of the right adductor longus muscle, resulting in a sideline of approximately twenty days, which will prevent the former Ajax player from participating in the next league match.

Rensch will certainly miss Roma vs. Sassuolo, scheduled for Saturday evening at the Stadio Olimpico.
