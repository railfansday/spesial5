Crystal Palace already part ways with Pierre Sage after just 5 months

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Pierre Sage’s English dream will have come to an end. Arriving at Crystal Palace with the ambition of discovering the Premier League and taking a new step in his career, the French coach is already leaving the London club, only a few months after his arrival. To try his luck across the Channel, the former RC Lens coach had nevertheless chosen to leave the Artesian project. But the adventure will not have resisted the first negative results. While the leaders hoped to continue the progress of the Eagles, the sporting situation quickly deteriorated, to the point of precipitating the departure of the French coach. “Crystal Palace can confirm Pierre Sage has left his role as manager», We can read in the official press release.

Pierre Sage’s results did not live up to expectations. In ten Premier League matches, the technician has only won two matches and conceded five defeats. A series of bad results which gradually weakened its position, to the point of making its future very uncertain. The last home defeat against Liverpool (1-3) was particularly difficult to take. In the standings, Crystal Palace is now bottom. A worrying observation for a team which hoped to establish itself permanently in the elite of English football and continue its progression under the direction of its new coach.

The difficulties are not limited to the championship. Engaged in the Europa League, Crystal Palace has only collected five points after four days. An insufficient result to calmly approach the rest of the competition and which illustrates the difficulties encountered by the club since the start of the season. Pierre Sage will never have succeeded in establishing a sufficiently solid dynamic to reassure his management. The defeats piled up, the results did not follow and the pressure became too great. After trying to stay the course, the London leaders decided to turn the page and put an end to a collaboration which did not meet their expectations.

To prepare for the future, Crystal Palace is already studying several profiles likely to take over. Thomas Frank is among the favorites to succeed the French technician, with Sean Dyche as an alternative. Free since his departure from Nottingham Forest in February, Dyche has solid experience in the fight for maintenance. At Burnley, he achieved two promotions to the Premier League and kept the club in the top flight for six consecutive seasons. He also managed to save Everton from relegation. For Pierre Sage, the English adventure is already coming to an end, far from the dream that pushed him to leave Lens.
