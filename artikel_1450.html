Watch: Milan put five goals past Frosinone in statement win – the highlights

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

AC Milan ensured they made the perfect return after the international break as they blew Frosinone away at San Siro.

It looked like a tough game on paper against a newly-promoted side who have surprised many already this season, and would have gone back into the European places had they got even a point away from home.

However, the performance from Milan was a ruthless one as they scored five times. Gabriele Calvani’s own-goal was a gift to get things going, before Goncalo Ramos finished a sweeping counter-attack to make it 2-0, but Masini halved the deficit before the break.

Ardon Jashari scored within three minutes of coming on to get his first for the club with a rocket of a shot. Alphadjo Cisse got his ninth of the campaign off the bench (after a VAR review for offside), then Christian Pulisic finished yet another counter to make it a rout.
