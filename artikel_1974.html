In your opinion, what are the key problems that Newcastle United face?

Not the greatest start to the season, so where has it all gone wrong compared to qualifying for the Champions League and winning the Carabao Cup in 2025?

After a poor 2025-26 season and the departures of Bruno Guimarães, Anthony Gordon and Sandro Tonali, the club needed to bring in proven replacements.

Instead, Newcastle United have signed young, unproven players. Bazoumana Touré, Aladji Bamba and others are not ready to replace players like Gordon and Tonali. There is also no guarantee that they will ever reach that level.

The summer recruitment simply wasn’t good enough.

Most Newcastle United fans still support Matthias Jaissle but there are concerns about the way the team is playing.

Some have criticised Jaissle’s tactics, he uses the Red Bull philosophy which is built around intense pressing, quick transitions, vertical attacks and athletic players. While this style can be effective, it is not always easy to get right.

Newcastle Unite need to find a way to make these tactics work with the players they have. In my opinion the performances have not been good enough, questions are starting to be asked.

Another major problem is the lack of experience in the Newcastle United squad, young players need time to develop.

When you are trying to compete in the Premier League you need experienced players in the here and now who can handle pressure and help the team through difficult periods.

Losing experienced players has left a big gap in the squad and Newcastle United need players who can make an immediate impact, not just players who might become good in a few years.

In my opinion, Newcastle United seem to have lost some of the identity that made them successful. The team used to play with energy, intensity and passion, difficult to play against.

Performances have become inconsistent, supporters need to see a team that fights for every ball and gives everything for the badge.

Newcastle United need to get their recruitment right, the club cannot overly rely on young players who are not ready to make an immediate difference.

They also need to find consistency under the manager. The tactics need to suit the players, the team needs to show the same energy and desire that made them successful in the past.

There is still time for Newcastle United to turn things around but changes need to happen soon.
