Scotland team to play North Macedonia: where McTominay fits in, new-look defence, striker decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Predicted Scotland XI for Nations League clash at Hampden on Friday

Sebastien Pocognoli will bid for his first Hampden victory at the third attempt when Scotland host North Macedonia on Friday.

The Scotland head coach endured an underwhelming start to his tenure last month by picking up just four points from a possible 12 in the Nations League while losing both of his home openers against Switzerland and Slovenia.

The away performances at least provided some encourgament with a strong second half in the goalless draw against Slovenia in Ljubljana in his first match in charge and a dominant 2-0 win over North Macedonia in Skopje.

However, the 2-1 defeat by Slovenia at Hampden ended his maiden camp on a sour note and raised questions over whether the squad is suited to the manager's preferred system, with Pocognoli using a 3-4-2-1 or 3-4-1-2 formation across his first four games.

The squad has been bolstered by the return of Scott McTominay, Kieran Tierney and Che Adams but defenders Stephen Welsh and Luis Binks have withdrawn and replaced by Ryan Porteous and David Bates, while Scott McKenna is also absent.
