YouTube rolling out app redesign without Subscriptions tab, and everyone hates it

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

After the change was announced last month, YouTube is now rolling out its mobile app redesign with a new navigation bar and no dedicated Subscriptions tab and initial reactions tell a pretty clear story – everyone hates it.

In its biggest change in around a decade, YouTube’s mobile app is ditching its five-button navigation bar for a new look that focuses on Shorts and creation, with a new “Home” page that includes recommendations, “Subs,” and more.

The biggest change here is that YouTube no longer has a dedicated Subscriptions tab in its app, with that now a swipe away on the new “Home” tab. Functionally, it’s not all that different, but it breaks a decade of muscle memory and shuffles the priority of content in the app.

We’re seeing the new YouTube redesign on Android today, with many also seeing it on iOS. It appears to be rolling out via a server-side change rather than a specific app update.

Just an FYI in case anyone doesn't know this or forgot about it but YT app has app shortcuts built-in so if you long press on the app icon one of the options you'll see is Subscriptions with a + next to it if you want the app to open straight into subscriptions like I do.

YouTube’s new app does come with the perk of being able to create a “custom feed” on that Home tab, though.
