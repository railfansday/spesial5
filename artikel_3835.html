GTA 6 Fashion Is Taking Social Media by Storm

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

GTA 6 is not only taking the gaming world by storm – it’s also taking the fashion world by storm, it seems.

While you don’t get to create your own character in Rockstar’s latest release, you can style protagonists Jason and Lucia however you like.

And while I had fun with this aspect during the review period, I’m flabbergasted by the creations people are coming up with.

I think what’s really interesting is how every individual Jason and Lucia is becoming a reflection of each player’s personality.

You can see the contrast in the two styles below.

One couple looks like they’re about to attend a music festival while the other looks like they’re about to go to an art gallery:

And then you have this version which looks like it’s stepped straight off the set of an expensive fragrance commercial:

The game is so malleable, with such a range of expression, that you can kinda present the characters however you like.

But this one looks like a 00s pop punk singer:

And it’s not just the makeup and hairstyles either – it’s the full range. Look at these ensembles put together by one user:

Pair all of that with one of the best photo modes ever included in a game, allowing you to place dynamic studio lighting, and you have a surprising outlet for creativity:

Never, in my wildest dreams, did I imagine I’d be writing an article like this about a GTA game. But it’s just a testament to the groundbreaking experience Rockstar has delivered.
