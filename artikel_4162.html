The Great Outdoors Walkthrough In World of Warcraft: Forever

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Stuck on The Great Outdoors quest? Quick fix for World of Warcraft: Forever camping bug and Boosted Rest.

World of Warcraft: Forever introduces several new features, including a camping system that provides useful buffs to help you explore Azeroth. One of the first quests you'll encounter that uses this system is The Great Outdoors, a short introduction to camping. If you've landed on this guide, I'm sure you may be looking for some extra help here.

Although the quest seems straightforward, I found that one objective doesn't always register properly. If you're having the same problem, here's how to complete the quest and get around the issue. We detail everything you need to know, below.

You can start The Great Outdoors early in your adventure, regardless of your race. The quest giver and location vary by starting area, but the objectives remain the same.

Here's how to complete The Great Outdoors:

While resting, you'll see the Welcoming Campfire effect counting down. Once the timer finishes, you'll receive Boosted Rest and complete the main objective.

Stop losing friends to phasing in World of Warcraft: Forever — quick world refresh tips to get your party back in Azeroth.

When completing The Great Outdoors, I found that the Sit near campfire objective didn't always register, even when I was sitting beside the correct fire.

To fix this, press X to sit next to the fire. Then, type /sit into the chat while already seated. This should help the game recognize that you are sitting. Look for the Welcoming Campfire icon in the top-right corner, where your normal buffs appear, to confirm it was triggered.

Once you've completed The Great Outdoors, you'll be introduced to additional camping quests, including ones that teach you about various professions like mining, cooking, and herbalism. Within these professions, you can craft new items that provide various buffs when added to a camp.

The camping system becomes more useful as you level, so be sure to use the bonuses and meet friends along the way.
