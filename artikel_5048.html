Strategy’s STRC stock crashes to $95 despite promise of 44 years of dividends

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

STRC has fallen toward $95 despite $2.7 billion in buybacks, including $1.4 billion spent near $100, as Peter Schiff warns of potential Bitcoin sales.

Strategy's (formerly MicroStrategy) STRC preferred stock plunged to its lowest level since August on Wednesday despite the company's multibillion-dollar repurchase campaign.

The Michael Saylor-led company's preferred shares fell to $95.51 before recovering to $96.16, according to Strategy's data. The decline left STRC roughly 4% below its $100 stated amount, pushing its annualized yield to approximately 12.5%.

The selloff comes despite Strategy spending about $2.67 billion repurchasing STRC shares since July to stabilize the security near $100.

An earlier analysis found that the company spent about $1.4 billion on repurchases between Sept. 28 and Nov. 15, with weekly average purchase prices ranging from $99.06 to $99.93.

This sustained intervention has left just $332.2 million available under its $3 billion preferred-stock repurchase authorization, raising questions about how much more capital Strategy will commit if selling pressure persists.

Despite the renewed weakness, Strategy CEO Phong Le has defended STRC's financial structure and its ability to generate income for investors.

“STRC is Digital Credit: high income, low volatility, and 44 years of dividend coverage. Built for income investors as TradFi and DeFi converge.”

His assessment contrasts with economist Peter Schiff, who warned that the security's continued decline could eventually force Strategy to sell Bitcoin.

“Despite the buybacks, $STRC is collapsing. At $96 the current yield is 12.5% and rising.”

Schiff argued that Strategy would have to exhaust its cash reserves to support the stock before selling Bitcoin to finance additional repurchases. He suggested raising or suspending dividends as an alternative.

However, Le's 44-year coverage assertion reflects Strategy's broader Bitcoin-backed financial capacity.

The company's latest disclosures showed that its $4.75 billion dedicated dollar reserve and $151.2 million in unrestricted cash provide approximately 3.4 years of coverage for preferred dividends and debt-interest obligations.

Strategy also held 850,849 BTC as of Nov. 15, providing another source of liquidity through potential asset sales.

The company has already authorized Bitcoin sales to support its preferred-stock obligations and repurchase programs, although it has recently relied more heavily on common-stock issuance.
