Binance’s $6.5B freeze order, Hong Kong ‘suspicious’ of HYPE: Asia Express

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Binance obtains court orders freezing assets stolen in October’s hack. Hong Kong warns citizens about Hyperliquid, and Thai police arrest four foreigners linked to a crypto scam.

Binance has secured a $6.5 billion worldwide freezing injunction from the Abu Dhabi Global Market Courts as it seeks to recover assets stolen in its record Oct. 29 hack.

The court orders target unidentified perpetrators of the attack and are intended to help issuers, exchanges and other partners freeze and return stolen assets,

The orders can be served via blockchain, allowing legal notices to be delivered to wallet addresses rather than through traditional channels. Market participants will also be notified of updates to a “living list” of 26,000 wallet addresses identified as holding stolen assets.

Despite losing billions in the hack, Binance’s APAC head SB Seker told Reuters this week its finances are sound and it plans to expand throughout South East Asia.

“We have ongoing conversations with at least five or six markets ... three of which are in quite mature stages,” he said. Binance currently has operating licenses in 15 markets.

Hong Kong’s Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) added decentralized perps exchange Hyperliquid to its Alert List. The SFC said Hyperliquid lacks a license to operate in the city and is suspected of actively marketing its services to the region’s public.

The SFC is now officially suspicious of 69 platforms.

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority and Swift will explore linking the HKMA’s market infrastructure, Project Ensemble, with Swift’s ledger, for cross-border payments using tokenized deposits in multiple currencies.

The Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) issued 100 million Singapore dollars ($77.5 million) in tokenized bills on Nov. 6, with banks using wholesale central bank digital currency (CBDC) to pay for them. Five banks submitted bids totaling more than twice the amount on offer, MAS said Thursday. The four-week bills mature on Dec. 4.

MAS issued the bills directly on the Singapore Dollar Test Network. That gives borrowers an asset they could pledge as collateral for loans on the network, the central bank said.

Singapore’s DBS has partnered with Circle to broaden customer access to USDC across Asia.

CryptoJobsList has acquired its rival crypto jobs service Crypto.jobs.

Wisdom Tree has registered three tokenized funds in Singapore and is working with Synthesys to make them available through regulated intermediaries.

Citadel Securities is expanding its Singapore and Sydney offices by 40% and 65% respectively.

The Shanghai Municipal Science and Technology Commission and the Shanghai Municipal Bureau of Data have issued a new policy that encourages the adoption of blockchain and other technologies as a way to share and track scientific data.Meanwhile the Cyberspace Administration of China has clarified the names of blockchain information services are not allowed to include words like “virtual currency”, “exchange” and “ICO”

Agribusiness solutions company Bunge and feed ingredient trading firm Bangkok Produce Merchandising Company have expanded their blockchain based soy traceability initiative to Vietnam. The collaboration has already established traceable soybean and soybean meal supply chains from Brazil to Thailand.

Thai police have arrested four foreign nationals in Pattaya over an alleged scam that defrauded victims of more than 400 million baht ($11.8M) through fake crypto investments.The operation followed the rescue of a 19-year-old Chinese student held for ransom three days earlier and uncovered a crypto romance scam allegedly run by three Chinese nationals and one Taiwanese fugitive.
