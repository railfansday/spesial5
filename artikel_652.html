Newsmax Fears Fox Freeze-Out on Roku if Merger Is Not Blocked

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Newsmax filed an antitrust lawsuit on Thursday that seeks to block the Fox-Roku merger, arguing that the deal gives Fox unlawful monopoly power over the free streaming market.

Fox Corp. closed the $22 billion acquisition earlier this month. The Department of Justice cleared the deal the same day, announcing that Fox had made commitments to keep Roku as an “open and neutral” platform. But in its lawsuit, Newsmax alleges that those commitments are unenforceable, and raises fears that Fox will deprive it of a key distribution channel.

“No single media company should have the power to determine which news voices Americans can see or hear,” said Christopher Ruddy, the CEO of Newsmax, in a statement. “Fox already dominates cable news and has repeatedly sought to undermine Newsmax as a competitor. Now Fox wants to control the very platforms and technology that millions of Americans use to access news and information.”

According to the complaint, Fox will control some 70% of the free, ad-supported streaming TV market through its ownership of Roku and Tubi.

“Fox has a long history of anticompetitive animus toward Newsmax,” the lawsuit states. “Most notably, Fox has refused to carry Newsmax on Fox’s own free streaming service, Tubi. Now that Fox has acquired Roku – along with (the Roku Channel) – the risk that Fox will further degrade distribution of Newsmax’s content is clear.”

The complaint was filed in the Northern District of California.

In response, Fox Corp. noted that regulators have already approved the deal.

“Newsmax’s complaint, which comes after a rigorous review process by regulatory agencies, is wholly without merit,” a Fox spokesperson said. “Upon closing, we commenced actively integrating this approved transaction for the benefit of consumers, partners and other stakeholders.”

Newsmax filed a separate antitrust suit against Fox last year in federal court in Florida, accusing its rival of using aggressive and illegal negotiating tactics to block it from getting wider cable and satellite distribution. The judge in that case, Aileen Cannon, dismissed it almost immediately as an impermissible “shotgun” pleading.

Newsmax then refiled in Wisconsin, where a judge granted Fox’s motion to transfer the case back to Florida. Discovery in the case is on hold pending the outcome of a motion to dismiss.
