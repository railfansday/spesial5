Starbucks drops new viral Bearista cups: How to buy Aspen, Reiny, Bunny, Joy, and ultra-rare Goldie

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

You're probably feeling pretty smug right now. You survived the first wave of Starbucks holiday merch without going into total financial ruin. Congrats! You've clearly grown. You're an adult who's strong enough to withstand even the craftiest of marketing tricks. Right?

Wrong. The second wave is even more tempting. At this point, our hands are tied. It's basically impossible to resist the call of the bear.

Starbucks’ viral star is returning to shelves with a collectible twist. Launching on Nov. 10, Starbucks is releasing the Bearista Glass Cold Cup Surprise Box Edition ($39.95) at participating U.S. coffeehouses. Each sealed box hides one of five festive glass Bearista cups, complete with a green straw, a matching lid, and a custom personality card that breaks down their holiday lore, seasonal vibe, and signature drink order.

I don't need to tell you that the whole world will go absolutely mad for these cups.

Starbucks is capping in-store purchases at two boxes per customer. A limited quantity will be available exclusively for Starbucks Rewards Reserve members on the Starbucks Shop starting Nov. 9 at 1 p.m. ET (limited to one per member).
