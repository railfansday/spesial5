Spike Lee and Willem Dafoe Board Short Film ‘Pankaja’ as Exec Producers (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Oscar winner Guneet Monga Kapoor has also joined Anooya Swamy's film, which premiered at Sundance.

Spike Lee is reuniting with his “Inside Man” star Willem Dafoe, with the two having boarded live-action short film “Pankaja” as exec producers. Guneet Monga Kapoor, who won the 2023 Oscar for her doc short “The Elephant Whisperers” has also joined the Oscar-qualified project.

From writer-director Anooya Swamy — who previously worked as Lee’s teaching assistant — ”Pankaja” is set in the Indian city of Bangalore and stars Harshini Boyalla as a mother who sets out with her young daughter Lalli (played by Padmashree G in her screen debut) to find her missing husband. As they search through the city’s informal settlements, the strains on Pankaja’s marriage and family gradually come into view. The film had its world premiere at the 2026 Sundance Film Festival and went on to win best live action short at Raindance and best student film at both Palm Springs International ShortFest and HollyShorts. “Pankaja” has now screened at more than 40 festivals and had a month-long theatrical run at New York’s IFC Center.

“Pankaja is evocative poetic events, every story a frame unto itself,” Bittersweet. Authentic. A small gem,” said Dafoe.

“Indian cinema has an extraordinary ability to bring deeply local stories to audiences everywhere,” said Monga Kapoor, whose credits “The Lunchbox,” “Gangs of Wasseypur,” “Masaan” and “Kill.”

“In ‘Pankaja,’ Anooya Swamy tells a story that asks urgent questions about whose labour we depend on and whose lives we overlook. Behind every invisible worker is a family living with the consequences of that uncertainty. I’m proud to join as executive producer and help bring this powerful new Indian voice, and the people at the heart of her film, to a wider audience.”

“Pankaja” is also supported by The Culture Fund, an impact fund investing in filmmakers and stories with the power to shape culture.
