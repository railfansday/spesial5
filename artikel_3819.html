Rockstar Wouldn’t Confirm Or Deny If That’s Studio President Sam Houser In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

CameoGTA 3GTA 6Rob NelsonRockstar GamesSam Houser

Grand Theft Auto 6 contains some surprising and fun cameos and moments, including what appears to be a strange appearance by Rockstar Games co-founder and company president Sam Houser. I asked Rockstar about this cameo and got some laughs and (mostly) confirmation that it’s him in the game.

During a pre-release interview with Rockstar’s head of production Rob Nelson, who acted as basically the director behind GTA 6, I asked a lot of questions about things I found while playing the newly released open-world sequel for over 150 hours. And I had to ask about a strange cameo that happens late in the middle half of the game’s main story. An older man appears wearing a shirt featuring the Rockstar Games logo. He’s called Little Sam and doesn’t say anything. All signs pointed to this being Sam Houser.

So I asked Nelson about this cameo and got a laugh from him and Rockstar PR in the Zoom chat.

“Little Sam?” replied Nelson with a smirk. “I mean, I can’t I can confirm or deny it. I think it’ll be very interesting when fans see that.” Nelson then laughed more. I pointed out that Sam Houser had a voice cameo as a gun store clerk in GTA 3, something Nelson was well aware of, and so it seemed very likely this was him. Little Sam looks a lot like Houser in this recent pic posted online a year ago.

“[He was in GTA 3,] so I wouldn’t be surprised if that’s him. I wouldn’t be surprised,” playfully responded Nelson. “That’s little Sam; his name is little Sam. He don’t say much.”
