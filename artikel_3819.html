Firaxis Games lays off a third of staff

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Firaxis Games, the 2K-owned studio behind the Civilization and XCOM series, has laid off around 60 people today, or about a third of its staff, according to a Bloomberg report citing people familiar with the matter.

Studio head Heather Hazen will depart as part of the cuts, a 2K spokesperson confirmed.

“Firaxis is restructuring and recentering around the studio’s core strength—designing world class strategy games that have set the industry standard for the genre for the last 30 years,” the spokesperson said.

They continued, “As part of these changes, the size of the studio is being reduced to focus on design, technology and the player experience, which means saying goodbye to talented colleagues who helped build Firaxis. These decisions are never easy, but they’re essential to build the next chapter of Firaxis.”

Bloomberg reports that Firaxis Games has been working on a new XCOM game that has faced development struggles and missed a significant milestone over the summer. Development will continue, but may face a shakeup as a result of the layoffs.
