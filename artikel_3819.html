Wuthering Waves Becomes a Stealth Iron Man Game in Next PS5 Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

If the wait for EA’s upcoming Marvel’s Iron Man is proving too tough to take, then Wuthering Waves’ forthcoming milestone v4.0 update may scratch your Tony Stark itch.

In this exclusive IGN footage of the gacha game’s new ‘Embers of Everbright’ arc – scheduled to release on 22nd December – dev Kuro has introduced the new Strider Mechsuit.

The new utility factors in both combat and exploration, and the studio says:

“Beyond combat, Version 4.0 introduces exciting new opportunities for open-world exploration, allowing players to pilot the Strider Mechsuit with full 360-degree mobility, take to the skies and traverse the region with greater freedom.”

But the new update will not only explore the skies – you’ll also be able to go underwater as well, massively increasing the scope of the sandbox.

Kuro says its new ‘Chapter of the Year’ format will span multiple updates, so expect a bigger, deeper narrative set to unfold over the course of 2027 here.

Two new characters that will feature in that story are Anna and Lucielle, and you can get a glimpse of their respective combat in the trailer above.

Again, it all looks amazing, with the time being the only thing holding me back from these many, many games at this point.

Kuro says it’ll reveal more about v4.0 ahead of its release next month, but this is just a first taste to whet your appetite.
