Eight years on since the launch of its first chapter, Deltarune seems to be rapidly approaching completion. With five chapters already released for Toby Fox's sequel to the beloved RPG Undertale, two more are expected before the game is complete. And according to Deltarune creator Toby Box, not only is chapter 6 close to being finished, but work has also already begun on chapter 7.

Fox provided the status update in his most recent newsletter. Kicking off with a general note that chapter 6 is "progressing well", Fox explains that "the game is now a structurally complete draft that can be played from the beginning to the end. He further elaborates that "all the maps, events and battles exist", with the exception of one optional event that's still being worked on.

As a result, the Deltarune team is now at the point where they feel comfortable commencing localisation and preliminary testing. Fox clarifies, however, that full testing won't begin just yet, as "some important scenes and details are still undercooked in their presentation". Indeed, while chapter 6 is progressing rapidly, players shouldn't expect an imminent release. "Even though chapter 6 is a rather short chapter, I would like it to be as good as possible. I won't be able to rush this," Fox points out.

As for Chapter 7, Fox says that development has "only just started". Designers who have finished their Chapter 6 work have moved on to work on the new chapter, while Fox has shared some of the dialogue and events with the Deltarune team. "Just when the story reached a critical moment, I stopped reading. I told my team they need to complete more of the game before I tell them what happens," he explained, adding that "Everyone agrees it's more fun this way."

It's impressive progress, considering that chapter 5 released in June. Then again, development of Deltarune has accelerated rapidly since 2025, when Fox released chapters 3 and 4 of the game in the same year. The launch of chapter 5 also demonstrated just how much fans want to see the story through, as 300,000 concurrent players flocked to Steam to check out the latest chunk of the game.
