As the name suggests, the Garmin Forerunner 265 is designed with runners in mind. If you're training for an upcoming marathon or simply trying to improve your daily run times, the Forerunner 265 serves as a great assistant. It supplies all the data you could possibly want. You'll get a morning report with recovery and training outlooks for the day, a training readiness score, personalized workouts, training tips, race performance stats, course details, and more. After each run, you can even zero in on specifics like cadence, stride length, ground contact time, and HRV. At $150 off, this early Black Friday deal brings it down to its best price on record.

It's the ultimate lifestyle watch, designed to help you understand your body better and make healthier choices. It's loaded with over 80 preloaded GPS and indoor sports apps, lets you log lifestyle behaviors like caffeine or alcohol intake, lasts up to 10 days per charge, and even packs a built-in LED flashlight. Not to mention, it looks gorgeous on your wrist. As an early Black Friday treat, the Venu 4 is $100 off for the first time ever. No need to wait until Black Friday to buy.
