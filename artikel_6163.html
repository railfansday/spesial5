Taking a cruise is even better when you get a great deal — and if you’re looking to book right now for a cruise (or cruises) in 2027 and beyond, your timing couldn’t be better. Cruise lines have launched Black Friday deals that include discounted fares, cabin upgrades, third- and fourth-guest-free promos, and onboard credits.

We’ve found the best promotions from leading ocean and river cruise lines, lesser-known small-ship operators and expedition brands. So whether your goal is beach time in the Caribbean, wildlife-spotting in Alaska, seeing the northern lights in the Arctic or enjoying culture and cuisine along Europe’s rivers, there’s a deal for every type of cruiser.

When considering Black Friday offers, however, remember that cruise demand remains high, so selection may be limited and fares — especially on the newest ships — will likely be pricier than in years past. To truly get a good deal, make sure you’re comfortable with the final price quoted, including the exact fare per person and the value of any added perks, before booking. Calling the cruise line’s reservations department may help clarify any questions.

Here’s a look at the Black Friday deals being offered by more than 40 cruise lines. Check back soon for Cyber Monday deal updates.

Through Dec. 3, AmaWaterways has a Black Friday offer that lets you choose between a complimentary pre- or postcruise land package or cruise fare savings of up to $2,500 per cabin (double occupancy). You’ll also receive a free cabin upgrade on select 2027 and 2028 sailings in Europe and South America.

Small-ship operator American Cruise Lines’ current cruise deals and special offers include free domestic airfare and savings of $1,000 to $2,000 per cabin on select late 2026 and 2027 departures. This applies to its eight-night Florida Gulf Coast and Keys sailings, eight-night Southeast Sea Islands cruises, eight-night Columbia and Snake River sailings, and eight-night Lower Mississippi River cruises.

From Nov. 24 to 28, you can enjoy Black Friday savings of up to 55% on select expedition cruises from Atlas Ocean Voyages and also receive air credit savings of up to $2,000 per suite on qualifying voyages. Through Nov. 23, guests and travel advisers may also submit priority reservations for eligible expeditions on the Atlas Ocean Voyages website ahead of the five-day Black Friday promotional event. Reservations will begin confirming Nov. 24, subject to availability, with Atlas Yacht Club members receiving first priority to secure special Black Friday savings once the sale begins.

Through Dec. 12, Avalon Waterways is offering a Black Friday Sales Event featuring free gratuities and free airfare on select 2027 European river cruises. Avalon’s offer also includes savings of up to $2,000 per couple on a Panorama or Royal Suite upgrade on certain 2027 departures.

The Black Friday Flash Sale at Azamara, available now through Dec. 4, includes up to $6,000 off per cabin on select sailings in the Mediterranean, South America, Japan and Alaska, plus up to $750 in onboard credit per cabin. The deal is valid on all four Azamara ships for sailings from January 2027 to April 2028. You can book a sale voyage online or via a travel adviser.

Carnival's Black Friday deals span Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Travel Tuesday with promotions that include discounted fares (four-night sailings start at under $200 per person), $49 per person deposits, room upgrades starting at $1, "Kids Sail Free" offers on select cruises (in third and fourth bunks), discounted beverage and Wi-Fi and shore excursion packages, and up to $50 in onboard credits. Sales and promotions are rolling out through Dec. 1.

Celebrity Cruises has a rolling Black Friday promotion offering savings of 75% on the second guest in the cabin on hundreds of applicable sailings in 2027 and 2028, including Caribbean and Mediterranean voyages on its newest ship, Celebrity Xcel. There are also limited-time offers that include up to $1,000 in additional savings per cabin plus $100 savings on select European sailings. This offer, for example, is valid through Nov. 24.

Through Dec. 2, Greek cruise line Celestyal has a “Big Sailaway” Black Friday promotion offering savings of up to 50%, plus third and fourth guests in a cabin sail free, on 58 Mediterranean and Greek islands voyages (from three nights to 14 nights) departing between March 2027 and February 2028. Celestyal operates two mid-size ships, Celestyal Journey and Celestyal Discovery.

Italy-based Costa Cruises is celebrating the start of the holiday season with a Black Friday sale offering fares as low as $399 per person (double occupancy) on select departures through June 2027, when reserved by Nov. 30. The included regions are the Caribbean (on Costa Fascinosa and Costa Favolosa), the Canary Islands (on Costa Smeralda) and the Mediterranean (on Costa Pacifica, Costa Toscana, Costa Smeralda, Costa Fascinosa and Costa Favolosa).

Through Dec. 7, small luxury cruise line Cruise Croatia is offering Black Friday savings of up to $500 per cabin on select seven-night spring and summer 2027 Adriatic sailings between Dubrovnik and Split, Croatia.

Book by Dec. 2 to take advantage of the Seasonal Savings deal offered by luxury cruise line Crystal. It can save you up to 40% on more than 200 upcoming voyages worldwide on Crystal Serenity or Crystal Symphony. The discount applies only to the first and second guest in a cabin. Full World Cruise and Full Grand Journeys are excluded.

Through Dec. 7, the Cunard Black Friday event can save you up to 40% on select sailings aboard Cunard's four ships — Queen Elizabeth, Queen Victoria, Queen Mary 2 and Queen Anne — with fares starting at $999 per person. Plus, you’ll enjoy a 10% refundable deposit and an onboard credit. Deals are available on Caribbean, Northern Europe, Mediterranean, transatlantic and Canada sailings. Guests booking Grill Suites will also have their Hotel Charge waived.

Although Disney Cruise Line doesn’t have Black Friday-specific deals, it is currently offering savings of up to 25% on select Caribbean sailings from Fort Lauderdale, Port Canaveral and Galveston, Texas; it's also offering deals on Mexican Riviera sailings from San Diego. These three- to seven-night cruises must be booked under the "guaranteed stateroom with restrictions" category. (In this case, "guaranteed" means you can’t select your specific room, but you're guaranteed one in the category you’ve booked or higher.) Your specific cabin will be assigned at a later date, but a full, nonrefundable payment is due at the time of booking.

Through Dec. 1, save up to 40% with Black Friday offers when you book select sailings with luxury cruise line Explora Journeys. Choose from two options: Full Payment Fare with savings of up to 40% (full payment is required at the time of reservation and is nonrefundable and cannot be changed), or Flexible Fare with savings of up to 30% (a 5% deposit is required, and standard cancellation policies apply).

Grand Circle Cruise Line is offering Black Friday sales to travelers 50 and older with savings of up to $3,000 per person on select January to July 2027 river cruises in Europe. Or you can get a $500 per-person discount plus an additional 10% off all 2027 departures when you pay in full. You can reserve by Nov. 30 by calling 800-221-2610 with code "BLACKFRIDAY26."

Available through Nov. 29, the Black Friday offer from Holland America Line is valid on more than 800 cruises to destinations worldwide, departing from December 2026 through spring 2029. The promotion includes up to 30% off cruise fares, free prepaid crew appreciation, 50% reduced deposits and free or reduced third and fourth guests in the same cabin. You can also combine the Black Friday offer with Holland America’s "Have it All" premium fare, which adds four high-value amenities: shore excursions, specialty dining, Wi-Fi and a beverage package.

Available through Dec. 2, Hurtigruten’s annual Black Friday sale offers savings of up to 50% on its 2027 Original Voyages, including the Coastal Express, which has cruised the Norwegian coast since 1893. Hurtigruten is also offering savings of up to 30% on its all-inclusive Signature Voyages in 2027, which include the North Cape Line and the Svalbard Line.

Through Dec. 2, HX Expeditions (formerly Hurtigruten Expeditions) is offering as much as 40% off select departures from late 2026 to 2028 with its Black Friday promotion. The sale offers savings on more than 50 all-inclusive expedition cruises to some of the world’s most remote destinations, including Antarctica, Greenland, Svalbard, the Galapagos Islands and the Northwest Passage.

Through Nov. 29, you can enjoy 60% off thanks to Black Friday savings on eligible itineraries through 2028 across the Jimmy Buffett-inspired Margaritaville at Sea fleet, plus third and fourth guests cruise for free on select sailings. These deals include sailings on the new Margaritaville at Sea Beachcomber, debuting in January 2027, as well as a buy-one-get-one deal on $499 Cruise Certificates that you can use on future voyages.

Through Nov. 30, receive a free veranda upgrade, plus a $250 onboard credit per cabin on eight Mitsui Ocean Cruises voyages sailing from February to April 2027. Mitsui operates two 458-guest, all-suite ships (formerly part of the Seabourn fleet) that are centered on authentic Japanese hospitality. It offers six- to 12-night itineraries in Japan that call on ports rarely visited by larger cruise lines. Both Japanese and English are spoken on board.

MSC Cruises' Black Friday sale is available through Nov. 27 and includes up to 40% off most 2027 itineraries around the world, plus up to $1,000 onboard credit, a free balcony upgrade and a "kids sail free" offer.

The Black Friday sale at Norwegian Cruise Line offers 50% off all cruises, plus a Free at Sea™ package. The offer is valid through Dec. 1 and applies to all itineraries, which visit nearly 350 destinations. The offer is good for all ships, including the cruise line’s two newest vessels: Norwegian Luna, which debuted in 2026, and Norwegian Aura, set to launch in spring 2027. Norwegian’s Free at Sea™ package on a standard seven-night sailing offers amenities that total more than $2,000 in savings. Such amenities include unlimited open bar access with premium spirits, unlimited Wi-Fi, three multicourse specialty dining meals for the first and second guest on the reservation, and $50 off each shore excursion for the first guest on the reservation.

Book by Dec. 1 to enjoy up to 50% savings on more than 200 Oceania Cruises voyages in 2027, thanks to the line’s Black Friday sale. The deal is available across the Oceania fleet, including its three newest ships: Oceania Vista (launched in 2023), Oceania Allura (launched in 2025) and Oceania Sonata (debuting in August 2027). Also included is Oceania Aurelia, the former Oceania Nautica, which is being refurbished and renamed ahead of its relaunch in late 2027.

Book during the Black Friday Savings event through Nov. 30 to save $1,500 to $10,000 per person on select 2027 Overseas Adventure Travel small ship ocean cruises. All 2027 departures are also eligible for savings of $500 per person plus an additional 10% when you pay in full. Discounted itineraries include small-ship adventures to Antarctica, the Baltic, the Adriatic, the Mediterranean, Egypt and the British Isles. Call 800-955-1925 and use code "BLACKFRIDAY26" to book.

The holiday sale from Tahiti-based Paul Gauguin Cruises offers savings of 10% on all 2027 departures to Polynesian destinations like the Society Islands, Tuamotus, Cook Islands and Marquesas. Cruises must be booked by Jan. 4, 2027.

Expedition cruise line Ponant Explorations has a holiday sale offering savings of up to 20% on most 2027 voyages in alliance with Smithsonian Journeys — the travel program of the Smithsonian Institution designed for immersive exploration and educational enrichment. Book through Jan. 4 using code "HOLIDAYSJ." The holiday sale also offers 10% savings on a wide selection of Ponant Explorations sailings to destinations such as the Panama Canal, Costa Rica and Madagascar. Use the code "HOLIDAY26" when booking.

Princess' “Blue Friday” sale runs through Dec. 7 and features up to 50% off cruise fares on select voyages to destinations such as the Caribbean, Alaska, Mexico, the Mediterranean and Asia. This includes itineraries on the cruise line’s two newest ships, Sun Princess and Star Princess. Additionally, third and fourth guests in a cabin sail free, and cruisers can enjoy up to $400 in instant savings.

From now until Dec. 3, save up to 40% on select Quark Expeditions voyages to the Arctic and Antarctica during its Black Friday sale. Limited cabins are available for Antarctic 2026-27 and Arctic 2027 expeditions, so check the deals if you’re looking to enjoy a polar expedition cruise to Patagonia and the Antarctic Peninsula or Greenland and Svalbard, Norway.

Luxury small-ship cruise line Regent Seven Seas Cruises is offering "The Black Friday Collection" — a limited-time offer of up to 40% savings on more than 60 voyages worldwide, including sailings in Europe, Alaska and Asia, if booked by Dec. 1. The promotion also includes $500 onboard credit per suite.

From Nov. 23 to Dec. 4, European river cruise line Riverside Luxury Cruises is offering 10% off full-board fares on select 2027 and 2028 sailings of five nights or longer during its Black Friday sale. You can combine this promotion with existing offers (Early Booking, Long Stay and Repeat Guest) for combined savings of up to 43%. All discounts will be applied at the time of booking; no code needed.

Through Dec. 7, U.K.-based Riviera Travel River Cruises is offering North American travelers a Black Friday sale with up to 50% off all 2027 and 2028 sailings and half-price deposits. For example, seven-night river cruises start at $1,999 per person (reduced from about $3,000 per person). Riviera will introduce two new ships in September 2027: MS Riviera Reflection on the Danube River and MS Riviera Alba on the Mekong River. It will also debut the world’s first solo-traveler-dedicated river ship, MS George Eliot, in June 2027. All Riviera ships qualify for Black Friday discounts.

Through Dec. 7, Royal Caribbean is offering Black Friday promotions and flash deals, including instant savings of up to $1,000 on select cruise fares, plus third and fourth guests in a cabin can sail free. These deals also include onboard credit starting at $150 and up to 50% savings on beverage packages, Wi-Fi and shore excursions. Savings, which vary by cabin and itinerary length, are available on more than 25 Royal Caribbean ships, including the line’s newest megaships — Legend of the Seas, Star of the Seas, Icon of the Seas and Utopia of the Seas. These ships sail to destinations in the Caribbean, Alaska and Europe.

Book by Nov. 29 to score Black Friday savings of up to 40% off cruise fares on select 2027 and 2028 river and ocean itineraries offered by Scenic Luxury Cruises and Tours. Scenic operates river ships and luxury expedition ocean vessels. Sale destinations include Europe’s top rivers, the Mediterranean and the Caribbean. To book, call Scenic’s reservations team at 844-323-1917.

Bookable through Dec. 8, the Black Friday deals from Seabourn Cruise Line are available for nearly all sailings through 2029 on Seabourn’s five luxury ships; offers include a free two-category Veranda Suite upgrade, plus up to a $2,000 shipboard credit per suite (for Penthouse Suites and above) and up to $500 shipboard credit per suite (for Oceanview and Veranda suites). The deal also includes a 15% reduced deposit on nearly all voyages.

Through Dec. 1, SeaDream Yacht Club, which operates two 112-guest yachts in the Caribbean, Mediterranean and Northern Europe, has a "Sail Away" promotion offering up to 25% off on select 2027 voyages. Use the code "SAILAWAY26" when booking.

With the Black Friday sale from luxury ocean and expedition line Silversea Cruises, you can save up to 45% on more than 850 sailings through May 2029 if you book by Dec. 7. By reserving now, you can also secure your suite with reduced deposits that start at 15%.

If you book a qualifying Star Clippers 2027 or 2028 Mediterranean voyage that visits Sicily by Dec. 31, the cruise line will offer a $500 (or $600 Canadian dollars) air credit and a complimentary 3GB Wi-Fi package per cabin. The offer is valid for North American residents booking a Category 2 to 6 cabin on one of Star Clippers' three sailing vessels: the 227-guest Royal Clipper, the 166-guest Star Clipper or the 166-guest Star Flyer. For qualifying sailings, reservations and questions, contact a travel adviser, call Star Clippers at 800-442-0551 or visit the Star Clippers website.

Through Dec. 4, expedition cruise line Swan Hellenic is offering up to $4,000 per person in Black Friday savings on 24 voyages worldwide, plus an additional $250 per person onboard credit. The eligible voyages sail to some of the world’s most remote places — including destinations in Antarctica, Africa, Asia Pacific and the Arctic — from March 9 to Oct. 27, 2027. Returning guests receive 10% loyalty savings, and back-to-back bookings earn a 5% discount.

Trafalgar has Black Friday deals, including BOGO 50%-off fares on featured European river sailings. The eligible 2027 itineraries, available through Dec. 3, change weekly (every Thursday), while other select departures are available at a 20% discount. Call 866-513-1995 to book, and make sure to mention Black Friday BOGO.

Luxury all-inclusive river cruise line Uniworld Boutique River Cruises — which offers sailings in Europe, Egypt, South America and Asia — has a Black Friday offer bookable through Dec. 18: The second guest pays 50% of the cruise fare when booking at double occupancy. This “Journey Together” offer is valid for select sailings throughout 2027 and is combinable with River Heritage Club member benefits for previous guests.

Family-owned, small-ship cruise line Variety Cruises has a Black Friday sale offering travelers up to 50% off on new reservations made through Dec. 7. Deals are available for seven-, 10- and 11-night cruises on Variety’s recently refurbished fleet of small ships (which hold no more than 49 guests). Available itineraries range from the Seychelles and Tahiti to signature Mediterranean voyages in Greece, Italy, Malta and destinations along the Adriatic coast.

Book any 2027 Victory Cruise Lines voyage by Nov. 30 to save $500 per person. Victory offers all-inclusive, small-ship cruises on the Great Lakes, coastal Canada, the U.S. East Coast and Florida.

While not technically a Black Friday promotion, Viking is offering its "lowest fares of the season" on select 2027 and 2028 river, ocean and expedition itineraries. River fares start at $1,999 per person, with reduced airfares and $25 deposits (just be sure to book by Nov. 27 with offer code "PLR27"). Ocean sailings are up to 35% off, with reduced airfare and $25 deposits (book by Nov. 30). Expedition voyages are up to 15% off, with reduced airfare and $25 deposits (book by Nov. 30).

Through Dec. 3, Virgin Voyages has a “buy one, get the second at 80% off” cruise fare promotion. Plus, you can score up to a $600 bar tab (depending on the length of the voyage) to cover onboard beverages. The deal is available for all cruises on Virgin’s four ships, including its first-ever Baltic sailings from Amsterdam on Valiant Lady in summer 2028. The promotion automatically applies when booking via the Virgin Voyages website.

Bookable through Dec. 2, the "Thankful for Travel Sales Event" offered by Windstar Cruises includes a complimentary all-inclusive package (which includes wine, beer, cocktails, Wi-Fi and gratuities, and is worth up to $1,386 per couple on a seven-night cruise). This applies to select sailings in the Mediterranean, Caribbean, Tahiti and Latin America through March 31, 2027. The promotion also lets you reserve with a $300 reduced deposit and gives you a $300 onboard credit.

In addition to booking directly with a cruise line, you can also reach out to a travel adviser to secure your reservation. Advisers have access to the same great deals that the lines are offering, but sometimes they can throw in extras, such as free bottles of wine or onboard credits. Before booking anything directly, it might be worth a phone call.
