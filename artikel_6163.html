Curated annually by TPG’s team of travel experts, this year’s Where to Go list highlights particularly exciting moments happening across the globe in 2027, including hotly anticipated and fabulous new hotels, milestone museum openings, travel-worthy celestial events, new UNESCO designations, new flight routes, major sporting events and so much more. And for 2027, we’ve pulled out all the stops: Below, you can explore our new interactive map displaying the 22 destinations that earned a spot on our list.

Now more than ever, exploring new and familiar destinations fosters curiosity, connectedness, and, perhaps most importantly, compassion. It’s time to put those hard-earned points and miles toward your dream trip — and we’ve got just the ticket.

Best in July through August + December through March

New sightseeing trains and upscale points hotels make Japan’s stunning northern island even more alluring in 2027.

For those who want to experience Hokkaido’s beautiful countryside by train, Hokkaido Railway Company will debut two scenic tourist trains in 2027: the Red Star luxury train in February, when the region is blanketed in Hokkaido’s signature fluffy snow, and the Blue Star premium class train in June, just in time for Hokkaido’s lavender season.

And for skiers and snowboarders who are looking to experience Hokkaido’s incredible winter sports scene, Moxy Niseko will debut in late 2026, just in time for the start of ski season. Other exciting Niseko openings to look forward to in the coming years include Six Senses Niseko and an Aman property. In nearby Sapporo, new and forthcoming points hotels are plentiful, too: 2025 saw the opening of the InterContinental Sapporo, and both Hyatt Centric and Fairmont are planned for 2027 into 2028. -⁠Ellie Nan Storck

World-class dining and hotels to help fans refuel between Women’s World Cup matches.

One of the most accessible host cities of the Women’s World Cup is also among the most captivating. Beyond the pitch, visitors can experience the city’s dining renaissance, the long-awaited Museum of Image and Sound and the one of the oldest national history museums in the Americas, recently reopened after a 2018 fire. Plus, Rio (and nearby Maricá) is the midst of a luxury hotel boom, with exciting points properties opening over the coming years. December will bring the opening of Accor’s Sofitel Rio de Janeiro Ipanema, and over the next few years, Marriott will debut an eco-luxe Ritz Carlton Reserve and an all-inclusive JW Marriott in Maricá. -⁠Rachel Craft

Best in March through May + October through November

A perennial favorite is experiencing a luxury hotel boom.

Mexico’s massive capital never lacked excellent hotel options, but a recent slate of renovations and openings has us more excited than ever to revisit the city. The St. Regis Mexico City completed a multimillion dollar renovation with Frida Kahlo-inspired design elements in Sept. 2026. Hyatt loyalists can look forward to the February opening of the colorful new Standard Hotel in the Tabacalera neighborhood, followed by the April debut of the luxe Park Hyatt in Polanco. Other upcoming projects to look forward to include a Mama Shelter (part of the ALL Accor hotel loyalty program) in the heart of Roma Norte, set to open by the end of 2026, as well as Pendry Mexico City and Rosewood Mexico City, both planned for 2027. -⁠Lyndsey Matthews

The perfect mix of new luxury hotels, a distinct wine scene and endless opportunities to get outside.

In the Texas Hill Country, charming towns steeped in German culture are surrounded by award-winning wineries, idyllic swimming holes and Main Streets packed with restaurants, shops and some of the friendliest people in the Lone Star State. Near Fredericksburg, the epicenter of the Hill Country’s must-visit charm, Waldorf Astoria is opening its first Texas property, while Aman has plans for a secluded ranch retreat of its own. Add mouthwatering barbecue, legendary live music, popular rivers to float and an expanding Austin-Bergstrom International Airport (AUS), and there are more reasons than ever to head for the hills. -⁠Tanner Saunders

Rich in natural beauty, this coastal California town is ready to step into the spotlight.

Wellness travelers, adventure travelers and everyone in between will fall for this sleepy coastal enclave along the northern California coast. Unwind at Marriott Bonvoy’s newest adventure lodge after exploring the recently opened Great Redwood Trail or sampling the latest additions to the town’s renowned sustainable food and wine scene. And thanks to nonstop flights from Alaska Airlines and Southwest and $20 million in roadwork funding, Mendocino is easier than ever to reach. -⁠Rachel Craft

Experience a quieter side of India in one of the world’s lushest biodiversity hotspots

Like many of the best places, it can take a lot of time and effort to get to Coorg, a hill station in India’s verdant, biodiverse Western Ghats. But it is well worth it to see this little-visited region rife with endemic wildlife including elephants, leopards and myriad avian species. Spend your days sampling world-class coffee at the area’s many plantations, hiking the misty mountain trails and recentering yourself with immersive multiday Ayurvedic wellness programs at the stunning new Leela Coorg Forest Sanctuary. -⁠Eric Rosen

In anticipation of hosting the EXPO 2027, the Serbian capital is undergoing a major glow-up

From May 15 to Aug.15, the Serbian capital will host a specialized world exposition with 140-plus countries gathering to showcase innovations in architecture and design. In anticipation of the event, Belgrade is debuting several major projects of its own, including a Nikola Tesla Museum designed by Zaha Hadid Architects. The former main railway station will become the new permanent home of the Historical Museum of Serbia, while the Museum of the City of Belgrade and the Museum of Natural History Belgrade will also open in new facilities. Finally, the Aeronautical Museum of Belgrade will also complete a modernization project in 2027. To accommodate the influx of visitors, the city already opened a St. Regis in 2024, while the InterContinental Belgrade is accepting reservations from next March, with a Ritz-Carlton also anticipated to open, too. -⁠Lyndsey Matthews

Best in May through June, September through October

A pair of new destination-worthy hotels, both bookable with points.

Hyatt and Marriott will both debut luxury properties in Detroit come 2027. Hilton’s new hotel, the NoMad Detroit, will open inside the historic Michigan Grand Central Station, a once-abandoned Beaux-Arts landmark restored by Ford. Later in the year, the Marriott Edition will open as part of a ground-up development, bringing downtown Detroit its first five-star hotel. -⁠Carly Helfand

A new film festival is just one facet of this art-meets-outdoors jewel.

Between hosting its first Sundance Film Festival and celebrating 50 years of the iconic Pearl Street Mall, Colorado’s trendy cultural hub is going all out. Visitors can also look forward to new dining concepts from Michelin-recognized chefs, new interactive art labs and easy access to new Rocky Mountain hiking trails. An hour away, Denver International Airport (DEN) is expanding to accommodate increased traffic from United, Southwest and even Delta. -⁠Rachel Craft

Jungle-covered islands and seas teeming with marine life beckon explorers beyond the bright lights of Bali.

Cruise companies are lining up new expeditions to explore the Indonesian archipelago’s untouched corners, including a seven-day sailing aboard Uniworld’s Aqua Blu that will stop in Komodo National Park and the Spice Islands, Ponant’s team-up with Smithsonian Journeys on a 15-day jaunt around the Maluku islands among other ports of call and specialized dive-focused sailings in wild West Papua, reef-rich Raja Ampat and untamed Borneo from Swan Hellenic and Noble Caledonia. It’s the best way to experience regions of the Indonesian archipelago most folks never see. -⁠Eric Rosen

Australia’s first Michelin Guide, new luxury hotels and experiences, nonstop access from the U.S. and an extraordinary mix of world-class wine, wildlife and wilderness make 2027 South Australia’s moment on the global stage.

Australia’s first Michelin Guide and United’s nonstop San Francisco-Adelaide flight are putting South Australia within easier reach — and on the global culinary map, too. A significant transformation of Adelaide Central Market, Australia’s first Treehouse Hotel and new luxury stays are adding fresh reasons to visit, alongside the Barossa and McLaren Vale wine regions and the wildlife and rugged beauty of Kangaroo Island. -⁠Tanner Saunders

Best in April through June, September through October

Natural wonders and a burgeoning culture scene without the crowds.

Though Kazakhstan first made a name for itself as a nature travel destination, this Eurasian country wants the world to know there’s more to it than its glorious landscapes. Travelers can enjoy a new art museum and immersive ethno-village in the cultural capital of Almaty, check into new hotels in the Burabay resort region or combine natural and cultural wonders at the newly inscribed Mangystau Rock Mosques UNESCO World Heritage Site. With plans to launch the first-ever direct flight from the U.S. in 2027, this hidden gem may not stay hidden for long. -⁠Rachel Craft

Best in May through June, September through October

Two iconic cultural institutions, back and better than ever.

The Pergamon Museum will reopen in June after a multi-year renovation, debuting a fully redesigned Museum of Islamic Art and making the Pergamon Altar accessible for the first time in more than a decade. Later in the year, the Bauhaus Archive will reopen after extensive renovations with a new annex building. With nonstop options from the U.S. on United, Delta and Lufthansa — and strong Hilton and Marriott footprints downtown — early points bookings are a smart play for visiting Berlin in 2027. -⁠Carly Helfand

Best in April through June, September through November

Experience a slower type of journey that has captivated travelers for over 1,000 years.

Special events will be taking place at waystations along this ancient pilgrimage trail throughout the Holy Year of 2027. Among the highlights are braving the wine battle in Haro at the end of June, or attending St. James’s Feast Day on July 25 in Santiago de Compostela, where you can see the famous swinging of the Botafumeiro silver incense burner and attend an awe-inspiring mass in the Praza do Obradoiro. Aside from a full calendar of culinary festivals and lively concerts in Santiago, you can take in quieter stretches of the trail among the forested peaks of Cantabria and the vineyards of Rioja. -⁠Eric Rosen

Astrotourism investments and a highly anticipated points hotel transformation.

Dramatic coastal landscapes, the new Atlantic Canada UNESCO Tourism Corridor and the re-opening of the transformed The Algonquin Resort St. Andrews by-the-Sea, Autograph Collection resort make a compelling case for a 2027 visit to New Brunswick. Specifically, we’re excited to check out the Bay of Fundy Dark Sky Corridor that’s set to open in 2027 — a 137-mile stretch that could be the densest cluster of dark-sky and observatory areas on the planet. A stargazer’s dream come true. -⁠Madison Blancaflor

A burgeoning wine scene, an exciting UNESCO designation and outdoor attractions aplenty give one of BC’s prettiest regions a well deserved moment in the spotlight

With plenty of sunny days and a generally milder climate than the rest of British Columbia, the Okanagan Valley is becoming an increasingly compelling alternative to larger wine regions; oenophiles can look forward to sipping wines from smaller producers (small but mighty; there are over 200) in a community-forward and breathtaking lakeside setting.

Those in search of a city getaway should head to Kelowna, the valley’s largest metropolis set along the breezy shorelines of Okanagan Lake, where a major culinary moment is afoot. Recently designated Canada’s first UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy — a designation that honors not only the city’s agricultural innovation and culinary creativity, but the traditional foodways and land stewardship of the indigenous Syilx people — Kelowna offers a fast-growing restaurant scene (many with a sustainable, farm-to-table focus) and its own array of impressive wineries. -⁠Ellie Nan Storck

Travelers can look forward to fully restored access to Denali Park Road for the first time in six years

With more access now than ever, thanks to both recently expanded Alaska Airlines routes to nearby Fairbanks International Airport (FAI) and the very exciting full reopening of Denali Park Road for the summer season, 2027 is the year to plan an epic trip to Denali National Park. And the fun doesn’t stop there — Holland America, celebrating 80 years of Alaskan exploration, will complete a massive transformation of Holland America Denali Lodge, debuting 72 new guest rooms, updated public spaces and refreshed dining programs. Beyond the park, the Alaska Railroad — which celebrated its centennial in 2023 — is a great way travelers can experience a legendary scenic trip between Anchorage, Talkeetna, Denali and Fairbanks. -⁠Ellie Nan Storck

Expanded access from the U.S. and newly awarded Michelin key hotel recognition.

Delta is adding a new nonstop route from L.A. to Manila and Philippine Airlines is joining the oneworld alliance, making a trip to the gorgeous islands of The Philippines easier than ever in 2027. Head to the islands early in the year for milder temperatures, vibrant festivals and the chance to ethically swim with majestic whale sharks in Donsol, Sorsogon. Nature lovers and beach loungers can also enjoy luxury properties like the Raffles Makati and the forthcoming Mandarin Oriental Makati, Manila (opening in December) or use points to book the brand new AC Hotel by Marriott Manila. -⁠Madison Blancaflor

Best in June through September, January through March

These lush African islands are ready for their moment in the sun.

Thanks to its pristine tropical forests and rare wildlife species, in 2025 São Tomé and Principe became the first country in the world whose entire territory, both land and sea, was designated as a UNESCO biosphere reserve. This past year, the country scored a second UNESCO designation for its historical roças, or colonial agricultural estates, making São Tomé and Principe ripe for discovery. Even better — you can use your Star Alliance points and miles to fly direct from Lisbon on TAP and Hilton points can set you up for a beachy stay at the luxurious Sundy Praia – Principe Collection. -⁠Eric Rosen

Best in April through May, September through October

For travelers ready to venture beyond familiar South American tourism destinations and experience a different side of the continent.

Travelers who have already visited Peru, Brazil, Argentina and other South American hot spots can find something different in the continent’s less-visited interior. Don’t go expecting a long list of world-famous sights or extensive tourism infrastructure. Instead, go for a quieter, slower-paced trip shaped by Indigenous Guaraní culture and landscapes ranging from the vast Chaco lowlands to the biodiverse remnants of the Atlantic Forest along the Paraná River. New attractions are adding reasons to visit: MUVA Museo Viedma, a museum exploring Paraguay’s Guaraní-Jesuit heritage through murals by artist Manuel Viedma, opened in Misiones in May 2026, and Asunción’s Museo de Ciencias plans to open a planetarium in 2028. -⁠Mackenzie Roche
