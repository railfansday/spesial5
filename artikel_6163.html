Washington Dulles Concourse E Opens, With Huge New United Club

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

United Airlines is on quite the roll when it comes to opening new concourses and lounges at its hubs. Just recently, I covered how a new United Club opened in Houston (IAH) Terminal B North. This is not only the biggest United Club in the world, but it’s the biggest airport lounge in all of the Americas.

There’s now another update, as we’re seeing the opening of a new United Club at Washington Dulles (IAD) Concourse E. This is the second biggest United Club in the entire system, and it’s also part of a long overdue new concourse for the airport. Let’s first talk about the new lounge, and then about the new concourse.

A brand new United Club is opening in Concourse E at Washington Dulles Airport. The new lounge is nearly 40,000 square feet, with seating for more than 600 customers, making it the carrier’s second largest United Club in the system. Crucially, this also increases United’s club and lounge space at the airport by nearly 70%.

The new lounge has a bar, two buffet stations, eight private phone rooms, complimentary Wi-Fi, and power access at nearly every seat. Unfortunately there are no showers or barista coffee.

As the design is described, it “reflects the character of the Washington, D.C. region through design elements inspired by the historic natural and built environment of the nation’s capital, like the iconic main Dulles terminal architecture, cherry blossoms and the Blue Ridge Mountains.”

The club is located across from gates E14 and E16, and it’s open daily from 5:30AM until 10PM, with an opening on Tuesday, November 10, 2026. The lounge follows the standard United Club access policies.

The opening of the new United Club is part of the larger opening of the new Concourse E at Washington Dulles. This is the first new concourse to open at the airport in two decades.

The new concourse is 435,000 square feet, and it has 14 new gates that will exclusively be used by United. United claims that these new gates will increase connectivity by nearly 30% during peak times. The concourse also has direct AeroTrain access, expansive new dining and retail options, and more.

It’s important to keep in mind what a mess United’s current facility at Washington Dulles is, which is what makes this all the more exciting. United primarily operates out of Concourses C and D, which were built as temporary facilities in 1985. 40+ years later, they’re still there. So this new concourse will be a massive boost to the United passenger experience.

We know that Dulles Airport is supposed to undergo a major modernization, which was unveiled by the Trump administration. However, the cost is astronomical (even by the typically inflated airport standards), and things are already well behind schedule.

As you’d expect, this press release has endless quotes from airline, airport, and Trump administration officials. There’s also a fair bit of spin here, like this, from Deputy Secretary of Transportation, Steven Bradbury:

“Under President Trump, America is building again. So far, the construction of United’s new Concourse E has supported 2,700 good paying jobs and brought $1.3 billion in local economic activity to the national capital region. USDOT’s broader partnership with MWAA and United on the Dulles Airport transformation is just beginning, with many more milestones on the horizon. The ultimate winner will be the American people who will enjoy a stunning new gateway to America’s capital for generations to come. Congratulations to MWAA and United for today’s grand unveiling, and we look forward to even greater results in the near future.”

Keep in mind that the “building” of this concourse started under the Biden administration, and not under the Trump administration.

Washington Dulles Airport’s new Concourse E opens to the public on Tuesday, November 10, 2026. The new concourse adds 14 gates for United, and most importantly, it looks like a real terminal, and not like a temporary facility. The concourse has a new United Club, which not only looks much improved, but also massively increases lounge capacity.
