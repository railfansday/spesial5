bold shapes and rounded partitions soften divisions within new york apartment by SABO project

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

in new york’s lower east side, SABO project renovates a compact apartment for a family of three using rounded partitions tucked beneath structural steel beams.

Alex Delaunay edited by christina vergopoulou November 23, 2026 3 min

Located in New York’s Lower East Side, Soft Division is the renovation of a compact apartment for a family of three designed by SABO project. Stripped back to its core structure, the residence is organized as a central living space that extends outward into surrounding alcoves. Rounded partitions pass beneath exposed structural steel beams to soften spatial boundaries and establish continuous visual flow between zones. Extending the interior footprint outdoors, a continuous built-in bench runs from the main living area into a compact terrace enclosed with steel mesh and light-filtering fabric.

Across the interior, raw structural elements, including exposed concrete masonry blocks and mechanical ductwork, are balanced by soft tactile textiles such as bespoke curtains, window shades, and rugs. Design studio SABO project introduces custom curtains featuring large-scale graphic compositions to delineate individual rooms without rigid walls. The bathroom is fully clad in ceramic tiles of varying dimensions arranged to create a continuous tonal gradient.
