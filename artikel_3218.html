Best AI reputation management firms in 2026 vs traditional ORM

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

When ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity describe your business, that answer is your first impression. Seven AI reputation management firms and platforms promise to track it or fix it.

When someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity about your business, the answer they get is now your first impression. ChatGPT alone reached 900 million weekly active users in February 2026. Traditional online reputation management was built to push bad links off page one. AI visibility and LLM reputation management is about what the answer itself says.

That shift has created a new category, and only a handful of companies work in it. Some are service firms that change what AI says. Others are software platforms that measure it.

Below, we rank the best AI reputation management firms and platforms for 2026. We explain how this work differs from traditional ORM and show which option fits which need.

AI visibility and LLM reputation management is the work of monitoring and shaping what large language models like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews say about a person or brand. It covers two jobs. One is making sure accurate, positive information shows up in AI answers. The other is removing or correcting the false, outdated or harmful sources those answers are built on.

Traditional online reputation management and AI reputation management share the same goal, but they work on different surfaces.

We looked at four things: whether each company tracks what AI platforms say and whether it can change those answers. We also looked at how many AI platforms it covers, and who it’s built for. Direct removal capability carried the most weight, since monitoring alone tells you about a problem without solving it.

Several entries below are software platforms rather than service firms. We included them because many businesses will use a platform alongside a service firm. Knowing the difference helps you pick the right mix.

What they do: Reputation Resolutions is a full-service online reputation management firm based in Englewood, Colorado, and our pick for the top spot among the best AI reputation management firms. It is the only firm on this list that combines AI monitoring with hands-on removal of the content behind harmful answers. Its team audits what every major AI platform says about a client, traces each claim to the page it came from and removes or corrects that source. It then builds accurate content to take its place.

Standout capability: Removal at the source. Most companies on this list measure AI visibility. Reputation Resolutions changes it, with platform-specific work for ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. It has also built free tools that put that removal expertise in anyone’s hands: RemoveNews.ai writes a personalized removal request for a news article and finds the editor’s direct contact in about 60 seconds. A free court record removal scan finds court and arrest records copied onto people-search and mugshot sites.

Track record: More than a decade of removal work, more than 5,000 clients in over 40 countries, a place on Inc. Magazine’s regional fastest-growing companies list and a Forbes Agency Council membership.

Best for: Individuals, executives and businesses dealing with false, outdated or damaging AI answers, and anyone who needs the problem fixed rather than just reported.

“We start every AI engagement the same way,” says Anthony Will, CEO and co-founder of Reputation Resolutions. “We ask each platform what your customers would ask, record every source it cites, and then go after those sources one by one. That’s the only fix that holds across every AI tool at once.”

What they do: Minc Law is a Cleveland-based law firm focused on internet defamation, online harassment and content removal. The firm says it has removed more than 200,000 pieces of defamatory and damaging online content and litigated more than 350 cases in 26 states and five countries.

Standout capability: Legal action. When false content won’t come down through outreach or platform policies, Minc Law can send demand letters and pursue defamation claims. It can also work to unmask anonymous posters.

Best for: Cases where the source behind a harmful AI answer is defamatory and needs a legal remedy. It pairs well with a removal-focused reputation firm: legal pressure on one side, removal and AI reputation work on the other.

What they do: Birdeye is a review and customer experience platform for multi-location brands. Its Birdeye AI Search product tracks brand and location-level visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Claude and Grok.

Standout capability: Location-level AI visibility scoring, citation tracking and sentiment analysis, tied to its review management tools.

Best for: Franchises and multi-location brands that want to monitor AI visibility location by location and keep review volume up.

Keep in mind: Birdeye is software. It can show you a negative source and help you publish better content. But it doesn’t remove third-party articles, records or posts.

What they do: Yext manages business listings and brand data across search engines, maps and directories. In March 2025 it launched Yext Scout, an AI search and competitive intelligence agent that tracks how brands appear across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok and Google.

Standout capability: Keeping business facts accurate and consistent everywhere AI tools look, with competitor benchmarking built in.

Best for: Enterprise and multi-location brands whose main AI problem is inconsistent or outdated business information.

Keep in mind: Yext focuses on data you control, like listings and web content. It isn’t built to remove negative content published by others.

What they do: Podium is a customer communication platform for local businesses that combines messaging, review requests and payments. Its AI Employee handles lead response and appointment booking around the clock.

Standout capability: Generating a steady flow of fresh, positive reviews, which feed the review sites AI tools pull from when they describe local businesses.

Best for: Local service businesses such as auto dealers, home services, dental and medical practices that want more reviews and faster customer response.

Keep in mind: Podium builds positive signals. It doesn’t track AI answers directly or remove harmful content.

What they do: Vendasta is a white-label software platform for agencies and resellers that serve small businesses. Its reputation management platform monitors reviews across more than 100 sites and uses an AI agent to post personalized review replies.

Standout capability: White-label delivery, so agencies can offer review and reputation tools under their own brand.

Best for: Marketing agencies that want to add reputation services for many small business clients at once.

Keep in mind: Vendasta is a toolset for agencies, not a removal service, and its focus is reviews rather than AI answers.

What they do: Profound is an answer engine optimization (AEO) analytics platform that shows brands how often AI models mention them and in what context, across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. The company raised $180 million at a $1.8 billion valuation in September 2026.

Standout capability: Large-scale measurement of AI share of voice and citation patterns for enterprise marketing teams.

Best for: Enterprise marketing and SEO teams that want detailed data on how their brand performs in AI answers compared with competitors.

Keep in mind: Profound measures and optimizes visibility. It doesn’t handle reputation crises or remove harmful content.

AI visibility and LLM reputation management is quickly replacing page-one rankings as the measure of how people see you. Monitoring platforms can tell you what AI says, and review tools can build positive signals. But changing a harmful answer means removing what it’s built on.

For anyone comparing the best companies for AI reputation management, that’s the deciding question: can they change the answer, or only report it? On that test, Reputation Resolutions ranks first.
