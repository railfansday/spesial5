Flight Attendant Finds Hidden Camera In Lavatory—Passenger Charged With Trying To Erase Evidence

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

A flight attendant found a hidden camera in a Scoot lavatory. The passenger onboard the Singapore Airlines low cost carrier accused of putting it there is also charged with deleting evidence. It’s the deleting that creates the greatest risk of imprisonment. The incident happened October 7 on Scoot flight TR821 from Osaka to Singapore. The 52-year-old British passenger was charged in Singapore on November 5. One Mile at a Time flags the story noting he’s currently jailed. He’s now been offered S$10,000 bail. The man plans to plead guilty. According to the Singapore Police Force, the man concealed a camera in a lavatory, left, and activated recording through an app on his phone.
