Camara to sacrifice F2 title bid to finish F1 season with Haas

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Rafael Camara will race again for Haas at the Qatar Grand Prix and Abu Dhabi Grand Prix ahead of joining full-time for the 2027 season; Camara leads the Formula 2 standings but will not feature in the final rounds

Haas have confirmed Rafael Camara will continue to feature for the team in the final two Formula 1 races of the season, rather than challenge for the Formula 2 championship.

Camara made his debut at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix earlier this month, following Esteban Ocon's departure ahead of the Mexico City Grand Prix and reserve driver Ryo Hirakawa stepping in for that race.

The 21-year-old also raced alongside team-mate Oliver Bearman at the Las Vegas Grand Prix on Sunday, finishing 16th after a collision with Cadillac's Sergio Perez.

Camara has already been confirmed to drive for Haas next year and will get two more Formula 1 race weekends of experience in Qatar this weekend and the season-ending Abu Dhabi Grand Prix next week before 2027.

"We're excited to be able to finish the season with Rafa driving and building on his first two race starts with the team in Brazil and America," said Haas team principal Ayao Komatsu.

"I'm delighted that Rafa's embraced every track session as a learning opportunity and embedded himself further within the team.

"We now get to finish the season together, which is advantageous to us all ahead of a relatively short off-season and as our 2027 preparations get under way."

Camara leads the Formula 2 standings by seven points over Red Bull junior Nikola Tsolov but is highly unlikely to win the championship, given there are four races remaining across the the Qatar and Abu Dhabi weekends

Invicta Racing, Camara's F2 team, shared on X: "What a journey it's been Thank you for everything Rafa and best of luck for the rest of the @f1 season with @haasf1team."

Meanwhile, Bearman says he's "disappointed" his future at Haas is unresolved as he's currently left without a seat on the 2027 Formula 1 grid.

Bearman has long been expected to be confirmed to stay at Haas next year, with the team already announcing last month that Camara will drive for them in 2027.

Although Bearman did not reveal what exactly he was disappointed by, he was clearly frustrated when speaking to the media on Thursday ahead of the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

The British driver told Sky Sports F1: "It's certainly disappointing when you have people within the team questioning why you haven't been announced yet, and things like that, it's certainly not ideal - not only for them but also myself from a mental side.

"I wouldn't say it's the best to be standing here at round 20-something without any confirmation. We are working on it. All I can say is I'm a bit disappointed that I'm standing here without anything to tell you because there's no excuses for that."

Asked in his written media session whether he was thinking about Ferrari long-term, Bearman said: "I'm on the contract with Ferrari and they have given me this incredible opportunity to step into Formula 1 with them for the first time.

"Of course, I'm still a Ferrari junior driver, and my ultimate dream is to race for Ferrari because, first of all, they're the most iconic team, and they're the first team that believed in me as a kid. It's still my ultimate dream.

"But there's no doubt that looking into the future, there may be some challenges there. There's no denying that, it's very clear.

"I don't really have much say in the matter, and we just need to see how things develop over time."
