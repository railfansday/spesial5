Price makes undisputed history as she shuts out Kotaskova

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Lauren Price beat Michaela Kotaskova at the Utilita Arena in Cardiff to become the undisputed welterweight world champion; winning a unanimous decision saw Price add the WBO title to the WBA, WBC and IBF belts she already had; Price is Wales' first undisputed champion in the four-belt era

Lauren Price made history for Welsh boxing when she beat Michaela Kotaskova to become the undisputed welterweight world champion.

With a unanimous decision victory over Kotaskova at the Utilita Arena, a shutout 100-90 for all three judges, Price added the vacant WBO 147lbs title to the IBF, WBC and WBA belts.

An Olympic gold medallist in 2021, Price is the first Welsh boxer to become an undisputed world champion in the four-belt era.

The first female British champion too, the skilful southpaw started busily and with intent. She planted stiff left crosses through the middle, that back hand arrowing between Kotaskova's gloves and catching her chin.

Price stamped her authority on the fight, teeing off with flurries. Firm right hooks shook Kotaskova.

Price moved backwards and off to the sides, leaving the tall Kotaskova striding after her. When the Czech planted her feet to swing wildly, Price beat her to the punch with rapid, accurate salvoes.

Kotaskova absorbed hits and marched forward. But her grim efforts yielded scant result and in the fifth round Kotaskova was badly cut by her left eye after an accidental head clash.

She didn't let that dissuade her but Price continued to dictate the action. In the sixth round the Welsh star dug her feet into the canvas to uncork a hard three-punch combination.

In the next round Price hammered the body once more and brought a right hook back up to the head. She moved away well, as Kotaskova ran out of ideas.

A swiping left hook from Price knocked Kotaskova away. Price ground in draining body shots.

Kotaskova desperately sought to apply pressure in the 10th and final round, but she could not pin the Welsh star down, who picked her off and raised her arms to celebrate in the final seconds before the bell to end it.

Kotaskova had made her work but the decision was still a formality, and a reflection of Price's dominance.

"I just feel so lucky to be Welsh," Price told the BBC. "This goes out to you lot, thank you, it means the world.

"Nothing really tops that. Winning the Olympic gold medal, that's a dream from the age of eight.

"As a professional everyone's dream is to become undisputed and I can honestly say I've ticked every accolade there is in boxing. I'm not finished. I've got many years left in me.

"It's all about the big fights now and it's all about that legacy," she continued. "I'm undisputed at welterweight but I can move up.

"I just want to stay on this journey with these fans."
