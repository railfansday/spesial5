Amazon Prime members now get free Uber Eats delivery: How to get

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Amazon Prime members can now get free delivery on eligible Uber Eats orders through a new benefit that lets customers order restaurant meals directly from Amazon.

Alongside the delivery benefit, Amazon is offering a limited-time promotion that gives eligible Prime members discounts on their first two orders.

Prime members can now receive a $0 delivery fee on qualifying Uber Eats restaurant orders of $25 or more. The benefit is available through the Uber Eats storefront on Amazon, rather than through Uber Eats’ own app or website.

To activate the offer, members need to visit Amazon’s Uber Eats storefront, sign in to their Amazon account, and select “Allow” to enable the benefit.

Once activated, customers can browse participating restaurants, select their food, and choose between delivery and pickup directly through Amazon.

Orders can also be tracked through Amazon’s website or shopping app, with customers able to receive real-time updates through push notifications.

The $25 minimum is calculated before taxes and additional fees, meaning customers will still be responsible for applicable taxes and service charges. The benefit is limited to qualifying US restaurants and does not require a separate Uber Eats account.

In addition to free delivery, Amazon is offering Prime members $20 off each of their first two qualifying Uber Eats orders placed through Amazon.

The promotion provides up to $40 in total savings, with each order requiring a minimum purchase of $25 before taxes and fees.

The discount is available exclusively to Prime members placing their first two orders through the Uber Eats storefront on Amazon during the promotional period. Customers can redeem the offer twice, and it cannot be combined with certain other promotions or discounts.

The limited-time deal expires on November 18, 2026, at 11:59 PM PST. Any orders scheduled in advance must be set for delivery before the promotion expires.

Unlike the introductory discount, Amazon has not announced an expiration date for the ongoing $0 delivery fee benefit.

The benefit is exclusive to Prime members, meaning anyone who cancels or pauses their subscription will immediately lose access. Customers who later reactivate their membership will need to enable the offer again.

The new Uber Eats partnership replaces Amazon’s previous agreement with Grubhub, which offered Prime members a complimentary Grubhub+ subscription.
