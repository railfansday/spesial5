MasterCraft (MCFT) Q1 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Legacy margins climbed as Chaparral and Robalo integration advanced.

MasterCraft Boat Holdings, Inc. (MCFT -3.17%) reported financial results for the first quarter of its six-month transition period, highlighting the strategic integration of the Marine Products acquisition. Management reported that the addition of the Chaparral and Robalo brands has diversified the portfolio across price points and categories, reducing dependence on any single boating segment. The company reported significant margin expansion in its legacy business, driven by a premium product mix and operational cost discipline. Management indicated that dealer health remains a priority, with inventory levels currently below prior-year levels. The company raised its guidance for the transition period based on first quarter performance and continued synergy execution.

Scott Kent: Thanks, Brad, and good morning, everyone. I'll start by reviewing our results for the first quarter, then cover our balance sheet and capital allocation and finish with our outlook for the remainder of the transition period. Net sales for the quarter were $154.1 million, an increase of 123.4% compared to the prior year period, including $59.3 million from our Recreation and Sport Fishing segment. On a Legacy basis, net sales were $94.8 million, an increase of 37.4% compared to $69 million in the prior year period, driven by higher volumes of our next-generation X Series models, which drove favorable mix, along with disciplined pricing and lower discounts.

Consolidated gross margin was 21.1% compared to 22.3% in the prior year period, reflecting the blended margin profile following the Marine Products acquisition. Legacy gross margins expanded 600 basis points to 28.3% from 22.3% in the prior year period, driven by improved fixed cost absorption, favorable mix, lower discounts and operating improvements across both our Performance and Wake, and Leisure segments. Operating expenses were $20.5 million compared to $11.6 million in the prior year period. The increase primarily reflects a full quarter of Recreation and Sport Fishing as well as additional acquisition and integration costs related to the Marine Products transaction.

Adjusted EBITDA was $20.1 million with an adjusted EBITDA margin of 13% compared to $7 million and 10.1% in the prior year period. On a Legacy basis, adjusted EBITDA was $16.7 million, with margin expanding 750 basis points to 17.6%. Consistent with what we outlined last quarter, intangible amortization and depreciation for the acquired company are now running at normalized levels of approximately $0.6 million and $2.6 million per quarter, respectively. Turning to the balance sheet. We continue to expect capital expenditures of approximately $9 million for the period. Our priorities for the remainder of the period remain unchanged, aligning wholesale with retail demand, protecting dealer health, maintaining cost discipline and executing our synergy plan.

We believe this disciplined approach positions us well to navigate the current environment while continuing to strengthen the business for the long term. As we look to the December quarter, we expect our underlying build rates and AUSPs to remain consistent with Q1. However, normal holiday seasonality will likely result in modestly lower sequential shipments due to fewer production days. Overall, we remain confident in the strength of our portfolio, our balance sheet and the long-term earnings power of the combined company. This quarter's performance demonstrates another step forward with strong execution, disciplined cost management and commercial optimization. Our financial flexibility will allow us to continue investing in innovation while also aggressively pursuing our synergy plan.

As we continue executing our focused plan and leveraging the scale of the combined company, we believe we have a meaningful opportunity for ongoing value creation. With that, I'll turn it back to Brad for closing remarks.
