Stock Market Today, Nov. 27: S&P 500 Posts Best Week in 19 Months as AI Stocks Rebound

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Wall Street capped a volatile Thanksgiving week with its strongest gains in more than a year as investors returned to beaten-down technology stocks.

By RJ Fulton – Nov 27, 2026 at 4:30PM EST

Wall Street ended the holiday-shortened trading week with a powerful rally on Friday, November 27, as renewed buying interest in technology stocks helped major indexes post their strongest weekly gains in more than a year. The rebound marked a sharp reversal from the recent sell-off, with investors returning to beaten-down semiconductor and artificial intelligence (AI) stocks.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC +1.60%) climbed 1.60% to close at 7,297.49, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +2.31%) surged 2.31% to 24,129.50. The Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI +1.18%) gained 1.18% to 50,774.94, capping a strong recovery across major indexes.

Elsewhere, gold jumped 3.75% to $3,668.50, while the 10-year Treasury yield declined to 4.85%. Consumer discretionary stocks led sector gains, reflecting renewed appetite for growth-oriented investments, while utilities and communication services lagged.

Amazon (AMZN +3.96%) climbed 3.96% as encouraging early Black Friday shopping data and optimism surrounding its artificial intelligence initiatives supported investor sentiment. Micron Technology (MU +7.60%) surged 7.60% as renewed buying interest in semiconductor stocks lifted shares of the memory-chip manufacturer.

Elsewhere, Kinetik Holdings (KNTK +9.25%) jumped 9.25% following reports of a potential acquisition, while Vertex Pharmaceuticals (VRTX -5.24%) declined 5.24% after the Food and Drug Administration delayed a regulatory decision on one of its kidney disease treatments until 2027.

Wall Street's strong finish to November provides some encouragement following a turbulent stretch for equities. The S&P 500's best weekly performance in more than a year, supported by renewed buying interest in semiconductor stocks, suggests investors are finding opportunities after the recent sell-off. However, a sharp rebound does not necessarily signal that the broader market correction has ended.

The sustainability of the recovery will depend heavily on corporate earnings, particularly among technology companies. Investors are increasingly focused on whether substantial investments in AI infrastructure will translate into stronger revenue growth, improved productivity, and higher profits. The rebound in companies like Micron suggests confidence in long-term semiconductor demand remains intact, although elevated valuations leave little room for disappointing results.

Looking ahead to 2027, expectations for AI-driven productivity improvements could provide an additional source of earnings growth beyond the technology sector. However, realizing those benefits will take time, and persistent inflation could complicate the outlook for interest rates and corporate profitability.
