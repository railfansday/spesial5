Alan Shearer has been asked to name his all time Newcastle United starting eleven.

The United and England legend accepting the challenge.

Not a bad Newcastle United starting eleven…

The Premier League record goalscorer talking to All Out Football and starting at the back.

Alan Shearer: “Shay Given in goal, Trippier on the right, I’m going to say Dan Burn because he scored in the final and helped us lift the trophy. I’m going to say Lewis Hall as a left-back and as the other centre-half I’m going to say, value for money it has to be Fabian Schar.”

The former NUFC number 9 then moving on to midfield: “Rob Lee, Bruno…”

Then finally Alan Shearer getting to the top of the pitch: “Beardsley without a doubt, Newcastle’s best ever player. Ginola, me and Les Ferdinand.”

So that makes it this all time Newcastle United starting eleven, according to Alan Shearer:

Shay Given Kieran Trippier Fabian Schar Dan Burn Lewis Hall Rob Lee Bruno Guimaraes Peter Beardsley David Ginola Les Ferdinand Alan Shearer
