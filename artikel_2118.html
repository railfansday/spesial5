Martin Drury's extraordinary Huddersfield Town admission has fans talking

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Huddersfield Town made it three wins from three to top their Jersey Mike's Trophy group on Tuesday night, but they did so without manager Martin Drury in the dugout.

Assistant coaches Jon Stead and Dale Tonge led the team as the League One leaders dispatched Liverpool's Under-21s 4-0, taking the lead after 40 seconds and controlling the game throughout.

It was a much-needed response after being knocked out of the FA Cup by Exeter City at the first hurdle on Saturday, which would have thrown up a rare stumbling block in what has been a wholly successful reign in charge at the Accu Stadium for Drury.

The defeat against the League Two outfit must have enraged the Huddersfield boss to an extent, though, as fractured ribs from shouting led him to take a back seat in their Trophy matchup on Tuesday.

It was by no means a weakened squad for Huddersfield as they breezed past Liverpool's academy. Joe Taylor started up front with Bojaz Radulovic, and the likes of Archie Collins, Bali Mumba and Joe Low were all involved from the off.

Therefore, seeing a change in personnel in the dugout may have raised some eyebrows, as Martin Drury watched on from the stands, with his assistants on the touchline.

This wasn't an intended night off for the Terriers boss, however. The 40-year-old still did his post-match duties and revealed that a couple of fractured ribs were the reason why he was taking things a little easier.

"I sat in the stands tonight. I've got two fractured ribs from shouting in training, so I've been told to try not to shout for a few days," he told BBC West Yorkshire. "I took a step back with full confidence that everything would be in the right place.

"Jon and Dale took the group and have been excellent, as they have all season. And again, full credit to the players to respond from Saturday. We weren't perfect tonight, but it's still pleasing to score four goals and keep a clean sheet."

After being quizzed further on the cracked ribs and how to recover from them, Drury responded, "You do nothing, stop shouting."

Even then, the 40-year-old stated he'll be trying to "get some pain relief injections over the next few weeks," so he can continue to do his job as intensely as he's done thus far.

It's quite ludicrous that Drury has broken his ribs from simply shouting during training, but that showcases how dedicated to his job he is, and it's working wonders in the early stages of the League One campaign.

Huddersfield fans have been in this situation before, with a strong start to a League One season before fading away, but week by week, they're starting to believe that things are different under Drury, and this could be the year they get promoted back to the Championship.

Town fans love how dedicated Drury is to winning, and his injury has sparked some positive reaction amongst the supporter base. "That is dedication!" one fan said. "Animal! Get well soon, my gaffer 🔥," another commented.

"Guys a genuine psychopath, and I love him for it #htafc," was also among the reactions from Huddersfield supporters, as was "some gaffer."

"Wow… Martin Drury really is putting EVERYTHING into his training sessions!" a further comment read. This level of intensity isn't like anything seen before, and with things going well, as they are now, it's very easy for a fanbase to buy into it.

It's safe to say that this Huddersfield side looks miles better than the two in previous seasons.

A lot of that can be down to the increase in quality within the squad, with the ex-Excelsior right-hand side of Ilias Bronkhorst and Derensili Sanches Fernandes proving brilliant business, and Taylor finally becoming that lethal finisher at the Accu Stadium, but the level of coaching has to be a driving factor, too.

Drury is so dedicated to his craft, dominating the metrics even in defeat, and is still dissatisfied with some parts of the performance after a win, as shown in that interview after the win over Liverpool's under-21s, or following a "chaotic" 4-1 win over Peterborough United last month.

It's still to be seen how this intense managerial style copes if Huddersfield undergo a bit of a dry spell on the field in terms of picking up wins, but for now, these Town fans are buying into their manager and this team, and it seems that this combination could bring them Championship football.
