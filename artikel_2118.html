"It'll never happen again" - Stoke City star sends message to fans after Birmingham win

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Stoke City have leapfrogged Birmingham City in the Championship table after a narrow 1-0 victory over the Blues at the bet365 Stadium on Saturday, as Potters wing-back Luca Bombino sends a message to the home supporters.

Stoke have climbed to 2nd in the Championship table after beating Birmingham at the weekend, remaining level on points with Middlesbrough, but with a slightly worse goal difference than the Teesside outfit.

The Potters, following their victory over Blues, have become the first club in the division to win ten league games this season, as one player has sent a message to supporters after his day ended early.

George Hirst's goal just after half-time would prove to be the decisive one, as the Scottish forward netted just his second goal for Stoke following his potential £10 million move from Ipswich Town over the summer.

With the home side in control and looking for a second, American wing-back Luca Bombino mistimed his challenge on Patrick Roberts whilst already on a yellow card, as referee Robert Madley had no choice but to give the 20-year-old his marching orders just past the hour mark.

Stoke withstood everything Birmingham could throw at them, and Bombino's red card ultimately didn't affect the result, as the American has taken to social media to share a message with supporters.

In his post, the 20-year-old apologised for his mistake, made known how proud he is of his teammates' performance despite his blunder, and thanked the Stoke fans for their support.

His red card means he will miss his side's Championship home clash with Millwall on Wednesday evening, where either Eric Bocat or Justin Devenny will presumably take his role at left wing-back, as Stoke supporters have shared their thoughts over Bombino's red.

After three defeats to open the campaign, Stoke have responded with an incredible ten wins in their next 13 league games and are flying.
