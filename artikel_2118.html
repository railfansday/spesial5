3 players who West Ham should offload in January, including £27m striker

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

A poor run of form has left West Ham outside the Championship play-offs in 9th, with several high-earning bench-warmers potentially looking to seek pastures new. WHUFC will also be keen to bolster their squad with new stars, but equally, shifting those on hefty wages could prove to be just as important.

New boss Scott Parker has taken charge of two games so far, salvaging a point at Cardiff City before losing to Blackburn Rovers. He will undoubtedly use this international break to assess which players are part of his plans for the remainder of the season and beyond.

Here, we take a look at three players West Ham should look to offload in the upcoming window as they bid to streamline their squad.

Despite the poor form of Mads Hermansen in the early stages of the campaign, the Danish shot-stopper is the undisputed number one with Alphonse Areola.

Areola hasn’t been the go-to number one since the 2023/24 campaign and is yet to feature this term. Having played a key role in their UEFA Europa Conference League triumph, the 33-year-old was one of several players West Ham tried to part company with in the summer, but his wages proved a stumbling block.

Academy graduate Finlay Herrick has shown plenty of promise and appears ahead of Areola in the pecking order, so a January departure may suit all parties to facilitate other moves.

Another example of a player who was keen to depart in the summer, Soungoutou Magassa has played just 45 minutes of football this season after strong links with Crystal Palace.

The move to West Ham’s London neighbours failed to come to fruition, and he was far down the pecking order under Nuno Espirito Santo. Magassa started at Cardiff City but failed to make an impression and was benched again versus Blackburn Rovers, with the likes of Arne Engels and Tomas Soucek more suited to Parker’s system in a deeper midfield role.

A January departure from WHUFC looks inevitable at the right price, and would likely suit all parties despite the belief in his potential.

After signing for £27m from Lazio in January, Taty Castellanos has notched a solitary Championship goal this season.

Rumours of a summer departure were put to bed and he was viewed as a key figure in helping to spearhead West Ham’s promotion push. However, the Argentinian striker has often been criticised for his work rate and has struggled to replicate the form he showed last term.

River Plate reportedly view the 28-year-old as a priority target in their striker search, although the finances involved in the deal could prove to be a major hurdle. However, cashing in could give WHUFC the chance to sign a true talisman to aid their promotion bid.
