Two goals, two assists: Derby County starlet staking claim for future first-team role as loan form continues

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Derby County saw Owen Eames break into their first-team picture last term. The youngster has made six appearances for the DCFC senior side after impressive academy displays brought him onto John Eustace‘s radar.

But, the call was made to send him out on loan for the 2026/27 campaign to find more game time. Scottish Premiership side Falkirk snapped Eames up, and he impressed early on with a goal against Hearts and drew high praise from boss John McGlynn.

Over the weekend, Falkirk played Hearts for the second time this season, and Eames was on the scoresheet once again. Playing on their left wing, the Derby County loanee is earning rave reviews and conversations over his next steps are bound to follow.

Eames netted against Hearts in August and followed that up with an assist in the 1-1 draw against St. Johnstone in the match after. On November 1st, he set up Scott Tanser for the second goal in a 2-0 triumph over league leaders Celtic.

And, in his next league appearance last Sunday, Eames netted the Bairns’ second in a rampant win over Hearts. Falkirk countered after defending a corner and the Derby County loanee showed pace to offer a passing option, before duly side-footing home.

Eames was a constant handful, getting three shots off and completing three dribbles. In one instance, he pulled off a slick one-footed drag-back past one man and rouletted around the other while being grappled to cut inside and get a shot off on goal.

Eames now has four goal contributions in nine games for Falkirk – two goals, and two assists. He has started every Scottish Premiership game since his arrival, locking down a starting spot and helping their five-match winning run to move up to 3rd in the table.

Eames’ first loan away from DCFC couldn’t be going much better, and it will be hoped he can continue in this vein.

Eames’ form has raised questions over the decision to loan him out in the first place, and some are wondering whether there is an option to bring him back to Derby County in January. It has not been publicly reported whether they have a recall clause at present.

At the moment though, it feels as though the youngster is in the perfect place for his development. He’s playing well and getting plenty of minutes for McGlynn’s Falkirk, and it will be doing wonders for his progress.

On his current trajectory, Eames seems bound to be considered for a place in the first-team squad in 2027. It just remains to be seen whether DCFC recall him if they can, or if the exciting prospect has to wait until next season for his Pride Park breakout opportunity.
