Why Taylor Swift Hasn’t Been Seen at Travis Kelce’s Chiefs-Broncos Game in Denver

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Taylor Swift has attended all of Travis Kelce’s home games this year, but during the Kansas City Chiefs’ away game against the Denver Broncos today, the singer wasn’t seen at Empower Field.

Swift was out earlier in the day in New York City, where she participated in a Q&A during a Toy Story 5 screening. She wrote the film’s song “I Knew It, I Knew You,” which has Oscar buzz.

Swift hasn’t been spotted at any of Kelce’s away games for the past two seasons, with security risks reportedly factoring into the decision. The tight end has a home game next Sunday, though, so she could be out in full force to support him and his teammates then as they face off against the New York Jets at Arrowhead Stadium.

While she has regularly attended Kelce’s games throughout their three-year relationship, Swift has never deliberately drawn attention to her appearances. As she told Time in December 2023, “I’m just there to support Travis. I have no awareness of if I’m being shown too much and pissing off a few dads, Brads, and Chads.”

“I don’t know how they know what suite I’m in,” she added. “There’s a camera, like, a half-mile away, and you don’t know where it is, and you have no idea when the camera is putting you in the broadcast, so I don’t know if I’m being shown 17 times or once.”

Last year, Swift attended some of Kelce’s games without being photographed. Over the course of her career, she has mastered the art of entering and leaving places undetected. In an October 2025 interview with Scott Mills on BBC Radio 2, she revealed that this would be her greatest asset as a spy.

“Just put me in a garbage can, roll me, I don’t care,” she said. “Honestly, I can fit in like, a purse. Sometimes I just can’t deal with it [the paparazzi and attention] and in those times, I won’t deal with it. I’m just digging tunnels under every building I go into, airlifting in through the skylight.”
