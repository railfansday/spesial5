Supercomputer Predicts 2026-27 Championship Table

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The final 2026/27 Championship table has been predicted in full by a Supercomputer following the opening months of the new season.

West Ham United, Burnley and Wolverhampton Wanderers dropped into English football's second tier after suffering relegation from the Premier League last season. That trio - which, for the first time in four years, was not made up of the same three clubs that had won promotion the season before - will all be desperate to bounce straight back at the first attempt.

Meanwhile, Lincoln City, Cardiff City and Bolton Wanderers would surely be more than content with consolidation after securing their Championship status on the back of impressive League One campaigns. For those clubs, simply keeping their heads above water and establishing themselves at a higher level may be viewed as success.

However, if the Supercomputer's projections, courtesy of Opta (as of 08/11/26), are to be believed, there could be plenty of twists and turns ahead. The Championship rarely follows a script and has a habit of throwing up surprises when least expected, and this predicted final table suggests several clubs may be in for a rollercoaster ride. In fact, the standings look markedly different from how many supporters and pundits would have expected the season to unfold.

Preston North End have been perennial mid-table finishers in the Championship for over a decade. However, after a dismal start to the 2026/27 campaign, Opta's Supercomputer thinks they will finish rock bottom this campaign and drop to League One alongside Derby County and Watford.

Charlton Athletic enjoyed a tremendous start to the 2026/27 campaign, picking up victories against Derby County, West Ham United and Preston. They couldn't maintain that incredible run, though, plummeting down the table following an 11-match winless run. They are now predicted to survive by the skin of their teeth after beating Sheffield United.

Burnley, who were relegated from the Premier League in 25/26, are expected to finish 20th with just 51 points. That would be a huge disappointment for the Lancashire side, who would have had aspirations of returning to England's top tier at the first time of asking.

Lincoln CIty are riding the crest of promotion, but it is set to be a tough campaign for them, having lost manager Michael Skubala to Bristol City in the off-season. However, wins against Bolton, Bristol City, Preston and Burnley have helped rocket them up into a much safer predicted position.

Bolton Wanderers are back in the Championship following a long time away. After winning five of their opening 15 games, they're expected to avoid relegation.

After coming from 4-1 down to beat Burney 5-4, Cardiff City are expected to finish eight points clear of the relegation zone and finish 17th, a place below Bristol City.

The Robins have firmly established themselves in the English second tier, qualifying for the playoffs in the 2024/25 season. However, despite success in recent years, the Supercomputer thinks they will struggle and finish in the bottom half.

Blackburn Rovers have flirted with relegation in recent years, with fans of the club regularly venting their frustration with their owners, the Venky's. They always find a way to escape the drop, though, and are expected to have more than enough to stay up once again this season.

Wrexham narrowly failed to make the playoffs last season as they finished seventh. After a very slow start to the season, they are again expected to miss out, with the Supercomputer predicting them to finish in a disappointing 14th with 62 points.

Millwall came so close to achieving promotion to England's top tier in 2025/26, eventually having to settle for third place and a spot in the playoffs. Opta's Supercomputer thinks they will just miss out on a playoff berth this time round.

Portsmouth were at threat of relegation in the 2025/26 campaign, but they avoided the drop after improving in the final months. They've had an impressive start to the campaign and are being predicted to finish in the top half.

Sheffield United endured a torrid campaign in 25/26. At one point, it looked as if they would have a battle on their hands to maintain their Championship status. They eventually finished mid-table, and they are predicted to end this campaign in a similar position.

The 2026/27 season will be the first year in which six teams qualify for the playoffs, expanded from four.

Norwich City ended the 2025/26 campaign on fire and would have been expecting to start 26/27 in the same manner. After a awful start to the campaign, they now occupy 12th place after four victories in a row. The Supercomputer thinks they'll end up finishing just outside the playoff places.

Queens Park Rangers have had a decent start to the campaign, picking up 24 points from their opening 15 matches. The Supercomputer thinks they'll fall short in their bid to make the playoffs, though.

Stoke City spent big in the 2026 summer transfer window, with Ethan Galbraith and George Hirst among the players who arrived for big money. Their decision to spend big has paid dividends so far, with the club currently flying high in third. The Supercomputer thinks they'll just about scrape into the playoffs, though.

Swansea City had an electric start to the season and led the way after 10 matches. However, after four defeats in their last five matches, they are now predicted to finish sixth with 73 points.

Tom Brady's Birmingham City are expected to have a shot at promotion this season. They are being tipped to finish the campaign in fourth with 78 points, two places above Midlands rivals West Bromwich Albion.

West Ham, the favourites in the eyes of many to win the Championship and return to the Premier League at the first time of asking before a ball was kicked, had an awful start to the campaign. They failed to win their opening three matches, with draws against Burnley and Watford, as well as a 2-1 defeat to Charlton at the London Stadium.

The Hammers climbed up the table after that poor start, but, after their form took a turn for the worse once more, Nuno Espirito Santo lost his job in early November and was replaced by Scott Parker. After going winless in Parker's first two matches in charge, they are now expected to miss out on an automatic promotion berth.

Southampton started the campaign on -4 points after they were punished for spying on several other teams in the 2025/26 campaign. Despite that disadvantage, the Supercomputer thinks the Saints will finish third after winning eight of their opening 15 matches.

Wolverhampton Wanderers boast an enviable mix of youthful excitement and older, wiser players, so with the resources at their disposal, it would be a shock if they didn't get that automatic promotion place. The Supercomputer thinks they will just about seal their return to the Premier League at the first time of asking.

Middlesbrough, who were beaten by Hull City in the playoff final in May, will be delighted if they don't have to go through the playoffs again this term as they chase a return to the Premier League. Despite dropping points to Watford in their last outing, Kim Hellberg's side are predicted to be crowned champions with 82 points.
