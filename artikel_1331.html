Champions League: Porto vs. Napoli – probable line-ups, where to watch on TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Injury-hit Napoli visit Porto in the Champions League tonight, with Rasmus Hojlund expected to return to the line-up, but Kevin De Bruyne unlikely to start due to an ankle problem.

It kicks off at 21:00 CET [20:00 GMT] at the Estádio do Dragão.

Napoli have only 13 outfield players available for selection, including De Bruyne, who was subbed off in Sunday’s 2-0 loss to Juventus with a twisted ankle.

Multiple sources, including Sky Sports and Gazzetta, claim that the Belgian is unlikely to start, so Billy Gilmour will get the nod in central midfield, replacing Scott McTominay and Zambo Anguissa.

Hojlund missed the most recent Serie A match against Juventus with the flu, but he has recovered and is ready to start. The Denmark international will partner Giovane up front, while Noa Lang will play slightly out of position as a left midfielder.

Alisson Santos, Costantino Favasuli, David Neres, Benoit Badiashile, Leonardo Spinazzola, Stanislav Lobotka, Giovanni Di Lorenzo and Mathias Olivera are injured.

Italian coach Francesco Farioli is in charge of Porto and is expected to field some familiar faces for Serie A fans: ex-Spezia defender Jakub Kiwior, ex-Juventus defender Alberto Costa and ex-Milan striker André Silva.

TNT Sports 5 and HBO Max will broadcast the match live in the UK and Ireland. Football Italia will provide live updates via a live blog.

Porto (4-3-3): Diogo Costa; Alberto Costa, Bednarek, Kiwior, Zaidu; Pablo Rosario, Froholdt, Gabri Veiga; Pepê, André Silva, Borja Sainz

Napoli (3-5-2): Meret; Beukema, Rrahmani, Rafa Marin; Politano, Anguissa, Gilmour, McTominay, Lang; Giovane, Hojlund.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
