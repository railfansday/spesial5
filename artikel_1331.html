Juventus and Gonçalo Ramos react as Cristiano Ronaldo confirms international retirement

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Juventus and Gonçalo Ramos were among those reacting on social media to Cristiano Ronaldo’s international retirement, sending messages to the Portuguese superstar on Instagram.

Former Juventus star Cristiano Ronaldo announced his international retirement on Thursday with a touching video published on Instagram.

The striker’s announcement quickly went viral, drawing several likes and comments on the platform.

Among those reacting to his retirement were his compatriot Gonaçalo Ramos, a Milan striker, and Ronaldo’s former club Juventus.

Juventus commented on Ronaldo’s video with two emojis: a red heart and a face holding back tears.

Ramos wrote in Portuguese: “Thank you for everything.”

His Milan teammate Diego Moreira reposted Ronaldo’s video on Instagram. The Belgian international recently spoke about CR7’s influence on his career, saying the Portuguese is his favourite player and biggest role model.

Italy international and Fiorentina midfielder Nicolò Fagioli, a former teammate of Cristiano Ronaldo at Juventus, commented on Ronaldo’s statement in just one word and a few emojis, calling the Portuguese superstar a “legend.”
