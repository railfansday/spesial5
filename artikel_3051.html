Iowa high school football playoff, IHSAA semifinal matchups released

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

It's officially time for indoor football.

The quarterfinal round of the Iowa high school football playoffs wrapped up on Friday, Nov. 6, and now only four teams are left standing in each of the state's seven classes. The remaining semifinal and state championship matchups will take place at the UNI-Dome in Cedar Falls.

Here is the semifinal schedule for every class, which was released by the IHSAA on Nov. 6.

Southeast Polk vs. Dowling Catholic, 7 p.m.

Both games scheduled for Thursday, Nov. 12

Carlisle vs. Sergeant Bluff-Luton, 4 p.m.

Both games scheduled for Saturday, Nov. 14

Clear Lake vs. Des Moines Christian, 7 p.m.

Both games scheduled for Saturday, Nov. 14

Aplington-Parkersburg vs. West Lyon, 1 p.m.

Both games scheduled for Thursday, Nov. 12

Maquoketa Valley vs. Woodbury Central, 10 a.m.

Both games scheduled for Wednesday, Nov. 11

No. 6 Gladbrook-Reinbeck vs. No. 1 Audubon, 1 p.m.

No. 3 Woodbine vs. No. 2 Iowa Valley, 4 p.m.
