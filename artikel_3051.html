Stock Market Today, Nov. 11: IREN Rises as AI Cloud Peers Rally on CoreWeave Results

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Trading volume ran well above its three-month average as the AI data center operator rode a sector rally, today, Nov. 11, 2026.

IREN (IREN +5.63%), a vertically integrated data center operator, closed at $20.84, up 5.63%. The stock rose as AI cloud peers gained, with CoreWeave's strong results easing financing and demand concerns. Investors are watching IREN's AI cloud execution and its next update on Microsoft deliveries and spending plans.Trading volume reached 80.3M shares, coming in about 58% above its three-month average of 50.7M shares. IREN IPO'd in 2021 and has fallen 15% since going public.

The S&P 500 closed at 7,618, up 1.29%, while the Nasdaq Composite closed at 25,466, up 1.60%. Among AI cloud infrastructure and Bitcoin mining data centers, Core Scientific closed at $14.57, up 10.97%, and Cipher Digital closed at $7.11, up 8.88%, showing the same sector rotation that lifted IREN.

As previously stated, IREN's rise was likely in response to CoreWeave, which delivered a stellar earnings report that showed growth, pricing power, and, most importantly, the ability to manage financing.

Financing has become critical to neocloud-oriented cloud stocks, which have run massive losses as they ramp up spending to meet the insatiable demand for AI-specific cloud infrastructure.

Moreover, the stock rose even though CoreWeave's success did not directly address IREN's struggles. This is critical, since, in recent weeks, investors soured on IREN stock amid a $4 billion ARR target that had slipped.

Also, news that some infrastructure deliveries to Microsoft were delayed due to requested modifications. Although that was likely not IREN's fault, it served as a reminder that delays could negatively affect IREN due to its heavy reliance on credit.

Indeed, investors can breathe a sigh of relief, as the rally takes some near-term pressure off IREN and its peers. Nonetheless, a funding gap remains, and amid rapid growth, the stock is unlikely to be out of the woods as it seeks to capitalize on that growth while meeting its considerable debt obligations.
