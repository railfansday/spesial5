Serie A Liveblog: Roma-Sassuolo, Parma-Bologna, Cagliari-Frosinone, Torino-Lecce

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Join us for all the build-up and action from today’s four Serie A games, building up to Roma vs. Sassuolo, after Cagliari-Frosinone, Torino-Lecce and local derby Parma vs. Bologna.

We begin at 14.00 GMT (15.00 CET) with high-flying Frosinone visiting Cagliari and Torino up against Lecce, then at 17.00 GMT (18.00 CET) there’s the Emilia-Romagna Derby at the Stadio Tardini.

At 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET), Roma try to shake off the Manchester United debacle and keep up in the Scudetto race against Alberto Aquilani’s Sassuolo.

Roma made short work of sorry Sassuolo with Mario Hermoso, an own goal and Rodrigo Mora, inflicting a fifth consecutive defeat on Alberto Aquilani’s side.

Fali Cande’ on for Sassuolo, Caleta-Car off.

It’s still all Roma, Pisilli strike charged down in the box!

Pisilli breaks down the left and cuts inside, right-foot finish to force a save out of Muric at the near post!

Castro and Koulierakis on, Malen and Hermoso off

Malen dribbles down the left, gets around Muric, but doesn’t get enough power or precision on the pull-back, allowing Caleta-Car to clear in the six-yard box!

Lipani and Adzic on, Thorstvedt and Sebastiano Esposito off for Sassuolo.

Nahuel Molina runs down the right and his angled drive goes wide, partly due to pressure from Caleta-Car.

Pisilli and De Roon on for Roma, Kone and Rodrigo Mora off.

Sudden Sassuolo strike from distance, but also well wide of the target from Sulemana.

Malen involved again, completing the give and go with Mora to drill through Caleta-Car’s legs into the far bottom corner from 14 yards!

Malen sent through by Hermoso, but Muric parries the central strike!

Roma continue to not score, pull-back from the right for Kone, desperate interception from a Sassuolo defender, then Mora attempt charged down too.

Malen into the box again, this time he fires wide onto the side-netting.

Soule’ runs onto a through ball, dribbles around the goalkeeper, could score into an empty net, instead tries to roll it across for Malen, who isn’t expecting it and falls over because his body is in the wrong position…

Sassuolo scrap the 3-5-2 formation, then.

Bowie and Van Der Brempt on for Sassuolo, Matic and Cinquegrano off.
