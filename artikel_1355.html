Eni Aluko: What Happened to Former England Star After Quitting UK Punditry

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Eni Aluko now leads a very different life, having taken on a completely new role just nine months after being forced to rethink her position in UK punditry following a distasteful public row with Arsenal and England legend Ian Wright.

Aluko, a former England international herself, crossed the line in April 2025 by accusing the national treasure of blocking pathways for female pundits. However, the irony wasn't lost on many, particularly after Wrighty's comments about her during the 2022 World Cup resurfaced.

She had claimed that her former friend's presence in the women's game was "dominating" roles and occupying space she believed should go to others.

But after spending the following months attempting to rebuild her reputation as a pundit, Aluko eventually decided to walk away from the job altogether in early 2026, having reopened old wounds and been ruthlessly shut down by Simon Jordan.

Nowadays, Aluko appears keen to distance herself from an environment that brought so much toxicity. And while she remains involved in football, her current role couldn't be further removed from the one that once saw her dissecting the beautiful game's latest issues and talking points.

Aluko, now 39, was most recently pictured attending the 2026 Ballon d'Or ceremony, where she was able to pose for photos with men's winner Harry Kane.

That is no surprise given that she's now involved in international football administration, global business ventures, and select overseas broadcasting after stepping away from UK television.

In April 2026, she was appointed to the CAF Women's Taskforce to help elevate and develop women's football across Africa on an international scale.

From Gary Neville to Alex Scott to Mark Chapman, UK pundits are paid very handsomely for the roles of television and radio.

Aluko also remains involved with FC Como Women (via her investment with Mercury 13), Charity:Water, and she holds global advocacy roles with UEFA and the United Nations (including outreach on cervical cancer prevention and vaccination confidence through Goal Getters).

While she had chosen to stay away from major British broadcasting companies, she does also work as a football pundit and analyst for SuperSport in Nigeria from time to time - in the country she was born.

In the event that Aluko grows tired of the sport she grew up playing entirely, then the former Chelsea striker does have a fall-back option, having studied for a law degree alongside her time as a footballer more than 10 years ago.

She studied law at Brunel University, where she graduated with a first class degree in 2008. Seven years later, she would announce that she'd take a step back from that industry to pursue a sporting career many could only dream about having.

But during that period, she became a qualified lawyer, meaning that she's certainly well-prepared for life after football if that day eever arrives.
