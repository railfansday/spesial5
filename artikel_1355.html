Milan substitute Ramos as a precaution after first goal in over a month

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Gonçalo Ramos was substituted early in the second half of Milan’s game against Frosinone, but the striker’s withdrawal appears to be only a precaution.

The Portugal international scored in the 19th minute at San Siro, converting Adrien Rabiot’s assist to make it 2-0. It was his first goal since 18 October, ending a run of more than a month without scoring.

Ramos was replaced by Alphadjo Cissè in the 51st minute, after he was seen hobbling. According to Sky Sport Italia, he’d felt a twinge and the change was purely precautionary, with nothing serious feared.

It would be a relief for Ruben Amorim, who relies heavily on Ramos as his first-choice No 9. The Rossoneri plan to sign a striker in January, with Endrick and Nicolò Tresoldi among the names linked, and Francesco Camarda expected to leave on loan.
