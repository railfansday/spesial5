Serie A | Milan 5-1 Frosinone: Magic Manita for Amorim’s side

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Milan put in a masterclass of counter-attacking football and tore Frosinone to shreds with five goals, including Goncalo Ramos, Christian Pulisic, and Ardon Jashari’s first for the club.

The Rossoneri had Mike Maignan after shaking off a knock on international duty, but Strahinja Pavlovic was not at 100 per cent, with Matteo Gabbia, Samuel Chukwueze, Fikayo Tomori, Ruben Loftus-Cheek and Alexis Saelemaekers still injured.

The Canarini were in sixth place, just four points behind Ruben Amorim’s side, although they went into the break off a 1-0 defeat to Cagliari after winning three of the previous four. Antonio Raimondo and Florian Grillitsch were still out, plus Gabriele Bracaglia suspended, so they surprisingly moved to a 3-4-3 formation with Romano Schmid as a False 9.

It was a lightning start for the visitors, as after 90 seconds Patrizio Masini’s effort was deflected off Sankhoun Diawara to clip the crossbar at the top corner.

However, Milan opened the scoring moments later when Adrien Rabiot sprung the offside trap on the Luka Modric through ball and rolled across for what was meant to be a Christian Pulisic tap-in, but Gabriele Calvani’s interception just prodded into his own net.

Giorgi Kvernadze tested Mike Maignan at the near post, then on the resulting corner nobody was ready to tap in the Giorgio Cittadini ball flashed across the six-yard box.

Milan doubled their lead with a classic counter-attack, which was started by Goncalo Ramos winning back the ball from Calvani. Diego Moreira sprinted onto the Yunus Musah through ball, rolled across for Rabiot, who in turn set up the Portugal international for a simple tap-in. It was his first goal at club level since October 18.

Frosinone did get back into the game when Kvernadze pulled back from the by-line and it was beyond Masini, but after Fares Ghedjemis only scuffed his attempt, it fell kindly for Masini at full stretch to toe poke it past the on-rushing Maignan.

The referee apologised for not playing the advantage with Goncalo Ramos on goal, as he stopped play to book Cittadini for a foul on Rabiot.

Ardon Jashari was introduced for the second half, moving Musah to left-back, and it paid off immediately with the midfielder’s first goal in the Rossoneri jersey.

Ramos knocked down a long ball for Rabiot, whose through ball sent Jashari running down the left, but rather than roll across for the centre-forward, he blasted it into the roof of the net from a tight angle with the left foot.

Frosinone kept pushing and only a desperate Musah interception prevented Anthony Oyono meeting the Schmid roll across, then Goncalo Ramos went off after feeling a twinge.

His replacement was Alphadjo Cisse, who promptly also scored off the bench. A Frosinone corner was cleared, Pulisic’s cross-field pass sent Moreira running, and Cisse was on target from the through ball. It was at first ruled offside, until VAR confirmed the Italy international was level.

Milan struck on the counter from another Frosinone corner for 5-1, as Pulisic robbed Aleksa Terzic inside his own half to sprint forward and dinked the delicate finish over Palmisani.

Moreira’s finish was too weak to trouble Palmisani on yet another Rabiot assist, and it went from bad to worse for Frosinone. Daniel Birligea didn’t start because he wasn’t fully fit, and limped off with a hamstring issue at the 82nd minute, leaving them down to 10 men with all the substitutions completed.

Milan were practically down to 10 too, as Strahinja Pavlovic made his comeback after illness, but was feeling dizzy and barely jogging for the closing stages.
