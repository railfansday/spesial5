Dewsbury-Hall Hails Work-Rate Of In-Form Teammate

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall believes Thierno Barry's impressive form for Everton is the product of relentless work to improve his game behind the scenes.

Barry has notched 13 goal involvements (10 goals, three assists) in all competitions so far this season, most recently a quick-fire brace in the Blues' 3-1 come-from-behind victory over Coventry City prior to the international break.

The French striker also produced the assist for Dewsbury-Hall's goal against Frank Lampard's side – and it comes as no surprise to the Toffees' No.8 to see his teammate thriving.

"I'm so happy for Thierno," Dewsbury-Hall told evertontv. "He's reaping the rewards for how hard he works. He's on the training pitch every day, working at his game.

"He's playing pretty much every minute for us, at the moment, which is tough in itself.

"Cream always rises [to the top] when you work hard. I'm a big believer in that.

"I know he'll keep working, keep a level head and I think he'll keep getting better and better, because he's got the right attitude."

Dewsbury-Hall has also praised the collective mentality of Everton this season.

He added: "[The Coventry game] was another example of us reacting positively to a setback. I think on the night their goal actually kicked us into gear, because it wasn't the best first half from us.

"I thought we reacted unbelievably, though, and we blew them away in the final half hour or so, which was important to top off a really good week for us.

"We've been in pretty much every game this season, even if we haven't been at our best, which is pleasing."
