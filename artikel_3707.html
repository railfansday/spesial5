Pete Davidson Unwittingly Reveals Nicholas Hoult and Wife Welcomed Twins

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Oops! Pete Davidson has seemingly revealed that his How to Rob a Bank costar Nicholas Hoult and his wife, Bryana Holly, quietly welcomed twins.

“[Nicholas is] a great dad, father of four,” Davidson, 32, declared during a Wednesday, November 4, Entertainment Tonight interview alongside castmates Hoult, 36, and Anna Sawai, adding, “[Nicholas has] four under what? 7 [years old] … which is nuts.”

Hoult nodded along to the comic’s surprising revelation without adding any further context.

Us has reached out to Hoult’s representative for comment.

The Man of Tomorrow star and his spouse, Holly, were previously confirmed to have welcomed two children — born in 2018 and 2022, respectively. (The couple have chosen to keep their children’s names private and have never publicly disclosed any details about their births.)

There had been some speculation earlier in the year that they’d welcomed two more babies when Holly clapped back at people body-shaming her for wearing a plunging halter-style dress at the Vanity Fair Oscar Party in March.

Holly responded to “the comments around my chest” online, explaining that her red carpet look was “the product of a breastfeeding mother who hasn’t fed her babies in hours.” At the time, the model did not elaborate on the arrival of her and Hoult’s twins.

The subject of parenthood came up in the How to Rob a Bank cast’s interview this week when a reporter pointed out that Davidson and his ex Elsie Hewitt‘s daughter, Scottie Rose Hewitt Davidson, will celebrate her first birthday in December. (Davidson and Hewitt, 30, split in May but continue to coparent Scottie.)

Davidson revealed that the timeline of him becoming a first-time dad matched up with Hoult making weekend trips home to visit his newborn twins.

“Elsie was pregnant while we were filming and [Hoult] just had his twins,” Davidson told Entertainment Tonight. “He would fly back every weekend to go see his family, and that was very inspiring and awesome.”

Hoult and Holly have largely kept their relationship out of the public eye since they first got together in 2017. It is not definitively known when they tied the knot, though Hoult referred to Holly as his “wife” at the Governors Awards in November 2024.

At the gala event, Hoult told People that it was “fairly clear” from his reference to Holly as his “wife” that they’d privately exchanged vows.

“I feel like it’s fairly [obvious] from my comments,” he added with a grin.

Prior to his marriage to Holly, Hoult famously had a three-year romance with former costar Jennifer Lawrence while working together on the X-Men movies.

Davidson and Hoult’s new movie How to Rob a Bank — an action heist comedy directed by John Wick and Deadpool 2 filmmaker David Leitch — will be released in theaters on Friday, November 13.
