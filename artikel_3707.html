A-One, A Two-Hoo, A-Three…

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Jensen Huang may have already resigned as CEO of Nvidia, but the reports of infidelity are still coming. The outgoing executive has now been accused of a third affair, and the second involving one of his employees.

Reuters reports that, according to “two people familiar with the matter,” Huang “had an intimate relationship with a second employee.” The outlet is not naming the second employee, whom it says it was unable to get in contact with, and the other parties involved have not yet commented on the newest allegation.

Huang already admitted to one affair with a woman accused of being a Chinese spy, and Nvidia’s board of directors initiated an investigation into his conduct following that revelation. Then The New York Times reported that Huang was also caught having intimate interactions with Nvidia’s VP of healthcare.

The Nvidia founder resigned from the company today after more than 33 years, and the speed of the departure has certainly fueled speculation about just how many ants there really are hiding in that kitchen.

The company’s stock price slid off a cliff this week, shedding hundreds of billions in value overnight, but news that CFO Colette Kress will take over as interim CEO has seemingly reassured some investors that the AI gravy train will continue even without Huang.
