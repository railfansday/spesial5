New Details About Patriots Quarterback Drake Maye's Injury Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

New details about New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye have come to light.

Maye, 24, fractured his right fibula, according to a Friday, November 20, ESPN report. The outlet claimed the pro athlete’s injury likely won’t require surgery, though he is expected to be sidelined for a portion of the remaining NFL season.

Maye sustained an injury after he was sacked by Detroit Lions’ Aidan Hutchinson during their teams’ Sunday, November 15, clash in Munich. Both Maye and Hutchinson, 26, left the game after the play. (Hutchison suffered a season-ending knee injury from his sack celebration.)

While Drake has not publicly addressed his injury, wife Ann Michael Maye shared her support.

“ILY always,” she wrote via her Instagram Stories on Tuesday, November 17, alongside a heart emoji.

Drake and Ann Michael, 23, have been together since middle school before getting married in 2025. The baking influencer has become Drake’s biggest cheerleader ever since he was drafted by the Patriots in 2024.

“We have great teammates and great friends here that have really made the New England area feel like home, which is great,” Ann Michael exclusively told Us in August of moving to the northeast with her spouse. “[Making friends] just makes it feel so much more like home and makes it feel less like we’re so far away from our families and stuff.”

She continued at the time, “It’s made it so fun and it’s really cool that he is obviously friends with all of his teammates, and I’ve become friends with the wives. It’s one big community where we can all spend time together.”

Drake has also opened up about their adjustment relocating to the Boston area.

“It’s been awesome to follow her journey. I know [I] was looking forward to her getting up here,” he told reporters during a January press conference. “After we got married, she came up here and had never lived up north. [She] had never seen this much snow on the ground, so obviously it was an adjustment for her.”

Drake further gushed how Ann Michael has already found her strides in New England and as a football wife.

“She’s loved it, she’s loved the people up here, living up here [and] she loves the support,” he added at the time, going on to praise her social media success. “She’s got her own little journey that’s been awesome, being herself and that’s the best thing about her and what I love her for.”
