Snoop Dogg Bringing ‘A Not-So-Silent Night’ Into Holiday Homes Thanks to ‘Christmas With Snoop’ Special

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Guests including Jamie Foxx & Martha Stewart are expected to pull up to enjoy some trees & eggnog.

Snoop Dogg is hosting a Christmas special.

The West Coast rap legend and media personality will be in America’s homes on NBC right after the lighting of the Christmas tree at Rockefeller Center on Dec. 2. The star-studded affair will feature appearances and musical performances from guests like Wayne Brady, Kristin Chenoweth, Adam Devine, Jamie Foxx and his daughter Anelise Foxx, Jane Handcock, October London, Craig Robinson, Nicole Scherzinger, Gwen Stefani and Snoop’s bestie Martha Stewart.

“Snoop is excited to make new Christmas traditions as he spends part of the holiday in his plush hotel suite celebrating with friends and loved ones,” reads the press release.

The show is executive produced by Snoop Dogg and Sara Ramaker for Death Row Pictures along with Jesse Collins, Dionne Harmon and Jeannae Rouzan-Clay for Jesse Collins Entertainment.

A Not-So-Silent Night: Christmas With Snoop will air on NBC on Wednesday, Dec. 2, at 10 p.m. ET/PT following the airing of Christmas in Rockefeller Center and will be made available to stream on Peacock the day after.

Snoop has been keeping himself busy on film sets these days. He’s been bringing his biopic to life, as casting news has been coming in almost daily it seems. Most recently, Cynthia Erivo joined the production to play 2Pac‘s late mother Afeni Shakur. Snoop will be directed by Hustle & Flow‘s Craig Brewer, while 8 Mile producer Brian Grazer will be producing alongside the Doggystyle rapper and it’s set to hit theaters on Aug. 6, 2027. Snoop was inspired to tell his story after watching N.W.A tell theirs. “After my brothers told their story in Straight Outta Compton, it’s my turn,” he said at CinemaCon.
