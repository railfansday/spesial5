Okafor the difference despite Van Hecke defiance - Leeds v Spurs ratings

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Leeds United's Nico Elvedi challenges Spurs striker Omar Marmoush in the Premier League game at Elland Road

Leeds United ended Spurs' mini-revival with a late goal from Noah Okafor giving them victory at Elland Road.

Here's how I rated the players - and you can see how you scored them below.

James Trafford: Always alert coming off his line when danger threatened. Safe handling. Vital late save from Van Hecke in the closing moments. 6

James Justin: Good deliveries and solid in defence. 6

Nico Elvedi: Won his physical battle with Marmoush. Headed a good chance wide. 6

Tarik Muharemovic: Very solid display. A bargain. 6

Jayden Bogle: Good going forward but can consider himself very fortunate not to get a red card for a late challenge on Mathys Tel. 5

Ethan Ampadu: Leeds United's captain kept things ticking over in midfield and always a strong presence. 7

Sean Longstaff: Booking for a late challenge on Fernandes. Saw a close-range effort saved. 6

Ao Tanaka: Worked hard but missed a great second-half opportunity. 6

Gabriel Gudmundsson: Excellent first half with some lively runs. Shame he had to come off at half-time. 7

Dominic Calvert-Lewin: Hard-working and unselfish when he set up Tanaka for a chance he should have taken. 6

Lukas Nmecha: Struggling. Headed straight at Kinsky when he should have scored. 5

Melvin Bard (on 45, Gudmundsson): Some fine forward passes in a decent display. 6

Noah Okafor (on 77, Lukas Nmecha). Inspirational substitution by Daniel Farke. Clean finish two minutes after coming on. 7

Harry Wilson (on 77, Ao Tanaka). Tried to create. 5

Pedro Porro: Always willing to join the attack. Loves a tackle but probably taken off to avoid a red card. 6

Micky van de Ven: Dependable presence at the heart of the Spurs defence. One vital second-half clearance. Booked for hauling back Bogle. 6

Jan Paul van Hecke: One of his best games for Spurs. Powerful in the air and composed with the ball at his feet. Almost saved a point at the end. 7

Andrew Robertson: Solid defensively and always energetic in lending weight to attack. 6

Archie Gray: Some nice touches against his old club but generally quiet. 6

Sandro Tonali: Anonymous. Needed to do more. 5

Mohammed Kudus: Lots of endeavour but little end product. 5

Mathys Tel: One or two lively moments but unable to continue after Jayden Bogle's poor first-half challenge. 5
