Jennifer Love Hewitt Says Marriage With Brian Hallisay Is a “Choice”

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Jennifer Love Hewitt detailed why she sees her 13-year marriage to husband Brian Hallisay as an everyday “choice,” noting she feels “very fortunate” to have him as a partner.

Jennifer Love Hewitt is a fan of putting her middle name into practice.

More than a decade into her marriage with Brian Hallisay, the 9-1-1 actress shared why her “heart still flutters” when she thinks about her husband.

“For us, it’s a choice every day,” Hewitt told People in an interview published Nov. 16. “That’s the romantic part. I don’t ever expect him to choose me. I want to earn that choice every day.”

But while the 47-year-old admitted her romance with Hallisay “ebbs and flows,” she is always reminded of how much she loves him when they’re apart for too long.

“I still want him to be right there. And it’s a good feeling,” she continued. “I think we just feel lucky and we keep it going.”

Since meeting on set in 2011 and tying the knot in 2013, Hewitt and Hallisay have welcomed three kids: Autumn, 12, Atticus, 11, and Aidan, 5. And having her husband on her team is a heartwarming change of pace.

“I didn’t have that growing up myself, and so I feel very fortunate that I have it,” she added. “He’s a really good partner. He’s a great dad. And he’s just a good man. And so I feel very lucky to have him.”

While Hewitt may know what you did last summer, she’s also been adamant that the secret to her marriage is opting to be dedicated to one another.

“We, I think, struggle like most parents with three kids and full-time jobs and all of the things to find our time,” she told People in 2024, “but I really do wake up and choose Brian every day, and I would choose him every time.”

As Hewitt sees it, “I genuinely feel that way, even when he makes me crazy. And he continues to choose me, thank God.”
