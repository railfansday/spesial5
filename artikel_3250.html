FIGS: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Supply fixes in Vietnam and Indonesia are racing a U.S. import ban, and inventory is down 20% heading into a strong fourth quarter.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 5, 2026, FIGS (FIGS +5.55%) announced net revenues grew 26% year over year to approximately $191 million. The apparel maker is expanding gross margins and accelerating customer spend while navigating supply chain friction in Asia. Three things management said stand out.

International net revenue surged 51% year over year to $36.8 million, accounting for nearly 20% of total sales. Management is localizing digital marketing across Europe, Latin America, and the Middle East to capture a global total addressable market, or TAM, of over 100 million healthcare workers.

A Withhold Release Order, or WRO, an import restriction issued by U.S. customs, restricted supply from the company's Jordan manufacturer and dropped inventory 20% year over year to $121.3 million. Management is mitigating stockouts by expanding production capacity across Vietnam and Indonesia.

Through TEAMS, the company's business-to-business enterprise sales platform, management is securing institutional accounts to drive recurring orders. New partnerships with corporate healthcare providers are expanding distribution beyond direct sales to individual medical professionals.

Revenue momentum is building into the fourth quarter. The company relies on replenishment demand from medical professionals, strong customer retention, and expanding operating margins to navigate near-term inventory constraints.

Operating leverage is expanding as average order values rise and marketing expenses stabilize. Long-term gains depend on maintaining gross margin discipline while scaling international and institutional channels.
