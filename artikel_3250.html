Texas vs. LSU Livestream: How to Watch the College Football Game Online Free

College Football Playoff hopes are on the line tonight when the No. 6 Texas Longhorns face the No. 21 LSU Tigers at Death Valley in Baton Rouge.

The Longhorns are at 7-2 with three consecutive wins, including a decisive 34-20 victory over the No. 22 Missouri Tigers last weekend. LSU is at a solid 6-3, but they’re coming off a tough 31-13 home loss to the Alabama Crimson Tide.

It’s a critical game for both SEC teams, and the first time they’ll play as conference mates. They last met in 2019, when then-LSU quarterback Joe Burrow helped lead the Tigers to a 45-38 win.

If you don’t have a way to watch the Texas vs. LSU game tonight, read on. Ahead are a few ways to stream the game online without cable, including ways to get a free Texas vs. LSU livestream.

Texas and LSU face off on Saturday, Nov. 14. Kickoff is at 7:30 p.m. ET/4:30 p.m. PT. The game will be broadcast nationally on ABC.

Cord-cutters can watch the Texas vs. LSU football game online using any live TV streaming service that carries ABC or the ESPN Unlimited app. Below are your best options, including a few that offer free trials — use those to stream Texas vs. LSU for free without cable.

ABC is included in most DirecTV packages, including its smaller MyNews and MySports Genre Packs and all of its larger Streaming TV Packages. All DirecTV plans begin with a five-day free trial that you can use to watch Texas vs. LSU for free, and plans renew automatically starting at $39.99 after the trial ends.

Fubo is another great all-around live TV streamer that carries ABC in its Core and Ultra packages. Subscriptions start with a five-day free trial before plans renew starting at a discounted rate of $63.99.

YouTube TV carries ABC in both of its live TV streaming plans, and you get a free trial between five and 21 days to start. Once the trial ends, subscriptions renew at a discounted rate of $49.99 a month for the more affordable of the two plans.

Fans can also stream the Texas vs. LSU game online with ESPN Unlimited, which delivers all ESPN and ESPN on ABC content in one place. This is the most affordable option, with plans starting at $31.99 a month, but you don’t get a free trial as you do with the options above.
