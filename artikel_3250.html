Zosia Mamet Says ‘Girls’ Revival Was “In Talks For A Very Long Time”: “I Kind Of Gave Up”

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Nearly 10 years after last playing Shoshanna Shapiro, Zosia Mamet was beginning to think a Girls revival wasn’t in the cards.

The actress, who starred in the Lena Dunham-created HBO comedy, admitted that she wasn’t sure the recently announced revival wouldn’t happen after the five-episode project had been in development “for a very long time.”

“I mean, it’s been in talks for a very long time,” she explained to Entertainment Tonight. “But I kind of gave up because we’ve been talking about it for so long, and I don’t trust anything until I’m actually on set.”

Mamet added, “Once they officially made an offer, it kind of came together pretty fast. So, I didn’t have to keep a secret for too long, which is good, because I’m not a good secret keeper.” Watch on Deadline

Last month, series creator and star Dunham was joined by Mamet and co-stars Allison Williams, Jemima Kirke and Andrew Rannells for a video announcing the HBO Original revival, which begins production in 2027.

Zosia Mamet thinks the internet’s theories about Shoshanna’s storyline in the 'Girls' revival may be a little too accurate. 😭 pic.twitter.com/b9vL1Ljvg4

Dunham created and starred in Girls as millennial writer Hannah Horvath, navigating her 20s in New York City. The show ran for six seasons from 2012 to 2017.
