Twelve South’s new Cablesnap charger has a built-in retractable USB-C cable

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Popular Apple accessory maker Twelve South is launching a new retractable Cablesnap 65W travel charger this week. It has a built-in USB-C cable, alongside an additional USB-C port and USB-A port.

You can order the new Cablesnap 65W on Amazon today for $49.99.

We’ve seen travel chargers with retractable cables before, and Twelve South’s is similar. It has a 29-inch (75cm) spring-loaded USB-C cable built right in, so you can pull out exactly as much as you need. Then, when you’re ready to pack up, just give it a little pull, and it snaps back into the charging brick.

In addition to the retractable USB-C cable, there’s a separate USB-C port and a USB-A port for charging other accessories. It can charge at up to 65W speeds, shared across each connected device.

You can order the new Cablesnap 65W on Amazon today. This is just the latest in what’s been a busy few weeks for Twelve South. Last month, the accessory maker introduced the popular AirFly Drive adapter that converts wired CarPlay into wireless. A few weeks later it unveiled the AirFly View, an evolution of its wireless audio dongle that’s perfect for planes.
