Ingevity Corporation (NGVT -13.59%) reported third-quarter results characterized by stable performance in its core carbon business and continued execution of its portfolio transformation. Management noted that strength in North American automotive demand, particularly for hybrid vehicles, helped offset softer demand in European and Chinese markets. The Pavement Technologies segment experienced margin pressure due to significant asphalt cost inflation, which prompted customers to prioritize road preservation and maintenance over new construction projects. The company remains on track with its long-term share repurchase commitments while narrowing its full-year earnings outlook based on performance during the first nine months of the year. Efforts to divest the Advanced Polymer Technologies segment continue, with an announcement potentially extending into early 2027 to optimize shareholder value.
