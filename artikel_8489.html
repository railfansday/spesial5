Is it true that VG will review the DPOAs that we submit on VG's forms and then deem them acceptable so that no issue should arise at the time when our Agent needs to act, whether or not one of us is incapacitated (we will opt for immediate/unlimited powers for the Agent)? Also, is it fine if we input the DPOA information (i.e. full authorizations) using VG's online function?
If you use vanguards forms for the DPOA (full agent authority ) you don’t need to provide a lawyer drafted DPOA. You both would need Vanguard accounts and my recollection is you will have to print them to be notarized but I don’t recall for sure.
I haven't looked at the Vanguard forms in a long time, but in general the problem most of us have with similar forms isn't the notary requirement. A notary is free or very inexpensive for almost anyone to have done. The problem is witnesses, when they have to be present during the notary process. I've had other documents that needed witnesses, but not at the same time as the notary (so, the notary wasn't notarizing the witness process), and that's been easy - surprisingly you can usually ask any random person, even someone who doesn't know you, and they'll sign as a witness for your documents. But it's much more of a burden to drag multiple people at the same time to meet with a notary.
