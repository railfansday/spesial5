Disney+ streams Disney’s movies and shows for kids and families alongside Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars, and National Geographic titles, plus recent films such as Toy Story 5, which premiered there in September 2026. Its bundle plans add Hulu’s shows and movies inside the same app, and Disney says the two apps finish merging into Disney+ in December 2026.

Families who’ll stream Disney, Pixar, Marvel, or Star Wars titles most months get their money’s worth from Disney+. Households that want only the occasional new Disney release pay less by rotating it with other services, subscribing just for the months they’ll watch.
