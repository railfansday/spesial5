Christopher Lloyd, 88, Posts Tribute to Wife Lisa, 56, on 10th Anniversary

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Christopher Lloyd is celebrating his 10th anniversary with his wife, Lisa Lloyd (née Loiacono).

In a Monday, November 23, X post, the Back to the Future star, 88, shared a photo of him and his wife decked in all black at a recent charity event in honor of his Back to the Future costar Michael J. Fox’s Parkinson’s research foundation.

He captioned the post, “Oh Lisa… I love you more than words can simply put. Happy anniversary to us now and always ❤️❤️❤️❤️.”

Lloyd met Lisa, 56, in 2005 and they tied the knot in 2016.

The actor was previously married four times, first to Catherine Boyd from 1959 until their divorce in 1971, Kay Tornborg from 1974 to 1987, Carol Ann Vanek from 1988 to 1991, and finally Jane Walker Wood, whom he wed in 1992 and divorced in 2005.

Earlier this month, the couple opened up about their marriage ahead of their 10th anniversary together.

“I appreciate his focus and his calmness. Because I’m very outgoing and gregarious. And sometimes he won’t talk. So it’s a balance,” Lisa praised the actor in a joint interview with People.

Lloyd added, “Well, Lisa says I’m kind of calm, even though I may be going crazy inside! But I think I’m pretty easy to live with, get along with.”

“He reads all day long,” Lisa also shared, noting that Lloyd doesn’t go online. “I’ve been with him almost 22 years. My head’s full with a bunch of stupid stuff that you see on the internet… I watch how contemplative and in control he can be, and I’ve always been more hyper and outgoing than him.”

Lloyd, meanwhile, praised Lisa as being “full of energy and excitement to do this and that. She’s far more naturally social than I am. I’m not a social bug by nature, and it just seems the gel just comes together.”

In his recent memoir, In My Own Time, Lloyd opened up about his past addiction to cocaine.

According to an excerpt published by Page Six, Lloyd took his first “snort” of the substance in the 1970s after meeting a woman at a comedy club in Los Angeles. The actor followed her to her car and they “did a couple of lines.”

“That was all it took. I was hooked almost immediately,” he wrote. “I loved it, and that was where my cocaine abuse began.”

“I wasn’t asking myself what it meant or what it was doing for me,” he added. “I just knew I liked it, and before long, it had a hold on me.”
