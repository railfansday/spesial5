How to unlock hurricanes in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

How to break into the North Leonard County compound

How to break into the North Leonard County compound

To unlock hurricanes in Grand Theft Auto 6, you must find and befriend Freddie, a prepper (and rapper) living in a hidden bunker. Complete Freddie's side mission, and the weather is sure to turn foul, though your chances of witnessing a hurricane first-hand depend on the mission's final choice.

Here's how to complete Freddie's mission and unlock hurricanes in GTA 6.

To unlock hurricanes in GTA 6, you must discover Freddie's side mission. First, visit the mysterious, unnamed, high-security compound in North Leonard County, just northwest of Lake Leonida. Approach the front gate on the south side, and a voice coming from the speakers will ask you to leave. Don't try to climb the fence; you'll be electrocuted!

Instead, walk north to the containers. There seems to be a gap beneath the orange container; use the bulldozer to push it aside and enter the tunnel below.

As soon as you emerge from the tunnel, use focus mode to see where the mines are. They'll appear as small red dots on the ground. You can shoot them, but it's much quicker to check their positions with focus mode and run past (if you don't hold a weapon, your focus won't deplete). Try to walk across rooftops, crates, and rubble as much as you can.

A bit further ahead, to the left, you'll see drones being released from a bunker. If you shoot the first drone and hurry up, you'll likely have enough time to reach the bunker (but if not, halt for a moment and shoot the next drone as well). Once there, try to open the bunker's front door to meet Freddie.

Freddie says he'll improve his defenses, and he'll text once he's done so you can try to break in again. You must wait a few in-game days to get Freddie's text. Leave through the front gate — the mines and drones have been disabled.

Once you've received his message, return to the compound to retest Freddie's defenses. The tunnel you used before is now blocked, so you must find another way in. To break into Freddie's compound, find the tall tower north of the fence. Climb up (one level above the ground floor is high enough) and jump onto the shed on the other side of the fence.

Go to the bunker while evading the mines and drones like you did the first time — beware that there will be more of each, though. You'll get an electric shock when you try the bunker's front door, but don't worry; Freddie will let you into the bunker immediately after.

After hanging out with Freddie for a while, you'll receive a hurricane warning. Freddie will give you a choice: stay in the bunker or leave.

Hurricanes should be unlocked as a permanent random weather event no matter your choice, but if you don't want to risk missing out on a guaranteed GTA 6 hurricane experience, the best choice is to leave Freddie's bunker. In both scenarios, Freddie will give you money (up to $10,000 depending on the chapter), and you can take his standout vehicle, Freddie's Dubsta.
