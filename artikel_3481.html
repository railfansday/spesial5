Star Wars Director Offers Encouraging Update 9 Years After Divisive Sequel

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The director behind one of Star Wars' most controversial sequels gives an update about his future with the beloved science fiction franchise.

Released in 2017, The Last Jedi served as the second installment in the Star Wars sequel trilogy, following 2015's The Force Awakens. The film continued the stories of Rey, Kylo Ren, Finn, and Poe Dameron while bringing Luke Skywalker back into the picture. Despite receiving positive reviews from many critics, the sequel generated mixed feelings among fans, especially about the characters and storylines.

Now, Rian Johnson has addressed the possibility of returning to the Star Wars universe nearly a decade after directing Star Wars: The Last Jedi. Although he currently has no plans to direct another installment in a galaxy far, far away, he is still interested in revisiting Star Wars if the right opportunity comes along.

In an interview with IndieWire at the 23rd Marrakech International Film Festival, Johnson discussed his relationship with the franchise and whether another Star Wars project could be in his future. The director explained that he is currently busy developing his own original projects but is still open to the idea of doing another Star Wars project. He also said that his love for the franchise has only grown since The Last Jedi.

Johnson also praised Lucasfilm's creative team and leadership, including Dave Filoni, who worked with him on The Last Jedi. His comments imply that he does have a good professional relationship with the studio despite not having directed another installment and that he is not closing the door on the Star Wars franchise for good.

However, this should not be taken as a confirmation that there is another movie with Johnson in the works. The director did not announce a new Star Wars movie and didn't speak about any negotiations with Lucasfilm. His response is simply stating that he has not ruled out returning to the franchise.

Johnson's latest statement comes nine years after Lucasfilm announced that he would develop an entirely new Star Wars trilogy. In November 2017, the studio confirmed that Johnson would helm three films that were independent of the main Skywalker saga. He was expected to write and direct the first installment, with Ram Bergman attached as a producer.

The scrapped trilogy would have introduced new characters and explored new areas of the Star Wars universe. However, the project never made it to production and no information, including casting or release dates, was ever announced for the three previously planned movies.
