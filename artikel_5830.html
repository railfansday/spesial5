Kamala Harris' former political mentor and boyfriend delivers stinging warning about 2028 comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Willie Brown, a longtime California Democrat and a power broker in the state party, said former Vice President Kamala Harris lacks the communication skills needed to run for president again in 2028.

"She’s not a communicator," Brown said in an interview with Tara Palmeri, an independent journalist.

"No Baptist preacher flavor in her soul," he added.

Although Harris remains one of the country’s most recognizable Democratic figures, Brown’s skepticism comes from a longtime political ally and casts doubt on her potential candidacy.

Kamala Harris, former vice president and 2024 presidential candidate, speaks with Al Sharpton during the National Action Network (NAN) Convention in New York, April 10, 2026. (Angelina Katsanis/AP Photo)

"I’m sorry that she even thinks about running for president. I don’t want her to run," Brown said.

"She’s reached the highest electoral level for any woman in America," he added, referring to her post as President Joe Biden’s vice president.

Brown, who once served as San Francisco mayor and California state Assembly speaker, has a long history with Harris. Early in her career, he appointed her to the California Unemployment Insurance Appeals Board and then the Medical Assistance Commission.

The two were also romantically involved between 1994 and 1995.

Brown, in a San Francisco Chronicle op-ed, openly discussed their relationship and his role in her political career in an article titled, "Sure, I dated Kamala Harris. So what? "

Former Mayor, Willie Brown laughs with District Attorney Kamala Harris at the reception before the rededication ceremony for Twenty-First Century Academy in 2005. (Carlos Avila Gonzalez/The San Francisco Chronicle via Getty Images)

"Yes, we dated. It was more than 20 years ago. Yes, I may have influenced her career by appointing her to two state commissions when I was Assembly speaker," Brown wrote.

"And I certainly helped with her first race for district attorney in San Francisco. I have also helped the careers of House Speaker Nancy Pelosi, Gov. Gavin Newsom, Sen. Dianne Feinstein and a host of other politicians," he added.

Representatives for Harris did not immediately respond to a request for comment from Fox News Digital about Brown’s comments about her prospects for 2028.

Several other Democrats have already hinted at their own interest in a 2028 bid, crowding the potential field.

Rep. Ro Khanna, D-Calif., a top progressive in the House of Representatives, has hinted at a run. Former Transportation Secretary Pete Buttigieg has said he is "inclined" to run, and California Gov. Gavin Newsom announced plans to decide on a presidential bid at the beginning of the new year.

California Gov. Gavin Newsom and former Vice President Kamala Harris both said in separate media interviews that they're keeping their options open for potential 2028 presidential runs. (Patrick T. Fallon/Bloomberg via Getty Images)

Harris has not officially announced plans for 2028, though she chose not to run for California governor last year, keeping her options open for a possible presidential bid. In April, she said in a CNN interview that she had not made up her mind.

"I go back and forth," Harris said earlier this year.
