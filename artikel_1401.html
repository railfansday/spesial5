Why ‘full package’ Jude Bellingham is the best player in the world right now

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

It says something if your compatriot wins the Ballon d’Or and you still get named your country’s player of the year ahead of him.

Jude Bellingham didn’t have a strong enough 2025-26 season with Real Madrid to really be in contention for the prize that Harry Kane won, but he did have a better World Cup than Kane, leading to the retention of his England player of the year accolade – and it seems to have been the springboard for better things at club level.

Last season, he scored eight goals and made five assists for Real Madrid. This season, he’s just moved on to seven goals and five assists. By the way, it’s November.

In other words, this is a player back to his best. But it’s not just about his productivity. He’s doing things with a football that few others can.

Take, for example, his goal for Madrid against PSV in the Champions League on Tuesday night. It’s one of those goals that looks good in the first place and yet still gets better with every replay and each new angle.

He collects the ball after a lay-off by Kylian Mbappe, flicks it up with his second touch and then sends it flying into the top corner with a swerving volley* across the outside of his foot.

* Some will say it’s not a volley in the strictest sense, but who cares? Just sit back and enjoy.

Would it be an exaggeration to say that could be a Puskas Award contender? It’s got to be worthy of a shout.

It’s a goal that demonstrates the confidence flowing through the veins of an elite talent. He’s playing with a nonchalance befitting of one of the best players in the world.

And what really ensures Bellingham is in that category is the fact that he’s the full package. He plays in a position that demands you can do it all – and he has all the answers.

Bellingham plays with purpose and direction. As an attacking midfielder, he can create chances and convert them in equal measure. And sometimes it’s spectacular.

How many players in the world are on top of their game like Bellingham right now? Only a few are in the same conversation.

If he can keep this up for the course of the season and produce a few more moments of magic, we might be looking at an English Ballon d’Or contender for the second year in a row.
