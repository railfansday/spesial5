Perrigo: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Retailers are ordering cautiously, yet Perrigo's European health brands grew well ahead of their categories and took share.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 4, 2026, Perrigo (PRGO +0.89%) announced an agreement to sell its infant formula business. The divestment removes a cash-draining operation and frees up capital for higher-margin health products. Three takeaways from management stand out.

Management is streamlining its portfolio to focus on high-return self-care products, which are non-prescription health goods. The sale reduces capital expenditures, or capex, and improves the operating margin profile.

Online shopping is outpacing growth in physical retail, and Perrigo holds a higher market share in e-commerce than in offline stores. Management is doubling down on digital distribution to capture shifting consumer habits.

Retailers conservatively ordered over-the-counter (OTC) products ahead of the cold season after two quiet years. Even with that drag, Perrigo picked up market share in store brands and European health products.

Retailers are keeping inventory light, yet early illness trends look encouraging. Perrigo relies on store-brand retail partnerships, digital scale, and cost savings from its efficiency program while tracking retailer replenishment and European demand.

Shedding noncore units helps management focus on execution.
