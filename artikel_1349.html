Serie A | Parma 1-5 Roma: Malen hero with four goals as big names return

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Donyell Malen bagged four goals to give Roma their 5-1 victory away to Parma, with Rodrigo Mora also on target, but Wesley was decisive too with a hand in four goals.

The Giallorossi received a massive boost with the return of Paulo Dybala and Mile Svilar, the goalkeeper after nearly two months out, with Manu Koné and Gianluca Mancini also recovered after missing international duty, leaving only Devyne Rensch and Lorenzo Pellegrini on the treatment table.

The Ducali had shown improvement under coach Alberto Gilardino, but the early Abdoulaye Ndiaye red card saw them lose to Bologna. He was therefore suspended, with David Romero and Dominik Drobnic out, plus Diego Carlos and Adrian Bernabé not at 100 per cent.

Mariano Troilo stung Svilar’s gloves at the near post in the opening 60 seconds, but a few minutes later the goalkeeper rushed out to gather a loose ball and was visibly struggling with a muscular twinge, although he insisted on staying on the field.

Koné went down far too easily when feeling Troilo’s hand on his shoulder, while Leonardo Balerdi threw himself in the path of an El Bilal Touré finish from 12 yards.

Defensive bodies were also important to stop Enrico Delprato turning in from six yards on an Emanuele Valeri cross, but on the resulting corner, Svilar made a sensational reaction save on the Giovanni Fabbian header from point-blank range.

Mario Hermoso blasted just over from the edge of the box, and Giovanni Daffara was brave off his line to smother a Dybala pass at the feet of Donyell Malen, getting a shinpad in the head for his trouble.

Roma did break the deadlock with a moment of pure class from Malen. He hooked the Emanuele Lulli cross out of the air with his back to goal, knocking it down so it was in the right place as he turned to sweep in from 10 yards.

Mancini and Touré got into an argument with some possible hair-pulling and arm-flailing, but on the stroke of half-time a desperate clearance prevented Malen meeting the Rodrigo Mora low cross from six yards.

Daffara flew to parry the Dybala effort after a Malrn back-heel flick, but just as Roma were pouring forward, they were beaten on the counter-attack.

Leonardo Balerdi felt that he had the loose ball under control, but Elphege flicked it away to sprint from the centre-circle and beat the on-rushing Svilar.

Daffara made a magnificent reaction save to push the Mancini header out from under the bar on a Roma corner, but Malen could not turn down the gift from Troilo.

The defender made a complete mess of trying to clear the Wesley cross from the right, so it bobbled into the path of the Dutchman to fire in from six yards.

Moments later, there was a very similar goal again prompted by substitute Wesley’s pull-back from the right, it took a deflection and Rodrigo Mora pounced to tap in from close range.

The introduction of Brazil international Wesley really was transformative, as he provided another assist, rolling across from the right for Malen to complete his hat-trick.

Matias Soulé tested Daffara from distance, as did Hans Nicolussi Caviglia at the other end with Svilar.

It wasn’t over, as Malen scored his fourth of the game when Wesley won it back in midfield and placed a perfectly-weighted pass to send him sprinting, took a touch to get around Daffara, and placed the finish into the tiny sliver of goal remaining as defenders scrambled back to cover.
