Exclusive: Mikael Silvestre Names 'Very Special' England Star as Most Underrated Teammate

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Former Manchester United, Arsenal, and France defender Mikael Silvestre has named the most underrated teammate from his playing career, while speaking exclusively to GIVEMESPORT.

The talented defender had an illustrious career in English football, making 275 appearances in the Premier League over 11 seasons, lifting the title five times, and also winning the Champions League, FA Cup and League Cup once.

He also had an excellent international career, winning the Confederations Cup twice and reaching the World Cup final in 2006, racking up 40 caps for Les Bleus between 2001 and 2006.

During those prime years of his career, Silvestre played alongside some legendary figures including Cristiano Ronaldo, Wayne Rooney, Carlos Tevez, Ryan Giggs, Roy Keane, Zinedine Zidane, Thierry Henry, and Patrick Vieira.

At different points in his time at Old Trafford, Silvestre was the first-choice left-back and also the first-choice centre-back, showing his incredible versatility and quality, with Sir Alex Ferguson a huge fan of him after signing him from Inter Milan in 1999.

His experience and quality convinced Arsene Wenger to take him to the Emirates Stadium when his time at Man Utd came to an end, which allowed him to continue his career in England and play alongside some more top stars such as Cesc Fabregas, Samir Nasri, and Robin van Persie.

But while speaking to GIVEMESPORT, via Action Network, a prediction market publisher covering Polymarket offers, Silvestre was asked to name the most underrated teammate of his playing career, and he didn't hesitate when providing his answer.

“Paul Scholes was underrated. Obviously, he got his flowers at Man Utd but for England and outside of Man Utd he wasn't rated as high as he should be in my opinion.

“He was a top, top player, and being his teammate in training, he was a very special talent.”

Scholes was part of the Man Utd squad for the entirety of Silvestre's time at Old Trafford, and is widely regarded as one of the club's best ever players. The midfield playmaker made 718 appearances for Man Utd during his career, winning 11 Premier League titles, three FA Cups, two League Cups, and two Champions League titles.

But outside of Manchester, his reputation is not as esteemed as you would think, with his quiet nature in the public eye during his playing days likely going against him.

Considering some of the talent that Silvestre played alongside, it's no surprise that the former England midfielder, one of the greatest midfielders of the Premier League era, is likely seen as underrated.

But Scholes is often heralded by his peers, and was recently described as the best player in Premier League history by Thierry Henry, who also felt the iconic midfielder was underrated.

"I don't think people have given him the credit he deserves. (He's) one of the best, if not the best, I've played against, he could do anything. Unfortunately for him, when he was playing for the national team, for England, sometimes people didn't see it like that."

The England star made an interesting choice.
