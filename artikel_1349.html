Rooney, Henry, or Drogba? Nemanja Vidic Named His Toughest Opponent

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Former Manchester United defender Nemanja Vidic is considered one of the finest centre-backs in Premier League history, having established himself as a commanding presence through his leadership, physicality and uncompromising defensive style during eight seasons at Old Trafford.

A two-time Premier League Player of the Season, Vidic played a pivotal role in United's success under Sir Alex Ferguson throughout the late 2000s and early 2010s. He helped the Red Devils secure five league championships, the 2008 Champions League trophy and numerous other domestic and European accolades.

Having previously identified Peter Crouch and Luis Suarez among the four toughest opponents of his Premier League career, Vidic was recently presented with another challenge: choosing the most difficult competitor between Thierry Henry, Didier Drogba and Wayne Rooney, three of the greatest strikers in football history.

During a recent conversation with former Manchester United colleague Rio Ferdinand, Vidic was asked to select the toughest opponent from Henry, Drogba and Rooney. He opted for Rooney, explaining that the former England captain's combination of power and pace made him particularly challenging during training sessions:

“Wayne Rooney. Obviously, Didier Drogba, so many big challenges against him, obviously the time we competed, but training every day with Wayne, [he was] powerful, fast.”

Rooney spent the entirety of Vidic's eight seasons at Old Trafford, having arrived from Everton in 2004 for £30.7m, which represented a world-record transfer fee for a teenager at the time.

Both players enjoyed tremendous success in Manchester, collecting numerous team and individual awards. Rooney was named Premier League Player of the Season for 2009/10, while Vidic received the accolade in 2008/09 and 2010/11.

Interestingly, the Manchester United pair only came up against one another away from club football on a single occasion. That meeting came during England's 2-1 friendly victory against Serbia and Montenegro in 2003, when Vidic completed the full 90 minutes and Rooney entered as a second-half substitute for Michael Owen.

GIVEMESPORT Key Statistic: Vidic represented Manchester United 300 times, registering 21 goals and collecting 15 major trophies during his spell at the club.

Rooney is widely recognised as one of the most accomplished forwards in Premier League history, having played a defining role in Manchester United's success under Ferguson across his 13 years with the club.

He made an unforgettable start to life at Old Trafford after moving from Everton as a teenager in 2004, finding the net three times on his Champions League debut against Fenerbahce. That performance offered an early glimpse of the remarkable career that followed.

During his time at United, the former England captain collected five Premier League titles, the Champions League in 2008 and the FA Cup in 2016, alongside several other domestic and international honours, including the FIFA Club World Cup in 2008.

Rooney named a United XI brimming with world-class talent and an interesting choice up front.

He also cemented his place in the club's history by becoming Manchester United's all-time leading goalscorer, finishing with 253 goals across 559 appearances and moving beyond Sir Bobby Charlton's previous record of 245 goals in 746 matches.

At international level, Rooney, one of the best English footballers of all time, earned 120 appearances for England and scored 53 goals. He held the national team's goalscoring record until Harry Kane moved ahead of him in 2023.

Rooney brought his professional playing career to an end in 2017 following spells with Everton, DC United and Derby County before transitioning into management. His latest managerial appointment came at Plymouth Argyle in 2024.
