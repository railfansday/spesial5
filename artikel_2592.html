2027 NFL Draft Order: Winless Chargers hold No. 1 spot as Titans, Dolphins loom in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It's never too early to start looking ahead to the 2027 NFL Draft, right?

Week 10 of the current NFL season isn't even close over yet, but the order for the draft in a few months is already starting to take shape. And, at least for now, the Los Angeles Chargers are still very much in control of the No. 1 overall pick.

The Chargers will enter their Monday Night Football matchup with the Baltimore Ravens as the league's only winless team. They sit at just 0-8 on the year.

But if the Chargers win on Monday night in Baltimore, they'll join both the Tennessee Titans and Miami Dolphins with 1-8 records. The Titans fell to the Jacksonville Jaguars in a 40-23 blowout on Sunday afternoon, which marked their third straight loss and moved them to 0-5 at Nissan Stadium this season. The Dolphins just barely lost to the Indianapolis Colts 24-22 on Sunday. The win snapped the Colts' five-game losing skid, yet it only moved them to 3-7 on the year. The Dolphins have now lost three straight heading into a road game against the Buffalo Bills next week.

While they have the same record, the Titans currently have the edge over the Dolphins for the No. 2 overall pick. The New York Jets and Philadelphia Eagles then round out the top five.

Now even though things are undoubtedly bad, the Chargers will realistically win at least one game the rest of the way. The NFL hasn't seen a team go a full season without winning a game since the 2017 Cleveland Browns. That means that either the Titans or Dolphins can sneak in and steal the No. 1 overall draft pick from the Chargers down the stretch. The Jets and Eagles, both with just two wins this season, aren't out of the question yet, either.

For now, though, the Chargers are in control of that top spot.
