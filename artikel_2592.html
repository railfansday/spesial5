As Chargers struggle, Jim Harbaugh's answer on defensive playcalling raises more questions

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Chargers head coach Jim Harbaugh’s non-answer to a question about which defensive player is wearing the helmet with a radio receiver to tell his teammates the play call is raising more questions about whether Harbaugh has become checked out on his team.

Harbaugh was asked by a reporter about adjusting to different players wearing the helmet, which is designated by a green dot, and calling the defense. Harbaugh didn’t seem to know how to answer the question.

“Um, yeah, I think, uh, I’m sure there — I don’t know exactly how much of an adjustment it is,” Harbaugh said.

Asked who was wearing the green dot in Sunday’s loss to the Jets, Harbaugh didn’t know and asked if someone else could tell him before he speculated that it might have been linebackers Marlowe Wax, Del’Shawn Phillips or Troy Dye.

“Um, a few, I think, uh — who had the green dot? — I’m not for sure. Marlowe Wax, Del’Shawn. Uh, Troy might have had a green dot as well,” Harbaugh said.

Asked whether more than one player was wearing the green dot helmet at different points in the game, Harbaugh said that was his understanding.

“Yeah, you’re allowed a couple,” Harbaugh said.

Harbaugh played quarterback in the NFL, was an offensive coach when he was an assistant, and has generally been more involved with the offense. So perhaps he doesn’t need to know every detail of what’s going on with the defense. But the Jim Harbaugh who coached the 49ers from 2011 to 2014 was a focused, detail-oriented coach who likely would have known the answer to that question.

Harbaugh has won everywhere he’s been a head coach. At San Diego, Stanford and Michigan he had great success in college, he led the 49ers to the Super Bowl, and until this year he had a winning record with the Chargers. But now he’s 1-9, and it’s hard not to notice that he seems less involved in running his team than he used to.
