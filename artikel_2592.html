NBA Injury Update: Latest news on Tyrese Haliburton, Jalen Duren, Donte DiVincenzo

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

In an NBA season that has seemed defined by high-profile injuries, there was a lot of good news on Nov. 24 about players nearing a return. Here is the latest.

There may be no more optimistic news about Tyrese Haliburton's return to the Pacers than this: When normally stoic, cautious coach Rick Carlisle was asked on Indianapolis' 107.5 The Fan if Haliburton might return this week, this was his response:

Earlier this week, the Pacers assigned Haliburton to their G League squad for a day to get in a full practice, a clear sign he is nearing a return. Haliburton had posted on Instagram that his goal was to return before the end of November, and the Pacers play two more games this month, a back-to-back Friday hosting the Philadelphia 76ers, then traveling on Saturday to face the Chicago Bulls. Indiana has gone 4-10 with Haliburton out due to a hamstring strain. The Pacers both don't want to push him back and need him — their offense is built around his playmaking. In the four games he played, Haliburton averaged 21.8 points and 9.5 assists per game, but that undersells how important he is to this team.

As noted in the Haliburton note, a clear sign a player is close to returning is him being assigned to the club's G-League team to get in a full practice (NBA teams rarely have those kinds of practices during the season). With that, the Detroit Pistons sent out this note Tuesday on injured center Jalen Duren:

"Jalen Duren (right hamstring strain) will be assigned to the Motor City Cruise, the NBA's G League affiliate of the Detroit Pistons, tomorrow for a practice as part of his return to play."

That's a good sign. The Pistons also noted that Duncan Robinson is now day-to-day recovering from a quad strain.

Donte DiVincenzo has been out since tearing his right Achilles tendon during the first round of the playoffs last April.

Now, he "has been cleared for team basketball activities and is progressing toward his return to play," the team announced Tuesday. There is no timeline attached, but the well-connected Jon Krawczynski of The Athletic reports he is targeting a January return, nine months after the injury.

• Denver's Julian Strawther, who sprained his knee in the preseason and has been out ever since, is off the injury report and could play as early as Tuesday against Dallas.

• Charlotte sharpshooter Kon Knueppel is "weeks away" from playing due to the hamstring strain that has kept him out all season, reports Rod Boone of The Boone Docs.

• If you missed the bad news from Monday, the Grizzlies star rookie Cameron Boozer is out at least a month with a hamstring strain. Also, the best player from the Nets' 2005 draft class, Nolan Traore, will be out 8-10 weeks with a hamstring strain.
