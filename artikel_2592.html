Tanggal: 2026-11-25

I hurried through New York's Moynihan Train Hall early on Nov. 15, anxious not to miss my Amtrak to Philadelphia. I had a noon deadline of sorts: getting to Xfinity Mobile Arena before the 76ers tipped off against the Indiana Pacers at 1 p.m. As a hockey reporter for The Athletic, spending my Sunday at an NBA game might seem like an unusual assignment. On most days, it would be. But this Sunday was no ordinary day at Xfinity Mobile Arena. Just seven hours separated the 76ers' afternoon matchup against the Indiana Pacers and the Flyers' evening showdown with Connor McDavid's Edmonton Oilers. Two professional sports teams, two entirely different playing surfaces and thousands of fans cycling through the same building in a matter of hours. In between, arena workers had just over two hours to transform an NBA basketball court into an NHL-ready ice rink. And I was there to see how they pulled it off.
