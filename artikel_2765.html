Drone pilot apologizes to Mauricio Pochettino after filming Manchester United training

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The drone operator who covertly captured Mauricio Pochettino's inaugural Manchester United training session has released an apology. Approximately 12 minutes of video was shot on Thursday and posted online before its subsequent deletion.

New boss Pochettino arrived on Wednesday following his departure from the U.S. Men's National Team after a 2-0 win over Haiti. The Argentine has taken over from Michael Carrick at United.

He conducted his maiden training session on Thursday, though Pochettino and his coaching team weren't the sole observers present. A drone pilot operating a YouTube channel called CC OVERVIEW filmed the session from above.

The operator, who maintained he violated no regulations by flying his drone in that location, captured the United squad during their workout. The video was then posted to YouTube, featuring live commentary, and drew considerable criticism ahead of United's fixture against Liverpool on Sunday.

After what supporters and analysts had described as a security violation, the account took down the footage on Friday morning. An extensive apology has since been published, with Pochettino addressed personally and any involvement of other soccer clubs - including United's upcoming adversaries - firmly rejected, as per the Manchester Evening News.

A statement reads: "I would like to speak about yesterday's incident. A total error of judgement on my part led to me filming some of the Manchester United training session at Carrington, whilst the flight was legal in terms of drone laws I fully accept it was unethical on my part.

"My aim was to picture Mauricio Pochettino in his United training kit for the supporters to see for the first time. I had wrongly presumed that with it being close to the start of the session that no meaningful training sessions would have started so would have been some harmless warming up shown.

"I also wrongly assumed that the training session would have been open to various media outlets. I would like to apologize to Manchester United, Mauricio Pochettino and the players for my actions and sincerely hope those actions do not affect the outcome of any match.

"I would also like to state that Liverpool Football Club or any other football club were not involved in my decision to film at Carrington yesterday. The video has been removed from my channel.

"I have spent the past four years building up my YouTube channel and were well regarded in the fields I film and would now like to get back to building up my damaged reputation and making well-received, responsible videos. Thank you."

The user had previously defended his flight by claiming that Carrington was in unrestricted airspace and that, since he possesses all the necessary licenses, the drone is correctly labeled and he is fully insured, no laws were violated. A zoom lens is believed to have been utilized, and the drone neither flew over anyone nor left his line of sight.
