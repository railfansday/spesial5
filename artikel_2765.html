Cardinals RG Isaiah Adams returns to practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Cardinals designated right guard Isaiah Adams to return from injured reserve, the team announced Wednesday.

Adams practiced with the team, opening his 21-day window. The Cardinals can activate him back to the active roster anytime in the next three weeks.

The Cardinals placed Adams on injured reserve on Oct. 10 with an ankle injury.

Jon Gaines started at right guard in Weeks 5 and 6, and Hayden Conner started the past two games there.

The Cardinals also announced they signed running back Jermar Jefferson and wide receiver James Proche II to the practice squad Wednesday.
