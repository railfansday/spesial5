NFL draft order: Chargers-Ravens could reshape first round

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Sunday night's game between the Cincinnati Bengals and the Pittsburgh Steelers was filled with playoff implications. Both teams entered the game with matching 6-2 records, and a spot atop the division was at stake.

Monday night's contest between the 0-8 Los Angeles Chargers, and the 4-5 Baltimore Ravens, does not have the same playoff weight. But it could go a long way towards deciding what team has the first pick in the 2027 NFL Draft.

At the moment, the Chargers are atop the first round thanks to their winless record. But should the Chargers beat the Ravens later tonight, that would see a change at the top of the first round. Los Angeles would move to 1-8 on the season, matching the Tennessee Titans, and the Miami Dolphins, and thanks to tiebreaker rules the Titans would move to the No. 1 spot, thanks to their weaker strength of schedule.
