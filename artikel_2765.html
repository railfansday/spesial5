3 potential X-factors for the Jets defense vs. Dolphins in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The New York Jets will have their rematch versus the Miami Dolphins on Sunday. They'll require a significantly better defensive performance than the one they received during a 28-25 loss to the Dolphins in Week 7. The Jets are coming off a victory over the Los Angeles Chargers, so Aaron Glenn's squad enters Sunday with some momentum.

Jets Wire has identified three potential X-factors for the Jets' defense versus the Dolphins in Week 12.

Rookie EDGE David Bailey continues to produce at a Defensive Rookie of the Year-worthy level. The No. 2 overall selection had two sacks and a forced fumble versus the Chargers in Week 11, bringing his season-long total to 9.5 sacks. Bailey could dominate the Dolphins' offensive line.

Veteran defensive tackle Harrison Phillips returned to Thursday's practice in limited fashion after missing Wednesday's session. That places him currently on track to play. The Dolphins will attempt to establish the run with bruising running back Ollie Gordon. Phillips' run-stopping efforts will be notable, and he also registered his first 2026 sack in Week 11.

This Jets defense has only recorded two interceptions in 11 games this season (after making history with zero in 2025). Veteran safety Minkah Fitzpatrick has one of those interceptions. Dolphins backup quarterback Kyle McCord is almost guaranteed to make a mistake.
