Report: DT Jordan Elliott to work out for Bears

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Defensive tackle Jordan Elliott is getting another look from an NFL team.

Adam Schefter of ESPN reports that Elliott will work out for the Bears on Tuesday. It's the first time that Elliott has met with a team since being released by the Titans last month.

Elliott was released after he went to his car to retrieve a gun in the wake of an argument with Titans offensive tackle JC Latham. Defensive lineman John Franklin-Myers helped de-escalate the situation and Titans head coach Robert Saleh called Elliott a good person who had "a terrible lapse in judgment."

Saleh said he hoped Elliott would get another chance and it could come in Chicago. He had 11 tackles in five games with the Titans and started 64 games over six seasons with the 49ers and Browns to open his career.
