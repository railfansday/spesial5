Broncos officially put JK Dobbins on suspended list

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Broncos head coach Sean Payton announced on Tuesday that running back JK Dobbins was being suspended for conduct detrimental to the team and the move became official on Wednesday.

The league's daily transaction report shows that Dobbins has officially been placed on the team's suspended list. He will forfeit a game check worth just over $222,222 as a result of the suspension keeping him out of Friday's game against the Steelers.

The Broncos will also be without safety Taylor Rapp (quad) and defensive end Sai'Vion Jones (hamstring) after ruling them out on Wednesday's injury report.

Running back Tyler Badie (thumb), wide receiver Pat Bryant (ankle), safety Brandon Jones (hamstring), tight end Caleb Lohner (foot), and safety JL Skinner (shoulder) were all full participants in practice.
