Rookie Cade Klubnik replaces injured Jets QB Geno Smith in loss to Bills as New York goes 9 straight quarters without a TD

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

New York Jets quarterback Geno Smith returned from a brief exit against the Buffalo Bills on Sunday due to a concussion evaluation, only to leave the game again for good with an ankle injury.

Rookie Cade Klubnik replaced him for his first NFL action as the Bills rolled to a 31-12 win. In the end, neither Smith nor Klubnik could solve New York's offensive woes as the Jets went a second straight game without a touchdown.

The Jets have gone nine straight quarters without a TD following a 35-12 loss to the Kansas City Chiefs last week. New York's last touchdown arrived in the third quarter of a 38-33 loss to the Las Vegas Raiders in Week 8.

The loss drops the Jets to 2-8, while the Bills improve to an NFL-best 8-1.

The hit that initially sidelined Smith took place on New York's final play of the first half. Smith got hit high and low after a pass and fell hard to the ground on his back. He remained down on the field for several moments.

Smith got up and walked to the sideline medical tent. The Jets kicked a field goal after the third-down incompletion and went into halftime with a 10-6 deficit.

Klubnik took the field with the offense to start the second half as the Jets announced that Smith was being evaluated for a concussion.

The Jets then announced that Smith had cleared concussion protocol, and he returned to the game on New York's next possession following a Bills touchdown.

But Smith left the game again on the following possession in favor of Klubnik with the Bills holding a 24-12 lead. This time, Smith was sidelined with an ankle injury. The Jets initially listed him as questionable to return before ruling him out in the fourth quarter.

The Jets selected Klubnik in the fourth round of April's NFL Draft out of Clemson. Sunday's appearance against the Bills marked his first NFL action in a regular-season game.

Klubnik led the Jets to a 61-yard field-goal drive on his first NFL possession, completing 2-of-4 passes for 39 yards. He then badly overthrew his target on a third-quarter pass that was intercepted by Davison Igbinosun.

Igbinosun fumbled the return, and the Jets recovered. But the Jets punted three plays later as their offense continued to flutter.

Klubnik finished his day completing 11-of-20 passes for 137 yards with no touchdowns and an interception. Smith completed 13-of-21 passes for 148 yards with no touchdowns or interceptions.

The Jets settled for four field goals for a second consecutive game as their offense tallied 376 yards, keeping pace with Buffalo's 389. But the Bills converted their drives into touchdowns.

The nature of Smith's injury wasn't immediately clear, and it wasn't clear how long he will be sidelined.

Whomever is at quarterback, New York's next opportunity to score a touchdown will arrive next Sunday against the Los Angeles Chargers.
