Aaron Jones TD ties Vikings-49ers at 7-7 late in first quarter

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Vikings have tied things up in Mexico City.

Running back Aaron Jones powered his way into the end zone for a an 11-yard touchdown, knotting things up at 7-7 with 1:13 left in the first quarter.

Starting with the ball at their own 10-yard line, the Vikings were able to move into 49ers territory with a 29-yard pass from Kirk Cousins to Justin Jefferson.

Cousins then converted third-and-3 with a 16-yard pass to T.J. Hockenson to put Minnesota on the 24-yard line.

The Vikings got some help from a questionable roughing the passer call against Nick Bosa, moving the ball half the distance to the goal.

A play later, Jones bulldozed his way into the end zone for his fourth rushing touchdown of the season.
