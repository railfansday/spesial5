Openda delivers cryptic message amid reports he will return to Juventus

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Lois Openda is injured and working towards a return, but uncertainty has emerged over his future at Lyon. Recent reports suggest the French club could end his loan spell in January and send him back to Juventus, with doubts reportedly growing over whether they want to keep him.

The Belgian joined Lyon on loan at the start of the season, when Paulo Fonseca was in charge. The manager trusted Openda and gave him regular opportunities, allowing the attacker to feature in many matches. Juventus were also confident that the move would eventually become permanent.

However, the situation has changed since Fonseca was replaced by Carlos Vicens. The Spaniard is assessing the squad available to him and deciding which players fit his plans. Some reports suggest that he is not particularly keen on working with Openda at present.

That has fuelled speculation that Lyon could cut the loan short in January. This would mark a change from the expectations earlier in the campaign, when Openda was receiving regular game time and Juventus believed a permanent transfer was likely. However, there has been no official confirmation that Lyon intend to send him back, so the claims should be treated with caution.

There may also be little substance behind the reports. Tuttojuve has been monitoring the attacker and says his latest social media posts appear to respond directly to the rumours about his future. He reportedly shared an image of himself wearing Lyon's kit, seemingly signalling that he remains a player of the Ligue 1 club.

The post could be seen as a show of defiance amid the uncertainty, with Openda appearing to indicate that his situation has not changed. For now, he remains on loan at Lyon, and there is no confirmed decision to end that arrangement before the season concludes. Until either club provides an update, the reports of a possible January return to Juventus remain speculation.
