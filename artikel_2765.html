Daiyan Henley being out with a knee injury changes nothing about the failed trade to Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The failed trade of Chargers linebacker Daiyan Henley returned to the front burner on Friday, with the news that Henley will miss Sunday's game against the Jets due to a knee injury.

It was a knee condition that prompted the Broncos to pull the plug on the deal — after trying to renegotiate the terms after the trade deadline.

From Mike Klis of 9News, who covers the Broncos: "Chargers ILB Daiyan Henley missed entire week of practices with knee injury and has been ruled out for game vs Jets. This validates concerns raised by Broncos medical team and GM George Paton. And proves Chargers were not on the up and up."

Does it really prove that the Chargers weren't "on the up and up"? The issue is that the Denver physical flagged a knee problem. In lieu of finding that the physical had been failed and that the trade won't happen, the Broncos were willing to give Henley a passing grade, if the compensation was changed.

Henley has played every game this season. He played on Monday night against the Ravens. As far as anyone knows, he injured the knee during that game.

As far as anyone knows, the currently injured knee isn't the same knee that created the concern during the Denver physical.

It's all just noise at this point. The core problem is the ability of a team — after the trade deadline has passed — to renegotiate the terms of a trade. And the related issue is the ability of a team to give a conditional grade on the pass-fail question of a physical that is tied to the specific compensation.

A player either passes the physical, or he fails it. The process shouldn't include an epilogue premised on the outcome of the physical being "maybe." Especially after the time for negotiating trades has ended.
