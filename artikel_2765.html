John Mateer Leads Sooners to Impressive SEC Victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Oklahoma Sooners delivered one of their strongest performances of the season on Saturday, defeating Texas A&M 28-7 at Gaylord Family Oklahoma Memorial Stadium. Behind a four-touchdown performance from quarterback John Mateer and a dominant defensive effort, Oklahoma secured its third consecutive victory.

The Sooners entered the matchup looking to finish their home schedule on a high note. They faced a Texas A&M team starting freshman quarterback Brady Hart, who was making his first collegiate start. After a competitive first half, Oklahoma took control late and pulled away for an important SEC victory.

Oklahoma improved to 7-4 overall and 5-3 in conference play, while Texas A&M fell to 4-7 and 1-7 in the SEC.

Mateer was the biggest difference maker for Oklahoma, overcoming two early turnovers to lead the Sooners to a convincing victory.

He completed 23 of 34 passes for 269 yards and three touchdowns while adding 66 rushing yards and another score on the ground.

Oklahoma initially struggled to find its rhythm, and Texas A&M took a 7-0 lead in the second quarter after Hart connected with Isaiah Horton for a 36 yard touchdown.

The Sooners answered before halftime with a 92 yard scoring drive. Mateer completed seven of eight passes on the possession, finishing it with a four yard touchdown throw to Rocky Beers to tie the game at 7-7.

The game remained tied through the third quarter before Oklahoma's offense finally broke through in the fourth.

Mateer guided a 96-yard drive that lasted more than eight minutes, finding Tory Blaylock for a five yard touchdown to give the Sooners a 14-7 advantage.

Later, following an interception, Mateer connected with Isaiah Sategna for a 25 yard touchdown. He added a seven-yard rushing touchdown in the closing seconds to complete the scoring.

While Mateer received plenty of attention, Oklahoma's defense played an equally important role in the victory.

The Sooners limited Texas A&M to just 185 total yards, including 54 rushing yards and 131 passing yards. They also forced eight punts and recorded three sacks.

After allowing the Aggies to score on their 96-yard drive in the second quarter, Oklahoma completely shut down the opposing offense.

Texas A&M managed only 89 yards after that scoring possession, struggling to sustain drives or create consistent opportunities.

Hart finished with eight completions on 20 attempts for 131 yards, one touchdown and one interception. Oklahoma's defensive pressure made life difficult for the young quarterback throughout the afternoon.

Saturday's victory gives Oklahoma another important boost as the regular season approaches its conclusion.

The Sooners have now won three straight games and continued their strong home performances against Texas A&M. Mateer's ability to produce through the air and on the ground gives the offense an important weapon heading into the final stretch.

Oklahoma finished with 421 total yards compared to Texas A&M's 185, showing just how thoroughly the Sooners controlled the matchup.

After a close first three quarters, Oklahoma delivered a dominant fourth quarter performance to secure the 28-7 win.
