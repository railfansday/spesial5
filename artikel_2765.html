Brian Robinson Jr. placed on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Atlanta Falcons RB Brian Robinson Jr. (hamstring) was placed on the Reserve/Injured list on Saturday, Nov. 7, and will miss a minimum of four games.

Why It Matters: Robinson has shown flashes of ability while working behind Bijan Robinson this season, but has missed the last two games with this hamstring injury and now will miss four more games. Khalil Herbert has worked in as Atlanta's backup and will continue to do so while Robinson is on the mend.
