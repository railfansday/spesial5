Drip Capital Ranks in Top 100 on the 2026 Deloitte Technology Fast 500™

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Credits Rapid Growth to Demand for Collateral-Free Trade Finance and Its AI-Powered Credit Technology

PALO ALTO, Calif., Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Drip Capital today announced it ranked No. 60 on the Deloitte Technology Fast 500â¢, a ranking of the 500 fastest-growing tech companies in North America, now in its 32nd year. The ranking reflects its revenue growth from 2022 to 2025.

Drip Capital has financed and facilitated more than $10 billion in trade since inception and now processes about $2 billion a year for small and mid-sized businesses. This is its first year on the list, and it also ranked No. 9 in the Bay Area. Alongside trade finance, Drip Capital runs a B2B commerce platform that connects businesses with verified global buyers and suppliers.

Founder and CEO Pushkar Mukewar credits the growth to Drip Capital's AI-powered transaction processing engine, which assesses credit, automates each step of a deal, and approves transactions in real time. "Small businesses that trade across borders have long struggled to get working capital and connect with trusted partners," he said. "We built Drip Capital to fix that, and this ranking shows how strong the demand is."

"The 2026 rankings demonstrate that established momentum and breakthrough growth can drive sustained market leadership," said Justin Yahr, Audit & Assurance partner and National Emerging Growth Company leader, Deloitte & Touche LLP. "Nearly 70% of this year's award winners returned as repeat honorees, reflecting the power of disciplined and sustained execution. More than 30% made the list for the first time, illustrating how quickly new players are shaping their respective sectors. Furthermore, private companies represented almost 80% of the list, a clear indication that the agility and strategic focus of private enterprises remain essential to scaling in competitive markets."

Drip Capital is a tech-enabled trade finance and B2B commerce platform that gives small and mid-sized businesses collateral-free working capital and connects them with verified global buyers and suppliers. It has financed and facilitated more than $10 billion in trade for over 11,000 businesses in over 100 countries. Its capital partners include Barclays, TD Bank, and the International Finance Corporation (IFC). It works with logistics firms, insurance brokers, and invoicing platforms. Drip Capital is backed by Accel, Peak XV Partners, Wing VC, Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, GMO Payment Gateway, and Y Combinator.
