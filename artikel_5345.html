Nvidia beat earnings estimates in first report since Jensen Huang's exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nvidia forecast fourth-quarter revenue of $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, excluding Data Center compute sales in China

Nvidia $NVDA reported third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion on Tuesday, up 103% from a year earlier, in its first earnings report since Jensen Huang resigned as CEO. Earnings per diluted share were $2.70 on a GAAP basis and $2.68 on a non-GAAP basis.

Analysts expected revenue of $109.10 billion and adjusted earnings of $2.48 a share, according to CNBC.

Nvidia stock climbed by up to 4% in after-hours trading following the report. The company will hold a call with analysts and investors at 5 p.m. ET.

Net income was $65.3 billion, up 105% from a year earlier. Data Center revenue reached $107.6 billion, up 110% from a year earlier and up 21% from the prior quarter. GAAP gross margin was 74.8% and non-GAAP gross margin was 74.9%.

For the fourth quarter, Nvidia expects revenue of $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, and the outlook excludes any Data Center compute revenue from China. The company expects GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins between 71.0% and 72.0%. The average analyst estimate for fourth-quarter revenue was $125.1 billion, according to CNBC.

Data Center shipments of Hopper products to China totaled $2.4 billion in the quarter, the company said.

"Our momentum is real," interim CEO and CFO Colette Kress said in a statement. Nvidia said its Vera Rubin platform has begun shipping.

Nvidia said its supply commitments rose to $390 billion from $279 billion in the prior quarter, mainly because of multi-year memory procurement. The company also said its guarantees tied to the SB Energy data center campus in Ohio, which will host Nvidia infrastructure leased to OpenAI, are capped at $105 billion.

The company returned $28.1 billion to shareholders in the quarter through buybacks and dividends. Its board raised the repurchase authorization by $150 billion in September, leaving $227.3 billion available. Nvidia will pay a quarterly dividend of $0.25 per share on Dec. 23 to shareholders of record on Dec. 2.

Huang resigned last week after admitting to an affair with a former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed, a major Nvidia customer. U.S. intelligence officials suspect she is an agent of Beijing's spy agencies. Kress took over as interim CEO while keeping her CFO role, and the board began a search for a permanent chief executive.

Nvidia's board also took a governance step on Tuesday when it established a committee to oversee national security policies and export control compliance. Huang said in a statement Tuesday that he'd be "honored" to take back the chief executive role should directors conclude that doing so is "in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America," according to The Wall Street Journal.
