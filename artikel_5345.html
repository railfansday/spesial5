/C O R R E C T I O N - Euler Foundation/

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In the news release, Euler Foundation appoints Euler Technologies to lead next phase of growth and adoption, issued 24-Nov-2026 by Euler Foundation over PR Newswire, we are advised by the company that changes have been made. The complete, corrected release follows, with additional details at the end:

GRAND CAYMAN, Cayman Islands, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The Euler Foundation today announced that Euler Technologies, a newly formed independent service provider, joined by Christopher Cameron as Head of Strategy, succeeds Euler Labs as the primary service provider responsible for maintaining and growing the Euler Protocol. The mutually agreed transition will take effect on 24 November 2026, with Euler Technologies and Euler Labs working together on the handover.

The appointment marks the completion of Euler Labs' founding mission. Euler Labs has developed the most flexible and robust lending and borrowing product on the market: a modular protocol on which anyone can deploy a customised credit market for any asset without compromising risk isolation. With that infrastructure built and battle-tested, the Protocol's next step is commercial.

With growth and protocol evolution as top priorities, Euler Technologies assumes responsibility for protocol development and maintenance, as well as the operation of the Euler app, documentation, and communications. Its mandate focuses on partnerships and integrations that bring more users to Euler, support for curators developing credit markets, and work with institutions and tokenised-asset issuers seeking access to onchain lending. The Euler Foundation will compensate Euler Technologies in EUL, Euler's governance token, aligning the company's economic incentives with the protocol's long-term success.

Euler Foundation said: "Euler Labs has made an extraordinary contribution to Euler. The team has built the most adaptable and resilient credit infrastructure in DeFi, and we thank them for the technology and ecosystem they have created. Euler Technologies brings the engineering capability to carry that work forward, and the commercial and governance experience to put it in front of more users and integrators. This transition positions Euler for its next phase of growth."

Euler Labs and Euler Technologies will coordinate the transition over November 2026. We do not anticipate any near-term material changes to the protocol, its markets or its interfaces. The Euler Foundation will continue to steward the Euler ecosystem and oversee its service provider relationships, working with Euler Technologies to support the products' development, maintenance and long-term growth.

Under the direction of the Euler Foundation, Euler Labs will transfer the administrative permissions associated with its service-provider role to Euler Technologies before the end of November 2026. Both teams will coordinate and verify each step, and the community will be updated once the transfer is complete.

Euler Technologies will hold the same limited administrative permissions that Euler Labs held, under the same safeguards. Changes to the protocol remain subject to the same controls and governance oversight as today, and Euler Foundation will continue to oversee how those permissions are held and used. Euler Technologies cannot exercise these permissions alone. Protocol upgrades require approval from a distributed multisig whose signers include the Euler Foundation.

Euler Labs will rotate the protocol's multisig signers to a distributed set that includes the Euler Foundation, Euler Technologies, leading crypto security firms, and investors in Euler Technologies.

"I'm incredibly proud of everything Euler Labs has achieved, from the v2 rebuild to the upgrades we've just shipped. We set out to build the most versatile credit infrastructure in DeFi, and that work is done," said Jonathan Han, CEO of Euler Labs. "What Euler needs now is a team focused on commercializing it. Euler Technologies has the business development capabilities and the market knowledge to expand its reach. We believe this is the best next step for Euler."

Cameron, known across the industry as PaperImperium, is one of the most experienced governance practitioners in decentralised finance, specialising in onchain credit and stablecoin markets. He was among the first elected delegates of MakerDAO (now Sky) and at one point its largest, representing more than 3% of the protocol's voting power. He organised one of the first public meetings between a DAO and a sitting member of the U.S. Congress. From 2021 to 2026, he served as governance liaison at GFX Labs, representing the firm in the governance of Arbitrum, Optimism, Uniswap, Morpho and other protocols.

"I have spent years inside the governance of DeFi's largest credit protocols, and I have watched Euler Labs build the most flexible and robust lending infrastructure on the market," said Cameron, Head of Strategy, Euler. "The infrastructure is proven. Euler's major need is distribution: the partnerships, integrations and institutional relationships that bring more markets and more borrowers onto it. Euler Technologies' job is to keep that infrastructure secure and well maintained while shepherding the next wave of curators and tokenized assets onto the platform."

"It's great news that someone with Chris' wealth of experience will be helping shape Euler through its next chapter," said Michael Bentley, co-founder and former CEO of Euler Labs. "I've long admired his contributions to MakerDAO and other credit protocols. He has a deep understanding of credit markets and knows what users, curators and institutions need from lending infrastructure. I look forward to seeing Euler continue to grow with his help."

Under its agreement with Euler Foundation, Euler Technologies will receive 4,077,420 EUL over two years, representing 15% of the total EUL supply as of November 17, 2026. Payments will follow a payment schedule of an initial distribution of 3% of supply, followed by monthly disbursements of 0.5% of supply for 24 months. The allocation will come from Euler Foundation's existing holdings.

The Foundation has published a Tokenomics 2.0 page, providing an updated account of EUL distribution as of 17th November 2026 and showing how it will change under the Euler Technologies agreement.

The vesting schedules associated with EUL's original token allocations have already concluded or will shortly conclude. In addition, the new page identifies Foundation and treasury balances, documented team and other holdings, and tokens in public circulation. It also details Euler Technologies' allocation and release schedule, the treatment of any remaining contributor grants, and relevant wallet addresses.

For more information on this transition and Tokenomics 2.0 disclosures, visit Dune.

Euler is a modular DeFi lending protocol that lets anyone permissionlessly deploy a customised credit market for any asset, without compromising risk isolation. Euler Foundation operates as a non-profit entity dedicated to furthering the development of the Euler Protocol by supporting community governance and providing strategic oversight.

Euler Technologies is an independent service provider engaged by the Euler Foundation to operate, maintain, and secure the Euler protocol. Its remit covers protocol development on every network where Euler is deployed, smart contract security and key management, hosting of the Euler app, docs and forum, developer tooling and user support, alongside administrative and communications support for the Foundation. Euler Technologies is the trading name of Web3 Development Ventures Corp., incorporated in the Republic of Panama.
