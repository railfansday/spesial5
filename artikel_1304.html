The Spanish media have cast a strong verdict on Trent Alexander-Arnold after the England international’s performance for Real Madrid against Celta Vigo on Saturday.

It’s 18 months since Alexander-Arnold made the controversial decision to leave his boyhood club Liverpool for the Bernabeu.

The right-back signed a six-year contract, with the Reds receiving a paltry £8.5 million fee from the Spanish giants.

Trent played 30 matches for Real Madrid during his debut campaign in Spain but failed to win a trophy. He provided five assists but ended the season without a goal.

The appointment of Jose Mourinho as Real Madrid head coach in the summer left Alexander-Arnold’s future in doubt, particularly after the club splashed out £17 million to sign another right-back: Denzel Dumfries from Inter Milan.

While Trent, one of the best English footballers in the world, possesses many extraordinary attacking qualities, it was anticipated by many that the Englishman’s defensive frailties would cost him game time under the revered Portuguese tactician.

And that’s largely been the case during the opening months of the 2026-27 campaign. Trent has started only five of Real Madrid’s 13 league matches this term and has been an unused substitute on five occasions.

He only played one minute during October’s Clasico against Barcelona, and has featured in just one of Real Madrid’s four Champions League fixtures.

The 28-year-old was handed a start against Celta Vigo on Saturday but lasted just 57 minutes before Mourinho subbed him off.

Celta took the lead through Pablo Duran after just six minutes, with the goal coming down Real Madrid’s right-hand side.

Kylian Mbappe spurned the chance to equalise from the penalty spot midway though the first half and Trent was one of three Real Madrid players withdrawn, along with Alvaro Carreras and Bernardo Silva, with just over 30 minutes remaining.

Mourinho’s changes had the desired effect. Real Madrid won the match 2-1 thanks to goals from Jude Bellingham and Carlos Espi.

But make no mistake, this was a difficult evening for Alexander-Arnold, who was booed by his own fans at one point during the match.

The highest-paid English footballers in the world have been listed, including Harry Kane, Jude Bellingham and Bukayo Saka.

Per Liverpool.com, Spanish outlet Marca handed Trent an unflattering 4/10 rating, highlighting how a “lack of precision in attack” - noted as his “strong point” - failed to make up for his defensive weaknesses on this occasion.

“Too closed down on the play that led to Celta’s goal, having to cover a lot of ground to block Javi Galan’s run,” Marca continued.

“A failed cross from a good position to create danger. Another poor pass from an excellent position to put a dangerous ball ahead of the defence.”

“He continued to struggle after the restart and Mou [Mourinho] took him off in the 57th minute, bringing on Fede [Valverde]. A bad day for the Englishman.”

AS added: “He was caught out again, this time during Celta’s goal.

“Mourinho stuck with him until the 57th minute and he received some boos from the Bernabeu crowd when his name was announced as a substitute.

“His face and body language, completely expressionless, didn’t help matters either.”

It wouldn’t be a shock if it’s a little while until we see Trent back in Mourinho’s starting line-up following Saturday’s unconvincing display.
