Report Bruno Fernandes targeted by Milan along with Goncalo Inacio

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Milan are reportedly pushing for more Portuguese players to join Ruben Amorim’s side, including Sporting defender Goncalo Inacio and Manchester United midfielder Bruno Fernandes.

The Rossoneri have had a definite Portuguese flavour in recent years, already going through coaches Sergio Conceicao and Paulo Fonseca, then hiring Amorim over the summer.

They brought in striker Goncalo Ramos as their most expensive ever signing, and while Diego Moreira plays his international football for Belgium, he was a Portugal player at youth level.

It was no secret that Milan had pursued Sporting CP defender Goncalo Inacio over the summer, and they are widely expected to try again in the January transfer window.

It won’t be easy, as the asking price is a minimum of €40m, because his contract runs to June 2030 and has a release clause worth €60m.

According to The Athletic, another Portuguese name on their radar is Bruno Fernandes, who is frustrated at Manchester United and the arrival of new coach Mauricio Pochettino might not revitalise his fortunes.

Juventus have already been linked with the former Sampdoria and Novara midfielder, whose contract runs to June 2027 with an option to extend.

Milan are now also showing an interest in his situation, hoping for a low-cost option adding more experience to the midfield that already has Luka Modric.
