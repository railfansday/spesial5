JetBlue Raises Baggage Fees Again, Now Charges Up To $140 Roundtrip For One Checked Bag

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

JetBlue has raised checked baggage fees again, and bringing one ordinary suitcase can now add as much as $140 to a roundtrip ticket. With holiday travel approaching, this is another reminder that the fare you see when shopping may be far removed from what your trip actually costs.

JetBlue has increased baggage charges for new bookings made on or after November 3, 2026. The airline’s updated fee schedule lists a first checked bag starting at $50 each way. The price depends on when you travel and when you pay.

That means one suitcase costs $100 roundtrip even at the lowest new rate. On peak dates, it costs $110 when paid in advance or $140 when purchased within 24 hours of each departure.

Consider a family of four traveling over Thanksgiving, with each person checking one suitcase and no baggage benefits.

Pay sufficiently early and the roundtrip baggage bill is $440. Wait until check-in for both flights and it becomes $560.

When does it become cheaper just to buy new clothes at your destination? We certainly try to pack efficiently when traveling with our children, but one standard checked bag per person hardly strikes me as an extravagant amount of luggage, especially for a longer holiday trip.

At these prices, baggage can materially change which airline offers the best deal. A cheaper JetBlue ticket may cease to be cheaper once you add the luggage you actually need…though it would not surprise me to see others follow.

Mosaic 1 members receive one checked bag, while Mosaic 2–4 members receive two. Eligible JetBlue Plus and Premier cardholders receive one bag. Premium-cabin allowances depend on the fare purchased: Base fares include one bag, while standard and Flex premium fares include two.

Those benefits become more valuable every time the underlying fees rise, though it’s still not a loyalty program or credit card product I can get enthusiastic about.

JetBlue’s latest increase pushes the cost of one checked suitcase to $100–$140 roundtrip for passengers without an exemption. For families, the additional expense can reach hundreds of dollars.

Compare the total cost before booking, and if you know you will check a bag, pay more than 24 hours before departure. I want JetBlue to succeed, but charging passengers more to bring their belongings does little to make it an airline people actively want to choose. Quote the contrary, JetBlue is doing a great job of alienating passengers during its great time of need.
