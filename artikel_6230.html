The Best Aluminum Luggage for Travelers, From Iconic Rimowa Suitcases to Sleek Tumi Trunks

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The first time I traveled with an aluminum suitcase, I emerged into the airport with a whole new energy. There’s an undeniable allure to rolling through a terminal with a gleaming statement piece, shimmering as it does against a sea of boring black suitcases. There’s no way to travel more smugly.

Commerce writer Charley Ward testing Rimowa's iconic aluminum carry-on in Mallorca

But while the style is an undeniable draw, beyond the immediate hit of glamour, aluminum luggage offers some practical perks too. It offers greater structural protection than polycarbonate and soft-shell options—you can’t really argue with that, given it’s the type of material they make planes out of. And these durable suitcases are typically fitted with secure latch locking mechanisms over temperamental zippers, with steel corners and frames designed to comfortably absorb bumps, drops, and scrapes on the go.

Investing in high-end aluminum luggage is a serious price commitment—especially when considering that there are plenty of excellent soft-sided and hard-shell suitcases on the market. It’s why putting these more expensive suitcases through their paces in real-world scenarios is essential for our testing.

Our editors and contributors took the market's leading aluminum suitcases with us near and far, from long weekends in Milan and Mallorca to extended three-week international journeys, considering wheel smoothness, internal compression systems, weight-to-capacity ratios, and overall durability. Below, the select few of our editors’ favorite aluminum suitcase to travel with for every type of trip.

Carl Friedrik has built a cult following across the globe for its elevated, leather-trimmed luggage. Its venture into all-aluminum suitcases is nothing short of exceptional, with all the same careful design details we know and love from its original Carry-On. This new iteration retains the brand’s signature understated ridged design, super-secure dual lock (no zips to bust here), steel-reinforced corners, leather detailing, and smooth, 360-degree spinner wheels.

I packed comfortably for three nights in Milan and Paris: a couple of outfit changes, pajamas, gym clothes, a toiletry bag, and a spare pair of shoes. For warmer destinations where you'd prioritize packing lighter fabrics, you could likely stretch this to four or five- days’ worth of clothing. I also lent the suitcase to a colleague for a trip to Lisbon, who managed five-days’ worth of packing with ease—and said she could have got in more. Ultimately, capacity will depend on how heavy a packer you are, but it’s ideal for a long weekend during the winter at minimum.

What are the benefits of aluminum luggage?

Aluminum luggage offers superior durability and protection compared to fabric or plastic alternatives. Its rigid, crush-proof shell and heavy-duty frame safeguard fragile contents against rough handling and impact during transit. Aluminum suitcases come finished with latches instead of vulnerable zippers, creating an airtight, water-resistant seal that deters theft. Aluminum is built to last a lifetime, developing a distinctive, travel-worn patina over time rather than tearing or cracking. Then there’s that effortlessly sleek aesthetic at the airport, which is another fun perk.
