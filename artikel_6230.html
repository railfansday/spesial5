Among major hotel loyalty programs, Marriott Bonvoy offers some of the most attainable lifetime elite status tiers. I already have lifetime Gold Elite status with Marriott Bonvoy and will secure lifetime Platinum Elite status in three years.

If you're wondering if it's within reach for you, here's what you should know about Marriott lifetime status.

Marriott Bonvoy lets its members earn three tiers of lifetime elite status as follows:

Marriott also historically let members earn lifetime Titanium Elite status, but the program stopped offering it Dec. 31, 2018. Additionally, keep in mind that some Marriott brands don't offer a full qualifying night per eligible night stayed; for example, City Express, Four Points Flex and Protea Hotels award half an elite night credit per qualifying night, while StudioRes doesn't award any elite night credits.

The years of elite status don't need to be consecutive, and years spent at higher elite tiers count toward the requirements for lower lifetime tiers. Marriott status you get as a credit card perk counts toward the calculation of years in a specific elite status tier.

In addition to elite night credits earned from qualifying stays (which include qualifying award stays), elite night credits from credit cards and the five elite night credits you can select as an Annual Choice Benefit also count toward lifetime status.

Members with lifetime Marriott elite status enjoy the same benefits as members who earn these statuses annually, including waived points expiration. Plus, upon earning lifetime Platinum Elite status, you'll also get a complimentary gift and access to a webpage that looks back at your lifetime journey.

However, lifetime Platinum Elite status doesn't automatically qualify you for Annual Choice Benefits. You'll still need to earn 50 or 75 elite night credits in a calendar year to unlock these benefits.

And while lifetime members get the perks of the status they've earned, there's no guarantee that Marriott won't change the benefits of a particular status tier. So, if you earn lifetime Platinum Elite status, you'll get the current benefits of Platinum Elite instead of the benefits available at the time you secured lifetime status.

Marriott has made it easy to check your lifetime status progress. Go to the Marriott website, log in to your account, click your name in the upper-right corner and select "Overview." Then, click "Nights this year" on the left side of the page.

On the right side of the panel, you'll see your lifetime statistics.

This panel shows your lifetime nights, lifetime years with each status and how far you are from earning the next lifetime status tier.

Earning lifetime Marriott elite status can be relatively easy if you frequently stay with Marriott Bonvoy, hold a Marriott credit card that provides elite status and elite night credits, or both.

I'm looking forward to securing lifetime Platinum Elite status in three years. Although Marriott could change the benefits associated with each status tier, I'm excited about eventually enjoying Platinum Elite perks without requalifying each year.
