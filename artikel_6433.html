Feast Your Eyes on Uranus at Opposition This Thanksgiving 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Why late November 2026 is prime Uranus observing season.

The outer planets are ever so slowly starting to sneak back into the evening sky this month. First, Saturn crossed this mark last month on October 4th, then Neptune followed suit on September 26th. Now, the planet Uranus makes the transition, crossing opposition next week.

Uranus reached aphelion 20 Astronomical Units (AU) from the Sun in 2009, and is now inbound toward perihelion 18.3 AU distant in 2050. In 2033, Uranus will pass the point in Taurus where it was discovered by Sir William Herschel on March 13th, 1781, between the horns of the Bull very near the constellation's border with Gemini. Uranus made spurious appearances on star charts of the era at least 20 times prior to discovery.
