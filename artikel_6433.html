If you’ve been waiting to find a great deal on emergency backup power for your home or portable power for a road trip, Black Friday and Cyber Monday are some of the best times to find the lowest prices on home generators, portable generators, power stations, and solar generators. The good news is that deals are already dropping, even before the holiday shopping season.

This year, Black Friday falls on November 27, and you can expect discounts as high as 40 percent or more, with prices as low as, or sometimes even better than, what you can expect to find on Prime Day. If you’ve been waiting for a good deal on backup power for your home or even just a few devices, this is a great time to buy.

Start by considering what you want to power during an emergency, like a refrigerator, a computer setup, or the entire house. Or, if you're camping or on a job site, consider the devices you want to power or recharge, such as power tools or camping gear like portable refrigerators.

Then consider the type of generator or power station you want. Fuel-based generators can run as long as you have enough fuel, but they’re loud and must be run outside because they emit potentially dangerous fumes. Power stations, on the other hand, run virtually silently and don’t emit fumes, so you can run them inside, making them great for city dwellers. They need to be recharged, but you can do that with AC power or a solar panel.

No matter what type you choose, it will cost several hundred dollars or more, so Black Friday and Cyber Monday are a good time to knock 20 to 40 percent off the regular price, including popular brands such as Jackery, Bluetti, EcoFlow, Generac, and Champion Power Equipment.

Power stations come in a wide variety of models to fit just about any need, from recharging a few small devices to powering small appliances to backing up home whole-house electrical boxes. Larger stations can also be expanded with additional batteries, which also go on sale for Black Friday. Power stations need something to recharge their batteries, whether that be AC power from the grid or energy from the sun’s rays using solar panels, which is the most eco-friendly option. Buying a station and panels together is usually a good way to save money, especially on Black Friday, when these bundles go on sale for some of the lowest prices.

Tried and true, fuel-powered generators are popular because they can run indefinitely, as long as you have enough fuel on hand. Some run on just gasoline, while dual- or tri-fuel generators add propane or natural gas as alternatives. They can be loud, so an inverter generator reduces noise pollution and delivers cleaner power that’s safe for sensitive electronics like TVs. Black Friday discounts on generators aren’t typically as deep as those on power stations, but you can still find deals of 10 to 40 percent off.
