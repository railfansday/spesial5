Handwritten 200-year-old dismissal letter found on Rugeley roof tile

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Where Readers Share Great Links. Join Free

A Staffordshire Blue roof tile discovered at a salvage yard in Rugeley appears to carry a handwritten dismissal notice for a worker named John Lewis, signed by Thomas Austin. The message was written into soft clay before firing, and researchers think it may date to the early 19th century. The yard says the tile is unusually rare because handwritten text on roof tiles is far less common than the pawprints and shoe marks sometimes found on them.
