Anthropic just gave Claude users a new way to make Mac apps

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Use Claude on Mac or Windows? Anthropic just released a new way to make personalized desktop apps using AI tools.

“Starting today, you can use Claude artifacts to create, run, and share desktop apps. The “Install as app” feature is rolling out in beta in Claude for Mac and Windows,” Anthropic says.

“When an artifact is installed as a Mac or Windows app, it gets its own icon and opens in its own window instead of inside Claude. You can share it with others, and it needs your permission before it can interact with your computer. You choose how much to allow.”

Additionally, Anthropic says apps made with this method can benefit from Claude connectors. Claude usage is tied to desktop apps made with artifacts.

This form of Claude-created app is capable of sending notifications, adding items to the menu bar, and having its own permissions.

You can learn much more about the new system here. This is the second Claude update this week made specifically for builders.
