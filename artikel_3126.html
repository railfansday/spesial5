Terrestrial networks cannot reach every location. Oceans, remote regions, and disaster zones often remain without coverage. 5G non-terrestrial networks (NTN) address this gap by using satellites and high-altitude platforms. Introduced in 3GPP Release 17, NR-NTN keeps evolving through Releases 18, 19, and 20. Satellite links bring new challenges. Signals travel long distances, satellites move at high speed, and Doppler shift affects synchronization and timing. These effects shape payload design, mobility procedures, and device behavior. They also call for new testing methods for devices, satellites, and networks. This paper explains the technology step by step. It also shows how NTN is shaping unified terrestrial and non-terrestrial networks in 6G.
