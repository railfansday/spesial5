Buying a new fan in the middle of November probably wasn't on your to-do list. But let me take you back to this past summer. With temperatures higher than ever before, we all wanted one thing — a fan to keep us cool all day and all night.

Plus, many women get hit with hot flashes on a regular basis regardless of what the calendar says. That's where the Black Friday come in, because you can get some of the best cooling fans from Dyson for a fraction of the price. These fans were in high demand this past summer, with the Dyson HushJet Mini Cool repeatedly selling out.

I've put together a list of the best options to make your home comfortable year-round, with up to $200 off fans like the Dyson Hot+Cool for $299, heaters and air purifiers.

The Dyson HushJet Mini Cool delivers fast, focused airflow of up to 25 m/s and is surprisingly quiet in operation. As a versatile and portable fan, you can wear it, pop it on your desk, or hold it in your hand — it's incredibly convenient. It's very rarely on sale, but you can currently grab it for $12 off, and it's available in Stone/Blush, Carnelian/Sky, and Ink/Cobalt.

This desk fan is the quietest cooling fan Dyson offers. It has a circular head for airflow and many customizable settings. It features Air Multiplier technology to amplify surrounding air by 13 times for an uninterrupted airstream. It has customizable settings, an added sleep mode, improved oscillation and a digital LCD screen, which all make this $180 shocking!

The Dyson Cool Tower Fan is a bladeless cooling fan with 10 different speeds. It uses a remote control and is very sleek-looking, so it's the perfect home-decor addition. It gives you strong, direct airflow and doesn't require any filter changes over the years. With $150 off this Black Friday, it's one of the best options if you want to stay cool for a budget price next summer.

The Dyson HushJet is a well-designed air purifier that excels in smaller spaces and is whisper-quiet for zero disturbance.
