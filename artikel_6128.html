On the first Wednesday of each month, Atmos Rewards unveils new Members Day offers, including new Global Getaways destinations. Here's a look at the best November Members Day offers.

Atmos Rewards just launched its November Global Getaways offers. Each destination has its own eligible travel dates, but you must book by Nov. 7.

*Blackout dates to Hawaii are Dec. 17 to Dec. 30, 2026, and Feb. 12 through Feb. 15, 2027, and from Hawaii are Dec. 27, 2026, to Jan. 4, 2027, and Feb. 19 to Feb. 21, 2027.

Related: Alaska and Hawaiian expand 'Atmos Communities' with 6 new options to save and earn

One example of a route covered by this month's promotion is the following itinerary from Seattle to Venice. It costs just 25,000 points and $48 in taxes and fees. While writing this story, we saw 25,000-point award options for this route on many dates.

If you are an Atmos™ Rewards Summit Visa Infinite® credit card holder, the taxes and fees on this award are only $27.60.

Prefer a trip to Singapore? We found awards from Los Angeles to Singapore on many dates for 30,000 points and $49 in taxes and fees (or $29 if you are an Alaska Summit cardholder).

Meanwhile, you can fly from Seattle to Honolulu on many dates for 11,500 points and $6 in taxes and fees.

You'll want to book quickly, though, as availability at the lowest rates will likely decrease as other members book. To book, go to the Alaska Airlines or Hawaiian Airlines website, log in to your account, click the "Use points" box and enter your desired itinerary before clicking "Search flights."

Keep in mind that you can book many routes besides the ones discussed in this story as long as your award is between the U.S. and an eligible destination on eligible travel dates.

Atmos Rewards Unlocked is the rewards marketplace where Atmos Rewards members can redeem or bid points on experiences and partner offerings.

This month, Atmos Rewards is letting members redeem 65,000 Atmos Rewards points for a 2026-27 Ikon Base Pass and 100,000 points for an Ikon Pass with $100 of mountain credits for use at participating resorts. These redemptions include one free day of ski butler service during the season with an equipment rental of two days or more. Active Escapes community members save 20%; redemptions close Nov. 23.

You'll also find plenty of other opportunities to redeem Atmos Rewards points for unique experiences, including an Iditarod insider experience, special getaways and even a behind-the-scenes tour of the Alaska Airlines Global Training Center. Head to the Atmos Rewards Unlocked page to see all the options.

There are multiple ways to earn Atmos Rewards points. You can, of course, earn them by flying Alaska Airlines or Hawaiian Airlines. The carriers also partner with over 30 airlines, including (but not limited to) members of the Oneworld alliance.

The Atmos Rewards cobranded credit cards are also a good way to accumulate points. You can earn points on purchases with these cards, but their welcome offers are also a way to quickly boost your account balance.

You can also transfer Bilt Points to Atmos Rewards at a 1:1 ratio and Marriott Bonvoy points to Atmos Rewards at a 3:1 ratio. And through Nov. 7, you can buy up to 250,000 Atmos Rewards points with a bonus of up to 110%. The 110% bonus kicks in when you buy 30,000 points or more. So, you could buy 30,000 points and get 33,000 bonus points for $1,128.75 (a purchase rate of 1.79 cents per point).

Related: Atmos Ascent vs. Atmos Summit: The Summit is better — but not for everyone

The Atmos Rewards Unlocked program provides some interesting ways to redeem points, but the Atmos Rewards Global Getaways deals are the true star of each month's Members Day.

Global Getaways are an excellent opportunity for travelers who want to book travel to the highlighted destinations, can book their flights within a few days and can travel during the specified time period. And if you are striving for Atmos Rewards status, keep in mind that you'll earn status points on Atmos Rewards award tickets.
