There's often a lot of hype about the strategic benefits in a restructuring. Tripadvisor admitted its November 2025 restructuring didn't work.

Tripadvisor has unwound a 2025 restructuring that combined its “things to do” and Viator experiences teams under one roof. The two businesses are back to separate teams, similar to how they were organized before the November 2025 changes, according to a source familiar with Tripadvisor’s operations.

Under the new structure, Tripadvisor’s things to do business will have its own team. That business mainly generates leads for Viator, the company’s main experiences brand and the focus of its investment. Viator’s experiences operations will have a separate team.

Tripadvisor has reversed the November 2025 restructuring that merged its "things to do" and Viator experiences teams, returning to two separate organizations with, as President Kristen Dalton wrote to employees, "far less overlap between them and fewer shared services." The company told investors the combination "has not proven out as we expected": over the past 12 months, experiences booked through Viator and partner channels grew in the low teens while the Tripadvisor channel declined by high single digits, and third-quarter experiences revenue fell 3% year over year to $284.4 million. Tripadvisor said November 5 it would cut 425 employee and contractor roles in its legacy Hotels & Other segment — about 60% of roles dedicated to hotel metasearch, display and sponsored ads, flights, cruise, and car rentals — with about 150 moving to Viator and a net reduction of roughly 275 positions, or around 17% of the workforce according to the Boston Globe. Cruise Critic, which sources said is profitable, is shrinking by 60% to around 20 employees, with all but one editorial staffer gone. The restructuring is expected to save about $40 million a year against $15 million to $20 million in charges, and it lands amid a leadership overhaul: CEO Matt Goldberg steps down in February, CFO Michael Noonan leaves December 31, and Dalton becomes interim CFO on January 1. Activist investor Starboard Value disclosed a 9% stake in July 2025 and gained four of 10 board seats in March; Tripadvisor has said no option is off the table for monetizing Viator.
