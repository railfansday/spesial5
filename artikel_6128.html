When Will Androids Outnumber Humans?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Earth currently hosts only about 10,000 humanoid robots, but some published scenarios foresee roughly one billion by 2050. At the same time, human population growth is slowing. The United Nations World Population Prospects 2024 median projection places the global human population at about 8.2 billion in 2025, rising to a peak of roughly 10.3 billion in the mid-2080s before beginning a gradual decline.
