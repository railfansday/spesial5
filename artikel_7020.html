On the Couch with Christopher John Rogers

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

In honor of his new collaboration with The Rug Company, the fashion designer shares his inspirations and style icons.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE Decor editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Christopher John Rogers has an eye for color. The fashion designer, born in Baton Rouge and based in New York, established his self-titled label in 2016. Over the past decade, he’s become known for his use of bold tones and voluminous silhouettes, and for dressing the likes of Michelle Obama and Cardi B. Now, Rogers is taking his style to the floor in a new collaboration with The Rug Company.

The collection, which Rogers designed with Will Cooper, creative director of The Rug Company, feels alive, with vibrant, swooshing marks, multicolored doodles in wool and silk, and wavy threads that appear to dance across the floor. “The scribbles, the brushstrokes, the happy accidents, the lines that don't quite meet,” Rogers tells ELLE Decor. “I love the idea of taking something spontaneous and seemingly incidental and rendering it permanent through this incredibly meticulous, centuries-old craft.”

Launching tomorrow, the collaboration includes six styles across ten colorways. But Rogers has more planned beyond womenswear, “exploring new ways for the brand to exist beyond the wardrobe,” he says. “I'm particularly excited by opportunities that allow us to think about color, proportion, texture, and materiality through different disciplines, whether that's furniture, interiors, or something entirely unexpected.”

Below, Rogers takes ELLE Decor’s On the Couch survey, sharing a few of his favorite pieces, destinations, and entertaining takes.

Favorite Sofa: Pierre Paulin's ABCD sofa for Artifort. The color theory is delicious, and I'd happily sacrifice a little comfort for something that fabulous.

Architect You Admire: Ettore Sottsass. I love the clarity of his vision and his ability to make the simplest shapes feel monumental.

Destination Museum: The Louisiana Museum of Modern Art in Denmark

Go-to Galleries: 52 Walker, Broadway Gallery, and Salon 94 in New York.

Favorite Artist/Artwork: I've recently fallen for Domenica Brockman. We have one of her paintings in the studio, and I'm constantly struck by how much emotion she can communicate through deceptively simple arrangements of color.

Vintage Piece of Your Dreams: An original Verner Panton Living Tower.

Hotels to Check Into: Hotel Saint Vincent in New Orleans, and La Mamounia in Marrakech.

Entertaining Tips: Great lighting, a boozy cocktail, and a playlist with a point of view. Don't overthink it!

Style Icons: Bert and Ernie, and Isaac Mizrahi.

Required Reading: Interaction of Color by Josef Albers and The Bluest Eye by Toni Morrison. Two very different but equally essential ways of learning to see.

Cocktail or Mocktail of Choice: It's a toss-up between a wet Roku martini and a Cynar spritz.

Favorite Restaurant: Cervo’s in the Lower East Side, Rolo's in Ridgewood, Frenchette in Tribeca, and Santa Panza in Bushwick.

Favorite Jeweler: Presley Oldham and Bernard James for contemporary jewelry, and vintage Alexander Calder or Alber Elbaz-era Lanvin for something a little more fantastical.
