What Bristol City fans chanted towards Michael Skubala sparks reaction online

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Michael Skubala has only been at Bristol City since the summer, but pressure is already starting to build under him, and this reached a new low with a 5-0 home defeat to Norwich City.

The Bristol City manager Michael Skubala is already coming under significant pressure after his team lost 5-0 at home to Norwich City on Saturday. Paris Maghoma was the standout Canaries player as Norwich strolled to an easy win at Ashton Gate which left the new boss on a run of just one win in his last seven Championship matches.

Skubala was only appointed as the Bristol City manager in the summer, having guided Lincoln City into the second tier for the first time since 1961 at the end of last season. But the Ashton Gate faithful weren't shy in expressing their feelings towards him as his team slumped to an embarrassing defeat which leaves them in 17th place in the Championship table as teams pause for another international break.

On the social media platform X, Championship Terrace Talk posted a video in which it was extremely evident how some Bristol City fans felt towards the end of their team's 5-0 home humbling at the hands of Norwich City on Saturday afternoon.

Against a backdrop of images of some fans leaving Ashton Gate, a song criticising Skubala can clearly be heard, with Championship Terrace Talk adding that, "All 4 sides of Ashton Gate letting the gaffer know what they think!"

The Norwich defeat was Bristol City's seventh in the Championship already this season, and all the more damagingly for the club's new manager, six of these defeats have come at home.

The anger of the Bristol City fans in the video is clearly audible, and this means that the new manager's future at the club could already be at risk even though he was only appointed into the position on the 29th May.

It'll be a crumb of comfort to Michael Skubala that his Bristol City team remain three places above the Lincoln City team that he left to join them, but this doesn't mean that he isn't clearly coming under considerable pressure in his new position.

Skubala was one of the toasts of the EFL in 2025-26, as he led his Lincoln team to the League One title with 103 points, finishing twelve clear of second-placed Cardiff City. The offer to jump ship and head to Ashton Gate came almost as soon as the season ended.

But if Bristol City fans were expecting a tilt at promotion to the top-flight for the first time since 1980 this season, they've so far been largely disappointed. A decent run of three wins from their first five games lifted them as high as fourth place in the Championship table, but their form has tailed off drastically since then, with them having only won twice since the first week of September.

Given that most home season-ticket holders don't also travel to away matches as well, Skubala's reputation has been particularly damaged by the fact that City have already lost six home League games this season. When the primary experience of the team for fans is seeing them lose at home, the pressure can be turned up even if form away from home is better.

And the reaction to this loss indicates that making the decision to swap Sincil Bank for Ashton Gate may have been an error of judgement on the part of the manager.

Expectations at Bristol City might not be quite as high as at some other Championship clubs, but with this season being City's twelfth consecutive season at this level and the team only having reached the play-offs once before, frustration is already growing at the team's inability to make the big step-up to be serious challengers for promotion to the Premier League.
