Birmingham City expected to offload winger in January – Plymouth Argyle should eye move: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Plymouth Argyle are currently positioned 6th in the League One play-offs as they eye promotion to the Championship. PAFC are in the top six along with Oxford United, Luton Town and Sheffield Wednesday after 15 fixtures as the likes of Reading, Bradford City and MK Dons chasing them down.

The Pilgrims are the lowest scorers out of the top six teams in the third tier at the moment. Therefore, they should bolster their attacking department this winter with the addition of Birmingham City winger Scott Wright.

Wright, 29, is expected to be available to leave the Blues in January and he knows what it takes to get out of the third tier after helping Chris Davies’ win the title back in 2025. He has fallen out of favour with the Midlands outfit now though and a change of scene would do him good.

Plymouth Argyle missed a hatful of chances last time out as they lost 3-0 to Sheffield Wednesday at Hillsborough and they will be eager to bounce back and return to winning ways as soon as possible. They face Peterborough United at Home Park this weekend.

Plymouth Argyle could do with some more competition and depth going forward. If PAFC landed Wright, he would give them something different to pick from on the wing.

He is quick, strong and experienced so would be a handy option for the Pilgrims. The attacker could battle it out with the likes of Xavier Amaechi and Ronan Curtis for a place in Tom Cleverley’s starting XI.

Wright’s contract at Birmingham City expires in June 2027 meaning he is due to become a free agent when this campaign is over. He has seen his game time dry up with the Blues so it wouldn’t be a surprise to see them let him head out the exit door soon for a new challenge.

The ex-Scotland youth international joined them back in 2024 and has since made 24 outings, as well as spending time away on loan at Dundee. Prior to his switch, he had spells above the border at Aberdeen and Rangers.

Plymouth Argyle can offer Wright regular minutes in the division below. PAFC could provide him with a platform to show what he can do.

The Aberdeen-born man need to get out of Birmingham City for the sake of his career now. He is surplus to requirements with the Blues and will feel like he has a point to prove in the EFL.

Cleverley’s men need a response on Saturday afternoon against Peterborough United after their disappointing defeat at Sheffield Wednesday. Their upcoming opponents are in the bottom four with Doncaster Rovers, Barnsley and Bromley.
