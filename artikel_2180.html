EFL expert drops ‘exciting’ West Brom promotion prediction

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

West Brom are positioned in 5th place in the Championship table after 15 fixtures and are in the play-offs along with Stoke City, Wolves, Swansea City, Southampton and QPR. WBA are only a point behind joint-top Birmingham City and Middlesbrough.

The Baggies beat Millwall 3-0 at The Hawthorns last time out before the international break. Isaac Price, Jayson Molumby and Felix Horn Myhre got their goals against the Lions as they secured another eye-catching win.

Elek has been impressed with the Midlands outfit and has delivered his verdict on the latest episode of the Not The 20 Podcast on YouTube: “They have been absolutely phenomenal in the past couple of games. They’ve gone from a team I thought are going to start well and then drop off to a relegation scrap. They’ve proven me completely wrong. In 5th place, a point off top spot. Talking about can Birmingham maintain this, can West Brom? The answer is yes. The numbers are good, the performances are good. The results are good. The players are good. It’s all looking quite exciting.”

West Brom endured a tough past campaign and churned through both Ryan Mason and Eric Ramsay before they settled on James Morrison. WBA were in a relegation battle before Morrison managed to keep them up in the end.

Morrison has managed to steady the ship with the Baggies and has done a solid job. His side are now in the top eight and are eyeing the Premier League.

It was a busy summer for them and they ended up bringing in the likes of Jimmy-Jay Morgan, Barney Stewart and Rabby Nzingoula, among others, to bolster their ranks. They also cut ties with players including Alex Mowatt, Jed Wallace and Karlan Grant to help free up space and funds to pave the way for reinforcements.

West Brom opted to go for young and hungry players in the last transfer window and they now have a nice blend of youth and experience at their diposal. Morrison will get the opportunity to sign some more new faces in January if needed ahead of the second-half of this term.

As Elek has alluded to, West Brom should be considered genuine promotion contenders. WBA are solid at the back and have threats at the top end of the pitch.

In goalkeeper Max O’Leary, they have an experienced stopper who knows the league well and is a safe pair of hands. Defender George Campbell has become one of their most prized assets in defence alongside the know-how of Nat Phillips and/or Chris Mepham.

Nzingoula, Molumby and Ousmane Diakite have bags of energy in the middle of the park, whilst Horn Myhre and Price have quality to hurt opposition defenders. Up top, Morgan has a very bright future ahead of him in the game and the Baggies’ decision to sign him earlier this year was a masterstroke.

West Brom are back in action on Saturday 21st October with an away trip to Swansea City. They should be in confident mood ahead of their trek down to South Wales.
