8 players set to miss Bristol City vs Wolves ft 130-cap international

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Bristol City and Wolverhampton Wanderers face off in league action against each other for the first time in eight years on Wednesday night, with both clubs looking to back up winning ends to October.

The Robins were involved in a dramatic contest against Bolton Wanderers, but one in which they emerged on the right side of a 3-2 scoreline courtesy of Scott Twine's 93rd-minute winner, whilst Wanderers made it successive home victories with a dominant 3-0 win against Cardiff City.

As a result, Wolves moved up to third ahead of the midweek round of fixtures, just two points behind the automatic promotion places after 13 games, whilst a timely success for the West Country outfit saw them move up to 14th.

An entertaining game will be expected given the two sides have scored 38 goals between them so far this term, albeit 23 of those have come from the visitors.

However, as many as seven players could be unavailable to Michael Skubala and Cesar Peixoto.

A guaranteed absentee for Bristol City is Swedish defender, Noah Eile, who has been sidelined since the beginning of the last international break due to illness.

The 24-year-old has been limited to just three Championship appearances as a result, but Skubala was able to provide an update on his wellbeing ahead of the midweek fixture.

"He's still building back," said the City boss. "He's probably the furthest away still, because of his illness, but he's doing better every day. He's back out on the grass now, as an individual."

It also remains to be seen if fellow centre-back, Rob Dickie, makes the matchday squad for Wednesday's clash, with the experienced figure missing City's last six matches with a knee injury.

Skubala revealed that he wasn't near the required level of match sharpness to feature against Bolton, but Dickie is now back in training after his lengthy layoff.

Elijah Adebayo has been absent since the 1-0 defeat to Sheffield United on October 17th, having made just six appearances for his new employers since joining in the summer.

The seasoned forward is yet to get off the mark in red, and it remains to be seen whether he is deemed fit enough to make the squad against Wolves after picking up a quad strain.

Skubala has revealed that the forward is "not too far off" a return, though.

A guaranteed absentee for the Old Gold and Black, though, is 130-time Mexican international and Molineux cult hero, Raul Jimenez.

The 35-year-old returned to the West Midlands in June on a free transfer and had already scored three times in his first six Championship games before sustaining a serious knee injury against Everton in the EFL Cup two months ago, which saw Peixoto receive plenty of criticism for his team selection.

Jimenez has since undergone surgery on the issue, but isn't expected to return to full fitness until January.
