Clone Drone in the Danger Zone finally drops release date for mobile version

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Action is arguably the cornerstone of gaming as a medium. Whether it's jumping Koopas or stacking blocks as the music increases in pace, excitement and thrills serve as the foundation for so many classics. And believe me when I say Clone Drone in the Danger Zone, releasing November 12th, is no different.

Clone Drone puts you in the shoes of a human gladiator, uploading yourself into the body of customisable robotic fighters in an arena, overseen by an evil empire of intelligent machines. It's up to you to survive the arena, link up with the resistance and fight back against your robotic overlords.

And while not explicitly stated, Clone Drone's 10th anniversary update is also set to drop on the 12th, coming packed with a host of new content. That includes new challenges, endless mode levels and quality-of-life updates, so there'll be even more to sink your manipulators into!

With its mix of single-player, action-packed gameplay alongside a simple but surprisingly robust narrative, not to mention co-operative play and PvP, Clone Drone in the Danger Zone comes jam-packed with stuff to do, no matter where your tastes might lie!
