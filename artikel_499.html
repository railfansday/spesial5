Latin America Currencies Sink on Rate Worries; Brazil Intervenes

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A selloff in Latin American currencies extended to a second session on Thursday, sending the Mexican peso to its lowest since June 2025 and pushing Brazil’s central bank to step in to prop up the real, amid the mounting prospects for more rate hikes in the US.
