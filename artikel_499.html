Kohls Black Friday 2026: See the ad and shop early deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Get your Kohl's Cash ready: the Black Friday Kohl's ad scan is here.

Here's what you need to know: Kohl's savings event starts Nov. 22, both in stores and online, five days before the actual day of Black Friday on Nov. 27. While the main sale runs through Saturday, Nov. 28, shoppers can save up to 50% across categories, stack an extra 20% off with coupons, and earn a boosted $15 in Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent (up from the usual $10).

If you plan to shop in person, keep in mind that Kohl's stores will be closed on Thanksgiving Day and will reopen at 5 a.m. local time on Friday, Nov. 27.

Across their catalog, there will be deals on apparel, electronics, kitchen appliances, toys and games, beauty products, and even vacuums. Looking for a preview of what to shop? We have you covered.

Note: Kohl's hasn't confirmed specifically what will be on sale in some categories. Products with a 🔍 emoji are ones we hope to see go on sale (or receive a better deal than they currently have) within confirmed product categories. All other products have been announced officially as deals.
