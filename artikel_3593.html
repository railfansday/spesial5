GTA 6 Promo At KFC Is A Fake Funeral So You Can Take The Day Off To Play

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The popular fried chicken chain KFC has announced a frankly absurd GTA 6 promotion: a fake funeral so people have a “legitimate” excuse to take the day off to play Rockstar’s latest.

The fake funeral for a woman, "Ann Alibi," will take place Thursday, November 19 at 11 AM at KFC Robina in Queensland. People can attend in person or watch the funeral by livestream. The local restaurant is part of a region of Australia known as the Gold Coast, which KFC says is the equivalent of GTA 6's Vice City (which itself is a stand-in for Miami).

"The service will include a hearse, coffin, floral tributes, a book of condolences, an order of service and even a heartfelt eulogy delivered by a surprise guest. All the appropriate arrangements for a fitting farewell," KFC said.

The restaurant is also handing out free fried chicken to anyone who shows up in person. For those who can't make the services, KFC will offer free delivery through its app across Australia on November 19.

Anyone planning to eat fried food and play video games should be mindful of what might happen to their controller ... unless you got your hands on the limited-edition "greaseproof" controller that Microsoft put out years ago. Check out the video below to see how an Xbox controller holds up to heavy grease.

A KFC spokesperson said of the GTA 6 promo, "We appreciate that Australians have responsibilities, but some occasions are simply too important to miss. And in accordance with Ann's final wishes, we've made sure the funeral takes place during working hours."

GTA 6 launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. People in Australia will be among the first in the world to play it since the game unlocks at midnight local time and not simultaneously around the world. This means people in the US will be among the last in the world to get to play GTA 6.
