Robert Pattinson Named the Next James Bond After Thorough Search

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After a years-long search, Amazon MGM Studios has found its next James Bond in Robert Pattinson.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime,” Pattinson, 40, said in a statement to Us on Monday, November 16. “I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”

When reacting to the news, the actor also gave thanks to director Denis Villeneuve and producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman for giving him the opportunity to play such an iconic character.

Amazon MGM Studios also confirmed the casting by sharing several posts on Instagram with the caption, “Identity Confirmed.”

According to The Hollywood Reporter, the casting process was so secretive that Villeneuve, 59, only informed Pattinson that he got the role on Sunday, November 15. Pascal, 68, is said to have called the runners-up.

“Before anything else, I want to express my deep gratitude to the remarkable actors who joined us on this journey. We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007,” Villeneuve said in a statement to Us. “I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin.”

Pascal added, “James Bond is one of the most beloved, inspiring, iconic and yet enigmatic characters in literature and movies. The legacy of this character is in great hands with Robert Pattinson. His fearlessness as an actor, his self-deprecating wit, and his ability to continually reinvent himself make him the perfect Bond for our times.”

Since breaking out in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire and the Twilight franchise, Pattinson’s acting career has continued to grow and impress fans and movie critics alike.

As the face of DC Studios’ Batman franchise, Pattinson also had a major supporting role in Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey and starred in The Drama. He is set to star in Dune: Part Three, which is due out in theaters on December 18. He is also shooting The Batman: Part II, whose production is currently underway in London.

According to Deadline, the process to find the next 007 goes back to the spring, when the studio announced that casting director Nina Gold was joining the project.

While not confirmed, sources told the outlet that Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, Nicholas Hoult and Josh O’Connor were some of the actors who also tested for the role.

The Bond casting comes nearly 21 years after Daniel Craig was announced as the suave British secret agent in October 2005. Craig, now 58, starred in Casino Royale, Quantum of Solace, Skyfall and Spectre before his final appearance as 007 in 2021’s No Time to Die.
