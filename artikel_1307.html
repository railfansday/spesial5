How Belgium defeat changes Italy’s qualifying campaign for EURO 2028

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Italy were knocked out of the Nations League at the end of the group stage following a 4-1 defeat away against Belgium in Brussels on Sunday night, which means that Roberto Mancini and his players will be involved in EURO 2028 qualifiers instead of the Nations League quarter-finals during the next international break.

Sunday’s defeat against Belgium was Italy’s final international fixture of the calendar year, and when they return to action in March, the focus will have already turned to qualification for the next major tournament: EURO 2028.

The 4-1 loss against Belgium saw Italy finish their Nations League group in third place, despite starting the night in first, and means that Italy will not be taking part in the Nations League quarter-finals in March. They will kick off their EURO qualifying campaign instead.

The nations involved in qualification for EURO 2028 will be divided into 12 groups of either four or five. As Italy have not qualified for the Nations League quarter-finals, they will be placed into a group of five rather than four and will begin their qualification campaign in March, rather than September.

Italy will face the other four teams in their qualifying group home and away. Nations that finish at the top of their group will qualify directly for the final tournament, as will the eight best-ranked runners up.

The four remaining runners up, along with the best-ranked non-qualified group winners from the Nations League will compete in the play-offs.

The EURO 2028 qualifying draw will take place in Belfast on Sunday, December 6 at 17.00 GMT.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
