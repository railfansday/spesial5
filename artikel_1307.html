David Raya Injury: Expert Predicts How Long Arsenal Star Could Miss

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Arsenal fans are fearing the worst after David Raya came off injured in a 2-2 draw against Newcastle United and an injury expert's verdict on the Spanish goalkeeper's recovery time won't help alleviate those fears.

Raya suffered a shoulder injury at St James' Park on Saturday (November 21st) after colliding with Newcastle forward William Osula in the second half. After trying to continue, the Spaniard pulled up and signaled that he needed to come off.

Mikel Arteta turned to Kepa Arrizabalaga and while his fellow Spaniard entered the fray, Raya trudged off in tears, visibly in pain. His manager said after the game that he would undergo an MRI tomorrow but was unsure about the extent of the injury.

Raya's injury came during a frustrating draw away to Newcastle having dominated the first half. The Spaniard has been superb ever since joining the Gunners from Brentford in the summer of 2023 and there are now fears Arteta could be without his shot-stopper for an extended period.

@physioscout on X has assessed Raya's injury and believes it could mean he is sidelined for up to over five months. That's in the worst-case scenario and depends on whether structural damage requires surgery.

Describing how the injury occurred, they wrote: "A collision with William Osula appears to briefly drive Raya's shoulder forwards while his arm is held against his body, causing pain before he hits the ground. This could place stress on the shoulder's stabilising structures, particularly the joint capsule and labrum, potentially causing instability."

Delving into the injury itself, they suspect Raya to have suffered a "glenohumeral capsular/labral injury or shoulder subluxation, with an AC joint sprain/rotator cuff injury also possible." As for how long Arteta may be without his goalkeeper, they claim the expected recovery time to be around 2-6 weeks.

Around 3–5+ months on the sidelines could be a possibility if surgical repair is needed, but the exact diagnosis remains unknown. It's described as "an innocuous-looking mechanism, with seemingly minimal contact causing pain, making it difficult to determine the exact injury from the footage alone."

Jamie Redknapp believes Raya is the one player Arteta can't replace and if he is out of action for a lengthy period, it will be a massive setback. The Sky Sports pundit said that Arsenal's squad player for player has two players for every position:

"The fact that their goalkeeper, the way he walked off that pitch he looked like he was in severe pain with his elbow. That is the one player that is irreplaceable in that team."

High summer spending failed to plug the goalscoring void as tactical lapses and missed chances reshaped the title race.

Meanwhile, Ian Wright said that it was a "massive worry" and "massive blow" before hesitating to say that he's petrified with Kepa replacing him. Redknapp asked whether Kepa or Illan Meslier was the better option to fill in for Raya and the former Arsenal striker believes Arteta will go with Kepa.
