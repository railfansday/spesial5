Mix of Yamal and Bale: Tottenham Set to Tie Down 'Special' Star

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Tottenham may have just made their best decision in years as an update emerges on the future of Luca Williams-Barnett.

Williams-Barnett produced a phenomenal first Premier League start for Tottenham, producing a goal and an assist as Spurs defeated Ipswich Town.

The young forward found the back of the net after just 15 minutes as he lost his marker at the back post to bundle the ball home.

Early in the second half, Williams-Barnett carried the ball from inside his own half before beating his man and getting to the byline, then delivered a phenomenal cross into the box for Dominic Solanke to tap home.

It was an incredibly impressive display from the young talent, and it shows exactly why many Tottenham fans were begging for him to be given more minutes.

There have been doubts surrounding the future of Williams-Barnett in recent weeks due to his lack of minutes, with Manchester United among those showing an interest.

However, some good news has now emerged for Tottenham fans.

According to the Press Association, Williams-Barnett has committed his long-term future to Tottenham after an agreement was reached over a new contract.

Roberto De Zerbi had been reluctant to give Williams-Barnett too many minutes so far this season, but he thrust him into the starting 11 against Ipswich. Williams-Barnett, described as 'special' by De Zerbi, will undoubtedly have been looking for more minutes already, but now is his opportunity to stay in the side.

His contract was due to expire in 2028, so with just under two years remaining on his current deal, a number of clubs were watching his situation closely.

Having under two years left is always a worrying situation for clubs, especially for a player who wasn't getting many minutes.

Rival clubs may have been looking to offer Williams-Barnett more of a pathway to the first team, but De Zerbi showed against Ipswich that that pathway is there for the youngster.

Now it's all about him staying in the side and keeping senior players out of it, and a goal and an assist will go a long way to doing that.

Tottenham Hotspur recorded their third successive home Premier League win

De Zerbi had some strong praise for Williams-Barnett following the victory over Ipswich.

The Tottenham manager compared him to Barcelona sensation Lamine Yamal, one of the best young players in world football, suggesting that he could reach similar heights to the young superstar.

Williams-Barnett certainly has the trickery and ball-carrying ability of a player like Yamal. He could also be compared to someone like Gareth Bale, especially looking at his assist against Ipswich.

Bale regularly carried the ball from inside his own half down that left-hand side of the pitch. Although they are different players, Williams-Barnett's run was undoubtedly Bale-esque as he got to the byline to deliver.

If Williams-Barnett can reach similar heights to Bale, one of the greatest Premier League players of all time, during his Tottenham career, then Spurs will certainly be happy.

Tying Williams-Barnett down to a new contract could be the best bit of business Tottenham have done in years, especially if he hits the heights that many expect him to.
