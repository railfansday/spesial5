The Division 2 Y8S3 Red Horizon Climax mission walkthrough

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Climax mission for Y8S3 Red Horizon is now available in The Division 2, and it’s your guaranteed shot at an Exotic. Once unlocked, you can then complete the mission at Master to guarantee yourself the Loaded for Bear Exotic gloves.

To unlock the mission, complete the three Scout challenges in Y8S3 that feature HVTs. You can also complete the remaining Scout weeks, but that’s completely optional. Once unlocked, head to the Washington National Airport to begin the mission.

Wait for the cutscene to play out, as Manny will provide you with the starting instructions. Your first task is to help the militia stuck at the clinic. The marker will directly lead you to the room where a few militia members are stuck.

Kill all the enemies you find at the location. There will be two waves of enemies, with a few elite ones to take down. They will fire at you from all sides, so be careful about incoming damage.

Once you clear all the enemies, your next task is to rendezvous with Avery Miles. Follow the marker to reach Avery and protect the NPC from all incoming damage. There will be several enemies to take down, and they will all primarily focus their shots towards Avery. Your best option is to kill the enemies after intercepting them.

Once the remaining Cleaners are killed, you can proceed to the next step. Follow the marker to reach the Hangar, and there will be plenty of enemies to kill. Since the fight takes place in an open area, use the fence coverings as much as possible.

Once you take care of the cleaners, you’ll need to disable two fuel pumps. The pumps are on either side of the deconstructed aircraft in the middle. You can just trace them by their pipes. However, focus first on the enemies since that will make things easier for you.

Keep proceeding through the airport and clear the incoming enemies until you get the objective to secure the concourse. Then, reach the tarmac to sabotage the pumps. Keep killing the enemies until the objective marker pops up to disclose the pump locations.

Finally, Bobby Guzman will appear. He is the main enemy of this Climax mission, and he will be a hard challenge. He has plenty of armor on him, and he is surrounded by other elite enemies.

Finally, a timer will commence, giving you approximately a minute to escape. Do so safely, and you’ll successfully finish the Climax mission. You get the guaranteed Exotic drop by playing at Master. Anything less means obtaining the Exotic will be luck-dependent.
