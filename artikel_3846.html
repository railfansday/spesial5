PlayStation Black Friday Sale Knocks Up to $100 Off PS5 Accessories, PSVR2

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Alongside its ongoing PS Store Black Friday sale and a new PS Plus promotion that goes live tomorrow, Sony has also detailed the range of discounts it'll apply to PS5 accessories from 16th November 2026.

Here are the discounts Sony is offering on PlayStation Direct and at participating retailers:

If you're a PS Plus member from Austria, Belgium, France, Germany, Italy, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Portugal, Spain, or the United Kingdom, then you can save an additional 5% on the sale price if you order through PlayStation Direct.

There shall also be discounts for physical copies of Marvel's Wolverine, Saros, Ghost of Yotei Complete Edition, Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls, and more.

Of course, what you won't find in the list of Black Friday discounts is any offers relating to the base PS5 console or the PS5 Pro. Neither machine is going on sale during Black Friday this year. That marks a change compared to last year's promotion, where Sony had a new PS5 bundle featuring Fortnite to sell at a $100 discount. No such thing appears to exist this year.

Since we are now just days away from GTA 6 launching, and with these discounts going live tomorrow for two weeks, I can't say I'm too surprised — Sony will know it can sell PS5 consoles at full price with the Rockstar title looming.
