Senior Accountant at Counterpart

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Counterpart is the first Agentic Insurance™ platform, combining trusted insurance expertise with cutting-edge AI to deliver tailored management and professional liability solutions. By integrating Agentic Underwriting™, Agentic Broker Services™, Agentic Risk Mitigation™, Agentic Claims Management™, Agentic Coverage™, and Agentic Capacity™ into one powerful platform, Counterpart empowers brokers - and the businesses they serve - to do more with less risk. Backed by A rated carriers, Counterpart has been recognized by CB Insights as a 2025 “Top 50 Insurtech,” by Great Place to Work, and by Business Insurance as one of its 2025 “Best Places to Work.”

As Senior Accountant, you execute the day-to-day financial operations within Agentic Finance - premium accounts receivable and cash application, accounts payable, bank and balance sheet reconciliations, bordereau and premium reconciliation, carrier and claims payments, expense management, and audit and tax support - that keep Counterpart, Agentic Programs™, and Counterpart Insurance Company running. You support premium cash application, accounts payable, bank reconciliations, expense management, and vendor administration, and you prepare the bordereau, carrier payment, close, and audit work that the Accounting Manager and Controller review. You build and use the agents that make this execution more efficient as the company scales. You report to the Controller and work closely with the Accounting Manager.

Our estimated pay range for this role is $110,000 to $130,000. Base salary is determined by a variety of factors, including but not limited to, market data, location, internal equitability, and experience.
