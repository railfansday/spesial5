Juventus boss reacts to Manchester United’s midfield link: Is he worth £28m?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Tuttomercato carries Luciano Spalletti’s reaction to the talk linking Weston McKennie with Manchester United, and the Juventus head coach played it down. He spoke to the midfielder as soon as the story broke and didn’t think McKennie had hinted at wanting to leave, calling his comments a general remark. Spalletti said their relationship is as good as the one he has with everybody else, and everything felt calm once McKennie returned from international duty.

Those comments came from McKennie himself, who suggested during USMNT camp that Mauricio Pochettino could bring American players to Old Trafford. La Gazzetta dello Sport then reported that United’s new head coach wants him in January, though McKennie says he isn’t planning to leave and Fabrizio Romano insists United aren’t working on a deal.

Pochettino inherits a club in trouble: one win and eight points from ten league games, 18th place and Michael Carrick’s sacking after their worst league start in 40 years. The Argentine takes charge at Anfield today, before Wednesday’s Champions League trip to Sporting CP.

McKennie, 28, offers versatility: Spalletti has used him on both wings, at wing-back and even as a striker, and he managed eight goals and five assists in 38 appearances before signing his new deal in March. This season the output has dipped, with FotMob showing one goal, no assists and a 6.98 rating from eight Serie A starts. His contract runs to 2030, and FotMob values him at about £28m.

Not as a January priority. United’s problem is a team that loses control of matches, and McKennie is a runner who arrives in the box rather than a midfielder who dictates tempo. Put him next to a struggling back four and his versatility becomes a drawback, because Spalletti gets the best from him higher up the pitch, where his goals come from.

Pochettino’s trust is a real advantage, since he knows exactly what work rate he gets, but trust doesn’t turn a 28-year-old with one league goal this season into a £28m solution to a defensive crisis. Juventus also just rewarded him with a contract to 2030, so they have no reason to sell cheaply.

Romano mentioned Youri Tielemans, Carlos Baleba and Andrey Santos in the same breath, and each offers more control from midfield. If Pochettino wants a familiar face, a rotation option on a short deal makes sense. Spending about £28m on a runner does not.
