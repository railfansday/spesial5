PSG: Warren Zaire-Emery discusses rivalry with Paris FC

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Questioned this Saturday evening in the mixed zone after Paris Saint-Germain’s victory against Troyes (1-0), Warren Zaire-Emery was asked to react to the rise in power of Paris FC, current runner-up to the Parisian club in Ligue 1. While the Parisian club’s neighbor is asserting itself as an unexpected competitor in the race for the top places, the Parisian midfielder prefers to keep his eyes fixed on his team rather than being distracted by the ranking.

“We try to focus on ourselves. Frankly, I don’t look at the rankings. Afterwards, the others, I don’t know, but we try to concentrate on ourselves and give the best possible performances and always be in first place in the ranking.explained the Parisian titi. A speech which testifies to PSG’s desire not to disperse and to maintain control of its race at the top of the championship, despite growing pressure from its neighbor.
