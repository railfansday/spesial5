The ranking of European top scorers

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

This Tuesday is the sixth assessment of the ranking of European top scorers for the 2026/2027 season. We thus find the scorers who shook the nets in Germany, England, Belgium, Spain, France, Italy, Netherlands, Portugal and Türkiye. And it’s still Raphinha in the lead. Scorer despite the 2-1 defeat against Real Madrid in the Clasico, the Brazilian has 13 goals in 9 matches this season.

Regular, the Greek Vangelis Pavlidis maintains the rhythm. Against Santa Clara (2-1), he offered himself a new achievement bringing his record to 9 goals in 9 matches. It’s a little better than Victor Osimhen who comes fifth. The Nigerian scored a double yesterday with Galatasaray against Fenerbahçe and has 8 goals in 7 matches. Similar statistics for Niclas Füllkrug who remains on a double with Werder Bremen against Mainz (3-2).

Seventh, Luis Suárez keeps the pace with 8 goals in 9 matches. The Colombian scored on Sunday despite a 2-1 defeat against Viseu. Gift Orban comes eighth and offered himself a new achievement with Amedspor against Samsunspor (2-1). Ninth, Mohamed Salah maintains the tempo with a goal last Monday against Beşiktaş (2-0). He did not score yesterday against Rizespor (4-3). Finally, Yassir Zabiri comes tenth with 8 goals in 10 matches. The Moroccan once again made a splash with Racing Santander against Espanyol Barcelona (2-1).

Lamine Yamal (FC Barcelona), Søren Tengstedt (Go Ahead Eagles) and Brynjolfur Willumsson (Groningen) failed to reach the top 10 with also 8 goals. Estéban Lepaul (Rennes), Nicolò Tresoldi (FC Bruges), Norman Bassette (Westerlo), Antonio Raimondo (Frosinone), Gustavo Varela (Monza), Sergio Camello (Rayo Vallecano), Ante Budimir (Osasuna), Vedat Muriqi (Fenerbahçe) and Eldor Shomurodov (Başakşehir) follow with 7 goals.
