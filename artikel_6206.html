American Airlines Launches 23 New Routes From 3 Busiest Hubs: See Full List Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

While it might not be surprising, American Airlines' top three hubs for flights are Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport (DFW), Charlotte Douglas International Airport (CLT), and Chicago O'Hare International Airport (ORD). In 2026, Cirium Diio data shows that the trio have an average of 1,949 daily departures (double for total movements).

They're so critical that they account for 60% of the oneworld member's total activity. Daily departures from the three hubs vary dramatically, from a collective low of 799 on Thanksgiving to a high of 2,121 on November 29.

Schedule analysis shows that American has added three routes from DFW. This reflects what is known as of November 10 and certainly could change. When writing, no markets are down to launch in 2027, but that'll inevitably change.

Only routes that are still due to run in 2027 are included. This rules out DFW to Zurich, which returned in May 2026 but was pulled. The carrier has also ended various other routes from DFW in 2026, such as Albany (September 2026).

American had flown from DFW to three destinations in Virginia (Norfolk, Richmond, and Washington Dulles) for years. The launch of flights to Roanoke has brought that number to four for the first time. Nebraska is in a similar position. While Omaha has been linked for decades, service from DFW to Grand Island materialized in 2011. Since then, only those two cities have been part of DFW's network. But now there's a record of three, with Lincoln, the capital, also having flights.

In 2026, Cirium shows that American has an average of 617 daily departures from CLT (double for total movements). However, that's lower than what was available in 2024 (634) and 2025 (652). And excluding the COVID-19 pandemic years, it is the lowest number of services since the North Carolina hub was inherited from US Airways.

The much lower number of departures is despite the introduction of the following routes that took off this year. As the only destinations to have been removed in the same timeframe were Vail and the unpopular Mexican resort gateway of Tulum, most of the cuts are from lower frequencies in already-served markets.

After an eight-year absence, American will reintroduce flights from CLT to Barcelona, which is Spain's second most populous city, a major tourist draw, and the location for Mediterranean cruises. However, while ex-US Airways Airbus A330s were previously used, it'll be the first time that the Triple Seven has been deployed. American will have nine routes from CLT to Europe next summer, with up to 11 daily flights.

Perhaps the biggest development for American is to Florida, with the Sunshine State accounting for an average of 83 daily flights from CLT in 2026. The launch of service to Naples and Vero Beach brings the network to 18 airports across Florida. In the 12 months to July 2026, US Department of Transportation data shows that American transported 6.9 million passengers between CLT and Florida, of which 76% connected to another flight in CLT.

Despite this and the FAA-mandated flight restrictions which are ostensibly designed to help with delays, American has launched many new and returning routes from its third-busiest hub. However, the carrier ceased flying to San José, Costa Rica, in April 2026. Not included below is the time-limited operation to San José, California, which only existed in February 2026.

Unlike DFW and CLT, all but two of American's additions from ORD have previously been part of its network. The sole exceptions are Staunton (for the Shenandoah Valley) and Lewisburg, both of which are now also served from CLT. Both cities are brand-new to American's map. As you might expect, this is because flights are being funded by the Essential Air Service. For example, SkyWest (for American Eagle) is getting around $25 million over four years to serve Lewisburg.
