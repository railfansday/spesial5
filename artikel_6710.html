Get Moving: The 2026 Vogue .Gif Guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The spirit of gift-giving is not a static, frozen one. No, it’s one filled with dynamism, energy, verve, and excitement—much like the holiday season itself. It’s bristling with unbridled feelings of joy and goodwill, almost jittery with anticipation. It’s practically bouncing off the walls like, well, a child on Christmas morning creeping down the stairs to gaze lovingly at the rich landscape of gifts spilling forth from the glittering tree.

And so this year we sought out gifts that matched the moment. Gifts that would swing, dangle, bounce, roll, rock, and more. To capture them in still lives was not to honor their form. And so, we decided to make not a gift guide, but a .gif guide, to imbue these wonderful baubles and delights with the vivacious momentum they so deserve. So take a gander at our favorite gifts of the season, now vibrating and twitching with life.

And with that, we present you with the Vogue 2026 .Gif Guide.

With its hinged cover, this compact swings open to reveal its hydrating and shimmering lip gloss. The perfect little reveal when you and your lips are feeling a bit dull.

Thanks to its structured top, metallic-frame closure, dainty handle, and soft lambskin body, this darling handbag is demure and alluring, but its squishy body gives it an unexpected dimension worthy of a hug.

Chanel’s fabulous shoes have become an instant best-seller, thanks to their fresh prints and whimsical use of color. Just imagine traipsing around town in these animal-print heels with their pop-of-red toes. You’ll get double takes for all the right reasons.

The games we play! From dominoes and chess to a spooky turn with some fabulously illustrated tarot cards, a game is the perfect gift for someone with a competitive streak.

Fitness nuts with a love of aesthetics, rejoice! Zara Home has a surprisingly stylish ab roller to help you tighten your core. Longines, meanwhile, sells an elegant and accurate timekeeper to ensure you’re not spending too much time working up a sweat.

Who doesn’t love to start the day with a warm cup of joe? This charming espresso maker to add a bit of Italian flair to your morning routine. Bonus points for urbane cups from which to drink.

Despite advancements in technology, sometimes the most enduring childhood toys are the classics. What youngin’ wouldn’t get a thrill on this old-fashioned wooden rocking horse? Perfect for a (pretend) day on the bridle paths.

The ongoing obsession with whimsical charms is only growing. This year, they’ve gotten daintier and more fanciful, many with their own moving components (think lockets and boxes that open, or animal figurines that wiggle and quake). Their, well, charms, remain ever enticing.
