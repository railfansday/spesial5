Lady Gaga Hits a Yoga Class in Slingback Heels

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

You will rarely catch her in a pair of flats

These days, many seem to be steering away from high heels and challenging the Carrie Bradshaw mindset, opting instead for comfier silhouettes, like ballet flats or opera shoes. Even heels themselves are getting more sensible: think kitten heels rather than teetering stilettos. But Lady Gaga rarely swims with the current.

Since the dawn of her career, the Grammy-winning pop star has worn shoes that seem to defy the laws of physics. She hits the streets in heel-less Nortek Tatehana pointe shoes and sky-high leather Pleasers. Her “Bad Romance” music video helped solidify Alexander McQueen’s scythe-shaped Armadillo shoes as an accessory icon. Sometimes, her heels are even covered in meat. So, it should come as no surprise that, in Gaga’s world, even a workout class calls for a fresh pair of heels.

On Tuesday afternoon, the “Abracadabra” singer was seen heading into a yoga class in Mill Valley, California, while in an easy all-black fit composed of your standard workout fare: a baggy T-shirt and joggers paired with a black baseball cap, plus a scrunchie on her wrist to help pull back her silvery hair. But in true Gaga fashion, she traded workout shoes for a pair of pointy black slingbacks, which gave the casual athleisure look a chic, witchy effect.

This is one of the first times that Gaga has been spotted in public since she and her fiancé, Michael Polansky, welcomed their first child together in June. And while Gaga’s life (and sleep schedule) has surely changed quite dramatically in these past few months, clearly her fun approach to style has not.
