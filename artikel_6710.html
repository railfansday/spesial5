The Best Stocking Stuffers, According to GQ Editors

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The best stocking stuffers are small gifts that feel like more than an afterthought. Not novelty socks for the back of a drawer, nor the sort of dreary practicalities anyone could pick up at a drugstore, but genuinely good things that happen to come in small packages. You know, like handsome accessories, clever gadgets, excellent things to eat, and the occasional charming oddity. We've rounded up a few ideas in that vein for the dads, boyfriends, brothers, and assorted other gentlemen on your list this year.

Your gift list giving you grief? Peruse our gift guides and sign up for the GQ Recommends newsletter for more excellent things worth giving (or keeping for yourself).

A perfectly sized Boat and Tote, provided you're transporting one almond.

A flask is already a pretty good stocking stuffer. Slap the Yeti name on it and it might just outshine the gifts under the tree.

A remote control for turning pages on your Kindle. Why did it take Amazon so long to think of this?

To the delight of anyone who wants their place to smell good, Le Labo brought its cult-favorite Santal 26 scent to incense this year.

Just a deck of playing cards for the flyest person you know.

18K gold-plated clovers for good fortune, silver-plated beads for good looks. Some people have all the luck. That luck being you shopping for them.

For smelling like a million bucks on the go.

This collaboration between Caran d'Ache and Paul Smith brings a dose of color to the Swiss penmaker's iconic hexagonal ballpoint.

Get the big tin, give a few bags away, and keep the rest for yourself. Everybody wins.
