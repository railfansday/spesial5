King Charles Had the Most Unexpected Response to Robert Pattinson Being Announced as James Bond

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The royals have long been fans of the franchise.

King Charles and Queen Camilla had the best response to the long-awaited news that Twilight and Batman legend Robert Pattinson is set to become the new James Bond.

The royal family has long been associated with the iconic British spy series, with the King and Queen—along with Prince William and Catherine, Princess of Wales—attending the No Time to Die premiere at London’s Royal Albert Hall in September 2021 (the last film that was released in the long-running franchise).

In response to the official James Bond X account sharing the news about Pattinson, the royal family's account (which acts as a news source for the King and Queen), re-shared the post along with the comment: “Welcome to His Majesty’s Secret Service, Mr Bond.”

So far, the quip has been liked over 250,000 times.

Prior to the 2021 premiere, the King had attended plenty of other screenings of the Bond series—dating as far back as 1981 for the launch of For Your Eyes Only, which had Roger Moore starring as the fictional MI6 agent.

The late Princess Diana and Princess Margaret also turned out for that particular premiere.

Commenting on his major career leap, Robert Pattinson said in a statement, “James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime.”

He continued, “I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya, and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”

Filming is expected to begin early next year, with an unspecified release date slated for sometime in 2028.

It will be the first James Bond movie produced by Amazon MGM, after the Broccoli family unexpectedly sold the rights in 2025.
