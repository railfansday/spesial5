GOP senator shares tragic update about husband's death

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Sen. Deb Fischer, R-Neb., being sworn into office by then-Vice President Mike Pence alongside her husband, Bruce Fischer, who died on Sunday. (Sen. Deb Fischer; Facebook)

This story discusses suicide. If you or someone you know is having thoughts of suicide, please contact the National Suicide Prevention Lifeline at 988 or 1-800-273-TALK (8255).

Bruce Fischer, the husband of Sen. Deb Fischer, R-Neb., died by suicide on Sunday, the senator's office said Friday.

"There has been a sad update in the death of Senator Fischer’s husband, Bruce," a spokesperson for Fischer's office said in a statement posted to X. "Initially, it was believed that Bruce passed peacefully at home. Upon further investigation, it was learned that Bruce chose to end his life."

"The family is deeply grateful for the recent outpouring of sympathy for their loss and asks for understanding in this time of mourning," the statement continued.

The spokesperson added that there will be no further comment on the matter, "and no other details will be provided."

The update comes after Fischer announced on Monday that her husband had died the past weekend.

"It is with a heavy heart that I share the passing of my beloved husband, Bruce," Fischer said in a statement posted on social media at the time. "Bruce was my partner in life, my greatest source of strength, and the heart of our family. His kindness, integrity, and quiet strength left a lasting impression on all who knew him."

"On November 22, Bruce passed away peacefully while doing what he loved most, spending time outdoors," the statement continued.

Fischer and her husband met as students at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, the Lincoln Journal Star reported. The couple married in 1972 and had three sons.

Republican Nebraska Gov. Jim Pillen on Monday extended condolences to Fischer and her family.

Sen. Deb Fischer, R-Neb., second from left, stands alongside her husband Bruce, third from left, and then-Vice President Kamala Harris. (Sen. Deb Fischer; Facebook)

"Suzanne and I are keeping Sen. Fischer and her family in our thoughts," Pillen wrote on X. "We share our prayers as she deals with this great personal loss."

Sen. Pete Ricketts, R-Neb., said Monday in a post on X that he felt "very sad" to hear about Fischer's passing.

"Bruce left his mark on this world through his love for family, his faith, and his ranch," Ricketts added. "Susanne and I send our deepest condolences to Deb, the entire Fischer family, and all their loved ones during this difficult time. The Fischer family is in our prayers."
