Bills have a clearer path to the one seed after Broncos loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Last week, we told you that the Bills only needed to win their remaining AFC games and they would clinch the one seed in the AFC. They could lose both games to the NFC opponents because of tiebreakers.

Now, with the Denver Broncos and Kansas City Chiefs both losing this week, the Bills have a new path to the one seed.

If the Bills win their three AFC East games against the Patriots, Jets, and Dolphins, they would either need to beat the Broncos on Christmas Day or both the Packers AND Bears in mid-December. Denver can top out at 14 wins, but those three AFC East wins gets Buffalo to 13 wins and beating both the Bears and Packers gets Buffalo to 15 wins.

Denver has a pretty easy schedule, with only one game remaining against a team with a winning record. It's the Bills on Christmas in Mile High.
