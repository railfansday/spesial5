Las Vegas Raiders vs. Cleveland Browns Week 12 TV Map

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Week 12 game between the Cleveland Browns and the Las Vegas Raiders will air on NBC at 8:15 PM ET on Sunday.

Ohio Coverage: All of Ohio will get to see the Raiders vs. Browns game. Locally, the game will air on WKYC (Channel 3.1).

National Coverage: Since it was flexed into Sunday Night Football, this will be the Browns' second nationally-televised game this season (but the first one on public television).
