‘Best win of the season’ – LeBron James couldn’t hide his feelings after 76ers win over Magic

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Philadelphia 76ers star LeBron James kept his thoughts simple after the team's 121-116 win over the Orlando Magic on November 18.

LeBron is excelling in his role as a complementary option on the 76ers, built to maximise the impact of scorers like Tyrese Maxey, Jaylen Brown and Joel Embiid.

He dropped 22 points with six assists in the 76ers' win over the Magic, putting up another strong performance in his 24th NBA season to continue defying his age.

LeBron gave an interview from the locker room after the 76ers win over the Magic, bluntly calling the performance one of Philadelphia's best wins of the season, if not the best.

"This is one of our better wins, if not the best win of the season so far."

The 76ers played this game without their leading scorer, as Tyrese Maxey missed the clash with injury. Despite a bigger load for James and the other 76ers stars, they handled it admirably.

Joel Embiid led the 76ers in scoring with 26 points, six rebounds and seven assists, while VJ Edgecombe contributed 24 points, eight rebounds and six assists. Anfernee Simons scored 18 points in his first start of the season, in a great team-oriented performance by the 76ers.

The Magic are missing Paolo Banchero, but enjoyed really productive nights from their entire starting five, including Anthony Black, Jalen Suggs, Desmond Bane, Franz Wagner and Wendell Carter Jr., with the lowest-scoring starters managing 15 points (Bane, Carter, Black).

The 76ers had to overcome a 13-point first-quarter deficit after a slow start, but they managed to respond instantly to cut it to five points by half, ultimately out-executing the Magic in the fourth quarter to seal the win.

The 76ers have the best record in the Eastern Conference with 13-3, while James is averaging 15.7 points, 6.8 rebounds and 6.8 assists this season.
