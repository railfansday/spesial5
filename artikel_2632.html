NBA Cup 2026: Standings, schedule, stakes and clinching scenarios for final week of group play

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The final week of NBA Cup group play is here, with games on Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday. Only five teams have been eliminated so far, with 25 teams still battling for the eight spots in the knockout stage, which will take place Dec. 4-11.

Who will advance? Here's a look at the current standings, remaining schedule and stakes in each group.

Note: Ties will be broken by (in sequential order): head-to-head record in group play; point differential; total points scored; 2025-26 regular-season record; and, if necessary, a random drawing.

Magic, Raptors, Pistons can still win group

Clinching scenarios for Tuesday and Wednesday

Magic clinch group with a win, and clinch home-court advantage in quarterfinals with a win + Celtics or Heat loss

Pistons eliminated with: a loss + Wizards win; or a loss + Bucks win; or a loss + Heat and Cavs wins

Nets eliminated with: a Wizards win; or a Nets loss, or Heat and Cavs wins

Heat, Cavs, 76ers, Pacers, Knicks are all still alive

Clinching scenarios for Tuesday and Wednesday

Cavs eliminated with loss + Wizards and Heat wins; or various point-differential scenarios

76ers eliminated with loss + Wizards or Cavs win

Pacers eliminated with Wizards and Heat wins; or Wizards and Cavs wins; or Heat and Cavs wins

Knicks eliminated with a loss or Wizards and Heat wins

Wednesday: Miami at Philadelphia, 5 p.m. (ESPN)

Wednesday: Cleveland at New York, 7:30 p.m.

Friday: Miami at New York, 7 p.m. (Prime)

Friday: Philadelphia at Indiana, 7:30 p.m.

Celtics, Hawks, Wizards can still win group

Clinching scenarios for Tuesday and Wednesday

Wizards eliminated with: a loss + Hornets win; or a loss + Bulls tiebreaker win; or a loss + Heat and Cavs wins or Knicks win by seven or fewer points; or a loss by 18 or more points + Pistons and Nets wins

Bulls eliminated with: a loss; or a Wizards win; or tiebreaker loss to Wizards; or Heat and Cavs wins; or Pistons and Nets wins + a win by 13 or fewer points

Tuesday: Boston at Washington, 8 p.m. (NBC)

Mavericks, Nuggets, Suns can still win group

Clinching scenarios for Tuesday and Wednesday

Mavericks clinch group with a win and Suns loss

Suns are eliminated with: a loss + Mavs win; or a loss + Thunder and Grizzlies wins; or a loss + Warriors and Blazers wins

Rockets are eliminated with: a loss; or a Mavs win; or a win by four or fewer points; or Thunder and Grizzlies wins; or Warriors and Blazers wins

Timberwolves, Thunder, Grizzlies, Clippers still alive

Clinching scenarios for Tuesday and Wednesday

Timberwolves clinch group with a win + Grizzlies loss

Thunder clinch group with a win + Clippers loss

Thunder are eliminated with: a loss + Clippers win; or a loss + Nuggets and Suns wins; or a loss + Warriors and Blazers wins

Grizzlies are eliminated with: a loss + Wolves win; or a loss + Nuggets and Suns wins; or a loss + Warriors and Blazers wins

Clippers are eliminated with: a loss + Thunder win; or a loss + Nuggets and Suns wins; or a loss + Warriors and Blazers wins

Wednesday: Minnesota at Oklahoma City, 7:30 p.m. (ESPN)

Friday: New Orleans at LA Clippers, 9:30 p.m.

Spurs, Lakers, Blazers, Warriors still alive

Clinching scenarios for Tuesday and Wednesday

Blazers are eliminated with: a loss + Kings win; or a loss + Nuggets and Suns wins; or a loss + Thunder and Grizzlies wins

Warriors are eliminated with: a loss + Nuggets and Suns wins; or a loss + Thunder and Grizzlies wins

Wednesday: San Antonio at Portland, 10 p.m. (ESPN)

Wednesday: Sacramento at Golden State, 10 p.m.

Friday: LA Lakers at San Antonio, 9:30 p.m. (Prime)

Friday: Golden State at Portland, 9:30 p.m.

The eight teams that advance from group play will take part in a single-elimination tournament. The NBA Cup quarterfinals will be held on Dec. 4 and 5, with the semifinals on Dec. 8 and/or 9, with the higher-seeded teams hosting matchups in their home arenas. The top two teams will advance to the NBA Cup Final on Dec. 11.

Players of the teams that participate in the knockout stage will earn prize money.

(During the knockout stage, the 22 teams that didn't qualify will each play two to-be-determined, regular-season games.)

The NBA Cup is leaving Las Vegas, where the league has held the semifinals and championship game for the past three years. This year, the final will be held at Butler's Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis.
