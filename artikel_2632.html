Patriots vs. Lions Week 10 inactives: Who's in, who's out?

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The New England Patriots had a few key injury updates this past week, getting their star receiver back as well as some others. And with that, it means good things for the offense in Sunday's game against the Detroit Lions.

Before the trip to Germany, the Patriots ruled out G/C Ben Brown (knee), CB Carlton Davis III (knee), and RB TreVeyon Henderson (illness). Then, on Saturday, Henderson was placed on injured reserve.

The team also listed nine players as questionable: WR A.J. Brown (ankle/knee), S Kevin Byard III (chest), FB Reggie Gilliam (knee), TE Cameron Latu (back), OL Walter Rouse (concussion protocol), S Brenden Schooler (hamstring), RB Rhamondre Stevenson (knee), OL Greg Van Roten (hamstring), and CB Charles Woods (hamstring).

As for TE Hunter Henry (foot), QB Drake Maye (right shoulder), and T Morgan Moses (foot), they were given no game status, indicating they are good to go.

Van Roten and Gilliam were both activated to the 53-man roster on Saturday as well. And the team elevated G Andrew Rupcich from the practice squad.
