Walker Kessler thankful for big stage with Lakers after beating former team

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Walker Kessler has a lot to be thankful for as the Los Angeles Lakers earned a 109-94 victory over the Utah Jazz at Crypto.com Arena on Monday, Nov. 23.

The Lakers clinched the season series against the center's former team, giving Los Angeles some momentum ahead of Thanksgiving and a showdown against the San Antonio Spurs on Friday, Nov. 27.

While the Lakers may only go as far as Luka Doncic and Austin Reaves can take them, Kessler is viewed as the third piece to a potential championship puzzle.

Los Angeles invested a lot to acquire Kessler before signing him four-year, $130 million deal during the offseason. In order to secure the big man, the Lakers sent unprotected first-round picks in 2031 and 2033 and first-round swaps in 2028 and 2030 to the Jazz.

All eyes will be on Kessler as he will be tasked with defending Victor Wembanyama of the Spurs on Friday.

The center finished with 10 points and 12 rebounds against Utah for his 10th double-double of the season on Monday.

Kessler had impressed Los Angeles in the season opener with a 26-point performance, having made all 12 of his shots against the Golden State Warriors. He had another high-efficiency night against the Atlanta Hawks, posting 21 points on 8-of-12 shooting and making five of his six free throw attempts.

"I'm thankful for this opportunity to play on such a big stage," Kessler said during his postgame press conference on Monday. "Sometimes you get so tied down in your individual performance and stats and all this stuff, but to be a professional basketball player like as a kid, you look at that, it's like, oh my gosh. That's the dream."

Kessler will be on the big stage later this week with a formidable opponent in Wembanyama.

Wembanyama and the Spurs are the standard in the conference, having won the 2025-26 conference finals and making an appearance in the NBA Finals.

Wembanyama is one of the league's top young stars while Kessler is looking to have a breakout season on a winning team.

Kessler did not play a lot of meaningful basketball during his first four seasons in the NBA, as the Jazz never finished with a record above .500.

Kessler and Wembanyama have an even 3-3 record when their respective teams have played against one another. Considering the differences in the roles those players may have had on those respective teams over the years, it's not surprising that Wembanyama has averaged more points, rebounds, blocks and minutes per game.

Kessler will have to find a balance during the game, having admitted that his emotions have factored into his play at least once this season.

Kessler had a strong start to the season, but an early matchup in Utah led to a two-point performance in a 132-123 victory for the Lakers on Oct. 25.

"I think going into the game I let my emotions get the best of me," Kessler said at the time. "But you have to learn in those moments and move on. I'm just glad we got the win tonight … even though it wasn't my best night, I really appreciate (my teammates)."

Kessler has averaged 14.3 points and 10.7 rebounds per game in 16 games played this season.
