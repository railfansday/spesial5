Bears rule out Kyler Gordon, Roschon Johnson

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Bears released their final injury report for Thursday’s game in Detroit.

Cornerback Kyler Gordon (shoulder) has been ruled out for the second straight game. Gordon appeared in four games after coming off the physically unable to perform list in October and had four sacks in those appearances.

Running back Roschon Johnson (ankle) is going to miss the game as well. The Bears elevated Salvon Ahmed from the practice squad to fill out the backfield.

Johnson was injured in last Sunday’s win over the Saints. Safety Elijah Hicks suffered a concussion in that game and won’t play against the Lions. Linebacker D’Marco Jackson (elbow) and cornerback Tyrique Stevenson (groin) are listed as questionable.

With Gordon and Hicks out and Stevenson uncertain to play, the Bears have also elevated defensive back Elijah Jones.
