Fenerbahce will hope the visit of Liverpool can spark a disappointing season into life, with the Istanbul giants sixth in the Turkish Super Lig and still reeling from back-to-back 2-1 defeats against arch-rivals Galatasaray and mid-table Goztepe.

Manager Ismail Kartal resigned in the wake of the loss to Galatasaray, which saw Dirk Kuyt step in as interim coach. The former Netherlands striker became a fan favourite at Anfield during a six-year spell between 2006 and 2012, scoring Liverpool's goal in the 2007 Champions League final defeat by AC Milan.

He is one of several ex-Premier League players in the Fenerbahce ranks, along with former Manchester City goalkeeper Ederson, ex-Leicester City and Chelsea midfielder N'Golo Kante and former Chelsea, Manchester United and Everton striker Romelu Lukaku.

Nathan Ake, another man with Premier League experience, is sidelined through injury, as is fellow centre back Kojo Oppong. It leaves Fenerbahce short in defence as they look to improve on a record of two points from their opening three league phase matches, which included a 6-0 defeat by Aston Villa.

Liverpool are in a much healthier position in the league phase table, with wins over Atletico Madrid, LASK and Villarreal putting them in a great position to finish in the top eight.

The Reds had appeared to build up a head of steam after winning four successive matches in all competitions but that run was halted by champions Arsenal, who claimed a 1-0 victory at Anfield on Sunday.

Nevertheless, Andoni Iraola will feel his side have more than enough quality to return to winning ways, especially after midfielder Dominik Szoboszlai and winger Victor Munoz returned from injury against the Gunners.

Of course, Istanbul is a special place for Liverpool after their extraordinary comeback against Milan in the 2005 Champions League final. They will expect to return from their latest trip to the Turkish capital with more happy memories.

Fenerbahçe XI: Ederson, Semedo, Skriniar, Oppong, Brown, İsmail, Guendouzi, Kante, İrfan Can, Greenwood, Lukaku.

Liverpool XI: Alisson, Gomez, Jacquet, Van Dijk, Tsimikas, Mac Allister, Nyoni, Munoz, Szoboszlai, Ngumoha, Barcola.

The big news coming from Liverpool's camp is that Ryan Gravenberch has not travelled following a heel injury.

Federico Chiesa and Wataru Endo are also unavailable, having not been included in the Reds' 'A-Squad' for the UCL.

Fenerbahçe, meanwhile, will be without Nathan Aké and Marco Asensio, who are both injured. Kojo Peprah Oppong is also unlikely to make the matchday squad.

The Turkish giants are in poor form and the Reds should be able to capitalise.
