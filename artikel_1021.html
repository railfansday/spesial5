Supreme hero Old Park Star makes chasing debut at Ffos Las

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Having finished as winner and runner-up in last season's Supreme Novices' Hurdle, Old Park Star and Sober Glory make there first foray into Chase action when they go to post at Ffos Las today - live on Sky Sports Racing

All eyes will be on Ffos Las today as Old Park Star jumps a fence in public for the first time against his Supreme rival Sober Glory.

Supreme Novices' Hurdle winner Old Park Star faces Sober Glory as he makes his eagerly-anticipated debut over fences in this Golwg Gwendraeth Beginners' Chase at Ffos Las.

Nicky Henderson's star novice was unbeaten in four starts over hurdles last season, and all eyes will be on this exciting prospect as he embarks on this new discipline. Favourite for the Arkle already, he could put down a marker for the campaign ahead under regular rider Nico de Boinville.

Supreme second Sober Glory is a huge danger for the Hobbs and White team. He has long been viewed as a fencing type, and it will be fascinating to see how to takes to it.

Alan King's Baron Noir, fourth at Cheltenham and ahead of Sober Glory at Aintree, cannot be ruled out, while Carlenrig and Bobby's Nelson complete the quintet.

The Ben Pauling-trained five-year-old claimed fourth in the Grade 2 bumper at Aintree and Ben Jones has recently put him up as his horse to follow on the At The Races website.

In opposition, Dan Skelton sends Big John Ollie up in trip after he was comprehensively beaten twice over two miles. Another stepping up in distance is Valiant Soldier, who boasts the services of Paddy Power Gold Cup-winning rider Harry Cobden.

A cracking clash at Hexham sees Julius Des Pictons and Derryhassen Paddy lock horns in this Weatherbys And Birdie Calendars 'Chasing Excellence' Beginners' Chase at 1.03pm.

Julius Des Pictons took Grade 1 glory when last seen in the Sefton Novices' Hurdle at Aintree 593 days ago and must be watched as he tackles fences for the first time.

Derryhassen Paddy skipped Cheltenham at the weekend and looks the obvious threat having shaped with promise on two fencing starts to date.

Fishery Lane and Pinot Rouge complete the line-up for Dan Skelton and Jessica Bedi respectively.

At Ffos Las, the card gets underway with an intriguing Dandara Maiden Hurdle at 12.48pm. No Walkover was a useful operator in the bumper sphere and could progress further tackling obstacles for the first time. The second division of the race at 1.18pm features Diamond Street, who is sure to be popular for the on-song Skelton operation.
