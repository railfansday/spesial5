Horschel faces setback over PGA Tour future as Svensson grabs lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Sweden's Jesper Svensson holds a one-shot lead after the opening round of the RSM Classic, the final PGA Tour event of the season; Brooks Koepka is five strokes back and Billy Horschel is seven behind; watch the second round live on Friday from 1.30pm on Sky Sports Golf

Jesper Svensson produced a late burst to grab the early lead at the RSM Classic, as Billy Horschel made a slow start to his bid to retain full PGA Tour playing rights.

Only the top 100 in the FedExCup standings after this week's event in St Simons Island remain fully exempt on the PGA Tour, with Horschel heading into the week 101st and projected to fall further down the rankings after opening with a one-under 71.

Horschel - an eight-time winner on the PGA Tour - carded three birdies and two bogeys on the Plantation Course, one of two used at Sea Island Golf Club, to leave him seven strokes behind Svensson.

Svensson birdied eight of his last 11 holes on the same course as Horschel - including each of his last four - to post an eight-under 64 and move into a one-shot lead, with Max Greyserman and Garrick Higgo sharing second after opening 65s.

Michael Thorbjornsen posted the lowest round on the Seaside Course, with a six-under 64 leaving him tied-fourth alongside Emiliano Grillo, Hank Lebioda, Maverick McNealy, Lee Hodges and Davis Riley.

Ramsus Hojgaard and Marco Penge, entering the week outside the top 100 in the season-long standings, sit in the group on four under alongside Ryder Cup player Sepp Straka - who finished inside top 50 during the regular season.

Koepka described his season as a "disappointment" ahead of this week's event, with the five-time major champion opening with a three-under 67, while England's John Parry - entering the week 96th in the ranking - started with a two-under 70.

All players ranked No 51 and below carry their FedExCup points over from the regular season and first play-off event into the 'FedExCup Fall', giving them the chance to boost their status for next season.

Those 51st to 60th after this week qualify for the first two Signature Events of 2027, while those who end between 101st and 125th on the PGA Tour only receive conditional status and be unable to feature in certain events.

Players outside the top 125 face a season on the Korn Ferry Tour or a trip to Q-School to earn their PGA Tour playing rights, with first-round leader Svensson entering the week in 135th spot.
