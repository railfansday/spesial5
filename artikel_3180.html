OpenAI fixes a GPT-6.2 billing mistake, and users hit limits 40% faster

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Fixing an OpenAI billing mistake means GPT-6.2 Astra users hit their limits 40% faster, and Sol users 25% faster. OpenAI is also merging Chat and Work, and has hired another Claude Code engineer.

OpenAI says it made a billing mistake when it launched its new models, GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol. Its fix means paying ChatGPT users will hit their usage limits faster. Compared with last week, the same work will reach the limit 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol. OpenAI executive Thibault Sottiaux announced the fix on X on Thursday, as Business Insider reported.

“We made a billing mistake at launch of GPT-6.2 that incorrectly discounted GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol usage for most users. We’ve fixed this now, so the same work will use more of your plan’s usage allowance,” Sottiaux wrote.

OpenAI will not charge users for the difference on past usage, Sottiaux said. Both models launched on 17 November.

“As a small token to apologize for the mistake, we’ll be adding one banked reset for everyone on Go, Plus, Pro, and Business,” he wrote.

Other models are not affected, Sottiaux said, according to Neowin. Users should still get more out of GPT-6.2 than out of GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol, he added. OpenAI’s pricing pages did not change, the site added.

Reddit users reacted with scepticism, TechRadar reported. One user called a single banked reset “pretty thin” as an apology. At launch, OpenAI had said both models would let users get more done within their usage limits.

Usage costs are a problem across the industry. Musk has said Grok Bot’s costs were unsustainably high relative to revenue, Business Insider noted.

The fix lands a day after OpenAI began merging ChatGPT’s Chat and Work tabs for some Pro users. Under the new system, both will draw on a single usage allowance, an OpenAI spokesperson told TechCrunch.

Most users should not hit their limits more often, the spokesperson said. A small share of the heaviest users may. Chats with the Instant model stay unlimited and do not count towards the shared allowance. OpenAI plans to roll out shared limits more broadly in December, according to its support page.

OpenAI also gained a new hire from Anthropic on Wednesday. Thariq Shihipar, who left Anthropic in October, said he has joined OpenAI. He named Sottiaux and Cat Wu, Claude Code’s former product lead, among his new colleagues.

I’m very proud of my time at Anthropic, and I’ll continue to root for my former colleagues. Both companies have important roles to play in the future.

There is a tremendous amount of work ahead of us to bring AI to the world, and it will demand the very best of all of us. I felt that I could personally contribute the most to this effort at OpenAI.

I believe this is one of the great strengths of Silicon Valley, the flow of ideas and people.

I’m excited to join a team I’ve long admired, building incredible AI products for over a billion people, and to work alongside friends like @_catwu, @thsottiaux, @ajambrosino, @sharifshameem, @jxnlco and many others to push the frontier forward.

More than anything, I hope to make your experience of using AI just a little bit better.

OpenAI’s Andrew Ambrosino welcomed him on X, writing that it is now “time to reinvent Codex”.
