‘Beast Games’ Sets Season 3 Release Date At Prime Video

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

We have a date for Season 3 of Beast Games. Prime Video has set Wednesday, December 16 for the premiere of Beast Games: World Champions. It will launch with three episodes, followed by two weekly episodes leading up to the season finale on Wednesday, January 13.

We’re also getting a look at the new season, hosted by MrBeast himself, in a new teaser. The first look teases contestants representing 196 nations around the world, a peek at the global stage including the Roman Colosseum, a stadium filled with tens of thousands of live audience members, and new challenges to win the $5,000,000 prize. It all was announced Sunday at TwitchCon in San Diego, CA, at the “Prime Video Presents Beast Games Live!” session. Watch on Deadline

In Season Three of Beast Games: World Champions, MrBeast unites competitors from every corner of the planet, each representing their nation in the battle for $5,000,000. Challenges of strength, skill, and strategy push every player to their limit, while bribes, betrayals, and twists reveal just how far they’ll go for a chance at life-changing money. Alliances cross borders. Rivalries divide continents. Only one will emerge victorious. Who will take home the glory for their nation and be crowned the Beast Games world champion?

Donaldson returns as host and will executive produce. Tyler Conklin serves as co-creator and executive producer with Donaldson and will return as series director. Michael H. Miller, Rob Buchta, Chris Keiper, Josh Kulic, Michael Cruz, and Jeff Housenbold will executive produce. Beast Games will premiere exclusively on Prime Video in more than 240 countries and territories worldwide.
