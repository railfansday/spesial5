GTA 6 Developer Thanks Rockstar for Immortalizing His Late Daughter in the Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

One of the names you can find on a memorial wall in GTA 6 belongs to the daughter of a Rockstar Games developer. Bradley Stone, a lead game tools programmer at Rockstar, has shared the story behind the inscription and thanked the studio for including it in the game. He and his wife lost their daughter Evelyn in 2023, while GTA 6 was still in development.

Stone shared a video of his visit to the memorial on X on November 27. The video shows Evelyn Stone's name with the date April 8, 2023.

As Dexerto reports, Stone and his wife had experienced several miscarriages before a scan in 2023 revealed that their unborn daughter had a severe case of spina bifida. After further examinations, the couple learned that she wouldn't survive and had to undergo a medical termination five months into the pregnancy.

Stone recalled how difficult it was to leave the hospital without their daughter. He also spoke of his wife's courage throughout the ordeal, which involved nearly a full day of labor. The couple has since welcomed another daughter after going through more difficulties in the years that followed.

Stone thanked Rockstar for giving his family somewhere to remember Evelyn and for supporting him through his grief. He also said the memorial helped “to show this game was made by real people.” He ended his post with a message to his daughter.

“You will forever be in our hearts,” he wrote.

Rockstar hadn't even released the first GTA 6 trailer when Stone and his wife lost Evelyn. That came eight months later, in December 2023. For those of us following the game, the years since then have been spent poring over trailers and wondering when we'd finally get to play it. Reading Stone's account puts that long wait into perspective.

If you'd like to visit the memorial yourself, you'll find it at Deep Roots Memorial Sanctuary, just north of Hamlet. You can also download Rockstar's official Leonida map to help get your bearings if you're unfamiliar with that part of the state. Stone isn't the first Rockstar employee to have shared the significance of one of the inscriptions there since the game came out.

As GAMES.GG reported earlier this week, Rockstar QA developer Matt Green's mother, Beverly, is also commemorated at the sanctuary. He recalled begging her for a copy of GTA Vice City as a kid. She was reluctant at first but eventually agreed, and Green credits that purchase with sparking the interest that led him to a career at Rockstar.

She was proud of his work on GTA 6, but passed away in March this year, before he could show her the finished game. Production designer Terrance Radcliff also lost two family members during development, his father Elhuntry and his first daughter Jade. Their names appear beside one another on the wall. Radcliff shared the tribute on November 22, as the launch week came to a close, explaining how much it meant to him to have both of them commemorated in the game.

What's particularly nice about the sanctuary is that it exists as an actual place within the world rather than a dedication that most of us would miss in the end credits. It's a place that the developers and their families can return to long after they've finished working on the game.
