MU: Michael Carrick responds cash to dismissal rumors

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Despite a sporting crisis which continues to worsen at Manchester United, Michael Carrick does not seem particularly worried about his future. After the heavy defeat conceded on Sunday at Old Trafford against Bournemouth (0-3), the 45-year-old English coach was expected to leave. With eight consecutive games without a victory in all competitions, the Red Devils are having their worst start to a league season in 40 years. A situation which pushed Mancunian leaders to organize a crisis meeting on Monday, before finally deciding to keep their coach in post. Asked at a press conference about his surprise at still being at the head of the team, the former midfielder did not hesitate to answer. “ No. I’m here to do my job and give my best for the team. I am determined to make things better. So no, it doesn’t surprise me “, he said. A firm statement while his future has been the subject of much speculation for several days.

Appointed interim coach in January after the departure of Ruben Amorim, then confirmed this summer with a two-year contract after qualifying Manchester United for the Champions League, Michael Carrick assures that he does not need to be reassured by his management. “ I don’t need any guarantees from anyone. I fully understand the situation, the position, the responsibilities and what it entails “, he explained. The English technician nevertheless admits to having been marked by the hostile atmosphere at Old Trafford during the defeat against Bournemouth, while claiming not to pay attention to external criticism. “ What the fans think and what they feel in the stadium matters to me. Honestly, I don’t pay any attention to the rest “, he added. From now on, Carrick will have to quickly find solutions to revive his team, with a tricky trip to Chelsea on Saturday. A new poor performance could put his future back at the center of discussions in Manchester.
