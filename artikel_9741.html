Amsterdam, Netherlands, November 19th, 2026/TechnologyWire/--G2A.COM, the world’s largest marketplace for digital entertainment, has polled just over 2,000 of its users to investigate whether Black Friday still plays a crucial role in the annual purchasing journey. The findings reveal that, beyond the appeal of discounts, players are making increasingly deliberate purchasing decisions, tracking prices and postponing purchases in anticipation of Black Friday. This follows a recent report by Newzoo and G2A.COM, titled Game purchasing behavior in an era of choice, which provided a detailed examination of how gamers are dealing with higher prices, their attitudes toward discounts, and the growing number of ways to buy games.

G2A.COM’s transactional data reinforces the commercial importance of this shopping period. During Black Friday–Cyber Monday 2025, total orders in the marketplace increased by 33% year-on-year, while video game orders surged by 87%, significantly outpacing overall growth.

G2A.COM’s survey findings reveal that, despite a growing trend of delayed purchases and consumer skepticism, Black Friday remains the peak value event for gamers. This data corroborates with the recent Newzoo report, which found that 73% of gamers feel AAA games are overpriced at launch and, on average, wait 4.9 months for a sale or discount. Together, the studies highlight how price awareness and patience increasingly influence purchasing decisions, with Black Friday proving a key opportunity for gamers to act on their plans.

"Black Friday is no longer just about impulse purchases. It has become a planned part of how gamers manage their entertainment budgets," said Paweł Wróbel, Chief Growth Officer at G2A.COM. "Players are increasingly willing to wait, track prices and prioritise the games they want most. Our findings show that getting the best value is becoming just as important as getting a game at launch."

The survey further reveals that 39% of respondents plan to spend more on gaming this Black Friday than last year, while 41% expect to spend roughly the same amount. These findings underline Black Friday’s continued importance as a major shopping period for gamers, increasingly shaped by preparation, informed decisions and the search for value.
