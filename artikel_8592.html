Wells Fargo Routing Numbers by State – Go by Where You Opened the Account

Your Wells Fargo routing number depends on the state where you opened the account, not where you live now. Use that state’s number for a direct deposit or an automatic payment, and confirm it on a check or in the Wells Fargo app first. A wire coming into your account uses a separate number.

An account opened in Ohio uses Ohio’s number, 041215537, even after you move to Florida. Give an employer Florida’s 063107513 instead, and the deposit may fail, arrive late, or bounce back, as Wells Fargo’s guide to routing numbers warns.

Wells Fargo’s routing number page lists its numbers by state. Checking, savings, and money market accounts opened in a state all use that state’s number. Some states share a number, so an account opened in Massachusetts that shows 121042882, the number California uses too, is correct.

Accounts opened in Southern California may show a different number on their checks than 121042882, and Wells Fargo says either one works.

A line of credit’s number depends on the first three digits of its account number instead, and a Brokerage Cash Services account uses 102307164.

Wires coming into your account don’t use the state numbers. For a wire sent from inside the U.S., give the sender 121000248 as the routing number. For a wire from another country, give the sender the SWIFT code WFBIUS6S, which international wires use in place of a routing number.
