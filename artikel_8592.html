Pay Less at the Movies – Go on Discount Day Before You Buy a Pass

Join your movie theater chain’s free rewards program and go on its discount day, when members pay far less than the usual evening price. Buy a monthly movie pass only if you’d see enough films for it to cost less than discount-day tickets, and share one snack or eat before you go.

On a discount day, a big theater chain can sell a member a ticket for half its regular evening price.

Members of AMC Stubs, including those on its free tier, get that 50% off on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, plus half off the small popcorn-and-drink combo in the theater. Surcharges for formats like IMAX and fees for booking online still apply, and some holiday periods can be excluded. AMC also discounts shows that start before 4 p.m. every day.

Members of Regal Rewards, also free, get discounted tickets and half-price popcorn on Tuesdays. At Cinemark, Discount Tuesdays take up to 50% off the weekend evening price for everyone, except on some holidays, and members of its free Movie Rewards program pay the least.

Say two evening tickets to a standard showing cost $16 each and two small combos cost $14 each, so a Saturday at AMC runs $60. Two Stubs members on the free tier who buy the same tickets and combos at the box office on a Tuesday pay $30, or $23 if they share one combo.

Or eat before you go and skip the snack counter. Paying with discounted gift cards lowers the rest of the bill, and Costco sells them for all three chains.

Judge a monthly pass against discount-day prices, which in the example above means $8 a ticket rather than $16. AMC Stubs A-List lets you see up to four movies a week in any format, and Regal Unlimited as many standard 2D movies as you want. Each costs up to $29.99 a month, and Regal makes you pay for at least three months up front. Cinemark Movie Club costs $10.99 to $12.99 a month for one ticket, 20% off snacks, and no online ticket fees. Counting the ticket alone, that $10.99 to $12.99 beats one $16 evening ticket but not one $8 discount-day ticket.

Three discount-day movies a month cost $24 and four cost $32, so a $29.99 pass pays off only from your fourth movie.
