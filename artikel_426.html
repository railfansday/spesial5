Its official: Robert Pattinson is our new James Bond

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Martinis are flowing from the chimneys at Pinewood Studios, which can only mean one thing: We have our new James Bond.

On Nov. 16, Amazon MGM Studios announced that the newest 007 will be Robert Pattinson. He takes over the role from Daniel Craig, whose last film was 2021's No Time to Die. The next Bond film, the franchise's 26th, will be helmed by Dune director Denis Villeneuve. Pattinson has also worked with Villeneuve on Dune: Part Three.

Aside from the Bond announcement, 2026 has been a banner year for Pattinson. He topped the box office with The Odyssey and cemented himself as an A24 darling with roles in The Drama and Primetime.

While Pattinson has starred in smaller, stranger indie projects, he has also developed a seemingly paradoxical reputation as a franchise man. He is Batman, after all, with The Batman: Part II set for a Feb. 18, 2028 release. Before Pattinson became Battinson, moody eyeshadow and all, he captured hearts as Edward Cullen in the Twilight franchise, as well as Cedric Diggory in Harry Potter.

Plus, while not part of a franchise, his role as agent Neil in Christopher Nolan's Tenet proved he's got the chops to play a suave agent.

Other contenders for film's most famous spy included Slow Horses' Jack Lowden, One Night Only's Callum Turner, Challengers' Josh O'Connor, and Superman's Nicholas Hoult, all of whom reportedly screen tested for the role. Frankenstein's Jacob Elordi and Sinners' Jack O'Connell were reportedly in the running as well, although did not end up screen testing.
