Jimmy Kimmel blasts Trumps chummy meeting with Putin

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin, and Xi Jinping staged a historic trilateral meeting on Thursday, during the APEC summit in Shenzhen. The leaders of the U.S., Russia, and China spent 90 minutes conversing around a massive table before issuing a "joint declaration of peace."

Jimmy Kimmel unpacked the meeting during his Thursday monologue, but especially went in on Trump's overtly chummy greeting for Putin, which saw the U.S. and Russian presidents shaking hands after Trump said aloud, "He's my friend."

"How is that OK with anyone?" asked Kimmel. "Vladimir Putin is a terrible person. He is a brutal dictator. He is a murderer. He kills people. He poisons them. He has them shoved out of windows. But I guess that's all OK because 'he's my friend.'

"My god, I mean, even if you voted for this guy, you have to admit he has the worst taste in friends," Kimmel added, showing a photo of Trump with his arm around convicted child sex offender Jeffrey Epstein.
