West Ham 1 – 1 Preston North End | Player ratings as Parker drops more points

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

West Ham again conceded in the first half and spent the rest of the game peppering Preston’s goal – four efforts hit the woodwork but to no avail until Mavropanos’ second half equaliser. A lack of clinical finishing cost Scott Parker’s side dear as the points were shared.

Encouraging early touches with his feet and a willingness to catch rather than punch initially inspired confidence. Failed to get his fists to a corner, the ball striking Piroe on the head and looping over Herrick to give Preston a simple goal to create their lead. A superb double save just before half-time kept West Ham in touch after Mayers’ poor header. Grew in stature in the second half, notably catching balls where Hermansen might have punched.

Good early interplay with Bowen down the right. Unlucky not to score when his effort crashed against the post immediately after Bowen had hit the woodwork. Replaced by Souček on 78 minutes.

Solid enough first half without really being tested. Slipped in to meet a deflected Engels cross and equalise in true Mavropanos style.

Looked far more comfortable in the combative centre-back role, without being seriously tested for pace. Met Solomon’s second half cross with a header that was blocked.

Solid enough first half until a dreadful defensive header gifted the ball back to Preston, with Herrick rescuing him through a superb double save. Booked after misjudging his control and pulling down an opponent. Replaced by Scarles on 78 minutes.

A great through ball released Piroe for West Ham’s first decent opportunity. Excellent work out wide, crossing with his weaker foot to allow Mavropanos to equalise. Lots of wide running in a much more advanced position than under Nuno. Looked better for it.

Making his first appearance of the season, showed little sign of rustiness. Should have equalised after a well-worked corner, curling into the side-netting. Looked increasingly at home sitting in front of the defence and moving remarkably quickly across the turf. Quick feet in the opposition box led to a shot poked agonisingly close, but just wide. A pretty good shift on his return; surprising he lasted until 90 minutes, when Magassa replaced him.

Should have taken a first-time shot with his right foot, but instead shifted onto his favoured left and scuffed the chance in the first half. Needs to produce more. Evidently Parker agrees as he was replaced by Castellanos on 57 minutes.

Struck the post with a right-footed effort as West Ham pressed for an equaliser. Had the beating of his full-back in the second half, making frequent runs down the right and crossing with his weaker foot. Several shots but lacked that killer punch – as did the whole attack.

Should have opened the scoring after ten minutes when sent through one-on-one by Engels, but the goalkeeper saved. Lots of trying and ‘nearlies’ but too little output. Booked on 82 minutes for a professional foul. Not enough from the striker.

A decent early effort from 25 yards went straight at the goalkeeper. Immediately after the restart, stepped inside and struck the bar with a brilliant curling right-footed shot. Replaced by Solomon on 57 minutes at which point West Ham’s pace and zip largely vanished. There’s a lesson in there…

On for Austbø, 57 minutes. One jinking run inside produced a cross met by Morato’s head, but the effort was blocked. Looked very right-footed and unable to beat defenders on the outside. West Ham were poorer for the substitution of Austbø and Solomon’s introduction.

On for Mukasa, 57 minutes. Fluffed his lines on 85 minutes, looping a back-post header over the bar.

On for Mayers, 78 minutes. One decent cross late on. Understandably rusty-looking.

On for Veltman, 78 minutes. Almost won it in the 94th minute with a back-post header tipped over by Preston’s goalkeeper.
