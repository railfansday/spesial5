England: How many games Elliot Anderson will miss after Spain red card

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Elliot Anderson is facing a suspension after being sent-off in England’s Nations League win over Spain.

Goals from Harry Kane, Ezri Konsa and Ben White had the Three Lions cruising to victory at 3-0 on Sunday night, before Anderson’s challenge on Lamine Yamal produced both a red card and a penalty.

Mikel Oyarzabal successfully converted the spot-kick before substitute Nico Williams reduced deficit with 15 minutes plus stoppage time remaining.

Ultimately, England held on for a famous win and topped Group A3 as a result.

England will discover their quarter-final opponents later this week, but they will be without Anderson for the first-leg because of the red card.

UEFA rules stipulate “a player or team official sent off by the referee is automatically suspended for the next match in the competition.

“In the event of a serious offence, the UEFA disciplinary bodies are entitled to augment this punishment, including by extending it to other competitions.”

Due to Anderson being given his marching orders in the Spanish capital for denying an obvious goalscoring opportunity, rather than violent conduct, his punishment will be a one-game ban.

Therefore, the Manchester City midfielder will be available for the second leg in March 2027 - if named in the England squad by Thomas Tuchel.

Tuchel, meanwhile, slammed the decision as a ‘double punishment’ during his post-match press conference, dissecting a classic England performance after becoming the first team to beat Spain in 43 competitive matches.

Tuchel commented: “The red card and the penalty is always a big decision - and I need to understand why it's a double punishment.

"Then it was a long, long 30 minutes - but I don't want to focus on that. There was so, so many positive things about the result after 60 minutes. It was well deserved.”
