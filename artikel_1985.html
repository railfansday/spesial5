Rangers contracts 26/27: Here are all 9 players out of contract at Ibrox in the summer

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Here are all 9 Rangers players who will see their current deal run out at Ibrox in summer 2027.

Rangers will be hoping their impressive unbeaten run can help them topple Celtic at the top of the table and claim their first Scottish Premiership title since the 2020/21 season.

Currently four points behind Celtic at the summit, with a game in hand, Derek McInnes men have lost just once this season and are currently on a 12-game unbeaten run that has dragged them right back into title contention after a difficult opening month.

It was a busy summer window at Ibrox, with over 14 new signings, as Kevin Kelsy, Ivor Pandur and Kim Min-su all headlined a list of big-money arrivals. There were outgoings too, though, with James Tavernier, Thelo Aasgaard and Mohamed Diomande all departing for pastures new.

But which other Rangers players are facing an uncertain few months ahead? Which stars are about to enter the final months of their Ibrox contract - and which of them could depart the club next summer?

Here are all 9 players set to see their Rangers contracts expire in summer 2027:
