“Staring Parker in the Face” West Ham’s Missing Link Hiding In Plain Sight

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

I wrote yesterday that I was struggling for answers regarding how West Ham can address their current form. And then, like a lightning bolt, it all became a lot clearer to me!

Some players influence matches in ways that do not always show up in the highlights package. And West Ham have two such players, one of whom has been overlooked by Scott Parker.

The player overlooked is none other than Pablo Felipe.

Now, before we get ahead of ourselves, I’ve openly never been a big fan of the player. He barely scores any goals, looks slow and has missed some great opportunities in front of goal.

However, while Pablo Felipe may not always grab the headlines, the numbers indicate he is one of the most influential players in our squad.

When Pablo starts, West Ham score first in 83.6% of matches. Without him in the starting line-up, that figure plummets to just 18.2%. In football, scoring first can completely change the course of a game, making this a significant indicator of his influence.

The defensive numbers are equally striking. With Pablo on the pitch, the team have scored 16 goals while conceding only five. Without him, they have scored 19 but conceded 19. While the attacking output remains similar, the defensive stability and overall balance are clearly stronger when he plays.

“Staring Parker in the Face” West Ham’s Missing Link? The gaffer could do worse than select Pablo

This highlights something many supporters already recognise. Pablo’s value lies in his work rate, intelligent positioning, pressing and ability to knit the team together. He helps the side control matches, protects the defence and creates the conditions for others to shine.

With few options and players underperforming, Parker may look to reintegrate the Portuguese striker into the first-team set-up for the visit of Stoke City on Saturday.

The other player who deserves a place in the starting eleven is Tomáš Souček. Despite featuring for just 182 minutes across nine matches, he has already registered three goals and eleven shots. With the team in desperate need of leaders, Souček’s influence could prove invaluable. Fiercely respected within the dressing room, the midfielder has earned the nickname “Iron Man” from his teammates and it’s easy to see why! As Kris has already reported, this hasn’t gone unnoticed elsewhere!

Former manager David Moyes once summed up his importance perfectly: “We rely on him a lot, we need him, and he is very important for us.”
