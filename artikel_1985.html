Tuttosport: Milan attempting to breach the rift as Pochettino prepares Fernandes meeting

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

AC Milan remain at the door for Bruno Fernandes and they will hope to hear a positive update from his meeting with Mauricio Pochettino.

When Fernandes returns from international duty, he’ll meet his new coach for the first time and perhaps it’ll reopen the door to a future at Old Trafford. Right now, the parties have never been so far apart and the Argentine may be their last hope at fixing things.

With their captain’s deal up in the summer – even if they have an option to extend it – his value will drop without some positive news from inside the manager’s office. Of course, for Milan, they are hoping to hear positive news in the sense of negative news.

According to Tuttosport, the Rossoneri are trying to breach the current rift in Fernandes’ relationship with the Old Trafford outfit. With him rejecting two contract approaches now, it’s clear that things are not as strong and an exit can no longer be automatically ruled out.

Thanks to an ‘excellent’ relationship with Ruben Amorim, there is already something to lure him to San Siro. However, that wouldn’t be the only reason.

Before any move happens, he will have a meeting with the new coach, Pochettino, to understand what all parties want for the future. He wants to understand his situation and his captain’s desire to play for him. Only then will the future be decided…
