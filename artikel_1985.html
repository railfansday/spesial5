L’Equipe: Initial tests positive as Maignan hopeful for start vs. Frosinone

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

AC Milan have received some fantastic news from France, with Mike Maignan seemingly going to be ok to play this weekend.

News arrived this morning that Maignan was going to have an MRI to see the extent of his injury tomorrow, after he was substituted at half-time in last night’s game against Turkey.

Milan had even begun preparing for this and it was reported this afternoon that Lorenzo Torriani had started to warm up for this weekend’s fixture. However, he may not be needed after all.

According to L’Equipe, there is some reassuring news regarding Maignan, as MilanNews relay. After leaving the pitch yesterday with an injury, he had some initial tests today, which have returned positive results.

In fact, he should be back in training midweek and then, all being well, he’ll be available to face Frosinone this weekend.

The caveat to this is that the tests were not conducted by the Rossoneri, who will conduct their own tests tomorrow, starting with an MRI scan. If the results are positive, there’s every chance that he’ll be in goal this weekend.

He was taken off by Zinedine Zidane’s staff as a precaution, not because he couldn’t continue. So, there’s certainly hope that he could feature at San Siro.
