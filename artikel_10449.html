AAWireless releases new, smaller Nexus and Nano wireless Android Auto adapters

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

AAWireless, the original wireless Android Auto adapter brand, is introducing new hardware this week with its compact Nexus and Nano adapters.

The new AAWireless Nexus and Nano are both designed as smaller adapters compared to the main Two and Two+ models. Both sold for $69.99 ($74.99 on Amazon), these are both drastically smaller – something that we’ve seen pop up from other adventurous brands, but now we’re getting it with a known name in the space.

AAWireless Nexus features a circular puck design with a USB-C plug that has a replaceable cable. AAWireless Nano, meanwhile, is an adapter on the end of a USB-A plug. It’s about as small as this concept can get.

Both adapters feature an upgraded chipset, Wi-Fi 6 support, and both Android Auto and CarPlay. They also feature physical buttons, just like the AAWireless Two series.

Pre-registration is open today, with early orders available starting on December 1. The actual release date is December 8.
