More Women on Testosterone; Hormone Patch Updates; Drug-Induced Bone Loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

— News and commentary from the endocrinology world

Testosterone use among females has risen since 2016 and is frequently prescribed without a documented diagnosis of hypoactive sexual desire disorder, the sole evidence-based indication. (Journal of Clinical Endocrinology & Metabolism)

After type 2 diabetes remission following metabolic-bariatric surgery, predictors of relapse included longer baseline diabetes duration and a greater number of preoperative glucose-lowering medication classes. (Diabetes Care)

In a phase III head-to-head trial, the investigational triple agonist retatrutide showed superior reductions in both HbA1c and body weight compared with semaglutide (Ozempic) in adults with type 2 diabetes, Eli Lilly announced.

The FDA removed boxed warnings about cardiovascular disease, breast cancer, and dementia from prescribing information for more hormone therapy patches for menopausal symptoms. (The Guardian)

Meanwhile, doctors are warning not to overlook the small increase in breast cancer risk associated with certain hormonal therapies for treating menopause symptoms. (New York Times)

The Endocrine Society established a new award honoring late diabetes pioneer Jesse Roth, MD, presenting the inaugural prize to C. Ronald Kahn, MD, of Joslin Diabetes Center and Harvard Medical School in Boston.

What do oral corticosteroids, aromatase inhibitors, androgen deprivation therapy, anti-seizure medications, and selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors have in common? They're all linked to bone density loss. (Washington Post)

Eli Lilly announced a partnership with telehealth platform Hims & Hers Health to expand access to its GLP-1 weight-loss drugs tirzepatide (Zepbound) and orforglipron (Foundayo), offering matched self-pay prices.

The nation's first detransition clinic, at Texas Children's Hospital, is now open, said Attorney Gen­er­al Ken Paxton.

AstraZeneca reportedly offered $10 billion to acquire Rhythm Pharmaceuticals and its first-in-class melanocortin-4 receptor (MC4R) agonist, setmelanotide (Imcivree), for rare genetic obesities. (FiercePharma)
