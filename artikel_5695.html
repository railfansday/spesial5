How Smarter Healthcare Environments Improve Patient Experience" style="max-width:100%; height:auto; border-radius:8px;" loading="lazy">

Patient experience begins before a patient reaches the bedside. From navigating the parking garage and finding the right entrance to checking in and receiving timely results, every touchpoint can shape comfort, trust and access to care. In this sponsored episode of Podnosis, healthcare leaders examine how thoughtful design and connected operations can create a more seamless journey for patients and families.

Candace LeVaughn of Schneider Electric and Debbie Gregory of SSR discuss the hidden operational challenges behind care delivery, including nursing and facilities workforce shortages, workplace safety, aging infrastructure and technologies that do not communicate effectively. They explore how robotics, wearables, building automation, interoperable platforms and meaningful data analytics can reduce workarounds, improve reliability and support caregivers.

The conversation also looks ahead to AI, ambient intelligence, transportation automation and the growing demands on power and the grid. Their practical message for healthcare organizations: view the health system as an interconnected ecosystem, avoid piecemeal upgrades and give clinicians, staff and patients a seat at the table. They explore how a holistic strategy can help healthcare environments evolve while keeping patient outcomes and experience at the center.
