No Org No Problem beat Stallions Esports 2-1 to reach VCT EMEA Kickoff MENA grand final

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

No Org No Problem won the opening and deciding maps against Stallions Esports to reach the MENA Qualifier Playoffs grand final. One more series win against Mix My Miks would send them to VCT EMEA Kickoff 2027 in Berlin alongside partner teams including Fnatic.

Against Stallions Esports, the semi-final went the full distance. No Org No Problem took the opening map in 48 minutes. Stallions Esports levelled on the second, winning a 54-minute contest. No Org No Problem then closed out the decider in 43 minutes.

The result came against a ranked opponent. Stallions Esports sat at rank 72 in the Elo power rankings, with a rating of 1628 and a 24-14 record in 38 rated matches. No Org No Problem are unranked. Stallions had also arrived in mixed form, with two 0-2 defeats to VillianArc in VCL MENA: Resilience Pulse Stage Middle East 2026. They beat Spire Esports twice and AXE in the same competition.

No Org No Problem reached the playoffs by winning Middle East Qualifier 1, held from 9 to 11 November, with The Spiders finishing second. Stallions Esports won Middle East Qualifier 2 from 16 to 18 November, ahead of Mayhem. Mix My Miks won North Africa Qualifier 1, a single-elimination event from 9 to 11 November, ahead of BAAM Esports. Each of those qualifiers had 64 teams.

Mix My Miks booked their final place by beating Gamax Esports in the second semi-final. That match also went to three maps. The four-team playoffs are being played online in a single-elimination bracket. The semi-finals were best of three.

Alongside the MENA winner, Europe sends two qualifiers to Berlin and Türkiye sends one. The partner teams already in the field are BBL Esports, Fnatic, FUT Esports, Gentle Mates, Karmine Corp, Team Heretics, Team Liquid and Team Vitality. Fnatic were confirmed in October 2026 as staying on as a VCT EMEA partner for 2027.

The tournament will be played in person at the Riot Games Arena in Berlin in early 2027. For the MENA winner, the trip carries direct financial value. Riot’s support for non-partner teams starts at Kickoff, and the sums rise for teams that progress further through the season.

Non-partner teams that qualify for Masters receive US$200,000, and those reaching Champions receive US$400,000, Dot Esports reported. Europe’s route to Kickoff runs in parallel. The Europe Kickoff Qualifier Playoffs begin with a Swiss stage from 25 to 29 November, followed by playoffs from 3 to 5 December.
