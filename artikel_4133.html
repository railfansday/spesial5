Loco Motive is bringing its comedy murder mystery to iOS in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Point-and-click adventures have always suited touchscreens, since tapping on things is most of what you do in them anyway. So, it’s good news that Loco Motive, the comedy murder mystery, is in development for iOS, with a release planned for some point in 2027.

The news came out alongside the Hands On update for the PC version, which marks its second anniversary and adds full touch controls on Steam Deck. You can switch between touch, a controller and a mouse at any point, which is probably a decent sign for how it’ll play on a phone. Chucklefish says more details on the iOS version will follow.

The game is set on the Reuss Express, a 1930s express train. Lady Unterwald, who ran the Wald-Bahn Rail Company that owns it, has been murdered on board at the age of 89, and you play as three of the suspects trying to prove they didn’t do it.

That includes a strait-laced lawyer, a novelist who’s become an amateur detective, and an undercover agent, whose stories link up as you go. I don’t see how being an undercover agent helps. The puzzles are mostly item-based, and the whole cast is voiced, by what Chucklefish describes as an all-star line-up.

I like the Dirk Chiselton Hotline. It’s a phone line inside the adventure itself that you can call when you’re stuck, and it points you towards the solution without spoiling it.

Chucklefish has brought a few of its games to phones over the last couple years. Wildfrost and Wargroove 2 both came out on iOS and Android, and Inmost was announced for both in September 2025. Loco Motive has only been confirmed for iOS so far. Nothing says Android is ruled out, but I wouldn’t count on it in 2027.

It’s £14.99 on PC and Switch right now. There’s no word yet on what the iOS version will cost.
