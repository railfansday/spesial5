MiNK Therapeutics, Inc. (INKT +2.14%) reported its first pre-commercial product revenue and the strategic advancement of its cell therapy platform across critical illness and oncology. Management detailed the launch of a named patient program for agenT-797, which allows for physician-directed access outside of clinical trials in multiple countries.
