Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Seer, Inc. (SEER -4.17%) reported a sequential revenue increase while lowering its full-year 2026 outlook due to persistent market headwinds. Management identified academic funding constraints and corporate uncertainty as primary factors delaying customer purchasing decisions. The company completed a strategic review by a Special Committee, resulting in its continued operation as an independent public entity. Operating expenses declined 23% year over year following cost-reduction measures in compensation and professional services. Management indicated that existing capital reserves are sufficient to reach cash flow breakeven under the current operating plan.

Turning to slide 3, let me turn to the scientific evidence from the PRECISE-SG100K cohort presented at the 2026 Human Proteome Organization World Congress, or HUPO. PRECISE-SG100K is Singapore's population health study of approximately 100,000 residents of Chinese, Malay, and Indian ancestry designed to understand how genetics, biology, lifestyle, and environment influence health and disease. Within this program, investigators selected our Proteograph workflow combined with Thermo Fisher's Orbitrap Astral mass spectrometry to deeply characterize the plasma proteome of 10,000 participants, complementing affinity-based measurements within the broader study. Professor John Chambers, Chief Scientific Officer of PRECISE and Lead Investigator of PRECISE-SG100K, presented preliminary findings.

Across 9,658 plasma samples, the Proteograph Astral workflow quantified approximately 8,800 protein groups across this study and approximately 88,000 peptides per sample. Performance was consistent across 126 processing batches with no reported sample failure. Population studies require both depth and reliable execution across thousands of samples, and these results demonstrate the ability of our workflow to deliver both. As subsequently reported by Thermo Fisher Scientific, the PRECISE investigators identified more than 4,200 proteins with significant cis-pQTL associations, which link nearby genetic variations with measured protein abundance. They also reported 2,762 genetic protein associations that had not been reported across the 12 reference studies used for comparison and connected proteins to 71 health-related traits.

When combined with disease genetics and other evidence, these relationships can help researchers investigate disease mechanism and prioritize potential therapeutic targets. The PRECISE-SG100K illustrates a central aspect of Seer's value proposition. Proteograph fundamentally expands the biology researchers can see with mass spectrometry. Published studies and customer presentations using matched plasma samples have repeatedly demonstrated that conventional mass spectrometry workflows without the Proteograph achieve approximately 10% to 15% of the protein coverage achieved when Proteograph is paired with a mass spectrometry. Used alongside advanced mass spectrometers, Proteograph substantially expands the biological information available for discovery.

Just as 10x Genomics enabled researchers to use DNA sequencing to resolve biology at the level of individual cells, Seer enables researchers to use mass spectrometry to explore a much deeper landscape of circulating proteins. By bringing thousands of additional proteins within reach of measurement, Proteograph opens new opportunities to uncover genetic protein relationships, investigate disease mechanisms, and identify potential therapeutic targets. This is the discovery capability Seer brings to population-scale research. The PRECISE team is now beginning to analyze the peptide-level data, creating opportunities to investigate protein variants and isoforms beyond the protein-level findings reported to date. For Seer, studies of this scale demonstrate both scientific value and the ability to support substantial sample processing programs.

Turning to slide 4, an independent study published in Clinical Proteomics provides another example of our differentiation. Researchers at CHA University in South Korea compared 7 plasma preparation approaches using accessible mass spectrometry instrumentation. Proteograph XT, together with the Orbitrap Exploris 480, delivered the greatest proteome coverage, identifying an average of approximately 6,060 proteins per sample and the lowest rate of missing protein measurements at 1.9%. These findings demonstrate that Proteograph enables deep proteomic coverage even in laboratories using earlier generations of mass spectrometers. PrognomiQ commercially launched ProVue Lung in November 2025 as a laboratory-developed test, or an LDT, and has reported early results from its ongoing real-world clinical evaluation. In a recent conference presentation, PrognomiQ's Chief Technology Officer Bruce Wilcox described how the protein biomarkers from the original discovery study were independently replicated using both targeted mass spectrometry and immunoassays in a separate patient cohort. The findings supported translation into a multiplex immunoassay for the launch of ProVue Lung. We also reported that the program progressed from initial Proteograph-based discovery to a validated laboratory-developed test in 31 months with clinical deployment to Allegheny Health Network and Penn Highlands Healthcare.

Turning to slide 5, AI is shaping biology, and the capabilities of a model depend on the data it learns from. Proteograph-enabled mass spectrometry produces information at the spectral, peptide, and protein levels. Researchers can use this data to investigate protein abundance, peptide-level differences, and signals that conventional identification-based analysis have yet to fully characterize. Professors Sang-Won Lee and Jaewoo Kang of Korea University presented preliminary findings for more than 5,500 plasma samples at the ASMS and HUPO conference this year.
