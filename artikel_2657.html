Cowboys sign OL Nick Leverett to active roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Cowboys made a change to their offensive line group on Tuesday.

They announced that they have signed Nick Leverett to the active roster from the practice squad. Ajani Cornelius was waived in a corresponding move.

Leverett spent time on the active roster earlier this season and appeared in five games. He also played in one game for the team last season and appeared in 22 games for the Cardinals, Patriots and Buccaneers from 2021-2024.

Cornelius played in three games for the Cowboys this season.

The move comes a day after Cowboys head coach Brian Schottenheimer announced right guard Tyler Booker will miss time with a high-ankle sprain. Leverett has experience as a guard and will help back up expected replacement starter T.J. Bass.
