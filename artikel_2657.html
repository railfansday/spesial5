Karl-Anthony Towns dominates Pacers in return to push Knicks’ win streak to five

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Karl-Anthony Towns returned from an illness to carry the surging Knicks.

The center dominated the Pacers on Sunday night, finishing with 30 points and 13 rebounds in a resounding 113-106 victory.

It represented a fifth straight win for the Knicks (11-6), who pulled away in the third quarter because Towns caught fire.

New York Knicks guard Jose Alvarado (5) and center Karl-Anthony Towns (32) react after scoring against the Indiana Pacers on Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. Jason Szenes for NY Post

He scored 16 points in just nine minutes of the period as the Knicks went on a 20-11 run to take a 89-78 lead entering the fourth quarter. It ended with a nifty dribbling display into a 3-point conversion from Jose Alvarado.

Then Josh Hart helped close the win in the fourth quarter, earning a triple-double with 10 points, 11 boards and 10 assists in 39 minutes. Hart, who holds the franchise's single-season triple-double record, needed the confidence boost after riding the struggle bus to start the season.

Towns, meanwhile, has been consistently excellent.

A night earlier in a nail-biting victory over the Blazers, he was a DNP with an illness. Towns was then listed as questionable before Sunday's Pacers game, with coach Mike Brown claiming about 90 minutes before tipoff that Towns' availability was a game-time decision.

Turns out, KAT was fine. His biggest problem was foul trouble, which kept him on the bench for all but three minutes of the fourth quarter. Luckily, Hart and Jalen Brunson (22 points, seven assists) picked up the slack to put away the plucky Pacers (5-12), who stink again this season but always play tougher — and more motivated — against the Knicks.

Towns, 31, is giving the Knicks plenty of reasons to reopen negotiations on a contract extension. He has 16 double-doubles in 16 appearances.

Josh Hart #3 and Jose Alvarado #5 of the New York Knicks react during a game against the Indiana Pacers on Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, in New York. Getty Images

The Pacers were playing without Tyrese Haliburton, who has been out since last month with a hamstring strain. That didn't matter nine days prior, when the Pacers toppled the Knicks in Indiana without Haliburton.

This time, the Knicks were home and better.

The Knicks had their own injury issues Sunday. OG Anunoby missed his second consecutive game with soreness, although Brown downplayed the severity of the injury. Miles McBride was a late scratch with left adductor tightness, a new injury that's concerning only because of the area. McBride missed a couple months last season after sports hernia surgery, which is in the vicinity of his adductor.

The first half was tight throughout, with neither team leading by more than four points and the score a modest 55-51, Knicks advantage, at the half. It was a continuation of New York's offense this season being behind the defense, which is apparently a league-wide trend with scoring down across the NBA.

"I didn't know it was down overall," Brown said. "I don't know. I don't know if the defenses are getting better. I do know we, just our team, we haven't shot the ball as well as we'd like. But every game we're getting a little bit better, everybody's percentages are going up just a little bit every game, and that's exciting. I think eventually for us, right now our defense is way ahead of our offense, but our offense is coming, and hopefully we'll get there sooner than later."

Towns changed the tenor in the third quarter by hitting 5-of-8 shots, including 2-for-2 from beyond the arc. His return meant a DNP for James Wiseman, who played well in Saturday's win over the Blazers but is still the third-stringer behind Towns and Andre Drummond.

New York Knicks guard Jalen Brunson (11) shoots during the first half of a game against the Indiana Pacers on Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, at MetLife Stadium. Jason Szenes for NY Post

Landry Shamet, whom Brown declared pregame as the clear frontrunner for Sixth Man of the Year, started again because of Anunoby's injury. He finished with 17 points in 32 minutes, including 12 points in the first half.

Hart looked like he was headed toward another frustrating night, appearing hesitant or even unwilling to take open 3-pointers. He had just two points at the half. But he picked up some momentum after the break, with the help of his podcast buddy Brunson.

Early in the third quarter, the two were running together on a breakaway, and Brunson, who had the open lane, bounced a pass to Hart for his first dunk of the season. It gave the Knicks a 12-point lead — their biggest of the night up to that point.
