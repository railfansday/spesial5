Cavaliers' Evan Mobley out at least another week with toe injury suffered in win over Knicks

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Cleveland Cavaliers will be without Evan Mobley for at least another week.

Mobley suffered a right toe sprain last week during their matchup with the New York Knicks and has been sidelined ever since. That injury has since been confirmed, the team announced on Monday afternoon, and he will now undergo treatment over the next seven to 10 days.

Mobley went down in the final minutes of the Cavaliers' 115-107 win over the New York Knicks on Wednesday. He tried to get up to block at shot at Madison Square Garden, but ended up slamming to the court hard and awkwardly. He remained down for a moment, clearly shaken up, and he hasn't played since.

Coach Kenny Atkinson said Friday, ahead of the Cavs' loss to the Miami Heat, that it was just a "sprain" and that they didn't "expect it to be a long-term thing at all." Mobley missed that game and Saturday's win over the Dallas Mavericks as a result.

But instead of just a game or two, Mobley will need at least another full week to recover. If he's out just the seven days, he'd be able to return for their matchup with the Chicago Bulls next week at the United Center. That would mean he'd missed five total games.

Mobley has averaged 16.9 points and 7.3 rebounds per game this season, his sixth in the league. The 25-year-old is in the second year of a five-year, $269 million deal with the Cavaliers, who selected him with the No. 3 overall pick back in 2021 out of USC.

The Cavaliers will enter Monday night's matchup with the Bulls with a 7-7 record.
