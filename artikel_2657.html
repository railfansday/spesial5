Rams vs. Packers: Game chat for Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Los Angeles Rams host the Green Bay Packers for the first ever NFL Thanksgiving Eve Game, and you truly aren't going to want to miss this one between two 6-4 teams trying to keep pace in a brutal NFC playoff race. Here's our how to watch guide.

The Rams come into this game on a four-game win streak and are hoping to make that number five with a victory over Green Bay. The Packers at times look like the best offense in the league, and then other times they cannot block for Jordan Love.

Both the Rams and Packers have incredible talent, both are flawed. Their 6-4 records reflect that. L.A. will try to figure out what their offense can be without Puka Nacua for the year. The Rams' receiving group is a major question mark, and Matthew Stafford may have to play his best game of the year for the win.

Myles Garrett and Aaron Donald may be the most exciting duo to watch today, but Micah Parsons could be the most fun player to watch as his highly anticipated return is expected to happen tonight.

This could make or break either team's season with regard to the playoffs, and in terms of a regular seaosn game, they don't get much more exciting than this. Comment with other Rams and NFL fans right here, and enjoy the game!
