Real Madrid could face Barcelona battle for 19-year-old defensive target &#8211; report

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

According to TeamTalk, Real Madrid are closely monitoring Brighton & Hove Albion defender Luka Vuskovic as a potential transfer target.

However, Los Blancos have been joined by their arch-rivals FC Barcelona in the race for the 19-year-old Croatian international.

Real Madrid have invested heavily in strengthening their defensive line over the past couple of summer transfer windows, but it appears that the restructuring of the backline will continue in 2027 as well.

After all, Antonio Rudiger is set to see his contract expire at the end of the ongoing season, and as things stand, Los Blancos do not plan on offering an extension.

Meanwhile, academy graduate Raul Asencio is also expected to be on chopping blocks either in January or in the summer, creating another vacancy in the centre-back position.

Real Madrid monitoring Luka Vuskovic. (Photo by Matt McNulty/Getty Images)

Therefore, the Spanish capital giants intend to bring in at least one new central defender in the summer of 2027.

Names like Nico Schlotterbeck of Borussia Dortmund, who was a target last summer as well, continue to be linked.

But, as per the aforementioned report, Real Madrid are also closely monitoring Vuskovic and his performances for Brighton & Hove Albion.

Vuskovic joined Brighton only this past summer in a deal worth £50 million from Tottenham Hotspur, after impressing on loan at Hamburg last season.

Since signing for the Seagulls, the teenager has made 16 appearances in all competitions for Fabian Hurzeler's side, even scoring one and assisting two goals.

Capped 12 times by Croatia, Vuskovic has attracted interest due to his aerial strength, physicality, and ability with the ball at his feet.

Vuskovic has a contract with Brighton until 2031. (Photo by Mike Hewitt/Getty Images)

However, signing him will be anything but easy for Real Madrid, as Barcelona have also shown an interest as they seek reinforcements in defence.

Premier League giants Liverpool are also keen as they see the teenager as a long-term replacement for Virgil van Dijk.

The biggest obstacle, however, will be Brighton's position as they do not wish to sell their star signing so soon after signing him.

Vuskovic has a contract with the Seagulls until 2031 and is happy at the club where he enjoys his manager's full trust.

A departure in January is completely ruled out and a potential sale in the summer would require a huge sum of money, making the deal difficult to pull off.
