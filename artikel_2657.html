Report: Aaron Rodgers did not report to Steelers facility on Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The story of Aaron Rodgers’ benching in Pittsburgh continues to unfold.

According to longtime beat writer Gerry Dulac of the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette, Rodgers did not report to the Steelers’ facility on Wednesday morning and his future with the franchise is uncertain.

This comes after, Dulac reports, that the situation with Rodgers and the Steelers had been “brewing for a while” and “reached the point of no return” with the stunning announcement that Rodgers was being benched on Tuesday night.

Rodgers has struggled in recent weeks, completing 52 percent of his passes for 143 yards in the Week 10 loss to Cincinnati before going 30-of-44 for 227 yards with one touchdown in Sunday’s loss to the Eagles. He also took five sacks against Philadelphia.

We’ll see how the ripple effects continue from head coach Mike McCarthy’s decision throughout the coming days.
