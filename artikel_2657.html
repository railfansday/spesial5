Eagles-Cowboys inactives set for Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The inactives are set for the Thanksgiving Day game in Dallas.

The Eagles’ inactives are OLB Arnold Ebiketie, QB Cole Payton, CB Jonathan Jones, OLB A.J. Epenesa, OL Ajani Cornelius and TE Grant Calcaterra.

The Cowboys’ inactives are RG Tyler Booker, WR Jonathan Mingo, S P.J. Locke, TE Jake Ferguson, DT Jonathan Bullard, LB Jaishawn Barham.

Eagles-Cowboys kicks off at 4:30 p.m. ET in Dallas.
