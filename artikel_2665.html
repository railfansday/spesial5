Fernando Mendoza: This week was an improvement, but still a lot to work on

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Sunday’s game in Denver opened poorly for the Raiders as the Broncos built up a 17-0 lead while the road team punted on its first three possessions, but things got better from there.

The Raiders cut the lead to 17-10 on a perfectly thrown pass by Fernando Mendoza to running back Ashton Jeanty on a wheel route and then tied the game when the rookie quarterback hooked up with tight end Ian Thomas for another score in the third quarter. Things swung back Denver’s way from there and they iced the 38-24 win with a Ja’Quan McMillan interception return for a touchdown late in the fourth quarter.

Mendoza ended the day 18-of-37 for 267 yards, three touchdowns and that pick. After the game, he said he felt “a lot more comfortable” than he did in his first start while also noting room for growth in future starts.

“I believe I saw the field better,” Mendoza said. “I believe that my accuracy was better this week than last week, stepping into throws rather than drifting back. Don’t get me wrong, although I believe it was an improvement there are a lot of things I need to improve on for next week.”

Among the things Mendoza and the Raiders will need to work on is the rookie having six passes batted down by Broncos defenders during the loss. He’ll try to be crisper in his Sunday Night Football debut in Cleveland next weekend.
