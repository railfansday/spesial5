Jayden Daniels: Brandon Aiyuk and I put the past behind us

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Commanders quarterback Jayden Daniels and Brandon Aiyuk were once Arizona State teammates and close friends, but Aiyuk seemed to burn those bridges this year, when he posted several critical comments about Daniels on social media. After Aiyuk signed with the Commanders this week, however, Daniels said that’s all behind them.

“I’m a grown man, he’s a grown man,” Daniels said. “Things happen. You don’t know what he was going through. You don’t know what I was going through at that time.”

Daniels said he looked up to Aiyuk when they were at Arizona State together and thinks they can have a good relationship as teammates again.

“That’s somebody that looked out for me when I was 18 years old, helped me out when I first got to ASU,” Daniels said. “So, like I said, I know the character and the person he is. He was man enough to settle our differences and just move forward from there. That’s like a brother to me. Like I said, we’ve been knowing each other for some time, looked out for me, took care of me. So, just put the past behind us.”

As for the social media posts that Aiyuk made, Daniels said he was never angry about that.

“I prayed for him because he’s like family to me,” Daniels said. “I think that was a very dark moment in his life.”

Daniels declined to go into specifics about recent conversations with Aiyuk but said he hopes Aiyuk will be a good addition to the Commanders.

“That’s between us, personal, but yeah, we did chat,” Daniels said. “He works hard. Before his knee injury he was All-Pro, very explosive, big catch radius, so excited for him to have a fresh start, new opportunity to prove himself day in and day out.”

Aiyuk hasn’t played in more than two years, but he was once one of the best receivers in the NFL. If Aiyuk can play at anything close to the level he used to play at, he can be a significant addition to the Commanders’ offense. Daniels is excited about that possibility.
