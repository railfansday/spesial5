Packers Injury Report: WR Skyy Moore designated to return

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Green Bay Packers opened up their practice window for receiver/returner Skyy Moore, who was a limited participant (ankle) on Wednesday. Moore is currently on the injured reserve, but the team has a 21-day opportunity to activate him to the 53-man roster. Along with Moore, running back Chris Brooks (also on the injured reserve) practiced with the Packers today (limited, ankle).

Edge defender Micah Parsons was listed as an estimated limited participant on Wednesday, despite head coach Matt LaFleur stating that Parsons would not be participating in today's action due to an illness. The only non-participants on the injury report today were safety Javon Bullard (ankle/knee), linebacker Zaire Franklin (shin), center Sean Rhyan (ankle), edge defender Lukas Van Ness (back) and tight end Josh Whyle (concussion).

Bullard and Van Ness were able to finish last week's action against the New England Patriots, while Franklin, Rhyan and Whyle never played. LaFleur seemed optimistic that both Franklin and Rhyan had a chance to suit up this week against the Minnesota Vikings.

The Vikings, who just got quarterback Kirk Cousins in a trade, did not practice on Wednesday. In their estimation, the non-participants would have been: linebacker Blake Cashman (concussion), tight end T.J. Hockenson (wrist/chest), defensive lineman Tyrion Ingram-Dawkins (ankle), receiver Jauan Jennings (calf), quarterback Kyler Murray (hamstring) and outside linebacker Kyle Van Noy (calf).
