Sam Darnold officially off injury report, set to start vs. 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

As expected, the Seahawks’ quarterback is off the injury report and set to start against the 49ers on Sunday, head coach Mike Macdonald told reporters on Friday afternoon.

Darnold has been listed as a full participant throughout the week after missing Seattle’s last two games with a quad injury. Darnold had also suffered a cracked rib in the Week 8 loss to the Bears.

Darnold has had turnover issues this season, throwing nine interceptions this season. He’s thrown at least one pick in each of his last six starts.

Seattle is 3-4 in games that Darnold has started and 3-0 in games with Lock as QB1. Lock also brought the Seahawks back in the Week 1 win over the Patriots after Darnold exited due to injury.

Additionally, the Seahawks have ruled out right tackle Abe Lucas (ankle), left guard Quenton Nelson (knee), and defensive tackle Jarran Reed (toe) for Sunday’s game against the 49ers.
