Mbappé and Konate reportedly intent on luring Bayern's Olise to Real Madrid

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

French outlet 'L'Équipe' reports that Ibrahima Konaté and Kylian Mbappé are again trying to persuade Michael Olise to join Real Madrid.

This story emanates out of France's current training camp as they prepare for Nations League duels with Italy and Türkiye.

Olise's current employers Bayern Munich are unlikely to be happy about this negative influence, from their perspective.

After all, fellow Bayern teammate Dayot Upamecano is also on board, and he would probably oppose Olise's move to Madrid.

As long as Olise has not yet signed a new contract at Säbener Straße, the Real stars will continue to leave no stone unturned.

It's believed the Real Madrid star's efforts to lure the 24-year-old to the Spanish capital will only increase with time.

Last summer, the transfer saga surrounding Olise and the Galacticos was intense before Bayern's management put a stop to rumours.
