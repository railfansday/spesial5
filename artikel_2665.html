Arkansas State reportedly fires coach Butch Jones in sixth season amid losing skid

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Arkansas State fired Jones on Monday, according to The Athletic's Chris Vannini. The move, which has not yet been made public, comes with two games left in the regular season.

Jones has been at Arkansas State since 2021. He'll now finish with a 30-43 record with the Red Wolves, though Jones has led the program to three straight bowl games, most recently the Xbox Bowl last season, and back-to-back winning records.

But this season has not been great. The Red Wolves have lost four of their last six games and now sit at just 4-6 on the year. They can still earn a trip to a bowl game, however they'll have to win out to become bowl eligible. They'll take on Louisiana Tech next on Saturday before wrapping up the regular season against Troy on Nov. 28.

Arkansas State marked the fourth head coaching job that Jones has had in his career. He got his start at Central Michigan, and had a short stint at Cincinnati before he was hired at Tennessee in 2013. Jones led the Volunteers for five seasons and compiled a 34-27 record before he was fired during the 2017 campaign. Jones did spend time as an assistant under Nick Saban at Alabama, too, before landing in Jonesboro.

According to USA Today, Arkansas will owe Jones more than $1.4 million.
