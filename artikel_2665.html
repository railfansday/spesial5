Miami Dolphins center Aaron Brewer in concussion protocol

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

During a week when the Miami Dolphins lost quarterback Malik Willis and defensive lineman Zach Sieler to injuries, center Aaron Brewer popped up on the Injury List with a concussion.

Brewer did not practice on Wednesday. His status for Sunday's game at the Indianapolis Colts will depend on if he clears concussion protocol. The backup center is Andrew Meyer, who was signed by the Dolphins an an undrafted college free agent in May 2024. He has started one game in Miami - Week 17 of the 2025 season, a 20-17 win against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers when Brewer was injured.

Brewer is a seven-year veteran and has been Miami's starting center for the past three seasons. He has been the team's most consistent offensive lineman.

Another player on the injury report is tight end Will Kacmarek, who left last Sunday's game but returned from a leg injury. He did not practice either and is listed with a quad/knee injury.
