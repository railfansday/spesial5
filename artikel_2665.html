Brock Bowers injures ankle

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Las Vegas Raiders were riding high after Fernando Mendoza's first touchdown. However, they received bad news after their first drive of the game.

Brock Bowers injured his ankle while blocking on a run play in the first quarter. They took him inside the tarp, and he returned briefly. After the touchdown, they took him to the locker room. Reports indicate he is questionable to return.

Times like this are why you paid Michael Mayer. He and Ian Thomas will have to step up and take over at tight end. Mendoza will have to lean on those two to make plays and other weapons on offense.

Hopefully it is not a serious injury for Bowers, as he is vital to the offense. He is the best tight end in football, and he will be missed if he can't go for now.

Update: Bowers has returned to the game after coming back from the locker room.

Now, Bowers went back to the locker room in the second quarter.
