MMBets Game of the Week: Mavs and Nuggets battle for Group A supremacy

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Dallas Mavericks are set to battle with the Denver Nuggets in an NBA Cup Group play game on Tuesday night. These two teams find themselves at the top of the Western Conference's Group A, and the winner of this contest will very likely secure their spot in the knockout stage of the NBA Cup. Should be an excellent night of hoops, and we've got you covered with our MMBets Game of the Week betting preview.

But before we get into the handicap, lets review last week's result.

(Photo by Michael Gonzales/NBAE via Getty Images)

Not bad at all if I do say so myself! But there is still more work to be done, and winners to hand out. So without further delay, lets dive into the handicap for the Mavericks and the Nuggets.

Dallas Mavericks (10-7) at Denver Nuggets (10-5)

Tipoff: We're set for a late night in Denver, as tipoff will be just after 9:00 PM CT from Ball Arena in Denver, Colorado.

How to watch: This one could be complicated. NBC and Peacock have the national telecast, however the Mavs will follow the Boston Celtics at Washington Wizards game that tips at 7:00 PM. Assuming this runs the typical two-and-a-half hours, Dallas might not be on NBC until much, if not all, of the first quarter is complete. However, KFAA Channel 29 and Mavs TV will have the local broadcast for you, along with 105.3 The Fan on radio.

Odds up to date as of 2:00 PM from the Fanduel Sportsbook

Moneyline: Dallas is +245 to pull off the upset.

(Photo by Garrett Ellwood/NBAE via Getty Images)

The Mavericks and Nuggets matchup offensively very well. Denver has the slashers to take advantage of Dallas' weak paint, and the Mavericks have the same. This is going to be a high scoring game with the stars showing out.

Cooper Flagg under 1.5 three-point makes (-108)

Nikola Jokic to record a triple-double (-105)

Dallas seemingly always is scrappy as an underdog, and we're hoping that trend continues in Denver. The Mavericks are an underdog for the tenth time this season in this game, and they have won four of their previous nine outright. However, I do not expect Dallas to be in the game because of Cooper Flagg warming up from deep. I think PJ Washington does, however, take advantage of some of the attention that Flagg and Kyrie Irving are able to draw. Finally, a Jokic triple double against this Mavericks frontcourt seems like more than a fair ask.
