NFLPA names Raiders DE Kwity Paye their Community MVP for Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The NFL Players Association announced on Friday that they have selected Raiders defensive end Kwity Paye as their Community MVP for Week 12.

Paye is being recognized after hosting a Thanksgiving event for 200 low-income families in the Las Vegas area. Paye’s foundation worked with the Donna Street Community Center to provide those families with turkeys and other items to us for a holiday meal.

“Giving back has always been important to me, and I’m grateful to have a platform that allows me to support families in meaningful ways,” Paye said. “I’m honored to receive this recognition from the NFLPA, and I hope we can continue to make a positive impact long after the holiday season. I’m grateful to everyone who helped make this food drive possible.”

The NFLPA will donate $10,000 to Paye’s foundation and he will join the other weekly MVPs in being eligible for the Alan Page Award at the end of the season.
