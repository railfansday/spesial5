Open Vanguard Digital Advisor if you want a portfolio of Vanguard index funds built, rebalanced, and managed with taxes in mind, for an advisory fee of about $15 to $16 a year per $10,000. Look elsewhere if you’d rather talk to a planner or own other companies’ funds, since it buys only Vanguard funds for IRAs and taxable accounts.

Digital Advisor is Vanguard’s robo-advisor, a service that builds and rebalances a portfolio for you by algorithm. It suits buy-and-hold investors who’d rather answer questions about their goals and risk than pick and rebalance funds themselves.

Hands-off investors who enroll both a taxable account and an IRA get the most for the fee, since it can harvest losses in the first and tries to keep bonds in the second. Investors who want a planner to call should look at Vanguard Personal Advisor, which starts at $50,000 and charges about 0.30% a year.
