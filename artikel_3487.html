GTA 6 Hacker Releases Full Xbox Build, And PC Modders Are Already Trying To Get It Running

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Infamous GTA 6 Leaker Cyberleek resurfaced last week, threatening to release a crucial file for the Xbox build of GTA 6 that could be used to create an unofficial PC port of the game. The hacker originally promised to publicly drop the .exe file for this version of the game depending on whether their memecoin hit a certain market cap, and has been taunting publisher Take-Two Interactive for failing to prevent the game files from leaking online.

"Months of takedowns, subpoenas, and wallet-tracing later, the hunt is still on and the uploads keep arriving," Cyberleek wrote in a now-removed post on Reddit, insisting they were "fighting anti-consumerism" in the video games industry with their actions and using crypto funds to work on a new project. "There is no other way to move funds without names," Cyberleek added. "And this work requires no names."

As pointed out online, Cyberleek has already cashed out on their memecoin in August and again in October, raking in hundreds of thousands of US dollars. So far, it looks like modders are already making modest headway with the full Xbox build of GTA 6, as the Project Leonida Discord server (via Eurogamer) has footage of the game booting up on PC. That's as far as it gets (for now), as that start-up process is met with a black screen shortly afterward. Modders and homebrew enthusiasts are reportedly using Claude AI to reverse-engineer the game on that platform, and the group is confident that a playable build will be ready within a week.

Rockstar has yet to officially announce a PC port of GTA 6, but rumors suggest it could be out in early 2027. This would be a very short gap between console and PC releases for a Rockstar-developed game, as the studio's console-first projects usually spend many months in incubation before heading to PC. The threat of a homebrewed PC port, however, could pressure the developer to release GTA 6 on PC sooner rather than later.

Meanwhile, GTA 6 has done phenomenally well on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S since it launched last week. The game kicked off with 32.5 million copies sold on its first day, and just a few days later, that number reportedly rose to 44.9 million. PS5 sales have dominated, and Sony says GTA 6 was the biggest launch in PlayStation history.
