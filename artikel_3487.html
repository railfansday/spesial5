GTA 6: Don’t Finish Chapter 2 Before Doing These Things

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

GTA 6's first chapter is a lengthy prologue that gets you up to speed on the gameplay, the characters, and the storyline. But once you've cleared the final mission and you start Chapter 2, that's when the game really begins. This is where the protagonists Jason and Lucia cement their decision to go full Bonnie and Clyde, and since this chapter unfolds at a faster pace, you'll want to keep an eye out for a few missable items, people, and missions before you hit the point of no return that leads directly into Chapter 3.

Which mission ends Chapter 2 in GTA 6?

Appropriately, Chapter 2 in GTA 6 ends with the mission Checking Out after you reach the Move to Vice City point on your map, as Jason and Lucia will leave their current residence behind as they embark on a new journey to Vice City. To get there, you'll need to complete the following missions:

  • What's in the Bag?
  • Ambulance Chaser
  • Just Moved In
  • Girls' Trip
  • Moses
  • Daily Driver
  • Slam Dunk
  • Breakfast Run
  • Coffee Break
  • Afternoon Delight
  • Checking Out

Optional missions

There's also a bunch of optional missions you can engage in, in case you want to score some more gear, unlockables, and cash before your Vice City trip:

  • Check Out McKittrick Farm
  • Collect Chemicals for Kyle
  • Hit Avery Pawn
  • Hit Kingpin Bowling
  • Impounded Vehicle
  • Pick Up a Gun
  • Thrillbillies
  • Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab
  • Veteran Hunted (Jason only)

Pay your room rent as quickly as possible

One of your primary goals in Chapter 2 should be settling your debt with the motel manager Linda, as the more you delay paying her, the higher your checkout fee will be. If you purchased a necklace for Lucia in Chapter 1, Linda will hold it as collateral until the debt is settled, so reclaiming that sentimental piece of jewelry should be a priority.

Talk to Wyman

Chatting to Wyman and helping him out with his truck will also earn you an invite for a bit of off-roading fun with two of the missions: Thrillbillies and Twist, Wrist, Smash, and Grab.

Find missing people

While you don't have to tackle the Gellhorn’s Missing Tourist challenge as part of GTA 6's missing people questline during Chapter 2, it is recommended that you get this out of the way early. Starting this quest from Chapter 3 will be more challenging--for spoiler-specific reasons--and you can find those people of interest by checking nearby bulletin boards, posts, and notices.

Play some pool

Before you rob Frankie's Delights, go shoot a few rounds of pool to check off another task in your quest to be the biggest pool shark around.

Grab some collectibles

Several collectibles can be found in the Chapter 2 motel, so before you say goodbye to that location for good, grab 'em and sell 'em to help you reach a 100% completion rate in the game.

Collectible cards

  • Bryson Powers: On top of the washing machine in the motel laundry room
  • Vicki: In the motel reception bathroom, on the floor underneath the toilet
  • Victor Maravella: Check the fridge in the motel cafeteria

Magazines

  • Help Me Help You Help Me: On the motel reception desk
  • Wizard Wang: In the motel cafeteria, next to the radio
