GTA 6 Officially Passes a Massive Sales Milestone Just 1 Week In

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Rockstar’s latest is selling about as well as we all expected

We might have to stop saying something sold like hotcakes and start saying something sold like Grand Theft Auto 6. Rockstar's latest has sold about as well as we expected, with estimates after only one week putting sales at over 50 million copies. That number will only go up in the weeks and months to come, especially if a new GTA Online mode and a PC version are eventually announced, as rumored.

According to estimates from Alinea Analytics, GTA 6 has sold 53 million copies in the week following the game's release on Nov. 19. That equates to $4.7 billion in revenue. By comparison, GTA 5 only sold 29 million copies in its entire first month. (It has since gone on to sell more than 230 million copies.) Other stats from the report reveal that nearly 7 million players have logged more than 50 hours in the game, with around 250,000 logging more than 100 hours.

The report also states that GTA 6 had an impact on sales for some of 2026's other major releases. On PlayStation, sales of several games (including 007 First Light​​​​​​​, Resident Evil Requiem, and Marvel's Wolverine) fell 34% during the week of GTA 6's release. ​​​​​​​Alinea Analytics points out​​​​​​​ that this isn't all GTA 6's fault, as typical sales decay for these titles was also in full swing. Interestingly, sales did actually go up slightly in the week before the game's release, possibly because some players​​​​​​​ ​​​​​​​bought a PS5 and picked up a game or two​​​​​​​ to hold them over until GTA 6's release.​​​​​​​

Of the more than 50 million copies of GTA 6 sold, more than 75% were sold for PlayStation 5.​​​​​​​ The report also shows that Xbox sales have continued to decline​​​​​​​ in the days following the game's release. This suggests​​​​​​​ that Rockstar and PlayStation's marketing deal has been a massive success. Prior to the game's release, GTA 6 had been heavily promoted as playing best​​​​​​​ on Sony's console. Granted, PlayStation does currently hold roughly 75% of the console market, making GTA 6 sales roughly proportional to its market share​​​​​​​. Fresh off the success of GTA 6 on PlayStation 5, Sony has already struck a similar marketing deal with CD Projekt Red for future releases, including The Witcher 4.

PC players have been left out of the fun so far, with no official word on a PC release for GTA 6. That hasn't stopped hackers from trying to make their own, with builds making speedy progress after an Xbox executable file was leaked to the public, kick-starting a race to create an unofficial PC port​​​​​​​. Hopefully, we'll hear official word on a PC release and multiplayer sometime in 2027.​​​​​​​

Grand Theft Auto 6 is now available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X.
