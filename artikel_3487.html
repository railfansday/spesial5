Sony's Bringing Back Spider-Man $2.5 Billion Hit With New Footage

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Sony's $2.5 billion superhero hit isn't done dominating movie theaters yet

As Marvel Studios' release of Avengers Endgame: Encore proves, blockbuster movies can still dominate the box office, even after a multibillion dollar run. Sony is planning a rerelease of Spider-Man: Brand New Day to bring web-head-heads back to movie theaters — and like Endgame: Encore, the studio is adding new footage of Spider-Man's latest flick as an incentive.

Thanks to AMC Theatres, we now know what that rerelease will be called: Spider-Man: (Brand New) Brand New Day.

According to the AMC Theatres listing, Spider-Man: (Brand New) Brand New Day will be seven minutes long, with a run time of 2 hours 32 minutes. It's unclear how much of that will be new or restored footage, but it will be longer than Brand New Day's original theatrical run.

Spider-Man: (Brand New) Brand New Day is expected to arrive in movie theaters on Dec. 4.

According to a previous report from Deadline, the new footage will include at least one cut cameo from Spider-Man: Brand New Day that connects to the larger Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Deadline reports that Sony's planned theatrical rerelease of Spider-Man: Brand New Day will feature extra footage that includes an appearance from Rosario Dawson's character Claire Temple, aka Night Nurse. Dawson played Temple in multiple Netflix Marvel shows: Daredevil, Jessica Jones, Luke Cage, Iron Fist, and The Defenders. The Night Nurse had her own Marvel comic series in the early '70s, in which she tended to the medical needs of New York City's vigilante crime fighters (though that version of the character was named Linda Carter).

Dawson has said in interviews that she filmed a scene that was cut from the theatrical release of Spider-Man: Brand New Day. Deadline's report implies Sony has more than just a Claire Temple cameo in its pocket, saying there will be "more surprises in the extra footage."

If accurate, this wouldn't be the first time Sony has rereleased one of its Tom Holland-starring Spider-Man movies in theaters. The studio released an extended cut of Spider-Man: Far From Home and a "More Fun Stuff Version" of Spider-Man: No Way Home, the latter of which featured deleted scenes.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day's rerelease will no doubt help goose the film's box office haul, which currently stands at $2.52 billion, according to Box Office Mojo. Brand New Day is the highest-grossing movie at the domestic box office to date, with $959 million after two months in theaters. It's also the third-highest-grossing movie worldwide, behind Avengers: Endgame and the original Avatar. It's unlikely the rerelease would put Brand New Day on top; it's still $419 million behind Avengers: Endgame.
