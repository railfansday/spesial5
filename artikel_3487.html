Will Ferrell Golf Comedy ‘The Hawk’ Canceled at Netflix After Just One Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

“The Hawk,” a Will Ferrell golf comedy that debuted just this summer, has been canceled by Netflix and will not return for a second season, an individual with knowledge told IndieWire.

Ferrell co-created the series in which he plays a former top golfer who is now trying to return to the tour. And while it figured to be a tentpole series for Netflix, one that carried possibly the most expensive budget for a comedy series on Netflix and reunited Ferrell with his “SNL” co-star Molly Shannon and co-creator Harper Steele, it didn’t connect with critics or audiences.

“The Hawk” spent just three weeks on Netflix’s Top 10 when it debuted back in July, topping out at #2 in its second week on Netflix with just 6.2 million views. Ferrell’s vulgar, loudly-dressed golfer with a personalized visor and big mess of bleached blonde hair played a lot like some of Ferrell’s other string of sports comedies throughout the years. Steele, in a recent appearance on “Late Night with Seth Meyers,” tipped her hand a little that the show was likely ending when she let slip that, “I was working on a short-lived but probably the best show ever, ‘The Hawk.’”

The cancellation comes in the wake of a changing of the guard at Netflix, with Peter Rice taking over for veteran creative chief Bela Bajaria. Our critic also recently wrote about the challenges Netflix is facing right now in getting audiences to discover new series in the ways they used to.

But at the same time, Netflix has already renewed a couple of other comedy series that even premiered after “The Hawk” later this year, including “Nobody Wants This,” “A Different World,” “Tires,” “Big Mistakes,” and “The Four Seasons.” Netflix is also still working with Ferrell’s Gloria Sanchez Productions on three new series, “All the Rage,” “Prep,” and another scripted series from Nikki Glaser.
