T-Mobile is giving away the iPhone 18 Pro for free this weekend — how to qualify

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

TL;DR: This Black Friday, the Apple iPhone 18 Pro is free when you switch to T-Mobile and bring your number on a qualifying T-Mix plan.

Black Friday deals don't actually wait for Black Friday. Instead, major retailers and mobile carriers drop heaps of early discounts throughout November — which is great news if you desperately need a tech upgrade without the wait.

Why wait until Nov. 27 when T-Mobile is already offering one of the strongest mobile promos of the season?

We’ve rounded up all the best Black Friday deals from T-Mobile, but the clear standout is a limited-time offer that gets you the Apple iPhone 18 Pro for free (100% off its $1,199 list price). Apple rarely offers direct cash discounts on current-generation Pro models during Cyber Week, and certainly never takes $1,199 off an unlocked handset. That makes this early offer is best way to upgrade right now.

T-Mobile keeps things surprisingly flexible when it comes to qualifying for this deal:

Switch without a trade-in: Port your existing phone number from an eligible rival carrier to a qualifying T-Mix plan, and T-Mobile covers the full cost of the phone.

Trade in a phone in any condition: Already a T-Mobile customer or adding a new line without porting a number? You can still get the iPhone 18 Pro for free by trading in an eligible phone in any condition on a qualifying T-Mix plan.

T-Mobile distributes the full discount as 36 monthly bill credits. If you leave T-Mobile or cancel your line before that three-year payment plan wraps up, the credits stop immediately, and you will owe the remaining balance on the device.

You also won’t walk out of the checkout process without paying anything. You are still required to pay local sales tax on the phone's full $1,199.00 retail value upfront, plus T-Mobile's standard $35 device connection fee per line at checkout. You can't escape those upfront charges, even on "free" Black Friday phone promos.

Score the Apple iPhone 18 Pro for free this weekend with T-Mobile.
