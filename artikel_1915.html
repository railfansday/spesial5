Ex-refs chief hits back at Mauricio Pochettino as Bryan Mbeumo accused of 'cheating' vs Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Mauricio Pochettino is fighting a losing battle after stating that Manchester United deserved a penalty in their 4-0 defeat to Liverpool.

That is according to former PGMOL chief and ex-FIFA referee, Keith Hackett, who exclusively told Football Insider that Bryan Mbeumo attempted to con the referee into awarding a spot-kick.

Two minutes before Leny Yoro was sent off at Anfield on Sunday, 22 November, the Red Devils had a penalty shout of their own at the other end.

Mbeumo, who had burst into the Liverpool box, threw himself to the floor under pressure from Jeremy Jacquet, who went shoulder-to-shoulder with the attacker.

Sam Barrott waved away the shouts, with VAR refusing to get involved either, and then Man United self-imploded near their own penalty box.

After the game, Pochettino was absolutely adamant that Mbeumo should have been awarded a spot-kick, but very few appear to agree with him.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Hackett, instead of taking Pochettino's side, has accused Mbeumo of an "exaggerated fall".

"I don't think that's enough for a penalty for me. I think the referee got it right. What creates the debate, if you like, is Mbeumo's exaggerated fall," he said.

"Players have just got to go down naturally and not exaggerate. So for me, I I didn't see that as a penalty kick decision."

When asked if Pochettino was "clutching at straws," he added: "He'll probably receive, at some stage in the coming weeks, operational advice on what he can and cannot say."

Such split-second decisions can have major impacts on a game at this level. Luckily for Barrott, it seems he made the right one on this occasion.

Yoro's red card, undoubtedly, changed the game completely, with Liverpool able to stroll comfortably to a 4-0 win at home.

There has been a huge debate over the decision, and Hackett is not entirely sure that sending the defender off was the right call.

It was a subjective one, and that's exactly why the former boss of the PGMOL believes that Man United have a case to appeal the red card.

It remains to be seen if the Red Devils are going to bother, but the news would certainly be a big boost, with the team currently in the relegation zone.
