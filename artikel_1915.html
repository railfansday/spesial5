Harry Kane injury: Bayern Munich and England striker facing ‘months’ out

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Harry Kane is facing several months out after suffering an ankle ligament injury during Bayern Munich’s Bundesliga game on Friday night.

The England captain was forced off after just 20 minutes following a challenge from defender Joel Schmied during Bayern’s 1-1 draw with FC Koln.

The 33-year-old, who won the Ballon d’Or last month, initially tried to carry on before he limped off the pitch alongside Bayern’s medical staff.

Bayern issued an update after the game which read: ‘Harry Kane suffered a tear of the syndesmosis ligament in his left ankle in Bayern Munich’s Bundesliga home match against 1. FC Koln on Friday evening.

‘This was confirmed by an examination by Bayern Munich’s medical department. The 33-year-old will therefore be out for the next few months.’

Bayern have seven more fixtures until their winter break in December. The German champions resume their Bundesliga campaign on January 9 and are also due to play Manchester United in the Champions League on January 20.

‘We have to do it without Harry, we have to do it without these guys,’ said Bayern head coach Vincent Kompany.

‘They are world-class players. You can’t replace them. But we have the mentality and we have to bring that.’

Kane may also face a race to be fit for England’s Nations League quarter-final against Norway at the end of March.
