Rangers to trigger James Penrice transfer 'sooner rather than later'

Rangers are planning to trigger their buy option for James Penrice as soon as possible, sources have told Football Insider.

The 27-year-old has established himself as a crucial part of Derek McInnes squad since his move on loan from AEK Athens in the summer, and could be set to stay permanently.

His involvement in the most recent Scotland squad under Sebastien Pocognoli is a testament to what he has achieved at Ibrox, and the club are delighted by what they have seen.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Rangers are keen to end any speculation about Penrice's future by securing a long-term deal.

The left-back has an option to buy included in his loan deal at Ibrox which is believed to be worth just £800,000, and could be a bargain signing for Rangers in the near future.

Having made himself a fans' favourite at Ibrox, as well as a favourite of McInnes', Penrice is now increasingly likely to make his stay in Glasgow a permanent one.

Football Insider revealed back in September that Rangers would step up their efforts to keep Penrice at the club following an outstanding start to his loan spell this season.

The left-back has quickly become an important part of this Rangers side, and will play a key role in any success they enjoy as they continue to hunt major silverware.

As a result, they are keen to keep Penrice at the club for the foreseeable future, far beyond the terms of his current loan spell from AEK Athens.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Yeah, a permanent move to Rangers is still the most likely outcome for James Penrice here.

"He's been an important part of this Rangers team so far this season as well, and has earned a call-up to the Scotland squad as a result.

"They've got the option to make the loan move permanent, and I think obviously there's a willingness from Rangers to get that done.

"Derek McInnes has spoken about that. He's a huge fan of Penrice and knows he will play a key role in any success his side have moving forward long-term now.

"So there is a willingness to get a deal done, and they'll look to get it done sooner rather than later, because it'll be a big deal for Rangers to try and secure the future of Penrice long term."

Rangers completed the signing of Penrice on loan in the summer, a deal which may saw as a shrewd one given his relationship with McInnes, who holds him in high regard.

Shortly after the deal was announced, it was revealed that the club would have the option to sign him permanently if he impressed, and they are now likely to take that up.

Football Insider previously revealed that Rangers believe they have landed a major bargain with Penrice, whose final transfer fee will be short of £1million.

Sources say those behind the scenes at Ibrox are delighted with the deal, and the fee is seen as insignificant compared to the performances he has shown this season.

Should this form continue, as it looks like it will, it will be a no-brainer for Rangers to trigger the buy option, and they are expected to do so soon.
