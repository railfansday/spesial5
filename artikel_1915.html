Matthijs de Ligt to be 'first out' at Man United as Mauricio Pochettino overhaul begins - Transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Matthijs de Ligt cannot remain a first-choice option for Manchester United when he continues to make glaring errors.

The 27-year-old has faced questions about his long-term furture at Old Trafford as a result of his injury issues, but was thrust into Mauricio Pochettino's first starting XI.

While the manager has faced some backlash and confusion over what proved to be a misguided decisions, there are now calls for the centre-back to be left out again.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that De Ligt does not possess any attributes that would make him a regular starter for Pochettino's side.

A lack of top-class defenders has left the manager in a difficult position, but after it has become clear that he is not up to scratch, the Netherlands international must be left out.

Plans can then be put in place to replace De Ligt, whether it happens in January or next summer, as the Red Devils plan an overhaul of their back line in the transfer window.

Former Tottenham scout Bryan King told Football Insider on 23 November that Pochettino will have to overhaul the XI that started against Liverpool last time out.

Major changes will have to take place under the new manager, who has been given an illustration of why Michael Carrick struggled to get results out of this side.

De Ligt is one of a few players who have come under fire since the defeat, but amid longer-term questions about his future in the side, he could be cast out of Man United.

"I couldn't believe Pochettino threw De Ligt straight back into the starting XI," Brown told Football Insider.

"I don't know whether he was poorly advised or just wanted to try something different, but he was never going to be ready to step straight in and make a real difference.

"It was partly his mistake that led to Yoro getting sent off, and he looked miles off the standard you would expect from a Man United defender.

"He cannot be a regular in this side, it's as simple as that, there were doubts about him before he got injured and he's only just coming back from months on the sidelines.

"It's a difficult situation for Pochettino, because where does he turn? It's clear something has got to change, because they've been shocking defensively.

"De Ligt cannot continue in that back line, there's doubts about Martinez, Yoro has made too many mistakes, Heaven is still only young, the only one I'd trust is Harry Maguire.

"It's a position that's going to need serious investment, because there are four or five players who need replacing, and I think De Ligt has to be one of the first cast out."

Man United have got the worst defensive record in Europe's top five leagues this season, and have been left scrambling to make changes and improve their back line.

Given the limited options available to Pochettino, there have been calls for a number of regular starters to be axed, as it has become clear that something has got to change.

Football Insider reported on 23 November that Lisandro Martinez should be dropped from Pochettino's starting XI after what was a dismal showing against Liverpool.

This has been a terrible season overall for the Argentina centre-back, with error-ridden performances leading to questions about his long-term future at the club.

Leny Yoro, meanwhile, has also cast doubts over his position in the XI after picking up a red card at Anfield, leaving Pochettino with very few reliable options to call upon.
