Most capped Scotland players 2026: Here are top 10 most capped Scottish footballers of all time

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Here are Scotland’s top 10 most capped footballers - including Andy Robertson, Scott McTominay and John McGinn.

Scotland return to Nations League action this week with games against North Macedonia and Switzerland - and it could prove to be an extremely special week for captain Andy Robertson.

Dating back to 1872, the Scottish men’s national football team have been represented by over 1,250 players since their first official international match against England at Hamilton Crescent in Partick, Glasgow.

However, only 45 players have reached the milestone of 50 appearances for the men’s team, earning a spot on the Scottish FA Roll of Honour, while even fewer have reached a century of caps for the nation - one of them being Robertson, who could become Scotland’s most-capped men’s player of all time if he features in the upcoming double header.

However, it is worth noting that none of the following 10 can match that of Scottish women’s football icons Gemmy Fay and Jo Love, Scotland’s two most capped footballers ever on 203 and 191 caps, respectively.
