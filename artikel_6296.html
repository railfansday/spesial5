The best rewards card for your wallet is usually one that rewards you with more of what you really enjoy. For those who love to travel, that might mean earning airline miles or hotel points to help fund the next trip.

But what if what you really love is sports?

The soon-to-be-released Fanatics American Express® Card is being designed specifically around sports fandom, rewarding fans with more of what they already love — from team merch and tickets to collectibles and more.

While the card isn't yet available for applications, we know that the upcoming Fanatics American Express Card will earn rewards in the form of FanCash, Fanatics' loyalty currency.

TPG Tip: You can join the waitlist now to be among the first to get the new Fanatics American Express Card when it is released.

FanCash is simple: $1 in FanCash equals $1 toward eligible purchases across the Fanatics ecosystem, from team merch on Fanatics.com to tickets in the Fanatics App and purchases on Topps.com. In eligible states, it can also be redeemed for rewards like bonus bets in Fanatics Sportsbook & Casino.

So whether you want your team's newest jersey, a collectible from a Fanatics auction or tickets to your team's next game, you'll have plenty of ways to put your FanCash to use.

When it comes to earning FanCash, here's what the new Fanatics American Express Card will offer:

And when you use the new card in the Fanatics App, you can stack rewards to earn 8% or more on purchases in the form of FanCash. That FanCash can then go toward things like apparel, tickets, trading cards, collectibles and event experiences, such as the fan favorite Fanatics Fest.

The card will have no annual fee** and will offer new cardmembers $100 in bonus FanCash after spending $1,000 on purchases within the first 90 days.***

Cardmembers can earn another $100 in bonus FanCash after spending at least $500 per month on eligible purchases for 12 consecutive months.†

Earning rewards on your spending is great, but the Fanatics American Express Card is also designed to give cardmembers something even harder for serious sports fans to get: access.

For example, cardmembers will get early presale access to select Topps releases, which could make the difference between getting your hands on something you want and missing out entirely.§

Cardmembers will also receive elevated Fanatics ONE Member Pro status, which includes 50% off ticket fees on eligible ticket purchases in the Fanatics app.‡

And while you'll get Member Pro status simply by having the card, your card can also help you make progress toward even higher Fanatics ONE status levels — and unlock additional perks along the way.

Cardmembers will receive 2,500 tier points and earn an additional 10 tier points for every $50 in card purchases, helping them work toward higher status levels that can unlock additional perks, such as tickets to Fanatics Fest, access to special events, bonus FanCash, a Fanatics ONE concierge and more.‡

With all the perks and rewards it comes with, it shouldn’t be a shocker that the Fanatics American Express Card was going to look cool, too.

And in fact, it’s almost a collectible of sorts on its own. You'll have 500+ card art options to choose from, featuring athletes across the NBA, NFL, WWE and UFC, with a unique Topps-inspired design that lets you rep your fandom every time you pull out your card.

The new Fanatics American Express Card won't just get you access to select drops and earn FanCash. Since the card is powered by the American Express Network, cardmembers will have access to Amex Offers, Amex Experiences™ — with opportunities like ticket presales — and built-in retail protections when you use the card.

There are a lot of ways the new Fanatics American Express Card stands out, but here's a truly unusual card perk.

Fanatics cardmembers will have the chance to participate in what Fanatics calls the Championship Challenge. Correctly predict the champions of the MLB, NBA, NFL and NHL, and you could get up to $100,000 of your annual spending on the Fanatics American Express Card back when the challenge launches.

That means you can put your sports knowledge to the test and have some fun following your picks throughout the year — even if you don't manage to correctly predict all four champions.

But if you do somehow nail all four? Getting up to $100,000 of your annual card spending back would certainly qualify as one of the most exciting credit card perks we've ever seen.

There are plenty of credit cards that sports fans could use, but very few are designed specifically for sports fandom.

Fanatics set out to create what it calls the “ultimate card for sports fans,” and they have certainly packed in a lot aimed squarely at that large audience.

Between early presale access to Topps releases, the ability to earn FanCash on everyday purchases and a path toward higher levels of Fanatics ONE status — not to mention the fun upcoming Championship Challenge — there are some genuinely unique perks packed into this no-annual-fee card.

If this card has your name (and favorite player) on it, know that it isn’t available quite yet, but it is coming soon.

If you want to be at the front of the line for the new card, you can join the waitlist now to be among the first to know when the Fanatics American Express Card launches.
