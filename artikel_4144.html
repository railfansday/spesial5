PSA: Splatoon Raiders Demo Splashes Onto The Switch 2 eShop Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Splatoon Raiders made quite the splash when it arrived on the Switch 2 earlier this year, and if you haven't already had a chance to try it out, there's now a demo.

You can download this demo from the eShop, and it comes loaded with three stages set in the Spirhalite Islands, three preselected loadouts, and reunites you with the crew from Deep Cut.

Of course, this title is somewhat different to the mainline experience. It's more of a story-driven experience, where you work your way through stages and waves of enemies to progress to the next stage and unlock more weapons, upgrades and content. It also offers co-op multiplayer, if you do want to team up with family and friends.

According to Nintendo's latest financial sales data, Splatoon Raiders has now sold 2.42 million copies, placing it in the top-ten best-selling games on the Switch 2. We awarded it an "excellent" 9/10 stars when it arrived in July, noting how it offers one of the most enjoyable gameplay loops on Nintendo's new system.

In some related news today, Splatoon 3 has been updated to Version 12.0.0. It includes changes to multiplayer and much more. You can find out more in our previous story. The last game update for Splatoon Raiders was in August.
