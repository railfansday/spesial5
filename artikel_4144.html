Best Wonderkids to Sign In FM 27

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The yearly search for the best young players in all of football continues as we take you through FM 27's best wonderkids for each position.

Finding the next big thing in Football Manager 27 is a huge part of any save, and while it's much easier to identify and sign wonderkids when you're managing the best clubs in the world, it's a fun hunt at any level.

We're going to run through the best wonderkid per position in FM 27, while also flagging a few honorable mentions. This list is based on average potential ability in the database, but note that players with similar potential ability ratings may peak differently depending on how their career goes in the game. No player is guaranteed to hit their potential, which is the inherent risk of signing wonderkids if you can't develop them properly.

This name will be familiar to many FM veterans, as Dennis Seimen has been one of the best young goalkeepers in the game for years, but he's arguably the best wonderkid in the position in FM 27. While there is another goalkeeper with a slightly higher potential ability, across multiple saves we've seen Seimen outgrow and outperform the closest rivals for this spot.

When you boot up a new save, he's playing for VfB Stuttgart II in the Bundesliga and will cost you anywhere between £50-80m to sign. He starts the game injured for up to four months with hamstring problems, which could affect your ability to sign him straight away.

It's been a long, hard road back to success for Sports Interactive, but FM 27 is the game I hoped it would be and so much more.

There's a much bigger gap between Barcelona's Pau Cubarsi and the rest of the pack than among the goalkeepers, as he is comfortably the best wonderkid at CB. In the saves we've played so far, he's always the best CB in the world within one or two seasons, and if you do happen to find him as part of your team, your only job is to keep him healthy, and you'll be rock solid at the back as a result.

Age, contract status, recent transfers, and injuries may be reasons why not every wonderkid on this list is signable in the first season.

Good luck getting anywhere near signing him, though, because he starts the game with a value close to £300m. Maybe start with Man City and get a few more completely normal sponsorships to help fund the transfer?

Next up at RB is Daniel Banjaqui, and just like Pau Cubarsi, he's game-ready from day one. Granted, he's much cheaper, costing around £28m at the start of the game, but it's money well spent if you can tempt him away from Benfica.

His mentals and physicals are astonishing for someone just 18 years old, and while his technical skills need some work, it won't take long to get him up to speed with a world-class coaching team.

Up next is a player we can admit to having never heard of before, but that may come down to age and location more than anything. Saba Kharebashvili plays for Basaksehir FK and is only 17, with a value of up to £5m when a new save starts. He has a relatively low starting ability compared to a lot of the players on this list, but on paper it doesn't show, as he's fairly competent across all statistical categories.

When it comes to potential, however, he's clear of any other LB/LWB in the game, so he's well worth trying to snatch up when available.

While he may have a rather funny name, there's nothing to laugh about when it comes to Nathan De Cat's stats. He starts off a new save at Hoffenheim at age 17 with a value of up to £52m. Already capped by Belgium, De Cat is a steady defensive midfielder and can go on to be one of the best players in all of FM if he hits his potential.

He's a much more proficient defensive midfielder than a center midfielder, which is why he's only an honorable mention on the list for CM, but given how many tactics rely on that anchor midfielder to protect your back line, this kid is a real must-have in FM 27.

Starting the save at Villarreal and contracted through to 2031, Carlos Macia is a baller at 17 and difficult to tempt away from Spain, but it's well worth trying to sign him given his massive upside.

The CM position is hotly contested when it comes to potential ability, but Macia almost always ended up as one of the best in the game in each of the saves we've played so far, and often finds his way to a Spanish giant in Barcelona or Real Madrid relatively quickly.

One of the older players on this list at 19, Estevao starts a new save at Chelsea with a value of up to £65m and some striking technical and physical attributes. Where he needs to improve is his mental ability, especially his determination, which can be make-or-break for some wonderkids.

Arguably the biggest risk on this list because we have seen saves where he turns into a bit of a dud in terms of performance and G/A return; he's still classed as a wonderkid for now, and it's up to you to get the best out of him.

I mean, this is the obvious one, right? Yamal is still the biggest wonderkid in FM, despite starting the game as one of the best players in the world. Yes, he can get better!

Lamine Yamal starts a new save with no value, classed as Not for Sale but in truth, it's because no club could afford the fee. We've rarely seen Yamal leave Barcelona in saves, and if he does, it tends to be later in his career as his production winds down. So, if you want to know what it's like to have Yamal on your team, the best way to do that is by starting as Barcelona.

Starting with a value of up to £150m, having just joined Real Madrid, Yan Diomande is the leading wonderkid on the left wing, thanks to Lamine Yamal being a very one-sided player.

While you won't be able to secure Diomande's services for at least a year since he just joined Madrid, he's one to keep an eye on given his current and potential ability, plus his ability to play on both wings. Before you ask, yes, we've seen teams sign both Diomande and Yamal, and it's basically a cheat code.

Finally, we close out this list with a player you've seen listed three times already as an honorable mention. That's right, Spurs fans, your 17-year-old wonderkid is a contender in FOUR positions.

The next Harry Kane in waiting, Williams-Barnett can start a new save for as little as £3.5m, but the reality is that Spurs will always ask for considerably more. His starting attributes aren't overly impressive, including very poor determination, but at 17, there is plenty of time to grow, and his progression will depend on where you choose to play him.
