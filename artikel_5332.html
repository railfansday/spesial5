So much for the rally: The Nasdaq drops as Nvidia slides and Anthropic falls below its IPO price

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Concerns over what the AI buildout costs and whether it can last came back into focus, along with the possibility of new tariffs

John Lamparski / Bloomberg via Getty Images

A selloff in tech stocks that had reversed to great fanfare on Monday resumed in force on Tuesday, with the Nasdaq $NDAQ Composite falling 2% before closing the day down 1.5%.

Nvidia $NVDA stock dropped about 6% on Tuesday, a day after Jensen Huang's reinstatement as CEO lifted the AI chipmaker's shares 31%, and powered a broad market rally. Adding to the tech woes on Tuesday, Anthropic stock fell more than 6% to less than $82 per share, below its $83 IPO price.

The S&P 500 ended the day off 0.5%, while the Dow Jones Industrial Average 423 points on the day, or 0.8%.

The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index lost 7.6% on Tuesday, leaving it 40% under the high it reached in June. Micron $MU Technology stock fell more than 10%, Advanced Micro Devices dropped 9% and Broadcom $AVGO lost 3%. Technology was the weakest sector in the S&P 500, down 3%, while health care firms and makers of household necessities held up best.

Concerns over what the AI buildout costs and whether it can last came back into focus on Tuesday, according to Bloomberg. The Trump administration is preparing a new round of tariffs to replace levies the Supreme Court struck down, with an announcement possible as soon as Tuesday.

"Nothing has significantly changed from Monday, but we're not out of the woods yet," said Adam Phillips, who is managing director of portfolio strategy at EP Wealth Advisors, said, according to Bloomberg. "There is still a whole lot of fear and uncertainty around the AI trade that needs to be worked through."

Nvidia reinstated Huang as CEO on Monday, less than two weeks after he resigned following revelations of multiple affairs, including one with a woman suspected of being a Chinese intelligence asset. Nvidia said a committee of independent directors is still investigating reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, and that the inquiry will proceed to conclusion. Huang supports the investigation and is committed to cooperating with it, the company said.

Huang apologized to employees at an all-hands meeting on Tuesday and told them he is in therapy.
