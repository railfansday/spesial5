ExperienceFlow Launches DATS, Signs $500 Million Partnership MoU with FPT to Transform Fortune 500 Enterprises into Super Intelligence-Native Businesses

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The new Domain AI Transformation Solution (DATS) lets Consulting Firms, System Integrators, and BPOs infuse their proprietary expertise with ExperienceFlow Super Intelligence models, on their own premises. Alwyn Joseph joins as Chief Domain Transformation Officer to lead the domain and vertical solutions business.

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- ExperienceFlow today launched the Domain AI Transformation Solution (DATS), a domain modeling platform that turns consulting firms, system integrators, and business process outsourcers (BPOs) into Super Intelligence-based domain experts, extending expert teams' capacity.

ExperienceFlow also named FPT Software its first DATS Super Intelligence Diamond partner. The companies have signed a $500 million Partnership MoU to transform Fortune 500 enterprises into Super Intelligence-native businesses. Alwyn Joseph, ExperienceFlow's new Chief Domain Transformation Officer, signed the MoU with Phuong Dang, Senior Executive Vice President of FPT Software.

Domain expertise that scales without leaking

Domain expertise within firms has long grown in step with headcount. Most generative AI approaches promise to break that link, but they require firms to send proprietary knowledge, prompts and client data to external models, where their hardest-won IP leaks into systems they don't control.

Launching DATS Center of Excellence and Industry Super Intelligence Transformation Diamond Partnership

ExperienceFlow is launching the ExperienceFlow Industry Super Intelligence Transformation Diamond Partnership, an initiative for a select group of consulting, systems integrators and BPO firms that want to transform enterprises into AI-Native businesses.

"We hold a fundamentally different belief from the GenAI companies trying to eliminate software, consulting, systems integration and BPO companies," said Giri Athuluru, CEO and co-founder of ExperienceFlow AI. "Domain expertise is irreplaceable, and these partners are more essential to the future than ever. DATS is the first platform that lets them accelerate the differentiation of their expertise, keep it on their own premises, and use it to make their clients Super Intelligence-native. In the era of Super Intelligence, our DATS Center of Excellence hardware and software package enables significant new growth trajectory for Consulting Firms, System Integrators and BPOs that beats the alternative market view painted by GenAI companies, which are commoditizing and are planning to subsume the $1.5T services market."

FPT Software: Founding Industry Super Intelligence Transformation Diamond Partner

Under the MoU, FPT and ExperienceFlow will jointly build industry specific Super Intelligence models for Fortune 500 enterprises, combining FPT's domain expertise and delivery scale with ExperienceFlow's Super Intelligence platform to move clients from AI pilots to continually learning, outcome-driven operations.

"AI will not replace the services industry. It will divide it, and the firms that lead will be the ones that change how their clients' businesses run. FPT brings three decades of domain expertise and enterprise transformation experience to build industry-specific solutions on ExperienceFlow's goal-seeking, continually learning Super Intelligence platform. Together, we will transform how enterprises make decisions, adapt and grow, making them Super Intelligence-native and delivering significant, measurable business outcomes. Across banking and financial services, insurance, supply chain and high technology, we intend to show the world's largest enterprises what that looks like in practice," said Phuong Tran, Senior EVP, FPT Software.

Alwyn Joseph joins as Chief Domain Transformation Officer

Alwyn Joseph leads ExperienceFlow's DATS business and owns its P&L, with responsibility for building, commercializing, scaling the company's domain and vertical solutions and its strategic domain partnerships. He has spent more than two decades building and scaling technology businesses, as President & COO, Deputy CEO, Chief Revenue Officer and board member at public and private companies. In earlier roles, he has transformed businesses to drive growth, expanded into new markets, scaled revenue and profitability over tenfold, through organic growth and strategic acquisition. Most recently he was President & COO of Sasken Technologies, a public engineering and technology company

"The firms that know industries best are the ones who should lead the transformation," said Joseph. "DATS gives our partners AI models built from their own expertise: models they own and continually improve. That is how a $1.5 trillion industry expands from delivering projects to delivering business outcomes, led by the firms that built it."
