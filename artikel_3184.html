Netflix Renews Unscripted Show ‘Physical: Asia’ For Second Season As Franchise Expands

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Netflix has confirmed that unscripted show Physical: Asia, which pits teams from different Asian nations against each other in high-stakes quests inspired by local cultures, has been confirmed for a second season, due to start streaming in 2027.

One of Netflix’s first unscripted Asian shows, the first season of Physical: Asia premiered in October 2025 and charted in the streamer’s Global Top 10 for three consecutive seasons.

Other international versions have been rolled out including Physical 100: Mexico and Physical 100: Italy, which each spent two consecutive weeks in Netflix’s Global Top 10 after release. Physical 100: Mexico has been renewed for a second season and U.S., French and Swedish versions are also in the works. Netflix Director of Content Kihwan Yoo said: “What began as a competition series created in Korea has grown into a global franchise, and that is incredibly meaningful to us. The strength of the Physical series lies in its universal appeal. At the same time, I believe its high production quality plays a key role in drawing audiences into the format so quickly. Seeing new versions of the series being created in countries like Italy and Mexico, each bringing its own cultural identity to the format, shows the incredible creative potential of Korean unscripted formats on the global stage.” Watch on Deadline

Yoo added that the second season would include even stronger nations and tougher competitors: “Through this, viewers will witness a truly ‘unbreakable’ spirit and extraordinary mental strength. The mutual respect, determination to push boundaries, and fierce competitive drive that emerge along the way will make this season even more special.”

Producer Jang Ho-gi said: “I want to sincerely thank our viewers for embracing the fierce passion and pride of athletes competing for their countries, as well as the incredible drama they created as teams pushing past individual limits.”

Jang also teased some elements of the second season: “We have designed an entirely different world from previous installments and developed quests capable of becoming new signatures for the Physical series. Beyond simply expanding the scale, we aim to showcase fresh strategies, matchups, and unpredictable outcomes unique to a nation-versus-nation format.”
