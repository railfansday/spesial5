GTA 6 Ties for the Best Reviewed Game of All Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Scott Baird is a Contributor from the UK with over a decade of video game writing experience.

Rockstar has finally proven the doubters wrong, as Grand Theft Auto VI is officially tied for the best-reviewed video game of all time, with only Nintendo’s greatest retro title managing to keep up.

Expectations for Grand Theft Auto VI were sky-high going in, especially after being delayed on multiple occasions, with Take-Two and Rockstar promising that the game needed more polish and would be worth it in the end. There were some doubters, but the fact that Take-Two was leaving money on the table meant that most fans had faith it would live up to expectations.

The embargo for Grand Theft Auto VI went up as soon as the game went live on New Zealand’s Microsoft Store, and the scores are in. It seems that Rockstar is ending 2026 with a bang, as Grand Theft Auto VI has obliterated the competition, at least in terms of reviewer opinion.

The Metacritic page for Grand Theft Auto VI is live, and the results are in. Grand Theft Auto VI’s MetaScore is 99, making it tied for the best-reviewed game of all time, with a huge range of 10/10s from outlets worldwide.

One outlet that gave Grand Theft Auto VI a 10/10 is DualShockers, with Steve Hennley writing, “While it may seem impossible for anything in our universe to live up to the hype and anticipation Grand Theft Auto 6 has garnered, Rockstar has done the impossible. GTA VI offers more in a single package than any video game before it, blowing past its status as one of the best action games of all time into one of the best life-sims of all time.”

Grand Theft Auto VI’s MetaScore is 99, making it tied for the best-reviewed game of all time, with a huge range of 10/10s from outlets worldwide.

The only game to match Grand Theft Auto VI’s MetaScore is the original version of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the Nintendo 64, which also has a 99. Funnily enough, the game’s remake came out this year, and it scores slightly lower than Grand Theft Auto VI, capping out at a 96 on Metacritic.

That being said, a trip to Hyrule might be the only option for a lot of people, as Grand Theft Auto VI is currently a PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S exclusive. This means that Nintendo Switch 2 and PC owners will have to find out games to play. Maybe it’s time to break out Garfield Kart and pretend he’s running over civilians?

It was always likely that Grand Theft Auto VI would score well, considering Rockstar’s amazing library of titles and the sheer amount of time and money that went into its latest game. The delays might have been frustrating, but if the result was the best-reviewed game of all time, then it was worth it.

The question now is what the fans will think, as only people in New Zealand can currently play the game. Oh, and the people on Xbox Series X/S who have suddenly traveled to Middle-earth and switched their region to New Zealand (don’t worry, no one will tell John Microsoft). After all, it’s not unheard of for the critical and audience reviews to differ greatly.

The verdict from the fans will filter in over the next few days and weeks, but until then, Rockstar and Take-Two can lean back and know they have a stacked accolades trailer ahead of them, and that Vice City will have millions of visitors over the upcoming holiday season. If the reviewers are correct, a once-in-a-generation game has arrived, and it’s time to dive in.

Grand Theft Auto 6 delivers an enormous open world, a gripping story, a vibrant Vice City and an astonishing range of activities.
