The Shopbop Black Friday Deals Worth Adding to Your Cart

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

ELLE editors found the best deals—and the brands with better discounts elsewhere.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

You could spend hours scrolling through individual fashion brands’ Black Friday sales—or you could do your shopping in one convenient place: Shopbop. Now through December 1, the Amazon-adjacent retailer is offering 25 percent off most items on its site with the code HOLIDAY. Plus, Prime members get free one-day shipping.

More than 25,000 items are included in the promotion, ranging from celebrity-worn handbags to winter staples. The retailer is also offering limited-time deals on menswear, beauty, and home products that make for great holiday gifts. To help you make the most of it, we’ve highlighted Shopbop’s best Black Friday deals—and detailed which brands you can find deeper discounts on elsewhere.

Shopbop is offering the same 25 percent discount on Hailey Bieber’s versatile Freja Caroline bag as you’ll find on the New York-based label’s site. We also spotted Selena Gomez’s JW Pei Delphine bag for under $100. Staud and Tory Burch are offering slightly deeper discounts on their own sites, so we’d skip Shopbop’s deals this time around.

Polo Ralph Lauren Bellport North/South Tote

Rag & Bone is offering 25 percent off sitewide for Black Friday, so you’re safe to shop for the label’s viral sweatpant jeans on Shopbop. We’d skip discounted denim from Paige, Madewell, DL1961, Levi’s, Good American, Joe’s Jeans, and Frame, because those brands are offering deeper discounts on their own sites. That said, we’re seeing Shopbop deals on pairs that are still full price at AG, Agolde, Mother, and Citizens of Humanity.

Shopbop is offering the same Black Friday discount as Reformation, Vince, and Leset, so jump on any deals you see from those brands. We’d skip La Ligne sweaters, since they’re up to 50 percent off on the brand’s site during its only sale of the year.

Reformation Caspia Brushed Cashmere Cardigan

Sold Out NYC Signature Cashmere Crew Sweater

Shopbop is offering Black Friday deals on over 2,000 pairs of shoes, so it’s worth peeking at categories your closet is missing. While we’ll be quick to add Puma and Asics sneakers, Flabelus flats, and Aeyde boots to our carts, we’ll pass on Loeffler Randall and Larroudé while those brands are offering 40 percent off on their own sites.

Shopbop’s sale is a smart place to invest in winter coats. The retailer is offering deeper discounts on Halfdays puffers than the brand itself. Stock of suede and leather jackets is already running low, so finalize your cart fast if that’s what you’re after.

Lola blankets, Bala bangles, Emi Jay claw clips, and other reliable gift ideas are competitively priced at Shopbop’s massive Black Friday sale. Why not check a few people off your list while you’re browsing for yourself?
