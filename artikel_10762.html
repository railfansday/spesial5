Burger King unveils bizarre new burger with popcorn as a topping

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Burger King has introduced a new limited-time burger that comes with an unusual topping: popcorn.

The fast food chain announced the Bacon King Pop on November 3, 2026, combining its signature burgers with the movie theater snack. Customers can either eat the popcorn separately or add it directly to their burger.

The new creation is currently only in Spain and Portugal, where it is available at Burger King restaurants nationwide for a limited time.

The Bacon King Pop comes in two versions, allowing customers to choose between a flame-grilled Whopper beef patty or Crispy Chicken.

Both options feature bacon, melted cheese, bacon-flavored bread, and a new Bacon Pop sauce with buttery notes.

Each burger also comes with a serving of popcorn, which customers can pour directly onto their sandwich for an added crunch or enjoy separately.

“The best innovations are born when worlds come together that no one expects to see together. With Bacon King Pop we have taken one of the most iconic snacks from entertainment to the Burger King universe to create a different, fun and amazing experience. It’s a new way to enjoy a burger, always in your own way,” said Yvette Altet, Chief Marketing Officer of Burger King Spain.

To promote the launch, Burger King has partnered with advertising agency DAVID Madrid on a campaign called “The missing popcorn arrives.”

The campaign includes two advertisements for television and digital platforms featuring a group of friends preparing to hear some gossip. Before the story begins, they stop to add popcorn to their burgers.

The Bacon King Pop is available now at Burger King locations across Spain, including through delivery and the chain’s AutoKing drive-thru service.

Burger King has not announced pricing, an exact end date for the promotion, or plans to bring the popcorn burger to the US.

The launch follows another unusual food combination from Tombstone, which recently introduced a limited-edition candy corn pizza, combining the Halloween candy with a traditional frozen pizza.

Burger King also recently unveiled an Avengers: Doomsday menu in India, featuring black-bunned Whoppers covered in bright-green spicy garlic sauce.

The Marvel collaboration also includes superhero-themed chicken nuggets, burgers, fries, and a green Sprite-based drink, with no announced plans for a US release.
