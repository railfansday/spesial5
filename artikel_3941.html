Demon Kill Demon: Yomi 1984 coming to PS5, PS4, and PC on February 25, 2027 in Japan

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Experience will release dungeon RPG Demon Kill Demon: Yomi 1984 for PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, and PC via Steam on February 25, 2027 in Japan, the developer announced. The PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 4 versions will be available both physically and digitally. Pre-orders will open on November 12.

Demon Kill Demon: Yomi 1984 first launched for Switch on May 28.

Like the previous Nintendo Switch release, the PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 4 physical editions are priced at 8,800 yen and include both the base game (“Transference Arc”) and the downloadable content “Revenge Arc.” The digital version includes only the base game for 3,900 yen, with the “Revenge Arc” sold separately for an additional 3,900 yen.

Here is an overview of the game, via Experience:

Form your team with character creation. Gather and upgrade equipment, and challenge the otherworldly realm of Yomi. Explore, fight, collect, and train over and over in this dystopian dungeon RPG.

Five years have passed since the “Imprisonment” incident involving an underground explorer. Once again, the darkness lurking in the underworld bares its fangs.

The targets were the students of Yomi High School.

In the hell where their entire classrooms were transported, their only means of survival was to resurrect the corpses of their classmates and use them as assets in battle.

That is not a choice. It is the only thing they can do in order to live.

The curtain rises once more on a dystopian dungeon RPG.
