Embracer begin yet another round of layoffs at Crystal Dynamics, while they're still midway through development on two Tomb Raider games

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Update: Studio confirms eight staff lost their jobs

Update 18/11: Crystal Dynamics have provided the following statement to RPS, confirming it cut eight staff members:

As initially reported by Vertical Slice (thanks, Chris!), the workforce of Tomb Raider studio Crystal Dynamics is being cut as part of a new set of layoffs by owners Embracer. As far as is currently known, those affected include senior game designer Joshua Mitchell, senior environment technical artist Kevin Baker, and UI software engineer Garin R. K. Richards, all of whom shared news of their redunancies on LinkedIn. There may be more affected, but neither Crystal Dynamics nor Embracer have offered comments on the reduction of staff as yet.

This now marks the studio's fifth round of layoffs since Embracer acquired it in 2022, following cuts in March 2025, August 2025, November 2025, and March this year. This is a drastic amount even considering the current state of the industry, with layoffs seemingly happening several times a week, but unfortunately, this is par for the course for Embracer. Even still, it makes one wonder just how many staff are left at Crystal Dynamics by now. Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis and Tomb Raider: Catalyst are still in production, so you'd hope that the studio won't be shuttered entirely.

According to Legacy of Atlantis' Steam page, generative AI has been used in the development of Legacy of Atlantis "to support some early exploration and temporary development content. Any AI-assisted assets were either replaced or refined by humans in order to maintain the creative and artistic vision of the development team." I would hope that this isn't a case of Embracer cutting staff in the misguided hope that they can just be replaced with the use of genAI, but who can say. Solidarity to those affected, who I hope can find new work soon.
