Jared Goff listed as full participant at Thursday's practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Lions quarterback Jared Goff was listed as a limited participant in practice on Wednesday, but it doesn’t look like there will be an issue with his availability for Sunday’s game against the Patriots.

Goff, who is listed with a chest injury, was upgraded to full participation on Thursday. The team will issue a final injury report for their game in Munich on Friday.

Safety Brian Branch (hip) moved up to full participation as well. Running back Jacob Saylors (neck, hamstring) and defensive end DJ Wonnum (concussion) remained full participants.

Wide receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown (rib) was limited for the second straight day and running back Sione Vaki (ankle) remained out of practice. The Lions have already ruled out safety Kerby Joseph (knee), center Cade Mays (ankle), and cornerback D.J. Reed (hip).
