Offensive fireworks in Madrid: Joe Burrow hits Ja'Marr Chase for 47-yard touchdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Bengals and Falcons are putting on a show for the fans in Madrid.

In the NFL’s showcase in Spain, Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow hit wide receiver Ja’Marr Chase for a 47-yard touchdown to tie the score 7-7 in the first quarter.

Both teams scored on their opening possessions, the Falcons on a seven-play, 80-yard drive and the Bengals on a five-play, 75-yard drive.

It’s been an entertaining first six minutes of football at Bernabéu Stadium.
