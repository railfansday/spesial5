Bears have a tough path to the playoffs, but are focused on 1-0

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year, we ask questions of the most plugged-in Bears fans and fans across the country.

The Chicago Bears may have dropped from the second seed to sixth in the conference standings during their bye, but they are still firmly in the NFC postseason mix at 7-2. The NFC North is the NFL's toughest division, and the Bears (on paper) have the toughest remaining schedule, but Caleb Williams and the Bears aren't looking ahead, and they're 100% focused on the New Orleans Saints this Sunday.

When asked how they stay in the moment and not get caught up in the division race and playoff positioning, Williams said, "The point of it is that we have to do our part, and our part is going 1-0 each week. I think that's what it comes down to. You don't focus on eight games from now because we haven't played those eight games. You don't focus on next week or the week after and who we got and prime times or the non-prime times."

"You focus on being prepared for that moment of when the the ball gets snapped the first time, whistle gets blown, and we focus on being 1-0 that week. And then when the next one comes up, we'll focus on being 1-0 that week. And if you go 1-0 each week and you focus on that each week, you'll turn out to kind of be where you want to be at the end of it."

Head coach Ben Johnson also met the media today, and it's not hard to figure out where Caleb's 1-0 mindset comes from.

When asked about his big-picture take on the NFC North, Johnson said, "It really doesn't matter right now. Once again, it's all about winning this week. So, we'll worry about everything else when it's time. It's not time for that. There's still eight games to be played, and this is the most important one because it's the next one."

The Bears host the Saints on Sunday at Noon.
