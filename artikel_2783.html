Robert Saleh: We played disgusting football on Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Titans were on the receiving end of a thrashing in Dallas on Sunday.

The Cowboys scored touchdowns on seven of their eight possessions and racked up 480 yards before kneeling out at the end of a 51-30 win. Their offense settled for three field goals in the first half and couldn’t keep up with the stream of points that the Cowboys generated over the course of the afternoon.

After the game, Titans head coach Robert Saleh said that his team “took a step back today.”

“There’s a lot of things that need to change,” Saleh said, via the team’s website. “Obviously, it’s disgusting football, it’s embarrassing football. And obviously that starts with me.”

Saleh’s frustration with Sunday’s performance is evident, but the performance wasn’t totally out of left field. It was the third straight game that the Titans allowed at least 40 points and at least 459 yards, so the defensive issues were already apparent before the Cowboys full exploited them.
