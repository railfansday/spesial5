S Talanoa Hufanga, WR Jaylen Waddle, CB Riley Moss set to return Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Broncos’ injury report Friday was not about who has a status, but who doesn’t.

The team will see the return of safety Talanoa Hufanga (ankle), wide receiver Jaylen Waddle (concussion) and cornerback Riley Moss (concussion). None of them have an injury designation for Sunday’s game against the Raiders.

All three players were full participants throughout the week.

Hufanga will play for the first time since Week 5 against the Chargers. He has two interceptions, including a pick-six, a forced fumble and a sack in five games this season.

Waddle and Moss were injured in Week 9 against the Panthers.

Safety Brandon Jones (hamstring) was the lone Broncos player listed as questionable. Jones, who exited the Week 8 win over the Chiefs, was a limited participant in all three practices this week.

Tight end Caleb Lohner (foot) was a full participant in all three practices as he returned to practice this week, but he was ruled out for Sunday’s game.
