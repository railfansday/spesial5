Are the Rams playing today? NFL Week 11 schedule, next game, NFC West standings

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It's been an up-and-down 2026 season for the Los Angeles Rams. Once the odds-on Super Bowl favorites, the Rams' outlook for the final half of their schedule is looking bleak after the devastating news that Puka Nacua's MCL surgery will leave him sidelined for the remainder of the regular season and the entirety of the playoffs.

That further decimates an already-depleted wide receiver room that's expecting to be without Davante Adams for at least the next two weeks. And after general manager Les Snead only made marginal moves to address depth at the position at the trade deadline, reigning NFL MVP Matthew Stafford will now be throwing the ball to a position group led by Konata Mumpfield, CJ Daniels and Tutu Atwell.

The good news for the Rams is they won't have to pick up all the pieces on Sunday due to their bye in Week 11. Tight end Terrance Ferugson will miss Wednesday's Week 12 Thanksgiving eve matchup against the Green Bay Packers, but is expected to be activated off injured reserve before his 21-day practice window closes. Defensive lineman Kobie Turner, however, will be coming off IR and is expected to suit up against the Packers.

Despite four straight wins, the Rams' season outlook has dramatically plummeted over the last 48 hours.

Here's a look at their record, the NFC West standings and the Rams' remaining schedule as the season continues.
