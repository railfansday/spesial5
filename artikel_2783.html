Bills see two practice-squadders poached by other teams

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Buffalo Bills suddenly have two vacant spots on their practice squad.

Core pieces of said practice squad, quarterback Shane Buechele and 2025 sixth-round pick Chase Lundt, were added to the 53-man rosters of other teams in separate transactions on Tuesday before the club's Week 12 Thanksgiving contest against the Chiefs.

Given the Colts released Daniel Jones on November 20, they were in need of more quarterback depth behind former first-rounder Anthony Richardson, who's become the team's starter. Buechle is presumed to be Indianapolis' new No. 3 quarterback behind Richardson and Riley Leonard.

Buechele spent from 2023 – 2025 with the Bills before re-signing in Buffalo in January 2026.
