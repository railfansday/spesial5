Browns Week 10 review: Grit, overcoming, big plays, and playoffs? Playoffs!

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

If you were to ask anyone to describe the 2026 Cleveland Browns, a lot of words will come to mind. Confusing. Frustrating. Resilient. Gritty. Heart. After falling into a 17-point deficit in the 2nd quarter against the Houston Texans, the Browns followed it up with 20 unanswered points in Week 10. It ended up being enough as Cleveland improved to 6-4 heading into their bye week with some momentum and bigger picture questions.

Now, on to our 4-Downs review from the Browns' Week 10 win over the Texans:

When the Houston Texans jumped out to a 10-point lead, it was looking pretty dark. Defensively, the Browns couldn't get a stop as Texans QB CJ Stroud was doing whatever he wanted in the passing game. Offensively, Cleveland couldn't sustain drives or get any form of rhythm going. It appeared that Houston was going to turn this game into a rout.

After that, the entire complexity of the game changed. In the 2nd half, Cleveland outcoached and outplayed the Texans.

After getting punched in the mouth early in the game, the Browns responded with a punch of their own.

After Cleveland's loss against the New Orleans Saints in Week 9, in which quarterback Shedeur Sanders started over quarterback Deshaun Watson, who missed the game while recovering from a concussion, there was a lot of quarterback conversation. Sanders played well, but the topic of discussion was whether Watson's leash was short after how Sanders played.

After Watson's performance on Sunday, any talk of a QB change should be over.

Watson's 1st half was rough, but he responded with a solid 2nd half. It's a night-and-day difference between how Watson looks now in 2026 and how he looked in 2024. He looks more confident, mentally tough, and has an unwavering belief in himself. Confidence is key when playing the quarterback position, and if you don't have it, it will be a short stint for you in the NFL.

It's clear that the Browns offense runs more efficiently and effectively with Watson under center, and Monken trusts Watson more than Sanders. Watson hasn't been perfect, but he has provided competent quarterback play.

Overall, the Browns defense had a solid day overall, but Cleveland's secondary (mainly their cornerbacks) were the star of the show. Cornerback Denzel Ward is one of the most underrated corners in our league. The Texans' wide receivers were finding success early and often with the route combinations, but Cleveland eventually found a way to slow them down.

The Browns are going to live and die by playing man coverage on the back end, but the corners are sticky in coverage, so it doesn't matter as much as it should. Cornerback Tyson Campbell also had a solid day and, overall, the secondary stood out once adjustments were made.

Defensive coordinator Mike Rutenberg's adjustments once again stood out. It feels as if Cleveland's defense was trying to figure out what Houston's offense was going to do, and it led to a ton of hesitation and overthinking early.

At their best, this defense is best at attacking and causing chaos. It worked as Stroud looked uncomfortable in the 2nd half. Credit to the defense for a solid performance, especially the cornerbacks.

If the season ended today, the Cleveland Browns would have the last playoff spot in the AFC. Yes, you read that right. For a team that many people thought would be picking towards the top in next year's draft, the Browns have completely changed that. The Browns have exceeded expectations, and it should be commended. Yes, Cleveland is 6-4 with a negative point differential, but who cares? Honestly? Winning in the NFL is hard, and the Browns are finding ways to win every week.

The scary part about this team is they haven't played their best football yet. Granted, no one is saying this team is a Super Bowl contender, but if they continue to get better, this team can find a way to get into the postseason and maybe make a little noise.

Houston Texans head coach DeMeco Ryans getting fired after this season is a strong possibility. The Texans have been a massive disappointment, and today might have been the nail in their coffin in terms of their playoff hopes.

The Browns offensive line had one of their best outings of the season.
