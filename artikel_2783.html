5 things to watch when the Cowboys host the Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Dallas Cowboys are in search of their first winning streak of the season and desperately trying to stay alive in the NFC East hunt. On the menu this Thanksgiving are the three-win Philadelphia Eagles, who are riddled with injuries and already peeking ahead into the future.

Struggling or not, the Eagles just snapped a seven-game losing streak on Sunday, winning their in-state battle against the Steelers, showing that these birds aren't completely cooked just yet. For the Cowboys, collecting more wins against weaker opponents is absolutely paramount as soon as the schedule turns and it gets brutal in a hurry. Nothing will ruin a holiday weekend faster than letting a limping rival deliver a crushing blow to their playoff chances. That absolutely cannot happen.

Here are five things to watch when the Cowboys host the Eagles on Thursday.

The Cowboys offense is riding hot right now, putting up at least 40 points in each of the last two games. The passing attack has been particularly strong, with Dak Prescott carving up defenses for nine touchdown passes in that span. Meanwhile, the Eagles have major issues in the secondary, first benching Riq Woolen and now watching top corner Quinyon Mitchell deal with a hamstring injury.

That vulnerability should open up a buffet of good eats with CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens at the front of the line. With the passing offense rolling, the Cowboys shouldn't get complacent or take their foot off the gas. Keep feeding the playmakers until they have no more room to put it.

Joe Milton III spent last year with the Cowboys, never started a game, and threw just 24 total passes before being released at roster cuts this year before being brought back to the practice squad. Now he is with the Eagles, making his third career start for them in a return trip to AT&T Stadium. Things haven't gone smoothly for him so far, as he has yet to throw a touchdown pass and has been sacked 10 times over the past two games.

Even so, he played well enough to help the Eagles beat the Steelers on Sunday. Playing against a backup quarterback cannot be taken for granted when it comes to this Cowboys defense, which already got shocked by rookie UDFA Jalon Daniels in Week 5 and Gardner Minshew in Week 8. Dallas cannot afford to serve up another holiday charity case to a guy elevated from the kids' table.

Speaking of Milton getting sacked an average of five times per game, five sacks happens to be the exact number the Cowboys defense has racked up in each of their last three home games. The defensive line is on a bit of a heater right now, thanks to what we hope is the influence of new defensive play-caller Mike Pettine. Keeping that streak alive on Thanksgiving would be fantastic.

Defensive tackle Quinnen Williams has been feasting in the backfield lately, while lesser-known players like James Houston and Elijah Clark are very hungry and have been bringing an appetite for destruction as of late. Against a young quarterback trying to find his footing and an offensive line struggling to keep him upright, expect Milton to have his hands full trying to stay off the turf.

If there is a scenario where the Eagles pull off the upset, it starts with a running game barrage that exposes a shaky Cowboys run defense. Stopping the run remains a glaring weakness for this team, as they have been hit hard on the ground a few times this season.

Philadelphia isn't the rushing juggernaut it has been in past years, and Saquon Barkley is only averaging 46 yards per game, but that doesn't mean he isn't capable of breaking out. If the Eagles find a rhythm on the ground, chew up the clock, and shorten the game, they can keep the Cowboys offense chilling on the sideline and open the door for an unpleasant ending.

There is no love lost between these two franchises, and even a festive holiday won't rekindle any tenderness. Older fans still remember the infamous 1989 Bounty Bowl, when Cowboys players were suspiciously victims of late hits all afternoon. While nobody is expecting things to go quite that far off the rails, Thursday contains all the ingredients for plenty of drama.

With edgy temperaments like Jalen Carter and George Pickens, and pesky personalities like Cooper DeJean and Brevyn Spann-Ford running around, it wouldn't take much to spark a fire. It would actually be surprising if this game didn't feature its share of personal fouls or taunting penalties as these bitter rivals go after each other. Happy holidays!
