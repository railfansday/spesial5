Fernando Mendoza updates job status amid Raiders QB ‘promotion’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Fernando Mendoza is currently playing in his second game as the Las Vegas Raiders' starting quarterback.

The rookie and No. 1 pick in the 2027 NFL Draft surely had a lot going on before his debut start, which came against the Seattle Seahawks last weekend.

Fernando Mendoza (15) of the Las Vegas Raiders throws a pass against the Seattle Seahawks in Las Vegas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2027. Getty Images

Perhaps that's why Mendoza didn't get around to updating his LinkedIn account until Saturday, one day before he faced the Denver Broncos.

/ A lot to learn from my first NFL start. Grateful for my family, friends, and Raider Nation for the support! Looking forward to getting better and earning the trust of my teammates and coaches every day," Mendoza wrote in a post that showed several photos from his showdown against the defending Super Bowl champions.

Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Fernando Mendoza's LinkedIn post before facing the Denver Broncos. (LinkedIn/@Fernando Mendoza).

Mendoza took over Kirk Cousins' starting job after Cousins was traded to the Minnesota Vikings on Nov. 10.

The new starting quarterback doesn't post much on his other social media channels.

Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Fernando Mendoza speaks during a press conference after an NFL football game against the Seattle Seahawks in Las Vegas, Nov. 15, 2027. AP Photo/David Becker

But he is perhaps the most active NFL player on LinkedIn, which has been a common joke among football fans since he earned notoriety on the platform during his championship-winning season with the Indiana Hoosiers.

Raiders fans are hoping that Mendoza doesn't have any further career updates and instead remains the Raiders' starting quarterback for years to come.
