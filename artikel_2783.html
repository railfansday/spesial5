Panthers sign CB Marco Wilson to the active roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Cornerback Marco Wilson had a hand in one of the Panthers’ two takeaways in Sunday’s win over the Saints and he moved up to a spot on the active roster on Monday.

Wilson was elevated from the practice squad three times this season and the Panthers announced that he has been signed to the active roster on Monday. Linebacker Nick Hampton was waived in a corresponding move.

Wilson broke up a pass to wide receiver Chris Olave in the first quarter on Sunday and the ball caromed to linebacker Devin Lloyd for an interception. He also had three tackles during the 20-17 victory.

Wilson has 15 tackles and four passes defensed over all three of his appearances.
