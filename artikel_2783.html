NBA Injury Report: CJ McCollum to miss a month with calf injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The injury bug quieted down for a bit last week, but it picked back up over the last few days. The most recent big news is that CJ McCollum is set to be re-evaluated in four weeks after being diagnosed with a left calf strain. McCollum had returned to the starting lineup with Kingston Flemings (back) sidelined for their last game, and they'll now have to turn to either Luguentz Dort or Aaron Wiggins to fill out the other starting spot on Wednesday against the Thunder, with Dyson Daniels, Nickeil Alexander-Walker and Jalen Johnson taking on more playmaking responsibility.

Flemings has been solid as a starter, but he has only played 23.1 minutes per game across his five starts. That was because he was splitting minutes with McCollum, so with McCollum set to miss an extended period of time, Flemings should see his minutes tick up. This is a great opportunity for the rookie to showcase why the Hawks made him the No. 8 pick back in June. The defense and passing have been there, but he needs to be more aggressive as a scorer.

There isn't an update on Curry's return timeline, but he did give an interesting quote on Tuesday. He acknowledged that his right knee issues, coupled with other various injuries, could mean his career is coming to a close, and that his current two-year contract could be his last. There isn't anything actionable here, unless you're a dynasty manager, but it's worth noting. Make sure you appreciate what could be the final year or two of Curry's historic career.

Mobley is set to be re-evaluated next week, meaning he will miss at least a few more games with a toe injury. Jaylon Tyson has been dealing with an ankle injury that is expected to force him to miss a second straight game on Wednesday. Prior to that, he had been starting, but it has been Tyrese Proctor filling out the open spot lately. Proctor had a triple-double two games ago (though that was the game that none of the starters played) and has averaged 19.5 points, 5.3 rebounds, 4.0 assists and 3.5 triples over his last four appearances.

There isn't an official timeline for Reaves, but until he returns, Quentin Grimes should be considered a must-roster player, especially after his performance on Tuesday. He finished with 47 points and 10 three-pointers after having 22 points and five three-pointers in the previous game. Even if Reaves is only out for another game or two, Grimes has seen more than enough usage to be worth picking up.

Brown has been ruled out for Wednesday's game with a hamstring injury and will reportedly be re-evaluated before they play again on Saturday. This could be just a one-game absence, but it may be more. Kentavious Caldwell-Pope is the likely starter, though there is a chance it is Dean Wade that gets the nod. Wade would be a better option for fantasy managers, though neither provides tremendous upside in place of Brown.

Jerome is set to miss a third straight game on Wednesday due to a hip injury, which means Cam Spencer should continue to start and be streamed with confidence. He has been awesome as a starter, averaging 14.5 points and 8.5 assists over the Grizzlies' last two games. Jerome has had a fantastic season, so hopefully this isn't an injury that will keep him sidelined for much longer. However, Spencer has been an excellent fill-in and should be rostered until Jerome returns.

Jackson suffered a fracture in his left foot and is set to miss at least three months. Jackson has been a decent scoring option off the bench for Memphis, but he hasn't been a critical part of their rotation. His absence should open the door for Taylor Hendricks to remain in the rotation, though his minutes also may end up going to Jerami Grant.

There isn't any definitive timeline for Murphy, but he will miss a sixth straight game on Wednesday. Saddiq Bey has been awesome and should continue to start until Murphy returns. While there have been rumors of a Murphy trade, this doesn't seem to be them holding him out with a deal imminent. Hopefully he isn't out much longer, but until he's back, Bey, who has averaged 23 points and 4.3 triples over their last three games, should be streamed with confidence.

The Pelicans have been listing Murray as out with an ankle injury, but we know that's not the main reason he isn't suiting up. Dejounte wants out. This is a situation where a trade could be close, but different reports suggest different things. What seems to be clear is that Murray has likely played his last game as a Pelican. Jeremiah Fears should continue to be the team's starting point guard and see a ton of extra usage. In six games without Murray this season, Fears has averaged 17.3 points, 6.0 rebounds, 8.5 assists, 1.0 steal, 1.0 block and 1.0 triples per game.

Leonard (knee) continues to progress in his recovery, and another update will be provided in a week. So, basically, no new information once again. Ja'Kobe Walter will remain a starter for at least another week, and he's coming off scoring a season-high 25 points on Tuesday.

There hasn't been an official report, but Harper exited just before the end of Tuesday's loss to the Pistons with what appeared to be a groin injury. Hopefully a statement from the Spurs will come soon with good news, but it could end up being a lengthy absence for him. Justin Champagnie will likely slide into the starting lineup, at least until Tobias Harris makes his season debut.

Robinson has gotten off to an excellent start to the season, but he is set to miss at least a week with a quad strain. That means the Kevin Huerter show will continue for at least a few more games. Huerter was quiet as a starter on Tuesday, but before that, he averaged 16.8 points, 3.8 assists, 1.3 steals, 2.3 blocks and 1.8 triples over a four-game stretch.

Sorber underwent an arthroscopic procedure on his right knee on Tuesday and is expected to return to on-court activities in six-to-eight weeks. Hopefully we'll get to see him make his NBA debut this year.

Newell is dealing with a plantar plate tear in his right great toe that will keep him on the sidelines for at least four months. The No. 23 pick in 2025, Newell hasn't really been part of the Hawks' rotation this season, so his absence shouldn't change much for fantasy managers.
