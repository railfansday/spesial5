Welcome to CricketNews.com's live coverage of the first ODI between New Zealand and India at Eden Park in Auckland.

Arshdeep Singh gave India the upper hand during the initial Powerplay, removing both New Zealand openers to finish his opening five-over burst with figures of 2/25.

Arshdeep extracted bounce from a short-of-a-length delivery in the fifth over (4.3 ov), inducing a top-edge from Will Young (9 off 11) that was safely pouched by Shreyas Iyer running back from square leg.

Henry Nicholls struggled for rhythm and perished in near-identical fashion in the ninth over (8.3 ov), lifting his front leg on a pull shot off Arshdeep only to pick out Naman Dhir at square leg for 9 off 11 balls.

While debutant Auqib Nabi conceded 19 runs across his first three overs, Anshul Kamboj applied the squeeze at the other end with an exceptionally miserly opening burst, conceding just 1 run from his 2 overs including a maiden.

Toss result: India have won the toss and have opted to field.

India and New Zealand switch their focus to the 50-over format after an entertaining T20I series.

The hosts lost the T20I series 3-2 but won the last two games and are in winning momentum as they begin their ODI campaign at Eden Park in Auckland.

The surface is expected to offer more assistance to bowlers than the recent T20I pitches, with good bounce potentially bringing the seamers into play.

The series also provides an important opportunity for both teams to fine-tune their combinations ahead of next year's ODI World Cup. Shubman Gill, Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli will be key to India's plans, while Shreyas Iyer is set to return to the middle order.

New Zealand, meanwhile, will be without Devon Conway for the opener following a concussion, alongside the unavailable Mark Chapman and Rachin Ravindra. Daryl Mitchell will be their key batter in the lineup.
