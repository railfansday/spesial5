Advent calendars have gotten a bit of a bad rap this year (*cough cough* Skims). People have sorta wised up to the fact that many of these calendars are hunks of paper and plastic with a few little samples inside that you’ll never actually use. And somehow, many of them are more than $100 a pop. Dropping hundreds of dollars on junk just didn’t hit the same in the year of our lord 2026, I guess!

My truth? I always hated beauty advent calendars. Why would I spend so much money on sample sizes of my go-to skincare or makeup that wasn’t my shade? I’d rather buy one or two full-size products and call it a day. But last year, while researching Black Friday beauty deals, I discovered that some beauty advent calendars can actually be a treasure trove. Gift cards, full-size products, and samples I’ll actually use—the gems inside some of these advent calendars are actually pretty damn good.

Before you buy one, peek below at my choices for the best beauty advent calendars of 2026. I’ve looked through every single one I could get my hands on, and these really set the tone. They include a good mix of full-size products and samples, they’re loaded with best sellers, and in some cases (we love you, Dermstore!!), the retail value is astronomical compared to the price you pay.
