30 Nordstrom Black Friday 2026 Deals That Go Harder Than Expected

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

While we're all recovering from too much Thanksgiving turkey and sides, the Nordstrom Black Friday sale is looking like the perfect distraction from helping to figure out the seating plan for your sister's wedding next summer.

That’s because, as always, the famed department store is your ideal all-in-one stop for any style need—a dapper new suit, a pair of comfier kicks, some killer watches. But these days, it’s also the place to lock in your new skincare routine or find a scintillating cologne, not to mention a whole new kitchen full of Michelin-starred gear and practically a full-on home renovation with luxurious bedding and tasteful decor. All that and more are on offer for the Nordstrom Black Friday sale, and we’ve looked through the entire thing to surface some truly gloat-over-Christmas level deals.

Whether you need a new suit for the holidays, a pair of boots to get you through Snowmageddon, or a head-to-toe closet refresh, Nordstrom’s got it all discounted. That goes doubly for Veilance's incredible, rain-defying outerwear. We don’t expect that to last long.

Accessories are the bits and bobs that really make your outfits pop. Whether it’s the watch on your wrist, the beanie on your head, or the duffle you carry on your travels, no detail—or discount—is too small.

Like Forrest Gump’s momma always said “you can tell a lot about a person by their shoes.” Whatever it is you want to telegraph to the world, there’s a shoe for you. Prep your feet for winter with a honkin’ pair of boots or pick up a new daily driver like a workhorse pair of derbies. Or maybe get a jump on your marathon training with a new pair of running shoes.

Whether it’s the scruff on your face or the scent you wear, grooming sets your ambiance and aura. That’s crucial if you expect to interact with anyone up close. So pick up a fresh new cologne or get your skincare routine locked down because you’ll never know who you’ll run into.

Even if you end up staying inside without seeing anyone, your home is your sanctuary. Whether your main hangout is the kitchen, the couch, or the bed, Nordstrom can make you feel even more at home with its wide range of deals on bedding, decor, kitchenware, and more.

This year in the US, Black Friday is on Friday, November 27, which is a day after Thanksgiving. But if not obvious already, retailers (like Nordstrom) tend to launch “early” sales to help you get a jump on shopping. And before you ask, the deals are just as good.

Cyber Monday falls on Monday, November 30. Note that most sales will extend through the end of the week, but that won’t be a given.

What are the best things to buy at Nordstrom?

Nordstrom is a legacy department store, so they’ve got you covered for just about everything. But if you’re probably thinking about clothes, specifically. We think that some of the best things to buy at Nordstrom are shoes, suits, and grooming products. Nordstrom also has a surprisingly good amount of designer and niche brands on their roster—you just have to dig through the generic in-house labels to find them.
