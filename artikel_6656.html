The 15 Best Airbnbs in Tokyo for an Authentic Japanese Experience

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It’s no secret that Japan has perfected the art of, well, a lot of things—design, cuisine, wellness, hospitality, and craft, to name a few. A trip to Japan offers a glimpse at living with intention and elegant restraint, from the traditional tea ceremonies to the ritual of long soaks in an onsen. In its populous capital, slowing down might feel like a contradiction, but the best Airbnbs in Tokyo offer travelers respite from the bustle.

Winter is an especially beautiful time to visit. The crowds thin out and the city is illuminated by trees glowing with little white lights—it feels like your own personal winter wonderland. Those visiting around New Year’s celebrations, or Shōgatsu, can participate in a long list of traditions, from visiting temples and shrines to eating a bowl of buckwheat noodles in warm broth, to ensure luck for the year ahead. Whether you’re looking to explore Shinjuku’s narrow alleys lined with tiny bars or prefer to spend your time in Shimokitazawa’s bohemian district amidst the cafes and vintage shops, these Airbnb rentals will situate you in Tokyo’s most compelling neighborhoods.
