Nina Garcia’s Favorite Gifts for Hosting in Style

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The ELLE editor-in-chief’s picks bring a personal touch to the holiday season.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Welcome to In Good Company, where we ring in the festive season with fashion-insider entertaining tips, expert etiquette advice, and the perfect editor-approved hostess gifts.

You’ve got your holiday soirée look sorted. Now, what to give someone who makes entertaining look easy? Rather than the clichés, ELLE editor-in-chief Nina Garcia gravitates toward gifts with a sense of place and a touch of surprise: hand-glazed Maison Pichon Uzès plates for the table, a La DoubleJ candle to set the mood, and Sisley exfoliating hand wash for the guest bathroom.

Whether they’re for your own home or a loved one, Garcia’s entertaining picks bring a variety of her favorite travel destinations to the table: the Milanese bronze work of Osanna Visconti’s coffee spoons, or the Pietra Red Marble Large Catchall Dish from her CB2 collection, inspired by ornate Italian flooring. They’re personal ways to bring a place you love into your home and share it with the people gathered around your table. (And they last much, much longer than even the freshest of flowers.)

Garcia’s entertaining edit favors pieces that balance form and function. Buccellati’s silver place card holders, shaped like ivy leaves, bring a sculptural touch to the table, while a yellow gold-plated S.T. Dupont lighter is handsome enough to leave on display. Its signature “cling” would be the perfect prelude to lighting Rebecca Gardner’s emerald-green ceramic candlesticks.

Ahead, shop Garcia’s picks for entertaining with a sense of style—and a sense of place.
