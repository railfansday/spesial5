Mauricio Pochettino’s latest Manchester United deal confirmed after Bruno Fernandes decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Manchester United have confirmed the latest deal of the Mauricio Pochettino era, with talented youngster Yuel Helafu signing a long-term contract.

Helafu has been with United since signing scholarship forms in 2025, and was a key figure in the side that went all the way to the FA Youth Cup final last season.

As well as being a regular for the U18s, Helafu has made the step up to the U21s, making three appearances in Premier League 2 this season, scoring once and providing an assist.

As reward for his progression, the right-back - who can also operate on the opposite flank - has signed a long-term professional contract at Old Trafford.

A Man United statement reads: "Talented youngster Yuel Helafu has committed his future to Manchester United. The 18-year-old defender has signed a long-term professional contract.

"Everyone at Manchester United is delighted that Yuel has signed a new deal and looks forward to seeing him progress."

This deal comes hot on the heels of Man Utd triggering Bruno Fernandes’ contract extension.

Standard Sport understands that Man Utd have activated their option to extend their captain’s deal by one year, though the club have the January transfer window in mind.

Fernandes’ current terms were due to expire at the end of the current season, meaning he would have been free to reach a pre-contract agreement with any team outside of the Premier League from the start of 2027.

However, to prevent any further distractions, Fernandes’ contract will now run until the summer of 2028 and is expected to return to the negotiation table once the club’s form improves after rejecting two offers.

"Yeah, the club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay,” he told Sky Sports. “And there are no negotiations going on.

"We spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side.

“I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most important thing now is that the club gets back to win games."
