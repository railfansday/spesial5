The Only Apple Deals Worth Your Dollar This Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Every product was carefully curated by an Esquire editor. We may earn a commission from these links. Here’s how we test products and why you should trust us.

AirPods, MacBooks, and Watches for up to 28% off—this year’s sale is hot.

If it feels like life is one long string of sales—most of them being mediocre—you’re not wrong. Despite the near-constant deluge of Prime Days, anniversary events, and the like, some sales do legitimately stand above the rest. You know the one: Black Friday. It tends to bring some of the best deals of the year, including record-low prices on Apple tech.

Apple is notorious for not marking down its own products—on the contrary, the tech giant actually raised prices on Macs and iPads earlier this year—so we turn to retailers like Amazon when looking to save on everything from AirPods to Apple Watches. This year, Amazon’s Black Friday deals span 12 days (the sale is called Turkey 12 if you’d like to know too much), which means plenty of Apple discounts are already live. In the event that you’ve spilled coffee on your MacBook or lost one of your AirPods, you needn’t wait until Black Friday proper to shop.

Sure, there’s always the chance these deals could slightly deepen, but we’d wager we’re only looking at marginal price cuts at this point. Consider this: Shop now, shave a couple hundred bucks off of those tech upgrades you’ve been avoiding, then spend your holiday weekend watching football and reheating Thanksgiving leftovers instead of scrolling through Amazon. Your move.

AirPods reliably go on deep discount during Amazon sale events, and Black Friday has proven no different. The third-generation AirPods Pro have dropped to just $179, only $10 shy of their record-low price.

Although the new AirPods 5 aren’t marked down right now, they start at just $129, which is still lower than the discounted Pros. We’re hoping that these will go on sale for the first time on Black Friday. For those who prefer over-ear headphones, the AirPods Max 2 are $120 off (not quite their lowest price, but nothing to scoff at). You can also save on Beats wireless headphones and earbuds, including $150 off the ultra-popular Beats Studio Pro, which have Apple’s chip under the hood.

No Apple product experienced higher price hikes than laptops. The MacBook Air jumped $200 to $1,299 and the MacBook Pro climbed $400 to $1,999, driven by rising RAM and storage costs. To ease the pain, both the 13- and 15-inch MacBook Air models are up to 19% off. We haven’t seen deals this deep on Macs in quite some time.

If you prefer the Pro, those aren’t quite as discounted, but you can still shave up to $250 off of a souped-up model. Plus, Apple’s most affordable laptop, the MacBook Neo, just went on sale for the first time. Sure, it’s only a $50 discount, but it’s still the cheapest model by far—and fits the bill for most basic needs like watching Netflix and renewing your lease.

Although Apple Watches haven’t fallen victim to price increases this year, deals have been few and far between, which means these Black Friday discounts are liable to sell out fast.

The brand-new Apple Watch Series 12—which ushered in Apple’s first stab at readiness scores—is $70 off, dropping this to its lowest price ever. If you’re looking for a budget-friendly Apple Watch, opt for the SE 3, which has dipped to under $200—a verifiable steal given Apple’s current pricing dynamics. While the rugged Apple Watch Ultra 4 isn’t on sale right now, we’ll keep you posted should any surprise sales arise.

Apple Watch Series 12 (GPS+ Cellular, 42mm)

Back in June, Apple bumped the price of its entire iPad range by up to $200, which makes scoring one on sale all the more crucial. As predicted, previous-gen iPad Minis (an editor favorite) are $100 off after the revamped A20 Pro model was released earlier this month. Should you prefer a full-sized tablet, entry-level iPads, iPad Airs, and iPad Pros are all on sale, so it’s mostly a matter of use case in determining which to buy. If you’re going to watch TV on it while traveling, opt for the $399 model, while the Pro is better suited for getting creative work done.

Accessories are reliably among the easiest Apple products to save on during major sales. If you want to stick with Apple-branded gear, the second-generation AirTag, complete with improved Precision Finding, is sitting at a record-low $23, in addition to discounts on Apple Pencils, Magic Keyboards, and the like. You can also save on plenty of third-party power banks, wall adapters, and wireless chargers, which tend to see steeper discounts than Apple’s own gear.
