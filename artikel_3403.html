The Flash's Dark Reboot Has Wally Facing His First God-Tier Villain

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

When one thinks about god-tier DC villains, names like Darkseid, the Anti-Monitor, Trigon, and Eclipso may come to mind. But some of the most incomprehensible terrors of DC's vast roster of villains, obscure to iconic, have proven themselves to be deadly without being tied to a franchise-defining run of comics. Such is the case for M'nagalah, first introduced in Swamp Thing, which made its Absolute Universe debut today. Despite some notably changed origins as part of a surprise encounter for the Flash in the Microverse, M'nagalah is largely as dangerous as ever, retaining the abilities of telepathy and the ability to enslave organic creatures.

Following his initial reveal in the very last issue, Absolute Flash #21 showed M'nagalah calling out to a vulnerable Grodd remembering the death of his father. While M'nagalah's attempt to take over Grodd was successfully resisted, he then moved onto the wounded Wally West, who was quickly taken under his control, and sent to attack Ray Palmer and everyone else aboard the Atom. The moment could have spelled disaster for everyone aboard Palmer's vessel, if not for the additional discovery of one key ally who'd be instrumental to overcoming an eldritch creature like M'nagalah.

Shortly after Grodd broke free from M'nagalah's influence, Wally's body was visibly infected and controlled by M'nagalah's fleshy mass, aiming next to take over Palmer and everyone else aboard. Given M'nagalah's abilities have largely been tied to controlling and absorbing organic matter, alongside powerful telepathy that non-telepathic characters can't reasonably resist, he quickly becomes an extreme danger to everyone aboard. With an old moniker like "Cancer God", it's a safe bet to be cautious around M'nagalah.

A classic Justice League hero gets a new origin story that might make them essential to the future of DC Comics' most popular timeline.

Described in this continuity as a "carnivorous mind-virus from a distant star," M'nagalah seems to hold onto his credentials as a god-tier threat. This is despite existing seemingly only in the Microverse as far as the Absolute Universe is concerned. The scope of his powers suggests extreme danger if he is left unchecked at least in the sense that he's a cosmic terror able to bend most organic life to his will, and absorb it into his essence. But fortunately for the Atom's passengers, Palmer's captive aboard the vessel turned out to be the perfect counter.

With M'nagalah's powers making him extremely dangerous to organic life, the presence of the Absolute Universe's Cyborg, Victor Stone, is certainly convenient for everyone during this attack. Already bonded with Kilg%re giving him his powers, Cyborg's suite of techno-organic enhancements turns out to be the perfect counter against something tailored to conquering strictly organic creatures. Cyborg successfully resists M'nagalah's attempts to absorb him next, turning the tables and instead encasing M'nagalah in a floating steel sphere, bringing him under his control.

The implications of having both Kilg%re and M'nagalah, if solely just not the tongue-twisters of their names alone, have yet to be fully revealed in Absolute Flash. After all, this is a pair of characters who have traditionally faced the Flash, Swamp Thing, and the Atom, among others, in the past through other continuities. Time will tell whether Absolute Cyborg can keep both threats under his control, and perhaps way more excitingly, what sort of powers he'll reap from having them both.
