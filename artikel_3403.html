Watch Rosalía Bring Operatic Flair to Latin Grammys Performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Rosalía has officially returned to the Latin Grammys stage. The Spanish superstar brought all of the drama when she emerged from a smoke-covered stage wearing a gold gown surrounding by a flank of dancers.

Rosalía embodied a sun goddess as she sang three songs, starting with “La Rumba Del Perdón” before diving into fan-favorite “La Perla” and finishing off with the sassy write-off “Dios Es Un Stalker.” Just before she belted out the final note in that song, she took a moment to commemorate the night. “Welcome to the Latin Grammys,” Rosalía told the audience. “Tonight is to enjoy yourself.”

This year, Rosalía is up for her fourth album Lux, which earned seven nods including Album of the Year, Song of the Year, and Best Pop Song. The Spanish singer is also up for Best Portuguese Language Song for Lux cut “Memória.” Portuguese is one of the 14 languages Rosalía employed on the album. The singer has earned 11 overall Latin Grammys in her career.

It marks the Spanish artist’s first performance at the Latin Grammys in three years. Back in 2023, when the ceremony was hosted in her homeland of Spain, Rosalía delivered a soulful rendition of Rocío Jurado’s ballad “Se Nos Rompió El Amor.” She hasn’t performed an original song at the Latin Grammys since her pop-forward offering of “Despecha” in 2022. That year Rosalía made Latin Grammy history with her Album of the Year win for Motomami, becoming the first woman to win the biggest award of the night twice. In 2019, she took home the award for her groundbreaking album El Mal Querer.

Karol G, Daddy Yankee, Feid, Silvana Estrada, Jorge Drexler, Mon Laferte, Anitta, Carín León, Rawayana, and Elena Rose, and more are also set to perform at the 2026 Latin Grammys. Maluma and Roselyn Sánchez are hosting the ceremony once again.
