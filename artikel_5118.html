Lighter loses $15.5M in sudden drain and blames a single API leak

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Bitquery traced 10.39 million USDC off Lighter, while a second alleged loss and the recovery outcome remain unresolved.

Perpetual futures exchange Lighter suspended withdrawals on Nov. 16 after a security incident that resulted in an estimated loss of roughly $15.5 million.

Bitquery traced 10.39 million USDC leaving its Ethereum contract, and Lighter stated in a subsequent update that its investigation indicated a leaked API key for a specific trader's account caused the incident.

It said the protocol and smart contracts were unaffected and that it was targeting a resumption of withdrawals by the end of Nov. 16.

According to Bitquery, the larger loss estimate concerns two market-maker accounts, which did not verify the internal account-loss allegations.

Bitquery identified one withdrawal of approximately 10,393,057 USDC on Nov. 16. The investigation separates that transfer from a second alleged loss of over $5 million. Bitquery says that if those funds left the second account, they had not left Lighter within its Ethereum observation window.

According to Bitquery, five orders on the CoW Swap exchange converted 10.36 million USDC into Ethereum between 10:13 and 10:17 UTC.

Bitquery also traced twelve payments of 160 ETH and associated accounts on the Hyperliquid exchange buying the XMR1 token. The firm reported 3,845 ETH remaining in the recipient wallet at its 11:55 UTC snapshot.

Lighter said it enabled what it calls Guardian mode and prioritized recovering funds involved in an additional withdrawal attempt. Its update said withdrawals remained paused and that the Lighter Security Council would upgrade the contract.

The update did not announce a recovered amount or a completed reopening, leaving both the eventual recovery and users' ability to withdraw separate from the funds already traced out.
