Opening a GTA 6 bank vault is one of the best ways to make off with a big score during a robbery, with potentially $100,000 or more in bills and valuables from a single heist.

But those huge bank vault doors are completely impenetrable, and confusingly, even after you've learned how to drill into a vault as part of a Chapter 3 story mission, you still won't be able to open vault doors in open world bank robberies.

Here's the trick: you've got to get someone to open bank vault doors for you. Only then can you steal all that sweet cash.

Your GTA 6 fence, Yasmani, will begin offering you tips once you start selling him stolen goods in Chapter 3: jewelry store heists targeting specific items and which ATM jobs have the most cash, for example. These are worth doing anyway as they get you the snap gun.

Complete every job Yasmani has and keep asking for more, and in Chapter 4 he'll offer you a tip for $10,000. Pay it, and he'll give you the locations of two security vans (armored cars) in Vice City. They'll show up on your map as pink icons.

There are multiple banks with vaults that armored cars visit. While driving around the areas near these banks and keeping your eyes peeled, you may spot an armored car without Yasmani's tip. It could take quite a while, though!

You can see which banks have vaults by looking under "Big Bank Jobs" in the "Robberies" category on your map. There are four big banks in Ocean Beach, two downtown, one in Tequesta, and one at Vice City International Airport.

Follow the armored car until it parks outside a bank. Watch as one of the drivers gets out and walks into the bank. You can follow him to see where he goes, though bank layouts differ and he may take different routes. He may walk through the front doors, or he may go in a side entrance or a roll-up door in the back.

Once he's all the way inside—not just inside the front doors, but deep into the bank—he'll head to the vault and open it.

You can't open that vault yourself! Only the armored car driver can. That's the trick.

Once the guard is in the back of the bank, begin the robbery. Search for the vault in the back. You may need to use explosives to open the door the guard went through, or you can look for a door you can open with the snap gun. You'll find the vault door open with the armored car driver nearby. Subdue him and grab all the cash off the cart inside the vault.

There are several safe deposit boxes in the vault you can open for extra loot. Use your focus ability to highlight which boxes you can loot, and shoot them to open them up. You can also blow open all the boxes at once using a grenade or sticky bomb. Grab the loot from each.

If you really want to clean the place out, there's still all the cash drawers in the front of the bank and a small safe, or multiple safes, somewhere else, usually in an office in the back. You can open these standard safes with safe codes.

The armored car parked outside has loot in it, too. The thing is, it may despawn while you're inside the bank. If you want to loot it, place a sticky explosive on the rear door, which won't let the truck despawn. (Be stealthy: there's another guard still watching the truck). When you leave the bank after the robbery, detonate the bomb and grab the loot.

The best way to enter a bank is through a back door, if the building has one, (using the snap gun to avoid alarms) or a roll-up door. If the guard you're following goes in the back, he may leave the doors unlocked behind him, and you may not even have to unlock any doors at all to reach the vault. Just be prepared to run into multiple security guards and employees you'll need to subdue.

You will want both Jason and Lucia along for any bank vault jobs you pull. There is so much cash and valuables to grab you'll easily fill up four bags, which requires two people to carry. (You can hand loot bags to your partner by interacting with them and choosing the "Hand over bag" prompt). You'll also want one character to work as crowd control while you're looting the bank.

A silenced gun is a good idea: you want to delay setting off alarms for as long as possible, and if bank employees or citizens hear gunshots they may panic and trigger the alarm, or flee from the bank and call the cops.

In a lot of robberies you can find the security footage and destroy it while the robbery is in progress. Big banks have cloud-based cameras which won't allow you to destroy the footage. Keep a lookout (using focus) for cameras and shoot them out (with silenced pistols) as quickly as you can.

If an alarm goes off during a vault robbery, you'll get an instant four-star wanted level, which means SWAT trucks, roadblocks, and helicopters. Park your car in a good spot outside so you can jump into it easily, and have some idea of where you want to drive to during your escape.
