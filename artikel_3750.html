FC 27 Hall of FUT: Unleashed Challenge 1 SBC solutions and rewards

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Hall of FUT: Unleashed Challenge 1 SBC is now live in FC 27, and we have a new puzzle challenge to solve. The SBC is relatively easy to complete, and it can be done at no cost. Let’s find out how to complete the challenge and unlock all the available rewards.

The Hall of FUT: Unleashed Challenge 1 SBC has just one task, but there are specific requirements.

Ideally, you should have all the cards required to complete this challenge for free. However, you also need to meet the requirements to do so. If you get stuck, you can use the following solution to complete the challenge.

You’ll get an untradeable Gold Pack after completing the challenge. The free pack provides you with at least two player items and nine golden cards in total. While the chances of a walkout are non-existent, it’s still nice to have the free fodder. You might also find valuable consumables like Shadow, Hunter, and more.

Hence, make sure to complete the challenge before it expires in Ultimate Team.
