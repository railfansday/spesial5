Bachelorette’s Andi Dorfman Gives Birth to Baby No. 2 With Blaine Hart

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Andi Dorfman and her husband, Blaine Hart, have officially welcomed their second baby.

The Bachelorette alum, 39, and Hart, 40, announced the birth of their newest bundle of joy via Instagram on Sunday, November 8, sharing a photo from the hospital featuring Dorfman and Hart’s hands holding their new bundle of joy’s tiny foot with the hospital ID tag still attached.

“Absolutely head over heels in love,” Dorfman captioned the post.

Reality TV fans followed the reality TV star’s journey to find love as a contestant on season 18 of The Bachelor and as the lead of The Bachelorette’s 10th season in 2014. She later reconnected with Hart in Italy in the summer of 2021 after previously meeting him through a mutual friend 15 years earlier.

The couple announced their engagement in March 2022 and went on to tie the knot in May 2023. One year later, Dorfman revealed in August 2024 that she was pregnant with her and Hart’s first child, and their daughter, Harper, arrived that December.

In June 2026, Dorfman and Hart shared via Instagram that they were expecting again.

“Harper, the BIG SIS coming this fall!” she gushed in the caption at the time. “So grateful for baby #2 to join the crew. These are truly what our dreams are made of 🥹🥰.”

In the accompanying photos, Dorfman and Hart beamed as Harper — wearing a denim jacket with “big sister” on the back — held up black-and-white sonogram images of her younger sibling. The former reality star also debuted her baby bump in a cream off-the-shoulder dress.

That same month, Dorfman revealed they were expecting another baby girl. She also offered an update on her pregnancy journey via her Instagram Stories.

“Secret is out. The bump has been bumping hard lately,” she said in a video at the time. “I feel like a lot of people have noticed, so [I am] relieved to finally announce that we are having baby No. 2, yay.”

She added, “Thanks for all the well-wishes, and lots of you are asking how I’m feeling. Thanks for that. I will say with the first pregnancy, I was like, ‘Blah,’ and then with this pregnancy, I have been like [mimics throwing up].”

Dorfman acknowledged that her second pregnancy had not been easy.

“And with that, I have just come to the conclusion that I am not one of those women [who] wears pregnancy very well,” she shared. “In fact, I guess you can say pregnancy wears me, but we’re doing good. Everything’s healthy, very grateful for that.”

As Dorfman prepared to welcome her second child, she expressed her eagerness to see Harper bond with her sibling.

“I’m really excited for Harper to become a big sis, little terrified honestly,” she joked. “I think she’s going to be great and I’ll keep you updated. Baby No. 2 coming this fall, ahh!”

Dorfman previously hinted at plans to expand her and Hart’s family after Harper’s birth.

“We’ve always said we wanted three but going to take it one at a time,” she revealed in an April 2025 Instagram Story Q&A. “I want some time this year for us to be with just Harper and to recover, so maybe [we’ll] start trying at the end of this year or early 2026.”
