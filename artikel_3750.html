Artist and developer Temmie Chang, best known for work on Undertale and all five current Deltarune chapters, has released the third game in a short trilogy that's separate from Toby Fox's seminal RPGs, though they also feature Fox in the credits for melodies.

Chang shared Of the World, part three after Escaped Chasm and Dweller's Empty Path, over the weekend. It's playable in-browser through Itch.io, but it's recommended that you download the game to ensure your progress is saved following future updates.

"It is recommended to play Escaped Chasm and Dweller's Empty Path first," Chang advises.

Escaped Chasm is described as "A small little RPGmaker game about a lonely girl with a connection to another world that she sees in her dreams." Dweller's Empty Path is very similar, focusing mostly on "walking around and talking to NPCs and objects."

The third game in the series, which keeps the recognizable pixel art and exploration but seems to add more complex combat mechanics, has sparked quite a response so far. Chang's reveal post on Twitter has 1.8 million views, according to Twitter metrics. The game's YouTube trailer has over 330,000 views at the time of writing.

"Thank you for taking an interest in my little game!!" reads an Itch update from Change. "I'm once again blown away and surprised people have taken interest in my little games." Change adds, "Thank you again for playing, it really means a lot! I'm a little embarrassed by all the attention like this, but it is super appreciated nonetheless!"

Just days ago, we heard that Deltarune Chapter 6 is now "structurally complete" and work on Chapter 7 has "just started." Fox says, "Unlike every other Chapter, I actually feel like I know what I'm doing this time, so I have a lot of confidence...!" So far, Deltarune has amassed a staggering 131,300 reviews on Steam, clocking in at 98% positive.
