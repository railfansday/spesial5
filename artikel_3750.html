How to get the Squiplash Sprite in Fortnite

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Squiplash Sprite is one of three new additions you can find in the third week of Treasure Hunters in Fortnite. This Sprite is quite funny to look at, but it comes with a really useful in-game ability. Moreover, it’s also available in all three special variants if you manage to find it.

As I said, the Squiplash Sprite has been added with two other Sprites: Mini Miku and Zee. Compared to the Zee Sprite, Squiplash is lot easier to get since it’s not a Mythic Sprite. However, there’s no guaranteed spot where you can find it (which isn’t the case with Mini Miku).

To raise your chances, you should aim to open as many chests as possible. You can also try your luck with Vaults since they offer you Rare Chests, which have better odds. Look out for Sprite Chests that spawn at random locations.

You can also find Squiplash near the Extraction sites, which happened in my case. If you kill another player who has the Squiplash in their inventory/equipped on them, you can add it to your inventory. Once done, make sure to extract the Sprite to add it to your collection.

Like all other Sprites, the Squiplash also has a unique in-game ability. When equipped, the Sprite deploys a healing smoke bomb whenever your health drops under a certain threshold. The same healing effect will be applicable for your allies as well, making it a really impressive Sprite to bring in team fights.
