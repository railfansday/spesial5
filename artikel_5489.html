Inside the $7 Million Historic Georgia Estate Straight Out of ‘Practical Magic’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Tucked away along Pine Log Creek in Fairmount, GA, sits a nearly 71-acre historic estate that feels less like a residential listing and more like a Hollywood set.

Known as Yarborough Mill, the $7 million property features a restored late-1800s Queen Anne Victorian—a 4,500-square-foot former grist mill and plenty of period charm.

"It looks like something that belongs in 'Practical Magic'," says listing agent Logan Fitts with Mountain Sotheby's International Realty. “It is definitely the most historic property I've had the chance to work with, and with that it is just so unique.”

The property has several structures, but the highlights are the former grist mill and a late 1800s Queen Anne Victorian.

“Everything's original, but it's all been restored because it was not in perfect condition when [the sellers] bought it. It's a complete restore project and everything is true to period,” Fitts explains.

“The exterior of the main house, the colors she chose, the roof tiles, the architecture, and the landscaping. Everything there was intentional.”

The Victorian home has some Georgian features and has five bedrooms and three full bathrooms.

“It looks like it could be in The Gilded Age or something like that. There are a total of eight fireplaces in the house. There's parlors, there's dens and small living rooms, and little nooks and crannies throughout the house,” he says, adding the kitchen is updated appliance-wise, but still has character.

“[There are] stained glass windows throughout, and pressed tin ceilings throughout. It’s insanely intricate.”

But it’s another room that caught Fitts’ eye.

“They have an old solarium on the lower level that they turned into the coolest bathroom that I've ever seen,” he says.

“You have to be in there to really see it. If you’re entertaining in the house, anyone what visits or goes to that bathroom is just totally wowed. It just it feels like you're in a solarium, but it is a bathroom.”

This home has been the seller’s primary residence, with a three-bedroom and three-bathroom cottage for guests and others, especially during events. The seller has operated a small wedding business on the property.

“She's done it herself. It has been more of a passion for her. It's a wedding venue for small groups and the guest cottage is used as a bridal suite.”

The groom’s suite and game area are above the barn, and the 4,500-square foot former grist mill is the event space.

Fitts says most of the wedding ceremonies happen along the waterfront along Pine Log Creek, which creates a stunning backdrop.

“You pull up to a gate and [after] the gate opens, you go down this long winding driveway with rows of trees and the river that winds through the entire property to your right,” he explains. “Then you turn in up the bluff a little bit, and then it kind of opens up to what you would see in the olden days, like its own little village town.”

An old general store and smokehouse are also on the property. Fitts says the sellers saw a for sale listing in the newspaper at least 15 years ago, drove up to see it, fell in love with it, and then restored it.

“I was impressed with the story behind it [and] the amount of love that went into owning the property,” he says, adding there are two kinds of buyers who would be perfect for this estate.

“It could be a visionary entrepreneur that really wants to lean into making this a hospitality-based venue, spa, bed and breakfast, a true sanctuary. There's potential for growth,” Fitts explains, adding the buyer could build other structures throughout the orchard and make the main home a gathering space.

“It could be somebody's true entrepreneurial dream or it could just be someone that truly loves historical properties that wants to preserve what is there, love it with their family or for themselves, and just cherish it for what it is.”
