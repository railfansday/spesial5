EXCLUSIVE: Betsey Johnson Relists California Home With Over $100K Price Cut

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Betsey Johnson has relisted her California property with an $140,000 price cut—putting its new asking price at $6.8 million.

The iconic fashion designer, 84, snapped up the 4,300-square-foot abode on April 11, 2025, paying $6.85 million.

Just over a year later, on July 27, 2026, Johnson listed the home for $7.5 million. By Sept. 15, the price had been slashed by half a million dollars, asking $6.99 million. A month later, on Oct. 17, the listing was removed.

Now, the home has once again appeared on the selling block with a reduced asking price, being listed with Chris Cortazzo of Compass.

The property, which was built in 1957, boasts five bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms.

"Tucked behind gates on Eldorado Meadow Road, this single-level contemporary residence enjoys an exceptional vantage point within Hidden Hills, with expansive views of rolling hills, distant mountain ridgelines, and beautiful open meadows," the listing reads.

Surrounded by lush greenery, the home boasts a spa-inspired pool and plenty of space for entertaining.

Upon entering the home, guests are greeted with an expansive floor plan and a chic lounging area that flows effortlessly into the dining space and opens up to the outdoors.

The kitchen is surely the crown jewel of the property as it comes complete with a black marble island and state-of-the-art appliances.

"Inside, wide-plank wood floors and exposed beams carry through the open living and dining areas. A glass-front wine display sits just off the entry, while a wall of pocketing glass opens the living room to the pool and mountains beyond. French doors beside the dining area lead to a covered terrace. In the kitchen, richly veined black marble extends from the backsplash to a waterfall island with counter seating. Woven pendants, wood cabinetry, a six-burner range with double ovens, Thermador appliances, and a concealed bar complete the space," adds the listing.

The bedrooms are the perfect place to relax and boast enough space for a queen-sized bed.

The primary bathroom features a large soaking tub and a glass-enclosed shower.

The property also comes complete with a large walk-in closet and an office space.

"Beyond, the primary suite occupies nearly an entire wing. A fireplace and white-painted beams frame the bedroom, and a sunlit sitting area opens directly to the poolside deck. The dressing room is lined with built-in cabinetry. In the bath, slatted wood walls complement a floating black stone double vanity, a glass-enclosed shower with dual showerheads, and a freestanding soaking tub set against a broad garden-facing window," the description continues.

Moving outside, the grounds offer a lovely area to kick back and enjoy the California sunshine.

"A poolside sun deck and outdoor shower complete the garden. Additional amenities include integrated audio, home automation, a two-car garage, and gated driveway parking for approximately 10 to 12 vehicles," adds the listing.

The designer's real estate portfolio has an impressive track record.

In 2019, the designer—who had previously spent 55 years living in New York City—sold her one-of-a-kind pink mobile home for $1.95 million.

The Malibu dwelling was located in Paradise Cove Mobile Home Park, which is considered to be one of the most famous and luxurious trailer parks in the country.

The picturesque community of 270 homes has attracted many notable residents, including Sarah Paulson, Stevie Nicks, and Pamela Anderson.

Before offloading the mobile home, Johnson spent over $500,000 turning it into a playful wonderland decorated with countless bohemian prints, florals, and electric colors. There was even a fully functional outdoor tub.

“I would like people to think of this as a funny little museum,” she told People magazine in 2018, referring to the expansive collection of antiques and vintage finds she had displayed in the home.

The mobile home had two bedrooms and two bathrooms, and plenty of room for her daughter, Lulu Johnson, 41, and two grandchildren, Layla and Ella, who reportedly also lived in Malibu, visiting frequently.

“I’ve made lots of mistakes with boyfriends and husbands,” said Johnson, who has been married three times. “I don’t think I’ve made more than two mistakes with my houses, purchases for my houses, or the clothes in my closet.”

It wasn’t long before Johnson would find herself in yet another pink mobile home in Malibu, moving into a 2,300-square-foot home in the gated Point Dume Club.

“I love the idea that, with a trailer park, you’re in a kind of community,” she told the New York Times Style Magazine in 2021. “At my age, if I fall down in the middle of the street, I want someone to go, ‘Hey! What’s that girl doing in the middle of the street?’”

Soon after moving in, Johnson papered the walls with vintage wallpaper that perfectly matched the floral print from her childhood home.

Pink homes are somewhat of a tradition for Johnson, who famously lived in a bubblegum pink loft in Manhattan in the 1980s—the photos of which still regularly circulate on blogs and social media accounts dedicated to whimsical design.

And although the exterior is not pink, Johnson does also have a four-bedroom, six-bathroom vacation home in Guerrero, Mexico, where her half-punk, half-ballerina touch can be found in every room.

According to Elle Decor, the property—aptly named “Betseyvilla”—was previously offered as a rental through Airbnb, although the listing appears to no longer be available.
