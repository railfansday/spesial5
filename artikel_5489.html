S&P Global Announces Pricing of Public Offering of $750,000,000 Senior Notes due 2031, $600,000,000 Senior Notes due 2034 and $650,000,000 Senior Notes due 2036

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

NEW YORK, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- S&P Global (NYSE: SPGI) (the "Company") today announced that it has priced a public offering of $750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.600% senior notes due 2031, $600,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.800% senior notes due 2034 and $650,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 6.000% senior notes due 2036. BofA Securities, Inc., Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC, Citigroup Global Markets Inc., Deutsche Bank Securities Inc., J.P. Morgan Securities LLC, Mizuho Securities USA LLC, Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC, UBS Securities LLC and Wells Fargo Securities, LLC are acting as joint book-running managers in connection with the offering.

The senior notes due 2031 were priced at 99.841% of their principal amount with a yield to maturity of 5.635%, the senior notes due 2034 were priced at 99.927% of their principal amount with a yield to maturity of 5.814% and the senior notes due 2036 were priced at 99.985% of their principal amount with a yield to maturity of 6.001%. Each series of notes will be an unsecured obligation of the Company and will be guaranteed by its subsidiary, Standard & Poor's Financial Services LLC. The closing of the sale of the notes is expected to occur on or about November 18, 2026, subject to customary closing conditions.

The Company intends to use the net proceeds from the offering for general corporate purposes, which may include, without limitation, funding of possible acquisitions, repayment, redemption or refinancing of indebtedness, capital expenditures, working capital, satisfaction of other obligations or repurchase of the Company's outstanding common stock. The Company may temporarily invest the net proceeds of this offering in short-term, liquid investments until they are used for their stated purpose.

The offering is being made pursuant to an effective automatic shelf registration statement on Form S-3 (File No. 333-299594 and 333-299594-01), filed by the Company with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the "SEC") on October 23, 2026, and only by means of a prospectus supplement and accompanying prospectus.
