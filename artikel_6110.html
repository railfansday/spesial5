Airbnb Floats Price-Matching: A Skift Test Shows Where Direct Booking Wins

Are we one day going to see an Airbnb best price guarantee for short-term rentals? AI agents may force the issue.

What happens to Airbnb when personal AI agents can look at a listing and instantly check the price on the property’s own website, where Airbnb doesn’t collect a commission?

Using the AI agent Instinct, Skift compared 16 Airbnb listings with rates on the properties’ own websites. The direct site was cheaper in 10 — by as much as 42% — and one was essentially a tie.

When an account on X questioned whether Airbnb’s profits would suffer if AI agents could compare prices right before guests book, CEO Brian Chesky replied: “You presume we could never offer a price-match guarantee.”

The post wasn’t an announcement that Airbnb will match prices on short-term rentals like it recently started for hotels. But it signals that Airbnb could, and the test suggests it may eventually have to.

Skift ran a small test using the AI agent Instinct to compare Airbnb listings against the same properties' own websites, and direct was cheaper in 10 of 16 comparable listings — by as much as 42% at Uniko Living in Mexico City, where a superior room cost $84 direct against $145 on Airbnb. The test followed a post on X in which Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky replied to a question about AI agents comparing prices at the point of booking: "You presume we could never offer a price-match guarantee." Airbnb already price-matches on hotels, but has not announced a comparable guarantee for short-term rentals, and declined to comment for the story. The comparisons were not apples to apples: Airbnb totals were pre-tax, several direct quotes excluded city or tourist tax, and cancellation terms were not matched, so any guarantee would have to define which total it matches. The test also exposed how little of this an agent can do today — of 45 listings, 23 had no direct booking site, and six of the 22 that did failed to return a price. Savvy CEO Eric Goldreyer told Skift that Airbnb can price match only by giving up its own margin or by forcing rate parity on hosts and managers, pointing to Vrbo's new clause requiring hosts to give it terms at least as favorable as those on their own sites.
