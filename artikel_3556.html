Thursday Night Football Had Lowest Viewership All Season On GTA 6 Launch Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Thursday Night Football matchup on November 19, the day that GTA 6 came out, had the lowest viewership of the entire season so far for a TNF game, but the low ratings might not be due entirely to competition with GTA 6.

The data shows that 12.23 million people, on average, watched the November 19 matchup between the Indianapolis Colts and Houston Texans on Prime Video. That was a 9% slide from the Week 11 Thursday Night Football matchup in 2025 featuring the New England Patriots and New York Jets.

Looking at week-over-week, the November 12 matchup this year between the Washington Commanders and New York Giants drew 16.27 million viewers, and was up 17% compared to the Week 10 matchup in 2025 featuring the Las Vegas Raiders and Denver Broncos.

Due to the poor ratings of the November 19 TNF game, the full-season average now for TNF games this season sank to 16.62 million on per game.

One expert told GameSpot that live sports like football probably won't be too heavily impacted by GTA 6 because people want to watch in real time. The low ratings for the November 19 matchup could be partially attributable to competition with GTA 6, but a likelier scenario is that people tuned out because it was a boring game in which the heavily favored Texans throttled the Colts by a score of 26-3.

The Texans were up 17-3 at the half, and the game only had nine more points total scored in the second half--all by the Texans. Not exactly thrilling stuff that people would stay glued to.

Ahead of GTA 6's launch, people also wondered how The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, which released on November 20, might be impacted. It may have been, but the film still put up massive numbers at the box office that exceeded expectations.

GTA 6 sold 32.5 million copies via pre-purchases alone and generated close to $4 billion on day one. The game will continue to sell, and it should get another jolt of sales when the PC edition arrives in 2027, as rumored.
