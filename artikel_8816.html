Stocking-Ready Tri Gifts $20 and Under

Summary: The Triathlete editors combed through dozens of websites to find under-$20 giftable triathlon accessories and essentials that will fit in a stocking.

Triathlon has a lot of gear, which usually means high price tags and a whole lot of required storage space. But these compact tri gifts pack a punch in value – they make training and racing safer, more comfortable, or more convenient, and all for $20 or less.

Even better: Some of these products are already on sale for Black Friday and the holidays. So get to shopping — these Santa-approved stocking stuffers will have the triathlete in your life ready to sleigh their next race.

Gift a little swim-spiration with this Rosie the Riveter–inspired message on a latex-free silicone swim cap. You can never have too many swim caps, and this one protects hair from chlorine, keeps goggle straps in place, and makes swimmers visible, especially on open-water swims.

The off-season is the perfect time for a handlebar retape, and triathletes will love this popular tape from one of the oldest brands in cycling. There’s a reason this cork tape has been around since the 1980s – it has the right amount of cushioning, a no-slip grip, and is self-adhesive, making it easy to install. Available in multiple colors, and it comes with black end plugs.

Sure, these belts for holding your racing bibs on the run are not the flashiest, but goodness do they make T2 simpler on race day. They need to be replaced every couple of years as sun and sweat break down the elastic, and this Huub one is great quality. The two toggles for bib attachment are as easy as it gets.

Gu acknowledges that eating energy gels on race day can get a little … sticky. They partnered with Hydrapak to design this 150-milliliter soft flask, which can hold up to five Gu Energy Gels (or three Gu Liquid Energy Gels), can easily slip into a pocket, and offers a high-flow nozzle. This makes a thoughtful upgrade for long-course triathletes who are tired of messy gels!

These nifty little laces turn any pair of running shoes into slip-ons, perfect for a speedy bike-run transition, and their elasticity keeps your shoes secure. The original 48-inch laces come in 13 different colors because we know: Aesthetics matter on race day.

Yes, we know this little multi-purpose towel was designed for backpacking and camping, but its super-absorbent design can come in very handy for triathletes – whether to dry your feet in T1, wipe down your bike after a sweaty indoor training session, or dry off or wipe down gear after a swim workout. It comes in a small carry pouch with a mini carabiner, so you can clip it to your swim bag or transition bag for easy access.

Keep goggles fog-free with this 2-ounce, easy-to-apply solution (and it’s a big upgrade from using your own saliva!). The formula is environmentally friendly and dries quickly.

Adjust your handlebar, stem, saddle, disc brakes, and seat post (and more!) with the hex wrenches, hex socket, and flathead screwdriver that all fit into this compact, 110-gram multi-tool from a well-established cycling tool brand.

Experience the wonder of toe socks with this low-profile Injinji pair – their signature five-toe design prevents blisters and allows your toes to splay naturally when running. They’re made from sweat-wicking fibers and post-consumer recycled polyester.

Keep your tire repair kit as lightweight as possible with this mini inflator head, possibly the smallest on the market. It uses a twist-style design – no trigger or pump action – that can simply be threaded and then unthreaded to start the flow of CO2.

Get on-the-go injury self-treatment with this 3-inch rubber spiky massage ball that provides deep-tissue relief. It’s especially effective for plantar fasciitis.

Make any post-sunset workout safer with these versatile flashing LED reflectors. It uses a dual magnet to attach to shorts, waistband, caps, hydration belt, or sleeves, and it weighs less than half an ounce, so you won’t even feel it.
