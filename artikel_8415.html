Personal Investments • Re: How to incorporate an inheritance into retirement plan??

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

I already responded upstream about how I view it as a contingency just like I view good/bad returns in the market. The part I've been thinking about recently is how would I invest the money when/if it comes. I don't think I have a handle on that yet, even though I think it would be prudent to have such a plan. Sure, putting the cart before the horse by doing so, but why not be prepared? For example, I know most of any inheritance will be in a taxable account (very small IRA as well). Approximately 50% of any inheritance I get I will never use (my kids will eventually inherit it). So, thinking the best investment there would be a TSM index fund like VTI. Or maybe it should be an total market int'l fund to realize the foreign tax credit? The other 50% I would probably use over the next 10-15 years. That will enable me to do Roth conversions before RMDs begin. For that money, thinking a TIPS ladder may be most appropriate even in a taxable account (I live in NY so will get some state tax benefit there). So, these are just thoughts in my head right now, not really a formal plan. But why not have a loose plan? If the day does not come for another 10 years, I am totally covered and happy to spend more time with family. If all the inheritance is used, I'm good too because I have a plan for that just like I have a plan if markets do poorly.
