Marketing for the Future Launches Free Marketing Budget Calculator for Home Service Companies

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

TUCSON, Ariz., Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Marketing for the Future, LLC, a marketing agency serving home service companies across the United States and Canada, today announced the launch of a free Marketing Budget Calculator. The tool helps business owners determine exactly how much they should spend on marketing to hit their revenue goals, based on their industry, current revenue, and desired growth pace. Available now at mktg4thefuture.com/home-service-marketing-budget-calculator, the calculator is also free for ad agencies and marketing firms to embed directly on their own websites.

Instead of basing recommendations on trailing revenue, the calculator works backward from a company's revenue goal. Users select their industry, enter their trailing-twelve-month revenue, set a target for the next twelve months, and choose a growth pace of Light, Moderate, or Aggressive. The tool then generates a recommended annual and monthly marketing budget, broken down by quarter and week, along with a suggested split across marketing channels. Results update instantly, with no email signup required.

"Business owners need clear, data-driven insight into what it truly takes to scale in today's digital landscape. This tool was built to provide exactly that. Having spent two decades in digital marketing, I can say with confidence that the level of competition we are seeing right now is unprecedented," said Caleb Tennenbaum, Founder & CEO of Marketing for the Future.

The calculator addresses this gap by calibrating budget recommendations to typical investment levels within each trade, accounting for factors like competition and customer acquisition patterns specific to that industry.

Ad agencies and marketing firms can embed the calculator on their own websites at no cost. Every embed includes the Marketing for the Future logo and a link back to mktg4thefuture.com, giving agencies a useful client tool while extending the company's brand reach. Embedding requires only downloading the calculator file, uploading it to a website, and pasting a short code snippet onto the desired page.

Marketing for the Future positions the calculator as a starting point for a broader conversation about marketing investment. The company encourages business owners and agencies alike to use the tool to benchmark their current spend, plan for growth, and avoid the common pitfalls of underfunding or overfunding their marketing efforts.
