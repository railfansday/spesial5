Switch 2’s Black Friday Bundle With Mario Kart World Is Available Now For $500

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It's Black Friday week, and one of the best gaming deals is a Switch 2 bundle featuring Mario Kart World and other extras for $50 off the normal price.

As announced previously, the $500 bundle comes with a Switch 2 and a digital copy of Mario Kart World, as well as a three-month Nintendo Switch Online membership. This is what the bundle cost at launch in 2025, but Nintendo raised the price to $550 due to tariffs and other factors, but now it's back on "sale."

This Switch 2 bundle is only on sale through December 5, or while supplies last. Get the Mario Kart World Switch 2 hardware bundle here:

  • Amazon
  • Best Buy
  • Walmart
  • Target

It’s not the only hardware bundle that people can buy, as Nintendo also offers a system with a digital copy of Nintendo Switch Sports Resort and three months of Switch Online for $530. This bundle is not on sale for Black Friday, though.

Beyond the hardware discount, Nintendo is offering games like Donkey Kong Bananza and Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2 for $20 off, but only through November 29.

There is an eShop Black Friday sale happening as well. Some of the deals include Metroid Dread, Oblivion Remastered, and Kirby and the Forgotten Land for $42 each. For more, check out all the discounts on the eShop.
