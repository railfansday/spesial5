Husband Kills Wife Before Dying by Suicide in Sheriff's Office Parking Lot

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Authorities in Mississippi confirmed that a husband was going to turn himself in for killing his wife before he died by suicide on Saturday, November 14.

The Tunica County Sheriff’s Office confirmed in a press release obtained by Us that the victims were identified as married couple Alanitra Adam-Banks and James Banks, who both lived in Robinsonville, Mississippi.

Authorities learned of the incident when deputies received a call on Saturday around 12:51 p.m. regarding someone who had been shot at the Lexington apartments in Robinsonville. The caller – who has not been identified – reportedly told officers that Banks, 54, had allegedly shot Adam-Banks, 31.

Upon their arrival, deputies found Adam-Banks suffering from multiple gunshot wounds. Pafford emergency medical personnel tried to revive and treat Adam-Banks’ injuries before the Tunica County coroner ultimately declared her dead at the scene.

After Banks was identified as the shooter, authorities managed to get in contact with him on a phone call and he reportedly told authorities that he was on his way to the Tunica County Sheriff’s Office.

Deputies later found Banks’ vehicle in the Sheriff’s Office parking lot and discovered that he had died by suicide, according to the press release.

Authorities said that the deputies and first responders attempted lifesaving measures on Banks, though he was eventually declared dead at the scene.

The Sheriff’s Office went on to say that the investigation into the killings remains ongoing and that the case was being investigated as a homicide-suicide.

Despite confirming the deaths, authorities have not yet released information regarding the events that led up to the shooting. It’s also not currently known if Banks and Adam-Banks had a history of domestic violence disputes in the past or if officers believe Banks had a motive in killing his wife.

Details about their marriage have also not been shared, including how long the couple was together, when they got married and if they shared any children.

The Tunica County Sheriff’s Office had no additional information to share about the findings of the investigation when contacted by Us about updates in the case.

Police are seeking information from the public and have asked that anyone with knowledge regarding the incident contact the Tunica County Sheriff’s Office at 662-363-1411.
