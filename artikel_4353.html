Toyota confirms the Highlander 9X will launch as its first EREV with top-tier tech in early 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Toyota Highlander 9X will go on sale in early 2027 as the brand’s first extended-range electric vehicle with advanced new tech from Huawei and CATL.

Toyota joins Hyundai Motor Group, including Kia and Genesis, Ford, Volkswagen, and several other major OEMs with plans to launch its first EREV in the first quarter of 2027.

Toyota’s Chinese joint venture, GAC Toyota, announced at the Guangzhou Auto Show on November 27 that the EREV will use its flagship SUV nameplate, the Highlander 9X, when it arrives.

The Highlander 9X will blend Toyota’s classic global car heritage with some of China’s most advanced technology.

At the event, GAC Toyota revealed that the flagship EREV will use a battery custom-developed through its partnership with CATL, along with Huawei’s advanced driver assistance system (ADAS) and smart cockpit setup.

The EREV will be Toyota’s fifth “electrified” vehicle, joining battery electric vehicles (BEVs), plug-in hybrids (PHEVs), hybrids (HEVs), and fuel-cell electric vehicles (FCEVs).

Unlike fully electric vehicles, EREVs have a small gas engine that acts as a generator to charge the battery when it gets low, extending driving range.

According to Nikkei, Toyota aims to produce 200,000 EREVs in China in 2027, and about 400,000 units in 2028.

We will have to wait until closer to launch for battery and powertrain specifics, but local reports suggest Toyota’s EREV will deliver a combined CTLC range of over 621 miles (1,000 km) on a full tank of gas and a full charge.

You can catch a glimpse of what appears to be the extended-range vehicle in the video GAC Toyota posted confirming the new EREV tech.

Since the Highlander 9X will be built in China, Toyota will not sell the EREV in the US. Instead, it will launch the fully electric Highlander BEV in the US in 2027.

The all-electric Toyota Highlander BEV will be available with front-wheel-drive and all-wheel-drive powertrains with two battery packs: 77 kWh or 95.8 kWh. The smaller (77 kWh) battery provides up to 287 miles of EPA-estimated range, while the larger (95.8 kWh) battery provides up to 320 miles in FWD models.

Like the outgoing version, the fifth-generation electric Toyota Highlander will offer three rows of seating and space for seven passengers.
