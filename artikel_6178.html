How Embraer and Porter Airlines Consider Matching the Jet to the Market

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Kevin Jackson, President, Porter AirlinesArjan Meijer, President, Embraer Commercial Aviation

Moderated by Meghna Maharishi, Airlines Reporter, Skift

Airlines choosing new aircraft must weigh lower per-seat operating costs against the revenue consequences of deploying more capacity than a market can support. Jackson and Meijer described how smaller aircraft can preserve frequency and support stronger fare mixes on thinner routes, while acknowledging that fleet simplicity remains an important operating consideration.
