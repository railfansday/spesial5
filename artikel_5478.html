Paradigm Vendo Partners with Angi to Support Home Improvement Professionals

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

MIDDLETON, Wis., Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Paradigm Vendo has partnered with Angi to provide home improvement professionals with access to digital sales and quoting technology.

Through the Angi Pro Perks program, eligible Angi pros can utilize their benefits to explore Paradigm Vendo, a guided selling and quoting platform that helps contractors simplify the in-home sales process, configure products, create accurate estimates and deliver professional proposals.

Paradigm Vendo and Angi unite lead generation and digital selling tools to help home improvement contractors grow sales.

"Home improvement contractors are constantly looking for ways to generate the right opportunities and make the most of every appointment," said Stacey Bentley, Paradigm Manager, Sales and Strategic Partnerships. "This partnership brings together two important parts of that process: Angi helps pros connect with homeowners looking to complete projects, while Paradigm Vendo gives contractors the tools to build accurate quotes, present options clearly and move opportunities toward a signed sale."

The partnership also provides new benefits for Paradigm Vendo customers to leverage Angi to connect with homeowners actively planning home improvement projects.
