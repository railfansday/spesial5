Musk and SpaceX are pledging 2 shares of SpaceX stock to every U.S. child, plus 1 a year

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

SpaceX's contribution is due in December, and Musk's gift follows when his lockup period expires in June next year

Elon Musk said he will donate another share of SpaceX stock to every child in the U.S. each year, on top of a two-share pledge announced this week, according to CNBC.

SpaceX committed on Wednesday to contribute a single freshly issued Class A common share to each active Trump Account, which is a children's investment account with tax advantages. Musk will pair that with a personal gift of the same size, the company said.

"I will be donating another share of SpaceX to all kids in America every year," Musk wrote in his post on X $TWTR, according to CNBC.

In a separate post, Musk said SpaceX's contribution would land in December, while his own gift would arrive once his lockup restriction ends in June of next year, according to CNBC.

The two-share gift for roughly 72 million U.S. minors, a Census Bureau estimate, would be worth about $18.35 billion at Wednesday's closing price, according to Axios.

SpaceX President and COO Gwynne Shotwell announced her own gift of more than 2 million shares of SpaceX stock, the company said. The statement did not say how the stock would be distributed, according to Bloomberg.

SpaceX stock climbed over 7% during overnight sessions on Blue Ocean ATS following Musk's post, and by premarket trading Friday it was up 4%, according to CNBC. It closed 1.3% higher on Wednesday.

Musk first pledged on Oct. 19 to give one share of SpaceX stock to every U.S. child the following year, writing that it would be the "first of many."

Close to 70 million Trump Accounts had been opened as of October, according to the White House.
