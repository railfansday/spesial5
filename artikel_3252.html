Report: Apple job listings detail plans for autonomous AI agents

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bloomberg reports that Apple is hiring engineers to develop an autonomous AI agent capable of browsing the web, writing code, and controlling apps on behalf of users. Here are the details.

In a report published today, Bloomberg notes that new job listings on Apple’s website indicate the company’s efforts to develop an autonomous AI agent capable of performing complex tasks on the user’s behalf.

Since the breakout success of OpenClaw, autonomous AI agents have become increasingly popular, with companies such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, and SpaceXSI among those offering their own solutions.

The increasing popularity of these agents has put Apple under pressure to catch up, while the company has also introduced new restrictions on how third-party AI agents can access and control Macs.

Over at the App Store, Meta’s Muse has become one of the fastest-growing apps in US history, surpassing 10 million downloads in just 40 days, according to Sensor Tower, and spending weeks at the top of the charts. As of today, it sits at No. 2 among free apps, behind TikTok.

And while there are differences in how phone-based and computer-based agents operate, these systems can usually work together, coordinating tasks across devices and apps to accomplish increasingly complex goals.

Back to Bloomberg’s report, it notes Apple recently posted new job listings that offer a closer look at its plans to develop autonomous AI agents:

In one, Apple said it’s seeking a senior engineering manager for a proactive, agentic AI assistant. The system will write code, browse the web, control apps and navigate devices on a user’s behalf, according to the post.

One job listing, which is for “Senior Engineering Manager, Agentic AI – Proactive,” features the following summary:

“Imagine an AI agent that can use a computer the way a capable person does. It browses the web, works across apps, runs commands, writes and debugs code, and calls services, and it chooses the right tool for each step. It does all this privately and with the polish people expect from Apple. Our team is building the foundation for that future, and we’re looking for a senior engineering leader to make it real.”

The listing also notes that Apple is building agents that can “plan and carry out multi-step tasks across apps, browsers, command lines, code, and APIs, and they can coordinate specialized sub-agents along the way,” much like existing solutions from frontier AI labs.

Today’s report on the new job listings comes just days after Bloomberg reported that Apple wants to make Siri more like an autonomous AI agent, with the ability to proactively carry out tasks without requiring users to initiate every step. According to Mark Gurman, the company could introduce these capabilities as soon as next year.

With the new job listings, Apple has offered a more detailed look at its plans for autonomous AI agents, although the language suggests that development is still in its early stages.

Of course, given how quickly the technology is evolving, the landscape could look very different by the time Apple is ready to introduce its own solution next year, much like how the industry had already moved toward autonomous agents by the time Apple finally launched its long-awaited Siri AI overhaul.
