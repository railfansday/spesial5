Liam Fitzgerald, a popular online coach and fitness influencer has passed away at just 29 years of age after injuries sustained during a fatal motorcycle accident. Tributes have poured in for the star, and his family have hailed him as an inspirational figure.

Fitzgerald, who was born in Evansville, Wisconsin, and was a social media influencer and OnlyFans creator, died on October 30, per announcement by his girlfriend Jamie Lyn Rinaldi. “It is with the heaviest heart that I share the devastating news that Liam passed away following a motorcycle accident on October 30th at approximately 2 AM,” she wrote via Instagram. “He suffered severe injuries, and despite the efforts of those who tried desperately to save him, they were unable to bring him back. There are no words to describe the heartbreak of this loss.”

Fitzgerald was an athlete before becoming an influencer and after a move to Washington state, his teenage years were highly active. Fitzgerald played football as a quarterback for the Camas Papermakers, skateboarded, and developed a love of working out. He later relocated to Arizona and finally settled in Miami, Florida, where he built an Instagram base of more than one million followers by sharing his fitness, fashion, and lifestyle advice.

Fitzgerald also collaborated with well known brands such as YoungLA, who posted a tribute to the star via its official Instagram account. “He brought an incredible energy, joy, and excitement to every shoot, making every moment that much more special,” read the statement. “His presence will be deeply missed, and we are grateful to have had the opportunity to work alongside him.”

The influencer’s family has also spoken about Fitzgerald’s struggles with alcohol. “This is shared in the hope that his story reaches someone who needs it,” read a statement. “He wasn’t perfect and never pretended to be. But he always strived to grow and do better. He had dreams, ambitions, and so much more life to live. He had just turned 29. So many places to see, memories to make, and people to love. His life was tragically cut short in a motorcycle accident, leaving behind a heartbreak that words will never be able to describe.”
