El Shaddai: Zero Nine Reversi now available for PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

crim has released El Shaddai: Zero Nine Reversi, “a board game that puts a bold twist on classic Reversi,” for PC via Steam at a 10 percent-off launch discount price of $3.59. The price will change to $3.99 after November 15.

El Shaddai: Zero Nine Reversi first launched for iOS via App Store and Android via Google Play on April 27. The mobile versions were updated alongside the PC release today with online multiplayer support and an archive.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:

Zero Nine Reversi is a board game that puts a bold twist on classic Reversi. Every stone has HP.

The game includes two ways to play: “Classic,” a deep single-player challenge, and “Online,” where you pick a character and battle other players.

In normal Reversi, flipping a stone is the end of the story. In Zero Nine Reversi, a flipped stone loses 1 HP. When its HP reaches zero, the stone is purified and vanishes from the board. This simple change turns every match into a battle of attrition, where positioning, timing, and resource management matter as much as territory.

A versus mode where you choose a character and fight. On top of stone HP, it adds the “BUMP & BURST” rules and a unique ability for each character.
