PS5 QSSR support added to Marvel’s Spider-Man 2, CONTROL Resonant, ARC Raiders, THE FINALS, No Man’s Sky, and Hell Let Loose: Vietnam

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

PlayStation 5‘s Quick Spectral Super Resolution—the base console equivalent of PlayStation 5 Pro’s artificial intelligence upscaling solution PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution—will add Marvel’s Spider-Man 2, CONTROL Resonant, ARC Raiders, THE FINALS, No Man’s Sky, and Hell Let Loose: Vietnam to its lineup of supported titles, Sony Interactive Entertainment announced.

“Having seen great results integrating [Quick Spectral Super Resolution] in Marvel’s Wolverine, our team decided to bring it to Marvel’s Spider-Man 2 as well, in an update that’s live now!,” said Insomniac Games head of technology Mike Fitzgerald. “We’re excited for players to swing back into our 2023 title and see [Quick Spectral Super Resolution]’s exciting image stability and sharpness benefits in both Fidelity and Performance modes.”

Remedy Entertainment is bringing Quick Spectral Super Resolution to CONTROL Resonant on PlayStation 5, with noticeable improvements in image stability and fine detail.

“It’s great to see PlayStation bringing the same kind of leap on the PlayStation 5 that [PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution] brought to PlayStation 5 Pro,” said Remedy Entertainment senior graphics programmer Erik Jansson. “[Quick Spectral Super Resolution] gives CONTROL Resonant a much more stable and detailed image, and we’ve worked closely with Sony to have it ready in an update coming in December.”

“With the new [Quick Spectral Super Resolution] coming soon to ARC Raiders and THE FINALS, we can deliver increased sharpness, better environmental clarity and overall visual improvements to even more PlayStation players,” said Embark Studios technical art director Mikael Linderholm. “We are very happy to see Sony’s continued investment in PlayStation technology translate into meaningful improvements for all players, bringing more immersive experiences across the PlayStation ecosystem.”

“No Man’s Sky gives players an infinite procedural universe to explore,” said Hello Games founder Sean Murray. “When every star in the night sky is a real solar system players can visit, we care a great deal about the quality of every pixel.

“The team has always been passionate about pushing the boundaries of technology on PlayStation. [Quick Spectral Super Resolution] allows us to render the universe in sharper, cleaner and more beautiful detail, while delivering the best possible framerate. Look for it in the next few weeks.

“It’s another step towards making the universe of No Man’s Sky even more immersive to explore.”

“We’re delighted to confirm that we are adding [Quick Spectral Super Resolution] to Hell Let Loose: Vietnam on PlayStation 5 through an upcoming update,” said Expression Games game director Paul Rustchynsky. “Console visuals are something our community has been passionate about, and [Quick Spectral Super Resolution] will bring sharper, cleaner image quality to the battlefield, from dense jungle to the chaos of a full-scale firefight. We can’t wait for our PlayStation players to see the difference.”
