Burberry stock jumps 9% as sales beat expectations amid weak luxury market

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Comparable retail sales rose 4% in the second quarter, the fifth straight quarter of growth, with the Americas up 11% and Greater China up 5%

Burberry reported first-half results on Thursday showing comparable retail sales growth of 4% in its second quarter, and its stock rose 9.1% in European morning trading.

Analysts had expected 3% growth in comparable sales for the quarter, according to The Wall Street Journal. The second-quarter gain followed a 5% increase in the first quarter and marked the fifth consecutive quarter of growth, the company said.

Revenue for the 26 weeks ended Sept. 26 rose 6% to £1.098 billion ($1.44 billion). Adjusted operating profit climbed to £62 million from £19 million a year earlier. Reported operating profit came in at £54 million, a turnaround from a loss of £18 million in the prior-year period.

Comparable sales in the Americas rose 12% in the half, and Greater China rose 7%, with a 5% gain in the second quarter. The EMEIA region fell 2%, which Burberry blamed on fewer tourists and softer foot traffic, especially at its outlet stores. Wholesale revenue rose 17%, and outerwear sales grew by double digits.

"Five consecutive quarters of growth and improved quality of sales demonstrate that our strategy is working," Chief Executive Officer Joshua Schulman said in a statement.

Burberry said it expects revenue to grow and margins to widen in the fiscal year ending in 2027, though it remains wary of an unpredictable external backdrop. The company expects wholesale revenue to grow between 10% and 12% for the year.

The results arrived as other luxury companies reported slowing momentum, according to The Wall Street Journal. The Wall Street Journal said LVMH's third-quarter results were weighed down in part by ongoing difficulties in China.

"We are outperforming a challenging luxury market," Schulman said on a call, according to The Wall Street Journal.
