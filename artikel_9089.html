Getty Images Demands Release of Veteran Photographer Detained in Iran

Getty Images is demanding the release of veteran Iranian photojournalist Majid Saeedi, a regular contributor to the photo agency who has been detained in Iran for more than two months.

Saeedi is an internationally recognized photojournalist who has won numerous awards for his coverage of Afghanistan, including a Lucie Award and the Robert F. Kennedy Journalism Award. His images have been published in The New York Times, The Washington Post, and Life magazine. Saeedi also received second prize in the 2013 World Press Photo Contest’s Contemporary Issues category.

According to a report by the Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ), Saeedi was arrested at his home in Tehran on September 9. More than two months after his arrest, Iranian authorities have yet to disclose whether the freelance photojournalist faces any charges or which agency is holding him, despite repeated efforts to obtain this information. Saeedi is believed to be detained in Kerman, a city in southeastern Iran, although his location has not been officially confirmed.

The CPJ and Getty Images have now urged Iranian authorities to release the award-winning photojournalist immediately and unconditionally. They say that the lack of information surrounding his detention has raised serious concerns about his safety, as well as the increasing threats facing independent journalists in Iran.

“Holding Majid Saeedi for more than two months, with no official confirmation of any charges, is unacceptable,” Getty Images VP of Global News Pancho Bernasconi says. “Journalism is not a crime. We join CPJ in demanding Iranian authorities account for Majid’s whereabouts and release him immediately and unconditionally.”

“For more than two months, Majid Saeedi’s family has been left in the dark about his whereabouts, the reason for his detention, and whether he is safe. No journalist should simply disappear into state custody, while Iranian authorities refuse to tell their family what has happened,” CPJ Regional Director Sara Qudah says in a further statement. “Majid’s detention is part of a broader campaign to intimidate and silence journalists in Iran — he must be released immediately and unconditionally.

Saeedi has previously been arrested on several occasions in connection with his photographic coverage of politically sensitive events and their aftermath, including in 2009, 2021, and June 2025. According to CPJ, the photographer’s latest detention comes amid an escalating crackdown on independent journalism in Iran, where authorities have increasingly resorted to arrests and lengthy prison sentences to suppress critical reporting.

Previously, PetaPixel reported on two female photographers, prominent photojournalist Yalda Moaiery and 18-year-old artist Shaghayegh Afshar, who were sentenced to a combined 24 years in prison in Iran. Meanwhile Tahmineh Monzavi, an acclaimed Iranian documentary photographer, was sentenced to 74 lashes for documenting an Iranian female singer’s concert.
