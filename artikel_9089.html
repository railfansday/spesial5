The best designers in the world right now

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

This week, the Dezeen Awards winners for 2026 were unveiled. Who are the names to know, and why did they win? And with Donald Trump’s White House ballroom now taking shape, should it be kept by the next president? Listen to the latest episode of Dezeen Weekly now.

In this week’s episode, Jennifer Hahn is joined by Tom Ravenscroft and Ben Dreith to reflect on the major winners from this year’s Dezeen Awards.

Then, the podcast returns to Donald Trump’s White House ballroom once again as the project starts to take shape, drawing a fresh round of outcry.
