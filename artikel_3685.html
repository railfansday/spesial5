Clash of Clans Town Hall 19 update release countdown: Expected release date and time

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Town Hall 19 is coming to Clash of Clans as part of November’s major update. Plenty of exciting features have been teased by Supercell, but for now, we will have to wait for the update to go live.

Unfortunately, Supercell hasn’t announced the exact date and time. However, we can make some speculative guesses based on previous major updates.

As of writing, the Hammer Jam event comes to an end later today on Nov. 15. Supercell has also teased that we are really close to the TH 19 update. Based on these factors, Nov. 16 seems like a safe bet. The following countdown is based on expected times regarding when the update is likely to go live.

This is the usual timeline that Supercell follows for major in-game events and updates. However, I will update the exact times once they’re revealed before launch.

While the release date and time might be matters of speculation, we have confirmed news about a few of the upcoming features. For starters, Town Hall 19 is coming to the game, and it will be guarded by troops.

We also have a new Squad Barracks feature that allows us to train a group of troops. Upgrading them improves their in-game stats and unlocks new abilities. At launch, there will be 12 different squads with six unique abilities to unlock.

Finally, Star Bonus is also getting a major rework. While you’ll no longer get rewards based on Ranked League, the new system will determine the bonus resources based on your Town Hall level. Once you unlock the second star, you can get Magic Snacks, and they will provide you with temporary buffs.
