Inside Airbnb.org’s Gala With Jimmy Kimmel, Charli xcx, and Brian Chesky

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Nothing says billionaire-hosted gala quite like an olive sphere. What’s an olive sphere, you ask? Imagine a bite-sized gelatinous membrane filled with olive juice that is served on a tiny silver platter, and requires two servers—two!—to pass out: one to offer guests the savory blobs, and another to collect the discarded platters.

Before taking the stage at Milk Studios in Los Angeles on Wednesday night, Jimmy Kimmel walked past two olive sphere servers and a third handing out caviar. He told me he’d been listening to The Howard Stern Show on the drive over, and something so X-rated came on that he had to quickly turn down the volume when he pulled up alongside another car. Evidently, it put him in a silly mood by the time he welcomed guests to the first ever gala for Airbnb’s independent nonprofit—or “gay-la,” as he put it, “depending on your sexual orientation.”

Kimmel had been tapped to serve as master of ceremonies at the fundraiser for the home-sharing behemoth’s nonprofit, which was created in 2020 to provide free temporary housing to people displaced by disasters and other crises. Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky announced a $500 million funding commitment over the next five years, allowing the organization to expand its services to families traveling for medical treatment and survivors of domestic violence.

Among the guests were people who had lost their homes in the devastating Eaton Fire in 2025. They shared a room with a group of people who presumably have little need for emergency housing: Charlize Theron, Serena Williams, Matthew McConaughey, Katie Couric, Joaquin Phoenix, Rooney Mara, Cynthia Erivo, and Demi Lovato, among others. It was a collision of two Los Angeleses: People who had lost everything, and people who wore bling worth as much as a house in Altadena.

Most of the men were dressed in tuxedos. Williams shined in a sparkly animal-print gown. Paul Bettany wore a black Chairman Mao-style shirt. Jaden Smith wore jeans, apparently missing the black-tie memo.

Kimmel, decked out in a perfectly tailored black tuxedo, had some ground rules as the emcee. Guests were expected to wash their dishes. No pets, except service animals, “which we know none of them really are.” “And absolutely no sitting naked on the furniture or on chef José Andrés.”

Chesky, who hard-launched his relationship with girlfriend designer Carly Helfrich on the red carpet, delivered his remarks with the cadence of Vanessa Bayer’s Jacob the Bar Mitzvah Boy Saturday Night Live character, staring down at the teleprompter positioned on the floor. “People are fundamentally good,” he declared, explaining that Airbnb’s business would have never worked otherwise.

On the red carpet, I asked Chesky whether the Trump administration had made it harder for his nonprofit to house people fleeing tragedy. “No, absolutely not,” he insisted, describing emergency housing as a cause that transcends partisan politics. This was a rather diplomatic answer from a man whose company once ran advertisements welcoming refugees in pointed opposition to Trump’s 2017 Muslim travel ban. But Chesky wasn’t taking the bait. “This is a very united kind of issue,” he said.

The night took an emotional turn when Kimmel talked about his son Billy’s three open-heart surgeries before introducing a video of a young boy receiving cancer treatments who was the beneficiary of the charity’s efforts. At that moment, the child, who was in attendance with his mom and grandma, stood on his chair and made a heart symbol using his two hands. The crowd went wild.

José Andrés, whose name appeared on the menu but who was rather noncommittal when asked whether he had actually designed it, insisted that the evening’s charitable mission was more than an excuse for Hollywood to get dressed up.

By dessert, Kimmel was shaking down the wealthy guests. “Don’t cheap out,” he warned, noting that anyone who could afford the new folding iPhone (which retails at roughly $2,000) could surely spare $110 to provide a family with a night of emergency housing. He also accidentally revealed the evening’s secret musical guest: Charli xcx.

“Oh shit!” he exclaimed, realizing his mistake. “Okay, it’s not a surprise anymore.”

Charli eventually took the stage, with will.i.am and Greta Lee dancing from the front row. “That was soooooo good,” Lee said to me on the way out.

And although the set wasn’t particularly long, Charli rocked it, sending heads banging everywhere. She got the Hollywood room doing what it does best: partying for a cause.
