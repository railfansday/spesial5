Atomfall is British Fallout and Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii is an action-adventure Yakuza spin-off that’s as ridiculous as it sounds. Both games have earned high marks from critics and players, but neither is as good as Kojima Productions’ Death Stranding 2. If you haven’t played the first one, though, you should do that before embarking on the sequel.

My fellow JRPG fans are also in luck this month. Freedom Wars Remastered, meanwhile, is a somewhat dated but still pretty fun sci-fi action-RPG about fighting post-apocalyptic monsters to complete an absurdly long jail sentence. The remaster is a great trip down memory lane for fans of the PS Vita original.
