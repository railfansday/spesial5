Ten-man Bournemouth come from behind to beat Celta at Balaídos and top Europa League

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Celta Vigo lost at home to Bournemouth even though the visitors played most of the game a man down. The defeat extends a difficult run for Claudio Giráldez's side, while the visitors moved to the top of the Europa League table.

Bournemouth came from behind to beat Celta Vigo 1-2 at the Municipal de Balaídos on Thursday 26 November 2026. With Javier Rueda García providing the assist for Aspas, Celta went into the break 1-0 up.

The game turned soon after the restart. Álvaro Rodríguez, the Uruguay international, headed Bournemouth level in the 47th minute from an Amine Adli assist. Rayan then scored the winner on a counter-attack in the 78th minute, set up by Marcus Tavernier. Aspas was booked for dissent in the same minute.

The match statistics show how much of the play Celta had. The hosts completed 527 passes from 607. Bournemouth completed 264 from 333. Fourteen of Celta’s attempts came from inside the box, against four for the visitors.

Celta’s finishing did not match that volume. They put five shots on target, while Bournemouth managed six. Seven Celta efforts were blocked and eight went wide. Celta goalkeeper Ionuț Radu made four saves.

Giráldez told EFE that his team did not show enough composure or intelligence to make use of the extra man after Diakité’s dismissal. In his view, Rodríguez’s equaliser brought back the anxiety of Celta’s poor run.

That run is visible in the results. Celta have won one of their last six games in all competitions, a 5-0 victory over Salamanca UDS. Before Bournemouth, they lost 1-2 to Real Madrid. They also drew 0-0 with Levante and lost to Union St. Gilloise and Rayo Vallecano.

Bournemouth’s recent run includes victories over Nottingham Forest, Ipswich, Leeds and Aston Villa.

The 2026/27 Europa League is the club’s debut in European competition. Before Vigo, they had beaten Real Sociedad, Sturm Graz, AC Milan and Sparta Praha in the competition. Rodríguez was the top-rated player in the match, with a rating of 7.9.

Both sides return to league action on Sunday 29 November. Celta host Villarreal in LaLiga, while Bournemouth travel to Fulham in the Premier League.

Giráldez still believes Celta can win all three of their remaining league-phase games. The last two league-phase matchdays are on 21 and 28 January 2027. Before then, Celta travel to Olympique de Marseille on Thursday 10 December 2026, with kick-off at 18:45 Spanish time.
