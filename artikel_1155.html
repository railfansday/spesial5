Gordon double leads rotated Barcelona to 4-1 win in Baku without Lamine Yamal

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Anthony Gordon scored twice as Hansi Flick's rotated Barcelona beat Sabah FK 4-1 in Baku without Lamine Yamal. The win lifts them to fourth in the Champions League league phase before Atlético Madrid and Manchester City visit.

In Baku, Barcelona completed the longest European away trip in their history and returned with a comfortable victory over Sabah FK. Hansi Flick rotated his side, and Lamine Yamal did not make the journey.

Joy-Lance Mickels scored a consolation goal for the Azerbaijani champions. Sabah had won only one of their first four league-phase matches. One of their defeats was a heavy loss to Manchester United.

Goalkeeper Stas Pokatilov failed to deal with an Adeyemi cross in the first half. Gordon was on hand to turn the ball in and put Barcelona ahead.

Flick said his side had more than 70% of possession in the first half. He was unhappy, though, with the easy mistakes his team made. He also noted that Sabah had nothing to lose.

The game was decided soon after the restart. Barcelona scored three times in the space of nine minutes. Gordon struck first, converting a rebound after Pokatilov saved from Jesus.

Four minutes later, Adeyemi finished Alejandro Balde’s cross. Jesus then made it four on the hour. He followed up after Jules Koundé’s header was saved.

The scoreline fits a side that controlled the ball for long spells.

The win also gave Flick room to manage his squad. Across 18 matches this season, Pedri had appeared every time, something no team-mate could match. He was taken off at half-time.

That decision had been agreed beforehand. Pedri was to play only the first 45 minutes whatever happened. Dani Olmo’s minutes were also limited to one half.

Resting key players in Baku could pay off over the coming fortnight. Barcelona are top of LaLiga and face two demanding fixtures at home.

The result keeps Barcelona on course for an automatic top-eight finish in the league phase. Manchester City sit eighth on 10 points. That makes the matchday 6 meeting a direct contest between two sides in the top eight.

City have not faced Barcelona at the Camp Nou since October 2016, the club said.

Before that, Barcelona turn to domestic business. They visit Deportivo de La Coruña on Sunday 29 November, then host Atlético Madrid in LaLiga at Spotify Camp Nou on Wednesday 2 December at 9.30pm CET.
