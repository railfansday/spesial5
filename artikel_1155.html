The eight players that should be on Man United's January list to turn season around - even with paltry £50m budget: From a goalscorer available for just £7m to star desperate to reunite with Mauricio Pochettino and a flying left back

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Published: 21:00 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 21:00 EST, 24 November 2026

Inside the corridors of power at Manchester United, there is not the same appetite for a January splurge as there is among the club's fanbase.

While the winter transfer window brings hope for supporters desperate to see the mistakes of the summer rectified, United's decision-makers are anticipating a far more modest outlay.

As exclusively revealed by Daily Mail Sport, United plan to spend less than £50million, with two additions targeted, one of which could be on loan.

Should United, currently 19th, remain in the bottom three by the time the window opens on January 1, there is a slim chance they go big in a desperate bid to guarantee their Premier League status.

But for now, the watchword inside United is discipline, and that means any recruitment will have to be more creative than ever before.

Here Daily Mail Sport looks at the transfer landscape and assesses eight players – a mix of Premier League proven and some lesser-known quantities – who could fit United’s needs AND their budget…

Mauricio Pochettino needs recruits in January - although Man United's budget being below £50m won't help his cause

Brentford centre back Nathan Collins is one of United's top targets, but he won't be available on the cheap

Position: Centre back, Age: 25, Club: Brentford

Signing Premier League proven players is absolutely the preference of the United hierarchy, as evidenced by their recent business. Six of their past eight arrivals joined with experience in the division under their belt.

Collins is one of the club’s top targets for the winter window. The 25-year-old Brentford captain would bring leadership, an aerial presence and is comfortable as a ball carrier from the back, which Pochettino values.

Collins is seen as having many of the same attributes that Harry Maguire has brought to United over the years. And while he is not a flash name, he is viewed as a player that can immediately deliver some solidity.

Highly durable and a threat at set-pieces, Brentford won’t be willing to part with their captain, who is under contract until 2029, on the cheap.

Signing Collins would eat up most of the budget – but United are not deterred.

Position: Left back, Age: 25, Club: Man City

United made a late play to land the Algerian on loan in January, only for City to tell them they would only sanction a permanent exit. In the end, time ran out and Ait-Nouri stayed put.

He has started just one game in all competitions under Enzo Maresca and is yet to feature at all in the Premier League this season. His situation is unlikely to improve in the near future, either.

A loan with an obligation to buy could suit all parties here, even if sending Ait-Nouri to their fiercest rivals may be difficult for City to stomach.

Rayan Ait-Nouri (right) has fallen out of favour under Enzo Maresca and is yet to play a minute in the Premier League this season. Could he make a move across Manchester?

Position: Left back, Age: 27, Club: Crystal Palace

An intriguing option given United have been keeping tabs on Mitchell for over a year.

Palace are quite secretive when it comes to Mitchell – they kept his last contract extension under wraps – but as it stands, his deal is set to expire at the end of the season.

Mitchell has the stamina to cope with Pochettino’s style of play and what he lacks in end product, he makes up for with his relentless pressing and recovery pace. The same is true of Fulham's Calvin Bassey, but sources are increasingly sceptical about United's chances of signing him in January.

For all of Palace’s struggles, Mitchell is proven at this level and would likely hit the ground running. The fact United are not his only suitor is a sign they aren’t straying down a blind alley.

With the 27-year-old’s contract yet to be extended and big clubs sniffing around, he could prove to be this January’s Marc Guehi should United nip in ahead of Liverpool, with Reds boss Andoni Iraola also keen.

Position: Forward, Age: 29, Club: Tottenham

Out of favour under Roberto De Zerbi, Richarlison could help Tottenham Hotspur, Manchester United and himself in one fell swoop.

In the summer, Spurs were looking for £20m for the Brazilian. Now the feeling is they would take a figure below £10m – some sources think £7m may suffice – to move on a player who has been kicking his heels since playing for the Under 21s away to Cheltenham Town in October.

Richarlison saw summer offers from Everton, Trabzonspor and Vasco da Gama fall through for various reasons and with United desperate to bolster their front line, this one could suit all parties. Richarlison is a strong presser from the front, versatile and better in the air than he is perhaps given credit for. He’s also no stranger to football's dark arts, something Pochettino appreciates.

The 29-year-old would offer a greater goal threat than Joshua Zirkzee, who saw moves of his own collapse in the summer, and showed last season what a threat he can be, scoring 11 league goals.

Spurs just want him out of the building and while that is normally a red flag, United need all the help they can get in a difficult market.

Richarlison is available for a bargain fee in January - even if he comes with some red flags attached

Position: Centre back, Age: 28, Club: AC Milan

United have rarely gone to the continent for recruits in recent seasons. But their limited budget makes Premier League players extremely difficult to acquire.

One option who might be available for a bargain fee is AC Milan's Fikayo Tomori, who is out of contract in the summer. The expectation in Milan is that Tomori will be moved on in January, allowing former United boss Ruben Amorim to add to his defence.

Tomori was initally frozen out by Amorim – he’d have a good ice-breaker should he pitch up at United! – and was even stripped of a squad number, before he was gradually reintroduced.

The England international has started just four games this season – only two in the league – and while Tomori has limitations in the air, he would bring Premier League experience and a level of leadership that is sorely lacking. His ability to bring the ball out and into midfield would make him a regular starter in Manchester.

When Milan won Serie A in 2022, Tomori was aggressive in his press and showed he could play on either side as a centre back, and also slot in at right back when required.

Milan want to sell him, United need defensive reinforcements, and a cut-price deal suits everyone. This one is a no-brainer, thanks to Amorim of all people.

Fikayo Tomori looks set to move on from AC Milan in January with former United boss Ruben Amorim keen to shuffle his defensive pack

Position: Left back/centre back, Age: 29, Club: Stuttgart

An experienced option that could cover multiple positions across the back line, Mittelstadt would prove really shrewd business.

Under contract until 2028, the player has not agitated for a move but sources in Germany have indicated no extension talks are underway at this stage.

Stuttgart are eighth in the Bundesliga and should European football start to look out of reach, a sale of someone like Mittelstadt may become a necessity.

United should look at him even though his resale value would be limited, as he would immediately bring experience and quality. Mittelstadt, who was Germany's first-choice left back at Euro 2024, is a proactive defender that doesn’t shirk challenges and his durability means United could count on him from the off – important given their lack of high-quality options at left back.

Signing Mittelstadt would go against everything the club's executives want to do – but they showed by bringing in the 29-year-old Youri Tielemans in the summer that they are prepared to make exceptions on some older players.

Germany international Maximilian Mittelstadt (right) would add quality at full back

Position: Left back, Age: 23, Club: Real Madrid

Carreras should never have been allowed to leave United, but now comes a chance to rectify the mistake the club made in letting him go two-and-a-half years ago.

The 23-year-old has not been a regular starter under Jose Mourinho at Real Madrid and a player of his undoubted quality won't be happy with that state of affairs.

Real don’t need to cut their losses on a young player who is contracted through to 2031, which is exactly why Pochettino should go to Mourinho and see if he can take Carreras on loan for the rest of the season.

He is a better creator of chances than Patrick Dorgu or Luke Shaw and while Dorgu offers greater threat in the final third, Carreras feels a more well-rounded option at left back that is a better match-up against the league’s top wingers.

Carreras needs regular starts to develop and here is a chance for Mourinho to do his former side a favour when they are cash-strapped.

United made a mistake in letting Alvaro Carreras (right) go in 2024 - now it is time to rectify it

Folarin Balogun forged a strong connection with Pochettino with the US national team and looks set to leave Monaco in the near future

A striker that looked bound for Everton until a last-minute, deadline-day U-turn in the summer, it is widely known among recruitment staff that Balogun is available in January.

The Arsenal academy graduate has worked under Pochettino extensively with the US national team and, having had a reduced role at Monaco since the botched Everton move, a fresh start under a manager who knows how to get the best out of him would suit everyone.

‘There were just floods (of tears) from many people, but it's bound to happen,’ said Balogun when Pochettino left the USA job. ‘We had a really close relationship, him, his staff and us as players. We were really connected. It was always going to be a sad goodbye for that reason.’

Pochettino and Balogun's desire to reunite will be strong, but money could be an issue here. A loan with a conditional obligation to buy could be the best compromise, with incentives based on United's league position or Balogun's goals scored.
