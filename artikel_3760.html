Best upgrades to get in Potato Please

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Potato Please might look like a simple game about peeling potatoes, but things can get quite tricky. It’s okay to start peeling with your hands when you’re setting up your work. However, the demand rises soon, and so does the number of potatoes.

Coins are limited, and upgrades are plenty. I will list the best upgrades you should prioritize first.

Upgrades in Potato Please are unlocked with credits. They’re also divided across multiple categories. While the game doesn’t tell you which upgrades you should get first, min-maxing will make your life easier (especially since the job can get quite stressful after the first hour if you’re doing everything manually).
