PlayStation Is Already Killing Off Ratchet & Clank Mobile Game Just 12 Months After It Was Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Insomniac GamesMobile gameratchet and clank

Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble, a mobile-only free-to-play multiplayer hero shooter, will shut down services on May 1, 2027. The developers at OhBibi made the announcement that the final update for the game is rolling out today, November 6th, and the game will be pulled from the App Store and Google Play on November 16th.
