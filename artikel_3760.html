What are the odds of getting the Pirate Shop Access token in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Pirate Shop has been the topic of many debates in Fortnite Treasure Hunters since its introduction. We got a wonderful feature on Nov. 1 when we could start redeeming all the premium shop rewards for free.

However, players quickly noticed that the drop rate was extremely low. Thankfully, we have official numbers from Epic Games ahead of the second week of the season.

Earlier on Nov. 7, Epic officially revealed the odds for receiving the chest. They drop like regular rewards from all types of Treasure chests, but the base odds are really low. If you open a regular chest in a normal match, here is the exact odd for you to receive the access token.

In simple words, you’re expected to find it once (you need to find it just once) from 137,500 regular chests. However, probability works in a funnier way, so it could take you a lot more (or lot less) chests to find the access token.

You asked for info on drop rates, so here’s how it works:The new treasure this season is found in chests just like other items. Treasure drop rates from chests are constant since season launch and don’t vary throughout the day, except during Treasure Hours. Other than Gizmos,…

While the odds mentioned above is for normal games, the numbers become more favorable during Treasure Hours. These were the odds of getting the shop access token from Saturday’s Treasure Hours:

However, Epic has also claimed that the odds might be boosted further. For example, the odds of finding the Pirate Shop Access token was doubled between 8pm and 10pm CT on Nov. 7.

Hence, your best shot is to open as many chests as possible, and playing in squads, since that will statistically make your life three times easier. Alternatively, you can make most of the boosted chances during the Treasure Hours.
