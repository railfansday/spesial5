All Secrets in Warhammer Survivors and how to solve them

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The two universes in Warhammer Survivors have their own respective secrets, and there are several to complete. These are different from standard objectives since the game doesn’t tell you what needs to be done.

Instead, you’ll find a clue associated with the task to decode. Let’s find out how to complete all the Secrets available in the game.

First, you’ll need to unlock the Secrets tab in both universes to gain access to these challenges.
