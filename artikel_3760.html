Meghan King Shares Sweet Photo of Reunion With 3 Kids After Custody Drama

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Meghan King is all smiles while reuniting with her three kids.

The Real Housewives of Orange County alum, 42, wrapped her arms around her three children, whom she welcomed with ex Jim Edmonds, in an Instagram photo shared on Sunday, November 8. In the pic, she and the three kids posed beside a firepit and adirondack chairs as fields were visible in the distance.

“In case I needed another reason to love Tennessee in the fall, here’s three 👩🏻👨🏼‍🤝‍👨🏻,” she captioned the photo, tagging the location as Leipers Fork, Tennessee.

King and Edmonds welcomed daughter Aspen via IVF in 2016, followed by twins Hart and Hayes two years later. (King also became a stepmother to the former baseball pro’s four children from previous relationships during their marriage.)

The pair, who tied the knot in 2014, separated five years later. King and Edmonds’ divorce was finalized in 2021 and the exes continued to coparent their children while sharing joint custody.

In 2025, however, an investigation from Child Protective Services led King to temporarily lose physical custody of the kids. While a hearing was initially set to determine King’s custody rights, it was later canceled after she and Edmonds reached an agreement.

“The custody hearing has been called off,” Edmonds’ rep, Steve Honig, told Us in December 2025, adding that “both sides have reached an agreement that is in the best interests of the children.”

At the time, a source told Us that the children would be residing with Edmonds in Tennessee full-time and spending several weeks with King over the summer. In 2025, King spent time with her children around the holidays. A separate insider told Us the arrangement was part of the pair’s agreement.

“Went to the holiday mart and only spent money on this,” King captioned an Instagram post with her three children while celebrating the holiday season in December 2025, with the kids playing a claw machine game. “We are a claw-loving clan!”

Before the year came to a close, King reflected on her 2025 and shared a meme via her Instagram Story that read, “It was a tough year, but at least I did not buy a Labubu.” She added her own caption to the post, writing, “Butttttt a bunch of ‘super-rare’ stuff happened,” adding the abbreviation for “just saying” and a shrugging girl emoji.

Neither King nor Jim, who married Kortnie Edmonds in 2022, have publicly addressed the custody situation.
