Syracuse University will undertake a review of its executive and administrative staff roles ahead of a projected budget deficit. Its chancellor likened the process — details of which are still being finalized — to the academic portfolio review the private New York institution completed earlier this year that resulted in 93 programs being slated for termination.

The cost of the deferred maintenance backlog faced by Louisiana’s public colleges. A report from Gordian, a building intelligence firm, to the state’s board of regents found that eliminating the backlog and addressing forthcoming maintenance needs will take $777 million in annual capital spending over the next decade.

“For too long, accreditors have effectively served as a cartel to protect their interests, rather than the educational value that institutions of higher education are expected to deliver for students.”

The U.S. Department of Education last week released the final version of its accreditation rule, setting up a significant overhaul of the longstanding process. Under the regulations, which take effect in July, accreditors will be required to consider colleges’ intellectual diversity policies and cannot enforce “illegal diversity, equity, and inclusion requirements” as defined by the Trump administration.
