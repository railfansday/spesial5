7 Peter Millar Staples That Work Wherever the Day Takes You

Good clothes, fewer decisions. What’s not to like?

Getting dressed for just about anything is a whole lot easier when you have the right essentials in your closet. Whether you’re heading into the office, catching a flight, or packing for a weekend away, a few well-chosen pieces can take you from one plan to the next without having to give your outfit much thought.

That’s the appeal of Peter Millar’s Crown Collection, which brings together classic menswear with thoughtful details, quality fabrics, and a relaxed approach to dressing well. There’s an emphasis on comfort and practicality, but never at the expense of looking polished. From tailored trousers and half-zips to quilted layers and other everyday favorites, these are pieces you'll find yourself reaching for again and again.

Ahead, seven picks from the collection, available at Bloomingdale’s, that deserve a spot in your closet. Because looking good shouldn’t require a whole lot of effort.

A chore coat is a great casual Friday-to-weekend staple when you want to look put together without feeling too dressed up. This navy style has a relaxed, cardigan-like feel, with a classic collar and patch pockets that work just as well with a T-shirt and jeans as they do with a button-down and chinos.

The Essex Vest is an iconic piece within Peter Millar’s Crown Collection and is designed as a versatile layering option for fall that works across a range of occasions. This black diamond-quilted style offers an extra layer of warmth without the bulk of a jacket, making it just as useful for your morning commute as it is for a round of golf or an afternoon apple picking.

Few pieces strike the balance between comfort and looking sharp quite like a good half-zip. This navy pullover is comfortable enough for a long flight but polished enough to wear over a collared shirt with tailored trousers. Prediction: You’ll want it in multiple colors.

A quilted coat is timeless, especially in a rich, dark olive shade. This one has a classic countryside feel, but its clean shape looks just as at home on city streets with jeans and boots or layered over a button-down and trousers. A bottle opener tucked inside the left interior pocket adds a handy signature touch. It’s a smart choice for chilly days that don’t call for a heavy overcoat.

Utilitarian pants are a wardrobe icon, and this five-pocket design offers a slightly more refined take on the classic. The smooth sateen fabric and rich blue color give them a polished look that works with everything from a polo at the office to a crisp shirt and loafers for dinner.

Every man’s shoe collection should include a pair of driving loafers, particularly when comfort and looking elegant are equally important. These walnut-brown suede drivers feature classic penny-loafer detailing and flexible, rubber-studded soles, making them a natural choice with chinos, tailored trousers, or jeans.

A checked button-down is a classic for a reason, but how about one with a little more personality? This soft twill style features a small-scale pattern and subtle grapeseed color that look particularly great with navy, gray, or khaki. Designed to be tucked in, it pairs easily with tailored trousers for work or sateen five pocket pants for a more relaxed look.
