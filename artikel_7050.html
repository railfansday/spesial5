Praxis Precision Medicines, Inc.: Manager CMC Materials Management

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

At Praxis, every material movement matters. We are seeking a proactive, results-driven Manager, CMC Materials Management to own domestic and international logistics supporting our research, development, and manufacturing programs.

This role ensures critical materials reach the right destination, on time and in the required condition, while maintaining compliance, inventory visibility, and cost efficiency across our global supplier and CDMO network. The ideal candidate anticipates challenges, solves problems with urgency, and takes ownership from planning through delivery. Success requires strong execution, attention to detail, and a commitment to advancing life-altering medicines for patients.

The physical and mental requirements of our roles include but are not limited to regular use of a computer, devices or other office equipment, clear communication, and occasional movement.  You'll need comfort with screen work, basic hand coordination, and focus.  Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform these functions.

We provide annualized base salary compensation in the range listed below. Final salary range may be modified commensurate with job level, education, and experience.
