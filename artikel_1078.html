England handed suspended supporter ban for discriminatory chanting in Spain

England have been handed a suspended ban for away supporters as a result of “racist and/or discriminatory behaviour” by fans during Sunday’s 3-2 win over Spain in Madrid.

The ban, which would apply to one match, is suspended for a period of two years, meaning any similar offence during that period would enforce it.

The FA has also been fined €15,000 (£12,600).

The punishment reportedly relates to anti-Catholic and anti-Irish chanting which was heard in the Spanish capital.

"We will not tolerate this unacceptable and deeply offensive chanting, and will work with the police, who attend all England home and away matches, to identify the perpetrators and take the necessary action," an FA spokesperson said.

England fans’ group Free Lions has condemned the offensive chanting, writing in a statement: “We are extremely disappointed to find that England fans face the possibility of an away ban as a result of chants by a section of supporters.

“There is no place for offensive chants aimed at religious figures. The time for these chants is over. No tit-for-tat arguments. No excuses.

“The behaviour and reputation of England supporters abroad has improved significantly over the years but these chants undermine that progress.”

The build-up to the match was overshadowed by concerns of racist chanting from Spain fans, with England boss Thomas Tuchel stating he would remind his players of Uefa's anti-racism protocol before the fixture.

Tuchel, who insisted he didn’t expect any incidents, then fumed at how his words were apparently “twisted” as Spain manager Luis de la Fuente issued a firm response to the German.

"What I'd tell you, and you can tell Thomas, is that in Spain, there's no racism,” De la Fuente said.

"Just like that, crystal clear. Just like in England, there's no racism in Spain. We aren't racist, any of us."

England ended Spain’s 43-game unbeaten record at the weekend, taking a 3-0 lead through Harry Kane, Ezri Konsa and Ben White but then hanging on after a red card for Elliot Anderson.
