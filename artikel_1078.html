Chelsea's roller-coaster season has taken another turn in the last seven days - and not for the better.

After chalking up back-to-back wins over Bournemouth and Everton, Xabi Alonso's inconsistent side fell to a 2-0 home defeat against Tottenham Hotspur before seeing one of their three chances of silverware disappear as they lost to Liverpool on penalties in the Carabao Cup.

The Blues suffered their latest injury blow at Anfield as Maxence Lacroix was forced off with a hamstring injury, the centre back joining captain Reece James and striker Joao Pedro on the sidelines.

However, they will be boosted by the return of Cole Palmer after the England international served a one-match suspension following his red card against Spurs.

While Alonso could do with a victory after a tough week, his counterpart Michael Carrick surely has to win to keep his job as Manchester United manager.

The Old Trafford hierarchy decided to stick with the 45-year-old after Sunday's 3-0 home defeat by Bournemouth, which extended the Red Devils' winless run to eight matches in all competitions and left them just a point above the relegation zone.

Captain Bruno Fernandes was benched for that game as a result of his public criticism of Carrick's tactics after the 4-1 defeat by Como 10 days ago, but he could return after it was confirmed he would remain skipper following clear-the-air talks with the United boss.

Striker Benjamin Sesko will remain out for some time, though, while midfielder Kobbie Mainoo's involvement is in doubt.
