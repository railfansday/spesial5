AO3 returns after 17 days offline following security incident

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Archive of Our Own is back online after more than two weeks offline following a security incident, though the fanfiction archive has returned with several features temporarily disabled.

The Organization for Transformative Works announced the restoration on November 15, shortly after AO3 passed the two-week mark of its outage. The site went offline on October 29, with OTW previously warning that restoring service could take between two and four weeks.

#AO3 is now up! Some features are temporarily disabled to bring the site back earlier than the four-week mark. There may be delays to manage traffic, please refresh if affected. No AO3 data has been deleted. For more information, refer to our status page: https://t.co/A6nVO4KceV https://t.co/hOA0akTvlF

OTW said the site is back earlier than the four-week estimate because some features have been switched off during the recovery process.

User and collection icons are currently hidden across the site, though OTW confirmed that none have been deleted. External work importing is also unavailable, while works can only be downloaded in HTML format for the time being.

AO3 warned that heavy traffic could cause delays when loading the archive. Users who see the downtime page have been advised to refresh and try again.

The site’s status remains listed as degraded performance while the incident is being addressed.

OTW said it has partnered with a security firm to review what happened and analyze AO3’s systems. The organization has not shared specific details about the changes being made during the investigation.

The nonprofit said it has no reason to believe that the AO3 user database was compromised, but it still cannot rule out that possibility. The database may contain usernames, email addresses, IP addresses, and hashed passwords.

OTW is recommending that users consider changing their AO3 password, make sure their account email is up to date, and change passwords on any other websites where they reused the same credentials.

No works, bookmarks, kudos, or other AO3 data were deleted during the outage. OTW also confirmed that draft deletion was paused while the site was offline and will remain paused for another two weeks. Normal draft deletion is scheduled to resume on November 29, meaning users should save or publish any drafts they want to keep before then.
