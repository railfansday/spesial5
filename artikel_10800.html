Apple introduces new 18+ age restrictions for dating apps and romantic AI companions

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Apple is introducing new App Store age rating requirements that will restrict dating apps and AI companions featuring sexual or romantic content to users aged 18 and older.

The changes were announced on November 2 and will begin taking effect on November 16, 2026, as part of an update to how developers classify their apps. The company says the new requirements are intended to ensure age ratings accurately reflect an app’s features and comply with local regulations.

The move follows Apple’s broader efforts to strengthen protections for minors, including the global rollout of age assurance technology and additional parental controls designed to give families more oversight of children’s online experiences.

Under the updated policy, developers will be required to disclose whether their apps include dating and relationship features, including interactions with virtual companions that engage users in sexual or romantic content.

Apple defines the new category as features that enable “dating or relationship connections between real people and/or a virtual companion that engages a user in sexual or romantic content.”

Any app containing these features will automatically receive a minimum age rating of 18+, regardless of whether dating or romantic interactions are its primary purpose.

The requirements could affect AI companion platforms such as Character.AI and Replika, which allow users to interact with virtual characters and create relationships ranging from friendships to romantic partnerships.

Elon Musk’s xAI also entered the AI companion market in 2025, introducing animated characters through Grok that included unlockable NSFW capabilities.

The growing popularity of these services has raised questions about how younger users interact with AI-generated personalities. By August 2025, leading AI companion apps across Apple’s App Store and Google Play had collectively accumulated approximately 220 million downloads worldwide.

However, Apple’s new classification specifically targets apps offering dating or relationship features and AI companions engaging in sexual or romantic content, rather than all AI chatbots or virtual assistants.

Alongside the dating and relationship category, Apple is introducing a separate content descriptor for apps that share users’ real-world locations with other individuals or groups.

Unlike dating apps, location-sharing services will not automatically receive a higher minimum age rating in every region.

In Brazil, local regulations require an A16 rating for apps featuring shared location functionality and an A18 rating for dating and relationship services. Developers offering family-oriented location-sharing tools can request an official lower age rating from the country’s Ministry of Justice and Public Security.

Developers must begin providing the new declarations when submitting app updates or modifying their age rating questionnaires starting November 16.

Apple has set January 12, 2027, as the deadline for all apps to complete the updated declarations, regardless of whether they submit an update before then.
