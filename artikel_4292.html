Killing Floor 3 Studio Announces Layoffs After Publishing Hire Spree

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Lauren has experience in the gaming and esports space, writing news, guides, features, reviews, lists, conducting interviews, and more. Before joining the team at DualShockers, Lauren was a Features Writer at Adventure Gamers, a Guides Writer at Gameranx, as well as contributing as a News Writer at PCInvasion and Daily

Layoffs are always devastating for the video game industry and the people affected. Unfortunately, they have become all too common, and whether it’s a small studio or a large household name, it seems nobody is safe in today’s climate.

Tripwire Interactive, best known for the Killing Floor series, is the latest studio to be hit by layoffs. The studio was founded back in 2005, and Embracer Group acquired Tripwire Interactive through its former subsidiary, Saber Interactive, in 2022.

In our Killing Floor 3 Review, we go over what could have worked and what didn't in the end for Tripwire's latest game.

In a statement published on the official Tripwire Interactive X account, the studio says it “has undergone a workforce reduction, affecting 36 talented people across our studio. This was certainly not an easy decision, but unfortunately, a necessary one for the organization.”

Tripwire Interactive describes the impacted employees as “truly great people who are exceptional at their roles,” and vows to “help and support these individuals with compassion and dignity during transition.”

The team will continue to focus on Maneater 2, which was officially announced as part of Sony’s September State of Play. It’s expected to launch sometime in 2027.

As the name suggests, Maneater 2 is the sequel to 2021’s Maneater, an action-packed RPG where you are the biggest threat in the open-world oceans that you’ll traverse. There are new evolutions, discoveries to be made, and a whole lot of opportunities to become the villain.

It has also been confirmed that work will continue on Killing Floor 3, as the developers try to salvage the rocky start the game had when it was released back in July 2025. Killing Floor 3 reviews remain mixed among critics and players, and updates are still rolling out, containing new content and fixes.

This isn’t the first time this year that Tripwire Interactive has held layoffs. On March 4, the studio cut its workforce by 23 people, meaning this new round of layoffs brings Tripwire Interactive’s total this year to 59. Only time will tell if any of Tripwire Interactive’s projects will suffer from delays as a result of these layoffs.

Similar to this instance, a real reason wasn’t provided for the March 4 layoffs, instead citing: “As the gaming industry continues to evolve, so do the needs of our studios, which means understanding our long-term strategy and aligning with business realities where needed in order to stay focused on our key creative goals.”

Tripwire Interactive describes the impacted employees as “truly great people who are exceptional at their roles,” and vows to “help and support these individuals with compassion and dignity during transition.”

On November 4, 2026, Tripwire Presents, the publishing sector of Tripwire Interactive, announced that it would be expanding its business to include scaled publishing support for indie developers to help put the spotlight on indie titles from across a variety of genres. The purpose of the initiative is to provide greater visibility for self-published indie games that Tripwire believes fans will enjoy.

It has been confirmed that Tripwire Presents will continue to go ahead with their plans, and the games that have already been chosen are The Boss Gangster: Criminal Empire, Starforged Legacy, Mars Attracts, One-Eyed Likho, and Eslabong.

This round of redundancies adds another to the tally of alarmingly large video game industry layoffs just this year alone. Other examples include Sucker Punch making staff cuts shortly after the launch of the Complete Edition of Ghost of Yōtei, and, of course, the mass layoffs conducted by XBOX.

Another three rounds of XBOX layoffs are slated to begin this month and last into the new year.
