Best racing games for Android

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

A list of the finest racing games that you can play on Android in 2026 to satisfy that need for speed.

Updated on November 11th, 2026  Do you love playing racing games but don't want to waste time reading through every review there is to pick the right one? Read on - you are in for a treat!

Remember when you had to pump a bunch of coins into a giant arcade cabinet if you wanted to play the latest and greatest titles? Now all you need is an Android phone, and it doesn't even have to be an expensive one.

Modern phones are a marvel, and they offer access to a vast and eclectic roster of racing and driving titles.

Why do we distinguish between racing and driving? It's simple, really - not all titles in which you control a vehicle are about dashing to the finish line. There's just way more variety out there these days.

Yes, you still get your arcade games on the Google Play Store. But there are also karts, simulators, management games, and weird little genre mash-ups that don't involve racing at all.

Comfortably the finest pure racing experience, GRID Autosport offers a startlingly faithful premium console experience with some truly stunning 3D graphics. It feels as good as it would on a console, and the touch controls are nothing short of amazing.

So, what differentiates GRID from the rest on this list? Besides the exceptional gameplay and realistic physics, it has a unique feature. You can turn back time! It's important because a crash here can leave your car broken, so you can reverse time for a few seconds and continue driving without making the same mistake again, hopefully. Of course, it has a limited number of uses; otherwise, it would just be plain old cheating.

Racing doesn't have to be constrained to the tarmac. Indeed, Reckless Racing 3 revels in the slidey delights that come from removing friction from the equation. It's a stunning title that prides itself on several features, including the smooth controls and spot-on genre touches.

You will find over twenty awesome vehicles, more than thirty unique routes, and fantastic modes, such as arcade, career, and others. While it's not as conventional as the other first-person car sliding titles out there, it will leave you wanting more. More speed, that is. Just read our review of Reckless Racing 3 - the dirt, drift, and obstacles are a must.

Physics and gravity are purely optional when it comes to Asphalt 9, a riotously OTT arcade game that can't help but make you smile. It's easy peasy to get into, and boy, does it look pretty. It's the successor to one of the best racing games for Android of all time - Asphalt 8!

From shiny cars to expensive yet extremely rewarding tuning features, there's something for everyone. When you compare the graphics with the rest on this list, Asphalt will probably win by a small margin. Just read our Asphalt 9 review - it'll give you everything you need to get you started on this crazy and fast racer.

Every old-school gamer might just get nostalgic the moment they touch Horizon Chase, as it will certainly bring up some of the best memories. Did you know we have a review of Horizon Chase? Make sure to check it out if you want to know why this blocky, colourful game is something that you need to play today!

It looks like Minecraft, but Blocky Roads builds its own physics racer universe with considerable style. Drive to the end of each track, keeping your car as upright and fuelled up as possible. Fair warning: it's more challenging than it first looks.

Ride this stunning blocky title into oblivion and pull out all the stunts you've got up your sleeve. If you think you've got what it takes, take it for a spin! Oh, and before you do that, read our review of Blocky Roads. You'll find all the answers you've been looking for over there.

CSR famously removes steering from the equation and focuses on going really fast in a straight line through well-timed gear shifts. In modern times, we call it drag racing! With less attention on the road, it can also focus on making its cars look amazing.

In our CSR Racing 2 tier list, we've shared some extremely important intel on the cars, which are the major head-turning points. They're absolutely stunning and realistic, so if you love a neat-looking Mustang or AMG, you bet you can find them here.

Prepare to run away from cops, play some drag races against other players and have fun in general. Car models look realistic, and it's easy to recognise one from another if you're a car enthusiast. The gameplay is a bit arcade-ish, as with most NFS titles, but it's all about having fun. Note that No Limits has plenty of pay-to-get mechanics, which might turn the F2P players away.

F1 Clash is a technically brilliant representation of elite-level motor racing, but with welcome accessibility. If you love your F1, you'll love being able to partake in one (or more). F1 Clash does just that, but does it brilliantly.

You'll be able to compete with some of your favourite drivers, from Hamilton to Vettel and Bottas, and compete against players from all around the world. It's exciting if you love professional races, and if you need some help, use this guide for F1 Clash.

The developers are adding new content every once in a while, so all of the teams and drivers that are present in F1 are featured.

A brilliantly balanced title that combines authentic muck-specked simulation with intuitive arcade racing. It looks fab, the touch controls are...good (for what could be considered touch controls), and the overall competitive element is there. You can build your garage and collect and upgrade many different cars as well.

We loved playing it so much that we even made a Rush Rally 3 review. It deserves a spot on this list of the best Android racing games, and it might deserve one in your pocket too!

A fun online side-scrolling platform-racer from the legendary Halfbrick Studios, in which characters from Jetpack Joyride, Fruit Ninja, and Dan the Man face off against one another. If you need fun gameplay to enjoy together with friends, it is probably one of the best options, with many races, character upgrades, and exciting weekly events.

It's a breath of fresh air from the four-wheel racers that we've overwhelmed this list with, so if you are looking for a competitive title that doesn't require fancy-looking fast cars to shine, check it out.

Nintendo's signature kart racer screeches onto mobile. While it's far from perfect, and perhaps a little too casual and 'freemium' for Nintendo die-hards, there's no doubt that its one-finger controls still feel like Mario Kart. And yes, it's a serious racer even though it looks cartoonish.

It's one of our favourites, even with the old-school phone and joypad doom - no one can count how many devices died because of the turtle that caught them up right before they were about to cross the finish line.

If you're interested already, our tier list of the best Mario Kart karts and best drivers in Mario Kart Tour will both come in handy.

With this one, we are closing out our list of the best racing games for Android. We hope that you found something to satisfy your thirst for speed! For those of you who are iOS enthusiasts, you can use our list of the finest racing games for iPhone instead.
