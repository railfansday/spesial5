Middlesbrough man to remain sidelined until December

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Middlesbrough will remain without the midfielder until the start of December. That means he is set to sit out of their upcoming games against Wrexham, Watford, Bristol City, Swansea City and Southampton.

Pukstas, 22, joined Boro in the summer on a long-term contract from Hadjuk Split running until June 2031. However, he has featured only once for the Championship promotion hopefuls.

In this latest update regarding his condition, his head coach Hellberg has said, as per a report by The Northern Echo: “He’s a month away, maybe, something like that. He’s a strong boy, he came from playing a lot and now he’s doing everything to come back stronger in every way he can. That’s how you try always to look at things, you can work on the things you can work at and try be stronger at that.”

Middlesbrough are sat top of the table after their first 13 fixtures ahead of the likes of Birmingham City, Stoke City, Swansea City and West Brom as they look to claw themselves into the Premier League. They face Wrexham away on Wednesday night.

Middlesbrough signed Pukstas in the last transfer window to bolster their midfield department. He gives them more competition and depth in the middle of the park.

At the age of 22, he has bags of potential and the capacity to grow and develop down the line. In the meantime, he faces a tough battle getting into Hellberg’s starting XI.

The USA youth international has some strong competition for minutes in his position. Boro have the likes of Aidan Morris, Sebastian Berhalter and Riley McGree, among others, at their disposal.

Pukstas started out at Sporting Kansas City before switching to Hadjuk Split as a teenager. He went on to become a key player for the Croatian outfit and scored 27 goals in 138 games before Middlesbrough lured him over to England for a new challenge.

Middlesbrough are having to be patient with Pukstas at the moment. He has struggled with injury since his switch and hasn’t played as much as he would have liked.

He is young though and still has plenty of time left ahead of him at Boro. He can still play a key role when he is back available and fit again as the matches come thick and fast over the December to January period.

Hellberg’s men will be full of confidence at the moment as they aim to keep their momentum going and build a gap at the summit. They beat Charlton Athletic 3-0 at home last time out and will be hoping for something similar against Wrexham next up.
