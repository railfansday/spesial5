Celtic B served an unflinching reality in defeat as big-name kids shown no mercy

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Ross County boss Stuart Kettlewell reckons his side could have put Celtic's star-studded B team to the sword by an even bigger margin after booking their place in the next round of the KDM Evolution Trophy with a 2-0 win in Dingwall.

The Staggies boss knew his side would face a stern test against a Celtic second string packed with pedigree. £3million summer signing Joel van den Berg was handed his debut, while highly-rated Chelsea youngsters Landon Emenalo and Shim Mheuka also started.

But County proved too strong for their visitors and Kettlewell believes the scoreline flattered the young Hoops after a dominant display in the Highlands.

Speaking to Ross County TV, Kettlewell said: "It was a competitive game. We were a bit frustrated at half-time because we didn't feel we'd handled the ball particularly well and we didn't think we'd quite got our pressing right.

"We knew Celtic would be comfortable in possession and would look to retain the ball, but we felt there were more opportunities for us to turn it over.

"That said, we scored a brilliant goal in the first half. The run from Dean Cornelius, the cross from Lewis Jamieson and the finish was first class. We were really pleased with that.

"We also had three, four or five other good opportunities, hit the woodwork a couple of times and, despite not playing as well as we would have liked, still created plenty of chances while limiting Celtic to very little.

"I thought we were better in the second half. We won the ball back more often, had a bit more composure in possession and created a lot of opportunities.

"Their young goalkeeper made some outstanding saves and, on another night, we probably score a few more goals. We hit the woodwork again and had numerous efforts on goal.

"You have to give credit to Celtic. They're a young side, but they're a good side. They've brought in players from elsewhere and have talented homegrown players as well. We always knew it would be a good test and another challenge for us to solve in this competition."

Van den Berg's first outing since arriving from PSV offered an early glimpse of the highly-rated Dutch defender, while Chelsea duo Emenalo and Mheuka both got 72 minutes under their belts.

The trio are not part of Celtic's Europa League squad and therefore won't be involved against Besiktas, but they will hope their contributions in Dingwall have strengthened their case for a place in Martin O'Neill's plans ahead of Sunday's Premiership clash with Kilmarnock.
