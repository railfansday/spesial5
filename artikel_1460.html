Gasperini to undertake legal action against Walter Sabatini

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Walter Sabatini apologizes to Gian Piero Gasperini following comments he made on Sunday morning to Quotidiano Nazionale.

The former Roma sporting director backtracked on his comments in an Instagram story, admitting he was wrong. “I have no problem admitting I was wrong and apologizing for it,” Sabatini wrote, referencing the interview, which focused on Albert Camus’s novel The Stranger.

During his remarks, the former director accused Gasperini of shoving Frederic Massara in front of the team last season and of trying to undermine him to favor those close to him.

“If he had done it to me, I would have punched him,” Sabatini added. The Giallorossi coach had been informed of these statements by several people and had decided not to respond publicly.

“I was deeply involved in the themes explored in the novel, attempting to explore the affinities and behaviors of the novel’s protagonist, with whom I inexorably identify,” Sabatini explained in a message posted on social media. He then addressed his apology directly to the coach: “I sincerely apologize to Gasperini and to anyone who may have been offended by my words.”

Gasperini, meanwhile, has already instructed his lawyers to defend himself following the former sporting director’s accusations. The coach does not intend to answer questions on the matter, either now or in the future.
