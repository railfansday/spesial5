Everton Women To Honour Armed Forces For Remembrance Fixture At Goodison Park

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Everton Women will pay tribute to the Armed Forces during their Subway Players Cup fixture against Durham at Goodison Park on Wednesday 11 November (19:00 kick-off) – with free tickets on offer to serving military personnel as part of the Club’s annual Remembrance fixture.

In recognition of their service, members of the Armed Forces are invited to attend free of charge, with tickets available via Tickets For Troops.

Service personnel can check their eligibility for Tickets For Troops membership by clicking here.

Those eligible can then register here, before accessing Everton Women tickets at ticketsfortroops.org.uk.

This initiative builds on the Club’s long-standing support for the Tickets for Troops charity, which provides sporting and entertainment tickets to UK Armed Forces personnel and veterans.

The match will see veterans from Everton in the Community's Veterans’ Hub take part in a pre-match tribute, which will include a performance of The Last Post. This will be followed by a moment of silence and reflection as players, supporters and staff from both sides unite to remember those who have lost their lives in conflict and those who continue to serve today.

Everton in the Community’s Veterans’ Hub provides vital support to ex-service personnel through mental health provision, social activities and employability programmes, and representatives from the charity will be present at the fixture to raise awareness of their work.

This year’s Remembrance fixtures continue Everton’s proud tradition of honouring the Armed Forces community across both men’s and women’s football – reflecting a commitment to respect, inclusion and the deep-rooted connection with its local community.

Everton’s Men’s team took part in a tribute to the fallen ahead of Friday’s Premier League fixture against Coventry City at Hill Dickinson Stadium. Poppy shirts from all men’s and women’s fixtures during the Remembrance period will be signed and auctioned in aid of the Royal British Legion. To find out more - or to bid for a shirt - click here.
