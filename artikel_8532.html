Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Moving everyday banking away from Fidelity for security. Vanguard Cash Plus or another bank?

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

I keep a BoA/ME account with a non-trivial amount of money in it in case something goes wrong with my Fidelity/CMA account. With that said, I am concerned about some of the motivations here, particularly about phone security. But let's first assume that you have face-id/fingerprint on your phone and you have "money lock" on non-CMA investment accounts.

My main concern is having access to almost my entire net worth on my everyday phone. Since I use Fidelity CMA for checking, I frequently need to log in to check transactions or transfer money. I’m increasingly uncomfortable with this, especially considering scenarios where my phone is stolen while unlocked.
I for security purpose I don't want to have my investment app in phone and want to keep the bank separately. So right now Vanguard Cash Plus seems like an option where I could use for everyday banking use and also for I can keep some emergency fund in one brokerage that would let me transfer between the cash plus and the brokerage quickly instead of actually do an external transfer from Fidelity.
I keep as little of my financial life on my phone as possible in case it's lost, stolen, or hacked; not even the email account to which banks and brokerages send me emails. The only financial app (which is somewhat hidden) on my phone I use for mobile check deposit at a regional bank at which I only keep a few thousand $.
It's counterintuitive, but your phone is FAR MORE SECURE than your computer. If your computer is hacked there are a wide variety of ways the hacker could jump from any app on your computer to compromising what is in your web browser or financial management app. While your phone could be stolen, the scenario where they steal it while it is unlocked and your Fidelity app is unlocked and then manage to do something to damage you is roughly inconceivable. Even if they get the phone while it's unlocked, doing something like creating a new linked external account would require re-authentication (face id) in the app. (I'll acknowledge that there is some debate about exactly how secure iOS and Android are, but it's in the noise compared to whether it's more secure than Windows or even MacOS.)

There are good reasons to have backup accounts, and I have mixed feelings about investment accounts being in the same place I go for daily transactions. But lack of security on a phone isn't one of them.
