Google Pixel prepares new ‘Priority Notifications’ feature – here’s what it will do

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Google has a new “Priority Notifications” feature in the works for Pixel phones with the ability to pull your most important information to the top of the screen and even highlight it with a bit of color.

Google Pixel phones already have tools to manage your notifications, but an upcoming update seems to be building on that functionality. The folks at Android Authority flipped the switch on some work-in-progress changes that reveal “Priority Notifications.”

This is a new feature that Google describes as “highlighting urgrent messages” and “time-critical updates” by adding color to the notification and placing it at the top of the stock. Android’s notifications list already tries to place messages at the top, but it sounds like this AI-powered feature will further emphasize that. It’s also mentioned that this is powered by on-device AI.

Highlight urgent messages, deliveries, and time-critical updates with hints of color at the top of your notifications.

Priority notifications use Al on your device, which may sometimes make mistakes. Your notifications are never sent to Google.

The feature would also integrate with HiLight on the Pixel 11 Pro series and the feature allows users to exclude certain apps. Google also seems to be shuffling the branding, turning “Notification intelligence” into “Notification organizer,” and the existing organizer section into “Notification bundles.”

Exactly when this might land on Pixel phones is unknown. It’s not mentioned what build this feature was found hiding in, but it seems likely it was in the ongoing QPR3 beta. QPR3’s stable release will arrive around March 2027, but there’s no guarentee it’ll bring this new feature with it.
