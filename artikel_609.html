I’m traveling more than ever these days, which is one of my favorite perks of being a journalist. I get to visit places I might not have otherwise, and it’s made me want to plan more trips of my own. The only problem is that traveling is expensive. Like, exorbitantly expensive.

Between flights, hotels, and meals, there’s a lot to account for before you’ve even packed a bag. (Not to mention figuring out who’s going to watch the pups.) I love having a trip to look forward to, but I’d prefer not to spend the weeks leading up to it wondering if it’s going to leave me in financial ruin.
