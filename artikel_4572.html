Ford’s Frostwarden Bronco Is a One-Off Rescue Vehicle with 1200 HP

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Frostwarden blends Ford’s new Megazilla 2.0 crate engine with an extreme search-and-rescue Bronco build.

A base Ford Bronco makes 300 horsepower. Opt for the V-6 option, and it’ll add 30 horses; if that’s not enough, the Bronco Raptor reaches 418. Not there yet? Try doubling the Raptor’s output and adding a few hundred more horsepower. Enter the Megazilla 2.0.

Ford’s most powerful crate engine ever is now on display for the first time at this year’s SEMA show in Las Vegas, Nevada. The Megazilla 2.0, which builds off the 7.3-liter V-8 crate engine lineup, uses a stronger block, upgraded pistons, larger fuel injectors, a different belt drive and damper, and a supercharger-specific camshaft to keep up with a 3.0-liter Gen 6 Whipple supercharger. All of that combines to make over 1200 horsepower and over 1000 pound-feet of torque.

To show off the engine’s massive output, Ford developed the Frostwarden: a search-and-rescue-inspired Bronco taken to extremes. The outfitted Bronco is lifted with a 74Weld portal lift kit and wears 42-inch-tall BFGoodrich tires mounted on custom 18-inch Weld beadlock wheels. Dana driveline hardware and custom Bilstein dampers round out the undercarriage upgrades.

The Frostwarden’s black-and-yellow livery, ultra-wide stance, and plethora of rescue tools—including off-road lights, a rear-mounted external gas tank, roof-mounted spare tire, and bright yellow front tow winch—make the Bronco hard to miss, even among the ranks of modded SEMA show cars. Inside, yellow-and-black upholstery lines the cabin and a special Megazilla wordmark rests along the bottom of the steering wheel.

The Frostwarden is a one-of-a-kind build, but the 7.3-liter supercharged V-8 that powers it isn’t. Ford says the one-off vehicle highlights the ability to launch extreme builds based around Ford-powered engines, including the massive Megazilla 2.0.
