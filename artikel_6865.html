C4Media Inc.: Enterprise Account Executive

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

C4 = Community of Communities created by the Community & for the Community.

Since 2006, C4Media has been serving the worldwide enterprise software development community with a place to track and facilitate change and innovation. All content published on our websites and presented at our events is put together by industry practitioners. We help software development teams adopt new technologies and practices. Our mission is to facilitate the spread of knowledge and innovation in professional software development by sharing the knowledge of experienced software engineers and architects.

C4Media Inc. is seeking a highly motivated Account Executive, Enterprise Sales to drive new business growth and expand relationships within existing accounts. This is a hunter-focused enterprise sales role, with approximately 85% of the role focused on proactively identifying, prospecting, developing, and closing net-new enterprise customers through strategic outbound engagement and relationship building. In this role, you will be selling the C4Media enterprise solutions, which are still evolving and currently include group tickets, online training and annual subscriptions to our software conferences. You will own the full sales cycle—from prospecting and qualification to closing—while building long-term, trust-based partnerships with senior decision-makers across industries.

This position requires a consultative, strategic seller who thrives in a fast-paced environment, values collaboration, and brings proven experience managing complex enterprise accounts.
