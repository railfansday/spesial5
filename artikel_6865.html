Perfume Jewelry Is the Most Whimsical Way to Wear Fragrance This Fall

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Fall fragrance is moving from your vanity to your wardrobe.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Fragrance has always been a subtle, almost invisible way to accessorize an outfit. This fall, it’s become something you can actually wear: around your neck, on your finger, or clipped to your bag. Perfume has taken a more literal form. The concept isn’t entirely new (see Guerlain’s Le Secret de la Reine, from 2022), but it’s gaining popularity as more modern brands take notice.

The most recent launch is the OUAI x Heaven Mayhem Amulet Necklaces, which the brands describe as “a collectible piece that brings scent and jewelry into one timeless object.” Each of the two pendants is inspired by one of OUAI’s iconic scents, Rue St. Honoré and Ibiza, and is meant to turn putting on fragrance into a ritual, wherever you go.

Lately, we’ve noticed beauty weaving itself into our daily lives rather than just sitting on the vanity. And honestly, our inner child is loving every second of this grown-up, luxe take on beauty. Accessories like these, along with other beauty-adjacent pieces (the Dior Médallion!), add a touch of whimsy to the everyday.

Ahead, we’ve rounded up more wearable perfume pieces to help you accessorize this fall, while keeping you smelling good.

Inspired by Heaven Mayhem’s Marlowe Necklace, this amulet comes with a tiny funnel, so you can wear your favorite OUAI scents and keep them close at hand. Choose between two designs, each holding perfume from its corresponding fragrance: Rue St. Honoré and Ibiza.

This sterling silver oyster hangs from an adjustable black satin cord and is designed to be filled with your favorite Liis fragrance.

A pink butterfly ring made to house Carolina Herrera’s La Bomba perfume is the coolest functional accessory for keeping you smelling good, with your perfume literally on hand.

A perfume bag charm won’t adorn you, but it will look adorable on your bag and be there whenever you need a roll of scent on the go.

This heirloom necklace is the kind of accessory future generations will love to receive. Made by Kindred Black in collaboration with J.Hannah, it isn’t designed to hold liquid fragrance. Instead, it works as a talisman, carrying your chosen scent like a shield. It comes with a small red fabric swatch to soak in J.Hannah’s Drink Deep, Psyche, then tuck inside the pendant.

Victoria Beckham Beauty’s necklace is all about keeping the romance alive. It holds Suite 302, a fragrance inspired by David and Victoria’s anniversary weekends in Paris. Even the white enamel coating has a story, as it’s a nod to the white dress Victoria wore when she surprised David with a vow renewal.

This solid perfume charm makes getting ready on the go feel effortless and intentional. Clip it to your bag, or slip it into a pocket for a touch-up on the go.

Like an adjustable bolo tie, this solid perfume pendant slides to fit your preferred length and holds Mind Games’ Kingside fragrance. It’s a cool, slightly offbeat way to keep your scent close, no spritzing required.
