Count down to Christmas with these nail advent calendars

There’s something especially satisfying about counting down to the holidays, one tiny surprise at a time. And while chocolate is always a welcome treat, nail polish might be the better option for anyone who considers a fresh manicure a more indulgent form of self-care. The best nail polish advent calendars of 2026 are packed with everything from festive glitter and rich winter shades to high-end files and hydrating hand creams. With a mani-focused moment each December morning, every day can feel like a trip to the nail salon—without ever leaving home.

Whether you’re shopping for a nail-art enthusiast, looking to discover a new favorite polish, or simply want to make your own holiday countdown a little more glamorous, there’s a calendar for you. We’ve rounded up the best options worth adding to your cart, from beauty brands offering miniature collections to luxe sets that deliver a little something special behind every door. Consider this your excuse to start your holiday manicure rotation early.
