Open Thread: Spurs offering grants to local organizations through Operation Renovation

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The NBA is known for its ties to community service. In each city where a team resides, numerous non-profits, businesses, and youth organizations receive support via the local franchise. When it comes to supporting local entities, the San Antonio Spurs go above and beyond. From the contributions of individual players (talking about you Harrison Barnes) to the Spurs organization as a whole, there is a balance of year round outreach and support.

"Spurs Give and Frost Bank (Frost) are inviting local organizations to apply for the 2026–27 Operation Renovation grant program, which supports facility improvements that create safe and inspiring spaces for youth to learn and play. Online applications for the 2026-27 Operation Renovation Grant are now open through Friday, Dec. 18 at 11:59 p.m."

Julie McCarty, Associate Vice President of Community Investments for Spurs Sports and Entertainment stated,

"Everyone deserves spaces where they feel welcome, supported and connected to their community. As we enter our ninth year of Operation Renovation with Frost, we're proud to support local organizations that do so much for San Antonio families and help improve the spaces they rely on every day."

Spurs Give and Frost have awarded over $1.3 million since 2018-2019. Every year, applicants are eligible to receive up to $100,000 for facility renovations.

Applications can be accessed HERE. Deadline to apply is Friday, December 18th. 2026.
