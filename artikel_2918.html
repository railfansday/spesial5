The ‘GTA VI’ Vice City court for the Heat is amazing

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

We're just two days removed from the biggest video game release of the year, heck one of the biggest video game releases of all time. The world is going to grind to a halt on Thursday with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI, and the Miami Heat are going all in.

The first photos of the Heat's transformed court have been released and they look INCREDIBLE.

The Heat will be playing on the new-look court for their Wednesday night game against the Milwaukee Bucks, just hours ahead of the release at midnight, Eastern time.

If we step back from this it's all pretty hilarious. Here's an NBA team completely embracing a video game that is focused on crime, chaos, and mayhem. Heck, maybe we'll have a side mission that involves a professional basketball player rigging a game for the purposes of gambling, who knows? Not that it's ever happened before. It will be fascinating to see how many sports venues are represented inside the game as well, which could include their own versions of Hard Rock Stadium or the F1 track in Miami.

We all can't wait for GTA VI, pretty much like the rest of the world.
