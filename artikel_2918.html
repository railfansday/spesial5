Nvidia's Stock Surges More Than 30% on Jensen Huang's Return. Will It Get Back to $200?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

On Monday, the company announced that Huang is back as CEO, after resigning just a few weeks earlier.

By David Jagielski, CPA – Nov 23, 2026 at 6:40PM EST

What a turnaround. In just a single day, Nvidia (NVDA +31.48%) stock jumped more than 30%. The tech company announced that it was reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO, who had resigned earlier in the month after multiple extramarital affairs came to light. It also comes after the board said it wasn't considering bringing him back.

Investors emphatically gave the move their stamp of approval, with the stock skyrocketing on the day, back to around a $4 trillion valuation. It is, however, still down more than 30% from its peak. Given the excitement around Nvidia's stock right now, could it be heading back to $200?

With the huge rally on Monday, Nvidia's stock is back up to around the levels it was at a few weeks ago, when Huang initially resigned. However, even before the scandal came to light, the stock was already falling from its highs; a month ago, the tech stock was trading at around $210.

The company's board initially said it would not take Huang back as CEO. However, by Monday, it unanimously voted to reinstate him. A big part of the reason appears to be due to the government's input. U.S. President Donald Trump has spoken positively of Huang and believed it was in the company's best interests to have him back, and says he convinced the board to take him back, saying, "I made the deal."

While there's one less question mark around Nvidia's business right now, there are still others looming, including what will happen involving Congress. Huang is scheduled to meet with Congress on Dec. 1, facing questions about a relationship with a possible Chinese spy.

Trump, however, says he gave Nvidia's board assurances about national security: "We gave them assurances at least with everything we know, and we know a lot." On the surface, it would appear that issues relating to national security may not be significant enough to warrant any further concern from the company's board.

Given how volatile the stock has been and the excitement around it, I wouldn't be surprised to see Nvidia shares get back up to $200 in the near future. However, the broader market risk of rising interest rates still looms, which could impact the business and its growth prospects in the longer run.

For now, the market appears to be focused on the short term; however, this can expose the stock to significant volatility. Investors should tread carefully because while Nvidia's hot today, if market sentiment sours next month due to rising interest rates, things could change quickly.
