Pistons at Pelicans final score: No Cade, No Problem

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

With as long as Detroit's injury report has been recently, it got a little longer with Cade Cunningham as a late addition tonight. After the Pistons' big win against San Antonio last night, it's no surprise Deuce got some rest on the second night of a back-to-back.

JB Bickerstaff, with his shorthanded roster, rolled out the unusual starting lineup of Daniss Jenkins, Kevin Huerter, Javonte Green, Ausar Thompson, and John Collins. This would've been a great game for Ebuka Okorie to get some minutes, but he hurt his hip with the Motor City Cruise just the other day.

This meant Detroit's key bench pieces were Paul Reed, Chaz Lanier, and Elijah Harkless. Even Tolu Smith saw some spot minutes when Collins and Reed both needed rest. Luckily, Isaiah Joe made his return to the lineup tonight after missing the last several games. Despite having to play guys from the end of the bench and on two-way spots, Detroit didn't struggle against New Orleans.

Remember that unusual starting lineup for Detroit? Well, every single one of them finished in double digits. Jenkins, Green, and Collins all made at least three three-pointers. Jenkins, Thompson, and Huerter all had at least four assists, though Huerter had seven and DJ added nine.

Both Lanier and Joe made three three-pointers off the bench. Reed was the key sixth man once again, though that's not all that peculiar nowadays. Harkless played his most minutes so far this season and did a great job sharing the rock with four assists.

In a game without their star player, this was another complete team win. There's no doubt the depth on this team is one of the best in the league, and Detroit had seven different players score in double digits. Your Detroit Pistons are now 10-4 on the year.
