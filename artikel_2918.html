Scouting the Enemy: Las Vegas Raiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Denver Broncos battle the Las Vegas Raiders on Sunday. It's a big division rivalry matchup for Broncos who are coming off their bye week. After starting their season 3-0, the Raiders have dropped six of their las seven games and are 4-6 on the season. Here is a deeper look at their Week 11 opponent.

2025 Las Vegas Raiders Regular Season Review

The Pete Carroll experiment in Las Vegas lasted just one season. The Raiders finished dead last in the AFC West with a 3-14 record. After his dismissal, the Raiders hired Klint Kubiak to be their next Head Coach.

2026 Las Vegas Raiders Offseason Additions and Departures

After a disappointing '25 season, the Raiders embarked on a tremendous overhaul this offseason. Both their offense and defense were awful. They added a handful of players on offense early on in free agency including center Tyler Linderbaum and wide receiver Jalen Nailor.

On defense, they overhauled their linebacking corps bringing in former Georgia Bulldog teammates Nakobe Dean and Quay Walker to man the middle of their defense. They also added edge rushers Kwity Paye and Malcolm Koonce to bolster their pass rush. Additionally, the Raiders traded for former Buffalo Bills defensive back Taron Johnson to shore up their secondary.

Head Coach Klint Kubiak and General Manager John Spytek were busy this past April making ten selections in the 2026 NFL Draft. Quarterback Fernando Mendoza, taken with the top pick, is poised to start his second career game with the Broncos. The Raiders found two starters on Day 2 with defensive back Treydan Stukes and offensive lineman Trey Zuhn. On Day 2, they made high upside picks with cornerback Jermod McCoy and running back Mike Washington Jr. who have seen a decent amount of reps this season.

Offensive X-Factor: Quarterback Fernando Mendzoa

Tight end Brock Bowers was going to get the nod here. However, Bowers injured his ankle against the Seattle Seahawks in Week 10. While the ankle injury won't land him on injured reserve, Head Coach Klint Kubiak stated he will be day-to-day and did classify it as significant.

With that in mind, I'll turn to rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza as their offensive X-Factor for the game. Last week against the Seahawks, Mendoza made his first start of his NFL. He struggled and completed just 17 his 29 pass attempts for only 143 yards. However, he did get his first career touchdown with 10-yard quarterback draw in the first quarter of the matchup.

The Raiders' offensive line had just allowed 11 sacks all season before the Seahawks game, but their defense was able to drop Mendoza for six sacks. This is a game where Denver's defensive front seven needs to play at a high level. I consider it a favorable matchup for Defensive Coordinator Vance Joseph and his players. If they can rattle the rookie signal caller and force him into bad decisions, I really like their chances on Sunday.

Defensive X-Factor: Edge rusher Maxx Crosby

For a moment earlier in the spring, Broncos Country was elated with the news that premier edge rusher Maxx Crosby would be heading out of the division to join the Baltimore Ravens. However, much like the Daiyan Henley trade, the deal was nixed due to a failed physical. So now, he is back for his eighth season with the Raiders.

At 29-years-old, Crosby has cemented himself as one of the league's best defenders. He has earned five-straight Pro-Bowl designations and has been a two-time All-Pro dating back to the 2021 season. In ten games this season, Crosby has 34 tackles, 9 tackles for loss, 21 quarterback hits, and 5 sacks.

During the bye and earlier this week, Head Coach Sean Payton emphasized that blocking up front, whether it's against the run or the pass, is one of the huge things Denver's offense needs to accomplish to finally hit their stride in the second half of the season. Going up against Crosby will be a big challenge for them. We will see if the offensive line is up to the task.

How the Broncos can achieve a victory over the Raiders

ICYMI: Yesterday, I wrote how the Broncos can achieve their eighth victory of the season over the Raiders. Overall, I feel confident about Denver's chances. The extra week of preparation should help them out, plus going up against a rookie quarterback in his second start should help matters. I believe the Broncos will achieve a decisive double-digit victory over the Raiders.

Hart's Prediction: Broncos 30, Raiders 16.
