Brock Bowers, Matt Gay questionable for Raiders-Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

There's a chance quarterback Fernando Mendoza will have his top target for his second start.

Raiders head coach Klint Kubiak told reporters on Friday that tight end Brock Bowers will be questionable for Sunday's game against the Broncos.

Bowers has been dealing with an ankle injury suffered in Sunday's loss to the Seahawks. Bowers was a non-participant on Wednesday but was back on the field and limited in Thursday's session.

Kubiak said that Bowers "got lots of work in" on Friday.

The third-year tight end has caught 50 passes for 676 yards with six touchdowns in eight games so far this season.

Additionally, kicker Matt Gay is questionable with his right hip injury. The Raiders have rookie Kansei Matsuzawa available on the practice squad if Gay is not available. Gay is 21-of-23 on field goals and 22-of-22 on extra points in 2026.

Defensive tackle Foley Fatukasi (toe) is also questionable for Week 11.
