Once little kids graduate to big kids, the fun really begins outdoors. Kids in the 6-to-12 range are adventurous, enthusiastic, and psyched to emulate their favorite adults—aka you—with rad outdoor activities. The best outdoor toys for kids feed that wild streak, so they’re ready to bike, climb, hike, or rip down a backyard zip line with aplomb.

We polled our editors and colleagues at Outside to find the best outdoor toys for kids in that post-toddler, preteen age bracket. Here are their favorites, every one tested by their own kids.
