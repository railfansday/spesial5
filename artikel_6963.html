Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Anchour is a creative agency of 38 and growing, focused on building unforgettable brands and digital experiences that help businesses punch above their weight. Headquartered in Maine, we specialize in serving SMBs and startups across industries like consumer goods, home services, financial services, retail, and professional services.

As a full-service agency, we offer strategy, branding, website design, development, copywriting, and performance marketing solutions. At Anchour, we believe in cultivating an inclusive environment where creativity, curiosity, and collaboration thrive.

We are looking for a driven and passionate Marketing Designer to join our creative team at our agency. In this role, you will be responsible for designing high-quality, visually appealing digital assets and campaigns for a variety of clients. Your designs will be used across a range of platforms, including compelling static and animated social ads, marketing emails, and landing pages for a variety of exciting brands.

You are a creative powerhouse with a deep understanding of digital platforms, a keen eye for detail, and the ability to push boundaries while meeting client objectives. As a Marketing Designer, you will take ownership of your work—from concept to execution—and collaborate closely with strategy, content, and digital teams to bring bold, effective ideas to life.
