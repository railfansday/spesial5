Showa American Story delayed to October 28, 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Publisher 4Divinity and developer NEKCOM Games have delayed “post-apocalyptic romance RPG” and “alternate history ensemble period drama” Showa American Story from its previously planned 2026 release window to October 28, 2027. It will be available for PlayStation 5 and PC via Steam.

A new trailer and the first hands-on demo for Showa American Story were shown off at Gamescom 2026, which ran from August 26 to 30 at Koelnmesse in Cologne, Germany.

Here is the full message from NEKCOM Games executive producer on the delay:

We finally have an official release date to share: Showa American Story will launch on October 28, 2027.

Reaching this milestone after five years of development means a lot to our entire team. With core game content now complete, we’ re dedicating our full focus to polishing and optimizing the experience so that when you step into Showa American Story, it feels cohesive, responsive, and complete.

Thank you for your continued support, enthusiasm, and patience along the way. We can’t wait to welcome you on October 28, 2027.
