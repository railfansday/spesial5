WeVote: Director of Content Strategy

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Help Shape How Millions of Voters Engage with Reliable Civic Information

WeVote is a nonpartisan civic technology nonprofit dedicated to increasing voter participation through innovative digital tools and trusted voter education resources. As a Fast Forward nonprofit technology grantee, WeVote empowers voters with the information they need to make informed decisions and participate confidently in democracy.

We are seeking a strategic and innovative Director of Content Strategy to lead the development and execution of WeVote's content vision across digital channels. This volunteer leadership role is ideal for an experienced content strategist, communications leader, digital marketer, or media professional who is passionate about civic engagement and audience growth.

As Director of Content Strategy, you will define how WeVote communicates with voters, volunteers, supporters, and stakeholders through impactful content initiatives. You will oversee content planning, messaging strategy, editorial priorities, audience engagement efforts, and cross-functional collaboration to expand WeVote's reach and influence.

Create and lead a comprehensive content strategy that aligns with WeVote's mission, organizational goals, audience needs, and voter engagement objectives.

Identify opportunities to expand audience reach, increase engagement, and strengthen relationships with voters, volunteers, donors, and supporters through strategic content initiatives.

Oversee content roadmaps, editorial calendars, and campaign planning across blogs, newsletters, voter education resources, thought leadership content, and digital communications.

Develop messaging frameworks and content guidelines that ensure consistency across all channels while reinforcing WeVote's nonpartisan mission and brand voice.

Partner with leaders across Product, Political Data, Communications, Marketing, Partnerships, and Volunteer Recruitment to identify content opportunities and align messaging efforts.

Monitor content effectiveness, audience behavior, engagement metrics, and communication trends to continuously improve content strategy and impact.

Provide strategic guidance and leadership to volunteer writers, editors, researchers, content creators, and communications team members.

Help position WeVote as a trusted leader in civic technology, voter education, and democratic participation through compelling storytelling and content innovation.

3+ years of experience in content strategy, communications, digital marketing, content leadership, audience development, media, publishing, or related fields.

Demonstrated ability to create and execute content strategies that drive awareness, engagement, audience growth, and organizational impact.

Experience understanding audience needs, developing content journeys, and optimizing content performance across digital channels.

Exceptional writing, editing, messaging, and storytelling abilities with the ability to translate complex topics into clear, accessible content.

Comfortable using content analytics, engagement insights, and performance metrics to guide strategic decisions.

Experience collaborating with diverse stakeholders and managing projects across multiple teams and initiatives.

Passion for democracy, civic engagement, voter education, and leveraging content to create meaningful social impact.

Ability to thrive in a remote, volunteer-driven environment while independently driving strategic initiatives forward.

Location: Remote (U.S.-based volunteers preferred)

Time Commitment: Approximately 5–10 hours per week
