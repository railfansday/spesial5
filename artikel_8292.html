Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: AI and tracking personal finances.

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Just wanted to chime in here and say I agree that AI has been extremely helpful and empowering. I’ve produced a lot of useful tools and trackers that have built on the ones I have produced over the years for budgeting, estimating savings, and tracking liquidity for example. It helps me evaluate contracts and summarize them, especially when I am considering certain types of insurance products. I find that it also helps me with idea generation, especially when dealing with certain middlemen like brokers, it can help draft emails and questions. What I like about AI is that if you give it more or less accurate information, it reads everything and can give you back good, thought-out results. Attaching documents like those from employers for benefits and prospectuses and anything complicated it can really help synthesize.

But the tool can and still does mistakes. I think it’s still important to go through everything extremely carefully. This is still an evolving technology with risks.
