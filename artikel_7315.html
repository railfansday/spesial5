Tanggal: 2026-11-05

When rumors about Taylor Swift’s and Travis Kelce’s wedding on the Fourth of July weekend began swirling on social media, Abdul El-Sayed wanted in on the moment.

At a holiday barbecue, the Michigan Democratic candidate—now the first Muslim elected to the U.S. Senate—pitched his staff the idea of doing a tribute, according to Ali Bibbo, digital director of El-Sayed’s campaign.

His team swapped in new lyrics taking a jab at Mike Rogers, the Republican opponent, and the video garnered 6 million views.
