Brands turn to handmade craft, handfuls of creators for holiday season

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Let Marketing Dive's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

Coca-Cola, Walmart and Levi’s are among the holiday marketers relying on the real thing as consumers grow weary of ads generated with artificial intelligence.

The holidays are here, and marketers are ready for a season when average household spend is expected to rise 4% year over year, per Deloitte’s annual survey. The battle for consumer attention and spend started even earlier in 2026, with the number of holiday ads running in September seeing an 11% increase over the same month last year, according to Nielsen research.

A major theme of this year’s holiday marketing is handmade craft as brands seek to engage consumers who have grown weary of ads generated with artificial intelligence. Other marketers have leaned into consumer desires for human connection by making creators a key part of their seasonal messaging.
