Savage Ventures Weighs $60M-$70M Bid for Insolvent Comments Platform OpenWeb

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The venture capital firm Savage Ventures, which, among other holdings, operates the digital assets of Vice, is weighing a bid of between $60 million and $70 million for the insolvent advertising technology company once valued at $1.5 billion, according to a person familiar with the matter.

The bid would cover OpenWeb’s outstanding debt, which stands at around $57 million. It would also enable the adtech firm to pay its employees and publisher partners, as well as inject capital to stabilize the business, the person said.

Savage Ventures, which has a track record of acquiring distressed media businesses, would fund the purchase with cash rather than a fundraise, according to the person.

Mark Stenberg is ADWEEK's senior media reporter.
