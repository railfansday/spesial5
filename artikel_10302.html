Google Meet unlocks 1080p video streaming for all accounts on web

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Streaming in 1080p will be the default in Google Meet, regardless of whether you pay for Google Workspace. That includes personal Google Accounts.

Google says users will default to sending and receiving 1080p video, at the minimum. Previously, Google Meet made 720p the standard for streaming, and a higher resolution had historically been locked behind the Google One 2TB plan. That’s been true since 1080p streaming was rolled out in 2023.

The upgrade will roll out to everyone, including those with Google Workspace accounts and personal Google Accounts. The only requirement to stream your own 1080p video in Google Meet for web will be hardware-based. Google’s press release states that devices with cameras capable of streaming in high definition will do so, and the feature will be turned on automatically.

It can be disabled, though. Users can head to Settings > Video in Google Meet for web. Video will also appear in a lower resolution if the user’s video tile is shrunk down on-screen.

The update appears to be centered on web-only instances of Google Meet, and might not include the app on Android or iOS. For the time being, those versions may still be limited to streaming in 720p for those who don’t have an active Google One subscription.

Google Meet started its extended rollout of 1080p streaming to everyone earlier this month, so it shouldn’t be much longer until the change has taken place completely.
