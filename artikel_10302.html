Android 17 QPR2 November update rolling out with 33 Pixel fixes

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Android 17 QPR2 is rolling out a month early with the November 2026 Feature Drop and security patch. The update is available for the Pixel 6a, 7, 7 Pro, 7a, Tablet, Fold, 8, 8 Pro, 8a, 9, 9 Pro, 9 Pro XL, 9 Pro Fold, 9a, 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL, 10a, 11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL, and 11 Pro Fold.

There are Pixel fixes across Apps, Audio, Battery & Charging, Bluetooth, Camera, Connectivity, Display & Graphics, Framework, Performance, Sensors, System, Telephony, and User Interface.

There are 135 security issues resolved in the Android 17 November patch dated 2026-11-01 and 97 for 2026-11-05. Vulnerabilities range from high to critical.
