Land Use Labs Launches Interactive Platform for On-Demand Zoning Lookups

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Land Use Labs—a zoning data and services company—has announced the launch of the LUL Platform, a subscription-based service delivering comprehensive zoning information directly to users across the real estate and property sectors.

According to a release, platform users can instantly retrieve, review and store zoning information for over 100 million parcels from Land Use Labs’ database. Each query returns the site’s zoning rules, including zoning district names, use permissions, setbacks, allowed density and parking requirements.

Users can also perform queries by selecting a point on the map, searching by address or passing latitude-longitude coordinates, with previous searches stored in an account’s lookup history.

The release states that for users who need to integrate zoning data directly into their models, each subscription to the LUL Platform includes API keys to access the Zoning API. The endpoint returns property-specific zoning information as JSON, enabling users to conduct site selection or feasibility analyses at a nationwide scale.

The LUL Platform also streamlines bulk data downloads for Land Use Labs suite of proprietary products, including Zoning Districts and Zoning Slices.

“Our Platform significantly expands access to the country’s most reliable and comprehensive zoning data,” Land Use Labs Founder and CEO, Sara Bronin, said. “We optimized the user experience to instantly deliver accurate, clear information to people who actually want to build.”
