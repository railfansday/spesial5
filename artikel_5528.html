Trace Systems Awarded Position on GSA TTS NEXT BPA for Cloud, Infrastructure, and Cybersecurity Services

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

TAMPA, Fla., Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Trace Systems Inc. (Trace) has been awarded a position on Support Area 3 (SA3): Cloud, Infrastructure, & Cybersecurity Services of the General Services Administration (GSA) Technology Transformation Services (TTS) Next-Generation IT Solutions (NEXT) Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA). This five-year BPA, awarded under the GSA Alliant 3 Governmentwide Acquisition Contract, has no ceiling value across its life. This positions Trace to compete for orders supporting the delivery of mission-critical technology solutions and modernization of IT infrastructure across the Federal Government.

The NEXT BPA provides TTS with streamlined access to innovative, secure, and scalable IT solutions. Under SA3, Trace will have the opportunity to deliver cloud and infrastructure modernization services, including cloud migration and optimization, enterprise architecture, network and systems engineering, cybersecurity, Zero Trust implementation, continuous monitoring, vulnerability management, and secure infrastructure operations. These capabilities will enable federal agencies to modernize legacy environments, strengthen cybersecurity postures, and improve the reliability and performance of critical technology systems.

"This award is an important step in Trace's continued growth and expands our ability to bring proven technical capabilities to a broader range of federal customers," said Stacey McGill, Vice President of Enterprise Solutions. "We look forward to supporting the acceleration of technological modernization and delivering solutions that enable Government agencies to operate more effectively."

This award is underpinned by Trace's proven expertise in provisioning secure infrastructure, cloud technologies, and cybersecurity services for Government customers. It cements Trace's position as one of the first contractors to receive an award on Alliant 3.

From orbit to edge, Trace builds the backbone for tomorrow's defense enterprise.
