Dynasty Warriors 3: Complete Edition Remastered Gets A New Switch 2 Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Includes additional functionality, adjustments and bug fixes

These past few months have been packed with many exciting game releases, and one title that might be worth taking another look at is Dynasty Warriors: Complete Edition Remastered.

Koei Tecmo rolled out a new update for the title earlier this week on the Switch 2 and other platforms. Version 1.1.0 features additional functionality (including a new language and the ability set two-player controls), adjustments and multiple bug fixes.

Here are the details via the official Koei Tecmo website:

This follows last month's release, which also included the release of Version 1.0.1 and 1.0.2. These updates included some bug fixes. In case you missed it, there's also a Switch 2 eShop demo available for this title.
