I gotta say, I am loving the Increased Class Exp boon. Quicker class mastery allows for early access to class exclusive abilities, which you can then experiment its synergy with other classes and abilities.

Lysander for example is my favorite creation with the most rewarding experience. First, I look at his personal skill, Racing Attack, which grants Avoid +15 when initiating combat. The Speed stat is what determines the Avoid rate, so I checked to see what Advanced Class grants him high Speed growth. Having completed Dietrich's path in Part I, I had access to the Ranger class boosting Lysander's Speed growth rate to 65. With the Ranger class in mind, I invested Lysander in archery while learning abilities that enhanced Avoid rate. I gave him the refined Tempest Bow+1 for passive Avo +10 and +3 when initiating battle, the In Shadow ability for one turn Avo +10 after defeating a foe, Dodge for Avo +5 against magic/bow users, and Regroup for Avo +20 when a foe avoids an attack. I thought Regroup would be useful since Lysander's accuracy averages around 80-90%, but in practice it was hardly ever used in combat nor necessary when foes couldn't counterattack in the first place. I could've given him the Phantom Ring accessory for Avo +5, but I wasn't sure if adding +3 Weight would make it worthwhile. However, the most unexpected bonus is Lysander's secondary exclusive ability, Accelerate, which grants extra speed for every kill. More speed means better avoid rate (and Attack Speed (AS)). As of now, Lysander has an initial Avoid value of 60. Combined with his abilities, he's nearly untouchable when foes struggle to hit him with their average hit rate of 10-30%.

My second favorite experiment is Theodora. She has naturally good growth rates on HP, Strength, and Defense and her personal skill Royal Resolve halves all damages when activated. It would seem natural for her to pursue armored classes, until I realized something about her Blaze Art. Their range. Her second Blaze Art has an attack range of seven. This reminded me of the Ballistician class back in Fire Emblem: Shadow Dragon, with their absurdly long-range attacks but abysmal movement. When I promoted Theodora to an Armored Knight, her low movement made her struggle to get involved in the thick of battle and low Resistance made magic users a threat to her. At first, I reclassed Theodora to the Wing Soldier class to boost her Resistance, but I also thought it would be funny if she could become a flying artillery. Wait. That actually sounds like a good idea. I also realized in battle that Theodora fills the niche of a bulky flying unit. Having completed Cai's path in Part I, I have access to the Dragoon class. I also gave her the Brace ability after mastering the Guardian class beforehand. Finally, as a Dragoon she can move across the map wherever she please. Combining high movement with long range attacks is a thing of beauty. She's basically a flying tank capable of neutralizing hard to reach targets. She could also charge right into enemy ranks after achieving Overblaze status, aggro a crowd of enemies towards her, and then dish out lethal counterattacks which would only boost her defense even more thanks to her Brace ability.
