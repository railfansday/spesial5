Street Fighter 6 'Winter Pocket Present' Fighting Pass Adds Super Gem Fighter Mini Mix

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Capcom continues to roll out regular updates for Street Fighter 6, and it's now lifted the lid on its "Winter Pocket Present" Fighting Pass, arriving this week.

This new pass is themed around the 1997 arcade chibi-style title Super Gem Fighter Mini Mix (aka Pocket Fighter) and includes all sorts of rewards inspired by this classic game.

This includes more EX-Colors, new titles, more music, seasonal avatar gear, more challenger screen illustrations, new emotes & stickers, and the retro title, which you can access via the title's arcade section. Here's a look at everything included and you can see it all in motion in the video above.

This isn't the first time Super Gem Fighter has been revived. Apart from being playable in Street Fighter 6 via the game's Fighting Pass (made up of free and premium tiers), it can also be accessed in the Capcom Fighting Collection, which is available for Switch. It's also in the collection series Capcom Arcade 2nd Stadium.
