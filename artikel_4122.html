New Zelda: Ocarina of Time Mod Brings Immersive VR Mode to PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Greg has more than than 10 years of experience in video game journalism, covering some of the biggest games on the 8th and 9th generations of console.

Less than three weeks after Nintendo launched its highly-anticipated The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake on Nintendo Switch 2, PC users are working hard to prove why they’re the master race by transforming the game entirely with an immersive new VR mode.

The mod, known as OcarinaVR, was developed by ReTracedGames and builds upon the previously released PC port. It is showing the true skills of the PC modding community, and at what speed they can work to develop player experiences beyond a game’s original console boundaries.

One Zelda fan just reminded everyone we never really forget their trickshot skills.

The power of the OcarinaVR mod is quite the sight to behold. It goes beyond just being a camera trick, and instead shifts the entire game into a true first-person perspective with accurate spatial scaling—meaning the world adjusts depending on whether the player is controlling young Link or the grown-up version.

The mod also brings intricate control mechanics and hand tracking, allowing players to map and control Link’s arms and individual fingers using their VR controllers. Swinging the Master sword and bashing enemies with the Hylian shield never looks so satisfying and adds a sense of depth and realism to the game beyond just its graphics.

OcarinaVR also manages UI elements and prevents clutter, displaying HUD readouts like heart count and the minimap as floating panels. Pre-rendered cutscenes are displayed on a curved virtual cinema screen for maximum immersion as the perspective shifts back to the 3rd person.

OcarinaVR … goes beyond just being a camera trick, and instead shifts the entire game into a true first-person perspective.

The mod is based on the Hookshot PC recompilation that was released on PC shortly after Ocarina of Time launched on Nintendo Switch 2 on November 5. That release already brought native widescreen and uncapped framerates, meaning it was a solid foundation for the OcarinaVR mod to build off.

Fan response to the Ocarina of Time remake has been monumental, with players praising its highly-detailed graphics and faithful retelling of the classic Zelda tale. Critics loved it equally as much, giving it one of the highest ratings on Metacritic of all time—a solid 95/100 aggregated score.

In DualShockers’ own review, Tayna Garcia wrote that the remake gives returning players an “opportunity to experience those same feelings again” from their childhoods, powered by nostalgia, but said it's a perfect place for newcomers to start their Zelda journey, too.

Fan response to the Ocarina of Time remake has been monumental [and] critics loved it equally as much.

The game was swiftly followed by Rockstar Games’ Grand Theft Auto 6—perhaps the most hyped game of all time (and the highest reviewed on Metacritic with a stunning score of 99). Nintendo knew to get Zelda out the door before the news cycle and social media turned to GTA 6 for who knows how long.

The new VR mod gives fans a chance to take on Ocarina of Time in a whole new way, snatching a little bit of attention back from the Vice City-drenched mediascape right now.
