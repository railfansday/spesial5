Cody Gakpo is Andoni Iraola's secret weapon ahead of Liverpool's clash against Manchester United on Sunday.

The 27-year-old has a superb record against the Red Devils, and will hope to add to it at Anfield.

But it is no guarantee that the Netherlands international is selected, with the competition on both wings intense this season.

Iraola will almost certainly deploy Bradley Barcola on the left of the attack, but it remains to be seen if Gakpo will be his pick for the right with strong competition from Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz.

Sunday's game will be significant for both bragging rights and for Liverpool to kick off their winter fixture list with victory, but for another reason, too.

Mauricio Pochettino will be taking charge of Man United for the first time at Anfield, and the Reds will hope to treat him to a baptism of fire.

The first time Gakpo ever came across Man United was during the 2022-23 season, just two months after he had arrived in the Premier League.

The winger opened the scoring that day, and would add the third goal in what turned into a 7-0 romping of Erik ten Hag's team at Anfield.

He has netted three goals since against the Red Devils, with all of them coming in the last three fixtures between the pair.

Surprisingly, Liverpool have not won any of those games despite the clear gap between the sides in the Premier League table, and they will hope to put that right on Sunday.

But it remains to be seen if Gakpo plays at all, with his position under threat from two of the next generation.

Both Munoz and Ngumoha have enjoyed strong seasons, and bring a youthful energy to the right side of the pitch.

Yet Gakpo's record and experience make him an equally strong option, and ultimately the way to go for Iraola.

It has been an interesting few months for Gakpo, who went close to joining Manchester City in the final days of the transfer window.

He ultimately stayed and started the season excellently, before tailing off in recent weeks following an injury.

Gakpo has retained Iraola's support, and Sunday's game could well be one to jumpstart him back into form.

"I would probably say Cody Gakpo," Iraola said last month when asked about Liverpool's most underrated player.

"He’s a player who has been putting up numbers, playing and performing for many years, and sometimes he isn’t valued because he’s just there and you take him for granted. His consistency, his personality. I would probably go with Cody."
