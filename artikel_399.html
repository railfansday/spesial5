The Best Early Black Friday MacBook Deals (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

It’s been a tremendous year for MacBooks. Between the launch of the MacBook Neo and the OLED MacBook Pro, there’s never been such a wide variety of Apple laptops to choose from.

Heading into Black Friday, there are already some really great deals on some of the best MacBooks you can buy.

For more WIRED-approved shopping recommendations, check out the Best iPad Black Friday Deals, the Best Apple Black Friday Deals, the Best Black Friday Laptop Deals, and the Best Absolute Best Black Friday Deals.

The M5 MacBook Air is the laptop most people should buy, and it's been on sale all week. The exact price, however, has been a bit volatile. It was down to $989 for most of the day on Monday, which was $310 off its regular $1,299 price. This was the first time the MacBook Air has dropped below $1,000 since the June price hike. Unfortunately, it reverted back up to $1,049 on Monday evening. This is still a solid deal for what you get though: 16 GB of RAM, 512 GB of storage, and all the other Mac hardware excellence we’ve come to expect. Fingers crossed that we'll see the price down to again, and I'll be continuing to check the price throughout the week.

While it’s true we may see an M6 refresh in the MacBook Air sometime in the first quarter of 2027, I wouldn’t wait. From my testing of the M6 MacBook Pro, I don’t think it’ll be worth the extra $300 when the M6 MacBook Air eventually lands, especially since we're not expecting anything beyond a minor chip bump this time around. The M5 is still ahead of the competition in performance and can handle heavier workflows and applications than you might assume.

I’ll also note that the larger 15-inch model is on sale right now. It’s down 20 percent to $1,199, which is also a new low for this laptop. This discount has been holding steady. The 15-inch MacBook Air is identical to the smaller model in most ways, including display quality, ports, and performance. Aside from the dramatic difference in screen size, the 15-inch MacBook Air’s only other advantage is its speakers. They are loud, clear, and full of bass—and a big upgrade over the 13-inch MacBook Air.

The MacBook Neo is Apple’s first true entry-level laptop, and unfortunately, it’s only gotten more expensive since it was announced. The Neo was first announced starting at $599. That price rose to $699 in June and has largely held steady since. But finally, Amazon has started selling it for $50 off, bringing it down to $649.

Now, choosing between this and the MacBook Air can be tricky. Read my full comparison for all the details, but I would only buy the Neo for a student or perhaps someone with more limited computing needs. Put it this way: If you sit in front of a computer all day for work, it’s worth springing for the MacBook Air for the extra memory and performance alone. You’ll also appreciate the faster ports and storage, backlit keyboard, better speakers, and Touch ID.

The 14-inch MacBook Pro is currently the only MacBook in the lineup to get the latest M6 chip. That doesn’t mean it’s the most powerful MacBook Pro, though, as the M5 Pro and M5 Max are still the options you want for true Pro-level performance. For now, the M6 MacBook Pro beats the M5 MacBook Air, but the M6 may arrive in the Air in a few months, so why pay more for the M6 MacBook Pro now? Well, it’s for someone who wants the premium experience of a brighter, HDR-ready screen. It also offers more storage, more ports, and broader external display support than the Air.

And while this deal isn’t a massive cut, it’s pretty impressive for a newly launched MacBook Pro. It’s only $30 more than the M5 MacBook Pro at this point.

MacBook Pro M5 Max for $3,599 ($500 Off): This is also a previous-gen MacBook Pro, but the upgrade to the M5 Max gives you significantly better GPU performance.
