Kohls is giving away free Kohls Cash and Sephora coupons on Black Friday — how to claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

TL;DR: Kohl's is giving the first 200 shoppers at each store a free $5 to $100 Kohl's Cash voucher on Nov. 27 (at 5 a.m.) and a $5 to $100 Sephora coupon on Nov. 28.

If you are looking to score free spending money for your holiday shopping run, Kohl's is giving early birds two big reasons to set an alarm this Black Friday weekend. For the first time ever, the retailer is pairing its classic Black Friday morning Kohl's Cash giveaway with a second wave of free Sephora at Kohl's coupons on Saturday, Nov. 28 — plus a four-day sweepstakes with $10,000 cash grand prizes.

Kohl's stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day (Nov. 26), though its main Black Friday Week sale is already live online. When physical stores reopen for the weekend, each giveaway comes with its own timing and same-day redemption rules, so here is how to claim yours.

Most Kohl's locations open bright and early at 5 a.m. local time on Black Friday, Nov. 27, though exact hours can vary by store. The first 200 customers through the doors at each participating Kohl's location will receive a free, instant Kohl's Cash voucher worth $5 to $100 — no purchase necessary.

However, you have to spend the promotional voucher in stores on that same day, and standard exclusions apply (it cannot be used on Sephora at Kohl's merchandise or gift cards).

If you would rather sleep past 5 a.m. on Friday — or you are mainly shopping for beauty gifts — Kohl's is running a second in-store giveaway on Saturday, Nov. 28. For the first time, the first 200 shoppers in line at participating Sephora at Kohl's locations will get an instant promotional coupon worth between $5 and $100 off at Sephora at Kohl's.

Just like Friday's doorbuster, the Sephora coupon is handed out on a first-come, first-served basis when doors open (limit one per household for shoppers 18 and older) and must be redeemed toward in-store beauty purchases that same day. Be sure to check your local Kohl's store page before heading out, since Saturday opening hours differ from Friday's 5 a.m. doorbuster schedule and not every Kohl's houses a Sephora shop.

Even if you skip the early-morning lines altogether, you can still enter Kohl's Black Friday sweepstakes from 5 a.m. ET on Friday, Nov. 27 through 11:59 p.m. ET on Monday, Nov. 30. You can submit a free entry either in stores or online — no purchase required — as long as you are 18 or older and live in the U.S. (limit one entry per person).

Before setting your alarm for Friday or Saturday, double-check your local store's exact opening time — and if you are mapping out a bigger morning route, check out our guide to Black Friday 2026 store hours to see when other major retailers open their doors.
