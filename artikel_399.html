Magic: The Gatherings Star Trek Bundle is close to its record-low price on Amazon — save $20

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

TL;DR: The Magic: The Gathering Star Trek Bundle is available to preorder for $49.99 at Amazon, down from its $69.99 list price.

With Black Friday just a week away, Amazon has given Magic: The Gathering's upcoming Star Trek Bundle a sizeable markdown — the perfect time to secure your preorder before the nine-pack box’s release date on Nov. 27.

As of Nov. 20, the Magic: The Gathering Star Trek Bundle can be preordered for $49.99 at Amazon, knocking $20 off its $69.99 list price. With that 29% discount, you’ll now only be paying $5.55 per booster before counting the added accessories. Mashable has confirmed that this is only 4 cents off its lowest-ever price.

Rather than through a third-party seller, this is sold and shipped directly by Amazon, with its preorder price guarantee included. That means you'll get the lowest Amazon price offered before release, although all sales are final.

At this price, you’ll be paying $8.83 above the box’s estimated $41.16 market price on TCGplayer.

The MTG Star Trek bundle contains nine titular Play Boosters from the expansion, with 14 trading cards in each. The Bundle packs in 30 Lands, an alternate-art Traditional Foil promo card, an oversized Spindown life counter, two reference cards, and a storage box.
