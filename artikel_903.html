Doyen Quest and Blow Your Wad re-oppose in Ascot's 1965 Chase

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

In Saturday's feature race, Blow Your Wad will attempt to gain revenge over old rival Doyen Quest in the 1965 Chase at Ascot; we also have competitive action from Lingfield Park and Newcastle live on Sky Sports Racing from 11am

We have a busy day's racing on Saturday with action from Ascot, Lingfield Park and Newcastle, live on Sky Sports Racing...

Doyen Quest and Blow Your Wad headline a classy field for the Ladbrokes 1965 Chase at Ascot.

Dan Skelton's Doyen Quest outpointed Blow Your Wad when claiming the Grade Two Oaksey Chase at Sandown on his final start last season but could only manage third of three on his return at Carlisle. With that run behind him he ought to be sharper here.

Blow Your Wad should handle this quicker ground and, having progressed through the grades last season, warrants respect as he receives weight under Freddie Mitchell.

Sam Thomas fields Steel Ally who is three from four over fences with his sole defeat coming in the Arkle at Cheltenham in March. He goes well fresh and looks to have the most scope to improve.

Classy hurdlers Lucky Place and Kateira clash in a trappy edition of the Grade Two Ladbrokes Ascot Hurdle.

Lucky Place took this race in 2024 and, having tried fencing, he reverted to hurdles with a few solid runs including when runner-up in the Grade Two Select Hurdle at Sandown. Third behind Alexei in the Molyneux at Aintree on his reappearance, this extra distance should be more to his liking.

The Dan Skelton-trained Kateira looks the obvious danger. She has a bit to find on the figures but produced an excellent effort to beat off La Conquiere at Wetherby and deserves a crack at this valuable prize.

Harry Fry's Altobelli was last seen finishing runner-up behind Potters Charm at Windsor in January and would have claims on the pick of his form.
