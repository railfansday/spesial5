Confirmed 2. Bundesliga Lineups: Eintracht Braunschweig vs. Energie Cottbus

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

A relegation six-pointer will take place in Braunschweig before the international break as newly-promoted Energie Cottbus aim for their first win since matchday 5. The hosts did win their most recent game at Eintracht-Stadion against Lower Saxony rivals Hannover, but they also have only ten points after the first ten matchdays of the 2. Bundesliga season.
