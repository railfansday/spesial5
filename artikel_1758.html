Club Brugge 0-3 Liverpool: Alexander Isak at the double as Reds cruise in Belgium

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Alexander Isak and Florian Wirtz continued their upward trajectory with the goals in a 3-0 victory over Club Brugge which returned Liverpool to winning ways in the Champions League and got them back on track for a top-eight finish.

Isak took his tally to 10 for the season with a double, his second a wonderful solo effort after shrugging off the attentions of giant centre-back Brandon Mechele to chip over the goalkeeper, while his fellow £100million-plus signing produced a goal equally exquisite.

The pair were the visitors’ best performers in a display which began well, lost its way in the middle, before finishing strongly with two expertly-taken strikes.

After defeat at Fenerbahce last time out it was imperative they responded against the Belgian champions, who had already beaten Inter Milan and PSV Eindhoven, and it was almost a textbook European away performance as they secured the points which lifted them into fifth.

Isak scored for the third successive game after 13 minutes and it was reward for pressuring goalkeeper Yann Sommer.

The Sweden international forced Sommer to misplace a pass which was picked up by Bradley Barcola.

He transferred the ball inside for Dominik Szoboszlai, captaining the team for the first time in the absence of Van Dijk, to cut back for his team-mate to poke home.

Isak was doing a good job of linking play, combining well with Wirtz especially, providing the first line of defence and was also a goal threat, with a snapshot wide from Barcola’s knockdown.

He looks more threatening and seems to have more understanding of Andoni Iraola’s gameplan by the day, but his new-found resilience will be tested to the full as it seems unthinkable he will not make his third start in a week against Everton.

The form of Wirtz, who scored his sixth goal in five matches for club and country, will also be a concern for the Toffees as the Germany international is brimming with confidence, highlighted by his goal.

When he received Rio Ngumoha’s pass 30 yards out he had only one thought in his mind: take a touch and shoot and he deceived Sommer by rattling a shot in off the goalkeeper’s right-hand post when it was easier to go the other side.

It was a classy finish to a game which had become a grind after the Reds failed to capitalise on their early advantage with Felix Lemarechal and Joaquin Seys having chances.

An injury to Victor Munoz was arguably worse for the hosts as his replacement was Sunday’s man of the match Ngumoha, who early in the second half wriggled into enough space to flash a close-range shot across goal.

Liverpool lost their fluency, although Wirtz and Isak did tee up Szoboszlai for what looked a certain goal before Sommer parried, and after Alisson responded in kind to deny Lemarechal at his near post, Wirtz and Isak delivered the killer blows.
