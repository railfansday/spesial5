Mauricio Pochettino warned £47m Man Utd star will push for transfer exit: ‘Not the future’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Glenn Murray believes Bruno Fernandes will look to leave the club if he doesn’t sign a new Manchester United contract before the January transfer window.

Fernandes won the PFA Players’ Player of the Year last year after a superb second half of the season under Michael Carrick, but has not hit those same heights so far this campaign.

The Red Devils captain was also dropped for a game after publicly criticising Carrick’s tactics following a heavy Champions League defeat to Como.

The arrival of Mauricio Pochettino represents a fresh start for Fernandes this season, but there remains uncertainty over his long-term future at the club given the 32-year-old is out of contract at the end of the season.

Fernandes has already turned down two contract offers from the club, and former Brighton striker Murray believes he could be eyeing up a move away from Old Trafford when the transfer window reopens in January.

‘I wouldn’t burst the bank [on a new contract] for him,’ Murray told Sky Sports. ‘I think he’s not the future of the football club.

‘Listen, he’s given his best years to Manchester United and unfortunately they haven’t won an awful lot, which he would have liked.

‘I think if he gets the option to move on, and it gets to January, and he can start speaking to other people, I think it will open his eyes up to see where he could go and if he could win things and finish off his career more enjoyably, because there have been years at Manchester United that have been really difficult.

‘He has been the standout player, but he is not the future and it could be time to part ways.

Pochettino, meanwhile, has confirmed that Fernandes will remain captain under his management and insisted the midfielder is an ‘important’ player for his rebuild.

‘All the players, we were chatting, it was amazing from day one, Bruno is our captain, an important player,’ Pochettino confirmed.

‘But all the players need to think they are important, from the bottom to the top.

‘I spent three hours, chatting in my office, with all the players, maybe I miss some injured players. In the next few days, we will talk in an individual way with all of them.

‘It’s important to listen to what they need to say, understand the place they are. Not only on the field, off the field, what’s going on. Some conversations, some collective way, meetings, but focus is on Sunday.’
