Supermicro's Sales Quadruple as Cash Drains: Rapid Earnings Reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Record sales, thin margins, and a cash problem that's growing with the business.

Super Micro Computer (SMCI +3.12%) reported fiscal first-quarter sales of $19.9 billion, nearly four times the $5.0 billion of a year ago. Shares rose about 6% in early after-hours trading. The AI server maker earned $1.6 billion but still burned cash, and its full-year outlook points to a slower second half.

Supermicro is winning volume at thin prices. Gross margin fell nearly 5 points from last quarter. The release doesn't explain the drop, but its forward-looking statements note that a small number of large customers could mean lower margins. Profit still jumped because operating expenses grew far slower than sales, up 67% against a 297% sales increase.

The cash story is weaker. Receivables and inventory soaked up about $6 billion this quarter. Customer deposits offset about half of that. The company still burned cash overall.

Growth also looks set to flatten. At the midpoint, the full-year sales outlook implies second-half quarters averaging about $18 billion, below this quarter's pace. Next quarter's GAAP EPS guidance is centered at $1.71, below this quarter's $1.75.

Watch whether operating cash flow turns positive as these receivables come due. Also check that the final quarterly filing matches, since the release calls these figures preliminary and unaudited.
