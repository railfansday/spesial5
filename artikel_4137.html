Pokemon's Next Life-Size Plush Is Riolu

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Some of Pokemon’s most popular (and most expensive) plushes are its life-size ones. Plush replicas of Pokemon designed to match the exact specifications in the games. They usually cost a lot of money, but sell out pretty fast, which is why a lot of Pokemon fans are going to be left disappointed by the latest life-size reveal.

The latest Pokemon to be getting the life-size treatment is Riolu. If its dimensions are accurate, which they should be as that’s the entire point of these plushes, then the new Riolu toy will be a little over two feet tall. Not one of Pokemon’s bigger life-size plushes, but that means it will cost quite a bit less.

Give me a life-size Tepig to sit and watch me while I play Legends: Z-A.

That’s the good news. The bad news is that, for some reason, according to Pokejungle, the Riolu plush is only going to be available in China. Exclusive to the Pokemon Center website in China and releasing on November 20, with no indication of it being available elsewhere, not even in Pokemon’s home country of Japan.

This is the part where I reassure you that the plush will eventually be released elsewhere, or, at worst, there are ways to get the plush shipped to you if you’re willing to pay significantly extra. That’s what usually happens when Pokemon merch is released in Japan before getting a worldwide rollout. In China, that doesn’t tend to be the case.

In 2025, life-size plushes of Ekans and Serperior were released in China to celebrate the Year of the Snake. As far as I can tell, those plushes have never been made available anywhere else. While still frustrating, at least in that instance the plushes were tied to a Chinese tradition. We are currently in the Year of the Horse and a few months out from the Year of the Goat. I’m fairly certain Riolu is neither of those.

Riolu is a dog Pokemon. The next Year of the Dog isn't until 2030.

There is hope for those of you with the life-size Lucario (that got a worldwide release) in need of its pre-evolved buddy. Razer’s Gengar collection was originally exclusive to China, and although it took a while, it was eventually made available worldwide. Make enough noise, and perhaps The Pokemon Company will do the same and eventually free Riolu from its region-locked prison.
