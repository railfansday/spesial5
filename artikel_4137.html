How To Build All Apples To Applin Event Habitats In Pokemon Pokopia

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Applin, Flapple, Appletun, Dipplin, and Hydrapple have finally been added to Pokemon Pokopia!

Pokemon Pokopia will soon release the second part of its Expansion Pass, Dazzling Decorations. However, to tide players over in the meantime, a new event is bringing five new Pokemon to Pokopia. This fall event, Apples to Applin, is available for a limited time and adds Applin and the rest of its unique evolution tree.

As with most events, players will be required to complete a specific task to collect an item that can then be traded in the Pokemon Center for the necessary furniture to build event habitats. In this case, players will be looking for Syrupy Apples to trade with Applin.

To unlock Applin, Appletun, Flapple, Dipplin, and Hydrapple, players will need to log into Pokemon Pokopia at least once during November 16 at 5:00 a.m. – December 1 at 4:59 a.m. local time. You will need to be connected to the internet the first time you open the event, so if you've been time-jumping, you may want to sync your clock when you first open up the game.

As soon as you start, you'll see Applin hanging out around the Pokemon Center. After speaking with Applin, you need to gather Syrupy Apples to trade Applin inside the Pokemon Center for several event items. The event items are required to build the other Pokemon's habitats, so you should aim to grab at least one of everything before the event ends.

One of the items for sale will be Syrupy Apple Tree seeds. I recommend grabbing as many of these as you can the first day and planting them around so you don't have to keep visiting Dream Islands.

If you missed part of the initial conversation with Applin, one thing he'll tell you is that you can find Syrupy Apples on Dream Islands. Now, these should appear on almost any Dream Island, but I would avoid using plushes like the Starmie Doll, with drastically different Dream Island environments. I used a Pikachu Doll and managed to find 10–15 Syrupy Apple Trees per Dream Island.

If you haven't visited a Dream Island in a while and have forgotten how, all you need to do is track down either Drifloon or Drifblim and show them any Pokemon Doll in the game. However, each doll has its own island environment and materials. So, try to pick a more common doll that's likely to have plenty of trees, such as Pikachu or Eevee.

Once you're on the Dream Island, you should see plenty of Syrupy Apple Trees. Knock them down and collect as many as you can before heading back. If you use some of these to buy seeds from Applin on day one, you should be able to plant enough trees and finish the event without visiting another Dream Island.

After logging in on the first day to trigger the event, you can time jump to visit more Dream Islands or grow your Syrupy Apple Trees. That said, while there are currently no known consequences for doing this, this is always at your own risk.

After you've collected all the items, built the habitats, and welcomed Applin and its evolutions, the event is complete. Per usual, Applin will remain in the Pokemon Center until the end of the event, after which you'll find it outside the Pokemon Center and can guide it to its new home.
