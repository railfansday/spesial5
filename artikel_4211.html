Civilization Developer Firaxis Lays Off A Third Of Its Staff

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Firaxis, the studio behind the Civilization series, has laid off around 60 employees, representing a third of its staff. The report comes from Bloomberg’s Jason Schreier.

In a statement provided to Bloomberg by a spokesperson from 2K, a subsidiary of Firaxis' owner, Take-Two, “Firaxis is restructuring and recentering around the studio’s core strength—designing world class strategy games that have set the industry standard for the genre for the last 30 years. As part of these changes, the size of the studio is being reduced to focus on design, technology and the player experience, which means saying goodbye to talented colleagues who helped build Firaxis. These decisions are never easy, but they’re essential to build the next chapter of Firaxis.”

In addition to the 60 affected staffers, Firaxis studio head Heather Hazen is also departing. Bloomberg also reports, via anonymous Firaxis employees speaking to the outlet, that the studio is working on an unannounced XCOM game that has had a challenging development, with the team recently missing an important development milestone. This project, if it releases, will be the first Firaxis-developed XCOM game since XCOM: Chimera Squad in 2020.

Firaxis’ most recent release is Civilization VII, which launched in February 2025 to favorable reviews but initially "slow" sales, according to Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick last August.  The game currently has a Mixed reception on Steam after Firaxis spent the last year updating the game to address player complaints. Before Civilization VII, Firaxis shipped Marvel's Midnight Suns in 2022, another critically well-received game that failed to meet sales expectations.
