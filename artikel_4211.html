Tanggal: 2026-11-12

These are all the correct responses to the Tomasso Guard Captain regarding the Melon Theft in Harvest of Greed.

Crimson Desert primarily focuses on Kliff’s combat prowess, but there are a handful of opportunities for you to play Sherlock and help investigate local crimes. One such example comes from the Harvest of Greed questline, which you’ll access later in the game once you travel to the northeastern area of Pywel.

When you reach the City of Tomasso, you’ll find a local guard captain who needs your help finding a local thief who has stolen some valuable Desert Melons, an essential source of water for the people in this small city. We’ll walk you through what to say to the guard captain during the Harvest of Greed.
