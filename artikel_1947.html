Mikel Arteta has Arsenal stars looking as hungry as ever with rest of season predicted

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal hold a five-point lead at the top of the Premier League table.

A 3-0 loss at the Amex Stadium against Brighton was the only one of their ten matches so far this season in which they have dropped points in the league.

With 10 points from a possible 12 in the Champions League as well, it has been near enough as good a start as Mikel Arteta and his players could have wished for.

The defeat away at Brighton was a horror show and the Carabao Cup defeat to Fleetwood was embarrassing, but otherwise it was a superb start to the season for Arsenal.

They have, on the whole, played better football than last season, even if many matches have not had the margin to make it feel more comfortable.

Arsenal went toe-to-toe with Bayern Munich at the Allianz Arena, a performance that should give them real confidence when the knockout stages of the Champions League come around.

It has been a tough run of fixtures to start the season, with so many difficult away trips already ticked off, and Arsenal have come through that sitting pretty.

Ezri Konsa has been by far the standout of the summer additions. He has slotted into the defence seamlessly and made an already formidable backline look effectively impenetrable.

There can be no greater compliment to Konsa than the fact he has given Arteta a major selection headache now William Saliba is fit again.

Bruno Guimaraes' best moments have come off the bench, with goals against Sunderland and Leeds. He has thrived when things have been more desperate and there is a chaotic edge to the match.

When starting alongside Declan Rice, Guimaraes has looked far less comfortable and his lack of mobility has been exposed on several occasions.

Further up the pitch, Christos Tzolis made a bright start to life at the club before missing a month with a hamstring injury.

It is still hard not to look at the summer transfer window as a huge missed opportunity in attack. Arsenal needed to do much more.

Konsa aside, it feels like the form this season is in spite of the business done in that window rather than because of it.

He did not get the statement attacking signing he wanted in the summer but Mikel Arteta has not let that slow Arsenal down.

After the mental and physical toll last season took on the squad, followed by many of them spending the summer at the World Cup, there was a strong chance that Arsenal could have stumbled into the new season.

That has not happened. Arteta has got his players looking as hungry as ever and the start to the league campaign has been brilliant.

The one criticism of Arteta would still be how he manages certain players. He has rotated this season, particularly in Europe, but it still feels as though unnecessary risks are sometimes taken.

Did Declan Rice need to play 90 minutes against Slavia Prague, for example? Gabriel, too, is surely in need of a rest soon.

I backed Arsenal to defend their crown in the summer and see no reason to change that stance now.

The defence has got even better and this is a squad remarkable in its depth, capable of dealing with injuries in key positions.

With the uncertainty surrounding Manchester City, and the likes of Chelsea and Liverpool looking extremely unconvincing, Arsenal are by far the club in the best shape to win the title again.
