Mauricio Pochettino has made his first big demand as Manchester United manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Mauricio Pochettino insists that he wants to be involved in "all the main decisions" at Manchester United.

Pochettino has been appointed as "head coach" of Man Utd, a role he held at both Tottenham and Chelsea, rather than be known as the first-team manager.

The main difference between the two job titles is often considered level of input or control of decisions beyond tactics and team selection.

Mikel Arteta has most notably been promoted from head coach to manager during his seven years at Arsenal, and is widely understood to have a say in almost every key aspect of the club's running.

Pochettino is seemingly not asking for such a level of input, but as the January transfer window approached he has made it clear that he wants a greater say on his players in the future.

"I was in all my career a coach, head coach, manager," he told reporters on Friday. "It does not change too much.

"I know as head coach I am the guy who tomorrow will decide the squad for Sunday, pick the 11, make the changes.

"I know in the future I would like to be involved in all the main decisions that affect the squad. At the end of the day, Manchester United is a football club."

Pochettino kicks off his Man Utd reign at Liverpool on Sunday.
