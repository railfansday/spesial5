'I'm amazed' - ex-Refs chief can't believe 'wrong' Kai Havertz decision in Arsenal win vs Hull

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The decision to ask referee Farai Hallam to go to the screen to check a penalty in Arsenal's 1-0 win over Hull City was an unnecessary one.

That is according to former head of PGMOL and FIFA referee, Keith Hackett, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The Gunners ultimately won the game courtesy of Martin Odegaard's second half strike, after a first half defined by one odd moment.

VAR Michael Oliver asked Hallam to stop the game, sending him to the pitch-side monitor to look at an incident of shirt-pulling between Nobel Mendy and Kai Havertz.

But as he looked, it became clear that the German had been in an offside position when the offence took place.

Hallam announced exactly to the crowd, who appeared, understandably, bemused by the decision.

While Mendy's shirt tug on Havertz was a clear foul, there were other facets for the referee to consider.

The German had never been getting to the ball, with it having deflected well away from him.

And on further review, it became clear that, when the shirt pull took place, Havertz had been in an offside position.

Hassam was sent to the monitor anyway, and explained that to the crowd after coming away.

"It is clear that offside is the first offence and therefore the game should restart with an indirect free kick to the defending team," Hackett told Football Insider.

"I'm amazed that the offside consideration was not looked at immediately. The correct decision, albeit the wrong way to get to it!"

Interestingly, an argument that Arsenal should have had a penalty for the incident involving Havertz has emerged.

Made clear by BBC Sport correspondent, Dale Johnson, the German must be attempting to play the ball or challenging for the ball to be considered to have committed an offside offence.

Havertz did no such thing, with the foul coming before any other consideration. Whether that rule is appropriate is a different debate - Arsenal may well feel hard done by.

Had Odegaard not found the net in the second half, referees may have had plenty to answer for.
