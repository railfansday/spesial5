Tottenham XI vs Sunderland: Predicted lineup, confirmed team news, injury latest for Premier League

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Sandro Tonali has returned to training ahead of Tottenham’s visit to Sunderland, but Savio is expected to spend a significant period on the sidelines after suffering the recurrence of a hamstring injury.

Tonali missed Spurs’ 2-0 victory at home to Ipswich last weekend due to illness, but is set to be available for selection when Roberto De Zerbi’s side travel to the Stadium of Light on Sunday.

Savio, meanwhile, was named on the bench against Ipswich, having missed the previous four games. The 22-year-old was introduced in the second half, but lasted just 26 minutes before he had to come off injured.

Savio was visibly upset as he headed down the tunnel, and De Zerbi confirmed at his pre-Sunderland press conference on Friday that the Brazilian would miss the festive period.

Asked if Savio may not play again this year, De Zerbi said: “Yeah, I think [he will miss the Christmas period].

“Unfortunately it’s a tough situation to accept. He’s a new guy and he’s suffering with these new injuries. But this is football, if you see other European clubs they’re all suffering these injuries.”

De Zerbi added: “Now he needs time. He needs hard work with the medical staff. Then in 50 days, 60 days, 80 days, I don’t know, when he comes back, he will be crucial for us in the last step of the season.”

Savio joins fellow Tottenham wingers Mathys Tel and Mykhailo Mudryk on the sidelines, meaning teenage sensation Luca Williams-Barnett, who scored and assisted on his full senior debut against Ipswich before being handed a new contract, could be in line for more minutes.

Fears over an injury to Omar Marmoush remain despite the forward appearing in training footage posted across Spurs’ social media channels on Thursday. The Egyptian was this week subject of online speculation that he required assessment over a possible knee problem.

Centre-back Tosin Adarabioyo remains a doubt despite returning to training following a calf injury, while Lucas Bergvall will be assessed having suffered from illness this week.
