XRP Exchange Inflows Hit 4.61 Billion in 30 Days

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

XRP has continued to see traders deposit large amounts of tokens into exchanges, including Binance, Bybit and others, as the prolonged market volatility has triggered increased fear across the broader crypto market.

Latest data from the crypto analytics platform CryptoQuant shows that about 4.61 billion XRP have flowed into crypto exchanges over the last 30 days.

According to the data, Binance has led all other crypto exchanges in the amount of XRP inflows received over the last month as its users appear to have panicked amid rising market struggles.

As such, Binance recorded about 1.221 billion XRP deposits during the period, representing the lion's share of the total inflows.

Ranking second on the list is OKX, which followed with about 866.51 million XRP, while Bybit was next with 662.57 million XRP.

The trend extended to other exchanges, even though they saw considerably lower inflows: Upbit saw about 176.96 million XRP flow into its exchange during the same period, while Bitget recorded inflows of about 140.02 million XRP.

Although the past month has seen billions of XRP transferred back to exchanges, further data shows that the 4.6 billion XRP inflow is substantially lower when compared with inflows seen in October.

In October, the total XRP that flowed into exchanges throughout the month was estimated at around 6 billion XRP, suggesting that XRP selling pressure has eased compared with the previous month.

This has drawn attention across the crypto market as the price of XRP suffered larger losses this month compared with last month, sparking questions about whether investor confidence is recovering.
