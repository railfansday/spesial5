GTA 6 boss says physical discs will be the vinyl records of gaming eventually

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick says physical games could become the video game equivalent of vinyl records, even as GTA 6 prepares to launch without a disc.

Rockstar’s sequel arrives on November 19, and although players can still buy a boxed copy from a retailer, the case only contains a download code, so they’ll have to download the game just like anyone buying it through their console’s store.

It’s a change some GTA fans have already pushed back against, as many fans hoped for an actual disc.

Now, Zelnick has explained Take-Two’s approach to physical media, confirming that it plans to keep it around, but it will only make up a tiny sliver of the business.

With digital now accounting for 98% of Take-Two’s business, The Game Business asked Zelnick whether GTA 6’s launch marked the end of discs for the publisher.

“It may not be the end,” Zelnick said. “I predicted that digital distribution would be well over 90% of the business more than 10 years ago. I don’t always get it right, but this one I got right.”

“Will there always be some physical business? Yes, in the same way that there’s still a vinyl business in recorded music. But it’s going to be a very small part of the business.”

Zelnick had already hinted at what that could look like for Take-Two. In August, he said discs “don’t really make sense” for some big games because players often have to connect to the internet anyway. He still expected the company to put out physical editions from time to time.

That leaves the door open for future Take-Two games to get a disc release. For GTA 6, the boxed edition with a download code is the option on shelves at launch, and Zelnick gave no update on whether a disc would follow.

This comes as PlayStation prepares to make a similar move, as Sony has confirmed it will stop producing discs for new games from January 2028.
