Taco Bell’s launches new Baja Blast alongside Naked Chicken Chalupa return

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Taco Bell is expanding its drink lineup with a new zero-sugar Mountain Dew flavor while preparing to bring back one of its most recognizable menu items.

The fast food chain officially launched Mountain Dew Baja Midnight Zero Sugar nationwide on November 12, offering customers a new version of its passionfruit-flavored fountain drink.

The release comes alongside plans to bring the Naked Chicken Chalupa back to restaurants across the US.

The returning Chalupa, which replaces a traditional shell with crispy chicken, has appeared on Taco Bell’s menu several times since its 2017 debut, including on the chain’s 2016 Decades menu in August 2026.

Mountain Dew Baja Midnight Zero Sugar features the same passionfruit-forward flavor as the original Baja Midnight, but without sugar or calories.

The new beverage is available nationwide in three sizes, with a small priced at $2.59, a medium at $2.79, and a large at $2.99.

Meanwhile, the Naked Chicken Chalupa is preparing for another nationwide release, bringing back its signature shell made entirely from crispy, all-white-meat chicken marinated in Mexican-inspired spices.

Rather than using a traditional fried flatbread shell, the chicken itself holds the Chalupa’s fillings, which include shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, cheddar cheese, and creamy Avocado Ranch Sauce.

The returning menu item will be priced at $5.99, although Taco Bell has not provided a specific nationwide release date in the announcement.

Both additions continue Taco Bell’s ongoing menu updates, with Baja Midnight Zero Sugar already available at participating restaurants and the Naked Chicken Chalupa expected to follow.
