Rockstar made a real-life GTA 6 Vice City supercar you can actually buy for $1M

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Rockstar Games has teamed up with West Coast Customs to bring an iconic GTA 6 supercar into the real world, and fans can actually buy it for a staggering £1 million.

With Grand Theft Auto 6 finally launching on November 19, Rockstar is going all out with its marketing, including a massive takeover of luxury British department store Selfridges.

The collaboration features an exclusive collection of GTA 6 merchandise, but the biggest attraction is a real-life Grotti Cheetah, one of the franchise’s most recognizable sports cars.

Built in collaboration with West Coast Customs, the retro supercar features a striking pink-and-blue paint job inspired by Vice City.

According to Selfridges’ official website, the vehicle is available to purchase for £1 million, putting its price well above $1 million USD.

Interested buyers will need to contact Selfridges Personal Shopping directly rather than simply adding the car to their online shopping cart.

The Grotti Cheetah isn’t just a promotional shell, either. According to Hypebeast, the vehicle features a 4.9-liter naturally aspirated V8 engine, lowered suspension, track-ready tires, and custom-stitched Italian leather upholstery.

It’s also one of six GTA 6 vehicles recreated by West Coast Customs, alongside the Progen Emerus, Invetero Coquette, Albany Buccaneer, Albany Manana, and Pegassi Tempesta.

The car is being showcased at Selfridges’ London location as part of a Vice City-themed pop-up running from November 9 through November 28.

The GTA 6 collection also includes more than 60 products, ranging from Burger Shot uniforms and Vice City sports merchandise to basketballs, hats, and accessories.

Some of the prices are almost as outrageous as the supercar itself. An exclusive Goodtime State Varsity Jacket costs £2,500, approximately $3,355, while a separate GTA 6 varsity jacket is priced at £1,750.

The expensive merchandise follows Rockstar’s reveal of a $400 GTA 6 Collector’s Edition that doesn’t even include the game.

Demand for official merchandise has already been intense, with some GTA 6 fans reselling promotional items for hundreds of dollars as excitement builds ahead of the game’s release.

Still, the Grotti Cheetah takes things to another level. For anyone who has ever dreamed of owning a car straight out of Vice City, Rockstar has made it possible, provided you’ve got a spare million pounds lying around.
