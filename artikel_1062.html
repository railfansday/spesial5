Brighton underline title credentials with impressive win over Man City

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

This Brighton and Hove Albion title challenge – as it must now be classed – flies in the face of the saying that money always talks in football. Manchester City have been found guilty of breaching financial rules and also fielded the most expensive midfield in Premier League history here, yet the match-winners cost a fraction in comparison.

Looking at Brighton’s winning goal, it was created by a cross that flashed across the City goal from Diego Gómez, an £11m signing who was playing in Major League Soccer in the United States not so long ago. Maxim de Cuyper, a £17.5m arrival from Club Brugge, was credited with the assist after Yasin Ayari tapped in.

Ayari cost £3.5m from Swedish club AIK and scored in the 5-0 wins over Coventry and Palace, and now has this winner to add to his collection. The 23-year-old is another example of Brighton polishing these gems and putting them together in a team that can win against any opponent in the Premier League.

It could keep them at the top end of the table for a good while longer. They have already shown they can defeat Arsenal in a one-off match and they look to have the ingredients to stay the pace with them. In Fabian Hürzeler they have one of the brightest young coaches in the Premier League and he has a squad that are all on the same page.

Leicester upset the odds 10 years ago with a settled team, while this Brighton team has a deeper squad. When the substitutes came on in the second half, they slotted into a well-rehearsed system.

“We faced adversity. There was a little bit of luck but in the end they deserved it because they worked hard for it,” said Hürzeler. “I want players who want to compete, to dominate. They all have the winning mentality inside of them. These kinds of performances are not about one or two players, everyone has to stick together.”

Hürzeler has played down a title challenge but his team have been among the most impressive in the first part of the season. The German coach, 33, has been in English football for two years but has learnt quickly the tried-and-tested response of taking each game as it comes. Next is Getafe in the Europa Conference League on Thursday.

While Hürzeler was reluctant to name individual performers, Dutch goalkeeper Bart Verbruggen was another key player in this win. He kept out Iliman Ndiaye and repelled Erling Haaland, who lost a physical battle with Lewis Dunk. “You’re going down with the Palace,” chanted the jubilant Brighton fans, with City now losing two Premier League games in a row.

City head coach Enzo Maresca will be content with the number of chances his team created but there must be concerns at the other end. He tried a change of formula, dropping Marc Guéhi to the bench for the first time this season, yet his team were still prone to individual errors.

“We are the second team in scoring in the Premier League. We are scoring goals but in this moment we are conceding silly goals. We can avoid that,” Maresca said. His team have conceded 10 goals in their last six games, which is not the foundation of a title challenge, even if City have been stacking up points.

Maresca picked a midfield that cost £327m to recruit this summer, with Elliot Anderson, Enzo Fernández and Ayyoub Bouaddi picked together for the first time. There were some signs they could all play together, although City looked more dangerous when Rayan Cherki came off the bench to break up the trio.

Pascal Gross cost a nominal fee when he returned to Brighton but his hard work and intelligent passing means Brighton can control periods of the game even against the very best.

The goal came when Brighton switched play from a free-kick, although City thought Anderson was victim rather than perpetrator. Former Inter Miami winger Gómez provided the cross that opened up City, with Gianluigi Donnarumma guilty of not coming out to claim the ball, then Matheus Nunes slipped at the vital moment.

“Unfortunately it is happening for our players but I think it is part of the game, part of football, it happens many times against us, we need to analyse and understand what we can do better,” Maresca added. City also felt Dunk used his arm to block the ball in the penalty area, although no spot-kick was given.

It was a sweet goal by Ayari, who had missed a penalty in the shoot-out last week when the teams played in the EFL Cup. Brighton stayed in Manchester after the defeat, masterminding a win that means they have now defeated all of the traditional heavyweight teams in the Premier League.

“I wasn’t aware of that. For me it’s more important, not about history, it’s about giving something back to the supporters. They’ve spent a lot of time and money,” Hürzeler said.

“We’ll take it game by game. See what happens because we have loads of games. Just focussing every day on what’s ahead of us and the job we have. We will continue to do that and see where it leaves us at the end of the season. We are happy with the way we are playing - not just the results but the way we are playing too. We have to continue to develop that style and ruthlessness and we can do great things.”
