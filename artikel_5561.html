Cartwheel Becomes ReceiveUp, Helping Staffing Companies Get Paid Faster

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Accounts receivable automation platform introduces new brand ahead of the SIA Healthcare Staffing Summit

DALLAS, Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Cartwheel, the accounts receivable automation platform built for the staffing industry, today announced its rebrand as ReceiveUp. The new name reflects the company's focus on helping staffing agencies, payroll funding companies and employers of record (EORs) get paid accurately and faster.

The rebrand does not change the company's product, team or existing customer agreements.

ReceiveUp automates the full accounts receivable cycle for staffing firms, from invoice creation and delivery to collections, payments and cash application. It connects to the staffing and accounting systems teams already use, so finance staff spend less time moving data between systems and more time managing cash flow.

"Our name should tell people what we do," said Mani Themadathil, founder and CEO of ReceiveUp. "For years, the first question we heard was 'What does Cartwheel do?' ReceiveUp answers that on its own. We help staffing companies get paid. As we invest more in billing, collections and cash application, we wanted a name that matches the work."

"Staffing firms make payroll every week while they wait weeks or months to collect from their clients," said Laura Boivin, VP of Client Partnerships at ReceiveUp. "Closing that gap is why we exist. The new name puts it front and center in every conversation we have with agencies."
