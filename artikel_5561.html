EIB dismissed U.S. objections to its record €3 billion Airbus loan

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

EIB President Nadia Calvino says the bank gave the European Commission all relevant information on the loan

European Investment Bank President Nadia Calviño dismissed U.S. criticism of the bank’s €3 billion ($3.4 billion) loan to Airbus on Monday, arguing that the administration has no basis to challenge it. She made the remarks in an interview with Bloomberg Television.

Calviño said the loan supports a major market participant as it funds its strategic investment program.

"I was very surprised that the U.S. authorities were trying to suggest there was something questionable in this loan," she told Bloomberg Television. "It is absolutely plain vanilla for the European Investment Bank."

Calviño said the EIB has supplied every piece of pertinent information via the European Commission, which she described as a "competent authority" on trade policy.

"I am quite sure this will clarify all matters and this will be put to rest because there's really no grounds for this question," she said, according to Bloomberg.

The loan is intended to bolster Airbus' technology across both defense and commercial aviation, according to Bloomberg. It ranks as the largest funding deal the EIB has signed off on for a company.

In October, U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer requested further information about the loan, according to Bloomberg. Greer warned that the loan put the transatlantic trade relationship at risk, and said he wanted a quick resolution.

Airbus has defended the loan, describing it as one of several funding avenues it can draw on, according to Bloomberg. Chief Financial Officer Thomas Toepfer called the loan "completely at market rates" in July, according to Bloomberg.

"It's part of our policy how we want to finance the company, with a buffer when this flexibility might be needed," Toepfer said at the time, according to Bloomberg.
