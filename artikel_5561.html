Anthropic is planning a live-streamed IPO event with Amodei and Dimon

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The chat is expected around lunchtime Friday in New York, as Anthropic markets an IPO with an expected price of $70 to $78 a share

Anthropic is lining up a livestream in which CEO Dario Amodei and JPMorgan $JPM Chase CEO Jamie Dimon will talk about the company's initial public offering, according to Bloomberg.

The conversation is expected around lunchtime Friday in New York, according to Bloomberg. It would resemble, but be less extravagant than, the June event with Elon Musk that was part of SpaceX's $86.2 billion listing. Representatives for Anthropic and JPMorgan declined to comment.

Anthropic launched the roadshow for its proposed IPO on Tuesday. The offering consists of 825 million shares of Class A common stock. Anthropic is selling 801,351,354 of them, and certain existing stockholders are selling 23,648,646. Anthropic will receive no proceeds from the stockholders' sales.

The company expects an IPO price between $70 and $78 a share. It has applied to list its Class A stock on the Nasdaq $NDAQ Global Select Market under the symbol "ANT." A registration statement has been filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission but has not become effective, and the securities cannot be sold until it does, the company said.

Pricing and the Nasdaq debut are expected before the U.S. Thanksgiving holiday, according to Bloomberg.

JPMorgan is among the 22 banks involved in the offering, and it is handling retail marketing in the U.S. and the distribution of shares to those buyers, according to Bloomberg. Anthropic has considered setting aside roughly 15% to 20% of the shares on offer for retail buyers. Citigroup $C is running the retail offering outside the U.S., which spans the U.K., Canada, Japan, Australia and parts of Europe.

Anthropic filed an amended registration statement on Tuesday saying AMD $AMD, Broadcom $AVGO, Google $GOOGL, Micron $MU, Microsoft $MSFT, Nvidia $NVDA and TSMC $TSM agreed to a $26.0 billion purchase of its Class N common stock. The purchase is a private placement priced at the IPO price and is contingent on the IPO being completed and on other conditions.

Anthropic's most recent funding round valued the company at $965 billion.
