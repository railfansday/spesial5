Eric Trump-backed robot startup missed payroll after CEO borrowed $4.6M

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Workers had not been paid as of Nov. 14, and CEO Sankaet Pathak is seeking funding at a $3 billion valuation, according to CNBC

Foundation Future Industries, a humanoid robot maker backed by Eric Trump, missed payroll on Oct. 30, and employees had not been paid as of Nov. 14, according to CNBC. The San Francisco-based startup's cash reserves have dwindled, CNBC said.

Some employees are angry because CEO and co-founder Sankaet Pathak took a $4.6 million loan from the firm in 2025 to pay for a house, according to CNBC. An entity for which Pathak is the lone listed principal paid $4.6 million for a Sausalito, California, property in October 2025, according to public records cited by CNBC.

Pathak said in a memo to employees last week that he was trying to line up funds to cover the unpaid salaries, according to CNBC. A spokesman for Pathak confirmed getting an outline of the story's key findings but did not reply to requests for comment, CNBC said.

Foundation also lacked the money to cover payroll in August, according to CNBC. Employees went without pay for roughly two days that month, until a current backer extended the company a bridge loan.

Pathak has been trying since at least January to bring in hundreds of millions of dollars, with the company valued at $3 billion, according to CNBC. Insiders are concerned that the company may go under unless fresh capital arrives, CNBC said.

Pathak is the company's sole board member and owns most of its shares, according to CNBC. That structure has left prospective investors uneasy about how the company is governed. Pathak described the $4.6 million as a temporary advance when he took it, and current investors learned of it from the company's financial filings.

Eric Trump was introduced as a Foundation investor and chief strategy adviser in an April television interview, according to CNBC. The size of his stake is unclear. He did not respond to CNBC’s requests for comment.

A spokesperson for Eric Trump has said he had "no involvement in Foundation's management, operations, strategic decisions or commercial activities," according to CNBC.
