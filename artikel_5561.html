What The Telecom Bust Taught Me About AI’s Debt Boom

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The AI buildout’s growing reliance on debt echoes the telecom bubble, raising the risk that infrastructure spending will outpace the revenue needed to meet fixed repayment obligations. According to guest author David Spreng of Runway Growth Capital, even if AI fulfills its technological promise, borrowers’ survival will depend on realistic demand forecasts and debt tied to dependable cash flows, with missed timelines potentially forcing restructurings and bankruptcies.
