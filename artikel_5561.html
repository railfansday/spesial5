Microsoft 365 outage stretches into a second day with Outlook and Teams disrupted

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Microsoft is working to restore service as the outage disrupts government offices, traders and businesses in more than 70 countries

Microsoft $MSFT said users may be unable to connect to multiple Microsoft 365 services, as problems with Outlook, Teams and the Copilot AI tool dragged on for a second straight day Friday, according to Bloomberg.

Microsoft said administrators may be unable to reach the admin center for service health information, so it posted details on its public status page. The page lists both Microsoft 365 for business and enterprise customers and Microsoft consumer products as experiencing service degradation.

The problems began Thursday afternoon, according to Bloomberg. Microsoft has posted updates almost every hour as it tries to shift users onto servers that are still functioning. "A core process on the affected servers is becoming overwhelmed, which causes the servers to stop responding and contributes to the capacity loss," the company said, according to Bloomberg. Microsoft said it has found nothing to suggest the disruption was malicious.

In a Friday status update, Microsoft said service was beginning to recover in Asia and that its next priority was bringing the same improvement to other regions, according to Bloomberg. Few earlier disruptions at Microsoft have stretched beyond a few hours.

Microsoft stock slid roughly 2.1% before the market opened on Friday.

Users spread across over 70 countries said they had trouble using Microsoft products, according to Bloomberg. Security software, services for sharing files and tools used by administrators also suffered interruptions.

The number of people affected is unknown, according to The Associated Press, which put the ceiling at 430 million, the number of people Microsoft says use Microsoft 365 apps.

Richemont and Workhorse Group postponed earnings releases this week because of the outage, according to Bloomberg. Richemont has filed its report.

The U.K.’s National Health Service pushed back certain procedures because the outage hampered its communications, according to Bloomberg. Several police forces in the U.K. said they were unable to accept online reports of crimes, according to Bloomberg, which cited The Guardian.

Japan’s Finance Ministry said no incoming email reached it through much of the day, according to Bloomberg. Government ministries there put up printed announcements to let reporters know the day’s schedules. Euronext cautioned that it could not be reached by email.

At the Reserve Bank of Australia, Outlook was unavailable across departments, though essential functions continued as usual, according to Bloomberg. New Zealand immigration officials halted work on every visa application.
