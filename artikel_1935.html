Double trouble for Koppen’s recruitment plan ahead of January window | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

A failed transfer bid from the summer had looked to be back on West Ham’s radar for Nils Koppen to revisit when the window opens for business in the next few weeks: Transfer talks in August between West Ham and Nürnburg proved fruitless – despite the Hammers reported £17 million bid – for striker Mohamed Ali Zoma.

The player was said to be hugely keen on the move but his club refused to sanction the deal that would have brought the 20-goal Bundesliga 2 talent to West Ham.

The Hammers settled on Joel Piroe instead, but were apparently hankering after another crack at signing Ali Zoma in January.

However there seems another fly in the ointment which could well prove a new stumbling block. According to West Ham World, the Hammers seem not destined to get their man this time around either:  Mohamed Ali Zoma now has a fitness issue which his manager claims will rule him out for the rest of this year.

Their article: “Miroslav Klose hands West Ham transfer blow” describes the shoulder injury sustained by the striker which more or less rules him out of West Ham’s thinking for the winter window.

Coming hot on the heels of yesterday’s report claiming Rangers’ Ryan Naderi wasn’t interested on a move to London Stadium it is clear that Koppen has his work cut out to bring a new front man to Scott Parker’s squad. Sliding towards mid table in the Championship will not make his task any easier so there will be added incentive for the Irons to start looking like genuine title contenders once again in what remains of this year in order to attract the best crop of talent in the transfer window ahead.
