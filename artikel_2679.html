San Francisco Mayor Daniel Lurie debuts custom Valkyries shirt during victory parade

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Mayor Daniel Lurie had his own fan fashion moment at the Golden State Valkyries' victory parade in San Francisco.

Standing next to the team's small forward, Gabby Williams, the politician sported a Valkyries-violet button-down shirt-jacket with "46" emblazoned on the back - a nod to his role as the city's 46th mayor - during the celebration along the Embarcadero on Wednesday, Nov. 4.

The shirt was made by fan apparel website Dannijo Pro and bears the inscription "Custom 1/Won For Daniel Lurie #46 'Ballhalla,'" according to a representative from the Mayor's office. Lurie purchased the shirt himself.

Oxfords from the company cost between $215 to nearly $600, although Lurie's exact violet shirt is not on the site.

This Valkyries championship season has seen a variety of memorable looks among the WNBA team's fanbase. Many loyal followers treat games as an occasion to dress up beyond usual sports loyalties, with sequins, fringe and rhinestones making jerseys sparkle. Some fans have even shown up in mythological-themed costumes with horned helmets in homage to the team name.

Lurie's look on Wednesday put him in the company of city mayors across the United States who have donned their local team's gear after major victories. In June, New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani and First Lady Rama Duwaji drew widespread attention for their custom New York Knicks apparel during the team's 2026 NBA championship celebration.

Prior to Wednesday's parade, while Lurie is not generally known for wearing team threads at games, he's been a sartorial supporter of the Valkyries, declaring Sept. 25 "Violet Day" and encouraging residents to wear shades of purple on game days.

He has also spotlighted local merchandise made during Super Bowl LX, which was hosted at Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara in February, and was gifted a custom Team USA soccer jersey for San Francisco's official "Jersey Day" on July 1.
