3 NFL teams that should be worried after Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

We have officially entered the second half of the NFL season, and the 10th weekend of football has left a few teams wondering about their future. Vibes were high for a few teams around the league after the trade deadline passed on Tuesday, as players now knew where they would be playing for the rest of this season.

A handful of teams pushed their chips into the middle of the table, declaring that they are in it to win it in 2026. Some other teams didn't make major moves despite their winning records, and three of them really struggled this week, leaving many to wonder just what the rest of this campaign has in store for them. Let's take a quick look at each of these three teams and why they may be in trouble as we head towards week 11.

Oct 4, 2026; East Rutherford, New Jersey, USA; New York Giants head coach John Harbaugh before the game at MetLife Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Vincent Carchietta-Imagn Images Credit: IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect (Reuters)

The loss to the Commanders was a tough one for New York, as there isn't one specific reason why they fell to their division rivals on Thursday night. Sure, some people are taking issue with how their backup quarterback tried to draw the Commanders' defense offsides, but this team didn't lose this game because Jameis Winston yelled: "Who let the dogs out???!!". The larger issue here is the fact that the Giants just aren't very good despite their 5-3-1 record.

Losing Jaxson Dart and Brian Burns certainly has affected both sides of the ball, but even with a full roster, this team isn't ready to compete at a championship level quite yet. This season isn't over because of one loss, but we have to temper our expectations for this team. The NFC East will likely still come down to week 17, when these Giants travel to Dallas to take on the Cowboys and it still feels like 9 wins will be more than enough to win this division.

Sep 27, 2026; Jacksonville, Florida, USA; New England Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel stands on the sidelines during the second quarter against the Jacksonville Jaguars at EverBank Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Melina Myers-Imagn Images Credit: IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect (Reuters)

The last of the international series games featured a matchup between the Pats and the Lions in Munich, Germany. As much fun as it was to wake up early to watch two of the biggest meathead coaches in the league match wits for 3+ hours, the game turned out to be a snoozefest. The Lions rolled over the Pats for the easy win, but the bigger loss for New England was the combo of Drake Maye and AJ Brown (yet again).

The Pats could have survived the loss of Brown, but having to pivot from Drake Maye to Tommy Devito in the 1st quarter turned winning this game into a Herculean task that Mike Vrabel's squad just wasn't ready for. The Pats are now 5-5 and have dropped down to 4 games behind the Bills in the AFC East. If Maye is going to miss extended time, this very well could be a lost season for the Pats. Luckily, they have their bye next week, so their young QB will have an extra week to heal.

Oct 4, 2026; Houston, Texas, USA; Houston Texans head coach DeMeco Ryans runs onto the field prior to a game against the Dallas Cowboys at Reliant Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Thomas Shea-Imagn Images Credit: IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect (Reuters)

They've been on this list a few times this season, but despite their slow start, they still had a very good chance at making the postseason. Well, those chances are next to zero now that they lost this Sunday to the Cleveland Browns. The AFC may very well send a 9-win team to the playoffs as the final wildcard team. If you consider the Bills, Broncos, and Chiefs, Bengals and Steelers, and Jaguars all as odds-on favorites to make the postseason, that leaves just one spot left for teams like the Patriots and Browns. Ravens and Texans (and Raiders if you are a Fernando Mendoza believer).

Houston needed this win against Cleveland badly, and losing after blowing a double-digit lead leaves them in dire straits for this season. Not only do they now sit 2 games behind Cleveland in the loss column, but they also now lose the tiebreaker, which makes them essentially 3 games behind with 8 games remaining. They basically need to finish the season with at least 6 wins in those 8 games, and they will need some help from the Pats and Browns. It's a steep climb for sure.
