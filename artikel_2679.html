WATCH: Bengals retake lead on MNF with Colbie Young’s first NFL TD

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Monday Night Football is starting to turn into the offensive showdown most people were expecting. After the Washington Commanders tied it up at 10-10, the Cincinnati Bengals responded by reclaiming a seven-point lead on an eight-play, 66-yard touchdown drive.

Cincinnati capped off their touchdown drive with a four-yard touchdown pass to Bengals fourth-round rookie receiver Colbie Young. It was Young's first NFL touchdown and just his third reception.

Washington has yet to have a lead in this game, although they have stayed competitive so far. The Bengals' largest lead of the night was by 10 points in the second quarter. But the Commanders responded with a touchdown just before halftime and a field goal on their opening drive. After everyone in their division won over the weekend, the Commanders desperately need a win to have a chance of winning the NFC East and getting back in the playoffs.
