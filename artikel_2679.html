Week 12 Fantasy Football Start/Sit Advice: What to do with Chase Brown, Mike Evans, more players

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Start-sit decisions can be vexing for even the best of fantasy football managers. If you need help setting your fantasy football lineups, Scott Pianowski offers some assistance with his Week 12 traffic report.

RB Cam Skattebo at Colts: It's been speculated that the New York coaches aren't enamored with Skattebo, but sometimes you have to love the one you're with. Skattebo has collected 56 touches over the last three weeks, good for 291 total yards, and he's scored a couple of touchdowns. The Colts have the second-worst run defense, according to DVOA.

RB Ashton Jeanty at Browns: Jeanty looked like one of the busts of the year not long ago, but the Raiders have finally unlocked him, making use of Jeanty's skills outside the numbers. He's had more receiving yards than rushing yards in seven of his last eight starts, and look for that trend to continue at Cleveland. So long as your league uses some form of PPR scoring, Jeanty gets the checkmark.

RB Ollie Gordon II vs. Jets: When the Dolphins play a one-score game, Gordon is basically a lock for 15-plus touches. The Jets aren't likely to blow Miami out here — the Dolphins have been better on defense than expected — and New York has allowed the ninth-most fantasy points to opposing backs. Look for Gordon to break his touchdown slump as well.

TE T.J. Hockenson vs. Falcons: He missed the first Kirk Cousins reunion game, but Hockenson and Cousins were in sync last week, with Hockenson posting a 6-55-0 line on seven targets. That's a decent return in what's been a messy year for tight ends, and Hockenson's role looks secure in Week 12, with Jordan Addison (thumb) expected to be out for another week.

WR Antonio Williams at Cardinals: He finally broke his touchdown drought in Week 10, and then it was a 9-122-0 splash against Cincinnati on Monday. Jayden Daniels has started to warm up as well, and Arizona is a funnel defense to exploit (31st against the pass, per DVOA, and eighth against the rush).

WR Mike Evans vs. Seahawks: To roster Evans is to be tethered to your phone, nervously scrolling through the latest injury news. But if Evans (Achilles) can go Sunday, you start him, no matter that he's physically compromised and up against the nasty Seattle defense. The parameters were similar last week against Minnesota, and Evans smashed with a 6-109-1 performance.

QB Deshaun Watson vs. Raiders: If injuries or underperformance have you considering a QB streamer, Watson makes sense in this spot. He's been over 300 passing yards in three straight games and he's also starting to run proactively, with 49 yards last week and two ground scores in three games. Even if Watson himself doesn't play great, it's possible his talented pass-catching teammates can drag him to relevance.

WR Jordyn Tyson at Bengals: It's been a curious stop-and-start for Tyson in recent weeks — he saw 13 targets and 74% of the snaps two weeks back, then fell to five targets and 50% of snaps last week. With Tyler Shough in poor form and the Saints the NFL's worst first-quarter team, Tyson is a reactive, not proactive, option for Week 12.

QB Anthony Richardson Sr. vs. Giants: Understand that Richardson has zero floor, as he's made just 15 pro starts and several of them have been messy. He's completed just 50.6% of his passes and he's thrown more picks than touchdown passes. But rushing production from a quarterback is nectar of the fantasy gods, and maybe Richardson will outrun his mistakes Sunday.

RB Chase Brown vs. Saints: He's stuck on one rushing touchdown for the year and Samaje Perine's role seems to grow weekly. And it's not like Brown is Cincinnati's clock-killing guy — he hasn't seen more than 13 carries since Week 2. Brown's summer ADP is a sunk cost; we need to focus on his current production level as we make start-sit decisions.

RB Tony Pollard at Jaguars: Pollard has been a rushing star in four of his last five games, but the Jaguars were the one team to solve him (9-33-0 on the ground). The Titans did lean on Pollard as a pass-catcher in that game, but given that Pollard saw no receiving work last week, it's tricky to rely on that backboard. Pollard moves down to the RB3/flex bucket for Week 12, not an automatic fantasy punch.

WR Ladd McConkey vs. Patriots: If Justin Herbert (concussion) is cleared to start, you can give McConkey a play against a slumping New England secondary. But if the Chargers have to lean on backup QB DJ Uiagalelei (seven career passes), the entire LAC passing game goes back on the restricted list.
