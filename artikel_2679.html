Big second half fuels comeback for No. 7 Texas over Saint Mary’s in 76-73 win

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The No. 7 Texas Longhorns are champions of the Rady Children's Invitational after overcoming an eight-point halftime deficit to beat the Saint Mary's Gaels 76-73 behind a bounce-back performance by junior center Matas Vokietaitis and a huge second half from sophomore guard Isaiah Johnson.

After fouling out in just 13 minutes on Thursday against Georgetown, Vokietaitis was a force against Saint Mary's, scoring a career-high 29 points fueled by 17-of-21 shooting from the free-throw line. Vokietaitis added 11 rebounds to finish a team-best plus-18 in his 31 minutes on the court.

While Vokietaitis was steady throughout, Johnson recovered from a poor shooting start to take over the game halfway through the second half, notching 18 of his 21 points after halftime on 6-of-6 shooting while hitting five free throws.

The performances from the two Texas stars helped the Longhorns overcome 1-of-15 shooting from three and first-half shooting struggles from the floor and from the line.

Vokietaitis made an early impact in the pick and roll, sending in an alley-oop layup on the first possession and then earning a trip to the foul line on the second possession.

Meanwhile, Saint Mary's tried to take advantage of the hard hedge ball-screen coverage employed by Texas this season by throwing skip passes, producing an early corner three and a paint score with that approach.

A tough second effort by Vokietaitis in the paint earned another bucket and junior forward David Punch scored on a second-chance opportunity to tie the game at 7-7 at the under-16 timeout, caused by a whistle against Vokietaitis for a sloppy hedge.

Over the ensuing stretch, Saint Mary's started to heat up from three, making three in a row, the last of which caused Texas head coach Sean Miller to call a timeout in frustration when sophomore guard Amari Evans allowed a clean look by helping off of sharpshooter Jeremy Dent-Smith.

On offense, the Horns bookended an 8-0 run by the Gaels with a lob dunk by Punch and a slashing slam by junior wing Elyjah Freeman on cut from the corner. After that, Texas missed five straight shots before freshman guard Austin Goosby tossed a short jumper far enough over the rim to rattle home.

It wasn't enough to keep the Longhorns from entering a danger zone as the Gaels extended the lead to nine points with threes continuing to fall for Saint Mary's and Texas mired in the midst of a 1-of-8 shooting stretch. Punch finally ended it with a high-low layup against a fronting defender, earning a trip to the foul line after the under-eight timeout, but it came after freshman guard Bo Odgen was beat off the bounce for a layup that gave the Gaels their first double-digit lead.

At that media break, Texas was 0-of-5 shooting from three while Saint Mary's was 5-of-9 from distance.

With the Gaels back up by 11, Vokietaitis drew a flagrant foul against Vangelis Zougris, hitting both free throws, but sophomore guard Isaiah Johnson had a three-point attempt blocked on the extra possession afforded by the flagrant foul. Back at the line, Vokietaitis converted two more free throws. Johnson earned his own trip with a late-clock drive, but missed the first before making the second.

From the field, the misses continued, including a bad decision by Spears to attempt a three prior to committing an offensive foul. After a paint basket by Zougris, Saint Mary's went into the under-four timeout leading 33-23.

Texas was missing opportunities at the line, too, as Johnson struggled to a 1-of-8 shooting start, including four misses from three. The first 12 threes for the Longhorns misfired — even freshman guard Joe Sterling missed three, sending up an airball and failing to connect just before the halftime buzzer, maintaining the 35-27 lead by Saint Mary's at the break.

From the floor, Texas shot just 29 percent and wasn't any better at the free-throw line, hitting only 52.9 percent. Vokietaitis was 8-of-12 shooting from the line, but his teammates missed four out of their five attempts.

At the start of the second half, the Horns kept drawing fouls, including the fourth on Zougris, with a three-point play by Vokietaitis cutting the deficit to six points. Both teams played even for two minutes after that with Saint Mary's leading 43-37 at the under-16 timeout.

Texas started to cut further into the deficit when Vokietaitis slammed home a lob from freshman guard Bo Odgen and got to the line, hitting both to cut the Saint Mary's lead to three points with help from a defense that held the Gaels to 4-of-16 shooting to start the half. At the under-12 timeout, Saint Mary's was up by five points.

After review, an offensive foul on Spears for a high elbow trying to create post position was upgraded to a flagrant, but forcing a turnover limited the possession to two points for the Gaels.

With giveaways plaguing the Texas offense, Miller had to use a timeout at the 8:28 mark when Goosby helped too far off Mantas Juzenas, who hit a big three to go back up by nine points. It was the first triple by Saint Mary's since 9:38 remaining in the first half.

By the under-eight timeout, the Gael still held a 53-46 lead.

Texas cut into that deficit when a poor inbounds pass produced a breakaway layup by Johnson, who scored again in the lane to respond to a long push shot by Zougris. Getting downhill, Johnson continued to take over with a physical drive and layup before working behind a screen from Vokietaitis to nail a three, and Freeman came up with a steal and slam for the lead, forcing a timeout by Saint Mary's.

Deep post position by Vokietaitis drew a foul and converted at the line. Skying for a defensive rebound, Freeman finished an effective defensive possession and Johnson asserted himself again, flashing high-level body control in the paint to get free for an easy shot, allowing Texas to go into the final media timeout with a six-point lead.

A baseline cut from Freeman earned a trip to the line and allowed the Longhorns to maintain that six-point advantage. After the Gaels hit two of their own, Johnson's ability to attack the basket in the pick and roll led to two more free throws.

Continuing to effectively run the baseline, Freeman earned a layup with one minute remaining, putting Saint Mary's on the ropes. Breaking pressure after a basket and timeout by the Gaels, Freeman hit two clutch free throws.

Up by five points late, Texas turned the ball over on an inbounds pass, but Joshua Dent missed a floater in the lane and Vokietaitis grabbed a a big board and got fouled, making both free throws with 21.6 seconds remaining.

Up next, Texas travels to Kentucky to face off against No. 25 Louisville on Tuesday in the SEC/ACC Challenge at 8 p.m. Central on ESPN2.
