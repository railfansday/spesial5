T.J. Hockenson not on practice field Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Minnesota Vikings TE T.J. Hockenson (chest, wrist) did not participate in practice Wednesday, Nov. 11.

Why It Matters: Fantasy players will need to keep an eye on the situation to see if Hockenson takes part in practice at some point this week. He has caught four passes in each of his last three games and has two touchdowns during that span. He can be considered a No. 2 fantasy tight end if he plays.
