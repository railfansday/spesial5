JK Dobbins suspended for ‘conduct detrimental’ in mysterious Broncos decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

J.K. Dobbins won't be suiting up against the Steelers on Friday.

Broncos coach Sean Payton was scant with details surrounding the team-imposed one-game ban for his veteran running back.

"I'm suspending J.K. Dobbins for this weekend's game," he told reporters Tuesday. "Conduct detrimental. That's all I'm going to say about it. Coach's decision."

Broncos running back J.K. Dobbins (27) makes a catch against the Carolina Panthers. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

The 27-year-old rusher started the season as the Broncos' No. 1 running back, but has steadily lost time to rookie Jonah Coleman, culminating in Denver's 38-24 win over the Raiders on Sunday in which Dobbins had one rushing attempt for zero yards to Coleman's 111 yards and three touchdowns on 24 runs.

It marked a career-low in attempts for the eighth-year pro.

"We certainly didn't go in with the idea it was just going to be maybe that one dimensional, but that was the way that game unfolded," Payton said Monday, adding that Dobbins is healthy and he's "going to be super important for what we're doing in this stretch."

What happened in the hours between is a mystery, with Dobbins not taking the field for practice on Tuesday.

Earlier in the season, as he ceded touches to Coleman, the Ohio State product became testy with reporters looking for insight.

Broncos running back J.K. Dobbins (27) celebrates a touchdown against the Arizona Cardinals. AP Photo/Rick Scuteri

"You keep calling me a vet. I'm not old. I'm 27 years old. Not old. I'm not 30. I'm not even close to 30," Dobbins said as he fielded questions after a 30-16 win over the Cardinals in Week 7. "So, let's be smart about it. And that's what it was, I believe. I'm not sure; you guys might make something bigger than what it is, but I'm just here to support my teammates and I love my teammates, and I think they love me too. So, there's your answer to that."

On the season, Dobbins leads the Broncos with 324 rushing yards and 99 attempts, adding three scores on the ground with nine catches and 34 yards through the air.
