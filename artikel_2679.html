Aaron Rodgers reunites with Jordan Rodgers

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Aaron Rodgers insists on keeping his personal life private. Except when he decides to take it public.

Before retreating to the position that it’s nobody’s business.

On Friday night, Rodgers reunited with his brother, Jordan. Via TMZ, Aaron Rodgers posted on social media a photo with Jordan on Friday.

Jordan was in Pittsburgh for the game between Florida State and Pitt.

“Great to see little bro @jrodgers11 in his element tonight @acrisurestadium,” Aaron Rodgers said in the caption attached to the photo. “Really proud of how hard he’s worked to get to where he is now.”

When Rodgers and his family first renewed acquaintances earlier this year, Jordan was omitted. It was no surprise; the public rift between the brothers started when Jordan spoke about the situation during his time as a contestant on The Bachelorette.

Here’s a prediction. If a reporter asks Aaron about the photo with Jordan after Sunday night’s game against the Bengals, Aaron will refuse to talk about private matters — despite the fact that he chose to make the matter anything but private with his social-media post.
