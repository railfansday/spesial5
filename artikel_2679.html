A.J. Brown, Amon-Ra St. Brown active for Patriots-Lions in Munich

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Patriots and Lions each listed their top wide receiver as questionable to play in Sunday’s game in Munich and both teams will have their leading wideouts in the lineup.

A.J. Brown is active for New England after missing the last two games with a knee injury. Brown also missed four games with an ankle injury earlier in the season, so this will be just his fourth appearance of the year.

Amon-Ra St. Brown has been dealing with a rib injury, but it would have taken a lot for him to miss this game. St. Brown’s mother is German, he speaks the language and he’s talked often about how much Sunday’s game means to him.

Running back Rhamondre Stevenson is also active for the Patriots. Stevenson has a knee injury that led to him being listed as questionable and his presence is important as the Patriots remain without TreVeyon Henderson.

Safety Brenden Schooler, guard Walter Rouse, offensive tackle Dametrious Crownover, quarterback Behren Morton, guard Ben Brown, and cornerback Carlton Davis are inactive for the Patriots. Cornerback D.J. Reed, center Cade Mays, defensive back Keith Abney, linebacker Amen Ogbongbemiga, defensive lineman Mekhi Wingo, and defensive end Ahmed Hassanein are out for Detroit.
