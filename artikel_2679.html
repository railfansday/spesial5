Payton Pritchard has to work through his struggles

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Payton Pritchard has not yet met his own high standards this year.

His numbers are down across the board. He's dropped from 17 PPG on 46/38/89 splits last season to 13.4 PPG on 41/34/86, having one of the least efficient seasons of his career so far. Saturday's loss to the Kings was another rough night, and it came when Boston needed him most.

Pritchard went 4-for-17 from the field and 0-for-8 from three in his first start of the season. It's his fifth time shooting below 30% in 17 games.

With Jayson Tatum and Derrick White out, the expectation is that Pritchard takes on more of the scoring load. Struggling against Sacramento, which ranks 27th in defensive rating, is unusual. This is a matchup where you'd normally expect a big night. Instead, Boston got dragged into a dogfight that came down to the ball in his hands.

His final two shots showed where things went wrong. He hit a go-ahead jumper with 11 seconds left, then airballed at the buzzer with the game on the line.

With the floor cleared for him, he settled for a heavily contested step-back a foot inside the three-point line instead of attacking the rim. Pritchard didn't defend the shot afterward.

"Didn't get to a good enough spot," Pritchard said. "Probably need to try to find a better look there."

That was the bigger issue all night. Sacramento allows the highest field goal percentage at the rim in the league, a staggering 76.6%. Boston needed rim pressure, and Pritchard took just two shots there.

Even his 8-to-0 assist-to-turnover ratio is a bit misleading. One assist came when he tossed Neemias Queta a grenade late in the clock after failing to create space. There were several other occasions where he missed the roller, making his lack of turnovers more about being overly cautious.

He finished -13, the only Celtics starter in the negative. Plus-minus can be noisy in a single game, but this one matched the eye test. Meanwhile, his new mentor Mike Conley picked up the slack with a season-high 19 points and nearly saved the game.

It hasn't been all bad for Pritchard. But outside of two big games against the Bulls with 30 and 28 points, he hasn't been as advertised. Coming off a career year and a new contract extension, the expectation for him was another leap. So far it's looked closer to regression.

Pritchard didn't hide his own disappointment.

"As many good games as I'ma have, I have bad games, too," Pritchard said. "And tonight was a bad game. So, I look forward to bouncing back."

He usually finds his footing, and a couple of good games in a row can get him there. The Kings looked like a good launch pad. That wasn't the case, but there's a quick turnaround tonight against the Warriors, and a chance to wipe the slate clean

"It can either make you or break you," Pritchard said. "So go back to work and figure it out."
