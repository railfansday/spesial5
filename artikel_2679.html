Cardinals – Rams Week 10 injury reports

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Arizona Cardinals are at the halfway point of the season. They need a win very badly. Where does the team head from here? They are facing the Los Angeles Rams once again. In the first match, Arizona's offense dominated the LA defense, but could not score touchdowns and instead came away with six Chad Ryland field goals.

This time around, there are plenty of changes for the Cardinals. The offense will sport its third starting quarterback of the year in rookie Carson Beck. Center Hjalte Froholdt is in concussion protocol; WR Michael Wilson was added to IR, as was QB Gardner Minshew; and no more Jeremiyah Love, Paris Johnson, or Will Johnson, for that matter.

Thursday's injury reports after both practices for both clubs this week.
