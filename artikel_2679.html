Do Arizona Cardinals fans have a whole lot to be thankful for this year?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Typically it is easy, family, friends, faith.

Yet, for Arizona Cardinals fans it gets really murky when you talk about football.

That's why I ask the question, do we as Cardinals fans have anything to be thankful for this year?

It is easy to pick one thing: Watching Trey McBride play every week.

That has been something to enjoy and be thankful to watch.

It seems like every iteration of the Cardinals has a McBride.

Larry Centers back in the 90's, when fullbacks were still a thing.

It was a little murky after Jake Plummer, but then we got to enjoy a run of Anquan Boldin, Larry Fitzgerald, Patrick Peterson, Tyrann Mathieur, Chandler Jones, Budda Baker, DeAndre Hopkins and now McBride.

Yet outside of McBride and the aging Baker, what are you thankful for from the Arizona Cardinals?
