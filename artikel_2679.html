Deion Sanders, Colorado score huge upset over No. 14 Houston

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

After the loss that dropped the Colorado Buffaloes to 3-6, coach Deion Sanders said, among other things, “The fans deserve better.”

Colorado upset No. 14 Houston on Friday night in Boulder. The 42-32 victory moved the Buffaloes to 4-6.

Houston led 20-7 in the first half. Colorado had taken the lead by halftime, 21-20. Eventually, Colorado scored 28 straight points, taking a 35-20 lead.

For Houston, the loss killed any realistic hope of qualifying for the final 12-team College Football Playoff. For Colorado, the win keeps them alive for bowl eligibility — and possibly saves Sanders from having his fourth year in Colorado be his last.

Especially since he has a $25.5 million buyout.
