Perhaps Bruins-Sharks is a Stanley Cup Finals preview. I wouldn't go recommend betting on that, but it's not impossible. Boston (4-2-0) is starting the California Swing tonight and a four-game roadie overall. It should be No. 1 Jeremy Swayman in net, as this isn't part of a back-to-back. The American has been outstanding at 4-1-0 with a 1.79 GAA, .932 save percentage and a shutout. Swayman's next win will be No. 137 career to pass Andy Moog for the solo eighth-most in team history.

The B's also get a big piece of the lineup back night for the first time this season in All-Star defenseman Charlie McAvoy. He was suspended the first six games for an incident during the first round of last season's playoffs. McAvoy isn't just good on the back end but a huge part of Boston's power-play unit after posting 61 points last season (11 goals, 50 assists) and 23 on the power play. He had a plus-13 rating.

"It was not easy to come out like this, 4-2. We're very happy," said Bruins coach Marco Sturm to the media on Monday. "Doesn't mean we can relax now because Charlie's back. No. Overall, they did a good job [without McAvoy] and now we need to figure out how we move forward."

Young guys like Frederic Brunet and Mason Lohrei chipped in well to fill the McAvoy void but with now eight defensemen, someone will sit tonight. Meanwhile, star forward David Pastrnak remains one goal shy of passing former teammate Brad Marchand for fourth place on the Bruins' all-time list, with both at 422. Pastrnak has two goals and five assists for a team-best seven points and is +145 to find the net tonight.

San Jose (3-2-0) has gone to overtime in three of its five games already, with each of the Sharks' first three wins taking place in either OT or a shootout. Only four teams in NHL history have had their first four or more wins of a season go beyond regulation. The club welcomed back their young superstar forward Celebrini (+950 for Hart Trophy) on Saturday from a two-game injury absence.

The former No. 1 overall pick had the team's only goal, his first of the year, in a 3-1 home defeat to Edmonton. He's the +125 anytime goalscoring favorite tonight and -240 just for a point. Since the start of last season, Celebrini has factored into 46.2% of all Sharks goals (121 of 262; six of 10 this year when in the lineup). Also in that span, San Jose is 26-7-2 when Celebrini scores a goal, 31-13-3 when he tallies an assist and 27-3-2 when he has two or more points in a game.

The Sharks are really fun to watch, as they fly around, but they still don't have a goalie who is going to carry them far, with Yaroslav Askarov (2-2-0, 3.71 GAA) their No. 1 and Alex Nedeljkovic (1-0-0, 1.97 GAA) the backup. It should be Askarov tonight, even though Nedeljkovic has had one good outing.

San Jose swept the 2025-26 season series against Boston, winning 3-1 at home and then 4-2 away. Celebrini had a goal and two assists combined. Askarov and Nedeljkovic each got a start. Pastrnak (goal, two assists) led Boston with three points, and Swayman was in net for both.
