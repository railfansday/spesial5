DFRobot HUSKYLENS 2 AI Vision Sensor Wins Teach Awards 2026 in the EdTech & Software Category for Secondary

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

SHANGHAI, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- DFRobot's HUSKYLENS 2 AI vision sensor has been named the winner in the EdTech & Software Category for Secondary at the Teach Awards.

The Teach Awards, organised by Teachwire, recognise education technology products, resources and projects that deliver real value in teaching and learning. Judging focuses on practical classroom impact, ease of use, and the difference a product makes to students' learning experience. The award for HUSKYLENS 2 reflects recognition of its value in secondary-level AI and STEM teaching.

HUSKYLENS 2 is DFRobot's second-generation AI vision sensor, designed for education and maker scenarios. It features more than 20 built-in AI models, an on-board touchscreen interface, support for training and deploying custom models, and compatibility with mainstream open-source hardware platforms. The product is designed to lower the barrier to learning artificial intelligence, enabling students to complete AI vision projects â such as object recognition, face recognition and line following â without first mastering complex programming, turning abstract AI concepts into classroom practice that students can observe, operate and verify.

DFRobot notes that secondary school is a critical period for developing scientific literacy and engineering thinking, and that AI education should go beyond concepts. Through a hands-on form factor, HUSKYLENS 2 helps teachers bring AI into the classroom more easily, so students can grasp models, data and algorithms through practical projects. DFRobot also hopes open development capabilities will let teachers, makers and developers co-create AI teaching content, building an ecosystem that runs from product to community and from classroom to project.

The Teach Awards recognition affirms DFRobot's continued investment in education hardware. Going forward, DFRobot will keep developing the HUSKYLENS series and teaching resources around real classroom needs, improve its developer toolchain and community support, and work with more educators and developers to advance AI education at the secondary level.
