ArcSpan Wins Best New Product or Service: Sell Side at The Wires Awards 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

ArcSpan AMS recognised for helping publishers turn first-party audience signals into revenue

LONDON, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- ArcSpan, the first-party audience monetisation platform for publishers, has won Best New Product or Service: Sell Side at ExchangeWire's The Wires Awards 2026. The award recognises ArcSpan AMS (Audience Monetisation System) and was presented on Thursday 12 November at One Marylebone in London.

The Wires celebrate innovation and performance across digital advertising, with winners chosen by an expert judging panel.

Publishers are under pressure as signal loss, AI-driven discovery and shifting buyer expectations reshape the market. ArcSpan AMS lets publishers build, activate and measure audience segments across direct and programmatic channels while retaining control of their data. ArcSpan customers include LADbible Group, Overwolf, Snack Media, The Atlantic and Fortune.

"This award marks a special moment for our team and for the customers, partners and investors who have helped us get here," said Arthur Muldoon, Co-founder and CEO of ArcSpan. "Publishers own the audience signals that power sell-side decisioning. Our job is to help them turn that advantage into revenue, with AI and agentic tools that keep them in control."

"UK and European publishers are generating bespoke, nimble audience activation with results they can measure, without lengthy implementation projects," said James Dempsey, VP International at ArcSpan. "LADbible Group migrated to ArcSpan AMS in 28 days and increased its average audience cohort size by 30%. That's the kind of result we're now delivering across the region."

The full list of winners is available on ExchangeWire.

ArcSpan Technologies helps premium publishers turn first-party audience data into advertising revenue. Its Audience Monetisation System, ArcSpan AMS, uses an Aggregate, Amplify, Activate framework to capture audience signals, enrich them with identity and AI, and activate them across direct, programmatic and curated demand. Built for publisher revenue, audience and operations teams, ArcSpan keeps publishers in control of their data.
