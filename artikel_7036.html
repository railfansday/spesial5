28 Thoughtful Gifts Under $25 That Don't Feel Tacky, Useless, or Weird

You'll want to get something for yourself.

Gift-giving during the holidays is a delicate dance: you want to find the right item that feels thoughtful and cool but also doesn’t kill your budget or feel like something they already own. Our team is passionate about helping people find the right items, from hosting gifts to products you can bring to your next white elephant to even what to put in your family’s stocking stuffers. The topic we’re tackling? Best gifts under $25 that don’t feel tacky or cheap.

If you dig hard enough, there are actually plenty of unique, thoughtful, and fun finds for under this price point. Whether it be a martini-shaped candle, colorful oven mitts, or a deck of tarot cards, the variety of gift ideas ahead will work for your groups’ gifting needs. These nifty finds are proof that you can buy something that feels special without spending a ton. We’ll raise a glass to that.

For the person who always orders a martini, gift them this candle that nods to a dirty martini! It has notes of greens, herbs, and juniper to make their home smell fresh. However, they may not even burn it, to keep the wax intact for maximum cuteness on the table.

The sentimentalist in your life will appreciate a framed photo, especially if they’re moving away, just moved into a new home, or have been talking about filling their space with meaningful items.

Bedding and towel brand Hommey recently launched tableware, and the pieces are just as colorful and fun as their sheets. We love these two-toned bowls, perfect for olives, nuts, and other small snacks for gatherings.

Store-bought lighters aren’t the prettiest, so give them a case to hold their lighter in. This option from Etsy can be personalized, and we’re guessing they’ll want to proudly leave it out on their coffee table right next to their candle.

Everyone talks about their coffee setup, but can we take a moment to talk about a proper matcha setup? Their morning ritual at home isn’t complete without a fun bowl and whisk stand set, and this Anthro pairing comes in many colorful options.

With winter heading our way, a heated blanket is a must. This soft flannel option comes in a classic plaid pattern that oozes holiday vibes and has 10 adjustable heat settings ranging from 86°F to 122°F.

If you catch them watching tarot readings on TikTok and YouTube, you know it’s time to get their own deck. This one is perfect for beginners because it comes with thought starters and features cat illustrations for a lighthearted touch.

Whether they just moved into their first apartment or recently invested in an espresso machine, they will need espresso cups. We love this set because it comes with six earthy shades, so if they have a few guests over, everyone has a cup.

Not only does a pie bird look adorable in the middle of a piping-hot apple pie, but it makes it taste better too. Pie birds (or whistles) vent steam during the baking process, preventing the filling from boiling over and the crust from getting soggy.

We had to test this mini water bottle ourselves, and we found it's incredibly handy to take on the go. It's just the right size to fit in a pocket, holding your morning coffee or a drink for the beach.

For the friend who loves a vinyl dig or a new pressing of their favorite artist, help them keep their collection in tip-top shape. This cleaning kit stores easily in a record cabinet, and keeps even the dustiest records sounding brand-new.

To fill your new taper candle holders (and support a small business in the process), snag a set of these hand-poured beeswax taper candles. They add instant drama, and they're paraffin-free. Plus, they're unscented, so they won't clash with the scentscape of your home.

Art therapy can be a great way to release stress and celebrate your inner child. This takes that joy on the road, with a brush that you only need water to use, and a design that disappears over time. No mess, and no requirement to get every stroke perfect.

Fluted designs are back in, and this adorable vase is perfect to display a few choice blooms. The U-shaped design also works as standalone decor, if you just want it to be a striking sculpture.

Though this giant farfalle looks like the real thing, it actually smells more like a traditional candle, with notes of jasmine, neroli, and orange blossom. It also comes in a rigatoni shape, if that's more the recipient's thing.

Of course, you can gift a loved one a candle and a set of matches from the drugstore, but that's hardly any fun. Instead, grab a much more stylish version of the traditional matchbox that they can leave out on display. Worry not; there's a striker on the bottom. The matches, however, are not included!

Anyone on your list who appreciates the kitschier things in life will adore this ceramic soap dish shaped like a claw-foot bathtub. Just like an actual tub, there is a tiny hole for drainage so that the suds don't sit (and get moldy) in the basin.

As far as we're concerned, no one can ever have too many oven mitts. After all, they get stained quickly, so having a fresh, clean pair on hand is always a good idea.

There's no shame in being afraid of the dark, so we say embrace the fear and snag this charming (and portable) touch lamp. It gets brighter and dimmer with just the tap of a finger. It almost mimics candlelight, but unlike a flickering flame, it is safe to use all night.
