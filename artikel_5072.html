Did Bitcoin’s volatility after the NVDA rally offer the first real warning?

In the last 24 hours, the market may well have seen some of the most active trading in Q4. Alongside the sharp rise in NVDA (+30%), there was also a re-investment into crypto. Bitcoin surged by almost 6% in 30 minutes on the back of the Jensen announcement, rising to $65k from $61.1k. This led to $375 million of shorts being liquidated. However, the sudden price spike did little to improve overall market sentiment.

Interestingly, Bitcoin’s Fear and Greed Index has continued to sit at 11, indicating that the market is still highly fearful. The result? Reversal came quickly. Bitcoin fell 5.17% from its intra-day peak, wiping out all the gains made after the announcement. Over the same period, $140 million of longs were also liquidated.

In short, the past 24 hours have been a textbook example of extreme market volatility.

However, the market is not dismissing this as a short-term price swing. Market makers have already pointed out that it is one of the most significant manipulative incidences of this cycle. According to AMBCrypto, if this was indeed a coordinated move, the risk of Bitcoin breaking below $55k cannot be ruled out.

Are whales preparing to sell more Bitcoin [BTC] and shake out weaker investors?

As previously discussed, it now seems that Bitcoin’s price action during the NVDA surge was not entirely coincidental. It appears big players are preparing for a further bearish trend. As identified by Lookonchain, AguilaTrades opened a BTC short on 1k BTC (worth $62.25 million). Thus, when BTC surged in response to Huang’s reinstatement, some market participants had already predicted a downturn in the rebound.

Notably, this is where Glassnode data becomes particularly interesting. As identified in the chart below, the $53k area has developed into a key area of resistance, with significant STH supply concentrated at this price point. Usually, ‘smart money’ looks to flush out poorer quality holders. It can then take on board this supply and set itself up for a possible recovery.

In this context, Bitcoin’s volatility after the NVDA rally may have offered the first real warning.

Despite widespread crypto market fear, an overall market rebound and almost $3 trillion of new cash entering the markets, Bitcoin has not managed to sustain its momentum. This seems to indicate that major investors are still not quite ready to enter the market. Combined with Glassnode’s data, the price action looks less like random volatility and more like strategic positioning, potentially aimed at clearing the $53k supply cluster before a stronger recovery.

Bitcoin failed to hold its post-NVDA rally, showing that buyers remain cautious.

Whale activity and the $55k supply cluster point to more downside risk before a recovery.
