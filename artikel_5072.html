MEXC Pulls The Plug On VISA Card Months After Launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Home » Blitz news » MEXC Pulls The Plug On VISA Card Months After Launch

Customers on MEXC’s global exchange can’t use their platform-issued debit cards following an issuer problem.

The exchange announced that its Global Card service ends today, November 18, 2026, at 15:30 UTC after its third-party card issuer made service adjustments. Existing cards will no longer process transactions and new applications are suspended.

The virtual Visa card launched on August 31 with features that included up to 10% USDT cashback and high spending limits. It lasted under three months.

The shutdown follows the earlier discontinuation of MEXC’s APAC Card on November 11 over similar issuer issues, and it comes after a temporary maintenance pause that began on November 14.

Cardholders can still withdraw their remaining balances to their MEXC Fiat account before the cutoff.

Any funds left on the card will be snapshotted on November 21 at 15:30 UTC and credited to Spot accounts within three business days.

Refunds for prior transactions will go to Fiat accounts once received, pending cashback continues on schedule, and users are advised to cancel or switch any recurring payments tied to the card.

MEXC has not identified the card issuer or detailed the exact nature of the adjustments. Funds are not frozen; they are being returned to users’ exchange accounts.
