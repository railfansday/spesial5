The Seagull’s wingspan

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

IC 2177, the Seagull Nebula, is a wide complex of ionized hydrogen straddling Monoceros and Canis Major roughly 3,600 light-years away. In this HOO-palette image, its hydrogen-filled wings appear reddish, while its teal-tinted core is oxygen gas excited by young, hot stars.

Samyang 135 mm lens at f/2.8 · ZWO ASI533MC Pro camera · Optolong L-Ultimate Hα/OIII dual-band filter · 30 hours of exposure
