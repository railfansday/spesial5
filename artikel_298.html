Everything to know about Black Friday 2026: Best deals, sale dates, doorbusters, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The biggest shopping event of the year is finally here: Black Friday.

Early Black Friday deals started rolling in weeks ago, but now that the actual holiday is upon us, retailers are in deal overload mode. (That will continue through the weekend into Cyber Monday, and likely a few days beyond.) To avoid getting overwhelmed by the hundreds of thousands of deals, it's good to head into the mega-savings event with a plan.

From preparing your shopping list to knowing when the major Black Friday sales start and end, we've got you covered on everything you need to know to shop Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

Almost every major retailer started offering Black Friday deals in early to mid-November. That said, some products have gotten additional price cuts on Black Friday proper, which falls on Nov. 27 in 2026.

Amazon: Amazon's Black Friday Week sale runs from Nov. 19 to 30, offering 12 consecutive days of deals.

Best Buy: Black Friday at Best Buy started on Nov. 19 and runs through Nov. 28. Its Cyber Monday sale runs from Nov. 29 to Nov. 30, followed by a Cyber Week sale that extends savings from Dec. 1 to Dec. 6.

The Home Depot: The Home Depot's Black Friday goes from Nov. 26 to Dec. 2. It will host a Cyber Week sale from Dec. 3 to 6.

Kohl's: The department store's Black Friday Week sale has been live since Nov. 22, and it ends Nov. 28.

Lowe's: Lowe's has a relatively short Black Friday sale compared to other retailers. It runs from Nov. 27 to 30.

Target: Target's Black Friday sale kicked off on Nov. 22 and goes through Nov. 29. Its Cyber Monday sale starts on Nov. 30 at 3 a.m. ET.

Walmart: Walmart is hosting a Black Friday sale from Nov. 23 to Nov. 29 (with deals online only from Nov. 23 to Nov. 26). Its online-only Cyber Monday sale is scheduled for Nov. 30 to Dec. 2.

Absolutely. Amazon's Black Friday deals cover everything from the year's top tech to home essentials. Each day of the sale features new "Today's Big Deals," where select products go on sale for up to 60% off.

On Black Friday and Cyber Monday proper, shoppers get two batches of Big Deals: one at 3 a.m. ET and another at 11 a.m. ET. The last time we checked, featured products currently include the Apple Watch SE 3, Bose QuietComfort Headphones, the Theragun Relief, and the last-gen Echo Dot (which comes with a bonus $25 credit for Prime members).

The Apple Store is hosting a Black Friday Savings Event from Nov. 27 to 30, but it's not offering any product discounts during the sale. Instead, it's just giving away free gift cards worth $25 to $300 with the purchase of select devices and accessories. For the best Black Friday Apple deals that actually save you cash, we recommend shopping at third-party retailers like Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, and Target instead.

Target has great Black Friday deals if you're looking for toys, Lego sets, Christmas decor, books, or small kitchen appliances. Target's Deal of the Day offers extra savings on hot items, and you can preview upcoming deals several days before they happen. Its Deals of the Day for actual Black Friday take 50% off pajamas and Crocs. Over the weekend, select skincare items, Disney toys, outdoor decor, and shoes will go on sale at deep discounts.

Walmart doesn't skimp on Black Friday deals, and they tend to compete with the best Amazon sales, like $149 Apple AirPods Pro 3. You can also get the Nespresso Vertuo Pop coffee and espresso maker for a rock-bottom price of $59.

It's worth paying close attention to Best Buy's doorbuster deals, in particular. They offer ridiculous savings but tend to sell out quickly (RIP, $149.99 Ninja Slushi). Like Walmart, Best Buy tends to price-match Amazon deals. If you wind up needing a gift in a hurry, Best Buy offers great in-store pickup options as well as quick shipping.

Total and Plus members of the My Best Buy program are eligible to earn double points on their purchases from Nov. 19 to Dec. 6. The Home Depot's Black Friday sale is a must-shop for homeowners and DIYers. It's slashing prices on power tools and appliances for Black Friday. Plus, if you're starting to feel festive, select holiday decor is also on sale there right now.

Over 100 Lego sets and gifts are on sale for 20 to 40% off during the Lego Store's Black Friday savings event, live now through Nov. 29. (Most of those deals are matched at other retailers like Amazon, Target, and Walmart, FYI.)

Lego also decided to drop its first-ever Godzilla build for Black Friday ($449.99). At 5,636 pieces, it's the biggest Lego Ideas set ever made.

While Black Friday brings many of the best prices of the year, it's not the end-all, be-all for every product out there. According to Google shopping data, tech like TVs and gaming consoles are likely to hit their lowest prices during Black Friday and Cyber Week (with the latter seeing one final dip right before Christmas). However, fancy cookware and suitcases hit their lowest prices in late December.

There can be some variance from category to category, so we recommend keeping track of Mashable's deals coverage. Our team has been covering Black Friday (and deals in general) for nearly a decade, so we can tell your firsthand whether or not a product is worth grabbing at any given time.

So, what deals are worth shopping now? It's an especially relevant question in 2026, a year that saw many historic price increases across everything from laptops to Kindles. In other words, shopping smart has never been a smarter decision, which is why we've gathered some of the best Black Friday savings so far.

The best Black Friday deals generally last through Cyber Monday, which falls on Nov. 30 this year. The main exceptions are deals that retailers have marked as doorbusters and limited-time daily drops — add those to your cart sooner rather than later.

Some holiday deals will stick around even longer past Cyber Monday, but we expect most to disappear as soon as the clock strikes midnight on Dec. 1. For the best shot at scoring big savings, shopping during Cyber Weekend is a good rule of thumb.

Amazon and Walmart don't price-match, but Best Buy and Target both offer price protection during the holidays.

If something you buy at Best Buy from Oct. 30 to Dec. 31 gets cheaper through Jan. 15, 2027, you can request a partial refund for the difference. Through Dec. 24, Target shoppers can do the same if they spot an extra price drop on something they've bought since Nov. 1.

Best Buy: 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. local time, with a ticketing system for high-demand items

Costco: Regular hours (9 a.m. local time for Executive members and 10 a.m. for other members)
