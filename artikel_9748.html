EXP 08/2026 on a medicine strip means 31 August, not 1 August

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

EXP 08/2026 ka matlab: 31 August 2026 tak, 1 August tak nahi.

A pack printed EXP 08/2026 is sellable through 31 August 2026. The rule is short. D&C Rules 1945, Rule 96, Explanation:

"the date of expiry shall be in terms of month and year and it shall mean that the drug is recommended till the last day of the month."

Software that stores this month as 1 August and stops billing from that date loses up to thirty days of sellable stock on that pack. We know how easy that is to get wrong: four places in our own code once resolved that month to four different days.

We are Team Nesayo, and we make pharmacy billing software. Everything below works whichever software you use.

This is the labelling rule, not advice for your particular case. For a question about a specific pack, please confirm with your drug inspector.
