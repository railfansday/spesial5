The Liberation trailer sees Carrie Coon joining Lily James cult

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

A big name cast combines with family drama and a possible cult in the trailer for The Liberation, Guy Nattiv's 1980s-set thriller about a mother who leaves her family for the allure of something new.

Carrie Coon plays Rita, a Holocaust survivor who leaves her husband (Tracy Letts) and two daughters (Odessa Young and Bella Ramsey) to join an all-female commune led by an enigmatic spiritual leader (Lily James). But things take a tricky turn when her daughters show up looking for her, and Rita has to make a difficult choice.
