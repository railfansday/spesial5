Is Your DualSense Running Low Playing GTA VI?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Those of you playing Grand Theft Auto VI on the PlayStation 5 might have noticed that you’re having to charge your controller a lot more than usual, especially if you are using the more expensive DualSense Edge. You also might have noticed that GTA VI uses every feature on that controller to its fullest potential. These two facts are not unrelated!

Grand Theft Auto VI makes exceptional use of the DualSense’s haptic triggers, vibrations, and speaker. You might not even realize how much it’s doing until or unless you turn these features off and find that you kind of miss all the things the controller is doing with them. While they’re a great value-add, they absolutely come at a cost to your battery life on the controller.

Based on our tests, an average session with GTA lasts about 4 hours and 30 minutes on a fully-charged standard DualSense — in this case, an OEM model that came with the PlayStation 5 Pro. A DualSense Edge is even lower, with reports averaging out to around 3 hours and 40 minutes. On a different game, like Phantom Blade 0, the Dualsense will last upwards of double that amount. Times will vary for people depending on whether they replaced the battery themselves, what they’re doing in the game, how old their controller is, etc. But by and large, you’re not going to get a ton of hours if you’re planning to sit around and spend all Thanksgiving weekend playing the game, at least not without a USB-C cable nearby.

But this is all sort of a PSA not to worry if you’re ending every session at one bar on the PS5 battery icon. Your controller isn’t dying, it’s just exhausted traveling around Vice City.
