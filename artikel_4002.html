Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea Of Sorrow DLC Finally Has A Release Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

We had our fingers crossed that Team Cherry might reveal the release date for Hollow Knight: Silksong's first DLC in its special presentation today, and what do you know, it did!

Sea of Sorrow will officially launch on Switch and Switch 2 on 11th December. This is a free expansion, so all those with the base game will be able to download and dive right in from this date.

Team Cherry initially teased the DLC late last year, leaving us for 11 months with the vague '2026' release window. It sees Hornet dive into a brand new area of Pharloom, packed with new bosses, tools, enemies and... fishing?
