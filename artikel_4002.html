Tanggal: 2026-11-25

It's official, Xbox's Black Friday sales are live! This year's offering is absolutely massive, so we're back to rummage through them all and pick out a banger or two for you.

The annual sale lives up to the hype yet again, with over 2,000 deals to get through. There's so many. Too many, in fact. Plenty of big games are reduced by 90%, whilst others are at historic lows. Lots to be excited about!

It's a hefty job sifting through Xbox sales, we get it. So, instead of having you waste your precious gaming time doomscrolling through the Xbox Store, we've handpicked 50 deals from Xbox's Black Friday 2026 sale. These recommendations come complete with trailers, descriptions, Game Pass availability, and whether each title supports Xbox Play Anywhere!

Of course you can find all of these games, and then some, by going to the "Deals" section of the Microsoft Store app on your Xbox console.
