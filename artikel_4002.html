Grand Theft Auto VI Reaches Highest Concurrent Player Count Ever For PS5, More than 683 Million Hours Logged Already

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Grand Theft Auto VI hasn't even been out for a week, and it's already broken so many records that it's hard to keep up. It made $3.79 billion in just 24 hours after its November 19 launch, making it the biggest entertainment launch of all time. It comes as no surprise, then, that Rockstar Games' latest crime saga is also shattering records specifically on PlayStation 5.

In a new PlayStation Blog post, the company has revealed that more than 39 million unique players have booted up GTA VI on PS5, with the game setting a new record for the highest number of concurrent players for any game on PlayStation, surpassing previous records across multiplayer games such as Helldivers II, free-to-play games like Fortnite, and single-player releases like Elden Ring. What's more, PS5 players have already logged a cumulative 683 million hours of GTA VI gameplay – that's nearly 78,000 years' worth of time.

Other records for GTA VI on PS5 include the game giving PlayStation the "largest number of newly connected PS5 consoles on a single day since the console's launch, outside of end-of-year holidays," and GTA VI being the first game played on 70% of new PS5 consoles since the game's launch.

This is likely only the beginning of records GTA VI will continue to break.
