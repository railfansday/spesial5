Axos ONE Review – High-Yield Savings When Your Deposits Qualify

Open Axos ONE if your paycheck already arrives by direct deposit. Each month with at least $1,500 in direct deposits and an average checking balance of at least $1,500, savings earns 4.21% APY, and the bundle charges no monthly or overdraft fees. If your deposits vary month to month, pick a savings account that pays its rate without conditions.

Most banks pay little on savings. The national average rate is 0.39% APY, or annual percentage yield, according to the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, so a better rate usually means a second bank, away from the checking account your paycheck lands in. Axos ONE pairs checking and high-yield savings at one online bank and ties the savings rate to the checking side. In any month the checking account meets its deposit and balance terms, savings earns the boosted rate. In a month it misses them, savings drops to 1.00% and checking earns nothing.

Axos Bank opened as Bank of Internet USA on July 4, 2000, one of the first banks built to run fully online, and took the Axos name in 2018. It’s based in San Diego, and its parent, Axos Financial, trades on the New York Stock Exchange and reported $30.8 billion in total assets at the end of September 2026. Axos also runs UFB Direct, which matters for the new-customer promotion and the deposit insurance below.

Axos ONE is two accounts opened together, and the checking account’s activity decides the rates on both.

Most of the bundle’s value is in savings. In a qualifying month it pays 4.21% APY on balances up to $249,999.99 and 3.50% on the portion above that. In any other month, the whole balance earns 1.00%.

Checking pays 0.51% APY in a qualifying month and nothing otherwise. There’s no monthly fee, no minimum balance, and no overdraft or nonsufficient-funds fee. A payment your balance can’t cover may still be paid or returned, just without a charge. A direct deposit can arrive up to two days early, depending on whether and when your payer funds it. The Visa debit card works fee-free at more than 95,000 ATMs, and Axos reimburses up to $10 a month in other banks’ domestic ATM fees.

The bank’s standalone Axos Checking pays 1.00% APY, a promotional rate, in any statement cycle with at least $1 in direct deposits, and reimburses domestic ATM fees without a cap, but it carries no savings boost. If you only need checking, Axos Checking or another free checking account without a balance requirement is the simpler choice.

Both accounts must also be open on the 25th and on the day interest is paid. A new account’s first window starts the day it opens, and one opened on or after the 25th starts qualifying the next month.

The fairest yardstick for those terms is Axos’s own Summit Savings, which pays 3.75% APY with no minimum balance and no monthly requirements. Take $20,000 kept in savings for a year at the rates Axos paid when this review was written. At 4.21%, qualifying every month earns about $842 in Axos ONE, $92 more than the $750 Summit Savings pays at 3.75%. At the 1.00% base rate, missing every month earns about $200, $550 less than Summit.

New customers who open the bundle by December 1, 2026 can earn 5.00% APY on up to $49,999.99 of savings for six statement cycles, the opening cycle included, or five when the account opens on or after the 25th. Each cycle still has to meet the monthly terms, and the boost is off in any cycle the savings balance averages $250,000 or more. Anyone who holds or has ever held an Axos Bank or UFB Direct account, loan, or investment is excluded, so opening another Axos account first, Summit Savings included, rules you out. At the rates when this review was written, the boost adds 0.79 percentage points, about $197 over six cycles on a full $49,999.99.

The fees Axos ONE does charge cover less routine moves. Outgoing wires cost $35 within the U.S. and $45 abroad, incoming wires are free, and debit card purchases and ATM withdrawals in a foreign currency carry a 1% conversion fee.

Axos works online, so the app and the phone carry most of the load. Its Axos All-In-One Mobile Banking app holds a 4.7-star rating from about 20,000 ratings on Apple’s App Store, and a Direct Banker answers the phone 24/7, Thanksgiving and Christmas aside. Checks deposited by phone are held five days.

Cash is the awkward part. You can deposit it at participating ATMs or at the register of retailers in the Green Dot network, such as 7-Eleven, CVS, and Walmart. The store may charge up to $4.95 per deposit, and each deposit can run $20 to $500, more at Walmart and ACE Cash Express.

Deposits are insured by the FDIC through Axos Bank up to $250,000 per depositor in each ownership category, such as individual or joint. Money you keep at UFB Direct or any other Axos brand counts toward the same limit, since Axos insures them all under one FDIC certificate.

For larger savings, the Axos Insured Deposit Program moves money from Axos ONE Savings to partner banks, each insuring up to the same limit, for up to $2.5 million in additional coverage. Enrolling online or in the app is free and leaves your rate and features unchanged. Checking stays outside the program. You also can’t exclude a partner bank, and Axos leaves it to you to watch which banks hold your money, which matters if you already bank at one of them.

Axos ONE works best as a home for a paycheck that already arrives by direct deposit. That deposit, plus a $1,500 cushion in checking, earns savings the boosted rate, no part of the bundle charges a monthly or overdraft fee, and the extra FDIC coverage is free. For a saver who wants checking and savings at one bank, that’s a strong combination.

It’s a poor fit for freelancers and others whose deposits arrive irregularly and for anyone who can’t spare $1,500 in checking, since Summit Savings or another account with no monthly terms would earn them more. Savers who move money out often or deposit cash will run into its six-withdrawal limit and paid cash deposits. Readers whose paycheck clears $1,500 a month should open the bundle and point the direct deposit to its checking account so it posts by the 25th.
