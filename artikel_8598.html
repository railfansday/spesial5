Personal Investments • Re: Wellington in Roth?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Do you own Vanguard Wellington Fund Investor Shares MF (VWELX) or Vanguard Wellington U.S. Growth Active ETF (VUSG)? They are not the same.

VWELX has about 1/3 bonds, but I think VUSG is 100% stocks. Many people opt to keep their Roth accounts 100% equity, assuming they have space in tax-deferred for bonds. YMMV.

Both are actively managed, with higher ERs as a result- 0.24% for VWELX & 0.35% for VUSG. Those numbers are not super high, but an index MF/ETF would give you a much lower ER & better returns over the years. Many Vanguard index funds (VTI, VOO, VUG) have ERs of around 0.03%.
Personally, I don't think actively managed funds are worth the extra cost.
A balanced fund is definitely going to cost you more in the long run, for sure. That being said, the cost of Vanguard's "balanced" funds is still pretty reasonable in the whole scheme of things. I think a balanced fund is a great option if your spouse looks at you like you're speaking latin when you talk with them about basic Boglehead principles like very low expenses and rebalancing. I don't do balanced funds now, but if it looks like my time is drawing to a close and I have some time to prep for that, I will definitely switch my investments to a single balanced fund in my IRA and my Roth in an attempt to keep from overwhelming them. In my mind, feeling overwhelmed usually means you call someone for help, and I would rather not chance my spouse calling Edward Jones for "help".
