FA Cup fever has taken hold at Altrincham as the Robins prepare to welcome League One side Notts County to the J. Davidson Stadium in the First Round.

The National League team will be confident they can spring a shock against a club who sit 38 places above them in the pyramid, with their strong home form underpinning a promising start to the season that has Neil Gibson's men seventh in the table.

The Robins are looking for their first win over an EFL side since 2015, when they dumped out Barnsley, while they have not reached the Second Round since 2019.

Altrincham boast several former County players, including Tom Crawford, Kristian Dennis, Kane Hemmings, Jack Hinchy and Jimmy Knowles. Meanwhile, Gibson has added former Hull City forward Pharrell Brown to his squad ahead of the cup clash.

The Magpies are part of a small collection of former winners to enter at this stage of the competition, having lifted the FA Cup way back in 1894.

As the oldest professional club in the world, County have a proud history but the days of battling at the business end of this competition are long gone, having not progressed beyond the Fifth Round since 1991.

Martin Paterson's current crop are looking to consolidate in League One after winning promotion via the play-offs last term, and they have stabilised after a bad patch, most recently beating local rivals Mansfield Town last Saturday.

It will be interesting to see how much Paterson rotates his squad, with goalkeeper James Belshaw and defenders Jacob Bedeau and Lucas Ness ever-presents in the third tier this season. Ollie Cooper has proved a useful source of goals from midfield, with four in 10 appearances this term.
