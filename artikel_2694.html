4 bold predictions for the Broncos in the second half of the 2026 NFL season

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Denver Broncos have played nine games this season and following their bye week, they will have eight games left. The team is at the exact midway point of the 2026 season.

The Broncos sit at 7-2 and are currently tied with the Kansas City Chiefs for the AFC West division lead. Though it's been unconventional, the Broncos have won seven of their last eight games and each of their last five. They are one of the hottest teams heading into the second half of the season and will have a favorable schedule to look forward to.

Not everything in this forecast will be favorable to the Broncos, however. You can decide what the team's chances of winning the Super Bowl will be should these predictions come to fruition.

Jonah Coleman rushed for just 191 yards in the team's first nine games, but he is clearly the RB1 going forward and stands a chance to be a huge part of the team's offense over the remainder of the season. He also missed several games while dealing with an ankle injury.

In order to quadruple the number he posted in the first nine games, Coleman would need to rush for at least 764 yards across the second half of the season. That would mean he would need to average about 84.9 yards per game the rest of the way. That is a lofty mark for the Broncos to reach considering the way they have run the ball so far this season, but Coleman will get the Broncos rolling into the postseason with a strong rushing attack.

After nine games, the Broncos have 26 sacks as a team, which puts them in the top 10 in the league, for now. Last season, they were on pace to break the mark of most sacks in a season, 72, by the 1984 Chicago Bears, but fell a bit short of the mark.

They aren't getting to the quarterback as often as they did last year and by the time the season ends, they will be outside the top 10. They might also watch the Minnesota Vikings, who already have 37 team sacks, break that 42-year-old mark.

This isn't a fun prediction, but it's certainly trending in that direction. The Broncos just have not been able to get from Jaylen Waddle what they were hoping to get when they made the big trade with the Miami Dolphins this offseason.

Waddle is averaging just 3.3 receptions per game this season, which would put him on pace for about 60 catches this season. The lowest single-season mark of his career came in 2024, when he caught only 58 passes. He won't even get to that number this year.

That's a far cry from the 104 catches he had as a rookie in 2021, which set a single-season record for rookie wide receivers.

The Broncos don't face a very difficult schedule across the remainder of this season. There is a chance that they will be favored in each of their remaining games this season, with a Christmas Day showdown with the Buffalo Bills looming large.

A predicted record of 14-3 means the Broncos would lose just one more time this season. We will also predict that will lead to them being the No. 1 seed in the AFC playoffs for a second straight season, meaning the road to Super Bowl LXI would have to go through Denver.
