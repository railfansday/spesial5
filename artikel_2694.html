3 things we learned from the Bengals’ uplifting win over the Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It's good to sometimes take a step back and appreciate how far the Cincinnati Bengals have come since drafting Joe Burrow.

The lead-up to primetime games used to be filled with anxiety. Bengals fans knew the likelihood of getting embarrassed was high,

But now, primetime is where the Bengals shine. And last night's game might be the best example.

Cincinnati went head-to-head with their heated rivals, who were tied with them for the division lead. And they absolutely destroyed them.

Here are three things we learned from the clinic Burrow and the Bengals put on against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Amarius Mims dominated T.J. Watt, making him look like a child.

And Orlando Brown Jr., considered to be the weak link at the start of the season, just delivered the best pass protection game of any left tackle this year, not allowing a pressure on 44 dropbacks.

Burrow had clean pockets almost the entire game, similar to the game against the Atlanta Falcons. And, as a result, he methodically picked those defenses apart.

In the past, the Bengals would fall behind early, trying to force a run game that wasn't there.

Against the Steelers, they used the short game to get the pass in a flow, and sprinkled in deep shots down the field.

Burrow and the offense came out firing, confident in their ability to have their way with any defense, even one as intimidating as Pittsburgh's.

After a slow start, Chase has 527 yards receiving in his last three games. He had probably the most casual 200 yard receiving game you'll ever see against the Steelers. And his 231 total was the highest for any receiver this season.

The combination of Burrow having time, the offense finding its identity, and Tee Higgins emerging as the most physically dominant receiver in the league have led to Chase being able to break out his entire bag on a regular basis.
