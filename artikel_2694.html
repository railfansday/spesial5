Nets gets crushing injury update on Nolan Traore — with Mikel Brown set for big chance

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

MINNEAPOLIS — Brooklyn's Nolan Traore will miss at least the next two months with a hamstring injury, in a tough break for both the fast-rising Frenchman and his struggling Nets.

Traore was diagnosed with a right hamstring strain, and it will be eight-to-10 weeks before he's even re-evaluated, according to the team.

The young point guard suffered the injury in the third quarter of Friday night's 113-111 loss to the visiting Orlando Magic. Traore limped off the Barclays Center floor under his own power and didn't return. With this news, he's not expected back until the calendar flips to 2027.

It's unclear what Brooklyn's plans are at the point guard position, though this does open the door wide for rookie Mikel Brown Jr. to make his first start. The lottery pick has been on a minutes limit and treated with kid gloves to this point.

A trainer checks on Brooklyn Nets guard Nolan Traore (10) during the second half against the Orlando Magic in Brooklyn, N.Y., Friday, Nov. 20, 2026. Robert Sabo for NY Post

The Nets play Monday night at the Minnesota Timberwolves, and coach Jordi Fernandez's decision will speak volumes.

Off guard Egor Demin is another option, with the 2025 lottery pick being the only other Net who has started at the lead guard spot this season.

After Demin had been starting at point guard during his rookie campaign — and sorely miscast — Traore supplanted him midway through the season. Now the young Frenchman had taken a quantum leap forward this year, and improved the most of the so-called Flatbush Five — Brooklyn's quintet of 2025 first-round picks.

Traore went from averaging 8.9 points on .380 shooting and .318 from 3-point range as a rookie to his current 12.9 points on .511 overall and .404 from deep.

The game had clearly slowed down for the speedy Frenchman, and he was learning how to not only use his quickness but his newfound strength and size to not get bodied off the ball.

As a rookie, Traore's finishing at the rim had ranked dead last in the NBA among players with at least 130 attempts, at just 44.6 percent according to Synergy. But after a summer spent bulking up, he'd raised that to 56.4%. He'd also worked on a repeatable shooting form, and improved on catch-and-shoot 3s.

Traore had averaged 15.5 points on white-hot .537 shooting and .420 from behind the arc in his last 11 games before getting hurt.

That injury does create opportunity for Brown.

The sixth overall pick — the highest selection for the Nets in 16 years — still has not started a game for Brooklyn. Fernandez has pointed to Brown's minutes limit, but the young guard has logged 27:43 in an overtime loss at Boston, 27:23 last Wednesday against Chicago and 26:56 in the Garden against the Knicks.

Demin has gotten the nod at point guard four times this season – including in a jumbo lineup with everybody 6-foot-9 or taller, the first in the league since April 21, 1990. But the Russian is best suited as an off-ball gravity player, and Fernandez has even admitted as much.

Brooklyn Nets' Mikel Brown Jr. dribbles down court against the New York Knicks at Madison Square Garden in New York, Monday, Nov. 2, 2026. Jason Szenes for the New York Post

Danny Wolf and Terance Mann will likely be used to at least help with secondary ballhandling duties — especially when Demin is at the point — and 20-year-old Ben Saraf could be called up from the G League. The Israeli — who is the Nets' second-youngest player behind Traore — is a pure point guard and has been playing well for Long Island, averaging 16.4 points and 8.6 assists.
