Sunday Night Football: Joe Burrow torching Steelers, Bengals lead 24-3 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Joe Burrow and the Bengals are putting it on the Steelers on Sunday night.

Cincinnati’s quarterback threw for 302 yards and three touchdowns in the first two quarters as the Bengals have a 24-3 lead at halftime.

Burrow completed 26-of-34 passes in the first half. Seven completions went to Ja’Marr Chase for 146 yards, including a 53-yard downfield pass. Tee Higgins — who went back to the locker room just before the half for an injury check — caught five passes for 66 yards.

Cincinnati scored on a 24-yard field goal to cap an opening drive, then used a 13-play, 92-yard drive to make the score 10-0 with Burrow’s 3-yard touchdown to Drew Sample. Chase Brown then caught a 2-yard touchdown pass to make the score 17-0.

After Chris Boswell put the Steelers on the board with a 45-yard field goal late in the second period, the Bengals got right down the field and scored on Burrow’s 15-yard pass to Dohnte Meyers.

The only thing that stopped Cincinnati’s offense in the first half was kicker Evan McPherson, who missed his first kick of the season in sending a 43-yard field goal attempt wide right in the second quarter.

Cincinnati’s defense has played just as well as its offense. Pittsburgh was limited to just four first downs and 40 total yards in the first half. Aaron Rodgers started the game 0-of-8 passing, finishing the half 4-of-12 for 25 yards.

Rodgers had never started a game 0-of-8 in his career, according to the NBC broadcast. His first completion was a 5-yard pass to Darnell Washington with 4:31 left in the second quarter.

Pittsburgh will get a shot to turn it around to start the third quarter, as the team will receive the second-half kickoff.
