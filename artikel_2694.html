This is where the Rams sit in the NFC playoff picture

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Los Angeles Rams remain out of the NFC playoff picture heading into their Wednesday night matchup against the Green Bay Packers. The Rams are on a bye in Week 11, so they couldn't help or hurt their playoff chances this week and nothing else that happened on Sunday moved the needle either.

The Rams are tied with the Green Bay Packers and Seattle Seahawks as all teams stand at 6-4 going into Week 12, with the Packers holding the final wild card spot.

Therefore, if the Rams beat the Packers on Wednesday, L.A. will temporarily move into the 7-seed and the final wild card spot. However, if the Seahawks beat the San Francisco 49ers next Sunday, then Seattle will leapfrog the Rams and move into the 7-seed.

On the other hand, if the Rams lose to Green Bay this week, that will be a damaging blow to L.A.'s playoff hopes. They will be 6-5 and sit a game behind the Packers in the race for a wild card berth.

The 49ers beat the Vikings on Sunday Night Football and they hold a one-game lead for the number one seed in the NFC, with a three-game lead in the NFC West.

If the 49ers beat the Seahawks on Sunday, it will be nearly impossible for anyone in the division to catch them.

The Rams have their rematch against San Francisco in Week 14. But L.A. can't afford anymore slip ups and losing Puka Nacua for the rest of the season can't be an excuse. The Rams hope to have back Davante Adams this Wednesday against Green Bay, a return that couldn't come soon enough.

The only team behind L.A. with a chance is the 5-6 Cowboys after Dallas romped the Titans 51-30. But the Cowboys are still two games back in the loss column and mostly fighting to win the NFC East.

The Rams are far from out of it, but they will need to beat the Packers on Wednesday to really stay in it.
