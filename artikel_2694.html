D.J. Reed returns to Lions practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Lions have gotten plenty of bad injury news on defense in recent weeks, but there was a positive development at practice on Wednesday.

Cornerback D.J. Reed was a limited participant in practice as the team began preparing for Sunday’s game against the Buccaneers. Reed missed the last two games with a hip injury that also kept him out of practice.

Center Cade Mays did not play last Sunday and remained out of practice with an ankle injury. Head coach Dan Campbell said earlier this week that he does not expect Mays to play this week.

Linebacker Joe Bachie (knee), cornerback Khalil Dorsey (ankle), defensive lineman Tyler Lacy (shoulder), cornerback Ennis Rakestraw (shoulder), and running back Sione Vaki (ankle) were all listed as limited participants.

Safety Kerby Joseph (knee) was a full participant. Campbell said he is expected to make his 2026 debut against Tampa and he was activated off of the physically unable to perform list on Wednesday.
