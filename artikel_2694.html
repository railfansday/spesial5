JJ Redick puts Lakers ‘trend’ on blast despite 8th straight win

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

PHOENIX — The Lakers' eighth straight win didn't stop coach J.J. Redick from getting real about one of the team's troubling trends.

The Lakers' victory over the Suns on Sunday played out similarly to other wins they've had this season: An explosive first quarter from Luka Dončić leading to the Lakers having an advantage and a double-digit lead going into halftime before a dominant third quarter gives them a comfortable lead.

Los Angeles Lakers head coach J.J. Redick, center, isn't happy with how his team has handled big leads late in games. AP Photo/Darryl Webb

And then they let go of the rope before pulling out a victory after nearly blowing a significant lead, which is exactly what happened when the Suns cut the Lakers' 31-point lead in the third quarter to five in the fourth before the Lakers won the game by eight.

"I told the team this after the game: We're working too hard and we're playing too hard to build these leads," Redick said. "It's a disservice to each other to take your foot off the gas."

The Lakers are one of the league's worst fourth quarter teams. AP Photo/Aaron Gash

The Lakers, who are tied for the league's best record at 12-2, have the league's second-best net rating in first quarters (plus-18.7), sixth-best rating in second quarters (plus-12.5) and best net rating in third quarters (plus-24.5).

But they have the second-worst net rating in fourth quarters (minus-24.4) because their defense falls off a cliff, leading to them blowing big leads.

The Lakers' 131.3 defensive rating in fourth quarters is the league's second-worst mark.

Los Angeles Lakers forward Ziaire Williams (11), Luka Doncic (77), Jake LaRavia (12) and Walker Kessler (14) wait during a timeout against the Phoenix Suns, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, in Phoenix. AP Photo/Darryl Webb

The Lakers left Phoenix with the win despite nearly blowing a 31-point lead. Getty Images

"You have to play for 48 minutes and we're going to need our defense to win us games," Redick said. "These guys want to be a top-10 defense, we think we can be a good defensive team, I believe we can be a good defensive team. This has been consistent now, this is a trend. Gotta be better in the fourth quarter."

The Lakers will tie their longest winning streak under Redick if they beat the Hornets on Tuesday.

But the former NBA sharpshooter isn't allowing his team's current success to distract from the areas they need to improve at.
