How to watch Warriors vs. Grizzlies

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Golden State Warriors' six-game road trip comes to an end Thursday night as they take on the Memphis Grizzlies on the second night of a back-to-back. The game will be played at 5:00 PM PT in Memphis and can be watched on NBC Sports Bay Area.

It was more of the same for Golden State during Wednesday night's 116-94 loss to the Dallas Mavericks. The Warriors struggled to generate much offense outside of the energy provided by Will Richard, who earned a spot in the starting lineup and finished with a team-high 20 points on 9-of-13 shooting from the field. The defeat marked Golden State's fourth consecutive loss, a drastic turnaround just over a week after completing a perfect six-game homestand.

The Warriors will look to salvage at least one win from this road trip with tonight's matchup against the Grizzlies. That being said, the impact of Steph Curry's absence continues to be felt throughout the lineup, particularly for rookie Yaxel Lendeborg, who has struggled to find his offensive rhythm in recent games.

Lendeborg thrived earlier this season as a secondary scorer and playmaker alongside Curry. However, with the Warriors superstar sidelined, the rookie is getting his first real taste of what it means to be a primary offensive option in the NBA. Between the increased defensive attention and greater responsibility of creating offense, it's been a challenging adjustment for an otherwise impressive rookie.

Thursday's matchup presents an opportunity for Lendeborg to regain his rhythm as he goes up against familiar opponent in fellow rookie Cam Boozer. The Warriors narrowly lost their first meeting against Memphis earlier this season, with Lendeborg finishing that game with 18 points to Boozer's 25 points. If Golden State hopes to end its losing streak, getting Lendeborg going offensively while setting the tone defensively from the opening tip will be key to closing out this road trip on a positive note.

Enjoy the game Dub Nation. GO WARRIORS!!!

Warriors: LJ Cryer, Brandin Podziemski, Will Richard, Yaxel Lendeborg, Al Horford

Grizzlies: Ty Jerome, Cedric Coward, Jaylen Wells, Cameron Boozer, Zach Edey

Who: Golden State Warriors (8 – 7) vs. Memphis Grizzlies (11 – 4)

When: Thursday, November 19th, at 5:00 p.m. PT
