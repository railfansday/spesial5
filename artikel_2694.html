Five-star Leipzig recover from Augsburg's fast start to extend perfect home record

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Despite conceding goals in the first and last minutes of the game, RB Leipzig managed to maintain their perfect home record with a 5-2 win over FC Augsburg.

Augsburg scored the fastest goal in Bundesliga history during the Bavarian derby earlier this season, and they once again led inside the first minute against another Champions League club. Willi Orban’s 400th competitive game for RB Leipzig started negatively as his mistake initiated the opportunity for Manuel Baum's men.

Maarten Vandevoordt stopped Arijon Ibrahimović's initial attempt, but Anton Kade scored from the rebound for his second goal in three games. The hosts responded well as they tried to create chances at the other end before Rodrigo Ribeiro's strike from an offside position.

Augsburg, who had issues keeping leads this term, restricted the hosts to half chances after that, but they once again crumbled just ten minutes before the half-time break. First, Ezekiel Banzuzi tied the score with a measured low effort from outside the box.

Brajan Gruda provided the assist after a mazy dribble, and he did it again a minute later with another impressive play down the right flank. Christopher Nkunku has the easiest job to tap it in from close range after Gruda's chipped assist.
