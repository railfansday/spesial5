'You're not cut out to be a football coach': Radio host slams Deion Sanders

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

It seems like ages ago when Deion Sanders was the hottest name in college football. But sports is always a "what have you done for me lately" industry. And in the case of Coach Prime and the Colorado Buffaloes, the answer is "not much."

Deion Sanders burst onto the scene at Colorado with his son Shedeur at quarterback and became the most talked about team in the country thanks to surprising victories early in his first season. In spite of several losses that followed, Sanders was named Sports Illustrated's "Sports Person of the Year" in 2023 in spite of his 4-8 record. His only winning season actually came the next year in 2024 when Travis Hunter won the Heisman and the Buffaloes went 9-4.

Since then, Colorado has gone 7-16 in the last two seasons. But when asked this week about his future, Deion Sanders gave the impression that he was truly not bothered by on-field results, whether good or bad.

"Do you think that bothers me? Do you think that causes me to lose sleep or rest? You think that affects my life? I'm here on assignment for these kids. If I'm not here longer, I'll move on to God's next assignment. So, that don't affect me like it may affect you or others. I'm in the third quarter of life. I may be losing games, but I'm winning life," Sanders said.

That didn't sit well with a slew of people both locally and nationally, including Rob Parker of Fox Sports Radio. Parker suggested that the comments could lead to the end of the road for Deion Sanders at Colorado and said it shows that he's not fit for the job anymore.

"If losing doesn't bother you and that's what he said, 'I don't lose any sleep over this. You might lose sleep over it.' What a slap in the face to the players and the fans, people at the school who went out and signed him and gave him this big contract and put his kid's name up and retired his number and all this stuff. It's the ultimate slap in the face that says you don't matter. The only thing that matters here is me and my life is good and I don't care. That's what he said. It might matter to you, but it don't matter to me. I sleep at night. I don't care. I'm winning," Parker said.

"How selfish is that? Deion's one of the all-time great athletes, ok? Pro Football Hall of Famer, played Major League Baseball at a high level, but to be dismissive of the whole notion on why we play the game? I'm telling you, if I'm the AD and I heard that, I'm like 'Deion, thank you. Thank you very much, appreciate what you did for Colorado bringing all the rappers here and Shannon Sharpe, thank you. But dude, you're not cut out to be a football coach where losing doesn't bother you.' I thought it was disrespectful and a disregard for Co lorado football."

Deion Sanders brought a huge cultural impact and a brought lot of revenue and exposure to Colorado. But that's when things were going well. It's been a long time since the primetime television windows, pregame shows, and celebrities were showing up in Boulder. And if the Buffaloes do decide to move on from Coach Prime, it's going to cost them maybe even more money in a massive potential buyout.
