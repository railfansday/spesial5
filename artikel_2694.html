Fantasy Basketball Rankings (Points leagues): Early-season surprises climb in rest-of-season value

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Here is the points-league version of my weekly risers and fallers list. Rankings are for rest-of-season points leagues, blending recent performance with what I expect going forward and accounting for injuries, so some fallers rank above their numbers (I expect a bounce back) and some hot starters below theirs (I expect regression). Let's get into it!

This one is almost all scoring. Dosunmu is at 21.9 points over his last seven games but only 3.1 rebounds and 2.7 assists. On the season, he's at 18.6 points, 4.6 rebounds and 3.4 assists in 15 games, filling in admirably for Anthony Edwards. He's averaging 32.27 FPPG, good for 67th in points leagues. Since Edwards will be out for a couple of months, the scoring and production should stick. I have him 63rd, and while that's growth, his efficiency isn't factored in, so if you trade for him, make sure it's in 9-cat formats.

The return of Jalen Suggs isn't phasing Black's production. Black is providing a mix of rebounds, assists, points and stocks, elevating him to a top-50 asset in points leagues. He's been Orlando's second-best player, an unexpected turn considering the Magic declined his team option earlier this season. Paolo Banchero could return by December, which would surely impact his numbers. For now, fantasy managers should enjoy the pace he's on for a career year, averaging 36.24 FPPG.

The second-year forward has stepped up over the past week, averaging 18-10-5 with almost 2 stocks per game. He's averaging 33.81 FPPG this season, with his rebounding being the real differentiator in his fantasy value for points leagues. He's up to 8.8 a night, and second on the team behind Cameron Boozer. I can see his rank holding long term because he touches multiple categories despite seeing under 20% usage.

Fears just gave us a look at his ceiling. He posted a 19-10-10 triple-double on Monday. He's started in every game for the Pelicans with a strong 25.6% usage rate. Dejounte Murray's trade request puts Fears in the lead guard role, and he already leads New Orleans in assists at 5.5 a game. Over his last eight, he's at 17.9 points, 6.1 assists and 5.4 rebounds. I have him 78th, and he should keep climbing with Murray likely playing his last game for New Orleans. If you can buy, do it.

Wagner is on a tear right now, averaging 28.7 points, 9.2 assists and 7.7 rebounds on 51% shooting. He's fifth in the NBA in assists at 8.3 a game and second in the league in minutes played at 36.8 and carrying a hefty 30.4% usage rate. His 4.3 turnovers cost him a few points a night, but his assists more than make up for it. I have him 21st, up from 39th. It'll be interesting to see how point Franz and the Magic offense looks when Paolo returns.

Murray asked the Pelicans for a trade on Nov. 13 and sat that night with a "left ankle sprain". His agent, Rich Paul, is already on the campaign trail, trying to get Murray to a contender. I doubt we'll see him play for the Pelicans again, as they've been better off without him. His trade value is pretty low, so fantasy managers will likely have to hold or include him in a multi-player package at a discount. In his last five games, he's at 10.0 points, 3.6 rebounds and 3.2 assists, with his usage trending down to 20% this season. Even a trade doesn't guarantee he'll be better, as ESPN's Brian Windhorst says he's viewed as a distressed asset.

It's been a rough stretch for Hart, posting 6.5 points, 6.3 rebounds and 3.5 assists through 11 games, down from 12-7-5 last year in almost the same minutes (29.8, versus 30.2). The last 14 days are the worst of it: 4.5 rebounds, 2.8 assists and 2.8 points in four games. His 12.6% usage rate is his lowest in four seasons and one of the lowest in the league for a 30-minute player. I have him 105th, down from 80th. Drop him in 10-team leagues and hold him in deeper ones since his rebounds, assists and steals are still valuable for fantasy. He also won't shoot 34% all season.

I dropped Green in a 12-team points league and I have no regrets. His fantasy value is contingent on his scoring and he's averaging a disappointing 12.5 points over his last 8 games, with just 3.3 rebounds and 2.5 assists. He's playing a shade over 26 minutes a night, shooting below his career numbers with no sign of a course correction in sight. I'm at 134th, down from 110th. Hold only in 14-team or deeper leagues, but he looks like he's losing his grip on his role with this team.

Pritchard and White are putting up almost the same line, and neither is a points-league difference-maker. Pritchard is at 13.9 points, 4.4 assists and 3.4 rebounds over seven games (13.6, 3.9 and 3.2 on the season). His 21% usage rate is consistent with last season. The difference has been his shot profile. He's taking two fewer shots per game, but he's getting up 3s at a 7% higher rate than last year. His assist rate is also down, so it's likely still an adjustment period with Tatum taking on more playmaking. I'd buy low.

White is down 21 spots, and it's a floor-without-a-ceiling problem. He's at 14.3 points, 4.7 assists and 2.9 rebounds over seven games and 12.7 points, 4.8 rebound and 3.0 assists on the season, with a 19.5% usage rate that leaves little room to grow. Like Pritchard, his shot volume is down and it's led to his scoring dropping from nearly 17 to 13 points per game. The stocks aren't as prevalent either, which has been his hallmark.  I have him 87th, down from 66th. Hold, but I wouldn't count on more in points leagues. He's always been a player who is better suited for 9-cat.
