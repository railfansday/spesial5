‘It’s not Madden’: Les Snead explains why Rams are reluctant to add WR help

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Los Angeles Rams have been dealt a terrible hand at wide receiver due to all the injuries suffered. Puka Nacua is out for the year and Davante Adams is set to miss his fifth straight game with a hamstring injury. Even Jordan Whittington landed on IR, and though not a wide receiver, Terrance Ferguson is out for the year, too.

Yet, despite all these absences, the Rams have yet to add any legitimate pass-catching help. No trades, no free-agent signings. Nothing outside of a practice squad poaching to bring in Xavier Smith and traditional internal promotions.

Many fans have wondered why the Rams are so reluctant to bring in some outside help, and Les Snead shed a little light on the matter during his appearance on the "Coach McVay Show" with J.B. Long.

Snead was asked if there's any consideration given to signing a free agent like Brandin Cooks, and though he won't rule anything out, he emphasized how difficult it is to onboard new receivers.

"We'll always do that. What we're mindful of now is, because we've had so much disruption, is not continuing to just engineer more disruption by bringing new in," Snead said. "Right now, we have who we have, they know what we're trying to do. Now we just need to get in a rhythm. Sometimes bringing in new parts is what they are: new parts. And if you do that, you have to start getting back into a rhythm. We've spent a lot of the first half of the season trying to get there. It's taken a little bit longer because of the disruption, so we'll be mindful of not engineering those disruptions. Like I tell people all the time, it's not Madden. You don't just acquire someone – in Madden, you don't have to teach them the playbook. It's like, when you make that trade, they just seem to know the plays. You get my point."

In addition to Cooks, Brandon Aiyuk also became available this week. The Commanders are the front runners to bring in Aiyuk, but now that he's a free agent, any team can sign him – including the Rams.

But based on Snead's comments, it seems unlikely that the Rams will make that call to Aiyuk or any other wideout on the market.
