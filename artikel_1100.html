If Wales need any extra motivation before tonight's clash with Portugal, they only need to look a mile down the road. Three days after the country's rugby union players delivered a stunning upset to beat New Zealand at the Principality Stadium, the country's footballers will be aiming to produce more heroics in Cardiff.

Granted, there is not as much riding on this Nations League clash at the Cardiff City Stadium, with Wales guaranteed to finish bottom of Group A4 and set for a promotion/relegation play-off next March. But if Craig Bellamy's side were to win, it might even trump last month's brilliant victory over Norway here.

It would be even more impressive given that influential midfielder Ethan Ampadu is suspended and full-back Neco Williams is a fitness doubt. Meanwhile, Ben Cabango and Sorba Thomas both return from their respective bans.

Portugal had already sealed their place in the quarter-finals before Saturday's game against Denmark, but the 4-1 defeat on home soil would have stung nonetheless.

The scoreline and result was out of keeping with a side who had won all four Nations League matches up to that point, as they look to retain a trophy they have already lifted twice in its short history.

Jorge Jesus' team just need to avoid defeat to top the group, or for Denmark to drop points against Norway, but they will be without suspended duo Ruben Dias and Joao Palhinha, as well as injured midfielder Bernardo Silva.

Portugal should bounce back from Saturday's result with a victory, but the manner of their defeat showed they can be vulnerable.
