Joe Burrow's second touchdown pass gives Bengals 17-0 lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Joe Burrow tossed his second touchdown of the game, hitting running back Chase Brown for a 2-yard score, putting Cincinnati's lead at 17-0.

Ja'Marr Chase is putting together another spectacular game, making a downfield catch for 53 yards to put Cincinnati in scoring position on the team's latest drive. He already has 135 yards on six catches with 5:26 left in the first half.

Burrow then hit Tee Higgins for a 13-yard gain down to the 3-yard line. But Higgins had to come off the field with an apparent leg issue.

Still, Burrow found Brown on the left side a couple of plays later for the score.

Cincinnati has 18 first downs and 280 total yards.

The Steelers have one first down and 14 total yards. Aaron Rodgers has not even completed a pass, starting the game 0-of-7.

The Bengals could've had more points, but Evan McPherson missed his first kick of the season. His 43-yard field goal attempt sailed wide right on Cincinnati's third possession.
