Bayern Munich News: Arsenal wants Michael Olise; FC Barcelona eyeing Man City’s Josko Gvardiol?; and MORE!

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Bayern Munich star Michael Olise will continue to be one of the most sought-after and speculated-upon players in the world until he signs a contract extension or announces his intention to transfer.

One club that is keeping a close eye on the Frenchman is Arsenal, which is looking to bring in a blockbuster-type player:

One Bundesliga superstar Arsenal are keeping tabs on is Michael Olise – despite not being a left-winger. More precisely, the Gunners are attentive to his contract situation and his impending negotiations with Bayern.

The Bavarians are desperate to hand the France international improved terms in order to guarantee that he will stay at the club beyond 2027. Olise has fielded interest from the likes of Real Madrid, PSG and Liverpool in recent months, hence the urgency.

We can confirm that a release clause will be one of the key points under discussion. Olise does not currently have one, but Bayern may have to compromise in order to get him to extend. The Munich outfit previously had a no-release-clause policy, but they have been forced to soften this stance in recent seasons, with both Harry Kane and Jamal Musiala – their best-paid players – having exit mechanisms in their contracts.

Given Arsenal's financial strength and willingness to pay whatever it takes for players of the calibre of Vini Jr and Olise, a release clause could make a transfer to North London possible even if he were to sign a new deal.

However, it must be emphasised that this is all hypothetical right now. Arsenal are not actively pursuing the 24-year-old at this stage; they are just keeping themselves informed on his situation.

Arsenal can join Real Madrid, Liverpool, Paris Saint-Germain, and the rest in line to take a run at Olise. Bayern Munich will not flinch until the summer, when extensive talks are expected to take place. In this case, it at least appears that everyone understands Olise will not be leaving midseason. If he decides that he wants to leave Bayern Munich, that is where it will all get interesting. With a contract that runs through 2029, the Bavarians would have some decisions to make at that point.

However, there is also the possibility that he signs a contract extension—a proposition that most Bayern Munich fans would prefer.

Manchester City defender Josko Gvardiol is on the radar of Barcelona, according to AS. Gvardiol, 24, has become one of the best young defenders in Europe in recent years and Barca are keen to strengthen their defensive options. However, while the club are interested in signing the Croatia international, they do not think a deal will be possible unless he pushes to leave amid the fallout from City's financial charges.
