NFL playoff picture after the Browns join the party in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

We have a new team in the playoff party after Sunday's action.

Week 10 of the 2026 NFL season is not over yet, but after a thrilling win over the Houston Texans, the Cleveland Browns are now on the No. 7 line in the AFC. If the playoffs began today, Cleveland would be on the road to take on the Kansas City Chiefs on Wild Card Weekend.

While there is a long way to go until matchups are officially set, this is another example of why the Browns are one of the biggest surprises this season.

Here is the full playoff picture as Week 10 draws to a close.

Here is the AFC playoff picture following all the Sunday games, including the big matchup between the Cincinnati Bengals and the Pittsburgh Steelers on Sunday night:

1. Buffalo Bills (8-1)2. Kansas City Chiefs (7-2)3. Jacksonville Jaguars (7-2)4. Cincinnati Bengals (7-2)5. Denver Broncos (7-2)6. Pittsburgh Steelers (6-3)7. Cleveland Browns (6-4)

8. New England Patriots (5-5)9. Baltimore Ravens (4-5)10. Las Vegas Raiders (4-6)11. Houston Texans (3-6)12. Indianapolis Colts (3-7)13. New York Jets (2-8)14. Miami Dolphins (1-8)15. Tennessee Titans (1-8)16. Los Angeles Chargers (0-8)

The Kansas City Chiefs now occupy that slot, thanks to a win over the Atlanta Falcons. That pulled the Chiefs to 7-2 and level with the idle Denver Broncos in the AFC West, as well as the Jacksonville Jaguars and the Cincinnati Bengals. Kansas City first takes the top spot in the division thanks to a better record in AFC West games (3-1 for the Chiefs versus 2-1 for the Broncos), and then they take the No. 2 spot ahead of the Jaguars and the Bengals thanks to a better conference record (6-1 for Kansas City against 6-2 for the Jaguars and 5-2 for Cincinnati).

Jacksonville takes the No. 3 spot over Cincinnati due to a head-to-head win. Cincinnati slots in at No. 4, and the Broncos take the No. 5 spot.

The other big change? The New England Patriots fall out of the No. 7 spot after Sunday's loss to the Detroit Lions, and the Cleveland Browns are now in following a dramatic win over the Houston Texans.

Here is how things stand in the NFC following all the Week 10 action:

1. San Francisco 49ers (8-1)2. Detroit Lions (7-2)3. New York Giants (5-3-1)4. Carolina Panthers (4-5)5. Minnesota Vikings (7-2)6. Chicago Bears (7-2)7. Green Bay Packers (6-4)

8. Seattle Seahawks (6-4)9. Los Angeles Rams (6-4)10. Dallas Cowboys (4-6)11. Washington Commanders (3-5-1)12. Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-6)13. New Orleans Saints (3-6)14. Atlanta Falcons (3-7)15. Arizona Cardinals (3-7)16. Philadelphia Eagles (2-7)

With the win over the Dallas Cowboys, the San Francisco 49ers maintain the top spot in the conference. Then things get interesting at No. 2 where three teams — Detroit, Minnesota, and Chicago — have matching 7-2 records. But the Lions have wins over both the Vikings and the Bears, Detroit takes the No. 2 spot. Minnesota slides into the No. 5 ahead of Chicago at No. 6, as the Vikings beat the Bears earlier this season.

Despite the loss to the Vikings, the Green Bay Packers remain in the seventh and final playoff spot. The Packers, Seahawks, and Rams all sit at 6-4, so under tiebreaker rules we first deal with the two NFC West teams. Seattle has the edge over Los Angeles right now thanks to a better division record, so the Rams drop out. Then between Seattle and Green Bay the Packers have the edge, thanks to a better record in NFC games.

Seattle takes No. 8 ahead of the Rams, thanks to Seattle's record in division games.
