NFL will consider requiring minimum-night stays for international games

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

After the 49ers and the Rams took dramatically different approaches to their arrival in Melbourne for the first-ever Australia game, we raised the question of whether the league should require teams to get there early enough to advance the goal of promoting American football to international audiences.

Via The Athletic, the NFL will consider adopting a minimum-night stay for teams playing internationally.

“Currently, we leave those football decisions on when the team arrives in the market to the club,” NFL executive V.P. of club business, major events, and international Peter O’Reilly told The Athletic. “It is something that we’ll consider moving forward on: Do we put some minimum nights in a market in there? It’s something we’ll look at.”

The league already does it for the Super Bowl, mandating a full-week experience that includes multiple press conferences, the annual “Opening Night” media extravaganza, and practicing at designated facilities.

“Having access to players and that type of experience when they’re in the market does have a lasting impact,” O’Reilly said. “That’s a factor when you start to think about encouraging or potentially looking at a policy where you’d want a club in the market for more than the day before.”

Beyond impacting strategic considerations, it would add to the total expense. The money would be worth it, if it further enhances the league’s overall reason for going global.
