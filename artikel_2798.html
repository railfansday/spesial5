Frimpong in contention for Liverpool vs Manchester United as Iraola assesses squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

That was more like it. Liverpool snapped a two-game losing streak with an ultimately commanding Premier League win at Crystal Palace on Sunday afternoon.

Andoni Iraola and his squad will now look to capitalize on that result when they return from the international break to host fierce North West rivals Manchester United the following Sunday.

Liverpool's recent record against Michael Carrick's relegation-threatened side makes for grim reading, with the Reds winning just one of their last seven encounters and none of the previous three.

Here, we take a look at the potential changes Iraola could make when his side return to action.

**Leoni close**, reports the Liverpool Echo.

Iraola will be desperately hoping for a clean bill of health during the next fortnight with so many of his players away on international duty.

The last international break saw Alexander Isak, Cody Gakpo and Dominik Szoboszlai all return with injuries, which played a significant role in the Reds' 3-0 home defeat to United's neighbors City.

The Liverpool manager will be encouraged, however, by the prospect of an improved defensive roster, with right-back Jeremie Frimpong expected to return to availability after sitting out the last six fixtures. Ronald Araujo has primarily filled the position, with Joe Gomez providing additional cover.

Conor Bradley has been sidelined since January with a serious knee injury.

Another long-term absentee, center-back Giovanni Leoni, has been scheduled for a return to training during the international break, though it will be some time before he is match-ready.

Jeremy Jacquet and captain Virgil van Dijk have established themselves as the primary partnership at center-back, with Araujo and Gomez providing additional options.

Alisson Becker showed at Palace why he remains the undisputed first-choice goalkeeper ahead of Giorgi Mamardashvili and Freddie Woodman, while Milos Kerkez and Kostas Tsimikas continue to compete for the left-back position with neither fully establishing themselves this season.

The Liverpool midfield has become a source of fascination for supporters, particularly regarding the two deeper roles.

Alexis Mac Allister has established himself as a guaranteed starter, but the position alongside him has seen significant rotation, driven partly by injuries.

Ryan Gravenberch has missed the last two games with a heel issue while Szoboszlai was substituted in the second half at Palace after extending his knee.

Teenager Trey Nyoni made his first Premier League start in the loss to Arsenal earlier this month and has stood out in both the League Cup and Champions League, and is challenging both Gravenberch and Szoboszlai. Florian Wirtz remains the undisputed starter at number 10, though the necessity of managing the Germany international's workload has seen Gravenberch, Szoboszlai and James McConnell deputize in the role.

Defensive midfielder Wataru Endo, however, has yet to feature for a single minute this season.

Iraola has made a habit of rotating his attacking line in recent weeks, and with a grueling stretch of nine games in 28 days looming before Christmas, that trend shows no signs of letting up.

Isak remains the first-choice striker and has now tallied eight goals this season, with Gakpo, Lewis Koumas and Bradley Barcola all having occupied the position at various points during the campaign.

Gakpo, Barcola and Ngumoha have also seen regular action on the wings, with Victor Munoz providing an additional option on the right side. Koumas has also earned a start out wide.

Federico Chiesa failed to make the matchday squad at Palace, while Hugo Ekitike remains sidelined with an Achilles injury sustained back in April. Jayden Danns has featured among the substitutes in Champions League action.
