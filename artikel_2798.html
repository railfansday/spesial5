Some musing on the Bills and their recent and upcoming games

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

This week was a bit wacky for me (had a lot of those lately, sorry). I wasn't able to get anything down to recap the win over the New York Jets before midweek hit but had some various thoughts on things from that game and others as well as the upcoming ones that I felt might make for good conversation points. And if not, it'll make for great conversation starters on telling me why I suck. Let's do it!

No, seriously. In fact, if you were casually scanning the box score there's quite a bit of "evidence" that the Jets had as good of a game as the Bills even. That brings me to one of my favorite Skare-isms; The only stat that matters is the score, and everything else is just context. Yes they had the same number of first downs, nearly equal yards, and the Jets even had slightly better third down conversion rate (both teams were lousy), and a significantly higher time of possession. But none of those things win the game.

Both teams had eleven drives. The Jets scored on four of them, and the Bills on five. Which again sounds like a close game. How do these stats add context to the only real stat of the "score?" When it mattered, the Jets' scoring drives ended in field goals. All but one of the Bills' scoring drives were touchdowns. You put all that context together and that's how you translate a box score into a blowout.

Yeah I know, we blew out the Vikings, how could a middle tier quarterback take a dominant win away from the Buffalo Bills? Well it's simple, I think Carson Wentz was truly THAT bad against us. Even a mediocre quarterback would have drastically increased their odds. Heck, even a bad quarterback would have significantly increased their odds. And Cousins is a good quarterback.

You might point out that we already beat Kirk Cousins once this year against the Las Vegas Raiders. You might also remember he dropped 30 on our defense. Further, we only put up 23 against the Vikings. Now I want to be clear. None of that guarantees a 30 – 23 loss if Cousins was on the Vikings when we played them. Our defense (see below) does seem to be improving and maybe we don't allow 30 to Cousins.

So I can't definitively say this as anything other than opinion that I think we lose that game against Cousins, but it's hard to argue they don't have a much better shot.

I'm going to do a deeper dive for my opponent preview but I've already taken a preliminary look at the Miami Dolphins' overall stats. With the point spread at seemingly ludicrous levels for this week I wanted to see if there was reason to think it was… well, reasonable. I think it might be.

Based on the heading you might immediately think "Los Angeles Chargers" and that's not a bad take. Both are now sitting at 1 – 8 and I think we've got a pretty good race on our hands. So let's go to rate metrics as our tiebreaker!

On defense, the Chargers rank 17 in points per drive. That's average by any measure. The Dolphins? They rank 29th which puts them in the "terrible" grouping per my rule of four. Now here's where the conversation will become divided, and rightfully so. The Dolphins stay put at 29th, or "terrible" in their scoring per drive rate. Not to be outdone though, the Chargers are dead last in scoring output.

So which team you taking for the top of the "Crap-O-Meter?" For my money, I stick with my rule of four. With the Chargers having an average defense and Miami scoring terrible on both sides of the ball I gotta go with the Dolphins. If there's one thing we can all agree on though, is that it's fun that we have two games against Miami to go.

You gotta play the teams on it and that's all there is to it. Have the Bills had some lucky breaks this season? Oh god yes. You already know how I feel about being the lucky winner of the "playing against Carson Wentz" sweepstakes. The Ravens should always be taken seriously, except when they're down to their third string quarterback about 42 seconds into the game. And again, by the end of it we'll have had three games against teams that are a combined 2 – 16 at the moment.

It's not like the Jets are a juggernaut and we have one more against them, the New England Patriots seem to be stumbling hard (lol), and we're already at eight wins. Things are looking great for Buffalo and yes, the schedule will almost certainly be a story come season's end.

But opponent fan bases can still bite me if they think I'm going to feel bad about it. We have also the Chicago Bears, the Green Bay Packers, Kansas City, and the Denver Broncos still ahead. We've already outgunned two 7 – 2 teams including dropping more points on a superb Vikings defense than anyone and a six-win Los Angeles Rams team.

Honestly, our schedule is a pretty mixed bag with only a few teams I'd put in the "meh" category. So sure, we'll stack some wins against the dregs but we've been also shown up against the elites. Which brings me to…

I think we should be as serious as we have been for a few years now. Yeah, I'm worried about the defense. I was worried LAST year and there's no objective argument to currently say that we're in better shape on that side of the ball this year. We COULD be by the end of it with Amani Hooker sliding right in and hopefully the entire team playing a bit more comfortably as the weeks roll by.

And like I said above, there's no one we CAN'T beat. NO ONE! I'm not claiming unbeatable. There are games that have me worried. There are teams that have me worried. I'm not so worried that I plan on going into a game all doom and gloom though.

Further, I've already hinted at this but we have a shot at the one seed. Matt Warren's already made content to this effect but if we take care of the games we should at the moment that puts us at a 12-win season (Jets, Pats, Miami x 2). That leaves us with four games that aren't "should win" but are also not "should lose" aka "could win." Splitting those is a 14-win season and that's a great shot at the one seed.

Now the competition is tight with a staggering four teams only a game behind us. Two of which can get a head-to-head win over us during our Holiday jaunts (Kansas City and Denver). That's not ideal, but only if THEY get the win. If we get the head-to-head win that's 100% ideal.

Realistically we just need to keep pace in wins to take it and there's some good news.

Kansas City has to face not just us but the Cincinnati Bengals (see below), the Rams, and the San Franciso 49ers so they don't have easy sledding either.

The Bengals are also one game behind us with the aforementioned game against Kansas City and two games against the six-win Cleveland Browns.

Jacksonville might be a little more of a worry with games against the Bears and Pittsburgh Steelers the biggest obstacles. If they go on a run they could be trouble.

Denver might be the biggest concern again this year and winning on Christmas would be huge for the Bills. The Broncos have games against the Steelers and us. They need to play the Raiders twice and they've been plucky at times but just traded away their quarterback. Making them a bigger concern for our one seed shot is that the Denver Broncos have that same nagging "schedule" complaint that fans are lobbing at us. Games against the Jets, Dolphins, and Chargers should be wins.

So what do I see? A prediction would be a nice way to wrap up an article like this one I think. I don't see a reason the Bills don't win the games they should win. I won't be so specific as to say which games for all four, but I really like our chances against Denver, and I'll call that a win and at least one more of the tougher ones. Bills go at least 14 – 3 in my opinion.
