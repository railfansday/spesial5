Ranking the 10 most expensive teenage Real Madrid signings ahead of JJ Gabriel move

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

JJ Gabriel’s prolonged move from Manchester United to Real Madrid looks close to being completed, but the much-hyped teenager is not the first of his age group to head to the Spanish capital.

For a club that usually buys ready-to-go superstars, Real Madrid has a surprisingly deep history of signing teenagers, often picking the best talents from across the globe.

It hasn’t always been a success though, with the likes of Martin Odegaard never really settling in, but while the now Arsenal captain was at least cheap, there have been other expensive failures.

Here are the 10 most expensive teenagers signed in Real Madrid’s history, ranked on their impact for the club.

Madrid paid €30m for the opportunity to never play Reinier.

He arrived in January 2020 from Flamengo and was immediately put in the reserve squad.

For the start of the following season, he was loaned out to Dortmund, where he stayed for two years but managed just one league goal in his time.

A return to Spain saw him move to Girona, where he managed two goals in half a season, and he spent the second part of that campaign at Frosinone in Serie A.

His final loan stint came at Granada, where he was equally poor, and Madrid cut their losses, allowing his contract to expire. He is now playing for Atletico Mineiro in Brazil.

Madrid spent big money to entice Hernandez from their city rivals Atletico, but the full-back never really made an impact at the Bernabeu.

He only played 23 matches, of which just 13 were in the league, and was second fiddle to Marcelo.

He found more success elsewhere, particularly at Milan, but it did not work out at Madrid.

Mastantuono was given a chance in the Madrid first team last season, but it looks like he failed to impress those in charge, as he has been sent on loan to Fiorentina.

Madrid spent €45m to bring him to the club from River Plate and put the 18-year-old straight into the first team, allowing him to make 23 La Liga appearances.

He managed just a single goal in that time, but he is still only 19, so could well resurrect his Madrid career after his loan away.

Of Madrid’s teenage attacking talents, Endrick is the one who has not really made an impact.

Breaking into a frontline that consisted of Vinicius Junior, Rodrygo and a newly arrived Kylian Mbappe was always going to be tough, but Endrick did not make the most of his limited opportunities.

He featured in 22 La Liga matches in the 2024/25 season but managed just one goal. He also scored only one in nine Champions League games.

His best form came in the Copa del Rey, with five goals in six games, but Madrid allowed him to go out on loan last winter when he moved to Lyon.

The Spanish giants clearly still believe in him, as they refused to let him leave permanently, but the Brazilian needs to prove that was the correct decision.

Solid would be a fair way to describe Camavinga at Madrid.

He is the team’s ultimate utility man and has been asked to play left-back, defensive midfielder and central midfielder. Perhaps that flexibility has been why he has struggled to nail down a starting spot.

He has made 11 league appearances this season, of which just two were starts, and his time on the pitch each game is barely more than 10 minutes long.

Still, he’s a decent squad player to have.

Arda Guler is just a fun footballer to watch.

Madrid paid the relatively cheap €30m to bring that fun to the club in 2023 and while it has taken him a while to settle in, he is now one of the most important members of the team.

His first year was disrupted by injuries and it was not until the 2025/26 season that he started to look like a Madrid player.

This year, he has once again gone up a level and provided three goals and four assists in his 13 La Liga matches.

And if you haven’t seen his goal for Turkey recently, treat yourself.

€125m is clearly a lot of money, but the early signs are good for Diomande.

He has eight goal involvements in his 16 games so far, and a timely injury to Vinicius Junior has provided him with a platform on which to shine.

He’s only just turned 20, but there is real talk in Madrid over who is now the starting left winger.

He goes under the radar considering the other attacking talent Madrid have had, but Rodrygo has been a very good signing.

He has scored a steady amount of goals since becoming a first-team regular in 2021 and can play anywhere across the frontline.

That versatility and ability has been missed since he suffered a cruciate ligament tear in March.

It’s hard to judge just where Vinicius Junior belongs on this list.

He was absolutely brilliant for a long time, but there has been a definite drop-off this season. The fact he is producing that form while being paid €600k a week will not go unnoticed.

Before writing off his career as a whole, he has been a clear success. 129 goals and 103 assists in 387 games, including 37 match-winners, show the impact he has had.

His best moment came in 2022 when he scored the only goal of the game as Madrid beat Liverpool in the Champions League final.

It was an easy pick for number one. There may be other more talented players on this list, but Madrid would pay €27m for that goal in Lisbon alone.

When Madrid needed digging out of a hole, it was often Ramos that provided it. During his Madrid career, he scored seven goals beyond the 90-minute mark. A reminder that he was supposed to be a centre-back.

Away from the last-minute heroics, Ramos was the captain of one of the most successful periods in Madrid’s history. Sure, he loved a red card, but for every one of those, you would get 20 excellent performances.

He is not just one of Madrid’s best teenage purchases, but one of their best purchases full stop.
