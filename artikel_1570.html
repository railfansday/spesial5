Report: Liverpool refuse to close door on €40m defender deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Liverpool need to upgrade their team soon.

Andoni Iraola has a lot of work that he still needs to do on his squad and, without this extra work, they're going to fall even further behind their title rivals.

Obviously, finishing fifth last season didn't give the players as much of a boost as people would have hoped with many fans believing that a Premier League title charge could be on.

Bradley Barcola was their marquee signing and, if we're being honest, his performances have left a lot to be desired.

The fact that £123 million was spent on him while the rest of Liverpool's team still needed upgrading felt a bit strange with questions being asked about whether the Frenchman was even worth that much.

Despite this huge outlay of money, even more upgrades are needed with the Anfield outfit now standing their ground and not giving up on a €40m deal yet.

Liverpool are refusing to rule out a €40m deal for Alejandro Balde

Left-back is another major issue for Liverpool at the moment.

The Reds are struggling to figure out who's the better player to use out of Milos Kerkez and Kostas Tsimikas because, when one plays, fans wish that the other was on the pitch and vice versa.

Their lack of form has led to many questions being asked about the future of Liverpool's defence with questions about whether spending another €40 million + on a full-back would be worthwhile.

At the moment, several players keep popping up as potential options for the Reds to explore with FC Barcelona's Alejandro Balde among the strongest candidates.

However, with almost every other Premier League club also wanting to sign him, securing a deal for the Spaniard is going to be rather difficult.

So, that's why even though Barcelona and Jorge Mendes expect Balde to earn around €40 million for the La Liga outfit, Liverpool still haven't ruled themselves out of the move.

"Liverpool are also keeping tabs on the player. The sporting directors at Anfield are aware of the conditions set by Mendes and haven't ruled out further negotiations, although they are still evaluating their options for the January transfer window," SPORT wrote.

Alejandro Balde could become Liverpool's first-choice left-back

Liverpool have already signed one player from Barcelona this season in Ronald Araujo.

The Uruguayan is currently on a season-long loan to the 2024-25 Premier League winners and, even though they have an option to buy included in the deal, the €55 million fee that Barcelona want is expensive.

Indeed, he has brought a lot of stability to Iraola's first-team squad but with him being 27 years old and his injury history not being the best, a €55 million transfer is a huge risk.

That's why Liverpool's options for left-back should try to include players who can also play at right-back, which Balde can do.

Versatility is massive in this day and age and, even though a left-footed player at right-back isn't ideal, anyone is better than Jeremie Frimpong.

Right now, it's unclear exactly what Liverpool's transfer plans are for the future but, if they don't include Balde, they'll be making a huge mistake.
