How to customize or disable the touchscreen on the new MacBook Pro

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

A headlining feature of Apple’s new MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display is the long-awaited addition of a touchscreen. However, not everyone has been so thrilled about this addition. Here’s how you can turn it off.

If you would rather disable or customize certain gestures rather than disable touch altogether, you can customize most gestures. Just choose the Touch Display option at the end of System Settings.
