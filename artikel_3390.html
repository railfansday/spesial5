Latin Grammys 2026 Winners: The Complete List

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

On Thursday night, Latin music’s biggest stars took over Las Vegas’ MGM Grand Garden Arena for the Latin Grammys, celebrating the best of 2026.

It’s no surprise that Rosalía made Latin Grammys history when she took home her third Album of the Year award after a night of several wins. Before she opened the ceremony with operatic flair, the Spanish superstar took home four trophies: Best Alternative Album for her showstopping album Lux, Best Pop Song for highlight “La Perla,” and Best Short Form Music Video for “Sauvignon Blanc.”

“The truth is I know I have a lot to learn. I know that I’ve probably made my best work but I know there’s still a lot left to be the best,” Rosalía said in her AOTY acceptance speech. “Thanks, mama, for making me this way. I’m going to bring this Grammy to your house. I don’t know if another one is going to fit in your house, but I’ll bring it to you anyways and see if you make room.You always find a way for anything,” she added.

In another emotional highlight, Karol G took home Best Urban Music Album for her 2025 LP, Tropicoqueta after being award Best Reggaeton Performance before the ceremony. “I’m going to very honest because God willing I’ve been up here a couple of times but this one is special,” the Colombian superstar said in her acceptance speech. “People always hear the end result but making Tropicoqueta meant a lot to me that people can’t even imagine.”

Karol continued: “The most important part is to have an album that I can tour because I feel when I’m singing it, people feel proud to be Latina and to carry their flags with pride. That’s the most beautiful part.” Before she walked off, Karol let out an impassioned yell: “Latina forever!”

Milo J had a big night as well, earning his first Latin Grammys ever and eventually walking home with four awards total. The 20 year-old Argentine musician nabbed Song of the Year for his track “Solifican12” and Best Folk Album for La Vida Era Más Corta.

Argentine duo Ca7riel and Paco Amoroso were early winners of the night, nabbing awards for “Best Electronic Music Performance” and “Best Pop/Rock Song.” With their wins, they helped both Fred Again.. and Sting become first-time Latin Grammy winners. Before the ceremony, superproducer Edgar Barrera led this year’s nominations with 13 nods. Barrera was recognized in the Producer of the Year category and in major categories like Album of the Year and Song of the Year for his work on albums like Karol G‘s Tropicoqueta and Carín León’s Muda, as well as Becky G and Santana’s powerful anti-ICE collaboration.

Trailing him were some of the biggest names in Latin music. Karol G, Rosalía, Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso and Jorge Drexler were all tied with seven nominations each, picking up nods in major categories.
