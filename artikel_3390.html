The massive success of this summer's The Odyssey, directed by Christopher Nolan, reignited interest in Greek mythology. During its theatrical run, the 2004 epic Troy and the Disney+ series Percy Jackson & the Olympians witnessed viewership spikes on streaming. The Odyssey was always expected to be a hit, but no one saw it becoming the highest-grossing hit of Nolan's career with a $1.8 billion worldwide haul. It's by far the highest-grossing period movie of all time, having generated more than three times as much at the global box office as Troy. It also vastly outperformed the last major blockbuster based on Greek mythology, which is currently streaming Paramount+ in the United States.

The movie won't, however, stick around for much longer. It was released in 2007 and directed by a filmmaker that Nolan would coincidentally collaborate with only a few years later. The filmmaker, Zack Snyder, was handpicked by Nolan to direct a grounded reboot of the Superman series, the blockbuster Man of Steel. By then, Snyder had established himself as an expert at comic book-inspired films and had earned uncommon creative freedom at Warner Bros.

Snyder broke out with a remake of Dawn of the Dead and went on to adapt the epic graphic novel Watchmen. He also directed the animated film Legend of the Guardians: The Owls of Ga'hoole and the original fantasy film Sucker Punch before landing the Man of Steel gig and subsequently shepherding the DC Extended Universe franchise. However, the movie that truly put him on the map as a visual stylist was an action epic inspired by Greek mythology.

You belong to Highgarden, where nobody raises their voice and everybody gets exactly what they came for. The Tyrells feed the realm, charm the realm and quietly steer the realm, and they have never once needed to win a battle to get their way.

We're talking, of course, about 300. Based on the classic graphic novel by Frank Miller, the movie was a breakout hit despite behind-the-scenes reservations from the studio. It defied the odds and emerged as a massive hit, grossing nearly $470 million worldwide against a reported budget of $65 million. By comparison, Troy grossed nearly $500 million against a reported budget of more than $150 million.

Starring Gerard Butler, Lena Headey, Dominic West, David Wenham, Rodrigo Santoro, and Michael Fassbender, 300 received mixed-to-positive reviews, with praise for its visuals and criticism for its writing. It now holds a 69% critics' score and an 89% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes, where the consensus describes it as "a simple-minded but visually exciting experience, full of blood, violence, and ready-made movie quotes." The movie spawned a sequel titled 300: Rise of an Empire and Snyder is currently working on a streaming spin-off series. You can watch 300 on Paramount+ until November 30 and stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
