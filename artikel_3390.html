‘The Rings of Power’ Considered Hundreds of Auditions Before Casting Celeborn

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Going from the Upside Down to Middle-earth might sound like a pretty significant career change, but if anybody can make the transition look effortless, it's probably Jamie Campbell Bower. After terrifying Netflix audiences as Vecna in Stranger Things, the actor has traded Hawkins for a very different fantasy world, joining the third season of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power. As it turns out, finding the right actor for the role was no easy feat.

Speaking exclusively with Collider's Maggie Lovitt for a new episode of our Behind the Scenes series, The Rings of Power co-showrunner Patrick McKay revealed that hundreds of actors auditioned for the role of Celeborn before Bower was selected to portray Galadriel's husband in Season 3, explaining that showrunner J.D. Payne had always intended to bring the character into the story in an unexpected way, but finding somebody capable of matching Morfydd Clark's performance as Galadriel was essential.

The casting process involved an extensive search led by casting director Theo Park, with a smaller group of candidates eventually invited to perform chemistry reads alongside Clark, but despite already being familiar with Bower's work, McKay admitted that the creative team didn't know whether they'd found their Celeborn until they actually met him.

"We always knew we wanted Celeborn to come back in an unexpected way and to be on Galadriel's journey. But then it comes time to make Season 3, and you've got to cast him. We work with a great casting director, Theo Park. We saw hundreds of tapes. She saw even more than that. And then we brought a few people in to do chemistry reads with Morfydd. We did not know Jamie. We knew and were fans of his work, and felt there was something there. But it wasn't until we walked in the room that we realized, we have a Celeborn, and this is it."

McKay explained that the experience reminded him of discovering Clark during the casting process for the show's first season, when the producers quickly realized they'd found the actress who could anchor their enormous fantasy series. "It was the same in Season 1 with casting Morfydd. We didn't know her; we had no idea. Then we met her, and within five minutes, it was like, we have a show," McKay told Collider. "And with Celeborn, it was scary. I mean, you're trying to match her energy. He has to be, you know, the yin to her yang in a way. You need to believe their chemistry and believe the history there. And you want someone who's going to bring something out in her. And you also want somebody who we all want to work with." He went on to sing Bower's praises saying:

"We work very closely with one another, and we don't suffer fools, and we don't need somebody who's not going to be able to integrate. But lucky for us, Jamie is a wonderful addition on screen and off. We just feel lucky we got him. It's the fact that he was interested in doing it and said yes, and we got really lucky."

When Lovitt pointed out that Bower seemed almost perfectly suited to playing an Elf, McKay couldn't help but agree. "Totally, he's such an elf. Yeah, the show has been waiting for him," he said.

Although Celeborn has been a significant figure in Tolkien's mythology for decades, The Rings of Power has spent its first two seasons exploring Galadriel's story largely without him. The character was previously mentioned as her missing husband, with Galadriel believing that he had been lost during the conflicts that preceded the show's events.

In Tolkien's writings, Celeborn is an important Elven lord who eventually rules Lothlórien alongside Galadriel. Movie audiences will also recognize the character from Peter Jackson's The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring and The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King, where he was portrayed by Marton Csokas.
