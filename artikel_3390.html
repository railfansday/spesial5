Michael B. Jordan Officially Replaces Pierce Brosnan in New Crime Thriller Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Michael B. Jordan is following in the footsteps of Steve McQueen and Pierce Brosnan in the new trailer for The Thomas Crown Affair. He's a billionaire by day and an art thief by night, but his double lives are about to collide. It's set to hit theaters on March 5, 2027.

The Thomas Crown Affair began as a 1968 heist thriller starring McQueen as bored playboy Crown, who robs banks to spice up his life. On his trail, and soon in his bed, is insurance investigator Viki Anderson (Faye Dunaway). Helmed by Canadian director Norman Jewison, the movie became one of McQueen's signature hits, and won a Best Original Song Oscar for "The Windmills of Your Mind." Die Hard's John McTiernan remade the film in 1999, with Brosnan as Crown, now an art thief, and Rene Russo as his nemesis-turned-lover Catherine Banning; Dunaway cameoed as a therapist. Both are now considered classics of the heist genre, giving Jordan a lot to live up to.

In the trailer for the new Amazon MGM Studios film, Crown (Jordan) is a billionaire who's acquired his incredible art collection, including a priceless Vincent van Gogh painting, via somewhat unconventional means...like rappelling up an entire skyscraper to steal it from the top floor. However, he may have met his match in a brilliant investigator (Adria Arjona) who sees through his facade: but is she friend or foe? One person who's definitely a foe is Kenneth Branagh, playing a man who's determined to bring down Crown, no matter the cost. The film also stars Lily Gladstone (Killers of the Flower Moon), Danai Gurira (Black Panther), Pilou Asbæk (Game of Thrones), and Aiysha Hart (A Discovery of Witches).

Your nemesis respects you, which sounds flattering until you work out what it means. He chose you because nobody else was worth his time.

Your nemesis has no personal interest in you whatsoever, and that is precisely what makes him unsurvivable. You are not a rival. You are a stop on the way.

In addition to starring, Jordan also directed the film; it's his second turn behind the camera after helming Creed III. The script is by Drew Pearce (The Fall Guy) and Jason Hall (American Sniper), based on the 1968 movie's screenplay by the late Alan R. Trustman; Kurt Wimmer and Leslie Dixon, who penned the 1999 remake, will also have screenwriting credits on the film. Oscar-winning musician Jon Batiste will score the film.

The Thomas Crown Affair will premiere in theaters on March 5, 2027. Stay tuned to Collider for future updates.
