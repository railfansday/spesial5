Robert Pattinson's James Bond Imagined In Gun-Slinging 007 Art

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Some moviegoers simply aren't waiting for Robert Pattinson's James Bond to arrive in theaters.

After much speculation by fans and the press and an intense casting race, it was announced on Monday of last week that Pattinson will be the new Bond in the upcoming Bond 26 movie from Amazon MGM Studios, with Denis Villeneuve in the director's chair. While Villeneuve is only confirmed to be a part of one Bond movie, Pattinson has already signed on for at least three.

The studio released a very short teaser featuring Pattinson in character to announce his casting. But unaffiliated creators are also at work, such as @cole on Instagram, who shared three concept art pieces of Pattinson as Bond on November 17. One image depicts Pattinson as Bond looking through a bullet hole; another shows him standing next to a vintage car for a mock Hollywood Reporter cover.

"Will Robert Pattinson be a better #JamesBond than Daniel Craig??" the creator ponders in the post's caption. Pattinson will be the seventh actor to play Ian Fleming's iconic British spy for the screen, with Daniel Craig being the most recent predecessor, starring in five Bond movies from 2006 to 2021. The other five previous Bond actors are Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, and Pierce Brosnan.

Bond 26 will mark Pattinson's second collaboration with acclaimed director Villeneuve; their first project together, Dune: Part Three, will hit theaters next month. Pattinson is a newcomer to the cast for this installment, while Villeneuve also directed Dune and Dune: Part Two (both receiving Best Picture nominations), as well as Arrival and Blade Runner 2049 before that.

Pattinson, meanwhile, is coming off a strong streak of movies, including Mickey 17, The Drama, The Odyssey, Primetime, and The Batman. The Twilight actor was chosen for the role of James Bond over peers including Callum Turner, Josh O'Connor, and Jack Lowden. Both Pattinson and Villeneuve have distinct creative styles, and the world will now have to wait to see how they will make Bond 26 their own.

Meanwhile, other creators speculate about what that vision could look like in works of fan art. @cole draws upon classic Bond imagery, but different artists and even the filmmakers themselves could come up with something more unconventional in the months to come.

Bond 26 is reported to release theatrically in 2028.
