Every Free Game You Can Claim On Amazon Prime In November 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

As always, the monthly Amazon announcement doesn't just kick off with a list of games, but it is paired with the first handful of games being made available to claim right away. This month, those games are WRC Generations and Squirrel With A Gun. Channel your inner rally driver, or channel your burning desire to know what it would feel like to be a regular-sized squirrel toting a comically oversized gun due to the rodent's small stature. Or just claim and play both. You can do that.

Every game coming to Essential, Extra, and Premium and the dates you'll be able to play them for free.

Prime's free games are added every Thursday, which means even if you don't want to be a rally driver or a squirrel, there will be more free games that might pique your interest coming soon - you just have to wait seven days. On November 12, Jurassic World Evolution 2 and Radiolight will be added to this month's haul of Amazon freebies. Think you can build Jurassic Park without falling into the same pitfalls as they do in the movies? Now you can put that to the test without having to spend a single penny.

Two more games are dropping on November 19 - Werewolf: The Apocalypse - Earthblood, and Clem. Masquerade as a werewolf in the game based on the popular tabletop RPG, and embark on a very different, gorgeous narrative adventure in Clem. Just the one game to round out the month five days later on November 24, when Arcadia Fallen is given away on a Tuesday instead of a Thursday.

A smaller haul than usual, but that combined with the final game being given away two days early has me thinking Amazon probably has something additional planned for Black Friday, which falls on November 27 this year. Nothing official has been announced, or even hinted at, but that seems likely.

As for the seven games we know you can claim for free this month, you can find a full rundown of when they'll be available for free and where to find them in the table below. Alternatively, you can just claim them as they arrive through the Prime Gaming website. Remember, most importantly, these games won't be taken from you. Unlike services like PS Plus and Game Pass where titles come and go from their libraries on a regular basis, once games are claimed through Prime, they're yours to keep forever and will still work even if your subscription lapses.

Xbox Game Pass has a stacked month of new releases, with several unique indies and major triple-A releases alike.
