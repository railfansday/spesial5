FIXD Automotive: Vehicle Condition Inspector - Remote

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

AppraisalPRO is FIXD's B2B product that helps dealerships during the vehicle appraisal process. We detect any problems with trade-in vehicles, provide estimated reconditioning costs for a vehicle, and a consumer-facing condition report for use in negotiations.

Dealers use AppraisalPRO's Inspect feature to take pictures of the vehicle and find cosmetic damage and estimate reconditioning costs in about two minutes. We need an experienced inspector to be the human standard for grading and providing feedback on the system's results. Your work will directly shape the future of the product. In this role, you will:

Hours: up to 20 hours per week and can be done remotely at any time of the day.

Pay: $20/hr to $30/hr, depending on seniority

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
