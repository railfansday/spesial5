Bournemouth blocked Birmingham City and Southampton deals - the tables have turned

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Birmingham City and Southampton have emerged as two of the early-season promotion candidates in the Championship, with the goals certainly flowing at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park and St Mary's respectively.

Chris Davies has addressed Birmingham's shortcomings from last season rather emphatically, whilst Saints haven't let their promotion ambitions be deterred by a four-point deduction they were served by the EFL.

Both managers have quality running right throughout their squads, and they have been particularly efficient in attack, with their sides scoring over 50 goals between them.

In the summer, the West Midlands and South Coast outfits were just two of the clubs heavily linked with a move for AFC Bournemouth attacker, Daniel Jebbison.

Jebbison is no stranger to the Championship, best-known for his time at Sheffield United, before moving to the Vitality Stadium.

However, based off early-season fortunes for the relevant parties, many will argue that Marco Rose has made the wrong call surrounding the 23-year-old's career.

The nine-time Canadian international's prior exploits have certainly garnered intrigue, as despite only scoring three times in 35 appearances at Bramall Lane, Jebbison sealed a £1.5m move to the Dorset club under Andoni Iraola.

Utilised as a bit-part player in 2024/25, the forward still made 34 appearances in all competitions, albeit many of those were as a substitute, before a loan move was sanctioned to Preston North End last summer.

Last season was one of extremely mixed fortunes for the Lilywhites and Jebbison, with the Oakville native netting six times in 38 appearances - the majority of which came in the first half of the campaign, before the form of Paul Heckingbottom's side dropped like a stone.

Despite seeing Rose replace Iraola on the South Coast, it was largely expected that Jebbison would be allowed to leave on loan or permanently in the summer window, and plenty of Championship sides were linked.

Two of those came in the form of Birmingham and Saints, with Davies looking to reshuffle his attacking options following Kyogo Furuhashi's failed transfer to St. Andrew's, whilst Eckert searched for even more firepower beyond Cyle Larin.

Blackburn Rovers and Wrexham were also credited with interest in the striker, before it emerged three days prior to the Championship season kick-off that Enas Unal's sale from Bournemouth to Getafe presented Jebbison with the chance to stake a claim for regular first-team minutes.

It has been an intriguing start to life in English football for Rose, with Bournemouth recording mixed results in the Premier League, although they have made a flying start to their maiden UEFA Europa League campaign.

There were reservations over how the German's squad would cope with the swift turnarounds that come with such short-term success, and Jebbison, who also spent time on loan with Watford, claimed in September that he would be ready whenever needed.

"I'll get my chance and every minute I get, every second or whatever, I'll work my hardest so that's what I'm trying to do, just take it day-by-day," he said.

However, that is yet to be the case, as he still finds himself behind Evanilson, Alvaro Rodriguez and Junior Kroupi in the pecking order, which could see him potentially have one eye on a January exit.

Whilst there were some concerns over the summer transfer policies at Birmingham and Southampton, it's fair to say neither club has been impacted negatively by such a domino effect.

Davies' tactical reshuffles have allowed his creative players to flourish, with January signing, August Priske, spearheading the attack in fine fashion, whilst Jay Stansfield is certainly now more settled in his 'number 10' role.
