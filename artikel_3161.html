Reformation: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Returning customers nearly double their spend in year two, a pattern that matters more to long-term holders than any single quarter's sales.

On its third quarter of 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 5, 2026, Reformation (REF -8.47%) announced full-year net revenue growth is expected to exceed 20%. The retailer scales direct-to-consumer sales made directly to shoppers without third-party retailers while expanding profitability through agile manufacturing. Three things management said stand out.

Reformation responds to shifting fashion preferences within weeks rather than planning inventory months in advance. This speed allows the business to test styles in small batches and quickly scale top sellers.

Expanding physical locations across major markets drives online traffic while generating store-level profitability. Physical retail introduces new shoppers to the brand at lower customer acquisition costs.

Active customers expanded 23% year over year to 1.25 million shoppers. While new shoppers spend less during their first year, returning customer cohorts double their annual purchases over time.

Net revenue momentum carried directly into the fourth quarter as third-quarter results exceeded internal targets. Reformation relies on flexible manufacturing and targeted store expansion, while executive teams track pricing discipline and average unit retail, the average selling price of merchandise.

International store expansion and selective wholesale partnerships offer clear long-term growth avenues. High full-price sell-through and disciplined cost control protect underlying margins as the business scales.
