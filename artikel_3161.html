SuperCom Ltd. (SPCB -22.74%) reported a 21% increase in quarterly revenue and a 17% increase in EBITDA, reflecting growth in its global electronic monitoring business. Management stated that the company has secured more than 50 new U.S. contracts since mid-2024 and is now operating across 22 states. The company introduced SafeGenie, a cybersecurity suite designed to monitor and control autonomous AI agents at the kernel level of devices. Financial performance included a $1.3 million foreign exchange loss, though the company strengthened its balance sheet through a $7.5 million capital raise and a reduction in net debt to approximately $7 million.
