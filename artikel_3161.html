Ms. Rachel Shows Support for Unrepresented Children Facing Deportation at Immigration Court

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Ms. Rachel met with children appearing without an attorney at federal youth immigration court in Manhattan on Monday. The children’s educator and YouTuber highlighted the lack of legal representation for children facing deportation and their families as they struggle to navigate the complex immigration system in the United States.

Ms. Rachel, a vocal critic of the Trump administration’s family detainment practices and harmful rhetoric against immigrant children, shared a video on social media of 9-year-old Angel, who appeared in court without an attorney amid immigration proceedings.

“Kids representing themselves in immigration court is so wrong. I saw Angel up at the mic without a lawyer and I couldn’t believe it. He’s 9 years old!” Ms. Rachel wrote in the caption. “Hearing kids hear the word ‘removable’ over and over about themselves… Heartbreaking. Stop the cruelty!”

When Ms. Rachel asked Angel to write a list of things he wanted for Christmas, at the top the nine-year-old asked for an immigration lawyer. “I am getting Angel a lawyer,” wrote the educator.

Ms. Rachel stopped by the court alongside Congressman-elect Brad Lander, according to ABC 7. The outlet said that a judge gave Angel’s mother until Dec. 15 to obtain legal representation.“It’s heartbreaking. I care so much about every child and I just want to see every child thriving in school and my heart goes out to anyone going through this,” Ms. Rachel said, per the outlet. “I think about how that makes a child feel whether or not you are removable…I don’t want any child to hear that word associated with them.”

The YouTube personality called attention to policymakers and said they needed to better prioritize the needs of children. “I think our problems would really be a lot less if we all just focus on taking care of kids and centered kids in policies,” she said. Ms. Rachel has also been outspoken about the violence, poverty, and disenfranchisement of children in Gaza amid the war with Israel. “When I see a mom who has a baby who is starving and they can’t get any milk, I see that and [think], ‘What if that was me and Susie?’” she told Rolling Stone in a recent interview.. “With Gaza, I couldn’t not speak out.”
