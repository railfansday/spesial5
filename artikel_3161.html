‘Sunrise On The Reaping’ To Gobble Up Thanksgiving; ‘Hexed’ $75M+ WW Opening; ‘Focker-In-Law’ $70M WW – Box Office Preview

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Lionsgate’s The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping will have all the fire to keep the Thanksgiving box office stretch cooking with at least $70M between Wednesday and Sunday per industry estimates at the domestic B.O.. Five-day forecasts are hard, duh, and once we’re into Black Friday, the richest day of the frame, does it become apparent how much overdrive is in the box office.

Already today Sunrise on the Reaping is heading to an estimated $16M which is both the best Tuesday Lionsgate has ever seen for a movie, and also a franchise Tuesday record besting Catching Fire’s $15.96M. Today is also bound to be higher than Wicked: For Good‘s first Tuesday a year ago which was $15.7M. That will take the Francis Lawrence directed pic’s running cume through five days to $148.2M, $18.1M away from overtaking the $166.3M lifetime North American total on the last Hunger Games: Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes three years ago. Remember, Sunrise on the Reaping will cede some of its Imax screens, specifically AMC’s, to Netflix’s limited run of Once Upon a Time in Hollywood sequel, the Brad Pitt starring The Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth which will be booked at 250 locations for a two week run and arrive on Netflix 28-days after its Wednesday bow. The David Fincher directed, Quentin Tarantino written feature is 82% certified fresh on Rotten Tomatoes with critics (Once Upon a Time in Hollywood was 86% certified fresh) and is looking at single digits over 5-days. Last year, Netflix’s Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery only made $4M over the Wednesday-Sunday stretch at 600 locations. Watch on Deadline

Among the new stuff, Walt Disney Animation’s original animated title Hexed should be stronger than Universal/Paramount’s Focker-in-Law when it comes to global openings (5-day domestic + the regular foreign weekend of Wednesday to Sunday), $75M-$80M WW to $70M.

Domestically right now via Universal, the bet is that the added high note of Ariana Grande to the 26-year old Ben Stiller and Robert De Niro Meet the Parents comedy franchise will have the upper hand with a $45M Wednesday to Sunday (there aren’t any Tuesday night previews, through showtimes start at midnight) at 3,350 theaters, despite awful reviews at 28% Rotten. Reviews have always been bad for the Fockers films, except for the 2000 original which stands at 85% certified fresh. Uni will have some PLFs. Older women over 25 are best in first choice, followed by younger women (under 25) and men over 25.

In the $90M net production, which was evenly co-bankrolled by Uni and Paramount, Greg Focker’s son, Henry (Skyler Gisondo), brings his girlfriend (Grande), a trained FBI hostage negotiator, home to meet the family. Greg soon gets worried when Henry tells everyone that he plans to propose to her. Paramount is a co-financier here having inherited the property from the days when they had an interest in DreamWorks, the previous co-studio on the franchise.

Paramount overseas will release Focker-in-Law in 62 territories except for Japan. Comps abroad for comedies, particularly, non-gross out films, are hard. Little Fockers in like-for-likes, the last movie in the series back in 2010, bowed to $25M abroad. The best comp post Covid is the Sandra Bullock-Channing Tatum adventure romcom, Lost City, which also opened to $25M in like-for-likes and $55.4M WW. There’s been a big effort by Paramount to tailor the jokes for each territory’s audience. On Little Fockers, Western Europe ruled with UK, Germany, France, and Spain the strongest followed by Australia. There’s been roughly an even split between the final domestic and foreign on the Meet the Parents movies. Through three movies, the Meet the Parents movies count $1.16 billion at the global box office.

Disney’s $200M production Hexed could be light. Domestic tracking three weeks ago was originally at $45M over 5-day and now it could be at $40M or below at 3,725 theatres, including 800 Premium Large Format screens, 1,800 3D screens, and 250 Motion screens. Reviews are not so hot at 65% fresh on RT. Previews begin today at 2pm, and they’re around $2M we hear (that’s just above Encanto‘s $1.5M which was a 5-day of $40.5M). Even though Moana 2 and Zootopia 2 opened to respective massive Tuesday previews of $13.8M and $10.2M, it’s always about the days after for the Disney animated pics. Unlike other Disney Animation movies, there aren’t any original songs in this movie about a mother and daughter witch, voiced by Rashida Jones and Hailee Steinfeld. It’s all needle drops of pop hits, like Dua Lipa’s “Levitating”. Best first choice by a stretch for Hexed is women under 25. It’s one point above in the demo from where Disney/Pixar’s Hoppers was before opening to a 3-day of $45.3M.

Overseas, Hexed is eyeing $35M-$40M with a 92% footprint. Like-for-like comps are Hoppers ($57M foreign opening), Elemental ($45M), Wish ($40M), and Encanto ($30M). Tomorrow the Jason Hand and Fawn Veerasunthorn directed movie will bow in Belgium, Czech Rep, France, Korea, Netherlands, Philippines, Poland, South Africa, and Switzerland. Thursday will be Argentina, Australia, Austria, Bolivia, Brazil, Central America, Chile, Colombia, Croatia, Denmark, Ecuador, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Italy, Malaysia, Mexico, New Zealand, Paraguay, Peru, Portugal, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand, Trinidad, UAE, Ukraine, Uruguay, Venezuela. Friday is China, Finland, India, Indonesia, Norway, Spain, Sweden, Turkey, UK, and Vietnam. Saturday is Hong Kong.

Also wide stateside is Angel Studio’s biopic, Hershey, about Milton Hershey, the chocolate guy, starring Finn Wittrock and Alexandra Daddario at 2,275 theaters. The pic opens Thursday and is looking at $7M. Reviews are quite stale at 17% on RT. Neon is expanding James Gray’s crime drama Paper Tiger starring Adam Driver, Scarlett Johansson, and Miles Teller from 28 locations to 1,500 with an eye at $3M-$5M over five days. There’s been a push to sell this to the Letterboxd and Barstool Sports dude demo with the studio buying spots in sports. Since its world premiere at Cannes, Paper Tiger is 86% certified fresh with critics and good at 70% currently with RT audiences.
