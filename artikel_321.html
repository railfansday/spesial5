John Mulaney and Phoebe Bridgers latest SNL promo contains a brutal roast

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

We know that Phoebe Bridgers can sing, but it appears she may also have a future in roast battles.

In the SNL promo above the Lost Weekend artist and Primetime star appears alongside this week's host John Mulaney and cast member Ben Marshall, who is convinced that Mulaney's about to start roasting him at any second.

"No seriously, I mostly do observational stuff about municipal arcana," Mulaney explains, before Marshall inexplicably storms off.

"What was his problem?" Mulaney asks Bridgers. The response? "He's just mad he looks like Temu Ron Weasley." Ouch.
