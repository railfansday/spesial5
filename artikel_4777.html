The 8 Best Men's Pajamas, Tested by Style Editors

PJ sets by Tommy John and Cozy Earth came out on top in our testing.

AS MUCH AS I love playing dress-up for the office, the second I get home I’m counting down to that moment when I can peel off the suit pants, kick the loafers across the room, and finally feel like a human. Don’t get me wrong, I’m a diehard cozy-hoodie-and-sweats guy (they’re my unofficial uniform for bodega runs and morning coffee walks). But when it’s time to shut my brain off and wind down, pajamas are the endgame. There’s something about slipping into a matching set that taps into that childhood sleepover and Saturday morning cartoons feeling—except I’m an adult who appreciates soft fabrics and a decent bedtime.

Most guys tumble into bed in whatever they happen to be wearing—an old tee, boxers, or absolutely nothing if you're feeling…efficient. But hear me out: investing in the best men’s pajamas is one of those tiny grown-up upgrades that makes a huge difference. They make you look more put-together, keep you warm if you run cold at night, and make bedtime feel less like a crash landing and more like a proper wind-down. Some of our editors even pack PJs for vacations because slipping into a proper set in a hotel bed feels downright luxurious.

If you're tired of tossing and turning in your worn-out sweatpants or boxers, we’ve got you covered. Our Men’s Health fashion team and I have tested more than 30 pairs since 2020 and curated the best of the bunch. From classic button-down sets in breathable cotton to slim jogger-style pajama bottoms with stretch at the waistband, side seams, and knees—it’s all here. Whether you're reserving the weekend to lie around like a couch potato or catching up on some serious Z's, these pajamas are your ticket to bedtime bliss.

This list of eight men’s pajamas checks every box—perfect for yourself or as a thoughtful, stylish gift.

If you’re a hot sleeper, look for cotton, bamboo, or modal. If you tend to get cold at night, opt for fleece or flannel. Below, we've broken down what to expect from each type of material.

A classic choice, cotton is lightweight, breathable, and super soft. It’s perfect for year-round wear, traditional button-down sets, and those who love the feel of natural fibers.

It’s silky, lightweight, and feels like second skin. It’s also stretchy, but that depends on the knit and fabric blend. Pajamas with a bit of spandex or elastane provide stretch so you can move easily while sleeping.

Nobody wants PJs that feel restrictive, but you also don’t want to look like you’re swimming in fabric. Aim for a fit that’s relaxed but still polished. Whether you’re into classic button-down sets or modern loungewear vibes, pick a style that makes you feel good lounging around the house.

When it comes to the perfect pair of pajamas, some special features can make a huge impact. For one, pockets are essential for your phone, remote, tissues, or late-night snack (but please, never in bed). An elastic waistband with a drawstring cord lets you adjust the fit to your exact liking. This way, they will stay in place and not dig into your skin, no matter how much you toss and turn at night.
