Nasdaq Drops 2.5% for a Third Straight Loss as AI Hardware Stocks Slide

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

NEW YORK, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- USA News Group News Commentary - Stocks tied to artificial intelligence infrastructure led Wall Street lower on Monday. The S&P 500 fell 1.92% to 7,416.59 and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 2.49% to 24,509.76 on Monday, according to CNBC's closing report. It was the third consecutive losing day for both indexes, and Wall Street's fear gauge, the VIX, rose 19%, according to CNN. The long-term demand picture remains large: Grand View Research, in a report released on PR Newswire, puts the AI data center market at USD 147.3 billion in 2025 and forecasts USD 810.6 billion by 2033, a 23.9% compound annual growth rate. Active Companies from around the markets with current developments this week include: Broadcom Inc. (NASDAQ: AVGO), CoreWeave, Inc. (NASDAQ: CRWV), GE Vernova Inc. (NYSE: GEV), Intel Corporation (NASDAQ: INTC).

Policy added to the day's news. The Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security released an interim final rule on Monday addressing remote access to advanced computing chips and servers, designed to ensure that existing restrictions on access to sensitive American technology cannot be circumvented through remote computing arrangements involving data centers in third countries. Under the rule, licenses are required to export, reexport or transfer advanced semiconductors to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil.

Rates offered some relief. The 10-year Treasury yield eased to 5.13% from 5.15% on Friday as lower oil prices reduced inflation pressure, according to the Associated Press. Investors are also heading into a week that includes a closely watched chipmaker earnings report on Tuesday.

In other industry developments and happenings in the market this week include:

Broadcom Inc. (NASDAQ: AVGO) fell 3.9%, its second straight loss, according to the Associated Press. In its most recent quarterly report, released September 2, 2026, Broadcom reported third quarter fiscal 2026 revenue of $29.6 billion, up 86% from the prior year period, and guided fourth quarter revenue to approximately $34.8 billion, an increase of 93% from the prior year period.

The company has said it will report its fourth quarter and fiscal year 2026 results after the close of the U.S. trading session on Wednesday, December 9, 2026.

CoreWeave, Inc. (NASDAQ: CRWV), which rents access to AI computing power over the cloud, fell 6.1%, according to the Associated Press. On November 10, CoreWeave reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $3.661 billion, up 168% from a year earlier, and a revenue backlog of approximately $132.5 billion at September 30, up from approximately $104 billion at the end of June.

The company raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $13.0 billion to $13.3 billion. It also reported a third quarter net loss of $709 million, compared with a net loss of $110 million a year earlier.

GE Vernova Inc. (NYSE: GEV), which helps power AI data centers, fell 4.9%, according to the Associated Press. In its third quarter 2026 results, GE Vernova reported orders of $29 billion, up 96% organically, and backlog growth of $20 billion sequentially to $197 billion in total.

Third quarter revenue was $12.6 billion, up 27%, and the company raised its 2026 guidance, increased its share repurchase authorization and announced an agreement to acquire Trench Group, a high-voltage grid equipment company.

Intel Corporation (NASDAQ: INTC) fell roughly 7% as selling spread across semiconductor stocks, according to CNN. In its third quarter 2026 results, Intel reported revenue of $16.5 billion, up 21% year over year, with Data Center and AI revenue of $7.3 billion, up 78%.

The company forecast fourth quarter 2026 revenue of $15.0 billion to $16.0 billion. Intel's CFO said the company sees the PC slowdown continuing at least through the first half of next year.
