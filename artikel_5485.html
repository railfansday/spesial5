LigaData Brings Data Flare and Barq to Google Cloud Marketplace

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Regulated enterprises can now buy LigaData's data and governed AI through Google Cloud and run them wherever their data strategy requires, from the cloud to sovereign on-premises environments with LigaData's Barq Citadel.

PALO ALTO, Calif., Nov. 27, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- LigaData, a provider of data and governed AI for complex enterprise data in regulated industries, today announced that Data Flare, its data integration and preparation product, and LigaData Barq, its governed AI platform for management intelligence, are now available on Google Cloud Marketplace.

LigaData software now processes 90 billion records a day across 27 countries for leaders across telecom, financial services, government and other regulated industries. Its products cover the path from raw data to governed AI: Data Platform, Data Flare, Data Governance and Barq. Data Flare prepares the data. Barq puts AI to work on it.

LigaData certified Data Flare and Barq on Google Cloud because BigQuery and Gemini are where enterprise data and AI are coming together.

Data Flare builds and runs the pipelines that bring data from separate systems into one consistent view, with one agreed definition of each business measure. Integration work that took months takes days. On Google Cloud it runs in the customer's own Google Kubernetes Engine cluster and writes to Cloud Storage, where BigQuery can query it without a separate load step. Every record is counted once, and every pipeline change goes through roles, approval and an audit trail.

Barq is the governed AI engine for management intelligence, working on the data an organization already has. Every answer traces back to the query and the source, and every request is checked against the customer's access rules. An answer can become a live application that keeps monitoring that part of the business, and external agents connect through the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and APIs. No agent holds more authority than the person running it. Questions that took a data team three days to a week are answered in about 15 minutes.

Google Cloud customers can buy through their existing account with consolidated billing, and put AI to work on data already in BigQuery without moving it, using Gemini models through Vertex AI under their own access rules, masking and audit.

"Our customers' data strategies don't fit a single deployment model. Some data has to stay on-premises under full sovereign control, and some belongs in the cloud," said Shekhar Rajvanshy, Chief Engineering Officer, LigaData. "With Google Cloud, we can now meet them across that whole range with the same products. Governance is built into Data Flare and Barq, so the rules a customer sets apply the same way wherever these products run."

LigaData is a Silicon Valley-based enterprise data and AI software company and a member of the Google Cloud Partner Network. Learn more at ligadata.com.
