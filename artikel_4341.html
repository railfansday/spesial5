Briggs & Stratton keeps the lights on longer with bigger 16 kWh LFP battery

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

For decades, Briggs & Stratton has helped keep the lights on after the storm with its gas-powered generators. Now, the company is bringing that legacy to the electric age with a new 16 kWh backup battery designed to scale with your growing energy needs.

Briggs & Stratton partnered with Texas-based EG4 to offer an all American-branded solution for battery backup power solution last year, and its stackable SimpliPHI batteries helped make it easy to right-size home battery projects – but those batteries were limited in size, and limited in what they could actually offer. With the launch of the brand’s new, 16 kWh SimpliPHI battery, the company is ready to meet your energy needs … even if those needs take you up to 512 kWh. (!)

For context, the average US home uses about 865 kWh of energy per month, so a 512 kWh battery should theoretically keep your lights on for nearly 18 days after a storm – or provide basic power for your whole block for a weekend, at least!

“This launch represents a significant shift in our battery strategy,” explains Sequoya Cross, vice president of energy storage at Briggs & Stratton. “Our combined resources allow us to bring a competitively priced battery of the highest quality to energy storage installers.”

An onboard battery management system (BMS) and built-in Wi-Fi gateway help to streamline the system’s setup for installers, while the Briggs battery’s automatic inverter sensing technology supports closed-loop communication with Sol-Ark, Solis, EG4, Growatt, Megarevo, Victron, and other leading inverters. Other installer-friendly features include an IP65-rated enclosure allows for indoor or outdoor installation in either ground- or wall-mounted setups without requiring an additional cabinet, and an integrated circuit breaker that allows technicians to isolate the battery during servicing.

The battery itself is rated for 8,000 cycles at 90% depth of discharge, with a charge and discharge rate of 0.5C and an end-of-life capacity of 70%. Briggs & Stratton backs its new battery packs with a 12-year parts warranty that includes labor and travel costs, as well as a US-based tech support service.

The SimpliPHI 16 is available for pre-order now, though pricing was not mentioned in the original press release.
