Barcelona eyeing up January swoop for Bayern centre-half Tah

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Reigning La Liga champions FC Barcelona are reportedly still interested in Bayern Munich's central defender Jonathan Tah.

Sky Germany journalist Florian Plettenberg even suggests that Barcelona have made an inquiry with a view to a potential January move.

The Catalan club's sporting director Deco is looking for an experienced centre-back to play alongside young talent Pau Cubarsí.

However, the German international is currently not thinking about leaving Vincent Kompany's team and is happy at the club.

Nevertheless, Tah's agent Pini Zahavi has a very good relationship with Barça president Joan Laporta which could swing things.

So far this season, the 30-year-old has made 13 appearances across all competitions, of which only 6 have come in the Bundesliga.
