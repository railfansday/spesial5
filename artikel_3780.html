If you somehow missed all the hype around the year’s biggest movie, The Odyssey sees Nolan turn his hand to the swords and sandals genre, delivering a mind-bending and introspective blockbuster in the way only The Dark Knight and Oppenheimer director can.

The film follows Odysseus (Matt Damon), the king of ancient Ithaca, in the closing days of the ten year Trojan War. With the city fallen, the weary warrior plans to head home, but the gods have other ideas, as the simple journey turns into an epic quest that sees Odysseus battle cyclops, sirens, witches, nymphs and even descend into hell, all while grappling with his own guilt and identity. Meanwhile, Odysseus’ wife Penelope (Anne Hathaway) and son Telemachus (Tom Holland) face various suitors, including new James Bond Robert Pattinson, who are looking to muscle in on the presumed dead king’s power and fortune.

With a huge cast that also includes Zendaya, Charlize Theron, Samantha Morton, Lupita Nyong'o, Jon Bernthal, Benny Safdie, John Leguizamo, Himesh Patel, Elliot Page, Mia Goth and Travis Scott, The Odyssey is sure to be a serious contender come awards season.

Despite its bladder-bothering length, The Odyssey stands as one of the year's most successful and thrilling movie experiences, and with the film now available to watch at your leisure, it's the perfect time to embark on the journey.

The Odyssey hit theaters back in July of this year, with a special encore run set for December. The home video release, on both physical disc and PVOD was Tuesday, November 17.

The home release of The Odyssey comes with a wealth of behind the scenes materials for film fans to sink their teeth into with Universal teasing: "Personally overseen by Christopher Nolan, this collection of 12 in-depth special features takes audiences inside every aspect of The Odyssey's ambitious production through exclusive behind-the-scenes footage and interviews with Nolan alongside key cast and crew who brought the epic to life. Together, these pieces showcase the artistry, innovation, and craftsmanship behind the landmark adaptation of Homer’s legendary tale."
