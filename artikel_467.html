GTA 6: The Album soundtrack gets vinyl and CD release: Where to buy

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto VI isn't coming to disc in video game form, but if you want to listen to some music from the game, you'll be able to do that on physical media.

Rockstar announced on X and its website that Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album will be released on the same day as the game on CD and vinyl. You can also listen via every major music streamer.

Three versions of the soundtrack will be released on physical media:

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, Limited Edition on vinyl — $124.98

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album on vinyl — $49.98

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album on CD — $19.98

Rockstar's announcement post said the album contains just 34 "original tracks," from a variety of artists, so it's definitely just a small selection of music from the game and not a full score release. It's an eclectic soundtrack with a wide variety of artists, and the full tracklist was recently confirmed.

Six singles also came out alongside the original pre-order announcement, and the collection of talent is pretty interesting:

Yung Lean - "That's It" (Feat. Future and Metro Boomin)

CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, PinkPantheress, Fred again.., & Etienne de Crécy - "Sexy Magic"

Keith Richards - "Bright Lights, Big City"

The announcement confirmed our prediction that a bunch of cryptic Instagram posts from the artists in question were related to GTA 6's soundtrack. Still, Keith Richards is a get we didn't expect.
