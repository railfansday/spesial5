The HomeView features a sleek 7-inch Liquid Retina display for viewing and comes with Siri AI built in. It also has speakers that can support Spatial Audio and can recognize up to six users. Mashable's Timothy Beck Werth is a big fan, writing in his hands-on that "this gorgeous little smart display is about to become a common sight in homes around the world."

It also serves as a central hub for controlling the smart home accessories you have, which is good news if you're planning to grab anything from Apple's Home Collection. Werth writes that, "If you plan on investing in Apple's other new smart home gadgets — the new Apple TV 4K, HomePod Mini 2, new home security cameras, and video doorbells — I'd even say the HomeView is a necessity."

If the Apple HomeView is on your shopping wishlist, don't miss out on this chance to save at Amazon.
