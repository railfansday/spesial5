Jon Stewart breaks down Trumps viral 60 Minutes interview

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Donald Trump was asked some tough questions in his recent 60 Minutes interview, and he gave some answers on immigration that appeared to confuse some people in his base.

In the Daily Show clip above, Jon Stewart breaks down some of the key moments — including the U.S. president saying "I'm really good at money and finances" before saying "I guess it's gone up, I don't even know" when asked about his own net worth increasing.

"So when it's global finance, Trump is Keanu Reeves from The Matrix," says Stewart. "But when it's his own $3 billion post-election windfall, he's Keanu Reeves from Bill and Ted."

Another entertaining moment? Trump being asked about what happens after we die and whether he'll go to heaven, with the president responding, "I hope so, I don't wanna go to hell."

"You know that's not how hell works, right?" says Stewart. "It's not a check your preference, like fish or steak."
