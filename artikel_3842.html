Shikhondo: Blue Pieta for PS5, Switch 2, and Switch launches December 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Fantasy bullet hell shoot ’em up game Shikhondo: Blue Pieta will launch for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and Switch on December 10, publisher CFK and developer DeerFarm announced. A physical edition will be available via Play-Asia. It will support English, Korean, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese language options.

Shikhondo: Blue Pieta first launched for PC via Steam and Stove on May 18.

Here is an overview of the game, via CFK:

Several years after the tragedy of the previous game—an apprentice Grim Reaper and a Vampire Hunter. Their intersecting fates open the doors to a new battlefield. The abyss of an “Urban Fantasy” encroaching upon everyday life… now manifests.

Shikhondo Act II begins on a brand-new stage!

Unleash your power all at once to obliterate your enemies with the ultimate skill: “Soul Collect.”

The thrilling tension and the sheer exhilaration of unleashing overwhelming firepower will deeply immerse you in the world of Shikhondo.

A Mesmerizing Dance of Bullets! Boss Battles

At the end of each stage, captivating bosses with diverse and unique designs await.

Will you be able to survive these fierce battles to the bitter end…?

Diverse Game Modes to Expand Your Experience

Furthermore, we’ve implemented a wide range of difficulties—from “Novice” for series beginners who want to comfortably enjoy the story, to “Extreme” for veterans looking to push their limits. It has been crafted as a title that anyone can truly enjoy.
