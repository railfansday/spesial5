Ace Combat 8's Online Season 2 Adds New Missions & Aircraft Next Month on PS5

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The next season of content is coming soon to Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve's online multiplayer mode.

Publisher Bandai Namco has released the below trailer detailing all the new bits and bobs:

Season 2 of Ace Combat Online begins on 1st December, 2026, and there's plenty of new stuff for frequent flyers to enjoy.

There are new aircraft, including the Su-30SM -Mihaly- and the X-40 Nightmare.

You can also deck out your F-15C with a new 'Climb King' skin, while the Mirage 2000-5 gets the 'Experimental' skin.

It's not just planes getting new looks; you can kit out your pilot with new clothing and emotes.

Speaking of pilots, a new Pilot Rank, Captain, has been introduced; reach it and you'll be able to purchase the aforementioned X-40 Nightmare.

Season 2 brings some new missions to Ace Combat Online, too. Co-Op Warfare gets the Kaninfot Valley mission, while Anchorhead Bay has been introduced to Co-Op Assault mode.

On top of all that, there are of course new premium and free Ace Passes to progress through, unlocking further cosmetic rewards.
