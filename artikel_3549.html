Taylor Swift Gifts Megan Moroney Personalized Letter After 2026 CMAs Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Megan Moroney made sure to give Taylor Swift her flowers — literally — after her highly anticipated 2026 CMAs comeback. And, no, the 14-time Grammy winner didn’t take it lightly.

“On cloud 9 at the CMA awards!” Moroney, 29, wrote via Instagram on Friday, November 20, alongside a carousel of images from the celebratory evening.

In one slide, Moroney revealed that Swift, 36, gifted her a personalized poster ahead of the big show.

“Megan! Thank you SO much for these beautiful flowers,” Swift wrote in her message. “That was incredibly thoughtful and I hope you have a fantastic night! I’d send you flowers back but also all I have in my dressing room is merch. Just pretend it’s a lovely bouquet?”

She concluded, “Go get ‘em!! Love, Taylor.”

Moroney and Swift both attended the 60th annual CMAs on Wednesday, November 18, in honor of their respective nominations. Moroney was recognized in the Song of the Year and Female Artist of the Year categories, both of which she lost to Ella Langley. Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas” also triumphed as Single of the Year over Swift’s “I Knew It, I Knew You.”

Amid their respective losses, Moroney and Swift each performed during the live broadcast. For Swift, her return marked the first time she sang on the CMAs stage in 13 years.

Dressed in a bedazzled Erdem minidress and cowboy boots, Swift mashed up the Toy Story 5 track with “Our Song” from her debut album. The pop star further leaned into her country roots by enlisting her former country collaborators Brad Paisley, Keith Urban, Little Big Town, Brooks & Dunn and Sugarland for brief cameos.

Swift famously got her start as a country artist before making the move to the pop scene with 2014’s 1989. Following the 2025 release of her 12th album, The Life of a Showgirl, Swift has dropped hints at embracing her twang-filled music history down the line.

“I love my country roots more and more as I go further in this path,” Swift said during a Grammy Museum retrospective in August. “I want to honor them more and more, the artist who shaped who I am, and that all happened in Nashville.”

“I Knew It, I Knew You” marked her official return when it dropped in June.

“The word daze is spelled ‘d-a-z-e’ in the lyrics, but you could also, if you wanted to think of it as days ‘d-a-y-s’ you could perceive it as that,” Swift said during a September interview on iHeartCountry House Party about the lyrics. “But, I thought of it, like, a childhood daze you look back and you see this sort of blurry, beautiful memory that’s kind of blurry at the edges and blades of the grass in summer that harkens back to childhood and playing and using your imagination.”

She continued at the time, “There’s the added meaning of they’re toys, they can only see up to the height of blades of grass. ‘Parachutes for the free fall of being younger.’ There’s the actual parachute army toys in Toy Story, as well as symbolically, somebody who helps you grow up, helps your landings be softer.”
