‘The Vow’ Star Jace Cates Addresses On-Camera Butt Grab: ‘I Made a Mistake’

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Vow star Jace Cates addressed backlash he received for grabbing the butt of one of his contestants.

“I made a mistake in that moment. I was in there. I’m not perfect,” Cates, 30, told the New York Post in an article published on Saturday, November 21. “What I’m happy with is that I apologized to the women, I took accountability … and I tried to make sure they know I want to be respectful.”

Cates’ comments come after episode 3 in the Fox Nation reality TV dating series, hosted by former Bachelor host Chris Harrison, saw the leading man oblige to Sierra Wood’s request to “grab my ass.”

Cates, a loan officer from Oregon who is dating 10 faith-based Texas women on the show in search of a wife, discussed his internal reasoning behind the on-air moment. “I think it’s no secret that Sierra and I had an initial connection right from the beginning. And what doesn’t really get talked about is, that’s a long day of shooting, and when that moment happened with Sierra, it was at the end of the day. I was trying to be very intentional with every single girl that day,” he told the newspaper. “Me and Sierra were having our moment, and it was a very romantic setting. I was in the moment with her when she made that request, and I acted on it.”

He continued, “It’s just really difficult because you have your faith at the forefront, and you have your intention at the forefront, and then you’re also trying to build a relationship with people you see a future with.”

In the aftermath of Cates’ butt-grabbing, several contestants in the series shared disapproval while filming confessionals. “We don’t want to watch butt-grabbing,” said one of the women, while another told the camera, “I would never.”

Harrison, 55, spoke to Us exclusively about The Vow, a show he and his wife, Lauren Zima, cocreated and coexecutive produced, earlier this month. “We kept saying, ‘We want to create the most authentic, intentional dating show ever,’” he said at the time. “OK, well to do that, every kind of wall we hit, we’re like, ‘Oh, we got to remove that. That’s an elimination ceremony. That’s kind of contrived. It’s produced and it’s forcing Jace into moments that aren’t real. Get rid of it. So trinkets, hiding behind something. Nope. Get rid of it. Fantasy suites. Get rid of it.’ So honestly, we kind of kept swiping and swiping at these things.”

Last month, The Vow attracted a cease-and-desist letter from ABC, which has aired The Bachelor and its spinoffs since 2002. The move sparked a response from Harrison, who hosted the Bachelor franchise from its inception until 2021, with the star telling Entertainment Tonight on October 26, “Sounds like somebody misses somebody really bad. Jealousy’s a tough thing. Green’s not a great color on anybody, is it?”
