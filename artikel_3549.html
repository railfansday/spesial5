Sofia Vergara Put a Cool-Girl Twist on the Classic Black Sweater

Sofia Vergara just reminded me that a black sweater doesn’t have to be boring. I love a classic knit as much as anyone, but her off-the-shoulder silhouette is making me want to show a little more skin this season. It’s such an easy way to give an everyday sweater a flirty, fashion-forward upgrade, and I think it’s especially chic for women over 50 because it’s fun without getting too wild.

Naturally, I’m ready to add a few to my own closet! I rounded up five off-the-shoulder sweaters that capture Vergara’s effortlessly cool look, from sleek fitted knits to cozy styles with a little extra slouch. They’re perfect for dressing up my favorite jeans or giving tailored trousers a more playful feel. My basic sweaters might have some competition!

1. Just Like Sofia’s: I’m adding this ribbed black sweater to my cart right now! It brings a little extra drama with voluminous balloon sleeves, and I love how the neckline shows off the shoulders without making the outfit feel too dressy. The cozy material also makes it an easy pick for everyday wear, whether I’m heading out for dinner or just want to elevate my favorite jeans. And at under $30? I’m sold.

2. Date Night Knit: This fitted ribbed knit has an elegant off-the-shoulder neckline that makes your go-to jeans and heels (like Sofia!) feel incredibly chic. It’s comfortable, soft and stretchy, so I don’t have to sacrifice style for comfort. Wear it straight from the office to dinner with just a quick accessory swap!

3. Wear It Two Ways: I’m a fan of any wardrobe staple that I can wear multiple ways, and this ribbed boat-neck pullover checks that box. It has a wide neckline you can drape over one shoulder for an effortlessly flirty finish, or wear off both shoulders if you prefer. The fitted waist defines your curves and adds shape compared with a slouchy, oversized sweater, so it’s a more polished pick.

4. So Soft and Cozy: If a cloud-soft sweater is on your wish list (like me!), this oversized knit might be the one. One reviewer said it’s so soft, it feels like wearing “a warm hug,” but it’s still interesting with batwing sleeves and a pretty folded-over neckline. It’s the kind of cozy statement piece I’d reach for on chilly date nights when I still want my outfit to feel a little flirty — but I’m tempted to curl up on the couch in it, too.

5. Easy Elegance: Consider this oversized top the answer to those days when a basic long-sleeve tee is all you want to reach for, but won’t really cut it for what’s on your calendar. It’s just as easy to throw on, but has a chic, elevated feel with an asymmetrical neckline that shows your shoulder. You can dress it up for date night or toss it on with leggings for errands! Just a little flirty, with no extra styling effort required.
