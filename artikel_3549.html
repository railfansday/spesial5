Inside Jack Nicholson's Dynamic With His Kids: Read Their Rare Family Quotes

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Jack Nicholson‘s kids are opening up about the man behind the shades after the famed actor decided to step away from the bright lights of Hollywood.

Nicholson, who has six children from five relationships, has largely retreated from public life since his last film role in 2010’s How Do You Know?. In his absence, his kids — Ray and Lorraine Nicholson, namely — have been offering new insight about their dad.

Jack shares Lorraine and Ray with ex Rebecca Broussard, as well as daughter Jennifer with Sandra Knight, son Caleb with Susan Anspach, daughter Honey with Winnie Hollman and daughter Tessa with Jennine Gourin.

Jack has rarely addressed his parenting role on the record, though he offered a glimpse of his life as a dad in a 2010 interview with AARP The Magazine.

“We’ve always gotten along,” he said. “I want to be inspirational, or some kind of good influence on them without overburdening them. It’s their time of life to find out who they are.”

Ray sat down with his sister Lorraine for a joint Interview magazine conversation published in November 2026, where he described the bond he shares with their father in disarmingly honest terms.

“I love sports so much. It’s also the language of love that me and dad share,” Ray said. “We can’t talk about a lot, but we can talk about the f***ing Lakers until kingdom come.”

Ray, who ditched a college football path to pursue acting, said he once feared telling Jack about his career pivot and found that time at the University of Southern California gave him perspective. Ray soon realized that his dad “isn’t god” but is “a beautiful part” yet “small part” of the film industry.

“Yes, of course, making dad proud is high on my list but this is really a way of finding who I am,” Ray added at the time. “And I feel that I’m on the right path to that.”

Ray has been increasingly candid about the weight of his last name. In a June 2025 interview with Us, the Borderline star said acting itself has deepened his connection to his dad — even when words don’t.

“Ultimately, I do know, by going step by step on this journey, I begin to understand [my dad] more,” Ray told Us. “And in a weird way, he becomes more human to me. I understand him more and it makes me love him more, even if we don’t necessarily talk about it.”

“We’re very different people, I was born in 1992, he was born in 1937,” he said in a 2024 interview with Deadline. “The things that affect me don’t necessarily affect him. Obviously, I love him. He’s my inspiration. I ate dinner with him every night. I studied it, that’s how I learned to be a human being. So, of course we’re gonna be kind of similar.”

Lorraine took her own trip down memory lane in a January 2025 Vanity Fair essay, recalling Lara Flynn Boyle‘s romance with Jack from 1999 to 2004.

Lorraine was 9 when she met the then-30-year-old Twin Peaks alum, who she compared to “the Mary Poppins of Mulholland Drive.” The two went whitewater rafting in Aspen, played Parcheesi and strategically lost at Uno so Jack wouldn’t win either.

“I’ll never forget the first day Lara was introduced to us children,” Lorraine wrote. “She came down the outdoor staircase to join us by the pool. For my father, Lara acted as a bridge between him and his children.”

When the actors’ romance ended abruptly in 2004, and Lorraine says she still has “no clue why” Boyle cut off contact with the kids.
