Stock-Split Watch: Is Micron Next?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Micron looks poised for a stock split, especially after Sandisk's recent announcement.

By Marc Guberti – Nov 18, 2026 at 7:43PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Micron Technology ›

Micron Technology (MU -1.00%) may be the next tech stock to perform a stock split. Even after a lengthy correction, it still trades above $700 per share. It's normal for companies to initiate splits when they get closer to $1,000 per share, and investors already saw that unfold when fellow memory stock Sandisk (SNDK -2.64%) announced an 8-for-1 split.

While an 8-for-1 split doesn't make sense for Micron at its current price, a smaller split may make sense in the near future.

Micron's memory chips have become a vital part of artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure, and investors have seen that truth play out in recent financial results. Each time that bears question the likelihood of Micron extending its gains, the company delivers robust earnings that beat expectations and hint at more future growth.

Fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter earnings capture how this story has been playing out. Micron told investors in the previous quarter to expect $50 billion in fiscal 2026 Q4 revenue at the midpoint, but it delivered $54.2 billion instead. Beating guidance helped Micron deliver 379% year-over-year revenue growth, and it told investors that fiscal 2027 will be even better.

If Micron continues to deliver tremendous fundamental growth, its stock could rally and reclaim its all-time high above $1,200 per share. A return to that level further justifies a stock split.

Micron has been securing multiyear deals with key tech companies, making it easier to forecast future revenue. These long-term deals were one of the biggest developments that shattered the bearish narrative about inventory gluts possibly weakening Micron's pricing power in the future.

This type of boom-and-bust cycle has been the norm in the semiconductor industry, but AI demand is still going parabolic. Grand View Research projects a 30.6% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for the artificial intelligence industry through 2033. That suggests more demand for Micron's memory chips and additional pricing power.

The growth stock trades at a measly 4.5 forward price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio, giving it a lower valuation than most bank stocks, which are achieving much lower growth rates. It's hard to find a stock that combines Micron's valuation and growth rates, and this one-two punch should ignite a stock rally in the months ahead.

Any rapid acceleration in Micron’s stock price, similar to what investors saw earlier this year, would provide further justification for a stock split. Sandisk initiating its own stock split may prompt Micron to follow suit.
