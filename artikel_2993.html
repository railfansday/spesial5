The 3 Best AI Stocks to Buy Now (Yes, Nvidia Is One of Them)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Nvidia may be going through a tumultuous period, but the business is still strong.

By Keithen Drury – Nov 18, 2026 at 8:44AM EST

The artificial intelligence sector of the stock market isn't the most popular place to invest right now. There are questions surrounding how the AI build-out will be allowed to proceed, with Democrats sweeping the U.S. midterm elections. This could put a pause on some data centers, affecting companies that provide the components. Still, many projects are underway, and many builds will likely be allowed to continue as-is, with newer facilities coming under greater scrutiny.

While AI investment sentiment is currently negative, I think there are plenty of great investment opportunities right now. They include Nvidia (NVDA -0.76%), Broadcom (AVGO +0.20%), and Micron (MU -1.54%). While Nvidia is in a bit of a mess with its CEO resigning last week, I think the stock has cratered to the point where it makes sense to buy shares. The other two are also genius buys and make for timely investments, with the market not being bullish on the AI outlook.

First, let's look at Nvidia. CEO Jensen Huang resigned due to relationships with staff and a potential Chinese spy. This is a huge loss for Nvidia because Huang co-founded the company and built it into what it is today. At the same time, Nvidia's products are so impressive and have such a dominant market share that they practically sell themselves. This means business will likely go on as usual at Nvidia, and investors should use the global sell-off as a buying opportunity for the stock.

After the sell-off, Nvidia trades at a dirt cheap price of 10.5 times next year's earnings estimates.

NVDA PE Ratio (Forward 1y) data by YCharts

That's a huge discount, and considering the S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.38%) trades for about 20 times forward earnings, I can see Nvidia having an impressive rebound in 2027.

While Nvidia is a timely buy, it still has some troubles. So, investors should also look to other stocks.

Broadcom has been taking market share from Nvidia for a while. Instead of making a broad-purpose GPU like Nvidia, it's partnering directly with AI hyperscalers to develop custom AI chips that are tailored to their workloads. This has major cost advantages, as running workloads on these chips is far cheaper than on GPUs. However, they cannot be used in every situation, so there will always be a large place for Nvidia GPUs.

Next year, Broadcom expects its AI semiconductor revenue to double, and double again in 2028. That's huge growth and will also outperform Nvidia over that time frame. As a result, I think it's a top option to buy in addition to or instead of Nvidia.

Micron is a key supplier to both Broadcom and Nvidia, as it makes memory chips for computing devices. The memory chip industry has seen practically all of its production capacity consumed by the AI build-out, causing chip prices to soar. This has led to a record-setting year for Micron, but it could get even better.

During Micron's latest earnings report, its management team noted that market conditions will be worse in 2027 and 2028 than they were in 2026. When Micron says "worse," it means worse for its clients, not for itself. The company will do well because high demand for memory chips has driven up prices, translating into pure profit for Micron.

Historically, Micron has been a cyclical stock, which is why the market isn't valuing it at its full potential.

The market is valuing Micron's stock as if its business will evaporate tomorrow; we know that won't happen for several more years, given the strength of the AI build-out. This mismatch is your investment opportunity, and I think Micron is a top stock to buy now.
