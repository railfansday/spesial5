Prediction: Dec. 9 Will Mark a Turning Point for the S&P 500

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The markets have been looking shaky recently, and things could get even worse next month.

Suddenly, the S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.45%) doesn't look so hot anymore. It has declined by 3% over the past month as bearishness in the tech sector has been weighing on the broader markets. Plus, with interest rates on the rise and questionable economic conditions, investors have been more eager of late to sell stocks, especially highly valued ones.

Meanwhile, the controversy surrounding Nvidia and its former CEO, Jensen Huang, has raised further concerns in the tech sector and broader markets.

However, the big date for investors to mark on their calendars is undoubtedly Dec. 9. That's the day the Fed makes its next interest-rate decision, and it could be a big one. Here's why I predict it'll be a turning point for the S&P 500.

The likelihood of another rate hike next month is all but certain. It's just a matter of how significant an increase it may be. According to CME Group's FedWatch tool, there is more than a 94% probability that interest rates will rise by 25 basis points, bringing the target range to 4.00% to 4.25%. The probability of rates remaining unchanged is zero, while the probability of a half-point rate hike is just under 6%.

But depending on the economic data that comes out between now and then, things could change. Christopher Waller, who is a member of the Federal Reserve's board of governors, has said he supports a 50-basis-point hike, particularly if consumer price data continue to paint a troubling picture. And last week's inflation report came in higher than expected, confirming Waller's concerns.

Even if a half-point hike doesn't happen, however, simply an increase in interest rates by 25 basis points could be enough to weigh on the markets, as it may effectively be confirmation that inflation isn't under control, and that given the Fed's serious stance on it, even more rate hikes could be on the horizon in 2027.

The S&P 500 has been falling, and its gains for the year are now around 8%, but the more concerning the outlook for the stock market, the more likely the index is to come down in the final weeks of the year.

I believe Dec. 9 will be a key turning point for the market, as news of another rate hike will trigger even greater selling, potentially leading the S&P 500 into a correction and possibly even a bear market before the year is over. It's thus more important than ever for investors to consider looking at safe investments to put into their portfolios.
