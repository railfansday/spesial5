Rockstar teases an entire animated TV show you can watch in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

As we drift closer to the release of GTA 6, set for next Thursday, we’re seeing levels of marketing unheard of before. Take-Two chief Strauss Zelnick warned us about Rockstar’s innovative marketing tactics, but never in my life would I have expected a full-blown animated TV show set in-universe.

As shared by Discussing Film on X, Rockstar Games has made an entire animated TV show called MACCA & FRIENDS, which follows Macca the Gator getting into all kinds of trouble in the state of Leonida. It is an in-universe TV show, meaning you will be able to watch it on GTA 6‘s fictional WEAZEL TV network (also appearing in GTA 5) and that it will be incorporating in-universe elements such as the mentioned state of Leonida (GTA‘s version of Florida). It’s breaking the first, second, third, and fourth walls with this show, and I fear this is only the beginning for GTA 6‘s wild marketing stunts.

Rockstar’s been selling MACCA & FRIENDS merch through its GTA 6 collector’s editions, with the gator serving as a sort of mascot for the entire game. I feel this won’t be the only time we see this little pink creature either outside or within the game, as the animation and voice acting quality are simply too high and probably cost way too much money for Rockstar to just leave it as-is (though, considering their unlimited budget, I wouldn’t be surprised if that exact thing happened anyway).

Miami HEAT is turning into Vice City for a spell and has already started promoting its GTA 6 jerseys and thematic equipment, and in the next few days we may yet see even wilder examples of what can happen when you have infinite money to spend on marketing and advertising.
