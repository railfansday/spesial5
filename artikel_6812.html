The latest AirTags, MacBooks, and AirPods are up to 40 percent off.

Our editors look forward to rare discounts on Apple devices every Black Friday, and this year’s are even better than expected. Now that Amazon’s Black Friday-Cyber Monday sale is here, we’re seeing up to 40 percent off Apple’s most coveted tech.
