It's easy for me, the style writer, to say you shouldn't determine whether or not you buy something based on how much it is (or is not) on sale. I know how the real world works, though. I write about denim all the time, and I use every opportunity I get to recommend the fancy stuff. Honestly though, I spend most days in a pair of $40 Wranglers that I bought on sale for $25, because I also am a regular American trying to get by. Used to be, good denim only cost a few bucks. Now, it's lower quality and costs more money. Reproductions of what used to be American working clothes now easily cost $300 or more. C'est la vie.

But I'm not here to talk about all that. I'm here to say like with my Wranglers, it's OK to wear cheap jeans. You can still be stylish if your pants cost $50. This month’s early Black Friday Amazon deals on Levi’s prove it.

We've got the 505, the modern Levi's fit I think works for most men, that's selling for between $45 and $55—depending on the wash you want and size you need. The 517, Levi's take on a Western cut, is going for a discount. And the 501—which I don't love in its modern iteration, but cannot deny the popularity of—is hovering just below $50, depending on the size and wash. Depending on the jeans you buy, you’re getting a discount of between 19 and 43 percent for the best cheap denim out there. If you're looking for a new jacket, which Levi's still makes decent versions of, Amazon has deals on two sherpa trucker jackets. Go with the classic denim for $63 or opt for the corduroy for $85.

Alright, that's enough explaining. Pick what speaks to you. Buy it. Then, go forth a new man with refreshed style.
