“Escape Velocity” Coming for Bitcoin with Repeat of 2017 Cycle | Michaël van de Poppe

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Fear & Greed Index has sat in extreme fear, and Michaël van de Poppe says people are upset that Bitcoin has underperformed other assets.

The Fear & Greed Index has sat in extreme fear, and Michaël van de Poppe says people are upset that Bitcoin has underperformed other assets. He says Bitcoin will survive the AI and quantum eras, and he expects the next bull market “to be comparable to 2017” as stablecoins, tokenization and the agentic economy finally find product-market fit. He says he hasn’t been this bullish since 2017.
