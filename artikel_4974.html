16 Best Early Black Friday Furniture Deals for Aesthetes (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Black Friday may still be a few weeks away, but furniture retailers aren’t waiting for the official festivities to begin. Some of our favorite brands are already rolling out discounts on living room furniture, bedroom sets, and statement pieces that have absolutely no business being in our shopping cart but somehow end up there anyway. Considering how quickly foundational pieces like a new sofa, bed frame, or dresser can gobble up a decorating budget, we’re certainly not complaining.

Of course, not every early November sale is technically a Black Friday sale. Some brands are soft launching their holiday shopping season while others are tempting us with fall clearance events and limited-time promotions. Either way, there is an abundance of furniture deals worth shopping well before November 27, some of which reach up to 70% off. We’ve rounded up early Black Friday furniture deals from top retailers that should be on your home decor radar, including AD-tested favorites, designer collaborations, and plenty of pieces that might finally convince you to update that room you’ve been meaning to refresh.

A good night’s sleep is an invaluable investment, but that doesn't mean you have to pay full price for it. Saatva is currently offering $350 off orders of $1,000 or more, giving you a head start on upgrading your sleep setup. Note: You need a mattress in your cart for the discount to apply. The Loom & Leaf is particularly attractive for memory foam devotees, which contributor Bailey Berg previously described as the “most breathable, comfortable mattress” she’d ever tried. And if a bedroom overhaul is in the cards, Saatva’s upholstered Halle storage platform bed offers a discreet place to stash extra bedding totally out of sight.

A little coastal influence can go a long way, even if the nearest beach is blanketed in snow during the off-season. Serena & Lily is currently hosting The Sale of the Year, with up to 40% off furniture, mirrors, and lighting, plus discounts across nearly every other product category. It’s a chance to bring the brand’s breezy, tailored aesthetic home in time for the holidays. We’re particularly fond of its outdoor lineup, including the Pacifica Sofa, which contributor Blake Bakkila put to the test for more than three years at her Newport Beach home. Despite its exposure to coastal moisture, she reported no fading, warping, or damage, and gave the sofa an overall A rating. For an indoor refresh, the Carter counter stool brings the brand’s signature natural texture into the kitchen.

There’s a reason Wayfair is a go-to destination when a room needs furnishing without a designer-size budget. The retailer’s Holiday Flash Sale is underway, with discounts of up to 70% sitewide. Its family of brands includes AllModern, a favorite for contemporary silhouettes at relatively approachable prices. Contributor Elise Portale has the AllModern Breanna chaise sectional and praises its soft upholstery. “At the end of the day, the best way I can describe it is ‘luxurious,’” she says. Take your living room refresh a step further with a ladder bookcase from Madison Park, or get a head start on decking the halls with festive finds at wallet-friendly prices.

For those with a penchant for clean-lined furniture, Article’s vast catalog of ready-to-ship mid-century-inspired pieces is a treasure trove. A sale section filled with Last-Chance pieces priced at up to 40% off only sweetens the deal. The Avenla side table is a space-saving addition beside a sofa or reading chair in a tight space—no assembly required. Don’t sleep on the Tessu bed frame, either, especially if you’re looking to soften up your bedroom with its low profile and ivory wool boucle upholstery.

A design classic rarely comes cheap, which makes Design Within Reach’s current Herman Miller promotion particularly noteworthy. Right now, you can score 20% off the brand’s iconic modern desk chairs and 25% off task and gaming chairs. For a home office that deserves a little more consideration than the average desk-and-chair combination, look to the Eames Soft Pad Management Chair or George Nelson’s Swag Leg Desk. Think of this as your sign to incorporate a little bit of legendary midcentury design into your workday for a fraction of the price. And don’t overlook Herman Miller’s lighting, tables, and home decor pieces, which are also included in this rare sale.

Shea McGee has the magic touch for making spaces feel collected and curated, and McGee & Co.’s furniture reflects the designer’s signature sensibility. The brand’s current sale features up to 70% off select pieces, with substantial reductions on dining chairs, end tables, nightstands, and bedroom furniture. The Beacon nightstand is 40% off, and the Gilmore canopy bed, a modern minimalist’s dream, is marked down to its lowest price ever. These contemporary heirlooms are more likely to be passed down from one generation to the next than they are to go out of style, so these prices are practically a steal.

Perusing Nickey Kehoe’s sale section is akin to sifting through buried treasure. The Los Angeles design studio and retailer is known for furniture that feels thoughtful, rich with character, and difficult to pin to any one decorating era. Its Last-Chance section offers up to 30% off distinctive pieces like the architectural Iron Canopy Bed and the Community Harvest Bench. Both have the kind of lived-in appeal that makes a room look as though it’s been furnished gradually, with pieces collected over time.

If your home office has been getting by with a less-than-ideal setup, Haworth’s current sale is a good reason to treat yourself. The office furniture specialist is currently offering 25% off select performance seating, desks, gaming furniture, lighting, and accessories, with ergonomic favorites like the Fern, Zody, and Soji chairs among its most popular pieces. But, the sale extends beyond seating. The Market Wire Frame Desk offers an industrial-leaning alternative to a conventional writing table, and the Elements Laminate Flare Leg Desk brings a cleaner, more minimalist silhouette to the workspace. Considering how much time we spend at our desks, a workspace that looks as good as it works hardly seems like an unreasonable indulgence.
