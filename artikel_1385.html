Fans Can Own Piece Of Iconic Tifo With New Limited-Edition Range

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Evertonians have the chance to own a genuine piece of supporter history, with material from the spectacular first fan-created tifo at Hill Dickinson Stadium transformed into a unique range of limited-edition accessories.

The original ‘On the Banks of the Royal Blue Mersey’ display, organised by supporters’ group The 1878s ahead of Everton’s Premier League fixture against Arsenal in December 2025, has been repurposed into a collection of tote bags, belt bags and bucket hats, giving supporters the opportunity to own a physical piece of one of the most memorable moments from the Club’s first season at its new waterfront home.

Created by Fanatics, the exclusive collection also includes royal blue and black T-shirts and a black hoodie featuring the distinctive design of the original display, complete with Everton’s iconic tower.

The striking tifo, which transformed the South Stand into a sea of royal blue, white and yellow, was a powerful demonstration of the passion and creativity of Evertonians and one of the standout moments of the Club’s historic first season at Hill Dickinson Stadium.

With only a limited amount of original material available, the tote bags, belt bags and bucket hats have been produced in strictly limited quantities, making each a special keepsake from a landmark moment in Everton’s history.

The collection also recognises the dedication and hard work of The 1878s, whose volunteers give their time to create spectacular displays, bring colour to the stands and help build the atmosphere at Everton’s new waterfront home.

In recognition of their contribution, a proportion of proceeds from the range will be donated to The 1878s, helping support the group’s continued efforts to create memorable displays and special moments for Evertonians.

The collection is a celebration of supporter culture and the important role fans continue to play in shaping the identity and atmosphere of Hill Dickinson Stadium, carrying forward the traditions and spirit of Goodison Park into a new era on the Liverpool waterfront.

With Christmas approaching, the range also offers a distinctive gift for Evertonians, whether it’s a T-shirt or hoodie bearing the tower design, or one of the exclusive tote bags, belt bags or bucket hats made from the original tifo. The T-shirts also make an ideal stocking filler for Blues looking for something a little different this festive season.

The limited-edition Tifo Collection is available now from official Everton stores and online

Supporters hoping to secure one of the exclusive tote bags, belt bags or bucket hats are encouraged to shop early, with only limited numbers available.
