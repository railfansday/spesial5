Multiple clubs are reportedly interested in signing Everton striker Thierno Barry as they look to strengthen their attacking options ahead of the upcoming transfer window.

According to a report from Fussball News, several clubs are closely monitoring the 24-year-old Frenchman, who has made an impressive start to life in English football following his summer move from Villarreal.

Barry has quickly become an important attacking figure at Everton, scoring ten goals in his first 13 appearances this season. His impressive performances have attracted attention from several clubs across Europe, with Newcastle United, Crystal Palace and Italian side Como also keeping tabs on his progress.

The French striker could be an interesting addition for the English clubs. They could use more firepower in the final third, and Barry’s physical presence, athleticism and goalscoring ability could make him an excellent option for their attacking department.

His ability to hold up the ball, compete with opposition defenders and contribute consistently in front of goal makes him an attractive prospect for clubs looking to improve their forward line.
