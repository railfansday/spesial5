Cristiano Ronaldo Fans Slam Portugal Duo After 3-2 Win vs Wales

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Portugal secured a hard-fought victory over Wales in Cardiff on Tuesday night, but the Nations League clash has been marred by an incident that occurred after the final whistle, and this has swiftly become the focus of heated online debate.

Jorge Jesus' side secured their first win of the post-Cristiano Ronaldo era, beating the Dragons 3-2 at the Cardiff City Stadium. Goals from Bruno Fernandes, Pedro Neto and Carlos Forbs cancelled out strikes from David Brooks and Sorba Thomas, earning Portugal redemption after they were thrashed at home by Denmark last week.

The result sees the Euro 2016 winners top their Nations League group, meaning they'll be seeded for the upcoming quarter-final draw.

Playing for just the second time since Ronaldo announced his retirement from international football, Fernandes and Joao Cancelo were involved in a minor post-match incident which hasn't gone down well amongst the legendary striker's most ardent fans.

Having been Portugal's talismanic figure for so many years, it's been a long time since the European nation have had a permanent captain leading them out who wasn't Ronaldo. The former Real Madrid star first wore the armband in 2007, and officially handed it over to Bernardo Silva after calling time on his international career earlier this month.

However, with Silva absent from the squad on Tuesday night in South Wales, Fernandes was given the honour of captaining his country against Craig Bellamy's men. The Manchester United playmaker was substituted in the dying embers of the game, and as he trudged off the pitch, he handed the armband to Cancelo.

As the two players walked over to applaud the travelling Portuguese supporters at full-time, the Sport TV broadcast captured a jubilant Fernandes asking the cameraman to film Cancelo. When the camera panned to the ex-Manchester City full-back, he repeatedly pointed to the captain's armband whilst smiling.

This seemingly innocuous moment hasn't been well received by many supporters online, who felt the gesture was intended to mock Ronaldo in some way, or perhaps to send the legendary forward an indirect message.

“Bunch of idiots. We know now the massive suffering Ronaldo faced dealing with these losers," one fan wrote on X.

"It’s obvious they don’t like him so let them be. Let’s just see how much progress they can make. Like I genuinely want to see how much progress they make after this," another added.

"Life will come at them so fast... let’s see what they win," another supporter chimed in.

Meanwhile, one fan labelled Fernandes and Cancelo as 'fools', claiming that the pair are 'jealous' of Ronaldo and his achievements.

Alexi Lalas' tweet about Christian Pulisic after Mauricio Pochettino's final USMNT game has caused a stir online.

"Look at those two fools. They think they have had it all. What goes around comes around. We all know these rats are so jealous of Ronaldo and they supported the idiot of a manager to drive Cristiano out," they wrote.

Elsewhere, one fan described the indicent as 'a disgraceful shame', while another wrote, bluntly: "They all hated Ronaldo."

Portugal will face one of Belgium, the Netherlands or Spain over two legs in the last eight of the Nations League in March.
