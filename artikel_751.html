Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando Are Married

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The couple began dating in 2021 after meeting on a blind date.

Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are married.

The couple tied the knot Saturday in Palm Springs, California, according to Entertainment Tonight. TMZ first reported that Cyrus, 33, and Morando, 28, were expected to wed over the weekend.

On Sunday, Cyrus confirmed her nuptials on Instagram with wedding photos of her and Morando. “M + M [white heart emoji] 11.21.26,” she captioned the post.

The Grammy winner’s stepsister Lily-Rose Purcell also shared photos on her Instagram Stories on Friday from the couple’s rehearsal dinner. One photo showed the dinner tables decorated with crimson flowers, along with a menu featuring “M+M,” the couple’s initials, printed at the top. The “Flowers” singer and Morando, also a musician, met on a blind date in 2021, she told British Vogue in 2023. The pair have also worked together, collaborating on her albums Endless Summer Vacation and Something Beautiful.

The couple’s engagement was confirmed in December 2025 after Cyrus debuted a shiny gold band on her left ring finger while posing for photos with Morando at the premiere of Avatar: Fire and Ash, for which she contributed the song “Dream as One.” People confirmed the engagement the following day.

More than two weeks after the news of their engagement broke, Us Weekly reported that the couple had “talked about eloping” instead of having a traditional wedding.

“Miley is really happy right now and in such a good, calm place,” an insider told the publication. “She’s not super into the idea of doing another wedding.”

Cyrus was previously married to Hunger Games actor Liam Hemsworth, whom she met while filming the 2010 film adaptation of Nicholas Sparks’s novel, The Last Song. After a decade-long, on-again, off-again relationship, the pair married in 2018 and divorced in 2020.
