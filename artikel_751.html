Sheehan to miss Ireland's Nations Championship finale after two-game ban

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Dan Sheehan has been suspended following a 'croc-roll' on Springbok loose forward Marco van Staden in the Nations Championship; he will miss Friday's fifth-place play-off against Australia

Ireland captain Dan Sheehan will miss Friday's Nations Championship fifth-place play-off against Australia after being handed a two-game ban for his red card against South Africa.

The forward was initially shown a yellow card by referee Christophe Ridley for a croc roll on Springbok replacement Marco van Staden in the 72nd minute of his country's 20-17 victory in Dublin.

The offence was subsequently upgraded to a red card by the foul play review officer.

World Rugby law 9.20d states "a player may remove the jackler from the tackle area by pushing/driving them backwards (including by grabbing the knee/leg), but must not roll, pull or twist an opponent".

Sheehan faced a disciplinary hearing on Monday evening where the panel ruled the incident was a "low entry point" offence and merited a two-week suspension.

The 28-year-old has accepted the sanction and will also miss Leinster's United Rugby Championship meeting with the Ospreys on Friday, December 4.

Typically a hooker, Sheehan started at number eight for the first time at Test level against the world champions.With Caelan Doris, Jack Conan and Sean Jansen all injured, head coach Andy Farrell now faces another reshuffle at the base of the scrum for the meeting with the Wallabies at Allianz Stadium, Twickenham.

Ronan Kelleher and Tom Stewart are set to continue as the two hookers in Ireland's matchday 23.
