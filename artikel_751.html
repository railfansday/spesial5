Why Ezra Frech's Ear Was Bleeding During DWTS Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Dancing With the Stars pro Daniella Karagach explained why her partner Ezra Frech's ear appeared to be bleeding during the Nov. 24 finale before the duo took home the Mirrorball trophy.

Clinching the Mirrorball trophy was music to Ezra Frech’s ears.

Especially since the Paralympian had to power through an ear injury while taking to the dance floor on the season 35 finale of Dancing With the Stars Nov. 24.

The 21-year-old raised eyebrows when fans spotted blood on his face after his freestyle performance with partner Daniella Karagach—but it turns out he’d gotten scratched from one of her costumes earlier in the show.

“I’m so freaking sorry,” Karagach exclaimed to Frech in her Nov. 24 Instagram Story. “Are you OK though?”

Frech assured her that he was indeed “fine,” joking that the cuts along his face and his ear were just “battle scars.”

Still, the dance pro was mortified by what her costume had done, exclaiming, “Oh my gosh, your ear. Dude. My dress.”

The track and field star acknowledged that the injury was quite noticeable, adding, “People were like, ‘Your ear was bleeding when you were getting interviewed.’”

Karagach gave her partner one final apology before the duo took to the ballroom for the live finale, waltzing into the history books as Frech became the first amputee and the youngest man to win Dancing With the Stars.

But it wasn’t just a bloody ear Frech was dealing with, as his prosthetic leg accidentally fell off during his freestyle dance to Linkin Park’s “Numb.”

“The leg just wasn't on correctly, and I felt it,” he explained to Variety following the episode. “Sometimes it'll readjust and I'll be OK. And so, as I was running up the steps, I'm like, ‘There's a 50 percent chance my leg will resuction and I'll be OK.’ And that wasn't the case.”

Thanks to the quick thinking of pro Alan Bersten as well as Karagach, Frech was able to take a moment and attach his limb mid-dance before the pair got back in step with the rest of their routine. And Karagach was certainly grateful to have had that moment to reset.

“I was just like, there is no other place in the routine that he can do this right now, and he cannot do the whole routine on one leg,” the 33-year-old explained. “So, I was like, ‘Just put your leg on.’ And it's not like—we had one little trick in there that he did on one, like a 540. It was really impressive. It was really great. But nothing's better than him just clicking on his leg properly and continuing on with the dance. I was like, ‘This is our only shot to do that.’”

The moment definitely left an impression on the audience and the judges, as Derek Hough told the stars, “This performance will have a ripple effect far beyond this show. What you did tonight was forever. Kids will look up to you and see what's possible."

Fellow judge Bruno Tonioli agreed, adding, "What we witnessed tonight goes beyond dancing. This is a powerful, beautiful, life-affirming statement that will inspire the nation and the world."
