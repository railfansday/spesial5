Marvel Started Planning for Robert Downey Jr.’s Doctor Doom Before Jonathan Majors Was Fired and ‘Avengers: The Kang Dynasty’ Fell Apart, Says Kevin Feige

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Before “Avengers: Doomsday,” there was “Avengers: The Kang Dynasty.” Marvel Studios boss Kevin Feige announced the latter title at Comic-Con in July 2022, with Jonathan Majors’ Kang the Conqueror poised to become the Marvel Cinematic Universe’s next Thanos-sized villain starting with 2023’s “Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania.”

Those plans, however, were upended a few weeks after “Quantumania” opened in theaters and earned some of Marvel’s worst reviews and box office. Majors was arrested in March 2023 after he assaulted his then-girlfriend in the backseat of a private vehicle. The actor was officially convicted in December 2023, with a six-person jury finding him guilty of two misdemeanor counts of harassment and assault. Marvel did not hesitate to cut ties with him, firing Majors immediately after his conviction was announced. The actor was then sentenced in April 2024 to one year of domestic violence counseling. With Majors’ firing, the Marvel Cinematic Universe would be overhauled, with “Avengers: The Kang Dynasty” being replaced by “Avengers: Doomsday.” The plan now moved to bringing back Robert Downey Jr. to the MCU, only this time not as Iron Man (who died in 2019’s “Avengers: Doomsday) but as the villainous Doctor Doom. However, Marvel Studios boss revealed in the latest issue of Empire magazine (via The Playlist) that Doom was in the conversation to be added to the MCU well before “The Kang Dynasty” fell apart amid Majors’ legal troubles.

“It probably would still have been called ‘The Kang Dynasty,’ but it would have been a set-up for Doom, midway through that movie,” Feige said, revealing the plan for “The Kang Dynasty” was actually for it to be a bait-and-switch movie in which Doom was revealed as the real Thanos-sized villain of the MCU’s multiverse saga.

According to Feige, Marvel first got in touch with Robert Downey Jr’s agent (telling him: “We have an idea, it’s time”) around the same time as the “Quantumania” premiere in February 2023, weeks before Majors’ arrest in New York City.

“It was very delicate,” Feige added about the Majors situation. “We didn’t want to rush into any rash decisions in terms of employment. People think there was a cause-and-effect with Jonathan’s issues, and that certainly accelerated some things. But the Doom of it all was well underway before that… ‘Quantumania’ was not received the way we would have liked. There are many elements of that that we’re proud of, the Kang performance being one of them. But we were changing [‘The Kang Dynasty’] even at that point.”

“Avengers: Doomsday” opens in theaters Dec. 18.
