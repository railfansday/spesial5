Inside the Zane Lowe Show, Apple Music & Why the Music Biz ‘Drives [Him] Crazy’ | On The Record

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Zane Lowe has spent his career at the intersection of music, culture and conversation. From his early days in radio to his role as Apple Music’s global creative director, he’s had a front-row seat to the evolution of the music industry — and the way artists share their work with the world. Lowe sits down with Billboard On The Record host Kristin Robinson at Apple Music Studios to reflect on his career, Apple Music’s editorial strategy and how artist press runs have changed over the years. With the interviewer in the hot seat, Robinson turns the tables to ask Lowe about his approach to conversations with artists, how he builds trust with his guests and what it takes to get them to open up.

It was it was the Jay-Z interview, which he insisted we make long form, and it got me back to almost where I started, which was this this space where conversation can run free.

I think about how many artists could have made it if the circumstances were different.

Much longer list than the ones that did, and I’ve just met so many people who are so talented and so gifted, but just made the wrong decision when it came to partnering with somebody, a label, a manager, even a lawyer or a PR person.

Do you ever get jaded being so close to the music industry?

Oh, yeah. I mean, there are days when I’m where I don’t. There are things I just don’t understand. I never get jaded by music. The listening of it, the playing it.

Zane Lowe, the New Zealand-born radio host and DJ, is arguably the most prominent music interviewer out there right now. Often nailing interview guests unwilling to do any other press but something with him, he also acts as Apple Music’s global creative director, defining the music streaming service’s strategy and editorial, and in connecting with artists. Today, we’re taking over Zane Lowe’s chair at Apple Music Studio in Culver City, California, for a special interview with the interviewer himself. Zane Lowe.
