Appalachian Trail 30 Years later

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Time for a quick introduction.  My trail name is Hops and I’m from Franklin, NC.  I’m a brewer at Lazy Hiker brewery and spend a lot time on the trail.  1997 was my first Thru Hike on the Appalachian Trail and now at 50 years of age I’m going for another walk in the woods.  Most of my summer days are backpacking on trail and paddling on the lake.  Winter time months I do ski patrol on the local mountain.  I’m excited to be apart of the 2027 crew on the Appalachian Trail.

I first stepped on the AT back in 1989 in VA.  Hiking up Mt. Rogers before Thomas Knob Shelter was built, I had a connection to the trail.  Years would go by and all I knew the AT was in VA.  After what I thought was a long day of backpacking, a older hiker stopped and setup camp.  His pack was as big as I was at the time!  I remember asking him where he was going.  He reply, ” Hiking to Maine on the AT”.  I thought you can do this!  I was hooked and took off on my adventure in 1997.  But this story is going to be about a Thru Hike in 2027.

I’ve always wanted to do another Thru Hike and really considered the CDT.  The AT has the culture that I enjoy and living in a trail town talking with hikers every season, well the AT just has ahold on me.  I thought wouldn’t it the cool to do the same thru hike 30 years later.  The trail is the same, sort of.  The gear is lighter, no maildrops, and a trail app on my phone.

I spend a lot of time outdoors, and I know the first 110 miles of the AT in my sleep.  The Smoky Mountains are in my backyard, and I love climbing 6,000 footers for fun.  Before returning to the east I spent most of my time in CO and WY.  Playing in the Tetons and skiing in CO is my fountain of youth.  I love new gear and I’m a little bit of a gear head, but I will talk about my gear in the next Blog.
