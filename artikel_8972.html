Stricter legislation is coming in, and it may affect access to CBD and THC products. The federal government is changing how they define “hemp” at the federal level. The new definition seeks to ban hemp-derived THC, the cannabis compound that causes a “high.”

The current law states that hemp is any part of the cannabis sativa plant that contains up to 0.3% of THC by dry weight. The change will restrict any hemp-derived products to a total of 0.4 milligrams (mg) of THC per container. The new law also bans synthetic cannabinoids, affecting CBD manufacturing. Cannabinoids, like CBD or THC, occur naturally in the hemp plant. However, chemical synthesis is often necessary to make CBD available on a commercial level, and this is where we may see further restrictions.
