The NFL officials who pulled double duty got double pay (as they should)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Some things go without saying. Nowadays, however, it’s sometimes important to state the obvious.

Via Mark Maske of Front Office Sports, the four officials who ended up officiating two games on Sunday (Don Willard, Terry Killens, Jabir Walker, and Justin Larrew) will get paid for working two games.

Why wouldn’t they? Getting twice the pay for twice the work should be a given. The real question is whether they should have gotten even more.

Folks who work overtime usually get time-and-a-half pay. In some instances, going above and beyond the call of duty results in double pay.

Here’s the real question — did the officials who were supposed to work one game in Week 10 but didn’t get still get their pay? Or do officials only get paid for the games they actually officiate, even if/when the NFL seemingly overreacts, reshuffles the deck, and cancels an official’s assignment that the official was otherwise ready and willing to fulfill?

It still seems that the NFL overreacted to the situation, turning the officiating assignments upside down in lieu of deciding to do whatever needed to be done to get the officials to their assigned game so that the existing crews wouldn’t be disrupted.
