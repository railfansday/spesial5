Georgia Dominates to Clinch Sixth Straight SEC Championship Berth

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Georgia Bulldogs are heading back to Atlanta. No. 1 Georgia overcame an early challenge before dominating South Carolina 34-6 on Saturday night at Williams-Brice Stadium, officially clinching a spot in the SEC Championship Game.

Behind a strong performance from quarterback Gunner Stockton, a two touchdown night from running back Nate Frazier, and another impressive defensive showing, the Bulldogs pulled away to secure one of their biggest victories of the season.

The win improves Georgia to 10-1 overall and 8-1 in SEC play. More importantly, the Bulldogs have secured their sixth consecutive appearance in the SEC Championship Game, becoming the first program to accomplish that feat.

With the 2026 season marking the final SEC Championship Game under the conference's current format before the College Football Playoff expands to 24 teams, Georgia has earned another opportunity to compete for a conference title.

South Carolina entered Saturday hoping to pull off a major upset in front of its home crowd. The Gamecocks came out with plenty of energy and managed to make things difficult for Georgia during the opening quarter.

South Carolina quarterback LaNorris Sellers helped lead the offense down the field on its first two possessions, putting the Gamecocks in position to score. They converted those opportunities into field goals and took an early 6-3 lead.

Georgia struggled to find its rhythm initially, but the Bulldogs eventually began to take control in the second quarter.

Stockton completed an important fourth down pass to Jaden Reddell before connecting with Talyn Taylor on a 37 yard completion that moved Georgia inside the South Carolina 5 yard line.

Frazier finished the drive with a one yard touchdown run, giving the Bulldogs a 10-6 advantage.

That score completely changed the momentum. Georgia's defense began limiting the Gamecocks' opportunities, while the offense found more success moving the football.

Georgia's offensive performance was led by Stockton and Frazier, who continued to demonstrate why the Bulldogs are one of the most dangerous teams in college football.

Stockton completed 19 of 23 passes for 281 yards, controlling the offense and consistently finding opportunities downfield. His ability to distribute the football helped Georgia maintain possession and keep South Carolina's defense on the field.

Frazier was equally important to the victory. The running back finished with 94 rushing yards and two touchdowns, providing the physical presence Georgia needed to take control of the game.

His second touchdown came on Georgia's opening possession of the second half. Frazier powered into the end zone from two yards out, extending the Bulldogs' advantage and making it 17-6.

Georgia continued to build on that momentum throughout the second half. Craig Dandridge Jr. added a 14-yard touchdown reception from Stockton, giving the Bulldogs another scoring opportunity and putting the game further out of reach.

The offense also benefited from strong contributions across the receiving group, with Stockton spreading the ball around and avoiding turnovers.

Although the offense eventually took over, Georgia's defense deserves plenty of credit for the victory.

After allowing South Carolina to move the football early, the Bulldogs completely shut down the Gamecocks for the remainder of the night.

Sellers finished with only 87 passing yards and was unable to generate the explosive plays South Carolina needed to stay competitive. Georgia also recorded three sacks and consistently pressured the quarterback.

One of the biggest defensive moments came when safety Khalil Barnes intercepted Sellers inside the Georgia 20-yard line. The turnover ended one of South Carolina's most promising drives of the second half and prevented the Gamecocks from getting back into the game.

Georgia also created problems on special teams. Sacovie White-Helton returned a punt 35 yards to the South Carolina 21-yard line, setting up another scoring opportunity for the Bulldogs.

By the end of the night, Georgia had turned an early six point deficit into a convincing 28 point victory.

The biggest takeaway from Saturday's performance is that Georgia has officially clinched its place in the SEC Championship Game.

The Bulldogs will make their sixth consecutive appearance, continuing a remarkable run of success under head coach Kirby Smart. Georgia has consistently remained among the conference's top programs, and Saturday's victory ensures that its pursuit of another SEC championship remains alive.

Georgia will now turn its attention toward its regular season finale against Georgia Tech. The Bulldogs will have an opportunity to finish the regular season strong before heading to Atlanta on December 5.

With a powerful running game, Stockton playing with confidence, and a defense capable of controlling opposing offenses, Georgia enters the final stretch with plenty of momentum.

The Bulldogs came into Columbia needing one more victory to secure their championship opportunity. They left with a dominant 34-6 win, another statement performance, and a ticket back to the SEC Championship Game.
