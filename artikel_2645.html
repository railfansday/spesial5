Why the Cowboys should beat the Eagles, and why they could fail

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Dallas Cowboys are after history on Thanksgiving this year, looking to beat the Philadelphia Eagles on this holiday for the first time in franchise history. The Cowboys have beaten the New York Giants three times on Thanksgiving, and the Washington Commanders nine times, and proven they can beat all of these division rivals under Brian Schottenheimer, but need a Turkey Day win over the birds to secure their first winning streak of this 2026 season. The Eagles are the smallest sample size of division opponents on Thanksgiving, but the Cowboys are 0-2 against them.

As has been a theme in this rivalry, this Thursday game will be yet another new quarterback matchup. Dak Prescott has been the mainstay in the last three meetings, with two of them coming against Jalen Hurts. The week seven meeting this year was Prescott against Tanner McKee though, and now on Thanksgiving it will be a red-hot Prescott against a passer many Cowboys fans thought would actually be Prescott's backup this season – Joe Milton.

Milton has started the last two games for the Eagles, and got them back in the win column this past Sunday off their bye, at home, against the Pittsburgh Steelers. It was an ugly win, but any type of win would do for Philly coming into that in-state battle on a seven-game losing streak. Now, the Eagles will be looking to go from beating Mike McCarthy in Week 11 to beating the coach that replaced McCarthy in Dallas in the same week.

The Cowboys will be looking to keep the NFC East race alive, and getting a fifth straight Thanksgiving win would go a long way in doing just that.

Let's get to our weekly scenarios on how this game could play out.

The Cowboys are 11-12 in the red zone scoring touchdowns over their last three games, and a perfect 9-9 in the last two games – both 40+ point outbursts at home. You have to go all the way back to week six to find the last nine times the Eagles have reached the red zone, covering five games where they are 1-4.

The Eagles have not scored more than 20 points since they did so against the Cowboys on that Monday night win for Dallas, and no more than those 21 points since week three when Hurts was still starting. They have only been to the red zone once in each of their last two games. They went 0-1 against the Steelers, but held them to 0-2 to continue to frustrate Pittsburgh when playing in Philadelphia and get a win. The Steelers offensive performance was so bad they've decided to bench Aaron Rodgers for their next game on Black Friday, in case there was any wonder about if it was more good Eagles defense or bad Steelers offense in Week 11.

Coming off a game where the Cowboys scored literally every time they were trying to do so offensively, the bar is low to be able to use their offense to keep the pressure on against another opponent with a losing record. It was the passing game that was the catalyst to achieve this against the Tennessee Titans, but back in week seven it was more of the run game that helped the Cowboys beat the Eagles with McKee on the road.

The Titans are also dead last in time of possession differential by a healthy margin right now, but in second to last is the next opponent for the Cowboys with these Eagles. A good blend of the run game and high percentage pass game that leads to drives ending in seven points will keep the Cowboys best players on the field deciding this game, and keep the defense playing in the positions they need to be in, rushing the passer against an Eagles offensive line that's a shell of its former self.

Everything we've covered about the Eagles so far hardly makes them look like a potent offense that's going to create explosive plays. In a game where that may be their only chance to win though, getting such plays against the Cowboys defense is still not out of the realm of possibility.

After all, of all the games they've played without Hurts, their best point total was against the Cowboys. They also went a perfect 3-3 in the red zone that night, and got a 29-yard run from Saquon Barkley. Barkley's longest run in the two games immediately following playing the Cowboys was just five yards.

Barkley did get back in the big play column against the Steelers with a 38-yard touchdown. Darius Cooper and Dallas Goedert also had receptions of 29 and 38 yards. The Eagles missed even more big play opportunities to Devonta Smith downfield, but when the game came down to converting a 3rd-and-7 to run out the clock, they went empty out of the shotgun and trusted Milton to hit Smith right at the sticks to win the Steelers game.

The Cowboys defense does not have nearly a big enough sample size under Mike Pettine yet to know what their potential is with this change of play-caller. The thought for now is that potentially it is at least sound enough to make big time performances by their own offense stand up, something they simply weren't doing under Christian Parker. However, with both coaches on the headset, putting a cap on giving up huge backbreaking plays has not been solved just yet.

Joe Milton will know the Cowboys defensive personnel well, is coming off a win, and has these home run threats in Smith and Barkley. If the Cowboys can't keep at top on their defense against Smith getting downfield for "bazooka Joe" throws, or contain Barkley on the ground, the Eagles could pull off an upset that would send Dallas into the final month of the regular season still without back-t0-back wins.
