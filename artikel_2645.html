Mike Macdonald: "Everything's positive" with Sam Darnold, but nothing official yet

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Seahawks head coach Mike Macdonald said at the start of the team’s bye week that he was “really optimistic” about quarterback Sam Darnold returning in Week 12 and that view didn’t change after the team’s return to work on Monday.

Macdonald said that Darnold “looked great” in the team’s practice and that “everything’s positive” regarding his recovery from quad and rib injuries, but he stopped short of making any definitive declarations about Darnold getting the start against the 49ers.

“I think before making any declarations with Sam, we’ll get through Wednesday, see how Wednesday goes,” Macdonald said, via the team’s website. “So nothing official yet. . . . He was able to train a little bit here, do some rehab here, he also was able to get away. He’s optimistic, but I think with today’s practice cadence and speed of today, it’s probably wise to wait to make any declarations until we get through Wednesday’s practice.”

The Seahawks are also monitoring players like left tackle Charles Cross, right tackle Abraham Lucas, and defensive lineman Leonard Williams as they recover from injuries, so Wednesday will be informative about how the Seahawks will line up on several fronts.
