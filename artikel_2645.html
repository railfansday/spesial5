College football stock up, stock down from Week 11: Playoff hopes rise and die

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Two top 15 teams lost this weekend in college football, and those were the only real upsets of the weekend. No. 14 Houston fell to Colorado, 42-32, on Friday night and you can now take the Cougars completely out of the College Football Playoff discussion.

The other came in Lexington, where No. 11 Kentucky fell to Florida. The Wildcats' offense could only produce 14 points, while Florida scored 21 on the 'Cats. Kentucky is now likely out of the playoff.

There is still one more game to be played this weekend. Ole Miss and Oklahoma will play at 1:00 p.m. ET due to the FAA grounding flights on Friday night. Ole Miss wasn't able to fly to Norman for the game, and the SEC opted to move it to Sunday.

Here's our stock up, stock down from Saturday.

Miami squeaked by Duke on Saturday and is all but officially headed to the ACC Championship Game. However, the 'Canes weren't sure who they would play in the game. The Wake Forest-SMU game felt like the game that was going to determine that.

And it wasn't even close. Wake Forest dominated the Mustangs, winning 49-7, and forcing three turnovers. This has been the story of Jake Dickert's Wake Forest team this season. The Demon Deacons score a ton of points, and their defense allows minimal.

Wake Forest plays Georgia Tech and Duke to finish the season. Barring a stumbling block, Wake Forest has proven it belongs in the playoff.

USC Trojans quarterback Jayden Maiava (14) passes during the NCAA football game against the Ohio State Buckeyes at Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum on Oct. 31, 2026. Credit: USA TODAY Network via Reuters Connect (Reuters)

It's going to be another season that Lincoln Riley can't get USC into the playoff. Riley, who has already been on the hot seat, is going to have to answer questions regarding why he can't get USC over the hump. The Trojans had a big win over Ohio State two weeks ago, and USC had chances to beat Indiana this weekend.

But the Hoosiers won 27-13 and they intercepted Jayden Maiava three times in the game. That was the one thing USC couldn't do in this game is give the Hoosiers' defense chances. Curt Cignetti already felt a chip on his shoulder have the playoff committee lowered Indiana's ranking after it had a bye week.

IU's offense wasn't overly impressive, but it did enough to get past USC, and now the Trojans are hoping to get to a respectable bowl game and upset rival UCLA in two weeks.

ESPN's 'College GameDay' was in Boise for the Broncos game against Oregon State, and it lived up to the hype. The Beavers didn't have Beau Atkinson behind center, and former Texas and Duke transfer Maalik Murphy started in his place, and he gave Boise State all it could handle.

The Broncos escaped with a 46-41 win and Boise State moved to 9-1. Not only did the Broncos get a key win, but their main competition, New Mexico, suffered a loss to UNLV. Barring a crazy turn of events, Boise State will capture the No. 12 spot in the playoff.

Nov 7, 2026; Knoxville, Tennessee, USA; Kentucky Wildcats head coach Will Stein looks on during the first half against the Tennessee Volunteers at Neyland Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Randy Sartin-Imagn Images Credit: IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect (Reuters)

It's been the story of college football this season — upsets. Florida has been reeling in recent weeks, but the Gators were the one-time No. 8 team in the land. That ended rather quickly and Florida has been going back and forth on quarterbacks.

But it wasn't the Florida offense that beat Kentucky. The Wildcats' offense was a disaster. Kentucky gained just 242 yards of total offense and after losing 21-14, the 'Cats now have three losses. There are a lot of teams that are going to be contending for the final spot or two with three losses.

With Missouri losing this week, the Wildcats' final two games will come against unranked teams, and it will be hard for Kentucky to get enough credit to get back into the playoff race. Still, a great season under new coach Will Stein.

Nov 7, 2026; Lubbock, Texas, USA; Texas Tech Red Raiders quarterback Thomas Castellanos (4) flips the ball in the first half against the West Virginia Mountaineers at Galaxy Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Michael C. Johnson-Imagn Images Credit: IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect (Reuters)

This was a game where Texas Tech could've lost on Saturday night. The Red Raiders went to Oklahoma State and were set for a shootout with the Cowboys. But it was Texas Tech that dominated. The Red Raiders would win 52-27, and it's arguably the best Texas Tech's defense has looked in recent weeks.

Thomas Castellanos has provided a nice spark for the offense, replacing Will Hammond at quarterback. The Big 12 is starting to trend toward having two teams in the playoff, and it's likely going to come down between Texas Tech and BYU.

Nov 14, 2026; Baton Rouge, Louisiana, USA; Louisiana State Tigers head coach Lane Kiffin looks on before the game against the Texas Longhorns at Tiger Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Stephen Lew-Imagn Images Credit: IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect (Reuters)

Back-to-back weeks, LSU falls at home. The Tigers have been favored in every game this season, yet Lane Kiffin and Co. are 6-4 after losing a heartbreaker to Texas on Saturday, 22-21. The Tigers found themselves up late, but Arch Manning found running back Raleek Brown for a touchdown reception with three seconds on the clock.

Last weekend, Alabama came to Death Valley and crushed LSU; this weekend, the Tigers lost to the Longhorns, despite Arch Manning throwing three interceptions. But to make up for that, Sam Leavitt tossed three interceptions of his own.

A win this weekend and LSU still had an argument to make the playoff, but that's out the window and Kiffin will already begin looking at 2027 with two weeks left.
