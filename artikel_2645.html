Cade Cunningham has just 5 points in Pistons' ugly loss to Hawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

ATLANTA – A late collapsed cost the Detroit Pistons on the road.

They fell to the Atlanta Hawks at State Farm Arena, 107-97, after giving up an 18-0 run to open the fourth quarter. It took more than seven minutes for the Pistons to score a point in the final quarter, and the Hawks turned an 11-point Pistons lead into a seven-point deficit, 92-85, with 6:39 remaining in the game.

Their first points weren't scored until the 4:52 mark, missing seven in a row before Isaiah Joe knocked down a 3-pointer to cut Atlanta's lead back to four. The Pistons lost the offensive glass, 24-8, and were destroyed 27-6 in second chance points.

The Pistons couldn't overcome a poor game from Cade Cunningham, who finished with five points, 15 assists and four rebounds. They were led in scoring by Joe (19 points). Ausar Thompson added 18 points, 11 rebounds, five assists and two blocks, and Collins also scored 18.

After going scoreless in the first half, Cunningham made his first bucket of the night – a midrange jumper – with 10:46 to play in the third quarter. His next bucket wasn't until the game was nearly over, draining a 3-pointer with 36 seconds left.

It was the first time this season Cunningham failed to score at least 10 points, and he shot just 2-for-9 overall. Foul trouble limited him early, as he picked up his third early in the second quarter. But he mostly was content to conduct the action – his 15 assists were a season-high.

In the first half, the Pistons were out-rebounded on the offensive glass, 13-4, with a 13-0 disparity in second chance points. Poor work on the boards coast the Pistons during the Hawks' 18-0 run as well, as they gave up four offensive rebounds and seven second-chance points during the run.

A strength for the Pistons last season has become a weakness. John Collins and Paul Reed have been unable to provide the physical, consistent rebounding presence in the paint that they are accustomed to with Jalen Duren. Beyond the cold stretch in the fourth quarter, the Pistons also were unable to win the possession game.
