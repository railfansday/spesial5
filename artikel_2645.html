Aljamain Sterling Reveals Umar Nurmagomedov’s Alleged Role in Yas Hilton Hotel Brawl With Merab Dvalishvili

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The controversy surrounding the altercation at the Yas Hilton Hotel during RAF Abu Dhabi has taken another unexpected turn. The incident involved Merab Dvalishvili and Saba Lazishvili, who were confronted by Usman Nurmagomedov and members of his Dagestani entourage.

Videos circulating online captured the moment the PFL champion approached Dvalishvili and slapped the Georgian fighter, setting off a mass brawl involving multiple individuals. Authorities subsequently detained all 10 people involved, with the group spending nearly 48 hours in custody.

Dvalishvili and Lazishvili eventually walked free after reportedly receiving a $14,000 fine without any jail time. The outcome was harsher for the Dagestani contingent. Eight individuals, including Nurmagomedov, were reportedly handed six-month prison sentences, alongside an $82,000 fine and deportation orders to be enforced after their prison terms.

Following a revision of the original ruling, all eight convicted Dagestanis have now been released, according to an update shared by the Abu Dhabi Criminal Court on X. While this development could appear to bring the legal proceedings to a close, the fallout from the hotel brawl may not be over just yet.

Dvalishvili's teammate and former UFC bantamweight champion, Aljamain Sterling, has revealed more harrowing details of the attack that were not captured on tape. First, Aljo slammed the Dagestani contingent for a cowardly attack.

In an interview with ESPN, Sterling did not hold back when discussing the incident, saying, "I wish I was there—me and the other boys—to see if they would have kept that same energy because they have this M.O. for doing this type of thing."

He further questioned the manner in which the Dagestani group confronted Dvalishvili, adding, "They don't approach guys one-on-one, and I would have respected Usman if he would have done that, or established he has a problem, or him slapping Merab and establishing he had a problem. But to do that with a group of guys, it's just soft. It's just animalistic behavior."

However, Sterling's biggest allegation concerned what happened after the cameras stopped recording. He claimed that Umar Nurmagomedov, who was absent from the viral footage circulating online, joined the altercation later and attacked Dvalishvili. Sterling said, "Umar was nowhere to be found, but after the camera turns off, Umar then comes up and punches Merab in the face."

The former champion also questioned Umar Nurmagomedov's conduct during the aftermath of the initial confrontation, stating, "It looked like he was going to de-escalate the situation because this was after everything kind of settled down. What about this is respectable?"

Sterling went on to describe the alleged off-camera events as particularly disturbing. He concluded, "I could have let the first clip that was shown on video go, but after I heard about everything that happened afterward — nah. To Umar walking up and doing what he did. Then them holding down Saba — pinning his arms so they could stomp his head. I think that's attempted murder."

These allegations also align with Dvalishvili's previous account of the incident on The Ariel Helwani Show. The Georgian fighter claimed that the 30-year-old bantamweight attacked him after the initial confrontation and attributed his injuries, including multiple cuts and lacerations, to the events that unfolded once the cameras were off.

Interestingly, despite the alleged severity of the incident, Dvalishvili requested the release of the men who were imprisoned over the brawl. However, Sterling's latest allegations raise further questions about what transpired inside the hotel and whether the initial footage captured the full extent of the violence.
