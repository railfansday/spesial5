NFC West Recap: 49ers lead grows to 3 games; Seahawks, Rams on bye

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The San Francisco 49ers walked into Mexico City on Sunday Night Football against the Minnesota Vikings, the defense allowing a league-low 14.5 points per game, and left with a 35-10 win that wasn't that close.

The big story is none other than Brock Purdy. He went 21-of-26 for 255 yards passing, 9.8 yards per attempt, three touchdowns, and no interceptions, good for a 146.0 passer rating. This is against the best defense in the league. Brian Flores' defense lives on pressure, and it got to Purdy once all night. Two of the touchdowns went to George Kittle and the other to Mike Evans, who finished with 109 receiving yards.

The one sour note: right tackle Colton McKivitz left with an ankle injury, and Vederian Lowe finished the game. Lowe didn't play bad at all, but now that he might be taking the starting job, defenses will be writing the book on how to get past him.

The Seattle Seahawks and Los Angeles Rams are both on a bye, so this recap will be a bit brief. In short, the 49ers' lead grew from 2.5 games to three. They have tough sledding, starting with the Seahawks in Week 11; if they can win that game, it might be time to start wondering when they can wrap this division thing up.

Is there a game left on the schedule anyone can see the Arizona Cardinals winning?

With rookie quarterback Carson Beck ailing, 40-year-old Josh Johnson, who signed off the Cincinnati Bengals' practice squad earlier this month, got the start against the Kansas City Chiefs. That makes his, like, 15th different team he started on in his career.

Arizona's lone touchdown, a 2-yard catch by wide receiver Simi Fehoko, came in the second quarter. It's the only offensive touchdown the Cardinals have scored in three games. I guess you can call this progress, but with all the injuries the Cardinals are sustaining, it's a matter of them getting out of the season without more injuries to the roster.

Of course, the Cardinals defense is always there to pad out Patrick Mahomes's stats; 324 yards and three touchdowns, two of them to tight end Travis Kelce. And the Sunday Night Football broadcast made sure you knew about it. The Chiefs got more than 400 yards of offense in a 34-7 win.

One touchdown in three weeks plus one of the league's worst defenses adds up to historically bad football, something 49ers fans know all to well, provided they can remember things a decade ago. The Cardinals were never going to be in contention this year, and their coaching is an upgrade over last year. It's just a matter of time.

The Rams kick off Thanksgiving week on Wednesday night, hosting the Green Bay Packers (6-4) at Levi's South SoFi Stadium on Netflix. Green Bay is also coming off a Week 11 bye, so neither side has a rest edge—which is probably why they are playing on a Wednesday to begin with. I can't believe I'm about to start rooting for the Packers, but here we are.

The Seahawks visit Levi's Stadium on Sunday. 49ers win here would push the lead to four games with six left. Drew Lock has won both starts since Sam Darnold went down. The Seahawks will be starting Darnold against the 49ers. Last time the Seahawks faced the 49ers at Levi's was 2025's Great Disappointment, so anything can be better than that.

The Cardinals host the Washington Commanders, who were 3-5-1 going into Monday Night Football against the Bengals. Good luck, Cardinals? I mean, that's all I got there.
