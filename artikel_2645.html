Chicago Blackhawks’ Connor Bedard scheduled to miss 4 months after right shoulder surgery on Monday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Chicago Blackhawks captain Connor Bedard will miss approximately four months after undergoing right shoulder surgery on Monday, team physician Dr. Michael Terry said in a statement.

Dr. Peter Millett, the surgeon who worked on Bedard's left shoulder in the summer, performed the operation, which the team statement called "successful."

The 21-year-old center re-injured his right shoulder during Tuesday's 6-3 win, after forward Nick Paul's faceoff swipe hit Bedard on the right hand. He first injured the shoulder last December when St. Louis Blues center Brayden Schenn's stick hit under Bedard's right arm in a faceoff, sidelining him for 12 games. The Hawks' center rehabbed that injury over the summer, opting out of events like the IIHF World Championship in May.

In July, he underwent surgery on his left shoulder after crashing into the boards during an unofficial practice in Burnaby, British Columbia. Hawks general manager Kyle Davidson had told reporters on Saturday that Monday's operation would "likely" be the same type of surgery.

Bedard also had a four-month healing timeline for his left shoulder surgery, but returned in three months and five days, missing just six games of this season. But barring a quicker comeback this time around, the center is set to have the shortest regular season of his career.

He missed 14 games in his Calder Trophy-winning rookie season with a fractured jaw, but played his entire sophomore season in 2024-25. The 2023 No. 1 draft pick has scored 221 points (82 goals, 139 assists) in 234 career games.

The Hawks (8-14-2) won back-to-back games this weekend over the Calgary Flames and Vancouver Canucks. They travel to play the Seattle Kraken on Wednesday and return home to host the New York Rangers on Friday.
