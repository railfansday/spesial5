Urbiha’s 13 help George Washington down Elon 69-50

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

WASHINGTON (AP) — Lovro Urbiha's 13 points helped George Washington defeat Elon 69-50 on Tuesday.

Urbiha added seven rebounds for the Revolutionaries (3-0). Dylan James scored 12 points and added 13 rebounds and three blocks. Jean Aranguren shot 3 of 7 from the field and 6 for 8 from the line to finish with 12 points.

Josh Dalton led the way for the Phoenix (2-1) with 15 points, 12 rebounds, two steals and three blocks. Hunter Harding added eight points and six rebounds for Elon.

George Washington held a 23-22 lead at halftime. James scored eight first-half points. George Washington pulled away with an 11-0 run in the second half to extend a seven-point lead to 18 points. Urbiha led the way with a team-high 10 second-half points.

Both teams next play Friday. George Washington hosts Harvard and Elon plays Chattanooga at home.
