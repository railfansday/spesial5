‘Only so much time and energy’: Mike McCarthy reacts to Mike Tomlin’s ‘dumpster fire’ remark

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Pittsburgh Steelers defeated the Denver Broncos 27-5 on Friday. It was an impressive showing wire-to-wire for the Steelers in Mason Rudolph's first start of the season, after the team parted ways following a tumultuous week in which veteran quarterback Aaron Rodgers was benched and released after airing out his many grievances with head coach Mike McCarthy on The Pat McAfee Show.

On Thursday night, Pittsburgh's previous head coach, Mike Tomlin, who spent 19 seasons with the organization, addressed the chaos that the franchise he led for 19 seasons was dealing with.

"I'm upset about it. It's a dumpster fire, and I don't say that in a judgmental way. I've presided over a few dumpster fires in my 19 years, but it certainly is upsetting and I'm sure Steeler Nation is upset," Tomlin told NBC's Mike Tirico.

Tomlin added that McCarthy's future with the Steelers would be dependent on his ability to rally and unite the locker room.

It certainly created some challenges. And challenges is probably an understatement if all of those things are true," Tomlin said.

"But his ability to overcome that or inability to overcome that in my opinion is based on relationships and the depth of those relationships and how those guys feel about him or how those guys buy into what he's selling," he added.

McCarthy seemed to overcome those challenges, if Friday's performance was anything to judge by, and after the game, he was asked about his predecessor's remarks.

"I really don't have one. I mean, let's just be real here. There's only so much time and energy in the day, and I think you have to, when you go through things like that, I have to put all my energy into winning," McCarthy said, via Blitzburgh. "And that's really what I have. I get that this is part of the responsibility, and the opinions and comments of others is why I'm in here to answer questions, but I'm focused on winning."

McCarthy and the Steelers will now have a couple of extra rest and preparation days before they face the Houston Texans next Sunday night.
