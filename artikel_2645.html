FC Bayern trio make FIFPRO shortlist after votes from nearly 20,000 professional players

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

FIFPRO released 26-player shortlists for both men and women categories ahead of next week's World 11 announcement. The award began in 2005, and it was held in partnership with FIFA between 2009 and 2023.

In addition to the selection of teams in men's and women's professional football, there will be an award for the best players. Despite missing out on winning the Ballon d'Or, Robert Lewandowski won the award in back-to-back years during his time at FC Bayern.

The Rekordmeister have three players in the shortlist for this year's World 11 as FIFPRO unveiled the nominations on Monday based on votes by 19,806 professional men's footballers worldwide. Harry Kane, Dayot Upamecano and Michael Olise will hope to join the previous 11 FC Bayern players who made the final list.

Surprisingly, the reigning Ballon d'Or winner has never made FIFPRO World 11 so far in his career. The striker is expected to rival Kylian Mbappe and Lamine Yamal for the big prize after coming out top in the Ballon d'Or voting. Kane's teammates Manuel Neuer (x4) and Joshua Kimmich made the selection in past years.

Similarly, 6,511 professional women's footballers from around the world picked the 26-player list for women's football. Legendary goalkeeper Ann-Katrin Berger is the only German on both lists, but Catalina Coll and Hannah Hampton are the clear favorites to be picked for the Women's World 11 that started in 2015.
