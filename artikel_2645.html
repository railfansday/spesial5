Javonte Williams runs for 63 yards in Week 10 vs. Niners

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dallas Cowboys running back Javonte Williams rushed 16 times for 63 yards in Week 10 against the San Francisco 49ers, while adding a single 4-yard catch on his lone target.

Why it Matters: Williams remains a solid RB1 in all fantasy leagues, but this was a rather mediocre performance. He'll look to get back on track, and perhaps re-discover the end zone, in Week 11 against the Tennessee Titans.
