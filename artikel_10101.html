Wyoming council backs felony sweepstakes casino ban that reaches payment and hosting suppliers

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A bill now sponsored by Wyoming's Legislative Management Council would make running a dual-currency sweepstakes casino a felony. The draft also targets the vendors behind these sites, which raises the legal risk for payment processors and B2B suppliers.
