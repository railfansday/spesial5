Watch: Own-goal by Calvani gives Milan anearly lead against Frosinone

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

AC Milan got themselves in front early on in Sunday evening’s game at San Siro, and it was thanks to some generosity from their visitors.

Frosinone actually started the game better and they hit the frame of the good with a deflected shot inside the opening exchanges. However, it was Milan who struck first at San Siro with their first real attack.

Luka Modric deserves the plaudits for an outstanding through ball to find Adrien Rabiot’s run in behind, and the Frenchman’s square pass was heading towards Christian Pulisic’s run but was turned into his own net by Calvani.

Amid rumours of this being an audition due to Milan’s interest, it is one way to leave an impression.
