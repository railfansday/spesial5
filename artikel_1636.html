Pulisic grateful for Pochettino and makes scoring admission: “It’s nice to be back”

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Christian Pulisic was back in the goals for the United States national team this weekend and the AC Milan star has spoken about his return.

In the last international break, Pulisic remained with Milan, so his call-up this time around was a return to normal. With this, the normality continuity with him proving pivotal in a big win for the USMNT.

Scoring a brace in a dominant win against Haiti, the American re-established himself as a player that the Stars and the Stripes could rely upon in these big games. Indeed, it has effectively put them through to the CONCACAF Nations League semi-final.

Speaking after their win against Haiti, Pulisic spoke to the media and MilanPress have relayed his words.

Pulisic on his return to the national team…

“It was fantastic to see everyone again. I’m really excited. It was fantastic. Great performance from the team. It’s nice to be back on the pitch and score some goals and win the match.”

On Pochettino’s farewell to the national team…

“I think he has a great opportunity at Manchester United. We just have to keep going and try to grow further as a team. I think we’ve had some great moments, and Pochettino has brought a lot of energy to the team, so I think that’s what he’ll leave us with.”

With another game still to go for America in this international break, Pulisic will hope to grab more goals before saying goodbye to Pochettino. However, with their qualification all but confirmed, he may be given a chance to rest.
