Napoli director Manna on McTominay chat, De Bruyne tension and Zirkzee report

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Napoli director of sport Giovanni Manna confirms a meeting with Scott McTominay’s agent, insists Kevin De Bruyne ‘is our player’ and shrugs off Joshua Zirkzee rumours ahead of the Manchester City match.

It kicks off at the Etihad in Manchester at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game and Como vs. AEK Athens on the Champions League Liveblog.

This is a familiar city to Manna, who has come to Manchester several times in recent years to secure reinforcements for the club, including De Bruyne as a free agent in the summer of 2025.

“I came to Manchester to pick up McTominay first, and then De Bruyne,” Manna told Sky Sport Italia.

“De Bruyne experiences the build-up to the game the same way, he was here last year too. I think he feels pride to be here after everything he achieved.”

De Bruyne tried to give a little more clarity on his future after his wife implied they might want a return to the Netherlands for PSV Eindhoven.

“Kevin De Bruyne is our player, he is under contract to the end of the season, and there is an option to extend. You know what the transfer market is like, all it takes is a comment to spiral out of control. It’s early days, anything could happen, we’re very calm and look to the future with a relaxed attitude,” added the Napoli director.

“This evening’s match might seem prohibitive, and maybe it is, but we have to give our best and then focus on Serie A from the weekend.”

There has been a lot of speculation over McTominay’s future and the negotiations to extend his contract, with reports Manna met the Scotland international’s agent in the team hotel while they were in town for the match against Manchester City.

“We just had a coffee, because his agent is from here. We had a chat about many things, not just football.”

It is also suggested that Manna and Napoli will swing back round here in January for Manchester United striker Joshua Zirkzee.

“There are many talented players in Manchester, both for United and City, so let’s focus on tonight’s game because we really need the points.”
