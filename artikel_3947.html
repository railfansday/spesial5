Guide: GTA 6: Vice City Easter Egg Walkthrough & Tommy Vercetti Shirt Unlock

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Find the portal and get your Tommy Vercetti rewards

GTA 6 is hiding an extremely cool Easter egg related to 2002's GTA: Vice City. While it's a really fun reward and absolutely worth looking for, it involves quite a complex set of steps to make it happen. In this GTA 6 guide, we're going to explain how to find the Vice City Easter egg.

Full credit to Memeio for discovering this secret!

On this page: GTA 6: Vice City Easter Egg Walkthrough & Tommy Vercetti Shirt Unlock

Below is a full walkthrough of how to do the Vice City Easter egg in GTA 6 and get the Tommy Vercetti rewards. Follow these steps and you can find the Vice City Easter egg for yourself.

Note: Because this is a walkthrough of the whole process, you may find spoilers below.

Your first stop is the Badger R&D radio tower in Key Lento, not far from Jason's home. Here it is on the map for reference:

Go to the tower and climb it, walking up the internal stairs and then climbing the ladders to reach the top:

In this room, there's an electrical box on the wall with a lever you can pull; set the lever to the upper 'ON' position:

On the opposite side of the room, push the button on the equipment:

You should hear a sound effect play (taken directly from GTA: Vice City).

On the back wall are a couple of posters that provide some clues as to what you need to do. First is a poster for LPL, the power company in GTA 6. The second advertises the JoySystem bearing the phrase, "Lose Track of Time":

"Lose Track of Time" is a reference to "Life Is Passing Me By", which is a well-known cheat code in GTA: Vice City that speeds up the in-game clock.

Your goal now is to visit 12 substations all across Leonida in a particular order. When you reach each one, you will need to press certain buttons, slowly entering the old school cheat code.

Note: You cannot fast travel to these locations, you must reach them all manually. Also, some of these substations are staffed and you will be trespassing while you're on the premises, so be careful.

Let's go through each stop on your substation tour:

Go inside and find the machine with a label on it that reads "Press D" — push that button:

Hop the fence and go inside the door next to the letters 'iF':

In the room inside, turn left and approach the machine with the label 'Press D' and push the button:

Substation #3 is back in Key Lento. Here it is on the map:

Hop the fence and make your way to the main building. You're looking for a door next to the letters 'Ei' on the wall:

Go inside; just to the right is a machine with the label 'Press •'. Push the button:

If you've visited all the above substations in the correct order and pressed/pulled all the correct buttons and levers, you will receive a strange text message that reads as follows:

What you need to do is travel to the T.H. Power Station between the in-game hours of 7pm and 6am. Here it is on the map:

This area is heavily guarded so be careful getting into the power station. Make your way to Reactor 2, where you will see a very obvious portal:

Next to the portal is a lever — pull it and you'll be transported into GTA: Vice City, complete with pixelated visuals:

When you leave, you'll receive a couple of special items as a reward!

Once you've completed the Easter egg, you'll be back in regular ol' GTA 6 with two very cool items for your trouble.

First, you get Tommy Vercetti's iconic blue Hawaiian shirt:

Second is the 'American Glory' motorcycle:

Important Note: You will need to register the American Glory motorcycle at a Pay ‘n’ Spray or park it in one of your garages to ensure you keep the bike across sessions.

Yes, after you go through the portal and unlock the Easter egg's rewards, both Jason and Lucia will be able to wear a Tommy Vercetti-inspired shirt and use the motorcycle.

The Vice City Easter egg in GTA 6 can take quite a long time to complete, because you have to drive all over the map and ensure you're at the right places and pushing the right buttons.

However, yes, it's ultimately worth the effort because you get to see something truly unique, plus you get some really cool, unique items that GTA fans will love.
