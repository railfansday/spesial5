HARMA launches in Early Access on November 17

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Roguelike cooperative deckbuilder HARMA will launch in Early Access for PC via Steam on November 17, publisher Astrolabe Games and developer INDIRECT SHINE announced.

The Early Access release will feature “more than 200 hours of content” and day-one support for up to four-player online cooperative play. Following launch, the studio plans to maintain a long-term update schedule featuring ongoing balance adjustments and a steady stream of new content, with a detailed Early Access roadmap to be unveiled on release day.

Here is an overview of the game, via Astrolabe Games:

“Why is this such a terrible tragedy? Why are the Knights—who are supposed to protect the people—slaughtering them instead?”

“Father… where have you been? What the hell is happening here at Harma?”

HARMA is a retro dark fantasy roguelike deckbuilding game featuring a story-driven campaign. Uncover the truth behind the fallen knights, the burning kingdom, and your missing father. Harness the power of mighty artifacts to defeat demons and save a world on the brink of destruction.

The knights who returned from their expedition began slaughtering their own people.

On the day the kingdom burned and everything was lost, you were left standing alone in the heart of the tragedy.

In a world filled with betrayal and despair, uncover the truth and stand against the darkness.

Unique and Strategic Battles through the Artifact System

In HARMA‘s battles, the strength of your cards is determined by the random activation of three artifacts. But with Focus, you can override chance—forcing artifact activations to seize control of your destiny.

Will you leave it to chance, or will you risk it all with decisive Focus? Your choice will completely alter the battlefield.

Combine two playable characters with over 300 unique cards and artifacts to forge your own deck and strategy.

Discover Harma’s Fate through Story Campaign

HARMA‘s campaign unfolds across four epic acts.

Witness the fall of the world and uncover the fate of Eden through dynamic battles, branching dialogue, and cinematic cutscenes.

Unlimited Challenges and Replayability via Dimensional Rift

Crave pure combat and strategy beyond the story? Enter Dimensional Rift—an endlessly replayable mode where cinematic storytelling takes a backseat to relentless, ever-changing battles.

Test your skills, forge new builds, and push your limits to the extreme.
