Devolver Digital and BlackLight Games announce roguelike auto-battler All Army Attack for PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Publisher Devolver Digital and developer BlackLight Games have announced All Army Attack, a “chaotic, large-scale roguelike auto-battler.” It will launch for PC via Steam in 2027. A demo will be playable from November 16 to 23 as part of Steam Auto-Battler Fest.

Here is an overview of the game, via Devolver Digital:

Hold onto your clucks! All Army Attack is a chaotic large-scale roguelike auto-battler. Unite animals and plants, build an army that spills off the screen, gamble it all with every battle, and fight your way toward a formidable cosmic threat!

Take on the role of Commander, recruiting unique troops and artillery from a vast pool of cards, defeating enemies, and using the resources gained to develop powerful new units and draft in massive reinforcements.

Build an army from scratch and scale it into hundreds of units in a fast-paced and highly satisfying gameplay loop as you attempt to build an army powerful enough to defeat awesome bosses.

Freely customize and modify your cards and units or craft and mutate every individual soldier to create wildly different builds and strategies in every run. Explore combinations between different characters, factions, cards, and battlefield setups to create devastating armies!
