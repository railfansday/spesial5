Mark Answers Listener Questions

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

In this Thanksgiving mailbag episode of Power On, Mark Gurman answers listener questions about Apple’s upcoming products and the future of its hardware and software. He explains why the iPhone Air isn’t dead and why Apple isn’t planning to bring macOS to the iPad anytime soon, despite testing the combination internally. Mark also discusses Apple’s plans for camera-equipped AirPods and smart glasses, the challenges facing a giant foldable iPad, and whether the company could return to the server business or finally build its own television. Plus, why the iPhone Duo launched without Face ID, what’s next for the MacBook Air, how Apple’s software teams are organized and the company’s political future under Ternus. Have a question for Mark?
