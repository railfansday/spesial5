NFL predictions: Fernando Mendoza’s time starts in Las Veags Raiders Week 10 matchup vs. Seattle Seahawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

I don't know about y'all, but I've been waiting for Sunday for a while. I appreciate what Kirk Cousins was able to do for the Las Vegas Raiders over the last nine weeks, but it's Fernando Mendoza's time now! It took a little longer than expected, but we finally get to see Mendoza make his first regular-season start for the Raiders in the Week 10 matchup against the Seattle Seahawks. And of course, our Silver and Black Pride Pick'em Competition is heating up, too!

Odds below are provided by FanDuel Sportsbook and are subject to change.

Holder's picks: LV +6.5, Under 40.5, SEA ML

For context, the line moved after the Cousins trade, as the opening odds had the Seahawks as a field goal favorite. That's fair seeing as it's a rookie quarterback making his first start, and Seattle's head coach, Mike Macdonald, has a great track record against first-year passers. It also doesn't help that the Seahawks have arguably the best defense in the NFL, so I'm not going to bet on Mendoza getting his first win in his first start.

However, the reigning Champs are beaten up right now, especially on offense, where backup quarterback Drew Lock is expected to start. Plus, they've struggled to run the ball this season, so this could be an opportunity for the Raiders' defense to have a "get right" game and at least keep it close. Granted, I'm sure people in Seattle are looking at Las Vegas' run defense and making similar statements about the Seahawks' rushing attack…
