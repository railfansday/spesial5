Warriors' Steph Curry sustained oblique abdominal tear, out at least two weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Warriors' Steph Curry sustained oblique abdominal tear, out at least two weeks originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

Steph Curry will be out until at least Thanksgiving.

The Warriors announced Thursday an MRI confirmed that Curry sustained an oblique abdominal tear and will be re-evaluated in two weeks.

Curry sustained the injury early in the Warriors' 115-108 loss to the Houston Rockets on Wednesday night.

The 38-year-old guard played just six minutes before exiting the game. He finished with six points on 1-of-3 shooting from the field.

It's a disappointing development for the Warriors, who began the season with an 8-3 record before Wednesday's loss.

Curry is averaging 27.8 points per game on 50.0 percent shooting from the field and 45.7 percent from 3-point range this season.

Now Golden State will have to navigate the next few weeks without their leader and best player.
