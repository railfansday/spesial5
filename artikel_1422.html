Gasperini wants to move Wesley back to right flank, requests new winger in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Roma delivered a top-notch performance against PSG at the Parc des Princes, securing a point in a 2-2 draw thanks to goals from Hermoso and Malen.

Among the most exciting duels of the match was the one between Hakimi and Wesley.

The Moroccan winger also earned a penalty after surprising the 2003-born Roma player with his pace, who brought him down just meters from Svilar.

The Roma winger, on the other hand, was also a thorn in the side of the PSG defense. Given Hakimi’s presence, Gasperini decided to deploy Wesley on the left flank, considering him the best player to contain and counterattack the Moroccan.

Roma is looking for a couple of signings in January. As reported by Il Messaggero, the Giallorossi will move to bring Gasperini a striker and a winger, precisely because the idea is to deploy Wesley permanently on the right flank.

The Roman club lacks a left-footed player who can complete a lineup that includes, in addition to the 2003 Brazilian, Lulli, Molina, and Rensch.
