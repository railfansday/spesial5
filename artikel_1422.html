Svilar suffers minor knee sprain, but will start vs PSG

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The big absentee from the last three Champions League matches will also be back on Wednesday against Paris Saint-Germain.

Mile Svilar will defend the Giallorossi goal at the Parc des Princes despite his scare in Parma. He collapsed to the ground early in the match, but decided to grit his teeth and stay on the pitch.

He has a slight knee sprain, writes Daniele Aloisi in Il Messaggero. Nothing serious, and he’s ready to return.

Balerdi, however, risks remaining out, having been dealing with an ankle problem for weeks. His physical condition isn’t the best, and his mental health could also play tricks on him.

The Argentine was Marseille’s captain for years and, as a result, the most targeted by the fervent Paris Saint-Germain fans. Last year, he was involved in a fight with Dembelé, who insulted him in a conversation with Hojbjerg: “He’s worthless.”
