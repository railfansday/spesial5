Rob Hamilton: The AI “Black Hole” is Pulling Liquidity Away from Bitcoin

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

AnchorWatch CEO Rob Hamilton is bullish on Bitcoin in the long run, even as he watches liquidity get pulled toward the Anthropic IPO and the AI trade, which he compares to a black hole.

AnchorWatch CEO Rob Hamilton is bullish on Bitcoin in the long run, even as he watches liquidity get pulled toward the Anthropic IPO and the AI trade, which he compares to a black hole. He argues this is part of Bitcoin maturing, and that the value proposition is still there. Quantum computing is a risk over the next five to six years, but he came away from Amsterdam believing cryptographers are sprinting to meet the challenge.
