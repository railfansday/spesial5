Inside Jude Bellingham's life in Madrid: Kisses from elderly ladies, coffee shop bliss and finding love - England star feels at home as he nears new £500k-per-week deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Published: 06:30 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 06:30 EST, 24 November 2026

Jude Bellingham has more than settled into his routine in Madrid, with the England star learning Spanish, integrating into his community and spending time with his girlfriend in a mansion worth a reported £5.3million ($7m) after three seasons at Real Madrid.

The 23-year-old's settled life in the Spanish capital is thought to play a significant role in Bellingham's decision to sign an improved deal with the club, with Daily Mail Sport reporting on Monday that the midfielder is set to become one of Europe's highest earners.

New terms would see Bellingham raking in around £12.5million a year after tax until 2029, and will reportedly put him on par with his team-mate Vinicius Junior, whose latest deal is thought to be worth around £500,000 per week after tax.

But beyond the money, Bellingham has been flourishing turning out for the Spanish giants – both on and off the pitch.

Here, Daily Mail Sport details Bellingham's lifestyle in Spain, and the rituals that have grounded the player after his explosion into superstardom.

Jude Bellingham is inching closer to a new deal at Real Madrid to become one of Europe's highest earners

But more than just enjoying his football, Bellingham has made himself at home in the city (pictured at the Grand Prix of Spain in Madrid this year)

A recurring theme when Bellingham speaks of his love of living in Madrid stems from an established daily routine.

Speaking to his club just six months after he joined Los Blancos in the summer of 2023, Bellingham had already set up habits which helped him feel at home in the new city.

'I get up early to go to training, I train and then I head home,' Bellingham said. 'Sometimes I'll go out for a coffee near home or head into town to grab something to eat. Then I go back home to chill out in the garden for a bit if the weather is good and the sun's out.

'If my friends are over, we'll go out for dinner and then back home. It's great. You get to know the people at the places you visit more often. They don't see you as Jude Bellingham the footballer, but as Jude the guy who comes in for a coffee and that's great. The people I meet in the city really look after me. I feel at home.'

Over two years later, and the patterns are no different. The Calvin Klein ambassador told Vogue this month that his ideal downtime involves 'being in the company of my friends and family, having a coffee, going for a walk.

'You're always going to get recognised, but you're a bit safer in terms of not getting mobbed,' Bellingham said of his favourite spots on the fringes of the city, where many of his repeat haunts are located.

Not that Bellingham is a little averse to integrating with the community. In fact, mingling with the locals has been a positive aspect of Bellingham's life abroad, with the player admitting in the summer of 2025 that Spain was very much 'home'.

'When I go back to play with the national team, it's nice seeing people that I don't normally get to see – my family and friends – but I always feel more comfortable when I'm back in Madrid,' Bellingham said in an interview with FIFA. 'It's the weather, the people are so loving, the food.

'You mix all that together, as well as, especially, playing for Real Madrid… When you go out in public and the people see you, you see it's like a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity that they've had to meet you.

'They talk to you about the club and how much it means to them. A lot of the older ladies give me kisses on the cheeks and stuff like that! It's really nice.'

The 23-year-old lives in his mansion with his mother Denise, who often drives him to training as he is without a license

Shortly after moving to Spain, Bellingham settled down in the exclusive La Finca neighbourhood, which plays host to a number of his team-mates including Kylian Mbappe and former club legends such as Zinedine Zidane, Cristiano Ronaldo and Iker Casillas.

The gated community is known as 'Madrid's Beverly Hills' to insiders and boasts extensive security to ensure the luxury villas inside the compound stay safe.

Bellingham is believed to have purchased his pricey six-bedroom home in 2023, moving into the complex, which boasts a home theatre, outdoor pool and gym according to Architectural Digest, with his mother Denise.

From there, his mum drives him to and from training, with the young star admitting in Vogue that despite his prodigious footballing talent, he remains borderline incapable in other aspects of life.

'I can't cook, I can't drive… I'm good at what I do, but everything else?' he joked. 'Not that great.

'I rely on my friends, my family to help me do stuff – chef, cleaner, mum, whoever else. That's where I'm at. I work hard every day and all day, and then when I get home, I've probably neglected learning how to do some of the more basic things. But there'll be a time and a place for that, I'm sure.'

In keeping with the idea that Bellingham has found his home in Spain, it stands to reason that the player has been working on his languages.

Bellingham revealed in Vogue that he has been learning Spanish for over two years, beginning in earnest just months after his move to the Santiago Bernabeu.

Confirming his aptitude for the language, Bellingham added: 'I think a big part of it is being confident enough to just speak.

'You know you're going to get stuff wrong, and you're probably not going to sound exactly right, (but if) people know what you're talking about, they'll probably think: "Fair play for trying".'

Castro and Bellingham have been dating for nearly two years since early 2025, having reportedly met on exclusive dating site Raya (Source: Ashlyn Castro/Instagram)

American model Castro is a frequent fixture in the stands whether Bellingham is playing for Madrid or England

With Bellingham settled in Madrid, his American model girlfriend Ashlyn Castro is, if not permanently based in the city, a regular visitor to both his La Finca villa and Real Madrid's home ground.

The former music video star and Bellingham have been dating since early 2025, with Castro becoming a frequent fixture at the Bernabeu alongside his mother Denise.

Castro has also been following his exploits for England too, travelling across the United States to follow the travails of Thomas Tuchel and Co as they reached the semi-finals at this summer's World Cup.

Most recently she was spotted in the stands closer to home however, with Bellingham and Castro snapped embracing after the final whistle blew on England's impressive win against Spain at Atletico Madrid's Metropolitano.
