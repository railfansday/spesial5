Crypto Market Today, Nov. 18: Bitcoin's 12% Weekly Plunge Has Triggered a Rare Signal With a Surprising History

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

As liquidity continues to evaporate from cryptocurrency markets, Bitcoin is testing a long-term technical benchmark that has historically helped investors identify periods of extreme market weakness.

By RJ Fulton – Nov 18, 2026 at 5:25PM EST

Cryptocurrency markets extended their recent sell-off on Wednesday, November 18, as deteriorating liquidity conditions and weakening risk appetite continued to weigh on digital assets. Bitcoin is once again approaching its recent lows near $62,000, while a rare breach of a long-term technical indicator is drawing considerable attention from investors.

As of early evening, Bitcoin (BTC -2.03%) was down 3.84% to $62,386.56, while Ethereum (ETH -0.68%) fell 3.80% to $1,753.33 and Solana (SOL -0.93%) declined 2.88% to $72.94. Although near-term downside risks remain elevated, historical market cycles suggest that periods of comparable weakness could present compelling opportunities for long-term investors.

Selling pressure intensified on Wednesday as investors awaited further insight into the Federal Reserve's monetary policy outlook. With Treasury yields remaining elevated and liquidity continuing to deteriorate across cryptocurrency markets, investors appear increasingly reluctant to take on additional risk. The ongoing unwind of leveraged positions has compounded selling pressure, while subdued spot market demand has made it difficult for cryptocurrencies to sustain meaningful rallies.

Spot Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs extended their recent losses on Wednesday, reflecting continued weakness in the underlying cryptocurrencies. The iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF (IBIT -2.16%) and Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC -2.16%) both declined 2.16%, while the iShares Ethereum Trust ETF (ETHA -2.63%) fell 2.63%.

Despite deteriorating conditions in the cryptocurrency market, one historical indicator offers a potentially encouraging perspective for long-term investors. Following last week's 12% decline, Bitcoin broke below its 200-week moving average, a long-term pricing benchmark that has historically helped identify periods of extreme market weakness. According to CoinDesk, comparable instances in 2015, 2020, and 2022 were followed by positive returns over the subsequent three months. However, Bitcoin experienced further declines before recovering each time.

Before becoming more constructive on the broader cryptocurrency market, investors should look for signs of stabilization. One relatively straightforward way to gauge whether broader market conditions are improving would be to monitor Bitcoin's ability to reclaim and sustain a price above its 200-week moving average.

While historical precedent suggests cryptocurrencies could be approaching attractive long-term valuations, lower prices alone are not necessarily indicative of a market bottom. Until liquidity conditions improve, capital flows stabilize, and major cryptocurrencies demonstrate renewed price strength, the risk of further declines across the digital asset market remains elevated.
