‘Caréme’ & ‘División Palermo’ Take Top Prizes At International Emmy Awards: Full Winners List

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

France’s Caréme was named Best Drama Series at the 54th International Emmy Awards, which were handed out Monday night in Manhattan. División Palermo from Argentina took the Comedy Series prize. See the full winners list below.

Hosted by Michael Urie, a two-time Primetime Emmy nominee for Shrinking,, winners were announced in 16 categories, drawn from 64 nominees from 22 countries, the UK taking home the most awards, with nine trophies.

Other winners tonight included the UK’s Bobby Schofield in Unforgivable for Best Performance by an Actor and Belgium’s Mona Mina Leon in Holy Sh!t for Best Performance by an Actress. Watch on Deadline

In addition to the competitive categories, two special honors were handed out Monday night in the ceremony at the New York Hilton Midtown: Former Netflix Chief Content Officer Bela Bajaria received the International Emmy Founders Award, and Austria’s Gerhard Zeilier accepted the International Emmy Directorate Award.
