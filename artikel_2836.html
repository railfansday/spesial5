Yes, there is a "Thursday Night Football" game on Nov. 12.

The New York Giants host the Washington Commanders in the Week 10 edition of "Thursday Night Football." The two NFC East teams last met on Oct. 11 and played to a controversial 27-27 tie that ended with a botched final play by the Commanders in overtime. Coach Dan Quinn and quarterback Jayden Daniels each faced criticism for their game management after Washington allowed the final seconds to tick away without getting off a potential game-winning field goal.

The Giants have won two of three games since that moment and enter Week 10 with a two-game lead over the Dallas Cowboys atop the NFC East standings. They've also made strides on offense with new quarterback J.J. McCarthy, though his status for Thursday is uncertain after he played through an injury in the team's 19-11 win over the Philadelphia Eagles last week.

The Commanders likely need to win Thursday to keep their faint playoff hopes alive after losing to the Los Angeles Rams in Week 9. Washington has a 1-8-1 record in the past 10 starts made by Daniels dating back to the 2025 season and coach Dan Quinn remains a name atop hot seat lists around the league.

This week's "TNF" game is scheduled to kick off at its usual 8:15 p.m. ET and will air nationally on Amazon Prime Video. Local fans for each team will be able to watch the game on a local networks.
