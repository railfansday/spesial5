Myles Garrett remains limited participant Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Rams outside linebacker Myles Garrett remained a limited participant in Thursday’s practice. Garrett returned to practice Wednesday, his first practice since Week 1.

Garrett underwent arthroscopic knee surgery in mid-September after playing 40 snaps in the season opener. He was given a six-week prognosis and has missed seven games.

He and Aaron Donald could play together for the first time on Sunday when the Rams play the Commanders.

Quarterback Matthew Stafford (right elbow) was a full participant again.

The Rams had only two changes to their injury report: Inside linebacker Grant Stuard (hamstring) did not practice after being limited Wednesday, and offensive lineman Steve Avila (hamstring) was a full participant a day after being limited.

The Rams again practiced without cornerback Jaylen Watson (shoulder), long snapper Joe Cardona (ankle), cornerback Trent McDuffie (back), tight end Colby Parkinson (ribs) and safety Kam Curl (foot).

Wide receiver Jordan Whittington (back) and tight end Terrance Ferguson (ankle) remained limited.
