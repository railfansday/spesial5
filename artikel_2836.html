Week 11 fantasy rankings: Rashee Rice projects as 17th-best WR

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

ESPN's experts rank Kansas City Chiefs star Rashee Rice as the 17th-best fantasy wide receiver after 10 weeks of NFL regular-season play.

Rice saw a significant increase in targets against the Atlanta Falcons last week and will likely see even more when the Chiefs face the Arizona Cardinals on Sunday.

Frankly, I had low expectations for Rice after wide receiver Josh Downs joined the Chiefs' offense at the trade deadline.

The former SMU Mustang has been underperforming all season, and I thought Downs' addition might be the final nail in the coffin. Thankfully, I was wrong. Downs is still expected to play a pivotal role in Kansas City's offense, but he is currently taking a backseat to Rice, who had his second-best game of the year against the Falcons.

I expect Rice to finally break 100 yards this week and add a touchdown for good measure.

Rashee Rice will be taking on the Arizona Cardinals for the first time in his three-year career on Sunday.
