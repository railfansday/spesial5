C.J. Stroud connects with Nico Collins to give Texans 7-3 lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Texans were outplayed for most of the first quarter, but they have a 7-3 lead.

Spencer Shrader kicked a 34-yard field but missed from 56, giving the Colts an early 3-0 lead. They had drives of nine plays, 44 yards and eight plays, 35 yards.

The Colts defense, missing cornerbacks Charvarius Ward and Sauce Gardner, intercepted C.J. Stroud on the Texans’ first drive. It was a fluke pick, first hitting AJ Haulcy in the helmet, then off the hands of Cam Bynum before landing back in Haulcy’s hands.

The Texans, though, answered on their second drive.

Stroud avoided the blitz of cornerback Kapena Gushiken, who grabbed a piece of Stroud’s jersey, by stepping up in the pocket before heaving a dime into the end zone to Nico Collins. Collins was covered by Justin Walley, but it was no match.

The 42-yard touchdown pass gave the Texans their first lead.

It was Stroud’s 20th touchdown. His career high is 23.
