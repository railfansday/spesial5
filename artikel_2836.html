NFC Playoff Picture: Chicago Bears fall to 6th with the Vikings win

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Chicago Bears are on their bye this weekend, but after the Detroit Lions beat the New England Patriots in Germany, 35 to 10, they've leapfrogged the Bears into first place in the NFC North. That also dropped the Bears down to the fifth seed in the conference.

The Packers and Vikings are playing right now, and a Minnesota win would move them up to the fifth seed, so adjust your rooting interests accordingly.

UPDATE: The Vikings beat the Packers 30 to 10, so they jumped ahead of the Bears for the fifth seed, dropping the Bears down to sixth. While three NFC North teams are 7-2, the Vikings have the tiebreaker over the Bears, and the Lions have the tiebreaker over Minnesota.
