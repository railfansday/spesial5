Report: Kendre Miller to visit Cowboys on Tuesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Cowboys need a running back with Tyler Goodson injured and they’re reportedly set to meet with a veteran option on Tuesday.

Todd Archer of ESPN.com reports that Kendre Miller will be visiting the team. Miller was released by the Saints ahead of their Week 9 win over the Browns.

Miller had 57 carries for 159 yards and two touchdowns in six games for New Orleans this season. The 2023 third-round pick had 184 carries for 656 yards and five touchdowns during his entire run with the Saints. He also caught 29 passes for 217 yards.

Goodson is expected to miss multiple weeks with a knee injury. Cowboys owner Jerry Jones said on Tuesday morning that the team was likelier to add help behind Javonte Williams via free agency than a trade.
