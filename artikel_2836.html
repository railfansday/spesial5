How much longer can Jordan Ott ignore the obvious?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Los Angeles Lakers have been one of the best teams in the NBA through the early stages of the season, entering Phoenix riding a seven-game winning streak and sporting an 11-2 record. As the game progressed and Los Angeles built a 31-point lead, I resigned myself to the fact that the chasm between their excellence and the Suns' mediocrity was painfully apparent. Phoenix still had a long way to go holistically before becoming a consistently competitive basketball team.

The Suns' second unit reminded us of what Phoenix Suns basketball can look like. They ripped off a 41-16 run, pulling within five points in the closing minutes. Luka Doncic, a player who seemingly possesses the ability to spit in your face and laugh at you while doing it, had to take the ice packs off and check back into the game to ensure a victory for Los Angeles. Considering where things stood earlier in the afternoon, that's a small victory in and of itself.

What Sunday afternoon ultimately reinforced, however, was how fundamentally broken the Phoenix Suns' starting lineup is. Because the brand of basketball we've come to associate with this team is alive and well in Phoenix. We saw it in the fourth quarter. The energy, the defensive aggression, the connectivity, and the collective determination to make life uncomfortable for the opposition. Unfortunately, those characteristics are often masked by questionable roster construction, duplicative player archetypes in the starting five, and a lack of clearly defined roles.

There's an identity within this basketball team waiting to emerge with greater consistency, and we continue to see flashes of it when Jordan Ott turns to his bench. Meanwhile, the starting unit remains heavily dependent on Devin Booker's ability to manufacture productive stretches of basketball through his individual brilliance. When he can't, the unit begins to crumble under the weight of its own deficiencies, particularly its inability to rebound the basketball.

How much longer can you continue rolling out a starting five that routinely puts your second unit in a position where it has to resuscitate the basketball team?
