Mailbag! Worried Raptors & Knicks fans, all-in Pistons, Trae trades & more + Ryan McCaffrey on Suns & GTA 6 + Deirdre Bosa on NBA AI Player comps

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Kevin O'Connor answers listener mailbag questions, covering the state of the Toronto Raptors without Kawhi Leonard, what pieces the Detroit Pistons are missing, whether the Warriors are a bottom-three team in the West without Steph Curry and more. Plus, is there any way for the Washington Wizards to get out of Trae Young's contract? And is it time for the New York Knicks to trade Josh Hart?

Then, Kevin is joined by Ryan McCaffrey to recap the Phoenix Suns season so far and, perhaps more importantly, share their early impressions of GTA 6.

Later, Deirdre Bosa of Yahoo Finance joins the show to talk the latest in tech and make comparisons between AI models and current NBA players with KOC.
