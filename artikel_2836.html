Hamilton and His Future at Ferrari: “There Are No Conversations”

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Lewis Hamilton has wanted to cool down the uncertainty about his future at Ferrari and during the Las Vegas Grand Prix he has revealed that the conversations to extend his contract with the Scuderia have not yet begun, although he has made clear that he is not worried about it. His current link with the Italian team ends at the end of the 2027 season and the possibility of prolonging it until 2028 is on the table, although for now no one has sat down to negotiate.

"I haven't even thought about it because I am in the middle of my contract, so normally the decision to renew or not happens at some point in your last year. I really don't know when we will start those conversations, but there is no need to talk about it or think about it" Hamilton declared at Thursday's press conference in Las Vegas. The seven time world champion will turn 42 in January and faces his second season with Ferrari with a very different perspective from last year's, when his performance was far below expectations and he himself described it as the worst of his career.

This year Hamilton has recovered his best version thanks to the regulation change and occupies fourth position in the drivers' championship with a 12 point advantage over his teammate Charles Leclerc, while he remains immersed in the fight for the runner up spot after achieving his first victory with Ferrari in Barcelona and his first pole position with the Scuderia in Mexico. However, the internal tension that arose after Leclerc's touch on the first lap of the Mexican race has fueled speculation about his continuity, especially after the Monegasque signed a long term contract at the beginning of this season.

Criticism also comes from outside after Bernie Ecclestone recently suggested that Hamilton should consider retiring. "When he gets in the car to start a race, he is already complaining and protesting about something before even reaching the first corner. He is reaching an age at which he has to consider retiring" the former Formula 1 boss stated in an interview, although the British driver has not entered to value the nonagenarian's words and has preferred to respond with facts to those who doubt his ability due to his age.

"Of course, there are challenges ahead that I cannot foresee and that come with age. But along this path there have been many preconceived ideas about the fact that your reaction time decreases. Did you see my reaction in the last race? It is faster than some of the youngest here. There are many tools you can use to stay always alert and with a fit mind" Hamilton noted, visibly sure of himself.

The Briton also wanted to make clear that his commitment to Ferrari remains intact and that he maintains the necessary motivation to continue competing at the highest level. "I suppose you keep going as long as you are willing to sacrifice everything, as I am right now, but I can't say what will happen in four years. I love what I do now. I am still totally committed and I will continue to be in the foreseeable future" he concluded.

Hamilton has clear that his immediate future is at Maranello and that the decision on a possible renewal will come when it has to come, while maintaining as his main objective to finish the season as high as possible and continue demonstrating that at 42 years old he remains one of the best drivers on the grid.
