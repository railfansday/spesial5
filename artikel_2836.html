Kawhi Leonard to begin return after completing left knee treatment

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Toronto Raptors superstar Kawhi Leonard has officially "completed treatment to address left knee swelling," according to the team on Tuesday.

Leonard has missed the last five games due to his latest injury. Toronto is 2-3 during that stretch.

In his last season with the Los Angeles Clippers, Leonard played 65 games, the most he's logged since the 2023-24 season, during which he played in 68 games. The last time Leonard appeared in at least 60 games in back-to-back seasons was during 2016-17 with the San Antonio Spurs.

In two games this season, Leonard is averaging 19.5 points, 7.5 rebounds and 3.5 steals while shooting 32.4 per cent from the field.

During his 2019 championship season in Toronto, Leonard's longest absence occurred when he missed four straight games due to load management. The Raptors recorded a 3-1 record during this time.

The last time Leonard dealt with a serious injury was when he missed the first 34 games of the 2024-25 campaign with the Clippers. At the time, he was dealing with chronic inflammation in his right knee.
