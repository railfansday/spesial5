S Talanoa Hufanga questionable for Sunday's game

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Broncos safety Talanoa Hufanga (ankle) is questionable for Sunday’s game against the Panthers.

Hufanga had a second consecutive limited practice on Friday after missing Wednesday’s session. Until Thursday, he had played or practiced since his injury in Week 5 against the Chargers.

Wide receiver Pat Bryant (ankle) is also listed as questionable, but he was listed as a full participant for the third consecutive day. Bryant had his 21-day return-to-practice window opened this week.

The Broncos will have to activate him from injured reserve on Saturday to make him eligible to play this week.

He has not played since Week 4 against the 49ers.

Outside linebacker Dondrea Tillman (hip) was added to the team’s practice report on Friday as a full participant. He is also questionable for the game.

Cornerback Kris Abrams-Draine (ankle), safety Brandon Jones (hamstring) and defensive lineman Eyioma Uwazurike (shoulder) were all ruled out after they missed practice all week.

Wide receiver Jaylen Waddle (chest) was a full participant Friday, and he did not receive a game status.
