Cavs vs. Grizzlies: How to watch, odds, and injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Cleveland Cavaliers will look to pick up their third straight win as they host a fun Memphis Grizzlies team.

Memphis has been one of the biggest surprises this season. They boast the fourth-best offense in the league thanks to having the best three-point percentage in the league (39.5%) and being the sixth-best offensive rebounding team (collecting 32.9% of their misses). We'll see how the Cavs handle the Grizzlies' high-powered offensive attack.

Ty Jerome, who has had a strong start to the season for Memphis, will not be making his return to Cleveland. He will be missing this game with a hip injury.

Cleveland has their share of guys on the injury report, but it is worth noting that Sam Merrill has been upgraded to questionable for this game (elbow).
