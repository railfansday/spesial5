Steelers’ DK Metcalf bounces from stadium early after dumb ejection in Eagles game disaster

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Week 11 was one to forget for the Pittsburgh Steelers, as the club couldn't do anything against a struggling Philadelphia Eagles team. But somehow, the 19-16 loss on Sunday wasn't the most embarrassing thing to happen for the franchise, as DK Metcalf was ejected from the contest in the third quarter.

Reports indicate that the 28-year-old wide receiver left the stadium almost immediately after the game concluded, according to Jenna Harner of WPXI Pittsburgh. Harner shared a video of Metcalf in street clothes leaving the stadium as his teammates were coming into the locker room.

Because he left the stadium so quickly, the former second-round pick was unavailable for any postgame interviews, per Rich Walsh of KDKA CBS. That means Metcalf will have field questions the next time he faces media members, whenever that may be.

"Metcalf won't be available for postgame interviews," Walsh posted on X. "He left the locker room as soon as the game ended."

Officials ejected the eight-year veteran after he committed two personal fouls in the third quarter. First, he was flagged for unnecessary roughness when he got into it with Eagles defensive back Cooper DeJean. Not long after that, referees flagged Metcalf again after he allegedly poked DeJean in the eyes, per Brooke Pryor of ESPN.

"Mike McCarthy says official told him DK Metcalf poked a defender in the eye," Pryor reported.

It's been a frustrating campaign for Metcalf, as he's been a non-factor in the Steelers' offense all season. That story stayed true on Sunday against the Eagles, as Metcalf finished the day with just one reception for two yards on four targets. That brings his season totals to 36 receptions for 434 yards and touchdowns.
