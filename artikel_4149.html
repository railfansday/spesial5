Little Library brings village life sim and bookshop management to iOS and Android this November

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Only a few weeks after Kettle Street gave you a bare flat and a list of odd jobs around Kettleby, another cosy life sim is handing you a struggling village to help out. Little Library launches on iOS and Android later this month, and the village in question is Maplewood, which has just got itself a brand-new library and not a lot of money to run it with.

Keeping the doors open is on you. There’s a small budget to stretch between new books and putting on events, and every time someone walks in, you’ll have to play librarian and work out what they want to read.

The collection features more than 70 books, including rare and collectible titles that boost the library’s reputation. There’s also a Local History section to expand, and as it grows, you’ll piece together Maplewood’s forgotten past.

You’ll organise book clubs and other events to bring the town together, all building towards the Folk Festival, which is more or less your deadline for transforming the library into the heart of the community.

If the books get a little boring, you can also tend a small farm for extra income and trade with villagers, fish in ponds and lakes for resources and requests, and explore the Whispering Woods on the outskirts of the village. The villagers range from aspiring writers to seasoned travellers, and you can build friendships with them or pursue romance too.

The obvious comparison is Tiny Bookshop, which had you sizing up customers and picking out books for them, and the recommending side of Little Library sounds very close to that. Although, I’d say this is closer to a Stardew-style life sim with a library in the middle. Farming and fishing are popular for a reason, but they’re also what most cosy sims already offer.

Little Library is coming to iOS and Android on November 27th.
