Here's A Closer Look At Marvel Cosmic Invasion's New DLC Game Mode

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Marvel Cosmic Invasion's Doom-themed DLC launches next week on 10th November, and ahead of its release, Dotemu has given us an extended look at what we can expect from the new roguelike game mode.

In the above video, Producer Rémi Lavoie and Lead Designer Kevin Chiasson play through 18 minutes of the Castle Doom mode, showcasing its copious number of pathways, central 'Siege Gauge' mission objective, run modifiers, and even the moveset of new hero, Sue Storm.
