Twitch Leaks Then Deletes GTA 6 Drops Campaign

You can count on one hand the days that remain until the launch of GTA 6. Players will finally be able to embark on a new crime-ridden narrative with dual protagonists, Jason and Lucia. With launch events on the horizon, it has been revealed that Twitch will be joining the action.

A GTA 6 launch celebration will take place on the online streaming platform, Twitch. It allows players to snag GTA 6 rewards to use on their profiles and when chatting in their favorite streams during the game’s launch week and beyond.

12.5 million American gamers plan to adjust their schedules for Rockstar's next big release.

Twitch leaked this campaign themselves, detailing all the rewards up for grabs. It has since been deleted, but thanks to Insider Gaming, we know all that the GTA 6 Twitch drops will entail ahead of time.

As part of the campaign, you can unlock six viewership Bling Badges by watching GTA 6 streamers for one hour each day on six different days between November 18 and November 30, 2026.

Although GTA 6 launches worldwide on November 19, the Twitch drops going live a day early are likely because streamers and fans alike will be doing the New Zealand trick in order to get access to the game as soon as possible.

The six Bling Badges available to collect depict a chain with “VI” as the charm, beginning with a purple chain, then bronze, silver, gold, diamond, and finally, the Miami chain, which is covered in the iconic GTA 6 purple, pink, and orange hue.

Other badges that are part of the Heist Collection include a Welcome 2 Miami palm tree, a Scored Big money bag, and a Bleed Miami heart, all following the same color scheme. The Emotes are BustedPlz, DinoBusted, and SabaBusted – all spinoffs of emotes Twitch viewers will likely recognize.

The final item you'll receive for collecting all 12 previous rewards is the aforementioned Golden Iguana Badge, which is quirkier compared to the chain badges, but it does exactly as it says on the tin.

They aren’t the traditional Twitch drops fans hope to see from games, as they usually involve items you can equip in-game, such as cosmetics. However, they’re still fun additions to your Twitch profile that make you stand out in a chat. Perhaps clothing, decorations, or other in-game items could arrive as a future GTA 6 Twitch drops campaign, but nothing has been confirmed.

It’s unlikely that you will need to link your Rockstar account to Twitch because only Twitch items are up for grabs. If you want to prepare for any potential future drops, you can connect your accounts by going to the Rockstar Games website and logging in, selecting the profile icon to go to your account and settings, and finding linked accounts and connections to authorize a Twitch account connection.

If you’re trying to avoid story spoilers, GTA 6 streams probably aren’t the place to be, but it depends on how badly you want the badges and emotes. You could always leave a stream running in the background for an hour while you explore Leonida yourself.

Rockstar is known for putting insane levels of detail into their games, but here is how they are going above and beyond with GTA VI.
