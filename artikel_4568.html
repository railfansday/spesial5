Genetic analysis reveals peak orchid root fungal diversity at one of the most biodiverse places on Earth

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The fungal communities living in the roots of epiphytic orchids vary with altitude, location and orchid species, according to research published in the journal Microbial Ecology. The study, conducted in Podocarpus National Park in southern Ecuador, found that mid-elevation forests harbor the greatest diversity of these microorganisms, while their richness decreases at higher elevations and their community composition differs.
