OPPO find X10 flagship smartphones capture beauty with 200mp hasselblad cameras

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

from open gate video to AI editing, OPPO’s new smartphones expand the toolkit for photographers and filmmakers.

Those with an eye for the beauty of the world judge by different criteria. Beyond impeccable performance, they ask a smartphone to offer thoughtful design and help them capture the beauty before their eyes with a camera that stays true to reality.

OPPO’s flagship smartphones, Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max, more than fulfill these expectations. The devices feature a camera system co-developed with Hasselblad, alongside the latest technology, editing, and AI tools. They also boast larger batteries, the new ColorOS 17 operating system, drop resistance, and upgraded displays.

The result of a joint effort between the smartphone manufactuer and Hasselblad, the new Find X Series introduces a generation of cameras that represents, in the words of Pete Lau, OPPO’s SVP and Chief Product Officer, ‘one of our biggest leaps yet in mobile imaging.’

While OPPO Find X10 features a Dual 200MP Camera System, OPPO Find X10 Pro Max brings 200MP resolution to all three cameras: the Ultra-Wide, with a 1/1.56-inch sensor capable of capturing twice as much light as the previous generation and a 124° lens; the main Ultra HDR camera, with a 1/1.28-inch ISOCELL HPC sensor jointly developed by OPPO and Samsung with DeepPix technology; and the Telephoto Camera, which combines an ultra-fast f/2.1 aperture with a minimum focusing distance of ten centimeters, allowing cleaner telephoto images in low light.

The collaboration aims to produce images that stay as faithful to reality as possible and provide a tool for documenting what catches a curious eye. To achieve this, alongside hardware improvements, the series introduces 26MP default capture rather than the conventional 12MP, delivering more detail while keeping file sizes manageable. For higher resolution, the devices’ Hasselblad Hi-Res Mode allows users to capture 50MP and 200MP images.

The phones also incorporate LUMO True Image Engine, a next-generation computational photography solution focused on reproducing the original colors, textures and details.

Another area dedicated to capturing beauty has also seen significant improvements. Find X10 supports 4K recording at 60fps across all rear cameras, while the main and telephoto cameras also support 4K at 120fps and 8K at 30fps. Pro Max supports 4K at 60fps, 4K at 120fps and 8K at 30fps across all rear cameras, while DeepPix technology in its main camera delivers up to 17 stops of dynamic range to preserve more information in scenes with complex lighting.

Both phones support Dolby Vision, ACES-certified O-Log2 and, for the first time on an OPPO smartphone, Open Gate Capture, which uses the sensor’s full imaging area to provide greater flexibility for reframing in post-production.

Alongside a phone that promises to become a primary tool for amateur and professional photographers, OPPO has launched the Tilta Khronos Creator Kit, designed to help Find X10 Pro Max meet the demands of professional filming.

The kit includes an additional handle, a 67-millimeter filter adapter, an adapter for the OPPO Hasselblad Teleconverter and an active cooling fan to manage the phone’s temperature during extended recording.

To ensure that captured beauty does not begin and end in a photo gallery, OPPO has also worked with Meta to optimize sharing on Instagram, minimizing the loss of video quality in the process.

The remaining specifications demonstrate OPPO’s commitment to design, inside and out. Its Borderless Design casing, made from a single piece of sculpted glass, and the subtle silver LUMO Camera Island, available in the Luna White finish, make an aesthetic statement. But since good design is useful design, durability has also been considered: the phones offer IP66, IP68 and IP69 water and dust resistance, alongside SGS Five-Star Drop Resistance Certification.

Beneath the Dolby Vision-certified OLED display, among the first to support the BT.2020 color gamut used in film production, runs the ColorOS 17 operating system. It delivers a more natural, fluid, visual and responsive experience, while improving app stability by 55% and keeping more than 25% additional apps running in the background.

Finally, the phones feature Mind Pilot with Agent Mode. Through OPPO’s collaboration with Manus, users gain access to an AI service that allows them to ask the agent to work directly with photos, edit stored files and coordinate multiple steps to complete complex tasks.

AI also supports the phones’ greatest strength: their cameras. Advanced image editing stays faithful to the original while the tool recommends targeted adjustments.
