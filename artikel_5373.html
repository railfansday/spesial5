Dow rallies nearly 600 points to close out its best week since 2022

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The S&P 500 gained more than 5% and the Nasdaq more than 6% for the week, which ended with an early close

The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 591.99 points, or 1.2%, to close at 50,774.94 on Friday. That was the index's steepest one-week climb since October 2022, according to CNBC.

The S&P 500 climbed 1.6%, settling at 7,297.49, and a 2.3% rise lifted the Nasdaq $NDAQ Composite to 24,129.50. U.S. markets were closed Thursday for Thanksgiving and shut at 1 p.m. ET on Friday.

Technology stocks led the rebound. Meta $META Platforms stock rose more than 3%, Amazon $AMZN stock gained about 4% and Broadcom $AVGO stock climbed close to 4%.

For the week, the Dow and the S&P 500 each gained more than 5%, and the Nasdaq gained more than 6%. The S&P 500 turned in its strongest week since April 2025, and the Nasdaq posted its largest weekly gain since April, according to CNBC.

The week began with Nvidia $NVDA reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO. Nvidia stock jumped 31% on Monday, and the Dow gained over 1,400 points, or 3%. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq each logged their strongest one-day performance since April 2025 on Monday, according to CNBC.

Nvidia and other artificial-intelligence names have since surrendered a portion of that advance, dragging on the S&P 500 and Nasdaq, according to CNBC.

Huang left the company earlier this month. Before his departure, sentiment on AI stocks had already soured, according to CNBC, because investors were uneasy about how the buildout is funded with debt, indications that companies were trimming capital spending, and an expected slowing of data center construction following Democratic victories in the midterm elections.

Some market participants attribute the recent weakness more to the prospect of Federal Reserve interest rate increases, according to CNBC. Traders' expectations of several rate increases in the months ahead were strengthened by this week's newest personal consumption expenditures price index data.
