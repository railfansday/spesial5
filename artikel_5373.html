Femto Sensing International completes its FBG Interrogators manufacturing facility expansion in Atlanta, doubling its production capacity

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

ATLANTA, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Femto Sensing International (www.femtosensing.com), a wholly owned subsidiary of Technica Optical Components (www.technicasa.com) has completed its Atlanta, USA, based manufacturing facility expansion to adapt to increasing demand for its solid-state Optical Sensing FBG Interrogators. The recent doubling of its Atlanta facility's production capacity places the company in a strong position to meet planned FBG Interrogators manufacturing for 2027 and beyond.

Whether ultra-high performance or a most competitive commercial entry FBG Interrogator is the goal, customers will find an exciting optical sensing solution at Femto Sensing International.

Femto Sensing International offers commercial grade highly accurate FBG Interrogators with scanning ranges from 30nm to 160nm. The company's portfolio includes the FAZT E Series, M Series, S Series, and the rapidly growing W Series Models of Interrogators: https://femtosensing.com/fbg-monitoring-instruments/

To fully benefit from Femto Sensing International's FBG Interrogator hardware platforms, the company also provides a world-class matching software platform, comprising of PicoSense X, PCS Logger, MODBUS TCP support, and a built-in Web Backend Server for the M Series and W Series Interrogators, FemtoSense for its FAZT E Series Interrogators, and NanoSense for its S Series Interrogators, all free of charge: https://femtosensing.com/ecosystem-for-m-series-fbg-interrogators/

Femto Sensing International FBG Interrogators are designed and manufactured in USA and in Canada, and are used in many industries to support both the development and commercial installation of Fiber Bragg Grating Sensors based Optical Sensing Solutions worldwide. The markets addressed include energy and power monitoring, structural health monitoring, aerospace, transportation, marine, industrial, medical, security, civil, and high-performance vehicles, vessels, and structures. Femto Sensing International's growing applications include sensing in high-temperature environments to 1000 Celsius, in cryogenic environments to -270 Celsius, in radiation environments, in chemical environments, and in remote otherwise un-accessible environments.

Femto Sensing International is a world leader in the fiber optic sensing industry, with a focus on Fiber Bragg Grating (FBG) Interrogators. The company supports its customers by continuously developing its team and diligently enhancing its manufacturing technologies through innovations and partnerships worldwide.

FSI's mission is to provide the highest quality and performance FBG Interrogators to meet or exceed customer requirements across a variety of industries. An ISO9001 certified company, FSI achieves this by making quality its number one priority, delivering products and services on time, reducing costs, and releasing products that are superior to the competition.

FSI is committed to providing state-of-the-art FBG monitoring instruments, and its vision is to make premium performance optical sensing affordable for a growing number of applications. Its solid-state designs are scalable for mass production, with volume-driven cost reductions passed directly to its customers.
