Behind COP31 failure: A showdown over fossil fuel interests

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Several countries and environmental groups expressed fury at the collapse of the COP31 climate conference in Antalya, Turkey on Sunday, amid a dramatic failure to thrash out an agreement, two days after the summit was scheduled to end. Oil-producing states blocked any mention of phasing out fossil fuels – the root cause of the climate crisis – with the EU then refusing to back a weakened text. Our Environment Editor Valerie Dekimpe tells us more.
