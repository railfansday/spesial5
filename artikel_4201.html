Nab A Free Shiny Legendary With Pokémon Legends: Z-A's New Limited-Time Mystery Gift Code

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Pokémon Company is keeping the 30th Anniversary celebrations coming, as it has today announced a brand new Mystery Gift event for Pokémon Legends: Z-A that will add a shiny Legendary 'mon to your roster.

By entering the code CELEBRATE30TH between now and 7th January 2027, you can net yourself a free Shiny Xerneas.

Of course, the collection wouldn't be complete with just one Gen VI Legendary on the cards. TPC has also announced that a Shiny Yveltal distribution will kick off on 10th December. We don't have the Mystery Gift code for that one just yet, but we'll be keeping an eye out next month and will let you all know once it lands.
