Witcher 3's New Expansion Pairs Geralt's Polish Voice Actor With AI, But It's A "Special Case"

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

CDPR got the actor's blessing, after he suffered a stroke

The Witcher 3 is getting a brand new expansion next year titled 'Songs of the Past' and it's been revealed CD Projekt Red has used AI to help out with the voice work.

Before you get those pitchforks out, this is a "special case", according to the Polish developer. As highlighted by Game File reporter Stephen Totilo, CDPR called on the AI company 'Respeecher' to "record and tweak" the Polish voice of Geralt, Jacek Rozenek, after he suffered a stroke in 2019.

When the third expansion got underway, the team at CDPR wanted him involved but noticed his voice was "lower" and "gruffer", and feared it would be jarring for the player experience (swapping between the old and new dialogue). So with Rozenek's blessing, it got this AI company (which had previously worked on Cyberpunk 2077 after a VA's passing) to help make his performance sound more like it used to.

CD Projekt Red's head of localization Mikołaj Szwed shared the following comment with Game File about this decision:

"I do think this is one of those ways where you can use AI ethically, with consent and also do something good".

It's worth noting Doug Cockle will return as Geralt's English VA in the upcoming expansion, launching next year. This will follow the third game's remaster, which was released on the Switch 2 and other platforms in September.
