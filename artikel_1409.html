Luis Enrique: “This was by far the hardest game so far.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Luis Enrique spoke to Amazon Prime Video to comment on the draw against Roma.

“It was definitely the most difficult match PSG have played this season, without a shadow of a doubt, against a Roma side whose level we knew they had.”

“Gasperini’s Roma were unique, with a lot of personality and very aggressive, and we saw it from the first minutes.”

“They scored on the first chance they created. It was difficult, really complicated, and the players said so too. Congratulations to Roma. I’m happy with this point, we could have even lost.”

“It’s better to talk about Roma’s future, not PSG’s. Roma’s future is wonderful; they can win the Scudetto. I have no doubt they can finish in the top 24, I hope so, and I wouldn’t like to face them again. Maybe in the final…”

Gasperini after PSG draw: “I will rewatch it, we were great. Soule is becoming a top player.”

Gasperini: “I’m happy and proud of my team. Castro deserved a chance, Hermoso was brilliant.”
