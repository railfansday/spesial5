Cosy Train Sim 'Station To Station' Arrives On Switch 2 Later This Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Do you like trains? Of course you do, who doesn't like trains. Stupid question. Anyway, you're in luck, dear reader. The cosy train sim Station to Station is making its way to the Switch 2, launching on 26th November 2026.

Boasting lovely voxel visuals, Station to Station starts you off with a handful of rural structures, tasking you with creating stations and connections to expand your world and transform it into a lush, thriving landscape.

While it's ultimately a relaxing experience, you can also earn high scores by engaging with more strategic gameplay, allowing you to stack bonuses and multiply your income.

On Switch 2, you can expect to find mouse controls along with the game's Creative Mode, letting you explore community designs and share your own. The main game will contain 54 levels set across 7 regions.

Over on Steam, Station to Station currently commands a total of 1,248 user reviews, of which 89% are positive. It's also earned solid scores from critics too, including 8/10 from IGN and 4/5 from Tech Radar.
