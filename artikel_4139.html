Grand Theft Auto 6 Outsold Pokémon Red & Blue in Its First Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Scott Baird is a Contributor from the UK with over a decade of video game writing experience.

Sales data for Grand Theft Auto 6’s first week has been revealed, and it’s already one of the best-selling video games of all time, soaring past Pokémon Red & Blue on the Nintendo Game Boy.

The joint best-selling video games of all time are Tetris and Minecraft, two titles with universal appeal, and can be enjoyed by people of all ages. The next best-selling game is Grand Theft Auto 5, an extremely violent and adult game starring three psychopaths who go on rampages. It has made so much money because of its online component, Grand Theft Auto Online, which still rakes in cash for Take-Two.

As of the time of writing, Grand Theft Auto 6 has been on the market for a week. The first sales estimates have been released by Alinea Analytics, which claims that Grand Theft Auto 6 has already sold a staggering number of copies.

Take-Two has issued a DMCA takedown notice against GTA 6 PC recompilation project Leonida, affecting 3,007 GitHub repositories.

According to Alinea Analytics, Grand Theft Auto 6 has sold roughly 53 million copies: 40 million on PlayStation 5 and 13 million on Xbox Series X/S. If these numbers are accurate, then Grand Theft Auto 6 is now the twelfth-best-selling game of all time, sitting between Animal Crossing: New Horizons and Human Fall Flat.

Some of the most impressive names beaten by Grand Theft Auto 6 include Pokémon Red & Blue, the games that kicked off the biggest multimedia franchise in history; the original Sonic the Hedgehog; Super Smash Bros. Ultimate; and Elden Ring. This is a staggering achievement, especially after only a week of availability.

The numbers are only going to get higher for Grand Theft Auto 6, as the 2026 holiday season is approaching, which is a time when lots of people are going to be buying consoles as gifts. More importantly, there’s the PC release, which will add a few tens of millions to the sales numbers, as well as the inevitable PlayStation 6 and Project Helix upgrades.

According to Alinea Analytics, Grand Theft Auto 6 has sold roughly 53 million copies, with 40 million on PlayStation 5, and the other 13 million on Xbox Series X/S.

The current version of Grand Theft Auto 6 is going to be chump change compared to what the game’s online component will bring in. Grand Theft Auto Online brought in a staggering amount of profit, and its successor could do even better.

Grand Theft Auto 6 still has a long way to go to become the best-selling video game of all time, something its predecessor never managed to achieve. The titans of Nintendo await it, with Wii Sports and Mario Kart 8 Deluxe both selling over 80 million copies, while Rockstar’s own Red Dead Redemption 2 is nearly at 90 million.

The two big dots on the horizon are the ones that staved off Grand Theft Auto 5 for so long: Tetris and Minecraft. These are games that sold over 400 million copies apiece, a staggering achievement, helped by the fact that they have no age restrictions. Grand Theft Auto 6 had an amazing start, but it’s unlikely to ever beat those two games in sales, even if the online component eventually makes it more profitable.

While Grand Theft Auto 6 might not break the record, its current sales are still an incredible achievement. The numerous delays had fans wondering whether the game was in trouble and would launch in a messy state. Those delays have paid off in droves, wth the result being the biggest success story of the current console generation.
