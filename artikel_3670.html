32 Spoiler-Free Tips As You Hop Into Grand Theft Auto 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Grand Theft Auto 6GTA 6Rockstar GamesTips

Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here. The long wait was totally worth it. It is one of the best games I’ve played in many years and easily the best Grand Theft Auto game ever made. It is also a big, complicated, and deep game, with so much to do and see.

So, to help you out, here’s a bunch of tips for players hopping into GTA 6 this week. Don’t worry, no spoilers are included below.

Without spoiling anything, there are some incredibly big surprises and really memorable moments buried in this game. I highly recommend that people avoid watching too many videos about GTA 6 ahead of playing it to avoid ruining one particular late-game moment that might be one of my favorite video game surprises in years.

Before you rush out the door to do some missions or rob a liquor store, take a moment to grab a bite and a drink. These items will buff your health, stamina, and focus meter, letting you take more damage, run longer, and slow down time more during combat.

And hey, don’t assume that just because you’re drinking a soda or eating a hot dog, you have to stop moving or shooting. In GTA 6, you can move, shoot, run, hold hands, and even drive while holding and eating food. Finally, you can crack a cold one while shooting a rifle.

Around the map, you’ll encounter dogs and cats. If you hold the left trigger, you can zoom in on them and then praise and pet them. And you should do this, not just because dogs and cats are great, but also because it buffs your focus and rewards you with some good behavior points toward your criminal profile.

In GTA 6, you can store guns and clothes in your personal car’s trunk. And you should do this, as changing clothes is a great and fast way to lose the attention of the police.

You can manage your clothing, create new custom outfits, and store clothes in your trunk at any clothing store with a changing room, so don’t feel like you have to head back home to change your outfits around or remove stuff from your trunk. Just find a store.

Also, Rockstar lets you rename your custom outfits using a list of pre-existing words, like clubbing, hunting, or everyday. I recommend doing that to make it easier at a glance to see what everything stored in your trunk actually is without having to put it on and check.

This will let you buy clothing for both of them, try on new items as either character, and modify their current attire. The world of Leonida is packed with random events and moments that you can encounter while just walking around. These can be as simple as a drug deal going bad, or as complex as someone who needs help finding their missing spouse after a night partying. Often these events will be signaled to the player via shouting or other loud noises. If you hear something like that, click the left and right sticks to activate focus mode and find the source of the commotion, and then go investigate it.

This section of Jason and Lucia’s phone will update as you play and will remind you to call certain characters or reply to texts. This can often reveal new story moments and dialogue between Lucia and Jason and their various friends and criminal cohorts.

Very early into GTA 6, you unlock the ability to explore underwater shipwrecks and find valuable loot. And you should do some of these in the early hours of GTA 6 to earn some easy cash. Plus, it’s just fun to explore the ocean.

On Jason and Lucia’s phones, you’ll find an app called Scooter Bros. This lets you manage your owned vehicles, spawn them near you, and even repair them. However, this isn’t free and early on money will be tight, so be careful how often you rely on this convenient app. It can also get vehicles out of police impound lots, but that ain’t cheap.

It costs $12,500 to upgrade to the premium version of the Scooter Bros service, which, for many hours in the game, is going to be more than you can spare. But once you feel comfortable spending that much, I recommend it. It gives you three free uses of the app every in-game day, which saves money in the long run. Plus, the app is upgraded and says you’re premium, and that’s nice.

Eventually, GTA 6 will let you buy garages around the map. These aren’t very useful while driving about, but they do give you more slots to store cars and motorcycles as you build up Jason and Lucia’s fleet of personal vehicles. Try to buy some when you can so you can have a wide array of customized bikes, cars, and trucks at your disposal.

You don’t have to, but if you want to do so, just tap left or right to signal to other drivers which way you are turning. In my testing, NPC drivers did seem to react to these signals. One more silly detail in GTA 6.Â

Jason and Lucia can both tag enemies while aiming at them with weapons or using binoculars. This will let you keep track of them and make sneaking around compounds much easier. Oh, and don’t forget to use focus to see enemies making noise and moving around, too.

Binocs can also be used to study the many, many animals in GTA 6 from a safe distance.

When sneaking into a place to rob it, use focus to spot which doors are connected to the alarm system quickly. These doors will glow red. Avoid kicking these doors in and work around them or lockpick them once you have that tool.

Zombix is the in-game medicine that you can pop during combat to heal up. You can find it in the world for free. But if you need some, you can also buy it at stores. Just talk to the clerk to buy some, as it’s often not stocked in the store like food and drinks.

Refueling cars is the main purpose of gas stations, but don’t forget you can also repair your car or motorcycle for a small fee at any gas pump, too. This is often cheaper than using the Scooter Bros app.

In GTA 6, you can hold down on the D-pad to slow down time and see nearby points of interest, missions, and other locations worth checking out. You can also mark any of these spots, and then the game will give you directions via the GPS. It’s faster than opening the map and useful for finding nearby gas stations if you’re running low on fuel.

If you steal a car and you spot a red tracking icon in the bottom left, near the engine and gas icons, you should probably get out quickly. That red icon indicates the car is fitted with an advanced tracker, and police will be able to find you on the map if you drive slowly for too long. You’ll want to avoid these cars until much later in the game when you get the ability to remove these trackers for a high cost. Use the Waink app on your phone by holding up on the D-pad to quickly scan cars and check for trackers.

In GTA 6, cops won’t always pull their guns out and try to arrest or shoot you. Instead, a one-star wanted level can be erased if you just talk to the cop and defuse the situation. Hold the left trigger to focus on the cop (make sure you don’t have a gun out), and you’ll see the option to defuse or provoke the cop. This works on any NPC and might let you avoid violence. Some people will still attack or harass regardless of whether you try to stay calm and defuse the situation, though.

While exploring the map, if you feel a sudden and intense vibration pulsing in your controller, stop and look around with the Waink app. There’s likely some art or something else worth scanning nearby.

Don’t be a litterbug. Giving a hoot boosts your focus and gives you some good behavior points.

While you’ll be able to find similar items and hairstyles at all stores and barber shops, most of these places also have exclusive options available only at that spot. So exploreÂ  and see what you can find.

If you just want a quick and simple trim, you can always look at your mirror in your safehouse bathroom and cut your hair down. You can’t change the style beyond that, though. I guess only the barbers and stylists in Vice City have the magic power to make hair grow back instantly.

Leonida is Rockstar’s digital recreation of Florida. And as someone who grew up in Florida, I was raised to always assume a gator is in every body of water. That’s a good rule of thumb when playing GTA 6, too.

While I encourage everyone to take their time (see next tip), if you really want to get somewhere fast, just use the RydeMe app to call a driver, and then once you’re in the car, hold the left trigger to focus on the driver and get the option to fast travel to your destination for $100.

Listen, how long have you already waited to play this game? Trust me, it’s worth slowing down and taking it all in. There’s so much to see, do, and discover out there, and most of it will pass you by if you speedrun the game.
