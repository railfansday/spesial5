In a candid podcast interview with The Valley’s executive producer, Taylor opened up about his struggles with addiction and where he stands today. "People ask you, 'Do you have an alcohol problem too?' I don't necessarily think I have an alcohol problem but the two go hand in hand," Taylor said on the March 4 episode of the “Hot Mic” podcast. "I can't do cocaine without drinking. So I just gave up both. I'm proud to say I'm 83 days sober right now, which is the longest I've ever gone in my life without either." "I'm the No. 1 sober guy in the group," he added. "I do still smoke weed a little bit. I need that to go to sleep. I prefer to do that over pills. So I'm what you call California sober and that's fine."

One week before The Valley’s season 2 finale was set to air, Taylor announced he would be taking a step back from the show for season 3. “After an incredibly challenging year and many honest conversations with my team and producers, I’ll be stepping away from the next season of The Valley,” he exclusively told Us Weekly on July 16. “Right now, my focus needs to be on my sobriety, my mental health, and coparenting. Taking this time is necessary for me to become the best version of myself — especially for our son, Cruz.” Taylor is expected to still appear in The Valley season 2 finale and reunion special.

Taylor opened up about a health scare via Instagram, revealing he had been diagnosed with cancer.  “This is something I’ve kept private for the past few months because it’s been one of the scariest times of my life,” he shared. “Back in July, I woke up one morning in horrible pain and ended up in the hospital with kidney stones. What I thought was just a kidney stone turned into something I never imagined. My CT scan also showed a tumor on my left kidney. After many tests and appointments I found out it was cancer.” Taylor continued, “I lost my father to cancer, so hearing that word just broke me. It put me in a really dark place. The hardest part was the waiting. Knowing there was a tumor growing inside me while trying to go about my life like everything was fine. It’s crazy how you can act like everything is normal when deep down you’re a mess.” The reality star revealed he had a five-hour surgery to remove the tumor, “but it was a lot larger than they thought so we are waiting on the pathology results. Taylor called the experience “eye-opening” and urged fans not to take their health for granted.  “I did for many years and I won’t ever do that again,” he said. “Next week on November 28 will mark my 2 year sobriety anniversary and I’m so fucking proud. It’s crazy how things happen…if I hadn’t ended up in the hospital with that kidney stone, I probably wouldn’t have known this tumor was there until it was too late.” He concluded, “I like to think my dad had a part in that. Life can really change in a second. I’ve been given a lot to think about these past few years and if there’s one thing I know for sure it’s that nothing else matters more than your health your kids and the people that show up for you when it matters the most.”
