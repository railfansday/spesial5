Dark Horse Estate, Where Taylor Swift and Dolly Parton Recorded Hits, Is Listed for $19.5 Million

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Dark Horse Estate, home to a famous recording studio in Franklin, TN, where artists such as Taylor Swift and Dolly Parton have recorded hit music, is now available for $19.5 million.

The estate was built in 1998 on a sprawling 10.01-acre lot. It was where more than 65 gold and platinum albums have been recorded and features 23,138 square feet of interior space, including a manor, the recording studio, The Cottage, The Bungalow apartment, and The Party Barn.

According to the listing, legendary artists such as Keith Urban, Reba McEntire, Amy Grant, and Peter Frampton have come here to “escape, focus, and create."

The retreat has multiple studios, private guest quarters, and public gathering spaces. It has also reached the top of the week’s most popular homes list.

The outdoor spaces are designed for entertaining and creative inspiration. More than 3,000 square feet of covered porches and walkways overlook the peaceful property.

Other properties grabbing attention this week include a transformed schoolhouse in Kansas, a Virginia farmhouse built next to an accessible cave that once housed a moonshine bootlegging operation, and a South Carolina estate perched on dramatic rock formations in the foothills of the Blue Ridge Mountains.
