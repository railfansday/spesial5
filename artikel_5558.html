FatPipe, Inc. (FATN) Reports Q2 FY2027 Results: 22% Y-o-Y Q2 Revenue Growth and 24.1% Adjusted EBITDA Margin

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

SALT LAKE CITY, Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- FatPipe, Inc. (NASDAQ: FATN) ("FatPipe" or the "Company"), a pioneer and multiple patents holder in enterprise-class, application-aware, secure software-defined wide area network ("SD-WAN") and single-stack cybersecurity solutions, today announced its second quarter fiscal year 2027 results for the period ended September 30, 2026.

Revenue growth during the quarter was driven by increased recurring billings, customer renewals, and new customer wins. FatPipe continued to see strong demand from customers seeking secure, high-performance alternatives to legacy networking and security vendors.

"We are pleased with our continued revenue growth and profitability this quarter, reflecting the strength of our business model and continued execution across the organization," said Dr. Ragula Bhaskar, CEO of FatPipe. "We remain focused on expanding our channel partnerships, growing our customer base, and building on the momentum across our SD-WAN, cybersecurity, and network monitoring offerings."

"Our second quarter results reflect continued progress in growing our recurring revenue base," said Kanishka Ragula, CFO of FatPipe. "With revenue increasing 22% year-over-year and an Adjusted EBITDA margin of 24.1%, we continue to demonstrate our ability to deliver profitable growth while investing in strategic initiatives. We believe our financial position provides a strong foundation to expand our sales organization, strengthen our partner ecosystem, and pursue additional growth opportunities."
