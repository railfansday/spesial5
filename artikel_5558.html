NovoTech Patent Firm Ranked in Inaugural Chambers 2027 DC Metro Spotlight Guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Washington, D.C. patent firm recognized among 128 small and mid-sized law firms across 35 practice areas

WASHINGTON, Nov. 6, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- NovoTech Patent Firm ("NovoTech") has been recognized for its Intellectual Property practice in the inaugural Chambers USA Spotlight 2027 DC Metro Guide, which identifies 128 leading small and mid-sized law firms across 35 practice areas in the Washington metropolitan region.

Chambers Spotlight rankings are based on independent market analysis and an evaluation of firms' experience, expertise and talent. The Spotlight guides are designed to identify smaller and mid-sized firms providing sophisticated legal counsel within their respective markets.

NovoTech focuses on patent strategy for technology companies, helping startups, emerging companies and established businesses build investor-grade patent portfolios designed to support fundraising, growth and long-term competitive advantage. The firm's 12-member team includes ten seasoned patent attorneys and two experienced IP administrators. Its attorneys combine legal experience with technical backgrounds spanning electrical engineering, computer science and other advanced technologies, advising clients in areas including artificial intelligence, software, robotics and telecommunications.

"I spent part of my career in Big Law, so I know the value of sophisticated legal work. NovoTech was built to deliver that level of patent strategy in a focused firm where clients work closely with experienced attorneys who also understand the technology," said Babak Akhlaghi, Founding Director of NovoTech. "To have Chambers recognize that model in its inaugural DC Metro Spotlight Guide is especially meaningful to our entire team."

Akhlaghi is a registered patent attorney and electrical engineer who graduated first in his electrical engineering class at the University of Maryland. Before founding NovoTech, he was a partner at McDermott Will & Emery, practiced at Fish & Richardson and served as in-house counsel at Panasonic in Japan. He is also an Adjunct Professor at the University of Maryland, where he teaches Legal Aspects of Entrepreneurship, and a co-author of the Patent Applications Handbook.
