The power of agile workflows

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Mortgage lending workflows built on data triggers can reduce handoffs and rework. Agile queuing can lower cost per file touch while improving borrower feedback loops.   AI Summary

Every leader in this industry knows that every dollar matters, every teammate matters and every borrower matters. However, when we try to prioritize all of these, something often has to give. What if we didn’t have to compromise, though? What if it were possible to reduce costs, optimize your team and create a fast, easy borrower experience?

Agile workflows aren’t made of “unicorns and rainbows” and “all things possible” energy, but they are the answer to this.

Our industry’s cost of origination is at one of the highest points in history ($10,936 per loan as of Q2 2026). When approximately 70% of that cost is our operating overhead, how effective and efficient our teams are REALLY matters.

Today, most of our industry’s teams and technology operate around an extremely linear process that requires our borrowers to fit into our process that was designed to move paper. So, the borrowers that give you 100% of what you need on day one have the same process as the borrower who drags their feet and gets you what you need piece by piece.

We might be able to get one cleared to close a bit faster than the other, but both borrowers went from their loan officer to their processor, to an underwriter, back to a processor, back to the underwriter, then to closing. Every single one of those handoffs adds time, costs and frustration to the borrower who just wants to know if they are approved quickly and if they can close on time.

Agile workflows queue work to be completed when the data and documents say that the work can be completed. They don’t rely on milestones or handoffs.

For example, if a borrower has all of their income and employment documentation and data submitted but they haven’t found a property yet, that loan’s income and employment can still be queued for review by a decision maker (technology or an underwriter…it’s up to your organization’s risk threshold) who can issue that borrower’s income/employment approval, NOT pre-approval. We don’t have to wait until they find a property or until they go through processing.

Queuing work this way gives certainty to borrowers with a faster feedback loop. So, that borrower who brings everything in on day one finally gets rewarded for it. It also ensures that your team is only working on what matters most. They have work queued to them when it’s needed and the rest of their time can be spent building relationships with borrowers and referral partners.

That’s the borrower and team side. Now, let’s talk about cost. Every loan we originate has to meet very specific criteria from the GSEs or from private investors. So we can’t necessarily eliminate work that has to be completed, but we can eliminate handoffs, assign work to technology solutions, and reduce rework.

Let’s start here: Have you ever measured your cost per file touch? It’s your fulfillment cost divided by the total number of touches across your files. If you really want to dig into this even further, you can measure this by role type to see the cost of how many times your underwriter vs your closer vs your processor vs your AI agent touches the file.

I’m not suggesting we eliminate roles reviewing things when it’s needed, but I am saying that unnecessary reviews do cost an organization more than most of us realize.

Reducing unnecessary touches is critical, but that’s not really the power of agile workflows.  Agile workflows are the answer because they queue the right touches exactly when they’re needed to the exact right person on your team or the exact right technology that can complete the work on your behalf. It’s orchestrating the work between your team and your technology in a way that reduces friction while controlling risk.

This isn’t easy to operationalize, though. It requires three major shifts: building your workflow around data triggers instead of milestones, assigning decision authority by risk threshold of the work and measuring cost per file touch. Then, you have to align your team’s roles, expectations and incentive plans around these shifts.

This is A LOT, but it is possible with almost every LOS provider out there (including the one you’re likely already on), and the rewards are incredible for your team, your borrowers and your company.

I know we’ve been told that we have to choose between efficient teams, happy borrowers and our bottom line, but there is a better way. Agile workflows are the answer.
