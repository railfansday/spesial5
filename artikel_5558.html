Pfizer is tapping Blackstone for up to $1.5 billion to fund its weight-loss drug

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Blackstone would be eligible for milestone payments tied to U.S. approval of monthly berobenatide, while Pfizer keeps full control

Pfizer $PFE and Blackstone Life Sciences announced a co-funding agreement on Wednesday in which Blackstone-managed funds will shoulder as much as $1.5 billion of berobenatide's R&D expenses between now and 2028. The drug is an investigational monthly injection being studied for chronic weight management and obesity-related conditions.

In return, Blackstone could receive payments tied to the achievement of certain milestones, the companies said. The payments depend on U.S. regulatory approval of monthly dosing of injectable berobenatide monotherapy for chronic weight management, and on certain commercial sales milestones. Any payments earned will be made over a multi-year period.

Blackstone does not acquire any rights or ownership in berobenatide, and Pfizer retains full operational control and decision-making over the program, the companies said. Funds managed by Blackstone's perpetual private equity strategy, BXPE, will invest alongside the Blackstone Life Sciences funds.

"Our aim is to bring important new medicines to patients while taking a disciplined approach that pairs internal research investments with targeted investment from capital partners," Pfizer Chief Financial Officer Cecile Guegan said in a statement. "Today's announcement helps us advance our comprehensive obesity program and maintain the financial capacity and flexibility to invest in our broader pipeline across all therapeutic areas."

Nicholas Galakatos, global head of Blackstone Life Sciences, said in a statement: "We are pleased to further expand our relationship with Pfizer and support the broad and rigorous development of berobenatide as the anchor of Pfizer's cardiometabolic pipeline."

Pfizer intends to move forward with over 20 studies covering obesity and related conditions during 2026, the company said. Those include 10 ongoing and planned Phase 3 trials for berobenatide, which is being studied for conditions such as knee osteoarthritis and obstructive sleep apnea. The company is developing the drug as a monotherapy and in combination with other peptides, including amylin analogs.

Pfizer is pushing into a weight-loss field currently dominated by Eli Lilly $LLY and Novo Nordisk, according to Bloomberg. The company acquired Metsera in 2025 in a deal worth up to $10 billion.
