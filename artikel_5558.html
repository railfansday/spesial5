Surfshark's no-logs policy verified by KPMG

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Cybersecurity company Surfshark just released its third no-logs assurance report. The independent verification by KPMG, one of the Big Four auditing firms, confirms that Surfshark does not monitor what users do online and does not keep logs of their activity.

VILNIUS, Lithuania, Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- "Users have no direct way to see how a VPN handles their traffic, so we rely on external auditors to answer that question on their behalf. One of the Big Four auditing firms, KPMG, reviewed our server configurations, handling of temporary login data, and employee access controls, confirming that we do not log or monitor online activity. Undergoing these reviews year after year, rather than pointing back to a single past audit, proves that our no-logs commitment remains true as our network expands," says Gabriele Sinkeviciute, Head of Product at Surfshark.

This is Surfshark's third independent assurance of its no-logs policy. Previous independent verifications and certifications are listed in Surfshark's trust center, and the company is committed to continuing these checks regularly.

Surfshark is a cybersecurity company offering products including an audited VPN, certified antivirus, data leak warning system, and tool for generating an online identity. Recognized as a leading VPN by CNET and TechRadar, Surfshark has also been featured on the FT1000: Europe's Fastest Growing Companies ranking. Headquartered in the Netherlands, Surfshark has offices in Lithuania and Poland. For information on Surfshark's operations and highlights, read our Annual Wrap-up. For information about Surfshark's previous independent verifications and certifications, visit our trust center.
