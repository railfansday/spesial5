"What Other Forms of Architecture Might Exist?": In Conversation With Participants at the 2026 Sharjah Architecture Triennial

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The third edition of the Sharjah Architecture Triennial (SAT03), titled Architecture Otherwise: Building Civic Infrastructure for Collective Futures, brings together practitioners exploring architecture beyond conventional building practices. During the opening week, ArchDaily's Editor-in-Chief Christele Harrouk spoke with Makram el Kadi and Ziad Jamaleddine of L.E.FT, Mohamad Nahleh, Khondaker Hasibul Kabir and Suhailey Farzana of POCAA, May al-Ibrashy of Megawra-BEC, Nathalie Harb and Mohamad Hafeda, and Hamed Bukhamseen and Ali İsmail Karimi of Civil Architecture about their contributions, which engage with spiritual practices, community-led spatial production, heritage, displacement, and material reuse. Through installations, collective research, and interventions in existing spaces, their work considers how architecture can emerge through different forms of knowledge, care, and engagement with everyday life.
