Google Pixel Tag is only available in 8 countries, and excludes Europe

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

With Google’s Pixel Tag now offiically available, Google has also confirmed where the Android Find Hub tracker will be on sale, and it’s a limited list.

Compared to the two dozen countries where Pixel 11 phones and the Pixel Watch 5 are sold, the Pixel Tag tracker is only being sold in 8 countries at launch. This could change over time, but that’s all that Google is officially supporting for the time being, as detailed on a support page.

Does this mean that Pixel Tag won’t work in countries where it’s not sold? There’s no definitive answer on that yet, but it likely will at least function – travel is one of the biggest places a tracker like this is used, after all. Buyers in other countries, though, may not be able to buy it from authorized retailers and won’t be able to use Google’s warranty if they obtained it elsewhere.

Google may end up expanding the list of countries where Pixel Tag is sold in time, of course. A very obvious gap here is Europe, where Pixel Tag simply isn’t sold (aside from the United Kingdom, which is part of Europe as a region, but not a part of the European Union).
