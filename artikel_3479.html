3 Best New Prime Video Series To Binge-Watch This Weekend (November 13–15, 2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The 3 best new Prime Video shows to binge this weekend, November 13–15, 2026, include a fantasy epic, a global romance hit, and an action thriller. The top trending Prime Video series heading into the weekend range from massive streaming hits like Neagley and Carrie as well as new arrivals such as The Greatest. That said, the following three series are the best recent releases to stream right now on Prime.

Earlier this week, ScreenRant recommendations for what to watch on Prime Video featured The Chosen, an epic biblical series that is set to return with its season 6 premiere this Sunday, as well as iZombie, a critically acclaimed crime/horror series that was just added at the beginning of November. Also, make sure to check out our updated streaming guides for Netflix, HBO Max, and Paramount+ this weekend.

The Terminal List remains one of Prime Video's most popular series of its Fall 2026 streaming slate, ranking #2 on its U.S. viewership chart and #2 worldwide following its season 2 premiere on October 21. After a four-year wait between seasons, The Terminal List season 2 delivers its most powerful chapter yet, bringing Chris Pratt back as former Navy SEAL officer James Reece, who gets an opportunity from the CIA to clear his name by taking out a new terrorist threat. Despite its success, The Terminal List season 3 has not yet been confirmed.

The Terminal List season 2 improved to a 73% Rotten Tomatoes score after season 1's mixed reviews. However, the series overall has a near-perfect RT audience score of 92% and an impressive 7.9 rating on IMDb. CBR gave The Terminal List season 2 an 8/10 score in its review, noting, "Prime Video's premiere military novel adaptation does not miss its mark and delivers an exhilarating and dark sophomore season." With eight new riveting episodes, The Terminal List season 2 is a must-binge on Prime Video, even as a rewatch this weekend.

Campus Drivers has officially taken over Prime Video's worldwide viewership chart, skyrocketing to the streamer's #1 global spot following its premiere on November 6. Campus Drivers is extremely binge-worthy at six short episodes, each between 28 and 46 minutes, for a total runtime of exactly 4 hours. If you enjoy Prime Video's other coming-of-age romance series such as Off Campus and Sterling Point, there is truly no better show on the platform to binge right now than Campus Drivers, which is based on the French book series of the same name.

Campus Drivers follows Lois, a student who enters college feeling heartbroken and down on her luck after losing her boyfriend and her apartment. Lois rebounds by meeting Lane, a popular student on campus known for creating a popular app called Campus Drivers, a ride-share service he founded and operates with his best friends. Similar to Off Campus, Campus Drivers shifts its protagonists between its several books, setting up a new budding romantic drama franchise for Prime Video that is well worth a one-night binge this weekend.

Prime Video's best new series to binge this weekend is The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power, following its 4-part season 3 premiere on Wednesday, November 11. The Rings of Power earned an 83% Rotten Tomatoes score across its three existing seasons, with a fourth currently in development, and will release two new episodes on Wednesday, November 18, ahead of its 2-part season finale on November 25. It's currently the #1 most-watched Prime Video show in the U.S. and remains one of Prime's biggest and most expensive franchises.

The Boys star Antony Starr leads a brutal 38-episode HBO Max action thriller about an ex-con posing as a sheriff that is a must-see for Reacher fans.

The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power is set thousands of years before The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings movie trilogies, telling the story of Sauron's rise and the forging of the Rings of Power, including the famed One Ring. The Rings of Power combines high fantasy with action and adventure and is one of Prime's most visually stunning series, although it has received mixed feedback from audiences. Still, all Lord of the Rings fans will want to binge the first four episodes of The Rings of Power on Prime Video this weekend and see for themselves whether the series lives up to its storied franchise name.
