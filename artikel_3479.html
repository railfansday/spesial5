We're talking, of course, about The Mosquito Bowl, directed by Peter Berg. The movie grossed $455,000 in its box-office debut, which represents a per-theater average of around $1,300. By comparison, La Bola Negra grossed $418,000 from just 38 theaters, as its running domestic haul passed the $1 million mark. The Mosquito Bowl currently holds a 39% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes, but its 92% audience score on the aggregator website bodes well for its eventual release on Netflix. The movie will land on the streamer on November 25, just two days after Netflix debuts The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth in IMAX theaters for two weeks.
