iOS 27.3 will give iCloud+ subscribers two new perks

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

When iOS 27.3 beta 2 debuted earlier this week, we detailed how the update introduces AI usage limits for Siri AI and select other features.

For AI features that depend on server-based models, each device running iOS 27.3, iPadOS 27.3, or macOS 27.3 will have limited daily access to advanced AI features. These include complex Siri AI requests, Image Playground, certain Photos app editing tools, and more.

The only iCloud+ tier that doesn’t include higher limits is the $0.99/month plan.

In the Shortcuts app, the ‘Use Model’ action lets you choose between a few different model options, including Cloud, Cloud Pro, On-Device, and ChatGPT.

People should at least have the 200 GB model. When I worked at The Apple Store (Did Retail and Corporate) I always recommended iCloud, and highlighted the backup feature. I can’t tell you the amount of people who lost or got their phones damaged / destroyed and lost precious photos, videos that they could never get back. Nearly all signed up.

I did offer alternatives like backing to your Mac or external SSD, but to have a proper backup safety system you need 1-2 backups at home and one off site (ie. friends house, storage facility, etc.) and you would have to be consistent with your backups and drop offs. Most people don’t have the time so they do iCloud, same as I.

I’m at 2 TB, probably going to 6 TB, cause I love taking photos and videos of special events.

Similar to Apple’s other AI usage limits, your access to the Cloud Pro model in Shortcuts increases if you have a higher storage plan. Per this support document, the 200GB plan supports standard limits, the 2TB plan offers 5x access, followed by 10x on the 6TB plan and 20x on the 12TB plan.

iCloud+ already provided far more than just increased cloud storage, but with iOS 27.3 it’s set to be an asset for AI power users too.

iCloud+ is available as a standalone subscription or through the Apple One bundle, which also includes Apple TV, Apple Music, and more.
