Lord of the Rings Returns to Theaters Next Week to Reveal an Iconic Moment

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

See the forging of the One Ring on the big screen

On Nov. 25, Prime Video will release the final two episodes of Lord of the Rings: Rings of Power's third season. The penultimate episode is already confirmed to depict one of the most important moments in Middle-earth's history, the forging of the One Ring. To give that moment the respect it deserves, the episode is getting a theatrical release.

The show's official X account announced this on Nov. 16, sharing a link where fans can find local theaters with screenings. As an added bonus, the cinematic event will actually be an early release of season 3's episode 7, as the screenings will take place on Nov. 23 and 24.

This isn't the first time Amazon has brought one of its original Prime Video series to the big screen, as The Boys' finale also received the treatment earlier this year. As for the actual conclusion to season 3, fans will have to be satisfied seeing that on their TV, phones, or tablets.

Since the start of season 3, audiences have been waiting for Sauron to forge the One Ring. The season, which began with a five-year time jump that serves as a soft reboot of the show, has heavily focused on the villain's attempts to finally create his all-powerful weapon. His failure to do so up to this point has largely been due to the lack of help from the Elven smith Celebrimbor, who forged the rings of power for the dwarves, men, and elves.

The One Ring is arguably the most important object in all of Middle-earth, as it features in Tolkien's novel The Hobbit as well as being the main McGuffin of the original Lord of the Rings trilogy. While we've caught glimpses of its forging in Peter Jackson's films, Rings of Power will be the first time we've seen the entire process in all its glory. If you don't mind the changes the show made to the source material, then this will surely be a moment worth seeing on the biggest screen you can find.
