Jennifer Lopez's Most Candid Quotes About Divorce From Ben Affleck

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Jennifer Lopez has spent the past two years publicly processing her second split from Ben Affleck — and she has not held back about what the experience taught her.

The singer and actress filed for divorce from Affleck in August 2024 after two years of marriage, and the split was finalized in 2025. In the months since, Lopez has opened up across multiple interviews and podcasts about heartbreak, self-reflection and her new “happy era.”

From calling the breakup “disappointing” to describing it as “the best thing that ever happened,” here is a look at everything Lopez has said about her divorce.

Lopez delivered some of her most pointed remarks yet during an appearance on the “Friends Keep Secrets” podcast hosted by Benny Blanco in November 2026.

“It was such a hard, disappointing last situation on every level,” she told Blanco. “On every level, it was so disappointing. I don’t ever want to delve into that.”

The “On the Floor” singer admitted she has historically been “a serial monogamist, one after another, trying to find the thing, hoping that this was it.” Following her divorce from Affleck, she said she sat in her heartbreak and did the internal work. “It changed me. I allowed it to change me.”

Reflecting on her advice for anyone navigating a split — Lopez was previously married to Ojani Noa, Cris Judd and Marc Anthony — she said divorces are “hard” and “disappointing,” but she tries to hold herself to a standard.

“My advice for divorce is my advice for everything, which is, always operate in a way that you’ll feel proud of when you look back,” Lopez said. “I can look back at these situations and be like, even as painful as some of them were — so bad, life-shifting pain and hurt — I can look back and be like, I loved completely. I acted in a way that I’m proud of as a human.”

She added, “That’s my advice for anything, because when you go through hard things like that, it’s easy to kind of be like, ‘F*** this.’ You’re in so much pain, you want to hurt somebody else, or somebody has hurt you so much that you’re like … but you can’t.”

On a June 2026 episode of the “Smartless” podcast, Lopez got candid about hitting a breaking point after the split.

“There comes a point where it’s all so puzzling and wrong. Where you have to sit there. After my last divorce, I just sat there,” Lopez said. “I canceled my tour and I sat there, and I was like, ‘You need f***ing figure yourself out.’”

Lopez told the hosts she had to resist the urge to blame Affleck for the marriage’s collapse. “[I asked], ‘What is going on with you?’ Forget about everybody else. There’s nobody to blame here except yourself in a certain way,” she said. “Not that people don’t behave in a way that’s not great. But, ‘What are you doing? What do you have going on?’ That was a turning point for me.”

The singer said recognizing “patterns” allowed her to revisit her younger self. “Looking at those patterns, I was able to go back to that little girl [I once was] and really look at her. That little girl with the ripped nightgown — I can see her right now. … Dirty feet, walking around barefoot all the f***ing time. And just kind of going like, ‘You did this. You did all of this. Give yourself a little fing something. A little love! Stop looking for love in other things and give it to yourself.’”

Lopez credited her sister Lynda Lopez with urging her to be her own “keeper.” She added, “These are all little pieces. I feel like the journey is little puzzle pieces that you fit in little by little as you sit there with the oat milk and go, ‘It’s me.’”

Lopez expanded on her year of self-reflection during an appearance on Good Morning America in March 2026.

“I took a year off,” she explained, adding that she had to “stop everything” following the split. “I canceled tours. I decided to just be home and sit in what had happened without running away from it through work, through another person through anything. Just sit.”

The Atlas star said she needed to be present for her twins, Max and Emme, whom she shares with Anthony. “I was just at a point where I was like, ‘What is going on with you?’ I couldn’t blame anybody else because I don’t think that’s where the lesson is. And so, I really needed to figure myself out.”

More than a year later, Lopez described herself as being in her “happy era.”

“I feel like for the first time in my life, I’m free. I’m on my own. It feels really good. I didn’t really know what that felt like since I was in my early 20s, and even before that I always had a boyfriend,” she said. “I’ve gotten to the point where I really trust myself and appreciate myself a little bit more.”

As for dating again? “God forbid,” Lopez quipped. “I don’t want to ruin anything. It’s so nice right now. I’m so happy. It’s the best.”

In a September 2025 interview with CBS Sunday Morning, Lopez credited Affleck’s production company, Artists Equity, with helping bring her film Kiss of the Spider Woman to the screen — and then opened up about the contrast between her set life and home life.

“It was hard not to think about stuff, but it was, like, the best and the worst of times. Every moment on set … I was so happy. And then … back home, it was not great,” Lopez shared. “But you get through it and, you know, honestly, I have to say, it was the best thing that ever happened to me because it changed me. It helped me grow in a way that I needed to grow … become more self-aware. I’m a different person now than I was last year.”

Lopez called the summer following the divorce “probably the best summer I’ve ever had,” adding, “I realized that joy is in living … and really embracing life and everything that it brings to you. The hard times are the lessons and you have to understand that.”

Lopez first broke her silence on the divorce in an October 2024 cover story for Interview magazine with comedian Nikki Glaser.

“I think my whole life I’ve just been trying to say I’m good enough, until where I am now, when I know. I’m giving myself credit. I’m telling that little girl that grew up in the Bronx, ‘You’ve done really good for yourself,’” Lopez told Glaser. She acknowledged that after her recent projects, “my whole f***ing world exploded.”

Rather than searching for another partner, Lopez said she felt energized by independence. “Yes, I’m not looking for anybody, because everything that I’ve done over the past 25, 30 years, being in these different challenging situations, what can I f***ing do when it’s just me flying on my own — What if I’m just free?”
