Eldritch Empathy is a Papers Please-esque roguelite about diagnosing cosmic horror creatures and trying not to lose your marbles as you do

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Oh I do love an eldritch horror creature, I do, especially one that at first glance may only be slightly different from what I'm used to seeing. Eldritch Empathy, then, seems like a grand ole game to satisfy my wanton need to experience such things. It's a puzzle roguelite that isn't exactly a Papers Please-like but certainly feels reminiscent of one, where you have to "analyse corrupted creatures, purify their emotions and learn how to communicate with an eldritch being."

You kick things off by embodying Eike, a "young woman who has strange dreams and sightings of twisted creatures in the woodlands neighbouring the sleepy Swiss village she lives in." She decides to investigate these mysterious circumstances herself, where she comes upon these corrupted critters in the woods. You need to thoroughly inspect them, using a book to determine what is going on with them and what they need exactly; it's akin to checking the passport rulebook in Papers Please, hence the comparison.

After figuring out what's going on with these creatures, you then "use and transform symbols to create paths through the creatures' insides. You may use care and precision to navigate to the correct corrupted emotion you have identified from your investigation and purify it. You may also use your tools to cause harm. Whatever you choose, your actions will have consequences." Ominous! You can also plan your runs ahead of time, heading down paths that may offer you some boons here and there, like the option to restore a bit of sanity (it's one of those Lovecraftian kind of horror games, as loathe as I am that we still have to reference that guy).

Also be wary of the fact that actions you take out in the woods can affect your home and community. It's a roguelite with consequences! Mwahahha! Looks pretty neat overall, and has some tasty pixel art attached to it too. No release date just yet, but I'd assume it'll arrive no earlier than next year. You can wishlist it right here.
