Curry Barker’s ‘Anything But Ghosts’ to Release in Premium HDR by Barco – Film News in Brief

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

“Obsession” director Curry Barker is returning with his second feature film, “Anything But Ghosts,” which will be released in premium HDR by Barco.

The film, which follows a group of fame-seeking aspiring “ghosthunters” navigating terrifyingly unfamiliar territory, premieres in theaters May 7 and features a cast including Violet McGraw, Chris Reinacher, Bryce Dallas Howard and Aaron Paul. Randy Newman to Receive Society of Composers & Lyricists Lifetime Achievement Award

Randy Newman, the composer and songwriter behind Disney classics including “Toy Story,” “Cars” and “Monsters, Inc.,” will be honored by the Society of Composers & Lyricists with the Lifetime Achievement Award on Dec. 9 at the 37th Annual SCL Holiday Dinner in Los Angeles.

Newman has received many honors over his decades-spanning career, including seven Grammys, three Emmys and two Academy Awards, as well as a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame. He was inducted into the Songwriters Hall of Fame in 2002 and the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2013.

In addition to Newman’s honor, the society will posthumously induct Marvin Hamlisch and Earle Hagen into its Hall of Fame at the event.

Hamlisch was the composer of more than 40 motion picture scores, including his Oscar-winning score and song for “The Way We Were” and “The Sting.” He has received Pulitzer, Emmy, Grammy, Oscar and Tony Awards.

Hagen’s credits included “The Dick Van Dyke Show,” “The Danny Thomas Show,” “That Girl,” “The Mod Squad,” “Planet of the Apes” and “Eight Is Enough,” among many others. The Emmy-winning and Oscar-nominated composer was inducted into the Television Academy Hall of Fame in 2011.

Other honorees include Jon Batiste, who will receive the Ambassador Award in New York on Dec. 1; Brent McCorkle, who will receive the Trailblazer Award in Nashville on Dec. 5; and Sen. Dick Durbin (D-IL), who will receive the Excellence in Advocacy Award in Chicago on Dec. 3.
