Intuitive Machines and Rocket Lab: A Comparison of Spiking Sales Versus Steady Revenue

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Intuitive Machines went flat for two years before tripling revenue, while Rocket Lab grew steadily -- but the gap is closing fast.

Intuitive Machines (LUNR -5.25%) primarily generates revenue by developing specialized aerospace products and delivering support services for robotic space exploration.

Between August and November, it secured tracking satellite contracts for the Space Development Agency and the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, and it completed the in-orbit commissioning of an SXM-11 spacecraft, while also booking a commercial order for two platform systems.

Rocket Lab (RKLB -3.79%) primarily earns revenue by providing orbital launch vehicle services and manufacturing advanced spacecraft components for global clients.

During the past three months, it completed its 99th mission for its Electron rocket, and finalized its largest commercial Electron launch contract to date, encompassing 20 launches. It also advanced its pending acquisition of Iridium Communications after stockholders approved the transaction and simultaneously adjusted the target launch timing for its developing Neutron vehicle.

Revenue shows investors the total money a business brings in before expenses. This metric reveals whether an organization is successfully attracting customers and growing its overall business volume over time.

The revenue trends for Intuitive Machines and Rocket Lab speak volumes about each's space-based businesses. Rocket Lab's steady sales growth over the past two years demonstrates its diversified revenue streams. While some of its income relies on government contracts, it has significant commercial launch sales.

Rocket Lab's success is evidenced by its consistent quarter-over-quarter top-line expansion since the start of 2025. This trend is expected to continue into the third quarter, with management forecasting revenue between $250 million and $265 million. The company's impending acquisition of Iridium Communications only adds to its aggressive revenue growth.

Intuitive Machines is heavily reliant on U.S. government contracts, leading to lumpy quarterly sales results. Its massive revenue jump in 2026 was driven by contract wins with agencies such as NASA, contributing to the company's record backlog of $1.8 billion at the end of the second quarter.

That said, Intuitive Machines' 2026 sales explosion was also helped by acquisitions, which should accelerate its commercial revenue growth. This will shift the company from a purely government-dependent lunar lander specialist into a diversified commercial space infrastructure provider.
