Sky Harbour Group Corporation (SKYH -2.75%) reported an expansion of its aviation infrastructure network through new ground leases and construction milestones across high-demand aviation markets. Management indicated that the company is expanding into third-party manufacturing and construction services to maximize the capacity utilization of its internal steel production facility. The company stated that its leasing strategy focuses on tier 1 airports where revenue per square foot potential is highest, while vertical integration of the supply chain serves to mitigate cyclical inflationary pressures. Management reported that the company maintains a fixed-rate debt structure to insulate the business from recent volatility in the interest rate environment.
