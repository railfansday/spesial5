Why Intel Stock Fell Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Intel says it has landed its first outside customer for 18A-P, but it's unclear how much that will matter financially.

By Johnny Rice – Nov 24, 2026 at 5:56PM EST

Intel (INTC -8.15%) stock finished down 8.1% on Tuesday, Nov. 24, as investors retreated from the same chip stocks they couldn't get enough of a day earlier.

The Nasdaq Composite sank 1.5%, while the S&P 500 fell 0.5%.

Nvidia (NVDA -5.86%)'s decision to bring back CEO Jensen Huang set off a huge rally across chip stocks on Monday as investors reacted to the stunning reversal just a week after the board told investors it wasn't an option. The Nasdaq had its best day since April of 2025.

But that rally reversed sharply today as investors digested what his return will mean in the long term and how it will affect chip rivals -- like Intel.

Just last week, Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan said the company had landed its first outside customer for 18A-P, an advanced manufacturing process important to the company's turnaround efforts. It's a positive step, but details were light, so it's unclear how much financial impact it will actually have.

Intel's foundry business posted a $1.85 billion operating loss last quarter, with the company's finance chief indicating the company probably won't generate positive free cash flow -- the cash left over after paying for investments like new factories -- next year as it keeps investing in manufacturing.

Long term, I think that will pay off. I'm just not jumping in any time soon given the current market.
