Instagram adds Stories editing

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The platform rolled out various updates for the format, including the much-requested option to make changes to Stories after posting.

Instagram has rolled out a range of updates for Stories, including a much-requested editing option, which will enable users to update their posts after publishing.

As shown in this image, Instagram users will now be able to edit published Stories for up to 15 minutes after posting. Which isn't a huge window of time, but it should be enough to catch and correct any typos or mistakes.

It’s a significant update, which will be particularly beneficial for social media managers, enabling them to revise their content instead of having to start over again.

Instagram has also made it easier to select and post multiple images in Stories and added more display options.

It's also launched an updated sticker selector with new stickers, as well as expanded color gradient options for photo backgrounds in Story frames.

The updates will provide more ways for users to share their experiences in Stories during the upcoming holiday season, while as noted, they'll also help marketers use Stories to promote their holiday offers.

On another front, Instagram added the capacity to search saved items in the iOS version of the app.

As shown in these images, posted by app researcher Radu Oncescu, Instagram users can now search within their saved items. It’s a minor functional addition, but it could be a big help in finding previously saved posts.
