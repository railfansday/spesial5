Target Clearance Drops in Steps – Hold Out for 70% Off Unless It’s Selling Fast

Read the number in the top-right corner of a Target clearance sticker. It’s the percentage off, and most clearance drops from 30% to 50% to 70%. Wait for 70% when the shelf is full, and buy now when only a few are left in the size or color you want.

A $40 lamp tagged 30% off costs $28 today and $12 at 70% off, so waiting saves $16 if it’s still on the shelf. Anyone else can buy it in the meantime.

Most items go on clearance at 30% off, and everyday food at 15%. Household basics tend to sell out before they reach 70%. A date near the bottom of the sticker tells you when the current price took effect.

Seventy percent is the last step for most clearance, and Target pulls what’s left from the shelves soon after. Holiday decorations and gifts drop faster and can go on to 90%. Each store sets its own timing, so the dates on your store’s stickers tell you more than any day-by-department schedule posted online.

Scan the barcode in the Target app before you decide. Stores can take days to put up new stickers, so an item can ring up at a deeper markdown than its sticker shows.

The deeper markdown is worth waiting for on anything the store has plenty of. When the shelf is down to one or two in your size or color, buy at 30% or 50%, since waiting saves nothing on an item that’s gone. Target’s price match excludes clearance, so you can’t get the difference back after a later markdown.

The day after Christmas, holiday lights at 50% off usually reach 70% within the week and 90% soon after, while a popular toy at 30% off can sell out before its next markdown. Hold off on the lights, and buy the toy now.

Regular clearance typically drops again about two weeks after the date on its sticker, so plan your next trip for that week.
