Macdonald sacked by Eastbourne Town

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Macdonald has paid the price for a winless run that has seen Town slip to second from bottom of the division in their third season back at step four after promotion from the SCFL in 2024.

But his ousting has drawn an angry reaction from many on social media, with some saying it is a ‘disgraceful’ decision.

A statement issued by the club a couple of hours after the first reports on X that the decision had been made said: “Eastbourne Town F.C have today parted company with First Team Manager Jude MacDonald and his coaching staff.

"The Committee would like to place on record their sincere thanks to Jude and his management team for their hard work over the last 4½ years, where, following their arrival, they quickly reshaped the remaining playing squad, and with some exciting new arrivals, just two years later launched an amazing mid-season recovery to storm up the SCFL Premier Division table to finish runners-up and then win the league play-off final.

“They then successfully kept the club in the Isthmian FL for successive seasons when largely up against many teams with much greater budgets and resources, a feat which we believe must be seen as a remarkable achievement in what is known by many to be a very tough division.

"The club wish them all the very best for the future and thank them once again for their service during their time at The Saffrons. No further statements will be made at this time.”

Earlier in the day Town held Whitstable – third in the league – to a 0-0 draw at The Saffrons , a week after a 1-1 draw with Sheppey, who are also in the top half.

At the end of the Whitstable game, the club’s X account said: “A brilliant performance from The Town. A hard earned point and it could have been three after an excellent second half.”

The news comes just a day after the club announced the return to the club of James Stone and James Waters.
