Aaron Hickey injury latest after Scotland blow as Brentford boss provides update

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Aaron Hickey has been ruled out for a “number of weeks” after suffering a hamstring tear on Scotland duty.

The Brentford full-back was substituted in the 37th minute of the 4-0 defeat to Switzerland in the Nations League on Monday.

In a worrying sight given his injury history, the 24-year-old clutched his left hamstring after heading the ball back to goalkeeper Angus Gunn and immediately signaled to the bench that he would need replaced.

Hickey was sidelined for nearly two years by a serious hamstring injury in October 2023 that required two surgeries. He also missed part of last season with a fresh hamstring issue but recovered in time to make Scotland's 2026 World Cup squad.

He won his 27th Scotland cap in St Gallen but the former Hearts and Bologna man now faces another period of recovery.

“They won’t be available for a number of weeks,” said Brentford coach Keith Andrews as he addressed the injuries picked up by Hickey and Republic of Ireland defender Nathan Collins over the international break.

“I was actually watching Aaron’s game as it happened, and I am just so bitterly disappointed for him. He has had such a painful journey on that front with injuries.

“Similarly with Nathan, he has obviously had a period out with his calf. He has done really well to get back ahead of schedule and played a couple of games.

“These international games came at a really good time for him to get good game time and obviously represent his country, and unfortunately he has hurt his knee.”
