Middlesbrough FC’s Will Lankshear stance outlined amid Arsenal, Tottenham Hotspur & Manchester United ‘interest’

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Middlesbrough are reportedly dismissive of the suggestion Will Lankshear could be sold in the winter transfer window.

The 21-year-old was a marquee signing from Tottenham Hotspur in the summer transfer window and has taken to life at the Riverside like a duck to water.

He has delivered a remarkable 15-goal haul over the course of his first 16 appearances, notching on 13 occasions in the Championship.

His scintillating form has fuelled talk of interest from the Premier League and a raft of clubs have been credited with interest.

According to TEAMtalk, Arsenal, Manchester United and his former club Tottenham are among those who have monitored the England youth international’s progress.

Newcastle United and Sunderland are also said to have taken a look, although the report has suggested Middlesbrough are not looking to lose their talismanic frontman.

There are not thought to have been any approaches and it appears unlikely one would be received warmly by Boro.

Lankshear cost the club an initial £10m that could rise to £20m, reports indicated, but Middlesbrough could pocket considerably more than that in years to come.

There is also the possibility of Lankshear not having to depart North Yorkshire for a taste of top-flight football. Kim Hellberg’s side are firmly among the automatic promotion contenders, sitting second in the table behind Birmingham City.

Last week, former Premier League scout Mick Brown told Football Insider: “Will Lankshear is obviously going to have a lot of Premier League clubs looking at him. He's proving already he's a top-class striker, it's not like he's just come out of nowehere, he's somebody these clubs have been aware of even when he was at Tottenham.

“But now he's showing he can do it at a high level, there's even been talk of an England call-up, so maybe he is ready for the Premier League.”
