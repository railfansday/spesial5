Barnsley FC's Arley Kay shows age is just a number, says Cieran Slicker

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Oakwell club's strategy revolves around a young squad of players who can be developed and sold for profit.

At times their inexperience has told, but 18-year-old defender Kay has been one of the success stories this season.

Kay went into Friday's South Yorkshire derby against Sheffield Wednesday a League One ever-present in his debut campaign.

"Massive credit to him," said Slicker, on loan from Ipswich Town.

"He's stepped up from the academy and speaking to him about his story when I first came here, I didn't know who he was. Probably a lot of the other boys didn't.

"He came to train with us in pre-season. I think one thing he did really well – and it's quite tough – is really hold his weight and he's really aggressive in challenges.

"That's something that I've seen and it's helped him a lot. He's been great for us.

"Him being able to play a variety of positions across the backline is really important as well.

"It's probably our responsibility as a team and the club to keep them on the same track because a lot's been made of the young age of the squad."

Slicker is only 24 himself, and thinks too much is made of birth certificates.

"People can speak about age all they want but you look at some of the best players in the world right now," he countered. You don't question Lamine Yamal's age (19).

"You don't question all the other players that are young and playing."
