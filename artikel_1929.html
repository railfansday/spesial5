Premier League table forecasted with fresh Leeds United, Hull City, Manchester United & Sunderland verdicts

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Opta’s latest Premier League table prediction with Leeds United, Hull City, Manchester United and Sunderland verdicts

It was yet another eventful weekend of Premier League action - the type that makes the international break considerably less enticing.

Leeds United did battle with Tottenham Hotspur and emerged as 1-0 victors, picking up a league win for the first time since September. Hull City defended with grit away at Arsenal, but it was not enough to prevent a 1-0 defeat at the Emirates Stadium.

Away from Yorkshire, Manchester United and Aston Villa played out an enthralling 4-4 draw at Old Trafford and Nottingham Forest scored an upset win over Manchester City.

Everton claimed a 3-1 victory over Coventry City, Fulham shared the spoils with Newcastle United and Ipswich Town were beaten at home by AFC Bournemouth. Sunderland edged past Chelsea and Liverpool left Selhurst Park with three points in the bag after a 3-1 win.

Following the latest twists and turns, data experts Opta have deployed their supercomputer to predict how the Premier League table will look at the end of the season.
