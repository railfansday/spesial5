‘Back in Leeds’ - Leeds United’s forgotten man Mateo Joseph ‘focused on comeback’ as he shares update

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Leeds United forward Mateo Joseph has declared he is “focused on the comeback” as he plots a return to the pitch.

The 23-year-old succumbed to an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury in March, while out on loan at Mallorca in La Liga.

He had requested a move away from Elland Road in the previous summer and scored two goals across 30 outings in Spain before being sidelined.

Joseph is now back on British shores, rehabilitating with the help of specialists at Leeds.

Posting on Instagram, the former Spain and England youth international said: “Back in Leeds, focused on the comeback.

“Every day brings progress, every step takes me closer, things are looking up and we’re moving in the right direction. One step at a time.”

It is hard to envisage Joseph carving out a future at Leeds given the progress made by the club since his loan departure.

Whites boss Daniel Farke does not boast a wealth of forward options, yet Joseph would arguably find it tough to leapfrog Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Lukas Nmecha in the pecking order.

Speaking to The Yorkshire Post in the summer, Leeds icon Jermaine Beckford said: “Mateo has got talent. He's got technical ability. He's got good pace about him as well. We've seen when he's been with the under-21s how good he is at finishing.

“He's had a couple of decent goals as well for the first team, I remember the Chelsea game at Stamford Bridge. He's got it, he just needs to knuckle down, get rid of the external noise, focus on working his socks off, and I think he could [force his way back in].

“Do I think Daniel Farke and the hierarchy want to keep him knowing that he could possibly upset the apple cart? I'm not entirely sure. But it's for Mateo to come back and show that this is a new and improved version of himself. He's got the ability.”
