GTA 6 New Zealand release time: The internet goes completely Kiwi

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Grand Theft Auto VI is just hours away from being released after a 13-year wait.

The game is set to unlock at midnight local time on Nov. 19 for players, which means New Zealand gamers will be the first in the world to play GTA 6. And while the wait is only a few hours longer than the rest of the world's, many are attempting to change their consoles' local settings to New Zealand in hopes of starting the game nearly a day early.

Dubbed the "New Zealand trick," the sudden influx of new Kiwis has not been lost on the internet.

Regardless of which country you play from, GTA 6 is just hours away.
