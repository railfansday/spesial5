2026-27 NBA Power Rankings Week 5: Spurs move into top spot, focus on Number to Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Welcome to the latest edition of the weekly NBC Sports NBA Power Rankings, where teams that are on 12-game win streaks and have Victor Wembanyama get to move to the front of the line. This week, we focus on numbers to know for each team.

Record: 12-2Ranking last week: No. 2Games this week: Detroit (on NBC), LA Clippers, New Orleans

Number to Know: San Antonio has a net rating of +12.6, just off Oklahoma City's historic rating (+12.7) in 2024-25 season, when the Thunder won 68 games and the NBA title. The Spurs are looking to become the first team to lose in the NBA Finals and then return to that big stage since the Cavaliers in 2018 — and they are off to a fast start. San Antonio has a top-three offensive and defensive rating. A lot of that has been because of Victor Wembanyama's impressive start to the season, but the guard lineups — with De'Aaron Fox, Dylan Harper, Stephon Castle and Devin Vassell, in whatever combination — are thriving as well. Check them out for yourselves Tuesday night on Coast 2 Coast on NBC and Peacock, starting at 8 ET.

Record: 11-2Ranking last week: No. 4Games this week: Orlando, at Denver, at Sacramento, at Golden State

Number to Know: The Boston Celtics are forcing 16.7 turnovers a game. Boston, under Joe Mazzulla, wasn't a team that forced many turnovers — until this season. The Celtics are forcing 4.3 more turnovers a game this season than a season ago as Mazzulla has adjusted his defensive philosophy (based on personnel) and it's working. Boston's win over Philadelphia last week was a reminder that if Paul George is healthy come April, the Celtics will be a very tough out.

Record: 12-2Ranking last week: No. 3Games this week: Charlotte, Utah, Sacramento

Number to Know: The Lakers have a -11.6 net rating when Luka Doncic is off the court. Luka Doncic is playing at an MVP level to start the season and is must-watch television — including Tuesday Night on NBC and Peacock this week when the Lakers host the Hornets. But the non-Luka minutes remain a concern, and they still have a -6.4 net rating when Doncic is off the court and Reaves is on. Doncic's incredible play and the Lakers' offense will make you forget all this. For now.

Record: 9-4Ranking last week: No. 6Games this week: Portland, Portland, New York, Memphis

Number to Know: Minnesota has the No. 1-ranked defense in the NBA, with a 104.5 defensive rating. After the Timberwolves front office reshaped the roster this summer, the expectation was that the defense would suffer, especially with the addition of LaMelo Ball. Not so fast, my friend — Ball is putting in the effort on defense, and Chris Finch has the right system around him with bigs protecting the paint. Minnesota is doing this without Anthony Edwards, a very strong perimeter defender in his own right. Rudy Gobert and Jaden McDaniels will get the headlines, but this has been a team effort.

Record: 11-3Ranking last week: No. 8Games this week: at Miami, at Indiana, at Denver

Number to Know: Jabari Smith Jr. is shooting 45% from the field, 38.6% from 3, and 85.7% from the free-throw line. He has been one of the unsung stars of the Rockets, hitting his open shots, and before the last two games I would have said Smith is in the running for a 50/40/90 season. Then he shot 5-of-24 over the past two games (including 2-of-12 from 3), and his season stats fell off. Still, he has benefited greatly from the improved spacing in Houston and has thrived this season. He is one reason the Rockets look like a threat.

Record: 11-3Ranking last week: No. 1Games this week: at Brooklyn, at Orlando, Milwaukee

Number to Know: Philadelphia is 9-0 in clutch games with a +43.6 net rating in those minutes. That clutch play is why the 76ers have the record they do and are ranked so highly. Philadelphia's +2 net rating for the season suggests a team that should be more like 7-6, but with LeBron James leading the way, this team wins its close games. If you're looking for a reason for the lower net rating, it's the pedestrian offense (18th in the league), but it was always going to take time to blend all those on-ball stars into a whole.

Record: 8-4Ranking last week: No. 7Games this week: at San Antonio, at New Orleans, Toronto, at Atlanta

Number to Know: Detroit has a -11.7 net rating in clutch minutes. There is some serious small sample size at play here as they have played in just five clutch games (within 5 points in the final five minutes), and the numbers look a lot better after Cade Cunningham's epic 52-point night in the double overtime win this week against the Wizards. However, overall, this low number speaks to the concern from last year's playoff run, when defenses loaded up on Cunningham and the team didn't have other good options. What helps is that their one option is elite.

Record: 9-3Ranking last week: No. 11Games this week: at Minnesota, at Minnesota, at Milwaukee, at New York

Number to Know: Portland has the best clutch net rating in the league at +55.3. This is some small sample size theater, the Trail Blazers have played in just five clutch games (inside 5 points in the final five minutes), but it doesn't feel like a coincidence that Damian Lillard returns and the Trail Blazers are winning close games. "Obviously, the closing of games, that's what this team struggled with last year," Coach Micha Nori said this week. "But that's where the effect of Dame coming back, the effect of calming. Deni (Avdija) is getting better in those situations, and the calming of Jrue (Holiday) and obviously Toumani's (Camara) defense."

Record: 7-4Ranking last week: No. 15Games this week: Phoenix, Boston, Houston, at Chicago

Number to Know: Aaron Gordon is shooting under 40% (39.8%) from the field and 27.3% on 3-pointers this season. Is this just a slow start to a season and nothing to worry about, or are these signs of Father Time and a lot of leg injuries catching up with him? Shot diet may have a lot to do with this, Gordon is taking a career-high 44.9% of his shot attempts from 3-point range and is averaging less than one dunk per game (the lowest in his career). Of course, this is the NBA, so some struggles leads to trade speculation, but Gordon is a Jokic favorite, so it would take a real haul in return for that to happen.

Record: 9-4Ranking last week: No. 14Games this week: at Washington, at Cleveland, Golden State, at Minnesota

Number to Know: The Grizzlies have a top-eight offense and defense in the league right now. Being in the top 10 in both is traditionally a sign of a contender (the Spurs, Celtics, Rockets and Timberwolves are the other teams doing it so far). However, with the Grizzlies the number feels inflated thanks to the hot start (6-0) and the easiest schedule in the league so far. Still, Memphis is beating the teams in front of them, which is all they can do.

Record: 7-6Ranking last week: No. 10Games this week: at Toronto, at Minnesota, Portland, Indiana

Number to Know: Josh Hart has gone scoreless in consecutive games, shooting 0-of-9 from the field in nearly 50 minutes of action. One of the stars of the Knicks' title run is off to a rough opening to this season, shooting 36.2% overall and 29.5% from 3. He's Josh Hart, so he gets a lot of rope, but is there cause for concern? Coach Mike Brown doesn't think so: "Josh is a pro. And he's been in this league long enough to understand what he needs to do to continue to get himself back on track because, at the end of the day, we need him. And he's a big part of what we do on both ends of the floor. And everybody knows that. And it's only a matter of time before he works his way out of it."

Record: 8-5Ranking last week: No. 5Games this week: Atlanta, New Orleans, at Miami

Number to Know: Shai Gilgeous-Alexander has shot 4-of-15 (26.7%) in the clutch this season. SGA is the reigning Clutch Player of the Year but this season he has struggled in the big moments. Part of that is the small sample size (just four games, the Thunder are 2-2 in those), and part of it is that Jalen Williams's injury (among others) allows defenses to focus more on SGA in those minutes. That was also the case for much of last season and he overcame it, nobody is actually worried about him, but it's a slow start.

Record: 8-5Ranking last week: No. 9Games this week: at New Orleans, at Dallas, at Memphis, Boston

Number to Know: The Warriors have a -0.7 net rating when Stephen Curry is off the court. Non-Curry minutes have long been an issue for the Warriors, but while he was healthy this season, Yaxel Landeborg and others stepped up and took over the offense while Curry rested. However, the Warriors have a -21.9 net rating in the past two games, the one where Curry got injured and the one where he did not suit up (abdominal injury that could have him out a month). It's going to be a rough few weeks in the Bay Area.

Record: 7-6Ranking last week: No. 19Games this week: Houston, Milwaukee, Oklahoma City

Number to Know: Miami has a -5.9 net rating in the third quarter this season. It was one of the things that stood out early in the season as the Heat stumbled out of the gate — they got blown out in the third quarter. That started to change recently, particularly when they put up 35 points on the Pistons in the third, in what the Heat feel like was a statement win last week. We'll see if this is a trend this week, with games against the Rockets and Thunder testing the Heat.

Record: 8-6Ranking last week: No. 16Games this week: at Oklahoma City, Charlotte, Detroit

Number to Know: Rookie Kingston Flemmings is finding his footing as a starter, averaging 8.6 points and 3.2 assists per game while shooting 56.3% in that role. Quin Snyder moved Flemmings into the starting lineup and he responded by scoring more, his usage rate went down but his efficiency went up, and the Hawks went 3-2 (he did not play Sunday vs. Milwaukee due to a lumbar strain). Snyder needs to find a way to keep either Fleming or Jalen Johnson on the court for most of the game.

Record: 7-7Ranking last week: No. 12Games this week: Chicago, Memphis, Indiana

Number to Know: James Harden is shooting just 39% this season. There is no single factor behind the Cavaliers' malaise at the start of the season, they have been pedestrian on offense and defense. However, James Harden has to play better — and he did against the Knicks, dropping 32 points and helping secure the win. Then two nights later, he was 5-of-13 shooting in a loss to the Heat.

Record: 7-7Ranking last week: No. 17Games this week: Golden State, LA Clippers, at Utah

Number to Know: Cooper Flagg has more 40+ point games than any teenager in NBA history, and this past week passed Kobe Bryant for second-most total points scored by a teenager. It's hard to overstate just how good Flagg has been in his second season, he has looked every bit an All-NBA player — and he doesn't turn 20 for a month. There are things to watch, such as Flagg shooting 26.8% on 3-pointers, but that should come around (he shot well in high school and college, his form is good, it just hasn't translated to the NBA yet), but overall this season could not have started better for the Mavericks' young star.

Record: 5-7Ranking last week: No. 22Games this week: at Denver, at Sacramento, at Utah

Number to Know: Phoenix is taking 19.6% of its shots at the rim, which ranks 25th in the league. Phoenix wasn't getting to the rim last season, either, taking 19.8% of its shots there (27th in the league) but the offseason additions of Jalen Green and Miles Bridges were supposed to change that. Instead, Suns fans are heading to the trade machines to come up with ways to get those two out of town. The Suns have 6-of-7 on the road starting Monday.

Record: 5-7Ranking last week: No. 23Games this week: at Boston, Philadelphia, at Brooklyn, at Washington

Stat of Note: Orlando's 109.7 offensive rating is 4.5 points per 100 possessions worse than it was a season ago. Chalk some of that up to injuries — Paolo Banchero remains out, Jalen Suggs just returned last week, Anthony Black missed time — but even so, this team still just does not seem to click on offense, and so far in this young season new coach Sean Sweeney hasn't been able to change that. Orlando's offense — and everything — looked better racking up wins against soft competition last week, that changes this week.

Record: 5-8Ranking last week: No. 13Games this week: New York, Washington, at Detroit

Number to Know: Toronto has the best fourth quarter net rating in the league at +16.6. Watch a lot of Raptors games and the pattern is obvious. They usually get an early lead, play the first half pretty even, struggle in the third quarter then try to come back in the fourth. That doesn't get a team consistent wins in the NBA. At least Scottie Barnes is healthy and back in the rotation after missing four games.

Record: 5-9Ranking last week: No. 20Games this week: Houston, at Cleveland, at New York

Number to Know: Indiana has a -4 net rating when Tyrese Haliburton is not on the court. It feels a little bit lazy to go to this stat, especially after a week when the Pacers picked up a quality NBA Cup win against the Knicks, but this remains the statistic that defines the Pacers' season. With Haliburton on the court, this is a dangerous team, without him they are inconsistent.

Record: 3-9Ranking last week: No. 26Games this week: Philadelphia, Chicago, Orlando

Number to Know: Jordi Fernandez is trying a super-big starting lineup — every player is 6'8" and up — and bringing his smaller young guards off the bench, to mixed success. For the past two games, the Nets starting five has been Egor Demin, Michael Porter Jr., Julius Randle, Danny Wolf and Day'Ron Sharpe, and in very limited minutes, it has a -2.7 net rating, and the team is 1-1. At this point, Fernandez should be throwing everything against the wall to see what sticks.

Record: 4-8Ranking last week: No. 25Games this week: Utah, at San Antonio, at Dallas

Number to Know: The Clippers are hitting 39.1% of their 3-pointers this season, the third-highest percentage in the league. LA doesn't take an extraordinary number of 3s (36.8 per game, 18th in the league), and it doesn't seem totally sustainable with Derrick Jones Jr. shooting 52.2% this season and Kris Dunn at 40%, but it's working for the Clippers so far.

Record: 4-10Ranking last week: No. 18Games this week: Memphis, at Toronto, at Chicago, Orlando

Number to Know: AJ Dybantsa is averaging 15.1 points and 6.2 rebounds per game. Wizards ownership/management wanted to win now and went out and got Trae Young (and gave him an oversized extension) and Anthony Davis, but Dybantsa is showing he is clearly the future. The Wizards need to focus on him and his timeline, and just give him the rock more.

Record: 4-9Ranking last week: No. 24Games this week: at Los Angeles Lakers, at Atlanta

Number to Know: The Charlotte Hornets have the worst offense in the NBA this season with a 102.5 offensive rating. Or, look at it this way: Charlotte is the only team in the league not averaging 100 points per game. Injuries have a lot to do with this, with Kon Knueppel missing time and Brandon Miller having just returned from missing a couple of games. That makes the second game on NBC's Coast to Coast Tuesday this week a real contrast, going up against the Lakers and the best offense in the NBA so far. Check the game out for yourself at 11 p.m. Eastern on NBC or Peacock.

Record: 3-9Ranking last week: No. 30Games this week: at Cleveland, at Brooklyn, Washington, Denver

Number to Know: Caleb Wilson is averaging 18.9 points and 6.2 rebounds per game. Which is really just a stat I'm using to say how much it sucks that a hand fracture is keeping him out for 4- 6 weeks. It's not that the Bulls are better or worse with him on the court — they have been equally bad whether he was playing or not — but it sucks for Bulls fans and is one less reason to tune in and watch this team.

Record: 4-10Ranking last week: No. 21Games this week: Golden State, Detroit, at Oklahoma City, at San Antonio

Number to Know: Jeremiah Fears is averaging 15.3 points, 5.2 assists and 4.5 rebounds a game this season. This is worth noting because he is clearly the point guard of the future, especially since Dejounte Murray wants to be traded, his agent (Rich Paul of Klutch Sports) told the team last week. Fears has work to do on his 3-point shot and efficiency, but toss the man the keys and let him drive the car and learn.

Record: 1-11Ranking last week: No. 28Games this week: Phoenix, at Los Angeles Lakers, Boston

Number to Know: Domantas Sabonis is averaging 14.2 points per game to start the season. As always with Sabonis, look at just the counting stats and it's not terrible — a double-double with 13.2 rebounds a game, shooting 63.8% — but watch him on the court while Darius Acuff Jr. takes over and it seems his mind is on Grand Theft Auto 6 (or somewhere else). Yes, the Kings are willing to trade Sabonis, but his contract means that is more likely a move next summer.

Record: 1-12Ranking last week: No. 27Games this week: at LA Clippers, at Los Angeles Lakers, Phoenix, Dallas

Number to Know: The Utah Jazz are already 5.5 games back of the final play-in spot in the West. The Jazz have lost 12 in a row, but give them credit for playing hard, as they did to push the Spurs (a little) on Sunday. Utah has been the most disappointing team in the NBA this season. This was the year they were going to turn things around, be competitive, make the playoffs, and instead fit and injury issues have derailed this team. It's just hard to find the positives right now.

Record: 1-13Ranking last week: No. 29Games this week: at Miami, Portland, at Philadelphia

Number to Know: The Bucks are bottom six in the league in three of the offensive four factors (that are the baseline of analytics): eFG% (50.9), free throw rate (23.8), and turnover percentage (16.7). At least they are pretty good on the offensive glass. It's hard to come up with positives, but Kasparas Jakucionis dropped 25 on the Hawks Sunday, he's been inconsistent this season but maybe he deserves more of a chance. What else is Taylor Jenkins going to do?
