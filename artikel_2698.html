Manchester City goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma was left angered by chants aimed at his family during his side's 2-2 Champions League draw with Napoli on Tuesday night.

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Manchester City's most divisive figure: Gianluigi Donnarumma?

Tuesday night's meeting with Napoli in the UEFA Champions League carried extra meaning for Manchester City's first-choice goalkeeper and Italy's international team captain, Gianluigi Donnarumma.

The Manchester City shot-stopper grew up just a stone's throw from the city of Naples, and coming up against a club with such close ties to his roots was sure to be on his mind in the build-up to the match.

As it turned out, the occasion soured for reasons more personal than the disappointing 2-2 scoreline, and when the time came to speak with the media, it was clear Donnarumma had been affected by events in the stands as much as anything that had unfolded on the pitch.

According to Corriere dello Sport, as relayed by Football Italia, Napoli's travelling supporters repeatedly aimed insulting chants at Gianluigi Donnarumma throughout the game, including offensive terms directed at the Italian's mother.

The abuse clearly got under the 27-year-old's skin and he made his feelings known once the full-time whistle had sounded. When asked about the chants by Italian reporters in the mixed zone at the Etihad Stadium, he offered a brief but pointed response.

"I'm angry because they insulted me," he said.

It was a footnote to a contest Manchester City had twice fought back to salvage a point from, with Ayyoub Bouaddi and Rayan Cherki cancelling out goals from Rasmus Højlund and Noa Lang.

The targeting of his family will inevitably have cut deep given his personal ties to the area with Gianluigi Donnarumma born in Castellammare di Stabia - just along the coast from Naples - and having spoken before about having Napoli fans in his family.

In fact, when Manchester City hosted the same opponents last season on his first UEFA Champions League start since arriving from Paris Saint-Germain, Donnarumma chose against bringing his mother to the Etihad Stadium because of the conflict of interest.

The way the latest meeting descended into abuse aimed at her surely justifies his decision to keep his mother away last time, while the incident itself comes amid an already testing spell for a goalkeeper whose form has been picked over in great detail in recent weeks.

The Italian will have to brush himself off before one of the biggest games of his Manchester City career so far when Enzo Maresca's side travel to face Arsenal at the Emirates Stadium.

Defeat at the hands of the Gunners on Sunday afternoon would leave Gianluigi Donnarumma and his City teammates nine points off the Premier League pace before December begins.
