Cardinals sign Jared Harrison-Hunte, Wydett Williams to 53-man roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Cardinals announced on Tuesday that they've made a series of roster moves.

Arizona has signed defensive lineman Jared Harrison-Hunte and safety Wydett Williams Jr. to the 53-man roster off of the club's practice squad.

Harrison-Hunte has appeared in two games for the Cardinals this season, recording four total tackles. Williams has appeared in five games with one start for Arizona, tallying seven tackles with one pass defensed.

Linebacker Jordan Burch and safety Andrew Wingard have been placed on injured reserve.

Offensive lineman Albert Reese has been released from the 53-man roster. He is subject to waivers.

Receiver Ronnie Bell and safety Jerrick Reed have been signed to the practice squad, while receiver James Proche has been released from the practice squad.
