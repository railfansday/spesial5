Emmett Johnson TD gives Chiefs 10-7 lead early in third quarter

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Chiefs got a little something going at the end of the first half.

But they have taken off to start the second.

Emmett Johnson punched in a 1-yard touchdown to give Kansas City a 10-7 lead over Buffalo with 11:28 on the clock in the third quarter.

The score capped a seven-play, 56-yard drive.

Returner Jalen Royals gave the Chiefs a great start with a 37-yard kick return to the Kansas City 44. Then Johnson started the possession with runs of 13 and 12 yards to put the Chiefs in Buffalo territory.

A 13-yard pass to Josh Downs put Kansas City at Buffalo’s 1-yard line. Johnson put it in from there.

In his third game starting for the injured Kenneth Walker, Johnson now has 53 yards on 11 carries with a touchdown.
