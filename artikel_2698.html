Texans rule David Montgomery out for Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Texans running back David Montgomery will not make his return to the lineup in Sunday’s game against the Browns.

Montgomery was listed as questionable to play on Friday’s injury report, but the Texans announced on Saturday that he will not travel with the team and has been ruled out. This will be the fourth straight game that he has missed with a groin injury.

The Texans also announced that cornerback Jaylin Smith (hamstring) will not be activated from injured reserve.

Linebacker Jake Hummel has been activated from injured reserve. He’s been out since Week 1 with a groin injury.

The Texans elevated linebacker Chad Muma and running back Dare Ogunbowale from the practice squad to round out the day’s moves.
