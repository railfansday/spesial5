Kevin Durant has perfect reaction to breaking Shaquille O’Neal’s record

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Kevin Durant has continued to strengthen his place among the NBA's greatest scorers, building an impressive career through consistency and offensive versatility. Since entering the league in 2007, the veteran forward has established himself as one of basketball's most reliable shot-makers.

Now in his 20th NBA season, the Houston Rockets star has added another historic achievement to his resume. Durant's latest milestone puts him ahead of another legendary name in the league's record books, further highlighting his remarkable scoring longevity.

Following Houston's victory, Durant was asked about moving ahead of O'Neal on the NBA's all-time field goals made list. He used the moment to acknowledge the greatness of the former Lakers center.

"Yeah, Shaq, yeah. It's always amazing being in the same category of greatness. Shaq is somebody who transcends the sport itself. To accomplish some of the things that he accomplished in this game is amazing, so I gotta keep moving," Durant said.

Durant also made it clear that he remains focused on what lies ahead.

Durant reached the milestone during the Rockets' 115-108 victory over the Golden State Warriors on Wednesday. He finished with eight made field goals on 17 attempts, taking his career total to 11,336 and moving past O'Neal's 11,330.

The 38-year-old recorded 20 points, four rebounds and five assists in 34 minutes. He shot 47.1% from the field and made one of his four attempts from beyond the arc.

Durant now has Kobe Bryant's 11,719 career field goals in sight as the next major mark above him.
