Drew Lock has another limited practice but does not have an injury designation

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Drew Lock was limited in practice Friday, his sixth consecutive practice without team drills. Like last week, though, Lock will play through oblique and elbow injuries.

Lock does not have an injury designation for Sunday’s game against the Raiders.

“He had a similar cadence and workload in terms of last week,” Seahawks coach Mike Macdonald said Friday, via John Boyle of the team website. “It’s good to have done it and gotten through the week well. I know he’s confident and optimistic, so we’re in good shape.”

The Seahawks ruled out starter Sam Darnold (ribs/quad) for another week, but he would return after the Week 11 bye.

Offensive guard Quenton Nelson (knee), offensive tackle Charles Cross (ankle) and defensive tackle Leonard Williams (stinger) also won’t play.

The Seahawks are getting back outside linebacker Uchenna Nwosu, receiver Rashid Shaheed and nose tackle Brandon Pili. All three were full participants Friday and do not have an injury designation.
