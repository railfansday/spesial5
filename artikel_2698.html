Blake Corum supplants Kyren Williams as the Rams' top tailback

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It’s been slowly building throughout the season. On Wednesday night, it became abundantly clear.

Blake Corum has become the Rams’ top tailback.

On his 26th birthday, Corum had a career night. Thirty-two carries, 184 yards, and a touchdown. Throw in 21 receiving yards, and he had 205 yards from scrimmage.

“Happy birthday, man,” coach Sean McVay told reporters after the game. “He was on the set of Netflix so we didn’t even get to enjoy it with him. So, I know that kind of pissed me off, honestly.”

Corum’s gains have come at the expense of Kyren Williams. Despite a report that he’s been dealing with turf toe for a month, this week was Williams’s first appearance all year on the injury report.

And while the fact that Corum got the start last night and 58 snaps while Williams was limited to 38 may have had something to do with the toe, as Mike Tomlin says, “One man’s misfortune is another man’s opportunity.”

Corum seized the opportunity on Wednesday night, as part of a very un-Rams-like offensive output that included 241 rushing yards and 132 passing yards.

For the season, Williams has 662 rushing yards and an average of 4.4 yards per carry. Corum has 731 yards rushing with a 5.4-yard average.

Lurking in the background (but soon moving to the foreground) is the business side of the situation. Corum is in the third year of a slotted, third-round rookie deal. He’s making $1.582 million in 2026, and he’s eligible for a second contract as soon as the regular season ends.

Williams is making $11 million per year, on a deal that runs through 2028.

They’ll need to reward Corum at some point, which could force them to make a decision about Williams.

That’s clearly coming. Last night’s breakout showing in a standalone game shows that it’s coming quickly.
