NFL Power Rankings Week 10: Commanders quickly running out of time to stick around in NFC East

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Washington Commanders are 2-5-1 and their 1-game winning streak came crashing to a quick end after losing to the Los Angeles Rams at home on Sunday. They "gave the Rams all they could handle", but that wasn't enough to get the victory. They actually rose in some power rankings after a loss which is easier to do at the bottom of the league than in the Top 10.

The NFC East remains the worst division in the NFL with the New York Giants still in the lead in Week 10. They pulled off a win, along with the Cowboys last week, to keep both teams at least 1.5 games ahead of the Commanders. Washington visits the Giants in New Jersey on Thursday night, and they'll either keep hope alive in the division, or head into a long week with some decisions to be made for the future.

Most important game of the second half: Week 10 at the Giants

With their current record, the Commanders are quickly running out of time to stick around in the NFC East. Because they tied the Giants last month, they could earn the tiebreaker with a win. Of course, they would still have to win a few other games down the stretch to make the playoffs. But they'd be alive mathematically. The bigger issue is that if they fall to 2-6-1, coach Dan Quinn's seat will become white-hot. The third-year coach needs to be much better after a 5-12 record in 2025. — John Keim Last week: 25

Jayden Daniels set the stakes for Thursday night's game against the Giants, when asked after the team's loss to the Rams whether time is running out to get back in the NFC East race.

"Yeah. I think next week is a must-win," Daniels told reporters after the game.

He's right. With a win, the Commanders would be two games behind the Giants for first place and hold the tiebreaker, since the teams tied in their first meeting. That's not easy, but Washington wouldn't be dead. If the Commanders lose, they'd be four games back and not have the tiebreaker, which would probably be impossible to overcome. It might not matter because the Commanders aren't very good — they're 20th in DVOA with matching ranks of 20th on offense and defense — but at least with a win they'd have something to keep playing for. Last week: 23

This Commanders team had no running game to speak of, they couldn't convert on 3rd downs, and it just generally feels like Dan Quinn has lost his touch.

Once again, situational football is the most difficult thing for this team to get a grip on. The Commanders had a clear shot for a Hail Mary at the end of the game, but decided to throw a 5-yard pass to Chig Okonkwo instead.

Washington's postseason hopes continue to fade. Without significant improvement, a second-half turnaround looks unlikely.

There are still reasons for optimism: The NFC East offers an opportunity, and the Commanders remained competitive against the Rams on Sunday.

After the game, head coach Dan Quinn emphasized the need for more consistent play across all three phases.

"Frustrating game," he said. "Once again, we have not nailed our complementary football, and I'll keep saying it until we do. We're halfway through the season, and that all has to click. After stops, I don't think we capitalized."

The Commanders just have too many deficiencies. There have been too many injuries on offense. There are too many holes on defense. Bad decisions continue piling up weekly—both by players and coaches.
