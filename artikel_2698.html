Cowboys CBs Joey Porter Jr., DaRon Bland are questionable

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Cowboys added cornerback DaRon Bland to the injury report Friday. He is questionable with an ankle injury after limited work in practice.

The team’s other starting cornerback, Joey Porter Jr., is also questionable. Porter returned to the practice field Friday with limited work.

The Cowboys ruled out outside linebacker Donovan Ezeiruaku (ankle), tight end Jake Ferguson (knee/ankle), running back Tyler Goodson (knee/ankle), safety P.J. Locke (concussion) and wide receiver Jonathan Mingo (ankle/foot).

Safety Juanyeh Thomas (hamstring) is doubtful.

Defensive tackle Jonathan Bullard (calf), safety Alijah Clark (illness) and cornerback Devin Moore (hip) are questionable.

Wide receiver George Pickens (hamstring) and linebacker DeMarvion Overshown (hamstring/back) do not have an injury designation.
