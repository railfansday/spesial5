Harrisburg beats Brandon Valley for SD volleyball Class AA title

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

No. 1 Harrisburg finished off its fifth consecutive South Dakota Class AA volleyball championship with a 3-0 (25-22, 25-13, 25-21) sweep of No. 2 Brandon Valley on Saturday, Nov. 21.

The Tigers exacted some revenge over the Lynx with the win. Brandon Valley handed Harrisburg its first in-state loss since 2022 on Oct. 3.

The first and third sets were highly competitive, but a 6-0 run with Sarah Holt serving turned a 21-19 deficit in the third into yet another dogpile on the court.
