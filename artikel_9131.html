Arpita Singh (1937–2026)Indian painter who bridged fantasy and reality

One of the most high-profile contemporary Indian artists, Singh fused violent realism and fantasy imagery, constantly innovating across a six-decade career. In the 1970s and '80s, for instance, she explored abstraction, producing spare compositions filled with dots and dashes. She became more widely known in the West due to an exhibition at Serpentine Galleries just last year.

"Singh never settles into a mode or style," critic John Yau wrote in a 2017 review of her exhibition at New Delhi- and New York-based Talwar Gallery for this magazine. "This body of work, which is being shown for the first time in America, is a singular achievement."
