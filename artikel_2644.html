Pat Freiermuth: Aaron Rodgers said what he said, I'm focused on Denver

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

While Aaron Rodgers was going scorched earth on the Steelers and head coach Mike McCarthy during Wednesday’s appearance on The Pat McAfee Show, he said that tight end Pat Freiermuth informed him of the team’s decision to bench him on Tuesday night.

Rodgers was on the show while the Steelers were practicing, but Freiermuth spoke to reporters in the team’s locker room later in the day. He said he “was just checking in on a close friend” when he called Rodgers after hearing the news of the quarterback change and said he otherwise had nothing to add to what Rodgers said earlier in the day.

“Anything I say about the situation is gonna be tough and obviously it’s tough,” Freiermuth said, via Mark Kaboly of the McAfee show. “I’m here and we’re just focused on trying to get a win against Denver. Obviously he said what he said, but I’m focused on the locker room and the team. That’s really all I have to say about that.”

Rodgers said that others on the roster share his dismay with the way McCarthy has handled his job responsibilities this year and Freiermuth was asked if he has confidence in the team’s direction. Freiermuth said “I’m confident” before declining other requests to comment on topics that came up during a Rodgers appearance that’s likely to remain a talking point around the team for some time.
