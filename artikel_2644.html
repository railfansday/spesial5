Vote for the Register male Athlete of the Week for Nov. 8-14

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The week of Nov. 8-14 saw the Iowa high school football state semifinals take place at the UNI-Dome in Cedar Falls.

In Class 5A, No. 1 Johnston and No. 3 Dowling Catholic advanced to the state championship game. Both teams cruised to victory in the semifinals on Nov. 13, with Johnston pounding No. 4 Ankeny, 42-7, and Dowing cruising past No. 2 Southeast Polk, 44-14.

Des Moines Christian is headed to its first state championship game in 3A. The No. 3 Lions pulled off a convincing 41-14 victory over No. 2 Clear Lake in the semifinals on Nov. 14.

Carlisle saw its season come to an end in the 4A semifinals. The No. 3 Wildcats suffered a 28-15 setback at the hands of No. 2 Sergeant Bluff-Lutton on Nov. 12.

There were several impressive individual performances by high school football players from the Des Moines metro area during the state semifinals.

Here are the Des Moines Register's male Athlete of the Week candidates from the Des Moines metro area for the week of Nov. 8-14. DesMoinesRegister.com readers vote to determine the winners, who will be announced at the end of the week.

Olsen was fantastic at quarterback for the Des Moines Christian football team during its 41-14 victory over No. 2 Clear Lake in the 3A state semfinals on Nov. 14 at the UNI-Dome in Cedar Falls.

Olsen completed 7 of 10 passes for 171 yards and two touchdowns with no interceptions. He also ran for 49 yards and one score to help the Lions reach the 3A championship game.

Chapman did a great job finding the end zone at receiver during No. 1 Johnston's 42-7 drubbing of No. 4 Ankeny in the 5A semifinals of the Iowa high school football playoffs on Nov. 13 at Cedar Falls.

Chapman made six receptions for 73 yards against the Hawks. Three of those catches resulted in a touchdown.

Blount was tremendous at linebacker in leading a dominant defensive effort by the No. 3 Dowling Catholic football team during its impressive 44-14 victory over No. 2 Southeast Polk in the 5A state semifinals on Nov. 13 at Cedar Falls.

Blount finished the game with eight tackles, including one tackle for loss. He also forced a fumble and intercepted a pass for the Maroons.

Burk played well on both sides of the ball for No. 3 Carlisle during its 28-15 loss to No. 2 Sergeant-Bluff Luton in the 4A semifinals of the Iowa high school football playoffs on Nov. 12 at Cedar Falls.

Burke had an active game on defense in recording a game-high 14 tackles, including six solo stops. On offense, Burk ran for 24 yards and one touchdown.

Yeager gave a gutsy effort on defense for No. 4 Ankeny during its 42-7 loss to No. 1 Johnston in the 5A semifinals of the Iowa high school football playoffs on Nov. 13 at the UNI-Dome in Cedar Falls.

The Hawk safety finished the night with a game-high 16 tackles. Yeager tallied six solo stops and he also forced a fumble.
