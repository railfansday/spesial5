Aaron Donald records first sack since coming out of retirement

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The first sack of the season has come for Aaron Donald.

Donald brought down Carson Beck early in the second quarter of Sunday’s matchup with the Cardinals for his first sack since coming out of retirement.

It’s Donald’s first sack since New Year’s Eve in 2023 — a span of 1,050 days.

The Rams are down to the Cardinals 6-3 midway through the second quarter. Donald’s sack ended an Arizona possession that had started after Matthew Stafford’s 10th interception of the season.

The Rams have another injury on the offensive unit, as center Coleman Shelton (hamstring) is doubtful to return.
