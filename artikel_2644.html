When: Tuesday, November 24th at 8:00 p.m. ET

Celtics: Mitchell Robinson – Questionable (Illness)

Wizards: Jamir Watkins – Out (Knee), Tristan Vukcevic – Out (Hip), Reece Beekman – Out (Shoulder), Justin Champagnie – Out (Foot)

The Washington Wizards will get their spot on primetime on NBC as they take on Boston Celtics in their 4th and final NBA Cup game. Right now, the Wizards still have a chance to get into NBA Cup Semifinals, but they will need to beat Boston and possibly get some help.

Washington is riding a 3 game regular season winning streak after holding off the Orlando Magic on Sunday in a dominating performance. After losing 8 straight games, it appears the Wizards have turned things around, but this matchup against Boston will be a challenge.

Boston has struggled a bit lately losing four straight games, including losses to the Stephen Curry-less Golden State Warriors and Sacramento Kings, who only had one win for the season going into that game. The losses can largely be contributed to Boston's offense going cold. This four game stretch has been their worst scoring output since April 2012.

This game looks like the perfect opportunity for the Wizards to pick up a big win against a big name opponent. In order to do so, expect the Wizards to feed their big men often against Boston thin front line. Anthony Davis and Alex Sarr, will both have advantages in this one, especially if Mitchell Robinson is not able to give it a go. The Wizards will need to show similar intensity on defense as they showed on Sunday against Orlando.

With this being a primetime game, featuring #1 pick AJ Dybansta, on a national stage for the first time on broadcast television, what better way to make a statement then to pick up a big NBA Cup victory. Let's see if the Wizards can keep their winning streak going.
