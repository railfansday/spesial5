Princess of Wales Reflects on Cancer Battle in ‘Heal’ Documentary Special

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Catherine, the Princess of Wales will discuss her cancer battle in an upcoming documentary special that will raise money for the hospital that treated her.

Heal: A Princess of Wales Story will air November 30 on NBC, a day after it premieres on the BBC. The documentary follows Kate Middleton as she reflects on her cancer battle while completing the Three Peaks Challenge — climbing the tallest mountains in England, Scotland and Wales in the span of 24 hours — this past June in support of the Royal Marsden Cancer Charity, the hospital where she was treated; proceeds from the distribution of Heal will also benefit Royal Marsden.

“Cancer does change you. It changes your body, it changes you physically. But there’s a much, much deeper part to what a cancer diagnosis shifts in you,” the Princess of Wales says in the trailer.

“It’s clear we’re not meant to carry life’s challenges alone. Things can renew and grow. After it is through pain that we realize there’s a greater beauty that lies beyond that.”

The news of Middleton’s initial diagnosis was disclosed to the public in March 2024, following an abdominal surgery in January. In a video statement, Middleton said, “At the time, it was thought that my condition was non-cancerous,” referring to her surgery. “The surgery was successful, however, tests after the operation on cancer had been present,” she added. By September 2024, the Princess of Wales revealed that she had completed the treatment. “The cancer journey is complex, scary, and unpredictable for everyone, especially those closest to you,” Middleton wrote at the time. “With humility, it also brings you face to face with your own vulnerabilities in a way you have never considered before, and with that, a new perspective on everything.”

In January 2025, Middleton announced, “It is a relief to now be in remission and I remain focused on recovery.”
