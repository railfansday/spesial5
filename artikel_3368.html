Latin music’s biggest stars are gathered in Las Vegas tonight for the 27th Annual Latin Grammy Awards at the MGM Grand Garden Arena.

Superproducer Edgar Barrera is this year’s frontrunner with 13 nominations for his work with artists like Karol G, Carín León, and Becky G. Behind him, with seven nods each, are Karol G, Rosalía, CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, and Jorge Drexler. Other big names with several nominations each are Silvana Estrada, Mon Laferte, and Milo J.

Daddy Yankee will make history as the first reggaeton artist to receive the Latin Recording Academy’s Person of the Year award. Daddy Yankee will also be one of several artists performing at the MGM Arena tonight, alongside Rosalía, Silvana Estrada, Jorge Drexler, and Mon Laferte.

Ahead is everything you need to know about watching the 2026 Latin Grammy Awards, including ways to stream the Latin Grammys for free online without cable.
