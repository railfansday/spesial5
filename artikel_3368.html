Conor Oberst Will Play a Cave and a Haunted Hotel on His First Solo Tour Since 2019

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Conor Oberst has announced a 2027 North American tour, which will mark his first solo headlining trek in eight years.

The 49-date “An Alleged Evening With Conor Oberst” tour will find the singer-songwriter traveling around the West Coast, Midwest, and the South. (While there aren’t any northeast dates on the itinerary now, more shows are expected to be announced.) The trek will kick off  Feb. 19 at Liberty Hall in Lawrence, Kansas, and will wrap with two nights, May 14 and 15, at the Stanley Hotel in Estes Park, Colorado.

While Oberst will primarily play theaters on the tour, there are some particularly unique shows on the schedule. The Stanley Hotel, for instance, is the famous haunted hotel that inspired The Shining. On April 22, Oberst will play La Rosa, a converted Benedictine monastery in Tucson, Arizona on April 22. And on March 19, Oberst will perform at the Caverns, a literal cave in Pelham, Tennessee.

Tickets for all shows will go on sale this Friday, Nov. 13, starting at 10 a.m. local time. Full information is available on Oberst’s website.

“An Alleged Evening With Conor Oberst” will be the singer-songwriter’s first solo headlining tour since 2019, when he was still promoting his most recent solo LP, 2017’s Salutations. (That year, he also toured extensively with Phoebe Bridgers as Better Oblivion Community Center.) After that, Oberst launched a run of residency-style shows in 2024, while also playing some shows with Bright Eyes.
