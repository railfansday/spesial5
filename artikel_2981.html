Here’s What $10,000 Invested in MP Materials Could Be Worth by 2030 as EV Demand Accelerates

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

MP Materials has a clear path to higher earnings.

Global electric vehicle sales are expected to reach 23 million in 2026, according to the International Energy Agency. By 2035, EVs could account for approximately half of worldwide new-car sales. That's encouraging for MP Materials (MP -3.41%), which produces rare-earth materials used in magnets in many electric motors. And it's already benefiting handsomely.

In Q3 2026, revenue jumped 176% to $147.7 million. Production of neodymium and praseodymium, essential ingredients in rare-earth magnets, increased 41% to 1,018 metric tons, and adjusted EBITDA reached $41.1 million, compared with a $12.6 million loss a year earlier.

MP Materials also completed General Motors' (GM +0.45%) qualification process, demonstrating that its magnets meet the automaker's requirements. Commercial deliveries are expected to begin in Q4, generating MP's first revenue from finished magnet production.

With MP Materials' shares trading around $38, a $10,000 investment would purchase approximately 263 shares. But to be clear, MP's growth potential is supported by more than projections of rising EV demand. Under its agreement with the U.S. government, the company's planned 10X magnet facility in Texas is guaranteed at least $140 million in annual EBITDA, subject to escalation.

MP has also published a scenario in which the facility generates $400 million in annual EBITDA, with approximately $255 million retained by MP. So with 10X scheduled for commissioning in 2028, the facility could contribute substantially to earnings by 2030.

Investment bank Moelis' April 2026 analysis also provides a useful benchmark. Its valuation figures imply approximately $984 million in EBITDA for 2030. Applying the 10.6 times EBITDA multiple Moelis reported for competitor Lynas Rare Earths yields an enterprise valuation of approximately $10.4 billion.

Using MP's September 2026 cash, debt, preferred-equity balance, and outstanding common shares, that translates to approximately $58 per share. At that price, a $10,000 investment would be worth roughly $15,260 by 2030, representing a gain of approximately 53%.

Of course, this is an illustrative valuation, not a guarantee. Changes in MP's earnings, debt, cash balances, or share count could significantly affect the outcome.
