Stock Market Today, Nov. 18: S&P 500 Breaks Key Support as Fed Minutes Signal More Rate Hikes Ahead

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Wall Street's troubles are mounting as expectations for further interest rate hikes overshadow strong corporate earnings and threaten to undermine the market's long-term upward trend.

By RJ Fulton – Nov 18, 2026 at 5:40PM EST

The S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.23%) fell 0.24% to 7,323, slipping below its closely watched 200-day moving average. The Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +0.42%) bucked the broader trend, gaining 0.42% to 24,430, supported by strength in select technology and retail stocks. Meanwhile, the Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI -1.11%) fell 1.11% to 50,198 as mounting regulatory concerns triggered heavy selling across managed healthcare stocks.

Selling pressure extended beyond equities, with gold plunging 3.51% to $3,728.60. The 10-year Treasury yield eased by two basis points to 5.10% but remains near recent highs. Industrial and consumer discretionary stocks provided pockets of strength, gaining 0.62% and 0.39%, respectively, while communication services fell 0.81%. Despite encouraging corporate earnings, elevated valuations and an increasingly hawkish Federal Reserve continue to complicate Wall Street's path toward recovery.

Healthcare stocks came under considerable pressure on Wednesday, with UnitedHealth Group (UNH -10.21%) tumbling 10.21% and Humana (HUM -12.98%) plummeting 13%. The sell-off followed a federal regulatory proposal to expand Medicare Advantage audits and recover improper payments dating back to 2018, potentially exposing insurers to substantial financial liabilities.

Meanwhile, The TJX Companies (TJX +8.05%) surged 8.05% after reporting stronger-than-expected comparable sales growth and raising its full-year guidance ahead of the holiday shopping season. Amazon (AMZN +3.82%) gained 3.82% as anticipation surrounding Anthropic's initial public offering drew renewed attention to the value of Amazon's multibillion-dollar investment in the artificial intelligence company.

Perhaps the most consequential development on Wednesday came from the release of the Federal Reserve's latest meeting minutes, which indicated that nearly all officials anticipate further interest rate hikes may be necessary to bring inflation under control. The increasingly hawkish outlook helped push the U.S. dollar to a 19-month high, creating another headwind for multinational companies whose overseas earnings become less valuable when converted back into dollars.

Meanwhile, the S&P 500's decline below its 200-day moving average marks a notable deterioration in the market's longer-term trend. Although a breach of this technical benchmark doesn't necessarily signal a prolonged downturn, the market's inability to sustain a rally despite strong corporate earnings suggests that elevated valuations and restrictive monetary policy are becoming increasingly difficult to overcome.

Looking ahead, investors will get another important reading on consumer health when Walmart reports earnings on Thursday. Encouraging results from Target and TJX suggest consumer spending remains relatively resilient, but upcoming economic data will be particularly important in determining the trajectory of interest rates.

For a more sustainable recovery to materialize, investors should look for a stabilization in Treasury yields, moderating inflation expectations, and the S&P 500 reclaiming its 200-day moving average.
