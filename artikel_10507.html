Police can’t wait for GTA 6 to be released so they can “catch a break”

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

A police department in the United States is desperate for the release of Grand Theft Auto 6, so people decide to stay at home, making their lives easier.

With November 19 just around the corner, Grand Theft Auto fans across the globe have started making their plans for the release of GTA 6. In the United States, an estimated 1.5 million people are expected to take time off work to play the long-awaited game, leading to around $1 billion worth of disruption to the economy.

Naturally, that figure dwarves other countries, even though worldwide disruption is expected once it rolls out.

However, the release can’t come soon enough for the Greesboro Police Department in North Carolina.

On November 6, the Police Department stated that GTA 6 “needs to hurry up” as it would make their jobs a little easier.

“GTA 6 needs to hurry up and get here so y’all can stay at home and let us catch a break,” they posted on X (formerly Twitter).

Naturally, the post drew plenty of eyes from GTA fans and others alike. “This is an all-time goated law enforcement post,” one said. “Damn, GTA 6 craze is real,” commented another

“Jason and Lucia are going to keep Vice City’s cops busy, so they won’t be staying home anytime soon,” another joked. “Crime rate gonna go down for years,” another replied.

Other police departments have leant into the hype around the new Grand Theft Auto as well. Derbyshire Police in the UK mocked a criminal who would miss the release of the new game after he was arrested for dangerous driving.
