YouTuber builds “world’s smallest” houseboat and it actually works

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

YouTuber David Rule set out to build the “world’s smallest houseboat,” and he’s actually managed to take it out on a 12-mile trip.

Over the years, plenty of people have set out to take normal-sized things and try to make them as small as possible. This has led to mechanics building the world’s “narrowest” car and it being perfectly road legal, as well as scientists coming up with the world’s smallest robot that actually thinks for itself.

At the end of October, YouTuber David Rule joined that party by revealing that he had built the “world’s smallest houseboat” that could actually fit in the back of a truck.

He described it as the “most ridiculous build” he has taken on yet, but Rule managed to navigate a 12-mile journey down the Pend Oreille River in Washington over three days.

The Tinytanic is constructed mainly of cedar, pine, and plywood, with it measuring 42 inches high inside. That means Rule was able to sit down and lie down inside, but standing up was out of the question.

Well, that was unless he was stood at the back and piloting the boat. On that small standing point, Rule was also able to fish across his journey too.

Additionally, it has a functioning stove – which the YouTuber used to cook himself some food – as well as a small fridge and sink. Rule noted it was built for “function,” so you could live in it, as long as you were super happy with tight spaces.

Rule has previously caught attention for building the “world’s smallest tiny house” with 30-inch walls and a one-foot-high roof.

You might think that something that size can’t contain the creature comforts needed for you to actually live in there. However, the YouTuber managed to fit in a shower, a bed, and a small galley kitchen so that you could live inside if you really wanted to downsize.

He ultimately doesn’t claim any Guinness World Records for his builds, but they draw plenty of eyeballs regardless.
