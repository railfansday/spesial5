CoreWeave is asking Texas's incoming governor to work with it on data centers

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

CoreWeave offered to help finish permitted projects and set standards for new ones after Hinojosa won on a data center moratorium pledge

CoreWeave wrote to Texas Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa to propose collaborating with her incoming team on data center projects both underway and still being planned, according to Bloomberg.

CoreWeave co-founder and Chief Executive Officer Michael Intrator offered to work with Hinojosa's transition team to see already-permitted projects through and to establish standards for later buildouts. "You have made clear that data center growth must work for Texas," Intrator said, according to Bloomberg. "We strongly support those principles."

Hinojosa became the first Democrat to capture the Texas governorship since 1990, according to Bloomberg. Her campaign promise was a statewide moratorium on data centers, and she has since sketched out ways to pair stiffer regulation with the state’s business-friendly reputation, Bloomberg said. “We have always been a pro-business state in Texas, but what changes now with me as governor is it has to be a partnership that benefits Texans,” Hinojosa said in an interview on Thursday, according to Bloomberg.

Carl Holshouser, who runs government affairs at CoreWeave, wrote in a LinkedIn post that Hinojosa had made it plain that projects already holding permits can be finished, according to The Next Web. The Next Web found no record of Hinojosa making that claim in her own words, and her team has not explained how a pause would be carried out.

Holshouser, who co-wrote the letter, said the cost of building new transmission lines and generating plants should not fall on families, and that new sites need to safeguard local water and electricity supplies, according to The Next Web.

CoreWeave's bonds tumbled on Wednesday after Hinojosa won the governor's race on a pledge to halt data center development. CoreWeave stock fell 9.9% that day to close at $48.42, its weakest finish since May 2025.

A CoreWeave spokesperson said in a statement that the company does not anticipate any impact to existing customer commitments or associated backlog from shifts in the political landscape. Republicans hold the majority in the Texas Legislature, which limits how much Hinojosa can change state policy.
