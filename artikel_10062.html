Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Pity Sam Altman: He's going to have a lot more explaining to do when OpenAI follows this work of abstract legal art

It's official: Anthropic is going public, and boy, are our eyebrows tired from poring over hundreds and hundreds of pages of questionable claims.

Anthropic is going to need to do a lot to make its massive IPO more than just a flash in the pan, as systems editor Tobias Mann and senior reporter Tom Claburn tell host Brandon Vigliarolo on this week's episode.

For one, the company has nearly a trillion dollars in compute commitments to pay for, which is going to mean it'll be burning through more cash than a datacenter gas turbine turned up to 11. In addition to its massive spending for the foreseeable future, there's also the fact that much of its computing power relies on customers who are also competitors.

In short, we're skeptical, but you probably already knew that. It's El Reg, after all, and we're not just taking what the AI industry claims at face value - if it looks like a house of cards, it probably is.
