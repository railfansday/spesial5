Sabrina Carpenter Would Love to Star in a <i><em>9 to 5</em></i> Movie Musical

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The star talks Dolly Parton, the French New Wave, and her Johnnie Walker short with Damien Chazelle.

For Sabrina Carpenter, a song and a role each offer a different way into a story. “Acting and music have always gone hand in hand for me,” she tells ELLE exclusively. “With music, I get to tell stories through my own perspective, and with acting I get to step into someone else’s world and bring their story to life, and that is always an exhilarating feeling for me.”

Carpenter gets to move between those perspectives in a new Johnnie Walker film directed by Damien Chazelle, the Oscar-winning visionary behind Whiplash and La La Land. With the Short n’ Sweet star at its center, the clip nods to French New Wave cinema through the director’s signature motion-driven style.

The short begins with Carpenter, in a mod embellished minidress, stepping into an unusually quiet party. As someone pours her a Go Go Highball, her signature Johnnie Walker cocktail, the scene comes to life: the guests spring into motion, and Carpenter gets swept up in the revelry while a meta quartet of directors looks on. Here, she opens up to ELLE about working with Chazelle, her favorite New Wave films, and the movie musical she’d most want to star in (hint: it’s a certain blonde’s workplace comedy).

This past year has been pretty surreal. Coachella will always be a standout for me, probably for the rest of my life. I had so much fun creating that show. Also getting to perform onstage this year with Madonna and Stevie Nicks. [I’m] still processing that.

Sometimes the best moments are just spending time with the people you love, and this summer spending some downtime with my close friends and my sister, we loved our Go Go Highballs for sure.

Getting ready for my Met Gala after-party comes to mind. But I can’t always reveal all the details to you guys; there’s no fun in that.

Working with Damien was a dream! I’ve admired his work for so long, and he has a brilliant way of making even the smallest moments feel larger than life. I love how he was able to capture the humor and playfulness that come with life’s unexpected moments.

I love La La Land! I think there’s something really special about creating a short moment or scene that feels almost like a whole movie, so I’m always trying to approach these projects from a cinematic viewpoint.

Two of my favorites are The Umbrellas of Cherbourg and Cleo from 5 to 7.

Well, I just rewatched 9 to 5 and you know I’m the biggest Dolly [Parton] fan. So that would be a fun one.
