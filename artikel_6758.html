Jennifer Lawrence Got The Boob Job She Wanted—And Shared The Details

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Last night, confessed Bravo superfan Jennifer Lawrence made her fifth appearance on Andy Cohen’s Watch What Happens Live, appearing alongside her Hunger Games co-star Josh Hutcherson. Conversations that happen in Cohen’s gray-blue chairs frequently create viral moments, and when combined with Lawrence’s incredible lack of filter, well, that’s what happened many times over. Our favorite? The actor opened up about her own breast augmentation story.

Lawrence and Cohen were cycling through some Bravoverse hot takes, getting to Real Housewives of New York City star Hailey Glassman’s claim that an implant had broken her nose. Lawrence shared she was skeptical of Glassman’s story. Her reasoning? “I have implants,” she said. The actor hadn’t been shy about her desire to get them, sharing in a New Yorker profile earlier this year that having her second child had made her want them. “They’re new!”

Next, following in the tradition of Kylie Jenner (whose breast augmentation is the prototype for my future one) and Denise Richards, she gave us everything else we wanted to know. “Dr. Teitelbaum in Santa Monica,” confirming the doctor who conducted the surgery. Cohen asked the size, insinuating that they looked like a more-humble B-cup, and Lawrence retorted with her signature killer sense of humor. “No, it’s a full C,” she said while touching her chest, pouting and preening dramatically. “I think? It’s the same size that they were.”

Earlier this year, Vogue sat down with Lawrence to talk about motherhood, skincare, and more. Of course, aesthetic procedures came as well. “I can’t really get very much done,” she said at the time. “I need to have movement in my face—I get baby Botox, but I can’t do a lot. I would love to have a buccal fat removal. I can’t because everybody would know, but just know that I want that.”
