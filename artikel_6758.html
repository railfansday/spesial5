Julianne Hough Defends Her ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Season 35 Style

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Julianne Hough had a lot of fun with her looks this season on Dancing With the Stars, and isn’t paying any attention to critics who have come for her style, tyvm.

“I really feel like I’m at this next inflection of my career,” the DWTS co-host recently told Glamour. “When you get into your late 30s, you just don’t give a fuck anymore. You’re like, ‘Who am I? What do I want to wear? What makes me feel incredible?’ It has more intention rather than trying to fit into what’s hot.”

“I feel like I’m in my leading lady era, and I mean that in the actual projects that I’m doing, but also me as a person,” she said. “I’ve gone a little bit more vixen-y this year than I have girl next door because we’re trying to find that character. It’s been very strategic.”

Julianne added that “apathy is the enemy,” and noted that “The reason things exist and have charge and stay visible [is] people either love it or hate it. They still want to talk about it.”

Julianne’s been working with stylist Brad Goreski, who said he has “an awareness” of what fans are saying online, “and I love it. Any conversation, good or bad, around fashion is great. I think people weighing in—they like the hair but not the dress; they thought it was a bad outfit; they think she should fire me—is totally fine, because we’re really excited by what we’re doing, and we put a lot of thought and energy into it.”

Brad also said that “Jules ultimately brings the looks to life, and I’m glad that there’s another part of the show that people talk about. Not every person’s going to love every look. I know that from being a stylist for 15 years. And that’s okay.”
