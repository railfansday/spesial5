Stranger Than Heaven Is So Big That All Its Acts Get a PS5 Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

I read on social media today that RGG Studio estimates the main story of Stranger Than Heaven can take anywhere from 50 to 100 hours to complete. When it's able to dedicate entire trailers to each of the game's acts, I can believe it.

Having covered Act 1: Kokura last month, the developer is back with an overview of Act 2: Kure. Catch it above.

At this part of the game, the campaign is set in 1929, in Kure of the Hiroshima Prefecture. Here, main character Makoto Daito joins the Iwaki family in an attempt to rise through the ranks. "As he becomes known as the 'Red Oni', we also see Daito wrestle with his life in the yakuza and confront the choices he has made," the YouTube description teases.

Stranger Than Heaven comprises five acts, so expect another three trailers after this one, set in 1943, 1951, and 1965.

Preview 3 Hours with Stranger Than Heaven Left Me Partly Convinced, Partly Concerned, and Craving Gyudon

We have played the upcoming PS5 game a few times now, and have continually come away impressed... except with its new combat system. Editor Sammy Barker didn't die quite so many times in the latest build, but came away saying:

"One big issue I have with the game is when you’re holding a one-handed weapon, like a knife. This feels like it skews all of your combat options towards one hand, so while you can still throw jabs with your other arm, obviously it’s a much weaker option in this context."
