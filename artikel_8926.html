Celebrating the Inaugural Art Week NYC, Organized by ARTnews, Art in America, and Artforum

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

ARTnews, Art in America, and Artforum just wrapped up the inaugural edition of Art Week NYC, a citywide event celebrating all the New York art world’s ecosystem has to offer, from museums and galleries to artists, collectors, and cultural tastemakers from the Lower East Side and Tribeca to Chelsea and the Upper East Side.

Art Week NYC kicked off with an opening party at the Mallory in Chelsea, a 19th-century warehouse building that used to be home to the Tunnel nightclub. Programming took place over four jampacked days, from November 11–14. A launch event at the Metropolitan Museum of Art had in attendance New York City Council Speaker Julie Menin, City Council Deputy Speaker Nantasha Williams, Manhattan Borough President Brad Hoylman-Sigal; New York State Senator Jose Serrano, and City Council member Virginia Maloney. On November 13 and 14, ARTnews hosted a slate of programming at PMC’s ground-floor studio across the street from the New York Public Library. The two-day event was presented by Amazon, in partnership with Louis Roederer Champagne, Piaget, and Flying Whale Wines.

Friday featured in-depth conversations with art world insiders, such as dealers David Nolan and Anton Svyatsky on what separates collectors from speculators; Tina Vaz, the MTA’s director of arts and design, speaking about several newly announced public art commissions; and photographer Stephen Shore detailing his new book, The Mental Image, a mix of memoir, pictures, and art history. Top 200 Collectors Larry and Allison Berg discussed the increasing crossover between sports and the art world, while art adviser Sophia Cohen gave insights on how a new generation of collectors is reshaping the market.

On Saturday, ARTnews welcomed two of our sponsors to the PMC Studio: Jean-Bernard Forot, Piaget’s Head of Patrimony, talked about Andy Warhol’s love of timepieces and how the watchmaker came to collaborate with the Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts, while Frédéric Rouzaud, president of Maison Louis Roederer, discussed lasting ways for private patrons, like the Louis Roederer Foundation, to support contemporary art.

The day also looked back on what has happened to art in the 2020s (so far), synthesizing the impact of the pandemic, the NFT boom and bust, the rise of AI, and social and political upheaval; featured a spirited chat with New York Magazine’s Jerry Saltz on the current state of art criticism and the art world; and explored the importance of art collecting as both a personal passion and a creative practice from the point of view of two fashion executives, Carolina Herrera global creative director Wes Gordon and Oribe cofounder Daniel Kaner.

Before bidding farewell to our very first Art Week NYC, ARTnews revealed the winners of the third annual ARTnews Awards. This year’s jury included top curators from across the United States, and you can read more about the categories and the winners here.
