Aman has confirmed what the paper trail suggested to Skift in January. The open question is delivery: Janu, the group's second brand, has one hotel open after more than six years since its launch.

Aman Group said Tuesday it's launching Atma, a third hotel brand built around sports, wellness, and "performance living." The first property, a hotel with branded residences and a private members club, is set to be completed in Miami in 2031.

Tennis champion Novak Djokovic is a "global partner" who will help lead the brand's focus on longevity, nutrition, and wellness programming, Aman said.

The Tuesday announcement confirms Skift's reporting in January that the ultra-luxury operator planned to open a brand under the Atma name and that the Miami project would be its first location.

Aman Group is launching a third brand, Atma, built around sports, wellness, and what it calls "performance living," with the first property — a hotel with branded residences and a private members club — set for completion in Miami in 2031. Tennis champion Novak Djokovic is a "global partner" who will help lead programming on longevity, nutrition, and wellness, Aman said. The Miami project sits in a planned 1,049-foot tower on the Brickell waterfront, with a 120,000-square-foot spa and wellness center including indoor tennis, squash, and padel courts, a thermal spa, and a medical wellness center. Residences run from one-bedroom units to six-bedroom penthouses, and owners get priority nomination to the invitation-only Atma Club. Developers are OKO Group, owned by Aman Chairman and CEO Vlad Doronin, and Oak Row Equities. Aman didn't disclose a room count, financial terms, or how it will fund the growth. The announcement confirms Skift's January reporting on the brand name and the Miami location. It also lands against a slow rollout record: second brand Janu debuted in 2020 and only Janu Tokyo has opened, while Janu Dubai has moved from a 2027 opening to 2029 on the brand's website.
