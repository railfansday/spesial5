Twenty One Capital (XXI) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Management pivots from Bitcoin holding toward cash-generating operations.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Twenty One Capital, Inc. (XXI -1.00%) reported a return to profitability in the third quarter, driven by the valuation recovery of its digital asset treasury. Management stated that the company is transitioning from a holding entity toward an operating business model focused on generating cash flow from sectors such as mining, lending, and energy. The company indicated that future capital allocation will prioritize investments that can potentially outperform direct digital asset ownership on a risk-adjusted basis. Management emphasized a patient approach to acquisitions, noting that several opportunities were passed over during the quarter due to unfavorable terms or economics.
