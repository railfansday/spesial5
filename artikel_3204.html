‘The Body’ Summons 86% Audience Boost To Lead Netflix’s Weekly TV Rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Body is summoning some interest on Netflix. The YA drama tallied 9.5 million views from November 16 to 22, taking No. 1 on the English TV list for its first full week on the streamer.

That’s an 86% increase from the 5.1 million views it managed in its first few days of availability after premiering on November 11. In The Body, a group of badly-behaved Catholic school girls begin having prophetic visions that set off mass hysteria in town after a dance-team initiation goes wrong on Halloween night in 2004.

Its premiere sparked some controversy when a petition emerged criticizing the “blasphemous anti-Christian series,” but, if anything, that buzz seems to have only led to more intrigue and a bigger audience. Second place went to Season 2 of Black Doves with 3.7 million views. The first season of the Keira Knightley thriller also nabbed a spot at No. 6 with 2.1 million views. Watch on Deadline

Viewers are also still enthralled with Taken: Natalee Holloway, which chronicles the murdered teen’s mother in her two-decade pursuit of justice. The docuseries was no. 4 with 2.2 million views.

Nobody Wants This spent another week on the English TV list, this time at No. 8 with 1.5 million views. In total, Season 3 has pocketed 21.3 million cumulative views since it launched last month.

As has been typical lately, the film slate garnered much more attention — especially now that we’re in the thick of the holiday season. Netflix kicked off its annual slate of holiday movies with the Leighton Meester–Jared Padalecki rom-com Guarding Stars, which collected 27.6 million views to be the top offering across the platform last week.

The mystery thriller Heartland came in second place on the English film list with 14.8 million views.
