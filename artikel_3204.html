TELUS Corporation (TU -3.29%) reported a strategic focus on its core telecommunications assets while launching a multiyear efficiency program designed to reduce debt and improve margins. Management stated that the company is advancing an asset monetization strategy for its non-core Health and Agriculture units and is exploring a partnership model to unlock equity from its PureFibre network. The company is also expanding into the Canadian AI infrastructure market, leveraging its existing data center footprint and secured power capacity. These initiatives aim to restore the leverage ratio to 3.0x or lower by the end of 2028 while funding organic growth in the domestic telecom market.
