Fredrik Wester Stepping Down as Paradox CEO in 2027

Paradox Interactive, best known for being behind some of the biggest strategy games such as Age of Wonders, Stellaris, and Crusader Kings, has revealed that a change in leadership is on the horizon.

Fredrik Wester first served as the CEO of Paradox in 2009. He stepped down in 2018 to become Executive Chairman of the Board, but made his return as CEO in 2021, and will remain in position until the new CEO transitions in.

The Board of Directors of Paradox Interactive announced on November 11 that Mattias Lilja, the current Deputy CEO of the company, will be the new CEO effective January 4, 2027. Wester, who informed the Board of his decision to step down, will become a member of the Board, focused on the future of the company.

The move has been called a “natural evolution of how the company is already led.” The statement continues, “Since 2021, Mattias Lilja has taken on increasing responsibility for operations and the execution of the company’s strategy, while Fredrik Wester has placed increasing focus on the company’s long-term development and goals. The company’s strategic direction remains firm, and Fredrik Wester remains the largest shareholder in Paradox.”

Wester says, “My plan was never to be CEO for life. The goal was to build the foundation before passing the baton on for the next chapter in the company’s journey.”

Lilja returned to Paradox when Wester took over as CEO in 2021, and in 2024, was appointed as Deputy CEO of the company. Wester believes Lilja “has had a central role in laying this foundation, and now is the right time to hand over. Given his experience and vision, he is exceptionally well-positioned to take the company to its next level.”

In relation to stepping into the role of CEO, Lilja explains that he intends to “approach this role with great humility. Paradox has a unique DNA that has made us one of the greats in deep and engaging strategy and management games."

As CEO, Lilja will be "stepping up the pace: we will make further investments in our core business, push the boundaries of our game design, work even more closely with our loyal players, develop more and better tools and services that benefit both modders and players, and continue to sharpen our ways of working and our organisation, with player value in focus.”

Paradox has had a rocky ride with its new releases as of late. Europa Universalis 5 launched in November 2025 to mixed reviews, while the Across the Pillars DLC, which made its debut in October 2026, failed to impress fans. Paradox Tinto admitted that the team is “disappointed” with the release of the DLC, outlining: “we have put care, love, and a lot of effort into it, but it hasn’t turned out the way either you, the players, or us, the devs, would have liked it to be.”

As the publishers of Cities: Skylines II, this title also had a disappointing start when it launched in October 2023, but the developers have continuously worked on improving the game ever since through regular updates.

It will be interesting to see how future projects will fare under this new leadership. Paradox Interactive has a new strategy game in the works named Afterworld, for example, with a release date yet to be confirmed.
