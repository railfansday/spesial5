U.S. Pilot Arrested At London Heathrow Airport, Admits Cocaine Possession—Passengers Owed Over $150,000 Compensation

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A 64-year-old U.S. pilot arrested at London Heathrow has pleaded guilty to possessing cocaine. Wayne Sigler, from Louisiana, was remanded in custody and faces two additional charges involving alleged drunkenness or unfitness for duty. According to the BBC’s report of information confirmed by the Metropolitan Police, Sigler was arrested on Sunday, November 1. He appeared in court on Saturday, November 7, and pleaded guilty to possessing cocaine. His next hearing is scheduled for December 7 at Isleworth Crown Court. Those charges fall under the Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003. The law also covers preparing to fly, duties after reporting for work, and standby. The charges don’t establish that he flew passengers while impaired.
