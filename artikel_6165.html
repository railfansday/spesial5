12 Standout 2026 Black Friday Cruise Deals to Take You Around the World for Less

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Deals and pricing updated as of 5:03 p.m. on November 27, 2026.

It's go time, cruisers. We've officially reached one of the best times of the year to book a cruise—Cyber Week. Despite falling just before wave season, cruise lines of all shapes and sizes are slashing prices on sailings this week, with some extending the travel-by window well into 2028. This means that whether you're considering something last-minute (perhaps spontaneous NYE plans?) or have a sailing in mind for 2027 or 2028, now may very well be the ideal time to book and save serious dollars on trips around the world.

Below, I've spotlighted Cyber Week deals from 12 cruise lines Traveler readers know and love. There's a variety of options—river cruises, ocean voyages, and expedition ships are all in on the fun—with itineraries taking guests everywhere from the Galápagos and the Nile to the Arctic and Antarctic regions. Read on for the best Black Friday cruise deals to book right now.

Quark Expeditions' Ultramarine sailing in the Arctic

If you've ever dreamed of hopping on a polar cruise and exploring the Arctic or Antarctic, now's your chance to do so without stretching your budget quite so far. Quark Expeditions is offering up to 40% off select polar sailings when booked by December 3. Limited cabins are available, and once they’re gone, they’re gone, so if you're eager, be sure to book before your dream trip fills up.

There are many enticing itineraries to choose from—Svalbard in Spring, Essential Patagonia, South Greenland: Alpine Exploration by Helicopter—all of which are particularly well-suited to travelers seeking out enrichment and immersion opportunities or the chance to come eye-to-eye with majestic wildlife. For those set on crossing the Great White Continent (Antarctica) off their bucket list but are wary of the Drake Passage, the Antarctic Express allows you to bypass that sometimes treacherous passage and fly in.

A contemporary premium line well-versed in sophisticated design, elevated dining, and high-quality service, Celebrity Cruises is regularly selected as one of our readers' favorite cruise lines. On Black Friday, take 75% off second guest fares and save up to $1,200 per room (both discounts are an increase from last year's sale!). With more than 600 sailings to choose from across all seven continents, you'll have no trouble finding a destination that suits the whole group.

Virgin Voyages carved out its own lane when it first set sail in 2021, and the cheeky adults-only line has maintained a loyal cohort in the years since. This year's Cyber Week deals are strong enough to have even recent sailors planning their next getaway, and might just bring along some fresh navigators too. Book by December 3 to score up to 80% off your second guest and up to $800 in free drinks (depending on your cabin category). Itineraries include transatlantic and repositioning cruises as well as sailings in North America, the Caribbean, and Europe. And with travel dates as far out as October 28, 2028, you're in no rush to put it on the calendar.

The resort-like pool deck on board Oceania Vista

With more than 600 ports of call, Oceania Cruises can take you nearly anywhere you want to go. During the culinary-focused cruise line's Black Friday sale, which runs through December 1, travelers can save up to 50% on all 2027 voyages. The geographic options are endless: Sail from Bali to Hong Kong, Barcelona to Rome, Singapore to Cape Town, Montreal to Miami, and more.

Seabourn's Black Friday sale is a good one: Book a cruise and get an autimatic upgrade to a Veranda Suite, 15% off your deposit, and $2,000 in onboard ship credit, which can be put toward spa services, curated shore excursions, and exclusive Reserve wine list bottles and optional wine pairings. As for the fine print: Full world and grand sailings are excluded, and you'll need to book by December 8, 2026.

A luxury experience awaits onboard Regent—and through December 1, it can come at a significantly lowered cost. Cruises are up to 40% off, with up to $500 in shipboard credit offered per suite, plus more savings for past guests. There are more than 90 sailings to choose from, with options in Europe, the Caribbean, Asia, and Australia. Birdwatchers, you'll want to check out the 7-Night Birdwatchers Haven Cruise through Alaska.

Royal Caribbean's newest ship, Legend of the Seas

No need to rush here—through December 7, take up to $1,000 off select cruise fares, enjoy free cruise fares for third and fourth guests sharing a stateroom, and snag up to $250 in onboard credits. Beyond that, you'll get up to 50% off onboard extras, including beverage packages, internet access, and shore excursions. These deals span tons of itineraries visiting the Caribbean, Europe, and Alaska, including ones on Royal Caribbean's newest ships like the Legend of the Seas, which just made its US debut from Fort Lauderdale, Florida, on November 8. Already itching for warmer weather? There are last-minute January and February cruises available starting at $199 per person!

Well-suited to couples looking for a laid-back but still buzzy cruise atmosphere, Holland America Line calls in ports around the world. During its Black Friday sale, travelers can get up to 30% off cruise fares, as well as perks like free prepaid stateroom tips, so long as they book by Sunday, November 29. These are some steep savings—sail to the Bahamas and Dominican Republic on a seven-day cruise for just $399 per person, or head to the Western Caribbean and Mexico for the same amount of time and pay just $449 per person.

MSC Cruises' Black Friday sale is officially live! Through Friday, November 27, take up to 40% off cruises and score up to $1,000 in onboard credit. Other perks include free balcony upgrades and kids sail free opportunities. This is a line with a little something for everyone—it's family-friendly, but the MSC Yacht Club ship-within-a-ship experience is ideal for those looking for a more peaceful ambiance. While it's particularly popular when it comes to Mediterranean cruises, its ships also sail to the Caribbean, Alaska, and Northern Europe.

Princess is a premium cruise line known for its Alaska sailings, onboard hospitality, and of course, playing host to The Love Boat in the ’70s. During its Cyber Week sale, the line is offering guests up to 50% off cruise fares, up to $800 in instant savings, and free fares for third and fourth guests on select voyages (family travelers, did you hear that?). The savings—which put starting rates at just $294 per person—are applicable across a range of itineraries, whether you're looking to explore Alaska, make Euro summer plans, escape the winter cold in the Caribbean or Mexico, or venture as far as Australia and New Zealand.

If luxury river cruising with smaller crowds is more your speed, consider sailing with Uniworld; its ships cap out at just 158 guests. You've got plenty of time to take advantage of its Cyber Week sale; the Journey Together discounts mean that if you book by December 18, the second guest (in the same stateroom) can sail for 50% off on standout itineraries down the Danube, Nile, Rhine, and more. Already planning further ahead? Book a cruise by March 31, 2027, to save up to 20% on select 2028 trips.

Is Norway on your 2027 travel bucket list? Expedition line HX knows the region well, and through December 2, is offering up to 20% off scenic journeys along Norway's coast with the region's majestic fjords in your sight line. Other all-inclusive expedition sailings are up to 40% off—sail to Antarctica, Alaska, Greenland, or the Galápagos Islands with HX.
