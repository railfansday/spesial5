Data Shows New Tactical Shooter Port Struck Gold With Console Plans

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The surprise announcement of a Squad console port hit harder than a tank rolling through an undefended spawn point.

Taking the world’s most popular tactical shooter and moving to a platform that is typically associated with run-and-gun titles like Call of Duty and Overwatch was a bold move, but the numbers are finally in, and it looks like Offworld Industries played a blinder.

The battles on the Ottoman Front are about to get deadlier as Gallipoli targets hitreg issues.

The Squad console alpha test ran from October 16 to October 25, with a free experience limited exclusively to the PlayStation 5. During the nine days of testing, console players got to try a battle between the Ukrainian Armed Forces and the Chinese People’s Liberation Army.

Although Squad will have full crossplay between PC, PS5, and XBOX Series X/S, the test servers disabled it to give Sony’s faithful a smoother introduction to the game.

Today, OWI has finally published the data from the playtest, and the results are quite encouraging.

According to a social media post by the official Squad account today, just over 110 thousand PS5 players participated in the playtest.

That number in a vacuum doesn’t say much, but what’s impressive is that the Squad playtest led to over 50 thousand wishlists on PlayStation 5 alone, meaning a conversion rate of around 45%. Many an indie dev would caution that treating wishlists as guaranteed sales is a highway to heartbreak, and they’d be right, but at the present moment it’s still a showcase of the positive effect of the playtesting sessions.

The strong reception to the Squad PS5 alpha preview is also reflected in the player ratings. According to OWI, the game closed the testing sessions with a 4.5 out of 5 score. The PC version of Squad has an 83% positive rating on Steam and a 4.3 out of 5 score on the Epic Games Store, landing the console port slightly ahead of its peers, but ultimately in the same bracket.

Squad first came out in 2015, with humble beginnings as a crowdfunded spiritual successor to Battlefield 2’s Project Reality mod. The early versions of the game feel like a far cry from what we call Squad today, especially on the technical side.

According to producer Peter Maurice (full interview here), Squad is done “turning that corner where a lot of the major tech investment is done”, and that has allowed the team at Offworld Industries to turn its focus to new platforms and content.

Taking the world’s most popular tactical shooter and moving to a platform that is typically associated with run-and-gun titles like Call of Duty and Overwatch was a bold move

Currently, Squad runs on Unreal Engine 5.7. The transition from Unreal Engine 4 was “a massive challenge for a small studio,” but the performance benefits were felt on PC once the game found its footing.

According to Maurice, OWI has been planning a console port for as long as 3 years, but the project only transitioned from an idea into reality about a year ago, following the release of space bug shooter Starship Troopers Extermination. The studio was able to move developers who had worked on bringing this game to console and put them to work on Squad, making the transition a lot smoother.

The next big challenge for Squad’s console release will be building a community that can support itself. As Maurice explained to me, consoles do not have a “veterans that have been with us for years like on PC,” and that means players will need the game to help people learn even when a more experienced player is not around.

Squad is coming to PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X/S sometime in 2027. Secret Mode’s Project Marketing Manager, Matthew Beckett, says a future playtest session is possible, but more info on that later.
