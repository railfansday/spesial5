This Beaten-Down Semiconductor Stock Could Soar in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Broadcom’s custom AI chip business is booming, but the market is pricing the stock as if it isn’t.

By Prosper Junior Bakiny – Nov 19, 2026 at 5:25PM EST

The semiconductor industry has been at the center of the ongoing artificial intelligence (AI) revolution, but several market leaders have underperformed this year. One of them is Broadcom (AVGO -2.09%), a semiconductor specialist whose shares have declined by 4% year to date, while the S&P 500 is up by 10%. Broadcom has encountered some issues, but in my view, the market is severely underestimating the company's prospects. Here's why Broadcom could be a winner in 2027.

First, let's review Broadcom's role in the AI value chain. The company designs application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs), which, as their name suggests, are custom AI chips tailored to handle specific workloads, such as training or running AI models. Specialization can improve these custom chips' performance per watt and reduce computing costs, sometimes making them more cost-efficient than market-leading GPUs (graphics processing units).

Broadcom is one of the leaders in the ASIC market and has attracted business from several of the most prominent AI companies. The list includes Anthropic (ANT +17.07%), Alphabet (GOOG -2.93%) (GOOGL -3.46%), and Meta Platforms (META -3.27%). The company's financial results have been strong. However, the company's guidance has been somewhat disappointing in recent quarters, weighing on its stock price.

Further, Broadcom's AI semiconductor business is growing much faster than its higher-margin infrastructure software segments, which could lead to lower gross margins in the medium term. Add that to the fact that investors are still worried about the cyclicality of the semiconductor industry, and it's not too difficult to understand why Broadcom's shares haven't performed well this year. But the stock could bounce back in 2027.

Broadcom's guidance for the next two fiscal years looks strong. During fiscal year 2027, the company expects $115 billion in AI semiconductor revenue. Note that the tech leader has generated $89.1 billion in revenue over the trailing-12-month period across the entire business. For its fiscal year 2028, it expects AI semiconductor revenue to reach $230 billion.

The market has already factored that in, but Broadcom could surprise Wall Street. Several ongoing projects may reinforce Broadcom's position in the custom AI chips industry, potentially helping the company sign more long-term agreements. For instance, Broadcom is on track to deliver three generations of Meta's MTIA custom chips by the end of 2027.

Meta is pouring small fortunes into its AI ambitions and may extend its agreement with Broadcom as their current partnership continues to bring value to both companies. We can also point to the Anthropic (ANT +17.07%) IPO as another boost to Broadcom's business.

The company behind the Claude LLM (large language model) has contracted for billions in AI chips from Broadcom, and its agreement is mostly non-cancellable.

We may also see an expanding relationship between Broadcom and Anthropic, as the latter is planning a massive AI infrastructure build-out, and access to additional capital from its IPO will help fund some of it. Broadcom also has an agreement with OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT, which arguably started the ongoing AI revolution. We don't know when OpenAI will go public, but it may well happen next year, according to reports.

An OpenAI IPO may also boost Broadcom's business for the same reasons that the Anthropic IPO might. Broadcom's AI semiconductor revenue guidance isn't set in stone, and, provided it signs more deals, which is likely in my view, the company could increase it. And beyond 2027, Broadcom's outlook appears strong if the AI industry continues to expand rapidly, as some projections suggest.

Meanwhile, Broadcom is trading at reasonable levels. The company's shares are trading at 18.5x forward earnings, versus an average of 17.6x for information technology stocks. Given Broadcom's position in the custom AI chips market, its shares look attractive right now. It might be a good idea to buy the stock before 2027.
