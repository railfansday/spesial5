Should You Forget Nvidia and Buy These 2 AI Memory Stocks Instead?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Memory chips may be cyclical, but this cycle has a long way to go before wrapping up.

By Keithen Drury – Nov 21, 2026 at 8:03PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Micron Technology ›

Nvidia has grabbed all the headlines in recent weeks, mainly over what's going on with former CEO Jensen Huang. This has pummeled the stock, but there are other stocks benefiting from the same tailwinds as Nvidia that don't have the same headline risk right now.

One of the biggest investment opportunities in the market right now is in memory chip stocks, with Micron (MU -13.89%) and Sandisk (SNDK -16.05%) being the two best options. But are they better than Nvidia? Let's take a look.

There are many unknowns surrounding Nvidia right now. Whether it's questions about how much of its chip sales may be going to China through third parties, or whether all of the demand was legitimate, nobody knows. However, one point that's not up for debate is that demand for AI computing is real, and it has stretched memory chip suppliers to their limits.

Both Micron and Sandisk have sold out of production capacity, and it could be that way for many years, despite both companies opening new facilities in the near future to raise capacity. Micron's management team told investors that it expects memory chip market conditions to be worse in 2027 and 2028 than in 2026, an incredible statement, given how bad things are right now. This will keep memory chip prices elevated for some time, which is fantastic news for investors.

Both have a long history of being highly cyclical companies, with good times when demand is high and downturns when there is excess supply. Historically, these cycles don't last for much more than a year, and that's exactly what the market is expecting this time around.

However, with AI hyperscalers needing much more computing capacity, this build-out could last until 2030 or beyond.

That's a lot of time for both Sandisk and Micron to produce a ton of profits and reshape their futures, while also delivering huge investor returns on the way.

They are doing this by reshaping their business models. Sandisk and Micron are starting to sign long-term agreements with their clients, which sets a floor and a ceiling on memory prices. While this cuts out some of the peaks, it prevents these two from going into deep downturns when demand isn't as high. That sets their businesses up for long-term success.

Because the market is expecting a cyclical downturn any day now, each of these stocks is valued at a dirt cheap level.

With both stocks at about 4 times this year's projected earnings (both companies are already in fiscal year 2027), that's a huge discount to the broader market. Nvidia is also cheap at about 10 times next year's earnings, but that pales in comparison to these two.

This makes these two excellent options, but only if the market comes around to valuing them properly. If the market keeps these two at low valuations due to concerns about their cyclical nature, these stocks may be duds. However, I don't see that happening.

Both Sandisk and Micron have been repurchasing shares with their profits, which allows them to artificially boost earnings per share by decreasing the number of shares outstanding. This is nothing new and has been done successfully by many companies in the market.

With their share prices low relative to their profits, each dollar spent on buybacks is more effective. This could deliver strong long-term returns and lead to outperformance of Nvidia.

Although I think Nvidia will be OK over the long term, these two memory chip stocks may be a safer bet. Investors may want to see who the next CEO of Nvidia is before jumping back into the stock, and these two make for great alternatives with huge upside.
