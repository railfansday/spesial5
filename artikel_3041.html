Vodafone (VOD) Q2 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Germany restructuring weighed on results despite strong digital services growth.

Vodafone Group Public Limited Company (VOD -1.21%) reported accelerated service revenue growth of 6.4% in the second quarter and upgraded its full-year organic growth guidance for adjusted EBITDAaL and free cash flow. Management reported that the company is entering a new chapter focused on simplification and digital services expansion, supported by a 1 billion euro net cost-saving target by fiscal year 2030. The company completed strategic transactions to gain full ownership of its United Kingdom operations and increased its stake in Safaricom to strengthen its position in African fintech and connectivity markets. While growth accelerated in most segments, the company reported ongoing restructuring in Germany, including significant headcount reductions and an asset impairment driven by rising interest rates.
