Anthropic IPO Demand Surges as AI Trade Roars Back

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Traders piled back into the artificial intelligence trade, lifting US stock futures following strong results from beaten-down neocloud firm CoreWeave and robust demand for Anthropic’s initial public offering. Ukraine and Russia started a second day of US-led peace talks in Abu Dhabi after Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called the first round of discussions “constructive and quite substantive.” Climate negotiators from almost 200 countries have gathered for COP31 in Antalya, Turkey with a narrower agenda than in the past. There are concerns that ambition to tackle climate change is waning but the global energy supply crunch and growing AI backlash may spur policymakers into action. Today’s guests: Saira Malik, Nuveen CIO; Michael Mueller, RWE CFO; Nathan Blecharczyk, Airbnb Co-Founder & Chief Strategy Officer; Kai-Fu Lee, Sinovation Ventures Chairman & CEO & 01.AI CEO (Source: Bloomberg)
