David Croft: Max Verstappen has Fernando Alonso's race intelligence but is faster

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Sky Sports Formula 1 lead commentator David Croft sees Fernando Alonso's smart racing in Max Verstappen, but adds that the four-time champion has more speed.

Speaking on Sky Sports' F1 Show podcast after Verstappen's Las Vegas Grand Prix win, Croft was asked to sum up what he had seen from the Red Bull driver. He started by comparing him with the two-time champion who is still racing at 45.

Croft said Verstappen already has the quality that has defined Alonso's career, and then some. "Max has got what Fernando Alonso has got, but more pace to go with it," Croft explained. "And that's an ability to strategise and think your way through a race and know exactly what you're doing at all times. But he's actually faster than Fernando Alonso."

That ability was on show in the closing laps in Las Vegas, where Croft said Verstappen was happy to run second into the final lap rather than lead it. He felt Mercedes' George Russell had a different plan.

"That's why he went for the move on Kimi, because he wanted to then make the move on Max and be out in front. Max wanted to be second. It was almost like a cycling velodrome sprint race," he added.

Croft was in no doubt where that leaves Verstappen, even if he does not always approve of what the Dutchman says or does.

"He is just the all-round complete driver at the moment," he said. "And whatever you think about what he says, whatever you think about what he does sometimes on track, there is no doubting he is the current greatest driver on the track. And you're going to have to go some to beat him in equal machinery, which makes next year so fascinating."

Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli claimed the drivers' championship title at the Mexican Grand Prix, but Verstappen is fighting for second. The 29-year-old currently sits second with 304 points, with Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton in third with 267 points.
