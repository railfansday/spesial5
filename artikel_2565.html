Chris Braswell out for the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Houston Texans defensive end Chris Braswell (knee) was placed on the season-ending practice squad reserve/injured list Monday, Nov. 23, due to a knee injury suffered on the opening kickoff of the Week 11 game against the Indianapolis Colts on Thursday night.
