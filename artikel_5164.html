Charles Schwab Opens Live Solana, Avalanche and Chainlink Trading to Eligible Clients

Charles Schwab has moved three major altcoins from its expansion plan into its live crypto trading menu. The brokerage announced on November 9 that eligible Schwab Crypto clients can now trade Solana, Avalanche and Chainlink, alongside Bitcoin and Ethereum. The distinction between ‘planned’ and ‘available’ is the new milestone here.

TL;DR: Schwab Crypto now supports SOL, AVAX and LINK trading for eligible retail clients, adding to its BTC and ETH offering. Schwab says its service became broadly available to qualifying customers in October 2026. The announcement concerns direct crypto trading, not approval of new exchange-traded funds for these tokens.

Schwab began offering early access to its crypto service in May and opened it to eligible retail clients in October. The November addition broadens a product already running in the market, rather than announcing a future concept with no users. That gives the three altcoins a route to customers who prefer established brokerage infrastructure and account management over a separate crypto-native trading platform.

NewsBTC covered the earlier Schwab expansion plan in August. The new press release supplies the operational update those readers would have been waiting for: the named assets are available, subject to Schwab’s account and jurisdiction requirements.

This is an important qualification because access is not universal. Customers must qualify for the service, and conventional brokerage protections or product terms should never be assumed to apply identically to every digital-asset holding. Investors should read the published risk disclosures and custody arrangement rather than relying on the familiarity of the corporate brand.

Solana is a smart-contract network whose economic activity includes trading, payments and tokenized assets. Avalanche operates a different blockchain ecosystem with an emphasis on customizable network infrastructure. Chainlink is associated with data and connectivity services used by smart contracts. They are all crypto assets, but they are not interchangeable bets on a single technology.

That distinction helps explain why broadening a brokerage menu does not immediately establish institutional conviction in any particular network. A broker chooses instruments for a combination of liquidity, client interest, operational readiness and risk controls. Adding a symbol is a meaningful access decision; it is not a research conclusion that the asset must outperform.

For further ecosystem context, NewsBTC’s recent look at Solana’s network upgrades and tokenized-asset activity shows why a network’s technical evolution should be assessed separately from the price of a token on a brokerage screen.

The launch raises useful questions: how many customers will open or fund Schwab Crypto accounts, how much activity will flow into the new assets, and whether more tokens follow? The announcement does not provide day-one trading volumes or say how much demand each token will receive.

A larger, more familiar distribution channel could change the market over time by widening who can participate. But whether it deepens liquidity or simply moves existing traders from one provider to another will require actual usage data. For now, the verified news is an operational launch with a clearly defined set of supported coins.
