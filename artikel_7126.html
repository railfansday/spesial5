Dropbox: Director, Strategic AI Partnerships

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Dropbox Business Development team builds strategic partnerships that expand product adoption, create new distribution channels, and drive long-term customer and revenue growth. We are hiring a Director of Strategic AI Partnerships to own a focused portfolio of Dropbox’s highest-priority AI relationships, including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta.

You will define and execute multi-year strategies across product integration, distribution, enterprise GTM, commercial agreements, and joint marketing. The opportunity goes beyond maintaining individual integrations: you will identify where Dropbox and each partner can create differentiated customer value, align executives and product leaders around a small number of high-impact workstreams, and move those workstreams from concept through agreement, launch, adoption, and growth.

This role requires enough technical fluency to shape product discussions involving AI assistants and agents, native content experiences, connectors and MCP, authentication and account linking, model and platform capabilities, and governed access to customer content. You will work closely with Product, Engineering, Sales, Marketing, Finance, Legal, Communications, and senior executives to translate strategic ambition into clear owners, milestones, commercial terms, and measurable outcomes.

The role is based in the San Francisco Bay Area to support frequent in-person engagement with partner teams and Dropbox leadership. Success requires executive presence, product and commercial judgment, and the ability to create momentum in relationships where the opportunity is large but the path is not predetermined.
