Our Last Chance to Save This Country Begins Today. Let’s Not Waste It.

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Don’t even start thinking about the 2028 presidential election. Start thinking about what we’re going to do tomorrow and the day after that.

(Optional Video Accompaniment to This Post)

It is a decade now since the president came down the golden escalator and the country went straight to hell. All we’ve done as citizens of a self-governing democratic republic is squabble over the seating in the handbasket. Until Tuesday. Tuesday was nut-cutting time. Tuesday felt—probably rightly—like the beginning of an extended last chance that the country will have to spend every waking hour pursuing.

Just on Monday, the president closed out his demented road show with a weird address in front of one of his administration’s most conspicuous blunders: the Reflecting Pool. Meanwhile, the Supreme Court, with his carefully manufactured conservative majority, gave the boot to his latest attempt to ratfck the California elections, and the court did so without even a hint of an explanation. Then the president capped a rich, full day by bombing an Iranian island in the Persian Gulf, although I guess he went a little bananas on his little boutique website.

Mama said there’d be days like this. But she never said there’d be so many. And all the rest of them are on us. If I can offer one piece of advice it would be this: Ignore anything written that references the 2028 presidential election. If things break the way the polls say they will, there will be so much concentration required to do what we can to ameliorate the damage this administration has done and is capable of doing over the next two years. Starting the 2028 horse-race coverage this week would be yet another crime against poor, battered journalism. As the late Senator Edward Kennedy told a Democratic convention long ago:

Run through the tape, America. Finish the race.
