Kyndryl: VRO Lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

At Kyndryl, we design, build, manage and modernize the mission-critical technology systems that the world depends on every day. So why work at Kyndryl? We are always moving forward – always pushing ourselves to go further in our efforts to build a more equitable, inclusive world for our employees, our customers and our communities.

In the Value Realization Office at Kyndryl, you will play a critical role in ensuring that our customers achieve and maximize the tangible business outcomes promised in their digital transformation journeys. You will act as the strategic driver behind establishing, measuring, and protecting value across complex IT outsourcing, enterprise resource planning (ERP), and large-scale infrastructure engagements post-contract signature.

In this role, you will define and execute the VRO operating model from the ground up, establishing governance cadences, RACI frameworks, and executive escalation paths tailored to each engagement. You will partner closely with client finance and operations leaders to rigorously construct and validate operational baselines, translating overarching deal cases into transparent, actionable benefits registers equipped with defined KPIs, milestones, and auditable proof points.

Your financial acumen and analytical rigor will be central to defining robust measurement models for hard cost savings, risk and cost avoidance, productivity uplift, and service quality enhancements. By designing automated scorecards and dashboards, tracking realized gains, and diagnosing root causes behind value leakage or process drift, you will keep critical customer engagements on course. Furthermore, you will lead high-impact value reviews with C-suite stakeholders and Kyndryl account executives, turning proven realized value into a catalyst for account renewals, expansion, and long-term client advocacy.

Kyndryl’s mission to modernize enterprise IT and drive business transformation means that within the Value Realization Office, you will be positioned at the vital intersection of technology, finance, and client strategy. You will lead high-profile transformation initiatives, mentor and direct VRO analysts across concurrent engagements, and actively contribute to building reusable assets, benchmark repositories, and measurement standards that scale across our global practice.

Kyndryl currently does not require employees to be fully vaccinated against COVID-19, however, if you are hired to work at a client, customer, or partner location, you may be required to show proof of vaccination to align with their respective COVID-19 vaccination policies.  Those who believe they are eligible may apply for a medical or religious accommodation prior to the start of employment.

You are good at what you do and possess the proven consulting and financial depth to prove it. You bring a growth mindset, navigating complex transformation headwinds with clarity and decisive problem-solving. You are relentlessly customer-focused—committed to ensuring our clients see verifiable return on investment and financial transparency. Finally, you are empathetic, collaborative, and borderless, partnering seamlessly across organizational change management (OCM), delivery, and executive teams to build trust and achieve shared success.
