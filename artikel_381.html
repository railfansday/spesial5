The Apple Watch SE 3 has dropped to its best-ever price in Amazons Black Friday sale — save $50

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

SAVE $50: As of Nov. 19, the Apple Watch SE 3 is on sale for $199 at Amazon. That's a 20% discount on the list price.

Amazon's Black Friday sale is now officially underway, and if you've been thinking about upgrading your fitness tracker, the retailer has slashed $50 off the Apple Watch SE 3. The price is down from $249 to $199. Better still, this matches the lowest price we've seen for this model.

The deal is also available across two colorways: the starlight aluminum case with a sand sport band and the midnight aluminum case with a navy blue sport band.

The Apple Watch SE 3 is packed with health and fitness features, all in a more affordable package than Apple's other models. It can track everything from your daily steps and sleep to your running, swimming, and strength-training sessions. There's also temperature sensing, which provides insights via the Vitals and Cycle Tracking apps, along with sleep apnea notifications.

Battery life lasts up to 18 hours, but if you're running low, a quick 15-minute charge can give you up to eight hours of use. You'll also get built-in Siri, plus safety features such as fall and crash detection, which can help contact emergency services if you're involved in an accident.

This Apple Watch SE 3 deal is available in Amazon's Black Friday sale now.
