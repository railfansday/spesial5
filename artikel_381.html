The chaos of the holiday season is here. Early Black Friday deals are coming in hot, the neighbors have the Christmas lights up, and it's time to plan the Thanksgiving menu. As of Nov. 11, the De'Longhi Magnifica Duo automatic espresso machine is on sale at Amazon for $699.95, down from the list price of $999.95. That's a 30% discount and the lowest price we've ever seen at Amazon.

With a single touch, the Magnifica Duo can make 20 barista-style recipes like an iced latte, cappuccino, and cold brew. The built-in conical burr grinder means you'll be able to use freshly-ground coffee for each drink.

The automations on the machine mean you won't have to put in extra work to have a treat during the busy holiday season. It's also convenient if you'll be hosting guests. Instead of teaching them all how to use your coffee maker, they'll be able to follow the machine's prompts on the display to figure it out without any help.

Since this is the lowest price we've seen at Amazon on the De'Longhi Magnifica Duo, it might be worth shopping today instead of waiting for Black Friday. Plus, shopping this week means you'll have the machine ready to go well before Thanksgiving.
