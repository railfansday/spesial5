The Rings of Power cast on Sauron and Celeborns kiss, Gandalfs hat, and the forging of the One Ring

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power Season 3 wraps up today, and what a season it has been.

Season 3 teased the Witch-king of Angmar. It pulled deep from J.R.R. Tolkien's legendarium to reveal the Dark Wizard's (Ciarán Hinds) true identity. It introduced Celeborn (Jamie Campbell Bower) and promptly flung him into Sauron's (Charlie Vickers) torture chamber. But most importantly, this season saw the forging of the almighty One Ring in the fires of Mount Doom.

In a Say More interview with Mashable Entertainment Reporter Belen Edwards, cast members Vickers, Bower, Cynthia Addai-Robinson, and Daniel Weyman, as well as series co-creator Patrick McKay, broke down pivotal moments from the season, starting with the One Ring's forging.

"It was two weeks of hammering," Vickers joked of filming the scene.

Extensive hammering aside, the sequence is a fiery symphony in which Sauron gives himself wholly to dark creation. Lava-drenched and menacing, it's wholly unlike the forging of any of the other rings of power we've seen onscreen in the series so far. For showrunners McKay and J.D. Payne, that was the goal.

"We wanted it to be more than he's making a weapon or a magical object," McKay said. "He's changing as a character forever. The world is changing in this moment."

In the interview, Bower and Vickers also discussed Sauron and Celeborn's surprise kiss. Plus, Robinson dug into Míriel's decision to marry Pharazôn (Trystan Gravelle). Be sure to stick around until the end for Weyman's moving discussion of what it means for Gandalf to don his iconic hat for the first time. It may leave your eyes mistier than the Misty Mountains.
