It’s November, and that means the holidays are just around the corner. We’re still a few weeks away from Black Friday, but the CVS started putting up Christmas decorations on November 1, and just about every brand and retailer is warming up their holiday deal engines.

Of course, Black Friday is still the biggest shopping day of the year. November 27 will bring tremendous deals on everything, including all the tech gifts on your shopping list. If you’re looking to give someone a new laptop (or need one yourself), you’ll likely find something special.

But if you’re ready to start shopping now, I’ve been checking around, and some great early Black Friday deals are already in effect. Whether you’re buying for a die-hard Mac fan, a gamer looking for a graphics upgrade, or a savvy Windows user, these systems all deliver on performance and design. The tech lover in you will be thrilled, and you won’t have to pay a premium to get them the gear they love.
