Arrests Made After Break-in at Prince William, Kate Middleton's Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Prince William and Kate Middleton’s Sandringham estate was allegedly broken into Nov. 23. Two men have been arrested in connection with the incident.

Prince William and Kate Middleton’s estate had a scary ordeal.

Two men were arrested after allegedly breaking into the Prince and Princess of Wales’ country home in Sandringham, England, Nov. 23, the Norfolk Constabulary confirmed to E! News.

“Police were called just before 6pm following a report of suspicious activity at the address,” local police authority said in the statement. “Officers attended and specialist resources, including police dogs, drones and the National Police Air Service helicopter, were deployed to assist with searches in the area.”

The statement continued, “Two men were arrested on the estate on suspicion of burglary and trespass. They remain in police custody. The property was unoccupied at the time of the incident. Inquiries remain ongoing.”

The family—including Kate and William’s three kids Prince George, 13, Princess Charlotte, 11, and Prince Louis, 8—was not present at the time of the break-in, E! News can confirm.

But it wouldn’t be the first time the royals have experienced a home invasion. In 2024, Burglars allegedly trespassed on the grounds of Windsor Castle, where William and Kate had been living in their primary residence Adelaide Cottage, police confirmed to NBC News at the time.

Thames Valley Police told the outlet that burglars entered a farm building and "made off with a black Isuzu pickup and a red quad bike" before leaving for a nearby town.

They added in their statement, “No arrests have been made at this stage and an investigation is ongoing.”

Today, Kate and William’s main home is Forest Lodge in Windsor, where they moved for a “fresh start” in 2025 after the death of Queen Elizabeth II and Kate’s cancer diagnosis.

“Windsor has become their home,” a royal source previously told NBC News of the move. “However, over the last few years while they have lived at Adelaide Cottage there have been some really difficult times.”

They added, “Moving gives them an opportunity for a fresh start and a new chapter; an opportunity to leave some of the more unhappy memories behind. This is a move for the long term. They see it as their forever home.”
