Champions League to be a factor in Paz move - Saturday's gossip

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Manchester United may lose the chance to sign Nico Paz without Champions League qualification, Arsenal also want the Como midfielder and Liverpool eye Lamine Camara.

Manchester United are still among the favourites to sign Como midfielder Nico Paz, 22, who has said he does not want to return to former club Real Madrid - but Champions League qualification could be a factor in the Argentine's decision. (The Athletic - subscription required), external

However, United could be upstaged by Arsenal with Mikel Arteta reportedly very keen to bring Paz to the Emirates. (ESPN Argentina via Goal), external

Liverpool are hopeful of securing a January move for Monaco midfielder Lamine Camara, 22, with Chelsea likely to be out of contention after the Senegal international's failed move to Stamford Bridge in the summer. (GiveMeSport), external

Everton are monitoring Cagliari forward Paul Mendy, 19, but the Senegalese teenager is also attracting interest from Inter Milan. (TeamTalk), external

Liverpool, Brighton, Bayern Munich, and Barcelona could enter a bidding war for Borussia Monchengladbach's 17-year-old German midfielder Wael Mohya in the summer. (TalkSport), external

Arsenal are leading the race for Club Brugge and Portugal winger Carlos Forbs, 22, but will likely have to wait until the end of the season. (Football Insider), external

Chelsea are interested in signing Velez defender Simon Escobar, 17, but face competition from Brighton for the young Argentine. (TeamTalk), external

Brighton are considering terminating English midfielder Harry Howell's loan deal at Leicester City as they are becoming frustrated with the 18-year-old's lack of game time. (Football Insider), external
