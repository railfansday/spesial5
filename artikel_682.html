Rob Lowe Asked Ben Shapiro to Cast Him in ‘Confession’: “I’ve Worked With Left-Wing Writers. Why Wouldn’t I Work With Right-Wing Writers?”

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Daily Wire founder recently said he told the actor he was "way too handsome" to take on the role.

Rob Lowe was ready to sign on to star in Ben Shapiro‘s Confession after he read the “fantastic” script written by the Daily Wire founder.

The Emmy nominee went on the Second Thought podcast Tuesday, where he recounted how he got involved with the film. He started, “I read the script; it was written by Ben Shapiro. I called Ben and said, ‘This is fantastic.’ And he was like, ‘Well, do you think you know anybody 50-ish, leading man who would not be scared just because I wrote it?’ And I go, ‘Yeah, me.’”

“And he goes, ‘You’re good with this?’ I said, ‘I’ve worked with the most left-wing writers in the world. Why wouldn’t I also work with the most right-wing writers in the world?’” Lowe added. During a recent appearance on the Conversations With Coleman podcast on Saturday, Shapiro said that the actor called him and said he was “deeply affected by the script.” When he asked if he could play the lead part, Shapiro said he replied by saying, “You’re Rob Lowe. If you ask to play a part, the answer is yes, but I’m just telling you you’re way too handsome for this. Like, you’re way too good-looking for this part.”

Of Confession, Lowe said the movie was “turned down by every single film festival.” He added, “Summarily frog-marched out of any conversation. Now, this is a movie with me, Nick Nolte, James Franco, Emile Hirsch. I see what they’re picking up, and it’s all political.”

“The thought would be we’d play in a film festival, we would get some buzz, never happened. And then you know Netflix would pick it up or this one would pick — crickets. Absolute crickets,” Lowe said. “So then it defaults to the Daily Wire to be the distributor, and I’m like, hell yeah. I want people to see this movie. And good for them for putting it out. But that wasn’t originally going to be the life of the movie.”

Confession was directed by Václav Marhoul and centers on the interrogation of a prisoner in a Stalin-era Russian jail.
