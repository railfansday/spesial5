Run the Big Sur International Marathon With Runner’s World

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

RW+ members can enter for a chance at one of seven free guaranteed entries to the 2027 race.

The Big Sur International Marathon presented by HOKA, taking place Sunday, April 25, 2027, offers a one-of-a-kind race experience along California’s stunning coastline. Following a point-to-point route on Highway 1 from Big Sur to Carmel, runners take in towering redwoods, dramatic cliffs, sweeping Pacific Ocean views, and iconic landmarks such as the Bixby Bridge.

While random drawing results for the 2027 race were announced in September, Runner’s World+ members have another chance to join the fun this spring.
