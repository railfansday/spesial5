3 strikers West Ham should target in January as Scott Parker sets new talisman demand

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

West Ham have the busy December schedule to see to before the January transfer window comes around, but Scott Parker is already intent on improving his squad. And, he’s identified two areas where he wants to sign new players: striker and left-back.

The centre-forward has been a position of contention for WHUFC, despite the fact they are the league’s top scorers with a remarkable 37 goals in 15 games. None of Joel Piroe, Pablo or Taty Castellanos have proven they can find consistent goalscoring form, and the latter reportedly wants to leave the London Stadium.

With that in mind, we put forward three strikers West Ham should target to resolve their dilemma.

Parker is said to prefer Championship experience, so who better than someone who is already starring in the division? Andri Gudjohnsen has been in scintillating form for Blackburn Rovers, with a goals per shot rate of 0.50 ranking him among the most clinical finishers in Europe this season.

The 24-year-old has scored 11 goals in 15 games this term and has starred on the international stage for Iceland too. Gudjohnsen scored four in two games over the November break.

A fast and powerful forward with smart movement and finishing, Gudjohnsen could come in and lead the line for West Ham and he’d be backed to make the step up to the Premier League too. It would not be a cheap signing, but he could be just what Parker needs.

Looking slightly further afield, one man who could catch the Hammers’ eye is Paulos Abraham. The 24-year-old was highly rated in his youth but a spell at Dutch side FC Groningen didn’t go as hoped, resulting in his return to Sweden with Hammarby IF.

Since returning to the Allsvenskan in January 2025, he has been one of the league’s most dangerous forwards. 17 goals in 28 league games this season take Abraham to 34 goals in 74 outings for the club, and he was rewarded with his first Sweden call-up earlier this season.

Abraham would bring electric pace to West Ham’s frontline, and his inventive finishing sees him score from unlikely situations. His ability to run beyond Championship defences could bring a new threat to a highly creative WHUFC outfit.

Last but not least is certainly the most ambitious of the lot. However, Aleksandar Mitrovic has had a slightly disrupted recent spell in the Middle East due to a ligament injury and may be tempted to return to London under the lead of Parker again.

The Serbian powerhouse played and starred under the new West Ham boss at Fulham, and he would be a dream addition for Parker. Mitrovic is one of the most prolific forwards in Championship history with 85 goals in 126 games at the level and he would certainly fit the bill as a prolific scorer who can lead the Hammers to promotion.

The main obstacle is obvious though. Mitrovic is on massive money in Qatar with Al-Rayyan, and even if WHUFC can persuade the club to let him leave, the 32-year-old might not be minded to leave his sizeable contract.

But, if the opportunity was there, Mitrovic would be a perfect solution for Parker’s striker issue.
