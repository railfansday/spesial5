League One clubs target Linfield winger amid impressive form

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Rhys Brennan’s form has attracted the attention of League One sides, who face competition from teams in the SPL. At this moment in time, none of the admirers are named in O’Rourke’s report.

Brennan, 20, is predominantly a right winger. He is out on loan with Linfield away from Bohemian, where his contract runs out next December.

Bohemian currently sit 3nd in the Irish Premier Division, whilst Linfield top the Northern Irish Premiership, having won 12 of their 16 matches so far. Brennan has contributed well to their success, locking down a starting berth on the flanks.

Brennan broke into the Bohemian side in 2025 and has since developed on loan at Cobh Ramblers before Linfield. A move to England would constitute a major step in Rhys Brennan’s career, should it take place.

Brennan’s debut for Bohemian came against Waterford at the end of the 2024 campaign. He soon followed that up with a first goal the following Monday, scoring as his side overturned a 2-0 deficit to win 3-2 against Shamrock Rovers.

Brennan has also featured for the Republic of Ireland U19s. The winger made his debut for the side away against Slovakia in a 3-1 friendly victory back in March 2024.

The young winger is an energetic presence on the wing. He’s shown he can offer a dual threat of goals and assists, but his crossing ability and work rate is where he stands out.

Furthermore, Brennan is a confident presence on the wing. He leads the way for Linfield with regard to successful dribbles per match this season, averaging 1.9 successful dribbles per 90.

This season is the first season where Brennan is fully flourishing in senior football. Should he make the move to England, he might need time to adapt given the step up from Northern Ireland to League One.

Brennan will not walk into a side challenging for promotion and integrate himself with regular minutes. Equally, a relegation battle would not be beneficial for his career.

A move to a mid-table side seems safe, giving Brennan a low pressure environment to develop and express himself on the wings.

Linfield will be reluctant to lose Rhys Brennan this season. However, should the player’s form continue, he may be a serious candidate for a move in the January transfer window, and a decision will ultimately be made by his parent club Bohemian.
