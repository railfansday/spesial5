'I'm not a quitter': Kim Kardashian on retaking the bar exam

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Kim Kardashian has said she will retake the California bar exam with the aim of becoming a lawyer in real life, after playing one in the legal drama All's Fair.

The reality TV star and actress discovered she had failed the exam after the first series of the Hulu programme aired last year. She had previously studied law for six years.

"I'll take it again and let you guys know once I've passed. I'll be very vocal about that," Kardashian told the BBC's Annabel Rackham, adding: "I'm going to be a little more quiet about my journey until I get there."

She was speaking alongside co-star Naomi Watts ahead of the second series of All's Fair, which was roundly panned by critics despite breaking streaming records.
