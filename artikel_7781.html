The holiday season brings warmth, magic, and, often, a welcome touch of festive cheer to our homes, but for those navigating small floor plans or packed closets, the aftermath can quickly turn into a post-holiday headache. Finding room for bulky holiday decorations can quickly take the joy out of this time of year. Fortunately, you can find ways to set the scene in a way that doesn’t require taking up too much of your precious storage space. From collapsible, fold-flat Christmas trees and re-plantable living trees to accents straight from your backyard or the grocery store, there are plenty of clever ways to celebrate big without cluttering your home.

One way to save space after the holiday is to plant your tree in your own backyard. By choosing a living Christmas tree, you’ll save space, reduce waste, and build memories that last for years to come. A living Christmas tree is sold with its roots still attached, often called a “balled and burlapped” tree. Not only will a living tree fill your home with a fresh pine scent, but replanting it provides environmental benefits, such as minimizing waste, reducing deforestation, providing wildlife habitat, and sequestering carbon. You’ll be able to extend your holiday joy by incorporating replanting the tree as part of your celebration with friends and family, and you’ll be able to enjoy it throughout the year. Look for a living Christmas tree at nurseries, garden centers, online retailers like Lynch Creek Farm and Trees.com, and specialized tree farms.

Minimize how much space you need to store holiday decorations by purchasing some that inflate or pop up. This Pop Up Christmas Tree, for example, is a smart choice. No assembly is necessary, and it comes fully decorated with Christmas balls, ribbons, bows, and lights already in place. The stand collapses and the tree folds flat, so you can easily tuck it away once the holiday ends. You can also look for inflatables like trees, gift boxes, and snowmen that can be used inside or outside. Once deflated, they’ll be easier to store.

Tapping into nature is one of the easiest ways to decorate for the holidays without accumulating endless bins of off-season storage. By foraging organic materials right from your own yard or neighborhood, you can create a naturally elegant scene. Beyond being completely free and eco-friendly, natural décor easily transitions across seasons or can be safely returned to the earth once the festivities end by adding items to a compost bin, shredding branches into garden mulch, using twigs to kindle a fire in your fireplace or fire pit, creating winter backyard shelters for birds, or setting them out for municipal tree recycling.
