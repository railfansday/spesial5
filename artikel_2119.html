West Ham Exclusive: Parker’s Promotion Clause And Nuno’s £3m Payoff Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Scott Parker is expected to sign a two-and-a-half-year contract with West Ham United this afternoon, with Claret & Hugh receiving fresh information about the proposed deal and the compensation package for departing manager Nuno Espírito Santo.

A club insider briefed C&H within the last few moments, revealing that Parker’s agreement includes break clauses linked directly to whether West Ham secure promotion back to the Premier League at the first attempt. We have also been informed that Nuno is set to receive just under £3million in compensation following his dismissal this morning.

The details represent a significant development following our earlier exclusive identifying Parker as the leading candidate to replace the Portuguese coach. The former Hammers midfielder now appears close to completing his return to the London Stadium, although the appointment has yet to be officially announced.

It will coast West Ham just under £3m to sack Rui Pedro Silva & Nuno Espirito Santo

According to our source, Parker has been more than happy to accept the proposed terms, with a genuine desire within the club to see him succeed.

Speaking to Claret & Hugh within the last few moments, the insider explained:

“Parker will need to get West Ham up this season. If he doesn’t, he’ll be released without compensation. If he gets promoted to the Premier League, it will activate a two-year deal.”

In effect, Parker’s longer-term future at West Ham will depend upon achieving promotion this season.

Failure to return to the Premier League would allow the club to part company with the manager without paying compensation, according to the information we have received.

Promotion, however, would activate the remaining two years of his contract.

Claret & Hugh have also been informed that Parker will be given the opportunity to manage West Ham in the Premier League should he secure promotion.

That assurance is particularly interesting given his dismissal by Burnley last season, despite having previously guided the Clarets into the top flight.

Parker’s experience of winning promotion with Fulham, Bournemouth and Burnley appears to have been a significant factor in West Ham’s interest.

The Hammers hierarchy are evidently prioritising a manager with a proven Championship promotion record, while protecting the club financially should their objectives not be achieved.

Meanwhile, Nuno’s departure, confirmed earlier this morning, is expected to cost West Ham just short of £3million in compensation.

The proposed agreement with Parker would therefore represent a very different financial arrangement, with the club’s promotion ambitions written directly into the terms of his employment.

An official announcement confirming Parker’s appointment is still awaited.
