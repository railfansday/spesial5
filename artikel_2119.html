Coventry & Stoke fans notice 'disrespectful' thing Mark Robins has been spotted doing

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Stoke City are flying in the EFL Championship now, with Mark Robins' side sitting third in the table heading into the November international break.

The Potters started the season with three straight defeats, but have lost just once in the Championship since August.

A 2-1 win over Portsmouth at the weekend was their third win on the spin, following victories against Preston North End and Blackburn Rovers.

Stoke's win at Portsmouth was a big one, particularly with Robins' side falling behind in only the sixth minute of the game to Tyrese Shade's goal.

Jack McGlynn equalised just before the hour, with the turnaround complete on 77 minutes after Ben Wilmot's goal.

Footage emerged of Robins showing his thanks to the travelling Stoke fans at Fratton Park post-match, with the 56-year-old giving some celebratory fist pumps towards the away end, before reminding the camera it's now five wins on the spin on the road - the first time the Potters have achieved that since 1947.

The controversy around the fist pumps comes from the Coventry City fans, with @JohnSmithtkdy pointing out that during his spells in-charge of the Sky Blues - the latest of which was 2017-2024 where he kick-started the Sky Blues' rise back through the EFL - he wouldn't celebrate like that.

@LeeDeeTicks backed up the statement, stating how Frank Lampard started that tradition straight away with the Sky Blues: "A bit harsh but I hear it. Super Frank did it on his first game with us 🩵"

@mrtjnss also responded, albeit with a touch or irony in their tone: "Yeah personally i cant believe it. Totally disrespectful. Devastated."

The Stoke fans also had something to say, including @ShaneeScFc, who wanted to point out how Coventry fans might seem 'plastic': "Cause he’s infront of a proper fan base and not a set of plastics who decided to turn up 5 years ago 👀"

@scfcprims pointed out how good the Stoke fans have been: "Because our fans deserved it. Packed out every away end and unbelievable support."

Without sounding too much like Roy Keane, celebrations in football have changed.

There was a time when managers wouldn't dream of fist pumping to the fans after a win (it's their job), but it's also now a big part of the game and managers up and down the country, whatever the level, do this kind of thing.

A fair few managers are still an exception to the rule, and before seeing this footage, you'd have thought Robins would be one of those. It doesn't really seem his style to be geeing up the crowd after a win, and in fairness here, it's quite a tame attempt.

That said, as he points out, his side have just matched a record that's stood since 1947, and the fans have made a long trip to Portsmouth. They deserve seeing their manager enjoying a win - just as Coventry do every time Lampard is celebrating three points in the Premier League.
