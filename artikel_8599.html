Recent release from Dylan:

Bob Dylan - The Rolling Thunder Revue 1976: Fort Worth Texas, May 16th - 2026


Hard Rain, recorded on the same tour and released that same year, was one of my favorite Dylan albums when I was in high school. In recent years it has kind of lost some of its luster for me. Great band, kind of raucous renditions of great classics which I certainly appreciated at the time, but something about Dylan's singing strikes me now as kind of bored with material. Anyway, saw that this came out and the first tune I sampled was Going, Going, Gone, a gem from Planet Waves (a masterpiece in its own right). I loved it. Lots of new lyrics to chew on! Wonder how many were added on the fly? Scarlet Rivera's violin adds a lot for me.

Going, Going, Gone (Live at Tarrant County Convention Center Arena, Fort Worth, TX - May 16, 1976) by Bob Dylan
