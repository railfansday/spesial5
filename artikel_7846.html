It pairs 27,000 Pa of suction with a self-washing roller mop and a bagless dock that empties the dust bin on its own.

For smaller budgets, the Shark AI Ultra Robot Vacuum with Self-Empty Base for $259.99 (was $549.99) and the Eufy C28 Robot Vacuum and Mop for $379.99 (was $799.99) are both 53% off, and robot vacuums start at $199.99.

Ecovacs’ newest flagship robot vacuum and mop delivers 27,000 Pa of suction for pulling dirt and pet hair out of carpets. On hard floors, its OZMO Roller 3.0 mop rinses itself continuously as it scrubs, and FocusJet sprays tough stains before mopping. The bagless OmniCyclone dock empties the robot without dust bags and cleans the mop with hot water.

This Roborock robot vacuum and mop takes on crumbs and debris with powerful suction, while a warm-water spinning mop tackles sticky spills and dried stains. The multifunctional dock empties the dust bin, washes the mop with hot water, and dries it with warm air, so both the robot and your floors stay clean with little effort.

At 53% off, this self-emptying robot vacuum is a budget-friendly way to automate everyday vacuuming. It maps your home for room-by-room cleaning in the app and returns to its bagless base to empty its bin, so you don’t have to empty it after every run.

Most of these models both vacuum and mop, and many come with a dock that empties the dust bin and washes the mop pads. Before buying, check the threshold height the robot can climb and whether the dock needs a water hookup or uses refillable tanks.
