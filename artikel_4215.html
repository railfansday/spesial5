Japanese Charts: Switch 2 Sales Get A Nice Bump Ahead Of Zelda: Ocarina Of Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

We are back with another look at the Japanese charts, courtesy of Famitsu, and it has been another big week for Nintendo.

On the software side of things, Nintendo Switch Sports Resort continues to lead the charge, holding onto its pole position from last week with another ~23,000 units to its name. The Switch 2 edition of Minecraft also makes its chart debut this time, grabbing a podium spot at third.

Elsewhere, Final Fantasy Resonance continues to perform well on both Switch 2 and PS5, and Hello Kitty Party Land has made its chart debut in seventh and eighth on Switch 2 and 1 respectively.

The Nintendo big hitter this week comes on the hardware end, where Switch 2 has seen a decent bump from the ~mid-20,000s we usually see to a meaty 68,733 sales. We can only assume that this is thanks to the swanky new Zelda Special Edition model and in anticipation of Ocarina of Time, which, when these numbers were recorded, was only a matter of days away.

The PS5 family once again wins the combined SKU race this week, coming in with a total of 16,235, while the Switch 1 followed behind with 5,853 weekly sales.
