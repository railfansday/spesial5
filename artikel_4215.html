How To Repair Jason's Hideout In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Gotta make the place look nice for the big homecoming.

At the start of Grand Theft Auto 6, when Jason gets a call from Lucia saying she's getting out of prison, he realizes there's a lot to do around the house to get it ready for her arrival. He notices the leaks in the roof, and it turns out that there's plenty more to do to get the place in shape.

While these tasks are optional, they will make Lucia happy and earn you points in your relationship. While they're relatively easy quick-time events, it's important to do them so Lucia feels welcome as soon as she's finally out of the slammer.

As all homeowners know, the work is never truly done to make the place perfectly livable. Jason quickly learns that, having to fix numerous problems, but it's worth it if it makes Lucia feel at home. He has to fix the roof, the railing by the gym equipment, the front stairs, and, most importantly, the bedroom.

Here is where to locate the giant crab, get the Crabshack VF5, and unlock the safe in GTA 6.

To fix up Jason's roof, head out the front door of the house, and you'll see a yellow ladder leaning against the wall to the right of the door. Climb up and walk over to the gap in the shingles. You'll get a prompt to interact with them, and you'll have to nail them on by pulling the trigger when prompted.

To repair the stairs, go to the bottom of the front steps, which end right by where Jason parks his car. At the bottom, you'll see a large pile of boards sitting next to the stairs, and when they aren't fixed, you'll notice they creak when you walk on them.

Interact with the boards to fix the stairs. Pull off the old steps by pulling the triggers, then use the right trigger to nail them down properly.

To fix the railing, go to where Jason has his squat rack set up on the deck. You'll see a gap in the slats, and when you're standing at the level of the workout equipment, you'll get a prompt to repair it.

Make sure you're on the lower level of the deck, as the prompt won't appear from the top.

Jason will then start taking the wooden posts and nailing them in place; you can do this by pulling the trigger when prompted. After a few of these, the railing will look nice and neat for Lucia.

Possibly one of the more memed parts of the early game so far, Jason needs to fix up the bedroom to make it feel like home. Go into the bedroom, go to the panel of drywall leaning against the wooden frame and interact with the prompt to start the repair.

First, he'll fit the drywall, and you'll need to use the trigger to nail the wall into place. Then, after a cutscene of him filling those nail holes, you'll get to choose the color. Once you have, you'll be prompted to move the joystick up and down to paint the walls and finish the room.

After those four tasks are completed, Lucia will feel much more at home once she gets there, which will make her happy. Of course, that, along with adding a bit of Feng Shui, will make the place really start to feel like home.
