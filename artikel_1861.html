Manchester United ready to move for Rayan Aït-Nouri

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

According to Florian Plettenberg of Sky Sports Germany, Rayan Aït-Nouri is a player to watch ahead of the January transfer window, with the left-back reportedly open to leaving Manchester City. The Algeria international is said to be assessing his options as his representatives explore the market.

Having been linked with Aït-Nouri towards the end of this year’s summer transfer window, Manchester United are interested in signing the 25-year-old and would be prepared to move if a deal became feasible. Personal terms are not believed to be a major obstacle, with the player’s camp understood to be receptive to the possibility of a switch to Old Trafford.

However, the biggest complication surrounds the relationship between the two Manchester clubs. Any negotiations between City and United would be highly sensitive, making a potential transfer difficult despite the apparent interest from the Red Devils.

For now, Aït-Nouri’s future remains unresolved, but his situation is expected to attract significant attention as January approaches. With his market currently being explored, further developments could emerge over the coming weeks.
