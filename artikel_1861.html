Report: Liverpool looking to recreate Jeremy Jacquet success

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Liverpool are preparing to action their transfer plans this winter window.

The Reds have spent over £650 million overhauling the squad since Arne Slot's Premier League title win, and now it is Andoni Iraola that sits in the dugout.

After a steady but somewhat underwhelming start to the season, the recruitment team at Anfield are now assessing what business they can do in the January window to bring the club back level with Arsenal and Manchester City.

Several players find themselves shortlisted for a Merseyside move, but according to a new report Liverpool have leapfrogged ahead of a Premier League rival in the race for an exciting 21-year-old.

Ismaelo Ganiou has been one of the most promising young defenders in France since he broke into Lens' team last season.

The central defender was so impressive that when Pierre Sage was appointed Crystal Palace manager in the summer he tried to bring Ganiou with him to London.

A move never came off however, and the Burkina Faso international was rewarded with a new contract by Lens in August.

Now, Liverpool have joined the race for his signature.

That's according to a report from Football Insider, which claims Liverpool have joined the list of clubs monitoring Ganiou and have leapfrogged Palace in their pursuit.

With Sage now sacked, the main driving force of Palace's interest is gone, but that doesn't mean the Reds will have a clear run at the defender.

The report claims that Ganiou's impressive performances both this season and last have attracted 'significant attention' and there will no doubt be other clubs ready to challenge for his signature.

With a contract running through to 2030, Lens are in a very strong position when it comes to negotiating a price for Ganiou, who is valued at €36.4 million (~£30.5m) by FootballTransfers.

Ganiou would be a smart addition for Liverpool, who would be looking to recreate the success they found in signing Jeremy Jacquet from Ligue 1, but only time will tell whether the 21-year-old ends up at Anfield.
