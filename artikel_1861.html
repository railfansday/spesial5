Prediction: Chelsea vs Leeds United

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Chelsea return to Stamford Bridge on Saturday afternoon aiming to bounce back from a disappointing defeat before the international break when they host Leeds United in the Premier League.

The two clubs already served up an entertaining encounter earlier this season, with Chelsea coming from behind to secure a dramatic 6-3 victory in the EFL Cup. This latest meeting could prove equally important, as only two points separate the sides in the league table.

Chelsea entered the international break with renewed questions to answer after a mixed run of results under Xabi Alonso.

The Blues had recovered from a surprise home defeat to Tottenham Hotspur by beating Manchester United at Stamford Bridge, only to suffer a disappointing loss away to Sunderland in their final match before the break.

Alonso’s side have collected five wins, one draw and four defeats from their opening 10 Premier League fixtures, leaving them fifth in the table. While they remain firmly in the hunt for a top-four finish, the race is becoming increasingly congested, with just three points separating Chelsea from 10th-placed Aston Villa.

As veteran midfielder Jordan Henderson pointed out, consistency will be the key for the Blues’ prospects between now and the end of the season.

A return to Stamford Bridge should provide encouragement. Chelsea have accumulated 10 of their 16 league points at home and have scored 13 goals in front of their own supporters, the second-highest home tally in the division.

The Blues also boast an impressive recent record against Leeds on home soil, winning eight of their last 10 meetings at Stamford Bridge across all competitions. However, they were held to a 2-2 draw in this fixture last season after surrendering a two-goal advantage.

Reece James, Maxence Lacroix and Jamie Gittens remain unavailable through injury.

There is hope that Joao Pedro could return after missing the last four matches due to a thigh problem, while Moises Caicedo, Jorrel Hato and Josh Acheampong are all fitness doubts.

Goalkeeper Emiliano Martinez is expected to return after recovering from a broken jaw and representing Argentina during the international break.

Morgan Rogers was one of Chelsea’s standout performers in the EFL Cup victory over Leeds, providing four assists, while Cole Palmer and Danny Welbeck both scored twice. However, Welbeck may start on the bench, with Alonso potentially favouring either Estevao Willian or Pedro Neto in a central attacking role.

Leeds arrive in West London in significantly better spirits after ending a difficult spell before the international break.

Daniel Farke’s side had gone five matches without a victory before recording an important 1-0 win over Tottenham Hotspur at Elland Road. Noah Okafor’s late strike secured all three points and lifted Leeds into ninth place, just two points behind Chelsea.

The victory continued an encouraging home run, but Leeds have found life much tougher away from Elland Road. They have won only one of their last eight league fixtures on their travels, drawing four and losing three.

A demanding sequence of away matches now awaits, with trips to Chelsea, Manchester City, Liverpool and Aston Villa on the horizon.

There are reasons for optimism, though. Leeds are unbeaten in their last two league meetings with Chelsea, following up a 3-1 home victory with a 2-2 draw at Stamford Bridge. They now have the chance to go three consecutive league games without defeat against the Blues for the first time since 2001.

Speaking to the press ahead of the game, Farke was full of praise for the quality in Chelsea’s ranks.

Mateo Joseph continues to recover from a serious knee injury and remains unavailable.

Anton Stach is a doubt after missing the victory over Tottenham with a broken toe.

Farke could continue with a front two consisting of Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Lukas Nmecha, while Ethan Ampadu is expected to feature in midfield.

Noah Okafor will be pushing for a starting role after scoring from the bench against Spurs, while Harry Wilson, Brendan Aaronson and Joe Rodon are all contenders to return to the lineup.

Chelsea’s recent performances have lacked consistency, and the potential absence of a natural focal point in attack has reduced their threat in front of goal.

Leeds arrive with renewed confidence after their victory over Tottenham and have shown in recent meetings that they can cause problems for the Blues. However, their away form remains a concern, particularly against a Chelsea side that generally performs much better at Stamford Bridge.

It should be a close contest, but Chelsea’s superior attacking quality and strong home record give them the edge.
