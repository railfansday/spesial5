Leeds United handed boost ahead of Chelsea clash as Xabi Alonso confirms trio of absences

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Chelsea will have to do without a trio of key players when they host Leeds United tomorrow (November 21).

The old rivals will meet again at Stamford Bridge having done battle in the Carabao Cup in August. Leeds will be out to avenge their 6-3 loss, while Chelsea will be keen to break into the Premier League’s top four with a victory.

Many will fancy Chelsea to claim three points but the Blues will not be at full strength when Daniel Farke’s side visit.

Influential forward Joao Pedro is the headline absentee, with the 25-year-old yet to return to training after picking up a thigh problem.

Alonso said: “He’s not yet fit to train with the team. We need to wait a bit more. He’s not going to be available for Leeds. We need to see day by day how he is feeling.”

Alongside the Brazilian on the absentee list is defender Maxence Lacroix, who is a hamstring problem and is in a “similar situation” to Pedro.

There will also be no place in the squad for Netherlands international defender Jorrel Hato, who has a back injury.

Alonso said: “Jorrel has a back problem. We need to be patient because it’s going to take a while.”

Chelsea will, however, be able to start Moises Caicedo despite the midfielder’s withdrawal at half-time in a recent friendly for Ecuador. Defender Josh Acheampong is also fit and available to face the Whites, as is goalkeeper Emiliano Martinez.

Regarding the latter, Alonso said: “He is in the squad and he is selectable. Against Sunderland two weeks ago probably there was risk, and the plan was for him to train more with Argentina and to play one game. It went well so he’s available for tomorrow.”

Chelsea have not yet welcomed defender Reece James back to action and although they have brought winger Jamie Gittens back in, he requires time to reach full fitness.

Alonso said: “Reece is getting there. He’s taking a step forward, but still needs some weeks.

“Jamie Gittens is better. He’s already training with the team, but we want to give him more time until he comes back in the squad.”
