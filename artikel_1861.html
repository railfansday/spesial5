Seattle ready to ‘suffer’ against RSL to win Wild Card match

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Seattle Sounders head coach Brian Schmetzer is ready to "suffer" in the MLS Wild Card matchup with Real Salt Lake, though he has confidence his team can advance.

The Sounders finished eighth in the Western Conference thanks to their Decision Day victory over Los Angeles FC as they held on to secure a 2-1 win.

That result followed a run of five without a win (D2 L3) in MLS, which stopped them from securing an automatic spot in the tightly packed table.

Seattle also had to endure a club-record winless run in midseason that had hampered their playoff push, but Schmetzer says his team have belief.

"Confident," Schmetzer said of his team's mentality.

"Look, RSL's done a good job. Pablo [Mastroeni] has done a good job. They've got some wins on the trot. We may not have done that, but right now vibes are good.

"The last game against LAFC was a hard game, a playoff-type game. We had to suffer. We understand this is going to be the same."

Sounders winger Paul Rothrock is also confident, as he wants to give the fans something to celebrate after a tough season.

"I think it's our job as athletes to entertain people, but also to inspire people," Rothrock said.

"And I hope we can do that for Seattle and for the Sounders fans who have been supporting us through a difficult year for them too."

RSL had also gone on a lengthy winless streak during the regular season, but their strong finish ultimately propelled them into the final playoff spot.

They lost just one of their last six matches (W4 D1), giving them confidence heading into the postseason.

Albert Rusnak finished the regular season with 15 assists, one shy of his career-high of 16 in 2024.

He is the only player in Seattle history to record multiple seasons with 15+ assists, with Nicolas Lodieiro the only other Sounders player to do so even once, back in 2018.

Sergi Solans tallied 15 regular-season goals, becoming the fifth player in Real Salt Lake history to score at least 15 in a single season.

His total is the second highest by a player in his first season with the club, behind only Jeff Cunningham's 16 goals in 2006.

Seattle lost 3-0 to Real Salt Lake in the first MLS Cup Playoffs meeting between the sides in 2011, but the Sounders are unbeaten in the five playoff games in the fixture since then (W3 D2).

Only Chicago Fire against D.C. United and Sporting Kansas City against Colorado Rapids have longer active unbeaten runs in the postseason (seven each).

The Sounders have won 21 of their 29 home playoff matches (D3 L5), and the 21 home wins rank third in MLS Cup Playoffs history behind LA Galaxy (38) and Columbus Crew (22).

Since the beginning of 2014, Seattle have lost just once in 22 home playoff games (1-0 to LAFC in 2022, W18 D3).

RSL are making their sixth straight postseason appearance but enter the Wild Card round having gone winless in their last eight playoff games (D5 L3). The only longer winless streaks in playoff history are Minnesota United's current 10-match streak and Colorado’s nine-game streak from 1997 to 2002.
