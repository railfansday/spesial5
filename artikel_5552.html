Bitcoin falls below $70,000 as Nvidia CEO turmoil hammers crypto

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Ether also fell below $2,000 as a technology stock selloff and expectations of higher U.S. interest rates weighed on risk assets

Bitcoin fell below $70,000 on Friday, and ether dropped below $2,000, as a selloff in technology stocks spread to cryptocurrencies.

Bitcoin recently changed hands at $69,342, a 3% decline on the day, and ether slipped to $1,951, off 3.2%. Bitcoin has slipped under $70,000, a level it had not traded below since August, is headed for a sixth straight weekly decline and sits more than 21% under its late-September peak, according to The Wall Street Journal.

"This crypto crash was kicked off by the Nvidia $NVDA scandal and the broader risk-off sentiment in tech stocks that's following as a result. But what started as downward price pressure has now snowballed," Nic Puckrin, cross-asset analyst and founder of Coin Bureau, told The Wall Street Journal.

Crypto exchange-traded funds have lost roughly $1.57 billion to net withdrawals across the previous five sessions, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Exchanges closed out crypto futures positions totaling above $1.3 billion during a 24-hour window, according to CoinDesk. Bitcoin accounted for $677 million of that total, and close to $500 million was liquidated as the price crossed below $70,000. Liquidations take place once losses have exhausted a trader's margin, prompting the exchange to shut down the leveraged position by force.

Interest rates and the dollar are adding pressure. Inflation in the U.S. has stayed high, so markets are now betting on additional Federal Reserve rate increases, and that makes assets paying no yield less attractive, according to The Wall Street Journal. The odds of a 50-basis-point Fed hike in December have climbed since October inflation data, published at the start of the week, came in above forecasts, according to CoinDesk. The Dollar Index traded above 103 on Friday, a 19-month high.

CoinDesk also linked the selloff to media accounts that military tensions in the Middle East had intensified again. Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps claimed it had wiped out an American early-warning radar based in Bahrain, according to CoinDesk.

Nvidia has been under pressure since the news about its CEO. On Thursday, reports of a possible leadership change at Nvidia weighed on technology stocks and risk appetite, and bitcoin fell as much as 2.2%. Nvidia stock fell as much as 10% in late trading Wednesday and was down about 7% in premarket trading Thursday.

Jensen Huang resigned as CEO and left the board on Thursday after reports of two extramarital affairs. CFO Colette Kress is serving as interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor. Huang apologized to employees on Sunday, the day the board announced an independent investigation.

Nvidia is scheduled to report fiscal third-quarter earnings on Nov. 17. Three House Republican committee chairs have asked Huang to appear for a private session with lawmakers on Nov. 18.
