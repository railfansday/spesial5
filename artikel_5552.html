Bitcoin slips to about $73,000 as Nvidia CEO turmoil rattles risk appetite

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nvidia stock fell as much as 10% in late trading Wednesday, and U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs have seen roughly $2.5 billion in net outflows since Oct. 30

Bitcoin fell as much as 2.2% on Thursday as reports of a possible leadership change at Nvidia $NVDA weighed on technology stocks and risk appetite. The cryptocurrency traded between about $72,900 and $73,400 in the early morning, and Nasdaq $NDAQ-100 futures were down 0.6%.

Nvidia stock fell as much as 10% in late trading Wednesday and was down about 7% in premarket trading Thursday. Nvidia is working with an outside firm to find possible successors to CEO Jensen Huang after reports that he had two extramarital affairs, one of them with a woman believed to have been spying on behalf of China, according to Bloomberg. Nvidia has yet to make any decision public, according to CoinDesk.

Withdrawals from U.S. spot bitcoin exchange-traded funds added to the pressure. Flows into the funds turned negative on Oct. 30, and net outflows since then total roughly $2.5 billion, according to Bloomberg. The funds shed $299.9 million Wednesday, extending their run of withdrawals to a fifth consecutive day, according to CoinDesk.

Bitcoin dropped to its weakest level in three months on Tuesday, when a stronger-than-forecast U.S. inflation reading strengthened bets on additional rate increases, according to Bloomberg. The Federal Reserve holds its next meeting Dec. 8-9, and CME $CME FedWatch pricing puts the odds of a rate increase near 90%, according to CoinDesk.

"Bitcoin is showing signs of a changing market structure, with selling pressure concentrated in derivatives while spot market activity remains comparatively resilient," wrote Dean Chen, who works as an analyst for the crypto exchange Bitunix, in a Thursday note, according to Bloomberg. Chen said traders are pulling back on leverage, though investors' positioning does not point clearly in one direction.

Open interest in bitcoin futures fell 2.9% over 24 hours, and bitcoin led all coins in liquidations with $62.9 million, according to CoinDesk. Render, a token tied to a decentralized network of graphics processors, fell 3.1% as Nvidia stock slid.

Nvidia's board was set to meet Wednesday evening to weigh removing Huang over two extramarital affairs, and no clear successor exists. Huang apologized to employees Sunday for being unfaithful to his wife. The board announced an independent investigation that day and said it had confidence in him.

Nvidia is scheduled to report fiscal third-quarter earnings on Nov. 17. Three House Republican committee chairs have asked Huang to appear for a private session with lawmakers on Nov. 18.
