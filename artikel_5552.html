Brokerage Engine Joins LeadingRE’s Solutions Group Program

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Conceptual artwork of businessmen shaking hands in intersecting circles symbolizing merger integration. Concept of goal alignment, and joint collaboration process.

Leading Real Estate Companies of the World® (LeadingRE) has announced the inclusion of Brokerage Engine to its Solutions Group program of preferred business resources for its global network of 500 real estate firms.

Brokerage Engine helps real estate brokerages run on a stronger operational foundation, according to a release. Its all-in-one back-office platform streamlines commission processing, accounting, agent billing, compliance and reporting. Replacing manual workflows and duplicate entries, Brokerage Engine helps create efficiency and accuracy within brokerages, without adding overhead.

“LeadingRE has built a network known for quality, collaboration, and a commitment to helping independent brokerages succeed,” said Matt Thompson, president of Brokerage Engine. “Those are values we share at Brokerage Engine. Our platform was born inside a working brokerage and built to support firms of every size, from large multi-office operations to boutique firms, so it adapts to the way each business runs. We’re honored to be part of the Solutions Group and look forward to building long-term relationships with its member firms.”

“That firsthand understanding of brokerage operations makes Brokerage Engine a strong addition to our Solutions Group,” said Jim Psyhogios, LeadingRE’s vice president of strategic engagement. “We are pleased to offer members a resource that can help simplify their operations and run their businesses more efficiently.”

Learn more about Leading Real Estate Companies of the World® at http://leadingre.com/.
