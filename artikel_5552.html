Bayer won U.S. FDA approval for a first-in-class stroke blood thinner

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The FDA approved Sunharbo, a once-daily pill, to cut the risk of another stroke; the trial showed a 26% reduction in ischemic stroke

Bayer received U.S. Food and Drug Administration approval on Monday for Sunharbo (asundexian), a once-daily pill that is the first factor XIa inhibitor cleared to reduce the risk of another stroke in certain adults.

The 50 mg tablet is approved for use with antiplatelet therapy in adults after a recent mild-to-moderate non-cardioembolic ischemic stroke, which is a stroke caused by a clot that did not originate in the heart, or after a high-risk transient ischemic attack, the FDA said. Sunharbo is not approved for stroke prevention in patients with atrial fibrillation.

Approval rests on OCEANIC-STROKE, a placebo-controlled phase 3 trial of 12,327 adults who all received antiplatelet therapy. Sunharbo reduced the risk of ischemic stroke by 26% over a median treatment of 14.2 months, the FDA said. A second stroke occurred in 6.2% of Sunharbo patients and 8.4% of placebo patients, according to STAT.

The rate of major bleeding was comparable between the two groups, the FDA said. Bleeding increased with Sunharbo in patients older than 75 and during the first 90 days of treatment, and gastrointestinal bleeding was more common. Diarrhea was the most frequent adverse reaction, occurring in 3% or more of Sunharbo patients.

The FDA does not recommend Sunharbo with medicines that are combined P-gp and strong CYP3A4 inducers, such as carbamazepine, or in patients with moderate or severe hepatic impairment. The drug should be withheld at least 24 hours before elective surgery or invasive procedures.

"Stroke is a leading cause of disability and death in the United States, and survivors are at risk of having another stroke," Hylton V. Joffe, director of the Office of Cardiology, Hematology, Endocrinology, and Nephrology in the FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research, said in a statement.

Sunharbo blocks Factor XIa, a blood-clotting protein, and represents a novel type of anticoagulant, according to BioPharma Dive. A 30-day supply is listed at $748.50. Sebastian Guth, Bayer U.S. president and Bayer Pharmaceuticals' chief operating officer, said stroke neurologists will be a "very important part" of the early rollout, according to BioPharma Dive.

Bayer stock gained up to 2% during the opening hours of Frankfurt trading on Tuesday. China approved the drug in late October, according to Bloomberg. Bayer's pharmaceutical unit is contending with generic rivals to its Xarelto anticoagulant and Eylea eye treatment, while Bristol Myers Squibb $BMY and Johnson & Johnson $JNJ have a comparable candidate, milvexian, in trials.
