PS5 and PS5 Pro Black Friday deals 2026: What to realistically expect

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

TL;DR: Standalone PS5 Slim and PS5 Pro discounts look unlikely for Black Friday 2026. Expect Sony's real savings to center on game-bundled consoles, DualSense controllers, and PlayStation Store deals.

If you are holding out for Black Friday on Nov. 27 to finally buy a PlayStation 5, it is time to set some realistic expectations.

Last year's Black Friday sale was a banner event for Sony shoppers, bringing $100+ markdowns and free pack-in games across almost the entire lineup. This year, however, the gaming market looks completely different, and expecting a repeat of 2025's record-low prices will set you up for disappointment.

Between a global RAM shortage that pushed Sony to raise console prices and the Nov. 19 launch of GTA 6, deep standalone console discounts are largely off the table. That does not mean you cannot save money on PlayStation gear during Thanksgiving week — you just have to know where the real deals will show up.

Driven by rising memory costs from the AI boom and supply chain pressures, Sony officially raised U.S. retail prices across its entire console lineup on April 2. Today, the PS5 Slim Digital Edition starts at $599.99, the PS5 Slim Disc Edition sits at $649.99, and the flagship PS5 Pro retails for $899.99.

Even if Sony or major retailers like Walmart, Best Buy, and Target shave $50 to $100 off a PS5 Slim during Black Friday week, those sale prices will simply bring the console back down to what it used to cost before April. Meanwhile, standalone discounts on the PS5 Pro look practically nonexistent.

The Pro is already largely sold out across major U.S. stores ahead of GTA 6 launch day, so a realistic Black Friday "win" for Pro shoppers is simply catching an official restock at regular list price rather than paying third-party reseller markups.

If Sony does anything for PS5 consoles during Black Friday week, expect game bundles rather than raw price cuts. Sony loves keeping the console at full sticker price while tossing in a free pack-in game voucher — which is nice if you wanted the game anyway, but not much help if you just want a cheaper console. To actually pay less out of pocket, look at PlayStation Direct's certified refurbished lineup. Sony currently sells official refurbished PS5 Slim models for $100 less than new units ($499.99 for Digital, $549.99 for Disc), making them the most reliable way to dodge this year's price hikes without risking sketchy third-party sellers.

While console hardware stays stubbornly expensive, Sony has already kicked off the digital side of its holiday promotion. The official PlayStation Store Black Friday sale launched on Nov. 4 and runs through Nov. 30, knocking up to 75% off thousands of PS5, PS4, and PS VR2 games, expansions, and DLC.

Sony is also scheduled to refresh the lineup with new titles on Nov. 16 (before select early offers wrap up on Nov. 20). Sony's initial Black Friday announcement made zero mention of hardware or console discounts, reinforcing that its biggest holiday push is centered on software rather than cutting console prices. DualSense wireless controllers are also consistently Sony's most dependable Black Friday discount. Retailers like Amazon, Best Buy, Target, Walmart, and PlayStation Direct discount standard and colored DualSense gamepads during Black Friday week, offering decent savings on one of the few PlayStation essentials that reliably go on sale every November.

You can also expect price drops on physical copies of first-party PS5 exclusives, as well as Sony's annual discount on 12-month PlayStation Plus memberships.

Let your gaming calendar decide when you buy. If you want a PS5 Pro or need a console hooked up to your TV for GTA 6 on Nov. 19, grab one at standard retail price the moment you spot legitimate inventory — waiting for a holiday discount will only leave you fighting empty shelves.

If you are hunting down stock right now, check out our guide to the best console deals ahead of GTA 6 to see which retailers still have units available at retail price.

On the other hand, if you are shopping for a December holiday gift, picking up extra DualSense controllers, or stocking up on games, waiting until Thanksgiving week to see what bundles and accessory markdowns drop is the smarter play.
