McDonald’s reveals Home Alone Meal with pizza-flavored fries and collectible socks

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

McDonald’s is teaming up with Home Alone for a new holiday meal featuring limited-time menu items, collectible socks, and a dessert inspired by the Christmas classic.

The Home Alone Meal will launch at participating McDonald’s restaurants nationwide on December 1, bringing several new food options inspired by Kevin McCallister’s adventures in the 1990 film.

The collaboration also marks the return of Macaulay Culkin as Kevin McCallister, with the actor reprising his iconic role for a new McDonald’s advertising campaign.

The Home Alone Meal will be available for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, with customers able to choose from different entrees depending on the time of day.

Breakfast options include the Sausage, Egg & Cheese McGriddles, while lunch and dinner customers can select items such as the Big Mac.

The biggest addition is the Little Nero’s Pizza McShaker Fries, inspired by the fictional pizza restaurant featured in Home Alone. The medium fries come with a seasoning blend designed to recreate the flavor of Kevin’s favorite plain cheese pizza.

The seasoning combines tomato, garlic, Italian herbs, and cheese. Customers can add their fries and seasoning to a McShaker bag before shaking everything together.

McDonald’s is also introducing Lovely Cheese Pizza Dipping Sauce, featuring tomato, aged Parmesan cheese, herbs, and garlic. The sauce can be paired with Chicken McNuggets or McCrispy Strips, or purchased separately.

Each specialty meal also comes with one of four collectible pairs of socks featuring designs inspired by characters, scenes, and quotes from the movie.

The socks will be available while supplies last, giving customers four different designs to collect throughout the promotion.

Alongside the nationwide menu launch, McDonald’s has confirmed that Macaulay Culkin is reprising his role as Kevin McCallister for the collaboration.

The actor originally portrayed Kevin in the 1990 film Home Alone and its 1992 sequel, Home Alone 2: Lost in New York.

“What I love about this collaboration is that the Home Alone Meal captures something bigger than just one character; it’s about the playful spirit that resonates with people across generations,” Culkin said.

“Bringing this to life with McDonald’s and getting to reexperience these moments with my own kids, reminds me why fans keep coming back to Kevin’s story. That’s the magic we’re celebrating here.”

McDonald’s is also hosting a Home Alone Challenge event in Los Angeles on December 4 and 5, running from 5 PM to 10 PM PT each day.

The immersive pop-up will feature interactive rooms where visitors can complete challenges inspired by the film, including sliding into a ball pit and testing their slingshot skills. Participants will have the opportunity to receive a free Home Alone Meal.

Additional promotions will be available through the McDonald’s app, including $5 off McDelivery orders of $20 or more and the ability to redeem MyMcDonald’s Rewards points for a 14-day free trial of the Disney+ Hulu bundle.
