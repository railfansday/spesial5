GTA 6 drops longtime hookup options and prostitution to focus on Jason and Lucia’s romance

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Rockstar has cut longtime ways to hook up with other characters in GTA 6, including hiring sex workers, as it builds the game’s romance around Jason and Lucia.

GTA 3 let players pay for sex back in 2001, and later games gave them more ways to pursue other characters. San Andreas gave CJ six potential girlfriends, while GTA 4 let Niko Bellic meet dates through the in-game internet.

GTA 5 kept paid hookups and gave players another option at the Vanilla Unicorn strip club. Flirting with certain dancers during a private dance could lead to leaving the club with them, hooking up, and calling them again later.

GTA 6 still puts Jason in suggestive situations in the main story with side characters attempting to seduce him, and strangers around Leonida flirt with him openly and invite him into explicit situations. But unlike GTA games from before, that’s as intimate as Jason is allowed to get.

Bloomberg reporter Jason Schreier said a trip to the strip club was the closest he found to a way to cheat on Lucia, and he later confirmed you cannot kiss anyone except your partner.

You can't kiss anyone except your partner

The Guardian’s review confirmed that either lead can get a private dance on their own or the couple can go together. Shacknews confirmed that players can no longer pick up sex workers.

Rockstar described Jason and Lucia as “a couple in a bind, lovers on the run” in a statement to Shacknews. The studio said it chose which features to bring forward based on how they fit the story and the gameplay systems it wanted to expand.

“While we have taken a lot forward from previous GTA titles, everything we have implemented in VI has been done to support the spirit of the story… Many aspects of previous titles are not present in GTAVI, but the elements we have we believe are all necessary and complementary to the feeling and motivations we wanted to emphasize as you play through Jason and Lucia’s story.”

GTA 6 gives players several ways to build intimacy between the two leads. Jason and Lucia can go dancing, hold hands, and make each other coffee. The couple can also become more affectionate and intimate when the relationship is going well.

How romantic that partnership becomes is up to the player. Rockstar development head Rob Nelson has said players can let the romance cool while Jason and Lucia continue to work together as the story unfolds.
