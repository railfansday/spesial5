Man City XI vs Napoli: Confirmed team news, predicted lineup and injury latest for Champions League

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Manchester City will welcome back Matheus Nunes for their Champions League clash with Napoli on Tuesday.

Enzo Maresca’s side are looking to arrest a slump that has seen them win just one of their last six matches in all competitions.

That victory came by way of a penalty shootout against Brighton in the Carabao Cup, and they were held to a surprise 1-1 draw by Fulham at the Etihad Stadium last time out.

One key man they will be without is Antoine Semenyo, who continues to be sidelined with a knee problem that is supposed to keep him out for a total of eight weeks. He could return to action just before Christmas.

As for their returners, Nunes, the versatile Portugal international, gives City another option at right-back.

He had been out with an ankle issue, but is “fine” to face Napoli.

Elsewhere, it looks likely that Maresca will keep mostly the same side, despite the disappointment last weekend.

Gianluigi Donnarumma will continue in goal despite shaky form, and Nunes could come back in at right full-back.

Marc Guehi returned from a hip problem that he saw him withdraw from the recent England international camp, and he will play alongside Ruben Dias.

Nico O’Reilly was replaced by Josko Gvardiol at the weekend, and it is a toss-up between the pair, with Maresca still undecided on his first-choice left-back.

In the engine room, big-money signing Enzo Fernandez saw himself benched in favour of Ayyoub Bouaddi on Saturday, but he could come back into the side here alongside Elliot Anderson.
