Opinion: Massive Real Madrid to Liverpool deal looks increasingly possible

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Real Madrid appear ready to open the door to a massive sale. Liverpool can swoop in here and get themselves a proper star.

Liverpool need to invest in their midfield. That should be obvious to everyone now after a start to the season that demonstrated an incredible weakness there.

That's the result of building a midfield group entirely out of attacking players. All of Liverpool's options want to get further forward, leaving no one to really cover gaps or stop counter-attacks.

It's led to Andoni Iraola calling on Trey Nyoni to learn the holding role but even he is better known as a more advanced midfielder. It also just means that Liverpool are essentially relying on a teenager to keep their side secure - hardly ideal.

We fully expect Liverpool to move for a defensive, controlling midfielder in 2027. Maybe that's in January or maybe it's in the summer but either way, some sort of move simply must be on the way.

Real Madrid look ready to do businessThere's a midfielder we have our eyes on at Real Madrid: Eduardo Camavinga. He's been heavily linked to Liverpool in the past and his situation in Madrid is becoming less and less clear. Or rather, it's becoming more clear that he's not viewed as a long-term piece. That looked the case recently with Real Madrid reportedly eyeing a move for Martin Zubimendi - someone who would replace Camavinga in midfield.

There's a midfielder we have our eyes on at Real Madrid: Eduardo Camavinga. He's been heavily linked to Liverpool in the past and his situation in Madrid is becoming less and less clear.

Or rather, it's becoming more clear that he's not viewed as a long-term piece. That looked the case recently with Real Madrid reportedly eyeing a move for Martin Zubimendi - someone who would replace Camavinga in midfield.

And now it looks doubly so as TeamTalk reports that they're considering a move for Chema Andres, a Real Madrid academy product. Again, it's a sign that Camavinga isn't necessarily needed as a midfielder is clearly on the agenda over there.

That's how Liverpool can swoop in, offering the Frenchman the key role he deserves. We're massive fans of his, believing he's woefully underrated because of Aurelien Tchouameni's efforts in midfield for Real Madrid. The Reds simply won't find a better defensive midfielder.

So if Camavinga is available, and it increasingly looks like he will be after almost moving in the summer, then Liverpool have got to act. This is the one, to put it bluntly.
