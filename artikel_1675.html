Opinion: Liverpool's genius loan transfer agreement is about to unleash the new Origi

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Liverpool have secured a genius transfer agreement to produce their next Divock Origi.

Remember that banner the club's supporters made? Football without Origi is nothing.

Those were the good old days. The Belgian international may now be on the other side of the planet, studying in Harvard's Business School after retiring from professional football, but he's still missed at Liverpool.

Origi might have moved on but us Liverpool fans still remember, still yearn for a game changer of his ilk.

A lot will be said about the greatest era in Jurgen Klopp's time at Anfield. The focus always goes on the big names and stars of the era. But Liverpool's success would not have been possible with a game changer like Origi coming off the bench and scoring last-minute winners.
