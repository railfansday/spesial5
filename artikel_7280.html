Carl’s Jr. taps David&Goliath as marketing focus turns to boldness

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Let Marketing Dive's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The agency of record appointment includes a mandate to lean into the QSR’s heritage and forward-looking initiatives like a Burger Revolution strategy.

Carl’s Jr. has appointed David&Goliath as marketing agency of record as it shifts focus to cooked-to-order burgers and tries to revive a bolder, boundary-pushing brand personality, according to news shared with Marketing Dive. Carl’s Jr. did not previously have a dedicated AOR, and instead worked with agencies on a project basis, Marketing Dive understands.

David&Goliath, which is part of Innocean Worldwide, will oversee brand strategy, creative development, experiential marketing, production and storytelling for the fast-food chain, with a mandate to lean into Carl’s Jr.'s heritage while shoring up cultural relevance with younger consumers. Its first work is expected to arrive in Q1 of next year.

CKE Restaurants Holdings-owned Carl’s Jr. unveiled its Burger Revolution strategy in September, which introduces a systemwide cooked-to-order approach to burger preparation. The announcement was accompanied by the release of a limited-time Angus Maximus 5.7-ounce burger. The changes are part of a larger effort by Carl’s Jr. to emphasize food quality, which has manifested in promotional areas like limited-time offerings.

Other fast-food companies, including rival Burger King, have thrown a spotlight on premium ingredients and restaurant improvements to win back price-sensitive diners. Carl’s Jr.’s franchised store count has declined in recent years. Franchisees of the brand and sister restaurant Hardee’s have faced their own profitability struggles, with a major one in Calirfornia in April declaring bankruptcy, Marketing Dive’s sister publication Restaurant Dive previously reported.

Carl’s Jr. has made internal changes to its marketing leadership this year as well. In the spring, CKE Restaurants named Burge Diemer, a restaurant industry vet who previously worked at brands including The Habit Burger & Grill, Boston Market and Jack in the Box, as CMO of Carl’s Jr. Diemer is tasked with developing breakthrough campaigns and driving product innovation. She credited David&Goliath with possessing the bravery necessary to build on Carl’s Jr.’s legacy.

“Together, we’re excited to push boundaries, expand our connection with customers, and bring the unmistakable Carl’s Jr. spirit to a new generation of burger lovers,” Diemer said in a press statement.

Carl’s Jr.’s advertising has not shied away from pushing buttons in the past. The chain in the 2000s became known for TV commercials featuring bikini-clad models suggestively eating burgers. It has dipped back into the racy well in recent years, including through a 2025 campaign with influencer Alix Earle that touted offerings like a hangover-themed burger.
