No-Bake Cranberry Orange Bliss Bites

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Disclosure: This post may contain affiliate links which provide us a small commission when used for purchase. We're grateful for your support!

Cozy, bright, and bursting with holiday flavor — these Cranberry Orange Bliss Bites are the perfect little treat when holiday baking season is in full swing and you’re out of time and energy. Inspired by Starbucks’ Bliss Bars, they pack the tart-sweet magic of cranberries and fresh orange zest into tender, no-bake bites you can whip up in minutes!

Even better? They’re vegan, gluten-free, and require zero baking — making them a lifesaver during the busy holiday season. A drizzle of white chocolate (or creamy coconut butter) makes them extra festive, but they’re just as delicious on their own. Let’s make bliss bites!

Besides tasting absolutely magical, these bliss bites get their name because they’re inspired by the legendary Bliss Bars from Starbucks (IYKYK!). The Starbucks bars have a chewy blondie base with cream cheese frosting, cranberries, white chocolate, and orange zest — total swoon!

The addition of orange juice + zest and dried cranberries kick things up a notch and sends them into Bliss Bar territory!

The final touch is an optional drizzle of melted white chocolate chips, coconut butter, or our favorite — a mix of the two! While the white chocolate creates a thicker, sweeter, and more decadent frosting, the coconut butter creates a drippier texture and adds a more pronounced coconut flavor that we enjoy. The combination is the best of both worlds!

We hope you LOVE these bites! They’re:ZestyBrightSweet with pops of tartnessDecadent yet wholesomeQuick + easy to make& SO delicious!

These are the perfect little treat to make during the fall and winter months. They’ve got you covered whether you want something on hand to satisfy sweet cravings during the work week or need a quick healthier dessert to bring to a gathering.
