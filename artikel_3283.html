‘Miss Scarlet’ Creator Behind New Masterpiece-UKTV Period Drama ‘Fleet Street’ Starring Silvia Presente & Guy Remmers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

EXCLUSIVE: The final season of Masterpiece and UKTV’s Miss Scarlet is just a few weeks away but creator Rachael New and writer Ben Edwards have something else cooking.

New and Edwards are creating a period drama set at the home of British journalism starring Outlander’s Silvia Presente and The Buccaneers star Guy Remmers. Fox Entertainment Global is selling outside the U.S. and UK.

Set in 1901 in London after the death of Queen Victoria, Fleet Street follows Kitty (Presente), a young woman from a wealthy family determined to become a journalist, and Samuel, one of Fleet Street’s brightest reporters haunted by the consequences of a past story. When Kitty saves Samuel’s job by filing an article under his name, she asks for something in return: the chance to learn his trade. The show comes from the established creative team behind Miss Scarlet (previously Miss Scarlet and the Duke), the hugely popular Masterpiece-UKTV period drama starring Kate Phillips as the titular Victorian detective. Miss Scarlet EP Patrick Irwin is aboard Fleet Street with his Mikey Productions. Watch on Deadline

Cameras will roll shortly on Fleet Street in Serbia. This comes just a few weeks before the launch of the seventh and final season of Miss Scarlet, freeing up New and Edwards, whose credits include Shameless and Grantchester.

Fleet Street will air on PBS Masterpiece and UKTV’s U&Drama network plus streamer U. Fleet Street is co-produced by Mikey Productions and Masterpiece. EPs are New, Edwards, Patricia Lenahan and Irwin for Mikey Productions. Susanne Simpson is EP for Masterpiece. The lead director is Ivan Živković. Fox Entertainment Global is handling international distribution (bar U.S. and UK).

Presente is repped by The BWH Agency and Greenlight Management.
