Taylor Swift Throws It Back at the 2026 CMAs With ‘Our Song’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It’s been 13 years since Taylor Swift last performed at the Country Music Association Awards (honestly, what other number would it be?). The superstar returned to the awards ceremony on Wednesday, performing her Toy Story 5 song “I Knew It, I Knew You” and — wait for it — “Our Song.”

Swift appeared backstage in a sparkly mini dress, donning a cowboy hat and passing by host Lainey Wilson, Brad Paisley, Eric Church, Sugarland, and Brooks & Dunn. After “Whispering Bill” Anderson handed her an acoustic guitar, she hopped onstage with a full band — including Keith Urban — as she segued into “Our Song.” It marked her first performance of the track in three years, and only her fifth time in 13 years.

Swift’s “I Knew It, I Knew You” was up for Single of the Year at this year’s CMAs but lost to Ella Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas.” It marked her first CMA nomination since 2022, when she earned a nod in the Video of the Year Category for her collaboration with Chris Stapleton, “I Bet You Think About Me.” She last performed at the ceremony in 2013, when she performed a stripped-back rendition of “Red” alongside Vince Gill, Alison Krauss, and others. “LOOKS LIKE I HAVEN’T YEE’D MY LAST HAW THANK YOU CMA!!!!” the superstar wrote on her Instagram stories after nominations were announced in September. It’s the latest instance of Swift’s big return to country music, which kicked off with the release of “I Knew It, I Knew You.” Since June, the megastar has been honoring her days in Nashville. Last month, she was inducted into the Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame where she became the youngest member ever.

“There is no music community like this music community. I know. I checked all the communities. There’s no one doing it,” Swift said in her acceptance speech at the Nashville HOF. She added: “It’s not lost on me how crazy this is. I will cherish it forever because Nashville is the place that honed and polished the thing about myself and the thing about my life that I am the most proud of and the most passionate about.”
