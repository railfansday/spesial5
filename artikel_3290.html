The re-branded Soul Train Awards are back tonight after a two-year break. The show was put on indefinite hiatus last year before it was revived for 2026 thanks to a partnership with Spotify.

The 2026 Soul Train Awards took place earlier this month in Chicago, where Don Cornelius created Soul Train in 1971. Chaka Khan, Elmiene, and Durand Bernarr received special awards. All three recipients also performed alongside Lucky Daye, Kirk Franklin, Muni Long, El DeBarge, Micky Guyton, Robert Randolph, and Erykah Badu. The show was hosted by Black-ish star Anthony Anderson.

“As a Chicago native, I’m proud to bring the Soul Train Awards back to the city where it all originated,” said BET president Louis Carr. “Coming off a landmark year for BET, this return reaffirms what we do best: bringing our community together around the culture and connecting the artists who built this sound to the ones carrying it forward.”
