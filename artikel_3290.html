Watch Ella Langley Find What Matters on ‘Loving Life Again’ at 2026 CMA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It’s been the year of Ella Langley‘s “Choosin’ Texas,” a song which has already racked up the award for Single of the Year and Song of the Year at Wednesday’s 60th annual CMA broadcast. But Langley, for her first performance of the evening, decided to keep the focus on the importance of finding the quiet, down-home moments among the superstardom: “Loving Life Again.” Written with Devin Dawson and Ernest, Langley joined a string section for a version of the song that was inspired by time that Langley took off the road to reconnect with her family and her roots — with a dreamy, bucolic set design to bring the feeling to life.

Langley is the most nominated artist at this year’s CMA Awards, airing live from Nashville. Langley landed nine total nominations, including Entertainer of the Year, along with Album of the Year for her LP Dandelion. “Choosin’ Texas” is also recognized throughout: for Single of the Year, Song of the Year, and Music Video of the Year. “Loving Life Again” is the fourth single from Dandelion, and reached Number One on country radio, making Alabama native Langley only the second woman in history, alongside Shania Twain, to log four or more chart-topping hits from a single album (“Choosin’ Texas,” “Be Her” and “I Can’t Love You Anymore,” all from Dandelion).

“Ella has a fiery spirit,” Miranda Lambert told Rolling Stone, who co-produced Dandelion. “She lives life in a big way and on her own terms. With this record, I wanted to honor her vision every step of the way.… It felt important to help her make choices that stayed true to who she is as an artist.”
