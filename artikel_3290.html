Pentagon CIO Kirsten Davies replaces Sean Cairncross as US cyber director

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

White House officials disagree on whether Sean Cairncross was fired or quit after a week of US outages. The Pentagon’s IT chief takes over as national cyber director for now.

Department of War Chief Information Officer Kirsten Davies speaks at a CIO town hall at the Mark Center, Alexandria, Virginia, 10 February 2026.

Kirsten Davies, the Pentagon’s chief information officer, is replacing Sean Cairncross as the White House national cyber director. Axios first reported the change on Thursday, after a week of tech outages across the US.

Two White House officials told Axios that Stephen Miller, President Trump’s new chief of staff, sought the change and pushed Cairncross out. One said Cairncross “lacked the strong cyber credentials to perform the job”. Two other sources described his departure to Axios as amicable.

A person close to the White House told Reuters’ Courtney Rozen that Cairncross was “fired”.

“The president fully supported firing Sean Cairncross from his position,” the person told Reuters.

Officials gave Semafor two versions. A White House official and a person familiar with the White House said Trump backed his firing. Replacing him was one of Miller’s first tasks as chief of staff, the official said. A senior White House official and another person familiar with the matter disputed that. They told Semafor he resigned of his own accord.

“It has been the honor of a lifetime to serve President Donald J. Trump,” Cairncross said in a statement, quoted by Axios.

He had led the Office of the National Cyber Director, which coordinates cybersecurity policy across the federal government, since August 2025. His last public interview in the role was on stage at Semafor’s San Francisco event this week, Semafor said.

The change follows outages at Microsoft, US banks and the National Security Agency, and a nationwide ground stop that disrupted more than 18,000 flights, according to Reuters. Senior officials have said the outages had no common cause, and that no cyberattack lay behind them. Banks and the NSA reportedly blamed rushed security patches for some of them.

Davies will take over temporarily, according to Reuters. A permanent national cyber director needs Senate confirmation. The Senate already confirmed Davies for the Pentagon role, Axios reported. Democrats take control of the Senate in January.

Before the Pentagon, Davies was chief information security officer at Unilever. She also held technology and cyber roles at Estée Lauder, Barclays Africa Group, Hewlett Packard and Siemens, according to her official biography.

She thanked Trump and Miller in a post on X late on Thursday.

Thank you @POTUS and @StephenM for your faith and steady confidence in me. And thank you @SecWar for your vigilant and visionary leadership.

Our nation stands in the crossroad of analog, digital, and quantum technologies, our American way of living increasingly interconnected. Our opportunity is to dominate the cyber landscape for generations to come …but only if we modernize and drive resilience into the foundations our great nation runs on, as well as build cyber talent for our future. Under President Trump’s leadership we will have a more secure, resilient; and supercharged technological future.

– _Kirsten Davies_ (@_KirstenDavies_) November 20, 2026

Cairncross’s team was already thinning. His former head of policy, Thomas Lind, left in June and joined OpenAI in October, Axios reported.
