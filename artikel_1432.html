Dewsbury-Hall On What 'Top Week' Can Do For Everton

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall spoke to evertontv after the Blues' satisfying come-from-behind victory over Coventry City on Friday night.

The Toffees fell behind shortly after the half-time interval at Hill Dickinson Stadium, before a brace from Thierno Barry and a well-taken strike by Dewsbury-Hall sealed a third consecutive win in the space of eight days.

Watch Dewsbury-Hall's full interview below to hear him reflect on a busy week, the character inside the Everton changing room, his appreciation for the support shown by Evertonians, England hopes and why he is optimistic for the future.
