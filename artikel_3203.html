See New Order Kick Off First Tour Without Drummer Stephen Morris

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

New Order launched a South American tour Monday at Espaço Unimed in São Paulo, Brazil, marking the first time in their history they played a complete show without drummer Stephen Morris, who stepped away from the group to be with his wife, New Order keyboardist Gillian Gilbert, as she recovers from cancer.

“We’re sorry Steve and Gillian can’t be here with us,” frontman Bernard Sumner told the crowd early in the set, “and we just want them to know how much we’re missing them.”

Sumner is now the only original member of New Order still in the touring lineup. He was joined by Phil Cunningham on guitar and keyboards, Tom Chapman on bass, and new member Elliot Barlow on drums. It’s the first time they’ve played as a four-piece since 1998.

The setlist was heavy on 1980s classics like “Ceremony,” “Age of Consent,” “True Faith,” “Bizarre Love Triangle,” “Your Silent Face,” and “Temptation.” But they also dipped into the Joy Division catalog with “Temptation,” “Isolation,” “Atmosphere” and standard show-closer “Love Will Tear Us Apart.”

With the exception of a 20th anniversary party for Sofia Coppola’s movie Marie Antoinette at France’s Palace of Versailles on September 22 where they played a surprise, truncated set, New Order last performed May 17, 2025, at the Cruel World festival in Pasadena, California. In June of this year, Morris and Gilbert announced they were temporarily leaving the band due to “personal health reasons.”

Shortly before Morris and Gilbert announced their departure from the touring lineup, word came down that Joy Division/New Order were finally getting inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame. Fans hoped the event could finally reunite New Order with estranged bassist Peter Hook, who left the group in 2007. In September, Morris said the Hall of Fame meant very little to him. “I don’t get awards,” he told Rolling Stone. “I do get awards, and in a way that’s the problem. I don’t understand the point of them. I’ve tried. I have really, really, believe me, tried. Unsuccessfully. I’ve tried giving awards. I’ve tried receiving awards. When induction night came on November 14, Hook was the only member of the group to show up. “I’m on my own, but I’m not alone,” he said in his acceptance speech. “I want to thank the fans for all their wonderful and priceless efforts in making this happen and putting me here tonight. This award is for them.” (He didn’t mention his New Order bandmates a single time.)

Hook now plays New Order and Joy Division classics on the road with his band the Light. New Order, meanwhile, wrap up their South American tour December 9 in Bogota, Colombia. A U.S. leg begins March 5 at the Moody Center in Austin, Texas. It concludes March 21 in Miami.
