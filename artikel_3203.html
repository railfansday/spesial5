NOV: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Existing facility capacity allows NOV to hit $1.5 billion EBITDA without major new capital spending.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 22, 2026 at 3:45PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, NOV (NOV -2.73%) reported revenue of $2.23 billion for the period. Operating efficiency gains and expanding international opportunities position the equipment provider for multiyear margin growth. Expanding order backlogs in key product lines provide clear top-line visibility.

Three key takeaways from management's comments stand out for long-term investors.

Offshore project sanctioning is gathering speed as deepwater development expands globally. Floating production, storage, and offloading (FPSO) vessels require high-specification processing technology, creating a multiyear demand tailwind for equipment procurement.

International producers are adopting North American shale techniques to develop unconventional resources. Demand is accelerating in Argentina, North Africa, and the Middle East for specialized drilling and midstream infrastructure equipment.

Management reiterated its pathway to $1.5 billion in annual adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (adjusted EBITDA), a key metric tracking cash operating profitability. Operational streamlining and existing facility capacity allow the business to expand margins without large capital investments.

Broad energy market recovery is accelerating across deepwater offshore and international land plays, where technical demands leverage NOV's engineering scale.

Leadership in subsea technology and automated drilling provides structural advantages. Management is watching pricing discipline and manufacturing throughput as margin expansion continues.
