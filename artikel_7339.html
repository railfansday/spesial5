A Bravo Star Decks the Bathroom Halls in Goodwipes&#8217; First Holiday Ad

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

As people dig out and dust off their holiday decorations, Goodwipes urges them to bring festive cheer to one of the most used, yet overlooked rooms in the house during the season: the bathroom.

“Do Your Merry Most,” the flushable wipe brand’s first-ever holiday ad, sees Bravo personality, fashion historian, and archivist Bronwyn Newport bring her signature maximalism to the restroom. As she decks the halls with over-the-top decor—from candy-striped wallpaper to pictures of talking dogs in Christmas hats—she also swaps out the “boring toilet paper” for Goodwipes’ limited-edition “Merry Mint” wipes.

The 60-second film launching across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube follows on from one of the privately-held company’s “biggest years yet,” its svp of marketing, Meredith Diehn, told ADWEEK.
