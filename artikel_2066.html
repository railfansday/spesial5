The Newcastle United stars in international action this month.

Great to see some excellent performances from United players.

So far, a perfect record of seven wins out of seven in this international break.

The senior international matches when Newcastle United players have played a part.

Yoane Wissa kicked things off with right foot volley to set DR Congo on their way to victory over Sierra Leone on Thursday.

That same day United teenager Ousmane Diabate played the full match as Guinea won an AFCON qualifier against Eritrea.

A perfect Thursday was completed when Dan Burn and Lewis Hall both excelled as England defeated Croatia 2-1 at Wembley to make it three wins out of three for Newcastle’s internationals this month.

Friday we saw Malick Thiaw make his third start in a row under Jurgen Klopp as Germany won 3-1 away in Serbia.

Moving on to Saturday and William Osula came off the bench to help Denmark to a stunning 4-1 away win over Portugal.

The same night Anthony Elanga created the opening goal as Sweden won 3-2 in a thriller against Bosnia & Herzegovina (Newcastle’s Amar Dedic was on the losing side but then it was impossible for both teams to win…).

Then on Sunday night a magnificent 3-2 England victory in Madrid. Lewis Hall playing the full match and excellent, whilst Dan Burn came off the bench to help see out this famous victory over Spain.

Monday will see Malick Thiaw potentially line up for a fourth start in a row for Jurgen Klopp as Germany take on the Netherlands.

Then on Tuesday, up to six different senior internationals will see Newcastle United stars on the pitch.

This is the November 2026 international schedule for Newcastle United players:

DR Congo 3 Sierra Leone 1 Yoane Wissa volleying home after 189 seconds for a 9th goal in his last 13 internationals, a 3-1 final scoreline in this AFCON qualifying group win.

Ivory Coast 2 Gambia 1 Bazoumana Toure an unused sub for the Ivory Coast in this AFCON qualifying group win.

Guinea 2 Eritrea 1 Ousmane Diabate played the full match in this AFCON qualifying group win.

England 2 Croatia 1 Lewis Hall and Dan Burn voted England’s best two players by the fans as they were excellent in this Nations League win, Rogers and Kane (pen) with the England goals.

Czech Republic 0 Spain 2 Lukas Hornicek an unused sub in this Nations League defeat.

Serbia 1 Germany 3 Malick Thiaw with a very good calm and composed performance in this Nations League win, the NUFC defender playing the full match.

France U21s 1 Italy U21s 2 Aladji Bamba coming off the bench in this friendly for the final 11 minutes, with Italy already leading 2-1.

Denmark U21s 0 Netherlands U21s 3 Sean Steur impressing as he helped put the young Dutch side in charge of this two-legged European Championship qualifying play-off, playing the first 78 minutes and providing an assist for the third goal.

Sweden 3 Bosnia & Herzegovina 2 Anthony Elanga impressing in the hour he was on the pitch, a great cross and assist inside five minutes for the opening goal. Amar Dedic playing the full game of this Nations League clash.

Portugal 1 Denmark 4 William Osula helped spoil this Cristiano Ronaldo tribute party as he entered the pitch with 17 minutes to go and the Danes 3-0 up in this Nations League match. Osula winning the free-kick that led to the fourth goal.

Spain 2 England 3 Lewis Hall with an excellent display and got the better of Lamine Yamal. Dan Burn came off the bench late on to help see out this famous England victory in the Nations League.

Croatia 2 Czech Republic 1 Lukas Hornicek an unused sub in this Nations League defeat.
