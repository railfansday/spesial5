Arsenal could use Berta connection to explore Endrick transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Arsenal may be able to pursue a January move for Endrick. Andrea Berta providing a useful contact for the forward’s representatives.

A move to the Premier League is being linked with the young Real Madrid player after he failed to play regularly for his current Spanish club. As such, his representatives want to find a new club for him in the winter window.

With Bukayo Saka injured, Arsenal may be looking to strengthen their attacking options. Endrick’s ability to play in central areas and wide areas can make him an attractive option for Mikel Arteta.

But it’s not that simple. The North Londoners are not currently amongst the clubs who are pushing hardest to sign the Brazilian.

The TalkSPORT report that Berta’s links to Endrick’s representatives could provide Arsenal with an ideal starting point if they wish to pursue a deal.

The forward has been offered to a number of clubs in the Premier League as his camp seek to resolve his lack of opportunities in Real Madrid.

Arsenal are known to have been interested in the forward in the past. Newcastle United, Aston Villa and Tottenham are the clubs currently gaining more concrete attention.

It doesn’t mean they can’t make a move to north London. Berta’s connections may help them understand what is needed to get the player to the Emirates.

However, Real Madrid may be more reluctant to let the player go. The Spanish club have been reluctant to allow Endrick to leave before, making a loan look more of a realistic option.

Endrick arrived at Real Madrid with an incredible reputation having made his breakthrough at Palmeiras. However, due to there being competition for places, he has struggled to play much in Spain.

If he were to go to the Premier League, he would be able to play regularly to help his development. Playing under Arteta and competing for major trophies at Arsenal would be a great opportunity.

However, Arsenal would have to consider whether he would actually play enough. Moving to another team with competition for attacking spots may not be the best move for his development.

Arsenal need to decide whether Endrick would fill a real gap in their attacking line-up or simply add to their talented front line.

Although Saka’s injury makes attacking additions more important, they can’t make a decision based purely on the injury. They need to look at how he would fit into their future plans.

Thanks to Berta’s link, Arsenal can look into the possibility of signing him. Whether he is a serious target will depend on Real Madrid’s willingness to let him go and what Arsenal can offer him.
