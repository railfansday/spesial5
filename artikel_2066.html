Major Sheffield Wednesday v Plymouth Argyle flashpoint causes big online stir

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Sheffield Wednesday fans went home happy on Saturday, as they cruised to a comfortable 3-0 win over fellow promotion-chasing side, Plymouth Argyle.

It was the perfect start to life in charge of the Owls in League One for Dave Challinor, who picked up three points at the first available opportunity in front of the Owls supporters.

Billy Mitchell opened the scoring on the day for Sheffield Wednesday, which came just before the half-time whistle. Davis Keillor-Dunn and Di'Shon Bernard then added two late goals to secure an eye-catching win at Hillsborough.

Bernard's goal late on was the pick of the bunch, as he fired home in emphatic style from distance, much to the surprise and delight of the home crowd.

The result means that Sheffield Wednesday moved ahead of Plymouth in the table, and up to fifth, as they look to finish in the top-six in League One this term.

One of the main talking points from the win over Tom Cleverley's side wasn't any of the goals on the day though, as a poor challenge from Plymouth's Ronan Curtis only saw a yellow card awarded to the winger.

Ronan Curtis' challenge on Jamal Lowe was at chest height, and the Hillsborough crowd were left furious that only a yellow card was awarded, with many feeling as though there wasn't any other option than a red card.

Lowe was able to carry on and play on, before being substituted for Liam Palmer with five minutes remaining in the match, and he'll be hoping he can kick on and feature for the Owls in their bid for promotion.

Curtis didn't appear to be looking at Lowe beforehand, and some Plymouth fans came to their player's defence for the incident which sparked plenty of debate on social media.

The Plymouth winger was apologetic after it occurred, but it certainly endangered Lowe with his boot being so high off the ground, and so there was real surprise to only see a yellow card shown by the referee.

Do you think it should have been a no-brainer for Curtis' challenge to be worthy of a red card instead of a yellow?

Speaking to The Star after the win over Plymouth, newly-appointed Sheffield Wednesday boss Dave Challinor issued his verdict on the challenge from Curtis, and felt as though it was right to appeal for a red card.

"Well, it's definitely chest high. I think you're always going to appeal for it. I think Tom (Cleverley) would have been appealing for it exactly the same, and he was open to that. What do you need to do? What constitutes a red card? If it catches him on the jaw, did Ronan Curtis mean it? I'd probably suggest no."

The ball's coming over his shoulder, but if one of our players would have done that, I would have been fearing the worst. Thankfully, it didn't make a huge impact at the end of the game."

"He (referee) said there was, I think, minimal force. I suggest if it's you on the end of it, it might have a different thought. It's high. It's probably one of them ones you toss a coin, 50% of referees flash a red without even thinking."
