Sheikh Mansour makes ‘statement’ with new £100m Man City investment – Club adviser

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Sheikh Mansour’s recent move to pump more money into Manchester City’s City Football Group is intended to reiterate his commitment to the club.

That is according to former Everton chief executive and ex-Premier League board member Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Citizens’ owners are still adamant that they are not going anywhere.

This comes amid the news that Mansour has just invested a further £100million into the running of CFG, despite the flagship Man City project continuing to be under investigation.

Wyness believes that is Mansour’s way of showing that he won’t be moved by the Premier League’s final verdict, and intends to stay put at the club regardless of the outcome.

City are increasingly likely to be hit with a significant points deduction as a result of their 114 financial charges, which could potentially be enough to see them relegated.

The Manchester giants have already appealed against the guilty verdict, and the saga is continuing to rumble on behind the scenes at the moment.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that City’s “in-house” legal department will be working “overtime” to try and absolve the club of guilt at the moment.

With that in mind, he feels that Mansour’s show of commitment to the project serves as a timely move from the ownership.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained the thinking behind the £100m boost.

“I think it's a statement of saying, we're not going anywhere. That's one thing,” he said.

“I think also, the money was probably needed in terms of the legal bills that are being racked up. And I know that City have got a very big in-house legal department, so they'll be working overtime at the moment.

“So I think overall, it's a statement from the owners. You know, there are so many rumours about what's going on behind the scenes regarding this case. Nobody really knows, and it has been kept very well in terms of secrecy so far.

“But it's got such a big impact on the whole football picture. We really need to know sooner rather than later. But yes, it's a statement, as I say, from the owners to say, look, we're not going anywhere.”

Former Manchester United chief scout Mick Brown recently told Football Insider that City’s squad are likely being “distracted” by the 114 charges to some extent of late.

Enzo Maresca’s side have been far from fluid of late, suffering defeats against Brighton and Aston Villa, while also struggling past both the Seagulls and RB Leipzig in cup competitions.

Brown believes that Maresca’s group will naturally be concerned by what is going on at the club away from the pitch, and feels that it could be having a detrimental effect on their displays.

City remain second in the Premier League table, but are already three points adrift of league leaders Arsenal.
