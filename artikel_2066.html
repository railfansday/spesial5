Bruno Fernandes clash and Matheus Cunha protest role: Four things you missed from Man United vs Roma

Manchester United's 3-2 win over AS Roma saw plenty of drama both on and off the pitch.

The Red Devils came back from being 2-0 down to hand Michael Carrick another lifeline at Old Trafford.

With another loss on the board, the Englishman's future would undoubtedly continue to be a hot topic.

While he is far from safe, it does at least take the heat off the Old Trafford boss for now.

With five goals scored, there were plenty of incidents and talking points emerging.

But here are four things you may have missed from the action.

Man United fans have been growing increasingly frustrated with the ownership over the last few years, and it is continuing to reach boiling point.

Against Roma, supporters in the stands opted to throw tennis balls onto the pitch as a form of protest.

The game was halted as the tennis balls were removed from the pitch, and that process was helped by Man United's stars.

One of them was Matheus Cunha, who assisted in removing the balls as quickly as possible to get the match back underway.

Jason Wilcox and Omar Berrada, who were perhaps the intended targets of recent frustration, were spotted watching on as tennis balls flooded the field.

According to The Guardian, Wilcox and Berrada had a "miserable" look on their face.

Their reaction offered an interesting insight into the mood inside the stadium, with United's hierarchy unable to escape the visible anger surrounding the club.

And fans will be hoping their presence at the stadium amid the protest is enough to enact the change they want to see.

Bruno Fernandes found himself involved in a heated exchange with Roma forward Matias Soule during the contest.

The Argentine winger was down on the ground injured, but the Portuguese winger decided against putting the ball out of play.

That sparked an intense reaction from the Serie A ace, with the two ultimately getting booked in the process.

Cunha was involved in plenty throughout the game, with him eventually getting sent off for a second yellow card.

The Brazilian looked to get onto the pitch following full-time celebrations, but Carrick had other ideas.

The Englishman told Cunha to go back to the dressing room, sparking a frustrated reaction from the United forward.

Cunha may have been coming on to confront the referee for his dismissal, so Carrick may have saved him from making his punishment worse.
