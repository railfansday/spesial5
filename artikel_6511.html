Polenta Fries With Roasted Garlic Dipping Sauce

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

These polenta fries flavored with Grana Padano cheese are reminiscent of the panisses, or chickpea-flour fries, of Nice. They’re served alongside an umami-rich dipping sauce of roasted garlic, Dijon mustard, and anchovies spiked with tangy Tabasco and smoky chipotle chile in adobo.
