We Tried Every Boxed Stuffing We Could Find—These Are The Only Ones We’d Buy Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Some mixes taste so homemade, they just might fool your Thanksgiving guests.

In my opinion, stuffing is the crème de la crème of a Thanksgiving feast. I’d choose it over any other carb or veggie side. Everyone knows stuffing balances a buttery, crispy top with a moist, tender interior and subtle aromatics. It should be the greatest complement to a turkey. But sometimes we can’t get around to making a homemade stuffing on Thanksgiving, so we settle for a box mix.

We tried a variety of stuffings so you don’t have to wonder which one to pick up at the grocery store. We were looking for stuffing that could be mistaken for homemade, with a balanced texture and real seasoning. We tried eight stuffings, but only five were worthy of adding to your holiday table or weeknight rotation.

This ShopRite stuffing is super weeknight-friendly, and I wouldn’t hesitate to make this for an easy dinner side. Because it’s made with chicken broth, you can use water to make it without losing too much flavor.

This Pepperidge Farm Stuffing was the most surprising one I tried. Because it contains whole wheat and therefore is a little darker in color, I wasn’t sure I would like it. The flavor was excellent, though, and I really appreciated the homemade feel of having to add your own fresh chopped onion and celery.

It's admittedly been years since I had my end-all, be-all favorite boxed stuffing as a kid, Stovetop’s Savory Herbs. And you know what? It really holds up.

As the name suggests, this boxed stuffing mix is Big-S Savory and loaded with herbs, with a buttery, peppery backbone. It gets perfectly crispy around the edges. It’s both nostalgic-good and delicious enough to keep a box on hand for after Turkey Day, if not just to make these sausage stuffing bites.

Here is where the fooling comes in, with Kinder's Brown Butter & Herbs Stuffing Mix. One bite of this garlicky, nutty stuffing had me say out loud, “Oh, this is deluxe.”

Not only that, but this might be the easiest stuffing of them all, with no fewer than three different ways to prepare and serve it. There’s a sausage-spiked version, a skillet-made version, and an ultra-savory version with leeks. In short, if you're really in the mood for a homemade stuffing experience, but you're running out of prep time, Kinder's stuffing will trick everyone into thinking you made this fully on your own.

While Kinder’s is great when you still want to eat like a Thanksgiving maximalist, some of us want the easiest (but no less delicious) route. In which case, Mrs. Cubbison’s Traditional Stuffing will take you there.

I’ve long been a fan of the brand’s herby, buttery croutons, so I’m not surprised that Mrs. Cubbison’s Traditional Stuffing is yet another one of its products I'll always keep on hand. The bread base is just the right amount of toasted and caramelized, making for a stuffing that's tender and crispy around the edges (no mush here). Plus, it'll even free up some space in your oven since it can be made in the slow cooker, too.
