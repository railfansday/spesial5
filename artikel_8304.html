Outside Days Returns to Denver with Jack Johnson, Jungle, Sierra Ferrell, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Mark your calendar: Outside Days is returning in 2027 with an incredible musical lineup and an inspiring mix of experiences celebrating outdoor culture and community. The fourth annual festival, presented by Capital One and REI Co-op, will take place in Denver, Colorado, at the Auraria Campus in June 2027. It’ll feature three days of music, from June 4 to 6, featuring Jack Johnson, Jungle, Sierra Ferrell, Big Wild, Trevor Hall, Dr. Dog, and more.

Tickets for Outside Days are on sale Friday, November 20 at 10 A.M. MT.

Music runs three days, June 4 to 6, and features Jack Johnson, Jungle, Sierra Ferrell, Big Wild, Trevor Hall, Dr. Dog, Almost Monday, Jalen Ngonda, Chance Peña, Medium Build, Amistat, Andy Frasco & The U.N., Steph Strings, Brothers Of Brass, DOGTAGS, The Galentines, and more.

Outside Days is more than a festival you attend; it’s one you join. From live music and inspiring conversations to hands-on experiences that invite exploration and discovery, the festival brings people together around a shared passion for the outdoors.

Beyond the music, attendees can explore adventure film screenings, hear from world-renowned athletes, explorers, and storytellers, and take part in activities ranging from climbing and overlanding to wellness, outdoor cooking, and gear demonstrations. Many of these experiences are created in collaboration with Outside’s brand partners, giving festivalgoers new ways to learn, play, and connect.

Also returning is the Outside Days Industry Conference, which precedes the festival on Thursday and Friday, June 3 and 4, and features thought-provoking panels, interactive workshops, and curated networking experiences for professionals across the outdoor, travel, and active lifestyle industries. An Industry Pass includes access to the conference and the festival’s opening night, while bundled passes extend access through the full weekend.

As the presenting sponsor of Outside Days, Capital One is offering cardholders early access to tickets and exclusive festival perks, including express entry, special discounts, and access to the Capital One Cardholder Lounge. Eligible Capital One Credit and Debit Cardholders, including REI Co-op® Mastercard® holders, can purchase presale tickets starting Monday, November 16 at 10 A.M. MT through Thursday, November 19 at 10 A.M. MT, while supplies last. Tickets must be purchased using an eligible Capital One card during the presale window. Excludes Capital One-issued private label cards. Terms apply.

Tickets go on sale to the general public on Friday, November 20 at 10 A.M. MT, with early bird pricing available while supplies last.

Prices are inclusive of fees for general admission. VIP packages feature access to premium main stage viewing and the VIP lounge. New for 2027, the Super VIP (SVIP) ticket offers the ultimate festival experience starting at $324.50 for a single day.

Outside+ members can add access to the Outside+ lounge when purchasing general admission or VIP three-day tickets and receive $25 off the upgrade. Special meet-and-greets with elite athletes and outdoor luminaries will be included with Outside+ lounge access, plus perks like private bathrooms and exclusive food and beverage options. Outside Days continues to be family-friendly, offering free entry for children ages 12 and under when accompanied by a ticketed adult.

Outside Days is possible with support from Capital One, REI Co-op, the Jeep® brand, Brooks Running, Visit Costa Rica, Visit Denver, and the State of Colorado. Additional programming details, including speakers, films, and the full schedule, will be announced in the coming months. For updates and tickets, visit outside-days.com.
