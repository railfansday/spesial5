Top 10 Resorts in the West for Challenging Terrain (2027)

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

If you’ve been putting in the work this fall and you’re looking for terrain that will push your limits, consider this your hit list. These resorts earned their reputation for challenging skiing with steep pitches, sustained mogul fields, tight trees, technical chutes, exposed lines, and hike-to terrain that demands fitness, confidence, and skills.

Whether you thrive on leg-burning bumps, navigate tight trees with ease, or seek out steep lines and mandatory air, these 10 Western ski areas deliver some of the most demanding inbounds skiing around. They’re the toughest resorts in the West, according to SKI readers who rated their favorite mountains in our annual Reader Resort Survey.
