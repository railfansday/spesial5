Can you name every club to have won the Serie A title?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Sixteen different clubs have won the Serie A title since it was founded in 1898. Can you name them all?

We’re sure you’ll name the three dominant clubs, and some of the other giants of Italian calcio, but this quiz will test your historical knowledge.

We’ve given you 15 minutes to get them all, and your clues are the number of Scudettos each club has won – as well as the season of their last title.
