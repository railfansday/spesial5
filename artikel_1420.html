Hermoso included in Champions League Team of the Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Mario Hermoso has received further recognition.

The Roma defender has been included in UEFA’s Team of the Week for Matchday 5 of the Champions League domestic season, thanks to his performance in the 2-2 draw against Paris Saint-Germain at the Parc des Princes.

The Spaniard was selected by UEFA’s technical observers, who recognized his performances in both defense and attack.

Hermoso was the star of the match against PSG, scoring the goal that temporarily led 1-0 after just five minutes.

The defender also stood out for his ability to counter the Parisian attackers, dominating duels and recording 11 recovered balls.

Two other Serie A players are also included in the all-time starting lineup for matchday five: Inter’s Nicolò Barella and Como’s Luis Milla.
