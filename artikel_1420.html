WATCH: The Hawks Of Hill Dickinson Stadium

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The latest episode of our YouTube series Behind Blue Eyes – presented by Official Training Kit Partner Christopher Ward – takes us behind the scenes with Marc Carney, the Club's resident falconer, who plays a unique role in looking after Hill Dickinson Stadium and Goodison Park.

Responsible for keeping, training and flying his hawks around both grounds, Marc helps deter nuisance birds such as pigeons and gulls, ensuring the stadiums remain in the best possible condition.

We join Marc for a fascinating insight into his day-to-day work at the Club, learning more about his birds of prey, the relationships he builds with them and the important role they play in maintaining Everton's two iconic venues.
