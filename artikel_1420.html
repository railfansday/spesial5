Soule and Wesley set to start vs PSG

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

After their victory at Parma, Roma began preparing for their Champions League match at the Parc des Princes against Luis Enrique’s PSG.

The Tardini game also changed thanks to the introduction of two key players in Gasperini’s tactical setup: Wesley and Soulé.

The Brazilian winger provided three assists, including the one where Troilo failed to save, and the former Frosinone player brought brilliance, quality, and intensity to the attacking play.

Gian Piero Gasperini is ready to make some changes to the starting lineup that started against Parma.

As Il Tempo reports, Wesley and Soulé will return to the starting lineup, with the Brazilian completing the wing pairing alongside Molina.

The former Frosinone player, meanwhile, is expected to partner Paulo Dybala behind the unstoppable Malen. Evan Ndicka is also ready to return in place of Leonardo Balerdi, who is struggling with an ankle injury.
