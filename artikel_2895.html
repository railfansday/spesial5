Cowboys trade deadline: Dallas holds and prepares for rest of season

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The NFL trade deadline has officially come and gone and the Dallas Cowboys did not do anything.

If we are being fair, the Cowboys absolutely did do something this season, they simply did not do it on deadline day. Dallas traded for Joey Porter Jr. over a month ago and that sort of painted them into a corner to not be able to do anything else given that they were strapped for capital.

1: Dallas owns a first-round pick, but it's the worse between their pick and Green Bay's as the better of the two belongs to the New York Jets

2: The Cowboys own their own second-round pick, nice and easy

4: Dallas owns Pittsburgh's fourth-round pick in the aftermath of the Broderick Jones deal

7: The Cowboys picked up a seventh-round pick from Houston in the Nate Thomas trade, but the Texans held it by way of the New England Patriots, so that is the team we are keeping an eye on to see where it lands

Again, the math always pointed to the team holding on Tuesday specifically and here we are.

Now the 2026 team is officially out to sea with no air-carrier reinforcements coming.
