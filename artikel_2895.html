Links to Varsity 845 high school and Varsity 845U college sports results and schedules for winter 2026-27

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Varsity 845 has changed the way it will present Section 9 high school sports scores. Instead of the weekly all-sports roundups, the website will host one web page at the top of RecordOnline.com/sports, with links to each individual sport - this will allow for readers interested in their favorite sports to find results and schedules quickly.

There will also be links to collegiate Varsity 845U seasonal scores and schedules of our Mid-Hudson schools for quick access.

To report Section 9 varsity scores (from OCIAA and MHAL schools, plus Delaware Valley, Pa.): Coaches or team representatives are asked to send scores and highlight information for both teams (first and last names, please), win, tie or lose, as soon as possible by emailing sports@th-record.com. Please include a name and contact number.
