Kayvon Thibodeaux could return to NY Giants, says NFL insider

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

"There is a scenario" in which free-agent-to-be edge defender Kayvon Thibodeaux returns to the New York Giants next season, according to NFL insider Jordan Schultz. Schultz made that statement during an appearance Monday on the 'Valentine's Views' podcast.

"I would have said no a month ago. I would have said no six weeks ago," Schultz said. "There is a real sentiment inside that building that Kayvon Thibodeaux has turned the corner. He's turned the corner emotionally, maturity."

Trade rumors have swirled around the 25-year-old Thibodeaux since the Giants made edge defender Abdul Carter the No. 3 overall pick in the 2025 NFL Draft. With Carter and Brian Burns, who is now out for the season with a torn ACL, it has seemed likely that Thibodeaux would leave the Giants for a bigger role and a bigger paycheck at the end of the season.

"I think Brian Burns has helped him from the outside looking in, and I've asked people what does that mean when a guy turns his corner? Does that mean he's playing better and there is a sense of that but there's also just more of a sense of urgency," Schultz said. "This was another really high pick. I think it was a top five pick and obviously has been someone that Giants fans have wanted more out of.

"You're starting to see it now and I do believe for the first time over the last let's call it year and a half, two years that there is a scenario in which Kayvon Thibodeaux is there, and I never would have thought that previously."

Statistically, Thibodeaux is not having a great season. He does not have a sack, has just three quarterback hits, per Pro Football Focus is averaging a career-low two pressures per game, and his PFF pass rush win percentage of 9.3 is the second-lowest of his career.

Yet, there is a sense that Thibodeaux does a lot of the dirty, unnoticed work that has helped the Giants' defense rank fifth in the league in yards and 11th in point allowed.

Will that be enough to keep him in New York? Maybe. If the Giants can find the money.

"It's really hard to pay three different dudes [at] essentially the same position the same type of money," Schultz said. "The Giants are not going to be able to pay three dudes $25-30 million dollars [at] essentially the same spot."
