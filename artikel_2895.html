Alabama high school football first-round scores, live updates in AHSAA playoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The road to the AHSAA football state championships started long ago, but now the pinnacle is just around the corner.

The first round of the AHSAA football playoffs kicks off this week, with most games taking place Friday, Nov. 6. Four rounds determine which teams advance to the state championships Dec. 2-5 in Mobile.

Most of the Tuscaloosa-area football programs are available to stream on the NFHS Network. It is a subscription service which costs $13.99 but gives to access to all content on the site, across all sports.

It is up to schools to provide the stream, so it's not a guarantee all games in all sports will be available, but most area schools look to have at least some football games on the site.
