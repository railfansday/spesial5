Watch Dillon Brooks' ejection after going nose-to-nose with Kevin Durant while play goes on

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

For context, the Rockets were starting to pull away from the Suns at the end of Wednesday night's NBA Cup game, and after a Fred VanVleet 3, he and Phoenix's favorite agitator, Dillon Brooks, got into it verbally and picked up double technical fouls. Then on the next Rockets possession, this happened — watch Kevin Durant and Brooks go nose-to-nose while everyone else plays 4-on-4.

Durant and Brooks were each given a technical, which was Brooks' second and he was ejected. The Rockets won 116-104, and we can even put a Thanksgiving bow on this one.
