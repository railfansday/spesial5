The Puka Nacua injury update is not good

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Los Angeles Rams will be without their biggest offensive weapon for at least the next four games. Wide receiver Puka Nacua is headed to injured reserve after suffering MCL and PCL injuries to his knee, creating a significant challenge for Sean McVay and an offense that has consistently relied on his production.

Nacua suffered the injury during Sunday's 20-13 victory over the Washington Commanders. The injury occurred on the Rams' final offensive possession when Nacua took a hard hit to his knee on a jet sweep. What initially appeared to be a concerning situation has now resulted in a trip to injured reserve and an extended absence.

The timing is particularly unfortunate for Los Angeles. Nacua was coming off an outstanding performance against Washington, recording 14 receptions for 150 yards and a touchdown. He has been Matthew Stafford's most dependable target and arguably the engine behind the Rams' passing attack.

Nacua has 70 catches for 715 yards and four touchdowns, averaging 102.1 yards per game. He led the NFL with 129 catches and 107.2 yards per game in 2025. Nacua's production is rare and offenses rarely rely on one player as much as L.A. has put on their number one receiver over the last 3.5 seasons.

With Nacua unavailable for the next four games, the Rams will need their remaining offensive playmakers to shoulder a much larger workload. Davante Adams is also on injured reserve, making the situation even more difficult for Stafford.

Los Angeles still has postseason aspirations, but losing Nacua for the next month puts additional pressure on McVay to find offensive production elsewhere.
