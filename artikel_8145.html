Someone on your holiday shopping list needs new skis. But a big-ticket item is a high-stakes purchase, which means the pressure is on to get it right. Below, you’ll find a handy cheat sheet pointing you to the right ski for every type of skier.

You’ll notice that every ski on this list is made by Vermont-based brand Renoun. That’s because Renoun skis feature patented VibeStop™ Technology, a vibration-dampening material embedded in the ski’s core that automatically adapts to changing conditions. As terrain gets rougher and vibrations increase, VibeStop responds to absorb chatter and improve stability without sacrificing responsiveness. The result? Smoother turns, greater control, and less fatigue, so you can ski harder, stay out longer, and still have energy left at the end of the day. As Renoun puts it, “It’s the only ski built to get smoother the harder you push it.” Which is exactly what makes it an incredible gift.

With that in mind, here are five skis to consider for the hardcore skier on your list this season—plus, the one gift for the skier who already has it all.

The highly adaptable Endurance 98 surfs through powder, holds firm on hardpack, and excels in tight trees. Embedded VibeStop channels dampen chatter, reducing fatigue on your legs while making tough turns, and a 20-percent early-rise tip and 10-percent tail rocker keep you agile in variable snow. Even better: it comes with two cores to choose from: a newly redesigned aspen/maple or a lighter full-aspen core.

A true all-mountain ski, the Endurance 88 ties back to Renoun’s Vermont roots. A maple core, two full sheets of Titanal, and six channels of VibeStop help it hold firm on icy corduroy,  while a 15-percent early-rise tip gives it enough lift to charge through even the chunkiest East Coast snow.

Built with input from women skiers and testers, the Earhart 88 is a lightweight, highly responsive ski that offers effortless steering in a wide range of conditions. An aspen core and a Titanal band boost control and edge hold without adding excess weight, and VibeStop reduces chatter and vibration, helping minimize fatigue on long ski days.

The Atlas 80 is built to carve. A maple core with two sheets of Titanal and VibeStop inlays ensures a smooth ride at speed, even on firm snow. Add to that a 95% camber and flat tail, and you’ve got an effective edge that locks you into turns. That makes the Atlas a perfect fit for resort skiers who need superior power, speed, and control.

With a wide waist and 30-percent early-rise tip, the Citadel 107 is a powder ski to its very bones. Eight VibeStop™ channels soak up chatter on crust and chunks, while an aspen core and full carbon laminate keep it light enough for touring.
