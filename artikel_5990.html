Hugh Hewitt’s Amnesty

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Trump should indeed change course, but this isn’t the way to do it.

Left: President Donald Trump speaks at a White House press briefing, February 20, 2026. Right: Radio host Hugh Hewitt speaks at Arizona State University in Tempe, Ariz., January 13, 2017.

Kevin Lamarque/Reuters; Gage Skidmore via Wikimedia Commons

The conservative journalist Hugh Hewitt suggests that President Trump issue a pardon for some illegal immigrants:

Hewitt believes that “the public would first be thunderstruck and then almost wholly united in praising his act of mercy.” The beneficiaries and their families would be grateful to Trump for the rest of their lives. And the Republican Party would benefit from the “course-correction” to the excesses of the administration’s deportation policies.

Ramesh Ponnuru is the editor of National Review, a columnist for the Washington Post, and a fellow at the American Enterprise Institute.
