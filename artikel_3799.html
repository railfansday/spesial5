How to get the Gold Lil’ Uzi Vert skin in Fortnite

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The second week of Fortnite Treasure Hunters is all about Lil’ Uzi Vert, and you can get his Gold skin to add to your collection. However, this will be easier said than done once you explore the core method.

Let’s find out how to get this character skin added to your Treasure Hunters rewards.

The Lil’ Uzi Vert skin isn’t part of any pass, if you’re wondering. Instead, you can get it for free by simply opening the chests on your runs. Chests now have a different look, matching the spirits of the season.

The Lil’ Uzi Vert skin is part of the several items that are in the pool of rewards for week 2. These rewards, including the skin itself, will remain in the pool until the next Sunday, Nov. 15. While some rewards, like the Pirate Shop access token, will continue in the pool of rewards, the same can’t be said about the Lil’ Uzi Vert skin.

Hence, you’ll want to open as many chests as possible. Playing in squads also helps since you can open more chests together. Everyone gets the skin if one member manages to get the skin as a loot drop.

You can also target the different vaults that are located around the map. Opening them will reward you with Rare Chests that have better drop rates for rarer items.

Thanks to Epic Games, we now have a fair idea about the exact drop rate of this golden skin. Under ordinary conditions, you’ll have a 1:1562500 chance of obtaining the gold skin from a regular chest.

This makes it rarer than the Pirate Shop access token, which is remarkable to say the least. Power Hours will improve the odds in your favor, but it’s fair to say that the skin isn’t going to be dropping around like eggs.

If you find it, consider yourself to be extremely lucky. Otherwise, the next Power Hours on Nov. 14 will be your best shot when the odds will be boosted by 7.5x.
