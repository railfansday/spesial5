GTA 6 pre-load dates and file size: When can you download the game on PS5

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Less than a month remains before GTA 6 goes live, and everyone is eagerly watching for breadcrumbs about what the final experience is like. While Rockstar remains tight-lipped about the final details, we have confirmation about the game’s file size and pre-load dates.

Based on the official sources, we won’t have to wait long before we can pre-load the game on our consoles.

As of writing, the file size for GTA 6 on PlayStation 5 is 212 GB, but this remains unconfirmed. For Xbox consoles, players will need to keep a similar amount of reserve space to download and install the game.

According to the official news, GTA 6 will be available to download starting Nov. 10. Hence, we get more than a week before the game actually goes live worldwide, which will reportedly happen at midnight.

If you’re on Xbox, you’ll need to wait until 7pm PDT/9pm CDT(Nov. 9)/12am GMT (Nov. 10) to begin the pre-downloads. All codes you find inside the box versions will be available for download starting Nov. 12.
