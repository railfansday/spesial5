Solving Reverse Pricing Across Step-Function Marketplace Fees in TypeScript (Poshmark $15 Cliff & 2026 Depop/Mercari Rules)

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Most online marketplace fee calculators only work in one direction: you type a selling price and it subtracts a flat percentage. When building Quick Fee Calculator, I wanted every platform tool to support accurate Reverse Pricing—letting a reseller or freelancer type a target net profit (say, $25 net or $100 exact payout) and immediately solve for the exact minimum listing or invoice price.

Modern US marketplaces like eBay, Whatnot, and Depop apply payment processing or boosted ad percentages to the entire buyer checkout total (item price + buyer shipping + sales tax). On Depop US in 2026, sellers pay 0% selling commission, 3.3% + $0.45 payment processing, and an optional 12% Boosted Listing fee—all applied on top of shipping and tax.
