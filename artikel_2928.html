Wizards at Bulls final score: Washington wins 98-94

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Washington Wizards defeated the Chicago Bulls 98-94 on Friday night to earn their second consecutive win and improve to a 6-11 record.

Anthony Davis scored 36 points and 11 rebounds to lead Washington to the win. AJ Dybantsa also added 20 points and 10 rebounds. For Chicago, Norman Powell led with 16 points.

Washington's next game is on Sunday when they play the Orlando Magic. Tipoff is at 7 p.m. ET. See you then.
