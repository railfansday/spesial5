Gary Woodland withdraws from Hero World Challenge following surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Gary Woodland announced on his Instagram page Friday that he recently underwent knee surgery and will not compete in the Hero World Challenge.

The 42-year-old won this year's Texas Children's Houston Open, weeks after revealing to Golf Channel's Rex Hoggard that he has suffered from PTSD in the wake of his 2023 surgery to remove a lesion from his brain.

Woodland finished the year 29th in FedExCup points, qualifying for the Tour Championship for the first time since 2019.

No replacement was immediately announced for Woodland in the Hero World Challenge, Dec. 3-6 in the Bahamas. Tournament host Tiger Woods announced earlier this week that he would make his return to competition in the 20-man field.
