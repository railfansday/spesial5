Broncos offense aiming to turn flashes into consistency

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Bo Nix and the Denver Broncos offense are seemingly always under the microscope. Analyzed, then reanalyzed, with hot takes thrown in to make it obtuse.

There's no doubt that through the first nine games of the season, both have been "meh." Yet despite that, the Broncos are 7-2. Over the last few weeks, Nix and Denver's offense have been trending in the right direction.

Now, coming out of the bye week, the Broncos need to start stringing together consistent offensive performances.

As Nix said to the media earlier this week:

"I think even if we were playing great that we would feel really good. I think with the numbers and stats, we will always want more. That's the thing about stats is that they are never good enough. For us, it's always about the win column and being efficient and helping our team by playing complementary football.

"Overall, during the season, we've done that. When we've needed to score, we've scored. When we've needed a stop, we get a stop, and then our special teams are playing really well. Overall, as a team, we are playing well and complementary off of each other. We just need to finish strong. We haven't really done much yet, so we have to keep going."

One of the most effective ways to help the Broncos offense play better is to run the ball effectively. That takes pressure off Nix and the passing game, but forces the defense to stop the run. In the process, it sets up play-action and the vertical passing game.

"I think we can hit them with the variables and run with more conviction," Broncos offensive coordinator Davis Webb said to the media this week. "We can do a better job of handling the fronts that we have, and dominating the five- and six-man boxes, and getting downhill, and being satisfied with five-, six-yard gains and getting a little bit more explosive after that.

"We've done a good job this year of not running too many negative runs. We haven't had negative one, twos. I know there were a couple last week, but for the most part of the last nine games, there haven't been many of those. We've been on track, but we can definitely do a better job of getting to second-and-5 as opposed to second-and-8 and second-and-9 and be in a better position on second down."

Added Broncos head coach Sean Payton: "(You) reduce fundamentals. All the things that you do as a teacher. … Let's get more reps at certain things to become better."

For Nix and the Broncos, it's about peaking at the right time.

The next step, especially for the offense, comes today against the Las Vegas Raiders. They had a good game against the Carolina Panthers the last time they were on the field. Now it's time to stack another good outing on top of it today.

"We have to keep progressing, and everyone on the team will tell you that there is room for growth," Nix said to the media. "I'm not too worried about the things that come with the wins. It's all about, 'Did I put my team in a spot to win the game?'"
