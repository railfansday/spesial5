LSU women's basketball v Texas Southern live updates, score prediction

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

BATON ROUGE – LSU women's basketball set a program for margin of victory in its 129-35 win over Southeastern Louisiana in its season opener.

Despite the 94-point victory from her team, Kim Mulkey was not happy with how the Tigers played on the defensive end in the second half as the Lions shot 50% from the floor.

No. 8 LSU (1-0) will look to play cleaner defense the whole game against Texas Southern (0-1) on Thursday, Nov. 5 (7 p.m. CST, SEC Network+) inside the Pete Maravich Assembly Center.

Led by senior guard MiLaysia Fulwiley's six swipes, LSU garnered 24 steals against Southeastern Louisiana and five players recorded multiple steals. For the game, LSU forced 38 turnovers which led to 48 points.

Texas Southern dropped its season opener at Tulane, 92-38, Monday. TSU struggled to get much going offensively as no players reached double figures in scoring. Ball security was an issue as well as it coughed it up 24 times which the Green Wave converted to 24 points.
