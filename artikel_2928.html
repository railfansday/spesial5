NFL predictions: Titans-Jaguars matchup for the second time in three weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

In a weird scheduling quirk, the Tennessee Titans will face the Jacksonville Jaguars for the third time in three weeks. The first matchup did not go so well for the Titans. They lost 40-23 at Nissan Stadium.

The only good news for the Titans here is that Parker Washington, who had 7 catches for 152 yards and a touchdown in the first matchup, will miss this game with a shoulder injury. I wish that was going to make a big difference, but it won't. Trevor Lawrence will still be able to light this secondary up.

Cam Ward had a solid game against the Jags, throwing for 222 yards with 2 touchdowns and an interception. The Titans will need him to throw for 300+ to have a chance to win here.

The Titans are a 13.5-point underdog in this one. It is tough for me to envision a world where they are able to cover that number. I'm going with Jaguars 44 Titans 27. What's your prediction?
