GrowthLoop: Partner Marketing Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

As our Partner Marketing Manager, you’ll own marketing to, with, and through our partners, building on an established partner strategy. You’ll work closely with our partnerships and marketing teams to bring joint solutions to market, build relationships across our ecosystem, and help partner sellers understand where GrowthLoop fits and when to bring us into a customer conversation. This is a hands-on role: you’ll shape the plan and drive the details that deliver results.

GrowthLoop built the first composable CDP, helping marketers leverage live, first-party data from their cloud warehouse to launch highly targeted campaigns. That composable foundation gave way to the Compound Marketing Engine, an evolution of the composable CDP that drives compound growth by using agentic AI to accelerate the marketing cycle.

Now, we’re building what comes next: an agentic system that uses causality data to find the actions that actually improve customer outcomes and gets smarter with every campaign cycle.

Founder-led, we’re partnering with the world’s most innovative brands to close the loop between people, data, and AI. Enterprise marketing teams at Google, Costco, Ford, and Whirlpool (to name a few) choose GrowthLoop to launch smarter, more personalized marketing programs that compound growth with every campaign.

You’re a builder who enjoys turning a shared idea into something real, whether it’s a partner event, a joint launch, or a campaign that opens doors. You take ownership from planning through follow-up, bring clarity when priorities shift, and care about the details that make the work successful. You build trust by listening, communicating directly and kindly, and doing what you say you’ll do. You’re curious about our partners, our customers, and how to do the work better, including using new tools thoughtfully. You pitch in where needed, make the people around you better, and measure success by what the team and our partners achieve together.

The on-target earnings range for this role is USD $125,000–$135,000. Total compensation includes equity.

Final base salary decisions will be based on a variety of non-discriminatory factors unique to each candidate, such as the individual’s skill set, location, depth of experience, and qualifications.

GrowthLoop is an Equal Opportunity Employer that considers applicants without regard to gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, race, ethnicity, disabled or veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law.
