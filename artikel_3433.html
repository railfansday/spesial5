Pokémon All-Star Collection Officially Releasing November 27

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Much of the excitement around Pokémon's 30th anniversary has started to fade away, though it would be fair to say that it has been something of an unmitigated success for The Pokémon Company and everyone else involved. The TCG release, in particular, has been a real highlight, but there was plenty of exciting stuff going on besides that, which really made it a year to remember.

We finally got confirmation of the first pairing of the tenth generation of games, as Winds and Waves takes players to an archipelago based on certain portions of South-East Asia. Game Freak and everyone involved in development could really do with taking a big swing on this one, and it does seem as though it could be a real evolution beyond what we have been playing for the last few years.

There have been plenty of other releases besides that have all played a part in the larger tapestry of celebration, and there's now another one to come that's easily one of the cutest of the bunch.

As first reported by CBR, some of the most beloved Pokémon in the history of the franchise are set to appear as part of a new collection released by San-ei Boeki. The San-ei Baeki All Star Collection Plush Toys Vol. 32 and All Star Collection Mascots Vol. 10 are both set to arrive for the first time on November 27, and they could be a great option for those with burgeoning physical collections.

The Vol. 32 release features five new plush toys, while the Vol. 10 set includes ten new toys. Specifically, the former allows fans to grab Psyduck, Slowpoke, Snorlax, Larvitar, and Paldean Wooper, all for between ¥2,200 and ¥2,400 (~$14), while the latter has Pikachu, Raichu, Poliwag, Swinub, Plusle, Minum, Swablu, Jirachi, Trubbish, and Minccino, all at ¥1,980 (~$12.50) apiece.

The only unfortunate caveat to everyone getting in on the action is that they have thus far only been confirmed for sale in Japan at the time of writing, meaning they might be a little harder to secure if you live in the West. That said, there are contingency options that will make it possible to get hold of them. You can either pick them up from resellers (which is usually quite easy for releases of this type), or buy them officially and have them shipped by a third-party, which won't be too pricey for items of this weight.

Despite coming out all those years ago, this release is also a sign that the original run of 151 Pokémon remains the most popular of any generation seen since. They are uniquely iconic, and the company's desire to keep them prominent has only furthered their legacy in the eyes of many.

Other than that, it's simply going to be a case of sitting tight and waiting to hear more about Winds and Waves for Pokémon fans. A full reveal should hopefully come in the first half of next year, hopefully giving us a proper look at some of the designs they have been cooking up for the tenth generation.

Pokémon is a Japanese multimedia franchise created by Satoshi Tajiri and Ken Sugimori, launched by Nintendo, Game Freak, and Creatures in 1996. Centered on fictional creatures called Pokémon, the series spans video games, trading cards, animation, films, merchandise, and competitive events. Players assume the role of Trainers who capture, battle, and trade Pokémon. It is one of the highest-grossing entertainment franchises in history.
