Beastie Boys’ Mike D Brings Solo Album — and His Sons — to Tiny Desk Concert

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Mike D of the Beastie Boys stopped by NPR’s Tiny Desk to perform six tracks off his solo album, Thank You, giving the effect-heavy songs a stripped-back spin.

,Fresh off a small tour with his live band, Mike D 5D — which includes his sons, Davis and Skylar Diamond on electronics — the 61-year-old kicked things off with the jumpy “Make It Stop,” followed by “That’s Right” and “I Don’t Care.”

The album’s August release date marked two milestones: the first time a member had released solo music and the first release since Adam Yauch (aka MCA) died in 2012 of cancer. Speaking to Rolling Stone earlier this year, Mike D reflected on what it took to make an album for the first time without Yauch and Adam Horovitz (aka Ad-Rock) by his side.

“With Yauch, there was so much intertwined about him being an older-brother figure to me, to what he represented to us being in this band together,” he explained. “I had to go through all that … I grieve him because I don’t get to live my life being able to call him up and say, “Yauch, you wouldn’t believe this thing happened, but I have to tell you about this … But I can think about what he might be thinking, or what his relationship would be to this thing that I’m going through, or just me making this record and what he might say.”
