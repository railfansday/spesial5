‘Buffy the Vampire Slayer’ Officially Returns in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Dynamite had announced in July 2025 that it had acquired the comic-book rights to Buffy the Vampire Slayer and Angel. Then things got much more concrete about resurrecting the IP in 2026 because Buffy got a release date for July 2026, and Angel was supposed to follow in August 2026. But then July came and went with no new Buffy ​​​​​​release.

It was also announced that Kelly Thompson, who is actively working on Absolute Wonder Woman, was attached to Buffy the Vampire Slayer as a writer, with Stephen Byrne as the artist. Thompson is now finally clearing the air about the delays and confusion, and recently went on the Off Panel podcast to unveil the actual reason behind the delays. Not to mention that Angel #1 was supposed to debut on August 19, 2026, and Dynamite Entertainment cancelled all initial retailer orders for this other Thompson series in late August 2026, too. This naturally made some fans wonder whether Dynamite had killed the projects entirely, especially considering that Absolute Wonder Woman kept moving forward.

It hadn’t, and Thompson at the time had clarified that the books had to be re-solicited, so the existing ones had to be cancelled first. During her chat with David Harper, however, Thompson went on to reveal that while “some of the delay was [her] own fault,” there were other legal factors at play. She went on to add: “Part of the reason I haven’t been doing a great job at that is that, for reasons I can’t discuss, that are complicated.” And then Thompson’s clearest possible answer perhaps came out while she expressed her sentiments about the delayed release in the following words:

“Especially with complicated IP and the legal side of things, whatever, there were some things that had to be worked out, and here we are. I’m very excited. We’re finally getting started. If people don’t know, March 2027 is the date. Buffy comes first, and then Angel.”

The contrast with Absolute Wonder Woman explains Thompson’s scheduling problem. Buffy #1 and Angel #1 were both pulled from their schedules and eventually shifted to March 2027 amid creative, publishing, and undisclosed legal complications. Meanwhile, Thompson and Hayden Sherman’s DC series simply kept moving. Absolute Wonder Woman #23 arrived August 26, #24 on September 23, and #25 on October 28, with Absolute Wonder Woman #26 scheduled for November 25. Thompson says Sherman works quickly enough that she has to keep feeding the book scripts. So while the other two faced delays, she had active work available for Absolute Wonder Woman that required her immediate attention, which is probably what she was hinting at while taking responsibility for some of the delays as her own.
