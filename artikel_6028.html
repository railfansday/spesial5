Held Hostage in the Skies

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

When aging telecommunications routers overloaded and forced the Federal Aviation Administration to issue a sudden nationwide ground stop, thousands of flights were frozen across the country. For the millions of travelers stranded in terminals, and for the air traffic controllers forced to manage congested skies with equipment built over two decades ago, the crisis was no surprise. It was the predictable result of a political system where lawmakers routinely put local earmarks and self-interest ahead of public safety.
