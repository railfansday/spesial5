Eagles injury updates: Cooper DeJean and 2 others ruled out on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Things did not start off well for the Eagles defense, with Cooper DeJean suffering a concussion in the first quarter against the Cowboys. DeJean appeared to lower his head when he went to make a tackle and ended up on the sideline in the medical tent before eventually heading into the locker room and being ruled out with a concussion.

The secondary already had depth issues before DeJean's injury, so it was Michael Carter who stepped up into his role at nickel.

It wasn't long after that, that the Eagles offense took the field, which resulted in Dallas Goedert leaving the game with a shoulder injury and then Saquon Barkley with a stinger. Both were listed as questionable to return, although Barkley did attempt to return later in the first quarter. Offensive lineman Tyler Steen was also briefly sidelined, but returned to the field shortly after.

Goedert was eventually ruled out in the second quarter, and like DeJean, won't return on Thursday.

Things didn't get much better after halftime, with Marcus Epps heading back into the locker room during the third quarter and then being quickly ruled out with a foot injury. Epps has been dealing with various injuries the past few weeks, and the safety position has mostly been a question mark for much of the season.

Injuries have plagued the Eagles all season, so it's not entirely surprising that they would have several coming off a short week, but hopefully none of them are long term issues, and they can be a healthier team for the back-half of the season.
