Eagles Injury Report: Quinyon Mitchell upgraded to limited

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Philadelphia Eagles issued their second official injury report of the week on Tuesday in advance of their Week 12 game against the Dallas Cowboys on Thanksgiving.

Note that this report is an estimation because the team held a walkthrough in lieu of a normal practice.

The Eagles listed two players under DID NOT PARTICIPATE: Jonathan Jones and Arnold Ebiketie.

Jones and Ebiketie are on track to miss Thursday's game.

Jones might've been starting at cornerback if he wasn't hurt. His likely absence moves Kelee Ringo and Mac McWilliams up the depth chart.

Ebiketie's likely absence means A.J. Epenesa could see playing time as a rotational edge rusher behind Jalyx Hunt, Nolan Smith, and Jonathan Greenard.

The Eagles listed three players under LIMITED PARTICIPATION: Quinyon Mitchell, Will Shipley, and Smael Mondon.

Mitchell was upgraded from DNP on Monday to limited on Tuesday. Vic Fangio said he thinks Mitchell will be fine enough to play. It doesn't really seem wise for the Eagles to put him out there with a hammy issue on short rest. Even if Mitchell does suit up, he could be hampered going up against CeeDee Lamb and/or George Pickens. Not really ideal.

Shipley was limited for the second day in a row. Guessing he'll be able to suit up and provide depth behind Saquon Barkley.

Mondon was upgraded from DNP to limited. He might be able to play, though the Eagles signing Chance Campbell to the roster indicates they're prepping to have more linebacker depth.

The Eagles listed nine players under FULL PARTICIPATION: Kelee Ringo, Saquon Barkley, Dallas Goedert, Tanner McKee, DeVonta Smith, Jalen Carter, Jonathan Greenard, Michael Carter, and Britain Covey.

Ringo is a new addition to the report with a neck injury. The Eagles can't really afford more cornerback injuries with Mitchell and Jones already banged up. Ringo is also a key special teams contributor. Seems like he'll be able to play but something to monitor.

Barkley, Goedert, and McKee were upgraded from limited on Monday to full on Tuesday. They'll be ready to play.

Smitty, Jalen Carter, Greenard, Michael Carter, and Covey were all listed as full for the second day in a row.

The Cowboys' report is also an estimation.

Cowboys WR4 Jonathan Mingo was downgraded from limited to DNP.

Cowboys linebackers Dee Winters and Jaishawn Barham were from upgraded from DNP to limited.

Cowboys starting right guard Tyler Booker and starting tight end Jake Ferguson are tracking to miss Thursday's game after being listed under DNP again.
