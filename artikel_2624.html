The Suns avoided the trap and found their offensive rhythm

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

If defense is your thing, you probably didn't particularly enjoy what you witnessed early on in Wednesday night's matchup between the Phoenix Suns and Sacramento Kings. With much of the country anxiously awaiting the release of Grand Theft Auto VI, nobody was being put in defensive jail on either end of the floor.

Concerning? Maybe. But as many had identified entering this matchup, it had all the makings of a potential trap game for the Phoenix Suns, facing an opponent that had won once in 12 tries. There was no reason to reach for the panic button, as it felt like the Suns were spending those early stretches feeling out their opponent. And quite honestly? Darius Acuff Jr. going nuclear was what kept Sacramento in the basketball game.

In the third quarter, however, the Phoenix Suns locked down and locked in, holding the Kings to 24 points.

When you look back at this matchup holistically, the Suns did exactly what they were supposed to do, and the outcome never truly felt in doubt. Sacramento put together a couple of runs here and there, but Phoenix maintained control throughout.

Perhaps the most impressive aspect of what we witnessed on Wednesday night was how the basketball was popping around the court, regardless of who was in the game. It was selfless basketball, with players passing up good shots in pursuit of great ones. And when those opportunities presented themselves, the Suns did a quality job of knocking them down.

When you play teams like Sacramento, you have an opportunity to work on certain aspects of your game while taking care of business. For Phoenix, a team that has struggled with offensive connectivity throughout the early stages of the season, collective playmaking appeared to be an area of emphasis.

Jordan Goodwin moved into second place in the BSB standings following one of the most competitive votes we've had this season, with four players legitimately in contention. Goodwin earned the honors for his performance against Denver with 21.4% of the vote, narrowly edging out Jalen Green at 20.7%, followed by Luke Kennard at 15.5% and Miles Bridges at 14.9%.
