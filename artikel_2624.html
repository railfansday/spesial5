Prendergast cited and could miss South Africa game

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Ireland fly-half Sam Prendergast is in danger of missing Saturday's Nations Championship match against South Africa after being cited for a tackle against Fiji.

Prendergast will face a disciplinary process for a hip-drop tackle on Fijian captain Peceli Yato in the first half of Ireland's 43-15 win in Dublin on Saturday.

The technique, when a tackling player drops their hips and body weight onto the ball carrier's legs, was outlawed by World Rugby ahead of November's internationals and the 23-year-old is the first player to be cited over its use in a game.

If found guilty and he accepts the verdict, Prendergast could face a pre-determined sanction through the Six Nations' expedited disciplinary process.

However, if he does not accept the charge then he would be referred to a panel to determine any possible outcome.

Australia's Angus Bell was yellow carded in for a hip-drop tackle against England, but Prendergast's alleged offence was not penalised by match officials during the match.

If Prendergast receives a suspension, Connacht fly-half Ciaran Frawley would be the prime candidate to start against the Springboks on Saturday [16:40 GMT].

In better news for Ireland, captain Dan Sheehan is set to return for the game against the back-to-back world champions at the Aviva Stadium after he completed return to play protocols.

Sheehan - who is deputising for regular skipper Caelan Doris - missed Saturday's win over Fiji after sustaining a concussion in the defeat of Argentina on 6 November.

But in a squad update issued on Monday, the Irish Rugby Football Union (IRFU) said Sheehan will return to full training this week before the Springboks' visit to Dublin.

Ulster back row James McNabney is unavailable while he undergoes 12-day return to play protocols after suffering a head injury that prematurely ended his eye-catching debut off the bench against the Fijians.

Tight-head prop Sam Illo is a doubt as he enters the return to play process, while utility back Jamie Osborne is ruled out with a pectoral injury that forced him off in the opening minutes of the Fiji game.

If Illo misses the Springboks game, Farrell has Thomas Clarkson and Tom O'Toole as back-up tight-head options with Tadhg Furlong expected to start.

Farrell will name his team to face the back-to-back world champions at 14:00 GMT on Thursday.

South Africa won last year's meeting 24-13 in Dublin but lost to France in Paris last weekend.
