CeeDee Lamb finds the end zone for a third straight game in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Dallas Cowboys WR CeeDee Lamb caught 11 of 12 targets for 132 yards and a touchdown Week 11 against the Tennessee Titans.

Why It Matters: Lamb posted double-digit targets for the sixth consecutive games (82 targets, 49 receptions, 601 yards, five TDs) and has been pretty much unstoppable all season. Take away a Week 5 performance against Tampa Bay (five targets, two catches, nine yards) in which he missed considerable time with an injury, Lamb has had at least eight targets and five grabs in every contest this season. He remains a WR1 option in all formats and will look to have another fine outing in store on Thanksgiving Day against Philadelphia in Week 12.
