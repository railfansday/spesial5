KC Current upsets No. 1-seeded Gotham in NWSL semifinal, setting up Final appearance vs. Spirit

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Kansas City Current are heading to the NWSL Final after getting revenge on Gotham FC, with a 2-1 upset on the road.

One year after Gotham stunned the Current with a 2-1 win in the quarterfinals, ending a historic NWSL Shield run, Kansas City flipped the script. Gotham entered the game as the No. 1 seed and the reigning champions, but will fall short of a back-to-back attempt thanks to a late goal from Haley Hopkins.

Kansas City took the lead in the 20th minute after a beautiful direct free kick from Croix Bethune. The 25-year-old midfielder curved the ball around the Gotham wall to buries it into the lower left corner.

For the second week in a row, Gotham failed to hit a single shot on target — but still managed to get a goal. This week, though, it wasn't enough.

Still, even with Gotham's offensive struggles continuing, the team was able to get on the board in the 69th minute, after Midge Purce's cross forced an own goal. It was the second week in a row, after the team's 1-0 win over the Seattle Reign in the quarterfinals, where Gotham's only points came off an own goal.

As time began to run out, Current captain Lo'eau Labonta nearly pulled her team ahead by hitting a strike that bounced off the right post. Meanwhile, Gotham keeper Ann-Katrin Berger, one of the finalists for NWSL Goalkeeper of the Year, continued her stunning run with a number of miraculous saves.

But in the 89th minute, Haley Hopkins hit a strike off a set piece that hit the crossbar, going down and in to send Kansas City to the Championship.
