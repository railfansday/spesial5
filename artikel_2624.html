Week 12 AP NFL Power Rankings: Chiefs climb to Top 3 ahead of Thanksgiving matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Somehow, it's already Thanksgiving. I don't know how we got here, but here we are. It's almost 2027, folks.

In my opinion, Thanksgiving weekend is the second-best weekend of football after the Divisional Playoff weekend. This Thanksgiving is shaping up to be no different. Each game scheduled for Wednesday-Friday could have major playoff implications.

Yes, we are being treated to a Thanksgiving appetizer with a "Thanksgiving Eve" game on Wednesday night between the Packers and Rams. This is followed by a huge NFC North matchup between the Bears and Lions on Thanksgiving, an NFC East battle between the Eagles and Cowboys that afternoon, leading up to the nightcap of the AFC rivals Chiefs and Bills. Finally, Black Friday will bring us the Broncos versus Steelers as both teams are fighting to maintain their playoff positions.

Before this feast of football on Thanksgiving week, let's take a look back at Week 11 and where each team stands in this week's edition of the Arrowhead Pride Power Rankings.

In honor of Thanksgiving, and to perhaps stir up even more debate, we'll tier-rank the teams according to the best Thanksgiving side dishes. As I think we can all agree—the best food on the Thanksgiving table, and what makes a Thanksgiving dinner worth having—are the side dishes.
