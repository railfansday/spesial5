Go fishing and fire Hornet out of a cannon in Silksong's December-bound Sea of Sorrow expansion, which Team Cherry say is "unlike anything we’ve done before"

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Honest to goodness news for Hollow Knight: Silksong expansion Sea of Sorrow? That I didn’t invent in a dream? Scarcely believable, yet entirely true. The watery DLC is releasing on December 11th 2026, a scant two weeks from now, and as with original Hollow Knight's expansions it’ll be free to those who own the base game. There’s also a new trailer, showing some of the gunpowder-jumping and fishy boss-battling Hornet will get up to while exploring those sorrowful seas.

Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow - Launch Trailer Watch on YouTube

Keen ramblers through Silksong’s sonorous Pharloom kingdom will note the new, aquatic locales: coral-coated ruins, damp algae forests, and bioluminescent cliffs that crackle with strange blue lighting. A piratical sub-theme also flashes up in a few places, particularly one yarr-ing jellybug boss and Hornet’s newfound talent for launching herself out of cannons as a movement aid.

There also looks to be at least another one of Silksong’s deviously tricky 'Climb a shaft while being chased by rising Bad Liquid' sections, as well as a moment when Hornet tries her claw at silk-fishing. I’m not the most accomplished Silksongist on the planet but I’ve played enough to know that whatever she catches will probably have two phases and a title card.

Or will it? In introducing the release date and trailer, as part of a Cherry Time showcase on the developers’ YouTube channel, Team Cherry claimed Sea of Sorrow is "unlike anything we’ve done before" – so further surprises are likely in store, even if I’d still wager that most of them involve needle violence. More fundamentally, Sea of Sorrow does look considerably larger in scope than any of Hollow Knight’s freebie DLCs, which at most – Godhome, basically – added a small handful of new locations and a smattering of boss challenges. Silksong’s first expansion appears to be showing off multiple new regions, legions of new enemy types, extra traversal mechanics, and that most desired of prizes among soulslike and soulslike-adjacent fans: untold lore.

And it’s out in two weeks? Time to get back in the water.
