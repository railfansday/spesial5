Xbox Game Pass Is Losing 14 Games In November

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

It's the start of a new month, which means new additions and departures on Xbox Game Pass. To kick off November, 14 games are being removed from the Game Pass library soon, including several simulators, one longrunning Dragon Ball game, and one of the greatest James Bond adventures of all time--at least until that upstart 007 First Light popped up earlier this year.

Simulator fans can say goodbye to Football Manager 26, Firefighting Simulator, Pigeon Simulator, and Police Simulator this month. Believe it or not, those games all come from different developers, as it feels like there's a simulator for every category and hobby out there. Another noteworthy departure is Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2, the action-RPG that was recently upgraded for current-gen consoles. The game has been around for several years and has grown with updates that add new characters and adventures, but with Dragon Ball Xenoverse 3 launching in the second half of 2027, it might be time to wave goodbye to the Time Patrol.

Also on the way are the first Moonlighter, the adorably cozy Winter Burrow, and Barbie Horse Trails. As a reminder, you can save 20% on these games if you decide to buy them before they leave Game Pass.

Leaving Xbox Game Pass on November 15

  • Football Manager 26
  • Barbie Horse Trails
  • Great God Grove
  • GoldenEye 007
  • Firefighting Simulator: The Squad
  • Winter Burrow
  • Wreckfest
  • 1000xResist
  • Pigeon Simulator
  • Police Simulator: Patrol Officers
  • Relic Hunter Legends
  • Moonlighter
  • Total Chaos
  • Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2

This wave of games leaving Game Pass will be followed by a second batch of exits on November 30. Last month saw quite a few titles rotated out of Game Pass, including Expedition 33: Clair Obscur, The Casting of Frank Stone, A Plague Tale: Requiem, Donut County, and several Double Fine classics. At least some new games are filling that void, as Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade, Plants vs. Zombies: Replanted, and The Séance of Blake Manor were all added at the end of October.
