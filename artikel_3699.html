Jax Taylor Shares Positive Update After Surgery to Remove Cancerous Tumor

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Jax Taylor is staying positive as he awaits the results of his surgery to remove a cancerous tumor.

“This is my incredible surgeon, @heydoctormike, who performed my surgery,” the Valley alum, 47, wrote via Instagram on Friday, November 20, alongside a photo with his surgeon. “He’s taken the time to explain everything to me every step of the way and I can’t thank him and his entire team enough.”

Taylor continued, “For those asking, I underwent a robotic-assisted left partial nephrectomy to remove a 4.1 cm cancerous tumor from my left kidney, classified as clinical Stage I (T1b) kidney cancer.”

The reality star added that he is now waiting to receive “the final pathology results,” which are expected in about a week.

“These results will confirm the exact type and characteristics of the tumor, its final stage and whether the surgical margins are clear, which will help us know if all the cancer was successfully removed,” he continued.

Taylor ended his update on a positive note, sharing footage of a rainbow on his way home.

“As I was driving home from the hospital, I saw one of the biggest rainbows I’ve ever seen. It gave me chills,” he wrote. “After everything, it felt like a sign of a fresh start and better days ahead 🌈.”

Taylor concluded, “Thank you to everyone who has reached out with prayers and support. It means more than you know🙏❤️.”

The Vanderpump Rules alum’s update comes one day after he announced his cancer diagnosis.

“This is something I’ve kept private for the past few months because it’s been one of the scariest times of my life,” he wrote via Instagram. “Back in July, I woke up one morning in horrible pain and ended up in the hospital with kidney stones. What I thought was just a kidney stone turned into something I never imagined. My CT scan also showed a tumor on my left kidney. After many tests and appointments I found out it was cancer.”

Taylor added that he struggled with the news because his father died of cancer.

“It put me in a really dark place. The hardest part was the waiting. Knowing there was a tumor growing inside me while trying to go about my life like everything was fine. It’s crazy how you can act like everything is normal when deep down you’re a mess,” he continued. “After a long 5-hour surgery today, the tumor has been removed, but it was a lot larger than they thought, so we are waiting on the pathology results to make we got it all.”

Taylor then gave a shout-out to his girlfriend, Lori Krebs, for her support, as well as his team of doctors. He also urged his followers not to take their health for granted.

“I did for many years, and I won’t ever do that again,” he added. “If I hadn’t ended up in the hospital with that kidney stone, I probably wouldn’t have known this tumor was there until it was too late. I like to think my dad had a part in that. Life can really change in a second.”
