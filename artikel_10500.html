CMA Awards 2026: How to watch the 60th anniversary event and celebrate all things country music

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grab your best rhinestoned cowboy hats and boots—it is time for the Country Music Association Awards.

Much like the hairstyles in Texas, this year is going to be big, celebrating 60 years of the CMA Awards. From a Dolly Parton tribute to Brooks & Dunn’s special award to the Taylor Swift of it all, there is a little something for everyone live from Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena.

Let’s see what all the yee-hawing is about.

Who is nominated for a CMA Award?

Speaking of the Lone Star State, Ella Langley’s hit “Choosin’ Texas” is one of the singles that helped her score nine CMA Award nominations, the most of anyone this year.

Miranda Lambert is nominated seven times. Ben West and Kacey Musgraves will go into the evening with five nominations each.

Greenhorns Avery Anna, Carter Faith, Emily Ann Roberts, Tucker Wetmore, and Stephen Wilson Jr. are all nominated for New Artist of the Year.

Fan favorites Luke Combs, Cody Johnson, Ella Langley, Chris Stapleton, and Lainey Wilson are all battling it out in the Entertainer of the Year category.

For a full list of nominees, check out the CMA Awards website.

A Swift return

For the first time in 10 years, Taylor Swift will be attending the CMA Awards.

She is nominated for Single of the Year for her Toy Story 5 jam “I Knew It, I Knew You.” Swifties rejoice because the news doesn’t end there. She will also perform for the first time in 13 years.

Who is hosting the 60th CMA Awards?

Guiding the audience through all the awards, performances, and tributes is host Lainey Wilson. This is the third year in a row she has had the honor of MCing the event.

Wilson will be backed up by a slew of presenters. Martina McBride, Glen Powell, Carly Pearce, Michael Bublé, Joe Buck, Jessica Chastain, and Shaboozey are just some of the stars helping hand out the awards.

Additionally, Matt Rife, Luke Bryan, and Lara Spencer are also scheduled to appear.

Who is performing at the 60th CMA Awards?

Swift is far from the only performer to grace the stage. It is a night celebrating country music, after all.

Miranda Lambert, Ella Langley, Tim McGraw, Kacey Musgraves, Luke Combs, Vince Gill, Mumford & Sons, and Chris Stapleton are just some of the big names singing their hearts out.

As part of her hosting duties, Wilson will lead a Dolly Parton tribute in the opening of the show.

Parton passed away in August of this year. The beloved singer has had many celebrations of her incredible legacy including, “CMA Presents: I Will Always Love You: A Celebration of Dolly Parton.” Wilson believes Parton deserved every remembrance possible.

“No amount of honoring will be enough for that lady, and what she has done for this genre, what she has done for the world, what she did for me personally, the friend that she became,” Wilson told Robin Roberts on Good Morning America.

What award is Brooks & Dunn being presented with?

Since hitting the country music scene in 1991 with their hit single “Brand New Man,” Brooks & Dunn have been a fixture in country music.

The duo have won 20 CMA Awards, including Vocal Duo of the Year. At the 60th Annual CMA Awards, they will be presented with the Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award.

Part of this honor is a special performance from the Brothers Osborne, Luke Bryan, Halestorm, HARDY, and Marcus King.

How can I watch or stream the awards live?

To catch all action, tune into ABC at 8 p.m. ET. This covers anyone with an over-the-air antenna and reception, meaning you can watch it live for free the old-fashioned way or via cable TV. And cord-cutters, rejoice. For the first time ever, the CMA Awards will also simulcast live on the Disney+ streaming service.
