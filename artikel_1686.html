‘Sabah could compete in LaLiga’ – Flick warns Barcelona of complacency

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Hansi Flick believes Champions League minnows Sabah would hold their own in LaLiga and urged his Barcelona team to take their opponents seriously on Wednesday.

Barcelona make the trip to Azerbaijan on the back of four consecutive victories across all competitions, the latest of which saw them come from two goals down to beat Villarreal.

This will be Barcelona's first-ever game against a side from this part of Europe, in what is the 636th game they have played across all European competitions (including qualifiers).

Flick's side won 3-0 in their last Champions League match against Aston Villa but have failed to keep consecutive clean sheets in the competition since October 2023 under Xavi, with this their 39th game since then.

Sabah, meanwhile, come into the clash at Baku Olimpiya Stadionu off the back of a 4-1 defeat to fellow Champions League debutants Viking in their last European encounter.

They have already been beaten in the competition by Manchester United and Borussia Dortmund, but a first Champions League win over Slavia Prague last month has Sabah sitting just a point adrift of the play-off places.

"They're a team that likes to play they're brave, playing out from the back," Flick told reporters when asked about Sabah. "The important thing is that we have a good attitude.

"It's the Champions League, and we have to go out there like we always do."

Warning against complacency against the minnows, Flick added: "We've already discussed it with the analysts.

"We think Sabah could compete in LaLiga because they have a very good philosophy. It is important for us, and we need to have the right attitude and the right intensity."

It has already been confirmed that Lamine Yamal will miss the match after experiencing some mild discomfort in his ankle during the win over Villarreal on Sunday.

Yamal has 14 goals and 13 assists in the Champions League, both the outright most goals and outright most assists of any teenager in the history of the competition.

"We've played without him on other occasions. We decided he should stay home. We hope he recovers well and is back with us as soon as possible," Flick said of Yamal.

Flick confirmed that Pau Cubarsi would be available after recovering from a hip injury, but remained tight-lipped on whether the match would be used as a chance to rotate.

"I haven't decided yet. We might have some changes; we have a lot of games until the end of the year," Flick added.

"It's important that all the players get playing time. We'll try to manage it in the best way possible."

Under Flick, Barcelona have won 10 of their 11 Champions League games against sides from outside Europe's top five leagues (D1), scoring 45 goals, including three or more goals in 10 out of 11 games.
