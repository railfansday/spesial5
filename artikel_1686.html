Barcelona 3-0 Aston Villa: Highlights, stats, man of the match as Barcelona blow the Villans away

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

By Ellis Stevens | 03 Nov 2026 21:55

Barcelona blew Aston Villa away with a crushing 3-0 victory in the Champions League on Tuesday night.

Barcelona were gifted an early lead when Lamine Yamal intercepted a misplaced pass inside the area and clinically converted, and although both teams had chances to add to the scoring, it remained 1-0 going into the break.

Barcelona quickly added to their lead in the second half as Raphinha's deflected effort found the back of the net eight minutes after the restart, followed by the Brazilian netting Barcelona's third and final goal just after the hour mark.

The 3-0 victory for Barcelona lifts them to second in the Champions League standings, while Aston Villa fall behind their hosts into third place after their first defeat of the league phase campaign.

Barcelona's sensational goalscoring start to the 2026-27 season continued with another three goals against Aston Villa, leading them to a second consecutive victory after back-to-back defeats towards the end of October.

Aston Villa's commitment to playing out from the back certainly helped the hosts, with numerous mistakes giving Barcelona possession in dangerous areas, including for Yamal's opening goal.

For all of Barcelona's attacking brilliance, however, their defence remains a vulnerability, with Aston Villa getting plenty of joy in behind the hosts' backline when they did decide to go long.

If not for a lack of quality at the decisive moment, Unai Emery's side could have made tonight's match much more of a contest against Barcelona.

Regardless, Barcelona claim another statement victory against an Aston Villa side that were undefeated in the league phase before tonight, certainly cementing themselves as one of the favourites to go all the way this season.
