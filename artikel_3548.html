Ocarina Of Time Is 2026’s Best-Selling Physical Game In The US, But GTA 6 Is Coming

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Nintendo's The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake for Switch 2 is the best-selling physical game of 2026, according to Circana, but GTA 6 is coming out soon and could topple that record.

Circana's Mat Piscatella said the Ocarina of Time remake for Switch 2 was the first game to surpass 1 million physical units sold in the US for its launch week since 2023's The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom.

Ocarina of Time for Switch 2 released on November 5, and Circana's reporting period ended on November 7, so the achievement was actually accomplished in just two days, not even a full week.

Ocarina of Time's physical edition, which includes a cartridge, sells for $70, compared to $60 for its digital counterpart.

GTA 6 launches on November 19, and while the game is fully digital, Rockstar is releasing a "physical" version of the game at brick-and-mortar retailers. Buyers don't get a disc--only a code for the game.

In any event, for Circana, code-in-a-box games are considered new physical game sales, so GTA 6 is no doubt going to topple Ocarina of Time's record.

Ocarina of Time's remake for Switch 2 sold more than 5 million copies over its first four days globally. The latest sales data for GTA 6 pre-purchases show that the game has passed 16 million units sold worldwide.
