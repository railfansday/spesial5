New Jersey Man Attacked Neighbor With Sword Before He Died in Fiancee's Arms

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

A New Jersey man was arrested after he allegedly fatally attacked his neighbor with a sword.

Middlesex County Prosecutor Linda Estremera and Chief James Ryan of the South Brunswick Police Department announced in a Saturday, November 21, news release that Jordan Williams has been charged with first-degree murder for his involvement in Altaray “Al” Cunningham’s death on Friday, November 20.

Williams, 28, was also charged with third-degree possession of a weapon for an unlawful purpose and fourth-degree unlawful possession of a weapon.

Authorities learned of the incident when the South Brunswick Police Department received a 911 call on Friday around 7:30 p.m. regarding a physical altercation that was taking place at the Royal Oaks Apartment Complex. Williams allegedly told Cunningham, 44, that he “had something” for him before he went into his apartment and returned with a large object, which witnesses said they believed was a type of stick, according to an affidavit viewed by NJ.com.

When officers reported to the scene, they found Cunningham suffering from blunt force trauma. He was ultimately pronounced dead at the scene.

Cunningham’s fiancée, Erin Bailey, claimed to ABC 7 that the attack was “premeditated.”

“He took his last breaths with me,” Bailey said, adding that “he couldn’t talk” in his final moments. “His face was just messed up. His face was distorted. It was so damaged and bloody.”

Authorities identified Williams as the suspect, but he fled the scene before he could be taken into custody.

“I was there when he came and tried to attack both of us. And my fiancé ran. And when he ran, he ran after him and attacked him,” Bailey said. “This was not ‘he went off and went crazy.’ No, he was waiting to act and kill.”

Officers eventually found Williams in Woodbridge, New Jersey, around 2 a.m. on Saturday. Williams allegedly “refused to step out [of his car] and began to fumble around inside and continued to ignore our orders,” per the affidavit.

Williams then fled to Franklin Township, where Woodbridge police officers managed to catch up to him.

The defendant barricaded himself inside his vehicle, and he was eventually taken into custody after the Somerset County SWAT team deployed gas to remove him from the car. As one Somerset County SWAT officer tried to remove Williams, his gas mask was struck with the suspect’s sword.

It’s not currently clear if Williams has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following his arrest. The South Brunswick Police Department did not immediately respond to Us’ request for comment regarding updates in the case.

Following the tragedy, Bailey said she will remember Cunningham as “amazing fiancé,” who was “an amazing father [and] an amazing son.”

“He touched so many lives, and he served his community,” she said of Cunningham, whom she was in a relationship with for 10 years. “He didn’t deserve this at all.”
