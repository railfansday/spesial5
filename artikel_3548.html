Keltie Knight Carries Style Gun to Keep Clothes 'Snatched' — What's in Her Bag

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Keltie Knight is unapologetically herself — and the contents of her bag are just as unique.

The “LadyGang” podcast cohost, 44, exclusively gave Us an inside look at what’s in her handbag, which spoiler alert: includes her sassy new book, The F*ck Them Theory: Self-Help for People Who Are Done Being the Bigger Person.

“It’s OK to be the villain in someone’s story,” Knight shared while hitting the streets of New York City. “I have a bag within a bag,” she added while pulling a pastel pink leather book bag out of her oversized black and white polka dot tote.

Knight wore a chic black furry coat over a black top, bootcut jeans and black stilettos and added, “This is my actual bag. So I throw this [down] and then I walk with this.”

What’s inside the bag? For starters a bubblegum pink “gun,” which Knight teased is actually her fashion hack when she needs on-the-go alterations.

Scroll down for a full breakdown of what’s in Knight’s bag:

“Say you need to be snatched like J.Lo style on the red carpet, you just [snap],” Knight told Us, demonstrating how the pink tag gun quickly attaches two pieces of fabric. “‘Woah, woah, I don’t have time to sew this, I gotta go.’ And now you’re snatched.”

Inside her tote, Knight carries a clear “health bag,” complete with everything a boss babe might need, including a Neom Energy Pen.

“Put it on your wrist,” she told Us, noting the scented roller gives you a quick jolt of energy. “Woah, I am alive!”

Knight pulled out a hormone replacement patch, joking that her hormones have no power over her day to day.

“What’s up perimenopause?” she said with a laugh, looking directly at the camera. “I’m ready. Oh, you can’t get me low estrogen.”

“I’ve got some stickers I made of Andy Cohen,” the E! News fashion correspondent revealed, showing off a sheet of stickers of Cohen, 58, rocking different looks.

Knight proceeded to peel one and place it on the side of a planter. “Little drops of Andy all over New York City,” she smirked.

“What, is this a book that I wrote? With my own brain?” Knight teased as she pulled out a copy of her new book, The F*ck Them Theory: Self-Help for People Who Are Done Being the Bigger Person, on sale Tuesday, November 24.

When asked what when was the “last time” that she was unable to apply her “f*** them theory” for professional purposes, Knight told Us, she sent a “really mean” email at 3 a.m. the night before.

“I expect perfection from myself. I expect perfection for the people that I work for,” she confessed. “The thing is, I know not everything is going to go right. And that’s what this book is about.”

Knight explained that her book is really about “taking the rejection and the heartbreak and your own sorta self-loating, and self stuff and turning it into rocket fuel.”
