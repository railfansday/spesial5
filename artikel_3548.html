JonBenet Ramsey's Dad Slams Claim Family Shouldn't Have Been Cleared in Case

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

JonBenét Ramsey’s dad, John Ramsey, fired back at Colorado prosecutor Michael Dougherty, who said he would not have ruled the family out as suspects if he had been overseeing the case when the 6-year-old pageant contestant was found dead in 1996.

“After 30 years, if they still think I am guilty, then please file charges, and we will settle this in court based on facts and evidence,” John, 82, told Fox News in a statement on Wednesday, November 18.

John continued, “They can’t selectively apply the unknown male DNA both ways. They have cleared a number of interesting suspects based on their DNA not matching unidentified male No. 1 DNA recovered from JonBenét’s person.”

John spoke out after the Boulder County District Attorney told local reporters that he “would not have” publicly ruled out JonBenét’s family as suspects like Mary Lacy, the prosecutor previously leading the case, did in 2008.

“I’ve never ruled out people in a case that’s unsolved,” Dougherty said on November 10, according to The Boulder Daily Camera. “Doing right by the victim demands that I remain open-minded and committed to following the evidence wherever it leads.”

Dougherty added that Lacy’s public exoneration of the family likely didn’t harm the investigation because her conclusion was not legally binding.

JonBenét’s case gripped the nation after she was reported missing on December 26, 1996, with her family telling authorities that they found a ransom note demanding $118,000 for her safe return. She was tragically found dead in her family’s basement later that day, and her cause of death was ultimately ruled as strangulation and head trauma.

While investigators have examined more than 20,000 tips and hundreds of pieces of evidence since JonBenét was found dead, the case remains unsolved. As part of the investigation, officials checked recovered DNA against more than 750 reference samples. In 2025, police said they still had some DNA collected from the 1996 crime scene that had not been looked into.

Conspiracy theories regarding JonBenét’s death have circulated for years. While DNA testing results were not able to match evidence to JonBenét’s immediate family, many people have speculated that John, her later mother, Patsy Ramsey, and the victim’s brother, Burke Ramsey, were behind her killing.

“Either they believe they have the killer’s DNA or not,” John told the outlet. “This problem of selective use of evidence to try and fit things to their opinion rather than use evidence to solve the crime has been the problem from the beginning.”
