Round Up: The Reviews Are In For Metaphor: ReFantazio's Switch 2 Port

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Metaphor: ReFantazio brings more JRPG action to the Switch 2 this week, and the reviews are now rolling in.

Starting with our own here on Nintendo Life, we thought this latest Switch 2 port was an extraodinary experience, but couldn't look past the poor optimisation.

"Anyone looking for a huge, sprawling adventure with lovable characters, compelling parallels to the real world, and progression systems that will keep you coming back for more will be far from disappointed with this game, but there's no metaphor for a dodgy frame rate and visuals that can occasionally take you out of the fantasy."

Review Metaphor: ReFantazio (Switch 2) - An Extraordinary JRPG That Could Use Another Optimisation Pass

"Metaphor: ReFantazio is a fantastic, fresh new take on the Persona formula, and the Switch 2 is one of the best ways to play it. With very little in the way of compromises, Atlus has delivered a solid port that makes one of its best games in years available to players of the best handheld console on the market."

"As a video game, though, I can’t recommend Metaphor: ReFantazio enough. It set a really high standard for job systems, time management, and dungeon crawling in an RPG. There’s just so much tight, considered design all over the experience, and the story still feels relevant today...While I wish the port was better, it’s been nice to revisit my favorite ATLUS game on my favorite system."

"This is a full-scope JRPG that comes dangerously close to being 100 hours without feeling like it could be consolidated down in any way, and it’s playable on the go. I do think that we sometimes forget to consider that it wasn’t that long ago that the compromises that it took to get games onto handheld consoles meant that such devices didn’t really work as the primary gaming device for most people. Yeah, the Switch 2 might not have the power of the PlayStation 5, but it’s to look at something like this running on it and think to yourself, “you know what? Close enough.”

This is a fantastic, imaginative tale that’s worth retelling. It speaks to the power of dreaming for a greater life and standing united against charismatic tyranny on a grand stage. It manages that while still giving its cast their own smaller moments to shine and without losing sight of being an excellent video game...it’s an ode to what’s possible when creatives set aside the safer path and strive for originality — something this industry could use more of while celebrating and holding fast to the talent that brings art to life.

"If you’re an RPG fan who’s especially fond of the offerings that come from Atlus, Metaphor: ReFantazio is a dead simple recommendation. While the story might not be for everyone, the excellent combat and novel Archetype progression system are worth the price of admission for folks whose love of all things turn-based burns bright. Applying a fantasy coat of paint to the demon realms of SMT and the real world settings of Persona works wonders, and it’s a welcome sight to see Atlus step outside of their sequel tradition and try something new. The Switch 2 version makes for a worthy ship on which to take this voyage, with options to adjust the experience based on your preference for performance or graphical fidelity. You might be here for a long time, but Metaphor is like a familiar friend LARP-ing in a high fantasy world."

"Between its combat, characters, politics, music, and striking fantasy world, Metaphor: ReFantazio is still one of Atlus’ best games of all time. Whether played docked or taken on the go, the Switch 2 version gives another audience a good excuse to finally experience it."

If you want to try before you buy, there's also a demo you can check out, and save data will carry over to the full game.

I ended up deciding to commit to it last minute. Not super far in but so far not really had any major complaints about the performance.

This is possibly the greatest rpg I’ve ever played. Theme-wise, art, character, and story-wise. I bought the FF7 remake games because they were so ridiculously cheap and they’ll probably be a good time but this is the game that has captured my imagination. I’ve been thinking about it a lot lately and listened to a great podcast about it. It almost feels like X-Men: the rpg to me. Not in any literally sense but there are so many themes that cross over between them. More is Prof. X. I’m not sure about the rest of the characters yet lol Also, I don’t think the wise but doe-eyed protagonist has an X-Men counterpart (maybe Jean Grey?).

My only real criticism at this point is that, like so many Atlus rpgs, Metaphor can feel like it’s on a “track” with set pieces that move the story along. While that allows them to tell an awesome story and I love the medieval magic fantasy anime clips in this game, it can feel like I lack a little bit of agency. I would greatly appreciate being able to have some open-world style areas with maybe different types of choices you can make. Or even the ability to outfit the protagonist with different shirts and pants and things. It doesn’t seem like a big deal but these little touches make it feel more like “my” story.

I appreciated in SMT Nocturne how you could align yourself with different philosophies to reshape the world with. I’m not sure if there’s anything like that in this game but it doesn’t feel like that’s where the plots going (and that’s okay). Just something else to give me more agency. So it doesn’t feel like I’m just moving the story from plot A to B. I have not gotten super far in the game and maybe there will be parts that give the player more agency or at least the illusion of it but I haven’t experienced them yet. All that said I truly love and appreciate the game for what it is.
