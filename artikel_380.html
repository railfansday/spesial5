Rocco’s Mini Mini Fridge Is Here (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

If you've always coveted Rocco’s vintage-inspired beverage fridges but haven't been able to swing the $1,999-plus price tag, here's some news you might like: Today, the brand released the Mini Mini, a desktop version of its legendary mini fridge. It comes in Green, Graphite, Cream, and new Stainless Steel. (Only Stainless Steel is available right now.)

We haven't tested it yet or even seen it in person, but Rocco says the Mini Mini can hold 12 standard 12-ounce cans and gets down to 41 degrees Fahrenheit. If you've ever tried other fridges of this size, you'll know this is an impressive stat. (Sorry, mini Frigidaire—you're cute but warm.) Rocco calls the Mini Mini “the first countertop fridge with real fridge standards.” It's got 360-degree ventilation with the refrigeration mechanics underneath the unit. That means it can sit flush against a wall, wherever that might be.

The only note is that, unlike the larger fridges, like the Classic and the Essential, this Rocco does not have a smart-beverage recognition feature—it's just for looks and performance only.

It's available for $295, and while it’s not currently on sale, unlike the brand's larger fridges, you can get it for $50 off when you buy a full-sized Rocco Classic or Essential.
