TikTok owner ByteDance is planning a buyback of up to $6 billion at about $400 billion

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The TikTok owner is proposing to extend by four years its deadline to go public or let outside investors sell back their stakes

ByteDance, the owner of TikTok, intends to repurchase as much as $6 billion in its own shares from investors, valuing the company at roughly $400 billion, according to Bloomberg.

The offer, outlined in a letter to investors, sets a price of $241.35 a share and implies a valuation 20% higher than the one used in a comparable program 12 months earlier, according to Bloomberg.

In the same letter, ByteDance proposed moving back, by four years, the cutoff by which it must list its shares or offer outside shareholders an option to sell their full stakes back to it, according to Bloomberg. The new deadline would be Dec. 11, 2030.

A ByteDance spokesperson did not respond to requests for comment on the proposals, according to Bloomberg.

The letter also lays out a plan to grant staff in ByteDance's artificial intelligence division 300 million stock units whose worth is tied to the performance of its AI business, according to Bloomberg. The company is stepping up incentives as it competes to hire and keep AI specialists, and it already gives those staff preferential equity terms unlike those offered to other employees.

Beijing-based ByteDance rose to the top of the startup rankings by value after algorithmic recommendations took hold through TikTok and its Chinese counterpart Douyin, according to Bloomberg. The company fell behind rivals when chatbots took off, but it has gained ground in AI after senior leaders called on employees in 2024 to step up their push. Its Doubao chatbot is China's most popular, and it runs a video-generation service called Seedance.

Co-founder Zhang Yiming has rallied the AI team behind a target of competing with the top U.S. labs within three years, according to Bloomberg. ByteDance is also preparing to roll out a personal AI assistant.

It remains uncertain at what point ByteDance, or any segment of its operations, might go public, according to Bloomberg. Certain current investors have priced the company at over $400 billion, and a number of secondary deals have pushed its implied value close to $500 billion.
