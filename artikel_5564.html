Product Pick of the Week: Kohler and Aerin Lauder Introduce Fleurette Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Kohler has partnered with Aerin Lauder, founder and creative director of AERIN, to introduce a new Artist Editions bathroom collection inspired by traditional Delftware.

Drawing on Lauder’s affinity for floral design and heirloom blue-and-white ceramics, the Fleurette Collection brings hand-applied botanical motifs to three classic Kohler fixtures, the Memoirs Stately toilet, Memoirs Stately pedestal sink, and Caxton rectangle sink.

Crafted in Kohler’s white vitreous china, each piece features hand-applied floral decoration inspired by Delftware, long associated with heirloom design and ornamentation. The collection will be offered in traditional blue-and-white and soft green-and-white colorways.

“This collaboration brings together two brands united by a shared appreciation for heritage, craftsmanship, and enduring design,” said Scott Edmunds, Kohler senior vice president of marketing. “Aerin brought a refined and deeply personal perspective to the collection, and together we created Artist Editions pieces that honor the decorative tradition of Delftware for the modern home.”

The collection will be available in early fall 2026.
