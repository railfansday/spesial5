SDI Presence CEO Hardik Bhatt Named Chicago Titan 100 Honoree for Second Consecutive Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Recognition celebrates Bhatt's visionary leadership, commitment to innovation, and continued growth of SDI Presence

CHICAGO, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- SDI Presence LLC (SDI), a leading IT consultancy and managed services provider, announces that Chief Executive Officer Hardik Bhatt has been named a Chicago Titan 100 honoree for the second consecutive year. The prestigious recognition celebrates the region's most accomplished CEOs and C-level executives who demonstrate exceptional leadership, vision, and influence.

Under Bhatt's leadership, SDI has continued to strengthen its position as a trusted technology partner to organizations managing complex, mission-critical operations. Bhatt's strategic vision has helped advance the firm's growth, expand its capabilities in emerging technologies, and deepen its commitment to delivering meaningful outcomes for clients across the public and private sectors.

The recognition comes as SDI celebrates its 30th anniversary, marking three decades of helping organizations navigate technological complexity and change. With a growing national presence and nearly 450 technical professionals, the firm continues to build on its established strengths in IT managed services, ServiceNow, technology consulting and advisory services, and emerging technologies, including data and artificial intelligence.

"Being recognized for a second consecutive year is a tremendous honor, particularly among so many accomplished leaders in Chicago's business community," said Bhatt. "At SDI, our success is driven by the talented people who bring expertise, dedication, and fresh thinking to our clients every day. As we celebrate 30 years in business, I'm proud of what we've built together and even more excited about the opportunities ahead."

Beyond SDI's business growth, Bhatt has championed initiatives that connect technology innovation with economic opportunity. His leadership has helped advance Xchange Chicago, an innovative workforce development and onshore IT delivery initiative launching technology careers while helping organizations access emerging talent. The initiative reflects SDI's broader commitment to investing in people, strengthening communities, and building a more inclusive workforce.

The Titan 100 program recognizes 100 distinguished CEOs and C-level executives in the Chicago region annually. Honorees are selected for their leadership, vision, passion, and contributions to their organizations and communities. A second-year recognition reflects continued distinction among the region's leading executives.

As SDI enters its fourth decade, Bhatt remains focused on accelerating innovation, strengthening strategic partnerships, and positioning the firm for sustained growth while preserving the client-focused culture that has defined its success.

SDI Presence LLC is a leading IT consultancy and managed services provider helping organizations modernize, manage, and optimize the technology that powers their most critical operations. With a 30-year corporate resume, SDI combines strategic insight, deep technical expertise, and hands-on delivery to solve complex IT challenges for public and private sector organizations across government, aviation, transportation, and utilities. From technology strategy and enterprise platforms to managed services and emerging technologies, SDI delivers solutions that strengthen resilience, accelerate innovation, and create lasting value.
