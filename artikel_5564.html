Used Truck and Trailer Inventory Down in October as Semi-Trailer Auction Values Jump Nearly 20%

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

LINCOLN, Neb., Nov. 6, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The newest Sandhills Global market reports, which cover October data for used trucks and equipment on TruckPaper.com, TractorHouse.com, MachineryTrader.com, and other Sandhills platforms, underscore inventory declines across most market segments. Inventory dropped lower than year-ago levels in every market except for aerial lifts and forklifts. Year-over-year inventory decreases were sharpest among used heavy-duty trucks, semi-trailers, and medium-duty trucks.

"Although inventory remains low, prices are solid," says Truck Paper manager Scott Lubischer. "There is higher demand for lower-mileage units, which are performing well compared to higher-mileage trucks. On the trailer front, demand is picking up and trailer inventory is getting some much-needed attention." Auction values for used semi-trailers were up 3.18% month-over-month and 19.64% Y/Y in October, led by used dry van and refrigerated trailers.

Used aerial lifts and forklifts showed the opposite pattern. Compared to year-ago figures, inventory levels in these markets were up and values were down. In the used farm equipment market, inventory levels for high-horsepower tractors were down 12% from the previous year, continuing a 17-month downward trend despite a slight monthly increase.

The key metric in all of Sandhills' market reports is the Sandhills Equipment Value Index (EVI). Buyers and sellers can use the information in the Sandhills EVI to monitor equipment markets and maximize returns on acquisition, liquidation, and related business decisions.
