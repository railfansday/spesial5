DossDocs and Mortgage Automator Announce Integration Partnership Connecting Loan Origination with 50-State Document Automation

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Expanding the lending workflow with Lendr by Mortgage Automator's loan origination technology and AI-powered underwriting engine with DossDocs' attorney-quality automated loan document software.

DossDocs is the most widely used on-demand loan document platform serving the real estate industry and was built by one of the nation's leading mortgage lawyers, Dennis H. Doss.

With the integration, lenders and brokers can manage origination and loan activity in Lendr, use AI-powered tools to capture key information, send loan data into DossDocs, and have completed documents returned to the Lendr loan file. Lendr maps a substantial volume of loan data directly into the DossDocs workflow, eliminating the need to re-enter data already captured in Lendr.

"When a loan is ready to close, our customers care about speed and about documents that match the loan they actually wrote. "DossDocs has always been good at the legal side, and now they can pull straight from the loan file in Lendr, so nobody's typing everything in a second time and hoping the two versions match. That's real time back on every file."
