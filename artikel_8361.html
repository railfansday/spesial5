Open a Kraken account if you’re buying your first crypto to hold rather than trade. The app makes buying simple, Kraken says no wallet it manages for clients has been breached since 2011, and Auto Earn pays weekly rewards on eligible idle coins once you switch it on.

The Kraken app lets you buy, sell, and convert crypto and schedule recurring buys of a fixed amount. It suits beginners who buy occasionally and hold, in any state but New York and Maine, where Kraken doesn’t serve residents.

Beginners who buy a few times a year and hold get an easy app, a long security record, and modest rewards on idle coins. Weekly buyers pay less on Kraken Pro, which charges 0.40% to 0.80% a trade at its lowest volume tier, against the app’s 1% plus a spread.
