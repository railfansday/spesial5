If You Loved 'The Good Daughter,' There's One Movie You Need to Watch Next

There are plenty of reasons Peacock’s The Good Daughter is so damn watchable. The performances, for one—Rose Byrne and Meghann Fahy are terrific as estranged sisters Sam and Charlotte Quinn. But it’s also a great story, tracing the corrosive effect of a years-ago trauma on the women and their lawyer father (Brendan Gleeson) amid the slow revelation of many extremely dark secrets. If you want more of that flavor of trauma-driven drama with a tight focus on family bonds, there’s one movie you should watch: 2015's Dark Places.

You want buried secrets? Dark Places has seriously buried secrets. Adapted from the Gillian Flynn novel, the film follows Libby Day (Charlize Theron), who survived the mass murder of her family in 1985. The crime is blamed on her brother Ben, a moody loner whose conviction was based in large part on Libby’s own testimony. For a long time, Libby is financially sustained by donations from people who sympathize with her plight, but three decades after the murders, the money has dried up, and Libby is desperate—so desperate, she agrees to help out a “true crime” club that believes her brother is innocent and is working toward his release.

Like The Good Daughter, Dark Places is anchored by a sibling relationship reshaped by the impact of horrifying violence. Libby and Ben’s experience mirrors Sam and Charlie’s in many ways, including in the way both Libby and Ben carry secrets for years after the terrible events that took family from them. The stories are also both compelling mysteries, slowly revealing the shocking truths behind one horrific night, and ultimately allowing the characters to better understand one another.

Dark Places also explores the insular, suffocating nature of small-town life, and how rumor and gossip become a poisonous presence in people’s lives. Libby, Ben, Sam, and Charlie all have to deal with others' assumptions and revelations that they've been betrayed by those closest to them, leading to powerful resolutions. If you loved The Good Daughter, you’ll love this movie. Stream Dark Places on The Roku Channel or Kanopy, or rent it on Prime Video.

Want more stories about family dynamics around secrets, violence, and explosive emotional confrontations? Here are a few more movies fans of The Good Daughter are bound to enjoy.

There’s no violent crime at the heart of this slow-burn drama—just an explosive chaos agent named Kym, played by Anne Hathaway. If your favorite aspect of The Good Daughter was the relationship between sisters Sam and Charlie, then this movie is perfect. Released from rehab to attend her sister’s wedding, Kym is self-hating, angry, and haunted by a terrible event from her childhood. Her sister, Rachel (Rosemary DeWitt), is welcoming, but has never truly forgiven Kym, and the simmering tension between the sisters and the rest of the family drives a drama that you won’t be able to look away from. Stream Rachel Getting Married on Apple TV or rent it on Prime Video.

If you really vibed on the Southern setting, the slow-burn mystery, and the exploration of flawed justice in the modern age depicted in The Good Daughter, check out Where the Crawdads Sing. It’s a slower, dreamier rumination on rural life, violence and its cost, and small-town justice, but it hits the same sweet spot in its story about a woman named Kya (Daisy Edgar-Jones) who was raised in an isolated house in the swamps of North Carolina. Accused in 1969 of a murder, Kya’s story stretches back to her childhood, when she’s slowly abandoned by her entire family, leaving her at age seven to survive on her own. Ostracized as the “swamp girl” by the town, Kya fights to live her life and be loved while facing the unwanted—and violently persistent—attention of the murder victim. It’s about broken families, deeply buried secrets, and what we do to survive. Stream Where the Crawdads Sing on Hulu or rent it on Prime Video.

The Good Daughter is about life in a small, Southern town, family trauma, and the legal system—which makes A Time to Kill the perfect movie to watch when you finish the show. When a Black man’s daughter is sexually assaulted in Mississippi and her attackers—local members of the Ku Klux Klan—look likely to go free, he violently and publicly murders them. Local lawyer Jake Brigance (Matthew McConaughey) takes on the case, assisted by law student Ellen Roark (Sandra Bullock). They face violent opposition, withering racism, and an unfair legal system that echoes the environment in The Good Daughter in ways fans will appreciate. Stream A Time to Kill on YoutTube or rent it on Prime Video.

The Dry might be as close as you can get to The Good Daughter in terms of tone and story. Although set in Australia instead of the American South, The Dry is a story driven by past crimes and buried secrets in a rural setting just like the show. Twenty years ago, Federal agent Aaron Falk (Eric Bana) fled his hometown when he was suspected of the murder of his girlfriend. When his childhood friend, Luke, appears to have murdered his wife and young son before killing himself, he returns to investigate. The slow revelation of two mysteries—one in the past and one simmering in the drought-stricken present—makes this an ideal companion film to the show. Stream The Dry on The Roku Channel, Kanopy, or Tubi, or rent it on Prime Video.

Set in Boston instead of the South, and focused on male friendships instead of a sisters’ bond, Mystic River nevertheless nails the same atmosphere of dread as The Good Daughter. In 1975, boyhood friends Jimmy, Dave, and Sean are traumatized when Dave is abducted and assaulted by two men. In 1990, the three men remain connected in their Boston neighborhood—Jimmy (Sean Penn) is an ex-convict still connected to local crime, Sean (Kevin Bacon) is a State Police detective, and Dave (Tim Robbins) is a blue-collar worker still haunted by his experience. When Jimmy’s daughter is found murdered and Dave seems implicated, Sean and Jimmy launch investigations from either side of the law, with Dave’s fate hanging in the balance. The ending is powerfully brutal, but this story of secrets and suspicion will resonate with fans of the show. Rent Mystic River on Prime Video.
