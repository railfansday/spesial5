Guy Ritchie’s Brand-New ’The Gentlemen’s Season 3 Cast Officially Revealed in First Images

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

2026 has been one of the busiest years of Guy Ritchie’s career as he’s expanded his TV empire at some of the biggest streaming services in the world and also returned to the big screen with a new movie. Ritchie’s year began back in March with the premiere of Young Sherlock on Prime Video, and despite all episodes of the show being dropped at once as a binge, it still spent weeks in the streamer’s top 10 chart. Just two months later, Ritchie finally brought his long-awaited actioner, In The Grey, to the big screen, but the star power of Henry Cavill and Jake Gyllenhaal wasn’t enough to avoid a disaster at the box office. He has found success with the second season of MobLand, though, despite some controversy surrounding Tom Hardy’s role in the show earlier this year.

However, a few weeks before the second season of MobLand, Ritchie returned to Netflix for Season 2 of The Gentlemen, his hit crime show directly inspired by his 2020 film of the same name. The show got off to a slow start on Netflix after returning to lower viewership than the first season, but it quickly turned things around on its way to picking up an official Season 3 renewal from the world’s biggest streaming service. The studio also wasted no time moving forward with The Gentlemen Season 3, which is now in production in London and targeting a return before the end of 2027. The first set images of returning stars Theo James and Kaya Scodelario surfaced online over the weekend, but fans have plenty of other stars joining the ensemble of the next season to be excited about.

It’s a requirement for any TV show to expand and evolve as new seasons are released, and Netflix is taking things to a new level in The Gentlemen Season 3. Set to star in recurring roles in The Gentlemen Season 3 are The Lord of the Rings veterans Orlando Bloom and Elijah Wood, who are famous for their respective roles as Legolas and Frodo in Peter Jackson’s 2000s film series. Saffron Burrows and Dexter Fletcher have also been tapped for key roles in The Gentlemen Season 3 on Netflix.
