You Only Have 4 Days Left To Watch Jon Bernthal's 132-Minute R-Rated Thriller Before It's Cut From Streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Time is quickly running out to watch The Accountant 2 on streaming, as the R-rated action thriller starring Jon Bernthal and Ben Affleck is leaving a major service in a couple of days. The Accountant 2 originally came out in theaters in April 2025 courtesy of Amazon MGM and director Gavin O'Connor.

Nine years after the original thriller became a major hit, The Accountant 2 struggled to find as big an audience on the big screen. But it's since become a consistently strong performer on streaming services, starting on Prime Video before moving to other platforms. The Accountant 2 is rated R for strong violence and language throughout and has a 2-hour and 12-minute runtime.

If you're looking for a great action thriller, act fast before The Accountant 2 leaves streaming.

The Accountant 2 changed the formula of the first movie by becoming a buddy movie between Affleck and Bernthal. They reprised their roles from the first film as Christian Wolff, an autistic accountant and former assassin, and Braxton, his brother and active hitman. After only sharing a few scenes in The Accountant, the sequel leans all in on their chemistry.

Affleck and Bernthal have plenty of experience excelling in action thrillers, and they prove to be a terrific duo here. Bernthal gets to be a bit more flamboyant and charismatic, while Affleck plays things a bit more seriously. Together, they make The Accountant 2's buddy-style narrative really shine, bringing the film's action, drama, and humor to life really well.

Thanks to this approach, The Accountant 2 is a superior movie to the original. The first film had a solid relationship at its core between Affleck's character and Anna Kendrick's Dana Cummings, but its semi-romantic nature is absent from the sequel, allowing a brotherly bond to take center stage and go even bigger on the action.

With Bernthal's elevated role, The Accountant 2 goes farther in its R-rating. That's the plus of having two trained killers at the heart of the story rather than just one. But The Accountant 2 works so well because it finds smaller moments to have fun, like Affleck dancing in a bar, and mixes in more comedy.

The plot can be a bit more ridiculous at times, especially with certain elements of The Accountant 2's ending, but there is a lot to like and enjoy overall. The sequel wound up scoring a 74% on Rotten Tomatoes with critics, but audiences adored the action-packed thriller, resulting in a 92% score. The Accountant has 53% and 77%, respectively.

Christian and Braxton's adventures aren't supposed to end with The Accountant 2, either. O'Connor confirmed years ago that he envisioned this story as a proper trilogy. He, Affleck, and screenwriter Bill Dubuque have teased The Accountant 3 being in development, but Amazon MGM has yet to give it an official green light.

If The Accountant 3 does happen, it will bring Affleck and Bernthal back for another mission. O'Connor previously confirmed the threequel's intent to include Kendrick's return, too. Broader plot details have yet to be revealed, but the director described it as a road-trip movie bringing all three characters together.

While The Accountant 2 will leave Hulu on December 1, it should come to another streaming service eventually. It isn't listed among Prime Video's December 2026 movies, though, so when and where it will pop back up is unclear. Go watch it now while you still can.
