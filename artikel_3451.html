What it's about: Set several generations after Caesar's reign, "Kingdom of the Planet of the Apes" follows Noa (Owen Teague), a young chimpanzee whose peaceful clan is attacked by a ruthless ape leader, Proximus Caesar (Kevin Durand). Determined to rescue his captured family, Noa embarks on a dangerous journey, meeting a human woman named Mae (Freya Allan) along the way. Together, they begin questioning everything they've been taught about humanity, ape society and their shared future.

Why you should watch it: With breathtaking visual effects, impressive motion-capture performances and plenty of gripping action, this action-packed film is a worthy addition to the franchise.
