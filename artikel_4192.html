Genetic mosaic helps explain diverse forms of Darwin's finches

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Darwin's finches are known for their varied beaks, which help them take advantage of different food sources in the Galápagos Islands. Much previous research has focused on seed-eating ground finches, but their relatives that search for insects in forested areas have received less attention.
