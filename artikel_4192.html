GTA 6 Fever Infects Infinity Nikki With Guns and Cars

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Infinity Nikki is making headlines with its latest controversy about including Maye Musk in their 3.0 introductory trailer for Chinese players, but it seems that there are actually great surprises, according to other livestreams.

Today, in the main dev livestream for Chinese players (translated by YouTuber Whimsy Haven in English), which is where most things are revealed before the patch actually drops.

In the Infinity Nikki universe, people are coming up with theories and release schedules, where we are all travelling to next, and what the next abilities are.

With Grand Theft Auto 6 grabbing everyone’s attention, it seems like a genius nod that the cosy styling game decided to show its claws and add guns to the mix.

Nikki, in all her infinite universes and extended lore, is no stranger to guns, but so far it has been discussed heavily by fans whether Infold would take a safer approach and not give Nikki guns in the latest game.

Thankfully for all lovers of shooters, that is not the case. Nikki already has a huge backdrop of dark lore, and it only makes sense that they would dim the vibes in-game even further with the addition of guns to use against foes and Esselings.

The addition of a pink Barbie-esque convertible makes the Grand Theft Auto comparisons even funnier for fans of both, as we are all consumed by the hype for the Rockstar instant classic.

Unlike Rockstar, Infinity Nikki fans also got a glimpse of her dancing with a new character, which looked very suspiciously like Oscar from Rose of Versailles. Some fans are taking it as a further nod to the queer fans and those locked into Nikki lore, where rumours of her sexuality have floated from the early games.

But of course, the main star of the show is Nikki and the new character using guns. Now, with her new 'gal pal,' you can switch guns on the go as you fight foes, from a semi-automatic pistol to a short-barreled shotgun, as shown in the livestream.

This is not the first gun in Infinity Nikki, as in 2.4 Nikki had a hunting shotgun which would only show up when the ability was used and wasn’t as flexible, leaving many fans disappointed but hopeful for more guns further on.

The other upcoming weapons seem to be wizard staffs, as we have already had a few wands in accessories rather than actual weapons, which makes sense in a game with a heavy dress-up mechanic.

The controversy with Maye Musk, Elon Musk’s mother, certainly put a dent in Infinity Nikki’s reputation, and the conversation between fans and devs has been strained. Nikki and her games are no strangers to controversy, and with the latest fiasco with Love and Deep Space still ongoing about Valko, only time will tell how Infold will react to all the communication between them and fans.

Infold has generally been receptive to Infinity Nikki fans, who have been asking for guns, darker storylines, more romance (even if only hinted at), among other things.

Sadly, we’re not sure if you can do drive-by shooting Grand Theft Auto style yet, as the footage of the car mostly focused on the streets of Cicia, but knowing Nikki and how you can summon the unicorn, bike, or motorcycle in different areas, the car should be able to breeze through weird forest terrain.

It may make sense to ask for drive-by shooting mechanics in the next questionnaires for more fun updates, as the devs do listen to what the fans want to see next.

All in all, interesting things are happening in the next big Infinity Nikki update! No mention of poor Giovanni, though; he’s still in the basement.

Infinity Nikki is available on PC, macOS, PlayStation 5, and iOS and Android phones.
