Here Are The Best-Selling Switch 1 & 2 Games As Of September 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Nintendo's released its latest financial data, so it's time for an updated look at the best-selling first-party games on Switch 1 and 2.

Starting with the Switch 1, there really isn't that much to talk about here. The numbers are going up, albeit pretty slowly these days, as to be expected. Mario Kart 8 Deluxe has hit 72 million, which is certainly notable, though there are no changes to the order.

So let's take a look at the current Switch 1 sales, with last quarter's figures included for comparison:

Okay, now let's take a look at the Switch 2.

We have a few new additions this time, with Splatoon Raiders, Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave, and Star Fox all making the chart.

Shout out to Star Fox in particular, we were really rooting for that one. We're not sure it'll be sticking around for very long, as titles like Pokémon Winds and Waves will no doubt boot it off in the coming months and years.

Speaking of Pokémon, no, that isn't a typo. Both Pokémon Legends: Z-A and Pokémon Pokopia are tied with 4.04 million units each. Naturally, that doesn't take into account Z-A's performance on Switch 1, but it's a pretty neat little coincidence.
