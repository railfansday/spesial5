Rockstar to Host Grand Theft Auto 6 Launch Festival in Miami

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Daniel is a lifelong gamer with a love for various genres and gameplay styles who enjoys exploring games and playing from various angles.

We are exactly 10 days out from the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6, and I can feel the boundaries of reality straining and groaning in anticipation. This game’s launch is slated to be so colossal, so era-defining, that analysts have already predicted the U.S. economy will lose a billion dollars in productivity costs due to everyone calling out sick. It’s the game that nearly every other game was too scared to launch in November because of.

It’s kind of fitting that such an apocalyptic-level release would be accompanied by a bout of mad revelry, and if there’s one thing that Rockstar has proven itself particularly good at, it’s running equally massive real-life advertising campaigns. To that end, the company has announced that, on November 21, a couple of days after release proper, a launch festival will be held on the shores of Miami, Florida.

Rockstar says GTA 6 pre-loading begins November 10 and could require up to 212 GB, though some official pages still show the old date.

Ticket sales will be exclusively by a lottery, so assuming you’re not too busy that weekend actually playing the game, you may have a chance to join in on a generation-defining party, slated to feature a swath of some of the biggest names in modern music.

The event is officially titled “Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival,” and is being jointly hosted by both Rockstar itself and PlayStation, as well as produced by the creators of the Ill Points music festival. According to the official website, the event will be a “one-of-a-kind launch event that features two stages of nonstop music,” held at the Mana Wynwood in Miami.

The participating artists include many of those whose tracks are featured on the official Grand Theft Auto 6 soundtrack, including, but very much not limited to, Cactus Jack presents Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Kodak Black, Sofaygo, and Cardi B.

The event will be a “one-of-a-kind launch event that features two stages of nonstop music."

The event will only be open to those ages 17 and up, with valid photo ID required for entrance and confirmation that you’re at least 21 for consumption of alcohol. Ticket sales will be handled by DICE via exclusive codes distributed by Laylo. These codes will be distributed via lottery; entries into this lottery are currently open, and will close on Tuesday, November 10 at 4:00 PM eastern time, with the winners drawn that same day at 7:00 PM.

Grand Theft Auto 6's Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell are taking over Miami Heat games.

Once the winners are selected, actual ticket sales will open on Wednesday, November 11 at 12 PM eastern. Codes are single-use and non-transferable, and tickets are limited to two-per-code. The official site doesn’t specify how much tickets will cost, but much like the game itself, don’t expect them to be cheap.

Even putting aside its efforts throughout the past year to drip-feed details about Grand Theft Auto 6’s release, Rockstar has been truly pulling out all the stops to maintain a constant presence in the public consciousness.

One amusing example of this has been the game’s recurring presence in the current season of South Park, throughout which characters have remarked variations of, “everything is gonna change in November. Y’know, when Grand Theft Auto 6 comes out.” This, of course, was a cheeky nod to the recent United States Midterm elections, though in the most recent episode, Rockstar actually sent the show’s creators exclusive footage of the game, which was showcased during the episode.

Rockstar also ran a takeover event of the Selfridges department store in London, with the Oxford Street London Corner Shop being done up in Vice City iconography and colors and selling a swath of GTA 6-themed apparel and accessories, some of which was priced in the thousands of Pounds. There was even a custom Ferrari parked inside the store right by the street window for all to see.

As Rockstar Games has taken its marketing efforts to Miami, lawmakers have expressed divided opinions on how the campaign impacts the real-life city.
