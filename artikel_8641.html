Get the Sam’s Club® Mastercard® if you buy a lot of gas and shop at Sam’s Club, even as a new member. On $500 a month of gas, its 5% back pays $300 a year in Sam’s Cash, or $240 after a $60 Club membership. Skip it if you rarely shop there, since Sam’s Cash works only at Sam’s Club.

That $240 is still more than the $180 the Wells Fargo Autograph Card pays on the same gas, at 3 points per dollar worth a cent each, with no annual fee or membership. For a new member, the $60 fee costs more than the extra 2% earns below $250 a month of gas, and above $750 the Autograph’s uncapped points pay more than this card does.

Drivers who shop at Sam’s Club get the most from it, current members most of all, since the card costs them nothing beyond the membership they already pay. If you rarely shop there, take the Wells Fargo Autograph Card instead, since its points can be redeemed as a credit to a Wells Fargo card or checking account.
