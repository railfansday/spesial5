Hopefully you watched all those great football games on Thanksgiving. Imagine how much better it would have been with a fancy new TV. Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, B&H Photo Video, Target, and Sonos all have deep markdowns live right now across 4K OLEDs, giant Mini-LED screens, triple-laser projectors, Dolby Atmos soundbars, and budget streaming sticks. Whether you want a 65-inch OLED for $1,300 off or a $199.99 QLED upgrade for a guest room, these are the deals worth your money before Cyber Monday wraps up. Our full list of Amazon Black Friday deals covers more categories.

LG’s 2026 65-inch OLED is $1,300 off at Best Buy, Walmart, and B&H

LG’s 2026 C-series OLED is down to $1,399.99 at Best Buy, Walmart, and B&H Photo Video, a $1,300 cut from its $2,699.99 list price. You get four full-bandwidth HDMI 2.1 ports, a native 120Hz panel that pushes up to 165Hz VRR for PC gaming, and LG’s Alpha 11 AI Processor Gen 3. It handles dark-room movie nights and console gaming equally well, and Dolby Vision is built right in.

A 50-inch 4K QLED TV for $199.99 is an easy upgrade for a guest room, garage, or dorm. The Roku 50-inch Select Series QLED TV lists for $299.99, runs Roku’s interface straight out of the box, and ships with a voice remote. Bluetooth headphone mode lets you listen late at night on wireless earbuds.

Thin TVs have thin speakers, and a compact soundbar fixes muddy dialogue in five minutes. The Sonos Beam (Gen 2) is $339 at Amazon and direct from Sonos, down $160 from its $499 list price. Inside the 25.6-inch cabinet sit five Class-D amplifiers, Dolby Atmos processing via HDMI eARC, and built-in voice control. You can also pair it wirelessly with a Sonos Sub Mini or Sub 4 later, and both subwoofers are discounted below.

A 4K triple-laser portable projector for over $400 under list

Optical zoom on a portable projector is still unusual, and the Hisense M2 Pro uses it to resize a 65- to 200-inch 4K image while the unit stays put on your coffee table. At $897.99 against a $1,299.99 list price, you get a triple-laser light engine and Dolby Vision support in a carry-friendly chassis. Hisense lists brightness at 1,300 lumens, so plan on watching after sunset or pulling the shades.

Black Friday is when 65-inch OLEDs and massive 98- to 100-inch Mini-LED sets drop into realistic living-room budgets. Mini-LED models handle sunny rooms with higher peak brightness, while OLEDs stay ahead for dark-room contrast and gaming response times.

Triple-laser home theater models and ultra-short-throw units carry the steepest dollar cuts this week, including $2,000 off Hisense’s flagship L9Q at B&H. Portable LED and laser units also dip below $400 if you just want a quick backyard or bedroom screen.

Sonos matched its own direct Black Friday pricing across Amazon and retail partners, while Best Buy and Amazon also cut standalone subwoofers and 5.1-channel kits by up to 50 percent.

An inexpensive streaming stick is still the quickest cure for a sluggish smart TV menu, and Roku and Amazon both have sub-$20 options on sale right now.
