Kia is riding a wave of global sales momentum, fueled by its successful lineup of practical, family-oriented vehicles. Now, a leaked product roadmap reveals exactly how the South Korean automaker plans to maintain that dominant market position through the end of the decade. An internal document detailing future production plans for Kia’s domestic manufacturing plants confirms several major product overhauls, most notably the arrival of an all-new Carnival minivan and a fully redesigned Sorento SUV.

According to the leaked timeline, both the next-generation Carnival and the all-new Sorento will enter production in 2028. This rapid development cycle proves Kia is committed to maintaining the Carnival’s status as one of the best minivans on the global market. Furthermore, replacing the Sorento quickly ensures the brand’s midsize SUV offering stays competitive against freshly redesigned rivals from Toyota, Honda, and Ford.

According to the original report detailing the leak from The Korean Car Blog, the most shocking revelation hidden within the internal document is the confirmation of a hydrogen-powered Carnival. Slated for production in 2029, this unique powertrain variant suggests Kia is actively exploring zero-emission alternatives beyond traditional battery packs for its largest passenger models.

While the hydrogen Carnival represents a fascinating engineering experiment, the document also confirms a more mainstream addition to the brand’s zero-emission lineup: the first-ever Kia EV7. While technical specifications remain strictly guarded, the EV7 name implies a midsize, three-row electric SUV positioned carefully between the compact EV6 and the flagship EV9. This strategic addition will help sustain the massive Kia hybrid boom and EV sales momentum the company continues to enjoy.

This aggressive future product strategy illustrates exactly why modern Kia vehicles are experiencing slow depreciation on the secondary market. By consistently refreshing its core lineup with advanced technology and in-demand powertrains, the brand has successfully shed its former budget-brand image, transforming into a true segment leader.

Buyers can expect the current iterations of these vehicles to remain excellent options. While the automaker recently implemented a tiny price hike for the 2027 Carnival, the popular family hauler remains an exceptional value. However, for buyers willing to wait, the leaked 2028 and 2029 product pipeline proves that Kia’s most innovative family vehicles are still on the horizon.
