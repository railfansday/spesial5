GTA 6 Owners Can Now Stream the Game to PS5, PS Portal with PS Plus Premium

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

GTA 6 has been updated to support PS Plus Premium cloud streaming directly to the PS5 and PS Portal.

To be crystal clear about this: the game has not been added to the PS Plus Premium catalogue and you will still need to own it.

However, assuming you’ve purchased the game – and millions of you have – then you’ll now be able to stream it directly from Sony’s servers, assuming you’re a PS Plus Premium member.

There are a number of use cases for this, but the most likely is you’re away from your console and still want to play on your PS Portal. Now you won’t need to rely on Remote Play; you can just stream it directly.

This feature wasn’t available at launch, but I’ve been able to test it on my PS5 here in the UK and it’s working well.

There was some reporting prior to release that Microsoft had secured “exclusive” streaming rights to this title, but they were quickly clarified. This update now confirms boss Asha Sharma misspoke.
