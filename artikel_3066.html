I’m a big fan of Hilton hotels and often use my Hilton Honors Bonus Points — earned largely from Hilton credit cards — to make my travels more affordable. Dream trips that seem impossible are suddenly within reach when I can leverage travel rewards.

With more than 9,400 properties and 28 different brands around the world, I can find a Hilton hotel pretty much anywhere I want to go — whether I just need a basic room to crash in for a night or I’m checking off my aspirational bucket list destination.

But there’s something I love even more than using points to book a room. Especially if I’m eyeing a luxurious, expensive hotel.

I’m talking about a Hilton Free Night Reward. Arguably one of the best deals in travel, you can use it at nearly any Hilton hotel, no matter how high the cash rate is.

And, for a limited time, new Amex cardholders of select Hilton credit cards can “fast track” their way to earning a Free Night Reward. Here are all the details.

In many cases, when a hotel credit card offers a free night certificate to cardholders, it comes with many asterisks. It may only be good for certain properties or categories of hotel. It may have a capped value. There may be a laundry list of blackout dates or other restrictions.

Hilton’s Free Night Rewards are arguably the most valuable because they are good for any available standard room at eligible properties with no cap. (Subject to availability, program exclusions may apply.)

Yes, as long as a standard room is available at most Hilton hotels, you can use your Free Night Reward to book it.

Like the iconic Hotel Del Coronado, part of the Curio Collection by Hilton, near San Diego. It’s a historic Victorian beachfront property circa 1888 that was once the largest resort in the world. Recently renovated and as majestic as ever, it can cost upwards of $650 a night, or 110,000 Hilton Honors Points, for a standard room.

Or Conrad Bora Bora Nui, a 5-star gem nestled in exquisite French Polynesia. A king lagoon view suite can run over $1,500 per night in cash, or 200,000 Hilton Honors Points per night.

Even the ultra-luxurious Waldorf Astoria Los Cabos Pedregal, a hotel carved into cliffside mountains where every room features an ocean view and a private plunge pool. Cash rates are usually at least $1,200 per night. Standard reward rates are 250,000 Hilton Honors Points per night.

A Hilton Free Night Reward can cover a night at any of these hotels (or any of their thousands of properties around the world) as long as a standard reward night is available.Better yet, resort fees and taxes are covered, too.Sometimes you think you got a good deal on a hotel room, only to find it’s nearly twice as much as you thought once you factor in taxes and those ugly resort fees.Luckily, this Free Night Reward applies to the room rate and tax. Hilton also doesn't charge resort fees on reward stays for members (which you are if you have one of these Free Night Rewards).

The only way you can earn these Free Night Rewards is by holding a select Hilton Honors credit card.

But with these new limited-time offers, earning a Free Night Reward just got easier.

I love being able to use my hotel points to book nearly-free rooms when I’m traveling. I also love staying in intimate, boutique hotel properties that feel nothing like a big chain. Thanks to a unique partnership, I was able to do both on a recent trip to Amsterdam.

I booked the luxurious HotelTwentySeven, an all-suite property that is part of the Small Luxury Hotels (SLH) of the World collection. It’s impeccably designed, centrally located and boasts a Michelin-starred restaurant on-site.

Hilton launched a partnership with SLH in 2024, allowing Hilton Honors members to use their points to book hundreds of small, unique hotels around the world.

Cash rates are pricey at this 5-star property — a one-night stay can go for around €850. I found a standard room bookable for 130,000 Hilton Honors Points for the night I wanted to stay.

But I was able to book with a Hilton Free Night Reward — one of the best benefits in award travel since it allows you to book a standard room at any Hilton-affiliated hotel with no caps or restrictions.I earned a Free Night Reward thanks to holding the Hilton Honors American Express Surpass® Card and spending at least $15,000 in purchases in a calendar year. The card also gives automatic Hilton Honors Gold Status, which allowed me to enjoy a complimentary gourmet continental breakfast at HotelTwentySeven.

If you open a new Hilton Honors American Express Surpass® Card now, you can earn a Free Night Reward once you meet the terms of the welcome offer. Plus, you can earn another Free Night Reward after you spend $15,000 on eligible purchases in a calendar year, each year you hold the card. Terms apply (see rates and fees).
