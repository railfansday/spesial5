Wizards at Bulls Discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Washington Wizards (5-11) finally snapped their eight-game losing streak with a 103-99 win over the Toronto Raptors on Wednesday. AJ Dybantsa scored a career-high 23 points, and Kyshawn George added 20 points to secure a much-needed victory.

The Wizards travel to Chicago on Friday to face the Bulls (4-10), who are 3-1 over their last four games. Caleb Wilson, who scored 30 points against Washington on Oct. 24, recently injured his hand and won't play Friday.

Brian Keefe altered his rotation in Wednesday's win. Tre Johnson didn't play in the second half, and Bub Carrington didn't play for the first time in his 180-game NBA career. Khris Middleton and Will Riley took their minutes.
