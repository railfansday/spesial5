The streaming integration is due to be completed soon, with updates planned for the app.

An integrated Hulu and Disney Plus streaming experience has been in the works for nearly two years, since it was announced during former CEO Bob Iger’s tenure. In August 2025, Iger said the two apps would be combined in 2026, and Hulu would be available to international customers. It’s still unclear whether there will be new packages and pricing models once the merged version is fully complete, as Disney recently raised prices on its subscription plans this past September.
