Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Fuel squeezed profits even as seafood growth and the Searay deal lifted sales.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

HF Foods Group Inc. (HFFG +1.36%) reported its seventh consecutive quarter of top-line growth, driven by seafood volume gains and pricing improvements in the produce and commodity segments. Management indicated that quarterly profitability was impacted by industry-wide fuel inflation and nonrecurring professional fees associated with the Searay Foods acquisition and credit facility refinancing. The company has transitioned its transformation program from the implementation phase to the optimization of warehouse and route efficiencies. The acquisition of Searay Foods establishes a platform for Canadian expansion and adds higher-margin proprietary brands to the seafood category, which currently constitutes 35% of total net revenue. Management reported that the company remains the leading scale food service provider in the Asian specialty market with over $1.2 billion in annual net revenue.
