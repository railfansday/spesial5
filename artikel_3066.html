Noodles: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ramen is now a permanent menu category, giving Noodles a steady sales base that seasonal flavor rotations can build on.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 26, 2026 at 1:38PM EST

On its third quarter of 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 13, 2026, Noodles (NDLS -2.61%) announced systemwide comparable restaurant sales growth of 9.5%, driven by customer traffic gains and store portfolio changes. For long-term investors, the chain's ability to boost sales while shrinking store count points to efficiency gains, though sustaining them is the test. Three key takeaways from management stand out.

Closing underperforming locations not only trimmed overhead from the restaurant network. Nearby locations captured off-premise orders from shuttered units, lifting average restaurant volume across the remaining footprint.

Management expanded its menu by making ramen a permanent offering to drive traffic. The category gives the chain a predictable sales base while allowing seasonal flavor rotations throughout the year.

Average unit volume, or AUV--the average annual sales generated per location--jumped over 15% to $1.54 million during the quarter. Higher volumes expanded restaurant-level contribution margin to 16.7% by leveraging fixed expenses across a leaner footprint.

Sales momentum continues as store efficiencies take hold. Higher unit volumes and disciplined cost controls support margin expansion while management tracks traffic trends.

A leaner restaurant footprint and lower debt strengthen financial flexibility entering 2027. Long-term value relies on sustaining customer traffic through continuous menu innovation.
