Prediction: Here's What a $10,000 Investment in Vanguard's Tech ETF Could Be Worth in 25 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

AI is currently driving high growth in the tech sector.

Artificial intelligence (AI) has been driving double-digit market gains for four years, and one of the best ways to play the trend is to buy the Vanguard's Information Technology ETF (VGT +0.23%). It's beating the S&P 500 this year by more than double, 25% to 12%, and it's beaten the market by a wide margin over many years. Here's what a $10,000 investment might be worth in 25 years.

The information technology ETF is Vanguard's highest-gaining ETF over the past 10 years, with an exceptional annualized gain of 23.9%. If you had invested $10,000 10 years ago, you'd have $85,910 today. That's more than double what you'd have if you'd invested in the standard Vanguard S&P 500 ETF. Since inception in 2004, though, the ETF's gains have been more modest at 14.6%.

The ETF has 318 components today and is anchored by the world's biggest companies, Nvidia, Apple, and Microsoft.

Stocks don't maintain the same growth they have delivered in the past, because as they get bigger, they slow down. However, ETFs work differently because they track indexes. Stocks get swapped out as necessary to maintain standards and growth levels, which means the index can continue to gain at similar levels.

For this exercise, let's assume that the ETF can continue to gain at an annualized rate of 15% over the next 25 years. That would turn a $10,000 investment into $329,190.

It's important to keep in mind that this is just an estimate, and the results over such a long period could vary.
