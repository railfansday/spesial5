Ravens upgrade Vega Ioane on 2nd injury report of Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Baltimore Ravens upgraded rookie right guard Vega Ioane to full participation on their second injury report of Week 11.

Ioane, 22, was limited during Wednesday's practice already playing through a knee injury when he hurt his shoulder on Monday night against the Chargers. Practice squad elevation Chuma Edoga put forth a strong relief appearance at right guard, but the Ravens would no doubt prefer their first-round pick is ready to play against the Carolina Panthers on Sunday.
