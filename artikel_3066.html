Dangote Refinery Gets $4.1 Billion Orders in Top Africa IPO

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Africa’s biggest refinery attracted about 5.85 trillion naira ($4.1 billion) of applications for shares in the continent’s largest public offering to date, almost three times the amount it sought to raise, according to people familiar with the matter.
