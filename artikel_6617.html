Everything You Need to Know About the 2027 Grammy Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Believe it or not, the 2027 Grammy Awards are just around the corner. With the nominees now announced—and including the likes of Taylor Swift, Rosalía, Ariana Grande, Madonna, Raye, Olivia Dean, Tinashe, and Cardi B—it’s officially time to start counting down to the ceremony.

Below, find everything we know so far about the 2027 Grammys.

The 69th Annual Grammy Awards will take place on Sunday, February 7, 2027.

The 2027 Grammys will be held at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, just as they were in 2026.

The main Grammys telecast will air live on ABC (and stream on Disney+ and Hulu) from 4 p.m. to 7 p.m. PST.

Well, we know who it won’t be: after six consecutive stints as host, Trevor Noah took the Grammys stage for the final time earlier this year. As for who will take his place? That’s still yet to be announced.

Raye led the awards ceremony with a whopping 11 nominations, closely followed by Rosalía with nine, Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert, Olivia Rodrigo, and Phoebe Bridgers with seven apiece, and Bruno Mars, Leon Thomas, and Olivia Dean with six each.

The official lineup for the 2027 Grammys hasn’t been announced yet—but watch this space!
