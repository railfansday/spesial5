GQ’s Best Gifts for Men 2026: 70+ Editor-Approved Presents

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

There's no shortage of stuff to buy for men. Stuff worth giving? That's another story. Fortunately, finding stuff worth giving is a salaried position around here. The results of that extensive retail research are all here: a carefully considered mix of great clothes, clever gadgets, handsome home goods, and a few things he probably wouldn't think to buy himself. We keep this guide updated throughout the year, so below you'll find our latest favorites alongside a few perennial heaters, all handpicked to make your gift-giving duties a little easier.

Still got names to cross off your list? Browse the rest of our gift guides and sign up for the GQ Recommends newsletter for a steady supply of good ideas.

This Banana Republic glen plaid double-breasted jacket features a rarely seen 2x1 button stance, which makes it a touch more casual but no less handsome.

Among Ralph Lauren's many contributions to American fashion is transforming the denim shirt from a Western novelty into a staple of the modern wardrobe. This weathered slub denim version is a handsome example, and something he'll wear for years to come.

Few knits occupy a place in the menswear pantheon quite like J.Crew's rollneck sweater. With its relaxed weave and raglan sleeves, it's an enduring heater that makes for an easy gift.

What makes Morjas' Belgian loafer so irresistible to those who've had the good fortune to wear it is just how comfortable it is. Doesn't hurt that it's also clean, understated, and versatile as all get-out.

Maple's Orbit bracelet is a great little way to add a fleck of personality to an otherwise understated fit. And if this design is a little too out-there for his taste, you may have to look elsewhere entirely. That's kind of Maple's whole thing.

Smaller, thinner cases are great, and the Timex Marlin is a stone-cold minimalist classic in that world. But it also sells out, so allow us to recommend another, newer Timex gem: the Marlin Jet Titanium. He'll love that it's an automatic, too.

Patagonia's Snap-T is an icon, and this new Re-Tool Air version gives it a fresh spin with an exclusive Polartec fleece that's remarkably lightweight, wonderfully warm, and made from 100% recycled polyester.

This merino wool crewneck from Arc'teryx is lightweight, great at regulating temperature, and just as good worn on its own as it is layered under a sweater or jacket. A go-to layering piece for just about any wardrobe.

If you ask us, the Moon Shoe is one of the Swoosh's best releases of the year. A perfectly retro, low-profile runner with a refreshingly affordable price tag.

A great hoodie is essential, and Reigning Champ nails fit-meets-function better than most, with an aesthetic inspired by old-school athletic training gear. This one is cut from the brand’s signature midweight terry, which is soft and substantial without feeling bulky.

A man can never have enough flannels. Filson's Rangeland is made from brushed 5.5-ounce cotton, and we're partial to the red and gray plaid.

Massachusetts-based Steele Canvas has been making sturdy canvas goods for generations, and its Everyday Tote is exactly the sort of gift they'll most definitely get some use out of.

When we think of field watches, we think of Hamilton. This no-nonsense mechanical number is hard to beat in the category, and you could spend a lot more on a lot less.

A good pair of cowboy boots can do wonders for a fit, and Tecovas' Warren makes a strong case for adding a little yee-haw to the rotation. With classic flame stitching and a sharp cutter toe, they're more versatile than you might think.

3sixteen's asymmetrical fleece is freaky, but not too freaky, with a wide boatneck, an off-center zipper at the neck, and an oversized pocket with a zip closure. There's a lot going on, but the proportions are spot-on.

Some of the best stuff Miansai makes is low-key its bead and stone jewelry. The Avi necklace is a welcome change of pace from the usual gold and silver.

John Smedley has been making knitwear in England for more than 200 years, and there's a reason it's still considered some of the best in the biz. This wool polo might look simple, but the quality of the knit, the fit, and the finishing make it something special.

OrSlow's 105 jeans have a classic straight leg and medium rise, with the kind of easy, timeless fit that never goes out of style. A very good upgrade for anyone whose denim rotation could use a little love.

Yes, it costs a pretty penny. But it also might be one of the coolest scarves we’ve ever seen: so robust you can wrap it around yourself a few times like a sweater, at which point the price starts to make more sense. Oh, and it’s Burberry.

Movado's iconic Museum Watch has been a masterclass in minimalism since 1947, with a Bauhaus-inspired dial that's blank save for a single dot at 12 o'clock. A remarkably sleek timepiece, especially at this price.

Some days call for a full wallet, others for just the essentials. Coach's 3-in-1 gives him both, with a removable ID insert that works just as well on its own when he wants to travel a little lighter.

For the record, we're all for giving socks as presents, provided they're of the same caliber as those from Japan's Rototo.
