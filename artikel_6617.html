Brownie Batter Bark

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Recipe by Molly Allen, Hood River, Oregon

This egg-free brownie batter bark is quick and easy, and tastes just like brownie batter straight from the bowl.

When I’m making brownies, I always want to swipe my finger against the edge of the bowl and taste the brownie batter. The problem? While it’s delicious, brownie batter contains raw eggs and raw flour, which can make you sick. When you want to lick the spatula but don’t want to risk food poisoning, this brownie batter bark recipe is the perfect solution.

Line a 15x10x1-inch baking sheet with parchment paper.

In a large bowl, combine almond flour, brown sugar, cocoa powder and salt. Stir in the melted coconut oil and vanilla extract until the mixture is smooth.

Spread the mixture on the prepared baking sheet into an even layer. Freeze it for 20 minutes.

Meanwhile, in a small microwave-safe bowl, microwave 1 cup of chocolate chips in 30-second increments until they’re melted, stirring between the intervals. Spread them over the frozen base. Sprinkle the bark with the remaining 1/2 cup of chocolate chips.

Freeze the bark for one hour or until it’s firm. Break it into pieces.

Brownie batter bark is best stored in a zip-top bag or airtight container in the freezer. The cold freezer temperature will keep the bark hard, and provides the best texture when you pull out a few pieces to enjoy. Keep the bark stored in the freezer for up to two months.

This is a great make-ahead potluck dessert. Make it any time, and then store it in the freezer. Grab a few pieces at a time, or serve a whole platter to a crowd.

No almond flour on hand? You can swap in all-purpose flour, but keep in mind the texture may not be the same. If you use all-purpose flour, it should be heat treated before consuming raw.

Spread the flour on a baking sheet and heat it in a 400ºF oven for about five minutes or until it reaches an internal temperature of 160º. Then add it to the dry ingredient mixture.

This recipe calls for unsweetened cocoa powder to deliver deep chocolate flavor. Semisweet chocolate chips are used for the melted chocolate topping for a balance of sweetness. It’s best to stick with semisweet chocolate for a more robust flavor (as opposed to milk chocolate).

You can melt chocolate either in the microwave or over a double-boiler on the stove top. Heat the chocolate slowly on low heat for the smoothest consistency.
