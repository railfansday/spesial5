As the Director of Sales Engineering, Southern Europe you will take a senior role in shaping how we engage with customers, partners, and the wider sales organisation. You will lead a small team of Sales Engineers, guiding their development and ensuring they are well equipped to succeed, while continuing to spend most of your time working directly on high value opportunities.

This is a senior hands on leadership role. You will set technical direction for key accounts, influence deal strategy, and help resolve complex architectural questions. You will act as a trusted advisor to customers and sales teams, while also representing the SE function in regional planning, forecasting, and cross functional discussions.

You will lead by example, taking ownership of strategic opportunities and stepping in where your experience can make a meaningful difference to outcomes. At the same time, you will coach your team to grow their own expertise and confidence, creating an environment where they can perform at a high level.

We believe in and facilitate a flexible, collaborative work environment. We’re growing quickly, but remain true to the innovative, can-do startup values that got us here. Most importantly, we keep hiring talented, smart, fun, and genuinely nice people because that’s who we want to succeed with every day.
