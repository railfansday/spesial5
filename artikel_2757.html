Supercomputer ranks most likely 2027 Super Bowl winners before Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Week 10 of the NFL season came to an end on Monday night and Opta's supercomputer has updated its Super Bowl winner rankings.

After the latest round of games, the Buffalo Bills and the San Francisco 49ers have been able to continue their impressive runs and are now 8-1 after beating the New York Jets and the Dallas Cowboys.

Other sides such as the Kansas City Chiefs, the Denver Broncos and the Jacksonville Jaguars (all 7-2) are also going strong this year.

But it is the 49ers who Opta's supercomputer is still naming as the most likely team to go on and win the showcase event in 2027.

Ahead of their Week 10 game, the 49ers had a 12.28% chance of winning the Super Bowl, which has since increased to 13.65% as we look ahead to Week 11.

The Cincinnati Bengals are then the second-most likely team according to Opta (12.28%), followed by the Detroit Lions (11.65%), the Jaguars (7.98%), the Chicago Bears (7.55%) and the Bills (7.43%).

Despite their 7-2 records, the Chiefs (6.75%) and the Broncos (5.55%) are given the seventh and eighth-highest chances of winning the Super Bowl this season.

But it will be interesting to see how Opta's rankings change after Week 11, which features some intriguing matchups.

For example, the 49ers will take on the Minnesota Vikings, who are 7-2 and looked much better on the offence this past weekend with Kirk Cousins in the quarterback role.

Should San Francisco lose, we may well see a new team given a higher chance of winning the Super Bowl after Week 11.
