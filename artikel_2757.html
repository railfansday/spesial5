Dan Lanning fires back at Kyle Whittingham after ‘outbid everybody’ comments

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Michigan and Oregon will meet in Eugene on Saturday, and the war of words between the two head coaches has already started. Kyle Whittingham took a not-so-subtle jab at Oregon's NIL budget earlier this week.

Now, Dan Lanning has responded. The Oregon head coach praised Whittingham as an "unbelievable coach," but he also fired back with a shot of his own, pointing to the money Michigan is spending on coaches it has fired.

Whittingham's first season at Michigan hasn't gone according to plan. The Wolverines fell to 5-4 after a 15-6 loss to Michigan State on Saturday, and the school fired offensive coordinator Jason Beck the next day.

The criticism has been relentless, with Dave Portnoy ripping Whittingham as "asleep at the wheel." Oregon, meanwhile, has bounced back after a coaching change of its own, beating Ohio State 41-28 on the road on Saturday.

On Monday, Whittingham praised Lanning but suggested that Oregon's resources play a big role in its recruiting success.

"Tireless recruiter. He really does a great job recruiting and it doesn't hurt that you have $50+ million dollars at your disposal to, in essence, outbid everybody," Whittingham said, at his weekly press conference. "Not everybody, but a lot of people. But that's not his fault. That's a credit."

Later in the week, Lanning was asked about Whittingham's comments, and he had a pointed response.

"I've got nothing to say to Coach Whit other than I think he's an unbelievable coach, and I get it. They're spending more of their money right now on their coaches that they're letting go, so maybe it makes it hard to sign players," Lanning said, at his press conference.

Lanning also pointed out that Michigan has outbid Oregon for some recruits of its own.

"But I know they've signed a couple of guys that we weren't able to compete with what they decided to do, and maybe a lot of that money is tied up for them in another player. I don't know," Lanning continued. "But Coach Whittingham is an unbelievable coach. He's a Hall of Fame coach. I've got nothing but respect for him."

Needless to say, Saturday's game in Eugene should have a little extra juice.
