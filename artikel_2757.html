Cowboys news: Rookie edge rusher to IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Dallas loses one edge rusher to gain another.

The Cowboys officially placed OLB Malachi Lawrence on injured reserve ahead of their Week 9 matchup against the Colts. Earlier in the week, Cowboys coach Brian Schottenheimer said Lawrence could potentially miss several weeks with an ankle injury. Now, he'll miss at least the next four games. Lawrence will be eligible to return as early as Dallas' Week 13 matchup against the Seahawks.

DeMarvion Overshown's bit hit on Cardinals running back costs him big bucks.

The NFL has issued a fine of $9,528 to Dallas Cowboys linebacker DeMarvion Overshown for the use of his helmet on a hit that knocked Arizona Cardinals running back Bam Knight out of the game last week, per multiple reports. Knight took the hit to the helmet from Overshown in the second quarter, which forced a fumble that the Cowboys recovered. Overshown led with his shoulder and made contact with Knight's head, but the play was not penalized.

What's on tap this weekend for the NFC East.

Last week, the Cowboys let a golden opportunity to gain ground on the Giants in the NFC East slip through their fingers. But even after their folly against Arizona, Dallas is still just a couple of games back from the lead with plenty of season still to go. And really, if the playoffs are still in the cards for the Cowboys, winning the division is the most likely path. The NFC North is monopolizing the bracket right now, with the Bears, VIkings and Packers holding all of the current wild card spots. Green Bay would be the seventh seed with a 5-3 record and a head-to-head win over Dallas, which will be tough to overcome.
