Eagles sign RB Jaydon Blue to the active roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Eagles signed running back Jaydon Blue to the active roster on Tuesday, the team announced.

They also signed defensive tackle Leki Fotu to the practice squad.

Blue has bounced between the practice squad and the active roster this season, with Tuesday's signing the eighth transaction involving him this season.

The Eagles most recently waived him from the active roster on Nov. 10 and re-signed him to the practice squad six days later.

He has played in two games, seeing action on eight offensive snaps and 13 on special teams.

Blue, a fifth-round pick of the Cowboys in 2025, had 34 rushes for 129 yards and a touchdown in five games for Dallas.
