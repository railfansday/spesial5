WR Brandon Aiyuk reportedly clears waivers after finally being released by 49ers, would like to sign soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After finally being released by the San Francisco 49ers, veteran wide receiver Brandon Aiyuk has cleared waivers, according to ESPN's Adam Schefter, who also reported Tuesday that Aiyuk would like to sign with a new team soon.

He's expected to attract interest from a few teams, including the Washington Commanders, per Schefter.

The Niners releasing Aiyuk marked the end of a months-long standoff between the team and player. Aiyuk missed the entire 2025 NFL season after recovering from a knee injury he suffered in 2024, just months after signing a four-year, $120 million extension with the team. He suffered a torn ACL and MCL in late October 2024 and hasn't played a snap in the league since.

San Francisco had a two-week roster exemption for Aiyuk. It expired on Monday, and as a result, the franchise opted to release Aiyuk, giving both the team and the wide receiver a fresh start.

Aiyuk was subject to waivers, but upon clearing them, he's reportedly a free agent. NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reported on Monday that a spot on the Commanders' practice squad is the likeliest next step for Aiyuk.

After being placed on the physically unable to perform list, reports later emerged that Aiyuk failed to participate in team meetings and the Niners were frustrated with the "lack of communication." Eventually, he was placed on the Reserve/Left Squad list in December 2025 due to his continued absence and lack of communication with the team.

Aiyuk was officially reinstated from the Reserve/Left Squad list on Nov. 9, at which time the 49ers were granted a two-week roster exemption to decide if they wanted to release him, trade him or add him to the 53-man roster. The reinstatement was the first step toward Aiyuk's eventual release.

Aiyuk's breakup with the Niners is no surprise. Leading up to the 2025 season, reports surfaced that guarantees in his contract were voided amid growing frustrations between the organization and the veteran wideout.

Then in January, 49ers GM John Lynch officially acknowledged the impending divorce between the two sides, stating in his end-of-season news conference, "I think it's safe to say he's played his last snap with the 49ers."

Aiyuk is 28 and hasn't played since Week 7 of the 2024 season. Before his injury, he had spent his entire five-year career with the 49ers.

San Francisco drafted him with the 25th pick in the 2020 NFL Draft. He had his breakout season in 2022, finishing with 1,015 yards and eight touchdowns. Aiyuk returned in 2023 and improved, finishing with 1,342 yards and seven touchdowns.

The Niners have turned to Deebo Samuel, Mike Evans and Keenan Allen in the passing game in Aiyuk's absence, with promising rookie De'Zhaun Stribling expected to return from injury soon.

If Aiyuk joins Washington, the Commanders will look to get him up to speed as No. 1 wide receiver Terry McLaurin has been ruled out for the season due to a torn ACL. Stefon Diggs and Antonio Williams have picked up the slack, but Washington could use Aiyuk if he can play close to his pre-injury level.
