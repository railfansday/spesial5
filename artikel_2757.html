Houston Rockets vs. Los Angeles Clippers game preview

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Houston Rockets return to the friendly confines of the Toyota Center tonight to take on the Los Angeles Clippers. LA played in Dallas on Saturday, so they haven't had a ton of travel and come in with a day off. The Rockets have been off for two days following a four-game road trip that included some long flights, a back-to-back, and three rest disadvantage games.

Brandon Ingram is making his season debut tonight. He'd been out with a heel injury, so this is actually his first game in a Clippers uniform after getting traded for Kawhi Leonard in the offseason.

The Clippers are an odd team. They're second in three point percentage but give up a top-5 shooting percentage from deep. They're 29th in turnovers but third in steals. They elect not to crash the offensive boards (28th), which is usually the sign of a team that likes to get into its base defense. However, their defense has been poor this season, as teams have a 57% effective field goal percentage against them. As a reminder, Jeff van Gundy is still on the coaching staff. Minus Kawhi and Ivica Zubac, the defense hasn't been as ferocious as past seasons.
