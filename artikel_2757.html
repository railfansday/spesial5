VOTE: CFBHN/Herald-Tribune Boys Athlete of the Week for Nov. 2-7

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Each week, the Herald-Tribune staff will select nominees for the Herald-Tribune Boys Athlete of the Week poll sponsored by Central Florida Behavioral Network, Inc. Nominees are derived from scores and stats emailed in by Sarasota and Manatee County coaches during the week to htincoming@gmail.com. Readers can then vote in the online poll to determine the Boys Athlete of the Week.

The Herald-Tribune Boys Athlete of the Week sponsored by Central Florida Behavioral Health Network for Oct. 26-31, 2026, was Sarasota High golfer Jacob Menard, who won the poll with 47 percent of the vote. Menard shot a 72 at the Class 3A-11 Championship, placing second in the competition. He helped the Sailors to a second place finish.

A poll will be posted at heraldtribune.com each Monday, and voting will end at 10 a.m. Friday each week.

The nominees for the week of Nov. 2-7, 2026 are:

Chase Marshall, Wellen Park: Marshall finished finished third at the District 2A-6 race, running a 16:44.4.

Colton Prater, Lakewood Ranch: Prate placed sixth in District 4A-6, running a 16:39 and helping Lakewood Ranch to a third place finish.

Jude Menard, Sarasota: Menard tied for eighth in Region 3A-3, shooting a 76.

Will Thomas, Lakewood Ranch: Thomas finished tied for fifth during the 3A-3 Regional, helping Lakewood Ranch to a regional title at Saddlebrook Resort.

Ty Wailand, Manatee: Wailand tied for eighth in Region 3A-3, shooting a 76.

Dion Donkor, Saint Stephen's: Donkor scored on a 64-yard run and a 4-yard score early in the fourth quarter in Saint Stephen's 29-6 win over St. Edwards.

Will Roseboro Jr., Saint Stephen's: Roseboro scored a 65-yard touchdown to give the Falcons a lead they never relinquished in Saint Stephen's 29-6 win over St. Edwards. .

Jackson Irwin, Sarasota: Irwin won the 500 free state title, swimming a 4:19.51. He was also second in the 200 IM with a 1:48.12 and helped the Sailors place third in the 200 free relay.

Andrew Malaj, Sarasota: Malaj finished second with a 1:37.28 in the 200 free and helped the Sailors place third in the 200 free relay.

Daniil Siutsou, Riverview: Siutsou finished fourth in the 200 free.

Riverview High quarterback Logan Carrillo was voted the Herald-Tribune Athlete of the Week, sponsored by Central Florida Behavioral Network, Inc., for the week of Aug. 17-22, 2026.
