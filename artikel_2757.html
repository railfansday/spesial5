Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The big story heading into the Cleveland Browns matchup with the New Orleans Saints is the visiting team's quarterback position. Shedeur Sanders will start, but Deshaun Watson has cleared concussion protocol and could be set to be the backup, after missing practice all week.

On the other side, the home Saints are coming off their bye week, made a position coach change, and the final injury report provided hope that WR Jordyn Tyson and OT Kelvin Banks Jr. could return from injured reserve.
