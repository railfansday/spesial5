Who was the Murfreesboro area TSSAA athlete of the week for Nov. 16-22?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Who was the Murfreesboro area TSSAA athlete of the week for Nov. 16-22?

Miles Alexander, Blackman basketball: Scored 19 points in a 58-44 win over Antioch.

Jayden Shannon, Cannon County football: Had both a rushing and receiving touchdown in a 42-7 win over Bledsoe County in the 2A quarterfinals.

Owen King, Central Magnet basketball: Combined for 27 points in two games.

Kam Linton, Eagleville basketball: Combined for 52 points in three games.

Eli Bell, Oakland basketball: Scored 22 points in a 52-46 win over Cumberland County.

Eva Birkofer, PCA basketball: Scored 17 points in a 59-45 loss to CPA.

Jack Liu, PCA basketball: Combined for 39 points in two games.

Gavin Hampton, Riverdale football: Was 14 of 18 for 294 yards and four touchdowns while adding a rushing touchdown in a 35-24 win over Cleveland in the Class 6A quarterfinals.

Arden Lewis, Rockvale basketball: Combined for 35 points in two games.

Kale Martin, Siegel basketball: Combined for 32 points in two games.

Christian Beattie, Smyrna football: Had 16 carries for 95 yards and a touchdown in a 38-14 loss to Maryville in the Class 6A quarterfinals.

Ze'Keice McCarver, Smyrna basketball: Scored 29 points in an 87-73 loss to Columbia.

Sean Goggins, Stewarts Creek basketball: Combined for 58 points in two games and had two double-doubles.

Amira Maynard, Stewarts Creek basketball: Scored 31 points with 11 rebounds in a 71-46 loss to Independence.
