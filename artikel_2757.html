Houston’s Dedan Thomas Jr. reaches Dwyane Wade territory after Player Era MVP run

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Houston guard Dedan Thomas Jr. added another accolade to his growing collection.

Thomas helped the No. 8 Cougars beat Kansas, 92-83, in the championship round of the Players Era 8 Tournament at Michelob ULTRA Arena in Las Vegas on Thursday. He had 16 points, eight assists, and three steals.

For his impressive three-game run, Thomas was named MVP of the annual tournament. He averaged 22.3 points, 4.7 assists, and 1.7 steals. He shot 51.2% from the field.

Houston remained undefeated at 6-0. Thomas, who is in his first year with the Cougars after transferring from LSU, reached a rare milestone, according to OptaSTATS.

"In his first six games in a Houston uniform, Dedan Thomas Jr. has: 120+ points, 30+ assists, 10+ steals, and a 6-0 record," said the post on X.

"In the last 30 years, only one other Division I player has done that in his first six games with a team: Dwyane Wade (with Marquette in 2001-2002)."
