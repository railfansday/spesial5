Jameis Winston used early-2000’s one-hit wonder as hard count vs. Commanders

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Washington Commanders beat the New York Giants, 24-21, on "Thursday Night Football" to start Week 10 of the NFL season. The Giants took the lead with 8:13 remaining and had two chances to put the game away, but went three-and-out twice in the final minutes before the Commanders finally took the lead for good.

One of those unsuccessful series by the Giants generated a truly remarkable moment as Jameis Winston went under center and sang a popular song from 26 years ago that will just never go away.

Facing fourth-and-one from deep in their own territory in the fourth quarter analytics said they should go for it. While Kirk Herbstreit and Al Michaels debated whether they would really do it, the Giants lined up in a short yardage package with Jameis Winston under center.

As Winston started to bark calls, it seemed like the Giants were just trying to draw the Commanders offside. Then after a shift it became clear as Winston asked, "Who let the dogs out?"

"Who let the dogs out," shouted Winston. "Who, who, who who?"

Winston would have been 6-years-old when this song was released as a single off the soundtrack of "Rugrats in Paris: The Movie." We can confirm that is the perfect age for someone to enjoy that song. And now it has replaced "Omaha!" as the most iconic thing anyone has ever said at the line of scrimmage in an NFL game.

The Giants are now 5-3-1 on the season and still control their own destiny in the NFC East. Just eight weeks to go, which is so much time for Jameis to work in other early 00's one-hit wonders.
