‘I hold my hands up’ - McAvennie issues fresh apology to Celtic star Auston Trusty

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Frank McAvennie has issued a full apology to Auston Trusty and his family after admitting his initial response to criticism over comments about the Celtic defender “didn’t go far enough”.

The former Celtic striker faced an online backlash after questioning the length of time Trusty had been away from football following the death of his father.

McAvennie has now returned to the issue and said he is “genuinely sorry” for any hurt caused by his remarks.

In a fresh apology, he said: “Frank here. I’ve had more time to think about what I said about Auston on the podcast and I know my statement yesterday didn’t go far enough.

“I’m genuinely sorry for any hurt I’ve caused Auston or his family. That’s the last thing I’d ever want.

“I hold my hands up and I’ve learned my lesson.

“I genuinely wish Auston and his family all the very best.”

McAvennie's original comments came on his Let Me Be Frank podcast as he discussed Trusty's absence from the Celtic team.

He said: “I’ve got every respect for that, but how long do you want off?

“Martin (O’Neill) says he’s away to celebrate the life of his dad.

“Celebrate the life? You’ve got football. You’re getting paid and we’re in trouble. How long is it going to be?

“It’s nonsense. My dad was the best man, the only man I looked up to, when he died.

Following criticism of those remarks, McAvennie initially acknowledged that he should have chosen his words more carefully.

He said: “I can understand why people feel my comments were insensitive, and looking back, I should have chosen my words more carefully.

“Everyone deals with grief differently, and I certainly meant no disrespect to Auston or his family.

“Celtic need the big man back, and that’s what I was clumsily trying to say.

“I wish him and his family all the very best.”

McAvennie has now conceded that statement did not adequately address his original remarks and offered a more direct apology to the Celtic defender and his family.
