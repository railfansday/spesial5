Mavropanos departure claim produces a ‘meh’ response – and here’s why | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Had the news filtered through in August, as West Ham prepared to do battle in the Championship, that Dinos Mavropanos had not signed the contract extension put in front of him after relegation, there might have been worry all around.

West Ham had just learned that Axel Disasi wasn’t coming back: The idea of Dinos leaving too would have been downright scary.

At least, going forward, Mavropanos was awesome. Five goals, from anyone, is not a bad return at all in just 15 out of 48 completed games. The lack of clean sheets, though, highlighted West Ham’s defensive vulnerabilities in which Mavropanos was, of course, a key component.

Reports first from Florian Plettenberg at Sky Germany, backed up more recently by KUMB, both claim that Mavropanos in fact did not sign the extension and that with a year and a half left on his West Ham contract, the club are open to selling him if the right offer comes along in January .

Great going forward but back to appearing a more shaky defender

‘Open to selling him’ is very different to ‘going to offload him’ and will presumably be dependent upon bringing a replacement into the club during the winter. West Ham have already parked Jean Clair Todibo in France for a season and so are short of one front line centre back.

Selling Dinos without a replacement isn’t going to be an option. However the willingness of the club to sell if the price is right shows common sense from a financial point of view and, let’s face it, Mavropanos has been no colossus defensively.

In any case there are any number of decent replacements available: Better to grab a solid transfer fee now than lose him for free in a year.

It is a reflection of his more modest defensive performances since Axel Disasi departed that news of his ‘possible’ departure creates little more than a shrug of the shoulders: In fact the back line has been poor for three or four years so this could be the start of something better.
