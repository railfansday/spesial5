Tenerife footballer Jesus de Miguel dies after on-field collision

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Tenerife player Jesus De Miguel has died after an on-field collision in a match on Monday, his club have announced.

The collision occurred as Andorra goalkeeper Jesus Owono raced from his goal to challenge De Miguel for the ball in stoppage time of a Spanish second-tier game at Estadio Heliodoro Rodriguez Lopez.

The Canary Islands club said in a statement on Tuesday: “Racked with pain, with a lump in our throats and unable to believe that this is true, from CD Tenerife we communicate the tragic passing, this Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at 8:00 a.m., of Jesus de Miguel Alameda.

“There are no words of comfort; there is absolutely nothing that can alleviate this feeling overflowing with sadness.

Late on Monday night Tenerife issued a statement confirming De Miguel had been admitted to hospital and was initially described as “stable despite the severity of his condition”.

An update in the early hours of Tuesday morning said his condition had “worsened neurologically”.

Owono was immediately sent off after he charged out of his goal to challenge De Miguel in the centre circle, his knee appearing to connect with the Tenerife player’s head.

Andorra and Tenerife players beckoned on medical staff immediately and appeared visibly upset by De Miguel’s condition.

The match was suspended after the incident.

Opponents Andorra issued a statement on X which read: “With broken hearts and devastated, we want to send our most sincere condolences to the family of Jesus de Miguel, to all his teammates, friends, and to CD Tenerife. Rest in peace.”

LaLiga said in a statement: “We wish to express our deep sadness over the tragic passing of Jesús de Miguel Alameda. We share in the grief of his family, his loved ones, and @CDTOficial during these very difficult times. All our affection and support. Rest in peace.”

Meanwhile, other Spanish clubs posted messages of condolence to Tenerife and De Miguel’s family.

Barcelona said: “From FC Barcelona, we are deeply saddened by the tragic news of the passing of Jesus de Miguel, CD Tenerife player. Our thoughts and support are with his family, friends and loved ones during these difficult times.”

Real Madrid said the club “wishes to express its condolences and affection to his family, his teammates, his club and all his loved ones”.
