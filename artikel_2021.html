The understanding of how the Newcastle United recruitment team worked is based on myth and rumour.

Steve Nickson left on good terms for a more senior role.

All signings were approved or rejected by PIF with neither the Sporting Director, Eddie Howe or the recruitment team having any authority to make any financial decision.

The allegation that Eddie Howe only wanted proven Premier League players is also a myth.

Here is the list January 2022 onwards whilst Eddie Howe was with Newcastle United: •Kieran Trippier (Atlético, £12m) •Chris Wood (Burnley, £25m) •Bruno Guimarães (Lyon, £40m) •Dan Burn (Brighton, £13m) •Matt Targett (Villa, loan) Summer 2022 •Targett (made permanent, £15m) •Nick Pope (Burnley, £10m) •Sven Botman (Lille, £35m) •Alexander Isak (Real Sociedad, £63m) •Loris Karius (free) •Garang Kuol (Central Coast Mariners) January 2023 •Anthony Gordon (Everton, £45m) •Harrison Ashby (West Ham, £3m) Summer 2023 •Sandro Tonali (Milan, £55m) •Harvey Barnes (Leicester, £38m) •Tino Livramento (Southampton, £32m+) •Lewis Hall (Chelsea, loan then £28m) •Yankuba Minteh (Odense, £6m) Summer 2024 •Lloyd Kelly (free) •Odysseas Vlachodimos (Forest, £20m) •William Osula (Sheffield Utd, £10m+) •John Ruddy (free) •Miodrag Pivaš (Jedinstvo Ub) •Vakhtang Salia (Dinamo Tbilisi, joined 2025) Summer 2025 •Anthony Elanga (Forest, £55m) •Malick Thiaw (Milan, £35m) •Jacob Ramsey (Villa, £40m) •Nick Woltemade (Stuttgart, £65m+) •Yoane Wissa (Brentford, £55m) •Aaron Ramsdale (Southampton, loan) •Antonio Cordero (Málaga, free) •Park Seung-soo (Suwon) Summer 2026 (before he left) •Ewen Jaouen (Reims) •Bazoumana Touré (Hoffenheim, £43m) •Sean Steur (Ajax) •Aladji Bamba (Monaco, signed 24 July)

Roughly £390m on players from English clubs and £420m on players from foreign clubs, about £810m in all.

An inconvenient truth when trying to support an anti Eddie Howe narrative – but factual.
