Mikel Merino speaks out on Arsenal role as January rumours grow

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Merino has played down any speculation around his potential exit during January. He has made it clear he will fight to keep his place in the squad.

He also spoke about speculation around Martin Zubimendi, who has been linked with Atletico Madrid.

Both players have been questioned over their role in Mikel Arteta’s midfield. There is increasing competition for positions at the Emirates.

Merino feels that there is always an intense fight for places in top-level football. He spoke about how strong and deep Arsenal are ahead of their Nations League clash against the Czech Republic.

Both Merino and Zubimendi were also keen to stress that they will be looking to gain more opportunities and not get distracted.

Merino has had to adapt to his new role at Arsenal after playing a key role on his team’s side. However, Merino has struggled to get into the starting XI.

He admitted that he wants to play more games. However, he acknowledged that it was difficult to get into the team, as there are so many quality players.

This shows that his playing time has not affected his relationship with Arteta.

Merino was able to understand what it is like to be a manager because his father was a football coach. He understands that tough decisions have to be made when selecting your starting XI.

This can be an important aspect in a difficult season, as all players need to play their part to make sure Arsenal stay challenging in multiple competitions.

Merino’s comments are very interesting considering the uncertainty around Zubimendi.

There are reports Zubimendi has been linked to Atletico Madrid and whether he will be looking to move in January.

Merino didn’t give a definite answer that these players will stay at Arsenal, but the comments do suggest they are looking to improve their position in the squad.

He also underlined the importance of having good cover, with Spain boss Luis de la Fuente believing players coming off the bench can be just as vital as those starting.

This is something Arsenal will want their squad to take on board. They will not always be able to play the same midfielders due to the demands of playing in both domestic and European competitions.

There is still time for Merino and Zubimendi to turn their situation around, so what they are doing now may not indicate how important they are going to be in the campaign.

Arsenal will be keen to keep Merino happy, and he has made his position clear. This should give some reassurance ahead of the January transfer window.
