Sheffield United must use Hayden Hackney in Sunderland transfer negotiations

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Sheffield United had a frustrating summer transfer window, with Chris Wilder losing several key players.

Gus Hamer, Callum O’Hare and Andre Brooks all moved on from Bramall Lane, and whilst they generated decent fees, the Blades chief was restricted to loans and free agents to bolster the squad.

Despite that, Sheffield United are in a decent position at this stage of the campaign, and there will be a belief that promotion is a possibility.

However, with January looming, fans of the Yorkshire outfit will once again be wondering if off-field issues could negatively impact Wilder and the team.

That’s because it has been reported that midfielder Sydie Peck is on the radar of Premier League clubs, including Newcastle United and Sunderland.

Such clubs monitoring Peck shouldn’t come as a surprise, as the 22-year-old is an exciting, energetic midfielder, and his performances in the previous campaign saw him nominated for Young Player of the Year.

As well as that, a recent hat-trick against Preston suggests Peck is adding more to his game, so clubs will see him as a player that has the potential to make his mark in the top flight.

The Blades can’t stop clubs taking a look at their players, and they should actually be pleased that they have individuals who are interesting Premier League clubs.

But, the reason this transfer update will worry fans is that Sheffield United’s hierarchy haven’t exactly done well when it comes to sales in recent windows.

Hamer, O’Hare and Brooks all went for fees that felt below their market value, and there will be a worry that history repeats itself with Peck, especially given the financial situation at the club.

When it comes to determining Peck’s worth, the most obvious comparison is Hayden Hackney, who joined Everton from Middlesbrough in a deal worth up to £24m in the summer.

It should require that sort of number before the Blades entertain a sale, as Peck is a player of similar ability.

Like Hackney, he is capable in possession, he plays with intensity, and he can travel with the ball. That’s not to say he is the finished article, but Peck has all the tools to develop into a very good all-round midfielder.

With Peck’s contract expiring in 2028, the Blades know his value will start to drop in the summer if a new deal isn’t agreed.

Yet, when it comes to an asking price going into January, Sheffield United’s current position must be a crucial factor, as they will still believe promotion is possible.

The extended play-off format means the top eight have a shot at reaching the Premier League, and Wilder’s men are just two points and two places away from that right now.

Even if a bid in excess of £20m arrives for Peck in January, the reality is that there’s no evidence to suggest the owners will pump that back into the team.

But, even if there was, it’s going to be difficult to spend that money to get players who can make an instant impact as January is a notoriously difficult month to do business.

Therefore, if Sheffield United do sell Peck, they may be throwing away any chance of winning promotion this season.

Ultimately, Sheffield United will know that they won’t be able to retain Peck for the long-term unless they win promotion, and it does seem a matter of time before he takes that next step.
