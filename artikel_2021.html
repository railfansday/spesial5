Declan Rice’s latest Arsenal evolution shows why Arteta wanted him tied down

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Declan Rice’s new contract shows how much of an asset he is to Mikel Arteta. His recent performances show why.

The England international has reportedly signed a new five-year deal which will keep him at the Emirates Stadium until 2031. The contract is a reward for the midfielder who has been instrumental in Arsenal’s success since he moved to the club in 2023 from West Ham.

Rice has not just provided the presence and protection that Arsenal were hoping for with their £105 million signing. He has continued to develop his game, especially after returning to his deeper midfield role this season.

Rice came in as a defensive midfielder, but Arteta quickly saw how much he could offer higher up the park.

His physicality, ability to run into the box and set piece threat made him a useful left-sided number eight. Rice had a huge impact in midfield for Arsenal, bringing goals and a new attacking threat.

But Rice has played in a more advanced position on the right-hand side of the midfield duo this season, and this has not limited his impact but instead highlighted another key part of his game.

Rice has made the most line-breaking passes in the Premier League this season, with Opta stats reported by Sky Sports showing he is second only to Premier League players.

This is of particular interest, as he has often come under criticism for his distribution. But he is averaging nearly double the amount of line-breaking passes he did last season.

While his running ability is still an asset, he is also becoming much better at penetrating through the defensive lines to find his teammates, giving Arsenal another option to move the ball forward with.

The development of Rice is key in securing a new contract at Arsenal.

The 27-year-old has already proven to be one of the most reliable members of Arteta’s squad and has shown great longevity by only missing five games out of his 119 Premier League outings in his first spell at the club.

Former West Ham teammate Jack Wilshere has also praised Rice’s strong work ethic, ability to adapt and natural leadership qualities.

The reported 5-year deal will keep Rice at Arsenal until 2031, into his early 30s. While the terms of the deal have yet to be signed, it is a huge statement from the club.

They are not only rewarding Rice for what he has done but also investing in what he can do.

With Arteta finding new ways to utilise Rice’s qualities in midfield, Rice could still have some of his best years ahead of him.
