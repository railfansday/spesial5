Sources: Newcastle make clear Matthias Jaissle sack call

Newcastle United are planning to stick with Matthias Jaissle despite growing concerns, sources have told Football Insider.

A 1-1 draw against Fulham last time out has piled increased pressure on the boss' position, with Newcastle now having won just three of their last ten games in all competitions.

Large sections of the supporters at St James' Park are losing patience with the manager, and have begun to call for his head, with recent results doing little to win their favour.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Jaissle still has the full support of the Newcastle board who hope he will turn things around.

The higher powers at St James' Park are prepared to endure some inconsistent displays this seasona and are aware the boss has been afforded a tough hand.

Major changes on and off the pitch during the summer has also contributed to the side's struggles, with Jaissle not seen as the main source of Newcastle's poor run of form.

Former Man United coach Mick Brown told Football Insider on 3 November that Newcastle are planning to back Jaissle despite some concerns about performances.

A draw against Fulham last time out is likely to add to the concerns, but it was an improved performance compared to previous weeks despite the late equaliser.

There is still optimism that Jaissle is the man to lead Newcastle towards their ambitious goals, and regardless of their recent dip, he has the support of the board.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I don't think they'll change their stance just yet. The board still have their full backing behind Matthias Jaissle.

"But look, it's not been a good run of form. There's no getting away from that. Four defeats from their past six games in all competitions.

"And probably the surprising thing is their struggles at home at St James' Park where they're normally so strong.

"Look, there's no getting away from it, you are judged on your results, so if results don't improve, it's just going to bring more pressure on Jaissle.

"They'll be hoping they can go into this international break after an improved performance against Fulham, because they've got a tough run of games coming after the international break."

Despite retaining the continuned support of the board, there remains a chance Jaissle could be sacked in the near future with significant pressure mounting on his position.

Football Insider revealed on 6 November that Jaissle is at risk of becoming the first Premier League manager to be sacked this season as a result of recent concerns.

Other managers in the frame to be sacked in the coming weeks include Crystal Palace boss Pierre Sage, with the Eagles already believed to be weighing up his future.

However, Palace captain Dean Henderson spoke ahead of their 1-1 draw against Hoffenheim, as he insisted the players are looking internally and still back Sage.

That is more than can be said for Jaissle, with recent reports coming out of Newcastle that suggest the players are unhappy and have concerns about the manager's role.
