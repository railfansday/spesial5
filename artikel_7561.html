The E.U. wants a new For You Page so that its citizens don’t get stuck in TikTok rabbit holes

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Does the European Union have the authority to mandate changes to TikTok's For You Page? The continental body is making that claim.

The E.U. announced on Wednesday that the addictive design of the FYP violates the Digital Services Act. A press release discusses the "rabbit hole effect" of TikTok's infinitely scrolling vertical feed, which is filled with personalized recommendations.

"Every time a user scrolls or likes a video on TikTok, the algorithm serves more of the same leading users down rabbit holes they cannot easily escape from," said Henna Virkkunen, the E.U.'s EVP of Tech Sovereignty, Security, and Democracy, in a statement.
