FELYRI CONSULTING LIMITED: Research Consultant

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Headquarters: United Kingdom
URL: https://www.felyri.com/homepage/

The Role:
This position is a strategic consulting role responsible for providing professional risk consulting and risk solutions aimed at supporting the sound operations and strategic development of organizations. The incumbent will be required to identify, assess, and address various complex risks arising from both internal and external business environments, including policy risks, market risks, compliance risks, and operational risks.

Candidates are required to possess more than five years of in-depth policy analysis experience. The role focuses on forward-looking risk forecasting by systematically analyzing government policy announcements, international geopolitical events, and industry statistical data. The consultant must accurately identify potential emerging risks and external uncertainties in advance, formulate targeted and executable risk response strategies, and provide professional solution recommendations to prevent and reduce organizational risk losses.

 

Key Responsibilities：

  • Conduct in-depth research on topics such as the macroeconomic environment, regional development, industry trends, the international landscape, and public governance; independently carry out data collection, information organization, data analysis, and the distillation of key insights.
  • Responsible for providing professional risk consulting and risk solutions designed to support the sound operations and strategic development of various organizations. The incumbent must identify, assess, and address a wide range of complex risks arising from both internal and external business environments, including policy risks, market risks, compliance risks, and operational risks.
  • Continuously monitor cutting-edge industry developments, policy trends, and changes in the external environment, and regularly publish various thematic research reports and trend analysis reports.

 

You idealy have:

  • Bachelor's degree or higher; at least 3 years of work experience in research and analysis.
  • Possesses strong writing skills, including excellent written expression, logical reasoning, and structured writing abilities, and is capable of producing in-depth research reports.
  • By searching, screening, and synthesizing information from publicly available online sources, we extract valuable leads from the vast amount of publicly available internet information and compile them into reports.
  • Diligent and responsible, able to submit reports promptly and efficiently in accordance with company requirements.

 

Salary:

We use a project-based billing system, with compensation for each report ranging from $500 to $1,000. The specific amount is determined based on a combination of factors, including the report’s complexity, comprehensiveness, and quality.

Please be sure to include your most recent resume when you drop off your application so that we can conduct a comprehensive evaluation of you.
