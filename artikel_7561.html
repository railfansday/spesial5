Customer.io: Sr Account Manager, AMER (Remote)

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Over 8,000 companies — from scrappy startups to global brands — use our platform to send billions of emails, push notifications, in-app messages, and SMS every day. Customer.io powers automated communication that people actually want to receive. We help teams send smarter, more relevant messages using real-time behavioral data.

Hi, I’m TJ Jackson, Manager of Account Management at Customer.io, and I’m hiring a Sr. Account Manager to join our North America's team. This is a sales role. You’ll own a portfolio of mid-market and enterprise customers and be accountable for generating expansion pipeline, closing new revenue, and securing renewals.You’ll build on existing customer relationships and prospect beyond them. That means cold calling people who haven’t worked with us, identifying new buying teams, and winning business with additional brands within the same parent company. You’ll lead discovery, build relationships with multiple stakeholders, and own each deal from the first conversation through negotiation and close.We’re looking for someone with a track record in B2B sales who can walk us through an expansion deal they sourced and closed. You should be comfortable generating your own pipeline, multi-threading across complex accounts, and using a sales methodology such as MEDDICC to qualify opportunities and move deals forward.This role is open to candidates based in US and Canada only.

We believe in transparency. Starting salary for this role is $142,900 - $171,000 OTE USD (or equivalent in your local currency) depending on experience and subject to market rate adjustment. This role is a 70/30 OTE split. In addition to base salary, this role includes an equity grant in the form of stock options, so you share in the success you help build.

Our inclusive benefits package supports your well-being and growth, including 100% coverage of medical, dental, vision, mental health, and supplemental insurance premiums for you and your family. We also offer 16 weeks paid parental leave, unlimited PTO, stipends for remote work and wellness, a professional development budget, and more.
