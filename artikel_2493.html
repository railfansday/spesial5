Lauletta’s XI: Fishlock lives, Gotham celebrates, and early playoff kicks

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Week 27 saw Gotham wrap up the Shield for the first time, the Reign slip into the playoffs to keep Jess Fishlock’s career going, and Ashley Sanchez fall short of the standalone goals record — and the playoffs.

Here are 11 thoughts from the weekend. We’ll do this every Monday following NWSL match weeks. Some points will be factual, some will be opinion based. And some will just be random statements or observations.

She missed a golden opportunity on a cross from Mia Fishel, but in the end Jess Fishlock and the Reign got by the Pride, 1-0 to secure the final spot in this year’s playoffs. As it turned out, Jordyn Bugg’s 68th minute goal was vital. The Courage scored late to defeat the Summit and would have grabbed the final spot with a Reign draw. Bugg’s goal came off a corner kick following a sequence that saw Fishlock animatedly call for a handball in the box that was not given.

Fishlock is retiring at the end of the season, and her departure from the match Sunday was met by a large ovation 16,095 fans at Lumen Field. It was a far cry from Fishlock and the Reign’s first home game in Seattle that drew 2,618 to Starfire Stadium in Tukwila.

Next stop is New Jersey where the Reign will attempt to knock off Shield winners Gotham in Sunday’s quarterfinal.

Ann-Katrin Berger said the Shield should now be the standard at Gotham. She spoke after her side shut out the Royals 2-0 to lock up the club’s first Shield as the top team in the regular season. Gotham have now won two NWSL Championships, a Challenge Cup, Shield, and Concacaf W Champions Cup since 2023.
