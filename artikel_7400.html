We are looking for a Client Services Associate I, Oncology (East Coast) to provide exceptional service and support to BillionToOne's customers. The ideal candidate for this role is an ambitious and intelligent individual who is highly self-motivated and can effortlessly help drive and influence a positive patient and customer experience. The Client Services Associate I is a full-time remote position and reports to the Client Services Manager.

Working hours are Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM ET.
