Bitwise offloads 23.7M XRP while silently stacking 126M more

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Bitwise’s XRP ETF sold 23.7 million XRP to meet cash redemptions in the third quarter, according to SEC filings.

Bitwise’s quarterly filings show its XRP ETF sold 23.7 million XRP to meet cash redemptions from July through September 2026, even as the fund increased its holdings by 126.3 million XRP.

The quarter’s redemption sales slightly exceeded the 22.9 million XRP the fund sold for the same purpose in the entire first half of the year.

Bitwise’s holdings climbed from 286.8 million XRP at the end of June to 413.1 million at the end of September. The Sept. 30 quarterly report, filed Nov. 6, records both the XRP the fund sold for redemptions and the larger amount it added for new shares.

Subtracting the six-month XRP schedule from the nine-month schedule isolates what happened in the third quarter. Bitwise bought 87.9 million tokens to back newly issued shares and received another 62.3 million XRP in kind.

Together, those flows added 150.2 million tokens before the fund’s cash redemption sales and roughly 241,383 XRP transferred to pay sponsor fees. Bitwise reported zero in-kind redemptions for the quarter.

Purchases and in-kind creations added 150.2 million XRP in Q3 2026. The ETF sold 23.7 million XRP for cash redemptions.

Bitwise ended September with 126.3 million more XRP than it held at the end of June.

The sponsor arranges a sale of the underlying XRP for a cash redemption and pays out the proceeds, less a transaction fee. In-kind redemption delivers the token itself to the authorized firm or its designee.

The Franklin Templeton XRP ETF’s third-quarter filing shows the firm issued 6.25 million shares in the quarter, including 3.8 million in kind and 2.45 million for cash. The fund received 41.3 million tokens in kind and bought 26.6 million for creations, and recorded zero share redemptions.

A separate Canary XRP ETF filing puts that fund’s cash redemption sales at 9.55 million XRP for the nine months through Sept. 30. Canary reported zero tokens delivered for in-kind redemptions over those nine months.

In its Nov. 18 update, Crypto ETF’s fund table estimated $6.1 million in net outflows for Nov. 17 based on three of five tracked funds.

Alpha Finance’s daily figures also put the rounded net outflow at $6.1 million, with data for all five funds it tracks. Alpha Finance estimated $5 million in outflows from Canary and $1.1 million from Grayscale, compared with $4.9 million and $1.2 million in the Crypto ETF snapshot.

Canary’s daily share history shows 490,000 fewer shares outstanding on Nov. 17 than on Nov. 16. Bitwise’s fund data showed 36.44 million shares on Nov. 16 and 406.8 million XRP in its Nov. 17 holdings table.
