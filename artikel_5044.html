Ripple News: XRP Grabs 15% of Japan’s Crypto Trading, but Heads for Its Worst November Ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Japan ranks XRP as its third most traded crypto, behind Bitcoin and Ethereum, even as XRP price faces its worst November on record.

It looks like Japanese traders really love XRP. In Ripple news from Japan, XRP was the country’s third most traded crypto asset in September, behind only Bitcoin and Ethereum. Japan’s crypto exchange association, the JVCEA, reported ¥132.8 billion in XRP spot trading for the month, about $850 million at September’s average exchange rate.

That puts XRP close to Ethereum, which traded ¥144.1 billion. Bitcoin led with ¥507.4 billion.

XRP was Japan’s third most traded crypto asset in September, behind BTC and ETH, with ¥132.8B (~$880M) in spot volume, per JVCEA’s latest data.

When September figures are compared with data from a year earlier, Japan’s crypto trading declined by 42% across those 12 months, while XRP’s volume dropped only 9%. Its market share rose from 9.8% to 15.3%.

Despite this, the price tells a different story. XRP is down about 25% in November and on track for its worst November on record.

So why is Japan still trading XRP, and will the price follow?

The Japan Virtual and Crypto assets Exchange Association (JVCEA) is the industry body for the country’s licensed crypto exchanges. Each month it publishes a table of the most traded coins, counting spot trades, where users buy and sell the coin itself.

The gap after the top three is huge. XRP traded almost seven times as much as Solana, the fourth coin on the list.

XRP has long been popular in Japan. SBI Holdings, a large Japanese financial group, is one of Ripple’s oldest partners and runs its own crypto exchange, SBI VC Trade.

The ranking hides a bigger shift. We set the JVCEA’s September 2026 tables against the same month in 2025. Total spot trading on Japan’s exchanges fell from ¥1.49 trillion to ¥868 billion, a drop of 42%. Ether’s volume fell 57%, Bitcoin’s 38% and XRP’s just 9%.

Counted in coins, Japanese traders actually swapped 75% more XRP than a year earlier, 592 million against 339 million. Much of that is the lower price: each coin is worth far less now.

This Ripple news looks even stronger next to global markets. In Japan, XRP traded ¥92 for every ¥100 of Ether in September. On Binance’s dollar-stablecoin pairs that month, XRP traded only $33 for every $100 of Ether.

Japan’s XRP crowd has not lifted the price, though, and that is the other half of this week’s Ripple news. XRP is down 25.5% in November so far, on our count of monthly prices from Yahoo Finance.

That beats its previous worst November, a 23.6% drop in 2019, and makes 2026 a year with seven losing months out of 11. US spot XRP funds have also lost about $26 million this month, per SoSoValue.

XRP trades at $0.941, below its 50-day exponential moving average (EMA), an average that gives recent prices more weight, at $1.16, and its 200-day EMA at $1.30. It hit $0.86 on November 21, its lowest price since November 2024.

For holders following Ripple news, Japan’s numbers show steady demand that a bad month has not erased.

Whether that turns into a price recovery depends first on XRP getting back above $1.045.
