Happy Thursday! Bitcoin fell to a 12-week low as Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's exit rattled markets, adding to pressure on crypto prices.

Also in today's newsletter, Strategy and Metaplanet lose MSCI index membership, Ethlabs proposes moving Ethereum's post-quantum timeline to 2027, the SEC enters the debate over stock perpetual derivatives, and more.

Meanwhile, crypto exchange Unocoin's 'login suspension' continues, DTCC plans to bring tokenized securities to Solana, and NYDFS sets new advertising standards for crypto firms.

Bitcoin fell to its lowest level in nearly 12 weeks on Thursday, dropping to $71,200 as markets reacted to Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's abrupt departure and concerns over potential selling pressure from MSCI's removal of Strategy from its indexes.

MSCI will remove bitcoin treasury companies Strategy and Metaplanet from its USA Index and Japan Small Cap Index, respectively, at the close of Nov. 30, according to filings published Wednesday.

Ethereum research group Ethlabs has proposed bringing forward the network's post-quantum upgrade to 2027 from the Ethereum Foundation's previous December 2029 target, citing faster advances in AI and growing uncertainty around quantum computing risks.

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission has filed a court brief supporting the Commodity Futures Trading Commission in a legal dispute over whether the CFTC can approve perpetual derivatives tied to individual U.S. stocks.

Tron, Tether and Chainalysis have formed a new Digital Crime Task Force to identify and disrupt illicit activity involving USDT on the Tron blockchain.
