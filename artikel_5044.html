Happy Friday! Bitcoin today fell below $59,000 for the first time since July, extending its weekly decline to nearly 14%. JPMorgan said the sell-off has widened the gap between bitcoin's price and its estimated $86,000 production cost, adding pressure on higher-cost miners.

Also in today's newsletter, the Senate passed a bill to ban prediction market contracts tied to death and war, Elliptic traced $149 million in bitcoin through coinjoin transactions following the Binance hack, and more.

Meanwhile, Ark bought $90 million worth of Anthropic shares on debut, and U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs recorded $582 million in daily net outflows as November's total approached $3.5 billion.
