Musk compares Jensen Huang’s exit from Nvidia to dropping Michael Jordan

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

At the DealBook Summit, Musk also said he regrets how much he spent on the US midterm elections.

Elon Musk at the World Economic Forum Annual Meeting in Davos, January 2026.

Elon Musk has questioned Nvidia’s decision to part ways with Jensen Huang, comparing it to taking a star player off a winning team. Musk spoke at The New York Times’ DealBook Summit on Wednesday, CNBC reported.

“You’ve got a winning streak … and then you kick Michael Jordan off your team,” Musk told interviewer Andrew Ross Sorkin. “Doesn’t seem like a smart decision to me.”

Huang resigned as Nvidia’s president and chief executive last week. He left after reports of affairs, including one with a woman US officials reportedly suspected of spying for China. Huang co-founded the chipmaker and had led it since 1993.

Musk said no one could really replace Huang. He added that President Donald Trump had also said publicly that Huang should have stayed, CNBC reported. Nvidia has ruled out a return for Huang.

Last week, Musk replied to a post on X urging him to hire Huang, saying it would be an honour. He has not said what role Huang could take.

Musk also said he felt foolish about how much he spent on this month’s US midterm elections, Al Jazeera reported. He spent at least $474m backing Republican candidates, according to CNBC.

Republicans lost heavily in the elections. Musk said the results showed that money alone cannot decide an election, but that his candidates might have done worse without it.
