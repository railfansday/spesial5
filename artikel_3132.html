Black Friday might not officially be here just yet, but early deals are already going live, making now a great time to shop for savings on some of the best Roku and Fire TV streaming devices. From entry-level streamers to premium 4K players and soundbars, there’s already plenty worth checking out.

These devices also bring together all your favorite streaming apps in one place, from Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ and Apple TV to various free and live TV services. They’re a simple way to upgrade your entertainment setup without spending a fortune.

Below are the best Roku and Fire TV Black Friday deals I’ve found so far, including early discounts worth taking advantage of before the official sale begins. The Roku Streaming Stick HD is a simple, affordable way to bring streaming to an older TV. It plugs straight into your HDMI port, giving you access to popular apps, hundreds of free live TV channels and voice search. It also comes with a remote that controls your TV's power and volume.

This Roku Streaming Stick is a neat little upgrade for any 4K TV, giving you access to your favorite streaming apps without adding extra clutter around your setup. It supports Dolby Vision and HDR10+ for sharper, more vibrant pictures, while long-range Wi-Fi helps keep your streams running smoothly. You also get a voice remote with TV controls.

Want to get the best possible streaming experience from your TV? The Roku Ultra is the brand's most powerful streaming player, offering 4K picture quality, Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos support, plus faster app loading. It also comes with a rechargeable Voice Remote Pro featuring backlit buttons, hands-free controls and a handy lost remote finder.

If you need a smaller TV for a bedroom, kitchen or spare room, the Roku 32-Inch Select Series Smart TV is a straightforward, budget-friendly option, with a built-in streaming platform, access to hundreds of apps and free live TV channels. It also includes a voice remote, Dolby Audio and Bluetooth headphone mode for more flexible viewing.

You don't have to spend a fortune to get a 50-inch 4K TV. The Roku Select Series offers QLED colors, HDR10+ support and plenty of entertainment options through its built-in Roku platform. There's also a selection of free live TV channels and voice controls if you prefer searching without scrolling through menus.

If your current TV feels a little too small, this 55-inch Roku model gives you more room to enjoy your favorite films and shows. It offers 4K resolution, QLED colours, and HDR10+ support, plus built-in Roku for easy access to streaming apps and free live TV.

The Roku Smart Mini Projector could be a fun alternative to a traditional TV, especially if you like the idea of a bigger picture for movie nights. It has popular streaming apps available through Roku, Dolby Audio and a rotating stand that gives you more flexibility over where you point the picture.

The latest Amazon Fire TV Stick HD is an affordable way to turn almost any TV into a smart TV, giving you access to popular streaming apps and live entertainment in Full HD. The 2026 model features a slimmer design, faster performance, Wi-Fi 6, Alexa+ and a voice remote.

If you're constantly losing your TV remote down the side of the couch, this upgrade could come in handy. The Amazon Alexa Voice Remote Pro features backlit buttons, two customizable shortcuts and a built-in remote finder that makes it easier to track down when misplaced. You can also use Alexa to search for shows and control your TV.

Looking to upgrade your streaming setup? This Fire TV Stick supports 4K Ultra HD and HDR, so you can make the most of a compatible TV. It also brings most of the best streaming apps together in one place, with Wi-Fi 6 support and Alexa voice controls thrown in.

If you want something a little more powerful than a standard streaming stick, the Amazon Fire TV Cube is worth considering. It supports 4K streaming, Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos, with Alexa built in so you can search for films, switch apps and control your TV using your voice.

If your TV's built-in speakers aren't quite cutting it, the Amazon Fire TV Soundbar Plus is a handy upgrade. It delivers fuller audio with built-in bass, Dolby Atmos and DTS:X support, while a dedicated dialogue channel helps make conversations easier to hear. You also get four listening modes for movies, music, sports and night-time viewing.

The Amazon Fire TV Soundbar Plus could make a noticeable difference if you're used to relying on your TV's built-in speakers. It pairs with a separate wireless subwoofer for deeper bass, supports Dolby Atmos and DTS:X, and includes a dedicated dialogue channel to keep speech from getting lost in the action.

Not every room needs a massive TV, and this 40-inch Amazon model could be a good fit for a bedroom or spare room. You get Full HD picture quality, HDR10 and Dolby Audio, alongside Fire TV for accessing your most-used apps and free live TV channels.

Want to go even bigger with your TV? The Amazon Ember 55-Inch 4-Series has a 4K Ultra HD display, HDR10+ support and enhanced brightness. It also comes with Fire TV built in, along with Wi-Fi 6 and Alexa voice controls, so you have everything you need to find something to watch.
