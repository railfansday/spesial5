Why Alphabet Stock Is Down Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It's never cost more to insure Alphabet's debt.

By Johnny Rice – Nov 25, 2026 at 1:55PM EST

Alphabet Inc. (GOOG -2.02%) (GOOGL -2.13%) stock is down 2.5% as of 1:55 p.m. ET on Wednesday, Nov. 25, after the cost of insuring its debt hit a record high.

The S&P 500 and the Nasdaq Composite are down 0.1% and 0.7%, respectively.

Alphabet's five-year credit default swap (CDS) spreads -- the price of insurance against it defaulting on its bonds -- hit a record high alongside spreads for SpaceX (SPCX +1.30%), Oracle (ORCL -5.13%), and neocloud CoreWeave (CRWV -0.08%).

Obviously, Alphabet's protection costs are small compared to those for highly leveraged players like CoreWeave -- that will run you more than $131,000 a year for every $1 million you want insured -- but it's the direction that matters. Investors are clearly worried about whether the company will see a healthy return on all of its enormous AI investments.

New inflation data showing the Federal Reserve's preferred metric is still running hot at 3.8% year over year -- about twice the rate it's looking for -- isn't helping matters. Investors expect borrowing costs to rise.

Capital spending jumped to $51.4 billion in the third quarter, which pushed free cash flow -- the cash left from the core business and all those investments -- to negative $9.1 billion. The company has been borrowing relatively heavily to fund the gap. I'm skeptical these bets will pay off.
