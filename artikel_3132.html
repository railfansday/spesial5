Robert Pattinson is staying booked and busy.

It’s safe to say Robert Pattinson is on a generational run, starring in The Odyssey, The Drama, Die My Love and Primetime just this year alone. Having previously played Edward Cullen in The Twilight Saga and Bruce Wayne in Matt Reeves’ The Batman (and gearing up for a role in Dune: Part Three this December), he’s no stranger to major film franchises.

Now he’s adding one more major role to his portfolio: James Bond.

Amazon MGM Studios named the English actor the new 007 on Monday, ending the hunt to replace Daniel Craig, who played Bond from 2006 to 2021 in five films: Casino Royale, Quantum of Solace, Skyfall, Spectre and No Time to Die.

Several other British A-list actors were speculated to be in the running for the role, including Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O’Connor, but it seems Amazon MGM Studios is banking on a big-name actor with a proven record of box-office success for its lead. Pattinson’s connection to director Denis Villeneuve, who was also director of the Dune trilogy, likely played a role in the decision, too.

“To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement,” Pattinson said in a statement. “I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”

Pattinson is the eighth actor to play James Bond on screen. The full list of actors who have portrayed 007 includes Sean Connery (1962-1983), David Niven for one (unofficial) film in 1967, George Lazenby for one film in 1969, Roger Moore (1973-1985), Timothy Dalton (1987-1989), Pierce Brosnan (1995-2002) and Craig (2006-2021).

The next Bond film has not yet been titled and is not expected to begin filming until 2027, with a 2028 release date. Steven Knight (Peaky Blinders) has been brought on as the screenwriter, with Amy Pascal (Project Hail Mary) and David Heyman (Harry Potter series) producing and Tanya Lapointe (Dune trilogy) executive producing.

Amazon formally closed its acquisition of MGM Studios in 2022 for $8.45 billion, giving it the rights to distribute all James Bond films. In 2025, Amazon MGM Studios announced a joint venture with longtime franchise producers Michael G. Wilson and Barbara Broccoli to remain co-owners of the franchise, while ultimately giving Amazon MGM Studios creative control.

“Few films carry the kind of anticipation that comes with James Bond, and we have strived to honor the passionate fans, the legacy and the future of this iconic character,” said Courtenay Valenti, head of film at Amazon MGM Studios. “We are thrilled to officially welcome Robert Pattinson as our 007 and are confident he will bring something truly special to this role. Robert, Denis, Amy, David and Tanya are embarking on an amazing adventure together.”
