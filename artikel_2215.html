We asked supercomputer to predict how long Dave Challinor lasts at Sheffield Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Sheffield Wednesday will be hoping they can leapfrog up the League One table now their new manager is in place.

After parting ways with Henrik Pedersen just over a week ago, the Owls have moved swiftly to bring Dave Challinor to Hillsborough, who comes off the back of a glittering spell at Stockport County, taking the Hatters from the National League into the third tier in just five seasons.

The 51-year-old will be keen to have further success in the Steel City, taking on massive expectations and pressure that comes with leading such a huge club in English football.

Wednesday currently sit ninth in the League One standings following an inconsistent opening 13 league games, and the immediate challenge for Challinor will be to get his side winning games on a regular basis - something expected of the club when they possess the likes of Barry Bannan at this level.

With such immediate pressure to perform and turn things around, Challinor’s time with the Owls could go one of two ways.

It could either be a long and prosperous partnership, or his tenure could go up in smoke within a few months.

So, with this in mind, FLW has asked AI to predict how long Challinor will last as Wednesday boss.

It had been reported previously by journalist Alan Nixon that one of the only jobs Dave Challinor would venture back down into League One for was at Sheffield Wednesday, with the club then moving quickly to secure his services.

Challinor was announced as the new boss on the 3rd November, penning a three-and-a-half-year deal through to the summer of 2030.

Ali Al-Hamadi has reflected on his start to life at Sheffield Wednesday on social media, with his team-mate Barry Bannan reacting to the post.

And speaking in his first interview as the new boss, he reiterated his desire to win and getting the club moving on an upwards trajectory again, insisting the Owls have an “amazing squad.”

Speaking to BBC Radio Sheffield, Challinor said: "The aim is to win. The aim is to move forward.

"It's an amazing squad. We're feeling a little bit of pain at the moment with injuries.

"This is a new group, there was 17 or 18 new players in the summer.

"You go through processes of how that works and comes together, and you throw in the adversity of not being able to name the same squad consecutively because of injuries and suspensions.

"We know we need our best players back and that will hopefully not be too far away."

Challinor’s first game as Wednesday boss is against fourth tier outfit Oldham Athletic in the first round of the FA Cup, while they host Plymouth Argyle in his opening league game in charge on Saturday 14th November.

When asking AI to give their prediction of how long Dave Challinor will be Sheffield Wednesday boss for, it has predicted that he will depart the club in the summer of 2029, meaning he will last around two years and eight months in the role.

AI believes one of the biggest reasons Challinor could enjoy a lengthy spell at Hillsborough is his relationship with sporting director Simon Wilson, who have worked together at Stockport County.

A previous working relationship means they already understand each other’s footballing philosophies and expectations, while the communication should be considerably easier to help sign the types of players Challinor wants to recruit in order to implement his ideas as quickly as possible.

Meanwhile, his impressive promotion-winning pedigree should buy him some considerable patience from those at the top of the club.

After ending Stockport’s lengthy exodus from the Football League, he then achieved promotion with the Hatters into League One, demonstrating his ability to deliver sustained progress, which in turn should give the supporters and hierarchy confidence that he is capable of delivering similar success in Yorkshire.

The third and final reason Challinor stays in the job for that length of time is the club's need for stability, particularly when Wednesday have had to keep chopping and changing managers in recent years.

Wednesday need to establish a clear footballing identity and long-term strategy, so keeping Challinor in the role who has had such rip-roaring success in the EFL can only bring about positive change.

However, AI has also detailed reasons why Challinor won’t last beyond the summer of 2029.

It has detailed how such big expectations at Hillsborough could eventually catch up with him.

AI notes that despite Challinor experiencing managing ambitious clubs, the scrutiny that comes with managing Wednesday is on another level.

If he can’t deliver progress that sees the Owls be more than just an established Championship outfit, then patience could eventually diminish, leading to Challinor’s exit.

Meanwhile, there is a fear that Challinor won’t get everything he wants in the squad when it comes to recruitment, and if there’s a period of time deeper into his tenure where results turn for the worse, then supporters may not be as forgiving as they would be in the early days of his stint and may begin to voice their dismay.

Overall, AI believes that Challinor will fall short in seeing out his three-and-a-half-year contract, predicting Wednesday will part ways with the 51-year-old in the summer of 2029 to move into a different direction.

Dave Challinor appeared to be the standout candidate to take over at Sheffield Wednesday, and most connected with the club will be delighted that he’s decided to take the job at Hillsborough.

His job at Stockport County was seriously impressive and cannot be understated, with Challinor winning 142 of his 263 games in charge at Edgeley Park and overseeing a fantastic rise from the National League to League One.

The Owls certainly have every chance of success under his guidance, with the 51-year-old having a proven track record of improving players and getting his teams consistently finishing towards the top end of a league table.

Paired with his strong relationship with Simon Wilson, it appears that everyone at the club is now on the same page, with Challinor having all the confidence that the club can help build a team that he wants to build and can get every drop out of.

Such a rising star in the managerial game, Wednesday could well have picked up a gem in Challinor, who has all the capabilities of turning the tide and getting Wednesday back fighting towards the automatic promotion places.

This next period is one all Wednesday fans should be looking forward to, with the club backed to start putting in consistent performances and earning positive results in the coming weeks and months.
