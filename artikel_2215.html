Aberdeen 'set' to sack Stephen Robinson as axe looms at Pittodrie

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Aberdeen look set to sack boss Stephen Robinson within the next 24 hours.

The 51-year-old is on the brink just eight months into his Pittodrie reign following a dismal start to the campaign.

Saturday’s bruising 4-0 defeat to Hibs has piled further pressure on Robinson, with the Dons languishing in 11th place in the Premiership after managing just three league wins all season.

According to the Scottish Sun, Dons supremo Dave Cormack is poised to convene a crunch board meeting, with Robinson facing the axe barring a dramatic late reprieve.

Robinson replaced Jimmy Thelin in March and worked alongside sporting director Lutz Pfannenstiel during a summer of major change at Pittodrie.

Furious Aberdeen fans turned on the manager with chants of “sacked in the morning” following the Hibs hammering, and Cormack now looks set to give them their wish.

Robinson was made to sweat yesterday as the Dons board stopped short of making a swift decision on his future.

But barring an unlikely last-gasp reprieve, new chief executive Adrian Bevington looks set to be handed the task of identifying the club’s next boss over the international break.

Robinson is braced for bad news but remained defiant after the heavy loss.

He said: “I’ve got belief in my ability. You don’t have 500 games as a manager if you don’t.

“It’s a difficult situation, difficult at the moment for myself, the players and the club.

“But I will keep fighting until I’m told otherwise.

“There’s no hiding away from it. It was an unacceptable performance and an unacceptable result.

“We have to get players that want to fight and try to get us out of this situation.

“Ultimately, the blame will fall on me no matter what, and deservedly so at times.

“I’ll just keep fighting and working as hard as I possibly can.”
