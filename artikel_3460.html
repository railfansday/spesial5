'Underworld' Star's New Psychological Thriller Unleashes Chilling First Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Like every year, the 2026 Toronto International Film Festival was stacked with exciting features either making their world premiere or debuting in front of a new audience. The big winner that had everyone talking was undoubtedly Javier Ambrossi and Javier Calvo's acclaimed drama La Bola Negra, which continued its powerhouse festival run by winning the People's Choice Award. It's expected to be a force in the Best Picture race next year, though it was far from the only standout, with Simon Stone's powerful Ian Charleson biopic Elsinore earning International People's Choice honors, and Wild Horse Nine, The Furious, and more building buzz. However, at the titular fest's market, Kate Beckinsale's latest thriller also found a buyer who'll be bringing it to viewers very soon.

In less than one month, Buffalo 8 Distribution will unleash The Patient on digital platforms, with the Underworld alum taking on one of her most sinister roles yet. Cruel Intentions star Ryan Phillippe leads the film as the titular amnesiac, who awakens partially paralyzed in the hospital after a horrific car accident. He has no memory of what transpired, though the doctors tell him his name is Dean Morris, which doesn't sit right. The man insists that he's actually a freelance nature photographer named Andrew Wright, though the strange occurrences only start there. Everything begins to spiral as the staff subjects him to a malevolent treatment plan and the truth of his identity begins to unravel, with Beckinsale's inscrutable Dr. Rose at the center of it all. Ahead of its release, Collider can exclusively share the official trailer, teasing the horrors that await the man in the less-than-above-board medical center.

The footage opens with Dr. Williams (David Cade) greeting "Mr. Morris" as if it's a run-of-the-mill doctor's appointment, though his stay appears to be anything but. As it turns out, he's been in a coma for a few days, and Dr. Rose is set to spearhead the cognitive reconstruction process. She comes off as a confident doctor with a twinge of venom in her words who is dedicated to getting the patient back on his feet as if nothing happened. However, flashes of his past, their sessions, and a voice telling the man they're "so sorry for what they did to you" indicate there's more than meets the eye. It all culminates in a furious attempt by the patient to escape, claiming that he remembers everything beyond the crash, and it's the doctors who are doing more harm to his head than the accident.

Mukunda Michael Dewil wrote, directed, and produced The Patient, reuniting with Philippe after the star led his previous feature, Prey, in 2024. His latest looks to be a mind-bending psychological thriller that will not only force the patient to question who to trust, but make the audience question who is telling the truth and who is trying to erase it. Phillippe, Beckinsale, and Cade are joined by Kelly Greyson and Michael Sirow to round out the main cast, while Robert Ogden Barnum, Michael Benaroya, Lucas Jarach, and Raja Collins served as producers with Dewil. In an official statement, Buffalo 8's Head of Distribution, Nikki Stier Justice, highlighted the starry main duo of Phillippe and Beckinsale as key to elevating their newest addition to greater, more pulse-pounding heights.

“The Patient immediately drew us in with its sharp premise and the way Mukunda sustains tension while constantly shifting what the audience believes. Ryan Phillippe and Kate Beckinsale bring tremendous intensity to the film, and we’re thrilled to partner with this accomplished team to bring it to audiences this December.”

The Patient arrives on digital and VOD platforms on December 4. A physical release is also coming at a later date. Check out the exclusive trailer in the player above.
