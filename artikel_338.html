Fraggle Rock: A Holiday Gift trailer spreads holiday cheer

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Fraggle Rock's 2026 holiday special just got its official trailer, and it's doing its best to spread the holiday spirit.

Fraggle Rock: A Holiday Gift sees the Fraggles name Wembley (voiced by Jordan Lockhart) as Spirit Captain, responsible for spreading holiday cheer throughout Fraggle Rock. Enthusiastically embracing his new role, Wembley leads Gobo (voiced by John Tartaglia), Mokey (voiced by Donna Kimball), Boober (voiced by Dave Goelz), and Red (voiced by Karen Prell) in sharing the holiday spirit. However, said spirit begins to fade when they mistakenly take Doc's (Lilli Cooper) beloved snow globe from her workshop.

"Wembley must find the courage to return it, learning that kindness, giving and helping someone in need are the true gifts of the holidays," reads Apple TV's official synopsis.

The musical special also features a performance by singer and Tony Award winner Nicole Scherzinger. Fraggle Rock: A Holiday Gift arrives Dec. 4 on Apple TV.
