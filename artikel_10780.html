Airline responds after passengers find bags covered in “doo-doo” after two-hour delay

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

American Airlines has responded after passengers found that their bags had been covered in a brown, liquid-like substance while waiting two hours for them.

When you get off a flight, there can be a sense of trepidation about what’s to come next. If you land at the wrong time, you’ll be stuck in a long security queue, meaning your bags might end up stuck on the reclaim belt or even taken somewhere else.

Well, for passengers on an American Airlines flight from Miami to Atlanta, they ended up having a two-hour wait for their bags after landing. Yet, that wasn’t the biggest issue.

As TikToker Derick McCallum, who had been aboard the plane, noted, the bags ended up coming out looking like they’d been covered in poop. “Underneath the flight, it flooded. The doo-doo water got all in the bottom,” McCallum said. “Now they’re bringing the bags out; there is doo-doo on the bags.”

The TikToker’s video has racked up more than 1.3 million views, as of writing, with plenty of viewers commenting with their dismay at the claims.

However, an American Airlines spokesperson confirmed that it wasn’t feces on the bags, but rather liquefied peanut butter.

“Unfortunately, a food item from a customer’s checked bag leaked during the flight, affecting several bags nearby,” a spokesperson told the New York Post. “Our team will work directly with the affected customers to make it right.”
