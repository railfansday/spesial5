Tracking snap counts and PFF grades from Arizona’s win over TCU

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Arizona pulled off some midnight magic in a 29-26 win over TCU. Pro Football Focus gave the Wildcats (7-2, 4-2 Big 12) an overall mark of 77.9. The offense was given an overall mark of 69.3 and the defense was given a 71.2 overall mark.

The best aspect of Arizona's performance was the run defense which received a 78.1 grading. The lowest overall grade given was 54.5 for Arizona's pass blocking.

Below are the snap counts and individual grades of all players who appeared on offense or defense against TCU:
