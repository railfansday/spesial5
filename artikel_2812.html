Cam Ward had the greatest garbage time touchdown in NFL history against Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Tennessee Titans' meeting with the Dallas Cowboys in Week 11 was always destined to be a shootout. The Cowboys and Titans have two of the worst defenses in the NFL. While Tennessee doesn't quite boast the offensive firepower that Dallas has right now, they are showing some signs of progress in quarterback Cam Ward's second season, and hope to get there someday.

The Cowboys blasted the Titans, 51-30, on Sunday in one of the highest scoring games of the season. Dallas QB Dak Prescott had most of the fun with four passing touchdowns and one rushing TD, but his young counterpart on the Titans may have had the single most impressive play of the game in garbage time.

The Titans were still trying to score despite the game being totally out of reach with less than a minute left. Tennessee went for it on 4th-and-8, ant that's when Ward made some magic happen. The former No. 1 overall pick rolled to his right, made multiple defenders miss, then launched a missile across to his body to the opposite corner of the end zone, where the ball found receiver Calvin Ridley Jr. This would have been the best touchdown pass of the year if it wasn't totally irrelevant. That's the type of pass you would expect to see from Caleb Williams or prime Patrick Mahomes. I'm not sure anyone else in the world is capable of magic like that outside of structure. Ward broke every rule for young quarterbacks on this play by throwing across his body into a crowd, but when you have a rocket launcher for an arm like he does, things can still work out just fine.

Ward's second season hasn't been all that good. There are still major questions on if he can be the Titans' quarterback of the future. Plays like this one give Tennessee at least a little hope for the future, though. Ward's natural talent continues to be off the charts. Let's see if he can ever put it all together when it actually counts.
