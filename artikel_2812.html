YouTube Gold: Cameron Boozers Highlight To Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

As we've noted before, Cameron Boozer is making a huge impression in the NBA. So far, his team, the Memphis Grizzlies, is 12-4, good for fourth place in the West.

Literally no one, with the possible exception of Boozer, expected this. Memphis was supposed to be in a rebuilding phase, and that was complicated by the health of Zach Edey, who was coming off an injury. A lot of people had questions about Boozer, Memphis's prize rookie: can he block shots? Is he quick enough to play in the NBA?

None of it really mattered, because Boozer has the ultimate basketball skill: he knows how to help his team win.

It might be on defense, or it might be a timely three. It might be a laser-focused pass.

Whatever his team needs, Boozer has always found a way to deliver.

This video of the best plays of his rookie season so far is pretty great. Lots of dunks, of course, but also passes well beyond his years.

The surest measure of his sensational season: no one is talking about former Grizzly Ja Morant anymore.
