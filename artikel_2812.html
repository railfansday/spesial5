Sixers Bell Ringer: Sixers barely avoid letdown in win over last-place Bucks

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Sixers Bell Ringer Season Standings:VJ Edgecombe – 4Jaylen Brown – 3.5Tyrese Maxey – 3.5Joel Embiid – 2LeBron James – 1Anfernee Simons – 1Dean Wade – 1

If you look up 'trap game' in the NBA encyclopedia, it would describe playing a 1:00pm Saturday matinee against an opponent entering play 1-15 on the season. The Sixers looked sluggish early on, trailing the Milwaukee Bucks for most of the first half. However, they then asserted control in the second half, climbing ahead by as many as 13 points for what appeared to be a comfortable, if not blowout win. Things turned in the final two minutes, though, with the Sixers' 10-point lead falling to as little as one. Tyler Herro, who exploded for a game-high 50 points, nearly had an overtime-forcing three-pointer, but he did not get it off before the buzzer. Philadelphia held on for the 132-129 win and are now 12-0 in clutch time contests. It was certainly not the most glorious of wins, but they all count as one. Now for Bell Ringer.

Joel Embiid: 26 points, 17 rebounds, 9 assists, 1 block, 1 turnover

It was not an exemplary shooting performance from Joel Embiid, who shot just 7-of-17 from the field. Every other part of his game was dynamite, however. The big man looked healthy as a very active participant on the glass, putting bodies on guys and hoovering every loose ball in sight. He had tied his season-high for rebounds midway through the third quarter and finished with a season-high 17 boards. Offensively, he made all the right reads from the high post, finding cutting teammates for a number of easy buckets and finishing one assist shy of the triple-double. Embiid began to get Myles Turner in foul trouble in the second half, altogether going 12-of-14 from the foul line, including some clutch makes in the final minute.

LeBron James: 21 points, 6 rebounds, 6 assists, 3 steals, 1 block, 1 turnover

Saturday wasn't a game that will go on a season highlight reel for LeBron, certainly not a career one. But he was such a steadying presence on both ends of the court. Defensively, he orchestrated the switches and created deflections to jumpstart a number of fast breaks. Down the stretch, it was once again James with the ball in his hands, making the right reads to manipulate the defense and ensure the Sixers got good looks at the basket. There are plenty of reasons the Sixers are 12-0 in clutch games this year, one being just plain old luck, but a big one is having one of the greatest players of all-time on the court who has seen everything a defense can throw at you.

Dominick Barlow: 12 points, 4 rebounds, 1 assist, 1 turnover

Barlow made his season debut after missing the early portion of the season with a back injury. He looked like he hadn't missed a beat, playing his role to perfection in cutting, crashing the offensive glass, and remaining active defensively. Barlow finished 5-of-6 from the field, including one three-pointer, and certainly looks like a guy who will benefit greatly from playing alongside LeBron, who always has his eyes open for someone flitting into open space around the basket. He and Jabari Walker formed a solid frontcourt pairing, with two guys who may not be traditional centers, but can guard multiple positions and keep the defense honest from the perimeter.
