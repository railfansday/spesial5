Bengals News: Embarrassing Trade Deadline for Cincinnati

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The 4 PM NFL trade deadline came and went on Tuesday, and the Cincinnati Bengals didn't make a single addition to their team.

They tried to send a sixth-round pick to the Eagles in exchange for edge rusher Arnold Ebiketie, a move most would have celebrated. At least at first.

They backed out, though, after they learned—likely on X from OverTheCap.com's Nick Korte and not even someone from inside their own building—they'd lose the third-round compensatory pick they earned in the '27 draft if the trade went through.

We learned the Bengals likely don't even have anyone on the staff that could foresee how trading for a guy who was signed as a compensatory free agent could affect their future. We also learned the defense that has shown flashes of really good and flashes of really bad is the one Joe Burrow and the offense is going to be tethered to for the entire season.
