Broncos are expected to revisit contract talks with CB Ja’Quan McMillian during the offseason

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

According to 9NEWS Denver's Mike Klis, the Denver Broncos are expected to revisit extension talks with slot corner Ja'Quan McMillian at the end of the season. Klis notes that McMillian remains on the Broncos' radar after they signed Riley Moss to a four-year extension today and engaged with his reps during the bye week, but all remaining talks will be on hold until the offseason.

Both Moss and McMillian entered the season as pending free agents, and the narrative was that the team would have to decide which of the two to sign to an extension. Now that Moss has signed his four-year extension, it would appear that McMillian might be the odd man out here.

I say this because the Broncos have former first-round pick Jahdae Barron on the roster, and he profiles best as a slot corner. He has played outside primarily when given playing time, but with McMillian healthy and performing as a top slot corner in the NFL, Barron hasn't played much at slot during his time in the NFL. It would make sense for the Broncos to pay Moss, which they did, let McMillian walk in free agency, and allow their first-round investment to take over at slot. Sounds great on paper; however, Barron has struggled a bit during his short career and likely would be a step back from McMillian in the slot. So, if they do go that route, I would anticipate the team adding some depth and potentially some competition for Barron this offseason.

We all know George Paton loves drafting corners, and I would not be shocked if he did it again this coming draft.

With that said, it should be a no-brainer to sign Ja'Quan McMillian to an extension. He is arguably the top slot corner in the NFL and has made multiple impact plays for the Broncos during his time as a starter, including his clutch interception of Bills quarterback Josh Allen in the Divisional Round of the playoffs. If you can find a way to retain a talent like that, you do it.

In the Broncos' defense, this current regime has not let many talented players leave. They have signed multiple key players to extensions, but they did let veteran pass rusher John Franklin-Myers leave via free agency last season. Will they do the same with McMillian this offseason?

My take is that he is likely gone. We all thought they had to pick one or the other, and now that Moss is signed, it appears that they have made their decision. Also, how often do they let a key free agent go unsigned heading into the offseason in which they become a free agent? I am sure they will have talks, but unless McMillian signs a team-friendly deal, I have a hard time seeing him re-signed at this point. The reality is that McMillian would be a top free agent on the market and would likely get himself a well-earned big-money, multi-year contract.

We also have to factor in responsible team building. You have signed Pat Surtain II and now Riley Moss to multi-year contract extensions and spent a first-round pick on Jahdae Barron. Signing McMillian to an extension, with Barron still on the roster, would be a lot of money tied up in one positional group and would continue to keep Barron buried on the depth chart. Now, you could argue he hasn't shown enough to be handed the job, which is very fair, but that's a decision the Broncos will have to make.

This will be a conversation and topic that we will revisit at the end of the season. For now, I think it is trending towards McMillian leaving, but I hope that I am wrong. Time will tell, and in the meantime, McMillian will continue to be a playmaking slot corner for the Broncos for the remainder of the season.
