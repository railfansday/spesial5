Star 2028 WR picks Oklahoma over Michigan, Oklahoma State

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Star 2028 WR picks Oklahoma over Michigan, Oklahoma State appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Oklahoma football pivoted to a physical ground based attack with Jason Witten calling the plays Sunday. But the Sooners added a future air attack weapon nearly 24 hours after beating Ole Miss. And OU beats out Oklahoma State and Michigan for local talent Kenyon Sweat in a College Football Recruiting win.

The state of Oklahoma's fifth-ranked 2028 prospect per Rivals shut his recruitment down Monday. Sweat confirmed his decision with Rivals' Sam Spiegelman.

"Oklahoma has been showing me a lot of love throughout my junior season, and that means a lot to me," Sweat said to Rivals. "Being able to stay home and represent my state is something special. I love the energy around the program and the coaching staff."

Sweat stars for Cache High and immediately became a Power Four conference talent. He stands at 6-foot-2, 175-pounds and has torched defenders with his mix of height and speed. While he's a three-star talent, schools like OU's rival the Cowboys and the Wolverines made a run. Sweat also got offered by 2025 Big 12 champions Texas Tech.

But he adds to a new recruiting surge going on in Norman. Oklahoma and Venables edged out Oregon for four-star linebacker Tylan Henderson out of Texas, who also landed offers from USC, Miami and Notre Dame. Henderson's decision came two days before Sweat's.

Furthermore, OU landed Kamieon Compton-Nero who's the state's top-ranked 2028 talent. The Owasso native handed the Sooners a four-star addition who turned down multiple SEC teams, including Ole Miss, on the recruiting trail.

So Oklahoma now owns eight verbal commits for '28, handing the Sooners the No. 1 spot nationally in recruiting rankings per 247Sports.
