Jordan Love and wife dress as blind refs for Halloween

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Jordan Love and Ronika Stone had jokes this spooky season.

The Packers quarterback and his wife dressed up as blind referees for Halloween, donning the classic black-and-white striped tops with sunglasses and canes. In the video posted to Stone's TikTok account, the pair bumped into each other and threw a penalty flag.

Stone captioned the video, "I'm jokin, immmmmm jokin."

Jordan Love of the Green Bay Packers reacts after a touchdown during a game against the Carolina Panthers in Green Bay, Wis., Thursday, Oct. 29, 2026. Getty Images

Fans seemed to enjoy the couple's trolling, while others were concerned that Love would be fined for his choice of costume.

Love's wife joked in the comments, "We're Footlocker employees."

Jordan Love and his wife dress as blind referees for Halloween. Credit: TikTok

The costumes are seemingly a callback to the Packers' troubles with NFL referees thus far this season, especially in a highly controversial 20-17 loss to the Lions in Week 7.

Love, who has been the most hit quarterback in the NFL through Week 8, has not gotten a single roughing the passer call in his favor this season.

It hasn't stopped Love from being one of the most productive quarterbacks in the NFL, though.

Love leads the league with 2,246 passing yards and is fourth with 16 touchdown passes.

Jordan Love and his wife wear blind referee costumes for Halloween. Credit: TikTok

His teammate, safety Xavier McKinney, has called out the refs directly.

"I don't know how the NFL's going to treat it, but I'm not a big fan of the refs right now," McKinney admitted. "I don't think it's great officiating, if I'm being completely honest. I think it's bulls—t a lot of the times."

Jordan Love of the Green Bay Packers stands with Ronika Stone before a game against the Philadelphia Eagles in Green Bay, Wis., Monday, Nov. 10, 2025. Getty Images

Love and Stone got married in June of 2025 after dating for five years. They welcomed their first child, a daughter, this past May. Stone's father, Ron, was an NFL guard for nine seasons.

Love and the Packers head to New England to face the Patriots on Sunday.
