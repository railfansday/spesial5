Important Jusuf Nurkic Injury Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Utah Jazz have had a dissapointing start to the season, but it looks like things might be changing for the better. According to the Utah Jazz, Jusuf Nurkic has been upgraded to questionable.

This is great news for the Utah Jazz because different elements of Nurkic's game should make life easier for the young core. Nurkic is a great screener, and the Jazz desperately need one. This season, the Jazz have the 6th-worst PPP (points per possession) at 0.84 when scoring off screens. They also have the 7th worst field goal percentage off screens at 35.9%. It's also worth noting that the Jazz are running the 8th-most off-screen trackable plays per game. That means the Jazz are running a lot of a play that hasn't worked well. Insert Nurkic and you should see this get better.

It doesn't stop there. Utah runs the fourth most dribble handoffs in the league while scoring the fourth-least PPP (Points Per Possession).

What does this mean? The Jazz are highly reliant on screens and dribble handoffs, and it has not gone well. Jusuf Nurkic is an ELITE screener. In 2025-26, can you guess who was the #1 player in the league in screen assists per game? You guessed it, Jusuf Nurkic.

We'll see if Nurkic comes back healthy and if he's moving well, but if he is, it's no understatement to say that this could turn the season around for Utah, considering the style of offense they run.

Now, it's just questionable and not probable, but for Utah to change the status is a good sign he will play.
