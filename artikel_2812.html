How the Denver Broncos can beat the Las Vegas Raiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Denver Broncos return to the gridiron this Sunday. It'll be an AFC West affair when the Broncos host their longtime rival the Las Vegas Raiders. At 7-2, the Denver is one of the top teams in the AFC. In order to have a shot at repeating as division champs, beating the Raiders is a must. Here are three ways they can make that happen.

This season, many fans across Broncos Country had optimism Denver's offense was going to finally take flight. They added Jaylen Waddle in a blockbuster trade and drafted Jonah Coleman to set up third-year quarterback Bo Nix for the best season of his young career.

Unfortunately, the Broncos' offense struggled in the first half of the season. The good news? Nix and his teammates have fared much better in the second half slate of games. Head Coach Sean Payton hinted on Colin Cowherd's show there could be some potential changes for the offense if things don't improve—particularly at the offensive line. With an extra week of preparation under their belt, it's time for the offense to start firing on all cylinders versus the Raiders.

For the most part, Las Vegas has really struggled defensively. Despite making numerous big-named additions in free agency, they are performing worse than they did in 2025. The Raiders sport one of the NFL's worst rushing defenses giving up over 150 yards per contest. With respect to pass defense, they are in the bottom 10 giving up over 250 yards per contest. To make matters worse, they just can't seem to get off the field on third down. Opposing teams are moving the chains over 45-percent in that situation.

From my perspective, this division rivalry matchup will be a big opportunity to see if the offense can finally get in sync. This is a game where I expect Denver to execute running the ball, throwing the ball, and producing a lot of points on the scoreboard. No more excuses—just take care of business.

Over the past few weeks, the Broncos have been banged up on the defensive side of the ball. For multiple contests, they've been without several key starters to their secondary. That has made things a bit tougher, but fortunately the unit has been clutch in big moments and are a big reason why the Broncos boast seven wins through the first nine games of the season.

This Sunday, Defensive Coordinator Vance Joseph and his players have an opportunity to come out of the bye week and make a statement. Why? They will be facing rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza, whose second career start will be against the Broncos.

Last Sunday, Mendoza struggled against the Seattle Seahawks in his NFL debut. He completed just 17 of 29 passes for 143 yards. Prior to Mendoza being quarterback with Kirk Cousins under center, the Raiders had allowed just 11 sacks on the season. Against the Seahawks, Mendoza was often hurried and flustered and was sacked six times.

Even though they have regressed compared to their historic sack rates in 2024 and 2025, the Broncos' pass rush has been formidable. Having their defensive trenches play at a high level will be a big part of a winning recipe. I'm confident that Joseph and company will be able to dial up the heat and cause Mendoza a lot of trouble on Sunday.

It's a theme we could use every week for these. However, there is no doubt the turnover battle will be important for the Broncos to win on Sunday. This season, Denver's defense has done quite well forcing turnovers. They have twelve takeaways alongside multiple stops on fourth down and several goal line stands.

While the Raiders' defense ranks lowly in many categories that I mentioned above, they've been opportunistic with turnovers. They had two against Seattle—both of them forced fumbles on Drew Lock. That brings their tally to sixteen on the season, which is one of the highest marks in the NFL.

Being ahead in the turnover margin would go a long way towards the Broncos earning their eighth victory of the season. Going up against a rookie quarterback gives them a shot at making some big plays. Will Denver's defense be able to rise to the occasion? I certainly believe they will.
