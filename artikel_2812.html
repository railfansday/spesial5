3 Liverpool players who should get new contracts soon — and 2 who shouldn't

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Virgil van Dijk and Alisson Becker after Liverpool vs Villarreal in the Champions League. -Credit:Ed Sykes/Sportsphoto/Allstar via Getty Images

Following Alisson Becker's impressive display against Crystal Palace, the goalkeeper spoke candidly about his contract situation. His "will", he said, is to remain at the club beyond the end of the season.

As it stands, Alisson's Liverpool contract is due to expire at that point. Having attracted transfer interest from Juventus during the summer, there was a prevailing belief that he might be tempted by a fresh challenge.

That doesn't appear to be the case, however: it couldn't be more apparent that the 34 year old and his family are content where they are.

"Yes, in my mind," Alisson told reporters at Selhurst Park when asked if he wants to extend his deal. "I'm focused on playing every game, but in my mind I'm happy to stay.

"You know, that depends on a lot of aspects and different situations. But my will is to stay. I'm still hungry for titles and for delivering good performances. I still want to play at the top level for as long as I can. So that's my thinking on that.", reports Liverpool.com.

Given that Alisson continues to perform at a high level, that he brings such vast experience, and that quality goalkeepers who meet Liverpool's demanding requirements are so difficult to find, extending his contract appears to be a straightforward decision.

However, including the Brazilian shot-stopper, five senior Liverpool players are currently set to see their contracts expire at the same time, meaning the club's decision-making process is far from over. Like Alisson, Virgil van Dijk is well into his 30s and now in the latter stages of his career, but the Liverpool captain falls into the same category.

He continues to perform at a high level and remains a significant presence in the locker room.

Van Dijk is currently helping mentor Jeremy Jacquet, and with Giovanni Leoni having been sidelined through injury for such an extended period, there isn't as clear a candidate to assume his minutes as there might have been had his transfer proceeded as planned.

Retaining Van Dijk, should he be open to signing a one-year extension, would seem to be a sensible decision.

Meanwhile, another defender in Joe Gomez is scheduled to be out of contract next summer. The long-serving, versatile star hasn't been a regular for quite some time, but his importance is evident too.

Beyond being a homegrown player, he can fill any defensive position. Should he be content to stay as a squad option, it would be the correct decision to retain him.

Kostas Tsimikas, however, represents a different scenario. Having returned from a loan spell at AS Roma, he has delivered inconsistent performances to date, and it would be beneficial to have a superior player competing with Milos Kerkez for minutes.

Even more clearly, Wataru Endo, who has yet to feature for a single minute under Andoni Iraola, is expected to depart as well. The Japanese midfielder, who turns 34 in February, has never provided the type of in-possession quality needed.

Beyond those five, there are decisions to be made on the likes of Alexis Mac Allister as well. The others, however, are already secured for longer periods.

Most urgent are decisions on Alisson and Van Dijk.
