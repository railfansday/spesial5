Get the BankAmericard® Credit Card if you want 0% for 21 billing cycles to pay off a big purchase or another bank’s card balance. It has no annual fee, and paying late won’t trigger a penalty rate. If you pay in full every month, pick a no-annual-fee cash back card instead, since this one earns nothing.

The BankAmericard earns no rewards, so its value is the interest it saves while you pay down a balance or a big purchase. On $5,000 moved from a card charging 22%, the 5% transfer fee comes to $250, less than three months of interest on the old card at about $92 a month.

The BankAmericard suits anyone who can clear the balance before the 0% ends, since whatever is left then is charged a variable rate of 15.24% to 26.24%. Cardholders who pay in full every month gain nothing from the 0% and should pick a no-annual-fee cash back credit card, while business owners should look at Bank of America’s Platinum Plus business card.
