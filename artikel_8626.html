Choose a travel rewards card whose annual fee your trips will cover. For a few trips a year, get the Chase Sapphire Preferred, whose $100 Chase Travel hotel credit covers its $95 fee. Move up to a premium card only for credits you’ll use, or take an airline or hotel card if you’re loyal to one.

Annual fees on these picks run from $0 for the U.S. Bank Altitude Connect, Capital One VentureOne, and Bilt Blue to $895 for the personal and business Platinum Cards from American Express. Each card below sits under the kind of traveler it serves best, with what it takes to cover its fee.

Most of them earn flexible points, which you can move to several airline and hotel programs or spend on trips booked through the card company’s own travel site. None adds a foreign transaction fee, the surcharge some cards put on purchases made abroad. If a card for trips abroad is all you need, several credit cards with no foreign transaction fees have no annual fee either. Each pick is judged on what it returns every year, not on a sign-up bonus that pays once. Several also cover the fee for Global Entry or TSA PreCheck, which shorten airport lines, but only once every four years.
