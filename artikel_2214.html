Furious Redknapp demands ex Rangers transfer chief is axed for transfer blunders

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Former Rangers technical director Nils Koppen should have followed Nuno Espirito Santo out the door, according to Harry Redknapp, who claims incoming West Ham boss Scott Parker is inheriting the club's worst squad in more than 65 years.

The London club axed Nuno after a disappointing start to their Championship campaign, with Parker set to be unveiled as his successor on Wednesday. But Redknapp believes attention should also turn to former Rangers recruitment chief Koppen, who oversaw a major squad overhaul and is now facing questions over the quality of the players brought to the club.

And that includes Arne Engels, the former Celtic star was signed by the Hammers for £22m but has yet to fully convince.

Parker faces an immediate test with away trips to Cardiff City and Blackburn Rovers before the international break.

However, Redknapp reckons the former Fulham and Burnley boss is walking into an unenviable situation.

The 79-year-old, who joined West Ham as a 14-year-old before later managing the club, delivered a damning verdict on the current squad and insists those responsible for recruitment must be held accountable.

Speaking to talkSPORT, Redknapp said: "Scott's got a great track record of getting teams up from the Championship. He knows the club, he was a very, very good player for them and I took him from West Ham to Tottenham.

"He's a very astute manager and I think it's a great fit for West Ham.

"I think the first thing he needs to do, though, is have a look at this West Ham team. Honestly, looking back over all those years, this is probably the worst group of players I've seen at West Ham in that time.

"So he hasn't got a very easy job on his hands."

Redknapp then turned his attention to the club's transfer business.

"The manager gets the sack, but who is bringing the players in?" he said.

"I don't know Nuno, but one after another managers come in and go. If I owned that football club, I'd be looking at the people responsible for recruitment.

"Who brought these players in? Who spent all that money on bang-average players?

"Whoever is responsible for that should be gone with Nuno, in my opinion."

Redknapp believes Parker's chances of guiding West Ham back to the Premier League will hinge on improving recruitment.

"Recruitment is all-important," he added.

"You can only work with the tools you're given. Back in the day, managers signed their own players and if they were no good, you took the blame. If they were good, you took the credit.

"You need players who can play if you want to play good football. West Ham was called The Academy because they had incredible players.

"It's a fantastic football club that needs resurrecting. If Scott's the man, hopefully he can give them that lift. But I'd be looking very carefully at whoever is bringing the players in."
