Jemima Goldsmith, Russell Brand’s Ex-Partner, Accuses Him of Abuse

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Ms. Goldsmith, a British TV producer, accused Mr. Brand of “violence” and “coercive control” during their relationship. She made the allegations publicly for the first time after his sentencing for rape and sexual assault.

Jemima Goldsmith, the British television producer, said on Monday that Russell Brand had been violent and abusive during their relationship more than a decade ago and that the police had removed him from her home during an episode in 2014.

Ms. Goldsmith, a well-known figure in Britain, made the statements in a social media post shortly after Mr. Brand was sentenced to 10 and a half years in prison for rape and sexual assault. It was the first time that she had made the allegations against Mr. Brand publicly.

Six women testified at Mr. Brand’s trial in London about episodes that they said occurred from 1999 to 2009, as he was gaining fame as a comedian and actor. The presiding judge in the trial said that the women’s testimony showed Mr. Brand’s “staggering arrogance” and his belief that “fame meant you could act how you liked, when you liked, unhindered by the rules by which decent people live.”

Ms. Goldsmith, 52, who is also known professionally as Jemima Khan, dated Mr. Brand for about a year before their relationship ended in 2014. She was previously married to Imran Khan, the former cricket star and prime minister of Pakistan.

“I have had my own personal experience of his violence, coercive control and abuse,” Ms. Goldsmith said of Mr. Brand, without elaborating. She added that she chose not to press charges or go public after the 2014 altercation because she worried how it would affect her children.

“I have neither seen nor spoken to Brand since that period in 2014,” she continued. “Whilst my pain in relation to this, pales in comparison to what the women who have testified in court have suffered, this was a difficult period in my life which has had a lasting impact.”
