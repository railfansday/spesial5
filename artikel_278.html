Here’s Everything You Can Do With With the New Gemini AI on Android Auto

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Google Assistant is being replaced by Google’s more advanced Gemini AI across all platforms, and that includes Android Auto. If the switch hasn’t happened yet on your own vehicle’s dashboard, then it should happen soon.

You’ll see a message onscreen announcing the arrival of Gemini, with the new “spark” icon (a small star by a sound-wave-like icon), instead of the traditional microphone when the AI is active.

If you've used Gemini before on your phone, on the web, or somewhere else, then you're going to know the extras that it brings over Google Assistant. It's a truly natural conversation partner that can dig deep into information available online, and respond to a much wider range of queries. Here's what the upgraded app can do specifically when you're traveling on the road.

All of the standard commands you might have already been using are still here, of course, like "navigate home" or “Where's the nearest Walmart?” With Gemini though, there's much more flexibility in what you can say without the AI getting confused or misunderstanding what you're asking about.

For example, your questions can be multilayered. Something like "find the nearest coffee shop that has a drive-thru and is open now" will look for those exact criteria. You're also able to ask context-sensitive follow-up questions, such as "What time will it shut?" or "How long will it take to get there?" if you need further details.

You can ask more general questions too, such as "tell me more about the place I'm heading to" or "Is there outdoor seating?” But bear in mind that Gemini will be limited to scouring the data on Google Maps for its information. (It's a disembodied AI that's never been anywhere.) If it doesn't know the answer to something, it should tell you that.

Overall, it's smarter in every aspect. I was recently navigating a difficult junction and couldn't work out whether the map was sending me left or right. I asked Gemini, "Will I be traveling east or west after the next turn?" and it figured out the answer. That helped me avoid veering off in the wrong direction.

One of the major benefits of having a voice-controlled AI in the car is having it handle messaging and audio tasks without you needing to take your eyes off the road or fish around for a button. If you tell Gemini to “read my last message,” it'll speak it out for you and give you the opportunity to reply. You can also specify the app, otherwise Google Messages will be used by default—Google Chat and WhatsApp are among the others that are supported.

Gemini is able to give you summaries of group chats, which is really handy when you're on a drive and the conversations you're involved in are building up message after message that you can't check. Name a group chat in a supported app and ask for a summary, or just request summaries of unread messages. You'll be able to get the gist without checking each individual message.

The usual array of audio commands are here, carried over from Google Assistant, so you can quickly start or stop audio in your music, podcast, radio, and audiobook apps. As well as continuing from where you left off, you can also name artists, albums, and playlists that you want to queue up. YouTube Music and Spotify are among the apps supported, if you need to specify one.

Another extra from Gemini is being able to ask about the artist or song that's currently playing, and get more information on it. You might want to learn the history of a particular band, find out how long a certain song was at number one, or even get recommendations based on what's currently playing—just ask Gemini.

Much more than Google Assistant, Gemini can help you learn something or develop some ideas while you're behind the wheel. If you're on the way to a movie premiere, you can ask Gemini about the history of the film, the director, and the actors, and it'll remember the context of your conversation. (It'll all get synced back to the Gemini app on your other devices.)

Voice commands such as “help me prepare for my interview” or “come up with ideas for a quick and easy meal to make for dinner" will initiate a chat with Gemini that you can continue throughout your journey—and hopefully arrive well-prepared or with dinner sorted (or whatever it is). Maybe don’t ask about anything too mind-bending, as you’ll still need to concentrate on driving.

Gemini will ask along the way for any information it needs about what you’re interviewing for or the ingredients you’ve got at home. You can also ask Gemini to quiz you on a certain topic or run a query on something you see on the journey—like a landmark or a restaurant you go past.

Finally, there’s always the weather: Gemini will give you forecasts for the areas and specific locations you give it, on whatever day you want within the next week or so. Perhaps more useful for journeys are questions like “When is the rain going to stop?” or “What are the weather conditions like at my destination?” which will help you plan your trip.
