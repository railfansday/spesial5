He Scored 47 Goals for Crystal Palace and Southampton - Now He’s a London Taxi Driver

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

A former Chelsea, Southampton and Crystal Palace star who scored 119 league goals now leads a life far removed from the football pitch, working as a London black cab driver.

The limelight can make or break any sports star, and while many embrace it to become some of the greatest players in the beautiful game's history, plenty of former footballers have gone on to forge a very different path, swapping the glitz and glamour for what many would consider a more 'normal' life.

Take former Barcelona ace Jeremy Mathieu, who now works in a French sports shop, 1993 Ballon d'Or winner Roberto Baggio, who lives and works on an off-grid farm, or Arsenal flop Tomas Brolin, who now sells vacuum cleaners, as prime examples.

This particular player may not be quite as high-profile as those names, but he still shared a dressing room with some of the Premier League's biggest stars. However, following several run-ins with the law, his life has taken a rather different turn, with a far more stripped-back existence awaiting him away from the game.

Neil Shipperley started his career as youth player at Chelsea, making 37 first team appearances before moving to Southampton, Crystal Palace, Nottingham Forest, Barnsley, Wimbledon, Sheffield United and Brentford.

He also had a second stint at Palace as well as a six-game loan spell at Watford, and his total league tally upon his retirement in 2013, the then-38-year-old has scored 119 goals in 446 appearances.

After hanging up his boots, and having briefly tried his hand at management with non-league clubs like Walton Casuals and North Greenford United, Shipperley, now 52, started to study 'The Knowledge', which is needed to become a London black cab driver.

The greatest Crystal Palace players in football history have been named and ranked, with Ian Wright and Wilfried Zaha both featuring.

Some reports claim he struggled to get to grips with the theory behind driving a taxi, but Shipperley himself claimed in a 2010 interview with Inside Croydon that he enjoyed the fact it was the one job that you knew everything about before starting.

“It’s very hard, but it’s just something you’ve got to crack on with because it takes a lot of time. As long as you’re aware of that then there’s no rush,” Shipperley said.

“This is the only job I can think of where you actually know everything before you start. You literally do know everything. You see other drivers with sat-navs, but there’s no way of cheating this."

He added: “You’ve got to do it, it’s hard graft but it’s up to you what you do after that. If you’ve got a punter who wants to go 15 miles towards the M1, then you put your sat-nav on because the knowledge is only within a six-mile radius of Charing Cross.”

In December 2019, as per the BBC, Shipperley made the headlines when he was found guilty of exposing himself in front of a mother and her 16-year-old daughter whilst driving his van.

He was given a 12-month community order and ordered to pay a £90 victim surcharge along with £85 costs and £200 compensation. He also admitted that he had previously sought counselling for various personal issues including gambling debts and the loss of his father and that this incident was a cry for help.
