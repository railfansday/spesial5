Big Mistake: Newcastle Must Replace Jaissle With Nagelsmann ASAP

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Newcastle United's poor start to the Premier League season continued on Monday night as the team fell to a 1-0 defeat at home to Everton.

Thierno Barry's second half goal was the difference between the sides, as the Magpies suffered a fourth defeat in nine games so far this season, and slipped down to 13th in the table, just four points above the relegation zone.

The defeat means Newcastle have now lost four of their last five games in all competitions and five of their last seven, and the level of performance has seen many fans and members of the media question whether the decision to appoint Matthias Jaissle as Eddie Howe's replacement was the right one.

When Howe surprisingly resigned just two weeks before the season began, the Saudi owners moved quickly to bring the German into the job because of his attacking style of football.

The season started well, too, with a draw against Liverpool at Anfield and a win over Tottenham, but since then the style of football has looked unsustainable with this squad. Jaissle has struggled to make the team pick up their tasks properly; the new signings are largely struggling to make an immediate impact, and he doesn't seem to trust his entire squad.

After the defeat to Everton, Jaissle publicly switched the blame to his players too, accusing them of going against the game plan by playing long balls despite him wanting the exact opposite.

"There is big frustration & disappointment. We could not not transfer what we saw in the week, training in a high level. Since the start of the game, not much was seen. That is difficult to understand.

"We were not brave enough to break the line and play on the ground. We saw it in training, but there was almost nothing to see from these aspects in the game. In possession, this was not the level we wanted to see.

"If you just play long balls, it’s not the style Osula prefers & that was not the plan. We need to analyse this in depth, why we could not start the game how we planned. I’m responsible for that, to make it work.

"We have a clear identity of how we want to play. To bring the opposition in stressful situations high in the pitch. We spoke about creating 1-on-1 situations. The plan was not to shoot the long balls."

When the management and players begin to go head-to-head, things usually only end in one way, and that is with a change in the dugout.

But Newcastle don't need to wait for results to worsen, or for the situation to get to a point of no return to make a change. They can get ahead of the curve, admit that his appointment was a mistake, and make an ambitious move to replace him with someone who is undoubtedly better.

With Jaissle seemingly on his way out at St James' Park sooner rather than later, barring any big turnarounds, Newcastle should be assessing their options to replace him.

Eddie Howe is obviously not going to return having just left, but Julian Nagelsmann is a manager who is available immediately and could make a huge difference to the squad. He and Jaissle play a similar style of football having both come through the ranks in Germany, but Nagelsmann has got experience at the highest level.

Having been described as a 'genius', he has the authority and coaching ability to come in and make a change quickly, and the current squad is likely to suit him well. He also has plenty of experience working with younger players, which can see him develop the likes of Lewis Miley, Bazoumana Toure, Matias Fernandez-Pardo, and others.

With so many mangerial vacancies reportedly set to open, Newcastle don't want to miss out on someone who would be the perfect candidate for them, and this is a move the owners should be looking closely at doing quickly, or else they may regret it.
