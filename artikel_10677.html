Cinnamon Toast Crunch and Reese’s Puffs are getting Dunkin’ coffee-inspired flavors

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

General Mills is teaming up with Dunkin’ to introduce three new breakfast cereals inspired by the coffee chain’s popular drinks, bringing new flavors to Cinnamon Toast Crunch, Reese’s Puffs, and Golden Grahams.

The limited-edition collaboration combines some of General Mills’ most recognizable cereal brands with flavors inspired by Dunkin’s coffee menu, offering a new way to enjoy the taste of the chain’s beverages at breakfast.

The three cereals are scheduled to arrive in stores in December 2026, with one flavor available exclusively at Walmart.

The collaboration includes Cinnamon Toast Crunch Dunkin’ Cinnamon Caramel Latte, Reese’s Puffs Dunkin’ Peanut Butter Mocha, and Golden Grahams Dunkin’ Vanilla Graham Latte.

Each cereal takes inspiration from a different coffee flavor while retaining elements of its original General Mills counterpart.

Cinnamon Toast Crunch’s new variation combines its signature cinnamon flavor with caramel latte-inspired notes, while Reese’s Puffs pairs its chocolate and peanut butter cereal with a mocha-inspired flavor.

Golden Grahams rounds out the lineup with Vanilla Graham Latte, combining the cereal’s graham flavor with a vanilla latte-inspired twist. Unlike the other two varieties, this flavor will be sold exclusively at Walmart.

Despite being inspired by coffee drinks, none of the three cereals contain coffee or caffeine.

The partnership marks the first time General Mills and Dunkin’ have collaborated on a cereal lineup.

“We’re thrilled to collaborate with Dunkin’ to create a taste-forward breakfast experience with fun flavor profiles,” said Megan Brooks, Business Unit Director at General Mills. “Cinnamon Toast Crunch, Reese’s Puffs and Golden Grahams are beloved cereal brands, and we knew Dunkin’ was the right partner to bring together two morning favorites in one delicious bowl: cereal favorites and Dunkin’-inspired flavors.”

The Cinnamon Toast Crunch and Reese’s Puffs varieties will be available at retailers nationwide beginning in December, while the Golden Grahams version will be limited to Walmart stores.

The Dunkin’ collaboration is the latest unusual flavor release for Cinnamon Toast Crunch. In July 2025, General Mills partnered with Hormel to launch a limited-edition bacon-flavored version of the cereal, combining its signature cinnamon coating with the smoky flavor of Hormel Black Label bacon.

The bacon-inspired cereal was sold exclusively through Walmart’s website, following an earlier collaboration that saw Hormel release Cinnamon Toast Crunch-flavored bacon.
