West Ham Target January Move For Arsenal Wonderkid

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

West Ham United are reportedly preparing a January loan move for highly rated Arsenal youngster Ethan Nwaneri as Scott Parker looks to strengthen his squad for the second half of the Championship season.

According to an exclusive from Steve Kay at FootballTransfers, the Hammers have revived their interest in the 19-year-old attacking midfielder, having previously targeted him under Nuno Espírito Santo.

Nwaneri is currently on loan at Borussia Dortmund, having joined the German club on a season-long deal in September. However, the youngster has played just 29 minutes across Dortmund’s last seven matches, and Arsenal could consider recalling him before sending him elsewhere.

Having the ability to operate both centrally and on the right wing, Nwaneri could offer Parker some much-needed attacking options.

Ethan Nwaneri has been linked with a move to West Ham
