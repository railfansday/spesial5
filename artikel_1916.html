League One attendance table: Sheffield Wednesday, Huddersfield Town, Bradford City, Barnsley FC & more ranked

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

League One attendance table with Sheffield Wednesday, Huddersfield Town, Bradford City, Barnsley, Doncaster Rovers and more compared

It is already shaping up to be an eventful season in League One - and plenty of fanbases are being tested.

Sheffield Wednesday have new owners and a new sense of purpose, yet have work to do if they are to bounce straight back into the Championship. The Owls, now led by Dave Challinor following the dismissal of Henrik Pedersen, sit ninth in the table.

Above them in seventh are Bradford City, who are finding their feet under Michael Duff after Graham Alexander’s abrupt move to Preston North End. Even higher are Martin Drury’s Huddersfield Town, flying high at the top of the three after just one defeat across 14 league games.

It has been a tough season so far for fans of Doncaster Rovers and Barnsley, who both sit in the bottom half. Grant McCann’s Rovers occupy 20th, while Daniel Stendel’s Reds are languishing in 23rd.

Different fanbases are having contrasting experiences but all remain loyal in support of their clubs. But how do League One clubs compare when it comes to average home attendance?

Here are the average attendances of every League One club, ranked from lowest to highest, with data courtesy of Transfermarkt.
