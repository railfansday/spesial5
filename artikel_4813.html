New atlas unites nearly 3,500 laser surveys to map forests across 56 countries

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

An international team of researchers led by the University of Bristol in the U.K. has unveiled the Global Canopy Atlas—the largest integrated collection of high-resolution 3D maps of forests, woodlands and savannas ever assembled.
