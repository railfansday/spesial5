MilanVibes: Endrick’s entourage visit Milan ahead of planned January pursuit – the details

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Today, AC Milan have been given another significant boost in their pursuit of Real Madrid’s young star, Endrick.

Talk about a move for Endrick has dominated for months now. Gerry Cardinale is reportedly obsessed with the youngster and that is not changing. His admiration for the Brazilian goes so far that he’s ready to offer a substantial deal to sign him in January.

It will be dependent on Jose Mourinho’s decision, but Milan are doing more than knocking at this moment.

Now, according to MilanVibes, the Brazilian’s entourage have taken the next step, as it were. Ahead of the January mercato and any possible move, they’ve been in Milan in the past few days ‘for a visit’.

Whilst Mourinho’s say will be crucial to any departure, this trip shows a clear interest in a move to San Siro. Relationships were strong with Roc Nation already, thanks to Cardinale and a previous agreement between the parties. Now, though, it has gotten stronger with Brett Yormark’s arrival.

If reports from Brazil are to be believed, they’re in the lead and the Diavolo could well have the upper hand with the latest information that has filtered through. They’ve told Madrid they ‘will do everything’ to sign him in January; now it’s up to Mourinho.
