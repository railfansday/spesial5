Charles Hoskinson Signals a New Cardano Era as Midnight Takes Center Stage Amid 23 Million ADA Treasury Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Cardano founder Charles Hoskinson is signaling a strategic shift in priorities, moving from building the network’s infrastructure to developing products and driving adoption across its extensive ecosystem, with Midnight (NIGHT) at the epicenter.

On a recent podcast, Hoskinson stated, “Now I’m going from building Cardano to building on Cardano.”

As pointed out by Cardanians, an educational platform focused on Cardano, Hoskinson described Midnight as the vanguard of this new direction, calling it one of the ecosystem’s most exciting products alongside Midnight City.

More notably, the shift does not mean Cardano’s existing roadmap is being abandoned; rather, Hoskinson suggests the focus is increasingly moving toward delivering practical applications that are instrumental in real-world adoption.

Cardano’s treasury has received 23 million ADA following the return of funds originally allocated to Project Catalyst Fund 15 and Input Output’s Catalyst administration contract.

Cardanians acknowledged that the funds were no longer required since Catalyst administration responsibilities transitioned to the Cardano Foundation.

Their return, therefore, restores previously committed ADA to the treasury, making it available for future ecosystem initiatives and community-approved funding proposals.

This development comes days after the rejection of a proposal seeking 22 million ADA to purchase and tokenize copper, showing the governance community’s ability to vet project funding.

On the price action front, Cardano is nearing a potentially decisive technical level, with Market analyst Lana Valentis highlighting that the cryptocurrency is approaching the midline of a descending channel on the weekly timeframe.
