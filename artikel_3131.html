It might only be Tuesday, but the Black Friday deals have already arrived. And I've gotta say, I'm surprised at the headphone deals this year. I'm Pete, reviews editor here at Tom's Guide, and this is my fifth Black Friday working on the site. Normally, I have to concentrate to find legitimate deals through all the crappy ones — especially this early in the week.

This year, even well before the big day itself, I can't move for deals on many of the best headphones around. My favorite deals so far are on the Apple AirPods Max 2, which are reduced from $549 to $419 at Amazon; and the Sony WH-1000XM6, reduced from $459 to $348 at Amazon. I'm also a Bowers & Wilkins fan (and daily user), so it's nice to see the fantastic-sounding Px7 S3 reduced $100 to $379.

But there are plenty more deals where those came from.

Soundcore's ever-faithful (and always cheap) Q20i have dropped in price early ahead of Black Friday. They're comfortable, lightweight headphones and sound great for the price, while also offering ANC — what more could you ask for under forty bucks?

In our Sony WH-CH530 review, we gave these plucky little cans a full five stars. Why? Because look at that $69 MSRP. That's why. For the money, these cans offer stellar sound, plenty of comfort, solid battery life and fun colorways! And now they're just $38.

"We heard you like bass, bro, so we put a bass slider in your headphones to make them literally shake on your head. There's a vibration motor in there. Seriously, bro, they're so bassy. And $110 off"

Sound, ANC, comfort, styling. These cans have it all. In fact, there was only one thing we didn't like in our Sony WH-CH730N review, and that was the U.S. price of $199 — way higher than the EU and U.K. relative pricing. Well, that's changed with this deal. For $99, the WH-CH730N are a hell of a lot of headphones for the money.

How about a very colorful pair of headphones for a great price? We're big fans of the fit, finish and sound of the JBL Live 780NC, and this Black Friday deal makes them a great bet if you've got a little more to spend (but still want to maximise those savings).

The venerable Beats Studio Pro hit different when they're $120 off MSRP. Solid ANC? Check. A cool, stylish look? Check. Good sound quality? That's a royal flush, baby.

Bose might have replaced the QuietComfort headphones with (an also reduced pair), but at this price, these are headphones to go for. Excellent ANC for the price, a comfortable fit, and lovely physical controls. Not much more you could ask for.

Wanna look like Lobot, that pilot/butler dude from Cloud City in (the best) Star Wars? The Nothing Headphone (1) can help you live out that dream while offering solid customizable sound and a great control scheme. Not bad for a Black Friday deal.

The Momentum 4 have a reputation of being a little... boring. If, that is, you consider some of the best-sounding headphones for the price and 60 hours of battery life boring. I don't — and neither does this massive $120 saving on the Sennheiser Momentum 4. They do look boring, though. I'll give you that.

Sony recently relaunched the original XM4, hence the XM4C. The new cans have upgraded to the latest Bluetooth 6.0, a new removable (and therefore replaceable) battery, and some AI audio upscaling. Look, none of that was strictly necessary, as the XM4 were still fantastic anyway — even in 2026. But new features is never a bad thing.

Remember I said up top that Bose made new headphones to replace its more affordable model? This be they, and they're $20 more than the ones that everyone is buying. If you want some snazzier colors and a new design, you've found the cans for you — the Bose QuietComfort Headphones 2nd gen.

Sonos's first attempt at headphones was a solid one, earning a coveted 4.5 stars in our Sonos Ace review. We loved the sound and noise cancelling, the battery life, the design and the integration with other Sonos gear. If you're an existing Sonos user, this is the deal for you.

Oh, look, boring raises its head again. If you consider 57 hours of battery life and excell... oh, I've made that joke before? Er... just buy the Momentum 4, not the Momentum 5. They're $160 and technically offer more battery life. Now that's no joke.

Sony's plastic fantastic weigh very little and sound excellent — and now you can get them for $100 off. Not that you should. You see, I'm something of a deals clairvoyant, and I can foresee a deeper discount in these headphones' future. I can also see you buying these anyway because you want to bag that low price. I don't blame you — the WH-1000XM6 are great after all.

Pure. Class. That's how I'd describe the Bowers & Wilkins Px7 S3. And I'd know, because I own and use a set of them on a near daily basis at home. The Px7 S3 have a warmer, bassier tune than the reference-tuned Px8 S2 (also on sale), but you still get that Bowers refinement and fidelity. Oh, and they're built like you'd expect from a luxury audio brand: bloomin' marvellously.

This isn't the lowest we've seen the AirPods Max 2 drop ($399)... but it's damn well close. And given these are the latest, greatest, and still rather new'n'shiny flagships from Apple no less, $130 is a surprisingly good chunk of cash off.

Look, I'm a Bowers fanboy, I'll admit it. And I own the Px8 S2. So I have no problem giving some sage advice here: buy the Px8 S2, you won't regret it. Yes, they're pricey, but they make up for it with beautiful, spacious, high fidelity sound, exquisite build quality and strong ANC.
