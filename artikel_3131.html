Why Accel Entertainment Was a Huge Winner on Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Investors were clearly impressed by the gaming company's third-quarter performance.

Holding Accel Entertainment (ACEL +13.15%) in a stock portfolio was quite the accelerant for net worth on Wednesday. The company's share price popped by over 13% across the trading session, thanks to a strong quarterly earnings report that featured twin beats on analyst estimates.

Accel, which concentrates on providing electronic casino-type games and ATMs for venues like bars and restaurants, published its third-quarter figures just after market close on Tuesday. These showed that the company's revenue grew by 10% year over year to almost $364 million. Net income not under generally accepted accounting principles (non-GAAP, or adjusted) flew 12% higher to more than $18 million ($0.22 per share).

Those numbers were high enough to exceed the consensus analyst estimates. Prognosticators tracking Accel were modeling under $357 million for revenue, and $0.17 per share for adjusted net income.

Much of the growth came from simple volume. Accel's count of gaming machines deployed was 29,548, 7% higher year over year. Its tally for locations rose 6% to 4,705. The company said it experienced notable growth in markets such as Georgia, Nebraska, and Illinois, with the latter state's Fairmount Park Casino & Racing complex being a particularly strong performer.

In its earnings release, Accel referred to diversifying its business into other markets, which could be a spur to increased growth in future periods. The same could be said for the dramatic expansion of its gaming footprint in Fairmount. I like the company's business model, which is straightforward and reliably produces decent profitability and positive cash flow, so I think investors were smart to snap up the stock following earnings.
