Crescent Capital BDC (CCAP) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Buyback and new joint venture unveiled as watch list ticks higher.

Management reported that Crescent Capital BDC, Inc. (CCAP +12.34%) achieved stable net asset value while introducing multiple capital allocation initiatives focused on deleveraging and addressing its stock price discount. The company announced a $50 million share repurchase program and a separate $25 million purchase commitment from parent company Sun Life. To facilitate these actions and return to target leverage ranges, the company is forming a new $150 million joint venture to be seeded with performing first lien assets. Management indicated that despite subdued buyout volume in the broader market, the company maintained improved interest coverage across its diversified portfolio of 190 companies.

No new investments were placed on nonaccrual during the quarter.

Turning to the market. Sponsor-backed M&A remained subdued during the third quarter. Direct lending volumes supporting leveraged buyouts was down roughly 21% from the prior quarter and 60% from the third quarter of last year. While limited new buyout activity has moderated new platform financing opportunities and delayed some realizations, demand continues through add-on acquisitions, refinancings and other needs within existing sponsored portfolios. Add-on financings remain an important source of activity for Crescent. Our private credit platform includes more than 250 lower middle market portfolio companies, providing a foundation for originations beyond new LBO transactions. Over the last 12 months, add-on financings accounted for approximately 50% of Crescent's total deal count and 25% of total dollars committed.

Competition for attractive credit remains even as portions of the nontraded BDC market continue to face redemption pressure. In this environment, our long-standing sponsor relationships and familiarity with existing borrowers allow us to remain selective on credit quality, structure and pricing. Despite subdued new activity, the Crescent private credit platform remained active, committing approximately $1.5 billion of capital during the third quarter. As we emphasized last quarter, CCAP remains an important part of Crescent's private credit platform. The permanent fee reductions implemented earlier this year demonstrate Crescent's economic commitment to those objectives. And today, we're building on that commitment with several actions designed to drive NAV accretion by capturing the current discount in our stock.

First, our Board has authorized a $50 million share repurchase program for the purpose of repurchasing shares of our common stock in the open market at certain thresholds below our net asset value per share in accordance with applicable law. We believe our current share price materially undervalues CCAP, making repurchases at substantial discounts to NAV a compelling use of available capital. In addition to the CCAP repurchase program, Crescent's parent entity, Sun Life, will purchase up to an additional $25 million of shares in the open market at certain thresholds below our net asset value per share in accordance with applicable law.

This commitment underscores Sun Life's confidence in CCAP's long-term value and further strengthens the alignment of Sun Life, Crescent and the leadership team with CCAP's shareholders. The timing, manner, price and amount of any share repurchases under the CCAP repurchase program and the Sun Life purchase program will be determined by the company and Sun Life, respectively, in their respective discretion, based upon the evaluation of economic and market conditions, stock price, applicable legal and regulatory requirements and other factors. In order to accelerate share repurchase activity, we expect to announce the formation of an unconsolidated joint venture. The joint venture is expected to be seeded with approximately $150 million of performing first lien assets from CCAP.

CCAP expects to commit $30 million of equity to the joint venture, along with a $30 million expected equity contribution from the joint venture equity partner. In addition to the equity contributions, the joint venture is expected to be capitalized with an ABL facility. We are targeting a low to mid-teens ROE at the joint venture, which we expect to be accretive to our gross yield. The joint venture is expected to be completed during the fourth quarter of 2026. CCAP expects to use the proceeds from the JV to reduce leverage to our target range and fund share repurchases. Together, we believe these actions support our deleveraging objectives and create opportunities for per share accretion.

Finally, I'd like to highlight a recent announcement regarding Crescent's broader platform. On October 26, Sun Life announced GreenOak, a unified global brand, bringing together Crescent, BGO, InfraRed Capital Partners, SLC Fixed Income and Advisors Asset Management. The platform represents approximately $326 billion in assets under management across credit, real estate and infrastructure, supported by a global distribution network. The brand transition will take place over the next 12 months. On the fixed income side, GreenOak brings together Crescent's alternative credit expertise and SLC Fixed Income's investment-grade public and private credit capabilities, providing a broader range of investment solutions and opportunities for collaboration. For CCAP, our investment processes, investment committees and investment personnel remain unchanged.

We will continue to manage the portfolio with the same disciplined underwriting approach and focus on income generation and capital preservation, supported by the resources and reach of the broader GreenOak platform.
