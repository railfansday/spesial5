Zcash Just Dropped Below $1,000 for the First Time Since September. Is This a Buy-the-Dip Opportunity?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Most of its recent decline has been caused by a turbulent market, which probably won't be improving much in the very near term.

By Alex Carchidi – Nov 21, 2026 at 6:03AM EST

With Zcash (ZEC -2.20%) falling to $922 during its first big drop below $1,000 since September, it's natural for investors to wonder whether it's time to buy the dip, especially considering that the coin is still up 73% this year so far.

Zcash's recent decline from its highs is to be expected, given the bumps in the road it has faced.

For instance, on Oct. 29, hackers stole $6.3 billion from Binance, the largest crypto exchange, marking the largest-ever crypto theft and worsening the climate of fear that has been intensifying since a spate of other crypto hacks earlier in the year. Similarly, when October's Consumer Price Index (CPI) data came in above market forecasts, it sparked a flight from risky assets like crypto amid expectations that the Federal Reserve will hike interest rates at its next meeting in December. Separately, investors pulled $46 million from Grayscale's Zcash exchange-traded fund (ETF) in October, followed by another $15 million in early November.

None of those issues were caused by anything related to Zcash itself. Its main investment thesis, namely that investors will continue to seek to conceal their transactions and account balances from prying eyes, is still valid.

Now, let's turn to whether it makes sense to buy this coin right now.

Zcash just launched its latest network upgrade, NU7, which makes the chain significantly faster. It cuts the target block time, the gap between each batch of its confirmed transactions, from 75 seconds to 25 seconds. It also sequesters 60% of the network's transaction fees into a reserve that will disburse its funds to pay miners starting in around 2031. Those changes are well and good, but they don't imply any need to rush with a purchase.

Overall, I believe in Zcash's long-term investment potential, but the market is a bit too fragile for me to buy. A coin that lost over 41% in just eight weeks can do it again, and I do not want to be taking risks like that in the current environment of uncertainty.

But for those with a very high risk tolerance and a five-year-plus holding horizon, not to mention already having a diversified portfolio, buying a small amount of Zcash right now could still pay off.
