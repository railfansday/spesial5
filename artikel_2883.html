Winnacunnet's Ryder Thurlow voted Seacoast Football Player of the Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Voters in a Seacoastonline and Foster's poll chose Winnacunnet High School's Ryder Thurlow as the Seacoast High School Football Player of the Week, based on his performance in last week's playoff game against Bishop Guertin.

Thurlow won the online poll with 392 votes, nearly 57% of the total. Somersworth's Carter Lambert finished second with 218 votes and Marshwood's Greg Castoras finished third with 85 votes.

Thurlow ran for 209 yards on 22 carries and scored four touchdowns in Winnacunnet's 42-41 Division I semifinal loss to Bishop Guertin on Nov. 21.

"Ryder set lofty goals for himself last December, one of which was to move his position to running back," Winnacunnet head coach Ryan Francoeur said. "When you lift at 6 a.m. five days a week with consistency, good things happen. Ryder changed the game Friday night, he set the tone like he did all season long. He will be the example I tell about setting goals and working hard for years to come."
