Saquon Barkley returns to limited work Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Eagles running back Saquon Barkley returned to limited work Thursday after missing Wednesday’s practice with hamstring and back injuries.

Barkley practiced only once last week, getting limited work Friday, before playing 22 snaps on Sunday against the Commanders. He played 10 snaps the week before against the Cowboys.

The rest of the team’s report remained the same.

Wide receivers DeVonta Smith (hamstring) and Dontayvion Wicks (ankle) were out of practice again, with both on track to miss a third consecutive game.

Linebacker Jihaad Campbell (hamstring/quad), defensive back Michael Carter (abdomen), wide receiver Britain Covey (knee) and cornerback Jonathan Jones (hamstring) also didn’t practice Wednesday.

Kicker Jake Elliott (right ankle), outside linebacker Jonathan Greenard (ankle) and outside linebacker Jalyx Hunt (knee/ankle) were limited.

Linebacker Zack Baun (shoulder), offensive tackle Markel Bell (finger), defensive tackle Jordan Davis (thumb), offensive guard Landon Dickerson (hip), tight end Dallas Goedert (knee), quarterback Tanner McKee (chest), defensive back Kenny Moore (glute) and defensive tackle Moro Ojomo (neck) were full participants.
