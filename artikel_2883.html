Bears DT Jeffery Simmons confirms injury status ahead of Week 11 game

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Chicago Bears have a relatively short injury report heading into their Week 11 matchup against the New Orleans Saints, which is certainly a benefit of coming off a bye week. But there was one surprising name that popped up this week: Defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons.

Simmons was a surprise addition to the injury report with a knee injury, which had him limited on Tuesday (in lieu of Wednesday) and Thursday practices. Defensive coordinator Dennis Allen said he didn't anticipate Simmons' availability being impacted by this, and the All-Pro defensive tackle echoed his sentiment.

"I got 99 problems but this ain't one," Simmons said. "I'll be out there on Sunday."

The Bears acquired Simmons in a trade with the Tennessee Titans less than a month ago, and his impact on Chicago's defense has been dramatic, shoring up a key weakness on the defensive line and elevating the play of teammates around him.

In two games with the Bears, Simmons has totaled two sacks (one in each game), 10 tackles, including three tackles for loss, two QB hits and two pass breakups. His presence along the interior has elevated the pass rush and given others an opportunity to shine while Simmons still makes life difficult on opposing quarterbacks.
