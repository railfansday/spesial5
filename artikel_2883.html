James Talarico’s girlfriend Brianna Menard steals show at Texas game in ‘thrifted’ dress

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It was a bloodbath in Austin on Saturday evening, with the Texas Longhorns steamrolling the Arkansas Razorbacks, 45-3, at Darrell K. Royal–Texas Memorial Stadium. Perhaps Senator-elect James Talarico was the team's good luck charm.

The Longhorns rolled out the red carpet for Talarico before the game, fresh off his win in the Texas House of Representatives race, defeating Ken Paxton to flip the Senate seat to a Democrat for the first time since 1988.

Talarico was galavanting along the sideline and on the field before conducting the pregame coin toss with his girlfriend, Brianna Menard. While Talarico is a rising star in the political world, social media was buzzing when Menard popped up on the screen.

Menard understood the assignment with a burnt orange dress and some Longhorns cowgirl boots. Unlike some other pre-game fits you see from influencers and WAGs on the sideline, it seems like Menard is much more frugal.

According to the Austin American-Statesman, it was the first Longhorns game the Senator-elect attended this season. Considering he was busy on the campaign trail, it makes a lot of sense.

As for Menard's viral fit, it turns out that she "thrifted" the look instead of shelling out the big bucks. How do we know? Talarico's mother shared the note on X, reposting the photo above and adding, "I love this picture of Jimmy and Brianna! My favorite part is she thrifted that dress last week!"

Talarico and Menard first met in 2022 when she served in the Texas Legislature, including a brief stint as Talarico's chief of staff. After she transitioned out of his office in 2023, the two began dating. She is a University of Texas at Austin alum, so she was right at home rooting for the Longhorns.

Menard serves as Director of TEXPAC and Political Education at the Texas Medical Association.

Up next for the No. 5 Longhorns is the team's regular season finale on Friday, November 27, against the Texas A&M Aggies at Kyle Field in College Station.
