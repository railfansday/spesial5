9 Ways GTA 5 Will Live On With the Release of GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

He specializes in lists and guides, with a focus on RPGs, horror games, survival horror, Soulslikes, cozy/sim games, and action-adventure titles. It is finally time to say goodbye to Grand Theft Auto 5 as we open the doors for Grand Theft Auto 6. This doesn’t mean that we didn’t have some good times with GTA 5, but all good things must eventually come to an end.

Now that we are turning to the next chapter, we have to look back on GTA 5 with a fond smile and tearful eyes. Some of the best memories for people were made with that game. Communities came together, lawsuits were filed, and more.

GTA 5 had a massive impact on the world, and it is now time to reflect on the game before booting up GTA 6 and moving on.

There are multiple memes that were spawned thanks to GTA 5. For instance, Trevor kidnapping a guy’s wife will always live rent free in my head, but one of the biggest was the “Davey, how you doing,” meme.

The scene is simple; Michael just meets up with Davey at the observatory. Well, this sparked a massive collection of memes, and it was mostly thanks to the ability to initiate the cutscene in different ways.

People started reenacting the cutscene in real life, and doing absurd things in game like crashing a blimp into the observatory to initiate the cutscene. It will forever remain in people’s minds.

While yes, GTA 6 is now here, that doesn’t mean that GTA 5 is gone. While people may be leaving the story behind, the online mode still exists, and it is still one of the most popular online games that exist.

It is estimated that there are around 8.5 to 10 million active users in GTA Online each week, and they aren’t going to just abandon the online because of GTA 6, at least not while there isn’t a new GTA Online to flock to.

GTA 5 will continue to live on through the online mode for the next year until the next iteration of GTA Online is released. At that point, I may have to write another article.

An ex-Rockstar Games developer has confirmed why Grand Theft Auto 5 never received any single-player DLC.

Grand Theft Auto 5 is the first and only game in the GTA series to have three protagonists: Michael, Trevor, and Franklin. Many people were concerned about there being three protagonists because it might dilute each character.

To everyone’s surprise, it didn’t dilute any of the characters, and it instead enhanced them. The characters work off each other, and it helped build the narrative.

Despite it being 13 years, there are people who still remember quotes from the game. People can describe full scenes and story beats. It is pretty amazing. That type of dedication never goes away. It’s similar to the adults who can still quote early episodes of SpongeBob.

Can we take a moment to reflect on how many copies of GTA 5 were sold worldwide? The game sold approximately 235 million copies within the last 13 years. This is one of the most sold games in the history of gaming.

That type of legacy doesn’t just go away. Only a few games have sold more copies than GTA 5, and they include Minecraft and Tetris. That is a very impressive feat.

Up until this year, kids have begged their parents to purchase them GTA 5, and adults have caved in to see what the hype was all about. With it being as big as it was, GTA 5 will never be forgotten. It will go down in gaming history and that is a fact.

GTA 5's lifetime sales are crazier than expected, and lead us to wonder what the future will be for its successor.

GTA 5 isn’t going to be forgotten anytime soon, and if you want proof, look at the current sales. I don’t mean the sales numbers over the last 13 years, I mean the sales numbers from the last week or so.

Within the last quarter, it is estimated that Rockstar sold another 5 million copies of GTA 5. That is ridiculous when you consider that the game is 13 years old.

The hype around GTA 6 has been increasing sales for past GTA titles, but it has mostly had the effect of increasing the sales number of GTA 5, which means that people will still be playing the story for the next year while they wait for GTA Online 2.0 to be released.

Grand Theft Auto 5 was released during a time when people still got together to play games. While the main focus is connecting with people online, there are many who got together during the time of GTA 5 and played the game together in the same room.

Parties were formed, and people played the game together. They formed a bond, and that bond carried over into GTA Online, where people continued to play together and make memories.

These memories are still with those who played the game together and continue to enjoy the online. I played online with multiple friends and I will forever remember those times we shared before the next evolution of the online is released.

The Grand Theft Auto series is record-breaking, and there is no doubt about that. GTA 5 alone broke multiple records, and that is something that people won’t forget.

As far as records go, GTA 5 broke the record for highest-24 hour entertainment revenue, best-selling video game in 24 hours, best-selling action-adventure game in 24 hours, highest grossing video game in 24 hours, fastest game to reach $1billion in revenue, and more.

When the game launched, it was a huge deal, and it shows through the number of copies that were sold, and it shows even more through the number of records that were broken by it. In total, it broke at least eight records. I can’t think of another game that has done that, and now it is GTA 6’s turn.

Whenever a massive game comes out that parodies stuff in real life, it is bound to attract a few lawsuits, and boy was GTA 5 the center of quite a few lawsuits over the years. One of the earlier ones was from Daz Dillinger who issued a cease and desist letter.

After that, other celebrities like Lindsay Lohan and Karen Gravano filed lawsuits against Rockstar over GTA 5, with one being over an alleged likeness and the other being about a character who mirrored their life.

There was even once a lawsuit over GTA Online because it didn’t launch with the release of the game and was instead released some time later. While it isn’t the best look, this attention still attracts people to the game and it makes people remember the name.

Do you believe that violent video games make people more violent in real life? Many don’t believe so, but there is a sect of those who think there is a link between violent behavior and video games, especially when the game has carjackings, murder, and other criminal activities.

In 2021, GTA V was mentioned in law HB3531 filed in Illinois. The goal of this law was to prohibit the sale of games featuring “psychological harm.” Ultimately, it wanted to restrict the sale of games like GTA 5 that featured criminal activity, meaning GTA 5 would get banned.

This wasn’t the first time the series came under fire from lawmakers, and it probably won’t be the last, but it leaves a lasting impression that people still remember and will help GTA 5 live on.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is poised to be the best game of 2026 and the best game in the entire GTA series.
