Kendall Jenner Introduces Jacob Elordi to Her Other Love: The Row

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Kendall Jenner and Jacob Elordi reached a new relationship milestone when the model introduced her new beau to someone very important in her life. I’m talking, of course, about Jenner’s all-time favorite brand, The Row.

Jenner and Elordi took their off-duty style for a spin earlier this week in New York City, where the model stuck to her guns in an army green chore coat: the Frank jacket from—you guessed it—The Row. She paired the unisex outerwear with thigh-hugging bootcut Éterne jeans, an oversized red leather tote, a leopard print scarf, and a pair of slightly heeled brown leather boots (seemingly the Olsen-fronted brand’s Oscar model).

Kendall Jenner and Jacob Elodri in New York.

While Jenner stays true to her longtime love, it’s clear that she’s also making quite the impact on Elordi’s wardrobe, too. The actor wore a very Jennerian outfit: a white T-shirt with baggy low-rise indigo jeans (studying the texts, I see), which he styled with a pair of The Row’s ultra-slim lambskin Clement sneakers that are more jazz shoes than they are tennis. Elordi covered up his allegedly blonde hair with a black beanie and light gray hoodie, and finished off the look with slim black sunnies and his go-to crossbody bag. Some things never change!
