10+ Winter Looks to Recreate From Fashion’s Beloved Style Icons

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A little wardrobe inspiration is always welcome, especially when transitioning between seasons. As we move into winter, what better way to reinvigorate your wardrobe than by revisiting the style signifiers of some of fashion’s iconic muses? From Jacqueline Kennedy Onassis and Lee Radziwill to Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy, their sartorial signatures remain as relevant today as ever.

For holiday dressing inspiration, look no further than Bianca Jagger and Sade for bold ensembles to recreate. For timeless winter layering tips, pay close attention to Ali McGraw and Iman; they shared a love for versatile neutrals (and knew when to rely on a statement belt for added punch). Off-duty ideas come courtesy of Princess Diana and Jane Birkin, who both had an affinity for dressing up more casual pieces.

Winter is a season where artful layers and rich textures take center stage—notes these women knew how to hit. Their enduring looks, which we’re breaking down for you below, are proof.

Jackie O knew the power of a statement coat—here, she went for a knee-length, animal-print style with layered knits peeking out from underneath for contrast. It’s a timeless yet modern look that’s easy enough to piece together, just pair with a fitted pencil skirt and heeled ballerinas or loafers.

For folklore flair, style a velvet duster coat with a turtleneck, corduroy trousers, and statement belt. Flat leather boots and a slouchy beanie are the finishing touches.

Of course, Lee Radiziwill was an early proponent of the pankou closure jacket which you’re probably seeing everywhere this season. Recreate her look for an 8 p.m. dinner res at Le Veau D’or, with matching velvet trousers and elegant heeled mules.

In this enduring CBK look—one of most recognizable style references—a simple black V-neck meets a cream-colored skirt, patent brown boots, and no-fuss black leather handbag.

Chic yet comfy, this borrowed-from-the-boys look inspired by Princess Di off-duty ’80s style feels perfectly right for a lazy Sunday in the neighborhood.

A reason—or an excuse—to embrace winter whites. One of Jagger’s most iconic looks, this tonal ensemble is simple enough to put together. The key is to round out with accessories in complementary shades of cream and white.

How Parisians dress for a rainy day. We love this mini short and tights combo for milder winter weather.

A layered fall look built around soft neutrals that can easily go from day to night. Lift a page from Iman’s style book by adding a hat to the mix, in whatever shape best suits you.

This outfit from Audrey Hepburn on set for Sabrina might be one of the chicest of all time. Make it yours it a matching cinched-waist blazer and skirt, topped off with a hat, kitten heels, and door-knocker earrings. Terrier not included!

Between the cuffs, the leather gloves, and the oversized belt—here’s proof that all-black works best as a mix of textures.

Mexican artist Frida Kahlo loved a skirt; she was often photographed in a statement print or pattern. Recreate her look with a jacquard floral maxi, paired with a velvet top, lace gloves, and silver earrings.
