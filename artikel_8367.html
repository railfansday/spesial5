Personal Investments • TIPS OID for 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

With the release of the October 2026 CPI today, I've updated my TIPS Interest and OID web page through the end of 2026. It shows interest and OID for each $1,000 face value of the following 58 TIPS. (OID or Original Issue Discount is how the IRS refers to the increase in inflation-adjusted principal.)

  • 48 bonds outstanding for all of 2026. The OID will be 3.7%. This exceeds the 3.1% increase last year. [1]
  • 5 bonds outstanding from the beginning of 2026 until redemption in 2026.
  • 5 bonds outstanding from initial auction in 2026 until the end of 2026. [2]
I've also updated my TIPS Interest and OID Calculator Excel workbook. It allows you to enter your own TIPS holdings and calculate their interest and OID for any year 1997 to 2026. For TIPS you've sold or held to redemption, it also calculates the overall OID.
  1. For TIPS held during all of 2026 the OID equals the increase in inflation-adjusted principal from Jan 1, 2026 to Jan 1, 2027. Because of a 3 month lag, this corresponds to the 3.7% increase in the CPI from 325.604 in October 2025 to 337.511 in October 2026 as shown here.
  2. The last five rows of the web page ordered by issue date lists these purchases at the initial auction. For example it shows OID of $7.35 for $1,000 face value of the 2-3/8% July 2036 bought at the 7/23/202 initial auction and issued 7/31/2026. To determine the OID for ones bought at the 2nd or 3rd auction, consult the TIPS' History or After Market pages. For example, the July 2036 purchased at the 9/17/2026 auction two months ago and issued 9/30/2026 will have $10.75 OID per $1,000 of face value for 2026, as shown on its history page and aftermarket page.
