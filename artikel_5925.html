Engine failure forces Sen Ashley Moody’s American Airlines flight to divert for emergency landing

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

An American Airlines flight carrying Sen. Ashley Moody, R-Fla., was diverted to Orlando after a reported engine failure. (Eric Lee/Bloomberg via Getty Images)

An American Airlines flight carrying Sen. Ashley Moody, R-Fla., was diverted to Orlando after a reported engine failure, according to a spokesperson for the senator's office.

American Airlines Flight 496 was heading from Tampa to Washington, D.C., when it was forced to divert to Orlando.

Moody missed Senate votes on Monday night due to the incident, but the aircraft landed safely, her office said.

"Earlier today, the American Airlines flight she was on had engine failure and had to make an emergency landing in Orlando," the spokesperson said in a statement to Punchbowl News.

"She got on the next available flight to DC but unfortunately landed just after votes closed," the spokesperson continued.

Sen. Ashley Moody missed Senate votes on Monday night due to the incident, but the aircraft landed safely. (Tom Williams/CQ-Roll Call, Inc. via Getty Images)

The spokesperson added that Moody is "grateful to the pilots and crew for getting everyone on board on the ground safely."

It was not immediately clear if anyone was hurt in the incident.
