GTA 6’s Astonishing $4 Billion Day One Includes 32.5 Million Copies Sold

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

GTA 6 is an enormous hit--it's the biggest gaming release ever. Take-Two just announced that GTA 6 reach 32.5 million copies sold before release, with revenue reaching $3.79 billion through the end of the first day on sale on November 19. This makes it the biggest entertainment release--for any medium--in history, Take-Two said.

For comparison, 2013's GTA 5 hit $800 million in revenue in its first 24 hours and $1 billion after its first three days, so GTA 6 is pacing well ahead of those numbers. GTA 5 launched at $60, compared to $80 for the cheapest version of GTA 6.

For comparison, the all-time highest-grossing movie ever is Avengers: Endgame, which made $2.94 billion during its entire run. GTA 6 has done nearly $1 billion more in revenue already. According to reporter Jason Schreier, GTA 6 is also now instantly one of the top 30 best-selling games of all time.

“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games
with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” Rockstar president and co-founder Sam Houser said. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”

Big numbers for GTA 6 were always expected. Sensor Tower had been sharing unofficial sales data for GTA 6 for a long time already, but Take-Two's sales numbers are even higher than the 25 million sales that Sensor Tower reported at launch.

GTA 6 is only going to get bigger when it comes to sales as more and more people buy the game. Additionally, GTA 6 is expected to come to PC in 2027, which will be another selling cycle for the game. Beyond this, GTA 6 is expected to add an online mode in 2027 that will no doubt feature microtransactions and other ways for Rockstar to make money off the fanbase.

For more, check out GameSpot’s GTA 6 review and our rundown of the five best things to do first. And if you’re eager for news on GTA 7? Get comfortable, because Rockstar is not going to announce it anytime soon.
