Hollow Knight: Silksong DLC Sea Of Sorrow Releases Free For All Players And Adds Fishing

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

DLCHollow Knight: SilksongRelease DateSea of Sorrows

After an extended period of silence regarding its much anticipated expansion for Hollow Knight: Silksong, Team Cherry is back with a big update on its upcoming DLC, Sea of Sorrow. The new content drops on December 11, 2026. Even better, it’s free for all players.

The Sea of Sorrow launch trailer shows off what appear to be several new biomes and lots of other features, including what appears to be a new crest based on how Hornet is moving int he new gameplay footage. The announcement was part of a Team Cherry showcase that promoted three other very cool-looking indie games that the studio will be publishing called Radio Rain, Untangle: Plant Problems, Blokkan: Secret of the Walking City.

Here’s the full thing (the Sea of Sorrow reveal is in the last few minutes):

It looks like a pretty major expansion. Fans are ecstatic. And yes, it looks like there’s a new fishing minigame coming to the Soulslike Metroidvania. Team Cherry also called Sea of Sorrow Silksong‘s “first major expansion,” suggesting there could be others as well.

The Hollow Knight sequel launched in September 2025 and was everything fans were hoping for. It’s tough as nails but, like Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom‘s relationship to Breath of the Wild, feels like a step forward in all the right ways.

The post-GTA 6 hangover was leaving December looking thin for new game releases, so the launch date is a nice surprise. “It feels like Christmas came early and Sanata is here, but instead it’s Team Cherry that came down the chimney,” one player wrote on YouTube.Â
