6 Best Early Black Friday Bed Frame Deals for Hundreds Off (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Black Friday may still be a few weeks away, but some of our favorite furniture retailers are already rolling out deals, giving us an incentive to upgrade our bedroom setups sooner rather than later. Early Black Friday sales are delivering discounts on the bed frames of your dreams, from plush upholstered designs to sleek platform beds and clever storage solutions that might finally give that extra pile of bedding in your closet a proper home. Considering how much a new bed can cost, we’re more than happy to get a head start on the holiday shopping season.

A bed frame does more than hold your mattress. As the focal point of your bedroom, it sets the tone for the rest of your space, so it’s worth choosing one that looks good and holds up over time. The best designs combine thoughtful craftsmanship, sturdy construction, and a little personality. Practical features like built-in storage or a padded headboard for late-night reading are an added bonus. Ahead, we’ve rounded up some of the best early Black Friday bed frame deals worth now, including AD-tested favorites, statement-making picks, and plenty of reasons to finally give yourself the gift of a bedroom refresh.

Not every bedroom has room for nightstands, so West Elm’s Midcentury Storage Headboard Platform bed pulls double duty. Its clean lines, warm wood finish, and minimal silhouette offer an updated interpretation of classic mid-century design, but the real draw is the headboard, which conceals shelving behind sliding doors. It’s a clever place to stash books, charges, and all those other bedside essentials that need a home. During West Elm’s early holiday promotions, you can snag the goes-with-almost-everything bed frame for $300 off its original price.

Contributor Yelena Moroz Alpert tested the bed in her son’s bedroom, where limited floor space made adding another dresser or nightstand impractical. “The doors run on little tracks and are embedded with little magnets that give it smooth and soft closure,” she shared. “Inside, you have a cutout for any wires, and these are accessible to all storage options.” She also appreciated how the blonde wood finish complemented the room’s contemporary aesthetic. A caveat for taller sleepers: the low-profile platform may make getting in and out of bed a little less comfortable.

Castlery’s Dalton Storage Bed may very well be the key to keeping your bedroom clutter-free. Its understated silhouette conceals spacious under-bed storage, making good use of otherwise wasted square footage. And if light-colored upholstery stresses you out for fear of spills and stains, the Dalton’s removable and washable linen-blend cover should offer some peace of mind.

Castlery’s early holiday sale is currently offering up to 50% off selected items, with additional tiered savings of up to $950 depending on how much you spend. The early access sale gives you the chance to upgrade your bed frame and increase your storage space in one fell swoop.

If the bedroom of your dreams is oozing with personality, CB2’s Russell upholstered storage bed in dusty lilac velvet is a worthy addition. Its subtle sheen and dramatic shelter-style headboard pair with a generously padded frame for a bed that feels akin to a piece straight out of a luxury boutique hotel. A large drawer hidden in the footboard adds a practical touch without interrupting the bed’s elegant silhouette. After all, who says statement-making furniture can’t also be cleverly designed?

Alpert tested the Russell in her own bedroom and was immediately taken with its unusual color, which she likened to amethyst. “This bed is a gem,” she said. The substantial headboard creates a cozy nook for reading, and she found that the footboard glides out smoothly, with plenty of space for spare quilts and bedding. “Climbing into this bed makes me think that I am going on a sleepcation,” she shared.

Serena & Lily has a knack for making rooms feel like they’ve been plucked out of a beautifully appointed beach house, even when the nearest coastline is nowhere in sight. A nod to 17th-century bobbin furniture, the Webster Bed brings that same breezy sensibility into the bedroom with turned-wood detailing and an open, airy frame. The intricate spindlework lends the bed plenty of character, whether you dress it up with crisp white linens or colorful patterned bedding. For the full seaside treatment, style the rest of your bedroom with woven accents and natural textures. With Serena & Lily’s current sale offering 26% off, it’s a particularly tempting excuse to bring a little vacation energy home.

If your ideal bedroom leans more serene sanctuary than grand hotel suite, Article’s Tessu bed frame might be the perfect gift to yourself before the holidays hit. Its low-slung design and softly textured boucle upholstery offer a refreshing alternative to the towering headboards and ornate detailing. The foam-padded frame makes reading or working from the comfort of your bed more comfortable, and wooden slats eliminate the need for a box spring. Originally $1,199, it’s currently marked down to $799, making this cozy, Scandi-inspired piece even more appealing.

A little bit of Old Hollywood glamour meets modern-day luxury in the Siren Queen Bed, designed by actor Laura Harrier and interior designer Tiffany Howell for Crate and Barrel. Inspired by vintage furniture and richly layered interiors, the Siren brings a distinctly designer look to the bedroom without much additional decorating. Keep the surrounding furniture simple and let this modern take on the classic sleigh bed take center stage. Right now, Crate and Barrel is offering over $1,000 off this unique piece, plus 20% back in rewards.
