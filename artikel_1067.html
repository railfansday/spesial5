Wales bid to salvage some pride in the Nations League as they host Portugal in Cardiff.

Craig Bellamy’s team lost 3-1 to Norway on Saturday and will finish bottom of Group A4.

But they are safe from direct relegation and will instead enter the relegation/promotion play-offs in March regardless of tonight’s result.

Portugal are aiming to beat Denmark to top spot, and will be looking to bounce back from their shock 4-2 defeat to the Danes on Saturday.

Portugal were playing for the first time since the retirement of their record goalscorer and former captain Cristiano Ronaldo.

It will be shown live on BBC Two, BBC One Wales and S4C, with kick-off at 7:45pm GMT in Cardiff.

Wales will be without Neco Williams after the right-back picked up an injury in the defeat in Norway, while goalkeeper Karl Darlow is a doubt. Key midfielder Ethan Ampadu is suspended on Tuesday after picking up a second booking against Norway. Centre-back Ben Cabango and winger Sorba Thomas will be available after missing out in Oslo through suspension.

The draw will take place at 11am GMT on Friday 20 November.
