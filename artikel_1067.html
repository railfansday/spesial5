Real Madrid winger Vinicius Junior has spoken about the reasons behind his underwhelming start to the season.

The Brazilian signed a new contract until 2032 in August, but so far his output on the pitch has ⁠fallen short of his own previous standards.

Press scrutiny in Spain has also intensified by the impressive start of Ivorian signing Yan Diomande, who has eclipsed Vinicius Jr in his preferred left-sided role.

Vinicius Jr said his reduced training schedule and summer transfer speculation may have affected his performances in the first part of the campaign.

“I don’t think I prepared as well as I could have after the World Cup, because the impact of that tournament is so significant for all Brazilians,” he told a press conference in Singapore on Monday.

“Being knocked out so early was something we ⁠hadn’t expected. So, I took advantage of my holidays to enjoy myself, rest, ​relax, ⁠and spend more time with ‌my family. I ended up training less than in previous years,” he said.

“That may have affected my start to the season.”

Vinicius Jr's future at Real Madrid was ‌a talking point in Spain for months as ‌he entered the final year of his contract before re-signing in August.

Premier League champions Arsenal were said to be interested at the time.

“On top of that, regarding my contract renewal, with so many clubs showing interest last summer, I felt truly overwhelmed, as with the situation at Real Madrid,” he said. “But it’s a very long season. “

The 26-year-old said he had a track record of hitting top form in the decisive moments of the season, and that he was on a positive trajectory.

“Maintaining such a high level for so long is always very difficult,” said Vinicius Jr. “I think only ‌Cristiano Ronaldo and (Lionel) Messi have managed to do this for so ​many years, reaching the same kinds of numbers.”

“I am human, I often make mistakes, but I always try to improve.”

Vinicius Jr has only scored one goal and recorded three ⁠assists in his 12 Real Madrid appearances this season.

Coach Jose Mourinho has only used him for a ‌full 90 minutes on ​three occasions.

Nevertheless, he did score twice on Saturday in Brazil's 3-2 loss to Japan, and is likely to feature in another friendly against Singapore on Tuesday.
