James Penrice earns first Scotland call-up while Scott McTominay returns to squad

Scott McTominay has returned to the Scotland squad while there is a first call-up for James Penrice for this month’s Nations League double-header against North Macedonia and Switzerland.

McTominay, the 29-year-old Napoli midfielder, underwent heart surgery in September and missed the first camp under new boss Sebastien Pocognoli.

Torino striker Che Adams, who was absent last time out due to a muscle strain, also returns while there is a first call-up for defender Penrice, currently on loan at Rangers from Greek side AEK Athens.

Celtic left-back Kieran Tierney withdrew from the first camp with an injury but is back while centre-back Scott McKenna misses out after reportedly picking up a back strain while playing for Dinamo Zagreb.

Josh Doig, Colby Donovan, James Wilson and Lennon Miller drop out.

Pocognoli named his 26-man group for the home game against North Macedonia at Hampden Park on November 13 with a trip to Switzerland three days later.

Scotland are third in Group B1 with four points from four games, three points behind Slovenia and eight behind runaway leaders Switzerland.

Goalkeepers: Scott Bain (Falkirk), Angus Gunn (San Jose Earthquakes), Liam Kelly (Rangers), Jon McCracken (Bradford City)

Defenders: Luis Binks (Brondby), James Penrice (Rangers), Luke Graham (Stoke City), Jack Hendry (Al-Ettifaq), Aaron Hickey (Brentford), Scott McKenna (Dinamo Zagreb), Nathan Patterson (Torino), Andy Robertson (Tottenham), Kieran Tierney (Celtic), Stephen Welsh (Swansea City)

Midfielders: Ryan Christie (Bournemouth), Lewis Ferguson (Bologna), Billy Gilmour (Napoli), John McGinn (Aston Villa), Josh McPake (Hearts), Kristi Montgomery (Blackburn Rovers)

Forwards: Kieron Bowie (Sassuolo), Findlay Curtis (Rangers), Ben Gannon-Doak (Bournemouth), Oli McBurnie (Hull City), Robbie Ure (Sevilla), Che Adams (Torino)
