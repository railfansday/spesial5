Olivia Rodrigo Shares Heartache for the Holidays With ‘Miss This Christmas’ Live Debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Olivia Rodrigo performed her somber yuletide ballad, “Miss This Christmas,” for the first time live at her concert in Nashville Monday night, Nov. 23.

“Miss This Christmas” was included on the B-side of the vinyl version of Rodrigo’s first holiday single, “Take Me Home (4 Christmas),”  which was released earlier this month. But the song still hasn’t gotten a wide digital release, and as Rodrigo noted in her introduction, “no one’s ever really heard it before.” (Though some unauthorized rips have popped up online.)

The performance began with Rodrigo singing over a simple piano accompaniment, the arrangement later blooming with choral vocals and a tender guitar line. Lyrically, “Miss This Christmas” is the obverse of “Take Me Home (4 Christmas),” with Rodrigo tending a broken heart and passing along a message to an ex: “Next year’s gonna look real different/Tell your folks I’ll miss this Christmas.”

Rodrigo spoke a little bit more about her two Christmas singles while introducing “Miss This Christmas.” She described “Take Me Home” as a “really fun song about my fantasy of having a boyfriend that takes me to his parents’ house for the holiday.” But, “in proper Pretty Sad for a Girl In Love fashion,” Rodrigo continued, “there’s a very happy A-side of the album and a very sad B-side.
