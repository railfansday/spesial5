Broadway’s ‘Two Strangers (Carry A Cake Across New York)’ Announces Off Broadway Move

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Those two characters carrying that cake on Broadway will continue toting after the production closes: Two Strangers (Carry a Cake Across New York) will move to the Off Broadway venue New World Stages this spring.

The current Broadway cast of Sam Tutty and Christiani Pitts is not expected to make the move; new casting for the Off Broadway staging has not been announced.

Nominated for eight Tony Awards, the musical, written and composed by Jim Barne and Kit Buchan, will begin previews Monday, March 22, 2027, at New World – just a couple blocks from the Longacre Theatre, its current Broadway home – with an opening night of Sunday, April 4, 2027.

The Broadway staging is set to play its final performance on Sunday, November 29. Watch on Deadline

The move was announced at the curtain call following today’s Broadway performance.

Directed & choreographed by Tim Jackson, Two Strangers opened on Broadway on November 20, 2025, starring Tutty and Pitts as, respectively, a starry-eyed, optimistic young British man and a sharp, no-nonsense New Yorker who are thrown together when they prepare to attend a wedding, for which they must complete the task that gives the romantic comedy its title.

The Off Broadway production of Two Strangers (Carry a Cake Across New York) will feature scenic and costume design by Soutra Gilmour; lighting design by Jack Knowles; sound design by Tony Gayle; orchestrations by Lux Pyramid; and music supervision by Nick Finlow.

The musical is produced Off Broadway by Kevin McCollum, Tim Johanson, Glass Half Full Productions, and Jamie Wilson Productions.
