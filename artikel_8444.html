For home maintenance I’d forecast 1-2% of value, and you could come up with a reasonable similar assumption for cars, and maybe even vacations. They’re just to be used as ball park estimates for planning purposes.

I’m not sure how useful detailed planning is - the results will vary dramatically depending on future real rate of return, as well as the timing of returns.

I tend to look at it in broad terms - $4 million times 4% is $160k - give or take
