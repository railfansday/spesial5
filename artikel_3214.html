Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Record profits and a metering exit set up a sharper fiscal 2027 focus.

Mueller Water Products, Inc. (MWA +2.45%) reported record financial performance for fiscal year 2026, including all-time highs for net sales, adjusted EBITDA, and adjusted net income per share. Management reported that full-year performance was supported by commercial execution and strategic pricing, which overcame volume declines in certain product lines. The company announced a strategic agreement to transition its subscale metering business to Zenner USA to simplify operations and improve earnings quality. Management stated that the business enters fiscal year 2027 focused on the Mueller Operating System to drive further margin expansion and cash generation. The company increased its quarterly dividend by 14.3% and expanded its share repurchase authorization by $300 million to reflect confidence in its growth strategy.
