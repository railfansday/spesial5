Core Scientific: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Industrywide capital strain may leave Core Scientific with cheaper powered sites, but the company's own acquisition pipeline remains unproven.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 5, 2026, Core Scientific (CORZ +3.74%) announced that its contracted data center portfolio will generate $1.8 billion in average annual run-rate revenue once fully operational. The company is exiting Bitcoin mining to become a pure-play colocation provider, housing specialized computing hardware for artificial intelligence customers. Long-term lease agreements offer predictable cash flows as new data center facilities come online.

Three takeaways from the call stand out for long-term investors.

Core Scientific is completing its transition into high-density colocation, housing specialized hardware for artificial intelligence workloads. Management projects its contracted capacity of 1.1 gigawatts (GW, a unit of power equal to 1,000 megawatts) across CoreWeave and Advanced Micro Devices will yield $1.6 billion in annual net operating income (NOI, profit generated from property operations after operating expenses).

Developing high-density data centers requires substantial upfront capital before facilities generate leasing income. Core Scientific deployed internal cash to fund its entire equity requirement for the initial 530 megawatts (MW, a measure of power capacity) contracted by chipmaker Advanced Micro Devices.

Heavy capital demands are pressuring smaller data center developers and private equity-backed operators across the industry. Core Scientific is evaluating opportunities to buy powered land and projects under construction from capital-constrained sellers at stable valuations.

Data center energization accelerates across multiple states, driving sequential revenue growth. Operating data center campuses and secured utility power offer structural development advantages, while management tracks project financing options.

Near-term execution centers on delivering contracted capacity before initial AMD deployments begin in 2027.
