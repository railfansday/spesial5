I've tried a few sticker-making machines recently, including Cricut's StickerPix, but the Liene PixCut S1 just pips those other machines because it's easier to use, has phone backing templates and costs a little less to run. It's a smart device that prints, laminates and cuts stickers in one go.

What I like about the PixCut S1 is that it doesn't try to be everything. You design in the Liene app, send your artwork over Bluetooth, and the machine handles the printing and cutting. No fiddling with cutting mats or designs, no setting up a separate printer, and far less messing about than a traditional Cricut workflow.

I found the print quality particularly impressive. It uses 300dpi dye-sublimation printing to produce vibrant, detailed stickers with a protective laminated finish. In my testing, a sheet took around one to two minutes to print and cut, and the batch-printing process was reliable, which makes it a good option if you're producing several designs for gifts, small businesses or craft projects.
