Ethena’s latest expansion is putting its potential $7.5 billion buyback threshold back in focus. USDe has crossed $100 million on Avalanche, while USDe and sUSDe have launched across the Stellar ecosystem. But ENA still needs a confirmed chart breakout before its bullish targets come into play.

Ethena announced its Stellar deployment on November 19, bringing USDe and sUSDe into the network’s ecosystem. Users can also bridge USDe, sUSDe and ENA to Stellar through Stargate Finance.

The deployment follows the same cross-chain standards used elsewhere, including hourly bridge caps and a diversified DVN set. It’s another integration, though it doesn’t automatically prove increased demand for ENA.

USDe is approaching a reported $7.5 billion supply threshold. Under the stated mechanism, 5% of protocol revenue is allocated toward token buybacks once that threshold is reached.

That gives the supply milestone significance beyond network expansion. However, the figures don’t confirm that the threshold has already been reached or that buybacks have begun.

The Ethena $7.5B buyback trigger could become an important catalyst if supply reaches the threshold. For now, Stellar expansion and Avalanche growth provide the backdrop, while ENA’s breakout remains the price-action test.
