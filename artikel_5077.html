XRP’s Extended Base Meets XLM Bounce From Lows

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Home » Altcoins » XRP’s Extended Base Meets XLM Bounce From Lows

Similarities within XLM & XRP’s previous trading behavior adds another layer to the rebound thesis.

Two recent technical takes have focused on XRP’s multi-year chart pattern and XLM’s bounce from a multi-year low, as both tokens trade well below prior highs. XRP sits near $0.92 while XLM trades around $0.125.

Analyst Maxi highlighted a weekly chart showing XRP breaking out of a multi-year consolidation triangle that stretches back roughly nine years.

The post described a “perfect breakout & back-test” and projected that the length of the base could support a move toward $8–10 or higher before any later utility-driven price discovery.

$XRP about to enter PRICE discovery 😳PERFECT breakout & back-test 🎯 Breaking out of a 9 year consolidation.The longer the base = the higher in space!We are looking at a $8-10+ $XRP, before we then enter true price discovery based on utility. pic.twitter.com/3rpFi9i0Hv

The chart itself marks a large symmetrical triangle with price currently near the lower boundary after earlier 2025 highs.

Such long-term patterns are subject to interpretation, and the projected targets represent the analyst’s view of the structure rather than a confirmed outcome. Still, XRP coin remains down substantially from its 2025 peak of $3.65.

Rudra noted that XLM hit a two-year low near $0.12 over the weekend before recovering to roughly $0.136, a move of about 13% off that low and outperforming Bitcoin and Ethereum on the day.

🚨 BOOM: $XLM repricing has started.Saturday: $0.12. A 2-year low.Today: $0.1361. Up 6.73% in one day.✅ +13% off Saturday's low✅ Ahead of Bitcoin. Ahead of Ethereum.✅ Stellar monthly payments: 10x since January✅ 12.7% of all tokenized real-world assets live on Stellar… pic.twitter.com/N153btw3e3

The post pointed to several network developments, including a reported 10x increase in Stellar monthly payments since January and the claim that 12.7% of all tokenized real-world assets currently sit on Stellar Lumens (XLM).

It also referenced the planned connection of DTCC’s tokenized securities platform to Stellar, with DTC-custodied assets expected on the network in the first half of 2027. Additional items mentioned included an upcoming validator vote on a protocol upgrade.

XLM’s market capitalization remains in the low billions, and the token is still far below its historical high.

Both posts emphasize longer-term structural or adoption arguments against a backdrop of recent weakness. XRP’s triangle analysis focuses on the multi-year base, while the XLM commentary highlights relative short-term strength and institutional tokenization progress.

Neither move has yet established a sustained reversal on higher time-frames.
