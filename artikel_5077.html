Solana Foundation takes ‘majority interest’ in CoinGecko – What changes next?

The Solana Foundation now holds a “majority interest” in CoinGecko, its Chief Product Officer Vibhu Norby said on X.

CoinGecko described the arrangement as “backing” when it announced the news at Breakpoint in London on the 17th of November. Neither announcement gave the exact stake, price, or governance terms.

Four days earlier, CoinGecko had promoted independent ownership as a reason to choose its data service over Binance-owned CoinMarketCap. It now plans to explore trading alongside its market data.

The Foundation said it planned to bring Solana-powered trading to CoinGecko’s website, app, and GeckoTerminal. Notably, CoinGecko said it was still exploring trading features.

For Solana, that could mean reaching traders where they already look up prices. CoinGecko draws well over 50 million monthly sessions, according to the Foundation.

On the 17th of November, Solana [SOL] recorded $50.44 billion in 30-day DEX spot volume, whereas Ethereum [ETH] recorded $53.86 billion.

Norby also flagged broader coverage of stocks and real-world assets [RWAs]. AMBCrypto reported that Solana’s RWA value reached $5 billion in October.

By the 12th of November, tokenized equity supply stood at $607 million, with xStocks accounting for 75%.

CoinGecko’s API guide warned that exchange ownership could raise questions about bias in rankings and trading volumes. AMBCrypto covered similar concerns when Binance bought CoinMarketCap in 2020.

After Norby’s disclosure, an X user called the new arrangement “a credibility problem.”

CoinGecko said its methods would stay “transparent, publicly accessible.” Readers can check how it calculates prices, but the announcements leave open who can approve changes to those rules.

Brera Holdings disclosed board rights in a separate 2025 deal involving Solmate. A later filing specified that the Foundation could nominate up to two directors, depending on board size. For CoinGecko, board seats and approval rights remain undisclosed.
