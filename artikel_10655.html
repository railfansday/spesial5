This company wants to pay you over $100,000 to play GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

A company is offering the chance to earn up to $120,000 by playing GTA 6, with an additional $15,000 up for grabs if they complete the job.

With Rockstar’s long-awaited sequel launching on November 19, millions of players are preparing to lose countless hours exploring Vice City and the state of Leonida.

But while most will have to fit their gaming sessions around work, one lucky applicant could actually get paid a six-figure sum to play it.

Kalshi, an American prediction market platform that allows users to trade on the outcomes of real-world events, is hiring a GTA VI Researcher to investigate the game’s economy and its impact on the real world.

According to the official job listing, the successful applicant will begin playing GTA 6 at midnight on November 19, streaming their entire playthrough on Twitch or YouTube.

They’ll be tasked with finding the most effective ways to make money in-game, analysing Leonida’s economy, and publishing weekly research reports.

The role also involves investigating the game’s real-world impact, including how many people call in sick on launch day and how much productivity is lost.

“We’re looking for someone whose parents said video games would never pay the bills. They were wrong, and you can tell them we said so,” the listing reads.

Kalshi is offering a fixed fee of between $100,000 and $120,000, plus an additional $15,000 upon completion. However, there are some requirements.

Applicants must be at least 18 years old, have 10 or more years of gaming experience, and at least three years of streaming experience. They’ll also need their own streaming channel and equipment, including a reliable internet connection.

Fortunately, Kalshi isn’t expecting anyone to play GTA 6 nonstop, with the listing confirming that breaks and sleep are allowed.
