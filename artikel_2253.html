Ex-player has say on Middlesbrough’s promotion chances amid ‘strongest’ team verdict

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Middlesbrough are sat joint-top of the Championship table at the moment along with Birmingham City and Stoke City after 15 fixtures. They drew 0-0 away at Watford last time out before the international break.

Kim Hellberg‘s side are back in action this weekend as they aim to return to winning ways after two games without a win. They face Bristol City at the Riverside Stadium.

Ikeme, who had a loan spell at Boro from Wolves back in 2011, has delivered his verdict on his ex-club, as per The Yorkshire Post: “I think they’ll be disappointed if they don’t go up automatically this year but that being said, with Wolves, West Ham and Burnley coming down, it’s going to be tough to get up for anyone. You’ve got the teams in and around the play-off picture, you’ve got the chasing pack as well.”

He added: “Out of the teams that were left in the Championship, they do look like the strongest. I think Wolves will be hoping to kick on as the year goes on but Boro do look like one of the teams that will be in and around it.”

Middlesbrough reached the play-off final in the last campaign. However, they were beaten at Wembley by Hull City.

Boro lacked a proper number nine last term and that was the main reason why they didn’t get into the top two in the end. They have solved that issue with the addition of Will Lankshear from Tottenham Hotspur and he has been prolific.

Lankshear, 21, has scored 15 goals in 16 games in all competitions since his summer switch. He has sat out of their last two matches due to injury against Wrexham and Watford respectively and his team haven’t won without.

Middlesbrough aren’t just a one man team though and have some eye-catching talent at their disposal. Aidan Morris, Sebastian Berhalter and Amario Cozier-Duberry, among others, will all fancy their chances of playing at the top level soon.

Middlesbrough can’t just rely on Lankshear to fire in the goals for them. They need other players like David Strelec and Kyle Joseph to step up to the plate as well.

Strelec has only just returned from injury and could still play a key role over the coming weeks and months. As for Joseph, he has struggled to make an impact since his switch from Hull City in the last transfer window.

The ex-Blackpool man hasn’t scored in the league yet for Hellberg’s side. Boro are having to be patient with him at the moment.

Middlesbrough have an ideal opportunity to return to winning ways this Saturday against Bristol City as they look to get back to the summit. Their upcoming opponents have been in poor form recently and pressure is starting to mount on their head coach Michael Skubala.
