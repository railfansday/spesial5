‘I’m sure’: EFL expert on Sheffield Wednesday’s promotion chances

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Sheffield Wednesday have turned to the former Stockport County man as their head coach. SWFC have picked him as their replacement for Henrik Pedersen, who was sacked at the end of last month.

Challinor, 51, left Edgeley Park at the end of last season. He has since been weighing up his next move in the game and is now back in the dugout at Hillsborough.

Elek has delivered his verdict on the Not The Top 20 Podcast on YouTube: “The expectation for Wednesday fans should be to get them promoted by the end of next season rather than this season and if he’s given time to do that, I think he’ll probably deliver it, to be honest. It’s a rock solid appointment.”

He added: “With Dave Challinor and Simon Wilson, they know each other, they know how to work together. I have no idea how they get on as humans but there is certainly that established connection. You would assume that they must like working together otherwise they wouldn’t have signed this deal. I think it’s a good appointment, I’m sure he’ll improve them.”

Sheffield Wednesday are hoping to get an immediate promotion from League One this season if possible. SWFC were relegated from the Championship earlier this year after they finished in the bottom three along with Leicester City and Oxford United.

It was a summer of transition for the South Yorkshire outfit and there were plenty of comings and goings. It has taken their new look squad a while to adapt to life in the third tier.

As Elek has said, it may be more realistic for the Owls to target going up in the next campaign rather than this one. They are still getting used to their new level and it now may take some time to adapt to Challinor’s style after Pedersen’s departure.

Sheffield Wednesday’s form in the league hasn’t been very good recently and they need to start picking up more victories. They have only won one of their last eight league outings and will be looking to improve over the coming weeks as the fixtures start to come thick and fast.

Sheffield Wednesday’s move for Challinor makes complete sense. He knows SWFC’s sporting director Simon Wilson from their time at Stockport County together and has also got the best out of Louie Barry in the past.

Challinor knows the league well, has got a great managerial record and is ready to take the next step in his career now. He has also managed Colwyn Bay, AFC Fylde and Hartlepool United in the past.

The Owls will get the chance to freshen up their squad again in the January transfer window. They will also have the opportunity to let some players head out the exit door too this winter to help free up space and funds to pave the way for reinforcements.

Sheffield Wednesday are back in action this weekend with an away trip to Oldham Athletic in the FA Cup. They drew 1-1 at home with Blackpool last time out.
