GTA 6: How to Find the Kraken

The Grand Theft Auto series is well-known for its easter eggs, and GTA 6 is no different. Some notable ones include Dexter’s apartment from the hit TV show, UFO sightings, and even nods to Red Dead Redemption 2.

One of the coolest easter eggs found in GTA 6 so far is the Kraken, the legendary sea monster from Norse and Scandinavian Folklore. While the Kraken sighting in GTA 6 isn’t exactly a living creature, but rather just a structure near the ocean floor, it is clearly a reference to the famous sea monster.

This is a brief guide that entails the location of the Fast and Furious green Eclipse in GTA 6.

If you want to see this easter egg for yourself, this guide has everything you need to know. Here’s how to find the Kraken in Grand Theft Auto 6.

The Kraken can be found underwater, south of Catalan Bay and east of Peacock Bay in Vice City. The exact location of the Kraken can be found in the image below.

The water map can be a bit tricky to read due to there being no land, structures, or streets, but if you look closely enough, you will notice that there are distinct shapes within the water.

The Kraken is located on the second oval-like shape down from the beach of Catalan Bay. The oval shapes represent large sand hills and rocks underneath the water’s surface.

When you reach the location shown in the image above, equip your scuba gear and dive downward until you reach the sand. Next, take a look around you until you spot a strange-looking structure. You may have to swim a bit in case your diving point wasn’t the exact correct location.

Tales of streakers were some of the first leaks to breach GTA 6, and those on the hunt may be a bit disappointed.

Upon approaching the structure, you will immediately notice that it looks like a ship tangled in the Kraken’s tentacles. Unfortunately, the tentacles don’t move or anything, but it’s still a really cool easter egg that just goes to show how much love Rockstar Games put into GTA 6.

In case you need some help actually diving deep enough to reach the Kraken in GTA 6, we've got you covered on how to find scuba gear.

There are a few ways to acquire scuba gear. First, you can find scuba gear on the small dock behind Jason’s house in Chapter 1, which is the earliest method. However, this method becomes unavailable to you if you have completed the first chapter.

A very upset parrot is more than willing to point players in the direction of cash if they beat down its jailers.

Another way to get scuba gear is by exploring docks near beaches. While not all docks will have scuba gear to grab, a good number of them do.

You can also get scuba gear from boats that are either docked or sailing the seas. I recommend visiting Kenton’s Charter Boat in Key Lento since it always has spare scuba gear on its seats.

Keep in mind that once you are finished scuba diving for the Kraken, or just scuba diving in general, and return to dry land, the scuba gear will eventually despawn. In other words, you will have to find more scuba gear the next time you want to go scuba diving.
