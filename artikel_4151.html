Cleanser Man Is Mega Man: Dual Override’s Boss Design Contest Winner

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Mega Man: Dual Override’s 2025 reveal brought back the classic boss design contest, asking fans to create and submit their own robot masters, with Capcom choosing one to include in the game. After thousands of submissions, we have our winner; meet Cleanser Man.

As his name suggests, Cleanser Man is a cleaning robot who attacks (and tidies) using a long, multi-cleaning hose arm. Capcom has provided the following character summary:

Cleanser Man is a cleaning robot with an obsession for pristine beauty. Fueled by an extremely sensitive dirt sensor, Cleaner Man’s ideal world is one without a speck of dust. However, his devotion to cleanliness has a cost. His aggressive scrubbing causes great damage to global landmarks and historic works of art. This fixation has caused the public to view him as more of a threat than a helping hand. Furious and feeling misunderstood, Cleanser Man seizes control of trash processing facility and uses it to pollute the environment with hopes of forcing the world to appreciate the importance of cleanliness.

Cleanser Man won out among the six finalists, a few of which were also cleaning-themed bosses. “In the end, we chose Cleanser Man because of the unique way the concept was brought together and the originality of the color design,” says game director Shinya Ikuta. “While staying true to and respecting the original submission, we adjusted certain details to better fit the world and style of the game.”  Unfortunately, Capcom has not revealed Cleanser Man's creator, but you can see the character in action in the reveal video below.

In addition to Cleanser Man, Mega Man: Dual Override's rogues' gallery includes the following revealed robot masters: Twinkle Girl, Jet Man, Tank Man, and Cook Man. The remaining three bosses have yet to be revealed.

Mega Man: Dual Override launches in 2027 for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC.  To learn more about the game, read our extended preview here. You can also check out the game's recently revealed amiibo figures for the Switch versions.
