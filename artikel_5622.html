Inside the 10,000-Square-Foot Billionaires Row Condo That Was Just Relisted for $75 Million

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

A sprawling eight-bedroom condo tucked away in the One57 tower on Billionaires Row has returned to the market.

The estate, with spectacular views of Midtown Manhattan, first hit the market for $90 million in June 2025 and was discounted to $75 million in August 2026. It is holding firm at that price.

Built in 2013, the 10,000-square-foot home high atop the Park Hyatt New York has similarly soared to the top spot on the week’s most expensive homes list.

The home occupies an entire floor and has walls of windows framing views spanning Central Park, the Hudson River, and beyond.

Amenities in what the listing calls “one of the most sought after homes in the world” include a chef's kitchen with high-end appliances, dining and entertaining spaces, and a living room with home cinema capabilities.

The primary suite has three walk-in dressing rooms, a sitting area, and a marble-clad bathroom with a soaking tub, a separate rain shower, a sauna, a steam room, and a treatment room.

There's also a corner library facing the Hudson River, a study, a playroom, and separate quarters for guests and staff, including a kitchenette that's ideal for “well-accommodated live-in assistance.”

More spectacular mansions to make the week's most expensive homes list include boxing legend Sugar Ray Leonard's Pacific Palisades, CA, compound; an ocean-to-lake Polynesian-style retreat in Manalapan, FL; and a reimagined desert estate in Paradise Valley, AZ.

The Colony at White Pine Canyon: This ski-in/ski-out estate is currently under construction and offers a buyer the opportunity to add personal touches.

The seven-bedroom home will have a “timeless” 14,406-square-foot interior and direct ski access to Canyons Resort. Amenities will include a wellness retreat with an infrared sauna, steam room, hot tub, cold plunge, and fitness studio, plus a home theater, private office, and ski preparation area. Other highlights will include natural stone details, “distinctive” ceilings, smoked glass, radiant heat, oxygenation-ready systems, and seven fireplaces. The 5,804-square-foot heated outdoor living space is designed for year-round entertaining.

The 5.35-acre mountain retreat will also have a guest casita with a private garage.

Kelly Hamlin of Summit Sotheby's International Realty is the listing agent.

Ocean Reef estate: This five-bedroom waterfront mansion has 180-degree views of the Intracoastal Waterway and Card Sound Bay.

Built in 2012, the 11,694-square-foot estate also has 5,300 square feet of outdoor living space. Architectural details include “century-old” reclaimed wood floors, rich walnut millwork, and Venetian plaster walls. The chef’s kitchen has concrete countertops, antique reclaimed tin accents, and custom tile sinks. The separate prep kitchen has a reclaimed Chicago brick arched ceiling and a stained-glass pocket door that connects the two kitchens.

Other highlights include two primary suites with private water-facing terraces, a media room with a star-detail ceiling, a clubroom with a custom bar, a 2,000-bottle wine cellar, two wine coolers, dual laundry rooms, an elevator, and built-in safes.

The half-acre lot also has an outdoor kitchen, a pool, a two-car garage with golf cart parking, a 240-foot continuous dock, a boat lift, and a floating dock.

Mary Lee of Ocean Reef Club Sotheby's International Realty is the listing agent.

The Leonard Estate: Boxing legend Sugar Ray Leonard is once again looking to part ways with his eight-bedroom compound, which he originally listed for $51,995,000 in 2019.

Built in 1997, the 16,773-square-foot home last hit the market for $36,995,000 in April 2026. The latest price represents a $1.5 million discount.

An oval pool and spa, a lighted tennis court with a viewing pavilion, a putting green, and a two-story guesthouse adjacent to the pool are also on the property.

Jonathan Nash of Carolwood Estates is the listing agent.

Gulf frontage: This five-bedroom estate was custom-built in 2015 and features beach views and 135 feet of frontage along the Gulf of Mexico.

The seaside retreat, with a wraparound seawall, has water-facing views from nearly every room of the 5,277-square-foot home. The open floor plan includes a living room with hardwood flooring, a modern kitchen with a wood ceiling and custom cabinetry, and a Gulf-facing living room lined with floor-to-ceiling glass walls. Other amenities include an elevator, three fireplaces, and an eight-car garage. A wraparound covered deck is designed for soaking in the stunning scenery.

According to the listing, the home is eligible for Port Royal Club Associate membership.

Charles Shumway of Gulf Coast International Properties is the listing agent.

Ocean-to-lake estate: This Polynesian-style retreat sits on 1.77 acres with a prime ocean-to-lake setting and more than 150 feet of frontage on the Intracoastal Waterway and the Atlantic Ocean.

Built in 2014, the seven-bedroom mansion has more than 21,000 square feet of space designed for both large-scale entertaining and everyday living. No interior photos are included with the listing, but it says the home integrates seamlessly with the expansive grounds, which include a resort-inspired pool with waterfall feature, a new private beach cabana with a rooftop pool, and a tennis court.

Margit Brandt of Premier Estate Properties, Inc.-Palm Beach is the listing agent.

Port Royal: This waterfront estate encompassing 1.5 lots has a boat dock, “exceptional wide and long bay vistas,” and “captivating views of nature.”

The 13,775-square-foot mansion, built in 2003, has formal living and dining rooms with custom millwork, two executive offices, a family room, and a home theater. Private guest quarters sit above the four-car garage, which is designed for the auto enthusiast. Spacious terraces overlook a pool with a spa, manicured lawns, and a covered poolside cabana bar.

The home is also eligible for membership at the Port Royal Club.

Richard G. Prebish II of William Raveis Real Estate - Naples - Broad Ave South is the listing agent.

South-facing water views: This five-bedroom Georgian Revival estate is located in the Estate Section overlooking the Intracoastal Waterway.

Designed by architect Marion Sims Wyeth, the 7,945-square-foot home was built in 1936. The thoughtfully updated interior has elegant entertaining spaces, an expansive family room, a newly installed elevator, built-in cabinetry, and marble, tile, and wood floors. The modern kitchen has a wine chiller, dual refrigerators, and an L-shaped breakfast bar. Glass French doors open to terraces overlooking the water. An upstairs family room has access to an outdoor terrace that spans the length of the home.

The 1-acre lot has 208 feet of water frontage and, according to the listing, “offers many choices for a dock location along the brand new seawall.”

Lisa Cregan of Sotheby's International Realty - Palm Beach Brokerage is the listing agent.

Reimagined residence: This six-bedroom desert retreat, tucked between Casa Blanca and Wilkinson, recently received a “$15 million-plus architectural, interior design, and landscape transformation” by Jaimee Rose Interiors and Berghoff Design Group.

The 23,417-square-foot estate is being offered fully furnished and features European-inspired details, including handcrafted plasterwork, an Italian marble dining table, and antiques sourced from Paris and Milan. The chef's kitchen has bronze countertops that complement bespoke Italian marble and Ceppo Sicilia stone. A catering kitchen with a walk-in cooler is designed for entertaining.

Other amenities include a hidden speakeasy with a wet bar, an indoor sport and pickleball court, guest accommodations with a private kitchen and separate entrance, a soundproof cinema room, a children’s area with a playroom, and a wellness wing with a gym, a massage and treatment room, a red-light sauna, a dry sauna, and a cold plunge.

Overlooking Camelback Mountain, the 2.48-acre lot is surrounded by “centuries old” olive trees. It comes with a shooting range, an underground bunker or safe room, a nine-car garage, an in-ground trampoline, a pool with a Splinterworks waterslide and underground chiller, and a dedicated pool bath with an outdoor shower.

Katrina Barrett of Walt Danley Local Luxury Christie's International Real Estate is the listing agent.

2.5 Gulf-front lots: This brand-new Barefoot Beach estate has 13,261 square feet of living space overlooking 120 feet of beach frontage, which comes with a private boat slip and lift.

Inside the home are French oak floors milled in Italy, Egyptian shell stone, custom cabinetry, white bronze fixtures, a sculptural steel staircase, and expansive windows framing views of the water. The primary suite has a dressing room, dual closets with an island, twin vanities, two showers, and a soaking tub. A two-bedroom wing, which doubles as a boardroom, is designed for extended family and guests, with its own kitchen, living room, garage, and beach entry.

Other highlights include two offices, four laundry rooms, a 3,900-bottle wine room, a chef’s kitchen with dual cooktops, a commercial-grade elevator, and a five-car garage with golf cart parking.

A rooftop lap pool and spa overlook the Gulf, and a cabana, a second outdoor kitchen, and entertaining spaces complete the outdoor offerings.

Dawn McKenna, Ashley Baird Fenttiman, and Kathleen Thanas of Coldwell Banker Realty are the listing agents.

Billionaires Row: This eight-bedroom condo in Midtown Manhattan’s luxury One57 tower occupies an entire floor and has spectacular views of Central Park and the Hudson River.

Originally listed for $90 million in June 2025, the 10,000-square-foot home was later reduced to its current asking price in August 2026 before being taken off the market in October.

Built in 2013 and designed by architect Christian de Portzamparc, the residence has bespoke finishes, including 11.5-foot ceilings, floor-to-ceiling windows that capture the skyline views, a living room with custom cabinetry, a corner library, a study, a playroom, and separate guest accommodations and staff quarters. The primary suite has three dressing rooms and a marble-clad bathroom with a soaking tub, a separate rain shower, a Finnish sauna, a steam room, and a treatment room.

A $16,000 monthly association fee provides access to a fitness center, an indoor swimming pool, a yoga studio, a full-service spa, a private dining room with catering services, a screening and performance room, a library with a billiard table, and a pet grooming room.
