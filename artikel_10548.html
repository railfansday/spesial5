Ex-police officer becomes VTuber to help stop viewers from committing crimes

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

A former Japanese police officer has become a VTuber, promising to share his experiences in patrol cars and riot police units to teach viewers about the law.

VTubers in Japan aren’t exactly strangers when it comes to collaborating with real-life law enforcement and government awareness campaigns. After all, Hololive’s AZKi was famously appointed a traffic regulation adviser and police station chief for a day by Kagawa Prefectural Police in March 2026.

However, it’s rarer for former police officers to leave their crime-fighting days behind and debut as a virtual creator. Well, that’s exactly what VTuber Sukenosuke Hongo has done, and the officer-turned-anime boy aims to share his practical advice with his viewers.

In his introduction video, which has garnered a whopping one million views since November 1, the former officer said he wants to teach his viewers about the law and how to protect themselves.

During his debut announcement, Sukenosuke singled out drivers as one group he hopes to help, explaining that his time responding to emergency calls showed him how often people struggle to handle these situations.

“When I responded to emergency calls and listened to people explain what happened, there were honestly plenty of times when I thought, ‘What is this person talking about?’” he said. “That’s why I want to teach people the correct information.”

Sukenosuke also aims to stop his viewers from becoming victims of crime as well, using his real-life experience working in “police boxes, patrol cars, and in riot police units” to keep fans more informed.

“The fundamental purpose of crime prevention is to stop you from becoming a victim in the first place,” said the VTuber. “Even if that next incident does happen, knowing how to respond properly makes it easier for the police to take action, too.”
