On Monday, November 23rd, Allstate began dealing with an outage that has impacted its website, mobile apps, and roadside assistance service. It’s just the latest company, including Microsoft and JPMorgan Chase, to suffer an outage in the last few weeks. As of Friday afternoon, access to accounts through the app and site appeared to still be down. When asked for comment, the company pointed us to a notice on its site reading:

Allstate customers can reach us in many ways, including online, by phone and through local offices. Some access has been restricted over the last 48 hours, but we continue to be available for customers and respond to and settle claims. To the extent service interruptions impact a customer’s ability to make a payment, they won’t lose coverage or be charged late fees.

Opening the app pops up a notification saying the company is working to “restore access as soon as possible,” and encourages customers to contact the company by phone to file and manage claims. Users have flooded X and Reddit with complaints about the outage, some of whom have either been left waiting for roadside assistance or have been unable to insure new vehicles.

Allstate hasn’t publicly identified a cause for the outage.
