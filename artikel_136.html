Israel's Eisenkot condemns 'Jewish terrorism' in West Bank

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Israeli opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot has condemned a recent series of arson attacks in the occupied West Bank as "Jewish terrorism." Israeli authorities have opened an investigation into the incidents.

Israeli authorities were on Sunday investigating arson attacks and vandalism against Palestinian properties and vehicles in the occupied West Bank which Israeli opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot condemned as "Jewish terrorism."

Images posted on social media showed vehicles in flames and slogans written on walls reading: "In blood and fire Judea has fallen, in blood and fire Judea will rise again," using a biblical name for the West Bank.

"Settlers attacked the northern area on the outskirts of the village at around two in the morning," Jihad al-Qaq, a local activist, told the AFP news agency.

"They set fire to around eight vehicles and wrote racist slogans on the walls of houses."

Israeli soldiers and police were deployed to the towns of Deir as-Sudan, Deir Abu Mashaal, Kafr Malik and Al-Tuwani following the reports, with the Israel Defense Forces (IDF) saying it "strongly condemns" the acts.

The suspected arson attacks were condemned by Israeli opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot, who is engaged in post-election negotiations to form a government to replace that of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.

"The night of arson … is Jewish terrorism in every sense of the word, and it must be called what it is," he said.

"Sabotage by extremist groups setting fire to vehicles and homes undermines regional stability, endangers lives, and severely damages the national security and foreign relations of the State of Israel," he said. Eisenkot used the biblical terms, Judea and Samaria, to refer to the West Bank territories in question.

More than 500,000 Israelis live in West Bank settlements, which are illegal under international law, alongside around 3 million Palestinians.

The West Bank has been occupied by Israel since 1967 but attacks against Palestinians have surged in the wake of Hamas' October 7, 2023 attack on Israel. Since then, more than 1,100 Palestinians in the West Bank have been killed by Israeli soldiers or settlers, according to data from the Palestinian Health Ministry.

Local resident Ahmad Ghneimat told the Associated Press that violence had increased after several Israeli settler outposts sprang up in the area. He said settlers have stolen sheep and violently harassed olive harvesters.

"We woke up at 2:30 at night to the sound of explosions [and] seven cars on fire," said Muntasir al-Malik, another resident. "We came out of the houses and panicked."

Eisenkot called for "severe sanctions" to be imposed on the individuals responsible.

Eisenkot's labeling of the violent settlers as "terrorists" follows the use of the same term by the United States' ambassador to Israel, Mike Huckabee, in September. US officials usually reserve the term for Palestinian militants.
