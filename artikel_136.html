Politics and peek-a-boo in Australian parliament as senator brings baby to work

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Politics took a back seat in the Australian parliament today when Senator Fatima Payman brought her baby daughter to work.

Payman was taking part in a parliamentary vote on a Digital Duty of Care bill, aimed at holding social media platforms to account for algorithms that surface harmful content.

While the lawmaker was speaking, baby Aaliyah captured the attention of Senator Lidia Thorpe and enjoyed a game of peek-a-boo.
