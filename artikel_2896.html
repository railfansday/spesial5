Tristan Wirfs to play Sunday, Emeka Egbuka questionable

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Bucs will have left tackle Tristan Wirfs in the lineup as they try to stretch their winning streak to three games on Sunday.

Wirfs did not practice on Thursday because of a toe injury, but was back on the field for Friday’s practice and head coach Todd Bowles said in his press conference that Wirfs will play against the Bears. Wirfs has not missed a game yet this season.

Wide receiver Emeka Egbuka could join Wirfs on the field against Chicago. Egbuka missed Week 7 with a concussion and Week 8 with a foot injury, but he returned to full practice participation on Thursday and Bowles called him questionable for this weekend.

Linebacker Yaya Diaby (knee) is the only player that the Buccaneers have ruled out at this point.
