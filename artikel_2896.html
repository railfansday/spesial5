Jake Ferguson gets good prognosis on ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Dallas Cowboys tight end Jake Ferguson (ankle) suffered an ankle injury in the Week 8 game against the Arizona Cardinals. While he left the game early, and he missed the rest of the game, head coach Brian Schottenheimer said the initial feedback was good.

"We'll see how he moves around. It might be later in the week that he goes, but we feel he tried to come back and just had strength to do it," Schottenheimer said. "But we feel like the results of the exams today were positive."

SOURCE: The Dallas Morning News - Joseph Hoyt
