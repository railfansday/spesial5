Josh Jacobs listed as limited in Packers practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

When Packers running back Josh Jacobs had surgery on his broken finger earlier this month, word was that he'd miss two or three weeks before being able to return to action.

Jacobs appears to be aiming for the shorter end of that window. The Packers listed him as a limited participant in practice on Sunday and that would seem to keep him in contention to return to action against the Rams on Wednesday night.

Defensive end Micah Parsons also drew a limited tag after his first padded practice since tearing his ACL last December. The Packers plan to have Parsons in the lineup this week as long as all goes well in practice over the next couple of days.

Cornerback Brandon Cisse (rib), linebacker Zaire Franklin (shin), linebacker Isaiah McDuffie (rib), wide receiver Bo Melton (thigh), wide receiver Skyy Moore (ankle), center Sean Rhyan (ankle), and guard Kevin Zeitler (triceps) landed in the limited group as well. Guard Aaron Banks (knee) was a full participant.
