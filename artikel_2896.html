The 2026 SEC volleyball tournament is set to bring five days of intense competition to Savannah, Georgia starting this weekend.

The beloved SEC tournament took a 20-year hiatus before returning last year, and it looks like it's here to stay. 16 programs are set to compete in Georgia for a conference title, including defending champions Kentucky, and nationally top-ranked teams like Florida, Texas, Tennessee and Vanderbilt.

Amidst a ten-game win streak, Florida has earned the No. 1 seed in the bracket. But a trophy is not guaranteed for the Gators — the tournament's single-elimination format means any team could make a campaign towards an SEC title this week.

Be sure to tune in for all the action from Savannah.
