Jets vs. Dolphins odds: Opening lines for Week 12 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The New York Jets broke a five game losing streak and got their third win of the season on Sunday against the Los Angeles Chargers. The Jets are now 3-8 on the season, in third place in the AFC East.

The Jets scored on the opening drive of the game and never looked back. The Jets never trailed in the game. They scored every time they had the ball other than their last possession, when they ran out the clock to seal the win. The Jets offense bullied the Chargers defense all day. The running game was efficient and effective. The Jets converted on better than 80% of their third downs. Geno Smith was accurate and efficient, spreading the ball around effectively. Practice squad call-up Sterling Shepard scored twice. It was a really nice offensive performance by the Jets. Defensively the Jets struggled, but David Bailey got another two sacks to get to 9.5 for the season and the offense was good enough to get the victory.

The win over the Chargers left the Jets with a 3-8 record to start the season, in 3rd place in the AFC East. Though mathematical elimination is still probably a month or so away, it has become obvious the Jets playoff drought will extend to 16 years and counting by season's end. The Jets are also just one loss away from their 11th straight losing season.

Now the Jets go on the road to face the 1-9 Miami Dolphins. The Dolphins' only win this season came at the expense of the Jets a few weeks ago.

Since the Dolphins' victory over the Jets in October the Dolphins lost three straight competitive games to the New England Patriots, Detroit Lions and Indianapolis Colts before getting blown out this Sunday by the Buffalo Bills.

Let's take a look at the current odds for this game according to FanDuel Sportsbook. The oddsmakers think the Jets will beat the Dolphins next Sunday. The current line has the Jets road favorites by 3.5 points. The game has an over/under of 41.5 points. On the moneyline the Jets are -215 while the Dolphins are +180.

A win against the Dolphins next Sunday would send the Jets going into their Week 13 bye with some momentum and a chance to finish the season on the upswing. Let's see if the Jets can put together their first modest winning streak of the season.
