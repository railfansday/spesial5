Chargers activate TE David Njoku, LB Denzel Perryman for Monday night vs. Ravens

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Chargers will officially have a couple of players back for Monday night's game against the Ravens.

Los Angeles has activated tight end David Njoku and linebacker Denzel Perryman off of injured reserve, the club announced.

Njoku (fibula) has been sidelined since September. In his first season with the Chargers, Njoku caught six passes for 67 yards in two games.

Perryman (pectoral) was injured during August and is now set to make his 2026 debut on Monday night.

Additionally, the Chargers have placed offensive lineman Branson Taylor (ankle) on injured reserve. The club elevated defensive end Myles Adams from the practice squad for Monday night's game.
