Joel Embiid scores 20 in 76ers' win over Raptors after knee injury scare; LeBron James sat out for back management

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

After being listed as questionable up until game time, Joel Embiid scored 20 points in the Philadelphia 76ers' 123-112 win over the Toronto Raptors on Thursday.

Embiid also avoided what initially appeared to be a knee injury early in the first quarter when Ja'Kobe Walter ran into him trying to avoid a screen. Walter's right knee collided with Embiid's right knee, sending the center to the floor. Embiid pointed to his knee as trainers came out to check him, causing fears that he may have been seriously hurt.

However, Embiid re-entered the game three minutes later and scored his first basket with under two minutes remaining in the first quarter.

Up until a half-hour before tip-off, Embiid was listed on the injury report as questionable with "right foot plantar fascia injury management." That had also sidelined him for two of the Sixers' game in the early season.

"I have no idea," Embiid said when asked by ESPN's Kris Rhim during shootaround if he was playing versus the Raptors. "We'll see what they have to say and then we'll go from there."

Tyrese Maxey scored 27 to lead the Sixers, also dishing out 8 assists. Jaylen Brown added 18 with 6 rebounds, Anfernee Simons contributed 17 points (hitting 5-of-6 3-pointers) off the bench and VJ Edgecombe finished with 15 points, 8 board, 8 assists and 3 steals. Philadelphia improved its record to 9-3, 5-2 at home.

For the Raptors, RJ Barrett led all scorers with 31 points (shooting 4-for-7 on 3s) in addition to 14 rebounds and 5 assists. Immanuel Quickley added 26 points, 4 rebounds and 6 assists. Collin Murray-Boyles scored 14 points with 4 assists and 5 blocks. Toronto dropped to 5-7 (3-4 on the road) with the defeat.

While Embiid played, LeBron James sat out his third game of the early 2026-27 season on Thursday. James was listed on the Sixers' injury report as out with "low back/sciatica management," which had been the reason he's sat two of Philadelphia's previous 12 games this season.

The 41-year-old was listed as questionable until participating in Thursday's shootaround. He was then downgraded.

Thursday's game was not part of a back-to-back for the 76ers, so that was not a consideration in sitting James and possibly resting Embiid. Philadelphia last played on Tuesday, losing 123-101 to the Boston Celtics in the hyped first meeting between former teammates Jaylen Brown and Jayson Tatum.

However, the Sixers do play back-to-back games on Sunday and Monday, hosting the Indiana Pacers then visiting the Brooklyn Nets.

So far this season, James is averaging 15.1 points, 7.2 rebounds and 6.5 assists on 43% shooting from the floor. Embiid is scoring 17.7 points per game with 9.3 rebounds and 3.8 assists. He is also shooting 37%, while making only 4 of 33 (12%) 3-point attempts.

Without Embiid, Philadelphia would have been short-handed at center with Adem Bona (cervical spine sprain) and Dominick Barlow (low back stress reaction) also out for Thursday. Perhaps that influenced the decision to play him against the Raptors.

James started at center when Embiid was sidelined on Oct. 25 versus the Detroit Pistons. Had Embiid been out, the Sixers would have faced going small with 6-foot-7 Jabari Walker in the middle (who subbed in when Embiid left the game), or perhaps played 7-foot-6 Tacko Fall for only the second time this season. Fall played one minute in Thursday's win.
