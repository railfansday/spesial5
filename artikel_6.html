William and Kate meet Indian Prime Minister Modi in New Delhi

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Prince and Princess of Wales have met Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi as part of their three-day visit to India.

William and Catherine arrived in New Dehli on Wednesday for the final leg of their first official oversees trip together in four years.

The meeting was a late addition to the couple's schedule and came after Modi confirmed he would be back from a scheduled trip to the Philippines in time to see the royals.

It comes a day after the royal couple were in Mumbai for the annual Earthshot Prize, which was founded by the prince and remains one of his biggest personal projects.

When he announced earlier this year that it was going to be held in India for the first time, the prince highlighted India's leadership in climate innovation, its biodiversity and its massive youth population as the key reasons for choosing the country.

A Kensington Palace spokesperson said ahead of the Modi meeting: "For the Prince, it represents another significant engagement as he continues to take on a growing role on the international stage,

"We've seen him increasingly support the UK's diplomatic relationships through his overseas engagements, and this meeting will provide an opportunity to build on those connections at the highest level."

The couple last met Modi on their official visit to India in 2016.

On Tuesday the prince and princess were in Mumbai to announce the winners of the annual Earthshot Prize.

The event, founded by Prince William in 2020, was hosted by Karan Johar, presenter of The Traitors India and one of the country's most well-known film makers.

Five separate winners were named during Tuesday's ceremony and each will receive Â£1m to scale up their projects.

Speaking at the ceremony, Prince William said the 2020s were the time when positive change becomes "irreversible", adding history was "not written by challenges alone", but also by those who rose to meet them.

The princess presented on the five awards and, as she did so, said the entrepreneurs, organisations and scientists tackling the environmental crisis brought her "hope".
