Why Ryan Phillippe Won’t Have Any More Kids Despite “Baby Fever”

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Ryan Phillippe, already dad to two adult kids with Reese Witherspoon and a teen daughter with ex Alexis Knapp, shared how time with a baby relative has left him with some serious “baby fever.”

Ryan Phillippe knows what he’s doing next summer—and it doesn’t involve changing diapers.

Though the Cruel Intentions star’s three kids have reached a more independent stage of life—he and ex-wife Reese Witherspoon share Ava, 27, and Deacon, 23, while he’s also dad to daughter Kai, 15, with ex Alexis Knapp—time with younger relatives has left him wishing for another chapter of parenthood.

“Good morning from Uncle Philz & baby Elkie,” Phillippe, 52, wrote on Instagram Nov. 10 alongside a picture of himself holding the youngster poolside. “I really do be having baby fever.”

However, the I Know What You Did Last Summer alum admitted that is likely not in the cards as he currently has “no woman” and believes he’s “too old.”

But it comes as no surprise that Phillippe would be down to continue growing his family. After all, he’s been vocal about how intentional he’s been about raising his kids.

“Well, you hope that while they're young and they are in the same house as you, that you instill enough of the values and the sensibilities that they'll need when they go out into the world,” he told Scary Mommy in February. “As a parent, you're always there for them and trying to guide and give them advice, but there has to be an element of release once they get over a certain age.”

And now that Phillippe is watching his eldest kids move through the world on their own, he confirmed, “I feel good and proud of who they are and how myself and my ex-wife have prepared them.”

Plus, the 9-1-1 Nashville actor is glad to see Ava and Deacon follow in his and Witherspoon’s acting footsteps, because as he previously put it, “What makes me proudest is seeing all three of my kids success and achieve.”

"I'm wanting and hoping that they have happy and well-balanced lives," he told People in May 2025. "That's where the biggest source of pride in my life comes from: seeing them achieve and be good people."
