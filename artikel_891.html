Chelmsford City captain Cav Miley missed a match after injuring himself in unusual circumstances, falling down a badger hole in training.

The 31-year-old had been due to start the non-league side’s game against Walton & Hersham on Saturday but was forced to sit it out after injuring his ankle in the awkward fall.

Manager Mark Cooper said after the 2-2 draw: “Cav Miley fell down a badger hole in training and twisted his ankle, so he couldn’t play.”

Miley was back in the squad for Monday night’s National League South game at home against Maidstone United, a 1-0 victory, after which Cooper elaborated.

He told Chelmsford’s in-house YouTube channel: “Everyone is telling me it's gone viral, but I told the truth - he fell down a badger hole. I was told at the start it was a fox hole, but then the guy at where we train told me it's a badger hole.

“We need to make sure we fill all of them in before we train now.”

There was a further scare during the Maidstone game as Miley went down injured in the 23rd minute, with play halted for him to receive treatment. Chelmsford posted news of the stoppage on their X account, prompting a fan to reply, “Not another badger hole”.

Fortunately the captain was able to quickly return to the pitch and play resumed, with Jack Barham scoring a second-half penalty against his former club to secure all three points.

Miley, who previously played in the National League for Eastleigh and Southend United, joined Chelmsford this summer.

The Clarets are seventh in the league after playing 18 games.
