Liverpool work on new goalkeeper signing after Alisson exit news

Liverpool have set their sights on signing a new goalkeeper as they prepare for Alisson to leave, sources have told Football Insider.

The 34-year-old is out of contract at the end of the season, and is attracting interest from a number of clubs across world football who could even make moves in January.

Andoni Iraola still views the Brazil international as an important part of his starting XI, having played every game in the Premier League and Champions League this season.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Liverpool are working on signing a replacement as new concerns have crept in.

The shot-stopper has made some high-profile mistakes already this season, and there is a growing feeling the time may have come for him to finally move on.

Juventus were leading the race to sign Alisson during the summer, and their interest has lingered, while clubs in his native Brazil could move to lure him away from Liverpool.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider in September that Liverpool have no plans to extend Alisson's stay and are preparing for his exit.

There are lingering concerns about his age and injury history which could force the Reds to consider his future, with his current deal set to expire next summer.

As part of their preparations, Liverpool are now eyeing a number of potential replacements for Alisson, and will be ready to land his replacement next summer.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "They're doing their homework on quite a few goalkeepers with Alisson's contract situation.

"He's 34 now, he came close to leaving in the summer when there was strong interest from Juventus, but Liverpool took up that option to extend his contract for another 12 months.

"He hasn't been at his best in recent games, he's made a couple of mistakes and now there's been talk of a move back to his native Brazil.

"So Liverpool are doing their homework on a number of players and they are putting contingency plans in place as they prepare for Alisson to leave in the summer."

Liverpool have been linked with a number of potential goalkeeper signings as they prepare to lose Alisson at the end of the season, with work ongoing behind the scenes.

During the summer, they were linked with moves for the likes of James Trafford, Diogo Costa and Bart Verbruggen, but ultimately opted against making formal moves.

Meanwhile, Football Insider revealed in September that Giorgi Mamardashvili is pushing to become the permanent number-one at Liverpool under Iraola.

The Georgia international has impressed whenever called upon to fill in for his Brazilian counterpart, and is staking a claim to make the position his own next season.
