Joao Pedro Injury: Chelsea Forward Misses Brentford Defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Joao Pedro missed Chelsea’s 3-0 Premier League defeat to Brentford with a knee problem, and the injury has since ruled him out of Brazil’s squad for the friendlies against Australia and India.

He was absent from Xabi Alonso’s confirmed lineups against Brentford, with Chelsea turning to other forward options instead.

Brazil’s coaching staff confirmed the withdrawal, pointing to the same knee issue that kept him out of the derby. Chelsea have not set a firm return date for Joao Pedro, leaving supporters to wait for further updates during the international break.

The Joao Pedro injury situation took a new turn when the Standard reported that he is seeing a specialist during the international break to speed up his recovery.

According to the Standard, the forward is using this pause in the calendar to get ahead of the problem rather than wait for Chelsea to resume domestic action.

Chelsea have not issued their own statement on the specialist visit, and the club’s official line on Joao Pedro’s fitness remains limited to his withdrawal from matchday duty.

Full detail on the reported treatment can be found in football.london’s reported injury update, which first carried the specialist claim alongside Xabi Alonso’s comments on the striker’s season.

Until Chelsea confirm otherwise, the Standard’s report stands as the clearest picture of how the club are handling the knee concern.

It suggests a measured approach, with recovery prioritised over rushing Joao Pedro back into contention for the next fixture.

Before the injury forced him out, Xabi Alonso had made his admiration for Joao Pedro clear. The Chelsea manager ranked the forward among the Premier League’s elite strikers, alongside Manchester City’s Erling Haaland.

“If you count how many top strikers we have in the Premier League, I’m sure that João is there, Haaland for sure is there.

“So I would rate him that way. He has a very strong goal this year: to have a great season, to score many goals, and to be a nominee for not just this month but more months. I am not surprised, but really happy with the kind of player that we have here with João.”

With Joao Pedro sidelined, Alonso must lean again on the depth built into his squad.

His approach to rotation, part of the squad management this season, faces another test while his in-form forward is unavailable.
