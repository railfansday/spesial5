Crowborough, Peacehaven and Telscombe take steps in right direction

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Crows picked up three points and The Tye one following weekend defeats for both teams.

Sean Muggeridge’s Crows won 2-0 at Ashford United thanks to goals by Rushaar Samuel-Smikle and Harry Forster.

The three points were enough to lift them out of the relegation zone, which they’d been left in after a 4-1 reverse at Whitstable on Saturday, in which Samuel-Smikle was also on target.

Peacehaven’s midweek haul was not as impressive but they took a point from a 3-3 draw at home to Erith Town after a crazy final half-hour.

Lewis Beebee put The Tye ahead on 66 minutes and Connor Tighe made it 2-0 two minutes later. Erith hit back with goals in the 73rd and 77th minutes then scored what looked like being a winner in the 92nd minute. But Harry Docherty pounced four minutes later to rescue a point.

Crowborough host Deal Town tomorrow and The Tye go to Faversham.
