Venice Audience Award Winner ‘Ish’ Gets North American Deal With Watermelon Pictures

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

EXCLUSIVE: Watermelon Pictures has acquired North American distribution rights from Global Constellation to coming-of-age drama Ish out of the American Film Market.

The feature directorial debut of British artist and filmmaker Imran Perretta won the Audience Award at the 2025 Venice International Film Festival’s Critics’ Week.

The film explores friendship, identity and the challenges of adolescence through the experiences of two young boys whose lives are forever changed by a traumatic encounter with police. The deal was negotiated by Munir Atalla, Head of Production & Acquisitions at Watermelon Pictures, and Fabien Westerhoff, Managing Director Film of Global Constellation, which represents worldwide sales on behalf of the filmmakers. Watch on Deadline

Co-written by Perretta and Tony and BAFTA-winning writer Enda Walsh (Hunger), Ish “follows 12-year-old best friends Ish and Maram, whose close bond is shaken following a distressing police stop and search. As the incident’s emotional repercussions unfold, the boys struggle to understand their changing relationship while confronting questions of masculinity, loss and belonging.”

The film introduces newcomers Farhan Hasnat and Yahya Kitana in the lead roles, alongside Avin Shah, Sudha Bhuchar and BRIT Award-nominated singer-songwriter Joy Crookes, making her feature film acting debut.

Pic is produced by BIFA-winning Breakthrough Producer Dhiraj Mahey of Primal Pictures (The Ghoul) and Bennett McGhee of Home Team (Mogul Mowgli), in association with Mike Goodridge of Good Chaos (Triangle of Sadness). The film was developed and financed by BBC Film and the BFI, alongside additional financing from Calculus Media and Dorian Grinspan’s Out of Order. Eric Kuhn (Grand Theft Hamlet) serves as executive producer.

The film features black-and-white cinematography by Jermaine Canute Edwards (My Father’s Shadow), editing by Adam Biskupski (Mogul Mowgli), and sound design by Jens Rosenlund Petersen and Academy Award and BAFTA winner Nina Hartstone (Bohemian Rhapsody). Perretta also composed the film’s original score.

“Ish is a beautifully realized and deeply affecting film that captures the complexities of growing up with remarkable honesty and sensitivity,” said Munir Atalla, Head of Production & Acquisitions at Watermelon Pictures. “Imran Perretta brings an extraordinary visual perspective to a story that feels both intimate and universal, exploring friendship, identity and the lasting impact of injustice. We are proud to champion his feature debut and introduce this powerful film to North American audiences.”

Faben Westerhoff, MD Film, Global Constellation, added: “Imran’s transition into feature filmmaking is a masterclass in visual storytelling and emotional honesty. We are delighted to partner with Watermelon on this release. They are the ideal North American distributor for Ish, fearless champions of distinctive, culturally resonant cinema that strikes a deep chord with contemporary audiences.”
