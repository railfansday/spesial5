‘Daredevil’ Star Deborah Ann Woll Officially Joins Prime Video’s ‘God of War’

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Deborah Ann Woll isn't just getting her own spin-off. Ahead of starring in God of War Laufey next year, the Daredevil star has been tapped to reprise her role as Kratos' wife, Faye, in Prime Video's upcoming God of War adaptation. This will be her third such appearance as the mysterious mother of Atreus, first joining the beloved PlayStation franchise in the 2018 reboot's sequel, God of War Ragnarök. Given that the series under Ronald D. Moore will be starting from the very beginning of Kratos' story, though, she's integral to have aboard, as Faye's passing sets the events of the Norse saga in motion, and Woll is quite literally the face of the character.
