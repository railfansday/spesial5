Movie Buffs, Take Note — Criterion Collection Discs Are Up to 50% Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

Between price hikes and the constant flow of movies and TV shows on and off various streaming services, it’s no surprise that there’s a devoted community of collectors committed to physical media. Nowhere is that more evident than in the fanbase around the Criterion Collection, which restores and reissues acclaimed classics and arthouse films.

And while their titles tend to command a premium, the Criterion Collection is currently available for up to 50% off at Barnes & Noble. For titles you can’t find at Barnes & Noble, Criterion has discounts on its own site as well, although some of the discounts are smaller.

Criterion Collection is perhaps best known for releasing arthouse titles and classic films, but it also distributes recent popular films, including KPop Demon Hunters. The sale is also a great time to pick up box sets, which are always the most expensive offerings. Some popular box sets, including Godzilla and Stanley Kubrick, sold out online, but you can still find other popular sets for half off.
