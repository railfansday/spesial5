CMA Awards 2026: The Complete Winners List

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The 60th annual CMA Awards aired live from Nashville on Wednesday, Nov. 18. Ella Langley was the leading nominee, with nine, and the Alabama singer nearly ran the board. She took home Single of the Year and Song of the Year for her juggernaut hit “Choosin’ Texas,” and Music Video of the Year for the song’s video. Langley also won Album of the Year for Dandelion, and was named Female Vocalist of the Year. The biggest surprise was when Langley’s name was called for the night’s top prize: Entertainer of the Year.

Her only loss? Musical Event of the Year, which went to Hardy, Tim McGraw, Eric Church, and Morgan Wallen for “McArthur.”
