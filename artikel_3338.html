FedEx Cargo Jet Veers Off Runway While Landing In Miami

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A FedEx cargo plane had a rough landing at Miami International Airport Wednesday as it veered off the runway -- but thankfully nobody was injured, according to police.

The Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office said officers responded to the airport Wednesday night after the cargo jet grinded to a halt on a patch of grass.

The agency also said Miami-Dade fire rescue raced to the scene ... but there were no injuries to tend to.

Witnesses posted videos and photos online showing the plane parked in the grass and surrounded by police and emergency crews.

A FedEx spokesperson tells TMZ ... "FedEx Flight 910 from Memphis to Miami experienced an issue upon landing Wednesday evening at Miami International Airport. We are grateful that our crew is safe and there are no reported injuries."

The Federal Aviation Administration issued an advisory saying all incoming flights to Miami International Airport were grounded from 8:16 PM until at least 9:35 PM ET.
