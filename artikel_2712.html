Broncos move to 8-2 with 38-24 victory over Raiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Raiders gave the Broncos plenty to handle on Sunday, but in the end, Denver’s defense came through to put the game away.

A pick-six off of Fernando Mendoza late in the fourth quarter sealed the Broncos’ 38-24 victory.

The Raiders got down 17-0 early but came back to tie the game up at 17-17 midway through the third quarter. The Broncos responded by ripping off a pair of touchdowns, one of which came off a turnover on downs in Denver territory when Mendoza’s pass on fourth-and-2 fell incomplete.

But Las Vegas wasn’t done, scoring on a 7-yard pass from Mendoza with 4:29 left in the contest. Then the defense got a stop, setting up a potential game-tying drive just before the two-minute warning.

However, after that short break, Mendoza was looking for Tre Tucker when his pass was instead picked off by Ja’Quan McMillan — who returned it for a game-sealing, 35-yard pick-six.

Broncos quarterback Bo Nix finished the contest 19-of-35 for 225 yards with a touchdown and an interception. Evan Engram led with 74 yards on six catches. Running back Jonah Coleman had 111 yards on 24 carries plus four catches for 18 yards.

On the other side, Mendoza finished 18-of-37 for 267 yards with three touchdowns and one pick in his second start. He was sacked five times. His first touchdown pass went to Ashton Jeanty, who had 90 yards on five catches to go along with 79 yards on 11 carries. His 46-yard run was his longest of the season.

Jack Bech caught eight passes for 88 yards.

With the win, the Broncos move to 8-2. They’ll have a short week, as they’ll face the Steelers on the road for next week’s Black Friday game.

The Raiders fall to 4-7. They’ll be on the road again next week in primetime, with the matchup against the Browns flexed to Sunday Night Football.
