Lions Week 9 preview: Breaking down Dolphins’ offensive, defensive schemes

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Detroit Lions (5-2) and Miami Dolphins (1-6) will square off in Week 9, with the Lions looking to extend their three-game winning streak—and the Dolphins seeking their second win of the season.

"(Dolphins Head) Coach (Jeff) Hafley's done a good job in his first year," Lions coach Dan Campbell said on Wednesday. "A lot of similarities to—just reminds me a little of what we were when we came in. They're young, they're hungry, they're active. They run, they hit, they hustle, they finish, try to play disciplined ball, and they're playing that way."

Let's take a closer look at the Dolphins' coaching staff, the scheme they run, and scouting notes from their most recently viewed games.

"Offensively, they really have an athletic offensive line that's got mass. They run a versatile scheme. (RB Ollie) Gordon is a heavy back. You give him a lane, and he can hit it, hit it with speed, build the speed, and that's where he gets dangerous. You let him hit the line of scrimmage with any type of speed and any crease and he can go. […] There's a ton of this eye candy and motions and cutting the defense off. It's a good scheme and they run it well, screens, gadgets, play pass off of it. (QB) Malik (Willis)'s done a heck of a job with all that stuff. (WR) Malik Washington, they got young receivers outside that are growing, up and coming, growing. Washington is a good football player, plays in the slot, he can play outside and he's also their punt returner. So, he's pretty dynamic in that regard. Look, this is a hungry team and they're playing good football."
