Laquon Treadwell posts touchdown in Week 10 vs. Dolphins

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Indianapolis Colts wide receiver Laquon Treadwell had three receptions for 24 yards and a touchdown on three targets in Week 10 against the Miami Dolphins.

Why it Matters: With the trades of Keenan Allen and Josh Downs at the NFL trade deadline, Treadwell bumped up two spots on the depth chart, and he now has a prominent role, at least until Alec Pierce is ready to return. He is worth a look, tentatively, as a low-end WR5 in fantasy leagues of 12 or more teams. The Colts play the Houston Texans on Thursday night in Week 11.
