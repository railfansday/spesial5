Steelers WR DK Metcalf appears to shout out Aaron Rodgers via sign language after scoring TD: 'Stand on business 8'

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

In case you somehow haven't heard, Aaron Rodgers was not playing for the Pittsburgh Steelers during their game on Black Friday. That doesn't mean his presence wasn't felt.

Steelers wide receiver DK Metcalf, one of Rodgers' closest teammates during his time in Pittsburgh, appeared to shout out the quarterback after scoring a touchdown in the first quarter against the Denver Broncos. After catching the 22-yard pass from new starter Mason Rudolph, viewers noted Metcalf making a series of hand signals.

The translation via American Sign Language: "Stand on business, eight." Eight was Rodgers' jersey number with the Steelers.

Pittsburgh entered the game carrying some very public baggage, after their starting quarterback burned every bridge in sight on his way out. The Steelers responded with a dominant performance — including two defensive touchdowns in the first half — thumping the Denver Broncos 27-5. Metcalf caught three passes for 36 yards and the touchdown in the win.

DJ Metcalf caught a touchdown pass from one quarterback then sent a message to another.

The trouble reportedly began when Rodgers made some sort of "ultimatum," as Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy put it, on Tuesday, which the team did not heed. Instead, Pittsburgh opted to bench Rodgers and start Rudolph against Denver, which led to Rodgers appearing on his friend Pat McAfee's show on ESPN on Wednesday and unleashing a torrent of grievances against McCarthy. The Steelers released Rodgers not long after.

Even more details were provided during the game Friday, as broadcaster Al Michaels shared the contents of a text from Rodgers, who claimed McCarthy ghosted him until the benching was announced and that the Steelers barred him from the facilities on Wednesday.

A larger question to come out of the dispute was how the rest of the Steelers players would respond to a dispute between quarterback and head coach. A lopsided win was a decent sign the team wasn't tearing itself apart, but Metcalf's gesture indicates Rodgers still has support from the roster.
