Georgia football defender has no idea how the CFP even works

The Georgia Bulldogs had already secured a spot in the SEC Championship Game when defensive lineman Joseph Jonah-Ajonye fielded a question Tuesday about the College Football Playoff rankings. The junior smiled at the podium and offered a straightforward answer about how he approaches the postseason.

Georgia entered the day at 10-1 after a 34-6 road win over the South Carolina Gamecocks. The Bulldogs held the No. 1 spot in the previous rankings, and the selection committee was scheduled to release its fourth set of rankings Tuesday night.

DawgNation's Connor Riley shared a video of the exchange on X, formerly Twitter. Jonah-Ajonye explained where his attention goes when the discussion turns to the playoff.

Jonah-Ajonye made five tackles and recorded half a sack against South Carolina. The former five-star recruit also earned SEC Co-Defensive Lineman of the Week honors earlier this season after recording his first career sack and forcing a fumble on the same play against the Alabama Crimson Tide. The 6-foot-4, 275-pound junior played high school football at Oak Ridge in Texas. He made two tackles in the win over the Oklahoma Sooners and one solo tackle against Florida.

The Bulldogs finished SEC play at 8-1 and close the regular season Saturday against the Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets. Georgia won last year's meeting 16-9 at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta. That win marked Georgia's eighth straight in the rivalry, setting a school record against Tech. The rivalry returns to Sanford Stadium this year, with kickoff set for 3:30 p.m. ET/12:30 p.m. PT on ABC.

The Bulldogs close the regular season against Georgia Tech in Athens, then head to Atlanta for their sixth straight SEC Championship Game appearance. After conference championship weekend, the selection committee will unveil the 12-team playoff bracket on December 6, setting the first-round matchups and byes.
