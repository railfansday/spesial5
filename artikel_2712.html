Kyren Williams makes it 13-3 Rams in second quarter

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Rams have had the ball three times and all three drives have ended in points.

Running back Kyren Williams finished off the third possession with a seven-yard sprint into the end zone that pushed the Rams’ lead over the Packers to 13-3 with 11:29 to play in the first half. It’s the first time the Rams have scored on their first three possessions this season and it’s also the first time the Packers have allowed their opponents points on their first three drives.

Most of the other movement on the drive came through the air. Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford completed passes to Williams, tight end Davis Allen, wide receiver Konata Mumpfield, and tight end Tyler Higbee for first downs before Williams’s touchdown. The throw to Williams came just before Stafford took a hit from Packers defensive end Micah Parsons.

Stafford is 10-of-12 for 87 yards while Jordan Love is 6-of-10 for 70 yards, including a 32-yarder to Christian Watson that set up Trey Smack’s 54-yard field goal.
