The Jacksonville Jaguars are going all-in.

Cardinals wide receiver Michael Wilson will miss the remainder of the 2026 NFL season with a neck injury, head coach Mike LaFleur announced on Monday.

LaFleur said that Wilson suffered the injury on the fourth play of the game in Week 9's loss to the Seattle Seahawks but played through it. He finished the game with six catches for 60 receiving yards and played 83% of offensive snaps.

It's a brutal outcome for the fourth-year wideout, who signed a three-year extension with the team this offseason, reportedly worth $75 million. He had a breakout campaign in 2025 with 1,006 receiving yards and seven touchdowns, outperforming former teammate Marvin Harrison Jr.

With Wilson out for the year, Harrison since traded and Kendrick Bourne out for the season, the Cardinals are now looking remarkably thin at wide receiver.
