NFL slams NBC's Mike Florio, Chris Simms for 'offensive' Mike Vrabel joke

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The NFL is not happy with one of its primary media partners.

During a recent episode of "Pro Football Talk Live" on NBC Sports, Mike Florio and Chris Simms made a joke at the expense of New England Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel that included a thinly veiled reference to Adolf Hitler, although neither mentioned his name.

"He got the German mustache to go along with that right now, too," Simms said during a Nov. 17 edition of the show while discussing the Patriots' 35-10 loss to the Detroit Lions on Sunday in Munich, Germany. "So he was hitting all themes there over in Germany."

"You gotta be careful trimming that thing," Florio said. "Yeah, if you're in Germany, you gotta be careful trimming the sides of that thing."

Between a lot of laughs, Simms and Florio kept the bit going for a few more seconds, although an off-air producer must have appeared to wish the duo didn't go down that road.

"It was eighty years ago," Florio said. "Let us have some fun, for crying out loud."

The exchange did not sit well within NFL headquarters.

"We found the remarks inappropriate and offensive," the NFL said in a statement to USA TODAY Sports from executive vice president of communications, policy and public affairs Jeff Miller

Both Florio and Simms contribute to NBC's elbow coverage of "Sunday Night Football." Simms had previously been part of "Football Night in America" but was removed from the show this offseason. The duo apologized Thursday.

"On yesterday's (Nov. 17) PFT Live show, we made comments that some found offensive and we apologize," Simms and Florio said in a joint statement via NBC Sports.
