Chiefs sign RB E.J. Smith, Emmitt's son, to the practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Chiefs signed running back E.J. Smith to their practice squad on Tuesday, according to the NFL’s personnel notice.

Smith is the son of the NFL’s all-time leading rusher, Emmitt Smith.

The Texas A&M product signed with the Chiefs as an undrafted free agent after the 2026 draft. He spent the offseason and training camp with the team but was waived with an injury designation Sept. 4.

He had remained a free agent until Tuesday.

Smith spent four seasons at Stanford and two at Texas A&M. He finished his career with 207 carries for 969 yards and nine touchdowns and added 470 receiving yards and a touchdown.

Smith does not have a jersey number yet on the Chiefs’ roster, but he won’t wear his father’s No. 22. Rookie defensive back Jadon Canady has that number in Kansas City. Smith wore No. 35 in training camp, but practice squad running back Nate Carter has that number.

The Chiefs also signed tight end John FitzPatrick to the practice squad. They terminated the practice squad contracts of wide receivers Jacob DeJesus and Bub Means.
