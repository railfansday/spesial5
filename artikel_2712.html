Zack Baun remains out of Eagles practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Eagles remained without a key defensive player at practice on Thursday.

Linebacker Zack Baun has missed both days of practice this week with a back injury. Baun was injured during the team’s Week 9 loss to the Giants and the time off during the bye week wasn’t enough to get him right.

The Eagles were also without linebacker Smael Mondon (ankle) and they will release injury designations for their game against the Steelers on Friday.

Defensive back Michael Carter (abdomen), wide receiver Britain Covey (knee), cornerback Jonathan Jones (hamstring), running back Will Shipley (foot), linebacker Nolan Smith (illness), and wide receiver Dontayvion Wicks (ankle) were limited participants.

Running back Saquon Barkley (hamstring), linebacker Jihaad Campbell (hamstring, quad), linebacker Jonathan Greenard (ankle), linebacker Jalyx Hunt (ankle), quarterback Tanner McKee (rib), safety Drew Mukuba (knee), and wide receiver DeVonta Smith (hamstring) remained full participants.
