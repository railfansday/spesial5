10 Takeaways from the Celtics horrible loss to the Warriors

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

For the first time since May of 2021, the Boston Celtics have lost 4 games in a row. This is also the longest losing streak of the Joe Mazzulla Era. The craziest thing about this streak for the Celtics is the fact that every loss has felt like its been following the same script.

The starting lineup for the last time the Celtics lost 4 games in a row was Jayson Tatum, Evan Fournier, Tristan Thompson, Tremont Waters, and Aaron Nesmith. That's how long its been since we've seen this amount of ineptitude from this team.

Start the game with the lead, make it close by halftime, hot shooting stretch to start the third quarter, offense starts to fall apart at the end of the third quarter, offense falls apart completely and lose the game. This script was followed again against the Warriors after making it a 2-point deficit at halftime. Boston shot out to a hot start in the third quarter, Golden State closed the third on an 8-0 run, Boston then only scored 12 points in the fourth and lost in the final minutes.

This was the same exact way they lost to the Orlando Magic in the first game of the losing streak. This losing streak has me feeling like Bill Murray from Groudhog Day, just reliving the same events over and over again. This has been brutal stretch for the Celtics and I am waiting for "I Got You Babe" by Sonny & Cher to stop playing over the alarm clock.

If you only watched the first half of this game, you saw arguably the best half of basketball Jayson Tatum played all season. Tatum was incredible, finishing with 23 points on 8-13 shooting from the field and 4-6 from three, scoring over half the Celtics points and with his teammates giving him no help at all. Tatum's final shot of the half was a three-point shot from the logo that put put him 8th on the Celtics all-time scoring list, passing Bill Russell.

The second half was a different story, finishing with 4 points on 0-7 shooting. The third quarter saw Tatum at least start to facilitate, finishing with 4 assists as the bench started to help him out a little bit. He entered the game again at the 6:32 mark of the fourth quarter and had a chance to tie the game at the free throw line with 55.6 seconds left but went 1-2. That ended up being costly as he missed a wide open three with 29.6 second left and the potential game winning fadeaway as time expired.

It was a hard second half to watch and once the Warriors neutralized Tatum in this game, it felt like the Celtics offense was completely out of sync. Part of this is on Tatum who needs to start hitting these shots in crunch time. In the second half of games on this losing streak Tatum is shooting 8-26 (30.8%) from the field and 3-14 (21.4%) from three. The other part of this is the fact that his supporting cast is just not helping him enough.

Another thought I have on Tatum is maybe Joe Mazzulla should change the rotations so Tatum can play more of the fourth quarter. The non-Tatum minutes to start the fourth quarter have been a brutal watch and maybe if he comes in the game earlier he can find a groove faster. But overall, this was a game that could have been great and just ended in frustration for Tatum.

Another underlying problem the Celtics have had during this losing streak is the lack of offensive rebounding in the fourth quarter and rebounding in general from anyone not named Jayson Tatum or Neemias Queta. The Celtics got out-rebounded by Warriors 45-41 in this game but the most costly of these rebounds came in the fourth quarter when Golden State had 5 offensive rebounds.

The Celtics had an 86-83 lead with 3:46 left in the game before the Warriors had a possession where they grabbed two offensive rebounds leading to a Brandin Podziemski fadeaway that made it a 1 point game. Hugo Gonzalez missed a three for the Celtics and on the next Warriors possession, Podziemski hit the three that gave the Warriors an 88-86 lead with 2:38 left. This would be the game winning three and it was all built on the momentum of the Warriors work on the offensive glass.

This followed the Celtics loss in Sacramento the night before where they lost on a game winning put back layup by Keegan Murray with 5 seconds left in the game. The importance of rebounding late in the game has never shown to be more important than the last 2 games the Celtics have played.

This might not be the most shocking takeaway in the world, but the Celtics are not shooting the three ball well. Over the last 4 games Boston is shooting 68-205 (33.2%) from beyond the arc and their struggles continued in this game against the Warriors. Boston shot 14-47 (29.8%) from three in this game and 1-8 in the fourth quarter.

This is not only an issue on the losing streak but a fundamental issue of the team on a whole. As a team who is so reliant on the three ball, Boston has 4 players who are shooting above league average (35.3%): Sam Hauser (43%), Paul George (40%), Luka Garza (36%), and Baylor Scheierman (36%). The problem with this is that out of these four players, there is only one who is one of the legitimate offensive options. Tatum and Pritchard are shooting 33% while White is shooting 28%. That's 3 of the 4 top offensive players on the team who are below average this season.

Now this could just be a cold stretch to begin the season but it just feels like there's only so long before the numbers can't just even out anymore. At some point the shots are going to have to start falling or more losses are going to be in the Celtics future.

It doesn't bring me joy to say this, but I am officially concerned about Derrick White and his offense. We established earlier that White is shooting 28% from three this season but in this game against the Warriors he struggled again. He was the Celtics leading scorer with 14 points but he shot 6-21 from the field and 2-12 from three. However, the most concerning play was the shot he didn't take.

After Hugo Gonzalez made an incredible steal of the inbound pass with 16.5 seconds left, Derrick White got the rebound and was wide open for a three. Instead of shooting it, he held on to the ball and waited for the rest of the team to come up the court before passing it off leading to the Celtics calling timeout with 8.2 seconds left.

At the time it felt like White was arguably the hottest shooter for the Celtics in the fourth quarter. He scored 7 points up to this point and although he had a possession with back to back misses, it just felt like he needed to put this shot up.

White is shooting 15-49 (30.6%) from the field and 3-25 (12%) from three in three games over the course of the Celtics losing streak which is even worse than his season averages. His defense has still be fine over the this stretch but its really getting hard watch him continue to struggle on the offensive side of the ball for what has felt like the last 2 seasons.

I feel like I have been pretty negative throughout these takeaways so far so I want to shed some light on a few of the positives from this game. One of them being the continued great play of Mike Conley. Against the Kings, Conley turned back the clock for 19 points on 6-7 shooting and against the Warriors, he didn't cool off. In this game he finished with 6 points on 2-2 shooting from three, making him 6-6 from beyond the arc over the last two games.

Conley completely shifted the energy for the Celtics in the third quarter where he played with the starting lineup instead of Hugo Gonzalez. He hit back-to-back threes to begin the quarter and he two man game with Tatum was beautiful on the second one. Tatum made a pass behind his head as he went to the basket and Conley splashed the wide open three.

Not only was Conley trusted in the third quarter, he was also put into the game for the most important last 20 seconds of the game. He didn't make a huge impact in those minutes but it shows the trust that he has earned from Joe Mazzulla that Conley would be put into the most important minutes of a close game. As a 20-year veteran in the NBA, it doesn't shock me Conley is able to still be a productive player. In a stretch of darkness for the Celtics, he has been a small bright light for Boston.

Another guy who I thought made a real impact on the Celtics in the first half was Ron Harper Jr. In 6 minutes during the first half, Harper Jr finished with 5 points on 2-3 shooting from the field and 1-2 shooting from three. He didn't play the rest of the game after this point but I still think his contributions were huge for the time.

He ended the first quarter with a three-pointer at the buzzer and then opened up the second quarter with a dunk off of a great cut from the corner. After that he was playing some incredible defense and felt like he was everywhere on the court.

This great play by Harper Jr was most prominent at the 9:39 mark of the second quarter when he made a great play for a steal. He passed it to White who missed the three, but Harper Jr would grab the offensive rebound and find Pritchard who who drove to the basket for a layup, forcing the Warriors to call a timeout.

I felt like the Celtics could have used his defense and energy at some point in the second half but they decided to use Hugo Gonzalez and Baylor Scheierman more in that situation. Both played fine, but it looked like Harper Jr was on the verge of having a great game and just didn't get the chance to continue it after the first half. Either way, it is nice to see him even in the rotation after his breakout season last year.

Neemias Queta had a quiet overall game on the stat-sheet but was still a huge part of the Celtics staying in the game during the fourth quarter. Queta finished with 6 points, 10 rebounds, and 4 assists on 3-4 shooting. In the fourth quarter we saw him put up 4 points and 4 rebounds with the highlight of the night.

Following a missed three by White, Queta grabbed the offensive rebound and went up with authority for a monster slam over Warriors rookie Yaxel Lendeborg. Queta was the only other player on the Celtics in this game that was a real presence on the glass for the Celtics outside of Jayson Tatum and has continued to show his value to this team.

Watching this game tonight really made me miss Al Horford on the Celtics. It all started pregame when Jayson Tatum went over to him and showed love. He also commented on his son Ean Horford, saying "You got big."

After that hit me in the feels, there was still a game to be played and even in only 18 minutes, I can say Al Horford still has it. He finished with 7 points, 3 rebounds, 2 assists, and 1 block on 3-9 shooting from the field and 1-4 shooting from three. Watching him hit a corner three over Jayson Tatum and block Luka Garza underneath the basket in the first half really made me miss watching him Boston.

Horford spent years taking up the opposing team's best big men and putting it all on the line on the defensive end. He was fearless and it led to the Celtics winning the championship in 2024. Even though he left on not great terms with the Celtics, I still would like to see one more reunion with Big Al before he retires. If it's not Boston, I would like to at least see him be on a team that can compete for a championship. But in Golden State he can mentor the new rookie Lendeborg and the one thing that will never change is the fact that he is just a winner.

Throughout the takeaways I have mentioned all of the concerning trends of the Celtics over the last four games. The shooting has been bad, the rebounding has been bad, the late game execution has been bad. It feels like it's all going down the drain and you would think its time to push the panic button, but I am just not there yet.

Yes, a four-game losing streak is awful but it is also a pretty common thing NBA teams go through sometimes. Good teams have bad stretches and the schedule in the month of November hasn't necessarily helped. This losing streak started on the second half of a back-to-back against the Magic, then Boston went on a west coast road trip where they played a great Nuggets team, and then another back-to-back against the Kings and Warriors.

This doesn't excuse the Celtics poor play by any means, especially against the Kings, but the travel and back-to-backs might have just caught up to them right now. Boston has already played 3 back-to-backs in November and still have one at the end of the month.

Another reason why I don't want to say the season is over is because it is still November. Like Joe Mazzulla said in the postgame press conference in this game, "if the season ended today I would be pretty concerned, but we have 64,63 games left." Just by watching the games, Boston has generated open shots, they just aren't falling. This isn't the first time we've seen this team go through a cold stretch and it probably won't be the last time this season.

It's a long season and there is plenty of time to turn things around. Now do I think the Celtics should make a trade for more help, yes. However, I still think this is a great team and a four game sample size isn't enough for me to say this is who they are.
