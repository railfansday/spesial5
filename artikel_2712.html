Diego Pacheco vs Darius Fulghum: Live blog, round-by-round and results

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Diego Pacheco will return to action tonight as the super middleweight contender faces Darius Fulghum in a Matchroom Boxing main event from Carson, California.

It's the second straight fight at the venue for Pacheco (26-0, 19 KO), who stopped Immanuwel Aleem in this building back in July. He now looks to close his year in the ring with a win over Fulghum (15-1-1, 13 KO), who has sort of learned on the job as a pro and hit some speed bumps in the last year and a half, but is coming off of a May win.

The show will also feature Anthony Olascuaga defending his WBO flyweight title against Yuberjen Martinez, a skilled former Olympic silver medalist who may have more upset potential than expected.

Wil Esco will be on the call tonight, leading the live blog and the round-by-round for the main event, with results and updates for the rest of the card. Join us!
