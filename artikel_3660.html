Andrew Shue Breaks Silence After Marilee Fiebig Wedding: 'New Beginning'

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Andrew Shue is celebrating his new chapter with wife Marilee Fiebig after their respective splits from Amy Robach and T.J. Holmes.

“Somehow, some way, this story has a happy ending and now a beautiful new beginning,” Shue, 59, wrote in a joint Instagram post with Fiebig, 49, on Sunday, March 15, alongside a black-and-white photo from their wedding.

News broke earlier this month that Shue and Fiebig had tied the knot at their 92-acre country estate near Stockton, New Jersey. Days prior to the wedding, Shu and Fiebig announced their engagement.

In an October interview with People, Shue said they were “very grateful and hopeful” about their future.

“I don’t know how it happened, but there’s a happy ending,” Shue added at the time.

Shue and Fiebig’s personal lives entered the spotlight in 2022 when the pair were married to Robach and Holmes, respectively, who were spotted spending time together outside of the GMA3 set. (Both couples were separated at the time and later filed for divorce.)

Robach and Holmes were axed from ABC in the wake of the scandal. The pair have since given fans a glimpse of their relationship via their podcast and before saying “I do” in a private Arkansas ceremony on September 19, according to a county clerk who spoke to Us in October.

Prior to her marriage to Holmes, Robach was previously married to Tim McIntosh from 1996 to 2008. The pair share daughters Ava and Annalise. She was later married to Shue from 2010 to 2023.

Holmes, meanwhile, tied the knot with Amy Ferson in 2004 and the pair welcomed daughter Brianna and son Jaiden. Holmes and Ferson split in 2007. He was married to Fiebig from 2010 to 2023. The exes share a daughter, Sabine.

Since pursuing their romantic relationship, Robach and Holmes have been candid about merging their blended families.

“My little one has known Robach since she was 1, and now she’s knowing Robach in a different way, in a different role, but it’s been the greatest,” Holmes shared during a 2023 episode of their “Amy and T.J.” podcast, adding that he was allowing Sabine to “guide” their dynamic. “I’m always just open. ‘Robach is here, we’re hanging, do you want to do this?’ The past few months she has volunteered. ‘Robach is there, can I come by? You and Robach are doing what? Can I get a piece of that?’ Like, voluntarily.”

Holmes went on to share that it has “been one of the most touching things” to see how much Sabine wants to be “included” and “enjoys time” with Robach.

From Robach’s perspective, she and Holmes have been “very thoughtful” and “very patient” as they navigate their children’s feelings. “But things are good,” she said. “They’re peaceful. We want to continue to build on that.”
