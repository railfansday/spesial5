Securitize Corp. (SECZ -6.99%) reported its inaugural quarterly results as a public company, highlighting the expansion of its blockchain-based financial infrastructure through strategic integrations and product launches. Management reported record volumes of tokenized assets on the platform, attributing the growth to increased institutional adoption by major global asset managers.

The company entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Rivera, an asset-backed financing platform, to introduce credit origination capabilities and connect stablecoin capital with real-world assets. Leadership emphasized that its regulated broker-dealer and transfer agent licenses provide a permanent framework for trading tokenized equities that does not rely on temporary regulatory exemptions. The company also secured registration as an investment advisor and initiated a capital management program through a newly authorized share repurchase plan.
