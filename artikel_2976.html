Hawks keep NBA Cup chances alive, defeat Hornets 111-97

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Atlanta Hawks were in action on Friday evening to face the Charlotte Hornets in an NBA Cup game. The Hawks came into this game 1-1 in group play, while the Hornets are 0-2 and looking for their first win. This was a big game for both teams if they wanted to stay in the race.

The Hawks were still without Kingston Flemings for the third straight game, while the Hornets were without Kon Knueppel, Grayson Allen, Dennis Schroder and Grant Williams.

Jalen Johnson got it started for the Hawks, turning defense into offense for an easy two points.

Dyson Daniels was up next for the Hawks, and he knocked down a 3-pointer to keep things rolling.

It was a six-point lead for the Hawks, but the Hornets were able to erase that quickly, and also extended their own lead to as much as eight halfway through the quarter.

The Hornets continued to extend their lead as the Hawks tried to find some rhythm on offense. They were able to do that late in the quarter and cut down their deficit. Going into the second, the Hawks trailed 31-23.

The Hawks went on a run to start the quarter, and it began with their defense. It was the unit that led the charge, and Zuby Ejiofor threw down a huge dunk over two defenders.

Daniels then got a steal, and it turned into an Aaron Wiggins 3-pointer.

The Hawks had a stretch where they were playing well, and then the Hornets put their foot on the gas and extended their lead once again. The Hawks did their best to stay in it, and Johnson turned up his level of play.

Johnson knocked down a 3-pointer during the Hawks' run.

The Hawks found themselves back in it, and they went into halftime trailing by just 57-56.

It was back and forth for both teams midway through the quarter, with neither team truly taking advantage. The Hawks started to pull away just a little, and it helped with Daniels creating this turnover for Johnson to get the dunk plus the foul.

As the quarter went on, the Hawks began to create more separation, and it was the second unit that led the way.

Going into the fourth, the Hawks led 87-76.

The Hawks continued to extend their lead in the fourth, and they got it to as much as 19 points. Daniels found Aaron Wiggins on the wing for this 3-pointer.

Johnson kept finding an open lane to the rim, and he did it off his fourth steal of the game.

The Hornets made their run down the stretch, but the Hawks continued to execute down the stretch. It helped them to ultimately win the game, and they had a solid point differential in the end to help them in the NBA Cup standings.

Johnson finished with 26 points, six rebounds and six assists, Wiggins finished with 19 points, Okongwi finished with 16 points and seven rebounds, and Daniels finished with 11 points, eight rebounds and 10 assists.

The Hawks will be back in action on Sunday afternoon against the Detroit Pistons.
