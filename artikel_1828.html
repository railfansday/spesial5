Leeds confirm huge injury boost for Chelsea clash as two stars return

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Daniel Farke expects to have Anton Stach in the squad when Leeds visit Chelsea on Saturday.

The Blues have only won one of their last three league outings, with defeats by Sunderland and Tottenham coming either side of a 2-0 victory over Manchester United, but have their work cut out for them if they are to get back to winning ways sa they host Farke’s Leeds.

Stach has proven influential since moving to Yorkshire last summer, with his performances enough to earn him a spot in the national team.

He was forced to miss the home defeat of Tottenham earlier this month, however, with a broken toe.

But Farke has hinted his countryman will be back in the fold for the trip to Stamford Bridge, having spent the past week training with his team-mates.

"He [Stach] was a full week back in training, today,” Farke explained at his pre-match press conference. “Last week, still a bit like part integrated and a bit of individual rehab work.”

He continued, adding that Noah Okafor, who has netted twice this season, is also training: “Both of them had a full training week and it was definitely beneficial.

"Dominic [Calvert-Lewin], since he came back from England duties, is also back in training and no problems, so definitely good news."

That leaves Farke with a fully-fit squad for the trip down to west London, a considerable boost for his chances. of picking up three points.

Chelsea, meanwhile, remain injury-hit. It is not yet clear whether Joao Pedro will be available to return from his own injury, while Moises Caicedo has also picked up an issue on international duty.

Reece James is in recovery from knee surgery, with centre-back Maxence Lacroix also out until the new year.
