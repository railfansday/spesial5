‘Alex Scott is a complete midfielder - he can play for a bigger club than Chelsea’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Bournemouth midfielder Alex Scott has been urged to reject any advances from Chelsea, and sign for a “bigger” club.

Chelsea’s pursuit of Scott was well documented in the summer, with the Blues seeing two bids rejected by their Premier League rivals, even though the England international had turned down an offer of a new contract.

As such, the 23-year-old, who is out of contract at Vitality Stadium in the summer of 2028, could be allowed to depart in the January transfer window for around £100million.

Standard Sport have previously reported that Bournemouth are open to selling their prized midfielder in the new year if Premier League survival is looking likely and Marco Rose’s side are currently seventh in the table following four straight wins.

It is said that Chelsea would be well-placed if they were to pursue their long-standing interest in Scott, but Tottenham great Chris Waddle has advised against a switch to Stamford Bridge because the midfielder is destined for the “biggest teams in Europe”.

“I’ve always liked Alex Scott. I think he’s a lovely, cultured player. There’s no doubt in my mind that in the last eighteen months, the kid’s taken his game to a new level,” Waddle said in a recent interview with iGaming.com.

“I followed him at Bristol City, and when he was there, I was speaking to someone that knew him who told me he was a Tottenham fan.

“Tottenham have been collecting players in the last six months, but he is a top-class player and someone that would walk into any squad. Will they move for him? It seems unlikely given the midfielders they signed in the summer.

“According to reports, Chelsea retain the strongest interest in him. No disrespect to them, but I think he can look higher. I think he’s got the ability to play for the biggest teams in Europe: Real Madrid, Barcelona, Bayern Munich, PSG, Arsenal.
