Broadbridge Heath dig in to end Erith Town’s home run – and move into top four

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

This one was not a match for the football connoisseur – but was one for fans of never-say-die defending as Broadbridge Heath put on a tenacious display of defending against promotion hopefuls Erith Town on a bitter cold Tuesday evening at the JM Clark Stadium.

Chris Simmons’ Heath side knew it would be a difficult match up against a Dockers side on top form, second in the division with games in-hand and with the best home record in the division having played eight and won all eight.

But it was the Bears who had the first shot on target on 9 minutes when Sam Hards was brought down some 30 yards from goal and Ben Royall stepped up to take the resulting free-kick – hitting a left foot shot that was saved comfortably low to his left by the Dockers goalkeeper Javern Pitterson.

On 16 minutes Erith were awarded a free-kick wide on the right and just outside the penalty area, James Dyer played the ball into the danger area where it pinged in and around the 6 yard box before being cleared by Brad Peters and that set the tone for the first half with the hosts having most of the play but unable to break down the solid Bears defence.

Broadbridge Heath did have a few chances, Royall tried to catch Pitterson out with a shot to the near-post but the keeper got back to gather and Ollie Rawlins had a left foot shot saved by Pitterson diving low to his left and it was 0-0 at the break.

The Dockers stepped up a gear in the second half, kicking down the slope, and had the Bears pinned back in their own half for long periods but without really troubling goalkeeper Alfie Hadfield, plenty of goal attempts but everyone charged down by a Broadbridge Heath player defending like their lives depended on it.

On 75 minutes a rare foray deep into the Erith half saw Charlie Connell brought down by a clumsy challenge some 25 yards from goal, Ben Royal stepped up to take the resulting free-kick striking a left foot shot that deflected off the wall wrong-footing Pitterson who could do nothing but watch the ball fly into the net out of his reach.

The hosts continued to push players forward in search of an equaliser but just couldn’t get past the Broadbridge Heath defensive lines who stood firm and dealt with everything the Dockers could throw at them – and it remained 0-1 at the final whistle with Heath continuing their remarkable run of having scored in every league game this season.

Simmons said afterwards: “This one was a bit of a smash and grab, Erith are a very good side and should of gone in front in the first half, defensively we were excellent and our shape and organisation was top notch and in the end got the lucky break the lads deserved.”

Next up for the Bears is the visit of division leaders Faversham Town to the Air Engineering Stadium on Saturday.

BBH: Hadfield, Cotton, Royall, Marino, Peters, Richardson, Connell, Bejashvili (Doughty 71), Rawlins, Holden, Hards.
