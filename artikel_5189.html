US crypto regulation has changed dramatically in 2025, as the Trump administration adopted a more pro-innovation and crypto-friendly approach. Since the start of 2026, the focus has shifted from major congressional reforms to new rules introduced by federal agencies.

This shift followed the CLARITY Act’s failure to pass the Senate. The bill aimed to create a clear regulatory framework for the crypto market, but its failure left regulators to move ahead using their existing powers.

As a result, the SEC and CFTC began introducing new rules, while banking regulators worked on implementing the GENIUS Act ahead of its January 18, 2027, effective date.

In 2026, U.S. regulators have taken a more crypto-friendly approach, focusing on clearer rules and bringing digital assets into the traditional financial system.

In 2026, U.S. crypto regulation moved toward greater institutional clarity, despite Congress failing to pass a comprehensive market-structure bill. The SEC and CFTC advanced new rules, while the GENIUS Act established a clearer framework for stablecoins.

New tax reporting requirements also strengthened compliance. However, major issues remain unresolved, including SEC-CFTC jurisdiction, DeFi regulation, and government Bitcoin purchases.

Overall, 2026 marked a shift from regulatory uncertainty toward broader institutional adoption, with the focus now moving to how crypto and traditional finance can operate within a clearer federal framework.
