The Rivian R2 is a crucial vehicle for the young brand, slotting in below the pricey R1S as a competitor to the bestselling Tesla Model Y.

The boxy design helps the R2 feel incredibly spacious inside, with a truly palatial rear seat with tons of headroom and legroom.

The R2 Performance packs a dual-motor, all-wheel-drive powertrain capable of 656 horsepower and 609 pound-feet of torque.

The punchy powertrain is enough for the R2 Performance to sprint to 60 mph in 3.5 seconds and complete the quarter-mile in 11.4 seconds at 126 mph.

Although the R2 is rapid, its taller ride height and compliant suspension lead to a bit of body roll in the corners, and its Pirelli Scorpion MS Elect tires posted 0.81 g on the skidpad.

Cargo space is also impressive, with the R2 able to swallow 10 carry-on suitcases in our testing, while the rear window rolls down in case you need to fit longer items.
