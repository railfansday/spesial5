New on the Beyond the Brief podcast: Automated license plate readers are a lot of things, but one thing they definitely are is great tool for stalking. Hoo boy!

New cases! Last year, in two separate airport seizures, DEA and CBP agents seized a total of nearly $1.4 mil in cash from IJ client Steven Bailey and his small currency wholesaling business, which, among other things, buys U.S. dollars in Mexico and brings them back to the states—all above board and in compliance with numerous federal rules and reporting requirements. What's not above board is each agency's failure to say what they think Steven did wrong, denying him a chance to meaningfully contest the deprivation of his property. That violates due process, and, in CBP's case, the agency's own rules. Click here to learn more.
