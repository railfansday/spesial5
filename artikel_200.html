Despite restrictive abortion laws, Poland's 'Abortomat' — a locker that dispenses abortion pills — helps women who want to end a pregnancy

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Abortion laws in Poland are very restrictive. Activists there have found a new way to help women wanting to terminate a pregnancy: the "Abortomat," a locker that dispenses abortion pills. But they face fierce opposition.
