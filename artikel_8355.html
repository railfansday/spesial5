Lucky Skier Survives Night in Crevasse With Two Broken Legs

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Thirty-nine-year-old Emeric Ossatte was ski touring alone earlier this week in the Savoie area of the French Alps, near the Italian border. He removed his skis to posthole up a steep couloir in his boots and crampons when an avalanche swept him downhill, depositing him into a crevasse and breaking both his legs.

The Grenoble-area man, who wasn’t carrying a beacon or radio, doffed his backpack and crawled 100m through the crevasse, looking for a way out. But the sides of the crack were too steep, and there was no cell service where he was. He remained beside an air pocket in the crevasse overnight. Meanwhile, when he didn’t check in that evening, his family raised the alarm.

According to a Facebook report by France’s CRS Savoie mountain-rescue unit, they saw the slide path but no immediate sign of Ossatte. The search continued until the middle of the night, when avalanche risk forced the rescuers to suspend their operations.

The following morning, a large contingent of 28 ground rescuers, dogs, three helicopters, and a physician to tend to Ossatte, if they found him alive, converged on the site, according to France 3. They found his gear and dug for five hours until they heard him calling out.

“He was really lucky,” said Major Yoann Haberey of the mountain-rescue team. “We knew he was under there. But we didn’t think we’d find him alive.”

By the time they extracted him, he had been there almost a day and a half. The hypothermic but lucky survivor was flown to the hospital for treatment and surgery on one of his legs.
