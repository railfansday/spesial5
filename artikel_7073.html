Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Job Title: VLSI Design EngineerLocation: United States (Remote)Work Mode: 100% RemoteExperience: 2–8 YearsSalary Range: $95,000 – $165,000 per yearEmployment Type: Full-Time

We are seeking a talented and experienced VLSI Design Engineer to join our semiconductor engineering team in the United States. The ideal candidate will be responsible for designing, developing, verifying, and optimizing digital integrated circuits (ICs), ASICs, and SoC architectures.

The candidate should have strong knowledge of RTL design, Verilog/SystemVerilog, digital logic design, and semiconductor design methodologies. This position offers a fully remote work opportunity for qualified candidates across the United States.

Design and develop digital circuits and RTL modules for ASIC and SoC platforms.

Write, review, and optimize RTL code using Verilog and SystemVerilog.

Perform RTL design, simulation, synthesis, and functional verification.

Work on ASIC design and implementation workflows.

Collaborate with physical design, verification, and semiconductor engineering teams.

Analyze timing, power, and area (PPA) requirements.

Debug design issues and resolve functional and performance-related problems.

Perform logic synthesis and support timing closure activities.

Review design specifications and prepare technical documentation.

Participate in design reviews and continuous improvement initiatives.

2–8 years of experience in VLSI Design or Semiconductor Engineering.

Strong programming knowledge of Verilog and SystemVerilog.

Experience with RTL design and digital logic design.

Understanding of ASIC and SoC design methodologies.

Knowledge of synthesis, simulation, and verification processes.

Familiarity with timing analysis and optimization.

Understanding of CMOS technology and semiconductor fundamentals.

Experience with EDA tools such as Synopsys, Cadence, or Siemens EDA.

Strong analytical, debugging, and problem-solving skills.

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, VLSI, or a related field.

Experience with FPGA prototyping and ASIC implementation.

Knowledge of UVM-based verification methodologies.

Familiarity with Static Timing Analysis (STA).

Understanding of low-power design techniques.

Experience with Python, Tcl, or Perl scripting.

Knowledge of physical design, floorplanning, placement, and routing.

Competitive compensation based on skills and experience.

Retirement savings benefits, where applicable.
