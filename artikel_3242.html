ChatGPT Health feature now works with Mac app, Dots, more

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

OpenAI relaunched its ChatGPT Health feature over the summer after piloting earlier in the year. Next, ChatGPT Health is set to expand to more platforms, including the Mac app and more.

With user permission, ChatGPT Health connects to Apple Health and supported medical records to provide useful context to health-related questions. The feature currently works in the United States on iPhone, iPad, and the web.

However, a recent notice from OpenAI points to ChatGPT Health coming to many more platforms.

For instance, ChatGPT Health will soon work with the desktop version of ChatGPT for the first time. This would bring ChatGPT Health to the Mac app.

OpenAI also says ChatGPT Health will work with Android, ChatGPT Sites, and ChatGPT Dots, the company’s proactive assistant feature.

The move follows a similar pattern with the rollout of ChatGPT Finances, which was originally limited to mobile and the web before recently reaching the Mac app as well.
