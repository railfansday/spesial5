Phibro Animal (PAHC) Q1 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Mineral Nutrition and Performance Products drove growth despite poultry softness.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Phibro Animal Health Corporation (PAHC -6.89%) reported first quarter growth driven primarily by its Mineral Nutrition and Performance Products segments, which offset softer demand in the poultry sector. Management maintained its full-year guidance for fiscal 2027, citing expectations for normalizing poultry demand and the integration of the recently acquired Zoetis medicated feed additive portfolio. The company highlighted a strategic focus on improving cash conversion through inventory reduction and the planned closure of the Chicago Heights manufacturing facility by late 2027.
