Thomas Markle Indicted on Charges of Having Child Sex Abuse Material

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

A federal grand jury has indicted Thomas Markle Sr., the estranged father of Meghan Markle, on charges that he knowingly possessed child sexual abuse material — including images where Markle himself was photographed with prepubescent children.

Markle remains in custody in Florida and has been charged with two counts of possession of child pornography. He is expected to be arraigned in United States District Court in Los Angeles in the coming weeks. According to prosecutors, Markle knowingly possessed 35 mm negatives that included at least one image involving a prepubescent minor and a minor who had not yet attained the age of 12 years old.

The allegations against Markle Sr. first emerged in October when his son Thomas Markle Jr. reported to authorities that he found the inappropriate images at his father’s home in the Philippines. Markle Jr. reportedly told detectives he found “hundreds, if not thousands” of child sexual abuse images where Markle Sr. was living.

In early November, Markle was arrested in Florida amid the investigation and charged with one count of possession of child pornography. Weeks later, the Philippine National Police filed three charges with prosecutors in the city of Cebu, where Markle reportedly lived until May, according to ITV News. While the investigation into Markle originated with his son’s claims in the Philippines, U.S. investigators picked it up after learning that Markle rented two storage units in Los Angeles. According to the U.S. Attorney’s Office, from those units FBI special agents seized approximately 78 items, including slides, film negatives, and books that contained suspected child sex abuse material. Several images allegedly showed Markle with children who were either naked or in various states of undress.

If convicted, Markle faces a statutory maximum sentence of 20 years in federal prison on each count.
