Amanda Seyfried Admits She “Told A Lot Of People” About Her ‘You Can See Everything’ Role: “I’m The Most Indiscreet Person”

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Although Amanda Seyfried‘s buzzy new role was a well-kept secret, discretion was maintained with no thanks to her.

The Oscar-nominated actress admitted that although she “was only involved for like a year” in You Can See Everything, she “told a lot of people” about her surprise appearance in Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim’s critically acclaimed documentary about Elizabeth Holmes.

“I’m the most indiscreet person I’ve ever met in my life,” she said on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon. “How the hell did I manage to keep it under wraps? I told a lot of people. But I guess I could trust them all.”

Seyfried added, “I mean, I did. I told a lot of people. I mean, one person tweeted something and Nathan was like, ‘What’s this?’ And I was like, ‘I don’t know.’ Watch on Deadline

In You Can See Everything, disgraced Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes invites Fielder into her and husband Billy Evans’ home to document everything in the month leading up to the start of her 11-year prison sentence on fraud charges.

After portraying Holmes in Hulu’s The Dropout (2022), Seyfried agreed to appear in the documentary, reprising her performance as Holmes in reenactments of moments Fielder was unable to get on camera.

With Seyfried’s appearance revealed during the doc’s surprise September premiere at Telluride, A24 has been building Oscar buzz for the actress, which would be the first time a documentary re-enactor has been nominated in an acting category.
