Paysafe Limited (PSFE -43.26%) reported third-quarter results characterized by modest top-line growth as expansion in digital wallets and North American iGaming was tempered by regulatory and macroeconomic pressures. Management confirmed the completion of a major debt refinancing in August that extended maturities, though the transaction contributed to higher interest expenses and cash fees. The company adjusted its full-year 2026 outlook downward to account for slowing activity in the cryptocurrency and financial trading sectors during the latter half of the year. The company stated its primary focus remains on reducing net leverage through disciplined capital management and driving higher engagement from its growing active user base.
