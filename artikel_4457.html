2027 Rivian Commercial Van

Although it started off as an exclusive delivery van for Amazon, Rivian is now offering its electric Commercial Van (RCV) for sale to other fleet-based businesses. They don’t come painted in that gray-blue hue like the ones Prime members are accustomed to seeing as their packages get dropped off, but you can buy the same short-wheelbase Delivery 500 and long-wheelbase Delivery 700 variants as Amazon uses.

Rivian claims a driving range of up to 161 miles per charge for the Delivery 500 and 153 miles for the Delivery 700. Both models come with a single electric motor making 320 horsepower and driving the front wheels. The cabin is spartan but thoughtfully laid out, and the cargo area is vast and can be set up to accommodate a variety of business needs. We also think it could make a pretty slick vanlife conversion.

Expert Tip: Electric full-size vans are no longer a novelty, as Ford offers one called E-Transit and Mercedes-Benz makes an EV version of its Sprinter van called eSprinter.

Rivian has made no changes to the Commercial Van for the 2027 model year.

If this year's model is largely unchanged and you're comfortable with a lightly used car, you may want to consider shopping previous model years. A one-to-three-year-old model will likely have a warranty and it'll cost less than buying new.

Both versions of the Commercial Van are powered by a 320-hp electric motor that drives the front wheels. All-wheel drive is not an option. We haven't driven either van yet, but given how quickly Amazon drivers move about, we suspect the electric motor provides adequate acceleration, even when the back is fully loaded with cargo.

Though not intended for towing, the Delivery 500 can haul a decent bit of gear, with a payload of 2663 pounds and a cargo capacity of 487 cubic feet. The longer-wheelbase Delivery 700 offers 652 cubic feet of space but a significantly lower payload rating of 2258 pounds.

Rivian claims the Delivery 500 can go 161 miles on a charge, while the longer Delivery 700 has a maximum range of 153 miles. Both models use a lithium-iron-phosphate battery pack, capable of 100-kW fast- charging, and the vans have both J1772 and CCS ports.

The EPA doesn't publish fuel-economy numbers for commercial vehicles, and Rivian hasn't released estimates. If we get a chance, we'll take the RCV on our 75-mph highway fuel-economy route and update this story with real-world test results.

First Row: Like the exterior, the RCV's interior is simple yet modern. Behind the sliding front door is an elevated driver's chair and a chunky plastic dash that supports a flat-bottom steering wheel and two large screens—one for instruments and the other for infotainment. There's also a fold-down jump seat for the occasional passenger. The RV-like wraparound windshield provides good forward visibility, and a standard 360-degree camera system ensures the driver can see what's behind them.

Cargo Capacity: Behind the roll-up rear door is an empty cavity that fleet owners can spec with shelves and various flooring options. The Delivery 500 van offers 487 cubic feet of cargo volume, matching that of the Ford E-Transit and coming in one cube short of the high-roof, long-wheelbase Mercedes-Benz eSprinter’s volume. The Delivery 700 bests all comers with its 652-cubic-foot hold.

On the dashboard are two large display screens, one serving as a digital gauge cluster and the other as an infotainment touchscreen. The software interface is essentially the same thing you'll find in the Rivian R1T pickup and R1S SUV models. A 360-degree camera system displays images on the central screen to aid in maneuvering.

Rivian equips the Commercial Van with a host of driver-assistance features, including lane-keeping assist, automatic high-beam headlamps, blind-spot monitoring, and adaptive cruise control.

For information about the RCV's crash-test results, visit the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) and Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) websites.

The Commercial Van receives less favorable warranty terms than Rivian’s other vehicles.
