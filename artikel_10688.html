Toronto couple claim landlord is evicting them for “farting too much”

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

A Canadian couple and comedy duo are fighting to keep their home after claiming their landlord is trying to evict them over their excessive farting.

S.E. Grummett and Sam Kruger, who perform together as Creepy Boys, have found themselves in a bizarre dispute involving an air-quality monitor at their apartment in Toronto.

The pair claim their landlord believes they’re smoking indoors, causing unusual readings on the device. However, they insist the actual explanation could be something much more embarrassing. Now, they’re raising money to fight their eviction.

In a GoFundMe campaign, the couple explained that the trouble began in December 2025, when their landlord, who lives upstairs, noticed elevated levels of potentially harmful substances in the air.

The two apartments share ventilation, and their landlord was concerned about the readings because of a recently diagnosed lung condition.

Grummett and Kruger said they tried everything from avoiding cooking to showering elsewhere, but the readings continued to rise.

After researching the issue, they discovered that the monitor could be reacting to normal bodily functions, including breathing and, as it turns out, flatulence.

“We are being wrongfully evicted because we fart too much,” they wrote on their fundraiser. “Really. This is not a joke. We wish we were joking.”

However, their landlord reportedly continued to suspect indoor smoking, which the couple denies. They were eventually served an eviction notice stating that the landlord wanted to reclaim their apartment for personal use.

“We believe this to be in bad faith,” they added. “In Ontario, such evictions have to be heard by the Landlord Tenant Board. So, we are going to court.”

The dispute has since reached Ontario’s Landlord and Tenant Board, where a decision remains pending, according to IBTimes.

Meanwhile, the couple’s fundraiser has surpassed its initial CAD $2,800 target, raising more than CAD $3,400 towards legal expenses.
