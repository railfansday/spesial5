Episode 900 – Midweek stalemate

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Welcome to another episode of the Arsecast – the original Arsenal podcast.

In this episode I’m joined by Tim Stillman to look back on Tuesday’s 1-1 Champions League draw with Borussia Dortmund. We discuss the efficiency of Arsenal’s attack, having scored early through Viktor Gyokeres, we ended up with just one goal from 30 shots. We analyse that particular ‘problem’, especially in relation to previous issues when chance creation was more of an issue for this team, before we chat about the manager’s coaching influence on defenders and midfielders versus attackers. As well as that, we talk about our standing in this season’s CL table, some interesting pre-game comments from Bruno Guimaraes about how he’s adjusting to life at Arsenal and under Mikel Arteta, and more besides.
