How to Watch Billy Evans’ First TV Interview About ‘You Can See Everything’ Online

Billy Evans is speaking out about his portrayal in Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim’s hit documentary “You Can See Everything.”

The longtime partner of disgraced Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes sits down with CBS News chief correspondent Matt Gutman for his first television interview on “CBS News Sunday Morning,” airing Sunday, Nov. 15, at 9 a.m. ET/PT on CBS and streaming on Paramount+. The interview arrives amid renewed public fascination with Holmes and Evans following the A24’s breakout documentary, which follows the couple in the weeks leading up to Holmes’ imprisonment for defrauding Theranos investors. The film has become a box office sensation, grossing more than $80 million worldwide.

“I would have preferred for that not to be the case, but it’s entertainment. I didn’t get to control that edit,” Evans tells Gutman in a preview of Sunday’s interview. He adds that his primary motivation for participating was to show audiences a more personal side of Holmes, who is currently serving a federal prison sentence for fraud.

Viewers can watch the interview live on Paramount+ with a Premium subscription, which costs $13.99 per month and includes access to a live feed of their local CBS station. The streaming service’s ad-supported Essential plan, priced at $8.99 per month, also offers CBS programming on demand, with new episodes generally available the day after broadcast.

Sunday’s broadcast, hosted by Jane Pauley, will also feature interviews with U2 marking the band’s 50th anniversary, John Travolta discussing his upcoming film “November 1963” and author Min Jin Lee on her latest novel, “American Hagwon.”
