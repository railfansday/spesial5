Malika Haqq Speaks Out Amid Rumors of Fallout With Khloe Kardashian

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

After reports that Malika Haqq and Khloe Kardashian's friendship hit a rough patch, the Kardashians star's longtime BFF warned that her "silence is your gift" in a blunt message.

Malika Haqq is keeping up with the rumors—and she's shutting some of them down.

After TMZ reported that she and longtime BFF Khloe Kardashian weren't on speaking terms because a personal photo from Malika's home made it into the final edit of The Girls: A Khloe Kardashian Project, she hinted there is more to the story.

"I don't have any photos in my house," Malika wrote in a Nov. 24 Instagram Story. "I'm not this petty or shallow. Play with someone else."

Though she noted that her home decor isn't at the center of their rumored feud, that doesn't mean there aren't some concerns behind the scenes. In fact, she also shared a warning against further provocations.

Malika concluded, "My silence is your gift. But keep poking the bear."

E! News has reached out to Khloe's reps and Malika's manager for comment and has not yet heard back.

Malika joined the rest of the Good American founder's inner circle—including twin Khadijah Haqq, as well as Yris Palmer, Nicole Williams English, Olivia Pierson and Natalie Halcro—as they navigated life in the public eye during the new Hulu series.

While the experience wasn't always easy, Khloe—whom she befriended as a teen—remained a steadfast support during filming, even when she wasn't there in person.

"Khloe is our executive producer and narrator for all things in this friend group," Malika told Us Weekly in August. "She does show up a little bit more as kind of like a therapist because she's not around the entire time that we're filming this series, but she's never missing."

It also helped that the reality show features an ensemble cast.

"Khloe provided the perfect umbrella for us to feel safe and enjoy doing reality television in a space that should really be enjoyable when it's a friend group," the 43-year-old explained. "We don't come without our flaws and difficult moments."

"But in the end," she added, "I think when we closed this out, we were like, 'You know what, guys? We can stand behind what we did. We made a good show.'"

And it wasn't lost on Malika that her and Khadija's careers were bolstered by their longtime friend.

"We started off acting, and moving into reality TV was because of our relationship with Khloe,” the Sky High alum explained to People in August. "I think every day that you film is something different and unpredictable."

"But this will be the first time that we've really shared more of the layers of our individual lives," Malika noted. "We're very honest about that. I don't think you should do reality television if that's a curtain you don't want opened."
