The Hunting Wives’ Brittany Snow Is Engaged to Hunter Moreno

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Two years after Brittany Snow debuted her relationship with Hunter Moreno, she revealed the pair quietly got engaged: "It's not that big of a secret."

Brittany Snow is taking the next step in her love life.

The Hunting Wives star confirmed she is engaged to creative director Hunter Moreno two years after debuting their romance.

Despite rumors of a ring circling in Nov. 2025, it wasn't until Snow sat down with Drew Barrymore on Nov. 19 of this year that she revealed, “For the first time, I’m going to tell you. Yes, we’re engaged. I haven’t said it before.”

She admitted their engagement was "not that big of a secret" given the rumors, adding, "Like, who cares?"

Of her future husband, she noted, "He's the best."

Speculation that the 40-year-old was set to walk down the aisle first emerged after she attended the New York City premiere of her new Netflix miniseries The Beast in Me Nov. 5, where her costar Claire Danes appeared to ask about the relationship milestone.

“You’re engaged,” Danes seemingly said to Snow in a video shared on Instagram by DeuxMoi. “When did that happen?”

While the Pitch Perfect alum, who finalized her divorce from Tyler Stanaland in 2023, has largely kept her romance with Moreno private, she did step out in public with the 32-year-old in October 2024, with the pair sharing a kiss during a dog walk in Manhattan.

Snow and Stanaland, 37, who wed in March 2020, announced their separation in September 2022 and she filed for divorce three months later. The actress later revealed how challenging her breakup from the Selling the OC star was, given that much of their split—as well as Stanaland's love triangle with costars Alex Hall and Kayla Cardona—played out onscreen.

“I'm collectively calling them ‘they' because I don't know any of them except my ex-husband,” Snow said during an appearance on the Call Her Daddy podcast in March 2024. “They took up a lot of energy and emotion and time and real estate in my head.”

“I don't want to give them any more time or energy,” she continued, “because then they would win and they would get what they wanted—which was getting my time and energy and attention.”

As for the rumors he had been unfaithful during their marriage, Stanaland spoke out about the situation, denying that he ever cheated on Snow.

“A mutual commitment was made, both personally and legally, to never speak about the complexities of my marriage publicly and I plan to honor that,” he said in an Instagram Stories post at the time. “I do, however, want to make it clear that I was never unfaithful in my marriage and the accusations of infidelity couldn't be further from the truth.”

But as Snow has since found love again, so has Tyler. The reality star has also moved on romantically, marrying Hannah Morrissey in Nov. 2025 and welcoming their first baby together a year later.

“Welcome, Westley Grace Stanaland. Born 11.6.26, 7lbs 6oz and 20” long,” he wrote on Instagram. “Both mom and baby girl are happy and healthy despite 36 hours of labor and an emergency c-section.”

“Still in complete awe of my wife, you’re a real life superhero @hannahstanaland_,” Stanaland added. “My cup is overflowing in a way I didn’t know possible. What a blessing it is to be your Dad, Westley.”
