Rosalía Reacts to Her 9 Grammy Nominations: See How She Celebrated

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The news comes on the heels of the Spanish artist winning big at the Latin Grammys.

Rosalía has reacted to the multiple nominations she received at the 2027 Grammy Awards. She’s the second-most nominated with nine, following Raye, who leads this year’s list with 11.

After the nominations were announced on Monday (Nov. 16), the Spanish artist celebrated with her more than 27 million followers on Instagram, sharing a short clip where she’s seen popping a bottle of champagne in a backyard and jumping around with joy.

In the caption, the artist wrote “LUX 10 NOMINATIONS!!!!!!!!!!!AAAAAAAAAAA.”

The set is up for nine nominations including album of the year with LUX, as well as song of the year and record of the year for “Berghain” featuring Björk and Yves Tumor. The set also received a special recognition for producer of the year, non-classical.

The news comes on the heels of Rosalía becoming the top winner at the 2026 Latin Grammys earlier this month, where she nabbed five awards including the coveted album of the year.

“This is the third time I’ve won this award. If El Mal Querer was a dream, and Motomami was a bold gamble, then Lux has been a leap into the unknown,” she said during her speech, referring to her winning albums of 2019 and 2022, respectively. “I believe it’s very important to speak your own truth — and for others to be able to speak theirs — and this record is mine. I thank God, and I thank my loved ones — my family and my friends. Thank-you for your love and for shaping me into who I am today. The truth is, I know I still have a lot to learn, and I’m certain that I likely haven’t even created my best work yet.”
