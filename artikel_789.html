Latin Grammys 2026: Full List of Winners (Updating)

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Edgar Barrera arrives at the ceremony with 10 nominations, followed by CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, Jorge Drexler, Karol G and Rosalía, with seven each.

The Latin Grammys have arrived, with winners starting to be announced at 3 p.m. ET — hours before Thursday’s (Nov. 12) telecast — at the Premiere, a non-televised event where most of the trophies are handed out.

This year, Edgar Barrera tops the list of nominees with 10 nods. The acclaimed Mexican songwriter and producer appears in the general categories of record of the year, album of the year and song of the year — mostly for his work on projects by Karol G and Carín León — as well as songwriter of the year, an honor he has won for the past three consecutive years.

CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, Jorge Drexler, Karol G and Rosalía follow with seven nominations each, while Silvana Estrada and Mon Laferte have six. All of them are vying for album of the year, alongside Anitta, Carín León, Milo J and Rawayana. Most also compete for song and record of the year.

Rosalía’s eclectic album LUX appears in fields including pop, alternative music, and Portuguese language music, in addition to album of the year. The Spanish superstar has already won the night’s top award twice before — in 2019 for El Mal Querer and in 2022 for Motomami — and she is once again widely considered a favorite.

At just 19 years old, Argentine singer and rapper Milo J arrives with five nominations, including in the three main categories, with a folk album. In addition, the album received a sixth nod, for best recording package.

Other contenders with multiple nominations include Arath Herce, with four; and BRUSES, Feid, Héctor Mazzarri, Jennifer Mosello and Eden Muñoz with three each. Artists such as Rauw Alejandro, Anitta, Bad Gyal, Beéle, Bizarrap, Ryan Castro, Daddy Yankee, Kany García, Joaquina, Juanes and Carín León, among others, received two nods.

Daddy Yankee is set to take the stage as the Latin Recording Academy’s 2026 Person of the Year, a day after he was celebrated at a traditional dinner-concert by his peers. The coveted Latin Grammy for best new artist will be contested by Delilah, Fabian, Loulu Gilberto, Arath Herce, Macario Martínez, Maye, Sofia Monroy, Kendall Peña, Dora Sanches, Aria Vega and Zeca Veloso.

The 27th Annual Latin Grammy Awards will be broadcast live from Las Vegas starting at 8 p.m. ET on TelevisaUnivision platforms. The Premiere began at 3 p.m. ET on the official digital platforms of the Latin Recording Academy, including its Latin Grammys channels on YouTube, Facebook and X.
