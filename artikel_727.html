Latin Grammys Full Winners List: Rosalía Wins Big With Album and Song of the Year Honors

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The 2026 Latin Grammys took place on Nov. 12 at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas. Going into the evening, Edgar Barrera led the nominees with ten nods, followed by Jorge Drexler, Rosalía, Karol G and Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso with seven each. See the full winners list below.

Album of the Year: “Lux (Complete Works)” – Rosalía

Song of the Year: “La Perla” – Noah Goldstein, David Rodríguez, Rosalía, Ryan Tedder & Dylan Wiggins, songwriters (Rosalía featuring Yahritza y Su Esencia) Related Stories Maya Jama Strikes Deal With Netflix (EXCLUSIVE) Netflix U.K. Layoffs Hit Up to 3% of London HQ, Production VP Anna Mallett Exits

Best Pop Song: “La Perla” – Rosalía (featuring Yahritza y Su Esencia)

Best Short Form Music Video: “Sauvignon Blanc” – Rosalía

Best Urban Music Album: “Tropicoqueta” – Karol G

Best Pop/Rock Album: “JuanEsteban” – Juanes

Best Electronic Music Performance: “Beto’s Horns – Fred Remix” – CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso with Fred Again..

Best Pop/Rock Song: “Hasta Jesús Tuvo Un Mal Día” – CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso with Sting

Best Long Form Music Video: “FREE SPIRITS (The Film)” – CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso

Best Folk Music Album: “La Vida Es Más Corta” – Milo J

Best Urban/Urban Fusion Performance: “Gil” – Milo J featuring Trueno

Best Latin Jazz/Jazz Album: “Gonzalo Plays Pino” – Gonzalo Rubalcaba

Best Rap/Hip Hop Song: “Antisistema” – J Noa & Trooko

Best Christian Album (Spanish Language): “Inquebrantable” – Marcos Witt, Marco Barrientos & Marcos Vidal

Best Contemporary Mexican Album: “Dosis” – Xavi

Best Contemporary Tropical Album: “Antes Que El Tiempo Se Vaya” – Fonseca

Best Tejano Album: “Bobby Pulido & Friends: Una Tuya y Una Mía (Vol. 2/En Vivo)” – Bobby Pulido

Best Roots Song: “El tambor chico” – Facundo Balta & Jorge Drexler (songwriters: Facundo Balta & Jorge Drexler)

Best Contemporary Pop Album: “¿Y Ahora Qué +?” – Alejandro Sanz

Best Traditional Pop Album: “Yo Canto 2” – Laura Pausini

Best Reggaeton Performance: ”Verano Rosa” – Karol G featuring Feid

Best Urban Song: “Daddy Yankee: Bzrp Music Sessions, Vol. 0/66” – Bizarrap & Daddy Yankee
