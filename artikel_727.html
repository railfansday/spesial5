U.K. Executives Respond to Strong Showing in 2027 Grammy Nominations: ‘British Music Has Gone From Strength to Strength’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

RAYE, Olivia Dean, Sienna Spiro and more all landed nods in major categories.

RAYE, Olivia Dean, Sienna Spiro and more British artists were included in the 2027 Grammy Award nominations revealed on Monday (Nov. 16).

The competing artists for the upcoming ceremony were revealed in a livestream broadcast on YouTube featuring appearances from Dean, Yungblud and more. The ceremony will take place on Feb. 7 at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, California.

Across the general field and genre categories, British artists performed strongly in the nominations. Overall, RAYE leads all nominees with 11 nods across a number of categories including album of the year (This Music May Contain Hope.), as well as song and record of the year for “Where Is My Husband!” With her 11 nods, she ties Beyoncé for the most nominations by a female artist for a single Grammy Awards.

Dean also appears in album of the year category for her sophomore album The Art of Loving with “Man I Need” landing in both song and record of the year as part of her six total nominations.

Sienna Spiro landed her debut nomination at the Grammys in the best new artist field, making it a second consecutive year that a British act has been in contention for the prize (Dean scooped the trophy in 2026).

Yungblud earned a spot in the best rock song category (“My Only Angel” with Aerosmith) following on from his win at the 2026 ceremony in the best rock performance category for his version of “Changes” at Ozzy Osbourne’s farewell concert.

Elsewhere nominees Fred again.. (best dance/electronic recording; best remixed recording), Muse (best rock album), Paul McCartney (best traditional pop vocal album; best music film; best audio book, narration, and storytelling recording), and The Rolling Stones (best rock performance; best rock song; best rock album) all flew the flag for British talent.

Speaking on RAYE’s success, Guy Moot, chair and chief executive officer of Warner Chappell Music (WCM), tells Billboard U.K., “RAYE has once again made history in what is already an extraordinary, groundbreaking career. Leading this year’s Grammys as the most nominated act with 11 – and tying the all-time record for the most nominations by a female artist in a single year – really is an incredible feat.”

He continued, “Raye is a generational artist and songwriter who pours every ounce of her soul into her craft, and seeing her fly the flag for Britain on the world’s biggest stage is the ultimate way to cap off an incredible year. Congratulations to RAYE and her entire team – we look forward to celebrating with her in February.”

Dickon Stainer, chairman and chief executive of Universal Music Group U.K. & Ireland said, “I’m delighted to see such a breadth of UK talent being recognised this year. In particular, Olivia Dean’s multiple Grammy nominations reflect her extraordinary ascent from Best New Artist to global breakthrough status as she’s shortlisted for Album, Song and Record of the Year.”

Speaking on Fred again..’s recognition, Briony Turner, co-president of Atlantic Records U.K., said, “Fred continues to rewrite the rulebook for modern electronic music with a global reach. He brings such an incredible amount of heart, instinct, and craftsmanship to every track, so I couldn’t be happier to see him acknowledged once again at the highest level. We’re endlessly proud of him.”

Dr. Jo Twist OBE, chief executive of the British Phonographic Institute (BPI) told Billboard U.K., “British music has continued to go from strength to strength on the world stage in 2026, and it’s wonderful to see this reflected in today’s Grammy nominations. U.K. artists are up for awards across everything from pop, rock and electronic music categories to video game and visual media soundtracks, as well as for some of the awards’ most coveted categories, from Song of the Year and Album of the Year (Olivia Dean, RAYE) to Best New Artist (Sienna Spiro).”

Twist continued, “We’re seeing a new generation of diverse talent from across the UK breaking through in key international markets, as well as established artists continuing to chart new growth and reach new audiences around the world, in no small part thanks to our world-leading recorded music industry.”

“Huge congratulations to Olivia Dean, RAYE, Sienna Spiro, Fred again.., Charli xcx, YUNGBLUD, Gorillaz, Muse, and the many other British artists nominated today,” Twist added, “and to the teams who work so hard behind the scenes to support them to success.”
