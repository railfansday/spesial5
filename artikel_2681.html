Dodgers General Manager Brandon Gomes Addresses Hyeseong Kim’s Future

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Los Angeles Dodgers utility man Hyeseong Kim spent nearly the entire year in the minors in 2026, but the Dodgers still have hope he can play a key part with the team in coming seasons.

Kim didn't have a great year offensively, as his OPS dropped from .699 in 2025 to .651 this year in an even smaller sample size, but general manager Brandon Gomes revealed the Dodgers are working towards improving his swing.

"I think Comet didn't have the year that he was expecting offensively, but he has so many skills that fit us well," Gomes said. "I know diving in this offseason to continue to chip away at the potential swing changes, we worked on some stuff at the end of the year that he felt pretty good about after presenting him plans, so I think he'll colntinue to work through those things and hopefully take a step forward on swing mechanics and getting him into a really good place offensively."

Whether or not Kim will implement those changes is a different story, but Gomes is fully confident in him to get the job done.

"I think somebody with that work ethic and care and desire to be good is something that we feel good about, that that is a guy you want to bet on that he's going to make the adjustments," he said.

Kim's only run of games in 2026 came between April and May, as Mookie Betts went down with an injury, giving Kim a spot on the roster.

Kim featured in 43 games for the Dodgers, hitting just one home run and tallying 11 RBIs during his stint in the majors. Additionally, his batting average and slugging percentage both drastically dropped from 2025, though his on-base percentage rose by nine points.

He continued struggling with strikeouts, going down on strikes 23.7% of the time, but that mark is much better than his 30.6% from last season.

While he may not have been the best option for the Dodgers last season, Kim is clearly working to improve, and his positive glove and baserunning clearly improve his standing with the Dodgers. If he continues to make strides with his improvement, the Dodgers could give him a longer run of games next year.
