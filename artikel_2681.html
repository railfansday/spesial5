John Harbaugh: The sacks are going to come for Abdul Carter

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Giants linebacker Abdul Carter scores well in a number of metrics that measure pass rush, but that hasn’t shown up in the ones that’s most obvious to those watching the game.

Carter has two sacks and eight quarterback hits in nine games this season and he has not had a sack in the team’s last four games. Some of that is related to increased double teams coming his way in the wake of Brian Burns’s season-ending torn ACL, but any explanation falls short for those who have been wanting to see Carter overwhelm offenses since he joined the Giants as the third overall pick in 2025.

On Wednesday, Giants head coach John Harbaugh said “we want sacks” when asked about Carter but that he’s confident they’ll start to come because of what he sees from Carter’s tape.

“He’s a disruptive guy. I promise you they’re accounting for him,” Harbaugh said, via a transcript from the team. “And the sacks are just going to come. You’ve got to go play football. I mean, you can have a bunch of sacks and really not be playing that good too. We’ve seen that before too. So he’s that kind of player. He’s a disruptive player. He’s a game changer, game wrecker, and I think there’s going to be a lot of game wrecks in his future.”

The Giants face the Jaguars this weekend and that’s the start of a tougher run of opponents than the ones they faced thus far. That makes it an ideal time for Carter’s effort to translate to the kind of big plays that can swing games in their direction.
