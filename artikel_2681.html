Zach Ertz: People don't understand how good DeVonta Smith is

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Eagles wide receiver DeVonta Smith missed five games with a hamstring injury earlier this year and the Eagles lost all five of them, which doesn’t feel like much of a coincidence after watching him in Dallas on Thursday.

Smith caught 11 passes for 200 yards and two touchdowns in Philadelphia’s remarkable 39-38 comeback win. The touchdowns came on 50- and 45-yard strikes from quarterback Joe Milton and Smith shook off a hard landing on his tailbone in the fourth quarter to finish out the game.

The 11 catches tied Smith’s career best and the 200 yards were a personal record, but the big day didn’t come as a shock to tight end Zach Ertz.

“It’s not surprising, honestly,” Ertz said, via Dave Zangaro of NBCSportsPhiladelphia.com. “I watched him when he was at Alabama, winning the Heisman. Comes in here right away, his game is just so smooth. I don’t think people understand how good of a player he truly is until he gets his opportunities. I don’t know what the final stats were. I know over 200 yards, which is crazy. But he’s just a receiver that really has no weakness. The way he runs routes is so smooth, the way he can make plays on the balls in the air, yards after catch. I think he’s probably one of the most underrated players in the league. I saw him as a rookie so where he’s at now doesn’t surprise me at all.”

The Eagles made Smith their No. 1 wideout when they traded A.J. Brown this offseason and the injury interrupted his bid to prove he’s the right guy to fill that role on their offense, but Thanksgiving offered some proof of concept for the Eagles’ passing game.
