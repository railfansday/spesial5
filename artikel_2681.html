Cooper Flagg Impresses Dwyane Wade

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Former Duke star Cooper Flagg, fresh off a Rookie of the Year campaign, is not having the dreaded sophomore slump. Instead, he's having a huge impact on the NBA in his second season, and his hard work is paying off. Everyone has noticed, but the most gratifying recognition for any professional in any field comes from his peers, because they know exactly what he is doing, and what he's done to get to this point.

The Miami Heat legend has a lot to say about Flagg, but this sums it up pretty well: "One thing I love about Coop is he plays with a motor. I love a guy that plays with a motor. I love a guy who don't play cool. We talk about this all the time. Stop playing cool, your job ain't to be cool. Your job is to be great. Cooper Flagg understands that."

Flagg is just 19, and he's already on the verge of becoming one of the NBA's elite players. His mother, Kelly, has always told him that when he becomes the best player in his gym, he needs to find a new gym.
