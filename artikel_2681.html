Tre’Davious White to the rescue? Bills’ battered secondary may need a familiar face

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

There were a couple of surprises on the Buffalo Bills' first injury report of Week 11, with the team beginning practice on Wednesday for Sunday's game against the Miami Dolphins.

None of the starting offensive linemen were sidelined today. Well, that's not entirely accurate — right guard O'Cyrus Torrence, the only lineman not dealing with an injury last week, was on Wednesday's report, but for personal reasons rather than an injury.

But the rest of the surprises on today's report? They weren't of the fun variety.

None of these are particularly surprising. Maybe Kincaid. The Bills' top tight end seems to be playing some version of "Spin the Wheel" when it comes to injuries this season. Weeks 8 and 9, it was a chest injury. Last week, it was an ankle. Now he's keeping the ankle ailment and adding a thumb injury to go with it for Week 11.

Otherwise, these injuries were evident during Sunday's game, so it's no surprise that any of the players listed here are getting another day — or three — off.

Here, we have the group that decided to surprise this week. None of them were on last week's injury list, so the injuries all came during or after Sunday's game at MetLife Stadium. That turf is unforgiving, but not all of these may have happened on game day.

We did see Hooker coming out of the medical tent Sunday, but never got any confirmation from the Bills as to why. Now we know it had something to do with his knee.

We saw Bishop getting attention from trainers on the sideline. He was seen wincing as they manipulated his arm.

It's Hamlin that gives us reason to pause. It's not great that the Bills have three injured safeties. Plus Hamlin has spent significant snaps replacing Alford, who's dealing with a concussion right now, at nickel.

Sal Capaccio with WGR550 reported seeing Hamlin in a boot and using a scooter at the end of practice. That, information — along with a limited designation — would indicate Hamlin started practice healthy. An in-practice injury that puts him in an immobilizer is not a good sign that Hamlin will play on Sunday.

The big news here is Shavers. Head coach Joe Brady announced before practice that the team was moving Shavers to the active roster. Shavers started the season on PUP after tearing his ACL in playoff action against the Jacksonville Jaguars this past January.

The Bills thought they solved their defensive back problem. They cut C.J. Gardner-Johnson, signed Amani Hooker, got Dee Alford back from an injury, discovered versatility in Damar Hamlin, and found that rookie Davison Igbinosun was doing well at boundary corner.

Now they're suddenly facing a shortage once again. Hamlin and Christian Benford aren't a lock to play Sunday. Alford has to work through the league's concussion protocol. And, if they do play, Bishop and Hooker may not be full speed.

The current uninjured Buffalo secondary would look like Jordan Hancock at NCB, Sam Franklin, Jr. and Jalon Kilgore at safety; Maxwell Hairston and Igbinosun on the boundaries.

The answer may just be a veteran. Today, Brady said that Tre'Davious White was brought in to help this team win. On Monday, defensive coordinator Jim Leonhard said that he feels White is ready to step up if needed.
