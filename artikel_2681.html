Report: Baker Mayfield has a foot strain that could keep him out Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Baker Mayfield has a foot strain that “seems to be trending” toward the Buccaneers’ quarterback not playing in Sunday’s game against the Lions, Ian Rapoport of NFL Media reports.

A report on Sunday indicated Mayfield spent time in a walking boot last week and his status for Week 11 was uncertain.

It seemed likely rookie Jalon Daniels would make his fourth start in place of Mayfield after the Bucs signed Cooper Rush to the practice squad and elevated Easton Stick to the active roster earlier this week. The Bucs went 1-2 in the three games Mayfield missed with a dislocated thumb.

The Bucs will release their first injury report of the week on Wednesday.

In six games, Mayfield has completed 60.6 percent of his passes for 922 yards with three touchdowns and four interceptions.
