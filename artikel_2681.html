Hubbard’s 22 help Hampton knock off Newport News 67-56

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

HAMPTON, Va. (AP) — Seth Hubbard had 22 points in Hampton's 67-56 victory over the Apprentice School on Tuesday.

Hubbard shot 7 for 17 (5 for 13 from 3-point range) and 3 of 4 from the free-throw line for the Pirates (1-2). Cornelius Robinson Jr. scored 14 points and added 14 rebounds. Aaron Davis had 10 points and shot 4 for 13, including 2 for 8 from beyond the arc.

Donovan Means finished with 14 points and two steals for the Builders. Devon Brown added 13 points and nine rebounds for Newport News. Jahlil Hope also had 11 points.

Hampton plays Saturday against Navy on the road.
