MLB sues players union to boost lockout as deadline looms

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Major League Baseball filed a lawsuit against the MLB Players Association on Wednesday seeking legal protection for a lockout expected to begin next week when their current collective bargaining agreement expires.

The complaint filed in US District Court in New York seeks a declaration that a lockout would be legal under US antitrust law.

The move would safeguard MLB if the players give up union status and try to sue MLB under antitrust law, exposing the league to potentially greater damages.

MLB team owners have said they will not accept any new deal without a salary cap, a system the players union has successfully fought for decades.

With a Tuesday deadline fast approaching, the lawsuit raised the stakes as both sides fought to determine how to divide billions of dollars in profits.

"Today's filing is a defensive measure simply intended to keep the parties at the bargaining table to work towards a new collective bargaining agreement and a full 2027 season," MLB spokesperson Glen Caplin said.

"We believe the union is preparing to follow the playbook its new leadership has used against other major professional sports leagues by pretending to 'disclaim' its status as the players' bargaining representative to pursue an antitrust lawsuit as an end run around the bargaining process.

"We have asked a federal court in the Southern District of New York to proactively disarm the union's nuclear option of disclaiming."

MLBPA interim executive director Bruce Meyer called the lawsuit "completely frivolous" in a statement.

"We will respond appropriately, but MLB's decision to run to court in the middle of negotiations rather than bargain in good faith is incredibly disappointing."
