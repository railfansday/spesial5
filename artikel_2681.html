'I had to show off': How Selena Gomez inspired Victor Wembanyama to go off for 50 in win vs. Pelicans

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

'I had to show off': How Selena Gomez inspired Victor Wembanyama to go off for 50 in win vs. Pelicans originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Victor Wembanyama wasn't the only wizard to make his mark during the Spurs' 114-91 win over the Pelicans on Saturday night.

San Antonio's talisman's skills were on clear display during the matchup. He scaled mountains to force the ball down on one end and send it wayward on the other. His numbers reflected his in-game dominance, too; for the second time this season, Wembanyama eclipsed the 50-point mark.

It was another wizard who took hold of his attention as the game wore on, however. Selena Gomez was in attendance during Saturday's fixture. Wembanyama said she played a major role in pushing him to record his latest masterclass.

With that, here's what you need to know about Wembanyama's epic display — namely, how Gomez contributed to it.

Wembanyama is plenty used to conjuring up historic metrics. On Saturday night, he added another to his collection. Wembanyama secured his third-ever 50-ball, notching an even 50 in San Antonio's triumph over New Orleans.

The 22-year-old fell two short of the career-high he set earlier in the month with his latest scoring outburst. He looked poised to throttle that record after two quarters, amassing 32 points, 13 rebounds, three steals and three blocks while nearly outscoring New Orleans on his own.

Wembanyama became the fourth player to ever net 50 or more points, 15 or more rebounds and five or more blocks in the same game. He's the second player to accomplish the feat more than once, preceded only by Wilt Chamberlain. He's the first player to post the final line of 50 or more points, 15 or more rebounds, four or more steals and five or more blocks since the block was introduced in 1973-74.

All told, it was just the sort of showcase that Wembanyama has become used to during his career. After the game, he had one person to thank for his outburst: Selena Gomez.

Gomez was in attendance during Saturday's contest, watching on as Wembanyama produced points in bunches.

He made sure to make mention of her while speaking with reporters after the game.

A noted San Antonio supporter — and Grand Prarie, Texas native — Gomez is plenty used to the spotlight in her home state.

On Saturday, she was merely a spectator. Her influence on Wembanyama's display was palpable, however, taking shape whenever the spindly superstar climbed above the rim for a rim-rattling slam or deafening swat.

Those of a Spurs disposition can't wholly thank Gomez for Wemby's epic display. But she certainly had a part to play. Perhaps she'll return to Frost Bank Center more to uplift the Frenchman and his teammates, all of whom could stand to gain something when she waves her magic wand at them.
