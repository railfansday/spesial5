8 Best Weapons in COD Modern Warfare 4 Multiplayer

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

It's that time of the year again when an annual Call of Duty launch has taken over everyone’s schedule, or at least alongside a renowned Zelda game at the moment. And for someone like me who’s dabbled in and out of modern releases from both Infinity Ward and Treyarch, the former has genuinely done a marvelous job with COD: Modern Warfare 4, making it one of the best releases in the franchise yet.

And despite my competitive nature subsiding since then, the COD Multiplayer gremlin teenager part of me never died, and so I’ve stuck around with MW4’s multiplayer. I load into a lobby feeling confident, then either get absolutely flattened or dominate with my team—no in-between.

This is DualShockers' review of COD: Modern Warfare 4, covering all the ins and outs of the campaign, multiplayer, and the new DMZ Extraction Mode.

The weapons matter equally with your game sense and skill here, since some feel amazing the second you pick them while others just don’t suit a certain playstyle. I’ve burned a few hours (and a lot of attachments) figuring out which is which, and I’d rather save you the trouble.

So I implore you to stick around today because whether you’re grinding for camos or just want to stop getting melted in Hardpoint, here are the best weapons to use in Modern Warfare 4 multiplayer.

Note: This list is being drafted as of Season 1’s patch notes in MW4.

I’m kicking us off with a gun that’s had a divided reception lately from both other players and a couple of my closest friends who play with me.

The Nyx is a flanker’s SMG since it’s quick enough to catch people off guard when you come in from the side or behind. But I’d be lying if I didn’t mention how it can feel like a pea-shooter compared to the other SMGs that outclass it.

Its performance has improved following the Season 1 balancing changes, which increased its second medium-damage profile from 15 to 16 and its minimum damage from 13 to 14.

I mean, that (sort of) makes it more forgiving outside its ideal close-range engagements, but yeah, it's still worth including as an alternative for someone who wants something different in MW4’s multiplayer, especially if they’re a run-and-gun player, since this SMG works wonders in that regard. It's also a fun close-range SMG to use in Hardcore game modes, where other guns can one-shot you easily.

Grinding enough in the early levels to unlock the Axion was worth it, because this AR deserves more recognition here as an ol’ reliable gun, especially after its recent improvements, where you can’t go wrong picking it for a loadout. Its Season 1 improvements have increased its medium damage range by 11% and muzzle velocity by 13%.

So this ends up making it much more dependable when challenging enemy players across longer sightlines or off-angles, which matters especially on larger multiplayer maps. If you’re on controller, try to dial the recoil down with the attachments; otherwise, get more range and ADS speed/stability on this bad boy, and it will hit like a truck in the right hands, trust me.

And speaking of hitting like a truck, if you want an AR that quite literally embodies that term, then look no further than the Kastov 762. Its biggest advantage is its damage output, especially when holding longer sightlines or on players in close-to-medium range. It's another weapon with a mixed fanbase, but if you don’t mind the slower fire rate, add it to one of your loadout arsenals immediately.

Also, the latest S1 balancing update matters: the weapon received a 4% increase to its medium damage, a 5% improvement to its medium damage range, and additional head, neck, and upper-torso damage multipliers. Also, if you’re one of the majority who finds this weapon hard to control, you can try the FSS Fireline Grip as an alternative underbarrel attachment.

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 multiplayer has arrived, and it features skill-based matchmaking, with no Open Matchmaking playlist options in sight.

As someone who absolutely adored his Pulse Rifles from Destiny, or even the BR55 from Halo 2, the Hyeon Burst in MW4 immediately became one of my favorite weapons to use, even if it's a bit of an annoyance against some of the faster TTK weapons.

It also ends up rewarding those players who can maintain their accuracy rather than simply holding down the trigger and hoping for the best. In the S1 patch notes, the Hyeon Hyperburst Apex attachment took a sizeable nerf, losing 23% of its maximum damage and 20% of its minimum damage. But you do get compensated since the regular Hyeon also received a small medium-damage buff.

I already know every weapon pick you off in Hardcore, but Hyeon Burst is so fun on its own here, with the 3-burst fire letting you pick off multiple targets from a safe distance. Keep in mind that while the Hyperburst attachment increases fire rate by 23%, it also adds a fair bit of horizontal kick, so if that’s a deal-breaker, take it off and use something else that won’t make it hard to use.

One of the earliest SMGs you can unlock is the ISO Nightshade. If the VEL 46 is for the aggressive players, the Nightshade is for everyone else. It kills nearly as fast up close, but it’s much easier to control, so you stay accurate when fights get messy while rushing.

And much like the rest of the aforementioned weapons on this list, it has also benefited from the latest weapon balancing changes, receiving an 11% increase to its maximum damage range alongside a 9% increase to its minimum damage, which is genuinely a welcome improvement for a weapon that I think previously relied heavily on making you get close to opponents.

With the Nightshade, you can use it at medium range and make it a hybrid run-and-gun playstyle with fast ADS and minimal sway. I also recommend mastering a hip-fire style with it since Modern Warfare 4’s hip-fire integration is actually very forgiving, at least in my experience. But hey, if that’s not your forte, totally fine; I’d just stick with making this an agile weapon for yourself.

I love using my MP7s across various shooting games whenever given the chance, so imagine my love at first sight when I picked up the Vel 46 and ran wild with it on the Transit 213 Map. Mixed opinions on the map aside, the VEL 46 is one of the best aggressive guns to grace MW4's multiplayer meta.

Its excellent close-range performance makes it extremely effective for rushing objectives or catching enemy players off-guard when they least expect it. And obviously, I don’t have to explain that its main drawback is its limited range, so don't even try to use it in lanes or main angles.

Interestingly, the Season 1 patch slightly reduced its maximum and intermediate damage ranges but increased its minimum damage from 13 to 14, so the bottom line is that it barely changed, and you can still use it as a meta option or even just a fun run-and-gun weapon.

The VEL 46 Snap Apex attachment is a must, and you should try to go all out on faster ADS and movement speed. One last pro tip: use the Ninja perk on this, or any other SMG build, so you can flank without making noise from your footsteps.

Introduced with Season 1, the Cronen Osprey is arguably one of the most exciting recent additions to the weapon roster, and it deserves serious consideration if you’re not already trying it out in your loadouts. That’s because it has an exceptionally fast fire rate and accuracy that makes it feel almost like a laser.

The Osprey is built around speed, so it fires very quickly, which makes it deadly when it connects and awkward when it doesn’t, because the recoil climbs and drifts sideways if you hold the trigger on the controller.

The trick is to fire in controlled bursts and add attachments that steady it without slowing your aim, since fast handling is a big part of why it’s worth using. The recoil is otherwise very manageable on keyboard, so I’d advise sticking with anything that gives you faster ADS or a wider damage-output range.

Finally, we come to one of the most popular weapons in Modern Warfare 4: the M4. It’s a weapon that you just can’t go wrong choosing, especially if you’re just jumping into the game’s multiplayer scene. I mean, that’s probably why I keep seeing other players bring it up in community discussions: it is fast, reliable, and easy to get comfortable with.

And the cherry on top is how the Season 1 update has made it even more appealing, increasing its maximum damage range by 8% and its headshot multiplier by 4%. But we’re not done here: by equipping the Apex attachment, you can practically transform the M4 into an SMG variant that's extremely viable at close range or for running and gunning, without feeling like a middle-ground weapon.

Keep in mind that this removes the option to change some other attachments, so if you feel like the Apex hinders the weapon’s viability for you, stick to the normal variant and make it feel as snazzy as possible without recoil or slow handling.
