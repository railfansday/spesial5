And you guessed it, the most essential part of that layering process is… her bikini underwear. I won’t judge what you need that piece of clothing for, so without further ado, this brief guide breaks down where you can find bikini clothing items for Lucia in Grand Theft Auto 6.

The exact clothing store where you can easily buy a Bikini set for Lucia is the De’Luge Swimwear shop located in the east-central part of Vice Beach. There are no restrictions on when you go there, but I'd suggest waiting until at least Chapter 3, since that’s when Vice City officially becomes available. Also, by then, you'll have enough extra funds to splurge on clothing items.

The De’Luge Swimwear shop sells a bunch of high-end clothing items for both main characters, but the notable items are definitely for Lucia, including bikini tops and bottoms, as well as one-piece bodysuits you can layer pants over for a sophisticated look. Besides regular bikinis, the shop also has variations in stock, along with full-body dresses, all of which you can easily access on the upper floor.

Now, here's where things get a little more interesting for those who enjoy experimenting with Lucia's wardrobe.

Unlike some other clothing options in GTA 6, bikinis are sold as separate pieces, so you don't have to stick with a matching top and bottom. When browsing the available designs, you can press R1 on PlayStation or RB on Xbox to switch between the two categories and see what other options are available.

You can purchase individual pieces rather than buying an entire matching set, and once you've bought them, they'll be available in her wardrobe at a safehouse. So this pretty much gives you a little more freedom to put together your desired outfit combinations, especially if you're trying to create a particular style for Lucia, as I've demonstrated above with the Bikini Bottom under her pants.
