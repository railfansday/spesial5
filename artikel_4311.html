GTA 6 Gets Massive New Screenshot Gallery Two Weeks Before Launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

After years of waiting, we're finally just two weeks away from Grand Theft Auto 6, and Rockstar Games apparently isn't done giving us things to obsess over before launch. The developer has just released 38 new screenshots, offering another look at Vice City and the surrounding state of Leonida before we finally get to explore it ourselves on November 19.

There's quite a bit to unpack here, too. The images cover everything from Jason and Lucia to the city's nightlife, criminal underworld, and quieter corners of Leonida. But what really caught our attention was the character detail. The facial details are astonishingly realistic, and we're not just talking about Jason and Lucia. A few of the background characters look good enough to have their own stories.

Of course, there's plenty more to admire beyond the characters themselves. The screenshots also give us a closer look at the people, places, and everyday scenes that could make Leonida feel like an actual place.

Rockstar unveiled the new collection on November 5 through its official GTA 6 launch gallery, inviting us to take another look around Leonida before release day.

While the screenshots include the usual assortment of cars, guns, and criminal activities, some of the most impressive images are simply close-ups of the characters themselves.

Lucia, for instance, appears in several tense situations, including one screenshot where she's aiming a handgun directly ahead. The detail in her face is striking, from the texture of her skin and individual strands of hair to the subtle expressions that make her look far more believable than the characters we've grown accustomed to seeing in open-world games.

Jason gets similar treatment, whether he's working out shirtless, confronting armed enemies, or finding himself in the middle of another chaotic encounter. Even the supporting characters and seemingly ordinary NPCs look remarkably detailed.

One particularly impressive close-up shows a man partially covering his face with his hand, with the lighting bringing out the finer details of his eyes, skin, and jewelry. Other screenshots feature heavily tattooed individuals, some sporting elaborate hairstyles and accessories, whose appearances are distinctive enough to suggest considerable attention has gone into the wider population.

That's particularly encouraging for a game like GTA 6, where so much of the fun comes from simply wandering around, getting distracted, and observing whatever nonsense the locals are getting up to.

A few of the background characters look good enough to have their own stories.

The nightlife screenshots are especially interesting in this regard. One shows a stripper performing under the neon lights of a cabaret, while another features several women gathered inside what appears to be a strip club dressing room. Elsewhere, packed dance floors, colorful lighting, and partygoers enjoying themselves offer another glimpse of Vice City's entertainment scene.

There are also several images featuring tattooed street crews and other figures who wouldn't look out of place in Leonida's criminal underworld. One shows a group of men posing together, while another features a man standing beside a muscular dog adorned with a gold chain.

There's even a gathering around heavily customized tuners, which makes us wonder just how much further Rockstar has taken GTA's car culture this time around. Between the customization and the street meets, there's certainly room for something more elaborate than what we had in GTA 5.

Taken together, these images give us a better sense of the people and communities we might encounter throughout Leonida, rather than focusing exclusively on Jason and Lucia's criminal exploits.

Of course, the latest collection isn't entirely concerned with its inhabitants. Rockstar has included several environmental shots that offer another look at the scale and variety of GTA 6's open world.

Vice City's waterfront appears particularly impressive, with boats gathered around a marina and high-rise buildings dominating the skyline. Another image captures the city after dark, with illuminated skyscrapers stretching into the distance.

There are quieter scenes, too. One screenshot shows an almost deserted landscape at sunset, with a solitary tree overlooking the distant city skyline. Others take us into the Grassrivers, where alligators and airboats feature prominently. An underwater shot at Leonida Keys also shows a coral reef teeming with marine life, making us wonder whether scuba diving will actually feel more rewarding this time around, especially if there's more to discover beneath the surface.

These contrasts have been an important part of GTA 6's marketing. Vice City might be the main attraction, but Rockstar has repeatedly emphasized that Leonida extends well beyond its urban areas, encompassing everything from tropical islands to swamps and rural communities.

Rockstar's previous open-world games have been praised for their environmental detail and the behavior of their NPCs, particularly Red Dead Redemption 2. Whether GTA 6 can achieve something similar on a much larger, more densely populated scale remains to be seen, but these images certainly make a promising first impression.

Naturally, screenshots alone can't tell us how these environments and characters will behave during actual gameplay. Nevertheless, the sheer amount of visual detail on display makes it difficult not to get excited about finally exploring Leonida.

Fortunately, that wait is almost over. GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with pre-loading beginning November 10.

After all these years, it's strange to think we're finally this close to getting our hands on GTA 6.
