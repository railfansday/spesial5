'Traitors: New Blood' Stars Victor and Katie Detail 1st Date After Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Warning: Spoilers below from the season 1 finale of The Traitors: New Blood.

Katie Fites declared, “No date for you!” after Victor Vollbrechthausen voted to banish her on The Traitors: New Blood, but she had a change of heart after they returned home from the castle.

“We have gone on that date,” Katie, 23, confirmed to Entertainment Weekly after the season 1 finale of the Alan Cumming-hosted NBC reality competition series aired on Thursday, November 19. “After incessant begging, let the kids back home know that persistence works.”

Victor, 26, quipped that he thought “hunting Traitors was hard,” but convincing Katie to go on a first date proved to be even more difficult.

“I think I’m prouder of that,” he added. “But yeah, we did. We finally got that date.”

Katie shared that she and Victor made a pact not to “talk about The Traitors” during their date so they could get to know each other better outside of the game.

“It was great,” she told the outlet, to which Victor agreed, “It was. It was fun.”

However, the costars played coy when asked whether they plan on going on a second date.

“We’ll see,” Katie replied, prompting Victor to joke that “getting that first one was an uphill battle, but I think there could be.”

Distance may make a relationship difficult for Victor and Katie, as he lives in New York City while she is currently based in Florida. Katie teased during the interview that she would like to move to the Big Apple soon — but “not for Victor.”

Victor was vocal about having a crush on Katie throughout the first season of the Traitors spinoff, but during Thursday’s finale, he correctly began to suspect that she may have been a Traitor all along. As a result, Victor and the other finalists banished Katie from the castle. However, Victor was sent packing right after Katie because the others were suspicious about him too even though he was a Faithful.

Ultimately, Shane Beatty won the $246,300 prize pot, blindsiding Faithful runners-up Wyatt Gillespie and Abbey Benjamin by tearfully revealing he had been a Traitor alongside Katie.

“It was hard for me to celebrate because I just felt so guilty,” Shane, 32, told EW on Thursday. “And I actually didn’t feel like a winner. … Yes, I won, but at what cost?”

The Traitors: New Blood is now streaming on Peacock.
