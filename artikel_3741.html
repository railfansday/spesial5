Best WoW Forever classes

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

There's 9 Classes to choose from in WoW: Forever, and a lot of factors to consider.

The nine Classes of World of Warcraft: Forever are the same ones vanilla launched with in 2004, and they look nothing like what we have in Retail more than 20 years later. But Forever reimagines the vanilla experience, and Blizzard wants to “upgrade” how the Classes feel this time around.

The goal is for Classes to feel as balanced as possible without ruining how interesting their playstyles are, so Forever had a starting roster that felt rather nostalgic but still new enough to be unpredictable. But since Classes are made to feel and play differently from each other, you’d eventually be drawn to certain Classes for specific playstyles.

Of the rulesets in Forever, you might find yourself gravitating towards the Normal or PvE experience first. This is where most standard-fare open-world adventuring, and where raids and dungeons attract the most number of players. Besides Level 60, getting geared up for these PvE experiences is part of your capstones as you reach the higher levels.

If you want to sink your teeth into PvP in Forever, you might notice that you’re getting into not one playstyle but actually three different types of mechanics. When you turn on your World PvP, anyone from another faction can fight you wherever, so if you can’t handle surprises, then you’re a goner.

Alternatively, there are PvP matches you can prepare for. Battlegrounds are faction-based matchups on instanced maps with point-based mechanics. Then there are duels, which are pretty much arranged fights between you and someone else.

The suggestions below will be based on what we can theorycraft with the Talent Trees, and what can be observed with Level 1 to 30 fights. Until we get to the real endgame in a couple of weeks, we can’t be 100% sure.

World PvP happens in the open, so the most ideal PvP Classes to take on enemies in the wild are the ones equipped for surprises.

Marksmanship/Survival Hunters specialize in long-range ambushes, which means they can run if they feel like they’re overwhelmed. With Track Humanoids on the map, they can set up slows and traps so targets never reliably reach them. And with Dismember, Sonic Blast, and Viper Sting in the mix, Hunters can pick targets easily… assuming they strike first. If Hunters are jumped on, they hardly have any way to disengage.

The Frost Mage is interesting, as they’re only as devastating as the movement debuffs they can dish out. In theory, a Mage controls PvP with freezes and slows, then finishes with Ice Lance and Frostbolt. But if a target knows their mobility, the Mage can find it difficult to keep up.
