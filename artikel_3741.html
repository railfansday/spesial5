Fortnite developer and publisher Epic Games hosted a generative AI-powered game jam – but its most popular game doesn't, uh, feature any AI at all, apparently.

It's no secret that Epic is largely pro-AI – and that's perhaps putting things lightly. Earlier this year, the company released a public announcement that it would undergo a "huge pivot" for "maximum AI acceleration," with studio head Tim Sweeney pushing for a new "AI first" approach. Time and time again, the CEO has also spoken against rival storefront Steam's required AI disclosure, dubbing it "unfair to developers who use AI to make good games."

So, it comes as no surprise that Epic just hosted a "2026 Epic AI MegaJam," which is, yes, a game jam focused on vibe coded projects. You'll find the details over on itch.io, which speak for themselves, honestly – just see "vibe your way to $50k" and some of the instructions, like those outlining how applicants may use "as many AI agents" as they'd like. Submissions have wrapped up now, though votes are running for a few more months yet. What's currently in the lead, then? Why, a humble little indie game called LSD:FIH – a game that hinges on no gen AI.

Yes, you read that right. An AI-free game is dominating Epic's AI game jam. LSD:FIH's creator introduces it on its official page as such, writing, "No AI was used to make this game" before describing it as, erm, a "computer game about a fih aura-farming." Does it really matter what that means? I'd argue no.

It's satisfying enough to see it taking over the rating feed, with votes absolutely pouring in. Reviews are amusing, too. "This is what people from the 2000s would think an 'AI-generated game' would look like, a fun and interesting title," reads one, "rather than generated slop. Great job, especially given the development time frame!" Said time frame was a mere four days – November 12-15, to be precise.

So, what will happen to LSD:FIH? Will it face disqualification for not employing the use of the ever-controversial tech Epic oh-so loves, or it will it prevail and continue to beat out the other entries? Stay tuned for the results come January 29, 2027.
