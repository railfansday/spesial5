Reddit: Staff Product Manager, Agentic Ads Buying

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

AI agents are starting to reshape digital marketing, changing how campaigns are planned, bought, and optimized. As more decisions and actions move into agent-driven workflows, the platforms that influence agents’ choices will shape where advertising dollars go. Reddit brings its audience and community intelligence into those decisions and makes it actionable wherever advertisers choose to buy.

This role offers a ringside seat to that shift. As Staff Product Manager for Agentic Ads AI, you will build zero-to-one experiences that help advertisers plan, create, manage, and optimize campaigns. You will turn emerging AI capabilities into reliable, intuitive products that solve real customer problems.

You will partner with Engineering, Machine Learning, Design, and Go-to-market to set the product direction and quality bar, then guide new experiences from early development through launch and scale. Customer research, competitive benchmarking, rigorous evaluation, and live product feedback will shape how you iterate.

Success requires strong execution, clear product judgment, and experience shipping agentic products that work exceptionally well for customers. You don’t need to be an ads expert on day one, but you should be ready to quickly learn the digital marketing industry.

The Ads Platform & Experiences team builds the tools businesses use to buy media and grow on Reddit. We work across campaign creation, management, reporting, and optimization to make advertising easier and more effective for businesses of different sizes and levels of experience. We combine advertiser goals, campaign data, and Reddit’s community context to help customers make better decisions with less effort.
