17 Easy Holiday Cocktail Recipes That'll Make You Feel Merry & Bright

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Whether you’re hosting a holiday party or just want to sip on a special beverage alone by the fire, now is the time when much of the world goes searching for easy holiday cocktail recipes. We all deserve a little treat after the year that was 2025. Well, rest assured, there’s a fun and festive drink out there for every palate (including if mocktails are more your speed). From spiked hot chocolate and eggnog concoctions for sweets lovers to wintry old-fashioneds and martinis, here are some of the best easy holiday cocktails on the internet.

If you’ve never put Bailey’s in your eggnog, now’s the time. Averie Cooks’ recipe for Santa’s magic potion will help you get the ratio just right, and hit it with a little cinnamon and nutmeg for that perfect holiday flavor.

Mimosas are a crowd-pleasing cocktail, so why not just put a holiday spin on them for your winter party? Damn Delicious’ cranberry mimosas blend orange, pineapple, and cranberry juice with sparkling white wine. Just top with a sprig of rosemary and a couple cranberries and serve.

If you’re not a bourbon fan, might I recommend Princess Pinky Girl’s drunken Rudolph, a hot chocolate cocktail spiked with Fireball instead? This recipe is even less work and is sort of the everyman’s version, if you will.

Family Fresh Meals’ starry night punch recipe is nonalcoholic, but obviously you can just swap the sparkling grape juice for the real thing. It will yield a classic Christmas red sangria, which you can top with star-shaped apple cutouts for the perfect finishing touch.

If you want a cocktail that absolutely screams Christmas, then Dude That Cookz’ peppermint cream martini is the drink for you. It’s a delicious mix of peppermint schnapps, white cream liqueur, and vanilla vodka, with the cutest crushed-candy-cane rim.

Why haven’t I thought of apple cider sangria before? Averie Cooks explains everything you’ll need and how to get the perfect balance of flavors. Then just load ‘er up with apples, cinnamon sticks, oranges, pomegranate, and whatever else your holiday heart desires.

Personally, I swore off blue drinks after one particularly rough hangover in college, but Princess Pinky Girl’s Jack Frost cocktail is admittedly very fun-looking. Just blend your ingredients together, rim your glass, and clink it with a friend’s.

One last mimosa idea before you go, courtesy of Budget Bytes: simply swap out orange juice for apple cider, pour it into your champagne flute until the color is just right, and bada bing bada boom. It doesn’t get any easier than that, and you know it’s going to be delish.

Oh, so you’re having a holiday party party — love that. Then you have to try Princess Pinky Girl’s Instagram-worthy candy cane shots, which only take about five minutes to whip up. Adding a little sweetened condensed milk to the gelatin mix gives it a sweet, creamy base that makes the candy cane flavor really come alive.

Biscuits & Burlap’s spiced pear and chai bourbon drink is the perfect reminder that apples are not the only seasonal fruit worth celebrating. It’s a bit more involved (you’ll need to make a fresh pear simple syrup), but the end result looks so worth that little bit of time and effort.

Speaking of pears, you’ve gotta try Dinners Done Quick’s pear gin fizz. When’s the last time you actually ordered a gin fizz out? They’re so good but they’re never really on cocktail menus anymore, and they give me an old-timey celebratory feel every time.

Moscow mules are the perfect drink to serve to guests if you’re not much of a bartender and you’re just out here doing your best as a host. Fresh Apron’s recipe is a matter of a few simple steps (complete with exact measurements); it’s the festive garnishes and copper mugs that really make this beverage shine.

Savory Spice’s recipe will teach you how to make a chai simple syrup (very useful if you’re an at-home coffee enthusiast, by the way) and how to balance it perfectly within a cranberry fizz. It’ll add a seasonal, cozy depth of flavor that’s unexpected yet oddly perfect.

White russian drinkers, you’ll love this easy apple pie cocktail, made with Crown and RumChata, from Dinners Done Quick. You can easily customize it to make it sweeter, heavier on the cinnamon, extra apple-y, however you take your cocktails.

Be honest: How many gallons of apple cider did you just add to your grocery list?
