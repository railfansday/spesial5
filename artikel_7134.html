Week of Nov 16 Evening News Ratings: NBC Nightly News Is Only Newscast with Gains

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

NBC Nightly News with Tom Llamas finished the week of November 16 as the only broadcast with growth in both total viewers and the Adults 25-54 demo.

Nightly News’ performance was even more impressive in the prized demo, narrowing the viewership gap with ABC World News Tonight with David Muir to just 29,000 viewers.

Note: The weekly averages of ABC World News Tonight, CBS Evening News, and NBC Nightly News are based on four days (Monday-Thursday). Friday’s (11/20/2026) ABC News’ broadcast was retitled to WNT-ABC, CBS Evening News and NBC Nightly News were retitled to CBS Evening Nws, and NBC Nitely News, respectively. These broadcasts are excluded from the weekly and season averages.

According to Nielsen national live+same-day big data and program ratings, ABC World News Tonight averaged 9.09 million total viewers and 1.267 million Adults 25-54 demo viewers for the week of Nov. 16. The evening newscast was down 2% in total viewers and 7% in the A25-54 viewers compared to the previous week. Compared to the same week in 2025 (the week beginning Nov. 17), WNT was up 14% in total viewers and 27% in the demo.

NBC Nightly News took the No. 2 spot in total viewers and the demo, averaging 7.366 million and 1.238 million viewers, respectively. Compared to the previous week, Nightly News grew by 3% in total viewers and 7% in the demo. Looking at its performance to the same week a year ago, Nightly News was up by 14% in total viewers and 43% in the demo.

CBS Evening News closed out the week of Nov. 16 with 4.628 million total viewers and 650,000 demo viewers. CBS News’ newscast was flat in total viewers and down 6% in the demo compared to the previous week. Year-ago week numbers showed CBS Evening News up 12% in total viewers and 23% in the demo.
