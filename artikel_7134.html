Pacaso: Principal Product Architect, Growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Pacaso exists to enrich lives by making second home ownership possible and enjoyable for more people. We've reimagined the second home category — pairing a thoughtfully designed co-ownership model with simplified financials, equitable scheduling, and dedicated local property management, so owners get the joy of a true second home without the hassle.

Since launch, Pacaso has facilitated more than $1.2 billion in gross real estate transactions and service fees across 40+ destinations in the U.S., Mexico, and Europe — including Aspen, Lake Tahoe, Napa, Malibu, Palm Springs, Cabo, Paris, London, and most recently Florence, Milan, and Rome. We've raised over $300 million in cumulative equity funding, closed the largest real estate Regulation A+ offering of 2025, and reserved the Nasdaq ticker PCSO as we build toward a public listing.

Our work has been featured in The New York Times, The Wall Street Journal, Fortune, Forbes, CNBC, Bloomberg, and others.

Pacaso buyers are high-net-worth individuals making a considered purchase over months, sometimes years. They move between our site, email, messaging, and our Sales team, and today each of those runs on its own tools with its own picture of the customer. We believe one personalized system that follows each customer from first interest to purchase, and keeps serving them as an owner, will shorten the path to purchase and make our growth product-led.

We're hiring a principal-level technical product IC to architect that system and own its outcome. You'll define the reference architecture: how we recognize a customer across channels, what we know about them, how the system decides what to do next, and which of those decisions AI agents can make without a person. You'll prove the design with working prototypes you build yourself with AI tooling, then lead Engineering and Data in taking it to production.

This isn't a roadmap-and-backlog PM role, and it isn't a full-time production engineering role. It sits between them. You'll make product calls with Sales, Marketing, and leadership, make architecture calls with senior engineers, and be accountable for both.

Some of this is already moving. AI agents talk to our customers today, and parts of the data and decisioning exist. None of it runs end to end on its own yet. You'll take what's there, decide what to keep, and design the system that can.

We'll ask for specific evidence of each of these. If you can't point to all five, this probably isn't the right fit yet.

We start with AI. It's one of our core values. We design processes with automation and intelligence in mind from the start, and our product team already has AI agents talking to customers.

Small team, real ownership. Product at Pacaso works closely with Engineering, Design, Data, Sales, and Marketing. If you see a problem worth solving, you're trusted to solve it. We ship weekly and demo working software, not slides.

We move fast and play the long game. We make decisions and change course quickly. "Embrace an infinite mindset" is one of our values: a quarterly number is information, not a verdict, and we'd rather send nothing than send the wrong thing.

We pick up the phone and empty the dishwasher. When async isn't enough, we get on a call. No task is beneath anyone, and we keep bureaucracy to a minimum. We're a distributed Crew.

We invest in our Crew the way we invest in our homes — thoughtfully, generously, and with care.
