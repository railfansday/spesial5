Lab experiments suggest bat RSV can enter human cells, but existing antibodies block it

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) causes severe respiratory disease and is responsible for numerous deaths each year. A research team led by DZIF scientist Dr. Wendy K. Jo and DZIF scientist Dr. Jan Felix Drexler at Charité—University Medicine Berlin, as well as DZIF board member Dr. Thomas Pietschmann and Dr. Sibylle Haid from TWINCORE in Hanover, has now characterized a virus in West African fruit bats that is genetically distinct from known RSV variants but belongs to the same viral family as RSV.
