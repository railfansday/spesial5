ATEEZ Earn Fourth No. 1 on Billboard 200 With ‘GOLDEN HOUR : Part.6’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Plus, U2 achieves its 14th top 10 with the debut of Carnaval de Luz.

ATEEZ earn their fourth No. 1 on the Billboard 200 albums chart as GOLDEN HOUR : Part.6 debuts atop the tally (dated Nov. 28). It’s the 10th top 10-charted project for the act, all of which have come since 2022. With the arrival, ATEEZ stand alone as the group with the most top 10s in the 2020s. Among all acts this decade, only YoungBoy Never Broke Again (15) and Taylor Swift (14) have more.

GOLDEN HOUR : Part.6 bows with 195,000 equivalent album units earned in the United States in the week ending Nov. 19, largely driven by pure album sales (191,000).

Also in the top 10, U2 collects its 14th top 10 as the band’s new studio album, Carnaval de Luz, debuts at No. 4.

The Billboard 200 chart ranks the most popular albums of the week in the U.S. based on multi-metric consumption as measured in equivalent album units, compiled by Luminate. Units comprise album sales, track equivalent albums (TEA) and streaming equivalent albums (SEA). Each unit equals one album sale, or 10 individual tracks sold from an album, or 2,500 ad-supported or 1,000 paid/subscription on-demand official audio and video streams generated by songs from an album. The new Nov. 28, 2026-dated chart will be posted in full on Billboard‘s website on Nov. 24. For all chart news, follow @billboard and @billboardcharts on both X, formerly known as Twitter, and Instagram.

Of GOLDEN HOUR : Part.6’s 195,000 first-week equivalent album units, album sales comprise 191,000 (it debuts at No. 1 on the Top Album Sales chart), SEA units comprise 4,000 (equaling 4.04 million on-demand official streams of the set’s songs) and TEA units comprise a negligible sum.

ATEEZ have logged their 10 top 10s on the Billboard 200 consecutively. The act claimed its first top 10 with the No. 3-peaking THE WORLD EP.1 : MOVEMENT in 2022. In total, with 10 top 10s in the 2020s, ATEEZ now solely have the most top 10s among groups this decade. The act was previously tied with Stray Kids (which got its ninth top 10 — and ninth No. 1 — in August with This & That).

GOLDEN HOUR : Part.6’s album sales were bolstered by its availability across 32 CD and vinyl variants (all containing collectible items such as photocards and posters, with some items randomized), including signed editions.

Olivia Rodrigo’s you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love falls to No. 2 on the latest Billboard 200 with 62,000 equivalent album units earned (down 1%). Morgan Wallen’s chart-topping I’m the Problem is a non-mover at No. 3 with a little more than 49,000 (up less than 1%).

U2 achieves its 14th top 10-charted album on the Billboard 200 as Carnaval de Luz debuts at No. 4 with 49,000 equivalent album units earned. Of that sum, album sales comprise 42,500 (it debuts at No. 2 on Top Album Sales), SEA units comprise 5,500 (equaling 5.77 million on-demand official streams of the set’s songs) and TEA units comprise 1,000.

U2 notched its first top 10 album on the Billboard 200 with the No. 1 The Joshua Tree in 1987 and was last in the top 10 with 2023’s Songs of Surrender (No. 5 debut and peak).

The new album’s first-week sales were aided by its availability across 18 physical variants (vinyl, CD, cassette and Blu-ray audio disc).

Carnaval de Luz was preceded by its lead single “Silencio,” which marked the group’s 45th entry on the Alternative Airplay chart earlier in November (tying Foo Fighters for the most in the chart’s history).

Two former No. 1s are next up on the Billboard 200, as Noah Kahan’s The Great Divide rises a spot to No. 5 (47,000 equivalent album units earned, up 8%) and Ella Langley’s Dandelion is pushed down 5-6 (45,000, up 2%). Kahan’s Stick Season is steady at No. 7 (36,000, up 2%) while SZA’s chart-topping SOS steps 9-8 (32,000, up 6%). Rounding out the top 10 are Olivia Dean’s The Art of Loving (down a spot to No. 9 with 32,000, down less than 1%) and Lil Baby’s FRESH (falling 2-10 with 31,000, down 47%).

Luminate, the independent data provider to the Billboard charts, completes a thorough review of all data submissions used in compiling the weekly chart rankings. Luminate reviews and authenticates data.
