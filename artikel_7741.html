Has the Supreme Court Undercut the Courts?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Mostly law professors | Sometimes contrarian | Often libertarian | Always independent

Prof. Goldsmith interviews Prof. Vladeck on whether the Supreme Court has undermined district courts.

In his Harvard Law Review Foreword, "The Court Against the Courts," Professor Stephen Vladeck argues that the Supreme Court has undercut the authority of federal district courts. Is this a fair charge? Professor Vladeck defends his argument in a discussion with Professor Jack Goldsmith who (like me) is quite skeptical of Professor Vladeck's argument. Whatever one thinks of the subject, it is a stimulating and worthwhile discussion.

As readers may recall, I debated Professor Vladeck on the "shadow docket" this past spring.
