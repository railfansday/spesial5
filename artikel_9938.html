Water usage has nothing to do with being in a closed loop. Nobody is supposing that data centers are so stupid to flush the water once it has passed by a GPU. The thing is, once you have moved the heat from the GPU to the water, you still need to get rid of the heat -- something has to chill the water before you can recirculate it.

Datacenters usually pick one of two options. They can run massive AC units (enormous electricity consumption), or they can use evaporative cooling (enormous water consumption).
