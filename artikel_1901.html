Liverpool confirm Ryan Gravenberch injury blow as Andoni Iraola names squad for Champions League clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Liverpool have been dealt a new injury blow as Ryan Gravenberch will miss the Champions League showdown against Fenerbahce.

Gravenberch has cemented himself as a regular in Liverpool’s midfield since the autumn international break, starting five of the seven matches across all competitions.

He did, however, miss the comeback win over Brentford through suspension after being sent off in the defeat to Manchester City and was then dropped for the 1-0 loss to Arsenal at the weekend.

Gravenberch was expected to be in contention for Liverpool against Fenerbahce on Wednesday, but it has since been revealed that the Dutchman will not be making the trip to Turkey.

A Liverpool statement reads: “A heel injury means the midfielder will not be part of the Reds’ travelling squad for Wednesday’s meeting with the Turkish side in Istanbul.

“He will instead remain at the AXA Training Centre to undergo treatment with the club’s medical team.”

As such, Gravenberch joins Hugo Ekitike, Conor Bradley, Giovanni Leoni and fellow Dutchman Jeremie Frimpong as the five Liverpool injury absentees against Fenerbahce.

Iraola issued an update on the Dutchman on Tuesday: "It is a day to day thing.

“The fracture healed, it is about the pain. He has some discomfort. It is going to be something every day we will ask him how he is."

Wataru Endo and Federico Chiesa, meanwhile, are unavailable due to not being named in the club’s squad list for the Champions League this season.

It remains to be seen if Gravenberch will be fit to face Crystal Palace at the weekend, though he has a battle on his hands to regain a midfield spot from Trey Nyoni.

“The thinking is that he's playing very well and we trust him," Iraola told Sky Sports pre-match of his decision to drop Gravenberch in favour of Nyoni against Arsenal.
