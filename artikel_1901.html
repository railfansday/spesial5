How the Scotland players rated against North Macedonia

Scotland earned their first home victory of Sebastien Pocognoli's reign with a 2-1 victory over North Macedonia at Hampden.

John McGinn was the hero in this Nations League B1 match, showing good composure to net the opener on 54 minutes and then scoring what proved to be the winner 20 minutes later when he converted a penalty.

North Macedonia had equalised on 58 minutes when striker Elmin Rastoder glanced home a header from a corner kick, but Scotland showed sufficient resolve to find a winner.

They remain in third place in the group, a point behind Slovenia, who drew 0-0 with leaders Switzerland. However, due to the head-to-head record, Scotland will need to defeat Switzerland in St Gallen on Monday if they are to overtake the Slovenians in the final game-week.
