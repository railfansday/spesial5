The Matthias Jaissle Brighton press conference has taken place on Friday.

This is ahead of the Newcastle United match on Sunday, a 2.05pm kick-off at the Amex Stadium.

Newcastle United looking to build on the second half performance against league leaders Arsenal.

Now up against the Premier League team who are the nearest chasers to the Gunners.

Brighton having though suffered a shock 2-0 defeat at Hull City in their most recent Premier League match.

Matthias Jaissle Brighton press conference, asked about Tino Livramento progress: “Always better when we need to make decisions when it comes to the starting eleven. Tino is on his way back, we need to see if he is ready to start. We still have two days. I don’t want to rush something. He is such a fantastic footballer, we need to be smart with him.”

Matthias Jaissle asked about Bazoumana Toure and Aladji Bamba’s squad omission v Arsenal: “They’re professional footballers, they’re getting quite a good pay cheque, so I expect from them that they come with a smile still on the training ground and try to improve.”

Matthias Jaissle on Arsenal reflections: “We analysed the game; it is really clear what we want to see. Many aspects from the second-half. We had mixed feelings, a little bit of frustration because we couldn’t deliver in the first-half but proudness and excitement from the second-half.”

Matthias Jaissle on improving performance: “Every game is so important. We can only focus on the next one. We are working hard on improving and creating more chances. Our aim and our goals are to perform [second-half against Arsenal] on a more frequent basis.”

Matthias Jaissle on Brighton challenge: “We need to be aware about their strengths and weaknesses. We want to use the momentum from that second-half against Arsenal. We will need to be smart, have good game management. Brighton have incredible quality, playing at a high level. They are a top team.”
