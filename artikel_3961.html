'It Felt Like a PowerPoint Deck of Executive Ideas': Beta Testers Share Thoughts on PS5's Cancelled Fairgames

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Haven’s heist shooter Fairgames – first announced more than three years ago – has been cancelled, rounding out a rough generation for Sony’s live service ambitions.

While we saw very little of the game publicly, a trailer for a title named Project Espresso emerged in September, giving us our first proper look at the title in years.

This video was used to promote a playtest, which ultimately got the game cancelled today. Sony executives said it failed to meet the “goals” it had set for the game.

To be fair, this is an example of the platform holder learning lessons from Concord, as it’s now abundantly clear Fairgames was never going to find an audience. Exactly why it took so long for the company to recognise that is up for debate.

While the abovementioned playtest was under NDA, some users are now starting to share their experiences online.

Writing on the ResetEra forum, user RedBullCola wrote: “I was in the late stage testing group and it wasn't very good. The feedback in matches was also not good. It felt like a PowerPoint deck of executive ideas, same thing with Horizon Hunters Gathering.”

Horizon Hunters Gathering could be the next game to get cancelled, as Dutch dev Guerrilla has been given until December to retool it. We’ll probably get word on the status of that project soon.

Over on Reddit, user Darkdhalias echoed the negative sentiment on Fairgames: “As someone who participated in said playtest, cancelling it was 100% the right move. [The] game was still years away from being compelling, and was one of the most unpleasant alpha/beta experiences I’ve ever had.”

Back on the ResetEra forum, user srrai was a bit more positive: “I played it and yeah just wasn’t for mainstream people. They weren't gonna buy and/or play this enough to keep it going. Though it was fun [in my opinion]. Would I have bought it? Maybe at $40.”

Still, this is the kind of feedback that PlayStation would have been receiving from the playtest, and the reason the game was ultimately cancelled.

Sony had bold ambitions for live service this generation, and while Helldivers 2 has admittedly been a big hit, virtually all of its other bets have ended up being blunders.

The good news, at least, in this instance is that Haven Studios staff will keep their jobs, and will be reassigned support work on other PS Studios titles for now.
