GTA 6 Smashes Multiple Sales Records And Earns Xbox Series S Praise In First 24 Hours

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Update: "Grand Theft Auto VI day one retail sales reached $3.79 billion"

Update [Fri 20th Nov 2026, 10:02pm]: GTA 6 publisher Take-Two is now reporting that day one sales reached $3.79 billion USD, while also selling 32.5 million units pre-release. The $3.79 billion figure is based on "company estimates".

"Rockstar Games, a label of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTWO), is proud to announce that Grand Theft Auto VI day one retail sales reached $3.79 billion, setting a new record for entertainment across any medium, and becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history."

"Achieving a near perfect score of 99 on Metacritic and resounding critical acclaim, Grand Theft Auto VI sold 32.5 million units pre-release, a new record across all platforms."

Original Story: Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 has reportedly smashed all sorts of records within 24 hours of launch, and it's off to a great start on Xbox Series consoles.

As shared by GTA 6 Countdown, GTA 6 has launched to a near-perfect 99 score on Metacritic (151 reviews), apparently sold 24.5m copies in under 24 hours, and seen 2 million Xbox players take a trip to New Zealand to play the game early.

Sensor Tower's sales tracker also estimates $2.36bn (£1.8bn) in revenue from GTA 6 sales, with the console split mostly in favour of PS5, at a 75% to 25% share over Xbox. Still, the data suggests Xbox still took a whopping $588m from 6.15m units sold.

GTA 6 dominated Twitch viewership too, with 50m hours watched and 3.3m viewers during its busiest hour, beating the livestreaming site's previous record (held by Hogwarts Legacy at 1.2m) by more than double.

Beyond all the big numbers we're chucking your way, we've also spotted a lot of praise for the Xbox Series S version of the game. Whilst Digital Foundry was hit-and-miss on the report, the general response online is majorly positive.

It's good news, then, to finally put to bed the lingering question of whether GTA 6 would run well enough on Xbox Series S consoles. Judging by IGN's comparison, it's not all that different of an experience to Sony's flagship PS5 Pro.

@HonestHick LGs and OLEDs are great, but every TV technology has pros and cons, there isn't a perfect one. E.g. OLED isn't as bright so may not be the best choice in brighter rooms. And for lower frame rates, like 30fps games, it often looks choppier on OLED because the screen is too responsive and you notice it more. OLED amplifies that lower frame rate, whereas LCDs slightly blurring between frames with "sample-and-hold" smooths the transitions. The thing that is bad at high frame rates, becomes a benefit at low frame rates.

Personally I found playing the Switch much less pleasant on my LG OLED, before it broke, and actually ended up playing most Switch games on another TV because of this. (They also looked better on a smaller screen)
