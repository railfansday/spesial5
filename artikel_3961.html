Sakuna Chronicles: Kokorowa and the Gears of Creation second teaser trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Publisher Marvelous and developer Edelweiss have released a second teaser trailer for Sakuna Chronicles: Kokorowa and the Gears of Creation, the November 2024-announced spin-off of Sakuna: Of Rice and Ruin.

The video depicts protagonist Kokorowa’s encounter with “Yagokoro no Kusabi,” a key figure who set her on the path to becoming an inventor goddess, her interactions with new characters such as Takebina and Aikichi, as well as the events that unfold in the story and the mystery surrounding the massive mechanical tower.

Platforms and a release date for the game remain unannounced.
