2026 Latin Grammy Awards Red Carpet: Best Photos

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Check out the best pics from Karol G, Rosalía and more at the 2026 Latin Grammys red carpet.

Latin music stars exuded elegance on the red carpet at the 2026 Latin Grammys — held on Thursday (Nov. 12) at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas — with white emerging as a standout color.

Rosalía and Karol G shone in ethereal, flowing gowns in this hue, as did Anitta and Mon Laferte — all featuring details such as draping, sheer elements and intricate braiding. Anitta accented her look with a red feather in her hair, while Laferte opted for a pink bridal veil, which has become a standard of her Femme Fatale Tour.

Others who embraced light tones included Camila Cabello, who wore a satin two-piece ensemble accented with lace and a feather boa; Kany García, in a two-piece outfit with lace at the cuffs and hems; and, of course, Elena Rose — who has made white a signature part of her artistic identity — looking elegant in a structured, strapless design.

Puerto Rican singer and actress Roselyn Sánchez, returning to host the awards ceremony for the tenth time, chose a silver hooded gown adorned with crystal detailing.

And while many women dazzled in white, black was the color of choice for many men — including the evening’s honoree Daddy Yankee, 2026 Latin Recording Academy Person of the Year. The Puerto Rican icon stood out in a tuxedo jacket embellished with crystals, paired with a matching shirt and belt.

Beto Montenegro from Rawayana, who arrived with actress-girlfriend Ana de Armas, also looked sharp in a dark grey double-breasted suit, while de Armas looked fresh in light blue gown with minimalistic flowers and an asymmetrical skirt.

During the Latin Grammy Premiere — a non-televised event held at the Mandalay Bay in Las Vegas — winners were announced for the majority of the categories, including CATRIEL & Paco Amoroso, Jorge Drexler, Milo J, and Rosalía.
