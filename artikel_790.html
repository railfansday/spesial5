Liverpool considering Verbruggen move - Sunday's gossip

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Liverpool are considering a move for Brighton keeper Bart Verbruggen, Bayern Munich remain in talks with Liverpool winger Rio Ngumoha's camp and Tottenham midfielder Conor Gallagher is being monitored by rival Premier League clubs.

Liverpool are weighing up a move for Brighton and Netherlands keeper Bart Verbruggen, 24, amid doubts over the future of Brazil international Alisson Becker, 34, whose contract with the Reds expires next summer. (Football Insider), external

Bayern Munich remain in talks with Rio Ngumoha's camp after initial contact earlier this year and hope Liverpool's 18-year-old England winger could be a transfer option in the summer window. (Christian Falk), external

Liverpool have a strong interest in Brighton's highly rated Croatia centre-back Luka Vuskovic, 19, as they step up their search for a successor to Netherlands defender Virgil van Dijk, 35. (Daily Briefing), external

Chelsea and Liverpool are both interested in 21-year-old Brentford and Senegal left-back El Hadji Malick Diouf. (4-4-2.ch - in German), external

Crystal Palace, Everton and Leeds United are monitoring Tottenham midfielder Conor Gallagher, although Spurs are unlikely to let the 26-year-old England international leave. (Football Insider), external

Chelsea may seek to open contract talks with England defender Levi Colwill, whose current deal runs until 2029, as Barcelona consider putting in a transfer bid for the 23-year-old in January. (Caught Offside), external

Manchester United have been impressed by Rennes and Cameroon left-back Mahamadou Nagida, 21, following a series of encouraging scouting reports. (Teamtalk), external
