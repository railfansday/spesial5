Blink-182, Bring Me the Horizon, Turnstile, Motley Crue and The Prodigy to Headline 2027 Download Festival

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The 24th edition of the event will take place at Donington Park in Leicestershire, England from June 9-13.

The 2027 Download Festival will feature headliners Blink-182, Bring Me the Horizon, Evanescence and the Prodigy. The event, which will take place from June 9-13 at Donington Park in Leicestershire, England, will also welcome Mötley Crüe, the reunited Faith No More, Pierce the Veil, Turnstile, Parkway Drive, President, 5 Seconds of Summer, I Prevail, The Hives, Bilmuri, Down, Primus and Ice-T’s Body Count. Tickets for the festival are available here now.

Among the other acts slated to take the stage are: Kreator, Carpenter Brut, Converge, Basement, Better Lovers, Cancer Bats, Castle Rat, PVRIS, Clutch, Currents, Dayseeker, Gwar, Health, Highly Suspect, Hot Mulligan, Jinjer, Murderdolls, Napalm Death, Static-X, The Devil Wears Prada, Waterparks and more.

Blink will be headlining Download for the first time and Evanescence will be the fest’s second-ever female-led headline act. To celebrate, the event released a three-minute teaser video in which a young woman walks through town at night wearing her headphones on her way home to plug in and practice electric guitar. She’s distracted when her phone starts blowing up with alerts about the Download lineup.

While playing an online quest game with a friend, the screen turns green and glitchy as a series of skulls float in the air and then project the festival’s lineup onto the ceiling of her room.

This June’s Download Festival featured headliners Limp Bizkit, Guns N’ Roses and Linkin Park, as well as sets from Cypress Hill, Tom Morello, Halestorm, Mastodon, Bad Omens, Babymetal and more.
