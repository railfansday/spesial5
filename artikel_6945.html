Timothée Chalamet Joins Kylie Jenner For a Date Night at Sushi Park

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

There’s nothing like a Sushi Park outing. Over the years, the celebrity-favorite restaurant has doubled as Los Angeles’s unofficial runway, with paparazzi stationed on the other side of the famed balcony, cameras in hand and ready to snap photos of the establishment’s starry patrons. Hailey Bieber, Zoë Kravitz, Kaia Gerber, and the Jenner sisters are particular staples of the West Hollywood eatery, but we’ve also seen everyone from Taylor Swift and Selena Gomez to the hard-to-spot Mary-Kate Olsen. Typically, the dinners double as GNOs, although on occasion—as was the case last night—Timothée Chalamet has gotten the invite to join his partner for a sushi roll or two.

Arriving at her go-to spot with Chalamet, Kylie Jenner was photographed wearing a black leather trench coat, designed with little lapels and a knee-length silhouette. She left the oversize piece open, revealing a plain white scoop-neck T-shirt underneath, and finished off the casual fall fit with a pair of classic blue jeans. She let her long dark hair down and was seen holding her boyfriend’s hand outside the restaurant.

As for the Dune actor, Chalamet—with a shaggy hairdo—wore an all-black look composed of a zip-up collared jacket, loose-fit tee, and matching trousers. He added a belt and a little gold chain to accessorize.

The couple first sparked relationship rumors in early 2023, before making things official that fall with a series of public appearances. Since then, they have accompanied one another to award shows (the Golden Globes, the Oscars), sporting events (New York Knicks games, World Cup matches), Broadway shows (Oh, Mary!), and premieres (Marty Supreme). Most recently, Chalamet cheered on Jenner during her Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show debut, where she modeled the one-and-only Fantasy Bra.
