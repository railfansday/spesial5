Palmetto Man Pleads Guilty to Illegal Disposal of a Deceased Body

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Tampa, Florida – Matthew Edward Zoladz (37, Palmetto) has pleaded guilty to one count of violating the Marine Protection, Research, and Sanctuaries Act, and one count of violating the Act to Prevent Pollution from Ships. He faces a maximum penalty of 15 years in federal prison. Zoladz will be sentenced on January 20, 2027. United States Attorney Gregory W. Kehoe made the announcement.
