Attorney General Blanche Honors Goodyear Police Officer Dakota Berry for Distinguished Service

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

WASHINGTON – Goodyear Police Officer Dakota Berry was among the law enforcement officers from around the nation who were selected to be honored by U.S. Attorney General Todd Blanche today for Distinguished Service in Policing.
