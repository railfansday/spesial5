Highlights! Derek Brunson Delivers Smashing TKO In BKFC Debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Derek Brunson hadn't fought in nearly three years entering his bareknuckle boxing debut on Friday night (Nov. 20, 2026) ay BKFC Myrtle Beach, but that didn't stop the former UFC contender from turning in a dominant knockout performance.

As mentioned above, it's been a while since fight fans have seen Brunson compete. The former UFC middleweight veteran had won five-straight fights from 2019-2021, but then coughed up back-to-back UFC losses to Jared Cannonier and Dricus du Plessis. Brunson parted ways with UFC shortly after that, but did go on to capture a decision win over Ray Cooper III under the Professional Fighters League (PFL) banner back in Nov. 2023. "The One" has been on the sidelines ever since.

That all changed Friday night as the 42-year-old Brunson turned in his mixed martial arts (MMA) gloves and threw down inside of the Bare Knuckle Fighting Championship (BKFC) ring. Brunson was matched up against combat punching bag, Theo Doukas, so it was a very winnable affair for the former UFC standout. However, Brunson had never fought in bareknuckle boxing before so fans weren't totally convinced he could pull it off.

Well, Brunson passed with flying colors. It took "The One" just two rounds to score three knockdowns against a very slow and limited Doukas. The third and final knockdown left Doukas in a tizzy and he wasn't able to continue. The referee waved the fight off and Brunson walked away with his first knockout victory since ending Edmen Shahbazyan back in 2020.
