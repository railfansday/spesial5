Wizards vs. Raptors preview: Washington looks to halt skid in Toronto

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Washington Wizards look to get back on the winning track Wednesday as they pay the Toronto Raptors a visit at Scotiabank Arena.

When: Wednesday, November 18th at 7:30 p.m. ET

How to watch: Monumental Sports Network, League Pass

Wizards: Jamir Watkins – Out (Knee), Tristan Vukcevic – Out (Hip), Reece Beekman – Out (Shoulder), Justin Champagnie – Out (Foot)

Raptors: Kawhi Leonard – Out (Knee), Scottie Barnes – Questionable (Illness)

The Wizards first took on the Raptors on Oct. 23 in a hard-fought 112-102 loss to a physical, playoff-tested Toronto team. Washington at least showed a lot of fight in the contest and had fans hopeful that the team would only get better with time, especially since AJ Dybantsa had yet to debut at that point.

Washington looked to be improving with wins in three of their next five games, before losing eight straight contests entering Wednesday's rematch. Over that eight-game span, the Wizards lost by an average of 16.7 points.

The Raptors, however, may be without their two biggest stars during the matchup. Kawhi Leonard has not played since first suiting up against Washington, while Toronto has listed Scottie Barnes as questionable for the front end of the team's back-to-back against the New York Knicks due to an illness. His status for the clash versus Washington, the latter half of the back-to-back, remains to be seen.

If both Leonard and Barnes are out, the Wizards could have a puncher's chance to win, even in a hostile environment at Scotiabank Arena. But given how Washington has played of late, it's hard to envision any victories until we actually see them happen.
