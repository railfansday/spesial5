What we're thankful for during a disappointing 2026 Saints season

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

As bad as the New Orleans Saints have been this year, there are still things to feel thankful for. There's a reason we keep coming back to watch each week even as the losses pile up -- there are still moments of highlight reel-worthy plays, individual accomplishments, and a couple of wins that make fans proud to wear black and gold on an all-too-rare Victory Monday. So what's your silver lining to a rough season? Here are our picks in the spirit of Thanksgiving.

I'm proud of Khristian Boyd for finally climbing the depth chart and making plays. The former sixth-round pick was stuck on the bench for his first two years, only appearing in ten games and producing a single quarterback hit from 2024 to 2025. And that's with unproductive veterans like Davon Godchaux and Nathan Shepherd ahead of him. Whatever the Saints needed to see from Boyd, he's shown them. He'll play in his tenth game this Sunday against the Cincinnati Bengals and is already looking for his third sack. It's early, but the 26-year old looks like someone the Saints can build around on the defensive line. He's given them some flashes and highlight plays after having to earn his reps the hard way. -- John Sigler

While there hasn't been much to feel positive about the 2026 New Orleans Saints, there are a few reasons to look forward to in the upcoming future. This team has the makings of a talented young nucleus with wideouts Chris Olave and Devaughn Vele, tackle Kelvin Banks Jr., and certainly edge rusher Chase Young. Even with the recent slump of quarterback Tyler Shough and small sample size from rookie receiver Jordyn Tyson, both players look like they could have a bright future in front of them. Additionally, rookie Barion Brown has shown explosive potential as a returner and with a competent coach, could have Rashid Shaheed-like offensive value. Shough, Banks, Tyson, and Brown are still on rookie contracts, while Olave and Young are locked up on reasonable deals currently. More good news for the Saints has been the emergence of Carl Granderson, who has put together his most consistently productive season and has been a fearsome complement to Young as a pass rusher. -- Bob Rose

I'm thankful for Chase Young looking like the player we all thought he could be coming out of Ohio State. It's been a tough 10-game start to the season for the team, but it's fun to watch Young beat a tackle off the snap and force a quarterback to get rid of the ball before he wants to. After all the talk about his potential, we're finally getting to see him put it together in New Orleans. The Saints have given fans plenty of reasons to look ahead to the draft. Still, Young gives them a reason to keep watching on Sundays. He's definitely a player to build around as New Orleans tries to get back to winning. -- Arye Pulli

I'm grateful that the pass rush for the New Orleans Saints finally has found two consistent sources of pressure. Ever since they let Trey Hendrickson walk, there has never really been a second option next to Cameron Jordan or a successor truly, but this season between Chase Young and Carl Granderson, there's something to work with. 16.5 sacks, 60 tackles, 19.5 TFL, 6 pass deflections, and 2 forced fumbles between the two of them denote just how productive they have been, and finally there's something to build around in that unit. -- Jeremy Trottier
