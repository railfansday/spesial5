Celtics Suffer Stunning Collapse As Kings End Eight-Game Losing Streak

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Celtics Suffer Stunning Collapse As Kings End Eight-Game Losing Streak originally appeared on NESN. Add NESN as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Boston Celtics couldn't have asked for a much better opportunity to end their losing streak Saturday night. Sacramento entered the game with one victory in its first 15 games, had lost eight consecutive contests and hadn't won at home all season.

Keegan Murray scored on a putback with five seconds remaining, lifting the Kings to a stunning 97-96 victory at Golden 1 Center.

The Celtics led 54-41 at halftime and appeared positioned to take care of business against one of the NBA's struggling teams. Instead, Boston endured a disastrous third quarter that completely changed the game.

The defeat was Boston's third consecutive loss and arguably its most frustrating of the season.

The Celtics controlled much of the first half despite playing without Jayson Tatum, Derrick White and Mitchell Robinson.

Boston opened the game with a 10-0 run and maintained a comfortable advantage entering halftime.

Then everything changed. Sacramento outscored Boston 35-13 in the third quarter, turning a 13-point deficit into a nine-point lead.

The Celtics missed all 13 of their three-point attempts during the period, an extraordinary shooting collapse that allowed the Kings to seize momentum. Boston finished the game shooting just 22% from beyond the arc despite attempting 58 three-pointers.

Those numbers help explain how a team that appeared comfortably in control suddenly found itself fighting to avoid an embarrassing defeat. The Celtics eventually recovered enough to give themselves a chance in the final seconds.

Payton Pritchard gave Boston a 96-95 lead with 11.8 seconds remaining, seemingly putting the Celtics in position to escape with a victory.

Sacramento had one final opportunity. Rookie Darius Acuff Jr. attacked the basket and missed, but Murray collected the rebound and scored over multiple defenders to put the Kings ahead.

Pritchard attempted a potential game-winning jumper at the buzzer, but the shot missed.

Paul George led Boston with 19 points. Acuff paced Sacramento with 21 points, eight assists and six rebounds, while Murray added 15 points and 12 rebounds.

The Celtics can point to their missing starters as an explanation for some of their difficulties.

But injuries don't completely explain surrendering a 13-point halftime lead against a team that had been struggling so badly. Boston's reliance on perimeter shooting also remains concerning. When the three-pointers stopped falling, the Celtics struggled to generate enough offense to protect their advantage.

The schedule offers little time for reflection. Boston visits the Golden State Warriors on Sunday, giving the Celtics another opportunity to end their skid. After Saturday's collapse, the urgency surrounding that game has increased considerably.
