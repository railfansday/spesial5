Derrick Henry on 10 carries for 10 yards: I don't have an answer, just not good enough

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

With the Ravens starting a quarterback, Jacoby Brissett, who had been on the team for less than two weeks, they hoped to get good production from running back Derrick Henry on Monday night. That didn’t happen.

Henry gained just 10 yards on 10 carries, and another 10 yards on five catches. Afterward he said he couldn’t explain what went wrong in a 23-20 loss to the Chargers.

“I don’t know. I don’t have no answer. Guys who are out there are healthy, trying to do their jobs, we just ain’t good enough,” Henry said.

Overall, the Ravens totaled 15 carries for 16 yards, their lowest rushing yardage output in more than 10 years. Henry did run for two touchdowns, but he also had five plays on which he got no gain or negative yardage. Asked if the absence of quarterback Lamar Jackson is the problem, Henry said the players who are there have to take responsibility.

“We just haven’t been executing. Of course Lamar is a big part of what we do, but it’s a grown-man league,” Henry said. “We just haven’t been doing our jobs good enough, it hasn’t been good enough, and I think losing five in a row shows that. Of course having 8 would help, but at the end of the day, we’ve got to go out there and do our jobs.”

The Ravens are 4-6 and the season appears to be slipping away, but Henry said he’ll always believe they can turn the season around.

“I’m never not gonna believe that we can turn it around and play the way we want to play,” Henry said. “You’ve still got to go to work. The season is still going to be here, you’ve still got to play. None of us are going to be like, ‘Let’s lose the rest of them.’ You’ve still got to do your best.”
