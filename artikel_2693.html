Brock Bowers questionable for Sunday night vs. Browns

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Raiders may get tight end Brock Bowers back for Sunday’s game against the Browns.

Head coach Klint Kubiak told reporters in his Friday press conference that Bowers is questionable for the contest.

Bowers is dealing with an ankle injury. While he was listed as limited on Wednesday and Thursday, he did not participate on Friday.

Bowers missed last week’s loss to the Broncos. If he does not play against the Browns, he’ll have some extended time to recover as the Raiders have a Week 13 bye.

The star tight end has 50 catches for 676 yards with six touchdowns in eight games so far this season.

Kubiak also noted that offensive lineman Jackson Powers-Johnson suffered a potentially significant injury during Friday’s practice and has been ruled out.

Defensive tackles Tonka Hemingway, Adam Butler, and Tim Keenan have all been ruled out for Sunday’s game as well.

Defensive tackle JJ Pegues and cornerback Darrell Luter Jr. are questionable.
