Ravens AFC playoff picture: What's at stake Monday night vs. Chargers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Ravens cannot move into one of the seven playoff spots on Monday night, but they can prevent the gap from growing. Baltimore enters its matchup against the winless Los Angeles Chargers at 4-5, needing a victory to remain one game behind Cleveland for the AFC's final postseason berth.

A win would put the Ravens at 5-5, within reach of the seventh-seeded Browns at 6-4. A loss would drop Baltimore to 4-6 and leave it two games back with eight weeks remaining in the regular season. That difference gives Monday's game considerable weight for a Baltimore team trying to stop a four-game losing streak. The Chargers are 0-8, but the Ravens have little room to overlook anyone after falling behind three division rivals in the conference standings.

Buffalo leads the conference at 8-1, followed by Kansas City, Jacksonville and Cincinnati at 7-2. Denver holds the first wild-card position, also at 7-2, with Pittsburgh and Cleveland occupying the remaining two spots.

New England is immediately ahead of Baltimore among the teams outside the playoff field. A Ravens victory would match the Patriots' record, although conference positioning would still depend on the applicable tiebreakers.

The Browns' 27-24 victory over Houston was particularly consequential for Baltimore. Cleveland erased a 17-0 deficit and won on Quinshon Judkins' touchdown run in the final minute, reaching six victories and keeping control of the final wild-card position.

Pittsburgh's 38-18 loss to Cincinnati prevented the Steelers from pulling farther away, but the Bengals improved to 7-2 and strengthened their position atop the AFC North. Baltimore remains fourth in the division entering Monday.

The NFL playoff picture supplied entering the game gives Baltimore an 18% chance of reaching the postseason. That projection rises to 21% with a victory and falls to 10% with a defeat. Those estimates underline the immediate stakes without making Monday an elimination game. Baltimore would have seven games remaining after facing Los Angeles, leaving opportunities to recover ground.

Still, a loss would require the Ravens to make up two games on Cleveland while also navigating the other teams pursuing a wild-card berth. Beating the Chargers would restore Baltimore to .500 and keep the final playoff spot one game away.
