Merab Dvalishvili and Usman Nurmagomedov brawl way more violent after cameras cut off, claims Aljamain Sterling

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Many expected the worst part of Merab Dvalishvili and Usman Nurmagomedov's brawl was caught on camera; they are wrong.

As he prepared for a slot on the RAF Abu Dhabi card in Abu Dhabi, Merab Dvalishvili was caught by surprise and ambushed by Usman Nurmagomedov and his team. Punches and kicks were exchanged, leaving Merab and his friend Saba Lazishvili to defend themselves against a group of Dagestanis. The altercation has been well-documented, with video footage showing the build-up and subsequent fight. But according to recent reports, fans didn't see the worst of it.

Aljamain Sterling shared his knowledge of the brawl. Despite Team Nurmagomedov citing an incident in Georgia as the reason for the fallout in Abu Dhabi, Sterling shut down those claims, insisting both parties had shaken hands and settled their differences before the viral clash occurred.

"These guys really went out of their way after shaking hands and making amends in Georgia," Sterling said. "Then coming back, nothing happening in Abu Dhabi. Khabib shut everything down, from what I was told, and told the guys not to create any issues. Especially because they already had an agreement. Not an agreement, but they came to terms; shaking hands, it's done."

"And then you do that. It's kind of like a mob movie. It's like, 'Yeah, we're going to pretend we're friends with this guy and then we're going to come behind his back and sneak up on him and run him down. What are we doing? This only ends up one way."

However, what was previously unknown to fans is that the exchange continued once the cameras shut off. Aljamain Sterling claimed that Umar Nurmagomedov approached Merab Dvalishvili once everything had calmed down and punched him in the face.

This was allegedly followed by Saba, Merab's friend, being pinned down and kicked in the head repeatedly.

"Umar comes up after the camera shuts off and decks Merab in the face," Sterling revealed. "After I found out what they actually did- the first part of the video, I can live with it and be like, you know what, we still did okay. Yeah, we might have gotten hurt, but it could have been a lot worse. And just done."

"Then after I heard about what happened after [the cameras shut off], the young kid that was with Merab: Saba. How they held his hands, pinned his arms down, and then start kicking him in the head. I don't feel no remorse for that. How am I supposed to feel remorse for that? What type of person does that? This guy is so much smaller than you guys, and that's what you guys decided to do. It's just not good. It's not okay."

"In the initial video Merab posted, when he was getting cleaned up, he mentioned Umar, and I'm scrubbing the video back and forth. I'm like, 'I don't see Umar. I don't see Umar.' And then he explained what happened afterwards. Obviously, I wasn't there. But who am I going to believe? Damn sure ain't Umar."
