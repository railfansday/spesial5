Aaron Rodgers calls DK Metcalf's ejection "disappointing," won't comment on message to him

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

On certain subjects, Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers doesn’t stop talking. On other topics, he won’t say a single word.

Regarding Sunday’s ejection of Steelers receiver DK Metcalf from the 19-16 loss to the Eagles, Rodgers said far close to nothing than something.

“That was disappointing,” Rodgers told reporters. “Obviously, we love DK. And I don’t know what was going on out there, what was happening. But, yeah, we can’t have two of those.”

“I’m not going to tell you that,” Rodgers said. “I’m going to talk to DK. We’re real good friends, so.”

The “I’m not going to tell you that” was unnecessary, but on brand. Rodgers flashes that aspect of his personality from time to time. And it surely will make those who have to ask him questions on a regular basis not miss him after he retires following the current season.

Besides, why is it such a secret? Rodgers already said it was “disappointing” and that “we can’t have” that. He could have just said that’s the message.

Really, what else would the message be? Metcalf needs to control his emotions. Given that Rodgers has been willing to call out others when it’s required, why should Metcalf be exempt?

Maybe that’s exactly what Metcalf needs in order to keep him from doing it again.
