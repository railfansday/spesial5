Embiid sidelined for Sixers-Blazers with illness

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Embiid sidelined for Sixers-Blazers with illness originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Sixers ruled star center Joel Embiid out with an illness Monday night for their game against the Trail Blazers at Xfinity Mobile Arena.

Embiid first popped up on the Sixers' injury report at 11:45 a.m. ET when the team listed him as questionable. He was downgraded to out about 90 minutes before the 7 p.m. tip-off.

The 32-year-old big man played in the 14 of the Eastern Conference-leading Sixers' first 17 games and averaged 20.4 points, 8.9 rebounds and 4.1 assists. He was the team's top scorer in each of the last three games and finished one assist shy of a 26-point triple-double Saturday in the Sixers' win over the Bucks.

The Sixers entered Monday 2-1 this season in games without Embiid. They were 21-23 last year.

Outside of Embiid, the Sixers are in good shape health-wise. Jalen Brown, Dean Wade and Adem Bona are all set to return from injuries against Portland. Ariel Hukporti (right Achilles tendon tear) is out indefinitely.

The Sixers went with a starting five of Tyrese Maxey, VJ Edgecombe, Brown, LeBron James and Jabari Walker. It's the second start of the year for Walker, who spent his first three NBA seasons with the Blazers and has been an early-season bright spot off the Sixers' bench.
