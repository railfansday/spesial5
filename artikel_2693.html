Geno Smith held out of the end zone in Week 9 loss at Kansas City

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

New York Jets QB Geno Smith completed 18 of 32 passes for 165 yards with no touchdowns or interceptions in Week 9 against the Kansas City Chiefs. He ran once for eight yards and was sacked five times.

Why It Matters: Smith was held without a touchdown pass for the first time this season since the opener against Tennessee and threw for his second-fewest yards this season against a tough Kansas City defense. It did not help his cause that his top receiver, Garrett Wilson, did not play due to a concussion. Smith has 12 TDs against just three interceptions this season after throwing an NFL-high 17 last season. He's still a QB2 option, although he should have some streaming appeal in Week 10 against the Bills.
