Rob Pelinka believes the Lakers are feeding off Luka Dončić’s leadership

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Behind Luka Dončić's wondrous play, which has thrust him into the center of early season MVP conversations, the Los Angeles Lakers are off to a roaring 15-3 start and tied for the West's No. 1 seed. Whether it's his elite self-creation, willingness to embrace more off-ball work, brilliant playmaking or growth on the defensive end, there are many on-court factors explaining why Dončić has been so impactful for Los Angeles this year.

But Lakers GM Rob Pelinka also believes Dončić's leadership is paramount to this team's success thus far. Pelinka deems Dončić's leadership as bred through plenty of personal experiences that have shaped him in recent years.

"Adversity in life is what makes us all grow and he's been through a lot. Getting traded, he's had some family stuff, making sure he can stay close to his daughters," Pelinka said prior to Friday's win over the Sacramento Kings. "That's all adversity and I've seen it really grow his character in the form of leadership just being able to go through some of that stuff that life throws all of us, it throws our way. The guys in the locker room feed off his leadership. The other thing I've really seen about Luka is he takes accountability. He has so many great games but if he has a down game, he looks around the guys, he's like, 'This one's on me. I've got to be better.' I think a great leader can recognize that and really have seen that growth in him this year."

Pelinka's mention of accountability was readily on display last week when the Lakers' nine-game winning streak came to a halt after they lost to the Utah Jazz. That night, Dončić scored 29 points on 10-of-28 shooting (2-of-12 from deep) and had seven assists to nine turnovers. Following the defeat, Dončić didn't mince words about his own play and refused to let the second leg of a back-to-back be any sort of excuse for the team's poor performance.

"I mean, honestly, I just got to be way better than this," Dončić told reporters. "This is unacceptable for my part. I've got to lead the team in better ways. I look at this game, got to move on and learn from this."

The Lakers will hope both Dončić's leadership and on-court splendor power them to a win Friday night when they face the San Antonio Spurs in a clash between 15-3 teams vying for the West's top spot.
