The Patriots will look to keep their momentum going Sunday when they host the Packers at Gillette Stadium. Mike Vrabel's team has won four of its last five games and enters the weekend at 5-3, giving New England an opportunity to make another statement against an NFC contender.

The Patriots are coming off a 24-19 comeback victory over the Dolphins in Week 8. New England trailed 13-3 at halftime, but it rallied behind Drake Maye to secure its fifth win of the season.

The Packers also enter this matchup at 5-3 after defeating the Panthers on Oct. 29. Jordan Love threw three touchdown passes against Carolina, and Green Bay's defense recorded six sacks to help secure the victory.
