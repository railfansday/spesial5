The Bills widened their first place lead in the the AFC with their win over the Jets

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Buffalo Bills beat the New York Jets on Sunday and with the Denver Broncos on a bye, the Bills have now opened a full one-game lead in the AFC. The Kansas City Chiefs actually pass the Broncos thanks to a superiors record in the AFC West, so Buffalo's lead is over K.C as well as the 7-2 Jacksonville Jaguars.

In the AFC East, the New England Patriots lost in Germany, which drops them to 5-5. The Bills have a 3-game lead in the division and the Patriots have games against the Bills, Vikings, Chiefs, and Broncos still upcoming.

With so many games going on still here, we will update but the headline won't change; the Bills have a playoff picture stranglehold.
