NFL Week 9 Survivor/Eliminator Picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

There's a strategy to NFL Survivor. You could, before Week 1, arrange the teams 1-32 in order of Super Bowl odds and simply go down the list week by week. But you'd run into issues — some obvious, some less so. And even if it went well, by Week 17 or Week 18, you could be playing for a title and have no one better than someone like the Buccaneers or Raiders left available to you. Do I want to write that being strategic explains why I have three losses in eight weeks? Well, yeah. It's not true, but any excuse, you know? But the strategic factor plays in every week. It's not just picking a team that should win; it's picking a team that should win while not burning all your good late-season options. Check out my Week 9 pick for Survivor contests. And while we're here, you can use our NFL Betting Model and our Bet Tracker to make a pick for yourself.

Every week, we have the same process. We eliminate teams in groups until we narrow it down to our real considerations. Start with all 32, whittle it down. And remember — this game is "once you use a team, they're off the board," so it's not always just a matter of taking the team you think will win. There has to be some strategy. I can't pick a team I've picked before, but you might not have tailed me, so previously used teams are eligible until the end.
