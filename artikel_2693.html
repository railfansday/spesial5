Pistons vs. Hawks Discussion: Game Time, TV, Odds, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Detroit Pistons look to rebound by hitting the road and visiting the 10-6 Atlanta Hawks. The Pistons will need to deliver an all-around better effort than they did against the Toronto Raptors, particularly inside. The Raptors owned the paint against the Pistons, and the Hawks promise to be an even tougher task for the short-handed Pistons. Jalen Duren remains out, as does Duncan Robinson. Duren's absence could be huge today as Jalen Johnson makes a living by carving up defenses in an effort to get downhill and into the painted area. Ron Holland is suiting up today, and that at least gives the Pistons a little extra defensive heft.
