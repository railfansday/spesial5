Colts activate Arden Key, place Mo Alie-Cox and Akeem Davis-Gaither on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Colts have not won a game since defensive end Arden Key went on injured reserve and he’ll be back as they try to snap their five-game losing streak against the Dolphins on Sunday.

Key missed all five of those games with a hamstring injury. He started the first four games of the season and had five tackles, a forced fumble and a half-sack in those appearances.

While Key will be back, the Colts will be without tight end Mo Alie-Cox (groin) and linebacker Akeem Davis-Gaither (knee) for at least the next four games. Both players were placed on injured reserve on Saturday.

Key takes one of those roster spots and the other has been filled by signing linebacker West Weeks off of the practice squad. Long snapper Jacob Bobenmoyer and wide receiver Quez Watkins were elevated from the practice squad in other moves.
