Crusaders Heaven tier list of every skin in the game

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

A detailed tier list of normal and exclusive skins in Crusader Heaven ranked by their effectiveness.

Crusaders Heaven is one of the best Roblox combat games based on JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure. As you play, you'll see that the exciting skins, trading system, and accessories make Crusaders Heaven interesting not only for JoJo fans but also for those who like collectables. In this article, you will find every in-game skin sorted into an impressive Crusaders Heaven tier list.

It includes normal skins available at the time of writing and limited exclusives that can be obtained only during a specific time. The tier list might come in handy not only for beginners but also for experienced players who want to expand their knowledge about Crusaders Heaven.

WHAT IS OUR CRUSADERS HEAVEN TIER LIST? The game has a comprehensive skins system, and our Crusaders Heaven tier list includes a particular Extra Tier for event-exclusive skins. They can't be obtained nowadays but are still worth a unique place in this guide.

Tier SS includes the rarest skins in Crusaders Heaven. These are a great way to stand out among other players and look completely unique. The only issue is that Tier SS skins are the rarest in Crusaders Heaven, so you might just end up spending a lot of time trying to get them.

The World: OVA The World: OVA is the most exciting skin you can get for The World stand, the main stand of Dio Brando. It's a strong stand that's most effective at close range. Using The World with OVA skin is the best way to make your character look more appealing.

Tier S still includes valuable skins. Even though getting them is a little bit more straightforward than tier SS skins, they are hard to get if you're not spending real money on the game.

Star Platinum Television Star Platinum Television is the skin for one of the most potent stands in Crusaders Heaven. Even though it only features visual improvements for your character, using Television Skin for your Star Platinum is one of the best ways to stand out among all players.

Tier A skins are way more accessible than tier SS/S skins. Using tier A skins is a great option if you want to look cool and don’t want to donate money or spend dozens of hours trying to obtain the best ones.

Star Platinum: The Galaxy The Galaxy is another skin for Star Platinum stand in Crusaders Heaven. Even though this skin is less rare than Star Platinum Television, many players still like to opt for The Galaxy instead of Television.

Tier B includes valuable skins that can be used even during later game stages. They are perfectly suitable for trading in order to get more expensive skins, as well as for wearing them just to look cool. You should never underestimate tier B skins in Crusaders Heaven.

Diego Diego, or Diego Brando, is an outfit of one of the secondary antagonists in Crusaders Heaven. Even though this skin seems pretty valuable, it belongs to Tier B. So, getting it is not a big problem even if you are a beginner.

Tier C includes the most accessible skins in Crusaders Heaven. Even though experienced players don't really appreciate outfits from tier C, they are still widely used among beginners. And if you're not a big fan, remember you can use tier C skins to trade for a better outfit.

Shiny King Crimson Shiny King Crimson is one of the most accessible skins for the King Crimson stand. It is a human-looking stand that appears slightly bigger than your character in Crusaders Heaven.

Tier E is the last tier and includes the worst skins in Crusaders Heaven. These skins have no value, which isn't surprising since most tier E skins are based on the appearance of minor characters from JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure.

Shiny Killer Queen Shiny Killer Queen is a copy of Shiny King Crimson, but for the Killer Queen stand of Kira Yoshikage in Crusaders Heaven. The only difference is that Shiny Killer Queen is more accessible, which is why it's in tier E.

The Event tier includes exclusive event stand skins that are available only during a specific time of the year. Sometimes, Event tier skins are more difficult to obtain than those in tier SS. So if you have one, you're sure to stand out from the crowd if you wear the exclusive skins from this tier.

Valentine Platinum Valentine Platinum is considered the most valuable skin for the Star Platinum stand, which will make you look unique even when compared to players with tier SS skins. The only issue is that it is nearly impossible to get Valentine Platinum nowadays.

And that's our Crusaders Heaven tier list! Keep in mind that this article is regularly updated. It always includes skins from the latest Crusaders Heaven updates, so feel free to share it with friends.
