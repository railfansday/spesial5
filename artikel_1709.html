Opinion: He's extraordinary, midfield sensation is now destined to join Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

One midfielder is looking withing Liverpool's grasp and he's extraordinary.

Andoni Iraola will be the first to concede that right now he probably doesn't have all the tools he would want in his garage in order to fix the car that is Liverpool.

This newer model that the Reds are at the moment has come to Iraola with a myriad of flaws. The engine barely works and the exhaust fumes are overloaded with excess debris that he needs to get rid of.

The frame of the car is weak, too. Vulnerable to crashes and he needs to make it strong in order to build a squad that is actually capable of surviving the arduous and demanding campaign where Liverpool aspire to compete on all fronts.

But there are signs as Iraola takes his Liverpool team for a spin every now and again that he's getting there.
