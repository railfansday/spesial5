Report: Liverpool battling Premier League rivals for African star

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Liverpool's start to life under Andoni Iraola has been steady, but the Reds aren't yet title contenders.

Despite spending over £650 million since lifting the Premier League trophy in 2025, Liverpool have been nowhere near the title race.

Iraola's side find themselves fourth in the league, but already nine points shy of first after 10 matches.

It is becoming clear that there is still work to be done on the squad, and the Anfield recruitment team are hard at work trying to identify the right deals for the upcoming winter window.

Several exciting names have been linked, but the Reds have now reportedly joined the race for another.

Bilal El Khannouss has proven himself as one of the best young talents in the Bundesliga since his move from Leicester at the start of last season.

The 47-capped Morocco international can play on either wing but is primarily used as an attacking midfielder, and has become a key part of Stuttgart's attacking unit.

The 22-year-old has had a fairly slow start to this campaign, scoring twice in 15 appearances, 10 of which were starts.

El Khannouss' talent is clear to see though, and the dynamic attacker produced 16 goals and assists for Stuttgart last season, also providing two assists for Leicester before his move.

The Moroccan was heavily linked with a switch to Brighton in the summer, but the deal ultimately fell through, and now several Premier League clubs are interested.

That's according to Ekrem Konur, who has revealed Liverpool alongside Leeds and Newcastle are chasing a deal for El Khannouss, with Galatasaray also closely monitoring the situation.

Liverpool are currently well-stocked for wide options but lack a true alternative to Florian Wirtz in the number 10 role, a gap that El Khannouss could easily fill for the Reds.

Konur doesn't reveal a potential fee, but with interest so high a bidding war could ensue, driving the 22-year-old's price higher.

Khannouss would be a sensible addition for the Reds and is definitely a player to keep an eye on heading into the winter window.
