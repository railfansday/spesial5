Strategy Buys $82M in Bitcoin as STRC Tokenized Stock Adds New Twist to Saylor’s BTC

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Strategy Bitcoin holdings climbed to 850,364 BTC after Michael Saylor’s company purchased another 1,036 Bitcoin for $81.9 million. The latest acquisition came as Bitcoin traded below the company’s average purchase price, putting its enormous treasury under renewed market pressure.

According to a November 9 SEC filing, Strategy bought the Bitcoin between November 2 and 8 at an average price of $79,012 per coin. Its total acquisition cost now stands at $64.16 billion, with an average purchase price of $75,456.

The latest purchase also coincided with a $188.3 million repurchase of Strategy’s STRC preferred shares. The company funded both transactions through its common-stock offering program, highlighting how Michael Saylor continues using capital markets to finance its Bitcoin accumulation.

Strategy sold 2,051,348 shares of its Class A common stock through its at-the-market offering program during the reporting period. The sales generated $283.8 million in net proceeds. The company allocated then $81.9 million toward Bitcoin purchases, while $188.3 million went toward repurchasing 1,887,464 STRC preferred shares. Another $13.6 million was allocated to its general USD cash balance.

The latest transactions show how Strategy accumulation involves more than simply issuing shares and purchasing Bitcoin. The company is also actively managing its preferred securities and liquidity.

Strategy maintained approximately $4.77 billion in its USD Reserve and another $146.2 million in general USD Cash. This reserve supports preferred-stock dividends and interest payments on outstanding debt.

Meanwhile, Bitcoin’s decline below Strategy’s average acquisition price has put its treasury underwater on paper. At $74,000 per BTC, the company’s holdings would carry an unrealized loss exceeding $1 billion. But the latest purchase shows that Saylor has not backed away from accumulation despite the weaker market environment.

The $188.3 million repurchase also puts Strategy’s preferred securities back into the spotlight. STRC, known as Stretch, is a variable-rate perpetual preferred stock designed to trade around its $100 stated amount. It pays cash dividends, with its rate adjusted periodically to encourage price stability.

The instrument has also entered the tokenized securities market through Ondo Finance’s STRCon. Launched in April 2026, STRCon provides blockchain-based economic exposure to Strategy’s Stretch preferred stock. The token is available across Ethereum, Solana, and BNB Chain, subject to geographic and investor restrictions.

Tokenholders receive economic exposure and dividend-related benefits, but do not obtain conventional shareholder voting rights. This gives Strategy’s digital credit securities another distribution channel. Investors outside traditional brokerage platforms can access tokenized exposure through supported blockchain infrastructure.

However, tokenized trading does not generate fresh capital for Strategy automatically. Secondary-market transactions change ownership between investors and do not directly fund additional Bitcoin purchases. Still, this fits into Strategy’s longer-term ambition to build digital credit products for an increasingly tokenized financial system.

Strategy’s latest purchase arrived during a difficult period for institutional Bitcoin demand. US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $1.3 billion in net outflows last week. Last Friday alone accounted for $411.9 million in withdrawals.

BlackRock’s IBIT led Friday’s outflows with approximately $403 million in redemptions. Fidelity’s FBTC recorded $42.8 million in inflows, offering only a partial offset.

Bitcoin also fell toward $74,000 this morning, reaching its weakest level since August. The decline followed broader crypto selling pressure and growing concerns surrounding regulatory uncertainty as AI stocks slide.

The contrast between ETF withdrawals and Strategy’s continued accumulation highlights two different approaches to Bitcoin exposure. ETF investors can reduce exposure through daily redemptions, while Strategy continues using equity financing and its preferred-stock structure to expand its treasury.

The latest Bitcoin acquisition from Strategy reinforces the company’s long-term commitment. Strategy Bitcoin holdings now exceed 850,000 BTC, leaving the company with more than 4% of Bitcoin’s maximum supply.

Whether that commitment pays off will depend heavily on Bitcoin’s next market cycle, alongside Strategy’s ability to maintain access to capital markets.
