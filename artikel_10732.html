Viral campaign urged people to push Chicago’s Bean to help “man trapped inside”

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Hundreds of people showed up in Chicago in an attempt to push The Bean around seven inches following a social media campaign about it.

Over the years, when social media has tried to organize people to show up for a cause, it can fall flat. Back in 2019, more than 1 million people signed up to ‘Naruto run’ past the Area 51 guards so that they could get inside. However, only a handful of people actually showed up.

On November 7, signs in Chicago started going viral as they urged people to help them push The Bean – otherwise known as the Cloud Gate statue – around seven inches to the left on November 8.

In the end, plenty of people turned up to help with the ultimately unsuccessful task, but it did draw plenty of attention online.

they’re trying to push the bean in chicago? anyone else catching this? pic.twitter.com/3DKfEUDdkJ

One invite to the push attempt stated that “there is a man trapped inside the bean” and the push would help them “be able to access that door and give the man inside the bean resources.”

Obviously, there isn’t a man living inside The Bean.

As per BookClubChicago, around 250 people showed up in the end, with some claiming that they managed to move the statue by at least one inch. Though, that probably didn’t happen, seeing as the weight of the sculpture would require some serious mechanical help to move it.

A post shared by Alex Saddler- Gatekeep Chicago (@gatekeepchicago)

Other outlets noted that there have been a number of attempts to pull off stunts around The Bean. That included a plan in 2017 to ‘Windex The Bean,’ with more than 2000 people saying they would help clean it. That didn’t happen either.
