Make the most of the Black Friday sales with a great deal on a new gaming laptop. At Best Buy, you can pick up Asus' ROG Strix G16 at the deal price of $1,749.99. This is a low price for a gaming laptop that houses a powerful gaming GPU such as the RTX 5070 Ti, a formidable card for playing the latest game titles. You're saving $550 from its all-time high $2,299.99 listing price.

Rammed inside this SKU of the ROG Strix G16 is the aforementioned Nvidia GeForce RTX 5070 Ti laptop graphics card, an AMD Ryzen 9 16-core 8940HX processor, 16GB of DDR5 RAM, and a 1TB Gen 4 SSD for storage. This combination of components is more than ample for laptop gaming, and should the prices of RAM and storage dip in the future, there's room to upgrade both if you require.

Nvidia's RTX5070 Ti can also make use of the latest DLSS tech to provide smoother frame rates in some circumstances. Pair that with a 16-inch IPS display that has a 1900 x 1200 pixel resolution, 16:10 screen ratio, 165Hz refresh rate, and 3ms response time, and you have a complete mobile gaming package.

The Asus ROG Strix G16 comes with a 16-inch screen, with an 1900 x 1200 pixel resolution, a 16:10 aspect ratio, and refresh and response rates of 165Hz and 3ms, respectively.
