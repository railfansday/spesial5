US-mediated talks on ending Russia-Ukraine war begin in Abu Dhabi

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Three-way talks between Russia, Ukraine and the US opened Tuesday, November 10, in the United Arab Emirates, as Washington made another push to end the war. The two-day session in Abu Dhabi is the first such meeting since February. It will focus on establishing future security guarantees for the warring countries.
