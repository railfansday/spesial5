Ronaldinho makes return at age of 46: Brazil legend plays full first-half for Italian third-tier team and hints he may play again despite 'publicity stunt' criticism

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Published: 15:51 EST, 15 November 2026 | Updated: 15:52 EST, 15 November 2026

Former Ballon d'Or winner Ronaldinho made a stunning comeback to professional football at the age of 46 as he turned out for a third-division Italian side on Sunday.

The former Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain and AC Milan superstar announced his retirement from football after a short stint with Fluminense in 2015.

Having enjoyed retirement for 11 years, he shocked world football by agreeing a surprise transfer to Serie C outfit Ravenna, where he was set to undertake a marketing role.

It was initially understood that he would not be making a playing return, but the Brazil legend laced up his boots on Sunday and played a part in his side's opening goal.

Ravenna's honorary vice president Ariedo Braida said in June that the World Cup winner would regrettably not be able to play for the club due to his age.

But it appears that went up in smoke, with Ronaldinho playing 45 minutes on his return in the league clash – despite his move being dubbed a publicity stunt by the club.

Brazil icon Ronaldinho has made a stunning comeback to professional football at the age of 46

He played the first-half of the game, playing a role in the first goal for his new side

He helped set up Giulio Donati's opening goal of the match, and raced across the field to mob the goal scorer as he celebrated.

He started the game, playing the first-half before being replaced by Matteo Maggio. Before his substitution, he embraced the fans in the stands at the interval.

The icon hinted there could be more games to come, however.

He told Sky Italia after the game: 'It went by too fast, that has always been football for me.

'Is this my last one? I don't know. If the president keeps calling me, I will come back. It is always a pleasure. I told the president, if he needs me, then I will be here.

'It was lovely to be here, playing, having the build-up in the dressing room. Football has always been the most wonderful thing in my life.'

Ronaldinho has also become a shareholder in the club.

During his illustrious career, he won the World Cup, a Champions League, two LaLiga titles and a Serie A trophy before hanging up his boots in 2015.

He also won a string of individual accolades including the Ballon d'Or in 2005 and FIFA World Player of the Year in 2004 and 2005.
