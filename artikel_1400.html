Gasperini’s Roma earns the respect of the football world

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Gasperini’s Roma showed off their true essence to the football world, and they did so on a Champions League night away to Paris Saint-Germain, European champions for the past two seasons.

There was no better stage to gain confidence in their abilities and earn the respect of the giants of the game. PSG was coming off a 12-match winning streak against: Slovan Bratislava (6-1), Brest (0-1), Marseille (1-2), Le Mans (3-1), Manchester City (2-4), Strasbourg (1-3), Barcelona (4-2), Lyon (2-0), Le Havre (1-5), Villareal (0-6), Troyes (1-0), and Nice (2-3).

Then came Gasperini’s Roma, who, after just five minutes, rocked the Parc des Princes with Mario Hermoso’s lead.

The match ended 2-2, but the Giallorossi may have some regrets, given that the Spanish center-back had chances to give his team the three points, even in the final minutes. “We were used to winning, then Roma came along,” Joao Neves bitterly commented.

Gasperini’s Roma gave the giants a run for their money, the strongest team in the world, which, despite the absences of Kvaratskhelia and Doue, remains among the best teams around.

The highest praise came from PSG members themselves, who acknowledged Roma’s strength: “These are the teams we enjoy playing against and competing against. I think it’s precisely games like this that allow us to grow, learn, and gain experience in this competition.”

Joao Neves was clear: the Giallorossi are a great team to face, a team that has won the last two Champions League titles and this year thrashed record-breaking Barcelona 4-2, currently leading La Liga. A remarkable demonstration of respect.

Luis Enrique went further: “It’s clearly the most difficult match of the season against a Roma side whose quality we know. It was very tough, so I congratulate Roma. We could have lost, without a doubt. I see a bright future for them and I think they can win the Scudetto; it wouldn’t surprise me.” Gasperini’s team is working to become a major force in Italy and Europe: they passed the toughest test at the Parc des Princes with flying colors. They’re on the right path, Gasperini promises.
