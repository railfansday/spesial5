The Dutch press is crazy about Xavi’s team, Maza will extend with Leverkusen

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Dutch press is completely under the spell of its selection! The Oranje crushed Greece (7-0), after a particularly successful performance. Algemeen Dagblad describes a wild team, which gave its opponent no respite. The daily particularly emphasizes the complementarity between Frenkie de Jong and Joey Veerman, whose association completely stifled the Greeks. Same enthusiasm in De Telegraafwhich speaks of a real “ stroke of genius » to describe this midfield duo. For his part, by Volkskrant evokes an exceptional football spectacle during this Mad Friday in Amsterdam. And this collective demonstration owes a lot to the understanding between the two circles. De Jong comes down regularly to organize the play and dictate the pace, while Veerman brings his passing quality to the more advanced areas. A balance which completely outwitted Greece. Even coach Xavi Hernández seemed to have a lot of fun on his bench, according to Algemeen Dagblad. The Dutch press is therefore unanimous: this team has something to do very badly!

Bayer Leverkusen does not want to lose its new jewel! Ibrahim Maza, who already attracts the desire of several European big names such as FC Barcelona, ​​PSG and Manchester City, would be very close to extending his adventure in Germany. According to BILDthe club sent a written offer to the Algerian attacking midfielder to extend his contract until 2031. And the German leaders are ready to make a significant financial effort to convince their nugget. The player’s salary could simply double to around 5 million euros per season. Arriving from Hertha Berlin in 2025, Maza quickly established himself and now embodies the future of the Leverkusen project. The club even sees him as its next big sale at 100 million euros! The formalization of this extension is expected before Christmas. Suffice it to say that Bayer wants to take the lead before the contenders take action.

A new page is opening at Manchester United! Officially appointed after the dismissal of Michael Carrick, Mauricio Pochettino had a particularly moving moment when talking about his departure from the American selection. The Argentinian even described this decision as “ most difficult of his career “, but the opportunity to return to Old Trafford was too good to refuse. And the English press has already started to list the jobs that await the new coach of the Red Devils. THE Daily Mail identifies four priority projects for Pochettino: quickly stringing together victories to get United out of the relegation zone, regaining real physical intensity, restoring order to a locker room under tension and above all solidifying a beleaguered defense. The daily also points out the difficulties of Bruno Fernandes, who is going through a complicated period, as well as the tensions between Lisandro Martínez and Tielemans. Finally, the new technician will have to quickly find the right settings in midfield and reassure his goalkeeper Senne Lammens. A huge project therefore awaits the former Tottenham coach, who will have to quickly convince before his first big meeting against Liverpool at Anfield!
