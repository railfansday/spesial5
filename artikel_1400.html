Thomas Tuchel faces fight to win trust of England players

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Thomas Tuchel faces a fight to regain the full trust of his England players, despite claiming that “99.9 per cent” of supporters do not talk about the semi-final defeat by Argentina.

Telegraph Sport reported that players were shocked and disappointed by Tuchel’s approach to the final 30 minutes of the World Cup semi-final.

It can now be revealed that doubts linger among England players over Tuchel’s ability to get the best out of his squad and ultimately lead them to glory in the next European Championship.

There is also some lasting unhappiness over Tuchel’s perceived unwillingness to take ultimate responsibility for England’s World Cup exit and his claim that ball possession was not in “the DNA” of the nation’s players.

Tuchel has spoken of fostering a “brotherhood” within the England squad, but a source told Telegraph Sport: “He still has work to do to get all the players fully behind him again. What happened at the World Cup and some of his comments caused some damage that has not just disappeared.”

Tuchel has no concerns over the commitment of his players and said: “I have no doubt how keen they are. No doubt. I know them, I was seven weeks with them. We had a qualification campaign together, we are 18 months together, I have no doubt that we step up and produce performances in the next cycle. I’m responsible for that.”

One player who did not travel to the World Cup and is not part of Tuchel’s latest England squad for four Nations League games is Morgan Gibbs-White. His partner, Britney De Villiers, took a swipe at Tuchel by posting an image on Instagram that referenced the fact the Nottingham Forest man is the only Premier League player to have created five or more chances in every match he has played this season.

There is no immediate sign that Tuchel is ready to change his approach, as he refused to accept the country were angered by his tactics and substitutions in the Argentina game.

In an attempt to gloss over the nature of England’s missed opportunity in the United States, Tuchel claimed that the bronze-medal victory over France was “one of the nicest experiences ever” for supporters and that he did not lose any sleep over his World Cup squad selection.

Tuchel’s defence of the World Cup campaign also included the revelation that his Football Association bosses had assured the England head coach that his job was safe before the last-16 victory over Mexico.

“I tell you something, like 99.9 per cent of the fans don’t speak about substitutions, tactical twists,” Tuchel said. “They speak about where they have watched the Mexico game, they speak about the second half, the energy of the Croatia match, they speak about how nerve-wracking it was to watch our match against Congo and where they celebrated.

“This is how it should be, this is what a World Cup is for, to connect with the people and to give them experiences that they will not forget. And this is the feedback that I get.”

Asked if he had received any negative feedback at all since the World Cup, Tuchel answered: “No. Not at all. Do you think that’s necessary? I had some quite bad stuff between Argentina and France. [But] No [not since I’ve been back in England].”

The FA publicly backed Tuchel after the defeat by Argentina, but the German has now revealed that he was privately told his job was safe well before that.

Even if England had lost to Mexico and failed to reach the last eight of the World Cup, Tuchel has claimed he would not have been sacked. He said: “There was a moment, a very special moment, before the Mexico game where we had a talk and my bosses told me ‘we know that anything can happen in a football match, we know that anything can happen in a match in Mexico, in a World Cup match, but don’t worry, we see what’s going on and we want to continue like this’.

“It was a beautiful moment for me as a coach. I felt this kind of freedom to take my choices and to just coach freely, and there was never a doubt in my mind that we would continue together and there was obviously never a doubt in their mind.”

England secured their best World Cup finish since 1966 by winning the bronze-medal game against France in a chaotic match in Miami.

“If you look at the 6-4 against France, people talk about it like it’s one of their nicest experiences ever, to watch a football match,” Tuchel said. “So maybe I have to compromise in my wish for control, for a little bit more chaos, embrace the chaos, and maybe it brings out the best.”

Tuchel has named his first England squad since the World Cup for four Nations League games against Spain, Czech Republic (home and away) and Croatia. He has recalled Cole Palmer and Trent Alexander-Arnold to his squad, but insists he has lost no sleep over not taking the pair to the World Cup.

“I was very happy with the [World Cup] squad selection, we put a lot of effort and emphasis into the squad selection,” Tuchel said. “And I had a lot of sleepless nights, but I had not one sleepless night about our selection, because I was more than seven weeks with this group of players, and they were nothing but fantastic. I think the spirit that we built and the mentality that we showed brought us to the place that we got.

“It feels unfulfilled, but, yeah, that’s how it is. We need to move forward with the decisions in the Argentina game. And sometimes as a coach, you can only win if you win. So now we know the result, and we didn’t win. Of course, I reflect and, of course, I’m very critical of myself. But the question that is also still lingering around is, who knows what would have happened? Who knows if we hadn’t made any substitutions what would have happened? And would we have the same sleepless nights?

“So, there’s not much sense in torturing ourselves, looking back. It’s moving forward, but the team, like I said, did brilliantly. And I will not allow 30 minutes against Argentina to override how we view this World Cup.”

Tuchel had warnings for Palmer and Alexander-Arnold, as he said: “It is one thing to be nominated, by the way, with Cole and getting your ticket back in the squad, but it’s another thing to play and keep your place. It’s about when he comes and he needs to show these kinds of performances.”

On Alexander-Arnold, Tuchel added: “Now it’s time to fight for his place. He always said he’s up for a fight, especially for England. So it’s a need to prove that.”

Tuchel said that Alexander-Arnold has been called up as a right-back, rather than a midfielder. Asked if the Real Madrid player offers enough defensively, he answered by saying: “He has to, he has to. It’s necessary. My conviction is that he will, but he has no choice.”
