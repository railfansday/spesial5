49ers stars send message to Mexican fans as Faithful set to invade

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The 49ers are known for having one of the NFL's most well-traveled fan bases.

And many of their players are gearing up to see the Red and Gold well represented Sunday night when they take on the Vikings in Mexico City.

George Kittle of the San Francisco 49ers. Getty Images

A handful of their players, including star tight end George Kittle and receiver Deebo Samuel, expressed their excitement for playing south of the border before their practice Thursday in Colorado Springs, Colo.

The 49ers are coming off a 46-40 road win against the Cowboys in which visiting fans came close to outnumbering Cowboys fans. The 49ers Faithful has a reputation for taking over road stadiums, most notably when San Francisco plays in LA against the Rams.

San Francisco 49ers wide receiver Deebo Samuel Sr. (1) runs off the field at halftime during an NFL football game against the Chicago Bears, Dec. 8, 2024. Getty Images

The Niners have official marketing rights in Mexico through the NFL's Global Markets Program. Their fans were well represented the last time they played there in 2022 when they beat the Cardinals, 38-10, despite a number of starters showing up to the game late because of traffic.

The Cardinals were technically the home team in the game, but the crowd was heavily tilted toward the 49ers. Coach Kyle Shanahan remembered the fans making noise in his team's favor.

"It was awesome," Shanahan told reporters this week. "It was definitely a home game that week. I always joke, I go to Cabo a lot. I feel like our Mexican fan base is off the chain. They're unbelievable. They're there. They come up to wherever we go, and (I) definitely expect them to be there in Mexico City. Last time we went there, it felt like almost the whole stadium."
