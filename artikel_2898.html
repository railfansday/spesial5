San Antonio vs. Los Angeles, Final Score: Spurs outlast Clippers in 124-112 win

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It didn't take long for the Spurs to get back on the winning track. The Silver and Black didn't amaze, but they took care of business at home against a shorthanded Clippers team. The 124-112 score might suggest that it was a comfortable victory, but it was mostly a close game in which the more talented squad simply stepped on the gas late to close it out.

The Spurs, facing an inferior opponent after the loss to the Pistons but missing Dylan Harper, didn't look as dominant as it would have been ideal to start the game. The pace wasn't great, and neither was the decision-making for most of the first half. Defensively, they had good stretches but also moments in which they allowed dribble penetration without much resistance or left shooters open. It wasn't the strong bounceback many were hoping for, but because of their tremendous talent, they managed to lead by 14 points at one point in the second quarter.

Unfortunately, the lead disappeared as the bench had one of its all-too-familiar poor runs. In all fairness, however, the second unit wasn't the only reason why the home team only led by three at the break, as the starters didn't return with the energy required to pull away. Some good moments showed the high ceiling the Silver and Black have, but it wasn't a particularly inspiring first half. The Clippers deserve credit for capitalizing on the at times lackadaisical and downright sloppy play from the Spurs, but a lot of San Antonio's wounds in the opening two frames were self-inflicted.

In the second half, the visitors went from simply being opportunistic to actively hurting the Spurs on the offensive end. The threes were falling, and they were not afraid to hunt mismatches, using Brook Lopez in the post, especially in the third quarter. Ty Lue's guys were confident after coming back to make it close earlier and put themselves ahead on the scoreboard with more consistent play, while San Antonio relied on individual stretches of greatness from different players. Castle had it going as a scorer, Devin Vassell made some defensive plays, and even some of the bench guys got in on the action.

A legitimate blowout was out of the question after three quarters, but the Spurs have been good at elevating their play when they need to throughout this season. It's been enough for them to beat lesser teams, and it happened again against the Clippers. It wasn't the prettiest of games, something that has also been a trend this year, but San Antonio did what it needed to. As the minutes passed, the Silver and Black got a double-digit lead, and they didn't squander it late. Hopefully, it will be the beginning of another long win streak.

As expected, Mitch Johnson started Julian Champagnie in place of Dylan Harper, out with a hip injury. The Fox-Castle-Vassell-Champagnie-Wembanyama unit started most of last season and outscored opponents by over 17 points per 100 possessions, so it should be fine. Still, there were some moments early when the lack of a third ball handler seemed to bother San Antonio. The turnovers piled up as some players, especially Stephon Castle, often forced the issue. The Spurs will miss Harper, which was expected.

Devin Vassell continues to be cold in the outside shooting department (3-for-16 in the last four games), but as usual nowadays, he made an impact elsewhere. Hopefully the shooting touch returns soon.

Carter Bryant was the first guy off the bench for the Harper-less Spurs. In the second half, Jordan McLaughlin got the call. Mitch Johnson is experimenting with his rotations and trying to find guys that can replace Harper's athleticism and ball-handling. We might see different players get minutes depending on what the team needs on any given night.

The second unit has been getting a lot of flak, mostly deserved, for their play early in the season, but they were huge for the Spurs on Thursday. They brought a lot of energy to a game that sometimes lacked it, at least from San Antonio's side.

Brandon Ingram, the big piece going back to the Clippers in the Kawhi Leonard trade, was out with an injury. The other player Los Angeles received in the transaction, Gradey Dick, wasn't all that effective in limited minutes. The Clippers' major summer move had little effect on the result of this one.

The release of Grand Theft Auto VI provided the Spurs' broadcast with the perfect excuse to show one of the best photos ever: the 99 team playing StarCraft together after winning the title. Look at those magnificent nerds.

The Spurs will host the Pelicans on Saturday. New Orleans will be in the second game of a back-to-back after playing the Thunder on Friday.
