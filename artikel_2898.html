No NY Giants on final injury report for Week 11 Jacksonville game

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The New York Giants do not have a single player with an injury designation for Sunday's key Week 11 game against the Jacksonville Jaguars.

The Giants' final injury report looks like this:

Out: NoneDoubtful: NoneQuestionable: None

Tight end Isaiah Likely missed practice with a groin injury. Head coach John Harbaugh called it a "minor thing" and said Likely would be ready to face the Jaguars. The lack of an injury designation for Likely means that will be the case.

Two Giants — cornerback Nic Jones and safety Elijah Campbell — were listed as limited in practice. Jones had missed practice on Wednesday and Thursday with a knee injury. Campbell, also with a knee issue, was listed for the first time this week.

For Jacksonville, starting center Robert Hainsey (knee) and defensive end B.J. Green (forearm) are out. Defensive tackle Matt Dickerson (neck) and defensive end Danny Striggow (concussion) are questionable.
