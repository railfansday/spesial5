Tramell Jones Jr. to start at QB for Gators against Oklahoma

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

GAINESVILLE — Tramell Jones Jr. will start at quarterback for the Gators, a source confirmed, after the sophomore took over after the third series and finished Florida's loss to Georgia last weekend in Atlanta.

Jones replaced Aaron Philo, who got the nod after he returned from a one-game absence with a shoulder injury suffered during an Oct. 10 home loss to South Carolina.

Jones stepped in for the injured Philo and led the Gators to a 27-24 upset at then-No. 7 Texas before Florida's bye week. The former Jacksonville Mandrain standout rallied Florida from a 17-3 deficit while making pivotal plays with his arm and legs.

Despite Jones' performance, coaches opted for Philo, who led Florida to a 4-0 start and No. 8 ranking as the SEC's third-rated passer. But the junior who transferred from Georgia Tech struggled during subsequent loss at Missouri and against South Carolina.

He completed 42-of-62 passes (68%) for 424 yards, averaging 6.8 yards per attempt, with two touchdowns and one interception while taking six sacks in the losses. He then threw two interceptions on the first three series against Georgia before coach Jon Sumrall switched to Jones.

Jones is 40-of-70 (57%) passing for 332 yards, two touchdowns and an interception the past two games and has 59 rushing yards and a touchdown. His interception was in the end zone with the Gators' trailing 24-0 with a little less than five minutes remaining in the third quarter.

Jones also took four sacks against Georgia, one leading to a scoop-and-score by the Bulldogs after a Florida blocked missed a pass rusher who hit Florida's quarterback.
