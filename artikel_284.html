Everything to know about Black Friday 2026: Where to find the best deals, sale dates, how to shop smart, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

One of the biggest shopping events of the year is finally here: Black Friday.

Early Black Friday deals started rolling in weeks ago, but now we're well into the official sales. Yes, instead of standing in a cold line at 4 a.m. the day after Thanksgiving, we get to shop online under cozy blankets while watching Christmas movies.

Since the deals between retailers can feel overwhelming, it's good to head into the mega-sale event with a plan. From knowing when sales from retailers like Amazon and Best Buy start to knowing which products will likely go on sale to preparing your own shopping list, we've got you covered on everything you need to know to shop Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

This year, Black Friday falls on Nov. 27, but that date isn't all that important these days. Almost every major retailers has already started official Black Friday sales. We expect to see some special deals drop on Nov. 27, but if you're looking to shop Black Friday sales, it's your time to shine.

Amazon: Amazon's Black Friday sale runs from Thursday, Nov. 19 to Nov. 30, offering 12 consecutive days of deals. Each day of the sale will feature "Today's Big Deals," where select products will be on sale for up to 60% off, except for on Black Friday and Cyber Monday proper. One batch of deals will go live at 3 a.m. ET, while another will go on sale at 11 a.m. ET.

Best Buy: Black Friday at Best Buy starts on Nov. 19, and runs through Saturday, Nov. 28. Its Cyber Monday sale runs from Nov. 29 to Nov. 30, with a Cyber Week sale that extends savings from Dec. 1 to Dec. 6. Total and Plus members of the My Best Buy program are also eligible to earn double points on their purchases from Nov. 19 to Dec. 6.

Walmart: Walmart has three major sale events throughout November: the early Black Friday sale from Nov. 12 to Nov. 15, the Black Friday sale from Nov. 23 to Nov. 29 (with deals online only from Nov. 23 to Nov. 26), and the online-only Cyber Monday sale from Nov. 30 to Dec. 2.

Target: Target's first Early Black Friday sale kicked off Nov. 5 to Nov. 7. From Nov. 1 to Dec. 24, Target's Deal of the Day offer kicks off, featuring new deals every day. Every weekend after Nov. 7, the retailer will also drop new limited-time holiday offers.

Considering prices went up this year on pretty much Apple devices except for AirPods, Black Friday will be the time to pick up new Apple devices. The short answer: Apple products will absolutely be on sale this Black Friday. The slightly longer answer: the best place to pick up Apple products during Black Friday sales will be at retailers like Amazon, Walmart, or Best Buy, which often duke it out to see who will drop a MacBook or pair of AirPods to the record-low price first (or, more impressively, keep it in stock the longest). Already in the lead-up, MacBooks are $250 off, and the AirPods Pro 3 are $100 off.

As for Apple itself holding Black Friday sales, the Apple Store features its own Black Friday sale every year: a free gift card for future use with the purchase of select devices. We expect to see the same this year, so we recommend picking up your Apple devices elsewhere if you're looking to save as much cash as possible.

Target has great Black Friday deals if you're looking for toys, Lego sets, Christmas decor, books, or small kitchen appliances. Target's Deal of the Day offers extra savings on hot items, and you can preview upcoming deals several days before they happen, giving shoppers time to set a reminder.

Walmart doesn't skimp out on Black Friday deals, and they tend to compete with the best Amazon sales, like $149 Apple AirPods Pro 3. Officially, Walmart's Black Friday deals are live through Nov. 29, followed by Cyber Monday from Nov. 30 to Dec. 2. If a deal you were hoping to grab at Amazon sells out, it's always worth checking stock at Walmart.

Best Buy gets in on the sale action, and it's worth paying close attention to the retailer's doorbuster deals which offer ridiculous savings but tend to sell out quickly (R.I.P $149.99 Ninja Slushi). Like Walmart, Best Buy tends to price-match Amazon deals. If you wind up needing a gift in a hurry, Best Buy tends to offer great in-store pickup options as well as quick shipping.

While Black Friday brings many of the best prices of the year, it's not the end-all, be-all for every product out there. According to Google-analyzed holiday trends, fancy cookware and suitcases hit their lowest prices in late December, while tech like TVs and gaming consoles are likely to hit their lowest prices during Black Friday and Cyber Week (with the latter seeing one final dip right before Christmas).

There can be some variance from category to category, so we recommend keeping track of Mashable's deals coverage. Our team has been covering Black Friday (and deals in general) for nearly a decade, so we can tell your firsthand whether or not a product is worth grabbing during the height of sales.

With Black Friday proper bringing some of the lowest prices, what deals are worth shopping now? It's an especially relevant question in 2026, a year that saw many historic price increases across everything from laptops to Kindles. In other words, shopping smart has never been a smarter decision, which is why we've gathered some of the best early Black Friday savings so far.

The more apt question is what doesn't go on sale during Black Friday. That's to say, if there's a product you're looking to grab on sale, whether that be a new TV or paper towels, Black Friday is the time to do it.

Beyond the deals we have listed above, we expect to see more savings on MacBooks, Windows laptops, video games and consoles, robot vacuums, regular stick vacuums, drones, trading cards, books, board games, and power tools. This list is by no means exhaustive — if there's a good deal worth writing up, you'll likely find it in our deals coverage.

The best Black Friday deals generally last through Cyber Monday, which falls on Nov. 30 this year. Some deals do linger through the week, and some Cyber Monday sales (such as Walmart's) are scheduled to extend beyond Cyber Monday proper. And as Target's deal-of-the-day sale demonstrates, there are almost always Black Friday-worthy sales through essentially Christmas. That said, for the best shot at scoring big savings (and for catching a deal while it's still available), shopping during Black Friday weekend is typically a good rule of thumb.

Amazon and Walmart don't offer any price matching policy. Best Buy's Holiday Price Match Guarantee offers price matches against itself through Jan. 15, 2027 on products purchased from Oct. 30 to Dec. 31. Target used to offer price matching against other retailers during the holidays, but now its policy resembles Best Buy's: Target shoppers who made a purchase on or after Nov. 1 in person or online can request a price match if they spot a drop on or before Dec. 24.
