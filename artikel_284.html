Druskis Hot Ones is a glasses-fogging Thanksgiving treat

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Druski followed up his hit SNL hosting gig with a round of Hot Ones and it requires defogging.

The internet-favourite comedian, Mashable 101 alumnus, and Collect & Praise star otherwise known as Drew Desbordes joined First We Feast's Sean Evans for the hot wing challenge. The pair chatted about Druski's career, from early content creation days to his upcoming Netflix series and working with Emma Stone and hosting SNL.

And of course, with each insightful question comes an increasingly spicy bit of chicken — and it all relatably fogs up Druski's glasses.

"I'm trying so hard for you 'cause I fuckin' love your show, man. I fuckin' hate this shit," Druski says. Druski says after a particularly large bite of a high-tier spicy wing. "I hate that I'm a fuckin' fan bro."

Honestly, the baritone roars that emerge after Da Bomb are something else, and it's fair enough Druski calls it after this titan of hot sauce. He did great!
