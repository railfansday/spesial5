Arsenal pay touching tribute to Jesus de Miguel after tragic death

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal paid tribute to Jesus de Miguel after the 30-year-old died from injuries suffered in a Spanish second-division match.

The Tenerife striker had serious injuries to his head and chest after colliding with goalkeeper Jesus Owono during a game against FC Andorra on Monday.

Owono's knee collided heavily with De Miguel, who was treated on the pitch for more than five minutes before being taken by ambulance to hospital.

His condition was said to be "stable despite the severity of his condition" on Monday, however that deteriorated and De Miguel died on Tuesday morning.

Tributes have been paid to De Miguel across the game, including from Kylian Mbappe and Gerard Pique, who co-owns Andorra.

Arsenal responded to the tragic news themselves as players and staff gathered at the start of their training session on Tuesday morning.

The Gunners huddled in a circle and observed a period of silence in front of a big screen with a picture of De Miguel on.

The Spanish Football Federation (RFEF) has postponed all matches scheduled for Tuesday.

UEFA announced that a period of silence take place before every European games this week, which will include matches involving Spanish sides Atletico Madrid, Barcelona and Villarreal.
