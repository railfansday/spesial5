Hull City handed injury concern as Senegal boss Patrick Vieira issues Nobel Mendy update

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Hull City defender Nobel Mendy is making an early return from international duty due to an injury concern.

The 22-year-old signed for Hull in the summer transfer window, joining from Rayo Vallecano for a club-record fee and penning a five-year deal.

He has since made eight appearances for the Tigers and completed 90 minutes in their 1-0 defeat to Arsenal prior to the international break.

Mendy then linked up with Senegal and was an unused substitute as they thumped Sudan 4-1 last week. However, he was absent for their reunion with Sudan yesterday (November 16).

Speaking ahead of the game, as reported by Ben Dinnery of Premier Injuries, Senegal boss Patrick Vieira confirmed Mendy had picked up an injury.

He said: “He felt a problem with his knee. We have therefore decided, after consultation with our medical department and that of his club, not to take any risks and to let him return to the club.

“We hope it's not very serious and that he can play again as soon as possible.”

Hull have not won in the Premier League since late August and cannot afford to lose too many influential figures. Supporters - as well as manager Sergej Jakirovic - now face an anxious wait to learn the extent of Mendy’s injury.

If Mendy does face a spell on the sidelines, the blow will be somewhat softened by the return of Charlie Hughes. The highly-rated centre-back has not made a single senior appearance this term having undergone a hernia operation, but featured for Hull’s under-21s last week.

Hull are due to return to action on Sunday (November 22), hosting Brighton & Hove Albion at the MKM Stadium.
