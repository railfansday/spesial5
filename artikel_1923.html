Sources: Coventry City fear ‘anarchy’ as Jack Rudoni transfer plan revealed

There would be outrage at Coventry City if Jack Rudoni is sold in the January transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.

Newcastle United and Leeds United have been credited with interest for the 25-year-old, who has enjoyed a promising start to his time in the Premier League.

The midfielder, who joined the Sky Blues from Huddersfield Town in 2024, will have just one year remaining on his contract next summer.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Coventry and Frank Lampard are keen to keep hold of Rudoni and will hope to see those terms extended.

It was a difficult start to the campaign for the Championship winners, who lost their opening four games without scoring a goal.

But recent weeks have seen some stability restored, with Lampard's side wining three of their last six league games.

Football Insider revealed this week that Coventry are confident of keeping Rudoni at the club next summer if they are able to stay up.

The midfielder has scored four goals in 11 games across all competitions for the Sky Blues this season so far, and is already attracting Premier League interest.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I don’t think so [that Coventry would consider a January sale]. You’d have anarchy at Coventry if they did that, selling arguably their best player midway through the season.

"I don’t think Frank Lampard would be too happy if that was to happen as well. But look, the growing interest is not really surprising given the impact that Rudoni has had in the Coventry team since stepping up to the Premier League.

"He’s arguably been their best player, has popped up with some crucial goals as well.

"Coventry will want to keep hold of Rudoni long term. He will be vital to their hopes of staying up this season as well."

Both Coventry and Lampard will be well aware that relegation from the Premier League will almost certainly see Rudoni leave the club.

Speaking in April about the midfielder, Lampard said: "He can play in the Premier League, we just want him to play with us. He's definitely got the ability to do that because he's confident, he's hungry, the ability is there.

"Since we've come in, he's just absolutely flowing. I'm not trying to take credit for that, I think he's found the confidence, he's found a role.

"He's a special player and I really believe he can go as far as he wants, but he's definitely levels. He looked like dropping a Premier League player onto the pitch. He looked like maybe a Champions League player there today."
