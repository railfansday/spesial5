Arsenal boost as three Newcastle stars miss training before huge Premier League clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Newcastle United will be without talismanic midfielder Joelinton for Saturday’s clash with Arsenal, but will at least have summer signing Matias Fernandez-Pardo available for the weekend.

Joelinton was already considered a doubt for the fixture before the international break, having missed the 1-1 draw with Fulham earlier this month due to a thigh issue.

And it has now been revealed the Brazilian has yet to return to training. Joelinton did not feature in an open session on Tuesday, with The Chronicle reporting he is now set for an MRI scan to determine the exact problem with his thigh.

There is optimism he can return before Christmas, with Matthias Jaissle expected to issue a clearer update at his pre-match press conference this week.

Before the international break, the Magpies’ boss explained: "Joelinton went out with an injury again. We need to make the final treatment plan and see how long he will be out."

Other absentees from Newcastle training were Joe Willock and Nico Gonzalez, although the duo were only ill and are expected to be fit to face Arsenal.

The big boon for Newcastle is that attacker Fernandez-Pardo looks to have finally recovered from a virus.

Fernandez-Pardo turned down the chance to represent Belgium during the latest international break, with his national team boss stating that he was not fully fit.

Belgium head coach Mark van Bommel shared: “He was ill for a very long time.

“He has only trained twice since October 17. As a result, he isn’t at 100 per cent, which is why he isn’t part of the squad. He still doesn’t feel quite right, so it was his own choice.”

The 21-year-old came off the bench to feature against Fulham, but the £51million man could be in line for a start against the Gunners on Saturday.

Since swapping France for Tyneside, Fernandez-Pardo has made six Premier League appearances.
