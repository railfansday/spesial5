AC Milan Women 3-2 Sassuolo: Grimshaw crucial after crazy first half

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Christy Grimshaw was crucial as AC Milan Women finally got back to winning ways, following what was a rather crazy first half against Sassuolo.

After two defeats and a draw, dropping points in a silly fashion against Napoli last weekend, Milan Women needed to bounce back with a win today. Thankfully, that’s exactly what they managed to do, even if it wasn’t the most controlled performance.

Just looking at the scoreline and the timing of the goals is enough to understand the nature of the game. All five goals were scored in the first half and it doesn’t end there either. All five were scored within the first 27 minutes!

The veteran Grimshaw opened the scoring for the Rossonere after nine minutes, but Sassuolo found a way back into the game just two minutes later. The Scottish international decided to restore the lead just four minutes later, i.e. in the 15th minute.

Angelica Soffia then made things more comfortable with a third goal in the 24th minute, or at least so we thought. Just three minutes after that, Sassuolo scored again and thus brought some life into the game, even if Milan looked the better side.

Suzanne Bakker’s side actually dominated possession, had more shots on target and also had a higher xG, so the win definitely feels deserved. And boy was it needed! The situation in the standings is still not great, but at least they are now four points behind the Champions League zone.
