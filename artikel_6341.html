Baby, She's Back! SpaceX Catches a Starship From Orbit for First Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

SpaceX brought back its Starship rocket's second stage today for a first-ever midair capture at its launch pad, capping a record-setting orbital flight test.

The 171-foot-tall (52-meter) space vehicle, known as Ship 42, settled between a giant pair of mechanical arms extended from the South Texas launch tower where its journey began 22.5 hours earlier. The arm-capture system, nicknamed "Mechazilla," had been used three times previously to capture Starship Super Heavy boosters during suborbital flight tests, but this was the first time for a second stage coming in hot from orbit.

SpaceX said the booster was diverted when its onboard system detected a fuel-system alarm. The root cause of the alarm is under investigation.

SpaceX plans to use the launch-pad arms to recover Starship's boosters and second stages on a routine basis, achieving company founder Elon Musk's goal of creating a fully reusable, orbital-class launch system.
