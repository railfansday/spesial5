The 6 Best Inflatable Hot Tubs for Backyard Relaxation

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Bliss out with one of these affordable inflatable hot tubs for the ultimate at-home spa experience.

Nothing beats a warm, bubbly soak under the stars, but not everyone has the space—or budget—to install a permanent hot tub in their backyard. That’s where inflatable hot tubs come into play.

They cost less than their hard-sided counterparts—some four-person models are under $500, if you can believe it—and are easy to store in the off-season. Another plus: inflatable hot tubs come with everything you need to get them up and running, so you just fill them with water and crank up the temperature.

Whether you’re looking for a small option for two or a larger model to soak with friends and family, read on for our best inflatable hot tub recommendations and the essentials to consider before you shop.

We scoured user reviews and chose options across sizes, styles, and price points, from value-packed to high-end. We spent a lot of time weighing different hot tub models—both permanent and inflatable—so we used our expertise to recommend only durable, comfortable options. As mentioned above, there are plenty of features to consider when shopping for an inflatable hot tub, and we highlighted the most notable ones for each model we cover.

A multicolor LED light is great for nighttime soaks, and two spare filter cartridges and a floating chlorine dispenser keep the water clear. It also includes a ground cloth and insulated cover to help retain heat between uses. Two removable headrests are included, so you can really relax after a long day.

A Freeze Shield feature turns the heater on automatically when the water temperature nears freezing, so the pump and liner won’t ice up. A power-saving timer also lets you schedule heating for when you’ll actually use it.

Setup needs no tools, and the box includes the heater pump, a ChemConnect chemical dispenser, a Type VI filter cartridge, and the insulated cover. The one compromise is size. At 26 inches tall with a 52-inch interior, it’s shallower and snugger than some of the six-person tubs on this list.

It’s a big improvement over models with exterior pumps that have hard-to-reach control panels on top. Budget aside, if you’re in the market for a hot tub, first nail down how many people you’ll need to accommodate. Most options comfortably fit four to six people, including our best overall pick, the Intex PureSpa Plus 6-Person Inflatable Hot Tub.

Larger models seat eight or more adults, and extra-small hot tubs are ideal for two. Square inflatable hot tubs generally offer more room to stretch out, but they’re typically harder to find than round models.

Most inflatable hot tubs are made of PVC and vinyl, and the best models have three-layer outer walls (often labeled as TriTech) to prevent punctures.

It may surprise you that more jets aren’t always better. It’s more important to consider jet placement, whether they’re adjustable, and whether they vary in size and function.

All inflatable hot tubs typically include a pump, heater, filtration system, and cover, and many also come with ground cloths, extra filter cartridges, chlorine regulators, and carrying bags. Nice-to-have extras include cup holders, multicolor LED lights, and removable headrests.
