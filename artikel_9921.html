when the question is too specific, and I can't be bothered to generalize it, "windows caffeine tray icon alternative possible to not keep screen on scoop-installable", "kdenlive resize video move to one corner of the screen instantly without animating" or I don't care that much and am just bored/curious about some fact like "is x niche youtuber dating y", being able to fall back on an llm for these kinds of queries is very useful.

It doesn't really matter what LLM you use for these, you could probably even use Google and it's AI overview, but I don't use google. I used to use Perplexity, then ChatGPT, Claude and now most recently DeepSeek for this; they all work just fine.
