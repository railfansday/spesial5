Bitcoin price fails to hold $65K as US stocks drop further on AI nerves

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Bitcoin and US equities saw pressure to start the week as traders weighed the impact of forthcoming Nvidia Q3 earnings and Anthropic’s IPO announcement.

Bitcoin (BTC) struggled to stay above $65,000 at Monday’s Wall Street open as tech-stock weakness dragged US equities lower.

Data from TradingView showed BTC/USD briefly reaching $65,600 at the start of the US trading session before reversing lower, down nearly 1.5% on the day at the time of writing.

BTC/USD one-hour chart. Source: Cointelegraph/TradingView

Sell-side pressure concentrated on AI-related stocks pressured the S&P 500 and tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite Index, which were 1.2% and 1.8% lower, respectively.

Nasdaq Composite Index one-day chart. Source: Cointelegraph/TradingView

Traders continued to eye uncertainty over the fate of Nvidia’s leadership direction amid growing calls to reinstate ousted CEO Jensen Huang. The company is due to report Q3 earnings after the end of trading on Tuesday, adding to market nerves as expectations see revenue circling $109 billion. Nvidia stock fell up to 2.4% on Monday.

“Amid AI’s pivotal role and tightening overall funding conditions, it’s important to remember that Nvidia-related uncertainties for the broader economy and markets are far from straightforward,” economist Mohamed El-Erian wrote in an analysis on the day.

El-Erian acknowledged Nvidia’s central role in circular financing, an internal investment mechanism within the AI sector, with the company functioning as a bellwether for the broader AI trade.

Nvidia stock one-hour chart. Source: Cointelegraph/TradingView

Separately, AI giant Anthropic is due to price its initial public offering (IPO) on Wednesday, further contributing to market tensions amid a target valuation of $1.4 trillion.

In his latest commentary, independent analyst Edward Corona described the coming days as the “biggest AI week of the year.”

“What strikes me about this morning is how much of the AI trade now hangs on things nobody can model. A spreadsheet can’t tell you whether a CEO comes back, or how many tech shares get sold to make room for Anthropic,” he summarized, noting that even a successful IPO debut might not reflect across AI stocks as a whole.

Bitcoin downside pressure meant that key resistance levels common to previous bear-market recoveries remained out of reach.

These included the 200-week simple moving average (SMA) and exponential moving average (EMA), two trend lines forming a “cloud” that BTC/USD flips from resistance to support in order to confirm a new long-term uptrend. The 200-week SMA and EMA currently sit at $68,184 and $69,638, respectively.

BTC/USD one-week chart with 200 SMA, 200 EMA. Source: Cointelegraph/TradingView

In commentary on Friday, Keith Alan, cofounder of crypto trading platform Material Indicators, directly linked a potential failure of the 200-week SMA as support to Bitcoin falling toward $62,000.

“A deeper correction doesn’t necessarily invalidate the macro bullish thesis, but where buyers step in matters,” he told X followers.

On daily time frames, Bitcoin’s relative strength index (RSI) fell back into “oversold” territory for the first time since early June. On Saturday, daily RSI reached 20 on its 0-100 scale.
