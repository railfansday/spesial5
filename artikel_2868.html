Panthers designate OL Brady Christensen for return from IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Veteran offensive lineman Brady Christensen will be back at Panthers practice this week.

The team announced that they have designated Christensen for return from injured reserve on Tuesday. Christensen injured his hamstring during the summer and has yet to play this season.

The hamstring injury occurred as Christensen was working his way back from the torn Achilles he suffered during the 2025 season.

“He’s healthy, he’s strong, he’s fit, he’s ready to go,” Panthers head coach Dave Canales said on Monday, via the team’s website. “But we’ve got to get some football under his belt here, so that’ll be a good addition to the group to evaluate where you can help us potentially.”

Christensen has played 59 games over the last five seasons. He has seen time at both guard and tackle.
