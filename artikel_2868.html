Jaylin Williams injury update: Will OKC Thunder forward return vs Trail Blazers?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

OKC Thunder forward Jaylin Williams will not play in a road game against the Portland Trail Blazers at 10 p.m. CST Tuesday.

Williams will miss a fourth straight game due to low back spasms. He did not play in OKC's 114-75 home win over the Washington Wizards on Saturday.

OKC (8-2) also continues to be without Chet Holmgren (right knee injury recovery), Thomas Sorber (right knee surgery), Nikola Topić (G League assignment), Cason Wallace (left shoulder surgery) and Jalen Williams (left ankle surgery).

Here's the full injury report for OKC's game against Portland:

Thunder: Chet Holmgren (right knee injury recovery) is out. Thomas Sorber (right knee surgery) is out. Nikola Topić (G League assignment) is out. Cason Wallace (left shoulder surgery) is out. Jalen Williams (left ankle surgery) is out. Jaylin Williams (low back spasms) is out.

Trail Blazers: Vit Krejci (left ankle fracture) is out. Ja Morant (left hamstring strain) is out. Shaedon Sharpe (right meniscus surgery) is out. John Tonje (G League two-way) is out.
