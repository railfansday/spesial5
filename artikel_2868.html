New York Jets News: Decision Time on NFL Trade Deadline Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

We have reached NFL trade deadline day. Tuesday, November 10 at 4 pm ET.

Last year, the New York Jets changed the entire outlook of their franchise ahead of the trade deadline. What will the Jets do this year?

Head coach Aaron Glenn expects the Jets to stand pat, but his opinion carries less weight since he might not be here in a few months.

What do you do with a guy like Will McDonald or Breece Hall? Should the current regime decide their future? Or should you hold onto players like this and let the next head coach have the ability to make that decision?

It's a difficult decision. The Jets are 2-7; their season is all but over, and yet we still have eight regular-season games to play.

The Jets already sent Andre Cisco away for a future pick. It feels inevitable that Nahshon Wright will follow him out the door in hours. Some of these decisions are easy. Those players were benched or aren't playing, so why not get something for them instead of getting nothing later.

Other decisions aren't as easy. Here's some popcorn, Jets fans; this should be a fascinating couple of hours as we sit on the edge of our seats. The most activity we might see could come from the Indianapolis Colts, who Jets fans are hoping they blow it all up, which would make that juicy 2027 first-round draft choice all the sweeter.
