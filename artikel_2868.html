James Harden's 51-point night moves him past Kobe Bryant on 50+ point game list

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

James Harden will go into the Hall of Fame one day, remembered as one of the great scorers the game has ever seen.

As evidence, we present Friday night, when he dropped 51 on the Indiana Pacers in a Cavaliers win — the 26th 50+ point game of Harden's career, moving him past Kobe Bryant and into third all-time in 50+ point games (Michael Jordan at 31 and Wilt Chamberlain at a laughable 118 are ahead of him).

"It's a different role for me. My usage rate isn't extremely high every game, we have somebody for that," Harden said of his scoring outburst postgame, via the Associated Press. "So mine is kind of scoring when I need to, but then distributing the ball. My number is called and you need to go out there and put the shots up. Ok, I can still do it. I knew I can do it, but it's just when you don't do something consistently and then it goes away a little bit or you just don't know if you got it or not."

He's still got it, he was 15-of-26 from the field and knocked down seven 3-pointers.

Cleveland has found a bit of a groove, having won 3 of 4 and is now 2-1 in East Group B of the NBA Cup. The Cavaliers still have a chance of making the knockout round of Cup play, but they need a convincing win over the Knicks in Madison Square Garden next Wednesday.
