Hockey Game Thread: Michigan State Spartans Vs. North Dakota Fighting Hawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Michigan State Hockey Team gets its Big Ten schedule going tonight with game one of a two-game series at Notre Dame. The past week has seen both the MSU Football and Men's Basketball Teams pick up significant wins over perennial thorns in our sides. Now it is time for the Hockey Team to keep the good times going.

Notre Dame will be a step down over our last two opponents – North Dakota and Boston University. The Irish currently are 4-6, though they did pick up a sweep at Penn State last weekend.

Game one of the series is tonight at 7 PM ET and game 2 is Friday also at 7. Both games will be televised on the Big Ten Network.
