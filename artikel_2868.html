Alabama flips three-star cornerback from ACC program

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Alabama Crimson Tide beat Chattanooga on Saturday, and on Sunday they notched a win on the recruiting trail, flipping three-star cornerback Jamel Griffen from the Syracuse Orange, according to Hayes Fawcett of Rivals.

Griffen is from Opelika, Alabama, and had been committed to Syracuse since June 8. The Crimson Tide were able to quickly steal him away from the Orange, offering him a scholarship on Nov. 11 and completeting the flip 11 days later.

He is ranked as the No. 1,019 overall player and No. 106 cornerback in the Rivals industry ranking. He is the No. 43 player from Alabama and their seventh in-state commitment.

Griffen had an excellent senior season at Opelika, helping lead them to a spot in the quarterfinals of the state playoffs. He finished the year with 63 tackles, three pass breakups, and a blocked punt in nine games.

He is Alabama's 16th commitment in the 2027 recruiting class and second at cornerback, joining three-starDarrius White. The Crimson Tide are set to sign a smaller recruiting class than normal, focusing on top-end talent. Their class is led by five-star wide receiver Osani Gayles and five-star quarterback Elijah Haven, the No. 27 and No. 30 players in the country.
