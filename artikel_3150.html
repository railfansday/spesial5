Marcus & Millichap: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Rate swings are slowing the brokers' own deal pipeline, even as $1.2 trillion of maturing loans pushes owners toward sales.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 21, 2026 at 5:54PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 6, 2026, Marcus & Millichap (MMI -1.04%) reported total revenue of $208 million. The broker's buildout of leasing, financing, and auction tools offers new revenue streams as property valuations reset.

Management outlines three takeaways on how the firm is navigating market volatility.

Frequent interest rate shifts force brokers to reprice listings and reunderwrite deals under contract. That administrative workload leaves experienced producers with less capacity for new business and slows onboarding for new agents.

Property owners face $1.2 trillion in maturing commercial real estate loans across 2027 and 2028. Because interest rates remain elevated, owners can't easily refinance without adding fresh equity, creating listing opportunities for brokerage specialists.

Management is expanding retail leasing, mortgage banking, and artificial intelligence infrastructure. These initiatives increase overhead expenses over the next 18 to 24 months, but management expects them to build long-term operating leverage.

Buyers and sellers are reengaging as property valuations adjust to interest rates. A zero-debt balance sheet with $373 million in cash supports targeted expansion into leasing and financing.

These strategic additions position the core brokerage platform for long-term growth.
