NiSource: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Over 90% of NiSource's $40 billion investment plan converts to revenue within 12 months, an enviable cash recovery speed.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 20, 2026 at 4:45PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, NiSource (NI -0.63%) raised its long-term financial guidance while expanding its capital investment commitments. The utility is blending standard regulated energy distribution with power contracts for giant technology facilities.

NiSource signed a 20-year agreement with Microsoft, marking its fifth data center power contract across three technology giants. The projects run through Genco, the company's power generation segment, and lower electricity bills for retail customers of NIPSCO, its electric utility subsidiary.

Executive Vice President Shawn Anderson detailed a new seven-year spending forecast that dedicates $32 billion to core regulated utilities and $8 billion to data center generation. Management expects this capital deployment to support compound annual adjusted earnings per share, or net income divided by outstanding stock, growth of 10% to 11%.

Utilities usually wait years to collect revenues on new construction, creating regulatory lag that drags on cash flows. NiSource uses forward-looking rate structures and tracker mechanisms, which are regulatory processes that let utilities recover specific investment costs quickly, to streamline cash generation.

Large technology companies are expanding heavily across Northern Indiana, where available land, water access, and fiber networks give NiSource an operational edge over surrounding power grids. Management is watching regulatory approval schedules and construction milestones to ensure projects open on time.

The utility couples steady 6% to 8% earnings growth from its base gas and electric utilities with higher-margin growth from data center power contracts. That combination creates two complementary income streams that insulate shareholders from regulatory shifts in any single state.
