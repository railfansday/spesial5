How to watch Avengers: Endgame Encore to prep for Doomsday

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Avengers: Endgame returned to theaters earlier this season to get audiences ready for the December release of Avengers: Doomsday. It features new footage that bridges the narrative between the two films, which Mashable's Belen Edwards graciously broke down for anyone who doesn't have the time for a rewatch.

For those who missed the latest theatrical experience or just want to watch Endgame again in preparation for the new film, be sure to watch the Encore edition when it hits streaming this week. As Edwards puts it, "It's the same tale of the Avengers battling Thanos (Josh Brolin) and trying to reverse his catastrophic snap, only this time, there are a few extra scenes tacked on at the end that tease what's to come."

Here's when and where to watch Avengers: Endgame Encore, as well as a few different ways to save some money on streaming.

Avengers: Endgame Encore makes its streaming debut on Disney+ on Friday, Nov. 13, 2026. That gives you plenty of time to stream it in preparation for the Avengers: Doomsday theatrical release.

Disney+ has also assembled a "Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday" collection on the app if you want to host a pre-release marathon at home.

You can save over $42 on a Disney+ subscription on its own by signing up for an annual plan upfront. The yearly plan without ads will cost you $214.99 per year, which breaks down to $17.92 per month instead of the usual $21.49 per month. That's 17% or a total of $42.89 in savings. When you check out, be sure to select Disney+ Premium, then "annual" to secure the discount for the year. Unfortunately, there is no annual plan available for the cheaper ad-supported tier.

Disney's bundle deal with Hulu is currently cheaper than paying for Disney+ on its own. For six months, you'll get both Disney+ and Hulu with ads for only $11.99 per month or without ads for $19.99 per month. Either option essentially gives you one streamer for free. After your first six months, the plan with ads will go up one dollar and the plan without will go up $3.
