Grab the Pokémon TCG Prismatic Evolutions ETB for under $140 in Amazons Black Friday deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Scarlet and Violet Prismatic Evolutions Elite Trainer Box is on sale for as low as $137 at Amazon across multiple sellers, down from its $162.88 list price.

With Black Friday just days away, Amazon has another worthwhile price cut on one of the Pokémon TCG's most sought-after Elite Trainer Boxes — bringing Prismatic Evolutions below $140 once again.

As of Nov. 19, the Prismatic Evolutions ETB now costs as little as $131.51 at Amazon, plus $5.49 delivery, bringing the total to $137. That's $25.88 below the usual $162.88 price. These sub-$140 prices are available on Amazon from third-party sellers.

Whichever way you buy, you'll at most be spending $26.83 above the estimated market price at dedicated trading card platform TCGplayer — now $113.05 at the time of writing.

Inside are nine Prismatic Evolutions Pokémon booster packs, an Eevee full-art promo, 65 card sleeves, Energy cards, dice, and other accessories — with nine chances to pull valuable SIR chase cards like Umbreon ex, Sylveon ex, and Espeon ex.
