Rangers hero Scott Arfield takes plunge into management with new player-boss role

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Former Rangers star Scott Arfield has been appointed the new player-manager of East Fife.

The former Canada international has replaced Dick Campbell, with the Fifers currently languishing ninth in League One.

The 38-year-old spent the final weeks of last season as Livingston's interim boss but will now get the chance to properly launch his managerial career at Bayview.

Arfield spent five seasons with Rangers between 2018 and 2023, making more than 200 appearances for the Ibrox club.

“I am absolutely delighted to be the new manager of East Fife and I cannot wait to get started,” said Arfield, who has signed a contract until May 2029.

“I feel I am ready to go into coaching after learning a lot from excellent managers during my playing career, and I am grateful to East Fife for believing in me and for giving me this opportunity.

“My aim is now to create opportunities for others and to build a team which will excite East Fife supporters and attract the attention of the local community.

“It is a highly respected club which has great potential, and it is a privilege to be given the chance to lead it forward.”

East Fife chairman Liam Anderson said: “We are delighted to have agreed a deal with Scott to become our new player-manager.

“This is an innovative appointment and gives a clear indication of our ambitions going forward.

“I am sure that the arrival of Scott will be welcomed by our fans, who are keen to see an exciting brand of football at Bayview.”
