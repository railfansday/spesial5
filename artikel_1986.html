Arsenal waste chances as Borussia Dortmund fight back to claim Emirates point

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Lucie Turner writes about Arsenal for Read Arsenal and Newcastle United for Read Newcastle. A long-time Arsenal supporter, she also follows rugby, snooker, darts and Formula 1. Her work covers match previews and reports, breaking news, player profiles and opinion pieces.

Arsenal failed to improve their Champions League prospects after a disappointing 1-1 game against Borussia Dortmund in which they lost their initial lead.

Despite Viktor Gyökeres’ spectacular goal, Mikel Arteta’s men allowed Maximilian Beier to level just after the halftime break. Although Arsenal were presented with several good scoring opportunities, they continually lacked composure in front of goal.

Despite winning their first three league phase games, it was an expensive night for Arsenal. They have maintained their perfect record in Europe, but for just the second time in the league phase at the Emirates, the Gunners have lost points.

Arteta will also be interested to see how much effort his team expended in their pursuit of a winning goal against Manchester City on Sunday.

Arsenal began confidently and quickly forced Dortmund to defend. Noni Madueke was troublesome on the right-hand side, and although Christos Tzolis was presented with a great chance, he failed to score from a cross.

Just ten minutes in, Madueke played a ball into the path of Gyökeres some 25 yards out. The Swedish forward then fired a curling shot over Gregor Kobel into the net.

A stunning goal from Arsenal’s forward was just what they required. Unfortunately, they were then unable to follow up this positive play.

Despite Tzolis’ effort prompting a save from Kobel, Madueke failed to score from the ball’s subsequent deflection. Likewise, Declan Rice was presented with a good opportunity when Martin Ødegaard set him up in a neat fashion.

Despite dominating play in the first half, Arsenal made a number of poor concluding decisions. Piero Hincapié failed to score from a good position, and Gyökeres also shot high from another good play.

Dortmund did not produce much throughout the game. However, Kepa Arrizabalaga did well to save from Joane Gadou in added time.

Arsenal should have gone into half-time with a more comfortable advantage. Instead, they gave the visitors a route back into the contest.

Shortly after play resumed, Beier failed to score despite being presented with a great chance following a low cross into the six-yard area.

Despite Arsenal successfully weathering that period of pressure, Dortmund soon retaliated. Gadou broke through the middle and passed to Beier, who then rounded Gabriel before coolly beating Kepa in the 55th minute.

Arsenal then faced a tougher challenge as they sought to add a second goal against increasingly confident Dortmund.

Arteta brought on Eberechi Eze, and the player quickly became involved. However, his shot was deflected just over the bar.

Continuing to trouble Dortmund, Madueke saw a header from Ødegaard also fly wide. A shot from Guirassy, also deflected, hit the post, and Rice then shot wide on the other side after Eze had provided an assist for him.

Despite having 29 shots at goal, the Gunners failed to make inroads. William Saliba, too, warmed up on the touchline and then did not play. Arteta continued with his existing defensive line.

Whilst Arsenal had much to be commended for in their attacking play, especially in the first half, good chances are of little use if they are not then converted.

Although Arteta will recognise that he should have secured three points, Dortmund have taken a well-earned point away from north London. Now, Arsenal need to swiftly refocus on Sunday’s game against Manchester City.
