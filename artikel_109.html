Here are some images from earlier today as Czech public media workers went on a one-day strike to protest a government plan to overhaul how public broadcasters are funded.

The legislation, led by populist billionaire turned prime minister Andrej Babiš, has been criticised as threatening the independence of the media organisations. The plan led to street protests in Prague last month.

Under the plan, the country’s public radio and television would be financed from the state budget, not from an existing system of regular fees from individuals, households and businesses. The media would receive about 15% less money than they do now, which could likely lead to staff layoffs, limited production and program cancellations.

Critics say the overhaul would give the ruling three-party coalition the means to take control of the media, similar to what populist governments did in Slovakia under prime minister Robert Fico and in Hungary before former prime minister Viktor Orbán was ousted from power in April.

Michal Kubal, chief news editor at Czech Television, said the strike today is unprecedented in the history of Czech public service media.
