This Ice Maker Makes Everyday Drinks Feel Like Treats

Good ice can make an ordinary drink feel special, and the GoveeLife Smart Nugget Ice Maker Pro lets you enjoy that feeling of luxury right at home. Designed to start producing ice in mere minutes and to fill its 3.5-pound bucket in about an hour and a half, it churns out small, consistently sized nuggets that scoop easily and chill drinks quickly. But weighing a little over 40 pounds, it’s not a countertop appliance you want to move around—and it’s not cheap either. Which begs the question: Is the Nugget Ice Maker Pro worth the space and investment? I tested it to find out.

The Nugget Ice Maker Pro started producing ice within minutes of setup, and filling the entire 3.5-pound bucket took less than 90 minutes. If you emptied it after each fill, that would mean you could make up to 60 pounds of ice in about 24 hours. While we have no need for that much ice, my family of three (and often more with my daughter’s friends around) never encountered an empty bin during our 2 weeks of testing. We used it for everything from filling our massive water bottles and making super-cold fruit smoothies to hosting a house full of teenagers on Halloween.

But that impressive quantity isn’t this machine’s best feature—the ice it makes is. Popularized by drive-thru restaurants like Sonic, nugget ice has a somewhat soft, air-pocketed texture that soaks up your drink’s flavor while also being easy to chew. That crunchable quality makes it as perfect for iced coffee, lemonade, and specialty cocktails as it is for blending smooth in a shake.

When we first set up the GoveeLife, we put it in our kitchen, since that seemed logical. But at 21.3 inches deep by 13.98 inches wide by 17.02 inches high, it was simply too big for our very small kitchen. Luckily, moving it to the garage was incredibly easy, and because the 4.9-liter water reservoir is detachable, we just carry that part inside when it’s time to refill.

Scooping from the Ice Maker Pro is equally straightforward. The scoop attaches magnetically to the side, so it’s never buried in the ice bin or accidentally set down in some odd place. And there’s a drawer catch so that you don’t pull too hard and end up with ice all over your floor. We went the set-it-and-forget-it route of simply programming it to make new ice any time we scooped some out, but you can also control production through the Govee app, Alexa, or Google Home. In terms of maintenance, the 10-minute quick-cleaning mode handles most of the work, but you’ll also want to run the 30-minute deep descaling mode and hand-wash the removable parts occasionally.

At roughly 43 pounds and almost $500, the GoveeLife Ice Maker isn’t small, and it isn’t cheap. But if you have the space and love nugget ice, its sleek finish, customizable RGB lighting, and quality cubes will be right at home in your kitchen or home bar. Regardless of location, however, the appeal is the same: quick production, consistent nuggets, and enough capacity to handle everyday drinks and entertainment. We love ours, and it’s officially staying put.
