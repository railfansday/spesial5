Cathay Pacific Asia Miles Announces Partner Award Devaluation, Starting January 5

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Starting in January, it’ll take more Cathay Pacific miles to fly British Airways first class from Los Angeles to London than to fly Cathay first class all the way from New York to Hong Kong. The London flight goes from 135,000 to 162,000 Asia Miles each way. Hong Kong stays at 160,000. For two people booking London, that’s an extra 54,000 miles one way. A week after Chase added 1:1 transfers to Cathay Pacific, Cathay tells me its partner airlines are charging it more for award seats. It’s raising the mileage price of selected partner awards and dropping Companion Ticket Awards. The new rates kick in at midnight in Hong Kong on January 5, 2027. In the U.S., get your ticket issued before 11 a.m. Eastern on Monday, January 4 (10 a.m. Central, 8 a.m. in Los Angeles) for future travel to book at the old rates. What Happens To The British Airways Surcharge Deal? One reason to use Asia Miles has been to pay less in British Airways fuel surcharges. It’s a way to save ~ $500 each way on taxes and fees between the U.S. and Europe. However, Japan Airlines generally charges fewer miles for the same award and offers equally low surcharges. JAL is a Bilt, Rove, Citibank, and Capital One transfer partner. Flying New York–London is a distance of under 5,000 miles. Cathay gave us a new first class price for that band, but hasn’t said what happens to business class. Los Angeles–London falls in the next band up. Business class there cost 89,000 miles before last May’s increase, costs 93,000 today and goes to 100,000 in January. That 100,000-mile award will take 100,000 Chase points or 125,000 American Express points. American Express gives you 800 Asia Miles per 1,000 points. Bilt, Capital One, Wells Fargo, Rove Miles and eligible Citi ThankYou cards all offer 1:1 transfers. When An American Airlines Connection Changes The Price The biggest reason I’d transfer points to Cathay is access to first and business class seats that American AAdvantage and Alaska Airlines Atmos Rewards often can’t book. Last year I flagged two first class award seats at a time between New York and Hong Kong, available only with Asia Miles. I’d still check American before transferring points. Just last week Seattle and Toronto to Hong Kong opened up in business class for 70,000 American miles each way. Cathay is keeping its Standard Award prices in place. Changes are only to partner awards. New York–Hong Kong is still 119,000 miles in business class. The oneworld Multi-Carrier Award chart which covers two oneworld airlines without Cathay, or Cathay plus at least two other oneworld airlines, isn’t changing either. Cathay says very few members used its Companion Ticket Award. If work bought you a qualifying Cathay business or first class roundtrip, you could use fewer miles to bring someone along on the same flights in the same cabin. Cathay will stop issuing companion awards in January as well. If you find the partner flights you want but might need to move the trip a day, I’d ticket before the cutoff. Changes to date only preserves the ticketed mileage price, subject to award availability and any change fee. Change the route, cabin or anything else after the cutoff and the new award prices apply.
