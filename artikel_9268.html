The Go Front Pack From Moment Always Keeps Your Gear Within Reach

Moment has announced the Go Front Pack, a small sling bag that the company says was inspired by hydration and running vests. It mounts on the user’s chest, giving them immediate access to a phone, lenses, filters, and cables without bouncing when the wearer moves.

The bag continues the trend of wearing front-facing small bags. Moment included a quick-access front phone pocket with a bungee hook to secure a phone while leaving the camera lens unobstructed, allowing creators to film while wearing the Go Front Pack. The phone can also easily be removed when needed.

“Shoot with your phone out front: a magnetic pocket with a bungee hook holds your phone, lens and all, on the outside of the bag, so you can film on the move. Travel with it: it’s light and weatherproof, with a hidden AirTag pocket inside the main compartment,” Moment says. “Carry it every day: stretch mesh pockets keep your cables, filters and lenses organized, and stashable wings let you wear it over your left or right shoulder.”

Moment employed what it calls a “no bounce” interior organization system that uses stretch mesh pockets for gear so items don’t jostle or bounce around while the bag is being worn, even during bouts of strenuous activity. A dual-wing design at the bottom of the pouch promises to give wearers a comfortable, body-wrapping fit when worn over either the left or right shoulder. The three-point strap, which keeps the bag snugly against the wearer’s body, can be removed easily.

The main compartment opens wide, though not so far that gear easily falls out, while on the larger Go Big size, a back compartment can hold a Moment SuperCage or iPad mini.

The exterior of the bag is made from Moment’s LightWeave Ripstop fabric, which features a dual-layer PFAS-free DWR coating to protect against water without feeling bulky. Moment says that a magnetic flap deflects water from the front-access zip, which the company notes reduces water ingress even in heavy rain.

The Go Front Pack comes in two sizes: a one-liter Go Small and a two-liter Go Big, the latter of which (as mentioned) can fit up to an iPad mini. Both are available starting today in either black or desert colors for $70 and $90, respectively.
