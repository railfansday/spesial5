Linkind Brings Holiday Cheer to Bowie's Crossfire Worship Center

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The community-focused project illuminated a local gathering place ahead of the Christmas season, demonstrating how smart lighting can help bring people together.

BOWIE, Md., Nov. 26, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- This holiday season, Linkind, a smart lighting brand specializing in connected lighting solutions, donated and installed permanent outdoor lights at Crossfire Worship Center in Bowie, Maryland.

Following installation, Linkind hosted a special lighting reveal at the church on Friday evening, bringing together members of the congregation and local community. Families counted down together and cheered as Linkind's holiday light show began. When the carolers started singing, bands of color moved across the church walls in time with the music. Neighbors joined in and shared warm drinks beneath the lights.

Christmas services bring families to Crossfire Worship Center during the holidays. In the months that follow, the same lights can welcome neighbors back for family gatherings and community events.

As part of its holiday community initiative, Linkind provided Crossfire Worship Center with a complimentary outdoor lighting installation, helping transform the church's exterior into a welcoming illuminated space for its congregation and surrounding community. The lights are permanently mounted along the edge of the church roof, so the church can change its display each holiday without taking them down and rehanging them.

The installation features Linkind's Smart Permanent Outdoor Lights ColorBlend System. Its dual-lens optical design casts overlapping bands of color on the church walls. The system offers 16 million colors, more than 120 preset lighting effects, and AI-assisted customization through the AiDot app. Church staff can use the app to switch the same fixtures from Christmas red and green to steady warm white above the entrance for an evening gathering.

"We're grateful for the opportunity to support Crossfire Worship Center by donating lights for this holiday display," said a spokesperson at Linkind. "We wanted to give something back to the Bowie community. We hope the finished display will be something the congregation and wider community can enjoy throughout the holiday season, and for years to come."
