Checking In On The Weird Little Guys In Ocarina Of Time To See How They Fared In The Remake

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Nintendo Switch 2The Legend of ZeldaThe Legend of Zelda: Ocarina Of Time

Back in June, upon the reveal that Nintendo would be remaking The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, I compiled a list of all the Weird Little Guys in that game that I was stoked to see make a reappearance. Coming fresh off my first full playthrough of the game this last January, I had a lot of ideas. The game is full of these fellas!

I am thrilled to say that every last one of the Weird Little Guys from my original list made it into the remake in some capacity. I am less thrilled to say that many of their appearances are a downgrade from the original, though a few stand-outs actually improved in the transition.

Let’s check in on all the Weird Little Guys and see how they fared on the Nintendo Switch 2:

In a still screenshot, these things now just look like dorky Halloween costumes. In action, they remain pretty terrifying. By blending these two conflicting energies, however, we end up with a Weird Dude.

His dialogue is full of little touches that suggest he’s kind of a little snot, that he loves making little jokes and getting into trouble. Can the fate of Hyrule really rest upon such a lazy boy?
