Ken Urker's Mom Says He Struggled With Split, Cyber Bullying Before Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Ken Urker’s mom, Raina Urker, gave a candid insight into her son’s final days.

Speaking to TMZ in an interview shared on Thursday, November 19, Raina said Ken was struggling with his split from ex-fiancée Gypsy Rose Blanchard, 35, as well as “cruel” cyber bullying before he died.

“He would internalize what people said about him on the internet and it bothered him when it was just so cruel,” she told TMZ.

Raina said Ken was particularly concerned about how the negative online commentary would affect his 22-month-old daughter, Aurora, whom he shared with Blanchard.

“It was such cruel things being said about him and that would bother him because of Aurora,” Raina continued. “He didn’t want Aurora to grow up and see these things and, of course, phone calls were coming out from prison and that bothered him.”

Confirming that Ken and Blanchard were not in a relationship at the time of his death, Raina added that the couple’s break up was also weighing heavily on his mind.

“Gypsy and him were going through ins and outs and that bothered him because he very much did not want Aurora to be raised with split parents,” Raina said, adding that a united family unit was something Ken “wanted his whole life.”

“He wanted the parents to be together,” she explained.

Raina also insisted that Ken never would have committed suicide despite the speculation surrounding his death, stating,  “He loved Aurora way too much to have done anything like that.”

News broke in October that Ken was found dead inside a Louisiana home on his 34th birthday.

The investigation into Ken’s death “remains ongoing” after a coroner confirmed he died by accidental overdose.

“The Lafourche Parish Sheriff’s Office continues to investigate the circumstances surrounding the death of Kenan Urker,” the sheriff’s office told Us in a Monday, November 15, statement. “While we understand the Lafourche Parish Coroner’s Office has released updated information regarding the cause of death, our investigation is separate and remains ongoing.”

Authorities added, “At this time, we have no additional information to release. We will provide an update when we reach a point in the investigation where additional information can be shared.”

Earlier on Monday, Lafourche Parish Coroner’s Office confirmed that Ken’s cause of death was an accidental overdose.

“The final cause of death has been determined to be accidental poly substance drug toxicity,” a statement read. “The case is now complete, and no further testing is pending.”

Ken had a complicated relationship with Blanchard. The pair were first connected when Ken wrote to her in prison in 2017 and they subsequently became pen pals.

At the time, she was behind bars for her role in the 2015 murder of her mother, Clauddine “Dee Dee” Blanchard.

Although Ken and Blanchard were engaged in 2018, they called it quits later that same year.

Blanchard went on to marry Ryan Anderson in July 2022 while still incarcerated. She was released in December 2023 but her marriage to Anderson didn’t go the distance. They called it off in the spring of 2024.

In December 2024, the same month her divorce to Anderson was finalized, Blanchard welcomed Aurora with Ken after they reconnected outside of prison.
