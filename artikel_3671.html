GTA 6’s Console Wars Are Already Over, And Xbox Lost

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Grand Theft Auto games have never shied away from satirizing life, and GTA 6 has plenty of digs at famous brands, trends, and people. Not even gaming is immune to GTA's patented satire, especially if you're playing on Xbox, because there's no escape from PlayStation — or its legally distinct cousin, the JoySystem.

Early in GTA 6, you'll get to bond with Jason's best pal, Cal. After maximizing the energy of Jason's bachelor pad with a little feng shui redecorating, the duo settles in for a night of booze, drugs, and video games on Booby's JoyStation console, which looks very similar to a PS5. It looks like this is the only gaming hardware in Leonida, since there are no Xbox Egg-Cube Stations X to play games on.

The JoySystem isn't just for decoration either; after you've hung out with Bobby, you can return and play games on it. You can choose between an avant-garde experience or more immediate gratification with a pair of shooter games, and it's impressive how good these three games are on their own. From top-down action to stunning mystery that pokes fun at auteur-driven games, they're worth checking out. The JoySystem interface is also a 1:1 copy of the PS5 user interface, with the exact same layout for games and other apps — but given how much Sony is making from GTA 6 sales, we're guessing the company sees it as a cheeky little homage.

In real life, PS5 sales have absolutely dominated GTA 6 preorders and sales. Video game research company Sensor Tower estimates that GTA 6 sales have reached 24.58 million total units via pre-purchases and copies sold at launch, which works out to about $2.36 billion in gross revenue. Of that number, 18.44 million units are said to be PS5 copies of the game, of which Sony will get a 30% cut of sales.
