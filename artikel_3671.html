Matt Damon was clearly trying to fly under the radar during an August 2026 trip to Disneyland to celebrate his daughter Gia’s 18th birthday. Then he ended up clearly visible in the background of internet comedian and content creator Loryn Powell’s video on Instagram, as her daughter was meeting Goofy for the first time. And the video of the movie star dad smiling as he watched and understood how special the father-daughter moment in front of him was unsurprisingly went vital.

Now Matt Damon himself has commented on the unexpected cameo appearance and the story behind it is even lovelier than I could have imagined. During an interview for The Morning Show in Canada, the star of The Odyssey (one of the biggest box office success stories on the 2026 movie schedule) explained how that sweet moment unfolding in front of him reminded him of all the moments he’d shared with his own daughter at Goofy’s Kitchen in the Disneyland Hotel. You can check out what he had to say about it in this Instagram clip:

A post shared by Loryn Powell (@lorynpowell)

When I first saw the video, I immediately thought, “Now there’s a dad who’s having a flashback of a memory with his own daughter.” So it warmed my heart to hear directly from the Good Will Hunting star that that’s exactly what happened. He, himself, has had that exact moment over the year on annual birthday trips that landed him in that very same spot.

When we think of Matt Damon, we picture the international film star, the public friendship with Ben Affleck, or even the Damon/Kimmel faux feud that started back in the early 2000s. We tend to forget that he has four daughters, which means countless family events, gatherings, birthdays, and shared experiences, and endless memories from when they were children. It was beyond heartwarming to see his reaction in real time as the father and daughter sharing time with Goofy brought him back to his own family.

He got called out on all of this in the interview because the host, Brittnee Blair, had recognized a similar look in her own husband watching their four-year-old daughter, knowing how fast time passes and that those moments will merely be memories. Being the movie star on a press tour that he is, the self-proclaimed “anti-Tom Cruise” also related it back to the movies of Christopher Nolan by saying:

It’s a theme in a lot of Chris’ movies, right? Of a father missing the childhood of their kids. That video, one of my kids sent that to me. We were at Goofy’s Kitchen in Disneyland, and we’ve gone every year for my daughter’s birthday, and she was turning 18… And I had no idea somebody was recording that.

I love when the timing lines up and someone can ask a celebrity to explain the story behind a viral moment, making them more relatable to everyone else. And there’s nothing more human than spending and missing time with our loved ones. I can’t imagine what the family in the video thought when they realized that not only was The Bourne Identity star at Disneyland on the same day, but he was in the background of their video, reacting to that special interaction.

There is no shortage of celebrity sightings at theme parks, from Disney super fan Charlize Theron to John Stamos, Adam Sandler, and so many others. Something like this moment with Matt Damon makes me not only want to keep an even more keen eye out, but also comb through my own videos for anyone making an unexpected cameo appearance in the background. Especially knowing that it could lead to another sweet backstory like this one.
