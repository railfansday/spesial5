Ethiopian volcano's mysterious methane outburst may have a culprit—magma that never erupted

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Experts were puzzled when satellite observations showed a volcano in Ethiopia emitting huge quantities of methane despite not erupting, but a team of researchers led by the University of Bristol believe they have discovered why this happened—and their findings could have implications for historical climate change research.
