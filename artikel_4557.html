Young emperor penguins encounter industrial krill fishing during first year at sea

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

New research has revealed young emperor penguins encounter industrial krill fishing in their epic first year at sea, strengthening the case for proposed new marine protected areas. The study was co-led by France's CNRS, the Center Scientifique de Monaco and the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution. The international research team tracked 48 juvenile emperor penguins from three colonies around the Weddell Sea: Snow Hill Island, Jason Peninsula and Atka Bay.
