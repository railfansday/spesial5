American Express Travel on the Next Chapter for Premium Airport Hospitality

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Rasha Masri, Vice President, Lounge Partnerships & Business Development, American Express Travel

Moderated by Carolyn Kremins, President, Skift

Airport lounge operators face a growing challenge in expanding access while preserving the premium experience that attracts customers. Addressing the tension between demand and exclusivity, Masri described a strategy that combines larger lounges, smaller hospitality concepts, and digital access tools to accommodate the needs of different travelers.
