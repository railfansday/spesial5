Transfer Capital One Miles To Qatar Airways Privilege Club With 15% Bonus: Worth It?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Capital One mileage program has just introduced a new bonus for points transfers to Qatar Airways Privilege Club. While the percentage bonus amount isn’t huge, there’s potentially value to be had, especially given the flexibility these rewards offer.

Between November 15 and December 14, 2026, it’s possible to receive a 15% bonus when moving points from Capital One to Qatar Airways Privilege Club. This is being offered on Capital One’s end, so when you go the mileage transfer page, make sure that you see this offer available.

Ordinarily rewards transfer between programs at a 1:1 ratio (in increments of 1,000 miles), while through this promotion you’d get 1,150 Qatar Airways Privilege Club Avios for every 1,000 Capital One miles transferred. Points transfers from Capital One to Qatar Airways are typically instant, and the bonus rewards will post the same time as the standard rewards.

We see transferable points currencies frequently offer bonuses on transfers to a variety of Avios currencies, and you can of course move rewards between those various currencies. We’ve definitely seen bigger than 15% bonuses in the past, but there’s no guarantee we’ll see a higher offer return anytime soon.

I’d argue that the Qatar Airways Privilege Club program is pretty lucrative nowadays. For one, keep in mind that Qatar Airways uses Avios as its rewards currency, so it’s possible to transfer Avios at a 1:1 ratio between the various programs that earn Avios. That includes Aer Lingus AerClub, British Airways Club, Finnair Plus, and Iberia Plus.

This means that you could use this offer to indirectly convert Citi ThankYou points into British Airways Avios with a 15% bonus as well. That’s something that will no doubt interest some.

However, there’s also huge value directly with the Qatar Airways Privilege Club program. Privilege Club has decent award pricing on Qatar Airways flights, and also has access to more business class award availability than partner programs (though availability still isn’t great). Privilege Club also has several unique airline partners, ranging from JetBlue, to RwandAir, to Virgin Australia, opening up valuable redemption opportunities.

Through December 14, 2026, the Capital One program is offering a 15% bonus on points transfers to Qatar Airways Privilege Club. If you’re looking to acquire any “flavor” of Avios, this could be a great value, as this is also a way to get rewards with British Airways, Iberia, etc.

However, if you’re thinking of making a speculative transfer to earn Avios, I might wait for a bigger offer, as we do sometimes see 30%+ bonuses on transfers to some Avios currencies.
