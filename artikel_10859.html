Umamusume’s US restaurant collab has an upside-down Oguri Cap rice bowl and Haru Urara float

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Umamusume fans in the US can now order food inspired by Special Week, Oguri Cap, and Haru Urara, alongside a slew of collectible merch.

The collaboration is with Japanese ramen restaurant Tsujita, which will be offering themed meals across its locations in Los Angeles, New Jersey, and Houston until December 20.

During the horse girl-themed food collab, customers can order five menu items inspired by Umamusume characters, including spicy miso ramen based on Special Week and a massive Oguri Cap rice bowl that reveals fried chicken when flipped upside down.

Kicking things off is Special Week’s Golden Spicy Cheese Miso Ramen, which is available until November 30 and takes inspiration from her Hokkaido roots. The bowl comes filled with grilled corn, melted cheese, bean sprouts, and a carrot-shaped garnish (can’t forget the carrots).

Next up is Fine Motion’s Cajun seafood tsukemen, a dipping noodle dish that pairs rich seafood broth with a spicy Cajun kick. The broth has shrimp, mussels, corn, sausage, bell peppers, and lime in it. So it’s a good pick if you’re a fan of rich seafood flavours. Fine Motion’s dish will be available from December 1 through December 20.

Just like in the anime, Oguri Cap’s Upside-Down Fried Chicken Bowl arrives as a massive mound of rice. You’ll need to flip the bowl to reveal chunks of fried chicken and egg, while Rice Shower’s Purple Bun Set pairs pork chashu and fried chicken with purple cabbage coleslaw.

And in typical last-place fashion, Haru Urara gets a Cheerful Carrot & Strawberry Float that combines generous helpings of carrot, mango, and strawberry, topped with a carrot ribbon. Not a bad call if you’re looking for something bright and fruity to wash all that ramen down with.

Each Uma-themed menu order also comes with one of four randomly distributed coasters, which feature chibi versions of the horse girls. Fans can also buy a $35 T-shirt or a $10 blind bag containing a random pin and sticker.

The collaboration is available at Tsujita’s Arts District location in Los Angeles, Fort Lee in New Jersey, and Houston in Texas until December 20.
