The It-Girl-Approved Airport Pants to Wear on Your Next Trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

From silk pants to slouchy trousers, there’s something for everyone

There’s nothing quite as chic as having good travel style. While we’ve certainly moved on from the days of wearing pencil skirts and heels to walk the glossy airport corridors (thank goodness), jet-set style has evolved into something a little more manageable and comfortable.

These days, sneakers and flats run the show in terms of footwear, but where it gets really interesting is in the pants.

It girls like Gigi Hadid and Kendall Jenner tend to lean into model-off-duty attire in cozy knit bottoms (paired with luxury designer bags) for their busy travel days. Others like Jennifer Lopez, Julia Roberts, and Amal Clooney remain loyal to their jeans, opting for comfortable styles that can support them through the airport chaos.

For effortless travel style, celebrities like Jennifer Lawrence, Zoë Kravitz, and Rihanna have a soft spot for the chic pull-on pant. It’s easy, sleek, and practical all in one. What more could you want?

And then there are other A-listers, like Nicole Kidman and Gwyneth Paltrow, who roll their suitcases in silky pants and tailored jeans, showing the range of sophisticated travel pants.

As we prepare for a busy holiday travel season, shop Bazaar’s guide to the best airport pants, inspired by a few of our favorite stylish celebs.

Supermodels like Kendall Jenner and Gigi Hadid always blend comfort and style when they travel, and knit pants are the perfect solution. As evidenced here, no one does model-off-duty quite like the Guest in Residence founder, who shows how to mix cozy layers effortlessly for a long day of travel.

An equally comfortable but more luxurious take on the lounge pant is a silk or satin pair. Here, Nicole Kidman styles silky black pants with a matching black button-up and sleek loafers for a minimalist airport ensemble.

Jeans and a T-shirt are an outfit formula that always works, even for a long plane ride. Luckily, slouchy, low-rise, and oversize denim styles are trending—as is super-soft denim so cozy, you can take a New York to London red-eye and sleep all night in them.
