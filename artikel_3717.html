How to complete Grand Hotel Heist in ROB RUN

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Grand Hotel Heist is one of the more trickier challenges to complete in ROB RUN and more so if you’re doing it all alone. Like all heists, you have to steal something from a specific location, and you’ll need to prepare accordingly.

It’s important to get the items that you’ll require for the heist. Some these are absolute necessity, while others are optional.

Once you have all the required items, you can start the heist. While it’s perfectly fine to complete the heist on your own, try to find a group since it will make your life easier.

Once you’re inside, destroy any cameras that you can spot. It’s best to avoid looting any item as long as a camera is active. This will ensure that the cops aren’t aware about your misdeeds.

You’ll find civilians inside. It’s best not to shoot at them. Instead, you can threaten them so that they don’t raise an alarm. Playing in a group is going to make this task easier since you can assign one of your allies to keep note of the civilians.

With all the civilians taken care of, you can begin looting the hotel. There will be multiple cabinets and trunks to loot apart from the two main safes. Once the coast is clear, use the jammer to steal the car. Escape the cops, and you’ll complete the Grand Hotel Heist.
