What’s Really Going On With Blake and Erika From ‘Love Is Blind’? Let’s Investigate

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

A new report suggests they’ve “missed each other’s company” since the show.

Confused by Blake Carver and Erika Ellis’ relationship status after Love Is Blind? You’re not alone. Though the pair called off their engagement due to “lifestyle differences” and claim they’re nothing more than friends, fans of the Netflix reality show keep raising eyebrows at their recent sightings.

What’s really going on between these two? The latest speculation is that they’re back on, and it comes after DeuxMoi reported several sightings of the pair since the season 11 reunion.

On Friday, November 13, the outlet posted a pic of Blake and Erika cozying up to each other in public as they’ve “missed each other’s company.”

What’s more: they also attended a Love Is Blind watch party together at The Anchor in Boston on November 8. Eyewitnesses told Deux that “Erika spent much of the evening sitting on Blake’s lap,” while a separate TMZ report claimed they “spent the weekend bopping around Boston.”

According to pics obtained by TMZ, they were spotted “looking awfully friendly… laughing and getting touchy just hours before their Season 11 reunion aired” while shopping near the Prudential Center in downtown Boston.

Though a source told the outlet that “both are single and just riding the wave of life,” Blake separately admitted “there’s definitely still feelings” for Erika—while sitting right next to her—during the November 12 episode of the He Said G Said podcast.

“I would say, our relationship — even though it didn’t end in the traditional way that the show was supposed to end — we fell in love with each other. I’ll never not love Erika. She’ll always have a piece of my heart,” he explained.
