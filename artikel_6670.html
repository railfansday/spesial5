Kendall Jenner’s Under-$200 Bag Is on Sale Ahead of Black Friday

When a fashion editor comes across a certain item once or twice, it’s certainly worth noting as one to watch. When one happens to see it virtually everywhere in a short amount of time, well, it’s safe to say we’re on the verge of meeting the next must-have item. Case in point: The Kate Spade Duo Mini Shoulder Bag.

Kendall Jenner donned the suede version of the saddle-style bag at 818 Outpost at Coachella, letting it serve as just the thing to complete her effortless white-tank-and-shorts ensemble. Now, many iterations of Jenner’s bag are on sale ahead of Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

Jenner wasn’t the only celebrity spotted wearing the sleek little shoulder bag. Alix Earle, Camilla Cabello, and Becky G have been spotted wearing the style too. It’s no surprise, given the compact size and shape, which is perfect for staying secured beneath the arm. The bag—which rings in at $158—is also just the right size for holding the essentials like your phone, a card holder, and your lip gloss and lipstick (which, really, are all one could need for a busy day of running around).

Of course, if you’re the type who needs a little more storage, Kate Spade also has the larger Duo Crossbody Bag, which has been spotted on Laufey, Lola Tung, and Ice Spice. Great news: It’s also on sale in select styles.

Whichever you opt for, one thing is for sure: Kate Spade’s Duo bags make the perfect gift, whether for yourself of another. Ahead, shop the styles that are already on sale.
