Anne Hathaway Has Welcomed Her 3rd Child

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Anne Hathaway is now a mother of three. People confirmed that she has welcomed her third child. The outlet didn’t share the baby’s name, sex, or birth date.

Hathaway announced her pregnancy in June with an Instagram Reel: “x Baby, I’m yours x,” she captioned the post.

She and her husband, Adam Shulman, also share two sons.

In July, she appeared on Late Night with Seth Meyers and spoke about how thrilled she and Shulman were to be expecting again, calling their new addition a “buzzer-beater” baby.

“It’s amazing. We knew what we were doing,” she said. “But we were so shocked it worked...we were just so shocked it went this way. So we’re calling this one our buzzer-beater.”

In April, she spoke lovingly about raising her family with Shulman in an interview with People. She highlighted the way he stepped up this year as she promoted five films (Mother Mary, The Devil Wears Prada 2, The Odyssey, The End of Oak Street, and Verity).

“He supports me completely,” she said. “This year in particular was unusual. He and I both know that it’s probably never going to happen like this again. And the way he stepped up, I mean, in every possible way, he’s the most extraordinary person I’ve ever met.

“I’m so lucky that he’s my partner that I spend my life with,” she continued. “If I didn’t know that before this past year, I think I really know it now because with absolutely everything he’s just, he’s on it. He holds it down. I hope that doesn’t sound like I’m bragging, but he’s a dream partner to me. It’s one thing to have dreams. It’s another thing to have somebody who helps you achieve them. I absolutely would not be able to have achieved what I’ve done without my husband.”
