Robert Pattinson’s Hair Is Already 007 Material

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Whether you like it or not (or placed bets on Kalshi), Robert Pattinson is our next James Bond. During the years-long process in casting 007, we used our best detective skills to narrow down the field to who would join His Majesty’s Secret Service. And if you were going by hair type alone, well, Pattinson makes a rather compelling case.

Let’s review the canon for a moment. In the 1957 novel From Russia, with Love, Bond creator Ian Fleming describes the spy’s mane thusly: “The hair was black, parted on the left, and carelessly brushed so that a thick black comma fell down over the right eyebrow.”

Pattinson’s hair might be naturally light brown, but a darker ‘do is hardly out of the question for the actor’s upcoming transformation. For his turn as Bruce Wayne in The Batman, he dyed his hair to a darker, almost pitch-black hue. The rest of Fleming’s description fits the actor nicely, particularly the bit about being “carelessly brushed.” In real life, the actor’s hair is rarely anything approaching immaculate. Even at more formal engagements, it tends to retain a certain studied dishevelment, with stray strands escaping an otherwise respectable comb-through. Fleming’s famous “comma,” after all, isn’t a carefully sculpted curl. It’s simply a lock of hair allowed to fall where it pleases, which just so happens to be very on trend in 2026.

We got our first glimpse of the 40-year-old actor taking on the role in a video posted by the official James Bond X account where he’s seen stepping out of the shadows pointing a gun at something (or someone) just outside the frame. It’s hard to tell whether the golden lighting is playing tricks on his hair color, but the accompanying promotional image certainly seems to suggest a shade of black. Plus, his hair’s parted on the correct side.

It’s worth noting that Daniel Craig’s casting as Bond was initially met with considerable resistance, much of it centered on one detail: He’s blonde. Fleming’s Bond, after all, was famously dark-haired, and Craig represented a departure from the physical template established by the novels and previous screen incarnations. We know how that turned out. Craig went on to deliver one of the most celebrated interpretations of 007, proving that a little deviation from the source material needn’t be a deal-breaker.

Pattinson will still need to prove himself to be agile in a tuxedo, nimble behind the wheel, and thirsty for a martini or two, but one thing’s sure: He's got the hair down pat.
