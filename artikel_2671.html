Lions vs. New England Patriots in Munich, Germany: Live updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The NFL's international schedule now settles itself in Munich, Germany as the Lions travel overseas to host last season's championship runner-ups in the New England Patriots. Participating in just their third official international game in franchise history, Detroit looks extend their winning streak to five-straight games.

The contest is also a homecoming for Lions wide receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown, who has wanted to play in the country where his mother hails from for years. While the Lions won't be getting Kerby Joseph back for this game, safety Andre Cisco will make his debut for the team after being traded to Detroit from the Jets, the only move in a quiet trade deadline for the organization.
