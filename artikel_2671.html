Bryce Young a full participant but with an ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Bryce Young was listed with a knee injury on the Panthers' practice reports over the past four weeks. The quarterback was listed with an ankle injury Wednesday.

Young, though, was estimated as a full participant.

Outside linebacker Jaelan Phillips (hamstring), left guard Damien Lewis (knee), linebacker Bobby Okereke (foot) and rookie cornerback Will Lee III (hamstring) were listed as non-participants.

Phillips has missed the past two games, and Lewis missed last week's game against the Packers.

Offensive linemen Corey Bullock (quad) and Torricelli Simpkins III (ankle) were limited.
