Dan Quinn on Stefon Diggs: I love his fire

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Monday night ended well for the Commanders, but the start of the game was rough for the team’s offense and frustrations about that boiled over on the sideline at one point.

Wide receiver Stefon Diggs was caught by ESPN cameras yelling in the direction of quarterback Jayden Daniels and lip readers shared that he appeared to be telling him to “throw the f—-king ball” after a sack ended a Washington drive in the second quarter. The Commanders bounced back with a touchdown the next time they got the ball and wound up with a 22-20 win on a last-second field goal.

The result made it easier to gloss over any bad feelings that cropped up earlier in the evening and head coach Dan Quinn said on Tuesday that he has no issue with Diggs using his voice in that manner.

“I love his fire, I do,” Quinn said, via John Keim of ESPN.com. “I love that. There’s a lot of good that comes with it where it can sound like he’s mad but it’s like some energy that comes behind it.”

Quinn added that “if something didn’t feel right I would step in and address it,” but that the current mindset in Washington appears to be all’s well that ends well.
