Lions, Bears Week 12 injury report: 4 defenders unable to practice; DJ Reed upgraded to FULL

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Detroit Lions (8-2) are back home in Week 12 and preparing to host the Chicago Bears (8-2) for their annual Thanksgiving Day game. With the Lions on a short week, they've elected to hold a walkthrough on Monday.

A walkthrough practice means that all participation levels are estimates from the coaching staff based on how the player would have participated in a traditional practice. So, with that in mind, let's look at Monday's injury report and get you up to date on what we know.

Note: Any changes from the most recent injury report will be bolded.

RB Isiah Pacheco (MCL/Back, IR) — expected to be out until December

C Cade Mays (ankle, IR) — eligible to return to practice in Week 15

DT Skyler Gill-Howard (knee) — New injury

CB Ennis Rakestraw (shoulder) — Downgraded from limited

Gill-Howard appeared to suffer a leg injury late in Sunday's game and was seen walking gingerly in the locker room, bypassing interviews to get treatment from trainers. On a short week, there is some concern for his ability to recover in time to play against the Bears, but it's too early to make any sweeping decisions.

Wonnum played nearly three-quarters of defensive snaps on Sunday, while Clark played every snap against the Bucs, right on par with both ot thier usual workloads. Neither appeared to be affected by their injuries, so it's possible they popped up after the game, and the Lions are being proactive. We should get updates from coach Dan Campbell at his press conference tomorrow.

Rakestraw was limited all week with a shoulder injury and was declared inactive at game time. With the Lions going light at corner, it's unclear whether he was inactive because of his injury, a coaching decision, or a combination of both. That said, starting the week by not practicing isn't a great sign.

Like Wonnum and Clark, LaPorta played his traditional number of snaps and didn't appear to be limited by injury on Sunday. However, his being estimated to have been limited is a positive sign that he has a real chance to play on Thursday. Hopefully, we will get an update on his status on Tuesday from Campbell.

Reed took in limited practices all last week, was listed as questionable, but did not play against the Bucs. This wasn't surprising, as Reed said earlier in the week he planned to take his recovery slow. He's been consistently making encouraging progress, so he could be close to playing as soon as Thursday, but his status could end up being a game-time decision again.

Dorsey was limited in practices last week with an ankle injury, but managed to play in the game, and is no longer listed as having an ankle injury. Unfortunately, Dorsey appeared to injure his wrist punching at the football and was forced out of the game for testing. On a positive note, Dorsey was able to return for special teams reps and is estimated to have practiced in full, meaning he's in a decent spot to stay in the mix on Thursday.

The players listed in this section are now healthy enough to not require listing on the injury report.

Joseph took the field for the first time in over a year, played two-thirds of the Lions' defensive snaps, and is looking "pretty good" in his post-game recovery process, according to Campbell. With an emphasis on recovery/rehabilitation and a short week ahead, look for the Lions to take it slow with Joseph; that said, no longer being listed is a great sign he should be good to go on Thursday.

RB Roschon Johnson (ankle) — injured last game

CB Kyler Gordon (shoulder) — missed last game
