Rockets clamber over Clippers 116-104

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Tonight the Rockets took on a Los Angeles Clippers team, a team that is staggering its way through a dense fog of official opprobrium, that is almost a mirror image of the Rockets. The Rockets stand at 13-5, the Clippers at 4-12. The Rockets are 8-1 at home, the Clippers 0-8 on the road. Would the Clippers find their way to a first road win tonight? If you read the title of this recap, obviously not.

The game, though, was surprisingly close until the end. We can't say that the Clippers fired up the tank machine late, because they don't have their own picks for the next five seasons. There's no reason for the Clippers not to try to win, and try they did. It's just that the Clippers team is currently an island of misfit toys, a clipper ship of the damned, a rag tag band of hard luck sports millionaires just trying for one last score. Well, not really, but it is a somewhat odd team.

The team features Darius Garland, Brandon Ingram playing for the first time all season, just to annoy me, cross town transfer Rui Hachimura, Derrick Jones Jr, shooting around 50% from 3pt range until this game, and the ageless wonder, Brooke Lopez still very large and able to shoot 3pters at 38.

It's not a terrible lineup, nor is it particularly good, with the exception of Darius Garland, who looks healthy for the first time in several years, and you can see the quickness, and ability to get anywhere, and shoot from anywhere, on the court. He scored 26pts on 10-21 shooting, 4-10 3pt, and had 8ast to 1 TO. That's about the extent of actually dangerous Clipper players.

That said, this game was simply awkward. The Rockets let the Clippers hang around, seemingly unable to string together both baskets and stops for very long. The Rockets would build a lead out to around 10 points, and the Clippers would bring that down to around 6 points, or less. There might have been a bit of mild panic that the Clippers would indeed manage to get their first win on the road, if someone got hot late for them, and the Rockets scoring dried up.

The Rockets scoring did not in fact dry up, mainly due to a Rockets player who has been quiet for much of the season, rarely asserting himself to lead the way. A deferential role player known to some as "The Slim Reaper" or "KD". This low shooting volume veteran, Kevin Durant, lead the way tonight with 32 points, on 11-19 shooting, 4-6 3pt, 9 rbs, 4 ast, 2 TO, 1 blk. He did this in only 33 minutes. In fact none of the Rockets starters played big minutes tonight, Durant's 33 minutes was the highest on the team. Alperen Sengun continued his good form, going 20pts. 7-11 FG, 1-2 3pt, 8 rbs, 7 ast, an uncharacteristic 5 TO, and 2 blk. Amen Thompson had a great return to form, and was able to get in close to score, also notching 20 pt, on 9-12 FG, 1-1 3pt, 4 rbs, 5 ast, 0 TO, 1 blk.

This game might have been easier if the Rockets had followed a first quarter where they only allowed 15 points to the Clippers, to 25 of their own, by not letting the Clippers back in the game, losing the second quarter 28-22.

The Rockets got a better, if not outstanding, performance from the bench than they did in Denver. Tari Eason had one of the most Tari Eason sequences ever tonight as well. He followed a powerful late clock dunk by ripping the ball from a Clipper at mid court. He then strolled down the lane, alone, to try, well, I don't know what sort of stylish shot he had in mind. He absolutely bricked this uncontested attempt, which was rebounded by a trailing Clipper, whom Eason immediately fouled. Tarissimo!

We saw 25 minutes from Marcus Smart, as he seems to be creeping towards starter level minutes, despite not contributing all that much on the scoresheet. What joy he brings to Ime Udoka's heart, I suppose we will never quite know, but I suspect it's less than he brings to mine.

One big takeaway from this game is that the Rockets moved the ball quickly, and decisively for the most part tonight, aside, oddly from Fred VanVleet who only had 4 ast. The team overall had 36 assists on 46 made baskets, against 13 turnovers. That sort of performance is good to see, as most of the shots generated were good ones, and we saw less of the over passing that sometimes characterizes the team when they want to move the ball around. The Rockets had six players with four or more assists tonight – Durant (4), Sengun (7), VanVleet (4), Thompson (5), Eason (4), Sheppard (5). Durant lead the team with 19 attempts, so it truly was a group effort on offense.

There's not much more about a game that felt awkward at times, but not exactly in doubt. The Rockets take on the Suns, looking for vengeance, in their final NBA Cup game on Wednesday.

The Rockets continue rolling through the regular season at a great win rate. Until next time, Happy Thanksgiving to all of you, and yours.
