Jets QB Geno Smith avoids major injuries after leaving loss to Bills multiple times with concussion, ankle issue

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Though he left Sunday's contest multiple times with various injuries, Geno Smith appears to have avoided anything major.

The New York Jets quarterback underwent tests on Monday that did not reveal any major issues, including on his ankle, according to the NFL Network's Ian Rapoport. Smith will continue to be evaluated this week, but he appears to be in a good spot to make the start again next Sunday for their contest against the Los Angeles Chargers.

Smith left Sunday's 31-12 loss to the Buffalo Bills multiple times. He went down initially at the end of the first half when he was hit both high and low after throwing a pass, and he fell hard down to the ground on his back. He remained down for several moments before finally getting up and walking to the sideline medical tent.

Backup Cade Klubnik then took the field for the Jets to start the second half.

Smith returned after clearing concussion protocol, but then left the game the following possession after a big hit. He was then sidelined with an ankle injury the rest of the way.

Smith finished throwing 13-of-21 for 148 yards in the loss. Klubnik wasn't much better, and the Jets have now gone nine straight quarters without scoring a touchdown.

Smith has thrown for 2,226 yards with 12 touchdowns and three interceptions this season, his first back with the Jets since 2016. The team holds just a 2-8 record and is well out of the playoff hunt already. They are well within range of securing the No. 1 overall pick in the 2027 NFL Draft at this point, too.

While the Jets haven't made a decision just yet, it sounds like Smith will be able to go in their matchup with the Chargers next weekend. The Chargers entered their Monday Night Football contest against the Baltimore Ravens as the league's only winless team, so Sunday will be a very big opportunity for Smith and the Jets to end their losing skid.
