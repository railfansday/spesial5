Former Jets advance step closer to immortality in Pro Football Hall of Fame

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The road to Canton just got a little shorter for three former New York Jets, including one of the most beloved players in franchise history. The Pro Football Hall of Fame announced Tuesday that 52 players have advanced in the selection process for the Class of 2027. Late center Nick Mangold, defensive end John Abraham, and running back Frank Gore are among those still in contention.

Mangold's inclusion carries particular significance. He spent his entire 11-year NFL career with the Jets from 2006-16, earning seven Pro Bowl selections and three All-Pro honors. A cornerstone of New York's offensive line during its back-to-back AFC Championship Game appearances, Mangold became one of the franchise's most recognizable figures.

He passed away in October 2025, making the possibility of a posthumous Hall of Fame honor especially meaningful to the organization and its fans.

Abraham also has a strong case built around his time in New York. He recorded 53.5 sacks and 19 forced fumbles during six seasons with the Jets from 2000-05 before continuing his career with the Atlanta Falcons and Arizona Cardinals. His 133.5 career sacks rank among the highest totals in NFL history.

Gore's connection to the franchise was considerably shorter, as he played his final NFL season with New York in 2020. Still, his place among the candidates reflects a remarkable career that included 16,000 rushing yards and five Pro Bowl selections.

The selection committee will now reduce the group of 52 to 25 players, with those results expected in approximately two weeks. The eventual player finalists will join coaching candidate Bill Belichick and contributor candidate Robert Kraft in the final selection process.

The Class of 2027 will be announced during NFL Honors in February, with enshrinement scheduled for August in Canton, Ohio. For Mangold, Abraham and Gore, the next step brings the possibility of football's highest individual honor a little closer.
