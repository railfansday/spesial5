Australia and England head into the third and deciding ODI in Hobart with the series level at 1-1 after two one-sided contests. It is the first time since 1979 that the teams have entered the final match of an ODI series in Australia with the score tied.

Bellerive Oval will also host a men's ODI for the first time since 2018 in a game that could offer both sides a valuable confidence boost ahead of next year's World Cup.

Despite missing several frontline players, Australia will look to make home advantage count, but England have an opportunity to secure a rare ODI series victory on Australian soil.
