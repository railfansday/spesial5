Thanks for nothing, 2026 Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

2026 has been a season of turkeys. Just about everything that could go wrong has gone wrong. Let us laugh at how thorough the Eagles disappointment has been.

The haters said he added nothing to the team, and they were right. Great call by the haters.

Trades and free agency: bust. Jonathan Greenard, Hollywood Brown, Riq Woolen, Arnold Ebiteke, and Andy Dalton were duds.

The draft: yes, it is early but the 2026 draft class looks terrible.

No one the Eagles acquired in the offseason has had even a decent year.

Would it surprise you to learn that the Eagles employ the previous two Jets GMs?

There is a silver lining, the trades that traded away players were fantastic.
