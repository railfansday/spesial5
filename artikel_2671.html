Fernando Mendoza's first NFL TD comes on designed run reminiscent of his iconic score that sealed Indiana's national championship

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

So far, so good for starting NFL quarterback Fernando Mendoza.

With Kirk Cousins playing and winning with the Minnesota Vikings on Sunday, the Mendoza era officially began in Las Vegas as the rookie quarterback made his first NFL start.

It began with reason for celebration on Las Vegas' first possession of the game against the reigning champion Seattle Seahawks.

Mendoza began his first NFL possession in good field position after a Seahawks fumble. He finished it 46 yards later with a 10-yard touchdown run for his first NFL score and a 7-0 Raiders lead.

Mendoza also completed all three of his first-possession passes for 20 yards before keeping the ball on a designed draw for the score.

The play was reminiscent of the touchdown Mendoza scored to cap Indiana's College Football Championship game win over Miami last season.

The Raiders used the No. 1 pick in the NFL Draft to select Mendoza after his Heisman Trophy-winning and national championship campaign. Save a brief appearance in a Week 5 loss to the Patriots, he spent his first eight NFL weeks learning from the bench with tutelage from Cousins.

But the Vikings traded for Cousins ahead of the trade deadline, starting the Mendoza era in earnest. And with the Super Bowl champions in town, Mendoza didn't blink on his first possession as an NFL starter.
