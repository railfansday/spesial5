Rakuten’s 100% Rebates Let You Buy Amex Points For 1 Cent Each—Bilt Members With Status Get Even More

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Rakuten is offering 100% back on several subscriptions, which means you can effectively buy American Express Membership Rewards points for 1 cent apiece. And if you have Bilt Silver status or higher, you can receive Bilt points at the same price. That’s the option I’d choose, because Bilt points are far more valuable. These deals can make sense even if you assign no value at all to the subscription. Spend $100 on an eligible purchase and choose 10,000 points instead of the $100 rebate. You keep the service, too. And there’s a best-ever Rakuten referral offer – Rakuten is an online shopping portal that earns points or cash back for the purchases you make anywhere, akin to the mileage malls that work with airlines like American, United, Alaska, et al. You can choose between cash back, Amex points or Bilt points. The easy, quick win before this offer is just to go to GiftCards.com through the portal and buy a $50 gift card for Uber, DoorDash, Southwest Airlines or Delta. You get $50 or equivalent points back, making this a free $50 (or better, since the points can be worth more than $50). I’ve covered using Rakuten offers to acquire inexpensive Amex points for years, and this summer’s Surfshark offer worked the same way. There are several opportunities available together now. The Current Offers Doctor of Credit flags this batch of offers. Each subscription offer is limited to a new customer or first subscription purchase, with a limit of one per Rakuten member. Merchant   Rebate   Terms That Affect The Deal Coveron, formerly NordProtect   100%   All listed individual and family subscription plans qualify. If you already earned a NordProtect rebate, the name change doesn’t make you eligible again. NordVPN   100%   Only the Basic plan portion of Plus, Complete or Prime bundles earns rewards. Orders must go through the U.S. site. NordPass   100%   First subscription signup only. Other Nord products purchased through this offer don’t qualify. Incogni   95%   First subscription payment only, with a 40 day verification period. Surfshark   95%   First purchase only. Subscription extensions and free trial promotions don’t qualify. The 95% offers work out to 95 Amex points, or 95 Bilt points with status, per eligible dollar. That’s about 1.05 cents per point before tax. Coveron is especially interesting because Rakuten explicitly includes its full individual and family plans. At the prices Coveron advertises: Two years of individual Full Protection costs $239.76, which would generate 23,976 points at 100 points per dollar. Two years of Full Family Protection costs $647.76, which would generate 64,776 points. Those calculations assume the advertised price and 100% rate are what you see when you click through Rakuten. The family plan lets you acquire a larger block of points at the same price per point. Rakuten also says Coveron, NordVPN and NordPass are separate products, so you can qualify for each. That doesn’t override the requirement to be a new customer for the particular offer. Sales tax doesn’t earn the portal rebate. If your purchase attracts 8.25% tax, a 100 points per dollar offer costs about 1.08 cents per point. The rewards from the credit card you use to pay are on top of this though. Why I’d Take Bilt Points Over Amex Points When Rakuten added Bilt as a payout option, I preferred it to Membership Rewards. I still do, provided I’m getting the full conversion rate. For a $100 eligible purchase with a 100% rebate, here’s the choice: Payout Choice   Points Received   Cost Per Point Before Tax American Express Membership Rewards   10,000   1 cent Bilt Silver, Gold or Platinum   10,000   1 cent Bilt Blue, without elite status   5,000   2 cents You need an eligible U.S. American Express card enrolled in Membership Rewards to receive Amex points through Rakuten. Without Bilt status, I’d choose the 10,000 Amex points over 5,000 Bilt points. Bilt gives me 1:1 transfers to Hyatt, Alaska Airlines’ Atmos Rewards, United Airlines and Japan Airlines, along with partners like Air Canada Aeroplan and Air France KLM Flying Blue. Buying Bilt points for a penny gives me access to those programs without having to decide today which one I’ll use. And Bilt’s Rent Day transfer bonuses can make those points go further. Set Up The Points Payout And Keep The Paid Subscription Choose Membership Rewards in your Rakuten account settings and link your Amex account. For Bilt, link your existing Rakuten account through the Bilt app or website and select Bilt Points as the payout method. The email addresses on your Rakuten and Bilt accounts need to match. If you’re switching between Amex and Bilt, Bilt’s instructions say to select check or PayPal first, then the other points option. Start your purchase through the relevant Rakuten merchant link, check the rate, and save the offer terms and receipt. Don’t apply a coupon from somewhere else when the merchant terms exclude it. These are subscription purchases. A refund can reverse the rewards. Turning off a future renewal is different from refunding the paid term: Coveron allows you to cancel auto-renewal while keeping access through the period you’ve paid for. Rakuten pays points quarterly. The next scheduled payout is February 15, 2027, and rewards need to be confirmed to be included. A pending entry can take weeks to become payable. Bilt uses your status at payout to determine the conversion rate. And Rakuten transfers don’t count toward Bilt elite status, so this isn’t a way for a Blue member to buy their way to Silver. If you’re new to Rakuten, the referral offer adds $50 after $50 in qualifying purchases within 90 days. With an Amex payout, or Bilt’s full conversion rate, that’s another 5,000 points. On the $647.76 Coveron example, that would mean 69,776 points for $647.76 before tax—about 0.93 cents apiece.
