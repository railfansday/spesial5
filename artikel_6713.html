The Best Apple Deals of the Year Are Here

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

AirPods, Apple Watches, and AirTags are at their lowest prices ever

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Black Friday is arguably the best time of the year to shop for Apple products. While the tech giant doesn’t run sales of its own, other retailers are offering markdowns during Cyber Week. You’ll find AirPods, MacBooks, Apple Watches, and other Apple products on sale right now. Amazon’s Black Friday sale is officially here, so you don’t need to wait any longer to get a head start on your holiday shopping.

Several Apple products are at their lowest price ever, including the AirPods Pro 3, Apple Watch Series 12, and SE 3. If you’re traveling for the holidays, take advantage of the retailer’s deals on Apple AirTags. Individual tags are at an all-time low price of $18, making them a slightly better deal than a pack of four. Plus, MacBooks are rarely on sale, but select styles are $300 off right now.

Apple Apple Watch SE 3 GPS 40mm Smartwatch

The new AirPods 5 remain at full price for now, but other Apple headphones are on sale. Currently, you can save a record 40 percent on the noise-canceling AirPods Pro 3, plus 24 percent on over-the-ear AirPods Max 2. As we’ve seen in years past, these products could easily sell out before the holiday weekend is over.

If you need a new laptop, now’s a good time to buy one. The 2026 MacBook Pro and 2026 MacBook Air are both on sale during Amazon’s Black Friday sale. We don’t expect prices to drop significantly, if at all, by Cyber Monday, so we don’t recommend waiting to shop.

Four iPad models—the Pro, Air, Mini, and classic 11-inch iPad—are also marked down. Now’s a great time to shop accessories like chargers and keyboards, too.
