A Guide to Cruelty-Free Skin Care

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

How to spot non-animal-tested formulas and the cleansers, serums, and moisturizers to start with.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Every skin care product should be cruelty-free, but in the world of beauty regulations, rules in the U.S. neither require brands to conduct animal testing to prove a product is safe nor prohibit the practice. Brands have proudly volunteered to be “cruelty-free” certified by organizations like Cruelty Free International (Leaping Bunny) and PETA, and a growing number of countries and states have banned or restricted animal testing. You can easily identify cruelty-free certified brands by their labeling (look for the bunny). And just because it’s not tested on animals doesn’t mean it’s any less effective or safe. To jump-start your not-animal-tested lineup or to introduce a new addition to your already existing range, here are some of the best cruelty-free skin care products.

The lightweight Cloud Cushion cream is loaded with brightening and texture-smoothing niacinamide, complexion-evening peptides, multiple moisturizers (squalane and snow mushroom), and ceramides to keep skin from losing moisture.
