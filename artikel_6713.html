All the Celebrity Guests at Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando’s Wedding

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are officially married! The singer and the drummer tied the knot during an intimate ceremony in Palm Springs, California, on Saturday, November 21. The couple exchanged vows surrounded by romantic white florals beside a swimming pool, according to photos published by People.

Back in March, a source told People that the couple didn’t want a huge wedding. “When they do get married, it will be something smaller and meaningful to them,” the insider said. “Not a big Hollywood production.”

The newlyweds reportedly had Wayne Coyne, frontman of the Flaming Lips and longtime collaborator and friend of Cyrus’s, officiate their nuptials. Several members of the Cyrus family were also in attendance, along with a few other familiar faces. Below, see who celebrated the couple’s big day.

The bride’s mother, Tish Cyrus, attended alongside her husband, actor Dominic Purcell. The couple coordinated in black for the occasion, with Tish wearing an off-the-shoulder, fitted floor-length gown. Tish shares Miley, Noah, and Braison Cyrus with her ex-husband Billy Ray Cyrus. She married Purcell, best known for his role on Prison Break, in August 2023.

Miley’s father, country singer Billy Ray Cyrus, was also there to celebrate his daughter’s wedding. He attended alongside his girlfriend, actress Elizabeth Hurley, in coordinated all-black velvet looks, with Hurley’s gown featuring a prominent slit with detailed embellishments and a feathered jacket to match. The pair first met while filming the 2022 holiday movie Christmas in Paradise and confirmed their relationship in April 2025.

Miley’s younger sister, singer-songwriter Noah Cyrus, was among the family members present for the celebration. Noah has released several projects of her own and once described her sister as “a great role model for how it all works.”

Hurley was joined by her son, model and filmmaker Damian Hurley. The 24-year-old has followed in his mother’s footsteps in the fashion and entertainment industries, and the two frequently attend events together. He was also photographed posing with the newlyweds and members of the Cyrus family at the after-party.

Dominic Purcell’s son, Joseph Purcell, was also photographed arriving at the wedding. The 27-year-old is Miley’s stepbrother following his father’s marriage to Tish in 2023.

Coyne was photographed officiating the wedding. The Flaming Lips singer and Miley have a longstanding creative relationship, having collaborated on her 2015 experimental album, Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz.
