Hackers have breached Cloud One, the US Air Force’s flagship cloud computing platform, Reuters reported. The attack could expose sensitive military, personnel and logistics data. The platform supports systems tracking weapons at over 200 Air Force locations, air defence radar feeds, aircraft maintenance and personnel records. The breach was reportedly discovered during a system upgrade, with attackers exploiting a software vulnerability to access its control plane. The attackers’ identity and the extent of data exposure remain unknown. The Air Force has suspended some updates and restricted administrator access, while US Cyber Command investigates the incident.
