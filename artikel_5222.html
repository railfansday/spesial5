In a new announcement, major crypto exchange Binance says it will launch an airdrop campaign rewarding all eligible users who hold Ripple USD (RLUSD) on its platform. Eligible users will share rewards from a grand prize pool of 600,000 XRP.

In its statement, Binance stated that eligible incremental RLUSD holdings may qualify for up to a 1.3× reward multiplier, with APRs updated weekly. The campaign will run from today, November 11, 2026, to December 18, 2026, at 00:00 (UTC).

Share 600,000 XRP by holding RLUSD in eligible Binance accounts. Eligible incremental holdings may qualify for up to a 1.3× reward multiplier. APRs are updated weekly. More here → https://t.co/CYCSS51Yrv https://t.co/a1IyTWSWHl

Eligible users must hold RLUSD in their balances (net assets) in either their Binance Spot Accounts, Funding Accounts, and Earn Accounts (RLUSD in Flexible Savings) or their Margin Accounts (RLUSD in Cross Margin, Isolated Margin, or Portfolio Margin). Eligible users must maintain at least 0.01 RLUSD in their Spot, Funding, Earn, or Margin Accounts to qualify for the rewards.

Rewards will be airdropped directly to the Spot Accounts of eligible users’ Binance master accounts and will start accruing on November 20, 2026, at 00:00 (UTC). Weekly rewards will be distributed by 18:00 (UTC) every Friday in XRP.

This continues Binance’s recent streak of rewarding RLUSD holders with XRP giveaways in recent months.

Meanwhile, RLUSD continues to gain traction. Ripple's dollar-pegged stablecoin now ranks as the sixth-largest stablecoin by circulating supply globally.

Currently, RLUSD has a market valuation of $2.8 billion; $746 million of this sits on the XRPL while the stablecoin continues to find new use cases on the blockchain.

An AI agent pays on the XRP Ledger by requesting a service and gets a price back. It signs a payment in XRP or RLUSD. The service is delivered, and the XRPL x402 facilitator, provided by t54ai, verifies and settles the payment.

In separate news, Binance says it will extend the Monitoring Tag to include more tokens on November 20, 2026, based on recent reviews. The tokens to be added to the Monitoring Tag list are Lorenzo Protocol (BANK), Initia (INIT), Mitosis (MITO), Harmony (ONE), THENA (THE), Zilliqa (ZIL). The tokens to be removed from the Seed Tag list are: Morpho Token (MORPHO), Hyperliquid (HYPE).
