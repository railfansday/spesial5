Bitcoin ETF investors have a way to hedge during Thanksgiving’s 40+ hour trading gap

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Arca-listed spot Bitcoin ETFs miss Thursday’s core session. Eligible CME futures can still trade, with Thursday trades submitted for clearing due to clear Friday.

Spot Bitcoin ETFs listed on NYSE Arca will miss a full core trading session on Thanksgiving. The exchange’s Friday core session will open 41.5 hours after Wednesday’s core close. During the Thursday closure, traders with access to CME Group’s derivatives market can still use eligible Bitcoin futures to adjust their exposure to Bitcoin’s price.

Arca’s core session closes at 4 p.m. ET Wednesday, Nov. 25, and reopens at 9:30 a.m. ET Friday, Nov. 27, according to the NYSE holiday calendar. On Friday, early trading starts at 4 a.m. ET, the core session closes at 1 p.m. ET and late trading ends at 5 p.m. ET.

CME says standard and Micro Bitcoin futures and eligible options trade around the clock on Globex, with weekday maintenance from 4:00 to 4:02 p.m. CT. Traders need an account with a futures broker that offers access. Spot-Quoted futures follow a separate schedule.

An investor holding the Grayscale Bitcoin Trust ETF, which holds Bitcoin and lists its shares on Arca, could sell cash-settled Bitcoin futures to hedge against a decline in Bitcoin’s price. If both the ETF shares and the futures fall, gains from the futures position could offset some of the loss on the shares. The investor keeps the shares, and their price can move by a different amount from the futures price.

CME says it will skip new daily settlement prices for crypto contracts on Thursday. It will still publish fixings, the prices used to determine exercise and assignment for crypto options expiring that day.

Under the CME Clearing holiday advisory, Wednesday’s settlement prices will carry forward for Thursday’s end-of-day settlement variation, the daily debits and credits tied to changes in settlement prices. CME will skip its intraday cycle. It will still recalculate initial margin, the collateral required to carry positions, at the end of the day.

Trades submitted to CME Clearing on Thursday will clear Friday. With settlement banks closed for Thanksgiving, CME says cash margin, collateral transfers and settlement variation payments will go unprocessed Thursday. A futures trade can change a trader’s exposure when it executes, while clearing and cash movements follow the holiday schedule.

CME has scheduled Friday’s daily equity and crypto settlement prices for noon Central. At that hour, 1 p.m. Eastern, Arca’s shortened core session will end.
