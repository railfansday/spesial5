Apple’s MacBook Neo is $100 off at Amazon — its biggest-ever discount for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

SAVE $100: As of Nov. 26, the Apple MacBook Neo with 512GB of storage and Touch ID is $100 off on Amazon — its biggest discount to date. Normally $799, it's now on sale for just $699.

It's an early holiday miracle: Amazon just gave the Apple MacBook Neo its biggest discount ever.

We were on hand when Apple launched its first-ever budget laptop in New York City this spring, and we (rightfully) predicted it was going to become a bestseller. The Neo put the entire Windows laptop world on notice with its speedy iPhone chip and elite build quality. And — bonus — it comes in fun colors.

An upgraded MacBook Neo with 512GB of storage, Touch ID, and a "blush" pink finish is now $100 off during Amazon's Black Friday Week sale. Not only does this deal completely erase the Neo's price hike from June, but it also beats the Apple Store's education pricing for the Neo — that's never happened before. (We expect this deal to sell out fast)

Meanwhile, the base MacBook Neo with 256GB of storage is $100 off at Amazon in all four colors (blush, citrus, indigo, and silver). This price drop couldn't come at a better time. Mashable recently named the Neo a Gift of the Year winner. If a teen or younger student you know needs a new laptop, we think they'll be delighted to unwrap this one. It's just as well-made as other MacBooks, has an all-day battery life (it lasted exactly 14 hours and 50 minutes in our battery rundown test), and runs basic tasks faster than most premium Windows laptops we've tried.

As we said when it originally launched, the "MacBook Neo is a shot across the bow of the Windows laptop market." To quote our tech editor, "This colorful budget laptop brings the MacBook's premium design elements and user experience to a whole new customer base."

In terms of performance, the A18 Pro chip held up surprisingly well in our stress testing. "I was able to edit a simple, cheesy video in Final Cut Pro using the MacBook Neo without struggling against the spinning wheel of death. Even when I layered video clips on top of each other and added a series of totally unnecessary transitions, the Neo handled the tasks just fine," our tech editor wrote.
