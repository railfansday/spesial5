The 10 Scotland squad winners and losers - from Rangers key man to Celtic starlet

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Scotland head coach Sebastien Pocognoli has made a handful of changes to his latest squad for the upcoming Nations League double header with North Macedonia and Switzerland.

The Belgian has named only one new call-up for the final two fixtures of League B1 later this month. Rangers defender James Penrice is included in the senior set-up for the first time, while Napoli midfielder Scott McTominay and Torino striker Che Adams return to the fold.

Pocognoli faces a significant early challenge in his Hampden reign as he looks to recover from consecutive home defeats. The Scots will need to collect at least four points from their home clash with bottom side North Macedonia and their trip to group leaders Switzerland to keep their hopes of finishing in the top two alive.

However, even that may not be enough. Scotland must also rely on Slovenia dropping points in both of their remaining matches against the same opponents if they are to secure a place in the promotion play-offs.

There is also the threat of a far worse outcome. A defeat to North Macedonia in Mount Florida would leave Pocognoli’s side facing the very real possibility of finishing last in the four-team section and dropping into League C.
