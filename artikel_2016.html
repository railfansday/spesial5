West Ham Must Use Souček Or Risk Losing Him

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Following the news that Tomáš Souček has the opportunity to leave West Ham for a Premier League club in January, I have to say that I’m becoming extremely worried about the direction of the football club.

Claret & Hugh spoke to one of Souček’s representatives earlier today, and whilst they made it clear that his preference was to help fire West Ham back into the Premier League, it’s clear that he is a man in demand.

One Premier League club in particular is understood to be surprised that Souček has not been playing more frequently for West Ham and, as a result, is happy to test the water to discover whether he’s surplus to requirements at the London Stadium.

In my opinion, this is not something West Ham should consider, not for a moment.

Even before this news, I was going to put forward my case for Souček being involved from the start against Stoke City.

He’s consistently posed a threat off the bench, not least during last night’s defeat at Portsmouth, and has scored three goals as a substitute this season.

In the absence of any sort of goalscoring form from our strikers, Souček would seem like one of the most likely sources of goals in a team which has gone from being free-scoring and among the top goalscorers in the division to one that struggles to do anything other than hit the woodwork.

A lot has been made about West Ham having Premier League players, but I’m not sure how many of them truly are. Souček, however, very firmly falls into that category.

He’s a European trophy winner, an international player, a proven Premier League goalscorer and more than capable of scoring goals in the Championship.

This is a player who signed a new West Ham contract only last month and whose commitment to the football club has never really been in question.

But what has just become abundantly clear is that if we don’t use him, we’re likely to lose him.

And I find it highly unlikely that West Ham will be able to bring in a better player than Tomáš Souček in the January transfer window.
