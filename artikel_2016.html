Sources: Crystal Palace's Thomas Frank appointment hits stumbling block

Crystal Palace have faced initial difficulties in completing the appointment of Thomas Frank, sources have told Football Insider.

After Pierre Sage's dismissal was officially announced on 12 November, plans to appoint his successor are being accelerated, with talks having taken place this week.

Sources say the former Tottenham and Brentford boss is still expected to be named as the next Crystal Palace manager, having now reached a verbal agreement with the club.

However, appointing his backroom staff is causing delays, with the Eagles currently working to negotiate deals with Frank's rather sizable team.

Crystal Palace are keen to provide Frank with their backing by bringing in a number of his most trusted allies, as they did with Sage following his arrival from Lens.

While the deal is not expected to face any major issues as a result, the ongoing negotiations explain the reason for the delays surrounding Frank's official appointment.

Football Insider revealed on 9 November that Sage would be sacked following a poor start to the season, with Frank already in talks to step straight in as his replacement.

The Eagles had hoped to move quickly to replace Sage, with the international break seen as the ideal opportunity for him to step in and spend time with the squad.

New talks were held with Frank following the 1-1 draw against Hoffenheim last Thursday, and have continued into this week, where sources say an agreement has been reached.

Other details, including finalising deals for his backroom staff, are being worked on in the background and his appointment will be announced when they are ironed out.

Upon his arrival at Tottenham, Frank brought in trustrated staff including Justin Cochrane, Chris Haslam and Joe Newton, all of whom also left Spurs following the boss' dismissal.

While sources have not yet confirmed, they are likely to now be targeted by the boss ahead of his appointment as the new Ctystal Palace manager.

Crystal Palace quickly identified Frank as the manager they wanted to lead them into a new chapter following Sage's exit, but was not the only name considered at Selhurst Park.

The likes of Sean Dyche, Rob Edwards and Vitor Pereira were also discussed behind the scenes, before they decided the former Brentford man was their best option.

However, former Man United coach Mick Brown warned Football Insider on 9 November that Frank may not be the right choice to save Palace from a relegation battle.

The 53-year-old was dismissed by Spurs in February after an awful spell in which he won just seven of his 26 Premier League games, leading to fears they would be relegated.

Questions have been asked about his managerial abilities as a result, and while Palace believe he will turn their fortunes around, some outside the club are having doubts.
