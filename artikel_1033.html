Liverpool 4-0 Man United PLAYER RATINGS: Who looked 'like a rabbit in headlights'? Who is 'playing 100 times better' than last season? And which 3/10 star 'is a shadow' of his former self?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

By LEWIS STEELE, FOOTBALL REPORTER and NATHAN SALT - FOOTBALL REPORTER

Published: 14:09 EST, 22 November 2026 | Updated: 14:11 EST, 22 November 2026

Mauricio Pochettino's Manchester United reign began in humiliating fashion as Liverpool put four past their bitter rivals at Anfield.

Pochettino's game-plan was in tatters inside 15 minutes when Leny Yoro was shown a straight red card and goals from Dominik Szoboszlai and Florian Wirtz took the game away from United.

Even after things settled down, following the introduction of Harry Maguire, United never looked capable of getting back in the game and eventually the worst defence in Europe cost them two more goals.

Rio Ngumoha, the best player on the pitch, converted after a blistering break from Bradley Barcola to make it 3-0.

Victor Munoz, sent on from the bench, added the cherry on top for Liverpool when he buried a fourth late on.
