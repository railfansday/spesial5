Elon Musk, who literally dubbed himself ‘Dark MAGA,’ says he isnt MAGA

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It's weird how much can change after an election!

Not just in the sense that a whole new party can suddenly be thrust into power, but also in how much those election results can affect major tech oligarchs who are heavily dependent on government contracts to suddenly start pretending the last few years never happened.

Musk appeared remotely on Wednesday at the New York Times DealBook Summit event, where he was interviewed by Andrew Ross Sorkin. Musk was attending the event remotely as he's currently in China with President Donald Trump for the APEC summit.

“I’m not MAGA,” Musk said during his conversation with Sorkin.

“Well, at least I’m not like — I’m not sure what MAGA means,” Musk continued. “If that means sort of blind adherence to a set of policies by the Republican Party, then I certainly don’t have that.”

Musk has become very open about his right-wing political beliefs in recent years, and the CEO of Tesla fully endorsed Donald Trump before the 2024 election. Musk also donated hundreds of millions of dollars to Trump's 2024 re-election campaign and joined Trump on the campaign trail. At one event at Madison Square Garden in October 2024, Musk declared himself to be "Dark MAGA" and sported a cap labeling him as such. Musk posted about being MAGA on his social media platform X. Musk then joined the Trump administration and ran DOGE to further the Republican Party's agenda.

Most recently, Musk donated a record $446 million to Republican candidates in the 2026 midterm election. Despite this, a Democratic "blue tsunami" swept across the country, winning the party both the House and the Senate back from Republicans.

“I feel pretty foolish about having spent so much money and not seen much for it. Or maybe it would have been much worse without that, ” Musk said on his midterm political spending. “But it certainly shows that, you know, American elections are not just about money.”

And despite constantly chiming in on X with far-right sentiments, Musk also declared at the event that his politics are moderate.

“I don’t think I have like, you know, any kind of extreme political views," Musk said.

Whether or not Musk has really changed his views can be up for debate. However, Musk is clearly trying to publicly change his MAGA tune after the Democratic sweep in the midterms. It just so happens that the now-ascendant Democratic Party will soon have the power to affect Musk's companies.
