Amazon knocks $70 off the price of the 2024 Kindle Paperwhite Signature Edition for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

SAVE $70: As of Nov. 20, the 2024 Kindle Paperwhite Signature Edition (32GB) is on sale for $179.99 in Amazon's Black Friday sale. That's $70 off its $249.99 list price.

Kindle deals have been a real highlight of Amazon's Black Friday sale. If you're in the market for a new e-reader, or looking to buy one as a gift for the holidays, the 2024 Kindle Paperwhite Signature Edition (32GB) is among the models on sale at the moment. As of Nov. 20, it's marked down to $179.99.

Compared to its full price of $249.99 (which it jumped to after a price hike earlier this year), this offer saves you $70. In addition to having double the storage space of the regular 2024 Kindle Paperwhite, the Signature Edition also features an auto-adjusting front light and wireless charging. They're similar in that they both have a 7-inch, glare-free Paperwhite display, a waterproof build, and up to 12 weeks of battery life on a single charge.

If your digital library is starting to overflow with books, this model is well worth picking up. Mashable's Samantha Mangino highlighted in her review that it "might be worth the splurge if you do have storage concerns," so why not jump on this Black Friday deal while it's still available?
