Micron's CEO security spending rose about 2,000% in the last fiscal year

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Micron's proxy statement attributes the increase to more comprehensive protection amid a heightened external threat environment

Jordan Vonderhaar / Bloomberg via Getty Images

Micron $MU Technology paid $764,748 for executive security for CEO Sanjay Mehrotra in fiscal 2026, the company said in its annual proxy statement. The fiscal year ended Sept. 3.

The company covered $35,636 in personal security arrangements for Mehrotra the year before, according to MarketWatch, which put the increase at about 2,000%.

The security cost was part of $1,865,915 in "all other compensation" for Mehrotra in fiscal 2026, up from $238,942 in fiscal 2025. His total compensation was $39,723,944, compared with $30,940,146 a year earlier.

Micron attributed the rise to more comprehensive personal security provisions, implemented in light of a continued heightened external threat environment. The fiscal 2026 security program included residential and personal security for Mehrotra.

Micron's Compensation Committee evaluated the need to respond to specific incidents and threats and engaged an independent security firm to study the company's security needs. The committee also set a policy to review security-related expenses quarterly.

Micron said it does not consider the arrangements a personal benefit to Mehrotra. It described them as a business expense that relates to the nature of his role, is necessary for his job performance and mitigates risks to the company. The company reported the costs as compensation in line with SEC guidance.

Micron also paid $75,175 for residential security for former Chief Business Officer Sumit Sadana, who served in that role through August 2026 and now serves as senior adviser to the CEO. The company said it previously paid for his security arrangements.

Micron began distributing its proxy materials on Nov. 24. Shareholders will vote on an advisory resolution on executive compensation at the company's annual meeting, which is scheduled for Jan. 14, 2027, and will be held virtually. Micron's board recommends that shareholders vote for the resolution.
