Walmart is doubling down on its fast-food delivery bet

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Walmart's delivery marketplace has grown to over 2,700 quick-service restaurants, with Papa John's among its partners

Walmart $WMT is expanding its restaurant delivery service to capture more takeout orders, with Papa John's $PZZA among the chains on board, executives said on Walmart's earnings call on Thursday.

Walmart reported third-quarter results on Thursday, including a 23% increase in Walmart U.S. eCommerce sales. Executives said the company's delivery marketplace now lists more than 2,700 quick-service restaurants, a number that has climbed over the past several months. The partners include Subway, Papa John's and Dunkin'.

John Furner, Walmart's president and CEO, told investors that customers can order restaurant food, a marketplace item, groceries, pharmacy products and fashion in a few clicks in the app. He said Walmart's e-commerce network can get orders to 96% of U.S. households within three hours, and to 60% within 30 minutes. Upwards of 80% of online orders placed in the U.S. are filled by stores, he said.

David Guggina, president and CEO of Walmart U.S., described restaurant delivery as an extension of the company's omnichannel strategy. He said customers and restaurant partners have both shown strong interest.

"It helps us capture more meal occasions, which then drives traffic as well as increased frequency to our site and to our app," Guggina said.

Walmart said about 90% of spending at fast-food chains takes place within a few miles of one of its stores, according to MarketWatch.

Walmart announced in September that customers would be able to order Dunkin' through its app and Walmart.com for delivery alongside groceries. The rollout was set to begin at 150 Dunkin' locations inside Walmart stores before expanding to most of the chain's roughly 10,000 U.S. locations outside them. Dunkin' orders are fulfilled through Walmart Restaurant Delivery, a platform that debuted with Subway as its first restaurant partner.

In a blog post at the time, Greg Cathey, a Walmart senior vice president, said roughly 65% of restaurant orders placed since the Subway launch were bundled with other Walmart purchases. One in five orders combining Subway and Walmart products was a customer's first use of Express Delivery, he wrote, and nearly 30% of those customers returned within 30 days.
