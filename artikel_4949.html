Christian Yelich’s Malibu Retreat Is on the Market for $30 Million

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

As Milwaukee Brewers captain Christian Yelich rides the high of the baseball team’s first World Series championship in history, the left fielder and designated hitter is angling for another win: the sale of his Malibu mansion, currently on the market for $30 million.

Six years ago, Yelich paid $5.55 million for the one-acre property, which came with architectural plans by Los Angeles–based firm KOVAC Design Studio. Yelich has decided to move his family to Wisconsin, a KOVAC representative told AD. It’s unclear if he ever actually lived in the 5,300-square-foot residence, considering it was just completed this year.

Overlooking the Pacific Coast Highway and the ocean beyond, the modern four-bedroom, six-bathroom home is lined with walls of glass that make the most of the sweeping water views and slide open to unite the indoor and outdoor spaces. Sprawling patios are connected to most of the rooms, with the main level hosting a particularly spacious deck with an infinity pool, an outdoor shower, a bathroom, and plenty of space to lounge or entertain, including multiple fire pits, a dining area, and an outdoor kitchen.

Upon entrance, guests are greeted by a sun-drenched, double-height foyer with a floating staircase leading to the upper floor bedrooms—uniquely, each one has its own refrigerator tucked in the closet. Deeper into the dwelling, a living area boasts a fireplace and a wet bar. Nearby, the blonde wood and white marble kitchen boasts a long center island with seating for five. Another highlight is the home theater, complete with a concession room, tiered recliner seating, an LED video wall, professional-grade acoustics, and surround sound.
