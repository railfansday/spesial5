If you’ve been waiting to finally try a smart ring fitness tracker, this might be the best moment yet. Oura just announced its first sale ever on the new Oura Ring 5, and this Black Friday deal is a good one: From November 19 through November 30, every Oura Ring 5 style is $100 off.

That means saving up to 25 percent right now, depending on the ring’s finish. Gold, deep rose, stealth, and brushed silver styles are all marked down to $399, which is 20 percent off. Silver and black styles are now $299, giving you the biggest savings at 25 percent off. And if you shop directly through ouraring.com, there’s the additional perk of free shipping through November 30.

So, what makes the Oura Ring worth considering? Unlike a smartwatch, the Oura Ring is screen-free and designed to look more like jewelry than a fitness tracker. In her Oura Ring 5 review, WH associate editor Olivia Luppino calls it the only fitness tracker she has not wanted to take off.

It monitors sleep, activity, heart rate, temperature trends, and cycle insights, then translates that data into scores and trends in the beginner-friendly Oura app. You can use it to check your sleep quality, track recovery, monitor stress, and better understand how your habits affect your overall fitness and health.

The Oura Ring 5 builds on the Oura Ring 4, which is still a popular pick for people who want health-tracking data without wearing a bulky device on their wrist. The Ring 4 tracks many of the same key wellness metrics, including sleep, activity, readiness, heart rate, and menstrual cycle data. It also introduced a sleeker design and improved sensing compared with earlier models, making it a strong option if you’re not ready to splurge on the newest version.

One more thing to know: Oura Ring is covered and reimbursable through FSA and HSA plans, meaning you might be able to use pre-tax dollars for the purchase. Just be sure to use the sizing kit for the model you’re going with, as each one has slightly different sizing.

Whether you’re buying it as a wellness gift or treating yourself, this is the biggest Oura Ring 5 discount yet, and it’s only around for a short time.
