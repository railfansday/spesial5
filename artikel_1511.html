Gasperini asks Roma to focus on acquiring two wingers

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Roma is on the attack, both on the pitch and in the transfer market.

Gian Piero Gasperini has led the team to score an impressive 31 goals in just 11 league games, a record for both the club and the coach.

Away from the pitch, the Grugliasco general’s pressure on the club remains relentless: there’s a desire to raise the level of the squad to maintain a high level of competitiveness, and to do so, they’ll need to make the right decisions in the upcoming transfer campaign.

Gian Piero Gasperini, as reported by Il Messaggero, expects two signings up front and is willing to forgo Wesley, as he did in the summer, blocking the sale of Rensch, who ultimately remained as a backup on the flank.

The Roma coach is willing to keep the four-man winger lineup, also thanks to Lulli’s growth. The 2007-born player has already played 546 minutes, scoring two goals and one assist.
