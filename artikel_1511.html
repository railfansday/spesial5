GdS: Loftus-Cheek could leave Milan in January with renewal talks silent

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

It seems Ruben Loftus-Cheek could leave AC Milan in January, with the club hoping to invest heavily in the squad and raise the base level.

Loftus-Cheek’s Milan career is a frustrating one. He started off with a fantastic season in Serie A, but the same injury issues that had haunted him in England were present. Unfortunately, that’s the tale of his career in Italy, too.

His talent is very clearly there, but we’ve never had too much of a chance to see it on a consistent basis. It’s an unfortunate reality and this is likely to be the final full year of it.

According to Gazzetta dello Sport, as Francesco Nasato relays on X, the Rossoneri could part ways with Loftus-Cheek in the new year. There are currently no renewal talks happening, so he’ll be able to leave as a free agent in the summer.

Pre-contract discussions can begin, alternatively, someone may be a small fee to bring him to the club six months early. Either way, it seems likely that he leaves the Diavolo in 2027.

He’s currently sidelined, as the report continues, and hopes to return in late November if not early December, so time is running out for him to earn a renewal with the club. At this stage, though, it’s probably too high a mountain to climb.

An unfortunate position for everyone to be in, considering how impressive his first season was.
