Rennes: Estéban Lepaul close to a return

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Good news for Stade Rennais, which could soon find Estéban Lepaul. Away from the field since October 18 due to an internal sprain of the left ankle contracted against Paris FC, the French striker is getting closer to a return. According to West Francethe Rennes striker should return to training this Thursday 5 or Friday 6 November, after more than two weeks of absence. The player notably missed the matches against Juventus (2-2) and Olympiakos (0-0) in the Europa League, as well as the matches against Strasbourg (3-0) and Nice (2-5) in Ligue 1. A significant absence for the Breton club, since Lepaul had scored seven goals in the league before his injury. Despite their desire to return quickly, the Rennes staff preferred to take precautions to avoid any relapse.

If his return to training is becoming clearer, his participation in the Lille reception on Sunday (3 p.m.) remains very uncertain. The time frame seems too short to consider tenure, or even a presence in the group. The most likely scenario would therefore be to take advantage of the international break to gradually regain rhythm and prepare for his return to competition. This injury could also compromise his chances of returning to the French team. While Zinédine Zidane must unveil his second list at the head of the Blues this Friday, November 6, Lepaul seems unlikely to be called up after several weeks without playing. A possible disappointment for the Rennes striker, who will probably have to wait before hoping for a new opportunity in the selection.
