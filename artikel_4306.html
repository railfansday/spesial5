The Legend Of Zelda: Ocarina Of Time Remake Is The Switch 2's Fastest Selling Game In Under A Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Scott Baird is a Contributor from the UK with over a decade of video game writing experience.

Nintendo has announced that The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake has become the fastest Switch 2 game in under a week, easily beating Donkey Kong Bananza and Pokémon Pokopia.

The Legend of Zelda fans have been begging Nintendo for a full remake of Ocarina of Time, one of the series’ best entries. While the N64 version of Ocarina of Time is a classic, it has frustrating design elements, lacks quality-of-life features, and is shackled to one of the worst controllers in video game history.

The remake's dev team included younger folks, several of whom never played the original Ocarina of Time, who provided a much-needed modern view of things.

In November 2026, Nintendo finally gave the retro fans what they wanted, with a modern remake of Ocarina of Time for the Switch 2. Fans turned up in droves, as Nintendo announced that the Ocarina of Time remake became the fastest-selling game on the console in under a single week.

According to Nintendo, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake sold 5.2 million units on both physical and digital formats within its first four days of release, which is the first time any game on the system has hit that number in that time frame.

It’s no surprise that The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake is a huge hit, considering the incredible critical reception it received. The game is currently the most highly-reviewed game of the year, with a Metascore of 96, and only Grand Theft Auto 6 has a chance of beating it at this rate.

Thomas Mahler believes Nintendo missed an opportunity to improve the level design in the Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake.

While the Ocarina of Time remake’s numbers are impressive, it bears mentioning that it’s still not the best-selling game on the Switch 2. That honor goes to Mario Kart World, which currently sits at 16.48 million copies sold. The Mario Kart franchise has been Nintendo’s MVP for the past few console generations, and since Mario Kart World was a launch title and part of a bundle, it’ll be hard to displace.

That being said, it’s not like the Nintendo Switch 2 has released many games that would be competition to a new Mario Kart game. There’s still no 3D Mario, mainline Pokémon games, Legend of Zelda game that isn’t a remake, Super Smash Bros. sequel, or, crucially, a new Animal Crossing. The fact that Mario Kart World had a lukewarm reception means that the Ocarina of Time remake can absolutely outsell it.

The big question now is how many consoles The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time shipped. Right now, Nintendo needs to get the Switch 2 into more hands, and a remake of one of its best games may have just done that. Fans likely won’t know the hard numbers until Nintendo’s next financial review, unless the company makes a big announcement over Christmas.

With Nintendo planning to continue remaking past games, these games should be considered for future remakes.

Now that Nintendo has broken the glass on the Ocarina of Time remake, the next question is whether a Majora’s Mask remake is next in line. This would make a ton of sense, given how closely the original games reused assets, but it would be wise for Nintendo to hold off for now and release something new. Fans already got a Star Fox N64 remake, and the Switch 2 doesn’t need a reputation for being a rehash machine.

The Ocarina of Time remake is closing out the Switch 2’s 2026 on a high, especially after a slow drip feed of exclusives, and the reveal that The Duskbloods has been pushed to next year. 2027 will have Metroid Ravenous and Pokémon Winds & Waves, so it seems that the Switch 2 might finally be hitting its stride and giving people a reason to shell out money for a new console.

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is one of the most beloved games of all time, but these remakes might be just as meaningful to fans.
