GTA 6 vs GTA 5 comparisons: See how the visuals stack up

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Grand Theft Auto VI is one of the most detailed games I've ever seen. We've waited 13 years, and I see 13 years of effort in the end result.

You can shoot basketball, play pool, enjoy video games, and so much more that is actually related to missions and the story itself.

But 2013's Grand Theft Auto V was a good-looking, immersive game for its time as well. So we took some time to compare how the games react in similar situations.

What happens when you shoot a window? What happens if you beat up a car? Basically, we tested some of the little things you can do in GTA 5 and GTA 6 between missions out of sheer curiosity.

When attempting to shoot car windows in both games, windshields tested did not shatter completely in GTA 6, while the damage appears slightly more detailed in GTA 5. Both games had car windows shatter in similarly reactive ways.

With building windows and glass doors, GTA 5 actually performed better, shattering and breaking apart when shot. In GTA 6, the windows did not shatter when shot, despite throwing an explosion of glass shards where the bullets struck.

Beating up cars reacted similarly in both games, though I'd say GTA 6 veers much closer to realism and gives the impression of weight being thrown into the strikes.

And if you're just trying to take in the vibes rather than smashing things, you can go to the water's edge and watch the sunset. This is just my opinion, but GTA 6's sunset is gorgeous to watch and is absolutely a vibe.

But you don't have to take my word for it; you can see the side-by-side comparisons for yourself.
