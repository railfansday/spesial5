1. On Sunday, South African authorities announced the results from Nov. 4 local elections. Why was the election a historic one for the African National Congress party?

The party won a majority in every province except one.

It marked the party’s worst-ever results.

The party won a majority in the Western Cape province for the first time.

Two-thirds of the party’s election winners were women.

No party won an outright majority, a further sign that South Africa’s political landscape is fragmenting, FP’s Nosmot Gbadamosi reports in Africa Brief.

2. This year’s United Nations Climate Change Conference kicked off on Monday in Antalya, Turkey. How many people are part of the official delegation from the United States, the world’s biggest cumulative carbon emitter?

U.S. President Donald Trump has withdrawn the country from the Paris Agreement at the start of both of his presidential terms. He has also tried to intimidate countries that want to take action on the climate, Noah Gordon wrote in January.

3. Which former leader requested new identity papers from their home country on Monday?

Hasina has been living in exile in India since fleeing Bangladesh in the wake of mass protests in 2024, FP’s Michael Kugelman reports in South Asia Brief.

4. French sports star Teddy Riner announced on Monday that he was joining the country’s presidential race. In what sport did Riner compete?

The three-time Olympic gold medalist expressed concern about the rise of the country’s far right, which has broken through the French political establishment’s long-standing firewall, Robert Zaretsky wrote in March.

5. Israel’s Knesset swore in its new class of lawmakers on Tuesday, but they still haven’t formed a government. Who was tasked this week with leading coalition-building negotiations?

Incumbent Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu

Eisenkot’s clearest path to power is joining forces with the country’s Arab parties—something that has only happened once in Israel’s history, FP’s Steven A. Cook writes.

6. Pope Leo XIV was greeted by massive crowds during a visit to Peru that began on Wednesday. According to estimates from local authorities, roughly how many people took to the streets in the capital of Lima to see him?

Leo became a naturalized Peruvian citizen after serving as a missionary in the country. His 12-day South America trip is the longest yet of his pontificate, with two earlier stops in Uruguay and Argentina, FP’s Catherine Osborn reports in Latin America Brief.

7. On Thursday, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned just days after admitting to having an affair with Alice Huang, a woman believed by U.S. officials to be a Chinese spy. Alice Huang was formerly an executive at a data center company called what?

Nvidia stock has plummeted this week in the wake of the scandal, which could have implications for the wider artificial intelligence industry, FP’s Rishi Iyengar writes.

8. This week’s resignations don’t end there. Following months of scrutiny, what prompted Indonesian Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka to submit his resignation to parliament on Thursday?

He was unable to prove that he graduated high school.

He also had an affair with an alleged Chinese spy.

He was implicated in a major vote-buying scandal.

He faced allegations of spying for Russia.

The move throws a wrench in the tenuous truce between the government and Gibran’s father, former President Joko Widodo, FP’s Alexandra Sharp reports in World Brief.

9. Also on Thursday, Russia agreed to allow which actor to participate in the next round of peace talks for the war in Ukraine?

Officials from the International Atomic Energy Agency

From bomb threats to drone incursions, hybrid threats from suspected Russian operatives have stoked fear in Europe in recent months, Beniamino Irdi writes.

10. Which embassy unexpectedly received attention around the world on social media this week after briefly closing on Tuesday for a staff event?

The Finnish Embassy in Canberra, Australia

Thousands of the embassy’s adoring fans shared jokes and commiserated over the closure—which prompted the embassy to celebrate its reopening the next day with a ribbon-cutting ceremony, the Guardian reports.
