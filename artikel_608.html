Edinburgh-Winning ‘Skintown’ Director Kieron J. Walsh Signs With Artists First (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Kieron J. Walsh, the Irish writer and director whose latest feature “Skintown” won the top prize at this year’s Edinburgh Film Festival, has been signed by Artists First, a Propagate Content company.

Set against the backdrop of the 1994 IRA ceasefire, the drug-fueled and music-soaked “Skintown” follows two best friends as they’re about to jump start their futures with the most reckless trip of their lives. In its review, Variety described it as a “more polite brother to the ‘Kneecap’ boys and a more cautious cousin to those ‘Derry Girls,” adding that it was a “breezy, loved-up, drugged-up guide to getting into trouble in the Troubles.”

The film — which Walsh directed and exec produced — had it world premiere in Edinburgh, where it won the Sean Connery Prize for Feature Filmmaking Excellence.

Based on the novel of the same name by Ciarán McMenamin, who also penned the screenplay, “Skintown” has since screened at TIFF and won the audience award at the Dinard Film Festival. It was recently acquired for U.K. and Ireland distribution by Conic and Break Out Pictures.

Prior to “Skintown,” Walsh co-wrote and directed “Jump,” adapted from Lisa McGee’s stage play. The film screened at TIFF, won best feature film at the New York Irish Film Festival, the Bridging the Borders Award at the Palm Springs International Film Festival and was nominated for best screenplay at the IFTAs.

For television, Walsh most recently directed “The Hardacres” for Channel 5 and episodes of “Inside No. 9” for the BBC. His other directing credits include “Finding Joy, “Rapt,” “Anjelica Huston on James Joyce: A Shout in the Street” for BBC Four, and “The Psychopath Next Door,” starring Anna Friel, for ITV Studios and Sky Living. He has also directed commercials for brands including Guinness, Bass, Lotto, Kellogg’s and Nissan.

Walsh continues to be represented by Independent Talent Group.
