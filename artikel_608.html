Will Ferrell’s Netflix Golf Comedy ‘The Hawk’ Canceled After One Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Will Ferrell is not going to be thankful for this Wednesday-before-Thanksgiving bad-news dump. Ferrell’s out-of-bounds golf comedy The Hawk has been canceled by Netflix after just one season, The Hollywood Reporter has learned.

Ferrell and Netflix are not done with each other, however: Ferrell is executive producing new Netflix series All the Rage, Prep, and Nikki Glaser’s first (unnamed) scripted series. (Part of her Netflix Is a Joke Studios deal.) Ferrell produces for his Gloria Sanchez banner.

The problem with The Hawk was, well, it was (very) subpar. The Hawk, co-created by Ferrell and Harper Steele, followed disgraced pro golfer Lonnie Hawkins (Ferrell) who seeks to regain his former glory. In his review, THR TV critic Daniel Fienberg called The Hawk “disposable,” writing that, “In general, The Hawk is a show that consistently operates at a barely minimal level, starting with the credits song, a slice of ’80s anthemic rock that contains the lyrics, ‘They call him the Hawk/ He’s a golfer/ The greatest golfer in the world.’ Wait. Sorry. The song doesn’t contain those lyrics — those are the only lyrics. It’s a piece of cheeky minimalism and a promise delivered.”

“There will be countless worse shows this year, but it’s doubtful that 2026 will offer a show as hollow and forgettable as The Hawk,” Fienberg scathed.

In addition to Ferrell and Steele, Jessica Elbaum, Alix Taylor, Rian Johnson, Ram Bergman, Nena Rodrigue, Chris Henchy, David Gordon Green and Andrew Guest also executive produced The Hawk.

The news comes as no surprise to the series’ creators. Earlier this month, Steele appeared on Late Night With Seth Meyers, calling The Hawk “short-lived” but also “probably the best show ever.”

The Hawk premiered on Netflix on July 16.

Deadline first reported the news of The Hawk‘s cancellation.
