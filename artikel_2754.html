Cameron Boozer grabs top spot in NBA rookie rankings as Grizzlies surge

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The NBA Rookie of the Year race is beginning to take shape after the first month of the 2026-27 season, though no player has managed to separate himself from the pack. The top two picks in this year's draft remain on the periphery of the conversation, while a trio of less-heralded contenders has emerged at the front.

Yaxel Lendeborg of the Golden State Warriors, Memphis Grizzlies forward Cameron Boozer and Sacramento Kings guard Darius Acuff, Jr. have been the early standouts, with Lendeborg and Boozer both playing key roles for winning teams. AJ Dybantsa and Darryn Peterson lurk closely behind after slower starts to the campaign, and there's a collection of other rookies contributing in significant ways with many teams emphasizing depth to combat the NBA's injury issues.

Indeed, as of Nov. 19, there are 20 rookies averaging at least 15 minutes per game.

Here's how the top NBA rookies stack up after the first month of the 2026-27 regular season.

Stats: 16.3 points, 6.6 rebounds, 2.3 assists

Dybantsa emerged from a three-game slump to produce the two best all-around performances of his young NBA career, including his first double-double (23 points, 12 rebounds) in a win over the Toronto Raptors on Wednesday that snapped an eight-game losing streak for the Wizards. He continues to struggle shooting from outside and is hitting just 21.3% of his 3-pointers as of Nov. 19. But his elite athleticism and ability to get to the rim have still allowed to make an early impact in Washington.

Stats: 16.5 points, 4.1 rebounds, 3.6 assists

Peterson set two marks that bode well for his career this week. When he scored a career-best 28 points in a Nov. 13 loss to the Houston Rockets, he became the second-youngest player in NBA history behind only Anthony Edwards to record five-straight games with 20 or more points. After Utah's stunning win over the Lakers on Wednesday, Peterson had 34 3-pointers and 29 stocks (steals + blocks) in his first 15 NBA games. The only other player to do that through his first 15 career games is Allen Iverson, according to the Jazz.

Stats: 26.2 points, 3.6 rebounds, 7 assists

The Kings haven't translated Acuff's prolific scoring into wins yet, but he keeps scoring at rates rarely seen by an NBA rookie. He had his fifth game with 30 or more points this season on Wednesday when scored 36 points on 50% shooting in a blowout loss to the Phoenix Suns. One of the few bright spots in another miserable Kings' season is still on pace to average the most points by an NBA rookie since Michael Jordan.

Stats: 20.3 points, 7.3 rebounds, 3.5 assists

Lendeborg endured the first rough patch of his otherwise stellar rookie season this past week with defenses keying on him while Stephen Curry remains sidelined by injury. Lendeborg shot less than 30% from the field, including 2-for-14 from 3-point range, as Golden State suffered three consecutive blowout losses. He nevertheless continues to lead all rookies in player efficiency rating while carrying a star-level offensive burden for a veteran-laden playoff contender. Few first-year players are asked to shoulder that kind of responsibility.

Stats: 16.6 points, 9.4 rebounds, 5.3 assists

Boozer vaults to No. 1 in the rookie rankings for the first time season after leading the surprising Grizzlies to four-straight wins, including a Nov. 16 win over the Washington Wizards in which he set career-highs with 29 points and seven 3-pointers matched up against Anthony Davis. Boozer ranks among the top three rookies in rebounds, assists, NET rating, plus/minus and player efficiency rating thus far, and his shooting numbers are beginning to normalize after a slow start from the field. No player in this rookie class has impacted winning more than Boozer through the season's first month.

Also under consideration: Caleb Wilson, Chicago Bulls; Aday Mara, Oklahoma City Thunder; Meleek Thomas, Cleveland Cavaliers; Keaton Wagler, Los Angeles Clippers; Brayden Burries, Milwaukee Bucks
