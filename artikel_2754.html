Christian McCaffrey outgained 4 different NFL teams in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

On Sunday, the San Francisco 49ers beat the Dallas Cowboys in a shootout, 46-40. A large part of their offensive firepower came from Christian McCaffrey.

McCaffrey was exceptional in the 49ers' win, hauling in four receptions for 161 yards and two touchdowns while adding 61 rushing yards and a touchdown on the ground. And with 222 total scrimmage yards, he managed to put up more total yardage than four different NFL teams in Week 10 – the Cardinals, the Packers, the Patriots, and the Raiders.

McCaffrey also scored the touchdown that helped put the game away for San Francisco – with the 49ers holding just a six-point lead late in the fourth quarter, McCaffrey hauled in a reception across the middle of the field and took it 68 yards for the touchdown to extend the lead to 46-32. And while Dallas scored again, they didn't have enough time to mount a comeback.

McCaffrey currently has 519 rushing yards, 10 rushing touchdowns, 431 receiving yards, and 3 receiving touchdowns this season, and is a crucial part of an offense that looks like the best in the NFC this year, after the 49ers moved to 8-1with the win.
