NFL Panic Meter: Should Steelers be worried after embarrassing blowout loss?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

One bad game shouldn't eliminate eight games of solid football.

But that was a seriously bad game for the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Get out-gained 337-40 in a half and there will be some reasons for concern. The Steelers were absolutely blown out by the Cincinnati Bengals, and it didn't help that it happened on a big Sunday night stage. The Steelers were 6-2 going into the game and while there was skepticism over their staying power, nobody expected them to get destroyed like that.

The Steelers don't seem too worried about it ruining their season. Aaron Rodgers handled a question about whether the loss would change the trajectory of the season with, "That's very dramatic," and head coach Mike McCarthy took a similar tack.

"It's one loss and it will be dealt with as such," McCarthy said, via the team's site. "I'm more focused on the details of the fundamentals and the things we needed to do. We're not going to drive down the drama road. It's a waste of time. It's a waste of energy. We're going to pour our energy into ourselves, look in the mirror and stay focused on the things that we can control."

Cincinnati Bengals inside linebacker Oren Burks brings down Pittsburgh Steelers running back Jaylen Warren.

That's a fine approach, because there's nothing else McCarthy can say. But the Steelers have some real concerns. They came into the season tied for the oldest roster in the NFL, with many older veterans starting with 42-year-old quarterback Aaron Rodgers. It's a long season and the Steelers have to worry about injury and fatigue setting in. The Steelers have gotten very little out of their receivers this season, which limits the explosiveness of the offense, and while the defense has been good it was picked apart by Joe Burrow.

But it's not time to get too worried. The Steelers had shown significant improvement before having a horrendous night in Cincinnati. That isn't all erased, nor are six wins in what is becoming a very soft AFC wild-card race. Rodgers was good enough with the limitations at receiver through eight games and while Sunday night started with him throwing incomplete on his first seven passes for the first time in his career, there haven't been signs of a steep decline before Sunday night. It could happen in the second half of the season but there's no clear and imminent danger of it.

Good teams can have bad games. The Bengals are hot. It happens. As McCarthy said, there's no reason to go down a drama road. The Steelers should be fine. But that's a little less certain after a truly miserable night in Cincinnati.

Panic meter: Let's not give up on the Steelers yet, but remember Sunday night as a turning point if things do go south.

When Drake Maye was sacked by Aidan Hutchinson, Patriots and Lions fans had no idea both teams' seasons were changing on that play.

The Patriots are concerned that Maye fractured his ankle, and we'll get to Hutchinson in a bit. It appears Maye is either going to miss significant time or the rest of the season. That's terrible news for the 5-5 Patriots less than a week after the trade deadline passed. Jimmy Garoppolo's name has been thrown around but realistically, it's hard to find any quarterback who is still unsigned in mid-November to save the season.

Former Patriots quarterback Matt Cassel, who is 44 years old, had some jokes.

It might not be that much worse than Tommy DeVito, who was awful in relief of Maye. He had 81 yards on 24 attempts. The Patriots had 91 yards on 11 possessions after Maye's injury. It was unwatchable football. And that's likely to be the case for the rest of the season.

Panic meter: Hey Patriots fans, at least the Celtics and Bruins are good.

Aidan Hutchinson was excited for a third-down sack on Drake Maye, so he jumped up and the landing might have ruined the Lions' Super Bowl hopes.

Hutchinson tore his ACL on the celebration, a brutal injury for Hutchinson and Detroit as well. Detroit was already without standout linebacker Jack Campbell. Hutchinson's knee injury removes the two best players from a defense that wasn't very good with them.

The Lions are 7-2 and Detroit fans will point to 2024, when the team went 15-2 after Hutchinson broke his leg. Perhaps they can get on a roll while being shorthanded. But realistically and unfortunately, the ceiling for the Lions was lowered with the injury.

Panic meter: The Lions shouldn't fall apart, but that celebration was a turning point in their season.

Everyone will have issues with the Vikings' blitz-happy defense this season. The Packers certainly have. They allowed eight sacks on Sunday and 12 in two meetings against the Vikings. While that's certainly too many, Green Bay has allowed only 13 sacks in their other eight games.

The problem is that the next opponent, after the bye, is Myles Garrett, Aaron Donald and the Rams. There's a lot of work to be done before that game the day before Thanksgiving.

The Packers lost 30-10 to the Vikings and they obviously haven't matched up well with Minnesota this season. But Green Bay was playing very well before Sunday's game and are still in fine shape in the NFC wild-card race.

Panic meter: As long as Brian Flores isn't on the other sideline, Green Bay should be fine.

The Texans' season can be viewed in an optimistic way, if you wish. Five of their losses this season have come on a drive by the opponent in the final minutes of regulation or in overtime. Close games like that should be close to 50/50 propositions, so with just a little more luck Houston would be right in the playoff picture.

But maybe it's not just luck. The Texans' defense, which was supposed to be good this season, has failed repeatedly when asked to close out a game. And regardless of how the Texans got to 3-6, that's where they are. And they have lost a key tiebreaker to the Browns, who are currently the No. 7 seed in the AFC.

Houston's playoff odds are 34.4% via DVOA, which seems high for a team three games under .500. That's because the AFC's wild-card race isn't very good. But, maybe the Texans aren't very good either.

Panic meter: The talent is still there, but the season is slipping away fast with bad losses.
