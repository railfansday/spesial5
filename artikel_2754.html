Lakers 'optimistic' Austin Reaves will play this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Lakers coach JJ Redick remains "optimistic" about Austin Reaves playing against the San Antonio Spurs on the road Friday.

Reaves missed his fifth game as the Lakers hosted the Utah Jazz on Monday, Nov. 23.

"Today was a big step in terms of getting a stay-ready game in," Redick told reporters on Monday before the Jazz game. "He played great, moved great, so we are optimistic for the rest of this week."

The Lakers had a successful start to the month with key contributions from the shooting guard. Reaves landed awkwardly on his left heel after missing a transition dunk attempt in the Lakers' eventual 123-112 victory over the Milwaukee Bucks on Nov. 11.

Reaves' return would be welcome, especially against the Spurs, who won the Western Conference last season before falling to the New York Knicks in the NBA Finals.

Reaves avoided serious injury and was listed as day-to-day by Redick.

Reaves and the Lakers' medical staff decided against having imaging done to evaluate the injury further.

Quentin Grimes has taken on more responsibility when the Lakers have been without a star player in the lineup.

"I'm not set in stone on anything," Redick recently told reporters about the possibility of fitting Grimes in as a starter. "The reality is that Quentin has been a starter; either A.R. or Luka has been off, so naturally he will assume some of that usage."

Grimes scored a game-high 47 points on 16-of-24 shooting from the field against the Charlotte Hornets on Nov. 18.

Reaves has started all 12 of the games he's played in for the Lakers this season. The guard is averaging 24.7 points, 3.3 rebounds, 4.7 assists and 1.2 steals per game, while shooting 51.3% from the field and 40.9% from beyond the arc.
