Eagles say Jaguars made an offer they couldn't refuse for Jordan Davis

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Eagles announced the trade that sent defensive tackle Jordan Davis to the Jaguars, saying the “Jaguars made an offer that executive vice president/General Manager Howie Roseman simply could not refuse.”

The Jaguars are sending a 2027 first-round pick and a 2029 second-round pick to the Eagles.

“We are extremely grateful to Jordan for his contributions to our team on the field and in the Philadelphia community since we drafted him five years ago,” the team said in a statement. “He has been a key contributor to our team culture and on-field success, which included two Super Bowl appearances and a world championship in 2024. His growth and development as both a player and a leader are a testament to his hard work and character. We wish Jordan nothing but continued success.”

The Eagles now have first-round picks in the 2027 draft, two first-round picks and two second-round picks in the 2028 draft and multiple second-round picks in 2029.

The Eagles made Davis a first-round pick in 2022, and he totaled 193 tackles, 9.5 sacks, 15 quarterback hits and 11 passes defensed in 73 games.
