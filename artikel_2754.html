Duke hits new low in 37 years with blowout loss to Illinois

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

History has the receipts proving Duke is a basketball powerhouse. However, nobody is completely invincible.

On Tuesday, No. 5 Duke reluctantly welcomed No. 10 Illinois to the famed Cameron Indoor Stadium in Durham, North Carolina. The Illini defeated the Blue Devils 99-78. It was not only a blowout loss, but also the first of its kind in a long time for Duke, per Joe Tipton of On3Sports.com.

The last time the Blue Devils lost by more than 20 points at home was in 1989, 37 years ago, to be exact. Additionally, the Blue Devils ' 35-game winning streak at home came to an end.

Illinois came ready to play. A major factor was Andrej Stojakovic scoring a record 46 points, the most points scored by an opponent at Cameron Indoor Stadium since Grady Wallace scored 43 points for South Carolina in 1957. Despite their size and height, Duke was outrebounded by Illinois in a 54-34 win. The Illini also grabbed 18 offensive rebounds and scored 19 second-chance points.

Plus, Illinois won the battle in the paint, 48-30. They also put up 22 points off 14 Blue Devils turnovers. The Blue Devils shot 24-for-64 from the field (37.5%) and 9-for-40 from 3 (22.5%). They were also only 21-for-33 from the field and missed 12 free throws.

For Duke, freshman center Joaquim Boumtje-Boumtje led the way with 17 points and five rebounds. Senior guard Caleb Foster added 15 points and six rebounds. Second-year guard/forward Sebastian Wilkins added 10 points.

The Blue Devils are now 3-2. They enter the season trying to shake off an Elite Eight exit to UConn after being the No. 1 seed. On Saturday, the Blue Devils will play against Utah in Pittsburgh.
