Colts get 2 starters back in lineup for Week 12 game v. Giants

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Indianapolis Colts will get two starters back for Sunday's game against the New York Giants.

On the team's final injury report, CB Sauce Gardner and OT Bernhard Raimann are without injury designations and are good to go for this week's matchup.

Gardner suffered a calf injury in Week 10 and then he did not play last week against Houston.

The calf injury that Gardner is now working through is the same one he's injured on two other occasions -- once earlier this season when he was forced to miss a game and originally last season, when he missed multiple games.

This season, Gardner is allowing a completion rate of just 55% when targeted, but he's surrendered some big plays, allowing 18.8 yards per reception. He has five pass breakups as well.

Raimann also missed last week's game after suffering a concussion in Week 10.

This season, Raimann has allowed three sacks and 20 QB pressures over 356 pass-blocking snaps. He has a run-blocking grade of 69.5.
