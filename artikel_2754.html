NBA rumors: Insider teases Nuggets making another run at Clippers trade target

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

NBA rumors: Insider teases Nuggets making another run at Clippers trade target appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Denver Nuggets are reportedly active in the trade market and could make a deal with the LA Clippers ahead of this year's deadline. The Nuggets reportedly expressed interest in Kevin Porter Jr., while trying to find a suitor for Zeke Nnaji, according to The Stein Line's Jake Fischer.

Between Nnaji's contract and a future first-round pick, the Nuggets are openly looking to land a deal to help bolster their roster, per Fischer.

"The Nuggets continue to try to find a workable trade featuring Nnaji's contract, which is valued at $7.5 million this season on top of a player option for the same amount in 2027-28," Fischer reported. "Denver's most coveted trade asset is an unprotected 2031 first-round pick from Cleveland that it acquired as part of the Peyton Watson sign-and-trade in August, but the Nuggets naturally prefer to hold onto that pick for their next major transaction."

The Nuggets defeated the Houston Rockets last week, 133-124, in an NBA Cup matchup. They've found their stride over the past two weeks.

"The Nuggets' in-season pursuits are likely to be more modest … especially now that they're starting to find their footing with a 7-2 surge sparked by the ever-reliable twosome of Nikola Jokić and Jamal Murray. David Adelman, in just his second full season as Denver's head coach, has more wins in 100 games (67) than any of his predecessors," Fischer added.

"Yet there is a leftover trade morsel from Watson's drawn-out restricted free agency to share and file away: Denver showed real interest in Clippers swingman Derrick Jones Jr. during the summer when LA was pursuing Watson in sign-and-trade talks. Let's see if Jones re-emerges as a Nuggets trade target as the winter unfolds."

Fischer also noted The Stein Line's Marc Stein's intel about the Clippers, and how their various expiring contracts could lead to a deal with the Nuggets.

"Old Man Stein wrote at length Sunday about how various Clippers who possess expiring contracts — like Max Strus, Brook Lopez and Kris Dunn in addition to Jones — are all expected to feature as prominent names on the in-season trade market," Fischer noted. "The Clippers, for what it's worth, are quietly insisting that they're not there yet in terms of fielding expected offers.

"They want to see what kind of team they have after Brandon Ingram, LA's prime acquisition in September in the trade that returned Kawhi Leonard to Toronto, made his season debut Monday night against Houston following offseason heel surgery. Yet it clearly behooves the Clippers to showcase their various veterans who are likely to generate trade interest because of their experience and favorable contracts. And teams are certainly calling already for the likes of Lopez and Jones."

Clippers' Brook Lopez is having a stellar season, averaging 14.9 points and 5.2 rebounds per game.

"Sources say that there is no imminent trade involving any of those aforementioned Clippers veterans, but we're certainly going to be monitoring their various statuses incessantly over the next 10-plus weeks," Fischer added.

A potential trade between the Nuggets and the Clippers is worth watching.
