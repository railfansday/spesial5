Tyler Bass misses first field goal of season, Bills lead 20-9

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Bills kicker Tyler Bass extended his run of made field goals to open the season to 22 in the first half against the Dolphins on Sunday, but it won’t get any longer.

Bass hooked a 54-yard field goal to the left in the third quarter for his first missed attempt of the year. Buccaneers kicker Chase McLaughlin is now the only qualified player without a missed field goal on the year.

Bass’s overall streak carried over to last season and reached 27 before his miss.

The miss gave Miami the ball on the Buffalo 44-yard-line, but a pair of running plays were followed by an incompletion that led to a Dolphins punt.

That left the score 20-9 with under six minutes to play in the third quarter.
