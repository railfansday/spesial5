VOTE: CFBHN/Herald-Tribune Girls Athlete of the Week for Nov. 2-7

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Each week, the Herald-Tribune staff will select nominees for the Herald-Tribune Girls Athlete of the Week poll sponsored by Central Florida Behavioral Network, Inc. Nominees are derived from scores and stats emailed in by Sarasota and Manatee County coaches during the week to htincoming@gmail.com. Readers can then vote in the online poll to determine the Girls Athlete of the Week.

The Herald-Tribune Girls Athlete of the Week sponsored by Central Florida Behavioral Health Network for Oct. 26-31, 2026, was Venice High volleyball player Kendall Balon, who won the poll with 51 percent of the vote. Balon had five kills and one block in Venice's 3-0 win over Jupiter in the regional final.

A poll will be posted at heraldtribune.com each Monday, and voting will end at 10 a.m. Friday each week.

The nominees for the week of Nov. 2-7, 2026 are:

Madison Muller, Riverview: Muller finished first in the District 4A-6 meet, running a 16:50.8 to win the individual district title. Muller's time is the second fastest all-time by a junior girl in the state of Florida.

Lexie Walder, Wellen Park: Walder placed third in the District 2A-6 meet, running a 20:32.2.

Avery Webster, Lakewood Ranch: Webster finished seven in the District 4A-6 meet, running a 19:29.5 and helping Lakewood Ranch to a district title.

Annika Zimmerman, Palmetto: Zimmerman finished 10th in the District 4A-6 meet, running a 19:52.4.

Olivia Agate, Parrish Community: Agate placed fifth in Region 3A-3, shooting a 72 and helping Parrish to a second-place finish.

Willda Kuang, Lakewood Ranch: Kuang finished eighth, shooting a 73 in Region 3A-3 and helping Lakewood Ranch to a third place finish.

Daisy Quintal, Out-of-Door Academy: Quintal placed 11th in Region 1A-3, shooting a 78 and helping ODA to a regional title.

Sydney Hardy, Riverview: Hardy helped Riverview to its sixth straight title and 13th overall, winning the 200 free relay, 200 IM (1:58.831) and tying second in the 100 bad (54.37).

Angelina Lista, Riverview: Lista placed first in the 200 free relay and third in the 100 fly. She was also fifth in the 50 free.

Alexandra Pearson, Riverview: Pearson placed first in the 200 free relay. She was also the state runner-up in the 50 free (22.80).
