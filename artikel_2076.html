Sources: Celtic make Kieran McKenna appointment decision

Celtic have set their sights on Kieran McKenna as the first choice to become their next manager, sources have told Football Insider.

Martin O'Neill is facing increasing pressure over his position at Parkhead after an inconsistent season so far, with recent Scottish Premiership results leading to major doubts.

McKenna would be available to step straight into the role, having left his position at Ipswich Town following their promotion in the summer, and is high on Celtic's list.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Celtic would have to pay a fee of around £8million to Ipswich if they approach McKenna.

The club ensured they would be protected financially in their agreement when the manager stepped down, and the fee is believed to have made it difficult for Celtic.

There is also a feeling they could stick with O'Neill despite their recent form, with a win against Besiktas last time out having eased the immediate perssure on him.

Football Insider revealed on 25 October that McKenna has strong backers behind the scenes at Celtic as they weigh up who could become their next permanent manager.

Regardless of whether O'Neill is sacked in the near future, the club are expected to make a change at the end of the season, and are eyeing multiple targets.

McKenna's hefty compensation fee is expected to make things difficult for Celtic, but the former Ipswich boss is at the top of their list of targets.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I think the interesting thing at Celtic as well is about that if they were to sort of pull the trigger on O'Neill, who do they go for?

"The one really being mentioned is Kieran McKenna, but he obviously has this big, I think, it's an £8million compensation fee.

"They would need to pay that to Ipswich if they wanted to appoint them, even though on paper he is available after he decided to leave Ipswich in the summer.

"That could obviously sort of throw a spanner in the works for Celtic. So it does make that decision maybe a little bit more difficult."

Speculation about McKenna's potential appointment has come amid increasing pressure on O'Neill's position, with Celtic currently weighing up a decision over his future.

The Hoops have won just four of their last 11 games in all competitions, and have lost six of those, raising long-term doubts about the 74-year-old's future in the dugout.

Football Insider revealed on 6 November that O'Neill is edging increasingly closer to being sacked, and Celtic could be forced into a decision sooner rather than later.

Despite a 3-2 win against Besiktas last time out, their first of the season in the Europa League which has restored some faith, the side's inconsistency remains a major concern.

Celtic are likely to make a decision about O'Neill's future if they lose top spot in the Premiership, where they currently hold a four-point lead ahead of Rangers.
