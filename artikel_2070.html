AFC Wimbledon vs Blackpool “shouldn’t have taken place”, says Ian Evatt after sickness-hit squad loses 1-0

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Blackpool fell to a narrow 1-0 defeat against fellow strugglers AFC Wimbledon, with Alistair Smith netting the all-important goal in the 13th minute with a sublime strike from distance.

To say the Tangerines’ preparations for the trip to South London were disrupted would be an understatement. Ian Evatt’s group was ravaged by a sickness bug, leaving 14 players and staff absent. Bailey Peacock-Farrell and Josh Bowler, two positives in an otherwise tough campaign, were two notable absentees at Plough Lane.

Speaking after the game, Evatt admitted he had never encountered anything quite like it as he spoke to the club’s media channels. He said: “I don’t want to be stood here and feel like I am making excuses; I can only tell the truth. We’ve had 14 players and staff have severe diarrhoea and vomiting for the last 24 hours – it started as soon as we got on the train yesterday, and it spiralled quickly.

“Some players and staff haven’t been able to get out of bed, but they put themselves forward to come on the pitch and play today. For me, we’ve been put in a position we shouldn’t have been put into and this game shouldn’t have taken place today and I would have been saying that if we had won the game.”

The game had a COVID-19 feel with the two sides emerging from the tunnel separately and skipping the customary pre-match handshakes.

Nonetheless, Evatt’s Blackpool dominated for large spells and should have taken at least a point back up to Lancashire, but they couldn’t beat the inspired Nathan Bishop in the Dons’ net.

The energetic performance was a testament to the character of Blackpool in challenging circumstances. They mustered 15 attempts on goal compared to AFC Wimbledon’s nine and created over double their opponents’ xG at 1.22 (FotMob).

They failed to take advantage of the several opportunities they had though, with Dale Taylor and Tom Bloxham struggling to make an impact leading the line.

Returning to Bloomfield Road last October after seven years as a player, Evatt is arguably facing one of his most challenging periods in the dugout.

He managed to steer Blackpool away from relegation last term, although not convincingly, but inconsistency this term has once again left them languishing at the wrong end of the table.

Following a bright start with wins over Stockport County and Peterborough United, a run of just one victory in 10 league outings has put the Seasiders in a very familiar position and staring down the barrel of another relegation dogfight.

Evatt raised his stock considerably at Bolton Wanderers but is yet to make his mark at Blackpool, with the visit of in-form Wigan Athletic next Saturday potentially proving decisive in his long-term future.
