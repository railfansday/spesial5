Arsenal transfer news: Arsenal set January transfer priorities as Fabrizio Romano delivers Piero Hincapié update

In Arsenal transfer news, Arsenal could approach the January transfer window with a different focus after Fabrizio Romano revealed that Mikel Arteta has no intention of weakening his defensive options.

Piero Hincapié has been linked with a move to Barcelona. There have been reports that a deal could be done involving Alejandro Balde, but Romano expects the Ecuadorian to stay at Emirates Stadium.

Arsenal have been strengthening their defensive department under Arteta. Hincapié only made the switch permanently from Bayer Leverkusen in the summer after joining on loan.

Despite competition, the 24-year-old is a key player in Arteta’s plans. His versatility to play in either a centre back or left back role gives Arteta a valuable option.

However, we can also gain insight into where Arsenal should target their recruitment from Romano’s latest comments. Instead of changing their defensive options, it could be an opportunity to improve their attacking options.

That leaves Barcelona needing to explore alternative targets if they want to strengthen their backline. A deal involving Balde and Hincapié does not currently appear to be a realistic option.

The more interesting part of Romano’s report is Arsenal’s potential business at the other end of the field.

He feels they will be looking to make a move if the right attacking player comes up for grabs. But he doesn’t think they will make a signing just because they can in January.

This highlights the issues clubs face with mid-season transfers. Often selling clubs will be unwilling to lose key players, and finding suitable replacements can be tough.

This is an important distinction. The point being made here is that whilst there is speculation around one particular player, Romano has indicated that Arsenal can strengthen their attacking options. This is not to say that any particular move is happening.

If Arteta keeps Hincapié, then Arsenal will maintain their defensive stability and possibly gain another option in attack.

Over the next few weeks we will get a better understanding of what is available to them. But for now it looks like Arsenal are not keen to lose Hincapié but will look to strengthen their attacking options if the right deal comes along.
