Hollow Knight: Silksong Free Sea Of Sorrow DLC Releases December 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Team Cherry promised us an update on Hollow Knight: Silksong's Sea of Sorrow DLC today, and thankfully it was not a tepid one where the studio gave the expansion a loose release window way off in the future. It was a far firmer reveal confirming that the free addition to Silksong will be here far sooner than you might have expected.

The trailer for Sea of Sorrow was part of today's Cherry Time update, giving us a first look at what horrors Hornet will be up against next. That first look at the expansion was coupled with the reveal that it will be available on December 11, 2026, so in just 13 days. The DLC will be free to all Silksong owners, no matter which platform you play on.

There is a lot crammed into what is a relatively short trailer showcasing what Sea of Sorrow has to offer. We're shown Hornet battling new enemies, traversing new areas, and judging by the last thing we're shown right at the end of the trailer, she's learned how to fish. Hornet is shown standing on the shore, casting her thread into the water and immediately getting a bite.

Although Team Cherry promised Silksong would be getting an expansion, Hollow Knight fans were fearful because we've been there before. Silksong itself was originally supposed to be DLC for the original Hollow Knight. However, the project wound up becoming so big that Team Cherry eventually decided it should be its own sequel. I'm sure Hollow Knight fans wouldn't argue with that decision given the benefit of hindsight, but it made the wait for Silksong especially excruciating.

There is no risk that Sea of Sorrow will be held back and used to complete the Hollow Knight trilogy. The expansion's release isn't even getting pushed into 2027. This time in two weeks, we'll all be playing it, and it won't cost you an extra penny to do so. If you own Silksong, you will get the DLC for free on December 11. And if you don't, the base game is still just $19.99 and is about to get a whole lot bigger.

Giving fans roughly two weeks between announcement and release appears to be a cadence that Team Cherry has settled into. There was roughly a two-week gap between the announcement of Silksong's release date and its launch, and that went incredibly well. I can only imagine repeating that pattern for Sea of Sorrow will have a similar effect, and the sequel will get a huge player count spike on December 11, along with a sales spike between now and then as those who missed out at launch play catch up before Sea of Sorrow arrives.

Take Sherma's guiding song everywhere with you.
