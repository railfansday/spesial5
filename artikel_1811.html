Dorking downed by depleted Mike Oak

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Oak gave full debuts to Josh Lawes and Alex Grey of their successful under-18s team and also included Luca Allsop and Louis Johnston on the bench, also welcoming back Brad Kilgarif from his American sojourn.

They started well and notched an early goal when the keeper spilled a cross and Sam Geard slotted home. With Grey showing a composure beyond his years at the back, Wanderers were given minimal opportunities to score while the home side continued to compete well.

A second goal came just before the interval when a sublime pass from Marley Amber sent Alex Joy in to score with some aplomb.

The second half began with intense pressure from the away XI and Oak were indebted to custodian Pat Sessions for a number of superb saves and a couple of clearances off the line to keep them ahead.

With a Dorking player receiving a red card for reasons that mystified most, there was hope of respite but the pressure intensified and when sub Jayden Walker scored things looked ominous.

Fortunately with a further two U18s on the field Oak held firm and even had chances on the break to finish off the game.

Satisfied Oak boss Sam Cornish said: “It was good to bounce back quickly after a disappointing result last weekend. We knew how Dorking would play and it was very pleasing to see our lads take on board the instructions given and carry out exactly what we asked of them.

"With several key players missing, there were opportunities for some of the younger inexperienced lads to come into the squad and others to gain debuts and they showed exactly how good we knew they were.

"Alex Grey was unbelievable and you’d have thought he had been ever present in the back line. Marley again worked tirelessly up top and although not on the scoresheet, had arguably his best performance of the season.

"We are on the road at the weekend in what looks like a really tough fixture against Clanfield, the question is, can we take some of this excellent home form and really start picking up points away from home.”

When they were comfortable at 3-0 ahead after half an hour, it all looked so easy for Yapton. but there were squalls head.

From being in control, they were under a lot of pressure in the second period and had keeper Jake Dean to thank for a number of brave and athletic saves to preserve a point.

Adrian Potocki scored after three minutes, Brandon Terry contributed a trade mark cross-shot goal for the second and before the half-hour, Dan Cave calmly rolled in the third. Hunston replied with a good free kick before the break and further goals on 66 and 80.
