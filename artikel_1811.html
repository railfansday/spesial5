Kompany back at Bayern’s training ground after collapse

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Bayern Munich have confirmed Vincent Kompany has returned to the club's training ground for the first time since collapsing last month.

The former Manchester City defender collapsed during a training session prior to Bayern's Bundesliga win over Freiburg in late October.

It was subsequently confirmed that Kompany was in a stable condition and undergoing tests in hospital, with sporting director Max Eberl saying he could miss "weeks".

Kompany's assistant Aaron Danks took his place on the sideline for five matches, overseeing victories over Freiburg, Magdeburg, Atletico Madrid and Mainz, as well as a draw with Klassiker rivals Borussia Dortmund.

On Tuesday, Bayern confirmed in a short statement that Kompany had returned to their base at Sabener Strasse, where he is also undergoing rehabilitation alongside his work, having suffered a shoulder injury when he collapsed.

There has not been any announcement regarding Kompany's chances of being on the touchline for Bayern's next game, at home to Koln in the Bundesliga on November 20.

Bayern then visit Lille in the Champions League five days later, before another road trip in the Bundesliga, to face Hamburger SV.
