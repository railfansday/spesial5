Frick promises his Dons will maintain their positive approach and go on the attack in Ibrox clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

By CALUM CROWE, SCOTTISH DEPUTY CHIEF SPORTS WRITER

Published: 13:28 EST, 23 November 2026 | Updated: 13:41 EST, 23 November 2026

Aberdeen  boss Mario Frick has vowed to go on the attack when his new-look team take on Rangers at Ibrox tomorrow night.

Appointed as the club's new manager during the international break, Frick's reign got off to a flyer as Aberdeen won 4-2 away at Kilmarnock on Saturday.

Renowned for a high-octane attacking style of play at his previous clubs, the 52-year-old Liechtensteiner has already generated a wave of positivity at Pittodrie.

Aberdeen will face a tough task against an in-form Rangers side who are unbeaten in their last 13 matches.

But Frick insists there is no prospect of his team turning up at Ibrox and parking the bus in their efforts to take a positive result.

'I'm looking forward to it and I'm pretty confident in our squad,' he said. 'Now we have to prove that and have another good performance.

'That's not our style (to sit in and defend). We want to play like we did at Kilmarnock. That's our style of play.

Frick is all smiles after his side put four goals past Kilmarnock in his first match in charge

'Rangers will be a tough game. They are very strong, especially at home. I saw a lot of quality, particularly in their forward areas.

'We have to defend better than we did on Saturday, that's for sure, and that's what we're working on.

'I have to get to know my players even better because it's only been a week since we arrived in Aberdeen, and that takes time.

'But, of course, it helps when you win the first game. We've got some confidence now and I'm pretty confident we can take something from Ibrox.

'At the weekend, you could see that the mentality is very good. We struggled during the first 20 minutes of the second half, but the substitutes were very good and had a huge impact.

'I think, in the end, it was a deserved win and now we're looking forward to meeting Rangers at Ibrox.

'Defensively, we have to improve. Otherwise, it will be very difficult. I'm pretty sure we can defend better.

'But, like I say, we want to play like we did at the weekend. It's not our style (just to sit back and defend).'

Kevin Nisbet is mobbed by team-mates after netting the first of his double at Rugby Park

After the doom and gloom of the Stephen Robinson era, Frick has immediately created a feelgood factor at Aberdeen.

The fans were singing his name after the victory at Rugby Park on Saturday - and he wants to strengthen that feeling of unity throughout the club.

'It's been very good so far, especially with the win,' he added. 'The team has some confidence now and I see many players laughing.

'That's very important because we do this for fun as well. When you go onto the pitch, you have to take things seriously, but always with enjoyment. That's very important.

'The relationship between the team and the supporters is important, not between the supporters and me, because the team is the most important thing.

'For the players' confidence, it was very important that so many supporters travelled to Kilmarnock and celebrated the win with us. That was the first step.

'It's not the time for me to make a statement. It's the first time we're playing together at Ibrox, and it's important that we stick to our style of play.

'That's the most important thing to me. It is an exciting game and we are looking forward to it.'
