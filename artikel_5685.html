Tom Holland and Zendaya Snap Up $10 Million London Home—Months After Tying the Knot

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Tom Holland and Zendaya may make London their primary residence after splashing out $10 million on a palatial pad across the pond.

The married lovebirds, both 30, snapped up the home for $987,000 under asking—securing a great deal on the property, which is situated on a one-acre lot and is surrounded in lush greenery.

The home sprawls across 5,000 square feet, according to the Daily Mail, which first reported the news of the couple's lavish purchase.

One of Holland and Zendaya's neighbors has already expressed their excitement to have the stars just a stone's throw away, telling the outlet, "My youngest is very excited that Spider-Man is going to be living across the street. I told him, 'You will have to kick a ball a long way to reach their back garden.' I doubt they will have a housewarming party."

The news of their new purchase comes after it was revealed that they were planning to install a climate-controlled room in one of their several properties to preserve her more than $269,000 wedding gown.

The couple said "I do" during an intimate wedding celebration at the Beaverbrook Hotel in Surrey, just outside London, on Aug. 4.

The actress marked the occasion by donning a stunning lace Schiaparelli gown featuring long sleeves and a high neckline.

In September, sources told The Sun that the actress wants to keep the gown close by rather than entrusting it to an outside storage company. The dress reportedly needs to be stored at a temperature between 59 and 64 degrees.

Rather than shipping the gown off for professional storage, Zendaya reportedly hopes to create a dedicated space for it inside one of the couple's own properties.

"Ideally, she wants it to be with her at their home so they're looking into getting a climate-controlled room installed so it's safe and that she can put it on if she wants to," the source added.

The musician and fashion designer picked up her first property for $1.4 million—a five-bedroom, five-bathroom Oakland, CA, mansion that is described as a “remodeled Mediterranean-style” residence complete with a curved entryway, which the star showed off to her 180 million Instagram followers.

In 2019, Zendaya expanded her real estate empire to a spread in Encino, CA, paying a reported $3.97 million for the posh property. The home sits on over 3 acres and includes hiking trails, a guesthouse, and a pool.

In 2021, the star staked out an East Coast perch, picking up a Brooklyn waterfront condo in the stunning Quay building, the New York Post reported. She is said to have paid $4.9 million for the three-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom aerie in the new building.

The "Spider-Man" star is understood to have snapped up his primary residence in Kingston upon Thames, not far from where he grew up, in 2018—before launching into a yearslong renovation in 2020.
