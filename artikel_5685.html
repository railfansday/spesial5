Opinion: STAT+: Candles and long baths can’t fix burned-out physicians

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Welcome to Chief Complaint, a new STAT+ column from Frances Mei Hardin, M.D. A former ear, nose, and throat surgeon, she writes about the many challenges facing those who practice medicine in a time of great change.

During my second year of residency, my husband drove me to the batting cages for the first time. He’d noticed I was coming home with an increasing level of rage, with nowhere for it to go. I had never swung a bat before, but we spent an hour that evening in a netted room, him slow-pitching and me swinging out of my shoes. My hands stung and my bones shook with every hit, but it was the best that I felt in months.

This batting cage salve is about the only type of “physician self-care” that ever actually worked for me, and it didn’t come from a hospital-mandated wellness module, nor a doctor appreciation pizza party. What actually gets prescribed to physicians is basic wellness fare: the mindfulness worksheets, the resilience webinars, the optional yoga in unused office room C in the old hospital annex building.
