Nvidia’s Jensen Huang: China Is 'Not Our Enemy,' US Chip Policy a 'Mistake'

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

In an interview filmed on October 14, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang defended engaging with China and criticized US chip restrictions. He has since acknowledged having an affair, and US intelligence officials reportedly suspect the woman was recruited by Beijing to influence him. Nvidia’s board is now investigating, and Huang said he would "cooperate fully." He spoke with Bloomberg’s Emily Chang for an upcoming episode of The Circuit. (Source: Bloomberg)
