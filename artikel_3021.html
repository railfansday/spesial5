The one matchup that will tip 49ers-Vikings: Dallas Turner versus Trent Williams

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Sunday night is a huge game for the San Francisco 49ers and the Minnesota Vikings. The winner will likely be in the driver's seat for the number one seed in the NFC with a tiebreaker over the other team. Now, there is no way to predict what will happen down the stretch, but this game feels like a playoff game and, for many, not all, the marbles.

The must-see matchup is clearly Kyle Shanahan against Brian Flores. Interesting subplots include Kirk Cousins facing the 49ers again and Jauan Jennings returning to face his old team. One matchup deserves a closer look and could tip the scales for both teams.

EDGE rusher Dallas Turner is in the mix for DPOY and rightfully so. However, Will Anderson IV might have a stranglehold after last night's performance. Everyone is all over Flores' blitz rates and the exotic nature of his coverages, but Turner has completely broken out in 2026.

The 23-year-old has a bright future ahead of him in the NFL. On the other side of the age spectrum, there stands 38-year-old Trent Williams. Sunday will be the first time the two will do battle.

Turner lines up everywhere, which is to be expected in Flores' scheme. The right side is where he has his highest snap numbers, with 253 snaps between RE and REO. Colton McKivitz will draw Turner also, but Williams will likely see Turner more.

According to PFF, Turner has generated 45 pressures, which is the sixth-most among qualified EDGE rushers. On 305 pass-blocking snaps in 2026, Williams has allowed one sack, two QB hits, five hurries, and eight pressures. Against two of the more ferocious pass rushes in Seattle and Denver, Williams posted back-to-back clean sheets.

Communication for the offensive line will be paramount on Sunday night as Flores will send the house more often than not, and many Vikings defensive linemen and rushers benefit from the confusion and chaos caused by Flores, but Turner vs. Williams is a matchup the 49ers have to win.

Sunday will be filled with plenty of star power, and Turner will have his impact, but against Williams, he must be turned away and shut out. This is the classic case of "old guard" vs. "new guard."

Regardless of your fandom, Sunday night is the best matchup of the weekend and possibly the season so far. Grab your popcorn, and watching two of the best in the business will be a delight for all.
