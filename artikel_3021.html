Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Offline growth and a first-ever third-quarter operating profit stand out.

Gaotu Techedu (GOTU -7.80%) achieved GAAP operating profitability for the first time in a third quarter, reporting net revenues of 1,868.4 million RMB. Management reported that the company focused on profitable growth by optimizing resource allocation and integrating artificial intelligence into teaching, services, and operations. The company expanded its offline service delivery capabilities and consolidated selected product lines to prioritize businesses with high returns and sustainable market demand. For the fourth quarter of 2026, the company provided revenue guidance reflecting a projected year-over-year increase of 4.3% to 5.5%.

This operating approach also informs how we manage our overall business portfolio. Based on the performance of each business and product line, we further refined our evaluation framework centered on unit economics and proactively adjusted our portfolio. We continue to consolidate selected products and businesses whose returns fell short of expectations and reallocated their resources in an orderly manner, directing more resources toward businesses with clearer market demand, differentiated competitive advantage, and attractive returns.
