Horsham FC lose by odd goal in five to AFC Totton – the match in 38 pictures

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Hornets failed to take anything from their tussle with the Stags, Saturday’s game at the Fusion Aviation Community Stadium finishing 3-2.

Totton led twice in the first half only for Horsham to strike back both times through Charlie Hester-Cook and Ryan Bartley.

Josh Sims hit what proved to be the Stags’ winner on 72 minutes to bring the Hornets’ unbeaten streak to an end.

Defeat to Totton saw Horsham drop one place to 11th in the table.

The Hornets host National League South rivals Torquay United in the second round of the FA Trophy this Saturday.
