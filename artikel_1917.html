'Difficult position to be in' - Leeds United and England man Dominic Calvert-Lewin on Harry Kane competition

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Leeds United star Dominic Calvert-Lewin has conceded he is in a “difficult position” behind Harry Kane in the England pecking order.

Calvert-Lewin has enjoyed a career rejuvenation at Elland Road, scoring 20 goals across 51 appearances since his arrival from Everton in the summer of 2025.

He has catapulted himself back into the England fold, but the presence of Bayern Munich’s Kane as the country’s chief marksman presents a significant challenge for the 29-year-old.

“His record speaks for itself,” said Calvert-Lewin ahead of England’s meeting with Croatia on Thursday (November 12). “He’s a fantastic player.

“He’s the captain of the country and he leads by example. And I think he’s deserving of his accolades at the moment. On a personal note, being a centre forward in competition for that place, it’s a difficult position to be in.

“So I think when I come away on England camps now, I think I have to be just as prepared as possible and ready for when my own personal opportunity comes, if and when needed.

“I suppose it’s somewhat an audition, but it’s what I do every week that is what will warrant a call-up. Obviously you then get a chance to shine in an England shirt.”

Calvert-Lewin had gone nearly five years without an England appearance when he was ushered back into the ranks earlier this year.

Alongside him in Thomas Tuchel’s Three Lions squad is Whites goalkeeper James Trafford, who faces the task of dislodging Jordan Pickford between the sticks.

“With age, I know what my strengths are and I know what I’m good at,” said Calvert-Lewin. “Showcasing that on a weekly basis with Leeds, it’s down to Thomas if he wants to pick me or not.

“We’re both very different in the way we play. His mentality is very strong. We’re similar in that sense. We’re both very strong in how we want to play. His finishing quality, his overall quality and leadership is something I appreciate.

“Removing our own personal competition, striker watching another striker, he’s one of the best in the world and you cannot argue with that.”

England will host Croatia at Wembley on Thursday before facing Spain in Madrid on Sunday (November 15).
