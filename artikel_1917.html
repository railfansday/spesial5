Barcelona’s Cubarsi a doubt for Villarreal clash with hip injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Pau Cubarsi faces a race against time to be fit for Barcelona's LaLiga clash with Villarreal on Sunday after sustaining an injury while on international duty with Spain.

Barcelona confirmed on Monday that Cubarsi was suffering with a hip injury he picked up during Spain's 3-2 defeat to England in the Nations League.

Cubarsi was replaced at half-time by Dean Huijsen after feeling discomfort moments after giving away a penalty for a late challenge on England captain Harry Kane.

The 19-year-old has started all 13 of his appearances across all competitions this season, while also completing more passes (1,178) than any other Barcelona player in 2025-26.

He also ranks first for aerial duels won (34), while only Lamine Yamal (114) has won more duels in Hansi Flick's ranks than Cubarsi's 53 (level with Fermin Lopez).

"Pau Cubarsi is suffering from tightness in his hip flexor (iliopsoas)," Barcelona's statement read. "His recovery will determine whether he is available for the match against Villarreal."

But one player that will be available for Barcelona is Frenkie de Jong despite the midfielder suffering a muscle strain and leaving the Netherlands camp earlier than anticipated.

De Jong is expected to play some part at the Camp Nou this weekend as Barcelona aim to continue their strong start to their LaLiga title defence.

Barcelona have earned 33 points from 12 games in the 2026-27 LaLiga season (W11 L1). Counting three points for a win, the Blaugrana have only accumulated more points at this stage of the competition in the 2012-13, 2013-14 and 2017-18 seasons (34 points – W11 D1), also earning 33 points in the 2024-25 season (W11 L1).

They have, however, lost two of their last five LaLiga matches against Villarreal (W3), twice as many defeats as they suffered in their previous 29 meetings with them in the competition (W22 D6 L1).
