Leeds United and Hull City’s Premier League rivals Crystal Palace confirm Thomas Frank appointment

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Leeds United and Hull City’s Premier League rivals Crystal Palace have unveiled Thomas Frank as their new manager.

The Eagles dispensed of the services of Pierre Sage last week, dismissing him after a run of four consecutive league defeats.

He has been replaced by Frank, who has previously led Brentford and Tottenham Hotspur, and has now taken charge of a London club for the third time.

Frank said: “It's an honour to sit here and be in charge of this fantastic football club. I'm really, really looking forward to it.

“Crystal Palace is an iconic club in England and in the Premier League. I will do everything I possibly can to help and support the players and everyone around the place, and I'm really looking forward to meeting them.”

Frank has quite the job on his hands, with Palace rooted to the bottom of the Premier League table after just two wins in 10 games.

Just above them in the relegation zone, in 19th and 18th respectively, are Sunderland and Manchester United.

Eagles chairman Steve Parish said: “I’m delighted to be able to welcome Thomas to Crystal Palace. He has a proven track record of both creating winning teams and improving players, making him the perfect fit for our club.

“I’m confident Thomas’ extensive experience in the Premier League will help us achieve our goals for the rest of the season.”

Frank will take charge of Palace for the first time this weekend, when they will visit Frank Lampard’s Coventry City. His first home game as boss is scheduled for November 29, when Hull are due to make the trip to Selhurst Park.

Paddy McCarthy will remain on the books as a coach having assisted Sage, while the Eagles have also retained goalkeeping coach and Frank’s former Brentford colleague Andy Quy.
