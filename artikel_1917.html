Estevao responds to Chelsea transfer question after impressing on Brazil duty

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Estevao declined to comment when asked whether his lack of playing time might push him to leave Chelsea in January.

Opportunities have been few and far between for Chelsea’s Brazilian wonderkid this season. Xabi Alonso has handed him just one Premier League start, in the October defeat of Manchester United.

His lack of involvement this season comes in stark contrast to previous campaigns. He played a sustained role under both Enzo Maresca and Liam Rosenior, but Alonso has been less ready to deploy him in the Premier League.

This has not been the case on international duty, though. Estevao remains a key part of Carlo Ancelotti’s Brazil side, and chalked up a goal and two assists in Tuesday’s 7-0 battering of Singapore.

His lack of minutes at club level has set the rumour mill spinning at pace, though, with reports in the Brazilian press claiming that Paris Saint-Germain are interested in taking him on loan from January through the end of the season.

But Estevao himself remains tight-lipped on his future. Per ESPN Brazil, the player was asked after the Singapore match to comment on a potential move to PSG, but simply smiled, said goodbye to the reporters, and left the mixed zone.

He did, however, discuss his lack of playing time with Chelsea, saying he is grateful for the opportunity to stretch his legs on international duty.

“I'm very happy to have gotten minutes in the friendlies,” he told reporters.

“I thank Ancelotti for his confidence, because every player wants to play and at Chelsea I've had few minutes, but coming here and knowing that the manager trusts me gives me a lot of motivation.”

Chelsea boss Alonso has repeatedly said this season that Estevao has an important part to play in his squad, and that he will have more opportunities as the season wears on.

He has also suggested Estevao’s lack of minutes so far this season is due to the hamstring injury he sustained late last term, which ruled him out of the World Cup, and a general lack of physicality.
