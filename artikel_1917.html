Two players under huge scrutiny – for very different reasons | Portsmouth v West Ham United

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Scott Parker withdrawing goal keeper Mads Hermansen from the firing line for a bit of breather has resonated well with West Ham supporters, who were all very much in favour of replacing him after a dreadful run of form. The statistics were pretty damning with the Dane unable to lay a glove on any one of the last dozen goals hit past him.

Herrick can cement his place with a decent performance at Fratton Park tonight

Herrick needs a clean sheet in the next couple of games to cement his place in the side and his confidence, after a cock up led to Preston’s goal at the weekend: All eyes will be on him come the first corner at Fratton Park tonight to see how he shapes up.

Portsmouth, however, have their own goal keeping problems and their very own Mads Hermansen in the shape of stopper Nicholas Schmid who has made his own string of errors in recent games, leading to a fairly frank rating last time out from Portsmouth.co.uk:

“Nicolas Schmid – 4: Poor mistake gifted Bolton an equaliser just five minutes after Blues took the lead. While a poor kick out of his hands almost proved costly too. That’s five goal-cost mistakes in six games now. Hugely worrying.”

Pompey’s boss John Mousinho has publicly backed his 29 year old Austrian, which means he’s likely to start against West Ham tonight and the message for Scott Parker’s team is 100% crystal clear.

Test the keeper. Again and again. And follow up on those shots. Sounds simple. West Ham however haven’t exactly found it easy to hit the target with 26 wayward efforts against Preston.

Either way there’ll be huge scrutiny on both the men between the sticks tonight.

Portsmouth v West Ham United, Fratton Park, kick off 7.45 pm.
