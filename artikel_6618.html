Cranberry Brie Wontons

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

If your friends love our cranberry brie bites, they’ll go nuts for these cranberry brie wontons. Brushed with butter and air-fried until crispy on the outside and gooey in the middle, these wontons are the party snack you need this holiday season. Don’t forget the bowl of cranberry sauce for dunking—it makes it feel extra-festive.

Stuffing the wontons:In order to have a satisfactorily cheesy center after the wontons are fried, you’ll need to cram a lot of brie into the wonton when stuffing it. Brie shrinks a lot when it melts, so while a 1/2-ounce cube might feel like a lot, it’s actually the perfect amount.

Cooking the wontons:While wontons are often deep-fried, I opted for the air fryer here since it makes for faster, easier cleanup and still yields a delicious result. Yes, the wontons don’t get quite as bubble-textured or evenly browned as they would if they were deep-fried, but they still crisp up really nicely—and the butter adds a delightful flavor all on its own!

Make-ahead tips:Once cooked, these wontons are best eaten immediately. But if you want to make ahead, you can form and freeze the wontons for up to 1 month. Freeze on a parchment-lined baking sheet until hard, then transfer to an airtight container. Wontons that have been totally frozen might take a few extra minutes to air-fry!
