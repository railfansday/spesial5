SpY installs giant black triangle in madrid square to explore urban surveillance

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

the 20-meter black triangular intervention is equipped with concealed viewing apertures and apex-mounted CCTV cameras that return fragmented live feeds of the surrounding plaza.

SpY edited by christina vergopoulou November 5, 2026 3 min

Installed in Madrid’s Plaza de Cibeles directly fronting the Palacio de Cibeles, PANOPTICON by urban artist SpY is a large-scale temporary architectural installation configured as an equilateral black triangle measuring 20 meters on each side. The dark, monolithic geometry introduces an abrupt formal contrast against the ornate historic facade of the Palacio, directly intercepting established sightlines across the civic square.

PANOPTICON is a large-scale public art installation designed by SpY in Madrid, Spainthe installation occupies the center of Plaza de Cibeles, facing the historic Palacio de Cibelesthe temporary structure takes the form of an equilateral triangle measuring 20 meters per side
