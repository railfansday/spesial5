Best Amazon Black Friday Deals (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Amazon’s Black Friday deals are now live. Its “Black Friday Week” sale runs November 19–30 (Cyber Monday), giving shoppers a head start on holiday shopping. The retailer has already discounted many items since last week and has now added even more to the sale. Even better, we’re seeing some items with even lower prices. To help you sift through the products, we’ve highlighted some of our favorites below. If you’re a big Amazon shopper, now is a great time to fill those holiday wish lists and spend Black Friday with friends and family.

Check out our guide to the best Early Black Friday deals, plus standing desk deals, Home Depot appliance savings, and early tech deals.

Updated November 19: Amazon’s Black Friday deals start today. We’ve updated this story to reflect that and added more products we’ve recommended in our guides and reviews.

With a WIRED rating of 9 out of 10, the XM6 is one of our favorite over-ear headphones, combining comfort with great sound quality and noise cancellation. This is the lowest price on Amazon; however, it has dropped to this price before. Note that Walmart and Best Buy are also offering the XM6 at this sale price.

Good gaming laptops are never cheap, and they’ve become even more expensive this year. If you need a recommendation that balances performance and budget, we recommend the Asus ROG Strix G16 with an Nvidia GeForce RTX 5060, one of our favorite budget gaming laptops. We prefer the RTX 5070 option, but it’s nearly $3,000. This on-sale model is more attainable.

The latest Echo Show 11 is our recommendation for the Best Alexa Smart Display. WIRED reviewer Nena Farrell found the screen bright and crisp, with immersive sound quality. Since the sale price matches the regular price of the smaller Echo Show 8, it’s a good deal—just $5 more than the sale price during Amazon’s October Prime Day event.

The Pixel 11-series phones from Google are among our favorite Android devices. Loaded with the latest software and Gemini AI features, the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, and Pixel 11 Pro XL are our pick as the smartest Android smartphones. They’re also the only non-iPhones to support Qi2 wireless charging and magnetic mounting, so you can use MagSafe accessories. During Black Friday, you can get all for under $1,000 each—a terrific deal for a flagship phone—and they’re at their lowest price ever on Amazon.

The Series 12 model is not only cheaper than the high-end Ultra 4, but it’s also the best Apple Watch you can buy, period. It has the latest health-sensing features, including a Readiness score that summarizes your recent health and activity. Its Amazon sale price is currently the lowest since its introduction.

High-quality three-in-one charging stands look terrific on a bedside table, but many cost over $100. This one from Mophie is one of our favorites, and it’s 49 percent off on Amazon right now.

The AirTag seems to be on sale all the time, but no matter—it’s currently the lowest price on Amazon and cheaper than buying from Apple. It’s one of the best Bluetooth trackers. If you can swing it, go for the four-pack.

Alongside the AirTag, the AirPods Max 2 and AirPods Pro 3 are also on sale. These are well-regarded among WIRED testers, but we recommend waiting until after Thanksgiving to see if they drop further—don’t worry, they are often on sale.

Phone accessories are aplenty on Amazon, but few look as good as Moft’s. You can pick up this ingenious wallet-kickstand hybrid that attaches to an iPhone via MagSafe, or peruse the rest of Moft's Black Friday deals.

You can expect Amazon’s own devices to go on sale during Black Friday. While Blink’s security cameras didn’t make our short list, WIRED reviewer Simon Hill still thinks they’re worth considering, especially if you’re on a budget. A downer is that they only work with Alexa, but if that’s you, it’s NBD. The latest version of its pan-tilt camera is usually $40, but it’s 50 percent off right now.

WIRED’s audio editor won’t stop praising Soundcore products, but he’s not wrong: This nascent brand (soon to be rebranded as Anker, its parent company) makes terrific audio products without the premium price. WIRED reviewer Louryn Strampe included this Bluetooth boom box in a roundup of her favorite festival gear. It’s the lowest price ever on Amazon currently, but it’s not the cheapest—keep an eye out at other retailers during Black Friday.

This EcoFlow portable battery is WIRED reviewer Simon Hill’s pick as the best power bank for laptops. It has a large 27,650-mAh capacity, which is plenty for charging smaller devices too. It’s great for travel since it’s allowed on a plane, and it has a retractable USB-C cable, so you can leave extras at home.

At $359, the second-gen Bose QuietComfort over-ear headphones seem overpriced considering the competition. But when they’re on sale at $199 to $219 (depending on the color), they suddenly become a great buy. As expected from Bose, the noise cancellation and sound quality are excellent. They are also comfortable, making them great travel companions. Note that the first-gen version is also on sale for $169 (it’s frequently at this price), but it’s outdated, and we think you should spend $30 to $50 more on the latest version instead.

Love them or hate them, but pod-based coffee makers are popular for their convenience. The Nespresso Vertuo Pop+ is our recommended espresso pod maker, and the Deluxe model is under $100. It has a larger water tank than the standard version, and the Vertuo pods are recyclable. For more savings on coffee-related products, check out our Early Black Friday Espresso Machine Deals.

WIRED’s home-cleaning expert, Nena Farrell, recommended only three robot vacuums, naming this Eufy floor-sweeping automaton her budget pick. It’s 43 percent off right now for a robovac with great suction power and a self-cleaning roller mop.

With quality espresso machines costing $500 and more (way, way more), a budget option seems improbable. Yet, this De’Longhi fits the bill. One of our recommended models, it usually costs around $200 or just a little more, but you can snag one for $150 if you’ve been wanting to get into the third-wave coffee game. It’s a great price, but we’ve seen it go as low as $120 in past sales.
