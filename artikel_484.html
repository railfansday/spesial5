Argentina’s $24 Billion LNG Dream Bets On Milei's Model Enduring

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Argentina is trying to push through one of the biggest energy investments ever in Latin America. The $24 billion plan to produce vast quantities of liquefied shale gas is a key part of efforts to turn Argentina into a significant world supplier. But while locals hope for enduring prosperity, fears linger over whether construction jobs will vanish once terminals go live—and whether heavy industry will threaten the gulf’s protected right whales. Jonathan Gilbert reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
