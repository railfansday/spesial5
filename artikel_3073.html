Williams-Sonoma (WSM -15.16%) reported a 5.8% increase in net revenue to $1.99 billion, supported by positive comparable growth across all brands and the acceleration of its e-commerce channel. Management reported that the non-GAAP operating margin expanded to 17.4% as the company prioritized full price selling and reduced promotional activity, particularly within the Pottery Barn brand. The company maintained a strong liquidity position with $992 million in cash and no debt while returning $253 million to shareholders through buybacks and dividends. Strategic focus remains on leveraging an integrated multi-brand platform and proprietary AI technology to drive operational efficiency and customer engagement despite macroeconomic challenges in the housing market and rising transportation costs.
