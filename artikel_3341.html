Kai Cenat on Creating Boundaries With His Streaming Audience: ‘I Got to Clock Certain Things’

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

For the feature, VIBE (and Rolling Stone) senior writer Jeff Ihaza — who had profiled the AMP collective, which includes Cenat, last year — spent time with Cenat at his fashion show in Manhattan in September. As the megastar streamer prepared for the event for his line, Vivet, he talked about building the brand. “The show was a representation of my two worlds colliding,” he told VIBE. “It was a staple moment for the world of streaming and the world of fashion to collab on a platform that I’ve been able to build.” Trending Stories Sade Are Not Attending Rock & Roll Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony The ‘You Can See Everything’ Interview You’ve Been Waiting For Megan Thee Stallion and Daniel Radcliffe Reunite for Season Two of ‘Reggie Dinkins’ Judge Sets Steven Tyler Sex Assault Trial Date

Last fall, after a breakneck few years building followers and stacking cash, thanks to seemingly limitless brand deals, he “abruptly hit stop on the stream,” Ihaza wrote in the cover story, “vanishing from Twitch without explanation.” But he didn’t stay off the platform for very long, and has since streamed from around the world, including from inside a volcano in Iceland. He also launched his second year of Streamer U, where he brings together content creators from across the globe to learn from one another and, of course, show up in one anothers’ streams. “You get to see how these storylines play out, and also you get to see how they collaborate,” he said.

Cenat also addressed controversy he’s faced, both because of fallouts with other streamers and from his own decisions, like when he was charged with inciting a riot after announcing an impromptu PlayStation 5 giveaway in Union Square in 2023. (He paid a $55,000 fine, and the charges were dropped.) He told VIBE that back then, he didn’t understand what the problem was. Now, though, he said he realizes that his influence is also his responsibility. “Why is it my fault? Why is it my fault? What did I do?” he asked himself at the time. “Whole time, bro, your influence is just so big, so you got to know.”
