Netflix's Haunting 6-Episode Period Mystery Is a Binge You Can Finish in an Afternoon

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Of those early thrillers, one that continues to stand out with TV fans is the limited series Alias Grace, an adaptation of the novel written by The Handmaid's Tale's Margaret Atwood. Filled with big twists, shocking revelations, potential otherworldly forces, and the harrowing experiences of a young woman in a world dominated by powerful men, Alias Grace is trademark Atwood. It will keep you on the edge of your seat from start to finish, and feels like even more of a masterpiece with each passing year.

If you're not familiar, Alias Grace is a period thriller about a young woman by the name of Grace Marks, who was convicted of murdering the man she worked for, as well as his housekeeper. Now, Atwood's novel is a fictional story, but it is based on a real-life murder case. Thomas Kinnear and Nancy Mongtomery were killed in 1843, and the real Grace Marks was convicted of the crime, alongside James McDermott. After reading the true story, as well as the controversy and debate surrounding Marks' conviction, Atwood wrote a fictional story about the events surrounding the murders.

The TV adaptation follows the same structure as Atwood's novel, using Grace's interactions with a psychiatrist years after her conviction to determine whether she actually played a part in the murders of Kinnear and Montgomery. Grace and Dr. Simon Jordan talk through her life story over the course of the six-part series, while much of the show takes place in flashbacks, exploring the various parts of her journey that ultimately led to the night of the murders.

Over the course of the series, viewers dive deeper and deeper into Grace's memories to find out if she was actually behind the deaths of Kinnear and Montgomery in any way. In the years between her conviction and meeting with Dr. Jordan, Grace maintains that she doesn't remember anything about the night of the murders, so the journey into her past is potentially enlightening for her as well.

Without giving too much away, Alias Grace does explore some spiritual and supernatural elements, keeping the case from being as totally straight-forward as some might want or expect. Could forces of evil have influenced Grace in some capacity? Could someone from beyond the grave have had a hand in the killings? Would either of those things be enough to absolve Grace — if anyone believed them at all? There are a lot of questions that get asked throughout the series, and not all of them are given concrete answers. The ultimate gray area where Alias Grace concludes is part of what makes it such a memorable experience, and why it remains such a rewatchable series today.

Sarah Gadon stars in the 2017 series as Grace Marks, while Edward Holcroft plays Dr. Jordan. The series also features Rebecca Liddiard, Zachary Levi, Kerr Logan, Anna Paquin, and famed director David Cronenberg. As if the cast wasn't already great, the creative team behind Alias Grace is even more enticing. Mary Harron, director and co-writer of American Pyscho, directed all six episodes of Alias Grace, while Sarah Polley, who won an Academy Award for her spellbinding 2022 film Women Talking, wrote the series.
