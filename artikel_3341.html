iCloud+ has new perk in iOS 27 if you pay for more storage

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The tentpole feature of iCloud+ is cloud storage, but Apple keeps improving and expanding the service to include other perks too. And in iOS 27, iCloud+ has a new benefit for high-storage tiers: AI features for HomeKit cameras.

With iOS 27, iCloud+ has added a new perk if you pay for more storage: Apple Intelligence features for compatible Home cameras. Per Apple’s website:

With Apple Intelligence in the Home app, you can automatically generate video descriptions for recorded clips from compatible Home cameras. You can then search your recordings using natural language

These AI-generated video descriptions provide a convenient summary of what’s happening in your home, let you browse important clips at a glance, and search the camera’s recent history of recordings.

The price ladder requirement for the 12TB plan in order to get 5 cameras is WAY TOO HIGH. I rather dedicate a local mac running some sort of AI model that can do this locally instead of paying that.

Apple charges $9.99, $29.99, and $59.99/month for each of these plans, respectively. So most users probably won’t find the monthly cost worth it for AI camera features alone. But if you already need a lot of iCloud storage, now you’ll get these new features thrown in too.

Note: these AI features have additional requirements too. You’ll need a home hub like a HomeView, Apple TV 4K, or HomePod, and your iPhone must support Apple Intelligence.
