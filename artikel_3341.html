CMA Awards Hit 7-Year Audience High With Taylor Swift’s Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Taylor Swift certainly brought her fans with her back to Country Music’s Biggest Night.

The 60th annual ceremony hit a seven-year audience high with 7.62 million total live + same-day viewers tuning in for the CMA Awards on Wednesday, per Nielsen Big Data + Panel.

The star-studded show, which featured Swift’s return to the CMA stage after 13 years to perform a mash up of Toy Story 5‘s “I Knew It, I Knew You” and her classic “Our Song,” also managed a 1.01 rating among adults 18-49 — a three-year best.

Hosted by Lainey Wilson for the third year in a row, the telecast was up 26% versus last year’s audience of 6.03 million, and ranked as the No. 1 program across broadcast and cable on Wednesday night.

Disney kicked things off the night before with Darius, Dolly and 60 Years of Celebration: Countdown to the CMAs with Robin Roberts. The special took viewers backstage ahead of the award show and earned 2.99 million views. The annual ABC News primetime special got its best viewership in three years. Watch on Deadline

The 60th Annual CMA Awards is a production of the Country Music Association. Robert Deaton serves as executive producer, Alan Carter is director, and Jon Macks is head writer.
