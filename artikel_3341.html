Techno Innovator Juan Atkins Will Be Honored With a 12-Hour Celebration of Life in Detroit

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The family of late techno legend Juan Atkins will celebrate his life and legacy at a special event in Detroit this Sunday, Nov. 22.

The public event will take place at the Lincoln Factory and run for 12 hours, starting at 2 p.m. and ending at 2 a.m. Fans will be able to RSVP to attend via electronic music website Resident Advisor. Full details about the event haven’t been revealed yet, including who might speak or perform. A statement shared on social media read, “The gathering will bring together family, friends, fellow artists and fans to honor his music and his influence. All are welcome. Please celebrate his life and work with us.”

Atkins died on Nov. 1 after being found unresponsive in a hotel room in Mexico City the day before. He was 64. An official cause of death has not yet been revealed, though Atkins had reportedly been battling health issues related to diabetes in recent years.

Born in Detroit, and raised in nearby Belleville, Michigan, Atkins played a pivotal role in the creation of Detroit techno, fusing elements of early Chicago house with the electronic music being made by groups like Kraftwerk and Yellow Magic Orchestra. Atkins began has career as a DJ before forming the group Cybotron, which released one influential album, Enter, in 1983. He went on to found his own label, Metroplex, and, under the moniker Model 500, released the seminal early Detroit techno classic, “No UFO’s,” in 1985. Atkins continued to record as Model 500 over the next several decades, though occasionally used other aliases and his own name.

“A great DJ is a leader. A mediocre DJ is a follower,” Atkins told Rolling Stone in 2024. “That’s what I try in DJ’ing and making music and presenting music. The public attention span is pretty short, and people are always interested in what’s next. Not so much what happened yesterday. Not so much what’s happening right now. But what’s next?”
