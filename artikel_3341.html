'Bachelorette' Alum Jordan Vandergriff Goes From Reality TV to NHRA Racing Stardom

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jordan Vandergriff went from receiving roses to burning rubber ... and now he's proving he's got what it takes to compete with the best in drag racing!!!

Jordan, who appeared on Gabby Windey and Rachel Recchia's Season 19 of "The Bachelorette" and Season 9 of "Bachelor in Paradise," just wrapped up a breakout 2026 season in the NHRA ... finishing third in the Funny Car championships during his rookie year behind the wheel of a 12,000 horsepower nitro machine.

But Jordan's road back wasn't a straight shot ... after competing in 11 Top Fuel events in 2019, he spent years away from the driver's seat, later returning as a FOX Sports NHRA reporter in 2024 and 2025.

He also made two Top Fuel appearances in 2025, stepping in for an injured driver. Those were his only races between the end of 2019 and the start of his 2026 comeback.

And what a comeback -- Jordan scored four Funny Car wins this season and finished third in the championship standings ... the highest placement among John Force Racing's four teams!

Not too shabby for a guy whose career once took a detour through reality TV!

With John Force Racing preparing for its 50th anniversary season in 2027, Jordan has given the legendary team plenty to be excited about!
