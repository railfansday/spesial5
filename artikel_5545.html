NRG Energy is selling $4 billion in natural-gas power plants to LS Power

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

NRG said proceeds from the 4.9-gigawatt sale will support additional share repurchases and debt reduction

NRG Energy agreed to sell six power-generation assets totaling 4.9 gigawatts in a $4 billion deal, the company said on Monday. The buyer is LS Power, which will pay $4 billion in cash for the natural gas-fired plants, according to The Wall Street Journal.

The sale is slated to be finalized during 2027’s second quarter, pending regulatory clearances such as Hart-Scott-Rodino and other standard closing requirements, NRG said.

NRG said proceeds will support its 2027 capital allocation plan, additional share repurchases, debt reduction and flexibility to redeploy capital at higher returns. The plan also calls for raising the yearly common dividend, according to The Wall Street Journal.

"Today's announcement is about returning more to our shareholders and supporting future growth investments," NRG CEO Robert Gaudette said, according to The Wall Street Journal.

After the sale is completed, NRG will own and operate 20 GW of generation capacity, the company said. Barclays is serving as lead financial adviser on the transaction. NRG said it will discuss the deal on its earnings conference call.

NRG stock rose 6.4% to $93.96 in premarket trading Monday.

NRG announced the sale with its third-quarter results. The company reported third-quarter net income of $561 million, up from $152 million a year earlier. Adjusted earnings were $4.68 a share, compared with $2.78 a share in the year-earlier quarter.

NRG also issued 2027 guidance of $8.05 to $10.05 in adjusted earnings a share. That range excludes the expected effects of the sale.
