Iraq's oil output climbed to its highest level in seven years, the IEA said

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Gulf crude exports reached 18.4 million barrels a day in October, still 26% below pre-war levels, the IEA said

Iraq pumped 4.6 million barrels of crude a day in October, an increase of 540,000 barrels a day from the previous month, the International Energy Agency said in its monthly oil market report on Friday.

That was the highest level since September 2019, according to Bloomberg.

Iraq's output ran 170,000 barrels a day above its implied target of 4.43 million, the IEA said. The agency put the country's sustainable capacity at 4.87 million barrels a day.

Gulf producers excluding Oman exported 18.4 million barrels a day in October, which is 26% below pre-war levels, the IEA said. Those exports rose by 950,000 barrels a day from the prior month, according to Bloomberg. Producers in the region are sending some cargoes on low-profile tanker runs across the Strait of Hormuz and are shipping other crude by alternative export routes that never touch the strait.

Global oil supply rose by 2.3 million barrels a day to 105.4 million in October, the IEA said. That followed a 3 million barrel-a-day increase in September. The agency said the gains came from high Gulf exports despite continuing attacks on energy infrastructure and tankers.

Saudi Arabia produced 9.99 million barrels a day in October, up from 8.93 million in September, the IEA said. Kuwait produced 2.03 million barrels a day, up from 1.58 million.

The IEA's estimates differ from figures that Gulf producers submitted to OPEC. Iraq told OPEC it produced 4.888 million barrels a day in October, above the IEA's 4.6 million. Saudi Arabia told OPEC it pumped 10.563 million barrels a day, compared with the IEA's 9.99 million.

The recovery in Gulf output follows disruption from the U.S.-Israeli war on Iran, which began in late February.
