Nationwide Equities debuts ReverseReach tool for mortgage broker lead generation help

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Nationwide Equities has launched ReverseReach, a tool and help desk program that lets mortgage brokers and loan officers screen their existing customer databases for reverse mortgage opportunities without deep product expertise. The platform pairs a simple scenario runner with access to full-time underwriters, aiming to lower barriers to reverse lending and give originators a way to generate new business from past clients amid a challenging forward mortgage market.  AI Summary

Nationwide Equities Corp. has launched ReverseReach, a new tool and support program aimed at helping mortgage brokers and loan officers uncover reverse mortgage opportunities in their existing customer databases and referral networks, the company announced Tuesday.

The New Jersey-based wholesale reverse mortgage lender said ReverseReach is designed for originators who may not specialize in reverse products but have years of past customers who are now older, equity rich and potential candidates for reverse mortgages.

“Mortgage brokers may be sitting on substantial untapped business opportunities without even realizing it,” Paul Lamparillo, CEO of Nationwide Equities, said in a statement. “Over the years, brokers have built relationships with thousands of homeowners, many of whom may now qualify for reverse mortgage products.

“We created ReverseReach to make uncovering those highly lucrative opportunities simple, efficient and accessible — even for brokers who have never closed a reverse mortgage before — so they can confidently and comfortably begin originating them.”

The company has largely pivoted to proprietary reverse mortgage production, mirroring recent changes in the broader industry. Modex data shows that Nationwide Equities has originated 111 reverse mortgages for $216 million in volume over the past 12 months, while data compiled by Reverse Market Insight shows that it has endorsed 26 Home Equity Conversion Mortgages (HECMs) through the first nine months of this year.

In 2020, Nationwide Equities launched its private-label reverse mortgage, EquityPower, which was built to offer higher loan proceeds than HECM products.

ReverseReach allows mortgage professionals to enter a borrower’s financial and property scenario and quickly gauge whether a reverse mortgage is worth pursuing. The program is positioned as a preliminary opportunity identification tool rather than a full underwriting engine.

Regardless of the initial result, brokers can connect with a live underwriter to discuss the file and potential structures, according to the company.

Nationwide Equities is pairing the technology with a help desk staffed by full-time Direct Endorsement (DE) underwriters who answer eligibility questions, help structure loans and walk brokers through complex borrower situations.

“One of the biggest obstacles to broader reverse mortgage adoption is simply recognizing when a borrower may be a good candidate,” Philip Parziale, president of Nationwide Equities said in a statement. “ReverseReach bridges that gap. Brokers don’t need to become reverse mortgage experts to identify a potential transaction. They need a simple way to recognize the opportunity and a knowledgeable lending partner to help them execute it.”

Parziale added that direct access to underwriters is a central part of the offering. “Regardless of how good your technology is, there is something special about being able to speak to a live person on the phone and walk you through a scenario and respond to follow ups,” he said.

The launch comes as volume in the broader forward mortgage market remains under pressure from higher rates, tighter affordability and intense competition for a smaller pool of qualified borrowers. Many retail and broker shops are looking to diversify into reverse mortgages, home equity products or nonqualified mortgages (non-QM) to stabilize production.

For brokers, the reverse space has historically been constrained by product complexity, reputation concerns and training requirements. ReverseReach aims to lower that barrier by giving originators a simple screening workflow across their existing loan origination system and past client lists, backed by lender-side expertise.

Lenders are investing in education and simple tools to capture aging-in-place demand from older homeowners with significant home equity. Brokers who have closed thousands of loans over the past decade may find that a meaningful share of these clients now meet age and equity thresholds for reverse products.
