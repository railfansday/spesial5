Sources: Celtic to accelerate exit as Anthony Ralston's stance clear

Celtic will be happy to sell right-back Anthony Ralston if he receives interest in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The veteran defender is firmly down the pecking order at Parkhead and is out of contract next summer.

Martin O'Neill has made it clear that the 27-year-old is effectively now third-choice right-back, and the player is not happy with that situation.

Sources say that an exit on a free next summer suits "all parties," unless the chance arises for Ralston to move on this winter.

He has been part of Celtic's senior squad since 2015, but Ralston is desperate to get back to playing regular football at this stage of his career.

The veteran has yet to feature in a single league game so far this season, and has played just 47 minutes of cameo roles in European action.

Ralston has been a key player for Celtic throughout his career, but his involvement has dwindled dramatically so far this season.

He played almost 1500 minutes of football across the Scottish Premiership and the Europa League last term, but has been firmly on the fringes of the squad of late.

O'Neill has made it clear that he won't be climbing the pecking order any time soon, and the player is now hoping to depart as quickly as possible.

He has been a mainstay in the Scotland squad in recent years, but his lack of game time prompted new boss Sebastien Pocognoli to leave him out of the squad back in September.

Ralston will be leaving Celtic when his contract expires at the end of the season, but both he and the club are prepared to accelerate that in January if the chance arises.

Football Insider revealed on Tuesday that Celtic will "open the door" for midfielder Luke McCowan to leave the club in January, if they can bring in a replacement.

O'Neill recently suggested that the 28-year-old still has a future at the club, despite him playing just six minutes of football since the defeat to Rangers back in September.

Sources say that it has been a challenging few months for the former Dundee man, who was "scapegoated" by the fans for his team's Champions League exit.

O'Neill has backed McCowan of late, and he is not prepared to lose any of his key players this winter unless a replacement can be secured.
