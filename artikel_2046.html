How Ayden Heaven feels about leaving Manchester United as West Ham show interest in January deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

West Ham boss Scott Parker is determined to bolster his ranks in the winter transfer window. A striker and a left-back is on the WHUFC chief’s radar, and reports have said there is an interest in Manchester United’s Ayden Heaven too.

The 20-year-old could leave Old Trafford to find more game time, be it on loan or a permanent basis, and the Hammers are monitoring his situation. According to The Athletic, Heaven is keen to play more, but his true desire is to stay at Manchester United, fight for his place in the team and help their battle to rise out of the Premier League relegation zone.

However, his need to play could force the issue and bring a January move to fruition. Premier League clubs have made contact to gauge Heaven’s availability and there may be a decision to make over his future in the coming weeks.

Heaven started his academy career in the West Ham setup, leaving in 2019 to join Arsenal. He cemented himself as one to watch while with Arsenal, but a desire to find more first-team opportunities prompted a move to Manchester United in the winter of 2025.

He broke into the side under Ruben Amorim but has primarily played as a backup since. The London-born prospect is held in very high regard, but the need for immediate results at Manchester United means new boss Mauricio Pochettino would be taking a gamble in entrusting Heaven with a starting berth.

As a left-footed centre-back, Heaven is adept at progressing the ball from deep with a good passing ability and a willingness to carry the ball in dribbles. This aspect of his game was nurtured at Arsenal, and it has helped him slot in at Old Trafford.

He deals with aerial duels well for a player of his inexperience, but his limited senior game time has seen issues regarding anticipation and one-on-one defending arise. Heaven can develop in these scenarios over time though – he just needs regular exposure at first-team level.

A drop to the Championship could benefit Heaven. He would be backed to start regularly at West Ham, likely taking Max Kilman’s place to slot in alongside Konstantinos Mavropanos at centre-back.

Playing in a team that wants to challenge for promotion and play possession football would make for good exposure for Heaven. However, it would not be a surprise if his sights are set higher.

Heaven is a highly-rated prospect who has already played Premier League football, and he will be confident in his belief that he can deliver at top-flight level if entrusted with a regular role. It could make clubs in higher leagues more attractive than WHUFC, especially on a permanent basis.

The Hammers should keep track on his situation, but be aware that they could be outdone if the door does open for Heaven to move on from Old Trafford.
