Lewis Hall was superb for England in the wins over both Croatia and Spain.

The rest of the world suddenly catching up with what Newcastle United fans have said for a long time.

Lewis Hall is an elite talent and he has been proving that for NUFC across recent seasons.

The lifelong United fan is the best left-back England have, as well as being the most talented in the Premier League.

Very amusing to hear so many people in the media going on like they are the ones who have suddenly ‘discovered’ Lewis Hall.

Somebody who has known Lewis Hall for a very long time is Ed Brand.

Brand worked with him when Hall was an emerging talent at Chelsea.

Former Chelsea Under 18s Head Coach Ed Brand declaring to Sky Sports about Lewis Hall: “What a good kid he is. A lot of the time [with the Chelsea Under 18s] I was playing a 3-4-2-1 formation with 10s…I would put him as a 10 quite a bit because he had always been a central midfield player. If I played a 4-2-3-1 formation then I would play him as one of the 6s or the 8s instead.”

Ed Brand going on to explain how Lewis Hall was then asked to play other roles, left wing-back and eventually left-back: “There were players who were older than Lewis Hall, good players, [so] in certain games where I wanted to keep him in the team, he might have to play at left wing-back even though I knew at that point it was not his favourite position. He was a midfielder. I was going to him [ahead of playing in a match against Tottenham], ‘Look, this is how I will fit you in.’ I just wanted 11 footballers in every position because we wanted to make sure we got on the ball. He started to play there a little bit more, a little bit more, and it just went from there. Now he is seen in most football fans’ eyes as a left back. Whereas I know that growing up, he has always been an attacking player with the ability to get goals and run beyond.”

Brand going on to say: “Lewis Hall was excellent when he was a 10. But people adapt and see your football differently. He is so comfortable at left-back because of where he is used to playing. When you are a player with his ability, used to playing in the centre of the pitch, seeing the game 360, you get moved to full-back, you look at it and think it is easy. He can see everything. Full-backs in the modern game are so important with how they invert inside, they do a lot of stuff infield anyway. With his talent, he excels there. What is so impressive about Lew is how much he has improved upon his defensive side. You cannot play in the Premier League and not be able to defend to a certain level, He will continue to get better the more exposure he gets to that.”

My view for a long time has been that Lewis Hall is such a talented player, he could play anywhere on the pitch and excel.

Ed Brand looking to the future and saying: “He has done brilliantly…[and] in time  he will be one of those England internationals whose name will be one of the first on the sheet. I am biased but I already think there is a big enough case for it.”
