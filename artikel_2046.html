Celtic treble winner formally announces bid to become FIFA president

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Former Celtic defender Ramon Vega has formally announced his candidacy to become the next president of FIFA.

The ex-Switzerland international confirmed that he intends to stand in the FIFA presidential election scheduled for March 18, 2027 in Rabat, Morocco.

Vega, who enjoyed a memorable spell at Celtic during the 2000/01 Treble-winning campaign under Martin O'Neill, launched his bid with a strongly worded statement calling for major changes to the way world football is governed.

The 55-year-old declared that "football belongs to the people" as he outlined his desire to restore trust in the sport's governing body.

Vega said: "After more than a decade of preparation, I formally announce my candidacy for the Presidency of FIFA in 2027.

"I bring the mentality of a player, the discipline of a defender and decades of experience from the pitch to the boardroom.

"I defended my team on the field. Now I am ready to defend football itself, its integrity, its people and its future."

Vega also insisted his campaign was not motivated by "personal ambition, power or privilege", describing his candidacy as a push for reconciliation and fundamental change within world football.

He added: "No individual is greater than football. No institution should ever place itself above the game.

"This is not merely an announcement of candidacy. It is a call for fundamental change, a commitment to reconciliation and a vision for a new era of world football."

Vega remains well remembered by the Celtic support despite spending only half a season in Glasgow.

He joined O'Neill's side on loan from Tottenham in December 2000 and played his part in the Hoops completing a domestic clean sweep that season, including scoring twice in an Old Firm victory over Rangers.

The former defender has now set his sights on one of the biggest positions in the game as he prepares to challenge for the FIFA presidency in 2027.
