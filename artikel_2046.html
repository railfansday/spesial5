Why under-rated Nico Elvedi is Leeds United's best summer signing

Leeds United centre-back Tarik Muharemovic has received plenty of plaudits of late, but his defensive partner Nico Elvedi has arguably been a better acquisition.

Both stars arrived at Elland Road in the summer as part of the Whites' bid to revamp their defence and add more proven top-level quality to their backline.

Muharemovic joined from Sassuolo in a £34million deal, and already looks likely to be a player who Leeds can secure a significant profit on in future.

The Bosnian gem has attracted plenty of attention for his displays so far, and that has taken the limelight away from Elvedi, who has arguably been equally as impressive.

The former Borussia Monchengladbach man was someone who Leeds spent several years chasing, and it is now clear while Daniel Farke fought so valiantly to land him.

Elvedi is a veteran of the Bundesliga, but has transitioned admirably into the style of the Premier League, and deserves more attention than he has been getting of late.

Were it not for Muharemovic's early success at Elland Road, the move to bring in Elvedi would be lauded across the Premier League right now.

The Whites secured his services for just £8.5m, as they snapped him up one year before his contract with Monchengladbach was due to expire.

Elvedi boasts over 300 Bundesliga appearances to his name, alongside Champions League experience, and he has shown that with his quality of performances so far.

Throw in the fact that he's been a key part of the Switzerland national team across six international tournaments, and he was always likely to be a success in the Premier League.

He has already won the trust of Farke, who views him as one of the first names on the teamsheet right now.

Muharemovic is rightly getting plenty of credit, but the move to bring in Elvedi has arguably been equally as important for Leeds.

Football Insider revealed on Sunday that centre-back Jaka Bijol could potentially be heading towards the exit door at Elland Road next summer.

Leeds have had a very settled backline so far this season, and the Slovenian has found himself down the pecking order, despite impressing last term.

Sources say that Joe Rodon's return from injury has cast further doubt over Bijol's future, with the Welshman also a trusted figure for Farke.

Bijol was one of Leeds' most impressive performers throughout last season, but his time with the club could soon draw to a close.
