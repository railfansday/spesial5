Parker’s past threatens to haunt him over “dire football’ warning | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Ask any West Ham fan after last weekend’s 3-1 home loss to West Bromwich Albion whether they’d settle for winning ways at all costs and most would have instantly nodded their agreement.

The second three goal loss at home within eight days had caused the boos to ring out as Nuno’s West Ham train derailed spectacularly, in what was to prove his last game and his ultimate undoing.

Right now there’s a relief flooding around the club as a former hero has returned to take over from negative Nuno. Long may it continue.

However the suggestions are that Parker may not prove to be the all-conquering hero that West Ham seek, judging from his impact at previous clubs. Yes, he knows how to get out of this division and yes, he sure can fix a porous defence – Burnley’s statistics speak for that.

But according to footballleagueworld.co.uk’s article this morning, his time at Burnley divided supporters with his grim, gritty style of play offering little in the way of entertainment:

“The football’s dire. He will bore you to death, and he will cause a fanbase to turn through little snide little comments. He will cause the West Ham fanbase to turn on it.

And that has happened at every single club consistently.”

Few will complain if Scott Parker gets West Ham promoted

Writes their Burnley fan columnist Aaron Wright, condemning Parker’s management style – not results – in the strongest possible terms.

“This is the biggest job he could get, if he can’t get them promoted slash win the league, and if he can’t then keep them in the Premier League, he needs to rethink pretty much everything. He is never getting a bigger job than this and his reputation as a player has helped him get this one. It’s the most guaranteed promotion you will ever have.

“The West Ham fans won’t enjoy it, but I think there’ll be a good respect to it if it turns out how Burnley’s did. 33 games unbeaten is something else.”

Which will have to be a price worth paying, simply to climb back out of this league after the mess of last season and save West Ham from the financial armageddon that not getting promoted would surely deliver.

To paraphrase Jacob Steiberg, Guardian journalist, on Parker’s football shortcomings not succeeding in the Premier League: worry about that next season – first you have to make it back.
