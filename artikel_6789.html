Can Men Have Multiple Orgasms?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

One of the many reasons I love having a vagina is because the vagina famously has no refractory period—meaning that once I orgasm, I don’t technically need a break after one orgasm to experience another (unless I’m feeling super sensitive). In fact, I can have multiple Os at once. Iconic.

But I’m just finding out that people with penises might be able to do the same thing.

Yes, the classic male orgasm comes with an intermission, but some men can have two, three, or even more in a single sexual encounter. And no, we’re not talking about round two after a 20-minute break. Some men report orgasming again within seconds or minutes, sometimes without ejaculating at all.

So what’s going on physiologically? And can men actually learn to do this?

To understand how multiples are possible, it helps to know what happens during a typical male orgasm. “At climax, the brain processes intense pleasure while the nervous system coordinates involuntary, rhythmic contractions of the pelvic floor muscles,” explains board-certified urologist Dr. David Shusterman. Heart rate and breathing climb, muscle tension peaks, and then the body relaxes. If ejaculation accompanies the orgasm, semen moves into the urethra and is propelled out by muscular contractions.

Then comes the refractory period I mentioned earlier, a recovery phase in which another erection or orgasm is difficult or temporarily impossible. It can last anywhere from minutes to hours, or even longer. That’s the main reason multiples are harder for men. Male and female orgasms both involve the brain, sensory nerves, and rhythmic pelvic contractions, Dr. Shusterman says. The difference is what happens afterward.

That’s also where orgasm and ejaculation come in. They usually happen together, but they’re separate processes: orgasm is the pleasurable sensation of climax, and ejaculation is the physical release of semen. One can happen without the other, which may be why some men who report multiples skip the recovery phase altogether. Without ejaculation, they may stay sexually responsive.

Considering how much attention other aspects of men’s sexual health get, there’s surprisingly little research on this phenomenon. A 2016 review in Sexual Medicine Reviews, “Multiple Orgasms in Men—What We Know So Far,” estimated that fewer than 10 percent of men in their 20s and fewer than 7 percent of men over 30 are capable of them. The researchers stressed that the evidence was limited. They also identified two patterns. Sporadic multiple orgasms involve one orgasm, a wait of several minutes, and then another. Condensed multiple orgasms are a burst of two to four within seconds or a couple of minutes. Either way, they don’t always involve multiple ejaculations.

In a 1989 study in Archives of Sexual Behavior, researchers interviewed 21 men who reported multiple orgasms. Some had orgasms without ejaculation, both before and after an ejaculatory one, and some had a series of orgasms without losing their erections. More recently, a 2021 survey in The Journal of Sexual Medicine of 122 men found that people reported anywhere from two to 30 orgasms in a single encounter, though most said two to four. Most also kept their erections throughout and ejaculated with every orgasm.

A 2025 qualitative study in Sexual and Relationship Therapy interviewed 12 men who said they were capable of multiples. Ten said they could have them without ejaculating. Their techniques included mental and breathing control, pelvic floor muscle control, sex toys, and abstaining from sex beforehand. Some said they’d always been able to, while others learned over time.

Still, this was a very small study based on participants’ own accounts, not a controlled experiment. These techniques may be worth exploring, but there’s no guarantee they’ll turn anyone into a multiorgasmic man.

And if you’re not one, don’t panic. “Sexual satisfaction is not an orgasm count,” Dr. Shusterman says. “Having one orgasm, needing a longer recovery period, or not experiencing multiple orgasms does not by itself indicate a problem.”

Multiple orgasms might sound like the ultimate sexual superpower, but they’re hardly a requirement for a great sex life. Whether you’re having one, several, or none at all, the goal is to enjoy yourself.
