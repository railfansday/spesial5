Grand Theft Auto 6 has managed to get its claws into just about everyone. Honestly, I can't go anywhere without hearing something from the soundtrack or someone talking about how much fun it is to joyride around Leonida. And now it's even infested the sports I watch.

To keep non-Formula One fans up to speed, it was the Las Vegas Grand Prix over the weekend, one of the newer circuits on the calendar. Third place went to Oscar Piastri. In second place was his McLaren teammate and previous world champion for 2025, Lando Norris. And first place went to four-time F1 world champion Max Verstappen, not exactly very surprising given his history with the track. But it is a little more impressive when you take into account that Verstappen has also put 40 hours into GTA 6, much of which would have been before the race.

"I played a lot in the evening and in the morning in Vegas," he says in a stream (via IGN), correcting the idea that he actually spent the plane ride home playing GTA 6 instead of spending time doing so around the race. "I mean I can't go out anyways [in the evening and morning], and walk around."

Verstappen did, however, explain that the aspect that GTA 6 has likely affected the most is his ability to play Minecraft, which he had planned to stream later this week: "I'm so bad when I play too many games at the same time. I can't, I just can't."

GTA 6 to Minecraft is certainly a jump, but you can't really blame anyone for trying to maximise their time in Leonida right now. I'll also take any chance to spend every free moment playing the game, the only difference being that instead of subsequently competing in an F1 race and winning it, I spend the next day of work acting like a zombie—a poorly functioning one at that. I suppose people are just built differently, or at least that's what I'll tell myself while I'm on my third cup of coffee in three hours.
