Tanggal: 2026-11-04

While you can’t get anything that’s worth real money and you can’t use it to buy gifts for others, all the skins, emotes, and anything else you can buy with V-Bucks are free to Pirate Shop Token holders for two weeks. Either way, this is something players really, really want, and they’re doing whatever it takes to get it.
