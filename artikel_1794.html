Griezmann expecting Champions League-like atmosphere in MLS wildcard clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Antoine Griezmann is anticipating a Champions League-like playoff clash in the MLS Wild Card match between Orlando City SC and FC Cincinnati.

Orlando finished eighth in the Eastern Conference after a Decision Day win over the New England Revolution (1-0) and only finished above Cincinnati due to winning more regular-season games.

Griezmann joined Orlando in July but has made an impressive start to life in MLS, resulting in him being named the league's best newcomer on Tuesday.

And he is determined to make sure Orlando enjoy their Playoff run, which begins on Thursday as they go head-to-head with Cincinnati for a spot in the postseason proper.

Asked what his team-mates had told him to expect, Griezmann said: "They told me the atmosphere is huge.

"They're amazing games and not like in the regular season. There's more tactics and more passion. I think I will find a game like in Europe, like the Champions League.

"That's why I'm here; this is why I chose Orlando, because I want this type of game. I think we're ready."

Cincinnati had been on a four-game unbeaten streak (W3 D1) heading into Decision Day but lost their final regular-season match to Supporters' Shield winners Nashville SC.

The result meant they missed out on an automatic playoff spot, and they are looking for an immediate response.

And Jose Martinez is hoping to use his experience to help Cincinnati progress to the next round despite not being able to get out on the pitch as he continues to recover from an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury.

"You don't have to be the captain to be a leader. Everyone can be a leader," Martinez said.

"I've been indicating here and there, but I've played a lot of minutes in MLS. I know how the league plays, and so for me, it's about just gaining that confidence.

"I think I showed that I can be a leader, and Pat [Noonan], knowing him, trusting him from Philadelphia [Union] as well, I think it's less about trying to be a leader to correct things on the field, but knowing that I can demand more of my team-mates in the minutes I've played."

Griezmann (14 goals, eight assists) and Martin Ojeda (16 goals, five assists) each recorded 20+ goal contributions this season.

In Orlando's MLS history, there have been two other individual seasons with 20+ goal contributions (Ojeda – 30 in 2025, Nani – 22 in 2019).

Evander, Kevin Denkey and Tom Barlow each scored double-digit goals this season, giving Cincinnati three different players with 10+ goals in a regular season for the third time since 2022. That is the most such seasons of any team in MLS in that time.

Denkey led the way for them in the regular season, netting 17 goals, with only three of those coming from the penalty spot.

This is the first MLS Cup Playoffs meeting between Orlando and Cincinnati. While the all-time series is even (W5 D5 L5), Orlando have not won at home against Cincinnati since May 2021 and are winless in their last five (D2 L3) at home in this fixture.

However, Orlando allowed 78 goals this season, tied for the second-most conceded in a single regular season in MLS history (behind Sporting Kansas City's 81 this season).

They have never won an MLS Cup Playoffs match when conceding a goal (D2 incl. two penalty shoot-out victories, L6).

Cincinnati have won five of their eight home MLS Cup Playoffs matches but are winless in their last four on the road (D1 L3). Cincinnati's lone road playoff victory came in their first-ever such game as an MLS team with a 2-1 win over the New York Red Bulls in 2022.
