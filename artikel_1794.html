Bartesaghi fully focused on Frosinone amid ‘important’ month: “One game at a time”

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Ahead of AC Milan’s clash against Frosinone this evening, Davide Bartesaghi spoke to the media as he’s back playing from start.

After bagging two consecutive wins before the international break, Milan’s goal is to make it three tonight for the first time this season. While the Rossoneri are the favourites, Ruben Amorim warned yesterday that Frosinone could cause some problems for them.

One change has been made to the starting eleven, with Davide Bartesaghi replacing Alphadjo Cisse. The youngster’s form has been a little bit up and down lately, so this is a good opportunity for him to show that he should be starting regularly at left-back.

Speaking to Sky Italia just a few minutes ago, as transcribed by MilanNews, Bartesaghi shared his thoughts on the game and the fact that he’s starting in defence again, as opposed to in midfield/attack.

“This month is important, we know that, but let’s try to take it one game at a time. Let’s start by thinking about tonight, which is the most important thing.”

Tonight, you are back in the left-back role…

“It will certainly change, but the principles are the same. We’ve prepared for the match to the best of our ability and will try to give it our all.”
