Championship table predicted after fresh Middlesbrough FC, Sheffield United, Wolves, QPR & Stoke City boosts

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Opta have issued fresh Championship forecasts for Middlesbrough, Sheffield United, Wolves, QPR, Stoke City and more

Data experts Opta have deployed their supercomputer following a hectic weekend of Championship action.

Sheffield United were ruthless as they roared to a 4-0 victory over Watford, who responded to the defeat by parting with head coach Alessio Dionisi.

Middlesbrough also claimed three points on home turf, securing a 2-0 win over Michael Skubala’s Bristol City courtesy of goals from Adilson Malanda and David Strelec.

Away from Yorkshire, Queens Park Rangers picked up a 1-0 win away at Millwall and Norwich City were 2-1 victors over Cardiff City.

Southampton claimed a 2-0 win over Derby County, Bolton Wanderers and Portsmouth played out a 1-1 draw and Lincoln City were edged out by Wrexham.

Stoke City clinched a narrow win over Birmingham City, while Wolverhampton Wanderers defeated Charlton Athletic 2-1.

It ended 2-2 in the meeting between Swansea City and West Bromwich Albion and West Ham United were held to a 1-1 draw by Preston North End.
