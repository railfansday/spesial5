How to watch France vs Turkey: TV channel and live stream for Nations League today

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

France will have Kylian Mbappe back from injury for their crucial Nations League meeting with Turkey tonight.

Les Bleus’ captain and attacking talisman was absent on Thursday evening as Zinedine Zidane suffered his first defeat as manager of the national team, with Italy coming from behind to win 3-1 in Milan.

It was a hugely disappointing performance and result for France, leaving them sitting second on goal difference behind the resurgent Azzurri in Group A1 ahead of the crunch final round of fixtures.

Zidane has since emphatically denied any suggestion of a potential rift between himself and Mbappe, calling such claims “nonsense”, “annoying” and “upsetting”.

Mbappe should return in Bordeaux on Sunday night following a minor hamstring issue, with France looking to seal a place in the Nations League quarter-finals in March at the same time as Italy travel to face Belgium in Brussels.

They will be strong favourites to overcome a Turkey side who sit rock bottom of the group with no wins and zero points to their name in five attempts, having sacked head coach Vincenzo Montella last month.
