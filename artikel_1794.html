Ramos remains dry while Milan score plenty: Dissecting the attacking paradox

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

AC Milan are certainly not struggling to score goals in Serie A, and yet big question marks remain around the attack’s focal point.

With 23 goals in 11 league games, Milan find themselves among the top scorers in the league. However, not all is rosy. There is a big gap to the teams at the top, while in the Europa League Ruben Amorim’s side have failed to score in three of four games.
