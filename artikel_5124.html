XRP Price Near Crucial Support That Could Trigger $3.50 Bull Run

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

XRP price could bottom out at $0.78, according to analyst Ali Martinez.

In a recent post on X, Martinez shared a chart with what appears to be an ascending triangle pattern. The triangle's closest low is at $0.78.

XRP has already fallen 43% from its October high of $1.55. According to Martinez, the correction might continue until the price reaches the bottom.

If the price holds $0.78, a possible bull run toward $3.50 might follow.

"A weekly breakout above $3.50, followed by a successful retest as support, could confirm the multi-year ascending triangle and trigger a bull run to $18," Martinez wrote.

On the weekend, XRP price recovered 7% from its previous crash, but couldn't reach the $1 level.

The RSI fell below 25 before the latest recovery, which suggests oversold conditions.

A rebound following a massive sell-off does not equal a trend reversal.

In October, the 200 EMA acted as strong resistance that XRP was unable to break.The $1 mark is the first psychological threshold XRP needs to reclaim, acting as both support and resistance.

The $1.3 level is where the breakdown started and where volatility spiked. The 200 EMA sits near $1.33 right now.
