Pinterest shares its first Creators to Watch list

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The platform highlighted 20 global influencers for 2027, which could offer valuable partnership insights for brands.

Pinterest has published a listing of 20 Pinterest creators to watch in 2027, which is part of the platform's expanded forecast for the year ahead. The list is designed to help users and marketers understand who some of the key trend setters are, which could help to guide their following and partnership decisions.

Pinterest’s inaugural collection of creators to watch includes a range of platform influencers across various categories.

As explained by Pinterest: “These 20 global creators are driving real-life trends across fashion, beauty, home, and beyond. Through inspiring Pins that reach millions of users every year, they are guiding people toward creating a life they love and are the key creators to watch heading into 2027 for hints about the trends of the future.”

Pinterest added that creators play a crucial role in driving platform trends, with 83% of Pinterest users indicating that in-app trends are informed by expert opinions.

For marketers, the list could help to highlight some of the best creators to work with on campaigns in order to enhance resonance and link into trending voices.

Last month, Pinterest added the ability for brands to sponsor creator Pins as another means to facilitate monetization and targeted promotion in the app.

Maybe these influencers could offer additional, valuable targets for this approach.
