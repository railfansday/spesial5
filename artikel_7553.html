Virginia school district under federal investigation after failing to comply with state background check rules

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The U.S. Department of Education is investigating Albemarle County Public Schools in Virginia, which temporarily closed all of its schools after failing to perform a state-required background checks on hundreds of employees.
