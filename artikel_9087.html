Taylor Swift’s Costumes and Instruments Are Coming to the MFA Boston

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Taylor Swift’s sellout tour “The Eras Tour” traveled to 21 countries and 51 cities between 2023 and 2024 with many outfit changes and props on tour that became viral. A selection of those rhinestone-covered costumes, among other archive pieces, will be the subject of an exhibition at the Museum of Fine Arts in Boston, titled “Taylor Swift: The Eras Collection,” scheduled to run from April 10, 2027 through August 15, 2027.

Fans can expect to see instruments and objects representing the 12 eras of her musical career, starting “Tim McGraw,” a single on Swift’s self-titled debut album in 2006, including a custom red Alberta Ferretti gown worn during the “Folklore” and “Evermore” sets in 2020; a bejeweled Zuhair Murad bodysuit that she wore in 2022; and more from her latest album, 2025’s “The Life of a Showgirl”. “This is the first exhibition in the MFA’s history devoted to a musician, and we’re thrilled to invite visitors for an up-close look at the fashion and instruments of one of the most influential cultural figures of our time,” Kristen Hoskins, the museum’s director of public programs, said in a statement. “I’m excited to welcome audiences across generations to this unique experience at the museum—especially Swifties, whose passion and community building are an essential part of Taylor’s story.”

Beyond dresses, the exhibition will provide deeper look at her practice. The show will include several instruments, such as a Deering banjo from the “Speak Now World Tour” in 2010, a green Gibson J-180 acoustic guitar from the “Reputation Stadium Tour” in 2017 and a mandolin from the music video for “Willow” in 2020. The museum said its goal is to take Swifties, and non-Swifties, on a journey through the super star’s career, which currently boasts more than 245 million album sells worldwide.

“With over a century of experience celebrating the art of musical instruments and fashion, the MFA is the perfect place to host an exhibition exploring Taylor Swift’s exceptional artistry,” Jared Katz, the museum’s curator of musical instruments, said in a statement.

The MFA is not the first museum to dedicate gallery space to Swift. In 2024, the Victoria and Albert Museum in London showcased 16 looks worn by the pop star during her “The Eras Tour,” which was attended by Tom Cruise to Prince William, and his children, Princess Charlotte and Prince George.
