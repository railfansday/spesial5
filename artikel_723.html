Ta-Nehisi Coates, Kamilah Forbes and Kenyatta Matthews Formally Launch Maceo-Lyn Productions

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The production company is backing 'When A Witness Recants' and the film adaptation of 'The Water Dancer,' among other projects.

Ta-Nehisi Coates, Kamilah Forbes and Kenyatta Matthews are formally announcing their company, Maceo-Lyn Productions, with the premiere of the HBO Original documentary When A Witness Recants.

The documentary, which airs Nov. 17, is one of several projects at the production company, including the upcoming feature film adaptation of The Water Dancer, the debut novel from Coates. Forbes is also directing the Broadway play Mix and Master, starring Tony Award winners Kara Young and Ruben Santiago-Hudson, in January 2027, as well as Boom for Real, a new musical in development about Jean-Michel Basquiat with a score by John Legend.

The company was formed in 2020, with the documentary film adaptation of Coates’ Between the World and Me, as their first project. Forbes directed the film, and helmed the prior stage adaptation.

While Coates said he didn’t set out to create a production company (“I actually wanted nothing to do with Hollywood at all,” Coates said), he valued working with close collaborators on the project. Matthews and Forbes became friends in middle school, and then met Coates at Howard University, with Matthews later marrying Coates.

“I think there was a level of trust between the three of us, and it was quite clear that we probably would continue to work in that space after this, even though it wasn’t a thing I necessarily saw for myself. I just did not want to be in business with people who I did not have a deep understanding of, and who did not have a deep understanding of me, both aesthetically and ideologically,” Coates told The Hollywood Reporter.

Forbes, former executive producer at the Apollo, notes that they’re prioritizing quality over quantity, with the projects meant to appeal to their varied backgrounds in journalism and writing, theater, as well as social activism, with a focus on Black storytelling and moving the culture forward.

“I think it comes to what’s moving us?” Forbes said. “I think our partnership is really unique simply because we come to storytelling from several different corners.”

“What moves the needle for us. What are the stories that we’re just haunted by?,” added Matthews. In the case of When A Witness Recants, which follows the wrongful murder conviction of three Baltimore teenagers, Matthews notes that she became fixated on the story after staying up late reading the 2021 New Yorker article. Coates, who grew up in the same neighborhood, had been similarly fixated by the case.

Future work is expected to span across film, television and theater, scripted and non-scripted, with the trio teasing an upcoming documentary about historical figures.

“One of the things that we really do prize at Maceo-Lyn, we value words. I mean, we can’t not with Ta-Nehisi being one of the three prongs of the company, and so we always start with what is the written piece that moves us. Be it a script, be it a magazine article, be it a play,” Matthews said.

Coates noted that he is excited to see the feature film adaptation of his debut novel, about a man born into slavery with the power to transport himself long distances, which has been in the works since 2019 with Brad Pitt’s Plan B.

“There’s been some changes in the industry that presented some challenges, but I think nonetheless both Kenyatta and Kamilah and our partners over at Plan B, we’re really excited about bringing that to film. It’s one of these things where you can’t get it wrong. I mean, something about making a bad slavery film is significantly worse than just making a bad film,” Coates said.
