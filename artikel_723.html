Pete Davidson, Elsie Hewitt Show Co-Parenting Dynamic on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Pete Davidson and ex Elsie Hewitt proved they are nailing co-parenting as Hewitt shared a cute glimpse into their Thanksgiving reunion alongside daughter Scottie, 11 months.

Pete Davidson and Elsie Hewitt had a stinking fun Thanksgiving.

The model gave fans a peek into her holiday reunion with the Saturday Night Live alum and their 11-month-old daughter Scottie as she documented the less than scent-sational smell they discovered.

“OK, we're driving through Jersey right now, and it smells so bad,” Hewitt told the camera in a Nov. 27 TikTok as she covered her face with a red scarf. She then turned to Davidson in the driver’s seat, asking him, “What did you have to say?”

To which he playfully responded, “You asked me if I farted. And I said, ‘No.’ And I know it’s not me because I don’t like it.”

The two burst out into a fit of laughter before Hewitt shut off the camera, captioning the video of the duo, “Coparenting.”

But their adventures were far from over that night as she also shared a look at her Christmas tree, which appeared to have sound activated lights.

In another video posted to TikTok, with the caption, “See what I did there,” the 30-year-old showed off her singing skills, doing her best version of the famous riff from Wicked’s “Defying Gravity,” as she attempted to get the tree to light up all the way.

She then panned the video to Davidson who quipped, “Go off, queen,” as Hewitt urged him to try lighting the tree with his own voice. And the 33-year-old was not messing about, as he came in with a deep, “Oh,” which made the tree almost fully light up.

Of course, this is hardly the first time the How to Rob a Bank star and Hewitt have proven that they’ve nailed co-parenting since their split in May, just months after welcoming Scottie in December.

In fact, Hewitt and Davidson also reunited for Halloween, as they dressed up Scottie as Tony Soprano from The Sopranos—a move that Davidson shared was all his ex.

“I’ve got to give Elsie credit, she did the whole wardrobe,” he raved on The Tonight Show Nov. 9. “She crushed it.”

“Before she was even born me and Elsie were like, ‘We gotta.’ Because her hair’s gonna grow. She’s got the perfect Tony hair, because she has no hair,” Davidson—a longtime fan of The Sopranos—joked. “It’s the only year we could.”
