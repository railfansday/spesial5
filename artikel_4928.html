F1 Academy Is Coming to Apple TV, and Its Drivers Are Ready for Their Close-Up

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The all-female racing series is getting a bigger platform in the U.S.

Starting in 2027, Apple TV will become the exclusive U.S. streaming home of F1 Academy, the all-female racing series developing the next generation of talent.

Fans will be able to watch every qualifying session and race on Apple TV, bringing more visibility to a series that is quickly gaining momentum. It also expands Apple's partnership with Formula 1, which began its five-year U.S. broadcast deal with the streaming service in 2026.

For Austrian driver Emma Felbermayr, who represents the Audi Revolut F1 team in F1 Academy, the announcement is particularly exciting.

“Apple TV is such a big platform, and it's all about F1 Academy getting more attention,” Felbermayr told Women's Health in Las Vegas ahead of the Grand Prix. “It just makes the interest into the sport much bigger, and that's what we need. We need to have more girls interested in the sport so that we get more attention. In the end, we want to get seen by people."

Launched in 2022, F1 Academy is a Formula 4-level development series designed to create more opportunities for women in motorsport. And its impact extends beyond the racetrack.

At a private dinner in Las Vegas celebrating the Apple TV announcement, F1 Academy managing director Susie Wolff reflected on the series' rapid rise.

“It's incredible how the sport has shifted in the last five to seven years,” Wolff said. “Women have a huge part to play in that shift, and that has been part of the reason why F1 Academy has had so much success in the short space of time.” The dinner event, co-hosted by Wolff and F1 Chief Commercial Officer Emily Prazer, brought together women from across the worlds of sports, entertainment, and advocacy, including activist Malala Yousafzai and model Kelly Piquet.

That growth is personal for Felbermayr, who says F1 Academy has helped her become more confident both in and out of the car. Once a self-described shy person, the 19-year-old has learned to navigate interviews, limited track time, and the pressure of competing at a high level.

“I got so much better in the language of English. That's maybe just a small part of it, but just getting more confident in front of the camera, but also in the car,” says Felbermayr, who just signed on for a third F1 Academy season with Audi. “Every challenge grows yourself as a person, and I think that's something I really saw in the last two years.”

With seven race weekends on the 2027 calendar, including stops in Austin and Las Vegas, American fans will have plenty of opportunities to get acquainted with F1 Academy's rising stars. And with Apple TV bringing their races to a wider audience, more young girls will have the chance to see what's possible behind the wheel.
