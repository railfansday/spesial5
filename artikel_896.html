Is Kylie Kelce Ready for Baby No. 5 With Jason Kelce?

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Kylie Kelce—mom to daughters Wyatt, 7, Elliotte, 5, Bennett, 3, and Finnley, 19 months, with husband Jason Kelce—addressed whether she's considering expanding their family once more: "We enjoy chaos."

Kylie Kelce is not gonna lie about her thoughts on adding another player to her team.

The 34-year-old—who shares daughters Wyatt, 7, Elliotte, 5, Bennett, 3, and Finnley, 19 months, with husband Jason Kelce—weighed in on the possibility of having a fifth baby in the future.

“It’s funny,” Kylie shared on the Nov. 4 episode of her Not Gonna Lie podcast when her guest Joanna Gaines asked if she’d be open to having more kids. “That was one of the questions that we talked about.”

As a mom of five herself, Joanna—who shares kids Drake, 21, Ella, 20, Duke, 18, Emmie, 16, and Crew, 8, with husband Chip Gaines—encouraged Kylie to have one more kid.

“Five is a good number,” the Fixer Upper star noted to Kylie, who asked, “Is it?”

“And it’s gonna be a boy,” Joanna added. “I think you should do it.”

While Kylie laughed at the interior designer’s quip about the possibility of having a boy after four girls, she appeared intrigued, especially when Joanna noted that she thinks Kylie and Jason—who married in 2018—“thrive in chaos.”

“We do,” Kylie admitted. “We enjoy chaos.”

“You think one more?” the podcast host asked Joanna with a smile, to which the HGTV star  replied, “Yes, obviously, give it some time.”

“Kylie, bust it out, five,” she continued, adding further encouragement. “It’s gonna be your little boy and it's gonna rock your world because it's totally different than girls and it's another spot in your heart. Or if it's a girl, great, ‘cause five girls is a party house.”

And Kylie couldn’t help but highlight that the number five does have quite the ring to it, especially in sports terms.

After all, she noted, “It's a starting five, isn't it?”

Although the Not Gonna Lie host didn’t give a direct answer about expanding her and Jason’s team, she did make it clear that she was taking Joanna’s words to heart.

“OK, if we have a fifth child, I'm gonna come right back to this moment right here,” Kylie shared with a laugh, before joking, “‘Jo did it!’”

But Joanna ended the topic by throwing in one last play.

As she told Kylie, “Let me do the nursery!”

While time will tell what the future holds, Kylie didn’t hesitate to tackle the bodyshamers who speculated she was expecting over the summer after she shared photos from a trip to Paris.

“Stop trying to get me to have more. Stop it,” she stressed on a July 30 episode of Not Gonna Lie. “That's between me, my husband, and my motherf--king uterus.”

As she emphasized, “Even if, which I am not, even if I was with child, that is between me and my husband to decide when we would like to share that information.”
