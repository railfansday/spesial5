Yooka-Laylee And The Impossible Lair Is Getting A Definitive Edition On Switch 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Publisher Playtonic is bringing Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair: Definitive Edition to the Switch 2 on 15th December 2026.

While the original release was already critically-acclaimed for its Donkey Kong-inspired platforming, the Switch 2 version will boast improved visuals in both docked and handheld modes. For those who value high performance, a 120fps mode will also be included.

Best of all, if you own the original on Switch, you can upgrade to the new release for absolutely nothing (except the 30 seconds or so it will take to navigate the eShop).

Here's a look at the full roster of Switch 2 features:

Higher resolutions in both docked and handheld modes

- Enhanced lighting and ambient occlusion effects.

In our original review for Impossible Lair, we called it "a fantastic sophomore effort that pays tribute to Rare's past and establishes Playtonic as one of the UK's most exciting studios", awarding it a score of 9/10.
