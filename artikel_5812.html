Nextnova unveils solar cell manufacturing facility

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The new state-of-the-art factory in Oklahoma City will create about 300 jobs, strengthen the domestic solar supply chain, and establish a new benchmark for transparency and quality.

This month, Nextnova celebrated the official inauguration of its new solar cell manufacturing facility in Oklahoma City, marking an important milestone in strengthening America’s domestic solar manufacturing industry.

The facility will manufacture high-efficiency solar cells using advanced automation, Industry 4.0 technologies, and comprehensive digital traceability.

Production is scheduled to begin in March 2027 with an initial annual capacity of 2 GW, designed to expand to 5 GW as market demand grows.

The new factory is expected to create approximately 300 skilled jobs in engineering, manufacturing, quality assurance, and operations, supporting economic growth while reinforcing the U.S. clean energy supply chain.

Nextnova celebrates the inauguration of its new solar cell manufacturing facility in Oklahoma City. Credit: Nextnova

“Today represents an exciting milestone for Nextnova and for American solar manufacturing,” said Nathan Kii Miller, Nextnova’s chief strategy officer, in a statement. “Our Oklahoma City facility combines advanced manufacturing technology, complete supply chain transparency, and rigorous quality standards to deliver premium solar cells while strengthening a resilient domestic solar industry.”

Designed for maximum quality and reliability, the facility features fully integrated manufacturing execution systems (MES), automated inspection technologies, advanced process control, and a dedicated analytical laboratory supporting continuous quality verification throughout production.

Nextnova notes that its commitment to transparent sourcing, manufacturing excellence, and scalable production provides customers with high-performance solar cells that support the continued growth of a secure and competitive U.S. solar industry.
