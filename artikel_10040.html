This is why I do not trust remote github CI I don't control.

I've found a few open source projects with easy access to their GH action secrets used in workflows triggered by forks. It is ridiculously trivial to do that and even after I notified the projects maintainers. Most of them just kept things as-is since convenience is often more important than security.

I really cannot recommend GH actions for enterprise use beyond basic use without secrets since they are just too dangerous. I'm surprised these flaws are not exploited more.
