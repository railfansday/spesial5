Restaurant hits out at “miserable and entitled” diners after viral outrage over tips rule

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A restaurant in North Carolina has hit out at “miserable and entitled” customers after their rules about tipping went viral on social media, and people thought that the answer would be to review bomb them.

Tipping in restaurants and bar has long been a divisive topic, with different places handling it in their own unique ways. Some establishments put an automatic gratuity charge on bills, while others still leave it up to the customer.

On November 15, the Freeman’s Grub & Pub in Greensboro, North Carolina, come under fire on the EndTipping subreddit after one user flagged a notice about their tipping rules.

“If our employees have two separate instances of you not tipping well without explanation, we will add a 20% autograt fee on your bill,” the rules state. “Your receipts will be saved if this is the case, and you are welcome to question it,” they also noted, urging customers to leave a name and number if they have any complaints.

The restaurant’s co-owner, Kevin Purcell, responded to the backlash on November 16, noting that he had been hit with review bombs online.

“It is 2026, and you should know by now the rules of engagement and expectations to tip at a bar or restaurant. Our staff is beyond competent; they’re professionals at what they do. You’re expected to tip for the great to excellent service that they provide,” Purcell said.

“Servers everywhere talk s*it when you leave about how pathetic it is that you still don’t know the very basic rules of dining out at this point in your miserable and entitled existence.”

Purcell added that if there are genuine mistakes, then the restaurants does assist on that front, but they “take pride in providing a top-tier dining experience.”

He noted that the restaurant will “stand behind our controversial policy and amazing staff,” despite what may come.

The response has garnered plenty of sympathy and fightback from customers who want to support the restaurant too. “I have never been to your restaurant but will go soon,” one said.

“Just left a review! You guys are the best, it’s great seeing restaurant owners truly care about their staff” another commented. “Thank you for being an exemplary business,” another added.
