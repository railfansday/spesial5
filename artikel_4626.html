Errors in judging friendships predict who will connect or drift apart six months later

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Decades of research show that mental representations of social networks are riddled with errors, with people commonly misidentifying existing ties and reporting relationships that do not exist. While these inaccuracies have been interpreted as noise arising from the limits of memory or attention, Brown University researchers have discovered a much more intriguing explanation: These "errors" are accurate representations of social connections that just haven't happened yet.
