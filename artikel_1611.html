El Nacional: Milan ‘on alert’ as Chelsea hope to offload Palestra to bring Kounde

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

In January, AC Milan could make a major move for a new right-back, with a former target back in the window.

There has been plenty said about Milan and a move for a defender in January. In recent months, a central option has been the priority, but it was reported yesterday that a left-back could be the new number one.

However, today, they’ve been linked to a right-wing back in Marco Palestra.

According to El Nacional, via Football Italia, Chelsea – and more specifically, Xabi Alonso – are looking to sign a new right-sided defender and a move for Jules Kounde is very possible.

Hansi Flick is open to a sale and for €50 million, the Frenchman could move to London.

So, where do the Rossoneri come into this? Well, to help a move, Alonso would be open to letting Palestra leave in the January mercato and there would be no shortage of options.

Indeed, Milan, Juventus and Napoli are all ‘on alert’ to the situation and could make another attempt to sign him. Presumably, a fee would need to be involved if Kounde is coming in for €50m. However, they’d be unlikely to make a profit on the €60m paid for the Italian a few months ago.

Considering they’ve got Alexis Saelemaekers and Samuel Chukwueze to come back, as well as Zachary Athekame being on loan. It feels like a strange move for the Rossoneri to potentially make…
