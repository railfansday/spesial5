CorSport: Barcelona set €50m price for Balde as Milan plot loan bid – the situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

AC Milan are looking at left-backs ahead of the winter transfer window, with a player at Barcelona having caught their eye.

Corriere dello Sport (via Radio Rossonera) report this morning that Gerry Cardinale and the incoming Head of Football Markus Krösche want to strengthen in the wide areas, and Alejandro Balde is the latest name on their list.

Throughout the season, it’s become clear that one of the Rossoneri’s weakest points is the left side of the defence. Pervis Estupinan has never shown himself to be up to par since arriving at Milan, while Davide Bartesaghi has been inconsistent.

Milan’s transfer team are therefore looking around to find the right man for Amorim, and Barcelona’s Balde appears to be one of the possible names. However, the paper claims that the Blaugrana want €50m for a permanent sale, amid potential competition too.

There are a couple of avenues that the Diavolo can utilise to try and aid their pursuit. Firstly, there is the fact that Balde’s agent is Jorge Mendes, someone who is already well acquainted with the top brass at Casa Milan.

Secondly, there is the idea of deferring the expense. The formula could be a loan with an option to buy, which could then become an obligation under certain conditions. This would help soften the potential €50m blow.

That being said, Cardinale has repeatedly stated that he isn’t afraid to spend if the target is deemed the ideal player. It’s therefore a matter of evaluating the 23-year-old’s characteristics and to get a definitive opinion from the coach.
