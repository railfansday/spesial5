Opinion: Proof that Rio Ngumoha is ready to start every week

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Rio Ngumoha was always destined to be a superstar, and he is continuing his excellent development at Anfield this season.

The 18-year-old has appeared in all but two games for the Reds this campaign, making eight starts and recently giving Andoni Iraola a genuine selection headache.

Having only made 11 starts in the entirety of last season, it is clear that the wonderkid is being trusted with more responsibility under the new regime in Merseyside.

Sunday's demolition of Manchester United was a breakout moment for the teenager and was a clear message to the coaching staff in L4 that Rio is ready to start every game for Liverpool going forward.

Rio Ngumoha is a real superstarThere are few bigger games for Liverpool than clashes against Manchester United, and Ngumoha leaving his mark on Sunday's win will be remembered as a key moment in the youngster's rise. With a goal and an assist from the left-wing, Rio produced a performance that earned him praise as one of the best players on the pitch. One of the standout statistics from the Anfield blow out was that Rio had 20 touches inside United's box, the most any player has had in the opponent's box in the Premier League this season, per WhoScored.

Cody Gakpo's absence from the team offered a perfect opportunity for Rio to show what he can do as a starter on the left, and he grabbed the opportunity with both hands.

The fluid nature of Iraola's front three this season have seen almost every wide player at the club play on both wings at times, and Rio has shone on both the left and the right when trusted.

With one goal and three assists already, the teenager is shaping up to be a real asset for Liverpool, and he has a real case to be starting every single game even when all his competition are healthy.
