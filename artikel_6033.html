Booking’s metasearch company is betting that live prices and comparison tools, not just conversations, will keep travelers from starting their search on Google or a chatbot.

Kayak debuted a raft of AI updates Tuesday, including bringing flexible-date flight searches and price alerts into Ask AI. The goal, the company said, is to let travelers find dates that fit their budget and compare bookable fares within the same conversation.

The metasearch platform announced 28 new and enhanced AI-related features Tuesday, spanning flight, hotel, and rental car searches. Ask AI is now available on iOS and Android.

The Booking Holdings company has long offered flexible-date searches and Price Alerts through traditional search. Ask AI itself launched in the U.S. in April with live search results.

Kayak announced 28 new and enhanced AI features Tuesday, pulling flexible-date flight searches, Price Alerts, and day-by-day itinerary building into Ask AI, its conversational search tool, which is now available on iOS and Android. Ask AI uses data behind Kayak's Buy vs. Wait tool to suggest promising dates, then checks live fares for bookable options. Kayak said 30% of sampled Ask AI conversations since the tool's U.S. launch in April involved flexible or open-ended requests, and that travelers who engage with Ask AI are about 2.5 times as likely to book — though the company did not disclose underlying booking rates, and the comparison does not establish causation. The updates arrive as Google brings more travel features into AI Mode, including hotel booking for U.S. travelers starting in August. At Skift Data + AI Summit Europe in London on Oct. 6, Kayak CEO Peer Bueller said Google's Gemini interfaces and carousels were pushing metasearch results farther down the page, turning traffic that once arrived organically into paid acquisition. Asked whether Kayak could recover those costs, Bueller said, "The initial thing is a step back in economics."
