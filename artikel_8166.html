Three Ground Rules for Getting Outside

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Getting outside is for everyone. That’s what Sierra is all about, finding top active and outdoor brands at prices that make it accessible to try a bunch of new adventures. That’s the spirit behind Sierra in the Sierras, in which Dylan Efron (Emmy-winning co-producer of Netflix’s Down to Earth with Zac Efron) hits the trail with a friend to try something completely new.

With gear and apparel from Sierra and guidance from Outside editors, in episode one Dylan explores the Sierra Nevada with Danielle Fishel, Boy Meets World icon and Dancing With the Stars co-star. Along with new adventures, they learn some fundamental principles for having fun outside. Ones you can use yourself.

Whether it’s your first hike or just your first hike today, no one likes to be uncomfortable. The right gear helps. If you’re like Danielle, a self-proclaimed “indoor person,” or simply trying a new activity, you’ll want to focus on comfort and versatility above all else. The aim is to A) feel good while enjoying your new hobby and B) have the freedom to explore further as you progress. The gear choices at Sierra make this easy, thanks to a combo of trusted brands, great prices, and an ever evolving inventory of fresh finds in-store and online.

Like most things in life, getting outside is better with friends. In the first episode of Sierra in the Sierras, Dylan recruits Outside’s Climbing Editor, Anthony Walsh, to give Danielle a lesson in bouldering. And that’s just the start. Next up is slacklining and then a highlining adventure.

If you’re nervous about trying a new sport, having a friend or guide to show you the ropes (literally or figuratively) can make a big difference. But the most important thing is trying something new—it can be just as fun to be complete beginners together.

Dylan and Danielle got the chance to explore the stunning terrain in the Sierra Nevada around Lake Tahoe, but you don’t have to wait for your next big trip to take an adventure outside. Wherever you live, there are more natural playgrounds in your area than you think. Use apps like Trailforks and Gaia GPS to plan your next outing, and discover the outdoors in your own backyard.

The world says to go harder, faster, stronger—but Sierra says, “go however you want.” From hiking up mountains to strolling down the block, Sierra loves it all. Whether you’re athletic or more athletish, Sierra has tons of active & outdoor essentials like top-brand gear, footwear, apparel & more for everything you’re into, all at unexpectedly low prices.
