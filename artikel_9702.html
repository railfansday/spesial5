PaddleOCR (92.6K stars) beats MinerU (83.5K) and Tesseract (77.5K) as the most-starred open source OCR project on GitHub. Below are all 138 OCR and document-parsing repos with 1,000+ stars in the Text Mining directory, ranked.

Every RAG pipeline eventually hits the same wall: the PDF. Before a language model can answer anything about your contracts, papers or invoices, something has to turn pixels and layout into clean text. The tools for that job are splitting into two camps. In HackerNoon's comparison of OCR and vision LLMs, template-based OCR breaks when layouts shift, while vision LLMs read documents more flexibly but cost more at volume. The author's verdict: "It could mostly be a hybrid approach."

Meanwhile, the vision models are shrinking. Xiaomi-OCR-0 handles text, tables and formulas with 0.8B parameters, and TeleOCR does structured document parsing with 1.2B. This list is the "show me everything" version of that landscape.

The Text Mining directory tracks the 3,141 most-starred open source text mining projects on GitHub (100+ stars, no forks or archived repos). Its OCR & Documents filter tags 321 of them, which together hold about 1.24M stars. We kept repos with 1,000+ stars (161) and removed 23 that are tagged OCR but aren't really OCR or document tools: generic AI course repos, an inference framework, a clipboard syncer, and captcha solvers, which we chose not to promote. That leaves 138. Star counts are from the directory's November 11, 2026 snapshot.
