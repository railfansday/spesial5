Broncos WR Jaylen Waddle, CB Riley Moss ruled out vs. Panthers with concussions

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Denver Broncos wide receiver Jaylen Waddle and cornerback Riley Moss both left Sunday's 26-21 victory against the Carolina Panthers with concussions.

Waddle took the contact to his head in the first quarter while trying to catch a pass over the middle from Bo Nix in between two defenders. Safety Zakee Wheatley hit Waddle's facemask with the crown of his helmet as Waddle leapt for the ball.

Waddle entered the sideline medical tent after the contact and was evaluated on the field for a concussion. He then jogged to the Broncos' locker room.

Later in the first half, Moss took a knee to the head while making a tackle on a run play.

Moss remained down on the field in pain for several moments before being evaluated for a concussion.

The Broncos went into halftime with a 13-7 lead. They ruled both Waddle and Moss out for the game after halftime, leaving them without starters on both sides of the ball.
