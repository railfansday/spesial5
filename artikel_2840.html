NFL injury tracker Week 10: Latest news, fantasy implications as Patriots' A.J. Brown (ankle/knee) and Chiefs' Kenneth Walker III (groin) questionable; Deshaun Watson to start for Browns

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Week 10 is underway, and we're tracking all of the injuries before game time.

A.J. Brown (ankle/knee) could return this week after a multi-week absence when the New England Patriots face the Detroit Lions in Munich. Brown traveled with the team to Germany and, along with running back Rhamondre Stevenson (knee), has looked "pretty good" in practice this week, according to head coach Mike Vrabel.

Brown has been limited to three games this season and has been out since Week 7. He is listed as questionable for Sunday, along with Stevenson.

After sitting out last week while in concussion protocol, Deshaun Watson will be back under center for the Cleveland Browns on Sunday. Shedeur Sanders got the start against the New Orleans Saints in Week 9, but head coach Todd Monken announced this week that Watson is healthy enough to start.

Kansas City Chiefs running back Kenneth Walker III did not take part in Friday's practice after reporting a tight groin. Head coach Andy Reid said the NFL's second-leading rusher will get treatment and the team will see how he feels on Saturday before making a decision on his availability.

"We're feeling it out just to see. We're not going to put him at risk," said Reid. "If he can go, he'll go. If he can't, he can't."

Walker is currently listed as questionable.

Bhayshul Tuten (ankle/foot) practiced on Friday after sitting out this week for the Jacksonville Jaguars. The team's RB1 has 531 rushing yards and four touchdowns on the season. He was questionable last week after not practicing leading into the game against the Baltimore Ravens. He did end up playing, but managed just 29 yards on 12 carries.

Dallas Cowboys wide receiver George Pickens (hamstring) sounds ready to return after missing last week's win over the Indianapolis Colts. When asked if he'd be available Pickens told reporters, "When you turn that TV on, get some food, make sure if you have a wife or girlfriend, make sure you hug her, love her and when you turn the TV on you should see me."

Sam Darnold (ribs/quad) is expected to miss another week for the Seattle Seahawks with Drew Lock (right elbow/oblique) taking charge against the Las Vegas Raiders this week. Darnold missed a Week 9 win after suffering the injuries the week before against the Chicago Bears.

Miami Dolphins quarterback Malik Willis (groin) and Los Angeles Rams receiver Puka Nucua (knee/turf toe) both were placed on injured reserve this week. Willis has been playing through a groin injury and, after undergoing an MRI, it showed he is dealing with a core muscle issue. Nacua sustained his injuries in Week 9. He was later diagnosed with MCL and PCL sprains in his right knee and a turf toe injury.
