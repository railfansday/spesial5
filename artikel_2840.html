Cleveland Browns news: Silencing the QB noise

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Cleveland Browns head coach Todd Monken is still new to town, so he can be excused for not understanding how things work around here when it comes to quarterbacks.

Every decision relating to the position must be questioned and defended on a daily basis. It does not matter how many names are on the infamous quarterback jersey; the mob always wants another one added to it.

Because where is the fun of simply knowing who the starter is and practicing for that week's opponent?

Monken appears to be catching on, however, as he showed during Wednesday's announcement that quarterback Deshaun Watson would be back as the starter for this week's game against the Houston Texans after missing one game with a concussion.

It is the type of standard move that NFL teams make all the time. A starter misses a game (or games) with an injury, and once healthy, they return to their former status.

Maybe the fact that Monken is trying his best to run the Browns like a real NFL team is throwing people off. After all, despite not playing well for a full game yet this season, Watson has been instrumental in helping the Browns stay relevant into November, which is a rare feat around these parts.

Why would Monken want to go away from that, especially after watching the same script play out against New Orleans only to end with a crucial loss instead of a key win?

The answer, as it has been since the start of the season, is that he never wanted to, and it was only Watson suffering a concussion that brought a conversation no one wanted to have back into the light.

Now that Monken has answered the question, the team can focus on getting ready for the Houston Texans and attempt to avoid going into the bye week on a three-game losing streak.
