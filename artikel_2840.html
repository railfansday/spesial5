DT Adetomiwa Adebawore agrees to two-year extension with Colts

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Colts took a break from working on trading away players ahead of next Tuesday’s deadline to assure that one member of the team will be sticking around for a while.

According to multiple reports, they have agreed to a two-year contract extension with defensive tackle Adetomiwa Adebawore. Per those reports, it is a $35 million deal with a $13 million signing bonus and includes $22.5 million through the 2027 season.

Adebawore was a 2023 fourth-round pick in Indianapolis and he matched his career high with four sacks through the first eight games of this season. He has 67 tackles, 10.5 sacks, 13 tackles for loss, and a fumble recovery over 51 career appearances.

With DeForest Buckner now on the 49ers, Adebawore is set for a bigger role on the Colts’ front and his new deal could prove to be a bargain if he builds on his strong start to the 2026 season.
