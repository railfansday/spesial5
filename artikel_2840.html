PFT's Week 12 2026 NFL power rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

1. 49ers (last week No. 3; 9-1): They made the best defense in the NFL look like the worst.

2. Bills (No. 1; 9-1): They're beating overmatched teams, but the wins are becoming less impressive.

3. Broncos (No. 4; 8-2): They need to start playing on the road the way they play at home.

4. Bears (No. 5; 8-2): We'll learn a lot about this team in Detroit on Thursday.

5. Chiefs (No. 6; 8-2): They're making it look easy, just like they used to.

6. Jaguars (No. 2; 7-3): They need to figure out how to win when it's not coming easily.

7. Lions (No. 9; 8-2): Can they slow down Chicago's offense? We're about to find out.

8. Bengals (No. 7; 7-3): A championship-caliber franchise doesn't choke against a team that is below .500.

9. Seahawks (No. 8; 6-4): The season may come down to whether they can win in San Francisco.

10. Giants (No. 15; 6-3-1): John Harbaugh has quickly remade this franchise in his image.

11. Vikings (No. 10; 7-3): With five of their last seven games at home, they're very much alive for the division title.

12. Rams (No. 12; 6-4): Wednesday night's visit from the Packers could be a de facto playoff game.

13. Packers (No. 13; 6-4): Can Micah Parsons make the difference?

14. Browns (No. 14; 6-4): Forget clinging to the seventh seed — the No. 6 seed is there for the taking.

15. Steelers (No. 11; 6-4): Playoffs or not, the Steelers aren't good enough to compete with the best teams in the AFC.

16. Panthers (No. 17; 5-5): Somehow, an NFC South team has gotten to .500.

17. Commanders (No. 21; 4-5-1): For the second time in three years, Jayden Daniels outdueled Joe Burrow in prime time.

18. Patriots (No. 16; 5-5): If they can't beat the Chargers, they've got no chance to get back to the playoffs.

19. Texans (No. 22; 4-6): C.J. Stroud is back. Will the defense ever be able to say the same thing?

20. Cowboys (No. 24; 5-6): The score-on-every-drive approach to offsetting a bad defense will only take them so far.

21. Raiders (No. 18; 4-7): The foundation is being laid for Fernando Mendoza to elevate the franchise in 2027.

22. Buccaneers (No. 19; 3-7): Monday night's visit from the Panthers could be an elimination game.

23. Ravens (No. 20; 4-7): Sunday's visit to the Texans is an elimination game.

24. Eagles (No. 28; 3-7): Playing for pride is going to screw up their draft position.

25. Saints (No. 23; 3-7): Time may be running out for Kellen Moore.

26. Falcons (No. 26; 3-7): They'd be better off if Sunday's game at Minnesota had been moved to prime time.

27. Jets (No. 32; 3-8): The win over the Chargers delays the inevitable coaching change.

28. Cardinals (No. 25; 3-8): At least they get 1/32nd of the shared NFL revenue.

29. Colts (No. 27; 3-8): For the past year, every big decision they've made has been the wrong one.

30. Chargers (No. 29; 1-9): Will Jim Harbaugh be back for 2027?

31. Titans (No. 30; 1-9): With franchise values increasing, doesn't it make sense for ownership to consider cashing out and moving on?

32. Dolphins (No. 31; 1-9): With the Jets coming to town, the Dolphins have one of their last, best chances to get another win.
