What are Spurs fans thankful for this Thanksgiving?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Spurs had a tough loss in Portland before the Thanksgiving break and will face arguably their biggest challenge yet when they face the West-leading Lakers after it. We are getting close to a quarter of the season being on the books, and games are starting to feel more meaningful as most teams round into shape.

As the season progresses, it gets harder to sit back and just enjoy San Antonio basketball without any concerns, but fortunately Thanksgiving offers the perfect opportunity to think about all the good times it has provided already and to reflect on how lucky fans of the Silver and Black really are.

Lee Dressie had a fantastic post about it that you should check out, but it would be interesting to see what you, the readers, are thankful for. Here's my list, as a way to get things started.

This one is self-explanatory. Wembanyama is the most tantalizing talent to hit the league in a generation, and he's a Spur. Luck got him to San Antonio, but he embraced the fanbase and seems to be happy to be in the city. He's lived up to the hype and seems like a perfect fit for the franchise, adapting to its traditions and ways while staying eager to forge his own path.

Watching Wemby is a joy for anyone who loves basketball, but it's extra special to watch him dominate while wearing Silver and Black.

The Spurs will be without Dylan Harper due to a hip injury for a while longer. Fans have not even gotten to see how Tobias Harris looks on this team, as he's been out since before preseason with a calf strain. A dirty play by Jeremiah Fears has forced De'Aaron Fox to sit out a few games. San Antonio has had its share of injuries.

Fortunately, none of those injuries have sidelined anyone for the rest of the year and, if everything goes well (knocks frantically on wood), all three players should be back relatively soon. Other contenders have not been so lucky, so it's important to be thankful for the injury luck the Spurs have had so far.

Sometimes fans are forced to root for players that, while talented, are not particularly likable. Whether because they are out of touch after becoming rich and famous or because of their personalities or bad behaviour, it can occasionally be hard to actually like the guys wearing the jersey. Luckily, that's not a problem with the current Spurs.

Sure, Wembanyama can come off as arrogant, but he also seems to have a good moral compass and is boring in a good way, putting basketball above everything. De'Aaron Fox always has a smile on his face and has adapted to any role asked of him. Harrison Barnes is the ultimate professional and a very charitable person. The young guards are hard workers who are willing to continue to improve instead of relying on talent and athleticism alone. Devin Vassell is soft-spoken but committed to winning, no matter what it takes.

Even the guys who have struggled, like Keldon Johnson and Luke Kornet, have great personalities that make them lovable. It's really hard to find someone to dislike on the roster, which makes watching the Spurs a lot more fun.

This one is important to remember, especially after rough losses. The Spurs have fallen short in four games this season and have had some close calls against inferior opponents. It's completely understandable to have some concerns about the team during the bad times, and occasionally even during the good times. The good news is, while there are some issues, none seem unsolvable.

The Spurs have a ton of talent, so they can overcome some mistakes, which is great. The three-point shooting has been so bad that it's bound to improve. The bench play has been poor, but it shouldn't be impossible to find some lineups that work better. Having Harris back will certainly help the second unit. And if all else fails, a trade or a signing could give the team a boost if it needs one. San Antonio is in a great place already, but things could realistically get even better.

Now let's get to the important part. What are you thankful for as a Spurs fan? Let us know in the comments.
