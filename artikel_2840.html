Chiefs-Falcons Week 10 Final injury report: Kenneth Walker is questionable

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Each practice day this season, the Kansas City Chiefs release an official injury report ahead of the next game. In Week 10, the Chiefs play the Atlanta Falcons on Sunday afternoon.

Here is the Chiefs' final official injury report of the week, courtesy of the team's official website:

Running back Kenneth Walker (groin) is questionable after missing Friday's practice suddenly; head coach Andy Reid told local media that he came into the facilities with a tight groin on Friday morning.

Rookie cornerback Mansoor Delane (shoulder) is questionable despite being a full participant in practice all week; he has missed the last two games.

Rookie defensive tackle Peter Woods (neck) has been ruled out; he hasn't practiced or played since exiting the Week 8 game against the Denver Broncos with a neck injury.

Starting right tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) has been ruled out; he didn't practice at all after leaving the Week 9 game in the third quarter with an ankle injury.

Second-year defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) was ruled out; he will need to be activated from the Physically Unable to Perform (PUP) list to the active roster next week in order to play this season.

Rookie running back Jaydn Ott (hamstring) is questionable after being added to Thursday's injury report as a limited participant; he was signed to the 53-man roster after running back Brashard Smith was put on the Reserve/Injured list a few weeks ago.

The following players were full participants in practice all week and have no game status:

Quarterback Patrick Mahomes (knee / right elbow)

Defensive tackle Gervon Dexter Sr. (illness) is listed as questionable after being a limited participant in Thursday's and Friday's practices. He has played 57% of the defensive snaps for Atlanta this season and has four tackles for loss, five quarterback hits. 1.5 sacks and a pass batted down.

Linebacker Divine Deable (forearm) is doubtful after he did not practice on Thursday or Friday. The team's starting linebacker has already missed two games this season due to an arm injury. He has 30 tackles, one for loss, two quarterback hits and a pass defended this year.

Edge rusher James Pearce Jr. (back) is questionable after missing Wednesday's practice. The former first-round pick made his season debut last week and played 15 snaps.

Rookie wide receiver Zachariah Branch (hamstring) has been ruled out. Branch leads Atlanta in punt returns (8) and kick returns (14) with a long of 61 yards on a kickoff return. As a role player on offense, he has 10 catches for 64 yards this season.

Linebacker Josh Woods (ankle) is questionable after missing the first practice of the week and being limited on Thursday. This year, Woods has 15 tackles and one tackle for loss while playing 91 total snaps on defense.
