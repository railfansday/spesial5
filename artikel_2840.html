Fan dies after falling during a fight at 49ers-Cowboys game

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

A man who attended Sunday’s game between the 49ers and Cowboys has died due to injuries sustained in a fight during the game.

Via The Independent, 36-year-old Marco Narro was involved in an altercation with another fan near kickoff to the game. During the fight, Narro fell. After the incident, Narro stayed and watched the game.

During the game, he started to complain about pain. He and a friend sought attention from paramedics after the game. Narro was then transported to a local hospital.

On Wednesday, Narro died. An autopsy is pending.

The Arlington Police Department has opened a formal investigation. The prevalence of security cameras at AT&T Stadium should be useful when it comes to reconstructing the events.

The situation underscores one of the basic risks of engaging in fights at sporting events. It doesn’t take much to suffer, or to inflict, a very serious injury.
