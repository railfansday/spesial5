Browns fans predict Cleveland’s record to finish off the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Cleveland Browns couldn't have found a better way to head into their bye, defeating the Houston Texans in a come-from-behind victory this past week. That allows players to get healthier and prepared for the final seven-game stretch. With a 6-4 record, fan confidence increased by 31 percentage points up to 88%. Is that too drastic of a leap? Maybe, but considering how down it was after the loss to Pittsburgh two weeks ago.

Speaking of their being seven games remaining, we asked Browns fans what they think the team's final record will be at the end of the regular season. The leading vote-getter was the team having a record of 10-7, a that got 48% of the vote. That outcome would involve Cleveland being 4-3 in these final seven games. 22% votes for the Browns being 11-6, and 5% votes for 12 wins or better. Altogether, that means 75% of fans think that 10+ wins are in store for Cleveland. The remaining 25% votes for either a 9-8 record, or 8-9 or worse.

Most Browns fans are hoping the team can split with Cincinnati, and still have 10 or 11 wins. I think the assumption among fans is that Cleveland would be a wildcard team.

There was also a national question this week that heavily involved the Browns, as it asked who fans think the Offensive Rookie of the Year has been so far. Chiefs RB Emmett Johnson scored 36% of the vote, but WR Denzel Boston (28%) and WR KC Concepcion (21%) were next up, with everyone else far behind. If you take that combined percentage (49%), it feels like Cleveland has the overall edge in the race, as both players are kind of stealing votes from the other.

According to FanDuel, the Browns are 4.5 point favorites against the Raiders next week on Sunday Night Football, with the over/under being 41.5. We'll post the official opening odds once the Sunday games conclude.
