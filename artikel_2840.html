Freshman tricky gives Arizona State an edge over Colorado

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

It was once again the freshman show at Mountain America Stadium.

Jake Fette made his home debut as a starter, and several others had starring roles as the Sun Devils surged past visiting Colorado 20-10 in front of another sellout crowd.

ASU (5-4, 3-3) got a field goal on its opening possession and never looked back.

Coach Kenny Dillingham's squad made it 10-0 thanks to a little trickery. On a fourth-and-goal at the 1, Cardae Mack took the snap and appeared trapped in the backfield, with a tackler around his ankles. He unleashed a toss to Fette, who was wide open in the end zone. The Sun Devils stayed in control from there.

A quick review of some of the keys to the contest.

Defense set the tone early: The Sun Devils were dialed in from the start, allowing just five first downs and 103 total yards in the opening half. Four of the first downs and 60 of those yards came on Colorado's last possession of the half, which came with under a minute and ASU playing conservative defense. Perhaps the biggest play came with Colorado going fourth-and-6 at the ASU 34. Albert Smith got his second sack of the game for a loss of 24, taking it back to the 42. ASU scored five plays later to make it 17-0. The defense made two fourth-down stops in the first half.

Back to ball control: The Sun Devils went back to the old reliable ball-control offense. On Oct. 31, Fette threw 42 passes, which isn't ideal for a rookie at the position. In this game, he attempted just 19, while there were a total of 42 rushing attempts. So ASU was leaning on the run game. That resulted in a time-of-possession advantage for ASU of 33:06 to 26:54.

Sun Devils caught a break: Penalties were few, which is surprising because Colorado came in as the most penalized team in the Big 12. The Buffaloes only had five for 34 yards, but the first was huge. With ASU up 3-0, Colorado quarterback Isaac Wilson connected with Ernest Mitchell on what appeared to be a 75-yard scoring play, with Ashton Stamps burned deep. Colorado was called for an ineligible player downfield, so that nullified the score. Colorado failed to get a first down on the drive and had to punt.

The punting game has progressed: Nick McClarty is handling punting duties, and he is an Australian who is still relatively new to the sport. He averaged 43 yards on six punts. He had two that were downed at the 5. It was true freshman Daquwan Dunn who was there to down both of those.

Players who have been out the entire season include LB Zyrus Fiaseu, TE Khamari Anderson, DB Nigel Pringle, and DT My'Keil Gardner. S Lyrik Rawls was added to that list this week.

Others who missed the game were OL Wade Helton, a starter, and RB Jason Brown, who plays primarily on special teams.

Cornerback Rodney Bimage left the game with 5:16 left in the first quarter with a right leg injury. His leg was put in a cast, and he was taken off by cart. He was replaced by true freshman Daquwan Dunn.

ASU hits the road for one of the season's longer Big 12 road trips, taking on Central Florida (5-4, 3-3) on Nov. 14 in Orlando. The game time has not yet been determined. Central Florida defeated Kansas 33-32 by getting the decisive touchdown on the final play of the game.

It will be the third meeting between the schools. The last came in 2024 in Tempe and was won by the Sun Devils 35-31.
