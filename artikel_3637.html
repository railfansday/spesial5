GTA 6’s Jason Can Live His Best Life By Drinking A Beer And Eating A Donut Simultaneously

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 looks like it will have an impressive attention to detail, and that includes how one of the main characters, Jason, can drink a beer and eat a donut at the same time. Now that's living.

Rockstar boss Rob Nelson geeked out to The Guardian about GTA 6's finer details, including this one. According to the report, Jason can "take bites of a donut with one hand while sipping a beer with the other."

It’s unclear whether this is under the player’s active control or it just happens. In any event, the report also mentioned how Rockstar paid close attention to "the way that Jason eats a hot dog." These are very important details.

GTA 6 features a body-shaping system, so if Jason, or Lucia, drink a lot of beer and pound hot dogs and donuts, they'll likely gain weight. But that weight can then be transformed into muscle by hitting the gym and pumping out sets.

Another example of Rockstar's attention to detail in GTA 6 is how Jason can buy a six-pack of beer, then start jogging, holding the beers in the crook of his arm. The game shows you the liquid sloshing around in the bottles as he runs.

GTA 6's attention to detail is a little ridiculous, with Rockstar seemingly going all out to create little details that players may never even notice.

GTA 6 launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Preloading is underway now, but the game itself doesn't unlock until November 19 at midnight local time.
