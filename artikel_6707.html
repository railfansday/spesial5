Friday the 13th Just Got a Little Luckier This Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

And you can thank Mercury Retrograde and Venus Retrograde finally coming to an end for that!

Friday the 13th may be tomorrow, but that doesn’t mean that luck isn’t in the air! This year on the longtime “unlucky” day, both Mercury Retrograde and Venus Retrograde (finally!) come to an end within a few hours of each other, bringing relief and clarity to your close relationships and ability to express yourself.

One retrograde is painful and frustrating enough, but over the past few weeks, you’ve navigated two at the same time, which can feel immobilizing at best and chaotic at worst. Fortunately, you’re reaching the finish line! Beyond the retrograde relief, Friday the 13th is actually considered an incredibly magical day, brimming with intuitive breakthroughs, despite its reputation.

For millennia, the number 13 was actually revered by many cultures and traditions all across the globe because of its connection to the average number of lunations we experience every year and also the average number of menstrual cycles a person experiences in a year. It was celebrated as a day to connect with and honor the Goddess. People who have periods are said to experience heightened intuition during their cycle and a strong connection to the other Realms. It’s through these associations that the number 13 carries deep and profound ties to magic, creation, manifestation, and birthing both new life and new desires and visions for your future.

Over centuries, the original meaning was distorted by patriarchal leaders and religions out of fear of collective feminine energy. By concocting false narratives and creating fearful myths about Friday the 13th, the patriarchal forces were able to exercise more control all while divorcing and dispelling many feminine people from reclaiming their own power and agency. Which is actually why many people consider this an “unlucky” day—but it’s really not at all! Once again, the patriarchy was out here ruining everything.

So clear your schedule because here come the divine downloads and revelations you’ve been waiting for. With Mercury Retrograde and Venus Retrograde ending, you’re ready to decide who and what from your past is worth clinging onto and which coffins you’re ready to put a final nail into. Here’s how this energy will manifest and how to make the most of it, based on your zodiac sign.

You’re done dealing with a lack of reciprocity in your connections. Whether it’s a current flame, ex, or BFF, it’s time for you to speak your truth and receive what you dole out so effortlessly to others. Your ability to be vulnerable and share your needs honestly can strengthen and deepen a connection in your life or protect you from someone who doesn’t deserve another ounce of your energy. There may also be a new contract or positive financial development on the horizon.

It’s time to prioritize your peace and set any necessary boundaries. Your daily commitments and responsibilities are just that—yours—and you can’t keep cleaning up other people’s messes. The end of the retrogrades ask you to confront close relationships in your life where you feel like your generosity is being taken advantage of. Standing up for yourself can bring more support and balance to your daily life as a result.
