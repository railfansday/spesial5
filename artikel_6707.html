Cranberry Cheesecake Lasagna

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Sugared cranberries:While the sugared cranberries are totally optional, they’re super easy to make and add a festive, fun look that takes this dessert over the top. Plus, once you’ve made sugared cranberries, you can use any extra in fall cocktails like our white cranberry cosmo.

Make ahead & storage:I love a dessert like this because you can make it ahead of time without worrying about keeping it warm or fresh. You can make the curd up to 5 days ahead and store it in the refrigerator, and the assembled lasagna can be made the day before. Just wait to top it with the sugared cranberries the day you’re ready to serve.
