Kevin Keegan has been inducted into the Newcastle United Hall of Fame.

This happened at an awards dinner on Tuesday night.

An awards dinner where Newcastle United Foundation were celebrating and highlighting inspirational community members of all ages and backgrounds.

It is actually the second time Kevin Keegan has been inducted into the official Newcastle United Hall of Fame.

The club announcing on their social media: ‘There’s only one King Kev. In July, the news of Kevin Keegan’s passing at the age of 75 sent Newcastle United and our city into mourning – but the way this city and the football community paid tribute to a true legend of the club and the game was simply incredible. We have officially inducted him once again into the Newcastle United Hall of Fame at tonight’s Newcastle United Foundation Celebration Dinner, as well as once again paying tribute to his legacy.’

Two other former NUFC greats were also inducted into the official Newcastle United Hall of Fame.

With Kieran Trippier and Paul Gascoigne also honoured last night.

Newcastle United CEO David Hopkinson and men’s and women’s head coaches Matthias Jaissle and Tanya Oxtoby were amongst the 400 people who attended the Newcastle United Foundation event.

They were joined by many of the Newcastle United Men’s first team, Newcastle United Women’s team, former players, Foundation supporters and participants.

With the prize-giving portion of the night seeing award winners meet with the likes of Harvey Barnes, Dan Burn, Joelinton, Lewis Miley and Jacob Ramsey, as well as Newcastle United Women’s Jessie Gale, Lois Joel, Emma Kelly, Jordan Nobbs, Jemma Purfield, Kayla Rendell and Charlotte Wardlaw.
