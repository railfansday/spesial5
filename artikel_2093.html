Arsenal transfer interest faces major obstacle as Santos secure £83m teenage talent

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Arsenal may have to rethink their interest in Santos forward Ignacio Pereira after the Brazilian club secured the teenager’s future with a substantial release clause.

The 16-year-old has caught the eye of a number of European clubs after an impressive campaign in Santos’ youth teams. The likes of Arsenal are said to have been watching his progress.

However, Santos have acted swiftly to secure the services of one of their most exciting young talents. Pereira signed his first professional contract with the club on November 4, tying him to the club until December 2029.

The deal also includes a 100 million euro release clause (around £83.4 million), according to Brazilian journalist Bruno Lima from UOL.

This poses a major problem for Arsenal if they are looking to sign the Argentine but also offers a lot of security for Santos, as they want to develop him as a first-team player.

Pereira has signed a new deal amid increasing interest in his development. Reports in Brazil have previously said that Arsenal, Real Betis, AC Milan, Lille, Standard Liège and Zenit are all keeping an eye on the rising star.

The club have now secured his services for another three years. The new agreement was awaiting registration with the Brazilian Football Confederation when the details emerged.

Pereira has spent three years at Santos, having joined from Juventude in 2023. He previously developed at Grêmio before moving to the São Paulo club.

Pereira has certainly lived up to the expectations. He scored an impressive 6 goals in 17 games in the Brazilian Under-17 Championship, making him the second-highest scorer for Santos. He also scored 4 goals in the Paulista Under-17 Championship.

This is very promising. It is his first season playing at under-17 level.

Pereira has been compared to Julián Álvarez due to his mobility and ability to play outside the box.

But the youngster cites another big name up front as his main inspiration. According to UOL, he cites Manchester City’s Erling Haaland as his inspiration, while Lionel Messi is still his football idol.

His profile suggests a versatile number nine who can play lower down the pitch, be an attacking midfielder and play with another striker. He has impressed the youth coaches at Santos with his finishing ability, physicality and desire to win.

He has a long way to go. Reports from Brazil suggest that Santos want to improve his tactical awareness and behaviour before making him more responsible.

He is being considered by the club for inclusion in their squad for the 2027 Copa São Paulo de Futebol Júnior (known as Copinha) and could start on the bench.

This interest from Arsenal does not necessarily mean that there is an imminent move for Pereira. The player is only 16, and a potential deal is made much more complicated due to a new contract.

Maybe the best course of action for Mikel Arteta’s recruitment team is to keep an eye on him. Santos has made it clear where they stand, and Pereira can now work towards his dream. Being in their first team.
