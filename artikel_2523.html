Arizona Cardinals Week 12 depth chart

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Arizona Cardinals face the Washington Commanders this Sunday in Week 12, and they released their depth chart for the week. They have made one roster move since then, adding quarterback Kedon Slovis, giving them four on the active roster.

This week's depth chart has four notable changes. Rookie receiver Harrison Wallace III is now a starter with Simi Fehoko, and cornerback Kei'Trel Clark has moved ahead of Max Melton for a starting spot. On the offensive line, Josh Fryar is now the No.1 left tackle and rookie Jayden Williams is now the starting right tackle, moving Elijah Wilkinson to the bench.
