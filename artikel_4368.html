The $700,000 Audi Nuvolari Will Be Extremely Rare in the U.S.

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Audi has announced the official price for its brutalist supercar in the U.S. and confirmed that owners will have to wait until 2028 to receive their Nuvolari.

Three years after the death of the R8, Audi revealed a new supercar. First shown in June, the Nuvolari wears a brutalist design and borrows the Lamborghini Temerario’s 789-hp twin-turbo V-8, mating it with a trio of electric motors for a total of 987 horsepower. Unlike the R8, though, the Nuvolari will be a limited-production affair, and now we know just how many will make it to the United States, and what they’ll cost when they get here.

The R8, which lasted from 2007 to 2023, was a true series production car, but Audi only plans on building 499 units of the Nuvolari. Of that total, just 182 will reach the United States. Once they do, each of their new owners will have to pony up at least $700,000. That figure doesn’t include the designation fee, which Audi has yet to disclose.

The lucky few who get to park a Nuvolari in their garage (or more likely a supercar-filled warehouse) will have to be patient. Deliveries won’t start until the first half of 2028, but once owners get their hands on the Nuvolari, we imagine they will be quite happy. That 987-hp powertrain propels the distinctively designed supercar to 60 mph in a claimed 2.5 seconds and to a top speed of 217 mph, making it both the most powerful and fastest production Audi ever.

Owners will have the choice of over 50 gloss paint colors and 10 matte paint colors for the exterior. The carbon-fiber bodywork can also be left fully exposed, which will surely be a pricey option. There are also 13 different colors for the brake calipers available. Given that the Nuvolari has only been shown so far in a matte gray hue, we look forward to seeing what it looks like in more vibrant colors.
