2026 BCDA Grand: Vista in Summit at Orchard Hills

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Brisa plan at Vista in Summit at Orchard Hills brings together flexible spaces and indoor-outdoor living in a design by Robert Hidey Architects and Brookfield Residential.

At the heart of the first floor, expansive corner doors open the great room to a covered loggia, creating a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor entertaining areas. A detached casita adds another layer of versatility, serving as a private guest suite, flex space, or independent accessory dwelling unit.

Inside, a central staircase anchors the home, while soaring ceilings on both levels enhance the sense of openness and allow natural light to move throughout the spaces. The open-concept great room serves as a central gathering place, with clear sightlines and easy circulation supporting everyday living and entertaining.

Award: GrandCategory: Production or Spec Home More Than 3,000 Square FeetArchitect: Robert Hidey ArchitectsBuilder: Brookfield ResidentialSize: 4,078 square feetLocation: Irvine, California

Fire-resistive materials and construction methods further support the home’s durability and resilience. Together, these features create a plan that balances refined architectural details with practical adaptability, accommodating multigenerational living, entertaining, and changing household needs.
