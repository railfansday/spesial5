Thomas Tuchel and Spain manager agree on England verdict after famous Nations League win

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

England goalkeeper James Trafford has been singled out for praise from both managers after the heroic 3-2 win over Spain.

Thomas Tuchel’s side finished top of their Nations League group above the world champions on goal difference after they avenged September’s 3-2 defeat at Wembley with victory by the same scoreline in Madrid.

England played the last half an hour with 10 men following Elliot Anderson’s red card.

Tuchel handed Jordan Pickford and Trafford two matches each during the last international break and again split their game time, with the Everton stopper playing in Thursday’s 2-1 win over Croatia and Trafford coming into the team against Spain.

The Leeds shot-stopper made a string of crucial saves late in the game as 10-man England held on for a famous win that brought an end to Spain’s 43-match unbeaten run.

Trafford’s double save to deny Fermin Lopez and Aymeric Laporte was particularly eye-catching, and his team-mates immediately mobbed him to praise him for those smart stops in added time.

The performance of the 24-year-old - vying with Pickford for the No1 shirt ahead of Euro 2028 - drew praise not only from Tuchel but also from his opposite number, the Spain head coach Luis de la Fuente.

“We did generate some chances, but England’s goalkeeper produced a fantastic performance and stopped two or three potential goals in the last 10 or 15 minutes,” De la Fuente said afterwards.

“I want to say how fantastic England were on the night,” he added.

Trafford’s own head coach was similarly impressed by the Leeds man’s influence on a high-stakes clash at the Metropolitano Stadium.

“The obvious save, where he deserves full credit, is the double save at the very end,” Tuchel said.

“Absolutely brilliant. There was a lot to do in the deep build-up as well, which he did very, very well, with everyone around him.

“Never lose the concentration, never lose the focus, never lose the vision where to find the free men to overcome the press and put the game to the opponent’s half. [Along] with everyone else, he was part of a fantastic team performance.”

Asked whether Trafford had closed the gap to long-time No1 Pickford, Tuchel said: “[He was] very good. That is his job: to close the gap. We want competition in all positions. We need to see Trafford in high-pressure moments.”
