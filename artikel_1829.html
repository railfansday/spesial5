Tottenham get fresh fitness worry as Roberto De Zerbi explains Sandro Tonali absence

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Roberto De Zerbi has confirmed that Sandro Tonali is missing from Tottenham’s matchday squad against Ipswich this afternoon due to illness.

The Italian midfielder, signed for a club-record £100m from Newcastle this summer, has started each of Spurs’ last four league games, but misses out after enduring a couple of days of illness.

Tonali’s omission is one of six changes made by De Zerbi, who has handed highly-rated teenager Luca Williams-Barnett his full Spurs debut.

James Maddison and Destiny Udogie also come into the side alongside Marcos Senesi, Conor Gallagher and Dominic Solanke.

Speaking ahead of the game, De Zerbi confirmed Tonali was ill, whilst adding that midfielder Rodrigo Bentancur, who was named on the bench, had returned late from international duty.

“(The reasons for the changes are) a combination of physical condition (and defeat to Leeds). Many players, they came back (from international duty) not in the right condition. (Sandro) Tonali was sick for a couple of days. Rodrigo Bentancur arrived yesterday, just yesterday working with us.

“I am lucky because I have a great squad. Many players. All players are very close in level so now we start a very tough period of three games in a row. We are ready.”

He joins a number of team-mates who are unavailable for this match, with Mathys Tel and Mykhailo Mudryk both injured.
