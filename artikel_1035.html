Alonso: Chelsea are fragile and need to be tougher

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Chelsea were dismantled by Leeds United in the Premier League on Saturday at Stamford Bridge; Xabi Alonso's side have lost three of their last four league games; Chelsea are eighth in the Premier League and have been beaten at home by both Leeds and Tottenham

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso said his side are "fragile" and need to be "tougher" after being embarrassed by Leeds United.

Noah Okafor's double helped Leeds to a stunning 4-1 victory, their first at Stamford Bridge since 1999, increasing the pressure on Alonso, whose team have lost three of their last four Premier League matches and slipped to eighth.

Chelsea took the lead through Emmanuel Emegha after Moises Caicedo went off injured, but once Leeds deservedly equalised through Noah Okafor, the home side never looked likely to recover.

"We are in a fragile moment mentally," said Alonso. "When we receive a punch, we didn't recover that strong, that tough enough and today that was my feeling."

He added: "After the equaliser, after the change (Caicedo), we lost the momentum completely," said Alonso. "Until then, we were doing OK. We were creating, playing a lot on the pitch, we were coming out from the pressure, but from that moment they started believing and we started not believing and it changed totally.

"And after that, we never got the good feeling mentality in any moment, and we were not able to cope with the pressure, and we didn't get close to the goal, so it completely changed it.

"We need to find solutions for sure because there will be setbacks during any game and to be tougher and to be able to go through that and to know that it's still so long to play, it's something mandatory in this competition."

Calvert-Lewin said Chelsea "couldn't handle" Leeds' intensity with the visitors outrunning their opponents by 6km, putting in more high-speed runs and further sprinting distance.

"I try to find football solutions as well," said Alonso when asked how he will attempt to fix being bullied by teams.

"That's our job, to try to find solutions, to work on that, to create that, to have bad experiences like today and hopefully we will have good ones when we can overcome, even during the game, a difficult moment because this is football and for sure that will happen.

"But in the progression, in the process that I often talk about, this is a very, very important topic."

Chelsea won more duels but Leeds' press won them possession nine times in the final third to the hosts' one.

"There were moments that we were not competing at the level required and we need to know that that's mandatory in this competition because you will face teams with that demand again," added Alonso.

"The physicality, the duels, that is not the only explanation. I said that yesterday and I repeat that. We could do that better in a football way, not giving a ball away, trying to progress a little bit more, choosing better options. That's the solution as well but the physical part and the mentality part we can do better."

Chelsea have lost at home to both rivals Leeds and Tottenham for the first time in 67 years, upsetting supporters, who booed their team off at Stamford Bridge.

Alonso said: "I understand the disappointment for sure and we share that with them and whatever we can do is work, try to give our best to turn it back and to do much better."
