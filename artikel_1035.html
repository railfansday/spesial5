England 2-1 Croatia: Kane surpassed Peter Shilton on 126 caps for England and then scored the winner from the penalty spot as the Three Lions booked a place in the Nations League knockout rounds

England booked their place in the Nations League knockout rounds by beating Croatia 2-1 on a historic evening for captain Harry Kane, who inevitably celebrated in style.

Kane, fresh off winning the 2026 Ballon d’Or, broke Peter Shilton’s long-standing record for most England men’s appearances. Shilton featured 125 times for England but Kane moved clear of the former goalkeeper and was presented with a series of gifts ahead of the match.

And he celebrated in the only way he knows how by scoring the winning goal, converting a second-half penalty – his 27th successful spot-kick for his country – to get the Three Lions over the line, after a Jarell Quansah mistake gifted Croatia an equaliser after Morgan Rogers’ first-half effort.

England are into the Nations League quarter-finals and victory sets up a mouth-watering clash with Spain on Sunday to determine which team will top Group A3, with Thomas Tuchel knowing a win will see them take theat accolade.

Thomas Tuchel spoke to ITV after tonight’s win

With defeat tonight, Croatia have lost four successive games for the first time in their history. A pretty remarkable stat really but the perils of being in Nations League Group A, I suppose.

That’s as many defeats as they had their previous 17 matches in all competitions. Where’s the next win coming from?

More from Harry Kane on ITV - discussing Sunday’s winner-takes-all clash with Spain. England know a win would see them top the group.

“We know we can improve on a few bits and we'll need to if we want to win on Sunday, but it was all about getting them win tonight,” said Kane.

“After the first game when we lost here against Spain, we knew we left a bit out there and knew we could have done a little bit better.

“We said to ourselves, let's win every game from now until the next one, and then see what happens. There's a perfect opportunity now on Sunday to try and prove ourselves.”

Harry Kane chatted to ITV after that win and was pleased with the result, although acknowledged there were a few areas where England weren’t quite at their best.

“It was an incredible gesture and reception before the game but once the game starts, it's just about getting the win and the three points,” said Kane. “We were playing against a tough team tonight who wanted to prove something.

“For large spells of the game we did well, got a little bit tired towards the end but a really good win and it sets up a great game on Sunday.

“We gave away a few too many easy balls, especially in our own half and just killed the momentum a little bit. I think we definitely had enough chances to score two or three. But we’re playing against a good team and it’s a tough run of games, so you can’t expect everything to be perfect.

On stepping up for the penalty: “It felt the same as ever. I missed my last one for Bayern, so it's always important to try and bounce back with the next one - try not to miss two in a row. I practised yesterday like I always do and then it's just about going out there and doing it on the pitch.”
