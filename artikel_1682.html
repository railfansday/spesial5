Millwall hit with partial stadium ban for discriminatory chanting

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Millwall will play December’s match against Sheffield United under a partial stadium closure after being found guilty of “ableist and homophobic chanting” from supporters.

The FA’s regulatory commission proved breaches of FA rule E21, which regulates supporter misconduct, observed during last season’s clash with Charlton Athletic at The Den.

They were handed a two-game stadium ban as a result, though the second of those two matches is suspended.

Four blocks of the Dockers Stand will be closed for the visit of the Blades.

They have another partial stadium closure suspended until the end of next season, have been fined £70,000 and had an extended FA Action Plan applied.

A club statement read: “Both chants are discriminatory and offensive, and the club condemns them in the strongest possible terms.

“Ableist and homophobic chanting, as well as any other form of discriminatory chanting, has no place in football or society.

“Any individual identified as engaging in such abuse will face the consequences.

“The club will continue the significant work already achieved under its dedicated anti-discrimination campaign, ‘All ’Wall’, while continuously reviewing and enhancing best practice to ensure The Den is always a welcoming environment.”
