Bastoni could miss remainder of 2026 as Inter gear up for Genoa double header

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Inter centre-back Alessandro Bastoni has suffered a left quadriceps strain, with reports indicating he will miss Saturday's meeting with Genoa and may not return until 2027.

The Nerazzurri confirmed on Friday that Bastoni had undergone tests in the wake of Wednesday's 2-0 Champions League victory over Stuttgart, in which Bastoni only lasted 41 minutes before being replaced by Federico Dimarco.

Bastoni has made 15 appearances for Inter across all competitions, with all 15 of those being starts. The only outfielder to better his 1,199 minutes for Cristian Chivu's side in 2026-27 is midfielder Nicolo Barella (1,298).

Bastoni has won 45 of the 79 duels he has contested this term, including 14 of 20 in the air, helping Inter win 29 points from the first 12 games of their Scudetto defence.

A club statement said: "Alessandro Bastoni underwent clinical and diagnostic tests this morning at the Humanitas Institute in Rozzano.

"The tests revealed a strain to his left quadriceps."

Inter did not offer a timeline for Bastoni's recovery, but with widespread reports indicating he could miss at least three weeks, he may not return until the new year.

Inter have seven games to play before New Year's Eve, the last of which is a Supercoppa Italiana final showdown with Lazio on December 22, a little over three weeks away.

Despite Inter's strong points haul, they sit third in Serie A ahead of Genoa's visit to San Siro, behind Lazio and Roma.

Yet Chivu was impressed by his players' reaction against Stuttgart in midweek, after their previous game saw them squander a 4-2 lead in a 4-4 draw with Atalanta.

"We didn't lose clarity of mind after the game in Bergamo: we played well for 75 minutes, but the final stages don't define the good things we produced in that match," he said.

"To tell the truth, you have to remain clear-headed and analyse what happened from a technical and mental point of view."

Inter will face Genoa twice in the space of three days, with the teams also being drawn together in the Coppa Italia's round of 16. The Grifone are 10th in Serie A, having beaten Sassuolo 2-0 away from home last time out.

Esposito, at the age of 21 years and 147 days, became the fourth-youngest player to score 12+ Serie A goals for Inter in the three-points-for-a-win era last week, behind Mario Balotelli (19 years and 78 days), Obafemi Martins (20 years and 17 days) and Ronaldo Nazario (21 years and 146 days).

If he scores a brace here (21 years and 153 days old on Saturday), he would become the third-youngest to reach 15 goals for the Nerazzurri, surpassing Ronaldo, who achieved this milestone at 21 years and 159 days.

Osmajic has scored six goals in his first 12 Serie A appearances since arriving from Preston North End.

Among players in their debut Serie A seasons, in the three-points-for-a-win era, only Diego Milito (11 in 2008-09) and Krzysztof Piatek (10 in 2018-19) have netted more goals in the first 13 games of a campaign for Genoa.

Inter are unbeaten in their last 14 Serie A matches against Genoa (W11 D3). Among current top-flight teams, they only have a longer ongoing unbeaten run against Atalanta (16).

At home, meanwhile, the Nerazzurri have posted 12 straight league wins over Genoa, keeping a clean sheet in 10 of those games and only conceding two goals in total.

Throughout their top-flight history, Inter have only registered a longer run of successive home wins against Como (15).

Inter's firepower should prove too much for Genoa here, too, as they have scored in each of their last 22 Serie A matches, netting 57 times in total at an average of 2.6 per game.

They have also won six in a row at home in Serie A, scoring at least two goals in each of those matches.

The Nerazzurri last enjoyed a longer Serie A winning streak at San Siro between October 2023 and March 2024 (nine in a row), while they last had a longer run while scoring multiple goals in every game between October 2016 and February 2017 (eight in a row).
