Nick Bosa’s shocking drought comes with a 49ers silver lining

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The San Francisco 49ers hold an 8-1 record, the best in the NFC, and yet they still haven't gotten quite the same type of production out of Nick Bosa that they're accustomed to.

NBC Sports Bay Area 49ers insider Matt Maiocco was asked if Bosa would ever be productive again and provided his insight on why he's a key contributor to the 49ers' pass rush.

"Nick Bosa does not have a sack in the four games he has played this season. Never before has he gone four consecutive games in the same season without a sack since coming into the NFL in 2019," Maiocco said.

San Francisco 49ers edge rusher Nick Bosa during team warmups at AT&T Stadium. Getty Images

Since Week 2 of last year, Bosa has missed an extensive amount of time dealing with a torn ACL last season and a calf injury this year.

Maiocco added that Bosa not being able to participate in training camp this past summer put him behind the rest of his teammates, and then when he was cleared to play again, he played in two games, and then strained his calf.

Bosa returned two weeks ago against the Raiders and recorded one tackle. He played 57 snaps against the Cowboys last Sunday, finishing without a tackle but registering a quarterback hit.

Nick Bosa coming out of the tunnel before an NFL game against the Cowboys. Getty Images

Maiocco added that Bosa is still winning despite the longest drought of his career.

"Bosa leads the 49ers' defensive line with a pass-rush win rate of 16.7 percent. That is down from past seasons, when he typically is around a 20 percent figure," Maiocco said.

On Monday, 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan appeared on a conference call that included Maiocco, and he was asked if he was worried about Bosa and told reporters, "I'm not worried about Nick."

"Nick is going to get better each week, and he'll eventually get there," Shanahan said.

While San Francisco is waiting for Bosa to reform himself, the 49ers defense can be patient.

Nick Bosa attempting to tackle Raiders tight end Michael Mayer. Getty Images

The 49ers rank second in the NFL in run defense, allowing 82.6 rushing yards per game. They're also giving up an average of 21.8 points per game.

Their pass rush has been inconsistent, especially Sunday, when the 49ers managed just one sack of Cowboys quarterback Dak Prescott, who threw for 343 yards and five touchdowns.

The 49ers are tied with the Eagles for 30th in the NFL with 16 sacks, underscoring their struggles to get after opposing quarterbacks.

Perhaps if Bosa can get back to his old self soon, he could give the 49ers' pass rush the jolt that they desperately need.
