'We want to win it all' | Bloom Carroll eyes state title

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

CARROLL — Even though the Bloom-Carroll volleyball team was winning during the regular season, it almost felt like they were on cruise control. However, during their four regular-season losses, the Bulldogs couldn't find their groove and were swept in all four of those games.

The Bulldogs knew something had to change as the regular season began to wind down and tournament time was coming at them fast. Since their last loss on Oct. 17 against Upper Arlington, Bloom-Carroll has been dialed in, focused and trusting each other.

It has resulted in them playing their best volleyball of the year at the right time, and it has carried them all the way to the state tournament for the second year in a row. Bloom-Carroll (22-4) will play Parma Padua Franciscan at 7 p.m. on Friday, Nov. 13, in a state semifinal at Vandalia-Butler High School. The state championship game will be played at 5 p.m. on Saturday, Nov. 14, at Wright State University.

"It is a privilege to go back to the state tournament because how many teams can say they have been to the final four two years in a row?" Bloom-Carroll senior Maddie Fox said. "We have been dedicated and pushed as hard as we can, and even though we've had some ups and downs, we have been so connected during this tournament run, and that has been huge for us."

The Bulldogs will be making their third state tournament appearance. A year ago, they finished as Division III state runner-up and made the final four in 2012. In the last two seasons, Bloom-Carroll has an impressive 49-6 overall record, which includes two consecutive Mid-State League-Buckeye Division titles.

Bloom-Carroll's regular-season losses came against Gallia Academy and Big Walnut. Both teams are 27-0 and in the state tournament. The other two losses came against Watterson, which lost to Big Walnut in a regional semifinal, and the last loss came against Division I Upper Arlington, so the Bulldogs are battle-tested.

Bloom-Carroll's opponent, Padua Franciscan, has a rich history. The Bears will be making their 10th state tournament appearance and have won five state championships (2017, 2016, 2013, 2009 and 2008), and they have been state runner-up three times (2020, 2019, 2018).

Regardless, the Bulldogs are playing with a lot of confidence, and they want to take the next step and win a state championship.

"A lot of teams underestimate us because we don't have that superstar player and we fly under the radar, but after teams see how hard we play, they have to take a step back, and then it's hard for them to recover. We need to play our game our way, no matter what the other team thinks or how good their record is," senior Kylynne Solberger said. "That is what matters to us.

"We have a fire under us, and we have a chip on our shoulder. We didn't end how we wanted to end last season, and I think we want to be back at that moment, and we want to win it all. We have realized that if we come together as a team and we trust each other, we are going to play well and we are going to win games. We learned some lessons along the way this season, and now we are playing our best."
