Draymond Green shoves Victor Wembanyama, earns Flagrant 1 in Warriors-Spurs game

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Draymond Green shoves Victor Wembanyama, earns Flagrant 1 in Warriors-Spurs game originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

It was only a matter of time before Draymond Green earned his first flagrant foul of the 2026-27 NBA season, and the Warriors veteran crossed it off during Golden State's NBA Cup game against the San Antonio Spurs on Friday night at Frost Bank Center.

Green sent Victor Wembanyama to the floor with 6:47 left in the second quarter as the Warriors trailed 45-22, making contract with the Spurs star's back that officials deemed included a "wind up" by Green. The hit earned Green a Flagrant 1 foul — his first of the season and 21st of his NBA career, including the playoffs.

Green is known for his physicality, and could have been trying to light a spark under the Steph Curry-less Warriors as they struggled early against the Spurs. But the action certainly was unwarranted, and he paid a price for it.

The 36-year-old was ejected following an on-court altercation against the Phoenix Suns on Nov. 2 that earned him back-to-back technical fouls, and many on the Warriors credited the fiery exchange for inspiring their eventual win.

Already short-handed and without veteran leaders like the injured Curry and Jimmy Butler, Golden State needs Green to stay out of trouble and remain on the floor as often as he can.
