Birmingham failed to weather Stoke storm - Davies

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Birmingham City manager Chris Davies admits his side did not deserve anything as the morning Championship leaders were removed from top spot by Midlands promotion rivals Stoke City.

Playing against an intimidating backdrop of a near 27,000-strong home crowd, the bet365 Stadium was back at its loud, challenging best - a tough place for any visiting team to go.

But Davies thought his table-topping side had got over the worst when they reached half-time at 0-0, only to lose by virtue of George Hirst's 48th-minute strike.

"We expected them to have their tails up at the start," he told BBC Sport. "As they did, without really creating much, but you could definitely feel the atmosphere was there.

"We weathered that reasonably well and we grew into it a little bit with some set pieces towards the end of the first half.

"We had a foothold and felt we could push on from there. The message at half-time was that we'd got through the hardest part of the game.

"But we weren't probably calm enough when we did win the ball back, whether that was to keep possession or break away.

"The goal we then did concede was poor from our point of view.

"When it then went 11 v 10 after the red card, we had a real lifeline. But they camped in for that last half an hour, understandably, and defended doggedly.

"We didn't deserve anything. We were just below the level you need to be and didn't create enough chances."

Blues had top scorer August Priske fit again after a three-week absence, having missed two games through injury, but Jay Stansfield's long run without a goal on the road went on - and they were also missing Colombia midfielder Jhon Solis, who sustained an injury on international duty.

Blues are now fourth, three points behind the top two of Middlesbrough and Stoke and go to struggling Burnley on Wednesday night before hosting Bolton Wanderers next Saturday.

They will be without skipper Christoph Klarer for the trip to Turf Moor after his fifth yellow card of the season.
