Brock Bowers returns to practice, Matt Gay dealing with injury ahead of Las Vegas Raiders’ Week 11 matchup against Denver Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Las Vegas Raiders received good news ahead of their Week 11 matchup against the Denver Broncos. ESPN's Ryan McFadden posted a video of tight end Brock Bowers in full pads and participating in warmups ahead of Thursday's practice, noting that Bowers is returning to the field.

The Raiders' leading receiver exited last week's game against the Seattle Seahawks at halftime and missed Wednesday's session with ankle and quadricep injuries. This was a step in the right direction for Bowers' availability on Sunday against the Broncos, as the Raiders listed him as limited on the injury report (h/t The Athletic's Sam Warren). Also, Las Vegas' offense really needs its top target.

The 'tight end' has racked up 50 catches for 676 yards and six touchdowns in eight games this season, leading the Silver and Black in all three categories despite missing the first two contests with a knee injury. According to Sumer Sports, the team's passing game averages 0.105 EPA per pass and has a 49.0 percent success rate with Bowers on the field this year. Without him, those numbers dip to 0.005 EPA per pass and 43.4 percent.

Factor in that rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza will be making his second career start against a Denver defense that ranks ninth in points per game allowed (20.7), and it's even more important that Bowers suits up on Sunday.

Additionally, Warren noted that guard Trey Zuhn III (illness), defensive tackle Tonka Hemingway (stinger) and defensive tackle Foley Fatukasi (toe) weren't spotted during the media viewing period of Thursday's practice. Zuhn wasn't listed on Wednesday's report, while Hemingway was a non-participant and Fatukasi was limited on Wednesday. Neither of the three linemen participated in Thursday's session.

If Zuhn can't go, second-year guard Caleb Rogers is likely the next man up. Rogers replaced Zuhn when the latter left the 49ers game with a concussion a couple of weeks ago. Hemingway's and Fatukasi's absence would really test the Raiders' defensive tackle depth, a position that's been an issue all season. That would likely mean more reps for Adam Butler, Tim Keenan III, Thomas Booker IV and JJ Pegues.

Warren also reported that the Raiders brought in kickers Lucas Havrisik, Younghoe Koo and John Parker Romo for a workout, as Matt Gay is dealing with a hip injury and sat out of practice. Tight end Ian Thomas added an ankle injury to Thursday's report after missing Wednesday with a calf injury, though Thomas was upgraded to limited on Thursday.

As for the Broncos, they managed to get healthier for the second practice of the week. The only changes from yesterday's injury report were cornerback Kris Abrams-Draine (ankle), defensive tackle Zach Allen (rest) and wide receiver Courtland Sutton (rest) going from limited to full participants.
