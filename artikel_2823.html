AHSAA football playoff scores, live updates from Round 2 in West Alabama

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Round 1 of the Alabama high school football postseason cut the slate in half, but West Alabama teams could see a drastic cut with a tough second-round slate.

The second round of the AHSAA football playoffs takes place Friday, Nov. 13, with four total rounds determining which teams advance to the state championships Dec. 2-5 in Mobile.

See live updates, scores from the second round of the 2026 AHSAA football playoffs across West Alabama below.

Most of the Tuscaloosa-area football programs are available to stream on the NFHS Network. It is a subscription service that costs $13.99 but gives to access to all content on the site, across all sports.

It is up to schools to provide the stream, so it's not a guarantee all games in all sports will be available, but most area schools look to have at least some football games on the site.
