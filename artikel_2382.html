Confirmed lineups: Serbia vs. Germany

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Germany head into their Nations League clash with Serbia knowing that victory is crucial to keeping their quarter-final hopes alive. Jürgen Klopp's side currently sit three points behind both Greece and the Netherlands in Group A2, with just two matches remaining.

After beating Serbia 2-0 earlier in the campaign, Germany will be looking to repeat that result and build momentum ahead of their final group fixture. Serbia, meanwhile, have endured a miserable campaign, losing all four of their matches so far. Veljko Paunović's men will be desperate to end their losing run and need a win to keep their hopes of finishing third alive.

With around an hour to go before the ball gets rolling, Jürgen Klopp has officially released his starting lineup alongside Serbia's team – both of which can be viewed below.

Milinković-Savić - Nedeljković, Gudelj, Milenković, Babić - Samardžić, Džodić, Milinković-Savić, Sukačev - Tadić, Cvetković

Germany (4-2-3-1): ter Stegen – Treu, Tah, Thiaw, Mittelstädt – Pavlović, Kimmich – Gruda, Wirtz, Scherhant – Havertz
