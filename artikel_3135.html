What channel is Auburn football vs Arkansas on today? TV, streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

AUBURN — Auburn football returns home after a brutal October in which the Tigers won once in four contests.

Granted, Auburn was never the favorite. It faced four ranked opponents and managed to beat the biggest, upsetting then-No. 1 Georgia in Athens. But that was near the month's midway point, and Auburn went on to lose its next two in blowout fashion.

In spite of its .500 record, Auburn still has a chance to secure the campaign it needs in the final third of the regular season, starting with Arkansas on Saturday, Nov. 7 (11:45 a.m. CT, SECN). The Razorbacks will play the underdog, as Auburn opened the week a 12.5-point favorite, and it starts a three-game stretch for the Tigers against not-so-stiff competition.

Taylor Zazour and former Georgia offensive lineman Matt Stinchcomb will be on the call for SEC Network, handling play-by-play and analyst duties, respectively. Michella Chester will serve as the game's sideline reporter.

Auburn owns the all-time series 21-13-1, as well as a home record of 9-7-1 against the Razorbacks, though this matchup's been back and forth in recent memory.

The programs have traded victories every season for the past five years, with neither winning a home game. Prior to 2021, under former head coach and Arkansas native Gus Malzahn, the Tigers won seven of their last eight against the Razorbacks.

PREDICTION, ODDS: Will Auburn football rebound against Arkansas?

If Auburn comes out and looks a cut above for 60 minutes, it'd be much to the delight and surprise of those on the Plains. That's hard to expect from this team however, but even if another sluggish start is in store, Auburn will have the home-field edge. It probably won't be perfect, and it may not even be inspiring, but Auburn should do enough to secure an expected win.
