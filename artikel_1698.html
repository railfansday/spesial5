Report: Agent working hard to complete €40m Liverpool transfer for January

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Liverpool's quest for a new left-back may have found a solution.

Fans are yearning for it. For almost a decade we have been spoiled. We glutinously watched the sheer joy of Andy Robertson and Trent Alexander-Arnold dominating the full-back positions.

These were two players who were put on a pedestal. Two players who not only epitomised what it means to play for Liverpool but who epitomised what a full-back needs to do.

Jurgen Klopp adored them. He brought the best out of them. It was just a special time. An incredible time.

How good would it be to go back and reminisce?

Well, maybe the dark days will be over soon. Maybe Liverpool are once again going to have full-backs they can be proud of.

Who could be the perfect full-back for Liverpool?Andoni Iraola's system requires a full-back with similar traits to those under Klopp. They need to be dynamic. They need to be good going forward, but they also need to bust a gut to get back and put in their fair share of defensive duties. Look at the full-backs who thrived under Iraola. The common denominator for him at Bournemouth was their ability to make an impact in the final third. On top of that they also needed to be good a receiving the ball and playing through pressure. Iraola wants his defenders to take risks and to progress the ball quickly into the opposition's final third. Right now, Milos Kerkez could become that player on the left flank, but he needs to improve. He's already done it before for Bournemouth and he can do it again.

Alejandro Balde is the new player on the club's radar. Liverpool are admirers of the Spanish defender at Barcelona, especially Iraola who knows him well. Now reliable X account Krrish is reporting that Balde's agent Jorge Mendes is working hard to complete a transfer for his client in the January window.
