Liverpool consider Jarell Quansah return and permanent Araujo transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

According to TEAMtalk, Liverpool are seriously considering a move to re-sign Jarell Quansah in 2027, as well as a permanent transfer of Ronald Araujo as part of a long-term rebuild of their back line.

Liverpool continue to monitor Quansah’s development following his move to Bayer Leverkusen in 2025. The England international has established himself as an important player for the Bundesliga side and gained considerable experience in the time spent in Germany.

Liverpool are believed to be preparing for the expected departure of Joe Gomez when his contract expires next summer, and there is also uncertainty over the future of 35-year-old captain Virgil van Dijk. As a result, the club’s recruitment department is exploring long-term defensive solutions, with Quansah viewed as a strong candidate to help fill the void.

Liverpool inserted a buy-back clause into the agreement that took Quansah to Leverkusen. The clause is said to remain active until the summer of 2027 and is valued at €70 million. However, the option apparently expires after that window, meaning Premier League giants will need to make a final decision on whether to bring him back to Anfield at the end of the season. Other top clubs are watching the situation closely as well.

Liverpool are also hoping to secure the permanent signing of Araujo following the Uruguayan’s impressive loan spell from Barcelona. The Reds could ultimately land both defenders as they seek to reshape their defence for the future.
