Maher optimistic Nashville can turn MLS Cup playoff tie against Cincinnati around

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Nashville SC defender Jack Maher revealed he is "so confident" his team-mates can defeat FC Cincinnati in their MLS Cup playoff tie.

Nashville won the 2026 Supporters' Shield in stunning fashion, setting a new single-season points record (77), but made a losing start to the postseason.

BJ Callaghan's side lost Game 1 in Round One at home 1-0 after an early own goal from Patrick Yazbek, marking their first defeat at GEODIS Park in MLS play this season.

Nashville must now defeat Cincinnati in Game 2 on Saturday to keep the series alive, and Maher is optimistic they can get the job done at TQL Stadium.

"First loss at home in MLS play for the whole entire year, and I think that that's something that clearly is frustrating," said Maher.

"The mood around the locker room was that this hasn't really happened before this year. But we're optimistic about the next game, and we're going to give it clearly everything we got.

"It's a hungry group in that locker room, and I think that if [Cincinnati] want to go on, they got to beat us twice.

"I'm so confident that we have the guys in this locker room to be able to go up to Cincinnati, get a great result, come back home, and finish the job."

Nashville boss Callaghan added: "This team's battle-tested, and the group will be ready to respond.

"You can feel that there's a bit of a hunger. This team has continually showed that we have the experience from early in the season in playing knockout-type games, and the group will be prepared to respond."

Evander was held without a shot or a chance created in the Game 1 victory.

The match was Evander's 69th MLS game with Cincinnati (regular season and playoffs), but just the second time he finished without a single shot contribution (the other was against Atlanta United in the 2026 season opener).

Over his eight MLS seasons, Hany Mukhtar has scored 37 goals and added 28 assists on the road in MLS play (including playoffs).

His 65 away goal contributions make him one of two MLS players with at least 60 goal contributions on the road since 2020, along with Carles Gil (71).

After a tightly fought contest in Game 1, the Opta supercomputer has predicted a similar outcome in this encounter, which could be decided by penalties.

With a 1-0 win in Game 1, Cincinnati levelled the all-time series against Nashville at five wins apiece (D4) across all competitions. Sunday's win was just Cincinnati's second clean sheet ever against Nashville, joining another 1-0 road win (March 2023).

The winner was the second time this season Cincinnati benefited from an own goal (the final goal in a 3-3 draw at Chicago in April). The own goal in Game 1 was the first playoff own goal from outside the box over the last 20 seasons.

The Game 1 loss ended a 10-match unbeaten streak against MLS opponents for Nashville (W7 D3). They have not lost consecutive matches in MLS play since October 2025, when they dropped their regular-season finale and playoff opener, both to Inter Miami.
