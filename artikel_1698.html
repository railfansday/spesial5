Tuchel: I absolutely believe England can win a big tournament

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Thomas Tuchel "absolutely believes" England "can win a big tournament" as they prepare for another Nations League clash with Spain.

England have already qualified for the quarter-finals after defeating Croatia 2-1 on Thursday, but could top Group A3 if they can beat Spain.

Spain have won seven of their last 10 matches against England (D1 L2), including the most recent two: 2-1 in the Euro 2024 final and 3-2 in the Nations League at Wembley in September.

However, England have won their last nine away games, their joint-longest-ever away winning run along with a nine-game streak between December 1965 and October 1967, which included a win in Spain.

England finished third at the World Cup in the summer, and Tuchel is confident his side can go all the way in a major competition.

"I absolutely believe we can win a big tournament," said Tuchel.

"I had the belief before the World Cup. That's why it was very painful we fell short. The belief is there and the attitude to push our limits.

"I'm happy with the progress and moment we're in. It's a good moment to challenge the world champions.

"We want to win the Nations League and we want to win the Euros."

In England's win over Croatia, Jude Bellingham became just the fourth England player on record (since 1980) to record six assists in a calendar year, after Les Ferdinand in 1993, David Beckham in 2004 and Raheem Sterling in 2019; no player has ever had seven or more in that time.

And Tuchel believes Bellingham will rise to the occasion when facing teams like Spain.

"He was already in the mix for this Ballon d'Or. I understand the obsession and scrutiny of this individual prize," said Tuchel about Bellingham.

"You can maybe understand me as a team coach, I believe in team spirit and togetherness. I'm not the biggest fan of that prize.

"But of course Jude is one of the most talented players on the planet. One of the most exciting midfield players. He's where decisive actions happen on the field.

"He had an incredible World Cup for us, was decisive and influential on the very highest level. He's one of the key players for Real Madrid every three days and will be up to the highest level. That's for sure the stage he loves and brings out the very best in him."
