Privacy coins face diverging fortunes as KuCoin moves to drop Monero

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

KuCoin has already suspended XMR deposits and plans to close withdrawals on Dec. 31. XMR fell 10.5% to $449.98 as of 7:00 p.m. UTC on Thursday. The drop extends a decline from about $625 on Nov. 3.

Launched in 2014, Monero conceals transaction amounts and the wallet addresses of senders and recipients by default. Unlike Monero, Zcash (ZEC), launched in 2016, allows users to choose between transparent and shielded transactions. Both cryptocurrencies have faced regulatory scrutiny over the past few years, with Binance delisting Monero in 2024 and Kraken ending support for it in the European Economic Area later that year.

Zach Pandl, head of research at Grayscale, which launched the first US spot ZEC exchange-traded fund (ETF) in August, said Zcash is positioned differently from Monero. He told Cointelegraph that the firm’s commitment to Zcash will continue for “many years to come.”

Pandl said that viewing keys allow Zcash holders to disclose transactions “to an auditor or regulator, law enforcement if they need.” He said the feature gives users greater control over who can access their financial information. “That is exactly how the financial system works today. It’s just that it gives the authority to individuals rather than institutions,” he said.

However, whether viewing keys alone can satisfy regulatory requirements remains a point of debate.

Pandl said that as AI has advanced, it has become “much easier to link pseudonymous addresses with offchain information.” Monero, he explained, uses ring signatures that obscure the source of transactions among multiple possible signers, a method Pandl said “may be vulnerable” as AI-powered blockchain analytics improve.

“We believe strongly that Zcash is the stronger tech,” he said, because “it takes a shielding approach rather than an obfuscation approach.” Zcash uses zero-knowledge proofs to verify shielded transactions without publicly revealing sensitive transaction data onchain.

Grayscale’s ETF reached $1 billion in assets under management on Sept. 24, but that figure had fallen to $406 million on Wednesday. Zcash more than tripled in value this year, rising to $1,653 on Sept. 26 from $524 on Jan. 1, according to Token Terminal data. Its price more than doubled in the month following the ETF’s launch but has since fallen back to $770. The cryptocurrency remains up about 50% year to date.

Pandl described Zcash’s price appreciation over the past year as “Bitcoin’s Solana moment,” referring to Solana’s growing market cap and its challenge to Ethereum’s dominance as the “leading smart contract platform.”

“At 1.2% of Bitcoin’s market cap, I would still consider Zcash at a relatively low value,” he said, adding that Grayscale has no price targets for ZEC.
