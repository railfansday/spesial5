Will Smith, Jada Pinkett Smith Step Out For Rare Family Outing

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith enjoyed a multi-generational family outing with their kids Jaden, 28, and Willow, 26, and their moms Adrienne Banfield-Norris and Caroline Bright in Malibu.

Now this is a story all about how the Smith family had a special day out.

Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith enjoyed a rare public outing with their kids Jaden Smith, 28, and Willow Smith, 26, as they stepped out to visit Soho House’s Little Beach House Malibu in Malibu, Calif., on Nov. 21.

And it was a multi-generational family affair, with Will and Jada’s moms Caroline Bright and Adrienne Banfield-Norris, respectively, also tagging along for the outing at the oceanfront social club.

While the occasion appeared casual, the Smith brood didn’t slack on the fashion, with the family all making a statement with their looks.

Will, 58, sported a bright red zip-up, white T-shirt and jeans, which he accessorized with a white baseball hat. Jada, 55, meanwhile, rocked a chic black button-up shirt under a black leather jacket, along with dark grey trousers. The Girls Trip actress paired the look with red shoes and a black handbag.

As for the former couple’s kids, Willow donned a long green patterned coat over a pair of beige pants and boots, while Jaden kept it classic with a white zip-up hoodie.

And the family matriarchs made sure to show who’s in charge as Adrienne, 73, wore a navy sweater over camouflage pants and Caroline, 89, rocked a black-and-white printed ensemble.

Though Will and Jada—who quietly separated in 2016—have maintained a rather low profile in recent years, they have stepped out on multiple occasions to publicly show their support for their kids.

In June, the pair were joined by Willow, Adrienne and Will’s son Trey—whom he shares with ex Sheree Zampino—for a joint appearance at Paris Fashion Week to attend Christian Louboutin’s Menswear Spring/Summer 2027 show. After all, Jaden is the brand’s men’s creative director.

During the trip to the fashion capital, the pair enjoyed a night out with Jaden, linking arms as they stepped out for dinner.

The Paris outing came five months after Will and Jada reunited to support Jaden when he debuted his first collection with Christian Louboutin at Paris Fashion Week in January.

Though it’s been over a decade since the Bad Boys actor and Red Table Talk alum separated, Jada—who publicly revealed the breakup in 2023—shared that she and Will don’t plan on filing for divorce in the future.

As she told NBC News in Oct. 2023, “I made a promise that there will never be a reason for us to get a divorce.”
