Kris Jenner Marks 71st Birthday With Kim Kardashian, Khloe Kardashian

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Kim Kardashian and Khloe Kardashian helped mom Kris Jenner ring in her 71st birthday with an elaborate cake and some sweet tributes on Instagram.

The Kardashian-Jenner matriarch celebrated her 71st birthday Nov. 5 alongside some of her family.

Kris' daughters Kim Kardashian and Khloe Kardashian were there to celebrate their mom on her big day, which they commemorated at home with a white cake adorned with green leaves, strawberries, blueberries and candles, as seen in Instagram Stories shared by both sisters.

The 71-year-old kept it casual in a loose-fitting striped red pajama top and wore her hair down for the celebratory occasion.

Kim also paid tribute to the momager with a carousel of pictures of them together over the years.

"Happy Birthday to the most special mommy in the entire world!” the SKIMS founder captioned the Instagram post. "I am so lucky to have another year to celebrate you and learn from you and show you how much we all love you! I love you beyond words mom."

Meanwhile, Khloe shared a lengthy post of her own in which she declared that one of the greatest blessings of her life is having Kris as a mother.

"I think one of the most beautiful parts of getting older is getting to know you in so many different ways," she captioned a video posted to Instagram. "Not just as my mommy, but as a woman, a friend, a businesswoman, a badass, a dreamer, and someone who is still discovering new parts of herself. And somehow, with every passing year, I find more reasons to admire you and even more ways to love you."

"As much as I admire the extraordinary, fabulous woman you are, it’s the simplest moments with you that I treasure most," Khloe continued. "Our precious conversations, calling each other 50 times a day, the way we can exchange one look and burst into uncontrollable belly laughs. Our shopping trips, our adventures, being each other’s partners in crime, and all the little things that nobody else would ever understand."

The 42-year-old finished her birthday tribute with a sweet note about her cosmic connection to Kris.

"If I had a thousand lifetimes to live, I’d find you in every single one," she said. "And without hesitation, I’d choose you to be my mommy EVERY! SINGLE! TIME! Happy Birthday, my beautiful queen mama. Loving you is one of the easiest and greatest joys of my life."

Meanwhile, Corey Gamble raised eyebrows with his birthday post for Kris in which he referred to her as his "wife."

"Happiest Birthday to the Holy Grail …. Love you forever," he captioned his Nov. 5 Instagram post. "Keep being Great. You're the greatest mother, Lovey & Honey to all your kids and im blessed to have you in my life lil wife."

Corey added, "To many more great years ….. Love you much."
