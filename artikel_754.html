Taylor Swift Scores Five Grammy Nominations, Including Album and Record of the Year Nods

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Swift‘s 'The Life of a Showgirl' album and track for 'Toy Story 5' are among the nominations for the February 2027 show.

Taylor Swift is headed to the 2027 Grammy Awards as a five-time nominee.

Swift received five nominations including record of the year for “The Fate of Ophelia“ and album of the year for The Life of a Showgirl. The Life of a Showgirl title track, which Swift performs with Sabrina Carpenter, is up for best pop duo/group performance. Meanwhile, her Toy Story 5 composition “I Knew It, I Knew You“ was nominated in the song of the year and best song written for visual media categories.

Last year, the singer-songwriter went home empty-handed despite receiving six nominations, including album of the year and best pop vocal album for The Tortured Poets Department.

Swift has already made Grammys history after winning the coveted the album of the year Grammy four times. Swift won album of the year for her albums Fearless in 2010 — she surpassed Alanis Morissette as the category’s youngest victor at 20 years old — 1989 in 2015, folklore in 2021 and Midnights in 2024.

Swift and Adele are the only women to have taken home an award in this category more than once, with Adele winning for albums 21 and 25. Meanwhile, Frank Sinatra, Stevie Wonder and Paul Simon have also won the album of the year award three times.

For Swift‘s The Life of a Showgirl, which was recorded in between show dates during the European leg of her record-breaking Eras tour, the superstar reteamed with producers Max Martin and Shellback, who worked with Swift on some of her biggest pop hits, including “Shake It Off,” “Blank Space” and “I Knew You Were Trouble.” She first revealed the project on Travis and Jason Kelce’s New Heights podcast in August 2025. Just weeks after making the surprise announcement, Swift and Kelce revealed their engagement. The couple got married in July of this year.

Swift‘s “I Knew It, I Knew You“ marked a return to Swift’s country music roots with the singer-songwriter set to make her return to the CMA Awards stage after last performing there 13 years ago. The song, which has been considered a top contender for a song of the year Oscar nomination, is nominated for single of the year at the upcoming CMAs.

For the 2027 Grammys, female acts make up the leading nominees with Raye topping the nominations with 11 followed by Rosalía with nine; Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert, Olivia Rodrigo and Phoebe Bridgers with seven each; and Olivia Dean with six nods, tied with Bruno Mars and Leon Thomas.

Dolly Parton was posthumously nominated for three Grammys. Hollywood names including Michael J. Fox and the late Tim Curry scored nominations for best audiobook, narration and storytelling recording for their memoirs Future Boy and Vagabond: A Memoir, respectively. Comedians Nikki Glaser, Trevor Noah, Wanda Sykes, Fred Armisen, Jeff Ross and Dave Chappelle received noms in the best comedy album category.

The Grammys are also set to honor the best compilation soundtrack for visual media with nominees including The Devil Wears Prada 2 (various artists), Stranger Things Season 5 (various artists), Wicked: For Good (various artists), The Wizard of Oz at Sphere and Wuthering Heights (Charli XCX).

Ludwig Goransson scored two noms for best score soundtrack for visual media, a category that honors the composer, for The Odyssey and Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu.

This year’s eligibility period includes recordings released from Aug. 31, 2025, through Aug. 28, 2026. Final round voting, in which Recording Academy voting members choose the Grammy winners, will take place between Dec. 10, 2026 and Jan. 7, 2027.

The 2027 Grammys will air on ABC, marking the first time in more than 50 years that the music awards show hasn’t aired on its longtime home of CBS. The ceremony will premiere live across ABC, Disney+ and Hulu from Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles on Sunday, Feb. 7, 2027, at 7 p.m. ET / 4 p.m. PT.

The Telecast will be produced by Fulwell Entertainment for the Recording Academy, with Ben Winston, Raj Kapoor and Jesse Collins serving as executive producers.

Swift was also recently announced as the recipient of The Hollywood Reporter‘s prestigious Sherry Lansing Leadership Award, which she’ll accept at THR‘s 2026 Women in Entertainment gala.
