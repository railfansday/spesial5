Blackburn Rovers win v West Ham was sweeter for one big reason

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Blackburn Rovers went into the international break on a high note by earning a 2-1 win at home to one of the favourites for promotion, West Ham.

Goals from Andri Gudjohnsen and Dapo Afolayan were enough to see off the Hammers, despite an 82nd minute consolation from Joel Piroe.

While the Lancashire outfit rode their luck a little, it was still an impressive display against one of the recently-relegated Premier League sides.

And the result meant that Rovers went into the November break sitting 13th in the table, but only three points behind eighth-place QPR.

The win against West Ham was especially enjoyable for Blackburn fans because of the London outfit’s decision to appoint Scott Parker as head coach earlier this month.

This was only the 46-year-old’s second game in charge after replacing Nuno Espírito Santo on 4 November.

His first time in the dugout resulted in a 3-3 draw against Cardiff City in which they came from 3-0 down to rescue a point late on.

But it’s Parker’s previous role that Blackburn supporters will be thinking about most, as he was recently in charge of their bitter rivals Burnley, so getting a win over someone with a close connection to Turf Moor will be extra sweet, particularly after Parker's Clarets took four points off Rovers in the 2024/25 season.

Parker reminded everyone of where his loyalty in Lancashire lies after the defeat to Blackburn, pointing out that he had not seen many fans of the home side on his way into the ground on the bus.

He suggested that the support from the travelling fans was far greater, taking aim at his former rivals.

“On the bus driving in here, we practically just see (West Ham supporters), we didn’t see a Blackburn fan,” said Parker.
