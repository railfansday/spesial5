Three goals from 1.09 xG: £20m star’s stunning form inspires Birmingham City’s rise to the top of the Championship

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Birmingham City defeated fellow promotion hopefuls Swansea City on Saturday, making it six wins from the last seven to move to 1st in the Championship. BCFC have put in some fantastic team performances in this run, but as far as individual efforts go, it is hard to overlook that of Jay Stansfield.

The £20m Blues man has moved back to striker in August Priske‘s absence for the last two games, and he has netted three goals. In victories over Millwall and the Swans, Stansfield has over-performed his xG of 1.09 by 1.91 (FotMob), providing a timely reminder of his goalscoring threat at St. Andrew’s.

Playing as an attacking midfielder, Stansfield has been more of a facilitator than a goalscorer in recent times, with his well-rounded game and fantastic work rate seeing him retain a key role in Chris Davies’ side. Blues fans needed no reminder of his importance, but the 24-year-old’s return to the scoresheet in the last two games has affirmed just how good he is as the lead man up top.

Stansfield is one of the faces of Birmingham City’s rebuild. He impressed on loan despite relegation in the 2023/24 season, and was the statement signing of Tom Wagner and co ahead of their record-breaking League One title win, joining permanently for a reported fee of up to £20m.

23 goals in 44 games across the 2024/25 campaign helped inspire City back to the Championship, and he followed that up with 11 goals in all competitions last term. However, a move out of his natural striker role has seen goals decrease for Stansfield.

Before November started, Stansfield had just three league goals in 2026. As said before, his work rate in and out of possession and his technical ability in knitting attacks together meant he has held an influential role regardless – for the most part. However, three goals in four days have made the Tiverton-born forward pivotal in BCFC’s rise to the Championship summit.

Stansfield was everywhere against Swansea City. Not only did he score twice, but he created two chances and one big chance, took seven shots and covered plenty of ground (FotMob). His heatmap showed that, despite playing as the traditional striker, he was still dropping deep and drifting wide to pick up the ball and keep the Swans’ defence thinking.

It was the sort of performance that everyone knows Stansfield is capable of. He led by example in terms of both attribution and ability as Davies’ side moved top.

Birmingham City’s faith in Davies has paid off. After doubts over whether he could lead them to the next level, he has weathered the storm and turned his side into deserving Championship leaders.

The Welshman has a tough call to make amid Stansfield’s return to goalscoring form though. At striker, it feels like his entire skillset is being used as he finds more shooting opportunities.

Stansfield is more of a scoring threat at striker compared to attacking midfield. However, the form of Danish ace Priske at the top of the pitch cannot be ignored, and he is not capable of dropping into a deeper role like his attacking counterpart.

It is highly likely that Stansfield drops back into the no. 10 role when Priske is fit again. But, recent performances and historic stats suggest that Blues get their best out of their big-money star when he’s deployed at the top of the pitch.

It is a welcome dilemma for Davies to have, as Birmingham City enter the break on a high.
