Waymo Has Been Banned From Operating Autonomous Vehicles on California Freeways, DMV Says

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The state has put a halt on the self-driving ride-share company’s high-speed operations due to an “imminent hazard to public safety.”

The California Department of Motor Vehicles has temporarily banned autonomous rideshare operator Waymo from operating on freeways in the Golden State.

The ban went into effect on November 11, and was prompted by an “imminent hazard to public safety,” according to the California DMV. The automotive regulator said two specific incidents in which Waymo vehicles disregarded construction zones and law enforcement, pushed the ban forward, CNN reports.

The California DMV said Waymo will remain banned until it “demonstrates to the Department that it has taken effective corrective actions to address the deficiencies that precipitated the restrictions.” Specifically, the Alphabet-funded autonomous vehicle operator will have to prove that its vehicles can safely detect and respond to construction zones and emergency vehicles.

The freeway restriction applies to revenue ride-share operations, as well as test vehicles. Waymo will continue to operate normally on surface streets in its California markets. Currently, Waymo operates in Los Angeles, San Diego, and the Bay Area.

“Safety is at the foundation of everything we do. Following recent freeway construction zone encounters on U.S. 101 in California that did not involve any injuries or collisions, the California DMV has implemented an operating restriction on freeway operations in the state,” Waymo said in a statement to Road & Track. “In alignment with our safety-first principles, we have also temporarily restricted freeway operations across our other operating markets to evaluate our performance. Our ride-hailing service continues to operate safely on surface streets, serving over half a million trips each week. We are working with the California DMV and other state officials diligently and responsibly to resume freeway operations.”

One of the incidents that allegedly spurred this ban was caught on video earlier this month. The video shows California Highway Patrol patrol cars creating a traffic break on Interstate 280 near San Francisco while a Waymo vehicle attempts to pass through the rolling roadblock. California Highway Patrol officers quickly cut in front of the Waymo, which appears to stop the Waymo from attempting to pass again.

This is not the first time Waymo has faced scrutiny related to its highway operations. The company filed a recall with the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration earlier this year over driving software that repeatedly entered freeway construction zones. Waymo also briefly paused highway operations in San Francisco and Los Angeles this May over similar construction zone issues, according to CNN.
