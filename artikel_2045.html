Man United's three agreements from Michael Carrick sack meeting, executives jet out for next manager talks

Manchester United are set to sack Michael Carrick but he took training on Tuesday and there is not expected to be an announcement before Wednesday.

Carrick arrived at the club's Carrington training ground as normal at 7.15am and neither he nor his coaches have been told their services are no longer required.

Sources say chief executive Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox have recommended to the club's owners that the Englishman is dismissed and the club appoint a new manager for their first match after the international break, while Carrick should continue to take training in the meantime.

The final decision will be made at a meeting attended by owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe and executives Omar Berrada and Jason Wilcox in Monaco on Wednesday.

Behind the scenes, managers on United's shortlist have been alerted to the likelihood of a change of manager should, as expected, minority owner Ratcliffe rubberstamp the recommendation of the club's executives.

The Premier League side are looking to install a new manager in time for the clash gainst Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday, 22 November.

There have been a number of names linked over the last few months, with Carrick's future uncertain for quite some time.

Eddie Howe and Unai Emery have been names that have continuously popped up, but the latter seems likely to remain at Villa Park.

Julian Nagelsmann is another who has been touted for the Red Devils' top job.

Howe and Nagelsmann are out of work currently, although hiring the former would still mean United have to pay out Newcastle.

Mauricio Pochettino is another who has been linked over the last day despite his current role.

The Argentine is employed by the United States' national team, but does have a break clause, which would also cost the Premier League side money.

Despite the great work Carrick did at United last season, results have been extremely poor.

And performances have not matched why the Old Trafford side hired him in the first place.

Manchester United being in the relegation zone after 10 games is far from where the Red Devils should be based on their pre-season ambitions.

United have slumped to their lowest league position after 10 games in the history of the Premier League.

The pressure has long been mounting this season, with a series of public outbursts from stars such as Lisandro Martinez and Bruno Fernandes certainly not helping either.

For a club with United's history and resources, such a poor start is simply unacceptable, and Carrick is now set to feel the brunt of it.

This will make him the third manager to be sacked since Sir Jim Ratcliffe's INEOS took control of the football side of Manchester United.
