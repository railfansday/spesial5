Fresh £30m-40m claim made on Birmingham City ace Jay Stansfield

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

A fresh claim has been made over the importance of Jay Stansfield to Chris Davies' Birmingham City side, with the club's record signing currently enjoying a strong patch of form.

The 23-year-old's time at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park has garnered plenty of attention ever since he was lured back to the club two summers ago for an eye-catching fee in excess of £15m from Premier League side, Fulham, on a seven-year contract.

Stansfield has since become the 'poster boy' of Knighthead Capital Management's ambitious rebuild in this part of the world, and that has brought its own advantages and pressures.

After recently surpassing 150 appearances in royal blue, the England youth international striker's brace against Swansea City last Saturday was enough to see him become the latest player to have scored 50 goals in all competitions for the club and send Davies' side top of the Championship.

Stansfield's current form is a stark contrast to the frustrations which he endured last term whilst adapting to a new role within Davies' setup, and with that being said, Football League World quizzed our Birmingham City fan pundit, Jason Moore, if the attacker is deemed 'untouchable' in January.

Stansfield was one of two Birmingham players who reached double-figures last season, albeit two of his 11 goals came after the turn of the year, leaving question marks over his performance levels and output.

It was then claimed in the summer that the forward was the subject of interest from clubs such as Everton, Hull City, Ipswich Town and Sunderland, with Stansfield's reported valuation coming in at the £25m mark as a result.

The forward later reaffirmed his commitment to the cause at St. Andrew's, although it was widely debated whether Birmingham should have potentially considered moving on their prized asset for said price.

Birmingham have seen goals fly in from all angles so far this season, with Davies' tactical reshuffles certainly getting the best out of Patrick Roberts, Liam Millar, Stansfield and August Priske in recent weeks.

Priske has netted six goals so far this season, but his absence in the victories over Millwall and Swansea allowed Stansfield to return to the main striking role, scoring three times in 180 minutes' worth of action, taking his seasonal tally to four strikes and one assist.

As such, Moore believes that even more would need to be coughed up if suitors were to even attempt prizing him away from B9.

"I got asked the same question in the August transfer window and back then I said 'take what you can get for him, go and reinvest in the squad', but I just can't do it," he told FLW.

"He's 'Blues', he gets what we're about, and I don't think you'll find a Blues fan that's got a bad word to say about him now. I was calling to probably sell him, but no, keep him. He is 'Mr Birmingham' at the moment.

"He's not scoring goals, but he just works so hard for the team, and he's literally just what we're about," Moore added.

"He's pivotal to what Chris Davies is doing with the way we're playing. So no, and I know I'm going to sound ridiculous here, but unless you're giving us like £30-40m no, I don't want him gone.

"You might get some fans who say 'that's silly, take £20m', not for me. I love him so much, I'd rather just find the money another way and keep him."

Stansfield's importance to Blues right now is way beyond his goalscoring exploits, even though some would still expect a strong return given his price tag.

Emphasising such a viewpoint, Davies recently said of the forward: "As a manager, you always go with the ones that you know are going to give everything, and one thing with him is, he gives absolutely everything in every game."
