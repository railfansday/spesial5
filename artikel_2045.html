Latest on John Eustace’s future as Derby County drop to bottom of the Championship

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Derby County have dropped to the foot of the Championship table after a 3-0 loss to Wolves on Tuesday night. The defeat, paired with Preston North End’s thumping win over Cardiff City, has left DCFC in a dire position.

The Rams have now lost four of the last five games and won just twice in 17 league matches. John Eustace is under serious pressure, but BBC Radio Derby reporter Dominic Dietrich says that, at present, the expectation is that the 47-year-old remains in charge for Saturday’s game against Watford.

Eustace had Derby County in the fight for a Championship play-off spot last season, but a dismal 2026/27 campaign to date has left his position in peril. Injuries have hampered his squad but patience is running out among supporters.

It appears that Derby County are sticking by Eustace for now, but he is facing a level of pressure that he has arguably never encountered in his managerial career to date. His Birmingham City sacking was a big shock, and he was scarcely in danger during his strong spell as Blackburn Rovers boss.

Although back-to-back defeats have worsened the Rams’ position while fellow strugglers are picking up points, the strength of schedule may be keeping him in place. After beating Bolton Wanderers, DCFC have had to face two top Championship sides in Southampton and Wolves – both away from home as well.

The Watford clash this weekend is a far more winnable one. The Hornets have named Edward Iordanescu as their new boss but are widely viewed as a firm relegation candidate this term.

If DCFC are beaten by them, it could be the final nail in the coffin for Eustace. A draw might not even be enough.

Derby County’s dismal season means calls for Eustace’s sacking are inevitable. However, the pressure grows when fellow strugglers take action.

And, as of Wednesday afternoon, four of the Championship’s current bottom five have changed boss. Burnley, Preston North End, Watford and now Lincoln City have all parted ways with their manager or head coach – DCFC are the only ones not to make a change.

Preston have found improved form with seven points from the last three games. As relegation rivals make changes and start to pick up important results, the Rams’ fingers will be hovering above the trigger if Eustace can not turn around the club’s fortunes.
