I Guess We’re Going Have to Deal with Pete Hegseth for Another Two Months

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The House vote to impeach the Secretary of Talking About War failed yesterday, partially because Democratic members were stuck in traffic and missed it. Great work by all.

Well, as of Tuesday evening, we learned that we will continue to be blessed with Secretary of Talking About War Pete Hegseth and his raging, bubbling hormones for at least two more months. The Republicans in the House of Representatives, undoubtedly exhausted from leaking anonymously what a shtunk Hegseth is, ran and hid when it was time to vote on Representative Thomas Massie’s resolution to impeach Hegseth and get his hands off the American military. Oh, Ted, you sweet, innocent child. It’s been a decade since the president insulted your wife and you still think he hasn’t stolen the conscience of your party.

Let us also commend the 11 Democratic members who got stuck in traffic and missed the vote entirely. Very strong work there, gang. And those people longing for the leadership of Nancy Pelosi should note that one of the 11 Democrats who missed the vote was ... Nancy Pelosi. This was a banjaxed moment comparable to Mitch McConnell’s decision to let the Democrats haul all the water on Impeachment II back in 2021.

Putative Speaker-elect Hakeem Jeffries said that Tuesday’s vote is “just the beginning.” However, if it were up to me, I would give up on trying to impeach Hegseth on the charges relating to the launching of the war in Iran. Congressional war powers? That horse left the barn halfway through the past century. I’d concentrate on his dreadful mismanagement of the Pentagon, particularly his purge of minority and female flag officers. Still, this was a body blow to Jeffries’s prospects. “Go play in traffic” is not a viable political axiom.
