Why These Very Unexpected Royals Are at Risk of Having Their Titles Stripped by Prince William

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Prince William reportedly wants to “liberate” them.

Prince William has made no secret of his plans to “change” the royal family when he becomes king.

Slimming down the monarchy (no, not with GLP-1s, but rather cutting the number of working royals) and reducing “ridiculous” protocols are just some of the stark ways that the royal family will be deeply different if the Prince of Wales gets his way. But while some of William’s proposed changes seem logical, others have come as a surprise to royal insiders.

Chatting to Radar Online about his new book, Tom Sykes said that friends of William revealed he plans to scrap his children’s titles in the name of “liberation.”

According to the author of Dukes of Hazard: Harry, Andrew and Edward VIII: The Spares Who Shook the Throne, while Prince George will inherit the throne, William is keen for Princess Charlotte and Prince Louis to have other options. In fact, by taking away their HRH titles, William hopes that his kids can pursue careers unrelated to royal life.

“He’s gone and looked under the bonnet and is now making really difficult decisions and changing an organization that hasn’t been changed for a very long time,” another insider told Radar Online.

But if William’s plan goes ahead, it wouldn’t be the first time that members of the royal family went without their HRH titles. Both Princess Anne and Prince Edward opted out of giving their children royal titles, and the former Prince Andrew was shockingly stripped of his just last year.

Previously speaking about the decision to not bestow her children with a fancy title, Prince Edward’s wife, Sophie Wessex, told The Sunday Times, “We try to bring them up with the understanding they are very likely to have to work for a living.”

The Duchess of Edinburgh went on, “Hence, we made the decision not to use HRH titles.”
