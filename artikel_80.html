Gazans flee shelters as Israeli army launches ground operation beyond Yellow Line

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Palestinians fled their tents and shelters in central Gaza on Wednesday after Israel launched a rare ground operation in an area it withdrew ​from under a ‌2025 ceasefire. The Israeli army said it crossed the Yellow Line separating Israeli and Hamas-controlled areas "to remove a threat posed to IDF troops operating in the Gaza Strip".
