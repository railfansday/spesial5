Jensen Huang has resigned, what's next for Nvidia?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Jensen Huang has resigned as CEO of tech giant Nvidia, the company's board says, with chief financial officer Colette Kress appointed interim boss.

The company's shares have recovered some ground after plummeting more than 13% when US markets opened shortly after news of Huang's resignation.

The BBC's North America correspondent Samira Hussain explains what's next for the company.
