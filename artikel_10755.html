Drunk raccoon who went viral passing out in bathroom raises $290K for shelter that rescued him

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

A raccoon who ransacked a Virginia ABC store last Black Friday has spent the year repaying the shelter that rescued him.

He dropped through a ceiling tile around 3:30 am, sampled a smorgasbord of rum, scotch, moonshine and eggnog, and broke 14 bottles worth under $250 before he passed out beside the employee toilet.

Officers then gave him a sober ride home, and since wildlife that breaks in for booze, like the Florida bear that raided a family’s fridge, usually leaves nothing but a mess, nobody expected a second act, let alone one with a receipt attached.

Jeffrey Parker, Hanover County’s chief of animal protection, told The Washington Post that merchandise emblazoned with a cartoon of the splayed-out raccoon and the words “Trashed Panda” has brought in more than $290,000.

The shelter usually collects under $20,000 a year in donations, so one night out-raised more than fourteen normal years of giving.

“We never thought a raccoon would do this for us,” Parker said. Photos of the scene traveled worldwide, Saturday Night Live built a sketch around him, and Virginia ABC named three cocktails after him, including a Trash Panda Old Fashioned.

County supervisors unanimously approved body cameras for the department’s eight officers on October 28, and roughly $39,000 of that five-year, $65,000 program came from raccoon money. The rest is funding new animal housing, toys, a temperature-controlled food shed and a community pet pantry, while Parker wants to set aside an emergency fund for injured animals.

The shelter retired the line on November 3, after a year of seasonal designs that ran from Hot Trash Summer to Trick or Trashed.
