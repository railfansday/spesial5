Personal Investments • Re: Low probability outcomes suck

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Dumping my frustration on the internet. If this helps one person avoid repeating my error then at least something good arises from the experience.

I invested recent windfall monies into VCRM (Vanguard's active taxable national municipal fund).
Within 25 trading days I had a loss position of $125,000.
I then fully harvested all shares (managing a wash sale I produced) and realized the loss.

My error: new fidelity rep pitched Fed tightening cycle, rising interest rates, and volatility in bonds. He suggested sitting in a 3-4% after tax equivalent muni money market was worth considering for the 30 day window. Essentially a tactical decision.

Rather than follow my IPS, which had VTEB (TLH partner), I followed this advice.

Today marks 30 days, meaning I can reenter my position in VCRM.

In those 30 days on the sidelines, VCRM went on a 1:1400 probability event (0.7% or 3.2 standard deviations) and rose 4.22%.

If, the proverbial if, I was in VTEB it would have been a $211,000 gain.

Fidelity rep may have been directionally right. Bond prices did what they did.

$30,000 in tax savings secured, fantastic.
I don't understand the thinking.

If you thought rates were going to rise / credit tighten, why would you go long in a bond fund with a duration of 7.5 years as a 30 day trade?
