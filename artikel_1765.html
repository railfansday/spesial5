Prediction: Egypt vs Malawi

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Getting their AFCON qualifying adventure back underway on Friday night, Egypt will welcome Malawi to Cairo.

While Europe’s elite domestic leagues might be in the midst of November’s international break, there are plenty of attractive betting promotions available.

Starting with the hosts, getting October underway with a toothless 1-0 friendly defeat against South Africa, Egypt are searching for an immediate response when they reignite their AFCON qualifying charge this weekend. Nevertheless, despite getting their Group B adventure underway with an uneventful 0-0 stalemate at home against Angola on September 25th, the Pharaohs did put a maiden qualifying victory on the board against South South Sudan. Signing off September with a routine 5-0 romp on foreign soil, Ali Maher’s side will be seeking a repeat performance on Friday night. Suffering an infamous World Cup collapse back in July as they watched their North American adventure come to an end thanks to a rollercoaster 3-2 loss against Argentina, Egypt are searching for some recent stability. In fact, Maher’s camp have registered just a single win from any of their previous six appearances across all competitions within 90 minutes.

Completing a blockbuster summer switch to Turkish outfit Trabzonspor and already registering 12 direct goal contributions in his first 11 domestic appearances this season (nine goals, three assists), former Liverpool icon Mohamed Salah will lead Egypt out in Cairo.

Likewise, making an initial season-long loan switch to Tottenham, Omar Marmoush will partner Salah in the final third and complete an all-star attack. Completing a permanent move to Scottish giants Celtic, Haissem Hassan has been included in Maher’s maiden squad.

As for the visitors, while Malawi might have opened their respective AFCON qualifying campaign with a 2-1 victory at home against South Sudan on September 25th, Callisto Pasuwa’s side immediately came crashing back down to earth last time out. Getting October underway with a routine 3-1 loss at the hands of Angola, Friday’s guests will be bracing themselves for a monumental challenge when they travel to Cairo this weekend. Registering just a sole competitive win so far this calendar year, Malawi will also be concerned by a nightmare record against Egypt. Hit with a 4-0 drumming when they last met the Pharaohs for an AFCON qualifying showdown back in March 2023, they have lost each of their previous three straight meetings by an aggregate score of 8-1. It should also be highlighted, Pasuwa’s camp have mustered just a single competitive victory on foreign soil over the past 20 months.

The likes of Innocent Nyasulu, Nixon Nyasulu and Lanjesi Nkhoma have all received recalls to Malawi’s squad this month. Arguably the most notable name in Pasuwa’s squad and making a maiden Bundesliga 2 appearance for Hannover prior to November’s international break, 19-year-old Mwisho Mhango will feature in Cairo.

Former Sherif Tiraspol midfielder Charles Pietro will lead Malawi out on Friday night. Appearing to have cemented his spot as Pasuwa’s number one choice, William Thole should keep his spot between the sticks this weekend.

Putting a maiden AFCON qualifying victory on the board as they signed off September with a dominant 5-0 victory away at South Sudan, Egypt will be seeking a repeat performance here. While the African heavyweights might be searching for some recent stability, they have certainly been handed a golden opportunity this weekend. Rightfully installed as a landslide contender when they return to Cairo on Friday night, Egypt also hold a dominant record against Malawi. Landing a routine 4-0 win when they last met Malawi back in March 2023, we are expecting a similar outcome.
