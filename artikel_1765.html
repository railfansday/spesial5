CM: Morocco’s hat-trick hero leaves Amorim ‘bewitched’ – Milan’s plan for Akram Jadid

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Akram Jadid’s name has been in the headlines again recently, and for good reason given his exploits on international duty.

Calciomercato.com begin a report by stating that Ruben Amorim had already made a note of Jadid’s qualities, given that he called him up to first team training at the end of August, just days before the win against Venezia.

The coach has been ‘bewitched’ by the young talent, and it was a first opportunity for the teenager to work with the senior side. The staff also got to see exactly what level he is at, given that his scoring record gave him a reputation after netting over 700 goals in the lower levels.

Jadid represents another diamond in the youth sector for Milan, one that needs to be nurtured in the best possible way. Having risen up the age groups, he is currently a key player in Vito Michele Antonelli’s U18 team.

Last season, he was a key figure in the U15’s Scudetto victory, thanks in part to his goal in the final against Empoli. The fact he has been promoted multiple levels since says a lot about how he is through of internally.

The report described Jadid as ‘a lively, mobile left winger who enjoys constantly taking on opponents to create numerical superiority and finish, or pass (depending on the situation). He is a No.10 for his technical ability and vision, and a No.9 in the penalty area for his eye for goal’.

In this first part of the season, Jadid has made eight appearances – all as a starter – and has scored four goals against Atalanta, Inter and Lecce. He continued this good form for his country with a hat-trick for Morocco U17s in the Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers victory over Tunisia.

He scored three times in five minutes. The third goal was particularly impressive: after anticipating the opposing defender and pounced on a loose ball, Jadid moved inside, evaded the return of another opponent, and concluded his solo effort with a curling right-footed shot that left the goalkeeper with no chance.

What comes next? For now, Akram Jadid will continue to grow with the U18 side, but the plan is that in the coming weeks he will be tested in the Primavera, so as to be able to compete against an even higher level of opponents.

Amorim is keeping an eye on him. Given his propensity for working with young players and instilling their trust, it cannot be ruled out that Jadid could appear on the list of players called up for a Serie A match in the near future.

For now, the parallel with Francesco Camarda – another record-holder from the Rossoneri youth team – is intriguing. Milan are enjoying him, and the Italian Federation are also monitoring things, as they could try to ‘steal’ him from Morocco.
