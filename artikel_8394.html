Open ZYNLO Tomorrow Savings if you want a high rate with nothing to meet, especially if you’d use ZYNLO’s debit card, since ZYNLO matches the spare change it rounds up into savings. Skip it if you need a branch, want the highest rate, or plan to move large sums out soon after opening.

Tomorrow Savings pays 4.10% APY, its yearly rate with compound interest included, on any balance, which is more than the 3.50% at better-known Marcus. ZYNLO is the online division of PeoplesBank, a Holyoke, Massachusetts, savings bank founded in 1885, so deposits carry the same FDIC insurance as at a big-name bank.

Savers who want a high rate with nothing to meet get one here, and those who’ll swipe ZYNLO’s debit card earn a full match on their round-ups for the first 100 days. Anyone who wants a branch, or who may need more than $5,000 a month out as a new customer, is better served by a bank with branches or same-day transfers.
