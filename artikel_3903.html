Guide: GTA 6: Secret Controls the Game Doesn't Tell You

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

GTA 6 has a complicated control scheme that allows you to interact with the game in a variety of different ways. And while Rockstar does a good job introducing almost all of the title's mechanics, several useful features and commands are never mentioned at all. In this GTA 6 guide, we're going to tell you about some of its secret controls.

On this page: Best Secret Controls GTA 6 Doesn't Tell You

Below are 12 secret controls in GTA 6 the game doesn't tell you about.

Please note, these all apply to the Default control scheme, which you can enable by pressing Options > Settings > Control Scheme.

Double tap Triangle to get out of your car while the engine's still running:

This can be useful for when you need to make a swift getaway, as you want waste time needing to start the engine.

One other quick tip here: if you're with a partner and need to quickly get into the back seat, hold Triangle so your character doesn't waste time running around to the passenger side.

If the police know your identity but not necessarily your car, one way you can avoid being detected is by hiding behind the wheel.

Just park up somewhere discreet, and hold Circle to hide from any pursuing police:

You can rest and lean on any surface by standing close to it and holding the Circle button, like shown:

This can be useful if you're casing a joint, or just want to watch the world pass you by.

You don't always need words to get your point across. There are situations where a middle finger will do.

Fortunately, GTA 6 has a solution for scenarios just like that.

While on foot, hold L2 and press R1 to give someone the finger:

You can also do this while driving by simply pressing R1 while behind the wheel:

Tapping Left and Right while driving will turn on your indicators, allowing you to signal to other motorists which way you're going:

This can actually be useful when you're trying to drive safely, as oncoming cars will understand which way you're going and react accordingly. It can also help when you're merging lanes on motorways.

While more complex radio controls can be accessed by holding Left, did you know you can also quickly turn the radio on and off by double-tapping Left?

This works both in a car and when you're out and about using your earbuds.

If you press Square while driving, you can quickly attack with your car door, as shown:

This is really useful when you're being flanked by unsuspecting bikers — or you just want to show cyclists who's king the road!

Much like the above car door attack, you can kick any nearby enemies by pushing Square while you're riding a bike, as shown:

This can help you knock rival motorcylists off their bike, or just give you a good core workout while on the highway.

You can go first-person at any time while you're on-foot and stationary by holding L2 and pressing R3:

You can move the camera around from this position and press R2 to zoom, but you do need to hold L2 down throughout.

It's also worth noting you can't move while doing this, as there's no traditional first-person mode in the game.

This can be useful for reading signs and other looking closely at other objects in the game, though.

Every vehicle has a full suite of options you can access by holding Left and using the right analogue stick to navigate up to Vehicle, as shown:

These options can include everything from winding down your windows to turning on your headlights. Speaking of which, you can also toggle your headlights at any time by pressing Circle:

And, if you're in a boat, you can use the above vehicle management options to set down an anchor, which will keep it in place while you go diving:

If you're playing on the PS5, the DualSense controller's adaptive triggers will help you observe the speed limit.

While you're driving, holding R2 until you feel the first point of resistance. This is the speed limit.

You'll need to have Adaptive Triggers Resistance enabled to feel this, and you can check it's on by pressing Options > Settings > Controller > DualSense Wireless Controller Features:

You can take two cars when you're with your partner by using the command menu.

Hold Right to bring up the menu and press Cross/X to instruct your partner to drive:

Then get in another car and hold Right again then press Square to follow you:

You'll notice that your partner will drive right behind you, which can be useful if you need to setup getaway cars during robberies:
