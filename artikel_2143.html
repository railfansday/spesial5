Cardiff City & Swansea City face joint Grimsby Town transfer cringe right now

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Cardiff City, Swansea City, Grimsby Town, and Iwan Morgan are all enjoying rather different fortunes in the 2026/27 campaign, but there is an interesting connection between the three clubs.

The two South Wales rivals are back competing at the same level again and both have plenty of reasons to be optimistic about their respective seasons. Cardiff City are rebuilding impressively following their relegation, while Swansea City are beginning to look a far more competitive outfit.

Neither has yet had the chance to get one over their rivals in the league, but that will change at the end of November when they finally meet for their first Championship clash since 2024/25. There is, however, another story they might both be keeping an eye on.

Iwan Morgan looks like a serious star in the making at Grimsby Town, with the 20-year-old already establishing himself as one of the most exciting young attacking talents in the EFL for the League Two outfit. His latest goal involvements came via a brace against Swindon Town during a 4-1 away win on Saturday.

Having left Brentford over the summer, the forward has hit the ground running, registering five goals and five assists already this season. What makes his emergence particularly interesting is his previous involvement in the academies of both Cardiff and Swansea.

He left Swansea after three years there in 2023, joining Brentford. Prior to that, the Cardiff-born forward had been with the Bluebirds for nine years. Also, these are two clubs with excellent youth-development structures and a strong reputation for giving homegrown players opportunities to establish themselves in senior football.

Morgan could quite conceivably have been starring for either side by now, or at least featuring in some capacity, but instead he is making a name for himself at Grimsby. With his performances attracting attention, both Welsh rivals could soon be left wondering how such an exciting talent slipped through their fingers.

One of the biggest frustrations for clubs developing young players is that talent can sometimes be overlooked until somebody further down the football pyramid recognises its potential.

Youngsters can be loaned out to levels that do not necessarily reflect their ability, while others are allowed to leave without ever receiving a proper opportunity in senior football.

Morgan appears to be another example of a player who might have been ready for a greater challenge much sooner. His performances suggest he is already capable of operating at League One level at the very least, despite being only 20 and playing for a Grimsby side in League Two.

Cardiff and Swansea may have missed their opportunity, but Grimsby's gain is enormous. They have secured a player whose ceiling could be considerably higher than the level at which they currently compete. If he helps to fire them up, he could yet be a major asset in the third tier next season.

It is not as though either Cardiff or Swansea have been particularly poor in their recent recruitment strategies. Cardiff have rebuilt impressively following relegation, adding plenty of players under the age of 25 to an existing core of academy graduates who have already demonstrated considerable quality.

They now boast one of the youngest squads in the Championship, with a project that has genuine potential to develop further. Swansea, meanwhile, have turned to Jamestown Analytics to help shape their recruitment under Vitor Matos.
