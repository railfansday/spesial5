The 16 players set to miss Sheffield Wednesday v Plymouth Argyle

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The November international break means a reduced slate of League One action this weekend, but one of the key games still going ahead takes place at Hillsborough, as Sheffield Wednesday face Plymouth Argyle.

New Owls manager Dave Challinor takes charge of his first league game in South Yorkshire, looking to bounce back from defeats against League Two opposition in the FA Cup and the EFL Trophy in his first two games in charge, although the latter, against Accrington Stanley on Wednesday, featured many debutants and an inexperienced side.

Ultimately, the former Stockport County boss has been brought in to oversee a surge back up the League One table towards the automatic promotion places, but a match against Argyle presents somewhat of a baptism of fire.

Tom Cleverley's side head to Hillsborough with the second-best away record in the division and with the opportunity to open a six-point gap over their opponents, further cementing their own promotion credentials a third of the way through the campaign.

Both sides have been plagued by injury so far this season, though, and this Saturday will be no different. These 14 players won't be available for the battle at Hillsborough.

Despite having four players on international duty this weekend, Sheffield Wednesday don't have enough players who fall under the required quota to postpone a game. The biggest absence will be Kieran Morrison, who was called up by Northern Ireland.

The Liverpool loanee has started only one of Wednesday's last five league games, but won't be available on Saturday, with his focus instead on a Nations League outing with Ukraine later that evening.

Jordi Liongola made his return to action after missing a couple of months with a hamstring injury, just last weekend against Oldham in the FA Cup.

However, his return to full fitness will now be managed by Burundi, who called him up for their meetings with Zambia in this month's AFCON qualifiers.

It was a proud moment for Sean Fusire on Wednesday night, as he captained the young Owls side in their EFL Trophy defeat against Accrington.

He'll now jet off to represent Zimbabwe in their AFCON qualifier double-header against Equatorial Guinea.

And the final international call-up sees Will Grainger represent the Wales Under-19 squad in their Euro qualifiers over the coming weeks.

The 17-year-old came off the bench to help Wales to a 2-2 draw against Finland on Wednesday night, and will face both Spain and Iceland in the coming week.

Onto the injury list, and Dave Challinor will have a big decision to make at left-back this weekend, with both of his recognised senior names currently sidelined, the first of which is Max Lowe.

The 29-year-old is one of the more experienced high-level names in the Wednesday squad this season, but has struggled to remain fit for lengthy periods in recent years. He'll be out for four to six weeks after re-aggravating an earlier injury against Oldham last weekend.

Backup left-back Reece James also continues to miss action after being left out of the last two games in all competitions.

The groin issue he picked up shouldn't see him back in action until mid-December.

There was some good news on the injury front on Wednesday, with Dave Challinor revealing that four key players could return, one of whom is the Owls' number one, Joe Lumley.

That's helpful, as the Owls were forced to play third-choice shot-stopper Connal Trueman for the majority of their FA Cup exit at Oldham and the entirety of Wednesday's Trophy defeat, as Ashley Maynard-Brewer was forced off with a bang to the head at Boundary Park.

Mason Burstow was starting to get a head of steam in the Wednesday fold under caretaker boss Matt Hamshaw, starting both games under him, scoring against Cambridge United and looking lively throughout.

Therefore, it was quite typical that the Hull City loanee would be sidelined afterwards, with Dave Challinor suggesting that the groin injury he picked up against Blackpool was due to him shooting from the halfway line. He'll miss four to six weeks.

Ali Al-Hamadi would usually be away on international duty with Iraq, but was forced to miss the squad this time around after picking up a foot injury towards the back end of October.

His return date is unknown, but the Sheffield Star indicates that next Friday's meeting with Barnsley is the target.

Tyler Onyango's crushing history of knee injuries during the infancy of his career reared its ugly head just 14 minutes into his Wednesday debut on the opening day at Leyton Orient.

The 23-year-old is set to miss the remainder of the campaign.
