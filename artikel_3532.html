Brad Pitt Exposes Hollywood’s Darkest Secrets In The Further Mis-Adventures Of Cliff Booth

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

A new trailer for Netflix and David Fincher's The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth has arrived. The sequel to Quentin Tarantino's Oscar-winning thriller arrives in IMAX theaters on November 25, with a Netflix release on December 23. Pitt is returning to reprise his role as former stuntman and war veteran Cliff Booth alongside Elizabeth Debicki, Scott Caan, Carla Gugino, Yahya Abdul-Mateen II, Peter Weller, Matt Groove, JB Tadena, Corey Fogelmanis, Karren Karagulian, and Timothy Olyphant.
