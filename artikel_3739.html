All Incantation codes in Warhammer Survivors

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Warhammer Survivors has several Incantation codes you can use from the secret menu in both universes. In simple terms, these are like cheat codes that allow you to bypass the objectives and missions that you would otherwise need to complete. ‘

While using them can definitely reduces the challenge, they let you immediately access an enhanced range of weapons and items.

The list of codes and unlocks is different across the two universes. Once you unlock the Secrets menu for the respective universes, you can then use the virtual keypad to enter the codes.
