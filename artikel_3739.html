Teen Mom's Cheyenne Carries a 'Protection Necklace' — What's in Her Bag

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Cheyenne Davis is taking on New York City one bag at a time.

The Teen Mom OG star, who looked chic in a long-sleeved, striped ensemble, was spotted out and about in the Big Apple and exclusively gave Us a peek inside her “inception” of bags while on the go — starting with her green tote featuring a geometric pattern.

From daily essentials to special keepsakes, Cheyenne revealed what she keeps on hand while juggling motherhood and a busy schedule like a total pro.

Keep scrolling to see everything Cheyenne keeps in her bag:

“So my bag is made up of a million other bags inside the bag,” the MTV star told Us. “Being a mom, I have to have, like, a million different things in case it’s doomsday. We have a First-Aid kit.”

Cheyenne is always ready for some extra hydration with a Liquid IV packet.

“I love Naturium,” she shared while showing off the biocompatible skincare and body butter. She also carried Summer Fridays to look extra refreshed and Pixi hand cream “to not be ashy.”

“I keep my wallet and then I keep random bills, and then I keep the protection necklace with the money because protect my bank account!” she quipped to Us.

Cheyenne had a stand, lights and “sticky things” to create content efficiently while in NYC.
