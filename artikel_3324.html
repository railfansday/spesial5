Apple announces Swift Student Challenge 2027 with new twist

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Apple has announced details for 2027’s Swift Student Challenge, and there’s a new twist this year: students can submit an app for any Apple platform.

Apple is proud to support the next generation of developers, creators, and entrepreneurs with the Swift Student Challenge. The Challenge gives students from around the world the opportunity to showcase their creativity and app development skills.

Submissions are open February 8–28, 2027. And new this year, students can submit an app for any Apple platform. Sign up now to get notifications about student activities.

February 8-28 is still a few months away, but Apple wants to give participants plenty of time to plan ahead and start working on their entry sooner rather than later.

And in an exciting change, students can build an app for any platform this year, including tvOS, visionOS, watchOS, and more.

This opens fresh opportunities for creativity, especially on Apple’s less saturated app platforms.

Apple will once again recognize winners across two tiers: “winners” and “distinguished winners.” The latter will be invited to join Apple in Cupertino “for an incredible experience.” All winners will receive a one-year Apple Developer program membership and a special gift.
