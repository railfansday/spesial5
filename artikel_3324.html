If you have some big home projects to tackle this winter or if you’re looking for a gift for a DIY enthusiast in your life, you might be looking for some new tools. Black Friday sales are often the lowest prices of the year on quality tools, so now is a good time to look for extra savings at Home Depot.

Getting a cordless combo set if you’re starting a new tool kit is a good way to save money and set yourself up with batteries and chargers for your new tools. Here are some deals on quality cordless tool sets that will keep working for years.

The Milwaukee 9-tool 18-volt combo kit is on sale for $699 at Home Depot, 50% off its regular price. This set comes with a drill, an impact driver, a work light, an impact wrench, a Sawzall, an oscillating multi-tool, an angle grinder, a circular saw, a blower, two 4-amp-hour batteries, a charger, and two tool bags. This is a good-quality starter set that has all the tools you need for simple maintenance and repair projects as well as projects like putting up shelves, fixing a fence, or installing a sink.

The Milwaukee 5-tool, 18-volt combo set is on sale at Home Depot for $349, 50% off its usual price. The set comes with a drill, an impact driver, an oscillating multi-tool, a Sawzall, a work light, a 4-amp-hour battery, a 2-amp-hour battery, a charger, and a tool bag. This is a simple tool set that allows you to tackle basic repairs like a drywall patch and also some maintenance tasks like changing air filters.

The Milwaukee 4-tool 18-volt Packout set is on sale for $699, 42% off its typical price. This set also qualifies for a free battery with purchase deal that gets you a free 18-volt, 8-amp-hour battery. Just click the “free gift” button on the menu to the right and select the battery to get the deal. The new price will show up in your cart. The set comes with a hammer drill, an impact driver, an angle grinder, a sawzall, a rolling tool case, two 5-amp-hour batteries, and a charger. If you plan to transport your tools, the Packout system is a convenient way to keep your tools organized.

Hand tools and bits are a big part of a well-appointed home tool set, and a part that is often overlooked until a third or fourth trip to the hardware store. Saving some money on these tools will also save you time and aggravation while you’re working on your DIY projects.

The Shockwave 15-piece impact-duty drill bit set is on sale for $19.97, 43% off its regular price. This set comes with 15 impact-compatible titanium SAE drill bits ranging from 1/16-inches to ⅜-inches. This is a good set for any projects that involve drilling through multiple materials, like installing a bracket for a flat screen in a wall stud.

A two-pack of Milwaukee utility knives with a fold-out screwdriver is on sale for $19.97, 55% off its usual price. These knives have blades that are simple to change out, and they’re a perfect stocking stuffer for anyone who works with their hands.

A two-pack of Milwaukee 25-foot magnetic tape measures is on sale for $19.97, 57% off its typical price. You can never have enough tape measures if you do any kind of woodworking or construction, and these make a great gift for anyone who likes to DIY.

The best early deals on tools right now are at Home Depot, where you can get up to 65% off on select Milwaukee tools, 50% off on DeWalt tools, and 30% off on Ryobi tools. They also have several offers for over 50% off on hand tools.

Home Depot and Lowe’s often compete with each other for seasonal sales, so it’s always a good idea to check both sites before buying. Right now, Home Depot has the best deals on DeWalt and Milwaukee cordless tool sets, but Lowe’s has a better deal on Craftsman hand tool sets.

The best prices of the year on tools are during the holidays in November and December. This is because of the holiday shopping push, as well as making space for new tools that come out in the spring. The second-best time to buy tools is in May and June during Father’s Day sales.
