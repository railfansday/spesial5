Bassam Tariq On Exiting Marvel’s ‘Blade’: “Life’s Too Short To Make Sh** Films”

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bassam Tariq was once attached to direct Marvel’s Blade with Mahershala Ali, but eventually exited the project.

In a new interview, the Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother director reveals why he decided not to helm the superhero movie.

“Life’s too short to make shit films, so that was my big lesson: I have to have full control over my script,” Tariq told The New Arab. “If we disagree, it’s between them and me. It’s quite sad because we had such high hopes for what we wanted to build.”

Tariq told the publication that he found it “funny and strange to start casting and building without a proper script.” What complicated things is trying to explain to actors who their characters and didn’t want to disappoint them if they didn’t live up to the expectations. Watch on Deadline

“That’s also very dangerous because if the text comes and it doesn’t add up to what you were saying, your credibility gets completely shot. All I have is my integrity, and I’ve been thinking about that a lot,” he added. “If you’re the bearer of bad news to your actors, but you’re not the one making any of these decisions — and it’s actually coming from a corporate entity — that’s not a great way to be a director. I know some people can take that and run with it, but I learned I was losing credibility with my actors, and it wasn’t a healthy way to operate as a filmmaker.”

The silver lining in walking away from Blade and the film never getting made was that some of the costumes for Blade were eventually repurposed for Sinners.

“That was awesome. I was so happy that that’s the film we got out of it, because Sinners feels so much bolder than what we could have accomplished. Also, it felt so singular for Ryan Coogler, who has been building all these IPs for other people,” Tariq said. “Now he’s doing his own in such an unapologetically beautiful way, celebrating so many different facets of blackness, being an immigrant and the transcendence of music.”

Although Tariq didn’t direct Ali in the Blade adaptation, they finally worked together on Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother.

“I’m blessed that Mahershala came along on this journey with me. The fights, I didn’t expect him to nail it on the first try. I think it was unexpected for him too, since he’d never done it before,” Tariq said. “What really blew me away was how he conducted himself with other actors. Sometimes actors can get greedy and want to make sure they give their best performance when the cameras are on them, and not be present when they aren’t.”
