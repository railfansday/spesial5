iOS 27.3: Three new iPhone features coming soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

iOS 27.3 is currently in beta and packed with new features, here are three that will hit your iPhone when the update launches next month.

Apple Health is getting some big changes in iOS 27.3 for all Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhones.

The new Health app adds Insights and Longevity tabs that provide personalized and intelligent recommendations related to your health. New features include educational videos featuring clinical experts, at-home movement evaluations using your iPhone’s camera, the ability to order and log labs, and a lot more.

Apple originally tested the new Health app in the iOS 27.2 beta, but it was removed before the public launch. The company said from the start, though, that the app would return and ship later this year, now confirmed with iOS 27.3.

Apple Music adds a new ‘Selfie Sing’ feature in iOS 27.3 that’s clearly designed to be used on social platforms like TikTok.

Selfie Sing lets you create karaoke videos of yourself using the iPhone’s camera and Apple Music’s built-in lyrics feature. As seen above, the feature puts lyrics onscreen below your camera feed so you can sing along to your favorite songs then share your video on social.

It won’t be for everyone, but Selfie Sing is a fun addition that represents one of Apple’s few attempts at a social-centric offering.

When iPhone Duo launched, it included a variety of exclusive features related to StandBy mode. But with iOS 27.3, Apple is bringing these StandBy improvements to all iPhones.

Additionally, iOS 27.3 lets you create multiple versions of each face for easily swiping between them. These features won’t be new for iPhone Duo users, but it’s great to see Apple bring them to more iPhone models.
