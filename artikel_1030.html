Who will win ATP Finals in Turin? Alcaraz favourite to land first title

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

World No 1 Alexander Zverev and second seed Carlos Alcaraz are among the favourites the win the season-ending ATP Finals in Turin - but what do our Sky Sports panel think?

Zverev is a two-time champion at the tournament, having lifted the trophy in 2018 and 2021.

The German is in John McEnroe Group with Ben Shelton, Arthur Fils and Rafael Jodar, while Alcaraz - aiming to triumph at the Nitto ATP Finals for the first time - headlines Ivan Lendl Group, which also features Daniil Medvedev, Flavio Cobolli and Frances Tiafoe.

The action takes place at Inalpi Arena from 15-22 November - live on Sky Sports Tennis - but who do Gigi Salmon, Tim Henman and Jamie Murray think will win the tournament? They pick their winners and surprise packages.

Speaking at the WTA Finals in Indian Wells, former Australian Open champion Madison Keys told Sky Sports:

"You have Carlos, who won in Paris, and then you have Ben [Shelton], who was in the finals in Paris and US Open.

"I feel like when you have Zverev as the No 1 in the world, you also have Jodar, who is having an incredible year, and Fils, who won Cincinnati in amazing fashion. The difference between all those players is so much closer."

On the American players, Keys said: "I'm cheering for Tiafoe to have an upset, absolutely. But Shelton is also my guy, and he's playing amazing tennis. Everyone is going to play amazing tennis.
