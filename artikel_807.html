Kristen Doute's Fiancé Luke Broderick Leaves The Valley After Season 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Kristen Doute's fiancé Luke Broderick is raising a glass to his appearance on The Valley after three seasons. As Doute put it, "That was the decision he made."

Luke Broderick is pumping the brakes on his reality TV career.

After more than two years of spotlighting the highs and lows of his and fiancée Kristen Doute's life on The Valley, the real estate agent is walking away from the Vanderpump Rules spinoff.

"I'll set the record straight," Doute told Page Six in an interview published Nov. 20. "Luke has decided not to film The Valley this season. It was a discussion that he and I had together, really just focusing on our family and Luke's mental health before we started production."

While she and Broderick—who share 16-month-old daughter Kaia—spoke at length about his choices for the show's future, she planned to support whatever decision he made.

"I really left it in his hands and said I would love for him to be a part of it, of course, but if it wasn't right for him," she explained, "then I wanted to be there to support that as well and that was the decision he made. So he gets to spend a lot more time with Kaia these days, which is wonderful."

The most recent season—which concluded in August—gave fans a front-row seat to the couple's relationship struggles, including sexual intimacy and communication, especially after welcoming Kaia.

"No secret, last season was very difficult for both of us," she added. "Two-and-a-half months after having Kaia, we started filming. I had a really difficult postpartum until Kaia was about 10 months old, and I think there was a lot of expectation versus reality as new parents that we didn't really know."

Although Bravo has yet to officially confirm a season four renewal, Broderick's exit is only the most recent cast shake-up. In fact, Danny Booko and Nia Sanchez are staying put in Santa Clarita.

"We're incredibly grateful for this experience," the couple announced on Instagram in September, "but our priority is protecting our peace and our family. We are fully committed to each other and our beautiful children."

Booko and Sanchez—parents to son Asher, 5, twins Isabelle and Zariah, 3, and Adelaide, 16 months—also raised a glass to their fans for all the love and support.

"We want to thank everyone for all the love and support you've shown us," the statement continued. "Thank you to Bravo and the team for everything. We are looking forward to what the next chapter brings."
