Madrid-bound Gabriel posts farewell Man Utd message

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

JJ Gabriel scored a hat-trick on his last appearance for Manchester United in September before walking out on the club

JJ Gabriel has confirmed his exit from Manchester United with a farewell video message in which he says the club will "will always be part of my story".

The 16-year-old forward, who has a verbal agreement to join Spanish giants Real Madrid, walked out on United in September.

He has subsequently been training on his own in Spain after relocating with his family.

The 50-second video montage, posted on social media, begins with Gabriel penning a letter to United, and includes some of his notable achievements, including coming on for his first-team debut during a pre-season friendly with Atletico Madrid in Stockholm in August, when he was just 15.

Gabriel then thanks United's coaches and staff, plus his team-mates as he "begins my next chapter".

He has not returned to United since scoring a hat-trick in a Uefa Youth League game with Sabah in September.

Although United still hold the teenager's registration until the end of the season, it does not prevent him moving to a club in the European Union as he now has an EU passport through his mother, who is of Cypriot heritage.

Speaking to Spanish newspaper Marca earlier this month, he said his "dream is to win the Ballon d'Or".

However, Gabriel has now broken his silence on his controversial decision to leave United, having won last season's Premier League Under-18 player of the year award, United's U18 player of the year award and been top scorer in a side that reached the FA Youth Cup final and Premier League U18 cup final.

"I arrived in 2020 as a 10-year-old boy with big dreams. Here, I grew as a player and a person, from scoring at Old Trafford to receiving the Jimmy Murphy [Under-18] award . You gave me moments I will always treasure.

"To every coach, every member of staff, to my team-mates and everyone who supported me; thank you for believing in me and helping me grow. As I begin my next chapter, Manchester United will always be part of my story.
