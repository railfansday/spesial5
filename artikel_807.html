Taylor Swift Is a Daydream at First CMA Awards Appearance in 10 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Taylor Swift stepped out for her first CMA Awards appearance in 10 years, and her red carpet look for the Nov. 18 ceremony is sure to be remembered evermore.

Taylor swift wore her best dress, fearless to the 2026 Country Music Associate Awards.

After all, it felt like a perfect night to dress up considering the “22” hitmaker attended the Nov. 18 event at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, Tenn., for the first time in 10 years.

For the occasion, the “Style” hitmaker certainly put her own on display as she donned a stunning bright yellow, sleeveless Oscar de la Renta gown featuring a voluminous high-low skirt. But what really made the whole place shimmer were her accessories, with the Grammy winner opting for blue and yellow dangling earrings, strappy gold heels and, of course, a red lip to top off her look.

And though she won’t be shedding some teardrops on her guitar in what will certainly be an emotional tribute to the late Dolly Parton, the organization confirmed Nov. 13 that Swift will take the stage performing at the event for the first time since 2013.

And it would be safe and sound to assume that Swift will be performing her 2026 single “I Knew It, I Knew You” from Toy Story 5, which is nominated for Single of the Year at the Lainey Wilson-hosted event.

“Writing this song felt like a musical departure and coming home at the same time,” Swift—who tied the knot with Travis Kelce July 3—said of the track on Instagram June 5. “Creating something for Jessie was a new challenge and also felt like second nature all at once. And being a @toystory kid from the age of 5 til now… is an adventure I plan to be on, to infinity and beyond.”

In addition to the Toy Story team, it was the “Opalite” hitmaker’s longtime collaborator Jack Antonoff who helped her get the track out of the woods.

“We wrote this with so much adoration for these characters that made us laugh,” Swift explained, “and helped us learn lessons and think outside the backyard all throughout our childhoods.”

And going back to her country roots was made even easier with the film’s director Andrew Stanton standing ten toes down for Swift’s involvement in the Disney movie.

“I found out that there was no other person on your mood board,” she said in a joint Q&A with Stanton Nov. 1. “Everybody said, ‘No, we just decided there wasn't going to be an end credit song [if you didn’t do it].’”

As she put it, “It was a struck-by-lightning moment of like, ‘I have to go home, get to my piano, and write this song, because I know exactly what I would want to say if I had you know the privilege of writing for this.’”
