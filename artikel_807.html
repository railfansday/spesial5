Sarr focused on Palace return after Liverpool interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Ismaila Sarr has made five appearances this season, but just one start

Crystal Palace forward Ismaila Sarr says he has moved on from Liverpool's interest in signing him and is keen for a "fresh start" under new manager Thomas Frank.

The Senegal international, 28, is keen to put that episode behind him after Liverpool explored a move at the end of the summer transfer window.

Ultimately, Palace rejected advances for Sarr, who scored 21 goals in 45 games in all competitions last season as his side won the Conference League, while Liverpool opted to keep Cody Gakpo despite interest from Manchester City.

In his first interview since Liverpool's pursuit of him, Sarr - who has been nominated for the African footballer of the year award - told BBC Sport: "Liverpool is one of the biggest clubs in world football and, when a club like that shows an interest in you, of course it's something you take seriously.

"I don't think any player would be indifferent to that kind of opportunity. It was an interesting possibility, and naturally, I considered what it could mean for my career.

"Today, that chapter is behind me. I've always believed that you can't move forward if you're constantly looking in the rear-view mirror.

"I'm a Crystal Palace player, I'm proud of what we've achieved together, and my focus is on getting back on the pitch and helping the team."

Sarr is still sidelined with a groin injury, but talks are ongoing with Palace over an improved contract - despite his current deal running until 2029.

He added: "Yes, there have been discussions with the club. For now, I prefer to let those conversations happen privately and concentrate on football."

Sarr missed most of Pierre Sage's spell in charge, making just two brief substitute appearances in the Premier League this season.

Sage was replaced by former Tottenham Hotspur and Brentford manager Frank this week.

"Of course, it's a difficult moment for everyone at the club," Sarr said. "After everything we achieved last season, nobody expected us to be in this position.

"But I know the quality of this squad. We have very good players, a strong group and the ability to turn things around.

"The arrival of Thomas Frank is an opportunity for a fresh start, new energy and a different approach. We all have to take responsibility, work together and fight for every point.

"I'm confident we can overcome this difficult period and achieve our objectives this season."

Sarr is on 10-man shortlist for the African Player of the Year award, which will be presented on 7 December in Lagos, Nigeria.

It follows Sarr being named Conference League Player of the Season, scoring nine goals as Palace won their first European title. He also collected both the club's player of the year and players' player of the year awards.

Sarr went on to score four goals for Senegal at the 2026 World Cup, after featuring in a controversial Africa Cup of Nations final in January.

In March, Senegal's final victory over Morocco was overturned after players left the pitch without the authorisation of the referee.

As Senegal appeal to be reawarded with their title at the Court of Arbitration for Sport (Cas), Sarr remains proud that his side "became the African champions on the pitch".

Asked about his chances of winning the African Player of the Year award, he said: "I'm proud of the season I've had, both with Crystal Palace and Senegal. It's been a special year, with important trophies and individual achievements.

"But African football has so many outstanding players, and several of them have also had fantastic seasons.

"I'm thinking particularly of my Senegalese brothers Sadio Mane and Pape Gueye, who also deserve recognition for what they've achieved.

"Of course, winning the award would be an incredible honour. Every African player dreams of that.

"But honestly, what matters most to me is seeing the trophy go to Senegal. I'm incredibly proud of what we accomplished together."
