Goodyear is replacing its CEO as the struggling tire maker's stock falls

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Board director Roger Wood takes over as interim CEO while Goodyear searches for a permanent chief and continues a strategic review

Goodyear Tire & Rubber appointed director Roger Wood interim CEO on Monday, replacing CEO Mark Stewart, who stepped down as CEO and president and resigned from the board. The company said the board will search for a permanent successor with an executive search firm.

Goodyear stock slipped 3.7% to $4.20 ahead of Monday's opening bell.

"Goodyear has made meaningful progress in becoming a more efficient and focused company, and the board believes this is the right moment to transition leadership to accelerate that progress and capture additional opportunity," Board Chair Joe Hinrichs said in a statement.

Wood said his immediate priorities are to support associates and customers, sharpen execution and build on work already under way. "I look forward to working closely with the board, leadership team and the Enterprise Transformation Office to advance the strategic review," he said in a statement.

Goodyear disclosed terms of the change in a regulatory filing. Wood, 64, has been a Goodyear director since July 2023. He was co-CEO of Tenneco from 2018 to 2020 and president and CEO of Dana Holding from 2011 to 2015. He will remain a director but will leave the Human Capital and Compensation Committee and the Governance Committee during his interim tenure.

Wood will receive a salary of $310,000 per month, the filing said. He will receive a monthly restricted stock unit grant with a grant date value of $1 million for five months or until a permanent successor is hired, whichever is shorter. He also gets temporary housing, car and travel allowances.

The filing said Stewart's separation counts as a termination "other than for Cause" under the company's Executive Severance and Change in Control Plan.

A special committee of the board, formed this year, has been conducting a strategic review of Goodyear's businesses and operations. The company said the review aims to improve how the business performs and to create more value for shareholders. The board and the company are weighing steps such as fine-tuning the mix of businesses and the footprint, raising efficiency and putting money into new products and capabilities.

Goodyear said it plans no more updates unless its board concludes that further disclosure is warranted or necessary.
