Kasper Hogh handed Celtic lifeline with slap in the face ahead of five-game gauntlet

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Kasper Hogh has been dealt a blow which could yet spark his Celtic redemption arc.

The Danish striker played in all four of his country's games during the extended September and October international break, but has now been left out amid his worrying form for the toiling Premiership champions.

Celtic desperately need something to change with their £11million man and, perhaps, a chance to pause and take stock rather than continuing to operate on the fringes could work in Hogh's favour.

The timing might help him, too. Celtic face Besiktas and Kilmarnock at home before the international break, then return against St Mirren before finishing November with trips to Torreense in the Europa League and Hearts in the Premiership.

It's a run which offers Hogh opportunities to rediscover his touch in front of goal. The club-record signing from Bodo/Glimt has scored in just one game for Celtic, albeit a hat-trick against Kilmarnock, and it's hard to dress up an international snub as a positive. In this case, though, it just might be.

A chance to reset, coupled with that potentially favourable run of fixtures, could give Hogh the opportunity to get himself back on track and chase Martin O'Neill's lofty 20-goal target for his much-maligned striker.

Hogh has now gone 14 games without scoring since his hat-trick against Kilmarnock on August 9, drawing blanks against Dundee United twice, LASK twice, St Johnstone, Rangers twice, Ferencvaros, Motherwell, Benfica, Hearts, Celta Vigo, Hibs and Falkirk.

And the big striker has now suffered another setback after being left out of Brian Riemer's Denmark squad for the crunch matches against Portugal and Norway.
