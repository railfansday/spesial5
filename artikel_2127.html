Middlesbrough missed 'ridiculous' Ipswich Town transfer - Brighton deal has made them forget

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

It has been an outstanding start to the season for Middlesbrough in the Championship.

After an eventful end to last season that culminated in defeat to Hull City in the play-off final, Middlesbrough are looking to bounce back from their Wembley heartbreak, and they are among the early Championship front-runners this campaign.

Middlesbrough invested heavily in their squad this summer as owner Steve Gibson provided head coach Kim Hellberg with strong backing in the transfer market, with striker Will Lankshear becoming the club's marquee signing when he joined from Tottenham Hotspur for an initial fee of around £10 million, plus a further £10 million in add-ons.

However, Lankshear was not Boro's only big money addition, with the likes of Radek Vitek, Ashley Phillips, Max Arfsten, Sebastian Berhalter, Rokas Pukstas and Kyle Joseph also arriving for significant fees, and, so far, their bold squad rebuild is having the desired effect.

It was a disappointing week for Boro as they followed up their midweek defeat at Wrexham with an underwhelming 0-0 draw at Watford on Saturday, but while the Teesside outfit were knocked off top spot by Birmingham City on goal difference, they remain in second place heading into the November international break.

Of course, there is still a long way to go, and there will likely be plenty of twists and turns to come, but Boro will be optimistic that this will finally be the year they secure their return to the Premier League.

It is fair to say that Middlesbrough's summer transfer business caught the eye, but it seems they may have been plotting even more ambitious deals, with Ipswich Town winger Jack Clarke said to have been one name on their radar.

Despite often being used from the bench, Clarke scored an impressive 16 goals and provided two assists in 46 appearances for the Tractor Boys last season as they won automatic promotion from the Championship, and Boro were reportedly eyeing a surprise loan move for the 25-year-old in the closing stages of the summer window.

The links with Clarke were greeted with approval from FLW's Middlesbrough fan pundit Liam Day, who revealed that the Ipswich star was his dream summer transfer target, even if he had doubts over whether a deal was realistic.

"I think just because he's been linked and can somewhat be seen as maybe a small little option, I think Jack Clarke would be my dream signing before the end of the window," Liam told FLW in August.

"I think he's skilful, I think he's one of the best players in the Championship whenever he's in it, and I really hope Boro can try and get that one done.

"Do I think it's realistic? Probably not. I think Ipswich will keep hold of him. And even if he did go out on loan, you'd have half the Championship after him. So it wouldn't be an easy one for Boro to get done.

"But you know, we've seen weirder transfers, we've seen crazier transfers, and I hope it's one that we can get done, because it's in a position we very desperately need.

"I think it'd be a ridiculous signing for any Championship team."

Clarke has proven to be something of a cheat code in the Championship over the years, so it is easy to see why he was on Middlesbrough's summer wishlist, but it is no surprise that they were unable to get a deal over the line for the winger.

While he has continued to be deployed mainly as a substitute by new manager Gary O'Neil, Clarke has scored two goals and registered three assists in 11 appearances for Ipswich this season during what has been a competitive start to life back in the Premier League for the Suffolk side.

However, Boro are unlikely to be watching Clarke in the top flight with too much envy thanks to the form of Amario Cozier-Duberry, who has starred since arriving at the Riverside Stadium on a season-long loan from Brighton & Hove Albion in the summer.

Cozier-Duberry scored 10 goals and provided 13 assists in 41 games to help Bolton Wanderers to promotion from League One via the play-offs last season, and he has seamlessly made the step up to the Championship with Boro.

The 21-year-old has netted three goals and registered five assists in his first 11 appearances for Boro, and he has arguably been a huge upgrade on the club's top scorer from last season, Morgan Whittaker, who departed for MLS side Colorado Rapids in September for a fee of $10.7 million (£7.9 million).

With Cozier-Duberry providing a constant threat on the right, Boro are currently the third-highest scorers in the Championship behind West Ham United and Norwich City, having bagged 30 goals in their opening 15 games.
