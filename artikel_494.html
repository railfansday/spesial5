Anthropic IPO Draws 'Massive Enthusiasm' From Investors: Ludlow & Bloxham

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Anthropic PBC and its seven co-founders raised a record $107.3 billion in an upsized IPO and private placement, becoming the first frontier AI lab to tap public markets for the development of thinking machines. The artificial intelligence firm sold 956.4 million shares and the co-founders sold 23.6 million shares for $83 each, according to a statement Wednesday confirming an earlier Bloomberg News report, above the marketed range. The company had marketed 801.4 million shares, while the co-founders offered 23.6 million shares, in a range of $70 to $78 each. For a preview, Bloomberg's Nathan Hager speaks with Bloomberg Tech host Ed Ludlow and Matthew Bloxham of Bloomberg Intelligence. (Source: Bloomberg)
