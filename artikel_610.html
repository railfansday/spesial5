The Kindle Scribe Colorsoft has hit a record-low price in Amazons Black Friday sale — score $200 off

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

SAVE $200: As of Nov. 19, the 64GB Kindle Scribe Colorsoft has dropped to its lowest-ever price of $479.99 in Amazon's Black Friday sale. That's $200 off its full price of $679.99.

Amazon has officially launched its Black Friday sale, and naturally it features a ton of great discounts on its own devices. Kindles are in the mix, with the sleek 64GB Kindle Scribe Colorsoft sporting a $200 price cut.

As of Nov. 19, the price of this Kindle has plummeted from $679.99 to $479.99. It's the lowest price we've ever seen at Amazon. Mashable's Samantha Mangino is a fan, writing in her review that it's an "exceptional e-reader with strong writing capabilities." From reading to taking notes, the Kindle Scribe Colorsoft can handle it all. Its slim, lightweight build (coming in at just 5.4mm and 400g) is perfect for slipping into a bag or backpack to take on the go, while its 11-inch glare-free Colorsoft display is comfortable on your eyes and brings out colors on screen.

It also comes with a pen you can use to take notes, which features a built-in eraser and a shortcut button to access tools like the highlighter. Plus, its battery life can last up to eight weeks for reading or up to two weeks for writing.

Mangino says in her review that this 64GB Kindle Scribe Colorsoft is "fast, bright, and well-designed, but it's still overpriced." Well, not anymore. If it's been on your radar, now is the time to strike while this Black Friday deal is live.
