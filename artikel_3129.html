Netflix Releases New Peter Berg-Directed Spot Inspired By ‘Friday Night Lights’ Highlighting Thanksgiving Eve Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Netflix is streaming its first Thanksgiving Eve Game and has dropped a spot directed by Peter Berg, which was inspired by Friday Night Lights.

The new spot titled “Thankful for Football” is narrated by Kyle Chandler and highlights the game between the Los Angeles Rams and the Green Bay Packers.

“Life is a collection of moments. We will be thankful for these moments, the good and the bad,” Chandler narrates. “These moments are indicators that we are alive, and life is precious.”

Chandler’s narration continued, “Today, we are thankful for our lives, our teammates’ lives, our families’ lives, and all the moments that we are blessed to experience.”

This was the second spot to tap into fans’ nostalgia for Friday Night Lights and the fictional Coach Taylor, played by Chandler. Earlier this year, Netflix streamed the first international game of the 2026 NFL season. The game, which saw the Los Angeles Rams face the San Francisco 49ers in Melbourne, also had a similar spot directed by Berg and narrated by Chandler, which you can relive here. Watch on Deadline

“We won’t let the high moments take us too high, and we won’t let the low moments take us too low. We will play with gratitude. I am thankful for your love. Right here. Right now,” Chandler says in the new spot.

Netflix will release two more spots with this duo, one on Christmas Day and the final one during Week 18 of the football season.

The Thanksgiving Eve spot ends with Chandler saying, “Happy Thanksgiving. Clear eyes, full hearts. Can’t lose.”

Friday Night Lights was a film (2004) and a series (2006) developed by Peter Berg based on a book by H. G. Bissinger. The series, which ran for five seasons, follows a high school football team from a small Texas community.

Netflix’s Thanksgiving Eve game streams on Wednesday, November 25, starting at 8 p.m. ET / 5 p.m. PT and will feature U2 during the halftime show.
