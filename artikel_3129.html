Three new iPhones will launch early next year, here’s what’s coming

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Apple recently launched the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo, but three more new iPhone models will reportedly arrive in early 2027. Here’s what’s coming.

iPhone 18 was conspicuously absent from Apple’s annual September event this year. Instead, the company launched iPhone 18 Pro and kept iPhone 17 around as the base model.

But the iPhone 18 is still coming, expected to launch in early 2027—likely February or March.

The basic design of the iPhone 18 is expected to stay unchanged as Apple prioritizes internal upgrades. Rumored new features include a more powerful A20 chip with at least a slight RAM bump, plus increased battery life.

We’re not expecting iPhone 18 Pro’s camera upgrades, smaller Dynamic Island, or vapor chamber to come to the base model. One question mark is the C2 cellular modem. iPhone 17 uses a Qualcomm modem, and it’s unclear whether that will be the case with iPhone 18 as well.

Apple also has a lower-cost iPhone 18e model reportedly in the works. It’s expected to launch around the same time as iPhone 18.
