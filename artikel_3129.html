I've been writing about the Apple Watch for the past decade, so I know what I'm looking for when the Black Friday deals drop. Whether you're after the new Apple Watch Ultra 4 to track your next marathon, or you're investing in your first Apple Watch, this weekend is a good time to buy.

Right now, the best deal for most people is the Apple Watch SE 3, which has hit a low of $179 right now, saving you 28% on it's normal price. Not for you? Read on to find all my top Apple Watch Black Friday deals.

The Apple Watch SE 3 is the most affordable Apple Watch on the market. It doesn't have Apple's latest heart rate monitor, or an ECG or blood oxygen sensor, but if you're after a smartwatch that'll give you a good indication of your overall health, you can't go wrong. The biggest saving right now is on the 40mm GPS version of the watch, which is down to $179.

The larger 44mm version of the Apple Watch SE 3 without cellular is also on sale today. The biggest saving is on the Midnight Sport band version.

If you're looking for cellular connectivity as well as GPS, the biggest saving right now is on the 40mm Apple Watch SE 3, which is down to $229 in the Black Friday sale.

You can also save 20% on Apple's newest Watch 12 this Black Friday. This deal is on the watch with GPS and cellular connectivity, plus it's packed with Apple's new heart rate monitor and Readiness score.

The larger 46mm version of the Apple Watch Series 12 is also on sale, but the savings aren't quite as big. You'll still save 18% by buying today.

The Apple Watch Ultra 4 is 19% off during the Black Friday sales. This is Apple's most rugged adventure watch, with the brand's newest heart rate monitor and Readiness score. It's the watch I wear every day, and the best Apple Watch if you're serious about tracking your training.
