‘Hooters: The Movie’ Sets Owen Patrick Joyner, Kyle Clark & Audrey Trullinger

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

EXCLUSIVE: Hooters: The Movie is officially open for business, meaning cameras are rolling in the Tampa Bay, Florida area with Owen Patrick Joyner, Kyle Clark and Audrey Trullinger starring in the origin story of the chicken wing chain.

Pic follows lifelong friends Ted and Lenny (Joyner and Clark) who unexpectedly build a restaurant empire. What begins as a spontaneous road trip to Clearwater Beach in pursuit of Diane (Trullinger), a young woman Ted first spots in a spring break commercial, turns into an adventure neither friend could have anticipated with Diane becoming the very first Hooters Girl. Tampa Bay is where the first Hooters restaurant opened in 1983. Joining the ensemble are Carmen Electra (Baywatch, Scary Movie), David Koechner (Anchorman), WWE Superstar and former Hooters Girl Liv Morgan, Tanner Novlan (The Bold and the Beautiful), Louie Lombardi (Oppenheimer), Caitlin Serros (Night Visions), and Ava Allan (Young Sheldon, BUSTED). The film also features current Hooters Girls actress Gwynn Ali, Kiana Mahabir, the 2025 Hooters Calendar Cover Girl, and Kenzie Davis, winner of the Miss Hooters International 2026 pageant. Casting was led by Lisa London and Catherine Stroud of London/Stroud Casting.

“There’s a whole lot more to the story than orange shorts and chicken wings,” said Marsha Droste, EP of Hooters: The Movie and leader of the Hooters Girl Alumnae community. “From the beginning, it was important to us that the women who helped build this brand had a voice in telling its story. Former Hooters Girls have shared their experiences, humor and memories to help shape the screenplay and bring authenticity to the characters. I’m especially proud that both current and former Hooters Girls are appearing in the film.”

“Even just a few days into production, the energy on set has been incredible,” said the pic’s director Jon Rosenbaum. “There’s so much personality in this story, from two friends stumbling into something much bigger than themselves to the people who helped make Hooters what it is today. We’re having a lot of fun bringing these characters and their stories to life, and I think audiences will have just as much fun watching it.”

Hooters: The Movie is also produced by Ed Droste, Chuck Melcher, Bill Williams, Elayne Schmidt, and Michael E. Brown.

Joyner (Final Destination: Bloodlines) is repped by Prototype Talent Agency, Protege Entertainment and Myman Greenspan Fox etc; Clark (Crew Girl, Riverdale) is repped by Nuance Talent Management, Linden Entertainment and Yorn Levine Barnes etc; and Trullinger (Law & Order: Special Victims Unit) is repped by Gersh, Authentic Talent and Literary Management, and Felker McGinnis & Ryan.
