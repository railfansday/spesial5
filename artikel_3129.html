Alabama vs LSU live updates: Time, TV channel, pregame news and notes

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

BATON ROUGE, La. — Alabama football's College Football Playoff hopes are on the line as the Crimson Tide heads to Baton Rouge.

Alabama kicks off against LSU at 2:30 p.m. CT Saturday, Nov. 7 on ABC.
