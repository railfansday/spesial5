Why Monte Rosa Therapeutics Stock Slumped Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The biotech aims to raise fresh capital, again.

By Eric Volkman – Nov 27, 2026 at 7:34PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Monte Rosa Therapeutics ›

Monte Rosa Therapeutics (GLUE -3.85%) is going to the investor well again for new financing, and Mr. Market wasn't happy about it. That feeling translated into a sell-off on Friday that pushed the company's share price down by almost 4%.

In a pair of regulatory filings, Monte Rosa disclosed that it is switching the underwriters assisting it in raising funds through share issues. It terminated its relationship with Jefferies (JEF +1.24%) and is now collaborating with TD Securities USA.

Pursuant to that, Monte Rosa announced an at-the-market secondary stock offering of up to $100 million worth of its common stock.

As is typical with such arrangements, the company will sell this equity from time to time through various means, including public offerings and private transactions. TD Securities USA will be paid up to 3% of the gross proceeds of such sales.

Monte Rosa said it will use the raised funds to finance research and clinical development of its experimental drugs, capital expenditures, working capital, and for "general corporate purposes."

Monte Rosa, which develops drugs called molecular glue degraders to treat a variety of disorders, raised $23.9 million in net proceeds under the arrangement with Jefferies. Investors were surely dismayed that the new relationship could potentially be more than four times that size and lead to significant dilution -- the company's market cap is $767 million at present.

While it’s not unusual for clinical-stage biotechs to tap investors for new financing, some are probably concerned that Monte Rosa has lately made a habit of it; meanwhile, it had over $578 million in cash and short-term investments on its books at the end of September. I think it'll have to demonstrate meaningful progress in at least one of its investigational programs to win back investors' hearts.
