GTA 6 Ditched Mission Replay Because It ‘Felt At Odds With The Story’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Story missions in Grand Theft Auto 6 are one and done. This means no going back and experiencing some of its biggest set pieces and most memorable moments, and no second chances to complete them perfectly. Mission replay wasn’t always available in Grand Theft Auto. Earlier entries didn’t have it either. Rockstar added the option starting with the Ballad of Gay Tony expansion pack for GTA 4. Replayable story missions returned in GTA 5. A medal system showed how well players had completed secondary objectives, with many going back until they’d achieved a mission’s highest rating.

GTA 6 is a different kind of GTA game, though. It has a lot of Red Dead Redemption 2‘s DNA in it, with an emphasis on immersive storytelling and realistically simulating elements of an open-world experience.
