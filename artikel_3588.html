Cooper Hoffman is quickly establishing himself as one of the most promising young actors in Hollywood. His breakout performance in Licorice Pizza earned him widespread recognition and a Golden Globe nomination. Since then, he's continued to build up and diversify his resume with a variety of films.

From the dark comedy of Wildcat, to the Stephen King classic The Long Walk, Hoffman has demonstrated just how skilled he is at taking on complex characters and working across different genres.

Hoffman continues to explore a range of genres with several more exciting projects on the horizon. Whether he's stepping into comedy, drama, horror, or something entirely different, his filmography is only getting better and better.

One of Hoffman's most high-profile upcoming projects is Artificial, a biographical drama directed by Luca Guadagnino and written by Simon Rich. The film explores the rise of OpenAI, tracing two decades of developments in artificial intelligence and the power struggles that shaped the company.

Specifically, it revisits the turbulent November 2023 boardroom crisis where CEO Sam Altman was removed from his position.

Hoffman stars as Greg Brockman, OpenAI's co-founder and president, alongside Andrew Garfield as Altman, Yura Borisov as Ilya Sutskever, Monica Barbaro as Mira Murati, and Mark Rylance as Geoffrey Hinton. Other ensemble actors include Ike Barinholtz, Jason Schwartzman, and Chris O'Dowd.

With Guadagnino behind the camera for this story about one of the most consequential developments in modern technology, Artificial is gearing up to be a particularly intriguing addition to Hoffman's filmography. It premiered at the New York Film Festival in October 2026 and is scheduled to release in theaters December 25, 2026.

Based on the unbelievable true story of the Danbury Trashers, this movie follows the rise of the infamous minor-league hockey team that became known for its aggressive playing and controversial reputation.

Hoffman stars as A.J. Galante, the team's 17-year-old general manager who became the team's general manager after his father, Jimmy Galante, purchased the franchise. David Harbour is cast as Jimmy and Olivia DeJonge will also appear in the movie.

Directed by Cooper Raiff, The Trashers explores the unusual family dynamic behind the team and the wild events that helped turn the Danbury Trashers into a hockey phenomenon. With a mix of sports, family drama, and controversial real-life events, Hoffman once again has the opportunity to take on another fascinating "based on a true story" kind of project.

The next exciting project on Hoffman's upcoming slate is The Chaperones, an A24 crime comedy-drama directed by India Donaldson and written by Sebastian Black.

Set in the days following Christmas, the film follows a drug dealer and his two slacker friends who are hired to transport a troubled teenager across the country. What begins as a seemingly straightforward job turns into an unpredictable road-trip adventure filled with unexpected chaos.

Hoffman stars alongside a stellar ensemble cast that features Alfred Molina, Paul Dano, David Jonsson, Danielle Deadwyler, Tim Meadows, and more. Produced by Plan B Entertainment, The Chaperones brings together a talented cast and creative team for a genre-blending story that gives Hoffman the opportunity to show his range as a comedic actor.

Hoffman is also set to appear in Jonah, a feature film adaptation of Rachel Bonds' acclaimed Off-Broadway play. Directed by Tony Award winner Danya Taymor in her feature film directorial debut, the drama stars Greta Lee as Ana, a woman whose sudden flood of memories forces her to revisit the relationships and experiences that have shaped her life over the last two decades.

Hoffman joins the supporting cast alongside Edmund Donovan and Hagan Oliveras, who reprises his titular role from the stage production. Bonds also adapted the screenplay herself. Filmed in Cincinnati, Ohio, the project brings together an exciting creative team to translate the emotional stage show into a feature film.

One of Hoffman's biggest upcoming projects is Durango, a crime drama series coming to Hulu. Created and written by Eliza Clark, the series follows Mikey (Hoffman), a ski bum and legendary snowboarder who finds himself on the run alongside Bunny (Kathryn Newton), a homeschooled runaway working as a waitress.

After they steal some money, they become targets for the local police, dangerous criminals, and Mikey's Russian wife, Irina (Abbey Lee), and form an unexpected romance on the journey. The series also stars Merritt Wever as Joni, a local detective, and Mason Gooding as Miles, a young detective caught up in the investigation.

Blending crime, romance, and dark comedy, Durango will marks another major step into television for Hoffman.
