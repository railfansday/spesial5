Summer Intern – Quality (Health Policy/Economics) – Remote

The American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) is currently hiring a remote Summer Intern for our Quality (CAPE) department. This part-time, seasonal position will offer challenging work/project(s) within an exciting industry. The Summer Intern will have the opportunity to collaborate with experienced Association professionals in an innovative, mission-driven, and member-centric environment.

The Center for Anesthesia and Perioperative Economics (CAPE) Intern will gain hands-on experience in health policy, economics, and strategy while supporting research on economic issues affecting anesthesiology. Working with staff and physician members, the intern will contribute to high-priority research and policy initiatives.

The American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) team provides education, resources, and advocacy to help our members improve the experience and safety of patients in their care. You can be a part of this important work—join more than 160 change-makers, collaborators, and advocates as we continually work towards excellence.
