Dybala resumes Roma training after recovering from injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Roma returns to Trigoria. And encouraging signs are starting to emerge from the pitch.

After the three-day rest granted by Gasperini following the win against Sassuolo, the Giallorossi resumed training today.

The coach will have to work without the players currently on international duty, but he will be able to use the break to try to recover the injured players ahead of the restart of the season.

The first good news concerns Paulo Dybala and Gianluca Mancini.

Both completed part of the session with the rest of the group, then continued with a separate training program.

Pierluigi Gollini and Mile Svilar, meanwhile, are working individually. For the Belgian goalkeeper, this is primarily a return to activity after days spent in the infirmary, while the former Atalanta player is also continuing his recovery.

The next stop is already scheduled: Parma at the Tardini, the first match after the international break.

The injured players’ conditions will be monitored daily, without rushing the schedule. However, there is great optimism from Trigoria regarding the possibility of recovering the players currently sidelined.
