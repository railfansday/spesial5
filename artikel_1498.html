Svilar to undergo final round of scans on injured foot

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Mile Svilar was the unexpected absentee from this second set of matches before the break.

From Como vs. Roma to the home game against Sassuolo: nine matches without the best goalkeeper in Serie A.

The Giallorossi maintained a high level of competitiveness, but conceded significantly more goals.

The Roman side went from conceding four goals in just six games (0.66) to 14 in nine matches (1.55): the average has more than doubled. Gasperini is waiting for the Giallorossi’s number 99 after the break to secure his goal once again.

After the break, Gian Piero Gasperini will be able to count on Svilar, Dybala, and Rensch again.

As reported by Corriere della Sera, the Giallorossi goalkeeper will undergo a final check-up in the next few hours before removing the brace from his right foot and receiving the green light to return to the field.
