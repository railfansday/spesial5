PSG sets 12 records in the Guinness Book

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Paris Saint-Germain continues to write its history. After its numerous successes in France and on the European scene, the capital club officially enters the Football 2027 edition of Guinness World Records, with 12 recognized world records, including seven collective and five individual. Among the most notable performances is the historic victory against Inter Milan (5-0) in the final of the 2025 Champions League, which became the largest ever recorded at this stage of the competition. PSG is also rewarded for its 45 goals scored during the 2025-2026 European campaign. On the national scene, the Parisians are seeing several of their exploits enter the history books: 39 consecutive trips without defeat in Ligue 1 between 2023 and 2025, a series of 36 matches without losing in the championship between 2015 and 2016, as well as 14 victories in a row during the 2018-2019 season. The record 96 points obtained in 2015-2016 and the club’s 14 French championship titles complete this list.

A publication shared by Paris Saint-Germain (@psg)

Beyond the collective performances, five Parisian players are also distinguished for their individual exploits. Khvicha Kvaratskhelia is rewarded for having been involved in at least one goal in seven consecutive Champions League knockout matches, while Vitinha holds the record for 1,602 passes completed in a single edition of the competition. Désiré Doué also appears in this list thanks to his three decisive contributions during the European final won against Inter Milan. In Ligue 1, Fabián Ruiz distinguished himself with an impressive series of 51 consecutive matches without defeat. Finally, Marquinhos now has a unique record in the history of the French championship, with 11 titles won under the Parisian jersey. This international recognition rewards several generations of players who have contributed to the success of PSG, from its national domination to its European performances.

Find all the records for the year 2027 in the football edition of Guiness Worlds Records available everywhere.
