There are six Great Fairy Fountains in Zelda OoT, each one with a power and upgrade

The Great Fairy Fountains in Zelda Ocarina of Time Remake are unlocked in different ways, and each of the six Fairies will grant you a brand new power or upgrade that can't be unlocked any other way. Some of these are hugely important, and though none of the Great Fairies are mandatory to progress, they can make the OoT hugely easier and arguably more fun - as long as you can afford the hefty prices involved in getting them to bestow their powers and gifts on you. There are six Great Fairies in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Below I'll lay out a quick summary of how to find all of them, and go into further depth on each one below.

North Death Mountain Trail (Child), left of the entrance to the Death Mountain Summit behind a cracked rock.

Din's Fire. Summon a firestorm around Link that burns nearby enemies at the cost of 2 Magic.

Hyrule Castle (Child), on the righthand side just past the gatehouse behind a cracked rock.

Gift of Improved Spin. Increase the range and damage of your spin attack.

Zora's Fountain (Child), on the Southeast Wall behind a cracked rock.

Farore's Wind. Place a marker that you can warp back to at any time at the cost of 2 Magic.

Death Mountain Crater, (Adult), on the West wall behind a stronger boulder.

Gift of Doubled Magic. Double the maximum capacity of your magic meter.

Evil Desert Colossus (Adult), on the North cliff face behind a cracked rock.

Nayru's Love. Trigger temporary invulnerability at the cost of 4 Magic.

Ganondorf's Castle (Adult), on the North wall, to the right of the entrance bridge, behind a very heavy stone you need to lift.

Gift of Halved Damage. Permanently take half damage from all enemies and damage sources.

Whenever you find a Great Fairy fountain, you'll have to give them a certain amount of Rupees in order to get their gift. Because of the high costs involved in the later fountains, you'll have to unlock larger wallets through the Gold Skulltula side quest simply to be able to pay for the high cost Fairies.

The first Great Fairy most will find is in the North of the Death Mountain Trail, behind a rock that can be destroyed with Bombs. You'll need to have cleared Goron City and Dodongo's Cavern to unlock Bombs, but you can come straight here as a child afterwards.

Straight after unlocking bombs, you can also head back to Hyrule Castle. Past the gatehouse, just on the right hand side, is another rock you can blow up with explosives. The Great Fairy's fountain is just past that.

As part of the quest to find Ruto in Zelda Ocarina of Time, you'll be given access to the Zora's Fountain, behind the Zora's Domain. Once you make it here, head to the Southernmost point to find a wall you can blow up with bombs to find the Farore's Wind fairy.

After having completed the Fire Temple as an adult, you'll be returned to this entrance area, now having unlocked the Megaton Hammer, used to destroy larger boulders. Go to the West side of the area and there'll be one you can smash to gain access to the fountain.

Having made it to the Desert Colossus as an adult, before you go inside, check the Northern cliffs to find a rock that can be destroyed either with Bombs or the Hammer.

The final, most powerful Fairy is found as an adult at the entrance to Ganondorf's Castle, on the East Wall. There's a boulder here you need the Golden Gauntlets to lift, which are found in Ganondorf's Castle itself. Once you have them, come back out and pay the 500 Rupees for this major endgame buff.
