These Thanksgiving Hairstyles Are Perfect for Everything From Friendsgiving to Family Dinner

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Thanksgiving is one of those holidays where I wanna look cute, but I also have approximately one million other things to worry about. Between figuring out what to wear, preparing dinner, and making sure the table is set, I’m also trying to look fashionable and stay comfortable. Ideally, I want to do both of those things without spending hours styling my outfit or my hair.

Luckily, I found a bunch of Thanksgiving-worthy hairstyles prove that looking put together doesn’t have to equal time-consuming. Plus, since you’re amongst family and friends, the holiday is the perfect excuse to experiment with some new or elevated styles. Think bouncy blowouts and sleek buns that pair perfectly with your favorite fall sweater or half-up styles finished with velvet bows for a lil holiday flair. Ahead, the cutest Thanksgiving hairstyles to screenshot now. I promise these are quick, effortless, and totally chic, so they won’t take you away from your food, football, or family for too long.

There’s nothing quite as classic as a big, bouncy blowout. Use a round brush and hair dryer or a blow dryer blush of your choice to get these voluminous waves à la Sabrina Carpenter. This big, glossy volume looks especially great with a fall sweater.

A festive accessory makes a simple style feel so special. Channel your inner J.Lo with this sleek half-up, half-down look, finished off with a velvet bow to get in the holiday spirit.

Take a cue from Olivia Dean and let your natural curls do the talking this Thanksgiving. These voluminous ringlets are equal parts effortless and glamorous. Work some mousse into your roots, scrunch to define your curls, and diffuse your hair for maximum bounce and body.

Ponytails are great, but give the classic look a playful upgrade with a bubble braid that’s cute and easy to recreate. Simply secure your hair into a ponytail, add elastics every few inches, and gently tug each section out to create the bubble effect. It’s a fun, trendy option for Thanksgiving that keeps your hair out of your face.

Leave it to Anya Taylor-Joy to make a simple braid look totally elegant. This low side braid proves that you don’t need an elaborate updo to make a statement. Braid your hair loosely on one side and secure with an elastic—you can opt for a discreet clear one for a subtle finish, add an elastic in a autumnal shade for a more festive finish, or really go all out by clipping a bow on the end. Either way, it’s a perfect Thanksgiving hairstyle that takes just moments to create.

Hailey Bieber can make even the seemingly simplest hairstyle look super chic, and her flippy bob is no exception. Channel her style with a retro-inspired sleek bob with ends that flip outward for some extra movement. Recreate the look with a round brush and blow-dryer or a flat iron, curling the ends away from your face.

Why settle for one braid when you can have two? Double braided ponytails are a playful way to switch up your Thanksgiving hairstyle. The how-to is simple: Split your ponytail into two pieces and braid each length separately.

Effortlessly cool, a half-up, half-down hairstyle is proof that sometimes less really is more. It’s the perfect balance between polished and undone, keeping hair away from the face while letting the rest of the lengths flow freely. It’s an especially chic and easy option for Thanksgiving dinner that’s Zoe Kravitz-approved.

Nobody does quiet luxury beauty quite like Sofia Richie Grainge, and her signature slicked-back low bun is the definition of understated elegance. You can get the look for yourself for your Thanksgiving hairstyle: Create a clean center part, smooth your hair back with styling gel or cream, and twist it into a low bun at the nape of your neck. It’s sophisticated, timeless, and practically guaranteed to survive the whole night, from appetizers to dinner to dessert.

Sometimes the chicest hairstyles are also the easiest. Case in point: a French twist with a claw clip. For a Thanksgiving-ready take on the classic French twist, gather your hair at the nape of your neck, twist it upward, and secure it with your favorite claw clip. Leave a few face-framing pieces loose for an effortlessly undone finish, or slick everything back for a more polished look.

Ayo Edebiri makes intricate braids look effortlessly cool. Her sew-in-inspired braided design adds a little extra personality to a classic protective style. It’s a chic option for Thanksgiving festivities, especially if you want a hairstyle that makes a statement without requiring much day-of styling. Plus, the intricate detailing means your hair practically doubles as an accessory.

This relaxed half-up, half-down hairstyle is proof that you don't need a complicated updo to look put together for Thanksgiving. The effortless style lets you pull back the top section of your hair with an oversized leopard print bow (animal print is so in this season). It’s the perfect balance between casual and chic.
