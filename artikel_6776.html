Queen Camilla Arrives in Antigua and Barbuda in a Meaningful Poppy Brooch

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

She’s gearing up for Remembrance Day on November 11

Yesterday afternoon, Queen Camilla joined King Charles III in Antigua and Barbuda amidst his state visit to the Caribbean, after he spent the last few days traveling solo around Guyana and the Bahamas.

As she made her arrival, Camilla wore a turquoise tunic dress paired with tan flats, but it was the pin fastened to her chest that added a meaningful touch, a styling choice she borrowed from her daughter-in-law, Princess Catherine.

On November 11, the United Kingdom will celebrate Remembrance Day, a holiday that honors armed forces members who have died in the line of duty. Traditionally, royal family members commemorate the day by wearing red poppies, a symbol for veterans’ organizations that originated from the war poem “In Flanders Fields.”

But while royals most commonly wear felt poppies, here, Camilla chose a golden brooch from the Royal British Legion poppy shop.

Notably, the piece beared a resemblance to one worn by the Princess of Wales at the Remembrance Sunday memorial service back in 2019. There, Kate wore the Codebreakers Brooch, which acts as a tribute to workers in signal intelligence, with the design meant to emulate the rotors of the Enigma machines used by Allied forces during World War II.

That particular brooch had a special significance for Kate, because her grandmother, Valerie Glassborow, served as a code breaker at Bletchley Park, where she helped decipher German messages during the Second World War.

Now, while Camilla appears to be sporting a slightly different style (hers features two diverging leaves on a golden stem), she’s following in Kate’s accessorizing footsteps for the holiday.

In fact, the queen consort seemed to favor the brooch so much, she chose to wear it with her two following outfits, too. We’ll be keeping an eye out to see how many more appearances it makes during the rest of the trip.
