10 Airbnbs in New Mexico to Book This Winter, from Santa Fe and Beyond

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

There’s something undeniably magical about New Mexico, especially in the colder months. Chile ristras line front porches, ski season revs up in Taos, and farolitos light the streets in December. The days are sunny and dry, but nights are cold enough for a kiva fireplace or a hot tub soak under the stars. It makes for a different kind of trip than a cozy New England getaway or fall foliage-inspired cabin retreat, the desert awash in sunset hues. Plus, New Mexico is prime road-trip territory, with its wide-open highways and dramatic landscape shifts.

From the powdery white dunes of White Sands National Park to the art-saturated streets of Santa Fe, the state has inspired artists and creative types for years upon years. Whether you want to lose yourself in the vast vistas that captivated Georgia O'Keeffe or spend a long weekend gallery-hopping and market-browsing in Santa Fe, we've rounded up a selection of Airbnbs that feel as distinctive as the state itself to serve as your home base.

Ahead, 10 New Mexico rentals that sit at the intersection of design and desert living.

Artfully designed by fashion designer Raquel Allegra, the 8,000-square-foot space sits on five acres in a quiet canyon about 20 minutes from Taos Ski Valley. The interiors are anchored by signature New Mexican architectural features such as arched windows, white plaster walls, high ceilings, and wood-burning kiva fireplaces, while the furnishings are design-forward yet restrained, allowing the home’s bones to really sing.

With six bedrooms, it’s an expansive enough space for a ski trip with friends or a creative group retreat; one Vogue writer even booked it for an off-the-beaten-path bachelorette. Radiant heated floors and a steady supply of firewood keep the home toasty after a day on the slopes. And with no cell service available near the home, it’s very much your chance to completely unplug.

Amenities: Radiant heating, wood-burning kiva fireplaces, furnished porch, WiFi, garden views

Location: Taos Ski Valley, Taos, New Mexico

This 1940s Santa Fe adobe, once a corner store, is now an art gallery-like home whose walls are dotted with more than 50 nichos (small, recessed niches carved into the plaster). They’re a staple of Southwestern and Spanish colonial architecture, each lit like a museum case and, in the case of this property, filled with ceramics and other artful pieces.

Polished white cement floors and a white oak kitchen give the entire home the feeling of a clean palette; a calming backdrop for whatever your Santa Fe vacation holds. Its four bedrooms make it a good fit for a family ski trip, with Ski Santa Fe 30 minutes away and a hot tub and fire pit waiting in the private backyard for your return. The River Trail is a block away, Canyon Road is close by, and dogs are welcome.

Amenities: Kiva fireplace, hot tub, firepit, soaking tub, chef’s kitchen, gated parking, dog-friendly

This hacienda was once a neighborhood shop where horses and burros carrying firewood were hitched out front. Today, sunny windows open onto a bright living room, and the house has three kiva fireplaces, shelves packed tightly with books and board games, and a renovated kitchen you can make your own during your stay.

Between the three bedrooms and the den (which has a sofa bed), this home sleeps eight, and two adjacent units can be rented for larger groups. Galleries and restaurants are right on its doorstep, too.

Amenities: Three kiva fireplaces, Sonos sound system, renovated kitchen, two private patios, washer and dryer

This listing has been high up on our Airbnb wishlist for years. Located in Abiquiu, not too far from O’Keeffe’s famed Ghost Ranch, this adobe sits squarely in the middle of 10 shared acres in the high desert landscape that drew O'Keeffe (and generations of artists and photographers after her) in.

This recently renovated home is a quiet base for exploring one of New Mexico's most creatively charged regions. The traditional architecture is intact with thick walls, desert light, wood beams, stone fireplaces, and classic New Mexican tile floors. The interiors are peppered with both man-made and natural relics, like a Noguchi-style hanging paper lantern or a sculptural tumbleweed hanging from the ceiling. It’s the kind of place O'Keeffe herself might have booked.

Amenities: Full kitchen, washer/dryer, mini-split AC and heating throughout, mountain views, dedicated workspace, WiFi

Designed by architect Bart Prince and featured in Architectural Digest, this sculptural home at the base of the Sandia Mountains is definitely a one-of-a-kind experience. Six viewing decks offer panoramic views of the mountains above and Albuquerque's city lights below, while floor-to-ceiling windows blur the line between indoors and out. A private hiking trail on the property connects directly to Sandia Peak; meaning the National Forest is essentially your backyard.

Amenities: 6 viewing decks, private hiking trail, floor-to-ceiling windows

Location: Sandia Mountains, Albuquerque, New Mexico

This mid-century adobe sits in Casa Alegre, a leafy pocket of Santa Fe’s Midtown District, where you’re a block from the River Trail and close to the galleries and shows at the El Rey. A traditional kiva fireplace anchors the living room, Sonos speakers run throughout, and one of the bedrooms comes with a cozy reading nook. With three bedrooms and a fully fenced yard, it works well for families young kids who want their own space for their holiday in the desert. On winter nights, the hot tub will be an especially beloved feature.

Amenities: Kiva fireplace, hot tub, Sonos sound system, fenced yard, washer and dryer, parking for three cars, dog friendly

Casa de la Cruz is a traditional adobe, built with stucco, giant vigas, and thick walls that stay warm in winter and cool in summer. The interiors reflect the Southwest aesthetic with wrought-iron light fixtures paired with Navajo blankets and pillows, leather sofas, and rustic, worn furniture sprinkled throughout.

Set on 12 acres abutting Carson National Forest in Abiquiu, the property has three kiva fireplaces, radiant heated floors, and a private box canyon to explore—which is an incredible selling point on its own. Plus, you can’t beat the location with Ghost Ranch being just 30 minutes away, the hot springs of Ojo Caliente another 30, and downtown Santa Fe under an hour, as well.

Amenities: Private box canyon, outdoor fire pit, full kitchen, outdoor grill, Starlink WiFi, 3 fireplaces, radiant heated floors

This charming home is located in Dixon—a small artist's village in the hills of Northern New Mexico—with wilderness walks straight from the door. The bones are traditional New Mexico adobe architecture with viga ceilings, Southwestern decor, works from local artists, and a newly remodeled kitchen. Surrounded by pinyon and sage bush views alongside the mountains, its exceptionally quiet and gorgeous sunsets are best taken in from the plush ramada with its built-in banco seating.

Amenities: Ramada, full kitchen, streaming TV, AppleTV, WiFi, central heating, pet-friendly

Featured in Dwell, this off-grid Earthship (a fitting description) outside Taos is a rare rental that beautifully intersects luxury and sustainability. The structure collects rainwater, generates its own solar power, grows food indoors, and uses water three times over—all without sacrificing comfort. Adobe interiors, a south-facing greenhouse, and whimsical touches (like a stained-glass mermaid) keep things interesting in the best way.

Amenities: Off-grid solar power, rainwater collection, indoor greenhouse, turntable, bluetooth, WiFi

This newly built guesthouse is part of the H.H. Dorman estate, rumored to have hosted D.H. Lawrence, Georgia O'Keeffe, Ansel Adams, and Aldous Huxley in the 1920s. The two-bedroom, two-bath home, which shares the grounds with the historic main house, is a short walk to Santa Fe plaza and features vaulted ceilings, a gas fireplace, one-of-a-kind antiques, and art throughout. It boasts two private courtyards, one with an outdoor dining area and another with a fire pit and hot tub, which guests are welcome to enjoy.

Amenities: Private hot tub, fire pit, two courtyards, full kitchen, gas fireplace, washer/dryer, gated parking
