Sugar Plum Thumbprint Cookies

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The sugar plums:Here, we’ve used walnuts, prunes, apricots, and dates, but feel free to switch it up as you like! Hazelnuts and dried cherries or pecans, almonds, and raisins would be lovely in the mix. The filling comes together in minutes in a mini food processor, but if you don’t have one, simply chop the mixture as finely as possible. (Use a little oil on your knife to make chopping a little easier.) It’s uncooked, but a little time in the oven gives the spices a chance to warm up and lets the flavor meld. To toast the walnuts, set them on a rimmed baking sheet and bake at 350° until fragrant and golden brown, about 8 minutes.
