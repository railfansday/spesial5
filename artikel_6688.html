Sydney Sweeney and Scooter Braun’s Complete Relationship Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Sydney Sweeney has entered a new chapter in her personal life. Months after ending her engagement to longtime partner Jonathan Davino, the Euphoria actress was linked to music executive Scooter Braun. Since their summer 2025 romance began, the pair has been spotted together in Los Angeles, New York City, Italy, and Lake Tahoe.

In April 2026, they went Instagram official.

Here is their relationship history so far.

Both Sweeney and Braun attended Jeff Bezos and Lauren Sánchez’s wedding festivities in Venice, Italy. A source later told People that the two “hung out in Italy for the Bezos wedding” and that “Syd was intrigued—Scooter’s a charmer. He’s confident, but also sweet and very attentive.”

Shortly after the wedding, TikTok clips surfaced showing Sweeney and Braun strolling through Venice. She wore a black floral dress, while he opted for a short-sleeve brown polo shirt and pale pink shorts.

Multiple outlets, including People and TMZ, confirmed that Sweeney and Braun were “casually dating” after being spotted on several outings. One source told TMZ, “Sydney has just ended a relationship and she’s doing what women in their 20s do—she’s dating.”

“Everything is casual. She is living her life and working hard,” an insider told People. Another source added, “He’s loving spending time with her and seeing where things go.” The pair also spent Labor Day weekend together at Lake Tahoe.

On September 24, Sweeney and Braun were photographed having dinner at Jon & Vinny’s in Brentwood.

A source speaking with Entertainment Tonight stated that they are attempting to keep things “low-key” at the moment.

“Sydney and Scooter are still seeing each other and trying to be as low-key as possible, while still dating and having fun,” the insider said. “They are just taking things slow and enjoying their time together.”

In late September, Sweeney and Braun were photographed holding hands at Universal Studios’ Halloween Horror Nights in Los Angeles, where they were joined by her parents, Lisa and Steven. The couple dressed in coordinating gray sweatshirts and baseball caps, with Sweeney wearing her hair in braids.

On October 5, the pair were seen in Manhattan holding hands.

On November 4, they were photographed kissing in Central Park and holding hands in Bryant Park.

Over Thanksgiving weekend, the couple was photographed at Sweeney’s home in the Florida Keys, joined by friends for a jet ski trip. Sweeney wore a burgundy bikini under her life vest.

At the start of the month, a source told People that Sweeney and Braun’s romance was no longer casual. “Scooter and Sydney are going strong and things between them are great,” the insider shared. “They’re in a committed relationship, and things are serious.”

On January 11, a source told People that Sweeney and Braun were doing well. “Sydney and Scooter are totally confident and comfortable in their relationship, and there are no trust issues going on,” the insider said. “Things between them are stronger than ever, and they’re in a great place.”

In late January, Sweeney and Braun were photographed holding hands during a casual daytime outing in Brentwood, California.

The sighting came after Sweeney addressed speculation about her love life in a Cosmopolitan cover story, saying, “Sometimes I’ll think that I can do a normal thing and have a date in a normal way, and then it gets perceived in a very non-normal way. It’s kind of sad.”

When asked how she decides when to go public with a relationship, she responded, “I understand that I’m a public person, but I’m still in my 20s. I’m still figuring out love, and it’s hard to do that with millions of people who have their own opinions of what that looks like. At the same time, for all of my 20s, I put my head down and focused on work—and now I want to experience things. But it’s hard deciding that I want to experience love in the public eye. I’m just navigating it all.”

On February 12, Sweeney and Braun were seen taking a stroll in Los Angeles. The pair held hands as they chatted. They were both wearing jeans and long-sleeve shirts, Sweeney in navy and Braun in white.

Braun joined Sweeney at the Euphoria season 3 premiere in Los Angeles on April 7. Roughly a week later, Sweeney went Instagram official with Braun, posting a photo of them together at the event with a white heart. He reposted her Instagram Story, adding, “Lucky bastard” as his caption.

On April 24, footage of the couple at Stagecoach started circulating online. In the clip, Sweeney is sitting on Braun’s shoulders as they watch Langley perform at the country music festival. The actress was smiling and singing along in a pair of coveralls and a lacy white shirt.

On May 1, Sweeney shared her first grid post featuring Braun. She shared photos of the two at Stagecoach, captioning the Instagram, “cowboy kind of weekend ♥️”

On May 28, Braun said more than ever before about their relationship in an episode of Second Thought with Suzy Weiss and The Free Press. When asked about Sweeney’s show, Euphoria, which was airing its newest season, he replied, “I’m catching it.”

“I’m biased, I like it. I think there’s been an incredible performance by a certain actress,” Braun said.

He went on to describe their relationship as “one of the biggest surprises ever” that offers him a sense of “self-worth.”

Braun added, “I’ve met an extraordinary woman who is kind and generous and smart, and real and down to earth.”

On May 30, they were seen in New York City, first in the afternoon and then the evening. Both times, the couple held hands as they walked. In the afternoon, Sweeney wore a dark violet dress with a cinched waist, matching heels, and sunglasses. Braun wore a black suit and charcoal tie.

That evening, she switched to a black babydoll dress with a plunging neckline, and he threw on a dark shirt under his suit.

On June 10, Sweeney and Braun attended Game 4 of the NBA Finals at Madison Square Garden, where they watched the New York Knicks secure a dramatic victory. Sweeney later shared glimpses of the outing on her Instagram Stories, including a courtside selfie of the pair in matching Knicks-inspired blue-and-orange jackets. She captioned one post, “This was an emotional rollercoaster.”

In another photo taken after the game, Braun embraced Sweeney as they stood inside the arena.

Braun publicly expressed his affection for Sweeney in a video shared on Instagram by Steve Rifkind after the New York Knicks won the 2026 NBA championship. While traveling aboard a private jet following the team’s victory, Braun raised a toast to the actress and said, “I love you so much.” He added, “You are the greatest good luck charm for a New York Knicks and Jets fan in history.”

On June 18, Sweeney celebrated Braun’s birthday with some photos of them dancing together in her Instagram Stories. Over the first picture, she wrote, “Happiest of birthdays to the man with the biggest heart I know,” adding a red heart emoji.

She added over the second, “Thank you for all the living room dances and making me smile again. A good man is rare. A man like you is once in a lifetime.”

On July 25, Sweeney shared photos from a trip abroad with Braun. The couple appeared to be enjoying some time in nature together, hopping around New Zealand in a helicopter. She included a photo of herself kissing Braun on the cheek against a gorgeous water and mountain backdrop.

In the caption of her carousel, she wrote, “jumped over to queenstown.”

On August 22, Sweeney shared a carousel of photos and videos from “camp,” or what looks like a vacation with friends and family on a gorgeous lake. The collection included two images of the actress dressed up as Wonder Woman in a body-hugging corset and of Braun as Superman, with a cape and all.

In a video, Sweeney can be seen wearing the outfit as she completes some sort of fun obstacle course on the water.

On Friday, November 6, Sweeney shared a glimpse of her fall in New York City alongside friends, family, and Braun. The actress shared photos on her Instagram Stories, including a photo of herself embracing Braun.

She also shared a video of Braun playfully hiding beneath his brown flannel and raising his arms to imitate the Headless Horseman.
