Ana de Armas’s Complete Dating History

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

From Ben Affleck to Tom Cruise and beyond.

Ana de Armas has experienced a stratospheric rise in Hollywood over the past decade. 2015’s Knock Knock—a thriller starring Keanu Reeves—provided the Cuban, American, and Spanish actress with her stateside breakout role. Since then, de Armas has appeared in numerous box office and critical hits, such as 2017’s Blade Runner 2049, 2019’s Knives Out, 2021’s No Time to Die, 2022’s Blonde, and 2025’s John Wick spin-off Ballerina.

Offscreen, the actress has had several public romances, too. Most recently, she has been linked to Venezuelan singer Beto Montenegro, with the pair photographed kissing during a summer getaway in Spain. In November 2026, they made their first red carpet appearance together at the Latin Recording Academy’s Person of the Year Gala in Las Vegas.

Here’s what you need to know about Ana de Armas’s dating history.

Prior to making it big as a Hollywood actress, de Armas relocated from Havana to Spain, where she met her first husband, Marc Clotet. The Ghosted star began dating the Spanish model and actor in 2010, with the pair marrying the following year in July 2011, Hola! magazine reported.

Unfortunately, the couple’s marriage didn’t last. During an appearance at the 27th Goya Awards in Spain on Feb. 17, 2013, Clotet revealed, “Yes, we’ve broken up. It was by mutual agreement. I’m calm.” According to Diez Minutos, Clotet also shared that he and de Armas were “still friends” despite the split.

According to La Razón, de Armas started dating Spanish screenwriter and director David Victori in August 2013. The publication also noted that the couple lived together in Los Angeles, but had returned to Spain in March 2014 for the Málaga Film Festival. Although it’s unclear when Victori and de Armas ended their relationship, it’s been reported that they split sometime in 2014.

Following her breakups from Clotet and Victori, de Armas decided to move to Los Angeles full time to further her career, telling The Hollywood Reporter, “I don’t like feeling stuck, like I’m not going anywhere. I wanted more.”

After permanently relocating to America, de Armas began “a serious relationship with agent Franklin Latt,” Vanity Fair reported. During her interview with the publication, the War Dogs actress was quick to dispel the idea that dating a successful Hollywood agent—Latt now manages Pedro Pascal at Creative Artists Agency—aided her career in any way. “For anyone who ever questions...how did I get to do this or that, fuck them,” she said. “They will not get to spend their New Year’s with me. They are not the people whose opinion I should care about. They are not the people I share my happiness with.”

In 2017, de Armas started dating artist Alejandro Piñeiro Bello, who was also born in Havana. During their relationship, the pair regularly posted photos of one another on social media, which have since been deleted from their respective accounts.

In February 2018, de Armas marked the launch of Piñeiro Bello’s pop-up exhibition in Los Angeles by writing on Instagram, “So proud of you,” and he returned the favor, thanking the actress for all of her “hard work and love,” according to Antena 3.

Porter later confirmed that de Armas had split from Piñeiro Bello in 2019 after two years of dating.

After meeting on the set of thriller Deep Water in late 2019, de Armas started a very public relationship with Ben Affleck. In March 2020, a source told People that the pair had “great chemistry right from the start,” but “there were no signs of romance” until filming had wrapped. In a March 2020 interview with Vogue Spain (translated by People), de Armas discussed working with the Oscar winner, saying, “His talent is infinite.” The same month, de Armas made her relationship with Affleck official when she shared photos of him on Instagram.

Affleck and de Armas were regularly photographed walking their dogs together in Los Angeles, where the couple quarantined together during the early days of the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020.

In June 2020, de Armas and Affleck attended a Black Lives Matter march together, per People. Following a double date with Affleck’s best friend, Matt Damon, and his wife, People reported that de Armas officially moved into her boyfriend’s property in August 2020. “She’s aware that his family comes first,” a source told the outlet. “And appreciates that. They work very hard at that balance.” For Affleck’s 48th birthday, the Overdrive actress gave her beau a custom-built BMW motorcycle, according to People.

In November 2020, the pair traveled to New Orleans for reshoots of their movie Deep Water. As de Armas appeared to be wearing a diamond ring in photos from the set, rumors started to swirl regarding a possible engagement. However, according to People, the jewelry item was simply a prop for the film. The very same month, de Armas and Affleck spent their first Thanksgiving together, along with the actor’s three children—Violet, Fin, and Samuel—whom he shares with ex-wife Jennifer Garner, E! News reported.

Unfortunately, by January 2021, de Armas and Affleck had called it quits. “Ben is no longer dating Ana,” a source told People. “She broke it off. Their relationship was complicated. Ana doesn’t want to be Los Angeles-based and Ben obviously has to since his kids live in Los Angeles.” People reported that the breakup occurred “over the phone.” Soon after the split, Affleck reconnected with his ex Jennifer Lopez.

Following her split from Affleck, de Armas was romantically linked to Tinder executive Paul Boukadakis in June 2021. In December 2021, an insider told E! News that the couple’s romance was “very serious.”

A July 2022 profile of de Armas in ELLE revealed that she met her new boyfriend “during the pandemic through a mutual friend.” With the majority of bars and restaurants closed during lockdown, they “spent their first dates at each other’s homes, drinking wine and talking.”

As for how she spent her 34th birthday in April 2022, de Armas told ELLE that she celebrated with “my man, my dogs, Chris [Evans] and the crew [of Ghosted].” She continued, “I wasn’t home having a romantic dinner; I was on set with my people doing what I love and at the beach and having a shot of Fireball...and it is the happiest I’ve been.”

The pair was pictured arriving at the Venice Film Festival in September 2022, and they posed for photos on the red carpet at the Vanity Fair Oscar Party on March 12, 2023. While it’s unclear when de Armas and Boukadakis ended their romance, People reported the split in November 2024, when the actress was spotted kissing another man.

The Exposed star surprised fans when she started dating Manuel Anido Cuesta, the stepson of Cuba’s president, Miguel Díaz-Canel Bermúdez. Having graduated with a law degree from the University of Havana in 2019, Anido Cuesta reportedly “became part of the Cuban regime’s inner circle,” La Nación reported. The outlet further explained, “Although he does not hold official positions in government institutions, he has been seen at numerous events and on official trips accompanying his stepfather and mother.”

Rumors began circulating about a potential romance between de Armas and Anido Cuesta in May 2024, when a Cuban influencer claimed the pair had spent 10 days together on the island of Cayo Santa María, off the north coast of Cuba, per Periódico Cubano. While the majority of de Armas’s romance with Anido Cuesta remained private, Hola! published photographs of the pair kissing one another during a night out in Madrid in November 2024. Breakup reports ultimately surfaced in May 2025.

Mission: Impossible icon Tom Cruise and de Armas first sparked dating rumors in winter 2025. The two actors had been spending time together due to work.

While promoting her John Wick spin-off Ballerina in June 2025, de Armas commented on her connection with Cruise, telling Variety, “We’re working together, so he got to see Ballerina and he actually really liked it.” She continued, “It is very special that someone like him is supporting [Ballerina].”

In mid-July 2025, a source downplayed the romance rumors, telling People that de Armas and Cruise simply had “a special work relationship.” According to the insider, the pair had been spending extended periods of time together while preparing to co-star in the thriller Deeper, from the director of 2005’s Mr. & Mrs. Smith, Doug Liman. “Tom is an incredible mentor to Ana,” the source alleged. “She has nothing but amazing things to say about him.”

The end of July brought two eyebrow-raising outings for de Armas and Cruise. On July 26, the pair made an unexpected cameo at an Oasis concert in London, appearing in the background of an Instagram video shared by music producer Goldie. The following day, de Armas and Cruise materialized in Vermont, where they were photographed holding hands, eating ice cream, and shopping together.

In October 2025, People reported that de Armas and Cruise were only ever friends. A source said Cruise was a “dear friend and mentor” to her and added, “She’s single, though, and has been for a while. She stays in touch with Tom and they still have an upcoming movie project that she’s looking forward to.”

De Armas sparked romance rumors with Venezuelan singer Beto Montenegro in summer 2026. The pair was seen together at a concert in Madrid, where Hola! captured a video of them dancing on July 29.

The speculation intensified in August when de Armas and Montenegro traveled to Formentera, Spain. On August 2, they were photographed holding hands and kissing while making their way to the beach.

On November 11, de Armas and Montenegro made their red carpet debut together at the Latin Recording Academy’s 2026 Person of the Year Gala in Las Vegas.

The actress wore a black Wiederhoeft look featuring a corseted top and sheer lace skirt, while Montenegro opted for a black suit with a light blue shirt and dark tie.
