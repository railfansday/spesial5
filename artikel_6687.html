Tanggal: 2026-11-04

I don't cook as often as I'd like. It's not because I dislike cooking or because I'm bad at it (although, compared to my Bon Appétit colleagues, I'll admit that I'm a novice). The real obstacle for me is grocery shopping. The nearest grocery store is a 16-minute walk away, and on the way home, when I'm laden with ingredients, that walk is uphill. Meal kit services like HelloFresh, which deliver everything you need for home-cooked meals to your door, make it possible for me to get dinner on the table in less time than it takes for takeout to arrive. (Plus, my husband and I can skip the part where we ask each other “But what do you want to eat?” back and forth for an eternity.)
