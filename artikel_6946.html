Kendall Jenner Reveals the $35 K-Beauty Gua Sha Cream She Uses to De-Puff, Sculpt, and Define

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

It’s sold out in store, but here’s where to shop it online.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

In a skin care space so saturated, it’s refreshing to see a brand get something so right. The product in question? Anua’s Collagen + Retinol Refining Cream. The K-beauty cream has been around since March, but with such burgeoning popularity, it’s been near-impossible to track down.

So why the sudden popularity, and why now? This isn’t just a product that’s risen to fame through celebrity influence. Kendall Jenner (who is a brand ambassador) recently posted a TikTok demonstrating how she uses the sculpting tool as a “post-working detox.” It’s sold out on multiple occasions, proving that its popularity goes beyond a Jenner approval—it’s a brilliant, budget-friendly product that delivers.

This cream is more than just innovative packaging. Beyond the gua sha-style applicator, it cocktails the ultimate trifecta of ingredients designed to smooth, firm, and boost the skin’s elasticity: retinol, collagen, and peptides.

Let’s break that down, starting with retinol: the gold standard for tightening and minimizing fine lines. It uses encapsulated retinol, which, while that may sound like technical jargon, is what makes it so effective and gentle to use. Essentially, it wraps the vitamin A molecule inside a protective polymer to ensure a slow and safe delivery to the deeper layers of your skin. This way, it’s less likely to cause surface-level irritation.

Moving on to collagen. A collagen-pumped moisturizer or mask is not hard to come by. But, despite what the bottle may tell you, even the most expensive players won’t miraculously boost your collagen levels—nor will this cream. The molecule is just too large to be used in topical skin care, so, in this instance, the inclusion of collagen—as well as plumping peptides—is simply used for hydration purposes. They also contribute to the sensorial experience of the cream: it’s rich yet never greasy, lending the perfect amount of lightweight slip to glide the tool over your skin.

The K-beauty skin care scene has always managed to one-up us, whether that’s through innovative formulas or smart packaging—and in this case, both. Cleverly, the cream allows you to combine the sculpting benefits of a gua sha with your moisturizing routine.

The metal, W-shaped tool is designed to seamlessly glide over your skin while slowly dispensing the cream as it goes. In upward strokes, simultaneously squeeze and slide the tool over the contours of your face. Work upwards from the base of your neck, then move on to your jawline, cheekbones, and up to your temples. Repeat this motion three to five times before moving on to the next area.

Move the tool up and down your neck to soothe “tech neck” (stiffness in the neck from too much time looking down at your phone—a sadly relatable truth for us all). But for the rest of your face, the trick is to always glide in an upward motion to gently de-puff and help ease redness.

As with any retinoid, start slow by introducing the cream into your routine three times a week—and temporarily pause any other actives until your skin adjusts to the ingredient. It is cushioned by the peptides, but it’s always good to ease into actives. And, of course, always use an SPF in the mornings.

So while it may not deliver the same sculpting results as seen through a professional lymphatic drainage facial, this is a great alternative to temporarily decrease puffiness until your next appointment calls.
