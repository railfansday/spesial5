Christian Barmore questionable for Patriots, Alijah Vera-Tucker will travel to L.A.

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Patriots defensive tackle Christian Barmore was added to the team’s injury report on Friday and his status for Sunday’s game against the Chargers is uncertain as a result.

Head coach Mike Vrabel said that Barmore is dealing with a knee injury and he will be listed as questionable for this weekend. Barmore was not on the field during the open portion of Friday’s practice.

Guard Alijah Vera-Tucker (hamstring) has not been activated from injured reserve, but Vrabel said that he will travel with the team to Los Angeles. Vera-Tucker will need to be activated by Saturday afternoon to play on Sunday.

Wide receiver Romeo Doubs (shoulder) has also been designated for return. Vrabel indicated he will not play and wide receiver A.J. Brown (hamstring), cornerback Carlton Davis (knee), and quarterback Drake Maye (fibula) have officially been ruled out.
