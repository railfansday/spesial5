LSU women’s basketball clobbers Texas Southern by 109 points, setting DI record

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

After being bounced from the Sweet 16 round of 2026's March Madness, LSU coach Kim Mulkey's revenge tour has officially kicked off with a bang. The Tigers dismantled Texas Southern 131-22 on Thursday, earning the largest win by a Division I team over a DI opponent.

In front of a home crowd in Baton Rouge, La., seven of the 10 Tigers who took the floor put up double-digit points. Freshman Anna Minaeva led with 21. LSU notched 55 points off turnovers, recorded a staggering 61 boards, and shot 60 percent from the field to Texas Southern's 15 percent.

"We just some hoopers. Everybody gone hoop," Jada Williams, who had 18 points and seven assists, said after the game. "Every game is gonna be somebody else. I think we just do a really good job of running and sharing the ball, we're a very unselfish team, and I think you can see that."

LSU had a 52-point lead at halftime, with the Tigers' bench posting 46 points. Texas Southern scored 13 points in the first quarter, 0 in the second, 3 in the third and 6 in the fourth, making their 22 points the lowest allowed during Mulkey's reign at LSU, which began in 2021.

Starting guard Jayla Cornelius led Texas Southern with 6 points in 28 minutes, and she committed six turnovers.

LSU senior Mikaylah Williams put on a show, with 20 points — 12 from 3-pointers — six rebounds, five assists and two steals. Former Tigers star Angel Reese, who was in the building Thursday night and played with her when Williams was a freshman, was impressed by her former teammate's growth.

"Her midrange is deadly, her 3-point range continues to grow, but her confidence — she continues to be confident," Reese said, sitting next to Mulkey in the postgame press conference. "She's stepping into a role where she's a senior now, and she has to take the big shots and the pressure is on her, and she takes it and she owns it, and I can see that from her. She's very coachable, honestly, a coach's dream."

Reese and the Tigers won the national championship in 2023, defeating Caitlin Clark's Iowa 102-85. LSU has not returned to the Final Four since. (Reese, a forward with the Atlanta Dream, is just days removed from losing in the WNBA Finals.)

"Since we got here in June, we've all just jelled really well," Jada Williams said. "We come in every single day at practice, the energy is good, everybody takes accountability. … Honestly, this is the best team I ever played on, so I'm just out here having the time of my life."
