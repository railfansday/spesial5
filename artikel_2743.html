Week 10 fantasy football WR start-sit: Concepcion, Meyers, Tucker, Flournoy decisions

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Browns wide receiver KC Concepcion Jr. and Jaguars wide receiver Jakobi Meyers are Week 10 fantasy football start 'em picks because Houston and Tennessee have allowed the second-most and most half-PPR points per game to receivers this season.

Raiders wide receiver Tre Tucker and Cowboys wide receiver Ryan Flournoy are sits against Seattle and San Francisco, which have allowed the fewest and fifth-fewest points to the position at 18.4 and 22.2 per game.

Concepcion is my WR26 for Week 10, and Meyers is WR28. Tucker ranks WR40, one spot ahead of Flournoy at WR41.

Starts: Patriots wide receiver DeMario Douglas (WR23 finish) caught five of nine targets for 102 yards in a 45-27 loss to Green Bay, good for 12.7 half-PPR points. The yardage and target totals were both season highs, and he led New England in each category.

Colts wide receiver Keenan Allen (WR56 finish) missed. He drew a team-high eight targets in a 27-23 loss to Dallas but caught only four for 26 yards, worth 4.6 half-PPR points. His lone second-half catch did move him past Hall of Famer Tim Brown for eighth on the NFL's career receptions list.

Sits: Saints wide receiver Devaughn Vele (WR78 finish) caught two passes for 17 yards in New Orleans' 23-22 overtime win over Cleveland, finishing with 2.7 half-PPR points.

Vikings wide receiver Jordan Addison (WR62 finish) caught two of three targets for 28 yards in Monday night's 23-6 loss to Buffalo. Carson Wentz completed 8 of 25 passes for 81 yards with two interceptions, and Addison's final catch came from Max Brosmer on the last drive.

Cleveland Browns wide receiver KC Concepcion (1) makes a catch against the Pittsburgh Steelers | Scott Galvin-Imagn Images

Week 10: vs. Houston, 32.0 FP/G to WRs (31st)

Week 9: at New Orleans, 4 rec. for 85 yds, 1 TD

Concepcion scored his first NFL touchdown in the overtime loss at New Orleans, and his 64-yard catch was the longest play of the game. The rookie briefly left with a right leg injury but returned and ran a team-high 42 routes, so his role held up with Shedeur Sanders under center.

The Browns plan to start Deshaun Watson against his former team, according to ESPN's Jeremy Fowler, and Concepcion's best game came with Watson throwing to him. He caught eight passes for 129 yards against Tennessee in Week 7, the first 100-yard game by a Browns rookie receiver since Corey Coleman in 2016.

"To be honest, I enter every week expecting to have a game like this," Concepcion said after that breakout.

Houston has allowed 32.0 half-PPR points per game to receivers, second-most in the league. The Texans have allowed 27.3 per game over the last three weeks, down from their season pace but still a favorable draw. Denzel Boston and tight end Harold Fannin Jr. compete for targets, but the Texas A&M product is a WR3 start at WR26 with Watson back.

Jacksonville Jaguars wide receiver Jakobi Meyers (3) celebrates following a catch during the third quarter against the New England Patriots | Melina Myers-Imagn Images

Week 10: at Tennessee, 33.0 FP/G to WRs (32nd)

Meyers led Jacksonville with 65 receiving yards on four catches in a 33-10 Thursday night win over Baltimore. That Thursday game gives the Jaguars extra rest before their trip to Nashville.

Tennessee has allowed the most half-PPR points per game to receivers this season at 33.0, and that number has climbed to 38.2 over the last five weeks and 45.3 over the last three. It's the same defense that gave up eight catches for 129 yards to Concepcion in Week 7.

Parker Washington is the Jaguars' top receiver in my rankings at WR7, and he caught a touchdown against Baltimore. However, Washington caught only two of seven targets in that game, and a defense allowing 45 points per game to receivers over three weeks has enough production to support two Jacksonville wideouts.

Volume is the concern, since Meyers caught two passes for 24 yards against Indianapolis in Week 8. Still, the veteran gets the most generous receiver matchup on the schedule and is a WR3 or flex start at WR28.

Las Vegas Raiders wide receiver Tre Tucker (1) warms up before the game against the San Francisco 49ers | Kyle Terada-Imagn Images

Week 10: vs. Seattle, 18.4 FP/G to WRs (1st)

Week 9: at San Francisco, 4 rec. for 55 yds

Las Vegas traded Kirk Cousins to Minnesota on Tuesday, so Fernando Mendoza makes his first NFL start against the defense that has allowed the fewest half-PPR points to receivers. The No. 1 overall pick has thrown eight regular-season passes, all in mop-up duty during a Week 5 loss at New England.

Raiders coach Klint Kubiak spent last season as Seattle's offensive coordinator, so he knows this defense well. But a rookie debut against the Seahawks is a tough setup for any receiver, and Seattle has allowed just 17.9 points per game to receivers over the last three weeks.

Tucker's production already swings week to week. He posted 97 yards and two touchdowns against Buffalo in Week 6 and 103 yards with a score against the Rams in Week 7, then was held without a catch against the Jets in Week 8.

He caught four of eight targets for 55 yards at San Francisco last week, while Brock Bowers matched his eight targets and turned them into 111 yards and a touchdown. With a rookie quarterback making his debut, the 5-foot-8 receiver belongs on the bench at WR40.

Dallas Cowboys wide receiver Ryan Flournoy (19) reacts after catching a touchdown against the Arizona Cardinals | Kevin Jairaj-Imagn Images

Week 10: vs. San Francisco, 22.2 FP/G to WRs (5th)

Week 9: at Indianapolis, 2 rec. for 53 yds

George Pickens missed Week 9 with a hamstring injury, and Flournoy ran a route on all 38 of Dak Prescott's dropbacks in Dallas' 27-23 win at Indianapolis. He still drew only three targets, and a 46-yard catch in the fourth quarter accounted for most of his production.

CeeDee Lamb caught 10 passes for 195 yards and a touchdown in that game instead. Pickens was limited in Wednesday's practice, and his return would cut into the route share Flournoy saw last week.

San Francisco has allowed 22.2 half-PPR points per game to receivers, fifth-fewest, and only 15.1 over the last three weeks. The 49ers forced five second-half takeaways against Las Vegas in Week 9 and held Tucker and Jalen Nailor to six catches for 64 yards combined.

Cornerbacks Renardo Green (back) and Deommodore Lenoir (foot) both exited that game with injuries, so their practice reports are worth watching. Even so, Flournoy's three targets against Indianapolis leave too little room for error at WR41 against this defense.

Plenty of start-sit lists tell you to start this week's RB10 or WR15 with a soft matchup, but nobody needs permission to play those guys. Or they'll recommend you sit this week's TE6 because he has a difficult matchup. Those players are already locked in to your starting lineups.

This column stays on the fringe for 12-team leagues, where the weekly lineup decisions get made: starts come from the WR25 to WR36 range, and sits come from the WR37 to WR45 range, give or take, based on my weekly rankings. The advice won't always apply to your league or team, but it's written for a broader fantasy football audience.

Within those windows, I pick out the players facing the easiest and toughest matchups, then build each case on fantasy points allowed, yards allowed, recent production and the week's news. The only judgment call is which players make the cut, and every argument after that is about whether a borderline player belongs in your lineup this week.
