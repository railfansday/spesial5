Timothée Chalamet and Kylie Jenner witness Lakers shocking loss to the Jazz

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Crypto.com Arena was once again the home to the biggest stars in Hollywood during the Lakers' 109-97 loss to the Utah Jazz on Wednesday night, snapping Los Angeles' nine-game winning streak.

In a turn of events, Kylie Jenner was sitting courtside with her boyfriend, Timothée Chalamet.

The two were spotted on the Spectrum Sports broadcast during the second half, when the Lakers were trailing 56-54.

Timothée Chalamet and Kylie Jenner spotted sitting courtside at the Lakers game on Wednesday night. NBAE via Getty Images

"There's a power couple. Timothée and Kylie," Lakers color commentator Stu Lantz said when the two were spotted.

It's well known that Chalamet is a die-hard New York Knicks fan, as he's been spotted as a regular sitting courtside at Madison Square Garden.

During the Knicks' NBA title run last year, Chalamet was spotted cheering on his beloved Knicks when they were home and when they were on the road in San Antonio.

Chalamet's appearance in Los Angeles likely happened because New York had the day off and the next time they play will be Friday on the road against the Minnesota Timberwolves.

Timothée Chalamet and Kylie Jenner sitting courtside at the Lakers game on Wednesday night. Getty Images

Though Chalamet's allegiance lies with the Knicks, Jenner is a Los Angeles native, but it's unknown if she grew up a Lakers fan.

Last August, she appeared on an episode of the "Better Half" podcast and revealed she's developed an allegiance to the Knicks.

"There's something about the energy in the stadium and [in Madison Square Garden] and being there," Jenner said. "It was so much fun, and then I followed them for those two seasons."

Luka Dončić running down the court during the Lakers' 109-97 loss to the Jazz on Nov. 18, 2026 in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

Her boyfriend, who is well known for his roles in Marty Supreme and Dune, has always made it known he's a big-time sports fan and even showed off his knowledge when he appeared on the ESPN show, "College GameDay," in 2024.

One reason that made Chalamet show up was likely because he came to watch early MVP favorite Luka Dončić, who's had two 50-plus-point performances this season.

Dončić came into Wednesday's game against the Jazz averaging 38.8 points with 7.2 rebounds and 7.7 assists.

Though the Lakers lost, Chalamet and Jenner also watched Dončić score less than 30 points in a game for the first time since Oct. 23, against the Clippers.

Dončić still managed to finish as the Lakers' leading scorer, having 29 points with five rebounds and seven assists.
