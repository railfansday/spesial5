Lions avoid Tampa Bay trap, move to 8-2

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It could have been a trap. And, for a while, it was a little too close for comfort.

But a 17-14 lead in the fourth quarter resulted in a 31-14 margin and the eighth win of the season for the Lions.

Running back Jahmyr Gibbs scored both of the late touchdowns to help the Lions pull away with a final score that makes the game look easier than it was.

The Bucs scored a touchdown in garbage time, cutting the final margin to 10 points, 31-21.

The loss drops the Bucs to 3-7. They’d be left in the dust in any other division; they’re still very much alive in the NFC South.

Next Monday night, Tampa Bay hosts Carolina. On Thursday, the Lions host the Bears in a critical NFC North showdown.
