Mike McCarthy: "That's clearly not to the standard of the Pittsburgh Steelers"

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Steelers coach Mike McCarthy did not mince words about his team’s performance in Sunday night’s 38-18 loss to the Bengals.

“That’s clearly not to the standard of the Pittsburgh Steelers. It’s not to any of our expectations,” McCarthy said. “I was very disappointed. We’re all disappointed.”

Heading into the game, the Steelers and Bengals were tied atop the AFC North at 6-2. Now the Bengals have a one-game lead and currently would have the tiebreaker edge over the Steelers as well. The Bengals are now the overwhelming betting favorites to win the division at -600, with the Steelers far behind at +700.

But as badly as McCarthy’s team played, he said he can’t overreact to one game.

“It’s one loss and it will be dealt with,” McCarthy said. “I’m more focused on the details, the fundamentals, and the things we need to do. We’re not gonna drive down the drama road of those types of things. It’s a waste of time, it’s a waste of energy. We’re going to pour our energy into ourselves, looking in the mirror and focusing on the things we can control.”

The Steelers were not even competitive on Sunday night, and now it’s time for McCarthy to search for answers.
