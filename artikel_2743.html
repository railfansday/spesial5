FSU football grades vs. Pitt: Defense earns high marks on road win

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Another road test brought another road win for Florida State football.

Behind a stifling defensive performance and timely special teams play, the Seminoles edged Pittsburgh 16-10 on Saturday night to improve to 5-5 and move within one victory of bowl eligibility. Under interim coach Rick Stockstill, FSU has shed its road woes and developed a winning formula built on defense, field position and mistake-free football.

Here's how the Seminoles graded out against the Panthers:

Daniels delivered an uneven performance through the air and on the ground, completing 16 of 24 passes for 163 yards.

Pittsburgh dialed up a variety of blitz packages that consistently pressured the senior quarterback and limited his ability to create plays outside the pocket. One of the game's biggest moments came in the fourth quarter when Daniels fumbled while attempting a forward pass with FSU clinging to a 13-10 lead.

While he never found much rhythm, Daniels avoided catastrophic mistakes and managed the game effectively enough to help lead the Seminoles to a crucial road victory.

Big plays were hard to come by for the Seminoles, who finished with just 260 total yards.

Facing the nation's No. 2 rushing defense, FSU managed 97 rushing yards and struggled to generate consistent movement on the ground. Amari Thomas scored the offense's only touchdown after Ryland Kennedy's interception set up a short-field opportunity.

After Thomas exited the game limping, Zay Parks stepped in and handled key carries during the fourth quarter.

Freshman receiver Jasen Lopez continued his emergence as a dependable target, leading the team with five receptions for 72 yards. His development has been particularly important with Duce Robinson sidelined.

Despite Pittsburgh's relentless pressure, the offensive line surrendered only one sack and held up well enough in key situations.

The defense once again carried the Seminoles.

FSU initially controlled Pittsburgh's third-string quarterback, Corey Dailey, before the freshman found success using his mobility in the second quarter. Even so, the Seminoles consistently limited explosive plays and made life difficult for the Panthers throughout the night.

The turning point came in the third quarter when Kennedy intercepted Dailey, setting up Thomas' touchdown that gave FSU momentum and the lead. The defense nearly added another takeaway when Charles Lester came close to a pick-six later in the game.

When Pittsburgh threatened late, Omar Graham came through with critical fourth-down stops to help secure the victory.

Linebacker Chris Jones, who was ejected for targeting, finished with a team-high seven tackles. Caleb LaValle, CJ Richard and Ashlynd Barker each added six tackles.

With offense at a premium, Gabe Panikowski provided a reliable source of points.

The kicker remained perfect on the season, converting all three of his field-goal attempts. Daniel Hughes was inconsistent at times across six punts, but he delivered his biggest kick when it mattered most, pinning Pittsburgh inside the 2-yard line with less than two minutes remaining.

The strong finish helped preserve the lead and put the Panthers in a nearly impossible field-position situation.

Stockstill and his staff deserve credit for having the Seminoles prepared for another pivotal road matchup.

The offense wasn't flashy, but the game plan minimized mistakes and allowed FSU's strengths to dictate the outcome. Interim defensive coordinator Ernie Sims was especially influential, overseeing a defense that repeatedly won on third and fourth down while controlling Pittsburgh's rushing attack.

The discipline, effort and execution displayed by the Seminoles reflected a team that continues to respond to its interim leadership.

It wasn't a masterpiece, but it was another gritty road win that keeps FSU's postseason hopes alive. The Seminoles relied on dominant defense, clutch special teams and timely plays to overcome an inconsistent offensive performance. With bowl eligibility now just one win away, FSU continues to show resilience and confidence in the games that matter most.
