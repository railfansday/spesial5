Observations after James posts triple-double, Sixers beat Heat in NBA Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Observations after James posts triple-double, Sixers beat Heat in NBA Cup originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

LeBron James posted his first triple-double as a Sixer on Wednesday night and led his team to a 111-104 win over the Heat at Xfinity Mobile Arena.

James notched his 126th career regular-season triple-double with 25 points, 12 assists and 10 rebounds for the 15-4 Sixers.

Tyrese Maxey scored 30 points and dished out six assists.

Giannis Antetokounmpo recorded 32 points and 14 rebounds for the 10-7 Heat.

The Sixers improved to 2-1 and Miami fell to 2-1 in Eastern Conference Group B of the NBA Cup.

Jaylen Brown was ruled out at halftime with left hamstring tightness. He had six points on 2-for-8 shooting over 17 minutes.

"He'll get some imaging and we'll get the update tomorrow," Sixers head coach Nick Nurse said.

Joel Embiid (illness) missed his second game in a row. The Sixers are set to visit the Pacers on Friday night in their final Cup group game.

"He's ill," Nurse said of Embiid. "I do not know whether he's going to Indiana yet or not. I guess we'll find that out late tomorrow afternoon."

Here are observations on the Sixers' victory over the Heat:

Nurse emphasized pregame that the Sixers needed an improved defensive performance.

"We've got to compete much better at the defensive end than we did Monday (against the Trail Blazers)," he said. "It's as simple as that."

The night did not begin in especially promising fashion on that front. Antetokounmpo powered through Brown and scored inside for the game's first bucket. His transition layup put Miami up 6-0. The Heat scored nine fast-break points in the first six minutes.

As he had Monday in the Sixers' loss to Portland, Jabari Walker started at center. He was among the Sixers who shared the daunting defensive assignment of Antetokounmpo.

Walker took a charge on the 10-time All-Star in the first quarter, but Antetounkmpo had a stellar start. He forcefully orchestrated Miami's offense, often plowing through the middle of the floor and finding efficient answers.

The Sixers' defense was better late in the first quarter and it didn't hurt when Antetokounmpo took a seat.

Maxey had an aggressive, productive first period, scoring 13 points. Dominick Barlow's tip-in gave the Sixers a 27-26 edge.

James drained three three-pointers early in the second quarter, including a confident, deep jumper that fired up a high-volume home crowd. He's shown that he remains capable of flipping a world-class switch and taking over games in a vast array of ways.

There's no question the 22-time All-Star is out of his early-season outside shooting slump. James has gone 8 for 11 from long range over his last two games. He's scored at least 20 points in four straight.

"I just play the game as it unfolds," James said. "I had a couple of opportunities and I was able to connect on them. I kind of know when my hand is feeling pretty good, especially from the outside. We'll see how long I can keep it going."

Adem Bona checked in early in the second quarter. He provided a string of excellent defensive possessions against Antetokounmpo, including an emphatic block and several well-guarded missed jumpers, although Bona did get called for three fouls in the second period.

Nurse was pleased with what he received defensively from both Bona (two blocks, six rebounds) and Barlow (nine points, eight rebounds) off the bench.

"They used their gifts," he said. "They're quick side to side, both of them. When they're in that kind of fighting mode like that … they did a good job. We tried to get Bona in there when Giannis was in, just for the rim protection. And Dom just played really, really (well) all over the place … on the defense, on the rebounding, on the running, on the cutting."

The Sixers played effective zone defense at the tail end of the second quarter and held a nine-point halftime lead. That grew as high as 16 points in the third quarter.

Miami pulled back within single digits, but the Sixers maintained a healthy advantage and James kept on making highlight plays. He lobbed the ball up to Edgecombe early in the fourth quarter and the second-year guard skied high to lay it in. James later connected with Barlow on an alley-oop.

Once the Sixers have a substantial lead, James' presence is a massive help offensively. He's very well-accustomed to conducting methodical half-court offense, identifying favorable matchups and turning to fruitful actions over and over. James will also still steam forward on the fast break when he sees the chance.

Maxey buried a step-back three to extend his team's lead to 111-95 with 2:29 on the clock and the Sixers polished off a professional win. The fans roared when James snatched the rebound required to reach his triple-double.

"We came out tonight with a different mentality, different energy," Maxey said. "Nurse got on us a little bit in the film (session) yesterday. He lit a fire for a lot of us, which is good. We needed it."
