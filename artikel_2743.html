Victor Wembanyama makes LeBron James point to clarify his perceived ‘statpadding’ dig at Luka Doncic

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Victor Wembanyama revealed that, like the greats, including LeBron James, he was not going to think about the ethics of scoring 50 points in an NBA game.

The 22-year-old went off for 50 points and 18 rebounds on an efficient 69% shooting from the field, while also contributing with 4 steals and 5 blocks.

In his walkoff interview, the French centre said: "Unlike many of my peers, I'm not very good at stat padding."

It was believed that Wembanyama's comments were a dig at Luka Doncic, who is the only other player to score 50+ points twice this season. However, in his press conference, he explained his statement by revealing what the greats of the game thought about this.

"The greats they don't think about that, I don't think Lebron, Shai, Jokic, Luka, I don't think they think about that. I have them as examples, so I am not trying to think about it either," said Wemby.

He also went on to explain what ethical basketball meant to him, as he believed it was not a defined word.

His 50-pointer against the New Orleans Pelicans was the third for him in his career. He is now tied with David Robinson for the most 50-point games as a San Antonio Spurs player.

While this is a terrific achievement, the two-time All-Star revealed what his thought process was while he was approaching the 50-point mark.

"I did try to get the three up, when I had 47 points," said Wemby. The reporter then asked him if he was happy about that; the Spurs centre then replied: "Yeah, I made it, didn't I? As I was saying before, the greats of the game, they don't care. I don't think Wilt thought about that when he had 98 points; I don't think he thought about making that extra extra pass, if he could have went for 100."

The win puts the Spurs on the same record as the Los Angeles Lakers. Wemby and his team will next play the Sacramento Kings on Monday to try and cement their place at the top of the Western Conference standings.
