The Celtics continue their road trip with a stop in Sacramento to take on the Kings. This is the first of 2 games between them this season. They will meet again in Boston on March 16. The Celtics won the series 2-0 last season. The Celtics are 195-120 overall all time against the Kings. They are 72-75 all time in games played in Sacramento.

The Kings are playing on the second night of back to back games, having played at the Lakers on Saturday night. This is their first back to back set this season. The Celtics are playing in the first of back to back games and will play at Golden State on Sunday. This is the Celtics 4th back to back set already this season and they have a 5th back to back set before the end of November. The Celtics are 4-0 on the first night of back to back games this season.

After 2 straight losses, the Celtics have dropped to 2nd in the East, 1.5 games behind first place Philadelphia and 1 game ahead of 3rd place Detroit, who lost to the Raptors on Friday night. Keys to the GameDefense – Defense is always the key to winning every game. The Kings have a defensive rating of 120.4(30th). The Celtics defensive rating is 106.0, 2nd in the league. The Celtics need to defend the paint as the Kings average 45.3 points in the paint per game. The Kings average 36.1 three pointers attempted per game, which is 22nd in the league and they are 22nd with 12.5 3 pointers made per game. Against the Nuggets, once again, the Celtics let up on defense in the 2nd half and allowed the Nuggets to come back from 17 points down to win the game. The Celtics need to make defense a priority and commit to playing tough lock down defense for 48 minutes, regardless of the opponent's statistics.

Rebound – The Celtics have to put out extra effort to grab the rebounds on both ends of the court. By doing so, they limit the second chance points and fast breaks for the Kings and also give themselves extra possessions. The Celtics are averaging 44.9 rebounds per game to 39.7 rebounds per game for the Kings. The Kings average 13.4 second chance points per game and extra effort from the Celtics can limit those. The Celtics must make rebounding a priority for every player on the team.

Move the Ball Carefully – The Celtics play best when they share the ball and keep it moving. They can't try to do too much individually or the offense gets bogged down. They need to move the ball to find the best shots instead of taking quick shots or over dribbling. That hasn't been a problem as much this season and hopefully they don't fall back into old habits after 2 straight losses. They have to move the ball carefully, however, and not turn the ball over. Against the Nuggets, the Celtics turned the ball over 10 times in the 2nd half alone and 15 for the game. The Nuggets scored 21 points off those turnovers in a game they won by 4 points. They had 8 consecutive turnovers at one point of the 3rd quarter. The Celtics have to focus on taking better care of the ball.

To 3 or Not to 3 – The Celtics are 3rd in the league, attempting 43.9 threes a game. When they fall, the Celtics are tough to beat. But, when they aren't falling, the Celtics struggle. Against the Nuggets, the Celtics shot just 25% on threes and went cold in the 2nd half. If the Celtics aren't hitting their 3s, they have to go to Plan B and take the ball to the basket or shoot from midrange. If the 3s are falling, great!! If not, go to Plan B.

X-FactorsFatigue on the Road – This is the 2nd game of a 4 game road trip. The Celtics have had to travel across the country and across 3 time zones and played at mile high altitude on Wednesday. Fatigue could come into play for them. Also they need to ignore the distractions of travel, an unfamiliar arena and a hostile crowd and concentrate on playing together, playing hard, and playing the right way.

Players Out – With Jayson Tatum, Derrick White and Mitchell Robinson all out due to the back to back games, other Celtics are going to need to step up and pick up the slack left by the 3 players being out. They will need to pick up both defensive and offensive slack along with more effort on the boards. With 2 key starters out, the reserves are going to need to play harder and smarter. Even though the Kings have only won 1 game so far, the Celtics should not underestimate them and they need to play their best, even with 3 players out.

Officiating – Officiating can always be an x-factor. Every crew will call the game differently. Some will call every little thing and the game will have no flow. Others will let a lot go and let them play. Some crews favor the home team while others call it evenly or have a personal agenda. In the second half against the Nuggets, the Nuggets shot 22 free throws to just 3 for the Celtics. The Celtics need to adjust to how the game is called and not let the officials mess with their focus.
