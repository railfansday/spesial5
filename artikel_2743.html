Derrick Henry's second TD has Ravens back within a FG

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Derrick Henry has 10 carries for 10 yards, but he has two touchdowns.

His second one has pulled the Ravens within 23-20 of the Chargers.

The Chargers have twice had double-digit second-half leads at 20-10 and 23-13. It’s the first time the Chargers have had a double-digit lead in the second half since beating the Cowboys 34-17 in Week 16 last season. That was Los Angeles’ last win.

The Ravens, though, have made it a game despite only 15 rushing yards.

Jacoby Brissett, making his first start with the Ravens, is 25-of-39 for 280 yards and an interception.

He led the Ravens on a 10-play, 71-yard drive that has them back within a field goal.

The Ravens ruled out defensive tackle Nnamdi Madubuike (hamstring) and list right guard Vega Ioane as questionable with a left shoulder injury. Ioane played through it into the second half before Chuma Edoga replaced him.
