Adam Schefter shares what other NFL teams are privately saying about Jacksonville Jaguars trades

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Jacksonville Jaguars were busy ahead of the trade deadline as they aim to reach their first Super Bowl.

Jordan Davis was acquired via trade with the Philadelphia Eagles, seeing the Jaguars give up a 2027 first-round pick and a 2029 second-round pick.

Elsewhere, Davon Godchaux was traded in exchange for a conditional sixth-round pick and Jessie Bates III was brought in for a 2027 second-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick.

Now, speaking via his podcast, Adam Schefter has suggested that other people inside the NFL 'feel like Jacksonville paid a high price for the players they got'.

He said: "And they don't worry that they've given up three picks in the last five drafts. And I think when people around the league talk about it, they feel like Jacksonville paid a high price for the players they got. But on the other hand, they filled needs.

"They got good players. So yeah, are they going to miss those picks when those turns come? Of course they are. But they recognised that they had an opportunity. This is a team that has been trying to build its fan base, growing internationally, playing over in London all the time. Their stadium is under construction; they're going to be moving into a new stadium.

"They're playing next year in Orlando. There are so many obstacles that this franchise has had to overcome. And it's almost like saying, okay, while we're doing all that, we're going to make a statement and be the best team in football."

The Jaguars are already 6-2 for the season so adding more quality to their team while they're on course to make it into the postseason makes some sense.

However, they do risk being weaker in the coming years if their recent arrivals don't pay off.
