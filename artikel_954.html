Bruno Fernandes is STAYING at Man United - for now! Red Devils trigger 12-month extension clause to prevent losing captain for free - as he weighs up long-term deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Published: 09:49 EST, 27 November 2026 | Updated: 11:55 EST, 27 November 2026

Manchester United have triggered the 12-month extension clause in Bruno Fernandes' contract after their captain put talks over a new deal on hold.

The move means Fernandes is tied to Old Trafford until June 30, 2028, and will not be able to negotiate with foreign clubs from January 1 or leave United as a free agent in the summer.

The Portugal star could still be sold at the end of the season, but having him under contract for an additional 12 months means United are guaranteed to receive a transfer fee.

It's understood that United and Fernandes aim to return to the negotiating table in future, but have decided that it is more important to focus on lifting the team away from the Premier League relegation zone.

Fernandes, who moved to Old Trafford from Sporting Lisbon in January 2020 in a deal worth up to £68million, spoke about the situation when he returned to his old club for Wednesday's 2-2 Champions League draw at the Jose Alvalade Stadium.

'There are no negotiations going on,' he said. 'Yes, the club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay.

'I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations. We spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side.

'I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most important thing now is that the club gets back to win games.'

Fernandes came close to a £100m transfer to Saudi Pro League side Al Hilal in 2025, and was again linked with a move away from United in the summer after setting a new Premier League record for assists last season.

He has struggled by comparison this term and was dropped for the defeat to Bournemouth at Old Trafford last month, but returned to form with two goals and a man-of-the-match performance against Sporting.

However, Fernandes is a doubt for Saturday's home game against Brentford after missing training on Friday through illness following United's return from Portugal.

'We don't know,' said head coach Mauricio Pochettino. 'He's affected for a virus from Portugal. He finished the game a little bit with a problem and today he cannot be part of the training session.

'We are going to assess during the day and tomorrow how is how. We wake up and see. We are not going to take any risks with this type of situation. We are going to pay attention.'

Pochettino revealed that £70m summer signing Carlos Baleba could be out for up to another nine weeks with the hamstring injury he suffered against Roma earlier this month.

Baleba has already been sidelined for three weeks, and Pochettino said: 'The medical staff say they were talking about 10-12 weeks.'

Marcus Rashford, who has been following an individual fitness programme for a hip and groin problem, has returned to light training on the grass at Carrington.
