Former champion Daniil Medvedev defeated home favourite Flavio Cobolli 7-6 (7-4) 6-3 in the other group match on Monday evening.

"I managed to serve well," said Medvedev. "It's a very fast indoor court, so the serve is important. He managed to [serve] well in the break points in the first set.

"There was one point where I could have done a tiny bit better, but other than that he was serving well. I'm happy I managed to stay tough and get the win."

The fourth-seeded Medvedev will next face Alcaraz on Wednesday, while Cobolli will look to continue his quest to join Matteo Berrettini, Jannik Sinner and Lorenzo Musetti as the only Italian men to record a singles victory at this event when he next faces Frances Tiafoe.
