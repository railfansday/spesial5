‘Dancing With the Stars’ Pros Are Allegedly Frustrated With This Judge for “Lower Scores,” “No Constructive Feedback,” “Bad Mood,” and “Phoning It In”

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Looks like there’s yet more drama behind the scenes on Dancing With the Stars—and this time Derek Hough is at the center of it. Take this with a grain of salt, but turns out the pros are (allegedly!) upset with Derek over his judging this season.

“There’s a lot of frustration from the pros towards the judges this season but surprisingly, it seems most of the animosity is aimed at Derek specifically,” a source tells The Sun. “He keeps praising the contestants while giving lower scores. He offers little to no constructive feedback and the pros feel it’s impossible to please him since he isn’t offering any critique.”

On top of that, “he just seems like he’s in more of a bad mood on set this season. It’s rubbing the cast the wrong way and they feel he just doesn’t want to be on set this year. It’s a strange shift from past seasons where he’s usually the most energetic on set. At least with Carrie Ann and Bruno, they justify their lower scores with feedback. Derek is just phoning it in.”

This comes amid Derek getting called out by fans for comments he made during Ezra Sosa’s livestream on TikTok last week, where he joked “Listen, I’ve had three hours of sleep. I came from Seattle this morning on tour. I’m going to Florida right after this Disney special, and then back on tour…That’s why I think I’m giving nines to everybody today.”

Nines aside, can’t wait to see Derek and the rest of the judges during the Dancing With the Stars finale next Tuesday!
