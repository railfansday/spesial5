A Look at All the ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Winners Over the Years—Including Our Latest Champions

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Mirrorball Trophy > Olympic/Paralympic Gold Medal.

At this point, Dancing With the Stars has been on for a frightening amount of time—meaning a truly shocking number of celebrities have wrapped themselves in stretchy glitter onesies and pirouetted their way around that hallowed ballroom while regretting all their life choices, deep in contemplation about firing their agent. In fact, 35 stars have already taken home the coveted Mirrorball Trophy—ranging from people only your mom could pick out of an early ’90s lineup to some pretty famous faces, including several Olympians/Paralympians and Bachelor/Bachelorette stars.

In honor of DWTS season 35 coming to a close (no, I’m not okay, but thanks for asking), let’s take a look back at allllllllll the Dancing With the Stars winners throughout history.

Runners-up: John O’Hurley & Charlotte Jørgensen

About Kelly: She’s a model and actress best known for playing Sam McCall on General Hospital. For more info, ask my grandma, who’s been watching that soap for years.

Runners-up: Jerry Rice & Anna Trebunskaya

About Drew: This dude is Nick Lachey’s younger brother who was also in 98 Degrees. You just fully forgot he existed, it’s fine.

Runners-up: Mario Lopez & Karina Smirnoff

About Emmitt: He’s a running back who played for the Dallas Cowboys! But obviously, this DWTS win is also important.

About Apolo: He’s a not-at-all casual eight-time Olympic track speed skating medalist. I mean...his talent.

About Hélio: He’s a Brazilian race car driver who won the Indy 500 three full times, no bigs.

Runners-up: Jason Taylor & Edyta Śliwińska

About Kristi: She’s just casually one of the most famous Olympic figure skaters ever. I’m sure this Mirrorball Trophy will look great next to all her medals!

About Brooke: Ironically, you probably know Brooke best from cohosting DWTS seasons 10 to 17, but she’s also an actress!

About Shawn: Just another casual Olympian! Shawn is a 2008 Olympic balance beam gold medalist and also the 2007 all-around world champion.

About Donny: Meet the man who recorded “I’ll Make a Man Out of You” from Mulan, otherwise known as the most famous member of sibling group The Osmonds.

Runners-up: Evan Lysacek & Anna Trebunskaya

About Nicole: I mean...she is only THE lead singer of The Pussycat Dolls. Need I say more?

Runners-up: Kyle Massey & Lacey Schwimmer

About Jennifer: Yes, she’s best known for playing Baby in Dirty Dancing—but to me, she’ll always be the sister from Ferris Bueller’s Day Off, aka my personal hero.

Runners-up: Kirstie Alley & Maksim Chmerkovskiy

About Hines: A football coach, former wide receiver, and Super Bowl MVP, Hines has also had cameos in The Dark Knight Rises and The Walking Dead.

Runners-up: Rob Kardashian & Cheryl Burke

About J.R.: An actor, veteran, and motivational speaker, J.R. has appeared on All My Children and really knows how to pull off a pair of suspenders.

Runners-up: Katherine Jenkins & Mark Ballas

About Donald: He played for the Green Bay Packers and helped them win Super Bowl XLV! Again, almost as impressive as the Mirrorball Trophy. Almost.

About Melissa: Um, she won The Bachelor only to have Jason Mesnick break up with her for Molly Maleney in what might actually have been the most dramatic season ever.

Runners-up: Zendaya & Valentin Chmerkovskiy

About Kellie: If she looks familiar, that is because Kellie was a star on American Idol and has since had a pretty successful country career!

Runners-up: Corbin Bleu & Karina Smirnoff

About Amber: Not only was she in Glee, duh, she’s a Grammy-nominated singer and starred in the Dreamgirls musical.

About Meryl: You guessed it, she’s yet another Olympic ice dancer who happens to be just as talented off her skates.

Runners-up: Sadie Robertson & Mark Ballas

About Alfonso: Not only does he host Dancing With the Stars and America’s Funniest Home Videos, he played Carlton on The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air!

About Rumer: She’s well-known for being Bruce Willis and Demi Moore’s daughter, but I stan Rumer for her movies—nay, films—The House Bunny and Sorority Row.
