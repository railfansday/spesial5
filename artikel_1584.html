Champions League draw: Arsenal to renew Mourinho rivalry with Real Madrid tie

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Arsenal will host old nemesis José Mourinho’s Real Madrid in the league phase of the Champions League.

Mourinho, the former Chelsea, Manchester United and Tottenham Hotspur manager, will return to London to resume hostilities against last season’s beaten finalists.

Other reunions from Thursday’s draw include Manchester City travelling to Barcelona, to whom they last week sold Rodri, and Liverpool visiting Inter Milan, who have signed Curtis Jones.

Arsenal will also travel to Harry Kane’s Bayern Munich, while United will host the Germans as well on their Champions League return in a repeat of the legendary 1999 final. Michael Carrick’s side will also visit Cesc Fàbregas’s Como.

Mourinho last managed at the Emirates Stadium in March 2021, barely a month before his sacking by Spurs.

During his two spells as Chelsea manager, Mourinho famously had run-ins with Arsenal’s Arsène Wenger.

Mourinho called Wenger a “specialist in failure” after the Frenchman failed to add to his three Premier League titles following his Portuguese counterpart’s arrival in London in 2004.

Last season, Mikel Arteta finally ended Arsenal’s long wait to be crowned champions of England but agonisingly missed out on European glory.

This season is the third under the new format, with a league phase instead of a group stage, and Uefa has confirmed that fixtures that have taken place in the league phase of the two previous seasons cannot be repeated this time around.

For example, Liverpool cannot host Real Madrid in the league phase this term after welcoming the 15-time European champions to Anfield at this stage of the previous two campaigns. Andoni Iraola’s side could, however, travel to the Bernabeu for an away meeting with Madrid.

Similarly, Arsenal will not be travelling to San Siro to face Inter, but could host the Serie A champions at the Emirates.

Arsenal finished top of the league phase last season after winning all eight fixtures and it resulted in a favourable run to the final, facing Bayer Leverkusen, Sporting and Atletico Madrid.

Liverpool, meanwhile, topped the league phase in the 2024-25 campaign – the first season with the new format – but fell at the first hurdle in the knockout stages after being drawn against Paris St-Germain.

The Ligue 1 side have proven that it’s not essential to be firing the whole way through the tournament, finishing 15th and 11th in the league phase in the last two seasons but going on to lift the trophy on both occasions.
