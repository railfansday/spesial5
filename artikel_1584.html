Juventus sell out Venezia game as Omonia sales pass 35,000

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Juventus have sold out Sunday's Serie A home game against Venezia despite a long injury list. Strong early sales for the Europa League visit of Omonia Nicosia keep the club in line for another strong European crowd.

Juventus go into Sunday’s home game against Venezia still short of players. Several remain unavailable for Sunday’s 12:30 kick-off. Edon Zhegrova has trained with the group and is considered available. Celik, Cambiaso, Nico Gonzalez and Kalulu remain out, Sport Mediaset reported. For Spalletti, a full home crowd arrives in the middle of a busy run of games.

General sale for the fixture opened at 3pm on Tuesday 6 October. Supporters living in the province of Venezia could buy only if they held an active Juventus Card. The fixture belongs to the 2026-27 Serie A Enilive season.

This season every Juventus match ticket is digital. Each one is managed through the Allianz Stadium App.

Season-ticket holders who cannot attend can list their seat on resale.juventus.com. The club, not the seller, sets the resale price. A few of those listings are now all that remains for supporters who missed the general sale, ANSA reported.

The season-ticket terms also reward turning up. To keep first refusal on a 2027-28 season ticket, a holder must use the seat for at least 70 per cent of matches. That means at least 13 of the 19 league games.

The Europa League has drawn big numbers to Turin this season. Against NEC, 39,308 match tickets were sold, with away fans counted separately. No away figure was released for the Rennes visit, but that section was full.

The Rennes game finished 2-2 on 22 October. Juventus needed a second-half Woltemade penalty to draw after trailing 2-1.

Omonia Nicosia are next. Bookings for the Cypriot club’s visit still trail the two earlier European crowds.

Juventus have set strict conditions for the fixture. Supporters of Cypriot nationality may buy tickets only for the away section. The club has warned that breaking UEFA rules can lead to cancelled tickets and refused entry.

Taken together, the figures are consistent with a home support that keeps buying despite the injury list. The weekend league fixture will be followed by a European night that is already well on its way to another large crowd.

Juventus host Omonia Nicosia in the Europa League on 26 November.
