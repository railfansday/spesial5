Sensational playmaker is the next Rio Ngumoha, and he wants to join Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Liverpool are ready to make a move for their next Rio Ngumoha, who dreams of joining Liverpool.

It was a quiet summer window in 2024. But looking back in hindsight even though it was a quiet window the club may have made its greatest transfer of the century with the acquisition of Ngumoha from Chelsea.

Those over in Cobham were furious that Liverpool were able to poach the mercurial talent from their grasp.

Now those people at Stamford Bridge will be seething with rage given how exciting Ngumoha has been since arriving at Liverpool.

The youngster's terrific performance against Manchester United cemented his place as England's greatest young player right now.

Ngumoha is still barely 18-years-old but the experience that he has under his belt is very impressive and the future ahead of him - only the sky is the limit.

How Liverpool are planning to have a team full of NgumohasJulian Ward has become the club's new sporting director, and he has a very different idea as to how he wants Liverpool to operate in contrast to Richard Hughes. Ward's mission is to find the superstars of the future. He has been working with Pedro Marques in a role behind the scenes playing a key part of moves for Mor Talla Ndiaye, Ifeanyi Ndukwe, Samuel Martinez and Lucca Brughmanns among others. It was also Ward who successfully negotiated contracts for Erik Farkas and Joshua Abe in order to retain Liverpool's leading young talents at the club for the long-term. Ward's mission is to bring in even more exciting gems from across the globe in the coming years, and this could see him sign the club's next Ngumoha.
