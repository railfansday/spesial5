MU: Bryan Mbeumo rushes to the aid of Michael Carrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

At the final whistle, Bryan Mbeumo did not hide his disappointment after Manchester United’s new debacle against Chelsea (2-0). The Cameroonian striker, however, believes that the Red Devils had a convincing first half and could even have taken the advantage of one or two goals before the break. “A lot of disappointment. I think we did a lot of good things, especially in the first half, where we could have led by one or two goals.”he regretted.

Asked about a possible responsibility of the players towards Michael Carrick after this new poor performance, Mbeumo defended the state of mind of the group. “No, of course not! I think the state of mind was there from the first minute. It was more difficult at 10 against 11. We always have confidence in everyone. Despite our numerical inferiority, we still felt a certain frustration at the end of the match. But I think we gave it our all as a team today. Maybe today we just missed a few opportunities. We could have taken the advantage from the start of the match, but it’s a positive point to take away from this meeting…”he blurted.
