Revolut Pay arrives on Uber with RevPoints perks and Uber One access

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Customers can redeem RevPoints for instant discounts at checkout, and Premium members get Uber One benefits in 10 markets.

Revolut Pay is now available as a payment option on Uber, bringing the fintech’s checkout service into one of the world’s most widely used consumer apps.

The integration also links rides and deliveries paid through Revolut Pay to the company’s rewards scheme. Moreover, the company says millions of its customers use Uber every month.

The main incentive is RevPoints, as customers in the UK and European Economic Area will earn boosted points on every Uber ride and delivery paid for through Revolut Pay, with millions of users eligible.

The points can also be used at checkout. Customers can redeem RevPoints directly against the cost of a ride or order, or spend them on other offers in the Revolut app. That makes the loyalty scheme more immediately useful, effectively turning points into a discount on the next trip or delivery.

The service is available across the EU, except in Bulgaria, Cyprus, Latvia, and Slovenia. It also works in the UK, Switzerland, and Norway, for users aged 18 and over.

Revolut describes Uber as the latest addition to its growing network of global partners, saying it is “setting the new standard for how the world moves, spends, and earns”.

The partnership fits into Revolut’s broader effort to become more than a bank. The company completed a share sale valuing it at $115bn last week. Chief executive Nik Storonsky has also outlined a future in which AI agents shop online using customers’ cards.

A payment button inside Uber is a more immediate version of that ambition. Revolut provides the payment infrastructure while the customer stays inside another company’s app.

For riders, the appeal is straightforward: they can pay through a familiar service, collect points on trips and deliveries they would have made anyway, and use those points to reduce the cost of future orders.

For Revolut, each additional third-party checkout gives it another opportunity to keep customers using its payment services beyond its own app.
