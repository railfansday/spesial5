The NFC North and NFC South are polar opposites this season – no pun intended – when it comes to the strength of the teams in each division. The NFC North features a three-way tie for first place as the Chicago Bears, Detroit Lions and Minnesota Vikings all stand tall with 7-2 records, while the NFC South doesn't have a single team with a win percentage above .44. That means that the 3-6 Tampa Bay Buccaneers, currently in second place in the NFC South, will have a difficult road ahead when they face the Lions this Sunday. A win for either team could put them in the lead of their division.

This weekend's Bucs vs. Lions game will air on Fox starting at 1 p.m. ET; out-of-market viewers can tune in with NFL Sunday Ticket. Here's everything you need to know about how to watch Sunday's game and how to watch every other Week 11 game on the schedule.

YouTube TV gets you nearly every channel you need to watch the NFL, including Fox, NBC, ABC, CBS, ESPN and NFL Network. Plus, YouTube TV is the home of NFL Sunday Ticket. The Sunday Ticket package, while pricey on its own, gets you all the Sunday out-of-market games (160+ games per season).

Current Black Friday pricing for new users for the 2026 Sunday Ticket is just $12/month for four months. Factor in the cost of YouTube TV's Sports and News plan at $49.99/month for your first five months, then $71.99/month thereafter.

That's $61.99/month for the next four months of YouTube TV + NFL Sunday Ticket. You can also opt, with or without a YouTube TV subscription, to pay for the rest of the season of Sunday Ticket upfront on its own for just $48.

Fubo's Sports package gives you access to ESPN, NFL Network, Fox, ABC, CBS, NBC and 20-plus more live channels. Starting at $54.99/month for your first month, the live TV streaming service is definitely pricey (though certainly not the most expensive option on this list), but it offers most channels you'll need to watch both the NFL preseason and regular season.
