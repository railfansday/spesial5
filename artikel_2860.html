Rams LG Steve Avila will miss time after aggravating hamstring injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Rams will place wide receiver Puka Nacua on injured reserve with MCL and PCL knee sprains and turf toe. Coach Sean McVay said left guard Steve Avila will also miss “a little bit of time.”

Avila aggravated his hamstring injury in Sunday’s victory over the Cardinals, playing only 18 snaps.

Adam Grosbard of the LA Daily News reports that Avila will miss “several games.”

The Rams, though, are running out of IR/designated-to-return slots, so Avila may remain on the active roster.

Beaux Limmer replaced Avila at left guard on Sunday, and he is expected to start in Avila’s absence.
