Nets' Josh Minott, Bulls' Matas Buzelis fined $25,000 after making obscene gestures toward fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Brooklyn Nets forward Josh Minott and Chicago Bulls forward Matas Buzelis were fined $25,000 by the NBA after making obscene gestures toward the fans the league announced Monday.

Minott's incident occurred at the end of the Nets' 129-110 road loss to the Memphis Grizzlies on Saturday.

Buzelis' occurred that same evening, and came with 11:19 left in the Bulls' 117-106 win over the Minnesota Timberwolves, per the NBA.

Buzelis' incident was caught on the broadcast. After hitting a free throw, the forward flipped off someone in the crowd.

Minott's obscene gesture occurred after the game ended, per the NBA's release. Video of his gesture wasn't immediately available.

Minott played just 1:46 minutes in the contest, going 0-for-1 from the floor. He was also responsible for a turnover.

The NBA did not announce additional details regarding Minott's gesture, though it may have come immediately after the game, when Minott threw up — and bricked — a final second 3-ball while down by 19.

Minott, 23, has seen his role reduced by a fair amount this season. After a midseason trade brought him to the Nets last season, Minott played in 16 games with the team, averaging 19.3 minutes a game.

In nine games this season, Minott is averaging just 6.7 minutes per contest.
