Raiders fans have a slight gain in confidence heading into Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Las Vegas Raiders have lost six of their seven games. It has been a rough time for fans after the 3-0 start. While they did have playoff aspirations at first, the team has come back to earth.

Raider Nation understands this is a young team. The Raiders are one of the youngest teams in football in terms of their starting lineup. Now that they're starting rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza, there will be ups and downs.

Now they face the Denver Broncos, and it will be a chance to get their second win in the division. It will be a tough road matchup, but the Raiders have to find a way to stop the bleeding.

In this Week's SB Nation Reacts, we ask Raider Nation how confident they are in the team's direction. We saw a slight lift from Week 10 to 79%.

Fans are taking a practical approach and most were impressed with Mendoza's first start. Hopefully Mendoza has his first breakout performance of the season against a tough Broncos defense.
