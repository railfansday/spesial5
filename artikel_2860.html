2026 NFL midseason: Our MVP, top rookies, best coach, surprises, disappointments and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

It's hard to believe we're already halfway through the 2026 NFL season. But before the rest of the regular season flies by, it's time to take a pause and reflect on the players, coaches and storylines that stand out the most through the first nine weeks.

The league gives out its major awards at the end of the season. We're going to take our best stab at each one of them now, while also mixing in some bonus questions from our staff of eight Yahoo Sports NFL and fantasy analysts.

While our experts see the MVP as a two-QB race at this point, the field appears more wide-open for the Offensive and Defensive Player of the Year awards as well as Coach of the Year. As for the top rookie awards, it looks like a battle between a first-round and second-round wide receiver on offense and a duel of top-five draft picks on defense.

Beyond the traditional award picks, our staff also revealed their biggest surprises and disappointments of the season so far (spoiler: all their disappointments revolve around the same team). Will many of these picks turn out to be wrong by December? Probably, but here's our best shot at the 2026 midseason awards.
