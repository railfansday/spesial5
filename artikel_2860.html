Ravens QB Joe Fagnano practices Tuesday with brace on ankle

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Ravens rookie quarterback Joe Fagnano has a heavy brace on his right ankle, but he is on the field for the open portion of practice on Tuesday.

Fagnano injured his ankle on a low hit by Bills defensive tackle Ed Oliver late in Sunday’s game and the injury is ill-timed given the loss of Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley to injuries earlier in the game. Jackson’s thumb injury is going to keep him out from an extended period of time while Huntley is dealing with turf toe.

The Ravens listed Fagnano as limited on Monday’s estimated injury report and he’ll likely get the same listing on Tuesday, but his presence on the field suggests he’ll do what he can do what he can to make the start against the Jaguars on Thursday.

Kedon Slovis was signed off of the Packers’ practice squad Tuesday, which gives the Ravens another healthy player on the 53-man roster. Austin Reed, who signed on Monday, is also available on the practice squad.
