The Columbus Blue Jackets Are Slumping, But Treating Adam Fantilli As A Scapegoat Is The Wrong Thing To Do

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Last night, in a 5-1 loss to the New York Islanders, Columbus Blue Jackets forward Adam Fantilli was seemingly benched.

He played a total of 12:59, and only 3:35 in the third period. He went 6/9 on his faceoffs and was a minus-3. He also had zero shots on goal for just the second time this season.

Don Granato said of Adam Fantilli's limited usage last night, "You manage your bench with decision-making in mind, right? We had 11 forwards. If there are guys that are going better and guys that are impacting the game in different ways, yes, we're making decisions based on that."

That's all fine and dandy, but you have to just let this team play at this point. You're in the middle of a serious slump, and young guys can't be looked at as scapegoats.

In Basketball, at pretty much all levels, when a kid is in a shooting slump, the coaches say, "Shoot your way out of it." And guess what? It usually fixes itself if they keep shooting.

Adam Fantilli is second on the team in shots with 53, behind Zach Werenski. He's only had two games in which he's had zero shots. Kent Johnson has had three games with zero shots, while Werenski has had shots in every game.

Fantilli is also leading the team in faceoff win percentage at 60.9%. He's third on the team in hits and takeaways, and second on the team in total shots attempted. In terms of fancy stats, He's 4th on Corsi and 8th in Fenwick. It's his 9.4 shooting % that's way down from his previous seasons, and that seems to be the issue for him right now.

The entire team minus Werenski and Johnson could be put on this same boat.

Let the kid shoot his way out of the slump he's in instead of benching him. It does no good to have him on the bench. Yes, the team is bad right now, but benching someone doesn't suddenly "Wake the rest of the team up."

It's going to take a lot more than a single guy breaking out of a scoring slump to get this team on the right track.
