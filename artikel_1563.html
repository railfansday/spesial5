Real Madrid: Kylian Mbappé on strike?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Jérôme Rothen has a very specific idea to move the lines at FIFA. Very critical of Gianni Infantino, the former PSG player believes that footballers must take their responsibilities and unite to obtain a change at the head of world football. And for him, this mobilization must necessarily involve the biggest stars on the planet.

In his show Rothen s’igne, on RMC, the consultant notably cited Kylian Mbappé as the figures capable of launching a real movement to demand the end of the Infantino era. “At the top, you have all the great players, who represent international football. The mobilization must start from them. Football belongs to footballers »launched the former French international, who also incited Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo to initiate the protest.

Jérôme Rothen does not hide his hostility towards the Swiss leader, whom he accuses of favoring economic interests to the detriment of the health of the players. “They know that Infantino is only there for business and that he is taking money off their backs. This is unacceptable. The health of football is in danger,” he denounced.

But how can you put pressure on the FIFA president? For the consultant, the answer is radical: stop playing. “Strike for 15 days, you’ll see if they don’t fire Infantino!” »he suggested. A spectacular call which places the biggest stars of world football face to face with their responsibilities, according to him. It remains to be seen whether his message will be heard for Kylian Mbappé and others.
