Dems' Majority Is Bigger, More Fragile, Than It Looks

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

This past election night was the worst night Republicans have had in decades, and the best Democrats have had since 2008. As of Friday, the Associated Press's count showed Democrats with 255 House seats won and Republicans with 169, with 11 seats left to be determined. In the Senate, the Associated Press has Democrats at 55 seats against 44 for the GOP, with one seat, Alaska, not yet counted for either side.
