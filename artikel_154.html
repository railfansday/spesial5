Cause of death released for partner of Gypsy Rose Blanchard

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Police investigation continues even as Louisiana coroner’s office releases findings on death of Kenan ‘Ken’ Urker

The cause of death of Gypsy Rose Blanchard’s partner, who died last month, has been released.

On Monday, the Lafourche parish coroner’s office in Louisiana determined that Kenan “Ken” Urker, 34, died from accidental polysubstance drug toxicity on 1 October, USA Today reports. The coroner’s office also said the case is complete.

However, law enforcement officials said that their investigation remains ongoing and is separate from the coroner’s inquiry.

“The Lafourche Parish Sheriff’s Office continues to investigate the circumstances surrounding the death of Kenan Urker. While we understand the Lafourche Parish Coroner’s Office has released updated information regarding the cause of death, our investigation is separate and remains ongoing. At this time, we have no additional information to release,” the sheriff’s office said.

Urker was found dead at his home on his 34th birthday last month after relatives requested a welfare check, according to the sheriff’s office.

In a police report, authorities said Urker was “cold to the touch” and “not breathing”. They added that officers at the scene were “able to see Mr Urker from the living room area lying on his back in [a] bed”.

Authorities also reported finding “paraphernalia (syringes and spoon) in the bathroom next to a brown powdery substance”.

Following Urker’s death, Blanchard, who served prison time for her role in her mother’s murder before being released in 2023, told People: “I don’t know if it was intentional or not … He was not in a good state of mind lately.”
