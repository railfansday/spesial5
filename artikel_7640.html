Disbarred attorney ‘flooded’ court with AI filings, panel says

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Fourth District Court of Appeal in Florida on Wednesday banned a disbarred Florida attorney convicted of fraud from submitting pro se filings in his case after he allegedly “flooded” a trial court with documents, many generated with artificial intelligence. (Photo by imaginima/Getty Images)

The Fourth District Court of Appeal in Florida on Wednesday banned a disbarred Florida attorney convicted of fraud from submitting pro se filings in his case after he allegedly “flooded” a trial court with documents, many generated with artificial intelligence.

After Willie Joe Jones of Florida filed more than 70 documents with a circuit court since April 2025, a panel of judges ordered the clerk of the court to reject any of his future filings unless they are signed by a lawyer in good standing with the Florida Bar.

“Abusive AI filing poses a serious threat to the orderly administration of justice,” the panel wrote.
