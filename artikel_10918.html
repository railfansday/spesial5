Former senator whose arrest went viral on YouTube wins another election

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

A former senator in Minnesota has been elected to a city council a year after a conviction on two felony burglary counts and being highlighted by a true crime YouTuber.

Back in August 2025, the true-crime YouTube channel Midwest Safety highlighted the story of Nicole Mitchell, the former Minnesota state senator.

Mitchell had been arrested by police back in 2024 after gaining entry to her stepmother’s house. The politician stated that she was there to retrieve belongings owned by her recently deceased dad because her stepmother “wouldn’t talk to me anymore.”

However, she then changed her story during a court trial, saying she wanted to check on the well-being of her stepmother. Mitchell was found guilty after a three-hour hearing and sentenced to 180 days in county jail. She was released in January 2026 after an early release appeal.

Mitchell initially said that she wouldn’t resign from her post, but ultimately did. Yet, she has now gotten back into politics.

The former state senator has been elected to a position on the Woodbury City Council, which has come under some scrutiny given her previous conviction.

As per local reports, the Minnesota Department of Corrections website previously stated that “if you are convicted of a felony you lose the right to hold public office until court-ordered discharge from probation or the end of your sentence.”

Some noted that was changed in 2023, but others have argued that the change only surrodned the ability to vote for a candidate, not actually run for office.
