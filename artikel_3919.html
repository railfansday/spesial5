Play 4 Hours of the Fantastic Control Resonant on PS Plus Premium

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Remedy has made the first four hours of Control Resonant playable through PS Plus Premium, in a new Game Trial that is on the service. It's available now and well worth checking out if you've not already played the game.

Experiencing the opening four hours of the title will give you a very good idea of what to expect from the entire campaign. You'll make it to the open zone structure and begin exploring, fight the Hiss, and be teased by some of the game's more unique elements.

I finished Control Resonant a few weeks ago, and it's one of my favourite PS5 games of the year so far. I strongly recommend you check this one out, especially now the first handful of hours are free on PS Plus Premium.

Should you like what you play, then the game is part of the PS Store Black Friday sale until 30th November — it's been discounted by 20%, down to £39.99 / $47.99. Will you give it a go? Let us know in the comments below.
