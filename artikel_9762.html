Show me the math: how a Telegram airdrop score stays reproducible

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Posted on Nov 23 • Originally published at dev.to

Our first post covered the payout side of NovaPoints — the TON Merkle claim contract that can only pay what a frozen snapshot says. This one is the other half: the score itself. If a contract can only pay the snapshot, the snapshot better be math anyone can redo.

Here's the entire Season 1 formula, straight from the service code (nova-score-v3):

Every input is a normalized count capped at a fixed ceiling — active days over 30, missions over 30, referrals over 10, special events over 5 — scaled to 0–1000. Reputation is already a 0–1000 internal score. The weights live in the season's config row, not in a spreadsheet someone could quietly edit.

Each score row keeps input_hash — SHA-256 over the canonical JSON of every input that produced it: active days, eligible days, mission count, referral count, reputation, special events, risk score, campaign multiplier, badge bonus in basis points, and the eligibility snapshot (wallet verified, qualified referrals, days active vs. required).

That hash is the audit hook. At snapshot time we publish the full allocation dataset; anyone can take a row, recompute the inputs from the disclosed event data, hash it, and check it matches. A score that can't be recomputed isn't published — it's asserted.

Anti-Sybil is the part everyone gets wrong. Ours doesn't delete accounts or hide allocations — it scales them. The risk_score maps to a band with a multiplier:

The multiplication is inside the same published formula — a flagged user still sees their real component scores in the app, and "why is my number X" always has a recomputable answer. Fraud signals (shared-IP clusters, device patterns, referral rings) feed the band; the band feeds the score.

The airdrop gates aren't a separate gatekeeper — calculateScore returns the verdict alongside the score, with machine-readable reasons (activity, wallet, referrals). Season 1 requires: active on at least half the days since joining, a verified basechain wallet, and 2 qualified referrals. Only eligible rows become claim-tree leaves — the contract never sees an ineligible address.

Same honesty section as before, because it matters: the rules are ours. Verification proves the payout matches the published formula — it can't prove the formula was generous. The risk bands' inputs are internal telemetry we haven't fully published yet; you can verify a score used a suspicious multiplier, not why the flag fired. And recomputation needs the dataset — which is exactly why it ships publicly on IPFS at snapshot.

Season 1 closes Dec 30, 2026 (20:30 UTC at the latest, earlier if 1,000 users qualify); claims target Jan 4. Every address and the full token page: https://nova-points.com/token
