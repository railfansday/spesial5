Three above a 7, two below a 5: Fan player ratings for Olympiacos 2-4 AC Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

AC Milan rose from the canvas floor on the outskirt of Athens on Thursday night, coming from two goals down to beat Olympiacos 4-2.

With 50 minutes on the clock, Milan trailed by two goals after a Mike Maignan own-goal and a strike from Jota Silva. However, Omari Hutchinson inspired a comeback, halving the deficit with a great solo goal then setting up Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic to complete the turnaround.

Diego Moreira scored on the break in the final seconds of added time to add a fourth and make sure of the victory, which boost the Rossoneri back into the top 24 with three games to go.

Looking at what our readers gave the players for the win in Piraeus, there is a real mixed bag of scores which in many ways matches the two-sided nature of the performance.

Starting with the negatives, Koni De Winter really struggled all night and played a stray pass for the second goal, which resulted in an average score of 4.5 out of 10 from our readers. Pervis Estupinan didn’t do much better, getting a 4.6.

Sankhoun Diawara had one of his tougher nights and got a 5, while Goncalo Ramos only got a 5.3 and Ardon Jashari also fell below a passing grade, but only just (5.9). Mike Maignan was scored a 6.1 and Adrien Rabiot a 6.2.

Then we get to the difference-makers: Diego Moreira averaged a 7, Alphadjo Cisse a 7.3 and Omari Hutchinson one of our highest grades of the season, with an outstanding 9 out of 10.

The subs were as follows: Yunus Musah got a 6.6, Christian Pulisic an 8.1, Fikayo Tomori a 7/1, Filippo Terracciano a 5.9 and Christian Comotto a 6.1.
