Nottingham Forest v Everton Ticket News

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

PREMIER LEAGUE NOTTINGHAM FOREST V EVERTON   THE CITY GROUNDSUNDAY 20 DECEMBER KICK-OFF 2PM Please note the supporter number of each attending supporter must be given at the point of sale.

We have received an allocation of 3,006 tickets; priced as below:

We have also received 14 pairs of wheelchair bays.

Nottingham Forest has a strict policy that children must be at least 4 years of age when attending fixtures at the City Ground. Babies in arms and children in buggies will not be admitted for their own safety. Children under the age of 14 will NOT be permitted entry to the ground unless accompanied by a person over 18 years of age.

Please note, the majority of the visiting supporter area is now an area of licensed safe standing. Visiting supporters will be subject to the Code of Conduct as per the conditions of the licence.

Tickets will go on sale in the following order of priority:

26/27 STM with 8 credits from 26/27 season from Monday 23 November at 10am

26/27 STM with 7+ credits from 26/27 season from Monday 23 November at 2pm

26/27 STM with 5+ credits from the 26/27 season from Tuesday 24 November 10am

*26/27 STM with 3+ credits from the 26/27 season from Tuesday 24 November at 2pm

*All tickets are subject to availability.

Please note our full allocation for this fixture will be issued as digital tickets.

Coach travel costs £31 and departs from Goodison at TBC. Supporters need to be members of the Travel Club to purchase coach tickets. The Travel Club Membership costs just £5.

Tickets can be purchased online, over the phone on 0151 556 1878 or in person via the South Stand ticket office at Hill Dickinson Stadium. Opening times for all our ticketing outlets can be found here.

It is an offence under Section 166 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 for any unauthorised person to resell tickets. Further information regarding the clubs stance against Ticket Touting can be seen here.
