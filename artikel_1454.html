Tuttosport: Jashari ‘into the cauldron’ – chances arrive to justify €35m price tag

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

AC Milan will be without Luka Modric for the must-win game against Olympiacos, presenting a chance to Ardon Jashari.

According to Tuttosport, Modric will be missing this evening at the Karaiskakis Stadium in Athens, having suffered a muscle strain in his right thigh in the closing minutes of the match against Frosinone. It will also force him to miss Monday’s trip to Cagliari.

In his place, Jashari will have a new chance at being the midfield anchor. The 24-year-old Swiss midfielder arrived in the summer of 2025 thanks to Igli Tare, who pursued him for two months, then snatched him from Club Brugge for €35.7m.

Jashari hasn’t quite blossomed yet and his story has been a complicated one. It started with a fractured fibula that sidelined him for two months at the end of August in his first season, while Adrien Rabiot’s arrival and top performances gave him extra competition.

Ultimately, Jashari made 17 appearances in his first season nine of them as a starter, with zero goals and one assist. Under Massimiliano Allegri, the former Luzern player racked up 880 minutes, but now under Ruben Amorim he’s already at 666, and it is destined to rise.

Tonight, as mentioned, he’ll be a starter and will already be approaching the total minutes he played in his entire first season by the end of November. So, has Jashari turned things around with Amorim? Not exactly.

In the summer, it was thought this would be his season, but after the initial tests the Portuguese coach decided to obviously continue with the two more experienced man Modric and Rabiot. Jashari then saw himself overtaken in the pecking order by Yunus Musah as well.

So far, he’s started seven times and has only impressed against RB Salzburg and Udines,, while he disappointed against Benfica, Bologna and Ferencvaros. Tonight, he’ll have another important chance to prove not only that he should stay at Milan, but that he can be a starter.

“I know I can do much better. Against Frosinone, it was a good match for me, but it’s not the level I want to show,” the Swiss declared on the eve of the game at a pre-match press conference.

“In the past, in the teams I played for, I was a central player or in the starting eleven, so I always played. I think with Milan I have to learn to be ready even if I don’t always play, and I hope to be decisive.” It all starts with a tough night in Athens.
