Time to take Portugal seriously again? Stats since 2022 show why Cristiano Ronaldo retirement could be a blessing

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Cristiano Ronaldo has announced his retirement from international football after 23 years with Portugal. But is the national side actually stronger without him? We’ve crunched the numbers.

Ronaldo bowed out as his country’s record goalscorer, having scored 146 goals in 234 appearances. In his retirement announcement, he spoke of “handing back the number seven shirt” after more than two decades representing his country. Portugal fans can bet on soccer at Bovada.

In 234 international appearances, he scored 146 goals and provided 45 assists, averaging a goal contribution every 97.4 minutes.

With those sorts of numbers, it begs the question of why people would think that Ronaldo is now a hindrance to his country, rather than one of their biggest assets.

This narrative started to gain prominence around the 2022 World Cup, when CR7 found himself in a rut for both club and country.

He only scored one goal during that tournament and his understudy, Goncalo Ramos, was looking sharper at the time.

This led to Ronaldo being benched for Portugal’s quarter-final clash against Morocco, which they ultimately lost 1-0.

Many predicted that was the end for Ronaldo on the international stage, but that couldn’t have been further from the truth.

Roberto Martinez put Ronaldo front and centre, leading to two underwhelming tournament performances and early exits.

Ronaldo has now finally called time on his international career, with his announcement following a turbulent period involving new manager Jorge Jesus.

With a lot of discourse currently around Ronaldo and Portugal, we’ve compared the national team results with and without him since the 2022 World Cup.

Since then, Portugal have played a total of 49 matches. Of those, CR7 has featured in 37 and has missed 12.

In the games Ronaldo has played, Portugal have boasted a win percentage of 70.2% and have scored an average of 2.05 goals per game.

Without Ronaldo, Portugal’s win percentage is higher at 75% and their goal-per-game ratio jumps to 3.41.

Of course, the sample size of games without Ronaldo is small, meaning that the odd freak result will have a larger impact on the numbers.

For example, Ronaldo didn’t feature in Portugal’s 9-0 hammering of Luxembourg back in 2023, which boosts the numbers quite a bit in this regard.
