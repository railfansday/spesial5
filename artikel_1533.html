French team: the first reaction of Eli Junior Kroupi after his call-up

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It was expected, it is now here. Cited for several months among the candidates for the France team, Éli-Junior Kroupi has just been called up by Zinédine Zidane following the injury to Marcus Thuram. A natural continuation for the Bournemouth striker, who has passed through all the youth categories, from U16 to Espoirs. At the FFF media microphone, Kroupi spoke about his first steps in the selection, and his pride in meeting up with former teammates.

“I made all my selections for the French team (from U16 to Espoirs), and to arrive with the A is just incredible. Very proud to see my teammates again, it’s a dream come true. (…) My first workout? We played a game, it was great to play with players like that, and even the intensity of the training was incredible. (…) I’m going to get to know everyone, I can’t wait to discover this team, I know quite a few: Adrien Truffert with whom I play at Bournemouth, but also the young people who came from the Espoirs: Désiré (Doué), Saël (Kumbedi), Leny (Yoro), Jérémy (Jacquet)”
