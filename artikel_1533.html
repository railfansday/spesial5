Roma only behind PSG in defensive output this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Roma have found an additional weapon in their defenders to fuel the best attack in Serie A, with eight of the 31 goals scored so far this season coming from their back line.

As La Gazzetta dello Sport points out, their contribution accounts for 26% of Gasperini’s team’s overall goals, with Hermoso, Balerdi, and Lulli each contributing two, while Ndicka and Ghilardi have each scored one. Including their nine assists, the defenders’ total contribution to goals rises to 17, almost double that of Inter and Lazio, both with nine.

This figure confirms the importance of the back line in the Giallorossi’s attacking output, as they consistently involve the center backs in creating chances and making runs into the opponent’s penalty area.

The performance of the Giallorossi defenders is unmatched even in the major European leagues, where only Luis Enrique’s PSG have scored more goals from their defensive line, with nine. The French side, Roma’s next opponents in the Champions League, are one point ahead of Gasperini’s team, while Atlético Madrid are behind them with seven goals, Mainz, Stuttgart, and Brighton with five.

The basis of these numbers is Gasperini’s system, which requires defenders to drop forward and participate consistently in the attacking phase.

The wing-backs often accompany the play into the attacking midfield, seeking space to insert themselves and create numerical superiority. Curiously, Gianluca Mancini, one of the players who most often plays this role, is still missing from the scorers’ charts, but he hasn’t scored in the league so far.
