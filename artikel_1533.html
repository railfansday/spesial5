Real Madrid: Marc Cucurella still believes in Vinicius Jr’s return to his prime

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Not really himself since the painful episode of the 2024 Ballon d’Or, which was lost to Rodri, Vinicius Jr is struggling to regain his influence at Real Madrid. Author of a particularly sluggish start to the season, the Brazilian winger is going through a zone of turbulence marked by an obvious drop in impact, further accentuated by the remarkable emergence of his competitor Yan Diomandé. In a context where doubts are accumulating around his performances, the Madrid striker is desperately looking for the trigger to restart the dynamic which made him one of the most destabilizing players in La Liga.

Faced with the criticism that escorts the performances of the Madrilenian, Marc Cucurella stepped up to provide strong support to his locker room partner. Passing the microphoneEl Chiringuitothe Spanish full-back displayed absolute certainty about the potential comeback of his teammate: “Vini?” If he returns to his best level, we will simply be unbeatable. » A firm statement intended to remobilize a key element for the continuation of the Merengue campaign.
