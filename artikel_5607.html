Christian McCaffrey Shows Off His Musical Talents Inside $14 Million California Home—Weeks After Welcoming Baby No. 2 With Olivia Culpo

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

San Francisco 49ers star Christian McCaffrey has showed off his unusual skill while also giving fans a glimpse of his and Olivia Culpo's $14.5 million property.

The pro athlete, 30, took to social media to showcase his skills off the football field while holding his 5-week-old son, Roman.

In the clip shared online, he played the harmonica to Billy Joel's hit "Piano Man," while his brother-in-law Gus Culpo worked the keyboard.

“Ear training with Uncle Gus,” McCaffrey penned alongside the video.

In the clip, baby Roman is nestled asleep on McCaffrey’s chest as the NFL star holds him in his left hand while he plays the harmonica with his right.The glimpse of his musical talent came mere days after his wife, Olivia Culpo, 34, took to Instagram to share a picture of the family on game day. It showed her, Roman, and daughter Colette, 1, stepping out to watch McCaffrey take on the Las Vegas Raiders at Levi's Stadium.

One of the snaps showed off their game-day attire, which included a 49ers shirt with "Daddy" on the back.

Another image showed Roman in his stroller and was captioned, "Roman's first game."

The beauty mogul also shared a photo of her and her daughter, which she captioned, “I’m not a regular mom, I’m a cool mom.”

Culpo also shared a video of her taking their children to embrace their dad after the game.

“We’re back with one more little fan in the stands this season," she added. "A little more love coming along for the game this season."

The model revealed that she welcomed their second baby on Sept. 29.

Months before, she revealed that she had transformed their guest room into a nursery. She shared that she overhauled the room her sister Aurora Culpo's kids Remi, 7, and Soleil, 5, use when they visit.

"New baby's room for now. I wanted to keep the twin beds because Remi and Soleil sleep here when they come over," she wrote alongside an image of the nursery she shared on her social media page in June.

The room featured a neutral aesthetic with an animal theme.

At the time, she explained, "We also don’t know the baby’s gender yet, so this feels perfect neutral for now. Honestly, newborns don’t really sleep in their room in the beginning anyway because they’re eating around the clock. That’s one of the reasons I’ve never felt the need to find out the gender to 'have the nursery ready.'"

That same month, Culpo revealed she and McCaffrey were in the process of overhauling their $14.5 million property to ensure it would look its best by the time their newest family member arrived.

Culpo and McCaffrey purchased the Fryman Canyon, CA, dwelling from actor George Clooney in 2025, but they are now removing all traces of the "Ocean's Eleven" star from the home as they turn it into their dream family abode.

Revealing a sneak peek at the changes, Culpo pointed to one particular area of irritation that she has since transformed: dual bathrooms that sat next to each other with their own doors.

"Remember the two bathrooms next to each other that made no sense? All gone! I moved the door to the kitchen and now it will be a scullery,” Culpo wrote alongside an image of her hallway with the offending bathrooms on full display.

The model did not stop there, moving on to other spaces in the house, all of which have been given a stunning face-lift with new wallpaper, paint, and upgraded floors.

She then shared before-and-after images of the dining room, with the space now cleared of all previous decor, including the industrial lighting fixture hung in the middle of the room, as well as the orange curtains and white wood-paneled ceilings.

The space was given a fresh aesthetic with patterned wallpaper, hardwood flooring, exposed wooden beams, and an elegant crystal chandelier hanging at the center of the space.

“Currently ... Dining table and chairs going in soon," Culpo captioned the video of the newly revamped room.

The dwelling serves as the primary residence for the model and her husband, who is currently one of the leading players on the San Francisco 49ers.

The opulent six-bedroom, six-bathroom abode spans 7,354 square feet, giving the couple plenty of room for their growing family.

Inside, the home boasts a library, screening room, and wine wall.

Culpo and McCaffrey purchased the home from Clooney, 65, who bought it from Fleetwood Mac singer Stevie Nicks for $2.2 million in 1995.

One month after splashing out a staggering sum on the property, Culpo gushed about it on Instagram.

"Now onto my next project, the most exciting one of all. Our forever home. So much original character to protect in this home, it’s going to be such a special project," she said.

The couple's purchase came four months after they tied the knot in a lavish Rhode Island ceremony attended by their close friends and family.

At the time, Culpo told People magazine, "I loved every part of my wedding. I love my husband and the people we got to celebrate that day with."

Three months before they said "I do," Culpo revealed she was ready to start expanding her family, joking that she was going to "rip out [her] IUD” and “start trying immediately” after the wedding.

The former Miss Universe also holds the keys to a $3.5 million property in Encino, CA, which she purchased in 2019 and which served as her and McCaffrey's main residence during the NFL offseason.

In 2021, she told Architectural Digest she purchased the five-bedroom, seven-bathroom property to get away from the "noise" of Hollywood.

“I realized I really wanted something that felt more calming, like a sanctuary almost. A respite from the city,” she said at the time.

McCaffrey previously owned a North Carolina mansion, which he sold in 2024 for $12.5 million, and a Charlotte, NC, condo, which he let go of in 2024 for $3 million.

In recent months, Culpo has confessed that the couple keep their romance alive during her spouse's busy season by "prioritizing date nights at least twice a month."

“During the season, it can be a little more difficult. Just prioritizing one-on-one time," she told E! News in September.
