PropStream’s Webinar Series ‘Connect to Close: Build Your 2027 Launch Plan’ Returns

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

PropStream announced that reservations to its Connect to Close: Your 2027 Launch Plan webinar are now open. The three-day live webinar series—happening Nov. 17 to 19—is designed to help real estate investors, wholesalers and agents build a more consistent process for finding opportunities and moving deals forward.

According to a release, across three hands-on sessions, viewers will hear from PropStream CEO Brian Tepfer, Brent Daniels from Wholesaling Inc., Justin and Ryan Dossey from Ballpoint Marketing, investors and PropStream power users Jenn and Joe Delle Fave, alongside PropStream and BatchDialer experts Eddie Arguelles and Burton Alicando.

Together, they’ll share market perspectives, practical strategies and live demonstrations to help viewers prepare for 2027.

Day 1: Search, Identify, Connect and Transact With Live Demos

Led by Arguelles and joined by Jenn and Joe Delle Fave, the webinar explores the four actions that help move opportunities forward: Search, identify, connect and transact. Through live demonstrations and practical examples, attendees will see how these steps work together and build a process to practice during the final weeks of 2026 and carry into 2027.

Day 2: Real Professionals. Real Results. Real Talk.

Joined by Brian Tepfer and Justin and Ryan Dossey from Ballpoint Marketing, Arguelles will lead conversations about where the market stands, where PropStream is headed and what it means for your business going into 2027. The session explores how direct mail fits into today’s outreach strategies and what it takes to build an effective campaign.

Day 3: Put Your Plan Into Action With Live Dialing

Alicando, joined by Wholesaling Inc. owner and CEO Brent Daniels—with Arguelles moderating—will make real calls, navigate conversations and respond to objections in real time, putting the framework and strategies from the first two days into practice.

Between calls, Alicando and Daniels will break down their approach, discuss what worked and what didn’t and share lessons attendees can apply to their outreach.

For more information, visit propstream.com.
