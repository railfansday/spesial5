Well that was a busy year! What started with non-league Macclesfield knocking FA Cup holders Crystal Palace out in the third round of the competition, ended with Harry Kane lifting the Ballon d’Or in London.

In between all of that, we saw Arsenal win their first Premier League title in 22 years, then miss out on the Champions League weeks later as they were beaten on penalties by defending champions PSG.

Manchester City won both domestic cups, then were found guilty of breaching Premier League financial rules. Manchester United began the year with Ruben Amorim in the dugout, end it with Mauricio Pochettino, and for 303 days in the middle had Michael Carrick in charge.

Then there was the main event of the year. A 48-team World Cup promised chaos and duly delivered, culminating with Spain conquering Argentina to sew a second star onto La Roja’s crest. England, meanwhile, gave us a wild ride to bronze in a 10-goal thriller against France.
