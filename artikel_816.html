Taylor Swift CMA Awards Performance: Every Easter Egg

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Taylor Swift made her return to the CMA Awards Nov. 18 after 10 years with a medley of “I Knew It, I Knew You” and “Our Song,” and her devoted fanbase has already found several Easter eggs.

It looks like Taylor Swift still has plenty of tricks up her sleeve—or rather snakes in her boot?

As the “I Knew It, I Knew You” singer took the stage at the CMA Awards Nov. 18 to perform a medley of her newest country hit as well as one of her first, “Our Song,” many fans noticed there may be several Easter eggs hinting at the re-release of her debut album.

The first clue fans noticed was dropped before Swift even started singing, as she began her performance in a backstage dressing room that was decorated with several familiar pieces of clothing, namely dresses she wore during her first rodeo.

For one, there was the butter yellow ballgown she wore to the CMA Awards in 2007—the year she scored her very first win—that was strewn across the couch behind Swift. Then, hanging on a clothing rack to her right, was a floral print halter dress that Swift wore to the Academy of Country Music Awards in 2006, which was one of the very first major red carpet walks of her career.

Even more noteworthy were the final two dresses fans noticed. One purple-looking dress that was directly behind Swift on a couch, mostly obscured from sight, appeared to be the fluffy strapless number she wore for a portion of the music video “Our Song.”

Meanwhile, the final dress was most obvious: the teal ballgown Swift wore not only in the “Teardrops on my Guitar” music video, but also to the Country Music Television Awards in 2007, when she won for Breakthrough Music Video of the Year Award for “Tim McGraw.”

But Swift’s old wardrobe weren’t the only Easter eggs Swifties spotted. Swift’s choreography appeared to walk her through the many eras of her career, including a red cowboy hat to denote her fourth studio album, as well as a pair of sunglasses briefly worn as a callback to the opening performance of her 1989 World Tour.

And the inclusion of several country music stars including Brad Paisley, Little Big Town, Sugarland, Brooks & Dunn, Eric Church, Lainey Wilson and Keith Urban led many fans to believe that the singers might be featured on vault tracks of the upcoming debut rerelease. (FWIW, however, Swift also has deep ties to many of these country stars, having covered or penned songs with or for several of them.)

Of course, Swift also included plenty of nods to Toy Story 5, the film for which she and Jack Antonoff wrote “I Knew It, I Knew You” for. In the dressing room at the beginning of the performance, a Jessie doll can be seen propped on one of the couches. And once Swift makes it to the stage, her blue guitar was adorned with Toy Story’s signature white clouding, while a tree with a tire swing engraved with the words “Jessie was here” was placed behind her.

Although Swifties have called Easter egg many times before, Swift has been teasing the release of her debut album since she confirmed she had purchased her master recordings back in May 2025. While the purchase signified the end of her Taylor’s Version era, she did admit that her first album was not only done, but would have its moment to “re-emerge when the time is right, if that would be something you guys would be excited about.”
