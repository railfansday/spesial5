James Blunt Says He Legally Changed His Name to Ryan Air: Here's Why

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

James Blunt said he legally changed his name to Ryan Air—in reference to the budget airline Ryanair—after his 2026 album Airplane Mode claimed the No. 1 spot on the UK music charts.

James Blunt is saying goodbye to his name.

After the “You’re Beautiful” singer vowed to legally change his moniker to Ryan Air if his 2026 album Airplane Mode made it to the top spot on the UK music charts, it appears he’s making good on his promise.

In fact, the 52-year-old revealed in a screenshot shared to Instagram Nov. 16 that he changed his full name from James Hillier Blount to Ryan Bel Air following the album’s success.

And it seems the weight of it all wasn’t too heavy. After all, the “Goodbye My Lover” singer captioned the post, “This cost me £53.05.”

Nearly a week earlier, Blunt made a public promise to change his name should his album, which dropped Nov. 6, get the top spot on the music charts—a proclamation he’s made in the past.

“Hi, I’m James Blunt—and you f--ked it,” he explained in a Nov. 11 Instagram video. “Two years ago, I promised to legally change my name to Blunty McBluntface if my album made it to number one on the charts. Didn't happen. Your fault. So now, I'm giving you a second chance.”

“You may have heard that my new album Airplane Mode is out now,” he continued, “and if it makes it to number one, I will legally change my name to Ryan Air.”

Getting some practice with the moniker, Blunt, dad to two kids with wife Sofia Wellesley, went on to say repeatedly, “The name's Air—Ryan Air.”

“So, stream it, buy it, just get it to number one,” he said, “or else I'll have to carry on being James Blunt. Nobody wants that.”

And if his new name sounds familiar, that’s because it’s inspired by budget airline Ryanair. At the time, the company trolled his video, writing on X, “At least people would know you then.”

Airplane Mode marks his first UK number one album in 19 years, according to the Official Charts Company. And while the Celebrity Traitors alum is known for his funny side, he got serious when it came to talking about the accomplishment.

“I can’t tell you how grateful I am to every single person who has gone out there and downloaded, or bought, or streamed my album,” he told the Official Charts Company Nov. 13. “From the bottom of my heart, thank you so much.”

As for how he plans to commemorate the milestone, Blunt added, “I shall celebrate by getting absolutely pissed.”
