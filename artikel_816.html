Dustin Hoffman Was Held at Gunpoint During Affair With Married Woman

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Dustin Hoffman detailed the time he was caught having an affair with a married woman, recalling the moment when her husband “walked in with a gun.”

Dustin Hoffman once had a real Mrs. Robinson in his life.

But whereas his character drove off into the sunset in The Graduate, Hoffman’s own affair with a married woman almost took a deadly turn when her husband, wielding a gun, caught the pair in bed together.

“I was having an affair with a married woman,” Hoffman recalled to Conan O’Brien during the Nov. 9 episode of his Conan O'Brien Needs A Friend podcast. “She thought that once her husband went to sleep, that he stayed asleep.”

But that wasn’t exactly the case for this midnight cowboy.

“I was invited to come over after he went to sleep,” Hoffman explained. “There we are, having a festival of sorts and he walked in with a gun. He pointed at me and I remember he said, ‘Get out, get out!’”

And while the incident happened in his youth, The Graduate star remembers fearing for his life as if it was yesterday.

“When you have a gun pointed at you, first of all, that hole is much bigger than the actual hole,” Hoffman said. “And you realize how, any second, any second he’s going to pull that trigger because his finger is on the trigger and you think any second he’s going to.”

“You realize, ‘That’s it, that’s it, I’m gonna die, I’m gonna die, the next second, the next second, the next second,’” he added. “And you’d do anything to avoid getting killed.”

The close brush with death was enough to douse the flames of his passion. In fact, when O’Brien asked whether the pair “continued the festival,” after her husband calmed down, the 89-year-old replied, “No.”

Today, it seems Hoffman’s love life is far less complicated. After all, the Tootsie actor tied the knot with wife Lisa Gottsegen in 1980 before they welcomed four kids together.

Coming up on 50 years of marriage, Gottsegen previously shed light on what has made her connection with Hoffman—who also shares two kids with ex-wife Anne Byrne—stand the test of time, telling Closer Weekly in 2017 that they’re “more in love” than ever.

“We work hard at it,” she added at the time. “I knew I was going to end up with Dusty, and that my life would be traveling around with him.”
