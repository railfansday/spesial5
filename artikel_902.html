Why was Leny Yoro sent off against Liverpool?

Life at Manchester United got off to a nightmare start for Mauricio Pochettino as his side was reduced to 10 men inside eight minutes in his managerial bow against Liverpool.

Liverpool started brightly and fashioned a chance out of nothing when Ronald Araujo’s hooked clearance left Matthijs de Ligt - making his first league start in a year - in no man’s land.

Alexander Isak latched onto the ball but was clattered into last man Leny Yoro just outside the box, prompting referee Sam Barrett to reach into his pocket and show the Frenchman a red card.

"It doesn't matter if you're playing with a back four, or back five, you've got to do the basics right. De Ligt - I'm amazed with him at this level,” Roy Keane said on Sky Sports.

"I know he's come back from an injury, he's just not reading the flight of the ball. If you do get it wrong, you run back, you sprint back, and then Yoro has got to be covering. If you're a defender, you always expect the worst."

There was a debate over whether VAR should have overturned the decision due to Isak’s heavy touch, which put into question he would have kept control of the ball - a key consideration in determining if a player has denied an obvious goalscoring opportunity (DOGSO).

"Isak's not getting the ball. There's no way he's getting the ball,” Keane added.

"It's clumsy from United. Obviously it's one straight ball over the top. You shouldn't be in so much trouble.

"But there's no way he's getting that ball. Absolutely not. I know Isak is quick and he's sharp, but we've looked at it again.

"It's really poor defending. Players not smelling danger. But Isak is not getting that ball. Absolutely not."

But VAR John Brooks did not call for a review, with the Premier League Match Centre writing in a statement: “The referee’s call of red card to Yoro for denial of an obvious goal-scoring opportunity (DOGSO) was checked and confirmed by VAR.

“REF: ‘It's a red card for DOGSO. On-field decision is outside [the box], red card, DOGSO’.

“VAR: ‘Defender bundles into him into the back, gets too tight, I think it's a foul’.”

Things then went from bad to worse for United as Dominik Szoboszlai stepped up to the free-kick and fired past Senne Lammens into the bottom-left corner, ensuring Yoro’s blunder was fully punished.

Liverpool doubled their lead just three minutes later, with Florian Wirtz volleying high into the goal as Pochettino’s side rapidly unravelled.

Liverpool’s outright domination of the opening stages, combined with losing a defender, sparked Pochettino into making an early change, replacing forward Bryan Mbeumo with centre-back Harry Maguire in the 22nd minute.
