Sofia Franklyn Calls Out Alex Cooper & Matt Kaplan for Memoir Comments

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Alex Cooper and Matt Kaplan's comments about Sofia Franklyn's memoir Daddy Issues have caused the former Call Her Daddy podcaster to wonder "how dirty" her ex-coworker will go.

That's how former Call Her Daddy co-host Sofia Franklyn—whose public falling out with Alex Cooper led to her split from the podcast in 2020—felt until she learned Cooper and her husband Matt Kaplan have allegedly taken issue with her memoir behind closed doors.

"I have heard that some people mentioned in the book, some main characters in my book, are working very hard to discredit this story," Franklyn revealed on The Bossticks podcast Nov. 9. "I thought I was bulletproof, and then I started to think how dirty are they gonna play?"

And the 34-year-old wasn't afraid to call out her former creative collaborator—who runs the Unwell podcast network with Kaplan—by name.

As Franklyn quipped, "I'm talking about Alex and Matt, hi. And if you're listening, hi."

The podcaster said that hearing what Cooper and Kaplan allegedly said about Daddy Issues in private has made her question what the pair might do in public to shut down her side of the narrative.

"Are they gonna go after people I love?" Franklyn questioned. "That, I do not like. If my family, my husband, becomes collateral, that actually scares me."

And the concerns have put a dent in Franklyn's armor as she rolls out her book, she said.

"I thought I was gonna go into this process being like, two middle fingers in the air," she continued, conceding, "We'll see how that plays out."

Cooper has previously shared why she continued CHD under Barstool Sports while Franklyn wanted to find a new network in her Hulu docuseries Call Her Alex, stating, "Sofia didn't want to take the deal, but I did, so I stayed. I realized the 'Daddy Gang' was bigger than both of us."

E! News has reached out to Cooper's reps for comment regarding Franklyn's allegations but has not yet heard back.

Still, Franklyn is hopeful that fans of Call Her Daddy who discounted her perspective six years ago may have a different opinion this time around.

In the author's words, "It's going to be a lot harder to discredit me now because the audience is smarter now," adding that she's witnessed online how "people are slightly more aware of how media PR headlines work."

Since adding multiple podcasts under the Unwell umbrella, Cooper has been accused of "sick and twisted" behavior by former employee Alix Earle. The bad blood over Earle's canceled podcast Hot Mess became public when the Florida influencer cosigned a TikTok claiming her ex-boss' success came from her "ambulance chaser" approach with vulnerable podcast guests.

In response to engaging with the video, Cooper invited Earle to speak openly about her allegations instead of "passive aggressive reposts" in her own TikTok.

As such, Franklyn believes she knows how Cooper might use her media savvy to retaliate amid her book's release.

"If someone's playing really gnarly, they are reaching out to your college roommates, people you worked with, people that you've had two, maybe one experience with," she explained, "and they're asking them, 'What can you tell me about this person?' And then I don't know exactly how they'll try to flip it."

And with that in mind, Franklyn made sure to be brutally honest when recounting her life story.

Even if the truth is "embarrassing," "shameful" or "horrible" to look back on, she said, "I'm going to be real as f--k."
