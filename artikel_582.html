The Pokémon TCG Delta Reign Booster Display Box hits a new low at Amazon — save over $15

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Delta Reign Booster Display Box is on sale for $210.48 at Amazon, down from its $227 list price.

The Pokémon TCG's Delta Reign expansion has not even been out for two weeks, but prices for its 36-pack Booster Display Box have just taken a huge hit ahead of Black Friday. Amazon has now knocked the box down to a new all-time low, letting trading card collectors pick up the Mega Rayquaza-focused set for less than $215.

As of Nov. 17, the Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Delta Reign Booster Display Box is yours for $210.48 at Amazon via third-party sellers, taking 7% off its recent $227 price. Now at the lowest price this box has ever been on the site, that brings the price of each booster down to $5.85.

For comparison, the lowest price we’d seen previously was the $239.94 record-low deal we covered on Nov. 9 — meaning the price has had another $29.46 shaved off in just eight days.

It's now closer to its estimated $184.05 market price on TCGplayer, and Amazon’s new offer beats any listing at the dedicated trading card marketplace.
