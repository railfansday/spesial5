National Sandwich Day 2026 deals: Score free food from Subway, DoorDash, 7-Eleven, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

We've only just recovered from a huge weekend of eating. Halloween obviously means a whole lot of sweets and chocolates, but it also means BOGO offers, limited-edition drops, steep discounts, and genuinely free giveaways from a number of restaurant chains.

Didn't make the most of those offers? Don't fret, because National Sandwich Day takes place on Nov. 3.

We've scanned the market for all of the deals that make events like this so special. We're seeing limited-time offers from the likes of Subway, 7-Eleven, Capriotti’s, Potbelly, and more of your favorite spots this week.

7Rewards and Speedy Rewards members can grab a $1 fountain drink or Big Gulp with the purchase of any sandwich on Nov. 3.

Enter promo code SAMMIEDAY at checkout online or mention it in-store on Nov. 3 to unlock a BOGO 50% off deal across all chicken sandwich builds.

CapAddicts Rewards members score BOGO 50% off on all sub sizes for National Sandwich Day on Nov. 3 (redeemable in-store, online, or via the Capriotti's mobile app).

Skip the midday desk trek: Jersey Mike’s is offering free delivery on all orders of $5 or more placed through the mobile app or official website on Nov. 3.
