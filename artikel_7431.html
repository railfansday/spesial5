Snapchat adds more creator partnership opportunities

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The platform is testing an updated version of its Snap Creator Network, as well as new Visited By listings on its Snap Map.

Snapchat announced several new creator discovery and monetization opportunities, including an updated creator marketplace, new location markers for where creators have visited on the Snap Map, and an advanced agency partner program.

First, Snapchat is testing a refreshed version of its Snap Creator Network, which aims to make it easier for brands to find relevant creators to work with on Snapchat ad campaigns.

As shown in this screenshot, Snapchat’s updated creator discovery process uses artificial intelligence to better match brands with relevant creators.

As explained by Snapchat: “Advertisers can describe the exact creator or content they’re looking for, including audience, category, or campaign goals, and the Snap Creator Network will surface the best fit. Then, brands can explore the performance of creators, save them to shortlists, message them to establish partnerships, or even boost organic content, all in one place.”

Snapchat originally announced this creator marketplace upgrade in June, but it’s now launched the platform in open beta testing.

Snapchat has also introduced a new way for physical stores and locations to benefit from creator content, with Visited By listings on the Snap Map.

As shown in these examples, Visited By will add markers to places where creators that the user follows have filmed content. The highlighted listings will also include links to the relevant posts.

This could help brands tap into influencer recommendations, while also providing more incentive for stores to collaborate with popular creators in order to increase their presence in the Snap Map display.

Snapchat recently reported that 450 million people use Snap Map every month.

Finally, Snapchat launched an expanded Creator Agency Partner Program, which will provide agencies with additional training and support to ensure that they’re offering their clients the best guidance on creator marketing campaigns.

As per Snapchat, its updated Creator Agency Partner Program will include two tiers:

That will ensure that more agency providers are aware of the company’s evolving creator partnership efforts, and how to utilize its advanced discovery tools to guide brands towards the right creator partners.

Snapchat said the updated Creator Agency Partner Program is now in operation for agencies in the U.S., U.K. and the Middle East and North Africa.
