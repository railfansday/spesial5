Isaac Ellis out for Players Era Festival, Seth Trimble will play

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Louisville reserve guard Isaac Ellis will be unavailable for this week's Players Era Festival games because of an ankle injury suffered during last week's win over Stetston.

"We do not expect him to play this week at all," U of L head coach Pat Kelsey said during a Zoom call with the media on Monday morning. "

Through four games, Ellis is averaging 3.3 ppg while playing 13.3 minutes per contest.

The better news for Cardinal fans is that starting point guard Seth Trimble will be available for Tuesday night's tournament opener against No. 9 Texas Tech. Trimble was held out of the Stetson game because of an undisclosed "minor" injury.

As for guard De'Shayne Montgomery, Kelsey says the Dayton transfer is headed in the right direction, but any hope that he could see the floor this week in Las Vegas is misplaced.

"His foot's getting better," Kelsey aaid. "He's still on the same timeline we laid out a couple of weeks ago. He's been sick the last couple of days, but other than that he's getting better."

Louisville opens its run at the Players Era Festival Tuesday night against Texas Tech at 8 p.m. ET.
