Ireland to make historic first-ever tour of Pakistan, six-match series confirmed

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Ireland are set to make history in January 2027 when their men's side tours Pakistan for the first time, with a six-match 50-over series scheduled in Lahore as part of their preparations for the ICC World Cup Qualifier.Cricket Ireland on Thursday confirmed that the team will arrive in Pakistan on January 9 for a series running from January 13 to 24, with all six matches to be played at the Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore.The tour will comprise three 50-over matches against Pakistan A, known as the Shaheens, followed by a three-match ODI series against the senior Pakistan side. The matches will be played on January 13, 15 and 17 against the Shaheens, before the two teams meet in the ODIs on January 20, 22 and 24. Match timings are yet to be confirmed.While this will be the first visit to Pakistan by the Ireland men's side, it will not be the first tour by an Irish cricket team. The Ireland women's side visited the country in 2022 and famously won the T20I series.

The six-match assignment assumes particular significance for Ireland as they prepare for the ICC World Cup Qualifier in February 2027, which will be held in South Africa and Namibia.Graeme West, Cricket Ireland's Director of High Performance, underlined the importance of the tour in providing the one-day squad with valuable match practice ahead of the qualification campaign."This is an extremely important tour for our One-Day squad as they prepare for the ICC World Cup Qualifiers in February. Following the nine games played in the second half of 2026, a further six 50-over matches against quality opponents will support the players readiness for the qualifier taking place in South Africa and Namibia."While we originally planned a multi-format series, both Boards have agreed that their respective squads would benefit most from playing 50-over cricket given our upcoming participation in the World Cup Qualifier and Pakistan preparing for the World Cup itself later in 2027."Over the years we have enjoyed a close relationship with the Pakistan Cricket Board and their players, and we look forward to this historic first tour – the first of many in the coming years, we hope."The Pakistan Cricket Board had initially planned a triangular series involving Ireland, Pakistan and Zimbabwe. However, Zimbabwe subsequently withdrew, citing its inability to travel to Pakistan. The revised arrangement will see Ireland play two separate three-match assignments instead.

Ireland and Pakistan have faced each other seven times in men's ODIs, with Pakistan winning five matches, Ireland claiming one victory and one encounter ending in a tie.Ireland's only win remains one of the most memorable results in its cricketing history. It came during the 2007 World Cup in the Caribbean, when Ireland stunned Pakistan to knock the former champions out of the tournament.Pakistan, meanwhile, will enter the series after winning the ODI tri-series involving England and Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi and Lahore last month, under the captaincy of Shaheen Shah Afridi.

The six-match series will also offer Pakistan valuable preparation ahead of its international assignments in 2027, including the ODI World Cup later in the year.

13 January: Ireland Men v Shaheens – 1st 50-over match (Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore)

15 January: Ireland Men v Shaheens – 2nd 50-over match (Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore)

17 January: Ireland Men v Shaheens – 3rd 50-over match (Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore)

20 January: Ireland Men v Pakistan Men – 1st ODI (Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore)

22 January: Ireland Men v Pakistan Men – 2nd ODI (Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore)

24 January: Ireland Men v Pakistan Men – 3rd ODI (Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore)
