Jaguars Keep Rolling With Dominant Win Over Titans

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Jacksonville Jaguars made another statement on Sunday, traveling to Nashville and taking care of business against the Tennessee Titans with a convincing 40-23 victory. Jacksonville's offense put up 40 points on the road, while the Jaguars continued to show why they're clearly one of the teams to beat in the AFC. With Sunday's victory, Jacksonville extended its AFC South divisional lead over the Houston Texans with just eight regular-season games remaining.

Here's everything you need to know from Jacksonville's Week 10 40-23 victory over the Titans in Nashville.

Travis Hunter's diving first-quarter interception to get the ball back for the Jacksonville offense, following their turnover, was a thing of beauty and my favorite highlight, as Anthony Campanile's group continues to force turnovers inside their own 30-yard line.

The first-half pick was Hunter's third of the season.

The stats sheet clearly shows that the Jacksonville defense had a great day on a down-by-down basis, holding the Tennessee offense to 4/14 (28.6%) on third and fourth-down combined conversions. Yet, explosive plays via blown coverages and missed tackles still resulted in the Jacksonville defense surrendering two touchdowns to the Tennessee offense, in a way that is normally foreign to this Jacksonville defense. Outside of the blown drives, Jacksonville's plan of forcing teams to put together extended drives, then clamping down in the red zone, worked, for the most part. Tennessee had just one red zone attempt, a successful one, and Campanile's defense forced three long field goals (50, 53, and 54 yards) for the other scores.

The Jacksonville Jaguars offense has historically been dominant in the red zone of matchups. However, in this one, Jacksonville was nearly 1/4 (25%) in red zone attempts at the end of the third quarter, needing two questionable calls by the officials in their seventh drive with a touchdown. Following a third-down drop by Jakobi Meyers, Tennessee was called for roughing the passer to extend the series. Following that penalty, Jacksonville decided to attempt a fourth and two from the three-yard line, where Meyers could have been called for offensive pass interference on what looked to be a pick play. In addition to the red zone problems, the team settled for field goals from the 21, 27, and 30 yard line – just outside of the red zone area. The Jacksonville offense ended the day going 3/5 (60%) in the red zone, with the notable above drive impacting the numbers.

Running back Bhayshul Tuten had a dominant game against the Indianapolis Colts in Week 7. However, since that time, the second-year running back has averaged less than 3.4 yards per carry in four of five games (Philly, Houston, Baltimore, ****and Tennessee*****). Similar to last season, when the Jaguars' run game fell off in the second half of the season, this issue seems to be presenting itself again in consecutive weeks. Tuten ended Sunday's matchup with 26 carries for 88 yards (3.4 YPC).

Parker Washington passed his full game, career-high in yardage in the opening half versus the Titans, while opening the day's scoring.

Washington climbed the ladder on multiple first-half catches, resulting in a dominant first half where the contract-year receiver ended the half with more receiving yards (148) than the Titans had passing yards (99).

The highlight show continued, as Parker put Titans slot cornerback Alontae Taylor on skates for another explosive second-quarter gain.

Parker's day cooled somewhat after the half, finishing with just one additional catch for four yards after halftime. His 152-yard day on seven catches led the team.

Sunday's matchup against Tennessee didn't start off as the cleanest affair for the Jacksonville Jaguars. Following an opening drive featuring a 28-yard explosive Josh Cameron gain, Jacksonville took the early lead. However a Trevor Lawrence tipped past interception set Jacksonville's defense up for a huge seesaw emotions, shifting from the down after the interception, to a high following Travis Hunters subsequent diving interception of Cam Ward.

Following the back-and-forth early turnovers, Jacksonville's mistakes allowed this one to stay closer than it felt.

With a busted coverage on defense that later resulted in a touchdown and two second quarter field goal drives by the Jacksonville offense, the Jaguars took a six-point lead into the half.

Time of possession: 18:49 to 11:11 Jacksonville

QB Trevor Lawrence ended the half 13/21 for 234 yards, while RB Bhayshul Tuten had 12 carries for 37 yards (3.1 YPC), while Parker Washington led the receiving group at 148 yards on 6 receptions and a touchdown.

Following the half, the Tennessee offense continued to find success collecting points on every series, while forcing the Jacksonville offense to answer. And while the Jacksonville offense did continue to answer each Tennessee field goal with a subsequent touchdown, the game somewhat stayed closer than most expected.

A late in the fourth quarter stop of the Titans offense on downs by Dennis Gardeck wrapped the game up, with the end tackling Tony Pollard for a loss on fourth and one. Running back Bhayshul Tuten put the final touches on the game, scoring a six-yard touchdown to confirm the 40-Burger.
