Is it time for the Saints to make a change at the top?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The New Orleans Saints have spent the better part of the last several years trying to find their footing following Sean Payton's departure, and the results are impossible to ignore.

While they have undergone multiple changes at head coach and quarterback, Mickey Loomis remains the man in charge after 25 years, leaving questions about whether the Saints need a fresh and modernized voice to lead the way at general manager.

Loomis' track record with Payton is undeniable. The two helped transform New Orleans into one of the league's most consistent contenders, including a Super Bowl and NFC South titles. However, the results since Payton left the building have painted a completely different picture.

Loomis is now 58-84 (.408) in games without Payton at the helm. That includes Dennis Allen's 18-25 record and Kellen Moore's 9-18 record since taking over in New Orleans. The Saints haven't been to the playoffs since 2020 and it's only gone downward since.

That kind of sustained disappointment raises legitimate questions about how much of the organization's success was tied to Payton rather than Loomis' ability to construct and manage a competitive roster, especially considering how Payton has turned the Denver Broncos around.

Loomis deserves credit for the success the Saints have had in the past, but eventually, previous accomplishments can't continue to outweigh present results. New Orleans has spent years attempting to return to their winning ways and simply just can't get over the hump.

With Kellen Moore's future in jeopardy, it's fair to question whether Loomis' seat should be hot as well.

If New Orleans wants to change their current trajectory, it may be time to seriously consider significant changes. That starts with the NFL's longest tenured general manager.
