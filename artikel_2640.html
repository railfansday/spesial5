Is This The Real Reason the Steelers Cut Rodgers Before Black Friday?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Pittsburgh Steelers have named Mason Rudolph as their starting quarterback for the Friday game against the Denver Broncos, according to a report. The move places Rudolph in a high-pressure role as he attempts to stabilize an offense that has lost its last two games. The Steelers enter the contest with a 6-4 record, while the Broncos arrive in Pittsburgh with an 8-2 record and six straight wins.

Rudolph has faced difficult moments throughout his nine-year career, and he views this situation as another chapter to overcome. He acknowledged that the rapid change from backup to starter creates an awkward environment for the team. Despite the chaos surrounding the quarterback room, he believes the players must focus on the game rather than the off-field drama. The game is scheduled for 3 p.m. ET at Acrisure Stadium and will be broadcast on Amazon Prime.

The last few days have been chaotic for the Pittsburgh organization. Head coach Mike McCarthy and Aaron Rodgers had a falling-out that led to the release of the four-time MVP. After being benched, Rodgers appeared on "The Pat McAfee Show" to express his frustration with the coaching staff and the team's direction. This public conflict has drawn significant attention away from the football itself.

Rudolph was not aware he would start until the night before the announcement. By the time Rudolph spoke to the media, the narrative had already shifted to the fallout between Rodgers and the coaching staff. The situation has placed the players in a unique position where they must support their coach while managing the departure of a future Hall of Famer. The team has since held a meeting to address the issues and move forward.

They now face a steep challenge with only seven games remaining in the regular season. If the season ended today, the Steelers would qualify as the seventh seed in the playoffs. The pressure is on to bounce back against a Denver team that has dominated its recent opponents. The coaching staff must now prove that they can lead the team to victory without their most high-profile player.

Mason Rudolph has encountered significant obstacles throughout his time in the NFL. The Steelers selected Oklahoma State's third-round pick in the 2018 draft. His career has included being benched for an undrafted rookie and suffering a severe injury in which his helmet was ripped off by Myles Garrett. He also spent a season with the Tennessee Titans before returning to Pittsburgh in 2025. These experiences have prepared him for the instability of the current situation.

Rudolph has spoken about the unique challenges he has faced, noting that they help him push forward. This mindset suggests that he is not easily rattled by sudden changes in his professional life. He has previously stepped in during the 2023 season to lead the team to three straight wins and a playoff berth.

In 2023, he posted a completion rate of 74.3% with 719 yards and three touchdowns. He now has the chance to replicate that performance under much different circumstances. The team looks to him for stability as the organization deals with the aftermath of Rodgers' departure. His ability to remain calm will be essential for the offense to function effectively.

The relationship between Rudolph and Rodgers was described as strong, with Rudolph calling him a first-class teammate. This bond makes the current situation even more complex for the new starter. Rudolph acknowledged that the situation puts the players in an awkward space. This quote highlights the difficulty of maintaining unity during a public breakup.

Rudolph has made it clear that he will not act as a mediator between the coach and the former quarterback. He believes that players have a job to do and must support their coach. He refused to referee the fight that occurred on national television. Instead, he focused on the team's needs and the upcoming game against Denver. This approach allows him to maintain his focus on the field rather than getting involved in off-field disputes.

The team must now navigate the locker room's emotions while preparing for a crucial matchup. Young players on the roster have not experienced a situation like this before. Rudolph sees part of his role as providing direction to these younger teammates. He wants to ensure that the team remains focused on their goals despite the distractions.

The Denver Broncos present a formidable challenge for the Steelers on Friday. They are currently leading the AFC West and have shown strong performance on both sides of the ball. The Steelers must find a way to stop the Broncos' offense, which has been scoring points consistently.

Denver's defense has struggled at times this season, ranking in the middle to lower half of the league in passing yards allowed. The Steelers' offense has a chance to move the ball if they execute their plays correctly. Rudolph will need to connect with his targets consistently to exploit these weaknesses. The previous quarterback, Aaron Rodgers, had a completion percentage of 60.9%, which was below the league average.

The Steelers will likely rely on a balanced attack to keep the defense guessing. Running back Jaylen Warren has been effective, averaging 5.53 yards per carry this season. Denver ranks among the bottom of the league in EPA per rush, which makes them vulnerable to a strong ground game. Coach McCarthy may use the running game to set up the passing attack and reduce the pressure on Rudolph. A successful game plan will require the offense to avoid the one-dimensional approach that hurt them in the last loss.

This start is an audition for Rudolph not just for the Steelers, but for every team in the league. He is in the second year of a two-year contract, and his performance will determine his future. If he plays well, he could secure a long-term role in Pittsburgh or attract interest from other teams. A poor performance could limit his options and harm his reputation as a viable starter.

The Steelers are still in a strong position to make the playoffs despite the recent turmoil. They sit just one game back of the division lead and have a favorable schedule ahead. The team needs to move past off-field drama and focus on the field. Rudolph emphasized that it is important to block the noise and support the team. He said, "Really, it's important to block the noise out and go play a football game, as cliché as it sounds, and support the black and gold."

The outcome of this game will set the tone for the final seven weeks of the season. A win against Denver would boost the team's confidence and silence critics. A loss could make the playoff push much more difficult.

Will Howard is expected to serve as his backup quarterback. The team also has rookie Drew Allar on the roster, who was selected in the third round this year. These young players provide depth for the offense in case of an injury or a struggle. The presence of multiple quarterbacks allows the coaching staff to adjust their strategy based on the game flow.

The offensive line will play a critical role in giving Rudolph time to throw. They have faced significant pressure in recent games, leading to sacks and hurried throws. If the line can hold up, Rudolph will have a better chance to make accurate passes. The running game will also need to step up to take some of the pressure off the passing attack.

The receiving corps must also step up to support the new starting quarterback. Rudolph will need his receivers to run precise routes and make catches in traffic. The team must hope that existing relationships can be leveraged quickly to deliver results.

The Steelers face a critical juncture in their season with the Friday game. The team must overcome the distraction of the quarterback controversy to focus on winning. Mason Rudolph has the experience to handle the pressure, but the situation is unique. He has been through tough times before and knows how to push forward.

Coach McCarthy will need to make smart play-calling decisions to keep the team competitive. The players must trust the system and the coaching staff to guide them through the adversity.
