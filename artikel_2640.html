Knicks pull off Toronto sweep thanks to Jalen Brunson’s late 4-point play

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

TORONTO — The Knicks channeled their inner Drake by going "Back to Back" in Toronto.

With Canada's famous rapper sitting courtside inside Scotiabank Arena, Jalen Brunson again snatched victory from the Raptors with a momentous late sequence.

The point guard pulled up for a 3-pointer with 1:25 remaining in a close game, which he buried while being flagrantly fouled because defender Ja'Kobe Walter stepped into Brunson's landing space.

The result was a 4-point play for Brunson and possession retained by the Knicks, who were fouled and quickly made two more free throws, courtesy of Landry Shamet, to basically put away their 111-102 victory.

Jalen Brunson shoots a 3-pointer over Ja'Kobe Walter en route to a 4-point play late in the fourth quarter of the Knicks' 111-102 win over the Raptors on Nov. 17, 2026, in Toronto. Dan Hamilton-Imagn Images

That six-point swing meant the Knicks left Canada with two wins in three days.

"That created the separation that we needed to be able to put them away," coach Mike Brown said. "So just a big shot. It was one of those things where the play broke down, and we kept looking for an opportunity while spacing the floor. And Jalen found one and let it fly."

Brunson crumbled to the floor because of a twisted ankle, but bounced up, walked to the bench while the refs reviewed the foul, and recovered to hit the foul shot.

"Adrenaline was pumping," he said, "and went back out there."

It followed Brunson's big trey to beat the Raptors on Sunday, reinforcing the Knicks' dominance over their division foe.

The Knicks (8-6) have won 15 straight against the Raptors (5-9), a streak dating to 2023. But Toronto entered Tuesday confident about its chances after stretching the Knicks into overtime Sunday.

Karl-Anthony Towns, who had 16 points and 16 rebounds, slams home a dunk during the Knicks' road win over the Raptors. Dan Hamilton-Imagn Images

"After rewatching and rewatching and rewatching film, we played against the NBA champions and, in my 3 ¹/₂ years here as coach, that's when we came the closest to beating them," Raptors coach Darko Rajaković said. "We're getting better. Just one free throw and the game is completely different."

He finished with 31 points in 34 minutes on 10-for-22 shooting. Karl-Anthony Towns recorded his 14th straight double-double to start the season — a franchise record — dropping 16 points with 16 rebounds.

OG Anunoby, who scored 16 points, slams home a dunk during the Knicks' road win over the Raptors. Getty Images

The Knicks closed the game with a 9-0 run, which followed their 19-0 run to end the first half.

After struggling in the clutch to start the season, the Knicks rediscovered their clutch gene in Toronto.

"A lot of times when we get in games like that, especially last year, the cardiac Knicks find a way," Towns said.

Bridges put together a third consecutive strong performance with 17 points in 30 minutes. Josh Hart scored his first points in three games, dropping five in 31 minutes. Landry Shamet continued his hot November with 12 points and was a team-best plus-26.

After a four-day stop in Canada, Brown left satisfied with a team win — solidified by an important Brunson moment.

"I thought our execution, especially down the stretch, the last few minutes of the game, was at a pretty high level," the coach said, "on both ends of the floor, but particularly offensively. Our pacing, our spacing, our screening — we took care of the basketball down the stretch.

Jalen Brunson, who scored a game-high 31 points, shoots over Alijah Martin during the Knicks' road win over the Raptors. Dan Hamilton-Imagn Images

"We got a lot of different guys that produced in a lot of different ways tonight. Obviously, KAT continues to be a double-double machine. OG [Anunoby] was our defensive player of the game. … Mikal was good for us, especially down the stretch. And Landry Shamet was huge."
