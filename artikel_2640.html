The Lakers will need help to advance in the NBA Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

In news that should shock no one, the Kings sit at the bottom of Group C in the NBA Cup this year, winless in four games.

Above them sit four teams, including the Lakers, at 2-1. Each has a path to advance as we head into Friday, the final day of NBA Cup play, setting up an exciting finish.

The Spurs and Lakers will have their anticipated showdown on Friday evening. At the same time, the Warriors and Blazers square off in Portland. All four teams need a win and results going their way in the other game to advance, setting up some good ol' scoreboard watching during the game.

So, let's look at how the Lakers can win the group and their much slimmer path to a wild card spot.

The Lakers' desire to beat the Spurs on Friday likely centers on making a statement against arguably the top team in the Western Conference. The added bonus, perhaps, is that they can advance in the NBA Cup as well.

Any path out of the group requires a win on Friday. A loss drops them to 2-2 in the group and with two teams guaranteed to finish with just one loss, the Lakers can't advance as a wild card with a loss.

But winning isn't enough. The Lakers' loss to the Warriors in Golden State — a game they led by 25 points — leaves them needing help to move on. If LA and Portland both win, the Lakers hold the head-to-head tiebreaker and win the group. However, if Golden State upsets the Blazers in Portland, then that Halloween Eve loss stings a bit more as the Warriors will top the group.

Those two-leg scenarios exist for each side. Portland must win and have the Spurs win to win the group while San Antonio needs a victory and a Warriors upset to do the same.

However, winning the group isn't the only way to advance to the knockout rounds.

Along with the three group winners, one wild card advances to the knockout rounds. For the Lakers, this only comes into play if they win and the Warriors win, leaving them with just one loss in the group.

Point differential is how the wild card spot is determined and the Lakers aren't in an ideal spot. LA's close win one week ago over the Kings didn't offset the big loss to the Warriors. As a result, they enter the final game with a -9 point differential.

The simplest way for all this to play out is for the Wolves, who may be without LaMelo Ball, to beat the Grizzlies, leaving no one-loss teams in Group B. Then the tiebreaker will be between the Lakers and the Nuggets.

Denver has a -3 point differential, but get to play the Jazz, who are winless in the NBA Cup. If both the Nuggets and Lakers win, LA would have to beat Denver's margin of victory by at least seven points, assuming Denver wins.

The potential wrench in these plans is the Grizzlies-Wolves contest. Coming into Friday, Minnesota has a +39 point differential and Memphis is at +18. If the Grizzlies win a close game, then the Wolves likely have the inside track at the wild card spot. It would take a really big Memphis win to bring the Wolves into the point differential range of the Lakers and Nuggets.

These wild card races always have so many moving parts, so here's perhaps the simplest rooting guide for Friday:

Grizzlies huge blowout win or loss at Wolves
