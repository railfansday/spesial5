Ex-Chiefs DB, Super Bowl champion works out for Steelers in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Former Kansas City Chiefs defensive back Nazeeh Johnson attended a workout with the Pittsburgh Steelers on November 17.

A member of Kansas City's Super Bowl LVII and LVIII rosters, Johnson is a battle-tested veteran who is searching for a new team after serving a six-game suspension for violating the NFL's policy against performance-enhancing substances.

Johnson started his career with the Chiefs as a seventh-round pick in the 2022 NFL Draft and played in Kansas City through the 2025 regular season. He signed with the Tennessee Titans on July 27, 2026, but was released on August 30, shortly after undergoing ankle surgery.

Pittsburgh's interest in Johnson is an indication that the former Marshall Thundering Herd standout is garnering attention as teams across the NFL prepare for a push to the playoffs in the second half of the 2026 regular season, and fans shouldn't be surprised to see the two-time champion sign with a contender in the coming weeks.
