I let AI agents build and run my T-shirt store from Slack in just 24 hours

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Most "AI agent" demos are one chatbot in one window. For the WeMakeDevs Flip the Switch hackathon I wanted to see what happens when you treat agents like a company instead: separate departments, separate permissions, people and agents in the same Slack channels, and work handed from one to another.

The result is Switch Gear, a fictional T-shirt brand from Bangalore with exactly one human: me. Everything else is an AI agent working in Slack through Switch. And the agents didn't just run the store. Most of the store was built by agents too, in a Slack channel, while I reviewed and merged.

It's a demo store: payments, printing, couriers and most orders are simulated. The agents, the Slack channels and the code are real.

Repo: https://github.com/adybose/switch-gear-hq
