XRP’s new euro cash-out route had less than €100 in its direct market

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

A November 8 snapshot found just 96.80 EURGH in the direct XRP market.

XRP holders seeking euros through GateHub's newly announced EURGH route faced just 96.80 euro tokens in the direct XRP/EURGH order book at a Nov. 8 ledger snapshot.

Wallet platform GateHub announced EURGH with Dinaro, the token's electronic-money issuer, on Nov. 5, saying its euro token on the XRP Ledger and Xahau could be redeemed through SEPA bank payments, subject to legal and verification requirements. For someone starting with XRP, however, obtaining that euro token comes before redeeming it. The measured public market offered little capacity for that first step.

At the validated XRPL ledger that closed Nov. 8 at 13:29:31 UTC, the direct order book returned one offer supplying 96.80 EURGH in exchange for 484 XRP. Its owner's available funds covered the token amount.

That works out to 0.20 EURGH per XRP before additional costs. Even treating each EURGH as one euro, as the issuer's stated redemption terms envisage, the entire displayed token inventory was worth less than €100.

The price was also far from the separate euro market. Kraken's XRP/EUR ticker, captured at 13:27:50 UTC, showed a best bid of €1.04544 per XRP. The observations were 101 seconds apart and covered different venues, before trading, transfer, redemption or banking costs. They were indicative quotes, not a guaranteed cash-out comparison. XRP's broader market was near $1.17 and down 6.70% over seven days in the Nov. 8 afternoon snapshot.

Still, the difference illustrates why a bank connection alone does not establish a competitive exit for XRP. The direct offer asked a seller to accept 0.20 EURGH for each XRP while a separate euro venue displayed a much higher bid. A token's redemption value and the exchange price available to acquire it are separate matters.

The issuer's outstanding XRPL obligations totaled 55,370.62 EURGH at the same ledger. That balance measures issued tokens, not XRP-buying liquidity. Xahau balances are outside this XRPL reading.

There was also no XRP/EURGH automated market maker pool at that ledger. Such a pool would be another place to look for conversion liquidity alongside the direct offers.

A request to convert XRP into 1,000 EURGH returned no route for the tested accounts. The sending account had sufficient XRP, and the receiving account already had room to hold more EURGH on its trust line, the ledger relationship needed to hold that issuer's token.

This was an account-specific routing result, not proof that every possible conversion was unavailable. XRPL's pathfinding documentation cautions that results are not guaranteed to find the best route. The resting offer also describes available terms, rather than a completed trade.

Hosted execution needs its own quote. GateHub's Exchange documentation describes market-based rates and immediate execution or cancellation; its Trade guide says Exchange calculates from current market orders. Those interfaces do not, by themselves, demonstrate a separate reserve of EURGH liquidity or a larger executable quote.

GateHub's Xaman on/off-ramp support also describes SEPA and SWIFT connectivity, with a verified GateHub account required. The measured EURGH constraint therefore cannot be extended to all GateHub bank-transfer channels.

GateHub says users can deposit euros by bank transfer and receive EURGH in their selected wallet. Dinaro is the issuer behind that token; GateHub describes its redemption into SEPA payments, subject to legal and verification requirements. Dinaro appears in Banka Slovenije's electronic-money institution register; that institutional entry does not separately identify EURGH.

The announced deposit flow does not itself require a large XRP trading position. XRP remains relevant for account reserves and transaction fees, while traders may choose to hold it as conversion inventory.

XRPL's auto-bridging mechanism can route between two issued tokens through XRP when suitable offers improve the exchange rate. It cannot create the missing inventory, and its existence does not demonstrate lasting demand to hold XRP.

For this euro route to become a more useful exit for XRP holders, the next evidence would be competitive funded quotes, successful larger conversions and attributable production usage. The Nov. 8 public snapshot exposed the conversion hurdle; GateHub's banking announcement addresses the redemption step after it.
