The XRP rich list shows that smaller holders continued accumulating XRP even as the price dropped below $1. Wallets holding between 10,000 and 100,000 XRP added around 504 million coins over the past year as per XRPScan data.

The number of wallets holding at least 10,000 XRP reached 343,897 on November 14, 2026, up around 5% since February, based on Santiment data.

However, the total XRP held by these wallets remained almost unchanged at 65.5 billion. This suggests that some existing holders may have spread their coins across multiple wallets rather than making new purchases.

The growth in the XRP rich list is mainly coming from wallets holding between 10,000 and 100,000 XRP.

This group added nearly 25,000 accounts over the past year. Its total holdings also increased by around 504 million XRP. During the November price decline, these wallets added another 39 million XRP.

The trend is different among holders with 100,000 to 1 million XRP. Their wallet count declined slightly from 30,500 in November 2025 to 30,300 on November 14, 2026.

This indicates that smaller holders are adding XRP, while the mid-sized holder group has remained largely unchanged.

Between November 6 and November 12, around 440 million XRP moved into three new or nearly empty wallets.

None of the receiving wallets had an exchange label on XRPScan. However, their owners remain unknown.

The transfers could represent existing holders moving their coins, a custodian managing assets for clients or a fund reorganizing its holdings. Therefore, the movement does not confirm fresh buying.

Exchange and company wallets account for a large share of the XRP rich list.

According to analyst cheeky crypto, at least half of the XRP held in wallets containing 10,000 coins or more belongs to accounts labeled as exchanges, companies or known individuals. Exchanges and companies alone account for more than 40%.

The concentration is higher among the largest wallets holding at least 100 million XRP, where around 84% of the coins are linked to exchanges and insiders, including Upbit, Coinbase, Binance and Ripple.

By comparison, only around 5% of the holdings in the 100,000 to 1 million XRP category are linked to labeled entities.

This difference is important because exchange wallets can hold XRP for many customers. Their balances do not necessarily represent the investments of individual whales.

Between November 4 and November 15, labeled exchange and company wallets outside Ripple added approximately 18 million XRP to their combined holdings.

During the same period, Binance-labeled wallets decreased by around 126 million XRP, while Upbit and Coinbase each added approximately 50 million XRP.

The figures suggest that coins moved between exchanges rather than showing a major overall increase in exchange holdings.

Previous market declines show that XRP has recovered after periods of increasing wallet numbers, but the trend does not guarantee a price rise.

During the April 2025 sell-off, XRP gained around 30% within 30 days of reaching its low while the number of wallets holding at least 10,000 XRP continued to increase.

A similar pattern occurred in August 2026. XRP recovered approximately 29% in the month after reaching a low of around $0.99 on August 16.

However, the token later lost those gains and declined further.

In November 2024, XRP gained around 174% in a month even though the 10,000 XRP wallet count remained relatively flat.

These examples show that changes in wallet numbers alone cannot explain or predict XRP price movements.
