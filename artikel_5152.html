Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse expects Bitcoin and XRP to recover after Anthropic’s massive IPO debut.

Garlinghouse says liquidity has shifted from crypto markets into Anthropic, similar to SpaceX’s IPO.

Bitcoin could reach nearly $96,063 if it repeats its post-SpaceX rally from current levels.

Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse believes the crypto market could see a strong recovery after Anthropic’s November 19 IPO, similar to Bitcoin’s 51% rally following SpaceX’s market debut.

His comments come as Bitcoin has fallen nearly 24% to a low of $62K since Anthropic filed for its IPO.

Speaking to CNBC on November 19, Garlinghouse said the recent crypto sell-off is closely linked to money moving into Anthropic’s IPO. He pointed to a similar pattern before SpaceX’s market debut on 12 June.

“People are taking liquidity out of a very liquid market in crypto, including Bitcoin, including XRP, and moving it into SpaceX.”

He added that the same thing happened ahead of the Anthropic IPO and expects crypto prices to recover once the listing happens.

At the time of SpaceX’s IPO, Bitcoin dropped roughly 24% before the company’s June 12 debut, falling from above $77,000 to around $59,000. At the debut, SpaceX then raised a record $85.7 billion through its IPO.

After a month, Bitcoin and the wider crypto market began to see a strong recovery. BTC moved from a low of around $57,717 to $87,249 by September 22, marking a gain of about 51.17%.

Anthropic’s IPO has created a similar liquidity squeeze. The company is raising around $81.3 billion by selling 980 million Class A shares at $83 each, while its total capital raise, including a private placement, reaches about $107.3 billion.

Anthropic is scheduled to begin trading on Nasdaq today, November 19, under the ticker ANT.

Since the company filed its Form S-1 on October 26, Bitcoin has dropped about 24%, falling from roughly $81,829 to around $62000. The broader crypto market also lost about $330 billion during the sell-off.

If Bitcoin repeats the 51.17% post-SpaceX move from its current level, BTC could climb toward $96,063.

XRP could also see a similar recovery if Garlinghouse’s expected post-IPO rally plays out. XRP is currently trading near $0.98, meaning a 51.17% gain, similar to Bitcoin’s rally after the SpaceX IPO announcement, could push its price to around $1.48.

For now, the Anthropic IPO could test whether fresh money returns to the crypto market after the recent sell-off.
