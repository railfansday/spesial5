Our semi-regular weekly survey this week is very heavy on people who want to build things of which many other people want no part.

We begin in the Commonwealth. (God save it!) South from Worcester on Route 146 lies the charming town of Sutton. Back in the day, Sutton’s Pleasant Valley Country Club was a regular stop on the PGA and LPGA Tours. (The last PGA event at the course was in 1998.) There also was a drive-in that showed films of ... ahem ... a sophisticated nature. Anyway, I used to drive through Sutton on my way to my job in the state forest. Route 146 got progressively more rural the further you got from Worcester. Now, Bob Kraft, the owner of the New England Patriots, would like to drop a data center on the town. From CBS News:

The idea of dropping a power-draining, water-gulping ten-building campus on a place as small as Sutton is exactly why data centers have become the hot-button local issue all over the country. It is sheer oligarchical bullying over which the country has raised its hackles. This would be the fourth-largest data center in the United States. And, as WGBH points out, some sports oligarchs are leading the parade on these projects, which is reason enough to give every one of these projects a serious side-eye.

It’s good to be rich in this country. You may not have noticed.
