Taylor Swift and Karlie Kloss Are Hanging Out Again—And Fans Think Taylor Soft-Launched It With Easter Eggs

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

An investigation for those still at the restaurant.

In case you needed a sign that the blue wave is blue waving after the midterm elections’ Democratic sweep, Taylor Swift and Karlie Kloss are hanging out again like it’s 2016.

The pair were spotted in a box together during the Kansas City Chiefs game against the New York Jets on Sunday, November 8, and based on their smiles and hugs, it seems like no time has passed between them.

Naturally, longtime Swifties lost any ability to chill when it came to their reunion—especially since they had seemingly kept their distance for a few years.

“When they said woke 3.0 was coming, I didn’t think that would mean seeing Taylor Swift and Karlie Kloss together again,” one person wrote, while another added, “That wasn’t on my 2026 bingo card.”

This also means that Swifties are back to theorizing about Taylor and Karlie’s connection. Some even suggested the Grammy winner soft-launched their game-day hangout with a series of Easter eggs while talking about her Oscar contender for Best Original Song, “I Knew It, I Knew You.”

“‘I Knew It, I Knew You’ is about Karlie and I’ll die on that hill,” one fan theorized, as another asked, “Wondering if Taylor wrote ‘I Knew It, I Knew You’ about rekindling her friendship with Karlie...?” alongside a clip of Taylor discussing the song during the Toy Story 5 premiere last summer.

“I think when people hear the song ‘I Knew It, I Knew You’ at the end of Toy Story 5, I think it would be really awesome if they thought of somebody that they had amazing memories with that they haven’t seen in a long time, or someone that they miss, knowing that that person could come back into their life,” Taylor explained during the film’s premiere.

She added, “Or just the feeling that, like, sometimes people’s paths diverge and that doesn’t mean that that’s the last time you get to feel those memories or know that person. Sometimes when you look back on your childhood, those memories, that’s special in itself.”

In case you need a refresher on why the internet is losing its mind over a 2026 Kaylor reunion, the girls became inseparable after they hit it off in 2012, from walking the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show to gracing the cover of Vogue together.

Fans eventually speculated that Taylor and Karlie had a falling out after the model married venture capitalist Josh Kushner in 2018, though it had never been confirmed. (Josh’s brother, Jared, married President Donald Trump’s daughter, Ivanka, in 2009.)

However, Karlie and Josh both attended Taylor’s wedding to Travis in July, so…it seems like there hasn’t been any bad blood for a minute.
