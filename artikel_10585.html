Reddit testing new feature that puts ads in search results

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Reddit has announced that it’s testing new advertisements in its search results, meaning users could start seeing sponsored products related to whatever they’re looking for.

In a November 3 announcement, Reddit confirmed that it has started experimenting with additional advertising placements on its search pages.

Rather than showing the same promotions to everyone, the company says the ads can be influenced by what individual users are searching for.

“Ads in search may appear based on a range of factors, including contextual relevance to a user’s search query,” the statement reads.

They used the example that someone looking for advice on building a home office could encounter advertisements for desks, monitors, or office chairs. Likewise, searching for ways to set up a coffee station might bring up promotions for coffee machines, beans, and mugs.

Reddit explained that the idea is to reach users who are already researching particular products or considering purchases. However, the company isn’t rolling out the new placements to every advertiser immediately.

“This is an initial test with a select group of eligible advertisers,” Reddit confirmed. “During this test, we will evaluate and understand how users respond to these ads so we can refine the experience for users and advertisers over time.”

It’s worth noting that Reddit already features advertising across its platform, so this isn’t the introduction of ads to the site for the first time.

Instead, the experiment focuses on additional ads while searching, potentially making sponsored products more prominent when users are looking for posts or communities.
