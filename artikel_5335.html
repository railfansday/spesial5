Bitcoin ETF Loses $570M: More Than Half of BTC Supply Falls Out of Profit

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The CryptoNews editorial team is composed of seasoned writers specializing in cryptocurrency and blockchain technology. Their expertise ensures comprehensive, accurate, and insightful content for...

Bitcoin sell-off is starting to hurt investors who bought well before the price crash. U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs shed $569.7 million yesterday, while on-chain data showed only 47.3% of BTC supply remaining in profit.

BlackRock’s IBIT accounted for $509.3 million of Monday’s withdrawals, dwarfing flows across the rest of the ETF market. Fidelity’s FBTC bucked the trend with $13.3 million in inflows, but that was nowhere near enough to offset the selling.

Bitcoin has struggled to turn its latest price rebound into a lasting recovery. Buyers briefly pushed BTC above $64,000, but then reversed the gain, sending Bitcoin back below $60,000.

BlackRock’s IBIT accounted for $509.3 million of Monday’s $569.7 million Bitcoin ETF outflow. The fund’s withdrawals alone contributed almost 90% of the day’s total, making it the biggest driver of the sell-off.

Fidelity’s FBTC was the lone fund to post an inflow, attracting $13.3 million. The remaining products either recorded no flows or saw smaller withdrawals, leaving IBIT firmly at the center of Monday’s exodus.

Glassnode’s supply-in-profit metric fell to 46%, down from 54.2% one week earlier. The shift means most circulating Bitcoin is now held outside the profit category, marking a sharp deterioration in market profitability.

The change is big and maybe bad at the same time because it alters how investors may react to another drop. Holders who were comfortably sitting on gains can wait out volatility. Those who have watched their positions turn red may instead use the next rebound to reduce exposure.

Short-term holders are facing difficult conditions, with net unrealized profit and loss reportedly dropping to negative 18.3% as pressure mounts on coins acquired more recently. Realized losses have also climbed as investors sell below their acquisition prices.

It does leave the market more vulnerable to disappointment. A weak rebound could convince some holders that waiting for a full recovery is no longer worth the risk.

Bitcoin Hyper is heading into its final two days before launch, and the presale is still offering $HYPER at just $0.0136. With more than $33.2 million raised and staking rewards of 35% APY, the project is attracting attention before its November 26 token launch.

The timing is what makes this interesting. Buyers are entering before named centralized exchange listings are disclosed and before broader market exposure could put Bitcoin Hyper in front of a much larger audience. At just 1.37 cents per token, $HYPER remains priced at its presale stage, with launch day rapidly approaching.

Bitcoin Hyper is also pitching more than another speculative token. Its Layer 2 aims to connect Bitcoin with Solana’s high-speed SVM execution environment, opening the door to faster transactions, decentralized exchanges, staking, and other DeFi applications. BTC could move beyond simply holding value and become usable across a wider ecosystem.

The project is trying to bring Bitcoin liquidity into a new infrastructure layer. With the countdown nearly finished, the window to enter at presale pricing is getting increasingly tight.

The Bitcoin price needs to reclaim the $62,000–$66,000 area to give the recovery some credibility. That zone could present a difficult hurdle after the recent breakdown, particularly if ETF demand remains weak.

A sustained move above the range would put $74,100 back on the radar, near the reported average cost basis for short-term holders. Reaching that level would offer recent buyers an opportunity to exit closer to breakeven, and could create fresh selling pressure.

On the downside, losing $58,500 would leave June’s low exposed. A deeper decline could bring Bitcoin’s realized price at $53,700 into focus, a level that could become important if losses continue spreading.

Bitcoin price needs fresh demand to turn these battered positions into a recovery.
