Doug Bowser 'Apologises' To The "Haters" Of His Icky AI-Generated Thanksgiving Post

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Happy belated Thanksgiving to all of our friends across the pond! As you all wake up to tummies full of turkey, you'll be pleased to see that former Nintendo of America President Doug Bowser has wished everybody a good one, too. The only difference is, he did it with a grim AI-generated post.

Oh, brother. Bowser shared the image yesterday, showing him sitting behind a Thanksgiving spread with Bowser and Donkey Kong posing next to him.

Only, that's not Bowser and DK, but an AI rendition of the pair. Bowser's cloak doesn't make sense, his crown defies the laws of physics, there's a mushed-up hand on Doug's shoulder, and the less said about DK's tie, the better. C'mon, man.

Bowser wasn't quite tone deaf enough to keep the image up for long, taking it down after receiving several comments expressing their disappointment that the former NoA President (who, we're sure, has a plethora of human-edited pictures of him and Bowser/DK at his disposal) would stoop to sharing something like this.

Oh hey Doug Bowser made a Thanksgiving po- Oh…

But Bowser just couldn't let it lie. After deleting the post, he shared the following message on X, 'apologising' to the "haters" of the image:

Apologies if I offended people with my AI generated post. It was sent to me by a friend and it made me smile. For the haters out there the message remains the same - I wish you a Happy Thanksgiving.

Ugh. A genuine apology would have been fine, Doug. In fact, just deleting the post and sharing something human-made instead would have sufficed. Throwing in a little "haters" line pushes the whole thing to another level of out-of-touch.

You'll be pleased to hear that there were no AI posts from the current NoA President, Devon Pritchard. Hey, as a palate cleanser, here's the post that Nintendo of America shared on its socials to mark Thanksgiving. Rest assured, it's far more wholesome.
