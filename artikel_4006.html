'Genital Exposure Has Never Appeared This Frequently': GTA 6 Was Almost Refused Release in South Korea

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

GTA 6 was, reportedly, very close to being refused classification in South Korea just a couple of months back. The reason? Too many penises.

According to Inven's findings, the country's ratings board — the GCRB — was completely split on its initial classification vote. Three committee members voted for an 'Adults Only' rating, and another three voted to have Rockstar's game refused outright.

The report claims that the "frequent background appearance of male genital nudity without any sexual context" was the big sticking point.

In other words, there are a lot of instances in GTA 6 where male genitalia is on full display — even when there's no sexual context attached. The ratings board found this to be "excessive" in comparison to other games where genitalia may appear.

"It is not that genital exposure is completely forbidden, but it has never appeared this frequently without context," reads one of the committee member's statements.

It continues: "I do not believe genital exposure to this extent falls within acceptable limits under the current [ratings] standard."

However, the opposition's argument attempts to bring context into the equation: "In this game, scenes with heavy exposure are not particularly sexual. Conversely, in sexual scenes, exposure is limited. We must judge the content holistically."

In the end, following multiple postponements and even some regulation adjustments, a second vote was cast and GTA 6 was handed an 'Adults Only' rating. This time around, only one member voted for refusal, and eight pushed for Adults Only — so the argument for the latter was clearly quite convincing.

Apparently, a fear of simply missing out on GTA 6 also factored into the decision. There was an unwanted "pressure" in being the only country to potentially ban the game from sale.

Adding a bit of extra spice to this story, Korean outlet Gameple is now reporting that South Korea may be the only East Asian country where GTA 6 is not censored in some form.

There's evidence to suggest that Japan's version will be "modified" in order to adhere to region's often strict classification regulations.

Meanwhile, in mainland China, it's looking likely that GTA 6 won't be eligible for government approval, due to the crime-filled, violent, and sexual nature of its content.
