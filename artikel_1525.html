Another huge European big name dreams of Michael Olise!

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

There are players whose talent always ends up attracting the attention of the biggest European clubs and Michael Olise now belongs to this category. Sparkling in the colors of Bayern Munich, the French international (31 caps, 12 goals) saw his popularity rise to the point of arousing the interest of several big names on the continent. For several months, Real Madrid has presented itself as one of the main favorites to snatch the London native from his current Bavarian ties. But that’s not all. This Friday evening, another candidate appears. According to the latest information from Sports BildArsenal would also particularly appreciate the profile of the French winger and would be among the clubs that would like to be able to attract him.

An interest which says a lot about the status taken by the former Crystal Palace player, who has become one of the attractions of European football. In London, Mikel Arteta could see Olise as a piece of choice to enrich his offensive sector. And for good reason. His creativity, his technical quality and his ability to change a match in a few gestures are enough to attract a club which has great ambitions. But Arsenal are far from alone in this race for stars. Besides Real Madrid, other major teams have also put his name on their lists. For Christian Falk, if Olise were to one day leave Munich, a departure for Spain could even represent a logical destination, after his already successful experience in the Premier League.

The fact remains that Bayern Munich has no desire to see its jewel escape. The Bavarian club has not set a price of 300 million euros, contrary to certain information relayed in Spain, nor has it made an extension offer that the player would have refused. Indeed, Munich management intends to keep Olise and is giving itself time to prepare for the future. Under contract for another three seasons, the Frenchman could be the subject of discussions around a new lease from next year, with summer as the preferred horizon. The message sent to suitors is therefore clear: Bayern is not a seller.

You will have understood, Arsenal can look at Olise with envy, but the path which leads to him looks particularly steep. In Munich, the club is even convinced of being able to offer him an environment that matches his ambitions, with the possibility of winning major titles and shining until he wins the greatest individual distinctions, starting with the Ballon d’Or. One thing is certain, given the various outings of the Munich leaders, Bayern wants to make the Allianz Arena the theater of the next stage of their career, and not a simple crossing point. The Gunners are warned: to hope to attract the French international, it will take much more than strong interest. It will be necessary to convince a club which intends to keep its artist.
