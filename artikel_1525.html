Comparing Cristiano Ronaldo and Lionel Messi’s stats in 2026 so far: Goals, assists, penalties…

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The year 2026 might just be the last dance for the rivalry between Cristiano Ronaldo and Lionel Messi, who are both near the end of their careers.

Which of the two era-defining icons is performing better during this calendar year?

Messi has continued to lift silverware after leaving European football back in 2023. Trophies haven’t been quite as forthcoming for Ronaldo since he joined Saudi Pro League outfit Al-Nassr, although he’s won the Saudi Pro League for the first time.

He continued to score goals, including two against Uzbekistan and his Croatia penalty, but was unable to stop Portugal from losing in the World Cup Round of 16. And now his international career is up in smoke.

Messi and Ronaldo have been at every World Cup going back to the 2006 tournament in Germany.

Messi didn’t get started until February. The MLS schedule is more like the calendar year, meaning that he started 2026 with a well-earned rest after his talismanic role in Inter Miami’s 2025 MLS Cup triumph.

Inter Miami didn’t make an especially convincing start to the new season, though. Messi’s old pals Jordi Alba and Sergio Busquets retired at the end of last season, while Javier Mascherano left his post as manager amid question marks over making this new-look team work.

Notably, Messi didn’t register a single assist in his first 13 appearances of 2026. But he continued to score goals.

Recent months, though, have seen Messi look like his old self. He approached the World Cup in inspired form and took that momentum into the World Cup, scoring eight goals and providing four assists to get Argentina to another final.

Now the two of them have turned their attention back to the club game. Messi has been in exceptional form for Inter Miami, which is now his sole focus after retiring from international football.

He scored braces against DC United and Atlanta United earlier in October, although Miami have since suffered a setback against New York Red Bulls and drawn with Cincinnati.

Ronaldo, meanwhile, has rediscovered his goalscoring touch after a relatively quiet spell at the start of the 2026-27 Saudi Pro League campaign. His latest hat-trick came in Al Nassr’s 5-3 victory over Al Kholood, taking his extraordinary career tally to 985 goals – just 15 away from the famous 1000 career goal milestone.

Messi has comfortably outperformed Ronaldo in front of goal in 2026, scoring 41 goals and notching 21 assists in 48 games. Ronaldo, meanwhile, has 29 goals and just one assist from 43 appearances.

That gives Messi a goal every 99 minutes and a goal contribution every 65 minutes, compared with Ronaldo’s 121 and 117, respectively. The Argentinian has been considerably more productive when it comes to goals and assists than his old foe.

Ronaldo’s recent hat-trick suggests he’s finding his scoring touch again, and Al Nassr’s improved form is encouraging as they look to defend their Saudi Pro League title. But Messi remains comfortably ahead in their individual numbers for 2026.
