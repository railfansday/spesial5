A good credit card for international travel should do more than just eliminate foreign transaction fees.

Depending on the card, you could earn bonus rewards on everything from meals abroad to hotel stays, save on flights or enjoy perks like airport lounge access and hotel elite status.

While premium cards can offer plenty of value for frequent international travelers, you don't need to pay a hefty annual fee to get worthwhile benefits. A card that earns solid rewards on your purchases abroad and doesn't charge foreign transaction fees may be all you need.
