Steelers vs. Broncos Week 12 picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Pittsburgh Steelers are coming off the one of the most embarrassing weeks in franchise history. But, the NFL season stops for no one, as Pittsburgh will host the Denver Broncos. The Steelers have yet to lose a home game this season, while the Broncos have won six straight. Who will come out on top? Our staff weighs in.

Mike Nicastro: Sometimes, a quarterback change can provide a spark that a team desperately needs. I think Rudolph will embrace the opportunity, play with confidence and give his teammates something to rally behind. I don't expect it to be pretty, and I certainly don't think many people will see this result coming. But I have a feeling this Steelers team will come together when it matters most. Steelers 23, Broncos 20.

Ryan Parish: Hard to believe the team will be focused and prepared with all of the dramatics of the last few days. Then of course, they haven't looked prepared on gamedays the past few weeks. It's too bad, Denver seems like a winnable game, and there is talent on this team despite recent performances. For head coach Mike McCarthy's sake, he'd better hope the Steelers rally together and show a spark. The Rooneys are a patient family, and they seem to be supporting the coach publicly so far, but if this season falls into complete disarray and McCarthy loses the locker room, his time on the job might be shorter than we could have imagined. I have a hard time being optimistic about this week. Broncos 23, Steelers 16.

Ryland Bickley: Denver is a good but not elite team, and if this was a normal season for the Steelers, I'd think they'd have a good chance at winning this one in Pittsburgh. But on a short week, after two demoralizing losses, and following Aaron Rodgers' highly-publicized, dramatic exit, it's hard to be optimistic about the Steelers' chances against the 8-2 Broncos. Even if the team is more focused on the game than we expect, suddenly losing a starting quarterback just two days before a game would make any team's game preparation chaotic. Pittsburgh isn't helpless in this one, but the Denver defense is a tough opponent and Sean Payton should have some success scheming against the Steelers' defense on the other side of the ball. Broncos 30, Steelers 20.

Joey Bray: After one of the most unbelievable weeks in franchise history, the Pittsburgh Steelers have the tall task of taking on the AFC West-leading Broncos on Black Friday. I have a bad feeling that all of this off the field banter will leak onto the field. Consider me a skeptic of the idea that Mason Rudolph can provide this major spark to the offense that Mike McCarthy is hoping he can provide. Sure, Rudolph will probably be more open to play action and be able to move a bit better, but this is a team that has problems offensively well beyond that in their struggling running game. The Steelers defense will probably look about the same, but the strong rushing attack of Denver will eventually wear them down as their offense will once again struggle to find consistency. I'll take the Broncos to dominate a game that will have many questioning the direction of the Steelers franchise. Broncos 28, Steelers 6.

Jarrett Bailey: The Steelers are going to win. Because they have to. After spending all week as the laughing stock of the NFL and being ridiculed, their terrific defense keeps the Broncos' offense from scoring more than one touchdown, and they eek out a low-scoring win under the leadership of Mason Rudolph at quarterback. Steelers 20, Broncos 13.
