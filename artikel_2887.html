Week 11 Power Rankings Round Up: Packers come back down to earth

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Well, Green Bay's matchup against Minnesota went about as badly as it could have, as the Packers fell 30-10. As it turns out, relying on Jordan Love to play at superhuman levels has its drawbacks. Love has answered the call at every turn and played admirably, all things considered, in Week 10, but it turns out that football is a team game, and offensive line play is an important part of that team.

As doom and gloom as Sunday was, there were some positives. J. Michael Sturdivant caught his first career touchdown, Kaleb Johnson showed some flashes when he had time to run, and Jordan Love finally drew a roughing the passer penalty, resulting in probably the loudest cheer of the day aside from the pregame flyover.

It really has been a drastic swing of emotions for the green and gold faithful. There has been some really ugly football, some incredibly good football as we saw in New England, and then a swing back to really bad in Week 10. As much as people blame the offensive line, it truly was a team effort, or lack thereof. Green Bay's defense gave up a touchdown on a 3rd and 23, Trey Smack missed another field goal, the line did let up 8 sacks, and the team coughed up 3 fumbles to go with a Jordan Love interception.

In last week's various Power Rankings, many had the Packers inside the Top 10, with Sports Illustrated having the team as high as the 5 spot in its rankings. One week later, Green Bay still remains in the top half of the league, perhaps as a testament to the strength of the Vikings. Or perhaps it is due to the Packers still finding themselves a playoff team, a true reminder that one game isn't the be-all and end-all in the grand scheme of things.

Thankfully, Green Bay will not lose this week, as the bye week is finally here. Every year, it seems like the bye week comes at the perfect time, but this year, that really rings true. It will be the shortest bye week in the NFL, as both the Rams and Packers will come off their respective bye weeks to play on Thanksgiving Eve a week from Wednesday. Josh Jacobs could be back, Micah Parsons should be back, and Kevin Zeitler will have another week to heal up and ramp up. So with the bye week here, let's see where Green Bay stands around the league:

NFL.com's Nick Shook has the Packers as the highest mark on the collective lists this week, keeping Green Bay at the 10th spot, dropping 2 spots from the 8th spot. Shook writes:

"We're now entering the edge of the quagmire. Green Bay's reliance on the explosive play became a weakness against Minnesota, as a lack of sustainable success contributed to its 20-point loss to the division rival. One might simply say the Vikings have the Packers' number; I tend to think Minnesota merely exposed a weakness that might hold back Green Bay as it fights to remain in the playoff hunt in the toughest division in football. That's not enough to knock the Pack out of the top 10, but it does shake my confidence in them."

Suffice to say, Green Bay is still viewed relatively favorably around the league. Despite falling 7 spots in Conor Orr's rankings on Sports Illustrated, the Packers still hold the 12 spot in his metrics. So it certainly could be worse. Week 11 will kick off in a couple of days, and with Green Bay sitting idle on the bye week this week, they do not control whether they rise or fall. Enjoy the week of stress-free football!
