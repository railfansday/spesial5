Pro Shop’s Chad Mumm on Skins Game Return To Prime Video

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

“The Farmers Insurance Skins Game” returns to Prime video this Friday for a second year in a row. This year’s event features PGA tour veterans and rookies vying for chance to win $4 million at the Atlantic Field Clubs in Florida. Chad Mumm, Co-Founder and President of the golf media and commerce company Pro Shop, joined Bloomberg’s Romaine Bostick to discuss the revival of the iconic game and what’s different this year. (Source: Bloomberg)
