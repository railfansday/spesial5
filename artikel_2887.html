The Daily: Why Red Wings’ Next Four Games Are Critical; Augustine Delivering in GR

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Detroit Red Wings (7-7-3) start a four-game road trip tonight against Colorado Avalanche and it feels these games represent a pivotal stretch for team.

"We can't shoot ourselves in the foot in these games if we put ourselves in a good position to win," Red Wings coach Todd McLellan said. "We've got to find ways to close it out, which we've done in Florida, done in Edmonton against some really good teams."

But the truth is every game is critical for the Red Wings as they continue to establish a new identity without Dylan Larkin, with younger player taking on key roles, with uncertainty surrounding Simon Edvinsson and with Shawn Horcoff in command as interim general manager.

The Red Wings have been competitive in the early going, but with three losses in the last four games, they don't want any of last season's bad habits creeping into their game. If they can get through these net four games with modest success, they will be in a position to take advantage of a more favorable schedule. They will only play five more road games among the next 16 games before Christmas.

The goal is to enter that stretch feeling good about how they are playing.

"We have to get off to good starts," McLellan said. "We need some production again from key people. When they get their looks and touches they are going to have to produce. Our penalty kill has been improving. It's going to have to stay at that level and our power play is going to have to get us something."
