49ers activate Demarcus Robinson, elevate KhaDarel Hodge

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The 49ers will have two of their Week 1 wideouts back in the lineup for Sunday's game against the Raiders.

Demarcus Robinson has been activated from injured reserve and KhaDarel Hodge has been elevated from the practice squad. Wide receiver Christian Kirk went on injured reserve in a corresponding move.

Robinson caught a touchdown in that Week 1 win over the Rams, but has been out since injuring his ankle the next week. Hodge played in the first three games of the season as a practice squad elevation, but was released in October. He returned to the team this week.

Kirk injured his knee in practice this week. He opened the season on injured reserve and made his 2026 debut in the 49ers' Week 7 loss to the Falcons.
