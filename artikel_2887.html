The Wizards are starting to free-fall in the power rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Washington Wizards are 4-11 in the standings and are now 12th in the Eastern Confernece. On the bright side, it's better than the last place Milwaukee Bucks who are 1-13. But really, are the Wizards content with being 4-11 when AJ Dybantsa, Anthony Davis and Trae Young are starting together?

Either way, the power rankings are starting to turn on the Wizards, bringing them back toward their all-too-familiar place at the doldrums. This week's rankings by John Schuhman of NBA.com were released yesterday, where Washington is now 23rd, down from 19th last week.

Law Murray of The Athletic was less kind, with Washington now No. 26, down from No. 20 in his most recent power rankings.

Power rankings aren't the whole story. But it's pretty clear the pundits don't have faith in this roster, given their recent performance.
