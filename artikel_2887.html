Eagles will be without top defensive star vs. Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Pittsburgh Steelers will travel to Philadelphia to face the Eagles in Week 11. The Steelers have six players that are questionable for Sunday, including edge rusher Nick Herbig, linebacker Patrick Queen, and receivers Michael Pittman and Roman Wilson. On the other side, though, the Eagles have ruled out one of their top defenders.

All-Pro linebacker Zack Baun has been ruled out for Week 11 with a back injury, the team announced. Additionally, wide receiver Dontayvion Wicks will be out with an ankle injury. However, Philadelphia will get wide receiver DeVonta Smith back.

The Eagles will be starting quarterback Joe Milton for the second straight game. They lost in Week 9 19-11 to the New York Giants with Milton under center. He went 18-of-34 for 169 yards and added 64 yards on the ground.
