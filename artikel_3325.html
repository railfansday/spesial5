Political Ad Spending On 2025-26 Cycle Topped $12 Billion, A Boon To Broadcasters, Streaming And Digital

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Political ad spending on the 2025-26 election cycle topped more than $12 billion, a particular boon to broadcasters, streamers and digital outlets.

The figures for the 2025-26 cycle are from a new report from analysis firm AdImpact.

Per the firm, this was the first midterm election to outspend a presidential cycle, with spending 8% above 2024, when spending was $11.2 billion. It also was 35% about the 2022 midterm and three times the 2018 cycle.

Broadcast saw $5.85 billion in political ad revenue, up 9% from 2024, while its share actually grew from 48% to 49%. Streaming, or connected TV, grew by 14% to $2.66 billion and digital rose by 11% to $1.91 billion. Spending on cable declined by 7%, radio dropped by 13% and satellite fell by 7%. Spending doesn’t guarantee wins: Republicans outspent Democrats, $5.51 billion to $5.11 billion, according to AdImpact. GOP spending rose by 27% while Democrats saw their spending fall by 4% compared to 2024. Per The New York Times, Elon Musk spent $474 million during the cycle, albeit not all of that figure was for advertising. Watch on Deadline

All that said, Republicans saw their biggest trouncing since 1974, with Democrats winning back control of the two chambers of Congress, among other gains.

October spending was $4.10 billion, a monthly record, and even earlier months saw huge sums. Almost $700 million was spent on ads in May, as primaries were in full swing, and the off year elections in 2025 saw $1.86 billion spent. That was a 32% growth over 2023.

Records were broken in spending on Senate races, with $3.39 billion, the House, with $2.44 billion and gubernatorial races, at $2.61 billion.

A Supreme Court decision allowed for coordinated buys by candidates and party committees, and that reached a record $258 million from September 1 through Election Day, up 62% from 2024. The cycle continued to see huge spending by SuperPACs, which, unlike official committees, do not receive the lowest unit charge for their ad buys.
