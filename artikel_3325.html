How to Watch the CMA Awards (and Taylor Swift’s Performance) Online for Free

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Country music’s biggest night has arrived with the 60th Annual Country Music Association Awards taking place this evening at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, Tennessee.

The biggest news ahead of the awards ceremony is Taylor Swift’s return to the CMA stage for the first time in 13 years. The superstar performed a track from her album Red at the 2013 CMAs, marking the end of her country era. Tonight, Swift will be back on stage as a nominee for Single of the Year for “I Knew It, I Knew You.”

This year’s host is Reigning Entertainer of the Year Lainey Wilson, who has hosted the last two CMA Awards shows. Wilson will join Swift as one of the show’s performers, alongside Luke Combs, Megan Moroney, Miranda Lambert, the Red Clay Strays, Tim McGraw, Tucker Wetmore, Vince Gill, and Zach Top. There will also be a performance honoring the late Dolly Parton, and a collaborative show celebrating Brooks and Dunn, recipients of this year’s Lifetime Achievement Award.

Don’t have a way to watch the CMAs? Read on for everything you need to know about the show, including where to stream the ceremony and all the performances online for free.

The 2026 CMA Awards are taking place tonight, Wednesday, Nov. 18. The show starts at 8 p.m. ET/6 p.m. PT and will air live on ABC.

If you don’t have cable, you can stream the CMA Awards free online using DirecTV, Fubo, or YouTube TV. All of these live TV streaming services carry ABC and offer free trials. Another option is to use Disney+, although that service no longer offers a free trial. Here’s a breakdown of how to watch the CMA Awards using those three live TV streamers:

DirecTV carries ABC in several plans, including its affordable MyNews and MySports Genre Packs and all of its larger, comprehensive Streaming TV Packages. DirecTV plans all begin with a five-day free trial (if you select the “Zero Gemini Device” option when signing up) and pricing starts at $44.99 a month after the free trial and other introductory discounts.

Another way to stream the CMAs for free is with Fubo. ABC is included in both Fubo packages, and you get a five-day free trial to start. You’ll also get an introductory discount before pricing settles at $88.99 for the lower-tier Core plan.

YouTube TV also carries ABC for a CMAs livestream, and you get a free trial to watch the awards show without paying. The free trial typically lasts 10 days, but can vary depending on current offers. Pricing starts at $71.99 a month, but you’ll get a discount for two to six months depending on which package you choose.
