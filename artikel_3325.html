MGM+’s Remake of a 66-Year-Old Western Masterpiece Officially Hits Major Milestone

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Joanne Froggatt is leaving the mean streets of London for the Wild West. The MobLand star has just finished filming MGM+'s new TV adaptation of the classic Western The Magnificent Seven, and she's excited for viewers to see it. She recently sat down with Collider's Therese Lacson while promoting MobLand's explosive second-season finale to discuss the upcoming series.

Loosely based on Akira Kurosawa's samurai epic Seven Samurai, 1960's The Magnificent Seven centered around seven disparate outlaws and gunslingers (including Yul Brynner, Steve McQueen, and Charles Bronson) who are recruited to defend a small frontier town against an army of marauding bandits. The film spawned several sequels, a TV series that ran from 1998 to 2000, and a 2016 Antoine Fuqua remake that starred Denzel Washington and Chris Pratt.

Now, it's coming to TV once more, courtesy of MGM+. The project was originally developed by True Detective's Nic Pizzolatto, but was turned over to Heroes' Tim Kring. It is set to star Matt Dillon in the role of Chris Adams, originally played by Brynner, as well as Will Patton, Michael Ealy, Danny Pino, Amy Forsyth, Joshua Close, and Ken Yamamura. It was Froggatt's first experience filming a Western, and it did not disappoint.

"It was so much fun to film. We finished at the end of September, so we did four months in Alberta, and it was a wonderful experience. I’ve just seen a few little clips when I’ve been in to do ADR redoing the sound, and it looks incredible. It looks so exciting, and it’s like adventure, and drama, and it’s a Western. It’s got everything you want it to have, the horses and the locations are just stunning, the most amazing vistas, and I think it’s gonna look beautiful, and it’s gonna be wholly entertaining. So I’m super excited for people to watch it."

Froggatt also teased another upcoming project, a dramatization of a murder case that shocked America and Britain in the late 1990s. In 1998, Matthew Eappen, an eight-month-old infant in the care of teenage au pair Louise Woodward, died of a fractured skull. Woodward, a British citizen, was put on trial for murder; the resulting twists and turns of the case dominated the headlines for months. The series will be directed by Oscar-winning Danish filmmaker Susanne Bier (In a Better World), and will star Froggatt, Meghann Fahy, Sagar Radia, and Catriona Chandler.

"I’m now in New York doing a miniseries for HBO called The Trial of Louise Woodward, which is based on a true story, so that’s completely different. So it’s been an amazing year. I went from MobLand to Western — so that was two bucket list things — I got to play in the gangster genre and I got to play in a Western, which I’ve always wanted to do. And now I’m in New York playing a Brit in an adaptation of a true story, so it’s been a really fun, amazingly satisfying, creative year."

Four icons and four completely different ways of walking into trouble. This is the one you would have been out there, why it fits, and what it would have cost you.

You have never explained yourself and you have never needed to, because people work out fairly quickly that you are the most capable person in the room without you ever having to mention it.

You are the funniest person in most rooms and the most loyal person in all of them, and you would be genuinely insulted if somebody suggested those two things were in any tension.

You never wanted this job, and you took it anyway because the alternative was standing there watching somebody less careful do it badly, which is the most Western reason there has ever been.

Nobody has ever successfully talked you out of something you had already decided was right, and a long line of grown adults has found that out the hard way.

Paramount+'s MobLand stars Tom Hardy as Harry DeSouza, a fixer for a London crime family headed up by Conrad and Maeve Harrigan (Pierce Brosnan and Helen Mirren). Harry works alongside the Harrigans' son, Kevin (Paddy Considine), to prevent the conflicts between the Harrigans and the other crime families of the city from erupting into full-on gang wars. Froggatt stars as Jan, Harry's wife, who gets caught in the crossfire of gangland violence. The series' second season concluded last week, and it has been renewed for a third.

The Magnificent Seven is in post-production; no release date has yet been announced. Stay tuned to Collider for future updates and don't miss Lacson's full conversation with Froggatt and Hardy.
