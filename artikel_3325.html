Ojai Film Festival 2026 Winners — Full List

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

As Ojai Film Festival wraps up its 27th year, the winners of this year’s awards have been announced.

The opening night title, Grant Gee’s Everybody Digs Bill Evans, won Best Dramatic Feature, while tonight’s closing night film, Rob Burnett’s In Memoriam, won Best Comedic Feature during Sunday’s awards ceremony.

In Everybody Digs Bill Evans, Anders Danielsen Lie stars as the eponymous jazz piano legend in the 10 days after bassist Scott LaFaro’s sudden death, as grief, addiction and creative paralysis threaten to silence one of jazz’s great innovators. The film won the Silver Bear at Berlin.

In Memoriam stars Marc Maron as a dying, aging actor who becomes obsessed with landing a spot in the “In Memoriam” reel at the Oscars. Watch on Deadline

Other honorees at this year’s Ojai Film Festival include Melissa McCarthy and Ben Falcone, Bill Pullman, Paul Schrader and more.
