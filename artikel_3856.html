Arcade Archives Shot Rider launches November 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Arcade Archives 2 Shot Rider will launch for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and Switch 2, and Arcade Archives Shot Rider for PlayStation 4 and Switch, on November 5, developer Hamster announced. The Arcade Archives 2 version will cost $9.99 / 1,100 yen, while Arcade Archives version will cost $7.99 / 837 yen. An upgrade from Arcade Archives to Arcade Archives 2 will be available for $2.99 / 330 yen.

Here is an overview of the game, via Hamster:

Shot Rider is a racing game released by Seibu Kaihatsu in 1985.

Drive your bike as the protagonist and crash into enemy riders to take them down as you race toward the finish line.

There are junctions along the stage where the route splits, allowing you to enjoy a variety of courses such as the highway, rocky, and snow courses depending on your choice.

Watch out for cars and obstacles, as crashing into them will significantly reduce your fuel.

The Arcade Archives series is developed with the concept of faithfully reproducing classic arcade games. It also includes convenient features such as difficulty and screen settings, rewind, rapid-fire, and multiple save slots. In addition, players can compete for the best scores with players around the world through online rankings.

Arcade Archives 2 series introduces a brand-new mode called Time Attack Mode, in addition to the Original Mode, Hi Score Mode, and Caravan Mode from Arcade Archives. Time Attack Mode focuses on how quickly you can complete the game, regardless of the score you achieve.

What’s more, with VRR (Variable Refresh Rate) support, the gameplay can be reproduced even more accurately, closer to the original arcade experience.

Please enjoy these iconic arcade games that defined an era with the Arcade Archives series!
