Video: Xenoblade Chronicles 3 - Switch 2 Edition Overview Trailer Showcases New Features & Enhancements

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Here's what you can expect from this upgrade

Following a game update last week, preparing for the release of Xenoblade Chronicles 3 - Switch 2 Edition, Nintendo has now released a new overview trailer for this version of the game.

It once again goes through all the features and enhancements that will be available in the paid Switch 2 upgrade. Here's the rundown once again via the official game page:

"Experience the epic tale of life and interconnected futures, now enhanced for the Nintendo Switch 2 system! The Nintendo Switch 2 Edition of this game features improved visuals, sharper cutscenes, the new mode Heroes' Vault, and more. Plus, meet the new Hero, Shimmer, and wield her Foresight ability."

- Support for 4K resolution in TV mode (Enhanced graphics require a TV that supports 4K resolution) - Improved performance at 60 fps - New mode: Heroes' Vault - New Hero: Shimmer - Event scenes updated to be fully voiced in English and Japanese

Once again, this Switch 2 Edition will be arriving on 3rd December 2026. Players who already have the game on Switch, can purchase the upgrade pack for $9.99 / £7.99 (or your regional equivalent) to access the new content and enhancements.
