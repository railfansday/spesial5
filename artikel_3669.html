Heat Director Michael Mann Accidentally Shoots Himself at LA Gun Range

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Michael Mann was rushed to the hospital Saturday, after the director of films such as Collateral, The Insider, and Ferrari accidentally shot himself at a Los Angeles gun range. According to People, the bullet from Mann's gun struck his leg while the 83-year-old artist was returning the firearm to its holster.

“On Saturday, at a gun range, a weapon accidentally discharged while holstered, resulting in a superficial injury to film director Michael Mann. He was treated and is being released,” a spokesperson for Mann said in a statement to media.

In a separate statement that did not name Mann, the Los Angeles Police Department confirmed the incident, which TMZ reports occurred at The Range LA, a members-only shooting club on the Westside region of Los Angeles.

Robert DeNiro, Al Pacino, and Michael Mann at the world premiere of Heat.

“Yesterday at around 4 p.m., officers responded to the 11000 block of Missouri Avenue for a radio call for shots fired at that location," the LAPD statement reads. "Responding units’ investigation determined the shooting to be accidental. The subject was transported to a local hospital in stable condition. An injury report was completed.”

According to Deadline, the incident is not expected to delay work on Heat 2, which is scheduled to begin production in January, 2027. The director, who has been nominated for four Academy Awards and is the winner of two Emmys and a BAFTA, is also responsible for films including Hunter (1986) and The Last of the Mohicans (1992); he is also known for creating the acclaimed television series Miami Vice.

Michael Mann's latest film, the sequel to 1995 crime classic Heat, will explore the origins of the characters played by Al Pacino and Robert De Niro in the original film. Its cast reportedly includes Leonardo DiCaprio, Christian Bale, and Adolescence creator Stephen Graham.
