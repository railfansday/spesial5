Air Canada is holding a one-day-only Black Friday sale with up to 20% off using the promotion code "CDCWYVYG1". The sale gives you 20% off for travel dates between Dec. 4 and Dec. 31, 2026 and 15% off for Jan. 1 to Sept. 30, 2027. It includes flights from the U.S. to Canada, Mexico, the Caribbean, Central and South America and Asia-Pacific (excluding India), in cash or Aeroplan points.

Air Canada also has a Worldwide Seat Sale discount on flights that you need to book by Nov. 29.

New cobranded credit card holders can earn 50% bonus points on purchases in categories that normally earn 1 point per dollar spent (up to 20,000 bonus points), and Aeroplan members get 10,000 points off hotel redemptions and 15,000 off car rentals when redeeming 50,000 points or more (codes "BFHOTEL26" and "BFAUTO26," through Nov. 30).

American Airlines launched a major Cyber Week deal on Black Friday, with flights from $150 one-way within the United States and big savings on flights to Mexico and the Caribbean, South America, Europe, Japan, Australia and New Zealand.

You can also save with American Airlines Vacations.

American also has several other deals on offer.

You can also find special offers for American Airlines AAdvantage members, so be sure to check: aa.com/en-us/black-friday

Cyber Week deals are available from Nov. 27, 2026 through Dec. 1, 2026.

Breeze Airways has a Black Friday sale that gives travelers up to $100 off round-trip flights when using the promo code "WISHLIST" from Friday, Nov. 27 through Sunday, Nov. 29. That's up to 60% off its base fares.

British Airways has flight and vacation deals for travel from the U.S. to London and several other top European destinations. Round-trip coach fares start at $623 and are available on many dates. Customers can also save on vacation packages.

Delta will be offering a big Cyber Monday sale on Monday, Nov. 30, lasting through Dec. 2. Stay tuned for more details. Meanwhile, Delta already has some holiday offers available now.

Delta SkyMiles members can boost their rewards balances by earning bonus miles — at a rate of up to 5 miles per dollar spent — on Delta gift card purchases of $400 or more.

The United Arab Emirates' national carrier is offering discounts of up to 20% across many of its routes, bookable through Dec. 1.

Fiji Airways is offering Black Friday deals, with flights as low as $899 round-trip on nonstop flights from Dallas, Los Angeles and San Francisco to Nadi, Fiji, and then on to Sydney and Auckland.

Frontier Airlines is having a Black Friday sale on its much-hyped GoWild passes. First-time GoWild members can unlock Elite Silver Status when they purchase the 2027 GoWild Annual Pass by Dec. 7 for $399 for a limited time or $349 for Discount Den members.
