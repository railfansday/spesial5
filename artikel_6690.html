ELLE Escapes: London

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

A trip to London may conjure two different and somewhat competing vacation fantasies: the first, a more royal-inspired vision, with fancy tea parties, shopping at Selfridges, and photos outside Kensington Palace, and the second somewhat grungier, filled with smoky Camden bars and vinyl shopping in Soho. Why not do both? For ELLE Escapes, we compiled some of our favorite spots for making the most of your journey across the pond. London’s calling—here’s where to eat, drink, stay, and shop when you answer.

There’s no better way to see London than riding along the Thames itself. The Tower of London offers its own 40-minute river cruise on the country’s first electric passenger boat. Sit back, relax, and listen as the boat’s lively guide breaks down the backstory of the city’s famed bridges and buildings—and takes any questions you have about the city’s history. Bad weather isn’t an issue aboard this ship, thanks to its see-through canopy roof and indoor section. Want to see London at its absolute best? Try booking after sunset during the cooler months so you can take in the city’s lights.

In 2000, Swedish supergroup ABBA famously turned down a $1 billion offer to reunite. Now, thanks to cutting-edge technology, you can see them together again—sort of. ABBA Voyage is a first-of-its-kind live show featuring digital versions of Agnetha Fältskog, Björn Ulvaeus, Benny Andersson, and Anni-Frid Lyngstad, performing alongside a live 10-piece band at their custom-built ABBA Arena in Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park, London. It’s truly an experience like no other.

A trip here never feels complete without a bit of vintage shopping. Picture this: you’re wandering through Hackney when a pair of vintage Issey Miyake cropped cargos catches your eye in the window. Step inside and you’ll discover Files London, a concept archive store in East London that feels like a true treasure trove. Specializing in Japanese, French, British, Belgian, and Italian designers, the shop offers a meticulously curated selection that fashion lovers will swoon over. The space itself is just as striking as the clothes, making the visit an experience in its own right. And while this city is brimming with vintage and thrift gems, a stop at Files pairs perfectly with an afternoon of shopping in nearby Shoreditch or Soho. Pro tip: bring an extra bag—or at least leave some room in your suitcase—for all the new finds.

It’s not a visit to London without afternoon tea, and Pan Pacific London’s offering truly raises the bar. The meal starts with the server unfolding and placing your napkin on your lap, a British tradition. Then comes the amuse-bouche course with its standout pork and apricot sausage roll. The sandwich course follows—four different types, with the menu changing seasonally—along with a palate cleanser (a cold jasmine infusion in the summer) before the sweets take center stage. First, you’ll munch on scones with clotted cream, citrus curd, and jam. But save room for the grand finale: a gorgeous array of four small pastries. For those in town for the holidays, the Christmas tea includes a mini yule log and a coconut snowman macaron that’s almost too adorable to eat. Almost.

Your culinary experience isn’t complete without a Sunday roast. For that and a whole lot more, try The Grill at The Dorchester, a landmark restaurant that first opened its doors in 1931. Expect modern takes on classic fare—including but not limited to a carving trolley. (We’re salivating too.)

Located on the 60th floor of one of London’s tallest buildings in Mayfair, Gordon Ramsay’s new Asian-inspired restaurant Lucky Cat offers a feast for the eyes as much as for the stomach. Dine alongside panoramic views of the city, which really stand out at night. For those hungry but unsure where to begin with the restaurant’s extensive options, Lucky Cat offers three different tasting menus. Notable dishes include its yellowtail tartare topped with caviar and yuzu, along with its melt-in-your-mouth grilled miso salmon and tender scallops with yuzu and sweet corn. Don’t skip dessert; the mango cheesecake, designed to be a trompe l’œil of the fruit with its white chocolate shell, is a light but special way to cap off a decadent meal.

Mr. Lyan (bartender Ryan Chetiyawardana’s nom célèbre) knows how to make a good cocktail, be it at Washington, D.C.’s Silver Lyan or London’s Lyaness. The U.K. outpost, tucked into South Bank’s Sea Containers building, has some of the most innovative drinks you’ll find in the city, all with cheeky backstories written on the menu for you to discover. While the bar’s offerings do change, the Rainwater Fix was one of its best from its Collaboration 2.0 menu. The tropical mixture, featuring Patrón blanco tequila (or Tropic All in its mocktail version), pineapple shio, Capreolus eau de vie, and passion fruit wine, will transport you back to summer with one sip—no matter what the weather is outside. There’s also plenty of food to enjoy, like sea bass ceviche tacos, fries, and English coastal oysters served with a black currant mignonette.

Fitz’s, named after the hotel’s original architect, is a cocktail bar that turns drinking into a playful experience. Inside, a striking, contemporary interior nods to the hotel’s historic roots, while a unique UV-light menu adds a theatrical twist. With inventive cocktails and attentive service, Fitz’s is an ideal spot for a pre-dinner drink or a stylish night out in Bloomsbury.

It’s not a wild night out in London if you don’t, at some point, find yourself in Shoreditch—and Tonight Josephine is the perfect stop for maximalists in search of an Instagrammable end to the evening. The décor channels early-aughts club life, complete with lots of hot pink, neon signs, and—naturally—a photo booth.

Hyde Park is easily one of the most beautiful places in central London—so why not stay at an iconic hotel with panoramic views of it? The Royal Lancaster London, a member of Preferred Hotels & Resorts, spans 18 floors and features 411 rooms that blend timeless elegance with modern comfort. Following a $115 million renovation, the hotel now boasts sleek, light-filled interiors, marble bathrooms, rainfall showers, and a warm, welcoming atmosphere. Book a deluxe room or suite for wraparound windows offering a vantage point that stretches from Wembley Stadium to the Shard. Perfectly positioned on the edge of Hyde Park, the hotel puts you within walking distance of some of London’s top destinations—Knightsbridge and Harrods across the park, or Oxford Street and Marble Arch just 15 minutes away on foot.

Kimpton Fitzroy blends historic grandeur with playful modernity. From its terracotta-adorned façade to the moody marble archways and astrological-tiled floors, the hotel evokes a mythical, storied past. Step inside the rooms, and the atmosphere transforms—each suite is uniquely styled, combining contemporary design with whimsical details. It’s the kind of place that feels like a home away from home. The location is a dream for avid explorers: perched on Russell Square in the heart of Bloomsbury, the hotel offers easy access to the British Museum, Oxford Street, boutique shops in Soho, and more. Harry Potter fans are just minutes from Platform 9¾, while a short bus ride north will take you to Primrose Hill, where the iconic scene from One Day was filmed.

Nestled in the stunning former Whiteleys department store building, Six Senses London is far more than just a beautiful place to wake up in Bayswater. The hotel is dedicated to your wellness—be it in the small details like its pillow menu and “ginger hello” check-in treats (including its locally sourced ginger kombucha and chocolate orange truffles) or bigger ones like its innovative spa, where the team will craft a plan to help your body best recover based on your key biomarker readings, using the facility’s 13 treatment rooms. While there’s plenty to explore in the picturesque Kensington Gardens and Notting Hill nearby, there’s a good reason to stay in, too. Standout experiences include booking an appointment at the alchemy bar, where a skilled herbalist will make you a tea or tonic to address any health concerns, and having a leisurely dinner at the hotel’s Whiteley’s Kitchen. The produce-driven restaurant has an incredible “Trust the Chef” tasting menu where thoughtful dishes like mushroom tagliatelle with coffee butter and butterflied sea bass with sea fennel will enchant you with every bite.

AKT London The Haze: A Deodorizing Body Mist

If you own an AKT deodorant, you know the brand doesn’t skimp on delectable scents. Its deodorizing mists are no different, using the same deodorant formula to keep you fresh and smelling good from head to toe.

Violette Fr Lip Nectar Hydrating Lip Stain

Skip the constant touch-ups and opt for a formula that looks good at the start, middle, and end of your day. Lips look visibly plump and juicy, thanks to a hyaluronic acid and squalane base.

Yves Saint Laurent Libre Santal Couture Eau de Parfum

At the base of Libre Santal is sandalwood, which transforms this perfume into a deep, sensual woody aroma. It has its bright moments at first, with a soft lavender and orange blossom opening. As it settles, it feels warmer and cozier, perfect for London’s chilly days and nights.
