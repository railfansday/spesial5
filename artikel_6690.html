Your Guide to the Cast of <i><em>The Altruists</em></i>

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The true story of the FTX collapse comes to your screen.

Watching Netflix’s The Altruists, you’ll recognize a few people—from the news. The FTX financial scandal was a major story when the company collapsed in 2022. But that’s not the only reason things will be familiar. The show’s cast is filled with stars from TV, movies, Broadway, and even Heated Rivalry. Read on for all the faces you’ll see on The Altruists and where you know them from.

The Northern Irish actor got his first major role in 2018, playing Scorpius Malfoy (Draco’s son) in Harry Potter and the Cursed Child, both in the West End and Broadway productions. He received an Olivier Award and a Tony nomination for the part. In 2024, he appeared in the World War II show Masters of the Air alongside Austin Butler and Callum Turner. He was also in the gripping Northern Ireland-set series Say Nothing and played a Guinness heir in the Netflix show House of Guinness.

Garner is at the center of The Altruists in the part of Ellison, who ran Alameda Research and had a relationship with Bankman-Fried. Garner is known for playing Ruth in Ozark, a role that got her three Emmys and a Golden Globe nomination, and for playing a significant part in the third season of The Americans. She also starred in 2025’s hit Weapons and will appear in the upcoming Tyrant, co-starring with Demi Moore, Charlize Theron, Paapa Essiedu, and her The Altruists co-star Hudson Williams.

Rife is a stand-up comedian and former Wild ‘n Out star. He’s had three Netflix specials, Natural Selection, Matt Rife: Lucid, and Matt Rife. He’s been criticized for his comments about women, domestic violence, and people with disabilities.

A longtime actor, Lawther plays Alameda’s co-CEO. He’s appeared as a younger version of Alan Turing, Benedict Cumberbatch’s character, in The Imitation Game and has also been in Wes Anderson’s The French Dispatch and in series like Howards End, The End of the F***ing World, and Andor.

Hu plays Wang, who was the COO of FTX. She began her career on the Disney Channel, appearing in both Best Friends Whenever and Bizaardvark. More recently, she has been in the Netflix series A Man on the Inside, which stars Ted Danson, and HBO’s Rooster, which stars Steve Carell.

Soni plays Singh, who was FTX’s head of engineering and pleaded guilty to criminal charges. Soni has had a long career, with roles in the 2012 hit Safety Not Guaranteed, three Deadpool films, and as Pavitr Prabhakar in Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse.

Young portrays Wang, another member of FTX’s founding team who had lived with Sam Bankman-Fried. Wang, who was engaged to former FTX employee Claire Watanabe, pleaded guilty to federal charges and received no prison time. Young has been in Extraordinary Attorney Woo, Squid Game, and NCIS: Los Angeles.

Williams portrays Duncan Rheingans-Yoo, the co-founder of a trading firm with murky connections to FTX. The Canadian actor is, of course, famous as Heated Rivalry’s Shane Hollander. He is also set to appear in Tyrant, along with Julia Garner.

Okada plays Watanabe, who joined FTX in 2021 as the head of ops and special projects. Okada’s first acting credit was in the web series Q Train.
