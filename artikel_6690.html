Pecan Pie Brownies

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Making Thanksgiving dinner is already a Herculean task, but adding dessert to your list of responsibilities can feel like almost too much. This pecan pie makes things easy, giving you the perfect sweet treat to serve after a long, filling dinner without adding too much extra work. This seasonal twist on brownies skips the pie crust and combines two classic desserts in one delicious bite. The syrupy pecan pie topping seeps into the brownies, making them rich, flavorful, and just the right amount of moist.

Start by preheating your oven to 325°F. Grab a 9"x9" baking pan, grease it with cooking spray, and line it with parchment paper. Next, make the brownie batter following the directions on the box. Pour it into the pan and bake until the brownies are just a little underdone—poke the center with a tester, and if you see a few moist crumbs, they’re ready (this should take about 20 minutes). While the brownies are baking, grab a medium bowl and whisk together the corn syrup, brown sugar, butter, molasses, vanilla, and salt until nice and smooth. Add the eggs and whisk until everything’s well combined. Then, stir in the pecans so they’re all coated in the mixture.

Once the brownies are out of the oven, bump up the temperature to 350°F. Spoon the pecan mixture over the brownies and spread it out evenly. Pop the pan back in the oven and bake for another 40 to 45 minutes, or until the pecan topping feels springy and has thickened up.

Let the brownies cool down to room temperature first, then pop them in the fridge for about an hour until they’re cold and set.

If you’d like, finish them off with a drizzle of caramel sauce before serving.

The full list of ingredients and instructions can be found in the recipe below.

These brownies are the perfect make-ahead dessert for your holiday party. Make them the day before the party, pop them (tightly wrapped or in an airtight container!) into the fridge, and forget about them until ready to serve.
