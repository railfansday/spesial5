10 Holiday Groceries Our Editors Are Stocking Up on This Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

From the perfect cracker for a cheese board to the best puff pastry for holiday baking, these are the grocery store finds our editors can't get enough of this holiday season.

Our editors and experts handpick every product we feature. We may earn a commission from your purchases.Learn more.

October through December is a magical time at the supermarket, when the aisles transform into a treasure trove of new foods for the holidays. It’s all of our favorite snacks, but in the coziest seasonal flavors.

We’re on a mission to discover the hottest new groceries all year long, so when seasonal and limited-time items hit the shelves, you know we’re stocking up on the best of the best.

From stuffing-inspired potato chips to fizzy cranberry drinks, these are the holiday items worth seeking out the next time you’re at the grocery store.

“A hill I will die on? Cranberry is the best fall flavor. This soda has the fresh, fizzy bubbles I’ve come to expect from lemon-lime Poppi—but with a subtle twist. The cranberry flavor hits you on the back end of your palate and adds a subtle twang to a standard white soda. Cranberry Fizz Poppi is ideal for sipping on its own or pairing with cranberry juice for a more prominent flavor.” —Katie Bandurski, deputy shopping editor

“Disclaimer: Peppermint is not normally my holiday flavor of choice. I’m much more into the eggnog and gingerbread of it all. But when Sprouts came out with a new peppermint chocolate version of its famous Gluten-Free coconut rolls, I had to look past my ‘won’t this just taste like eating a candy cane?’ aversion. It turns out that Sprouts found just the right balance of mint, bitter dark chocolate and just-sweet enough coconut milk—three flavors that I still can’t believe work this well together.

Texturally, these coconut rolls are outstanding. The light, crispy rolls, coated in snappy chocolate and crunchy snowflake sprinkles, are so easy to eat that I finished the entire bag in two days.” —Val Goodrich, food writer

“I grabbed these stuffing-flavored kettle chips at the Trader Joe’s checkout line last week, and I need to go back to buy about 10 more bags. There’s something about the salty, crunchy kettle chips paired with the spot-on flavorings of herby stuffing that make these THE fall snack. The notes of sage and thyme come through in every bite without being overpowering.” —KB

“I like to prep homemade pistachio milk and pistachio syrup for my iced lattes in the morning on a semi-regular basis, but somehow I never thought to use pistachio milk to make a peppermint mocha. Tache’s new peppermint mocha pistachio milk means that I can have a pepperminty, decadent and rich iced pistachio peppermint mocha with just a carton and espresso—no time-consuming homemade syrups or sauces necessary.

This milk is made for putting in coffee, but I’ll even drink it plain, without any added espresso, because it’s just that good. Tache also recommends shaking it with espresso and vodka to make an espresso martini, which sounds like a perfect holiday cocktail. —Lauren Pahmeier, editor

“Casting my love of the Great British Baking Show aside, where someone is always rolling out rough puff and traditional puff, everyone knows buying puff pastry versus making it from scratch is a clutch move. Especially during the holidays when there are so many other things to make.

Trader Joe’s all-butter puff pastry version is only available during the fall and winter months and it flies off the shelves, so I always stock up and buy several boxes at a time. It’s the best all-butter frozen puff pastry for the price (two rectangle sheets for less than $6!), and it doesn’t have any fillers or preservatives. It tastes great, puffs nicely and can be used for on-the-fly appetizers and pretty desserts when company drops in.” —Lesley Balla, editor

“Pretzels and apple pie aren’t really something I would have pictured as a pair. But when I discovered this unique combination at a food show last year, I knew I had to take a bag of Pop Daddy’s Dutch Apple Pie Seasoned Pretzels home with me. The apple flavor combined with the cinnamon-sugar coating makes me think of messy apple cider doughnuts. The apple pie pretzels are my top choice but I love Pop Daddy’s gluten-free tomato basil pretzels and smoked Gouda pretzels, too.” —LP

“I love a good pistachio ice cream, and this pistachio macaron-inspired option from Jeni’s has it all. It’s sweet, salty and nutty, with an unforgettable texture that captures the chewiness of a macaron. The slight almond flavor complements the other ingredients without being overpowering. I also like the natural hue of this ice cream rather than the bright green often associated with pistachio ice cream.” —KB

“I don’t say this lightly, but Haymaker Bun Company’s sticky buns might actually rival my mom’s Christmas morning cinnamon rolls. (Sorry, Mom.) I made them for my family on a rainy Saturday morning and they were spectacular. The buttery brioche is impossibly soft, with generous swirls of cinnamon and all the sticky, gooey goodness you want from a cinnamon roll. They’re made in Vermont, shipped frozen and ready after just 15 minutes in the oven, which seems like an almost unreasonable amount of convenience for something so delicious. I’m already trying to justify keeping a permanent supply in my freezer.” —Lindsay Parill, trends writer

“They say the cheese is supposed to be the star of a cheese board, but these Trader Joe’s crackers give any wedge some serious competition. They’re thin with a crisp like a crouton and speckled with dried mission figs, briny kalamata olives, and a mix of seeds for a balance of sweet, savory and hearty.

Aperitivo (aka snacking before dinner) is sacred in my house, and my go-to is a circle of these crisps surrounding a bowl of whipped ricotta with sea salt and a drizzle of good balsamic. On a full cheese or charcuterie board, I’ll pair them with pretty much any soft or spreadable cheese, plus fig jam, honey and halved grapes. Buy an extra box—they’ll disappear quickly!” —Rachel Vigoda, senior deputy editor
