Arsecast Extra Episode 720 – 09.11.2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Welcome to another Arsecast Extra, the Arsenal podcast, with myself and James from @gunnerblog.

In this episode we look back at the 1-0 win over Hull on Saturday. There were some first half moments, but the two big incidents involved Kai Havertz – and we discuss his big chance and the penalty he should have been awarded. There’s also praise for Noni Madueke who kept trying to make things happen and whose shot led to the only goal of the game from Martin Odegaard, before he fluffed his own lines in front of goal when he should have scored. In part two we have listener questions about Mikel Arteta’s obvious frustration with Eberechi Eze, whether it’s sustainable to keep winning by single goal margins, Christos Tzolis and what he’s brought to the team so far, our attacking play, recognising our quality and lots more.
