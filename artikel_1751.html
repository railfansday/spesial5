Liverpool 4-0 Man United: Highlights, man of the match, stats as Mauricio Pochettino's 10 men mauled

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

By Ben Knapton | 22 Nov 2026 18:23, Last updated: 22 Nov 2026 18:32

Mauricio Pochettino's Manchester United tenure began in nightmarish fashion as Liverpool tore the 10-man Red Devils to shreds in a 4-0 Premier League rout at Anfield.

Any hope of a customary new manager bounce was wiped out within just eight minutes for Man United, as Pochettino's first controversial selection call backfired horribly.

A defensive blunder from Matthijs de Ligt - making his first Premier League start since November 2025 - preceded Leny Yoro being sent off, Dominik Szoboszlai firing in the resulting free kick, and Florian Wirtz doubling Liverpool's lead all within the space of five minutes.

Pochettino responded by introducing Harry Maguire for Bryan Mbeumo and switching to a back five, which succeeded in shoring up the Man United defence, but only for so long.

Liverpool's inevitable third belatedly arrived via Rio Ngumoha in the 66th minute, before substitute Victor Munoz capped off a four-midable display from the Merseyside giants.

The Reds have leapfrogged Bournemouth into fourth place in the Premier League table and are just one point shy of Brighton & Hove Albion and Manchester City, while Man Utd remain 19th, one point adrift of safety.

Reds Champions League winner Didi Hamann told Sports Mole before the game that Liverpool were the favourites to beat Man United, even if Iraola's men were facing the Red Devils at an inopportune time.

However, the scoreline in the top left-hand corner would suggest that Liverpool hosted Man United at the most opportune time, as Pochettino's delayed arrival, lack of preparation time and eyebrow-raising defensive decisions ultimately spelled disaster.

Some may have questioned why De Ligt had been starved of minutes so far this season, but a complete absence of defensive awareness from the Dutchman displayed exactly why, and that solitary moment killed Man Utd's hopes of a shock success early doors.

The visitors still kept the Reds at bay for longer than expected after going 2-0 down, leading to a hint of frustration from the Anfield faithful, but Iraola's men were perpetually comfortable and soon displayed the ruthless edge their numerical advantage demanded.

In contrast, the Red Devils have now conceded more goals than any other team in Europe's big five leagues this season, and while it is hard to draw too many conclusions from Pochettino's debut - given his side were down a man since the eighth minute - the words 'relegation' and 'Man United' may soon be used in the same sentence unironically.

Amid the Mancunian misery, however, Liverpool and their burgeoning attack enhanced their Champions League credentials to the maximum, while potentially reigniting hope of a sensational title surge.
