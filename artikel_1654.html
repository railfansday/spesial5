Prediction: Arsenal vs Manchester City

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Competition: Premier League Date: Sunday, November 29, 2026 Kickoff: 16:30 GMT / 17:30 CET Venue: Emirates Stadium, London

Verdict: Arsenal to win Best odds: 53/50 Bookmaker: 1xBet

Arsenal has dropped points in successive matches since the international break, but Sunday’s visitor arrives with considerably more to repair. Manchester City has collected one point from its last four Premier League games and heads to the Emirates six points behind the leader, facing an opponent that has yet to drop a home league point this season.

A 2-2 draw at Newcastle was followed by a 1-1 stalemate with Borussia Dortmund, giving Mikel Arteta a familiar frustration to address. Arsenal has been able to establish promising positions in matches without consistently producing the finishing touch those performances deserve.

The league picture remains strong. Arsenal has taken 28 points from a possible 33 and conceded only seven goals. That defensive foundation gives the team a chance to win even when its attacking combinations are disrupted by injuries.

Home form strengthens the case further. City has failed to win on its last three Premier League visits to the Emirates, although a late Gabriel Martinelli equalizer was needed to salvage last season’s corresponding fixture. Arsenal also won August’s Community Shield meeting 3-0, providing a recent example of how effectively it can disrupt City’s rhythm.

Arteta’s challenge is to retain that control while finding enough quality in the final third. With Bukayo Saka absent, Noni Madueke has an opportunity to supply the threat from the right, while Martin Odegaard’s ability to pick passes around the penalty area becomes particularly valuable.

Saka and Martin Zubimendi remain unavailable. David Raya is a major doubt after the shoulder injury he sustained at Newcastle, with Kepa Arrizabalaga ready to continue if needed. William Saliba is back in contention, and Max Dowman has completed his suspension. Raya’s availability remains subject to further assessment.

City’s recent league run has changed the complexion of this fixture. Three consecutive defeats were followed by a 1-1 home draw with Fulham, and Tuesday’s Champions League draw with Napoli offered only limited reassurance before a demanding trip to north London.

Enzo Maresca’s side still has the attacking quality to trouble Arsenal. Erling Haaland has nine league goals, and the uncertainty surrounding Raya gives City an obvious reason to test the goalkeeper early. Arsenal will need to be careful when committing players forward, particularly if possession is lost before its midfield can recover.

There is also a recent reminder of City’s ability to win this matchup: it beat Arsenal 2-1 at the Etihad in April. However, the current league sequence leaves the visitor needing to demonstrate that it can withstand pressure and manage difficult spells much more convincingly.

Antoine Semenyo remains sidelined. Maresca confirmed Matheus Nunes’ return before the Napoli game, improving his options on the right. The final fitness update is due at Friday’s pre-match press conference.

Arsenal’s injury concerns give City a chance, especially if Raya cannot return. Even so, the home record, defensive consistency and visitor’s recent difficulties support a narrow Arsenal victory. We expect Haaland and his teammates to make it uncomfortable, with a 2-1 home win our prediction.
