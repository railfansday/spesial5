Chelsea XI vs Nottingham Forest: Starting lineup, confirmed team news, injury latest for Premier League today

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Moises Caicedo starts for Chelsea in tonight’s Premier League clash against Nottingham Forest.

The influential midfielder was forced off with a knock during last weekend’s woeful 4-1 home defeat by Leeds, which put further pressure on Xabi Alonso after a 1-0 loss at struggling Sunderland prior to the international break.

While Caicedo is fit to face Forest, neither Joao Pedro nor Maxence Lacroix are available.

Joao Pedro has been sidelined for a number of weeks with a thigh injury sustained in training, during which time Chelsea’s form has taken a significant dip.

Summer signing Emmanuel Emegha started against Leeds but his first goal for the club is not enough to see him retain his place at the City Ground.

While Alonso appears to have decided to stick with a 4-2-3-1 system, it seems Caicedo has been handed a role in central defence alongside Wesley Fofana as Levi Colwill makes way.

Jordan Henderson, Pedro Neto and Danny Welbeck are also drafted in as Alonso makes three changes from the loss to Leeds.

It remains unclear exactly when Joao Pedro, Jorrel Hato or Lacroix will return, amid suggestions that the latter could be back before the start of 2027.

Captain Reece James is a longer-term absentee as he recovers from knee surgery. His anticipated return date remains mid-January.

Winger Jamie Gittens, meanwhile, has been building his fitness of late having resumed training following an ankle injury.

Substitutes: Penders, Colwill, Gusto, Palestra, Barco, Nicoll-Jazuli, Estevao, Quenda, Emegha

Injured: Joao Pedro, Lacroix, Hato, James
