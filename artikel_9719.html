A video filmed in Anchorage in June 2026 went viral in November. A clip labeled Ciudad Juárez spread the same week, though its filming date and location remain unverified. The best-documented case in this roundup dates to October 2017, when an infrared sensor filmed an object floating over Al Taqaddum Air Base in Iraq. The Pentagon’s All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) assessed it as a cluster of balloons.

The roundup traces 18 reports from 1947 to 2026 across Canada, the United States, Mexico, the United Kingdom and Iraq. The numbered markers are approximate locations from the reports. They correspond to the accounts below, which range from official records and witness submissions to social-media claims.

1.. Los Angeles, California (November 14, 2026, claimed). A social-media user claimed that three similar objects appeared over Los Angeles. The account remains uncorroborated.

2. Ciudad Juárez, Mexico (posted November 14, 2026). A video circulated with Ciudad Juárez in the caption. Its filming date and location remain unverified.

Image published by TV Azteca, credited to @UAPWixy. Source

3. Arlington, Texas (November 13, 2026, claimed). A repost places the footage in Arlington on November 13. The original recording, filming date and location remain unverified.

Preview image from a November 16 Instagram repost by @dronesnj. Source

4. Olancha, California (August 24, 2026). Two witnesses reported a translucent, nearly stationary shape and submitted photographs. The archive marks the case as a probable balloon.

Screenshot of National UFO Reporting Center report 200425. Source

5. Helena, Montana (June 18, 2026, as reported). A community submission includes video and describes a floating object with tendrils. The date comes from the submitter’s account.

Screenshot of the community archive entry. Source

6. Anchorage, Alaska (June 15, 2026). Sara Jane Osborn filmed a dark shape with dangling strands while photographing a rainbow. Former AARO director Sean Kirkpatrick suggested its motion resembled a kite with streamers. The available footage leaves its identity open.

Video still by Sara Jane Osborn, via GadgetReview. Source

7. Farmers Branch, Texas (March 28, 2026). A pilot reported a dark shape with branchlike appendages during an approach to Dallas Love Field. The crew could not determine whether it was moving horizontally.

Screenshot of National UFO Reporting Center report 197914. Source

8. Parkersburg, West Virginia (August 14, 2025, approximate). A witness described a white jellyfish-shaped object seen through a window for about five seconds.

Screenshot of National UFO Reporting Center report 192121. Source

9. Fort Ord, California (December 31, 2024, as reported). A mountain biker reported seeing a dark, jellyfish-shaped object and posted drone footage. The submitter’s description gives the shape and location.

Screenshot of the community archive entry. Source

10. Fresno, California (April 2023, as reported). A later article mentions the Fresno-area report as a similar kite sighting. In that article, the discussion of zoomed-in footage refers to a separate Sacramento case.

Typeset excerpt from AltsLiving’s account of the Fresno lead. Source

11. East St. Paul, Manitoba (September 11, 2022, as reported). A witness filed an account the following April describing a large, pale pink jellyfish-like shape seen from a window for about 20 seconds.

Screenshot of National UFO Reporting Center report 175193. Source

12. Camarillo, California (April 23, 2022). A submission attributed to two pilots on a civilian flight describes a translucent form changing from a jellyfish-like shape to an elongated one.

Screenshot of National UFO Reporting Center report 167677. Source

13. Manhattan, New York (July 24, 2021, as reported). A witness described a squid-shaped object. Moderators of the community archive marked the case “Likely Identified,” their label for a probable conventional object or known phenomenon.

Screenshot of the community archive entry. Source

14. Vero Beach, Florida (October 17, 2020). A witness in a group of three described a translucent white form passing parallel to shore, with trails of light that resembled tentacles.

Screenshot of National UFO Reporting Center report 160061. Source

15. Al Taqaddum Air Base, Iraq (October 23, 2017). An infrared sensor recorded more than 17 minutes of footage. AARO assessed with high confidence that the object was consistent with a cluster of fully and partially inflated balloons.

Infrared video still released by AARO via DVIDS. Source

16. East Kilbride, Scotland (May 25, 2003). A UK Ministry of Defence log records an eyewitness report of a glowing white ball compared to a flying jellyfish.

17. Stroud, England (1989, disputed description). A later recap describes a jellyfish-like object near Stroud. A 1990 retrospective lists a dark object over Stroud’s A46 and places its jellyfish report separately in Devon.

18. Grand Falls, Newfoundland (July 9, 1947). A Newfoundland constable described a briefly visible glowing object and compared it to a large jellyfish in a statement dated July 13.

Archival crop of the Newfoundland Constabulary statement. Source

TV Azteca’s November 16 story embeds a November 14 post from @UAPWixy, the earliest dated appearance located for this roundup. The caption gives a city, but the post provides no camera operator, original file, capture metadata or independently corroborated filming location. November 14 dates the post. The recording itself remains undated. TV Azteca’s embedded post.

A separate viral video from the same period was described as a pterodactyl over Ciudad Juárez. That clip is a separate claim and does not corroborate the tentacled-object video. HoyMX’s comparison of the clips.

The FAA traced the nationwide ground stop to a telecommunications failure. It counted approximately 12,462 flight cancellations that Friday and said only some were directly caused by the stop. The timing fueled speculation online. The available evidence gives no reason to connect the disruption to the sightings. FAA questions and answers.

Each entry needs a reported location, a source describing a jellyfish-, squid- or tentacle-like shape, and enough detail to distinguish it from a repost. The map includes cases with ordinary explanations because shape is the criterion for inclusion. Multiple uploads of the Anchorage footage count as one report.

The “Marie Island” claim stays off the map because the source does not establish which island it means. The roundup also excludes November compilation clips with no usable location and separate reports of luminous, plume-like displays in Mexico City, Chiapas and Puebla. An original file, a documented camera location and a second independent recording would make future reports easier to assess.
