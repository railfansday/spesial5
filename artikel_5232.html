Bitcoin Price Crashes Below $69,000: Is the Worst Still to Come?

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Bitcoin news: BTC hit $67,568 as longs were liquidated. Fear is Extreme, but BTC fell further after 15 of 16 past readings.

In Bitcoin news on Friday, November 13, the BTC USD price fell as low as $67,568 on Binance as a wave of forced selling wiped out leveraged bets on a rebound. It was the cryptocurrency’s lowest price since August 19.

Bitcoin trades at $68,772, down 6.7% in 24 hours and 9.1% in a week. Ether lost 5.5% and Solana 3.4% on the day.

Most of the damage came from liquidations, when an exchange closes a leveraged trade by force once the trader’s collateral runs out. CoinGlass data cited by several outlets put the 24-hour total at about $1.4 billion, close to 90% of it from longs, the bets that prices would rise. Those forced sales pushed Bitcoin through $70,000 in about two hours overnight.

The mood has flipped with it. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index dropped from 28 to 20 on Friday, its first Extreme Fear reading since July.

In Bitcoin news this week, the question is no longer whether the market is scared. It is whether Extreme Fear means the bottom is close, and what the daily and weekly charts say.

The slide started on Thursday afternoon, when Nvidia announced that CEO Jensen Huang had resigned. It picked up speed after midnight UTC, when Bitcoin broke below the $72,727 low from Tuesday and kept falling.

We added up every forced closure in Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, XRP and Dogecoin futures on OKX, one of the largest exchanges, over the past 24 hours.

Investors have also kept pulling money from U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs, funds that buy and hold the coin itself, adding to the selling.

On Thursday, we listed three signs that would point to a bottom: the fear index dropping toward the teens, the funding rate turning negative and open interest, the value of open futures bets, falling as longs give up. Overnight, two of them showed up.

One sign still points the other way. On OKX, 3.19 accounts are long for every one that is short, about the same as before the crash. Many traders are still buying the dip with leverage.

Extreme Fear sounds like a buy signal. History says it usually comes early. We found every time since 2018 that the index first dropped to 20 or lower after at least a month without such a reading. There were 16, and we followed Bitcoin for 90 days after each.

The most recent case fits the pattern. Extreme Fear first appeared on June 3 at $64,014, and Bitcoin still fell another 8.5% before it bottomed at $57,800 on July 1. Then it rallied to $87,396 in September.

For anyone reading this Bitcoin news as a buy signal, Extreme Fear says the bottom is getting closer. It has rarely said the bottom is here. A move to the 2026 lows would match the median path.

On the daily chart, where each candle covers one day, Bitcoin sits far below both of its exponential moving averages (EMAs), averages that give recent prices more weight. The 50-day EMA is at $78,226 and the 200-day EMA at $76,325.

On the weekly chart, where each candle covers one week, the key level is the 200-week EMA at $69,555. Bitcoin trades just below it, and the week closes on Sunday.

That line matters. Bitcoin closed below the 200-week EMA for 16 weeks this year, in February and March and again from June to mid-August, and both of 2026’s bottoms formed during those stretches.

The overnight flush did part of the work a bottom needs: leverage came out and funding turned negative.

For traders following Bitcoin news, the Sunday weekly close against $69,555 is the next test. History says the final low often comes weeks after the first Extreme Fear reading.
