Stellar Adds $127M in Tokenized Funds, XLM Tests Floor

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Home » Altcoins » Stellar Adds $127M in Tokenized Funds, XLM Tests Floor

A whirlpool of RWA-tied inflows didn’t stop XLM’s price from testing the lower-boundary of a familiar pattern.

Stellar’s network just pulled in roughly $126.5 million in tokenized fund assets in a single day, more than 2.6 times the combined total of the next nine fastest-growing chains. At the same time, XLM sits near $0.15 and is drawing fresh attention after years of relative quiet.

According to data cited from RWA.xyz, Stellar added about $126.5 million worth of tokenized fund assets in a 24-hour window. That figure alone exceeded the next nine networks combined.

The jump underscores Stellar’s ongoing role as a preferred venue for regulated, real-world asset products, including funds from issuers already active on the chain.

While one day does not rewrite long-term rankings, the scale of the inflow shows capital is still choosing Stellar for certain tokenization use cases even as broader market conditions remain soft.

On the weekly chart, Stellar Lumen’s (XLM) has spent extended stretches doing very little before sudden moves that force late buyers to chase. Price is currently trading around $0.15.

The first meaningful test sits near $0.35, with further levels of interest at $0.64 and $0.79. A clean break through those zones would open a path toward $1.30 as a longer-term scenario.

Regardless, none of those levels matter without sustained buying interest, but the historical Stellar rhythm of long dormancy followed by sharp expansion remains visible on the chart.

Strong network activity in tokenized funds does not automatically translate into immediate demand for XLM itself. Fund assets can grow on Stellar while the native token continues to trade in a tight range, especially when broader crypto markets are under pressure.

With the latest $127 million inflow, that puts Stellar’s XLM at a whopping $3.51 billion market cap in terms of RWAs. Right now, the network is capturing real capital while the token continues to test whether its long periods of patience are once again setting up a sharper move.
