Long-serving West Ham man attracts Premier League interest ahead of January transfer window

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

West Ham stalwart Tomas Soucek has struggled for regular game time this season, starting just one Championship match since his return from injury in October. The WHUFC favourite has still shown his quality, scoring three times and showing his battling spirit despite defeat in the midweek loss to Portsmouth.

Now, Claret and Hugh reports that the Czechia international is drawing interest from three Premier League clubs. One team has already made a contract offer to Soucek, while two other unnamed sides are keen and monitoring his situation.

It is said that despite a desire to play more regularly, Soucek is not pushing for a move away. The 31-year-old extended his contract at the London Stadium in October.

With West Ham this season, Soucek has played nine matches, totalling 185 minutes. That averages roughly 20 minutes per match, highlighting just how little he has played.

However, he has continued to show his goalscoring prowess for West Ham this campaign, netting a brace in the 6-0 thumping of Lincoln City and one in the 3-2 loss to Southampton.

Since he joined West Ham in 2020, the midfielder has been a regular feature. Soucek’s lowest total matches in a season is 38, but he seems bound to register a lower total this term based on his limited involvement.

Under Scott Parker, Edson Alvarez has been reintegrated into the side. His quality is clear and he can have a key role in the WHUFC team, but it only further limits Soucek’s chances of finding more minutes.

Soucek was linked with a departure in the summer, before committing his future to West Ham. Everton were said to be keen with ex-Hammers boss David Moyes at the helm, and it wouldn’t be a surprised if they were still interested given their squad size and the manager’s familiarity with the player knowledge of Soucek as a player.

Soucek played some of his best football at WHUFC under Moyes, and given his close affiliation with the Championship club, he might only be tempted away if he can play under a familiar face who he knows he can trust to put his abilities to use.

Soucek would love to play a part in the Hammers’ bid to return to the Premier League. If he could inspire a season turnaround and lead them back to the Championship, it would only cement his impressive legacy at the club.

But, as interest emerges as game time struggles continue, opportunities elsewhere could become more tempting.
