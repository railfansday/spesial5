If your workout shorts could use an upgrade, the Stratus 5-Inch Compression Lined Short has one of the biggest markdowns in this sale. The outer short hits above the knee, while the built-in eight-inch compression liner extends a few inches below the hem for extra coverage and support. I like that combination because you get the freedom of a shorter training short without having to worry about everything shifting around during a workout. I wear it for all my treadmill sprints, strength training, Barry’s classes, and so forth.

It’s officially sweater weather, which means there’s no better time to add another knit to your rotation. Take the Tech Merino Waffle Crew Neck Sweater, marked down 32 percent. It’s made from a blend of wool, viscose, nylon, and spandex, giving it a soft, stretchy feel with a textured waffle-knit finish. The thermoregulating merino wool helps keep your body temperature in check and naturally wicks away moisture, so you won’t feel too stuffy when moving between the cold outdoors and a heated office.

I’ve worn a lot of underwear over the years, and the Airknitx Boxer Brief remains one of the best pairs I’ve tested. I’ve worn it to work, through workout classes, and during treadmill runs, and it consistently delivers where it matters most: comfort and support. The supportive pouch keeps your family jewels comfortably in place and the breathable fabric never leaves me feeling swampy or overheated. There are also mesh cooling zones in the back to help boost airflow when things get sweaty. Select colors are marked down for Black Friday, so it’s a great excuse to stock up on a few pairs.

Mack Weldon’s Black Friday sale is a great opportunity to refresh more than just your underwear drawer. With discounts reaching up to 60 percent off, there’s plenty worth shopping across the brand’s clothing and accessories. But I wouldn’t wait too long to pull the trigger. Popular sizes and colors have a way of disappearing during big sales, and there’s no telling which of these deals will still be around once Black Friday officially arrives.
