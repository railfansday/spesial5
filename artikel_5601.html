Los Angeles-Based Alaris Becomes First U.S.-Founded Skincare Brand Backed by Bloomage Biotech

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

LOS ANGELES, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Alaris has secured an investment from Bloomage Biotechnology Corporation (SSE: 688363), a global biotechnology company and the world's largest hyaluronic acid producer, becoming the first U.S.-founded skincare brand it has backed. Bloomage Biotech also supplies most of Alaris' ingredients, including hyaluronic acid. Terms were not disclosed.

Co-founder Cherrie Rao, former head of global PR and brand communications at Florasis, took the makeup brand's story to international recognition. Co-founder Chelle Zeng, a creator-economy strategist with 400,000 followers of her own, led social commerce at Pixi and Sacheu. The brand launched Oct. 16.

"Most people don't need a 10-step routine. They need a few products that earn their place," said Rao.

Both are formulated with sensitive skin in mind, not tested on animals and priced at $20 per box of four. By year-end, Alaris will add Peptide Glow Oil Serum, Ectoin Jelly Mist and Rose PDRN Firming Balm Stick.

"Bloomage Biotech has spent over two decades advancing hyaluronic acid science," said Yizheng Li, director, vice president and board secretary of Bloomage Biotech. "The U.S. is an important market for us, and Alaris brings our research to American shoppers through founders who know this market. We are proud to be its partner from day one."

Alaris is a Los Angeles-based skincare brand built on "From Soil to Science," pairing Himalayan black goji and other botanicals with science from investor and ingredient partner Bloomage Biotech. Available at alarisbeauty.com and TikTok Shop. Instagram: @alaris.beauty. TikTok: @alarisbeauty.

Founded in 2000, Bloomage Biotechnology Corporation (SSE: 688363) is a global biotechnology company powered by synthetic biology. Bloomage pioneered the biomanufacturing of hyaluronic acid and has long held the world's No. 1 market share in the ingredient. It runs one of the world's largest synthetic biology manufacturing platforms and spans raw materials, pharmaceuticals, medical aesthetics, nutrition and dermatology.
