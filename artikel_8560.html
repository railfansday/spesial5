Get the Prime Business World Elite Mastercard® if you already pay for Prime and your business buys heavily from Amazon or AWS. It pays 5% on those purchases, up to $150,000 a year, with no annual fee. Elsewhere, only your three biggest of its 15 categories earn 2%, and most other purchases earn 1%.

U.S. Bank issues this Mastercard to business owners with an eligible Amazon Prime membership, and it replaced the Amazon Business Prime Card from American Express on August 14, 2026. Unlike Chase’s personal Prime Visa, whose 5% at Amazon.com and Whole Foods Market has no yearly cap, this card’s 5% also covers Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Amazon Business but stops at $150,000 a year.

Prime members whose business buys heavily from Amazon or AWS should use it for those purchases, where its 5% beats a flat 2% card by 3 cents on each dollar, on up to $150,000 a year. If your spending that earns 1% runs more than three times your spending that earns 5%, a no-fee card paying a flat 2%, such as the U.S. Bank Business Essentials Card, earns more.
