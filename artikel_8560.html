Join a free store loyalty program only where you already shop, and it pays you back on purchases you were making anyway. Then check how long its rewards last, since some expire in 30 days, and never buy extra just to earn them.

Most of the programs below return 1% to 5% of what you spend, so one purchase you didn’t need can wipe out the rewards. At 1% back, $1,000 of planned shopping earns $10, while a $20 item bought only to unlock a $5 bonus leaves you $15 behind.

The catch in most of these programs is a clock. Some rewards here last 30 days and others a year or more, and a short-lived reward pays only a shopper who comes back in time.

That favors programs that cut the price at the register, especially for an occasional shopper. Target Circle’s member deals come off at checkout with nothing to save up, while a points program pays only once you reach a reward and spend it before it lapses.

All 12 below are free at their basic level, listed with what each pays and its catch.

Target Circle 360, CVS ExtraCare Plus, and a store card’s checkout discount are pitched alongside these programs, but each is a separate decision from the free membership. Every free program also records what you buy, and that record is what you trade for the perks.

Start with the two or three stores where you spent the most last month, join those programs at your next checkout, and note each reward’s expiration date the day it arrives.
