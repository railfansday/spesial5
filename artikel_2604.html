Christian McCaffrey puts 49ers’ loss of ‘true warrior’ in perspective

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Christian McCaffrey had the closest view of anyone on the 49ers roster when Colton McKivitz suffered a gruesome injury that ended his season during Sunday night's 35-10 victory against the Vikings in Mexico City.

On Monday, McCaffrey posted a photo of himself consoling the 49ers' right tackle on his Instagram story, sharing his gratitude.

49ers Colton McKivitz seen carted off the field in Sunday's game against the Vikings. AFP via Getty Images

"A true warrior. A staple of this team. Love you brother

@cmckivitz53," McCaffrey captioned the photo of him hugging McKivitz after the injury occurred.

McKivitz was seen landing on top of McCaffrey in an awkward position on the play that derailed his season.

On Monday, the 49ers announced that he suffered a fractured and dislocated left ankle on the play.

San Francisco 49ers running back Christian McCaffrey rushing down the field during Sunday's game against the Vikings. Getty Images

McCaffrey was one of the many 49ers players who were seen left devastated following the play, as left tackle Trent Williams was seen slamming his helmet on the ground after the injury happened.

Before McKivitz was carted off the field, McCaffrey was one of the last 49ers players to talk to him, and that's when the 49ers running back was consoling the offensive lineman.

49ers' offensive lineman Colton McKivitz was carted off the field during Sunday's game against the Vikings. AP Photo/Eduardo Verdugo

When the game ended, McCaffrey told reporters "[He's one of the heart and souls of this team. He is the standard and we're gonna miss him. We all love him, and we're praying for him," McCaffrey told reporters following the game. "It's one of the worst parts of this game, is stuff like that."

McKivitz is in his seventh year in the NFL and has turned into a franchise right tackle after the 49ers selected him in the fifth round of the 2020 NFL Draft.

This season, he's played a vital part in the success of McCaffrey, and quarterback Brock Purdy has been among the fewest-sacked starting quarterbacks this year.

McCaffrey has run the ball 127 times through 10 games this season and is averaging 4.5 yards per rush with 11 touchdowns.
