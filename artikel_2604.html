Seahawks fan chat: Packers vs. Rams is huge for NFC playoff race

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Little did anyone know Thanksgiving Eve was such a big thing. This is what happens when the NFL wants to be in our lives every waking minute of the fall and winter. We've got a big game that is relevant to the Seattle Seahawks, as the 6-4 Green Bay Packers take on the 6-4 Los Angeles Rams at SoFi Stadium.

If you didn't catch our earlier article, if the Packers win then the Seahawks will be stuck in the No. 8 spot in the NFC regardless of what happens in their Sunday game versus the San Francisco 49ers. If the Rams win, then the Seahawks control their own Wild Card destiny and can leap into a postseason spot with victory in Santa Clara. The one bad combo is a Rams win and a Seahawks loss, which would put Seattle behind both the Rams and Packers heading into December.
