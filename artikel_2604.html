Pair of ex-Seahawks safeties find new teams

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A pair of former Seattle Seahawks safeties have made their way onto practice squads elsewhere, including someone who started on late notice earlier in the season.

According to Tuesday's NFL transaction wire, A.J. Finley signed with the Pittsburgh Steelers practice squad, having been released last week by Seattle. Finley was on injured reserve last season with a torn ACL suffered in training camp, and alternated between active roster and practice squad to start this year. He started in place of the injured Julian Love in Week 3 against the Washington Commanders, played exclusively special teams in Week 4 vs. the Los Angeles Chargers, but hasn't had any playing time since then.

Meanwhile, D'Anthony Bell has joined the Minnesota Vikings practice squad after remaining a free agent after the Seahawks let him go at the end of September. Bell was part of Seattle's PS and active roster throughout 2025 as a special teamer and reserve safety, but when he was waived late in the year, the Carolina Panthers claimed him. Seattle nevertheless gave Bell a Super Bowl ring for his efforts.

Seattle has had Ty Okada and Julian Love starting in tandem since Week 5, with Okada missing a month due to a hamstring injury. Rodney Thomas II has been the main reserve safety throughout the year, with Maxen Hook as additional practice squad depth.
