Who should Bills Mafia root for in Week 11 to keep Buffalo atop the AFC?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

I really don't need to say anything more than that, do I?

But since I know someone is going to try to argue, let's get a few things out of the way.

Don't come at me with the number of backup quarterbacks the Bills have faced. How about we talk about the fact that those quarterback changes have forced Buffalo's defense to adjust its game plan in the middle of a game? And don't come at me with strength of victory, either. A lot of those teams looked pretty strong on paper when the schedule came out.

Joe Brady has had his team ready to play week after week. Some wins have been fun. Some have been downright ugly. But every single one of them counts exactly the same in the standings.

They have the AFC East all but locked up at the midway point of the season, and they're sitting in the AFC's No. 1 seed. Is there still plenty of work to do? Absolutely. Do they control their own destiny? Yes. We know all of that.

But we also know that somewhere along the way, the Bills might have a bad day. And there are other teams whose wins — or losses — can give Buffalo a little breathing room if that happens.

So, let's see who we're rooting for — and against — this week.

_____________________________________________________________________________

____________________________________________________________________________

Miami Dolphins at Buffalo Bills Rooting for: Buffalo Bills

Buffalo gets its first shot at Miami this season, and a win would do more than keep the Bills sitting atop the AFC standings. It would also give them their second division win in as many weeks and put a little more distance between them and the Patriots.

With New England enjoying its bye week, the Bills have a chance to turn their current three-game advantage in the win column into four. That's a pretty nice opportunity heading into Thanksgiving.

New York Jets at Los Angeles ChargersRooting for: New York Jets

I know. I know. We're rooting for the Jets?

But hear me out. At 2-8, New York isn't much of a threat to Buffalo in the AFC East. What the Jets are in contention for, however, is a very high pick in the 2027 NFL Draft. And every loss helps their chances of getting one.

So, as much as we'd normally enjoy watching the Jets lose, maybe it's time they started winning a few games. At this point in the season, with their record, a New York win is really a loss for the Jets.

_____________________________________________________________________________

Indianapolis Colts at Houston Texans Rooting for: Houston Texans

We're going with Houston here for strength of victory (SOV). The Bills beat the Texans earlier this season, so every Houston win makes that victory a little more valuable if Buffalo finds itself needing a tiebreaker down the road.

But there's a consolation prize if Indianapolis wins. Remember, the Colts traded their 2027 first-round draft pick to the Jets. Every Indianapolis win makes that pick a little less valuable for New York.

So, we're rooting for Houston, but we're not going to be too upset if Indianapolis pulls this one off.

Baltimore Ravens at Carolina PanthersRooting for: Baltimore Ravens

The Ravens aren't exactly breathing down Buffalo's neck in the AFC standings, so we're not going to worry too much about giving them another win. Plus, the Bills hold the tiebreaker if they did randomly start a comeback tour.

Buffalo already beat Baltimore this season, which means a Ravens victory helps the Bills' strength of victory. And if Buffalo ends up tied with Kansas City, Denver, or another AFC contender for playoff seeding, we'll be glad we rooted for the Ravens this week.

Tennessee Titans at Dallas Cowboys Rooting for: Tennessee Titans

This one is all about the 2027 NFL Draft. Tennessee currently holds the No. 1 overall pick, and we'd love to see the Titans win enough games to give that spot up.

But there's a catch. The Jets acquired a 2027 first-round pick from Dallas in last year's Quinnen Williams trade. The Cowboys had two first-round picks, their own and Green Bay's, and the Jets get whichever one is higher. That means losses by either Dallas or Green Bay could make New York's pick more valuable.

So, we're sticking with Tennessee here, hoping the Titans can win their way out of the top draft spot. But if Dallas wins, at least there's a consolation prize.

Arizona Cardinals at Kansas City Chiefs Rooting for: Arizona Cardinals

This one shouldn't require much thought. Kansas City is just one game behind Buffalo in the AFC standings, and the Chiefs aren't going to make the race for the No. 1 seed any easier.

An Arizona win would give Kansas City its third loss and provide Buffalo with a little more breathing room at the top of the conference. And with the Bills and Chiefs set to meet in Week 12, a little help from Arizona this week could keep a Thanksgiving letup from becoming a letdown.

Pittsburgh Steelers at Philadelphia Eagles Rooting for: Philadelphia Eagles

Pittsburgh is still very much in the AFC playoff picture, and at 6-3, the Steelers are close enough to Buffalo that another loss would be welcome. The Eagles are in the NFC, so Philadelphia winning doesn't create any additional competition for Buffalo in the conference standings.

Las Vegas Raiders at Denver Broncos Rooting for: Las Vegas Raiders

Denver is another one of those 7-2 teams sitting just behind Buffalo in the AFC standings. A Broncos loss would give the Bills a little more separation from another potential challenger for the No. 1 seed.

And there's a bonus here. Buffalo already beat Las Vegas this season, so a Raiders win would improve the Bills' strength of victory. Denver, meanwhile, only factors into strength of schedule at this point.

A Denver loss and a boost to Buffalo's SOV? We'll take both, please.

Jacksonville Jaguars at New York Giants Rooting for: New York Giants

Jacksonville is right there with Kansas City and Denver at 7-2, which makes this another opportunity for Buffalo to put some distance between itself and the competition.

The Giants have their own problems in the NFC, but none of those are Buffalo's concern. A New York win would hand Jacksonville its third loss and help the Bills hold on to the AFC's top spot.

Cincinnati Bengals at Washington Commanders Rooting for: Washington Commanders

Cincinnati is another 7-2 team chasing Buffalo, and potentially the hottest team in the league right now. We'd certainly appreciate the Commanders cooling the Bengals down.

At this point, every loss handed to one of those teams sitting just behind the Bills is another little bit of insurance in the race for the No. 1 seed. And the road to the Super Bowl running through Orchard Park has a nice ring to it.

Bills Mafia has plenty of teams to root for this week, but the most important result is still the one that will come in Orchard Park. At 8-1, Buffalo controls its own destiny in the race for the AFC's No. 1 seed. Of course, they control it until they don't, and the easiest way to keep it that way is to keep winning.

A little help from around the league wouldn't hurt, especially with Kansas City waiting on Thanksgiving night. But first things first. Let's Squish the Fish on Sunday.
