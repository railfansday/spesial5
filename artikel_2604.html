Jaylen Brown to miss 76ers game Wednesday vs. Magic due to 'hamstring strain'

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Philadelphia 76ers will take on the Orlando Magic on Wednesday night, but without star shooting guard Jaylen Brown.

He is dealing with a hamstring strain and has already been ruled out for tomorrow night's game. Brown picked up the injury during Philadelphia's win over the Brooklyn Nets on Monday.

The guard is in his first season with the 76ers after the infamous trade that sent him to Philadelphia from Boston. Brown has played in 14 games so far this season and is averaging 22.6 points, 6.6 rebounds, and 3.6 assists.

The Philadelphia guard had a slow start with his new team, scoring 19 points in his team debut. The 76ers have tried to find the best fit among Joel Embiid, Tyrese Maxey, Brown, and free-agent signee LeBron James.

It finally seemed to be clicking for Brown ahead of the injury. He logged back-to-back games with 31 points, his season high. The guard finished Monday night's win with a double-double: 31 points and 13 rebounds. He shot just 47.4% from the field, but he made 10 of 11 attempts from the charity stripe as Philadelphia beat Brooklyn 135-123.

Without Brown in the lineup, the 76ers will likely give more minutes to Kentavious Caldwell-Pope and Jabari Walker.

Wednesday's game against Orlando will be Brown's second missed game this season. He also missed Philly's first game of a back-to-back against the New York Knicks.

Brown was dealing with both a head and a hamstring injury coming out of Monday's win against Brooklyn. After the game, he said that he may have to get imaging done on Tuesday. Brown has been diagnosed with a left hamstring strain, but it remains unclear whether it will keep him out beyond Wednesday's game.

Philadelphia has the Magic, Milwaukee Bucks, Portland Trail Blazers, Miami Heat, and Indiana Pacers in its next five games.
