Ezee Fiber Wins Two 2026 Fierce Network Innovation Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

New Mexico fiber expansion named Broadband Build of the Year;

CEO Matt Marino named Innovator of the Year

HOUSTON, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Ezee Fiber won two categories in the 2026 Fierce Network Innovation Awards, a leading telecommunications industry awards program. Its fiber-to-the-home expansion in Albuquerque and Santa Fe was named Broadband Build of the Year, and CEO Matt Marino was named Innovator of the Year.

The awards were announced and presented Nov. 19 during Broadband Nation Expo at the Hilton Riverside in New Orleans. The ceremony marked the first live, in-person presentation of the awards in the program's more than 15-year history.

New Mexico build recognized for measurable community impact

The Broadband Build of the Year category honors completed builds with measurable community results. At the end of 2022, only about 4% of Bernalillo County and 9% of Santa Fe County could buy fiber at home. Ezee Fiber's $250 million New Mexico expansion, announced in 2023, now offers symmetrical residential speeds up to 8 Gbps and enterprise connectivity up to 400 Gbps, with no data caps.

A July 2026 Ezee Fiber-sponsored study by Dr. Brian Whitacre of Oklahoma State University ties roughly 661 local jobs to the rollout from 2021 to 2024 and estimates $77 million in home-value gains through 2025. More than 95% of the company's New Mexico roles are filled locally, and it has invested $98 million in in-state supply chain spending.

After a 2025 stop-work order in Albuquerque, Ezee Fiber worked with city leaders on "neighbor-first" construction protocols that cut construction-related 311 calls by 47% year over year.

"This award belongs to our New Mexico team," said Pedro Checo, chief operating officer of Ezee Fiber. "New Mexico shows what fiber can do when it's built with local people, local partners and a long-term commitment to the community."

Since joining as CEO in 2023, Marino has built Ezee Fiber around the customer experience, pairing fast, reliable fiber with 24/7 local customer service. Under his leadership, that model is expanding from Texas and New Mexico into Washington, Illinois, Oregon and Michigan.

"Together, these awards recognize the commitment of our teams to building better networks, delivering exceptional service and earning the trust of customers and communities," Marino said. "Innovation is not simply about technology. It is about how effectively we deliver on our promises, solve problems and create lasting value for the people we serve."

The recognition marks the inaugural presentation of Fierce Network's Innovator of the Year award and highlights the growing influence of emerging fiber providers in an industry historically dominated by much larger companies.

Hiring and recognition continue as the industry contracts

Fierce Network honors add to a growing list of national industry recognitions for Ezee Fiber. PCMag named the company the best overall internet service provider in the U.S. for 2026 and included it among the Best Gaming ISPs (2025, 2026) and Fastest ISPs in the U.S. (2024, 2025).

Ezee Fiber has also earned 65 Speedtest by Ookla awards. A recent edition of Consumer Reports ranked Ezee Fiber among the nation's top 10 internet providers, with consumers praising its value, pricing and simplicity.

The recognition comes as Ezee Fiber continues to expand its network, customer base and workforce across six states. While several major U.S. telecommunications companies have announced substantial workforce reductions, Ezee Fiber continues to invest in new markets and hire employees in sales, field operations, customer service and other functions. The company employs approximately 800 people and is targeting 200,000 customers and 800,000 homes passed by the end of 2026.
