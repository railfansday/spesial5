Winning in a buyer’s market

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

With inventory rising and pending sales hitting historic lows, today's buyers have leverage they haven't seen in years. Jennifer Beeston breaks down three common client hesitations—high rates, high prices and closing costs—and outlines how agents and lenders can use seller concessions and buydowns to get deals across the finish line.   AI Summary

As we head into the end of the year, we are at a truly magical moment for buyers across much of America.

Buyers have more houses to choose from, along with the ability to inspect the property, negotiate repairs and make homeownership more affordable. It is something buyers have been wanting for years, but where are all the buyers? Pending sales were recently recorded as the lowest since they started tracking pending sales!

Currently, many buyers are holding back for three main reasons. As a mortgage lender or real estate agent, knowing these reasons and how to solve them is the key to helping your buyers win right now. Would I recommend waiting for lower rates?  Absolutely not; the lower the rates, the more competition, and it is the lack of competition where true opportunity lies.

Ask the seller to pay to buy down the mortgage rate.  I like a permanent buydown as it gives certainty for the long run. Many buyers can afford the house when the rate starts at 6%, so use a seller buydown to get there.

Offer less. Many buyers do not know that you can offer less. They are waiting for the seller to drop the price when, in reality, they should just write an offer now with what they want to pay.  It is up to the seller to say yes or no.

Whether the seller agrees depends on their financial situation and their motivation to sell. I have chatted with clients who want the house, but not at the current price and are afraid to offer $10,000 less. To me, that’s a missed opportunity.  The seller could be sitting on the other side praying they get an offer.  Write the offer.

Did you know many buyers don’t realize they can ask the seller to pay for closing costs? Over 42% of recent sales have seller concessions, but many buyers still do not know you can ask for this. You could have a buyer you talked to last year who was in a seller’s market, who is saving away, who does not even know that their market is now a buyer’s market. This is your opportunity as a real estate professional to help them get home. Ask the seller to pay their closing costs and help them get home for the Holidays.

As I type this, we have buyers across the country negotiating for lower sales prices, closing costs and rate buydown covered by the seller. Does every seller say yes?  No, but that is the beauty of the buyer’s market; there are so many sellers to choose from. There is tremendous opportunity in the market right now for buyers to win. Help them seize it.
