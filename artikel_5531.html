Nvidia stock slips as board probes Jensen Huang's ties to suspected Chinese spy

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nvidia's board said it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised as Huang's ties to the suspected spy have roiled the company

Samuel Boivin / NurPhoto via Getty Images

Nvidia $NVDA's board of directors launched an independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang on Sunday, and the company's stock fell in premarket trading on Monday.

Nvidia stock was down about 5% to 6% in premarket trading. A committee of independent directors, assisted by outside independent counsel, will oversee the inquiry, the company said.

The board said the investigation follows recent reporting that Huang had been singled out by a foreign intelligence effort. It said it has seen nothing to suggest that national security or Nvidia's confidential information was put at risk, and that the inquiry will examine all available information. The board said it stands behind Huang and his leadership team, and that Huang backs the review and has pledged to cooperate.

Huang acknowledged an affair in a message to employees. "I was unfaithful to my wife. My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry," Huang wrote.

Reports identify the woman as Alice Huang, a former executive at Megaspeed International whom U.S. intelligence agencies suspect of spying for China. Alice Huang is no relation to the Nvidia CEO. A memo passed around Washington policy circles earlier in 2026 alleged that China's Ministry of State Security had enlisted her to gather intelligence on Nvidia's CEO.

China's Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun said on Monday that the "so-called Chinese spy narrative" was unsubstantiated, according to CNBC.

President Donald Trump said on Sunday that Huang is "an honorable guy" who has done "an amazing job for our country." Trump has said Huang should stay on as CEO.
