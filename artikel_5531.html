Panduit to Showcase Integrated AI and Data Center Infrastructure Solutions at SC26

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Interactive booth demonstrations will feature Direct-to-Chip cooling, high-density fiber connectivity, power distribution, and infrastructure management for next-generation computing environments

TINLEY PARK, Ill., Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Panduit, a global leader in innovative electrical and network infrastructure, will present its portfolio of technologies for HPC and AI environments at SC26 (Supercomputing 2026), The International Conference for High Performance Computing, Networking, Storage, and Analysis, Nov. 15 â 20 at McCormick Place in Chicago. At booth #4000, Panduit will demonstrate how power, cooling, connectivity, resiliency, and visibility work together to help organizations scale next-generation computing environments.

As HPC and AI workloads drive greater compute capacity, power consumption, and density, the physical systems supporting them have become increasingly critical. Organizations must manage rising thermal loads, deliver reliable power, accommodate high-density connectivity, and maintain visibility into essential equipment. Panduit helps address these interconnected challenges with an integrated portfolio spanning cabinets designed for Direct-to-Chip (DTC) cooling solutions, fiber connectivity, power distribution, and rack-level monitoring and management.

"AI and HPC are raising the demands placed on the physical infrastructure that supports computing environments," said Tom Kelly, CTO, Panduit. "At SC26, Panduit is demonstrating how power, cooling, connectivity, and infrastructure management work collectively to help customers meet today's requirements and adapt as compute capacity and power density continue to grow."
