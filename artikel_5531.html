‘Landman’ Season 3 Is Filming at Taylor Sheridan’s 600-Acre Texas Ranch—Which He Previously Charged Paramount $50K a Week To Use

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Actor and director Taylor Sheridan has created an impressive slate of ranch-themed TV shows, but what is perhaps more remarkable is that some of them have been filmed on his very own properties.

The “Yellowstone” creator, 56, boasts an extraordinary property empire that’s estimated to be worth more than $600 million. And one of his most recognizable properties—the 600-acre Bosque Ranch near Fort Worth, TX—will reportedly be a filming location in Season 3 of the Sheridan-produced show “Landman.”

One of the series’ cast members, musician Clayton Q, confirmed the plans in a Nov. 6 Instagram post.

“Hey, y’all, Clayton Q here. I’m here at the world-famous Bosque Ranch right here in Weatherford, TX,” he said in the video. “Taylor Sheridan’s horse ranch. We got a lot of stuff going on. I’m on location today.”

Season 3 of the series began filming in October and is expected to premiere in mid-2027. It comes at a slight delay, as the season was green-lit in December 2025.

The Bosque Ranch Headquarters website confirms the legendary spot has been used to film a handful of projects, including “Yellowstone,” “Lawman: Bass Reeves,” and “Lioness.”

According to the Wall Street Journal, Sheridan had reportedly charged Paramount $50,000 a week to film at the ranch. His cattle was rented in herds, priced at $25 a cow.

Sheridan snapped up the property in 2013 for a whopping $264 million. At the time, the ranch served as a home base for his family, who had relocated to the Lone Star State from Los Angeles, where he previously worked as an actor on shows such as “Veronica Mars” and “Sons of Anarchy.”

Today, the home remains Sheridan’s primary residence, although he and his wife, actress-model Nicole Muirbrook, also spend time with their son at another property they own in Texas—the Four Sixes Ranch he purchased for $320 million in 2022.

“The main headquarters where we live is more of a horse ranch, because I’m pretty deep into the horse deal. And then the ranch out by Jacksboro is more where we run the cattle,” he explained in a 2020 interview.

Sheridan also has a $4.95 million ranch in Wyoming’s Star Valley, which he reportedly acquired in August 2024. The 179-acre homestead is known as Papa’s Creek Ranch and is located about 65 miles from Jackson Hole.

Despite his jaw-dropping portfolio, Sheridan hasn’t always been well-off financially. In an August 2026 interview with Town and Country magazine, he recalled a time when he was down on his luck.

“I was homeless twice,” he said. “I lived in my car. Sometimes I’d go to Ojai, just somewhere where I could safely sleep. I did that for six months.”

The multihyphenate noted that the struggle was technically his own choice.

“I could have decided at any point, ‘I’m going to get a full-time job and I’m going to give up this dream,’” he said of his acting career. “And I chose to sleep in my car over that.”

However, when he became more successful, after his stint on “Sons of Anarchy,” he developed a strategy to make sure he could solidly stand on his own two feet from there on out.

After turning to screenwriting, he sold his first screenplay for a drama called “Hell or High Water.” Afterward, he “bought a little house on an acre on a river in Wyoming.”

“We moved there, and I wrote a check for it,” he said. “Then, as I would start to have more success, I would buy a little bit more land beside it and get a couple horses. I’m still so averse to having to ever owe anybody anything.”

Sheridan explained that now that he’s built a career tied so deeply to ranching, he can avoid Los Angeles, a city he disliked living in.
