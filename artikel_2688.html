Rams Named Potential Suitor for $108 Million Wide Receiver

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Los Angeles Rams and head coach Sean McVay entered the 2026 NFL season as Super Bowl favorites to many. What has transpired since has been a nightmare.

No, the Rams are not out of playoff contention. In fact, they currently hold a 5-4 record entering Week 10 action against the Arizona Cardinals.

What has caused the nightmare if they are still very much alive as a contender in the NFC? Injuries have completely derailed the season that they were supposed to have.

Myles Garrett is back, but he has missed most of the season due to a knee issue. Davante Adams is currently out with a ruptured hamstring. Most recently, Puka Nacua went down and is expected to miss extended time due to a knee injury suffered on the final play of last week's win.

At wide receiver, things have gotten bad. It may not help this season, but Los Angeles has been linked as a potential suitor for a $108 million wideout during the offseason.

Oct 12, 2026; Inglewood, California, USA; Los Angeles Rams head coach Sean McVay reacts during the fourth quarter against the Buffalo Bills at SoFi Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Jayne Kamin-Oncea-Imagn Images

Bleacher Report has released a new column looking ahead at the top free agents of the upcoming offseason. When it came to Parker Washington, they listed the Rams as one of the top potential landing spots for the Jacksonville Jaguars' impending free agent.

Not only did they named Los Angeles as a team to watch, they also projected that Washington will receive a four-year, $108 million contract in free agency.

"While a lack of elite size may keep Washington out of the upper tier of receiver contracts, the 24-year-old should command a deal that falls somewhere between those signed by Wan'Dale Robinson and Alec Pierce this past offseason," they wrote.

Washington would be a huge addition for the Rams. With Davante Adams set to become a free agent as well, he would make perfect sense as a long-term replacement.

Back in 2025, Washington had his breakout season with 58 receptions for 847 yards and five touchdowns. In eight games this year, he has racked up 29 receptions for 493 yards and five scores.

Washington is just 24 years old right now. He's going to be one of the most sought after playmakers in free agency. Watch out for Los Angeles as a potential suitor.
