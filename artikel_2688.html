Grizzlies reveal Zach Edey, Isaiah Stewart's playing status vs Wolves

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Injuries have caused the Memphis Grizzlies to shake up their lineups and rotations drastically the past two weeks. All three of the players who started the season playing power forward are out, and the center position entered a pivotal NBA Cup matchup against the Minnesota Timberwolves with two players listed as questionable.

Grizzlies center Zach Edey (left ankle soreness) will play against the Timberwolves on Nov. 27 (7:30 p.m CT, DAZN-MEM). Isaiah Stewart (right hip soreness) is out.

Stewart and Edey were added to the injury report a day before the game.

The Grizzlies and Wolves are playing in a winner-take-all NBA Cup matchup in West Group B.

Edey left a Nov. 25 win against the Los Angeles Clippers after the first half due to left ankle soreness. He has had two surgeries on his left ankle in the previous 18 months.

Memphis is already without Cam Boozer (right medial ankle sprain), Jerami Grant (left calf strain), GG Jackson (left foot fracture) and Scotty Pippen Jr. (lumbar disc herniation).

The Timberwolves are also battling key injuries entering this matchup. Anthony Edwards (right knee anthroscopy) and LaMelo Ball (left ankle sprain) are both out for Minnesota.
