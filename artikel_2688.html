How to watch Warriors vs. Pelicans

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Golden State Warriors bring their road trip to the Bayou on Monday night as they get set to play against the New Orleans Pelicans. Tip-off is scheduled for 5:00 PM PT in New Orleans and can be watched on NBC Sports Bay Area.

Last Friday night's NBA Cup matchup against the San Antonio Spurs will be one the Warriors are hoping to forget about moving forward. Playing their first game without Stephen Curry after he suffered an oblique injury earlier in the week, Golden State struggled mightily right from the opening tip, falling behind 18-0 before finally scoring their first points six minutes into the game. Ultimately, facing the reigning Western Conference champions while shorthanded proved too much to overcome as the Warriors suffered a 123-85 blowout loss.

Rookie Yaxel Lendeborg, who has endeared himself to Dub Nation with his honest, unfiltered interviews, didn't shy away from taking accountability for his own performance following Friday's loss.

The good news for Golden State is that Monday's matchup against the Pelicans should be the perfect opportunity to bounce back and snap their two-game losing streak. New Orleans has lost six consecutive games, including a 51-point blowout at the hands of the Warriors last week. However, the Pelicans are expected to have All-Star Zion Williamson back in the lineup after he missed the previous meeting between these two teams. Williamson leads New Orleans in scoring with 21.9 points per game, though the team is still expected to be without another key offensive piece in Trey Murphy III, who continues to deal with a knee injury.

With Curry still sidelined, look for Lendeborg to lead the way offensively as the Warriors attempt to get back into the win column. The rookie will also be sporting a new haircut heading into tonight's game, hoping that a fresh look will lead to a bounce-back performance after Friday's struggles.

Enjoy the game Dub Nation. GO WARRIORS!!!

Warriors: Brandin Podziemski, De'Anthony Melton, Will Richard, Yaxel Lendeborg, Draymond Green

Pelicans: Jeremiah Fears, Herbert Jones, Saddiq Bey, Zion Williamson, Yves Missi

Who: Golden State Warriors (8 – 5) vs. New Orleans Pelicans (4 – 10)

When: Monday, November 16th, at 5:00 p.m. PT

Where: Smoothie King Center — New Orleans, Louisiana
