Purdy, McCaffrey lead 49ers offense in win vs Cowboys: winners, losers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The San Francisco 49ers offense was just about unstoppable against the Dallas Cowboys in their Week 10 matchup.

The Niners put up 584 yards against the Cowboys on the way to a 46-40 win on Nov. 15 at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas.

San Francisco quarterback Brock Purdy had another MVP-like performance, throwing for a career-high 389 yards, completing 22-of-28 passes for three touchdowns. But he wasn't the only one to notch a career-high. Niners running back Christian McCaffrey had his best day as a receiver catching four passes for a 161 yards (career-high) and two touchdowns. Deebo Samuel Sr. had 109 total yards and two touchdowns on the day.

The Cowboys defense pulled a no-show, but their offense wasn't too shabby. Dallas gained 439 total yards on offense as quarterback Dak Prescott tossed five touchdowns on 30-for-51 passes for 343 yards. Most of their damage was caused by George Pickens, who went off for 11 catches, 145 yards and three touchdowns.

The 49ers defense fended off the Cowboys when it mattered to advance to 8-1 on the season. Their Week 11 matchup is against the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City on Nov. 22, while the Cowboys face the the Tennessee Titans ahead of their annual Thanksgiving Day game.

49ers quarterback continues to have a scorching-hot 2026 season, leading San Francisco to the best record in the NFC. Against the Cowboys, in his first appearance at AT&T Stadium, Purdy didn't disappoint. Matter of fact, he went out for a career-high in passing yards with 389 in the win. He remains undefeated against the Cowboys, now 4-0, including the postseason.

Purdy put some good clips on the tape against the Cowboys. One play he danced in the pocket before escaping, extending the play, to find Christian McCaffrey across the middle who took it 74 yards for a score in the second quarter.

In the third quarter, the Niners drew up an end around play that saw Deebo Samuel Sr. out in the open field with Purdy as a lead blocker. He did just enough for Samuel to flip into a 70-yard touchdown.

Christian McCaffrey is one of the best dual threat running backs of all-time. He showed why he's a cheat code against the Cowboys. He had three total touchdowns. On the ground, he went for 13 carries, 62 yards and a touchdown. McCaffrey had a career-high 161 receiving yards, including a 74-yard score which was the longest catch-and-run in his career.

He also scored his eighth consecutive rushing touchdown, passing Joe Dorsey's record that spanned across the 1953-54 seasons. He's been a valuable asset to the Niners offense providing them with a dependable touchdown machine out the backfield.

Niners rookie Kaelon Black is making the most of his touches out the backfield sharing carries with McCaffrey. Black had a solid outing against the Cowboys going 11 carries, 53 yards and a touchdown.

His 25-yard rushing touchdown was arguably the run of the game, maybe the week. Black found the endzone but not before running through a couple of Cowboys defenders along the way. It was the type of smash mouth football that you love to see from your second-string back, let alone a rookie.

He has 79 carries for 356 yards and two touchdowns in his first season. He averages 4.5 yards per carry.

When it comes to some of the best fans, and well-traveled many think of the Raiders, Cowboys, Steelers and Packers. However, you have to mention the 49ers' fanbase, at least you do after how they showed up in Arlington, Texas. Members of the FOX broadcast mentioned how loud, and present, the 49ers fans were at AT&T Stadium. It sounded like they were playing at Levi's Stadium.

If there's any place of concern after the Niners' win against the Cowboys, it's in their secondary. The caveat is that their starting cornerbacks were questionable to play going into the game. However, both stuck it out. Renardo Green finished with seven total tackles while Deommodore Lenoir had five tackles and four pass deflections.

Cowboys quarterback Dak Prescott threw for 343 yards, but wide receiver George Pickens went off for 11 catches, 145 yards and three touchdowns. CeeDee Lamb had a rather quiet game, but still effective with seven catches, 72 yards and a touchdown.

Lenoir took responsibility after the game saying, "I got to play better. They paid me to make plays."

The Cowboys defense took the biggest "L" of the day. They were historically bad, allowing the 49ers to go for 584 total yards. It was the most yards allowed at AT&T Stadium. The 49ers scored three touchdowns of at least 60 yards from scrimmage, which tied for the most by a team in a game over the last 50 years, according to the Elias Sports Bureau.
