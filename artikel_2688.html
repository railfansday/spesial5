Prep basketball roundup: Sherman Oaks Notre Dame holds off Palisades in high-scoring opener

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

As opening games go in high school basketball, Tuesday night's matchup between preseason No. 2-ranked Sherman Oaks Notre Dame and No. 12 Palisades produced a game that proved both teams are headed for lots of victories this season.

In the end, Notre Dame, despite playing much of the second half without standout guard NaVorro Bowman (shoulder injury) held on for a 94-87 victory. The teams combined to have nine players score in double figures and make 26 threes.

Junior guard Zion Lanier, who has been earning rave reviews at Notre Dame practices from coach Matt Sargeant, scored a career-high 26 points. "I had a meeting before the season with coach," he said. "I had to adjust to a new role and get out of my shell."

Bowman had 18 points before leaving the game in the third quarter. His arm was in a sling. Ilan Nikolov made four threes in the third quarter and finished with 18 points. Sophomore Nico Mecilli came off the bench to score a career-high 16 points and Josiah Nance had 14 points. Notre Dame made 15 threes.

Palisades, which was down 18 points at halftime, made a determined second-half comeback and left little doubt that it will be an overwhelming favorite to defend its City Section Open Division title. Sophomore guard Phil Reed scored 15 of his 26 points in third quarter. EJ Popoola had 20 points, OJ Popoola 18 and Jack Levey 17.

The Dolphins have aspirations of challenging top Southern Section teams, and Tuesday's performance sent a clear message they can do it. They play St. Francis on Saturday.

"We dug ourselves a hole," Palisades coach Jeff Bryant said. "We made proper adjustments and fought back in the second half and came up short. That was a shootout. That's why we schedule these games to see where we're at."

St. John Bosco 83, Newport Harbor 27: David Conerly led the way with 16 points and Chace Patterson had 14 points.

Milken 79, St. Monica 63: Grayson Coleman started his senior season with a 35-point performance.

Harvard-Westlake 93, St. Genevieve 35: Alex Floyd scored 20 points, Nairobi Ebi 17 and Pierce Thompson 15 for the Wolverines, who also received seven assists from Cole Holden.

Anaheim Canyon 78, South Torrance 50: Tolu Akomolafe made seven threes and finished with 27 points.

Damien 81, Torrance 39: Zaire Rasshan had 22 points for Damien (2-0).

Etiwanda 64, Rancho Verde 45: Devin Mitchell had 34 points for the Eagles.

Village Christian 107, El Camino Real 39: Sophomore Will Conroy Jr. had 30 points and 11 rebounds and Deion Lewis added 24 points for the Crusaders.

Sherman Oaks Notre Dame 73, Palisades 29: Sophomores Alexa Castillo (15 points) and Elsie Hodge (12 points) led the Knights. Freshman Cali Bowman scored 12 points in her high school debut. She's the sister of boys' star NaVorro Bowman.

Oaks Christian 74, Oxnard 47: Presley Kushner scored 28 points and Bonita Liu added 23 points.
