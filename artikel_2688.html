Forbes To Host Inaugural SportsMoney Event Exploring The Intersection Of Sports, Business And Wealth With Josh Harris & Rich Paul

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

NEW YORK (November 19, 2026) - Forbes today announced it will host its inaugural Forbes SportsMoney: An Evening at the Intersection of Sports, Business & Wealth on December 3 in New York City. The exclusive, invitation-only gathering will bring together leading athletes, investors, team owners and business executives to discuss the evolving sports economy, including the growing influence of private capital, athlete entrepreneurship and new opportunities across the industry.

As sports franchise valuations continue to rise and investment in the industry expands, the business of sports is entering a new era. Building on Forbes' longstanding authority in sports business, valuations and wealth, the event will set the stage for an exciting year ahead for the expanded Forbes SportsMoney platform. Through intimate conversations with influential leaders across the industry, the evening will offer unique perspectives on the opportunities shaping the future of sports.

Josh Harris, Founder, 26North, Founder and Managing General Partner Harris Blitzer Sports & Entertainment and Managing Partner Washington Commanders, Philadelphia 76ers & New Jersey Devils and Rich Paul, Founder & CEO, Klutch Sports Group will participate in exclusive fireside chats discussing how team ownership, private investment and the growing influence of athletes are reshaping the sports business landscape and creating new opportunities for investors and entrepreneurs.

To learn more about this event, visit 2026 Forbes SportsMoney: An Evening at the Intersection of Sports, Business & Wealth.

For sponsorship inquiries, please reach out to Megan Hennessey at mhennessey@forbes.com.
