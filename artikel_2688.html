Steelers host former Chiefs cornerback for workout

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Pittsburgh Steelers are hosting former Kansas City Chiefs cornerback Nazeeh Johnson for a workout on Tuesday, per Jordan Schultz.

Johnson was drafted in the seventh round of the 2022 NFL Draft by the Chiefs out of Marshall. He appeared in 11 games as a rookie, but missed all of 2023 with an injury and was placed on IR in July. He was part of the Chiefs' 2024 Super Bowl-winning team, appearing in 16 games and registering 56 tackles and three pass breakups. He found himself on IR again ahead of the 2025 season and appeared in just two games. He signed with the Tennessee Titans this past offseason, but was suspended for the first six games of the year due to violating the NFL's policy against PEDs, and he was waived in early September.

The Steelers have an obvious need at cornerback after Ja'Marr Chase torched their secondary for 231 receiving yards. Should the Steelers add Johnson to the roster, it could be the beginning of the end for Asante Samuel Jr, who was credited with giving up five receptions for 135 yards against Cincinnati, per Next Gen Stats.
