Mike McCarthy doesn't rule out internal discipline for DK Metcalf

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Steelers receiver DK Metcalf had an unfortunate stat line on Sunday.

One catch, two yards, two personal fouls, one ejection.

After the 19-16 loss to the Eagles on Sunday, Steelers coach Mike McCarthy addressed the possibility of internal discipline for Metcalf.

“We can’t have players getting ejected from games,” McCarthy told reporters.

Last year in Detroit, Metcalf inexplicably wasn’t ejected after an altercation with a fan. He was later suspended by the league.

And the Steelers opted not to void his guarantees and release him after the season. Given his production in 2026 (35 catches and 432 yards in nine games), the Steelers probably wish they had.

They would have saved $25 million. They also would have paid him $35 million for one season, after giving up a second-round pick in the trade that brought him from Seattle to Pittsburgh.
