Former Commanders DL Jonathan Allen on ‘emotional’ return to Washington

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jonathan Allen played his first eight NFL seasons in Washington. The 2017 first-round NFL draft pick went to two Pro Bowls. He was a Walter Payton Man of the Year nominee. He was a solid interior pass rusher and run defender, and that got him paid, to the tune of $72 million over the four years following his rookie contract.

Even the good professional relationships between team and player eventually come to an end, and Washington cut Allen before the 2025 campaign. It was time, and both sides have moved on.

He played the Commanders as a Viking last season, but Monday night marks his first return to Washington after all those years here.

"It was of those things where you try not to make too much of it before the game plays," Allen said during a Wednesday appearance on the Up and Adams Show with Kay Adams. "Obviously, it's going to be emotional come game time. I'm excited to see a lot of guys I was with the first eight years of my career and guys who helped me become the player I am today.

"But, that was in the past. I've moved on to a new chapter. And I love those guys and I wouldn't change (the experience) for the world but I'm excited to be where I'm at. For me, it's just trying to keep everything the same, not be too high to low, just be consistent and go out there and play a great game from the first quarter through the fourth."

Adams followed up with guys he's looking forward to seeing on game day. He included strength coach Chad Engelhart, who arrived in Washington in 2017, same as Allen. The Alabama product would spend winters in Washington and they would spend tons of time together.

"We've all got those one or two guys that we're super close with," Allen said, "and he's definitely that one for me."
