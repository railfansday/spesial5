Yahoo Fantasy Football Expert League Recap: Jayden Daniels' MNF heroics lift Winks to thrilling Week 11 win

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

If you're unfamiliar with the Yahoo Fantasy Football Expert League, let's fill you in a bit before we go over Week 11. Yahoo Fantasy houses some of the leading analysts in the industry, and we were fortunate enough to get all of them in a league for the 2026 NFL season. To keep you up to date on things, we'll be providing weekly recaps on all the action. Let's get into it.

Here's a look at the standings as we hit the Thanksgiving week slate:

Here's a look at the Yahoo Expert League standings after Week 11.

It was a good week for a few bottom-dwellers in the standings, but let's focus on the top first.

First-place Hayden Winks was given quite the scare by Ray Garvin in their matchup, before Winks escaped with a 114.06-112.86 victory. Winks entered Monday night trailing in the matchup but with Jayden Daniels and Samaje Perine left to play. The contest was decided on the final drive, when Daniels hit Antonio Williams for back-to-back gains equaling 64 passing yards, enough to push Winks ahead by a hair. It also helped that Perine was able to hit 11.7 fantasy points, thanks mostly to a 4-71-0 receiving line.

The win keeps Winks in first place at 8-3 going into Thanksgiving week. The close loss for Garvin could sink his playoff chances with three more matchups to go before the postseason begins in Week 15.

The other two marquee matchups from Week 11 were Matt Harmon versus Scott Pianowski and Justin Boone versus Eric Edholm, with all four managers entering the week in playoff position.

Pianowski keeps finding ways to win, and stashing Emmett Johnson has paid off big time for the Yahoo analyst. This matchup was decided on Sunday Night Football with Harmon rostering Kirk Cousins and Pianowski Brock Purdy. The latter was able to post 22.2 points while Cousins only had 8.4. After the loss, Harmon is now at 6-5 and outside the playoff picture in seventh place.

Boone and Edholm played to another low-scoring affair. But when you get 28 points from your kicker and D/ST, you're usually going to be in good shape. That was the case for Boone, who won the matchup 97.2-89.8. Boone got most of his points from the SNF game with George Kittle, Jordan Mason, Deebo Samuel Sr. and T.J. Hockenson in action. With Quinshon Judkins and Zach Charbonnet on bye, Edholm had to turn to Rachaad White, who laid an egg on MNF despite being active.

The hottest squad in the Expert League belongs to Joel Smyth, who has ripped off four wins in a row to get to 6-5 and has the second-most points in the league en route to a fifth-place standing. It was a tough draw for Josh Norris, who had a decent showing with 109.36 points, which would have outscored six other managers. Unfortunately, Norris couldn't withstand Kenneth Walker III being out again and DK Metcalf being ejected against the Eagles.

Eric Froton and Dan Titus topped the Week 11 scoreboard, each manager improving to 4-7. It just goes to show you that, even after a slow start, you can still salvage a season. Titus opened the season with five straight losses but has gone 4-2 over the past six. Froton started 1-4 and is still kicking. Both managers are still in the playoff hunt, two games back with three to go.
