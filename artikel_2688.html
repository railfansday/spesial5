Rovers striker Gudjohnsen scores hat-trick for Iceland

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Blackburn Rovers striker Andri Gudjohnsen scored a hat-trick as Iceland thrashed Bulgaria 5-0 in their Nations League tie in Plovdiv on Friday night.

The 24-year-old opened the scoring for his country with a 17th-minute penalty before adding two more goals in the second half.

The treble takes Gudjohnsen's total to 15 goals for Iceland in 44 appearances, having made his debut in 2021.

Iceland are top of their League C group, two points clear of Estonia and complete their campaign at home to Luxembourg on Monday, 16 November (17:00 GMT) knowing a win will promote them to League B.
