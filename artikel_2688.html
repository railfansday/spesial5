Thursday Night Football: Sauce Gardner is officially inactive vs. Texans

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It’s official: Colts cornerback Sauce Gardner will not play Thursday night.

Gardner is among the team’s inactives for tonight’s game against the Texans.

The Colts had already ruled out cornerback Charvarius Ward (knee).

They have Justin Walley, Kenneth Harris, Kapena Gushiken, Johnathan Edwards and Natrone Brooks at the position against Nico Collins, Tank Dell and company.

The Colts’ other inactives are quarterback Riley Leonard, tight end Will Mallory (hamstring), left tackle Bernhard Raimann (concussion), right tackle Jalen Travis (knee) and defensive lineman Jerry Tillery (calf).

The Texans won’t have offensive lineman Wyatt Teller, who was questionable with a back injury. He is among their inactives.

Their other inactives are running back Woody Marks (concussion), wide receiver Marvin Harrison Jr. (hamstring), offensive lineman Nate Thomas, offensive lineman Jake Andrews, defensive end Danielle Hunter (hamstring) and tight end Brevin Jordan (foot/illness).

The Texans do get running back David Montgomery back in the lineup, though, as his groin injury is finally healed.
