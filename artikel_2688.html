Saints’ Tyler Shough highlights 1 thing New Orleans offense lacked in crushing Panthers loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Tyler Shough addressed one of the big issues plaguing the New Orleans Saints offense after their 20-17 loss to the Carolina Panthers on Sunday. Though Shough has shown the ability to deliver in crucial situations this season, the latest loss exposed several areas where he and the Saints offense continue to struggle.

Following the loss, Shough spoke on the Saints' offensive struggles.

"It was a lack of execution, some of those critical moments as players," Shough said.

Shough also mentioned a lack of attention to detail as a key reason behind the disappointing performance.

The Saints struggled to protect possession throughout the game, committing three turnovers. Their rushing attack also failed to provide much support, finishing with just 49 yards on 23 carries.

As for Shough, he completed 25 of his 35 passing attempts for 187 yards and one touchdown while throwing an interception. He also contributed 25 rushing yards on six carries but lost a fumble against the Panthers.

Despite earning recognition for his progress this season, Shough once again failed to reach the 200-yard passing mark. Sunday's game marked the third time he has finished below that threshold this season.

Saints head coach Kellen Moore was also asked to assess Shough's performance, and he expressed confidence in the quarterback.

"He's playing fine," Moore said. "He's battling."

Moore took responsibility for some of the team's offensive shortcomings, pointing to his own play-calling while acknowledging that New Orleans has found too many ways to hurt itself.

Shough finished Sunday's game with more turnovers than touchdowns, something that has happened only twice this season. While the Saints' inconsistent offense has made life difficult for the quarterback, Shough's struggles with routine throws have also affected the team's pass catchers, limiting their opportunities to make plays.

The Saints have now fallen to 3-6 overall and face a challenging three-game stretch. New Orleans will begin that stretch with a game against Chicago before facing the Cincinnati Bengals. The Saints will then return home to take on the Green Bay Packers.
