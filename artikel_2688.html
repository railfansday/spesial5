Bucks Reacts Survey Results: It’s a mixed bag, but Bucks fans are still having fun

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

You almost wouldn't know the Milwaukee Bucks are in the midst of their worst season in what feels like forever. That's right, despite the Bucks collecting Ls as if they're Ash Ketchum trying to fill out his Pokédex, fans are riding the ups and downs of this season. In fact, it seems a lot of Bucks fans have found their proverbial opium (sorry, Karl).

Of course, the timing might've helped a bit—the poll ran after the Bucks beat the Toronto Raptors but closed before they lost to the 3-13 (at the time) Brooklyn Nets. And we caught resident hoop-head naveed2000s on his birthday. Hurrah!

A plurality of voters (38%) consider the season a "mixed bag," which is completely understandable. As I wrote in the initial survey, while the losing sucks and some players have underwhelmed, most notably the last two years' second-ranked players in our Ranking the Roster series, Myles Turner and Ryan Rollins, there has also been plenty of positivity. Ironically, considering all of his blunders, much of this can be attributed to Jon Horst's drafting of Brayden Burries and Nate Ament, as well as the play of his acquisitions, including Tyler Herro and Kasparas Jakučionis. Yes, it's a complex relationship.

Complexities aside, 37% of fans are having a good time this season, which, in all honesty, is a pretty darned good place to be, all things considered. Digging deeper, 30% are "enjoying it," while 7% are either hardcore Bucks sickos (guilty) or have legitimately been struck by Cupid's arrow. Either way, they're loving it.

One final thought: prior to opening night, fans were overwhelmingly more excited about this season than last (a 3:1 ratio). Now, with 37% of fans vibing, 25% of fans not enjoying it, and 38% somewhere in between, things appear to be changing. What then, outside of the obvious (win), will help lift your spirits?
