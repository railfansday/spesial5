CB Nazeeh Johnson visited the Steelers Tuesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Cornerback Nazeeh Johnson is trying to find a way back into the NFL.

According to multiple reports, Johnson visited with the Steelers on Tuesday.

Johnson signed with the Titans in July, but hurt his ankle and was placed on injured reserve in August. He was released off that list later in the month and then served a six-game suspension for violating the performance-enhancing drug policy to open the season.

Johnson was a Chiefs seventh-round pick in 2022 and he played 29 games while with Kansas City. He had 66 tackles, three tackles for loss, a sack and three passes defensed in those appearances.
