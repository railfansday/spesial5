Dijon Balsamic Chicken Thighs

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

This dish is built for those nights when it’s chilly outside, and you want something that tastes like it’s been simmering for hours but actually comes together extremely easily. It's a simple one-pan dinner, but the sauce is the star. It’s a mix of tangy balsamic, sharp Dijon mustard, and hot honey. The hot honey adds a glossy, spicy sweetness that makes this perfect for colder weather and coats every bite of the tender, juicy chicken perfectly.

Cooking the chicken:The real secret to this dish is all in the sear. We’re starting the chicken skin-side down to fully render out the fat. This step is important as it keeps the skin from being chewy and lets all that rendered chicken fat melt into the sauce, giving it a nice boost of savory flavor. Serve it with white rice or a fluffy rice pilaf to soak up every drop of that sauce. Tip: If you don't have a bottle of hot honey on hand, regular honey plus a teaspoon of your favorite hot sauce is a great backup.

Make ahead & storage:To make ahead, you can whisk the sauce ingredients 2 days ahead and keep it in a jar in the fridge. When you're ready to cook, just sear the chicken and pour it in. For storage, you can keep leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
