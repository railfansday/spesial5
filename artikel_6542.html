Celebrating the Eater Awards Winners With a Party in NYC

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Eater Awards celebrated the best restaurants, chefs, and more of 2026

If you buy something from a link, Vox Media may earn a commission. See our ethics statement.

What a night. On Sunday, November 15, the winners of the 2026 Eater Awards, presented by Capital One, celebrated together at Nine Orchard in NYC. More than 200 chefs, restaurateurs, bartenders, and friends of Eater — many of whom traveled across the country to be there — partook in live portraiture, custom engraving, and, of course, plenty of food and drink.

Now in its 17th year, the Eater Awards recognize the year’s best new restaurants, bakeries, bars, dishes, and chefs in markets across the U.S.. More than 30 Eater staffers and contributors spent months traveling to 44 cities to weigh contenders on food, service, atmosphere, and the stories behind them. The prize? A tomato can trophy. And yes, they’re real.

The night started off strong with photos at the tomato can-wrapped step-and-repeat and a welcome shot of Zbiotics, before guests headed up to the roof of Nine Orchard for panoramic views and more fun. The party was filled with unique moments, from custom lighter engraving presented by Toast to live water color portraits presented by Capital One. Rounds of dishes inspired by the winning restaurants were passed.

A champagne toast commemorated the resiliency of the hospitality industry. “Thank you for what you do, every single day, to bolster your team, your community, and the restaurant industry at large,” said Eater editor in chief Stephanie Wu.

Rose Previte of DC’s Maydan, Medina, and Souk, and 2026 Best New Restaurant winner Maydan Market LA joined Wu on stage to present the first-ever Pre Shift Future of Hospitality Award to Maison Bar à Vins. This peer-voted award honors one winner whose work is particularly forward-thinking, challenges conventions, sets new standards, and points toward the future of hospitality. Matt Conroy and Jason Chavenson took home the surprise award.

“It’s really amazing to win an award like this that’s voted by everybody here and, you know, so many people we look up to, so it’s really incredible,” said Matt Conroy.

All attendees received an Eater tote filled with editor-curated products including Bubs candy, Zbiotics, TimeSavvy’s self-care and travel essentials kit, sweet matcha drops and fiery garlic chili sauce from Bokksu, a Wood Cabin candle from Keap Candles, chef socks from Cargo Crew, and a gift certificate for a pair of shoes from Snibbs.

It truly was a great night celebrating the hard work these chefs and their teams put into creating the best dining experiences of the year. Feeling FOMO? Check out the full list of winners and see for yourself what made these restaurants and chefs stand out.
