Why Blake Griffin Couldn’t Leave Basketball Alone

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Twelve years ago, I was sitting in Blake Griffin’s house, listening to him tell me that under no circumstances would he stay around basketball after he retired. “Well, look who’s a liar,” he says now.

Griffin is a former NBA All-Star who played for 14 years, many of them for the Clippers, before announcing his retirement in 2024. He now has his own show, The Stepback with Blake Griffin, and a weekly gig with NBA on Prime, as part of their studio show. “I always thought that there was a little bit too much negativity when it came to scrutinizing players,” Griffin says. “This is a job that 400-something guys have. It’s extremely hard. I don’t need to go on TV and I don’t need to tell everybody that the guy that went three for 17 sucks at basketball. He doesn’t.”

On The Stepback, Griffin puts on a suit, sits behind a desk, and addresses a big, sports-related topic each week: the media, taxpayer-financed stadiums, the rocketing costs of youth sports, and so on. Like him, the show is unexpectedly funny, frequently insightful, and a good hang. In this conversation, he and I discuss the Clippers then and now, retiring at 34, and what it was like to have some rude journalist visit his house, all those years ago.
