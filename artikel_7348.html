Arnold Schwarzenegger Finally Lands the Role of Santa in Amazon&#8217;s Holiday Ad

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

This holiday season, Arnold Schwarzenegger is Santa.

In the 60-second ad, “Santa Schwarzenegger,” Schwarzenegger takes on the role of Santa to promote The Man With the Bag, a new original holiday movie for Prime Video premiering Dec. 2.

In the action-comedy film, Santa enlists a former thief to recover his magical red bag after it’s stolen. Alan Ritchson and Awkwafina also star in the movie.

First, Schwarzenegger (as himself) is swarmed by paparazzi outside of a hotel in New York City. Once inside, he spots a billboard for The Man With the Bag and realizes that a Santa costume would help him go incognito in public.
