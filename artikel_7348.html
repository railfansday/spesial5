Carrot is expanding its telemedicine clinic with a network of part-time, fully remote physician consultants who deliver evidence-based virtual care to members across multiple U.S. states. Depending on your licensure, certification, and area of expertise, you will provide consultations in menopause and perimenopause care aligned to The Menopause Society and ACOG guidelines, early fertility support aligned to ACOG and ASRM guidelines, or both.

Metabolic health is a core, cross-cutting component of this program. Weight, insulin resistance, and cardiometabolic risk are central to outcomes across every journey we support, from ovulatory function and preconception readiness to menopausal symptom burden and long-term cardiovascular and bone health. You should expect metabolic assessment and management to be a routine part of your visits, and clinicians comfortable prescribing and managing anti-obesity medications, including GLP-1 receptor agonists, within clinical protocols are especially encouraged to apply.

This is a flexible, part-time contractor engagement of approximately 5-40 hours per week, with a target commitment of 10-15 hours per week.

Carrot's telemedicine clinic is one of the most direct ways members experience our care. Every virtual visit shapes how members navigate some of the most personal moments of their lives, from trying to conceive to managing menopause. The clinicians in this network are the face of that experience, and the quality of their judgment, communication, and documentation determines whether members receive care they trust.

This role also anchors Carrot's clinical point of view that metabolic health is inseparable from reproductive and hormonal health. As the telemedicine program scales across states and journeys, the clinicians who join this network will directly expand access to guideline-aligned, whole-person care for members who often cannot find it locally.
