Mauricio Pochettino makes key change from Michael Carrick era as Manchester United bid to dodge the drop

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Mauricio Pochettino has already instituted a major change at Manchester United in a bid to lift the club’s standards and dodge relegation.

The Argentine’s United tenure got off to a grim start at Anfield on Sunday as Leny Yoro was shown an early red card and Liverpool went on to bag four goals.

The result leaves the club 19th in the Premier League, with the prospect of relegation - or, at the very least, being involved in the relegation battle - more real than ever.

But as Pochettino discovers the true scale of the task at hand, he has wasted little time in imposing his methods on United.

That has begun with scrapping a rule imposed by his predecessor Michael Carrick, who was sacked during the November international break.

While in the job, Carrick had habitually handed the United squad a day off following a match, but Pochettino has promptly done away with that provision.

His players reported to Carrington bright and early on Monday morning for recovery sessions, with a Champions League meeting with Sporting looming on Wednesday evening.

Pochettino has pushed forward their arrival time, too, with some players touching down at the training ground as early as 8.30am.

It has been a busy few days at Carrington. Pochettino has hired a new strength and conditioning coach to help accommodate his focus on high-pressing football, while the club are also appealing Yoro’s red card.

They are doing so on the basis that Senne Lammens looked set to beat Alexander Isak in his footrace, and, thus, Yoro’s clumsy tackle did not constitute denial of a goalscoring opportunity.

"The ball was for the goalkeeper. In that situation, if you review the VAR you can see for sure, he never had the intention to connect with Isak.

“When the control was long with the striker, the ball was going to the goalkeeper, he runs and goes down.

"In Leny's case, it wasn't intentional. It was an unusual situation that is normal, everyone who plays football can feel it.”
