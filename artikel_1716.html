Unai Emery makes brutal Alejandro Garnacho claim after Aston Villa defeat to Galatasaray

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Unai Emery delivered a blunt assessment of Alejandro Garnacho’s situation at Aston Villa after the Argentine was left out of the squad for the defeat to Galatasaray on Tuesday night.

Villa were beaten 1-0 in Turkey and now sit ninth in the Champions League table, one place outside the automatic qualification spots.

Garnacho, meanwhile, trained with his Villa teammates on Monday but he was not even named on the bench against Galatasaray.

The on-loan Chelsea winger was also not included in Villa’s squad for their goalless draw at home to Sunderland on Saturday.

For the game against Galatasaray, Emery only picked 11 substitutes out of a possible 12 and also included two goalkeepers.

When asked about Garnacho’s absence after Villa’s defeat, Emery replied: ‘Today the players were here who I think are the best players to be here with us, to play with us.’

Garnacho has made just seven appearances since joining Villa in the summer and is yet to score a goal or register an assist.

Villa have an obligation to buy Garnacho from Chelsea for £43 million but only if he makes a certain number of appearances this season.

Chelsea signed Garnacho in a £40m deal from Manchester United last year but the Argentine struggled to make an impact during his time at Stamford Bridge, scoring eight goals in 43 appearances.
