Seville will grind to a halt today for the latest edition of 'The Great Derby' between the city's two biggest clubs, Sevilla and Real Betis.

A rivalry that dates back to 1915 remains as intense as ever and the hosts will be desperate to reclaim the bragging rights after losing 2-0 in this fixture last season.

The international break may have come at a good time for Sevilla, who took just one point from their last two games before the enforced pause, and they will be desperate to remain towards the top of the table after starting the weekend sixth.

Manager Luis Garcia Plaza will welcome back midfielder Manu Bueno from suspension, but winger Ruben Vargas is a fitness doubt and Marco, Peque Fernandez and Miguel Sierra are all expected to be unavailable through injury.

After finishing in the top seven in each of the last six seasons under longstanding manager Manuel Pellegrini, Betis are once again flying high in La Liga.

Tonight's visitors are fourth in the table, with 26 points from just 12 games, and would go third for at least 24 hours if they triumph at Estadio Ramon Sanchez Pizjuan for the second successive campaign.

Betis have had the upper hand in this rivalry over the past few seasons, both in head-to-head results and league positions, and are aiming to extend the gap between the sides to a huge 11 points.

Troy Parrott looks set to start after scoring twice for Republic of Ireland against Kosovo on Tuesday, but Facundo Bernal is suspended while Diego Llorente and Cucho Hernandez are injury doubts.

Betis have had the better of this rivalry recently and we see no reason why that can't continue after their excellent start to the season.
