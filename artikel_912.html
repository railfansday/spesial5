Owono undecided over future following De Miguel death

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Owono has 47 caps for Equatorial Guinea but was not called up during the most recent international window

FC Andorra manager Carles Manso says goalkeeper Jesus Owono is yet to decide whether he will return to football following the death of Tenerife forward Jesus de Miguel.

Owono was sent off after colliding with De Miguel during a Spanish second division match on 2 November, with the Tenerife forward passing away the following day in hospital.

Manso said the goalkeeper, 25, is currently spending time with his family and is "completely free to make any decision" with regard to a potential return.

He added: "I feel enormous pain over the loss of Jesus. I think of him, his family, and everyone who loved him."
