Professor Preeti Arya, PhD, specializes in textile development at the Fashion Institute of Technology. She says that the right fabric for leggings depends on your needs. But if you're worried about maintenance, "you can go for something that is petroleum-based because care is easy," she says. Petroleum-based fabrics include spandex, polyester, and nylon. These options are also typically much cheaper, according to Arya, but you can also opt for wool or silk, which sometimes feel better on your skin since they're natural fibers. Heads up: these fabrics are a bit harder to care for, though, and require special attention on laundry day.

Rise is really up to your personal preference, but higher-rise leggings usually have tummy control, which is a plus when you want to feel snatched at the gym. However, tummy control typically comes with a bit more compression, which may not be great for lower-impact activities like yoga or casual walks.

Again, length is up to your personal preference, but the most flattering cut is typically just above your ankle. For most people, that means a 7/8 style of leggings. Full-length leggings, or ones that extend all the way to your feet, might be better for barefoot workouts like yoga. Other lengths, like capri leggings, are really up to your personal style.
