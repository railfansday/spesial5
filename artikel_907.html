This Limited-Edition Holiday Collab is Already Selling Out

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The limited-edition collaboration brings customizable bags and pouches, cozy holiday apparel, a seven-day advent calendar and more, with prices starting at just $7.95.

In its first ever collaboration, Victoria’s Secret’s PINK has partnered with Stoney Clover Lane on a covetable holiday collection that will be topping every Christmas list this year—and it just landed online.

The limited-edition collaboration launched November 12 online at VSPINK.com, bringing together PINK’s playful essentials and Stoney Clover Lane’s signature customization. And if we know anything about buzzy collaborations, it’s that they have a history of selling out quickly.

The PINK X Stoney Clover Lane collaboration includes apparel, sized XS to XXL, and accessories ranging from $7.95 to $149.95.

The new collection includes seasonal apparel, like cozy PJ sets and Foldover Flares, plus customizable pieces including signature bags and pouches in exclusive designs and prints. You’ll also find winter-ready pieces, like fleece outerwear and a reversible puffer jacket, along with those iconic letter and motif patches in festive colors and collaborated designs.

For more giftable finds, the collaboration features two limited-edition beauty scents and a collectible PINK x Stoney Clover Lane Maxi Dog that comes with mini patches for customization. There’s even a stylish advent calendar for a festive countdown with daily goodies.

Shop the collab in PINK stores as of Friday, November 13, at locations across the U.S. and Puerto Rico, as well as select locations in Canada.
