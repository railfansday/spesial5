Weekend Winners: Nobody Knows the winner of the Doncaster feature?

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Weekend Winners panel of Kate Tracey, Callum Helliwell and Sam Boswell offer their views ahead of action from Aintree and Doncaster - which plays host to the flat season finale; Watch the episode in full on the At The Races YouTube channel

Ahead of another Saturday of racing, the Weekend Winners team reconvene to discuss the best betting angles.

Our Weekend Winners panel have nominated their best bet in the feature November Handicap, with Kate Tracey and Callum Helliwell sweet on Richard Hughes' runner.

"I'm kicking off with the favourite in Nobody Knows, which feels risky but as soon as you see Ryan Moore booked for Richard Hughes, in the Mulryan silks, you must think 'this is interesting'. Four starts to his name with two victories makes him a good prospect. He had Classic entries earlier in his career.

"He has a slightly high head carriage and plateaued at York but that was his first start for six months and he came from a long way back, so with fitness on his side he is the one for me."

"I want Nobody Knows to get beat on the card, so I am against Kate. The one horse I like here is last year's second in the race in Miss Dolly Rocker. She ran massive in the race last year and looks set for another big run with plenty of the runners contesting the race from last year.

"The Faye Bramley yard is slightly quiet at the minute which is the negative but has the draw you want and only a pound higher than last year. Will also strip fitter for that performance the other week, she's a massive price!"

"This is a really good race but I am siding with Nobody Knows, with the main reason being he wasn't given the greatest ride in the world when ridden cold from a long way back at York. He came back from a six-month break and just looked like he needed that run.

"He won a shade cosily at Wolverhampton and Ryan Moore has got down to his lowest weight possible. I think this team can do some damage here and this has been the plan all along."
