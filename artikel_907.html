Sunderland held by Jagiellonia as banned travelling fans ejected

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Sunderland vs Jagiellonia Bialystok. UEFA Europa League Round 1.

Stadium of LightAttendance: Attendance37,658.

Report as Sunderland are held 1-1 by Jagiellonia Bialystok in the Europa League; Black Cats expressed disappointment after some Jagiellonia fans were in attendance in home area at the Stadium of Light; hosts struck early in second half through Timur Tutierov; Kamil Jozwiak soon levelled

Sunderland have expressed their disappointment after some Jagiellonia Bialystok fans were in attendance in home areas during their 1-1 Europa League draw at the Stadium of Light.

A UEFA away supporter ban had been imposed on Jagiellonia for Thursday's fixture on Wearside.

However, in the opening minutes of the match, police and stewards were seen attending to some disruption in a home area in the ground.

A club statement said: "Sunderland AFC is disappointed that a number of Jagiellonia Bialystok supporters were present in home areas of the Stadium of Light during tonight's UEFA Europa League fixture.

"Jagiellonia Bialystok were subject to a UEFA away supporter ban for tonight's Matchday 5 fixture, following a disciplinary sanction imposed by European football's governing body.

"In the build-up to the match, the club had put measures in place in line with this sanction and maintained regular communication with our supporters, Jagiellonia Bialystok and UEFA. Unfortunately, Jagiellonia Bialystok failed to engage and cooperate on this matter.

"We are disappointed that these measures and communications were disregarded by some visiting supporters and will review the circumstances surrounding their entry to the stadium."

Jagiellonia Bialystok are yet to respond.

As for game itself, Sunderland were left frustrated again in their Europa League campaign after another draw.

The Black Cats enjoyed the best of the first-half chances through Malick Fofana and Nilson Angulo, but the visitors had an opportunity when Robin Roefs saved Kajetan Szmyt's free-kick.

Sunderland struck early in the second half through Timur Tutierov's header, his second goal in the competition, but Kamil Jozwiak levelled only minutes later after capitalising on a mistake at the back.

A point means the Wearsiders stay in the knockout phase play-off places after being held to their second draw from five games in the competition.
