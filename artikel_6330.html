Craftsman’s First Advent Calendar is Finally Back in Stock—and Packs $200 Worth of Stuff for Just $65

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Expect full-size tools, a battery voucher, and fun extras, plus a storage case for all the loot.

Gear-obsessed editors choose every product we review. We may earn commission if you buy from a link. Why Trust Us?

Craftsman is getting in on the Advent-calendar game this holiday season. The iconic red-and-black tool brand’s first limited edition box set is back in stock and bound to sell out fast. All in all, it amounts to $200 worth of stuff—including a battery voucher—reasonably priced at just $65.

I got my hands on an early mailing and can attest that the more than a dozen tools found inside the perforated windows are the real deal and feel solid in hand, protected by Craftsman’s lifetime warranty. That includes an 8-inch ratchet, a magnetic bit driver with 11 bits, two sets of sockets, and two stubby screwdrivers.

There’s also a magnetic pickup tool that was strong enough to lift a multitool and a folded pocket knife off my desk. Most of the loot fits inside the hard case hiding behind Door Number One. Like a kid putting a puzzle together, I had fun fitting each item inside and snapping the case shut.

As you can probably guess, this Advent calendar isn’t all serious business. There’s no chocolate or booze inside, but there is a deck of playing cards, a cute key-chain tape measure, and—my favorite—a bottle opener with a retro acetate handle.

Just keep in mind that this Advent calendar isn’t meant to replace a compact tool kit—something we think makes a great stocking stuffer for the person in your life who loves to tinker.
