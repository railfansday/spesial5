3D action adventure game Ryn: And the Thread of Life to be published by Top Hat Studios

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Top Hat Studios will publish Innombrable-developed 3D action adventure game Ryn: And the Thread of Life when it launches for PC via Steam, the companies announced. A release date was not announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:

The world is unraveling: The dark witch Stella has torn the sacred Thread of Life, and now magic bleeds in corrupt shreds.

As Ryn, a rag doll born from the last fragment of light, journey through the dying heart of a withering realm. Each step reveals new challenges, horrors… and new powers.

The five wicked dolls guard the stolen fragments. Defeat their nightmares, master their abilities, and reweave fate with the edge of your magical scissors!

Fast, fluid, and visceral hack-and-slash combat keeps the action moving in constant motion. Chain light and heavy attacks, dodge with precision, and unleash powerful abilities stolen from fallen enemies.

Quick Time Events turn battles into cinematic showdowns that demand reflexes, and reward skill. Every strike with Ryn’s enchanted scissors doesn’t just wound… it cuts through destiny itself!

Dive into a retro-inspired interconnected platforming world, where every area is filled with secrets, shortcuts, and hidden paths.

Discover the stories and tribulations that tore this realm apart. Scour over every nook and cranny for unlockables, diaries, and experience rich environmental storytelling.

To confront the evil Witch Stella, you must first vanquish the five corrupted dolls that guard her domain. Each hard-fought victory strips away her influence, and provides powerful new abilities to claim as your own.

These gifts grant not only the strength to crush Stella’s minions, but also the tools to uncover what was once unreachable – opening new areas, hidden paths, and the methods to reveal new secrets within familiar territory.
