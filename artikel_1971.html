'One to watch' - Keith Wyness: Everton well placed for Giorgi Kveradze deal after development

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Everton are increasingly likely to be able to prise winger Giorgi Kveradze away from Frosinone in the January transfer window.

That is according to former Toffees chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Italian side will be willing sellers this winter.

Everton have been heavily linked with a move for Kveradze of late, as he already has three goals and four assists from just 11 games in Serie A so far this season.

Wyness believes that Frosinone's limited budget by Serie A standards means that they are likely to seriously consider selling their star this winter to reinvest cash into their squad.

The top-flight returnees were only promoted from Serie B last term, but have quickly climbed as high as sixth in the early stages of the season.

Kveradze has been a central part of their impressive early-season form, but they are unlikely to be able to keep him long-term.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Kveradze has already proven that he can make the "leap" and perform in a top European league.

The Georgian international is already close to matching his goal and assist tallies from last season in Serie B.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness made it clear that Everton will continue to "watch" the 23-year-old closely.

"Yeah, look, Frosinone have done well, promoted for the first time this season. I think they came up with Monza and Venezia again, so of the three clubs that came up, they're doing the best right now," he said.

"So yes, I think there is a chance there (for Everton). Look, for them, it'll be all about staying in Serie A. But also, I think with the budget situation in Serie A, they probably would be sellers if they had to.

"For Kveradze, it's all about can you make that leap, and he's come from Serie B up to Serie A, and he's doing okay in Serie A,

"They've only played a few games, so you've really got to judge him over a longer period before you can guess if he can do it in the Premier League. So that's one to watch."

Wyness told Football Insider earlier this week that The Friedkin Group will genuinely consider a move to sign Kveradze, given that he is only expected to cost around "£15million."

However, the boardroom expert is adamant that he could be a shrewd signing, and feels that the wideman's value could potentially soar up to the £40m mark if he proves himself in English football.

Wyness expects a move for him to be firmly within Everton's capability this winter, despite the current ownership looking to sell the club.

Kveradze has created more chances in Serie A this term than the likes of Kevin De Bruyne and Paulo Dybala, and could yet be playing in Everton's colours very soon.
