Wick made to wait for Worthing’s visit and ‘biggest game’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Advanced ticket sales were very good and Dragons officials were expecting their biggest attendance in recent years, surpassing the 359 who watched Lee Baldwin’s team beat Worthing United in a penalty shootout on their way to winning the Southern Combination League Division One play-offs.

Tickets will be honoured for the rearranged fixture – which has now been set for Tuesday, December 1 (7.30pm) – and Dragons boss Baldwin said: ‘We worked so hard to get the game on and were happy to go ahead.

‘The pitch was wet but playable but we understand and respect the officials’ view that it work churn up in the second half and wouldn’t be fit for football.

‘We look forward to an evening which will be a marvellous occasion in front of a big crowd.’

Wick drew 2-2 at Midhurst last Saturday with Charlie Robinson and George Bingham scoring and Baldwin added: ‘We were disappointed not to take all three points as we had the chances to win the game but didn’t take them.

‘It’s strange because we have played so many games on artificial surfaces this season that we didn’t adapt well to performing on a heavy grass pitch, it was a bit of a strange scenario.’

Wick travel to AFC Varndeanians this Saturday (3pm).
