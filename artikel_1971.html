The Cristiano Ronaldo tribute party hilariously ended in disaster.

The Estádio Municipal de Braga hosting what was intended to be a celebration of the selfish prima donna’s international career.

It had always been about Cristiano Ronaldo rather than the team.

The 41-year-old embarrassingly walked out of the Portugal camp in September after he was told he would not start the in the Nations League away match against Denmark.

Cristiano Ronaldo was given a token £215 fine and 30-day suspension by Portugal.

Fast forward less than two months later and ahead of the reverse fixture with Denmark, he was at it again.

Rather than doing things properly, Cristiano Ronaldo retired from international football via a video he posted on his social media last week. No advance warning given and Portugal coach Jorge Jesus later revealed that Ronaldo would have been picked for these Uefa Nations League games against Denmark and Wales in November.

With his out of the blue announcement, Portugal then quickly made this Saturday night Portugal v Denmark match all about Cristiano Ronaldo.

All of the Portugal players and the mascots walked out wearing a ‘Ronaldo 7’ shirt.

The fans displayed the word ‘Ronaldo’ in the top tier of one of the stands.

A banner that translated as ‘thank you, captain’, plus a similar message appearing on the scoreboard in the seventh minute.

How brilliant it was then that this all ended in disaster.

Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg scored a penalty just before half-time, then Rasmus Hojlund scored twice in eight minutes to make it 3-0 with more than half an hour remaining.

With 73 minutes on the clock Newcastle United striker William Osula came off the bench and within a minute had won a free-kick towards the right wing. The resultant free-kick seeing Joachim Andersen head the fourth and the Cristano Ronaldo fan club left in tears as his tribute well and truly had fallen apart.

Osula came close to adding a fifth but unlucky to see his late effort blocked.

Bernardo Silva did get a no consolation goal for Portugal in the third minute of added time but the final 4-1 humiliating scoreline at home, was such a fitting tribute to Cristiano Ronaldo.
