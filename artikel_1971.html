Man United players 'wanted Michael Carrick out' after 'breakdown' - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Michael Carrick's Manchester United squad have been ignoring his instructions because they want him out.

That is the opinion of experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 45-year-old will be dismissed in the coming hours, with decision-makers currently working on finding his replacement.

Man United have suffered their worst ever Premier League start under Carrick, winning just one of their opening 10 league games.

The former midfielder, who was appointed permanently in the summer, guided the Red Devils to the Champions League last season but has been unable to provoke a turnaround at Old Trafford.

United States manager Mauricio Pochettino is among leading contenders to replace Carrick, while names such as Julian Nagelsmann and Eddie Howe have also been mentioned.

Carrick will join a long list of Man United managers to have failed to create something strong at Old Trafford in the years since Sir Alex Ferguson left.

The Englishman will feel that he was not backed enough in the summer transfer window, and it is hard to blame him.

Transfer chiefs at Man United left holes throughout Carrick's squad, which the next manager will also be forced to cope with.

"The players, they don't follow what Carrick wants them to do," King told Football Insider. "Whether it's a breakdown in communication or that they wanted him out, which is a dangerous thing.

"Again, they're going to fall into this situation where the team really aren't good enough. It's the team that aren't good enough.

"I know about the manager, but he's working with what he's got. I don't think Manchester United are a challenging, championship-winning team.

"He's been lumbered with it. I think he did well to get them into Europe last year, but they don't look like a good team."

Carrick's replacement will arrive to see a squad in desperate need of reinforcement throughout.

Man United are without a consistent striker amid the injury to Benjamin Sesko, with best winger Bryan Mbeumo often forced to play through the middle.

The midfield was heavily invested in during the summer transfer window but it often looks devoid of energy and quality, and will need significant coaching work.

But, without a doubt, the defence is the area of most concern. Man United are without a single consistently strong option at full-back on either side, and seem to chop and change centre-back partners on a weekly basis.
