'Just to make it clear...' - Expert: Unai Emery agreement 'unlikely' to stop Aston Villa exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Aston Villa agreeing a new contract with Unai Emery will not rule out the possibility of his departure amid lingering interest.

The Spaniard has been linked with a number of top jobs in recent months, including positions at the likes of Real Madrid, Bayern Munich and most recently Man United.

While he has shown no inclination to walk away from Villa Park in pursuit of any of those jobs, speculation about his long-term future is refusing to die down.

Former Aston Villa scout Mick Brown warned Football Insider that while a new contract would commit Emery's immediate future to the club, it would not completely rule out an exit.

It would, however, allow Villa to demand a significant compensation fee as clubs would have to fork out to buy him out of his contract should they wish to appoint him.

Again, any departure is considered highly likely in the near future as Emery remains fully committed to his project at Aston Villa, but it cannot be ruled out long-term.

Football Insider revealed on 27 October that Aston Villa had opened talks with Emery over a new contract in a bid to deter the lingering speculation about a move to Man United.

Emery was never believed to be interested in the position at Old Trafford, and is prepared to reject any advances should they arrive, amid pressure on Michael Carrick's situation.

However, while signing a new contract at Aston Villa will come as a huge boost to the club, it will not make it impossible for Emery to leave at some stage in the duration.

"Just to make something clear, signing a new contract is unlikely to mean any exit is ruled out," Brown told Football Insider.

"It obviously makes it a lot harder, it's a commitment from Emery and it's a commitment from the club, but we all know contracts can be broken and they can be paid out.

"What getting Emery to sign a new deal does is, it means that any compensation fee is now likely to be huge, and so that could put clubs off making a move.

"In the immediate future, it's clear he has no intention of leaving Aston Villa, and that's obviously going to be great news for them.

"But it doesn't rule out the possibility that he could, in the duration of any new contract, still leave Aston Villa if a club makes an approach.

"It's not something I expect to happen because by all accounts things are going well and everybody is happy with what he's doing, but it's still a possibility for the future."

Speculation linking Emery with the potential vacancy at Man United has led to plenty of drama in recent weeks, including with Villa's legal threat towards Jamie Carragher.

That issue has since been resolved, but Villa have been prepared to fight for their image and show their commitment to Emery, with that feeling going both ways.

Brown told Football Insider on 3 November that Emery is keen to extend his stay at Aston Villa and will be happy to put pen to paper on a contract extension.

Their recent strong form in the Premier League has led to renewed optimism for the season, despite a turbulent summer, with an ambitious project in place.

The boss' current contract is set to run until 2029, and there are no major concerns about a potential departure, but they are keen to secure his position amid ongoing speculation.
