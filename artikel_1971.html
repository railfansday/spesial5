The extreme lengths Scott Brown took to avoid being recognised at the darts

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Scott Brown has revealed he once attended the darts dressed as a Minion to avoid being recognised.

The Celtic great made the hilarious admission after swapping the football pitch for the oche to take on former European champion Ross Smith at an exhibition event in Edinburgh.

Brown even had his iconic No.8 on the back of a specially-made darts shirt as he went up against Smith. The former Scotland international was handed a sizeable advantage, beginning on 501 while Smith started on 1001, but he was unable to pull off an upset.

Ahead of taking to the stage, Brown admitted he was a big darts fan and revealed the lengths he had previously gone to in order to enjoy an event without attracting attention. He said: “I have been to the darts before dressed up as a Minion; nobody ever knew I was there!

“I really do enjoy the darts. My wee brother is coming along as well, and he enjoys the darts so he’ll be buzzing that I get beat on stage. Playing in front of 60 or 70,000 is OK when I know I can control the ball and pass it, make a tackle and stuff.

“I can’t really judge my darts quality, shall I say. Standing in front of 1,000 people, if I miss the board they’ll be delighted. If I hit the board I will be delighted.”

Brown also revealed his admiration for the elite players who regularly cope with the pressure of performing on stage. The Celtic hero is friends with World Cup winner John Henderson and admitted seeing top players up close gave him an appreciation for the hours of practice required.

He added: “See, when they stand up there with nerves in front of people, I understand the pressure they are under and how well they play. The experience and practice they put in, as well as standing in front of a dartboard, some people might see it as draining. But some really, really enjoy that.

“The ones that stand there and practice are the reason they are world champions, and they are up there in the top 10.”

And despite his love of darts, Brown was not prepared to trade his haul of football medals for success at the oche. He joked: “I’d love to say it would take over them all.

"No. If I get down to just a double, I will be delighted. I cannae remember the last time I hit a double.”
