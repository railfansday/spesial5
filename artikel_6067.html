One of the best reasons to apply for a travel rewards card is the welcome offer. By hitting certain minimum spending requirements, you can often earn tens or even hundreds of thousands of points or miles within the first few months of opening a card.

But welcome offers change frequently, and knowing when to apply can make a big difference. After all, you don't want to miss out on bonus points by applying too early or too late.

To help you determine whether an offer is worth pursuing, we've compiled the welcome offer histories of several popular American Express cards, along with our recommendations for when to apply.

Apply now for the American Express Platinum Card®: Find out your offer and see if you are eligible for as high as 175,000 bonus points after spending $8,000 on purchases in the first six months of card membership. (Welcome offers vary, and you may not be eligible for an offer.)

You can also check our frequently updated list of the best increased card offers currently available.

Here are some of the most popular Amex cards, with guidance on when you should apply for each.

While American Express offers several types of welcome bonuses, we’re only discussing publicly available welcome offers.

Public offers are generally available to eligible applicants, while targeted offers are extended to select individuals and may feature higher bonuses or different spending requirements.

Amex also uses personalized bonuses on certain cards, allowing applicants to see which welcome offer they're eligible for before deciding whether to accept the card. The advertised "as high as" amount represents the maximum potential bonus, not necessarily what every applicant will receive. Additionally, offers may change at any time, and some of the offers mentioned below may not be available.

Keep in mind that American Express only allows you to earn one bonus per lifetime on each of its cards, so make sure to wait for the best time to apply. In addition, you may not be eligible for a welcome offer if you've already held a higher-tier card in the same family.

For example, if you currently have or have previously held the American Express Platinum Card®, you may not be eligible for a welcome offer on the American Express® Gold Card.

Find out your offer and see if you are eligible for as high as 100,000 bonus points after spending $6,000 on purchases in the first six months of card membership. (Welcome offers vary, and you may not be eligible for an offer.)

The Amex Gold is an excellent everyday choice for foodies since it earns 4 points per dollar spent at restaurants worldwide on up to $50,000 per calendar year (then 1 point per dollar spent thereafter) and on up to $25,000 per calendar year at U.S. supermarkets (then 1 point per dollar spent). These are solid earning rates for a card with a $325 annual fee (see rates and fees).

The Amex Business Gold card is great for small businesses thanks to its strong earning structure. It earns 4 points per dollar spent on your top two eligible spending categories each billing cycle (up to $150,000 per calendar year; then 1 point per dollar spent). Cardmembers pay $375 each year to hold this card (see rates and fees).

The American Express Graphite Card carries a $295 annual fee (see rates and fees) and is aimed at high-spending businesses that want to earn cash back with American Express.

While we can't say for certain what future offers on the Amex Graphite Card will look like, we encourage you to apply for the current offer if you're interested in the card.

The card has a $0 introductory annual fee for the first year, then $95 annually thereafter (see rates and fees).

American Express is the sole issuer of Delta's cobranded consumer and business cards. Amex's Delta cards feature elevated offers more commonly than most of their proprietary cards, so it's very important to know when it's the right time to apply.

If you're interested in one of Amex's Delta cards, check out our guide on Delta's cards' offer history to help you decide when it's the best time to apply.

American Express offers a range of cobranded Hilton cards to suit every traveler, from beginners to hardcore Hilton loyalists. Like Amex's Delta cards, the Hilton cards receive elevated welcome offers more frequently than the cards we've featured in this guide.

If you're interested in one of Amex's Hilton cards, we have a guide on when it's best to apply, based on the offer history of all Hilton cards.

Marriott's portfolio of cards can be more challenging to track because it includes cards issued by Chase and American Express. The two issuers don't necessarily put elevated offers on their Marriott cards at the same time. Plus, both issuers have their own application rules and restrictions.

If you're interested in one of Amex's Marriott cards, we have a guide to the offer history of all Marriott cards to help you know when it's the right time to apply.

Pay attention to the welcome offers of any travel rewards cards you may be interested in, as offers can change frequently. Applying at the wrong time could mean missing out on thousands of bonus points or hundreds of dollars in cash back.

Before applying for any new card, look at the range of its previous welcome offers. Then, when an offer hits the higher end of that range, you can feel sure you’re getting a great deal and making the right choice for your rewards strategy.
