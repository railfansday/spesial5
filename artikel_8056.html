19 Great Gift Ideas That Are HSA/FSA Eligible

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bonus: They’re all literally proven to be good for you, too!

Here’s a fun fact about your Flexible Spending Account (FSA): whatever you don’t spend by the deadline is gone. Granted, some plans give you a little grace period or rollover a small amount, but most of us are on the use-it-or-lose-it clock. And while Health Savings Account (HSA) money doesn’t expire, it’s still sitting there earmarked for your health, and the holidays are a good excuse to finally use it.

What’s great is that you can buy a lot more with it than just your basic bandages or the odd bottle of contact solution. We’re talking heating pads, period underwear, sunscreen, smart rings... even ergonomic office chairs can qualify! Some of the bigger-ticket items here (like the Lettuce Grow) may require a letter of medical necessity, but that typically just means filling out a quick online health questionnaire at checkout through a service like Truemed or Flex. Everything else you can usually just swipe your HSA/FSA card for. Of course, rules vary by plan, so check yours if you’re not sure.

One important caveat: HSA/FSA funds can only be used for you, your spouse, and your dependents. That means the following picks are perfect for treating yourself, stuffing your kids’ stockings, or, you know, finally getting your snoring partner something that’ll ultimately help you both sleep.

Here are some of our favorite items that are often covered by HSA and FSA guidelines.

If you know someone who gets painful period cramps or has endometriosis or adenomyosis, these little menstrual heat pads are such a thoughtful stocking stuffer. I stick them inside the waistband of my pants, and it genuinely gets hot enough to offer relief and go wherever I go all day. They last for hours, and I just love that they let me get relief without feeling stuck to a heating pad (and therefore a wall outlet). — Katie McPherson

Isn’t it great when really trendy, great-smelling, cute beauty products are also good for you and eligible to buy with tax-exempt money? This is the case with Touchland hand sanitizer, which is all the rage with tweens and teens, and for good reason. It’s so cute and fun that it makes keeping germs away a blast. This is so perfect for stocking stuffing. — Sarah Aswell

It’s also nice that there are so many upholstery options (I ordered the black boucle!). — Julie Sprankles
