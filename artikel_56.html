French student protests: A 'very bizarre' government response

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

“The government had a very bizarre way of dealing with the student protests,” says Jean-Rémi Girard, a secondary school teacher and union representative in Asnières-sur-Seine. He explains how teachers sought to defuse tensions by listening to students, and respecting their right to protest, while keeping classes running. He recounts a police intervention last October and the case of a student facing trial for defending a classmate, and has the full support of several teachers. Girard questions the government's heavy-handed policing and largely symbolic proposals on class sizes and university admissions. “There wasn't really a negotiation,” he says, also highlighting the number of education workers injured during the protests.
