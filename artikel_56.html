French 2027 presidential race: Green leader Tondelier draws closer to hard-left Mélenchon

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Six months before the first round of France’s presidential election, Green party leader Marine Tondelier is moving closer to Jean-Luc Mélenchon, the leader of the radical left. The move has caught the other candidates on the left and in the centre off guard, notably Raphaël Glucksmann. FRANCE 24's Clovis Casali explains.
