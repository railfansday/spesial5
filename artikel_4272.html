UK Charts: Switch Sports Resort Slightly Slips In The Second Set

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Last week was a slightly quieter one for big new releases, so the UK charts aren't quite as awash with newbies this time as we have seen previously in October.

The month's two big hitters continue to perform well, with Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 holding onto the top spot, while Nintendo Switch Sports Resort falls off the podium and drops down to fourth. Old faithfuls EA Sports FC 27 and Minecraft were there to fill in the gap, worry not.

Resident Evil Requiem and Onimusha have both rallied after some weeks away and now find themselves in fifth and sixth, respectively (products of another Capcom sale, perhaps?), and Evercade's Banjo-Kazooie Double Pack makes its chart debut in 14th.
