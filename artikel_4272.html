Delta Force has you dodging riptides and throwing shields in their newest season, Aurora

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Delta Force, Timi Studio Group and Team Jade's popular tactical shooter, is getting a major suite of new content today. The launch of Delta Force's latest update, Season Aurora, comes packed with a new operator, map and plenty of quality-of-life fixes, which means you'll definitely want to log in and have a go!

Of course, the major feature here is the new Operator. And Veronica Cardozo, codename: Martelo, is certainly hard to miss, especially when she's smashing her bulletproof shield into your face. Able to charge forward with her shield to clear enemies, fire smoke grenades, and grow stronger with the intensity of the fight, Martelo is perfect if you like to get up close and personal.

Speaking of hazards, you'll want to keep your eyes peeled for Supercritical, a new Operations map that's releasing in December or early 2027, teased here with an image of an exploding reactor. Oh good.

But, potential radiation poisoning aside, there's plenty of other titbits to jump into Delta Force for. Like the new AN-94 assault rifle, or tweaks to the Brakkesh map, and a whole host of tweaks to mechanics such as character animations and camera angles. Check out the full changelog here!
