Hearst Magazines Launches a Creator Studio Built Like a Talent Agency

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Hearst Magazines launched Hearst Creator Studios on Monday, a division that pairs hand-selected creators with its editorial brands and advertisers to produce original series, branded campaigns, and other content, according to Lisa Ryan Howard, the executive vice president and global chief revenue officer of Hearst Magazines.

The studio debuts with 50 creators across Cosmopolitan, Delish, Elle, Good Housekeeping, and MotorTrend, along with seven creator-led series. Hearst plans to triple that roster in the next six months and expand the studio across the rest of its more than 30 brands in 2027.

The launch reflects a broader push among digital publishers to borrow from the creator economy, both by partnering with outside talent and by encouraging staff to adopt the tactics of creators.

Mark Stenberg is ADWEEK's senior media reporter.
