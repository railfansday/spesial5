1 in 5 Marketers Is Looking to Quit—Double the Average in Other Fields

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Did you dread the idea of coming to work today? Answer honestly, please.

If you said yes, and you work in marketing, you have plenty of company.

While roughly 16% of white-collar professionals admit to dreading their jobs, that figure spikes to 26% for people who work in advertising and marketing.

That’s one breakout stat crunched for ADWEEK by Kickresume, based on a recent survey of 1,002 professionals across a gamut of careers aimed to take the temperature of job satisfaction and the prevalence of job hunting.

Across sectors, the survey paints a decidedly “meh” picture of how professionals feel about the office.

Robert Klara is the senior editor of brands at Adweek, specializing in the evolution and impact of brands.
