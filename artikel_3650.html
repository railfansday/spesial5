Tim Allen and Jane Hajduk's Relationship Timeline: Marriage, Parenthood, More

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Tim Allen has been riding alongside wife Jane Hajduk for two decades.

The Shifting Gears star and the actress/producer celebrated their 20th wedding anniversary in October 2026 — a milestone that arrived as the couple’s teenage daughter, Elizabeth, began her senior year of high school.

From welcoming their only child together to Disneyland adventures and everything in between, here is a look back at Allen and Hajduk’s relationship through the years.

Allen and Hajduk first crossed paths while they were both working in Hollywood in the early 2000s. They dated for about five years before getting engaged.

Allen and Hajduk said “I do” in an intimate, outdoor ceremony in Grand Lake, Colorado.

Reflecting on two decades with Hajduk, Allen credited their shared sense of humor for keeping the romance intact. “I think a sense of humor saves us so many times,” he exclusively told Us in June 2026. “I’d forgotten how much I adore her sense of humor.”

The actor was previously married to Laura Deibel, with whom he shares daughter Katherine “Kate.”

The couple welcomed their only child together, Elizabeth Allen-Dick, when Allen was 55. Speaking to Us, the actor opened up about how his approach to fatherhood differed the second time around.

“I was not sober for some of [Kate’s] formative years. I made amends to her,” Allen said. “With the younger one, I see how much different it is when I’ve been sober almost 30 years. She never knew any of that guy.”

Allen added that Hajduk — whom he described as a “staunch Catholic” and a “very good athlete” — helped shape their family’s rhythm. “She got me involved in training, and she’s a staunch Catholic, and I joined her with that,” he shared. “I’m not a Catholic, I don’t belong, so I don’t have to obey the rules, but I think she admires that I made that move. We both pull a bit on either side. We’re pretty good buddies.”

In 2023, both Hajduk and Elizabeth got in on Allen’s holiday revival. Hajduk accompanied their then-14-year-old daughter to the Disney+ set of The Santa Clauses each day while Elizabeth played Allen’s onscreen daughter.

“It was a new experience for both of them, and they just worked their butts off,” Allen told Us at the time. “It’s very impressive to watch.”

Working alongside Elizabeth became a career highlight for Allen. “It’s one of those moments that I’ll carry with me forever,” he said. “To see the professional that she’s turned into, [she] treats everybody well, shows up on time, does her job, works long hours, [with] no complaints.”

Allen later recalled an emotional scene he filmed with his daughter. “There was a scene where she was very upset [and] dare I say, she’s a natural,” he told Us. “I hugged her as Santa Claus, but it was Dad and daughter there, and I told her as I walked away [feeling] very emotional, ‘This is going to sit with me forever.’”

The family of three spent a day at Disneyland to mark Elizabeth’s 16th birthday. Allen and Hajduk were photographed at the time riding the Matterhorn Bobsleds, Mickey and Minnie’s Runaway Bobsled and Tiana’s Bayou Adventure. The couple also enjoyed celebratory ice cream during the park visit.

The outing arrived during a busy stretch for Allen, who had returned to the small screen that January in Shifting Gears after famously starring on Home Improvement from 1991 to 1999.

Allen told Us that raising daughters alongside Hajduk opened his eyes to a new dynamic. “I come from [a family of] seven boys and two girls, and it’s a very different world to me with girls,” he said. “I have a different view of what will make a strong woman. The girl stuff I have little interest in — clothing, looks, gossip, and all that — [I focused on] learning finances, learning how to take care of yourself, and that works for boys and girls.”

He also praised Hajduk’s influence on their daughters. “My sisters are well-rounded because they were around a very strong mom, and these girls are around strong moms,” Allen said. “There’s a connection between moms and sons that’s undeniable, and there’s a connection between fathers and daughters that’s undeniable.”

Ahead of their October 2026 anniversary, Allen told Us that he and Hajduk have grown into “pretty good buddies” despite being “very different people.” The actor recalled a moment on the golf course that reminded him why the marriage works.

“We both struggle with golf, and [we were talking about how] Elizabeth will be a senior next year, and we’re going, ‘What’s that going to be like?’ and then Jane sat in the golf cart and said something really funny,” he shared.

Allen also opened up about the bittersweet reality of their only child together finishing up high school. “We’ve gone down to her room to say ‘good night’ since she was a little kid, and now she’s up with glasses on and with her computer, finishing her math homework,” he said. “She still says, ‘Are you guys coming down to say good night?’ I told her I’m probably going to come down for quite a while after she’s gone. I’ll just sit in that room because I will really miss her presence.”

Allen and Hajduk appeared together at the 2026 Women’s Guild Cedars-Sinai Stardust Ball at the Beverly Wilshire in Beverly Hills. The Home Improvement alum sported a black suit for the occasion, while his wife opted for a brown high-neck silk dress.
