GTA 6 Is Getting A Physical Map–Here’s How To Get One For Free Or Very Cheap

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Rockstar Games has confirmed more details about Grand Theft Auto 6's impending release on November 19, including more information about physical goodies. While the game itself won't be available on a disc, Rockstar is offering a printable physical GTA 6 cover art poster and a map of the game's setting, Leonida.

The poster and Leonida map will be available for free as digital downloads on GTA 6's official website on November 19. A double-sized physical version of the poster will be available "shortly after launch" from the Rockstar Store for 99 cents. Alternatively, it's free with any purchase from the Rockstar Store.

Back in the day, Rockstar used to include fold-out posters in the box for GTA games featuring the game's cover art and map. But GTA 6 is a digital-only game. Players can buy a physical GTA 6 box at retail stores, but it won't include a disc, only a code for a digital download. Rockstar hasn't said if it will include anything extra in the box.

Some retailers are apparently trying to sweeten the deal to encourage people to buy from them. It was recently reported that Walmart orders of GTA 6 will include some kind of "surprise." When the Switch 2 released, some Walmart orders for the console included "game-ready" snacks like soda and chips.

In other news, Rockstar just recently announced when GTA 6 preloading begins and when the game unlocks--players in the US will be among the last people in the world to get in.
