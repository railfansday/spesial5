Why ‘Home Alone’ Director Chris Columbus Thinks a Reboot Won’t Work

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Original Home Alone director Chris Columbus doesn’t think a reboot of the hit Christmas movie can work without its late star, Catherine O’Hara.

The director shared his opinion about continuing the Home Alone universe with Variety on Friday, November 20 — shortly before reports of a potential Home Alone reboot broke.

“Catherine, as I’ve always said, is 50% of Home Alone,” Columbus, 68, told the outlet. “There’s the yin and yang of Home Alone: Catherine O’Hara and Macaulay Culkin.”

He further explained, “Without those two in the film, there is no film, because they create the emotional epicenter of that film.”

Before he was aware that another Home Alone with Culkin, 46, could be in the works, Columbus added the original cast — as opposed to new actors — would be key to its success.

“I think you potentially could still entertain the idea of doing another Home Alone with the cast,” Columbus added. “Not any of these ridiculous follow-ups to the first. And I only say ridiculous because it’s weird with filmmakers. They don’t understand why that cast works so well. It’s always about the cast. It’s like The Goonies. It’s about the cast.”

However, he still believed the mother-son dynamic between O’Hara and Culkin’s character was crucial.

“It was really about Catherine and Macaulay, if anyone ever wanted to do a sequel and unfortunately that can’t happen now,” he said.

Multiple outlets reported on Friday that Culkin is in talks to return to the Home Alone franchise in a sequel developed by Disney and 20th Century Studios. Seth Rogen and his producing and writing partner, Evan Goldberg, are reportedly attached to pen the script, which is based on an idea that Culkin himself pitched to Disney in recent months.

The plot centers on Culkin’s character, Kevin McCallister, as a father, according to Deadline. Us reached out to reps for Disney for comment.

As a child, Culkin starred in the original 1990 comedy directed by Columbus. The classic holiday movie follows 8-year-old Kevin, who is accidentally left alone at his parents’ home in Chicago while his family heads off on a Christmas vacation in Paris. Kevin must fend for himself and defend his home when a pair of burglars attempts a robbery.

Culkin reprised his role in the 1992 sequel, Home Alone 2: Lost in New York, which finds Kevin alone in New York after he accidentally boards the wrong plane.

Several sequels without Culkin followed, including 1997’s Home Alone 3, Home Alone 4 in 2002, Home Alone: The Holiday Heist in 2012 and the latest incarnation, 2021’s Home Sweet Home Alone.

O’Hara, who played Kevin’s mother, died in January at the age of 71. The actress’ cause of death was listed as a pulmonary embolism, a blood clot in the lungs, with rectal cancer as the underlying cause.

“Mama. I thought we had time,” Culkin wrote via Instagram at the time. “I wanted more. I wanted to sit in a chair next to you. I heard you but I had so much more to say. I love you. I’ll see you later.”

In an interview with The Gentleman’s Journal in May, Culkin opened up about how O’Hara’s death affected him.

“When Catherine passed away in January, that hit me,” Culkin told the outlet. “That hit me pretty good ’cause, you know, it was just too soon. And I felt that we had unfinished business.”

He continued, “I definitely feel like I had unfinished business with her, you know? I feel like I owed her a favor — and I don’t like having an outstanding debt.”
