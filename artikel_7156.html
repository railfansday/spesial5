Daft Punk, Doctor Doom, and E.T. Unite in Lego&#8217;s Action-Packed Holiday Ad

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

What happens when you let Daft Punk, K-Pop Demon Hunters, E.T., and Doctor Doom loose in Lego’s brick factory? The Danish toy brand’s holiday campaign has the answer.

The brand has unveiled “The Lego Holiday Workshop,” an epic spot that follows a single Lego brick on an action-packed journey through its workshop.

Set to 2001’s “One More Time” by Daft Punk, the one-minute film was directed by The Greatest Showman’s Michael Gracey. It opens with minifigures of the French electronic duo, which recently inked a partnership with Lego, racing through the snow towards a tiny, glowing yellow door.
