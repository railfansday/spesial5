Arsenal outclassed in defeat to dominant Lyon

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Arsenal Women vs Olympiques Lyon Women. Women's Champions League.

Arsenal register a single shot on target, which came in stoppage-time, as they were outclassed by Lyon at Meadow Park; Jule Brand and Melchie Dumornay scored in each half but the French side could easily have finished with more goals in what was a dominant performance

Arsenal’s hopes of automatic qualification to the Women’s Champions League quarter-finals are hanging by a thread after they were beaten 2-0 at home by a dominant Lyon in foggy conditions.

The French side, who beat Arsenal in the semi-finals last season, got the better of the Gunners again at Meadow Park on Thursday night with goals from Jule Brand and Melchie Dumornay in each half sealing the victory.

Arsenal barely laid a glove on Lyon - who are yet to concede a goal in this season's Champions League - and now sit eighth in the standings, three points adrift of fourth-placed Chelsea, after their first loss in five league phase matches.

Emily Fox registered Arsenal's only shot on target of the game deep in stoppage-time and the home side could easily have conceded a few more had Marie-Antoinette Katoto not spurned two big opportunities.

Arsenal were also indebted to their goalkeeper, Misa, who produced three big saves in the second half to keep out Tabitha Chawinga, Dumornay and Vicki Becho.

Renee Slegers' side, now without a win in three matches in all competitions, are relying on teams above them to drop points in the final round of matches to avoid having to face a play-off match to secure their passage to the quarter-finals.

Meanwhile, Lyon, who currently sit second in the table, have secured a top-four finish with a game to spare after picking up 13 points from five matches.

Arsenal Women boss Renee Slegers to ESPN:

"Tough battle. Congratulations to Lyon who were better than us today. They play good football, physically strong in the duels, fast with the ball and without the ball.

"We had a tough time dealing with it in the first half. Second half we controlled the game better without creating clear-cut chances.

"But then you see how clinical Lyon were from their moments."

On her half-time team talk: "It was 1-0, so we were in the game. Like they had, we need moments as well. We are going to get moments and then how we are going to create it."

Sky Sports News reporter Uma Gurav at Meadow Park:

"The bigger picture here is that Arsenal have played the best two teams in this competition in two consecutive weeks and they've got just one point out of the two games.

"You have to admit the draw was very tough on them to get both Barcelona and Lyon, but it's also a harsh reality check.

"This Arsenal team won the Champions League just 18 months ago. Now, they're not quite up there with the European elites. Can they get back there anytime soon?"

Ex-Arsenal striker Lianne Sanderson to ESPN:

"I like Renee but there has to come a time [when we question] why is it that all the players look out of form? Apart from Alessia Russo, how many of those players are actually performing?

"There has to come a point where you have to start questioning Renee. As much as I like Renee and everything she's done.

"Not to compare the two, but it feels like Michael Carrick at Man Utd where you feel like someone has taken the team so far but now what?

"We're all waiting for Arsenal to kick on. That tonight disappointed me because it looked flat."
