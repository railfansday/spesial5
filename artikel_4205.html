Vital to survival, missing from policy: Why working donkeys are being overlooked

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

New research from a project led by the University of Portsmouth in collaboration with Maasai Mara University in Kenya and Haramaya University in Ethiopia has found that working donkeys in East Africa are routinely overlooked in drought planning, veterinary services and emergency relief, despite their importance in transporting water, food and other essential supplies.
