Grand Theft Auto 6 has plenty to do, from battling the Prince of Darkness to rescuing members of Congress caught in sex swing accidents. Between all the zany and off-the-wall shenanigans, sometimes the most fun encounters happen right in your own backyard.

Tales of streakers were some of the first leaks to breach GTA 6, and those on the hunt may be a bit disappointed.

Hidden within the humble little town of Hamlet is a wrestling legend ready to take on all challengers, while putting his own pride on the line. If you’re up to snuff and want to become the next Backyard Wrestling Champion, read on to claim your spoils.

In order to find the Champ, you’ll need to head back to Hamlet once you regain control of Jason in Chapter 2. Head to the street between the grocery store and the gas station towards the middle of town. Look for the backyard of one of the houses containing a ring, a trailer, and a big Backyard Wrestling banner.

Knock on the door of the trailer to get some dialogue from the Champ, where he’ll refuse to come out. Toss a nearby can or bottle, then knock again for him to make his entrance, toss Jason to the ground, and drag him into the ring for a little one-on-one playtime. If you can last against the Gentrifier, you’ll be the new Champ.

Once he’s introduced himself, the Gentrifier will put Jason into a series of grapple-based Quick Time Events. Make sure you boost your Stamina with a drink or snack of some kind before you get started to ensure you’ve got as much time to mash as possible.

Once you clear two of these mash-fests, the Gentrifier will toss Jason to the ground before going for his finishing move, the Eviction Notice. React with Square on PS5/X on XBOX to dive out of the wave, leaving him open for a counter. Don’t waste your time stomping on him. Instead, climb the same post he tried to use, and end it with a soaring People’s Elbow.

Once he’s down and out, you can ransack his trailer for some cash, some drugs, his own stylin’ shades, and a one-of-one Luchador mask that can serve as a disguise for either character. A fantastic little token of a victory hard-fought, cementing Jason as the Backyard Wrestling Champion.
