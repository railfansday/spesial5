Why iQIYI Stock Tanked Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Chinese company released underwhelming quarterly results.

Chinese online entertainment and video streaming platform operator iQIYI (IQ -17.90%) posted its latest quarterly results Monday morning, and they didn't impress U.S. investors. The company's American Depositary Shares (ADSs) took a nearly 18% hit following the financial release, which came up short on both the top and bottom lines.

iQIYI's third-quarter revenue was just over 6 billion yuan ($900 million), down 10% year over year. That was due in no small part to a 6% decline in membership services revenue.

In a slightly more positive development, the specialty tech company narrowed its net loss under generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) to 173 million yuan ($26 million) from the year-ago deficit of nearly 249 million yuan ($37 million). On a per-ADS basis, the quarter's net loss was 0.19 yuan ($0.03).

Those results compare unfavorably to the consensus analyst estimates, which were revenue of 6.1 billion yuan ($915 million) and a net loss of 0.08 yuan ($0.01) per ADS.

iQIYI is a company in transformation, as it's attempting to shift to long-form video social media offerings. It's also emphasizing the increased role of artificial intelligence (AI) in its operations; it particularly hopes to leverage the technology to bolster its content and improve its financial results.

The latter goal is clearly not being met, as iQIYI remained well in the red despite narrowing its losses. Investors also weren't forgiving of the top-line slide, particularly since management took pains to say its content was No. 1 in terms of popularity across several major categories.

Popularity doesn't match performance, and I'd imagine the company's equity won't recover meaningfully until it does. I'd pass on owning iQIYI for now.
