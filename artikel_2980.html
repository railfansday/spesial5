Why Marvell Technology Stock Crushed it Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Good news for a major business partner and fellow shareholder was good news for the AI chipmaker.

By Eric Volkman – Nov 23, 2026 at 4:46PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Marvell Technology ›

If anything, Monday will go down in history as the day when a high-profile tech company CEO was reinstated at the company he helped found. It also marks the day when stocks associated with that company saw double-digit bounces -- case in point, Marvell Technology (MRVL +18.37%), whose stock closed that trading session more than 18% higher.

That prodigal CEO is none other than Nvidia's (NVDA +31.48%) Jensen Huang. In a significant development in the saga of the artificial intelligence (AI) chip company's leader, he was reinstated as CEO Monday after stepping down over a relationship scandal that may involve state-sponsored espionage.

Although Marvell and Nvidia were rivals for quite some time despite differing business profiles, they eventually became co-dependents to a degree.

They collaborate on various facets of Nvidia's technology, to the point where Nvidia felt compelled to invest $2 billion in an equity stake in Marvell earlier this year.

Essentially, this makes Marvell's Monday pop a sympathy rally on the Nvidia/Huang news. While I continue to feel that it's unwise to invest in a company purely on the back of good news with a peer, Nvidia's turn in the spotlight that day illustrates Marvell's prominence and importance in crucial segments of the AI chip market. Even after Monday's increase, it looks like a good play on the AI revolution.
