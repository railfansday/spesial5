Montverde Madness in Michigan, as Scottie and ‘Em take down the Pistons

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Raptors needed a win last night, like a person dying of thirst needs a beverage. As the adage goes, you can lead a horse to water, but you can't make them drink; and if you're Scottie Barnes, you can lead the Raptors to a win, even if you're playing without RJ Barrett and Collin Murray-Boyles.

In a game that came down to the wire, Scottie Barnes took over in clutch time, taking the ball to the bucket and dropping it in to put the Raptors up 103-102 over the team that's currently third in the Eastern Conference. A Cade Cunningham pass that was picked off by Allen Graves got the Raptors a crucial possession that resulted in them getting two free throws from Immanuel Quickley.

This forced a three from Detroit to stay alive, giving Scottie Barnes the game-winning contest over Kevin Huerter, letting Toronto seal the game, 105-102. While the first win against the Chicago Bulls came easier and let the Raptors make their mark on the NBA, this win was a life vest for Raptors fans, showing the masses what this team can do, even when down numerous crucial pieces.

The drafting of Collin-Murray Boyles and Allen Graves in back to back years felt like asking for clones of Scottie Barnes on the Raptors, and with Boyles sitting out tonight's game on concussion protocol, the rookie had his chance to put his stamp on the game, and that, he did. The rookie was the team's second leading scorer after Scottie, posting 20 points and 10 boards, career highs in both, as he stepped into the starting role. Deadly from three, the forward hit two treys for the Raptors.

Cade Cunningham had kind words for Barnes after the game, reminiscing that "that's my brother, we had English class together," while praising his development into an all-around offensive talent. Indeed, Cunningham and Barnes were teammates and classmates in high school, part of the legendary 2019-2020 Montverde Academy basketball roster. "Cade and 'Em" have formed a crux of high school basketball discourse, with endless debate about a hypothetical matchup between them and the 2015-16 Chino Hills team, led by the Ball brothers Lonzo, LiAngelo, and LaMelo.

But, with Scottie Barnes ascending in the league, he's making Montverde proud, even if it's at the expense of perhaps their favourite son.

Barnes himself had 29 points, 5 assists, and 5 rebounds, a balanced statline that demonstrates yet again Scottie's development being far from a straight line. Holding down the fort without many of his usual primary or auxiliary scorers, Scottie gave the Raptors their first win in seven games, in what may be a signature victory for the struggling team. This may be the Raptors' most impressive win of the season, and may be the turning point for a team to rally around the ascent of Scottie and Allen Graves, riding that rocket high into the Eastern Conference standings.
