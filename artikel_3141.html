‘Daredevil’s Charlie Cox Wanted To Audition For James Bond But Was Told He Was “Too Old”: “But Robert Pattinson Is The Same Age As Me!”

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Charlie Cox wanted the opportunity to audition for the role of James Bond in the upcoming Denis Villeneuve-directed film.

However, the Daredevil actor was told he was “too old” to play the 007 agent. Amazon MGM Studios ultimately cast Robert Pattinson in the iconic role.

“Can I say something? I asked to audition for that,” said in an interview with Virgin Radio UK. “Do you know what they told me? ‘Too old.’ But Rob is the same age as me! He does look good for it, I have to be fair. Pattinson turned 40 this year, while Cox is turning 44 in December. Other names who reportedly tested for the role included Jack Lowden, Josh O’Connor, Callum Turner and Nicholas Hoult. Watch on Deadline

“I thought it was going to be Jack Lowden or maybe Callum Turner,” Cox said. “What an amazing career [Pattinson] has carved out. Some of the stuff that guy has done… worked with directors who have gone on to be huge, huge auteurs, but when he worked with them, it was their first film or stuff.”

After Amazon MGM Studios announced Pattinson would take on the James Bond role, the Dune: Part Three actor said, “James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime. I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya, and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”
