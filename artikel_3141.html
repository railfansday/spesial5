Why Duolingo Stock Skyrocketed Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The education company's patience is paying off.

Shares of Duolingo (DUOL +25.99%) spiked 26% on Wednesday after the popular learning app operator announced a surge in users.

Duolingo's daily active users soared 29% to 64.9 million in the third quarter. That's a significant acceleration from the 23% growth the education company reported in the second quarter and 21% in the first quarter.

"This year, we're prioritizing teaching better and user growth, and we're seeing that strategy paying off," CEO Luis von Ahn said. "Our user growth in every region of the world and in every subject we teach shows that we still have an enormous opportunity ahead of us."

Yet Duolingo's monetization efforts are beginning to bear fruit. Revenue rose 14% to $309 million. Operating cash flow also increased by 6% to $89.4 million.

These strong results prompted Duolingo to lift its full-year financial forecast. The company now expects revenue to rise by 18% to $1.2 billion in 2026.

Duolingo's upbeat guidance helped assuage investors' fears about artificial intelligence (AI)-driven threats and bolstered their confidence in management's long-term growth strategy.
