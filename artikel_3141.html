Nickelodeon Kids’ Choice Awards: BTS, ‘Stranger Things’ & ‘KPop Demon Hunters’ Among Winners

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

BTS, Stranger Things and Percy Jackson and the Olympians were among the big winners at today’s Kid’s Choice Awards on CBS and Nickelodeon.

BTS took the award for Favorite Music Group or Duo, while Stranger Things was named Favorite Family TV Show. Individual acting awards went to Jack Griffo (Max Thunderman, The Thundermans: Undercover) and Jenna Ortega (Wednesday Addams, Wednesday), among others, while Favorite Song From A Movie went to KPop Demon Hunters‘ “Golden.”
