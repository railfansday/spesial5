Uber Eats starts drone deliveries with Zipline in Houston

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Customers can choose "by drone" in the app, with Dallas and more US cities next.

Zipline’s delivery pod sets down an Uber Eats order. Houston customers can now choose “by drone” in the app.

Zipline has started delivering Uber Eats orders by drone in Houston, the first launch under the companies’ August partnership. Dallas comes next, and the pair are aiming for 1 million drone deliveries a day by 2029.

Uber Eats customers in Houston can now get food delivered by drone, Zipline announced on Tuesday. Customers pick “by drone” on the Uber Eats home screen, and a Zipline aircraft brings the order to their door. Dallas is next, followed by more US cities.

It is the first launch under the companies’ August partnership, which aims for 1 million drone deliveries a day by 2029. Uber also agreed to invest in Zipline, but did not say how much.

Zipline’s aircraft fly at up to 70mph and carry up to 8 pounds over 10 miles. They stay about 330 feet up and lower orders to the ground on a tether, which Zipline says keeps the noise down.

“Being able to reach people’s homes autonomously in under 10 minutes opens up a whole new opportunity for Uber Eats and its customers,” said Uber’s autonomous delivery head, Aaron Emrich.

Zipline says more than 80% of its orders are new business for restaurants, not orders they would have got anyway. It has made 1 million deliveries in the Dallas area alone.

Houston is not new ground for Zipline. In April, it opened early access there for the first 5,000 residents through its own app.

The drone delivery race is speeding up. Zipline raised $1.5B at a $21.5B valuation this month, as Walmart agreed to add 300 drone sites. Amazon plans to offer drone delivery in nearly 500 US cities and towns by the end of the year.

Food delivery rivals are moving too. DoorDash has unveiled its own drones, and Irish startup Manna is building a US drone factory in Oklahoma.
