YM go Global as new stadium sponsor is named

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The new name will replace The Herbert Direct Stadium, with new signage recently installed at the ground.

Horsham YM have thanked Herbert Direct for their valued support and sponsorship, saying they are grateful for the company’s backing and the part they have played in supporting football in Horsham.

Herbert Direct are a family-run business based in Broadbridge Heath, supplying kitchen and furniture components to customers across the UK. Founded by John and Sharon Herbert in 2003, the company has strong local roots.

The club now welcome HSM Global as the new stadium naming sponsor. Founded in 2008, HSM Global are a specialist logistics and supply chain business with operations in Horsham and South Wales. Their services span aerospace logistics, global logistics and fulfilment, supporting customers across the UK and internationally.

Geoff Foreman, chairman of Horsham YM Football Club, said: “First and foremost, we want to say a heartfelt thank-you to everyone at Herbert Direct. Their support has been greatly appreciated, and we are proud to have carried their name at our ground.’

"We are also delighted to welcome HSM Global as our new stadium sponsor. It is fantastic to build relationships with businesses that has a presence here in Horsham, and we look forward to welcoming the HSM Global team to the club.”

Hanson Masih, Managing Director of HSM Global, said: "We are delighted to be supporting Horsham YM and to become the club’s stadium naming sponsor.’

"Horsham is an important part of our business’s story, and this partnership is an opportunity to give something back locally. We look forward to getting to know the players, volunteers, and supporters, and to building a positive relationship with the club.”

On the pitch, YM continue to struggle. They lost 3-0 at AFC Varndeanians in the SCFL Premier last Saturday and had Conner Collcutt sent off.

YM will hope for better in Saturday’s derby at home to Billingshurst.
