Transfer news LIVE: Paz to Arsenal, Nusa deal; new Man Utd target; Chelsea, Liverpool latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The January transfer window is approaching, with Arsenal, Chelsea, Manchester United, Liverpool and Tottenham all looking to strengthen their squads moving into the second half of the season. Arsenal are believed to hold an interest in Como midfielder Nico Paz but face competition from several clubs, including Real Madrid and Paris Saint-Germain.

Bournemouth’s Eli Junior Kroupi and Antonio Nusa of RB Leipzig are also thought to be admired at the Emirates Stadium. Juventus’ Kenan Yildiz is another that Mikel Arteta likes as the Gunners look to reinforce their frontline. Mauricio Pochettino was announced as the new Manchester United manager last week, replacing the departed Michael Carrick, and he could make Calvin Bassey his first signing in an attempt to bolster a leaky defence.

Chelsea are confident of securing a deal for Alex Scott when the window opens, but Bournemouth insist that he is not for sale in another U-turn from the Cherries. Liverpool are eyeing Gilberto Mora, the Mexican starlet, and they also like Lamine Camara, who almost joined Chelsea on Deadline Day in the summer. Follow all the latest transfer news, rumours and gossip live below!

Manchester United have been dealt a fresh blow in their pursuit of a left-back.

The Red Devils are worryingly short in that position, especially after injuries to both Luke Shaw and Patrick Dorgu this season.

It left now-sacked manager Michael Carrick to field right-backs Diogo Dalot and Noussair Mazraoui in that role in the absence of his two natural options.

Newcastle United’s Lewis Hall was a top target during the summer transfer window, but United did not act on that interest, with the former Chelsea defender penning a new deal on Tyneside instead.

Manchester United’s alternative targets to Mauricio Pochettino have been revealed.

The Red Devils were on the hunt for a new manager after sacking Michael Carrick last week, with the former midfielder relieved of his duties after just one win in 11 matches.

It left the Premier League side mired in the top-flight relegation zone, and searching for their eighth permanent head coach since Sir Alex Ferguson’s retirement in 2013.

Vinicius Junior has admitted that Arsenal’s push to sign him in the summer has impacted his form this season.

The Gunners held strong interest in him as they looked to kick on from their Premier League title win and make a marquee signing in attack.

Vinicius had entered the final 12 months of his contract at Real Madrid in the summer and there was strong speculation that he could leave the club.

With Real and Vinicius at a contract impasse and little progress made in negotiations, Arsenal made it clear they would meet the winger’s financial demands.

Mikel Arteta was desperate to sign the 26-year-old but the Brazilian ultimately ended up signing a new six-contract at the Santiago Bernabeu.

However, Vinicius has had an unhappy time at Real this season. In 12 appearances under Jose Mourinho, he has registered just one goal and three assists.

Crystal Palace are bottom of the Premier League table and without a manager after parting ways with Pierre Sage last week, after the Frenchman took just five wins from 16 games in all competitions.

They are, though, closing in on the appointment of former Tottenham and Brentford boss Thomas Frank on a two-and-a-half-year deal, with the Dane set to be in the dugout against Coventry.

It is now seven goals and nine assists in 20 appearances across all competitions for both club and country for Antonio Nusa so far this season, the latest of which has prompted Arsenal fans on social media to demand a January transfer.

On X, formerly Twitter, one user wrote: “Bring Antonio Nusa to Arsenal”, while another Gunners supporter went one further to suggest a stunning double deal.

They wrote: “Nusa + Kroupi. £170M combined. “Two elite young attacking talents who can transform our frontline and secure the future. Berta, get it done. We are building a dynasty.”

Erling Haaland has said he is happy at Manchester City, after commenting on the club’s financial scandal.

During the autumn international break, an independent commission found Man City guilty of all charges related to serious breaches of the Premier League’s financial rules over a nine-season period between 2009/10 and 2018/19.

While City deny any wrongdoing and have launched an appeal, serious sanctions - such as points deductions, fines likely and even expulsion from the Premier League - are likely if that fails.

According to reports, a hearing is to take place in December and a decision on the appeal expected in early 2027.

Should Man City relegated or be issued a huge points deduction, many have speculated that a number of the club’s stars will likely depart, including Haaland.

However, the Norway international - who is valued at £183million on transfermarkt and would have a host of clubs queuing up for his signature were he to leave the Etihad Stadium - has set the record straight on his future with a transfer update.

Declan Rice insists that he is relishing the competition from Alex Scott with England, as Arsenal are linked with a potential big-money transfer swoop for the Bournemouth star.

Scott continues to go from strength to strength for both club and country, earning his first senior caps for the Three Lions during the previous international break in recognition for his fine form in the Premier League.

The 23-year-old made his England debut off the bench in September’s 2-0 away win over the Czech Republic in the Nations League, with Rice having pulled out of the squad, before then being handed his maiden start by Thomas Tuchel in the 7-0 demolition of Croatia last month.

Scott picked up a thigh injury during that camp that it was feared could keep him out for up to two months, though the issue was not as serious as first feared and he was back on the Bournemouth bench in their last game against Ipswich.

Arsenal are said to be the latest club to show an interest in Como attacking midfielder Nico Paz.

However, the Gunners could face competition from Premier League rivals Chelsea and Manchester United for the Argentina international’s signature, while Paris Saint-Germain have also been linked.

All four clubs, though, could be trumped by Real Madrid as they retain a buy-back clause.

Fulham defender Calvin Bassey has emerged as a shock target for new Manchester United boss Mauricio Pochettino.

That is according to the Telegraph, who claim the Nigeria international is expected to feature prominently on Pochettino’s list of targets for the January transfer window.

Bassey is approaching the final six months of his contract at Fulham, so could be available for a cut-price fee.

Belgium manager Rudi Garcia has revealed that he had two interviews at Manchester United about taking over as the club’s manager.

He told The Athletic: “I’d like to work in England and I met Darren Fletcher and John Murtough.

“It was an interim role and they asked me what I would do once it was over. I told them I would do so well that I would be staying in Manchester, but they gave the job to Ralf [Rangnick].”
