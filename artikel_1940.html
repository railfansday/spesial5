Celtic to 'auction Benjamin Nygren' after source's Premier League reveal - Keith Wyness

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Celtic are likely to sell star midfielder Benjamin Nygren by playing the interested clubs off against each other next summer.

That is according to former Aberdeen chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Bhoys could potentially net around £25million for the Swedish star.

This comes after Football Insider revealed at the end of October that Nygren is already being tracked by several Premier League clubs.

Wyness believes that Celtic are likely to block any exit for Nygren in January, but could then look to cash in during the early stages of next summer.

The 25-year-old ended as the top scorer for the Glasgow giants last term, and already has 15 goal involvements so far this season.

Nygren's stock has risen dramatically since his move from Nordsjaelland in 2025, and clubs in England are beginning to take notice.

Former Aberdeen chief Wyness - who served as CEO at Pittodrie between 2000 and 2004 and now runs a consultancy business advising elite clubs - believes that Celtic will embrace the high level of interest in Nygren next summer.

The Scottish businessman expects Martin O'Neill's side to try and drive an "auction" for their ace, in order to force his price tag as high as possible.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how Celtic are likely to handle Nygren's future.

"I don't think a January sale is the right thing, and I don't think it's the right solution for Celtic either because they've been very patchy in their form this year," he said.

"They've got to keep one eye on Europe again, so they've got to win the league if they can. I'd keep him, but I'd sell him into an auction in the summer. But with Celtic, as we discussed, sell him early and reinvest the money early is what they've got to do with Nygren.

"Now in terms of valuation, I think you're looking at £25m. I think he's that sort of quality of player and again on the back of a good season.

"Celtic can't make the mistake of selling him and then waiting until August or September to reinvest the money; get it done immediately and have a plan ready to start financing things even ahead of time before you actually have the sale completed."

Football Insider revealed on Sunday that Celtic have set their sights on a move to sign BK Hacken holding midfielder Abdoulaye Doumbia in January.

The 19-year-old has enjoyed an impressive campaign in the Swedish Allsvenskan, making 25 appearances so far, and chipping in with two goals and three assists.

However, sources say that Doumbia is on the radar of several clubs across Europe, which could make it difficult for Celtic to win the race for him.

The young starlet is building a growing reputation for himself, and that looks set to potentially secure him a big move this winter.
