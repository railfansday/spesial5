Tenth-tier Westfield celebrate player’s international call-up

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Westfield – of the Southern Combination League Division 1 – have had Michael Rwabigwi called up to represent Rwanda in the upcoming U23 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Cameroon.

The club said: “It’s an incredible achievement for Mikey and a hugely proud moment for everyone connected with Westfield FC.”

Rwanda are due to play at home to Cameroon on Wednesday (Nob 11) and away to the same opponents six days later.

Westfield added: “Everyone at the club wishes Michael the very best of luck as he prepares to pull on his country's shirt and represent Rwanda on the international stage.

"Congratulations, Mikey! The whole of Westfield FC is behind you!”

A Westfield spokesman told us: “We were contacted by the federation asking if we could release him for international duty. It’s a deserved call-up.”
