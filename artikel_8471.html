Some beauty brands and stores still pay you for clean empty containers in store credit, loyalty points, or a free product. Save the empties from products you already rebuy, rinse them out, and return them when you restock, since some credit must be spent that day.

Lush and Kiehl’s pay about $1 in credit for each eligible container of theirs you return clean, and Summer Fridays sends a $25 gift card for five.

Because the reward is credit with the same brand or store, it saves money only on something you’d buy there anyway. A $1 credit toward a $30 cream you didn’t need still costs you $29.

Programs change, too. MAC used to give a free lipstick for six empties, and its Back-to-MAC program now takes them only for recycling. Check a program’s current terms before you save up containers for it.

Lush, Kiehl’s, and Summer Fridays count only their own containers, so take other brands’ empties to L’Occitane or Credo.

Rinse every container before you go, since Lush and Credo pay only for clean empties. Sign up for Kiehl’s and Credo’s rewards programs before your first drop-off, because their points go to a member account.

Keep a bag of rinsed empties under the bathroom sink, and take it along the next time you restock at one of these stores.
