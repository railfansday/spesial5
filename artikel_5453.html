Mediwhale Announces First FDA 510(k) Clearance for Its Retinal-AI Cardiovascular Risk Assessment Software

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

SEOUL, South Korea, Nov. 27, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Mediwhale, an AI healthcare company that uses noninvasive retinal imaging to assess the risk of cardiometabolic diseases, today announced that it has received U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) 510(k) clearance for Dr. Noon CVD, its retinal-AI cardiovascular risk assessment software. The clearance paves the way for Mediwhale to introduce Dr. Noon CVD into U.S. clinical practice.

Dr. Noon CVD combines AI analysis of retinal images with age and sex to estimate an individual's 10-year risk of a first atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) event. Results classify patients into one of three risk categories to support clinicians' cardiovascular risk assessment alongside other clinical information.[1]

Cardiovascular prevention in the United States is placing greater emphasis on earlier intervention informed by individualized risk assessment, as reflected in the 2026 ACC/AHA multisociety guideline on the management of dyslipidemia.[2] Mediwhale aims to contribute to this approach by offering clinicians a noninvasive way to assess cardiovascular risk through retinal imaging.

"Retinal cameras are already in primary care clinics across the United States, where they are used every day for diabetic eye exams," said Kevin Taegeun Choi, CEO of Mediwhale. "With FDA clearance, that same retinal exam can now do more: support cardiovascular risk assessment. We intend to be first to bring this to U.S. primary care, working with partners that already operate these networks and building U.S. real-world data as we go."

Mediwhale is an AI healthcare company that uses noninvasive retinal imaging to assess cardiometabolic disease risk. In the United States, Dr. Noon CVD is FDA-cleared software for cardiovascular risk assessment. For more information, visit https://mediwhale.com/.

[2] 2026 ACC/AHA/AACVPR/ABC/ACPM/ADA/AGS/APhA/ASPC/NLA/PCNA Guideline on the Management of Dyslipidemia. J Am Coll Cardiol. Published March 13, 2026.
