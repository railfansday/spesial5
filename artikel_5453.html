THINK Surgical Receives the 2026 AAHKS Industry Innovation Award

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

DALLAS, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The American Association of Hip and Knee Surgeons (AAHKS) recognized THINK Surgical as the 2026 AAHKS Industry Innovation Award recipient during the 2026 AAHKS Annual Meeting in Dallas, Texas. The Industry Innovation Award, now in its fifth year, is a competitive product award for companies exhibiting at the meeting, recognizing cutting-edge innovation in the field of orthopaedic hip and knee arthroplasty by exhibitors. All industry partners and exhibitors are invited to highlight their newest products and services by applying for the award.

The Industry Innovation Award was conferred in the Exhibit Hall during the Annual Meeting reception. Representatives from THINK Surgical were present to accept this prestigious honor. The award includes recording an episode of AAHKS Amplified, to discuss the details of their award-winning innovation and being recognized as winners of the Industry Innovation Award for the next year.
