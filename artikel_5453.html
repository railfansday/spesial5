McDonald's is bringing Pokémon Happy Meals back to U.S. restaurants

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Each meal comes with a toy and two trading cards, and the promotion runs through Dec. 14 while supplies last

McDonald's $MCD is bringing Pokémon Happy Meals back to participating U.S. restaurants, with the offer starting Nov. 10, the company said.

The meals include 15 collectible toys and 15 collectible trading cards, according to McDonald’s. The Pokémon Company said on its website the offer runs through Dec. 14 at participating McDonald’s restaurants, while supplies last.

Each Happy Meal is packaged in a box with one of three designs themed after Pikachu, Umbreon or Torchic, Pokémon said. Inside is a miniature toy shaped like a Happy Meal box that resembles Pikachu, Rowlet, Sprigatito or another Pokémon character.

Every meal also includes two Pokémon Trading Card Game cards, Pokémon said. The 15 cards in the set include seven foil cards.

McDonald's said characters in the toy collection include Pikachu, Eevee, Charmander, Squirtle and Umbreon. The miniature toys can be used as charms on items such as keys and bags, among other accessories, according to ABC News. The foil cards showcase Pokémon including Pikachu, Mimikyu, Lucario, Snorlax and Eevee.

Customers can scan a QR code on the Happy Meal box to reach a digital game, McDonald's said. In the game, players hunt for the 15 Pokémon toys concealed throughout a virtual setting that includes a classroom and a playground.

The release coincides with the 30th anniversary of Pokémon, according to ABC News. McDonald's previously offered a Pokémon-themed Happy Meal in January 2025 that included stickers and posters along with trading cards.

Pokémon Happy Meals are also rolling out in other countries, according to ABC News. Australia, New Zealand, Canada, France, Fiji and Spain are scheduled to receive them this month, and additional countries will get the collection through February 2027. Pokémon said the meals will reach McDonald's restaurants in other regions on a schedule posted on its website.
