Lucid missed earnings expectations — and tapped its Saudi backer for $1.75 billion more

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Lucid's total liquidity would reach $3.75 billion with the expanded Public Investment Fund facility, up from $2.0 billion at quarter-end

Lucid $LCID Group fell short of Wall Street's third-quarter estimates on Monday, according to CNBC. The electric vehicle maker also announced a $1.75 billion increase to the size of a term loan facility it can tap in stages, provided by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, its anchor shareholder.

Lucid reported revenue of $350 million, up 4% from a year earlier, and a loss of $2.74 per share. Analysts compiled by LSEG expected revenue of $407 million and a loss of $2.28 per share, according to CNBC.

The net loss was $830.9 million, compared with $978.4 million a year earlier. The adjusted EBITDA loss was $726.3 million, compared with $717.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. Free cash flow consumption fell 30% from the prior quarter to $1.04 billion.

Lucid ended the quarter with $2.0 billion in total liquidity. With the increased facility, that figure would be $3.75 billion. The company said it expects that level to bankroll its day-to-day activity and major spending through Q4 2027, a window that covers the launch of production for its midsize vehicle.

"We are grateful for the continued financial support from our anchor shareholder, which further energizes us to remain focused on execution and on our commitments as we build a stronger, sustainable Lucid," CEO Silvio Napoli said in a statement, according to CNBC.

Lucid said it has achieved $0.8 billion in savings so far this year under its $1.4 billion cash flow improvement plan and remains on track to meet the full-year target. Construction of its second manufacturing plant in Saudi Arabia is progressing to plan, with inauguration expected in early 2027. Midsize production remains planned for the second half of 2027, and the company expects to announce reveal and production dates in the first quarter of 2027.

Lucid plans to share its medium-term roadmap and objectives in the opening months of next year. The company withdrew its forward outlook and several of its goals in May, according to CNBC, explaining that new management needed time to put together a turnaround plan.

The results follow the "operating reset" Napoli put in place after taking over as CEO in June. In early October, Lucid produced 2,954 vehicles and delivered 3,806 in the third quarter as it cut output to work down inventory. Its Arizona plant ran on a single shift for the full quarter after previously running two.

Lucid stock dropped about 5% in extended trading on Monday.
