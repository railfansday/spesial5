Stock futures rise as Nvidia gains on an earnings beat and a solid outlook

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nasdaq-100 futures were up 1% and S&P 500 futures added 0.58% Wednesday morning after the S&P 500 fell 1% on Tuesday

Nvidia $NVDA stock rose close to 3% in premarket trading on Wednesday after the chipmaker boosted its sales forecast for the fiscal year that ends in January 2028. The company is forecasting 75% revenue growth year over year. Its fiscal third-quarter profit and revenue beat analyst estimates, according to CNBC.

Nvidia stock had jumped 4% in extended trading on Tuesday. The results came after days of turmoil over Jensen Huang’s resignation as CEO, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Nasdaq $NDAQ-100 futures were up 1% on Wednesday morning, S&P 500 futures added 0.6% and Dow Jones Industrial Average futures gained 0.3%. Shares of CoreWeave and Nebius Group rose 8% and 10% in extended trading on Tuesday.

"The fact that they gave guidance is huge," Gene Munster, managing partner at Deepwater Asset Management, told CNBC. "That is a very bullish sign. I expect the stock to move higher based just on that alone."

Wall Street fell on Tuesday. The Dow dropped 1.6%, the S&P 500 slipped 1% and the Nasdaq Composite shed 0.7%. The S&P 500 closed at its lowest level since July 29, according to CNBC.

Traders are also waiting on figures for new building permits and homes started. The Federal Reserve is due to release minutes from its October meeting at 2 p.m. ET. Fed policymakers held the federal funds rate’s target range steady at 3.75% to 4% at their meeting last month. Oil prices fell as figures pointing to larger U.S. stockpiles counterbalanced renewed clashes in the Persian Gulf.

Lowe’s, Target $TGT and TJX Cos., the parent of TJ Maxx, reported earnings Wednesday morning.

Anthropic is due to launch an initial public offering later this week. Its stock is likely to price higher than the existing $70-to-$78 band, according to CNBC.

European markets were mixed in morning trade. The Stoxx 600 traded a touch below unchanged, while the FTSE 100 lost 0.3% and the CAC 40 shed close to 0.1%. Germany’s DAX advanced 0.2%.

Most Asian markets closed higher. South Korea’s Kospi led the region, gaining 2% to 5,067.44 as chip stocks rose. Japan’s Nikkei 225 ended the session up 0.3% at 59,024.28, and mainland China’s CSI 300 rose 0.2% to finish at 3,983.77. Hong Kong’s Hang Seng Index had fallen 0.9% as the session entered its last hour. Australia’s S&P/ASX 200 slipped 0.7%, extending its losing run to five consecutive sessions.
