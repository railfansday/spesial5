Is Capital Abandoning Altcoins? Ethereum and Solana Price Struggle as Bitcoin Dominance Rises

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The crypto market is showing another worrying sign for altcoin investors. Ethereum and Solana price are struggling to recover as Bitcoin dominance climbs toward 61%. Ethereum trades at the $2,050 level, while Solana changes hands below $85. Both coins remain under pressure as market sentiment deteriorates.

Bitcoin dominance has reached 60.5%, gaining one percentage point over the past 30 days. The shift shows investors are favoring Bitcoin over altcoins during the market downturn.

Bitcoin dominance measures BTC’s share of the total crypto market cap. When the metric rises, Bitcoin is outperforming the rest of the market. The reading puts Bitcoin’s market share at 60.4%, compared with 59.4% one month earlier. Ethereum dominance, meanwhile, has fallen to 10.3%, losing 0.65 percentage points over the same period.

One metric to understand is that Bitcoin dominance can rise even when BTC itself loses value. Investors do not necessarily need to buy Bitcoin aggressively. Altcoin valuations simply need to fall faster.

This appears consistent with Wednesday’s market conditions. The total crypto market cap has dropped around 4.1% over 24 hours. Only 27 of the top 100 cryptocurrencies were trading higher.

Ethereum has also struggled to maintain its position. Its market share has declined while Bitcoin continues commanding a larger portion of the market. The latest dominance figures offer little encouragement for altcoins.

The sentiment picture is equally concerning. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index currently reads 29, placing the market firmly in fear territory. Fear & Greed Index has fallen from 34 a week earlier and 57 one month ago. Investors appear increasingly reluctant to take risks across crypto markets.

The Ethereum price has struggled to attract buyers despite trading near $2,060. ETH has lost 9.1% over seven days. Solana price faces similar pressure. SOL has fallen 9.6% over the same period, trading under $83. Both assets are struggling to establish a convincing recovery.

Ethereum price weakness is particularly important because ETH traditionally plays a major role in altcoin market recoveries. Its declining dominance suggests investors remain cautious about moving beyond Bitcoin.

Solana’s performance tells a similar story. Despite its established position among major altcoins, SOL has struggled to outperform during the latest downturn. Neither asset currently provides convincing evidence of an aggressive return to altcoin risk.

Bitcoin dominance has gained one percentage point in 30 days while Ethereum’s market share has declined, and sentiment remains firmly negative. This creates an uncomfortable combination for altcoin holders.
