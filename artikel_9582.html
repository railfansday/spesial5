A Major Liquor Brand Is Making A Comeback After Filing For Bankruptcy

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

And its comeback comes with a few more spirits to its roster.

Earlier this year, college kids everywhere had reason to worry about losing Stoli Vodka. Back in January, creditors were pushing for the liquidation of Stoli Group's U.S. operations, putting both Stoli Vodka and Kentucky Owl bourbon at risk. Now, however, the company's future in the States is looking a whole lot brighter.

According to the company, it has completed its restructuring after filing for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in 2024. If the case had shifted to Chapter 7, a court-appointed trustee could have taken control of the affected U.S. operations and overseen their liquidation. But Stoli Group has since reached a settlement with creditors of Stoli Group (USA), allowing it to resume supplying products to the American market.

In a press release, Stoli says it already has inventory in a U.S. customs warehouse and is working with longtime distribution partners to get products into participating markets.

In addition to Stoli Vodka and elit Vodka, Stoli Group is expanding its lineup of products in the States with Moskovskaya Vodka, Rooster Rojo and KAH tequilas, and The Irishman and Writers' Tears Irish whiskeys. Bayou Rum is also getting updates to the rum itself, its packaging, and pricing, with U.S. production of flavored Stoli vodkas and Stoli Ginger Beer, a nonalcoholic mixer, also to come.

"The U.S. has always been an important market for Stoli, and we're eager to share that Stoli is back and better than ever," said Marina Troyanovskaya, chief commercial officer of Stoli Group. "Our products are already here, we have longstanding partners who know our brands, and we're focused on reconnecting with consumers across the country."
