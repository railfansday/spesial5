AS A MEN’S HEALTH style editor, I spend most of my office days wearing business-casual and semi-dressy fits. But when I clock out, I have a closet stacked with hoodies that I love to slip on. From freezing winters in New Jersey during my teen years to long days on campus at Howard University, they’ve always been a core piece in my wardrobe. Thankfully, hoodies have come a long way since then—gone are the days of big, boxy sweats that make you disappear into your clothes. Today, the best hoodies for men aren’t just better in fit, fabric, and function—they actually look good, too.

A hoodie is basically your favorite long-sleeve tee or crewneck—but with a hood attached. Pullovers give you that classic, laid-back vibe, while zip-up styles make it easy to throw on or peel off without messing up your hair. The best hoodies for men are made from soft, smooth fabrics like cotton or fleece that feel great on your skin, and some even come with moisture-wicking tech if you’re hitting the trails, the gym, or just dealing with unpredictable weather.

As Men’s Health editors, we practically live in sweats during the fall and winter, whether we’re heading to the gym, running errands, going on work trips, or curling up on the couch. Over the last five years, our team has tested countless options, from OG brands like Abercrombie and Champion to newer athleisure labels like Alo and Vuori. The best hoodies for men are produced from high-quality materials that will keep you warm and comfortable. Most styles are made of a mix of fabrics like cotton fleece, terry cloth, or a cotton-polyester blend. Look for a hoodie that's naturally breathable, feels good on the skin, and will last for years to come. If you work out in a sweatshirt, opt for a lighter option with moisture-wicking and quick-drying fabrics designed for layering.
