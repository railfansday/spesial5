The 2026 season saw Brooks Koepka make his return to the PGA Tour. Although Koepka had ups and downs and even missed the FedEx Cup Playoffs, he was content with the reaction his return to the league received.

"I felt very at peace with coming back. The reception was fantastic from all the players, caddies, just everybody on tour," Koepka said recently, via Chris McKee. "I kept a good relationship with everybody even when I left, I think that played probably a huge part in that. Yeah, everybody was really receptive. I love the conversations that I've had just throughout the year… Everything was great.
