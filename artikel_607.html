How to Stream 2026 Soul Train Awards, Featuring Tributes to Dolly Parton and Freddie Jackson

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

After last airing in 2023, the awards show makes its TV return, hosted this year by Anthony Anderson.

Following a three-year hiatus, the Soul Train Awards are back for 2026, hosted by actor and comedian Anthony Anderson, and featuring musical tributes to both Freddie Jackson and Dolly Parton.

Broadcast on Friday, Nov. 27 at 8 p.m. PT/ET on both BET and CBS, the awards show is available to watch live on any streaming service that carries either network, namely DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), Philo (with a seven-day free trial), Fubo (with a one-day free trial), Sling or Hulu + Live TV.

The 2026 awards show airs on Friday, Nov. 27 at 8 p.m. PT/ET on BET and, for the first year ever, CBS. The event will also be simulcast across the following networks: BET Her, MTV2, VH1 and Logo.

Since the ceremony airs on BET and CBS, it will also be available to watch live on any streaming service that carries either of the two networks, including DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), Philo (with a seven-day free trial), Fubo (with a one-day free trial), Sling and Hulu + Live TV. The Soul Train Awards will also stream live on the higher-tier Paramount+ Premium plan, however the streamer does not offer a free trial.

To claim the free trial, select your preferred Signature package. Once it prompts you to choose how many Gemini devices you’d like to connect, you’ll select “0 – Stream without the Gemini Air” as the five-day free trial is only available to customers without a leased Gemini device.

Learn more about each plan option, including how to build your own channel lineup (starting at just $20.99 per month), at directv.com.

Watch BET on Philo, one of the most affordable cable alternatives. Following the Essential plan’s seven-day free trial period, a subscription costs just $25 per month for 70-plus channels.
