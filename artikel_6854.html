Alveole: Product Designer

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Alvéole is on a mission to connect people with nature in cities, one building at a time. As a certified B Corporation since 2018, we believe business can be a force for good.We partner with the world’s leading real estate companies to turn sustainability into an experience that tenants can see, touch, and feel. Through rooftop beehives and educational biodiversity programs, we help our clients meet their environmental goals while engaging communities and strengthening their brands.You can find our work in (and on) thousands of properties across North America and Europe, driven by a belief that business and biodiversity can thrive together.Learn more at alveole.buzz

We're looking for a Product Designer to join our Product team and lead the design of our two digital products: MyHive, our customer engagement platform, and Aura, our biodiversity data and insights platform.

Until now, design has been handled by a mix of engineers, product team members, and an external designer. It's worked, but as our products grow, we need someone who can take full ownership of design, make decisions, and help us build better experiences for our users.

You'll work closely with our Product Director and Engineering team, getting involved in everything from understanding user needs and improving existing features to designing new ones. We're a small team, so you'll have plenty of autonomy, a direct impact on both products, and the opportunity to shape how we approach design at Alvéole.

Location: Fully remote within CanadaType of contract: Permanent, Full-time (40h/week)Salary: 85,000–110,000 CAD annually

🌱 Join a mission-driven company creating meaningful environmental impact across North America and Europe.

🎨 Take real ownership of product design across two growing digital platforms, with the freedom to influence how our products look, feel, and evolve.

🚀 Work closely with a small, collaborative Product and Engineering team where your ideas and decisions will have a visible impact.

🏥 Comprehensive health benefits from Day 1.
