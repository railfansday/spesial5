Faux Fur Jackets Make a Chic Return and H&amp;M’s Chocolate Brown Style Elevates Any Outfit, From Jeans to Dresses

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Winter glamour, without the luxe price tag.

We may earn commission from links on this page, but we only recommend products we love. Promise.

Faux fur has a habit of resurfacing every winter—because nothing else adds instant glamour to a casual outfit quite as quickly. This season the look has gone shorter and chicer, trading floor-sweeping drama for cropped, collared shapes you can bundle up in for a chilly commute and wear straight through to cocktails come evening.

H&M has the version we want. The Faux Fur Jacket sits at the waist and has a chic crop, so nothing interrupts all that plush texture. A funnel collar frames the face, there’s a lining inside for comfort, and it is perfect for tapping into the current chocolate brown trend.

Soft comes up in almost every review, closely followed by warm and stylish. Reviewers also said it is a bit cropped, so take that into account when picking sizing and how you want it to fit. A few also purchased the coat in multiple colors, which shows just how quality it is.

It fits true to size overall, though a few found it to run a little small on the arm length, so if you’re a taller gal you might want to size up as a precaution. The weight is light enough for milder winter days, with enough space for a chunky knit underneath when the temperature drops.

Chocolate brown is the one we’d buy first. That rich, chocolaty shade warms up denim, looks expensive, and complements black and cream alike. Navy blue is the party-season pick, and a fun addition if you’re looking for another fall color outside of the very popular chocolate brown.

Style the brown tonally, with a camel polo neck and tan boots, for that old-money winter vibe. Navy blue comes alive against black—think a velvet mini skirt, sheer tights, and pointed court shoes for the Christmas party.

By day, throw it over a fine-knit sweater, straight-leg jeans, and slouchy suede boots. For drinks or a date night, layer it over a slip dress with strappy heels and gold hoops. This is the perfect coat to carry with you throughout the fall and winter season when you want to elevate a simple outfit.
