Best-Ever Pavlova

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Pavlova is essentially a massive meringue that melts in your mouth. We top our version with whipped cream and fresh berries for an airy and fruity bite that will keep you coming back bite after bite. It may look like a complicated dessert made by only the best chefs, but it's surprisingly easy to make! You just need some patience. The key to perfection is drying it out, which requires you to leave it in a (turned-off) oven overnight after it bakes.

If you’re looking for something a bit more festive, check out our Christmas wreath pavlova for a holiday spin on the classic.

Arrange a rack in the center of the oven; preheat to 300°. In a large bowl, beat egg whites with a large pinch of salt on medium-high speed with a handheld mixer (or in the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the whisk attachment) until foamy, which should take about one minute.

While beating, slowly add sugar and beat until meringue is thick and glossy, which should take about 7 minutes. Beat in vinegar, cornstarch, and vanilla until well combined.

Pile the meringue in the center of a parchment-lined baking sheet. Using a spatula or spoon, spread the mixture into an 8" circle. Make a small well in the center.

Transfer to the oven, and immediately reduce the oven temperature to 250°. Bake the meringue until set but still white, which should take about an hour. Turn off the oven and let the pavlova cool completely in the oven for at least 2 hours and up to overnight. Transfer cooled pavlova to a platter.

Store the unassembled pavlova in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days, keeping it away from humidity. Once topped with whipped cream and fresh fruit, refrigerate and enjoy within a day, as the meringue will soften over time.
