Starbucks Just Dropped An Adorable New Merch Collection—And Fans Are Already Obsessed

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Why yes, I do need another Starbucks cup.

I think I’m convinced Starbucks is determined to take up every inch of cabinet space in our kitchens. Just when I think I’ve seen enough adorable drinkware to last me a lifetime, the coffee chain gives me another reason to reconsider.

Starbucks has teamed up with FriendsWithYou, the Los Angeles-based artistic duo of Samuel Borkson and Arturo Sandoval III, to launch a limited-edition merchandise collection celebrating joy and friendship. The colorful lineup features six drinkware designs starring two adorable characters: Little Cloud Boy and his new barista friend, Fluffy.

The collection officially launches November 24 at select Starbucks locations across the U.S. and Canada, just in time for holiday shopping.

“So we’re all buying these,” one fan commented.

“Yeah I need that,” another wrote, while someone else added, “Omg so freaking cute,” prompting Starbucks itself to respond, “Exactlyyy.”

But one commenter perfectly captured what I imagine many Starbucks merch collectors are feeling right now. “I need you guys to stop with these drops because I keep on buying 😭,” they wrote.

The limited-edition collection is available beginning November 24 at select coffeehouses, although Starbucks hasn’t announced an end date for the release.

And if you’re currently trying to justify buying another Starbucks cup, I wish you and your kitchen cabinets the best of luck.
