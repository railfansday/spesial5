If you have an active subscription to GTA+ you will be able to access optional in-game benefits in GTA 6. I'd say that these are nice to have rather than essential, but it's good to know that the subscription service is still providing some nice unlocks while you step away from GTA Online to explore Leonida.

The GTA+ membership unlocks for GTA 6 include a sports car and garage to store it in, some new ways to farm money in Grand Theft Auto 6, and some new ways to get around the absolutely massive GTA 6 map.

Naturally, to access and claim these bonuses you'll need an active GTA+ membership and an internet connection. What you'll find below are details on all of the unlocks and how to access them, some more details on the GTA+ price and benefits, and other key information you'll need before committing to a purchase.

Remember: If you pre-ordered the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition, you'll get a free month of GTA+ thrown in. Head into the GTA 6 pause menu, access the Store tab, and you can redeem your month of GTA+ from there – this will auto-renew monthly until you cancel it.

So long as you have an active GTA+ membership, you'll be able to access the following in-game benefits in GTA 6. It's worth noting that you won't be able to access all of this from the beginning of the game, with certain aspects only becoming available once you reach certain milestones in the story.

GTA+ is a premium membership service for Grand Theft Auto players. While it's primarily used as a great way to get monthly cash injections in GTA Online (and a whole host of other items), it also lets you access exclusive GTA 6 in-game benefits and access a rotating library of the best Rockstar games.

GTA+ Membership costs $7.99 / €7.99 / £6.99 per month on PS5 and Xbox Series X. The service is completely optional and can be canceled at any time, with membership renewal occurring monthly. For PS5 and Xbox Series X owners, GTA+ membership can be purchased directly from the PlayStation store, Microsoft store, or from within the Start menu in GTA 6 and GTA Online.
