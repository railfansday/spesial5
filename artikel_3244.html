Fortuna Mining Corp. (FSM -1.15%) reported third-quarter results highlighted by significant progress on its West African growth projects and a 17% increase in sales. Management announced the final investment decision for the Seguela plant expansion and the authorization of $250 million in construction contracts for the Diamba Sud project. The company also completed the acquisition of the Bambadji exploration project in Senegal to consolidate its regional land package. While operational performance at the Lindero and Caylloma mines showed growth, the Seguela mine experienced a production shortfall due to a temporary blockade and contractor equipment issues.
