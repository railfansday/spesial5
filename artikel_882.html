Charlton sweep to victory over Lincoln

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Charlton Athletic vs Lincoln City. EFL Championship.

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Charlton Athletic and Lincoln City at The Valley on Tuesday night; Addicks secure their most emphatic win of the season so far as they thump four past Imps.

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

Charlton swept Lincoln aside at The Valley with an impressive 4-0 Sky Bet Championship victory.

Joe Rankin-Costello's first goal of the season quickly broke the deadlock with Ivan Mesik doubling the Addicks' advantage inside the opening quarter of an hour.

Tyreece Campbell's finish early in the second half ended any lingering doubts that the Imps were heading for a fifth straight Championship loss.

Miles Leaburn's stoppage-time goal was nothing less than the utterly dominant home team deserved.

Charlton moved up to 15th in the standings after easily their most fluid performance of the season to make it back-to-back wins on home soil.

The Addicks were rewarded for a fast start as they took the lead in the eighth minute. Rankin-Costello showed clinical composure to strike first time with a right-footed volley after Karlan Grant's cross was headed into his path by Tyler Bindon.

Some of the hosts early play was very slick and they made it 2-0 after 13 minutes through Mesik. The Slovakian international converted at the back post from Campbell's cross from the right side of the penalty box.

Lincoln would have been facing a far heavier deficit by the interval if goalkeeper George Wickens had not made a number of vital saves.

The Imps number one twice pushed over full-blooded attempts by Grant and also denied Matty Godden in the 34th minute as the rampant home side looked to add to their haul.

Wickham made one more stop on the stroke of half-time to swat away Conor Coventry's looping shot which threatened to dip in.

Lincoln failed to have a shot in the first period but their bench was aggrieved that an early tackle in the box by Bindon on Mikkel Ladefoged was not penalised by referee Oliver Langford.

The Imps made a triple change at the start of the second half but quickly fell further behind. Campbell was in the right place to drive the ball home - his sixth goal of the campaign - after Wickens could only parry Godden's shot in the 48th minute.

It continued to be one-way attacking traffic in favour of the Addicks and Wickens turned Grant's deflected strike around his left post.

Coventry was guilty of a glaring miss after being gifted the ball by Ben House in the 81st minute. The Republic of Ireland international had the goal at his mercy but was unable to hit the target from 16 yards.

Campbell kicked the advertising board after Wickens denied him a second goal from a tight angle, the Jamaican international showing electric pace to latch on to Coventry's long pass.

Leaburn added the fourth, pouncing from close range from fellow substitute Josh Edwards' low cross.

"That is probably the best performance since I've been at the football club. I know we have won games here by big margins but that was in League One. We went away to Ipswich and won 3-0 but that was the most complete performance.

"We started really well and should've scored before we did. But when we got the goal we went on. Their keeper was outstanding. We had 33 shots tonight and 13 on target.

"I'm not disappointed with 4-0 because I would've taken it all day but we've had some clear-cut chances tonight which we haven't taken.

"It is a wonderful night. The way we passed the ball, the penetration we had and the chances created, we were excellent.

"We use the word relentless at our football club quite a lot and we demonstrated that tonight.

"Today there was a lot of pressure. It was a must-win game, considering where we were and Lincoln were. The players showed a quality and confidence that defies any negativity anywhere."

"There wasn't anything to be proud of tonight and I can only apologise to the supporters that have travelled and make sure that doesn't happen again. It can't happen again - we can't play like that against any team in the Championship, let alone Wolves on Saturday afternoon.

"We weren't good enough from the first minute to the last, we didn't do the basics well enough. We will go away and assess everything tactical and technical.

"We didn't give ourselves a chance because five minutes into the game it felt like everything they did was positive and we didn't connect passes or take the positive action, which we do whenever we play well.

"We had a little bit of illness in the camp in terms of the team that would have been playing instead of the team that started the game which wasn't ideal, but there is no way we're using that as an excuse. The team was good enough to come to Charlton and put in a better account than what we did."
