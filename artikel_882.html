Blackburn earn derby draw for Mowbray and extend Burnley’s wait for home win

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Todd Cantwell’s penalty earned Blackburn a 2-2 draw at Burnley in their first match since Tony Mowbray stepped back because of health issues.

In a gripping, physical East Lancashire derby, the home side led twice, through Largie Ramazani’s rising first-half drive and Jaydon Banel’s steered finish after the break. But Andri Gudjohnsen scored Blackburn’s first equaliser, then earned the penalty which Cantwell converted.

Burnley remain deep in relegation trouble, without a home league victory for 400 days, having once again failed to hold on to a lead. They have now dropped 25 points from winning positions in the Championship this season.

Blackburn wanted a victory not just for local pride, but to honour Mowbray, who turned 63 on Sunday, and for whom this was to be a 900th match in management before his announcement on Tuesday that he would be stepping back. Their players warmed up in T-shirts bearing the message “We’re with you, Tony” on the back and the number 900 on the front.

Coaches Damien Johnson and Mike Dodds have taken charge in Mowbray’s absence and had to reshuffle the defence when Tom Atcheson was concussed after receiving a boot to the face in the third minute.

The stand-in goalkeeper Aynsley Pears, playing after Balazs Toth sustained a broken hand on international duty with Hungary, pushed over a fierce deflected shot from Banel, but was at fault when Ramazani gave Burnley a 27th-minute lead.

The on-loan Leeds winger cut in from the right and fired a shot that flew into the roof of the net right by Pears, who was too slow to react.

But Gudjohnsen equalised four minutes later, racing on to a Harry Pickering long ball that drifted over Burnley centre-back Max Alleyne, and steering a sweet shot beyond goalkeeper Max Weiss for his 12th goal of the season. The Icelander celebrated by grabbing a Mowbray-tribute T-shirt from a photographer and displaying it for the cameras.

Blackburn finished the first half strongly, with Cantwell firing over a good chance, before Banel restored Burnley’s lead six minutes into the second half, running unchallenged to place a shot into the far corner as Hayden Carter backed off.

The home side were fortunate to escape a strong penalty appeal just before the hour mark as Cantwell burst into the penalty area and saw two attempted crosses both strike the arm of Igor Julio.

Blackburn did get a penalty with 16 minutes left as Josh Laurent, attempting to clear a low cross, failed to spot Gudjohnsen darting in from behind him and caught the striker’s leg. Cantwell drove the penalty straight down the middle to make it 2-2.

Gudjohnsen thought he had given Blackburn the lead two minutes later, when he controlled and drove a low shot against the base of the post with Weiss a spectator, but neither side could find a winner.
