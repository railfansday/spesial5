Anthropic IPO Price Could Top $80 And Pre-IPO Traders Are Copying the SpaceX Script: What Traders Should Expect

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Anthropic IPO may price above $80. Hyperliquid’s pre-IPO contract sits 24% higher, the setup SpaceX had before it listed.

The Anthropic IPO could price above $80 a share on Wednesday evening, above the $70 to $78 range the Claude AI maker set last week, The Wall Street Journal reported on Tuesday. The newspaper cited stronger-than-expected investor demand. Its advisers have discussed a valuation of $1.5 trillion to $1.6 trillion.

The shares are due to start trading on Nasdaq on Thursday, November 19, under the ticker ANT.

Crypto traders have been betting on the Anthropic IPO for weeks. On Hyperliquid, a pre-IPO contract that tracks one ANT share trades near $99, about 23% above $80.

We have seen this setup before. The night before SpaceX listed in June, the same kind of contract traded 28% above its $135 IPO price. SpaceX then closed its first day 19% up, below what the contract had promised.

So what should traders know before the bell rings on Thursday?

Anthropic launched its roadshow, the week when a company pitches its shares to large investors, on November 10. Its announcement set the terms:

Demand has run ahead of that. Pricing above $80 would lift the valuation toward the $1.5 trillion to $1.6 trillion the advisers discussed. The final price is set after the US market closes on Wednesday, and the shares open on Thursday.

That price is what large investors pay in the Anthropic IPO. Most retail traders will buy at the opening price instead, which can be far higher.

Hyperliquid, a crypto exchange, lists contracts that let traders bet on a company’s share price before it lists. We compared its Anthropic contract with the one it ran for SpaceX, counting back from each listing day.

The SpaceX contract was right that the shares would jump, but too optimistic about how far. If Anthropic followed the same pattern, a 7% gap from Wednesday’s contract price would put ANT’s first close in the low $90s. That is a pattern from one listing, not a forecast.

On the hourly chart, Hyperliquid’s ANT contract trades at $98.83, down from $138 when it started trading on October 26.

The number to watch before the Anthropic IPO opens is tonight’s final price. Every dollar above $80 shrinks the contract’s 24% premium, and with it the room for the first-day pop that pre-IPO traders are betting on.
