Altcoin Market Loses $408 Billion As Memecoin Dominance Falls to 2%

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The altcoin market has contracted sharply, losing about $408 billion in market capitalization since its September peak as declining investor interest and persistent selling pressure continue to weigh on the sector.

Notably, the memecoins have been among the hardest-hit assets, with their share of the total altcoin market falling sharply from 11% to 2%.

According to CryptoQuant analyst Darkfost, the altcoin market has lost approximately 34% of its value. The decline is reflected in the performance of assets listed on Binance, where the percentage of altcoins trading above their 200-day moving averages has plunged from 90% down to 19%.

Meanwhile, meme tokens have suffered an even deeper loss of investor interest as selling pressure continues to outweigh buying activity. Binance’s Cumulative Buy/Sell Volume Difference shows a $1.5 billion imbalance in favor of sellers, a trend that has persisted since October 2025.

This shift has significantly reduced the segment’s share of the broader market. During the memecoin frenzy in November 2024, these tokens accounted for 11% of total altcoin market capitalization.

Adding to the pressure, more than 207 crypto exploits were recorded during the first half of 2026, setting a new historical record. Combined with declining liquidity and increased volatility, these security concerns have further weakened altcoins’ appeal.

Despite the widespread weakness, some market participants believe the sector could eventually regain momentum if Bitcoin establishes a stronger price base.

Crypto enthusiast Michael van de Poppe argued that Bitcoin needs time to recover from its recent correction, suggesting that several weeks of consolidation could precede a decisive breakout. He is looking to buy below $59,000, identifying that level as a potential entry point ahead of a recovery toward $70,000-$72,000.

Additionally, the analyst suggested that a Bitcoin rebound could create opportunities across the altcoin market, highlighting VVV and NIGHT as two tokens that could experience breakouts during such a move.

Meanwhile, analyst “@el_crypto_prof” offered a more optimistic assessment of altcoins’ longer-term prospects.

Pointing to the OTHERS/BTC chart, which tracks smaller cryptocurrencies against Bitcoin, the analyst noted that it has remained relatively stable despite the recent market weakness. He believes the trend could eventually support an altcoin recovery and urged investors to remain patient.
