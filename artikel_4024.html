Stellar Blade's Free Bayonetta Crossover Outfits Are Available Now on PS5, PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Action game royalty has officially arrived in Sony and ShiftUp's fan favourite hack-and-slasher, Stellar Blade.

The sci-fi action title has been updated with a selection of new outfits for Eve, all inspired by SEGA's beloved Bayonetta series. They're freely available to all players.

You can check them all out in this new video:

This collaboration was announced back in September, and the costumes arrive alongside the Switch 2 port of the game.

To recap, the new outfits include Bayonetta, Nun, Jeanne, and Black Tresses; the latter is an original design by ShiftUp, while the others are based on looks and characters from SEGA's series.

It's a neat bonus for Stellar Blade players who, probably, are fans of Bayonetta too.

Interestingly, Stellar Blade has also collaborated with Overwatch recently, appearing in Blizzard's shooter in the form of various character skins you can grab via the in-game store.
