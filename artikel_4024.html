"I Don't Feel This Is A Good Direction" - Chrono Trigger Composer Thinks It's Time To End Dragon Quest

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

"it’s no longer possible to create anything new"

Chrono Trigger composer Yasunori Mitsuda has posted a blog in which he states it might be time for the Dragon Quest series to end.

As covered by our pals over at Time Extension, Mitsuda-san believes that without Akira Toriyama – who sadly passed away in 2024 – the Dragon Quest franchise cannot continue imitating the iconic artist's signature style.

The catalyst for this comment was the release of a new mobile game titled Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree And the Chosen Isle, which features artwork strikingly similar to Toriyama-san's work.

“Dragon Quest RPG‘s Toriyama-san-styled illustration has become a hot topic, but for the illustrator, for Akira Toriyama fans, and for the now-deceased Akira Toriyama himself, I don’t feel this is a good direction for everyone involved. In my personal opinion, since it’s no longer possible to create anything new no matter how hard one tries, it might be time to end the Dragon Quest series, and for Yuji Horii to create a new RPG. (There are a lot of remakes out there, after all).”

Online speculation, highlighted by Power Up Gaming, also points at the potential involvement of generative AI to create the in-game characters. However, despite enthusiasm around the technology from creator Yuji Horii, there's doesn't seem to be any legitimate evidence that this is the case.

Regardless, Mitsuda-san does have a point. Dragon Quest is synonymous with Toriyama-san's art style, and it might be tricky to maintain this without the man himself at the helm. Indeed, reaction to Dragon Quest XII: Beyond Dreams hasn't exactly been universally positive, with some online users criticising the design of the main protagonist.

We think it's unlikely that Dragon Quest will end anytime soon, however. The series is still incredibly popular to this day, and continues to sell ludicrous numbers in Japan especially. Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World is scheduled to release on Switch 1 and 2 on 3rd December 2026.
