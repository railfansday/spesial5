GTA 6 Vintage Vice City Pack Now Available To All Players On Xbox, And It's Pretty Cheap

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

"Flash back to when the neon burned brightest"

Ahead of the release of GTA 6, there were three types of content made available for it - the standard game, the Ultimate Edition content, and the Vintage Vice City Pack. The latter was intended as a pre-order bonus across Xbox and PS5.

Now that the game is out in the wild though, Rockstar Games has decided to make the Vintage Vice City Pack available to purchase on the Xbox Store, carrying a price tag of £4.49 / $4.99. That's obviously not as good as getting it for free, but it's pretty cheap all the same, and a nice option to have if you missed out on the pre-order period.

Similar to the Ultimate Edition content that you can also buy separately for £20.00 / $20.00, these items are made available "as [the] story progresses", so you don't necessarily get it all at the beginning of the campaign.

I have to admit that I haven't really engaged with the Ultimate Edition stuff all that much yet, although it's certainly a nice bonus to get extra vehicles, liveries and outfits. The game is more than stacked enough that you don't need to buy anything more than the standard edition, but the option is there if you want the most content possible.

We'll leave a poll down below to find out which version you personally have, and let us know in the comments if you think the Vintage Vice City Pack and/or the Ultimate Edition content is worth buying on top of GTA 6 itself!
