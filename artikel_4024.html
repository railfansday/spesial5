Poll: So, What Do You Think Of GTA 6?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It's early days, but let us know your thoughts so far

We've been talking GTA 6 all week here at Pure Xbox, but now it's time for the community to take centre stage! We want to know what you think about the new Grand Theft Auto based on what you've played so far.

It's no secret that GTA 6 has been reviewing better than pretty much any game in existence since its release a few days ago. As things stand, it sits alongside the original Ocarina of Time as the best game ever made on Metacritic!

We helped contribute to that with our "Outstanding" 10/10 review score here at Pure Xbox:

Review GTA 6 (Xbox) - The Most Breathtaking Open-World Game Of All Time

This is going to dominate your life for months!

I have no doubt that it's a 10/10 in my mind, but is it the joint-best video game of all time? That's a much harder statement to make, and I think it's going to need months and years before we can really evaluate it in that regard.

It should also be pointed out that despite the reviewers heaping praise on it, and the Xbox and PlayStation store ratings being extremely high for GTA 6 right now, the game's user score on Metacritic is surprisingly low - so clearly there are plenty of people who aren't so hot on what they've played and/or seen so far.

We're well aware that many Pure Xbox users are probably just a few hours into their experience with GTA 6 so far though, and therefore it's very early days as things stand. We don't want you to give a definitive verdict on the game down below, but just let us know your initial thoughts and whether you're satisfied or not with your purchase.

Also tell us what you think of the vibe compared to GTA 4 and GTA 5 - we're very curious about that!

What did you pick in the poll above? Let us know in the comments section down below.
