"We're Going Bananas For Our Newest Furry Friend" - Donkey Kong Out Now On Build-A-Bear Workshop

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Update [Sun 22nd Nov 2026, 12am]: Following the teaser earlier this week, Build-A-Bear Workshop has now officially revealed its new Donkey Kong plush. He'll set you back $50 USD (or the regional equivalent) and can be purchased now.

Original Story: [Thu 19th Nov, 2026 00:55 GMT]: We understand GTA 6 is a big deal, but Build-A-Bear Workshop has also teased some important news this week.

On its official Instagram page, the store where you build your own bear, has announced it's "going bananas" for its newest furry friend, who is "coming soon from the jungle". As you can see, it's a silhouette of Donkey Kong!

If you still aren't convinced it's DK, you can see there's a Nintendo copyright attached to the teaser. The message also tags 'Nintendo'.

We're going bananas for our newest furry friend! Coming soon from the jungle ​#TheStuffYouLove #AddALittleHeart #Nintendo

Build-A-Bear has previously teamed up with Nintendo to release Super Mario plush, so a DK plush is a welcome extension.

In recent years, Donkey Kong has also branched out with LEGO. Apart from this, his N64 game joined the Switch Online service, Donkey Kong Country Returns HD got a free Switch 2 update, and Donkey Kong Bananza's limited-time events are back.
