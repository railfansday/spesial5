Restaurant owners left in tears after discovering their family business in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

A family-owned restaurant called Oscar’s has been left stunned after discovering that their business seemingly appears in GTA 6.

GTA 6 has finally arrived, allowing players to explore Rockstar Games’ fictional state of Leonida, which takes heavy inspiration from Florida and features countless locations modelled on real-life landmarks.

Since its November 19 release, players have been discovering familiar locations throughout the game, but one family was particularly surprised to find their own restaurant recreated in Rockstar’s open world.

On November 21, X user @sirenaslloran shared her emotional reaction after discovering that her family’s restaurant, Oscar’s, had seemingly been recreated in GTA 6.

“Guys my family’s restaurant made it onto GTA 6. I’m crying so bad,” she wrote. “My parents are immigrants who came here with a dream and now we’ve been voted by the city as the number one breakfast spot and now this.”

She explained that her parents had immigrated to the United States hoping to build a successful business and that their restaurant had since been voted the city’s number-one breakfast spot.

Oscar’s has two locations and is family owned & operated ! Thank you so much for the support & kind messages. I’m reading them all to my family & my abuela says “gracias y que Dios los bendiga siempre”. 🩷 https://t.co/9EgmdobDsj pic.twitter.com/8fIGviTcnU

In a follow-up post, she revealed that Oscar’s is a family-owned and operated business with two locations. She also explained that she had been reading the messages aloud to her family, sharing a particularly touching response from her grandmother.

“Gracias y que Dios los bendiga siempre,” her grandmother said, which translates to “Thank you, and may God always bless you.”
