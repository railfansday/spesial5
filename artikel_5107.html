Adam Back Explains Why Bitcoin (BTC) Fell to $17,000 on Kraken's Canadian Dollar Market

Blockstream CEO Adam Back has explained the overnight sell-off on Kraken's Canadian-dollar markets that sent the exchange's BTC/CAD pair to C$24,250, or roughly $17,000, on Saturday morning, and said that the drop started with the exchange's local USD/CAD market maker.

The Blockstream CEO addressed the issue on X on Saturday in a reply to a post that tracked large sell orders hitting Kraken's BTC/CAD order book at regular intervals throughout the night.

The $17,000 figure is based on the Bank of Canada's Friday rate of C$1.4325 per U.S. dollar. Kraken's BTC/CAD pair printed the C$24,250 low on Saturday morning, according to TradingView data.

"No, the reason was USDCAD Kraken local spot FX market-maker ran out of money, as it has depegged away from market rate 1.434," Back replied.

In the same post, the Blockstream CEO said that it was "more accurate to say you could buy CAD discounted."

Later, another X user argued that the Canadian dollar was in fact overvalued on Kraken during the sell-off, since anyone who held CAD could buy anything at a discount. Back agreed with the correction.

"You are right. BTCUSD normal price, USDCAD CAD mispriced, like a shortage of CAD bid the price up," Back wrote.

On Sunday, the Blockstream CEO described the whole chain of events in two more posts on X.

According to Back, if the USD/CAD market maker failed, there were few buyers of BTC in Canadian dollars, and so the BTC/CAD price fell. In his view, traders would normally close such a gap with an arbitrage trade "by selling USD to CAD to buy if it BTCCAD doesn't track BTCUSD."

Back wrote that the drop "liquidated people" and triggered stop losses "based on a local false price."

"And then USDCAD followed because you could buy BTC cheaply in CAD, so it became logical to buy CAD way over par to take that arb," he added.

The BTC/CAD pair recovered later on Saturday, and on Sunday, it traded at C$86,236 on Kraken, according to TradingView.

Kraken said in a statement that its Canadian-dollar trading pairs faced heavy selling pressure starting around 20:00 UTC on Friday, and prices on these pairs fell significantly below those on other markets.

The Kraken sell-off started on the same day that Bitcoin dropped to $59,370, its lowest level since July 2, according to CoinGlass data. On Friday, CoinGlass also recorded $1.57 billion in liquidations across the crypto market over 24 hours, and long positions accounted for $1.43 billion of that amount.

Still, the exchange said that every order was processed correctly and that its security teams found no evidence of a breach or compromised accounts.
