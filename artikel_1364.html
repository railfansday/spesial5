Gary Neville believes the four defenders who finished the game for Manchester United in Sunday’s wild 4-4 draw against Aston Villa will get the club relegated, slamming the boardroom hierarchy for failing to address obvious defensive frailties during the transfer window.

The former United captain launched a scathing attack on the club’s defensive recruitment, accusing the hierarchy of failing to strengthen an area that desperately needed investment during the transfer window.

Speaking on NBC Sports following the final whistle, Neville delivered a damning assessment of Manchester United’s defensive options.

He argued that the club’s decision not to prioritise defensive reinforcements during the transfer window has contributed directly to their alarming struggles.

“The reason Manchester United have conceded four goals is because they haven’t invested in the defence in the transfer window. Every one knew they had to do that, they needed defenders. The current ones are making mistake after mistake after mistake.

“The defenders that finished that game will get you relegated. Watch the defensive errors… we all screamed in the transfer window that they have to bring some defenders in. They had to bring the transfer budget forward and bring defenders in.”

Neville’s criticism echoes post-match comments from United striker Matheus Cunha, who expressed deep anger over the team’s recurring defensive errors.

Cunha bluntly stated after the final whistle that United “can’t concede goals like this” and urged his teammates to stop digging early holes.

United fell 2-0 down within four minutes and later trailed 4-2, forcing desperate second-half comebacks that masked the vulnerability in the backline.

Despite the dramatic stoppage-time heroics from Joshua Zirkzee and Dorgu to salvage a point, Manchester United enter the November international break sitting 18th in the Premier League table.

With 10 games played, United remain winless in their last eight league outings, their worst domestic run in 40 years.
