11 Best Number 10s in World Football Right Now (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

When Argentina went to the 1982 World Cup as defending champions, they adopted the same alphabetical numbering system which had served them so well four years earlier. Midfielder Ossie Ardiles wore number one while first-choice goalkeeper, Ubaldo Fillol, was stuck with seven. There was, however, an exception.

By the prevailing logic, Diego Maradona should have been left with the number 12. But as the undisputed star of the side, he would have the honour of wearing the number 10. Argentina were not alone in handing that particular digit to the team's talisman. Almost since shirt numbers were first dreamed up in the 1930s, 10 has carried an unrivalled allure.

The rise of pressing and physical demands of the modern game has changed the requirements of a successful number 10. No longer can they saunter around the final third, shirt untucked and socks unfurled. But that doesn't take away from the talent of the individuals fortunate enough to be draped in those iconic digits.

We rank the best 30 footballers in the world right now - in order.

Nico Williams became just the fifth player to take on the burden of Athletic Club's number 10 shirt this century when he inherited the digits from former hero Iker Muniain. It was fitting that the first goal Williams scored in his new shirt was teed up by his personal hero and the club's number nine - his older brother Inaki.

The jet-heeled winger has blossomed into one of La Liga's best attackers in recent years and spearheaded Spain's triumphant Euro 2024 campaign. A blur of chops, jinks and shimmies, the direct dribbler was pursued by most of Europe in 2024, only to stay at his boyhood club. Mixed form has since followed, but the best is yet to come from the Spaniard.

Mikel Oyarzabal is one of the most underrated players in Europe. The Real Sociedad man is consistently crucial for Spain, playing a key part in their Euro 2024 win and 2026 World Cup victory. At club level, meanwhile, he's scored over 100 goals for Sociedad and has become a modern-day legend, captaining the side.

One of the best and most consistent number 10s in the world, Oyarzabal has been a thorn in the side of several teams at both international and club level. When all is said and done, he will have an incredibly decorated career to look back on.

There's every chance that Nico Paz rises up this list in years to come. He is the next best thing in European football. The Como 1907 star has already put the likes of Manchester United to the sword and now looks like the next in line for Lionel Messi's Argentine throne.

A move to Real Madrid is already on the horizon for the winger, who could play a transformative part alongside a number of other world greats. One of the best dribblers, best wingers and best No.10s in world football, Paz is destined to take his place at the very top of world football.

A Ballon d'Or push may even be in his future if he reaches his full potential.

The number 10 is typically the brain of the team. Few players have a higher footballing IQ than Alexis Mac Allister. Raised in a family of footballers, the Argentine has brothers, cousins and an uncle who all played the game professionally. Mac Allister Sr, Carlos Javier, even lined up alongside one of the greatest number 10s to ever wear the famous shirt, Diego Maradona, who became a close family friend.

Alexis greedily gobbled up the knowledge he has been afforded unfettered access to since birth. Typically an attacking midfielder - hence the shirt number - Mac Allister scarcely looked out of place after injuries and absences forced him into a deeper role after moving to Liverpool. The salve to all the team's open wounds, no wonder former manager Jurgen Klopp once lauded him as "a football doctor".

Erling Haaland, Bruno Fernandes and Declan Rice are all in the top 20 best players in the Premier League.

Such are the daunting expectations that come with the number 10 shirt in Italy, Lautaro Martinez was forced to address his decision to wear those digits after joining Inter Milan in 2018. "Wearing the number 10 shirt doesn't weigh down on me because I already wore it at Racing," the Argentine shrugged. "It was free, and I'm very happy to be able to wear it."

Sandro Mazzola, Lothar Matthaus, Ronaldo, Roberto Baggio, Clarence Seedorf... The list of Lautaro's legendary predecessors in Inter's number 10 would be daunting for most. But the prolific forward has thrived under the scrutiny, tacking on the added pressure of club captaincy while racking up more than 20 league goals in each of the last four seasons - a feat none of the aforementioned figures ever matched.

Some players are just the epitome of the No.10 shirt, and Cole Palmer is one of them. The Chelsea and England star has a rare composure, gliding through the frontline and so often finishing with ease, before wheeling away to perform his vintage celebration.

The former Manchester City man has proven doubters wrong ever since arriving at Stamford Bridge, and looks destined to be one of the best players in the Premier League for years to come. Manager Xabi Alonso is one of many to deliver impressive praise, describing Palmer as a "special talent" during the early days of his tenure.

Is the Premier League really the best in the world?

There have been some complaints among Premier League fans that English football is lacking the same magic that previously made it the most entertaining league in the world. Rayan Cherki is here to solve those complaints. The Man City magician is one of the most skillful players in the world and didn't let a move into an intricate system block that trait out.

No one is safe from the Frenchman's dazzling feet and he so often has the finish on the end of it. Even amid complaints about work rate out of possession throughout his career, Cherki has enjoyed enough spectacular moments to silence any of those headlines, especially at the Etihad.

Ousmane Dembele didn't just stumble upon some consistency in Paris, he stumbled upon excellence. The Frenchman has gone from big-money Barcelona flop to Ballon d'Or winner at PSG. It has been an unexpected rise from the ashes, but one which was applauded by everyone in 2025 after he scored 20 goals on his way to the award.

A back-to-back Champions League winner and a consistent name among the world's best players these days, Dembele's struggles are long behind him and he rightly has a place on this list as a result.

When Lionel Messi joined Barcelona's academy as a desperately shy teenager, no one understood one of the few words he dared to utter. When asked what position he played, Messi said: "Enganche." This translates as 'hook' and is the Argentine word for the number 10 position, acting as the coupling link between midfield and the front line.

Two decades of unprecedented success later, arguably the greatest footballer of all time is still wearing the number 10. The MLS defenders bamboozled by his unrivalled array of flicks and feints may not be the best he's ever come up against, but Messi proved during the 2026 World Cup that he can still mix it at the elite level. As the great man reflected: "My connection with the ball has always been the same."

When Barcelona lost Messi to PSG in 2021, there may have been an internal belief that they'd have to wait years for their replacement to arrive. As it happens, however, the La Masia academy was always ready to spring Lamine Yamal into the first team and those at the Nou Camp have not looked back since.

Yamal is an early contender for the Ballon d'Or and is yet to even reach his prime years. Messi left the throne vacated when he left Spain in 2021, but the Spaniard is now ready to take his seat at the very top for the next decade. Already a World Cup winner, Yamal has more than earned his place.
