Milan owner Cardinale on Endrick: ‘One of the best players in the world in his position’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Gerry Cardinale has described Endrick as one of the best players in the world in his position, although he insists Milan’s project is bigger than any one player.

Speaking to MilanNews.it ahead of Sunday’s game against Frosinone at San Siro, the Rossoneri owner confirmed Milan will spend in January, but only on signings that fit the club’s plans.

“We’re ready. We’re doing a lot of work. As we discussed, it’s complicated, and the January transfer market is always tricky, so you have to be prepared and opportunistic,” Cardinale said. “One thing I can admit is that we’re not making any decisions we don’t feel great about. We’re going to spend money, but we’re going to spend it smartly, consistent with the project and in close coordination with Ruben and the guys here at Milanello.”

Milan have been strongly linked with Endrick, with reports suggesting they’re ready to meet Real Madrid’s €60m valuation, but Club Brugge’s Nicolò Tresoldi is also being considered as an alternative.

“Look, that name is also mentioned a lot. This project is more than just one player,” Cardinale said. “He is one of the best players, if not the best player, in the world in his position. I have a tremendous amount of respect for him. We’ll see what’s possible in the market.”

Cardinale wouldn’t confirm Markus Krösche’s arrival as head of football. Corriere dello Sport has reported that the Eintracht Frankfurt director will start on 1 January.

“You guys are very focused on Markus Krösche. That name has been bandied about. When we’re ready to make an announcement, I’ll come back to you on that.”

He also spoke warmly about Adrien Rabiot, who is in talks over a new contract.

“I love Adrien, he’s one of my favourite players. I think he’s fantastic, and I have a tremendous amount of respect for him and his family. We’re in discussions and I hope we’ll get a good result. If it were up to me, he’d be a franchise player for this team.”

Fourth-placed Milan face sixth-placed Frosinone on Sunday at 18:00 CET [17:00 GMT].

“We’ve had a great couple of days and great sessions at Casa Milan on the January market, and on all our plans for the rest of this season and into next summer,” Cardinale added. “The mood here is really upbeat. We had a great practice today, and the team is really coming together. I spoke to a lot of the players, and they understand, as Ruben said, how important this game is. Hopefully we come out strong and get the win.”
