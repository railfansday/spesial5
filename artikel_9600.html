The Returns Policy Was Written in Four Places. Three of Them Disagreed.

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Our store states its returns window in four places:

At one point those four places said 14 days, 14 days, 7 days and 30 days. A customer could read four pages and get three different answers. Google could too.

No one sat down and decided to contradict themselves. It happened because each surface was built at a different time by a different mechanism:

When we changed the policy, we updated the visible policy page. The other three kept their old numbers. In a store with thousands of template-generated pages, a policy sentence lives in code, not in a document.

Search results showed "14-day returns" under product pages while the policy page said 30. Structured data on some pages carried one number, visible text another.

One source of truth in code. The product meta description is generated by a single function. We changed the promise string there — one edit covers 27,197 pages.

Search the database, not just the pages. Two regional pages had the old window hidden in postmeta fields that feed FAQ structured data. Reading the rendered page was not enough. We queried wp_postmeta directly and found two more copies.

Verify both layers. After the edit we checked:

We first checked by fetching pages and grepping the HTML. That found the visible copies but missed the postmeta ones until we looked at the rendered FAQ JSON-LD output.

If you run a template-driven store, grep the database, not just the rendered pages. Content that feeds structured data often lives in meta fields, and it will happily contradict the page you just fixed.

For any claim that appears on more than one surface — returns window, shipping time, warranty, price promise:

We now have a morning check that inspects all four surfaces. It doesn't fix anything automatically; it reports the mismatch so a human decides. That's deliberate. Auto-fixing policy text is how you get a different kind of contradiction.

The number is now 30 days everywhere: policy page, FAQ, About, six regional pages, product meta descriptions, and schema. The GSC merchant-listing warning about missing return information cleared once we also added hasMerchantReturnPolicy and shippingDetails to the Product schema.

If you want to see how we present the policy now, our returns page and shipping page both state the same window.
