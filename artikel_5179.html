SHIB Price Tests Support While Burns Tick 49% Higher

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Home » Shiba Inu (SHIB) » SHIB Price Tests Support While Burns Tick 49% Higher

Absolute numbers stay modest even though the popular canine coin saw a wider burning campaign this week.

The Shiba Inu coin (SHIB) has drifted toward the $0.00000426 area, a level that has become the immediate line of defense as broader crypto weakness keeps pressure on the token. Buyers are now testing whether they can absorb selling there or whether the next leg lower opens up.

SHIB has tracked the wider market’s soft tone in recent sessions, leaving the $0.00000426 zone as the nearest area of interest. A clean hold could give the token a base to stabilize.

A decisive break would leave traders looking for the next pocket of demand, particularly while major cryptocurrencies remain under pressure.

This Shiba Inu (SHIB) price level matters less as a long-term pivot and more as a short-term filter.

Hold it and the near-term downside is at least temporarily limited. Lose it and the path of least resistance stays lower until broader sentiment improves.

SHIB burn trackers recorded a 49.21% rise in activity over the past seven days, with the chart showing a clear peak around November 7-8 after several quiet days.

Total SHIB sent to burn addresses now stands at roughly 410.845 trillion, or 41.08% of the original 1 quadrillion supply, across more than 21,900 transactions.

The 49% percentage jump looks sharp on the seven-day window, yet recent weekly totals have remained in the tens of millions of SHIB (worth only a few hundred dollars at current prices).

Most of the lifetime burn total still dates to the project’s early years. The recent uptick adds a small deflationary note but does not yet move the needle on circulating supply in any material way.

Shiba Inu’s near-term direction continues to depend more on overall crypto sentiment than on token-specific developments. Meme-linked assets tend to see stronger participation when risk appetite is high; when the market is cautious, buyers have less room to push against selling.

For now, the $0.00000426 area is the practical marker. Holding it would show that some demand is still present even in a soft tape. Losing it would reinforce that sellers remain in control while the wider market stays under pressure.
