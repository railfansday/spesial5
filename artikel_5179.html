NYSE Selects Avalanche for Tokenized Stock Settlement: Can AVAX Reclaim $9.00?

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Home » Altcoins » NYSE Selects Avalanche for Tokenized Stock Settlement: Can AVAX Reclaim $9.00?

Wall Street’s tokenization push gives Avalanche an institutional boost, but AVAX technicals demand caution at important level.

In a major endorsement of enterprise blockchain infrastructure, the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) has chosen Avalanche (AVAX) technology as the core ledger for clearing and settling trades on its planned tokenized stock platform.

According to an Ava Labs statement, Avalanche will provide the core ledger technology for the clearing firm of the NYSE-affiliated digital trading platform.

Subject to regulatory approval, the platform is designed to enable 24/7 trading, clearing and onchain settlement of tokenized securities.

It will support equities issued across multiple blockchains, while Avalanche will provide the underlying settlement infrastructure, offering predictable performance and costs, controlled network access and regulatory flexibility.

The announcement comes amid intensifying competition in financial asset tokenization. The NYSE has expanded its transfer agent network to six firms, including Broadridge, Computershare and Superstate.

Nasdaq is also developing tokenized securities infrastructure, while DTCC launched its tokenization service in October, using a private Besu blockchain and the Canton Network.

Meanwhile, the SEC’s new innovation exemption is opening the door to tokenized equity trading, with OKX and ICE pursuing a separate initiative.

Following the announcement, AVAX broke out of its previous consolidation range but faced resistance at $8.80–$9.00, triggering a pullback. As of November 10, AVAX is trading at approximately $8.48, up 7.2% over 24 hours.

The rally pushed Bollinger Bands wider, signaling increased volatility. AVAX is now trading below the middle band at $8.62 and the moving-average reference at $8.56, suggesting that upward momentum has cooled.

Resistance stands at $8.60–$8.65 and $9.00–$9.28, while key support can be located around $8.00, followed by $7.70 and $7.30.

Over the next few days, AVAX’s direction will largely depend on whether buyers can reclaim $8.65.A recovery above $8.65 could put AVAX back on course to test $9.00. A break above $9.28, supported by stronger trading volume, would signal renewed bullish momentum.

Failure to regain near-term resistance could leave the token consolidating, while a break below $7.95 would expose lower support levels at $7.70 or $7.30.

The NYSE announcement strengthens Avalanche’s position in institutional tokenization, but its implications for AVAX remain less direct. The platform is expected to run on a dedicated blockchain built using Avalanche technology rather than the public Avalanche network.

The extent of any resulting demand for AVAX will depend on how the platform is implemented and whether it creates mechanisms for value to accrue to the token.

A sustained recovery could put $9.50–$10.00 within reach, with further resistance around $10.50–$11.50. However, failure to overcome overhead resistance could leave AVAX in a broader consolidation range.

The NYSE announcement is a significant development for Avalanche’s institutional prospects, but it has yet to translate into a confirmed bullish reversal for AVAX. After the initial AVAX price rally, traders are now assessing whether buying demand can sustain the move beyond the immediate resistance level.
