Mariah Carey Announces 2027 European Summer Festival Tour Dates

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Firefly tour will find MC touching down at nine fests next summer.

Mariah Carey has booked a busy summer of touring next year. The singer announced the dates for a nine-show summer 2027 European tour on Monday morning (Nov. 9). The Firefly outing is slated to kick off on June 24 at Molleparken in Sonderborg, Denmark before moving on to the Powderham Castle Grounds in Exeter, U.K. on June 27 and then Cardiff Castle in Cardiff, U.K. on June 29. The nine-show swing will march on with a July 1 date at Blenheim Palace Grounds in Oxfordshire, U.K., then hit Scarborough Open Air Theatre in Scarborough, U.K., followed by the Jazz Open Stuttgart in Stuttgart, Germany, the Lucca Summer Festival in Lucca, Italy, Francofolies De Spa in Spa, Belgium on July 14 and wind down with a set at the Moon & Stars Festival in Locarno, Switzerland on July 20.

The singer announced the run on her Instagram, writing, “Lambs!! Join me next summer on the Firefly tour!! Excited to see all of you in Europe!🦋💫🎙️ Tickets on sale this Friday.” A general onsale for the dates will open at 9 a.m. on Friday here and through the individual venues’ websites.

Carey, who dropped her 16th studio album, Here For It All, earlier this year, debuted at No. 7 on the Billboard 200, giving the legendary singer her 19th top 10 LP overall on the album tally.

For those of you keeping score, Carey also launched her traditional countdown to Christmas last week with one of her signature “it’s time” videos. This time, however, after the calendar shifted to November, the singer popped out in a bedazzled Santa jumpsuit as the opening notes of her holiday standard, “All I Want For Christmas Is You,” played in the background. “IT’S TIME!!!!” she wrote in the caption, while her son, Moroccan, commented, “ITS FREAKING TIME YO.”

Carey’s most recent run of concerts concluded last December when she wound down her Christmastime in Las Vegas residency at Dolby Live at Park MGM in Las Vegas.
