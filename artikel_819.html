Why injured Joao Pedro may be Chelsea's most important player

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Joao Pedro has scored five goals in six Premier League games this season

Cole Palmer and Moises Caicedo are names that would probably feature in any debate, but Joao Pedro's recent absence has certainly strengthened the argument in his favour.

The debate started again among Blues fans over the past couple of weeks following their 1-0 defeat by Sunderland, the latest in a run of poor results without the Brazil striker.

The 25-year-old will miss Leeds United's visit on Saturday with a thigh injury after Chelsea lost three of their previous four matches without him, although one defeat came in a penalty shootout against Liverpool in the Carabao Cup.

Having suffered a 2-0 home defeat by Tottenham in that run, manager Xabi Alonso has faced criticism over his side's lack of identity and some of his personnel decisions, particularly when attempting to replace Joao Pedro.

Danny Welbeck, who was expected to provide cover after his summer arrival from Brighton, has started only one Premier League match during Joao Pedro's injury absence.

Instead, Pedro Neto has twice been deployed through the middle, in the home win over Manchester United and the defeat by Sunderland, while Morgan Rogers started as the central striker against Spurs.

Striker Emmanuel Emegha, signed from Strasbourg over the summer, hasn't started a league game but has struggled with fitness issues of his own.

The continued absence of Joao Pedro is certainly proving costly before the busy winter schedule, with fifth-placed Chelsea already 11 points behind Premier League leaders Arsenal.

Having been handed both a new contract until 2034 and the number nine shirt vacated by Liam Delap in August, Chelsea regard their Â£55m signing from Brighton in 2025 as a key figure in Alonso's plans.

Asked about scoring more goals without the striker on Friday, Alonso said: "Yes, we need the whole team for that. It's not just the strikers who can score, we can score in set-pieces from defenders, midfielders, so it's teamwork for sure.

"Joao is an important player but we need to be this time without him. So far we have tried different options and we need to keep trying. I think that there are more explanations for one defeat than missing just one player.

"The other players, they need to step up and they need to show that they are ready to compete and ready to play for Chelsea."

There is little doubt Alonso has suffered when Joao Pedro has been unavailable, as did his predecessors. The numbers are undeniable.

Since the start of the 2025-26 Premier League season, Chelsea have averaged 1.8 goals per game with Joao Pedro, compared to 0.6 without.

Their expected goals (xG) drop from 1.8 per game with him to 1.1 without, highlighting a decline in Chelsea's attacking output rather than simply their finishing.

Chelsea average 4.8 shots on target per game when the Brazilian plays, compared with 3.9 when he does not.

Chelsea have failed to score in only 12.2% of Premier League matches involving him, compared with 71.4% when he has been absent.

Those numbers are also reflected in the results.

Chelsea have lost without scoring in just 7.3% of league games featuring Joao Pedro, compared with 71.4% without him. Three of their Premier League defeats this season were without him, while they have scored in every game that he has featured.

Chelsea's points-per-game average falls from 1.5 to 0.9 when he is unavailable.

Joao Pedro, who has also operated as a number 10 and on the wing for Chelsea, appears to improve the output of those around him.

During the past season and a half, Palmer has played 2,317 Premier League minutes alongside him and 528 without him.

Palmer has scored 12 goals and registered four assists with the Brazil forward in the side, equating to 0.6 goal involvements per 90 minutes. Without him, Palmer has managed one goal and no assists, equivalent to 0.2 goal involvements per 90.

Palmer's chance creation actually increases when Joao Pedro is absent, rising from 1.4 chances created per 90 minutes to 1.9. However, his goals return falls from 0.5 per 90 to 0.2, suggesting the striker's presence is more closely linked to Palmer's goalscoring output than his creativity.

The pair have developed a productive partnership. Joao Pedro has assisted four Palmer goals, while Palmer has provided three assists to the ex-Brighton forward..

There is less data available for Rogers given his more recent arrival, but the trend is similar.

Rogers has played 523 Premier League minutes alongside Joao Pedro and 360 without him. With Joao Pedro available, Rogers averages 0.7 goals per 90 minutes and 0.9 goal involvements per 90. Without him, those figures fall to 0.3 goal involvements per 90.

While the sample size remains relatively small, the figures suggest Chelsea's attacking players generally produce more when Joao Pedro plays.

All three of his assists this season have been for either Rogers or Palmer, sparking early season excitement about Chelsea's new trio, with nicknames like 'JP Morgan' and 'CPR' having been given to them.

With 20 goals and eight assists, Joao Pedro has been directly involved in 36.4% of Chelsea's 77 Premier League goals since joining the club.

Whether he is the Blues' most important player remains open to debate.

The statistics do show, however, that their points return with him in the side is consistent with Champions League qualification, whereas their return without him is not.

Alonso's team have yet to demonstrate they can perform at the same level when the Brazilian is unavailable.
