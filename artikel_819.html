Kim Kardashian, Jennifer Lawrence Step Out for NYC Outing

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Kim Kardashian and Jennifer Lawrence stepped outside the Four Seasons hotel in New York sporting matching SKIMS pajamas, proving their friendship is stronger than you think.

Odds are Kim Kardashian and Jennifer Lawrence are closer than you’d think.

After all, the All’s Fair actress and Oscar winner stepped out together arm-in-arm for a walk in New York Nov. 4, going so far to sport matching SKIMS Onyx Floral Deer Print Cami and Pan sleep sets.

Kim and Jennifer are all smiles while linking arms in photos and sporting matching shades and brown robes along with their pajamas, which are being sold as part of SKIMS new holiday campaign.

An avid reality TV fan, Jennifer’s friendship display with Kim has been a long time coming, as the Hunger Games actress has been a friend of the family for years—and a fan of their series.

“The Kardashians were just in Thailand,” Jennifer joked in an interview with Vanity Fair in 2014. “So that was a big deal.”

Also noting she was binging Real Housewives at the time, she quipped, “I’m trying to stay up-to-date on all the pointless gossip that goes on in Hollywood.”

Through the years, Jennifer has been a devout watcher of the family’s series, even joking to E! News that watching it can make her emotional.

“I was crying about the Kardashians the other night,” The Silver Linings Playbook actress revealed to E! in 2016. “They’re under a lot of pressure. Even if it’s elective, it’s pressure—and I get that!”

Kim, for her part, has revealed it was jarring for Jennifer to be a such a big fan of her—as the feeling was mutual.

“I was in NYC and I ran into Jennifer Lawrence,” she said during an E! International junket in 2015. “We said, 'Hi' and walked into the elevator and as the doors were closing, she screamed across the lobby, 'I love your show!' We were laughing so hard.”

Fast forward 10 years later, and the duo are stepping out of NYC hotels together. But trust, Jennifer is as devout to The Kardashians as ever, and even recently spilled her latest hot take on the series.

“Kourtney [Kardashian] is more annoying than ever,” she said in a Vanity Fair video segment released last year. “She drives me nuts.”

Lawrence wasn’t trying to be f--king rude, as Kim would say, but she had to get her irritation toward the eldest sister off her chest.

“Everything has to be an announcement,” she explained. “‘I’m not gonna wear outfits anymore.’ Just wear whatever you want. Don’t make an announcement about it. Or, ‘I don’t have a TV in my room.’ Just don’t watch TV. Stop announcing it.”

Unlike other reality TV fans, though, Lawrence got to see a direct response to her take airing on her favorite show.

In a recent episode of The Kardashians, Kourtney joked that if Jennifer wants to be considered the sixth sister in her family and share her opinions, “I'll f--king slap the s--t out of her.”

As the 47-year-old quipped, “Invite her over. And I’ll go for it like she’s my sister.”
