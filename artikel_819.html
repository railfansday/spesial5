The Hilarious Reason Channing Tatum Is “Useless” to Daughter Everly

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Channing Tatum brought his instincts as a dad to his new movie Josephine, in theaters Nov. 20, but as he exclusively told E! News, those instincts don’t extend to his daughter Everly’s dance lessons.

Channing Tatum may not be able to step up as a dance teacher.

Though the Step Up actor may have plenty of dance scenes throughout his filmography, he considers himself more sporty, which isn’t exactly helpful to his and ex-wife Jenna Dewan’s “dancey” 13-year-old daughter Everly.

“All my years of sports are absolutely useless,” Tatum told E! News’ Will Marfuggi in an exclusive interview. “I can help a little dance, but she picked a certain style of dance that I also cannot help with: Irish Celtic dancing. So, I'm useless to her.”

In fact, when the 46-year-old attends Everly’s Irish step dance recitals, he admitted, “I have to look at her instructor when she comes off and I'm like, ‘Was it good? It looked good to me.’”

Still, the She’s The Man actor knows he isn’t completely useless to Everly. As he joked about telling his daughter, “I’m like, ‘If you ever want to throw a football 100 yards, let me know.’”

While Tatum’s ability to recognize his strengths pays off when parenting Everly, it also helped him prepare for his new film Josephine.

In the movie—in select theaters beginning Nov. 20, before expanding nationwide Dec. 4—he and Gemma Chan play a couple who have to console their daughter, played by Mason Reeves, after she witnesses a heinous crime.

“There was a lot of the character that was my instincts,” Tatum explained, before noting he wanted to give his best effort for director Beth de Araújo. “I just wanted to be able to have given her as much to work with inside of that because sometimes pushing really hard is what is what really works.”

Regardless of the effort that the 21 Jump Street star puts into his work, he knows he’ll “never be cool” to Everly, no matter how many of his movies she’s seen.

“She has seen tons of them,” he told E! last year. “She’s like, ‘Whatever, I don’t wanna see those.’ She acts like she doesn’t care.”

Josephine hits select theaters on Nov. 20 and will expand nationwide Dec. 4.
