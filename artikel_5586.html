McDonald's is making Uber its preferred delivery partner in 14 countries

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The multi-year deal gives Uber precedence over DoorDash for McDonald's app orders and adds Uber ride discounts to MyMcDonald's Rewards

McDonald's $MCD is naming Uber $UBER the preferred delivery partner for orders placed in the McDonald's app as part of a multi-year partnership expansion across 14 markets, the companies announced on Monday.

The markets are the U.S., Australia, Belgium, Canada, France, Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Poland, Portugal, Spain, Switzerland and the U.K., the companies said. Uber Eats will serve as the preferred integrated delivery fulfillment partner for McDonald's app orders in participating markets, including the U.S., Australia, Canada, France, Germany and the U.K.

Uber Direct, Uber's white-label delivery service, will handle most of those orders in partnership with Deliverect, the companies said. Customers will be able to order delivery and track it from checkout to doorstep without leaving the McDonald's app. They can still order McDonald's through the Uber Eats app.

McDonald's and Uber first partnered in 2017 in the U.S. McDonald's is available on Uber Eats in 30 countries, the companies said.

Uber will be McDonald's exclusive delivery and rideshare rewards partner in participating markets. Starting this month, MyMcDonald's Rewards members in select markets can trade their points for Uber ride discounts via a seasonal activation in the McDonald's app. The companies plan to add features that would let customers earn MyMcDonald's Rewards points on eligible McDonald's orders placed through Uber Eats and redeem them for rewards, including Uber ride offers.

McDonald's has handed delivery orders to Uber or DoorDash interchangeably until now, according to Bloomberg. The new agreement puts Uber first in the markets it covers, and McDonald's will still appear as a restaurant choice on both companies' apps. McDonald's counts over 45 million U.S. loyalty members who use its app.

"McDelivery has grown into a global business of more than $20 billion a year," Mason Smoot, McDonald's senior vice president for global franchising and delivery, said in a statement.

"This marks our broadest expansion with McDonald's to date," Andrew Macdonald, Uber's president and chief operating officer, said in a statement.

DoorDash agreed last month to acquire Instacart in a deal with an equity value near $17 billion. The deal is expected to close at the end of 2027 or in the first half of 2028.
