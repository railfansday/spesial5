Inflamed Hearts on CT; Drawback to BP-Monitoring Wearables; Body Positions for HF?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

— Recent developments of interest in cardiovascular medicine

Coronary inflammation, quantified by a fat attenuation index score on contrast coronary CT angiography or noncontrast CT, improved cardiovascular risk prediction and reclassification beyond coronary artery calcium scoring alone, a cohort study found. (JAMA Cardiology)

Contact pressure matters when it comes to wearables and other devices using photoplethysmography for cuffless blood pressure (BP) monitoring. (NPJ Biosensing)

An artificial intelligence (AI)-ECG model was trained to identify phenotypes of dilated cardiomyopathy (DCM), and it reliably ruled out incident 5-year DCM in familial cohorts. (European Heart Journal)

Could just changing body positioning improve oxygenation and dyspnea in heart failure (HF) patients? (Clinical Cardiology)

Prefrail and frail patients undergoing coronary artery bypass grafting had similar clinical outcomes at 30 days whether the surgery was off- or on-pump, a randomized trial found. (Circulation)

For guiding revascularization of intermediate coronary stenoses, a randomized trial could not come to a conclusion of whether angiography-derived vessel fractional flow reserve (FFR) is on par with pressure wire FFR guidance. (Journal of the American College of Cardiology)

Despite a fast-track protocol for heart attack diagnosis, patients with phonetically uncommon surnames in Switzerland tended to experience longer delays from first medical contact to diagnosis. (Heart)

Proteomic work suggests new pharmacological targets for stroke prevention. (Stroke)

Pregnancy in women with repaired tetralogy of Fallot was associated with low risk of maternal cardiac events, though low birth weight and preterm birth were still frequent, according to a contemporary cohort in Spain. (American Journal of Cardiology)

Fourteen-day ECG patch monitoring after transcatheter aortic valve replacement did not suffice for detecting clinically relevant rhythm disturbances, a Swiss center found. (Heart Rhythm)

Village health workers, equipped with a clinical decision support app on a tablet, delivered hypertension care that was noninferior to traditional nurse-led, facility-based care, as shown by a trial in rural Lesotho. (Lancet Global Health)

A car crash killed interventional cardiologist Shahab Moossavi, MD, of Concord, New Hampshire. (Concord Monitor)

Daniel Arnett, MD, the Massachusetts cardiologist found dead near a cow pasture after going on a bike ride, was determined to have died from suicide, the Office of the Chief Medical Examiner in Boston said. (New York Post)
