Jodie Foster Makes Rare Comment on Family Life With Sons Charlie & Kit

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Hexed's Jodie Foster detailed the parenting lesson she's learned as mom to sons Charles “Charlie” Bernard Foster, 28, and Christopher “Kit” Bernard Foster, 25, in an exclusive E! News interview.

Jodie Foster will never silence her lambs.

In fact, the Hexed star encourages her sons Charles “Charlie” Bernard Foster, 28, and Christopher “Kit” Bernard Foster, 25, to explore their passions.

"Being a mother of two boys, you learn the lesson of that they have to discover things for themselves," Foster exclusively told E! News' Lauren Herbert at the Los Angeles premiere of her new Disney movie Nov. 12, "and that you can't protect them and that you have to just be positive for their life choices."

The 63-year-old continued, "If they make a mistake, they make one and you just hope that they learn from it instead of intervening."

But for Foster—who shares her kids with ex Cydney Bernard—that's a parenting lesson she didn't need to pass on to her costar Hailee Steinfeld, who gave birth to daughter Harper with husband Josh Allen in April.

As the Oscar winner put it, "She is mama extraordinaire."

"It's so incredible that Hailee did this movie, first, pregnant," Foster raved, "and then holding the baby, basically."

What's more, watching Steinfeld, 29, experience many motherhood firsts brought back fond memories for Foster and their castmate Rashida Jones, who's mom to 8-year-old son Isaiah with longtime partner Ezra Koenig.

"All of the feelings and thoughts that were going through her mind at that time, realizations about her own mother and how she'd been parented, I remember that when my kids were little," Foster shared. "It's like we're all of the different eras."

But Foster also experienced a personal first while working on Hexed: playing a Disney villain.

"I've always wanted to be a villain," she said. "Now, I get to be a Disney villain, which is the most special thing, because this one is very grounded. It, in some ways, feels like somebody that you could know and weirdly, that makes it more emotional and special."

Disney's Hexed is out in theaters Nov. 25.
