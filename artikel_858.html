Rinderknech denies refusing doping test and expects to be cleared

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

French newspaper L'Equipe reported Arthur Rinderknech's alleged refusal to take doping test; International ‌Tennis Integrity Agency has since confirmed charge; former ⁠Wimbledon champion Marketa Vondrousova handed four-year ban in June after tribunal found she refused test

France's Arthur Rinderknech is confident he will be cleared of an alleged refusal to undergo a doping test.

The International ‌Tennis Integrity Agency [ITIA] confirmed on Wednesday that the 31-year-old had been charged for refusing a test in Brittany in June after French newspaper L'Equipe initially reported in their story that he was facing a four-year ban.

Rinderknech denied the allegation on Instagram, while stressing that he has never taken a banned substance and revealing he has asked for the ​matter to be heard by an independent tribunal.

The ITIA said in a statement: "Following media reports and the player's social media post, we can confirm that Arthur Rinderknech has been charged with refusing a test.

"We go to great lengths to keep cases confidential, and we are disappointed that the details have become public in this way.

"The case will be heard by an independent tribunal early next year and we will not be commenting further on the specifics of the investigation."

The ITIA also explained that the maximum length ban Rinderknech could face if found to have refused a test is four years, but could be up to two years if the panel determines there is no significant fault.

Rinderknech had earlier written: "I am aware of the reports regarding my alleged test refusal case ​with the ITIA which according to the ITIA, was ⁠supposed to be kept strictly confidential.

"I have already advised the ITIA that I deny the allegation, and I have ‌already requested that the matter be heard and determined by an independent tribunal as is my right.

"It is my belief that when the matter is presented to the independent tribunal I will be ⁠exonerated of the test refusal allegation.

"I have ​never taken any banned substance and I am ​proud of my accomplishments in professional tennis, all of which ​have been achieved through hard work and with integrity always."

Former ⁠Wimbledon women's champion Marketa Vondrousova received a four-year ban in June after an independent tribunal found she refused an anti-doping test in December ⁠2025, with the Czech since appealing to the Court ​of Arbitration for Sport.

Rinderknech has a highest career ranking of 24 and reached the final of the Shanghai Masters 1000 event in 2025 before losing in straight sets to cousin ‌Valentin Vacherot of Monaco.

Rinderknech's best Grand Slam result is reaching the fourth round of the US Open last year, while he beat current world No 1 Alexander Zverev in the first round of Wimbledon in 2025.
