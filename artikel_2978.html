Bengals Film Room: Amarius Mims is the best right tackle in the NFL

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Amarius Mims is finally getting the national recognition he so rightfully deserves this season. Mims has been playing at an All-Pro level at right tackle for the Cincinnati Bengals.

Often left on an island, Mims has allowed just 6 total pressures the entire season. Allowing just 6 pressures is the lowest of any right tackle in the league that has played at least 50% of their team's required snaps. The only tackle in the league with a lower number is Denver left tackle Garrett Bolles. Mims also has 40 more pass-blocking snaps than Bolles has taken on the season.

No matter the numbers you want to look at, Mims has been the most dominant right tackle, if not overall tackle in the NFL this year.

I guess we should get the one pressure he allowed out of the way. He gave up a pressure to Jack Sawyer against Pittsburgh, the only one he allowed all game. That was on 47 total pass-blocking snaps. Mims has allowed just 2 pressures in his last 200 pass-blocking snaps played.

On the pressure to Sawyer, it appears he thought he would have help on the inside by Dalton Risner, but he could not get over quickly enough. Either way, Burrow was able to escape the pressure and avoid a negative play.

Beyond that, Mims was his usual dominating self again in primetime against the Steelers. He and the rest of the Bengals offense set the tone early against Pittsburgh. Mims escorted Watt up the field away from Burrow, Brown picked up the blitz, and Gesicki made a tough catch over the middle of the field.

Mims really has kept TJ Watt in check this season. To be honest, he kept him in check last year too. His performances against Watt last year is what had me believing he was going to take the leap into being an All-Pro talent this season, and that is exactly what has happened. Mims had TJ Watt in hell all night long, not letting him get any kind of pressure on Joe Burrow.

Maybe it doesn't mean a lot to most of you, but I loved that Mims was willing to give a little extra shove to Watt when he started to show his frustration later in the game. The Steelers love to be the more physical team, and set the tone for what type of game you will be playing against them. That wasn't the case this week, as Mims and the rest of the Bengals team were the tone-setters.

There is still a lot of season left to be played, but Amarius Mims should be an obvious selection for All-Pro if he continues playing at the level he has all season. I think it is safe to say that TJ Watt would agree with that being the case.
