The Frogs in The Legend of Zelda Ocarina of Time are found in the Zora River, and need songs played to them to complete their side quest. Referring to themselves as the Fabulous Five Froggish Tenors, Link's goal is to play five songs to them that they want to hear, which then unlocks a new minigame that you can play. If you can complete the frog song minigame, that then grants you a new piece of heart, a very valuable commodity. If you want to know how to complete this side quest, I'll take you through a full walkthrough below and what you need to know about the frogs and their love of music.

To find the Five Froggish Tenors and start this side quest, you need to go to Zora River, which is accessed via the Northeast edge of Hyrule Field.

Halfway along this route, on the North side, you'll find a small alcove with five pillars in it. This is where the Frogs are waiting. Simply interact with them like any NPC to begin the quest.
