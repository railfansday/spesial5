GTA 6 Too Dark? Here's How to Fix It on PS5

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Optimal HDR calibration settings included

If you’re playing GTA 6 and finding it too dark, there’s a good chance you may have your HDR calibration wrong – and no, it’s not your fault!

An extensive research video by HDTV Test – performed on a pricey Sony reference monitor – reveals the game’s HDR calibration may not be working quite as intended.

So while you may have followed the steps perfectly, setting your values until the Rockstar logo is only just visible as instructed, it may not be displaying optimally in your game.

I’d recommend you watch HDTV Test’s video for the full scientific analysis, but in short he explains that the various values in the HDR calibration settings output unexpectedly in-game.

Furthermore, the Rockstar release completely ignores Sony’s global PS5 HDR settings, so while you may have those calibrated perfectly for your particular screen, that won’t help you here.

Here’s how to fix it on PS5, according to HDTV Test – although do keep in mind your optimal personal settings will depend on your display, room, and preferred ambient lighting.

Load the game and press Options > Settings > Display > HDR Calibration.

HDTV Test recommends ignoring Rockstar’s own brightness calibration, so don’t worry too much if the company’s iconic logo is visible or not.

Instead focus on the two sample images of the party on the boat, which you can toggle between light and dark using R1.
