T1 brings TenZ out of retirement a day after re-signing crashies from Fnatic

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

T1 has added two-time Masters champion Tyson 'TenZ' Ngo to its Valorant roster for VCT Pacific in 2027. The move ends his retirement and builds on T1's signing of crashies, who has just left London-based Fnatic.

T1 has ended Tyson ‘TenZ’ Ngo’s retirement. The organisation confirmed on 12 November that he will join its Valorant roster for VCT Pacific in 2027. It made the announcement with a post and a welcome video.

T1 called him a ‘living legend’ of Valorant. It said he was starting a new chapter with the organisation.

TenZ retired from professional play in 2024. He has spent this year at T1 as a content creator. The announcement moves him from that role into the competitive squad.

His record explains the attention. With Sentinels, he beat Fnatic 3-0 in the Masters Reykjavík 2021 final. He then won Masters Madrid 2024 with a 3-2 win over Gen.G. That made him the first Americas player to win more than one VCT Masters event, Liquipedia records.

The TenZ news came a day after T1 confirmed Austin ‘crashies’ Roberts. He returns six years after T1 released him in September 2020. crashies called T1 the place ‘where it all started’. He said he was excited for the new challenge.

He played for Fnatic from 11 December 2024 until 4 November 2026. He joined T1 on 11 November 2026.

His exit is part of a wider reshuffle at the London-based organisation. Fnatic lost the Masters Toronto 2025 grand final 3-1 to Paper Rex. It then lost the Valorant Champions 2025 grand final in Paris 3-2 to NRG. On 2 November, Fnatic released head coach Andrey ‘ENGH’ Sholokhov. It said it was making changes ahead of the 2027 season.

T1 has also kept some continuity. It extended BuZz’s contract on 6 November 2026. The new deal runs until the end of 2028.

The VCT structure for 2027 was announced on 28 October. Under it, the eight Pacific partner teams go straight into Kickoff in January 2027. The other four Kickoff places go to teams from regional open qualifiers.

Before Kickoff, T1 has a date in Los Angeles. It is one of six teams directly invited to Red Bull Home Ground 2026. Fnatic, Sentinels, G2 Esports, NRG and ZETA DIVISION have also been invited. That puts the former clubs of both TenZ and crashies in the same field as T1.

The event runs from 10 to 13 December. Group stage matches are on 10 and 11 December. Bracket play and the semi-finals follow on 12 December. The grand final is on 13 December.
