Fortnite Players are Scrambling for an Exceedingly Rare Treasure

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Daniel is a lifelong gamer with a love for various genres and gameplay styles who enjoys exploring games and playing from various angles.

In One Piece, the titular mysterious treasure left behind by the late Pirate King Gol D. Roger is said to be something of such unfathomable power, it could overturn everything people know about the world itself. Over 20 years later, we still don’t know what the One Piece actually is, but there have certainly been more than a few treasure hunts inspired by its legendary mystique. Rather fittingly, one of those treasures has manifested in the midst of Fortnite’s newest pirate-themed mini-season, and it's driving the internet bonkers.

On Sunday, following the end of the Fortnitemares Halloween event, Fortnite began an interstitial mini-season to tide players over until the next major content drop in December. This mini-season, Treasure Hunters, is most predominantly One Piece-themed, featuring a new map with various One Piece locales and a handful of One Piece characters in the shop and Battle Pass.

The people gave Sweeney and Epic all the attention and support it needed when it rose up to Apple. As a reward, players got a significant price hike.

However, in addition to that, outside of the actual announcements, a new element has been quietly added to the game, and following its discovery by the playerbase, a mad scramble to locate it on par with One Piece’s own Great Age of Piracy has begun. Players have set sail from all over in search of the Pirate Shop Token, the treasure that will make their (cosmetic-centric) dreams come true.

The Pirate Shop Token is a new item that has begun spawning in chests on the new map during Fortnite's pirate-themed Treasure Hunters mini-season. To put it succinctly, obtaining the Pirate Shop Token is a free pass to almost everything in the Fortnite shop.

Obtaining the Pirate Shop Token is a free pass to almost everything in the Fortnite shop.

If you happen to obtain the token, as the game will inform you after the fact, you can activate it at any time until its expiration on December 16, 2026. When the token is activated, any item in the Fortnite shop will become free to claim, even if you don’t have a single V-Buck to your name. From pickaxes to outfits to gliders, it’s all fair game. Not only that, but from the moment of activation, the Pirate Shop Token remains in effect for two weeks, which means you can keep helping yourself to the shop’s contents day after day until it expires. You’ll also receive a second token that can be activated at a currently-indeterminate point in 2027.

Following the arrest of a featured artist, Fortnite is allowing players to refund associated cosmetics.

There are some caveats here: real money offers, gifting and passes levels and rewards are excluded, and you can only claim one free sidekick that you don’t already own. Beyond those few bits, though, the rest is plunderable. We’re talking cumulative values in the hundreds of dollars in assorted cosmetics here, and suffice to say, a lot of people would very much like that.

Perhaps unsurprisingly, such a fabulous treasure isn’t going to be easy to find. The only way to obtain the Pirate Shop Token is to find it on the map after it randomly spawns in, for which there is no particular indication or notification. The actual odds of this spawn occurring are a black box secret held by Epic, though some rumors have circulated that they’re in the ballpark of 1 in 700,000. There have also been rumors that you’re more likely to find it if you’re lower-leveled, but these are probably bunk.

The only tangible thing you can do to improve your odds is grouping up. If one player in a party finds the token, everyone in the party gets it. This has sparked a new wave of mercenaries in the Fortnite community, with players offering their services to others to party up and systematically sweep the island for its chests in the hopes of finding a token.

On a less savory note, some users with alleged Pirate Shop Token access have begun attempting to sell their accounts on eBay, with proposed prices in the thousands, while others have attempted to circulate cheating tools to fudge the spawn rates. Epic issued a statement on these practices via the Fortnite Status X, formerly Twitter page, writing, “Buying an account or using fake tools is a scam. This is just like any of the chests in Battle Royale, so every player has an equal opportunity to find Pirate Shop Access.” Buying and selling Fortnite accounts is also a bannable offense, so don’t do that.

The group is seeking over €100 million in combined refunds and damages on behalf of underage players.
