Nintendo May Have Just Teased a Beloved Zelda Game Coming to Switch Online

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Covering the video games industry since 2017, with experience in news, articles, and reviews. Currently a writer at DualShockers and gaming magazines.

It's been a little over a week since the release of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake, but Zelda fans are already hungry for more (and I include myself there!).

And while Nintendo hasn't announced another major release for the franchise just yet, a curious discovery on the company's official website has sparked speculation that one beloved adventure could finally be making its way to Nintendo Switch Online.

Following the remake’s launch, Nintendo recently launched a new section on Nintendo.com dedicated to the full catalog of games in The Legend of Zelda franchise.

Called “Game History”, the page lists the series' entries in release order, allowing visitors to explore the history of Hyrule and see which platforms each adventure originally launched on. You can also sort and filter the games however you like, and there's even a lovely little feature that shows how old you were when each Zelda game came out!

Nintendo has announced that the remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time became the fastest selling Switch 2 game in under a week.

However, the most interesting detail is that Nintendo's website also indicates which games are playable through Nintendo Switch Online. And that's where things get a little strange.

As spotted by fans on Reddit, The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds is currently listed as playable with Nintendo Switch Online on the official website. That's particularly interesting because the 2013 adventure was originally released for the Nintendo 3DS and isn't currently available through the subscription service.

As spotted by fans on Reddit, The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds is currently listed as playable with Nintendo Switch Online on the official website.

Naturally, this has led some players to believe Nintendo might be preparing to bring the beloved handheld classic to modern platforms and just let that accidentally slip! But I gotta say, A Link Between Worlds is widely regarded as one of the standout entries in the franchise, and its unique mechanic of allowing Link to transform into a painting and move along walls would definitely make for a welcome addition to the Switch Online library.

The first gameplay trailer for The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake has confirmed that it has added a beloved feature from the 3DS version.

Still, not everyone is convinced this is an intentional tease. Some players believe the listing is simply a website error that Nintendo will eventually correct – after all, the company hasn't announced any plans to introduce Nintendo 3DS games to Switch Online, and the service doesn't currently offer a dedicated 3DS game library.

It is important to highlight that, at the time of writing, Nintendo hasn't publicly addressed the listing or announced that A Link Between Worlds is coming to Nintendo Switch Online. And the game is still marked as playable through the service on the official website, but that alone isn't enough to confirm anything!

Of course, this wouldn't be the first time Nintendo fans have found themselves digging through official websites for clues about upcoming releases. And considering how many classic Zelda adventures remain unavailable on modern hardware, it's easy to understand why this particular discovery has generated excitement.

For now, I'd recommend keeping expectations in check! A Link Between Worlds joining Nintendo Switch Online would be fantastic news, but until Nintendo says something officially, this is still firmly in rumor territory. Hopefully, it's a mistake that the company won't be correcting anytime soon.
