Relic Entertainment has announced in a YouTube video that its military tactics game Company of Heroes 3 is getting an official macOS port in December, with no exact date specified. Apple-bound players will finally get their chance to play the latest title in the prestigious Company of Heroes series.

World War 2 has been a fixture of gaming practically as long as it has existed, and with its prevalence throughout hundreds of titles, new entries have stepped up to showcase theatres of war outside of Eastern Europe. Company of Heroes 3 makes its mark by focusing on the Italian and North African campaigns, two less glamorous theatres of war that have long earned the spotlight.

The macOS version of Company of Heroes 3 is also launching with the major shake-ups of all previous balance and content patches, including the 3.0 overhaul, but it appears all DLC will need to be purchased separately, one of the biggest criticisms of the game’s current Steam release. To celebrate, the game is currently on sale on Steam as well, giving curious players a chance to hop in as the potential player base grows.

Mac players will also be able to nab the standalone Final Stand mode, a survival battle using most of Company of Heroes 3’s systems to simulate an endless onslaught of attackers. The mode itself has also launched to mixed reviews, as players seem rather tired of the DLC practices of the game itself. The macOS version also requiring the DLC feels like a missed opportunity to win back some fans.

Whether it’s Linux or Mac, Windows being the expectation and central platform means plenty of releases just miss the alternate PC operating systems, leaving them out to dry when they’d love to join.

All in all, the port is exactly what it says on the tin, a chance for macOS fans of the franchise to finally jump on the 2023 release, almost 4 years after its initial drop. Anyone still loyal to the series who hasn’t gotten the chance to play it is likely chomping at the bit and can only hope Relic implements a sale when it finally does make the leap over.

Relic Entertainment has been in a bit of a rough spot as of late, after being passed over as the team for the newest Dawn of War title. Dawn of War, the real-time Warhammer 40K tactics series, has long since been Relic’s baby alongside Company of Heroes, and seeing them off the project is a massive shake-up.

For now, running off this port and last year’s Dawn of War 2 remaster, Relic Entertainment is looking for some small wins to get themselves back in the game, something this new macOS port should really help with. Opening their latest titles to fresh faces should lead to a big boost both in revenue and players, something they desperately need as they move onto their next project.

Relic is stacking up an easy win with the upcoming port of Company of Heroes 3, one of their stronger titles. Fans know the DLC song and dance and are far more willing to play ball, but Relic needs newcomers now more than ever.
