Reacher's Prime Video Replacement Is 2026's Best Thriller Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Reacher has held its place in the top spot as Prime Video's best action series since it first aired in 2022. After the polarizing movies featuring Tom Cruise as Lee Child's towering Jack Reacher, it seemed likely that the bestselling crime thriller book series would never have another chance at an adaptation. However, not only is Reacher much better in TV format, but Alan Ritchson physically embodies the character in one of the best TV performances of the 2020s. While Reacher has not yet beaten its first season, it remains one of the most consistently great shows on the platform.

It is hardly surprising that later action TV shows have tried to be the next Reacher, as the show has an unbeatable anthology format that allows new viewers to begin the franchise at any time, and a range of supporting characters who last long enough to be exciting, without overshadowing the titular characters. However, while none have succeeded in being the next Reacher, a surprising entry has beaten Reacher as the best action thriller TV series of 2026, largely because the show actively worked to avoid copying the iconic series. Neagley now holds Prime Video's top spot.

Reacher's spinoff series centering on his friend and colleague Frances Neagley (Maria Sten) was initially met with understandable skepticism. Competent, intriguing, and with a very different outlook on life to Reacher, Neagley is easily the best of Reacher's allies, but that does not necessarily translate to her carrying an entire series on her own. Fans fears were soon set to rest, though, as Sten proved just as compelling as she was in Reacher when Neagley would arrive to give him some useful information, skewer him with some well-chosen comments, and be on her way.

Neagley might follow a similar set-up to Reacher, with Neagley investigating the suspicious death of her childhood friend and linking it to a terrifying conspiracy involving a corrupt pharmaceutical company, but the shows are immediately different in the ways that count. Rather than trying to copy Reacher, Neagley is immediately set apart for the way she interacts with her allies. Neagley is afraid to be touched, but this does not make her a solo drifter like Reacher. Instead, the show expands on her relationships and background, building her a life and investigation team like Reacher's 110th Special Investigators.

Viewers reacted positively to the emotional depth and vulnerability Neagley shows, which drove the plot and characters forward rather than bogging them down, allowing the series to still shine as an action thriller, but become one with more heart than Reacher. Season 4 of Reacher was the weakest yet in terms of fan ratings, with Rotten Tomatoes fans criticizing Reacher's actions and the wooden side characters, giving it a 64% positive score. On the other hand, Neagley's new take on the genre, complete with a cameo from Alan Ritchson, was a breath of fresh air.

2026 has been an excellent year for action thriller TV series, but most of the critically acclaimed entries have continued already-popular shows. Guy Ritchie dominated the genre with MobLand and The Gentlemen both airing their second seasons, Reacher aired its fourth season, The Boys had its finale, and The Night Manager aired its long-anticipated second season with Tom Hiddleston. The fact that a new show has taken the title of the best action TV series released in 2026 is a testament to its quality. That said, Reacher is not far behind.

The fifth season of Reacher is based on the book Make Me, which follows Reacher as he visits the small town of Mother's Rest, intrigued by the name, and discovers horror at its center. The remote location hides a farm presenting itself as a euthanasia facility, while actually being a place that appears straight out of the horror movie, Hostel, where people are tortured and murdered with the videos for sale on the Dark Web. This is a wildly different direction for the show to go in, and Make Me is known for being one of Child's most disturbing books.

Season 4 saw Reacher's biggest weakness becoming its greatest strength as Reacher developed significantly as a character, with the ending montage being genuinely moving. With the subject matter for Make Me being potentially traumatizing, he must not come out of this unscathed, and possibly even with a longer-lasting partner. Make Me introduced Michelle Chang, who is a rare love interest for Reacher, as she is mentioned in more than one book. Reacher is a drifter by nature, so most of the books are standalone stories, but the show could stand to depart from this once in a while.

One criticism of Reacher is the fact that the show's formula has been growing slightly same-y, even with seasons 2 and 4 involving far-reaching conspiracies. The show has so far failed to capture the small-town mystery setting of the first season that gave us Reacher's best love interest, Roscoe, fan-favorite Oscar Finlay, and nailed the exact tone of Lee Child's books. Reacher's fifth season is going to change genre entirely, even verging into horror, and this could be exactly the kind of shake-up that could give the series back its crown.

While Reacher and Neagley are different from each other, which was essential for Neagley's success, the points they have in common have been the reason they stand out against other action thrillers. Both series work so well because they combine hard-hitting action and smart writing with extremely distinctive central characters that cannot be emulated. Reacher made some necessary changes to the books in order to give its titular character more lines instead of interior monologs, but the show kept his stoic and deadpan personality, without making him overly angsty.

Neagley could have followed Reacher's formula and style, with a different character at the center of the action, but this would have made the series into the watered-down spinoff that many viewers and critics feared it could be. Instead, the focus on her sharper detective skills, tactical abilities, and unintentionally hilarious quips, which all set her apart from Reacher, allow her to carry the show rather than have the spinoff be "Reacher-lite."

Neagley's role was expanded for the TV series, as she only appears in four main Jack Reacher novels, and two short stories.

The question of whether Neagley season 1 or Reacher season 4 is better has been answered, with Neagley taking the top spot. That said, no other action thriller series comes close to the two. The 2020s have given action fans a lot to watch, but Reacher and Neagley have dominated their niche through well-realized main characters with different strengths, making both series must-watches before their next seasons, which are expected to air in 2027.
