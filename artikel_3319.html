Nicolas Cage & Chris Pine To Star In Vegas Drama Series ‘American Epic’ From B.J. Novak & A24

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

EXCLUSIVE: Nicolas Cage and Chris Pine are set to star together in American Epic, a drama series package, which has been taken out by A24, generating interest from multiple buyers, Deadline has learned. The Office alum B.J. Novak is writing the project, which comes from Joe Hipps’ A24-based company Cut To.

In American Epic, in a final bid to forge his legacy, magnetic Vegas casino mogul Wyatt Pascal (Cage) squares off with Midwestern pastor Bill Massey (Pine) to pull off one last impossible act: building something eternal in the most temporary place on earth.

Novak executive produces alongside Pine and Ian Gotler of Barry Linen Motion Pictures and Hipps and Patrick McDonald of Cut To. HBO and Amazon are among the platforms interested in the project, I hear, with the latter’s involvement intriguing as Cage, coming off a starring role on Prime Video’s Spider-Noir, recently called Amazon “a very AI-friendly company” and said that he was “probably not going to be working with that studio again.”

This would be the first on-screen pairing for Cage and Pine who were part of the voice cast of the 2018 animated film Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse where their characters did not overlap.

American Epic marks Hipps’ latest big package with top talent attached, which has become the former Fifth Season executive’s calling card since he segued to producing with a deal at A24 in fall 2024. The four previous ones all sold in competitive situations with straight-to-series orders, the most recent, thriller drama The Apology, headlined by Gwyneth Paltrow, landing a two-season pickup at Netflix. The others are Discretion, starring Nicole Kidman and Elle Fanning, at Paramount+; Trigger Point, headlined by Joel Edgerton, at Netflix; and an espionage thriller starring Millie Bobby Brown and David Harbour from Adolescence co-creator Jack Thorne, also at Netflix.

Oscar winner Cage, who plays the title role in the upcoming biopic Madden, is set to star alongside Antonio Banderas in Season 5 of HBO’s crime anthology series True Detective. He also has coming up Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse, Lords of War, Fortitude and The Prince. Cage is repped by WME, Stride Management and attorney Patrick Knapp.

This would mark Pine’s first major TV role since the 2019 TNT limited series I Am the Night, which he starred in and executive produced. In features, the Star Trek alum has coming up The Catch romantic comedy, in which he stars alongside Emma Stone, Alpha Gang, Yeti and My Darling California. Pine is repped by CAA, Brillstein Entertainment Partners and Gendler, Kelly & Cunningham.

Novak is best known as a cast member, writer and executive producer of NBC’s Emmy-winning The Office. He wrote, directed and headlined the 2022 movie Vengeance. As an actor, Novak was recently seen in The Devil Wears Prada 2. He is repped by CAA and Johnson Shapiro Slewett & Kole.A24’s upcoming TV slate also includes Hulu’s The Spot and Peacock crime drama Superfakes. The studio is behind Peacock’s breakout series Crystal Lake and Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim’s hit documentary You Can See Everything. A24 also is taking out a new Jesse Eisenberg series, written, executive produced and to be directed by Eisenberg, executive produced by Peter Rice under his A24 deal.
