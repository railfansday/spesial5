The 35+ Best Gifts for Dads, According to My Father’s Favorite (and Only) Daughter

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Your dad has been there through it all: Helping you at the kitchen counter while you were crying over math homework, showing up to tee-ball games where maybe you weren’t exactly the best player on the team (OK, I cried during those, too), and being your biggest support system through just about every stage of life. The least you can do is return the favor this holiday season with a gift that shows him how much you appreciate all those years of showing up.

But where do you even start shopping for the man who treated your middle school musicals like they were Tony Award-winning Broadway productions? That’s where I come in. Here’s a quick rundown of my credentials: I shop for a living (seriously, I spend eight hours a day scouring the internet for exciting new products), I’ve tested thousands of different items, and, perhaps most importantly, I’m the daughter of a dad who also happens to be my best friend. So while your father and I haven’t officially met, I feel pretty confident I can help you find something he’ll love.

When shopping for Dad, start with his interests. (And if you don’t know what those are, I’m going to assume your sibling is the favorite child.) Is he obsessed with Bruce Springsteen like my dad is? Does he live for golf, or prefer a snow-based sport like skiing? And why is he watching all those rogue YouTube videos? If you can answer those questions, great: There are plenty of picks on this list for him. If not, don’t worry. I’ve rounded up enough crowd-pleasers and unexpected finds to give you a fighting chance at finding something he’ll actually want to unwrap.

Your dad may have prepared you for plenty of things in life, but hopefully, one of them was the importance of being on time. That’s especially true when holiday shopping. Hanukkah starts Dec. 4 this year, Christmas Eve is Dec. 24, and shipping deadlines typically fall about two weeks before each holiday. So, just like he used to tell you during your hockey games, you’d better get moving.

Shop the 35+ best Christmas gifts for dads below. And while every pick here is a considerable upgrade from the ladybug holder I got my dad from the dollar store in kindergarten, I know he’d cherish any one of these gifts just the same.

It took me a while to convince my dad to retire his ratty Dallas Cowboys pajama bottoms in favor of these soft sets, but once I did, he said he’d never go back. They’re one of the best sellers in our holiday gift guides, and you can grab a pair for your dad for less than $105.

When I was little, my dad would sit with us in the kitchen for hours, making paper airplanes and holding flying competitions. We never won, mostly because he always made sure his plane was just a little bit better than ours. Last year, I upgraded him to a smart paper airplane that he can control with his phone, bringing one of our favorite childhood hobbies into the 21st century. And, for the record, I still lose.
