The Best Early Tech Deals From Walmart’s 2026 Black Friday and Cyber Monday Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The first of Walmart's three major sales for Black Friday and Cyber Monday is now live, and there are already some amazing tech deals to take advantage of. I've gathered my top 10 tech products from the sale to make your life a little easier. For more, keep tabs on our Black Friday coverage, where we post the latest deals from not just Walmart, but Amazon, Best Buy, and other retailers' Black Friday deals.

Apple's latest smartwatch is the Series 12, but don't count out the Series 11. By sacrificing a few of Apple's newest features, you can save some money on the more premium version of this last-gen Apple Watch. Right now, the Titanium Series 11 GPS + cellular model is on sale for $429.

During WWDC 26 earlier this summer, Apple announced new Siri AI upgrades with watchOS 27—but only some Apple Watches have compatibility. Luckily, the Series 11 is one of them, at least for many of them. Some other upgrades in the new OS include a dedicated Siri app, a new app grid, a new single-tap gesture, among other features. Just note that only Series 12 and Ultra 4 can run Audio Intelligence.

The Garmin vivoactive 5 was $299.99 when it first came out in 2023 as their midrange lifestyle-focused fitness smartwatch. It features a 1.2-inch AMOLED touchscreen and an 11-day battery life (four to five days with the always-on display setting). You can get it for $169.99 right now. It's a great option for anyone looking to get into Garmin for the first time or upgrading from anything below it.

Bone conduction headphones are ideal for activities where it is vital to be able to hear your surroundings. They use a technology that sends audio vibrations to your inner ear while leaving your ear canals open. Shokz is one of, if not the best, brands making bone conduction headphones right now. At the high end of Shokz's offerings sit the OpenRun Pro 2, which impressed me when I tested them out for review. Walmart currently has a restored pair for $95 (originally $135.95 and $179.99 new).

The latest AirPods Pro are a big step up. The third iteration of Apple's iconic earbuds comes with a number of quality-of-life and software improvements that make them worth the typical premium over the AirPods Pro 2—and right now, the AirPods Pro 3 are even cheaper than the previous generation. They're $179 on Walmart as an early Black Friday deal, down from $249 and matching their lowest price ever.

Most, if not all, TV speakers aren't great. While you have plenty of options, the quickest and least expensive way to improve your TV's audio capabilities is to buy a soundbar. Compare a soundbar's size to the thin, small speakers jammed into modern TVs. The Vizio 4.1 Pro Soundbar and rear speakers come with Dolby Atmos support and cost just $79 (originally $148). They won't win any audio awards, but it's better than any built-in TV speakers, and for $79, it'll improve your experience without breaking the bank.

If you want to take things up a notch, consider this 5.1 channel sound system. Not only does it include far more channels than a single soundbar, but you're also adding a dedicated subwoofer to the mix. A subwoofer will make those thuds, explosions, and low vibrations much more pronounced than on a soundbar alone. For $149.99 (originally $249.99), you can get the ULTIMEA 5.1 surround sound system, which comes with a soundbar—replicating left, right, and center channels—two rear speakers, and a subwoofer.

I love projectors and Roku. To me, a Roku projector just makes sense, especially at $119.99 (originally $389.99). This projector is portable and has the Roku OS already installed, so it's easy to project it against a wall, ceiling, or screen, whether it's indoors or outdoors. It's easy to set up; you just need an outlet nearby and to dim or turn off the lights. If you have Bluetooth speakers, you can hook them up to create a more immersive sound.

The TCL Q51GB is a QLED Google TV that is a great value for anyone looking for size, color, and features. The 98-inch class is heavily discounted at its $898 price (originally $1,499.99). TCL makes one of the best QLEDs for the money, and their technology keeps getting better. This TV supports Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos, and if you're a gamer, you get a refresh rate of 240Hz that is automatically triggered with its Auto Game Mode feature.

Gaming monitors can get pricey fast. At $159.99 (originally $299.99 at launch), this 27-inch Samsung Odyssey G55C curved gaming monitor offers a 1440p resolution, a 1000R curve, a 165Hz refresh rate with FreeSync and G-SYNC compatibility to keep screen tearing to a minimum, and features like Samsung’s Virtual Aim Point and Contrast Enhancer. It's a solid choice for gaming, especially at this price point.

Alienware makes great gaming laptops, and the Aurora is a great option for anyone who wants to get all the essentials without spending too much. At its current $1,099 price (originally $1,999), the Alienware Aurora 16 becomes a mid-tier gaming laptop at a "budget" price. We reviewed the higher-tier Aurora 16X and were impressed with it. The Aurora 16 has a slightly slower processor, a dimmer display with half the refresh rate, a smaller battery, and a weaker graphics card, but given its price, it's great for a gamer looking for a solid gaming laptop in the $1,000 range.
