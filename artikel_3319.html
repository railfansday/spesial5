Matt Shakman Launches Foolscap Production Banner, Unveils Exec Team

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Matt Shakman, the multiple Emmy and DGA Award nominee behind the likes of WandaVision and The Fantastic Four: First Steps, has launched a new production banner, Foolscap.

Shakman describes Foolscap as a filmmaker-first company that will work to redefine genre through elevated commercial storytelling. The company will work across film & TV, always with a focus on character, heart and humor.

Andrew Stearn has been appointed as President of Foolscap, with Alie Rivier Gutman as Vice President of TV Development and Lulu Dillon as Vice President of Feature & TV Production. The company has a first-look deal at 20th TV, with projects set up at 20th, A24, Hulu, FX, Onyx, Paramount +, ABC, Mattel and Fifth Season. Their key collaborators include esteemed writers such as Akiva Goldsman, Bruce Miller and MJ Cerar, among many others. Shakman is perhaps best known for his work within the MCU, as director of The Fantastic Four: First Steps, and producer and director of WandaVision, a Marvel miniseries that brought him multiple Emmy nominations, alongside one from the DGA. Over the years, he’s also worked on series such as Monarch: Legacy of Monsters, The Consultant, Welcome to Chippendales, The Great, It’s Always Sunny in Philadelphia, Succession and Game of Thrones. Also a theatre director, he enjoyed a six-year run as the artistic director of the Geffen Playhouse in Los Angeles. Watch on Deadline

Earlier this year, Shakman directed the pilot and finale, and served as executive producer on Wild Things, Apple’s limited series on Siegfried & Roy starring Jude Law and Andrew Garfield. He just wrapped filming on the pilot for the upcoming Paramount+ show Discretion, starring Nicole Kidman and Elle Fanning, and is set to direct and produce a new Planet of the Apes film for 20th Century Studios.

Foolscap President Stearn most recently finished the first season of Hulu’s Good American Family, where he served as exec producer. Before that he had his own production company, Andrew Stearn Productions, based at ABC Signature. While there, he completed production on Rebel, starring Katey Sagal and Andy Garcia, for ABC.

The recipient of three Emmy Nominations, as well as a Peabody, AFI and GLAAD Award, Stearn spent three and a half years as President of Working Title Television U.S., where he served as executive producer on the Netflix series Gypsy, starring Naomi Watts, and Tales of the City, starring Laura Linney, as well as HBO’s docuseries, The Case Against Adnan Syed. Before joining Working Title, he worked with John Wells for 17 years, including a lengthy stint as president of Warner Bros/ TV-based John Wells Prods. During his time at that company, he served as a producer on The West Wing and Third Watch at NBC and as an executive producer on Southland for NBC/TNT, Shameless for Showtime and Animal Kingdom for TNT.

VP Dillon has worked across high-profile projects for Disney, Netflix, Marvel and independent filmmakers. She also founded Covid relief nonprofit Cook 19 and serves as an associate producer on Discretion at Paramount+, with additional producing credits including Fantastic Four for Marvel Studios and Wild Things for Apple TV. She will also be part of the producing team on Shakman’s Planet of the Apes.

VP Gutman began her career at 20th Century Fox Television and ABC Signature Studios before transitioning to producing with James Frey. During her time alongside Frey, she worked on projects including the series Relationship Status (Verizon Go90), American Gothic (CBS) and The Kicks (Amazon) and the feature film Queen & Slim. She then produced Rebel and Hulu’s Good American Family with Stearn through his deal at ABC. Currently, she’s in prep on Season 2.
