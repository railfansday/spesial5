Moritz Wagner’s first-ever chance to face his brother Franz is nothing short of ‘awkward’

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Brooklyn's Moritz Wagner has been in the NBA for nine years and alive for 20 more, and he had never, ever played against his brother, Franz, in a game that counted.

That is, until Friday night. The Wagner siblings faced off when the Nets hosted Franz's Orlando Magic, and they squared off against each other for the first time.

"It's awkward, I'm not gonna lie. I don't really know how to compartmentalize it, really deal with it, and try to focus on the game plan and see what happens emotionally," Moritz said beforehand. "It's a dilemma because you want him to do well. At the same time, you game plan for him, you prepare to limit him. So, it's an awkward feeling. I've never been in this position.

Brooklyn Nets center Moritz Wagner (13) warms up before an NBA basketball game against the Orlando Magic at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, NY, Nov. 20, 2026. Robert Sabo for NY Post

"It's weird. It's definitely a new role I've got to grow into. But it's also fun, and we talked about that [Thursday]. It's an absolute privilege, and for our family it's really cool because we've established each other as independent NBA players on two different sides and get to play against each other in this league and have 20 people here watching us. So it's very special, and it's gonna be an emotional night for our family."

Moritz played for Michigan and was a 2018 first-round pick by Orlando, where he'd spent his entire career until signing with the Nets this summer.

Franz, four years his junior, had followed in his footsteps and starred for the Wolverines before being picked eighth overall by the Magic in 2021.

Orlando Magic's Franz Wagner handles the ball during an NBA Cup game against the Brooklyn Nets at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, N.Y., Friday, Nov. 20, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

"My whole NBA career I played with him. The first time in my life playing against him," Franz said. "But as weird as it's going to be, it's a blessing to share the court with your family.Not a lot of people get to do that, so that's how I look at it."

The younger Wagner is breaking out this season with Paolo Banchero injured, entering the meeting averaging 24.4 points, 8.5 assists and 6.9 rebounds.

And it was 30.7 points, 8.5 assists and 7.7 boards over his prior six games.

"We've got a new staff, a new system and a lot of clarity on roles and what's expected of everyone. That helps everyone with their rhythm," Franz said.

"He's playing incredible," Moritz said. "New coach, new system, found a way to be a playmaker and scorer at the same time, dominating the pace, the paint. Obviously with an enormous usage rate as well, which you've got to give him credit for the physicality he plays with. One thing that's really underrated about Franz is his defensive ability. There aren't many players that dominate on both ends of the floor like he does. … It's a joy to watch him as a big brother, regardless of my situation."

Young fan Dylan wears a custom jersey featuring Orlando Magic forward Franz Wagner and Brooklyn Nets center Moritz Wagner in Brooklyn, N.Y., Nov. 20, 2026. Robert Sabo for NY Post

That situation is serving as backup center and veteran voice on a struggling Nets squad.

Many have suggested that Brooklyn could target Franz if Orlando breaks him and Banchero up. But for the moment, the Nets have to figure out their own woes internally, and Franz was the enemy Friday.

"I grew up with him. When he's mad, when I see that look in his eyes, it takes me back to when we were 9 and 5 and playing against each other. I don't think I know a human better than him. So on an emotional level, just knowing what he goes through with every step, it's weird to be the opponent," Moritz said.

"Obviously, [he's] a big part of my development as a person and as a player," Franz said. "So it should be fun out there."

The Wagner family was on hand to watch Friday. They even had specialized jerseys made with both of the brothers' names and team colors, and their mother Beate is especially nervous.

"We made these corny split jerseys," Moritz said. "I know my mom's going to go through it [Friday]. I know that. … That's going to be really difficult for her. Definitely a new spot for her. … But she's going to figure that out, and it's also a really good problem to have, obviously.."
