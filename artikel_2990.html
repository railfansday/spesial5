Precision Optics: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Satellite subassembly dipped to $2.3 million in the quarter, yet a fresh follow-on order is set to restore output to $2 million a quarter.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 26, 2026 at 4:07PM EST

On its first-quarter fiscal year 2027 earnings call, held on Nov. 16, 2026, Precision Optics (POCI +0.48%) reiterated that full-year revenue will reach $30 million to $33 million as production ramps across core medical and optical programs. Sustained operating progress across custom optics and medical device lines provides the foundation for long-term margin expansion, even as near-term order timing creates quarterly noise. Three takeaways from the call stand out for long-term investors.

Quarterly subassembly revenue dipped to $2.3 million as an existing satellite customer adjusted order timing, with shipment volume expected to bottom at $1.2 million in the second quarter. Management secured a follow-on order that supports anticipated production of approximately $2 million in each of the third and fourth quarters.

Management initiated production on a urology program during the quarter and restarted robotic surgery endoscope manufacturing this month, marking the first two of four planned commercial transitions this fiscal year. These four programs create a steady baseline of recurring manufacturing revenue.

Manufacturing throughput gains and higher overall volume expanded gross margin year over year despite lower sequential satellite shipments. Higher gross profit and lower operating expenses narrowed the quarterly net loss to approximately $475,000.

Production volume is rebounding across core business lines, giving management confidence in a stronger second half following an expected challenging second quarter. Precision Optics holds proprietary manufacturing capabilities in micro-optics and single-use medical devices, positioning the company to capture expanding market demand across healthcare and satellite communications. Management is focusing on product development pipeline execution and gross margin stability.

Expected production for a second satellite customer, slated to begin in the fourth quarter, and new medical product launches point toward record quarterly revenue levels by early fiscal year 2028
