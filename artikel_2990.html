HP (HPQ +11.13%) reported fiscal fourth quarter results characterized by significant growth in the commercial personal systems market and announced the appointment of Emma Walmsley as the incoming Chief Executive Officer starting Jan. 4, 2027. Management stated that the company is transitioning its reporting for stock-based compensation to align with industry peers while executing a multiyear transformation plan to reduce structural costs. The company indicated that its strategic focus remains on expanding high-value hardware categories and software solutions to offset anticipated declines in the broader personal computer market. This approach includes the deployment of AI-enabled devices and services designed to manage complex workloads at the edge of the network.
