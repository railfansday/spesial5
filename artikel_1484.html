PSG, Spain: Fabian Ruiz chooses between the Champions League and the World Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

If there is a player on this planet who has had particularly happy endings to seasons in recent years, it is Fabián Ruiz. The Spanish midfielder has, since 2024, won a Euro, a World Cup and two Champions Leagues. In an interview given to OK Diariothe Andalusian however decided between the title he prefers between the Champions League and the World Cup.

“The World Cup”he responded when asked to choose between the two. He still confirmed that he obviously wants to raise the big-eared cup a third time in a row: “it was already difficult to win one, two too. Now, we know that it will also be very difficult to find a third. But if we can win it, that would be incredible.”.
