Qapital is a savings app with three plans, none of them free. The fee pays off only if its rules get you saving more than a free weekly transfer from checking would.

Qapital is worth its fee for savers who put money aside only when an app does it for them. Anyone who can keep up a weekly transfer does better in a high-yield savings account, where $1,000 at a 3.5% APY earns $35 a year while Qapital Basic pays no interest and charges $30 to $36.
