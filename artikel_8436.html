Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Fidelity offers 2% cash back on all purchases and I have my monthly rewards deposited directly into my CMA. Super easy.

If you are an Amazon user, you get 5% cash back on Amazon purchases and can get 7-8% during heavy shopping periods if you will delay shipping.
The OP doesn't mention if they travel or not, but the other advantage of having the Fidelity CMA is their debit card, which doesn't have any transaction fees for getting cash at an ATM in a foreign country, as well as reimbursement of any fees imposed by the ATM operator. So opening a CMA account in order to get their credit card also gets you their debit card if you want it.

Of course, these days you don't need much cash in many countries, but it's nice to have a small amount for those occasions when you can't use a credit card.
I mean I travel, but I don't "travel". I travel now and again, but it's not a significant part of my identity. It's more of a means to an end. Cash back and travel rewards are nice, but not necessary, I guess.

I like the idea of putting some money into the Fidelity cash management account and then getting credit cards.
