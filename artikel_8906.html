SHISHI Dates Cafe / Naqsh architectural & engineering consultancy

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Text description provided by the architects. Located within the industrial fabric of Al Quoz, Dubai, SHI SHI brings together the worlds of production, retail, hospitality, and work within a single architectural environment. Designed by NAQSH Architecture, the project explores how the utilitarian character of a factory can coexist with the warmth and intimacy of a café and boutique. Rather than concealing the rawness associated with its industrial setting, the design embraces it as the foundation of its architectural identity.
