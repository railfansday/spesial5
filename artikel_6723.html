Your Gemini Monthly Horoscope for December 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Try something new and spice things up, Gemini!

It’s time to push your relationships outside of their comfort zone, Gemini! The month starts out with the Sun moving through Sagittarius, highlighting your closest ties. The next few weeks invite you to invest more time and energy into your one-on-one relationships so you can address issues, discuss the future, deepen connections, or part ways if necessary. This is also a season of collaboration—others are eager to work with you, which can bring more contract and client work into your world.

There’s finally more balance in your daily life when Venus enters Scorpio on December 3. You’re better able to navigate certain obligations in your life that have left your feeling like you’re in the trenches. There is more support available to you and you’re also gaining more recognition for all your hard work and effort. This can show up as a raise, a well-deserved compliment, or finally having the emotional courage and resilience to break a bad habit.

There are important conversations that need to be had in your closest relationships once Mercury enters Sagittarius on December 6. The next few weeks help you explore relationship dynamics—both how you show up in your closest relationships and also how others show up for you. There may be some issues you need to address, or even helpful conversations that pave the way for the future. This is an exciting time to share ideas and collaborate with others, bringing new ideas, perspectives, and opportunities your way.

A New Moon in Sagittarius on December 8 brings new relationship milestones your way. This can mark the start of a new relationship or business contract. You might also feel a sense of renewal in existing relationships, helping you to address what’s been left unsaid so you can create a healthier future together. Shared experiences today that push you outside of your comfort zone also bring more shared memories and joy. Try something new and spice up your closest connections.

You’re setting boundaries in your friendships when Saturn stations direct in Aries on December 10. You might need to confront an issue or even cut someone off! The energy can feel isolating today but also help you recognize the people in your life with whom you feel a genuine sense of belonging. This can also help you sign contracts or stay the course and commit to a longterm goal. You’re ready to lock in!

It’s time to go after your dreams when Neptune stations direct in Aries on December 12. This can show up as a surge of creative inspiration that helps you approach a desire in a new way. You might also stand out more for your creativity, ability to problem solve with your intuition, or your compassionate nature. People are mesmerized by you and eager to support you, so put yourself and your ideas out there!

Conversations leave you with you a lot to contemplate when Jupiter stations retrograde in Leo, also on December 12. You could share something big and important with others that brings a sense of relief, healing or celebration. You might learn lucky or hopeful news. You could also learn something that impacts you personally and shifts your perspective in a big way. Be open to other opinions and conversations; words and decisions you make are impactful today.

The Sun enters Capricorn and the Winter Solstice takes place on December 21, asking you to turn inward. It’s time to stick with a plan to break down some of your own walls. You’re ready to commit and do some inner work, even if it feels uncomfortable. This energy can also help you get back on track financially. You’re also more aware of the give and take in your relationships, and ready to set boundaries accordingly.

A Full Moon in Cancer on December 23 brings a financial situation to a climax. You could leave a job, wrap up a contract, or accept a promotion! There are changes in the way you resource yourself and work with your budget and finances. This can also signify a major purchase or an event that puts your self-esteem front and center. Are you embodying your personal values and making yourself proud, or could you use some adjustments and a course correction?

The month wraps up with Mercury entering Capricorn on December 25, helping you stand up for your worth and what you deserve. You’re feeling more confident when it comes to asking for what you need, both emotionally and financially. Mentally, you’re also diving deep and exploring your psyche and inner world, which can lead to breakthroughs. You know the more uncomfortable the topic, the more healing and transmutation that can occur. So go ahead and reclaim your power!
