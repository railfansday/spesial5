I didn’t have the best sleep setups growing up: I slept on rock-hard mattresses as a kid, then on a super-soft twin with zero support in college, plus the occasional sofa when I crashed with friends. When I got into my twenties, my poor mattress habits caught up with me, and I found myself dealing with random (or not so random) pains in my back, hips, and neck.

I had to be more discerning about what I slept on, and at the time, I was spending a lot of time at my boyfriend’s place, and he had just purchased a Nectar. It changed my life. Nine years later, we’re married, and we’re still sleeping on a Nectar. The Classic memory foam is the best mattress I have ever tested, and the one I keep coming back to because it’s affordable, has soft but firm-like support, and gets better with age.

Most memory foam mattresses start to cave in, turn yellow, sag, pill, or fray, but not the Nectar. It becomes more comfortable and molds to your body over time. The Classic bed has six layers, each of which provides a different perk, from cooling to support to motion isolation. We have a queen-size mattress at home, and I can hardly feel my husband get up or move around in bed. Plus, changing the fitted sheet is a breeze because the mattress isn’t heavy to lift.

I have tested mattresses that cost thousands of dollars and slept on countless beds at five-star hotels, yet I keep coming back to the Nectar as my ultimate favorite. The queen is only $699 right now during Black Friday/Cyber Monday, which is an insane steal. Typically, it’s $1,567, which still isn’t a terrible price considering most luxury mattresses will cost over $3k.

Over the years, we’ve managed to convince a few people to get this mattress, too, especially if they spend a night or two on it. After my husband’s cousins crashed on our Nectar Classic mattress once, they immediately asked what brand it was and bought one for their home, too.

A couple of months ago, I ordered one for my in-laws’ guest room, where my husband and I stay while we’re in town, because I could not spend one more holiday season on their super-hard mattress. Their old mattress gave me back pain, and I would just deal with it for the week I was there, but then I realized, why not just buy them a Nectar in King? They have the space in Virginia, and I would get to replicate my dream setup from home. Suffice it to say, this Thanksgiving I am spending seven days with them, and I’m already sleeping so much better thanks to the new Nectar Classic in our room.

Funnily enough, my sister-in-law and her husband were also complaining about the mattress in their guest room, and now I’m thinking...should I just replace theirs too with a Nectar? I’m sure my in-laws won’t mind, right?
