Tapered Jeans Are the Polished Way to Wear Denim Every Day This Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Jeans have always served as an off-duty style staple, with many of us turning to them as the base of a reliable outfit formula when we don’t know what to wear. Paired with leather boots and a shirt, they make for an easy, “smart casual” approach, while a cashmere sweater and sneakers is the ultimate weekend attire.

Now that we’re firmly in the fall season, we’ll be leaning on denim more than ever. And how better to achieve a level of polish for your outfits than with a tapered leg?

Usually, high-waisted barrel-leg jeans are notable for their extremely tapered leg, with a slim ankle and baggy knee creating the perfect combination of relaxed and sleek. But a tapered leg jean is even more refined than that, with just slightly less material around the thigh, meaning you can create a more streamlined silhouette.

Zara’s take on the trend leans into minimalism (despite the fact that embellished denim is in right now), with a front-pressed crease detail to add a feeling of precision. For under $80, the jean style comes in a range of color options, including a dark wash indigo—great for smarter occasions—a light blue, and a dark gray.

Style these jeans casually with a pair of ballet flats, a close-cropped, fine-gauge cashmere sweater, and a slicked-back bun. Or, layer them under a slip dress (don’t knock the dress-over-denim approach until you’ve tried it) and wear them with a pair of knee-high heeled boots for something more refined.
