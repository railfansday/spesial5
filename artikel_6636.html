Chocolate-Covered Strawberry Turkeys

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Turkey is the star of Thanksgiving dinner, but it can also be the star of dessert if you stretch your imagination enough. This idea first gained popularity on TikTok, using chocolate-dipped strawberries, marshmallows, pretzel rods, and melted chocolate to create these adorable little treats.

They're so simple to make: To make a leg, you spear two mini marshmallows with half a pretzel rod, then use kitchen shears to trim the edges into a long oval shape. Repeat for a second leg, then cut one mini marshmallow diagonally in half to make the wings. You'll stick these to the strawberry with a dab of melted chocolate (which acts as your glue), then let that sit to adhere. All that's left is a quick bath in melted chocolate.
