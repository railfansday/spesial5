MLS roundup: AI, fraud prevention and data sharing take center stage

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Multiple MLS organizations announced new partnerships focused on AI assistants, fraud prevention, and cross-market data sharing. Deals include Property Shield with Big Sky Country MLS, Scout inside Flexmls, RPR View data sharing, and RealReports for 15,000 agents.  AI Summary

MLS organizations are expanding their technology offerings with new partnerships focused on artificial intelligence, property fraud prevention and access to listing data. The latest developments include tools designed to streamline agents’ work, protect listing information and improve property research across markets.

The MLS will also use Property Shield’s automated IDX compliance monitoring to identify potential violations across licensed data feeds. The partnership addresses the growing problem of scammers copying legitimate listings to solicit deposits from unsuspecting buyers and renters.

FBS has introduced Flexmls Scout, an AI assistant that allows real estate professionals to search MLS data, analyze market activity and access business insights using natural-language questions.

Built directly into Flexmls, Scout uses permissioned MLS information and enterprise controls to help agents research properties and understand market trends without switching platforms. The product is available to Flexmls MLS customers as an enterprise solution.

Stellar MLS in Florida and MIBOR Broker Listing Cooperative in Indiana have announced a data-sharing agreement through Realtors Property Resource’s RPR View platform.

RealReports combines property research, prospecting, comparative market analysis and client engagement tools with information covering more than 150 million U.S. properties. Its AI assistant, Aiden, helps agents identify potential business opportunities and provide clients with more detailed property insights.

EquityProtect has partnered with Real Estate Business Services, the for-profit subsidiary of the California Association of Realtors, to make its property fraud-prevention platform available to association members.

The program provides tools to help agents educate homeowners about deed theft, seller impersonation and other property fraud risks. The partnership is designed to help agents maintain relationships with clients beyond closing while offering preventive protection.

Platinum is HAR.com’s subscription-based suite of marketing and productivity tools designed to help real estate professionals grow their business, strengthen their brand and work more efficiently. It was developed by HAR.com’s in-house technology team in 2016 and includes tools for lead generation, client relationship management, marketing and market insights.
