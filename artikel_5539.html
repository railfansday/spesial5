UWM rolls out 40 bps year-end pricing bonus, expands Bilt partnership

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

United Wholesale Mortgage introduced “40 to the Finish,” a year-end pricing incentive that adds 40 basis points to all products for loans with 700+ FICO scores when brokers use UWM’s Underwriting+, TRAC+ or virtual closing tools through Dec. 31. At the same time, UWM expanded its partnership with Bilt to integrate Bilt Neighborhood OS into its servicing platform.   AI Summary

United Wholesale Mortgage (UWM) this week announced the launch of a year-end pricing incentive for broker loans and an expansion of its partnership with Bilt.

Effective immediately through Dec. 31, brokers who use UWM’s Underwriting+, TRAC+ or virtual closing tools will receive 40 basis points on all agency and nonagency products when the borrower has a credit score of at least 700, UWM said.

The incentive is intended to give brokers more pricing flexibility in the final weeks of 2026 while encouraging use of the lender’s underwriting and closing technology. Year-end pricing specials are common in the wholesale channel as lenders seek to pull volume into the current year, clean up pipelines and help partners hit production goals.

UWM – which was more aggressive on pricing in the past but is seeking to deleverage its balance sheet amid a deal with Oaktree Capital Management and the Ishbia family – saw its gain-on-sale margins decline to 124 basis points in the third quarter, down from 133 basis points in the second quarter.

UWM chairman, CEO and president Mat Ishbia told analysts he expects margins to be steady, adding that “people don’t use UWM because we’re the lowest rates” and arguing that competitors offering lower rates are not good partners.

Separately, UWM said it’s expanding its partnership with Bilt, a neighborhood commerce and rewards platform, to bring Bilt Neighborhood OS into its servicing ecosystem.

Bilt Neighborhood OS is designed to help businesses reach customers and build loyalty through neighborhood-level experiences. Through UWM, it links mortgage servicing with homeownership tasks and local commerce, while keeping independent brokers visible to borrowers after closing.

“The broker who helps you buy your home should be your first call when you’re ready to take the next step, whether that’s refinancing, accessing equity or buying again,” Ishbia said in a statement. “With Bilt Neighborhood OS, we’re making the servicing experience more valuable to homeowners and creating more reasons for them to stay connected to their broker between those major decisions.”

UWM already services hundreds of thousands of loans through Bilt, giving these homeowners a single place to manage their mortgage and access rewards. With Neighborhood OS added, UWM said the experience will extend into more day-to-day homeownership activities, from finding a local contractor to exploring neighborhood offers, or reconnecting with a broker about a home equity or refinance opportunity.

Bilt said it has delivered more than $1 billion in value to its 8 million members, who redeem rewards toward travel, housing payments, student loans, dining and other categories. The company also says it powers one in four U.S. rental homes, primarily through the Bilt Alliance of large owners and operators.
