Intel is being sued by shareholders who say it hid a PC sales slowdown before stock fell 29%

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

A proposed class action led by a Michigan pension fund says Intel was slow to disclose weaker PC sales and excess inventory

Intel $INTC is the target of a proposed class action by investors who allege the company kept quiet about the toll of weak PC demand before its shares dropped 28.6% in one trading day, according to Reuters.

The shareholders filed the complaint on Nov. 13 in federal court in San Jose, California. The City of Southfield Fire and Police Retirement System, a Michigan pension fund, is leading the case. The suit names Intel, CEO Lip-Bu Tan and CFO David Zinsner as defendants and seeks unspecified damages for investors who bought stock between July 23 and Oct. 29.

The plaintiffs say Intel waited too long to tell investors what softer PC sales and bloated inventories would mean for revenue. They say those troubles more than offset the lift Intel was getting from the AI boom and the buildout of data centers. According to TechSpot, the complaint calls Intel’s statements “materially false and/or misleading” and says they implied demand was improving.

The shareholders also say Intel had an incentive to conceal the problems because, in mid-August, halfway through its third quarter, it raised $23 billion through a stock sale priced at $95 per share. Intel had priced an upsized $20 billion stock offering at $95 per share that month, with underwriters holding an option to buy additional shares.

Intel forecast fourth-quarter revenue of $15 billion to $16 billion on Oct. 29 and projected a slowdown in PC sales that it expects to last at least through the first half of next year. Intel stock tumbled 28.6% a day later, cutting roughly $140 billion from its market capitalization.

Tan told analysts that shortages in manufacturing supply, along with an "unprecedented memory crunch" and the PC softness that followed, left him disappointed, according to Reuters.

Intel stock closed Friday at $59.66. It has gained 62% so far this year, measured through Friday, but is well off its June 30 high of $142.35.

Intel had no immediate response to requests for comment on Sunday, according to Reuters.
