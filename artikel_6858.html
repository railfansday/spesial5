Jenna Dewan Reveals Her and Val’s Routines for ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Finals Before Surprise Elimination

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy were eliminated from Dancing With the Stars during Grammy Night—a shocking turn of events that Jenna thinks “online noise” contributed to. While they’re no longer in the competition, apparently the couple had big plans for “really great numbers” during the semifinals and finals, which Jenna spilled to E! News.

“We were starting next week's routine, which was going to be a really beautiful foxtrot, which I was really excited about, and a salsa,” Jenna said, adding that it “was going to be fierce and absolutely insane.” Oh, and apparently she and Val were also already planning a freestyle for the finale, which “was obviously going to be full out and fun.”

So, will any of these routines see the light of day? Never say never! Jenna reminded everyone that Mark Ballas and Whitney Leavitt shared their freestyle after being eliminated last season, saying “What was beautiful about Whitney and Mark is they really paved the way, I think....Listen, I never say never. Maybe we can put it out there into the world in our own way, you never know.”

Reminder that Jenna spoke about her and Val’s surprising elimination to Extra after last week’s show, and said “I know it’s hard to know how this all plays into it. I know this was a particularly tough week for a lot of the online noise and all stuff that comes with the show. I don’t know, I’ll never know how, or if, any of that plays into this. But I know that we went out there and had the best time of our life.”
