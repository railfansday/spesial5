KFC Franchisee Files For Bankruptcy With 84 Locations At Risk Of Closure

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Colonel could use some good news right about now.

The future of 84 KFC and KFC/Taco Bell restaurants across 10 states is uncertain after one of the chain’s franchisees filed for bankruptcy. The operator, Tasty Chick’n, filed for Chapter 11 protection in Texas on November 16.

The company entered into franchise agreements in 2021 with KFC and Taco Bell, both owned by Yum! Brands. Tasty Chick’n’s portfolio includes 75 KFC restaurants and nine co-branded locations across Tennessee, Florida, Virginia, Arkansas, Kentucky, Illinois, Missouri, Alabama, Mississippi, and Indiana.

In its bankruptcy filing, Tasty Chick’n notes that sales have declined by 15.6 percent over the past three fiscal years while operating costs have skyrocketed. So far in 2026, restaurant-level profit margins have dropped to just 4 percent, down from 11 percent in 2023. And under its franchise agreements, nearly half of the franchisees’ locations require substantial and expensive upgrades through 2029.

Tasty Chick’n began deferring royalty payments in mid-2025 and generally stopped paying them altogether that November. This past September, KFC and Taco Bell’s franchising entities issued a notice of intent to terminate their agreements, claiming the operator owed nearly $16 million in unpaid royalties and other fees. Tasty Chick’n also faces approximately 14 lawsuits from landlords in five states over unpaid rent and other charges. The bankruptcy filing revealed that the operator has accrued nearly $73 million in funded debt.

The fate of the restaurants remains undecided. Tasty Chick’n is planning a court-supervised sale of its portfolio, either in its entirety or divided into different regional markets. Whether buyers will bite is another story, especially given KFC’s overall trajectory. A report earlier this year revealed that KFC had shuttered more than 300 locations between July 2025 and July 2026 alone.

No matter what happens to these KFC locations, this bankruptcy is another reminder of just how tough 2026 has been for fast food operators.
