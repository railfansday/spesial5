We’re firm believers that a party isn’t complete without some kind of floral arrangement, be it real or faux. Whether you’re hosting a dinner party and want a centerpiece, or a scattering of buds in vases, or you’re throwing a bigger event and need florals to fill empty corners, they will bring your space to life. That said, decorating with florals can be expensive, plus they die in a matter of days. So what’s the alternative? This season, we highly suggest getting the famous LEGO dried flower centerpiece. It will never die on you, and you can reuse it year after year!

You may remember that this initially went viral at Costco a year or two ago and that it’s also available on Amazon, though the set tends to sell out quickly. Right now, as we write this, there are only 12 left in stock! As far as LEGO sets go, this is one of the chicer options, and the brand's entire botanical line is actually rather elegant, especially if you're looking for unique faux greenery to fill your space. Plus, LEGO botanical sets make great gifts for anyone who struggles to keep real plants alive.

This Thanksgiving set is designed for ages 18 and up, and comes with 812 pieces, so it should only take a few hours to assemble—and if you build it right now, you can enjoy it for the whole season, and keep it out for your Thanksgiving feast. Our very own shopping editor Janae McKenzie took a stab at it and admits that it is quite fun.

Once built, it can be used year after year to add festive elegance to your dinner table, mantel—or even hung on a wall. Featuring a giant gerbera and rose surrounded by additional foliage, LEGO has really captured the look of dried flowers in brick form.

If you've been eyeing this set for a while, take it from me, a LEGO collector: This is the year to get it. Not only is it marked down, but LEGO has also retired this product, which means that once existing stock is sold, it will be much harder to get your hands on it.
