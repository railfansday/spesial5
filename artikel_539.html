Dont worry, Artificials unsettling AI poster was actually made by a human

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

NEON has released a new poster for Luca Guadagnino's upcoming OpenAI film Artificial, and the visual is peak uncanny valley.

The illustrated poster centers on Andrew Garfield as OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, who stands in a redwood forest overlooking San Francisco Bay. He holds out a hand, inviting us into an unknown future.

For anyone who's ever wanted to go hiking with Garfield, the image seems like a dream come true. But stare at the poster for more than one second, and your brain starts to reject everything that's in front of it. Why does that redwood have a face? Why is a grassy hill glitching into a tunnel? Why does Altman look like he's about to call me "Spider Monkey" and take me on a piggyback ride through the woods, Edward Cullen-style?

Most importantly: Why does everything carry the discomforting sheen of AI? Could NEON have missed the point of Artificial's cautionary tale entirely and made promotional materials using AI?

Don't panic. A line of text alongside the bottom right edge of the poster affirms that the artwork was made by humans. To back that affirmation up, NEON also released a video of the poster being digitally illustrated, tagging creative agency Gravillis Inc. and artist Mike Thompson.

Comments under NEON's initial post of the poster appreciated the clever AI-adjacent marketing tactic.

"Chefs kiss on the poster which was made by an artist satirizing the AI aesthetic we all know and (should) hate," one user wrote.

"Poster meant to look like AI, but still has the humanity of an artist, so it’s still pretty to look at," wrote another.

If you were still somehow on the fence about whether Artificial would be pro- or anti-AI, look no further than the poster's background, which shows the Golden Gate Bridge partially submerged. Whatever future Garfield's Altman wants to bring us into, it's clearly calamitous.
