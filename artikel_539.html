Audibles 99-cent deal is back — how to get 3 months for under $3 and a free $20 bonus

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Pretending you are going to plow through a towering stack of physical books this winter is a noble goal, but audiobooks are what actually get finished while you are commuting or running errands. If you want to stock up on audiobooks before winter travel kicks in, Audible is discounting its Standard membership to $0.99 per month for your first three months and adding a $20 promotional credit.

That saves you over 80% on the normal price, bringing your total to less than $3 for three full months. With the $20 credit included, you're getting way more value back than what you pay to sign up.

The Audible Standard plan works a little differently than the pricier Premium Plus tier. Normally running $8.99 per month, Standard lets you pick one audiobook every month from Audible’s massive catalog of bestsellers and brand-new releases. You also get unlimited streaming access to a wide library of bingeable podcasts, all playable on your phone, tablet, or web browser.

Your monthly audiobook selections remain in your library to enjoy for as long as your membership is active. The one detail to remember is that Standard does not roll over unused monthly selections. You will want to pick your title each month before your billing date refreshes, or that month’s pick evaporates.

The $20 promotional credit is credited to your Audible account within 48 hours of payment verification, followed by a confirmation email. It cannot be redeemed for cash or gifted to someone else, but it applies directly toward cash purchases of audio titles sold on Audible. Unlike your monthly Standard picks — which stay tied to an active subscription — any audiobooks you buy using this cash credit are yours to keep permanently, even after canceling.

The credit, however, does have an expiration date: you must spend it by Jan. 31, 2027, at 11:59 p.m. PT. You qualify as long as you don't currently have an active Audible account or trial, and haven't used more than two Audible promos in the last 14 months.

Once your three discounted months end, the plan automatically renews at the regular rate of $8.99 per month plus tax. You can cancel online at any time on your Account Details page before the fourth billing date to avoid regular charges.

Remember, changing your credit card on the final payment screen can accidentally invalidate the promo code. If the price jumps back to the normal price after you update your card info, customer service can help you reapply the $0.99 rate and credit it before you buy.

The offer runs through Dec. 28 at 11:59 p.m. PT.

Trade three loose dollar bills for three months of listening and pocket a free $20 bonus before time runs out.
