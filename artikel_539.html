Dutch Bros CEO: Gas Prices Are Impacting the Consumer

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Dutch Bros CEO Christine Barone says the coffee chain delivered more than 30% year-over-year growth in revenue and EBITDA in the third quarter. The company also recorded its 14th consecutive quarter of positive same-store sales and ninth consecutive quarter of positive traffic, while continuing to open stores; it plans to open 250 new locations in 2027. Barone credited beverage launches, including the returning cookie butter drink, and targeted offers through Dutch Bros’ rewards program. She spoke with Romaine Bostick on "The Close" on November 5, 2026. (Source: Bloomberg)
