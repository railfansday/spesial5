When is Nations League draw? Start time, confirmed pot numbers and TV channel for quarter-finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The 2026-27 Nations League draw for the quarter-finals takes place this week, as the business end of the competition comes into focus.

England are among the five teams to have qualified for the knockout phase, as things currently stand, with Thomas Tuchel’s side enjoying a near perfect group phase.

Their only defeat came against reigning world champions Spain on matchday one in September, though the Three Lions exacted revenge on Sunday night as they held on after being reduced to 10-men to seal a famous victory.

As a result, England topped Group A3 at Spain’s expense due to a superior goal difference and that means they are likely to avoid another European heavyweight in the quarter-finals.

Elsewhere, France sealed top spot in Group A1 with a 2-1 win over Turkey, while Belgium thumped Italy 4-1 to finish as runners-up.

In A2, Germany topped the table with a 5-0 thrashing of the Netherlands, who slipped to second on goal difference after such a swing.

Portugal’s position in Group A4 is still to be confirmed after they were humbled at home by Denmark in their first game since Cristiano Ronaldo announced his retirement from international football.

The Nations League draw for the 2026/27 quarter-finals will take place on Friday, November 20, following the conclusion of the group phase fixtures.

As well as the quarter-final draw, the promotion/relegation play-offs draw will also be conducted.

Coverage of the draw is due to start at 11am GMT, with the draw itself expected to begin shortly afterwards.

The Nations League draw will take place in Nyon, Switzerland.

TV channel: In the UK, the draw will be televised live UEFA TV.

Live stream: You can also stream the draw for free online via the UEFA YouTube page.

Live blog: You can follow the draw as it happens on the day through Standard Sport’s live blog.

The four group winners will face the four runners-up in a two-legged Nations League quarter-final.

The group winners will all be seeded and will have home advantage in their respective second leg ties. Teams from the same group cannot be paired together in the knockout stage, therefore England and Spain - for example - will be kept apart.

The four group winners will be seeded in Pot 1, with the four runners-up will be unseeded in Pot 2.

*Portugal are guaranteed a Nations League quarter-final spot, though can still finish first or second in Group A4.

The Nations League quarter-finals, as well as the League A/B and League B/C promotion/relegation play-offs, will be played in March 2027.
