Mile Oak FC hail young stars after they earn Sussex schools call-ups

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Harry Pagey, Abel Manton, Mayson Paulo, Jacob Onwe, Teddy Alwali and Themba Phiri have earned their places in the county squad, marking an outstanding achievement for the boys and an incredibly proud moment for the club.

Their selection is a testament to their hard work, dedication and commitment to improving as young footballers.
