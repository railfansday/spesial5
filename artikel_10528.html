Clash Royale recruits creator of AI cheating tool to help catch cheaters

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Clash Royale developer Supercell has recruited the creator of an advanced AI cheating tool to help crack down on cheating in the game.

AI cheating has become a growing controversy in Clash Royale, with increasingly sophisticated bots reportedly capable of outperforming skilled human players. Players highlighted claims that an AI-controlled account named ‘Clasher’ had reached the top of the game’s global leaderboard.

The situation prompted calls for Supercell to intervene, with the developer addressing the controversy on November 20. In a statement posted on X, Clash Royale warned that using AI or other tools to cheat was a “royal crime,” declaring that offending players would be banished from the Arena.

On November 21, the official Clash Royale X account announced that it had caught the creator of an AI cheating tool and recruited them to help develop new anti-cheat technology.

“The creator of the AI cheating tool has been caught,” Supercell announced in a statement framed as a royal decree from the King. “He’s agreed, entirely of his own free will, to join forces with the Clash Royale team.

“And who better to help us catch cheaters than the person who built the most advanced cheating tool we’ve seen in the Arena? Together, we’re building new technology to outlaw this behaviour,” the statement concluded.

Supercell has not publicly identified the individual nor revealed if they were behind the ‘Clasher’ account. Supercell’s existing fair-play measures already combine automated detection with human moderation, including regular checks of leaderboards and competitive players.

The newly announced collaboration suggests the company is looking to strengthen those systems against increasingly sophisticated AI cheating tools.
