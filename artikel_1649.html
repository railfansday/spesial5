Andoni Iraola confirms it, superstar is coming back to Liverpool in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Andoni Iraola's words will be music to the ears of Liverpool supporters across the world.

The Spaniard has become an endearing figure at Anfield. Iraola may not have the alluring charisma of Jurgen Klopp but there is a warmness and charming quality about him that makes him a lot more appealing than the often cold and methodical Arne Slot.

Iraola has some wit. He smiles, and he jokes with reporters. You can see that he also gets what being a member of Liverpool is about.

Even though he is now managing one of the biggest clubs in the world, Iraola has maintained his normal status. He's been going to local grassroots games and actually spending a lot of time in the city centre embracing the culture.

What Andoni Iraola needs to do nextNaturally, for any manager, while those aspects are important, the most imperative thing is what happens on the pitch. At Liverpool, we've seen encouraging signs that the club is heading towards a positive trajectory. Inheriting a messy squad, Iraola has navigated the challenges he's faced with skill. He's embraced the club's youth system and has already promoted some burgeoning talent into the first-team. We're still very early on in the season, but Trey Nyoni has played more minutes under Iraola than he has in two years under Slot. Lewis Koumas, who was discarded by Slot and banished to go out on loan is now a key member of the squad as well. In the coming years, Iraola will be expected to promote even more young talent. That is going to be the next step in his metamorphosis as coach.
