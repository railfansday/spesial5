UEFA Women's Europa Cup: Wolfsburg advance to quarter-final with 10-0 aggregate win over Feyenoord

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Three goals in the last twenty minutes secured a 3-0 second leg win for VfL Wolfsburg against Feyenoord in the UEFA Women's Europa Cup. The two-time Champions League winners won the Round of 16 tie with a 10-0 aggregate score.

The visitors were dominant from the first whistle, but the Vrouwen Eredivisie side held them scoreless this time until the 72nd minute. After a patient play from Stephan Lerch's side, Katharina Piljic opened the score with a beautiful long-range strike that caught Oliwia Szymczak by surprise in Feyenoord's goal.

Substitutes Thea Bjelde and Anny Kerim-Lindland combined for the second goal as the 19-year-old striker produced a decent finish after Bjelde's chipped assist. The tenth goal of the tie came three minutes before the end of regular time, with Kessya Bussy's effort after a quick one-two with Lena Lattwein.

Wolfsburg will face either Rosenborg or Brøndby in the quarter-final stage that will be played in February 2027. The two Scandinavian teams will meet tomorrow in Brøndby after finishing their first leg duel 1-1 a fortnight ago. The other six ties will also be decided on Wednesday, including Eintracht Frankfurt's tie against PSV.

Wolfsburg will face FC Bayern in a marquee Frauen-Bundesliga fixture on Saturday as they will hope to end the champions’ 50-game unbeaten streak. The teams are only separated by three points after nine matchdays.
