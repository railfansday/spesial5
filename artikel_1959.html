Manchester United injury news: Bruno Fernandes doubt for Brentford clash on Saturday

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Shumail Sajid writes about Manchester United for Read Man Utd and Crystal Palace for Read Crystal Palace, with occasional pieces on other Premier League clubs. He has also written for FanSided's Red Devils Dispatch, Yardbarker, Anfield Central and The Sports Side.

Manchester United could be without Bruno Fernandes for their Premier League meeting with Brentford after the captain missed Friday’s training session at Carrington through illness.

Bruno Fernandes informed Mauricio Pochettino and the medical staff that he was feeling under the weather, prompting the club to leave him out of the final training session as a precaution, as per ESPN.

The midfielder’s availability will be a late call ahead of Saturday’s game at Old Trafford, with Mauricio Pochettino still looking for his first win at United.

Bruno Fernandes has been a major game changer for Manchester United since his arrival at the club. Despite a slow start on the current season and off-field issues, including contract renewal taking the news, Pochettino will be hopeful of Bruno getting back in form.

Fernandes has been one of United’s most regularly available players since arriving from Sporting in January 2020 and has missed just six games during his time at the club. His potential absence would be a major concern for Pochettino.

The 32-year-old was in action against Sporting CP on Wednesday, scoring twice in a 2-2 draw. United returned to Manchester on Thursday afternoon before holding their only full training session on Friday morning ahead of the Brentford game on Saturday.

There is better news regarding Harry Maguire, who is expected to be available despite picking up an injury against Sporting.

The centre-back reportedly felt discomfort during the match and there were initial concerns over his fitness for the weekend. However, Manchester United remain hopeful that he will recover in time to feature against Brentford.

Maguire’s availability will be a huge boost for Pochettino as United continue to leak goals. His chances to feature in the game are even more important, particularly with uncertainty surrounding Fernandes and Pochettino needing leaders in his lineup.

Manchester United are reluctant to take unnecessary risks with one of their most important players, especially with the midfielder having played through a busy run of fixtures. Pochettino will have to wait before knowing whether Fernandes can lead his side out against Brentford.
