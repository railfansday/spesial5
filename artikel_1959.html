Oliver Glasner has plan to replicate 'unpleasant' Crystal Palace move at Nottingham Forest - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Nottingham Forest boss Oliver Glasner will attempt to turn the City Ground into a fortress in a similar way to what he did when he was at Crystal Palace.

That is the opinion of experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

Selhurst Park became a difficult place to visit when the Austrian was in charge, with the Eagles attempting to create an intimidating atmosphere for their opponents.

Before Saturday, Glasner had failed to oversee even a single goal scored at the City Ground this season, with the pressure mounting on him at Nottingham Forest.

But his team looked transformed as they beat Manchester City 3-1, taking full advantage of the hostile atmosphere to crush Enzo Maresca's team from the first whistle.

Nottingham Forest and Glasner will hope to keep the momentum building after the international break, with Chelsea the next team set to visit the City Ground.

It was clear from the first whistle that Nottingham Forest were determined to get into Man City's faces.

Centre-back Jair Cunha went as far as to press Josko Gvardiol mid-way into the Man City half, winning the ball back and opening the score when he finished well past Gianluigi Donnarumma.

"The way the game started, you had to worry. The first 15 minutes, they were flying, Nottingham Forest," King told Football Insider.

"They were putting in tackles, and they were hurting people. Closing down quick. Forest looked a bit like Crystal Palace when they played at home under Glasner.

"Selhurst Park was an unpleasant place to go to for teams, and the City Ground could certainly be the same, if he gets them going like they did on Saturday."

On paper, the City Ground should be one of the most difficult places to travel to in the Premier League.

Nottingham Forest's ground is almost always loud and very enclosed, with little space in between the pitch and the fans.

Under Nuno Espirito Santo it lived up to that tag, with his team losing just five of 19 games played there in the 2024-25 Premier League season.

Last season, the City Ground saw just four Premier League wins at home - that number must be addressed by Glasner, and Saturday's game was a good start.
