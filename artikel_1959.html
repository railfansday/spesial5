Transfer news LIVE: Arsenal plot double deal; Man Utd scout Bellingham; Chelsea, Liverpool latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Arsenal, Chelsea, Manchester United, Liverpool and Tottenham are stepping up their preparations for the January transfer window, with less than two months until it opens. Arsenal are expected to target a striker after Viktor Gyokeres and Kai Havertz struggled for consistency, with Bournemouth forward Eli Junior Kroupi among their leading options. Real Madrid’s Endrick and Atletico Madrid’s Julian Alvarez have also been linked with moves to north London.

Manchester United are sticking with Michael Carrick for now, but his future remains uncertain ahead of matches against Roma and Aston Villa. The club are expected to pursue reinforcements regardless, with former Brentford striker Ivan Toney linked with a move to Old Trafford and Everton defender Jarrad Branthwaite reportedly on their radar. Chelsea, meanwhile, are making progress towards a potential deal for Bournemouth midfielder Alex Scott, while their interest in Lamine Camara has cooled, leaving Liverpool in contention.

Tottenham are also monitoring Scott and Kroupi as they look to strengthen their squad. A new striker is expected to be among their priorities when the window opens, with several Premier League rivals already assessing their options ahead of a busy January. Follow all the latest transfer news, rumours and gossip live below!

Sunderland have announced that Brian Brobbey has signed a new long-term contract.

Pascal De Maesschalck has admitted he was surprised to be appointed Arsenal's academy director.

The Belgian began work at Arsenal in the summer, arriving from Strasbourg where he had been technical director since 2024.

De Maesschalck oversees the Gunners' academy programme, taking over from Per Mertesacker after he left at the end of last season having been in the role for eight years.

In an interview with Sporza, De Maesschalck revealed he viewed the interview process with Arsenal as a good opportunity in itself, before he ended up then getting the job.

Scott Parker is set to succeed Nuno Espirito Santo as West Ham head coach.

Nuno was relieved of his duties with immediate effect on Tuesday morning following back-to-back home defeats to Southampton and West Brom.

The Irons are four points off the automatic promotion places and have won six of their opening 13 Championship games this season.

West Ham invested heavily over the summer, breaking the Championship transfer record to sign midfielder Arne Engels from Celtic for £22million, and were among the favourites for promotion back to the Premier League.

Now, though, after a mixed start to the season, they have decided to part ways with Nuno and have landed on club legend Parker to take the reins as they look to get their promotion bid back on track.

Well, West Ham are not wasting any time in finding a replacement for Nuno Espirito Santo.

The Hammers announced this morning that they had parted company with Nuno and now the Athletic claim an agreement has been struck with former midfielder with Scott Parker.

There is hope Parker will be in appointed in time to take charge for Wednesday’s match against Cardiff.

Chelsea and Liverpool have both made approaches to sign Brentford left-back El Hadji Malick Diouf ahead of next summer’s transfer window.

The 21-year-old Senegal international only joined the Bees towards the end of the summer window but has already attracted interest from two Premier League rivals.

According to D Sports, both clubs have contacted Diouf’s agent to register their interest. The defender is under contract until 2031.

No decision has been made over his future, but a potential departure could develop sooner than expected.

West Ham have sacked Nuno Espirito Santo as head coach.

A statement reads: “West Ham United can confirm that men’s first-team Head Coach Nuno Espirito Santo has left his position with immediate effect.

“Following a thorough review of the start to the EFL Championship season, the Board of Directors believe that a change is necessary at this time in order to ensure that the Club’s objectives and expectations remain on track.

“Assistant Head Coach Rui Pedro Silva and Goalkeeper Coach Rui Barbosa have also departed.

“West Ham United would like to thank Nuno, Rui and Rui for their hard work and commitment during their time at the Club, and wish them all the very best for the future.

“The process of recruiting a new Head Coach is already underway.”

Atletico Madrid have reportedly reached an agreement in principle on personal terms with Manchester United defender Lisandro Martinez.

The 28-year-old is said to be keen on joining Diego Simeone’s side, with his current contract due to expire in 2027.

According to North Bank Insider, several meetings have taken place in Madrid and discussions between the player's representatives and Atletico are advanced.

However, the Spanish club have yet to make official contact with United. A January move or a departure next summer remain possible.

Manchester United sent scouts to watch four Borussia Dortmund players during Saturday’s 2-2 draw with Bayern Munich as they assess potential transfer targets.

Serhou Guirassy, Jobe Bellingham, Felix Nmecha and Daniel Svensson are all reportedly on their radar.

According to TEAMtalk, Guirassy, Bellingham and Nmecha have been monitored throughout the season, although deals for the trio would be difficult due to their high valuations.

Svensson has also emerged as an option to strengthen United’s left-back position, but a move for the 24-year-old is considered more likely next summer than in January.

Victor Ozhianvuna has bid an emotional farewell to Shamrock Rovers ahead of his move to Arsenal.

The highly-rated midfielder will head to north London after turning 18 in January, following a deal that was first agreed in October 2025.

Ozhianvuna is viewed as one of the brightest young talents in Irish football and has already made 37 senior appearances for reigning League of Ireland Premier Division champions Shamrock Rovers.

He also became the second-youngest player ever to feature in the UEFA Conference League last year.

His performances quickly caught the attention of Arsenal, who will add the Republic of Ireland Under-17 international to their academy ranks in the New Year.

Arsenal have Eli Junior Kroupi and Kenan Yildiz firmly on their transfer radar ahead of the January window, new reports claim.

Bournemouth forward Kroupi was understood to have been a prominent target for the Gunners during the summer, only to suffer a foot fracture during pre-season that required surgery.

The France Under-21 international was sidelined for three months, halting his significant momentum after a fantastic debut season in English football following a move from Lorient in which he netted 13 goals in 35 appearances across all competitions.
