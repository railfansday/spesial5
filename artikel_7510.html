Databricks: Lakebase Sales Specialist, Nordics

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Databricks is seeking a Lakebase Sales Specialist to help customers modernise their operational data foundation with Databricks Lakebase, our fully-managed Postgres offering for intelligent applications.
