Video: How Ramos started and finished Milan’s second-goal move

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Gonçalo Ramos scored Milan’s second goal against Frosinone in Serie A on Sunday: a video shows how the Portuguese forward sparked the Rossoneri’s attack from deep in his own half before finishing at the other end of the pitch.

Milan striker Gonçalo Ramos scored his first goal in his last five Serie A matches on Sunday, adding his name to the scoresheet in a commanding 5-1 win against Frosinone at San Siro.

A video Milan shared on X shows Ramos making the first and final touch in the build-up to their second goal against the Ciociari.

The ex-PSG striker recovered possession near Milan’s defensive box and made the run forward, while Diego Moreira and Adrien Rabiot sparked the attack on the right flank.

Rabiot’s final pass found Ramos alone inside the box, allowing the Portuguese to score his third Serie A goal this season.

Ramos has also provided two assists in 13 league games so far.
