Martin O'Neill praises Colombian goal machine Camilo Duran as he saves the day for Celtic... yet again

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

By JOHN MCGARRY, SCOTTISH CHIEF SPORTS FEATURE WRITER

Published: 17:56 EST, 26 November 2026 | Updated: 18:04 EST, 26 November 2026

Martin O’Neill hailed ‘magnificent’ Camilo Duran for helping Celtic win ugly against Torreense to keep their Europa League dream alive.

The Parkhead men looked to be in for a rare comfortable night on the road when Torreense defender Javi Vazquez scored an own goal after nine minutes.

But sloppy defending by the visitors allowed the Portuguese to level 10 minutes later through Dany Jean.

With the minnows sitting in and hoping for a point late on, though, veteran James Forrest climbed off the bench to set up fellow sub Duran for his 15th goal of the season.

On the late impact of the Colombian, O’Neill said: ‘I think you always have to keep the faith. But we were gambling almost everything. They were camped outside their penalty area and we were hoping at some stage or another somebody might come up with something.

‘And of course, once again, Duran has done it. He is just a magnificent footballer.

Celtic manager Martin O'Neill praised Camilo Duran for once again riding to the rescue

‘Fifteen goals and we’re not into December yet. He’s been flying, he’s a terrific player, there’s no question about it.

‘That’s two games he’s come up big for us as well (on back of Besiktas). Maybe 22 games, in all honesty. But he’s been great. He didn’t start the game although we knew he could come on.’

Although delighted with the result, O’Neill was the first to admit that the performance was not all it might have been.

‘Despite the fact that we’d plenty of possession, I thought that we should just accelerate once or twice, not only just into their half, but also be more penetrative,’ he added.

‘And I think that was the point. I thought we had a lot of ball in the second half as well.

‘There’s always a possibility on the break that you could concede but we had to take the chance because the three points tonight were of vital importance.

‘But, yeah, there’s lots that we can still do to improve our game.’

Duran's downward header sealed the points for Celtic and ignited their qualification hopes

The dramatic outcome ensured Celtic are now up to 25th place in the 36-team group – from 32nd – after losing their opening three matches.

O’Neill’s men next face Omonia with Marseille and Union Saint-Gilloise to come in January.

‘I’m just delighted to have won — it keeps us going,’ added O’Neill. ‘We’ve got six points on the board and we’ve still got a couple of games to go.

‘So, from an absolutely almost impossible position, having lost the first three games, we’ve given ourselves some hope.’

Celtic’s star man on the night, Jota set up the opening goal and relished the return to his homeland almost two years after his last European match.

‘It’s been a long time that I wanted to come back to Portugal and play in front of my friends and family,’ he said. ‘And there’s no better way than with a win with three points. It was something that we wanted to achieve today and luckily we did it.

‘It was a long road and I did my maximum to be as fit as possible. And right now it’s all about enjoying. There’s no expectation — just to be a professional, having the responsibility of being a Celtic player and delivering every day.’
