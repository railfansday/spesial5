Zielinski at risk of missing Inter vs. VfB Stuttgart Champions League clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Inter are preparing to take on Bundesliga side VfB Stuttgart in the Champions League on Wednesday night, but the Nerazzurri may have to go without midfielder Piotr Zielinski, who did not train with the team on Tuesday morning.

Inter have won two and lost two of their first four league phase matches in this season’s Champions League, which is enough for 15th place in the table ahead of the fifth round of fixtures taking place this week.

Up next for the Nerazzurri is a visit from Stuttgart, who are one point and one position above them in the standings ahead of Tuesday night’s matches.

Inter trained in Milan on Tuesday morning ahead of their visit from the German side, and according to the latest reports from Gianluca Di Marzio, Poland international Zielinski was unable to train with the rest of his teammates and was left to work individually at Appiano Gentile.

Zielinski had also missed Inter’s 4-4 draw away against Atalanta in Serie A on Sunday evening due to a problem with the arch of his foot. He is now at risk of missing out on the Stuttgart clash as well.

Inter are also waiting to hear back on the results of medical tests for Hakan Calhanoglu, who was substituted off at half-time of the Atalanta match with a thigh problem over the weekend. He is not expected to be back in time to face Stuttgart and is likely to miss the Genoa match in Serie A this coming weekend as well.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
