Claude Can Now Track Every Attendee And Booth At Big Conferences, RainFocus Says

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Posted on Nov 12 • Originally published at fathom.news

RainFocus has launched a connector that lets Anthropic's Claude look up attendees, check room capacity and track booths live at conferences run on its platform, with no spreadsheet exports needed.

RainFocus announced the connector on Tuesday for large conferences, trade shows and field events. The Utah company sells an event marketing platform.

RainFocus says event data usually reaches sales and marketing teams slowly, because someone has to export it by hand. With the connector, Claude can search attendee profiles by name, email or company, query session schedules and speaker lists, check room capacity, follow check-in status and review exhibitor and booth records during the event.

The tools span 17 areas, including registration, sessions and content, meetings and one-on-one scheduling, and exhibitors and booths.

Reading data is the default, but any action that changes data has to be switched on by a RainFocus admin, one tool at a time. That way Claude only gets the access each team needs.

"With the Anthropic Connector, Claude becomes an extension of those teams we serve," said RainFocus CEO JR Sherman, adding that event managers and revenue leaders can ask questions and act in the moment without waiting for manual exports.
