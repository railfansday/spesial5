Amazon’s Black Friday MacBook Deals Are Pretty Impressive

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Considering the price of RAM, we’ll gladly take $500 off a new MacBook.

If there’s one Apple product line where the recent price hikes hit the hardest, it’s the MacBook. Rising RAM and storage costs have pushed laptop prices up across the board, which makes scoring a new MacBook on sale all the more mission critical.

As luck would have it, Black Friday deals on Apple tech have landed on Amazon, with up to 20 percent off MacBooks—including the brand-new M6 MacBook Pros on sale for the first time ever. Given that these prices have reached record highs lately, these discounts are certainly nothing to sneeze at. In fact, now that the MacBook pricing status quo has been reset, we’d say this is probably as low as MacBook prices will get these days.

Of course, not all MacBooks were created equal. While the standard MacBook Air is great for most, those who are planning on running hardcore software might benefit from a beefed-up MacBook Pro. And if budget is your primary concern, we’re of the opinion that the MacBook Neo is a worthy buy, even if it’s only $50 off right now.

The best MacBook Air deal you’ll find right now is this $300 discount on the 15-inch model. With this laptop, you’ll get Apple’s M5 chip in a fanless, 3.3-pound frame that handles heavy multitasking and light video editing without breaking a sweat. Base storage doubled to 512 GB this year—a great upgrade for storing more apps, photos, and files—and the 15.3-inch Liquid Retina display is sharp and vivid. You also get a 12 MP Center Stage camera that’s great on video calls, Wi-Fi 7 for fast downloads, and up to 18 hours of battery, enough for a busy workday and then some.

If you’d prefer a smaller laptop, 13-inch models are up to 19 percent off. They’ve got everything the 15-inch models have, just in a more portable package. The rule of thumb for choosing between a Pro and an Air? The slimmer Air won’t replace a MacBook Pro for serious creative work or gaming, but for school, business, and general use, it’s the one to get.

Given that Apple just dropped a new slate of M6 MacBook Pros, in addition to the company’s first-ever touchscreen laptop, we were anticipating discounts on the old range of M5 models to clear out inventory. Sure, the best MacBook Pro deal you’ll find right now is $250 off the 14-inch M5 model—but, if you don’t mind paying just $50 more, you can scoop up the shiny new M6 on sale, complete with improved speed and boosted AI capabilities. It’s extremely rare for Apple to discount its latest laptop so soon after release, and even more rare for it to be $200 off.

Both the M5 and M6 models feature Liquid Retina XDR displays, up to 24 hours of battery, three Thunderbolt 4 ports, HDMI, an SD card slot, and up to 2 TB of storage.

Why go Pro over the Air? The GPU is fast, which makes a difference when editing video, working with high-res photos, or gaming, and the XDR screen is significantly brighter. If your daily workload has started becoming too much for the Air to handle, this is where you should look next. If you want the biggest, most powerful model possible, souped-up MacBook Pros with more RAM are up to $500 off.

The MacBook Neo is Apple’s most affordable laptop, even with the recent price hike from $599 to $699. Right now, the Neo is $50 off, sitting smack in the middle between its debut price and post-increase price, at $649—making it far and away the cheapest MacBook.

The Neo is an easy recommendation for anyone looking for their first Mac. It runs on Apple’s A18 Pro chip and is surprisingly quick for browsing, sending email, and streaming video. The 13-inch Liquid Retina display looks great for the price, and the battery lasts a solid 16 hours. It also comes in four fun colors, with the yellow-green citrus being a fan favorite.

There are some trade-offs with going with Apple’s budget model, including storage that starts at 256 GB, just 8 GB of RAM, and no keyboard backlight. You also get only two USB-C ports, so getting a USB-C hub is a must if you plan to connect a lot of accessories. Spending an extra $100 gets you a Neo with 512 GB of storage and a Touch ID sensor for quick unlocking. For students or anyone who mostly sends emails and browses the Web, the Neo is hard to beat.
