Mission Over Metrics: Rethinking the Shopper Journey with Liz Roche

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Retail media is no longer just about putting products in front of shoppers and measuring the resulting sales.

As the retail landscape becomes increasingly complex, brands need to rethink how they connect with consumers across the entire shopper journey.

The opportunity lies in building connected campaigns that bring together shopper insights, creative strategy, and coordinated experiences across digital, social, and in-store touchpoints.

In this episode of Adspeak by ADWEEK, host Zoë Ruderman sits down with Liz Roche, VP of media, measurement, and in-store solutions at Albertsons Media Collective, to explore how brands and retailers can move beyond traditional retail media strategies to create more meaningful shopper connections.

They discuss why understanding shopper missions and need states matters more than simply showcasing products, and why shopper data should inform creative development from the very beginning of a campaign.

The conversation examines what makes a genuinely connected campaign. Rather than repeating the same message across multiple channels, brands need to understand where shoppers are in their journey and build experiences that reflect their immediate needs.

Liz explains how seemingly ordinary purchase signals can reveal valuable shopper insights, helping marketers understand the motivations behind buying behavior and make smarter decisions about creative, SKU selection, and promotional strategy.

They also explore the importance of moving beyond vanity metrics like return on ad spend. By combining incrementality, category insights, and brand performance, marketers can develop more meaningful measurement frameworks that demonstrate the true impact of retail media.

The discussion also looks at the untapped potential of the physical store as a media canvas, from in-store screens to audio experiences that can extend brand storytelling beyond traditional advertising.

Liz Roche is the VP of Media, Measurement & In-Store Solutions at Albertsons Media Collective. A seasoned executive, she specializes in first-party data strategy, media measurement, monetization, commercialization, and go-to-market strategy.

With leadership experience spanning platforms, agencies, and publishers, Liz drives business transformation through innovation, strategic partnerships, and collaboration. She holds an Executive MBA from the Kellogg School of Management, where she was honored as the commencement speaker in June 2024.
