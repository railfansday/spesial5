That disallowed Haaland goal: According to Ifab Law 12 “A goalkeeper is considered to be in control of the ball with their hand(s)/arm(s) when: the ball is between their hands/arms or between their hand(s)/arm(s) and any surface (eg ground, own body.”

Going by that and having seen several more replays, it’s this humble minute-by-minute reporter’s view that the decision to disallow Haaland’s effort was the correct one.
