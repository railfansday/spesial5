See Cardi B’s Out-of-This-World Birthday Party for Son Grey

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Cardi B celebrated her and Stefon Diggs' son Grey on his first birthday with an extravagant space-themed party. The rapper—also mom to her trio with ex Offset—dubbed the bash "Grey's World."

Cardi B’s universe revolves around her kids.

And the "Bodak Yellow" rapper made that clear as she threw an over-the-top party for her youngest, Grey—her son with Stefon Diggs—to celebrate his first birthday.

"Y'all don't even understand. This look crazy in person," Cardi said while exploring the space-themed set-up in footage shared to Instagram Nov. 15, before adding "Your world, Grey!"

In addition, Cardi—also mom to kids Kulture, 8, Wave, 5 and Blossom, 2, with ex Offset—completed the lavish affair with a sushi bar and live music for her entire family to dance to. As such, the Grammy winner's sister, Hennessy Almánzar, shared the dance floor with a Transformer robot, while Cardi and the birthday boy shared a sweet moment set to salsa music.

The festivities followed the 34-year-old's birthday tribute to her son with the NFL star, in which she revealed his name after previously keeping it under wraps.

"Happy 1st birthday to Grey Marsean Diggs!!" she wrote on Instagram Nov. 4. "It’s only been 1 year since meeting you but it feels like I’ve had you at my side forever."

And the moniker is particularly meaningful for Cardi, as it reflects the journey of bringing her fourth-born into this world.

"I carried you through one of the most uncertain high pressured times of my life but having you inside of me through it all gave me a extra strength," she shared, adding, "You gave a new meaning to what a Grey day feels like. You light up my life and every room with your smile your laugh and your precious spirit."

The sweet message also showed off the toddler's clever Halloween costume, having scrubbed in as an adorable doctor from Grey's Anatomy.

And while Stefon's role at the party was kept behind the scenes, Cardi previously shared how the New England Patriot was a key part of their growing family's success.

"I'm doing all this work, but I'm doing all this work while I'm creating a baby," a then-pregnant Cardi explained on CBS Mornings, in 2025. "Me and my man, we're very supportive of each other."
