Taylor and Lewis suffer five straight defeats on Modus Super Series return

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Phil Taylor came out of retirement to partner Adrian Lewis for England at Thursday's Modus Super Series International Pairs Tournament in Portsmouth, but the duo endured a disappointing return, losing all five of their matches; Taylor retired from professional darts in 2018

Phil 'The Power' Taylor and Adrian Lewis suffered five losses from five matches for England at Thursday's Modus Super Series International Pairs Tournament in Portsmouth.

Taylor, now 66, retired from the competitive circuit in 2018, five years after claiming his 16th and final world title. However, the 16-time world champion returned to action to partner his long-time friend Lewis for the tournament.

Their opening match on Thursday saw them face Scotland's John Henderson and Robert Thornton, with the England pair suffering a narrow 4-3 defeat.

Things failed to improve in their second outing, as Taylor and Lewis were beaten 4-2 by Sweden's Andreas Harrysson and Anton Ostlund.

A shock 4-3 defeat to unheralded Swiss duo Ansh Sood and Marcel Walpen followed, before Taylor and Lewis suffered a fourth consecutive loss, going down 4-1 to Germany's Daniel Klose and Liam Maendl-Lawrance.

Their fifth and final group-stage outing saw England take a 2-1 lead against Denmark, the only other side in their pool yet to record a victory. However, Ivan Springborg Poulsen and Andreas Toft Jorgensen responded by winning the next three legs in succession to condemn the English pair to a 4-2 defeat and a fifth straight loss.

Taylor revealed in September that the opportunity to partner Lewis was enough to tempt him back to the oche, although his return is not expected to signal a permanent comeback.

The 16-time world champion, who recently underwent successful hip replacement surgery, previously enjoyed considerable success alongside Lewis, with the pair winning four World Cup of Darts titles together.

The Modus Super Series International Pairs Tournament continues in Portsmouth until November 28.
