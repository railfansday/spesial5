Roma in talks to renew Hermoso’s deal until 2029

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Roma is ready to secure Mario Hermoso, who has become a key figure in Gian Piero Gasperini’s squad.

After a difficult period that kept him away from the Giallorossi project for approximately six months, the Spanish defender has completely turned his situation around. In the summer of 2025, his stay in the capital seemed far from certain, with the real possibility of a separation.

Today, however, Hermoso is considered a key element in the Roma squad’s stability, so much so that he is close to reaching a new contract.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, a renewal could be reached as early as next week.

The agreed-upon deal would include a two-year contract with the option for a third season, a formula that would allow the centre-back to significantly extend his experience with Roma.

Gasperini has transformed a player considered on his way out into one of the team’s leaders, leveraging his experience and technical skills.
