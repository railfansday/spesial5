Conte convinced he will return to Juventus one day

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Antonio Conte believes "sooner of later the circle will close" on his time at Juventus as he hinted at a possible return to the Bianconeri in the future.

Conte is currently out of work after agreeing to terminate his contract with Napoli a year early following the 2025-26 season, when they finished second to champions Inter.

The 57-year-old led the Partenopei to their fourth Serie A title a year earlier, while it was only their second Scudetto in the post-Diego Maradona era.

His 62% win rate in Serie A with Napoli is the third highest of all Partenopei coaches, behind only Maurizio Sarri (69%) and Luciano Spalletti (68%).

Conte had been linked with a return to the Italy national team after their failure to reach a third successive World Cup, with Manchester United also seen as a possible destination before the Red Devils chose to appoint Mauricio Pochettino last week.

But Conte enjoyed most of his success, both as a player and as a head coach, with Juventus, where he spent a decade representing the club on the pitch.

He was appointed as Luigi Delneri's successor in May 2011 and went on to win three successive Serie A titles, along with the Supercoppa Italiana in 2012 and 2013.

Turin remains the only stop in his coaching career where he has lasted longer than two years, and he believes unfinished business will eventually draw him back.

"I think that sooner or later the circle will close," Conte confessed in an interview with Cronache di Spogliatoio, which was teased on Wednesday.

"I know I left something behind, and I know that eventually the circle will be closed with Juventus.

"There have been moments in the past where we could have reunited, but it's been difficult because two things have to align.

"I have to be available and Juventus have to be looking for a coach who isn't currently employed."

Conte explained that two recent vacancies in Turin slipped away because of timing, pushing him toward Serie A rivals Inter in 2019.

He also revealed he had been on the verge of becoming head coach of Juventus in 2009 just before leaving Bari.

But a disagreement over the use of Brazilian playmaker Diego saw the deal collapse, and he would go on to join Atalanta.

"There have been two instances. Once, when Juventus decided to hire Maurizio Sarri, I was available and later ended up at Inter," Conte added.

"Then there was a second time when I was free, just as Giuntoli arrived. Giuntoli decided to go for Motta and I went to Napoli.

"I went, walking in as the Juve manager and then we started talking. They asked me how I wanted to play, and I replied with a '4-2-4'. They said 'but where would you play Diego?'

"I answered 'you were the ones who signed him, even though I said that we already have four forwards. So I would play him alongside one of the strikers'.

"Then they told me 'well, Diego wants to play in a 4-3-1-2'. Imagine my anger at the time. Although, that anger shows that I'm a direct person who doesn't make compromises.

"Anyone else, literally anyone else, would have said 'sure, I'll even play him in goal if you want, just let me sign the contract and I’ll join Juve'.

"I stood firm in my ideas, firm in my convictions, and firm in the fact that I wasn't going to make any compromises or promise anything I knew I wouldn't deliver.

"So, I told them 'if a player decides how we play, then I’m not the right coach for you'. And that was it. I walked away and by the time I left that meeting."
