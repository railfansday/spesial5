Mauricio Pochettino explains his shock team selection in nightmare first Man Utd match

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Mauricio Pochettino may have left Gary Neville ‘absolutely stunned’ by picking Matthijs de Ligt against Liverpool, but the Manchester United manager says there was a simple reason for it.

The new Red Devils boss had a tough opening game in charge as he took his side to Anfield and it ended up being a nightmare debut for the Argentine.

Leny Yoro was shown a red card after eight minutes and Dominik Szoboszlai scored from the resulting free-kick.

Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz also netted to complete a comfortable 4-0 win for Andoni Iraola’s men.

Manchester United have now picked up just eight points from 11 games and sit 19th in the Premier League table. Every other club in the division has more wins than the one they have managed.

While the Red Devils clearly have many problems, De Ligt has been a focus of criticism after his performance at Anfield.

The Dutch defender has recovered from a long-term injury, but was starting in the Premier League for the first time in nearly a year, having made two Champions League appearances this season.

The 27-year-old looked a long way off Premier League pace as he was run ragged by the Liverpool front line.

Asked why he went for De Ligt alongside Yoro over Harry Maguire, who started on the bench but has been playing regularly this season, Pochettino said: ‘He was training, he played in Champions League before the international duty. I think, when we arrived we saw the combination.’

He then quickly turned his attention to claiming Liverpool defender Jeremy Jacquet could have given away a penalty for a challenge on Bryan Mbeumo before Yoro was dismissed.

‘I repeat again, if Jacquet was penalty and the referee say that it’s a penalty, for sure that action after never comes and maybe we’re talking another way,’ he said. ‘But one centre-back made a mistake, wasn’t punished. Us, we made one mistake and we were punished.

‘And now I accept all the situation, but the circumstances were the circumstances and it’s so important to put the circumstances in front, always.’

Gary Neville watched De Ligt play in the defeat to Como in October and was surprised that he was given the nod at Anfield after his performance in Italy.

‘When I saw Matthijs de Ligt’s name on the teamsheet, I have to be honest with you, I was absolutely stunned,’ Neville said on Sky Sports.

‘The reason I was stunned was because of this: if you watched him against Como four weeks ago, he was very much well off it.’

Neville added: ‘We saw it against Como and he’s been out for a year, trying to come back to this level of football. It’s really tough.

‘To have De Ligt out there in a game like this, in a back four, he’s been exposed inside the first ten minutes.’
