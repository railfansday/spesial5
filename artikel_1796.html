Christensen and Cubarsi injuries hand Barca boss Flick defensive headache

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Hansi Flick has been left with a defensive headache ahead of this weekend's clash with Villarreal, with both Andreas Christensen and Pau Cubarsi suffering from injuries.

Flick confirmed Christensen would miss the next two matches after sustaining a calf issue in training, having only just returned from an injury lay-off earlier in the month.

The Danish defender played 30 minutes of Barcelona's 2-1 win over Atletico Madrid before the international break, but his absence offers Flick a problem he must try and tackle.

Cubarsi is another player likely to miss out on Sunday's clash after he was injured during Spain's 3-2 defeat to England in the Nations League, with the 19-year-old replaced at half-time having given away a penalty on Harry Kane in the first half.

"Andreas was really in a good moment, so this is why the decision was made not to go with the national team so he could train here," Flick said of Christensen's injury.

"I think it was the right decision, but unfortunately he's out for maybe two games. He is really disappointed.

"For me, when fit, he's one of the best central defenders. We give him our support and also we have to help him to maybe improve a little physically."

When asked about Cubarsi's issue, Flick added: "It's not a big thing, but we have to take care. He could be on the bench [vs. Villarreal] and we will see if he travels to Baku.

"With Cuba, he played amazingly last season, but with the World Cup he had a lot of minutes, and he's only 19 years old. So, it's now about taking care of him."

It remains to be seen who Flick will deploy in his reshuffled back line, with Xavi Espart, who signed a new long-term contract earlier this week, also out with an injury.

Joules Kounde and Eric Garcia are, however, available again after recovering from knocks, though Flick does have a more unconventional solution in Frenkie de Jong.

De Jong impressed at right-back during Barcelona's win over Atletico, and the Blaugrana boss hinted the Netherlands international could be utilised there again.

"We will see," Flick added. "I am very happy with Frenkie. He felt something [with the national team] but it was a small injury and he is available."

Higher up the pitch, Dani Olmo is set for his first appearance since October after recovering from an injury, but Rodri remains out with a hamstring problem.

"We have to wait day by day, but I think it's a good moment," Flick said of Rodri's fitness. "We also take care about this because, last season with Manchester City, he had a similar injury.

"I spoke this morning with him, and it took a little bit longer than they expected, so now we have to take care. It's not the same injury, it's not the big injury like he had in the past, but I think we have to take care."

Despite being favoured in the pre-match simulations, Barcelona have lost two of their last five LaLiga matches against Villarreal (W3), twice as many defeats as they suffered in their previous 29 meetings with them in the competition (W22 D6 L1).

Indeed, after a run of 10 consecutive home wins against Villarreal in LaLiga between 2010 and 2020, Barcelona have lost three of their last five home games against them in the competition (W2).

Since the 2021-22 season, Barcelona have not suffered more home defeats in LaLiga against any other opponent than they have against Villarreal (P5 W2 D0 L3), also three against Real Madrid (P6 W3 D0 L3).

Barcelona have, however, earned 33 points from 12 games in the 2026-27 LaLiga season (W11 L1). Counting three points for a win, they have only accumulated more points at this stage of the competition in the 2012-13, 2013-14 and 2017-18 seasons (34 points – W11 D1), also earning 33 points in the 2024-25 season (W11 L1).

Flick's side have also outperformed their expected goals by more than any other LaLiga side this season (+8.4, with 48 goals from 39.6 xG), while Villarreal have underperformed theirs by the most (-7.4, with 17 goals from 24.4 xG).

And they face a Villarreal side that have picked up seven points from their last four LaLiga games (W2 D1 L1), just one point fewer than they managed in their first eight games (eight points – P8 W2 D2 L4).
