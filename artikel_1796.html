Despite enjoying a dominant start to proceedings when opening up the 2026/27 campaign, Enzo Maresca’s superstar squad have come crashing back down to earth in a remarkable fashion. Including a dramatic 3-1 drumming against Nottingham Forest prior to November’s fortnight interval, Maresca’s side have failed to muster a single clean sheet from any of their last nine straight matchups across all competitions.

Sustaining a knee injury last month, Manchester City will once again be without Ghanian international Antoine Semenyo here. Missing out against Fulham on Saturday lunchtime, Maresca’s camp are still sweating over the fitness of Matheus Nunes.

Despite enduring a frustrating afternoon at the Etihad on his return from November’s international break, attacking talisman Erling Haaland has registered five goals in his first three Champions League appearances and will lead the line. Settling for a spot on the bench over the weekend, record-breaking summer recruit Enzo Fernandez will be handed an immediate full recall.

As for the visitors, claiming an essential domestic boost over the weekend as they marked their return from November’s international break with a 1-0 win at home against Torino, Napoli have drastically failed to find any kind of fluidity this season. With the pressure still firmly on Max Allegri’s shoulders, the Azzurri have recorded just a trio of their previous nine appearances across all competitions. Coming under even more scrutiny earlier in the month as they slumped to a nightmare 4-1 Champions League defeat away at Porto, Napoli have also gained an unwanted reputation for their glaring issues on the road. Opening November with a 2-0 loss at the hands of historic rivals Juventus, Tuesday’s guests have lost four of their last five matchups away from the Stadio Diego Armando Maradona. Likewise, the former Serie A champions have mustered just a sole win outside of Campania since back on August 22nd.

Limping off after just 13 minutes against Torino on Saturday night with a suspected hamstring injury, Napoli will be without Italian midfielder Antonio Vergara here. Likewise, picking up his own respective muscle issue earlier in the month, club captain Giovanni Di Lorenzo remains on the sidelines.

Replacing Vergara in the opening exchanges on Saturday, former Manchester City icon Kevin De Bruyne should be handed an immediate full recall on his emotional return to the Etihad. Bagging what turned out to be the winner in Campania over the weekend, Scotland international Scott McTominay will play a leading role.

Putting in another concerning display over the weekend as they were eventually forced to settle for a 1-1 stalemate against Fulham, Manchester City are desperately searching for an essential boost here. Remarkably mustering just one-point from any of their previous four consecutive Premier League showdowns, Maresca’s superstar squad have also shown some rather glaring defensive struggles. However, winning their last three straight battles against Napoli by an aggregate score of 8-3, Man City will have plenty of expectations on their shoulders in midweek.
