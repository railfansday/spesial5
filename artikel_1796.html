Fabian Hurzeler issues Stefanos Tzimas injury update ahead of Brighton’s Conference League clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Stefanos Tzimas will not be available for Brighton against Craiova, despite making significant progress in his recovery from a serious knee injury.

Fabian Hurzeler confirmed the Greek striker is closing in on a return to competitive action but will first need to feature for Brighton's Under-21s.

"Yes [Craiova] is too soon,” said Hurzeler to Sussex World. “His first step will be a game with the Under-21s and then we'll go from there.

“But it's a joy to see him training and huge credit to him so far," the German added.

Tzimas has been out since last December, when he suffered an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury during Brighton's 4-3 defeat against Aston Villa. The 20-year-old has now spent almost a year on the sidelines.

Brighton signed Tzimas from German side FC Nurnberg in January 2025 for a reported £20.8m and has made 12 first-team appearances for the Seagulls, scoring three goals and providing two assists.

Brighton - who are second in the Premier League - face Newcastle at the Amex on Sunday but Hurzeler insists his full focus is on Craiova, who are top of the Romanian league.

“The next game is the most important game, and that's Craiova. That's what we focus on,” said Hurzeler.

“We also know that Craiova are a good team. They have seven points, like we do, and they're top of their league. They play some good football, so we have to give them credit and we can't underestimate them.

“Therefore, we are fully focused on tomorrow.”
