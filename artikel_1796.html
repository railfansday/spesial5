MN: Maignan and Pavlovic expected to return for Milan this weekend – the latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

AC Milan can breathe a sigh of relief, with Mike Maignan and Strahinja Pavlovic expected to be starters this weekend.

It’s been a week of nerves for Milan and Ruben Amorim, with questions about two of their most important players: Pavlovic and Maignan. Both have struggled with injury issues during the international break, so there have been concerns about their chances of starting. However, they seem likely to be on the field on Sunday.

According to MilanNews, there will be two welcome names in the XI this weekend with Maignan and Pavlovic expected to be in the team from the get-go. Both have been hit-and-miss this week, but it seems they’ll both be present at San Siro.

Sankhoun Diawara is also expected to be in the defence at left-back, with Mario Gila and Koni De Winter making up the rest of the back four. Then, in midfield, Luka Modric will start with Adrien Rabiot and Yunus Musah either side of him.

Amorim will hope that Christian Pulisic can play after his three goals in the international break, and he’s currently in the expected XI. Then, Diego Moreira and Goncalo Ramos are expected to partner him.

Expected Milan XI vs. Frosinone (4-3-3): Maignan; Gila, De Winter, Pavlovic, Diawara; Musah, Modric, Rabiot; Moreira, Ramos, Pulisic.
