8 Times Luffy Should Have Died in One Piece

Luffy is the main protagonist of One Piece, which means that he has a degree of plot armor that will take him through to the end of the series. However, there are times where this plot armor has gone a bit far, saving Luffy’s life when it probably shouldn’t have.

We all know that Luffy is a lucky kid. He has escaped death on more than one occasion. He could have had his head chopped off, or he could have drowned at this point in the story, but somebody has stepped in or something has happened to prevent him from dying.

Even when we thought he was down for the count, he was saved, and managed to turn the tide of battle in his favor. He is a force that simply cannot be stopped.

The earliest time that Luffy should have died in One Piece is when he was taken out to sea by Higuma. Out in the ocean, the Lord of the Coast comes around, and Luffy gets thrown in the water.

His journey should have ended there, especially since the Lord of the Coast was about to completely devour him. Instead, Shanks stepped in, drove the Lord of the Coast away, and lost his arm in the process.

This is where Luffy’s journey should have possibly ended. After all, he was a rather impulsive young kid, and he still is to this day. However, like every entry on this list, there has been something that has prevented him from meeting his end.

There are some times where Luffy gets saved by other people, and then there are times where divine intervention has to play a role. That is, unless you want to work with the Monkey D. Dragon weather Devil Fruit theory.

While visiting Loguetown, Luffy manages to get himself capture by Buggy and his clownish crew, and as a result, Luffy is placed on the execution platform. At this point, it looks like Luffy is going to die, and he even calls out to everyone to say that he is dead.

Of course, divine intervention plays a role, because Buggy’s sword is struck by lightning, causing the platform to fall apart, leaving Luffy unscathed. As we know, Dragon is in Loguetown during this time, and it is theorized that he has a Devil Fruit that can control the weather, so maybe he saved his kid.

Early in Luffy’s pirating journey, he almost died several times, but one of the most memorable moments is his first interaction with Crocodile in Alabasta. While out in the desert, Luffy gets skewered by the then warlord of the sea.

It’s a very sudden hit, as Luffy is telling Crocodile to stop at the time, but the screen goes red, and we see Luffy hanging on the end of Crocodile’s hook. Crocodile then tosses him down into a sand pit to die.

Despite struggling to escape, Luffy can’t get out of the sand, and he is soon completely buried. He would have died here, but Nico Robin stepped in and saved his life. Still, it’s another moment that Luffy should have bit the dust.

Set sail and discover One Piece's darker characters like Sir Crocodile, Kaido, and Doflamingo.

As the vice warden of Impel Down, it is Magellan’s job to keep the prison running smoothly. Of course, Luffy completely ruined this when he entered Impel Down, and ultimately chose to pick a fight with the vice warden.

At this point in the story, Luffy wasn’t very strong. Well, at least not when you compare him to many of the other characters in the story. Going up against Magellan, Luffy ended up being coated in poison and was on his deathbed.

He should have died during this part of the story, but he was ultimately saved by the absolute legend that is Bon Clay. Bon Clay managed to get Luffy to Ivankov, who ultimately saved Luffy with hormone therapy which shortened his lifespan by 10 years. Still, this should have meant the end for the main character.

During the Whole Cake Island arc, Luffy decided to go toe-to-toe with Katakuri - Big Mom’s second son, and a man with a bounty of over 1 billion Berries. His signature move was the use of his observation haki to completely dodge attacks with his mochi powers.

During this fight, Luffy took hits nearly nonstop. It seemed like in every episode he was getting beaten up and there was no end in sight. Then, during episode 867, Luffy got hit by Katakuri’s spear in what was a devastating blow.

Many don’t realize that the hit was worse in the manga, and it tore open Luffy’s side. This, and many of the other hits, like being buried alive in mochi, should have ended Luffy’s pirating career, but he muscled his way through thanks to his plot armor.

Every One Piece fan is familiar with all the Emperors, but how do they rank against each other based on strength? Well, here's your answer.

One of the most one-sided fights in the entirety of One Piece is when Luffy foolishly tries to step up to Kaido in the early chapters of the Wano Country arc. At this point, Luffy fully believed he was ready to take on the yonko, but he was sorely mistaken.

Despite using his Gear 4 abilities, Luffy did nothing to Kaido, and instead got knocked out by one single swing from Kaido’s club. Ending the fight quickly without Kaido even needing to break a sweat.

It showcased just how far Luffy still needed to go to bridge the gap in power level. At this time, Kaido could have chosen to kill Luffy, but decided to imprison him instead. It’s a moment that Luffy should have died, but Kaido made a foolish choice by letting the boy live.

Luffy unlocking Gear 5 is one of the most memorable moments in the entire anime and manga. Seeing him go down, the light drain from his eyes, and then his hair turning white and awakening his Devil Fruit was something that was celebrated.

Unfortunately, it is another moment where I have to say that he should have died. Realistically, Kaido was too much for Luffy before he unlocked Gear 5.

While Luffy put up a good fight, he straight up died during his battle against Kaido. He even goes so far as to confirm that he died, but his fruit resurrected him in one of the ultimate displays of plot armor.

Before going forward, I want to give a major spoiler warning if you haven’t been caught up and been actively reading the Elbaf arc, as the anime has not reached this moment.

Now that you are aware of the consequences, Luffy received yet another degree of plot armor by surviving an attack that mirrors what happened during the Alabasta arc. Instead of Crocodile doing the impaling, this time it is Nerona Imu.

Imu comes down from his holy land of Mary Geoise, and Luffy is not strong enough to handle the fight. After taking numerous hits, Imu impales Luffy multiple times, leaving him for dead. He is saved by being in his Gear 5 form, and miraculously heals. This is a major use of plot armor, and one that might be a step too far.

Luffy has faced villains that ought to end his journey, but he always survives the face-off because of his willpower.
