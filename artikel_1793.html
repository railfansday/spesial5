Celtic make addition to Europa League squad after Mika Baur injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Celtic have added Jota to their Europa League squad in place of the injured Mika Baur.

The Portuguese winger was initially left out as he fought his way back from a serious knee injury. But he could now feature in his homeland against Torreense on Thursday after continuing his comeback with a start in Saturday’s 4-1 win over St Mirren.

Baur was recently ruled out for up to 10 weeks with ankle ligament damage but manager Martin O’Neill had been waiting to see how the German international reacted once his protective boot was off.

Baur could miss the rest of the league stage though and O’Neill has acted before setting off for Portugal.

With Benjamin Nygren nursing a heel problem, O’Neill started with a midfield three of Callum McGregor, Luke McCowan and Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain on Saturday, while Oliver Sorensen is another option.

Meanwhile, O’Neill has challenged Celtic to complete their Europa League comeback after resurrecting hopes against Besiktas.

Celtic were heading for a fourth straight defeat and bottom spot when they went behind against the Turks but they were inspired by the introduction of Camilo Duran, Callum Osmand and Colby Donovan and sealed a 3-2 win.

They now face away games against Torreense and Omonia Nicosia as they bid to move up the table.

Looking back at their recent win, O’Neill told Celtic Player: “I thought it was terrific. Really terrific.

“Besiktas are a very fine football team. Very fine side. They were expecting to win and win handsomely. They’ve spent a lot of money. I think their wage bill is in excess of £100million. So from that aspect, it was a terrific win.

“The character of the side was really brilliant. Really, really brilliant to win so late, and to win in the fashion in which we did was terrific. So it gives us a little bit of a chance and we have to go and try to do something for these next two away games.

“If we make it through to the qualifying stages, it will be the first time any team has been beaten in their first three games and made it.

“So that in itself should be a target for us. The games are not easy, we know that. We need to win at least one of these two away games and we might even need four points to give us a chance in the last two matches.

“We have to take confidence from recent games and take that into the game on Thursday.”

Torreense sit 13th in the Portuguese second tier and will host Celtic 60 miles away from their home in Leiria, as their own stadium only has about 2,500 seats.

But they have already beaten Lillestrom and Ararat-Armenia in the Europa League and took the lead against Sunderland before the Premier League side netted a stoppage-time winner.

“They won the Portuguese Cup against Sporting Lisbon, which is some achievement,” O’Neill said. “I’ve been learning as much as I can about them and watching footage.”
