Brighton v Newcastle: Fabian Hurzeler delivers honest verdict on Ibrahim Osman after two-goal blast

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Brighton head coach Fabian Hurzeler has urged Ibrahim Osman to remain patient but be “ready and alive” as he waits for an opportunity to establish himself in the Premier League after starring in Europe.

Winger Osman scored twice – his first goals for the Seagulls – and also provided an assist in Thursday evening’s 4-0 Conference League win over Romanian champions Universitatea Craiova.

The 21-year-old has been limited to 41 minutes of top-flight action across four substitute appearances this season after being loaned to Feyenoord, Auxerre and Birmingham since his 2024 arrival from Danish club Nordsjaelland.

“It’s not always the fact that he has to do more; sometimes the players who are currently on the pitch do a lot to stay on the pitch,” said Hurzeler.

“Therefore he needs to keep working hard, he needs to be patient, he needs to be there when we need him.

“We have plenty of games in the next weeks and I always emphasise that we need the full squad, that we need everyone, everyone will play an important role for us.

“No matter how many minutes you play, you need to contribute to the success for this club and we need to always put the team first. Some players might have to be a little bit more patient but in the right moment you need to be there so you have to always be ready and alive to support the team.”

Brighton, who sit second in the Premier League, will look to bounce back from last weekend’s surprise 2-0 defeat at Hull when they host Newcastle on Sunday.
