Hailey! Demi! Keke! An Exclusive Behind-the-Scenes Look at the 2026 CFDA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The biggest and brightest names in American fashion all came together last night for the 45th annual CFDA Awards, which are often referred to as the Oscars of Fashion. Taking place at New York’s Natural History Museum, the night celebrates both the unique contributions of American designers throughout the previous year and honors those who have made an indelible mark on the industry throughout their illustrious careers.

“Continue to share important stories and do work that means so much to you,” said CFDA chairman Thom Browne at the ceremony. “Work that you couldn’t imagine doing anything else, because the true marker of success is being able to do something you love always at the highest level. And never sacrifice the true creativity and quality of what you do.”

Awards were presented to Ralph Lauren, Rachel Scott of Diotima, the jewelry designer Ana Khouri, and Colleen Allen. Fashion world luminaries were also honored, including Tory Burch, Linda Fargo, Peter Marino, Susan Cianciolo, and Inez van Lamsweerde and Vinoodh Matadin.

The night welcomed a retinue of A-listers to help celebrate, proving that fashion now touches every part of culture, from entertainment to tech to sports. Bold-faced names included the night’s host, Keke Palmer; presenters Zoe Kravitz, Hailey Bieber, Rosé, Lupita Nyong’o, Matt Damon, Naomi Osaka, Demi Moore, and Cole Escola; and celebrity attendees Gracie Abrams, Megan Thee Stallion, Travis Scott, PinkPantheress, and many more.

“Anthony is not only an extraordinary designer, but also an extraordinary human being,” said Zoe Kravitz while presenting Anthony Vaccarello with his honor for International Designer of the Year. “He has this rare ability to make you feel completely understood, and that is reflected in everything he creates.”

“Anthony doesn't just design clothes,” added Rosé. “He creates an attitude, a sense of freedom and confidence that is distinctly his own.”

Donatella Versace, who attended with Vaccarello, had this to say about the honor. “I love what Anthony has built at Saint Laurent over the last decade and cannot wait to see what he does next. New chapters are always inspiring.”
