Denmark’s Queen Mary Delivers a Trifecta of Designer Looks During Her Historic State Visit to Colombia

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

From a D&G pencil skirt to a Saloni maxi dress

Denmark’s Queen Mary is looking posh and joyful on day two of her historic state visit to Colombia—a trip which makes her the first Danish queen to travel to the country.

Today, the monarch was given a tour of the Gold Museum in Bogotá, and later walked through the city’s main square, Plaza de Bolívar, with Mayor Carlos Fernando Galán.

The official visit runs through November 26, and the queen’s agenda includes learning about the country’s post-earthquake reconstruction efforts (three months after a 7.4-magnitude earthquake struck Colombia) and ongoing sustainability projects.

To arrive in the country, Mary went sharp in a black Forte Forte single-breasted blazer with a frilly sky-blue Stenströms Darya blouse, which she paired with black trousers, Malone Souliers Marion 70 pumps, and black Gucci glasses, plus some gold jewelry.

Then, for this afternoon’s engagements, she slipped into a sophisticated business look, the highlight of which was a black-and-white tweed pencil skirt from Dolce & Gabbana and a black collared top from Red Valentino. To round out the ensemble, she reached for sheer black tights, plus matching stilettos and a leather handbag.

The royal performed another quick change for her evening engagement: a reception in the Biodiversario greenhouse at Bogotá’s Botanical Garden. The look was a breezy wine midi dress with a fun dotted print by Saloni. In semi-sheer fabric, it featured a high neck, long sleeves, a cinched waist, and a long skirt that reached her ankles. Black-and-white heels added the perfect contrast to the colorful maxi.
