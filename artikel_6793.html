Cecilie Bahnsen Rethinks the Fleece for Her Second Uniqlo Collaboration

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The collection is a Scandi winter balletcore dream.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Cecilie Bahnsen has a cult following for a reason: the Danish designer’s big blooming dresses and dedication to sporty, easy balletcore have been collected in droves by fashion far and wide. So when Uniqlo came calling for her to design a second collaboration, it was time again for the designer to take the reins and merge her playful girlish Scandi style with the utilitarian wearability of the Japanese giant. The collection, which includes womenswear and childrenswear, puts a focus on cozy winter wardrobe dressing and is available now. In it, Bahnsen is rethinking and retooling the classic winter fleece.

For the occasion, Bahnsen spent half a year designing the collection and also collaborated with her friend Fumiko Imano, the artist, to shoot the campaign in Sweden amidst a picturesque landscape. “It was very much about sisterhood, the journey you do of exploring together, and inspiration also from when I’m in the countryside with my son, and we collect flowers, and how we kind of, he sometimes pinned them on his jumper,” Bahnsen tells ELLE of the new collection. “I think it was really kind of that romantic or nostalgic feeling that I wanted to put into the collection. And then there was also this notion of just working with one fabrication and working with the fleece for winter and how you can work this as layering.”

While Bahnsen in particular is known for her flouncy babydoll dresses, when it came to her second collection for Uniqlo, she tells us it was all about “refinement and balance and creating beautiful kind of everyday pieces.” Her personal favorite piece of the lineup is the fleece in the palest shade of soft pastel pink, embroidered with small flowers. “For me, the fleece is such a classic layering piece,” she says. She’s already been wearing it herself for countryside outings. “There’s romance and nostalgia to pink, and I think with this collaboration, it was nice to do the fleece in pink,” she says. “Showing that it could have that femininity to it was special, and it is also that right tone as a super soft pink that I love.”

The entire collection is designed to be layered, and the fleece is at the center of it. There’s a cropped fleece vest, a sheer long T-shirt printed with subtle bouquets of flowers, a floral-embellished fleece skirt and matching socks, gloves, and hats. Bahnsen herself recommends taking a cue from her own runway shows. “I always love a big jumper or a big fleece over a poof-y dress,” she says. “It has both a super boyish vibe to it but also a princess-y, romantic feeling, too, so I love those kinds of contrasts between each other.”

“These are pieces that you want to not just to work for one season, but to be timeless; it’s become about the perfection of these small details that makes it unique and maybe more romantic than what is normally in the Uniqlo collection,” adds Bahnsen. “I feel like the palette has a really Scandinavian winter tone to it, so how do you balance this soft pink into the darker blacks and blues, and kind of build it up, not in a monocomatic way, but really putting the tones and the layering together.” It’s definitely a chicer way to stay warm. The designer’s latest is the ultimate study of form and function.
