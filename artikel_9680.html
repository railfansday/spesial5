This guide uses a form backend, Formkove, which has a free plan with 300 submissions a month. The HTML is the same on all three hosts; the differences are in step 2. On GitHub Pages or Jekyll? Follow How to add a contact form to GitHub Pages instead.

Every Formkove form is rate limited and checked by an AI spam filter, which holds spam in your dashboard instead of emailing it to you. New forms also have the _gotcha honeypot turned on, which the hidden input in step 3 uses. If bots still get through, add Cloudflare Turnstile with your own keys.

Submissions the AI flags as spam count toward your monthly limit; ones stopped by the honeypot or Turnstile don't. How to stop contact form spam explains what each layer catches.
