These 23 Games Are Coming To Xbox Game Pass (November 3-17)

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Microsoft has revealed the first official roundup of Xbox Game Pass titles for November 2026, with 23 games joining various tiers of the service from November 3-17, including the likes of John Carpenter's Toxic Commando, Tanuki: Pon's Summer, and Football Manager 27.

We're happy to see Toxic Commando thrown into the mixer. It's a decent co-op game that's perfect for Xbox Game Pass if you're looking for something to muck around with friends playing.
