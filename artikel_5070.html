Bitcoin could double by 2032 and UniCredit investors would still make just 65%

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

UniCredit’s Bitcoin-linked certificate offers conditional capital protection at maturity in 2032, with gains capped at 65%.

UniCredit set Nov. 24 as the opening date for Italian subscriptions to its €1,000 Bitcoin-linked certificate.

The terms provide for a minimum payment of €1,000 at maturity in December 2032, provided UniCredit meets its obligations. A saver who needs the money sooner could sell at a loss or struggle to find a buyer.

The gross payout at maturity is capped at €1,650, and early sales occur at the prevailing market price. UniCredit’s key information document warns that holders may be unable to sell when they want to.

The certificate’s resale price can move with the iShares Bitcoin ETP, an exchange-traded product linked to Bitcoin. Interest rates, UniCredit’s creditworthiness and the time left until maturity can also affect the quote.

Certificate holders are unsecured creditors of UniCredit. The certificate falls outside deposit protection.

Certificate holders are unsecured creditors of UniCredit, since the certificates fall outside deposit protection. If the bank enters resolution, authorities can cut or cancel what holders are owed or convert it into shares or other obligations.

A separate clause lets UniCredit redeem the entire issue early at €1,000 per certificate under a narrow regulatory eligibility provision, subject to notice and other conditions. The bank controls that option.

The formula uses the iShares Bitcoin ETP’s euro closing price on Deutsche Börse Xetra. The starting price is scheduled to be set on Dec. 21, after the standard subscription deadline of Dec. 18. The final observation is scheduled for Dec. 15, 2032, with cash payment scheduled for Dec. 22.

A rise in the ETP between the two observations increases the scheduled payout by the same percentage, up to the €1,650 cap. A flat or lower ETP price leaves the payout at €1,000 if UniCredit meets its obligations.
