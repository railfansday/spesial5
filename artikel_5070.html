Strategy (MSTR) Adds 485 Bitcoin for $33.4 Million as It Spends $228 Million Buying Back Preferred Stock

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Strategy bought another 485 bitcoin last week. A Monday 8-K filing shows the Saylor-led firm spent $33.4 million at an average of $68,887 per coin between November 9 and November 15, lifting total holdings to 850,849 BTC bought for $64.20 billion at an average of $75,452.

The coins were funded with net proceeds from MSTR common stock sales. Strategy sold 2,087,040 shares for $244.7 million, put $33.4 million into bitcoin, used $206.3 million of those proceeds to buy back its STRC preferred and parked $5.0 million in USD Cash. No preferred stock was sold under the ATM during the week.

The STRC buyback was larger than that ATM slice. Strategy retired 2,282,510 STRC shares for $228.1 million, funding the other $21.8 million with interest earned on its cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments. It still has $332.2 million left under its preferred repurchase program and $1.0 billion under its MSTR buyback authorization, with about $18.1 billion of MSTR still available under the ATM.

The $228.1 million preferred repurchase was nearly seven times the $33.4 million bitcoin spend. The 485-coin add was also less than half the 1,036 bitcoin Strategy bought the week before.

The filing lands as the spot bitcoin price sits well below Strategy’s average cost. At the time of writing it trades near $64,100, about 15% under the $75,452 basis, leaving the stack roughly $9.7 billion underwater on paper. A week earlier, the same treasury was only modestly below cost after bitcoin slipped under $75,000.

The new coins do not close that gap. They were bought at $68,887, above today’s price but well below last week’s $79,012 average, and they shaved the company’s overall cost basis by only $4, to $75,452 from $75,456.

Strategy now holds more than 4% of bitcoin’s 21 million supply cap. The pace of buying has slowed as the firm has put more ATM cash into preferred buybacks than into bitcoin, a pattern that also marked the prior week’s purchase. Between June 30 and August 10, Strategy sold bitcoin in four consecutive filings to fund preferred dividends and STRC buybacks before flipping back to accumulation.

Strategy used $18.9 million of its USD Reserve during the week to pay preferred dividends. As of November 15, the reserve stood at $4.75 billion and USD Cash at $151.2 million.

On November 15, the company said it will keep the regular dividend rate on STRC at 12.00% for record dates in December. Daily STRC dividends for the month total $1.00 per share. Management said it will recommend keeping that 12% rate until STRC “has demonstrated sustained, healthy trading near $100 per share.”

The board also declared quarterly cash dividends, payable December 31, of $2.50 a share on STRF and STRD, $2.00 on STRK, and €2.50 on STRE. Strategy said it expects those December payments, including the daily STRC dividends, to be treated as non-taxable returns of capital to the extent of a shareholder’s tax basis.

The filing covers a week that ended with a sharp weekend slide in the bitcoin price, a move that also weighed on bitcoin-linked stocks. MSTR last closed Friday at $111.62.

The update comes as Strategy faces index-fund selling after MSCI removed the stock from its USA index, with funds required to sell by November 30. The SEC 8-K is the primary disclosure; Strategy also posts the same bitcoin figures on its public dashboard.

At the time of writing, bitcoin is near $64,100. The test now is whether Strategy keeps buying through a deeper underwater stack or leans even harder on preferred buybacks while the ATM stays open.
