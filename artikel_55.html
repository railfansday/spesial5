Russia's war on Ukraine: 'Both sides are prepared to operate outside of the conflict zone'

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Charli James is pleased to welcome Gavin Hall, Teaching Fellow in Political Science, and International Security at the University of Strathclyde. Hall examines recent Russian and Ukrainian operations beyond the main conflict zone, the conditions necessary for a negotiated settlement and the implications of diminishing direct US military support for Ukraine. “Both sides are prepared to operate beyond the main battleground and outside of the conflict zone,” he explains. Hall discusses why both sides want to negotiate from a position of strength and the conditions under which a “mutually hurting stalemate” could lead to a negotiated settlement.
