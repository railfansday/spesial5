Russian strike kills 2 in Kyiv

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A Russian attack struck an apartment building in the village of Dymer, just north of Kyiv, on Sunday, killing at least two people and wounding four others. The morning strike destroyed a five-storey building, while rescuers evacuated nine people, including a child. At least 11 others are believed to remain trapped under the rubble.
