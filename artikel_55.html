Comedian impersonates Trump's dance moves, mocks trade talks at Canada press dinner

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Watch: Carney and Trump impersonators take stage with real Mark Carney

Prime Minister Mark Carney brought a Donald Trump impersonator on stage at Ottawa's annual press gallery dinner, where he poked fun at strained US-Canada relations and on-again, off-again trade talks.

The impersonator, played by comedian Mark Critch of the sketch comedy show This Hour Has 22 Minutes, came out after Carney jokingly pressed an "emergency comedy button" during his speech.

Critch appeared alongside another actor impersonating Carney, played by Canadian sketch comedian Mark McKinney.

"We've got two Carneys here, it's a disaster," the Trump impersonator said after he walked on stage.

Using some of Trump's signature mannerisms, like his dance moves and his slightly hunched posture, he joked about the latest US midterm elections, which led to Trump's Republican Party losing control in Congress.

"I'm so popular we just had a big BLUE WAVE in the states," the impersonator said at the Saturday press gala.

He also took aim at ongoing US-Canada trade negotiations, saying,"We're going to have a great deal. There's only one thing standing in the way: me, because I'm crazy."

There were also jabs at Trump's search for a new White House press secretary following Katie Zacharia's exit, and at US ambassador to Canada Pete Hoekstra, who is unpopular among Canadians.

"I said 'find somebody who is less likeable than me,' and we got one," the Trump impersonator joked about Hoekstra.

Carney stood behind him occasionally laughing. The prime minister also delivered his own zingers, saying at one point: "This is a tough room. And I've been in the Oval Office. Alone. With its new interior decorator."

Both Critch and McKinney - the two impersonators - are fixtures of This Hour Has 22 Minutes, a long-standing Canadian sketch comedy show that has recently enjoyed viral internet fame for its timely skits mocking the Trump administration.

The skits, published directly on Tik Tok and YouTube, have depicted Zacharia's quick ouster, external and poked fun at rapper Nicki Minaj's, external support for Trump.

They have garnered millions of views, and even captured the attention of major US media., external

Critch and McKinney's appearance at Saturday's dinner is part of an evening of traditionally light-hearted humour, where Canadian politicians poke fun at one another at a gala hosted by the Canadian Parliamentary Press Gallery.

In a separate speech, Canada's governor general, Louise Arbour, joked about Carney's popularity and frequent global travel.

"Our prime minister is already getting a lot of attention. Actually, I heard a guy the other day compare him to God. Now seriously, do you know the difference between God and Mark Carney? God is everywhere, and Mark Carney is everywhere except in Ottawa where he's supposed to be," Arbour said.
