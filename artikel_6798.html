Three celeb endorsements make a one covetable trend.

Sometimes celebrities create trends, and sometimes celebrities wear trends. But then there are times when you really can’t tell which happened first. It’s a chicken-or-the-egg situation. That’s what we have on our hands with the leopard-print coat trend.

Here’s the fact: Inde Navarrette, Elizabeth Debicki, and Michelle Randolph all wore leopard coats last week. Three of anything make a trend, especially A-list spottings. Also true: Leopard coats never really go out of style, so I’m not sure how much credit we can actually give the stars. But you can get Navarrette’s exact Rixo coat and Randolph’s from Aflalo.

In case you feel compelled to make the trend your own, let me break it down a little further. Your leopard-print coat can—but doesn’t have to be—faux fur. It could also be more of a trench coat-like material or even jacquard. Many of the styles we’re seeing this season are collarless, but that’s simply a matter of preference.

Really, I can’t think of a better fall/winter item. Pair your coat with a white tee and blue denim for a look that is as polished and cool as they come. Alternatively, layer up in your finest party attire for the holiday season. Even worn with a little black dress, this trend will make a statement.

Whether you’re looking for a designer investment piece or an under-$400 option, I’ve found all the best leopard coats to browse now.
