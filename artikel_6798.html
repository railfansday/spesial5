Who Is Sara Boustany, the Breakout Star of Netflix’s ‘The Body’?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

What’s that? A TV show that isn’t a prequel, a spin-off, or a remake and that contains a cast of relatively unknown yet talented actors, none of whom are nepo babies?

It sounds too good to be true, but The Body is one of this year’s big hitters. The Netflix series—about a dance troupe at a Catholic high school in 2004 who accidentally send one of their friends into a “minimally conscious state” after a Satanic ritual at a Halloween party—is the teen drama you shouldn’t sleep on. Think Jennifer’s Body meets The Crucible, and you’ll come close. “It’s not just about being gay,” explains Gabby Windey, one of the show’s supporting players. “It’s about being hot and gay.”

But who are the show’s big breakout stars? Lebanese-American actor Sara Boustany, who plays Elise—the mean girl with a heart of gold—appears to be emerging as one to watch already, with well over half a million Instagram followers and counting (every time I check, it goes up), unusual for someone with such a sparse IMDb page. It’s easy to see why viewers have fallen in love with her character, though. As Elise, she’s fierce, independent, emotionally complicated, and kind of an outcast despite being one of the “popular” girls. Oh, and she’s a killer dancer. (An improvised dance to Jeff Buckley’s “Lover, You Should’ve Come Over” is a standout moment from the show.)

But who is Sara Boustany, the actor, and what do we know about her?

While the characters in the show don’t reveal their ages, we can estimate that they’re between 16 and 18 years old. (The sneaky weird girl Lisbeth, played by Geena Meszaros, another standout, at one point refers to herself as a “senior,” which would make her 17 or 18.) In reality, Sara Boustany is 23. Considering that Rachel McAdams was 25 years old when she played Regina George in Mean Girls, this discrepancy in age is in keeping with the show’s very Noughties spirit.

As mentioned earlier, Boustany has a relatively sparse list of credits under her belt, with The Body being her breakout starring role. However, she did appear in Dolly Parton’s Heartstrings, an eight-episode anthology series on Netflix that tells the story behind some of Dolly’s most famous songs. Before then, in 2019, she made her professional debut on Maya Erskine and Anna Konkle’s cult-favorite teen comedy PEN15, also set in the 2000s.

If you’ve never heard of Rommulas, all you need to know is that he’s a 22-year-old Mexican-American musician with around 430,000 monthly listeners on Spotify. And, while neither has confirmed their relationship in any official or public capacity, both he and Boustany have been pictured at events together, including at Gucci’s 2027 resort show.

Fun fact: Boustany originally auditioned to play Elise’s co-dance captain—and complicated love interest—Anya (Kristina Bogic). The casting directors, however, immediately saw her as Elise instead. “I weirdly didn’t get why,” Boustany told Teen Vogue earlier this month. “Elise is so quippy, and her defence mechanism is to be bitchy, which isn’t really me. But I’d only read the pilot (“Slutty God”), and I was so focused on Anya and trying to get on the show. If I’d stopped to think about it for even 30 seconds, I would’ve been like, Oh, yeah. This is a much better direction for me.”
