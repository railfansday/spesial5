21 Delightful Old Goldie Hawn Pictures, Just Because

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Goldie Hawn has never taken herself too seriously—and unlike other actors of her generation, her star has never waned. Rising to fame on the NBC comedy show Rowan & Martin’s Laugh-In in the late 1960s, she quickly became a household name, and by 1969 she had scooped an Oscar and a Golden Globe for best supporting actress for her stellar performance in Cactus Flower.

Over the decades that followed, she would play a string of leading roles in films like Shampoo, Private Benjamin, Death Becomes Her (with Meryl Streep), and The First Wives Club. She and her longtime partner, Kurt Russell, also brought up three more talented actors: Oliver Hudson, Kate Hudson, and Wyatt Russell.

Here—for no good reason except that we love her (oh, and it’s her 81st birthday)—we’ve gathered 21 archival photos of the effortlessly charming, utterly beguiling Goldie Hawn. Enjoy!

A young Hawn fixes her hair in the mirror before a photoshoot in Arlington, Virginia.

Hawn captures all the energy of the swinging ’60s in a fun portrait photo.

Sporting a darling babydoll dress and Mary Janes during a portrait session.

A young Hawn graces the pages of American Vogue in 1969, wearing a black silk chiffon minidress by Jay Kobrin for Maisonette.

All feathered hair and smoky lids at the premiere party for her 1970 rom-com There’s a Girl in My Soup, co-starring Peter Sellers.

Hawn and Warren Beatty on the set of the ’70s comedy-drama Dollars. Hawn was just 26 at the time.

With Julie Christie—her future co-star in Hal Ashby’s 1975 rom-com Shampoo—at a fundraiser for George McGovern.

Aged 27, Hawn holds her own in a glittering, midriff-flossing gold gown during her Las Vegas nightclub act.

Cuddling a tiny kitten and chic parasol, Hawn poses for a German publication.

With Bruno Wintzell at London Heathrow, Hawn made the airport her runway.

On the set of TV’s The Goldie Hawn Special, Hawn was beautifully captured enjoying a peaceful moment with her dog in between takes.

Wide-eyed and lovely in a promotional shot for The Goldie Hawn Special.

Could there have been a better pairing than Hawn and Liza Minnelli? They took to the stage for the TV special Goldie and Liza Together.

At the 52nd Academy Awards, where Hawn and Steven Spielberg presented the best-director prize to Robert Benton for his work on Kramer vs. Kramer.

Ready to party in a sheer leopard-print blouse and cami, Hawn exits New York’s Carlyle Hotel with Kurt Russell in style.

In a shimmering, feather-trimmed gown, Hawn was the most interesting woman in the room at the American Ballet Theater Gala fundraiser in Beverly Hills.

Hawn and Russell on the set of the rom-com Overboard.

In black tie with Russell at the Tel Aviv Gala in California, honoring Hawn’s work.

Dressed like her character in Overboard, Hawn makes a red strapless gown and sleek black gloves look elegant yet cool in 1987.

In a ruched dress, Hawn strikes a pose while being honored at the American Museum of the Moving Image.

Hawn walking the red carpet at the 71st Academy Awards, dressed in a glittery, baby blue number and chic dark shades.
