John Apparently Brought an Escort Home During ‘Love Is Blind’ Cheating Scandal

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The only marriage from season 11 has already come crashing down.

John Shannon and Sarah Williams were the only Bostonians to walk down the aisle on Love Is Blind season 11, but their marriage has unfortunately already come to an end. During the reality show’s reunion special, John revealed he “made a mistake” and committed a “major breach of trust” after a “wonderful year of marriage.”

According to John’s own admission, he had “crossed a line” when he secretly exchanged graphic texts with a stripper and escort while he was still married to Sarah.

“I don’t blame her at all for leaving,” John said during the reunion. “It’s been really hard.”

Now, the account executive is facing new allegations, making his cheating scandal even messier.

According to Rikki Wise and Ashley Thomson’s Reality Receipts podcast, John apparently did more than just text the escort. In a separate video Rikki shared, one of her sources alleged that John brought the other woman home.

“I am told that John did invite this stripper slash escort back to his Airbnb, and the only reason nothing physical happened is because he did not want to pay her,” Rikki reported, adding, “I’m not sure if she actually went back and then payment was discussed or if the discussion happened in the text messages.”

She continued, “What I do know is that John apparently thought that him and the stripper had a connection. So he felt like he didn’t need to pay her because he thought it was an organic connection and not a paid transaction.”

Rikki clarified that further details about the alleged text messages between John and the other woman haven’t been verified, so take it all with a grain of salt.

Morrey Elgin, who butted heads with John since he also connected with Sarah in the Love Is Blind pods, also weighed in on the scandal. He made a dig at John’s infidelity with a playful Instagram video where he skims through pages of the Bible.

“I just can’t seem to find the chapter where it says you can cheat on your wife,” he said in the video as he grabbed the book. Ouch.

Sarah and John have yet to comment on Rikki’s reported intel, so stay tuned for more.
