Arsenal are set to make a late decision on the fitness of David Raya ahead of Manchester City’s trip to north London on Sunday.

The Spaniard missed the draw with Borussia Dortmund due to the shoulder injury he picked up at St James’ Park last weekend.

However, while there were fears that was a significant problem, scans have showed that Raya avoided serious injury and his time on the sidelines is not expected to be a lengthy one.

It remains to be seen whether Raya recovers in time to face City on Sunday, with Kepa Arrizabalaga ready to fill in again if required.

Ezri Konsa appears set to keep his place at centre-back, as Arsenal continue to cautiously manage William Saliba’s fitness.

Saliba was in the matchday squad against Newcastle and Borussia Dortmund but is yet to make a first-team appearance this season. It would be a surprise if he came straight back in to face City.

It is expected that Riccardo Calafiori will return to the starting line-up after being rested in midweek, while Eberechi Eze could well get the nod in front of him on the left wing after impressing at St James’ Park.

Viktor Gyokeres scored against Dortmund but Kai Havertz is usually trusted in these games by Mikel Arteta.

The German was given a full rest in that draw with Dortmund and he looks set to lead the line against City.

Arteta will provide an update on the latest Arsenal team news at his press conference on Friday afternoon.
