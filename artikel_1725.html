Scaroni comments on ‘fantastic week’ and Cardinale’s tears: “His passion shows”

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

AC Milan president Paolo Scaroni believes it has been a ‘fantastic week’ for the club, also commenting on Gerry Cardinale’s passion.

This evening, Milan will host Hungarian giants Ferencvaros at San Siro at 18:45 CET in the fourth game of their Europa League league phase campaign. The Rossoneri hope to build on the derby win and start moving in the right direction in Europe, having lost two of three so far.

Arriving at the customary UEFA lunch, Scaroni was interviewed by the MilanNews correspondent who was present. He spoke about an important past few days, which saw the official unveiling of the new stadium, followed a few hours later by the 3-1 victory in the Milan derby.

First the stadium and then the derby, a fantastic week…

“It’s been a fantastic week. We’re always confident, Gerry Cardinale is too.”

“His passion shows when things are going well and when they are going badly, like everyone else. This time, things went well.”

Milan will have to be at their best tonight, given that their opponents from Budapest have won all three games in Europe thus far, and are 20 unbeaten across all competitions in a run that stretches back to the end of July.
