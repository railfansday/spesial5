Arsenal: Mikel Arteta backed for shock selection call to cause Man City ‘major problem’

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Paul Merson has urged Mikel Arteta to start Viktor Gyokeres up front when Arsenal host Manchester City this weekend.

The Gunners will hope to pile on the misery for City on Sunday, with Enzo Maresca’s side on a four-match winless run in the Premier League.

There are big selection decisions for Arteta to make all across the pitch. That includes in goal, where it remains to be seen if David Raya will be available after a shoulder injury.

In defence, William Saliba is fit but Arsenal have been cautious with his recovery and he is yet to play a single first-team minute this season following a back problem.

Arteta is not short of options in midfield, either, while up front Gyokeres is pushing to make only his second league start of the campaign.

Gyokeres scored a brilliant goal from distance against Borussia Dortmund in midweek but then missed several big chances as Arsenal were held to a 1-1 home draw in the Champions League.

Kai Havertz did not feature at all in that match, suggesting he is being saved to face City, but Merson wants Arteta to put his faith in Gyokeres instead.

Despite his lack of Premier League involvement this season, Merson believes the Sweden international would cause “major problems” for Marc Guehi and Ruben Dias in the City defence.

“He has not played a lot of football, and he is a bit hit and miss, but I would probably play him against Man City,” Merson told Sky Sports.

“The centre-halves won’t like that hustle and bustle. If you look at Man City, that has been their problem this season, when their defenders have been put under pressure, and there has been a bit of a fight.
