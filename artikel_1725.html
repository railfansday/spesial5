‘Benzema traumatised me’ – Chelsea’s Fofana haunted by Champions League loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Chelsea defender Wesley Fofana revealed "Karim Benzema traumatised me" in their Champions League tie with Real Madrid in 2023.

Los Blancos were drawn against the Blues in the 2022-23 Champions League quarter-finals, with the Spanish side progressing after a 4-0 aggregate win.

Madrid won both legs 2-0, with Benzema scoring the first goal of the tie, while Vinicius Junior assisted three of the four strikes.

Madrid became the first team to win multiple Champions League away games against Chelsea, following their victory at Stamford Bridge in the previous season's quarter-final.

Fofana admitted the likes of Benzema, Vinicius and Rodrygo proved too much to handle for Chelsea.

"Benzema traumatised me. He took us out all by himself," Fofana told the YouTube show Zack, as quoted by Foot Mercato.

"Him and Vinicius, it was just nonsense. Rodrygo, he came to finish the job, because I'm not driven by goals.

"If we talk about football, the two of them, Vinicius and Benzema, it was too much, it was nonsense. We played against Modric, they had a dream team."

Now, Fofana is looking to return to form at Chelsea after suffering multiple injuries during Enzo Maresca's reign, including an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury.

The 25-year-old admitted he was close to calling time on his career due to the number of injuries, but was convinced to continue by his agents.

"There was a period when I came back from the ACL and meniscus injuries, I injured my thigh twice under Maresca. A period where I wanted to quit football," Fofana added.

"I sat down with my agents, I told them: yeah, that's it, I'm quitting football. After that, they started talking to me about the contract and all that.

"They said: 'Anyway, it's simple, you've still got so many years left on your contract, and so much money to earn.

"Even if you're going to give up football, you'll turn up every day, put on a smile, you'll even stay on the sidelines whilst injured… but you've got a contract, you've got to see it through.'"
