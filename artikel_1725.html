Agony of tragic footballer's girlfriend, stuck alone, while he died in hospital: How family could not reach Jesus de Miguel's partner - after his father burst into tears at a bar watching the horror collision on TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

By MICHAEL PAVITT, SPORTS REPORTER and GERARD COUZENS

Published: 20:03 EST, 4 November 2026 | Updated: 08:03 EST, 5 November 2026

A friend of the Spanish footballer who died after a horror tackle this week has spoken of the heartbreak of the late sportsman's girlfriend, who had been watching the game with their one-year-old daughter.

Tenerife striker Jesus De Miguel, 30, died on Tuesday morning hours after receiving a flying knee to the chest from Andorra FC goalkeeper Jesus Owono, who was sent off before the match in Spain's second division was abandoned.

Yesterday, a post-mortem found De Miguel died from 'accidental chest trauma'. It is described in the preliminary report as a 'cause consistent with the player's death.' His funeral is due to be held today.

De Miguel's close friend Fernando Roman offered a tribute on social media and in an interview with Vamos a Ver.

Roman, who plays for Spanish side Talavera, explained that he had not been watching the match on Monday, but was alerted to it by his girlfriend after De Miguel had been injured by the tackle.

Roman had then been in contact with De Miguel's girlfriend Maria, who had been at the stadium with their one-year-old daughter Carla. Roman revealed she had been alone at the hospital and has been hit 'very hard' by the tragedy.

She was eventually joined by the De Miguel's parents, who travelled to Tenerife after being informed their son was in intensive care.

De Miguel died on Tuesday morning following a horrifying clash with Andorra FC goalkeeper Jesus Owono in a Spanish second division match

A neighbour of De Miguel's parents in Navahermosa told Y ahora Sonsoles that his father 'started crying' as soon as he saw the impact of the collision on television at his bar and immediately gathered his family to travel to Tenerife.

Roman told Vamos a Ver: 'Yesterday, my girlfriend and I were talking all afternoon and all night with María, his girlfriend, who was alone in the hospital.

'I've spoken with his girlfriend, and you can imagine how she is. Now we have to be with the family.

'We have to send our love to his family, to María, to his daughter Carla, who is a year and a few months old.

'It was unbelievable because we were talking about traveling to Disney with the girls, with my daughter too, and look what's happened.'

Roman also shared a letter on social media in tribute to De Miguel, reflecting on their friendship and stating he had 'brought happiness to everyone'.

'My friend... I can’t believe you’re gone... you have no idea how much I’m going to miss you. I didn’t just consider you a friend; I considered you a member of my own family,' Roman wrote.

'You were always there to help me when I was struggling—you were by my side during my worst moments.

'Many people didn’t truly know you, but having known you, I can say you were one of the best people I’ve ever had in my life: humble, hardworking, never one to look for trouble. You brought happiness to everyone around you, and above all, you were a truly good person.

The striker was struck in the chest by Owono's flying knee as he attempted to clear the ball near the halfway line

De Miguel was stretchered off the field following the collision with Owono

'All the moments we shared keep coming to mind... that trip to Punta Cana with the four of us - a trip I will never forget... all the lunches and dinners we enjoyed together, the time we spent with our daughters, the escape rooms we both loved so much...

'Tamara and I are absolutely devastated right now, but we know that from up above, you’ll still be with us—and, most importantly, with your daughter, with María, and with your entire family.

'My friend, I’m going to miss you so much... I love you dearly! REST IN PEACE!!'

Confirming receipt of the preliminary autopsy report yesterday and the authorisation of the transfer of the footballer's body, the Canary Islands High Court of Justice said: 'The court investigating the footballer's death, which is Court of Investigation Number Two of the Santa Cruz de Tenerife Court of First Instance, received the preliminary autopsy report today.

'The court has authorized the transport of the young man's remains to the mainland for burial.'

Police investigating De Miguel's death are probing the emergency medical response as well as the possibility rival goalie Jesus Owono's horror tackle could be involuntary manslaughter.

It emerged on Tuesday that homicide squad officers based in the Tenerife capital Santa Cruz had launched a probe as court officials explained any death 'not resulting from natural causes' automatically triggers a judicial investigation.

Legal experts have said they don't expect the ongoing investigation to lead to criminal charges against Andorra FC goalkeeper Owono because the clash occurred during open play, although he is expected to be hit with a lengthy ban.

The goalkeeper shared a statement on Wednesday, revealing he is in 'enormous pain' over the death of De Miguel.

Owono said: 'These are being the most difficult days of my life. I am struggling to find the words and find it very difficult to assimilate anything that has happened.

'I feel an enormous pain for the loss of Jesús. I think of him, his family, and everyone who loved him. I know there will be many issues to address; I will face whatever comes with the utmost respect.

'But right now, honestly, I cannot think of anything else except Jesús and the pain we are experiencing. Rest in peace, Jesús.'

Thirty-year-old father-of-one De Miguel died on Tuesday morning at Nuestra Senora Candelaria University Hospital after his second-division club revealed his neurological condition had worsened.

Medical personnel involved in the footballer's care are expected to be questioned and detectives will sift through medical reports as part of the probe.

Equatorial Guinea international Owono, who has lived in Spain since he was eight, was red-carded for kneeing Tenerife striker Jesus de Miguel in the chest near the halfway line late into stoppage time. The match was suspended when the two sides were drawing 2-2.

De Miguel, whose partner Maria and one-year-old daughter Carla were watching from the stands, was reportedly revived by paramedics on the pitch after going into cardiac arrest before being rushed to hospital.

His club confirmed around 9.30am local time yesterday morning he had died at a hospital in the Tenerife capital Santa Cruz about 8am, saying they were 'heartbroken' and had a 'lump in their throats'.

It said in a statement, after revealing earlier the footballer's neurological condition had worsened: 'Heartbroken, with a lump in our throats, and unable to believe this is true, CD Tenerife announces the tragic passing of Jesus de Miguel Alameda, which occurred this Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at 8am.

'There are no words of comfort, there is absolutely nothing that can alleviate this profound sense of sorrow.

'We stand with his devastated family, his loved ones, and the entire 'tinerfeñismo' community, who today mourn this cruel outcome.

Owono is reportedly seeking to apologise to De Miguel's family before making any kind of public statement.

FC Andorra, reacting to news of the Tenerife striker's death in hospital, said yesterday morning: 'Heartbroken and devastated, we wish to extend our deepest condolences to the family of Jesus de Miguel, all his colleagues and friends, and to CD Tenerife.
