Shiba Inu (SHIB) gained 2.36% over the past 24 hours and was trading at $0.00000389 on Nov. 19, close to its lowest level of the year. Earlier this month, the meme coin set a new yearly low of $0.00000373, according to TradingView data. SHIB is now heading into December, and CryptoRank data shows that December has been the worst month for the token so far.

SHIB's median return in December is -17.6%, and its average December return is -11.4%, according to CryptoRank. November comes next, with a median return of -16.4%.

SHIB has closed December in the green only once, in 2023, when the token gained 24.6%. In December 2021, 2022, 2024, and 2025, SHIB lost 29.5%, 13.5%, 21%, and 17.6%, respectively.

The December 2021 drop came a month after SHIB reached its all-time high of $0.00008845 on Oct. 28, 2021. At the current price, the Shiba Inu token is about 95% below that level.

SHIB started 2026 at $0.00000692, and on Jan. 6, it rose to $0.00000925. Since January, the token has lost 43.6%, according to CryptoRank. SHIB is down 32.3% in the fourth quarter, and it lost 16.7% in November alone.

Before November, SHIB's yearly low was $0.00000411, set at the July 17 close. In October, the token fell from $0.0000062, and in mid-November, it dropped below $0.000004.

On Nov. 12, the day Nvidia announced that CEO Jensen Huang had resigned, SHIB fell to $0.0000043. Nvidia shares dropped almost 12% in early trading that day, and SHIB's market capitalization was about $2.53 billion. According to CoinGecko, SHIB was down 7.7% over seven days and 17.7% over 30 days at the time.

On Nov. 14, a death cross appeared on SHIB's daily chart for the first time this year.

Shiba Inu developer Mazrael responded to the drop on X. "1 SHIB is always 1 SHIB," he wrote in reply to a user who pointed to the falling price.
