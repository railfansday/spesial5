The crypto market cap declined by more than $120 billion on Thursday to $2.57 trillion.

Bitcoin, ETH, XRP, and Solana led the broader cryptocurrency selloff over the past 24 hours.

Despite the renewed selling pressure on Thursday, a possible recovery is anticipated in the near future

As Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), XRP, and Solana (SOL) capitulated on November 5, 2026, the crypto market capitalization flash-crashed by more than $120 billion.

The crypto market cap fell to $2.57 trillion at the time of reporting, down from $2.69 trillion on Wednesday. As a result, the total cryptocurrency market cap declined by roughly 4.46%, according to data Coinpedia analyzed from CoinMarketCap.

Crypto market cap change on November 5. Source: CoinMarketCap

The free-fall was driven by a significant selloff in top crypto assets. At the time of writing, Bitcoin, ETH, XRP, and SOL had shed 4.46%, 6.68%, 4.45%, and 5.9%, respectively.

Top 7 crypto assets by market cap on Thursday. Source: CoinMarketCap

The cryptocurrency market faced heightened selling pressure following renewed geopolitical tensions amid a broader bear market in the artificial intelligence (AI) stocks.

Over the past 24 hours, the Nasdaq-100 Index (NDX), an equity benchmark tracking the 100 largest non-financial companies listed on the Nasdaq Stock Market, plummeted to its lowest in 196 days.

As such, the cryptocurrency market faced increased selling pressure catalyzed by broader macro headwinds.

Most importantly, the crypto market experienced a long squeeze, a situation in which falling prices force long holders to sell, thereby accelerating the decline. Over the past 24 hours, the cryptocurrencies’ derivatives had recorded around $1.06 billion in total liquidations, with $934.73 million involving long trades, as per metrics from CoinGlass.

Cryptocurrency liquidation heat map on Thursday. Source: CoinGlass

Even though the cryptocurrency market faced increased selloff fueled by a possible AI bubble burst, a possible bull run is expected before the end of this year.
