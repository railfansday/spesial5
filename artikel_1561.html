Liverpool vs. Man Utd injury, suspension list, predicted XIs

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

By Ben Knapton | 21 Nov 2026 16:30

Anfield hosts both this weekend's Premier League main event and Mauricio Pochettino's Premier League homecoming, as Manchester United do battle with Liverpool on Sunday afternoon.

The Red Devils trailed the Reds by 14 places and 10 points in the Premier League table heading into the international break, but only one of Man Utd's last nine managers has suffered defeat in his first game.

Here, Sports Mole rounds up the team news for both sides.

Out: Hugo Ekitike (Achilles), Conor Bradley (knee), Giovanni Leoni (knee)

Doubtful: Ryan Gravenberch (heel), Jeremie Frimpong (heel)
