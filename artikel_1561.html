Ballon d’Or: Bixente Lizarazu praises Harry Kane and reframes Kylian Mbappé

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Bixente Lizarazu welcomed Harry Kane’s coronation at the 2026 Ballon d’Or, while taking advantage of his time in Téléfoot this Sunday morning to send a particularly direct message to Kylian Mbappé. The former French international and Bayern Munich legend first chose the English striker as his highlight image of the week: “my image of the week is the Ballon d’Or. It’s Harry Kane. It’s been forty years since Bayern, since Karl-Heinz Rummenigge. And it happened and they celebrated it with dignity. It’s a very nice Ballon d’Or, with a very collective spirit. » A tribute to the Bavarian center forward, whose reward Lizarazu appreciates as much as the collective values ​​he embodies.

But the tone changed radically when discussing Mbappé and his attitude during the Ballon d’Or sequence. While he defended the Frenchman last season, the former left-back did not hide his disappointment: “he had a great World Cup. He was consistent with this France team, with a collective speech, and during the Ballon d’Or sequence, he spun. I didn’t like it at all. He went into something hyper individualistic, a lack of humility, self-promotion, a bit forgetting others. It was that he and the others didn’t exist. » Lizarazu then advised him to find a state of mind focused on the collective: “he must come back to the French team with collective values, because in any case, it is these collective values ​​which make the French team win. (…) He may score a lot of goals, but you have to make your team win. That’s what’s most important.”. The message has gotten through.
