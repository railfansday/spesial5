Holiday Ads Arrive Earlier, With Retailers Getting a Head Start

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

If it seems like holiday ads came earlier this year, that’s because they did.

According to Nielsen, there were six times more holiday ads this September than five years ago and 11% more compared to 2025. Retailers accounted for most of the early start, airing nearly 64% of holiday ads in October, though the sector accounted for only 45% of all holiday ad spend over the past five years, per Nielsen.

The return on holiday ads can be significant. Historically, they’ve outperformed non-holiday ads in Nielsen sales lift studies of connected TV campaigns. Cosmetics holiday ads produced 3.2 times greater sales lift than non-holiday ads, with retail and restaurant holiday ads outperforming non-holiday ones by 2.4
