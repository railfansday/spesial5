Opinion: Liverpool have done it, Mo Salah regen is coming to Reds' first-team

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Mo Salah's regen is coming to Liverpool's first-team. The Reds have already found him.

For much of last summer, the conversation focused on Richard Hughes' plans to replace the club legend. Liverpool knew Salah was leaving after agreeing to cancel the final year of his contract in January.

There was a plan in place. The club already had the perfect replacement lined-up. Yan Diomande had talked up his father's love for Liverpool.

The Ivorian was putting in some breathtaking performances at RB Leipzig, a club from where Liverpool have brought in a fair few players over the last half a decade.

And yet - the move didn't happen. Diomande opted to reject the Reds. He wanted the allure of Paris and later Madrid.

How Liverpool planned to replace Mo SalahAmid Diomande's rejection, there was a final frantic scramble towards the end of the summer window. Liverpool made offers for Ismaila Sarr and Yankuba Minteh. So the solution the Reds concocted to replace Salah was to rotate their attackers. They signed two left-wingers in Victor Munoz, Bradley Barcola to add to their existing duo of Cody Gakpo and Rio Ngumoha.

Now the four of them are rotating every game, with Ngumoh and Munoz playing on the right the most often so far.

It's definitely a temporary solution. Especially because Liverpool might soon have a long-term player joining Iraola's first-team squad.

Iraola was bold enough to promote a merely 15-year-old Joshua Abe to Liverpool's first-team in the summer. Having later turned 16-years-old, he made his debut for the club's senior side against Wrexham in a pre-season friendly.

Abe has since been injured. But he returned to action for England's U17 side during the international break. In two games, Abe has scored two goals in just 46 minutes spent on the pitch.

Still recovering from his injury he has made a very impressive impact.

A right-winger with a wand of a left-foot. Abe is literally identical to Salah in his style of play. He makes similar vertical forward runs and loves to cut onto his powerful left-foot. He is quick just like Salah and has everything to be the perfect right-winger in Iraola's system.

Now that he is back from injury, he's going to be joining Iraola's first-team - just as Ngumoha did at the same age under Arne Slot.
