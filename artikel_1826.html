Milan ‘building principles and understanding’ after thumping Frosinone, says Amorim

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Ruben Amorim says AC Milan are "building principles and understanding" following their 5-1 rout of Frosinone.

The Rossoneri made it three straight wins in Serie A on Sunday, climbing to fourth place in the table following a dominant performance at San Siro.

Gabriele Calvani's own-goal broke the deadlock after just four minutes, while Goncalo Ramos, Ardon Jashari, Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic were also on target during a clinical display by the hosts, who outperformed their 2.5 xG from 12 shots.

It was Milan's biggest league win under Amorim, while they have now scored 28 goals in his first 12 Serie A games in charge – a tally only bettered by Lajos Czeizler (31) and Nereo Rocco (29) among Rossoneri coaches.

"The main thing is that we have a plan in the game, and the team believe in the plan," Amorim told DAZN Italia. "We are building principles and understanding the game.

"You know Frosinone as well as I do, they are all about full pressure. I understood that with a lot of points coming to San Siro (20), they would be really brave.

"We knew Frosinone are about marking man-to-man. That's why Pulisic looked like he was in every position, because we knew they would follow us, and we could create a lot of danger with changing roles," continued Amorim.

With the pace we have with [Adrien] Rabiot, [Diego] Moreira, Pulisic and Ramos, we could make transitions. We changed a little bit the way we play the game because of our opponents, and it was a good night for us.

"It doesn't matter who plays, every player in training needs to understand every position on the pitch, so that we can change the characteristics.

"There are so many things I saw in the game that we need to improve, but we stuck to the plan, and that is something I am pleased with."
