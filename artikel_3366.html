Kieran Culkin Reveals He’s Reuniting With ‘Succession’ Creator Jesse Armstrong On Movie

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Kieran Culkin and Jesse Armstrong are getting the rotten cabal back together.

Culkin revealed that he is planning to reunite with the Succession creator on a new movie, though he didn’t disclose any further details about the project.

On the promotion tour for The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, the Oscar-winning actor told Global radio station Heart: “I’m about to work with — I don’t know if I’m allowed to talk about — I’m about to work with Jesse on a movie. He wrote something.”

Armstrong’s most recent project was HBO’s Mountainhead, a feature telling the story of a group of tech bros on a mountain retreat. Armstrong has been working with his Peep Show co-writer Sam Bain on A24 film, Jonty, starring Jesse Plemons and Cole Escola. There are conflicting accounts on the status of the movie. Culkin said he missed playing Roman Roy: “It was a lot of fun because I decided early on, this is a guy who’s never had to suffer consequences in his life. He can just talk his way in and out of any situation, like how fun is that to play. Completely unhinged.” Watch on Deadline

But he ruled out Succession returning in some form. “Could there be no spinoff?” he was asked by Heart breakfast host Amanda Holden. “Of course not. I think that would be a terrible idea,” Culkin replied. “What are you hoping for? Cousin Greg: The Sitcom?”

Culkin won the Best Supporting Actor Oscar for Jesse Eisenberg’s A Real Pain last year. He is currently starring as Caesar Flickerman in prequel movie, The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.
