Stephen Wilson Jr. Announces Double Album and Unveils Stunning ‘I’m a Song’ With Chris Stapleton

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Stephen Wilson Jr. is having a great Monday.

Alongside the Grammy news that his offering “Gary” is up for Best Country Solo Performance and “Hardwired” for Best Country Song, Wilson announced his sophomore double album Impressiøn of a Human // Children of the Cørnerman, which is slated to arrive Feb. 26, 2027. The ambitious LP will follow his 2023 Bon Aqua EP, which featured the searing “American Gothic” and “Year to Be Young 1994,” and Wilson’s colossal 22-track debut album, Søn of Dad. The double album announcement also dropped alongside the rollout of Wilson’s single “I’m a Song,” a stunning duet with Chris Stapleton that the two will perform at the CMA Awards on Wednesday.

The song, which was penned years ago and previously released as a live recording, was a favorite of Wilson’s father, Stephen Wilson Sr., who died at 59 in 2018 and has inspired much of Wilson’s work. When discussing the origins of “I’m a Song” on Theo Von’s podcast, Wilson described how his father watched him perform it live for the first time at Bluebird Cafe in Nashville, capturing his performance on his giant cellphone. A month later, on Father’s Day weekend, his father told Wilson that was his favorite song of all time. “He was actually my first fan. He believed in me so much more than I ever believed in myself,” said Wilson. A month later, his father died, and Wilson played “I’m a Song” at his funeral. “So when he said, ‘Write a good song for me,’ [my dad] knew I had already written one, at least in his eyes, because it was his favorite song ever,” Wilson said. “I guess his last charge was to please keep going and don’t stop.”

Over the weekend, Wilson performed “Wonderwall” for Oasis at the 2026 Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony. He is also gearing up for a big week in Nashville, where he is up for several CMA Awards, including New Artist of the Year and Song of the Year for his solo composition “Gary.”
