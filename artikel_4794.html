The AD Team's Favorite Tea Kettle

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

I initially bought the Fellow Stagg EKG Electric Kettle amid my latest health kick: I'd read somewhere (see: watched an Instagram Reel) that oolong tea can help ease bloating and support digestion. (For what it’s worth, there’s also a bit of science behind the claim.) I already had a ton of Dona oolong tea at my office desk, so a desktop kettle seemed like a natural purchase for completing the setup. My plan was to have a cup of oolong at 4 p.m. every day. While my daily tea service lasted about 10 days before I forgot about it, the kettle has stayed a fixture of my routine, and, as it turns out, many members of my team's routine, too.

Coworkers ask to use my kettle often enough that I’ve stopped thinking of it as entirely mine. Using it means you don’t have to walk all the way to the Condé Nast kitchen station, there's almost never any line, and the adjustable temperature means you can brew French press coffee, green tea, black tea, or any herbal blend you're working through at the right heat. The kettle is especially popular around the 3 p.m. slump, when everyone needs a quick caffeine hit to finish out the day before hustling off to their evening events.

As kettles go, the Fellow Stagg is a good-looking one: Made up of a matte body, slim gooseneck spout, and small control screen, it doesn't call attention to itself on a desk. the kettle also comes in more than half a dozen colors, allowing it to blend into most spaces.

Senior commerce editor Nashia Baker, a self-proclaimed "a social tea and coffee drinker," uses her Fellow at home. She likes displaying her beverage supplies so guests can make their own cup when they come over, and she credits the spout and ergonomic handle with "a steady, even pour" that spreads hot water equally over coffee grounds or a tea sachet. That control is the whole point of a gooseneck, which is designed for pour-over coffee.

The feature I didn't expect to love was the hold setting, which keeps water hot for 15 to 60 minutes after it boils. In an office setting, this capability makes the difference between a warm cup of tea and a cold one you forgot about during a meeting that ran long. The 30-ounce capacity also means the second and third person in line for a fresh mug don't have to wait for a new boil.

I won't claim a kettle will change your life, but mine has at least endeared me to my coworkers. If you've been considering buying one, now is a good time, as it's 20% off for Black Friday. May we all enjoy our 4 p.m. oolongs all winter long.
