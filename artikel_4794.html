Vuori vs. Lululemon? These Early Black Friday Deals Have Me Torn

WHEN IT COMES to athleisure, I’m usually torn between two brands: Vuori and Lululemon. Both have become staples in my wardrobe, whether I’m heading to the gym or catching a flight. And I don’t care what anyone says about dressing up for the airport—I’m always going to travel in sweats. Comfort is nonnegotiable when I’m sitting on a plane for hours, and both brands make some of the best joggers and sweatshirts I’ve put on my body. But what I appreciate most is that neither brand is limited to workout clothes. They’ve got plenty of everyday pieces, from sleek trousers and versatile jackets to tees and button-downs, that look just as fly outside the gym.

Now, with Black Friday around the corner, there’s even more reason to shop both brands. Vuori and Lululemon are rolling out almost 50 percent off deals on everything from performance gear to casual wardrobe staples, so it’s a great time to refresh your workout rotation or grab something comfortable for your next trip. But which brand has the better Black Friday deals? I’ve rounded up the styles worth shopping from both to help you decide where to spend your money.

Starting with Lululemon, we’re seeing deals up to 41 percent off, with plenty of workout gear and everyday staples worth adding to your cart. The biggest markdown that caught my eye is the Pace Breaker Jacket, which is designed for running and training. It’s made from a water-repellent nylon and elastane blend, with a close-fitting hood and a classic fit that leaves enough room to layer a workout tee underneath. There’s also a solid selection of hoodies and gym shorts on sale. Speaking of, if you’re not a fan of built-in liners, the Zeroed In Linerless 7-Inch Short is a great option for a freer feeling during running and training. It has a relaxed fit through the glutes and thighs, giving you plenty of breathing room, plus sweat-wicking, quick-drying fabric to keep you comfortable when things heat up. Beyond that, you’ll find workout tanks, compression shirts, and even comfortable joggers and pants that are for running errands or casual meet-ups with friends.

Over at Vuori, there are deals up to 50 percent off. The Sunday Performance Jogger, one of the best joggers we’ve tested, is 40 percent off. I’d reach for it whether I’m catching a flight or spending a lazy Sunday at home. The Kore Short, another editor favorite from our testing, is also marked down by 21 percent. It’s a solid pick for lifting weights and tackling a sweaty cardio session. You’ll also find deals on training jackets, quarter-zips, and workout tees, along with pants that are sharp enough to wear to the office followed by a nice dinner.

With Black Friday still a few weeks away, these deals are already worth jumping on. Popular sizes and colors are disappearing fast, so if something catches your eye, I wouldn’t wait around to see if the price drops any further. Your size might not be there when you come back.
