Parents jailed for torture and death of four-year-old girl found along French motorway in 1987

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The mother of a four-year-old girl killed in central France and found along a motorway almost 40 years ago has been sentenced to 20 years in prison.

Halima El Bakhti, 72, was found guilty on Tuesday of "torture and acts of barbarity resulting in death without the intent to kill", French media reports.

The girl's father, 74-year-old Ahmed Touloub, was handed a 15-year sentence as an accomplice.

She was buried in an unmarked grave near the location where her body was found. Unidentified for three decades, she was known only as the "Little Martyr of the A10".

The jury concluded that the child's mother was suffering from impaired judgement at the time of the girl's death, which reduced the maximum potential sentence.

French media outlet France Info described the two defendants as sitting stoically in chairs before the court with their heads bowed as the reasoning behind the verdict was read out.

El Bakhti and Touloub have 10 days to appeal the verdict.

Inass's death sparked one of France's biggest ever investigations, with the girl's photograph posted in public places and an alert sent to more than 30 countries.
