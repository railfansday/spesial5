2025 summer signing is slowly becoming a key midfielder for Millwall under Alex Neil: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Millwall have been a very dangerous side since Alex Neil was appointed boss. After narrowly missing out on promotion following a Championship play-off defeat to Hull City last term, the club are looking to bounce back once again.

Neil’s side have built a reputation for identifying players capable of making an impact in the Championship without spending excessively. This summer has seen Kyrell Lisbie come in and impress, while Lyndon Dykes has been an excellent free transfer.

While immediate impacts are always looked for with transfers, patience is also required sometimes. Derek Mazou-Sacko is becoming another example of that, and is showing that Millwall’s long-term recruitment working well despite a mediocre start to the campaign.

The French midfielder arrived from Rodez in August 2025 for a reported fee of around €500,000. There was little fanfare surrounding his arrival, but he has gradually established himself as an important part of Alex Neil’s side.

At just 22, he is already showing the qualities needed to become a reliable Championship performer. Mazou-Sacko is not a midfielder who will regularly grab headlines with goals, assists or eye-catching creative numbers.

His value lies in the less glamorous aspects of the game, and his latest statistics underline why Neil has increasingly trusted him. His aerial ability is another useful asset, standing at 6ft 1in, That physical presence helps Millwall compete for second balls and provides additional protection in front of their defence.

There is little suggestion that he should be expected to offer much more creatively. His five chances created place him in the 11th percentile, while his 440 touches rank in the 16th. However, that should not necessarily be viewed as a weakness.

He has completed 267 passes at an accuracy of 83.4%, including 46 line-breaking passes, showing he can retain possession and move the ball forward without taking unnecessary risks.

His development has been gradual. Having progressed through Troyes, spent time on loan at Nancy and subsequently made around 34 appearances for Rodez in Ligue 2, the move to English football represented an important opportunity.

He contributed to Millwall’s play-off push last season, making 23 league appearances and registering one goal and one assist. Now approaching 35 appearances for the club, he looks increasingly comfortable at this level.

Millwall will need more than one dependable defensive midfielder if they are to achieve their ambitions, but Mazou-Sacko is proving to be an excellent piece of business.

He might never become the most exciting player in Neil’s squad, but he makes the team more difficult to play through. Over a 46-game Championship season, that reliability can be just as valuable as an attacking match-winner.
