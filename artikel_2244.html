Vitor Matos must give 21-year-old his Swansea City debut after latest suspension blow: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Swansea City enter the second international break of the season, 6th in the Championship, but when they return to action, they will be without midfielder Marko Stamenic. He picked up a red card last weekend, and he’ll be suspended for three games.

The Swans have good depth in midfield, so replacing him shouldn’t be an issue. However, it does give the Welsh outfit a great opportunity to give Tom Iorpenda his first start. The 21-year-old joined from Huddersfield Town in the summer but has yet to appear for Vitor Matos’ side.

Swansea City have the youngster under contract until 2029, and there is plenty of time to unlock his talent. However, they cannot let the midfielder stay on the bench all season, and a decision must be made about his short-term future in the next month.

Replacing Stamenic won’t be an easy task for the Swans after his bright campaign so far. The New Zealand international has a successful pass rate of 90.8% and has helped his side keep three clean sheets. Elijah Just and Melker Widell are both options for Matos, but Iorpenda should be his chosen successor after the international break.

Despite no senior appearances this term, the 21-year-old was inspirational during his loan spell at Notts County last season. He made seven assists from an xA of just 4.35 and made 180 defensive contributions across 53 games for the Magpies.

Iorpenda’s downfall in comparison to Stamenic comes through experience. He has yet to feature in the Championship, and a first game back against West Brom will be a tough test. Moreover, starting the youngster would make the Swans a little more vulnerable at the back.

The Englishman operates as a more advanced midfielder, although his 98 tackles last term make him a strong option for Matos to consider. Realistically, if Jay Fulton retains his spot in the starting XI in two weeks, there’s no reason why it can’t be the occasion for the 21-year-old’s first cameo.

If Iorpenda’s lack of game time continues over the winter period, then a loan move away could be the best decision for Swansea City to make. The Swans can’t afford to let the midfielder’s development stagnate, and getting him minutes elsewhere could be beneficial.

The 21-year-old would excel in League One despite few minutes at that level. His dominance in League Two makes a spell there pointless, and plenty of sides in the third tier would welcome the youngster’s talent.

Sooner or later, though, Matos must give an opportunity to Iorpenda. If Swansea City can unlock his potential, then the £1m deal to sign him could turn out to be an exceptional deal.
