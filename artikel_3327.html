Lisa Edelstein, Kevin Pollak & Joe Swanberg Among New Additions To SM Goldberger’s ‘Your Heart Is A Hummingbird’

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

EXCLUSIVE: Your Heart is a Hummingbird, former adult film actress SM Goldberger’s semi-autobiographical indie drama starring Chloë Levine, has added six to its cast. The new additions are Lisa Edelstein (Long Story Short), Kevin Pollak (The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel), Arye Gross (Minority Report), Sylvia Kuhar, Mila Brener (I Love LA) and Joe Swanberg (The Sun Never Sets).

In Your Heart is a Hummingbird, Levine plays successful adult film star Rebekah Rosenberg — aka Bex Bacall — who upon learning of her mother’s cancer diagnosis, finds her very carefully separated worlds collide forcing her to reconcile the girl she was with the woman she’s becoming. Edelstein and Gross play Rebekah’s parents, Tzippy and Noah. Brener plays her sister, Judy, with Pollak as agent Isaac Zelnick, Kuhar as Kimmy Kelley, a new starlet in the adult industry, and Swanberg as Garrett, an adult film director. Watch on Deadline

Marking Goldberger’s feature debut as writer-director, the film was developed through the 2024 Antigravity Academy Screenwriters Camp and recently wrapped production in Los Angeles. Winsor Yuan, Chelsea Davenport (Bubble & Squeak) and Kimberly Hwang are its producers. Levine, Chris Quintos Cathcart, Valerie Bush and Carlos López Estrada are executive producing.

Edelstein is represented by Gersh, Genuine Artists and Anthem Entertainment; Pollak by Gersh and Nelson Davis; Gross by SDB Partners; Kuhar by CESD Talent Agency and Luber Roklin Entertainment; Brener by CESD Talent Agency and April Mills Entertainment; and Swanberg by Entertainment 360.
