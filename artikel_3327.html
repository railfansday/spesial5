Where to Watch the 2026 National Dog Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

It’s a Thanksgiving Day tradition to gather the family around the TV to watch the best athletes in America contend for glory — we’re, of course, referring to the dogs of the National Dog Show, which sees two hours of canine competition across seven groups.

The National Dog Show has historically followed the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade. This year will look a little different, as the Thanksgiving Day Parade was postponed to Saturday, Nov. 28 due to severe weather. This marks the first time the parade has ever been postponed, although it was cancelled for several years during World War II. The National Dog Show, which was pre-taped earlier this month at the Greater Philadelphia Expo Center, is still scheduled to air as planned.

The show will air at noon, local time, on NBC and will be preceded by a replay of last year’s parade. Last year’s Best in Show was Soleil, a Belgian sheepdog, who is looking to defend her title this year. The dog breeds are split into seven groups: herding, hound, non-sporting, sporting, terrier, toy, and working.

The competition is presented by the Kennel Club of Philadelphia and sponsored by Purina, and the broadcast will be hosted by John O’Hurley and David Frei, who are returning for their 25th year.

If you’re looking to watch the National Dog Show, you can catch it on NBC. If you don’t have cable or an antenna, you can stream it by subscribing to Peacock, or by getting a free trial to a live-TV streaming service like DirecTV or Fubo. Here are a few options.

DirecTV is one of the best live-TV streaming services thanks to its broad selection. You can get NBC a variety of packages, all of which come with a five-day trial.

Fubo carries NBC in both of its tiers and is a particularly popular streaming service with sports fans. Like DirecTV, you can get a five-day free trial. You also get a discount on the first month after the trial.

Hulu + Live TV has a shorter three-day free trial, and it includes NBC in its main plan. The service costs $99.99 a month once the trial ends.
