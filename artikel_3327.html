Well, it’s officially happened: We’ve nearly made it to December, and the holidays are in full swing, which makes it a fine time to settle in with a fresh batch of titles new on Hulu and Disney+. December’s lineup across the streaming siblings is packed with everything from a fan-favorite mystery-comedy heading across the pond to an animated holiday misadventure and gaming’s biggest awards show making its Disney+ debut.

The month kicks off on Dec. 4 when a certain middle-school troublemaker and his family trade snow for sand in a new animated holiday comedy, only for their tropical resort getaway to unravel fast. A few days later, on Dec. 8, a beloved true-crime-podcaster trio packs their bags for the U.K. to crack the murder of an audio rival in a newly retitled sixth season. Then, on Dec. 10, host Geoff Keighley takes the stage as gaming's biggest night streams live on Disney+ for the very first time, handing out Game of the Year and rolling out the surprise trailers the show is known for.

Greg Heffley and his family swap what could be a cold, stressful Christmas for a tropical island resort in this animated holiday comedy, and true to form, the vacation falls apart almost immediately. As you can imagine, things like sunburn, stomach bugs, and some awful bad luck turn what could be a fun getaway into a stressful side quest.

Adapted from Jeff Kinney’s 12th book, it’s the fourth animated "Wimpy Kid" film for Disney+ after last year’s “The Last Straw,” and the second with a Christmas theme after 2023’s “Cabin Fever.” And you'll love the familiar voices, too: Fred Armisen and Beau Bridges star in this hit for the whole family.

Stream on Disney Plus beginning December 4

Charles, Oliver and Mabel are leaving the Arconia behind. For season 6, the Emmy-winning Hulu whodunit relocates to London, taking Steve Martin, Martin Short, and Selena Gomez across the Atlantic. The movie is a big enough deal that the series has even been retitled “Only Murders in London.”

The trio is on the trail of whoever killed true crime podcaster Cinda Canning (Tina Fey), the cliffhanger that capped season 5. You can expect the same kind of twists and sardonic humor that you fell in love with throughout the rest of the five seasons, but now with a fun new location that promises more of the same. If you're like us, December 8 can't come fast enough.

Stream on Disney Plus and Hulu beginning December 8

Gaming’s biggest night is headed to Disney+ for the first time this year. Hosted by Geoff Keighley, "The Game Awards" hands out Game of the Year and dozens of other trophies, though the show is just as well known for its stream of world premieres and surprise trailers.

The 2025 edition drew a record 171 million livestreams. This year, it'll air live from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles so you can catch nominees like Grand Theft Auto VI, Resident Evil: Requiem, and Big Walk vie for some of the most coveted recognition in gaming.
