‘Yellowjackets’ Creators On Ending Show Ahead Of Original Five-Season Plan: “We Chose To Try To Squeeze It In”

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

While many Yellowjackets fans are struggling to cope with the inevitable end, for its series creators, it was the right time to land the plane.

Discussing the fourth and final season with Deadline, Ashley Lyle and Bart Nickerson explained that they didn’t want to “run the risk of overstaying our welcome” with another season when they were reaching the conclusion “more rapidly.”

“We’d always said that we had pitched a five-season plan, but we’d always given the caveat that we felt like the show would tell us when it wanted to end, and just in the breaking of this story, it started to feel like we were sort of moving towards the end more rapidly,” explained Nickerson. “And so, then the decision kind of became, ‘OK, do we sort of squeeze a lot into Season 4 or run the risk of overstaying our welcome out of the fifth season?’ We chose to try to squeeze it in, and that ended up really just kind of working out nicely from our perspective.

“The show has always had to be very kind of efficient, with the storytelling. It’s a double ensemble with a lot of story that we’re telling, and so I think we’ve gotten pretty good or comfortable kind of operating a at a certain pace and a certain kind of grammar, for lack of a better word, and so that was what we did,” he added. Watch on Deadline

As Nickerson explained it was an “incredible gift” to know they were ending after Season 4, Lyle noted that they were “reinvigorated” to get to return to civilization, which had always been their plan for the final season.

“We had been looking forward to that for so long now. It was part of the original pitch, and to finally get there—everything, when you’re pitching a show, it all feels like a pipe dream,” said Lyle. “You don’t think you’re actually going to be able to do any of this, especially something that you know is only gonna be if you get a first season, a second season, a third season. So, to actually reach that point and get to dive in, it reinvigorated everybody.”

In Yellowjackets, a 1996 New Jersey soccer team’s plane crashes in the Canadian wilderness, where they struggle to survive while they’re stranded for 19 months as a dark supernatural force seemingly lurks in the woods. Meanwhile, their adult counterparts cope with the memories of what went down in the wilderness.

With new episodes available to stream Fridays on Paramount+ with Premium Plan, the fourth and final season sees the Yellowjackets rescued and returned home, where they must adjust to civilization and take their secrets to the grave.
