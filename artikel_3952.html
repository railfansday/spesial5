GTA 6 Subscription Will Include NBA 2K27 for Free on PS5

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

One month of GTA+ bundled into GTA 6 pre-orders

If you've pre-ordered GTA 6, did you take note of what bonuses you got with that? A one-month membership to GTA+, a subscription service from Rockstar, is bundled with all GTA 6 pre-orders, and that offer gets even better next week.

From 27th November 2026 until 15th January 2027, basketball simulator NBA 2K27 will be added to GTA+, providing a fully playable version of the standard edition. This is just like PS Plus Extra, except it's specific to games developed by Rockstar and participating publishers, like 2K.

When you pre-order GTA 6, you automatically receive a code to redeem one month of GTA+. If you have not already claimed it, then you can save it for when NBA 2K27 is included and give it a go along with all the other titles included.

It'll be included in the subscription service until 15th January 2027. GTA+ costs £6.99 / $7.99 per month.

Just be aware that activating the code triggers a standard subscription once the initial period is over, so you need to turn off automatic renewals.

Once the online portion of GTA 6 is available, the GTA+ membership will become more valuable, as demonstrated by the current GTA Online. You get plenty of benefits in-game when you have a subscription.
