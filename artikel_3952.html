I'm modding GTA 5 every day in a quest to either transform it into a PC version of GTA 6 or break it entirely. Come back tomorrow to find out what new horrible thing I've done to Rockstar's baby, or check out what I got up to earlier.

It is time to say goodbye to Grand Theft Auto 5. Did we not say goodbye literally yesterday, when I installed a mod named Farewell Los Santos, and there was even a video and everything, and we put Komm Susser Tod over it?

No. 53 mods ago, I told you, the reader, that my mission here was to either mod GTA 5 into a version of Grand Theft Auto 6 on PC, or break the game trying. Admittedly, my mission was successful: GTA 5 is actually better than GTA 6 now. But that means only one thing: as a final send-off, I can think of nothing better to do than finally fulfil the other potential outcome of our initial gambit. It is time to break GTA 5 entirely.

I have installed Fences Network, Simple Weapons Saving, Dragon Trainer V – How to Train Your Dragon Mega Pack, Empire: Singleplayer Overhaul, In The Hood (Gang), BodyGuard Menu, Addon Car Spawner Plus, Rideable Sandworm, Buckme Money App, Realistic Tactical Loadout - GTA VI Inspired Visible Weapon Carry, Pickup, & HUD System [Legacy & Enhanced], Gang Mod Respect System, Los Santos Collapse [Legacy & Enhanced] Zombie mod, The Last Heist - An ending for GTA V [Story Mod | 10 Missions], Real Life Mod [Legacy], Raul's Revenge DLC, Warzone Infil, RageHandlingEditor (RHE) [Legacy & Enhanced], On foot radio (with animations and headphones customization), Keep Right, and Ending C with M83 Midnight City.

In other words, everything that was on the front page of GTA 5 Mods dot com's scripts section when I loaded it up this morning. This took some doing, involving quite a lot of faffing around with OpenIV and myriad .rpf packages to get everything installed.

Would you be surprised to hear (you will not be surprised to hear) the game does not start anymore? GTA 5 has gone to that great sleep that awaits us all. Oh, don't get me wrong: it will boot to the main menu, but try to resume a game or start a new one and GTA 5 will, valiantly, spend about a minute and a half with its cogs audibly grinding against another before simply dumping you back at your desktop—no fanfare, no error message, just swift death. After enduring 53 other mods without completely breaking, it rests now in the great beyond.
