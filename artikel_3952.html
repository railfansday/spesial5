When half the waking world is playing something else, you have to take a hint

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

The industry is on a go-slow this week, due to a certain new game from Rockstar's imminent arrival. As such, I wouldn't expect many updates from other popular releases, Palworld being among them, because it's just not a good time for headline space.

The main man for public relations on the Pokemon-inspired survival game states as much on Twitter. A fan chastised Pocketpair for not providing news on 1.1 and Palfarm, to whom the situation was plainly explained.

"Sharing news about Palworld 1.1 or Palfarm the week GTA 6 is about to drop is probably not the best idea," John 'Bucky' Buckley, head of communications and publishing at Pocketpair, says. "From a PR point of view!"

@RaiderNite Sharing news about Palworld 1.1 or Palfarm the week GTA6 is about to drop is probably not the best idea... :) From a PR point of view!November 17, 2026

The original tweeter agrees, but there's some division in the replies otherwise, because several people point out they're not Grand Theft Auto fans. Regardless, Buckley's statement is one of mere practicality.

The mainstream video games world is centered on the sixth mainline GTA right now. It's a release 13 years in the making, with an advertising budget to rival the GDP of a small country. Even the Weather Channel is doing sponsored posts.

This is not a good time to try and break ground on anything substantial for any other big games. Chances are high that some portion of your fanbase is distracted, and your coverage will be stifled because less resources are available for other projects and communities at the minute.

The GTA 6 storm will subside – it'll become background noise at some point. But release week? It's like putting another blockbuster film in cinemas around Avengers: Doomsday. You're just not going to get the same airplay. Palworld 1.1 and Palfarm updates will come, just not for the time-being.
