Exodus Reveals Female Protagonist After All the Drama Around Its Male Lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Exodus has lifted the veil on the female version of its protagonist Jun, via a brand new trailer.

The context around this one is interesting, because developer Archetype Entertainment famously redesigned its male lead just a few weeks back, following on from fan criticism of his original mug.

For what it's worth, the initial reaction to female Jun has been much more positive, with the comments section of the aforementioned trailer housing plenty of praise. Here's hoping that the studio's nailed this one first time!

It's worth pointing out that you can customise Jun's appearance but not to any significant extent. You can edit their hair, facial hair, eye colour, and makeup, can't change their facial structure.

The trailer doesn't start and end with female Jun, however. A feature on Jun's personality and background follows, giving a little more insight into the kind of character you'll be playing as.

To be clear, you'll be able to shape Jun's story through your actions throughout the game. A Mass Effect-style alignment system determines the narrative's direction as you go from mission to mission and get to know your allies.
