Sci-fi RPG Exodus continues evoking Mass Effect by revealing female lead and nicknaming her FemJun

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Sci-fi role-playing game Exodus continues to play into the Mass Effect comparisons by revealing the female version of its main character Jun, and with studio Archetype referring to her in marketing material as FemJun.

If that moniker sounds familiar it's because it is: the female Commander Shepard character in the Mass Effect series was nicknamed FemShep. FemShep became an iconic part of BioWare's iconic series, eventually proving that female leads could front games just as successfully as male leads could, and be every bit as popular.

Is the nickname FemJun too blatant? Possibly. But there's well publicised BioWare pedigree at Archetype Entertainment, in the studio's co-founders and in the people leading the project. Key Mass Effect writer Drew Karpyshyn is in charge of writing Exodus, for example, and the similarities to Mass Effect are something the Exodus team has not been shy about in the past. In the wake of Mass Effect 5 being cancelled and BioWare being closed, they become even more alluring.

There are, however, big and important differences between Exodus and Mass Effect, key among them, time dilation. In Exodus, there are special Exodus missions whereby you leave your home planet and fly far away, and in doing so experience time differently to the people back home. Many years will pass for them while only days will pass for you, which provides the game with a way to show you long-term consequences of your actions when you return.

Archetype hasn't shown the female version of Jun before, despite having released many videos of Exodus, perhaps to build anticipation for her reveal. It's a press beat that's also provided Archetype - a studio owned by Dungeons & Dragons company Wizards of the Coast - with an occasion to reveal the two actors playing Jun in the game.

Female Jun is portrayed by Carina Conti, who played the lead in the recent Mafia game, The Old Country - Isabella Torrisi. She's appeared in HBO's Winning Time and Hulu/Freeform's Good Trouble as well. Male Jun, meanwhile, is portrayed by Stephen Oyoung, who played Mister Negative in Insomniac's Marvel's Spider-Man 1 and 2 games, and Red Hood in Gotham Knights. He also appeared in Mission: Impossible - The Final Reckoning, The Wrecking Crew, and Twisters. The game also famously stars Matthew McConaughey.

Archetype announced a Dark Horse hardback art book to go on sale alongside the game next year as well. This arrives a week after the game; Exodus arrives 7th April 2027 on PC, PS5 and Xbox Series S/X, and the art book on 13th April.

Matt played Exodus at Gamescom relatively recently and came away impressed, referring to it as "the best kind of surprise". "I wasn't expecting quite such a bombastic 45 minutes when I first picked up a controller and settled into Exodus, admittedly, but the more I played, the more it made sense," he wrote. "I might not be much of a Mass Effect fan (damn you M35 Mako once more), but Exodus is now firmly on my radar ahead of its arrival next year."
