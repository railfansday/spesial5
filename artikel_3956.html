Dragon’s Dogma II: Dark Arisen x Delicious in Dungeon collaboration announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Capcom has announced a collaboration with manga series Delicious in Dungeon for Dragon’s Dogma II: Dark Arisen, which brings Official Pawns and special collaboration outfits inspired by characters Laios and Marcille into the game. The limited-time collaboration is available now for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC, and is coming to Switch 2 on November 11.

Official Pawns modeled after the heavily-armored Laios and the elven mage Marcille from Delicious in Dungeon can now be recruited from the Riftstones in places such as towns to support Arisen throughout their journey. Arisen seeking their expertise should strike while the iron is hot, as these culinary explorers will be available to aid in adventures until the title update on Thursday, January 28, 2027 on all platforms. Please check the official website for recruitment details and notes.

Arisen who load their save data for Dragon’s Dogma II or Dragon’s Dogma II: Dark Arisen during the Delicious in Dungeon collaboration period will automatically receive special crossover outfits modeled after the attire of Laios and Marcille. These collaboration outfits will automatically be added to in-game storage, and can be equipped by both the Arisen and Pawns. Once redeemed, these outfits will be available after the collaboration event ends.

The title update featuring the Delicious in Dungeon collaboration also contains additional bug fixes for various platforms. Please see the official Dragon’s Dogma II website for more details.

After his sister is devoured by a Red Dragon deep within a dungeon, the adventurer Laios—joined by the elven mage Marcille and other companions—braves the dungeon once again to rescue her. The series has gained widespread acclaim for its unique worldbuilding that blends dungeon crawling with culinary adventure, as the party cooks and eats the monsters they encounter while fighting their way through the labyrinth. In 2024, an anime adaptation aired to immense popularity both in Japan and internationally, making it a beloved manga and anime franchise supported by fans around the globe.

Dragon’s Dogma II: Dark Arisen is available now for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and PC via Steam.
