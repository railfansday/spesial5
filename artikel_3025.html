Here's How Much a $5,000 Investment in SpaceX Stock Could Be Worth by November 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Space Exploration Technologies' stock price could be noticeably higher by this time next year, but successful investing requires thinking about the long term.

Analysts' 12-month price targets aren't guarantees of where a stock could trade down the road. But with a company like Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +0.72%), price targets can help retail investors establish a risk-to-reward framework to inform their investment considerations.

After all, with SpaceX not only involved in the space sector but also viewing its biggest opportunities in artificial intelligence (AI), it helps to get a ballpark sense of what an investment today could be worth a year from now.

Today, we'll look at how analysts are valuing the company to get an idea of how much a $5,000 investment made now could be worth by November 2027.

According to data tracked by CNN, analysts generally have a favorable outlook on SpaceX. Out of the 47 who cover the stock, 77% rate it a buy, 19% a hold, and 4% a sell.

Among that group, the median one-year price target is $245, which would be an 82.4% gain from the Nov. 6 closing price of $134.27. We'll ignore the highest target of $800, as SpaceX is unlikely to reach that in just a year. We will, however, focus on the lowest target, $118, which would amount to a 12.1% loss.

If someone bought $5,000 worth of SpaceX at the Nov. 6 closing price of $134.27, that would yield approximately 37 shares. With 37 shares, if SpaceX reached the median one-year price target of $245 per share, that $5,000 investment would be worth $9,065. If the stock price sank to the lowest target, $118, that $5,000 investment would be worth $4,336.

SpaceX is showing accelerated revenue growth, as evidenced by its third-quarter results, with sales growing 161% to $14.5 billion. The company also reported net income of $4.4 billion, a nice change of pace from the $1 billion net loss it reported in the prior-year period.

The company is enjoying a noticeable sales growth trajectory. It's forecasting first-quarter 2027 revenue of between $33 billion and $37 billion, which is impressive given that it reported $18.6 billion in revenue for all of 2025.

If SpaceX can keep growing its sales and trimming its net losses, investor enthusiasm could provide some fuel to help the stock price reach that $245 target.

There are a few reasons why SpaceX's stock price could fall to $118, and it could always slide even further. One of the factors putting pressure on it now is the recent broad sell-off in AI stocks. While SpaceX's AI segment went from generating $813 million in the third quarter of 2025 to $7.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026, it's still spending heavily to build its data center infrastructure. Its capital expenditures totaled $19.6 billion in the third quarter, with $16.4 billion of that spent on its AI division alone.

The share price could also fall amid a broad market sell-off or due to a few underwhelming quarterly performances.

The risk-to-reward framework looks favorable over the next year, but doesn't make SpaceX an automatic investment just based on one-year price targets. Ultimately, meaningful wealth isn't typically created after just one year of owning a stock, and it will take years for SpaceX to execute on its ambitions.

Even in this stock's short trading history, there have been many wild price swings. Those are only likely to continue. To hold onto this stock through the rockiest moments requires shareholders to have strong conviction in the company. While it's common to hear people talk in retrospect about how much money retail investors could have made by investing in one of CEO Elon Musk's other companies, Tesla, in its early days, what is less often shared are the tales of investors who sold their stock along the way because they couldn't handle the volatility.

With SpaceX, capturing the biggest gains will require patience and a strong enough belief in the company that a shareholder won't react with a knee-jerk sell if they see the stock price head significantly lower.
