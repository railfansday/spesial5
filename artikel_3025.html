What it's about: In "Way of the Warrior Kid," bullied middle schooler Marc (Jude Hill) struggles with confidence and feels out of place among his classmates. Things begin to change when his injured uncle Jake (Chris Pratt), a decorated Navy SEAL, moves in for the summer. Determined to help Marc stand up for himself, Jake puts him through a training program designed to build resilience, discipline and courage.

Why you should watch it: "Way of the Warrior Kid" is a lovely choice if you're in the mood for an uplifting underdog story. It's an enjoyable feel-good watch with plenty of heart.
