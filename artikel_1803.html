Mancini urges Italy to learn from Belgium thrashing amid Nations League near-miss

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Roberto Mancini acknowledged that Belgium were by far the better side in Italy's 4-1 Nations League defeat, but vowed that his team would learn from the experience after they missed out on a quarter-final spot.

Italy went into Sunday's clash leading Group A1 and even took the lead at Stade Roi Baudouin through Francesco Pio Esposito, but then collapsed before half-time, shipping three goals within 11 minutes.

Maxim De Cuyper, Nathan Ngoy and Jeremy Doku led Belgium's comeback, with Diego Moreira striking late on to seal a spot in the quarter-finals at Italy's expense.

Pio Esposito also saw a penalty saved by Senne Lammens in the 91st minute as the Azzurri were forced to settle for a third-place finish following France's 2-1 win over Turkiye.

And Mancini wants his team to learn from missing out on qualification.

"They were better, we suffered too much on a physical level and in one-on-one situations," Mancini said.

"We started well, yes, but they were better on the night.

"When the ball got to the wings, we struggled to have the right pressing, they caused us problems.

"We felt some fatigue from the France match, but they deserved the victory, there's nothing more to say."

Belgium created chances worth 3.42 expected goals (xG) to Italy's 1.90 xG, aided in part by Pio Esposito's missed penalty late on.

Though his team were ultimately well beaten, Mancini feels Italy are on the right track as he begins a rebuild following their failure to qualify for the World Cup earlier this year.

"These nights can happen in the growth process, you need to learn from these experiences," Mancini continued.

"Considering the way we got here, we had the chance to qualify, but we knew it would be tough, as Belgium are a strong team.

"Of course we are sad now, but this night will help us going forward."
