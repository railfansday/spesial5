Bradford City boss Michael Duff on his first return to Wycombe Wanderers after controversial League One sacking

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

For the second successive game, Duff, whose side eased past Barnsley 3-0 in midweek, will face one of his old clubs.

Wanderers axed Duff after just five games of the current campaign, making him the first managerial casualty of the 2026-27 season.

The former Huddersfield Town, Swansea City and Barnsley chief had been at the helm for almost a year after replacing Michael Dodds last September.

He pulled the Chairboys away from trouble at the wrong end of the League One table with the Buckinghamshire club finishing in the top half by the end of 2025-26.

Duff, who was appointed by the Bantams just shy of a month after leaving Adams Park, said: "It's a good club with a lot of investment off the pitch and they spent a lot of money on the pitch. There's some good people there.

"I don't carry around the bitterness and all things like that. Part of the job is they wanted to change the manager and they did it.

"I wouldn't be here (otherwise). It is what it is.

"It was a pretty good year, I think we would have been fifth in the league in the period of time I was (there). But obviously, it wasn't deemed good enough, so you move on."

Duff, who has lost just once in his opening 10 matches in charge at City, says captain Antoni Sarcevic is 'touch and go' ahead of the trip south.

Sarcevic missed the victory over Barnsley after picking up a knock in last weekend’s draw against Doncaster Rovers.

City have potential issues in midfield, with loanee Kieran Morgan absent for the foreseeable future after breaking his foot on Tuesday.

George Lapslie returned to full training after a hamstring issue on Wednesday, but Duff says he will be guided by the club's medical staff ahead of deciding whether to involve him or Sarcevic this weekend - amid the club's hectic forthcoming schedule.

The City chief has confirmed that adding to his options in the middle of the park is an area where the club are looking to strengthen in January.
