Tottenham XI vs Leeds: Starting lineup, confirmed team news, injury latest for Premier League today

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Tottenham are boosted by the return of Rodrigo Bentancur from suspension, but a number of injury absentees remain sidelined.

Lucas Bergvall has been out since suffering a knee injury against Manchester United at the start of October, while Bentancur is available after a three-game suspension served following his late dismissal in the same game at Old Trafford.

In the pair’s absence, Roberto De Zerbi has relied on Sandro Tonali and Mateus Fernandes in midfield, and they are joined by Archie Gray at Elland Road. Pedro Porro returns to right-back.

Today’s trip to Elland Road, where Spurs are looking to make it four wins in a row for the first time in more than 1,000 days, will come too soon for Mykhailo Mudryk, though.

De Zerbi confirmed at his pre-match press conference on Friday that Mudryk and fellow winger Savio would not be back until after the international break, though the latter has been seen back with the squad at Hotspur Way this week.

James Maddison was not risked against Palace following a groin issue suffered in the win over Chelsea, and is available to play some part at Leeds off the bench, while Wilson Odobert and Xavi Simons remain long-term absentees.

However, there have been reports this week that Simons is now entering the final stage of his recovery from an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) rupture suffered back in April and is expected back in training later in November, well ahead of schedule.
