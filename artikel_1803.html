Konstantinos Tzolakis kept Hull City 'alive' to pull off famous win, says Sergej Jakirovic

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Tigers welcomed in-form Brighton and Hove Albion, and beat them 2-0 with goals from Oli McBurnie in stoppage time.

That did not tell the whole story, with Brighton the dominant side for much of the game, only to be denied by another brilliant performance from Hull goalkeeper Tzolakis. The Seagulls also hit the woodwork, though in fairness so too did Hull.

The combination of defensive brilliance and clinical finishing on the counter-attack made, in Jakirovic's words, for a "good recipe”.

"We played against an excellent team," he said. "They're playing very intensive football. A lot of movement.

"Some moments of the game, I couldn't track them on the pitch.

"But congratulations to our lads, they gave everything. We had problems with injuries, with cramps.

"We survived the first half because Tzolakis was amazing there. I thought a few balls were in the net, to be honest. But this is his quality.

"We are very happy because of these three points, especially a victory after almost three months, which is excellent.

"I feel very good. I wish this feeling would never stop.

"I know how demanding it is and you see every week and every game, it's very difficult.

"Everyone fights there for the points, but it will be very big confidence for us and a big boost for the next game."

Jakirovic acknowledged the part Tzolkais played in keeping his team in the game until the coach could make some decisive substitutions.

"Because of him we were still alive," he said. "It could be two, 3-0 and then finish the game, ciao.

"With Sorba (Thomas, who hit the crossbar), with (Lucas) Gourna (who set up the first goal), with (Elliot) Stroud and after (that Ryan) Giles, especially on the left side, we had much more stability. Jens (Hjerto-Dahl) also gave us a lot of stability, a lot of energy."

Brighton opened the door to Hull by having Joao Costinha sent off for two fouls on Thomas, the second in the 89th minute.

"After this red card, I want to take more risks," said Jakirovic. "We scored two goals from counter-attacks, but I think this first goal, they played brilliantly, very precise.

"(Regan) Slater to Jens, Jens to Slater, to Gourna, and then Oli McBurnie.

"That was a very good signing, because it's about age, it's about his experience, he's young goalkeeper but very experienced, and an international. He had difficult times now in the last international break (when Greece lost 7-0 in the Netherlands), but this is not his fault.

"Okay, maybe he could stop some goals (this season), but also he could concede some goals, so he's doing very well and I hope he will continue like this."

The biggest negative was that midfielder Tim Iroegbunam went off injured at the end of a strong first half for him.

"I was thinking it's knee, it doesn't look good, but he said to me now that he has a problem with his hamstring," said Jakirovic. "We will see."
