Kyren Williams has a toe injury, is expected to be ready for Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Another key offensive player for the Rams is dealing with an injury, but it is not expected to keep him from playing against the Packers on Thanksgiving Eve.

Head coach Sean McVay said on Saturday that running back Kyren Williams has a toe injury. McVay said the team is not concerned about his availability for this week.

Williams has run the ball 139 times for 616 yards and six touchdowns this season. He also has 38 catches for 267 yards and a touchdown, so he's played a significant role in both offensive phases this season.

The Rams officially designated defensive tackle Kobie Turner and guard Justin Dedich for return from injured reserve Saturday. The team will issue its first Week 12 injury report on Sunday.
