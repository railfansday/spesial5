Why CrowdStrike Stock Was Down Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

CrowdStrike beat Wall Street's estimates, but its $43.9 billion deal to buy Zscaler sent the stock lower.

By Johnny Rice – Nov 23, 2026 at 5:40PM EST

10 stocks we like better than CrowdStrike ›

CrowdStrike Holdings (CRWD -8.92%) stock finished down 8.9% on Monday, Nov. 23, after the cybersecurity company agreed to buy Zscaler (ZS +17.67%).

It was one of the few tech stocks to see red today, with the Nasdaq Composite up 6.2%. The S&P 500 jumped 4.7%.

CrowdStrike announced Monday that it would buy Zscaler, a cloud security company, in an all-stock deal valued at $43.9 billion. Zscaler shareholders would own about 16% of the combined company, and CrowdStrike shareholders would own the rest.

The deal still needs approval from both regulators and Zscaler shareholders.

The offer represents a 34% premium to Zscaler's Friday closing price; investors seemed to think that's a lot to pay.

CrowdStrike makes software that protects devices, while Zscaler helps companies control who can access their apps and data. Bringing the two together makes sense from a product standpoint and gives the company more to sell to its own customers as well as a shot at selling its software to roughly 8,000 Zscaler customers who don't already buy from CrowdStrike.

The announcement overshadowed a strong earnings report this morning in which the company beat Wall Street estimates for revenue and adjusted earnings per share. Sales jumped 28% year over year.
