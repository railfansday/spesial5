Jaxon Smith-Njigba Ridiculous Streak Comes To An End in Seahawks-Cardinals Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

By his very high standards, Seattle Seahawks star wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba had a subdued performance in Week 9's game against the Arizona Cardinals on Sunday.

The former Ohio State Buckeyes star wideout finished with only 51 receiving yards and zero scores on seven receptions and 10 targets in Seattle's 17-3 victory over Gardner Minshew and the Arizona Cardinals at Lumen Field in Seattle.

It was just the first time in the 2026 NFL season that Smith-Njigba did not lead the Seahawks in receiving yards in a game. That also ended a streak dating back to the 2025 NFL regular season.

Jadarian Price led Seattle with 54 receiving yards on four catches. Jaxon Smith-Njiba (seven catches, 51 yards) had led them in receiving in each of the last 13 regular-season games. Per Elias, that's tied for the longest streak in NFL history. JSN topped 1K yards for the season.

Prior to Sunday, the last regular-season game in which Seattle was led in receiving yards by a player not named Jaxon Smith-Njigba was in Week 13 of the 2025 campaign against the Minnesota Vikings. Tight end AJ Barner paced Seattle in that game despite only having 35 receiving yards. Smith-Njigba, meanwhile, only had two catches on four targets for 23 receiving yards.

However, Smith-Njigba still managed to join an extremely exclusive Seattle franchise club with his performance versus the Cards.

The two-time Pro Bowler wide receiver's output in Week 9 was enough for him to clear 1,000 receiving yards in 2026, making him just the third player in franchise history to have at least that many receiving yards in three consecutive seasons. Hall of Famer Steve Largent and Tyler Lockett are the other two.
