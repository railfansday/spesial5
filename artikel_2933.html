NBA Cup scores, standings, and scenarios: Who can clinch and who's nearly toast?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It's the fourth night of the NBA Cup, and for some teams who want to raise a banner, this might be their last chance to stay in the tournament.

Entering the games on Nov. 20, or "Moving Day," each group leader has two wins, while six teams are looking for their first win, including the reigning NBA Cup champions, the New York Knicks.

The night kicks off with the Charlotte Hornets, currently sporting the league's worst offense, facing the surprising Atlanta Hawks. The nightcap features the Houston Rockets battling the Denver Nuggets, while the Los Angeles Lakers take on the lowly Sacramento Kings, who have the worst record in the Western Conference.

Charlotte Hornets at Atlanta Hawks (East Group C) – 7 ET, NBA League Pass

Orlando Magic at Brooklyn Nets (East Group A) – 7:30 ET, NBA League Pass

Indiana Pacers at Cleveland Cavaliers (East Group B) – 7:30 ET, NBA League Pass

Toronto Raptors at Detroit Pistons (East Group A) – 7:30 ET, Prime

Washington Wizards at Chicago Bulls (East Group C) – 8 ET, NBA League Pass

New Orleans Pelicans at Oklahoma City Thunder (West Group B) – 8 ET, NBA League Pass

Phoenix Suns at Utah Jazz (West Group A) – 9:30 ET, NBA League Pass

Houston Rockets at Denver Nuggets (West Group A) – 10 ET, Prime

Sacramento Kings at Los Angeles Lakers (West Group C) – 10 ET, NBA League Pass

Orlando is the only team in action on Nov. 20 that can reach 3-0 by beating the Brooklyn Nets. Miami and Boston are also undefeated, but they don't play their third Group games until next week.

The Hornets, Jazz, Kings, and Pelicans need to win their first NBA Cup to stay in contention, while the Nets will be eliminated if they lose and Detroit wins. Milwaukee, whose next Group game is Nov. 25, will exit stage left if both Toronto and Orlando win.
