Best Black Friday Phone Deals: Save Hundreds Now on Your Next Upgrade

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

These editor-approved phones are on sale for Black Friday, so get in before the holiday shopping season officially starts.

Gear-obsessed editors choose every product we review. We may earn commission if you buy from a link. Why Trust Us?

Black Friday week is under way, and in a year of rising tech prices, everyone’s hunting for an affordable deal on a new phone. Many retailers like Amazon, tech brands, and even phone carriers have rolled out deals. If you’re holding out for a cheap upgrade or need an impressive tech gift for someone in your life, now is a surprisingly prime time to shop.

These are the best Black Friday deals I’ve found so far. I’ve tested every major phone release this year, so I know which phones are worth your time and money, and which ones aren’t worth buying at any price. I have been and will continue to be keeping an eye on every nook and cranny of the internet to find the best smartphone deals this holiday season, so check back here for more deals as we get closer to the shopping world’s biggest day.

Before you dig into the selection of phones on sale for Black Friday, I highly recommend visiting my guide to the best phones of 2026. It includes in-depth reviews of all leading phones that launched this year—from the budget-friendly Google Pixel 10a to Apple’s newest icon, the foldable iPhone Duo.

Speaking of iPhones, keep in mind that Apple generally doesn’t put its latest phones on sale, even on Black Friday. If you’re in the market for an iPhone 18 Pro or the iPhone Duo, your best option is to check with your wireless carrier or, if you’re open to switching, one of its direct competitors. In my experience, you're most likely to get a great iPhone deal when switching service providers.

That said, most retailers offer a variety of deals on accessories for those phones, including cases and battery packs. If you know someone who’s getting a new phone, helping them get set up is probably a great gift idea, too.

So the best deals are inevitably on Android phones. And, as luck would have it, there are already some good deals on new unlocked phones from Samsung and Google. These make great gifts because you don’t have to worry about anyone’s carrier situation.

As always, be ready to add the phone you’ve been coveting to your cart if you see it priced well below MSRP, even before Black Friday. Some of the deals on the latest models are available only for a limited time. Between this factor and the possibility of a product running out of stock, I suggest acting fast once you are ready to buy.

The entire Google Pixel 11 phone lineup is already on sale. Depending on your preferences and budget, you can get any model, including the shape-shifting Pixel 11 Pro Fold, for between 10 and 30 percent off.

I think the best offers are on the Pixel 11 Pro and the Pixel 11 Pro Fold, since they’re also my favorite Android phones. You can get either one for almost 30 percent off. With what you save, you’ll have enough cash to cover a new case and maybe an extra tech upgrade, like a new Fitbit.

To my pleasant surprise, the new Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 is also on sale now. It has the same “passport”-style form factor as the iPhone Duo, and unlike Apple’s phone, you can actually get one today. If you’ve been thinking about a foldable, it’s a no-brainer upgrade at $300 off.

The Black Friday Motorola smartphone deals cover the entire 2026 Razr lineup. If you are in the market for a top-notch foldable Android, you can snag one for almost 20 percent off.

In the lower price tiers, I highly recommend the 2026 Moto G Stylus at 30 percent off. The generous discount brings it to $350, so you’ll get a lot more phone for your money.

The most notable Black Friday deal I found from other phone makers is the Nothing Phone (4a) Pro at 20 percent off its original price. When I reviewed it earlier this year, the device impressed me with its cooler design and more features than the Google Pixel 10a at the same price. For less than $400, I’d take it over any alternative.

The discounted Fairphone Gen.6+ deserves attention from anyone who wants to keep their phone for as long as possible. It’s easy to repair and backed by a robust warranty. As its name suggests, the super-affordable NXTPAPER 70 Pro Android phone by TCL, on the other hand, has an anti-glare screen that’s perfect for avid readers.
