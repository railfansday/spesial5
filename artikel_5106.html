David Lawant: Seeing “Very Strong Inflow” for Bitcoin at $60k

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

David Lawant, Head of Research at Anchorage Digital, joins BMTV to break down what's really driving Bitcoin's sell-off and who's doing the selling.

David Lawant, Head of Research at Anchorage Digital, joins BMTV to break down what’s really driving Bitcoin’s sell-off and who’s doing the selling. He says much of the drop isn’t specific to Bitcoin and has more to do with how much capital the AI trade is pulling from many parts of the market. Early on, he says, Coinbase represented up to 60% of the sell pressure, a sign of US investors pulling capital to position for Anthropic’s IPO. He says roughly $2 billion in liquidations then exacerbated the move down to 62K. Even so, he was impressed by how well Bitcoin held the low 60K level.
