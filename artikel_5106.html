Jensen Huang Steps Down: Are the Cracks Beginning to Show in Circular AI Financing

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang has resigned after the company's board met to discuss possible removal, and BMTV hosts Grace Remington and Sean Hagan walk through what that means for the AI buildout.

NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang has resigned after the company’s board met to discuss possible removal, and BMTV hosts Grace Remington and Sean Hagan walk through what that means for the AI buildout. The New York Times reported a second affair, this time with a senior executive reporting directly to him, after an earlier report of a relationship with an alleged Chinese spy. NVIDIA has lost roughly $1 trillion in market value since the first report, according to Axios, and House Republicans are threatening a subpoena over national security concerns. Sean argues the bigger issue is not key-man risk — he says plenty of people inside the company know how the sauce is made — but systematic circular financing, backstopped by chips that are devaluing over time.
