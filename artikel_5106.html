BTC Price Drops 15% as Gold and Silver Dump: Can December’s Fed Decision Turn It?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

BTC price fell 15% since Nov 5, more than gold and silver, as a December Fed hike nears 95% odds. Could this shift price action?

Gold (XAU) fell 2.5% to about $3,770 an ounce on Wednesday, November 18, as traders bet on a Federal Reserve rate hike in December. Silver dropped 3.3% to $45.70. Both metals are at their lowest since September 2025. The BTC price held near $64,600, but it has fallen even harder than the metals since the hike bets took off.

We compared the moves since November 5, the day before a strong October jobs report changed the rate outlook. Bitcoin is down 15%, silver 12.5% and gold 8.4%. Over the same days, the U.S. dollar index rose 2.2%, and the 10-year Treasury yield climbed from 4.84% to 5.10%.

Higher rates hit everything that pays no interest, and gold, silver and bitcoin all fall into that group. So the next big test is the Fed’s December 8-9 meeting.

Gold peaked near $5,320 an ounce on COMEX futures in late January. It has since lost 29%, and silver has lost 60% from its January high of $115. On Monday, gold for immediate delivery touched $3,832, and Wednesday’s drop pushed it lower still.

The reason is the Fed. Markets now treat another hike as almost certain:

A quarter-point hike would lift the Fed’s target range to 4.00%-4.25%. It would be the second this year, after September’s. Waller has gone further, saying a half-point move, or “a 50-basis-point hike in the policy rate should be considered.”

Central banks are still buying gold, 675 tonnes in the first nine months of the year. That hasn’t been enough to offset a stronger dollar and higher yields.

Some investors call bitcoin “digital gold,” but in this selloff the BTC price hasn’t acted as a hedge.

With a quarter-point hike at 95%, that move is largely expected already. For the BTC price, the surprise would be a bigger move or a pause:

The minutes of the October meeting come out today at 2 p.m. ET and could move the odds before December. In October, two officials, Lisa Cook and Beth Hammack, voted to hike when the rest voted to hold.

The BTC price stands at $64,590 on Binance, down 48% from its October 2025 record. On the daily chart, it sits below its 50-day exponential moving average (EMA), an average that gives recent prices more weight, at $75,780, and its 200-day EMA at $75,760.

Gold, silver and bitcoin are all trading as bets on the Fed right now. Until December 9, the BTC price is likely to keep moving with yields and the dollar, not against them.
