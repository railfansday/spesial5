Wall Street is entering the final weeks of 2026 with investors already assessing where the next phase of the U.S. stock market rally could emerge. Artificial intelligence remains central to corporate earnings, but rising infrastructure expenditure, valuation concerns and changing interest-rate expectations are creating opportunities across other industries. Bloomberg consensus estimates project S&P 500 earnings growth of 20.2% in 2027, with significant differences between sectors.

Technology leads the forecast, while healthcare, communication services and industrials offer additional growth opportunities. This report examines the earnings data, investment catalysts and publicly traded companies that could shape Wall Street’s sector leadership in 2027.

The S&P 500 is expected to maintain positive earnings momentum in 2027, although growth could moderate from this year’s elevated levels. Bloomberg consensus estimates published November 17 project index earnings to increase 20.2% next year, compared with 29.9% in 2026. Revenue growth is expected to slow from 13.8% to 9.1%.

The forecasts suggest that profitability will remain an important market driver, but the distribution of earnings growth could change. Information Technology, Healthcare, Communication Services, Industrials and Consumer Discretionary are all projected to deliver double-digit earnings expansion.

The moderation in revenue growth makes operating margins particularly important. Companies will need to convert sales expansion into earnings through productivity improvements, pricing power and cost discipline. A market rally supported by actual profit growth would also be more sustainable than one driven primarily by expanding valuation multiples.

Goldman Sachs expects the S&P 500 to deliver an 18% total return through the end of 2027, while forecasting 11% earnings growth. Its more conservative earnings projection highlights the uncertainty surrounding corporate profitability and AI investment returns.

Information Technology remains the strongest projected earnings-growth sector heading into 2027. Bloomberg consensus estimates indicate that sector earnings could increase 41%, following estimated growth of 64.6% in 2026. Revenue is projected to expand 29.7%, significantly above the S&P 500’s expected 9.1%.

The numbers indicate that AI-related demand could remain a significant source of corporate growth, even as the pace of expansion moderates. Semiconductor manufacturers, networking suppliers and cloud infrastructure providers are positioned to benefit from continued investment in computing capacity.

Nvidia remains central to AI accelerator demand, while Broadcom and AMD provide exposure to custom silicon, networking and data-center processors. Microsoft, Alphabet and Oracle offer additional exposure through cloud infrastructure and enterprise AI services.

However, the investment cycle is becoming more demanding. Hyperscalers are committing substantial capital to data centers, while investors increasingly want evidence that AI revenue can justify those investments. Higher depreciation, financing costs and competition could affect future margins.

Healthcare presents one of the most notable changes in the 2027 earnings outlook. Bloomberg consensus estimates project sector earnings growth of 19.6%, compared with only 2.9% in 2026. Revenue growth, however, is forecast at 4%, suggesting that improved profitability could contribute more to earnings expansion than sales growth alone.

Pharmaceutical innovation, obesity treatments, oncology drugs and medical technology remain important investment themes. Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk have established significant positions in the obesity-treatment market, while AbbVie and Merck offer exposure to specialty pharmaceuticals and oncology.

The sector’s earnings recovery could also attract investors looking to diversify away from expensive technology stocks. Nevertheless, the difference between projected earnings and revenue growth requires attention. Margin improvement, product mix and cost management will determine whether the forecast translates into stronger reported results.

Communication Services is projected to deliver 19.3% earnings growth in 2027, compared with 10.8% in 2026. Unlike several other sectors, its revenue-growth outlook is also improving, with estimates rising from 12.6% to 14.6%.

Digital advertising is a major driver of this outlook. Meta and Alphabet operate large advertising ecosystems, where AI-powered recommendations, improved targeting and automated marketing tools could increase monetization.

Netflix provides another growth avenue through subscriptions, advertising and international expansion. However, competition for advertising budgets, regulatory scrutiny and rising content expenditure could affect operating performance.

Industrials could become an important beneficiary of the AI investment cycle as spending moves beyond semiconductor manufacturing into physical infrastructure. Data centers require electrical systems, cooling equipment, power generation, construction services and transmission networks. Bloomberg consensus estimates project industrial earnings growth of 15.4% in 2027, with revenue expected to increase 9%.

The sector’s investment case extends beyond data centers. Grid modernization, manufacturing expansion and defense procurement could support industrial demand. Companies supplying electrical equipment and infrastructure services may benefit from projects that require long-term investment and specialized engineering.

GE Vernova and Eaton provide exposure to power generation and electrical distribution, while Vertiv is positioned in data-center cooling and power management. Quanta Services offers exposure to electricity transmission and grid construction.

Financials have a more moderate earnings outlook for 2027, with projected growth of 6.4%, compared with 15.2% in 2026. Revenue growth is expected to decline from 9.5% to 5.1%.

The sector’s opportunity lies in capital markets, lending, wealth management and trading activity. A stronger mergers-and-acquisitions environment could support investment banks, while stable credit conditions may benefit commercial lenders.

JPMorgan Chase remains a major diversified banking exposure, while Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley provide greater sensitivity to investment banking, trading and institutional activity. BlackRock and Charles Schwab offer exposure to asset management and brokerage.

The expansion of data centers and industrial electrification is increasing attention on electricity generation and grid infrastructure. Nuclear power, transmission equipment and energy storage could become important long-term investment themes. However, the earnings forecasts show that rising structural demand does not automatically translate into immediate sector-wide profit growth.

Energy faces a difficult comparison after exceptionally strong projected earnings growth in 2026. Oil and gas prices, production levels and refining margins will remain important drivers.

Utilities have a different investment profile. Rising electricity consumption could support long-term demand, but infrastructure expansion requires substantial capital and can expose companies to financing costs and regulatory constraints.

Materials earnings are projected to increase 9.3% in 2027, compared with 31% in 2026. Revenue growth is expected to moderate from 7.7% to 4.4%.

Copper is an important investment theme because it is used in electricity transmission, data centers, manufacturing and electrification. Demand from infrastructure development could support mining companies, although supply additions, production costs and global industrial activity will influence prices.

Gold provides a different exposure. Central-bank purchases, geopolitical uncertainty and monetary conditions can influence demand, making gold producers relevant to investors seeking exposure to precious metals.

The Bloomberg consensus estimates reveal a clear gap between the leading and lagging sectors. Information Technology is projected to generate more than twice the earnings growth of the broader S&P 500, while Healthcare and Communication Services are expected to deliver the strongest improvement from their 2026 growth rates.

AI Capital Expenditure Could Outpace Revenue Growth

Technology companies are committing substantial capital to AI infrastructure. If spending rises faster than monetization, depreciation and financing expenses could weaken margins. The market may increasingly reward companies demonstrating measurable AI revenue rather than those simply increasing infrastructure expenditure.

Higher Bond Yields Could Pressure Equity Valuations

Interest rates remain central to the market outlook. Higher Treasury yields can increase corporate borrowing costs and reduce the present value of future earnings, particularly for companies whose valuations depend on long-term growth expectations.

Earnings Estimates Leave Less Room for Disappointment

With S&P 500 earnings projected to grow 20.2% in 2027, companies face demanding expectations. A slowdown in revenue, weaker margins or disappointing corporate guidance could trigger valuation adjustments across sectors.

The broader market’s ability to sustain its rally will depend on whether earnings growth spreads across more companies and industries. Investors will need to distinguish between structural demand and actual profitability, particularly in sectors requiring significant capital expenditure.

As 2027 approaches, the strongest investment opportunities could emerge from companies combining above-market earnings growth with sustainable revenue, improving margins and disciplined capital allocation.
