Bitcoin ETF Inflows Return After Seven-Day Losing Streak as IBIT Adds $76.5M: Is the Sell-Off Finally Over?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Spot Bitcoin ETFs record $7.9M in inflows after seven days of outflows, as BlackRock’s IBIT leads the recovery.

In the latest Bitcoin news, US spot Bitcoin ETFs took in a net $7.9 million on Monday, November 16, ending seven straight days of outflows. BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) did all the buying, with $76.5 million, while most other funds kept losing money.

It’s a thin green day after a heavy run of selling. By our count, the funds lost about $2.99 billion over the 11 sessions from October 30 to November 13. Over the same stretch, Bitcoin fell about 20%, and it trades near $65,000 on Tuesday.

The last time the ETFs turned green, on November 4, the next day brought $430 million of outflows and a 5.5% drop. So is Monday a turning point for BTC, or are more tests coming?

Monday’s inflow came from a single fund. BlackRock’s IBIT took in $76.5 million, its first inflow since October 29. It had lost money for 11 sessions in a row before that, about $2.35 billion in total.

Monday’s $7.9 million is also small next to the funds’ history. Since they launched in January 2024, they have taken in $54.7 billion, about $76.5 million on an average day.

On the daily chart, Bitcoin trades at $64,974. It sits below its 50-day exponential moving average (EMA), an average that gives recent prices more weight, at $76,233, and its 200-day EMA at $75,871.

Monday’s inflow is welcome Bitcoin news, but one small green day does not make a trend. Watch the next few sessions: a few days of steady IBIT buying with BTC back above $65,500 would make the case for a turn. Another red day after Nvidia’s report would mean more tests are coming.
