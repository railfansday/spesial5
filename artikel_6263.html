We Keep Going Back To Germany. This Family Trip Reminded Me Why.

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

We landed in Zürich on SAS and drove to my in-law’s home in Southwest Germany, about an hour away.

There are border checks now as you enter Germany from France and Switzerland, though usually (I’m just saying…) when they see it’s white people they just waive you through.

And I’ll leave off the politics here (because I’d rather focus on getting our own house in order in the USA), but I think this picture says it all about Germany these days:

We tend to stop at DM (a drug store chain) at the start of each trip because it’s on the way home and we always come with a list since so many products here are cheaper and better than their American counterparts, even with the stronger Euro. One thing I like about DM (well, liked…) was that they had a free water dispenser with cups.

In Germany water is an annoyance when going out…most restaurants and coffee shops frown on tap water (unlike in France and the Netherlands), so you’re paying everywhere €5-8EUR for bottles you can buy for 50-80 Euro cents in the grocery store. It’s nice to “hydrate” at DM, but for TWO YEARS the water machine has been broken.

How in the heck can you not fix this for two years (unless, as I suppose, the point is that you don’t want to offer free water any longer)?!

It was a bit warm for the time of the year, but not unpleasant. How nice it was to be back and over the next several days we enjoyed a lot of quality time together, with good döner, coffee, and ice cream.

There was a fairly intense lighting storm that was just beautiful to sit out on the patio and watch:

One morning I decided to ride by myself to Basel for coffee…there was a new place I wanted to try called Mantila Coffee…it was SO GOOD (my new favorite coffee spot in Basel) and I had such a nice chat with the owner. I’m going to update my Basel Coffee post with a couple of places I tried on this trip.

Augustine was livid that biked to Basel without him, so we returned the next day (sadly Mantila is closed on Saturday), but we found another great place called Tinka. Augustine wanted his own coffee…I had a cortado, he had cappuccino, and then we split a delicious sparkling blood orange drink.

My father-in-law likes a restaurant called Gasthaus Krone in the hamlet of Lehnacker, located really right in the middle of the Schwarzwald (Black Forest). The food is delicious indeed.

One cool part of this trip is that my daughter finally got the hang of riding her bike herself…we just don’t feel it’s safe to ride bikes at home (due to fast drivers…see here for a laugh) but it was fun to see her so excited.

To celebrate, we had (more) ice cream together.
