Conor Bradley's Liverpool comeback delayed in new twist

Liverpool are planning for Conor Bradley to make a return to action in January as he edges closer to a full recovery, sources have told Football Insider.

Bradley returned to team training over the international break for the first time since he suffered a major knee injury at the start of the year.

He went down in agony during Liverpool's goalless draw at Arsenal in January and subsequently underwent surgery to repair both bone and ligament damage.

The 23-year-old right-back has been working away in the background, and despite being spotted in training, fans must not get overly excited about his return just yet.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Liverpool's rigorous recovery plan will likely see Bradley make his first senior appearance of the campaign after the turn of the year.

Ronald Araujo and Jeremie Frimpong will both continue to share right-back duties in his absence.

Araujo has impressed this season, but Bradley retains hopes to make the Reds' right-back position his own in the years to come.

The 23-year-old must avoid more of the long-term problems that have plagued his short Reds career so far, with nearly 100 club and country games missed through injury in the last three full seasons.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I think Liverpool will be careful with Conor Bradley. They don’t want to take any risks with him. Obviously, it will take him a number of weeks to regain full match fitness and sharpness.

"[There is] no exact time-frame on his comeback return date, but I would imagine they are probably looking at around January 2027. They'll wait until the new year, and then obviously if Bradley can get some minutes under his belt, he could be a huge extra option for Iraola going forward."

Iraola is understandably upbeat about Bradley's injury status, with Italian defender Giovanni Leoni also pictured alongside him in training for the first time since he tore his ACL in September 2025.

Addressing the media this week about their respective upcoming returns, he said: "Very good news from both sides. I think the international break was the best moment for us to try to introduce them slowly, slowly with the rest of the players. They have done this and now even in the last part of the week they have already started doing things with the team.

“Obviously they are still not available but it’s very good news that they are able to do things with the group. It’s true that they have to be loaded carefully – they don’t train every day with us.

"One day, yes. The next day, not. The next day, yes. We are in the last part of this process, a long process. But both of them are looking good and I hope they can play in some weeks.”
