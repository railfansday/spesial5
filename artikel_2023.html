Bruno Guimaraes returned to Newcastle United with Arsenal.

Some three and a half months after leaving in early August, the former Newcastle United captain and talisman returns to St James’ Park.

No surprise that his return has dominated the pre-match headlines.

Much of that concentrating on how the Newcastle United fans will react to the return of Bruno Guimaraes in opposition colours.

This is my perfect Bruno Guimaraes scenario to be played out today.

Named amongst the Arsenal subs, the ex-NUFC player during first half warms up at side of pitch

The ex-NUFC player during second half warms up at side of pitch

With Newcastle 1-0 up, the former NUFC captain comes off the bench with 20 minutes left

The score still 1-0, the Brazil international wins a penalty in the final seconds

The Brazil international insists on taking the penalty himself

The Arsenal player does his trademark stutter run-up and trips up, the ball only moves a couple of inches, the final whistle blows immediately and United have won 1-0

A standing ovation for our former captain and hero, Bruno Guimaraes cries.
