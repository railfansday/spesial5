When it comes to England Caps, the Newcastle United running total has grown further.

Although not selected when the England squad was named after recently returning from injury with United, Dan Burn responding to Thomas Tuchel’s call when Marc Guehi was forced to pull out of the squad for the November 2026 internationals.

Thursday night saw Dan Burn pick up his 12th England cap as he started against Croatia as England won 2-1.

Meanwhile Lewis Hall was awarded his 8th cap as he also played the full match.

Lewis Hall the best player on the pitch and Dan Burn also very good, as England fans voted the pair of them the two best performers on the night.

From 1901 up to the present day, these last 125 years, it now means a total of 345 England caps have been picked up so far by 44 different Newcastle United players (whilst they were also playing for NUFC).

An incredible eight different Newcastle United players played for England during the time Eddie Howe was in charge at NUFC, with seven of them signed by him.

Under Eddie Howe there was almost 70 England caps won by Newcastle United players whilst also playing for NUFC, Lewis Hall and Dan Buen now responsible for the first few under Matthias Jaissle.
