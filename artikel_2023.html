Birmingham City urged to 'say no' to ex-Celtic star and eye Leeds United, Ipswich Town transfers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Birmingham City are in the chase for a place in the Premier League, but one of the players they've been connected with for the January transfer window is giving FLW's Birmingham correspondent doubts.

Following a disappointing 1-0 reversal at Stoke City last weekend, Birmingham City got themselves back on track with a 2-0 win at struggling Burnley on Tuesday night. The Blues are in fourth place in the Championship, and the race for a coveted place in the Premier League is looking extremely tight, with just three points separating the top five sides in the division.

The January transfer window offers a final opportunity to tweak squads for the second half of the season, but this could mean departures as well as arrivals, and one player who's been linked with a move away from St Andrew's in the new year is winger Carlos Vicente, who's been reported to be of interest to the Spanish club Sevilla.

In the event that this should come to pass, Birmingham will have a gap to fill in that position, and one name who has been put forward is that of the Israeli international Liel Abada, who's currently playing in MLS for Charlotte FC. With a place in the Premier League worth a minimum of £200 million potentially at stake, it's an important window for the Blues.

The decisions to be made in the January transfer window could be highly consequential for Birmingham City, so Football League World have been in contact with our Blues fan pundit Jason Moore for his assessment over a potential replacement for Carlos Vicente.

Jason has doubts over whether Abada is the right man for the job: "If that's the replacement for Vicente, I'd say no. He played in the Scottish league but how many players from the Scottish league are really good nowadays? We got Furuhashi, who played in that league, and he was absolutely shocking for us."

Jason feels that Birmingham can do better than Abada, if they do have to part ways with Vicente: "I don't think the Scottish league is quite up to scratch any more. In the nicest way, he's playing out in MLS and while there are some good players in MLS, I think for the calibre of player we're looking at, I'd rather we were looking elsewhere. If we were going to lose Vicente, I'd be wanting something a bit better."

But there is one Championship player who Jason believes could be a good replacement for Vicente, and he wants his club to show some ambition in the January window: "For me, I'd want to get an out and out winger who's pure pace but has got a bit more of a pedigree. I wouldn't mind getting Ramazani from Burnley, get him off Leeds permanently, because I think there's a player there, I think he looks really good, and then get Mehmeti as well; make a real statement in January if we're still there or thereabouts near the top of the league, just go for it. And if we lose Vicente, I'd love someone like Ramazani."

Birmingham City have reignited their interest in former Celtic winger Liel Abada to join Wolves in the race for his signature this winter

With 16 appearances so far in 2026-27, of which 10 have been starts, Carlos Vicente has been an integral part of the Birmingham City squad this season, and the appeal of a move to La Liga for a Spanish player is extremely clear.

Should Sevilla make their move and Vicente be open to it, a space will open up in the Blues first-team squad which needs filling before the end of the month, and Liel Abada's numbers are strong. He's a regular in the Charlotte FC team, and has made 28 appearances for them so far throughout their 2026 season, of which 20 have been starts, and he has scored 11 goals and contributed five assists.

But while the former Spurs manager Ange Postecoglou once described Abada as "outstanding", the winger has no experience of playing in the English leagues. And this may make Largie Ramazani of interest to them. Ramazani has been one of the few bright spots of a dismal first half of the season on loan at Burnley from Leeds United, with five goals and one assist in 14 appearances for the Clarets so far in 2026-27. He started his career in the Manchester United academy and also made 31 appearances, scoring seven goals, for Leeds United in their promotion-winning 2024-25 season.

The upcoming January transfer window is likely to be an extremely busy, given that there remain so many clubs who, with a good run of results, put themselves in contention for a place in the Premier League. With the limitless ambition of the club's owners and it having been 16 years since they were last there, Birmingham City have a huge opportunity this season, but this makes it crucial that they get their decisions right in the new year.
