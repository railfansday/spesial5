Key dates have now been made public for the 2027-28 Premier League season.

An official announcement made on Thursday.

The first match of the 2027-28 Premier League season will be played on Friday 13 August 2027.

The statement confirming that this gives 75 clear days from the final round of Premier League matches on Sunday 30 May 2027.

The 38th and final round of games in the 2027-28 Premier League season will be played on Sunday 21 May 2028. The statement confirming that as usual, all ten matches will kick off at the same time on that final day of the season.

The official Premier League statement revealing: ‘The 2027/28 Season will consist of 33 weekends and five midweek match rounds, and will conclude one week before the UEFA Champions League Final.’

It then goes on to say: ‘During the Christmas and New Year period, no two match rounds will take place within 60 hours. This aligns with commitments made to clubs to address the congested schedule across the festive period.’
