Liverpool should pull out all the stops to get a new contract with Trey Nyoni over the line during the international break.

The 19-year-old's current terms run until 2030, with a string of clubs undoubtedly ready to pounce if any problems emerge.

The midfielder has broken into Andoni Iraola's team this season and is rapidly emerging among the most exciting young players in the Premier League.

Nyoni, who made his debut in the 2023-24 season, has even been preferred to Ryan Gravenberch of late, and could well take his position on a long-term basis.

Liverpool will be keen to protect the England youth international, however, with his minutes being managed.

The Reds also need to make a contract decision on Alexis Mac Allister, who has less than two years left on his current deal despite featuring heavily this season.

Nyoni was largely forced to sit on the bench and wait for his opportunities at the start of the season.

But his display against Tottenham in the Carabao Cup seemed to prove his readiness, with Iraola clearly having liked what he saw.

The teenager has been heavily involved since, with the ultimate show of faith coming when he started against champions Arsenal at Anfield earlier this month.

And rightly so, with Nyoni's stats backing up just how impressive he has been since making the step up to senior football.

In the 224 minutes the midfielder has featured for so far in the Premier League, he is averaging a 90.9 per cent pass success rate and 18.5 line-breaking passes.

Even more impressively, Nyoni is winning 4.02 duels, 2.81 tackles and 2.81 interceptions per game.

His 3.62 clearances per game is in the 95th percentile of Premier League midfielders, with the 19-year-old very much making a splash.

Liverpool have often struggled for midfield depth in recent years, but it appears that the solution to their woes has come from within.

In fact, Nyoni has been so impressive that he may even push Gravenberch away from the club - a player who was considered among the Premier League's best just a season ago.

"I think he has done very well. Really well. It’s good for the young players especially to live these experiences in cup competitions, where if you lose you are out," Iraola said about Nyoni in October.

"We have been losing and then the draw. Then they haven’t been there in the penalty shootout but they lived the feelings.

"They played under pressure against a very good team. And Trey has finished physically in a good place, so well done, yes.”
