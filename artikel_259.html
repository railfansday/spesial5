Amazon’s early Black Friday promos are already underway, and we’ll be highlighting some of our favorites over the next few days on our Shopping page. Among them is the Fanttik S1 Pro Cordless Electric Screwdriver, which is currently matching its all-time low of $39.99 ($40 off) at Amazon for a limited time.

Fanttik’s rechargeable tool is designed to make everyday household repairs and DIY projects easier, and it does a good job. Verge commerce editor Brandon Widder once called it his go-to electric screwdriver, noting he’s used it to assemble a bed, tighten bike seats, and perform other small- to medium-sized tasks around the house. It offers three adjustable torque settings, so you can use a gentler setting for more delicate jobs like electronics and more power for tougher projects.

Beyond being a capable electric screwdriver, the Fanttik also comes equipped with a few extra conveniences. For example, it features a built-in LED light to help you see screws in darker spaces, such as under a desk or inside a cabinet. The screwdriver also comes with 16 S2 hardened steel bits, including flathead and Torx bits, all of which fit inside an included magnetic storage case. The rechargeable 2,000mAh battery should handle up to 340 screws on a single charge, too, and it supports USB-C so you can quickly charge it between projects.
