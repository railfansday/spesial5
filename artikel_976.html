Arsenal willing to make sales to beat Chelsea and Liverpool to Scott signing

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Premier League champions Arsenal would require a sale to accommodate Alex Scott in their midfield and it is understood they would be willing to make a change in that department to beat rivals Chelsea and Liverpool to the 23-year-old's signature with Bournemouth demanding up to £100m

By Rob Dorsett, James Savundra and Mark McAdam

Arsenal are one of three top Premier League clubs interested in signing Bournemouth and England midfielder Alex Scott.

Sky Sports News understands the Premier League champions would be willing to make changes in midfield to beat Chelsea and Liverpool to the 23-year-old's signature.

Discussions about Arsenal's 2027 transfer plans are believed to have taken place between manager Mikel Arteta and sporting director Andrea Berta already.

It is understood they would need to sell at least one of their current midfielders to facilitate a deal for Scott in January or next summer.

With Declan Rice, Bruno Guimaraes and Myles Lewis-Skelly all doing so well this season, Mikel Merino is arguably the most likely candidate having struggled for game time. He has made only one Premier League start and said last week while on Spain duty that he wants more minutes.

His club and country team-mate Martin Zubimendi could be another candidate, although illness and injury have impacted his minutes so far this campaign and he has impressed recently when he has played. There is still a long way to go in the season and any injury to a key midfielder could impact Arsenal's ideas.

Merino said while on Spain duty: "We know what our roles are, and we're both calm and eager to keep training hard to show that we can continue to be there." But he also said: "Not being on the pitch most of the time isn't ideal for a player. Arsenal is currently the best team in the world, and it's very difficult to get minutes."

Scott is likely to be one of the biggest names potentially on the January transfer market, with Bournemouth believed to have softened their stance on the idea of a sale, having been unable to agree a new contract with the 23-year-old.

However, they will still command a high fee for the England international, hoping for close to £100m including add-ons. Sources have indicated something around £75m to £80m plus bonuses could get a deal done.

Bournemouth are also going strongly this season however, including in the Europa League. Should that continue, they may feel keeping Scott until next summer would give them a better chance of going far in the competition and securing European football again for next season.

Chelsea also remain persistent with their interest in Scott, and they are believed to have the financial edge on other clubs - but he is not their only target.

Manager Xabi Alonso remains open to signing another midfielder after the departure of Enzo Fernandez in the summer, although Romeo Lavia's improvement has lessened the necessity, after seeing a deadline-day deal for Lamine Camara of Monaco fall down in the summer.

Liverpool are keen to add to their midfield options in the longer term, and they see Scott as the perfect option to add some guile and passing ability to their squad.

Scott was a key part of Andoni Iraola's system at Bournemouth before the Spanish coach moved to Anfield. He remains a big admirer and there is a wider belief at Liverpool that he could also be a key tactical piece in Iraola's plans going forward.

Scott will have only 12 months left on his existing deal next summer, at which point his value will reduce.

Arsenal are continuing to track the likes of RB Leipzig's Antonio Nusa and Brentford's Kevin Schade as they assess potential forward options ahead of improving their squad in 2027.

Adding a marquee name to their attack was one of the objectives Arsenal were unable to tick-off during the summer window, failing to agree deals for Morgan Rogers, Vinicius Jr, Bradley Barcola and Julian Alvarez.

With Alvarez not keen to leave Spain, the Gunners are believed to be moving on from their pursuit of the Argentina star and assessing again how to improve in attack, primarily on the left side but with someone versatile enough to operate across the frontline.

A move for Nusa is now something Arsenal are keen to progress with, sources have told Sky Sports News, but he is not the only potential target they are monitoring.

Sky Sports News revealed their admiration for Schade last month amid the forward's talks with Brentford over a new contract.

Nusa, who has two-and-a-half years left on his Leipzig deal, has no exit clause so a deal would need to be negotiated - and Leipzig do not want to sell him January come-what-may, according to other sources, so a move next summer seems more feasible as things stand.

Nusa actually almost signed for Brentford in the summer they brought in Schade from Freiburg.

Nicknamed the Norwegian Neymar after his childhood hero, Nusa is an international team-mate of Arsenal captain Martin Odegaard and has been on the radar of Premier League clubs for some time.

He is now into his third season and producing his best performances to date. The 21-year-old has directly contributed to 14 goals in just 15 games in all competitions this season, with six goals and eight assists.

One of his goals was a Champions League strike against Manchester City earlier this month and this season's efforts add to the 10 goals and 11 assists he recorded across his first two seasons at the German club.
