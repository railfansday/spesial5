Nacua handed three-match suspension by NFL for policy violation

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Puka Nacua won't miss any games that he wouldn't already miss because of injury; Nacua is eligible to return both from IR and his suspension when the Rams (6-4) host the Dallas Cowboys on December 20; watch the NFL throughout the season live on Sky Sports

Los Angeles Rams receiver Puka Nacua has been suspended for three games for violating the NFL's personal conduct policy.

The suspension begins immediately and Nacua will be eligible for reinstatement on December 14, following the Rams' Week 14 game against the San Francisco 49ers.

The All-Pro receiver won't be paid during his suspension but, thanks to the timing of his suspension, won't miss any games because he is currently out due to knee and toe injuries.

Nacua went on injured reserve last week and he is eligible to return from both IR and his suspension when the Rams (6-4) host the Dallas Cowboys on December 20.

The Rams and Nacua have been awaiting his probable suspension for nearly a year. The suspension of Nacua stems from an incident on New Year's Eve in 2025, where he allegedly made an antisemitic remark and bit a woman.

The accuser filed a civil lawsuit on March 25 against Nacua for assault and battery, while Nacua's attorney denied his client made any such statements and described the biting as "horseplay."

Nacua entered a holistic care facility earlier that month, before he was sued, the concluded his time in the rehab facility in time to be part of Los Angeles' off-season program in mid-April.
