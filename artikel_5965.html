What If Democrats Don’t Succumb to Disarray?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Even with the left’s infighting, the GOP should not discount the possibility that a lasting majority party could be forged.

House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries speaks alongside Senate Democratic Leader Chuck Schumer (left) during an event with congressional Democrats in Washington, D.C., May 21, 2026.

The emerging conventional wisdom now holds that while Democrats have a momentous political opportunity on their hands, they will probably mess it up. The Democratic Party will “get in their own way,” succumb to “infighting,” and, when the victory parties subside, revert to a general and familiar state of “disarray.”

House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries speaks alongside Senate Democratic Leader Chuck Schumer (left) during an event with congressional Democrats in Washington, D.C., May 21, 2026.
