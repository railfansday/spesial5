pliavo’s portable piano folds 84 keys accordion-style into a compact block

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

an accordion-like system of hinges allows musicians to compress an entire seven-octave keyboard into a block just over 6 inches long.

Denver-based Pliavo introduces its Fold-and-Go Piano, a portable digital instrument whose 84 keys collapse into a compact bundle small enough to carry inside a backpack. Each octave occupies its own rigid section, and a system of hinges allows the otherwise standard black-and-white keyboard to fold accordion-style into a vertical stack.

At its full length, the seven-octave instrument stretches 113 centimeters (44.5 inches) across a tabletop. Once folded, it measures just 16 centimeters (6.3 inches) along its length and weighs under two pounds. The team has also developed a smaller, five-octave edition with 60 keys.

Part of the appeal of Pliavo’s design is that it retains the physical geometry of a conventional keyboard. While many compact electronic pianos take the form of flexible silicone mats, here the musician plays across a continuous arrangement of rigid keys with three millimeters of vertical travel.

The mechanics of the folding design come with some compromises. The keys are unweighted and lack velocity sensitivity, which limits the expressive range of the instrument. Pliavo equips the piano with a detachable wireless speaker and a rechargeable battery designed to last around five hours.

The piano’s internal sound library comprises 128 instrument tones alongside an equally extensive selection of rhythm patterns. Through Bluetooth or a USB-C connection, the keyboard doubles as a MIDI controller. For a musician traveling with a laptop, an entire seven-octave workspace could occupy a small corner of their luggage.

Pliavo launched the instrument on Kickstarter in November 2026 and has already exceeded its initial funding goal. The team has developed a playable prototype, with refinements to its sound and finishes underway ahead of an anticipated February 2027 delivery.
