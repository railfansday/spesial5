Big XRP Holders Add $72 Million While Supply Hardens

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Home » Ripple (XRP) » Big XRP Holders Add $72 Million While Supply Hardens

Big-time holders draw attention to XRP’s liquidity pockets sub-$1: is a bounce back signal getting delivered?

Large XRP holders have added roughly $72 million worth of tokens in recent days, even as major Bitcoin and Ethereum investors have reduced exposure during the broader market pullback.

The buying has coincided with a drop in available XRP on Binance, where reserves have reportedly fallen below levels last seen in July.

The move does not yet signal a clear bullish reversal. XRP was trading near $0.97, down about 3.6% over the previous week and still below its 200-day moving average near $1.26.

The combination of whale accumulation and thinner exchange balances has drawn attention to whether modest returning demand could produce a sharper price response.

An exchange supply-shortage measure for XRP rose to 2.4 on November 19, 2026 above its July reading of 2.09. Usually, readings above 2 are generally associated with unusually limited immediately available supply on the venue.

#XRP supply on Binance is getting tight. The Supply Shortage Index spiked above 8 in November and sits at 2.4 now, while price is still near $0.96.Supply is tightening and price has not responded yet. pic.twitter.com/xMTksTkKim

Large wallets also pulled more than $127 million in XRP from Binance on a net basis between November 10 and 16, 2026.

Some of those movements may reflect internal exchange reserve management rather than pure investor withdrawals, particularly after operational disruption at the platform, so the flows are not a definitive accumulation signal on their own.

Lower balances can still matter if spot demand returns. Fewer tokens sitting on exchanges may reduce readily available selling pressure, especially if a portion of the withdrawn XRP is moving into longer-term storage rather than being repositioned for immediate trading elsewhere.

US spot XRP ETFs have gone 15 consecutive trading days without a net inflow, their longest dry spell since launch. Since their last positive day on October 29, the funds have recorded net withdrawals of about $26.3 million.

Total XRP ETF assets have fallen to roughly $1.08 billion from $1.77 billion in late September, though cumulative inflows since launch remain close to $1.78 billion. Existing holders have not abandoned the products in large numbers, but fresh capital has paused.

The divergence is the central feature of the current setup: large holders have been willing to buy into weakness, while ETF demand has stayed on the sidelines.

Tighter exchange supply could amplify a recovery if broader buying returns, but without renewed fund inflows and a reclaim of key technical levels, scarcity alone is unlikely to reverse the trend.
