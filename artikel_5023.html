BitMine’s $655 million Ethereum hit wipes out nearly three years of staking income

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The company's 6.09 million ETH treasury remains highly exposed to price swings, with a $40 decline enough to offset an entire year's projected staking revenue.

BitMine's Ethereum (ETH) holdings lost about $655 million in market value in one week, even as the company bought 7,819 more ETH.

The decline equals 2.67 years of its projected $245 million in annual staking revenue. Two SEC exhibits show the position moving from 6,077,745 ETH worth over $11 billion on Nov. 15 to 6,085,564 ETH worth $10.4 billion on Nov. 22.

The $655 million in Ethereum is a mark-to-market move between two snapshots, a paper valuation of an unsold position at reference prices. The price drop cost $668.5 million on the original holdings, while the new tokens added roughly $13.4 million at the Nov. 22 reference price, about 2% of the loss.

The releases omit the prices BitMine paid, so $13.4 million reflects the new tokens' value at the latest reference price.

BitMine has staked 5,227,037 ETH, about 86% of its holdings and the same quantity as a week earlier. Management projects $245 million in annualized staking revenue on that balance.

A $40.26 move in ETH (2.35%) equals a full year of that projected revenue in treasury value, so the week's 6% drop was more than twice the threshold. At the Nov. 22 balance, every $100 move in Ethereum moves the treasury's marked value by about $608.6 million.

BitMine added 7,819 ETH during Nov. 15–22, 2026. The lower ETH reference price outweighed the value of the additional tokens.

Staking rewards arrive in ETH, so a lower price shrinks their dollar value.

Chairman Tom Lee used the Nov. 23 announcement to argue that Ethereum deserves recognition as hard money, citing annualized supply growth of 0.9% for ETH and 0.82% for Bitcoin since June 30, 2025.

Bitcoin follows a fixed halving schedule toward 21 million coins, while Ethereum issues new ETH to validators and burns ETH through fees under EIP-1559, so its net supply varies with network activity.

A similar recent rate describes one period, and the next period can differ. BitMine holds about 4.98% of Ethereum's 122.2 million circulating tokens, a concentration in one volatile asset.

The same annual filing reports about $13.6 billion in gross equity offerings and acknowledges that repeated issuance could dilute existing shareholders.

A larger Ethereum balance leaves ETH per share open, and that measure needs diluted-share data the weekly releases omit.

If Ethereum recovers, the same exposure works in BitMine's favor. At about $1,821, the current balance restores the Nov. 15 value of $11 billion, a 6.3% recovery from $1,713.

An illustrative move to $2,000 would lift the treasury to about $12.2 billion, a $1.75 billion gain, and raise projected staking revenue to roughly $286 million at a constant 2.74% ETH yield.

If ETH slides to an illustrative $1,500, the treasury falls to about $9.1 billion, a $1.3 billion loss, and projected staking revenue drops to roughly $215 million. BitMine would then carry lower asset values and lower dollar income together.

The annual filing warns that treasury-company equities can move far more than the underlying token, illustrating a 20% ETH correction alongside a 50% equity drawdown.

Both scenarios hold ETH at 6,085,564, staked ETH at 5,227,037, and the yield constant, and they exclude purchases, expenses, and dilution.

Lee's scarcity argument covers supply, and the Nov. 23 filing shows that Ethereum's price determines what the treasury is worth.
