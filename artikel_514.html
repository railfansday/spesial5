Anthropic IPO Arrives at a Pivotal AI Moment

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Bloomberg News senior equities reporter Bailey Lipschultz and Bloomberg Intelligence’s Mandeep Singh are on Bloomberg This Weekend to examine Anthropic’s potentially record-setting IPO and how Nvidia’s upheaval following Jensen Huang’s departure is changing the outlook for AI investment and computing capacity. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, they discuss Anthropic’s roughly $1.4 trillion targeted valuation, investor demand and compute costs, as well as what its market debut could signal for OpenAI and the broader AI trade.
