The Pokémon TCG Delta Reign Booster Display Box hits best-ever price at Amazon — save $15

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Delta Reign Booster Display Box is already on sale for $239.94 at Amazon, down from its $254.93 list price.

The Pokémon TCG Delta Reign expansion has only been out for a few days, but Amazon sellers have wasted little time pushing its 36-pack Booster Display Box down to a record-low price.

As of Nov. 9, the Pokémon TCG Delta Reign Booster Display Box has dropped to $239.94 at Amazon. That's a $14.99 cut from its usual $254.93 price. With that extra 6% off, you're effectively paying $6.67 per pack of the newly released expansion centered around Mega Rayquaza.

That’s an extra $3.05 below the previous record-low $242.99 preorder deal we spotted on Nov. 5, and a full $30.05 cheaper than when Amazon preorders first opened at $269.99 back in October.

According to price tracker camelcamelcamel, this is the lowest price on Amazon so far. The Delta Reign Booster Display Box is now only $30.54 above its $209.94 market price — determined by dedicated trading card marketplace TCGplayer.

It’s also important to note that this particular offer comes courtesy of third-party seller Rare Goods & Deals, with free shipping and delivery estimated for Nov. 19. If you want yours even faster, you can pay just a cent more for Amazon's main $239.95 listing, sold by TCG & MORE, with delivery currently scheduled for Nov. 17.
