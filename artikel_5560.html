Sanofi is buying the biotech Insmed for $41 billion, the companies said Tuesday, the largest biopharma acquisition in years and one that will give the French firm two new approved drugs.

The purchase, which includes another drug in Phase 3 trials, reflects the pressure Sanofi has been under as it faces looming revenue losses from its massive blockbuster Dupixent, an inflammation-targeting drug approved in nine indications. The company, where Belén Garijo took over as CEO earlier this year, had been vocal that it was looking for takeover targets with late-stage or even commercial assets that fit with its existing focus in immunology and rare diseases.

Sanofi is paying $175 per share for New Jersey-based Insmed, through a combination of debt financing and cash. Insmed’s share price was roughly $120 before Bloomberg reported last week that the deal was in the works, which sent the stock up.
