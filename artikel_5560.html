Pyramid Systems teams with Bell Federal Systems to bring AIR-Quire to NASA SEWP VI

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Following SEWP VI ordering activation, agencies may request eligible AIR-Quire solutions and related services through Bell Federal's NASA SEWP VI Category C prime contract, subject to applicable ordering requirements.

FAIRFAX, Va., Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Pyramid Systems, Inc., an employee-owned federal technology company, is teaming with Bell Federal Systems, Inc. (BFSI), a NASA SEWP VI Category C prime contract holder, to offer AIR-Quireâ¢ to federal agencies through NASA's Solutions for Enterprise-Wide Procurement (SEWP) VI program.

Through this teaming, eligible agencies will have a pathway to request AIR-Quire-related solutions and services under Bell Federal's Category C prime contract, subject to SEWP VI scope and ordering requirements.

Under the teaming arrangement, Bell Federal Systems Inc. (BFSI) will serve as the NASA SEWP VI Category C prime contractor (contract number: 80TECH26D1234), leading prime-contract coordination and engagement with federal agency customers. Pyramid Systems will provide AIR-Quire technical expertise, product information, implementation, and related support. The companies will collaborate on customer requirements, proposed solutions, pricing, and responses to applicable SEWP VI requests for quotation. Any resulting orders are subject to agency procurement procedures, applicable contract requirements, and award decisions. The specific responsibilities of each company will be governed by their applicable written agreements.

Susie Field, President and Chief Operating Officer, Pyramid Systems"Contracting offices are being asked to finish more acquisitions with the same people. We built AIR-Quire to take on the drafting and checking, so their teams can put more of their time into the decisions. Teaming with Bell Federal gives agencies a familiar way to buy it, and we look forward to working with Adrian and his team."

Adrian Bell, President, Bell Federal Systems"As a NASA SEWP VI Category C prime contractor, Bell Federal Systems is focused on connecting federal agencies with technology that strengthens mission performance. Our teaming with Pyramid Systems brings its AIR-Quire acquisition technology together with our federal contracting experience. We look forward to helping agencies evaluate solutions that make acquisition processes more efficient while keeping people accountable for critical decisions."

About Pyramid SystemsPyramid Systems is an employee-owned federal technology company founded in 1995 and based in Fairfax, Virginia. Its AIR-Quire platform is an AI-native system of intelligence for federal acquisition. Learn more at www.pyramidsystems.com.

About Bell Federal SystemsBell Federal Systems Inc. (BFSI) is a U.S. small business federal government contractor specializing in information technology, professional services, acquisition support, and mission-focused solutions.
