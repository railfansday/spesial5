Digital Information Resources Announces Launch of CoffeeBarista.com Amid Growing Demand for Specialty Coffee and Home Brewing

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

New digital media platform aims to help coffee enthusiasts explore brewing techniques, equipment, and coffee culture as U.S. coffee consumption reaches record levels.

NEW YORK, Nov. 6, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Digital Information Resources (DIR), a technology and digital media company that builds, acquires, and operates niche online properties, today announced the launch of CoffeeBarista.com, a new digital destination dedicated to coffee, brewing techniques, equipment, and the growing culture surrounding one of the world's most popular beverages.

The launch comes as coffee consumption remains a central part of American life. According to the National Coffee Association's Fall 2026 National Coffee Data Trends report, 66% of American adults drink coffee daily, while 48% consumed specialty coffee in the past day, a new record. Additionally, 83% of past-day coffee drinkers consume coffee prepared at home, highlighting the continued importance of home brewing.

CoffeeBarista.com aims to serve this growing audience by providing accessible information about coffee preparation, brewing methods, equipment, and the broader coffee industry. The platform is designed to appeal to everyone from casual coffee drinkers to enthusiasts interested in improving their at-home coffee experience.

"Coffee is much more than a daily beverage. It's a global culture with an incredibly passionate and engaged community," said Michael Gursha, Co-Founder and CEO of Digital Information Resources. "With CoffeeBarista.com, we're building a destination that makes coffee knowledge more accessible, whether someone is learning to brew their first espresso or exploring new techniques and equipment. We see tremendous opportunity to serve this audience as specialty coffee and home brewing continue to evolve."

The broader coffee industry also represents a significant economic opportunity. According to the National Coffee Association, coffee contributes nearly $350 billion annually to the U.S. economy and supports approximately 2.2 million American jobs.

The launch of CoffeeBarista.com represents another step in DIR's strategy of developing specialized digital media properties across high-interest consumer categories. Through proprietary technology, scalable content infrastructure, and a shared operating platform, DIR continues to expand its portfolio of online destinations spanning sports, gaming, technology, lifestyle, and consumer interests.

Digital Information Resources (DIR) is a technology and digital media company that builds, acquires, and operates niche digital properties. Through proprietary technology, scalable content infrastructure, and a shared operating platform, DIR develops specialized online destinations designed to inform, educate, and engage audiences across a range of industries.
