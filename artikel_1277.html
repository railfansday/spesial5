Official: Inter provide Bastoni injury update as recovery time revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Inter confirm Alessandro Bastoni has suffered a left quadriceps strain, which, according to reports in Italy, rules out the Italy international for three weeks.

Inter provided an injury update on their defender Bastoni on Friday after the 27-year-old was forced off in the first half of Wednesday’s Champions League match against Stuttgart.

“Alessandro Bastoni underwent clinical and diagnostic tests this morning at the Humanitas Institute in Rozzano. The tests revealed a strain to his left quadriceps,” Inter said.

According to several sources in Italy, including Sky Sport Italia, Bastoni will be ruled out for at least three weeks.

This leaves Bastoni in doubt for the Supercoppa Italiana Final against Lazio on December 22.

The last Serie A match of the year will be just a few days before, on December 18, when the Nerazzurri will visit strugglers Lecce.

Inter will play seven matches in the next three weeks, including two consecutive matches against Genoa: one in Serie A tomorrow and the following one in the Coppa Italia Round of 16 next Tuesday, both at San Siro.

On Wednesday, December 9, they’ll face Borussia Dortmund away in the Champions League Round 6.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
