Man’s virtual memorial for his dog goes global and now has tributes to 17,000 pets

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

A website is giving people the chance to pay tribute to thousands of pets who have passed away, allowing visitors to light virtual candles, leave flowers, and share messages with their owners.

Losing a pet can be incredibly difficult, especially when they’ve been part of your family for years. While many people keep photos or other reminders of their beloved animals, one website is offering a different way to pay tribute to them.

The website’s creator explained that the idea came from losing his own dog Bella at 18, so he made a place for her to be remembered. Now, it’s home to more than 19,000 memorials, dedicated to dogs, cats, and other animals from around the world.

“I made this place after losing my own dog. I wanted a quiet place to visit the ones we loved, for every companion, from everywhere,” a message on the site reads.

Called Light a Paw, the free online memorial lets people from around the world honour their pets in a virtual space that anyone can visit.

Rather than just displaying a list of names, it features a 3D memorial hall that visitors can explore by controlling a small character.

The walls are covered with photographs of pets who have passed away, each with their own dedicated memorial.

My dog Bella died at 18, so I built her a place where the light never goes out.17,000+ pets from 80+ countries are there now, lit by strangers.Lost one too? Tell me their name, or make them a spot and drop the link. I'll come light a candle 🕯️https://t.co/nCyn77liLs pic.twitter.com/cm0ZdSPq6F

Clicking on one allows you to learn more about the animal, light a virtual candle, leave flowers, or write a message in its guestbook for the owner to read.

Visitors can also browse the full collection of memorials, searching for individual pets or filtering by animal type.

Anyone who has lost a pet can create their own memorial by uploading a photograph, adding their name, and writing a few words about them.

With thousands of animals already remembered, Light a Paw offers visitors a place to celebrate the pets they’ve loved, even long after they’re gone.
