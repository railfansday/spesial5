Pattie Gonia’s GLP-1 Sponsorship Debate, Durston’s $849 Tent, and Broadway’s PCT Finish | This Week on Trail #1

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Welcome dear listener, to the very first episode of This Week on Trail, produced by The Trek. Hosts Jess “Sassafras” and Katie “Double Dip” bring together trail gossip, news, gear reviews, and the conversations getting hikers panties all bunched up online. This week, they debate Pattie Gonia’s response to a $50,000 GLP-1 sponsorship offer, weigh the value of Durston’s premium freestanding tent, and react to Michael “Broadway” Grant’s PCT finish. Plus, a hiking pants hot take from Backpacker Radio and listener feedback on fabricated hiking stories.

Jess shares screenshots she says she saved from a since-deleted Pattie Gonia post about turning down a $50,000 GLP-1 sponsorship. She and Katie discuss influencer advertising, body-image messaging, and whether publicly rejecting a brand deal is a useful stand, self-promotion, or some combination of both.

Katie shares her experience testing the Durston X-Dome Pro 1+, including its roominess, pitching options, and performance in the rain. It has become her go-to solo backpacking tent, but she questions whether saving 7.2 ounces over the standard X-Dome 1+ justifies paying an additional $430.

Jess and Katie watch Michael Grant’s PCT finish video and reflect on the negativity he encountered while sharing his hike online. There are tears, congratulations, and a discussion about why strangers feel compelled to tell hikers they won’t make it.

A clip from Livvy “Stumbles” Weld’s Backpacker Radio interview gets the comments section going. Jess and Katie discuss shorts versus pants, the conditions that influence their choices, and the dismissive reactions to an experienced thru-hiker sharing her personal preference.

A listener responds to an earlier Trail Goss discussion about a YouTube channel presenting fabricated hiking tragedies as real stories. Jess and Katie revisit their frustrations with misleading outdoor content and welcome more listener feedback.
