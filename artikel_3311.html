25 Strongest One Piece Devil Fruits, Ranked (So Far)

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Naruto has its chakra and jutsu, Hunter x Hunter has Nen, Bleach has Reiryoku, and One Piece has its devil fruits. A distinctive power system is the hallmark of a successful battle shonen franchise. This holds true even for new-generation hits like Jujutsu Kaisen with its complicated cursed energy system and My Hero Academia with its simple yet boundlessly creative quirks. Simple as the concept may have been at the start, One Piece's devil fruit power system was unmistakably charming, with room for endless innovation.

This is why devil fruits have been the backbone of One Piece's power mechanics since the very start and are still holding strong even with the introduction of new powers like haki. The scale and scope of devil fruit powers have also grown exponentially over the years. Some defy the rules of time, physics, and reality, while others bring the mythical to life, expanding what started out as one boy's ability to stretch his body to a power capable of bringing literal gods to life across millennia.

The Blackbeard Pirates have specifically hunted down many of the One Piece world's strongest devil fruits, making them a colossal threat to nearly everyone in the series' final arcs. Without question, the largest fighter on the team is the giant among giants, Sanjuan Wolf.

Eating the Huge-Huge Fruit allowed the giant to adjust his size, making Sanjuan the size of a several-story building or as large as a mountain capable of standing in the ocean. It's currently unclear what the limits of the Huge-Huge Fruit are, but it's already proved capable of considerable destruction.

If devil fruit powers were to be measured by simple, plain strength, then the winner would surely be Jesus Burgess's Strong-Strong Fruit. During manga chapter #1063, Burgess casually lifts a mountain to throw at his foes.

While the limitations of the power have yet to be explored, for a devil fruit with such a name, it likely grants the user as much brute strength as they need to accomplish their goal, such as throwing a mountain or breaking swords.

The Paramount War saw the Whitebeard Pirates and many of the world's strongest fighters clashing, spoiling audiences with several shocking displays of power. Many of the arc's most significant attacks from powerhouses like Mihawk and Admiral Akainu failed to get past the defenses of Whitebeard Commander Jozu's Twinkle-Twinkle Fruit.

By covering his entire body with diamonds, Jozu became unbreakable, allowing him to withstand any attack and reflect damage as he led his men into battle. Combined with his considerable strength, he could freely toss huge icebergs, showing that Jozu was a formidable force for anyone to reckon with.

The Sick-Sick Fruit is often overlooked, mainly because its user, Doc Q, is not in the best state of health himself, making it seem like the devil fruit is as dangerous to its user as it is to other opponents. And while his weapon, the double-sided scythe, seems to allude to that, Doc’s past suggests he was born chronically ill.

The fruit can be quite dangerous since it doesn’t have to afflict people with disease through touch, but can extend quite far into becoming an area of contamination, as seen by the Heart Pirates when they were turned into women. This also suggests it doesn’t have to be a harmful disease, but something that could distract or debuff one’s opponents.

The Sick-Sick Fruit is often utilized given Doc Q’s illness, but in the hands of someone healthy, it could easily become just as deadly, if not more so, than the Venom-Venom Fruit.

The Rumble-Rumble Fruit is an electricity-based Logia-type devil fruit that can deliver power output just as great as Kaido's devil fruit. It also provides the user with an absurd amount of abilities. Like all Logia devil fruits, the Rumble-Rumble Fruit allows a user to become intangible, becoming outright invincible to foes that don't yet possess Haki.

Through the Rumble-Rumble Fruit's powers, Enel can travel great distances in the blink of an eye, control the weather to an extent, and even use the heat from his lightning-based body to reforge weapons. The user can also use their nearly limitless electrical energy supply to power large machines and weapons.

It is often believed that the only reason why Enel was defeated is that he found his "natural enemy" in Luffy, a human made of rubber, which is a material immune to electricity. Also, Enel relied on his devil fruit powers too much, was arrogant and cocky, and clearly never developed his abilities to their extreme.

Introduced first by the user, Portgas D. Ace, the Flame-Flame Fruit proved to be a pretty powerful devil fruit that could incinerate leagues of areas within an instant or otherwise be compressed to fire a deadly bullet. As a Logia-type, it also turns its user into flame, making it difficult and dangerous in a physical fight.

“Fire Fist” Ace didn’t get a lot of chances to shine in the manga, but the anime would go on to depict many more fights where he was involved, bringing his battles to life in a way that carries on his legacy. Now that he’s passed, Sabo inherited the Flame-Flame Fruit.

While there hasn’t been much screen time for Sabo to properly showcase how he uses the fruit now, there are still plenty of opportunities in later installments. Ace may be gone, but his devil fruit resides in one of his brothers now, carrying the torch quite literally in his stead.

Zoro battled a mountain-sized foe named Pica who could manipulate stone to create a gargantuan version of himself, but Blackbeard's Titanic Captain Avalo Pizarro's Island Fruit is even better. Pizarro can become one with an island he touches and freely control it to morph into any shape they want.

Pizarro can manipulate almost any landmass but does take damage to his natural body if large sections under his control are destroyed. Still, the Isle Fruit is so destructive that its users must be extremely careful not to hurt their own comrades.

The Hobby-Hobby Fruit is a Paramecia-type devil fruit eaten by Sugar, one of Doflamingo's top officers in the Doflamingo Pirates. With the Hobby-Hobby Fruit, anyone Sugar touches is turned into a living toy, and as a side effect, the fruit stopped Sugar from aging after she ate it, leaving her physically a child despite actually being an adult.

While Sugar wasn't the main villain of the Dressrosa arc, her devil fruit vis nonetheless a force to be reckoned with. Not only can Sugar force her toys to follow her every command, but any victim of the Hobby-Hobby Fruit is erased from the memory of everyone in the world, thus adding a sense of existential dread to its abilities.

Fujitora is one of the newer Admirals introduced in One Piece, but that doesn't make him or his fruit any less powerful. The Press-Press Fruit allows Fujitora to manipulate gravity. He has used this power for some small-scale feats such as levitating objects and pinning enemies in place, his fruit also has a much more extreme destructive potential.

In fact, Fujitora's potential is so great it risks rivaling even One Piece's ancient weapons like Uranus. Fujitora can call down large meteors from the sky to crush his enemies. This ability could cause mass destruction, making the Press-Press Fruit easily one of the most powerful in the series.

The Venom-Venom Fruit is a Paramecia devil fruit eaten by Magellan, the former warden of Impel Down. The Venom-Venom Fruit allows Magellan to create poisons of varying toxicity and acidity, and while it protects Magellan from being hurt by poison, it doesn't prevent him from having massive amounts of diarrhea when he eats poisoned food, which he loves to do.

The Venom-Venom Fruit is easily one of the strongest Paramecia fruits in all of One Piece. The strength of Magellan's poison makes it virtually impossible to touch Magellan without getting poisoned, and the versatility of his techniques means that he always has a surefire way of attacking someone.

Magellan was strong enough to defeat both Luffy and Blackbeard with zero difficulty, and that, above everything else, is a perfect testament of the Venom-Venom Fruit's power.
