There are a few gifts that can immediately change someone’s day-to-day routine. Laptops are one of them. If you’re shopping for someone who’s going back to school, working on an old piece of hardware, or just looking to stream, browse, and stay connected, a good new laptop will make their life easier long after the holidays have come and gone.

If you’re considering a laptop for someone on your list this year, we recommend checking out the Lenovo Googlebook 15. Released just last month, the cutting-edge laptop boasts tons of Gemini integration for easy, helpful AI tools and fast speeds, no matter what your giftee is using it for. All of this comes in a sleek package, and it’s priced very competitively for a laptop of this caliber at $1,099.

The first thing users will notice about the Lenovo Googlebook is the 15.3-inch 2.8K OLED screen. It’s a beautiful high-resolution display that comes in especially handy when doing things like streaming movies, working with art, or juggling different windows. If you’re unfamiliar, OLED displays typically offer richer color and better contrast that provides a premium look, no matter the task at hand (or, fingertips, rather). And to cap things off, it’s also a touchscreen for natural interaction — especially helpful for students or other young people who might not have a ton of laptop time under their belt.

Inside the Googlebook, you’ll find an Intel Core Ultra 5 processor with 16 gigabytes of memory 512 gigs of storage. That’s ideal for quickly executing all everyday tasks, from video calls and spreadsheets to browsing and streaming. On top of the operating system, you get Gemini integration, which unlocks a host of AI-powered tools. This can do things like automatically adding dates to the calendar with Magic Pointer or capture thoughts with Rambler.

The battery, meantime, will last up to 12.5 hours on a single charge, ensuring all-day work anywhere without the worry of a dead device.
