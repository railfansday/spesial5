If you keep X years in bonds, what happens in a stock crash? Do you spend out of bonds, thereby having less than X years in bonds? Do you buy any more stocks? How far below X are you willing to go? If you go below X, do you at some point try to get back to X? If so, how and when does that happen?
Since I am in the withdrawal phase of life, I presently have 12x in bonds. In my IPS, I only rebalance from stocks into bonds, not the other way around.

At my rebalance date, if the stock is not above my AA, I will not rebalance and will continue to draw down my bonds until such time the stock recovers.
It appears that you use an AA approach, rather than a bucket of bonds approach. That’s easier to manage.
