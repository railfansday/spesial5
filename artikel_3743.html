Third-person survival horror game Ugly Within to be published by Serafini Productions for PS5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Serafini Productions will publish Yamaitachi-developed third-person survival horror game Ugly Within when it launches or PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and PC via Steam and Epic Games Store, the companies announced.

Here is an overview o the game, via its store pages:

Ugly Within is a third-person survival horror game set within traditional Japanese houses, where you confront unknown supernatural horrors.

Shu Onodera, a boy with the power to see the past, and Reiko Miyama, an exorcist who banishes supernatural entities, delve into the Kamata family’s hidden history and the being it has long worshipped.

A sinister past. An enduring faith. Traces of lives consumed by beauty and ugliness. When you uncover the truth, what will you find beautiful—and what will you find ugly?

This is a story of tragedy that could befall anyone.
