How to rob the Bank Vault in ROB RUN

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Looting the Looty Bay Bank in ROB RUN isn’t an easy task, but the rewards are well worth the effort if you manage to pull off the heist. However, the bank isn’t your ordinary houses that you can randomly raid for loot. You’ll have to plan carefully, with the ultimate aim being looting the bank’s vault.

Let’s find out how to rob the bank vault for maximum loot in ROB RUN.

As I said, raiding the bank vault is not an ordinary task. You are going to require several items without which the heist will be unsuccessful. You will require these items since there are multiple areas to breach.

You can find the Drill at Lenny’s Motel through the suspicious door, which allows you to exchange materials for it. It will specifically require an action camera, two USB hacking tools, and 50,000 credits. Once you exchange these items, you will get the Drill.

The Drill is also available as a legendary item while you are raiding houses, but I have come across it just once so far.

You can find Mini Drills from Gadgets Shipment tree, only after you live in level 2 Settlement or better and have at least $100,000. Each Mini Drill costs $18,000 buy, and you’ll need it to open the larger sages that are located within the main vault.

While the ID Card isn’t mandatory to rob the vault, you can bring it with you to get more loot. You can exchange a Deposit Box Key at Lenny’s Motel to get the ID Card.

Once you have your drills, go to the northeastern part of the map. Head to the bottom floor and go behind the teller’s counter to find the vault.

Before you can use the drill, you need to change your status to Criminal. When this happens, however, you can expect alarms to trigger by your presence. In simple words, you need to use the drill, get all the loot, and escape as quickly as possible.
