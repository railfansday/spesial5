Analyst: ‘Altseason’ May Start From These Three Charts

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Home » Altcoins » Analyst: ‘Altseason’ May Start From These Three Charts

Market veteran indicates bounce setups on a selection of altcoins with these thresholds ought to be cleared.

Three major altcoins are flashing potential trend-reversal signals on the 3-day chart after steep declines, with the TD Sequential indicator highlighting buy setups on Ethereum, Solana and Cardano.

The signals provided by Ali Martinez still require buyers to step in and follow through, but the levels now in focus mark the first clear technical checkpoints since the recent pullback.

Ethereum fell 22.64%, sliding from $2,806 to a low near $2,171. On the 3-day chart the TD Sequential has now flashed a buy signal, suggesting the correction could be nearing exhaustion.

If buying pressure builds, the first rebound target sits at $2,500. Now, the top-tier altcoin is testing this in real-time, as Ether’s already trading in the low $2,300s.

Solana dropped 27.31%, falling from $124.89 to $90.78. A TD Sequential buy signal is forming on the same 3-day timeframe.

If the signal completes and the $90 area continues to hold as support, the next upside checkpoints sit at $105 and then the setup resistance trendline near $120.

SOL is currently trading in the low-to-mid $90s, keeping the $90 zone as the immediate line that needs to stay intact for the bounce scenario to stay valid.

Cardano has been hit hardest of the three, declining 35.41% from $0.2822 to $0.1823. A fresh 9 and a Sequential 13 buy signal have appeared on the 3-day chart. If buyers respond, the first level to watch is $0.2268.

A move through that zone would bring the setup resistance trendline near $0.2677 into focus. ADA is currently hovering around $0.19, still below the first target but above the recent low that produced the bullish signal.

The three altseason setups share the same structure: sharp multi-week declines followed by TD Sequential buy signals on the 3-day chart and clearly defined resistance levels above. None of the signals guarantee a sustained rally on their own.

They simply mark zones where selling pressure has stretched and where a reversal becomes possible if buyers appear and defend the recent lows. Ethereum’s path runs through $2,500 then $2,700. Solana’s runs through $105 then $120. Cardano’s runs through $0.2268 then $0.2677.

Whether any of those levels get tested will depend on whether the buy signals attract actual demand or simply fade as the broader market remains soft.
