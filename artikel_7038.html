Mood on Set of ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Is “Very Dark” According to Sources

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

“Everyone agreed this was really unfair.”

Dancing With the Stars is entering its final week, and sadly Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson won’t be there. Despite getting perfect scores, the couple was voted off during the semifinals, and apparently the vibe behind the scenes has been pretty rough. A source tells The Sun that Jenna was “crying” and “really upset” about the elimination, and that “the whole mood was dark” on set.

“Everyone agreed this was really unfair,” the insider explained, reiterating that the vibes were “very dark” and that while Harry was “extremely kind” after the news dropped, Jenna was “visibly upset.”

Harry spoke about his elimination to E! News after the semifinals, saying, “I oddly feel at ease. I don't feel sad—it's really weird. I kind of felt like we accomplished everything we wanted to.”

He added of working with Jenna, “I want to make more art with her, and that's what it's really about. I like making art that people enjoy. So, to me, I felt that energy [in] the people that I did see and the people that really loved what we were able to do in this dance. I'm now a trained dancer because of her.”

Meanwhile, Jenna said she was “most heartbroken” about not being able to do their finale dance, saying, “We had concocted this incredible journey that the ballroom won't see this time. But maybe we'll put it out there somewhere.”
