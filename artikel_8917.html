Private House in Kaklebi / Werkraum Design Architecture

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Text description provided by the architects. The private house in Kaklebi, near Tbilisi, is composed of a series of timber-clad volumes rather than a single block, arranged to follow the slope of a wooded site and to preserve its existing mature trees. Distributed across multiple levels, the building reduces its own visual presence in the landscape while giving each level a distinct relationship to the terrain below.
