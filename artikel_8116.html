12 Gifts to Level Up Your Next Family Adventure

Your kids might not remember what was under the tree, but we promise they’ll never forget the nights spent under the stars with their family. Lucky for parents, outdoor gear is more family-friendly than ever. Think: tents you can stand up in, paddle boards that pack into a backpack, campsite games, passenger-friendly e-bikes, and so much more. These 12 adventure-ready gifts are for the families on your list who’d rather spend the weekend at the campsite, on the water, or on the bike path than on the couch with an iPad in hand.

The best part of birding is that there’s no real age requirement or limit. It’s arguably one of the most accessible (and relaxing) ways for families to enjoy the outdoors. Everyone from toddlers to grandparents can find joy in spotting bird species with these lightweight binoculars. Heck, you can get the grandparents out there, too! Psst…we have lots more birding gear recs where this came from.

You don’t have to give up hiking if you’ve got little ones. It just takes some helpful gear to keep the adventure going strong. Add a hiking backpack carrier to your gear closet so it’s easy to get your kids on the trail. Backpack carriers are best for youngins under 5 years old or 55 pounds. Our favorite pick for longer hiking trails is the Deuter Kid Comfort Child Carrier, which fits like a backpacking pack and includes tons of features to make hiking with kids much easier. And if you’re lucky, you just might squeeze some trail naps in.

Camp games, anyone? ENO’s new TrailFlyer game is like bags (or cornhole, as we call it in the Midwest) but designed for camping. It packs down into a small carrying case and takes just a few minutes to set up. Wrap the adjustable straps around a tree trunk or sturdy anchor point, stake out the feet, and start tossing.

Our family has put the heavy-duty Victory Patio Blanket to use as a play area for our crawling baby at camp, and we’ve taken it to plenty of beach days and park picnics. The underside is coated in a thick, waterproof material that keeps moisture and dirt from seeping through. You can also toss it in your tent to make a cozier floor or dress up your campsite with an outdoor rug.

Good luck wrapping a kayak (or fitting it in your garage). Skip the bulky gift for the packable and portable inflatable ISLE Switch 3. This hybrid SUP-kayak fits in a backpack with everything you need to hit the water, including a paddle, pump, and inflatable kayak seats. It’s the perfect adventure gift for a family who loves getting out on the water.

Keep it old school with this point-and-shoot camera that’s perfectly nostalgic. Don’t let its lack of a screen fool you. This screen-free digital camera is rechargeable and can capture up to 500 photos per charge. It also offers several built-in filters—standard, vintage, analog, and black-and-white—that you can swap with the push of a button. Bonus: it comes with a microSD card already inside, so you’ve got room for around 2,000 photos from day one.

If you want to take the littlest adventures with all their gear along, this wagon makes it easy. It works three ways: as a wagon for hauling, as a stroller with two 5-point safety harness seats, and as a car seat carrier with the included adapter (car seat not included).

The Wrangler Deluxe holds up to 110 pounds and brings the tough, trail-ready spirit Jeep is known for, with all-terrain puncture-proof airless tires and swivel front wheels for easy maneuvering. A removable cooler bag holds up to 16 cans, and the wagon also has a UV-blocking canopy, pull-down shades, a cup holder, a parent organizer, and multiple storage pockets. When it’s time to pack up, it folds compactly.

Cozy up around the campfire with the ones you love in this two-seater camp chair. Its low-profile, reclined design puts you at just the right height for roasting s’mores and soaking up the fire’s warmth. And for a chair that fits two (or three if you’ve got a kiddo on your lap), it packs down surprisingly small.

We can’t mention s’mores without plugging our favorite roasting sticks. These reusable, telescoping metal roasting forks make great stocking stuffers. Our set has lasted through years of camping, and we love using them not only for marshmallows but also for hot dogs, sausages, and the occasional Twinkie.

Due for a new family cooler? Keep beers, bubbly water, juice boxes, and everything in between ice-cold in the Hydro Flask 15L Carryout Soft Cooler. It’s easy to carry and easy to clean, which makes it a go-to for camping trips, road trips, soccer tournaments, and backyard grill-outs. Don’t worry about scratches, broken buckles, or dings, either: Hydro Flask offers a lifetime warranty.

Who needs a minivan when you’ve got the Aventon Abound LR? This cargo e-bike is made for family adventures in the city and beyond, featuring an extra-long rear rack that fits two kids (or one kiddo plus panniers full of gear). Hills are no match for its powerful 750W motor, and you can ride up to 60 miles on a single charge. We opted for the smaller Abound SR since we’re a “one-and-done” family, but the LR is a great upgrade if you have more kids and gear to haul.

Give the gift of camping! Hipcamp takes the stress out of snagging a campsite before it sells out. Its database of private land available for rent connects you with one-of-a-kind stays—from tent sites on farms and vineyards to cabins, glamping tents, and treehouses. All you have to do is dream up your family’s perfect night under the stars.

The perfect family camping tent doesn’t exis—oh wait, there’s The North Face Wawona 6. It’s tall enough to stand up in, sleeps six, features tons of interior pockets, and has color-coded poles to make setup a breeze. Speaking of a breeze, oversized mesh windows help ventilate the tent on muggy summer nights. The best part? A huge vestibule that works like a front porch, giving you a shady and dry spot to kick back in camp chairs in hot or rainy weather.
