ADWEEK Launches the Next Leaders Vanguard Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

New in 2027, ADWEEK is launching the inaugural Next Leaders Vanguard Awards.

Every CMO and agency chief was once the person running the brand or the biggest account. They were already the ones making the calls that move the business.

For years we’ve recognized those executives as part of our Marketing Vanguard Awards program. This year, we’re honoring them with their own distinction of ADWEEK’s Next Leaders Vanguard.

Part of the Marketing Vanguard and Agencies Vanguard umbrella, Next Leaders Vanguard is a separate award with its own entry and judging process. Honorees may be early in their careers or decades in. What matters is position and proof: They are the clear next-in-line for a top job, and their results already show it.

Nominees must hold a role one level below the CMO or agency C-suite, with real ownership of strategy, budget, and results.

Current CMOs and agency C-suite executives are not eligible and should be entered in Marketing Vanguard Awards or Agencies Vanguard Awards, respectively. Self-nominations and nominations by managers or colleagues are both accepted.

Potential is not enough. Every entry must show how their work delivered real business results.

Entries without measurable outcomes will not be considered. Confidential figures may be shared as percentages and will be used for judging purposes only.

The final deadline to submit is Friday, January 29, 2027.

Winners will be published in the May issue of ADWEEK in print and online.

To enter, please use the submission form below, and reach out to awards@adweek.com if you have any questions.
