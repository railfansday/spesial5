William Gallas says only one team can rival Arsenal for the Premier League title

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

William Gallas believes that only one team has the ability to rival Arsenal in this season’s Premier League title race.

Arsenal are now five points clear at the top of the league table following their hard-fought 1-0 win at home to Hull City on Saturday.

But it was another bad weekend for Manchester City as their third successive league defeat saw Enzo Maresca’s side slip to third.

Brighton are now second in the table as Fabian Hurzeler’s side continued their superb start to the season with a 2-0 win over Brentford.

The Seagulls have already beaten both Arsenal and City in the league this season, with no team scoring more goals and only Arsenal conceding fewer after ten games.
