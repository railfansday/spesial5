League Two table predicted with Rotherham United, York City, Barnet, Port Vale and Chesterfield calculations

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Opta have issued fresh predictions on York City, Rotherham United, Barnet, Port Vale, Chesterfield and more

Data experts Opta have deployed a supercomputer to predict how the League Two table will look at the end of the 2026/27 campaign.

International football took a wrecking ball to the EFL fixture calendar over the weekend but there was still plenty of fourth-tier action.

Rotherham United’s meeting with Crawley Town did not go ahead but York City were in action and secured their first away win of the season, defeating Rochdale 1-0.

Minstermen boss Scott Lindsey said: “It’s a massive monkey off our back for us to get that first away win. There have been times when we’ve deserved to win away, there haven’t been many where we’ve not played well, we’ve been good away from home. But you do think ‘when is that first away win going to come?’ and it becomes psychological.”

Away from Yorkshire, Salford City were held to a 1-1 draw by Northampton Town and Shrewsbury Town claimed a 2-1 win over Oldham Athletic. Grimsby Town completed a 4-1 rout of Swindon Town, Fleetwood Town edged past Tranmere Roevrs and Port Vale saw off Accrington Stanley.

Crewe Alexandra drew 2-2 with Newport County and Colchester United got the better of Gillingham, while Bristol Rovers lost to Chesterfield and Exeter City clinched a narrow 1-0 win over Cheltenham Town.
