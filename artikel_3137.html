Altruist’s Wenk on AI and the Next Era of Financial Advice

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Altruist CEO Jason Wenk joins Bloomberg's Katie Greifeld for a wide ranging conversation on what joining Vanguard could mean for Altruist, his push to make financial advice more accessible and how technology and AI could reshape the economics of wealth management. He also addresses the future of the RIA custody market and Altruist’s plans to expand its product offering. Wenk spoke at the Bloomberg ETFs in Depth conference in New York on November 19, 2026.
