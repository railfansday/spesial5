FC 27 Road to the Knockout Challenge 1 SBC rewards and solutions

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Road to the Knockout Challenge 1 SBC is now live in FC 27, and you can complete it to get a free pack. While the rewards for such SBCs have been better in the past, this one is easy to do.

Let’s find out how to complete the task to unlock all the available rewards.

The Road to the Knockout Challenge 1 SBC has just one task, but there are few requirements.

Ideally, you’ll use cards that are already available in your Ultimate Team squad. However, you’ll also need to meet the requirements while completing the challenge. This can force you to get stuck. If that happens, you can use the following solution to solve the puzzle.

Completing the challenge will get you a Small Electrum Player Pack. In terms of packs, there are better ones to get. However, the untradeable reward features three Silver and three Gold items. If you’re lucky, you might end up getting a walkout/promo item, including one of the new RTTK players. All RTTK cards have boosted stats and are eligible for upgrades in the future.

At worst, you can find cards that can be used to complete other FC 27 challenges in the future for cheap.
