My hands are always freezing. Winter runs can be torture. I’m not just talking about slight discomfort; my hands are cold-sensitive to the point that they become agonizingly painful. I’ve cut long runs short because the finger pain is unbearable, and I know there are a lot of people out there with this same affliction.

In my youth, running cross-country in Maine, I relied on cheap gas station gloves—and to be honest, they worked really well until November. Then, as an adult runner living in Utah’s mountains, I discovered alpaca wool mittens, which look silly but keep my hands comfy.

Of course, gear tech just keeps getting better, and I continually experiment with new running gloves for cold hands. I don’t think anything can live up to my alpaca mittens, but for long runs, I’ve switched to ski mittens, which is my greatest winter running hack yet.

We’ve put in the testing hours so you can invest in a pair of winter running gloves that actually work. Here are our top choices, worn and recommended by editors, testers, and contributors to Outside Online and Outside Run.
