£3.5m man needs Ipswich Town exit: Birmingham City & Stoke City must plan move

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The January transfer window is fast approaching, and as the new year draws closer, transfer news will begin to ramp up, with one Ipswich Town player almost certain to return to the English second tier this winter.

The two-week international break has given Championship clubs, and the rest of Europe, a chance to reassess their current situations and squads ahead of the upcoming festive period.

The weeks leading into the new year are especially busy for teams across the EFL, who must contend with immense fixture congestion, as whilst the work on the pitch will be almost non-stop, so will the work off of it, with the January transfer window right around the corner.

Plans for the winter window will almost certainly already be being put in place by clubs across the continent, with the English second tier being no different, as one player will surely be on the shortlist of almost every club in the division heading into the new year.

Anis Mehmeti joined Ipswich Town from Bristol City in January 2026 for around £3.5 million, having announced himself as one of the best attacking midfielders in the division over the past couple of years at Ashton Gate.

Mehmeti, 25, was the subject of interest from numerous clubs in the Championship over the summer window, with Pete O'Rourke reporting on Birmingham City's and Wrexham's interest in the Albanian, whilst Alan Nixon reported via his Patreon on Derby County's pursuit.

Anis Mehmeti Championship statistics 2025/26 (Data from FotMob)

The attacking midfielder ultimately opted to remain in Suffolk and fight for his place in the Premier League under new boss Gary O'Neil, but after ten gameweeks, he has yet to make a single matchday squad in the top flight.

Mehmeti cannot afford to be sat in the stands much longer and let an entire season of football pass him by, as Football League World has taken a look at two Championship clubs that must look to strike an agreement with the Tractor Boys this January to bring him back to the EFL.

Birmingham City have previously been credited with an interest in Mehmeti as recently as last summer, and it would be no surprise to see Chris Davies' side head back into the market for him again.

Jay Stansfield has more often than not been used in the number ten role in Davies' preferred 4-2-3-1 shape at St Andrews, though in recent weeks, Max Bird has been pushed further forward as Stansfield deputises up top in the absence of August Priske.

An injury to either Stansfield or Bird would all of a sudden see Davies scrambling for options in that area of the pitch, and having registered an interest in Portsmouth's Adrian Segecic in the summer as well, another option at attacking midfield heading to the Second City this winter wouldn't come as a surprise to anyone.

Mehmeti, whilst best through the middle, can play anywhere across the final third, and though Birmingham can boast the likes of Carlos Vicente, Patrick Roberts, Demarai Gray, and Liam Millar out wide, adding Mehmeti to the mix only creates more competition and ups everyone's game.

The Blues head into the international break sitting pretty at the top of the Championship table with a superior goal difference over Middlesbrough and Stoke City, who they are level on points with, as automatic promotion looks like a real possibility this year.

Bringing in Mehmeti could be the final piece of the puzzle Davies needs to secure a historic year for Birmingham, who must go all out to secure his services in January.

Stoke City, like Birmingham, find themselves in a promotion battle this year after an excellent start to the campaign.

The Potters, despite losing each of their three opening fixtures to Swansea City, Southampton, and Wolverhampton Wanderers, have bounced back with just one defeat in their last 12 league games, winning nine, as no team in the Championship has won more games than Mark Robins' side at this stage.

It's a miraculous turnaround in form from Stoke, who enjoyed an equally excellent beginning to last season as well, as this time around, Robins will have no intention of dropping off as they previously have.

Switching to a 3-4-2-1 shape, this system change has been the catalyst for their rise up the league table, with Jack McGlynn shining in one of the attacking midfield roles, Ethan Galbraith dictating games from deeper, and Viktor Johansson consistently keeping the scoreline down in goal.

McGlynn has been an almost ever-present in that role since his introduction to the side, though alongside him, Bae Jun-ho, Justin Devenny, and Million Manhoef have each been used, but none of them has been able to nail down a place in the starting XI.

With Bae out of contract at the end of the season, a January exit wouldn't be a shock, as Stoke can kill two birds with one stone by shifting the Korean and upgrading with Mehmeti.

If the North Staffordshire outfit are still in a battle for promotion, they must go all out to get over the line, as Mehmeti could help the Potters to secure a memorable year.
