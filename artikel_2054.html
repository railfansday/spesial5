Celtic vs Hibs moved to new date as a result of Premier Sports Cup final

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The clash between Celtic and Hibernian scheduled for December has been moved due to the Premier Sports Cup final.

Hibs were due to travel to Glasgow to face the champions in the Premiership on December 13 but will now be at Hampden taking on Rangers instead, after overcoming Hearts in their semi-final derby on Sunday.

Rangers trip to St Johnstone has also been moved to the same date.
