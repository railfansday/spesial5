'Very inventive' – Kevin Blackwell impressed by Crawley Town owners as transfer plans take shape

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The 67-year-old has been made head coach for the remainder of the 2026/27 season after his month-long stint as interim head coach.

The former Leeds United boss is unbeaten in two league games but has overseen defeats which saw the Reds exit the FA Cup and the Jersey Mike’s Trophy.

And it was after the embarrassing FA Cup exit to seventh tier Wimborne Town that Blackwell gave a scathing verdict on the club’s recruitment over the past 12 months and said somewthing has to change.

Speaking before the Fleetwood game, Blackwell said: “That was the discussions that we've had over the last week, quite heavily about the recruitment and what we need to do. We know where we are. We're identifying and trying to identify players.

“It won't be easy to get them in at this time of the year. It's not the best time of year to be making recruitment, but the owners know that we have to do that and they've been 100 per cent behind it. They've been very inventive in some of their ideas and I've been very impressed.”

Since Blackwell has been in charge, the club have brought in three new, experienced players – Connor Wickham, Ben Heneghan and Ayman Benarous – and now the new boss has a huge squad to work with. “When I was taking all of them, all 28 on my own, that was tough,” he said.

"That's why I was so pleased with the attitude of the players. Now that we've got Jordan [Tait] in, which is another step forward, another club coach that we've got in here, I'm looking forward to working with the club, working with the players and doing what we've got to do.”

And are the likes of Wickham and Heneghan fit too start? “Well, we've had to gradually get Conor and Ben a bit fitter and that's why maybe the international break has helped us a little bit.

"But both of them have got great attitude and have been senior professionals.

“Ben has come in and he quite surprised me with his fitness levels, so we've been able to use these two weeks to try and get them up to speed a little bit better. But they've brought know-how, experience and a voice and those three things are going to be massive for us.”

Reds face Fleetwood on Saturday before hosting Barnet on Tuesday night.
