Earlier today Newcastle United made an official announcement about the Arsenal away match.

That official club update covered (see below) by ourselves on Wednesday afternoon after they released it via official club media.

Newcastle United telling the fans that the away game at The Emirates had been moved due to Sky Sports selecting it for live TV coverage.

The match originally scheduled for 3pm on Saturday 23 January 2027 but the NUFC official media saying it would now kick off at 5.30pm on that day.

We linked to that official Newcastle United release but now some visitors to The Mag have informed us that this is now a dead link.

Having checked it out, it appears the club have deleted that original official release and have given no explanation as to why.

Having looked into it further, none of Sky Sports, Arsenal, nor the Premier League have any mention of this Wednesday afternoon claim of the Arsenal v Newcastle United having been moved.

So it looks like somebody at Newcastle United has messed up this afternoon and the club then simply deleting that official release. Surely they have a duty to clear up the confusion and confirm to fans what the situation actually is, which appears to be that there is yet to be any decision (or announcement…) made on when that Arsenal v Newcastle United match will definitely be played.

Our article on The Mag earlier today (Wednesday 18 November 2026):

The Arsenal v Newcastle United match has now been moved.

This news comes via an announcement on Wednesday afternoon.

This Arsenal v Newcastle United game in the Premier League was due to be a 3pm kick-off on Saturday 23 January 2027.

However, Sky Sports have selected it for live UK TV coverage.

Sky Sports moving the game to 5.3opm on that Saturday 23 January 2027.

This news comes 48 hours before Matthias Jaissle’s side will host Mikel Arteta’s team on Saturday at St James’ Park.

That game is also to be shown on Sky Sports this weekend, a 5.30pm kick-off on Tyneside.

Bruno Guimaraes is set to return to St James’ Park for the first time since leaving in the summer, Arsenal paying £75m to take the now 29 year old to The Emirates.

Arsenal currently lead the Premier League, five points clear of Brighton who are in second place.

However, Bruno Guimaraes only a squad player now at the reigning champions.
