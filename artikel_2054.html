Mauricio Pochettino shares Bruno Fernandes injury update ahead of Brentford game

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes is a doubt for the Premier League meeting with Brentford after missing Friday’s training session through illness.

Bruno Fernandes informed Mauricio Pochettino and the club’s medical staff that he was feeling unwell, forcing Manchester United to leave him out of their final training session ahead of Saturday’s fixture at Old Trafford.

Pochettino addressed the situation during his pre-match press conference, revealing that the midfielder had been affected by a virus.

“We don’t know, he is affected by a virus from Portugal,” Pochettino explained in his press conference.

“He finished the game with the problem and today he can’t be part of the training session. We will see and assess today and tomorrow, how he wakes up. We won’t take any risk.”

The United manager is expected to make a late decision on his Bruno’s availability, depending on how he responds over the next 24 hours.

Bruno Fernandes was in action for United during their midweek Champions League fixture against Sporting CP, scoring twice in an entertaining 2-2 draw against his former club.

However, his absence from today’s has now put his involvement against Brentford in doubt.

The timing is far from ideal for Pochettino, who is still searching for his first victory since taking charge at Old Trafford. Having Fernandes available would be a boost, particularly given the midfielder’s importance as United’s major attacking outlet.

Fernandes has been one of United’s most regularly available players since arriving from Sporting in January 2020 and has missed just six games during his time at the club. United will therefore be reluctant to take unnecessary risks with his fitness.

Pochettino has made it clear that he will not rush his captain back. Whether Bruno Fernandes is fit to play for tomorrow’s game will only be confirmed closer to the match time.
