Michael Skubala to Lincoln City prediction made as Bristol City pressure mounts

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

An intriguing prediction has been made on the immediate future of Michael Skubala as the 44-year-old's disappointing start to life as Bristol City head coach continued on Saturday.

The Robins were thrashed 5-0 by in-form Norwich City at Ashton Gate on November 7th, before suffering a 2-0 loss to Championship leaders, Middlesbrough.

Skubala was handed a three-year contract in the West Country in May after becoming the long-term successor to Roy Hodgson, who was drafted in as head coach in March following Gerhard Struber's somewhat controversial exit.

City have won just two of their last 11 Championship matches and have slumped to 18th in the table as a result, accumulating just 18 points.

With his side losing four of the last six games and conceding 12 times during that timeframe, supporters have begun to make their feelings known when it comes to Skubala's tactics.

However, circumstances at his former club, Lincoln City, don't appear to be particularly rosey despite the fact the Imps are residing outside the bottom three, which has caused an interesting debate between EFL pundits, Callum Fowler and Jimmy Smith on the Pyramid podcast.

Skubala has won just five of his first 17 matches in charge of Bristol City, culminating in a win ratio of just 29%, and his future is very much up for debate.

It was recently claimed by Alan Nixon that Ashton Gate chiefs have the money to end his three-year reign prematurely, and that background checks were being made on potential successors.

That is despite the fact Skubala believes he has retained the hierarchy's support, with CEO, Charlie Boss, revealing that a meeting had been held and that the head coach had been afforded the opportunity to turn the tide.

The length of said opportunity remains unclear, but it has got off to the worst possible start despite the lofty position of Saturday's opponents, who netted through Adilson Malanda and David Strelec.

Skubala, of course, masterminded Lincoln's first return to the Championship since the 1960s last season as the Imps won League One at a 12-point canter, amassing 103 points and embarking on a 29-match unbeaten run.

Upon his departure to the Robins, Skubala issued a heartfelt message to supporters after 140 matches in charge, of which 71 ended in victory, but given Lincoln's four-game losing streak under joint-managers Chris Cohen and Tom Shaw, Fowler and Smith discussed the potential for the former head coach to return to the ProAmpac Stadium.

"I said it lots of times last season about Lincoln, it (Skubala's style) is disgusting," Fowler stated. "It's 'prison football', but in League One, that's class, and when you're someone like Lincoln, I guess that's class.

"For Bristol City who are a 'steady away' side anyway, with the money they've spent, you expect a bit more."

When asked if Skubala could return to his former club, Fowler debated: "Would they want him?," to which Smith responded: "Yes. I think when a manager's won the league at a canter, you've got fond memories of him, haven't you.

"We look at Parkinson at Wrexham, and we think, 'what are they doing' if they want to kick on, but there's that connection."

"Would the players buy into it if he went back," said Fowler. "I definitely think it could happen, but I don't know if Lincoln are thinking about a sacking at the minute."

Both then agreed that Skubala may lose his current role by the end of the three-game week.

We have already seen four managers sacked in this season's Championship, with three of the sides opting for a change in the dugout currently below Bristol City in the table, in the form of Burnley, Watford and Preston North End.

Skubala will have no doubt been grateful to have received the backing from the board during the international break, but defeat to Wrexham on Tuesday night would leave them even further adrift from where many expected the Robins to be under his guidance, which is to compete for a second play-off berth in three seasons.
