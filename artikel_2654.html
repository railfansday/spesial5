Vikings’ Christian Darrisaw returns from injury scare vs. 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Vikings' Christian Darrisaw returns from injury scare vs. 49ers appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Coach Kevin O'Connell's Minnesota Vikings headed to Mexico City to face the 49ers on Sunday Night Football, falling behind 35-10 in the fourth quarter after a disastrous Justin Jefferson fumble. The Vikings were linked to a high profile recent NFL cut, a surprising development prior to kickoff against coach Kyle Shanahan's team.

The game went worse than expected both on the scoreboard and on the injury front as key offensive tackle Christian Darrisaw sustained an injury.

"Vikings played that down without LT Christian Darrisaw, who is questionable to return because of a shoulder injury," Kevin Seifert wrote on X. "Ryan Van Demark filling in."

Seifert reported that Darrisaw was back for the second half as the Vikings dodged a bullet. Darrisaw has played in eight games this season with Minnesota, starting all eight of them. He has played in 66 games during his career with the Vikings, which started in 2021, starting 64, and serving as an important blind side protector for Minnesota's QBs, most recently Kirk Cousins.

Minnesota trailed 28-10 at halftime of the pivotal NFC showdown as the 49ers' passing game tore through their oft-ballyhooed Brian Flores-led defense. Brock Purdy had 248 yards and three touchdowns as of the 7:25 mark of the fourth quarter while Mike Evans paced San Francisco in receiving yards.

Evans got the go-ahead to play through an Achilles tendonitis issue before the game and made his time count with six catches for 109 yards and a touchdown.

Cousins wasn't at his best, throwing an interception and zero touchdown passes as of the fourth quarter as he was unable to keep pace with Purdy, Evans, Christian McCaffrey and the 49ers.

Darrisaw's injury update was a positive one, but there wasn't much else to be excited about on a day when Aaron Jones' short touchdown run was the Vikings' only play that went for six points as of the fourth quarter.
