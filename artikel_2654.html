Commanders reportedly set to meet with free-agent WR Brandon Aiyuk. Will they sign him?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Commanders will meet with receiver Brandon Aiyuk on Wednesday, according to a report from ESPN's Adam Schefter. Citing the information frim agent Drew Rosenhaus, Schefter says Aiyuk will meet with Washington brass on Wednesday.

This meeting comes after the 49ers released him on Monday, and Aiyuk then cleared waivers on Tuesday. A long dispute with the 49ers had previously been resolved when he reported to the team after months on the team's exempt-did not report list. At that time, the NFL gave San Francisco a two-week roster exemption, which expired this week, and which they rook advantage of before finally parting ways.

Explaining everything that went wrong between Aiyuk and the 49ers would involve thousands of words, but we can sum it up by saying there was a disagreement about his rehab plans that devolved into a messy situation that left the player and team at odds.

Then there was a rash of social media post by Aiyuk slamming 49ers brass, including general manager John Lynch and head coach Kyle Shanahan. Aiyuk also took shots at good friend, former Arizona State teammate and current Commanders quarterback Jayden Daniels, but Aiyuk reached out to Daniels and they reportedly squashed their friction.

The fact he was even given a meeting with the Commanders supports that claim. Aiyuk has made it clear for months that he wants to sign with Washington — he regularly wears Commanders gear in social media posts — who could use some receiver help with Terry McLaurin done for the year with a torn patellar tendon.

Aiyuk is expected to draw interest from a few teams, with a practice squad signing as his likeliest path back to NFL action. As a note, the Commanders have a practice-squad spot open after releasing running back Craig Reynolds from that unit.
