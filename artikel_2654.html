Derrick Henry placed in concussion protocol, limited in Wednesday's practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Derrick Henry has been placed in concussion protocol, but Ravens head coach Jesse Minter told reporters on Wednesday that the running back is likely to play on Sunday against the Texans.

“Derrick had a couple brief symptoms of a concussion right toward the end of that game,” Minter said. “Tested [and] cleared. We put him back in [the] protocol, just to kind of really make sure. So when it comes out today, he would’ve been limited. We do expect him to be back, obviously.”

Henry rushed for 94 yards on 13 carries with a touchdown and caught four passes for 17 yards in Sunday’s loss to Carolina.

Henry has not rushed for 100 yards in a game since Baltimore’s overtime victory over the Falcons on Oct. 4.

In 11 games so far this season, Henry has 793 yards with 11 touchdowns, averaging 4.0 yards per carry. He was up to 5.2 yards per carry last season.

Baltimore’s full Wednesday injury report will be released later in the day.
