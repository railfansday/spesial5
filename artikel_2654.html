4 key matchups in Bengals vs. Commanders

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Cincinnati Bengals will travel to Washington this week and will be facing off against the Commanders on Monday Night Football. The Bengals are coming off of a big victory against the Steelers to improve to 7-2 on the season and take sole possession of the AFC North lead.

The Commanders are coming off of a must-win victory against the New York Giants that keeps them alive for the NFC East. The Commanders improved to 3-5-1 with the win against the Giants on Thursday Night Football last week, who are still leading that division at 5-3-1 on the season. Let's take a look at some of the most interesting matchups for the primetime showdown this week.

Dexter Lawrence will draw double and even triple teams on a weekly basis; he is just that much of a dominant force. One of those players who will be tasked with slowing him down is likely to be Lucas Patrick. Patrick spent the 2025 season with the Bengals but saw limited playing time after suffering an early-season injury.

This year, Patrick has been the starting center for Washington for the majority of the season. He did miss the last game, as he suffered a concussion the week prior, and the Commanders were playing on a short week. He is expected to be back this week, though.

If Patrick cannot go, it would be Nick Alliegretti drawing the start at center. Either way, that player gets to go against the dominant force that is Dexter Lawrence. Lawrence did not get credit for a sack against the Steelers, due to an errant snap ruling that credited Rodgers as a runner on the tackle, but he was a wrecking ball once again. He drew so many double and triple teams that it opened things up for his teammates to have big games last week.

However the Commanders choose to play it, they will have their hands full with Sexy Dexy.

Chaisson is leading the Commanders with 25 pressures on the season. He has 3.5 sacks this year. Odafe Oweh is leading the team in sacks, having recorded 5 of them on his 21 pressures. Amarius Mims gave up one pressure against the talented Steelers defense last week, and has given up just 6 total pressures all season. Orlando Brown Jr. is playing his best stretch of football recently, and didn't allow a single pressure against Pittsburgh.

While Chaisson and Oweh are talented players, I will take our guys at tackle on paper in about any matchup left on the schedule. They have faced a ton of good pass rushers to this point in the season, and have more than held their own against the majority of those players.

Sonny Styles was the Washington Commanders' first-round draft selection this past season. Styles has been impressive during his rookie season. This is a guy that can do it all at the linebacker position. He can effectively rush the passer, play the run at a high level, and can cover as a former safety early in his career at Ohio State. One guy that he will be tasked with covering this week is Chase Brown.

Brown had arguably his best overall game this past week against Pittsburgh. He had over 80 all-purpose yards and caught 2 touchdown passes from Joe Burrow. The Styles vs. Brown matchup should be a fun watch both in the run game and in the passing game on Monday night.

It will take a team effort to slow down Jayden Daniels. The last time the Bengals played against the Commanders, Daniels had more touchdowns than incompletions thrown. Daniels went 21 of 23 for 254 passing yards and 2 passing touchdowns. He also added a rushing touchdown and 39 more yards on the ground. The Bengals defense will have to account for his dual-threat play-making ability all night long.

This is a different Bengals defense for sure, but that doesn't take away from what Daniels is capable of on any given night. Daniels and the Washington offense likely realize they are going to have to put up a lot of points to try and keep pace with the Bengals offense. They were able to do that last time these teams matched up, but can they do it again against Dexter Lawrence, Jonathan Allen, Boye Mafe, and the rest of this revamped Cincinnati defense?

The Bengals defense is coming off of two quality performances in a row. They held Atlanta to just 17 points in Madrid a couple of weeks ago. Then, they put on a dominant performance in a 38-18 victory over the Steelers, in a game that wasn't even really that close. This should be one heck of a matchup, and a big test for the Bengals defense in primetime on the road.
