Ohio State-Michigan tickets plummet in value compared to last year. See prices

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

It's more than $100 cheaper to get into Ohio Stadium on Nov. 28 to watch Ohio State football take on Michigan than the 2025 edition of "The Game."

Both Ohio State and Michigan are unranked in the US LBM Coaches Poll. Ohio State is 7-4 record and lost its fourth game of the season on Nov. 21 at Nebraska. The Buckeyes last lost four games in a regular season in 2011 while interim head coach Luke Fickell was at the helm for Jim Tressel, who resigned before the season.

Michigan is 6-5 under first-year head coach Kyle Whittingham. The Wolverines won their latest against previously undefeated UCLA 18-16 in triple overtime.

Ticket prices for the Ohio State-Michigan game start at $140 on StubHub and $169 on Vivid Seats. Seats in the lower bowl start from $215-250 on each site, with the cheapest tickets located in the north end zone.

During the week before the game last season, ticket prices at Michigan Stadium started at $319.
