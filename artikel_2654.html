Selby faces tough UK opener, with Allen set to feature

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Four-time world champion Mark Selby will begin his defence of the UK Championship title against Matt Selt on Saturday at York Barbican.

It represents a tough opening match for Selby, with fellow Englishman Selt having beaten world number 17 Jak Jones, the highest-ranked player in the qualifiers, to reach the televised stages.

Mark Allen, who spoke of his mental health struggles in an emotional interview last month, is set to face Yuan Sijun or 2023 Crucible winner Luca Brecel.

Allen, from Antrim, lost to his friend Jordan Brown in the first round of the Northern Ireland Open in October after five months away from the sport.

Prior to that, Allen had not played since missing what would have been a match-winning black off its spot in the World Championship semi-final against eventual champion Wu Yize in May.

Record eight-time UK Championship winner Ronnie O'Sullivan, whose form has deserted him since reaching the semi-finals of the Wuhan Open in August, will play Jack Lisowski or Mark Joyce in the first round.

World number one Zhao Xintong will meet either Scott Donaldson or Dylan Emery, while world champion Wu Yize gets under way against qualifier Michael Holt.

Elsewhere, John Higgins, 51, is up against one of snooker's new generation in 20-year-old Artemijs Zizins, who is the first Latvian player to reach the final stages of a Triple Crown event.

Mark Williams will play Julien Leclercq or Stephen Maguire - the latter was UK champion in 2004 and also reached the final in 2007 and 2019 - and Judd Trump will come up against Hossein Vafaei or Ben Mertens.

Kyren Wilson, who is in a rich vein of form after winning the Champion of Champions, the International Championship and the Northern Ireland Open already this autumn, begins his quest to reach a first UK final against Fan Zhengyi.

The first Triple Crown event of the season, which will be shown live on the BBC, takes place from 28 November to 6 December and offers a top prize of £312,500.
