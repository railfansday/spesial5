Ian Wright urges Arsenal to fix one part of their squad in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ian Wright remains pleased with the quality available in the current Arsenal squad, but the former striker believes Mikel Arteta must address one particular area if the Gunners are to reach their full potential.

Defensively, Arsenal have established themselves among the strongest teams in world football, while their midfield also possesses enough quality to dominate matches against top opposition. However, their attacking performances have raised concerns, particularly when it comes to converting chances into goals.

Arteta has built a team that is difficult to break down, with much of their success coming from their defensive organisation and ability to prevent opponents from scoring. While this approach has delivered positive results, Arsenal also need to become more productive in front of goal if they want to compete consistently for major trophies.

The Gunners have several talented attacking players, but their performances have not always reflected the quality within the squad. There are concerns about the depth and consistency of their forward options, and Wright believes the club must consider strengthening that department.

The former Arsenal striker is particularly concerned about the options available to Arteta in wide attacking positions. Although Noni Madueke has impressed and provided additional opportunities to manage Bukayo Saka's workload, Wright does not believe the squad has enough quality across the entire forward line.

He also expressed concerns about the departures of Leandro Trossard and Gabriel Martinelli, while questioning whether other attacking options have demonstrated enough consistency to justify his confidence.

Speaking about Arsenal's attacking depth and the need for reinforcements, Wright said via the Metro:

'Noni is playing unbelievably to the point where he's actually giving Saka even more rest, just as Ezri Konsa's done with Saliba.

'I'm just thinking we haven't got enough. To get rid of Trossard, to get rid of Martinelli. We've got Tzolis who looks like he's, the last couple of games he's not really looked impressive enough where I'm putting my money on him because he looks like he's going to get to do this because I don't see it.

'Up front, missing those chances, the same with the last game. Something has got to happen in January.'

(Photo by Justin Setterfield/Getty Images)

Wright believes Arsenal cannot afford to ignore their attacking shortcomings, particularly with important fixtures ahead. The January transfer window could provide Arteta with an opportunity to strengthen his forward options and give the team greater firepower for the remainder of the campaign.
