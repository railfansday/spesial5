Jaguars vs. Titans: Matchup History Through the Decades

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Following their win last week during Thursday Night Football, the Jags will be on the road this Sunday in Nashville. Football has been fun lately, so it pains me to talk about the Tennessee Titans (fuck 'em). They're like taxes: you know it's happening every year and dread it, but persevere. The two teams have crossed paths 63 times, and the bad guys currently lead the series 35-28. While we hope we get another iconic Trevor stiff-arm moment, let's revisit the Titans losing at home against the Jaguars throughout the decades.

Week 10 on November 2, 1997Final score: Jaguars 30 – Oilers 24Attendance: 27, 208

The Titans were the Tennessee Oilers at this point in time, and this was their first loss to the Jags after leaving Texas. Chris Hudson, Pete Mitchell, and Mark Brunell scored touchdowns. Brunell completed 17 out of 31 attempted passes for 169 yards and was sacked three times for a loss of 14 yards. James Stewart led the team with 99 rushing yards, and Jimmy Smith led with 50 receiving yards. Mike Hollis made the three attempted field goals. Chris Hudson had a six-yard interception, and Steve McNair was sacked twice for a loss of 16 yards.

Week 10 on November 11, 2007Final score: Jaguars 28 – Titans 13Attendance: 69,143

I'm attempting to manifest another Week 10 win in Tennessee. Greg Jones, Maurice Jones-Drew, and Fred Taylor scored touchdowns. Quinn Gray completed 13 out of 23 attempted passes for 101 yards and was sacked once for a loss of five yards. Maurice Jones-Drew led the team with 101 rushing yards and 28 receiving yards, averaged 14.50 yards in kickoff returns, and averaged 14.00 yards in punt returns. Mike Peterson and Brian Williams intercepted two of Vince Young's passes. Reggie Hayward, Clint Ingram, and Jeremy Mincey sacked Young four times for a loss of 27 yards.

Week 13 on December 5, 2010Final score: Jaguars 17 – Titans 6Attendance: 69,143

Rashad Jennings and David Garrard scored touchdowns. Garrard completed 14 out of 19 attempted passes for 126 yards and was sacked once for a seven-yard loss. Maurice Jones-Drew led the team with 186 rushing yards, and Marcedes Lewis led with 36 receiving yards. Josh Scobee made one of the three attempted field goals. Derek Cox and William Middleton recorded interceptions. Jeremy Mincey recorded a sack on Kerry Collins for a loss of six yards. Rashean Mathis and Daryl Smith led the team with five combined tackles each.

Week 13 on November 30, 2025Final Score: Jaguars 25 – Titans 3Attendance: 54,098

The Titans scored their only points during the first quarter, and it was all Jacksonville football afterwards. Jakobi Meyers answered with a touchdown after a 50-yard catch-and-run brought the Jags to the five-yard line. Special teams forced a fumble and recovered the ball. Lawrence found Brenton Strange for another touchdown, then Trevor ran the two-point conversion in himself. In the second half, we saw a Tuten touchdown and a loose ball recovered by the Jags. Another game with Big Ham filling in at long snapper.
