Marlon Humphrey back with Ravens, Jesse Minter doesn't commit to Week 12 return to lineup

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Ravens cornerback Marlon Humphrey was back at the team’s facility on Monday.

The Ravens sent Humphrey home last week and deactivated him for Sunday’s loss to the Panthers after Humphrey made derogatory comments about the city of Baltimore on his podcast. Humphrey apologized, but Minter said the decision to sit him out last week was made after conversations inside the organization.

Minter did not divulge details of that conversation and declined to share any of what he and Humphrey talked about on Monday. He said a decision on Humphrey’s return against the Texans in Week 12 will be made later in the week.

“I expect us to work through the week and see where we’re at,” Minter said.

Humphrey started the first 10 games of the season and has 40 tackles, two forced fumbles and a fumble recovery this year.
