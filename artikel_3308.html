Hans Christian Andersen Animated Biopic In The Works With Beloved Characters Part Of The Story

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

EXCLUSIVE: An animated biopic of legendary children’s author Hans Christian Andersen is in the works from Illustrious Media Group and a pair of Black List writers.

Hans: Fairy Tale Of My life, a feature-length animated family film, is currently in development from veteran producer Christopher Milburn, whose credits include The Strangers franchise, Hypnotic, and the upcoming animated family film The Angry Birds Movie 3 (on which he’s an exec producer).

Black List writers Devon Kliger (Daredevil: Born Again) and Daniel Persitz (Ouija) are scripting. Milburn will produce alongside Max Scott and Sean Finegan. The script “follows a young Andersen as he struggles to find his voice as a storyteller, drawing him into a fantastical world inspired by his own imagination.” Watch on Deadline

The story is said to weave together iconic characters from Andersen’s beloved fairy tales, and producers are positioning it as a potential launchpad for a larger animated universe.

Nineteenth century Danish writer Andersen was best known for his fairy tales, including The Little Mermaid, The Emperor’s New Clothes, The Princess And The Pea, The Snow Queen, The Ugly Duckling and Thumbelina. Many have inspired ballets, plays, and animated and live-action films.

Hans: Fairy Tale Of My Life marks the third animated feature for Illustrious Media Group, which currently has Lock & Grange: Gone Bananas in development and Angry Birds 3 upcoming in collaboration with Dimension Studio and DNEG.

Kliger and Persitz are also developing Key Of Genius, a live-action feature about musical savant Derek Paravicini, which Persitz is attached to direct. The film is currently casting and targeting production in the UK.

Kliger is represented by IAG and Frankfurt Kurnit Klein & Selz.
