Regional Spotlight: California Home Sales Hit Lowest Level as Prices, Mortgage Rates Rise

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

(TNS)–As the year comes to an end, California’s housing market has slowed down.

Existing home sales have dropped to their lowest level in nearly three years as high mortgage rates and affordability challenges continue.

“Homebuying activity weakened across California in October, pushing sales to their lowest level in nearly three years as elevated mortgage rates and low affordability remained significant hurdles for prospective buyers,” the California Association of Realtors said in its latest housing report, released on Tuesday, Nov. 17.

Existing single-family home sales totaled 246,830 in October.

That is a decrease of 5.2% from the 260,410 sales in September and 12.7% from the 282,760 sales in October 2025.

“When mortgage rates jumped to a nearly three-year high in late September, we saw many buyers step back and reassess what they could afford, which led to fewer closed sales in October,” California Association of Realtors President Mark Peterson said in the report.

Despite the slowdown in sales, real estate experts said, home prices remained relatively steady, while mortgage rates continued to pose a challenge for potential buyers.

What can California homebuyers expect to pay?

Here’s what to know about home prices, mortgage rates and where buyers can find more affordable options across the state:

How much does buying a home cost in California?

The statewide median home price was $886,760 in October, according to the California Association of Realtors.

That was down 1.4% from $899,720 in September but barely changed from $887,330 in October 2025.

California’s median home price previously reached a record $930,260 in May.

“Home prices held steady, suggesting that buyers are still engaged and sellers remain confident in the value of their homes despite the near-term slowdown,” Peterson said.

Still, affordability remains a major obstacle for many prospective homebuyers across the state.

How much are current mortgage rates in California?

Mortgage rates continued to climb in October, adding to the challenges facing homebuyers, according to the California Association of Realtors.

The group’s report put the average mortgage rate at 7.33%—up from the 6.86% in September and 6.25% in October 2025.

Zillow listed the average 30-year fixed mortgage rate in California at about 7.38%, as of Thursday, Nov. 19.

The combination of higher borrowing costs and economic uncertainty could continue to weigh on home sales in the coming year, according to Jordan Levine, the California Association of Realtors’ senior vice president and chief economist.

“The housing market is facing renewed headwinds as inflation remains elevated and the stock market loses momentum,” Levine said in the housing report. “Home sales will likely remain soft in the near term, but pent-up demand continues to build as many prospective buyers stay on the sidelines.”

If geopolitical tensions and economic uncertainty ease, Levine said, California “should see more of those buyers gradually return to the market.”

According to the California Association of Realtors, the state’s most affordable homes can be found in Northern California, with a median price of $385,000.
