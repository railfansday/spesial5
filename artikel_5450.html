Aura Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results, Highlights Sustained Business Momentum and Transformative Platform Innovation

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

BOSTON, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Aura Consolidated Group, Inc. ARBN 695 488 843 (ASX: AXQ) ("Aura" or "the Company"), a global leader in online safety and wellbeing, today announced its financial results for the third quarter of 2026, the first quarter since completing the acquisition of Qoria on July 17, 2026 ("Transaction Close").

On a pro forma basis, Aura exited the quarter with $355.0 million in annual recurring revenue ("ARR"), up 24% year over year, and generated positive Adjusted FCF of $12.4 million, a $15.5 million improvement year over year. The Company reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance of 20%+ ARR growth and positive Adjusted Free Cash Flow from Transaction Close to year-end.

Reflecting on the achievements in Q3'26, Aura's Founder and CEO, Hari Ravichandran, said:

"This quarter, we laid the foundation for our expanded long-term vision for Aura while delivering sustained revenue growth and positive free cash flow. At the same time, we are accelerating our pace of innovation, and earlier this week introduced Solstice, our in-house AI engine, along with an updated Aura experience, the first product built on that foundation.

"For decades, consumer security has pushed the hard work back onto the user. We're reimagining protection from the ground up with Solstice and will unlock what no consumer security product has had: a deep understanding of the family, and the ability to help take action when the family asks."

Aura's Chief Financial Officer, Brian DeCenzo, added, "As generative AI fuels more frequent and sophisticated scams and new risks to children online, more families, schools, and employers are turning to Aura as their trusted source of protection. We are translating broad-based customer growth and strong product uptake into robust, sustainable revenue growth. That momentum, paired with our ongoing focus on operational efficiency, leaves us well positioned to deliver on both our 20%+ ARR growth and positive free cash flow targets this year, while building toward our medium-term financial objectives."

Aura is executing ahead of plan on the $55 million cost-out program outlined in February 2026, with year-to-date and planned Q4 actions representing approximately $66 million in total savings. This includes $42 million in annualized direct and operating cost savings, driven by organizational realignment that significantly reduced the Company's fixed cost base. Direct-to-Consumer ("D2C") marketing spend is expected to decline $24 million in 2026 compared with 2025, while continuing to support investment in efficient growth.

Aura's acquisition of Qoria became effective on July 17, 2026. As such, Q3'26 unaudited statutory financials reflect Aura's full-quarter results, together with Qoria's post-close results from July 17, 2026 through September 30, 2026. To provide a more representative view of the combined group's performance and facilitate period-over-period comparability, this release and the related materials focus on unaudited pro forma results, combining Aura and Qoria for the full duration of each period presented. All dollar amounts are reported in U.S. currency unless otherwise noted.

Aura continuously evaluates strategic opportunities to optimize its business. As part of that ongoing assessment, management is evaluating a potential dual listing on a U.S. stock exchange alongside its ASX listing. While the Company remains secure in its capital position, management believes a dual listing could support its objective of maximizing long-term shareholder value through an expanded investor base and improved trading liquidity. Any potential dual listing remains subject to approval by Aura's Board of Directors and the completion of required regulatory and other processes.
