Siteimprove.ai Accessibility Agent Now Available for AI Agents Through Agentic Commerce on AWS Marketplace

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

COPENHAGEN, Denmark, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Siteimprove, the agentic content intelligence platform, announced today that it is a launch partner for Agentic Commerce on AWS Marketplace, making the Siteimprove.ai Accessibility Agent available for AI agents to pay for per request through Amazon Bedrock AgentCore payments, with charges in US dollars on the buyer's existing AWS bill.

With Agentic Commerce, AI agents can discover and pay for the Siteimprove.ai Accessibility Agent at the moment they need it, within the spending limits and policies their organization sets. This lets Siteimprove turn each agent request into a monetization event, while buyers keep governance and cost visibility in the tools they already use. This helps digital teams put accessibility checks and fixes to work in their agents faster, with accessibility issues in their content caught and closed as it's created.

Agentic Commerce on AWS Marketplace lets AI agents pay per request for APIs listed by AWS Marketplace sellers through Amazon Bedrock AgentCore payments, using an extension of the x402 payment protocol. Charges appear in US dollars on the buyer's existing AWS bill, with no crypto wallet required. Builders using Amazon Bedrock AgentCore can discover Agentic Commerce listings through AgentCore Gateway. Organizations keep control through spending limits, approved vendor lists, and IAM-based policies. Transactions are limited to verified AWS Marketplace sellers that have completed KYC compliance checks, and charges appear on the buyer's AWS bill with visibility in AWS Cost Explorer and audit records in AWS CloudTrail.

"AI agents are becoming part of how digital teams get work done, and they need trusted insight to do it well. By making the Siteimprove.ai Accessibility Agent available through Agentic Commerce on AWS Marketplace, we let our customers' AI agents get what they need the moment they need it, with the governance their organization requires," said Clay Garrard, CTO, Siteimprove.

The Siteimprove.ai Accessibility Agent is available through Agentic Commerce on AWS Marketplace today. To learn more, visit our page on AWS Marketplace.

About SiteimproveFounded in 2003, Siteimprove transforms access to the digital world by providing an agentic content intelligence platform that unifies accessibility, analytics, SEO/AEO, and content strategy. Today, global 2000 clients across manufacturing, government, higher education, financial services, and healthcare rely on Siteimprove.ai, an agentic content intelligence platform to deliver both content that performs and that is compliant. Based in Copenhagen, Bellevue, Minneapolis and London, Siteimprove is a single, actionable source of truth for digital content and development teams across many of the largest global enterprises, government entities and learning institutions. Siteimprove is majority-owned by Nordic Capital.
