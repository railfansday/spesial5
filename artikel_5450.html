Iraq is pushing OPEC to raise its oil production ceiling before Sunday's meeting

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Oil Minister Basim Mohammed Khudair said Iraq wants OPEC to weigh its history of war as the group sets output benchmarks

Iraq is negotiating with OPEC to raise its oil production ceiling, Oil Minister Basim Mohammed Khudair said at a press conference in Kirkuk on Friday, according to Bloomberg.

"We are negotiating with OPEC to raise Iraq's production ceiling," Khudair said, according to Bloomberg. "Iraq has faced major challenges and wars for more than five decades. Therefore, we hope that OPEC member countries will take Iraq's circumstances into consideration."

"As a founding member of the organization, Iraq needs to rebuild its infrastructure and improve the living conditions of its citizens," the minister said, according to Bloomberg. "It therefore needs its production ceiling to be raised and to be given special consideration."

OPEC hired an outside firm to estimate the volume of crude its members are able to produce, according to Bloomberg. The results will serve as reference points when future production quotas are set, and a bigger figure gives a country room to pump more. Each member learned its own assessed figure over the course of this month.

Baghdad refused to accept a cap that falls under 5 million barrels daily, according to Bloomberg, which cited unnamed sources.

Iraq had turned down the commissioned review of its capacity, which put its maximum at less than 5 million barrels a day. Khudair wrote to OPEC to insist that Iraq can reach 5.2 million barrels a day.

OPEC and its allies, under Saudi leadership, plan to rely on the findings to set production targets at an online meeting on Sunday, Nov. 29. The group has been examining what each member can technically produce as groundwork for 2027 output targets.

Iraq said earlier this year that it could have to exit OPEC unless it was granted a sufficiently generous ceiling, according to Bloomberg. Iraq withdrew the threat. The United Arab Emirates had left the group by then.
