Medicare’s Part B premium keeps climbing past $200, and CMS blames health care spending

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

CMS set the 2027 Part B deductible at $292, up from $283, and the Part A hospital deductible at $1,776, up $40

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services set the standard monthly Medicare Part B premium at $209.40 for 2027 on Tuesday, up $6.50 from $202.90 in 2026. The agency linked the higher premium and deductible to expected increases in health care spending.

The annual Part B deductible for all beneficiaries will be $292 in 2027, up $9 from $283 in 2026, CMS said. Part B covers physicians' services, outpatient hospital services, certain home health services and durable medical equipment.

The inpatient hospital deductible under Part A will climb $40 to $1,776, from $1,736. Daily coinsurance for the 61st through 90th day of a hospital stay will be $444, up from $434. Skilled nursing facility coinsurance for days 21 through 100 will be $222, up from $217.

Most Part A enrollees pay no premium. Those who buy in after earning 30 or more quarters of coverage, either on their own or through a spouse, will pay $328 a month, up from $311. Enrollees who must pay the full Part A premium will pay $597 a month, up from $565.

Higher-income beneficiaries pay more for Part B. Total monthly premiums for people with full Part B coverage range from $293.10 for individual filers with modified adjusted gross income above $112,000 up to $711.80 for those at $500,000 or more. Joint filers face the same range at income thresholds of $224,000 and $750,000. The standard premium for the immunosuppressive drug coverage available after a kidney transplant will be $125.70.

The $6.50 increase works out to 3.2%, according to Money. Money also reported that the increase falls short of last year's $17.90 jump, the program's second-steepest on record. The Social Security Act requires the premium to cover roughly a quarter of the projected costs of serving beneficiaries age 65 and older.

The 2027 premium would total $2,512.80 over a year, according to MarketWatch. MarketWatch said the figure was close to expectations the Social Security and Medicare Board of Trustees set in June, and that the 2026 premium was the first to top $200 a month.

The Social Security Administration announced a 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment for 2027 on Oct. 14, which lifts the typical retiree's monthly check by $75 beginning in January. Part B and Part D costs are often withheld directly from monthly Social Security checks. The standard Part B premium had been projected at $209.50 for 2027 in a report from Medicare trustees.

Medicare enrollees can see their 2027 premium amount in their my Social Security Message Center starting in late November, the Social Security Administration said. Information on Medicare changes for 2027 will be available at www.medicare.gov.
