Oreo refreshes holiday creative with spin on Santa’s milk and cookies

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Saint Nick takes revenge on a dad who sneaks a bite of his Oreos in the brand’s first new seasonal work in five years.

Oreo turned to Jost’s No Notes to develop the lighthearted campaign while Dentsu handled socials. Publicis Production served as consulting agency on the push while production was handled by Good Company and Merchant.

Mondelēz started the year with a review of its U.S. creative agencies amid business pressures on its biscuits segment. In May, the packaged foods giant switched Oreo and Ritz from Omnicom’s Martin to Stagwell’s 72andSunny, among other changes, Ad Age reported.

Mondelēz saw organic net revenues rise 2.1% in Q3 while volumes were up 1.8% over the period. Product innovations, including a zero-sugar version of Oreo, contributed to some of the momentum.
