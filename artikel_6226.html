The closest real example might be my strategy for the 2011 US Airways Grand Slam promotion. You earned a big bonus by completing transactions with different partners. Getting to 36 qualifying activities earned 100,000 bonus miles.

Transferred points from five different hotel programs. Converted four American Airlines miles into one US Airways mile. Spent 500 miles on a magazine subscription I didn’t want. Installed the airline’s browser toolbar, performed three searches to earn one mile, and then disabled the toolbar.

This isn’t even something from a less mature period of my life.
