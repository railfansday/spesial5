Spalletti: ‘Ekhator will play against Omonoia, Yildiz is doing great’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Luciano Spalletti has confirmed that Jeff Ekhator is set to play for Juventus in their Europa League match against Omonoia on Thursday evening, says that Kenan Yildiz is progressing well in his recovery from injury, but grew frustrated when asked about his striker situation during his pre-match press conference on Wednesday.

Juventus are due to take on Omonoia at the Allianz Stadium at 20.00 GMT on Thursday evening in their fifth match of the Europa League season.

The Bianconeri have started encouragingly in the Europa League with three victories and one draw across their first four matches, which has them in fifth place ahead of this week’s round of fixtures. Omonoia have won two and lost two of their first four games, leaving them in 18th place on six points.

Spalletti’s press conference began with a quick injury update. The Bianconeri are without long-term absentees Yildiz, Khephren Thuram and Manuel Locatelli, and will also remain without Nico Gonzalez or Pierre Kalulu on Thursday night.

“We have got some players back, except for Nico and Kalulu, the others are available. We have to prepare as best we can because training sessions are starting to get busier. That is a positive, though.”

Spalletti then grew frustrated when asked for clarification on his recent comments about wanting a new striker in January.

Speaking after Juventus’s 4-0 win over Venezia in Serie A over the weekend, Spalletti had discussed the characteristics of his current striking options. Neither Randal Kolo Muani nor Nick Woltemade are considered ‘traditional’ no.9s and Ekhator has been impacted by injuries since joining.

There have been suggestions that Juventus could move for a physically imposing centre-forward in January, but Spalletti was not pleased at his comments being labeled a ‘desperate cry’ for intervention in the transfer window.

“Calling it a ‘desperate cry’ were your words, not mine,” Spalletti argued. “How many times have you asked me about Vlahovic? Even after he left. I spoke about the need for certain characteristics, not a certain player.

“I want to try Kolo in a different position. Woltemade has done very well since coming in, but can’t play all the time. I thought I would put Kolo Muani back there [at centre-forward] for the Venezia game. That was not a desperate cry. I never said that I don’t like Woltemade. We also have Ekhator now and we have to see how he’s doing. He’s a promising young player, we’ll let him play a few times and see if we already have those qualities in-house. When Nico returns, he also has the qualities of a no.9.”

Returning to the topic of tomorrow’s game, Spalletti gave his assessment of the visiting Omonoia.

“Omonia is a team that has picked up six points and beat Celta Vigo. They’re third in their league and have a Norwegian coach who knows modern football very well. They’re a team that plays in a 4-2-3-1 but they are willing to sacrifice and go into a five when they defend. They’ve struggled in some matches, but they always create difficulty for their opponents. We will need to play a very serious game to win.

“We have a lot of top quality players and they’re all good. We also need to develop some young players. We’ll play Ekhator tomorrow because we want an extra solution moving forwards. We will make rotations that we trust in. We’re aware of what is at stake.”

Commenting further on Ekhator’s qualities, Spalletti added: “I think he’s a physical striker who attacks the space well. He’s a player who makes bad balls playable. Baldini speaks very highly of him and Baldini is one of my close friends within football. I’m getting to know him a bit better now and I agree with Baldini.

“I expect him to be calm, but he’s a player with good prospects and all great players started out as a talent with good prospects before reaching that level.”

Spalletti also offered an encouraging update on Yildiz, who has not featured since the first game of the Serie A season.

“He’s doing great. This morning, he started a specialised training session and I can’t wait to have him back in games. We have to wait for the right timing. Being without a player of his level creates a void we somehow need to fill. Being able to replace him with Alajbegovic, Boga or Kolo is great, though, but Yildiz is a champion you can’t simply just replace.”

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
