What If Money Wasn’t the Barrier to Your PCT Dream?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Pacific Crest Trail is a dream for thousands of people. Spanning 2,650 miles from Mexico to Canada, the trail offers the opportunity to step away from everyday life, immerse yourself in the outdoors, and experience something that can be difficult to put into words.

But getting to the trail takes more than a dream. For many aspiring thru-hikers, the financial commitment is one of the biggest barriers. Between gear, food, transportation, and the ability to step away from work for several months, the cost of a thru-hike can put the experience out of reach before someone even takes their first step.

The PCT Grant was created to help change that.

Through the Trail Angel Fund, the PCT Grant provides $5,000 annually to a Washingtonian (and so far this year, we have raised enough money to send off TWO hikers!) between the ages of 18 and 33 who dream of thru-hiking the Pacific Crest Trail. Its mission is to make long-distance hiking more accessible by easing the financial burden and giving someone the opportunity to pursue an experience they might not otherwise be able to afford.

At its heart, the program is about expanding access to the outdoors and recognizing that the transformative experiences found on trail shouldn’t be limited to those who can afford them.

2026 was the first year the PCT Grant came to life and chose its first recipient to take on thru-hiking the Pacific Crest Trail. That person was me! The face behind the screen: Cappy. Post trail, I have joined the PCT Grant Committee to spread some magic with hopes of creating that same opportunity for others.

Receiving the grant was a lifeline. It wasn’t just financial support; it was the difference between being able to pursue this dream and having to put it on hold. It gave me room to take a chance on myself and say yes to an experience that changed the way I see what’s possible in life.

As this year’s recipient, I experienced firsthand what this kind of support can make possible. The trail taught me a lot about the power of choice and trust. The power of choosing something before knowing exactly how it will unfold, doing it scared, and learning to become comfortable with the uncomfortable.

Spending five months on trail offered so much more than the physical accomplishment of a thru-hike. I discovered how much we are capable of when we give ourselves the opportunity to find out. This trail changed my life, and it will continue to do so.

As the program enters its second year, the focus is on growing awareness, strengthening community support, and building a foundation for future recipients.

Currently, the grant is funded to support one hiker annually, with a longer-term vision of expanding its reach and helping more aspiring thru-hikers access the trail. From our fundraising efforts (a post trail Q&A with Cappy and other media platforms helping to spread the word) we have raised enough money to fund a second hiker this coming year.

Donations play an important role in making that possible. Every contribution helps sustain the program and move it closer to supporting additional hikers in the years ahead. We want to continue to grow this program so that even more hikers can set foot on the trail.

Applications for the 2027 PCT Grant are open through January 31, 2027. If you’re a Washingtonian between 18 and 33 with a dream of hiking the PCT, this could be an opportunity to make it happen.

And for those who have already experienced the magic of the trail, consider helping someone else get there.
