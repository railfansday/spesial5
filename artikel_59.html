The forgotten victims of femicide: Children orphaned by violence in Italy

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

November 25th is International Day for the Elimination of Violence Against Women. The forgotten victims of femicide are the children who find themselves motherless, often because their mother was killed by their own father. Italy recorded 97 female homicides in 2025 – 62 of them were committed by a partner. These crimes leave whole families devastated and children who have to grow up without their parents. FRANCE 24’s correspondent in Italy, Natalia Mendoza, reports.
