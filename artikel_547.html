Telefloras new Hunger Games bouquet collection brings Panem to your doorstep — 20% off promo code

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Ahead of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping hitting theaters on Nov. 20, Teleflora is teaming up with Lionsgate to bring a piece of Panem straight to your doorstep. The floral delivery service just rolled out Let Hope Bloom, a limited-edition collection of bouquets and plant baskets inspired by the world of the upcoming prequel.

Directed by Francis Lawrence and adapted from Suzanne Collins' bestselling novel, The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping jumps back 24 years before the original trilogy to follow a 16-year-old Haymitch Abernathy (Joseph Zada) during the 50th Annual Hunger Games — also known as the Second Quarter Quell — where he forms an alliance with fellow tribute Maysilee Donner (Mckenna Grace) to challenge President Snow (Ralph Fiennes) from inside the arena.

Teleflora built the "Let Hope Bloom" collection around the story's themes of hope, remembrance, and the bold colors of the Arena. Every arrangement is handcrafted and delivered by a local florist in a keepsake vase, planter, or woven basket, with prices across the collection ranging from under $50 to $120. Here's a closer look at a few of our favorites from the lineup above:

Teleflora's Modern Chic Bouquet — Green disbud and button chrysanthemums, white cushion spray chrysanthemums, blue eryngium, blue sinuata statice, dusty miller, spiral eucalyptus, and lemon leaf arranged in Teleflora's Classic Contemporary Planter with a wooden base.

Spring Has Sprung Mixed Basket Plant — Four potted plants (purple African violet, yellow begonia, hot pink kalanchoe, and white polka dot plant) arranged inside a natural handled basket.

Secret Garden Basket Plant — A round wicker basket packed with five live potted plants: maidenhair fern, Boston fern, pink kalanchoe, pink hypoestes, and green nephthytis.

Teleflora's Garden Symphony Bouquet — Green roses, bells of Ireland, green carnations, green button spray chrysanthemums, variegated aspidistra leaves, scented geranium leaves, and variegated pittosporum set inside the concrete-inspired Modern Paradise Cube.

Teleflora's Floral Spring Iris Bouquet — Purple hydrangea, yellow roses, yellow tulips, white asiatic lilies, blue iris, lavender freesia, lavender matsumoto asters, white sinuata statice, bupleurum, and lemon leaf gathered in a clear glass vase.

Colors of the Rainbow Bouquet — A bright mix of purple hydrangea, pink roses, yellow spray roses, orange asiatic lilies, purple alstroemeria, blue delphinium, pink snapdragons, blue eryngium, huckleberry, oregonia, Israeli ruscus, sword fern, silver dollar eucalyptus, seeded eucalyptus, and lemon leaf delivered in a jordan vase.

The "Let Hope Bloom" collection is available nationwide through Dec. 31, and readers can enter promo code HUNGERGAMES at checkout for 20% off bouquets from the collection.

Outside of online orders, Teleflora also built a larger-than-life floral display at Westfield Century City in Los Angeles as part of Lionsgate's The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping Cornucopia Immersive Experience near AMC Theatres, which stays open for fan photos through Nov. 22.

You can check out the full Let Hope Bloom lineup and apply code HUNGERGAMES for 20% off.
