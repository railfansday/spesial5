NYC rent freeze beats landlord challenge

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

While a state judge upheld the rent freeze, he called the mayor's campaign promises to enact it "reckless and irresponsible."

MANHATTAN (CN) â New York City’s “rent freeze” affecting more than a million apartment units survived landlords’ challenge on Wednesday as a state judge dismissed entirely their lawsuit aimed at blocking the measure.

The Thanksgiving Eve ruling upholds the NYC Rent Guidelines Board’s June 25 order adjusting the annual adjustment for rent stabilized units to 0% for one- and two-year leases that begin between Oct. 1, 2026, and Sept. 30, 2027. The so-called freeze was a core campaign promise for Mayor Zohran Mamdani.

According to the landlords challenging the rent freeze, the mayor packed the board with loyalists who’d carry out his rent-curbing agenda while ignoring rising property operating costs. The city, defending the freeze, said in turn that data shows tenants are financially struggling, and citywide income increases allow landlords to absorb increased costs.

Judge Brendan T. Lantry noted in Wednesday’s ruling that rent regulation is well-established as a “point of contention among building owners and tenants,” and that the city’s rent board “acted in accordance with the law.”

Based on the current record, Lantry said, the landlords’ claims that the board didn’t act independently of the mayor’s office are “without merit.”

But the judge had harsh words for Mamdani’s campaign pledge to freeze the rent, writing, “the court would be remiss not to state that such comments, promises and guarantees to freeze the rent were reckless and irresponsible.”

“The mayor of the city of New York has no authority to unilaterally raise, ‘freeze’ or otherwise adjust the rent of stabilized units in this city âÂ that responsibility is within the power of the board and the board alone,” Lantry continued. “However, without a showing that the board simply adopted Mayor Mamdani’s campaign promise instead of engaging in an evaluation … Mayor Mamdani’s calls to ‘freeze the rent’ are inconsequential to this proceeding.”

That board members share Mamdani’s politics “is of no moment and does not indicate any level of collusion,” the judge said.

Mamdani said in a statement Wednesday that the NYC Law Department had “made clear that the board had acted within its authority and that tenants needed this relief.”

“Today, Judge Lantry affirmed what we knew all along: the Rent Guidelines Board had the authority to deliver for the more than two million New Yorkers who call rent-stabilized apartments home,” the mayor said, calling the ruling a victory for tenants.
