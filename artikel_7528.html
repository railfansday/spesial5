Breaking news: a new James Bond has been chosen!

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The new James Bond has been announced!! Finally, after five years of waiting (since Daniel Craig departed the franchise with/in 2021’s sentimental No Time to Die), we have a new 007.

There has been much, much speculation about who would be the new Bond—the eighth actor to play Bond (in a legitimate adaptation… go away, 1967’s Casino Royale), and the star of the twenty-sixth Bond film. Would it be Slow Horses‘s Jack Lowden? Perhaps Josh O’Connor or Callum Turner? What about a dark horse, like CrimeReads favorite Josh Bowman? Remember way back when, when we all thought it would be Idris Elba? And remember how after that, we all thought it would be Aaron Taylor-Johnson?

Nope! Turns out, the next James Bond will be….

Yes, Robert Pattinson, the creative and hardworking English actor who will likely be nominated for a Supporting Actor Academy Award for his role as Antinous in Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey, this year. Robert Pattinson! Robert Pattinson is Bond!

The Bond franchise has the power to catapult an actor from a lesser-known realm into wild international fame. As I wrote previously,

The individuals I refer to as the James Bond Selection Committee chose Daniel Craig last time; a well-respected actor of stage and screen, known mostly for supporting roles. The point of Bond casting is not to choose someone so closely associated with another leading man role that it becomes distracting; the actor must fully embody Bond.

This time, the selection committee chose Mr. Franchise, himself! Cedric Diggory of Harry Potter, Edward Cullen of Twilight (no matter how hard he tries to forget it), and Batman of The Batman! He’s about to be in Dune: Part III.

Pattinson, who is 40, has worked with countless extraordinary filmmakers in many challenging parts, distancing himself from the heartthrobby roles he played in his youth. He has already lead films directed by David Cronenberg, Claire Denis, Bong Joon Ho, Werner Herzog, the Safdie Brothers, Robert Eggers, James Gray, Lynne Ramsay, Christopher Nolan, Fernando Meirelles, and Denis Villeneuve (who will be directing the next Bond film).

Normally the James Bond persona subsumes the actor… the actor becomes Bond and always remains Bond, no matter what else he does. But Pattinson is so well-known already for his esoteric roles and big-budget blockbuster starring turns, and he himself has played so many famous characters, that this time (for the first time, I dare say), it’s the other way around.

Ben Roberts, the CEO of the British Film Institute (BFI), noted this, remarking, “He’s a different kind of choice… he comes to the table with a huge amount of baggage, adding that the “amazing thing about Robert Pattinson is his ability to be a chameleon in his roles.”

Personally, I think Pattinson is an extraordinarily skilled performer, one who manages to put a creative and memorable take on even the most generic or hard-worn characters. He’s certainly one of the best actors of his generation. I’m sure that he will be a fabulous Bond, even though I am admittedly surprised by the decision. Pattinson won’t let us down, that I know.

Well, this news also means that Matt Reeves’s sequel to his noirish 2022 masterpiece The Batman, titled The Batman: Part II, will probably be the end of that Batman series. I (and others) cannot imagine Pattinson doing a whole Batman performance in between James Bond performances. But… who knows? He’s a wild guy.

Still, Bond is a very demanding role. Just look at what poor Daniel Craig suffered through!

Pattinson was chosen over Nicholas Hoult, Jack Lowden, Josh O’Connor and Callum Turner. The five actors underwent screen tests in London in late October. The auditions involved stunts, dialogue and camera presence; they were overseen by Nina Gold, an Oscar-nominated (“Hamnet”) and Emmy-winning (“Game of Thrones”) casting director. Bond auditions can be revealing: Henry Cavill, who lost the role to Craig in the previous casting search, told Men’s Health in 2019 that he was asked to re-enact a scene in which Sean Connery’s Bond emerged from a bathroom in nothing but a towel.

Notably, however, this is the first James Bond film that will proceed without the famed Broccoli family/EON productions at the helm, ending their six-decade-long stewardship of the brand. In 2022, the tech giant Amazon acquired 50 percent control over the Bond property as part of its 2022 purchase of MGM (for $8.5 billion). Barbara Broccoli and her half-brother Michael G. Wilson, who inherited the rights from her father Albert R. “Cubby” Broccoli upon his passing in 1996, had retained the other 50 percent, and had been guaranteed a final say on virtually everything. They had “ironclad creative control, deciding when to make a new Bond film, who should play the title role and whether remakes and television spinoffs got made” and “also had final say over every line of dialogue, every casting decision, every stunt sequence, every marketing tie-in, as well as every TV ad, poster and billboard.”

But in February 2025, after reported longstanding creative tensions between Barbara and Amazon, Barbara agreed to transfer creative control to Amazon. Wilson and Broccoli have chosen more passive roles. They will remain co-owners of the franchise, but are relinquishing all creative control to Amazon. In March 2025, it was announced that Harry Potter producer David Heyman would be in charge of the Bond property for Amazon, along with Amy Pascal. Their companies Heyday Films and Pascal Pictures, would produce the future Bond installments.

Almost nothing else is known about this next Bond film, except that the screenplay will be an original story (as there are no more of Ian Fleming’s Bond stories to adapt), written by Steven Knight, the creator of the series Peaky Blinders.

Stay tuned for more updates… and in the meantime, congratulations to Robert Pattinson! Cheers!
