Nvidia stock slides again as Bessent calls for a leadership overhaul

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The Treasury secretary said the board should look outside the company for a new CEO as Nvidia prepares to report earnings Tuesday

Photo by Jonathan Raa/NurPhoto via Getty Images

Nvidia $NVDA stock fell about 3.6% in premarket trading on Monday after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called for a "dramatic shakeup" at the chipmaker. Bessent said on Sunday that Nvidia's board ought to find its next CEO from beyond the company's current ranks.

The stock changed hands at $156.97 in early trading as artificial intelligence shares retreated across the board. It fell 2% on Friday.

Bessent suggested that scrutiny should also fall on other Nvidia executives for how well the company has adhered to U.S. export controls. He said Nvidia's sales in China fell while its sales in Singapore surged, and that the company then quit disclosing those figures, according to Forbes. Bessent said he wanted to look into whether Nvidia brushed aside red flags in its dealings with overseas firms, or drove sales that looked illicit in order to boost its revenue and earnings.

"I can tell you that if this was a bank and they hadn't complied like this, the OCC would be in there right now and a lot of the executives would be removed," Bessent said, according to Forbes. He called Nvidia a "wonderful company" and Jensen Huang an "incredible innovator."

Nvidia is set to report fiscal third-quarter earnings on Tuesday, Nov. 17. Scrutiny is moving away from Huang’s private misconduct and toward how Nvidia has managed its chip sales into China, according to Bloomberg.

Some of Huang's allies in Silicon Valley are campaigning to bring him back, according to Bloomberg. An online petition urging that he be restored to the CEO role has gathered over 170,000 signatures, and tech executives including Elon Musk are backing him, according to CNBC. U.S. lawmakers are calling for wide-ranging probes of the scandal.

Huang, who co-founded Nvidia in 1993, resigned as CEO on Thursday after reports of two extramarital affairs. One was with Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed, a major Nvidia customer. U.S. intelligence officials suspect she is an agent of Beijing's spy agencies. Huang disclosed an affair to employees in a Nov. 8 email, according to CNBC.

Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress is serving as interim CEO. The board said it will consider internal and external candidates for a permanent successor.

Nvidia stock fell as much as 14% on Thursday before closing down 4.3%. The company lost hundreds of billions of dollars in market value last week.
