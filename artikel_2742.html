BVB v Bayer – or: Bundesliga’s best defence v the home hoodoo

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

While Borussia last lost in Leverkusen in February 2020 and won four of their next five Bundesliga away games on the Rhine, the Werkself have become something of a bogey team in Dortmund. Marco Reus secured Dortmund's 20th and most recent home win over Bayer on 6 August 2022, scoring the only goal in a 1-0 victory. Since then, BVB have taken just one point from their three subsequent Bundesliga home meetings.

Remarkably, none of the last eight competitive meetings have been won by the home side, with the away team winning every one of the last five encounters!

The two clubs met three times last season. Dortmund's 2-1 win in Leverkusen was followed three days later by a 1-0 defeat in the DFB-Pokal round of 16. The return Bundesliga fixture in April also ended in a 1-0 defeat.

Dortmund's current home record offers encouragement, however: four wins and a draw, with 10 goals scored and three conceded. Borussia have taken 13 of a possible 15 points in front of their own fans, while Leverkusen have collected just seven points from five away games (two wins, one draw, two defeats).

Indeed, BVB are enjoying a historically strong first half of the season. For only the third time in their Bundesliga history, the Black & Yellows are unbeaten after ten matches. They previously achieved this only in 2002/03 under Matthias Sammer and in 2018/19 under Lucien Favre. Another win would take Borussia Dortmund to 27 points – their highest tally after 11 matches in eight years.

Borussia's greatest strength is their defensive solidity. Seven goals conceded in ten matches is the best record in the league. Only Bayern Munich have allowed their opponents fewer big chances than Dortmund (nine). Leverkusen, meanwhile, have already conceded 17 goals, 12 of them away from home. That is five more than BVB have conceded in all their league matches so far this season.

Notably, the Werkself have allowed their opponents fewer attempts on goal (112) than Dortmund (124). That makes the difference between the goalkeepers all the more striking. Gregor Kobel has saved 81 per cent of the shots on his goal, compared with just 58 per cent for Leverkusen's Mark Flekken – the lowest save rate of any Bundesliga goalkeeper.

There are also major differences between the two teams when defending corners: while BVB have yet to concede from one, Bayer have already let in three goals this way, the joint-highest tally in the league. In attack, however, both sides have room for improvement: BVB, who were so strong in this area in the first half of the year, have scored just once from a set piece, while Leverkusen have scored twice. Neither team has been awarded a penalty yet.

Under coach Carles Martinez, Leverkusen are increasingly focusing on wing play in a 4-2-3-1 formation. Their six goals from open-play crosses are the most in the league. Niko Kovac's side are known for their efficiency, patience and staying power. Five of their seven wins this season have come by a single goal. The Black & Yellows have scored eight of their 17 goals from the 80th minute onwards – more than any other Bundesliga side. The options on the bench also work in Kovac's team's favour: their substitutes have scored five goals, compared with two for Leverkusen.
