Big Ten VB quarterfinals - Wisconsin vs. Michigan State: Live updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The No. 3 Wisconsin Badgers are set for their first Big Ten volleyball tournament game in program history.

This is nothing new for No. 11 Michigan State, a team that had to win on both Friday, Nov. 20, and Saturday, Nov. 21, to get to this point.

The Spartans are the lowest seed remaining, and they're the only double-digit seed to make it into the quarterfinals.

MSU swept No. 14 Rutgers in the first round and then upset No. 6 Minnesota in four sets in the second round.

UW has been off since Tuesday, Nov. 17, and comes into the tournament having won seven of its last eight matches.

Keep in mind, Wisconsin beat Michigan State in Madison on Thursday, Nov. 5, 3-1.
