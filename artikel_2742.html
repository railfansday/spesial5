2027 NFL draft order: Browns stay the same; Rams move up despite both on byes in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

In some ways, the only thing that could make Week 11 better for the Cleveland Browns is signing a helpful free agent and the Cincinnati Bengals losing on Monday Night Football. Other than that, the Browns bye week went splendidly, with the Baltimore Ravens, Pittsburgh Steelers, and Las Vegas Raiders all losing, pushing Cleveland's AFC North position and AFC playoff seed each up a spot.

With the 2027 NFL draft order also important for Cleveland's future, our continued "Rams Watch" with the Los Angeles Rams due to the Myles Garrett trade was anticlimactic, with them having a bye in Week 11 as well. The Rams did lose WR Puka Nacua for the year due to an MCL surgery, so that was a big update during the bye week.

Los Angeles' closing schedule is also very difficult:

Week 12: Green Bay Packers with Micah Parsons returning

The Rams and Browns both have a 6-4 record, but Cleveland is slotted into an AFC playoff spot, while Los Angeles is two spots out of one in the NFC. That puts the Rams' first-round pick coming over to the Browns at #17, a spot higher than last week, while Cleveland remains in the 20th spot in the 2027 NFL draft order:

Dallas Cowboys – Goes to the New York Jets

Los Angeles Rams – Goes to the Cleveland Browns

Green Bay Packers – Goes to the Dallas Cowboys

Jacksonville Jaguars – Goes to the Philadelphia Eagles

Chicago Bears – Goes to the Tennessee Titans
