Rough Hawks injury news: CJ McCollum out 4 weeks (calf strain), Asa Newell likely out for season (toe)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

This is rough injury news for Atlanta, which at 8-6 sits tied for the 4/5 seeds in the East:

• Guard CJ McCollum suffered a strained left calf and will be reevaluated in four weeks, the team announced.

• Big man Asa Newell has surgery to repair a left great toe hyperextension, and while the Hawks' announcement says he will be re-evaluated in four months, that will be mid-March, weeks away from the end of the regular season (April 11). It's unlikely we see Newell again this season.

After eight games as a starter to open the season, McCollum was moved to a bench role in favor of rookie Kingston Flemmings (who has made the most of that opportunity). McCollum has accepted and thrived in his bench role, averaging 14.9 points per game (scoring 20+ in each of his last three appearances), and shooting 37% from 3. Atlanta is going to miss his scoring punch off the bench.

Expect Corey Kispert to get extra run and touches with McCollum out.

Newell was poised for a bigger role this season after bouncing between the Hawks and their G League affiliate last season. However, he played in just five games this season before the toe injury and never really got the chance to show how much his game had grown. That said, the Hawks had already picked up his option for next season, so he isn't going anywhere.

Newell joins a few injured frontcourt Hawks players: Rookie center Henri Veesaar is out for the season with a torn ACL, while Mouhamed Gueye is out at least another month following foot surgery.
