‘Joe Burrow is that guy’: ESPN star says there’s no other quarterback he’d take in the NFL

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Joe Burrow and the Cincinnati Bengals have caught fire over the last month, and the star quarterback is playing as well as anyone in the NFL. And one ESPN analyst thinks that he's the best quarterback in the league right now.

Appearing on The Dan Patrick Show on Monday, Louis Riddick said that there is no quarterback he would take over Burrow at the moment. Riddick said that Burrow is "special in every sense of the word," putting him ahead of stars like Patrick Mahomes and Josh Allen.

The Bengals' season didn't get off to a perfect start. Burrow faced some heavy criticism after a late fumble in a 30-27 loss to the Pittsburgh Steelers in September, and he later called for the team to throw the ball more after a 22-17 loss to the Jacksonville Jaguars.

But Cincinnati has won four straight games since then. Burrow threw five touchdown passes and ran for another in a 43-36 overtime win over the Tennessee Titans, and he followed that up on Sunday in Madrid.

Burrow completed 24 of 29 passes for 271 yards with three touchdowns and ran for another score in a 34-17 win over the Atlanta Falcons. Ja'Marr Chase caught 10 passes for 155 yards and a touchdown, and Tee Higgins added two touchdown catches as the Bengals improved to 6-2.

During an appearance on The Dan Patrick Show this week, Riddick didn't hold back in his praise of Burrow.

"Burrow is in that really, really, really, really small category of he knows how to throw a football, and it makes it look easy. Dan, he's special. He's special in every sense of the word," Riddick said, via The Dan Patrick Show. "And I know in a business where everyone wants to jump on top of everyone else and say, 'No, this guy's transcendent. No, this guy is. No, this guy is. No, this guy is.' Joe Burrow is that guy."

When Patrick asked if there's a quarterback that he would take over Burrow right now, Riddick didn't hesitate.

"No. No. And I love Jordan Love and I love Josh Allen, and everyone knows I've loved Mahomes since day one. But Burrow has an edge to him right now," Riddick said.

Riddick also recalled a recent conversation with Burrow, in which the quarterback told him that he feels "as good as I've ever felt in my life," and said that Chase and Higgins could tell that Burrow was locked in during warmups in Madrid.

"Joe right now is throwing the ball better than they've ever seen him throw it. There's no quarterback I'd want right now," Riddick said, in the full clip. "If I had to pick out of all of them, number nine from Cincy is the one."

Clearly, Burrow has at least one big believer at ESPN.
