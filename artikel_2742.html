Patrick Mahomes earns a special honor for Week 10 performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes was named Nickelodeon's Valuable Player after the Week 10 win over the Atlanta Falcons.

Mahomes completed 22 of 35 passes for 348 yards and five touchdowns in last Sunday's 35-13 victory at Mercedes-Benz Stadium.

Mahomes had previously earned weekly NVP honors during Week 10 of the 2021 season and Week 16 of the 2022 season. He also received the award in the postseason after the 2021 AFC Championship Game when he was named the Super Bowl LVIII NVP after defeating the San Francisco 49ers.

Winners are highlighted on the weekly Nickelodeon show 'Nick Explains the NFL with Dora,' with animated slime graphics, blimp-topped trophies, and slime celebrations that explain the game for kids.

Mahomes also received a nomination for this year's Kids' Choice Awards alongside tight end Travis Kelce for Favorite Male Sports Star, alongside several prominent athletes. He didn't win at last week's ceremony and continues to pursue his first win at the award show.
