Caitlin Clark details key areas where Fever can improve in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Indiana Fever superstar guard Caitlin Clark detailed where she thinks her team needs to improve in the offseason on this week's episode of New Heights.

While speaking with the Kelce brothers on their popular show, Clark highlighted perimeter defense and depth as two key areas of improvement for Indiana in the offseason when it comes to talent acquisition in free agency and the draft.

"We had the most talented offense in the league [last season], but we really struggled defensively," Clark said on the show. "That's our next step."

She also spoke to how the game is evolving and how the Fever could update their scheme to keep up with trends.

The Fever are on the search for a new general manager, with a handful of NBA candidates shared earlier this week.

Stephanie White is still expected to return to Indiana, but that could change when the new general manager arrives.
