‘You’re a moron’: Dana White gets blasted for being a part of Donald Trump’s ‘propaganda’

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Comedian Stavros Halkias has gone viral in the NFL community for his viral "Ronnie" character, a problematic die-hard Ravens fan who gives post-game analysis of Ravens games on social media.

In 2024, Ronnie even made his way onto the field via invitation from the Ravens organization.

"Look where we are, yo. Finally, Ronnie is gettin' the respect he deserves in this city," he said into the camera, before claiming his professional football career was derailed after he threatened a high school quarterback with a "makeshift, homemade weapon."

It's safe to say Ronnie was probably disappointed in Tuesday's midterm results, which were disastrous for President Trump and the Republican Party. The real Halkias, however, is quite progressive, and recently revealed how he feels about anyone who still supports Trump and how his feelings on the UFC have changed because Dana White continues to support the President.

"Dana White is evil," comedian Sarah Sherman said while appearing on Halkias' Stavy's World podcast.

"Well, just like, if you think Trump is awesome now, you're the dumbest person alive," Halkias responded.

"You're stupid or evil or both, probably. And if you're still sucking Trump's c*ck, you're a f*cking moron. And I believe Dana White has been throating him pretty well recently. I was more of a fan when I was younger, actually. My brothers, when we were kids, we would go to Buffalo Wild Wings and watch every big pay-per-view. It's one of the best, like an awesome memory from my teenage years.

"And I would get in and out, and I was getting back into the sport. And then yeah, just the, like, the MAGA like suck-off has really put a damper on it. Look, every owner of every sports team is a piece of sh*t typically, like maybe a couple aren't, but yeah, there's something kind of uniquely, like 'we suck Trump's c*ck' coded about the UFC and their willingness to be a part of propaganda to me has made it impossible to enjoy the sport as much as I do."

As the midterms showed, the country's feelings on the current administration have shifted dramatically since Trump was reelected in 2024. White may want to recalibrate.
