Will Garrett Wilson play today? Status update for Jets WR in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Superstar wide receiver Garrett Wilson was listed on the New York Jets' injury report ahead of the team's Week 9 matchup against the Kansas City Chiefs at Arrowhead Stadium.

Will the Chiefs' secondary have to account for the 2022 NFL Rookie of the Year this afternoon?

Here is the latest update on Wilson's injury status:

Wilson has been dealing with a concussion and did not participate in any of New York's practice sessions in Week 9.

Wilson has officially been ruled out for the Jets' tilt with Kansas City this afternoon. The former Ohio State Buckeye remains in the NFL's concussion protocol and will not be able to return until the league determines he is fit to play.

With Wilson sidelined in Week 9, New York's air attack is set to feature Omar Cooper Jr., Tim Patrick, Malik McClain, and Isaiah Williams.
