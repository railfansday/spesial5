Tyrese Maxey a late scratch vs. Magic

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Tyrese Maxey, who warmed up before Wednesday night's game in Orlando, has officially been ruled out with right foot soreness.

Maxey was not on the injury report so this is truly a late scratch. We'll wait to hear more from the team after the game.

The Sixers are already missing Jaylen Brown with a hamstring strain and Dean Wade with hamstring tightness/soreness.

With all that, the starting group is going to look a bit different tonight. Anfernee Simons will get his first start of the season and Kentavious Caldwell-Pope will start his second game as a Sixer. They'll be joined by VJ Edgecombe, LeBron James and Joel Embiid.

There's no silver lining to any type of Maxey injury, but this could be a big opportunity for a guy like Labaron Philon Jr. The Sixers will need more ball-handling when Edgecombe goes to the bench and Philon will be the best option. This also could be a night where Nick Nurse decides to give Justin Edwards a shot.

Unfortunately, Adem Bona and Dominick Barlow remain out of the lineup. Jabari Walker and James will be the top options behind Embiid at center. This could also be another night where Nurse has to turn to two-way signee Tacko Fall, especially when factoring Orlando's size at the center position with Wendell Carter, Jr., Goga Bitadze and Nikola Vucevic.

The Sixers will have two days off before hosting the Milwaukee Bucks on Saturday afternoon. After that, the schedule does get a bit stiffer.
