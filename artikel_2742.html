NFC playoff picture: NFC East looks like two-horse race with Giants and Commanders

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Dallas Cowboys' stunning meltdown on Thursday night against the Philadelphia Eagles, blowing at 31-10 late-third quarter lead in a 39-38 defeat, changed the NFC East title race. The Washington Commanders are now the only team that appears to have a realistic chance of catching the first place New York Giants.

As of Friday morning, NFL.com gives the Giants a 70% chance of making the playoffs. That rises to 78% if rthey defeat the Indianapolis Colts on Sunday. It falls to 53% should they lose.

The Commanders currently sit with a 24% chance to make the playoffs.

The Cowboys? NFL.com says Dallas (5-7) now has just an 8% chance of making the playoffs.

With a two-game lead over the 4-5-1 Commanders, the Giants need to take care of their own business. This week's game against the 3-8 Colts, likely the weakest team remaining on the Giants' schedule, is critical.

"I'm not really worried about what the Commanders and Cowboys (are doing). ," Giants right tackle Jermaine Eluemunor said earlier this week. "I'm just going to keep my focus on winning every single week with the Giants. If we do what we need to do, then we'll be exactly where we want to be come January."
