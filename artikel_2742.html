Lakers coach has savage reaction to embarrassing loss to lowly Jazz

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

JJ Redick entered the Lakers' locker room on Wednesday night following the team's 109-97 loss to the Jazz at Crypto.com Arena with a simple question.

Who on the team felt like they played well in the Lakers' first loss in nearly three weeks?

There was no sugarcoating Wednesday's loss.

"We didn't play well," Redick said. "Nobody played well. It's not Walker, it's not Luka, it's not Q, it's not Sandro [Mamukelashvili]. Nobody had a great game."

Los Angeles Lakers head coach JJ Redick reacts during a game against the Utah Jazz at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles on Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026. Getty Images

Luka Doncic #77 of the Los Angeles Lakers attempts a shot against Mo Bamba #44 of the Utah Jazz. Getty Images

A quick look at the box score explains why.

Yes, Luka Dončić had 29 points, seven assists and five rebounds, but fatigue appeared to catch up to him – evident by his nine turnovers, 10-for-28 shooting from the field and his streak of scoring at least 30 points ending.

Yes, Quentin Grimes followed up his 47-point masterpiece during the win over the Hornets with a 20-point showing against the Jazz, but he scored two points and shot just 1 for 6 from the field during a fourth quarter the Lakers were outscored 31-21 on Wednesday.

Yes, Walker Kessler extended his double-double streak to six games, but he was the first to admit his scoring touch wasn't present (he shot 4 for 10 on free throws).

Ace Bailey of the Utah Jazz drives to the basket during a game against the Los Angeles Lakers. NBAE via Getty Images

The Lakers weren't overreacting to their nine-game winning streak game ending to a Jazz toward the bottom of the Western Conference standings.

The Lakers recognize that there are going to be games where it won't be their night in an 82-game season

Wednesday was one of those nights, with their shooting from all over the court falling well below the standard they've established for themselves.

Luka Doncic of the Los Angeles Lakers passes in front of Josh Green, Mo Bamba and Svi Mykhailiuk of the Utah Jazz. Getty Images

"You can't overemphasize a loss because we're still in a good spot," Jake LaRavia said. "We're going to stay connected as a team. We're not going to let this loss get to our heads too much. We got another opportunity Friday to bounce back."

Kessler added: "With 82 games, when you have a bad game like this, you don't want to spend too much time lingering on the losses. You want to learn from it and move on because you have to. Our offense, we were getting good looks. Just wasn't falling necessarily. So I don't think there's cause for concern."
