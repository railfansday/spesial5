NFL Draft: Tracking declarations for 2027 prospects

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

With the college football season winding down, underclassmen are going to start making their declarations for the 2027 NFL Draft in the coming days. This year, underclassmen declarations are going to be more important than ever, as only fifth-year seniors are going to run out of college eligibility this season. Moving forward, college athletes have five years of eligibility to exhaust at the college level, rather than just four.

Because of that rule change, the strength of this class is going to depend on underclassmen more than ever, as only 34 of the top-150 prospects in this class, according to the consensus draft board, are fifth-year seniors. With fourth-year seniors now able to return to school, it's anyone's guess what the upcoming draft class will look like.

For perspective, 30 of 81 (37 percent) of top-150 prospects returned to school last season. Last year, though, only 54 percent of the top-150 prospects had eligibility remaining. For the 2027 draft, 77 percent of the top-150 are considered "underclassmen," now that fourth-year seniors have future eligibility.

Let's take a look at the players who have declared to leave school early or return to school, on a position-by-position basis. The number by these prospects' names are their current rank on the consensus draft board.

There are only two top-40 fifth-year seniors in the group, and only seven top-60 prospects who don't have the opportunity to return to school for NIL money.
