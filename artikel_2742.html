Mariners News: Maikel García, Sonny Gray, and Nick Fortes

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Mariners kicked off the offseason last night with a bang, acquiring Gold Glove third baseman Maikel García from the Royals in a six-player swap that sent Bryce Miller, Logan Evans, and José Ferrer to Kansas City.

Let's check in on the fallout from that deal and more from around the league.

Check out the Royals' side of the García trade analysis over at our sister site Royals Review.

Kate Preusser broke down the Jerry Dipoto press conference following the big move.

Ben Clemens at Fangraphs gave his take on the big Mariners-Royals swap.

The Twins inked right-hander Sonny Gray to a two-year, $55M deal. It'll be Gray's second stint in Minnesota after playing there in 2022 and 2023.

The Angels have acquired infielder Jace Jung — the younger brother of Rangers infielder Josh Jung — in a deal with the Tigers.

The Red Sox acquired catcher Nick Fortes from the Rays in exchange for right-hander Angel Roa.

Former Mariners first baseman Rowdy Tellez has agreed to a deal with the NPB's Rakuten Golden Eagles.

Tarik Skubal's next meeting will be with the Padres this weekend as he appears to be nearing the end of his free agency.

Rob Manfred came out with some fightin' words yesterday, saying that owners will not compromise on the salary cap during CBA negotiations. It seems we are in for a very long winter.

The Orioles have introduced a new on-field hat for the 2027 season.

What's the opposite of Buyer's Remorse? Passer's Relief? That's what Alex Rodriguez is feeling since Steve Cohen beat him out in his bid to become controlling owner of the Mets.

Maddie Landis at Baseball Prospectus thinks that MLB players should attend the 2027 Met Gala if the lockout drags into May. ($)

With the long-awaited Grant Theft Auto VI coming out last night, the review embargo was lifted during the day yesterday. And folks, it appears we have a masterpiece on our hands.
