46 Gifts For Tweens & Teens That Won't Get An Eye-Roll (Probably)

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Here are the gifts that’ll actually pass the vibe check.

Buying gifts for tweens and teens can be a special kind of holiday torture. Don’t get us wrong; it’s so much fun when you get it right. But it doesn’t always go that way, largely because their likes and wants are a moving target. Last year they wanted everything from the toy aisle. Now they want... a hoodie? A lip balm that ~everyone~ has? “Just money”? And boy, if you get it wrong, they’ll let you know. (It’s in the eyes.)

Since many of us on the Scary Mommy team are living with these outspoken arbiters of taste, we’ve done the reconnaissance for you. Some of these gifts are cozy staples they’ll wear on repeat until spring, like ultra-soft sweats. Some are creative outlets that aren’t too “kiddish,” like a retro digital camera. And some are the niche little things they’ll be weirdly thrilled to find in their stocking, like cloud-shaped body mists.

Basically, it’s a big ol’ list of things they’ll love... even if they’d never admit it out loud.
