Adecoagro (AGRO) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Fertilizer gains offset sugar softness as debt climbed on the Caarap3 deal.

Adecoagro S.A. (AGRO -6.41%) achieved a record 9-month Adjusted EBITDA of $471.8 million, driven by the integration of its fertilizers business and operational improvements in its dairy operations. Management stated that the company successfully integrated the Caarap3 mill into its Mato Grosso do Sul cluster, expanding its total industrial capacity. The company reported that it is prioritizing ethanol sales to capture high domestic prices while maintaining a 100% hedge on its 2026 sugar production. CFO Emilio Gnecco indicated that the company is on track to reach its year-end net leverage target of 2.5x as seasonal working capital requirements reverse in the fourth quarter. Management indicated that the fertilizers segment reached new production records for urea and ammonia, supported by the completion of a scheduled maintenance turnaround in late 2025.
