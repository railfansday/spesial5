What channel is Oregon vs. Ohio State on today? Time, TV, streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Oregon football team, somehow still alive in the College Football Playoff race despite a trio of losses, faces its stiffest test of the season Nov. 7 when it heads to Columbus to take on Ohio State.

The Ducks (5-3, 3-2 Big Ten) are ranked No. 23 in the initial CFP rankings released Tuesday and still have a small chance to play for a Big Ten championship, which would be an automatic berth to the playoff, if a number of things go their way. But first things first: Oregon needs to win out, starting with the Buckeyes (6-2, 4-1) this weekend.

Here's how and where to watch Oregon football take on the Buckeyes this weekend.

Oregon vs. Ohio State will broadcast nationally on CBS in Week 10 of the 2026 college football season. Brad Nessler and Charles Davis will call the game from the booth at Ohio Stadium, with Jenny Dell reporting from the sidelines. The game can be streamed on Fubo, which includes a free trial for new members.

The Oregon vs. Ohio State game will kick off at 12:30 p.m. PT from Ohio Stadium in Columbus.
