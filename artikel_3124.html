Kyndryl: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Gross margin climbed to 22.5% in the first half, and management expects further gains, pointing to better profit quality beyond sheer deal volume.

On its earnings call, held on Nov. 4, 2026, for its fiscal 2027 second quarter ended Sept. 30, Kyndryl (KD +30.14%) announced that it reaffirmed its full-year financial outlook. Long-term investors care because the company is shifting toward higher-margin advisory work to expand free cash flow, or cash left after spending on operations and capital projects. Three things management said on the call stand out.

Kyndryl is expanding beyond traditional infrastructure maintenance by winning larger enterprise agreements. Over the last 12 months, the company secured 43 contracts worth over $50 million each, with 11 of those signed in fiscal Q2 alone, as customers upgraded core computing systems.

Gross margin, the percentage of revenue remaining after direct delivery costs, reached 22.5% in the first half of the fiscal year. Management expects cost reductions and automation in service delivery to drive additional margin expansion through the rest of the year.

Enterprise clients are turning to Kyndryl Consult, the company's advisory unit, to deploy artificial intelligence (AI) and upgrade cybersecurity defenses. Over the past 12 months, new contract commitments in consulting exceeded realized revenue.

Demand for cloud infrastructure and operational automation remains steady across enterprise clients.

The company retains structural cost advantages from its operational data, which creates high switching costs for customers. Management is monitoring contract pricing discipline and progress toward generating $400 million to $500 million in annual free cash flow. As corporate technology environments grow more complex, Kyndryl is positioning its consulting services to capture broader software management spending.
