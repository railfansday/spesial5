Why Anthropic Stock Fell Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

By Johnny Rice – Nov 24, 2026 at 4:35PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Anthropic ›

Anthropic, PBC (ANT -6.54%) stock dropped 6.6% on Tuesday, Nov. 24, as a sell-off across AI stocks pushed shares below their IPO price for the first time.

Anthropic went public last week in the largest IPO in history -- taking the briefly held crown from SpaceX (SPCX -5.65%). With more demand than supply, the stock opened at $111.50, 34.3% above the offer price of $83. But after an explosive start, shares have slid each day since, even falling 1.4% yesterday amid a sea of green.

Jensen Huang's dramatic reinstatement as Nvidia (NVDA -5.86%) CEO -- announced Monday morning, nearly two weeks after his equally dramatic resignation -- sent the market flying, with the Nasdaq's 6.5% gain marking its biggest single-day jump since April of 2025.

Investors hoped his return would breathe life into a struggling AI trade, but just a day later, that optimism looks like it may have been premature.

But Anthropic's drop is also weighing on the Nasdaq Composite. I think investors have come to have serious doubts about the long-term economics of the Claude creator and frontier AI in general. I think they're right, and I would caution readers from jumping in.
