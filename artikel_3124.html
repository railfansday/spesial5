Ambience’s AI now helps hospitals plan rounds and discharges

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The new tools sit beside software that already writes notes and suggests billing codes.

Ambience’s new tools suggest tasks for each shift and flag barriers to discharge.

Ambience Healthcare has added Rounding Assistant and Discharge Readiness to its hospital AI software, to help care teams prioritise tasks and spot barriers to discharge earlier. The company also points to customer results on billing, including a 6.5% rise in case mix index at Onvida Health.

Ambience Healthcare has added two AI tools that help hospital teams decide what to do next for each patient, the company announced on Tuesday. They join its existing software, which writes clinical notes and suggests billing codes for hospital stays.

Rounding Assistant pulls together overnight events, changes in a patient’s condition and a list of suggested tasks for each shift. Discharge Readiness flags medical, practical and social problems that could delay a patient going home, earlier in the stay.

Ambience is backed by Andreessen Horowitz, Kleiner Perkins and the OpenAI Startup Fund. It counts Cleveland Clinic and Mayo Clinic among its customers, and ties its fees to results. Cleveland Clinic has also backed Dyania, another hospital AI startup.

Much of the pitch is about money. Ambience aims to raise hospitals’ case mix index, a measure of how sick patients are that helps set what insurers pay. At Onvida Health, discharges handled with Ambience scored 6.5% higher than a year earlier, the company says.

“To justify the investment, we needed roughly a 1.5% improvement in case mix index,” said Mike Lewis, chief financial officer of Onvida Medical Group.

Lewis said Onvida’s own finance team checked the results. Ambience also says admission notes written with its tool captured 59% more of the diagnoses used in quality rankings, in one health system’s review of 1,100 stays. None of the figures come from independent research.

AI note tools are still under scrutiny. In August, England’s patient watchdog warned that AI scribes were getting drug names and diagnoses wrong. Rivals are growing fast too: Heidi raised $340M in September, and Google launched Gemini for healthcare last week.
