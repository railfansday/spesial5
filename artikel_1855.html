Rocks’ first-half double hurts Hayes and Yeading

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

It was a second league win in a row after a shaky spell of results and left Danny Hollands’ men third in the table and just three points behind leaders Egham.

Early on Hayes’ Ben Shroll put a low ball into the area but Kaylen Hinds shot over from the edge of the box.

Roberts put Bognor in the lead as his run down the left saw him beat his marker before coolly sliding it under Rhys Forster in the Hayes goal on eight minutes.

Amari Fashanu won a corner for the visitors with his low strike but Benny Read headed it out. Liam Ferdinand created an opening but missed his chace in front of goal.

For the Rocks, Henry Nevill hooked a cross in from the left but Callum Laycock looped it up into the air with his head. Lennie Smith sped down the right but his cross-shot was saved.

Bognor extended their lead on 25 minutes when Roberts' shot was pushed out only as far as McDonald, who diverted it into the net on the rebound, despite Forster getting something on it.

From McDonald's corner, Tommy Block was denied in a crowded penalty area. On 38 minutes Joshua Edwards had to receive treatment as he took a bash to the head in a challenge with Laycock.

Smith got on the breakaway for Bognor and he fed McDonald, who struck a shot wide along the deck. Harry Grant blasted over as Hayes were very wasteful in front of goal.

Four minutes after the break, a fierce ball in from Dernell Wynter saw Ferdinand divert it in from close range to make it 2-1.

Shroll got another dangerous ball into the box but Amos Nasha's header was directed straight at Tommy Scott. Shroll went into the book for a shoulder barge into the back of Smith. Kaya Tshaka's free-kick picked out Block but his header was saved.

Tshaka swiped at a chance of his own and he had to come off injured. Ethan Robb replaced him.

Nevill blazed a cross from the left too high before Fashanu hit a curling effort with his right foot and Scott palmed it out. Fashanu hit a corner deep towards the back post with Shroll under it, but Laycock and Scott combined to keep it out.

Nasha did well to pass the ball to Ferdinand and he hit the right post with his low effort on 69 minutes. Roberts was replaced by Ben Anderson for the Rocks and Nasha went into the book as he pulled Nevill back.

Scott pulled out a wonderful save down to his right to keep Grant’s low volleyed effort from just outside the area. Ferdinand was offside on the rebound.

Ollie Lovell came off on 80 minutes for Bognor, then the referee pulled up injured with a strained calf so Bognor's public address announcer, Peter Kelly-Sullivan, had to take up the flag for linesman’s duty for the remainder of the game.

Brendan Matthew had a header diverted wide when he had the goal at his mercy in the 94th minute.

It was a nervy final moments for the Rocks with the visitors always a danger but they held on for the win.

Next up is the highly-anticipated game away to Jersey Bulls – and over 200 Bognor fans are expected to make the trip to the island on Saturday.
