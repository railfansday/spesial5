Jamie Redknapp names Arsenal star who is ‘not up to the level’ needed by Mikel Arteta

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Jamie Redknapp fears an injury for David Raya will ‘change everything’ for Arsenal after the goalkeeper limped off in the 2-2 draw with Newcastle United.

Raya was replaced midway through the second-half after he was involved in what appeared to be an innocuous coming together with William Osula in the penalty area.

The Spain international was in real distress as he left the pitch, clutching his left arm and using his sleeve as a makeshift sling as he was replaced by Kepa Arrizabalaga.

The 31-year-old has grown into a crucial figure at Arsenal, vital in their Premier League success last season having established himself as the best goalkeeper in the league.

He has missed just one game through injury since joining the Gunners but his withdrawal at St James’ Park sparked huge concern for the champions.

Kepa joined Arsenal in the summer of 2025 to serve as Raya’s understudy. After a run of games in the Carabao Cup last season, he was trusted with a starting role in the final against Manchester City where he was at fault at Wembley.

Arsenal boast the strongest squad in the English top flight but former Premier League star Redknapp insists Raya is ‘irreplaceable’, warning Kepa is not of the required standard to come in and replace him.

Redknapp added any absence for Raya could prove extremely costly.

‘Arsenal’s squad, player for player, they have two players for every position,’ Redknapp told Sky Sports.

‘The fact that their goalkeeper, the way he walked off that pitch he looked like he was in severe pain with his elbow. That is the one player that is irreplaceable in that team.

‘Kepa is not up to the level of him, I think he struggles in the Premier League and we saw him in the Carabao Cup last year in the final.

‘He is now going to have to play some games and that will be difficult for him and that will change everything for Arsenal for a while.

‘That’s a massive blow depending on how long he is out because he is irreplaceable and probably the best goalkeeper in the league and one of the best in the world.’
