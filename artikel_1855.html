‘I’m not afraid to say that’ - James Justin on Leicester City exit and new lease of life at Leeds United

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Leeds United defender James Justin has conceded he “wasn’t great” in his final season at Leicester City.

The 28-year-old played in Europe and represented England during his six years at Leicester, but his Foxes spell ended on a sour note.

He suffered relegation from the Premier League with the club in 2025 and by his own admission, struggled for form during that ill-fated campaign.

When he was snapped up by Leeds for a reported £8m plus £2m in add-ons in August 2025, many Leicester fans were quick to point out his below-par performances.

He told Leeds Live: “I would say I obviously didn't leave Leicester on the best of terms in terms of getting relegated twice in three seasons, and we weren't great that year at all, and I wasn't great that year, I'm not afraid to say that.

“I didn't play to the best of my ability, for whatever reason, I don't know. But it's been a good start here so far.

“I'm grateful that the club had that faith in me and hopefully I've repaid that so far.”

To say Justin has enjoyed a new lease of life at Elland Road would be an understatement. A full-back by trade, he has slotted seamlessly into a central back three with Tarik Muharemović and Nico Elvedi and kept £15m man Jaka Bijol out of the side.

Praise has been lavished upon the Luton Town academy product, but he is not thinking about a recall to the England squad.

He said: “Do I think about an England call-up? Not really. I understand football and the way it goes in terms of England and I feel obviously the man sitting over there (Jayden Bogle), and we've spoken about him before, is due a call-up before I am to be honest.

“He's got a lot of potential. I'd back him as a player to go into that group and perform to the abilities that I know that he's got.

“When that day comes, and I'm sure it will hopefully come soon if we obviously keep it up as a team throughout the season, I'll be cheering his name.

“I feel like he's a top-class player, and especially with the dominant style that England play in the majority of their games, it'd be perfect and definitely be a luxury player in that England team.”
