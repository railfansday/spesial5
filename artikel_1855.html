Crystal Palace given boost in striker pursuit by Harry Kane - Transfer Expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Ivan Toney is eyeing a return to the Premier League in the January transfer window amid reported interest from Crystal Palace.

The 30-year-old has spent the last two years playing in the Saudi Pro League with Al-Hilal following his move from Brentford, and has long been tipped for a return.

Crystal Palace have now been suggested as a potential landing spot, with his former manager Thomas Frank in charge and a reunion potentially on the cards in South London.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Toney wants to return to English football to give himself a shot at a regular place in Thomas Tuchel's England squad.

This comes after Harry Kane suffered an ankle ligament injury which could see him sidelined for around three months, leaving Tuchel short of options up front.

Other contenders for the position include Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Will Lankshear, but a return to the Premier League could place Toney in with a strong chance.

Brown told Football Insider on 18 November that Crystal Palace are preparing to back Frank in the mid-season window, as he plans to improve his squad in key areas.

The addition of a new striker is not seen as a priority for the Eagles, but amid links with a move for Toney, they could be seen as a good destination for the striker.

While Tuchel has shown he is willing to consider players outside of the Premier League, there is a belief that a return to England could push Toney back into his plans.

"Ivan Toney is going to be one to watch in January, he will want to come back to English football," Brown told Football Insider.

"This thing with Harry Kane is interesting, he's picked up quite a serious injury and that's led to questions about who would come into the England squad to replace him.

"We've got Calvert-Lewin, maybe Lankshear, but outside of those two, there aren't any real options until you look at Toney in Saudi Arabia.

"The problem is, he's not playing at a high standard over there compared to what the players in England are doing, so he might think he has to come back to be in with a chance.

"Crystal Palace are one club who have been mentioned because of his relationship with Thomas Frank, and they're not going to be the only ones interested.

"So as I said, that's going to be something to keep an eye on, because I'm sure Toney is considering his options to make a move."

Crystal Palace are not the only club being tipped as a potential landing spot for Toney, with a number of Premier League clubs keen to land a striker in the mid-season window.

The forward's previous record in the Premier League, and potential availability, has placed clubs on high alert and there is a belief he could soon be on the move.

Former Everton CEO Keith Wyness told Football Insider on 14 November that Newcastle United could be leading contenders for Toney ahead of the January transfer window.

The Magpies' owners, PIF, could utilise their connections within the Saudi Pro League to move ahead of their rivals in pursuit of the striker.

Matthias Jaissle, meanwhile, is keen to add attacking options to his squad after a slow start to the season which has seen them struggle for a reliable goalscorer.
