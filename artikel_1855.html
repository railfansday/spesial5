Braver and stronger – Mansell lays down challenge as Burgess Hill Town seek league turn-around

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

So says boss Gary Mansell after the Hillians let a 2-1 lead slip at Stanway Rovers on Saturday to make it three points from the past 18 available.

Harry Lawson and MoM George Vorster put Hill 1-0 and 2-1 up in Essex but despite the second coming with only 15 minutes left, Mansell’s men conceded with eight to go.

They should be lifted for the league campaign by Tuesday night’s 6-1 Velocity Trophy win at Crowborough, with Jack Strange, Yoan Edoukou, George Vorster (2) and Shamir Fenelon (2) scoring.

Mansell was asked after the Stanway draw how he felt and said: “The same as after the last few games with this little run that we’re in.

"We just can’t get over the line. We can’t fault the boys’ effort and work-rate, which was superb. In the second half we were on the front foot again.

"But we’re a little nervous going the other way at times – particularly when we’re in front. And ultimately, that mindset is not helping us.

"When we go 2-1 up we stop doing what we were doing. People think the right thing to do is to drop deep and tighten up. But all you do is invite pressure into areas where you then give away a cheap free-kick and you get punished.

"We need to be mentally stronger and control a game better by staying brave in possession and keeping the ball, which doesn’t allow them to have the ball. It’s all about playing the game in their half rather than our half.”

Mansell does not think Hill were a long way off becoming a winning team again.

"We’re struggling to get over the line because of nervousness around the group. This is a situation we haven’t been in for the three years I’ve been at the club,” he added.

"All the games have been tight other than the Enfield game. I don’t think we’re a million miles away. We’re drawing games that could go either way. We just need to get one over the line to kick-start something.”

Mansell thanked fans for continuing to turn up in good numbers. “It was a good turnout (at Stanway) considering how far we were from home. We do appreciate the support away from home.

"Stick with us, we’ll turn it round and get some wins and we will be smiling again.”

They host in-form Eastbourne Borough on Saturday.

Mansell said: “We’re not going to shy away from anything. We’ll keep plugging away trying to win games. We’ll go into Saturday as positive as we can.”
