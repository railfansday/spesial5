10 Longest Goal-Scoring Streaks in Football History (Ranked)

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Initially starting off as a writer for FootballFanCast, Vijievan became assistant editor of the site and weekend editor of This is Futbol. After a few years working across both sites, he took up the role of assistant editor to the exclusives project at GiveMeSport, speaking to a number of top journalists like Fabrizio Romano and Christian Falk on the latest transfer news across European football.

Now taking on the role of evergreen editor, Vijievan is responsible for longer-form content based on everything from the Premier League to international football. Outside of that, he's also passionate about cricket and the NBA.

The hardest job in football is putting the ball in the back of the net, and it's no surprise that forwards who can do that on a regular basis often get paid the big bucks. Whether it's scoring a screamer from 30 yards or bundling home from a goalmouth scramble, the absolute best in the business don't just deliver in a one-off game, but can be relied upon week-in and week-out.

Erling Haaland and Kylian Mbappe proved exactly that in the autumn of 2025, embarking on simultaneous scoring runs for club and country. Mbappe's came to a halt at 11 games against Juventus, while Haaland's reached 12 before Aston Villa finally kept the Manchester City striker quiet.

But as remarkable as Haaland's run was, it isn't the longest in football history. Using data available from European football's top five major leagues from the year 2000, find out below the 10 longest goal-scoring runs for both club and country the game has ever seen, including some familiar faces like Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo.

Starting off this list, unsurprisingly, is Lionel Messi. The former Barcelona star actually had two separate streaks of ten games of goals, first in the autumn of 2010, when he scored for the Catalan giants as well as Argentina, before repeating the incredible feat with Barcelona alone across December 2012 and January 2013. The first of those runs brought 16 goals – 15 for Barcelona and one for his country.

The 40 greatest footballers of all time have been named but who comes out on top; Messi, Ronaldo or Maradona?

The 2012/2013 season was particularly special as Messi claimed a record-breaking feat of scoring in 21 La Liga matches in a row, but his all-competition run stalled at ten, meaning his club and country record of scoring in consecutive games stayed at 'just' ten.

A man who has spent his entire career in Serie A after leaving South America, Duvan Zapata enjoyed his best spell in football while with Atalanta, with his performances during the 2018/2019 campaign especially noteworthy.

His streak of 10 consecutive games of scoring a goal included a particularly dominant performance against Frosinone in January 2019, when he scored four times, becoming the first Atalanta player to do so in the league since Hasse Jeppson in 1952. Zapata's goals would fire the club to a superb third-placed finish in Serie A and the Coppa Italia final, bagging 23 times in the league alone.

The first of two entries for Robert Lewandowski on this list, the Poland international can rightly be regarded as one of the greatest number nines of all-time. The fact he was still scoring regularly for Barcelona well into his late 30s, before swapping Catalonia for MLS side Chicago Fire in 2026 is only testament to his capabilities, but he was arguably at his absolute peak in a Bayern Munich shirt during the early 2020s.

Indeed, between May and August 2020, as football returned from the COVID-19 shutdown, Lewandowski scored in ten consecutive games in all competitions for Bayern. The run powered the club's charge to the treble, ending only in the Champions League final against PSG, where Kingsley Coman's header settled the contest, at a time where many believed he was due a Ballon d'Or.

Amid all the recent injuries and heartbreak, it's easy to forget that Neymar was one of the best players in the world during his time at Barcelona. The Brazilian would then, of course, make the big-money move to PSG to become part of their own version of the Galacticos. And while it didn't result in the Champions League title the Parisian club so desperately wanted, Neymar did produce some moments of magic that left the Parc des Princes in awe.

His ten-game run straddled two seasons. It began in May 2022 as the 2021/22 campaign drew to a close, continued through Brazil's June friendlies, and carried on into a blistering start to 2022/23 – including the Trophée des Champions and PSG's opening Ligue 1 fixtures – before finally coming to an end at the start of September.

Once upon a time, Radamel Falcao was considered one of the greatest strikers in the world, enjoying hugely prolific spells at the likes of Porto, Atlético Madrid and AS Monaco. An ACL injury suffered while playing for the Ligue 1 side would then prove to be the start of an unfortunate decline that even counted spells in the Premier League at both Manchester United and Chelsea on loan.

However, it was his time at Atlético that really saw his name be discussed among the greats, including an 11-game scoring streak in 2012 between August and October 2012 that featured a hat-trick against Chelsea in the UEFA Super Cup, which is one of his best-ever showings. Brilliant in the air and a deceptively quick player who could finish off both feet and with incredible ingenuity, Falcao even finished fifth in the 2012 Ballon d'Or race, coming only behind Messi, Ronaldo, Andrés Iniesta and Xavi.

One record Cristiano Ronaldo has over Lionel Messi, is the longest goal-scoring streak for both club and country. While Messi's best effort was 10, CR7 managed to go one better and notched in 11 games straight, doing so between February and April 2014 while he was with Real Madrid and arguably at the peak of his athletic powers.

Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Erling Haaland and Luis Suarez feature in list of players with the most hat-tricks since 2000.

What perhaps made Ronaldo's achievements all that greater, was the teams he scored against. As well as bagging three goals in two separate games against Los Blancos' arch-rivals Atlético Madrid, Ronaldo would also net in the Champions League against Borussia Dortmund and the El Clásico against Barcelona.

Amid all the talk of Erling Haaland's incredible goal-scoring run to start off the 2025/2026 campaign, it's easy to forget Kylian Mbappe enjoyed a similarly prolific run in front of goal too. The France international netted in 11 consecutive games for club and country, scoring 15 total goals in the process.

The Real Madrid superstar bagged against Real Sociedad, Espanyol, Atlético Madrid, Villarreal, Getafe, Levante, Marseille and Kairat Almaty at club level, before also scoring at international level with Les Bleus against the likes of Ukraine, Iceland and Azerbaijan. His great run, however, came to an end when he failed to net in the Champions League clash against Juventus, when Jude Bellingham netted the only goal in a narrow 1-0 win.

Between March and May 2003, Ruud van Nistelrooy found the net in 12 straight games for club and country. The run mixed eight Premier League matches with both legs of Manchester United's Champions League quarter-final against Real Madrid and two internationals for the Netherlands.

The league portion of that spell also made Premier League history. For quite a while, former Manchester United striker Ruud van Nistelrooy held the record for the most goals scored in consecutive games in just the Premier League alone. The Dutchman bagged in ten top-flight games straight from 22nd March 2003 to 23rd August 2003, which actually broke his own record of eight matches that he achieved just 19 months earlier.

It stood until Jamie Vardy scored in 11 successive Premier League games for Leicester City in 2015. Van Nistelrooy's goals during that run included a hat-trick against Fulham and a brace against arch-rivals Liverpool.

Haaland's start to the 2025/2026 campaign was nothing short of extraordinary, firing Man City back into the conversation of being Premier League and Champions League contenders again. The striker's feat began when he scored in the Premier League against Brighton, and he netted in each of City's next five top-flight games, including striking twice in a victory over Manchester United in the derby and netting in the 1-1 draw against Arsenal at the Emirates.

Cristiano Ronaldo, Harry Kane, Erling Haaland and Kylian Mbappe all feature in the top 20 strikers in world football.

Haaland also bagged in all three of City's Champions League games during the run, scoring against Napoli, AS Monaco and Villarreal respectively. For Norway, he scored the winner against Finland in a friendly before hitting five against Moldova and a hat-trick against Israel in World Cup qualifying.

In all, he plundered 22 goals across the 12 games. The run finally ended in a 1-0 defeat at Aston Villa, where he thought he had equalised in the second half, only for the goal to be ruled out for offside.

The man who still currently holds the record for the most number of consecutive games of scoring, is none other than Robert Lewandowski. The striker was at the peak of his powers during his time at Bayern Munich, and having already netted in 10 games straight in 2020, he went even better just a year later. A streak of 14 games of scoring came for both Bayern and Poland, and it served to remind the world just why he was considered the ultimate number nine at the time.

Between February and May 2021, Lewandowski scored in ten Bundesliga games, both legs of Bayern's Champions League last-16 tie against Lazio and two World Cup qualifiers for Poland. The final goal of the run came on the last day of the season, as he struck a 41st Bundesliga goal of the campaign to break Gerd Muller's long-standing single-season record.
