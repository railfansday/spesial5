Donnarumma insulted: What really happened with Napoli fans in Manchester

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Napoli fans once again made their feelings towards Gianluigi Donnarumma clear during Tuesday’s Champions League clash with Manchester City, directing insulting chants at the Italy captain: we talked about it in the latest episode of the Football Italia show.

In the latest Football Italia video, Lorenzo Bettoni is joined by Luke Taylor, who was in attendance for the match, to provide a first-hand account of the atmosphere inside the stadium and the events that unfolded around the Azzurri goalkeeper.

The discussion focuses on how Donnarumma reacted to the chants, whether they had any impact on his performance, and how the situation was perceived by those present in Manchester.

The video also explores a question that continues to divide Italian football supporters: why does Donnarumma still attract so much hostility from some fans in his home country? From his controversial departure from Milan as a teenager to his status as Italy’s undisputed No. 1, the goalkeeper remains one of the most polarising figures in Italian football.

Lorenzo and Luke analyse the latest episode involving the Manchester City shot-stopper, discuss the atmosphere at a subdued Etihad Stadium, and examine the reasons why criticism of Donnarumma continues to follow him despite his achievements for both club and country.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
