The Best Gifts at Bass Pro Shops This Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Nothing gets us in the gift-giving spirit like roaming through a big box retailer, and for outdoor gear, Bass Pro Shops has got to be the biggest name. You used to spend days flipping through a catalog, or hours perusing the store, but now you can just hop online, find a product, and have it shipped directly to you (or your giftee) or get it delivered to your nearest Bass Pro for curbside pickup.

Sure, it may take away from the romance of gift-giving, but it also gets rid of the headache of crowds and lines. You have the stock and convenience of a big box retailer, but the luxury of shopping from your own couch. To make your shopping even easier, we’ve compiled this list of some of our favorite giftable gear, from fishing rods and reels to Benchmade knives and Yeti mugs. If you’re looking for the best gifts at Bass Pro Shops, we’ve got you covered.

The Benchmade Bugout is a lightweight, versatile pocket knife that’s equally at home in the woods, truck, or tackle box. Its slim design and easy-to-use AXIS lock make it a great everyday carry for anyone who likes to have a quality blade close at hand.

The Huk Rogue Wave deck boots are arguably the most popular fishing boots out there, and for good reason. They’re completely waterproof, lightweight, durable, and, most importantly, comfortable enough to wear all day on your feet. The grippy outsoles provide plenty of traction on even the most slippery surfaces (like wet boat decks and piers), too.

When the weather warms up and they drop the boat in, they’re going to need a good pair of fishing shorts. And for that, we give you these from AFTCO. Not only are they stain-resistant and well-made, they feature a stretchy waistband for comfort and a lined pocket for sharp tools like pliers. Available in 15 colors from navy and khaki to coral and turquoise, they come in sizes 28 to 46.

I’m a firm believer you can’t have too many coffee mugs. This speckled stoneware cup features the iconic Bass Pro logo along with a generous 16-ounce capacity for your morning brew.

Forward-facing sonar is one of the coolest and also most controversial trends of the year. Thus, the Berkley Finisher FFS bait is sure to be a crowd-pleasing gift (and a conversation starter!). It’s highly versatile for a range of applications and will lead your giftee right to where all the fish are biting.

If your fly fishing friend wants to start tying their own flies, they’ll love this White River Fly Shop kit. It has everything they need to get started in the art of fly tying, including pliers, scissors, bobbins, tweezers, and other essential tools. An added bonus? It all comes neatly packed in a sleek wood carrying case.

These retractable fishing line snips are a total game-changer. No more using bulky scissors or rusted nail clippers. The corrosion-resistant stainless steel blade may be small but it’s mighty—it can easily cut through up to 50-pound test line. Plus, it hooks right onto their pocket or vest for easy access.

One of our experts’ favorite fish scales, this digital one from Bass Pro is a must-have for measuring their next catch, with a 50-pound weight capacity and easy-to-read scale. After all, if you don’t get to brag about how big that smallmouth was, does it even count?

There are regular fillet knives, and then there are electric fillet knives. The latter gets the job done with less time and less effort—just what any angler wants. This cordless knife from Bubba boasts a comfortable non-slip grip and a durable blade that’s equal parts sturdy and flexible.

Any angler would be thrilled to unwrap a pair of Costa sunglasses. One of the most popular styles from the eyewear brand is the Fantail. The polarized glass lenses are scratch-resistant and designed to increase contrast while blocking glare, so your giftee can easily zero in on their next catch. They come in four frame colors and two lens colors.

Give them everything they need to clean their catch with this all-in-one fillet kit. They’ll get two stainless steel fillet knives (7- and 9-inch), a cut-resistant glove for safe handling, a knife sharpener to keep those blades razor sharp, a stainless steel odor bar for washing their hands afterwards, and a roll-up sleeve for storage.

Maybe you’re getting a gift for a bass nut. Not as stuffy as the fly fisherman, but just as (maybe more) obsessive about their gear. Abu Garcia’s Revo5 X is our standout baitcaster. You can rip lures in with a 10.1:1 gear ratio—pulling in 41-feet of line with a single turn. Internally, you’ve got 10+1 corrosion-resistant bearings to keep things smooth. Externally, you’ve got a super tough alloy frame and side plates. It’s lightweight, durable, and capable. Everything your bass bum needs.

Give them an easy way to keep all their lures, lines, and tools organized on the go with this tackle bag backpack. It’s made of UV- and water-resistant material so it’ll hold up for years to come and it has enough interior storage space to hold up to 11 tackle boxes. Bonus: It comes with four boxes to get them started.

A landing net can be a great asset to any fisherman or woman. This one conveniently folds up to make it more compact and easy to stash on your boat or kayak. The rubberized net prevents tangles and doesn’t damage even the rowdiest of fish thrashing around.

Sunburn is an easy way to ruin a good day of fishing. Protect them from the sun’s rays with this popular booney hat from Columbia. The combination of the wide brim and quick-drying, UPF 50 material offers ample coverage, while the breathable mesh will keep them nice and cool.

For those days when they want to relax as much as they want to fish, gift them this lounger fishing chair. It’s lightweight yet heavy duty with padded armrests and a headrest for added comfort. The adjustable rod holders, water-resistant fabric, and under-seat pocket just for tackle boxes makes it a no-brainer for lazy afternoons on the bank.

Multi tools always make a good gift for anyone interested in the outdoors or who just likes to do maintenance projects at home. The Gerber Dual-Force comes with eleven different tools and a well-made 3.25-inch steel blade.  —SM
