GTA 6 Nearing 25 Million Copies Sold And $2.4 Billion In Revenue On Day 1, Report Says; Take-Two To Reveal Official Sales Data Soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The video game research company Sensor Tower has updated its GTA 6 sales tracker to reveal the latest, albeit unofficial, sales data for the open-world crime game. According to Sensor Tower, GTA 6 reached 24.58 million total units via pre-purchases and copies sold at launch today--this works out to about $2.36 billion in gross revenue. GTA 6 is now available worldwide, so the numbers are expected to continue to climb higher.

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said on TBPN today that the company is going to share official sales data for GTA 6 soon, so it may not be much longer until we get some hard data directly from the source.

The report said PS5 sales are dominating the mix, with GTA 6 selling 18.44 million units on PS5 so far, compared to 6.15 million on Xbox Series X|S. That means of the 24.58 million GTA 6 sales so far, 75% are on PS5 and 25% on Xbox Series X|S.

In total, GTA 6 sales for PS5 have so far reached $1.77 billion in revenue, compared to $588 million for Xbox Series X|S. Sony and Microsoft keep about 30% of revenue due to their platform fees, so GTA 6 being a huge success directly benefits both of those companies.

The huge gap in sales between platforms is not surprising because the PS5 is dominating Xbox Series X|S in terms of console sales. In fact, GTA 6 is breaking records on Xbox, Microsoft has said, and the sales numbers for GTA 6 on Xbox line up with Xbox's share of the console market.

Sensor Tower projected as many as 25 million total pre-purchases for GTA 6 by launch, and that prediction is looking pretty accurate.

GTA 6 was the most preordered game of all time for PlayStation and Xbox.

Sony holds the marketing rights for GTA 6 on PS5, but that doesn't mean that Microsoft is doing nothing to try to promote the game. Just recently, Microsoft announced a GTA 6-inspired custom Xbox console that sits inside an alligator.

Circana has released new data that shows that GTA 6 holds 29% purchase intent among US console and PC gamers. That means nearly 1 in 3 console and PC gamers in the US plan to buy GTA 6–this is the highest purchase intent that Circana has ever measured for any game.

GTA 6 may indeed be a gigantic hit commercially, and one that helps lift the entire industry with increased sales of hardware and accessories. But anyone looking to buy a new console to play GTA 6 is set for major sticker shock, as consoles today are more expensive than ever. Not only that, but GTA 6 is not going to save the industry’s many serious and deep-rooted issues, experts believe.

GTA 6 launched on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The game scored rave reviews, including a 10/10 from GameSpot. But Take-Two's stock price continues to take a beating this year. Not that it matters, though, because the stock is not the company, and the company is not the stock.
