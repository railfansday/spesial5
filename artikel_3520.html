McDonald’s Knows You Won’t Want To Pause GTA 6 To Make Dinner

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Contrary to popular belief, you'll need some nutrients to sustain your Grand Theft Auto 6 sessions this week. That means losing valuable time whipping up a meal or driving to your nearest Cluckin' Bell, but McDonald's in the US is aiming to make refueling easier by teaming up with the instant messaging app Discord.

While the fast-food chain already offers a delivery service via its McDelivery app, you won't need to reach for your smartphone to access it. Instead, you can open the McDonald's activity in Discord--which is available on console--place a McDelivery order on Uber Eats, and go back to GTA 6 while you wait for your order to arrive. Alternatively, you can also play Fast Lane Feast, a Discord-exclusive minigame where you collect burgers and chips while racing for a spot on the leaderboards.

Wait for that knock at the door, grab your grub, stuff your food-hole, and get back to your GTA 6 session after you've washed your hands. Repeat as necessary until either the game or the fast food fills the void inside you.

As the GTA 6 week kicks off across the globe — starting in Middle-earth AKA New Zealand — it looks like multiple brands are looking to get involved in what is shaping up to be a major cultural moment for interactive entertainment. KFC is holding a fake funeral in case you want to use up some of your compassionate leave to attend it and take the day off; The Weather Channel has shared a preview of Leonida's weather system, and Xbox is doing its own thing while not explicitly mentioning GTA 6 due to the marketing deal that PlayStation has with the game.

Another big promotion tonight is with the NBA's Miami Heat, as the team will represent the game with GTA 6-inspired gear and a Vice City-themed court for their match against the Milwaukee Bucks.
