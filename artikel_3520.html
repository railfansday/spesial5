An NES Power Glove Just Became The Most Dangerous Weapon In Pro Wrestling

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

All Elite Wrestling's recent Full Gear event had no shortage of great moments, but Austin Creed stole the show with a move straight out of Street Fighter. After teaming up with his Next Level partner Kofi Kingston and Swerve Strickland to take on Bandido, Hangman Adam Page, and Brody King in a Texas Death Match, anything went in this brawl — including vintage NES accessories.

Looking the worse for wear at King's hands, Creed busted out an NES Power Glove and landed a Shoryuken uppercut that exploded in a shower of sparks, knocking his burly foe flat on his back. The moment quickly went viral, and Creed's team would eventually triumph, emerging as the AEW World Trios Champions.

Creed is no stranger to video games; during his run with WWE, he ran the Up Up Down Down YouTube channel, where he competed with fellow performers in a wide variety of games. Wrestling and video games have formed a symbiotic relationship over the years, with top performers like Kenny Omega referencing games like Street Fighter and Final Fantasy 7 for his signature moves. Video games have also positively influenced professional wrestlers, with top WWE star Roman Reigns saying it's a popular downtime activity with his fellow wrestlers and better than some of the "extracurricular" exploits of previous generations.

In another video game crossover, former WWE champion Cody Rhodes played Guile in the live-action Street Fighter movie, which was released last month, and he even cosplayed as the character at WWE SummerSlam. Sadly, the film wasn't a big box-office hit, making only $100.7 million worldwide during its run (via Box Office Mojo). In comparison, the other Capcom-based movie, Resident Evil, was far more successful, generating over $416 million worldwide and making it the highest-grossing Resident Evil movie of all time.
