Alejandro G. Iñárritu Officially Joins Season 12 of Mubi's Most Slept-On Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Mubi isn't just a film distributor and streaming service; it also produces Mubi Podcast, an essential behind-the-scenes look into the world of movie making. Collider is proud to exclusively announce that Mubi Podcast will return for season 12 this month, and this time they're covering a spate of films that were made under the most difficult circumstances imaginable.

Mubi Podcast began in 2021 with the aim of revealing how great cinema is made, and it's delivered on that promise for eleven seasons and counting. It's hosted by writer and radio broadcaster Rico Gagliano, and has previously explored topics like international cinema, horror movies, famous flops, and the cinema of the late David Lynch.

Season 12 of Mubi Podcast is titled Under Pressure: it will look at a series of films that were made under trying circumstances despite intense political opposition and under trying circumstances. Guests on this season include Alejandro G. Iñárritu, the Oscar-winning director who recently graced cinemas with Digger; Fatih Akin, the Turkish writer-director who won the Golden Bear for the drama Head-On; and Ali Abbasi, the director of the controversial Iranian crime thriller Holy Spider and the even-more controversial The Apprentice, which examined the relationship between a young Donald Trump (Sebastian Stan) and his lawyer, Roy Cohn (Jeremy Strong). Season 12, which will includes five all-new episodes, will premiere on all major podcasting services on November 12.

Episode 1 of the season will tell the tale of Yılmaz Güney, a Turkish tough-guy actor turned left-wing film-maker; he made his masterpiece, Yol, despite being sentenced to a 30-year prison term on dubious charges. Iñárritu and Akin will join Gagliano for the episode. Episodes 2 and 3 will cover the stranger-than-fiction story of Pulgasari, a giant-monster movie made in North Korea by director Shin Sang-ok and actor Choi Eun-hee, a married couple who'd been kidnapped to the hermit kingdom by Kim Jong-il. Paul Fischer, who wrote the non-fiction book A Kim Jong-il Production, will guest on the two-parter. Episode 4 deals with Julia Loktev, a film-maker who covers independent journalism in Russia, which is being crushed under the thumb of Vladimir Putin. Loktev will discuss her two-part documentary My Undesirable Friends. The season will then wrap up with episode 5, in which Abbasi will detail the political pressures he's faced making movies in both Iran and the United States.

Season 12 of Mubi Podcast will premiere on November 12. Stay tuned to Collider for future updates.
