It's an exciting time in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. Fresh off VisionQuest's run for those with a Disney+ subscription, folks are looking forward to the upcoming Marvel movie release of Avengers: Doomsday. Folks are excited to see entire teams of heroes make up the Doomsday cast, although Kevin Feige confirmed more surprise characters are coming. In a recent interview Anthony Mackie might have spoiled one of those secret appearances.

What we know about Avengers: Doomsday is limited, so there are countless fans and theories about what The Russo Brothers might have up their sleeve. In a clip from ET's Twitter, Mackie spoke about the experience of shooting the mysterious blockbuster offering:

These movies, they're like summer camp. You know, everybody comes together. And it's 'Oh how have you been? Oh, you have a kid now. Oh you're married now.' It's one of those things where you catch up with everybody. I got to hang out with Paul Bettany, who I love.

You hung out with WHO?! Hardcore Marvel fans will know that there's been no official confirmation of Paul Bettany appearing in Avengers: Doomsday. So if Mackie didn't misspeak here, this is huge news that Vision could seemingly appear in the highly anticipated crossover event. Coming straight off VisionQuest, this is a thrilling concept that's sure to quickly go viral.

Only time will tell if the studio responds to these comments, or if we'll have to wait until December to see if Bettany actually appears in the next Avengers flick. The actor has had a very long history with the MCU, starting by voicing J.A.R.V.I.S. in the first Iron Man movie. So it would be thrilling to see him join the battle against Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom.

Tom Holland is known for accidentally revealing Marvel spoilers, but he might have some competition if Paul Bettany ends up showing up in Doomsday. If Mackie did spill the beans, he'd be in good company, as Mark Ruffalo went so far as to accidentally spoil Infinity War's twist ending.

Marvel is known for its tight security, and the contents of The Russo Brothers' movie is being especially kept under wraps. Although as we get closer to its December release, more information is sure to start trickling out. Only time will tell how many surprise characters end up appearing that weren't in the cast list. I mean just look at Chris Evans' Steve Rogers, who got his own trailer after it leaked online.

Our answers will come when Avengers: Doomsday hits theaters on December 18th as part of the 2026 movie release list. If Mackie's comments are to be believed, we should expect to see Paul Bettany appear. But considering how many characters he's portrayed in the shared universe, there's no guarantee he'll actually be Vision. My head is truly spinning from all the possibilities.
