Presley Gerber's Hollywood Home Reportedly Listed for $3.6M After His Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The late model Presley Gerber‘s 3,000 square-foot Hollywood Hills home is reportedly for sale following his shocking September death at age 27.

Real estate records reportedly indicate that the mid-century dwelling has been listed for $3.595 million, featuring four bedrooms and three baths in addition to outdoor and indoor living spaces, per TMZ.

With an impressive canyon view of the Hollywood Hills, the residence’s reported amenities include a jacuzzi, a cold plunge, a heated pool and a sauna. The property also features a separate guest house.

Gerber reportedly purchased the Los Angeles area home in March 2023 for $3.4 million.

News broke on September 21 that Presley — son of supermodel Cindy Crawford and Casamigos co-founder Rande Gerber — died one day earlier from a suspected overdose while staying in a Santa Monica rehab center. (The Los Angeles County Medical Examiner confirmed to Us on September 21 that Presley’s cause of death was listed as “deferred” initially because its staff was in the “early stages of investigating” and that “limited information is currently available.”)

Us subsequently learned that officers from the Santa Monica Police Department responded to a call regarding a possible overdose at an address matching the rehab facility on the day of Presley’s death.

“The Los Angeles County Department of Medical Examiner has identified the individual as Presley Gerber, 27,” a September 21 statement read. “SMPD detectives are investigating the death as a suspected overdose. At this time, there is no indication of foul play. The official cause and manner of death will be determined by the Los Angeles County Department of Medical Examiner.”

Presley had been open about his struggles with addiction and mental health, including speaking to reporters on a red carpet about his journey of self-acceptance just two months before his untimely death.

“The most free I felt was when I was finally honest with myself,” Presley said at a June We Are ENOUGH x Aviator Nation launch event. “Move your body regardless of how you’re feeling. Get outside. Get some sun. If you don’t feel like moving, lay on the grass.”

During a 2023 “Studio 22” podcast appearance, the Dolce & Gabbana and Ralph Lauren model stated his desire to help people struggling with addiction by sharing his story.

“Whether I help one person or a 100 people get out of that place that I was in at one point in my life, that’s all I need to do,” Presley told podcast listeners at the time. “I’ve had a lot of time to look into myself and learn about myself with therapists. I’ve seen a lot of stuff, and I’ve learned a lot of things, and what I hope I can do is have made the mistakes for some other people so they don’t have to make the same mistakes that I made.”

His parents, Crawford and Randy, 60 and 64 respectively, broke their silence about Presley’s death in October by sharing a new statement reflecting on their son’s life.

“Presley Walker Gerber. July 2, 1999-September 20, 2026. We are beyond heartbroken to lose our beloved son and brother. We feel so blessed to have had Presley in our lives for 27 years. He is a sweet, beautiful soul who loved and is loved, now and forever,” the couple wrote in a joint statement shared on October 18. “We are so proud of how he showed up in the world, with kindness and vulnerability. We are grateful for the outpouring of love and support from so many who know and love our family and even strangers who feel our pain. Til we meet again our sweet boy.”

The same month, Presley’s sister, model and actress Kaia Gerber, penned her own poignant memorial post about the ongoing process of grieving.

“Prez, I don’t have the words, and maybe the tiny mercy is that we didn’t need them all along. Words fail and words subtract and words summarize and words always lack truth. And how naive of me to believe that language could transcend grief,” Kaia, 25, wrote via an October 18 Instagram post. “I’ve read all of the books and I have none of the answers. My articulation is no match against this, the magnitude of losing you. I am, quite literally, down on my f***ing knees.”

The Shards star described her late brother as “inherently good,” adding that she considered herself to be Presley’s “lucky eternal witness” throughout their lives.

“My best buddy, my great protector, the hardest I’ve ever laughed and the one true love of my life — you lit all of my candles and took all of my monsters away. And I love you. And I love you. And I love you. Only you know how much. Thank you for being here,” she concluded.
