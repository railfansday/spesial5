RHONJ’s Melissa Gorga Reveals Her Holiday Plans With Teresa Giudice

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Melissa Gorga and Teresa Giudice’s love will be on display this holiday season as they celebrate Christmas together.

“I do Christmas Eve,” Melissa, 47, exclusively shared with Us at The Real Housewives of New Jersey season 15 premiere party on Tuesday, November 17.  “Christmas Eve is at my house, so yes, we’re very excited. We’re going to spend it together.”

When RHONJ returns in January 2027, viewers will see the sisters-in-law in a much better place after years of ups and downs.

“I hope that we inspire [viewers] in some sort of way because I know we brought them a lot of stress for many years,” Melissa, who has been married to Teresa’s brother, Joe Gorga, for 22 years, told Us at The Crane Club in New York City. “But I also know everyone was always rooting for us, and I’m so happy for our kids to see how we kind of came full circle. If we can do it, anyone can.”

Instead of sharing what she hopes fans see, Melissa revealed the lessons she hopes viewers take away from her journey with Teresa.

“If anything, I hope we inspire someone out there to pick up the phone and call a family member — an aunt, an uncle, a cousin, a sister that they don’t speak to anymore,” she continued. “That’s what I hope.”

More than two years after RHONJ last aired a new episode, Bravo’s hit reality show is ready for a return in 2027.

Melissa, Teresa and Dolores Catania will join Malvina Blloshmi, Xio Cammarota, Juliette Collazo, Deeba Patel, Natia Rufolo and Vanessa Barrera for a season filled with fresh friendships, family dynamics and business ambitions.

Before the season premieres, Melissa is planning to launch the “Sunday Sauce” video podcast on December 6.

The Real Housewives of New Jersey season 15 premieres on Bravo, Tuesday, January 19, 2027. Stream new episodes the next day on Peacock.
