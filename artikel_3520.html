Sister Wives’ David Says He Doesn’t Deserve Christine After Unfaithful 1st Wife

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

David Woolley is breaking down his trust issues with wife Christine Brown on Sister Wives, revealing they stem from him being cheated on in the past.

“When we’re at home and we’re not doing all this other work, she is the best ever,” David, 62, tells a therapist about Christine, 54, in Us’ exclusive sneak peek at the Sunday, November 29, episode of Sister Wives. “Like, everything that she does, I’m like, ‘I don’t deserve that.’ And I still don’t deserve it. I still wait for her to say, ‘I’m done.’”

Christine calls David’s fear that she’ll want out of their marriage “sad,” while the couple’s therapist wonders why he has these walls up.

“My wife wasn’t faithful,” David reveals, referring to his late first wife. “And so I have a lot of jealousy come up and I have a hard time trusting women.”

A melancholy David notes that his trust issues are “on me” and “it’s not on her,” pointing to his current partner, Christine, whom he wed in 2023.

“She has done nothing wrong. I want to make that very clear,” David says. “It’s all about me. And how can I overcome that part?”

David was married for 20 years before his first wife and the mother of his eight children died by suicide in 2012. More than a decade later, David met Christine online following her split from husband Kody Brown in 2021.

Christine and Kody, 57, were in a spiritual marriage for more than 25 years before they called it quits. The exes share six children. (Kody was also married to wives Meri Brown, Janelle Brown and Robyn Brown during their plural union. Janelle, 57, announced her separation from the father of 18 in 2022, while Meri, 55, walked away in 2023. Kody and Robyn, 48, are still married.)

David and Christine tied the knot in October 2023 and have since let the TLC cameras into their monogamous relationship — including growing pains of how to balance Christine’s past life with her present one.

In Us’ sneak peek at Sunday’s episode, Christine admits that she’s seen David’s jealousy come out when she wasn’t paying “attention that much” to how others were treating her in public.

“I’m friendly, just super, super friendly. And so for me, I’m just being nice,” Christine explains. “And he’s like, ‘That’s not being nice. They’re flirting, they’re coming on to your whatever.’ And I’m like, ‘I just don’t see it.’”

Christine tells the counselor that she is aware she needs to see things from David’s perspective “a little bit more” and then back off a little because “no one wants to feel like that.”

“I think a lot comes down to trust. Like, a lot of the things that we have to work on is trust,” Christine says in a confessional while sitting beside her spouse.

David agrees, noting that because Christine was “brought up in a system to lie” he’s seen issues in how they communicate. (Christine was raised as a polygamist, who has been vocal about her ups and downs with the community.)

“I like honesty because … if you’re not honest with me, how do I trust you on anything you say, no matter how hard it hurts?” David asks.

Christine admits, “There’s a fine balance with giving all of the information. Is all the information necessary or is this on a need to know basis? So I’m learning that more information isn’t a problem.”

Sister Wives airs on TLC Sundays at 10 p.m. ET.
