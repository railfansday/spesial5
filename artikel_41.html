Turkey's deadly factory blast: 'Investing in safety costs more than letting people die'

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

An explosion at a steel factory on November 9 in Turkey's Aliaga, near Izmir, killed 19 people. This tragedy follows several others and highlights the issues surrounding workers’ safety in Turkey, our Observers say. They also condemn the repressive responses from the government and the company.
