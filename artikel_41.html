Endurance athlete swims in Hormuz Strait

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Strait of Hormuz remains largely blocked, with Iran attacking vessels attempting to cross it. As concerns grow over the environmental impact of the war, with toxic chemicals spreading through the air and sea, one swimmer and ocean advocate has gone a step further than issuing warnings: he plunged into the polluted and dangerous waters of the strait, calling on all sides to end the conflict.
