Pick the Model Per Task, Not Per Project: My Aider Cost-Control Rules

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Aider is free. The model isn't. Every message you send carries the repo map, your added files, and the chat history — so cost control in Aider is mostly context control. These are the rules that keep my bill boring.

Aider has three model roles: the main editor, a weak model (commit messages, summaries), and — in architect mode — an architect plus an editor. My simplified matrix:

Cheap models fail predictably: mangled edit formats, ignored instructions, loops that burn 10 turns doing what a strong model does in one. My rule: if a session needs more than ~3 corrective prompts on the same edit, I switch the main model up a tier mid-session (/model). My time is the scarcest token in the system.

And one honesty note: Aider's in-session cost figures are estimates. Your provider's usage dashboard is the bill of record — check it, and check current prices before a long session, because they change too often to print.
