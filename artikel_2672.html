How to Watch Alabama Football vs. Auburn: Kickoff Time, TV Channel, Radio and Weather

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

TUSCALOOSA, Ala. — Alabama football returns to Bryant-Denny Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 28, for the annual Iron Bowl against Auburn.

The Crimson Tide will close their regular season against their biggest rival, with College Football Playoff positioning on the line. Meanwhile, Auburn will look to spoil Alabama's championship ambitions in one of college football's most heated rivalries.

Matchup: Auburn Tigers at Alabama Crimson Tide

Location: Bryant-Denny Stadium, Tuscaloosa, Alabama

Sideline reporters: Holly Rowe and Laura Rutledge

ABC will televise the Iron Bowl nationally as its featured Saturday night college football matchup.

The broadcast will also feature a 4K presentation, with an alternate SkyCast+ viewing option available through SEC Network.

Additionally, fans can stream the game through the ESPN app with an eligible subscription or television provider.

The Crimson Tide Sports Network will provide Alabama's home broadcast, with Chris Stewart handling play-by-play duties.

Former Alabama quarterback Tyler Watts will serve as analyst, while Damion Square reports from the sideline.

Roger Hoover will also contribute to the network's game-day coverage.

Fans in Tuscaloosa can listen through the Crimson Tide Sports Network's local radio affiliates.

Additionally, Alabama's broadcast will be available on SiriusXM channel 84.

Fans can also access the network's digital audio coverage through Alabama Athletics' official platforms.

Saturday's weather forecast looks favorable for Alabama and Auburn fans attending the Iron Bowl.

As of Wednesday, Nov. 25, Tuscaloosa is expected to see abundant sunshine with a high near 63 degrees.

Temperatures should fall to approximately 51 degrees around kickoff before dropping into the upper 40s later in the evening.

Rain appears unlikely, with the current forecast calling for mostly clear skies during the game.

Fans attending afternoon tailgates should enjoy comfortable conditions, although jackets will be useful after sunset.

Alabama enters the Iron Bowl at 9-2 overall and 6-2 in SEC play under coach Kalen DeBoer.

The Crimson Tide remain firmly in the College Football Playoff conversation and will look to strengthen their postseason position.

Meanwhile, Auburn enters the matchup at 7-4 overall and 4-4 in conference play under first-year coach Alex Golesh.

The Tigers are coming off a 59-17 victory over Samford, while Alabama defeated Chattanooga 47-0 last weekend.

Alabama quarterback Keelon Russell has passed for 2,480 yards this season, providing the Crimson Tide with a young offensive leader.

However, Auburn has an opportunity to disrupt Alabama's postseason plans in its first Iron Bowl under Golesh.

The rivalry has produced numerous memorable finishes, and Saturday's meeting brings another high-stakes atmosphere to Bryant-Denny Stadium.

For Alabama fans, the game represents an opportunity to close the regular season with a victory over Auburn before the postseason begins.
