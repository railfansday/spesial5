Mike LaFleur "really encouraged" by Carson Beck's first start

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Cardinals quarterback Carson Beck had a rough go of it in his first start, falling 34-6 to the Rams.

Beck’s opportunities were limited, as the Cardinals possessed the ball for just 17:37 to the Rams’ 42:23. He finished the contest 11-of-18 for 108 yards with two interceptions — though one of them went right off his receiver’s hands and into the arms of a defender.

In his Monday press conference, head coach Mike LaFleur said he’s encouraged by what he saw from the young QB.

“Was he doing his job? Was he getting in and out of the huddle? I thought he used the cadence really well, got that first free play on that third and seven,” LaFleur said, via transcript from the team. “Actually, I chickened out a little bit because we talked about using cadence all week when we were kind of in those disadvantaged situations. The first drive, I kind of wanted to chill out and make sure that we weren’t going from third-and-seven to third-and-12, and he stuck with it. He stuck with the cadence there, got the free play, and I thought [he] delivered an excellent ball to our receiver. I know our receiver can make that play.

“When you just look at his element and what he had to do, like I said yesterday, he did everything we asked him to do, and I am really encouraged by it.”

LaFleur went on to say it was a “sharp” debut from Beck, though he also noted that some moments may have been a bit overwhelming for the rookie QB.

“I think we all probably could notice that, right? I mean, you guys could see with your eyes,” LaFleur said. “As I was talking to you guys right after the game, you guys saw it, and then you confirmed it probably with some tape today or whatever you guys were watching. That wasn’t shocking at all. It’s just like I’ve kind of said with him, you can meet all you want, and you can watch all the film, [but] he keeps it very simple. At least that’s what it comes across as.

“It’s just going to be getting as many reps as he can. That’s what stinks about yesterday [was] only having 34 reps and trying to get a little bit of a run game going, which we obviously didn’t. Not a huge sample size as of yesterday, but hey, have another one this week, and we’ll take it from there.”
