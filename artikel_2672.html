Lions Announce Passing of Chair Emeritus Martha Firestone Ford

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Detroit Lions received unfortunate news on Tuesday. Their owner and chair emeritus, Martha Firestone Ford, passed away at age 101 at her home surrounded by family. The team issued a statement following her death.

Martha and her husband, William Clay Ford Sr, purchased full control of the NFL franchise in 1963. She assumed sole control following his passing in 2014. However, Martha stepped down as principal owner and passed control to her daughter, Sheila Ford Hamp, in 2020.

The couple's four children, Sheila, Martha Ford Morse, William Clay Ford Jr. and Elizabeth Ford Kontulis all took on leadership roles with the Lions. She was born in Akron, Ohio, in Sept. 16, 1925 and was the granddaughter of Firestone Tire and Rubber Company founder Harvey S. Firestone.

The Detroit Lions confirmed the death of Martha Firestone Ford. They posted their statement on their social media accounts.

"It is with heavy hearts that we acknowledge the passing of Martha Firestone Ford," the team wrote. "Mrs. Ford inspired us all as a pillar of the Detroit community and the passionate matriarch of the Detroit Lions football team. She will always be remembered for her fierce loyalty to her family, her community and the city of Detroit. Her presence on our sideline, in our building, and in our community will be greatly missed. We wish the entire Ford and Firestone families peace in this time of mourning."

Detroit will play its next game on Thursday versus the Chicago Bears with a heavy heart. The team is on a a six-game winning streak and will have extra motivation to earn another victory. During Ford's principal ownership, the Lions made two playoff appearances (2014 and 2016). They are 8-2 this season and in a good position to return to the playoffs after missing it last year.
