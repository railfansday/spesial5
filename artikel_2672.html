Josh Allen did not have a rushing attempt in Sunday's win

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Bills quarterback Josh Allen has built his career on being a two-way threat with the ball in his hands, but he did not play that way against the Dolphins on Sunday.

Allen is one of the most feared running quarterbacks in the league and is 14th in NFL history with 90 rushing touchdowns for his career, so it was a surprise to realize that he did not run the ball once in Sunday’s 26-9 win over the Dolphins. It is the fourth time that Allen has started a game and not run the ball a single time, but the other three were token starts in the final weekend of the regular season to keep his consecutive starts streak alive.

Allen had one carry in a 41-7 win over the Titans in 2022 and he’s had seven starts with two carries in his career.

While Allen wound up with 304 passing yards, it wasn’t a stellar day for him throwing the ball either as a pair of interceptions helped keep the Bills from putting an early wrap on their one-win visitors from Miami. He’ll need to be sharper putting the ball in the air against the Chiefs on Thursday and it seems like a good bet that he’ll find ways to impact the game as a runner as well.
