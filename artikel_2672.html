Aaron Rodgers: Mike McCarthy fell asleep in meeting last week, broke down Steelers with "1-2-3 Packers"

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Aaron Rodgers made his first public comments since being benched by the Steelers on The Pat McAfee Show on Wednesday afternoon and they were very critical of head coach Mike McCarthy.

Rodgers said he did not hear the news of the quarterback change directly from McCarthy or anyone else on the coaching staff, but got the word from tight end Pat Freiermuth after it was announced on Tuesday night. Rodgers said that the change came after “months of conversation” about the offensive operation, including the playcalling, and that “things went to a different place” in recent days.

Rodgers said that McCarthy fell asleep in an quarterback meeting last Wednesday and missed other meetings leading into their loss to the Eagles on Sunday. Rodgers said McCarthy also broke down the team with “1-2-3 Packers” at halftime of that game and that McCarthy was not regularly watching film while preparing for games. Rodgers said he also believes McCarthy has lost the team in general.

“Everybody in that building knows the truth,” Rodgers said.

Rodgers said similar things happened when the two men were with the Packers and that he believes McCarthy blames him for being fired in Green Bay after the 2018 season. He said he saw that McCarthy was not connecting with Steelers players early in training camp, but tried to help smooth things out.

“I protected him,” Rodgers said. “I didn’t talk about the things publicly. I didn’t talk about his habits.”

Rodgers also said he pushed to have changes made to the playcalling and game-planning that he believed were going to take place before Tuesday’s benching pushed things in a different direction. Rodgers said that he told the team that if they weren’t going to make those alterations that they should release him and said that there was no chance of a reconciliation with the team after how they handled things on Tuesday.

The comments guarantee that Steelers players will be asked for their accounts of how things have been in the building since McCarthy’s arrival this offseason and their responses will likely determine whether or not things go completely off the rails for the team in the final weeks of the season.
