One Possession: Baylor Scheierman provides key late-game scoring for Celtics

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

One Possession: Baylor Scheierman provides key late-game scoring for Celtics originally appeared on NBC Sports Boston

Baylor Scheierman produced the prettiest pass of the Boston Celtics' entire four-game road trip on Tuesday night … but didn't even get the assist.

That's kind of the way things have gone lately for Boston's bench, which has been well intentioned throughout Boston's four-game trip, but simply misfired on an absurd amount of quality looks.

This time it was rookie Chris Cenac Jr. who smoked a cutting layup off Scheierman's pretty feed through traffic. But QB1 still found a way to leave his stamp on Boston's streak-busting win over the Wizards on Tuesday night.

Scheierman shook off his own rough shooting on this four-game road trip to score eight points in a 75-second span late in the fourth quarter. The Wizards had whittled Boston's 34-point lead down to five in crunch time but Scheierman, getting the late-game nod from coach Joe Mazzulla, hit a clutch 3-pointer off a feed from Jayson Tatum to put Boston up nine with 2:31 to go.

Some hustle hunting an offensive rebound on Boston's next offensive trip put Scheierman at the free throw line for two more points, then he capped his late-game outburst by knocking down a 31-foot bomb off another feed from Tatum.

Scheierman was a mere 3-of-19 shooting (15.8 percent) during Boston's four-game losing streak before finding his groove in the fourth quarter against the Wizards.

Boston desperately needs some of their young wings to differentiate themselves this season and Scheierman continues to be the steadiest of the bunch. Even as his shot defied him on this trip, Scheierman made good things happen with his basketball IQ.

That should have included the touchdown pass to Cenac Jr., with Boston's bench ready to explode after the highlight-worthy feed.

Still, as the Celtics risked letting another big lead evaporate, it was Scheierman who made some clutch late-game shots. And might have saved Thanksgiving for the Celtics.
