Vikings "don't necessarily expect" Jordan Addison back this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Vikings didn’t have wide receiver Jordan Addison in the lineup for Sunday night’s game against the 49ers and his availability for Week 12 came up during head coach Kevin O’Connell’s Monday press conference.

Addison has missed the last two games with a thumb injury that required surgery, but O’Connell said last week that the outlook was “really encouraging” for Addison’s return. The Vikings opted not to put him on injured reserve, which points to a return before missing four games but O’Connell didn’t make it sound like that would happen against the Falcons this week.

“Encouraged about where Jordan’s at,” O’Connell said, via Kevin Seifert of ESPN.com. “Don’t necessarily expect to have him this week but he’s progressing really well for having just gotten that thing fixed.”

The Vikings will host the Panthers in Week 13 and O’Connell’s next update on Addison’s progress may not come until closer to that matchup.
