This year’s Black Friday deals have launched in waves over the past several weeks, but now the floodgates have officially opened. You can now take advantage of the lowest prices we’ve seen in-store and online for video games, wireless earbuds, TVs, e-readers, robot vacuums, and so much more. We can’t guarantee a deal you’ve had your eye on will last through Cyber Monday; however, if prior years are any indication, most will stick around through the long holiday weekend.

As usual, we sorted through thousands of deals to find the ones truly worth sharing, with an eye for the products and services we’ve tested here at The Verge. With prices up across multiple categories due to RAMageddon, some deals may not seem as big as in prior years. For example, Apple’s latest MacBook Air recently fell to its pre-price-hike level for the first time since July. That’s a deal to us, and several other discounts are taking a similar shape. We’ll provide context when possible and keep this post updated throughout the day.

Apple’s AirPods Pro 3 deliver the best noise cancellation and sound quality of any AirPods yet, along with a more comfortable, secure fit and improved water resistance. They also feature built-in heart rate tracking for workouts and support live translations for a handful of languages. Read our review.

You can get an ad-supported Disney Plus and Hulu bundle for $4.99 a month ($8 off) for an entire year. Both streaming services feature a deep catalog of new and classic TV shows and movies, including The Simpsons, Andor, Toy Story 5, and The Fantastic Four: First Steps.

Anker’s durable travel adapter delivers up to 100W of power and works in over 200 countries. It doesn’t support AC plugs, but it does offer a trio of USB-C ports and a nifty LCD display to keep tabs on the charge percentage.

With a 4.3-inch E Ink screen, the Xteink X4 is smaller and more pocketable than any Kindle and Kobo model currently available. It has some drawbacks — it’s limited to TXT and EPUB files — but it’s great for tinkering; it also has a magnetic mount so you can stick it to the back of MagSafe-ready phones.

The Nintendo Switch 2 features a 7.9-inch LCD display, magnetically attached Joy-Con 2 controllers, backward compatibility with most original Switch games, and exclusive titles like Donkey Kong Bananza and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Read our review.

An HBO Max Basic subscription provides access to shows like A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, The Last of Us, and The Pitt, as well as films like Superman and Weapons. You can get a year of the monthly ad-supported subscription for $5.49 a month (about $6 off) until December 1st.

The Eufy Omni C28 combines excellent mopping performance with a small self-cleaning dock that’s well suited for apartments and other smaller spaces. It navigates around furniture and into tight areas with ease, while its roller mop does a great job cleaning hard floors.

Do you ever wish you could turn on your coffee maker or hit the light switch without getting up? The SwitchBot Bot lets you do that. All you need to do is stick the inexpensive contraption on a standard button or rocker switch, which will allow you to control or schedule your pet feeder, garage door, and a wide range of other devices from your smartphone.

Apple’s latest AirTags feature an upgraded ultra-wideband chip for more precise location tracking, a noticeably louder speaker, and an expanded Bluetooth range. Read our review.

The Chamera is not a good camera, but that’s not the point. Kodak’s charming, keychain-friendly tchotchke lets you snap lo-fi memories on a whim, with an ultra-small 1.6-megapixel sensor, a built-in LED flash, and the tiniest amount of storage.

LIFX’s dimmable, color-changing bulbs offer Wi-Fi connectivity, simple setup, and Matter compatibility, so they work with any major smart home platform. The company is currently trialing a Thread beta that will bring the option to add Thread connectivity to all its Matter devices.

Apple’s latest MacBook Air features an M5 chip and starts with 512GB of storage, with significantly faster performance than prior generations. It’s still an excellent machine despite the higher base price, especially given the battery life and speakers. Read our review.

Who needs magnets when you have suction cups? Octobuddy’s cheap silicone mount works with most phones to provide hands-free flexibility whenever necessary. It features 24 single-sided cups, which allow you to stick your phone to a mirror, window, or any other surface you think will provide that perfect TikTok angle.

Peacock is a great way to catch up on a wealth of content from NBC’s various networks, whether you plan to rewatch The Office or catch up on all things Bravo. A Premium subscription also grants access to films from studios like Universal Pictures and DreamWorks, along with live sports and a host of Peacock Originals.

The P9 is great if you need more storage for your Nintendo Switch 2. The microSD Express card offers sequential read speeds of up to 800MB/s, but only gadgets with a microSD Express card slot can take full advantage of the highest speeds. That includes certain cameras, tablets, and drones with a microSD USH-I slot.

Apple’s 11th-gen iPad follows up the 10th-gen model with a new A16 chip and few other changes — and is the only Apple tablet to not support Apple Intelligence.

The handy USB-C cable splits at the end, giving you the ability to charge up to two devices from a single port. That means you can split power between a phone and tablet or juice a 16-inch MacBook Pro to 50 percent in just 20 minutes using a single port.
