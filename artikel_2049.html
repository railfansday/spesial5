Man United identify 'perfect' signing as January priority made clear - Transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Manchester United want to sign a physically dominant centre-back as a priority amid growing concerns about their current options.

Despite the ongoing rumblings about Michael Carrick's dismissal and who will be named as their next permanent manager, work is taking place ahead of the transfer window.

Plans are still being put in place behind the scenes to back whoever they choose as their next manager, as there are serious concerns about key areas of the squad.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that the addition of a "big, physical" centre-back will be a priority as the current options are too weak.

Leny Yoro and Ayden Heaven finished the 4-4 draw against Aston Villa as the centre-back partnership, but lack the physicality provided by the more experienced Harry Maguire.

There are concerns that, when Maguire is not in the side, United lack fight and authority at the back, and they are now putting plans in place to resolve those issues.

Football Insider first revealed back in September that Man United would look to strengthen their back line in January, and results since then have seen their plans accelerate.

Improvements are needed across the back line after a dismal start to the season, but the addition of a new, more physical centre-back has risen to the top of their list.

Multiple names are under consideration behind the scenes, but it has become clear that it is a key area of weakness which must be addressed sooner rather than later.

"As soon as Harry Maguire goes off, the defence becomes so much weaker," Brown told Football Insider.

"They lose that physicality, that willingness to step in and win the ball, to head it away, to battle with strikers, because Maguire is the only one who is capable of doing it.

"He comes off, and they're left with two kids at the back, Heaven and Yoro, who are good players and will become very good players, but they're not ready to be relied upon.

"They do lack that strong, authoratitive ability, and that will come with time, but Man United can't really afford them that much time given the position they're in.

"It has to be a priority for them in January to bring in a big, physical centre-back who can step up, organise the defence, and take authority of these situations.

"Maguire does it at the moment, but he's not doing it every week and they obviously don't think he can play for 90 minutes for whatever reason.

"So it has to be something they address in January, a big, physical defender would be perfect for them and is just what they need to help sort things out at the back."

Man United have been putting plans in place to sign a new centre-back for a while, and have identified a number of big-name targets ahead of the January transfer window.

Names linked with a potential move to Old Trafford include the likes of Jarrad Branthwaite and Nico Schlotterbeck, both of whom would match the physical profile in mind.

Meanwhile, Brown told Football Insider that Man United have set their sights on signing Fulham star Calvin Bassey, who has impressed scouts with his performances.

There is a belief that the Nigeria international could be tempted to make the move to Man United if an offer was tabled, as he ticks a number of boxes for the recruitment team.

That is despite Alvaro Arbeloa's recent comments about his plans to keep hold of Bassey long-term, who he has labelled "one of the best centre-backs in the world".
