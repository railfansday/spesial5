Everton predicted lineup vs. Coventry: Fresh Jack Grealish decision has been made

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

By Matt Law | 04 Nov 2026 11:53, Last updated: 05 Nov 2026 21:44

Jack Grealish will again not be involved when Everton tackle Coventry City in Friday's Premier League clash at the Hill Dickinson Stadium.

The on-loan Manchester City attacker has missed the team's last two matches due to a disciplinary issue, and he has also been ruled out of this contest.

"Jack is not available. I wouldn't give you the plan, but we will be working with Jack again soon," Everton head coach David Moyes said in his pre-match press conference.

Moyes could otherwise have a full squad available for this game, and it would not be a surprise to see the same XI from the Newcastle United clash last time out, although a late check needs to be made on Jarrad Branthwaite, who is a slight doubt at this stage.

Thierno Barry has scored eight goals in 12 appearances this season and will continue in the final third of the field for the Merseyside team.

Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall should again feature as the number 10, with the 28-year-old a standout performer for the Toffees of late.
