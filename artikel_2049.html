Report makes puzzling ‘West Ham’s most important player’ claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

‘Silly season’ – or as it is better known, the ‘International break’, the two week hiatus in real West Ham-related football content to report, does occasionally throw up controversial, eyebrow raising or nonsensical articles which harassed authors have produced under the demands of editors or when faced by the blank page of content schedules. And I should know.

One such article caught my eye today: It certainly got me clicking and thinking – which was probably the motive behind the bold claim made by the report which claimed that one of the new young arrivals at London Stadium has usurped Jarrod Bowen as the ‘most important’.

Such is the claim made by footballfancast.com in their article this morning:

With their spurious argument that Arne Engels – yes indeed, do not adjust your specs – is now ‘West Ham’s most important player’.

I’m all for a little creative license during these weeks of no-football journalistic desperation but.. that’s just plain wrong. And few of you, I feel, will rush to the comments section to argue with me.

Bowen’s influence on games is unparalleled – whilst he may drift in or out of focus during a game, at any stage in the ninety minutes his contribution may be a goal, an assist or very often both. West Ham’s leading goal scorer in both the Premier League and the Championship is irreplaceable and vital to their hope of automatic promotion.

Now don’t get me wrong, Arne Engels is turning into a very useful midfield player and as he’s pushed further forward into an advanced role, should become more influential on games as befits his £22 million championship record-breaking transfer fee. But .. West Ham’s most important player?…steady on. I suggest there’d be a few hundred thousand who might disagree.
