Blackpool managerial race takes surprise turn as 33-year-old emerges as leading contender after recent retirement

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Conor Coady announced his retirement at the start of the month to turn his attention towards coaching. He is currently an assistant coach for the England U21s side, but Blackpool could offer him a first club role.

The Tangerines are searching for a new permanent manager after Ian Evatt’s dismissal last week. They sit 20th in the League One table at present with 13 points to their name in 15 games.

Reporter Pete O’Rourke states that Coady is emerging as a ‘top contender’ for the job at Bloomfield Road. He is said to have impressed FA officials early on in the England youth setup and could earn a club role as a result.

Following a run of one victory in 10 league outings, Steve Thompson stepped in for his third spell as caretaker boss and has overseen a defeat to Wigan Athletic and a draw with Leicester City.

At 33, Coady would become the second-youngest manager in the EFL, marginally older than Southampton’s Tonda Eckert. He might not have pedigree in the dugout, but he would bring a wealth of experience from an illustrious playing career.

Best remembered for an incredibly fruitful eight-year stint at Wolves, Coady asserted himself at the heart of their backline as he played an influential role in their back-to-back 7th-place finishes in 2018/19 and 2019/20.

Having captained Wolves to the Championship title and in subsequent UEFA Europa League campaigns, the ex-Liverpool youngster became renowned for his vocal leadership and infectious personality as he quickly established himself as a fan favourite at Molineux.

A move into coaching felt inevitable and he has impressed in the Three Lions’ setup since joining in September. He is passing on his wealth of experience to the next generation, and it could serve him well in a lead role.

Coady has only been involved in coaching for a matter of months, and a move to Blackpool would be the definition of being thrown in at the deep end.

With the prospect of relegation to League Two for the first time since 2017 looming large, the Tangerines are a club divided and there will be pressure to immediately deliver results if he joins.

Taking on his first managerial role in the midst of a relegation dogfight would be a tough task for Coady, particularly with a free-falling side who have shown little signs of building upon last season’s 13th-placed finish.

However, the Blackpool hierachy will certainly take encouragement from fellow strugglers Bromley’s recent upturn in form after parting company with Andy Woodman in favour of Karl Robinson, hoping similar success can be emulated at Bloomfield Road.
