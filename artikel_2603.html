Rachaad White active for Commanders, Dax Hill active for Bengals

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Running backs Jacory Croskey-Merritt and Rachaad White will both be available for the Commanders against the Bengals.

Croskey-Merritt was activated from injured reserve on Monday afternoon and White is active after being listed as questionable due to a heel injury.

Cornerback Amik Robertson, quarterback Athan Kaliakmanis, running back Robert Henry, defensive tackle Ricky Barber, and guard Tanoa Togiai are inactive for Washington.

The Bengals will have cornerback Dax Hill and left guard Dylan Fairchild in the lineup on Monday night. Both players were listed as questionable, but they avoided the inactive list for Cincinnati.

Right guard Dalton Risner was ruled out on Saturday with knee issues. Safety Kyle Dugger, offensive lineman Myles Hinton, defensive end Cam Grandy, and defensive tackle Landon Robinson are also out for the Bengals.
