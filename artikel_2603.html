NBA rumors: 76ers insider targets Bulls’ breakout star as ideal trade target

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

NBA rumors: 76ers insider targets Bulls' breakout star as ideal trade target appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Philadelphia 76ers are coming off a rough 128-111 loss to the Portland Trail Blazers in their first game against a Western Conference opponent on Wednesday. The main reason for that loss was that Joel Embiid didn't play due to illness, leaving LeBron James to start at center against a team featuring Donovan Clingan and Robert Williams III.

The backup center position has been a huge topic for a 76ers team believed to be in contention for a championship. 76ers GM Mike Gansey is believed to be working on improving the roster construction and eyeing centers that could join the team and fortify their front line.

Philly Voice writer Adam Aaronson wrote that Chicago Bulls big man Jalen Smith is an option who stands out for the Sixers, with the caveat that it depends on whether he becomes available.

"One option that continues to stand out should he become available: Jalen Smith of the Chicago Bulls."

Aaronson said the cost of a deal is an open question, but he was firm on how well Smith fits.

"Whether or not the Sixers should (or can) meet the price for Smith – both in terms of trade value and outgoing salary – remains very much up in the air. But his fit on this roster, and the lift he could give this team, are unassailable."

He reported that Philadelphia has pursued Smith before, and he described Smith's current contract and situation in Chicago.

"Smith is a player the Sixers targeted at last year's trade deadline, a source told PhillyVoice. Now, he has an expiring contract worth about $9.4 million and is playing for a rudderless Bulls team firmly focused on the future."

Smith is having a standout year for the Bulls, averaging 14.1 points, 7.5 rebounds, and 2.2 assists while shooting 54.7% from the field. He recently had a stellar game against the Denver Nuggets in a 151-145 overtime loss. Despite going up against Nikola Jokic, widely considered as one of the best centers in the league, he scored 26 points, added 4 rebounds, 2 blocks, 2 steals, 1 assist, and knocked down 6 three-pointers while shooting 71.4% from the field.

Perhaps Smith could be the answer to the Sixers' problems, but Gansey will have to weigh what he could move to get a player like Smith on the roster.
