Boston Celtics Jayson Tatum and Paul George need more support

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Boston Celtics Jayson Tatum and Paul George need more support on the offensive end of the court. Or at least that is the point of view of the folks behind the "WEEI Boston Sports Original" YouTube channel put together a clip from their "Jones and Keefe" show that took some time to talk over what they think is behind an ongoing stretch of inconsistent offensive play with the Celtics.

Speaking to guest Justin Turpin, also of WEEI, they hash out why they believe that George and Tatum need some scoring punch they can rely on if they want the season to go as they would hope it can. Do the Celtics need to trade for some offensive punch, or is there a way to solve this slump from the inside?
