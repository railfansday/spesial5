The Bucks will “continue pushing NBA officials” to …

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Bucks will "continue pushing NBA officials" to award Milwaukee an All-Star Game despite losing the 2028 event to Miami, according to Ricardo Torres of the MILWAUKEE JOURNAL SENTINEL. The club believes Milwaukee "has everything the NBA needs to host such an event." Bucks President Josh Glessing said, "We're going to constantly bring it up with the league." Glessing admits the pitch for Milwaukee does have some "challenges." Glessing said, "What we know is there's a minimum threshold of hotel rooms and we have work to do there." He added that the team "has helped build two new hotels in The Trade and AC Hotel by Marriott which is currently under construction."
