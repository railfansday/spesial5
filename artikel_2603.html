Lakers star’s status vs. Jazz will be ‘dictated on the level of soreness’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Austin Reaves wasn't available for the Lakers' home game against the Hornets, but his status for Wednesday's matchup against the Jazz at Crypto.com Arena hadn't been determined as of Tuesday night.

Coach JJ Redick said the Lakers star guard will get on the floor on Wednesday ahead of the game against the Jazz before his availability is determined.

Austin Reaves of the Los Angeles Lakers arrives at Fiserv Forum in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, on Wednesday, Nov. 11, before a game against the Bucks. NBAE via Getty Images

Reaves was sidelined for the second straight game because of a left heel bruise/contusion he suffered during the third quarter of the Lakers' Nov. 11 road win over the Bucks.

Redick was consistent with his previous comments that Reaves' availability will be determined by his pain tolerance.

Austin Reaves of the Los Angeles Lakers looks on during a game against the Detroit Pistons in Detroit, Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

Kevon Looney got his first start of the season Tuesday. NBAE via Getty Images

"Obviously, we'll see how he's feeling [Wednesday] morning," Redick said. "That's going to be dictated on the level of soreness there."

Reaves is averaging 24.7 points (51.3% from the field, 40.9% from beyond the arc), 4.7 assists and 3.3 rebounds for the Lakers, who entered Tuesday's game tied for the best record in the league (12-2).

The Lakers started a lineup of Luka Dončić, Quentin Grimes, Matisse Thybulle, Jake LaRavia and Kevon Looney against the Hornets with Reaves and Walker Kessler both sidelined Tuesday.
