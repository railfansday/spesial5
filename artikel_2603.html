UEFA Nations League: Kane starts as Bayern superstar set to smash Peter Shilton's England record

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Harry Kane will make his 126th England appearance with the Three Lions hosting Croatia on Thursday evening. The FC Bayern striker will hold the appearance record all by himself tonight as he surpassed legendary goalkeeper Peter Shilton. The England captain will be honored at Wembley stadium before the game for his appearance record and winning the Ballon d'Or.

Meanwhile, Jarrell Quansah is also in the starting lineup as the Bayer Leverkusen defender continues to be a regular fixture in Thomas Tuchel's team. Jobe Bellingham will be hoping for his first cap against world champions Spain, with the Borussia Dortmund excluded from the matchday squad for tonight's game.

Kane's Bayern teammate Josip Stanišić starts for Croatia in defense. Freiburg's Igor Matanović will lead the attacking line for the visitors.

Here are the lineups for tonight's game at Wembley as Croatia hope to avenge for their 7-0 defeat last month.

England: Pickford; Trent, Quansah, Burn, Hall; Rice, Anderson; Rogers, Jude Bellingham, Gordon; Kane ©

Croatia: Livakovic; Smolcic, Stanišić, Vusković, Gvardiol; Misic, Kovacic ©; Vlasic, Sucic, Baturina; Matanović
