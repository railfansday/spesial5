AFC playoff picture: Denver Broncos fall to the 5th seed during their bye week

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

After last Sunday's victory over the Carolina Panthers, the Denver Broncos moved into the #2 seed in the AFC and still had control of the AFC West. However, after their bye week, things have changed a bit. The Chiefs moved to the top of the AFC West after their victory over the Atlanta Falcons due to a tiebreaker, and that dropped the Broncos to the 5th seed in the AFC.

The Broncos will have a chance to reclaim their AFC West lead moving forward, but for now, they trail the Chiefs and would currently be a wildcard team if the playoffs started today.

As for the rest of the AFC, we saw some big changes. The Bengals moved into the top spot in the AFC North, and the New England Patriots dropped out of the playoffs after their loss to the Lions. The Bills, Chiefs, and Jaguars all won their matchups this Sunday.

Kansas City Chiefs (7-2, 3-1 AFCW, 6-1 AFC)

Jacksonville Jaguars (7-2, 6-2 AFC, win over CIN)

Cincinnati Bengals (7-2, 5-2 AFC, loss to JAX)

Baltimore Ravens (4-5, 2-4 AFC) – Monday Night Football

Los Angeles Chargers (0-8) – Monday Night Football

Since we last talked, the Bills outlasted the Vikings on Monday Night and then dominated their AFC East rivals, the New York Jets, this past Sunday. Now, the Bills have an easy path to an AFC East title with the Patriots, Dolphins, and Jets all struggling, but can they win the one seed?

Looking ahead, they host their division rivals, the Dolphins, before going on a tough stretch where they face the Patriots and Packers on the road, host the Bears, and then face the Broncos at Mile High on Christmas. If they want the one seed, they have to earn it going against some of the better teams in the NFL this season.

Looking ahead: vs. Dolphins, vs. Chiefs, @ Patriots, @ Packers, vs. Bears, @ Broncos, @ Dolphins, vs. Jets

Even without Kenneth Walker, the Chiefs dominated the Atlanta Falcons. Patrick Mahomes had a vintage performance, while rookie running back Emmett Johnson filled in as the starter and had 171 yards from scrimmage and 2 touchdowns. With this win, they moved back into the top spot in the AFC West, but this will be a battle that will continue throughout the coming weeks.

Next week, they host the struggling Arizona Cardinals before going through a tough stretch of games that will define their season. They will travel on the road to face the Bills, Rams, and Bengals before hosting the Patriots and 49ers. This is the stretch of games the Broncos need the Chiefs to struggle so they can hopefully extend their lead in the division and win the AFC West for a second straight season.

Looking ahead: vs. Cardinals, @ Bills, @Rams, @ Bengals, vs. Patriots, vs. 49ers, @ Chargers, vs. Raiders

The Jags remained one of the hottest teams in the NFL with a convincing victory over the Titans on Sunday. Trevor Lawrence continues to play well; their run game is explosive, and Parker Washington continues to be one of the best playmakers in the entire NFL. We can already crown them as the 2026 AFC South Champions.

Looking ahead, they have an easy road ahead and appear to be strong contenders for the one seed in the AFC. They have a road game vs. the Giants next Sunday, followed by a home game vs. the Titans, a road game vs. the Bears, followed by games against the Steelers, Texans, Cowboys, Commanders, and Colts.

Due to their victory earlier in the season, but the Broncos own a tie-breaker vs. the Jaguars.

Looking ahead: @ Giants, vs. Titans, @ Bears, vs. Steelers, @ Texans, @ Cowboys, vs. Commanders, and @ Colts

Joe Burrow and the Bengals' explosive passing offense remained hot on Sunday Night with a big win against their division rivals, the Pittsburgh Steelers. This win put them at the top of the AFC North and likely the favorites to win that division moving forward.

After their big win tonight, the Bengals will travel on the road to take on the Commanders before facing the Saints at home and then another divisional road game vs. the Browns. Then, they have a big game vs. Patrick Mahomes and the Chiefs before finishing out the season against the Panthers, Colts, Ravens, and Browns.

Looking ahead: @ Commanders, vs. Saints, @ Browns, vs. Chiefs, @ Panthers, @ Colts, vs. Ravens, and vs. Browns

The Broncos enjoyed a much-needed bye week and will hopefully emerge from this week healthier than they were coming into it. Both starting safeties, Talanoa Hufanga and Brandon Jones, missed multiple games; Riley Moss and Jaylen Waddle left the Panthers game with concussions, and the rest of the roster needed some time off after a tough 9-game stretch to start the season.

Coming out of the bye week, the Broncos host rookie QB Fernando Mendoza and the Las Vegas Raiders before traveling to the East Coast on a short week to face the Steelers on Black Friday. After that, they face the Dolphins, Jets, and Raiders before two important games vs. the current one seed, the Buffalo Bills on Christmas, followed by a road date in Foxboro to face the Patriots before ending the year against the currently winless Chargers.

Looking ahead: vs. Raiders, @ Steelers, vs. Dolphins, @ Jets, @ Raiders, vs. Bills, @ Patriots, and vs. Chargers

The Steelers ran into Joe Burrow and the Bengals' offensive buzzsaw on Sunday Night and suffered a tough loss to their division rivals. This loss knocked them to 6-3 and out of the AFC North lead and into the 6th seed in the AFC. They have a tough defense and a really good run game, so they are built to be productive as the days turn colder as we near closer to winter.

Looking ahead, they take on a banged-up Eagles team before hosting the Broncos on Black Friday. They will follow that up with a game vs. the Texans, a tough road game vs. the Jags, a game vs. their division rivals, the Baltimore Ravens, before finishing out the year against the Panthers, Titans, and Ravens again.

Looking ahead: @ Eagles, vs. Broncos, vs. Texans, @Jaguars, vs. Ravens, vs. Panthers, @ Titans, and @ Ravens

It hasn't always been pretty, but the Cleveland Browns keep finding ways to win and had yet another comeback victory vs. the Houston Texans on Sunday. After the Patriots were blown out in Germany, the Browns moved from the "In the Hunt" category into the 7th seed in the AFC.

Can they keep it? Time will tell, but they are in a good position moving forward.

The Browns will enjoy a week off next week before starting their second half of the schedule with the hopes of continuing their playoff run. Coming out of the bye, they have three home games in a row vs. the Raiders, Bengals, and Falcons before having road games vs. the Giants and Ravens, a home game vs. the Colts before ending the season on the road vs. the Bengals.

Looking ahead: BYE, vs. Raiders, vs. Bengals, vs. Falcons, @ Giants, @ Ravens, vs. Colts, and @ Bengals

8. New England Patriots (5-5): The Patriots lost in Germany and saw quarterback Drake Maye leave the game with an ankle injury. No word on the severity, but they are trending in the wrong direction.

9. Baltimore Ravens (4-5): The Ravens play on Monday Night and will look to keep their slim postseason hopes alive. A win puts them right behind the Browns, but a loss could all but knock them out of postseason contention. Can Jacoby Brissett save the day?

10. Las Vegas Raiders (4-6): After a hot start to the season, the Raiders continue to lose, and that continued this week with rookie Fernando Mendoza making his first career start. If they want to keep their slim postseason hopes alive, they will need to upset the Broncos next Sunday.

11. Houston Texans (3-6): I am keeping them here simply due to their preseason expectations. The reality is, their tough loss to the Browns, where they allowed a big second-half comeback, may have ended their hopes of making the postseason. They cannot afford many more losses moving forward.
