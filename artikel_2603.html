Carlos Alcaraz To Remain In ATP Finals Despite Injury: Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Carlos Alcaraz will remain in the ATP Finals in Turin despite a left hamstring/thigh injury, according to a report from @partidazocope

There had been reports that Alcaraz would withdraw from the year-end event.

Alcaraz suffered the injury Wednesday during his 6-2, 6-4, victory over Daniil Medvedev and appeared to let up slightly on balls he might otherwise have gone for despite winning the match.

"These are moments I've experienced before. I'm getting to know my body better and better," Alcaraz told reporters. "The experience of having been through situations like this several times before helps me respond the right way, both on and off the court."

The seven-time Grand Slam champion has won 11 straight indoor matches after collecting titles at the Vienna Open and the Paris Indoor and is slated to face Flavio Cobolli on Friday in the Group stage.

If Alcaraz goes on to win the event, he would become just the fourth man in history to win all four Grand Slam titles and the ATP Finals. The others are Andre Agassi, Roger Federer and Novak Djokovic.

Rafa Nadal came close, winning all four majors, but never once claimed an ATP Finals title.
