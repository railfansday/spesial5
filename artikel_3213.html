This Growth ETF Has Crushed the S&P 500 Since 2004. Here's Why It's Still a Buy.

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The upside outweighs the inevitable volatility.

For a growth investor, the goal of any investment, whether an individual stock or ETF, should be to grow as much as possible. However, when it comes to comparisons, the S&P 500 (^GSPC +2.35%) remains the benchmark for measuring performance.

One ETF that has routinely outperformed the S&P 500 since it began trading in January 2004 is the Vanguard Morningstar Growth ETF (VUG +2.77%). In that time, it's up 895% compared to the S&P 500's 539%. Only time will tell what the future holds, but signs point to that trend continuing.

VUG holds 135 large-cap growth stocks across all major sectors, but it's heavily concentrated in tech (72.1%). That checks out given its growth focus, but it also helps to have a hedge in other sectors. All of its top 10 holdings are tech companies (including the "Magnificent Seven" stocks), but its top 20 includes blue chip stocks like Eli Lilly, Visa, Mastercard, and Costco Wholesale.

With VUG down 11% in the past month, and the Nasdaq Composite officially in correction territory as of market close on Nov. 6, investors may wonder whether now's the time to invest, with bear market fears looming and uncertainty high.

While we can't predict how the market will move (particularly in the short term), I trust VUG to weather the storm. It has experienced three bear markets -- the financial crisis, the COVID-19 pandemic, and the 2022 bear market -- and bounced back from each:

Data source: YCharts. Returns are based on VUG's $82.17 closing price on Nov. 6. Percentages rounded to the nearest whole percent.

Past results don't guarantee future performance, so we can't assume it will continue. However, the companies leading the way in VUG are built to succeed over the long haul. That doesn't mean there won't be hiccups and rough patches along the way (there absolutely will be), but the long-term trajectory matters much more than the short-term surges or plunges.

I wouldn't invest a lump sum in VUG right now because of the volatility, but it's a good time to start a stake or slowly add to an existing position.
