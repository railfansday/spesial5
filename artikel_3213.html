Netflix Edits Schizophrenia Scene Out Of Tyson Fury Doc After Wife Reveals Boxer Does Not Have Condition

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Tyson Fury does not have schizophrenia and a scene in which his wife claims he does has been edited out of the Netflix movie about the British boxing superstar.

The doc, titled Tyson Fury, provides a tell-all account of Fury’s mental health struggles as he builds up to his fight with Anthony Joshua next month, which is also on Netflix.

The trailer initially showed Fury’s wife Paris Fury saying the boxer is a “mild schizophrenic,” but amid questions from the boxing regulator, this has now been revealed to be untrue. “I want to clarify a statement I made in the original version of the Tyson Fury documentary,” a statement from Paris Fury said. “I recalled a conversation from many years ago when discussing Tyson’s condition and I used the word ‘schizophrenic.’ While Tyson did show some symptoms associated with schizophrenia, he doesn’t have this condition and has never received a diagnosis.” Watch on Deadline

A Netflix spokeswoman said the scene has been removed from the doc, which dropped today.

“Following new information shared by the family relating to Tyson’s official mental health diagnosis, we have worked alongside them to update the documentary accordingly ahead of launch,” she added.

Following the schizophrenia comments, which were also included in a Netflix press release about the doc on Monday, the British Boxing Board of Control said it had been examining whether what was thought to be Fury’s diagnosis would get in the way of his long-anticipated fight with fellow British boxer Joshua.

That fight has now been cleared to take place on December 11 in Cardiff and could prove to be one of Netflix’s most lucrative live sporting events.

Fury’s doc is billed as a “raw, real, unfiltered” look at “the man behind the boxing icon.” Fury has also appeared in two seasons of Netflix’s ob-doc At Home With the Furys, with a third in motion. Both the feature doc and TV show are produced by Banijay-backed Optomen.
