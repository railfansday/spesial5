2026 Lexus ES

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

More than just a gussied-up Camry, the 2026 ES is Lexus's luxury ethos summed up in a handsome four-door package. The ES is known for its quiet operation, premium interior, and comfort-first road manners, and although it's been redesigned for the 2026 model year, that hasn't changed. This year, the ES ditches its nonhybrid powertrain for hybrid and electric ones (we review the EV model separately).

The ES350h is powered by a four-cylinder hybrid setup with 244 horsepower and front- or all-wheel drive. Entry-level models may show their Toyota roots a bit, but loaded versions of the ES350h are better equipped to rival other mid-size luxury sedans that start at higher price tags, which gives the Lexus a bang-for-your-buck appeal that's unique in this segment.

Expert Tip: If you want even more luxury in your Lexus sedan, the brand's flagship LS500 is the way to go. But act fast, because that model is on the chopping block and won't return for 2027.

Lexus's entry-level luxury sedan is the beneficiary of a makeover for 2026. Styling is dramatically changed over the 2025 model, and the new ES is over six inches longer than the outgoing one, which Lexus says makes the cabin more spacious. Inside, a new 14.0-inch touchscreen infotainment system replaces last year's smaller 12.3-inch unit. The new screen also features Lexus's latest infotainment interface and features integrated climate control knobs with their own individual temperature readout screens in the center.

The ES is offered only in ES350h guise, and front-wheel drive models are powered by a hybrid powertrain setup consisting of a 2.5-liter four-cylinder and two electric motors that combine for 244 horsepower. A continuously variable automatic transmission is standard. All-wheel drive models get a third electric motor that powers the rear axle, but combined horsepower remains the same at 244 horsepower.

The ES has always been a smooth operator, and this generation is no different. Lexus says this new model has more chassis rigidity than older models, and more attention has been spent on reducing body vibrations. When we drove the 2026 ES350h, we were impressed by its ride quality, but noted that it's no sports sedan, as its handling is still easy-going rather than sharp-edged.

In Comparison: The ES350h is quicker than as the similarly sized Toyota Crown, which needed 7.2 seconds to hit 60 mph at our test track.

The EPA rates the ES hybrid as high as 48 mpg city, 44 mpg highway, and 46 mpg combined. The all-wheel-drive version is slightly less efficient, with ratings of 47 mpg city, 42 mpg highway, and 44 mpg combined. On our real-world 75-mph highway fuel economy route, the all-wheel-drive ES350h matched its EPA rating, delivering 42 mpg. For more information about the ES's fuel economy, visit the EPA's website.

First Row: With its stylish dash and high-quality materials, the ES's cabin creates a plush environment for passengers. Ambient interior lighting and 3D-printed faux bamboo wood trim are used on the door panels for an upscale look. The seats are comfortable, and there's plenty of space for adults of all sizes to find their ideal driving position.

Second Row: A pair of passengers will also have no issues settling into comfort in the ES's back seat. Similar environs await you in the rear compartment, with nicely cushioned seats and dedicated rear-seat air vents for the climate control.

Cargo Capacity: A large trunk gives the ES ample cargo space, and we expect to find it similarly sized to rivals. When we get a chance, we'll stuff the trunk with carry-on suitcases and report our findings here.

A 14.0-inch infotainment touchscreen takes a prominent location on the ES's dashboard. It features wireless connectivity for smartphones with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, as well as native navigation. A 10-speaker stereo is standard, and a 17-speaker Mark Levinson premium stereo system is available as an option.

A host of driver-assistance features comes standard, including convenience favorites such as adaptive cruise control, blind-spot monitoring, and automatic high-beam headlamps.

For information about the ES's crash-test results, visit the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) and Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) websites.

Lexus provides longer warranty coverage than rivals such as BMW and Mercedes-Benz. However, it trails Genesis, which offers one of the best warranties in the segment.
