Dow dives 748 points as stocks hit session lows the moment Anthropic starts trading

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Anthropic stock opened at $111.50, up 34% from its IPO price, as the S&P 500 fell 1% and the Nasdaq dropped 0.9%

Anthropic stock opened at $111.50 on the Nasdaq $NDAQ on Thursday, 34% above its $83 IPO price, and the major U.S. stock indexes fell to session lows as trading began.

The Dow Jones Industrial Average was down 748 points, or 1.5%. The S&P 500 slipped 1%, and the Nasdaq fell 0.9% after having climbed by as much as 2% at its peak. Anthropic trades under the ticker ANT and opened before noon.

Anthropic set its share price higher than the $70 to $78 band it had signaled to investors and enlarged the deal, according to The Wall Street Journal. The offering is the largest on record. Semiconductor shares and other artificial-intelligence names had rallied earlier in the morning once the pricing was announced.

At one stage, Anthropic stock dropped over 10% from where it opened, but it later recovered to a 20% gain, near $100 a share.

Nvidia $NVDA, Micron $MU Technology and Meta $META Platforms gave up their gains. Advanced Micro Devices stock fell over 8% after Hunterbrook Capital revealed it was betting against the shares, citing possible violations of U.S. export controls tied to China, according to CNBC.

Steve Sosnick, chief strategist at Interactive Brokers, described the decline as part of a pattern, according to CNBC. "It seems to be merely the latest round of 'sell the rally' behavior that has pervaded market activity this month," Sosnick said. "The drop was quick, stunning, and meaningful. It appears that traders were waiting for the deal to begin trading to unleash another bout of selling, and once SPX broke through its recent support, that led to another round of sharp declines."

Not everyone on Wall Street viewed the drop as a concern. “Today doesn’t matter,” Ted Mortonson, a tech strategist at Baird, told CNBC. “It’s a blink of an eye of their journey. It’s an important event, but how they navigate the next three years that will determine valuation.”

Investors have grown warier of AI lately, questioning if the massive outlays to construct the technology's infrastructure will eventually deliver substantial returns, according to CNBC.
