Watch: Alaskan flying team airdrops Thanksgiving turkeys to remote communities

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

For the last few years, "turkey bombers" Esther Keim and Heidi Hastings have taken to the skies to airdrop Thanksgiving turkeys to residents in off-grid communities in south-central Alaska.

Hastings pilots the plane while her best friend Keim drops the turkeys out the plane window, delivering to families who may not otherwise get one of the birds, since they are cut off by snow and ice.
