Middlesbrough fans think Kim Hellberg got one thing wrong v Wrexham

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Middlesbrough fell to their second defeat of the Championship season on Wednesday night, as Wrexham bounced back from consecutive defeats to pick up a vital three points at the Racecourse Ground.

In a game with very few chances, Phil Parkinson's side broke the deadlock with 20 minutes to go through George Thomason, and made sure the points were safe after Ollie Rathbone scored a second six minutes from time.

It's the first time that Middlesbrough have failed to score all season. Kim Hellberg's side have been electric going forward, led primarily by Will Lankshear, and it wasn't a coincidence that his absence after suffering a muscle issue led to Boro being a little less dangerous in front of goal.

However, it wasn't just the fact that Middlesbrough lined up with the likes of Kyle Joseph, Tommy Conway and David Strelec as the central nine at times on Wednesday night which frustrated Boro fans, as for the first time in quite a while, Hellberg himself wasn't without criticism.

Kim Hellberg is one of the more tactically fluid head coaches in the Championship today. He's comfortable deploying a back four or a back three, and spoke in his press conference last week about being happy operating with two strikers.

Unfortunately, the switch to a back three in possession and a back five out of it against Wrexham on Wednesday night has drawn quite a bit of criticism from Boro fans.

With Hellberg's side having a chance to extend their lead atop the table to four points last night against a Wrexham side who have underwhelmed this season and had lost their last two, the supporters were disappointed to see a perceived 'negative' lineup.

"Far too negative against a bang average side Kim. I hope you've learned from this? You know the score mate, sort it out. UTB!🇦🇹" one fan said on X in response to the Swede's post-match interview.

"5 at the back? More defensive against Moore? A team sat in the bottom half of the league, shambles Kim," another said.

Other fans accused Hellberg of "inviting Wrexham to give us a game," while others expressed frustration at setting up in a five "against a poor team." "He has to take responsibility for the formation and selections!" was also among the comments.

"Lost for words Kim? Poor team selection and formation tonight. Never play with a back 3 again," was said bluntly. Evidently, the majority of Middlesbrough fans are hoping Hellberg reverts to a back four on Saturday at Watford.

Ultimately, while Wrexham haven't enjoyed the start to the season that many expected they would have, Phil Parkinson's side is still full of talented players who can hurt any team on a good day.

This one can be chalked up to a sub-par performance from a Middlesbrough side and a bit of a better one from a Wrexham perspective, and the fact that Boro are still sitting top of the division regardless, just shows how strong they have been to start the season.

No league champion goes the whole campaign without losing, and those at the Riverside will be hoping that this is just a mere bump in the road en route to a Premier League return.

Despite how many perceive the Hornets down near the bottom of the Championship, they have been good at home this season, winning their last two and taking points off West Ham United and Southampton as well.

It won't be a cakewalk, but Middlesbrough will see a matchup against a side near the bottom of the division as a good way to bounce back before the international break.

While a lot of the focus at 1:45 when the team sheets are revealed on Saturday will be on whether Kim Hellberg sticks with the back three or rotates back to a four, there'll also be eyes on the striker position and if Lankshear is back fit again.

That's the hope from the Swedish boss, but it will be among the Boro fanbase too, as the small sample size does show that they're a much worse team without the 21-year-old leading the line.

That's now a game and a half that Lankshear has been out for Middlesbrough, either rested or injured. Against Cardiff City last month, the league's top scorer started on the bench but had to be introduced at half-time with his team 2-0 down. Now, Boro have lost by that exact same scoreline with him missing from the squad.

As Hellberg stated after the defeat, losing your top scorer would be detrimental to any side in the division, but Middlesbrough have to find a way to take chances and be better without theirs.

If this muscle setback proves to be worse than feared, Boro fans won't have to worry about a switch to a back three in the near future.
