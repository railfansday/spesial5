The 15 Best Beauty Minis That Fit Right in the Palm of Your Hand

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Fun-size favorites from Prada, Rare Beauty, and more make perfect holiday stocking stuffers

The ideas that things can be “small but mighty” and that “good things come in small packages” are especially true for your favorite beauty products. There’s plenty of room, metaphorically and literally, to curate a collection of the best beauty minis, perfect for packing in your carry-on or gifting to friends and family for the holidays.

Itty-bitty, often limited-edition versions of rotation staples you know and love are simply adorable, like Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez Soft Pinch Liquid Blush and Prada’s tiniest set of stylish metal claw clips. Plus, these staples are not only aesthetically pleasing, but also won’t take up too much real estate on your counter or in a festive stocking. If you’re looking to expand your collection of hair, makeup, skincare, and fragrance goodies while keeping it small, I invite you to peruse our short list of the best, too-cute-for-words beauty minis.

Sure, your best friend could use regular bar soap, but why not gift them with this distinguished set of four minis instead? This second edition of Soft Services’s Buffing Bar Miniature Collection might be even better than the first and is entirely dedicated to gourmand scents. From the vanilla-coconut cream, espresso-, and citrus-scented Affogato L'Orange to their best-selling and extremely fresh Green Banana bar to try, and more, chances are your friend will be taking a bunch of calls from the bathtub.

Whether you’re between washes or just want extra volume, K18’s keychain-friendly AirWash Dry Shampoo is great to keep on hand. The non-aerosol shampoo absorbs excess scalp oil and eliminates odor thanks to translucent microbeads and the brand’s patented Odorbind technology.

A dab on each cheek will do you when it comes to Rare Beauty’s viral, highly pigmented blush, and this set also contains their Liquid Luminizer and Luminizing Lip Gloss in limited-edition shades your younger sister can enjoy. Since the blush features a blendable formula that lasts up to 12 hours, she can use it with the rest of the kit for plenty of makeup looks to come.

If you don’t live in New York City, Sofie Pavitt Face’s mini three-step system makes it easy to bring a little of the Acne Whisperer’s treatments to your bathroom. Along with Sofie’s Kylie Jenner-approved Clean Clean Cleanser, your giftee will also discover travel-ready versions of the Mandelic Clearing Serum and Skin Jelly Oil-Free Gel Moisturizer to complete her routine.

What’s more elegant than a Louis Vuitton perfume? A tiny trio of individual Louis Vuitton perfumes. Even the pickiest fragrance connoisseur in your circle will be dazzled by this elevated perfume set, featuring the brand’s Perfumes of the World collection, long-lasting fragrances inspired by the Chinese art of living. She can rotate between Louis Vuitton’s fresh, floral Tea Storm; woody, warm Ink Mark; and floral, fruit-forward Moon Tale, depending on her mood.

Westman Atelier’s Petite Party set is perfect to toss in your bag for quick touch-ups all season long. Along with mini versions of its highlight and Baby Cheeks blush sticks, the collection also includes mini eyeliner and mascara to cover your makeup essentials.

Any Lip Butter Balm superfan will be thrilled to unbox this four-piece mini set of some of Summer Fridays’ best sellers. This limited edition includes Birthday Cake, Strawberry Soft Serve, Pink Guava, and Cherry flavors your friend can enjoy for weeks, plus supremely hydrated lips.

If your friend is an avid Alix Earle fan who likes just about everything the creator posts on TikTok, this mini five-piece skincare set is likely the perfect holiday surprise. The limited-edition set includes travel-ready versions of her brand’s Makeup Melting Cleansing Balm, Exfoliating LHA + BHA Gel Cleanser, 10% Azelaic Acid Anti-Redness Calming Serum, 8% Mandelic Acid Serum Concentrate, and Barrier-Boosting Moisturizer, created with acne-prone and sensitive skin in mind.

Here’s a set of branded claw clips that are almost too cute to wear. But if you do, I’d suggest stacking them for maximum hairstyling impact.

The person in your family with an ever-evolving hair and body mist collection can add four more to their list of favorites. Ouai’s mini bundle of beloved scents, including St. Barts, Ibiza, Santorini, and Melrose, can help mentally transport them to their next beachside vacation with just a few spritzes.

Consider this mini Eadem set an added boost to your go-to skincare routine. Inside, you’ll find the brand’s brightening, smoothing Cashmere Peel, plus its Ultra-Calming Mist and plant ceramide and peptide moisturizer. And to top it all off, the collection also includes a tiny tube of Eadem’s cult-favorite Le Chouchou Lip Softening Balm.

Consider this group of minis as part gift, part skincare treat, particularly for vanilla lovers. Your aunt can rotate through three of Josie Maran’s delectable whipped body butters featuring a blend of shea butter, argan, and avocado oils to keep their skin plump and hydrated from the neck down.

She doesn’t need a bunch of clunky equipment to get the perfect cat-eye manicure at home. Le Mini Macaron’s petite manicure kit includes just about everything an aspiring nail tech needs for a fresh look, including an LED lamp, cat-eye gel polish, a magnet, a nail file, and more.

This mini set of YSL Beauty’s Lash Clash mascaras includes black and brown shades for different makeup looks. No matter which shade you choose, each mascara is ophthalmologist-tested for contact lens wearers and those with sensitive eyes.

This giftable kit includes all four versions of Glossier’s beloved You fragrances: the original You, the woody, spicy You Doux, the more floral You Fleur, and the warm, solar You Soie. Your favorite cousin can wear them alone or layer the scents for added longevity.
