Sorry to My Sweatpants, But These 8 Thanksgiving Outfits Are Just as Comfortable and So Much Chicer

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Pass the pie and the compliments, please.

Thanksgiving is just a couple of weeks away. Aside from figuring out which dish you’re going to bring to the gathering (Mashed potatoes! Stuffing! Pumpkin pie!), you’re also charged with finding the perfect outfit. Not one look fits all occasions; it’s heavily influenced by the caliber of the celebration. Is the table set with fine china and crystal glassware, or are you watching the football game over finger food? You also have to consider who’s in attendance. If it’s a group of your girlfriends, you might opt for a flirty dress and save the knit sweater for a family brunch.

To take something off your plate and leave room for all that food, we’ve curated a lineup of eight versatile and effortlessly chic Thanksgiving outfit ideas for all types of occasions. Each one can be achieved by reaching for simple style combinations, so keep scrolling for editor-approved inspiration—but remember that the ideal look is one you feel most comfortable and confident in.

Styling a long, draped sweater dress with heeled boots is always a good idea. For an extra cozy look, consider a ribbed style featuring intentional details, like a turtleneck and asymmetrical hemline. Belts, clutches, and jewelry help dress it up.

This is the perfect in-between look for a semi-dressy dinner. The bold coat—whether faux fur, color-blocked, or fringed—elevates the ensemble when paired with sleek, closed-toe pumps. You can fill in the gaps with virtually any other basics, including denim, tees, and slouchy bags.

If a game of football and an afternoon nap on the couch is in your future, then slip on a pullover sweater and track pants. Wear a T-shirt underneath and maybe swap your dress shoes for casual sneakers. In a matter of seconds, you can master a laid-back outfit change.

Thanksgiving is a day meant for cozy vibes, so slip on your favorite fleece-lined slippers or booties. Pair them with a warm-toned flannel, and dress it up (just a smidge!) with relaxed jeans and a suede coat.

Make a scene this holiday season with a bold, printed dress and over-the-knee boots. The shorter the hemline and taller the footwear, the more dramatic the final look.

Sometimes, less is more. A coordinating sweater-and-pants combo looks instantly polished. If your look is light and airy, add visual interest with dark, moody shoes and accessories and vice versa.

When two aesthetics clash, fashion magic is created. A feminine ruffled dress directly contradicts a heavyweight bomber jacket, making it an intentional and trend-forward look.

Slip a pair of sheer tights underneath your favorite skirt to give the outfit a little oomph. From there, you can let your fashion imagination run wild and reach for everything from timeless coats to loud animal prints.
