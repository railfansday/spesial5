The Hoka and On Black Friday Sneaker Deals We’re Shopping This Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

A new pair of walking or running shoes is even better when you can get it on sale.

If a fresh pair of walking shoes or running sneakers is on your wish list, chances are Hoka and On have both caught your eye. The two brands have earned plenty of fans, and both are offering Black Friday deals worth taking a look at if your shoe rotation needs a fresh pair (or two!) added to the mix. But which brand should you go with? Ultimately, the best pick for you comes down to how much cushioning and support you want, plus the fit that feels best on your feet.

Both brands make sneakers that can take you from your daily walk to a full day of errands. Hoka is known for its plush cushioning and rocker-shaped soles, while On offers a range of cushioning levels and its signature CloudTec technology. Each has multiple models to choose from, so it’s always worth comparing individual shoes rather than assuming every pair from one brand will feel the same.

The Cloudrunner 3 offers a medium level of cushioning and is WH’s best overall On walking shoe, and it’s currenlty on sale for a 30 percent discount. Executive Health and Fitness Director Jacqueline Andriakos found it “firm and supportive (and not overly cushioned).”

For travel and casual walks, the lightweight Cloud 6 is a breathable, easy-on option. The trade-off: It offers less arch and ankle support than a more structured shoe, so it may not be the best fit if stability is a priority.

The brand’s thick, supportive soles are a draw for walkers who want a soft feeling underfoot. The Clifton 11 is WH’s best overall Hoka walking shoe: Associate Style and Reviews Editor Shannen Zitz says its “supportive yet flexible cushioning” gives her a spring in her step.

Since the Clifton 11 was just released earlier this year, now’s the time to pick up a pair of last year’s model, the Clifton 10, at a 20 percent discount. Editors loved this shoe just as much.

For longer walks, consider the Arahi 8, which offers a firmer feel and stability and is 20 percent off for Black Friday. Fitness editor Cori Ritchey praises its “well-balanced cushioning,” noting that it feels plush without compromising her stability on long walks or runs.

Whether you’re leaning toward Hoka’s plush cushioning or On’s range of supportive styles, don’t wait too long to shop; both of these brands regularly sell out during Black Friday weekend. This is especially true for popular sizes.

When ordering online, just make sure the return policy gives you the flexibility you need in case you need to return or exchange a pair after trying them on. We’ll keep this story updated with the latest brand sales, so check back as you shop.
