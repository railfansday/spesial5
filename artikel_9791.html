A 5-day client onboarding workflow for solo consultants

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

A project often starts before anyone agrees on the basics: who approves the work, what access is needed, how feedback will work, and what is explicitly out of scope.

The first week sets the tone. If it is scattered across email, calls, and half-filled documents, the project starts with missing assets, unclear decisions, and quiet scope creep.

Here is a 5-day onboarding workflow that keeps the first week visible.

Ask for platform invitations or a shared password manager. Do not ask the client to email passwords.

If an asset is not ready, ask for the date it will be available. “Later” is not a date.

Run a 45-minute call with a written agenda:

The most important question is: who is the final decision-maker? If the answer is “the team”, ask who breaks a tie.

Send it for approval. Do not start dependent work until it is approved.

Send a short confirmation that repeats the scope in plain language:

Ask the client to reply “approved”. A written approval is enough; it does not need to be a legal document.

Confirm access, communication cadence, and the first milestone. Update the tracker. If a dependency is late, move the timeline by the same number of days and document it in writing.

A three-week rebrand project is fixed at $3,500.

On day 1 the client has not sent the logo files or hosting access. The access checklist catches it. On day 2 the kickoff agenda identifies the founder as the only decision-maker. On day 3 the brief records the dependency: “Logo files and hosting access needed by Wednesday.”

The files arrive two days late. The timeline moves by two days. There is no argument because the dependency was written down before work started.

If two or more are true, slow down and fix the basics before starting production work.

This is a practical workflow, not legal, tax, or financial advice. Adapt the documents to your contract and jurisdiction. A template cannot guarantee that a client will behave well; it can only make the first week easier to see.
