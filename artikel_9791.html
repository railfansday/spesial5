How I Built a Zero-Account Chair Workout App for Women 40+ (Kotlin + Custom 3D Animations)

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Most mobile fitness apps either force signups on launch or assume users want high-impact burpees and floor planks. I wanted to build an Android app tailored specifically for women aged 40+ who want safe, low-impact seated strength, posture, and mobility training from any sturdy chair.

That led to launching Chair Workout for Women 40+ on Android:

No Account Wall (100% Local Jetpack DataStore): Instead of gating workouts behind email signups or remote health databases, workout streaks, 28-day progress, hydration logs (ml / fl oz), and weight/BMI entries stay directly on the user's phone using Android Jetpack DataStore.

Structured 28-Day Progression Over Random Lists: The program splits into four weekly themes—Move, Strength, Endurance, and Vitality—with Beginner, Standard, and Challenge difficulty tracks so users build consistency safely without joint strain.

Custom Seated Exercise Animations & Voice Prompts: Every seated movement pairs clear visual demonstrations with spoken timing and breathing cues, plus a built-in Android Home Screen Hydration Widget for daily water intake reminders.

If you're testing it out on Android, you can grab it on Google Play or read the full breakdown on ZeroBloatTools. Feedback from fellow Android builders is always welcome!
