Week 10 Cardinals TV map

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Arizona Cardinals, under new head coach Mike LaFleur, are at the midway point of the schedule and might be in trouble. At 3-6-0, the team could easily fall into the abyss of the NFL and be gone forever. They need a win. Thankfully, they are playing against the 5-4-0 Los Angeles Rams, whom they have already beaten this year.

They need a win, but haven't won a home game yet this year. The Cardinals have a last-place schedule this year, yet have one of the most difficult schedules.

The Week 10 home game for the Cardinals against the Rams will air on CBS at 2:05 (MT) and 4:05 PM ET on Sunday. Also, NFL+ and Paramount+.

Announcers: Andrew Catalon, Logan Ryan, A.J. Ross (sideline)

Arizona Cardinals Radio: Arizona Sports 98.7 FM (English flagship for the Cardinals)
