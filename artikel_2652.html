What a Ravens win or loss in Carolina means for playoffs and NFL Draft

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Baltimore Ravens can cut their deficit for the final AFC wild card to one game with a win Sunday at Carolina. A loss would leave them three games behind Cleveland with six to play. The Browns are on a bye, so Baltimore's result will determine how much ground it gains or loses against the team it is chasing.

Tankathon had Baltimore at No. 14, between Dallas and Las Vegas. All three were 4-6, with strength of schedule separating them. In the ESPN Analytics update after the Nov. 16 loss to the Chargers, Baltimore had a 4.1% chance of making the playoffs and an 8.6% chance of landing a top-five pick. Those were the estimates before the Carolina game.

A win would put the Ravens at 5-6, one game behind the 6-4 Browns and half a game behind the 5-5 Patriots, who are also on a bye. Baltimore lost its first meeting with Cleveland and hosts the Browns in Week 16. Winning that rematch would give the Ravens a chance to split the season series while handing a loss directly to the team holding the final playoff spot.

In the AFC North, a Ravens win and a Bengals loss Monday at Washington would cut Cincinnati's lead over Baltimore to 1½ games. If Cincinnati wins, the gap stays at 2½. A Pittsburgh loss at Philadelphia would pull Baltimore within one game of the Steelers, whom the Ravens still play twice. Baltimore also visits Cincinnati in Week 17, giving it a direct chance to gain ground on the division leader.

A loss would drop Baltimore to 4-7 and cap its best possible finish at 10-7. Cincinnati's division lead over the Ravens would grow to 3½ games if the Bengals beat Washington and stay at 2½ if they lose. Baltimore would still have four division games to close the season, but it would need more help before those games arrive. For the draft, watch Dallas against Tennessee and Las Vegas at Denver. If Baltimore loses and either of those 4-6 teams wins, the Ravens would move ahead of that team in the order by record. If they lose alongside Baltimore, strength of schedule would sort the tie. A Ravens win would reverse that immediate effect against any team that falls to 4-7. Dallas' first-rounder belongs to the Jets, but the Cowboys' record still determines where that pick falls.

Draft-order source note: The Tankathon page available during this check showed an older week. The timestamped Nov. 19 snapshot above supplied the strength-of-schedule figures, and NFL.com corroborated Philadelphia at No. 5 and Baltimore at No. 14 entering Week 11.
