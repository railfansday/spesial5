Kings are 1-12 and coaching rumors have begun. Two familiar names are surfacing.

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Sacramento Kings are off to a 1-12 start that has started heating up the coach's rumor mill.

The Kings are led by second-year head coach Doug Christie, who took over the position in December 2024 after Sacramento fired Mike Brown. After Christie lead Sacramento to a 27-24 record, the Kings removed the interim tag in April 2025.

Since then, Christie has a 23-72 record, leading to speculation of an eventual coaching change.

According to NBA insider Marc Stein, there are already rumbles in league coaching circles that have named both Tom Thibodeau and Mark Jackson as "potential down-the-road" head coaching candidates for the Kings, if Sacramento eventually makes a coaching change.

Both candidates would be intriguing, however, the Kings have indicated they have no plans to move on, as they are hesitant to fire Christie, according to The Athletic's Sam Amick.

Thibodeau is a two-time NBA Coach of the Year in 2011 and 2021 and is known for coaching defensive-focused teams that had perennial regular-season success and playoff appearances with the Chicago Bulls, Minnesota Timberwolves and New York Knicks. The Kings have a dynamic point guard in Darius Acuff Jr. and Thibodeau is no stranger to talented point guards having coached Derrick Rose and Jalen Brunson.

Jackson coached the Golden State Warriors for three seasons from 2011 to 2014. He hasn't coached since but has often been credited with helping build the foundation of the future Warriors dynasty led by Stephen Curry, Klay Thompson and Draymond Green. When the Warriors were an afterthought in the league, Jackson's coaching, eye for talent and how to maximize it helped the Warriors become a playoff team before they ascended into a championship team under Steve Kerr.

Christie addressed hot seat rumors after the Kings' Nov. 13 loss to the Portland Trail Blazers.

"I think this seat is hot always, so it doesn't really matter," Christie told USA TODAY. "Obviously, we want to win games. Nobody wants to win more than me, from the standpoint that the record doesn't go on the player's numbers. The record goes on coach's number."

Christie is in the final year of a guaranteed contract. He signed a three-year deal for approximately $2 million a year with the final year (2027-28) a team option.

Sacramento is Christie's first position as a head coach in the NBA. He was the Kings' assistant coach from 2021 to 2024, including the original "Beam Team" that ended a 16-year playoff drought in 2023.

Christie played for seven teams in a 15-season NBA career, including as a starter and defensive enforcer with the Kings during their best era in the early 2000s. Christie made the NBA All-Defensive First Team once and the NBA All-Defensive Second Team three times during his Kings tenure between 2000 and 2005.
