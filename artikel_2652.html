Caleb Williams remains frustrated with four interception game

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

In his last game, Caleb Williams threw four interceptions. The quarterback has not had a chance to truly put it behind him yet as the Bears had an off week in Week 10.

He called two of the picks “funky” and a third “a bad decision.”

“The biggest thing is, the frustration comes from the feeling of, for me, personally, I always don’t want to let my teammates down,” Williams said, via Courtney Cronin of ESPN. “That’s always big for me. And in that situation, in those situations, I feel like I did. And so, that’s the frustrating part that lingers.”
