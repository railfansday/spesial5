Player-turned-doctor Laurent Duvernay-Tardif is working with the Chiefs' medical staff

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Former Chiefs offensive lineman Laurent Duvernay-Tardif, who got his medical degree while playing in the NFL, is back in Kansas City working with the Chiefs’ medical staff.

“Feeling privileged to be back on the sidelines, this time shadowing the medical team to gain experience in sports medicine and on-field emergency response,” Duvernay-Tardif wrote on social media.

A native of Quebec who has been completing medical training there since retiring from the NFL, Duvernay-Tardif is currently spending a month with the Chiefs’ medical staff to learn more about treatment of athletes.

Listed at 6-foot-5 and 321 pounds during his playing days, Duvernay-Tardif appears to have lost weight and blended in on the sideline during Sunday’s Chiefs game, although CBS briefly showed him on camera.

Duvernay-Tardif was a starter for the Chiefs from 2015 to 2019. He chose not to play in 2020, instead lending his medical expertise to covid patients. He returned and played for the Jets in 2021 and 2022.
