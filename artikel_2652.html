Christian McCaffrey touchdown gives 49ers 35-10 lead in fourth quarter

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

It’s just about all but over in Mexico City.

49ers running back Christian McCaffrey capped a 13-play, 98-yard drive with a 6-yard touchdown to give San Francisco a 35-10 lead with 13:26 left in the fourth quarter.

Minnesota had put San Francisco on the 2-yard line with a well-covered punt. But San Francisco was nevertheless able to methodically make its way down the field, converting three third downs on the way to the end zone.

Newly acquired Keenan Allen caught two of the third-down conversions, moving the chains on third-and-4 with an 8-yard catch and on third-and-9 with a 13-yard reception.

The drive covered 7:04 across the third and fourth quarters, putting Minnesota in an even more unfavorable position as the team tries to get back in it.

McCaffrey now has 11 rushing touchdowns this season along with three receiving TDs.
