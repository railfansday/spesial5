Lions star Aidan Hutchinson reportedly sustains torn ACL while celebrating sack of Drake Maye

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Detroit Lions star Aidan Hutchinson made his presence known early against the New England Patriots. On the Patriots' first offensive drive, Hutchinson broke through the line on third down and sacked Drake Maye.

Following the play, Hutchinson got up to celebrate the sack. He jumped into the air and immediately fell to the ground after landing. Hutchinson appeared to be holding his right knee.

Reports Monday suggested Hutchinson tore his ACL during the celebration. He'll miss the rest of the season recovering from the injury.

Hutchinson was able to walk off the field, but headed straight to the medical tent on the sideline. He was later spotted on the broadcast heading to the locker room.

He was deemed questionable to return with a knee injury, but late in the third quarter, he was ruled out for the remainder of the game.

Detroit eventually cruised to a 35-10 victory.

Preliminary reporting Sunday hinted at a potentially "significant" knee injury for Hutchinson.

After the game, Lions head coach Dan Campbell said the team would get more information on Hutchinson's injury when it returned to the United States.

"We'll know more when we get back in the States," head coach Dan Campbell said postgame, according to the Detroit News' Nolan Bianchi. "Anytime you lose a player of his caliber, the type of teammate he is, he's a captain of ours, that doesn't make things easy."

Hutchinson wasn't the only player injured on the play. Maye also left the game. He was deemed questionable to return due to an ankle injury before eventually also being ruled out. Maye is also in danger of missing the rest of the season due to the injury.

Losing Hutchinson for any significant period of time would be a devastating result for the Lions. Coming into the game, Hutchinson was Pro Football Focus' top-graded edge rusher on the year.

While NFL players rarely get injured while celebrating plays, it has happened before. Famously in 2001, Arizona Cardinals kicker Bill Gramatica tore his ACL after celebrating a first quarter field goal in a game against the New York Giants.

More recently for the Lions, in 2014 former linebacker Stephen Tulloch celebrated a sack of Aaron Rodgers and suffered a season-ending ACL injury.
