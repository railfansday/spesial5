Joe Brady can become the fifth coach ever to win 10 of his first 11 games

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Buffalo’s Joe Brady is off to one of the best starts for any head coach in NFL history.

The Bills are 9-1, which means that if they beat the Chiefs on Thanksgiving night, Brady will be just the fifth coach in NFL history to win 10 of his first 11 games.

The four who have done it before are Jim Caldwell (11-0 with the 2009 Colts), Wally Lemm (who went 9-0 as interim head coach of the 1961 Houston Oilers and then won one of his first two games after becoming head coach of the 1962 St. Louis Cardinals), Steve Mariucci (10-1 with the 1997 49ers) and Red Miller (10-1 with the 1977 Broncos).

Brady took over a good Bills team and has kept them near the top of the NFL. His record to start his head-coaching career is impressive, but he was brought in to win in the postseason, and so it’s too soon to say whether the Bills’ decision to fire Sean McDermott and replace him with Brady will pay off.
