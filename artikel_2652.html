Ravens vs. Chargers Week 10 ’MNF’ open thread

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Okay Rumblers, it's time for another amazing NFL matchup in primetime, this time featuring the Baltimore Ravens taking on the winless Los Angeles Chargers for Week 10 of Monday Night Football. It's unfortunate this game isn't a better-on-paper matchup since it's been rare this season to see an AFC-exclusive game.

Quarterback Jacoby Brissett makes his debut for the Ravens tonight, and the fan base hopes he can save a season that feels lost without injured quarterback Lamar Jackson. Brissett and Baltimore would be wise to lean on Derrick Henry and a rushing attack that can still hurt any defense in the NFL.

As for the Chargers, well… it's ugly. Offensive coordinator Mike McDaniel has been brutal and the team in general just can't get out of its own way. Unfortunately, quarterback Justin Herbert is stuck on a team that refuses to set him free. Soon enough, he may be able to breathe a sigh of relief if the front office proves merciful enough to dismiss McDaniel and head coach John Harbaugh. The game has proven too big for both, at least in LA.

Here's your open thread for Week 10 of Monday Night football. As always, be kind to one another — and in all things… Go Bills!
