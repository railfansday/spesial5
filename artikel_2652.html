What’s your grade for the Mike Pettine Cowboys defense?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

On Sunday, the Dallas Cowboys got an all-timer from their offense. 51 points, seven touchdowns, a field goal and a kneel down. Chef's kiss.

The defense? That was a little harder to judge, and under a lot more scrutiny. We all know about Christian Parker remaining the defensive coordinator, but having the play-calling duties taken away. They were handed to Mike Pettine, who joined the Cowboys mid-season to try and stabilize that side of the ball.

Sunday's defensive performance can be looked at in two ways.

1 – They gave up 30 points to a 1-8 team (1-9 now). Defenses giving up 30 points and doing some really dumb things like forgetting to touch a player down doesn't exactly scream competence.

2 – They made stops when it counted, gave up some very fluky touchdowns, including in garbage time, and generally looked much more organized on the field. Plus that pass rush is really cooking now.
