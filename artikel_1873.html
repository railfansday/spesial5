Injury blow as Brighton star withdraws from international duty

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Brighton full back Jaouen Hadjam has withdrawn from the Algerian national team due to injury.

Algeria are due to play at home against Togo on Saturday, November 14 and then go to Togo on Tuesday, November 17 for an African Cup of Nations qualifier.

The extent of the injury to Hadjam is at this stage unclear but the player is expected to return to Brighton where he will be assessed.

Hadjam, 23, joined Brighton for around £10m last summer from Swiss club Young Boys on a five-year contract.

The Algerian has impressed so far with seven appearances for Brighton in all competitions with one goal and two assists. His goal for Brighton arrived in the 2-0 away win at Sunderland.

Hadjam is competing for minutes with Brighton's regular left back Olivier Boscagli.

Second placed Seagulls resume Premier League action after the international break at Hull City on November 22.
