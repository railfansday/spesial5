Fulham are in talks with captain Calvin Bassey over a new contract as they look to fend off interest from Manchester United, writes Standard Sport’s Sam Tabuteau.

The 26-year-old centre-back, whose contract expires next summer, is understood to be on United’s shortlist as they look to sign defensive reinforcements in January.

Fulham hold the option to extend Bassey’s current deal by a further year.

The Cottagers are keen to keep hold of Bassey, especially after Alvaro Arbeloa promoted him to captain in the summer.

Nothing has been agreed yet, but Fulham have opened talks with Bassey over a new contract and do not intend to sell him in January.

Thomas Frank has confirmed Crystal Palace are working to appoint Chelsea set-piece coach Bernardo Cueva, with a deal “very close.”

Frank was appointed the Eagles’ head coach this week, replacing the Frenchman Pierre Sage, who lasted just 16 matches in the job and left the club dead last in the Premier League.

As he looks to establish his coaching staff, though, Frank is looking into a reunion with Cueva. The pair were colleagues at Brentford between 2020 and 2024, when Cueva was poached by the Blues, who reportedly spent £1m to secure his services.

But Chelsea have upgraded on Cueva this season, signing Aston Villa’s Austin MacPhee in a similarly pricey move.

Palace have reportedly been in talks with Chelsea over Cueva’s transfer for some time, and Frank, speaking in his first press conference since taking over, has now confirmed the widespread reports that a deal is imminent.

Club Brugge winger Carlos Forbs is attracting a growing number of suiters, including Arsenal.

Fichajes reports that an impressive run of form in Belgium has alerted a number of Premier League clubs.

The 22-year-old has revitalised his career after difficult spells at Ajax and Wolves, producing five goals and eight assists in 15 appearances across all competitions this season.

Arsenal are continuing their search for a marquee forward ahead of the January transfer window.

Sky Sports report that the Premier League champions are tracking RB Leipzig’s Antonio Nusa and Brentford’s Kevin Schade as potential options, with a preference for a left-sided attacker capable of playing across the frontline.

Arsenal are also monitoring Bournemouth midfielder Alex Scott and could sell a midfielder to compete with Chelsea and Liverpool for the 23-year-old’s signature.

Manchester United have joined Tottenham in the race to sign Benfica striker Vangelis Pavlidis as Mauricio Pochettino looks to strengthen his attack in January.

The 27-year-old has scored 19 goals in 19 appearances across all competitions this season, attracting interest from several clubs.

CaughtOffside report that Pavlidis is open to a Premier League move, while United are also monitoring Victor Osimhen, Ivan Toney and Serhou Guirassy.

Pochettino is expected to receive backing in January as he looks to improve his squad, with defensive reinforcements also being considered.

Nico Paz is a transfer target for Manchester United following Mauricio Pochettino’s appointment as manager.

The Sun reports that Paz, who has sent a timely reminder of his talent after scoring two stunning goals for Argentina in their 7-0 victory over Costa Rica, is on United’s radar ahead of the January window.

Real Madrid retain an €80m option to re-sign Paz in 2027, while Arsenal, Chelsea and Paris Saint-Germain are also believed to be monitoring the Argentine.

Born in Tenerife, he began his career at Real Madrid before moving to Como in 2024.

Manchester United are set to back Mauricio Pochettino the January transfer window.

Pochettino, who arrived at Carrington to begin work on Wednesday, will be tasked with guiding United away from the bottom three and as high up the top-flight table as he can, while also seeing how far he can get his new side in the Champions League.

Mauricio Pochettino has listed the three things he is hoping to bring to Manchester United in his first message to fans.

The Argentine arrived at his new club on Wednesday, touching down in Manchester immediately after overseeing his final game in charge of the United States.

“I have heard so many good things about you. I hope we bring all the happiness, working together and connect[ion], so that it is going to be a special season, and to make our players fight on the field and get the results we deserve.”
