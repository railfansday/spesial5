It’s agony for Worthing at Scunthorpe as 93rd minute goal denies them another away win

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Jordan Young gave the away side a half-time advantage with a well-taken effort on 17 minutes, but the Rebels backline could not quitewithstand the Iron’s second half resurgence and Eastbourne-raised Norwood came up with the late leveller for new boss Andy Preece.

It was gutting for Adam Hinshelwood and his players and the manager was shown a red card at full-time. But the Rebels remain in the play-off one, in seventh place.

Returning after an 11-day break from action, the side featured two changes to the eleven that began the 3-0 defeat by Sutton United in their last league outing.

Harrison Foulkes regained his place in goal while Charlie Waller returned to the defensive unit. New signing Sam Clucas featured on the bench, with Daniel Isichei also named amongst the substitutes.

A sense of optimism among the home faithful in Preece’s first game at the helm was combatted by a strong start from the away side, spearheaded by the dynamic movement of Young to initiate some promising moves early on.

Having asserted themselves on the ball through the opening quarter of an hour, the Rebels found the breakthrough in the 17th minute. Brilliant link-up between Kendall and Young saw the latter pick up the ball in a central position just outside the box. The former Torquay United man let off a fierce low effort on his left side which clipped off the foot of Maison Campbell’s left post before nestling inside the opposite upright.

The movement of Razzaq Coleman De-Graft and Young to pick up space behind the Iron midfield gave the away side a constant outlet on the break, from which they were able to fashion several dangerous breakaways.

Scunthorpe came close to drawing level with three minutes of normal time remaining at the end of the first half. An in-swinging corner delivery was swept onto the crossbar by Kyle Hurst, before Norwood saw two follow-up attempts blocked from just outside the six-yard box.

The Rebels’ counter-attacking prowess remained their best route towards goal heading into the break, with Kendall having a good chance to double the visitors’ lead 90 seconds before the interval.

After jinking away from one challenge at the edge of the box, the forward drilled a low effort narrowly wide of the mark down to Campbell’s left-hand side.

Having grown into the game towards the end of the first 45 minutes, Preece’s hosts started the second half with renewed intensity, keeping play largely in the Worthing half as Adam Hinshelwood’s side were forced to defend their box.

Alfie Beestin had two attempts on goal, first being denied by Foulkes from a long throw before having a strike blocked from around 20 yards.

The attacking movement and nuisance of Norwood lent itself to Scunthorpe’s direct approach to getting back level, with the former Oldham striker constantly winning first balls.

Just after the hour mark, Pat Jones’ delivery from the right flank was stabbed over the bar by Norwood at the edge of the six-yard box, as tight defending from Joe Cook and Jayden Moore prevented the veteran from getting a more telling touch.

Pressure continued to mount on Worthing with minutes ticking away. With two minutes of the 90 to play, the Attis Arena was plunged into momentary silence as substitute Declan Howe’s header deflected agonisingly wide.

With 30 seconds remaining of three additional minutes, all the resolute defending from the visitors came undone as Scunthorpe found a leveller. After the away side surrendered possession inside their own half, Howe’s clipped ball towards the back post was headed back across goal and eventually poked home by Norwood.

It was a gutting conclusion from a Rebels perspective, especially for Foulkes, who commanded his box astutely through numerous waves of lofted deliveries into the area.
