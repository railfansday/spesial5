Crawley Town need reinforcements up front – and hunt is well under way

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

As it stands, the Reds have two out-and-out strikers, Danilo Orsi and Pemi Aderoju, while Dylan Morgan, Ade Adeyemo and Stephen Duke-McKenna could all play in that role if needed.

Speaking after the Reds’ battling 0-0 draw with 2nd-placed Barnet on Tuesday night, which extended Blackwell’s unbeaten league record since he arrived, he said “We tried to go with two strikers, but I’m not sure that game after game after game suits a couple of them.

"I looked at the bench and there was nothing I could do. I tried to bring Osman (Kamara) to play up top and he naturally drifted out wide left and I didn’t want that.

“I wanted him to play as a two and he’s got to learn a lesson that when he goes on and he’s asked to do a job, do your job – and that’s what frustrates me a little bit.

"We didn’t have an actual sort of midfielder to come in and play. I think Dukey (Duke-McKenna) started to drift a little bit out to the right and I wanted him in alongside Kai, but once again, Dukey came in and did a job for me, pressed as hard as he could.

“So, I can’t complain. It was a situation that if we’d said before the game that we’d remain unbeaten, you’d have been happy with that. So, we’re unbeaten and I’m happy with that.”

Since Blackwell took over from Colin Kazim-Richards, Crawley have won two and drawn two, scoring four goals and conceding only two. The Reds are now level on points with Rotherham in 23rd with a game in hand, and only two points behind Rochdale in 22nd with two games in hand.

When asked if he has started looking at bringing anyone in to help out in the Janury transfer eindow, Blackwell said “To be fair, we were here from 12 o’clock today.

"We brought some new scouts in, a new recruitment guy and we had five hours today before the game kicked off looking at potential targets for January, and the club’s well on its way to do that.”
