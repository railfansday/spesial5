West Ham youngster is the real deal according to his loan boss

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Just for a change one of West Ham’s young signings is being lauded rather than slated today – Edvin Austbø came in for a fair degree of stick in a report yesterday claiming he was heading towards ‘flop’ status – but the club’s pursuit and purchase of Dan Cummings from Celtic last summer is being complimented by his new manager.

Cummings went out on loan this month to fifth tier Aldershot Town and came off the bench to score within six minutes of his debut, as we covered yesterday.

As reported this afternoon by insidefutbol.com, The Shots’ manager Scott Davies has been waxing lyrical over Cummings and West Ham’s shrewd purchase of their future star:

“Aldershot Town boss Scott Davies has admitted that he can see why West Ham United invested heavily in Daniel Cummings, following the former Celtic talent’s promising start to life at the National League side:

“To bring on someone like Dan’s quality on with sort of half an hour to go, or 25 minutes to go, and then he gets a goal within five minutes is like a dream world for me as a manager.

“And for us as a management team, that takes the pressure off.

Listen, he’s been great in training and you can see the quality he’s got. No wonder why West Ham bought him for a lot of money. So, we’re really lucky to have him on loan and hopefully we’ll see more of him in weeks to come.”

£600,000 in Aldershot’s world is a lot of money indeed for a teenager: For West Ham a mere drop in the ocean but good to hear that Cummings is making all the right moves.

Hopefully, the start of a stream of good news coming out of Hampshire as the young Scot finds his feet.
