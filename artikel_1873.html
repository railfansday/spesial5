Tchouameni and Courtois follow Guler in signing new Madrid contract

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Aurelien Tchouameni and Thibaut Courtois have both put pen to paper on new deals with Real Madrid.

Tchouameni has committed his long-term future to the LaLiga giants and has signed a six-year deal, just days after Arda Guler agreed fresh terms with the club.

On Friday, it was also announced that Courtois had agreed to extend his stay in the Spanish capital for two more years.

The news comes ahead of Madrid's LaLiga clash with Celta Vigo on Sunday, with Jose Mourinho's side aiming to keep pace with rivals Barcelona at the top of the table.

Following his arrival from Monaco in 2022 for a reported €80m transfer fee, Tchouameni has become a mainstay in the Madrid midfield.

He has made 205 appearances across all competitions, scoring seven goals as well as providing nine assists.

During that time, Tchouameni has lifted eight major honours, including a LaLiga title and the Champions League back in 2023-24.

Tchouameni was of reported Premier League interest this summer, with Manchester United linked with a move for the France international.

"Real Madrid C. F. and Aurelien Tchouameni have agreed to extend the contract of our player, who will remain at the club until June 30, 2032," the club announced.

"Aurelien Tchouameni joined Real Madrid in 2022. In his five seasons wearing our shirt, he has made 205 appearances and won eight titles: one Champions League, two Club World Cups, two UEFA Super Cups, one LaLiga title, one Copa del Rey and one Spanish Super Cup.

"He is a French international, having made 50 appearances for his country to date and won the UEFA Nations League in 2021."

— Real Madrid C.F. (@realmadriden) November 19, 2026

Courtois, meanwhile, has established himself as one of the best goalkeepers in the world since joining Madrid from Chelsea back in 2018.

Having also played for city rivals Atletico Madrid, Courtois has gone on to appear 348 times for Los Blancos, winning 12 major honours which include three LaLiga crowns.

Since making his Madrid debut, no goalkeeper across Europe's top five leagues has made more saves than Courtois' 998. He also ranks fourth for clean sheets (133), behind only Alisson Becker (141), Ederson (143) and Jan Oblak (149).

"Real Madrid C.F. and Thibaut Courtois have agreed to extend our player's contract, which ties him to the club until June 30, 2028," a clubs statement read.

"Thibaut Courtois joined Real Madrid in 2018. In his nine seasons wearing our jersey, he has played 348 matches and won 14 titles: 2 Champions League titles, 3 Club World Cups, 2 UEFA Super Cups, 3 La Liga titles, 1 Copa del Rey, and 3 Spanish Super Cups.

"His contribution to all these titles has been marked by decisive performances that have established him as the best goalkeeper in the world. He is an international with Belgium, having played 115 matches for his national team so far."
