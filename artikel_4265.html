Best GTA 6 Settings You Should Change Right Away

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here after years of waiting and has somehow managed to surpass the ridiculous expectations set up by its predecessors and advertising. While sales and reviews are both at an all-time high, making GTA 6 officially one of the best games ever released, one aspect fans are still struggling with is the game's performance on consoles.

While the PlayStation Pro fares a bit better than the competitors, one of the most common complaints about the monolithic GTA 6 experience comes from a few visual and accessibility choices that can thankfully be fixed with a few simple adjustments.

Although fans are still stuck with a 30fps cap until the PC version releases, players like kidxvintage on X have been urging others to rush to their settings menu and change a few simple things, which has been described as making for a "night-and-day experience."

For the settings you'll want to adjust, the most common recommendations are turning off motion blur to reduce the smear when moving your camera, in addition to disabling camera shake and camera bob entirely for a smoother cinematic experience, as well as setting the steering dead zone to 7 on your analog sticks for more control when driving.

As we all guessed, Rockstar and PlayStation have been working together to make GTA 6 on PS5 the "best experience".

While the recommendations have received plenty of support, other players have responded with their own optimal settings like lowering the dead zones and turning up aiming sensitivity, while noting that leaving motion blur on helps cover up GTA 6's stuttering when the frame rate drops below 30 during action sequences.
