Install Capital One Shopping if you shop online often and don’t mind a tool that sees the products you view and buy. It costs nothing, tests coupon codes at checkout, and flags lower prices from other sellers. Its rewards, such as 2% back on eligible Walmart purchases, come only as gift cards.

Capital One runs it, but anyone 18 or older in the 50 states and D.C. can use it without a Capital One card or account. It comes as an extension for Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari and as an app for iPhone and Android.

Frequent online shoppers who accept that data collection get code tests and price checks at no cost, plus gift card rewards at partner stores. Shoppers who want cash, or don’t want an extension watching their shopping, do better starting purchases on Rakuten’s website, which can pay by check or PayPal every three months.
