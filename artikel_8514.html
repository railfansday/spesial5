Before you buy a product to fix a small household problem, figure out the one job it does, then check your kitchen, closet, and junk drawer for something you already own that does it. Fruit flies, scuffed walls, and tangled cords rarely need a purchase, and skipping one saves money and drawer space.

That’s because most single-purpose products do one simple job.

A fruit fly trap lures flies in and keeps them there, a non-slip hanger adds grip, and a cord clip holds a cable within reach. Vinegar, rubber bands, and binder clips do those same jobs, and you probably own all three.

These 15 swaps run from the kitchen to the front door. They’re quick fixes for single problems, not recipes for homemade cleaners.

The fruit fly trap, the non-slip hangers, and the cord clips can stay on the store shelf. Buy a product only for a job nothing in your house already does.
