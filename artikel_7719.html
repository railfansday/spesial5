Winter storms are the most common reason for a long power outage, and Black Friday is a good time to get a generator in the garage before one hits. The Home Depot, Lowe’s, Walmart, Amazon, Ace Hardware, and Tractor Supply have cut prices on inverter, dual-fuel, and home backup generators, plus battery power stations, by up to 52%. For camping, tailgating, and running a few essentials, the PowerSmart 2500-Watt Portable Inverter Generator for $259.99 (was $489.99) at Walmart is 47% off. This dual-fuel home backup generator runs on gasoline or propane and puts out up to 13,000 starting watts, enough to back up major household circuits through a transfer switch. Electric start means no pull cord in the cold. It’s $1,299 at both Amazon and Tractor Supply.

This portable inverter generator puts out clean power that’s safe for phones, laptops, and other electronics, and it’s light enough to carry to a campsite or tailgate. At 47% off, it’s one of the deepest discounts on a gas generator this Black Friday.

This dual-fuel portable generator runs on gasoline or propane, so you can use whichever fuel is easier to stock before a storm. With 4,550 starting watts, it can keep a refrigerator, sump pump, and lights running during an outage.

Inverter generators run quieter and deliver cleaner power for electronics, while conventional portable generators offer more watts per dollar. Dual- and tri-fuel models add propane or natural gas as a backup fuel. Battery power stations run silently indoors with no fumes, but they store less energy than a tank of gas. Never run a gas generator indoors or in a garage.
