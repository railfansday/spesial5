French far-left candidate Mélenchon demands ‘thorough overhaul’ of Germany ties

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Jean-Luc Mélenchon, leader of the hard-left France Unbowed (LFI) party, on Friday declared he was "hostile" to German economic liberalism and its dominance of the European Union. The septuagenarian presidential candidate in the 2027 race also warned that France could be drawn into unwanted conflict as Germany's growing military strength makes it a "target" for other countries.
