Europe Haggles Over Who Will Represent It in Talks With Russia

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

After coveting a seat in the Ukraine war discussions, Europe is poised to get one. Deciding who should speak for the continent is its own negotiation.

Reporting from Dublin, where European leaders are meeting, and from an airfield near the city where Americans and Ukrainians debriefed on Thursday night.

European leaders have waited on the margins of negotiations to end Russia’s war in Ukraine for months, eyeing a seat at the table. Now they finally expect to get one, and they don’t know who should sit in it.

“If Europe could identify one person to speak on their behalf, that would be a bigger achievement than getting the Russians to include them in the negotiation,” Jared Kushner, President Trump’s son-in-law and a U.S. envoy, said on Thursday.

Mr. Kushner’s dig reflected a harsh — and, for Europe, potentially embarrassing — reality that internal divisions could cost European officials time and influence. Mr. Kushner and his fellow U.S. negotiator, Steve Witkoff, pushed this week to add Europe to the talks after Russia insisted during discussions in the United Arab Emirates that postwar security in Europe be a topic of negotiation.

Russian negotiators made it clear during the talks with Ukraine and the United States that as a condition of ending the war, they want Europe to change how it protects itself. Russia has a record of pushing to pare back NATO operations near its border.

European officials have rejected those suggestions, insisting that the continent, not the Kremlin, decides its security.
