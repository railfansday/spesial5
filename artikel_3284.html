Never Mind the PR Stunts, Here’s the Sex Pistols Claiming to Part Ways With Frank Carter

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Does Frank Carter have Rotten luck? For the past year and a half, the 42-year-old British punk singer whose credits include Gallows and his solo work fronting the Rattle Snakes, has served as the singer for the Sex Pistols. Most recently, the band performed a tour celebrating the 50th anniversary of its only proper album, Never Mind the Bollocks. But it seems the bollocks have come swinging back like a pendulum for Carter since the Pistols have announced there’s no future for Carter after the next run of U.K. shows.

“It’s been a glorious ride with Frankie baby, we’ve loved every minute,” guitarist Steve Jones told Kerrang! “The shows were spectacular and of course, a big shout out to all the fans. Without you lot, it’s nothing. This is not the end of the Pistols… but let’s see what happens.”

Carter’s statement also expresses several emotions that are easy to never mind with the Sex Pistols: gratitude, optimism, and hope. “As a young kid growing up on Sex Pistols, the scale of the opportunity to step into one of the most iconic roles in music history was not lost on me,” he said. “I never thought for a second that three shows to help save Bush Hall would turn into all this. To Steve, Paul [Cook, drums] and Glen [Matlock, bass], thank you for trusting me with such an important task. And thanks to the crew and every fan who’s been smiling, dancing and screaming the words back at me all over the world; it has been a huge honour.

“All good things must come to an end, and I’m really looking forward to celebrating my run with the Pistols one last time around our Bloody Kingdom,” he continued. “As always, I’ll see you in the pit.”

Carter’s final Anarchy in the U.K. tour (which includes Ireland) with the Pistols will begin on Dec. 4 and end on Dec. 20.

The band’s reunion with Carter has been fraught with vicious acrimony since it was the result of Jones, Matlock, and Cook aligning against original singer Johnny Rotten (a.k.a. John Lydon). Lydon, who has spent the past year touring with his post-Pistols band Public Image Ltd., expressed his displeasure with his ex-bandmates earlier this year in a Rolling Stone interview. He accused the other Pistols of colluding against him at a time when he was taking care of his dying wife and threatening him with an expensive lawsuit. When asked on a scale of zero to 100 the odds that he’d ever consider singing for the Pistols again, he replied the figure was in the “minus infinities.” “The dirt that they unloaded on me in a time where I was in real serious grief … No, I cannot forget about that,” he said.

Whether or not the Pistols really are done with Carter will be revealed with time since this is a band who loves a good publicity stunt.
