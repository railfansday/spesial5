Tom Jackson Dies: Broncos Alum & ESPN Hall Of Fame Broadcaster Was 75

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Tom Jackson, a former Denver Broncos player and an NFL analyst for ESPN, has died. He was 75.

Jackson’s family told ESPN that he passed on Friday, in what his longtime co-host Chris Berman called an “unexpected” death, having previously spoken “at length” with Jackson on Tuesday.

“He was a vital 75,” said Berman on ESPN‘s Sunday NFL Countdown. “Tom was poignant. He was active. This is a shock.”

Berman added in a statement released by ESPN, “All of us are stunned. I’m stunned, we’re stunned and you’re stunned. We’ve lost one of the best human beings ever to walk the planet, Tom Jackson. Watch on Deadline

“I was fortunate to work side by side with Tom for 30 years, primarily on NFL Primetime and Sunday NFL Countdown,” he added. “It was a labor of love for our whole team, both in front of the camera and behind it. We always hoped our viewers enjoyed the shows as much as we did bringing it to them every week.”

In honor of the Broncos “Orange Crush Defense” linebacker, the entire Countdown crew wore orange ties during Sunday’s show.

Born April 4, 1951 in Cleveland, Ohio, Thomas Louie Jackson was recruited by University of Louisville head coach Lee Corso, playing college football for three seasons from 1970-72, during which he was a two-time Missouri Valley Conference player of the year.

During the 1973 NFL Draft, Jackson was selected 88th overall by the Broncos, where he played a 14-year career as linebacker, helping lead the team to play Super Bowl XII and Super Bowl XXI.

Jackson went on to work for ESPN Studios in 1987, paired with Berman on Sunday NFL Countdown and NFL Primetime. His years of broadcasting experience as an NFL analyst earned him the 2015 Pete Rozelle Radio-Television Award from the Pro Football Hall of Fame.
