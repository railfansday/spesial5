D’Angelo’s ‘GTA VI’ Song ‘Kamikaze’ Released as Brother Teases Future Posthumous Songs

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

D’Angelo’s contribution to the Grand Theft Auto VI soundtrack, “Kamikaze,” has officially left Vice City and landed on streaming platforms.

“Kamikaze” was not mentioned in any of the press surrounding the GTA VI soundtrack in the lead-up to the game’s release. And it wasn’t until people actually started playing the game that the song was heard on one of the many in-game radio stations, Worldwide FM. (Another D’Angelo classic, “Lady,” also plays on another station, Honey FM.)

The song was officially released to streaming platforms Monday (Nov. 23), with a statement from D’Angelo’s brother, Luther Archer, Jr., accompanying its arrival. In it, Archer shared additional information about “Kamikaze,” including its full credits, and offered new details about future posthumous D’Angelo releases.

According to Archer, D’Angelo approved “Kamikaze” “in its current form” for release in GTA VI in November 2024. D’Angelo, Archer continued, “was a tremendous admirer of Rockstar Games and a past collaborator of theirs, and he was enthusiastic (to say the least) to have this record included in the game and released to a wide audience as a single.” (In 2018, D’Angelo contributed a new song, “Unshaken” to Rockstar’s Red Dead Redemption 2.)

D’Angelo produced “Kamikaze” himself, working with his collaborator Slingbaum (who shared Archer’s statement on Instagram Stories) and engineer Ben Kane. D’Angelo and Slingbaum played most of the instruments on the song, alongside drummers Yussef Dayes and James Gadson.

“Kamikaze” is the first D’Angelo song released since the musician’s death in 2025 after a battle with pancreatic cancer. In his statement, Archer said his brother had entrusted him with the “incredibly daunting task” of “overseeing the release of his posthumous works.” He continued, “It is a mission that must be taken on with the exacting standards and integrity that our beloved would’ve insisted upon. He was a singular genius and we are profoundly blessed to have shared our time with him — his undeniable gift from God was powerful beyond any words I can find.”
