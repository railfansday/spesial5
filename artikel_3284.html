Here’s how Webex adapts meetings and multitasking for iPhone Duo

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Webex has supported iPhone Duo since launch, and now Cisco is detailing how its collaboration app takes advantage of Apple’s first foldable iPhone.

As Cisco notes, the company treats Apple platforms as priorities to support from the start:

At Cisco, we have a long history of partnering with Apple to bring thoughtfully designed collaboration experiences to the devices people use every day. From iPad, CarPlay, Apple TV, and Apple Watch to spatial collaboration on Apple Vision Pro, each platform has challenged us to rethink how to help people connect and get work done seamlessly.

iPhone Duo is no different. Apple has been promoting Webex as a unique iPhone Duo app, and today Cisco is detailing the work that went into the app, which is already on the App Store for customers.

Webex is built natively for iPhone Duo and adapts to both displays and every position. Open the device to see more meeting participants, keep shared content in view, or work together on a whiteboard. Prefer to multitask? Use Webex alongside another app with Split View.
