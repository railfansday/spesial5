Raúl Esparza Still On Concussion-Caused Leave From Broadway’s ‘Galileo’; Michael Park Stepping In

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

UPDATE: Raúl Esparza extended his two-day leave of absence from Broadway‘s Galileo to include two performances today (Wednesday, November 18). In his place, Michael Park will take the title role at both today’s matinee performance and tonight’s evening performance.

The one-day extension was announced on the production’s Instagram Story page earlier today.

Michael Park (Stranger Things, As the World Turns), the production’s alternate Galileo, filled in for Esparza last night and will do so again tonight. Esparza is expected back tomorrow. Watch on Deadline

In an Instagram video posted yesterday evening, Esparza says, “Unfortunately, I’m not going to be able to join you for performances of Galileo tonight and tomorrow. The Saturday before we started performances, I suffered a minor concussion in rehearsals, and I still wanted to go on and still wanted to do the show, and it was an amazing week, and I wouldn’t have missed it for the world.

“But now,” he continues, “out of an abundance of caution and per doctor’s orders, because I’m stubborn and I have to listen to them, I’m going to have to take a little bit of time off to to get better. Everything’s going to be cool. I cannot wait to join you all and join the cast of this amazing musical. It’s just been one of the most extraordinary weeks of my entire life, getting to share this with audiences, and I can’t wait to have so many more. And I’ll see you all real soon.”

Galileo began performances at the Shubert Theatre on November 10 (the rehearsal during which Esparza suffered the concussion was November 7). During the show’s first week of previews, the musical was a near sell-out, grossing $710,252 for five performances.

Park’s previous Broadway credits include, among others, Carousel, Smokey Joe’s Cafe, Little Me, How To Succeed In Business Without Really Trying, Cat On A Hot Tin Roof, Tuck Everlasting, Dear Evan Hansen and, most recently, 2025’s Redwood.

The show’s synopsis: The synopsis: “When luminary Galileo (Esparza) peers into the heavens, what he sees challenges humanity’s understanding of the world. Unwilling to deny the truth, Galileo finds himself in a fight with the most powerful institution on Earth. As his trusted confidant Barberini (Kushnier) ascends to power, friendship leads to betrayal, and the dreams Galileo has envisioned for himself and for his brilliant daughter Virginia (Woods) begin to slip away. Belief, ambition, and love pull the trio out of orbit, each confronting what they’re willing to sacrifice for what matters most.”

In addition to Esparza and Park, the musical features Jeremy Kushnier, Joy Woods, Bradley Dean, Jordon Lee Garcia and Javier Muñoz. Opening night is Sunday, December 6.

Galileo features a book by Danny Strong and an original score by Zoe Sarnak and Michael Weiner, and is directed by Michael Mayer and choreographed by David Neumann. Producers are Amanda Lipitz, Henry Tisch and Jordan Roth.
