Apple TV reveals new series with Andrew Garfield, Jude Law as magicians

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Jude Law and Andrew Garfield star as Siegfried & Roy, the famous magicians, in Apple TV’s forthcoming series Wild Things. Here’s the release date and full details.

The year is almost over, and Apple TV has started revealing more of its schedule for 2027.

In January alone, Apple has several big premieres coming:

Another noteworthy series coming is Prodigies, a rom-com with The White Lotus and The Bear stars that arrives in February.

Now today, Apple has announced another new show premiering in early 2027: Wild Things.

Wild Things is coming Friday, April 16, and the first teaser was just shared on X:

Inspired by the wild true story. Jude Law and Andrew Garfield are Siegfried & Roy.#WildThings premieres April 16 on Apple TV. pic.twitter.com/BJUPuMlhzR

Jude Law and Andrew Garfield play the famous magician duo Siegfried & Roy in the limited series, which is created by John Hoffman, creator and showrunner of Only Murders in the Building. Here’s the official summary:

“Wild Things” tells the wild ride relationship tale of two of the greatest showmen-magicians in history who, along with their white tigers, are tasked with turning Sin City into a family-friendly destination. The duo push the concept of illusion versus reality to the extreme, both personally and professionally, until tragedy reframes their story and opens a mystery surrounding their last fateful Las Vegas show.

The show is based on the Apple Original Podcast Wild Things: Siegfried & Roy, all eight episodes of which are available now.
