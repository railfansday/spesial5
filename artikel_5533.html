A Democratic senator wants Congress to assess if Nvidia is 'too big to fail'

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Sen. Jack Reed wants analysis of how a possible Nvidia failure would hit U.S. banks and credit markets after CEO Jensen Huang resigned

Samuel Boivin / NurPhoto via Getty Images

Sen. Jack Reed, a Rhode Island Democrat and the top Democrat on the Senate Armed Services Committee, is asking Congress to examine whether Nvidia $NVDA counts as “too big to fail” and what a collapse at the chipmaker could mean for U.S. banks and credit markets, according to Reuters.

Reed said on Thursday that Nvidia “has backstopped the AI ecosystem through complex financing transactions.” He called for more disclosure about those deals.

"We need full transparency into these deals, an assessment of whether the company is 'too big to fail,' and an analysis of how these events may affect the U.S. banking system and credit markets," Reed said.

"Millions of Americans also have economic exposure to Nvidia through their retirement accounts and pension funds," Reed said.

AI-linked stocks traded lower around the world on Thursday after Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang left the company.

Huang resigned as CEO and as a board member on Thursday, the company said in a statement. Nvidia stock was down about 10% after the opening bell, and it had fallen by more than a fifth over the past month.

Colette Kress, the chief financial officer, will serve as interim CEO while the board searches for a successor. Harvey C. Jones, a former Synopsys $SNPS leader, becomes Nvidia's first board chair and will direct the search, which will consider internal and external candidates, the company said.

Huang's departure followed reports of two extramarital affairs. He admitted to infidelity in a note to employees on Sunday.

Nvidia’s revenue for fiscal 2026 reached $215.9 billion, up 702% from $26.9 billion in fiscal 2022. The company is set to report fiscal third-quarter earnings on Tuesday, Nov. 17.
