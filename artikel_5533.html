WASHINGTON — The Senate health panel on Wednesday rejected a top health official nominated to lead the National Science Foundation, with Chair Bill Cassidy (R-La.) expressing doubts about his willingness to stand up for scientific integrity.

Jim O’Neill, who held high-ranking posts at the Health and Human Services Department and Centers for Disease Control and Prevention under health secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr., would have led the NSF for six years if confirmed. Cassidy canceled the vote to confirm him, saying he didn’t have the support to advance.

O’Neill “did not do enough to stand up for science and against irresponsible decisions and statements” from Kennedy in those roles, Cassidy said at a meeting of his committee.
