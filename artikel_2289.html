Blackburn Rovers vs West Ham predictions: Scott Parker backed for first win in ‘entertaining contest’

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Blackburn Rovers head into Saturday’s clash with West Ham sitting 14th in the Championship table. Tony Mowbray’s side have endured an inconsistent start to the campaign, collecting 18 points from 14 games, with five wins, three draws and six defeats leaving them just outside the Championship play-off picture.

Rovers have shown signs of their attacking potential, though, beating Wrexham 2-0 at Ewood Park two weeks ago. Andri Gudjohnsen scored both goals in that victory, taking his league tally to nine and accounting for almost half of Blackburn’s goals this season.

West Ham arrive currently 8th in the Championship with 22 points and have been among the more prolific sides in the league, averaging more than 2.5 goals per game. Scott Parker, appointed following Nuno Espirito Santo’s departure, will be looking for a response after a disappointing result last time out.

WHUFC went three goals down against Cardiff City before eventually rescuing a 3-3 draw. Divine Mukasa, Arne Engels and Max Kilman were all on target in South Wales, but Parker will be concerned by his side’s defensive shortcomings as they prepare for another difficult away assignment.

“Blackburn Rovers have shown enough attacking quality to suggest they can cause West Ham problems, particularly with Gudjohnsen enjoying an excellent run in front of goal. The Icelandic striker has become an important figure for Mowbray, while Todd Cantwell’s creativity gives the hosts another route to hurting their opponents.

“However, West Ham possess considerably more individual quality and should have enough firepower to trouble Rovers’ defence. Jarrod Bowen has been particularly influential, registering seven goals and seven assists in 14 league appearances.

“The visitors will need to improve defensively after their collapse against Cardiff, but their attacking options should ultimately make the difference. Blackburn can contribute to an entertaining contest at Ewood Park, although I expect Parker’s side to come away with all three points.”

“Blackburn Rovers have had some tough games of late. They were narrowly beaten by a great West Brom side, thrashed by Swansea City, and then hard done by to lose to Stoke City after a very soft disallowed goal late on.

“I think Rovers are going to see form come and go this season, largely because their squad isn’t very deep and relies on the form and availability of key figures like Andri Gudjohnsen, Todd Cantwell and Sam Morsy. Despite this, they’re dangerous with their front-footed play under Tony Mowbray and have had some good results at home.

“West Ham will be one to keep an eye on this weekend though. They will have had a couple of days to work under Scott Parker on the training ground after that great comeback draw at Cardiff City.

“We’ll get a better idea of what to expect from WHUFC as they settle into Parker’s management. I do think they have the talent to win the midfield battle though, and with quality players like Jarrod Bowen, I can see them creating the chances to win this one.”
