Köln president makes feelings clear on potential winter exit for El Mala

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Although Said El Mala signed a long-term contract extension with 1. FC Köln until 2031 over the summer, transfer speculation has continued to surround the 20-year-old.

There have even been suggestions that El Mala could leave the Billy Goats as early as the upcoming winter transfer window, but club president Jörg Stobbe considers that scenario highly unlikely.

“I can't imagine him leaving in the winter at all. It would be absurd to let him go in January,” Stobbe underlined in an interview with Bild.

Stobbe is not only optimistic about El Mala staying beyond the winter transfer window, but hopes the young winger will remain at Köln for several more years.

“I would say it would be good if Said played for us for at least another two years. Why not?” Stobbe asked rhetorically.

“I believe the longer he stays with us, the better it will be for him, his family, his brother Malek and 1. FC Köln,” he added.

According to reports, Köln have now raised their asking price to more than €50 million for the highly-rated German international, who continues to attract interest from several top clubs.
