If You’re Trying to Dismantle the Smithsonian As We Know It, Maybe Don’t Open the Plan on Your Laptop in Public

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

In Washington, the mic is always hot, the camera is always on, and the person next to you on the subway has ProPublica’s phone number.

Sometimes you luck into a story. Sometimes you’re in the right place at the right time. Sometimes you’re the person in the newsroom who happens to pick up the phone, or the person standing on the corner when the alarm in the bank goes off. Sometimes you’re Abraham Zapruder. And sometimes you’re riding the Metro in D.C. and somebody opens their laptop next to you and reveals something amazing and/or utterly dreadful.

It is no secret that this administration has had the Smithsonian’s museums in its chicken-greased-fingerprinted sights. It has determined that it was designated by the Almighty to save White People’s History from barbarians of actual historical truth. But how they planned to do it was on the down-low, until an administration apparatchik opened his laptop so that the person sitting next to him could read the planned atrocities, take screenshots of them, and send the screenshots to our friends at ProPublica.

In which we will be transported back to those glorious days for fourth-grade history class.

So who was this indiscreet fellow anyway? In Washington, Sparky, the mic is always hot, the camera is always on, and the person next to you on the subway has ProPublica’s phone number.
