Jennifer Lawrence Breaks Her Silence on Viral Connor Wood Smoothie Comment—and Corrects a Specific Detail

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Might need to slightly edit my new favorite vocal stim.

“I saw you on 8th Avenue drinking a smoothie, and you paid me no mind.” Jennifer Lawrence typed these words on her keyboard, and they have lived rent-free in Connor Wood’s mind ever since. The viral moment even landed the Dancing With the Stars finalist a Smoothie King collab and had him pulling up to rehearsals in this iconic tee:

But the internet’s new favorite vocal stim might need a slight edit since J. Law herself publicly addressed it for the first time—and made a very specific correction.

During a joint appearance with her Hunger Games co-star, Josh Hutcherson, on Watch What Happens Live!, Jen broke her silence on the hilarious domino effect of her initial comment when a fan asked if she’s seen all the memes and reactions online.

“I don’t know how to feel about it. I did not expect that to go viral. I don’t...I don’t really get it,” she admitted, before Josh jokingly chimed in, “I don’t know what any of those words mean.”

She then clarified that she had accidentally “swapped” the location where she actually spotted Connor.

“Yeah, it was also Greenwich,” she explained, later adding, “It was across from my old apartment.”

This entire saga kicked off when Connor admitted that Jen was his celebrity crush in a BuzzFeed Celeb interview earlier this month, saying, “I just want just a little acknowledgment from Jennifer Lawrence that I’m funny, and then that’s all I need.”

The Oscar winner then dropped her now-infamous comment under a clip of the interview, and the rest was history!

I don’t know about you, but “I saw you on Greenwich Avenue drinking a smoothie, and you paid me no mind” just doesn’t hit the same. But for the sake of being biblically accurate, I’ll lock in and remember that!
