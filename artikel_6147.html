Delta Outage Delays Hundreds of Flights

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A problem with one of Delta’s weight-and-balance systems delayed hundreds of flights Sunday, with backups most acute at the nation’s busiest airport.

According to FlightAware, Delta delayed over 2,100 flights Sunday and canceled 274.

Atlanta Hartsfield-Jackson International Airport was the hardest hit, with about 700 flights delayed, though not all were Delta connections.

Delta said in a statement that its employees were using manual workarounds for baggage processing. It also said it would rebook customers on the next available flight and provide meals and hotel rooms where needed.

The FAA said on X that the problem was specific to Delta and had nothing to do with the agency’s air traffic control system, which crashed on Nov. 13.

Delta is working to resolve issues with the airline’s weight and balance automation system. These delays are not related to any FAA air traffic systems. Please check with Delta for the latest information. https://t.co/q4Fc59VMYg

The outage was resolved Sunday afternoon.

As of Monday morning, Delta’s delay rate was down to 6%.

While resolved fairly quickly, the system failure could hardly have come at a worse time. Millions of Americans are flying this week ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday, and there are concerns that technology outages could again snarl air traffic during the busiest time of the year. The single busiest day for air travel is expected to be Sunday, Nov. 29, after Thanksgiving.
