British Airways Unveils 7 New International Routes: See Full List Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

British Airways has disclosed five new or returning routes. Not only are they from London Heathrow and London City, but also from Edinburgh and Glasgow. BA mainline and BA CityFlyer will operate them.

Other routes have already been revealed for 2027. BA will relaunch flights from Heathrow to Melbourne, this time via Kuala Lumpur rather than Singapore, while beginning service from London Gatwick to Kilimanjaro and Zanzibar.

On April 15, BA will take off from Heathrow to Bergen. Not only does Bergen have the second most populous metro population in Norway, but it is also a significant tourist destination for the highly beautiful fjords and cruises. BA will fly five times weekly, mainly on the Airbus A319. The lack of consistency in the following schedule is, of course, primarily due to LHR's renowned slot shortage.

This is not a new route for BA. OAG shows that the oneworld member previously flew from Heathrow to Bergen between 2012 and 2017, with up to two daily flights. BA had inherited the short route from bmi, which had served it from early 2011. BA will be one of only two operators from London to the Scandinavian city next year, joining Norwegian to Gatwick.

Then there's the Greek tourist destination of Skiathos, which is reasonably well-known for the very low final approach over a road. It is the St. Maarten of Europe. Covering 1,245 nautical miles (2,306 km), BA will operate three times weekly using the A320neo from May 20. It'll replace London City-Skiathos, which BA CityFlyer has served since 2017.

Significantly, it'll be the first time that the UK's busiest airport has had flights to Skiathos. While subject to change and reflecting the extent of higher-end premium leisure travel, Heathrow will now have nonstop flights to 12 destinations in Greece. Only Gatwick and Stansted will have a more extensive network (16 destinations).

All the following additions involve BA CityFlyer, whose fleet consists entirely of the 106-seat Embraer E190 due to its London City operations. On May 1, the regional jet unit will take off from London City to Perugia, with a weekly operation.

It'll replace Heathrow-Perugia flights, which have operated since 2022. This will be the first time the Italian destination has been served from LCY. The 'downtown' London airport will now have flights to four cities in Italy.

On May 15, BA CityFlyer will introduce a weekly service from Edinburgh to Split and Glasgow to Nice. Using aircraft that would otherwise be grounded for half the weekend due to London City's closure, the BA unit is used to flying from airports across the UK on weekends, especially during the summer. However, it appears that summer activity from Stansted has been axed next year.

Reflecting what is known as of November 11, BA will introduce the following routes in 2027. Flights from Heathrow to Melbourne will resume after a 20-year absence. Significantly, BA will follow the launch of fellow oneworld member Finnair, which debuted in Melbourne at the end of October 2026.

One of the most notable developments is BA's return to Tanzania. The airline last flew there in 2013, when it served Dar es Salaam, which is the largest city but not the capital. Things will be very different in 2027, when BA will fly to tourist-driven Kilimanjaro and Zanzibar. The operating Triple Seven will fly Gatwick-Kilimanjaro-Zanzibar-Gatwick.
