Judge finds Thomas Markle mentally fit to stand trial on child abuse charges

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A judge in Florida has declared Thomas Markle Sr, the father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, to be mentally competent to stand trial for possessing indecent images of children.

The hearing came after an evaluation by a court-appointed psychiatrist conducted on Prince Harry's 82-year-old father-in-law.

Markle, who has denied possessing images of child sex abuse, was ordered to stay in jail pending trial earlier this month after allegedly removing a location monitoring device.

The FBI arrested Markle on 6 November at an elderly care home that specialises in dementia. His arrest came after a search of a storage unit he had allegedly rented in Los Angeles uncovered the abuse material.

Markle appeared in court in Tampa in a wheelchair, and his lawyers did not dispute the court-appointed psychiatrist's finding that he is mentally competent to understand the nature and consequences of the charge against him.

His next court hearing is scheduled for early December, but any trial is expected to take place in California, where the federal charge was filed.

Inside the storage units in Los Angeles the FBI says agents found about 78 items, including film negatives, slides and books that they allege contained child sex abuse material.

Witnesses "positively identified" Markle in two of the images believed to contain child sex abuse material.

In those images, the man pictured "appears to be in the age range of 30s to 40s", which suggests the alleged abuse dates back decades.

Markle has called the allegations "sick lies".

He moved to the Philippines last year after he had a series of strokes, and lived in Cebu until May. He was reportedly learning to walk again in Florida after having his leg amputated.

On Monday, police in the Philippines asked prosecutors to charge Markle with possession of and intent to distribute child sexual abuse material.

Last month, police there obtained a warrant to examine devices allegedly removed from Markle's former home in the country.

Four boxes of material, including computers, phones, hard drives and old video tapes, were handed to officers by Meghan's half brother Thomas Markle Jr.

The duchess has been estranged from her father since 2018, and he did not attend her wedding in London that same year.

The BBC understands she broke contact with him last year, telling him she and the authorities knew he had abused young girls.

In a previously shared statement, Meghan confirmed the existence of the letter and wrote: "I am deeply distressed... my thoughts are with any children who may have been harmed."

She said she hoped it could be understood why she had largely cut off contact from her father and did "not allow him to have contact with our children".
