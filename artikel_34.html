Big EU defence projects under threat from proposed budget cuts: Commissioner Andrius Kubilius

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

We speak to the EU's Commissioner for Defence and Space, Andrius Kubilius. The former prime minister of Lithuania – who grew up during the Soviet era – tells us how he sees the confrontation between Europe and Russia. He also sounds the alarm over proposed cuts in the EU's future budget, saying that they could jeopardise major European projects in defence and space. Kubilius praises the EU's SAFE defence loans programme, remarking that it has been "really very successful".
