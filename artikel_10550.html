Man buys two-bedroom apartment for just $1.70 in China’s Black Friday-style sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

A man in China has purchased an apartment for just $1.70 during a shopping promotion event, and it’s just as absurd as it sounds.

Shopping sales usually mean saving money on a shiny new TV or picking up a discounted game or two, but one lucky man in China took money-saving to an entirely new level by purchasing a two-bedroom apartment for just $1.70.

The property was sold through Chinese bidding platform Alibaba Auction as part of the country’s Double 11 event, an annual shopping festival that’s similar to Black Friday.

Located in Heilongjiang province in northeastern China, the seventh-floor apartment measures 68 square metres (roughly 732 square feet) and has two bedrooms.

According to the South China Morning Post, bidding started at one yuan (around $0.15) and was capped at 11 yuan (around $1.65). While 1,677 bidders took part, it was an anonymous shopper from eastern China’s Jiangsu-Zhejiang region who managed to secure the property.

Despite landing a new home for less than a cup of coffee, the buyer revealed he has no plans to relocate. Instead, he intends to use the two-bedroom property as a place to stay during winter holidays.

While the man may have purchased the apartment for $1.70, he will need to pay around 2,000 yuan (approximately $300) in deed taxes to complete the title transfer. SCMP also cited a warning from Beijing Jinsu Law Firm, which said buyers considering ultra-cheap properties should check for issues such as unclear ownership, unpaid taxes, and outstanding fees.

Either way, securing an apartment for just $1.70 puts it right next to owning an entire Japanese castle for $64,000 (yes, this is a real thing).
