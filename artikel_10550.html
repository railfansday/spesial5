Apple Watch now listens to in-person conversations & lets you rewind them

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Apple is introducing a new AI feature that allows Apple Watch users to rewind in-person conversations and see what someone just said.

The feature, called Live Rewind, is part of Apple’s new Audio Intelligence technology, which began rolling out to developers with the iOS 27.3 beta on November 3.

First announced in September, the technology also lets users generate AI summaries of conversations, helping them remember key details without taking notes.

Live Rewind allows users to double-press their Apple Watch’s digital crown to see a text version of what was said during the previous 15 seconds.

For example, someone could use the feature to remember directions, check what a waiter said, or recall a name they missed during a conversation.

Users can also ask Siri questions about what was said or save the text for later. Otherwise, it automatically disappears after 30 seconds.

Apple is also introducing Siri Recaps, which uses AI to summarize conversations and highlight important details such as dates, deadlines, and other information.

Unlike Live Rewind, Siri Recaps can run automatically throughout the day. Users can choose when it operates, such as during work hours, and turn it off whenever they want.

The summaries can be saved to Apple’s Notes or Journal apps. Any that aren’t saved will automatically disappear within seven days.

The company says Siri Recaps does not save raw audio, identify individual speakers, or create complete transcripts of conversations.

Instead, audio is securely processed using the Apple Watch, a paired iPhone, and Apple’s Private Cloud Compute technology to generate summaries.

Live Rewind also plays a sound and displays an animation when activated to alert nearby people that the feature is being used.

Audio Intelligence includes two other features that can identify songs playing nearby and recognize important sounds such as alarms, sirens, and doorbells.

The technology is limited to the Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4, paired with a compatible iPhone.

It’s currently available through the iOS 27.3 developer beta, with a public beta planned before the features become widely available.
