Records of the Leonids go all the way back to Arab annuals dating from 902 AD. 1833 saw an historic storm from the shower, as frightened residents along the U.S. Eastern Seaboard awoke to a sky awash in meteors. This single event was not only responsible in part for several religious movements of the day in the U.S., but also helped to push the modern scientific understanding of meteors from a purely atmospheric phenomenon to one originating from space.
