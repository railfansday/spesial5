Anne Hathaway Welcomes Third Child With Husband Adam Shulman

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

After a busy 2026, Anne Hathaway has ended the year with a new addition to her family.

The Oscar winner has welcomed her third child with her husband, Adam Shulman, according to photos shared by TMZ on Tuesday. The actress was spotted in public in New York City for the first time since giving birth, with her newborn baby strapped to her in a front-facing carrier.

The Hollywood Reporter has reached out to her rep for more details.

Hathaway revealed her pregnancy in June, showing off her baby bump in a video posted on Instagram. She added in the caption, “x Baby, I’m yours x,” as the song ”Baby I’m Yours” by Barbara Lewis played over the video. The following month, the actress made an appearance on Late Night With Seth Meyers, where she admitted she and Shulman were “shocked” to be expecting again. “It’s amazing. I mean, we knew what we were doing,” Hathaway quipped to host Seth Meyers at the time. “But we were so shocked it worked … we were just so shocked it went this way. So we’re calling this one our buzzer-beater.”

The birth of Hathaway’s third baby makes her and Shulman now a family of five. The pair, who married in 2012, already have two sons, Jonathan, 10, and Jack, 6.

This year has been notably jam-packed for the actress, as she had five films hitting theaters, including Mother Mary, The Devil Wears Prada 2, The Odyssey, The End of Oak Street and Verity. And with all those projects came their respective global press tours.

Once her final film of the year, Verity, was released in October, Hathaway quipped that she planned to “vanish into a plume of smoke” and take some much-needed personal time away from the spotlight to be with her family.

“There’s been more than enough of me for quite some time,” she previously said on CBS Mornings. “And I’ve been really, really happy, but I think it’s time to leave the party.”
