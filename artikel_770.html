Luann de Lesseps Debuts First Look at "To All the Boys" Music Video

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Golden Life's Luann de Lesseps released her first music video in over two years for her new song "To All The Boys." Get an exclusive first look from E! News.

Luann de Lesseps is raising the curtain and lowering the lights to take center stage with her new music video.

The Golden Life star is set to premiere the music video for her first single in two years “To All the Boys” on YouTube at midnight on Nov. 11, but E! News has your exclusive first look.

In the video—directed by Jerry Chia and Phillip Henry and sponsored by MISTR—Countess Luann sends a message to all the “boys and billionaires” reassuring them to “have no fear” as their Countess is here.

And the 61-year-old was feeling extra chic, sitting poolside in a one-piece among all her swimsuit-clad “boys” and shining for the camera, before taking to the center of the pool on a float to send her message.

“To all the boys I've loved before / who traveled in and out my door,” the Real Housewives of New York alum croons. “Those boys who came along / I dedicate this song to all the boys I've loved before.”

Of course, the reality star has always known that one should never count out the Countess to deliver a song of epic proportions—especially one that she was so inspired to sing.

“I’m a hopeless romantic, ‘cause I've been married twice and I love my boys,” Luann told E! News at the premiere of The Golden Life in October. “That's why I sang ‘to all the boys I've loved before.’”

She felt especially privileged to bring the song to life as it was her take on the 1984 Julio Iglesias and Willie Nelson duet “To All the Girls I’ve Loved Before.”

“I am so lucky to be able to sing and recreate that song into a dance song,” she raved, “and make lyrics for it that are mine.”

The new track also served as the inspiration for her upcoming The Love Tour, with Luann exclaiming that she’s now “a global touring artist.”

“That's really how the tour started, which is the song came to me. I said, ‘You know what, I have to make it my own,’” she recalled, “and I did.”

“It’s a great song. It's an epic iconic song that I think everybody knows,” she added. “To recreate that and be able to tour with that globally for The Love Tour is really exciting.”
