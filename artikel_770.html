Brooks Nader Reveals If She Approves of Sister Dating Pete Davidson

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Brooks Nader addressed speculation that her sister Sarah Jane Nader is dating Pete Davidson during an exclusive interview with E! News at People and InStyle’s Drive-By Party in Las Vegas Nov. 20.

Brooks Nader loves thy sister Sarah Jane Nader, but she isn’t too thrilled about a potential update on her love life.

Amid rumors her younger sister is dating Pete Davidson, the Baywatch actress shared her thoughts on the speculation.

“I don’t approve of it. No,” Brooks exclusively told E! News at People and InStyle’s Drive-By Party at the Las Vegas Grand Prix Nov. 20 as she gave a thumbs-down to the camera. “She can do whatever she wants, but I’m not into it.”

And according to the 29-year-old, she’s not always privy to details about Sarah Jane’s dating life.

“You’d have to ask her,” Brooks said of how long her sister and Pete have been seeing each other. “She doesn’t get into it with me. When she knows I don’t approve of someone, she doesn’t tell me anything, so I find out online.”

While the Sports Illustrated model made her views on Sarah Jane potentially dating the comedian clear, a source close to Pete previously told E! News there is no truth to the dating rumors.

In fact, while it’s been nearly four months since the Dancing With the Stars alum and Bodies Bodies Bodies actor made waves when they stepped out together in Brooklyn, the source noted that they’ve only seen each other a handful of times since.

For the August outing, Sarah Jane, 24, and Pete, 33, were spotted getting into a car together. The Meet Cute actor opened the passenger door for the Love They Nader star, before he moved over to the driver’s side. But at the time, a source with direct knowledge told E! News the duo are just friends.

As for Brooks, the news of her sister hanging out with Pete—dad to daughter Scottie, 11 months, with ex Elsie Hewitt—was unexpected.

“Oh wow, I thought she was into girls this week,” she joked to TMZ after Sarah Jane and Pete’s night out. “That’s what her vibe has been.”

The model added that she needed to chat with Sarah Jane, jokingly admitting that she was “confused” and “lost” about the rumors.

“I really did think that she was into girls recently,” Brooks quipped, “but she keeps us on our toes.”

While it’s unclear what the future will hold for Sarah Jane’s love life, keep reading for a closer look at Brooks’ dating history…

Leaving her Louisiana home to pursue her modeling dreams at the age of 19, Brooks Nader knew she had to make some big deals. "When I moved to New York, my dad was like, 'You're living a full-ride scholarship. I have no money to support your life in New York. You better find a boyfriend,'" she explained to People. "Which I did, thank God, for just the rent purposes."

Though, she arguably grew quite fond of advertising executive Billy Haire, the duo marrying in New Orleans' French Quarter in December 2019.

Her mom and dad were less than thrilled to hear about their separation nearly five years later. "When I told my parents I was getting divorced, you’d think I killed somebody," she shared on the Love Thy Nader reality series she filmed with sisters Sarah Jane Nader, Grace Ann Nader and Mary Holland Nader. "They were like, 'This is the worst decision ever. Divorce is a sin.'"

But the Sports Illustrated Swimsuit model would beg to differ. "Once I got to that other side, I realized, 'Wait, I got me. I can do anything I f--king put my mind to,'" she reflected to Bustle in 2025. "I was with someone for 10 years, and I cut it off and came out better. I can push out a human; I can start a company; I can get over anybody. It’s euphoric, that feeling."

As for Billy, he went on to charm another reality star, he and Southern Charm alum Naomie Olindo announcing their engagement in December 2025.

In the months following Brooks' divorce, it seemed she had snagged a pass from retired NFL quarterback Tom Brady. Though she and the NFL on Fox color commentator were spotted at Michael Rubin’s 2024 Fourth of July party in the Hamptons, any fireworks had dissipated by fall.

"She’s having fun as a single girl," sister Sarah Jane told Daily Mail that September. "I think it’s as exciting as it possibly could be."

Cast on Dancing With the Stars shortly after her 2024 divorce, Brooks was eyeing more than just the mirrorball trophy. Paired with pro Gleb Savchenko, the two quickly became partners IRL as well.

But much like their dances, their romance was largely physical. Single for the first time since she was 18, and doing the tango with "the hottest guy I've ever seen in my life," she detailed on Love Thy Nader, meant things escalated quickly.

"All we did was have sex," Brooks shared on the Freeform series, "because I didn't really talk to him that much."

Perhaps that lack of communication is why he felt blindsided when she confirmed in April 2025 that they were no longer in step.

They weren't even following the same routine, to be honest. "I was surprised to learn through an article published today … that Brooks has ended our relationship," Gleb said in an April 7 statement to Us Weekly. "The last communication I received from her was a text on April 6, asking to speak. I called her today in response, but she has not replied."

Jimmy Kimmel took a swing at getting Nader to confirm the rumors she was matched up with tennis phenom Jannik Sinner. "Are you dating Italian superstar Jannik Sinner?" he asked during her September 2025 appearance on Jimmy Kimmel Live. Her response? "Is this like an interrogation? I'm so scared right now."

Though she suggested Stephen Colbert could potentially serve up the truth. "Well, ask Stephen what match we were at," she said of sitting next to the late-night host during her Aug. 31 outing to the U.S. Open. Because, as she noted of Sinner, "I don't think he was at that one. You're close. You're warm, but we weren't at that match."

What Brooks was trying to hint at in her back-and-forth volley on Kimmel was that she'd found a potential partner in Spanish athlete Carlos Alcaraz. "The rumors are true," sister Grace Ann exclusively told E! News at the Raising Cane's New York Fashion Week Show days later. Still, she cautioned it wasn't exactly love-all just yet. "Dating is such a loose term," she added. "But I do know he’s the man of the hour."

And while she was waiting for an introduction, he'd already made the acquaintance of their other sisters. "I'm dying to," Grace Ann said of meeting the seven-time Grand Slam champ. "Such a cutie."

As for those finding fault with the swimsuit model doubling up with the tennis stars, "I mean, dating is such a loose term these days," she mused during a September 2025 appearance on Watch What Happens Live. "Guys do it all the time. So why can’t I do it?"

Oh, how it felt so real when Rocketman actor Taron Egerton held his tiny dancer close during a March 28, 2026 date night in Los Angeles. Following a three-hour dinner at Capo in Santa Monica, Calif., he and Brooks walked arm-in-arm to nearby Shutters on the Beach to drink each other in.
