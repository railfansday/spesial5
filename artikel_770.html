HYBE, Home to BTS and KATSEYE, Forms Strategic Partnership With CAA

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The new partnership leverages HYBE's artist roster alongside CAA's expertise in live experiences, brand partnerships, content and more.

HYBE and CAA have formed a strategic partnership, the companies jointly announced on Monday (Nov. 16). Through the deal, HYBE seeks to expand the reach of its artists, intellectual property and brands.

The companies will collaborate on brand partnerships, live experiences, licensing and merchandising, sports and content. It will include initiatives in markets HYBE dominates, including Korea, Japan, China, India and the Americas. The relationship encompasses HYBE’s roster, including BTS, KATSEYE, ENHYPEN, SEVENTEEN, Rosé and aespa.

“This partnership with CAA marks an important step in expanding creative possibilities for HYBE’s artists,” HYBE CEO Jason Jaesang Lee said in a statement. “By bringing together our complementary strengths and global networks, we aim to extend the reach and value of our artists and their IP across content, brands and live experiences. Together, we will explore new creative opportunities that transcend borders and formats, bringing audiences closer to our artists through more immersive and meaningful experiences.”

Bryan Lourd, CEO and co-chairman at CAA, added, “HYBE artists do not just entertain; they connect with audiences in extraordinary and innovative ways that define the future of our industry and the intersection of art, influence and fandom. We are honored to help carry that forward, building on CAA’s experience and relationships across the region, to show the world what is possible when two organizations that believe in creative talent come together.”

The deal follows closely on HYBE’s record-breaking third quarter, largely driven by BTS’ return. The company reported that it earned 1.5257 trillion Korean won ($1.135 billion) in revenue. Recorded music, concerts, appearances and advertisements across all of its artists accounted for 70% of revenue, totaling 1.0753 trillion Korean won ($800.06 million).

“What stands out about HYBE is how thoughtfully it builds around each artist, from the music and storytelling to the fan experience,” Lucy Dickins, the managing director at CAA who was involved in the signing, said in a statement. “Our role is to build on that foundation across brands, partnerships and live opportunities, creating new ways for artists to expand their reach, engage audiences in new ways and shape what comes next.”

Maha Dakhil, managing director at CAA, added, “This partnership represents a defining moment for our industry. HYBE has built an enduring legacy around the world. Together, we will set a new standard for what global entertainment collaboration looks like.”

In October, HYBE appointed Juan S. Arenas as president of HYBE’s Global Label Services. Also that month, the company launched its first Latin pop label, TRU Records, which is led by GM Alejandro Mejía and launched with Santos Bravos as its first signing.
