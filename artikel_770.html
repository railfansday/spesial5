Miley Cyrus Marries Maxx Morando in Poolside Ceremony Led by the Flaming Lips’ Wayne Coyne

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It comes one month after the couple performed "Let's Get Married" together at the Hollywood Bowl.

Miley Cyrus married Maxx Morando on Saturday (Nov. 21) in a poolside ceremony in Palm Springs, Calif., with the Flaming Lips’ Wayne Coyne officiating, one month after the couple sang “Let’s Get Married” together on the Hollywood Bowl stage. Explore Miley Cyrus See latest videos, charts and news

Coyne, who worked with Cyrus on her 2015 album Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz and joined her onstage at the Hollywood Bowl in October, led the vows as Cyrus, 33, and Morando, 28, stood beneath an arch of white flowers in front of family and friends, according to photos published by People. Cyrus wore a long-sleeved white satin gown with a high neck, open back and small train, and was photographed wiping away tears during the vows, while Morando wore a black suit. Among the guests were her mother, Tish Cyrus, and husband Dominic Purcell; her sister Noah Cyrus; and her father, Billy Ray Cyrus, along with Elizabeth Hurley. The celebrations began the night before with a rehearsal dinner, glimpsed in Instagram photos posted by Cyrus’ stepsister Lily-Rose Purcell that showed tables dressed with dark red flowers and small candles, and a menu topped with an “M+M” monogram.

The wedding caps a year in which the relationship moved from the edges of Cyrus’ public life to the center of her work. “Let’s Get Married,” which Morando co-wrote and co-produced, appears on Bass Persuades, her 10th studio album and her first released simply as Miley. Its black-and-white video, directed by Mert Alas and shot at West Hollywood’s Chateau Marmont, casts Morando opposite her — a dress rehearsal, as it turned out, for the real thing two months later.

That album also brought Cyrus back to the top of the Billboard 200, debuting at No. 1 on the chart dated Oct. 3 with 61,000 equivalent album units, according to Luminate. It gave her a sixth No. 1 album and her first since Bangerz in 2013.

It marks a striking shift for a couple who spent years largely out of view. Morando, the drummer for Los Angeles rock band Liily, was first linked to Cyrus in late 2021, when the pair attended a Gucci event together. The two had been set up on a blind date — “Well, it was blind for me and not really for him,” she told British Vogue in 2023 — and he later joined her at the 2024 Grammy Awards, where “Flowers” won record of the year. Morando proposed in October 2025 in a garden on the grounds of their hotel in Kyoto, Japan, telling Wonderland he held out “until the very last moment of the very last day” to keep it a surprise. The engagement went public in early December 2025, when Cyrus wore the ring to the Dec. 1 premiere of Avatar: Fire and Ash in Los Angeles.

It is Cyrus’ second marriage, following her December 2018 wedding to Liam Hemsworth, from whom she separated the following August. Speaking at a press conference for Avatar: Fire and Ash, she described her relationship with Morando as “about starting again,” reflecting “the consciousness of a more grown and adult person of what I want this time around.”

Billboard has reached out to Cyrus’ representatives for comment.
