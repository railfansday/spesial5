Miley Cyrus Celebrates 34th Birthday With Look at Maxx Morando Wedding

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Miley Cyrus celebrated her 34th birthday Nov. 23 with several sweet snaps from her Nov. 21 wedding to Maxx Morando in Palm Springs: "Waking up on my 34th birthday feeling so grateful."

Miley Cyrus has turned the rest of her life into a holiday.

As the Hannah Montana alum turned 34 on Nov. 23, she gave fans a special gift: a peek into her and Maxx Morando’s wedding celebration.

“Waking up on my 34th birthday feeling so grateful,” Cyrus posted to Instagram alongside a carousel of photos from the Nov. 21 ceremony in Palm Springs, Calif. “Thank you for all the wishes & to everyone who made this weekend a memory we’ll cherish for a lifetime and beyond.”

Among the snaps she included by photographer Jose Villa was a look at something beautiful: her wedding accessories, including her white heels, her and Morando’s wedding rings, as well as sunglasses and a purse.

The “Wrecking Ball” singer—who wore a long-sleeved, backless wedding gown designed by Saint Laurent creative director Anthony Vaccarello—posted a few snaps from the wedding itself as she and Morando stood under a floral awning at the Frank Sinatra Twin Palms Estate.

The photos also included several of Cyrus with her family, including one grooving with mom Tish Cyrus and sister Noah Cyrus, as well as another loving look at her and dad Billy Ray Cyrus sharing a father-daughter dance.

The Grammy winner also reshared the carousel to her Instagram Stories, set to her song “Let’s Get Married” from her 2026 album Bass Persuasion.

Cyrus—who was previously married to Liam Hemsworth from 2018 to 2020—began dating Morando in 2021, with the Liily drummer revealing they crossed paths after the friend of his bandmate’s sister set them up on a blind date. As he told Wonderland magazine in July, they hit it off immediately and “just never stopped hanging out.”

And Cyrus knew exactly what it was about an angel like Morando, to whom she got engaged in 2025, that made their romance work.

“We've been together six years, and I've never gotten the ick," she told Zane Lowe in an interview shared by Apple Music in September. Although she did panic slightly before the wedding, saying, "All of a sudden I got in my head, I was like, ‘Oh my God, we’re about to get married. What if I get the ick?'"

"I asked him, 'What happens if you get the ick once you’re married?'" she continued. "He’s like, 'You deal with it and you move on and you don’t be a baby. The ick is for when you’re in fifth grade.'"
