Chance The Rapper Celebrates Birth of Third Daughter, Hayli: ‘Mom n Dad Love You’

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The 33-year-old rapper shares two other children with his ex Kirsten Corley.

Chance the Rapper is a girl dad three times over. The “No Problem” MC announced in an Instagram post on Sunday (Nov. 15) that he recently welcomed his third daughter, Hayli. “Welcome to the world Hayli,” he wrote in the caption. “Mom n Dad love you. The accompanying slideshow featured images of an unidentified woman snuggling with the newborn in the hospital, as well as snaps of the hip-hop star with his hand on her very pregnant belly and another in which he painted a carved pumpkin image on his partner’s stomach (“Hayli’s first Halloween costume”) and some of the “nesting” prep for the family’s new addition; the post did not detail when and where the child was born. Chance, 33, (born Chancelor Bennett) did not identify the woman in the photos. He shares two older daughters with his ex-wife, Kirsten Corley, 11-year-old Kensli Bennett and 7-year-old Marli Grace Bennett. The couple were married in 2019 and announced their separation in April 2024, finalizing their divorce in January 2025.

The birth of his third child is not the only big change in Chance’s life lately. During an appearance on Raven-Symoné and Miranda Maday’s Tea Time podcast last month, Chance said that after using it for more than 15 years he’s thinking of switching up his stage name.

“I think when I first got it, it was just a name that a girl I went to high school with came up with,” Chance told the duo. “I think over time, yes, people would tell me, ‘Yo, you should drop the ‘rapper’ part out of your name because you’re more than rap.’” Chance explained that in the past he has hesitated to shorten his name because he always wanted whatever he did to be focused on and influenced by hip-hop.

“I want to carry rap the same way that rap has carried me through life,” he said. But recently, he added, he’s been “thinking about changing my name,” perhaps to just “The Rapper.”
