Newbury's Coral Gold Cup: Jamie Lynch's horse-by-horse guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Ahead of the big Coral Gold Cup showdown on Saturday, Sky Sports Racing's senior form analyst Jamie Lynch offers his verdict on every runner going to post in search of top honours; watch the race at 3pm on Saturday - LIVE on Sky Sports Racing

Jamie Lynch assesses the confirmations and reveals his top tip for one of the most prestigious handicaps of the season this Saturday, live on Sky Sports Racing.

The Coral Gold Cup at Newbury at 3pm is one of the biggest and best handicap chases of the entire season, although conditions will be a break from the norm - the reason the race is a little lighter at the top end this time, with only one rated above 150.

But there is the usual stack of classy second-season chasers with high trajectories and higher aspirations, meaning that - as always - it's going to take a lot of winning. Let's look at all those entered for this high-prestige prize, just one part of a stellar Saturday on Sky Sports Racing.
