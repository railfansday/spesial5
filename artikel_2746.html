Broncos vs. Raiders practice participation report: Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Not much movement on the injury front for the Denver Broncos. Both rest day guys are back at full participation and cornerback Kris Abrams-Draine moved from limited to full. That Broncos' secondary has been quite battered and bruised for the last month or more. They are finally getting fully healthy again with only safety Brandon Jones still limited by his hamstring issue.

For the Las Vegas Raiders, it was a mixed bag with several guys downgrading their participation levels on Thursday. However, the one bright spot for them is their star tight end Brock Bowers is progressing well from his injuries. It is looking like he might be good to go for this Week 11 matchup in Denver.
