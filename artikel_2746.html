Travel information for the Bundesliga home match against Leverkusen

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The special TRI train (Borussia-Express) departs from Hamm Hbf at 17:34 and stops at Signal Iduna Park station at 18:05.

The RE 57 service from the Sauerland will make additional stops at Signal Iduna Park station at 17:14, 18:14 and 19:14, and at Dortmund Central Station six minutes later in each case.

The RE 57 service from Dortmund towards the Sauerland will make additional departures from Dortmund Hbf at 17:41 and 18:41, and from Signal Iduna Park station five minutes later in each case.

The special TRI train (Borussia-Express) departs from Signal Iduna Park station at 22:06 and terminates at Hamm Hbf at 22:40.

Towards Lüdenscheid: Due to engineering works, RB 52 trains between Rummenohl and Hagen Hbf are cancelled and replaced by buses.

Towards Unna/Soest: Due to engineering works, the RB 59 service will only run between Unna and Dortmund, with a few exceptions. A rail replacement bus service will operate between Soest and Unna. Trains can only run through to Soest between 21:00 and 23:00.

Towards Lünen: Due to engineering works further along the RB 51 route, no special train can run from Signal Iduna Park. Additional carriages cannot be added to RB 51 trains either. Passengers travelling towards Lünen/Coesfeld should use the regular services from Dortmund Hbf.

Match tickets include a public transport ticket for one outward and one return journey. The ticket is valid throughout North Rhine-Westphalia. The DSW21 ticket can be ordered free of charge via the KombiTicket portal.

All services can be found in the online journey planner (www.bus-und-bahn.de) and in the DSW21 and VRR apps. Further timetable details are also available at NWL-Info.de and zuginfo.nrw.

Anyone cycling to the home match can use the free supervised bike parking provided by the ADFC. There are a total of 1,000 parking spaces for bicycles and privately owned e-scooters around SIGNAL IDUNA PARK.

The supervised parking areas are located here:

At the corner of Strobelallee and Rabenloh, opposite FanWelt

Opposite the Helmut-Körnig-Halle, next to the Rosenterrassen

To the southeast, at the corner of Bolmker Weg and Schwimmweg.

The supervised bike parking areas open around 100 minutes before kick-off and close no later than 60 minutes after the final whistle. Important information for rental scooter users: An exclusion zone is in place around the stadium, so you cannot ride directly to SIGNAL IDUNA PARK.

"Let's Dance" is taking place at the Westfalenhalle at the same time as BVB's home match. Due to this event, many parking spaces are likely to be occupied by the time you arrive in Dortmund.

For all fans travelling by car, DOPARK sells parking tickets for home matches in advance. Tickets are sold exclusively through the DOPARK ticket shop. Ticket holders benefit from having their booked parking space reserved until kick-off, avoiding the sometimes lengthy search for an available space. Arrival is also easier, as there is no need to pay at the car park entrance. If you do need to drive, please help protect the environment by car-sharing and keeping the number of cars travelling to the match to a minimum.

Borussia Dortmund recommends car-sharing and keeping the number of cars travelling to the match to a minimum. The Bundesliga also offers its own car-sharing portal. The Bundesliga car-sharing portal connects fans who want to travel together to home and away matches. This makes the journey greener, cheaper and more sociable.
