How Daniil Medvedev’s ranking has changed since the end of 2025 as he concludes 2026 campaign

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Daniil Medvedev has enjoyed an excellent campaign on the ATP circuit.

Medvedev has bounced back emphatically from a poor 2025 season, winning multiple titles and producing some of his best tennis when the stakes have been highest.

The Russian's good season was encapsulated in Turin over the past week; Medvedev advanced to the semi-finals, beating Flavio Cobolli and Frances Tiafoe – before losing to a rampant Alexander Zverev.

Following the conclusion of Medvedev's season, it is worth assessing just how much Medvedev has progressed over the past year.

Medvedev's 2025 season was littered with poor results and disappointing performances.

The Russian star won just one match at Grand Slam events throughout the entire year and captured just one title – the Almaty Open.

Medvedev was ranked fifth on the ATP Tour at the start of 2025. After 12 months, his ranking had fallen to 13.

However, Medvedev refused to let one poor season define his future campaigns, utilising all of his experience to bounce back in 2026.

Medvedev has performed at a much higher level throughout the 2026 campaign, winning four ATP titles and reaching at least the semi-finals at three Masters events.

However, the Russian has continued to struggle on the major stage, failing to advance past the fourth round at any of the Grand Slam events.

Australian Open: Fourth round [lost to Learner Tien, 4-6, 0-6, 3-6]

Roland Garros: First round [lost to Adam Walton, 2-6, 6-1, 1-6, 6-1, 4-6]

Wimbledon: Third round [lost to Jan-Lennard Struff, 6-7, 6-7, 5-7]

US Open: Fourth round [lost to Frances Tiafoe, 6-7, 4-6, 6-7]

Despite these struggles, Medvedev's ranking has risen consistently throughout the year. As per the ATP live rankings, Medvedev will end 2026 ranked fifth in the world once again.

Medvedev is set to begin his 2027 ATP campaign at the Brisbane International in Australia.

Medvedev is the defending champion in Brisbane. He defeated Brandon Nakashima in the 2026 final, 6-2, 7-6.

"I want to dedicate this to my family," Medvedev said during his winner's speech. "They couldn't make it to Australia, it is a bit too far guys, about 23 hours from where I live and with two young kids it is not easy.

"I decided to play this tournament and we thought we would be coming all together but we stuck to the decision and my daughter had a birthday when I was here, so I dedicate it to her first birthday. This [trophy] is a bit too heavy for her though."

Medvedev is set to join Alex de Minaur and Alexander Zverev at the event. On the women's side, Elena Rybakina, Iga Swiatek and Naomi Osaka have confirmed their participation.
