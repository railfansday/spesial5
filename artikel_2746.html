2026-27 NBA News, Injury Updates Nov. 16: Stephen Curry injured, Dejounte Murray makes trade request

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Welcome to our weekly Monday Morning NBA notebook, a quick hit of NBA news and injuries from around the league to catch you up on what happened in the past week. Maybe one of these weeks I can write a summary that doesn't start with critical injuries.

The Golden State Warriors have been one of the great stories of the young season, with rookie Yaxel Landeborg grabbing headlines and winning games. The Warriors have beaten the Lakers, Timberwolves and Nuggets at points this season.

But this was still Stephen Curry's team, and his throwback start — 27.8 points per game, shooting 45.3% from 3-point range — is the foundation of all that winning. Now he is out with an oblique abdominal tear and will be re-evaluated in a couple of weeks, but reportedly could miss more like a month. In the game where he got injured and the game he missed so far, the Warriors got blown out. It might be a rough few weeks coming up in the Bay Area.

Caleb Wilson hasn't been the best rookie out of this class so far this young season, but he's been the most entertaining, averaging 18.9 points and 6.2 rebounds a game. More importantly, he gives the Bulls and their fans a real direction for the future after too many years of mediocrity (at best).

Now he is out 4-6 weeks with a hand fracture. Which just takes the air out of the sails in Chicago. It shouldn't impact winning or (more often) losing, they have been about the same with him on or off the court this season, but he brought a joy to the Bulls that is going to be missing for a bit.

We have our first trade request of the season: Dejounte Murray's agent (Rich Paul of Klutch Sports) told the New Orleans Pelicans Murray wants to be moved, something the Pelicans came out and confirmed.

When the Pelicans traded for Murray in the summer of 2024 it seemed like a good move on paper, he was the kind of guard who could bring together a talented roster that wasn't quite fitting with Zion Williamson, Trey Murphy III and others. It didn't work out that way, as he ruptured his Achilles that first season in New Orleans and missed the majority of his first two seasons there.

Jeremiah Fears is the point guard of the future in New Orleans, and he and Murray do not mesh well as a backcourt. This is best for everyone. That said, after injuries and a couple of down years because of it, the market for Murray is not going to bring a big return, so don't be surprised if this drags out into 2027 and closer to the February trade deadline.

The NBA is going to take its talents to South Beach.

The 2028 NBA All-Star Game will be in Miami, the first time the NBA's midseason showcase has been in the city since 1990 (Magic Johnson was your MVP that year). Miami has everything the NBA wants in a venue — warm weather, plenty of hotel rooms for fans, and players love the city and aren't looking for an excuse not to attend/skip town early — but the game traditionally falls on Presidents Day weekend, which is also when the city hosts the massive Miami International Boat Show. That show took up hotel and convention space the NBA needed, but in 2028 the boat show is moving back a weekend, reports the Miami Herald. The NBA stepped into that gap.

This is a win for everybody — Miami was made for this game.

• Cooper Flagg making history. Flagg is having a "he has arrived" season — looking every bit a top 15 player in the world already — and this past week he made history: Flagg passed LeBron James to be the teenager with the most 40+ point games in league history, and he also passed Kobe Bryant and now has scored the second most points as a teenager. He turns 20 next month.

• Lauri Markkanen is out with a wrist injury. Utah has dropped 12 straight, and this isn't helping matters: He has a non-displaced fracture in his right hand and will miss about a month (until mid-December). It likely means more touches for Darryn Peterson and Ace Bailey.• In the Jayson Tatum vs. Jaylen Brown showdown, Paul George was the star, reminding everyone he is still very good at basketball (and the Celtics got a convincing win). If he is healthy in April and May, the Celtics are a playoff threat in the East.

• The Bulls' Matas Buzelis and the Nets Josh Minott each have been fined $25,000 by the league for "making an obscene gesture towards the spectator stands." As a rule, the NBA frowns on players flipping off paying customers.

• Sacramento added veteran center Mason Plumlee to the roster. The Kings have a lot of centers on the roster, but that's not working as planned: Domantas Sabonis starts but is slumping, the future at the five may be Maxime Raynaud but he is injured, then there is Dylan Cardwell and, for small-ball lineups, Precious Achiuwa. Plumlee is not going to see a ton of minutes but the Kings could use the mentor.
