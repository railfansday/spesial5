Who should be The Providence Journal Boys Soccer Playoff MVP? Vote now!

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The end of the high school boys soccer playoffs means a new Most Valuable Player poll. This one covers the last two weeks of action, but it's generally more weighted to what we saw Sunday, Nov. 8, in the finals. Those were indeed the most important games of the fall.

Our last winner of the regular season was Collin Etheridge, and he was among the reasons Ponaganset entered the Division II playoffs in strong form. Etheridge netted two goals in a 4-3 win over Davies and chipped in an assist in a 3-0 shutout of eventual fellow semifinalist Middletown. He brought home 54.3% of the vote to top the field.

Voting in this online poll will end Saturday night. Phone calls, emails and social media contact won't be considered. Logging into this story and participating through the link provided is your only option.

Acosta collected four goals and a pair of assists as the Panthers won all three of their playoff matches, including a 2-0 shutout of Ponaganset in the Division III final. He scored the opener against the Chieftains, a pair in a 3-1 semifinal victory over Middletown and a single goal in a 4-0 quarterfinal blanking of Woonsocket.

Brown scored one of the goals of the weekend to win the Division II final against Central Falls, a 2-1 thriller in extra time. His cushioned right-footed volley hit the right corner to snap a 1-1 draw in the 83rd minute. Brown also set up the opener for the Purple, an intelligent free kick he rolled to Leandro Orellana for a pinpoint strike into the top left corner.

Jackson helped drive the Rams from the midfield all season, and the playoffs were no different. He posted a goal and two assists in a 7-1 semifinal thumping of Bishop Hendricken and set up Lucas Vrees for the eventual winner in a tight 3-2 finals triumph over North Kingstown.

Jenison built on the promise he showed as a freshman by developing into one of the premier attacking talents in the league. He netted single goals in a pair of 2-0 shutouts against East Providence and Barrington, results that pushed the Skippers into their fifth Division I finals appearance since 2021.

Laforest sent a shockwave through Rhode Island College by scoring in just 11 seconds to open the Division I title match against North Kingstown. He added a second later in the first half to help the Rams post a 3-2 victory and claim the crown. Laforest also tallied a semifinal goal in a 7-1 cruise past rival Bishop Hendricken.
