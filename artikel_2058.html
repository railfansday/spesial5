Writing’s on the wall for West Ham stalwart’s international career

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Time catches up with all of us, footballers just the same. Few are given much indication that the writing is on the wall, until suddenly that place which was automatically theirs is going to a younger, fitter, faster team mate – and then, suddenly, without a fanfare, almost always without even a ‘thank you’ –  it is over.

That, sadly, is likely to be the outcome for Czech stalwart and former captain Tomas Soucek who found himself an unused substitute last night after appearing for just five minutes in his countries’ previous game – during which Tornado Tom’s mistake in added time gifted Spain the injury time second goal in their 2 – 0 defeat of the Czechs.

A total of 93 appearances – 42 as captain – and nineteen goals is a pretty stellar international career and for Soucek to have fought his way back from such a bad injury at the World Cup last summer shows his fighting spirit.

You’d think it unlikely that Soucek will make the squad in the March window, after being virtually discarded for the two Nations League games just gone – both of which his Czech Republic side lost.

Hopefully it’ll give the veteran a little more gusto to complete the job and see his West Ham side over the line and promoted back to the Premier League – which would be a fitting swan song for Soucek who’d be 32 next season and probably a bit-part player if the Hammers make it back to the top tier.

At least it’d mean domestically, he could go out at the top. Which he thoroughly deserves after more than 250 West Ham caps.
