Blackpool set sights on League Two boss as potential Ian Evatt replacement

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Blackpool sacked Ian Evatt last week, leaving the Tangerines on the lookout for a new boss. They were defeated in their first game under Steve Thompson’s interim lead, losing to fellow strugglers Wigan Athletic 2-1 at home.

Now, a new name has been linked with the vacancy at Bloomfield Road. According to reporter Pete O’Rourke, one man under consideration for the job at Blackpool is Fleetwood Town boss Matt Lawlor.

Lawlor has drawn high praise for his work with the Seasiders’ North West coast neighbours, where he has been in permanent charge since March. The 38-year-old is under contract with Fleetwood until the summer of 2028 at present, so a compensation fee would be required to bring him in as Evatt’s replacement.

Born in Poulton-le-Fylde, Lawlor is a North West native. He played for Blackpool’s youth academy as a youngster before going on to play non-league football with Farsley Celtic, AFC Fylde and Bamber Bridge.

He coached alongside his playing career from a young age, working with Manchester United before joining the Fleetwood Town setup in 2019. Lawlor held various roles in their academy and spent a few spells at their sister club Waterford FC, serving as assistant manager once and caretaker boss twice.

An impressive stint as temporary boss of the Cod Army in January 2026 saw Lawlor given his maiden full-time gig in senior football, and he has made a good impression. He isn’t blessed with resources at Highbury Stadium, but he’s delivered respectable results through fine match preparation and some smart in-game coaching, as shown by the shock Carabao Cup win over Premier League champions Arsenal.

Lawlor’s future at Fleetwood is secure with a contract until 2028, but that might not be enough to fend off interest from elsewhere.

Lawlor’s good work at Fleetwood Town means interest from Blackpool is more than justified. It could prove a tempting opportunity too.

Geographically, he could make the step up to League One without having to move away from home. His past as a youth player at Bloomfield Road could mean there are sentimental temptations as well.

That said, it does feel as though Lawlor is in a good place right now. After various roles in the Fishermen’s setup and at Waterford, he has developed well. He’s still only 38 and in the formative years of his time as a first-team boss too, so may be wary of making the step up too early.

Time will tell whether the rumoured interest comes to anything, with Blackpool sure to be considering plenty of contenders.
