Dan Quinn: Lucas Patrick to start at center vs Bengals

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Dan Quinn spoke to reporters after the Washington Commanders finished their last full practice of the week. The team hosts the Cincinnati Bengals on Monday Night Football this week, and they're coming off a mini-bye following last week's Thursday Night Football win over the Giants.

That break was good for the team's injury report, as some players got a little healthier. The Commanders are still dealing with several injuries, including one that happened yesterday. CB Amik Robertson was seen limping after yesterday's practice, and Quinn said he suffered a lower leg injury on the last play of the session. They'll work with him over the next 48 hours to determine his game status for Monday night. Quinn said it would be a combination of Trey Amos and Rasul Douglas with Mike Sainristil and Fabian Moreau also getting reps on the inside if Robertson would miss time.

Bill Croskey-Merritt is set to return from injured reserve after missing the last four games with a groin injury. Quinn wasn't ready to committ to Bill playing Monday night, but he's trending in the right direction and itching to get back on the field. Rachaad White didnt practice today due to heel soreness, while WR Treylon Burks was back at practice and should be good to go with his lingering foot injury.

Lucas Patrick suffered a concussion two weeks ago and was replaced by Nick Allegretti vs the Giants. That was the best the line and protections have looked in a while, but Quinn said the team is going back to Lucas now that he has cleared concussion protocol. Allegretti returns to a reserve role, able to back up the three interior OL positions.
