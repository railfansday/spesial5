Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Motorcycle volume soared as premium bicycle demand lagged in the quarter.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Niu Technologies (NIU -4.14%) reported total revenue of RMB 1,713.6 million for the third quarter, representing a 1.2% year-over-year increase. Management reported that while total sales volume grew 15.4%, the top-line performance was constrained by two transitional factors: the aggressive clearance of aged international micromobility inventory and soft demand in the domestic premium electric bicycle segment. In response to shifting regulatory standards in China, the company has strategically expanded its presence in the electric motorcycle market, which achieved triple-digit volume growth during the period. Management also noted that gross margins expanded two percentage points sequentially to 18.0%, indicating that structural efficiency improvements are beginning to offset inventory and market-driven headwinds ahead of the 2027 fiscal year.
