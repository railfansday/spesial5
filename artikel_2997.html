Autodesk (ADSK -3.87%) reported third quarter results that exceeded its guidance ranges for revenue and earnings, driven by sustained momentum in its AEC segment and emerging markets. Management reported the signing of the largest enterprise business agreement in the company's history and highlighted the successful initial integration of MaintainX, which contributed $33 million in its first quarter under the platform. The company raised its full-year guidance midpoints for revenue and billings to reflect the quarterly outperformance and improved underlying expectations. Strategic initiatives remain focused on integrating artificial intelligence into design and manufacture workflows to increase efficiency and deliver better project intelligence across the asset life cycle.
