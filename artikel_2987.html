Why AMD Stock Fell Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Investors are deciding what Huang's return means.

By Johnny Rice – Nov 24, 2026 at 6:05PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Advanced Micro Devices ›

Advanced Micro Devices (AMD -8.86%) fell today, finishing down 9% amid a sea of red as chip stocks gave back some of Monday's Nvidia-led gains.

The Nasdaq Composite fell 1.4% on Tuesday, while the S&P 500 lost 0.5%.

AI-related stocks, especially chip stocks like AMD, jumped Monday after Nvidia (NVDA -5.86%) brought back CEO Jensen Huang -- a move many thought wasn't under any serious consideration. That rally faded today, however, as investors tried to decide what, if anything, Huang's return means long term for companies outside of Nvidia.

AMD's data center revenue nearly doubled in its last quarter, helped by booming sales of server processors and the launch of its latest AI chips. The chipmaker expects overall sales to reach about $17 billion this quarter. Its massive chip deployment with Anthropic (ANT -6.54%) is due to start in the coming year.

That's all positive, sure, but with a stock trading at 15 times sales -- even after its 36% decline over the last seven weeks -- I would be cautious about buying AMD at the moment. Especially with all of the uncertainty in the market.
