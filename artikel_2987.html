Bills to retire Andre Reed's No. 83 in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Bills will be retiring the number of the top wide receiver in franchise history next year.

Andre Reed piled up more catches, receiving yards and receiving touchdowns than any Bills player while helping the franchise to four straight Super Bowls in the 1990s and the Bills announced that they will be enshrining his No. 83 alongside the numbers of his longtime teammates Jim Kelly, Thurman Thomas and Bruce Smith at some point during the 2027 season. All four players are also in the Pro Football Hall of Fame.

"He's one of the main reasons I'm up on that wall," Kelly said, via the team's website. "He's one of the main reasons I'm in the Pro Football Hall of Fame; it takes both of us. When your quarterback goes in, usually you're probably going to have a receiver or two go in. . . . To have Andre for my whole career was just totally awesome. I thought it should have been done a few years ago, but I'm glad that it's getting done, and I'm glad I'm able to be here and celebrate his going on the wall to join me, Bruce and Thurman."

Reed had 941 catches for 13,095 yards and 86 touchdowns over 15 seasons in Buffalo. He was added to the franchise's Wall of Fame in 2006 and voted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2014.
