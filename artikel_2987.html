JPMorgan Cuts Borrowing Costs on $2.85 Billion CCC Debt Deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Banks led by JPMorgan Chase & Co. have lowered borrowing costs on $2.85 billion in loans and bonds to help fund the acquisition of CCC Intelligent Solutions Holdings Inc. by GTCR and Elliott Investment Management, on strong investor appetite for the leveraged buyout financing.
