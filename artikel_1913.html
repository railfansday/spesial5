‘I’m certain’ - Melvin Bard insight provided by Leeds United loanee’s ex-teammate Morgan Schneiderlin

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Melvin Bard’s former teammate Morgan Schneiderlin is “certain” there is more to come from the Leeds United defender.

Leeds signed Bard on loan from Nice on the final day of the summer transfer window and there is an option to make his move permanent.

The 26-year-old has found himself behind Gabriel Gudmundsson in the left wing-back department but made an impressive appearance from the bench in Leeds’ 1-0 win over Tottenham Hotspur before the international break.

Former Manchester United and Southampton midfielder Schneiderlin, who played with Bard at Nice, has compared him to Red Devils star Luke Shaw.

He told ComeOn: “I played with Melvin Bard at Nice and saw his development first-hand. When he first arrived, he was already a good player. I compare his progression a bit to Luke Shaw.

“He put in a lot of work on his conditioning and physique during his early time at Nice. He did the work, and now he is much fitter.

“He's very difficult to beat one-v-one, possesses great stamina, and has a very good left foot. Ultimately, whether to stay will be his choice, but he's a quality player and I'm certain he still has plenty of room to improve even further.”

Gudmundsson has established himself among Daniel Farke’s most trusted lieutenants since his arrival in the summer of 2025 and could prove difficult to dislodge.

Bard, however, will have his sights set on a more prominent role heading towards the end of the calendar year.

After putting pen to paper at Elland Road in the summer, Bard said: “Playing in the Premier League has always been a dream of mine, ever since I was a child.

“Leeds is a historic club in the Premier League so it was an easy choice. Leeds has a great history which makes the club an attractive one.
