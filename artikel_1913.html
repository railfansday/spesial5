Manchester United would struggle to complete a deal for Chelsea defender Malo Gusto in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The 23-year-old has reportedly told the Blues that he would like to explore a move away in the upcoming transfer window, having started just three Premier League games this season so far.

Xabi Alonso has largely opted for Josh Acheampong at right-back in the absence of Reece James since moving to a back-four, and preferred Pedro Neto at right wing-back earlier in the season.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Man United could be interested in Gusto due to Mauricio Pochettino's experience of working with him.

The Frenchman arrived at Stamford Bridge when his former manager did, making 37 appearances across all competitions in the one season they shared together.

That season was arguably Gusto's best since making the move to England, with the right-back providing nine assists - a number he has only matched in his subsequent 107 appearances for Chelsea combined.

Malo Gusto is open to a move away in January. The France international hasn't started a PL match in nearly two months. His view has been amicably communicated internally. Chelsea valued him at £75m in the summer. https://t.co/N1MV5mdfXE

Football Insider revealed in July that Chelsea had placed a mammoth £75million price tag on Gusto's head.

The likes of Manchester City were said to be interested in the defender, who is under contract until 2030.

It remains unclear if Chelsea will be willing to reduce that fee, with Man United also potentially keen on a deal for Gusto.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I think it’d be one to watch. Again, it’s an area that United need to strengthen in the fullback positions.

"Diogo Dalot has come in for a lot of flack from fans for his performances. Obviously Pochettino will know Gusto from his time at Chelsea as well.

"But again, I think for United, I don’t think they could go out there and spend the type of money in January that probably Chelsea would demand for Gusto, so again, it looks like another difficult deal to do in this window."

Gusto arrived at Chelsea as a talented but raw defender, having broken through for Lyon in the 2021-22 campaign.

He quickly adapted to life under Pochettino, however, with the Blues barely missing the frequently injured James.

"He is so young, he looks more mature than he is. Came from a difficult situation at Lyon, adapting to a new country, new football," the Argentine said in 2024.

"He is doing well. I am so happy with him. Always with a young kid, some mindsets are different; I am so happy that he can perform when Reece is out. It’s a good contribution for the team."
