The talks came amid a global outage of Microsoft services that paralyzed public services across Europe, an incident that underscored Europe’s dependence on American tech services. Only about 20% of Europe’s digital services run on European tech infrastructure.

In Ireland, the host country of Friday’s informal talks, the Microsoft outage caused widespread disruption for email and online services used by hospitals, government agencies and courts, according to news reports.

Patients at the Mater Hospital in Dublin were told to contact hospital staff by telephone rather than email. Meanwhile, Irish courts using Microsoft Teams, an online workspace and video conference platform, were forced to cancel online hearings.

Similar problems were reported across Europe, including in Great Britain, Denmark, Norway, the Netherlands, Germany and Italy.
