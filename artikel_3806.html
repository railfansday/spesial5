If you'd been considering picking up a Paramount Plus subscription, now's your chance; the streaming service has been hit with a Black Friday offer that lowers the monthly price to $2.99 in the US, or by 75% in the UK.

While the US cost would normally be $8.99 per month with ads or $13.99 per month without them, this year's Black Friday sale has slashed the price of both tiers to $2.99p/m for your first two months. Basically, you're paying $6 for a couple months of Paramount Plus regardless of which membership you choose. That's perfect timing if you want to blaze through what the streaming service has to offer over Thanksgiving and Christmas. Just be quick; the offer ends on November 30, 2026.

Meanwhile, UK fans can save up to 75% in total. You can either pick up the Standard (with ads) plan for £2.50 per month for two months rather than the usual £9.99p/m, or grab the ad-free Premium plan for £3.25p/m across two months instead of £12.99p/m.

UK Paramount Plus Black Friday deal: Save 75% per month for 2 monthsThis offer slashes the price of both Paramount Plus tiers across two months, giving you access to everything for much less than usual. The tier with ads costs £2.50 per month rather than £9.99p/m, while the ad-free equivalent is £3.25p/m rather than £12.99p/m. Just remember to cancel before your two months is up, or your subscription will eventually revert to the original cost.
