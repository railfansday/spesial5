Probably Stolen Eleanor quest guide: All choices and endings

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

One of the first major NPCs you can meet in Probably Stolen, Eleanor visits the store after having her life thrown upside down by a conspiracy and finding refuge at the Lower Levels.

The game then allows you to see her story through and help her along the way via a few key choices. Here’s a full walkthrough for Eleanor’s quest in Probably Stolen.

Following her first appearances at the store, Eleanor’s quest will progress automatically no matter whether you sell to her or buy any of her items, with its first key event playing out within your third to fifth week (depending on RNG). The event sees a new NPC claiming to be her uncle visit the shop with a ”care package”, which must be delivered to her and her alone.

You will now be given three choices: accept the package and deliver it to Eleanor, accept the package and keep it, or read the signs and call security on the NPC. You can check out what each option will result in below.

With the crate taken care of, Eleanor will return to the store a few more times before resolving to take matters into her own hands and find the truth behind her mother’s imprisonment. To progress through her quest, help her accumulate 800 Credits by purchasing her stock. There’s no time limit here, so don’t feel rushed.

After raising the necessary amount, Eleanor will visit the store again, revealing that her mother was framed after discovering a way to grow Nutrifruits with seeds, something that can break the corporations’ hold on the population. A new NPC will also visit the store shortly after, asking you to persuade Eleanor to turn in her mother’s research in exchange for the latter’s life and 300 Credits.
