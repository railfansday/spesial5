While more adult versions of the childhood favorite are seeing a revival year-round, it fits seamlessly with the dazzle of the holiday. There are, of course, plenty of affordable options to choose from, but as with other categories this time of year, a little splurge is warranted after all the hard work you’ve put in this year. So whether you’re treating yourself or gifting someone else, these luxury barrettes will add just the right shine to your seasonal looks and beyond.
