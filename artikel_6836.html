We Combed Through All of Amazon’s Holiday Decor and Found 30 Non-Tacky Picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Every item on this page was hand-picked by a House Beautiful editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Elevated festive decor, starting at just $12.

It’s beginning to look a lot like Christmas, and if you’re settling on a holiday decorating vision, you’re likely looking for pieces that feel festive without dipping into tacky territory. So our editors sorted through Amazon’s vast selection of holiday decor to find 30 non-tacky finds you’ll be proud to display.

Whether you’re drawn to vintage-inspired charm, opulence and glamour, or a traditional Christmas look, these gems make decorating easier—and many are timeless enough to bring out year after year. Shop classic tree trimmings, wreaths and garlands, elegant mantel accents, and elevated pieces for your holiday tablescape. Whether you’re looking for a tabletop tree or plaid pillow covers for a Ralph Lauren-inspired holiday look, you’ll find it ahead starting at $12.

Every holiday season, MacKenzie-Childs manages to outdo itself. Whether you choose between a quirky snow globe inspired by the brand’s signature tea kettles or this versatile tabletop tree, it’s sure to make a statement in your space.

Nothing says Ralph Lauren-inspired Christmas quite like tartan. These plaid pillow covers are available in a handful of coordinating color combinations, so at just $21 for a two-pack, we suggest snagging a few options to mix and match.

Even those who are picky about bedding can see the charm in swapping your linens for the holiday season. This red-and-white set is actually reversible, giving you the choice between a toile awash in a festive holiday print and classic stripes.

This pre-lit garland brimming with berries and pinecones is a low-stress garland option. Wind it around a dramatic staircase, place it on top of your mantel, or even drape it across your dining table.

Although Amazon has plenty of solid ornament sets, we’d rather go for colorful baubles that give off a collected-over-the-years vibe—even if you just picked them up in this 25-pack.

Whether you want to add a secondary tree to a bedroom or live in a small space and lack room for a full-size tree, check out this adorable tabletop option. It’s equipped with over 50 lights, has flocked branches, and is easy to add tiny baubles to.

Dress up your kitchen or bathroom with these festive cotton towels, featuring a traditional English toile print and classic plaid. They’re a charming complement to your favorite holiday hand soap.

Your Christmas decorating isn’t complete until you add a wreath to your front door. This year, snag a pre-lit option adorned with pinecones, flocked branches, and berries. Reviewers are impressed by how lifelike it looks.

For a vintage-inspired Christmas, these golden-hued jingle bells bring a warm, antique feel to your decor. Hang them on the tree or a front door wreath to welcome guests, or use them as a festive accent on your table.

Leave it to Ridhi, the designer-loved brand, to drop the tree skirt of our dreams. With green and white stripes, contrasting red piping, and functional bows, this is the perfect blend of trendy and timeless.

You don’t need to spend a fortune to add a dose of holiday cheer to your space. Case in point: For less than $20, you can scoop up a 30-pack of these flocked bottle brush trees to scatter around your bookshelves and tables.

Christmas calls for having sweet treats at the ready. Lenox makes this possible with this adorable candy jar that’s shaped like an ornament.

Building a collection of unique and meaningful ornaments takes time, which means you probably need to buy some solid baubles to fill space. Add texture with a set of velvet ornaments—this dusty blue will pop nicely against your tree’s green branches.

Glasshouse’s holiday candles are an editor favorite for setting a festive mood. Their seasonal scents linger in the air for hours, and they make a thoughtful and stylish gift for any holiday host.

It doesn’t get more traditional than a cable-knit stocking—especially when they’re “hung by the chimney with care.” We like how this set comes with red, green, and cream varieties.

If you’re looking for timeless Christmas decor that you’ll be able to use year after year, look no further than Balsam Hill. This classic beaded garland will never go out of style.

Give your table a touch of candy-cane-striped flair with these taper candles. We’d suggest styling them with candles of varying heights and colors for a glowing, layered effect.

Something about these ceramic trees feels so nostalgic. This 18-inch version is the perfect size for tabletop styling and lends a pop of color to any space.

Sick of the traditional tree toppers? This year, finish off your spruce with a metal bow. Not only will it look lovely with a variety of home decor styles—from farmhouse to maximalist—but just imagine the way it’ll reflect the twinkle off of your Christmas lights!

Dreaming of a coastal Christmas? Look no further than this rattan tree collar, which will even make a holiday spent in a landlocked place feel like you’re lounging by the ocean. Plus, it’s available in three natural shades to best suit your existing decor pieces.
