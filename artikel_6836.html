The Only 9 Pieces You Need for a Chic Winter Capsule Wardrobe

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Getting dressed is hard enough on an ideal day, but add frigid temps and icy conditions into the mix, and I’m constantly on the verge of canceling my plans to stay in bed all day. As a fashion editor, one thing that I’ve found immensely helpful in this struggle is having a winter capsule wardrobe, replete with versatile staples that lend themselves to mixing and matching. It really takes the guesswork out of my mornings.

In case you can relate, I’ve decided to share my winning formula here. To maintain style while staying warm, you’ll need foundational pieces like a good coat and boots, plus a few winter accessories and, of course, a healthy dose of shearling. You’ll notice this list doesn’t include a pair of pants. This isn’t an invitation to go pants-less this winter, but rather a reflection of the fact that there aren’t any seasonal staples in the category. Utilize your jeans, trousers, or a pair of satin pants for a more holiday-friendly look.

Below, find nine key building blocks for all your wintery outfits and shoppable options that transcend trends. Good luck out there, friends.

Out of all the available outerwear options, I’ve decided that a wool coat is the first and foremost necessity. Every winter, I’m reminded of the importance of stylish jackets that can make or break my cold-weather ensembles. One of these will keep you warm, and if you shop smart, it will last for years.

The entrance to my high school was a long, uphill ramp, and I have many embarrassing memories of near or total wipeouts. I shudder at the thought of walking on an icy sidewalk. So, as an adult, I’ve traded my boat shoes for lug-sole styles (though they would not have been uniform-compliant) that offer more traction. I recommend a weatherproof option that can withstand rain, snow, and ice. As hard as it may be, avoid suede.

Turtlenecks are the heroes of winter layering. Whether you’re bundling up before hitting the slopes, adding warmth under a sweatshirt, or shielding your skin from that scratchy sweater, your winter capsule needs at least one of these bad boys.

I know cashmere can be costly, but it really is worth it. Plus, getting dressed for work during the colder months is so much easier when you have a trusted lineup of sweaters and knit tops.

Vince Boiled Cashmere Turtleneck Pullover

A personal favorite, I confess! I adore a full-length, long-sleeve dress for the winter months. At a time when I can hardly be bothered to change out of pajamas, slipping into a dress can have a transformative effect. And there are so many delectable options to be had.

Everyone has their own opinion on furs—faux or real. Regardless of where you stand, this is the one time of year when you can lean into this look. Here at ELLE, we have a policy against real fur, so you can rest assured that these options are all faux.

Balaclavas, hoods, or ushankas—I’m not picky. Better yet, why not have a few options on hand? By avoiding them to steer clear of hat hair, you’re only hurting yourself. These make such a difference in staying warm.

Be it a scarf coat or a homemade take on Lenny Kravitz’s giant wrap, your arsenal isn’t complete without one.

If you want to do après-ski correctly, you’ll need a bag with a touch of shearling. Options abound this time of year, so feel free to be selective. I’ve rounded up a few favorites here.
