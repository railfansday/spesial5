Cardinale, Krösche, Endrick: Sky journalist reveals Milan’s plans ahead of busy period

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It will be a busy next few days in the offices of Casa Milan with a number of developments expected, according to Sky’s Peppe Di Stefano.

While Milan continue to play twice a week on the field, there is a lot going on away from the pitch too. Rumours are still swirling about the arrival of Markus Krösche as the new Head of Football, as well as some ambitious transfer targets in view of the winter window.

What seems certain above all is that Gerry Cardinale is more involved than he’s ever been when it comes to events at Milan, and he even provided a comment after the comeback in Piraeus last night. The coming weeks could bring further steps forward in the project, it appears.

Peppe Di Stefano spoke live on Sky after Milan’s victory against Olympiacos to get their Europa League campaign back up and running, with MilanNews transcribing what the reporter had to say.

“Cardinale, as far as the entire sporting world is concerned, will appoint a Head of Football who, barring any surprises, will be Markus Krösche,” he said.

“He will clearly work alongside the structure he created on that famous May 24-25, namely Bobby Gardiner and Estevao, with the scouting team, and probably with other collaborators as well. It remains to be seen whether or not he will arrive with one or two collaborators.

“What is certain is that he should arrive in Milan for the first time next week, and then the official announcement is nearing. As I was telling you, six months have passed since that famous post-Milan-Cagliari game, and I can assure you, and I can also testify to a bit of the change in Gerry Cardinale.

“[He was] Very instinctive – I say this with a smile – very American, full of slogans in the early days of that revolution, now much more cautious, much more thoughtful, much more attentive. He understood that a field manager who lived the field was needed, and Krösche will arrive also and above all for this.

“He will be alongside the coach, he will be alongside the team, he will obviously direct the transfer market, but not only the transfer market. He will have a very important role within the Milan world, but what I can say is that Cardinale will no longer delegate like before.

“I understand that this time he doesn’t want to be serious: this time he wants to put his face on the line, he wants to put his money and also his commitment. He will travel and continue to travel. From Italy, from Milan to New York and from New York to Milan, as if it were a simple Milan-Rome journey, and he’ll do it practically every week because he doesn’t want to take a step back.

“So, ok, Krösche, but Milan remains and will remain in his hands, in my opinion, including his decisions. In fact, Endrick was an idea born even before Krösche, and I believe that Milan is pursuing it and will continue to pursue it in the coming weeks.”
