Dybala: “We respect PSG, but we musn’t fear them.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Ahead of Wednesday’s Champions League match at the Parc des Princes against PSG, Paulo Dybala spoke in an interview to be published on December 12th on France Football.

“La Joya” spoke about the European champions and the attitude the entire team must have against such a strong club.

On PSG, Dybala said, “Undoubtedly the best team in Europe right now, maintaining impressive consistency, with incredible players led by a fantastic coach. The players take to the pitch with their eyes closed, the team plays at full speed: the way they press, the way they attack as a team. The forwards and wingers get the ball and immediately create space. You can’t leave them space, otherwise they’ll punish you instantly.”

“It’s an excellent test to gauge our level. We have to respect them, that’s for sure. We’re fully aware of their strength. But we shouldn’t be afraid of anyone. We have our weapons. I have full confidence in my teammates, our coach, and our preparation for a match like this. We showed we’re strong against Real Madrid.”
