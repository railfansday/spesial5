Predicting how the USA would line up under Guardiola if dream appointment succeeds

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Has anyone considered how the USA feel in all of this? They’re about to lose one of the most high-profile managers in the history of their men’s soccer team.

Manchester United are hurtling towards the appointment of Mauricio Pochettino at long last. He’s been on their radar for ages and now looks set to get the job.

But United’s gain will be the USMNT’s loss. Pochettino was a big appointment in 2024 in terms of global pedigree after the sacking of Gregg Berhalter and now US Soccer chiefs will have to find a suitable successor.

And according to reports, they’re dreaming big again. Even bigger this time, in fact. They’ve been talking about Pep Guardiola and, according to The Athletic, are likely to place him near the top of their wish list – probably after realising he’s pretty damn good.

It’d be ambitious. Guardiola walked away from management in the summer when he brought an end to his decade in charge of Manchester City. After all he achieved there, and at Barcelona and Bayern Munich before, he’s a GOAT contender when it comes to managers.

He’s earned a break from the touchline, but we wonder if the lower intensity of an international job might just reel him back in.

In the unlikely event the USA were to succeed in appointing him, here’s how we reckon he could set them up.

Guardiola has actually worked with an American goalkeeper before with Man City, but Zack Steffen has only played once for his country in the past four years.

Matt Freese is currently number one for the USA, but the New York City keeper isn’t the most convincing with the ball at his feet.

That’s usually a problem for Guardiola, who wasn’t afraid to bin off a long-serving starter in Joe Hart at the Etihad when he took charge over similar concerns.

He later regretted his treatment of Hart, so might not be so hasty in future, but there are other options waiting to take over in the American setup.

Kochen, currently on loan at Lyngby from Barcelona, is someone who might intrigue him. The 20-year-old has a better ball-playing reputation and will be looking to establish himself as the long-term number one for his country.

No need for a change at right-back. Dest is a pretty easy pick, nearly 50 caps into his international career.

Whether it was true or not, who knows, but there’s no doubting the 29-year-old would still be a dependable option at left-back.

Guardiola would know all about what Robinson can offer from his time in the Premier League.
