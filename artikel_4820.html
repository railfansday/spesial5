The Oura Ring 5 Is On Sale for the First Time Ahead of Black Friday

THE OURA RING has been my go-to daily fitness tracker for almost a decade, and for Black Friday, Oura is offering a massive discount on their latest model. Right now, every single style of the Oura Ring 5 is $100 off. That’s anywhere from 20 to 25 percent off something that you can use daily to track your health metrics, from sleep to activity.

If you’ve been interested in getting the Oura Ring 5, now’s your time. The Oura Ring 5 was just released this summer, which is why we haven’t seen any offers like this yet. And after this event ends, it’s unlikely it will go on sale again soon. So if you are looking for its cutting-edge smart ring tech at a discount, this is your best opportunity. Also, heads up: if you have an FSA or HSA plan, Oura Ring 5 is covered.

Here is everything you need to know about the Oura Ring Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale.

The Oura Ring 5 is Oura's latest offering, and a few upgrades earned it a spot on our Best Smart Rings list. This version has no noticeable sensors, while previous models had three interior nodes that could be a bit uncomfortable. It still collects health data seamlessly.

The Oura Ring 5 is lighter and slimmer than any previous models. The ring provides real-time stress tracking, continuous heart-rate monitoring, and more precise temperature sensing. All of this is designed to give you more precise, helpful feedback throughout the day. The ring’s interior has also changed from an epoxy to all-over titanium for a lighter feel.

The charging case is another great upgrade available to Oura Ring 5 users. This case lets you store a charge so you can power up your ring without being limited by nearby outlets. This means you can keep an extra charge for your ring while you're traveling or on the move so you don't miss out on any insights. The new charging dock is also improved and can charge your ring in about an hour.

Oura recommends wearing the ring on your forefinger, since that's where your pulse is strongest, which can mean slightly better data, but really any finger works. I know a few people who use their Oura Ring as their wedding band. So if you know your ring finger size you’re good to go on that.

If you're unsure about your size, it could be a good idea to order one of Oura's sizing kits. It’s now $10 on Amazon, and that gets applied to your future ring purchase, so technically it's "free." And it allows you to lock in the sale.

When you purchase on ouraring.com the process of getting a sizing kit is a little more involved. You order the ring first, and then they send you a sizing kit to use to get your correct size in the system. Depending on which route you choose for the sizing kit, you'll use that same route to purchase your ring when you decide to buy.

If you want to make sure you lock in the sale, you can purchase the size you feel is right, and as long as you return it within 30 days, you can resize after. You can find all of the details on those returns and exchanges on their site.

You might not be looking for a super-slim smart ring. If you’re used to wearing rings with some weight, or you just like something with a little more heft, the Oura Ring 4 is the first version I think is worth keeping, even if you’re buying a new Oura Ring 5.
