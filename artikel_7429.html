Meta says social media is key driver for holiday discovery

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

People who shop via social media are more likely to purchase across multiple categories and self-gift, according to the company.

Meta’s has shared some key data notes on shopping activity in its apps, which could provide additional guidance for brands to assist with their holiday campaign planning.

The data was published earlier this year, and is based on research conducted by Ipsos, which surveyed 14,473 shoppers in 18 countries across North America, Latin America, Europe and Asia-Pacific.

The data found that social media is a key driver of discovery for holiday season shoppers.

As per Meta: “Across all 18 markets surveyed, 37% of festive shoppers use social media for gift discovery and inspiration. That places it on par with search engines (41%) and ahead of TV advertising (27%).”

The data also showed that video is the most influential content type, while high-quality product photography is also a valuable driver.

The report also looked at what drives purchase decisions among social media shoppers.

While it also explored key spending behaviors.

As per the report: “Social shoppers are far more commercially engaged than the average holiday shopper. They plan to spend more (49% intend to increase spending year-on-year, compared to 36% overall), shop across more product categories (5.4 vs 4.3 average) and are more likely to self-gift (83% vs 67%).”

There’s also insight into how shoppers use messaging apps, and what they’re looking for in brand DMs.

Based on these insights, Meta advised marketers to invest in discovery on social platforms, and to use customer endorsements and testimonials to assure potential customers of their quality.

Meta also said that marketers should design their campaigns for cross-category shoppers: “Use consistent creative systems and shoppable catalogues to reduce the friction between ‘that looks interesting’ and ‘I'm buying this’.”

These notes could help to refine holiday campaign approaches.
