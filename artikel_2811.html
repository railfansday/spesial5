Sean Payton: "There's no loophole" in the trade process

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After last week’s failed trade that would have sent Chargers linebacker Daiyan Henley to the Broncos, L.A. coach Jim Harbaugh said that the Broncos used a “loophole” to squeeze the Chargers into better terms before the deal imploded.

The Broncos had no comment last week, and coach Sean Payton hadn’t spoken to reporters because Denver had a Week 10 bye.

On Monday, Payton was available to the media. And the first question asked what changed as to the Henley trade.

“Nothing changed,” Payton said. “It was already stated. Everything was pretty clearly spelled out. I’m not going to get into the specifics. He’s a prospect we thought — and still do [think] — very highly of. It just didn’t work out and it’s not uncommon with trades. That’s kind of where it went, and I’ll leave it at that.”

“There’s no loophole,” Payton said. “It just is part of the trade process every step of the way.”

He’s technically right, but there is a loophole. A team that has buyer’s remorse can try to rely on issues with the physical to negotiate better terms. And an unscrupulous team could finalize a deal before the deadline with the plan of trying to improve the deal later, under the guise of a failed physical.

The easy fix, when it comes to deadline deals, is to take the position that, after the window for making trades closes, the player either passes the physical and the trade goes through as negotiated or the player fails the physical and the deal dies.

There should be no “maybe” box when it comes to physicals. Especially after the period for making trades has ended.
