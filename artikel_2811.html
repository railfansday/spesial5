CB DJ Turner upgraded to full practice for Bengals

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Cornerback DJ Turner’s status took a step in a positive direction on the Bengals’ Thursday practice report.

Turner was listed as a full participant in practice. He was listed as out of practice on Wednesday due to a heel issue, but that upgrade suggests he should be good to go against the Falcons in Madrid this Sunday.

Turner has started all seven games this season. He has 17 tackles, two forced fumbles and five passes defensed.

Defensive tackle Jonathan Allen (rest) and safety Kyle Dugger (quad) did not participate on Thursday. Safety Jordan Battle (hamstring), defensive tackle B.J. Hill (rest), and defensive end Cedric Johnson (ankle, foot) were limited participants.
