Will Anderson to play against Browns

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Texans defensive end Will Anderson was added to the team’s injury report on Thursday and he missed practice on Friday due to his ankle issue, but he will be on the field against the Browns on Sunday.

Anderson was listed as questionable, but he got the green light to play in Cleveland 90 minutes ahead of kickoff. Anderson leads the league with 9.5 sacks this season, so Browns quarterback Deshaun Watson will have his eye on No. 51 as the game unfolds.

The Browns had wide receiver Denzel Boston listed as questionable with a toe injury that kept him off the practice field for two days this week. Boston is also active and Watson will be looking his way as long as he’s able to avoid Anderson.
