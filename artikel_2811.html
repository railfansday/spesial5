Hawaii Football Final – Nevada reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The University of Hawaii football team suffered another frustrating setback Saturday, falling 16-8 to Nevada in freezing conditions in Reno.

The loss dropped the Rainbow Warriors to 3-8 overall and 2-6 in Mountain West play as they closed out their conference schedule.

Despite another strong effort from the defense, Hawaii struggled to generate offense. The Rainbow Warriors held Nevada to 21 points or fewer for the eighth time this season, but the offense was limited to 182 total yards and failed to score more than one touchdown for the seventh time in 2026.

The defeat was made more difficult by an injury to starting quarterback Micah Alejado, who left the game in the second half and was taken to a Reno hospital with a leg injury.

Bjorn Jurgensen finished the game at quarterback, throwing one touchdown and one interception as Hawaii came up short once again.

Following the game, Rainbow Warriors co-captain and linebacker Jamih Otis spoke with KHON2 Sports Reporter Emily Paulson.

The latest episode of Hawaii Football Final, a Hawaii Sports 2Night production, features KHON2 Sports Director Rob DeMello and analyst Rich Miano breaking down the loss to Nevada, answering viewer-submitted questions in the HFF Mailbox segment and looking ahead to what's next for the Rainbow Warriors.

Miano, a former University of Hawaii player and coach and an 11-year NFL veteran, joins DeMello for an in-depth discussion of the team's latest performance and the challenges facing the program.
