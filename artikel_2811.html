Lafayette-area high school football scoreboard for LHSAA First-round playoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The road to the Superdome will be filled with elite matchups and stars being born on gridiron as every team hopes to make the 2026 Allstate Sugarbowl LHSAA Prep Classic.

Thirty-one teams hailing from Lafayette Parish and its surrounding areas earned a spot across the eight brackets, with Notre Dame as the only area team to earn a No. 1 seed in the Select Division III bracket.

Next to the Pios, Westgate and Lafayette Christian Academy earned the next highest seed at No. 2 in Non-Select DII and Select III brackets, respectively. Overall, eight teams hailing from the 337 earned a bye to start the playoffs including Eunice, Kaplan, St. Thomas More, Lafayette Renaissance Charter Academy and Westminster Christian Academy-Lafayette. Notre Dame, Westgate and Eunice all enter the playoffs fresh off going undefeated in the regular season.

But all those records are thrown out the window and all that lies ahead is win or go home. Only a handful of teams will see their postseason dreams end in victory but it all starts now in the opening round.

With the first round of the playoffs set to kick off this Thursday and Friday, here are the matchups happening across the area through the start of the LHSAA football playoffs.
