Cowboys injuries: 49ers game status for George Pickens, Joey Porter Jr.

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Just when you were out on the Dallas Cowboys, the NFC East pulls back in. The Washington Commanders did the Cowboys a tremendous favor last night by dealing the New York Giants a loss on Thursday Night Football.

If Dallas can somehow win at home versus the San Francisco 49ers, they could be right back in the mix for a return to the playoffs. Both Dallas and San Francisco are dealing with a lot of injuries at many key positions. Here's what we know after Friday's report.

The Cowboy are pretty banged up entering Sunday's game. Donovan Ezeiruaku (Ankle), Jake Ferguson (Knee/Ankle), P.J. Locke (Concussion), Tyler Goodson (Ankle/Knee), and Jonathan Mingo (Ankle) have all been ruled out. Joey Porter Jr. (Ankle) was limited today at practice and is cited as questionable to play. George Pickens (Hamstring) and KaVontae Turpin (Ribs) carry no injury designation for this week's game and will play.

For the 49ers, Mike Evans has been ruled out with his Achillles injury. Several other veterans for San Francisco are listed as questionable. Deommodore Lenoir (Foot), Trent Williams (Shoulder), Dre Greenlaw (Knee), Renardo Green (Back), and Upton Stout (Shoulder/Neck) are all cited as questionable. Deebo Samuel will play against the Cowboys.
