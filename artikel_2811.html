DJ Moore limited, Dion Dawkins did not practice on Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Bills have a lengthy injury report to begin Week 9, with the club playing the Vikings on Monday night.

Receiver DJ Moore (shoulder), receiver Keon Coleman (shoulder), tight end Dalton Kincaid (chest), and offensive tackle Spencer Brown (back) were all limited. Returning from injured reserve, defensive back Jordan Hancock (hamstring) was limited as well.

Offensive tackle Dion Dawkins (rib), defensive lineman Greg Gaines (calf), running back Ty Johnson (illness), receiver Joshua Palmer (hamstring), and offensive lineman Alec Anderson (ankle) all did not participate.

Cornerback Dee Alford (ankle), linebacker Joe Andreessen (knee), outside linebacker Bradley Chubb (chest/thumb), defensive lineman Ed Oliver (knee), outside linebacker TJ Parker (knee), guard Ar’maj Reed-Adams (back/elbow), defensive lineman T.J. Sanders (shoulder), and receiver Tyrell Shavers (knee) are full. Shavers is still returning from the physically unable to perform list.
