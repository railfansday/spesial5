Ian Rapoport thinks he knows which NFL team Lamar Jackson will be playing for next year

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

There has been much speculation about the future of Lamar Jackson.

Nick Wright recently shared his view that 'Jackson is the week one starting quarterback next year for the Miami Dolphins'.

Meanwhile, Stephen A. Smith suggested he does not believe that the quarterback has played his final game for the Baltimore Ravens despite a recent injury issue.

Now, Ian Rapoport has come out to say he thinks Jackson will still be with the Ravens next year.

In a video shared by the NFL Network on YouTube, he said: "Well, let me start with the Lamar Jackson piece. I do not see a scenario where Lamar Jackson is not the quarterback of the Baltimore Ravens next year.

"Yes, it's the NFL. Yes, we've been surprised. We've seen massive trades plenty of times you've had great players get traded where you thought there's no way this guy goes.

"Could that happen? Anything is possible. But I do not see it. I see a situation where Lamar Jackson goes into the off-season, re-ups with the Baltimore Ravens, there's mutual interest in doing so, and then keeps it moving forward."

Jackson joined the Ravens in 2018 and has since gone on to secure the MVP award on two occasions: 2019 and 2023.

But now that he is out injured, his team seems to be struggling, most recently losing 33-10 against the Jacksonville Jaguars.
