Penn State Opens as Favorite Over Maryland in Regular Season Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Penn State is favored to end the regular season in the win column, despite a crushing last-second loss to Rutgers. The Nittany Lions have opened as a 7.5-point favorite on the road against Maryland, per FanDuel.

Penn State entered Saturday on a three-game game win streak and owned an 18-game winning streak over Rutgers dating back to 1988. However, the Nittany Lions fell to Rutgers despite being a 22.5-point favorite over the Scarlet Knights. Penn State started the game off with a 16-0 lead, but allowed 10 quick points before halftime to allow Rutgers back into the game. The Nittany Lions also held a 24-10 lead in the fourth quarter before allowing the Scarlet Knights to score the final 15 points of the game. Rutgers game-winning score came on a lateral on the final play of regulation, followed by a walk-off two-point conversion to pull off the 25-24 shocker in the 2026 Beaver Stadium finale.

The result overshadowed a stellar performance by linebacker Tony Rojas, who finished the game with 15 tackles, 1.5 sacks, 2.5 TFLs, 3 quarterback hurries and two pass breakups.

Maryland enters the game with a 4-7 record. They had previously won two straight over Purdue and Wisconsin before falling to #18 USC, 35-21, on Saturday. The Terps are currently being lead by interim head coach Aazaar Abdul-Rahim, who took over following Mike Locksley's midseason dismissal. Maryland is undergoing its first head coaching search since hiring Lockley in 2015.

Penn State and Maryland will kick off at 3:30 p.m. on Saturday, with coverage provided by FS1.
