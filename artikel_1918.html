Suarez planning longer stay with Miami alongside Messi

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Luis Suarez confirmed he was in talks with Inter Miami to continue beyond his current contract, while noting neither he nor Lionel Messi had plans to retire any time soon as they set their sights on the MLS Cup again.

Miami are the reigning MLS Cup champions and begin their playoff run in Round One against the Columbus Crew on Saturday.

The Herons finished second in the Eastern Conference behind Supporters' Shield winners Nashville SC despite winning just one of their last four regular-season matches (D2 L1).

Messi was a driving force for the team, with Suarez, whose current contract runs out at the end of this year, also chipping in with 17 goals, and the Uruguayan insists the pair are not thinking about hanging up their boots in the near future.

"As long as you feel you are able to contribute and help the team, we are talking with the club about continuing to move forward, because I feel I am in good shape and the club is happy," Suarez told 'Tot Costa' on Catalunya Radio, as quoted by Goal.

"It's difficult, but we [Suarez and Messi] will carry on as long as circumstances allow us.

"As long as we are enjoying it and continue to have fun, and we keep that competitive edge and the desire to win, we won't stop, neither him nor me nor anyone who has that ambition to achieve things."

Miami face a tough test from Columbus, who finished the regular season on a six-match winning streak which saw them claim seventh place by a single point.

They edged the Chicago Fire 4-3 on Decision Day, and though goalkeeper Patrick Schulte would have liked to avoid Messi and co. until later in the playoffs, he is thrilled they managed to avoid the Wild Card round.

"To be honest, you never want to play against one of the best in the world who can change a game on its head," Schulte told MLSsoccer.com.

"But I think for us, it wasn't even the matchup of who we were going to play. It was more, let's be grateful that we're going to get the opportunity to play in the playoffs. We could have easily been playing in the [Wild Card] game.

"You're going to have to play anyone at any point if you want to be lifting the trophy on December 18. So I think that's been our motto: we're obviously going to have to play who we play. No matter who it is, we just gotta embrace it."

Messi was the highest scorer in the regular season, netting 26 goals and overperforming his 20.9 expected goals (+5.1 differential).

He also had the most shots (183) and shots on target (75), while he averaged a goal every 102.5 minutes.

Schulte more than played his part in Columbus' impressive finish to the regular season, keeping three clean sheets across the six matches.

He kept eight clean sheets in total, making 108 saves, and he will be important if Columbus want to keep out Miami's front line.

This will be the first-ever playoff meeting between Miami and Columbus.

Columbus are unbeaten in the last two meetings between these sides (W1 D1), coming on the back of a four-game losing streak against Miami in MLS.

Miami have never lost at home to the Crew, though, winning four and drawing two of the previous six such meetings between the pair.

The Herons have six wins, nine draws, and two losses in 17 MLS contests at home this term, while Columbus have five wins, three draws, and nine losses on the road this season in league matches, though they have won each of the last three.
