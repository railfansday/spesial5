46 photos from Eastbourne Borough's 4-4 thriller at Enfield Town

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The sides were second and third at the start of play and served up a memorable match.

Terrell Egbri put the Sports ahead after eight minutes but Thomas Avgoustidis quickly levelled for Enfield, only for an own goal to restore Borough’s lead midway through the first half.

It looked to have swung Town’s way when Luke May-Parrott scored twice in the opening 17 minutes of the second half and Avgoustidis got his second to make it 4-2 on 71.

But Kai Corbett wasn’t in the mood to be on the losing side and struck in the 74th and 81st minutes to make it 4-4. Enfield stay second but Borough slip to fourth and now turn their attentions to a home clash with Aveley on Tuesday night.
