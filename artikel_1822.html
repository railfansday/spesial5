Jeglertz not worried despite Man City’s ‘wake-up call’ loss to Spurs

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Andree Jeglertz wants Manchester City to use their loss to Tottenham as a "wake-up call" as they missed the chance to go top of the Women's Super League.

Spurs stunned City at home, coming from behind to beat the reigning champions 3-1 and deal Jeglertz his first home league loss in charge of the club.

City started well, with Khadija Shaw breaking the deadlock just 10 minutes in. Matilda Vinberg was caught in possession, and Jade Rose set up the Jamaican for her 16th WSL goal against Spurs.

But the visitors responded well, with Cathinka Tandberg drawing them level five minutes before the break, and City offered little in their attempts to regain their lead after the break.

But with 16 minutes to play, Vinberg – who was a last-minute replacement after Olivia Holdt was taken ill – put Spurs in front before she then teed up substitute Jessica Naz for the insurance goal five minutes later.

City have failed to win a WSL home game under Jeglertz for the first time, ending the second-longest winning streak on home turf in the competition (17 in a row).

Despite the loss, Jeglertz is not too concerned about the result as he feels he will get a strong response from his side.

"We want more, and I think no matter what, we're such a good team with great players that we need more from each other," Jeglertz said.

"Sometimes it's not your day, but sometimes also, it's almost a wake-up call. We know this league is tough, and we won’t take anything for granted.

"If you don't have a good day, the opponent will punish you, which I think they did. We just need to deal with that in a good way and then move on from it.

"It's always going to be like that; it's a tough league. It'll be a long race, so we need to deal with it in a good way and come back. I'm not worried about that."

A win would have taken City above Chelsea, who dropped points against Liverpool on Saturday, but they remain two points behind the league leaders.

Their city rivals Manchester United close in on the top three, though, with a dramatic 2-1 win over Birmingham City.

Having gone 1-0 down inside four minutes thanks to Lee Geum-Min's goal, United rallied with a late comeback, with Simi Awujo netting a 94th-minute winner after Elisabeth Terland had equalised.

Arsenal's struggles at the Emirates continued, however, as they remain without a league win on home soil this term after a 0-0 draw with West Ham.
