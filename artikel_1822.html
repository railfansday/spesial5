Fernandes: Not the right time for Man Utd contract talks

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes has put contract talks on hold.

The Red Devils, who have just appointed Mauricio Pochettino as their new head coach, sit in the Premier League relegation zone ahead of facing Liverpool on Sunday.

Fernandes, who was the standout performer in the Premier League last term, has not been at his usual best.

However, the Portugal international has still contributed directly to nine goals in 14 games (six goals, three assists), while only four players in the Premier League can better his 23 chances created this term.

Fernandes' deal expires at the end of the season, but even though United have offered him a new contract, the 32-year-old has paused those negotiations.

"The club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay," Fernandes told Sky Sports.

"There's no negotiations ongoing, but I've seen people saying that 'he's asked for more money, he's asked for more years'. If I wanted more money, I wouldn't be here.

"I had somewhere to go to get much more money than being at Manchester United. I could have been in a place that is much warmer than Manchester.

"Two summers ago, three summers ago, last summer, I always had the chance to leave and I always chose to be here.

"We spoke about the contract at the end of last season.

"We came in at the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to one side because I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations.

"The most important thing now is that the club gets back to winning games."
