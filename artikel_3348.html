‘Jumanji’ Officially Expands With New Release Ahead of Final Movie

With only a month until Jumanji's fourth and final film, Jumanji: Open World hits theaters, fans will have the chance to immerse themselves in its adventurous world. A brand-new VR experience has been announced, letting players face challenges, bond with exotic animals, and solve puzzles as the "fate of Jumanji" falls into their hands. If you want to know what it's like to be Dr. Bravestone, this would be your chance.

The Jumanji film series began in 1995 and was based on the 1981 children's picture book by Chris Van Allsburg. The movie stars Robin Williams, Kirsten Dunst, David Alan Grier, and Bonnie Hunt, and the story follows two kids who play a magic board game whose effects spill into real life, like animals and hunters appearing in their world. In 2017, the board game returned, this time transformed into a video game, where a new generation of players were sucked in and had to complete it to get home.

Now, free-roam VR entertainment company Zero Latency has announced a brand-new game coming out this December called Jumanji: The Lost Levels. According to a press release, the company worked closely with Sony Pictures to create an original story that ensures that it "captures the wild, funny and chaotic Jumanji energy." This is what the game's description has to say:

"Players will navigate the perils of Jumanji across a series of escalating challenges: forge bonds with exotic animal companions, survive an exhilarating river rafting gauntlet, bust out their best moves in a snake dance-off (yes, really), crack the secrets of ancient temple puzzles, saddle up and ride dinosaurs through uncharted jungle, and face off against an epic boss battle with the fate of Jumanji itself hanging in the balance."

The trailer for the upcoming game shows players controlling the same avatars seen in the movie as they explore the jungle and dangerous locations that thrust them straight into the adventure. Zero Latency first started in 2013, and since then, it has expanded worldwide, with over 30 locations in the U.S. alone, with six more venues opening soon.

Jumanji: Open World is the fifth installment and final entry in the Jumanji franchise. The movie takes place immediately after the events of Jumanji: The Next Level, as the world of Jumanji breaks free from its video game console and enters the real world. The video game avatars of Dr. Smolder Bravestone (Dwayne Johnson), Professor Sheldon Oberon (Jack Black), Franklin Finbar (Kevin Hart), and Ruby Roundhouse (Karen Gillan) roam the real world in "demo mode." It's now up to Spencer Gilpin (Alex Wolff) to set things right, and joining him in the game are his mother, Janice (Marin Hinkle), kitchen worker Donatas (Dan Hildebrand), and Tyson Shepherd (Jack Jewkes).

Jumanji: Open World will enter theaters on December 25, 2026, while Zero Latency's Jumanji: The Lost Levels will be released worldwide on December 4, 2026. Follow Collider for more updates.
