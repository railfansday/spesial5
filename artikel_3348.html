Dua Lipa, Tame Impala, SZA to Headline 2027 Coachella

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Dua Lipa, Tame Impala, and SZA will top the lineup when Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival returns to Indio, California in 2027. The fest returns the weekends of April 9-11 and April 16-18, 2027.

Dua Lipa headlines Friday. She will be joined by Madonna, Zara Larsson, Steve Lacy, Jisoo, Diljit Dosanjh, Peggy Gou, Kehlani, Solid Grooves, Giorgio Moroder, Social Distortion, Pinegrove, Angine de Poitrine, Leon Thomas, Drain Gang, Snow Strippers, Mike D 5D, and many more.

On Saturday, Tame Impala leads the festivities. The night’s lineup also includes Phoebe Bridgers, Weezer, Tiësto, Martin Garrix, Robyn, Role Model, Adéla, Måneskin, Solomun, NCT, Enigma, Underworld, Charlotte de Witte, Leon Bridges, Cloonee, Magdalena Bay, Barry Can’t Swim, and more.

SZA tops the bill on Sunday, following sets by Don Toliver, Dom Dolla, Kygo, Empire of the Sun, Four Tet, the Neighbourhood, Becky G, Babymonster, Amelie Lens x Sara Landry, Remi Wolf, Tove Lo, Victoria Monét, EsDeeKid, Caroline Polachek, Chase & Status, and Muna, among others.

Passes go on sale Friday, Nov. 13 at 12 p.m. PT via the festival’s official website. Official hotel packages — which includes festival passes, local lodging and transportation — for both weekends may be purchased now.

Daddy Yankee is also set to appear, though it’s unclear what dates he will be performing. This also marks Madonna’s return to the dance tent, for the first time in more than two decades. Last year, she made a surprise appearance during Sabrina Carpenter’s set during Weekend Two. “I have a few things I wanna get off my chest,” Madonna said at the time. “Twenty years ago today, I performed at Coachella. I was in the dance tent, and it was the first time I performed Confessions on the Dance Floor, part one, in America,” Madonna continued. “That was such a thrill for me, so you can imagine what a thrill it is for me to be back 20 years later in the same boots, the same corset, the jacket I had on earlier, the same Gucci jacket. This will not only be Dua Lipa’s first time headlining Coachella, but her first proper performance at the festival ever. She did previously appear onstage at the desert early in her career, joining DJ-producer Martin Garrix for a performance of “Scared to Be Lonely” at Coachella 2017. (This was just a few months before Lipa released her self-titled debut album.)

Lipa released her most recent album, Radical Optimism, in 2024, embarking on a world tour that ended the following year. After the trek, she released a concert film, Dua Lipa (Live From Mexico), stayed busy with various non-musical projects, including her popular book club, and married longtime partner, Callum Turner.

Coincidentally, one of Lipa’s most recent onstage appearances was this past May when she joined Tame Impala for a few songs at their show in London. Tame Impala frontman, Kevin Parker, worked extensively as a producer and songwriter on Radical Optimism.

As for Tame Impala, the Australian psych-pop band are longtime Coachella favorites and this will be their second time serving as festival headliners. Tame Impala released their most recent album, Deadbeat, in 2025, and have tour dates in support of the record scheduled through next June. Deadbeat has become one of the group’s most popular albums to date, spawning a couple of hits, including “Loser,” My Old Way,” and “Dracula,” which was boosted by a blockbuster remix featuring Jennie.

Grammy-winning artist SZA last released an album, SOS, in 2022, and wrapped the accompanying tour in 2024. She was rumored to be among the headliners, and seemed to confirm the news herself by dropping a comment in an Instagram post. The leak, according to sources, prompted Coachella parent company Goldenvoice to scramble and announce its full lineup in due haste.
