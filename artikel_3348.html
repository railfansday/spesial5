Pokémon Officially Brings Back a Legendary Alongside 2 New Mega Evolutions

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

Pokémon GO players have plenty to look forward to before the franchise wraps up its 30th anniversary celebrations. Between the introduction of new Mega Evolutions in Pokémon Legends: Z-A and its Mega Dimension DLC, some Pokémon that haven't received much love in years are finally getting their moment. That includes a Ghost-type from the Sinnoh region and one of the more unusual Dragon-types introduced in Pokémon Sun and Moon.

The winter months will also give Trainers another opportunity to add one of Unova’s most powerful Legendary Pokémon to their collection. Introduced in Pokémon Black and White, Kyurem became the mascot of both sequels thanks to its ability to fuse with Reshiram and Zekrom. Its return is especially exciting for Trainers still looking to collect its Shiny variant, and December’s encounter won’t require a paid pass.

Pokémon GO’s new season, Daydream Destinations, begins December 1, 2026, at 10 a.m. local time and runs through March 1, 2027. The season will introduce Mega Froslass and Mega Drampa through dedicated Raid Day events, alongside Max Battle Day events featuring Gigantamax Inteleon and Gigantamax Toxtricity. Meanwhile, the free December GO Pass features a guaranteed Kyurem encounter, with the possibility of encountering a Shiny. Players can progress through the pass until January 5.

December's GO Pass will also reward Trainers with Rare Candy XL, a Premium Battle Pass, and a Lucky Egg. Those who upgrade to the $7.99 GO Pass Deluxe can unlock a Timed Incubator at Rank 20, giving them an additional unlimited-use Incubator until January 12. That should come in handy once players reach Rank 75, which grants increased XP and Stardust from hatching Eggs. Other milestone bonuses include double Daily Adventure Incense duration at Rank 50 and increased Gift limits at Rank 25. Several Community Days have also been confirmed for the new season:

The biggest event of the season, however, will take Trainers back to the Alola region. Pokémon GO Tour: Alola will take place in Los Angeles and Kaohsiung from February 19–21, followed by a global event on February 27–28. Inspired by the seventh generation of Pokémon games, the celebration will feature Alolan Pokémon and new raid opportunities, with additional Special Research and increased Shiny encounter chances available through a paid global event ticket.

Pokémon GO: Daydream Destinations begins December 1, 2026.
