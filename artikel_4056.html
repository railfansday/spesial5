Rumour: Atlus May Be Gearing Up For A Huge Persona 6 Reveal

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After what felt like an age waiting for the Persona franchise to make its Switch debut, Sega and Atlus are now all-in on the Switch 2. Persona 4 Revival is scheduled to launch on 20th May 2027, while Persona 6 has also been confirmed for the hybrid console.

Atlus is in full marketing mode for Persona 4 Revival, but our pals over at Push Square have reason to believe that the company may be gearing up to properly reveal Persona 6 in the coming weeks.

This comes from Reddit user ronakwya, who notes that Atlus recently updated the official website for Persona 6. Apparently, however, this was the kind of update that allows Atlus to perform significant changes to the website in preparation for a big public reveal.

Ronakwya has also pointed to the gameplay reveal of Persona 4 Revival as a potential hint. Its own website was also updated in a similar manner roughly one month prior to its big reveal at the Xbox Games Showcase in June 2026.

So, which big gaming event is taking place in just less than a month from now..? Yep, The Game Awards 2026.

This all seems incredibly convenient though, and to be honest we're a little sceptical. As pointed out by Push Square, it would make little sense for Atlus to pull attention away from Persona 4 Revival at this stage to host a full reveal for Persona 6.

In our opinion, we could definitely see a similar reveal during the Xbox Games Showcase in 2027 after the release of Revival in May (February for other platforms). We're happy to be wrong, as we're proper itching to see some gameplay from the next mainline entry, but we're not holding our breath.
