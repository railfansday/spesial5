The Super Cucco-findin' Game in the OoT Remake depends on speed and luck

Super Cuccos in Zelda Ocarina of Time Remake are part of a game given to you by Talon at the Lon-Lon Ranch. Like in the original OoT, the Super Cucco-findin' Game is all about trying to pick out the three Super Cuccos from the group of chicken-like birds, going largely by luck and by efficiency. If you can find all three, Talon will reward you with some milk straight from the ranch, so I'll explain for you how to get these rewards as easily as possible.

Winning the Super Cucco-findin' Game in the Zelda Ocarina of Time Remake is about observation at first, then about speed and a little luck.

To win, you need to pick up all three Super Cuccos in 30 seconds, but there are no visual indicators that actually mark them out differently from the others.

There is a certain amount of luck and speed involved here, and unlike the Zelda Ocarina of Time Treasure Chest Game, there's no item that will allow you to cheat the system and effectively guarantee victory.

Each attempt will cost you 10 Rupees, but if you win, you'll get A Bottle of Lon-Lon Milk, a consumable that restores hearts when drunk. He'll also sell you refills from this point on for 30 Rupees each.

The more important reward is actually the bottle itself. These containers are rare in OoT, and can be reused to hold all kind of useful items, from spirits to bugs to potions. Treasure them!
