My AT Thru-Hike: By the Numbers

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Hey folks, back at it with another post here.  I’ve been meaning to write a recap of my hike pretty much sense I finished hiking the trail back in August, but alas, I am only getting to it now. Now that I am settled firmly into post-trail life, back home and working again, I took the chance to analyze my hike a bit. Compiled below are some interesting or funny insights (at least to me) of my thru-hike of the Appalachian Trail.  Hopefully you will find this insightful if you are planning your own AT thru-hike or at least entertaining…

Here is a complete breakdown of the costs that I incurred while on my At thru-hike.  While I don’t think I was egregious in my spending, I didn’t exactly skimp either.  I could have easily shaved off some expenses, especially in the food department.  However, after long hiking days, I simply enjoyed the comforts provided by cold beverages and warm food!

Right out of the pouch, just like God intended!

Along the trail I saw and experienced much, including wildlife!  The highlight here were the Fishers!  I only got a quick glimpse of both of them, as they are quite elusive creatures, but I will take what I can get!  And while I saw quite a few deer all along the trail, the deer in Shenandoah and the Smokies were shameless!  Often coming right up to my tent at night or in the morning, including one that didn’t even flinch when I popped my head out and was close enough I could have pet it!

This was surprisingly close to Tinker Cliffs in the Virginia Triple Crown area!

For sleeping along the trail I definitely preferred my tent over shelters, and spent a good deal of the trail actively avoiding shelters!  In Maine I softened this stance quite a bit for a few reasons.  The first being that Maine was the rainiest state of my hike, so there was some shelter hopping from one to another in the attempt to keep some of my gear and myself dry.  The second being I was exhausted a lot towards the end and saving a little bit of time and energy by sleeping in the shelter was worth it.  And finally, towards the end I just couldn’t bother to care about mice running around me at night that much.  Plus, the rest of the Board (Shooter and Birdie) was doing it!

First night on trail at Hidden Pond during the March Megastorm. I met friends that I would keep through the end of the trail. Especially Shooter, Queso, Zipper, and Beer Run!
