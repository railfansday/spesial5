Norwich City sent clear Ante Crnac transfer message ahead of January

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Norwich City have been told that Ante Crnac would benefit from a loan move away from Carrow Road in the January transfer window.

There was a lot of excitement when the Canaries spent an initial £8.5m to sign the attacker from Polish side Rakow in the summer of 2024, as it was a significant outlay for the Championship side.

So, even though the 22-year-old was seen as a long-term investment, there was an expectation that he could quickly become a key player for the team.

However, it was a mixed first season for Crnac in English football under Johannes Hoff Thorup, as the Croatian managed seven goals and five assists in 38 league games.

Whilst Crnac struggled for consistency, there were flashes of his undoubted talent, and it was hoped that the adaptation period would help him moving forward.

Yet, in October of the 2025/26 campaign, Crnac suffered a serious ACL injury that would rule him out for around ten months.

Pleasingly, Crnac has now returned to full fitness, and he has featured in six Championship games so far, as well as scoring in the EFL Cup against Cardiff City.

Despite his return to fitness, boss Philippe Clement is understandably managing Crnac carefully, and the reality is that the ex-Slaven Belupo man has fallen down the pecking order during his time out injured.

Therefore, Crnac hasn’t actually made the matchday squad in recent games, and he knows he faces a real battle to get into the XI.

With that in mind, it’s perhaps no surprise that Norwich fan pundit Zeke Downes identified Crnac as the one player who could do with leaving in January when asked by FLW.

“I would say that one player who could do with a loan is Ante Crnac because the timings haven’t really worked for him. He looked really good under Thorup, but he then got his injury under Liam Manning, I think.

“So he missed out on that whole period with Clement, and I think that he has had such a long time out with such a bad injury, that has then also coincided with us having more depth now.

“We’ve got three other strikers who are really good, which means he’s not going to get much game time there. We also have good options on the wing, and they’re all playing really well, and we’re in a good place right now.

“That means Crnac isn’t playing much, and he’s not always making the squad, but that’s mainly down to the fact that we’ve got a big squad now, so players don’t make it. That doesn’t mean they’re not good enough, it’s just that we’ve got depth now, which is needed in a long season.

“For Crnac’s confidence, experience and match fitness, I’d say a loan would be the best option, and then get him back at the end of the season when he has had more game time, and we can see what he could offer.”

Given the nature of his previous injury, Crnac will be desperate to get back playing regularly, but most would agree that Norwich have better options in attack right now.

So, a loan move could make a lot of sense, and Norwich certainly won’t be writing off the 22-year-old, who still has a contract at Carrow Road until the summer of 2028, with the Canaries also having a 12-month option.
