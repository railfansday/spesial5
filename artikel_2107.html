West Ham Exclusive: Scott Parker Emerges As Leading Candidate

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Claret & Hugh can reveal this morning that West Ham United have already held discussions with a number of potential replacements for Nuno Espírito Santo, who was dismissed with immediate effect earlier today.

Some of those meetings took place as recently as yesterday, although the club have been describing the discussions as a sounding-out process rather than formal interviews.

Nevertheless, it appears the decision to part company with Nuno was effectively made following Saturday’s 3-1 defeat against West Bromwich Albion, with the Hammers hierarchy already exploring their options before this morning’s announcement.

A West Ham insider told Claret & Hugh this morning:

“There are a few candidates, at this moment Scott Parker is the most likely but it needs to be rubber stamped.”

Former West Ham midfielder Scott Parker has enjoyed something of a chequered managerial career, but his record of winning promotion to the Premier League is likely to appeal to those making the decision.

Parker has previously guided Burnley, Fulham and Bournemouth into the top flight, giving him valuable experience of precisely the challenge West Ham now face.

Former Hammer Scott Parker knows how to get out of the Championship

That promotion record could prove particularly attractive to Daniel Křetínský and the club’s other decision-makers, with an immediate Premier League return remaining the priority.

However, our source has stressed that nothing has been finalised and other candidates remain under consideration.

Mark Noble, Steve Potts and Gerard Prenderville will take temporary charge of preparations for tomorrow night’s Championship fixture away at Cardiff City.

The appointment process is understood to be a collective one, involving shareholders, recruitment chief Nils Koppen and chief executive Karim Virani.

With discussions already underway, West Ham are clearly wasting little time in searching for Nuno’s successor.
