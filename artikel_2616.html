Mike Evans, Dre Greenlaw don't practice Wednesday, 49ers hope they can on Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Wide receiver Mike Evans didn't travel with the 49ers for last Sunday's game in Dallas, but he was in Colorado Springs with the team as they prepare for their game against the Vikings in Mexico City.

Evans remained out of practice due to the Achilles injury that has sidelined him for the last three games. Head coach Kyle Shanahan said that he hopes Evans will be able to get back on the field on Thursday.

Linebacker Dre Greenlaw (neck, rest) was also out of practice and Shanahan hopes that he'll be able to work on Thursday as well.

Running back Christian McCaffrey and left tackle Trent Williams both had rest days on Wednesday. Safety Ji'Ayir Brown (ankle), linebacker Luke Gifford (chest), cornerback Renardo Green (back), quarterback Mac Jones (illness), defensive end Khalid Kareem (ankle), safety Siran Neal (knee, foot), and cornerback Upton Stout (shoulder, neck) were limited participants.

The 49ers listed cornerback Nate Hobbs (adductor), long snapper Jon Weeks (chest), defensive tackle C.J. West (knee), and defensive end Mykel Williams (hand, ankle) as full participants.
