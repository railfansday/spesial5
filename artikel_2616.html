Mike LaFleur: Defense needed a fresh voice

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Cardinals head coach Mike LaFleur made a significant change to his coaching staff on Monday.

Defensive coordinator Nick Rallis was dismissed a day after the Cardinals gave up 435 yards in a 34-7 loss to the Chiefs. They lost 34-6 to the Rams the prior week and LaFleur said the team is trying to find solutions for a unit that hasn’t “stopped the bleeding enough.

“I just felt in my heart this morning we needed a change,” LaFleur said, via the team’s website. “A fresh voice.”

LaFleur acknowledged that an offense that’s scored 16 points while starting three different quarterbacks in the last three weeks hasn’t “held our end of the bargain,” but the team has not made any coaching changes on that side of the ball

Senior defensive assistant Teryl Austin will take on the defensive coordinator role on an interim basis. Lafleur said he will “put his own spin” on how the team plays on that side of the ball over the final six games.
