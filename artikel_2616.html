NFL Week 10 gambling preview: Bet on Ryan Flournoy, plus other picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

It's hard to believe we have already reached the 10th Sunday of the 2026 NFL regular season. It's time to sit back, relax, and enjoy some football. Before the Sunday festivities kick off, here are our Week 10 gambling picks.

Rodriguez and the Jaguars should be able to have success on the ground this week against a Tennessee Titans run defense that has been struggling. The Titans have given up an average of 141 rushing yards over their last three games, two of which came against sub-par offenses. Rodriguez has gotten at least 10 carries in two of Jacksonville's last three games. This one could get out of hand quickly, so the 26-year-old should get plenty of chances on the ground.

After missing the first seven games of the season with a hamstring injury, rookie wideout Jordyn Tyson played in his first NFL action last Sunday. The first-round pick caught four passes for 30 yards to go with two first-down receptions in a productive debut. This week against the Carolina Panthers, the Saints will likely take some of the reins off and let Tyson have a bigger role. Look for the rookie wideout to have a big day on Sunday.

Bengals' wide receiver Tee Higgins has been on a tear of late. The veteran wideout has surpassed 140 receiving yards in three of the Bengals' last five games, with five touchdowns over that span. Pittsburgh's defense has been susceptible against the pass the past two weeks, which should allow Higgins to continue his hot streak.

The Cowboys will be getting George Pickens back in this matchup, but don't sleep on Ryan Flournoy's ability to make an impact. The wideout has surpassed 37 receiving yards in Dallas' last five games. If the Cowboys are going to move the ball against the 49ers defense, it's going to have to come through the air. Flournoy should have plenty of chances to surpass this number.
