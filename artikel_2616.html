Longtime Iowa State coach Bill Fennelly suspended 1 game for 'inappropriate contact' with player during win over UIC

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Longtime Iowa State women's basketball coach Bill Fennelly has been suspended for a single game by the university after he made "inappropriate contact" with a player during their win over the University of Illinois Chicago, the school announced on Sunday night.

Fennelly will serve that suspension during the team's game against Northern Iowa on Wednesday. Associate head coach Caroline McCombs will take his place during that game in Cedar Falls.

The incident occurred with about two minutes left in the contest on Sunday. Fennelly was spotted yelling at guard Sienna Harvey in a huddle during a timeout, and he lightly shoved her in the head. Harvey didn't appear to react much in the moment, and Fennelly continued on as if nothing had happened.

The Cyclones held on down the stretch to take a 69-64 win over the Flames, which pushed them to 5-0 on the year. It's unclear what sparked the incident, but Fennelly has since apologized to Harvey.

"I want to apologize to the student-athlete and her family, her teammates and all of Cyclone nation for allowing my emotions to get the best of me during our game," Fennelly said in a statement, via Cyclone Fanatic's Chris Williams. "I fully recognize that there are no situations where it is appropriate to make contact with a student-athlete in that way.

"I accept responsibility and will serve the suspension and I will do better moving forward."

Fennelly is in his 32nd season at Iowa State this fall. He's led the Cyclones to the NCAA tournament 24 times in his career, including in each of the last four seasons, and he now holds a 661-335 record in Ames.

Fennelly announced earlier this fall that he would retire after the season.

"We cannot tolerate inappropriate contact between a coach or staff member and a student-athlete in practice or in competition, whether intentional or not," new athletic director Jared Benko said in a statement. "We have high expectations of all of our coaches, staff and student-athletes, and we will not shy away from holding accountable those who fail to meet those expectations.

"I have met with Coach Fennelly and he has apologized to the student-athlete involved and regrets his actions."

The Cyclones, now 5-0, will take on Northern Iowa next on Wednesday. They'll go up against No. 3 USC and No. 10 Iowa, among others, before Big 12 play gets started next month.
