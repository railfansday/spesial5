Gotham’s Jordynn Dudley wins Rookie of the Year after injury-abbreviated season

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Forward Jordynn Dudley's rookie season was so good, she didn't even need to play all of it to win NWSL Rookie of the Year on Wednesday night.

The Gotham FC star beat out fellow rookies Kat Rader of the Houston Dash and Mimi Van Zanten of the San Diego Wave for the honor after she had an impressive first season in the NWSL.

"It's been surreal. I wasn't expecting it," Dudley told The Post shortly after she won the honors. "There's so many amazing rookies in this class, so [I'm] really grateful."

Jordynn Dudley warms up before Gotham FC's 1-0 win over Racing Louisville FC on May 3, 2026, in Harrison, N.J. Noah K. Murray for NY Post

Dudley had five goals and three assists across 26 appearances for Gotham across all competitions, in a season that ended abruptly in October following a foot injury. She had signed with Gotham in January after her junior season at Florida State University.

She was twice named Rookie of the Month and included on the Best XI twice during her rookie campaign.

The 21-year-old made a somewhat seamless transition from college to the pros during the course of the season, falling into line with a Gotham roster that included several big names from the U.S. women's national team such as Rose Lavelle, Midge Purce, Jaedyn Shaw, Jaelin Howell, Tierna Davidson and Emily Sonnett.

"What Jordynn has accomplished in her first professional season is incredibly impressive," president of soccer operations Yael Averbuch West said in a statement. "She came into a highly competitive environment and immediately showed that she could make a difference at this level. Her fearlessness in the attack and ability to create opportunities have been exciting to watch, and we believe she has a very bright future ahead of her."

Dudley credited "all" of her Gotham teammates for helping with the ease of her jump to the NWSL in 2026, while noting the "speed" and "decision-making and patience" as the biggest changes from college to the pros.

Rookie Jordynn Dudley plays the ball between defenders Courtney Petersen (8) and Ella Hase (6) during Gotham FC's 1-0 win over Racing Louisville FC on May 3, 2026, in Harrison, N.J. Noah K. Murray-NY Post

"I think at Florida State they had really high expectations for us and we kind of strived for a professional environment there," Dudley explained. "So I think they really kind of got me ready for this next step, and then obviously coming into a team that really supports me. That also made it a lot easier."

While Dudley became a mainstay for Gotham, her season was cut short after she suffered a foot fracture around October during training. The club put her on the season-ending injury list on Oct. 12.

The Georgia native said that her recovery has been "good" and that it "shouldn't be too long."

"I think that's a big thing that I try to get out of myself is for myself to be confident, and I think my teammates and the staff here at Gotham have really helped me with that," Dudley said about the recovery process. "It's always hard, but I think I've had a lot of support."

Gotham FC forward Jordynn Dudley practices on Sept. 1, 2026, in Whippany, N.J. Noah K. Murray-NY Post

Dudley did have to watch Gotham's two playoff games, including Sunday's 2-1 loss to the KC Current in the semifinals.

"Definitely disappointing that I couldn't help in the games, but I had fun cheering them on," she said. "I knew that they were ready for each game."

Now looking toward next season, Dudley is looking to pick up where she left off this year and continue to develop into a "complete" and "consistent" player.

Dudley was one of two Gotham players to win awards on Wednesday night at the NWSL Awards.

Gotham FC goalkeeper Ann-Katrin Berger took home Goalkeeper of the Year after a season in which she had 11 clean sheets and 15 wins in 27 starts.

Berger, defender Tierna Davidson and midfielder Jaelin Howell were named to the NWSL Best XI First Team, and midfielder Rose Lavelle was named to the Second Team.
