College Football Playoff projections: Here's what the picture looks like after a Week 11 devoid of upsets

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Our projected College Football Playoff field did not get shaken up on Saturday.

Sure, teams like Kentucky, LSU and South Carolina all lost, but we didn't think any of those three were ultimately making the postseason. Every team in our initial post-Week 10 projections won in Week 11.

But while the 12 teams in the field stay the same, we do wonder about some movement within the seeding. Here's our second round of CFP projections.

The Bulldogs hardly had to sweat against Missouri on Saturday. LB Raylen Wilson intercepted Missouri QB Austin Simmons on the first play of the game and before anybody realized it, Georgia was up 21-0.

Georgia can lock up a spot in the SEC title game in Week 12 with a win at South Carolina. The Gamecocks will likely drop to the bottom of the CFP top 25 on Tuesday after they lost at Arkansas without QB LaNorris Sellers. We assume that Sellers will be able to go against Georgia.

If you stayed up late to watch Utah's win over Arizona, the Utes and Wildcats didn't bore you. Utah got a 40-39 win thanks to a 70-yard catch-and-run TD by Creed Whittemore late in the fourth quarter. Arizona had the chance for a game-winning field goal, but their fourth-down pass just before the two-minute warning fell incomplete.

The Utes travel to TCU in Week 12 as the Horned Frogs need to get a win over Utah or Texas Tech in the final two games of the season to make a bowl game.

Utah Utes quarterback Devon Dampier runs for a touchdown during the first half of his team's win over the Arizona Wildcats on Saturday. (Christopher Hook/Getty Images)

The Fighting Irish had no problem with Boston College in a 41-7 win on Saturday. But we're wondering if Notre Dame's seeding will be impacted by another blowout that they had no part in. As Wake Forest demolished SMU — more on that game in a bit — the Mustangs now have three losses. And they're going to be significant underdogs and will likely get a fourth in Week 12 in South Bend. It's not Notre Dame's fault that many of the teams on their schedule have underachieved, but the Irish's second-best win is over a 6-4 Wisconsin team that lost to Maryland on Saturday.

We were surprised the Hoosiers fell five spots on their Week 10 bye week but ultimately have no concerns about their chances of getting a first-round bye. If Indiana wins its final two games of the season and then the Big Ten title, they're in the top four. Period.

The Hoosiers also scored their most points since Oct. 3 in their 27-13 win over USC. That's not the mark of an offensive juggernaut, but Indiana doesn't have to be with a defense as good as it has. After QB Grant Wilson started against the Trojans, we wouldn't be surprised to see him take over for the rest of the season.

The Hurricanes had little trouble with Duke as they pulled away in the second half. Boise State, meanwhile, won a 46-41 shootout with Oregon State to wrap up a spot in the Pac-12 title game.

The Broncos are now the prohibitive favorites to make the College Football Playoff from the Group of Six conferences after New Mexico lost at home to UNLV. The Lobos now have two losses and it's hard to see how they have any playoff hopes barring a late-season collapse from Boise State.

Darian Mensah was 30-of-41 passing for 382 yards and a TD in Miami's 36-25 victory over his old school as Cooper Barkate — also a Duke transfer — had eight catches for 134 yards.

Another week, another win for the Bruins. UCLA took down Illinois 22-14 on Friday night despite an injury scare from Ty Dieffenbach. Remember, he's been starting for Nico Iamaleava after Iamaleava's season-ending knee injury. He'd be watching this game between his current and former schools from the sidelines.

The Volunteers dominated a Texas A&M team that is just offensively hapless. But with LSU dropping out of the rankings and Vanderbilt also in the cellar of the SEC, their final three games aren't going to be seen as a gauntlet by the committee. If Wake Forest and Texas Tech keep piling up the points, the Vols could end up the last team in the field at 10-2.

Again, this is a ranking predicated on Alabama losing the SEC title game to Georgia. We don't think the committee would put a one-loss UCLA behind a three-loss Alabama, but we also wouldn't rule it out, either.

Keelon Russell threw three TDs in the Tide's win over Vanderbilt on Saturday as Alabama overcame a sluggish first half.

There was no sluggish first half in Dallas for Wake Forest. The Demon Deacons absolutely blitzed SMU 49-7. Wake had a 42-0 lead at halftime and five different players rushed for a touchdown. Ty Clark III had 11 carries for 77 yards and a score, QB Gio Lopez rushed for two TDs and threw for another and breakout wide receiver Carlos Hernandez had six catches for 94 yards and a score.

This would be a fantastic first-round game. The Red Raiders seem to have found something offensively with Thomas Castellanos at quarterback. Tech beat Oklahoma State 52-27 Saturday night and has now scored 139 points across his three starts. Saturday night, Castellanos was 12-of-23 passing for 263 yards and four touchdowns while also rushing for 38 yards before leaving the game early after a big hit.

Arch Manning produced the signature drive of his Texas career as the Longhorns knocked LSU out of CFP contention. It was a great rebound for Manning after what had been a brutal game. It was the first three-interception game of his career, as he finished 19-of-35 passing for 174 yards.
