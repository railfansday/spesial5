Spurs guard Dylan Harper slips on court late in loss to Pistons, limps off with apparent groin injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The court at the Frost Bank Center got Dylan Harper on Tuesday night.

With about a minute left in the San Antonio Spurs' 105-93 loss to the Detroit Pistons, Harper suddenly went down on the wing right as Stephon Castle was passing him the ball. While it looked like Harper was maybe hit in real time, a closer look revealed that he just slipped on the court and his legs went opposite ways.

Harper was rolling around on the court holding his groin, clearly in a lot of pain, as play continued the other direction. He eventually stood up and walked back to the locker room after play stopped and the athletic training staff came out, but Harper looked very uncomfortable moving as he worked his way into the tunnel.

"It just looked like he slipped, obviously, so hoping for the best, but don't know anything yet," Spurs head coach Mitch Johnson said, via the San Antonio Express-News' Tom Orsborn.

Harper did not return to the game. He finished with 20 points and four rebounds while shooting 8 of 14 from the field in the loss, which was the Spurs' third of the season and snapped their 12-game win streak.

Harper entered Tuesday's contest averaging 18.3 points, 5.5 rebounds and 4.7 assists per game this season, all of which are improvements from his rookie campaign. The Spurs selected Harper with the No. 2 overall pick in the 2025 NBA Draft out of Rutgers.

The Pistons rallied in the second half Tuesday night to secure the 12-point win in San Antonio. They ended the contest on a 10-3 burst and held the Spurs scoreless in the final 90 seconds.

Cade Cunningham led the way with 27 points in the win, which pushed the Pistons to 9-4 on the season. Daniss Jenkins added 25 points, and Ausar Thompson finished just shy of a triple-double with 14 points, 12 assists, 9 rebounds.

Victor Wembanyama led the Spurs with 25 points and 18 rebounds. He had two ridiculous self-alley-oop dunks in the first quarter. Devin Vassell added 12 points, and Castle finished with 10 points and seven assists.

Though it's unclear how long Harper may be sidelined because of the injury, losing him for any significant stretch will make things much more difficult for the Spurs as they try to remain at the top of the Western Conference standings.
