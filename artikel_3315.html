Guy Ritchie and Tom Hardy’s Hit Crime Series Officially Casts Its First New Character

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Editor's Note: The following contains spoilers for 'MobLand' Season 2Given where we left Tom Hardy at the end of the second season of MobLand, the last thing Harry Da Souza probably needs in his life is more people to contend with. The Paramount+ crime thriller has managed to assemble quite the cast over the course of its first two seasons, and there's something particularly enjoyable about watching Pierce Brosnan and Helen Mirren play two people you'd want absolutely nothing to do with in real life. With production on the third season having gotten underway last week, we now know who'll be joining them when the series returns.

As reported by Deadline, Gregg Sulkin has officially joined the cast of MobLand Season 3 in an undisclosed recurring role, making him the first new addition to the cast for the upcoming season. Sulkin will probably be best known to a generation of television viewers for his work opposite Selena Gomez in Wizards of Waverly Place, in which he played werewolf Mason Greybeck, a role he recently reprised in Wizards Beyond Waverly Place.

Sulkin has taken on considerably different roles in recent years, appearing in two World War II dramas, World on Fire and Tyler Perry's The Six Triple Eight, and earlier this year venturing into horror with Spiders. He's also set to join the third season of The Forsytes, the British period drama based on the novels of John Galsworthy.

Created by Ronan Bennett and executive produced by Guy Ritchie, MobLand features Hardy (Venom) as Harry Da Souza, Brosnan (GoldenEye) as Conrad Harrigan, Mirren (1923) as Maeve Harrigan, Paddy Considine (House of the Dragon) as Kevin Harrigan, Anson Boon (Pistol) as Eddie Harrigan, and Joanne Froggatt (Downton Abbey) as Jan Da Souza.
