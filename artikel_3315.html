Latin Grammy Awards 2026 Winners: Rosalía Wins Her Third Album Of The Year — Full List

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The 27th Latin Grammy Awards have concluded in Las Vegas, and Rosalía is walking away the night’s big winner.

During Thursday’s awards ceremony, the Spanish artist won Album of the Year for Lux, marking her third victory in the category after El Mal Querer (2018) and Motomami (2022). She also won Song of the Year and Best Pop Song for “La Perla” (feat. Yahritza y Su Esencia), Best Alternative Music Album for Lux (Complete Works) and Best Short Form Music Video for “Sauvignon Blanc.”

Other winners of the night included CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, Milo J and Karol G. See the full winners list below.
