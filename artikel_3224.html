The 1980s was a golden age for action movies, giving us some of the most iconic stars, characters and explosive blockbusters in cinema history. From Arnold Schwarzenegger and Sylvester Stallone to Harrison Ford and Sigourney Weaver, the decade was packed with leading men and women who helped define the genre. Whether it was James Bond saving the day or Rocky stepping back into the ring, these films have remained firm favorites with movie fans for decades.

But how well do you remember the action classics that made these stars household names? We've picked out 10 famous stars and want to see if you can match each one to their correct '80s action movie.
