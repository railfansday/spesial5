A lot depends on how quickly you do this and if you have a spouse, whether they desire the same thing. Our discretionary spending actually increased in retirement since we are in the go go years while we still have the time, energy and desire to travel.

I was tired of spending going up as fast as my paycheck .

Bank every net raise in tax deferred.


------


Every raise, I calculated how much I could expect
after required payroll deductions. That was my net raise.

I increased payroll deduction 50 to 100% of that net raise in tax deferred.

Even with a 100% reduction of the net increase, I still ended
up with either 15 or 25% of that net raise in my check,
because that was the marginal tax on the net raise.

I did modify this, saving some of the net raise in Roth and/or taxable,
and sometimes adjusting so my pay went up with inflation,but often
the raise was less than inflation.

I did this until I could project retirement discretionary spending at or above that of pre retirement .

Once the kids were out of college and the house was paid off,
I was saving over 50% of my check.
