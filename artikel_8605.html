A secured card’s credit line usually equals your deposit, so $200 down gets you a $200 limit. The Discover it Secured Cash Back can open a $200 line on a deposit as low as $49, depending on your credit.

Every card here charges no annual fee, and you get the deposit back when the issuer moves you to a regular card or you close the account paid off. Some of these issuers start reviewing you for a regular card after six months. If you can’t spare a deposit, look at unsecured cards for bad or fair credit instead. Add up their fees first, because unlike a deposit, a bad-credit card’s fees never come back.
