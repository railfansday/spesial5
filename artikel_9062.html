House Around a Column / fala

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Read the original version in Portuguese here.

Text description provided by the architects. The compact plot sits within a dense piece of urban fabric, surrounded on all sides by neighbouring houses. Its shape is skewed. The project begins with the challenge of inscribing a house into this distorted figure. The longest wall of the plot becomes the guide. From it, a set of geometric rules gradually unfolds, quietly organising the plan.
