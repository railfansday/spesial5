Let the Deals Pick Your Destination – Start With What’s Cheap From Your Airport

Pick your vacation spot first, and you pay whatever it costs to fly there. Instead, search a fare map from your home airport with the destination left blank. Set price alerts on a short list of trips you’d happily take, and book when one drops to a price you’re willing to pay.

Say a couple wants Paris in June, and round-trip economy tickets from their home airport cost $1,030 each. When an alert flags Lisbon in May at $480 round trip, two tickets there cost $1,100 less than two to Paris.

A fare map shows you swaps like that before you’ve picked a place. On Google Flights, open Explore, enter your home airport, leave the destination blank, and choose flexible dates, such as a one-week trip in the next six months. The map then pins a round-trip price on cities near and far, so you can pick from the ones that are cheap from your airport right now.

If you’re set on one city, save by searching flexible dates and nearby airports for that one flight, or by going in its shoulder season, the weeks just before and after its peak season.

Next, let the deals come to you. Pick three to five trips you’d happily take, search each on Google Flights, turn on Track prices, and choose Any dates. Google then emails you when a route’s lowest price drops significantly over a month, and otherwise sends a regular email with its best fares.

For trips you didn’t think to watch, add Going, a deal newsletter whose free plan emails a sample of economy deals from your home airport to the continental U.S. Its $49-a-year Premium plan adds international deals. Join the email lists of the airlines that fly from your airport, too, to hear about their fare sales.

Keep a trip fund and a few open weeks on your calendar, so you can say yes to a deal right away. Book the hotel at a refundable rate too, so a cheaper option that turns up later can still replace it.

Write down the most you’d pay for each trip on your list, and book the day an alert comes in under it.

Start your short list of trips on Google Flights.
