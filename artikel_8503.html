Regifting is a fine way to save money as long as the gift is new, unopened, and something you’d have bought the recipient anyway. Keep a list of who gave you what, and check it before you wrap anything, so a regift never reaches the original giver or anyone close to them.

Before you start your holiday shopping, go through the unopened gifts on your closet shelf. Each one that suits someone you shop for is a gift you don’t have to buy.

You can tell whether a gift is new and whether it suits someone by looking at it. You can’t tell where it came from, and a year later you may not remember whether the candle set was from your sister-in-law or your neighbor.

So keep the list in your phone’s notes app, with each gift you set aside and who gave it. Your phone is with you when you shop, so you’ll see what you already have for someone before you buy.

Then check each gift against four rules before you give it.

Not every gift on the shelf will pass. Return a gift that fails a rule for store credit while the store’s return window is open. After that, sell what’s still sealed and donate the rest.

Start the list tonight with what’s already on the shelf, and add each new gift the day you decide not to keep it.
