Kim Hellberg footage after Middlesbrough's draw v Watford is becoming a talking point

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Middlesbrough dropped points and failed to score for the second time this week on Saturday at Watford, and Boro boss Kim Hellberg was seen visibly frustrated after the final whistle.

The draw at Vicarage Road saw Middlesbrough relinquish the top spot in the Championship ahead of the November international break, with Birmingham City climbing above them on goal difference.

It's not how Boro would have wanted to sign off this mini block of fixtures between international breaks, and the performance against Watford, especially, would have provided plenty of food for thought in the dugout.

Indeed, this was the second successive game without leading talisman and Championship top scorer Will Lankshear due to injury, and it's almost universally agreed that if the 21-year-old was leading the line on Saturday, they'd have cruised to victory, as Boro had 18 shots, 34 touches in Watford's box and four big chances, but couldn't score.

Middlesbrough boss Kim Hellberg has shown how passionate he is about the game in the 12 months he's spent in charge at the Riverside. Therefore, it was clear that a result like this, following such a dominant performance, was going to affect him.

After the game, the Swede was filmed staring blankly at the ground for around half a minute, just reflecting on the game, and Boro fans have reacted accordingly.

Some have made jokey comments about the state of the frontline when Lankshear is injured. One Boro fan took it upon themselves to caption the video, "What having Kyle Joseph as an attacking option does to man." Another commented, "Please god make Will be fit, please god make Will be fit…..🙏."

"He’s got Kyle Joseph depression," was also among the reactions to the video.

The majority of comments in response to Hellberg's post-match reaction were about how invested he is in the game, though, and Middlesbrough fans know how lucky they are to have the Swede in charge.

"You can just tell this isn’t performative. Like the fist bumps and the celebrations when we win, or the tears during the play-off debacle last season, Kim ‘gets it’, he’s immersed himself into the #Boro way. Never seen a manager so invested in us," one Boro fan tweeted.

"Carries it all, the highs and lows just like the fans. “A good leader leads the people from above them. A great leader leads the people from within them," was also among the comments, as was, "The reaction of a man who puts the weight of the world on his own shoulders #UTB."

"Very powerful, but also makes me want to give him a hug and tell him he's one of my favourite ever Boro gaffers and everything will be alright," someone commented in response to the video, showcasing the love and respect Boro fans have towards their head coach.

This is the reaction of a man spent after a disappointing result, having coached what was effectively the perfect game.

Plenty of head coaches nowadays are focused on the journey, the performance levels and good underlying numbers, and Hellberg is definitely one of those, but he also recognises how important it is to win games, too.

As one Boro fan mentioned, he carries the weight of expectation amongst Middlesbrough fans, who are desperate to return to the Premier League after a decade away, and you can imagine he takes not winning as seriously as some of those in the stands do, too.

It's reactions like this that are why many are touting Hellberg to become a top-flight manager in the near future, and Boro supporters hope it'll be with them.

However, it's hard to ignore the fact that, while Lankshear sits on the sidelines, Middlesbrough struggles to score goals and win games.

Boro's wasteful nature in front of goal dominated the narrative surrounding them in the final few months of last season as they slipped out of the running for automatic promotion and into the play-offs, and they could have been out of sight in the first half of their play-off semi-final first leg against Southampton if not for poor finishing, too.

Lankshear has papered over those cracks, as he's been brilliant at finishing the plenty of chances Middlesbrough create in a game, but the alarm bells are starting to ring without him.

Hopefully, the 21-year-old will be back after the international break and won't suffer any more injury setbacks, as that could be the difference between promotion and some grim de ja vu.

It's something Hellberg will be mulling over for the next two weeks, and it seemingly started immediately after the final whistle at Watford.
