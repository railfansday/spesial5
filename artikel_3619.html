Former Bachelor Peter Weber and Pregnant Girlfriend Jadeyn Carr Are Engaged

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Former Bachelor Peter Weber popped the question to Jadeyn Carr before the arrival of their twins.

“Marry Me @jadeyncarr,” Weber, 35, sweetly asked his fiancée via Instagram on Wednesday, November 11.

Congratulatory messages rolled in after the Bachelor Nation alum shared a carousel of photos of his proposal in New York City’s Central Park. Carr, 26, put her oval diamond engagement ring on full display, including covering Weber’s face with her hand in one funny photo.

“Omgggg you guys congratulations!!!!,” Bachelor in Paradise alum Blake Horstmann‘s wife, Love Is Blind star Giannina Gibelli, wrote in the comment section, while Bachelorette season 15 contestant Dustin Kendrick sent love by sharing some emojis, “❤️❤️❤️❤️👏”

Bachelorette season 16 star Dr. Joe Park made a cute reference to the newly-engaged couple expecting twins, writing, “Wooo!! Congratulations to both (all 4?) of you!! 🥰♥️🥹.”

Weber and Carr’s engagement comes after the pair announced they are expecting not one but two children. In a September Instagram post, the couple shared black-and-white photos of Weber kissing Carr’s baby bump.

“1 + 1 = 4 ♥️,” the couple captioned a joint Instagram post at the time, to which Weber’s brother, Jack, replied in the comments section, “Wohoo! Can’t wait to meet them!!”

The pair also received love from several Bachelor Nation members, who shared messages of support in the comments section.

“Congratulations!!” season 28 Bachelor Joey Graziadei wrote, while former Bachelor contestant Jamie Otis said, “Ohhh your family is going to double in the best way! People will warn you it’s a lot but trust me .. it’s a lot of fun ❤️❤️.”

Bachelor in Paradise alum Kendall Long added, “As a fellow twin I can say twins are the best 😜🤍 Congrats to you two!!”

Weber confirmed his relationship with Carr in August, sharing a photo of the model in an Instagram carousel caption, reading, “Summer ‘26.” In the images, Carr appeared to join the Bachelor Nation alum and his family on a trip to the Adirondacks in upstate New York. Less than one week later, Weber uploaded more photos featuring Carr.

Weber’s mom, Barbara, celebrated her son’s love in a separate Instagram post of the pilot and Carr.

“SO HAPPY YOU BROUGHT HER HOME!!❤️,” Barbara captioned the image, to which Weber replied, “So this is what you meant all along… ✅❤️.” Barbara added, “Exactly! I’m so happy for you two! She fits into all of our hearts❤️.”

Bachelor Nation met Weber in 2019 when he vied for the affections of Hannah Brown on season 15 of The Bachelorette. After being eliminated, Weber then served as the Bachelor the following year and ultimately introduced his family to finalists Hannah Ann Sluss and Madi Prewett.

After meeting Sluss, Barbara tearfully asked Weber not to “let her go” and to “bring her home.” Weber went on to propose to Sluss in the finale episode, but the pair split weeks later. He then briefly moved on with Prewett before calling it quits days later. He was also romantically linked to contestant Kelley Flanagan and former show producer Julie LaPlaca before finding The One in Carr.
