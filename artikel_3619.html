Pregnant Savannah Chrisley Flaunts Huge Engagement Ring After Secret Wedding

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Savannah Chrisley still can’t get over her massive engagement ring from husband Jason Galanis — and honestly, who can blame her?

“My man just said, ‘You’re not too expensive, you’re my best investment,’” Chrisley, 29, wrote via her Instagram Stories on Friday, November 6, alongside a selfie of her eyebrow-raising diamond sparkler.

She added, “Ladies … Find a man with his mindset.”

Chrisley and Galanis, 56, have been together since July 2025, nearly one year before the businessman popped the question.

“When he gave me the ring, he was like, ‘Before I open this, I just want you to know that this is just an expression of love to you,’” Chrisley said of her engagement moment in the Cayman Islands on her “Unlocked” podcast last month. “[He told me,] ‘This can be whatever you want it to be. This can be an engagement ring, a promise ring, a cocktail ring for all I care. I just want you to know how much I love you.’”

Galanis designed the ring, a three-stone bauble, with Genovese Jewelers.

Accepting the token as an engagement ring, Chrisley planned to say “I do” to Galanis in January 2027. The pair’s wedding timeline abruptly shifted, however, after the Chrisley Knows Best alum found out that she is pregnant with their first baby.

“Let me tell you, it was going to be a phenomenal time. It still is going to be a phenomenal time,” she quipped of her future wedding celebration via her Instagram at the time. “It has just been pushed a few months to May of 2027. And that is because we got the surprise of a lifetime.”

Despite delaying their wedding and reception, Chrisley and Galanis exchanged vows in a small ceremony in the Grand Caymans this summer.

“We went and legally got married because honestly, when we go to the hospital, I want everything to be easy,” Chrisley said on the Tuesday, November 3, episode of her podcast. “There is no baby having different name. No, we’re all going to be a family. So, here we are, we are legally married.”
