Ten thousand dollars in a few months rarely comes from one source, so build it from several. Start a bank bonus and a card bonus first, since each gives you about three months to qualify, sell what you don’t use, and add one gig or skill you can work every week for most of the total.

The fastest money pays once: a bonus, a sale, a new plasma donor’s first month. Money that keeps coming, from a gig or a skill, takes hours every week.

So the hours are the real cost of $10,000. Uber drivers made a median $21.18 an hour from fares and tips in 2025, before gas, insurance, and car costs, by the count of Gridwise, an app drivers use to track their earnings. At that rate, $10,000 takes about 470 hours, or 36 a week for 13 weeks. Each one-time payment below cuts those hours, so start them first.

Each pays once, after you meet a requirement on time: a bank account bonus needs direct deposits, a card sign-up bonus needs purchases, and a brokerage bonus needs money moved in from another firm.

Chase Total Checking pays new Chase checking customers $400 once direct deposits totaling $1,000 reach the account within 90 days of enrolling in the offer, and the bonus arrives within 15 days of qualifying. Open it by January 27, 2027, and keep $500 a month in direct deposits coming to avoid the $15 monthly fee.

The Chase Freedom Unlimited pays a $200 bonus once you spend $500 on purchases in the first 3 months, and Chase posts it 6 to 8 weeks after you qualify. Charge only bills you already pay, and pay the full balance every month.

J.P. Morgan Self-Directed Investing pays $50 for $5,000 moved into a new account from another firm within 45 days, rising to $1,000 for $250,000 or more. Keep the money there until 90 days after you enroll, and the bonus arrives within 15 days after that. It’s worth doing only if you already have investments elsewhere to move.

Most of these pay within days of starting, while you wait for the bonuses.

Sell furniture, bikes, tools, and clothes you no longer use to local buyers on Facebook Marketplace for cash at pickup, or ship them to buyers on eBay. eBay holds your earnings until you withdraw them, and a withdrawal usually reaches a checking account in 1 to 3 business days. Price each item at what the same thing recently sold for.

Worthy sells jewelry, diamonds included, in online auctions that run 48 to 72 hours and draw bids from more than 1,000 professional buyers. It keeps 18% of a winning bid up to $5,000, falling in steps to 10% on the largest sales, and typically pays 9 to 14 business days after the sale. A piece that doesn’t reach your reserve, the lowest price you’ll accept, comes back to you free.

An ecoATM kiosk pays on the spot for an old phone, in cash, Venmo, or PayPal, and its online pricing tool gives you a quote before you go. Compare that quote with other places to sell your phone, such as mail-in buyback sites, and bring a government-issued ID, such as a driver’s license, since sellers must be 18 or older.

New donors at CSL Plasma can receive up to $800 in their first month, an amount that varies by location, and the money goes onto a prepaid card once you’ve made your first donation. Bring a photo ID and proof of where you live to a first visit that includes a health screening. Federal rules allow two donations in 7 days, at least 2 days apart.

Healthy volunteers earned a median $3,070 per trial, ranging from $150 to $13,000, in a study that tracked their earnings from 2013 to 2017, and many of these trials require days or weeks living at a research clinic. Search ClinicalTrials.gov for studies near you. Under FDA guidance, pay should build as a study goes rather than wait until you finish.

DoorDash drivers averaged $12.43 for each hour spent on an order in 2025, before gas and car costs, by Gridwise’s count, which leaves out time spent waiting between orders. You keep every tip, and Fast Pay sends earnings to a debit card once a day for $1.99. Dashers must be at least 18, or 19 or 21 in some states, with a car, a scooter, or in some cities a bike, and a Social Security number for a background check.

Uber, the earner the math above uses, lets you take weekly deposits or cash out to a debit card after every trip. You need at least a year of licensed driving in the US, an eligible four-door car, insurance, and a screening, and the minimum age varies by city.

On TaskRabbit, you set your own hourly rate for jobs such as furniture assembly and mounting, and you keep all of it plus tips, since TaskRabbit charges its service fee to the client. Its pricing guidance recommends rates, but the rate stays yours to choose.

These pay for what you already know how to do. Writing, design, tutoring, and admin work are ways to make money from home that pay by the hour or the project, once a client has seen your samples or your profile.

Members of the Editorial Freelancers Association who write blog posts for hire reported median rates of 25 to 45 cents a word, or $74.50 to $87.50 an hour, in the rate chart it published for 2026. Pitch businesses whose blogs cover what you know, send two or three samples, and agree on when you’ll be paid before you start.

Graphic designers earned a median $30.27 an hour in May 2025, the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) reports, and bookkeeping, editing, and social media work can be sold the same way, by the project or by the hour. Start with a skill you already use at work, so you don’t have to learn one before your first job.

On Wyzant, tutors set their own hourly rate and keep 75% of it, with Wyzant taking a 25% platform fee. Cambly, which pays $10.20 an hour for English conversation practice, is limiting how many new tutors it takes.

The closest benchmark for remote admin work, such as handling a small business’s email, calendar, and bookings, is secretaries and administrative assistants, who earned a median $23.23 an hour in May 2025, by the BLS count. Offer it first to business owners you already know.

Hand laborers and movers earned a median $18.38 an hour in May 2025, and grounds maintenance workers $19.27, the BLS reports. Offer moving help, mowing, and leaf cleanup to neighbors first, since they can pay you the day the job is done.

Each platform keeps a share of every booking, so check what similar listings near you charge and what you’d keep after that share.

Airbnb keeps 15.5% of most hosts’ bookings under the single fee it is moving every host to, or 3% under the older split fee it is phasing out. Before you list, check your lease or HOA rules and your city’s limits on short-term rentals.

On Neighbor, you can rent out a garage, a driveway, or a shed as storage. Neighbor charges the renter at the start of each rental period, sends your payout at the end, minus a 4.9% plus 30-cent processing fee, and the money reaches your bank in two to three business days.

Turo pays hosts 70%, 80%, or 90% of each trip’s price in most US markets, by the protection plan they pick. The plan that pays 90% leaves you paying up to $2,750 of damage per trip, against $250 on the plan that pays 70%.

On RVshare, you set your own nightly price and minimum stay. RVshare releases your payout the first business day after each rental starts, and the money typically reaches your bank 1 to 2 business days later. Price it against similar RVs near you, and count insurance and cleaning as costs.

Say your one-time money comes to $2,400, made up of $600 from the two bonuses, $800 for a first month of plasma, and $1,000 from things you sell. That leaves $7,600 for a steady earner, about 360 hours at the Uber median above, or 28 a week for 13 weeks, before costs.

Set aside part of every gig, freelance, and rental payment for taxes. The IRS counts gig income as taxable, and net self-employment earnings of $400 or more also bring self-employment tax of 15.3%.
