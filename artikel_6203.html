Ultrathin 'rainbow' sails could power spacecraft and shade the Earth

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

For years, whimsically minded space enthusiasts have been dreaming of sailing across the vastness of the void on beams of sunlight. Photons, the massless particles that make up those beams of sunlight, still carry momentum and can therefore impart a gentle push when they bounce off a surface. This force is the concept behind "solar sailing," which has been having a bit of a moment in the last decade as several proof-of-concept flights have shown it is indeed feasible.
