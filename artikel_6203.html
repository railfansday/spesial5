Citi Shop takes some of the work out of saving money online. Once you've installed its browser extension, it acts as a shopping portal and coupon hunter all-in-one, rewarding you with statement credits.

It has broad reach, too, with more than 5,000 participating online merchants. That's easier than a traditional online shopping portal, which requires you to remember to start at the portal and click through before filling your cart.

Its ease of use makes it a great resource, but other portals or special discounts may save you more.

Here's how Citi Shop works and what to consider to determine if it's the best savings path for you.

Citi Shop is a free browser extension for primary cardholders with an eligible Citi credit card, including ThankYou points cards and cobranded cards. Citi retail and commercial cards are usually ineligible.

Offers come as either a percentage back or a fixed amount back, and your earnings are applied as a statement credit on the Citi card you selected during enrollment.

Citi Shop is separate from Citi Merchant Offers, which requires you to enroll in targeted offers with a specific Citi card and then use that card for the qualifying purchase.

Both Citi Shop and traditional portals give you an extra reward for making purchases they track.

But Citi Shop brings potential rebates to you, rather than requiring you to initiate your savings search. The extension automatically displays a pop-up with an available offer when you visit a participating website. You'll have the option to activate the offer before checkout.

All of Citi Shop's rebates are provided as statement credits, unlike the potential airline miles, hotel points or credit card rewards of traditional portals.

What I like about Citi Shop is that it also acts like a shopping assistant. Instead of opening six coupon sites only to discover that every code expired last Tuesday, it handles the scavenger hunt for you and tests available promo codes on your behalf.

Like Capital One Shopping, Citi Shop uses a browser extension. However, Capital One Shopping doesn't require a Capital One card to enroll and its rewards can be redeemed for eligible gift cards instead of a statement credit.

Generally speaking, Citi Shop clashes with other shopping portals. Both work by referring and tracking your purchase, so they can be mutually exclusive. You'll need to make your purchase through whichever offer is better.

Unless Citi Shop finds a promo code, it won't lower your total at checkout. Its returns arrive later as statement credits and may require patiently waiting two to six months, sometimes longer.

Even though you need an eligible Citi card to enroll, you don’t have to pay with it. Citi allows any payment method accepted by the merchant.

I really appreciate the flexibility to choose the best card for each purchase. For electronics, I might pick a card with an extended warranty while I might stick to a travel rewards credit card for hotel bookings. You can even pay with a gift card if you wish.

Regardless of how you pay, the statement credit will post to the Citi card you enrolled.

If you have an eligible Citi card but rarely remember to click through a shopping portal, Citi Shop is an easy win. Install it, activate offers when the pop-up appears and let the credits come to you. That convenience can also be a big benefit if you have partners or other family members who aren't as motivated to strategize on rewards as you are.

But with a little extra effort, you might be able to earn even more. When the Citi Shop pop-up appears with an offer, take a minute to compare before you click Activate.

Checking a shopping portal aggregator such as Cashback Monitor shows payouts from multiple cash-back, airline and credit card portals, making it easier to compare a Citi Shop statement credit with points or miles elsewhere.

Suppose a $200 jacket is marked down to $160. Citi Shop finds a $10 coupon, bringing the checkout price to $150, and offers 8% back. Assuming the full amount qualifies, you'd receive a $12 Citi Shop credit. If you pay with a card that earns 2% back and has an activated merchant offer for $5 off a $100 purchase, you'd collect another $8. After all the credits and rewards post, your net cost would be $130.

Read the terms before getting too excited. Some brands, products, gift cards or coupon codes may be excluded and you might not be able to stack every layer every time. Still, you can often use multiple discounts simultaneously for a better deal.

Citi Shop is not a program for instant gratification. Citi says statement credits post 61 to 180 days after a completed purchase, though some can take longer.

In many cases, offers may show as pending long before they officially pay out, to account for potential returns. Hotel rewards may remain pending until after the stay. Eventually, you'll see it on your statement as "CITI SHOP STATEMENT CREDIT."

If a credit hasn't posted after the expected window, contact Citi using the number on the back of your enrolled card. You may want to keep good records along the way, like a screenshot of the offer and an e-receipt from the purchase.

Citi Shop's biggest advantage is convenience. It does the heavy lifting by reminding you to activate offers so you don't miss out on earnings from your online orders, while testing coupon codes along the way. The offers themselves may or may not be the highest available, but they are certainly the easiest.

To truly optimize your returns, you'll want to compare Citi Shop earnings with those from other shopping portals — and stack other forms of saving along the way. But when that sounds like too much, the ease of this extension can still be a great way to tack on some extra returns.
