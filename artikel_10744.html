Duolingo slammed for posting ‘sexy’ owl after teen mascot fan art takedown

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Duolingo is facing backlash after its Brazilian X account posted a suggestive image of its green owl, roughly two weeks after an artist said the company’s lawyers made them delete fan art of its teenage character Lily.

Duo, the mascot that reminds users to do their language lessons, has been the face of several Duolingo social media stunts. In 2024, the company posted a TikTok of the owl getting a Brazilian butt lift.

On November 3, digital artist Movail said they had deleted their Duolingo-related posts at the request of the company’s legal team. One animation featuring Lily in suggestive poses had racked up nearly 30 million views before it was removed. Duolingo describes Lily as a teenager, a detail some users raised when defending the removal.

The new post shows a muscular Duo crawling out from under a blanket. In the Portuguese caption, the owl jokes that he’s so scared of the blackout he’ll have to sleep with your husband. The post had more than 2.8 million views on November 17, as a rumor about a worldwide blackout that day circulated on social media.

Some users pointed to the Lily controversy in the replies. YouTuber Chibi Reviews quote-posted the image, asking, “Didn’t you force an artist to delete their artwork because of sexual content? What the hell is this then?”

Others questioned how Lily fan art went “too far” while the company posted suggestive images of their other mascot.

Duolingo Brasil kept the joke going in its replies. The account replied to one user who posted a photo of Duo from the rear, “I told you not to take it from that angle.” When another asked what language Duo planned to teach other people’s husbands, the owl replied, “My tongue. Want to get to know it?”

Duolingo celebrated Duo’s 13th birthday in a June 2025 LinkedIn post, joking that he’d been acting like a teenager his whole life. The company tied the celebration to the app’s anniversary, which would make Duo 14 in 2026 by that count.

Duolingo has played with the owl’s age before. Its mock death announcement in February 2025 gave him a birth year of 1000 B.C., while other promotional materials have listed the mascot’s age as lining up with the age of the company.
