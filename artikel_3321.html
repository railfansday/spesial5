HBO Max Buys British Flatshare Comedy ‘Break Clause’ Starring Samuel Bottomley, Lara Ricote & ‘Rooster’s Charly Clive

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

EXCLUSIVE: HBO Max has bought British flatshare comedy Break Clause starring Samuel Bottomley, Lara Ricote and Rooster’s Charly Clive.

The show, created by Jess Bray (The Outlaws) and directed by Starstruck’s Alice Snedden, follows a couple who break up but are stuck together in a tenancy.

Ben and Lil then have to navigate their breakup whilst being stuck in the same apartment, discovering that moving on from your first love is hard enough, but moving out is even harder.

Kicking off on Channel 4 last month, Break Clause stars a wealth of top British comic talent including Bottomley, Ricote and Clive, the latter of whom will be familiar to HBO audiences after playing Katie Russo, daughter of Steve Carell’s Greg Russo, in Rooster. The show also stars SNL UK breakouts Hammed Animashaun and Jack Shep. Leo Reich, whose own Gen-Z comedy It Gets Worse is doing well for Channel 4 and Paramount+, was in the pilot. We’ve charted Break Clause’s journey from Channel 4 pilot more than two years back to Channel 4 comedy boss Charlie Perkins telling us exclusively it had been taken to series to now reaching audiences across the Atlantic.

The show lands on HBO Max tomorrow. It will air on Germany’s ZDFneo next year and is sold by BBC Studios, which brokered the HBO Max deal. BBC Studios Comedy produces Break Clause. Executive producers are Bray, Mark Freeland, Molly Seymour and Josh Cole for BBC Studios Comedy and Laura Mae Harding for ZDFneo. Olly Cambridge is producer.
