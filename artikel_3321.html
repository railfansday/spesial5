Only Murders In The Building Season 7’s Major Change Confirmed By Creators & Stars

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Only Murders in the Building is confirmed to be making a major change in season 7.

After five seasons of Only Murders in the Building primarily taking place in New York City, Hulu's mystery comedy series is going overseas in season 6 as Charles-Haden Savage (Steve Martin), Oliver Putnam (Martin Short), and Mabel Mora (Selena Gomez) investigate the death of true-crime podcaster Cinda Canning (Tina Fey) in London. Season 6 has even been renamed Only Murders in London, and production took place in the city as well.

In an interview with Deadline, series co-creator and showrunner John Hoffman shared that "I love New York like nobody else and this show is so steeped in New York. So, going back to New York is going to be a thrill for season 7. I do hope that might happen and then beyond that, though, we've learned a lesson from London, which is that the show can travel, and it really excites everybody and excites us to imagine where it might be able to go next."

Martin, who co-created the show with Hoffman, echoed his collaborator's sentiment by telling Deadline that “we’re always open to it [filming in new cities] but right now, our goal is to head back to New York City.” Short's follow-up comment of "yes, what he said," confirms that he agrees with the move back to New York as well.

These remarks from Hoffman, Martin, and Short confirm Deadline's Only Murders in the Building season 7 report from September, which is that the story and production are intended to return to New York, and the writers' room is moving there as well. As indicated in the comments from the series creators and stars, Hulu has not officially renewed season 7 yet, but ending the series would come as a surprise given how much of a hit it has been for the streamer and with the creative team already in the process of developing what the trio's next case will be.

The consistently positive reception and high streaming viewership is helped by the fact that the series has returned on an annual basis without fail since it debuted in 2021. That continues to be the case with season 6 premiering in December 2026, and it is a streak that the show will want to continue if Hulu makes the decision to officially greenlight season 7.

Hoffman, Martin, and Short's comments also confirm that after a potential season 7, taking the story and production to locations other than New York is very much a possibility following the successful experience of making season 6. Only Murders in the Building season 4 did partly take place in Los Angeles, but its episodes were still more so rooted in New York, which is what makes the show's newest iteration and what it opens the door to all the more significant.

Charles, Oliver, Mabel, and Cinda aren't the only established characters in Only Murders in the Building's season 6 story, with Michael Cyril Creighton, Da'Vine Joy Randolph, and Meryl Streep reprising their respective roles of Howard Morris, Detective Donna Williams, and Loretta Durkin. New actors joining them this time include Simone Ashley, Matthew Beard, Jim Broadbent, Peter Capaldi, Olivia Colman, Nicola Coughlan, Jamie Demetriou, Martin Freeman, Richard E. Grant, Geri Halliwell-Horner, Jane Horrocks, Sharon Horgan, Lesley Nicol, Jennifer Saunders, Sean Teale, David Tennant, Anjana Vasan, and Jodie Whittaker.

In terms of Tennant's Only Murders in the Building season 6 role, the Doctor Who actor previously clarified that "I’m the definition of a cameo. I think you may also call it a very small part, but it’s either a very small part or it’s a cameo, depending on how confident I’m feeling. But lovely to be part of that, and with those legends, what a treat!" The season features three stars who have played Doctor Who between him, Capaldi, and Whittaker, but with character details under wraps, it's unknown if any of them have scenes together.

Only Murders in the Building season 6 premieres on Hulu on December 8.
