While the Nintendo Switch 2 has surged to prominence again, the Steam Deck and other handheld PCs of its ilk have shown there’s huge demand for consoles that let you take your Steam (and other launcher) libraries on the go.

One of our most pleasantly surprising entrants in this field, the MSI Claw 8 EX AI+. It's one of the best you can find, but it would be fair to say its price tag put a few folks off. Now, thanks to a handy voucher code at Very (valid until tomorrow!), you can save £300 off the sticker price.

Use voucher code FLASH20 for 20% off, which brings the price down to £1199.

The code you’ll need to apply at checkout is FLASH20, and it’s not just limited to gaming handhelds. In fact, it applies to selected Copilot+, laptops, and PCs.

If you’re just using it here, though, you’re unlikely to be disappointed. My colleague Jason England reviewed the Claw 8 EX AI+ at launch and found its only real drawback to be the price. In fact, he even dubbed it the “best PC gaming handheld” he’d tested, awarding it four stars out of five.

The MSI Claw 8 EX AI+ has the Intel Arc G3 chip driving things under the hood, which is essentially a supercharged chip that offers impressive performance while not instantly draining the battery the moment you attempt to play something more demanding.

It’s also packing 32GB of RAM (which is probably a good reason the price is as high as it is, given the ongoing RAM crisis) and 1TB of storage, as well as a microSD card slot. It has a unique “chin” to the display (more on that in a moment) that actually helps with ergonomics, and there’s improved button feel and haptics over MSI’s prior models.

While we’d have loved OLED (especially at this price point), the IPS panel here has a variable refresh rate for smoother gameplay, and 8-inches, is actually big enough to get some work done in a pinch with a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse combo. In fact, because it’s running Windows 11, you also get the Xbox Full Screen Experience for a more console-like experience: The best of both worlds.
