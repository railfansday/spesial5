ELLE Decoroscope: Your November Design Horoscope Is Here

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The retrogrades end just in time for hosting season.

Welcome to Decoroscopes, ELLE DECOR’s monthly astrology column. Each month, we tap into the cosmic forecast to guide your home design decisions, from major renovations to subtle refreshes. Whether you’re considering a bold paint color or debating a furniture upgrade, let the stars be your guide.

November brings a welcome development for anyone tired of discussing the same thing over and over again: a chance to make a decision. Mercury and Venus both turn direct on November 13, ending weeks of reconsideration around communication, money, relationships, and taste. Before then, the Scorpio New Moon on November 9 asks for some honesty.

Once the decisions start coming, watch how quickly the plans grow. Mars meets Jupiter in Leo on November 16, lending creative projects plenty of confidence—and very little restraint. The Gemini Full Moon on the 24th brings conversations to a head, followed by Mars’s move into practical Virgo on the 25th. Read on for each sign’s November horoscope, design note, and color of the month.

November begins with the less photogenic side of sharing a life: joint expenses, obligations, and agreements that may need updating. The Scorpio New Moon on the 9th encourages a fresh approach, but start by establishing what everyone is actually contributing. When Mercury and Venus turn direct on the 13th, a financial discussion or relationship question may become easier to resolve. Then Mars, your ruling planet, meets Jupiter in your creative sector on the 16th, giving you plenty of appetite for something more enjoyable. Pursue the idea, but leave enough time and money to finish it. Mars enters your routines sector on the 25th; the practical work will want its turn.

Design note: Give a creative project a place to stay out between sessions. This month’s enthusiasm deserves better than being packed away after every use.

Your preferences are not necessarily the problem this month. The challenge is making room for someone else’s without abandoning your own. The Scorpio New Moon on November 9 falls in your partnership sector, opening a conversation about what a relationship or collaboration needs next. Venus, your ruling planet, turns direct on the 13th, alongside Mercury, helping you move beyond the review stage. Meanwhile, Mars and Jupiter meet in your home sector on the 16th, potentially expanding your domestic ambitions. Before the project grows another room, the Gemini Full Moon on the 24th asks you to look at the cost. Enthusiasm is useful, but an agreed budget is better.

Design note: Before expanding a project, finish the part you originally planned.

The first half of November is about making your days less cumbersome. The Scorpio New Moon on the 9th highlights work and routines, while Mercury, your ruling planet, turns direct on the 13th. A process that has required repeated explanations may finally be ready for a simpler version. Later, the Full Moon in your sign on November 24 brings your own needs into focus, particularly where you have been accommodating other people’s expectations. Then Mars enters your home sector on the 25th, bringing energy to domestic projects—and potentially less patience with anyone standing in the way. Ask for help before issuing instructions.

Design note: Start with the part of your home that creates extra work every day—fixing that will matter more than taking on the most visible thing first.

The Scorpio New Moon on November 9 puts creativity and enjoyment ahead of another round of household administration. Give an idea you like enough attention to discover whether you want to pursue it. Venus turning direct in your home sector on the 13th may also bring clarity to a decorating choice or family discussion that has been difficult to settle. Mid-month, Mars and Jupiter meet in your money sector, encouraging confidence about your resources. Just distinguish between having room in the budget and feeling briefly invincible. By the Gemini Full Moon on the 24th, a quieter schedule may sound more appealing than another invitation.

Design note: Spend on something you’ll enjoy using, not something you’ll feel obliged to save for company. Pleasure is part of the budget, too.

You have energy to spare this month, but the people at home may have their own ideas about where it should go. The Scorpio New Moon on November 9 brings attention to your living situation, family, and domestic plans. Mercury turning direct there on the 13th can help restart a conversation that has gone in circles. Then Mars meets Jupiter in your sign on the 16th, making you more inclined to take the lead. That confidence is useful, but assuming everyone has already agreed is less so. When Mars enters your money sector on the 25th, the focus shifts from what you want to do to what you are prepared to pay for.

Design note: Separate what you want to change from what genuinely needs changing.

November starts with a crowded inbox and several details that would benefit from being written down. The Scorpio New Moon on the 9th highlights communication and everyday logistics; Mercury turning direct on the 13th helps you revisit a muddled exchange with a clearer head. The pace changes late in the month: The Sun enters your home sector on the 22nd, the Gemini Full Moon highlights your career on the 24th, and Mars enters your sign on the 25th. You may suddenly feel ready to tackle everything at once, but choose an order. Being capable of handling a project does not mean you need to begin it tonight.

Design note: Decide which project gets your attention first—and let the others wait.

Venus, your ruling planet, turns direct in your sign on November 13. After weeks of reconsidering your preferences, relationships, or presentation, you may feel less inclined to ask for one more opinion. Mercury turns direct the same day in your money sector, where the Scorpio New Moon on the 9th has already encouraged a closer look at income and spending. Together, these shifts favor a clearer sense of what is worth your investment. Late in the month, the Gemini Full Moon draws your attention toward travel, study, or an idea outside your usual frame of reference. Look beyond the accounts and shops you already know by heart.

Design note: Choose the piece you want to live with, not the one that gets the most compliments.

The New Moon in your sign on November 9 gives you a chance to set a personal priority before the month becomes crowded with other people’s requests. Mercury turns direct in Scorpio on the 13th, making it easier to explain a decision you may still have been working out yourself. You do not need elaborate justification, just a clear answer. Mars and Jupiter meet in your career sector on the 16th, supporting a more visible or ambitious move. Then the Gemini Full Moon on the 24th brings shared money and commitments into focus. Make sure the terms support the opportunity, rather than hoping the opportunity will make the terms worthwhile.

Design note: Leave room in the budget for the unglamorous parts of an upgrade.

The first part of November is quieter than your birthday-season reputation might suggest. The Scorpio New Moon on the 9th falls in your private sector, favoring time to think without immediately turning the result into a plan. Mercury and Venus turn direct on the 13th, helping a private concern or friendship question become less tangled. Your season begins on November 22, followed by a Gemini Full Moon in your partnership sector on the 24th. Someone else’s needs may be especially clear, but that does not make yours negotiable by default. With Mars entering your career sector on the 25th, agree on what you can realistically give before work claims the rest .

Design note: If your home doubles as an office, make it possible to put work away. You shouldn’t have to look at tomorrow’s assignments throughout dinner.

November’s opening weeks turn your attention towards friends, collaborators, and plans that require more than your own effort. The Scorpio New Moon on the 9th could help you establish a new connection or give an existing project a clearer purpose. When Mercury and Venus turn direct on the 13th, discussions inviting your wider circle and professional direction may start moving again. The Gemini Full Moon on the 24th highlights your workload and routines, making it easier to see what has become unnecessarily complicated. Before accepting another responsibility, consider whether you want it—or whether everyone has simply become accustomed to you saying yes.

Design note: Retire a household routine that creates more work than it saves.

The Scorpio New Moon on November 9 brings a professional goal into focus. Mercury is still retrograde in that part of your chart until the 13th, so use the early days to refine what you want to say rather than assuming the first version is final. Mid-month, Mars meets Jupiter in your partnership sector, giving a collaboration considerable momentum. It may also make another person’s confidence difficult to distinguish from a workable plan. Ask practical questions. The Gemini Full Moon on the 24th turns attention towards creativity and enjoyment, reminding you that a personal project does not need a business case to deserve your time.

Design note: Give an unfinished creative project another chance before clearing it away.

November begins with an invitation to expand your references. The Scorpio New Moon on the 9th favors study, travel, or a subject that draws you beyond your usual interests. Mercury turning direct there on the 13th helps you return to a plan with better information. Later, the Gemini Full Moon on November 24 lands in your home sector, bringing a domestic matter to a point of clarity. You may recognize that a room, arrangement, or family expectation needs to change. Mars enters your partnership sector the following day, making direct discussion more important—and potentially more heated. State the problem without assigning someone the role of villain.

Design note: Identify the change you want before discussing how to achieve it.
