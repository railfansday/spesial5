Art Manager (Creative Services) at Yodo1

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

You will: Lead and rebuild our in-house Creative Services team into a fast, reliable art partner for our games, PC publishing and marketing teams.

Must have: An art background plus proven experience running art production: scoping, estimating, scheduling and budgeting work across in-house artists and external vendors.

At Yodo1, we don't just build and grow games, we turn them into global successes that become household names.

With 14+ years in the games space, we've reached 3.5 billion+ players and supported 6,000+ developers worldwide. From games like Transformers: Earth Wars, Rodeo Stampede and Ski Safari, to bringing iconic IPs from Hasbro, AMC, Netflix, Paramount and Sony Pictures to leading studios like Rivergame, Candivore, 37Games and Tencent Studios, we connect creativity with global expertise.

Our work spans game development, publishing, IP licensing and ad monetization across mobile, PC and console. We build games, grow franchises and help developers reach players worldwide. We love games. We play games. And together, we grow games that last.

We believe in the power of games and that everyone deserves the opportunity to build a life they love, from anywhere in the world.

We're fully remote by design. We embrace Work From Anywhere, with no fixed hours or limits on time off. We trust our people to own their work and deliver results.

We're driven by performance, curiosity, and continuous improvement. We experiment often, challenge assumptions and value impact over titles.

Yodo1 is looking for an Art Manager to lead our in-house Creative Services team and turn it into the go-to art partner for our game studios, our PC publishing label Spicy Koala and our marketing team. You will sit between the people who need art and the people who make it: understand what each team needs, plan how and when it gets delivered, decide whether it is made in-house or outsourced, and make sure it ships on time, on budget and on brand.

Focused on impact. You understand the 80/20 rule and focus on activities that matter.

Performance-driven and growth-oriented. You have an intrinsic will to succeed while unafraid to try, fail, and learn by experimenting with new approaches and testing ideas.

Cooperative. You help others succeed; you give and receive feedback well.

Humble. You ask for help; you are quick to admit and learn from your mistakes.

SMART. You work and communicate in ways that are Specific, Measurable, Actionable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

Lead and manage the Creative Services team as the single point of contact for all art, video and creative requests.

Assess the team's skills and capacity, close the gaps and restructure it for a growing developer and publisher, deciding what we make in-house and what we outsource.

Run a clear request process, from brief to delivery, with confident estimates of time, people and cost, and say no early when something isn't feasible.

Deliver ad creatives, videos, store assets, key art, logos, trailers and in-game art support, within each game's art direction and licensor guidelines.

Manage freelancers and outsourcing partners, own the team's budget and report on turnaround, cost and stakeholder satisfaction.

Stay hands-on with paintovers, feedback and asset production, and keep improving pipelines and tools.

An art background (2D, 3D, motion or video) with 6+ years in games including 2+ years managing artists or art production.

Proven experience scoping, estimating, scheduling and budgeting art work, and comfortable working like a producer.

Experience managing outsourcing studios and freelancers, from briefing to final delivery.

Experience producing PC/Steam publishing assets (capsules, key art, trailers) and mobile UA creatives.

A strong eye for quality and the ability to work within an existing art direction or style guide and give clear, actionable feedback.

Solid understanding of art and video production pipelines, tools (e.g. Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Premiere Pro), file formats and delivery specs.

Strong stakeholder management: you set clear expectations, communicate proactively and keep internal customers informed.

Excellent communication and collaboration within cross-functional and globally distributed teams.

Experience building or restructuring a creative services, shared art or marketing art team.

Experience working with licensed IP and licensor approval processes.

Experience with game engines (e.g. Unity) and integrating in-game art assets.

Proficiency in production tracking tools (e.g. Jira, ShotGrid, Notion or similar).

Familiarity with AI-assisted art and video tools and how to use them responsibly in a production pipeline.

Experience working with teams across different locations, cultures, and time zones.

Compensation. We pay based on qualifications, experience, and fit for the role. We provide an allowance to purchase your own device or equipment and offer variable compensation through our annual bonus program.

Growth. We offer an environment where you will have the trust and freedom to try, fail, learn fast, and make a real impact while accelerating your career, and where anyone with a great idea can pitch it directly to our management team.

WFA. Work from anywhere in the world, travel when and where you like, get paid in your currency of choice, and enjoy flexible hours and unlimited paid time off.

Community. Work with an energetic, fast-learning, supportive, and diverse team of Yodies across six continents and 30+ countries who share and support new ideas and get things done.

New remote jobs delivered to your inbox, every week.

Location independence is the future of work. It serves companies with best talent from around the globe. And benefits employees with a greater work life balance and the ability to work in their pyjamas.- Nithur, founder.
