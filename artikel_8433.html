i agree with those that say an AA target should not be your portfolio driver

I would recommend HNW investors keep 7 to 10 years living expenses in fixed - either in tips or treasuries or CDs and keep the rest in equities...obviously you have to sell equities and replenish your fixed every year or 2, and/or make adjustments if you have sudden expenses or situations that require you to spend more fixed than planned.

of the fixed expenses i would keep a rolling year in MMF so there is nothing to sell prematurely, replenish this quarterly or semi-annually.

this could result in your AA being anywhere from 90/10 to 10/90 depending on your living expenses vs your portfolio size.
With HNW folks having larger amounts of div income why the need for 7-10 years of fixed if they really have all expenses covered?
