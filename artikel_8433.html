Open FourLeaf Simple Savings if your balance will average $1,000 or more every month. It pays a 4.50% annual percentage yield on every dollar, and anyone in the U.S. can apply to join the credit union. Below that average, its $10 monthly charge costs more than the balance earns, so keep smaller savings in a no-fee account.

Simple Savings is a high-rate savings account from FourLeaf Federal Credit Union, renamed from Bethpage Federal Credit Union in March 2025, and it suits members who keep a steady balance. At a 4.50% APY (annual percentage yield), a balance averaging just under $1,000 earns about $3.70 in a month, so that month’s $10 charge leaves you more than $6 behind.

Savers who will keep well over $1,000 here every month earn 4.50% on every dollar and never pay the charge. Anyone starting smaller, or likely to dip below $1,000 in a month, should pick a high-yield savings account with no monthly fee instead.
