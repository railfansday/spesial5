Texans claim Nikko Remigio, release Dare Ogunbowale

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Texans have made a couple of roster moves on Friday.

Houston announced the team has claimed receiver/returner Nikko Remigio off of waivers.

The Chiefs let Remigio go earlier this week.

In his fourth season, Remigio averaged 26.5 yards per kick return and 5.6 yards per punt return this season for Kansas City.

As a corresponding move, the Texans have released running back Dare Ogunbowale, who is subject to waivers now that the trading deadline has passed.
