Bills add Ed Oliver to injury report, list him as questionable

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Bills made a late addition to their injury report for Sunday’s game against the Dolphins.

Defensive tackle Ed Oliver is now listed as questionable to play in Buffalo’s home game against their AFC East foes. Oliver is dealing with an oblique injury.

It’s not the first time that Oliver has been a late addition to the injury list this season. He hurt his hip during pregame warmups in Week 2 and missed the Bills’ home win over the Lions. Oliver has 13 tackles, a half-sack and a fumble recovery in six games this season.

The Bills ruled cornerback Christian Benford and linebacker Dorian Williams out on Friday. Safety Damar Hamlin and defensive tackle T.J. Sanders are the other defensive players listed as questionable for Sunday.
