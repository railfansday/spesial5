Buffalo Bills DB Te’Cory Couch signed to practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Buffalo Bills announced a series of roster moves as they begin preparations to host the Miami Dolphins at Highmark Stadium for Week 11. Buffalo has signed defensive back Te'Cory Couch to the practice squad, bringing him back to One Bills Drive after being released ahead of last weekend's game and once he cleared waivers Monday afternoon.

The 27-year-old defender out of the University of Miami has played in four games for Buffalo this season. In those games, Couch has made eight tackles (6 solo), showcasing a knack for limiting big plays after the catch with solid tackling. Couch joined the Bills in 2024, entering the NFL as an undrafted rookie free agent.

To make room for Couch, Buffalo once again parted ways with veteran offensive guard Nick Broeker, who has been re-added to the team's practice squad several times already this season. Broeker has yet to appear in a regular-season game for the Bills, though he has previously played in 12 games (2023 and 2024 seasons) for the Denver Broncos. Broeker was originally drafted by Buffalo in Round 7 (230th overall) of the 2023 NFL Draft.

The Bills also announced that rookie offensive guard Ar'maj Reed-Adams has been placed on Injured Reserve, which will sideline him for at least the next four games. With Reed-Adams heading to IR, that opens up a spot on Buffalo's 53-man roster.

A potential candidate to take over that roster opening is veteran wide receiver Terrell Shavers, whose return-to-practice window was recently opened as the next step toward a return to the field following a devastating knee injury against the Jacksonville Jaguars during the Bills' 2025 NFL playoff run.
