New York Jets News: Insider Says There is Geno Smith Extension Interest?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The New York Jets' 2026 record sucks: 2-7. But don't blame Geno Smith for the team's struggles; he's been playing at a very high level.

So high that the team is very interested in re-signing him this offseason. Zack Rosenblatt of The Athletic wrote, "They are expected to seriously consider bringing him back in 2027."

The Jets love what he has brought on and off the field. Smith is scheduled to be an unrestricted free agent this offseason. The Jets are going to need a bridge quarterback in 2027; why not Smith?

People respect him in the locker room. He holds guys accountable, and his individual stats on the field are eyebrow-raising. Rosenblatt highlighted some of his success statistically in his column:

18th in EPA per dropback — and the 6th-best EPA on deep passes (thrown 20-plus yards in the air, per TruMedia)

Per TruMedia, Smith has only charted a negative EPA per dropback in three games

This interest in a potential extension makes a ton of sense. Especially since the Jets apparently rejected trade interest from other teams in Smith at the deadline.
