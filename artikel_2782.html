Game Preview #16: Wolves vs Knicks Game Thread

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Following Thursday's drunken back-and-forth beautiful mess of a come-from-behind win, it's time for Karl-Anthony Towns and the NBA Champion New York Knicks to come to town for their first matchup of the season. Despite winning seven of their last eight games, this will be the Wolves' third game in four nights after having a very physical back-to-back against the Portland Trail Blazers.

Meanwhile, the Knicks have won six of their last eight games and will now travel to Minnesota at the vert tail end of a four-game road trip. They most recently played Tuesday night in Toronto where they took care of a Kawhi Leonard-less Raptors team in the clutch.

It is Karl-Anthony Towns that deserves first billing in this matchup. In his previous two visits to Target Center since the trade, KAT is averaging 36 points, 16.5 rebounds, 3.5 assists, and is a +37 in all his minutes in the opposing team's uniform in Minneapolis.

At an 8-6 overall record, don't let the Knicks middling record fool you into thinking this team is just cruising through the regular season with a championship hangover. Since their 0-3 start, the Knicks are 8-3 with all of their losses in that time coming within single digits. In those 11 games, they are the third ranked team in net rating, trailing just the San Antonio Spurs and Boston Celtics. They are traveling to Minneapolis with a fully healthy squad and will be looking to stack more wins to climb out of the mire of the middle of the Eastern Conference.

Who: Minnesota Timberwolves (11-4) vs New York Knicks (8-6)

Where: Target Center, Minneapolis Minnesota

Key Strength Stat: Points in the paint (5th)

Key Weakness Stat: Assist Percentage (28th)

Spider-Man Pointing Meme Stat: Second Chance Points (3rd)

Key Strength Stat: Offensive Rebounding Percentage (1st)

Spider-Man Pointing Meme Stat: Second Chance Points (1st)

Over the last ten games, the Wolves are second in the league in points in the paint thanks in large part to Ayo Dosunmo's increased usage and desire to win a footrace to the rim each time he catches the ball. The Knicks are the third best team at defending the paint thanks to a core of very versatile wing defenders who are able to switch on everything and stay in prime position defensively. Couple their paint defense with being the best rebounding team in the league and it is clear where their money is made on the defensive end.

They want to force you to hit shots from midrange and beyond, and then grab rebounds with their long and athletic wings.

The Wolves are no slouches themselves with the sixth best rebounding percentage. They utilize a very similar front court when they are fully healthy. The Knicks play at the slowest pace in the league and utilize a half-court offense that is going to feature a lot of Jalen Brunson trying to get to the rim and draw (or at least make it look like he's drawn) contact. The Wolves defend well without fouling (5th best opponent free throw rate), but the NBA Finals MVP always has a few tricks up his sleeve that could get Wolves defenders in foul trouble.

If things are mucked up in the paint like the above numbers suggest, the importance of the Wolves making the "one extra pass" to an open shooter and converting could well be the secret sauce to unlocking offense against a very versatile defensive team. Isolation basketball that leans too heavily into the Wolves' 28th ranked assisting percentage will absolutely make this game feel like the Wolves are pushing a boulder uphill all night long.

The Knicks starting group of Jalen Brunson, Mikal Bridges, Josh Hart, OG Anunoby and KAT is a rare starting group that is as versatile as the Wolves. If Kuminga can't go and Minnesota chooses to put Jaden McDaniels on Jalen Brunson, there are going to be a lot of matchup issues for the Wolves. Assuming LaMelo Ball takes Josh Hart, Dosunmo will shift to Bridges, leaving OG Anunoby with a tantalizing matchup with either a size advantage on Cody Williams, or a speed and athleticism advantage on Trey "Elite Rim Protector" Lyles.

While Dosunmo could be on Brunson duty instead McDaniels, the defensive scheme makes a whole lot more sense if Kuminga is good to go.

My prediction is all based on the X-Factor of Jonathan Kuminga's availability Thursday. If he plays, the Wolves win a physical back-and-forth defensive slug fest 106-102 where Kuminga can match physicality with OG Anunoby.

If he's out, the Wolves just won't have quite enough bodies to throw at the trio of Brunson, Bridges, and Anunoby, losing 116-106. Wait for Kuminga's game status to be announced then go place your bets as the outcome has already been foretold!
