Will Pochettino Have The Players To Improve Manchester United?

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Manchester United has a new manager. Or at least it will once Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of his final two matches as head coach of the US men's national team. Once those games are out of the way, Pochettino will take over at Old Trafford in time for United's next Premier League fixture against Liverpool.

Pochettino has long been linked with the Manchester United job, stretching all the way back to his time at Tottenham Hotspur. For years, it had seemed destined that the Argentine would one day take the reins at Old Trafford and now Pochettino will be United manager after a two-year stint in international soccer as USA boss.

United is in a perilous situation. The club finds itself in the bottom three of the Premier League table with recent results and performances suggesting Manchester United is in the midst of a relegation battle. Pochettino is picking up United at its lowest ebb in the post-Sir Alex Ferguson age.

It's highly doubtful that the current group of players at Old Trafford will be able to play the way Pochettino favours. The Argentine is known for his aggressive, attack-minded style of play and demands the highest fitness levels from his players. So far this season, though, Manchester United's physical data has been unimpressive.

At Tottenham, Pochettino built his team on a sound defensive foundation. He was able to call on the likes of Toby Alderweireld and Jan Vertonghen. Manchester United, in contrast, is a defensive mess at the moment. The defensive ranks have been neglected over the years with Diogo Dalot, Harry Maguire and Luke Shaw still hanging around when they should have been replaced a long time ago.

MANCHESTER, ENGLAND - NOVEMBER 08: Kobbie Mainoo of Manchester United during the Premier League 2026/27 match between Manchester United and Aston Villa FC at Old Trafford on November 08, 2026 in Manchester, England. (Photo by Michael Regan/Getty Images)

There is quality in the United squad. Bruno Fernandes was the Player of the Year in the Premier League last season. Matheus Cunha and Bryan Mbeumo are proven performers at the top level of English soccer while the likes of Kobbie Mainoo, Carlos Baleba, Amad Diallo, Shea Lacey and Leny Yoro the sort of young players Pochettino likes to work with.

With the January transfer window just around the corner, Manchester United needs strengthening in a number of key areas. Pochettino surely needs new full backs and at least one new central defender. A back-up centre forward for the injury Benjamin Sesko would also be handy.

In the short term, Pochettino must focus on pushing Manchester United up the Premier League table and away from relegation trouble. Doing that with the squad he has been left by Michael Carrick, however, won't be easy.
