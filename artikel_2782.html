Bright Side Wonders, Vol 9: Devin Booker, a surging bench, and a team finding its identity

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Week 5 of the NBA season delivered the Phoenix Suns their first undefeated week of the season. I don't think many fans would have expected to read those words this month when the squad started the year 1-5, but here we are. Phoenix beat their three opponents by a combined 43 points this week and are 3-0 with wins against the Nuggets, Kings, and Jazz to start their five-game road trip.

The bench is finding their rhythm, Devin Booker is leading the scoring attack, and their win against Denver showed they can compete with some of the Western Conference's best teams.

Going into week six with the team on a three-game win streak, here's what we want your answers on for this week's edition of Bright Side Wonders:

How have the Suns been able to not only string together three-straight wins, but seven of their last nine and turn what once looked like a spiraling start to the season into a team over .500?

The second unit is playing with harmony, with Jordan Goodwin, Collin Gillespie, and Luke Kennard providing defensive toughness, playmaking, and shooting. The offense has been able to withstand opponents' second-half runs. Devin Booker has consistently provided the team with a reliable scorer, as he has throughout his career, and Oso Ighodaro has provided some much-needed depth to the starting lineup since he replaced Khaman Maluach at center.

Early season trades are very rare in the NBA, and only a handful have happened in the past few seasons, with James Harden to the Clippers in 2023 being the most notable. Most trades result from pressure, specifically the trade deadline in February.
