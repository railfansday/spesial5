Opponent Breakdown: Pitt Panthers Defense

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Pat Narduzzi knows defense, and he is fielding another great group this year for the Panthers. After losing Randy Bates to retirement, Narduzzi elevated long-time assistant Cory Saunders to the defensive coordinator spot. The Panthers haven't skipped a beat with the second-ranked run defense in the country, and defenders at all three levels making big plays each week.

Nick James sets the tone up front for Pitt from his defensive tackle spot. The veteran big man leads the team with 32 pressures to go along with his 8 tackles for loss. Pitt has always relied on at least one defensive tackle who can cause havoc up front and chew up blocks to let their linebackers make plays behind them. James is a mix of all of the very good players they've had over the years. He can be a menace for blocking schemes, but he can also go and make plays himself. He will be a handful for the Louisville offensive line this weekend.

The Panthers were without Sean Fitzsimmons this past weekend, but the defense didn't skip a beat. The mainstay in the middle of this defense is nearly impossible to move at the point of attack, and like James, he is steeped in this defensive scheme. Look for Jeremiah Warren to pick up the slack if Fitzsimmons can't go.

The edges are manned by Isaiah Neal and Jimmy Scott, who are also returners after being key cogs in this defense last year. Both have the size and motor that are valued by Pitt, and they play their role well to stretch out outside runs so that the full defense can attack the ball. They are also good at pushing the pocket as pass rushers. While these guys are the starters, Jaeden Moore is the guy to watch on third downs. He leads the team in sacks and tackles for loss.

Linebacker is the position that has fallen off on this defense, which is to be expected when Kyle Louis declares for the draft, and Rasheem Biles grabs a massive bag from Texas in the portal. Braylan Lovelace is a returning starter, but he has not been able to account for the lost production. He and Jeremiah Marcelin have not been making the havoc plays that we saw from Louis and Biles. That's not to say that they are playing poorly, but the defense needs those big plays, and they typically come from the linebacker position.

Pitt is solid in the secondary as usual, but they have an emerging star in Shawn Lee. The true sophomore broke out as a freshman, and he has only improved his game this season. Lee has not given up a touchdown this season while being an absolute lockdown coverage corner in zone and in man assignments. He and Raion Strader make up the best cornerback duo in the ACC, in my opinion. Strader has allowed a couple of touchdowns, but he is holding opposing quarterbacks to just a 59.2 passer rating, per PFF. These guys are the real deal.

Cruce Brookins is the top player on a very good defense for Pitt. He leads the team in tackles from his safety spot, and he does just about everything else you want from your top safety. He is very good in run support, but I've been impressed with how well he has played at the point of attack in pass defense. He has 6 pass breakups, with some of those plays looking like a cornerback in coverage. The Panthers have struggled to a degree against the pass this year, but it's not due to a lack of talent in the back end.
