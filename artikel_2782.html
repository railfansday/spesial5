Buccaneers claim OL Trevor Keegan off waivers

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Buccaneers are adding some depth to their offensive line.

Per Mike Garafolo of NFL Network, Tampa Bay has claimed Trevor Keegan off of waivers.

Keegan, 26, appeared in seven games for Minnesota this season. He was on the field for 30 special teams snaps and three offensive snaps.

The Vikings had claimed Keegan off waivers in September. An Eagles fifth-round pick in 2024, Keegan has also spent time with the Cowboys.
