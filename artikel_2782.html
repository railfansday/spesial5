Jets 2027 first-round pick tracker: Where things stand after Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

In a lost season, Jets fans can only start looking to the NFL Draft. The good news is the team is set to have three first-round picks, and all three of them are looking like they will be high.

Following the Thanksgiving matchup between the Cowboys and Eagles, the Jets now own three of the top fourteen picks in the 2027 NFL Draft. Philadelphia's incredible comeback victory moves Dallas' pick up in the order before the bulk of the weekend games

The Jets have their own first round pick. They also have Indianapolis' from the Sauce Gardner trade and whichever first round pick between Dallas' and Green Bay's is higher from the Micah Parsons trade.

Picks 19 through 32 are determined by the results of games in the NFL olayoffs.
