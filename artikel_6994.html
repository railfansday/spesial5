Senior Architect, Enterprise Agentic Automation at Camunda

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Camunda is the enterprise platform for agentic orchestration, enabling organizations to coordinate AI agents, people, and systems across complex, end-to-end business processes. With built-in governance, auditability, and human oversight, Camunda gives enterprises the control they need to move AI from pilots to production — safely and at scale. Trusted by over 700 organizations worldwide, including 9 of top 10 US banks, Camunda helps enterprises boost operational efficiency, accelerate time-to-value, and deliver better customer experiences.Fully remote and global, we are in the middle of something bigger: transforming into an AI-first organisation, built on our own platform. We use Agentic AI to automate, orchestrate intelligent processes, and elevate human contribution across every team.Named GP Bullhound’s Top 100 Next Unicorn list, 2025 Great Place to Work certified. Visionary in 2025 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Business Orchestration and Automation Technologies. ranked 3rd in Flexa's 2026 Most Flexible Companies, We’re growing fast and looking for top talent to join our team. If you want meaningful work, visible impact and put something genuinely rare on your CV, keep reading.

We run Camunda on Camunda. Our Enterprise Agentic Automation Team (EAAT) builds production-grade agentic orchestration using Camunda's platform for Camunda's own operations, from Finance to Support to Go-to-Market. As Senior Architect, you own delivery on our EAAT-led initiatives end to end. You partner with process owners to uncover where the biggest value lies, redesign how the work should flow, decide where agents, rules or people handle each step, and shape it into scope our engineers can build from directly. Then you lead technical delivery management with our engineers, steering increments, dependencies and decisions until you get it live and prove the value. You won't design and hand over: you stay until it works in production, and keep evolving it.

Own delivery of our use cases end to end, business and technical, from discovery to production and value realization

Run discovery with process owners: as-is baseline, target process, and where agents, rules or humans handle each step

Turn target processes into epics engineers can build from directly: scenarios, acceptance criteria, increments

Design solutions within our reference architecture, and bring new patterns based on lessons learned

Lead technical delivery on your initiatives: plan increments with the engineers, manage dependencies and risks, and keep process owners and sponsors aligned on progress and decisions

Define and track value per initiative and increment, and keep business decisions moving

Feed delivery learnings back into the reference architecture, our delivery method and Camunda's product

Ability and/or willingness to use our product.

7+ years in solution architecture or technical/process consulting, with an engineering background (Java, JavaScript or similar) and significant time delivering for the business or customers

A track record of leading process orchestration initiatives (Camunda or comparable) from discovery to production, not just designing them and handing over

Strong business analysis and process management skills: runs discovery and process redesign workshops with business stakeholders, models processes in BPMN, and turns the outcome into solid solution designs

Solution and data architecture in an enterprise context: integrations and APIs, and the data a process and its agents need across systems

An AI-first mindset, including first experience designing and building enterprise-grade agentic solutions

Holds their own with senior stakeholders and gets decisions made

Experience in Finance or other back-office process domains and operations (P2P, Q2C)

Experience building value cases from operational data (baselines, volumes, value per increment), value engineering or benefits tracking

Experience with process intelligence or mining tools

Working knowledge of enterprise systems such as NetSuite, Salesforce or Zip
