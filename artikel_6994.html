Coder Technologies Inc.: Paid Media Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

How do you reach people who don't want to be marketed to? You lead with value. That's our approach with developers and the platform and security leaders who buy Coder: ads that speak to the problems they're actually solving, like where their AI agents run, who governs them, and how to keep code and data under their control.

Coder is defining the AI Operating Layer, and paid media is one of the main ways we take that story to market. As our Paid Media Manager, you'll own the strategy and performance of our always-on programs across LinkedIn, Google Search, YouTube, and newer platforms like ChatGPT ads, aimed at our enterprise and target accounts. The goal is better, not just more: every dollar should reach the right people, tell the right story, and turn into pipeline.

You'll direct our paid media agency and give them the Coder context they can't get on their own (ICP, positioning, target accounts, business goals). You're just as comfortable in execution as in strategy. You can audit accounts, fix what's broken, or jump in to launch campaigns, and you know what good looks like down to the platform settings.

You'll report to our Demand Generation lead and work closely with product marketing, design, web, marketing ops, ABM, regional marketing, and GTM engineering.

Own the strategy and performance of our paid channels: LinkedIn (sponsored content, document ads, thought leader ads, video and CTV), Google Search, YouTube, and ChatGPT ads.

Lead our paid media agency day-to-day. Set priorities, review launches and ad copy, and hold the work to clear timelines and results.

Regularly audit our ad accounts, including campaign structure, settings, audiences, conversion tracking, and spend, and jump in hands-on to build, restructure, or fix campaigns when needed.

Own budget allocation, forecasting, and monthly pacing across channels and regions. Recommend where to scale, cut, or pause based on pipeline quality, not just clicks.

Run a structured test-and-learn agenda across audiences, keywords and match types, offers, creative, formats, and new channels. Define the hypothesis up front and document what we learn.

Build targeting around our ICP and target account lists, including retargeting, exclusion audiences, and intent signals.

Tailor programs for NAMER, EMEA, and APAC and for priority verticals like financial services, partnering with regional marketing on what each market needs.

Partner with PMM and design to keep ad copy and creative current with our positioning, and share back what's resonating. Work with web on landing pages and conversion paths.

Own paid reporting: monthly readouts that connect spend to pipeline and revenue, with a clear point of view on what we should do next.

Deliver paid air cover for launches and key moments like AWS re:Invent.

5–7 years of paid media or performance marketing experience, including owning multi-channel paid strategy and budgets.

Deep hands-on expertise in LinkedIn Campaign Manager and Google Ads (Search and YouTube). You can build, restructure, and audit campaigns yourself, not just review an agency's work.

Experience managing agencies or vendors. You hold partners accountable to results and are still someone they want to work with.

A track record of owning and scaling meaningful budgets across multiple channels, including pacing, forecasting, and reallocation.

Experience leading global paid programs and adjusting strategy to align with each region's priorities and performance.

Comfort working with data. You can connect ad platform data with attribution and CRM tools like HockeyStack, HubSpot, or Salesforce, and turn it into decisions.

A strong grasp of B2B targeting and measurement: ICP, account lists, segmentation, retargeting, exclusions, and conversion tracking.

Strong project management skills across multiple campaigns, deadlines, and stakeholders, and clear communication when things shift.

Curiosity about using AI tools and workflows to speed up analysis, reporting, and execution.

Experience marketing to developers, platform engineers, or other technical buyers.

Experience with ABM or intent tools such as Vector, 6sense, Common Room, or HockeyStack.

Experience with emerging or programmatic channels like ChatGPT ads, CTV, StackAdapt, or Reddit.

Paid experience from B2C or other industries. We value people who bring ideas from different playbooks.

You don't need to know every tool on day one. This role works across LinkedIn Campaign Manager, Google Ads, HockeyStack, HubSpot, Salesforce, GA4, Asana, and AI tools like Claude.
