Spotify's Ringer podcast network dropped its ChatGPT deal after fan backlash

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Bill Simmons said the AI segments on a couple of Ringer shows were not a fit because the audience did not like them

Bill Simmons, chief executive of Spotify $SPOT’s Ringer podcast network, and Spotify Technology canceled their partnership with OpenAI’s ChatGPT after fan backlash, according to Bloomberg.

Simmons told Bloomberg in a statement that the Ringer began running segments built on AI-driven research on a couple of its shows in the spring, as some rivals and leagues such as the NFL were doing. "This time we learned almost immediately that those segments weren't a fit for us — our audience just didn't like them," he said.

The backlash built in August, according to Bloomberg. Sean Fennessey and Amanda Dobbins, hosts of the Ringer show "The Big Picture," appeared on a podcast hosted by Sam Fragoso. Fragoso asked how they felt about Simmons promoting ChatGPT on air. Simmons had asked the tool to channel the late film critic Roger Ebert and predict his opinion of a movie. He also had read ads for ChatGPT.

Fennessey, who co-hosts "The Rewatchables" with Simmons, said he had raised the issue with his boss. "I don't like it," he said, according to Bloomberg. Dobbins said she had not known about the ads. "Oh, brother," she said. "That's not my decision level."

Fans cut the exchange into short clips that spread widely across social media, according to Bloomberg. The cancellation came sometime afterward.

Other podcast hosts have faced similar reactions, according to Bloomberg. Ashley Flowers, host of "Crime Junkie," deleted an Instagram post this week in which she described relying on AI to look into candidates in the midterm elections, after commenters responded with anger. In May, self-help host Mel Robbins shared a post saying she had turned to Microsoft $MSFT Copilot to get her family's money matters in better shape. Commenters reacted angrily, and she added a reply beneath her own post urging followers to be "thoughtful" regarding the details they hand over to AI.

Not every host has met resistance, according to Bloomberg. Joe Rogan, Theo Von and Ben Shapiro have delivered sponsor spots for Perplexity AI without much objection from listeners, and "The Rest Is History" advertises Anthropic's Claude.

AI companies are increasing podcast spending, according to Bloomberg, which cited ad measurement service Podscribe. OpenAI is estimated to have put $11.2 million into podcast advertising during the last three months, a jump of more than 600% from a year earlier. Anthropic is estimated to have committed $2 million in that same window, a 93% increase.

Together the two companies represent a small share of the podcast ad market, which is worth $2.9 billion, according to Bloomberg.
