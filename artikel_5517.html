Davidson Homes, loanDepot launch in-house mortgage JV

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

loanDepot and Davidson Homes have launched Davidson Mortgage, a mortgage joint venture serving buyers in Alabama, Tennessee, Georgia, North Carolina, Texas and Arizona. The move reflects a broader builder trend toward affiliated mortgage operations to reduce fallout, manage incentives and improve closing predictability in a volatile rate environment.  AI Summary

Davidson Homes and loanDepot have formed a new joint venture, Davidson Mortgage, to provide integrated mortgage services for Davidson Homes buyers across six states.

The partnership expands loanDepot’s joint venture channel while giving Davidson Homes an in-house mortgage option aimed at keeping buyers inside the builder’s ecosystem from contract to closing, the companies said in a joint announcement.

Davidson Mortgage will serve homebuyers in Alabama, Tennessee, Georgia, North Carolina, Texas and Arizona, where Davidson Homes currently operates communities.

The JV combines loanDepot’s mortgage platform and product menu with Davidson Homes’ regional footprint and new-home sales pipeline. For builders, this type of structure can help reduce fallout, shorten cycle times and create more predictable closings, especially in a higher-rate, high-friction lending environment.

“Davidson Homes has built tremendous momentum by continuing to grow while building homes that reflect what today’s buyers want and need, with a strong focus on quality and the customer experience,” loanDepot President of Partnership Lending Dan Peña said in the announcement. “Davidson Mortgage builds on that success, pairing loanDepot’s lending platform and service with Davidson Homes’ customer-first approach to deliver a smooth financing experience homebuyers can trust.”

Davidson Mortgage is part of loanDepot’s strategy to grow its core mortgage franchise through a multi-channel platform that includes joint ventures, distributed retail and direct lending. For Davidson Homes, a private builder ranked No. 43 on the 2026 Builder 100, the move aligns with a broader industry push to capture more of the homebuying value chain through affiliated services.

“Our success has always been grounded in the relationships we build with homebuyers and the communities we serve,” Davidson Homes CEO Brandon Jones said. “With loanDepot’s reputation for combining technology with a customer-first approach, we’re providing buyers with a frictionless and personal financing experience.”

Joint venture and affiliated business models have become increasingly important profit and risk-management tools for builders as they navigate volatile rates, tighter underwriting and shifting buyer demand. Integrated mortgage operations can help builders manage incentives more strategically, coordinate rate locks with construction timelines and identify qualification issues earlier in the sales funnel.

LoanDepot, founded in 2010 and licensed in all 50 states, reports it has funded $600 billion in volume directly to consumers. Davidson Homes, founded in 2009 and based in Huntsville, Alabama, builds communities across Alabama, Arizona, Georgia, North Carolina, Tennessee and Texas and continues to expand into high-growth markets, according to the company.
