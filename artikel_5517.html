Single-Family Starts Rise 4% as Permits Drop in October

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Single-family housing starts rebounded in October, but a drop in permitting activity suggests builders remain cautious about new construction heading into the end of the year.

Single-family starts increased 4% from September to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 864,000, according to the latest data from the U.S. Census Bureau and Department of Housing and Urban Development. September’s single-family starts rate was revised to 831,000.

Overall, privately owned housing starts increased 9.9% from September to an annual rate of 1.309 million. The October pace was 2.8% above the October 2025 rate of 1.273 million.

Permitting activity moved in the opposite direction during October. Single-family permits fell 8.7% from the prior month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 772,000, down from a revised 846,000 in September. Overall building permits declined 7.4% month over month and 8.7% year over year to an annual rate of 1.295 million. Permits for buildings with five or more units were at a rate of 475,000.

Single-family completions also gained ground in October, rising 6.3% from September to an annual rate of 900,000, compared with a revised 847,000 the previous month.
