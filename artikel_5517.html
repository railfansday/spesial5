San Antonio Organizations Partnering with BridgeCare to Launch Integrated Early Childhood Education Search and Eligibility Platform

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

New digital portal connects local families with early care options, subsidy eligibility matching, and real-time open seat tracking in English and Spanish.

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- San Antonio families have a new way to find and access child care. BridgeCare, a leading provider of early care and education (ECE) technology, is partnering with Pre-K 4 SA and Early Matters San Antonio to deliver EarlyConnectSA, a one-stop shop for child care search and financial assistance eligibility to families. The new tool will make it easy for families to search for child care using the criteria that matters to them, including quality ratings, programs offered, and locations that qualify for military assistance programs.

Finding quality early childhood education can often be a complex, time-consuming process for parents and caregivers. Building on Pre-K 4 SA's longstanding mission to transform San Antonio's educational trajectory through high-quality early learning, this new platform helps connect families to the early childhood options in our community that are the best fit for their children.

By directly integrating public licensing standards alongside financial subsidy guidelines, the platform gives families a complete picture of program quality and safety while automatically identifying every financial support option available to them.

"Connecting families to high-quality early childhood education is critical to supporting child development and helping families thrive," said Jamee Herbert, CEO and Co-Founder of BridgeCare. "By bringing search, subsidy eligibility, and real-time provider seat availability together into one platform, San Antonio is making early care and education far more accessible and equitable for the entire metropolitan area."

"The EarlyConnectSA tool is a win for San Antonio and surrounding communities," Pre-K 4 SA CEO Sarah Baray said. "By connecting participating child care providers with families seeking care, the tool helps providers grow their business, enables parents to return to work with affordable child care, and advances economic mobility across San Antonio. We could not have done this without the expertise and technology BridgeCare brought to the table."

About BridgeCare BridgeCare provides modern technology solutions that help government agencies and community organizations streamline program operations, improve service delivery, and strengthen oversight across the entire early care and education ecosystem.

About Pre-K 4 SA: Pre-K 4 SA is a comprehensive early childhood initiative aimed at changing the education and workforce trajectory of San Antonio in one generation. Pre-K 4 SA offers full-day prekindergarten for 3- and 4-year-olds living in the City of San Antonio. Pre-K 4 SA is free for qualifying children and accepts tuition-paying families. Pre-K 4 SA has four Education Centers located throughout the city, and the Gardendale Early Learning Program, a partnership with Edgewood Independent School District. Pre-K 4 SA supports over 90 child care providers through the San Antonio Shared Services Alliance. Each year since 2018, the City of San Antonio has received a gold medal from CityHealth for its high-quality, accessible pre-k, which sets the "gold standard" in this policy area. Visit www.prek4sa.com for more information.
