Toyota is recalling more than 314,000 vehicles over aging Takata airbag risk

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The recall covers Toyota and Lexus models from the 2002 through 2017 model years, and dealers will make repairs free of charge

Toyota $TM is recalling more than 314,000 Toyota and Lexus models sold in the U.S., the company said on Nov. 12, citing Takata-made inflators in the front passenger airbag that can rupture and send metal fragments into the cabin.

The inflator propellant can degrade over time in certain temperature and humidity conditions, Toyota said. If the airbag deploys, a ruptured inflator can drive metal fragments through the airbag at high speed, which may injure or kill vehicle occupants.

The recall applies to 314,029 vehicles built for model years 2002 to 2017, according to Fox Business. Toyota reported no ruptures involving the affected inflators and is carrying out the recall as a precaution.

The affected vehicles include 2002-2010 Lexus SC430 convertibles, 2011 Lexus IS250 sedans and convertibles, and 2017 Lexus GX460 SUVs, according to Consumer Reports. The list also has 2003-2008 Toyota Corolla sedans, 2005-2006 Toyota Tundra trucks, 2005-2007 Toyota Sequoia SUVs and 2016-2017 Toyota 4Runner SUVs.

Depending on the model, Toyota said, dealers for both brands will swap out either the front passenger inflator or the entire airbag assembly at no cost to owners.

Owners can enter a vehicle identification number or license plate information at Toyota.com/recall or nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether a vehicle is involved, Toyota said. Toyota customer support is available at 1-800-331-4331.

Notification letters to owners are scheduled to go out from Toyota between Dec. 28 and Jan. 11, according to Fox Business. Owners can reach out to a dealer to arrange a fix without waiting for the letters to show up.

The recall adds to a string of Takata airbag recalls dating back to 2014, according to Consumer Reports.
