Farmer Mac Declares Quarterly Dividends on Common and Preferred Stock

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

WASHINGTON, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The Board of Directors of the Federal Agricultural Mortgage Corporation (Farmer Mac) has declared a fourth quarter dividend of $1.60 per share for each of Farmer Mac's three classes of common stock â Class A Voting Common Stock (NYSE: AGM.A), Class B Voting Common Stock (not listed on any exchange), and Class C Non-Voting Common Stock (NYSE: AGM). The quarterly dividend will be payable on December 31, 2026 to holders of record of common stock as of December 15, 2026.

Farmer Mac's Board of Directors has also declared a dividend on each of Farmer Mac's six classes of preferred stock. The quarterly dividend of $0.35625 per share of 5.700% Non-Cumulative Preferred Stock, Series D (NYSE: AGM.PR.D), $0.359375 per share of 5.750% Non-Cumulative Preferred Stock, Series E (NYSE: AGM.PR.E), $0.328125 per share of 5.250% Non-Cumulative Preferred Stock, Series F (NYSE: AGM.PR.F), $0.3046875 per share of 4.875% Non-Cumulative Preferred Stock, Series G (NYSE: AGM.PR.G), $0.40625 per share of 6.500% Non-Cumulative Preferred Stock, Series H (NYSE: AGM.PR.H), and $0.4296875 per share of 6.875% Non-Cumulative Preferred Stock, Series I (NYSE: AGM.PR.I) is for the period from but not including October 17, 2026 to and including January 17, 2027. These preferred stock dividends will be payable to the holders of record of those classes of preferred stock as of January 4, 2027, on the business day following January 17, 2027, with the same force and effect as though paid on January 17, 2027.

About Farmer MacFarmer Mac is driven by its mission to increase the accessibility of financing to provide vital liquidity for American agriculture and rural infrastructure. The secondary market served by Farmer Mac provides liquidity to our nation's agricultural and infrastructure businesses, supporting a vibrant and strong rural America. We are uniquely positioned to facilitate competitive access to financing that fuels growth, innovation, and prosperity in America's rural and agricultural communities. Additional information about Farmer Mac is available on our website at www.farmermac.com.
