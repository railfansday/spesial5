Stanley Tucci’s Airplane Snack Is the Messiest Meal Ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Stanley Tucci isn't settling for pretzels and peanuts on his next flight!

Between busy airport security lines and delays, eating while traveling can be unpredictable. And sure, maybe you ate breakfast before leaving the house. But then suddenly it’s six hours later and you’re struggling to navigate your way to baggage claim with a headache and a growling stomach. Trust me, I’ve been there too many times!

Which is why I have one simple rule when traveling: Eat when you can. Packing plenty of plane snacks is important, but getting a full meal is really ideal. So when I saw the kind of food that Stanley Tucci brings on flights, I felt incredibly validated. However, I don’t know if I could manage something quite this messy in the air.

In a very charming TikTok video, Stanley Tucci’s wife Felicity Blunt records him as he unwraps his plane snack. I watched in shock and awe as he unwrapped aluminum foil to reveal a sandwich. But not just any sandwich—I mean, this is Stanley Tucci we’re talking about. He’s known for whipping up Italian meals for pop stars and cooking cozy comfort meals on a whim. It’s no real surprise then that he brought eggplant Parmesan on a plane!

His wife joked that they should “film a before and after” of his shirt. I mean, this sandwich looks incredible. It’s got tons of creamy marinara sauce and is piled high with thinly sliced eggplant and cheese. The bread is toasted and really looks like it’s doing the best job it can at containing the feast within.

Would I have the confidence to eat an eggplant Parmesan sandwich on a plane? Probably not. I am a notoriously messy eater to begin with, and if bibs were more socially acceptable for adults, I would wear one with every meal. So I can’t imagine diving into this on a plane!

But Stanley loves it. In fact, he’s the one who made the sandwich, or “Stan-wich” as Felicity calls them, for the plane ride. So maybe I need to amend my earlier assertion: I would eat eggplant Parmesan on a plane if Stanley Tucci made it for me.

What’s your go-to airplane snack? Is it as messy as this one? Sound off in the comments below!
