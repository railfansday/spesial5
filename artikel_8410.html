Open OnPath Rewards High Yield Checking if you keep about $10,000 in checking and make at least 15 debit card purchases a month, the hardest of its three monthly rules. Meeting all three earns its 6.00% annual percentage yield on the full $10,000. Anything above that earns only 0.50%, so keep the excess in a high-yield savings account.

OnPath Rewards High Yield Checking comes from OnPath Credit Union, a Louisiana credit union that anyone can join by donating $5 to the OnPath Foundation. The account rewards everyday debit card spending rather than direct deposits, so it fits members who pay for most purchases by card.

Members who keep $10,000 in checking and qualify every cycle earn about $584 a year, about $90 more than Genisys pays on the same balance with its higher rate and lower cap. Anyone holding well over $10,000 should move the excess to a high-yield savings account, since every dollar past the cap earns 0.50% here.
