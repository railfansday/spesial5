Nvidia spent $58.1bn on stakes in other companies in nine months

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Nvidia’s stake purchases nearly match its $61.2bn of buybacks, and its credit guarantees have reached $118bn. One analyst says Nvidia is now ‘double exposed’ if AI spending slows.

Nvidia’s Voyager building at its headquarters in Santa Clara, California

Nvidia spent $58.1bn buying shares in other companies in the nine months to 25 October. That is almost 12 times the $4.95bn of a year earlier. Fortune found the figure in the quarterly results Nvidia posted on Tuesday. It nearly matches the $61.2bn Nvidia spent buying back its own stock, up from $36.3bn.

The investments focus on “frontier labs, infrastructure financiers, and other private companies”, according to Nvidia’s CFO commentary, Fortune reported. Nvidia has invested in OpenAI and Anthropic.

Fortune noted that this raises the question of circular financing: whether Nvidia is funding the demand that drives its own sales. The IMF warned last month that such deals could deepen a market correction. If AI spending slows, Nvidia could lose twice: through weaker chip sales and through falling values for its stakes. James Kisner, managing director of technology at Water Tower Research, made that point to Fortune.

“It’s magnified and you feel more pain because you’re kind of double exposed here,” Kisner said.

The stakes produced $1.8bn of net gains in the quarter, Fortune reported. That figure includes a $1bn loss on its private holdings.

Nvidia’s backing goes beyond shares. Neoclouds are firms that rent out computing power on Nvidia chips. On the earnings call, interim chief executive Colette Kress said Nvidia’s future commitments to them totalled $32bn in the quarter. Its credit guarantees reached $118bn. That includes $105bn for the PORTS-Pike data centre in Ohio, which OpenAI will lease for 20 years.

Some of those guarantees only start once a customer has financing in place, Kress said.

“If all of that begins and unfortunately they are delayed or they have a challenge in terms of building that, our guarantee doesn’t exist anymore,” she said.

Kress called shareholder returns a “super top priority”. Nvidia has $227.3bn left under its buyback programme and plans to use it by the end of fiscal 2028, she said. She also told analysts on Tuesday that Nvidia was rigorously evaluating future investments and guarantees.
