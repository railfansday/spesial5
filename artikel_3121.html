I test a lot of running shoes and while the Pegasus is rarely the most exciting launch each year, it’s always a dependable and durable pick.

It’s also naturally quite a stable shoe, and has a thick outsole that I’ve found holds up well when running on light trails, and grips well on wet sidewalks too.
