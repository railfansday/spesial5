Anthony Albanese is back in the country after appearances at international summits in China and the Philippines.

Labor is under significant pressure as the cost of living rises – so the PM is hopeful this will bring some relief to both household purses and his government.

double quotation markToday we will introduce legislation to extend the freeze of the cost of PBS medicines at just $25 until the end of the decade.

New nest of red imported fire ants found and destroyed in Sydney

The NSW government discovered a new nest of red imported fire ants in Sydney over the weekend, with findings suggesting the nest could be about a year old.

Officials destroyed the colony, found in Auburn in the city’s west, after it was deemed to contained red imported fire ants, a major invasive species that poses threats to both human health and the environment.

A separate nest was discovered two months ago at the Enfield logistics centre, more than 6km from the site found this weekend. The new nest was found outside a biosecurity control zone and officials are investigating if it is a different infestation originating from another source. Detection dogs are set to be on site soon and a new biosecurity control order is being developed.

Tara Moriarty, the NSW minister for agriculture, said:

double quotation markHuman movement of materials is the primary way fire ants spread, which is why we’re asking everyone to stay alert and report anything suspicious.

Everyone has a part to play in biosecurity, and I thank the member of the public for reporting this site in Auburn.
