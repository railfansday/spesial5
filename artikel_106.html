Ukraine's allies gather in Berlin for 'Coalition of the Willing' summit

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ukraine's European backers are convening in Berlin, with US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner also set to attend the meeting. Follow the latest.

Follow the latest on Thursday, 19th November, below:

The Crimean Bridge was attacked for a third time this week, a Russian official said on Thursday.

Traffic had been temporarily suspended, according to Crimea's Russian-installed governor.

"The damage is currently being assessed," Sergei Aksyonov, the governor, said on Telegram, adding that emergency services were at the scene.

The bridge, which supports a four-lane roadway and a railroad, is the only bridge linking mainland Russia with occupied Crimea.

The UK pledged to provide Ukraine with long-range cruise missiles capable of reaching areas in Russia beyond the front lines.

Prime Minister Andy Burnham is expected to make that announcement at the "Coalition of the Willing" summit in Berlin.

The drones are a part of the support package for Kyiv worth £260 million pounds ($338 million, €311 million) ahead of the winter.

"Plunging millions of Ukrainians into the freezing cold and starving them of heat and warmth won’t see Putin win this war, it will only cement our resolve to support Ukraine," Burnham said, adding that he is "determined" to help Ukraine defend itself.

According to Burnham, Ukraine's backers are "as united as ever" to secure an end to the war with Russia.

German Foreign MinisterJohann Wadephul said the meeting of Ukraine's backers in Berlin should send a signal to Russia.

"It's really about time to be serious with the negotiations," Wadephul told DW.

According to Germany's top diplomat, Ukraine's allies are readying themselves for all possibilities,including the failure of negotiations, which would require supporting Kyiv's energy and food needs.

"We will also be on Ukraine's side militarily," he said.

Germany's capital will host a meeting between a group of European leaders that support Ukrainein the war against Russia.

The meeting, dubbed the "Coalition of the Willing," includes German Chancellor Friedrich Merz, French President Emmanuel Macron, British Prime Minister Andy Burnham, Polish Prime Minister Donald ‌Tusk and Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni.

NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte and US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner will also be in attendance.

According to German officials, the meeting is intended to demonstrate the countries' commitment to legally binding security guarantees should a ceasefire agreement be reached between Russia and Ukraine.

The timing of the meeting is intended to allow the group to react quickly to announcements that come out of the meeting between US President Donald Trump ,Russian President Vladimir Putin and China's President Xi Jinping in China.

Trump might also meet Putin for a bilateral summit.
