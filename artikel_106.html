Germany: IG Metall union says deal reached with employers

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

IG Metall, representing millions of workers in Germany's metal and electrical industry, is the country's largest trade union. The deal came after more than 24 hours of talks with employers.

Germany's powerful IG Metall trade union said Thursday it has reached a pay deal with employers after more than 24 hours of the fourth round of talks.

IG Metall represents 3.7 million workers in the German metal and electrical industry in total. It is Germany's largest trade union.

"The result is a compromise under difficult conditions," Baden-Württemberg IG Metall boss Barbara Resch said.

The talks come amid job cuts across multiple sectors in the German economy, such as the automotive sector.

IG Metall represents workers in major German companies such as Volkswagen, Mercedes-Benz, Siemens, ThyssenKrupp, and Salzgitter AG, among others. All of those aforementioned companies have announced job-cutting plans within the last two years.
