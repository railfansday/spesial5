'Broadchurch' Meets 'Virgin River' in Cozy Crime Thriller Officially Returning for Season 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

One of TV's most bingeworthy small-town dramas has finally announced its return date, months after being renewed for Season 3. A cross between ITV's Broadchurch and Netflix's Virgin River, the series follows a big-city detective who moves to a quiet coastal town for a fresh start, only to uncover a disturbing string of local murders he and his team must solve. Rather than a gritty, bleak noir, the series unfolds against the scenic, close-knit backdrop of a small town, with a swoony romance at its center.

Adapted from L.R. Wright's nine-book series, Murder in a Small Town is a Canadian crime drama that's well worth adding to your watchlist. The series premiered on September 24, 2024, on Global in Canada and Fox in the U.S., with its eight-episode first season wrapping up on November 26. Renewed the following year, Season 2 premiered on September 23, 2025, and proved even more successful: ratings rose 11%, and the show climbed the streaming charts, helping secure a Season 3 renewal.

TV Insider can now confirm that Murder in a Small Town Season 3 will premiere on February 1, 2027, at 8/7c on Fox, followed by the Season 2 premiere of Memory of a Killer. The show joins Fox's mid-season schedule, launching about eight months after series star Kristin Kreuk shared on social media that production on the third season wrapped in June. Kreuk and Rossif Sutherland will both return as co-leads, playing local librarian Cassandra Lee and police chief Karl Alberg.

In addition to Kreuk and Sutherland, supporting stars Dakota Guppy, Aaron Douglas, and Bethany Brown are expected to return for Season 3 as Holly, Sid Sokolowski, and Layla, respectively. However, it's uncertain whether Savonna Spracklin will return as police officer Isabella since her character has expressed interest in attending law school. Similarly, it hasn't been confirmed whether Marcia Gay Harden, who joined Murder in a Small Town Season 2 as Mayor Christine Holman, will return.

Meanwhile, Peter Gallagher (The O.C., Grace and Frankie) joins the Murder in a Small Town Season 3 cast as a series regular, playing Rod Finlayson. Described as charismatic, fiercely independent, capable, yet unreliable, Rod arrives at the Gibsons' marina aboard his beloved boat, setting off a series of upheavals that Karl Alberg and Cassandra will need to navigate. The cozy crime drama was created by Ian Weir, who also serves as executive producer.

Murder in a Small Town Season 3 premieres on Fox in February. Stream the first two seasons on Netflix.
