These MAMMOTION Robotic Lawn Mowers Are up to $800 Off for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Mowing the lawn is one of those homeowner chores that never gets less annoying—more so if you have a sizeable lawn. Keeping the grass in check can take up most of your weekend, but like a robot vacuum taking the brunt of your housework duties, a robotic lawn mower can do the yardwork for you.

Right now, MAMMOTION is offering substantial Black Friday savings on its LUBA lineup of lawn mowers, bringing the LUBA mini 2 AWD 1500H down to $1,599, from $1,999. Meanwhile, the more powerful LUBA 3 AWD 3000 is down to $1,999 from its usual $2,799. Both deals include a free mower wash stand, valued at $79. That’s up to $800 of combined savings, and the offers run through Cyber Monday on Nov. 30.

If you’re picking between the two lawn mowers, your choice largely depends on the size of the area you’re mowing. The LUBA mini 2 AWD 1500H is designed for small and medium-sized yards, with a 360-degree LiDAR navigation system to map out your lawn and identify obstacles. The all-wheel-drive system can tackle slopes of up to 80%, and the side-mounted cutting disc helps it get closer to walls, fences, and flower beds.

For larger properties, the LUBA 3 AWD 3000 is a solid fit. This lawn mower includes the same 360-degree LiDAR navigation system, but pairs it with 4G-based Network RTK functionality and AI vision, so the mower stay on course without boundary wires or a separate antenna. It can also handle slopes as steep as 80%, and its 15.7-inch cutting width and two cutting discs can handle up to 5,400 square feet per hour. The AI vision system allows it to recognise and avoid 300 different types of obstacles, including toys and pets.

Both mowers are designed to remove much of the manual work associated with yard maintenance. Once configured, these mowers can manage multiple mowing zones, customize schedules, and varied cutting heights.

Priced at $1,599, the LUBA mini 2 is a fairly substantial investment, but the $400 Black Friday discount and free wash stand make it a bit easier to justify. Similarly, the LUBA 3 gets a more significant $800 savings, plus the wash stand. If you’ve been considering a robotic lawn mower to bring some automation to the outdoors, these Black Friday offers are worth a look.
