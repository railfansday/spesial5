Stefan Borson drops verdict on Daniel Farke contract U-turn at Leeds - 'Interesting things are happening'

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Leeds United's hierarchy are unlikely to consider a U-turn on Daniel Farke's future at Elland Road despite recent form.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that it is not even a consideration in West Yorkshire.

Farke was expected to put pen to paper on a new contract during the previous international break, but as it stands, his deal will expire in the summer.

The Whites had been as high as third in the Premier League table at one point this season, but after five games without a win, they've slipped to 12th.

Leeds next face Tottenham Hotspur on Saturday, 7 November, and the Elland Road faithful will be hopeful of all three points at home.

The team's current form has raised some concerns over the manager's future, but he is still expected to sign across the dotted line very soon.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson doesn't see any chance of Leeds' owners doing a U-turn on Farke's contract negotiations.

"No, I don't think so. I think they're playing OK from what I can gather. I wouldn't think there's going to be huge change there," he said.

"Clearly, when you're on a bad run like that, every game then becomes indicative of the trend. But they're at home over the weekend.

"Spurs obviously could win and follow on the bounce, but it's not impossible that Leeds can win the game. I think he's well set.

"People are generally happy. There are some interesting things going on at Leeds. There's no way they're going to turn around."

Not everyone shares the same confidence in Farke, it would appear, as Mick Brown predicts a "backlash" against the manager this season.

Farke has often been criticised for his inability to adapt to different situations. If he can't turn things around now, those same whispers will return.

There are concerns that Dominic Calvert-Lewin could be injured against Spurs, and that would be a huge problem for the Whites.

His handling of Jean-Matteo Bahoya's situation has also raised some alarm bells, but that is the least of Farke's concerns right now.
