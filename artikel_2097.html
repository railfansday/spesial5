Sources: Aston Villa in advanced talks to agree deal after Unai Emery green light

Unai Emery is in advanced talks to agree a new Aston Villa contract after he indicated he wants to extend his stay and continue his stunning transformation of the club, sources have told Football Insider.

The Spaniard has done an outstanding job at Villa Park, particularly in winning last season's Europa League, and the club are determined to reward him for his success.

As a result, the boss has been linked with moves away from Villa in recent months, with the likes of Real Madird and most recently Man United showing an interest.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Aston Villa have taken steps to deter interest from elsewhere and keep hold of Emery.

He is believed to be happy at Villa Park, where he has turned things around despite a rocky start to the season and a turbulent transfer window which led to some doubts.

Aston Villa are now closing in on an agreement which will see Emery commit his long-term future to the club beyond the terms of his current deal which runs until 2029.

Former Aston Villa scout Mick Brown told Football Insider on 3 November that Emery has been offered a new contract in an attempt to secure his long-term future.

The boss' current contract is set to run until 2029, and there are no major concerns about a potential departure, but they are keen to secure his position amid ongoing speculation.

Talks are now advanced to extend his stay at Villa Park, with all parties edging towards an agreement, and with Emery happy to commit to Aston Villa's project.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Of course, I think that's a no brainer for Villa to try and tie down Unai Emery to a new contract.

"Talks are advanced between all parties to try and secure his long term future. He's still under contract until 2029, so the club are just looking to reward him for the work he's done.

"They also want to end any speculation over a potential move with that talk of a potential move to Man United if they do decide to dispense with the services of Michael Carrick.

"I think they'll be pretty confident I get this deal agreed and end any speculation that the Spaniard could be lured away from Villa Park."

Emery was always considered an outsider for the Man United job given his and the club's stance on leaving, but work is being done to show that commitment publicly.

Questions are still being asked about Michael Carrick's future, however, with inconsistent results causing uncertainty about how long he will remain in the position.

Former Everton CEO Keith Wyness told Football Insider on 29 October that Carrick could even see out the season at Man United as he will be given a chance to turn things around.

This comes after it was expected that the boss would lose his job after a dismal start to their Premier League campaign, but the board have stuck by him so far.

With the board and large sections of the Old Trafford support on his side, there is now a belief that he could make a success of his time despite what has been a slow start.
