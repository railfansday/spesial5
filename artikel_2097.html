Five wins against Inter in 16 days: Milan’s youth teams continue derby dominance

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

AC Milan’s dominance of the derby continued as two of the youth teams both recorded victories over Inter.

The most dramatic encounter was the one involving Baldo’s U16 team, snatched victory in the third minute of stoppage time to spark wild celebrations at the home of their city rivals.
