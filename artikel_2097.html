Mikey Moore trapped in a German nightmare as Rangers loan hero caught in a 'mess'

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Mikey Moore’s loan spell at FC Koln has been branded a “mess” by German media with the former Rangers loan star failing to register a goal or assist in his first eight appearances.

Moore - who started Tottenham's Premier League opener before his loan exit - netted seven times and claimed eight assists in 47 outings at Ibrox last season before returning to Spurs and heading to the Bundesliga.

The winger has teamed up in Germany with ex-Celtic defender Jahmai Simpson-Pusey and highly-rated English prospect Reigan Heskey, son of former Liverpool and England striker Emile.

But Moore has been unable to hit the ground running at Koln and was once again forced to settle for a place on the bench for Saturday’s 1-1 draw away to Union Berlin.

The England Under-20 international was introduced as a substitute, but his wait for a first goal contribution in Germany continued. And afterwards, leading German outlet BILD delivered a scathing assessment of his performances so far.

Fellow German outlet Express has gone one step further, claiming Moore is becoming “impatient” over his lack of minutes and suggesting the youngster is simply trying too hard to make things happen when his opportunities arrive.

Koln boss Rene Wagner admits seeing Moore finally break his duck with a goal or assist remains on his “personal bingo card”, although he insists the youngster has already shown enough to suggest he can make a success of his spell in Germany.

Speaking after the draw with Union, Wagner said: “An assist or a goal from Mikey Moore would still be on my personal bingo card.”

Asked about the decision to leave Moore on the bench, he explained: “Mikey started against Schalke and in the cup. He’s already shown from the bench that he can bring a lot of energy. That was the idea. We wanted to become dangerous again late in the game with his substitution.

“We anticipated Union would play with a back five and wanted to have two holding midfielders on the pitch to be able to switch formations effectively. We had to do that after just five minutes.”
