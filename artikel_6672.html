Move Over, Bearista: Panera Has New Limited-Edition Snowman Cups For The Holidays

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Just when you thought you had enough cute drinkware to last a lifetime.

Starbucks' Bearista cups have already proved that people will go to some pretty extraordinary lengths for an adorable drinking vessel. We're talking painfully early lines, instant sellouts, and resale prices that might make you reconsider how much you really need another piece of glassware.

Now Panera Bread wants in on the fun. Only instead of a cuddly bear, the bakery-cafe is going with another winter favorite, the snowman. And wouldn't you know, there's even a chance to snag one for free.

On December 3, Panera will be giving away its new glass Snowman Cups with qualifying purchases at participating locations. There's one rather important detail, though: Each cafe will have only 12 available for the promotion. Not exactly a generous supply, and something tells me these won't be sitting around collecting dust for very long.

There are two designs to collect, although which one you'll end up with is anyone's guess, as they're packaged in mystery boxes and the reveal comes after you've already gotten your hands on one.

If you're not interested in competing for a freebie, there's another way to get your hands on one. Panera will also sell a limited quantity through its online shop for $25 each, plus tax and shipping.

And if you're making a Panera run anyway, you might as well check out what's new on the holiday menu. The chain is introducing Peppermint White Mocha and Pistachio White Mocha, each available as a latte, iced coffee, or Frozen Java. The iced coffees get sweet cream cold foam, while the lattes and frozen drinks come with whipped cream. You'll also find peppermint bark or pistachio pieces on top, depending on which flavor you choose.

The new drinks and treats are available at Panera locations nationwide starting this week. As for the Snowman Cups, I can't say whether they'll cause the same frenzy as Starbucks' beanie-wearing bear—but I have a feeling those freebies will melt away pretty damn fast.
