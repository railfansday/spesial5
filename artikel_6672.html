The way to any young woman's heart is a perfectly curated, can’t-believe-I-ever-lived-without-it gift. No pressure! It's not like we're terrifying to shop for or anything!! I know the joy/chaos of being a 20-something-year-old woman, and while it can look incredibly fun and cool (hi, late nights dancing, new jobs, and first apartments), most of us are just starting to figure this adult thing out. And gifts make everything better, right? When a bestie, significant other, parent, or loved one takes the time to find a present they know we’ll like and actually use, it’s a big deal.

The good news: Thoughtful doesn’t have to mean complicated, expensive, or planned months in advance. The best gifts for young women are the ones that feel current, useful, and just indulgent enough. So whether you’re shopping for a beauty-loving roommate, a little sister, or a best friend this holiday season, this guide has got your back.

Take a peek at the best gifts for young women below that will actually impress—no matter what the too-cool millennial or Gen Z girl in your life wants you to think.
