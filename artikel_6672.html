Taylor Swift Watched Travis Kelce’s Chiefs-Jets Game With Her Family and Karlie Kloss

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Taylor Swift returned to Arrowhead Stadium this afternoon, making her first appearance in two weeks at a Kansas City Chiefs game to cheer on her husband, Travis Kelce. The singer wore a red top while watching the team face off against the New York Jets. She was seen with her parents, Andrea and Scott Swift, and was filmed talking to Karlie Kloss. Kloss attended Swift’s wedding earlier this year, marking the two friends’ first public moment in years.

Swift wasn’t spotted at either of the Chiefs’ last two away games. Last Sunday, the star was in New York City, where she made headlines for a surprise appearance at a Toy Story 5 screening. She then remained out of the public eye for the rest of the week.

For the past two seasons, Swift has only been seen at home games, partly due to security concerns. Before today, her most recent appearance was at Kelce’s October 18 home game. She traveled to Kansas City after performing at the Academy Museum Gala in Los Angeles the night before and had some star-studded company in the stands, including WNBA star Caitlin Clark and musician Este Haim.

Next Sunday, the Chiefs will take on the Atlanta Falcons at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Georgia.

Swift and Kelce haven’t publicly revealed their plans for their first Thanksgiving as a married couple, but the tight end has hinted that he’s looking forward to spending the holiday season with his wife. (Kelce won’t have a game on either Thanksgiving or Christmas this year.)

“I’m excited for my first holiday as a married man,” he said in his winter Tommy Hilfiger campaign, via Women’s Wear Daily. “I love all the old traditions and can’t wait to start some new ones. I start celebrating as soon as that tree goes up.”
