Starbucks Red Cup Day is here: See the full list of qualifying drinks and how to get your cup for free

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

According to Starbucks, it’s time to get in the holiday giving spirit.

Today (Tuesday, November 17, 2026) is Starbucks Red Cup Day. The annual tradition sees the coffee chain give out free reusable red holiday cups with the purchase of any fall or holiday beverage. 

This year, the Starbucks Creative Studio decorated the limited-edition 16-ounce cup with a coffee tree branch tied in a red bow. A green and white ribbon wraps around the outside of the cup. 

How to get the Starbucks Red Cup for free? 

The 2026 holiday red cup is available at participating Starbucks locations while supplies last. You can also get one while using a Starbucks drive-thru or when ordering delivery on DoorDash, Grubhub or Uber Eats. 

You can use the red cup (or any of your own drinkware) in the future for 10 cents off your drink. 

What Starbucks beverages are included in the Red Cup Day deal?

The free red cup is only available when purchasing any size of a fall or holiday drink—other than the Starbucks Christmas Blend and Starbucks Reserve Christmas brewed coffee. 

Here are all the qualifying Starbucks Red Cup Day beverages:

  • Apple Pomegranate Refresher
  • Caramel Brulée Chai
  • Caramel Brulée Latte
  • Gingerbread Chai
  • Gingerbread Latte
  • Hot Chocolate
  • Iced Pumpkin Cream Chai
  • Peppermint Hot Chocolate
  • Peppermint Mocha
  • Pecan Crunch Oatmilk Latte
  • Pecan Oatmilk Cortado
  • Pumpkin Cream Cold Brew
  • Pumpkin Spice Latte
  • Snowy Apple Drink
  • Sugar Cookie Latte
  • Sugar Cookie Matcha
  • Tiramisu Velvet Cold Brew
  • Tiramisu Velvet Latte
  • White Hot Chocolate
  • Holiday and Fall Cold Foams 
  • Protein Cold Foams 

Following its weekend announcement of Red Cup Day, Starbucks shares (Nasdaq: SBUX) closed 2.17% up on Monday. The coffee chain’s stock price is up 29.11% year-to-date, outperforming the S&P 500.
