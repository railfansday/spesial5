Coinbase Reveals November Delisting Schedule, Issues Warning on Solana Token Listing

Coinbase crypto exchange has shared upcoming changes to its cryptocurrency listings for the month of November, including its delisting schedule and more details on the listing of a Solana-based token.

In a recent post, Coinbase said trading for Stargate Finance (STG) and Vulcan Forged (PYR) has been delayed until November 20, 2026 at 2 PM ET. Trading of these tokens will be suspended on Coinbase (Simple and Advanced Trade), Coinbase Exchange and Coinbase Prime on this date.

Coinbase regularly reviews listed assets to make sure they meet required standards. In keeping with this and per recent reviews, Coinbase will halt trading for Ampleforth Governance Token (FORTH) on November 23, 2026 at 2 PM ET.

Also, according to recent reviews, Coinbase will delist Neon EVM (NEON) and Syndicate (SYND) on November 16, 2026 at 2 PM ET. Trading for these tokens will be suspended on Coinbase (Simple and Advanced Trade), Coinbase Exchange, and Coinbase Prime on this date, with users urged to act accordingly to prevent potential losses.

In recent listings, Solana-based token Collector Crypt (CARD) went live on the Coinbase platform and in its app, allowing Coinbase users to buy, sell, convert, send, receive, or store these assets.

In order to avoid a repeated ticker with an existing Coinbase listing, while the asset is natively known as CARDS, it will be traded under the ticker symbol CARD on the Coinbase platform and in the Coinbase iOS and Android apps.

This clarification remains essential to avoid confusion that may arise from token mix-ups, especially those with similar tickers that have been previously listed by Coinbase.

Coinbase reports that the conversion of Aerodrome Finance (AERO) and Velodrome Finance (VELO) to the new Aero (AERO) token is complete, with sends and receives for AERO and VELO re-enabled.

Coinbase no longer supports deposits or withdrawals of the legacy AERO token. Users are urged not to send the legacy AERO token to their Coinbase deposit address, as these deposits may not be recoverable.

Perpetual futures trading for BMNR, WDC, RKLB and AAOI will go live on Monday, November 9, 2026 at 10:00 AM UTC. Perpetual futures are available to retail traders and institutions on Coinbase, Coinbase Advanced, and Deribit in select jurisdictions.
