XRP Recovery: Price Prints First Uptrend Since September

XRP has finally printed a local reversal: the price is forming one higher high after another. There's a good chance this quick recovery turns into something more significant sooner than we anticipate. XRP is trading at $0.9927, up around 11% from the $0.89 low of the drop. The price is back at the $0.99 area, which was the August low.

Hopefully, bulls pick up the rally, though for now the momentum is slow. The descending volume on the price upside suggests fading. The divergence of moving averages is an anti-volatility signal right now rather than anything else. All of them still sit above the price, with the nearest one around $1.13.

Multiple indicators suggest a retrace, but the overall market picture is grim, to say the least. XRP hadn't been below $1 since mid-August, when it dropped under that level for the first time since November 2024. It mostly followed the general uptrend but tumbled quickly due to the general reversal of the cryptocurrency market.

The worrying part is, ironically, the recovery of RSI, which is slowly moving above the oversold zone and opens up the way for an additional leg down. RSI is around 33 now, up from its low near 27.

The issue is the historic pattern XRP is forming: a constant downtrend with occasional recoveries that quickly turn into new breakdowns. In early November, XRP bounced from around $1.20 to the $1.30 area. The breakdown that followed took the price down by around 30% in under two weeks.

The downtrend is way stronger than the bullish reversal attempts XRP had earlier this month. Unfortunately, it can create a sustainable downtrend that can last for months, and considering XRP's price of $0.99, this is not something investors want.

The market is weak, and it might get weaker. The confirmation of a market recovery would only come if we witness specific signs of a bull run: higher highs and sustained buying.
