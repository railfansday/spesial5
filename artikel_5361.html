Booking Holdings is combining its partner businesses into a new B2B travel division

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The Booking Partner Division will combine Booking.com Strategic Partnerships and Rocket Travel by Agoda starting in January 2027

Booking Holdings $BKNG announced a new global business-to-business organization on Wednesday that will merge the partner operations of Booking.com and Agoda. The unit, called Booking Partner Division, will be operational from January 2027, the company said.

The division builds on Booking.com Strategic Partnerships and Rocket Travel by Agoda. It will work with banks, financial institutions, airlines, online travel companies, travel technology platforms, affiliates, advertisers and publishers. Teams and leadership from both units will join the organization, which will operate across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East and Asia-Pacific.

Its solutions span White Label, API, Branded Solutions and Chain B2B Rates Distribution. The company said the portfolio covers more than 4.7 million properties and over 33 million accommodation listings, along with upwards of 500 airlines and 200-plus car rental providers. Existing partners can continue to rely on current relationships and capabilities as the organization comes together, it said.

Omri Morgenshtern, CEO of Booking Partner Division, will lead the unit. He is also the CEO of Agoda, according to CNBC.

"By bringing together the strengths we've built and developed within Booking Holdings, we can bring more of what we do best to partners and their customers today, while creating the focus and agility to innovate faster, harness emerging technologies like AI, and build the solutions they need to thrive," Morgenshtern said in a statement.

Glenn Fogel, CEO of Booking Holdings, said in a statement that connecting the company's strengths "in new ways" would help it "accelerate innovation, open up new opportunities for growth and ultimately deliver more for partners, their customers and travelers."

Morgenshtern told CNBC that focusing on both product and go-to-market efforts could let the company "grow faster and serve better partners." He would not put a figure on what the division might add to Booking Holdings' revenue and profit, and he pegged the size of the total addressable market at $2 trillion, according to CNBC.

The company said the division will develop AI capabilities to help partners build more personalized travel experiences. Booking expects those tools to build on what it already knows about travel, while keeping token costs low, according to CNBC. Morgenshtern told CNBC he is "super optimistic" about a vertical agent that Booking will offer partners.
