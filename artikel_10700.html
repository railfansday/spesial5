Twitch streamer’s PS5 catches fire after they pause GTA 6 for a bathroom break

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A streamer’s PS5 began smoking while they were away for a toilet break mid-way through their play through of GTA 6.

GTA 6 finally launched on November 19, following years of anticipation, with millions of players diving into Rockstar’s latest open-world blockbuster.

However, the launch hasn’t been entirely smooth, with PS5 and Xbox players reporting game-breaking crashes. Some have encountered recurring errors that prevent them from progressing, while others have been unable to get past the opening minutes of the game.

On November 21, Twitch streamer JoJoJosiah took to X to share news that their PS5 had begun smoking during a GTA 6 livestream, apparently leaving the console unusable.

“Was playing GTA VI on stream but had to go poop,” they explained. “Paused it and put on a sensory vid for my chat.”

However, while they were away, the streamer claimed their “PS5 started just smoking out its a**.” The post was accompanied by a video of the incident.

In the video, JoJoJosiah arrives back at their streaming setup to find the room filled with smoke, while they panic and figure out what to do, before the fire alarm goes off.

Was playing gta VI on stream but had to go poop. Paused it and put on a sensory vid for my chatPS5 started just smoking out its ass and now i need to get a replacement 😀 pic.twitter.com/qSdfcCi4l1

In a reply to the original tweet, the streamer said that they were uninjured as a result of the fire. “I’m fine and everything is good tho,” they wrote.

It’s still unclear what caused the console to malfunction, or whether GTA 6 itself played any role in the reported hardware failure, but the streamer revealed that they now need to purchase a replacement console as a result.
