Hot Honey Chicken &amp; Brussels Sprouts

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

If your weeknights are as busy as mine, you need something quick and easy to plate up. This hot honey chicken and Brussels sprouts recipe is perfect for hectic nights. With a scrumptious but simple glaze, I guarantee you’ll be making this one-skillet meal all cozy season long.

This dish is best the day it’s made, but if you have leftovers, you can refrigerate them in an airtight container for up to 3 days. The crispy glazed skin will never quite regain its former crispy glory, but it will still pack a punch of flavor.
