Read Online Reviews for Patterns, Not the Star Average – Start With the Three-Star Ones

A product’s star average is easy to fake, so before you buy online, look at what the reviews have in common. Read the recent three-star reviews from verified buyers for a problem several of them describe the same way, and distrust a burst of short, gushing five-star reviews posted within a few days.

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) warns that you often can’t tell a fake review from a real one by reading it, and that some fake positive reviews give fewer than five stars to seem believable. A pattern across dozens of reviews is harder to fake than any single one.

The FTC says to be wary of a burst of reviews in a short span and of a reviewer whose account seems to exist for one review of one product. Skip praise so vague it could describe anything, too. Real or fake, it tells you nothing about this product.

Then make sure the reviews describe the item you’re buying. In 2023, the company behind Nature’s Bounty supplements agreed to pay $600,000 to settle FTC charges that it merged new products into the Amazon listings of older ones with different formulas. The new products then showed the older ones’ ratings and reviews.

When a listing looks rigged, report it to the site, then to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov with “fake review” in the comments field. An FTC rule in effect since Oct. 21, 2024, bars businesses from writing or buying fake reviews and from using threats to get bad ones removed, and it lets courts fine those that knowingly break it. The FTC based its December 2025 warning letters to 10 companies partly on shoppers’ complaints.

To find out what goes wrong with a product, sort by newest and start with the three-star reviews marked as verified purchases. A buyer who gave three stars found something wrong and usually says what, and recent reviews cover the version on sale now, not one from years ago.

Say a blender averages 4.8 stars, but its newest three-star reviews keep naming the same failing part, the jar’s seal, which starts leaking within a few months. Those repeated complaints tell you more about the blender than its 4.8 average does.
