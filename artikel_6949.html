The Remarkable Election-Night Story We Should All Be Talking About

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Don’t let the sound of the blue tsunami drown out what happened in Wisconsin.

Drowned out by the sound of the blue tsunami making landfall everywhere, a remarkable story took place in Wisconsin. The state’s Democratic party, almost gerrymandered out of existence a few years ago and helpless, succeeded in electing not only a governor but also majorities in both houses of the state legislature.

The Democrats already enjoy a majority on the state supreme court, so I feel confident in saying that the state is now completely free of the dead hand of the administration of Scott Walker, the goggle-eyed homunculus hired by Koch Industries to manage their subsidiary once known as the state of Wisconsin. They also flipped two congressional seats: Paul Ryan’s old seat, now held by one of his old aides, and that of the inexcusable Derrick Van Orden, famous corrector of lounging congressional pages.

(What is truly delicious is that Van Orden was dimed out on that rampage by his fellow Wisconsin congressman Mark Pocan, who was reelected Tuesday night, though he ran unopposed.)
