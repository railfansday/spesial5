Whoop is cutting membership prices for Black Friday — check out every deal live now

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

TL;DR: Whoop's Black Friday sale officially kicks off Nov. 19, knocking up to $60 off new annual memberships (One, Peak, and Life) and taking 20% off accessories. Existing members also get discounted renewals and a free MG-compatible charger.

If you’ve been thinking about getting a Whoop band for tracking your training vitals — sleep, strain, recovery, and more — you'll already know that the main hurdle is the cost of a Whoop subscription. Whoop packages its hardware directly into annual memberships, which means discounts on the service are frustratingly rare.

That changes ahead of the holidays. Whoop is now rolling out its biggest promotional sale of the year, which will knock up to $60 off annual memberships and 20% off accessories and apparel. The main Black Friday event kicks off on Nov. 19, from 8 a.m. ET, giving both new and existing members a chance to lock in lower prices.

If you miss this initial wave, don't worry. There will be a secondary holiday sale that runs Dec. 12 through Jan. 7, offering 10% off new Peak and Life memberships along with 20% off Advanced Labs and GRAIL multi-cancer screening tests for existing users. Every tier in the lineup is HSA- and FSA-eligible, boasts 14-plus days of battery life, features haptic wake alarms, integrates with Natural Cycles, and continuously tracks sleep, strain, and recovery.

Here's a quick guide on how each tier breaks down, the hardware you get, and what you’ll actually pay:

Whoop One is the entry-level tier built for anyone who wants essential workout, sleep, and recovery metrics without an overwhelming wall of clinical diagnostics. It comes with the new Whoop 5.0 sensor, a basic wired charger, and a CoreKnit Jet Black band (with five additional color options available). You get core features like heart rate zones, VO2 Max, recovery scoring, and women’s hormonal insights, but it skips the real-time Stress Monitor, the Health Monitor dashboard, and Healthspan biological age tracking.

New member Black Friday price — $189 for your first year ($10 off the regular $199 rate)

Whoop Peak takes things up a level. This tier is designed for fitness enthusiasts who want a deeper dive into things such as biological aging, daily stress, and actual workouts. It bundles the Whoop 5.0 sensor with an upgraded on-the-go wireless PowerPack and a SuperKnit Obsidian band (with 14 color options).

In addition to everything you get with the One tier, Peak then adds the real-time Stress Monitor and a Health Monitor dashboard with vital-sign alerts. Meanwhile, Healthspan tracking helps to calculate your Whoop age and pace of aging.

New member Black Friday price — $199 for your first year ($40 off the regular $239 rate)

New member December sale price (Dec. 12 to Jan. 7) — $215 for your first year (10% off)

Whoop Life switches things up with its health monitoring. It shifts the focus from daily fitness tracking into clinical cardiovascular screening. Instead of the 5.0, it features a distinct physical sensor called the Whoop MG, which pairs with the wireless PowerPack and a choice of 10 band colorways. Along with everything in Peak, the MG hardware unlocks medical-grade, on-demand ECG Heart Screener readings, AFib detection alerts, and daily blood pressure insights.

New member Black Friday price — $299 for your first year ($60 off the regular $359 rate)

New member December sale price (Dec. 12 to Jan. 7) — $323 for your first year (10% off)

Current members aren’t left out of the Black Friday sale. Starting Nov. 19 at 8 a.m. ET, existing members can also renew or upgrade to Whoop Peak for $199 or Whoop Life for $299, both of which come with a free MG-compatible charger.

If you just need extra gear, Whoop is taking 20% off apparel and accessories site-wide (excluding standalone battery packs and the Meridian line), while select bundled accessory packs get a generous 25% price cut. Existing members can also grab a health perk later in the season, with 20% off Advanced Labs and GRAIL testing in the second holiday sale, from Dec. 12 through Jan. 7.

Discounts on Whoop subscriptions are practically nonexistent outside Black Friday — grab your membership while these rare discounts are live.
