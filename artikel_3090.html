eToro Group Ltd. (ETOR -7.55%) reported third quarter results that reflected a strategic emphasis on AI integration and a diversified product model to mitigate seasonal volatility in crypto and equity markets. Management reported that funded accounts and equity assets under administration reached record levels, even as lower overall trading volatility created headwinds for net contribution and profitability. The company is transitioning into an "agentic era," where AI agents like Tori and external connectors for ChatGPT and Grok are becoming primary tools for user engagement and portfolio management. Management confirmed the continued pursuit of geographic expansion through the planned acquisition of TradeZero for U.S. and Canadian markets and the recent agreement to acquire LB Finanzas in Argentina. Strategic capital allocation remains a priority, evidenced by a new $250 million share repurchase program and a focus on maintaining a 20% to 25% marketing spend ratio relative to net contribution.
