Apple TV just added new movie to expanding lineup, more coming soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Apple TV (now $6.99/month for Black Friday) just added a new original movie: Way of the Warrior Kid starring Chris Pratt. The streamer also has a new batch of beloved classic films coming next week.

Way of the Warrior Kid stars Chris Pratt, Linda Cardellini, and Jude Hill and is directed by McG (“Charlie’s Angels,” “Terminator Salvation”).

The film is based on the novel of the same name by bestselling author and retired Navy SEAL Jocko Willink, who also stars.

Here’s the plot summary followed by the trailer:

Middle school has been a nightmare for Marc (Hill), a good kid who’s bullied by his classmates and feels like he’s behind in everything, from academics to P.E. to just making friends. That changes when his injured Uncle Jake (Pratt), a decorated Navy SEAL, comes to spend the summer and recuperate with Marc and his mom, Sarah (Cardellini). To help Marc defend himself, Jake devises an ambitious program he calls “Operation Warrior Kid” based on his SEAL training. Instead of teaching his nephew how to fight, Jake shows Marc what real courage is, facing down his own demons along the way.
