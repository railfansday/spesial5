MSU takes on U-M in Ann Arbor. How to watch B10 game

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Michigan and Michigan State will renew their annual football rivalry under the lights on Saturday, Nov. 7.

The Wolverines will host the Spartans at The Big House in Ann Arbor with kickoff scheduled for 7:30 p.m.

The Spartans (0-5 Big Ten, 2-6 overall this season) are coming off a bye week, while the Wolverines (2-3, 5-3) are coming off a 28-21 win over the Rutgers Scarlet Knights.

Last year, the Wolverines defeated the Spartans 31-20.

For those attending the game in Ann Arbor, expect much cooler temperatures than we've been enjoying. The high will reach only about 50 degrees under partly sunny skies. Temperatures will fall to about 40 degrees by kickoff and drop into the 30s later at night.

Here's what to know about the Michigan-Michigan State game:

On gameday, Nov. 7, in Ann Arbor, the morning will be chilly with temperatures in the 30s rising to the 40s. The high of 51 degrees will come during the mid-afternoon. Conditions are predicted to be partly sunny.

By kickoff, the temperature will fall to about 40 degrees, dropping into the 30s as the night continues.

Kickoff: 7:30 p.m. ET on Saturday, Nov. 7, at the Michigan Stadium in Ann Arbor.

Announcers: Paul Burmeister (play-by-play) and Yogi Roth (analyst). Lewis Johnson will be the sideline reporter. Reggie Smith will serve as the rules analyst.

Radio: Fans can tune in to the Learfield Michigan Sports Network, and the Spartan Media Network — Lansing: WMMQ (94.9 FM); Detroit: WJR (760 AM); Grand Rapids: WBFX (101.3 FM). The game will also be available via SiriusXM on channel 257 (MSU) and channels 231 (U-M).
