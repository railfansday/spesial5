Toast's 2027 Outlook: Ecosystem Expansion Drives Sustainable Profitability

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

This under-the-radar restaurant technology stock boasts a Superscore of 89 from our Hidden Gems Primary database, part of The Motley Fool's Moneyball Database system. Here's why.

By Robert Izquierdo – Updated Nov 26, 2026 at 1:32PM EST

When a bustling restaurant finally closes its doors for the night, the staff doesn't just lock the register and go home. They reconcile payments, manage inventory, adjust payroll, and track the day's sales to see if they actually made money. Toast (TOST +0.06%) does all of this work inside a single, cloud-based platform that replaces the tangled mess of separate services most restaurants used to rely on. As of Nov. 19, the stock traded at $32.30 per share, reflecting a period of intense growth despite a performance drop of about 2% over the last year.

Toast is poised for multi-year success. It added about 8,500 net new locations in Q3, up 21% year over year and demonstrating that its business continues to capture market share. The company is expanding internationally, with the addition of locations in Canada and Australia in the third quarter. It's also extending beyond small and mid-sized U.S. restaurants into larger enterprise chains and retail stores.

Moreover, Toast's transition to profitability positions it to invest in its business, which it is doing with the implementation of artificial intelligence software agents to automate back-office tasks such as employee scheduling. Its Toast IQ Team product launched in Q3 and is designed to perform this work for restaurant operators, further deepening platform stickiness and generating incremental revenue.

The company exited Q3 with $1.2 billion in cash and equivalents on hand. This provides ample liquidity to fund product innovation, weather economic downturns, and execute share buybacks without taking on heavy debt. Its strengthening financial health and expansion into enterprise customers, retailers, and international markets position Toast to see ongoing growth over the next several years.
