BeyondTrust: Front-End Engineer, UX/UI

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

BeyondTrust is a place where you can bring your purpose to life through the work that you do, creating a safer world through our cybersecurity SaaS portfolio.

Our culture of flexibility, trust, and continual learning means you will be recognized for your growth, and for the impact you make on our success. You will be surrounded by people who challenge, support, and inspire you to be the best version of yourself.

The Web Development Team, part of Brand, owns beyondtrust.com end to end: a React and Next.js front end backed by an enterprise headless CMS, serving a global cybersecurity audience and the pipeline that runs through it. beyondtrust.com is BeyondTrust's largest go-to-market surface, and this role builds it — the campaign and launch experiences, the shared component system underneath them, and the front-end quality of the whole site.

This is a hands-on, mid-level individual contributor role for an engineer who is strong in React, TypeScript and Next.js, has the visual judgment to make what they build look right without every state being specified, and uses AI tooling as an everyday part of how they work. Direction comes from the team; execution and craft are yours. The role reports to the Sr. Manager, UX/UI & Digital Experience.
