Washington Spirit and Kansas City Current have booked their places in the final after successfully navigating the play-offs. The two sides will meet at Audi Field on November 21 to decide who lifts the trophy.

Kick-off is scheduled for 8:00pm ET / 5:00pm PT (1:00am GMT on Sunday, November 22).

With live streaming options available for fans around the world, including FREE coverage in the UK, read on for everything you need to know about how to watch the 2026 NWSL Championship final.

Kansas City Current enjoyed a strong regular season, finishing fourth in the standings before edging past Angel City 1-0 after extra time in the quarter-finals and knocking out reigning champions Gotham FC with a 2-1 victory in the semi-finals.

The 2022 runners-up will be looking to go one step further and claim their first NWSL Championship title, with back-to-back MVP Temwa Chawinga and Croix Bethune key to their attacking threat.

The 2024 and 2025 Championship runners-up finished third in the regular season this year and made a statement with a 4-1 quarter-final victory over Portland Thorns.

After beating Utah Royals 2-1 in their semi-final, Washington will be hoping to turn their recent final heartbreak into success. With Trinity Rodman leading the attack, they will be determined to finally reclaim the Championship trophy, last winning it in 2021.
