Avengers: Doomsday Officially Skips Awards Screenings to Avoid Spoiler Leaks

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Marvel’s upcoming blockbuster won’t be getting any love at the BAFTAs or Golden Globes

Don't expect to see Avengers: Doomsday show up on the nomination lists at some of Hollywood's biggest awards shows in the coming months. That's because Disney and Marvel are taking extreme precautions to protect the intimate details of their upcoming superhero blockbuster, going so far as to withhold screeners in hopes of avoiding plot leaks. Still, this strategy won't dash all of Doomsday's award hopes.

As reported by Variety, Marvel has chosen not to screen Doomsday for a number of upcoming awards shows with deadlines ahead of the film's Dec. 18 release date. The biggest awards shows Doomsday will miss out on because of this strategy​​​​​​​ are the BAFTAs and Golden Globes, though the list also includes the AFI Awards' top 10, Critics Choice Awards, New York Film Critics Circle, and more. Perhaps most notably, this means the film will not be present in the Cinematic​​​​​​​ and Box Office Achievement category at the Golden Globes, which recognizes movies that gross more than $150 million, something Doomsday will do with ease.

This isn't the first time Disney has employed this type of strategy to preserve its biggest tent poles. Both Star Wars: The Force Awakens and Spider-Man: No Way Home skipped a number of awards shows to keep their plots under wraps from the general public. These films did go on to get some awards​​​​​​​ attention in later shows, something Doomsday​​​​​​​ could also do.

Preliminary voting for the Oscar nomination shortlists concludes on Dec. 11, before Doomsday's release.​​​​​​​ Despite this,​​​​​​​ Variety has confirmed the film was submitted for consideration in relevant categories. Academy rules for the Best Visual Effects category, where Marvel films have had the best nomination record, allow eligible films to submit​​​​​​​ selected clips in lieu of a complete screening. This helps avoid the exact type of plot leaks Marvel is worried about, though Variety did not confirm whether the Academy was shown such clips​​​​​​​ or was given a full screening.

Even with its eligibility​​​​​​​ confirmed, Doomsday has an uphill battle to gain recognition at the Oscars​​​​​​​. Sure, Marvel movies have been nominated in the past, but the MCU​​​​​​​ has won only four awards in total​​​​​​​. Black Panther won three, and its sequel won the remaining one.​​​​​​​ Crucially, none of those were for Best Visual Effects. Doomsday is likeliest to get a Best Visual Effects nomination but other categories​​​​​​​, and a win, don't seem that likely.
