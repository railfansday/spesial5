GTA 6: Does Jason Have To Participate In The Safe Word Mission

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

GTA 6 has a lot of variety in its story missions. A few even test the relationship between Jason and Lucia, leading to decisions that could affect other elements of the game. It's a lot to keep in mind, especially as you decide which characters to bring on any given adventure. Some missions are done solo, but other open-world activities are more open-ended.

Spoilers for Grand Theft Auto 6 are below.

One of the biggest missions that takes all of that into account is Safe Word, encountered in Chapter 3. Jason will be faced with a choice, and it seems like a pretty big deal at the time. Here's what happens if you don't participate.

During the Safe Word mission, Jason will be roped into acting in an adult video. Masquerading as the pool boy, the director will ask Jason to do a few risqué things, then ask if Jason wants to participate further in the scene.

Naturally, players will be reticent about going through with it, especially if they have a good relationship with Lucia. Either way, if you choose not to participate, the mission can be completed as normal with no drawbacks.

Simply choose no, and the scene will continue. The director will then have Jason brawl with another actor, and you'll put up a short playfight. An interruption will stop the filming, and you'll have the chance to sneak into the house. Choosing to participate in the scene is not required to progress.

Whatever decision you make, after the scene ends, you'll be able to sneak around the side of the house with Jason. Go over to the side door (facing away from the pool; it's on your right) and head in. From there, you can make your way to the marked safe room.

Using the latest chain technology, Jason will latch onto the safe and let the truck pull it out of the house. It's messy, but you end up winning. Note all the carnage that the safe makes while chained up behind you. You can use it to your advantage, swinging into and wrecking vehicles.

While it might not always be apparent, many events in the game can impact the relationship between Jason and Lucia. For instance, making a choice the other partner may not agree with can change the relationship status, though it's very hard to notice when you're paying minimal attention to your partner.

Always make sure you're going on missions together when you can, and call your partner to check in as much as possible. At certain stages of the game, this isn't always an option, but a lot of small acts can add up and contribute a lot to your relationship status.

If you play your cards right, you can even hit the third (of four) relationship tiers right out of the gate in Chapter 1, before anything really starts picking up in the campaign. There are more wild GTA 6 missions to come, but Safe Word is definitely one of the more standout quests from the campaign.
