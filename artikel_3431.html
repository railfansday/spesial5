How to whistle to distract enemies in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Knowing how to whistle in Grand Theft Auto 6 might sound like the least useful skill that Jason and Lucia would need to achieve their goals. As you progress through the game, you unlock different tools to help you deal with enemies and invade locations. However, none of the fancy technology you may have will work as well as a simple whistle in certain situations.

If you want to be prepared for all kinds of mission, then you should learn how to whistle in GTA 6.

Whistling in GTA 6 is quite simple, although it is only available in specific situations. To whistle, hold L2/LT to target an NPC and bring up three options appear in the bottom-right corner of the screen. Among them, you will find the "whistle" option, which you perform by pressing Triangle on PS5 and X on Xbox.

The whistle action is only available when targeting hostile NPCs, such as guards and gang members. I tried hiding behind a wall while targeting a regular NPC on the street, but the option didn't appear. Hiding or crouching is not a prerequisite to using the whistle command, but you should try to stay out of sight, because if the target has already spotted you, the whistle won't work.

When you whistle at an NPC, they will move to your location. Using this method to distract them is a great option when you want to remain stealthy and avoid attracting too much attention. However, whistling does not always work on more than one target at the same time. On some occasions, all the NPCs near my target looked for the sound, while on others, only one became curious about the sound. Keep this in mind, because it might affect your plan on how to handle a group of enemies.
