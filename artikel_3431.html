‘The Other Bennet Sister’ Christmas Special Trailer: Can Mary Bennet Still Be Herself and ‘the Perfect Wife’?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Any news about the upcoming “The Other Bennet Sister” Christmas special — three episodes released in time for Christmas, but not set at Christmas — is worthy of celebration, but the first trailer for the upcoming BritBox treat also packs one heck of a wallop for fans of breakout character Caroline Bingley. Brace yourselves!

Following the first 10 episodes of the smash hit series, based on Janice Hadlow’s novel of the same name, these three new episodes pick up after the very happy ending of the first season finale. But while the marriage of Mary Bennet (Ella Bruccoleri) and Tom Hayward (Dónal Finn) was an unabashedly joyous occasion, the special dares ask: well, now what? Or, more appropriately, how does someone like Mary retain her unique qualities and serve as a “perfect wife”? That’s a tall enough order, and that’s before (gasp) the return of Caroline Bingley (Tanya Reynolds) and Mr. Ryder (Laurie Davidson), who return back to London from their jaunt to Italy with lots of big reveals. We won’t spoil anything that the trailer doesn’t, but the three episodes look to already have plenty of drama long before the Ryders (yes, you read that correctly) come back into the fold with a baby in tow. We’re scandalized and delighted.

Also returning for the special are Ruth Jones as Mrs. Bennet, Varada Sethu as Ann Baxter, Poppy Gilbert as Lizzie Darcy, Maddie Close as Jane Bingley, Grace Hogg-Robinson as Lydia Wickham, Molly Wright as Kitty Buncock, and Anna Fenton-Garvey as Charlotte Collins. Nerys Amber Stocks also joins the cast as Mary’s housemaid Lucy. Screenwriter Sarah Quintrell also returns, writing all three additional episodes of the special. Asim Abbasi has also returned to direct.

BritBox also announced today that its podcast series “On the Box” will launch “On the Box with The Other Bennet Sister” video companion episodes. Following each installment of the special, executive producer Jane Tranter and stars Bruccoleri, Finn, Reynolds, and Davidson join hosts Edith Bowman and Michelle Collins to “unpack the episode, share behind-the-scenes stories, and discuss key moments from the series.” The official companion podcast episodes will be available to watch and listen on BritBox and wherever podcasts are available as soon as each episode premieres.
