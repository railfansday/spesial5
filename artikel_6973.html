Platform Engineer - Database at Supabase

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

We're looking for a Platform Database Engineer to join our team to drive improvements to our internal databases.

The focus of this role is around owning stability and deployment of our internal databases. Supabase’s internal databases are the backbone of our service offerings. You will play a critical role in architecture and optimizations of the databases in partnership with the platform service teams, both strategically and tactically.

In this role, your primary responsibilities will include:

Partnering closely with service teams to understand their workloads and growth, so our databases are sized, configured, and pooled to sustain current and future load.

Performance tuning AWS RDS instances running postgres

Automation and Improvement to operations, including infrastructure as code and scripting

Schema and query optimizations across multiple services

Timely PostgreSQL major, minor, and extension upgrades.

Engaging in proactive identification and resolution of production issues in customer deployments.

Spearheading the creation of best practices, tools, and tests to ensure enhanced stability and decreased deployment risks over time (minimal downtime, blue/green deployments, rollback plans).

Incident response, including driving RCA and followups.

This role demands the following pivotal qualities:

AWS RDS + PostgreSQL Expert: A profound understanding of PostgreSQL running on RDS and its associated tools (AWS Aurora experience also great!) . Familiarity with extensions, performance optimization, and best practices is essential. Experience with connection management (RDS proxy, Pgbouncer, Supervisor) is a huge plus.

Infrastructure Obsessed: Proven expertise in infrastructure management, tooling, and optimization.

Optimization Wizard. Engages across the full stack to identify bottlenecks and drives deep changes (architectural, code, process) that will completely resolve the challenge.

Self-directed & Bias Towards Action: At Supabase, we value independence and proactive problem-solving, driving clarity in complex situations. We anticipate you to be a dynamic self-starter, thriving in asynchronous collaboration across diverse teams. Your knack for spotting areas of improvement and commitment to realizing those enhancements will be key. You'd rather ship a safe, incremental fix today than wait for the perfect redesign next quarter.

Improve the utility and efficiency of our PostgreSQL offerings.

Drive best practices across service teams; assisting with migrations and operations

Own tooling and solutions within the Supabase ecosystem.

3+ years experience with PostgreSQL in AWS setting (RDS or Aurora).

3+ years experience with infrastructure as code technology (terraform, pulumi)

Computer Science Bachelors (or related field)
