Bristol City release video statement on Michael Skubala - it's caused a reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Bristol City CEO Charlie Boss has confirmed that manager Michael Skubala retains the club's backing, despite their recent poor run of form in the Championship.

After leading Lincoln City to the League One title with a total of 103 points last season, Skubala departed Sincil Bank in May to take over at Bristol City, but his tenure at Ashton Gate has not gone to plan so far.

Skubala was given strong backing in the transfer market this summer after his arrival at Bristol City, and among the 11 new faces, the club spent significant transfer fees on two of League One's top scorers last season, Lorent Tolaj and Dom Ballard.

After Skubala picked up 10 points from his first five league games, the Robins sat as high as fourth in the Championship table in early September, but they have won just two of their 10 matches since then, with their home form proving to be a particular cause for concern.

Bristol City suffered their fourth consecutive home defeat as they were beaten 5-0 by Norwich City on Saturday, with goals from Paris Maghoma, Harry Darling, Andre Brooks and Jovon Makama and Mathias Kvistgaarden's late penalty sealing an emphatic victory for the Canaries and ensuring it would be another miserable afternoon for Skubala's men on their own patch.

As we head into the November international break, the Robins currently sit 17th in the Championship table, six points from the play-off places, and some sections of the fanbase called for Skubala to be sacked after their humiliating defeat to Norwich.

Many Bristol City fans were expecting a managerial change to be made during the international break, but the Robins released a video statement on Wednesday afternoon, in which CEO Boss confirmed that the 44-year-old will be given more time at Ashton Gate.

"The game against Norwich City was a long way short of our standards," Boss said in a video on the club's official X account.

"In the days that have followed, there have been a lot of tough and honest conversations around the building.

"Earlier this week, the board met, and we agreed to give Michael Skubala the opportunity to turn our home form around."

When asked how disappointing Saturday's defeat to Norwich was, Boss replied: "It was hugely disappointing, and a lot of the fans made their feelings known loud and clear at Ashton Gate on Saturday.

"I want them to know that everyone shares their frustrations, from the owners, to the board, to James Ellis, the players.

"Every single person at this club feels that frustration.

"Michael Skubala, more than anybody, knows that we need to improve."

"We wouldn't have made this decision if we didn't believe that, together, we could get through this difficult period," Boss added.

"My job as CEO is to look for improvement in every area, and that begins and ends with first team performances."

Boss then delivered a message to the disgruntled Bristol City fanbase, stating: "I'd like to thank them for all of their support home and away, I know it's hugely appreciated by the players, and I know it's hugely appreciated by Michael."

After many Bristol City supporters turned on Skubala during Saturday's defeat to Norwich, it is fair to say that Boss' update on his future was not well received, with plenty taking to X to express their frustration in response to the club's video.

While Skubala is currently the main target of supporters' anger, some Robins fans also directed their ire towards Boss and Sporting Director James Ellis, claiming that the pair are not qualified enough to make the correct decisions.
