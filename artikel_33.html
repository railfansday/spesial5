Russian nuclear satellites plan 'foolhardy', expert says

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

NATO chief Mark Rutte said Monday the alliance would defend against threats in space when asked about German media reports that Russia may be preparing to launch a nuclear-armed satellite. Speaking with FRANCE 24's Jennie Shin, Dafydd Townley, Senior Teaching Fellow in International Security at the University of Portsmouth, says that the alleged plan would be "foolhardy because the impact would be indiscriminate, they wouldn't be able to choose only Western or non-Russian satellites".
