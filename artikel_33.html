Live: France deploys 70,000 police for new wave of anti-government protests

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Between 50 and 60 marches are expected in France on Saturday, according to Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez, who plans to mobilise approximately 70,000 police officers and gendarmes across the country. In Paris, the march will commence at 2pm from Place de la République heading towards Place de la Nation via Bastille.
