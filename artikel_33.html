Moldova issues emergency kit guide, prepares citizens for possible expansion of Ukraine war

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

As the war in Ukraine has highlighted the importance of civilian preparedness, Moldova has launched its own emergency readiness programme. Medicine, batteries and three days' worth of food are among the essentials citizens are encouraged to keep at hand, in case they need to leave at a moment's notice.
