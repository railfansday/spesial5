Trump in Asia ahead of talks with Russian and Chinese presidents

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

US President Donald Trump has arrived in the Philippines' capital on the first leg of a trip to Asia, which will end in a meeting in China with its President Xi Jinping and his Russian counterpart Vladimir Putin.

A red carpet was rolled out for Trump as he walked off Air Force One in Manila. A band played one of his favourite songs, Y.M.C.A., before he was whisked away by helicopter to a hotel.

Trump's first meeting at the Asean Summit was with US partners in the Quad - India's Narendra Modi, whom he described as a "very tough man", Japan's Sanae Takaichi, who "may be even tougher", and Australia's Anthony Albanese.

"Our countries are the four corners of the free, open, stable, prosperous Indo-Pacific," the US president said.

"We have outstanding relationships and co-ordinate closely... my phone is always open," Trump added.

Many countries in South East Asia have been hit hard by Trump's tariffs and his war on Iran - but their exports to the US rose 30% last year thanks to the AI boom for which the region manufactures many of the components.

The US is hoping to draw more countries here into its "Pax Silica" tech and AI ecosystem, and away from China.

But China's huge influence in this region cannot easily be challenged, and certainly not in the few hours Trump is in the Philippines.

Later on Tuesday, Trump signed a trade deal with Thailand alongside the country's Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul.

The US is Thailand's largest export market, accounting for 24% of its total shipments this year.

Trump then joined South East Asia leaders at a dinner, telling those gathered: "This is one of the world's most vibrant and productive regions filled with the tremendous potential."

After an overnight stay in Manila, the US president will fly to Shenzhen in China to attend a summit of Asia Pacific leaders and have nuclear talks with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin.

Asked if he would also be open to a one-on-one meeting with Putin, Trump said: "If it's available, I would".
