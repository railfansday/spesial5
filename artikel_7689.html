Lindsay Clancy judge considers retrial at hearing to dismiss murder case

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Lindsay Clancy was being tried for first-degree murder. She admitted to killing her three children and claimed that she suffered from postpartum psychosis. (Photo by CJ Gunther/The Boston Herald via the Associated Press)

The possible retrial date for Lindsay Clancy, a former labor and delivery nurse charged with strangling and killing her three small children in 2023, might be March 2027, according to a Massachusetts judge during a Monday hearing to determine whether the triple murder case should be dismissed.

Massachusetts Superior Court Judge William Sullivan offered the tentative retrial date, and prosecutors in the case said they fully intend to retry Clancy, 36, who admitted to strangling to death her three children—5-year-old daughter Cora; 3-year-old son Dawson; and 8-month-old son Callan—during a postpartum psychosis episode. She later cut her wrists and neck and jumped from a second-story window, which paralyzed her from the waist down, according to coverage by Courthouse News Service.

The five-week trial resulted in a jury deadlock after one juror held out to convict Clancy. Eleven jurors were prepared to present a not guilty by reason of insanity verdict, which would have sent Clancy to a psychiatric facility instead of sentencing her to life in prison.

Michael Desronvil, the lone dissenter, wanted to convict and faced scrutiny after his name was leaked on the internet.

Jennifer Sprague, a Plymouth County, Massachusetts, assistant district attorney, defended Desronvil’s decision but did not comment on what charges that Clancy would face in a new trial.
