Former White House press secretary Zacharia investigated by California bar over lawyer claims

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Then-White House press secretary Katie Zacharia speaks during a news briefing Oct. 13, 2026, in Washington, D.C. (Photo by Chip Somodevilla/Getty Images)

A former White House press secretary for President Donald Trump—whose tenure lasted five days in October—is facing an investigation by the State Bar of California after describing herself as a lawyer without being licensed to practice law.

Katie Zacharia, who graduated with a JD from the Pepperdine University Rick J. Caruso School of Law in 2011, never passed the bar but has repeatedly referred to herself as a lawyer. A California code says a candidate must have passed the bar exam to be called a lawyer.

Zacharia has said she worked as the outside counsel to the World Bank, an international financial institution, and as the general counsel for a conservative California nonprofit, according the Campaign for Accountability, a nonprofit government watchdog group.

The Campaign for Accountability received a notice of the probe in a Thursday letter from the state bar and told the nonprofit that its complaint was assigned to an investigator and legal adviser.

“Zacharia may have thought she was just playing a lawyer on TV, but by repeatedly holding herself out as an attorney on news programs, she was pretending to have authority she lacks,” said Michelle Kuppersmith, the executive director of the Campaign for Accountability, in a statement Thursday.
