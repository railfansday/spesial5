NFL Star Matthew Stafford’s Wife Kelly Claps Back at Facelift Comment

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Kelly Stafford, wife to Los Angeles Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford and mom to their four daughters, reacted to a user’s criticism after revealing how she takes care of her “tired mom" skin.

Kelly Stafford is ready to tackle some online criticism.

After the wife of Los Angeles Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford shared her skincare routine on social media, she clapped back at one user who left a foul comment.

In the Nov. 12 Instagram video, Kelly—mom to 9-year-old twin girls Sawyer and Chandler, as well as daughters Hunter, 8, and Tyler, 6, with Matthew—began with no makeup and made a quip about her “tired mom skin” and “tired mom eyes” before detailing her regimen.

While many praised the 37-year-old’s skin, one viewer named David told her, “Get a facelift……. You have money.”

For her part, Kelly called an audible and shared the comment on her Instagram Stories, writing in response, with a heart emoji to boot, “David, my guy. I pray no one ever says this to your beautiful daughter.”

Of course, in the 18 seasons that she’s been a WAG to a star quarterback, Kelly—who tied the knot with Matthew in 2015—has made it an extra point to be outspoken about fairness.

That’s why she also recently called out the NFL after learning that the Rams won’t receive the typical amount of days off this season.

"Just curious to hear the thought process behind making the Rams the only team in the NFL without a true bye week," she wrote on her Instagram Stories Nov. 3. "Because damn, after 18 years around this league, even I need a week off during the season… and I have never played a single snap."

The registered nurse added, "I'm tired just watching from the stands. Can't imagine how the guys feel."

Still, Kelly wouldn’t change her life of supporting her husband and being team mom to her girls for anything.

“One day, they’ll look back and remember the stadiums, the cities, and watching their dad do what he loves,” she wrote on Instagram Nov. 4 about traveling to away games with her daughters. “But I’ll remember the airport scootering, running through the hotels, jumping on the beds, dancing on the sidelines, playing charades far past bedtime and all the little moments in between.”

Noting she doesn’t take “a second of it for granted,” Kelly added, “These adventures have become some of my favorite memories of being their mom.”
