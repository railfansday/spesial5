Former NBA Star Jalen Rose Launching Sports and Culture Talk Show “Pop The Trunk” (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The ex-ESPN analyst will have skits, interviews, live performances and more in his new show, which will stream on YouTube.

Jalen Rose, the NBA star-turned media personality, is launching his own talk and variety show, Pop The Trunk, he tells The Hollywood Reporter. The show will debut in January, with twice-weekly episodes running on YouTube and other podcast platforms.

Rose played for six teams in his career (his longest tenure was with the Indiana Pacers) before moving into broadcasting, where he worked for Fox Sports, NBC Sports and TNT Sports, though it is his run as a basketball analyst at ESPN where he is likely best known to sports fans. In fact, it was his work on Fox’s Best Damn Sports Show and on his Jalen & Jacoby podcast for ESPN that inspired Pop The Trunk.

“I was really fortunate to get into the multimedia space while I was still a player in the NBA, working on the Best Damn Sports Show,” he tells THR, adding that his segments there inspired him to pitch Bill Simmons (then the head of ESPN’s Grantland) on what became Jalen & Jacoby. “I was always proud of the content of the show. We used sports as a backdrop, you know. If Benny the Butcher is at a Bills game, he’s the correspondent. We were running a different race with story times and animations and things of that nature, these are all things things that I still don’t see happening in the marketplace that I’m excited to bring back as a brainchild.”

Rose compares Pop The Trunk to an old-school TV variety show, with interviews, live performances, skits, animated segments and more, adding that he is “going to draw inspiration from the shows of the golden era, like when it was appointment viewing.”

“I grew up watching Saturday Night Live and In Living Color, Dave Chappelle, and when you turn on those shows, you don’t know who’s gonna be on the show. So that’s kind of what I’m looking to recreate,” Rose adds. “I’ve done the sitting in front of the microphone show for an hour, I’ve done that on television, I’ve done that on radio, and I’ve done it in podcasts. And so this is a graduation of that idea.”

So what can viewers expect? Rose says the A block will likely be a monologue (“it’s probably going to upset a lot of people, but similar to like what you might consider a roast of whatever current event topics are happening,” he quips) though the next segments will evolve over time.

“Then the guest on the show, for example, whoever it is, will get a chance to hear what I say and agree or disagree with what I have to say, and then we’ll get into the hot button topics that are happening around fashion, sports, entertainment, multimedia, life, politics, whatever, and have people that are knowledgeable about those topics on to discuss them and create quality content. It’s not gonna just be me sitting in front of a black microphone for an hour and a half.”

Sada Baby and Detroit Diamond are among the confirmed recurring guests.

But Pop The Trunk is also serving as a launchpad of sorts for Same Page Entertainment, the Detroit-based media and production company founded by Rose and Platinum Equity founder, chairman and CEO, and Detroit Pistons owner Tom Gores. The company has, so far, released South West High for Tubi, and has wrapped production on the film The Streak, which stars Ed Helms.

During the conversation with THR, Rose took a call from someone working on the set in downtown Detroit, underscoring the work that he and Gores are doing.

“The person that just called me was actually calling me about the LED screen that they’re putting in the studio, so along with my partner Tom Gores and Same Page Entertainment and our team, I’m building a company plus launching a show, so it’s brick by brick,” Rose says. “The theme song, which really doesn’t exist for shows right now, it’s the animations, the story times. Those are things that fans loved from me back in the day that we were able to do and create a level of enthusiasm for us.”
