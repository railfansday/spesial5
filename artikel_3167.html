Down 24% This Year, Is MercadoLibre (MELI) an Obvious Growth Stock to Buy in November?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

MercadoLibre has been a highly volatile stock, but it has significant growth potential.

MercadoLibre (MELI +1.50%) is often described as a cross between Amazon.com and PayPal, as it encompasses both a dominant online marketplace (MercadoLibre) and a major "fintech" business (Mercado Pago). You might not have heard of it, though, as it's focused on the Latin American market, which is rather... huge. According to one report, the retail market is valued at $2.0 trillion in 2026 and is expected to reach $3.4 trillion by 2034.

So why is the stock down, and is it worth considering for your portfolio? Let's see.

Founded in 1999 and headquartered in Montevideo, Uruguay, MercadoLibre sports a market value near $76 billion (as of Nov. 6). It's active in 18 countries and was recently delivering 3.2 billion items annually. It has 136 million unique buyers and 93 million monthly active users (both numbers as of the third quarter).

For example, the shrinking profit margins are partly due to the company extending more free shipping and credit to shoppers. That can build loyalty and can help it compete against rivals and would-be rivals. It's also due to the company investing in expanding its fulfillment capabilities, which can pay off with better service for customers.

Meanwhile, the stock is appealingly priced at recent levels, with a forward-looking price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio of 28, well below the five-year average of 50, and a recent price-to-sales ratio of 2.0, well below the five-year average of 4.8.

Long-term investors seeking businesses with solid growth potential, especially those looking for investments based outside the U.S. for diversification, should take a closer look at MercadoLibre.
