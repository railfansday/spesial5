The GEO Group, Inc. (GEO -8.57%) reported record-breaking quarterly financial performance, achieving its highest-ever levels of quarterly revenue and Adjusted EBITDA. Management stated that the results were driven by the activation of new facility contracts signed in 2025 and an expanded scope of services for federal agencies. The company completed the $950 million sale of the Adelanto complex to the federal government while retaining a multiyear contract to provide support services at the site. This divestiture enabled the redemption of $650 million in senior secured notes, significantly reducing corporate debt and lowering net leverage to approximately 1.0 times Adjusted EBITDA. Management also increased its share repurchase authorization to $1.25 billion, signaling a shift in capital allocation toward shareholder returns as the company transitions toward a support services-focused business model.
