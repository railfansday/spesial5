There are four key GTA 6 Pegassi Vitello locations you can head to either for yourself or for Randy's client

The GTA 6 Pegassi Vitello is one of the expensive sports cars on the list of cars Randy's trying to collect for his prominent client. As ever, you can look around in affluent areas and outside nightclubs, but there are four easy Vitello locations you can hit which make this challenge much easier.

Three of these are different variants of the GTA 6 car, while the fourth base model is at a location recommended by Randy himself if you wait long enough. Luckily, Randy only needs one, so it's well worth taking the base model to him, then returning to grab one of the other GTA 6 Pegassi Vitello variants for yourself when you can afford the registration fee. If, like me, you end up totalling the first one, it won't respawn, so at least you can head to one of the others locations to finish the job before locking in your own.

Fortunately, none of them are any harder than your usual car heist, you might face a few security guards or cops, but grabbing them all is easy, and they all make a fairly decent price when sold on.
