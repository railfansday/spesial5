GTA 6 Launch Leads to Special Holiday for All Palworld Dev Employees

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

GTA 6 launches in one week's time, and the anticipation and excitement for the game is hard to ignore.

Rockstar Games' inbound open world action game is going to be a major cultural event when it launches on 19th November, and it seems some companies are recognising that — most notably, Pocketpair, the team behind Palworld.

The Japanese studio has announced that all employees are officially entitled to a period of leave from 19th to 23rd November so they can enjoy GTA 6 over an uninterrupted five days.

"On November 19, millions of gamers around the world will find themselves sitting at school or work, gazing longingly out the window, knowing that a new era of gaming is about to begin," the statement reads.

"At Pocketpair, we believe that such a historic occasion deserves our full attention. Therefore, after careful consideration, we have decided that all employees who intend to play Grand Theft Auto VI will be granted a special five-day holiday."

It's a move that's going down extremely well on social media, as you'd expect.

GTA 6 is reportedly causing a big spike in employees taking annual leave, with US employers estimated to lose a combined $1 billion of business on the 19th thanks to staff staying home to play.

Pocketpair isn't the only company offering time off; Logitech has announced Logi Game Day, an initiative that grants all employees a single day off for the purpose of playing video games.

While this can be used at any point over the next 12 months, the timing of its arrival suggests it's been implemented with GTA 6 in mind.

A while ago, a smaller business, Burger Motorsports, announced its intention to observe a day off for the game too.

It seems there are some businesses out there trying to get ahead of the curve. For what is almost certainly the biggest game of the decade, I think it's a smart move.
