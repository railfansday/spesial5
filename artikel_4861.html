The 8 Best Slippers for Women for Max Coziness, Tested By a Style Writer

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

From couch-rotting to errands, these kicks have you covered.

Now that the weather has cooled down and leaves are on the ground, my style writer brain is in peak outfit mode. I’ve already imagined the ways I’ll wear my leather jackets, chunky knits, and bootcut jeans. But before I start styling my way through the fall and winter, there’s one essential I always reach for first: slippers. I test a lot of shoes in the name of journalism, and few spark more joy than a pair of cute slippers that add the perfect cozy feel to any outfit. Whether I’m channeling my inner couch potato on a lazy Sunday or running errands around town, the best slippers in my stash hit the sweet spot of soft, supportive, and actually stylish.

A lot of the same principles that apply to shopping for supportive sneakers also apply to slippers, says Anne Sharkey, DPM, a podiatrist at Solely Podiatry in Austin, TX. After all, a slipper is the ultimate cozy shoe, but it’s also incredibly useful for keeping your arches happy while walking around hardwood floors at home. That’s why Sharkey says cushioning and arch support are key. Your slippers should feel plush underfoot, but not so squishy that you lose stability. Where you plan to wear them also matters: A pair of house slippers needs less traction and durability than a pair for outdoors, which should have a thicker, more grippy bottom.

After over a year of testing, I found slippers that are plush enough to lounge in, supportive enough for a spin around town, and stylish enough that you’ll actually want to wear them.

When it comes to a slipper that checks every box (plush, supportive, and legitimately stylish), the Ugg Cozy Slipper is the one I always come back to. I’ve been wearing this slipper since fall 2024 with everything from jeans and Henleys to matching sweat sets.

The slipper earns its name with extra-cozy materials, from the sheepskin fur lining to the sweater-knit outer and fluffy collar. I love that it keeps my feet warm and toasty, even during cold winter months. Board-certified foot and ankle surgeon Bobby Pourizaee, DPM says this slipper is excellent for comfort and lounging. Podiatrist and board-certified foot and ankle surgeon Bruce Pinker, DPM agrees, adding that the slip-on style makes this slipper easy to wear.

Beneath the fluff, there’s real structure that keeps my feet fatigue-free. The contoured footbed and firm-yet-plush midsole made with 17 mm of sheepskin hug my arches just right. The outsole is sturdy and grippy, made with dense EVA foam and tons of traction. I wear it on long walks with my dog around the Long Island suburbs and never worry about slipping or falling, no matter the weather. However, it’s still lightweight enough to feel like an indoor slipper.

Pinker says that it could be improved if the heel region were closed for more support and stability, but the easy-on, easy-off fit is part of why I love this slipper. If you’re after something warm, cozy, and super stylish for both indoor and outdoor wear, I can’t recommend this slipper enough.

For a cozy, budget-friendly slipper that doesn’t skimp on comfort, the Dearfoams Lacey Memory Foam Slipper is the one I’ve been living in lately. At under $30, this slipper is way more cushy, warm, and supportive than lots of other house slippers I’ve worn.

I padded around my house in this slipper nonstop for about a year, and it held up beautifully through coffee runs to the kitchen, long hours at my desk, and quick trips to grab packages. It’s definitely a house slipper, but in the best way possible—pure comfort, with just enough structure to feel supportive.

The memory foam cushioning is what makes this slipper so comfortable, Sharkey says. Each step in it feels super plush, molding to the shape of my foot for that “walking on pillows” feel. The fleece lining also keeps my feet cozy without overheating, and the soft knit upper gives it a lounge-all-day kind of vibe that pairs perfectly with pajamas and sweatpants. I absolutely love the heel and arch cushioning, especially when my feet ache after a long day commuting to and from Manhattan, but Sharkey says they should only be worn in the house since they lack the structure needed for long periods on your feet.

But for a house slipper, there’s way more support than I expected. The raised heel area keeps the slipper from sliding off my foot, even when I’m walking up or down stairs. It’s a small but thoughtful feature that makes it feel a bit more secure than your average slip-on. And while the outsole isn’t meant for outdoor adventures, it’s durable enough for quick steps onto the porch or grabbing the mail.

I wore this slipper for nearly a year straight while trodding around my house, and absolutely love them. However, I will say that at about the 10-month mark, they needed to be replaced, as they stretched out a bit and lost some of the much-needed cushioning, support, and traction.

Thanks to its incredible level of cushioning, the Vionic Gemma II Mule Slipper keeps my feet pain-free even after walking in it all day. Pourizaee says it's a strong recommendation thanks to the contoured footbed. The midsole is made from a flexible EVA foam that not only feels super plush under my foot but is designed to absorb shock and reduce stress on the joints. I have lingering ankle and foot pain on one side from a previous high ankle sprain, and while some shoes exacerbate those symptoms, this slipper keeps my feet happy. That comes as no surprise, since this slipper earned an APMA Seal of Acceptance.

It also has great support and stability features. Pinker says it would be improved with a closed-heel design, but I find that the deep heel cup helps, and the strap closure keeps my foot firmly planted—even while speed walking on city sidewalks. Pourizaee adds that the contoured footbed helps keep feet aligned and relieves foot strain. While I often wake up with sore feet that hurt as soon as I step out of bed, I find that when I’ve worn this slipper the day before, foot fatigue is next to zero.

If you like a cozy slipper, the Gemma II delivers. I have the cream color, which is made with a wool upper that keeps my feet warm and looks cute with all my cold-weather loungewear. Other styles have a faux-fur or flannel upper, which also looks super warm and cozy. Plus, since the slipper is designed for indoor and outdoor wear, the synthetic outsole is ultra-durable with a patterned tread design. I’ve even gone for post-rain walks with my dog on wet pavement, and never felt like I was going to slip.

Overall, this slipper is warm, cozy, and ultra-cushioned. It keeps my feet happy with the plush feel underfoot, and I love keeping it by the door when I need a cute shoe to slip on fast.

If you need a slipper with serious arch support, the Orthofeet Charlotte Slipper delivers. Plus, it comes with additional insoles and spacers for days when my feet need extra support.

The Ortho-Cushion system (which includes the removable orthotic insole, ergonomic sole, and fitting spacers) helps to customize the fit and level of cushioning and support. I didn’t realize before selecting my normal size that Orthofeet recommends ordering a half-size down for a more snug fit, but placing the smallest blue fitting spacer (1/16 inch) under the insole kept my foot feeling super secure. Even without any customization, I was impressed by the high level of cushioning that hugs my arches and feels really nice under my heels. The ergonomic sole not only has ample cushioning underfoot, but also the subtle “rocker” design, which refers to a little lift under the heel and toe so those two parts of the shoe don’t touch the ground when standing still, similar to a rocking chair. That shape propels you forward with each step and also helps take some pressure off the joints. Plus, the EVA outsole also has lots of traction patterns to keep every step feeling stable and secure.

I also love the faux fur lining and insole that feels super cozy and keeps my feet warm even while walking outdoors on chilly mornings. The suede outer adds to that cozy vibe and looks super cute. I initially wasn’t sure if this slipper would be my style, but I find myself reaching for it all the time when I leave the house for quick errands and coffee dates with friends. I have the taupe color and often pair it with ankle jeans and sweaters for a comfortable fall fit.

While it does run slightly small, the brand offers half sizes, and the slipper comes with customizable features, so I didn’t find it an issue at all. However, if you’re concerned about fit, I recommend ordering a half size down.

The Sunday Mule Slipper from Bombas is perhaps the most stylish casual shoe that I own. The elongated, oval-toe shape looks super chic, and it's wrapped entirely in a fluffy sherpa material for that cozy-yet-put-together feel. Whether I’m couch-rotting on the weekend, grabbing a latte, or taking a casual stroll around town, I can always count on this slipper. It looks great with a wide-leg lounge pant or leggings with slouchy socks, but I’ve also worn it with a cropped pair of jeans.

Sharkey says it's a good cozy and comfortable option, and that Bombas did a great job with the level of cushioning. That’s thanks to the memory foam footbed, which has a nice squishy feel, contours to the shape of the foot, and softens impact with every step. That contouring feature does wonders for making sure the slipper stays on, even while trekking all around town. However, Sharkey says the backless design does tend to slip off the feet if you’re not careful. I haven’t had any issues there, but it’s something to keep in mind if your feet need an ultra-supportive shoe—in which case, the Orthofeet Charlotte Slipper might be a better choice.

The rubber outsole gives this slip-on shoe a major upgrade, since it helps provide traction when I wear it outside. I keep this slipper mainly for wearing around the house or quick errands, but I have worn it during short, casual walks without worry. The fluffy sherpa material, inside and out, makes it a super cozy option for lazy days cuddled up on the couch and keeps my feet warm even when I take it for a spin outdoors.

Plus, there’s plenty of chic color options. I went with the cream, which I find pairs well with almost anything in my closet—but there’s also a black and brown for additional neutrals, and a dusty purple or tomato red option for something more bold. While this slipper from Bombas might not be the most supportive option, I’d recommend it to anyone looking for a plush, super stylish slipper.

Clogs get a bad rap for making your feet sweat, slide around, and (sorry!) being kind of ugly. But this slip-on clog from Rothy’s nails it on the style front, and is also durable, breathable, and super comfortable. The Casual Clog is well-loved by me and executive editor Abigail Cuffey—oh, and Sharkey recommends it, too.

This slipper/clog hybrid is made with the brand’s signature cushioning made from recycled water bottles and arch support that Sharkey says is ultra comfortable. She also says she likes that the heel cup is a bit deeper than your standard slipper, which helps with stability. I find that the deep heel cup helps cover the back of my foot, so it doesn’t slip off when I’m running around the house doing laundry or walking up and down stairs. The outsole is also made from recycled water bottles and has a grooved design that makes it super durable with tons of traction. I usually wear it for casual outings and meeting up with friends, and find it looks great with baggy jeans and sweaters for a casual-yet-put-together look.

While I own the merino wool version, Cuffey owns both the merino wool and hemp styles. She’s been wearing the hemp clog since fall 2024, and loves that the ultra-cushioned footbed molds to her feet. “It feels like a hug for your heels and toes,” she says. She wears it as a lounge shoe and an outdoor shoe, because of the grippy outsole and substantial feel. Cuffey’s thoughts on the merino wool clog are generally the same, but she loves that the wool feels a little cozier while still airy and breathable. “I usually go through slippers so fast because they get so gross, but Rothy’s Casual Clogs are the first ones that have staying power in my closet,” she says.

It does run a little wide, but the fabric is stretchy, so I haven’t had any issues with it feeling too loose on my feet. But if you’re in between sizes, Rothy’s recommends sizing down for a better fit.

The L.L. Bean Wicked Good Moccasin has become my favorite ever since I got it in the mail. A moccasin is a slipper made of soft leather that wraps around the foot with a single gathered seam at the front. This style is the cushiest, coziest, most supportive I’ve ever worn, earning a permanent spot in my fall and winter footwear rotation.

Unlike some moccasins, Pinker says this style feels especially stable thanks to the rubber outsole with added grip and traction. Plus, the suede material is more structured than other moccasins I’ve worn, which makes for a snug, secure feel. I’ve worn this moccasin weekly over the past month, and I can tell it’s extremely durable. The suede has a lot of heft, and combined with the hand-stitched construction, this moccasin stands up to wear and tear. The fur lining is also extremely comfortable and warm, and I love wearing this shoe on colder days to keep my feet toasty. The insole is also made from the same fur material, which delivers a super plush, cushioned feel underfoot.

There’s also plenty of fur lining the outside of the slipper, which I find super stylish, along with the rawhide laces and bow. It looks great with both joggers and wide-leg sweatpants, and I even wore it recently with cuffed jeans and a boxy sweater for a casual brunch. I have the brown pair, but with seven shades to choose from (including blue, red, and purple!), there’s no shortage of styling potential.

One note: this slipper is spot-clean only, unlike others that can go in the washing machine. However, I wear it nearly every morning lately to walk my dog when the ground is still dewy, and I haven’t gotten any stains that took more than a quick pass with a dry washcloth to remove.

I desperately needed a new house slipper a few weeks ago, so I decided to snag this pair from Fulton—and I couldn’t be happier with my decision. While you could totally get away with wearing this slipper outside (it’s super cute and definitely sturdy), I love keeping this pair inside and free from outside germs—IYKYK.

At first, I honestly wasn’t sure this pair would be a winner: the footbed was super uncomfortable the first day that I wore them. It felt like all the raised areas were in the wrong places, which made it a little difficult to walk. Had I known then that the Fulton website explains there’s a 10-hour break-in process, I’d have been way less concerned.

Lo and behold, by the following day the slippers felt much better—and only continued to get more comfortable as the cork footbed molded to the exact shape of my foot. I wouldn’t call this slipper plush by any means (though the wool upper is quite cozy), so if that’s what you’re looking for, the Ugg, Dearfoams, Orthofeet, or L.L. Bean styles might be better options. But this shoe makes my feet feel so good.

I’ve been suffering from some pretty gnarly foot pain from recently developed plantar fasciitis, but since I started wearing this slipper around the house every day, my feet feel brand new. My arches and heels feel way less achy throughout the day, and it no longer hurts to plant my feet on the ground first thing in the morning.

If you prefer a slide over a slipper, this pick from Oofos is perfect. I often bring it to the gym to wear in the locker room, sauna, or on the way to my post-workout smoothie spot, and the cushy foam insole feels great on my achy feet.
