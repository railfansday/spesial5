THE ROAD TO WORLD HERITAGE — ARATA ISOZAKI STREETART PLAZA: A Road to World Heritage

The name ARATA ISOZAKI STREET expresses our hope that ART PLAZA—the former Oita Prefectural Library designed by Arata Isozaki—will one day be inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List. If that hope becomes reality, this street will quite literally become a road leading to a World Heritage site.
