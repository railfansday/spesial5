Wednesday’s episode of AEW Dynamite is combined with AEW Collision for a special Thanksgiving airing. You can watch this week’s episode live on TV on TBS, available on most basic cable packages.

Don’t have cable? There are also a few ways to livestream AEW Dynamite online for free.

There are a few ways to find a live TBS feed online, to stream AEW Dynamite at home.

DIRECTV is a streaming service that lets you watch hundreds of live television channels online, without needing a cable box or subscription.

DIRECTV’s “Entertainment” package comes with TBS along with dozens of other live local and cable channels. The package is on sale for $49.99/month right now but DIRECTV offers a five-day free trial that you can use to watch this week’s episode of AEW Dynamite & Collision for free, if you sign up now. There’s no commitment required and you can cancel before your free trial is up to avoid being charged.

I personally watch live TV over the internet using Fubo (or FuboTV).

With prices starting at $63.99 per month for the first month of service ($88.99 per month afterward), Fubo’s “Core” plan is a cable-alternative streaming service that offers TBS. The plan features more than 200 other channels, including ABC, CBS, FOX, Bravo and others. Fubo comes with a five-day free trial too, so you can watch AEW Dynamite‘s Thanksgiving special online for free.

For even more content, Hulu + Live TV bundles more than 100 live TV channels with Hulu, Disney+ and ESPN Unlimited, to offer even more exclusive and original sporting events, TV shows and movies.

The streamer offers a free trial that’ll get you your first three days free to access TBS, and the entire Hulu + Live TV lineup. Pricing starts at $99.99/month after that.

HBO Max is the official streaming home for AEW Dynamite and the streamer lets you watch the action live and on-demand.

A subscription to HBO Max is the best way to watch AEW Dynamite online. The streaming service starts at $10.99 per month for the ad-supported plan but is currently discounted to just $5.49 as part of a Black Friday streaming deal.

All of the above streaming services let you watch AEW Dynamite & Collision on your phone, computer, tablet or smart TV.

The AEW Dynamite Thanksgiving Eve special features the AEW Women’s Tag Team Championships pitting Wrathlander (Harley Cameron & Kris Statlander) vs. Divine Dominion (Megan Bayne & Lena Kross). There will also be a match between Kyle Fletcher vs. Andrade El Idolo, with MJF also scheduled to appear.

MJF of course, threw down the gauntlet recently when he challenged current AEW champion Will Ospraey for the AEW World Championship. Their matchup (if it happens) would likely take place at AEW Worlds End in December.
