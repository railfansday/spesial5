Hello Sunshine’s Lauren Neustadter to Leave Reese Witherspoon’s Company at End of Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Lauren Neustadter, president of film and television at Reese Witherspoon‘s Hello Sunshine, is set to step down from the company at the end of the year, the collaborators announced on Friday. Her exit comes after Hello Sunshine let go of about 50% of its staff last month after Witherspoon and Mediawan partnered to buy the company back from Candle Media.

“Lauren’s taste, passion and leadership have been integral to Hello Sunshine’s film and television success,” Witherspoon said in a statement. “I’m so proud of what we accomplished together and grateful for her partnership and the lasting foundation she built around telling powerful, complex female-driven stories that resonate.” Related Stories ‘Chicago Fire,’ ‘Med’ and ‘P.D.’ Set New Crossover Episodes on NBC in January

Neustadter added in a statement, “Building Hello Sunshine alongside Reese and our incredible team has been the privilege of a lifetime. I’m deeply proud of all the shows and movies we’ve made, the doors we’ve opened for new voices, and the ways we have put women in the spotlight both in front of and behind the camera. I am grateful for every moment of it, and I will always be cheering for Hello Sunshine.”

Neustadter joined the company in 2017, and has served as executive producer on series including “The Morning Show,” “Big Little Lies,” “Little Fires Everywhere” and “The Last Thing He Told Me.” She oversaw adaptations of books from Reese’s Book Club including “Tiny Beautiful Things” and “Daisy Jones & the Six.” She also worked on feature films including “Where the Crawdads Sing” and TriStar’s upcoming “The Nightingale.”

Neustadter started her career as a film executive at Miramax and Twentieth Century Fox, then became an executive at FOX network, where she oversaw “Brooklyn Nine-Nine,” “The Mindy Project” and “Lethal Weapon.”

Hello Sunshine plans to focus on premium film and television and Reese’s Book Club, as well as expanding into new storytelling formats and distribution models.

The news was first reported by the Hollywood Reporter.
