‘Selling Sunset’ Sets Season 10 Premiere and Announces Full Cast; Christine Quinn and Heather Rae El Moussa Officially Return (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

More than two years after its ninth season dropped on Netflix, “Selling Sunset” Season 10 — premiering on the streamer on Dec. 4 — has officially set its new cast, which features a number of new faces.

OG “Selling Sunset” cast members Christine Quinn, who left (with a bang!) after Season 5, and Heather Rae El Moussa, who hasn’t been a series regular since Season 6, are returning. Along with Jason Oppenheim and Brett Oppenheim, Amanza Smith and Bre Tiesi — who’ve been on the show since Season 2 and Season 6, respectively — are also coming back as full-time cast members.

Beyond that, though, creator Adam DiVello and his Done and Done Productions have done a major overhaul of the luxury real estate reality series, which premiered on Netflix in 2019. Gone are Chrishell Stause, who quit after Season 9, Mary Bonnet, Chelsea Lazkani, Emma Hernan, Alanna Gold and awkward villain Nicole Young. Sandra Vergara (sister of Sofía Vergara), who appeared on Season 9 of “Selling Sunset,” is also no longer on the show.

Bolstering the ensemble at the Oppenheim Group are four new agents. Jordyn Braff, who appeared on DiVello’s New York-set “Selling the City” — which was canceled after one season — is now among the cast (viewers might remember her as the one who dated Trevor Noah). Sonni Pacheco, who was briefly married to Jeremy Renner and shares a daughter with him, has also joined the show, as have Tara Burns and former “All American” star Sasha Lance. Though the future of spinoff “Selling the OC” is unclear, its star Alex Hall will also appear a few times on “Selling Sunset” in Season 10.

The synopsis for Season 10 reads as such: “The O Group is getting a major glow-up for its milestone 10th season! Original cast members Christine Quinn and Heather Rae El Moussa make their grand returns, joining Bre Tiesi and Amanza Smith, while a crop of fresh new agents storm the office, raising the stakes, elevating the fashion, and serving up delicious drama. Against the glossy backdrop of Los Angeles real estate, the brokerage must adapt to a bold new reality as each agent’s willpower is put to the test, new dynamics quickly begin to shift, and drama from the past comes back to bite.” Related Stories Macaulay Culkin Returns as Kevin McCallister in McDonalds' 'Home Alone' Christmas Ad

Netflix confirmed in the spring that Quinn and El Moussa would be reentering the world of “Selling Sunset.” El Moussa’s departure had been amicable, and she appeared occasionally on the show after she left. Quinn, however, went on Alex Cooper’s podcast “Call Her Daddy” in 2022 while promoting her book “How To Be A Boss B*tch,” and blasted DiVello, seemingly burning that bridge forever.

All appears to have been forgiven now, though. Since leaving the show, Quinn went through an acrimonious divorce and moved to Texas with her son. In the Season 10 announcement, DiVello shouted her out especially. “I’m most proud of the fact that the show has had such an impact on pop culture and has kept fans entertained this long,” he said. “Being a reality show, you never know what you’re going to get but thanks to the amazing cast members that have made this show so engaging we really haven’t slowed down! There have been so many stand-out moments over the past 10 seasons but I have to say this season, to me, really defines the show’s legacy. From the epic return of Christine (that only Christine could provide) to the introduction of four new cast members! The fact that they all so seamlessly worked together really proved that the show, the cast, and the real estate is as entertaining as ever.”

“Selling Sunset” is produced by Done and Done Productions and Lionsgate, and in addition to DiVello, its executive producers are Skyler Wakil, Sundee Manusakis, Suzy Ratner, Megan Roger and Jason Oppenheim.
