Gibbs-White stars, Fofana struggles - player ratings for Forest v Chelsea

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Premier League highlights: Nottingham Forest 3-2 Chelsea

Morgan Gibbs-White scored twice as Nottingham Forest beat Chelsea at the City Ground for the first time since 1997.

Igor Jesus' brilliant volley opened the scoring after just five minutes, before Gibbs-White struck twice in the second half as Chelsea were consigned to a fourth defeat in five matches.

Forest ran out 3-2 winners despite Emmanuel Emegha twice pulling goals back.

Here's how I rated the players and how you rated the players.

Matz Sels: The Belgian goalkeeper could do little about Emegha's goals but did not need to make many saves. 6

Jair Cunha: Cunha made one brilliant last-ditch tackle in the second half as Chelsea pushed for an equaliser. 7

Nikola Milenkovic: The Serbian was solid in the middle of the back three but otherwise unremarkable. 7

Murillo: The Brazilian centre-back made a host of important tackles and no-nonsense clearances throughout. He set the tone for a physical display and had a powerful volley saved. 7

Daniel Munoz: A typically energetic presence on the right, he also set up the opening goal. 7

James McAtee: The midfielder was good in possession but his shots lacked threat. His set-piece delivery was also poor. He did, however, win a few fouls for his team to take the pressure off. 7

Xaver Schlager: The combative midfielder set the tone early by winning the ball back in Chelsea's half on multiple occasions. He screened the backline well as Chelsea pushed for an equaliser. His wonderful pass through the eye of the needle created Forest's third. 9

Neco Williams: The Welshman battled hard with Pedro Neto in a tactical contest that ended with both players emerging with credit. 7

Igor Jesus: His sensational first-time volley opened the scoring just five minutes into the match, while the rest of his play was neat and tidy. 8

Morgan Gibbs-White: The forward scored his first goal in 11 games against Chelsea from the penalty spot in the second half. He took that chance and then another when played through by Schlager. 8

Liam Delap: The former Chelsea striker proved his worth to Forest by leading the line brilliantly, carrying a threat, looking dangerous in transition and ultimately winning a penalty for his side. 8

Subs not used: John Victor, Netz, Diomande, Yates, Hudson-Odoi, Kalimuendo

Chelsea have now lost four of their past five games

Emiliano Martinez: The Argentine goalkeeper was sent the wrong way from the spot and could not have saved Jesus' volley but made a couple of other saves. 6

Josh Acheampong: The young defender played within himself at the City Ground. 5

Wesley Fofana: The Frenchman was booed by his own supporters and told he is "not welcome here". It was a tough end to a horrible week which only got worse as he gave away the penalty having first been caught under a long ball. He was substituted in the 56th minute while also on a yellow card. 3

Moises Caicedo: A shock selection to start at centre-back for first time in his 210 games in English football, the Ecuadorian looked out of sorts with possession and in his positioning. He failed to cover for Fofana before he gave away the penalty. 5

Pep Chavarria: The Spanish full-back was an anonymous presence but did not make any obvious mistakes. 5

Romeo Lavia: The Belgian recovered from a poor touch early on to set the tempo for possession domination and his passes had real purpose. 7

Jordan Henderson: The 36-year-old was one of the weaker Chelsea performers as he filled in for now-defender Caicedo. 5

Pedro Neto: The Portuguese winger showed impressive work-rate competing with Williams, while threatened with his running in behind. His second-half shot was blocked but led to Chelsea's first goal, while his cross set up his side's second. 7

Cole Palmer: The forward was creative and highly involved for Chelsea but could not deliver that moment. It owed more to his team-mates not performing than himself. 6

Morgan Rogers: Rogers squandered multiple chances, including a huge opportunity to equalise from a corner midway through the second half. 4

Danny Welbeck: The 35-year-old striker had absolutely no service but was also completely anonymous in the first half and was taken off at the interval. 4

Emmanuel Emegha (on 45, Welbeck): An outstanding second-half display, with a brilliant touch and shot to pull one after the ball deflected into his path. He headed in a second at the far post. It raises the question about why he did not start having also scored against Leeds last weekend. 8

Levi Colwill (on 56, Fofana): The England international will be frustrated to have been dropped for a midfielder but was forced on amid Fofana's struggles and did not have much chance to shine. 5

Subs not used: Penders, Gusto, Barco, Nicoll-Jazuli, Estevao
