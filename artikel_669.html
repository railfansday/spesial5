Actor Awards Predictions: Andrew Garfield, Inde Navarrette and ‘The Odyssey’ Lead an Unsettled Film Race as ‘Widow’s Bay’ and HBO Max Could Rule TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Variety Awards Circuit section is the home for all awards news and related content throughout the year, featuring the following: the official predictions for the upcoming Oscars, Emmys, Grammys and Tony Awards ceremonies, curated by Variety chief awards editor Clayton Davis. The prediction pages reflect the current standings in the race and do not reflect personal preferences for any individual contender. As other formal (and informal) polls suggest, competitions are fluid and subject to change based on buzz and events. Predictions are updated every Thursday.

2027 Actor Awards Predictions Commentary All Categories (Updated: Nov. 21, 2026): Seven. That’s how many nominations “The Odyssey” is projected to collect, which would tie the all-time record for a film at the Actor Awards, going back to the SAG Awards days. That record is only a year old, when “One Battle After Another” earned a record-breaking seven nods last season. Christopher Nolan’s epic is projected to land: cast ensemble, stunt ensemble, Matt Damon in lead and four supporting slots split between John Leguizamo, Robert Pattinson, Anne Hathaway and Samantha Morton.

Andrew Garfield’s turn as Sam Altman is the early favorite for “Artificial” that could bring him his first trophy. Breakout Inde Navarrette would be the season’s most unlikely lead actress nominee and maybe its most deserved with her possessed girlfriend in “Obsession” carrying a microbudget horror film to the top of Focus Features’ box office history. If there’s any place that can truly challenge Julianne Moore’s remarkable turn in “The Debut,” it’s here.

And noteworthy reminder, Amanda Seyfried is not eligible here for “You Can See Everything.”

On the TV side, HBO Max takes 13 of the 45 projected slots and “The Pitt” returns as the reigning drama ensemble champ. Last season, Noah Wyle made history by winning all five major acting awards in one season for his role as Dr. Robby, and he could do it again. But the most-nominated series isn’t predicted to be the medical drama. It’s “Widow’s Bay,” Apple TV’s comedy, with five: ensemble, stunts, O’Flynn and a potential head-to-head in lead comedy between Matthew Rhys and Stephen Root. That matches the total from last year, when “The Studio” led TV with 5 noms. “DTF St. Louis” and “The Diplomat” follow with three each, and the drama actress race may be the one to watch. Keri Russell, who earned the honor for Best Actress in a Drama Series for “The Diplomat” last year, is back in the lineup, but she’ll have to fight off newcomer Emmy Rossum (“Furious”) to take her title.

Actor nominations will be announced Wednesday, Jan. 13, 2027.
