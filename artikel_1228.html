Ronaldo reveals why he left Barcelona for Inter in 1997

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Ronaldo has revealed that money was the main reason he left Barcelona for Inter in 1997, with the Nerazzurri offering him four times his salary at the Camp Nou.

The Brazilian legend was speaking on the Stick to Football podcast with Gary Neville and Jamie Carragher, in a special episode released today on Disney+.

Ronaldo spent just one season at Barcelona after joining from PSV, but it was a spectacular one. He scored goal after goal in La Liga and the Cup Winners’ Cup before Inter paid his release clause to bring him to Serie A.

“Barça bought me for 20 million, and I received a good chunk of that. There, I was earning two million a year, and Inter offered me eight, so I decided to go,” he explained.

Ronaldo said he was still angry about the 1996 Ballon d’Or, when he was voted in second place behind Matthias Sammer.

“I lost it to Sammer, who was a defender. I was angry because I’d played fantastically with Barcelona and scored so many goals,” he said.

He made up for it in his first season in Milan, winning the Ballon d’Or in 1997 as an Inter player. He won it again in 2002.

Il Fenomeno also suggested modern forwards don’t know how lucky they are.

“Today’s strikers have an easier life than we did. I’d have liked to have the same protection. We suffered so much. Our generation was full of killers, and nobody protected us,” he said with a laugh.

Ronaldo was less upbeat about the Brazil national team. He admitted Argentina’s fighting spirit is “painful” to watch for Brazilians.

“I think we’re copying European players. Before, there was always very high confidence in our country based purely on our talent. But Europeans today are stronger, faster, more organised, and play better,” he said. “Brazil aren’t strong enough to win the World Cup. We don’t have a strong team, we’re not united.”
