Opinion: Sensational English left-back is now ready to join Liverpool, he just showed it

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

He's ready to join Liverpool; his performance just confirmed it.

Most fans love glory. The sheer passion and unbridled joy of your team lifting a trophy - nothing ever compares.

But victory tastes even sweeter when you've had to grind through the difficult times in order to achieve that success.

Sometimes, the journey towards glory is the best part. The good old days under Jurgen Klopp were the beginning. Those first successes and milestones.

Lifting the Premier League and the Champions League, after so much defeat and disappointment happened along the way.

Now, Andoni Iraola is trying to do something similar. Expectations are high, but people should also remember how long it took for Klopp to get to the pinnacle of the sport again.

Andoni Iraola's problem in the full-back departmentOne of the biggest issues Iraola is facing early on in his Liverpool career is the lack of depth at his disposal at full-back. He doesn't really have a lot of recognised full-backs, and the ones he does (mostly) don't really suit his style. For example, Jeremie Frimpong just cannot be relied upon defensively when playing that role. But Ronald Araujo while great isn't necessarily a good player going forward. On the other side Kostas Tsimikas is clearly out of form and lacking in confidence. He has been a great servant for Liverpool but he isn't quite at the level the team needs him to be at right now. Milos Kerkez has been in and out of form but he is still young and Iraola already knows how to get the best of him so he can still be valuable for Liverpool.

Archie Brown confirms he's ready to join LiverpoolLiverpool are searching the market for defensive solutions. One of them, according to TEAMtalk, who the Reds are monitoring is Archie Brown. The English left-back has three goal contributions in four matches in the Champions League this season and he does look like an interesting profile. He looked impressive coming up against Victor Munoz in the Champions League tie between the two sides. His performance confirmed he would absolutely be ready to join the Reds. Brown scored and registered an assist in a terrific performance. He also looked compose on the ball, making seven passes into the final third. At 24-years-old he looks like a perfect all-round left-back. Someone who is ready to join the Reds and provide competition for Kerkez's spot. Someone who also very much suits Iraola's philosophy.
