'The Gilded Age' Just Made Larian's Love Story More Complicated Than Ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Editor's note: The below contains spoilers for The Gilded Age Season 4 Episode 4.

The Gilded Age Season 4 has already delivered more than a few surprises, ranging from the identity of George's (Morgan Spector) shooter to Jack's (Ben Ahlers) new romance. There are plenty of storylines to watch, but with the season hitting its halfway point, one reveal about Marian (Louisa Jacobson) changes everything for her future. Throughout the series, Marian's romance with Larry (Harry Richardson) has had its ups and downs; even once they got engaged in Season 3, things didn't go smoothly, with the two ultimately deciding to call things off despite lingering feelings.

Season 4's latest episode makes Marian and Larry's romance more complicated than ever when Marian realizes she could be pregnant with Larry's child. Following their night together in the season premiere, fans speculated about the possibility of a pregnancy occurring, but the confirmation is still a shock that will impact their potential future. Given the time period, this twist is especially big, and it will permanently alter Marian's life, however she chooses to proceed. With The Gilded Age now renewed for Season 5, it's safe to say fans will get to see the fallout — however it plays out for the remainder of Season 4 and beyond.

The Gilded Age's latest reveal comes at the end of Episode 4 when, after a few nosebleeds, Marian's friend at the Settlement House, Virginia Saville (Elizabeth Marvel), suggests it could be related to menstruation, prompting Marian to realize how long it's been since she had her last period. This pregnancy is shocking for many reasons, first and foremost being the potential danger to her reputation. After her near-elopement with Mr. Raikes (Thomas Cocquerel) in Season 1, Marian has taken care to protect what standing she has in society. Her caution partly contributed to her breakup with Larry last season, but this new turn of events will be even more challenging to keep under wraps.

The Gilded Age has touched on similar scandals before, most notably with Mrs. Chamberlain (Jeanne Tripplehorn), who remains an outcast in society decades later. Though the two cases aren't the same, this storyline illustrates how Marian may be treated if or when the truth comes out. Her aunt Agnes (Christine Baranski) will undoubtedly disapprove, putting Marian in a difficult spot at home. Although Agnes continues to evolve in her views, she has previously shown a willingness to distance herself from scandal, most recently including her initial reaction to Aurora Fane's (Kelli O'Hara) divorce. Unless she can hide this pregnancy, Marian is headed for social ruin and ostracization — but there may be a way to salvage the situation.

While Season 4's storyline is a major twist for Marian, it could end the will-they-won't-they between her and Larry, which would be welcome, while also helping her conceal that she became pregnant beforehand. Although the show's most recent episode ends before she has time to consider her options, a wedding for Larian isn't out of the question. Larry has already offered to marry her, but his unfortunate phrasing turned Marian off. She didn't want their night together to be "an excuse" to get married, but her unexpected pregnancy is just that storyline with higher stakes.

Though marrying Larry is the only legitimate act that could save her from ruin in society's eyes, Season 4 has emphasized how complicated their current relationship is, making Marian's choice much less clear. She could choose to remove herself from society to give birth or give the child away, neither of which is appealing. It's somewhat ironic that Marian seems to be the character with a baby on the way, while Gladys (Taissa Farmiga) tragically cannot have more children, and Enid (Kelley Curran), despite Agnes' hints at the importance of a van Rhijn heir, isn't pregnant either. For now, Marian's pregnancy changes everything for her storyline on The Gilded Age, especially since she only has so long to decide what her future will look like — and whether it will involve Larry, too.
