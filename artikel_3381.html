The Spy Thriller That Inspired ‘Reacher’ Officially Rules Out 2026 Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

2026 has been a big year for Alan Ritchson with new projects scattered across various streaming services, but the biggest project of them all has been the fourth season of Reacher. Ritchson first suited up as Lee Child's iconic hero in the first season of the Prime Video series in 2022, which was also the same year that the first season of The Terminal List (starring Chris Pratt) was released.

However, while Prime Video only just released the second season of The Terminal List, the studio has put out four full seasons of Reacher with Seasons 5 and 6 already in the works. Ritchson even returned as Jack Reacher to star in the first Reacher spin-off, Neagley (starring Maria Sten), which has also been renewed for a second season by Prime Video.

Reacher has become so popular in the last few years that it has forced other streaming services like Netflix to search for suitable alternatives to the show for its own audience. Peacock has settled on The Day of the Jackal as its official answer to Reacher, but the shows are more intertwined than you might realize.

The Day of the Jackal is based on the hit spy novel of the same name by Frederick Forsyth, and Lee Child has confirmed that Forsyth's writings helped inspire him when he was writing the original Jack Reacher series. Peacock aired the first season of The Day of the Jackal back at the end of 2024, and the show was also picked up for a second season. Despite an initial wave of optimism, Peacock has already confirmed that The Day of the Jackal Season 2 will not premiere until 2027.

The first season of The Day of the Jackal, which stars Eddie Redmayne (Fantastic Beasts) opposite Lashana Lynch (Captain Marvel), follows the Jackal, an elusive assassin who makes his living carrying out dangerous jobs for the highest bidder. However, he finally meets his match with a ruthless British intelligence officer who is determined to track him down, no matter the cost. The first season of The Day of the Jackal was written and showrun by Ronan Bennett (MobLand), who is stepping down to a producing-only role in Season 2. David Harrower will write and showrun The Day of the Jackal Season 2.
