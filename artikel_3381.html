Elijah Wood Says “Muscle Memory” Kicked In Playing Frodo On ‘The Hunt For Gollum’ Set

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Elijah Wood told Deadline tonight that playing Frodo again after more than two decades was “beautiful and surreal and it felt like no time had passed.”

Wood reprised the role earlier this year on director Andy Serkis’ new Middle-earth set tale, The Hunt For Gollum.

The actor has spoken in anticipation of returning to the part, but these may be his first public observations after having done so.

Serkis confirmed yesterday that principal photography for the film has now wrapped.

The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum reunites beloved figures from across Middle-earth, including Ian McKellen as Gandalf, Wood as Frodo and Lee Pace as Thranduil. New actors announced for the film include Anya Taylor-Joy, Kate Winslet, Jamie Dornan and Leo Woodall. Serkis directs and returns as Gollum in the film. Watch on Deadline

“I think it really is a creative ‘getting the band back together.” Wood told Screen Rant before production in January. “It’s very much that core original group getting back together to tell this story that will feel like a really fun exploration of this character that we all love so much.”

Wood spoke tonight about being on set in his costume and hobbit feet again.

“It felt like stepping back into time. You know, looking down…having this sort of muscle memory that I’ve seen these feet this way. I know this wardrobe. And that feeling is so wild. It’s so transportive. Suddenly 27 years are gone and you’re back in it again.”

Elijah Wood on returning as Frodo for ‘The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt For Gollum’ pic.twitter.com/srVYx98Ud4

The Hunt for Gollum follows Aragorn’s perilous quest to capture Gollum before the creature can reveal the One Ring’s location to Sauron. The story, much of it developed from Tolkien’s footnotes, is set between the events of The Hobbit and The Fellowship of the Ring.

Last month, Serkis posted a video slideshow from the set. One of the final images shows what looks to be Wood as Frodo entering Bilbo’s hobbit house. You can watch it below.

Warner Bros. Pictures will release The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum worldwide on December 17, 2027.
