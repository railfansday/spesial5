Texas Man Who Beheaded 3 Kids Over 'Demonic Possession' Set to Be Executed

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

John Allen Rubio is set to be executed for the murders of his 3 children, whose decapitated bodies were discovered inside the family's Texas apartment.

In an on-camera interview with KRGV, Rubio claimed he had been witnessing bizarre things in the days leading up to the murders, which convinced him someone was practicing witchcraft against his family.

But things took an even darker turn on March 11, 2003, when Rubio claimed his 3-year-old daughter Julissa began acting strangely.

According to Rubio, Julissa started claiming she was his grandmother ... something he said was particularly disturbing because he'd previously dreamed his daughter was possessed by his grandmother.
