Get the BILL Divvy Card if several people spend for your business and you want budgets, virtual cards, and receipt tracking without paying for software. Don’t get it for the rewards. Its points are worth about half a cent each, and you lose a month’s points if you charge less than 30% of your credit line that month.

It’s a charge card, so there’s no interest, and the full balance comes due at the end of each billing cycle, which you set to weekly, twice a month, or monthly. Registered businesses with an employer identification number and a U.S. business bank account can apply, and the card comes with Spend & Expense, BILL’s software for budgets, employee and virtual cards, receipts, and accounting sync.

It’s worth getting for a business with several people spending, since the software costs nothing, and weekly billing gets the most from its points.
