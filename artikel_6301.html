I Found $1.70 Burritos At Denver Airport. Thank You, Taco Bell.

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

While I usually just make a beeline for the Capital One Lounge while in Denver International Airport, I recently noticed that Taco Bell is back…now airside instead of landside..and from a cost perspective, probably the best deal for food in DEN.

If you’ve flown from Denver for years, you might remember that there used to be a Taco Bell in the Jeppesen Terminal in the food court above the security checkpoint. At some point that closed while the airport was refurbishing that area and did not re-open.

But was I (very pleasantly) surprised to see a Taco Bell Cantina in Concourse A (near Gates A47 and A49) during a recent trip through DEN. I had no idea!

Even better? The prices were street pricing, not airport pricing. $1.70 for a burrito? What a lovely way to get your junk food fix without breaking the bank.

And street pricing not ubiquitous in the airport. I had a salad with some pulled brisket at Brothers BBQ and it was almost $20….

I also want to note that I tried the more “local” option, called Denver Street Eats, located in The B Gates, which offers street tacos. A steak taco was $6.25. That may seem reasonable at first glance, but it was just a small flour tortilla with very little to it: a bit of steak, a couple spinach leaves, and a few tiny slices of picked onions. It was good, but sorry, Taco Bell is better.

So if you’re looking for an affordable meal at Denver Airport, Taco Bell is your best bet.

And for new readers, yes, I have a strong affinity for Taco Bell…wherever I am in the world. Check out the archive.
