Whether it’s competitive head-to-head matches or family game night, air hockey tables are an endlessly replayable way to bring the fun of the arcade and the ice rink into your home. Just like pool tables and ping-pong tables, air hockey tables come in several sizes and styles to suit your space and aesthetic.

A basic tabletop air hockey table runs under $100, though for an upgraded model with a frame and legs, expect to pay $300 to $500. For the most premium air hockey tables, you’re looking at what’s essentially a significant piece of furniture that typically costs $750 and up. High-quality tables often have wood or metal frames, LED lighting, and powerful fan motors for smoother gameplay.

While air hockey tables miniaturize the sport, buying one isn’t a small decision. Before you drop some change on a table, check out our list of the best air hockey tables we recommend, plus all the key features to look out for as you shop.

Air hockey table sizes range from full regulation length (84 inches), which you see at an arcade, to miniature tabletop options that are not much bigger than a board game. Between the two of them are a large number of mid-sized choices that fit comfortably into game rooms without monopolizing the entire space. If you plan on building a room around your air hockey table, we recommend regulation size. Otherwise, measure out the space you want to place the table in and buy one with dimensions that fit it.

There’s a big difference in the price and quality of the tables we selected below depending on their use case. For casual fun, you can get away with choosing a smaller, less expensive table. Tabletop options that can be set up and stored when not in use are also a great option for the less competitive air hockey fan.

More serious players should stick with larger options, which have powerful motors and well-placed fans that create a consistent feel across the entire playing surface. They should also look for a table with wide, sturdy legs that keep it in place even as you move and dive for the puck.

Many air hockey tables come with more than just a rink and pucks.
