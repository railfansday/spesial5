FAA's Nationwide Ground Stop Causes Cross-Country Chaos, Pilot Preaches Calm

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Federal Aviation Administration has ordered all flights on the ground in the United States to stay there ... issuing a nationwide ground stop due to a communication issue -- and it's leaving many Americans feeling confused and scared.

The federal agency issued the ground stop Friday afternoon ... stating in a press release that it made the decision "following communication issues impacting multiple Air Route Traffic Control Centers." Technicians for the FAA are investigating the issue.

Videos show the chaos and confusion all over the country ... including one plane at Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport heading to Tampa, Florida, and hearing from their pilot, it could take up to 7 hours for the ground stop to be lifted.

Another person shared that all passengers' luggage was being removed from the plane ... even though they expected only a two-hour delay.

Denver Airport says the ground stop could last up to 6.5 hours.
