Pokémon Black & White Return With Impressive Single-Screen Remaster

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

There have been all sorts of brilliant Pokémon games over the years, and while everyone has their own favorites, it's widely agreed upon that Black and White are some of the best releases in the series to date. In fact, the DS/3DS era as a whole was just a magical time for franchise releases more generally, and they remain popular games for fans to play in 2026.

That movement has only been furthered by the rise of PC emulation, which has become easier and considerably more competent in recent years thanks to rapid advances in technology. One of the main issues with emulating DS games is the dual screens they were originally designed for, which don't lend themselves particularly well to modern, single-monitor setups.

Happily, it seems as though those concerns are about to entirely be a thing of the past, as a new and ingenious piece of tech finally solves the issue with aplomb.

As first reported by Retro Dodo, new software has been made publically available that converts dual-screen Pokémon games into a single-screen 60FPS output. Known as Pokémon FullDex, it's available as a free download from creator mamesj's GitHub, and it already works for a ton of iconic games from the DS era.

The description for the project reads as follows:

"All your favourite Pokémon games on a PC (Windows and Linux), a Mac, a Steam Deck or other handheld PC, an Android phone, an iPhone or iPad, or a modded Nintendo Switch: one screen, widescreen, 60 fps, up to 4K on a PC and 720p on the Switch. No more squinting at two tiny stacked DS screens."

The videos posted to the page are also hugely impressive, and you'd never really know that the games weren't designed to run like that in the first place. It works far beyond Pokémon Black and White as well, with more titles on the way all the time. The full list as it stands includes:

DS: Black 2, White 2, Black, White, HeartGold, SoulSilver, Platinum, Diamond and Pearl.

GBA: Emerald, Ruby, Sapphire, FireRed, LeafGreen and Ultra Violet.

3DS (beta): Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire on Windows, Android and Switch; X, Y, Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon coming soon.

It's a hugely impressive, lightweight piece of tech, and it comes with a bunch of mod-cons like support for RetroAchievements and cheats. The only potential drawback here is that it seems as though the project was heavily assisted by Claude AI, and that comes with its own set of risks, as well as potential moral quandaries for those of us who want to push back against the increased use of AI in gaming.

As for Pokémon fans more generally, the 30th anniversary of the franchise has now largely drawn to a close, and things are expected to go a little quiet for the time being. The first games of the tenth generation (Winds and Waves) are set to be the next big stopping point on that journey, with more news expected on that in the first half of next year.
