New York Dem addresses bizarre 'lizard people' claims after viral clip behind Mamdani

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A clip showing New York Democratic Assemblyman Jordan J.G. Wright making an unusual eye movement while standing behind Zohran Mamdani during a press conference has sparked wild "lizard people" conspiracy theories online. (Credit: Mayor Mamdani)

A Democratic New York City assemblyman addressed bizarre "lizard people" conspiracy theories after a viral video appeared to show his eyes rolling backward while standing behind Mayor Zohran Mamdani during a press conference.

Jordan J.G. Wright was spotted during a press conference behind Mamdani on Friday, when the city announced plans to eventually shut down Rikers Island.

As Mamdani spoke, Wright appeared to flash a slight grin before blinking and rolling his eyes in a circular motion toward the back of his head, prompting a wave of bizarre speculation online.

"I have no idea what was going on in that video. I’m really not sure," Wright told the New York Post in a statement.

He added that his work as an assemblyman speaks to his human character, pointing to a project he championed in Harlem that serves as the city's first community-based center dedicated to climate action. The five-story hub provides residents with environmental education and advocacy.

"Obviously I’m a human being, and my humanity can be seen through my legislation like the $4 million for the WE ACT environmental justice center in Harlem," he said. "I can’t speak to any reptiles or aliens."

The video sent social media users into a frenzy over the weekend, reigniting a decades-old conspiracy theory that extraterrestrial reptilians secretly control the Earth by disguising themselves as world leaders and celebrities to manipulate society.

"This is how lizards blink… I am pretty sure there is full-blown ‘Men in Black’ aliens walking among us disguised as human beings, and they are hiding in plain sight," one social media user said, according to the Post.

Another X user suggested the clip showed that aliens were becoming increasingly brazen about revealing themselves to the public.

"The ‘reptile’ is standing right behind Mamdani, as he announces a full scale-release of dangerous prisoners from NYC jails," the X user said.

"The aliens are not even bothering to hide themselves anymore. They intend to create so much chaos on Earth, that we will be unable to defend ourselves," the user added.

The discussion also spilled onto Reddit, where some users questioned whether Wright was human.

"Yes, the guy in the back is a reptilian, 100%" the Reddit user said.

Wright, the son of former Assemblyman Keith L.T. Wright and grandson of former New York Supreme Court Judge Bruce Wright, represents New York's 70th Assembly District in Harlem.

He was reelected without opposition in November after defeating Democratic socialist Conrad Blackburn in the Democratic primary in June. His legislative priorities include affordable housing, health care access and public safety, according to his profile.

A lifelong New Yorker and Central Harlem resident, Wright graduated from Syracuse University in 2017 with a degree in psychology.

Wright stood behind Mamdani on Friday as the mayor announced plans to cut New York City's jail population by 2,500 inmates over the next five years and close the city’s largest jail by 2032.

The remaining inmates, estimated at 4,100 detainees, will be moved to four new, smaller borough-based jails opening in Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens and the Bronx.
