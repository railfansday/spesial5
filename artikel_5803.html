5,387 Dead in Outbreak; Cheese Recall Expands; Breast Cancer Chemo May Speed Aging

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

— Health news and commentary gathered by MedPage Today staff

Note that some links may require registration or subscription.

According to the latest data, 11,042 confirmed cases have been recorded in Congo's record-breaking Ebola outbreak, including 5,387 deaths. (AP)

Ahead of a formal evaluation on whether the U.S. will retain its measles elimination status, scientists told the New York Times that genetic data indicate the virus is once again endemic in the U.S.

CDC Director Erica Schwartz , MD, MPH, said her agency will not fundamentally change its response to measles outbreaks if the U.S. loses its elimination status. (Reuters via AOL)

A tool developed by Johns Hopkins University researchers estimated that the roughly 5,100 measles cases in the U.S. this year generated nearly $83 million in economic costs. (CNBC)

Family Farmstead Dairy expanded its recall to include all its cheddar cheese products due to potential Listeria contamination, according an FDA notice.

At the American Medical Association's (AMA) interim meeting, members adopted a policy designating moral injury as a significant threat to physicians and medical students.

The AMA also adopted policies to strengthen its opposition to payer downcoding practices, to support medication deprescribing, and to urge the federal government to rescind its newly relaxed rules on unapproved peptide compounding.

"I don't think anything is going to get in our way now," said Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr., who accused Senate Republicans of blocking his "Make America Healthy Again" agenda. Meanwhile, Democrats are planning investigations into Kennedy's actions. (Washington Post)

Democrats pressed drug manufacturers to immediately release complete, unredacted copies of their drug pricing deals with the Trump administration, a source familiar with the matter said. (The Hill)

Support for the death penalty for convicted murderers dropped to 49% in a Gallup poll, dipping below a majority for the first time since 1971.

In a controversial vote, Californians approved $8.4 billion in state bonds for immunology research. (CBS News)

The American Society of Addiction Medicine released its first clinical guidance for managing medetomidine intoxication and withdrawal.

Breast cancer chemotherapy may accelerate biological aging and raise mortality risk, a modeling study suggested. (JAMA Network Open)

Air ambulance company PHI Health and other health providers are suing employers to pay arbitration awards for their employees' out-of-network bills. (STAT)

Emergency medicine residents in private equity-backed programs had lower rates of passing the written qualifying board exam in a cross-sectional study, but the gap disappeared after adjusting for program characteristic differences. (JAMA Network Open)

The Trump administration refused to pay more than $112 million in Medicaid reimbursements for Minnesota, citing administrative documentation deficiencies as a reason to reject the claims. (MPR News)

The administration also announced $10 billion in awards for the Rural Health Transformation Program's second year.

Google Cloud said it launched Gemini for healthcare.
