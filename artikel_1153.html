The assist of the season! Remarkable new footage shows moment Bruno Fernandes created the divot HIMSELF before scoring one of the most bizarre goals EVER in Man United's chaotic draw with Sporting Lisbon

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Published: 18:33 EST, 25 November 2026 | Updated: 12:51 EST, 26 November 2026

Footage appears to show Bruno Fernandes created the divot in the turf that led to his fortunate second goal against Sporting Lisbon.

The Manchester United captain scored a bizarre goal against his former side on Wednesday night, with his long-range shot taking a fortuitous bobble off the Estadio Jose Alvalade pitch to leave goalkeeper Kaique Pereira helpless.

While fingers were initially pointed at the ground staff, footage shows the divot was actually created by Fernandes himself when he planted his boot into the turf while pressing high up the pitch moments earlier.

Reminiscent of former Liverpool forward Stan Collymore's famous bobbling goal past Blackburn Rovers goalkeeper Tim Flowers in 1996, Fernandes' speculative effort would undoubtedly have been easily gathered by Pereira had it not struck the bump in the turf.

The goal summed up another chaotic evening for United, in which goalkeeping calamities, controversial refereeing calls, three penalties and a red card resulted in a 2-2 draw and left them 19th in the Champions League standings.

United trailed at the break after goalkeeper Senne Lammens saved one penalty before conceding another 75 seconds later that was converted by Luis Suarez after the Belgian's reckless challenge on Max Araujo.

The visitors were left furious when Amad Diallo saw a first-half leveller ruled out after referee Szymon Marciniak blew his whistle too early for VAR to intervene, but United turned the game around shortly after the restart.

Lisandro Martinez, who had given away the first penalty, won one at the other end that Fernandes converted before the former Sporting star fired in the long-range effort that bounced over Pereira and into the net.

More drama followed as Lammens flapped at a cross under pressure from Suarez, allowing the striker to score, before Patrick Dorgu was adjudged to have put undue pressure on Pereira when forcing an own goal from the goalkeeper.

United were left frustrated by what they viewed as a lack of consistency after Marciniak was sent to the pitchside monitor to review the second decision, and their mood darkened further when Youri Tielemans was sent off for a second booking late on.

Former United player Ashley Young claimed that 2022 World Cup referee Marciniak's decision-making during the clash was a 'disgrace'.

Young told BBC Radio 5 Live: 'The referee’s performance was just simply wow. The decisions that he gave.

'Youri Tielemans getting booked to eventually getting sent off later because you booked him when it should never have been a booking and it should have been an equalising goal.

'The third goal that should have stood because he’s saying Patrick Dorgu fouled the keeper. The keeper comes out and flaps at it before he is even touched. How that has gone to VAR is beyond me.

'His decision-making is nothing more than a disgrace.'

Young’s former United team-mate Rio Ferdinand echoed that frustration on X.

Writing alongside a pain image of himself, he said with two emojis indicating angry swearing: 'REFEREE. What have I just witnessed tonight!

'Regardless – individual mistakes again proving costly. Positives to take from tonight, recharge the batteries!'

In what is proving to be an eventful first week in charge, Pochettino was asked to provide his assessment of the game. The Argentine said: 'It depends which way you prefer (to analyse the game). So proud of the players. It was a very tough game. I like the team for the 90 minutes, the personality, the character.

'We showed the way we want to play. We've only been working together one week.'

But, he added: 'Always we need to concentrate more and focus more. Try to recover better. We start to fix. With a performance like today it's the best medicine to prepare the next game.'

United have little time to take stock before their next outing, with Brentford visiting Old Trafford on Saturday.
