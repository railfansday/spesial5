Empower Personal Wealth comes from Empower, the retirement company that bought Personal Capital, not the cash advance app that dropped the Empower name for Tilt. Its dashboard is free for anyone who wants every account in one view, and its advice is what Empower sells to the people who sign up.

Investors with accounts at several firms get their net worth, fund fees, and retirement plan in one free view here, and never have to pay for advice to keep it. Savers who only want a portfolio managed pay less at a robo-advisor such as Wealthfront, whose software manages the portfolio for 0.25%, $250 a year on $100,000, against $890 at Empower, an extra $640 for an advisor and planning.
