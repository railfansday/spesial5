Use Priceline when any hotel at a chosen star rating and neighborhood will do, since its deepest discounts, called Express Deals, name the hotel only after you pay. Those rooms usually can’t be canceled or refunded, so if your plans might change, pay more for a named room you can cancel.

Priceline, part of Booking Holdings, sells hotel stays, rental cars, flights, and vacation packages, either at regular rates or as deals that name the hotel, rental company, or airline only after you pay. It suits travelers who put price ahead of a particular hotel or airline.

Travelers who’ll take any well-rated hotel in the neighborhood they pick get Priceline’s deepest discounts, and bigger VIP discounts once they’ve completed two trips. Travelers whose plans could change, or who collect hotel points, should skip Express Deals for a named, refundable room booked direct with the hotel.
