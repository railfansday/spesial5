Arizona Republican holds House seat, surviving re-election scare

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Chief congressional correspondent Chad Pergram joins 'Fox Report’ to discuss how the newest members of Congress descended on Capitol Hill today. Pergram says new members outlined their legislative priorities such as agricultural economy concerns and fighting political corruption.

Rep. Eli Crane, R-Ariz., narrowly won re-election to a second term, defeating Democratic challenger Jonathan Nez in a tight rematch, The Associated Press projected Monday.

Crane, a former Navy SEAL combat veteran, previously defeated Nez, a former president of the Navajo Nation, in the 2024 general election.

Crane and Nez were separated by 3,834 votes at the time of the AP race call. The Fox News Power Rankings had rated the seat Likely R in the lead-up to Election Day.

Nez has not yet conceded the race. His most recent social media post, from Nov. 5, said that counting every ballot "takes time" in the large district.

"I'm going to keep fighting against the status quo in Congress," Crane wrote. "Both parties have been driving this country into the ground for decades, and the American people are sick of it."
