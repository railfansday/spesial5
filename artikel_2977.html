How Much Is Nvidia Stock Worth Now That Jensen Huang Is Back?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Nvidia stock soared more than 30% in one trading session.

By Adria Cimino – Nov 24, 2026 at 6:10PM EST

When most investors think of Nvidia (NVDA -5.86%), they think of Jensen Huang. That's why, when the famous chief executive officer stepped down and then came back to his post 11 days later, the stock reacted. Huang's departure resulted in declines, and his return resulted in significant gains -- the stock soared more than 30% in one trading session.

We all know that CEOs are important, but Huang's visionary role at Nvidia, as well as his prominence in the artificial intelligence (AI) space worldwide, make him a key player in this high-growth field. Without his leadership, Nvidia shareholders worried that the company might not continue to expand at the same pace and in the same direction.

So, clearly, investors celebrated Huang's return. Now let's consider how much Nvidia stock is worth with Huang back at the helm.

Jensen Huang. Image source: Getty Images.

First, though, let's take a look at the Nvidia story so far, including the recent turmoil. Nvidia has trailblazed in AI, with its early entry into the AI chip market and innovations that have kept it ahead of rivals. This has resulted in jaw-dropping earnings growth, and this deep involvement in AI has transformed Jensen Huang into an expert in the field. His knowledge and visibility about what's ahead have made him a valuable asset -- and one that investors weren't eager to replace.

That's why when he resigned following allegations of two affairs, the stock slid, and investors questioned the ability of the company to continue with the same momentum. Huang's reinstatement reassured investors, as we can see through the stock's performance. It's important to keep in mind, however, that Nvidia isn't out of the woods yet. The company is investigating Huang's relationship with a woman U.S. intelligence says is a spy for China. And lawmakers have called for Huang to testify on the matter -- that should unfold early next month. Meanwhile, press reports say that regulators have opened a probe into Nvidia's accounting practices.

All of this, as well as investors' growing caution regarding AI, may periodically weigh on the stock price. Tech giants aim to spend more than $700 billion on the infrastructure build-out this year, and investors worry that the revenue opportunity may fall short. This concern has represented a headwind for AI stocks in general since the start of this year.

So, now, let's consider how much Nvidia may be worth with Jensen Huang back in the leadership role. Analysts upgraded the stock following Huang's return, and most price targets landed considerably above $200, with one even at $270, according to CNBC. Nvidia stock closed at about $165 on Nov. 23, the day of Huang's reinstatement.

That said, price targets are generally for a 12-month period, so this doesn't mean Nvidia will reach these levels tomorrow. In fact, the stock might face volatility in the weeks and months to come, considering the investigations I mentioned above as well as the general concerns weighing on AI stocks.

Nvidia gained nearly $1 trillion in market cap in one trading session upon Huang's return, bringing it back to its level prior to the CEO's departure. But, as we can see in the chart below, Nvidia's market value has dropped considerably from its 2026 peak.

Since Nvidia's earnings continue to march higher and demand is strong, the stock does have room to run -- and that makes the price forecasts of more than $200 look attainable. It's important to remember, however, that although Nvidia may be worth more than $200 per share on fundamentals, the stock may not quickly climb to that level. As I mentioned above, a number of headwinds could impact stock performance and slow the company's path to the price targets.

But here's the good news: Nvidia's fundamentals as well as the overall AI demand picture offer us reason to be optimistic about the tech giant's value over the long term.
