Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Software margins climbed as the company pulled back from hardware resales and managed services.

Stem (STEM +6.10%) announced a formal review of strategic alternatives while shifting its business model to focus on high-margin software through its PowerTrack platform. Management reported record non-GAAP gross margins and adjusted EBITDA that exceeded the top of the full-year guidance range within the first nine months of the year. The company is deemphasizing managed services and battery hardware resale to reduce capital intensity and prioritize recurring revenue streams. Strategic actions during the quarter included the $10 million sale of Athena software intellectual property and the exit of legacy financing entities to eliminate $30 million in balance sheet liabilities.
