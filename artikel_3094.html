RB Cam Skattebo doubtful, but WR Malik Nabers has no injury designation

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Giants wide receiver Malik Nabers said he would be ready to go despite missing Thursday's practice with a knee injury. Indeed, the team's Friday injury report confirms that.

Nabers does not have a designation after returning to full participation on Friday.

He was not on the injury report Wednesday.

It's unknown whether Nabers' current knee issue is the same knee as the torn ACL he had in his right knee last season. Coach John Harbaugh called it "an in-season type thing."

"It's not an issue. He'll be full-go for the game," Harbaugh said.

Nabers leads the team with 48 receptions for 605 yards and two touchdowns.

Running back Cam Skattebo, though, is doubtful with a hamstring injury. He did not practice all week.

Skattebo leads the team with 98 carries for 418 yards and two touchdowns. He has added 11 catches for 76 yards.

The Giants signed running back Eric Gray to the practice squad as insurance earlier this week.

Wide receiver Charlie Jones (hand) is questionable. He has two catches for 66 yards.
