Colliers International Group Inc. (CIGI -4.48%) reported growth across its three global platforms of commercial real estate, engineering, and investment management despite increasing market volatility. Management reported that consolidated revenue rose 12% in local currency, supported by a 37% increase in the engineering segment and solid performance in capital markets. The company revised its full-year 2026 outlook to low-teens growth across key financial metrics, citing geopolitical tensions and interest rate volatility that slowed transaction velocity late in the third quarter. Management stated that the company remains focused on its long-term strategy of compounding value through a mix of organic growth and strategic acquisitions, noting that 70% of earnings are now derived from resilient, recurring service lines.
