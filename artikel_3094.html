Owlet: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 13, 2026, Owlet (OWLT -2.33%) announced a voluntary camera safety update and withdrew its full-year financial outlook. The safety action creates immediate revenue cuts, but long-term value hinges on core product demand and subscription growth.

Three takeaways from the call stand out for long-term investors.

Management pulled full-year financial guidance after initiating a voluntary safety update for its camera cord attachment, which requires accepting product returns from retail partners and shipping replacement parts.

The Owlet360 subscription service reached 142,000 paying users, providing recurring software revenue that expands customer lifetime value beyond initial hardware purchases.

By late September, Amazon removed competitors that lacked Food and Drug Administration (FDA) clearance, solidifying Owlet's regulatory position in vital-signs tracking.

Near-term revenue faces headwinds from camera cord replacements, but demand for the core Dream Sock monitor remains unaffected. Management is relying on expanding software features, expanding international distribution, and pricing discipline to protect long-term margins.

Owlet retains a distinct competitive position as the sole infant health monitor with FDA clearance.
