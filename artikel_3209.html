Elastic: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Agentic AI tools from AlertZero and Nightshift aim to automate security and reliability work, while the HoneyHive deal extends monitoring into AI applications themselves.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 20, 2026 at 3:35PM EST

On its second-quarter fiscal 2027 earnings call, held on Nov. 19, 2026, Elastic (ESTC -2.02%) reported total revenue of $496 million, an increase of 17% year over year. The company achieved sustainable GAAP operating profitability while continuing to adopt its enterprise software platform. Three takeaways from management explain where the business is heading.

Elastic accelerated its subscription momentum as clients turned to its search and security tools to counter cyber threats and manage enterprise data. High-value enterprise adoption expanded, with 40% of customers spending over $100,000 annually now utilizing Elastic for artificial intelligence workloads.

Management launched new agentic artificial intelligence offerings, including AlertZero and Nightshift, to automate cybersecurity and software reliability engineering tasks. The addition of HoneyHive extends visibility into language models, enabling developers to evaluate agent performance in production.

Operating leverage boosted non-GAAP operating margin to a record 22%, and Elastic reached sustainable GAAP operating profitability for the first time. Supported by cash generation, the board authorized a new $500 million share buyback program to return capital to shareholders.

Enterprise data demands are surging as artificial intelligence transformations take root across global operations. Elastic is expanding its addressable market in software reliability while maintaining pricing discipline and monitoring subscription renewal schedules throughout the second half of the fiscal year.

Platform flexibility across cloud and self-managed environments positions Elastic to capture market share from security incumbents and deepen enterprise commitments.
