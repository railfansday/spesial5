X finally gets six parental controls for teen accounts, but kids must consent

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Most social media platforms have long had parental controls to give oversight into the ways teens are using the service and to ensure they get appropriate content in their feeds. X has finally joined the party.

A parent or guardian now has control over six different aspects of the teen account, though the teenager does have to agree to permit it …

X’s Safety account made the announcement, saying a parent or guardian now has access to six options:

There are limits on the access granted to those supervising teen accounts.

Parents and guardians cannot read private messages, see searches or viewing history, post as their teen, or access the account.

Notably, X says both sides must agree before supervision is permitted. The arrangement ends automatically when the teenager hits their 18th birthday.
