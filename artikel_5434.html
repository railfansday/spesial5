Sandisk is completing its 8-for-1 stock split after Thursday's close

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The memory-chip maker announced the plan in October after its stock price rose following its February 2025 spinoff from Western Digital

Sandisk is scheduled to carry out an 8-for-1 split of its shares when trading ends Thursday, according to MarketWatch.

Once the split is completed, holders of Sandisk stock will own eight times as many shares, each priced at about one-eighth of the pre-split level, so the total value of their holdings won’t change.

The memory-chip company, which trades under the ticker SNDK, announced the split in October, according to MarketWatch. Sandisk cited a rise in its stock price since it separated from Western Digital $WDC in February 2025, MarketWatch said.
