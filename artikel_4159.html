GTA 6: Should You Shoot or Spare Felipe

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Adam Beck is a Canadian who has covered the gaming industry for over twenty years. He has a passion for RPGs, action, stealth and horror games.

Choice is a huge component in a lot of games, and it has always had an impact in Grand Theft Auto. In Grand Theft Auto 6, there are various missions and side quests where you will be able to not only play stealthily but be restrained in how you approach a situation. It isn’t always about guns blazing but also talking your way out of a situation.

For Felipe, he probably deserves what comes to him, but if you put away your itchy trigger finger, you will find that there is a greater reward.

Should you sell pure product or Tyrone’s cut batch in GTA 6? Compare the outcomes, risks, and rewards of each Dealer’s Choice option.

If you have gotten this far and don’t know what Felipe did to deserve to be shot or spared, then it’s best you turn back. This is a significant spoiler near the end of chapter 3 of Grand Theft Auto 6.

With that said, Felipe essentially bailed on the plan by Raul, resulting in the death of Nicole. Andrea especially isn’t too happy about it and gets information that Felipe is still in town and a general area of where you can look to find him.

He is located all the way up in the north in Millport, so drive or fly all the way up there and pay him a visit. When you get to a local bar, enter and start asking around for him. Eventually, he will come out of the back and immediately recognize you, initiating a chase. Chase him down only for Lucia to tackle him. Now it’s time to confront him.

Now that you have caught up to the backstabber who threw off the entire plan, you have to deal with him. You are able to question him, but in the end, he will just respond with requests to just get it over with.

Shooting Felipe will essentially get you nothing but the satisfaction of taking out someone who screwed you.

Now that you have cooled your head, there is another option for Felipe: sparing him. Depending on how you play the game, maybe this isn’t for you, but there is a benefit to letting him live.

Jason will tell him to get out of here and that killing him will change nothing. If you change your mind, he does walk away very slowly giving you a chance to reconsider. With that said, you do get rewarded.

After texting Andrea that you couldn’t find Felipe, Felipe will text you. He will thank you for letting him go and left you a bike at the old church by Lost Heaven Cemetery. In the end, you will get rewarded with a new set of wheels. Not bad for restraining yourself.
