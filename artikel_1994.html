How to watch Germany vs Netherlands: TV channel and live stream for Nations League today

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Nations League fates will be decided this evening as Germany and the Netherlands face off in a mouth-watering showdown between two European heavyweights.

Both teams are largely in control of their own destiny, though a draw would certainly suit the Dutch more as it would be enough to top Group A2 and secure home advantage in the quarter-final second leg - which will be played in March 2027.

Germany, now managed by former Liverpool and Borussia Dortmund boss Jurgen Klopp, can finish first but must beat the Netherlands and hope Greece fail to do so against a Serbia side that has lost all five matches thus far.

A point here for the Germans would leave qualification for the knockout phase hanging by a thread and they would be left relying on Serbia to beat Greece in Volos.

The reverse fixture between Germany and Netherlands finished all square in September as Cody Gakpo denied his former manager (Klopp) victory in his first game in charge of the national team with a 92nd minute equaliser in Amsterdam.

The Dutch have gone on to win three times since, including a 7-0 thumping of Greece on Friday night, but are without a win over Germany since September 2019.

Here’s everything you need to know about the Nations League, A2 clash...

Germany vs Netherlands is today, Monday, November 16, 2026, with kick-off at 7.45pm GMT.

The game will take place at the Olympiastadion, in Berlin.

TV channel and live stream: UK viewers can watch the game on Prime Video UK, who is the UK broadcaster for Nations League matches that do not involve England, Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland.

Each game costs £2.49, and coverage begins around 10 minutes before kick-off.
