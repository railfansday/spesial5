West Ham-Linked Rangers Giant Features In Spain Victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

West Ham United transfer target Ryan Naderi was handed a starting place for Czechia against Spain in Thursday night’s UEFA Nations League encounter, which ended in a 2-0 victory for the Spanish.

The 6ft 4in Rangers striker, who has recently been linked with a January move to West Ham, played 66 minutes before being substituted. Spain took the lead through an Álex Baena penalty in the 39th minute, with Marc Pubill adding a second deep into stoppage time.

There was another West Ham connection to the match, with Tomáš Souček returning to international action as an 85th-minute substitute as Marin covered earlier today. Former Hammers Vladimír Coufal and Alex Král were also in the Czech starting line-up.

As Claret & Hugh reported yesterday, West Ham have been linked with Naderi following his impressive start to life at Rangers.

The 23-year-old has reportedly attracted interest from several clubs, although Rangers are understood to be reluctant to sell their striker during the January transfer window. Spain’s victory leaves them with five wins from five Nations League fixtures.

That sets up a particularly interesting encounter with England at the Metropolitano Stadium in Madrid on Sunday evening. The three lions spluttered to a 2-1 victory against Slaven Bilic’s Croatia last with Harry Kane scoring the winner from the spot.

The two sides are competing for top spot in Group A3, with the match kicking off at 7.45pm GMT.

Meanwhile, from a West Ham perspective, it is encouraging to see Souček back on the pitch following his recent injury problems, even if his involvement was limited to the closing stages.
