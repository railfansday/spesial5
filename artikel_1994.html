Sources: Middlesbrough set Will Lankshear valuation as Newcastle explore deal

Newcastle United would have to pay a fee of £30million to sign Middlesbrough striker Will Lankshear, sources have told Football Insider.

The 21-year-old has excelled for Boro since his summer move from Tottenham, and already has 13 goals from his opening 13 games in the Championship.

Lankshear's form has prompted interest from Newcastle of late, but Boro will not make it easy for them to land him.

Sources say that the starlet's price tag is increasing weekly at the moment, and has already "tripled" from the initial £10m that he moved for in the summer.

Lankshear is tipped to have a huge future ahead of him in the Premier League, and is already being monitored by the England setup.

Newcastle are hoping to bolster their attacking options within the next few windows, but a move for Lankshear will not come cheaply for them.

Lankshear has made waves in the Championship so far this season, and is quickly becoming one of the hottest properties in English football.

Newcastle are just one of several clubs who are currently paying close attention to his progress on Teesside, with a view to potentially making a move.

Despite the youngster's growing reputation, Middlesbrough remain "relaxed" over his long-term future at the Riverside Stadium.

They already have Lankshear tied down to a long-term contract until 2031, and have no intention of selling him in the middle of the campaign.

That is set to take a deal firmly out of reach for Newcastle in January, but it could be a different story next summer.

Former Manchester United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider on Monday that Newcastle have scouts watching Jobe Bellingham with a view to a move.

The Borussia Dortmund midfielder is excelling in the Bundesliga so far this season, and has just earned his first senior England call-up.

Brown believes that Bellingham is on the radar of all of the Premier League's top clubs, but feels that Newcastle are the "standout" to potentially land him.

That would represent a major coup for the Magpies if they could get it done, not least since Bellingham is a former Sunderland player.
