How to get a free iPhone 18 Pro or iPhone 17 ahead of Black Friday (its easy)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

TL;DR: The Apple iPhone 18 Pro is free when you switch to T-Mobile and bring your number on a qualifying T-Mix plan. You can also get the iPhone 17 for free by switching to T-Mobile on a qualifying T-Mix plan.

If you’ve been holding off on a phone upgrade until Black Friday, wireless carriers are dropping deals to reward your patience. And T-Mobile is leading the way with a range of iPhone deals that are hard to beat.

Right now, you can get the Apple iPhone 18 Pro for free (100% off its $1,199 retail price) when you activate a new line on a qualifying T-Mobile T-Mix plan. Because Apple rarely drops cash discounts on current-generation Pro hardware during Cyber Week — and never slashes $1,199 off an unlocked unit — this early Black Friday carrier offer is the most effective way to get Apple's latest flagship without paying full price.

But that's not all, because you can also walk away with the iPhone 17 by following the same process: Simply switch to T-Mobile and bring your number on a qualifying T-Mix plan. It's that easy.

The full savings are distributed as monthly credits over a 36-month payment plan. If you leave T-Mobile before the end of the three-year contract, you will be charged the remaining balance. You also need to pay local sales tax on the phone's full retail value upfront, plus a standard $35 device connection fee per line at checkout.

Score the Apple iPhone 18 Pro or iPhone 17 for free ahead of Black Friday with T-Mobile.
