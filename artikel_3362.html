13 Most Exciting K-Dramas Releasing In 2027 & Beyond

K-drama fans have much to look forward to in 2027. The South Korean film industry has soared to new heights in the past ten years, due in part to the success of internationally distributed series on streaming services. With an influx of new viewers, the genre has continued to innovate, with many 2026 K-dramas aiming to impress and contribute to the latest Hallyu wave.

From dark and gritty K-dramas on Netflix to tooth-rotting romantic comedies airing domestically, there are plenty of exciting projects on the horizon for next year. Without a doubt, classic K-drama lovers who enjoy trope-ridden tales, recent fans who seek out boundary-breaking titles, and everyone in-between will be eagerly awaiting an expected 2027 release.

The Trauma Code: Heroes on Call may be one of the most successful medical K-dramas of the decade thus far. Much like its Western counterparts (The Pitt, House, Grey's Anatomy, etc.), the series follows an ensemble of doctors at a central hospital, including the jaded surgeon who acts as the drama's protagonist.

Between exciting medical cases and some palpable main cast chemistry, the K-drama quickly became a streaming success on Netflix. Its debut was so impressive, in fact, The Trauma Code: Heroes on Call seasons 2 and 3 entered production simultaneously. The exact release date is unknown, but season 2 will likely drop sometime in 2027.

Based on the hit webtoon of the same name, Men of the Harem will center on Empress Latil, a powerful ruler who collects a small ensemble of male concubines. As her love life flourishes, however, Latil will face more political trials than ever.

While there will surely be some steamy romance in accordance to the eponymous harem trope, the main draw of Men of the Harem is its star-studded cast. Bae Suzy is attached as the female lead, while five renowned actors fill out her cabinet of lovers. Sadly, no details have been confirmed beyond the royal K-drama's slated premiere in 2027.

In this upcoming K-drama thriller, fan-favorite male lead Nam Joo-hyuk will take a morally grey turn as antihero Yun Tae-ju, a lawyer desperate to climb the corporate ladder. When he gains access to a mysterious app that grants the user's every wish, his lofty career ambitions finally feel within reach.

Nevertheless, everything has a price— a sentiment all forms of media have echoed since "The Monkey's Paw" (1902). Alongside an expert hacker and a leading detective, Tae-ju will seek the truth, though it may cost him everything he desires in the process.

The Twilight Between Us will follow two nobles from opposing families who are forced into an arranged marriage to end a century-long feud. In the north, disgraced heir Mu-yeong (Gang Dong-Won) lives in derision. Similarly, Su-ryeon (Kim Go-eun) is discounted by her kingdom and trapped by outdated royal decrees.

Together, the pair form a strategic bond, but— like any good fake dating scheme— their feelings are bound to become real. Aside from its stacked cast, The Twilight Between Us seems like a brilliant inverse of forbidden love K-dramas, and its 2027–2028 release window can't come quick enough.

Set around the 1988 Seoul Olympics, Born Guilty will follow the journey of Paeng-i (Park Seo-joon) as he unexpectedly inherits a criminal empire. As he stumbles into a massive drug ring, however, the man's greed soon eclipses his conscience.

While K-dramas might be known for their romantic dramas, many of the best shows have great action sequences and intense storylines to match.

Born Guilty is one to watch simply for the fact that it's taking an acclaimed romantic lead— Park Seo-joon— and turning him into a complex K-drama villain. Plus, weaving such a sordid tale amidst the real-life chaos of 1980s Korea will make for a fascinating period drama.

Another entry on Netflix's 2027 lineup is She, Wolf (previously known by working title Beauty In The Beast). The romantic fantasy K-drama will focus on Min-su (Kim Min-ju), a bright young woman who can secretly transform into a werewolf.

As she tries to adapt to normal life as a college student, Min-su will find herself caught in a love triangle with bad boy wolf-human hybrid Do-ha (Lomon) and apathetic heartthrob Hae-jun (Moon Sang-min). Admittedly, She, Wolf isn't trying to reinvent the wheel, but it will certainly be a must-watch for any rom-com lover.

Unlike the supernatural splendor of She, Wolf, Detective Park Mi Ok will be a K-drama based on a true story. Inspired by the life of the titular Park Mi-ok— a trailblazing homicide detective— the upcoming K-drama will follow the triumphs and tribulations of a woman working in a notoriously male-dominated field.

While K-dramas based in reality aren't always streaming hits, star actress Kim Ji-won has signed on as the title character, already giving Detective Park Mi Ok a leg-up. Luckily, filming is currently underway, and the K-drama will likely drop on Netflix in the second half of 2027.

Also known as 1/24 Romance, Love O'Clock will be a quintessential office romance K-drama in 2027. After reality TV producer Cha Ju-an (Shin Hae-sun) and former tennis pro Yoo Do-jin (Na In-woo) cross paths on a company retreat, the two inexplicably begin switching bodies for one hour each day.

Yet, with Ju-an being a relentless workaholic and Do-jin being a tender-hearted marketing newbie, the pair couldn't be more different. What follows will likely be a heartwarming opposites-attract romance with a captivating fantasy flair.

Similarly, The Dealer seems like a mix of a gripping crime procedural and a superhero K-drama. Expected to release in 2027, the series will follow Jung Geon-hwa (Jung So-min), a dealer at a casino who enters the dark world of gambling after falling victim to a financial scam.

Unbeknownst to her detective boyfriend Choi Woo-seung (Ryu Kyung-soo), however, Geon-hwa has the uncanny ability to detect when someone is lying after she's had alcohol. Consequently, the female lead has, in theory, the perfect way to recoup her life savings— but the scheme may jeopardize her entire relationship.

From the creators of Hotel del Luna comes Grand Galaxy Hotel, a spiritual successor that flips the script of the smash-hit flagship series. In the fantasy romance helmed by the Hong sisters, Eun-ha (Lee Do-Hyun) takes over ownership of a paranormal hotel after the CEO suddenly vanishes.

Although Lee Do-hyun previously appeared in Hotel del Luna as Go Cheong-myeong, his starring role in Grand Galaxy Hotel will be unrelated.

With the help of the human manager Sang-soon (Shin Shi-a), Eun-ha will encounter countless lost souls looking for solace on their way to the afterlife. It will be interesting to see if Grand Galaxy Hotel can go toe-to-toe with its predecessor, but chances are if you enjoyed anything about Hotel del Luna, the new ghostly K-drama will fit the bill in 2027.
