Michael B. Jordan's Involvement In New Prime Video Boxing Series Clarified By Creator

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: The Greatest creator has cleared up how involved Michael B. Jordan was in the making of the new boxing series.

Earlier this month, The Greatest premiered on Prime Video, chronicling the life of heavyweight icon Muhammad Ali. While Jaalen Best performed the man originally named Cassius Clay, Jordan, who has experience playing a boxer character thanks to the Creed movies, worked behind the scenes as an executive producer. Ben Watkins, who created The Greatest, opened up about how the Adonis Creed actor contributed to the series.

In an interview with ScreenRant's Brandon Zachary, Watkins recalled how Jordan was already a producer on The Greatest when he came aboard. The two men had “crossed paths many times before” and previously worked together on Burn Notice, though when the time came down for them to meet and discuss Watkins’ “approach” to The Greatest, it had “been a while” since they last talked. Regardless, Jordan was immediately receptive to Watkins’ ideas and promised that he would “protect” his “vision for the show,” feeling that “there was nobody else who could tell the story.” The showrunner continued:

That started our journey once I joined, and then the second time was when there was a moment where this show was on the fence about whether it was going to get greenlit or not. We knew we had a big meeting coming up with Prime Video executives, and we had a great presentation planned. We touched base with Mike, and he knew it was coming, and he was in the middle of a press tour or in production. Whatever was happening, he dropped everything and came to L.A. so he could be in that meeting in person and help seal the deal. Then we had a green light.

Then the bigger stuff was the support over time as a producer. He couldn't be in the trenches with us because he had so much else going on, but he could weigh in at very key times, and if it's push a button here, nudge something here, do this email, it was just really great to have someone like that. I've had really powerful entities in projects before, but I haven't had one that really put their ego to the side and said, 'What's best for the project? I know at this point I'm one of the biggest stars in the world, but what I'm right here right now is just this producer on this show trying to help you get your vision realized.’

While Jordan is best known for his roles in movies and TV shows like Friday Night Lights, Parenthood, Black Panther, Just Mercy, Without Remorse, and Sinners, he also has a sizable amount of producing credits on his resume. That includes TV shows like Gen:Lock, Raising Dion, and 61st Street, though like the latter series, he doesn’t appear on camera in The Greatest. As Watkins laid out, while Jordan couldn’t contribute to the Prime Video TV show on a day-to-day basis, he was nonetheless instrumental in making sure that the project turned out just the way Watkins wanted.

Michael B. Jordan has been a part of two franchises that have had critical and commercial success and in between, he's had fantastic smaller roles.

Best is joined in The Greatest’s main cast by Omari Hardwick as Cassius Clay Sr. Dana Gourrier as Odessa Clay, Kai Parham as Rahaman Ali, Giovanni Ribisi as Angelo Dundee, and Amin Joseph as Sonny Liston. The series has earned primarily positive critical reception, boasting an 85% Tomatometer on Rotten Tomatoes based off 20 reviews. Watkins also shared his hope that The Greatest will return for two more seasons to explore other chapters of Ali’s life.

The Greatest season 1 can be streamed in its entirety on Prime Video. Jordan will next be seen starring in The Thomas Crown Affair, which he also directed and executive produced, and releases in theaters on March 5, 2027.
