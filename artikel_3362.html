Gordon Ramsay Teams With ‘The Traitors’ Host Claudia Winkleman To Reboot ‘Hell’s Kitchen’ On ITV

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Gordon Ramsay and Claudia Winkleman are reopening the doors to Hell’s Kitchen in the UK.

The presenters will front a reboot of the cooking format more than 20 years after it originally premiered on ITV in 2004. The show will return in 2027, opening a Michelin-standard restaurant for two weeks, staffed by 10 celebrity contestants.

Hell’s Kitchen will be co-produced by Lifted Entertainment (part of ITV Studios), which created the format, and Studio Ramsay Global.

Ramsay said: “Hell’s Kitchen is returning to the UK bigger, bolder, and 100% hotter than ever before. A brand-new restaurant, an all-star celebrity cast and culinary challenges that will push even the most confident cooks. Winkleman added: “It’s fast, thrilling and unpredictable live television. I can’t wait to guide our viewers through the heat of the kitchen and am really hoping he goes easy on them, and asks for toast.” Watch on Deadline

Hell’s Kitchen originally ran for four seasons in the UK, with Angus Deayton hosting the first three seasons. Winkleman took over for Season 4.

Winkleman and Ramsay did not overlap on the UK series because Marco Pierre White was running the kitchen by the time The Traitors presenter was on hosting duties. They have shared a screen before: Winkleman appeared as a guest on Gordon Ramsay: Cookalong Live.

Hell’s Kitchen was commissioned by Katie Rawcliffe, ITV’s director of entertainment, reality and daytime, and Louise Major, entertainment and reality commissioner. Mike Spencer-Hayter is the director of unscripted for Lifted Entertainment (ITV Studios), and Lisa Edwards is the chief creative officer of Studio Ramsay Global.

Hell’s Kitchen launched in the U.S. in 2005 and remains a fixture on Fox.
