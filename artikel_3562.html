Brady Bunch's Barry Williams Needed 'A Year of Recovery' After 'DWTS' Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Brady Bunch alum Barry Williams gave his all throughout Dancing With the Stars, which admittedly took him a while to recuperate from after leaving the ballroom competition.

“At Dancing with the Stars, I brought out 100 percent of everything; everything I had. I have never been so tested,” Williams, 72, said on a recent episode of the “Pop Culture Preservation Society” podcast. “I’m not going to lie: There was a year of recovery after those two, three months.”

Williams danced with DWTS pro Peta Murgatroyd on 2023’s season 32, finishing in seventh place.

“It was really hard on my knees, et cetera,” he said, adding that he “loved” the journey overall. “It was a great experience. It was so challenging and so much fun.”

While Williams went into DWTS following a professional career in musical theater, he felt like a complete novice when it came to the ballroom and Latin styles.

“There’s no way that you can actually practice for ballroom. It is completely different,” the actor acknowledged. “It is like what pop music dancing is to ballet is what ballroom is to pop, and so it helps to have a really, really great partner, and I did, [and] that is what really carried it. Whatever it was going to be, I just knew that I would leave it all on the dance floor. [I had a] great time.”

Throughout his ballroom tenure, Williams even had his family, friends, Brady costars and fans rooting for success.

“There is a lot of support and with this show, not only friends and your family involved, but fans, and then they participate in the process so that people are voting,” he told Us exclusively in October 2023. “They’re actively a part of it. They get to share the joy of moving forward to the next week when I can take up their time for another Tuesday night.”

In particular, Williams felt “great” as he learned to nail the dance steps and improve his overall stamina.

“I think it feels like it’s starting over each week because we start a new dance,” he acknowledged at the time. “It has a new impulse … and it’s unfamiliar to me, so it’s not part of my wheelhouse. The most difficult part is to readapt every week.”

Weeks after Williams’ elimination, actress Xochitl Gomez and pro dancer Val Chmerkovskiy clinched the mirrorball trophy.
