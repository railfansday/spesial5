Nikki Bella Hints at Romance With WWE Star, Brie Mentions 'Lots of Flirting'

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Nikki Bella got called out by her twin sister, Brie, for some spicy text messages she exchanged with current WWE star LA Knight.

Brie, 42, said her sister did “a lot of flirting” with Knight, 44, during a WhatsApp group chat during the game between the Philadelphia Eagles and Washington Commanders on November 1.

“So? We’re both single, so yeah,” Nikki responded on the Friday, November 6 episode of their “Nikki & Brie Show” podcast. “Who cares?”

Brie called the back-and-forth between the pair “very entertaining.”

Nikki — who settled her divorce from ex-husband Artem Chigvintsev in November 2024 after two years of marriage — exclusively told Us last month that finding a new partner isn’t exactly at the top of her priority list, but said a mystery man was working on changing her mind.

“I think it’s because I work hard when I’m on the road, and I go home and I go straight into mom mode,” she explained. “So I literally haven’t been on a date. I might text with a few people. I have some guys in my text messages that make me smile. Actually, one, he’s gained a lot of points in making me smile.”

Nikki and Chigvintsev, 44, share 6-year-old son, Matteo.

“Raising him is absolutely amazing,” Nikki told Us. “And my advice to a lot of people who are in coparenting relationships … the more that you can keep your relationship with your ex-partner, like, you keep that out of it. You put your child first, and it’s all about your child.”

She added, “The minute I land, whether it’s from wrestling or [an] appearance, I’m driving straight to my baby boy. I pick him up, and I’m in mom mode that whole time. And he’s just the smartest, sweetest, cutest little human being, and he’s such a mama’s boy. And it’s not only, like, the best job, but it’s just the greatest thing to be a mom, and he’s my everything. And I would quit everything just to be with him all the time.”

Earlier this year, Nikki exclusively admitted to Us that she lives “pretty much like a nun at this point.”

“Super celibate,” she joked in August. “I should just put on a ring … I wish I was out there getting wild. I just don’t have energy for it.”

Brie explained she has “been trying” to set Nikki up on dates, but she’s been “too busy.”

“I get it,” Brie exclusively told Us. “Matteo is her world right now. She’s like, ‘I have no time between being on the road, our wine company and our podcast, and then Matteo. I try. But she’s like, ‘No, no.’ This is the thing with her: If she has a free night, she wants to Netflix and chill. She’s like, ‘I don’t want to go on a date. I don’t have to dress up.’”
