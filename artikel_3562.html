Kanye West and Bianca Censori Renewing Wedding Vows Next Month, Relative Says

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Kanye West and Bianca Censori could be heading down the aisle once again.

A woman who identified herself as Censori’s aunt Kelly spilled the beans on an Australian radio program on Wednesday, November 25, claiming that the couple are set to renew their wedding vows next month.

“A lot of the family’s going over for her wedding next month,” shared Kelly, who said she is first cousins with Censori’s mom, on the Fifi, Fev & Nick show. “They are married, but they’re redoing their vows again.”

Kelly said she was not invited to the apparent vow renewal.

Us has reached out to West’s representative for comment.

West, 49, first wed the Australian architect, 31, in December 2022, just one month after finalizing his divorce from Kim Kardashian.

“Kanye and Bianca were legally married [in 2022] when they had their private ceremony in Beverly Hills; it was real and legal,” a source told Us at the time, adding the couple decided to wed “because of religious reasons.”

West shares daughters North, 13, and Chicago, 8, and sons Saint, 10, and Psalm, 7, with Kardashian.

In a new authorized biography, Citizen Kim: The Woman Who Created the Future, Kardashian, 46, revealed that she communicates with her ex-husband through Censori or his manager.

“I’m always like, ‘Oh, Dad’s traveling,’” she said. “I still make up stories and protect [the kids] because I want them to think their dad is the greatest. But I invite him over all the time. ‘Do you want to come to dinner? I’ll leave the house. Come and have dinner with the kids in their house so that they can feel comfy. Sleep over for a few nights?’ Last Christmas, I said, ‘Sleep over on Christmas Eve. Bring your girlfriend.’ I’m easy! ‘Let’s all do Christmas together.’ And I’ll recommend it again this year. I will sweep everything under the rug, all the horrible things he has said about me, so that the kids can see their father.”

The Skims cofounder also shared an update on her coparenting dynamic with West.

“Oh, I worry about [Kanye] all day,” Kardashian admitted. “All the time. That’s the father of my kids, I’ll never not worry about him. And that’s a big burden of having kids with someone who you don’t necessarily get along with. My kids would never know that. I do everything in my power to forget about all the negative things that happened. I have to be realistic, and my therapist definitely tells me, ‘Your boundaries have to go up,’ but we manage to coparent without too much conflict.”

She continued, “It’s been pretty smooth for a while. I guess because we don’t talk that much. But when we see each other, we give each other a hug. I’m really close with his family. I welcome anyone. I tell them all, open-door policy. Anytime you’re in town, come for dinner with us, see the kids as much as you want, take them out, come over and hang out. That’s their family. So I encourage the relationship with their dad so much.”

West and Censori recently attended his latest Yeezy fashion show with all four of his children, with daughter North posing for pictures with her stepmom.
