How To Play GTA 6 Early On Xbox Using This Trick

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Due to GTA 6's non-simultaneous release, people in New Zealand will be the first in the world to play the highly anticipated open-world crime game when it releases at midnight local time there.

If you want to become a Kiwi for a day and you plan to play on Xbox, you can trick your console into thinking it's in New Zealand to play early.

The following trick has worked before, but there is no guarantee that it will work for you this time with GTA 6, or you might not want to do it at all. After all, Microsoft's own policy says people should only change their region if they have moved to a different country or are already in a new region.

If that all sounds OK to you, here's how to change your Xbox region to New Zealand (via Insider Gaming).

  • Go to Settings
  • Click System, then Language & Localization
  • Switch your location to New Zealand
  • Restart your console

The process of changing regions on PlayStation 5 is not as straightforward and involves numerous other steps--Insider Gaming has a rundown. However, it's also been reported that players changing their PS5 region have begun receiving account suspensions without warning.

GTA 6 launches on November 19, but midnight in New Zealand on November 19 is 3 AM PT / 6 AM ET on November 18. So people in the US will have to wait a very long time to play GTA 6 compared to the people who live in New Zealand.
