Roma could put Rensch on the transfer block in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Roma’s transfer window will soon be in full swing.

Negotiations will be green-lit in January, but discussions are beginning in Trigoria about the next steps.

Gian Piero Gasperini wants an attacking winger and a fifth man: the budget available to Tony D’Amico to satisfy the Giallorossi coach remains to be determined.

And to raise funds, there’s Devyne Rensch, who hasn’t scored or provided any assists this season. The Dutchman could end up on the market.

The club is savoring the capital gain with the No. 2: the player arrived in January 2025 for a total fee of €6 million, including fixed fee and bonuses. Should he leave, the Roman club will have to pay Ajax 10% of the difference between the selling price and the signing fee.

In addition to the capital gains issue, which is not a priority for the Friedkins, Rensch could also leave the capital due to the repeated injuries that have kept him sidelined. His first injury problem with the Giallorossi dates back to February 2025, when the defender was sidelined for two matches. In total, the winger has missed 12 games in the capital since his arrival due to muscle problems, and is still out today.

Devyne was expected to return to the squad for the European away match against PSG, but that was not to be: after the first-degree injury he suffered during the match against Manchester United, the number 2 suffered a medium-grade tear to the adductor longus in his left leg. The 2003-born defender is targeting the match against Juventus on December 19th.
