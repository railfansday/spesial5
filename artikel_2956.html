Commanders-Giants: Jameis Winston attempts to draw Washington offside with rendition of 'Who Let the Dogs Out'

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Jameis Winston didn't start for the New York Giants in "Thursday Night Football," but he sure made an impression.

The veteran quarterback had a few cameos in relief of J.J. McCarthy during a 24-21 loss to the Washington Commanders, including when the Giants were facing a 4th-and-1 deep in their own territory with less than four minutes left and a 21-17 lead. New York brought out the offense and it soon became clear the hope was to draw Washington offside.

It began with the usual tricks, like fake hard counts and players in motion. Then the Prime Video cameras caught Winston singing "Who Let the Dogs Out" by Baha Men.

Yes, really. It didn't work, as the Commanders stayed on their side of the line of scrimmage.

The Giants took a delay of game penalty that pushed them back to their own 7-yard line and punted out of their own end zone. Punter Jordan Stout sent the ball 68 yards downfield, with the Commanders taking over on their own 37-yard line.

The New York defense held after that, with safety Ar'Darius Washington picking off Commanders quarterback Jayden Daniels, but a subsequent three-and-out gave Washington the ball back. A 32-yard touchdown pass from Daniels to Stefon Diggs put the Commanders ahead, and a Rasul Douglas interception ended the game.
