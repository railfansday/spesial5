Sixers Bell Ringer: Sixers back in East’s top spot on back of big performances from Embiid, Brown

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sixers Bell Ringer Season Standings:Jaylen Brown – 3.5Tyrese Maxey – 3.5VJ Edgecombe – 3Joel Embiid – 1LeBron James – 1Anfernee Simons – 1Dean Wade – 1

The Sixers traveled to Brooklyn on Monday night for their second night of their back-to-back after defeating the Indiana Pacers Sunday. LeBron James sat out this one for rest, but the Sixers had the services of Joel Embiid. Embiid wasted no time making his presence felt, pouring in 13 first-quarter points to lead the Sixers, who led 33-26 after one.

The Sixers exploded in quarter No. 2 for 45 points to stretch their lead after two to 78-59. Embiid led all scorers with 24 points at the halftime break. Jaylen Brown posted a dominant half of his own with 19 points and 10 rebounds.

The Nets countered with a big quarter of their own cutting the lead to 105-95 after three quarters. A big three at the buzzer from Jabari Walker helped the Sixers stall the Nets' run.

Brown left the game early in the fourth quarter after sustaining a blow to the head from Julius Randle but would later return. The Nets got the Sixers' lead down to just four points with under a minute to go when Embiid knocked down a pull-up corner three with 52 seconds to go to give the Sixers the needed cushion. The Nets fought hard and made the Sixers play all 48 minutes to close out this game. The Sixers, who led by as many as 24 points, would go onto win by a final score of 135-123. The Sixers moved back to first in the Eastern Conference.

Joel Embiid: 37 points, 5 rebounds, 3 blocks, 13-for-22 from the field, 9-for-10 from the line

Joel Embiid put together his best scoring game of the young season thus far. He looks to have battled through the foot injury that has limited him over the last few weeks and is back moving as good as he has all season. Embiid found his touch in the midrange where he dominated the game, catching a rhythm in an area of the floor where he has made a living throughout his career. Embiid, who has really struggled from three this season, also knocked down a pair from beyond the arc, including a pull-up dagger with under a minute to go. A healthy Joel Embiid is a dominant Joel Embiid and he appears to be trending in the right direction physically — and the touch has returned as well.

Jaylen Brown: 31 points, 13 rebounds, 4 assists, 9-for-19 from the field, 10-for-13 from the line

Jaylen Brown put up a 19-point and 10-rebound double-double in the first half alone in this one. He continues to stack good performances and show why he is the All-NBA and All-Star performer that he has been for so many years. Brown poured it in from all three levels as he often does putting on a clinical scoring performance. It was nice to see him return late in the game after suffering the blow to the head earlier in the fourth quarter. Brown's 13 rebounds are a season high for him. Defensive rebounding is another area himself and the Sixers continue to excel, which was a large concern for the team overall coming into the season. The Sixers' unselfish brand of basketball continues to lead to wins and standout performances for all different cast members.
