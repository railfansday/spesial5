Android app developers will have to publicly post address, phone number in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Following new regulations, Google Play will require Android app developers to publicly post a business address starting in 2027 and, while there are workarounds, it’s not going to be the easiest thing.

New Digital Services Act regulations in the EU and UK will require developers to share a phone number and address publicly. While this is only required in those regions, this information will be visible worldwide. Google does not allow PO boxes to be used for this requirement, meaning that many smaller developers will be left with limited options beyond posting their own personal address.

Exceptions to the rule include apps that are not published in the UK or Europe, or developers that qualify as “non-traders” which is essentially those making no money off of their apps.

The Verge notes that a “registered agent service” can be used as a business address for those who don’t have a physical office, and that Apple is allowing iOS developers to use a PO box if they prove the box is linked to a physical address.

Google will require Android app developers to confirm their trader status and their address by February 1, 2027. Account-wide status can be found in the Google Play Console via Manage account > Account details > About you, while status on a per-app basis can be handled via Monitor and improve > Policy and programs > App content.
