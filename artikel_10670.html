GTA 6 is causing internet issues as players rush to preload

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The demand for GTA 6 is already putting a strain on internet providers, with one UK company reporting widespread issues as players rush to preload.

After years of waiting, Rockstar’s next Grand Theft Auto is finally set to arrive on November 19, and players are already preparing for launch by preloading the game ahead of time.

However, the sheer number of people trying to download GTA 6 at once appears to be causing problems, even for those who aren’t planning to play it.

UK internet provider YouFibre warned customers that the surge in downloads was affecting its network, resulting in slower internet speeds.

In an email sent to customers, YouFibre confirmed that GTA 6 was responsible for the disruption.

“We are aware of a network capacity issue affecting the YouFibre network, due to the high demand of GTA 6 downloads,” the company explained. “Unfortunately, this has caused the overall download speeds of the service to decrease.”

Meanwhile, YouFibre CEO Jeremy Chelot also addressed the situation on X, revealing that parts of Yorkshire were experiencing problems.

“GTA6 is causing issue with one of our [Broadband Network Gateways]…trying to rebalance the traffic…therefore people around Yorkshire are currently suffering congestion… bear with us please,” he wrote.

Fortunately, the disruption didn’t last long, with YouFibre later sending a second email confirming that the emergency work had been completed.

“Our Network Team have completed the emergency traffic management to spread the load of the network surge we had this evening across the wider network, and the service has returned to normal,” it said.
