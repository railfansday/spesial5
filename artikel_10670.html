Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO amid ongoing foreign intelligence investigation

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Nvidia has reinstated Jensen Huang as President and CEO after its Board of Directors unanimously voted to bring him back despite an ongoing investigation into national security concerns.

Huang had been temporarily replaced by Nvidia Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress following reports that the executive had been targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. The reports prompted the company’s board to establish an independent committee and retain outside legal counsel to investigate the matter.

On November 23, Nvidia announced that Huang would return to his leadership positions effective immediately following a series of meetings with the board and consultations with senior US government officials.

The controversy began in late October when The New York Times reported that Huang had a close relationship with Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based cloud computing company Megaspeed whom US intelligence officials suspected of working for Chinese intelligence.

Megaspeed has also faced scrutiny over allegations that it helped Chinese companies obtain advanced Nvidia AI chips subject to US export restrictions. The Chinese government has denied the espionage allegations.

On November 8, Huang admitted in a message to Nvidia employees that he had been unfaithful to his wife. Nvidia announced its independent investigation that same day.

Further reporting alleged that Huang had also engaged in an extramarital affair with Nvidia Vice President of Healthcare Kimberly Powell. On November 12, the company announced his resignation, ending his 33-year tenure as CEO.

However, less than two weeks later, Nvidia’s board reversed course, saying it was confident that Huang’s reinstatement posed no national security risk.

“The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers,” Board Chair Harvey C. Jones said.

“I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution,” Huang said.

“I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment.”

Despite his return, Nvidia confirmed that its independent investigation remains ongoing and that Huang has committed to cooperating with it.

The company has also established a National Security and Export Control Committee to oversee trade compliance and national security risks. Nvidia is additionally searching for a Chief Compliance Officer and will continue cooperating with government investigations.
