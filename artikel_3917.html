If you want the very best offers, there are a couple of things I'd keep top of mind.

Use price comparison software: The most reliable way of making sure you're getting a good discount is using sites such as CamelCamelCamel. These track and report on a product's price history, including everything from record lows to average cost. That means you can quickly discern whether a saving is actually good or is just paying lip-service to the idea.

Beware of third-party knock-offs: I'm calling it now, the shelves are almost certainly going to be filled with knock-offs pretending to be something they're not. While I'm sure some of these are going to be worthy of attention, I'd avoid most nonetheless – there's no telling what their quality is going to be, and whether the product you're receiving is going to be anything like the one in promo shots. If in doubt, check user reviews or for official brand stores in the likes of Amazon.
