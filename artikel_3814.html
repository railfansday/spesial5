Beware The Warehouse Filled With Creepy Sex Bots In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

In Grand Theft Auto 6, players can explore a massive digital recreation of Florida and Miami that is incredibly realistic and grounded. Oh, also, there’s a creepy warehouse filled with out-of-control AI sexbots that want to rip you apart. And one of them has two giant dicks for hands.

As people are discovering, Grand Theft Auto 6 can be a very weird game if you go off the beaten path and explore. People have already found literal Easter eggs, Bigfoot, giant monster men, mutated crabs, and more. So with that in mind, it’s not too shocking to learn that hidden in Vice City is a warehouse just waiting to be discovered that contains a horror-like experience involving male and female sex dolls. At one point, a male sex doll with two giant dildo hands crawls onto Jason and begs him to finish him, daddy. Yikes.

Interestingly, these creepy AI sex dolls are hinted at earlier in the game in a few ways, including a tiny jump-scare moment involving Lucia during a mission that has her opening up shipping containers. Also remember that all this work went into a moment that a lot of players will never encounter. You need to be poking around the world between missions to find this place. Just one more reason to slow down while playing GTA 6. Unless you want to avoid sex doll robots attacking you. In which case, maybe speed up near warehouses.
