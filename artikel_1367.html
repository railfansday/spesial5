Blues Beaten By Brighton Under Goodison Sun

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Everton saw only their second loss at Goodison Park this season in a narrow 1-0 defeat to Brighton in the WSL on Sunday afternoon.

Coming off the back of a three-game unbeaten streak against a trio of top teams of the division, the Blues struggled to take the edge in Sunday’s showdown with the Seagulls.

In the first ten minutes, Brighton’s aggressive pressing pushed the Blues’ backline and forced an error of miscommunication, giving them an early lead.

The next 80 minutes saw the Toffees battle it out to take back control on home ground, but ultimately, they couldn’t find the back of the opposition’s net.

Head Coach Scott Phelan’s line-up was rotated from the team that took on Manchester United in the Subway Players Cup showdown in midweek with Courtney Brosnan in net, a fresh backline and Ruby Mace and Yuka Momiki starting.

After the early concession, Brosnan was called in to action once more to deny Kiko Seike. The forward latched on to a threaded through ball and stabbed at goal, with the Republic of Ireland international standing her ground to deny the visitors a quick second.

On the other end of the pitch, Noemie Mouchon and Momiki took turns to test the Seagulls’ defence, but neither chances could escape Brighton’s Chiamaka Nnadozie in goal.

That concluded the action for the first 45, with both sides battling to take what felt like an imminent second goal of the match, but neither found the back of the net.

Substitutions for the second half saw Jutta Rantala come on for Mouchon at the break.

Just three minutes after the whistle, Everton forward Ornella Vignola received a narrow cross from Momiki just outside of the box. She sent a powerful strike slightly wide of the goal, much to Brighton’s luck.

The Seagulls retaliated with a furious counter-attack. That was no match for Brosnan’s reflexes that guided striker Seike’s shot onto the post, and away from the back of the net.

The remainder of the second half saw a more dangerous side of the Toffees, with Vignola and Rantala both going one-on-one with Brighton’s defence in the 60th and 66th minute after balls from midfield powerhouse Ruby Mace. Yet despite their best efforts, there was still no goal.

With 68 minutes gone, Phelan looked for fresh legs and took off makeshift winger Maz Pacheco for Hannah Cain.

In the 76th minute, another chance fell to Hikaru Kitagawa. This time, the Japanese defender broke through midfield up the left wing and came head-to-head with Brighton’s defence. But, her efforts came to a close when she was faced with pressure and volleyed the ball sharply over the crossbar.

Immediately afterwards, final changes were made to the team, with Aurora Galli coming on for Hannah Blundell.

The action-packed second half came to a final showdown in the 85th minute. Seike crossed to Marisa Olislagers who was waiting outside the box. The Dutch midfielder pelted the ball towards the Toffees' net, but it was met by Brosnan who in yet another odds-defying save, punched the ball over the crossbar to the Seagulls' despair.

Late offences saw the Toffees win two corners, but the Blues were still unable to penetrate Arturo Ruiz’s goal line in a series of struggles in the box.

The final whistle blew and the game came to an end – the affair decided by the unfortunate own goal inside six minutes.

Everton will next play Arsenal at home in the WSL on Friday 11 December. Until then the squad will break off to play in a series of international friendlies.
