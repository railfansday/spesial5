Man Utd Fans Who Threw Tennis Balls on Pitch Learn Punishment

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Manchester United have sparked backlash after banning four fans who threw tennis balls during a protest against the club's ownership.

The incident occurred during November’s Champions League tie against Roma at Old Trafford. The game itself was a thriller, with the home team going two goals down inside the opening 17 minutes before Bruno Fernandes's penalty and a brace from Matheus Cunha helped complete a second-half comeback.

Shortly after going two goals down, however, Man Utd fans in the Stretford End threw tennis balls onto the pitch. Play was stopped in the 20th minute, with players on both sides forced to remove the balls.

Ahead of the game, there were more protests as hundreds of green and gold balloons — the colours used to protest against the Glazers — were released by supporters during the Champions League anthem. Four individuals have now been punished.

As per The Athletic, Old Trafford stadium bans have been issued to four supporters who threw tennis balls onto the turf as a protest against the Glazers. The report states that at least one fan has received a three-year ban, with the club’s sanctioning guidelines stating that a “level four” offence of “missile throwing” is punishable with a three-year suspension.

At the time, as confirmed by Greater Manchester Police (GMP) earlier this month, three fans were arrested for their actions. While one fan appears to have avoided arrest, they've still been punished by Man Utd.

There has been much anger at Old Trafford this season, with the club winning only one Premier League game from their opening 10 matches. As such, Michael Carrick has been sacked, leaving the team 18th, in the relegation zone, on just eight points.

However, instead of directing their anger at Carrick, many fans showed support for the Englishman during his final games. It was notable how he was applauded off the pitch, with anger instead aimed at the players and the ownership.

Someone else wrote: "The absolute state of our football club." Another said:

"Utterly pathetic. Not only are the owners ruining the club, they have ridiculously thin skin too."

In a lengthy post, one fan summed up the mood of any, noting: "This is exactly why the Manchester United board deserves every bit of criticism they get. A little protest against the ownership, even if it wasn't loud or disruptive, is enough to trigger stadium bans??

"Fans have every right to express frustration with incompetent leadership. Instead of addressing the failures of this board, they’re punishing supporters who are fed up. The problem at Manchester United has always been this clueless leadership. What a disgrace."
