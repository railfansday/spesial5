Flight Centre Travel Group Limited (FGETF) Shareholder/Analyst Call November 10, 2026 7:01 PM EST

Gary SmithRobert BakerGraham Turner - Founder, Global MD, CEO & Executive DirectorHaydn Long - Investor & Media Relations OfficerGareth TurnerJohn EalesAdam Campbell - CFO & CEO of Global Business ServicesAndrew Stark

Good morning, ladies and gentlemen. Welcome to our 31st Annual General Meeting and the first ever in our global headquarters. My name is Gary Smith, and as Flight Centre Travel Group Limited Board Chairman, I will chair this meeting. Moving to our official AGM duties as we have the necessary quorum, I declare the meeting open. This meeting is a hybrid meeting with shareholders able to attend either in person or online via the Computershare meeting platform.

Shareholders online can listen to our live webcast and watch our presentation. They can also ask questions and submit votes online. But first, I'd just like to acknowledge that today is Remembrance Day. And on the 11th hour of the 11th day of the 11th month in 1918, the guns of the Western front fell silent. Since then, this day has been set aside for us to remember the men and women who have served and who continue to serve in our armed forces and particularly those who gave their lives in war and conflict.

We remember their courage, their sacrifice and their service. We honor the families who carry the loss and the veterans and serving personnel who live with the experience of service every day. Lest we forget. I would also like to acknowledge the traditional custodians of country, the Turrbal and Jagera people. We recognize their continuing connection to the land, waters and skies in this place known as Meanjin, now Brisbane, where we meet today. We pay our respects to them, their cultures and to elders past, present and emerging.
