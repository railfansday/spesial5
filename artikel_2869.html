Saints "working through some things" with Justin Reid after he requests release

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It appears safety Justin Reid and the Saints are parting ways.

Defensive coordinator Brandon Staley was asked at a Thursday press conference about reports that Reid has requested his release. Staley said the team is “working through some things” with the veteran safety and that the request took him by surprise.

“Justin’s been a professional since he’s been here and I know that I’ve really enjoyed working with him,” Staley said, via Katherine Terrell of ESPN.com. “There’s some things that we all want a little bit more of from a productivity standpoint. But in terms of the fit, in terms of how he’s been here, he’s been a pleasure to coach. So we’ll just leave it at that.”

Staley said he expects there to be a resolution to Reid’s status soon.

Reid has started all nine games this season, but his playing time has dropped in recent weeks. He has 46 tackles and two tackles for loss this season.
