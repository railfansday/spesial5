The Knicks may need seven points and an Indiana favor to stay in the Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Knicks can win their seventh straight game Friday and still hand back the NBA Cup. The difference between a good night and another game in the tournament may be the margin, or something they can't control in Indiana.

New York is 1-2 in group play after Wednesday's 121-102 win over Cleveland. The Knicks were minus seven in Cup point differential before that game. The 19-point win moved them to plus 12. That's why the late points mattered even after the Cavaliers were done making it interesting.

Nov 25, 2026; New York, New York, USA; New York Knicks guard Jalen Brunson (11) makes a three point basket during the second half against the Cleveland Cavaliers at Madison Square Garden. Mandatory Credit: Vincent Carchietta-Imagn Images Credit: IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

The NBA's Friday scenarios give the Knicks a path to the group title: beat Miami by at least seven, get a Philadelphia loss at Indiana and finish ahead on the group tiebreakers. A seven-point win would lift New York's current differential to plus 19 and take seven off Miami's. It doesn't tell you what Indiana might do to its own number. And if the Sixers win, they take the group.

There is a wild-card path, but it's not a backup plan the Knicks can force. They still have to beat Miami. The league's scenarios depend on results involving Toronto, Detroit and Atlanta, with Indiana's result and New York's group finish also in play. Point differential still matters when comparing the teams that finish second. Lose to Miami and none of that is worth checking.

I wouldn't manage the first 43 minutes like the Knicks are already down six. Jalen Brunson and the starters get no extra credit if they spend the night hunting early threes to chase a margin and wind up in a one-possession game. If New York actually has a lead late and the other scores make it meaningful, then it's worth caring what the final number says.

Tipoff at the Garden is at 7 p.m. Eastern. Indiana and Philadelphia are also on the Friday slate. It may take a second screen and a few extra possessions to know whether this is merely win number seven.
