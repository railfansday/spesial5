Cricket Australia backs Pat Cummins as test captain despite fallout from ball-tampering scandal

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

MELBOURNE, Australia (AP) — Cricket Australia said Saturday that Pat Cummins will remain as Australian test captain despite controversy over his role in the so-called Sandpapergate scandal.

Cummins' tenure as skipper had been questioned amid conflicting accounts of what he knew about Australia's ball tampering in the 2018 South African series in Cape Town.

Following a board meeting, Cricket Australia confirmed Cummins would stay as captain for the three-test series against New Zealand starting Dec. 9 in Perth.

"As part of this meeting, the board resolved to express its unanimous support for Pat Cummins as captain given ongoing speculation about his position," Cricket Australia said.

The statement said Cricket Australia chair David Boon and chief executive Todd Greenberg met with Cummins in Sydney on Friday. That followed the announcement that players from the 2018 series would be invited to participate in an independent forum that would allow them to give their accounts of the incident.

"We've expressed our support to Pat and our concern for everyone impacted by the events of the 2018 tour of South Africa," Boon said.

Cummins said in the same statement that he backed the forum and intended to participate.

"I support the forum 100%. I will be there," the fast bowler said. "All of the players and staff I have spoken with are very keen to go and to have their say. There will be a lot of difficult conversations, but that is what's needed."

Cummins, who has held the captaincy since late November 2021, has denied any knowledge of Australia's use of sandpaper on the ball in the 2018 series in South Africa.

His stance has been repudiated by Cameron Bancroft, Usman Khawaja and David Warner, all Cummins' teammates on the tour eight years ago.

Cummins has described Khawaja's claims the entire touring party knew of scheming to tamper with the ball in South Africa as "nonsense" and has been backed by fellow pace bowler Josh Hazlewood.

Meanwhile, Steve Smith will be back in the spotlight after being included in the New South Wales state side for the final round of Sheffield Shield domestic first-class games before Australia names a squad for the New Zealand series.

Smith was stripped of the test captaincy and received a 12-month ban from all international and Australian domestic cricket for his role in the cheating saga, but the 37-year-old has maintained his silence since Khawaja made the claims in his recently published book.

Bancroft was suspended by Cricket Australia for nine months over the Cape Town incident. Warner, then the vice-captain of the test team, was suspended from all cricket for a year.
