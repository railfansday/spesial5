Houston Christian vs. McNeese: How to watch, date, time, TV channel

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

After consecutive road losses, Houston Christian returns home looking to right the ship.

McNeese leads the all-time series 6-2, with HCU's two-game winning streak in the rivalry halted last season in Lake Charles, La.
