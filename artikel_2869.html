Patriots activate Mack Hollins from IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Patriots won’t have A.J. Brown or Romeo Doubs on Sunday, but they will be getting another wide receiver back from injury against the Packers.

They have activated Mack Hollins from injured reserve. Hollins returned to practice this week after missing the last four games with a calf injury.

Hollins had 15 catches for 209 yards in four appearances before his injury. He had 46 catches for 550 yards and two touchdowns in his first season with the Patriots.

Brown will miss his second straight game with a knee injury and Doubs remains on injured reserve. Pop Douglas, Efton Chism, and Kyle Williams join Hollins as the available receivers on the 53-man roster for New England.
