Here's how you get to all three Sha-Chi Towers and Primal Stones for A Severed Mindseal in Phantom Blade

Completing the Phantom Blade Zero A Severed Mindseal quest takes a long time as you've got to help the friendly Mandate Shadow Warden at three Sha-Chi Towers scattered across the game. The Weeping Creek Valley tower is part of the main story but the other two are optional: the Pangzhen Tower, which is confusingly actually in Meigu Lake, and the Crimson Forest Tower, which can't be accessed unless you've sufficiently progressed the main quest and a particular side quest – in short, if you've just started this quest, you don't need to worry about the other towers for ages.Specifically, you've got to find a Primal Stone for the warden to chow down on at each tower, which involves unique exploration efforts for each one. So, to make this quest as simple for you as possible, I've explained every step of A Severed Mindseal below, including how to get to each Sha-Chi Tower and find all the Primal Stones.

The Primal Stone is on the first floor of the tower around the back of the central furnace, but you need the Sha-Chi Furnace Inspection Crank to collect it:

To reach the Friendly Shadow Warden at the top of the Pangzhen/Meigu Lake Sha-Chi Tower, you need to run along the Pangzhen docks in the northeast of the city. Keep going along the wooden jetties until the walkway structure starts climbing up the cliff. Follow it all the way to the top and you'll get to a rope bridge and the Pangzhen Sha-Chi Tower Upper Level 1 Bell. This leads to the tower's south entrance and the start of your ascent.

However, the Primal Stone is in the lower section of the tower, which can only be reached by boat. Get the Sha-Chi-powered boat from the Pangzhen docks and sail around to the north side of the tower. Follow the red lanterns and you'll end up going down a channel that leads to the lower portion of the tower, then proceed as follows:

You can't reach this Sha-Chi Tower until you've reached the Mirage Estate in Pangzhen and have found all three Aberrant Three-Faced Puppet Memory Flowers within to trigger the next step of the Phantom Blade Zero Triple Threat quest. Only then can you fast travel to the now frozen Mirrorwood area of the forest where the Sha-Chi Tower is:

There unfortunately aren't any specific rewards for completing this quest, but now that you have the tower key, you can unlock that door near the lift and plunder the rest of the tower. When you get to the roof, you'll bump into some familiar faces and can take on the Phantom Blade Zero Heart of the Fist quest and get a few crucial Soul Tempering materials for some of great weapons.And this also isn't the last you'll see of Spectral Blade. I won't spoil what happens but, now that you've completed his quest, you'll find him in Soulrend Cliff where he will also give you a Soul Tempering material for a weapon.
