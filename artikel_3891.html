I'm not going to ask you how many hours of GTA 6 you've played so far, instead, I'm going to ask you how many hours you think you'll play over the next month, year, or generation? The arrival of GTA 6 is a cultural moment, and for us gamers, I think it's worth investing a little extra in that to help make it special.

The only issue? Gaming setups in 2026 are expensive. Buying a new controller, headset, TV, or place to sit is no joke, and most of us will probably have struggled to afford a console and the game on their own. Thankfully, GTA 6's launch is coinciding with early Black Friday gaming deals, meaning that I've been able to find a bunch of our favorite gaming hardware for discounted prices.

Obviously, GTA 6 is only available to PS5 and Xbox players at the moment, so you won't find any specific-to-PC recommendations on this list. Nevertheless, that doesn't exactly narrow things down because there are loads of our top-reviewed products with discounts at the moment.
