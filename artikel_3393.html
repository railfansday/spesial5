Quentin Tarantino Play ‘The Popinjay Cavalier’ Adds ‘MobLand’ Star Nigel Lindsay & ‘The Crown’s Harry Hadden-Paton To Cast Led By Sacha Baron Cohen

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Quentin Tarantino has rounded out the cast for his first West End stage show, The Popinjay Cavalier.

The Sony Pictures Entertainment and Sonia Friedman Productions play has already cast Sacha Baron Cohen. Joining the Borat star will be MobLand actor Nigel Lindsay and Harry Hadden-Paton, who has starred in Downton Abbey and The Crown.

Other key cast include Mark Hadfield (Outlander), Kat Ronney (Finding Emily), and musician Anna Phoebe. Completing the cast are George Blagden, Isis Cass-Clunie, Bryony Corrigan, Gavin Dunn, Ellis Elijah, James Gill, Chris Gordon, James Grimstone, Florence Guy, Shane Hampden, Tom Kelsey, Stephen Louis, Eoin McKenna, Sam Newton, Emily Odunsi, Jaden Oshenye, Sebastian Parker, Claire Price, Alfie Sherwood, Seb Slade, Hadley Smith, Jason Thorpe, John Vernon, Kai Virgo and Amber Weston. The Popinjay Cavalier, a “swashbuckling comedy” set in 1830s Europe, begins previews on February 23, 2027, and the production opens March 18 at London’s Phoenix Theatre. Watch on Deadline

The synopsis: “The Popinjay Cavalier is a rambunctious comedy of deception and disguise inspired by the grand swashbuckling epics of stage and screen. A sweeping celebration of theatre and its heightened romance, told with Tarantino’s signature style and unmistakable wit.”

The creative team joining Tarantino includes: Scenic and Costume Design by Tom Pye; Lighting Design by Natasha Chivers; Sound Design by Ian Dickinson for Autograph Sound; Fight Direction by Philip d’Orleans; Action Co-ordination by Sam Lyon-Behan; Music by Tim Phillips; Casting by Jim Carnahan and Alexandre Bleu, CSA; and Associate Direction by Jack Bradfield.
