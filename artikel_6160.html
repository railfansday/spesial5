From bonus points on your next hotel stay to sales on award tickets, we're always looking for travel deals that help you earn more points and miles and redeem them for maximum value. In other words, we want to help you book the vacation of your dreams.

This week, we've got a flash sale on Target gift cards (plus pro tips for stacking your rewards). And there's still time to earn a valuable Southwest Companion Pass the easy way, but you'll need to act fast.

Each week, we help our readers by rounding up promotions so they don't have to scour the internet for the best reward redemptions and offers. This includes everything from frequent flyer programs and hotel rewards programs to credit card offers and rental car deals. Here's our latest list of noteworthy new points and miles promotions for early November.

Airline loyalty programs can offer trip discounts on specific dates, the opportunity to earn more points or miles for future travel and exclusive deals to accumulate points or miles toward elite status. Below are some of the best offers we found for November.

Qatar Airways Privilege Club is one of several airline programs that use Avios as their rewards currency. Right now, members can get 25% off redemptions to select destinations, including Ethiopia and the Seychelles in economy and Nepal and Malaysia in business class.

But this sale won't last long — you must book by Nov. 14 and travel by Dec. 31.

If Canada is in your travel plans, you won't want to miss these limited-time deals from Air Canada Aeroplan.

You can earn double Aeroplan points (1 point per dollar) on cash flights between the U.S. and Canada in premium economy. Plus, you can earn even more (2 points per dollar) on cash tickets between Canada and Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) in New York.

Book by Nov. 15, 2026 for travel until April 30, 2027.

The holidays are just around the corner, and that means pre-Black Friday sales are popping up across the travel space — including this lucrative deal from Sonesta Travel Pass.

With the Sonesta Holiday Travel Presale, members can earn 7,500 bonus points per eligible stay, up to 30,000 bonus points total. Plus, you can save up to 25% with flexible rates or up to 30% when you prepay.

Book with code EARLYACCESS by November 16, 2026, and stay through June 30, 2027.
