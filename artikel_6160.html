Popular luggage brand (and TPG favorite) Away is running its biggest sale of the year, offering huge 25% off savings sitewide on luggage, bags and accessories through Dec. 1. Plus, if you spend $750, you'll also get a free Foldaway Travel Tote worth $88.

Related: Away soft-sided luggage review: Easy glide and space to overpack

It's rare for Away to offer such a large discount on nearly its entire collection, making it the perfect time to shop for yourself or your favorite travel buddies.

Not only is Away offering massive savings on its most popular items, but the brand also has several new collections just in time for the holidays. Unfortunately, limited-edition luggage in the Moonlight Gloss holiday collection and the new Series 3 suitcases are not eligible for the 25% savings.

Away's Cyber Week sale includes best-selling styles, such as The Bigger Carry-On and The Everywhere Bag, as well as must-have accessories like compression packing cubes and travel pouches.

Away also offers stocking stuffers, such as a travel wallet and a slim jewelry case, that your travel-addicted friends and family will love to find in their stockings.

Here are some prices for a few popular items in the Away Black Friday sale.

If you make a purchase, you can also start your order via Rakuten or the Capital One Shopping portal to get some extra cash back in your pocket. At the time of writing, Rakuten is offering 2% cash back, and Capital One Shopping is offering 5% on purchases made through Away’s online store.

Away offers sales this good on its full product lineup only once a year. With 25% off almost everything it sells, now is a great time to make an Away purchase if you've been planning to buy.
