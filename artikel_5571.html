Customers get one accountable partner for interoperability, migrations, archives, and network connectivity - not another vendor to coordinate.

The benefits of one partner, one platform

Frost & Sullivan cited ELLKAY's move beyond interoperability alone, toward running the full data journey for customers, as a strength. Three customers show what that looks like.

San Ysidro Health has worked with ELLKAY since 2022. Ahead of its move to Epic, it chose ELLKAY's healthcare data orchestration platform for interoperability, legacy data management, and migration instead of choosing a separate vendor for each capability. This work spanned across 50+ sites under Epic.

"Selecting ELLKAY enabled us to implement a holistic approach to our healthcare data strategy resulting in both cost savings and a sound foundation for future growth," said Daniel Howard, VP & CIO, San Ysidro Health.

MultiCare Health System started with ELLKAY's legacy data management offering. It then expanded to interoperability at one hospital, before entirely migrating its legacy interface management product to ELLKAY's healthcare data orchestration platform.

"The future of healthcare belongs to organizations that can orchestrate data across every system, every workflow, and every care setting. ELLKAY enables that opportunity for MultiCare," said Nick Shepard, AVP of Data Orchestration, MultiCare Health System.

West Feliciana Parish Hospital has worked with ELLKAY since 2012. The rural Louisiana hospital had been sending identical data from its EHR to every vendor endpoint over separate direct connections, straining the bandwidth required to get radiology images to off-site specialists. ELLKAY consolidated their connections to a single platform. The hospital has since added legacy data management functionality, so it can retire aging systems and keep long-term access to patient records as it moves to modern EHRs.

"ELLKAY assists us with data migration, creating an archive where records can be securely stored for long periods of time, ensuring nothing is lost in the process," said Denise Paul, IT Director, West Feliciana Parish Hospital.

Frost & Sullivan's research found that customers now expect semantic interoperability, meaning systems that agree on what data means, not just exchange it. It also found that organizations increasingly see interoperability partners as "strategic trustees rather than infrastructure suppliers."

Healthcare data orchestration unifies data across its full lifecycle, from connection to coordination to continuity, so the right data reaches the right place at the right time. Connectivity gets systems talking. Orchestration keeps that data usable once they are. ELLKAY has 95,000+ interfaces built and is backed by 4,000+ reusable interface templates. ELLKAY also supports the infrastructure behind CommonWell, a Designated QHIN under TEFCA. More than 10.8 billion health records are exchanged through the CommonWell network every year.

AI programs are only as good as the data underneath them. ELLKAY's AI-assisted interface translations are trained on the interfaces it has built over the last 20+ years of customer work. Engineers review and approve every suggestion before anything goes live, and every action is logged.
