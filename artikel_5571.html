CREFC Releases Updated Data Center Primer Amid Growth and Increasing Complexity in Financing Markets

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Second edition examines expanding capital markets, regulatory developments, rising borrowing costs, and power constraints shaping data center investment

NEW YORK, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The CRE Finance Council (CREFC) today announced the release of the second edition of its Data Center Primer, providing an updated examination of the rapidly evolving financing market for data centers and the opportunities and risks facing commercial real estate lenders and investors.

Since CREFC published its inaugural primer in January 2026, the data center financing market has continued to expand and become increasingly complex. The updated report examines the growing use of securitization and secured project bonds, recent regulatory developments, higher borrowing costs, and the power and infrastructure constraints affecting development and financing decisions.

The scale of the market is substantial. As of October 8, 2026, Barclays counted approximately $116 billion of secured project bonds outstanding in the data center sector, alongside $44.2 billion in asset-backed securities (ABS) and $22.4 billion in commercial mortgage-backed securities (CMBS).

"Data centers have emerged as an increasingly important segment of the financing markets. This includes but is not limited to CMBS, ABS, Corporate Bonds and Syndicated Bank Loans. The sector is attracting significant equity and debt investors," said Paul T. Vanderslice, President and CEO of CREFC. "As the market evolves, CREFC is committed to providing the information and resources that help investors, lenders and other market participants better understand the opportunities and risks shaping this rapidly changing sector."

"While demand for data center capacity remains strong, lenders and investors need to look beyond traditional real estate fundamentals to fully assess the credit risks associated with these assets," said Raj Aidasani, Managing Director, Research at CREFC. "Power availability, infrastructure costs, tenant obligations, and refinancing assumptions can all have significant implications for a project's economics. Our updated primer examines how these factors intersect with evolving financing structures and what market participants should consider when evaluating transactions."

The second edition builds on CREFC's ongoing efforts to provide research, education, and market intelligence to support informed decision-making across the commercial real estate finance industry.

About CREFCThe CRE Finance Council (CREFC) is the trade association for the over $6 trillion commercial real estate finance industry with a membership that includes more than 400 companies and 19,000 individuals. Member firms include balance sheet and securitized lenders, loan and bond investors, private equity firms, servicers, rating agencies, and borrowers. For over 30 years, CREFC has promoted liquidity, transparency, and efficiency in the commercial real estate finance markets, and acted as a legislative and regulatory advocate for the industry, playing a vital role in setting market standards and best practices, and providing education for market participants.
