Shaquille O’Neal is famous for his massive physical presence and equally massive wallet. However, during his prime years with the Los Angeles Lakers, the basketball icon made an impulsive automotive purchase entirely fueled by ego. After casually walking into a luxury car dealership wearing sweatpants and flip-flops, O’Neal aggressively dropped $1.3 million on three Rolls-Royce Phantoms just to prove a point to a disrespectful employee.

Shaq was simply passing time in the showroom alongside Mike Tyson. When O’Neal began asking about pricing, an impatient salesman completely misjudged his casual attire. The employee condescendingly called him “sonny boy” and flatly asked if the massive athlete could even afford the luxury vehicles. Insulted by the assumption, O’Neal immediately bypassed the salesman, found the manager, and purchased three V12 Phantoms on the spot.

According to a recent report from Autoevolution, before taking immediate delivery of his newly acquired luxury trio, O’Neal had one strict mechanical demand. He required the dealership to permanently relocate the driver seats as far back as physically possible. Because standard luxury cars are rarely engineered for his massive frame, O’Neal frequently relies on extreme modifications, like when he famously stretched his Corvette to safely fit behind the steering wheel.

Unfortunately, even with the seats pushed against the rear cabin wall, the stock Rolls-Royce Phantoms still lacked sufficient headroom and legroom. As a result, the expensive sedans essentially became permanent garage art that he rarely drove. O’Neal later admitted the spiteful transaction was one of the dumbest financial decisions of his entire life, especially since the condescending salesman ultimately walked away with a massive commission check from the triple sale.

Despite the financial misstep, O’Neal’s sprawling vehicle collection remains one of the most eccentric in the celebrity world. He eventually unloaded the three Phantoms, famously gifting one directly to teammate LeBron James, while another from that fateful afternoon is currently listed for auction on Bring a Trailer. The Hall of Famer routinely funds other ridiculous custom projects, ranging from a customized Impala jet boat to a bizarre electric salvage coupe that completely confused the automotive industry.

Beyond luxury flexes, the big man has occasionally channeled his automotive obsession into much more sentimental projects. O’Neal recently made headlines when he meticulously restored Kobe’s Cruiser to honor his legendary late teammate. While he no longer spite-buys British luxury sedans, his massive and chaotic footprint on global car culture remains entirely undeniable.
