New model identifies priority areas for large-scale ecological restoration in Chile

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Researchers from Chile's Institute of Ecology and Biodiversity (IEB) have developed a multiobjective model to identify priority areas for large-scale ecological restoration in Chile, South America. The tool integrates four ecological objectives: reducing species extinction risk, prioritizing carbon stocks, restoring riparian areas and assessing ecological feasibility. The findings show that including ecological feasibility changes restoration priorities, while 80.4% of the selected areas contribute to more than one ecological objective. By identifying areas where different priorities overlap, the model can help guide strategic restoration planning and support better-informed conservation decisions amid growing environmental challenges and limited resources.
