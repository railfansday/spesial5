Was This New Fable Gameplay Meant to Sell the Game?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

I am pretty confident Fable will be a fantastic RPG when it finally releases in February 2027, but for those still sat on the fence, Xbox is going to have to do a better job of promoting it than this latest example.

To mark the 25th anniversary of Xbox, four minutes of gameplay taken from a quest has been uploaded. However, all of maybe 30 seconds of it actually has anything going on. Watch it above.

Obviously, you do indeed do a lot of walking during open world RPGs, but it's not something you usually feature much in marketing beats. That's what most of the latest gameplay slice for Fable is, though: walking.

Partway through, there is a combat sequence showing off swordfighting, archery, and magical abilities. It's over quickly, however, and then we're back to walking. The video then concludes with a cutscene lacking context as it's been plucked out of the full game to be seen online.

I grew up playing the three mainline Fable games on the Original Xbox and Xbox 360; I have very fond memories of this series, so I want the new rebooted instalment to do well. It'll need better presentations and trailers than this for a good chance at that. It's out for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC on 23rd February 2027.
