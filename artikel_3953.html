Lego Batman: Legacy Of The Dark Knight Update Adds GameShare Support

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A new update has landed for Lego Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight on Switch 2.

It's not a massive one, all things considered, but it does add GameShare support for those who want to play online multiplayer via GameChat. We've also got a bunch of miscellaneous improvements to performance, stability, etc. Gotta love that stability.

- Added GameShare support for online multiplayer via GameChat - Various stability, performance, and quality of life fixes

An update on other systems adds a new menu narration accessibility option, however this is not available for the Switch 2 and nor does it seem imminent.

In our review for Legacy of the Dark Knight, we said that "with a frequently hilarious script, endless callbacks, and a cracking open world to explore, TT's love for the Caped Crusader always manages to shine through". We awarded it a score of 8/10.
