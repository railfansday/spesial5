Carra: Lammens confidence worrying but Poch will feel Man Utd were robbed

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Jamie Carragher felt Man Utd were 'unlucky' to be controversially denied two goals by refereeing decisions and said Mauricio Pochettino will feel like his side were 'robbed' of what would have been a 'really big win'

Jamie Carragher says Manchester United goalkeeper Senne Lammens' confidence is a cause for concern after his howler cost Mauricio Pochettino a first win since taking charge.

It was a nightmare performance for Lammens, who unnecessarily gave away the penalty for Luis Suarez's opener just 75 seconds after saving the Sporting striker's poorly taken first spot-kick.

Lammens, 24, then spilled a routine cross late in the second half which allowed Suarez to equalise for 2-2, denying United a first away win of the season and first away Champions League win for five years.

The Belgium international, signed from Club Brugge last summer, restored calm between the sticks last season after Andre Onana's calamitous spell as No 1, but has had a poor start to his second season at Old Trafford.

Delivering his verdict on Lammens' performance, Carragher said on CBS: "You worry about the confidence of that goalkeeper now.

"That mistake for the equaliser, the 2-2 goal, you say sometimes 'an amateur goalkeeper wouldn't make that mistake'. If that was an amateur goalkeeper you'd be thinking 'that can't happen'. That just can't happen at this level."

Carragher questioned if United had the options in their goalkeeper department to bring Lammens out of the spotlight for a brief while.

Pochettino could call on Karl Darlow if he wanted to make a change at goalkeeper. He was signed in the summer on a free transfer from Leeds, where he was the No 1 for the second half of last season.

"It is getting to the stage with the goalkeeper now," Carragher added. "The focus on a Man Utd goalkeeper is always big if he's making mistakes. I don't think the back-up's even at the level where, no matter how poorly the goalkeeper plays, you change it."

Carragher felt United were "unlucky" to see two goals controversially ruled out but criticised the players for allowing games to spiral out of control when they suffer set-backs.

The Sky Sports pundit said it must frustrate Pochettino but will make for an "unbelievable" watch when the fly-on-the-wall documentary being filmed this season is released.

"Why do they keep shooting themselves in the foot?" said Carragher. "As a manager, what can he really do about the goalkeeper or Tielemans making a stupid tackle at the end?

"He'll be really frustrated because that could have been a really big result for them tonight at 2-1, and there were unlucky with the 3-2 [goal being disallowed].

"He will feel they've been robbed by the referee and he'll say they should have won the game.

"But he'll be looking at isolated incidents and it feels like every game is mad. There's never a normal game where you win 1-0 or you draw 1-1 and nothing really happens. Something happens all the time that you're almost laughing at, or you've got your head in your hands if you're a Man Utd fan.

"There is always madness or craziness. I mean, the documentary is going to be unbelievable isn't it."

The last thing Pochettino needed after the 4-0 defeat at Liverpool - and Leny Yoro's now overturned red card - was more refereeing controversy. However, there was contention around both goals United had ruled out in Lisbon.

Amad had the ball in the back of the net toward the end of the first half to equalise - but not before the whistle had gone for what was judged to have been a foul by Tielemans, who clearly won the ball with his slide tackle.

The United midfielder received a booking for his follow through on Sporting defender Inacio, who went down dramatically despite receiving the slightest of contact. VAR could not intervene as the whistle had gone.

Morrison, who said Tielemans "won the ball", was adamant that it was the wrong call.

United's second disallowed goal, which was ruled out following VAR intervention, saw Dorgu penalised for making contact with Sporting goalkeeper Kaique before he punched into his own net.

Carragher felt it was "very harsh" for the referee to disallow the goal after being sent to his monitor.
