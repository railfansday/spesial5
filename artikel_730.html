Maresca: You can't compare Man City and Arsenal

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Arsenal and Man City face off in the Premier League this weekend with the Gunners holding a six-point lead over Enzo Maresca's side; Mikel Arteta says the game is important for the title fight; watch Arsenal vs Man City live on Sky Sports this Sunday, kick-off 4.30pm

Enzo Maresca said Premier League title rivals Manchester City and Arsenal couldn’t be compared after his side's early-season form raised expectations.

Maresca described Sunday's fixture, live on Sky Sports, as "four months vs seven years", referencing the transition City are going through following Pep Guardiola's departure and the longevity of head coach Mikel Arteta at Arsenal.

"We have been top of the league for two and a half months until November 1. Now we are not there and everyone is comparing," said Maresca.

"For me, it's seven years together against four months together so you cannot compare. I understand everyone is trying to do that, but from my point of view everything is clear."

City have fallen six points behind Arsenal after failing to win in their last four league games while they've only won two of their last seven in all competitions.

"It's more than easy to understand why because we've had four months together," said Maresca when addressing his side's form.

"Transition requires time. Probably because we won seven games in a row, eight with the Champions League, everyone was thinking that there was no transition.

"I said many times, I'm not a magician. There's a transition, it's normal. We have eight or nine new players, the manager is new, so you need time.

"You can see with different clubs when they transition, how complicated it is. For me, it's completely normal.

"I'm completely relaxed, no panic at all, understanding that we need to win games because it's our business. For me, it's all normal."

In midweek, Erling Haaland made some concerning comments, claiming that "you have to question everything" after their draw against Napoli in the Champions League. But Maresca insists there is no issue with the striker.

"It's normal that results affect the mood, that's why we need to win games," said Maresca. "I don't see any problem with Erling."

"Erling is a winner. It's normal that he's not happy when we don't win games. Not just Erling, everyone. Don't forget, three weeks ago, he was dancing on the pitch."

Maresca is prioritising the long-term vision he has for City. "The present is important but the future is much more important in terms of improving and building something with the team.

"Where we can be on Sunday night if we win, lose or draw, it's important but it's a long season. We'll see where we are in February and March and from there you can set the goals.

"Now, I think it's useless speaking about targets or where we can be at the end of the season."

But facing champions Arsenal on Sunday will give them idea of their progress.

"You need to show personality," he said. "That's why it's also a nice game for us to see where we are in terms of personality and from there try to understand the situation."

In the red corner, Arsenal manager Arteta knows how important matches are against their rivals in their bid to retain the Premier League title for the first time.

"When we played the home games against big teams, that becomes crucial in the title challenge," he said. "We know how important the home games are to make the difference.

"But we're in November, we know how long we have to play. But that's for every single team."

Asked whether City's form has anything to do with the club being found guilty of 115 serious breaches of the Premier League's financial rules in September, Arteta admitted that "it's a question for City."

He then went on to say: "All I can say is when I watch them play is a lot of games that they have done more merits than the opponent, but this is football. To win games, that's merit to the way they play."

Asked if City are Arsenal's closest challengers for the title, Arteta replied: "We don't know, I think the challenge is for us, and the opportunity is for us.

"We cannot control what the opponents are going to do; we just need to give ourselves the best version, and I'm sure the atmosphere is going to be incredible on Sunday, and we need to enjoy and embrace those kinds of games and go for it."

Arteta did not, however, want to be drawn on the atmosphere the Emirates Stadium could produce in the wake of City's guilty verdict. The club deny all the charges and have lodged an appeal against the verdict. No sanctions have yet been announced.

On the pitch, Arsenal are sweating on the fitness of goalkeeper David Raya while centre-back William Saliba will have to wait patiently to work his way back into the side following injury.

For City, the game comes too soon for winger Antoine Semenyo, who could return next week.
