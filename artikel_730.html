From pet parents to home chefs and wellness-lovers, these gift will get tons of use well after the holiday season is over.

When it comes to holiday gifting, we put a lot of pressure on ourselves to find something truly showstopping. It's understandable, as no one wants to give something that will sit in a closet and collect dust, or even worse, be regifted.

Often, the best gifts aren't the most expensive but rather, the most practical. And we found practical gifts that are anything but boring—and that will actually get used.

Finding a practical gift is all about knowing what those closest to you are interested in.
