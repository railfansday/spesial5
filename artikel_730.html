Khloe Kardashian Details Finding Lamar Odom in 20-Person "Circle Jerk”

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Khloe Kardashian and Kris Jenner recalled finding Lamar Odom in a 20-person “circle jerk” during the Nov. 5 episode of The Kardashians: “The most crazy thing that Khloe and I ever did.”

Khloe Kardashian fought for her marriage with Lamar Odom—quite literally.

Kris Jenner took a stroll down memory lane during the Nov. 5 episode of The Kardashians, recalling an evening when she and her daughter barged in on the NBA player in a hotel room amid his challenges with substance abuse.

“The most crazy thing that Khloe and I ever did was she realized that her significant other at the time, Lamar, was smoking crack,” Jenner—who had taken an edible ahead of filming the scene—told the group consisting of Kim Kardashian, Nikki Glaser, Brenda Song and Fortune Feimster. “We had intel that he was going to be at a certain hotel. Khloe goes, ‘Okay, we're gonna get him.’ And I'm like, ‘Okay.’ She goes, ‘Dress in black, hoodie, with a beanie.’”

Meanwhile, the Good American founder took some issue with her mom’s recollection of events. After sarcastically remarking during a confessional, “This is a good story I want to bring up right now,” she told the gathered group it was really Jenner who “put the drama on” with the all-black outfits.

“I just said, ‘We’re going to get Lamar,’” Kardashian emphasized. “That’s all I said.”

But the matriarch’s storytelling was not to be deterred, regaling the captivated audience with her dramatically rendered recollection of events.

“We’re gonna extract him from the situation because he’s being held hostage by these hookers,” Jenner said. “We were in the hotel going down the hallways, and Khloe’s security burst into this room.”

Which is when Kardashian supplied the following detail: “They were literally having a circle jerk.”

Yes, as Kardashian—who was married to Odom for seven years before their 2016 split—claimed, there were approximately 20 people in this hotel room participating in group sex.

The sight, according to Jenner, immediately took the wind out of her spy-like sails.

“I went from Charlie’s Angles to, ‘Ah!’” she exclaimed, adding of Kardashian, “She runs into the room like she's Hawaii Five-O. She's like, ‘Okay, everyone, like, basically hands up.’”

This piece of her story was corroborated by Kardashian, who said she did indeed “start swinging” at the individuals inside the room.

“My mom is hiding in the bathroom,” she said. “She's getting a trash can, and she's like, ‘Okay, honey, keep going.’ And she's literally swiping all of the drugs, everything in trash bags.”

As for who Kardashian was punching? As she told the inquiring Feimster, “Random whores, I never even introduced myself.”

The unexpected retelling of this particular moment in her life made Kardashian—who shares kids True, 8, and Tatum, 4, with ex Tristan Thompson—do some reflecting on what she’s really looking for in a future man.

“This is solidifying how I will never date a freak again,” she said in a confessional. “I didn't even know I needed to put this on my manifest list, but maybe I should add no circle jerks, no orgies, and like maybe no hardcore drugs.”
