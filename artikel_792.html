Off Campus Star Reveals He Won't Be Back for Season 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Riley Davis confirmed that he won't be returning to Off Campus as Allie Hayes' ex Sean McCall. Season two, premiering April 28, will follow Allie (Mika Abdalla) and Dean Di Laurentis (Stephen Kalyn).

Riley Davis is dropping out of Briar University.

After starring as Sean McCall, the on-again-off-again boyfriend of Allie Hayes (Mika Abdalla), in Off Campus, the actor revealed he won't be back on campus for the upcoming second season.

"I'm not gonna be in the next season," Davis told People in an interview published Nov. 19. "There was a plan for some Sean in season two, and we just had a bit of a scheduling thing on my side and a bit on their side."

E! News has reached out to reps for Prime Video for comment and has not yet heard back.

It's a surprising move, given that season two—which is set to premiere on April 28—will focus on hockey player Dean Di Laurentis (Stephen Kalyn) and Allie's game-winning romance.

As Garrett Graham (Belmont Cameli) and Allie's BFF Hannah Wells (Ella Bright) found love in the first season, Allie was focused on making her mark on center stage after breaking up with Sean—who believed her dreams of being an actress were far-fetched—for good.

Although her ex was sure she would eventually come back to him, he did not account for a certain blonde hockey player casually hooking up with Allie. In fact, she actually decided to bed the athlete to avoid slipping back into her relationship with Sean.

Unfortunately for the friends-with-benefits, their fling—which Elle Kennedy's third Off Campus book, The Score, centers on—isn't as chill as either of them expected.

But Davis—who also hit the water as Noah Bainbridge in Netflix's YA series Crew Girl—told the outlet that fans have "so much to look forward to for season two."

Introduced in season one, Allie and Dean's secret relationship will bring even more heat and ice to Briar U in its second installment as they come to terms with their budding feelings. As their love story continues, Sean does make appearances to wreak emotional havoc on Allie.

But Sean isn't the only man to come between their blossoming romance. In fact, Dean punched former friend Hunter Davenport (Charlie Evans) in the season one finale after learning that he and Allie—who did not know the animosity between them until the fight—had a one-night stand.

While Hunter isn't an antagonist in Kennedy's series, showrunner Louisa Levy shared insight into reworking the character for the TV adaptation.

"Some things had to work a little differently because of the timing and the way we platform them," she explained to Variety in May, "but I promise all the fan-favorite things are still intended and planned. There's still a lot of road with those two."

"He also comes from the same world as Dean, which made his arrival feel organic," she explained of Hunter. "We needed to throw a wrench into Dean and Allie's arrangement to elongate their story, and pulling from that universe felt right."
