Bakole: Late-notice Wilder fight not like Parker | 'I like danger!'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

In his first interview since agreeing to step in to fight Deontay Wilder, Martin Bakole explains his decision and why he believes this late-notice assignment will be very different from when he flew in to fight Joseph Parker; Bakole says victory will lead him to a world title shot

Martin Bakole explained his decision to step in as a replacement opponent for Deontay Wilder on the Tyson Fury vs Anthony Joshua undercard next month.

After Jai Opetaia withdrew from his December 11 bout with former heavyweight world champion Wilder, Bakole came in to take his place on the Principality Stadium bill.

He insists it is a very different scenario to his short-notice assignment against Joseph Parker last year.

Bakole came in to box Parker for the WBO Interim title, landing in Riyadh just a day before the fight. Parker promptly put him down heavily and stopped him in their second round.

For this fight with Wilder though he's had more warning. He was training in Las Vegas to box on the same bill, albeit originally for a much lower-level opponent.

"When I heard Wilder doesn't have an opponent, his opponent cancelled, they proposed me and I said why not? I'm ready to give a big fight," Bakole told Sky Sports. "Why not jump from a comeback fight to a big fight?

"I was surprised they mentioned my name and I said yes," he added. "I'm ready to come back again because I want to take over now the heavyweight division.

"I don't see Deontay beating me. He doesn't have the strength I have.

"I don't see any danger because I like danger, I like to impose myself.

That wasn't the case for Parker when he took that fight. "I was out in the Congo on holiday, I jumped in," Bakole said.

"In that fight I was giving Joseph Parker a hard time in the first round but because I was not fit and my head was not moving, he landed a punch. But I don't regret that fight. It's just part of my story.

While Bakole has been training in Las Vegas, he will still have long-time coach Billy Nelson in his corner for the fight.

"I came here to learn something new. I was getting ready for everyone," Bakole said. "Everything has changed [with his preparation], conditioning, nutrition."

He believes victory over Wilder will lead him to a world title shot next year.

"Of course," Bakole said. "That's why I wanted to change everything because I know next year, with my team, with His Excellency's [Saudi financier Turki Alalshikh] help, I will get a world title next year."
