Wilson eyeing survival step as Wales tackle Norway

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Harry Wilson has 17 international goals, the most of any player in the current Wales squad

Harry Wilson believes Wales can shock Norway again in Oslo on Saturday and move a step closer to survival in Nations League A.

Wales upset Erling Haaland and company in Cardiff last month to register a first ever win in the Nations League's top tier.

Craig Bellamy's side know another success in the return fixture would be a major boost to their hopes of remaining among Europe's elite.

As it stands, Wales are third in Group A4, above Norway based on their head-to-head record.

Should that happen, they would be separated by goal difference in the games involving those three sides.

For the moment, however, Wilson says all eyes are on toppling the Norwegians.

"I feel like we know what we've got to do on Saturday to keep us in Group A and it's full focus and full steam ahead to do that," the attacking midfielder said.

"I think [the target] before we started this campaign was to try to remain in Group A.

"It's what we've worked so hard to do and to play against the best teams in Europe and ultimately down the line when the qualifying games start, it puts us in a better position the better we do in this group.

"I think a win on Saturday gives us a good chance of staying in Group A and then we have the massive game against Portugal on Tuesday [17 November], so ultimately the focus is on getting a win in Oslo on Saturday."

Wales could still finish in second, third or fourth place in their group.

A top-two spot would take Craig Bellamy's side to the Nations League quarter-finals in March 2027.

Finishing third would mean a relegation play-off against a League B team in March, while finishing bottom of the group would result in either a relegation play-off or direct relegation, depending on the ranking with other League A groups.

Crucially, finishing either second or third would mean Wales are seeded in the draw for the next European Championship.

The return of attacking midfielder Wilson is a significant boost for Bellamy's squad as they look to end a challenging Nations League campaign on a high.

The 29-year-old is yet to feature in the group having missed the four-game international window in September and October because of a muscle injury.

Wilson says he was "absolutely gutted" to pick up the fitness issue just before the previous international camp.

"Whoever came up with that four-game win, he needs sacking," he said with a smile.

"You get an injury a couple of days before that and it rules you up for four games and you should only miss two. Whoever's come up with that, it cost me another four games.

"For me personally, I'm itching to get back out there."

Harry Wilson signed a four-year contract at Leeds in the summer

Wilson is with the national squad for the first time as a Leeds United player.

With his Fulham contract up, Wilson signed for the Yorkshire club on a free transfer in July, joining fellow countrymen Joe Rodon, Ethan Amapdu, Daniel James and Jayden Lienou at Elland Road.

After a spectacular final season at Fulham, during which he was a first-team regular and scored 11 club goals, Wilson has had to be patient at Leeds.
