Dalot Struggles Again - Man Utd Players Rated and Slated After Villa Draw

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Manchester United will spend the entire international break in the relegation zone after drawing 4-4 at home to Aston Villa on Sunday afternoon.

Villa stormed out the blocks, stunning Old Trafford by establishing a two-goal lead inside the opening four minutes. First, Alysson swept the ball underneath Senne Lammens after John McGinn had found the Brazilian in space in the box. Then, Lammens spilt the first goalscorer's effort into the path of Nicolas Jackson, who tapped home from close range.

The Red Devils fought back, reducing the deficit to one goal when Bruno Fernandes converted from 12 yards, after Bounacar Kamara was adjudged to have brought Matheus Cunha down inside Villa's penalty area. This goal sparked Michael Carrick's outfit into life, dominating much of the rest of the first half. While they were largely limited to speculative long-range efforts, the home side did create a clear-cut opportunity for Amad Diallo shortly before the break, but the Ivorian was unable to finish after being slipped in by Youri Tielemans.

However, the equaliser did come moments later on the stroke of half-time when Noussair Marzaoui's cutback found Tielemans, who powered beyond a hapless Zion Suzuki.

Unai Emery's team started the second half just as they did the first, on the front foot and hungry for a lead. They were awarded a spot kick two minutes after the interval when Diogo Dalot tripped Alysson inside United's box. John McGinn made no mistake with the ensuing penalty, emphatically smashing the ball past Lammens to re-establish Villa's advantage.

The mountain got bigger for United to climb five minutes later as Leon Goretzka tapped in on the goal line following a bizarre backwards header from Yoro. This goal looked to have killed the game, which faded into a lull in the final half an hour as Villa attempted to preserve their lead.

Joshua Zirkzee gave United hope late on, tucking home nicely from a Patrick Dorgu pullback, before the Dane dramatically drew the Red Devils level with a header in the final minute. The result sees Carrick's outfit enter the international break in the bottom three, only spared the embarrassment of being rock bottom by a single point.

The United academy graduate was surprisingly astute defensively, snapping into tackles to prevent Villa from running at his team's vulnerable backline.

However, his passing was largely tame and conservative, failing to aid United's ball progression.
