Scott Parker interview after West Ham loss to Blackburn is causing a big reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The new West Ham United manager Scott Parker is still chasing his first win following a 2-1 defeat at Blackburn Rovers, and his post-match interview has caused a stir among fans.

It's been an eventful start for the new West Ham United manager Scott Parker, though probably not in the way that he'd intended. His first match as the club's new manager ended in a 3-3 draw at Cardiff City in which his team came back from three goals down, but his first Saturday match in charge ended with a 2-1 defeat at Blackburn Rovers which leaves them one place outside the Championship's play-off spots.

This result means that West Ham are now four games without a win, a run which came out of the blue somewhat, considering that they'd gone nine League games unbeaten prior to this, and Parker's post- match interview has provoked a response from West Ham fans which indicates how much work the new manager may have on his plate to start turning their season around again.

Sounding somewhat hoarse after his team's 2-1 defeat at Blackburn, Scott Parker spoke after the match about his disappointment at the result. Parker said that he'd talked about his team's need to get a result from this match, even if it wasn't playing the sort of football that he want his team to play.

He had sympathy for West Ham supporters, saying that he "wanted to get three points" for them, adding that, "What I can say is that I'm fully committed here, and I understand the pain, I understand where the fan base are."

Parker added that he understands that the unhappiness of fans isn't entirely a response to recent performances alone, saying that, "That's not just this season, it's come over a little bit of time."

And he admitted that he was also hurting from this result: "I think everyone's hurting here, and tonight I'm hurting because I understand that the only way we can change things is winning, and we have a big game in our next game at home, and we'll go from there." West Ham's next fixture is at home to bottom of the table Preston North End on the 21st November.

West Ham United's return to the Championship has been one of ups and downs. After only taking two points from their opening three games of the season, the team went on an excellent run which featured 6-0 wins against both Wrexham and Lincoln City, which took them all the way to the top of the table.

But the last four games have seen three defeats - two of which came at home - and a draw, and in the division which looks quite tight, this has seen them fall all the way down to ninth place, one place outside the play-off positions even though the EFL expanded the number of play-off spots in the Championship from four to six for this season.

And Parker's comments will have been at least partly provoked by the size of the club's travelling support. Just over 7,000 West Ham fans made the long trip up to Lancashire, making up over one-third of the crowd of 20,471. Such loyalty demands a better performance from the players, and although Parker stopped short of directly criticising the players, the extent to which the manager feels that his team needs to improve was very clear in his post-match interview.

With a 60,000-capacity stadium and Premier League parachute payments, West Ham have a significant competitive advantage over their Championship rivals this season, and this has been reflected in the unhappiness that has already been expressed by the club's fans over their downturn in form.

As Nuno Espirito Santo found to his cost, falling short of expectations can come at a cost, and his first two games in charge have already demonstrated just how much work Scott Parker has to do to get his team's season back on track.
