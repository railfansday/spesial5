10 Most Unhinged NPC Interactions in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Grand Theft Auto series is known for its absurdity, and it gave us some many memorable moments throughout its run. However, Grand Theft Auto 6 is taking the absurdity to a new level, with NPCs that are far more over-the-top than ever before.

Leonida is no stranger to the strange and weird, and since the first trailer for GTA 6, you could tell that it would be a place that is off the wall. There are old people running away from the cops on mobility scooters, people you can spot in… unfortunate positions, and even some strange moments that catch you completely off guard.

If you are planning on taking a trip across Leonida, whether it is by fan boat or sports car, get ready to see some strange happenings all across the state.

Shortly after you start up GTA 6, Brian will ask you to stop by a girl named Mandy’s house to collect the rent. This sounds like a fairly simple job, and there shouldn’t be anything that you have to worry about with it.

Of course, nothing is ever simple in GTA, and you have to put up with a girl who desperately needs a tall glass of Jason. She is perhaps the thirstiest character I have ever seen, and she consistently tries to lure Jason into a compromising position.

She goes so far as to catch Jason off guard by wearing next to nothing while he tries to fix her sink. Plus, you find out she clogged the sink herself to get Jason to come over. She has issues.

One of the many side characters in GTA 6 is Cal. He is Jason’s friend, and he is also one of the most absurd characters you will run into throughout the game. He constantly has something to say, and it is often hilarious.

During one of the nights early on in the game, Cal comes over and installs a JoyStation for Jason. The two of them rearrange the house, and then they have a few drinks while they chat through the night.

It ends with things getting a bit wild and the pair end up spooning in the bedroom. You awaken to a strange sight, and it fits Cal’s character perfectly.

Jason and Lucia are played by Dylan Rourke and Manni L. Perez, respectively.

It is normal for NPCs to get angry at you while you are driving in a Grand Theft Auto game. After all, you probably don’t drive very well and have little regard for the rules of the road. Well, the NPCs in GTA 6 take things to a new level.

Sometimes, when you bump into a vehicle while driving around Leonida, the NPC will get so angry that they chase you down. This can go on for a while, and the result is often the NPC plowing into your vehicle to stop you.

After this, they get out and assault you for hitting their car. It is a much bigger reaction than NPCs had in GTA 5 and it is something that fits in the land of Leonida.

Bert is a strange character who is obviously meant to signify the less reasonable members of the right-wing political ideology. He appears next to Mandy’s house with his pants down, flashing his genitalia to Mandy’s neighbors while screaming at them.

It is a strange sight to behold, but what is even more strange is that the landlord shows up to fight Bert. The man gets out of his truck, starts yelling at Bert, and tells him to get off his property.

However, things take a turn when they realize that they see eye-to-eye, with both of them wearing shirts of a similar fashion. Bert then gets offered a job while his family jewels are blowing in the wind.

There are plenty of random events that can happen in GTA 6, especially when you are exploring Vice City. Some of the NPCs around here get a little bit crazy. One such way is through their driving.

One of the game’s random events involves a car that comes careening down the highway only to take a sharp turn, flip, and get destroyed. It is a wild turn of events that you can get involved in.

If you stop to see if everyone is okay, the NPCs involved will freak out about the crash and high tail it out of there before the cops come. This leaves you with a bag of drugs that you can take to a stash house. Pretty convenient.

During one of the main story missions, Jason and Lucia have a night on the town, which leads them to a fun time in Vice City with Mattia. The night culminates in a massive party in a hotel room. During this party, however, Lucia gets separated from Jason.

While separated from Jason, you get to wander into various hotel rooms, and some of them are not for the faint of heart. For instance, one room has you walking in on a man dressed head to toe in leather, with a woman who is …inserting something into his body that is usually exit only.

It is a very unexpected scene that will catch you entirely off guard, and it is also one of the most unhinged things I have seen in a game between two consenting NPCs.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is a nominee for Game of the Year. It was selected at the last minute, but there are plenty of reasons it deserves the award.

There are some NPCs who are into doing some wild stuff in Leonida, and I am not just talking about wild in the bedroom like the NPC from earlier. Instead, I am talking about some extreme stunts. This one might also count as an easter egg, but it is an interesting interaction.

At the top of a radio tower, which just so happens to be one of the tallest spots in the game, you can find an influencer who is climbing the tower. She can be found hanging off of it, and if you interact with her, you will question why she is doing it.

She will respond by telling you that she climbed the tower because another influencer named Kelly Explores did it. Either way, it is weird and makes the list.

There are some pretty desperate NPCs in Grand Theft Auto 6. Some of them are willing to go to great lengths to make some money and get ahead in life. One guy in particular is willing to risk it all by robbing a store, but his weapon of choice isn’t a pistol, shotgun, or assault rifle.

Instead, it is a banana. That’s right, a banana. You can happen to walk into a store and see a man threatening the clerk, but while the store clerk can’t see the man’s weapon, you can clearly see that he is holding a banana behind his back.

You can put a stop to the man by threatening him, or you can watch the scene play out. Either way, it is pretty hilarious and pretty unhinged of him to attempt a robbery with produce as a weapon.

It is hilarious the type of people who think they can get away with something in GTA 6. For instance, there are some elderly folks who think that they can just rob people on a mobility scooter.

There are a few outcomes that this can have, but it all starts with a woman with a mullet driving around on a mobility scooter. She will pull a gun on someone, who will end up giving her their money.

She will then pull a gun on you, and you can choose to give her the money, wait for the cops to arrive, and see her get arrested, or you can decline. If you decline, she will curse at you and drive away. Better yet, you can just push her over and take her money.

There’s a chance that you will run into some NPCs in the Grassrivers who got their van stuck in the mud. This kind of thing happens to everyone, and so you may be inclined to help them out. After all, they will actually ask for your assistance.

If you help them out, they will reward you with some drugs and be on their way. Nothing too special, unless you end up looking in the back of the van, in which case, things get wild.

If you walk up to the back of the van, one of the men will tell you to stop, but it is too late. Inside, is a tiger, and opening the doors will cause it to jump out and pounce on one of the NPCs, taking his face clean off.

Grand Theft Auto 6 delivers an enormous open world, a gripping story, a vibrant Vice City and an astonishing range of activities.
