Anne Hathaway Welcomes Her Third Child with Husband Adam Schulman

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Congratulations are in order for Anne Hathaway and her husband, Adam Schulman. It appears the couple has welcomed their third child, after "The Devil Wears Prada" actress was spotted with a newborn in New York City on Nov. 17.

The photos, shared first by TMZ, depict Hathaway, 44, strolling around her hometown with a friend, and an infant snuggled up into a baby carrier.

The actress first shared news of her pregnancy in June, revealing a growing baby bump in an Instagram video captioned, "Baby, I'm yours."

Together, Hathaway and Schulman already have two sons: Jonathan Rosebanks Shulman, 10, and Jack Shulman, 7.

Shortly after sharing the news, Hathaway appeared on "Late Night with Seth Meyers," and said that so far, the experience of a third pregnancy had been "amazing."

She noted that she and her jewelry designer husband were surprised to learn they had another bundle of joy on the way.

"I mean, we knew what we were doing, but we were so shocked it worked," she revealed on the talk show. "We were so shocked it went this way, so we're calling this one our buzzer-beater," she joked.

According to a People magazine source, up until the announcement, Hathaway had worked diligently to keep the news of her pregnancy a secret during "The Devil Wears Prada 2" press tour.

"She wanted to reveal the pregnancy on her own terms. The press tour was about celebrating the movie and getting fans excited to see it," shared the source.

Press tours for "Verity" and "The Odyssey" have kept Hathaway busy shortly in the time since, but according to the source, she was able to "focus on family" and soak up "a little downtime" in the time between the films.

"She's grateful for where she is in her career and for the opportunity to be part of a film that means so much to so many people. Complaining just isn't Anne. She approaches things with gratitude and professionalism, and that's exactly how she handled the press tour," the source added.

Although Hathaway is a longtime New Yorker who originally hailed from New Jersey, the family primarily resides in an Ojai, CA home the couple purchased for $3.6 million in 2014.

The couple told Architectural Digest that it was a love at first sight situation with the property, and they knew they had to have it from the moment they first viewed it... even if it needed a handful of updates.

"It was the ideal combination of romance and great design," Hathaway said. "Our initial instinct was that this was going to be a very important place in our lives. I could really see raising a family here."

The 3,400-square foot home has a gorgeous Swiss chalet-inspired aesthetic that's been customized after the couple spent years renovating the home.

According to a previous listing, the home had four bedrooms and four bedrooms before it was purchased. However, records indicate that Hathaway and Schulman also purchased a parcel of land directly next to the property, so there's a good chance they may have expanded.

Based on baby's first public outing, it appears as though they may be splitting their time between the Sunshine State and the East Coast.

The "Princess Diaries" actress has previously shared glimpses of her personal life, explaining that she really lives for simple pleasures.

"I still get the paper delivered every day and I love sitting down with it," she told People magazine in an interview for her "World's Most Beautiful" cover story.

She explained that on a slow day, she loves cuddling with the family's new puppy, and finding time to speak to loved ones.

"I'm always zipping from one thing to another," she admitted. "I'd love to have breakfast with a friend. I'd love to go for a walk, maybe do a little window-shopping, maybe go to a museum. I'm very meandering on my days off."

In the interview, she also shared her appreciation for Schulman, noting that he "supports" her "completely," especially in a year as busy as the one she's having now.

"I'm so lucky that he's my partner that I spend my life with. If I didn't know that before this past year, I think I really know it know because with absolutely everything, he's just, he's on it. He holds it down," she gushed. "I hope that doesn't sound like I'm bragging, but he's a dream partner to me."
