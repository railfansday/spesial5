AlphaPoint Introduces Approval Device to Help Protect Businesses in an Era of AI-Driven Threats

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Dedicated hardware lets authorized employees review and approve sensitive requests separately from their computers, adding an independent checkpoint as attacks become harder to spot.

NEW YORK, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- AlphaPoint, a provider of institutional digital asset infrastructure since 2013, today introduced the Approval Device, a dedicated hardware device with its own secure display and an optional FIPS 140-3-certified secure element, designed to help businesses protect their most sensitive actions in an increasingly hostile, AI-driven threat environment.

Designed to integrate with existing systems, the Approval Device adds a hacker-resistant approval step to the workflows that matter most, such as administrative changes, payments and access to sensitive data. AI hacking makes laptops, workstations and mobile phones vulnerable, and the Approval Device helps protect administrative and security-critical workflows. Its software is written using security-first best practices and formally verified programming languages for safety and correctness, and it uses no complex operating system such as Linux, Windows or macOS, keeping its attack surface to an absolute minimum.

Even if an attacker has taken over an employee's other work devices, the Approval Device clearly shows the action about to be processed. Hardware wallets protect cryptocurrency transactions the same way, and the Approval Device brings that model to traditional business workflows beyond the blockchain.

An easy-to-use SDK lets human and AI programmers connect the device to the processes that matter most to your business. Once connected, it protects them from employee system compromise.

AlphaPoint is providing an evaluation kit to businesses, please contact us for more information.

Employee systems can perform consequential actions through business administration software, websites, and mobile applications. Multi-party approval flows offer enhanced security and continuity. During onboarding, humans and AI systems help identify what needs protection and who can approve those actions.

When one of those actions is requested, the designated approvers review it on the device's own screen and deliberately hold to approve or decline. A compromised computer can show one request while submitting another, but the device shows only the real one, and each approval is cryptographically bound to that exact request. Policy controls then confirm every required approval is in place before the action goes ahead.

"Attackers keep winning with the same classes of vulnerabilities. They rarely need anything new, because the old weaknesses keep working. We decided to stop fighting them one patch at a time. The Approval Device is designed to take entire classes of attack off the table, not just the latest example of each. That means a far smaller attack surface, far fewer ways in, and employee systems that are far harder to compromise," said Joe Ventura, CTO of AlphaPoint.

Potential applications include changes to administrative permissions, updates to critical system settings, releases of sensitive information and transfers of funds. Organizations can define approval requirements around the consequences of an action, including additional approvers for higher-risk requests.

The Approval Device works with Secure Core, which is designed to keep sensitive business logic, data and credentials separate from everyday application systems, protect keys in hardware, and keep signed, exportable records of every approval.

AlphaPoint works as a design partner to identify the workflows an organization needs to protect, define approval policies and support integration and validation within its existing systems. A 30-minute security briefing under NDA provides an opportunity to review the architecture, threat model and verification evidence.
