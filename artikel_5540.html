Silverfort Named a Leader in the IDC MarketScape for Worldwide Augmented Identity Security Solutions 2026 Vendor Assessment

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Silverfort's Runtime Access Protection acts as an identity control plane to stop AI attacks, securing identities from AD to AI

PLANO, Texas, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Silverfort, the Identity Security company, today announced it has been named a Leader in the IDC MarketScape: Worldwide Augmented Identity Security Solutions 2026 Vendor Assessment (doc# US54116526, November 2026).

The need for Augmented Identity SecurityIdentity Security is evolving. For years it focused on the front door: verify who is logging in, grant access, and trust policy to govern what happens next. That approach partially worked when identities were human, but quickly fell apart when machine identities, then AI agents, arrived on the scene. AI attacks target identity directly, and since the attack surface is outgrowing most organizations' ability to govern it, control has to extend beyond login to what happens next. Identity Security is now where and how organizations can control access, contain risk, and stop attacks. Silverfort protects identities at runtime, before access is granted, so teams can act on identity risk as it happens, without waiting for patches, policies or tech debt to be cleared.

IDC MarketScape's recommendation for when to consider Silverfort

In its guidance to buyers, IDC suggests considering Silverfort when, "Your organization wants visibility across on-premises and cloud identities, or runs Active Directory and legacy protocols that cannot take an agent or an application change, and wants real-time MFA, risk-based access, just-in-time access, and service-account protection on its existing stack, alongside vault, governance, and compliance tooling."

"For years, identity was a checkpoint, built wherever the technology allowed. Everywhere else, protection was taken on faith. Risk never agreed to that arrangement," said Emanuel Figueroa, Senior Research Analyst, Identity and Access Management Security, IDC. "Organizations need identity protections that reach modern and legacy infrastructure alike. Silverfort enforces controls at the authentication layer, including for systems and protocols that cannot support endpoint agents, which gives organizations a way to extend those protections to the environments they already operate."

"Not before the attack and not after it, but at the moment of impact. That's when Identity Security has to step up and stop attacks. AI-powered attacks don't break in, they log in, and they move faster than any human can respond," said Ron Rasin, Chief Strategy Officer of Silverfort. "It's great to see the IDC MarketScape recognize Identity Security as a category. It's a sign of how far it has come and of the market catching up to where it needs to go. Organizations now need to budget for protecting the identity layer, because that's where AI-driven attacks succeed or fail. We're proud to be named to the Leaders Category in the IDC MarketScape."
