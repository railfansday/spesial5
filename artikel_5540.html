Scout Motors has about 212,000 reservations as South Carolina plant nears completion

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

CEO Scott Keogh said reservations for the Terra truck and Traveler SUV could top 400,000 before customer production starts

Scout Motors, the Volkswagen-backed electric vehicle maker, has about 212,000 reservations for its Terra pickup truck and Traveler SUV, CEO Scott Keogh told CNBC.

Scout said on Thursday that construction of its $2 billion plant in South Carolina is in its final phase, according to CNBC. Keogh said the plant is roughly 85% finished, and crews are still installing related infrastructure as well as a supplier park valued at $300 million.

Full production of the two vehicles is expected to begin in 2027, with customer deliveries starting in 2028, according to CNBC.

Scout is building validation vehicles at the plant, with stamping, welding and paint operations under way, according to The Drive. Those builds are set to carry on into 2027 while the company gets ready for pre-production, according to The Drive. Scout also said most of the plant's manufacturing equipment is in place, The Drive reported.

“What we are building in Blythewood is no longer a promise on paper,” Keogh said in a statement.

Keogh told CNBC that reservations could top 400,000 ahead of the start of customer production. He said the bigger challenge is getting early enthusiasm to become buyers, and he expects Scout to have 12 to 18 months of orders lined up before production starts.

"The big thing for us is how much of the initial production can we 100% presell," Keogh said.

Reservations are no guarantee of sales, according to CNBC, which cited the Ford $F F-150 Lightning and Tesla $TSLA Cybertruck as electric trucks whose early excitement never grew into large numbers of buyers.

Scout will offer all-electric versions and extended-range electric vehicles, known as EREVs, which carry a gas engine that acts as a backup generator, according to CNBC. Slower adoption of electric vehicles, along with rising expenses, pushed Scout to work the EREVs into its plans.

Keogh said about 87% of reservations are for EREV models and 75% are for SUVs. He said Scout plans to launch the EREVs ahead of the all-electric versions and to begin with mid-level models rather than the highest-priced ones.

Keogh said the goal is to reach 100,000 units quickly, the point at which he said the company can "start to punch through."

Reservations opened in October 2024, when Scout revealed the Traveler and Terra, according to CNBC.
