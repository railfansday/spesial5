Morgan Stanley says S&P 500 correction is nearing an end, with a likely year-end rally ahead

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Strategists led by Mike Wilson say the S&P 500 is nearing the 7,100 to 7,300 downside target but advise waiting before buying the dip

Morgan Stanley $MS strategists see the stock market correction entering its final phase, with a likely year-end rally to follow, according to MarketWatch. Chief U.S. equity strategist Mike Wilson and his team advise investors to hold off on purchasing the pullback.

The correction began in June. Morgan Stanley expected its last phase to be a narrowing of S&P 500 breadth, according to MarketWatch. Wilson's team says that is now unfolding, with the index nearing the firm's downside target of 7,100 to 7,300 while breadth still sits well above its lows.

Morgan Stanley's strategists had said in late October that the level at which chip stocks find support may sit as much as 15% below where they trade. Wilson's team described chipmakers and other companies that profit from heavy capital spending as the "epicenter" of funding concerns. Wilson left the firm's year-end S&P 500 target at 8,000.

Technology stocks fell further in early November. The Nasdaq $NDAQ Composite fell 5.5% on Nov. 5, entering correction territory, as investors sold AI-related chip and data center stocks after the midterm elections. The S&P 500 shed 4.4%, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average lost more than 1,800 points, or 3.4%, that day.
