Infineon is raising its data center revenue forecast to nearly €4 billion

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The chipmaker forecasts fiscal 2027 revenue of about €20 billion, compared with €16.353 billion in fiscal 2026

Infineon raised its fiscal 2027 forecast for data center revenue to close to €4 billion ($4.5 billion) as it reported fourth-quarter results on Tuesday. The German chipmaker said revenue from power supplies for data centers is projected to reach that level in the year ending in September 2027.

Infineon forecast total revenue of roughly €20 billion ($22.4 billion) for fiscal 2027, up from the €16.353 billion it booked in fiscal 2026, assuming a EUR/USD exchange rate of 1.15. The company expects a segment result margin in the mid-20s percent band, up from the 19.7% it recorded for fiscal 2026.

Infineon's earlier forecast covered AI data center power solutions only, at €2.5 billion, according to Bloomberg. The new figure combines those solutions with traditional data centers, which generate about €500 million a year.

JPMorgan $JPM analysts had expected fiscal 2027 data center revenue of between €3.5 billion and €4 billion, according to Bloomberg.

"Supported by an ongoing cyclical recovery and strong demand in our structural growth areas, we expect a significant increase in revenue and profitability in fiscal year 2027," CEO Jochen Hanebeck said in a statement. He cited electromobility, software-defined vehicles, energy infrastructure and semiconductor solutions for data center power supplies as key contributors.

Fourth-quarter revenue was €4.706 billion, with a segment result of €1.119 billion and a margin of 23.8%. Fiscal 2026 revenue rose 12% from a year earlier. For the first quarter of fiscal 2027, Infineon expects revenue of about €4.6 billion and a segment result margin of about 23%.

For fiscal 2027, the company is guiding to an adjusted gross margin between the mid- and high-40s in percentage terms, and it has earmarked €3.0 billion to €3.5 billion for investment. It forecast adjusted free cash flow of about €2.2 billion and free cash flow of about €1.8 billion.

The main investment areas are production equipment at the Dresden facility in Germany, capacity for silicon carbide in Malaysia and gallium nitride output using 300-millimeter wafers in Villach, Austria, according to Bloomberg.

Infineon's management board intends to propose a dividend of €0.38 a share, up €0.03, at the annual general meeting in February 2027. Infineon stock rose 3.3% to €50.23 in Frankfurt trading on Tuesday morning.
