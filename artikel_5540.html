French bank Crédit Mutuel Arkéa said hackers stole money in cyberattack

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The French lender said customer assets were not affected, while reports put the amount taken at $133 million, a figure it has not confirmed

Crédit Mutuel Arkéa said it was the target of a cyberattack on Wednesday that enabled fraudulent transactions at the French bank.

The bank said the incident has been contained and operations are resuming during the morning, after the group and its subsidiaries completed verifications and added security measures. It said customers' assets were not affected and that the attack delayed the execution of certain transfers.

Hackers took money from the bank once they had broken into its IT networks, according to Bloomberg. A bank spokesperson declined to say how much was stolen.

Reports put the amount at €118 million ($133 million), according to AFP. The bank has not confirmed that figure, which independent investigator ZachXBT first made public earlier this week. The bank said the amounts affected are still being assessed and would not undermine its financial strength and soundness.

ZachXBT said the stolen funds were moved via several accounts set up at cryptocurrency exchanges, according to AFP. The bank has not disclosed the method of the attack or identified the party responsible.

After detecting the attack, the bank said, it contacted the relevant authorities and supervisory bodies. As a precaution, it briefly halted some transactions to protect its operations and stop additional fraudulent transfers.

The bank warned that phishing attempts are possible following the attack. It urged customers to share no personal or banking information, including login IDs, passwords, bank card numbers and SMS validation codes. Customers who are in doubt about a message should contact their bank adviser directly, it said.

Crédit Mutuel Arkéa, based in Brest, includes the federations Crédit Mutuel de Bretagne and Crédit Mutuel du Sud-Ouest. It has more than 5.8 million members and customers and total assets of €211.5 billion.

The bank said its teams remain mobilized to assess the consequences of the attack and strengthen security, and that it will provide updates as new information becomes available.
