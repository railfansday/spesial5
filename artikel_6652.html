Anne Hathaway Is a Mom of Three

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

After a busy year of projects, the actress has welcomed her baby

Anne Hathaway is a mom of three! The Devil Wears Prada 2 star has welcomed her third child with husband Adam Shulman. Per a new TMZ report, she was spotted walking around New York City with her newborn earlier today. She was spotted carrying the baby in a brown carrier as she walked with a friend while in a casual winter fit, the outlet reported. No photos have been published just yet.

The Mother Mary star’s newborn joins brothers Jonathan and Jack, who were born in 2016 and 2019, respectively.

As readers may recall, Hathaway announced her third pregnancy with a sweet video shared on Instagram in June. In the clip, she is seen walking into the frame in a breezy white set which reveals her growing baby bump. “X Baby, I’m yours x” she wrote in the caption, as Barbara Lewis’s “Baby, I'm Yours” played as the soundtrack.

Hathaway then spent the rest of the year on the red carpet, promoting her many, many film projects—from Mother Mary to The Odyssey, The Devil Wears Prada 2, Verity, and The End of Oak Street. And she did it all while cradling her baby bump in a parade of designer looks, sporting everything from Cult Gaia to Carolina Herrera, Prabal Gurung, McQueen, and La Ligne. (Take a glance back at her winning maternity style streak here.)

Hathaway and Schulman, who married in 2012, have yet to reveal the sex or name of their third child or confirm the news of the baby’s birth.
