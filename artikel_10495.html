Shaq reality show makes contestants hide him in plain sight like viral meme

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

NBA legend Shaquille O’Neal is getting his own hide-and-seek TV show, where contestants will have to figure out how to hide the 7-foot-1 basketball star in plain sight.

Shaq is one of the most recognizable athletes on the planet, having spent nearly two decades in the NBA before becoming a television personality and businessman.

But while his enormous size helped him dominate on the basketball court, it would make him a nightmare to hide in public, as we all know from the viral meme of him hiding behind a tree.

That’s the challenge facing eight contestants in a new reality competition series called “Can You Hide… Shaq?,” which is set to premiere on ABC in December.

As reported by Variety, the series will see contestants attempt to disguise the former basketball player and prevent members of the public from recognizing him around Dallas, Texas.

Hosted by comedian Nicole Byer, the show begins with the eight competitors splitting into two teams, each tasked with completing challenges within a time limit.

The team that manages to complete its mission while getting Shaq spotted the fewest times will earn immunity for one member, who becomes captain in the following round.

Contestants will also compete to build up a cash prize known as the “Shaq-pot,” with one eventual winner taking home the money.

“I’m 7-foot-1. I’m not exactly easy to miss,” Shaq said in a statement. “I love meeting people wherever I go, but for years I’ve always wondered, could somebody actually make me disappear? Well, we finally decided to put it to the test.”

The competition will take place at various locations around Dallas, including the Perot Museum of Nature and Science and Reunion Tower.

Can You Hide… Shaq? premieres on December 3, with another two episodes following on December 10. But whether anyone can successfully hide one of basketball’s literal biggest stars remains to be seen.
