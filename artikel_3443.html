Macaulay Culkin Officially Returns as ‘Home Alone’s Kevin McCallister

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

Macaulay Culkin gets recognized ten times more between November and January, by his own estimate, something he refers to as having “cornered the fourth quarter market.” Kevin McCallister spent Home Alone trying to get everybody to leave him alone, and now the holidays are when people are most likely to stop Culkin in the street.

Culkin sees it at holiday screenings, too. Adults who grew up watching Kevin take on the Wet Bandits are now bringing kids who have no idea what’s about to happen when Harry grabs that doorknob. Culkin has seen that handoff in his own living room, too. In a new interview with Collider's Hannah Hunt, he expressed that the movie has “taken on its own life,” which goes a long way toward explaining why he’s still game to play Kevin 36 years later.

Macaulay Culkin is officially back as Kevin McCallister for McDonald’s new holiday campaign. The limited-time Home Alone Meal starts rolling out internationally on November 24 and hits participating U.S. restaurants on December 1, while supplies last. Each U.S. meal comes with one of four collectible sock designs inspired by the movie, and the menu runs from a Sausage, Egg & Cheese McGriddles at breakfast to a Big Mac at lunch or dinner.

Little Nero’s may still be the only place Kevin can order a lovely cheese pizza, but McDonald’s is putting that flavor on its fries. The lunch and dinner meals come with Little Nero’s Pizza McShaker Fries, and there’s a Lovely Cheese Pizza Dipping Sauce you can get with McNuggets or McCrispy Strips. The Everything Sundae McFlurry is sold separately and comes with hot fudge, Oreo pieces, and M&M’s. Check out the lineup below. Fans in Los Angeles can also register for the Home Alone Challenge, a pop-up running December 4 and 5 from 5 to 10 p.m. PT. There’s a ball pit, a slingshot challenge, and a free Home Alone Meal for fans who complete the experience.

Culkin’s first thought about bringing Kevin back for McDonald’s was that “it’s about time.” He put the movie and the menu in the same category. You know what the McNuggets are going to taste like, just as you know exactly what’s going to happen when Marv takes his shoes off. You order them and put the movie on anyway. “The actual food and the movie are comfort food, really,” Culkin said. His wife, Brenda Song, was excited about the collaboration, too. “Brenda thinks it’s really cool,” he said. “And that’s really what matters most.”

Culkin had “no notes” when he saw the meal, and he decided to unbox the collectible socks right there on the call. He gave Grimace his due as “the most famous McDonald’s character of all” before holding up a pair with his own face on them. “If you ever wanted to walk on my face,” he joked, “you can just go to McDonald’s, go get yourself some socks.”

As for what Kevin would be like as a grown-up, Culkin figures he’d still get a kick out of “defying authority," which is part of why the movie works so well on kids in the first place. They spend all day being told what they can and can’t do, and here’s somebody their size who gets to tell two grown men exactly where they can step. Culkin thinks the fantasy holds up when you’re old enough to pay for your own pizza, too. “You always have a boss,” he said.

At Culkin’s house, one kid has taken that identification with Kevin a little further. Neither of Culkin’s sons knows their dad played Kevin McCallister, even though they’ve watched Home Alone with him. “They don’t know it’s me,” he told Collider. His oldest, Dakota, has his own explanation. “He thinks he is Kevin.” He looks so much like Culkin did at that age that even a cousin saw the poster while scrolling through a menu and asked why Dakota was in the movie.

The boys ask plenty of other questions while they watch, but Culkin can see Dakota only now starting to put that particular one together. He’s already guessed how the news will break: some kid on the playground, probably named Lenny or Nino, will tell Dakota before he gets the chance. Culkin figures he’ll sit him down afterward and have what he calls a “Beaver Cleaver” moment. Until then, Culkin is happy to let Dakota watch Home Alone and see himself in it, comparing the whole thing to letting his kids believe in Santa Claus:

“You want to keep that magic as long as possible."

For now, there’s still time for another Christmas where Dakota gets to be Kevin and his dad gets to sit beside him and watch. The Home Alone Meal comes to McDonald’s in the U.S. on December 1. The movies are available to stream on Disney+.
