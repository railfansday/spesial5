Prime Video has become a major destination for military action thrillers, particularly stories built around elite operators, high-stakes missions, and complicated conspiracies. The genre has given the streamer several opportunities to build franchises around recognizable characters, with audiences returning for stories that extend well beyond a single season.

That strategy has also helped Prime Video develop a growing slate of adaptations based on popular action novels. When one of those shows connects with viewers, it can become more than a one-season success — and the streamer's latest streaming numbers suggest one military thriller still has plenty of life left in it.

The series making the biggest impression is The Terminal List. According to Nielsen's latest streaming report, covering the week of October 19 through October 25, the Prime Video thriller secured the fifth spot on the overall top 10 list after accumulating 830 million minutes watched.

That's a substantial audience for the series, especially as it continues to build on the success of its first season. Starring Chris Pratt as James Reece, The Terminal List follows a Navy SEAL whose entire team is ambushed during a covert mission. After returning home, Reece begins investigating what happened, uncovering a conspiracy that reaches far beyond the operation itself.

Based on Jack Carr's novel of the same name, the series combines military action with a conspiracy thriller, giving it an appeal that extends beyond viewers who typically seek out war dramas. Its latest streaming performance is another indication that the franchise has maintained an audience even as Prime Video expands its collection of action-oriented originals.

The show's future is also looking promising. The Terminal List has recently received another major update, with a new season confirmed alongside plans involving Carr's Savage Son. That announcement gives viewers another reason to keep following the franchise, and the latest news about Chris Pratt's Terminal List future offers more details on what's ahead.

Part of the show's appeal comes from Pratt's performance as Reece, a character who is forced to navigate the aftermath of extreme violence while trying to determine who he can trust. The series also benefits from its grounded military details and willingness to make its action sequences central to the story rather than simply using them as background spectacle.

The franchise has continued to develop since its first season, with its second installment giving Reece another chapter in his story. The Terminal List season 2's Rotten Tomatoes score offers another look at how the latest episodes have been received, adding a critical perspective to the show's continued streaming performance.

Carr's work also gives Prime Video plenty of material to explore beyond Reece's story. The author has developed a larger collection of military thrillers, and viewers interested in similar adaptations will soon be able to tune in to a new detective series with a military twist called Worth in the works.

A Navy SEAL commander returns home after a disastrous covert mission leaves his entire platoon dead, only to find conflicting memories and growing evidence of a far reaching conspiracy. When tragedy strikes his family, grief turns to determination as he hunts those responsible, uncovering corruption that reaches deep into the military, government, and intelligence communities.
