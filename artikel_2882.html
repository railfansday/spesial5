Bucs add LT Tristan Wirfs to the practice report with a toe injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Buccaneers added left tackle Tristan Wirfs to the injury report on Thursday.

Wirfs did not practice with a toe injury.

Wide receiver Emeka Egbuka (foot) returned to full participation after sitting out Wednesday, and running back Kenny Gainwell (hip) went from limited on Wednesday to a full participant on Thursday.

Defensive lineman Elijah Roberts (personal) returned to full work after missing Wednesday's session.

Outside linebacker Yaya Diaby (knee) is the only player who has not practiced this week.

Safety Tykee Smith (ankle) had a second consecutive limited practice.
