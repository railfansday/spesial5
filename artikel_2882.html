Rams activate Myles Garrett, put Jaylen Watson on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Myles Garrett is officially back on the Rams' active roster.

The Rams announced on Saturday that they have activated Garrett from injured reserve. Head coach Sean McVay had already confirmed that the linebacker will play for the first time since Week 1 and the roster move clears the way for him to take the field against the Commanders on Sunday.

Running back Ronnie Rivers will also be available after being activated from injured reserve. Tight end Terrance Ferguson was expected back as well, but was scratched on Friday with a hamstring injury.

Cornerback Jaylen Watson will not play as he suffered a season-ending shoulder injury. He was placed on injured reserve Saturday and the Rams also elevated long snapper Jake McQuaide and cornerback Damarion Williams from the practice squad.
