Oregon Ducks Football: Duck Tape Film Review Ohio State Buckeyes 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Author's note: A full transcript of the video will be available by Monday evening.

Here is a written account of my conclusions about the game for those who prefer to use the video only for play breakdowns.

Oregon won this game because they were able to exploit Ohio State's defense for big plays in the passing game, while the Buckeyes could not hit explosive plays to WR #4 Jeremiah Smith.

Oregon's offense was not efficient, but Ohio State was not consistent enough pressuring QB #8 Dylan Raiola who hit big plays when his receivers found holes in zone coverage.

The Ducks' defense shut down the short passing game the Buckeyes committed to with backup QB #13 Fahey in, and Ohio State's run game was never a threat.

The game definitively entered garbage time when the Ducks took a 38-14 lead early in the 4th quarter. This occurred at the end of Oregon's 11th meaningful possession, whereas Ohio State only had 10 before they were too far behind to come back.

The following players listed as "out" on the game day availability report are presumed lost for the season: QB #5 Dante Moore, DB #21 Aaron Flowers, ILB #23 Brayden Platt, and OL #56 Trent Ferguson. In addition both DL #55 Matthew Johnson and OL #74 Dave Iuli have been listed as out for several weeks in a row. For, presumably, different reasons WR #15 Messiah Hampton was also listed as out and will likely remain so indefinitely.

Both starting running backs, RB #0 Jordon Davison and RB #6 Dierre Hill, exited last weeks game and didn't return. Davison was out again this week, but Hill started and received the majority of the carries.

In some good news, WR #3 Jeremiah McClellan was listed as a "Game Time Decision" after breaking his foot during warm ups before the Portland State game. I only spotted him once when he caught a short five yard pass in the second quarter.

Oregon's offense punished soft zone coverage with intermediate throws for explosive plays in the passing game against Ohio State's defense. A couple well timed interior screens against Buckeye blitzes also resulted in explosive gains on touchdown drives.

The offense benefited from favorable field position thanks to defense and special teams.

Informed fans should not let the recent success mask lingering weaknesses with this offense:

The offensive line is stable but still notably underperforming relative to what this unit should look like given the talent and experience in the room.

QB #8 Dylan Raiola has once more proven he can be effective but he still has his limitations.

There is some lingering irrationality in play calling (e.g., running too much between the tackles against this defensive structure and too many perimeter screens).

There were no changes to the offensive line from the previous two weeks. Oregon did break out their "TACO" look, meaning "tackle over" or moving an offensive tackle into a tight end position and pushing the tight end into a tackle spot, on one play but that was it.

Ohio State's first touchdown drive was the result of a cluster of otherwise sparse errors made by the Ducks' defense in this game.

The second touchdown was the result of Buckeyes' WR #4 Jeremiah Smith being a "glitch in the Matrix" and a coverage bust.

After this Ohio State didn't trust backup QB #13 Luke Fahey to consistently get the ball to Smith deep, and made the decision to use Smith as a decoy while feeding other targets on short routes. The Ducks never took the bait, didn't over commit to Smith, and without a reliable run game the Buckeyes' other pass catchers weren't productive enough to score again until garbage time.

We continue to see DB #4 Brandon Finney bounce between nickel and outside corner. The other dedicated corners CB #2 Na'eem Offord and CB #7 Ify Obidegwu play outside exclusively while DB #5 Davon Benjamin rotated in at nickel and is also the dedicated dime back. Boundary safety is now a three man rotation with Jr. DB #12 Peyton Woodward getting the most reps but playing time also going to freshmen DB #24 Jett Washington and DB #22 Devin Jackson.
