Carson Beck dealing with back spasms, Josh Johnson in line to start vs. Chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Cardinals may have a fourth starting quarterback in four weeks when they play the Chiefs on Sunday.

Rookie Carson Beck is once again dealing with back spasms, which puts veteran backup Josh Johnson in line to start in Week 11.

Beck made his regular-season debut last week in the 34-6 loss to the Rams. He was able to practice in full on Wednesday but was listed as limited for Thursday’s session.

The rookie QB was previously sidelined with back spasms earlier in the season.

“It’s basically just responding to the treatment, see how he feels when he wakes up tomorrow,” head coach Mike LaFleur said in his Friday press conference.

LaFleur added that he doesn’t think Beck will be out multiple weeks with the injury, but he’s unsure at this point.

“You go with the people who know what they’re doing to treat it. It’s not like this is the first time he’s dealt with it,” LaFleur said. “We’ll see more tomorrow.”

If Johnson does start, it will be his first time playing for Arizona in the regular season and add to his long list of clubs. Johnson has appeared in regular-season games for seven teams, including the Jets back in 2021 when LaFleur was the team’s offensive coordinator.

“Cool part is, if it is Josh, I’ve called plays for him before and had a really great experience calling plays for him,” LaFleur said. “So, we’ll go shoot our shot and I expect nothing less from our guys.”

Still, the instability within Arizona’s QBs room is remarkable. In Week 8, Jacoby Brissett made his last start for the club before Gardner Minshew came in and engineered a comeback to send the game to overtime, ultimately beating the Cowboys. Minshew then started Week 9 against Seattle, but suffered a season-ending elbow injury in the loss. Beck made his first start last Sunday and now Johnson is in line to be under center against Kansas City.

The Cardinals’ full injury report with game statuses will be released later on Friday.
