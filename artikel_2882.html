‘Crazy’: Chandler Parsons shares what’s really ‘weird’ about watching Victor Wembanyama right now

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Victor Wembanyama made the world stop and take note of his performance for the San Antonio Spurs against the Dallas Mavericks.

The Spurs were the slight favourites to win the game because of their French superstar, and he more than delivered for them on the floor, scoring over 50 points.

Everyone in the basketball world was in awe of his display, including Chandler Parsons, who says he finds it weird to watch Wembanyama now.

Wembanyama was on another level when the San Antonio Spurs faced the Dallas Mavericks.

The Frenchman scored 52 points and also recorded 24 rebounds, two assists, two steals and eight blocks in one of the most dominant performances we've ever seen.

Wembanyama has publicly claimed that he wants to win the MVP award this season, and numbers like this could help him achieve his dream.

Parsons was really impressed by the Spurs star, but he also found it 'weird' because it reminded him of playing basketball with his son and his friends.

He said on the Run It Back show: "It's just weird watching this dude play basketball. It's so crazy how you just watch him, flat-footed, just beat dude's shots up and alter the entire game defensively. It really is crazy.

"It reminds me of when I play with my son and his little friends in a Nerf hoop game, and I'm just beating the ball all over the place. It's weird.

"I don't know how we got here, and I don't know how it continues to amaze me, but offensively and defensively, the way this guy just gets snatches, rebounds, puts the ball behind his back, gets out in transition after…

"There was one possession where he blocked Cooper Flagg, he blocked PJ Washington, he blocked Daniel Gafford, he had three blocks in like 30 seconds. I was like, "What the hell am I watching?" Like, it really was mesmerising.

"So, just to see this is fun to watch. And the way he just completely takes teams away at offence is… You'll see a guy dribble, and then they'll see Wemby and they literally crab-dribble backwards because they just don't want any smoke. It really is unbelievable!"

We're witnessing a true great in Wembanyama, but what's crazy is that he's nowhere near his prime. The French star is still only 22 years old, which means he will only get better going forward.

That is crazy, not for the San Antonio Spurs, but for every other team in the league.
