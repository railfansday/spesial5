Drake Maye likely out for season with fractured fibula

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

There is more information on the injury sustained by Drake Maye and it’s not good news for the Patriots.

According to a report from ESPN, Maye suffered a fractured right fibula during last week’s loss to the Lions in Munich. While Maye is not dealing with any additional ligament damage, he’s is set to be out for the vast majority of the regular season — if not all of it.

Maye is likely to return only if New England is still in contention for a postseason spot.

While Maye is a candidate to be placed on injured reserve, the Patriots will have to figure out their roster spots because they’ve had so many players on the list this season. Only eight players can return from IR in any given season per team.

In 10 games this season, Maye has completed 65.6 percent of his passes for 2,330 yards with 18 touchdowns and 11 interceptions.

Notably, Maye will be eligible for a contract extension after the conclusion of the 2026 season.
