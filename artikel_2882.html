Rapid Reaction: Northwestern’s comeback bid falls short in 76-71 loss to UIC

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Northwestern (1-2) was not able to overcome a 21-point deficit against UIC, falling 76-71 to give UIC (2-1) its first win against a Big Ten team since 2013.

Jasmyn Cooper-Derba scored a career-high 22 points and Casey Harter notched 17 points. But much of the offense for the 'Cats came in the second half, when the team was already down large. The defense also struggled to contain UIC's Julia Coleman, who had 29 points — including 19 in the first half.

The biggest thing to monitor after this game is the status of Lily Carmody, as she suffered an apparent head injury in the third quarter. She was eventually able to re-enter the game in the fourth quarter, but took another hit to around the same area which clearly shook her up. She had scored 13 points prior to getting hurt.

It was an ugly start as both teams struggled with turnovers and fouls. Cooper-Derba eventually opened up the scoring with a driving layup at the 7:34 mark. However, Northwestern's offense continued to falter as UIC went on a 10-2 run. Coleman's 10 first-quarter points powered UIC's scoring attack. Northwestern went an abysmal 4-for-16 from the field and 1-for-9 from deep, and got crushed on the glass, giving up five offensive rebounds and eight second chance points.

Things seemingly got worse, as near the end of the first period of action, Cooper-Derba got a bit hobbled underneath the basket, but appeared to shake off the injury and later re-entered the game.

The second period also opened up sloppily, as both teams turned the ball over on their opening possessions. The Flames eventually found success down low in the paint with guard Tutu Clayton delivering a beautiful pass to center Evyn Carrier. Carrier would continue to be a problem for the 'Cats as she drew fouls on Camdyn Nelson and Cooper-Derba, getting rebounds and blocking shots.

UIC jumped out to a 12-0 run as NU went 0-for-6 from the field and its defense couldn't stop Coleman, who finished with 19 first-half points. Casey Harter struggled to find her shot in the first half, going 2-for-6, which hampered the Wildcats' offense. Lily Carmody helped bring some life to NU's offense; she ended the drought and cut into the 36-16 deficit with a triple. She went on to score an additional eight points and finished with 13 at halftime, including going 3-for-3 from beyond the arc. However, despite her efforts, UIC still held a 45-29 lead at the break, largely because of Coleman's scoring.

Northwestern came out much stronger in the third quarter, opening up with a 7-2 run to bring the deficit down to 11. Harter was putting the pressure on defensively, forcing turnovers and disrupting Clayton's ball handling.

Unfortunately, after going up for a layup and getting called for an offensive foul, Carmody took a tough fall and got injured, forcing her to head to the bench. However, her offense was picked up, as Cooper-Derba, Harter and Nelson scored nine, eight and six points, respectively.

Northwestern put up a much better effort on the boards, outrebounding UIC 10-to-5. NU's defense also improved dramatically, allowing only three field goals, which helped them chip away at the lead as the offense found its groove. At the end of three quarters, Northwestern had cut the deficit to just five points, 57-52.

The final quarter began with Harter's aggressiveness, as she opened up with a layup to make it a one-score game. UIC struggled to find the brilliance it put on display in the first half, turning the ball over and missing open looks. However, Coleman continued to be a threat, finding her way to the charity stripe multiple times, helping to extend UIC's lead back out to eight with less than six minutes remaining.

Northwestern was not able to find consistent offense down the stretch of the game. The 'Cats did not score a field goal for over two minutes, and when they did there was less than a minute remaining. UIC also started to dominate the offensive glass, securing six in the fourth quarter. This helped the Flames burn the clock and limit Northwestern's possessions. And despite a valiant Northwestern effort to gain more shot attempts by forcing turnovers and quick baskets, time ran out, and UIC emerged victorious, 76-71.

The Wildcats will have a full week of practice before heading out on the road again for a matchup with No. 24 West Virginia on Friday.
