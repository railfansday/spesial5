Parsons’ return imminent but doesn’t solve Packers’ biggest problem

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

For all of the Green Bay Packers' issues through ten games of the 2026 season, getting to the quarterback generally hasn't been one of them. The Packers' defense ranks fourth in the NFL in sacks this year, with pressure helping the team rank second in interceptions and fifth in overall passer rating against.

Now the cavalry appears set to arrive. Micah Parsons is on his way back, seemingly with the intent of playing next Wednesday against the Los Angeles Rams, and his presence will only further bolster the team's ability to get after the quarterback. It remains to be seen how he will be deployed — expect a light workload for the next few games, perhaps with him only playing on passing downs — but his return should provide an emotional lift as well as a physical one for a defense that's already playing well.

As that last phrase suggests, though, the defense isn't really the Packers' problem right now. That is of course the offensive line, where some recent front office decisions have helped set the table for a catastrophic season up front. One can't help wondering if this team would be functioning better had Elgton Jenkins been kept around for one more year; had the team not invested so much money in Aaron Banks; had Anthony Belton — wait, the front office drafted him as a guard and he's been thrust in at tackle by circumstance and the coaching staff, so that one probably isn't on the front office.

And then there is that coaching staff. From a poor overall offensive game plan last week against Minnesota to players seemingly having no idea what they're doing on a play-to-play basis, the offensive coaches should have spent their few off days in a shortened bye week doing some serious self-evaluation.

So, while Parsons' return is an exciting storyline for the Packers, it almost feels like we're adding the whipped cream on top of an excellent pumpkin pie while smoke is billowing out of the oven as the turkey burns. So let's get that main course under control, yeah?
