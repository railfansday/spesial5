Hudson football score vs St. Edward High School, OHSAA playoffs live updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

LAKEWOOD — Hudson faces one the biggest challenges in school history, as the OHSAA high school football playoffs move to the regional semifinal round.

No. 4 seed Hudson (9-2) travels to Lakewood Stadium Friday, Nov. 13, to take on top-seeded St. Edward (10-1) in a Division I, Region 1 semifinal. This is the first game between the two since a Division I state semifinal in 2014.

Hudson will look to upset the Eagles, who have advanced to the state semifinals each of the last five years.

Here are live results from Lakewood Stadium:

Hudson will look for QB Hayden Lipinski to continue his playoff form with both his arm and legs. Lipinski went 13-of-16 passing for 122 yards and two TD vs. Massillon Jackson in Week 12, along with 106 yards and a TD rushing. For the season, Lipinski has gone 86-of-135 passing for 1,002 yards with 15 TDs and three interception. RB Reno Ferri continues to have a solid season. With 144 rushing yards and two TDs against Jackson, he upped his season totals to 1,499 yards and 22 TDs. WR Anthony Paolucci has been Lipinski's favorite target with 31 catches for 438 yards and six TDs. LB Trent Smith has been a monster in the playoffs for the Hudson defense, and he had three sacks and several hurries against Jackson. Fellow linebacker Carter Markey leads Hudson with five interceptions. …

St. Edward's top weapon is QB Langston Lucas, who is 100-of-163 passing for 1,415 yards with 16 TDs and six picks this season. He also is the Eagles' top rusher, with 777 yards and seven TDs. RB Richard Neal has run for 499 yards and four TDs. Lucas' favorite target is TE Joe Moore, who has 36 catches for 607 yards and eight TDs.

Hudson coach Jeff Gough is in his 11th year in charge of Explorers. His record stands at 92-38.

Tom Lombardo is the head coach at St. Edward. He led the Eagles to state tiles in 2023, 2022, 2021, 2018 and 2015.

The winner of this game will face the winner of the Mentor-Canton McKinley game in the Division I, Region 1 regional final Friday, Nov. 20 at a neutral site.
