Are the Seahawks playing today? NFL Week 11 schedule, next game

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Sam Darnold has spent the last two Seattle Seahawks games on the sideline. The bye gives him an extra week to get off it.

Seattle is off in Week 11, and the break lands at the right time. Darnold hurt his ribs and quad in the Nov. 2 loss to the Chicago Bears and hasn't played since. The Seahawks are optimistic he'll be back when they visit San Francisco on Nov. 29.

Drew Lock has kept the defending Super Bowl champions afloat. He beat the Arizona Cardinals 17-3, then threw for 240 yards and two touchdowns in a 21-13 win over the Las Vegas Raiders on Nov. 15. Lock is 3-0 as a starter this season. He also rallied Seattle past New England in the opener after Darnold got hurt on the first drive. He still hasn't thrown an interception.

In Las Vegas, Jaxon Smith-Njigba did most of the damage. He caught eight passes for 171 yards, and his 71-yard touchdown in the third quarter put the game away. He was over 1,000 receiving yards by Seattle's eighth play.

The defense sacked Raiders rookie Fernando Mendoza six times in his first NFL start. It hasn't allowed a 100-yard rusher in 39 straight games, playoffs included.

The line needs the week, too. Left tackle Charles Cross (ankle) and left guard Quenton Nelson (knee) sat out in Las Vegas, and right tackle Abraham Lucas got hurt in the second quarter.

At 6-4, with three wins in four games, Seattle is still 2 1/2 games behind the 8-1 49ers in the NFC West. The Seahawks are eighth in the NFC, one spot outside the playoff field, with the same record as the Green Bay Packers and Los Angeles Rams.

The 49ers beat them 32-29 in Seattle in October. They get another shot after the bye.
