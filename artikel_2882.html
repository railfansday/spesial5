Kansas State vs Arizona injury report: Who's in, who's out?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

MANHATTAN — The final injury update ahead of Kansas State football's final home game of the season was released 90 minutes before kickoff.

K-State hosts Arizona at 6 p.m. CT at Bill Snyder Family Stadium in Manhattan, on Saturday, Nov. 21.

K-State continues to be without quarterback Avery Johnson, who has missed the last four games. He is expected to miss the remainder of the season.

Kansas State will also be without defensive tackle Eli Jackson, who had been emerging in the middle of the Wildcats' defensive line. In a game that is expected to be impacted by rain, Arizona will likely have to run more than it has most of the year.
