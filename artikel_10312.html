Google Health update fixes my biggest Fitbit Air complaint with ‘in-progress’ workout card

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Google’s Fitbit Air is one of my favorite products of 2026 and my preferred workout companion, but not being able to see a workout while you’re in the middle of it has been a huge complaint of mine, and the latest Google Health update is fixing that.

Quite literally at the start of this week, I was on a walk through my neighborhood and though to myself, “it’s still pretty annoying that I can’t see my workout progress in the Google Health app.” It’s been a complaint of mine for a while, as various treadmill sessions and more have left me wondering what my heart rate is looking like, how many steps I’ve taken, etc, and it’s only a problem when using the Fitbit Air, seeing as the Pixel Watch (or Fitbit Edge) I’d wear otherwise will show that data front and center.

In the changelog for Google Health 5.13, Google details a new “exercise in-progress” card on the main “Today” screen. This will show what exercise is being tracked and also let you end the workout early.

If you use your Pixel Watch, Fitbit Air, or Fitbit Edge to automatically track your exercises, you’ll now see an exercise-in-progress card on your mobile app’s Today screen. This card lets you know that your device is tracking your exercise and gives you the opportunity to end the tracking and get your summary whenever you want.

We don’t know exactly what this card looks like just yet, but it sounds like it fits the need perfectly.

Google Health 5.13 is now rolling out on Android and iOS.
