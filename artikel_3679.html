A Ton Of Big Blockbuster Games Are Already On Sale For Black Friday 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Black Friday seems to arrive earlier and earlier each year. It’s gone from a single-day event into a month-long deals bonanza where a ton of the discounts are fake. For gaming, the Black Friday “season” is already well underway, and actually a bunch of the deals are pretty good.

Marvel’s Wolverine was a surprising miss for Sony’s first-party PlayStation game factory, but it delivers on spectacle and cinematic comic-book storylines, and it’s currently just $50, which is a very good price for a big-budget blockbuster that came out less than three months ago.

There are a bunch of other recent games that are pretty cheap, too, as well as some older ones:

The other big highlight on this list is 007 First Light, an action-adventure from the makers of Hitman that will probably appear on a bunch of people’s best games of 2026 lists. It’s half off digitally on PS5 right now. Star Wars Zero Company, another strong release this year, is also on sale. Saros, a fairly challenging 3D bullet hell shooter, is more of an acquired taste, but also easy to recommend at just $40.

Final shout-outs go to Borderlands 4 and Mafia: The Old Country. Published by the same company, they couldn’t be more different. Borderlands is a grindy RPG loot shooter with a silly sense of humor. Mafia is a narrative-driven cinematic adventure that you can finish in just 10-12 hours. It’s perfect for people who want a rock-solid game that’s not caught up in all of the minutia and busywork of most modern gaming blockbusters.

At $20 a piece, they’re both a steal. Why so many steep discounts so early in November? Probably to catch your attention before Grand Theft Auto 6 drops in just a few days.
