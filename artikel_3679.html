Tiger Woods and Elin Nordegren Reunite to Celebrate Son's Latest Milestone

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Tiger Woods and his ex-wife Elin Nordegren came together to celebrate a major milestone in their son Charlie’s life.

According to pictures obtained by USA Today on Friday, November 6, Woods, 50, and Nordegren, 46, were in attendance as their 17-year-old child was honored during signing day at his Florida high school

Woods opted for a Florida State T-shirt and a black baseball cap. As for Nordegren, she wore her son’s future college colors with a maroon dress as she posed with boyfriend Jordan Cameron.

Charlie verbally committed to play golf at Florida State University in February. He confirmed the news via a rare social media post.

“Excited to announce my commitment to play golf at Florida State University — go Noles!” he wrote via Instagram on February 10.

He is expected to join a talented recruiting class, which includes Miles Russell, the Palm Beach Post reports. Russell, 17, is currently the No. 1 player in the American Junior Golf Association’s Rolex Rankings.

“It’s been very different, the recruiting process,” Woods told reporters in December 2025 about watching his son find the perfect college. “Now, you have cellphones. We didn’t have cellphones. We would have written letters that would show up in the mailbox. It’s just very different how fast coaches can communicate with the family members and the player that they’re trying to recruit. It’s just a different world. Not saying it’s good or bad, it’s just different.”

As Charlie prepares for a new chapter of his golf career, his parents — who also share 18-year-old daughter Sam, 18 — have been known to come together for their children’s biggest milestones.

In February, the exes reunited to celebrate Charlie winning a state championship at The Benjamin School in Palm Beach, Florida.

A few months earlier, the pair supported their son at the Florida 1A state championship in Howey-In-The-Hills, Florida.

Woods and Nordegren married in 2004 but divorced in 2010 after the professional golfer was involved in a cheating scandal. Nordegren has been dating Cameron, 38, since 2017, while Woods is currently seeing Vanessa Trump.

“My relationship with Tiger is centered around our children, and we are doing really good — we really are — and I am so happy that is the case,” Nordegren told People in May 2014. “He is a great father.”

She continued, “In the beginning, you tamp down the animosity for the kids’ sake. I’m not going to deny that I went through the wringer. But I don’t think I doubted we’d end up here. That was always my dream, that the kids can have two loving parents that show respect for each other. And I feel that’s what they have.”
