The Celtic youngster Martin O'Neill was keen to check on during Ross County clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Martin O'Neill has namechecked one Celtic youngster in particular he was keen to watch when taking in the B team's clash with Ross County.

The Parkhead side's academy players took on the League One side in the KDM Evoluton Trophy, losing 2-0. First teamers, and summer arrivals, Landon Emenalo, Shim Mheuka and Joel van den Berg.

O'Neill wanted to check on the trio's performances but was also keen to check out the young players, in particular midfielder Liam Kennedy.

The 17-year-old has played 38 minutes for East Kilbride as part of a cooperation loan agreement and played 90 minutes for Celtic B last night.

Asked about his appearance in Dingwall, O'Neill said: "Well, in my first spell years ago, I don't remember going to too many Celtic meetings, but then I'm not so sure that we had supposedly that set of crises at the time, if that's what you can call it. But yes, I am actually or used to be a very, very hands-on manager.

"But I wanted to see the players, primarily the players. First of all, there's a couple of young lads who are trying to come through, like young Liam Kennedy, lads like that there who I think have a chance. They're young and also we had Joel van den Berg playing a game after injury and the two young lads from Chelsea who haven't played that much football even since summer time.

"So it was important for us and I thought the opposition were going to be very strong, which they were, and I have to say I thought Ross County played really well, really well to win the game and deserved to win it. But it was great to see the game."
