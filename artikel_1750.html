Mauricio Pochettino seals Manchester United coup as ex-Arsenal man arrives

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Manchester United have hired former Arsenal coach Sam Wilson, who has already started work as part of Mauricio Pochettino’s backroom staff.

Wilson left Arsenal in the summer, having been at the club for 12 years.

He was the lead physical performance coach under Mikel Arteta up until the end of last season, playing his part in Arsenal’s Premier League title-winning season.

Wilson left the Gunners as part of the club’s overhaul of their medical department, with significant changes made in an attempt to address the injury problems that have hindered the squad in recent seasons.

After departing his role at Arsenal, Wilson briefly joined Parma, where he was appointed head of performance by Arteta’s former assistant Carlos Cuesta.

However, Cuesta was sacked in September and Wilson left the club a month later. He has now made a swift return to England.

Wilson was on the bench at Anfield on Sunday as Pochettino took charge of United for the first time.

He has joined United as a first-team fitness coach and will play his part in what is expected to be a key focus of Pochettino’s reign.

United rank second-last in the running stats in the Premier League this season, in stark contrast to the stats recorded by Pochettino’s teams at Southampton, Tottenham and Chelsea.

During his time at Arsenal, Wilson oversaw a lot of the individual training work with players returning from injury issues.

When Bukayo Saka came back from injury to face Fulham last year and scored within seven minutes, he ran straight to Wilson on the bench to celebrate with him.
