Report: Liverpool set transfer strategy to sign £45m star

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Liverpool have been linked with a lot of players recently.

The Anfield outfit are massively underperforming with fans demanding that replacements for a decent chunk of their players are sourced in the upcoming transfer windows.

Long-term links with Alex Scott and Adam Wharton have remained while new rumours with players such as Arthur Dias have now started to emerge.

However, before the January transfer window even opens, there are a couple of deals that already feel close to completion.

And now, the Reds seem to have created a transfer strategy to get one deal done.

Liverpool want to negotiate Ronald Araujo's price down before signing him

Right now, Ronald Araujo is probably Liverpool's best-performing summer signing.

The Uruguayan came to Anfield on a season-long loan with a €55 million option to buy.

And, even though he's been one of their best defenders, his injury history and age are a cause for concern.

Whether he's been used as a right-back or a centre-back, he's always done well and has certainly helped solidify Liverpool's back line.

At the moment, Liverpool are more than pleased with Araujo's contributions but, according to an update from Diario Sport, they're not willing to sign him permanently unless they can knock his price down a bit.

"Liverpool want to keep Ronald Araújo, but not at any price," they wrote.

"The English club is very pleased with the Uruguayan center-back's performance, on loan from Barça until the end of the season, although they don't intend to automatically exercise the €55 million purchase option agreed between the two clubs. Their aim is to negotiate a lower fee.

"Liverpool's strategy is to wait until the end of the season to sit down and negotiate with Barça. The Anfield management considers €55 million too high and are confident they can reduce it considerably.

"Liverpool intends to leverage the player's contract situation and high salary to put pressure on Barcelona in negotiations. Barcelona will have to decide whether to accept a lower fee than initially agreed or bring back a player who is proving his worth in the Premier League."

Liverpool signing Ronald Araujo for cheaper than €55 million would be ideal

As mentioned, Araujo has been in fine form this term.

The defender has been a revelation at Anfield but, the €55 million fee is obviously quite steep.

So, if Liverpool manage to knock the price of the defender down and Barcelona agree to sell him, they'll be in the money.

The fact that Araujo can cover both positions is one of his main selling points with versatility being a key part of football in this day an age.

Obviously, the fact that he's got plenty of Champions League experience and will have a year of Premier League football under his belt is also a major selling point.

There's no mention of how much Liverpool would want to pay for Araujo but, if it's anywhere near €35 million - €40 million, it would be an absolute steal.
