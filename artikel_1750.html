Joao Pedro injury update with five Chelsea stars to miss Nottingham Forest clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Joao Pedro remains unavailable for Chelsea’s Premier League clash with Nottingham Forest this weekend.

The Brazil international has missed his side’s last five games as a result of a thigh injury he suffered in training in October.

The former Brighton star sat out of games against Tottenham, Manchester United, Sunderland and Liverpool in the Carabao Cup with the Blues losing three of those four in his absence prior to the 4-1 hammering at home to Leeds United last weekend.

Speaking last week, Xabi Alonso revealed Pedro was still to return to training with the club assessing him ‘game by game.’

Speaking at a press conference on Thursday ahead of the trip to Forest, Alonso was asked if any injured players would be ready to return this weekend, flatly responding: ‘No.’

Maxence Lacroix is also not ready to return. The former Crystal Palace star, almost ever-present at the back since his summer arrival from Crystal Palace, came off with a hamstring injury in the Carabao Cup defeat to Liverpool.

‘It’s one less week than from the last game,’ Alonso said of the Frenchman. ‘So we are closer in his recovery, but not for this game.’

Reece James, Jorrel Hato and Jamie Gittens also remain sidelined.

There was good news on Moises Caicedo after the Ecuador international was forced off at half-time against Leeds last time out.

During an aerial dual with James Justin in the first-half at Stamford Bridge, the midfielder suffered a heavy landing after twisting in mid-air.

While he was initially able to carry on, Caicedo went down again off the ball a few minutes later, covering his face with his shirt in disappointment as he was replaced after just 22 minutes.

Offering an update on his midfielder, Alonso said: ‘Yes [he has been able to train this week] and he is going to be okay for tomorrow.’
