Champions League suspended players: Manchester United blow as quartet to miss this week's games

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

By Darren Plant | 23 Nov 2026 10:53

The league phase of the Champions League continues this week as the 36 teams head into matchday five looking for the win that could prove decisive in their quest to progress through to the knockout stages.

Arsenal's showdown with Borussia Dortmund is arguably the pick of the matches, with those teams sitting at the top of the Premier League and Bundesliga tables.

Nevertheless, with all of the favourites for the competition being kept apart on this particular matchday, they will view it as an opportunity to edge closer to making the top eight.

As highlighted by Sports Mole, there are just four players serving suspensions ahead of matchday five.

Fresh off the back of a 4-0 defeat at Liverpool, Manchester United make the trip to Sporting Lisbon without Matheus Cunha.

Having scored what proved to be the winner in a thrilling contest with Roma, Cunha received a second yellow card for a needless challenge during the closing stages.

Meanwhile, Man City will face Napoli at the Etihad Stadium with the visitors being unable to select defender Rafa Marin.

He was dismissed for denying a goalscoring opportunity when Porto had the chance to move 4-1 up in Portugal.

As for AEK Athens, Aboubakary Koita is unavailable for their match against Como due to the red card that he was given versus Real Madrid.

Koita was handed a red card after the final whistle for a prolonged confrontation with the officials.

Sabah defender Aden McCarthy is the fourth player to miss the MD5 for his dismissal in the 4-1 defeat to Viking FK.

He now misses the home fixture against La Liga champions Barcelona.

Although Koita and McCarthy will miss matches this week, UEFA are currently indicating that an extra game may be added to their suspensions in due course.
