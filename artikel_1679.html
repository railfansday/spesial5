Spain 2-3 England: Highlights, man of the match, stats as world champions lose 43-game unbeaten run

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

By Ben Knapton | 15 Nov 2026 21:48, Last updated: 15 Nov 2026 22:02

England inflicted Spain's first defeat in 43 games on the World Cup winners with a sensational 3-2 victory in the Nations League, thus progressing to the quarter-finals as group winners.

Evidently learning their lessons from their gut-wrenching World Cup semi-final loss to Argentina, the Three Lions exploded out of the blocks in the Spanish capital, but Luis de la Fuente's backline weathered the early storm.

The world champions soon began to threaten James Trafford's goal too, but England got the reward their endeavours warranted on the stroke of the break, as Harry Kane dispatched a controversial penalty.

Neither the home nor away fans could then comprehend what they witnessed inside the opening 15 minutes of the second half, as Arsenal duo Ezri Konsa and Ben White incredibly extended England's lead, but the visitors were then forced to strap in for a nail-biting ending.

Barely moments after England's third went in, Elliot Anderson was sent off for a last-man foul and Mikel Oyarzabal dispatched the resulting penalty, before an Anthony Gordon error ended with Nico Williams cutting the arrears further.

However, the 10 men of England heroically held out in the dying embers to condemn Spain to their first loss since March 2024, and usurp the world and European champions at the top of League A Group 3.

The last time Spain lost a football match before tonight, Jurgen Klopp was Liverpool manager, Manchester City were the Premier League champions, and Gareth Southgate was preparing the Three Lions for Euro 2024.

England fans could have been forgiven for writing their team off before the first ball was kicked, but once the first ball was kicked, Tuchel's men took the game to the world champions in a wholly unexpected manner.

Spain also needed two individual mistakes from England to earn their route back into the game, but when the Three Lions maintained their discipline, the best in the world had no answers.
