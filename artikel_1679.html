Manchester United XI vs Sporting: Predicted lineup, confirmed team news, injury latest for Champions League today

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Standard's journalism is supported by our readers. If you click links to other sites on this page, we will earn commission.

Luke Shaw has resumed training before Manchester United head to Sporting in the Champions League tonight.

The left-back has missed the last eight games across all competitions with a muscular injury, but could make his return in Lisbon.

New head coach Mauricio Pochettino endured a horror start to his Man Utd tenure with a heavy 4-0 defeat by rivals Liverpool in the Premier League on Sunday.

The Red Devils were down to 10 men after just eight minutes at Anfield when Leny Yoro fouled Alexander Isak and received a red card, which has since been overturned following a successful appeal.

United suffered no new injury blows on Merseyside, but the result was “painful” according to Pochettino, and he will be looking to put things right immediately in Europe’s top competition on Wednesday night.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea boss sprung a surprise with the inclusion of Matthijs de Ligt in his starting XI against Liverpool, with the Dutchman having previously been sidelined for a year with a persistent back injury.

The centre-back looked awkward for much of the match, and his selection was scrutinised intensely afterwards.

It remains to be seen whether De Ligt will play again in Lisbon, with Harry Maguire introduced in the first half at Anfield following Yoro’s dismissal.

Maguire should start against Sporting, perhaps alongside Yoro, who would have been available in Europe regardless of United’s appeal being successful or not.

That could see Lisandro Martinez plugged in at left-back once more if Shaw is not ready to start, although the Argentine had to shift inside after Yoro’s red card against Liverpool, with Patrick Dorgu then moving back.

Diogo Dalot failed to impress on Sunday and could see himself rotated out for Noussair Mazraoui, while in the engine room, Youri Tielemans was hooked for Mason Mount in the second half.

On the injury list remain Benjamin Sesko, Marcus Rashford, Carlos Baleba and Manuel Ugarte, while back-up goalkeeper Karl Darlow has also yet to return.
