Report: Fabrizio Romano confirms mega Liverpool update that could change everything

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Fabrizio Romano has confirmed a huge Liverpool update that could change everything at the club.

Julian Ward is the new man in charge of the club and it looks like he wants to do things differently to his predecessor.

For a while at Liverpool, there were directorial changes, but the motto was always that it's business as usual.

However, this time around things have to be a little bit different.

Richard Hughes was not a hugely successful sporting director during his time in charge. If anything, Hughes' time can objectively be looked at as a failure.

Liverpool overspent and made decisions that have resulted to a squad that looks weaker in comparison to the one he inherited. Meanwhile, Liverpool also have a lot less financial might in the market due to their lavish spending.

Speaking on his YouTube channel Fabrizio Romano has confirmed that Liverpool are now in talks over a contract renewal with Alexis Mac Allister.

Now the transfer guru has emphasised that these are preliminary talks at the moment. Both sides are trying to establish whether an extension is even viable.

However, this is still a stark contract to Hughes' stance, who held no such talks with Mac Allister. And let's be honest it could change everything for Liverpool.

Previously, the notion has been that Liverpool will sign a midfielder if they can sell Mac Allister. Now Liverpool still want to sign a midfielder but also keep Mac Allister.

Which means one of Ryan Gravenberch or Dominik Szoboszlai could be up for sale instead. And that could have a drastic impact on the squad.
