‘You don’t win 345 matches defending’ – Simeone thrilled to break LaLiga win record

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Atletico Madrid boss Diego Simeone quipped, "You don't win 345 matches defending" after he broke the record for the most wins as a manager in LaLiga history.

Simeone levelled Luis Aragones' record on October 31, but after a loss to Barcelona before the international break, he had to wait for his record-breaking victory.

And he got it on Sunday, with Atletico brushing aside Getafe 3-0 with a confident performance.

Alex Baena and Johnny Cardoso put Atletico 2-0 up by the half-hour mark, and Alejandro Grimaldo added some late gloss from the spot with a stoppage-time penalty.

It means that Simeone has now won 345 matches in LaLiga, officially surpassing Aragones in the standings, and doing so in 189 fewer games.

Simeone reached that total in 567 matches (also D123 L99), while Aragones tallied 344 wins in 756 outings.

Asked how he felt about breaking the record, Simeone said: "An enormous joy, I cannot deny it.

"A lot of work behind it, a lot of effort from many people, many players thrown into an idea.

"That constant criticism we always receive, which I don't value, but I keep observing it: that defensive phase they've already imposed on us, and it seems we always play defensively to defend.

"You don't win 345 matches defending. Is it allowed?"

— Atlético de Madrid (@atletienglish) November 22, 2026

Atletico remain third in LaLiga, five points behind leaders Barcelona but only two behind second-place Real Madrid.

They have been particularly strong on the road this term, winning each of their last five away games, while conceding just one goal in the process.

Overall, they have only lost one away game in LaLiga this season (a 3-0 loss to Athletic Club), and Simeone is thrilled with this particular strength.

"We are interpreting away games better," he added. "Goals give you that calm to have continuity in the match.

"We need to stay in the same direction. We came off a very good 55 minutes against Barca. The result was tough; it was hard for almost all the teams in Europe to compete again.

"Because it's tough when you come back from the national team, especially as visitors. At home, there's always the people and the biggest support.

"Playing away from home, like after the other break against Alaves [a 2-1 away win], we managed to resolve it, and it makes me happy."
