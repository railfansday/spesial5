Disney says more layoffs are on the way as company announces double-digit Q4 revenue growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Walt Disney Company (NYSE: DIS) announced its fourth-quarter fiscal 2026 results today, and it exceeded investor expectations.

But while shareholders may be buoyed by the news, Disney’s employees are likely feeling less cheerful. That’s because the company, which has already laid off thousands this year, says more job cuts are on the way. Here’s what you need to know.

Disney’s Q4 beats on revenue and EPS

Today, Disney announced its Q4 2026 results for the period that ended on October 3. Overall, the results were positive for the company, which saw double-digit growth in several key areas:

  • Revenue: $25.9 billion (up 16% from the same quarter a year earlier)
  • Income: $3.5 billion (up 71%)
  • Total segment operating income: $5 billion (up 43%)
  • Entertainment operating income: $1.3 billion (up 92%)
  • Sports operating income: $1.3 billion (up 53%)
  • Experiences operating income: $2.2 billion (up 20%)

Adjusted earnings per share came in at $1.77, up from $1.11 in the same quarter a year earlier. Disney’s Q4 results this year were helped by a 53rd week in the company’s fiscal calendar.

To put some of those numbers into perspective, the company exceeded analysts’ expectations on revenue and adjusted EPS. Analysts had expected revenue of $25.1 billion and an adjusted EPS of $1.67, notes CNBC.

Experiences, which include Disney’s popular theme parks, were one of the highlights for both Disney’s Q4 and full year fiscal 2026, with the company’s Q4 Experiences revenue increasing 15% and its full fiscal year 2026 Experience revenue up 9%.

“Our continued growth in both guests and guest spending at Experiences once again highlights the differentiated strength of our diversified global portfolio,” Disney’s CEO Josh D’Amaro and CFO Hugh Johnston wrote in its shareholder’s letter.

Other areas of strength for Disney in Q4 included 22% subscription revenue growth and 12% consumer product growth.

Shares of The Walt Disney Company were down roughly 1.6% in premarket trading on Monday after the report. The stock is up 6.12% year to date.

Disney talks AI…

Artificial intelligence is top of mind for the business world, so it’s no surprise that Disney touched on the topic in its most recent earnings announcement. 

The company said that its customers at its parks are “increasingly using AI-powered tools to discover, plan, and book their travel,” and Disney, in turn, is “exploring several new ways to meet them where they are.”

Disney said it wants its experiences “to appear as a vacation option within the AI tools guests use for inspiration and planning,” likely referring to AI chatbots such as ChatGPT and Gemini.

To achieve this, the company said it is integrating its “offerings directly into those tools so guests can book more efficiently.”

…and says more layoffs are coming

In its Q4 results, Disney also touched on cost-cutting efforts, including layoffs.

During 2026, Disney has initiated several rounds of job cuts, including 1,000 in April at its television and movie division, more than 100 at Pixar in July, and hundreds in HR and technology roles in September.

Then, just this month, CNBC reported that Disney plans to lay off several thousand employees in an effort to reduce costs by up to 20%.

Unfortunately, Disney seems to have confirmed that report today. Disney says it is planning about $3 billion in cost-cutting efforts by fiscal 2029 and CFO Johnston told CNBC that part of those efforts will include more layoffs.

“We do expect more layoffs,” Johnston said.

Fast Company reached out to Disney for comment. We will update this story if we hear back.

The job cuts will reportedly help fund Disney’s content spend for fiscal 2027, which is projected to reach $26 billion, up 9% from the year before.

Disney has not yet stated which divisions the job cuts will affect, or how many employees will lose their jobs.
