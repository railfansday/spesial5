Overlooked symmetry group offers new geometric blueprint for the Standard Model and topologically protected vacuums

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

For more than half a century, physicists searching for a grand unified theory have followed one unspoken rule: go bigger. If you want to weave nature's fundamental forces into a single mathematical framework, the classic recipe says you need a giant "simple" symmetry group—structures like SU(5) or SO(10) that act like massive algebraic warehouses large enough to hold every force at once.
