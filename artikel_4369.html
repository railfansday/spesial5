For those who decided to dish out the extra cash to buy the Ultimate Edition of Grand Theft Auto 6, then you’ll get quite a bit for your dollar. The Ultimate Edition comes with a slew of in-game items, but most of them aren’t unlocked immediately.

While some will appear on Jason’s property right away, most are tied to shops within the game or are found after certain events further into the story, some of which are only available basically at the end. With that said, this will hopefully give you a good idea where to find everything.
