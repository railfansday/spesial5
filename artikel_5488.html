Compass bars employees from Realtor leadership roles

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Compass International Holdings adopted a policy preventing salaried employees and brokerage managers from holding leadership roles at state and local Realtor associations. Compass said the change was informed by a similar policy previously implemented at Anywhere, and the policy scope remains unclear for non-Realtor MLSs.   AI Summary

Compass International Holdings has confirmed that it has adopted a new policy preventing salaried employees and brokerage managers from holding leadership roles at state and local Realtor associations.

In remains unclear if employees and managers currently in Realtor leadership role have been instructed to step down from their positions. Additionally, Compass did not clarify if this ban also extended to non-Realtor owned MLSs.

In an emailed statement a spokesperson for Compass International Holdings told HousingWire that the policy was “informed by a similar policy previously implemented at Anywhere.”

“We periodically review our policies and have decided to extend this approach across our business,” the spokesperson added.

This news was first reported by Inman and confirmed by HousingWire.

Compass acquired Anywhere in early January of 2026. In October of 2023, as part of its proposed commission lawsuit settlement, Anywhere said that it would no longer require brokerages, brokers or agents affiliated with its brands to be Realtor association members, leaving the decision to join up to the agents, brokers and affiliates.

In an emailed statement, a spokesperson for the National Association of Realtors (NAR) told HousingWire that the association has “served the industry as a member-led organization since 1908.”

“NAR welcomes all staff, agents, independent contractors, brokers and others involved in the real estate industry who align with our mission,” the spokesperson added. “Our members and leaders throughout the nation empower the work NAR does to preserve, protect and advance the American dream of homeownership and the right to real property for all, including by getting the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act passed into law with bipartisan support.”

In July, Compass announced this it was joining the American Real Estate Association (ARA) and that all agents affiliated with the firm through franchises or owned-brokerage operations would receive complimentary membership for remainder of 2026 and all of 2027.
