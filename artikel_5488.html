Papa John's is using customer pizza photos to build an AI quality-control system

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The chain is giving a free pizza for photos of delivered orders; the promotion runs through Nov. 20 and has drawn thousands of photos

Papa John’s is handing out a complimentary pizza in exchange for a picture of a delivered order plus feedback, part of an effort to train an AI system that checks pizza quality, according to Reuters.

CEO Todd Penegor told Reuters the promotion aims to raise the standard of pizzas coming out of the chain's franchise-run locations. He said it targets inconsistencies that have become the "Achilles' heel" of the brand.

Penegor said the goal is software that can replicate the quality checks performed by the cameras installed inside the chain's stores. Those cameras rely on AI to verify that the cheese has not burned, the pepperoni is neatly sliced and the crust is properly shaped.

The in-store cameras exist at only a few hundred of the chain's 6,000 restaurants around the world, Penegor said. Customer photos might let the company bring photo-driven quality checks to many more restaurants at a lower price, according to Reuters. The customer images are less accurate than the in-store cameras.

A dashboard collects the incoming photos and customer feedback, Penegor said. The promotion began on Nov. 9 and generated thousands of photos in its first few days. It runs until Nov. 20.

Penegor said the technology was developed in-house and extends AI tools the chain previously built jointly with Google $GOOGL Cloud.

He said the responses will guide the chain in ranking which problems to tackle first and will show franchisees the areas where their stores may be lagging. The company disclosed on Nov. 5 that its global same-store sales fell 6.5% in the latest quarter.
