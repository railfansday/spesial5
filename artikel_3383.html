Taylor Swift Celebrates Travis Kelce's Touchdown With Niece, Her Parents, Donna & Kylie Kelce

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Kansas City Chiefs are playing at home, so you know what that means ... Taylor Swift is in full WAG mode cheering on her hubby Travis Kelce ... who has already scored a touchdown!

Tay has attended every Chiefs home game this season, but this time she's brought along some very special guests ... her parents Andrea and Scott Swift.

The Chiefs-Cardinals game isn't just a Swift family affair -- Kelce matriarch Donna and Jason Kelce's wife Kylie are in the box drawing more attention than the players themselves. In fact, Taylor celebrated Trav's TD with her niece Wyatt.

Taylor is coming off the CMAs, where she returned to country's biggest stage after 13 years performing a medley of "I Knew It, I Knew You" and "Our Song."

Event though Taylor didn't go home with a win at the Country Music Awards, hopefully she'll be the good luck charm Travis needs to keep the Chiefs' winning streak going for a third week in a row.
