Ariana Grande’s New Focker Movie Officially Defies Critics on CinemaScore

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Two decades ago, comedy was one of the reliable genres when it came to box office hits. The likes of Jim Carrey's Bruce Almighty, 2009's The Hangover, and the comedy sequel Meet the Fockers all made roughly $500 million at the box office, and the future looked bright for Hollywood's funniest faces. However, in the past 16 years, the comedy genre's share of the theatrical market has dropped from 20% to 10%, and production companies are losing faith in pure comedy, instead choosing to weave their humor through other genres, such as horror.

For the first time in 16 years, a once bankable comedy franchise is venturing into an entirely new box office landscape with its long-awaited fourth installment. This week, Focker-in-Law, the fourth film in the Fockers franchise, hopes to challenge the likes of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping at the Thanksgiving box office. The franchise began with the 2000 rom-com Meet the Parents, and a lot has changed since.

That is, except for the main cast, with Robert De Niro, Ben Stiller, Owen Wilson, and Teri Polo all returning for this fourth installment. The biggest new addition to the franchise is Wicked star and Academy Award nominee Ariana Grande, who plays the trained FBI hostage negotiator girlfriend of Greg's son Henry (Skyler Gisondo), as she is quickly welcomed into the Circle of Trust, much to Greg's dismay. Blythe Danner and Beanie Feldstein also feature in the film, which is written and directed by John Hamburg, based on a story by Hamburg, Ilana Glazer, and Austen Earl.

We've already reported that Focker-In-Law has been met with a terrible batch of first reviews, earning just 31% on review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes. Collider's Gabrielle Moss awarded the film just 5/10 in her review, saying that "Stiller and Grande never generate the joyful friction that Stiller and DeNiro produced in the original film." However, fans of the franchise have been pleasantly surprised, scoring 87% on Rotten Tomatoes, with this fairly glowing response helping the film earn a positive CinemaScore, which is officially a "B+."

Focker-In-Law is now in theaters. For all the latest movie news, make sure to stay tuned to Collider.
