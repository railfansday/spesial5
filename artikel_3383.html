The 10 Best Tech Deals From Walmart's 2026 Black Friday and Cyber Monday Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The first of Walmart's three major sales for Black Friday and Cyber Monday is now live, and there are already some amazing tech deals to take advantage of. I've gathered my top ten tech products from the sale to make your life a little easier. For more, keep tabs on our Black Friday coverage, where we post the latest deals from not just Walmart, but Amazon, Best Buy, and other retailers' Black Friday deals.

Apple's latest smartwatch is the Series 12, but don't count out the Series 11. By sacrificing a few of Apple's newest features, you can save some money on the more premium version of this last-gen Apple Watch. Right now, the Titanium Series 11 GPS + cellular model is on sale for $699. That's $100 according to Walmart, but really, you're saving $50—this version of the Series 11 was $799 when it launched, but now, the Titanium Series 12 is $749.

During WWDC 26 earlier this summer, Apple announced new Siri AI upgrades with watchOS 27—but only some Apple Watches have compatibility. Luckily, the Series 11 is one of them, at least for many of them. Some other upgrades in the new OS include a dedicated Siri app, a new app grid, a new single-tap gesture, among other features. Just note that only Series 12 and Ultra 4 can run Audio Intelligence.

Bone conduction headphones are ideal for activities where it is vital to be able to hear your surroundings. They use a technology that sends audio vibrations to your inner ear while leaving your ear canals open. Shokz is one of, if not the best, brands making bone conduction headphones right now. At the high end of Shokz's offerings sit the OpenRun Pro 2, which impressed me when I tested them out for review. Amazon currently has a restored pair for $95 (originally $135.95 and $179.99 new).

Beats are a more affordable Apple alternative to AirPods, and you can often find them with substantial discounts. You can now get the Beats Solo 4 for $69 (originally $199.95) after a 50% discount. These on-ear headphones are Siri-compatible, so you don't need to use your iPhone to ask the assistant questions. They also have personalized spatial audio, which produces "3D audio" that follows the movement of your iPhone (as long as that media supports spatial audio), as well as an impressive battery life of 50 hours.

Most, if not all, TV speakers aren't great. While you have plenty of options, the quickest and least expensive way to improve your TV's audio capabilities to buy a soundbar. Compare a soundbar's size to the thin, small speakers jammed into modern TVs. The Vizio 4.1 Pro Soundbar and rear speakers come with Dolby Atmos support and cost just $79 (originally $148). They won't win any audio awards, but it's better than any built-in TV speakers, and for $79, it'll improve your experience without breaking the bank.

If you want to take things up a notch, consider this 5.1 channel sound system. Not only does it include far more channels than a single soundbar, but you're also adding a dedicated subwoofer to the mix. A subwoofer will make those thuds, explosions, and low vibrations much more pronounced than on a soundbar alone. For $149.99 (originally $249.99), you can get the ULTIMEA 5.1 surround sound system, which comes with a soundbar—replicating left, right, and center channels—two rear speakers, and a subwoofer.

I love projectors and Roku. To me, a Roku projector just makes sense, especially at $99.97 (originally $389.99). This projector is portable and has the Roku OS already installed, so it's easy to project it against a wall, ceiling, or screen, whether it's indoors or outdoors. It's easy to set up; you just need an outlet nearby and to dim or turn off the lights. If you have Bluetooth speakers, you can hook them up to create a more immersive sound.

The TCL Q51GB is a QLED Google TV that is a great value for anyone looking for size, color, and features. The 98-inch class is heavily discounted at its $898 price (originally $1,499.99). TCL makes one of the best QLEDs for the money, and their technology keeps getting better. This TV supports Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos, and if you're a gamer, you get a refresh rate of 240Hz that is automatically triggered with its Auto Game Mode feature.

Gaming monitors can get pricey fast. At $179.99 (originally $299.99), this 27-inch Samsung Odyssey G55C curved gaming monitor offers a 1440p resolution, a 1000R curve, a 165Hz refresh rate with FreeSync and G-SYNC compatibility to keep screen tearing to a minimum, and features like Samsung’s Virtual Aim Point and Contrast Enhancer. It's a solid choice for gaming, especially at this price point.

Alienware makes great gaming laptops, and the Aurora is a great option for anyone who wants to get all the essentials without spending too much. At its current $1,099 price (originally $1,999), the Alienware Aurora 16 becomes a mid-tier gaming laptop at a "budget" price. We reviewed the higher-tier Aurora 16X and were impressed with it. The Aurora 16 has a slightly slower processor, a dimmer display with half the refresh rate, a smaller battery, and a weaker graphics card, but given its price, it's great for a gamer looking for a solid gaming laptop in the $1,000 range.

Walmart has the AirPods 4 without ANC for $89 (originally $129.99). This model comes with USB-C charging (no more Lightning charging). These are powered by Apple's H2 chip, so you'll get Personalized Spatial Audio and the ability to use head gestures to tell Siri "yes" or "no" (this also works for answering or denying calls). They are rated IP54 for dust and water resistance, offer up to five hours of listening per charge (up to 30 hours with the charging case), and can handle automatic switching with your other Apple devices. You can read more about the non-ANC AirPods in PCMag's "excellent" review.

In short, yes, Black Friday still offers discounts that can be rare throughout the rest of the year. If there’s something you want to buy, or you’re shopping for gifts, it’s a good time to look for discounts on what you need, especially tech sales, home improvement supplies, and fitness tech. Of course, if you need to save money, the best way to save is not to buy anything.

Yes. Walmart, Best Buy, Target, and Home Depot will all have in-store deals, some of which you might not find online, so if you're able to take a trip to your local retailer, keep your eyes open for different deals.
