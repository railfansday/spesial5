The Sims 4 Officially Makes 13 DLCs Free for a Limited Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Sims 4 has received a steady stream of contentsince it launched 12 years ago. However, 2026 has been particularly dry. The last major expansion, Royalty & Legacy, was released in February, and aside from kits, the game hasn't received much content this year. This may be because of the team's focus on Project Renee (which may be on hold) or because of internal matters related to Electronic Arts' private-equity buyout.

Thankfully, The Sims Team is going to end the year on a positive note with eight weeks' worth of giveaways. From Nov. 26 to Jan. 14, players can log on to The Sims 4 and claim a free Maker Pack. The first freebie is the Rock & Carry Maker Pack created by Bostyny. It includes a decorative handbag — perfect for cluttering up the house! — and a set of headphones, which can be used as a discreet speaker.

Maker Packs are paid custom content that is usually soldin The Sims 4 Marketplace. It's a great way to reward fans and promote CC creators. Throughout this holiday event, gamers will be able to claim a total of 13 Maker Packs. We won't know what's coming until each pack is released.They're expected to drop at the same time as regular updates, so players can check what's new at around 1 p.m. EST whenever there is a giveaway. The first reward is available until Jan. 6, which means players have 41 days in total to claim it. Therefore, players should have about six weeks to claim each reward once it's unlocked. So, as long as gamers log in between Jan. 5 and Jan. 14, they will be able to nab all The Sims 4 presents this holiday season.
