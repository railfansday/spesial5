Scotland talisman sets out March plan of attack, defends Pocognoli and names what team must improve

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Scotland midfielder John McGinn has warned his international teammates not to hide behind fans’ formation frustrations after pressure mounted on head coach Sebastian Pocognoli following a 4-0 defeat to Switzerland.

McGinn, 31, was powerless to resist as Scotland’s poor Nations League campaign ended in a dismal defeat against a heavily rotated Switzerland side. A Lewis Ferguson own goal opened the scoring early on, before second half strikes from Winsley Boteli, Alvyn Sanchez and Zeki Amdouni saw the hosts coast to victory in St Gallen.

A third defeat in Pocognoli’s first six games, the result meant Scotland ended their Group B1 campaign in an underwhelming third place, leaving many supporters frustrated by Pocognoli’s refusal to turn away from a rigid 3-4-2-1 formation, with some believing that he is struggling to adapt his tactics to fit the strengths of the squad.

However, Aston Villa captain McGinn has refused to blame Pocognoli, insisting that formations and the new manager’s methods become irrelevant if the players aren’t displaying their qualities on the pitch.

“Obviously the trajectory of results means it’s impossible for me to say yes,” said McGinn when asked if Scotland are on the right path. “But we have been here before, and unfortunately we’ve been in moments where you need to stick together, show your qualities. Players need to come into the group and add more. And you need to turn results around, which we have shown before.

“The manager can’t kick the ball for us. It’s up to us to make the right decisions in those moments, execute it, show a bit of quality, and be a lot better on the ball and off the ball. And that doesn’t matter what tactics you have or formation you play, you need to be a lot better in yourself and not a lot of us can say that tonight.”

The 4-0 hammering was Scotland’s final game of the calendar year, with the team not due to play again until their European Championship 2028 qualifying campaign begins in March. Desperate to qualify for their third successive Euros, McGinn set out a plan of attack for the new year and insists the best way for the squad to support Pocognoli is by improving their performances on the pitch.

“We’ve qualified for three out of four major tournaments,” said McGinn. “If we’re going to qualify for a fourth, we need to be much better, We know that. We know what it takes, but you don’t have time in football as a bunch of players to do that. So, come March, we need to be a lot better and improve.”

Meanwhile, Pocognoli defended the performance against the Swiss, and insisted the scoreline did not reflect the action on the pitch, saying: “The score is heavy with the 4-0, but I don’t think it’s a reflection of the game, if I’m really honest and objective. I think we had some very good moments in the game, we started the game like we wanted, aggressive away from home. We played a good first half, we created chances, we were dominant in some moments in the game.”
