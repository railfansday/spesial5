Manchester United chief delivers surprise verdict on summer transfers amid relegation fears

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Omar Berrada insists Manchester United’s squad has been made “fundamentally better” by its summer transfers, despite sitting in the relegation zone after ten matches.

The Red Devils have endured a historically poor start to the campaign, which earlier this month resulted in the sacking of head coach Michael Carrick.

The club’s transfer business in recent years has been heavily criticised, and another uninspiring summer has done little to cool fan frustrations. While glaring issues in defence and attack went unaddressed, United spent around £155m to add Andrey Santos, Youri Tielemans and Carlos Baleba to the midfield.

The quality and price of those transfers have both been questioned, particularly as none of the three has particularly impressed in the early days of this campaign, with United having won just one league outing by late November.

Club CEO Berrada, though, insists the squad is in a better place now than it was in May, saying inflated transfer fees made it difficult to navigate the summer transfer window.

Speaking in a rare interview with Sky Sports, he explained: "When we look at this summer, we went in knowing what we needed to do. Of course, there have been different dynamics at play. It's probably been the summer or the transfer window where there's been the highest inflation in transfer fees.

"And we've said publicly and we've said consistently in the last few years that we need to stay disciplined. We need to take the right decisions. We need to buy the right players for the right value at the right time in terms of what the squad needs.

"And when we look at the summer transfer window of 2026, I know we're receiving a lot of criticism for it, but we're actually happy with the players that we bought.

"We think that Carlos Baleba, Youri Tielemans, Andrey Santos, and Karl Darlow - and let's not forget that we've also welcomed back Marcus Rashford, who had a very good season on loan at Barcelona - these are five additions that we think have made the squad fundamentally better.”

Come the end of last season, United’s back line appeared to be the area most in need of updating, with the current corps ageing and underperforming.

Berrada, though, felt the squad had sufficient numbers in defence already, which allowed him to focus on bolstering the midfield.

“There’s a lot of discussion about the back line,” he continued. “But we did come into it thinking: we have depth. We have nine players that can play in the back line, in the back four positions.

“So that's why we looked primarily at investing in the midfield. But we were always looking for opportunities. I think it's been widely reported that we were looking at potentially the left-back position as a reinforcement.

“So could we have signed an additional defender to strengthen the depth and the quality? Yes, but we have to find the right players and you have to find the right players at the right value.

“There's no point in us spending £40m, £50m for players that we're not convinced are going to add value to the team.

“So it's always about being disciplined and finding the right opportunities and getting the right players that are going to take us forward.”
