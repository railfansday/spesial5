Brighton have been one of the surprise packages in the Premier League this term.

Fabian Hurzeler's Seagulls have enjoyed a fine start to the season and are second in the table after Sunday's 2-0 win against Brentford at the Amex Stadium.

Albion are the highest scorers in the division with 26 goals and have conceded just seven times from the first 10 matches - only leaders Arsenal have conceded fewer with five.

Brighton - along with the rest of the Premier League - now pause for the international break and resume top flight action on November 22 at Hull City.

Sixth-placed Everton and seventh-placed Bournemouth are also flying high and are among the clubs challenging the established elite.

Albion fans are starting to get excited on what Hurzeler’s men can achieve this term. The head coach, speaking after the win against Brentford, said: “They should be always excited to watch our game and that's our job, to make them excited.
