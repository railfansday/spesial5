Trump arrives in Philippines ahead of Xi Jinping summit in China after GOP midterm losses

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Fox News' Mark Meredith reports the latest on his visit from Manila. The Heritage Foundation's Victoria Coates also weighs in on the trip during 'Fox & Friends First.'

President Donald Trump touched down in the Philippines on Tuesday as part of an Asia trip that will take him to China for a historic summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping.

The trip marks Trump's first overseas visit since Republicans suffered bruising defeats in the U.S. midterm elections.

Trump noted during a gaggle on Monday that while in China this week he will meet with Chinese President Xi Jingping and Russian President Vladimir Putin.

President Donald Trump boards Air Force One on Nov. 16, 2026, at Joint Base Andrews, Maryland. (Win McNamee/Getty Images)

"And then we go to China. And we'll be with President Xi, he was here a month ago. And we're also gonna meet with President Putin. And we may meet with Kim Jong Un also," Trump said, referring to North Korea's leader. "He may come. I don't know. That's up to President Xi," Trump later added.

Trump indicated that during the meeting with his Chinese and Russian counterparts, they would discuss issues including denuclearization, Iran, Ukraine, economics and trade.

Asked on Tuesday if Kim Jong Un would come, Trump said, "He would like to, yeah. We'll see what happens.

The trip also comes after tech giant Nvidia announced last week that President and CEO Jensen Huang had resigned from the company — the resignation announcement was made after Huang admitted to cheating on his wife amid reporting that he had a relationship with a suspected Chinese spy.

US President Donald Trump waves U.S. and Philippine flags as he arrives at Ninoy Aquino International Airport to attend the 49th Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) Summit in Manila on Nov. 17, 2026. (Eloisa Lopez/POOL/AFP via Getty Images)

Trump met Tuesday in the Philippines with leaders of the Quad, which includes the U.S., Japan, Australia and India.

He signed a document with Thailand Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul on Tuesday as the two nations moved closer toward inking a trade deal.

"Today's deal lays the foundation for much stronger trading relationship that will bring the United States and Thailand closer than ever before," Trump said.

"Thailand will eliminate all tariffs on U.S. exports ... and remove substantial non-tariff barriers on American vehicles, medical devices, pharmaceuticals, ethanol and poultry. Thailand will also be buying an estimated $2.6 billion in additional U.S. agricultural products," he said.

"This is an incredible victory for both countries, both the people of Thailand and the American people. And I look forward to finalizing the details of our formal agreement in the coming weeks," Trump said, noting that today they were signing "a joint statement talking about the deal," which will eventually be finalized.

"Actually, it's a very big trade deal," Trump later said.

The title on the document they signed reads, "Joint Statement on an Agreement Between the United States of America and the Kingdom of Thailand on Reciprocal Trade." The statement says that the two men "have reached substantial conclusion in their negotiations" toward a trade agreement.

Trump also addressed world leaders during an Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) gathering in the Philippines on Tuesday.

"The United States is a Pacific nation in a true sense. And we have been engaged in trade and commerce and diplomacy across Southeast Asia for hundreds of years," Trump said. "I'm here today because America is deeply committed to the success, sovereignty and prosperity of our many friends and partners — many of whom are at this table right now — but partners in this region, and we always will be, we're with you always," he noted.
