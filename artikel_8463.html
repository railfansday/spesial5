Get the Wells Fargo Signify Business Cash® Card if your business buys mostly in the U.S. It pays 2% cash rewards on every business purchase, with no cap or annual fee. Abroad, its 3% foreign transaction fee costs more than the 2% you earn, so use a card without that fee for those purchases.

The card is a Mastercard for small businesses that would rather earn one flat rate than track bonus categories. A business with no tax ID of its own can apply with the owner’s Social Security number, and Wells Fargo also asks for details on each person who owns 25% or more of it.

A business that buys mostly in the U.S. earns 2% on all of it here, while the American Express Blue Business Cash drops to 1% after the first $50,000 of purchases each year. One that buys abroad saves the 3% fee with Bank of America’s Business Advantage Travel Rewards, and one whose spending centers on a single category can earn more with its Business Advantage Customized Cash Rewards.
