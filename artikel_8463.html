Get the Coinbase Card if you hold dollars or USDC, a coin pegged to the dollar, on Coinbase and want crypto rewards on a debit card with no credit check or annual fee. Pay from those balances, since a purchase paid with bitcoin or other crypto sells it and can leave you a taxable gain.

The Coinbase Card is a Visa prepaid debit card, issued by Pathward, N.A., that pays for each purchase from the dollars, USDC, or other crypto you choose to spend in your Coinbase account. It isn’t Coinbase’s credit card, the Coinbase One Card, or the BitPay Card, the prepaid crypto card BitPay ended in June 2023.

Coinbase users who pay with dollars or USDC get crypto rewards with no fee, while a purchase paid in bitcoin or other crypto is a sale, taxed on any gain since the coin was bought. Anyone who wants a published reward rate is better served by a crypto credit card paid in full each month.
