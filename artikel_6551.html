Joulutorttu (Finnish Star Cookies)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Tip: Docking, or pressing through the pastry a few times with a fork, allows steam to release from the tiny holes, which is helpful for keeping the Joulutorttu from opening up in the oven and securing the jam in place. But don’t worry! If the jam slides out, you can always add a little bit more in the center when they come out of the oven. The egg wash isn’t essential, but it gives the finished pastry a lovely golden shine.

Storage:These treats are best eaten warm from the oven. Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
