8 Essentials for Your Family's First Trip to Disney World

You’ve booked your tickets. You’ve planned your itinerary. You’ve figured out the My Disney Experience App (you think.) You’re as ready as you can be for your family’s first trip to Disney World. Then it dawns on you: What do you need to pack? Shopping for essentials for your first family trip to Disney World is an art – it’s about knowing your family and what you all need to thrive on one of the most exciting, most magical, and yes, sometimes stressful family vacations.

A little online searching will quickly convince you you'll need to buy extra luggage just to pack park essentials, but don’t stress. As an easily-overwhelmed mom who recently braved Disney World for the first time, I've got some hard-won advice to help guide your vacation prep and inform your packing list. Plus, I've scoured the internet for top picks of the most-used sanity-saving products to make the happiest place on earth live up to its name for you and your family.

Tired of the practically impossible task of packing up the family for a holiday? Here are the best packing cubes for busy families on the go.

For many parents, taking the kids to Disney World at least once during their childhood is a bucket list goal. When that dream is finally realized, there is a mix of idyllic anticipation and anxious uncertainty. It’s not surprising that many parents find planning for a trip to Disney World to be a stressful experience.

When it comes to Disney World, there are two types of parents: those who have fond memories of their own childhood Disney vacation, and those who hope to give their own kids the trip they didn’t get to experience growing up. Whichever type you are, the goal is the same: To give your kids a core-memory-making family vacation they’ll cherish forever. Of course, a love of Disney also evokes childhood nostalgia that is so fun to share with your own children. The legacy of classic and modern Disney characters, movies, and parks is a special thing to pass on to the next generation and something that carries a lot of weight for many.

Along with all the hopes and expectations wrapped up in a family trip to Disney World, the cost of the vacation only adds to the pressure to make it the best trip ever. For budget travelers, the average weeklong Disney World vacation for a family of four can cost $6,000 or more, according to NerdWallet, so ensuring you get your money’s worth is a huge factor. Not only that, but with so much to see, do, and experience in the parks, a vacation to Disney World is a complex trip to plan. Some families even turn to Disney vacation planners and travel agents to help with the sheer volume of logistical planning involved.

As tempting as it may be, you don’t need to add-to-cart every single item you see on ‘Must Have Disney Essentials’ lists. Before you start shopping, it pays to think through what will help your family enjoy your trip the most. The best travel products for families will vary, so evaluate these factors before you start shopping:

Of course, start with the makeup of your family. How old will your kids be on the trip, and what needs and preferences correspond with their ages? Strollers are a big decision for many, and there are lots of pros and cons to consider: Will it help or hurt to have a stroller for airports? Is it worth it to lug a stroller to the parks? Would renting Disney World strollers be a better option? The answer will be different for every family, so don’t listen to advice that insists on a right answer. Our youngest was six on our trip and I seriously considered taking or renting a stroller. She ended up walking the parks like a champ and I was glad we didn’t have to deal with a stroller! Had she been a year younger, though, I am sure a stroller would have been our saving grace.

What are your kids most looking forward to in the parks? How will they handle long lines? Will you need to anticipate an afternoon nap each day? Should you stock up on a few fun toys ahead of time so your kiddos aren’t pining for the theme park loot, or do you just need to upgrade your child’s luggage? A Disney World vacation will look very different depending on the ages of your kids, so narrow your list of essentials based on them.

A surefire way to preemptively ruin your first trip to Disney World is to forget your own needs in the process. For parents, Disney World is fun, sure, but there’s also a lot of planning, incessant crowds, unrelenting heat, not to mention the never-ending soundboard of the kids' needs and wants. Take a moment and think about what essentials you need to make the most of the trip, and perhaps even a few indulgent spa day gifts to help melt stress away at the end of a non-stop Disney World day.

A travel-size umbrella that offers heavy-duty weather protection.

Weather is a big factor in whether Disney World lives up to being “the happiest place on earth.” You likely know to expect warmer temperatures year-round, and extreme heat in the summer. But surprise rain showers are also a common occurrence in Orlando. Luckily, the Repel Compact Umbrella can help on both fronts. Not only will you be happy you have it if you get caught in a shower, but it will also filter UV rays when the sun is beating down. In fact, Repel even offers a UPF 50+ UV protection version of the umbrella to block out 99% of UV rays.

The umbrella's double-vented canopy is designed to withstand winds up to 85 MPH, so you won’t have to worry about it inverting. It is a heavy-duty umbrella packed into a compact travel size, opens and collapses with a push of a button, and is definitely worth tossing in your park bag. Reviewers say it performs as advertised – one even said the umbrella “loves wind” – with just a few saying the only issue they’ve had is closing it. (And yes, Disney World does allow umbrellas!)

This microfiber towel will save the day in all sorts of ways when you’re out and about, and packs into its own travel bag.

When you’re at the parks from sun up to sun down, unexpected needs are bound to arise, and the Rainleaf Microfiber Towel will be a lifesaver for many of them. Kid drop Dole Whip down their shirt? Want to dry off a wet seat? Need to add more sun shade to the stroller? Chances are you’ll be reaching for this towel all day long. Not only is it compact and made for travel, but it also absorbs five times its own weight and dries incredibly fast. If it’s super hot, drench the towel in cold water to help you cool off, then drape it over your backpack or stroller as you walk around to let it air dry.

Running from $9 to $20 a piece, depending on the size, it’s worth giving each family member their own, or grabbing a few to designate for certain purposes – one for clean up, one for shade and sweat, and one as a picnic blanket for parades or just a quick rest.

This compact travel charger keeps your devices topped up all day long.

Between taking a million pictures and using the Disney app to do everything from making meal orders and reservations to finding the next ride (or nearest restroom!), you’ll be using your phone a lot in the parks. The Anker Portable Charger ensures your devices stay charged up all day long. It has a universal USB-C cable and delivers 10,000mAh (milliamp hours), delivering the equivalent of two full smartphone charges. At 30W charging speed, you can expect it to fully charge a phone in about an hour – which is how long you’ll be lining for some rides anyway.

The charger is just a tad bigger than a credit card, and weighs about as much as a phone. It can charge three devices at once (you just need extra charging cables) and the cable connector stores in the battery pack for safekeeping between uses. Coming in under $30, it’s well worth the peace of mind so you don’t miss a single picture-perfect moment, even as the last firework illuminates the night sky.

One tip I got from a Disney World cast member is to take screenshots of the QR codes, order confirmations, lightning lane arrival windows, and anything else you may need for reference from the My Disney Experience App so you aren’t constantly having to access the app, which can drain your phone battery.

Take a rest whenever you need to with this super practical compact folding stool.

The Boreeman Folding Stool is as close as it gets to realizing the idea of pants that turn into a chair, but arguably way more practical. If your back just isn’t what it used to be, or you’ve got a kiddo who you know will complain about standing in line, this folding stool will single-handedly save your Disney World trip. The collapsible seat locks into place at various heights up to 17.72 inches. It packs down into a disc that is 9.84 inches by 2.36 inches with a carrying strap for added convenience. Use it in ride lines, waiting for parades or shows, or just whenever you need to take a load off. It also comes in handy to help little ones with bathroom needs.

The stool is highly rated with 4.4 stars from over 8,000 reviewers, but some say it’s not super comfortable, while others warn theirs broke while sitting on it. Still, other reviewers say they still use theirs often over a year later. It comes in tons of fun colors and can slide into bags, backpacks, or stroller compartments. It’s also light enough at 2.5 pounds for many kids to carry it through a ride line themselves.

Make any moment magical with these Disney-themed bubble wands.

The struggle to not buy every cute thing you see in Disney World is real, and the Little Kids Disney Light and Sound Musical Bubble Wand is here to help. Light-up bubble wands are all the rage in the parks, and they come with a hefty price tag. If you’re looking to save money where you can on your trip, surprise your little one with a pre-bought bubble wand for a fraction of the cost of the park varieties, which cost upwards of $35.

These wands are themed with all their favorite characters, including Mickey Mouse, Tiana, Elsa, Moana, and more. They light up, play music, and come with extra bubbles so your kiddo can join in the bubble wand fun all day long. Some reviewers were not satisfied with the durability and quality of the wand, while others say it was perfect for their Disney Parks trip.

Outsmart unpredictable weather with this family pack of reusable ponchos.

My family didn’t end up needing the Lingito Rain Ponchos Family Pack for rain, but we were so glad we used them on Tiana’s Bayou Adventure! We got in one of the first virtual queue time slots of the morning, and without the ponchos, we would have been utterly soaked for the rest of the day. This family pack gets the job done at keeping your family (and your backpacks) dry, whether the water is weather or ride-related. The adult ponchos have drawstrings, and while I understand they might pose a hazard for kids, I wish the kid ponchos could tighten for a better fit. My kids held theirs closed just under their chins when we dropped down the log flume.

They come compactly packed in small disposable bags, and you’ll be hard-pressed to fold them back in after you open them. The Amazon listing describes these ponchos as both reusable and disposable, which is accurate. While they can be dried out and used again on a trip, they might be too much trouble to save for your next adventure (however, one reviewer reports their family has used their set again and again). Still, at $10 for a pack of four (two adult and two kid-sized ponchos), they are well worth throwing in your bag every day in case you get caught in one of those notorious surprise Orlando rain showers – or just so you stay dry on wet rides.

This wearable fan rests around your neck for hands-free cooling wherever you go.

If you're worried about the relentless Orlando heat more than any other part of your Disney trip, do yourself a favor and pack the FrSara Portable Neck Fan. It’s encased blade vents blow air all the way around the back of the neck and out the ends to keep you noticeably cooler without having to worry about hazards like tangling hair. With a noise level of 25 dB – the equivalent of the ambient noise of a ‘silent’ PC – you can even keep it running indoors through those winding ride lines without being a nuisance to yourself or others …though it’s worth noting that some reviewers find it too loud.

The fan's working time ranges from four to 16 hours, and its USB-C charging port means you can recharge on the go with a portable device charger. With three speeds and 360-degree surround airflow, it might not quite make you forget you’re in Orlando, but it can certainly take the edge off the heat.

This lightweight travel stroller folds down easily and has four recline positions with tons of storage.

To pack a stroller or not to pack a stroller, that is the question for families making the pilgrimage to Disney World. If you decide to bring your own, the Summer Infant 3Dlite Stroller is our pick for Disney World for a few reasons. First, the stroller complies with Disney World’s list of prohibited items regarding stroller size, and reviewers do confirm that they used this stroller in Disney World. Second, the price is right at under $100, though it’s worth noting that single-stroller rentals at Disney World go for $15 per day, and $13 per day for multi-day rentals.

The stroller is lightweight, has four recline positions for napping on the go, a pop out weather canopy, and tons of extra storage, including a cup holder, close-top bag, and large storage compartment underneath the seat. Reiewers agree that this is a great, budget-friendly stroller for travel, and is highly rated at 4.6 stars with over 7,000 reviews. Plus, it has a weight capacity of 50 pounds, which is on the upper end for most travel strollers. So if you have an older kid who might not be able to last the entire day on foot, this stroller can be a lifesaver.

Take it from me, even anxious, stressed-out parents can make the most of their family’s first trip to Disney World. Resolve to manage your stress level by taking good care of yourself, setting yourself up for success by planning based on what will make it magical for your family, and packing essentials that will help everyone enjoy the trip.

The key is managing expectations, especially your own. Knowing that a vacation with kids is still parenting, just in a different location – and understanding that parenting in Disney World isn’t always a walk in the park – can help you make this trip every bit as magical for your kiddos as you hoped, though it likely won’t feel like it to you. So make sure you stop and take in their sweet faces when they meet their favorite characters, when they go on the ride they were so excited for, and as they watch as fireworks illuminate the night. You’ll experience the magic, guaranteed!
