The Best-Rated Game Of 2026 Has Just Changed, But Xbox Still Has Two Titles In The Top 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The news cycle has undoubtedly been dominated with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake today, and for good reason - it's become the best-rated game of 2026 according to Metacritic with a whopping 96 average rating.

That means the top 10 has some shuffling to do, but even with Zelda taking top spot (and even if GTA 6 surpasses it in November), there are still two Xbox first-party releases that are almost certainly locked into the top 10 for 2026.

The first of these is Forza Horizon 6, which now sits in seventh position across all platforms with its 90 average rating, serving as the best full game that Xbox has released this year according to Metacritic.

The other is DOOM: The Dark Ages - Revelations, which might be an expansion to an existing title, but is still counted by the platform (and to be fair, basically serves as an extra game anyway) and sits in sixth place with a 91 rating.

Go down a bit further, and Gears of War: E-Day is the next highest-rated Xbox first-party game with an average rating of 87, followed by Modern Warfare 4 at 85, Diablo IV: Lord of Hatred at 84 and Halo: Campaign Evolved at 81.

In a nutshell, it's been a pretty incredible year for video games! Don't forget that Sektori, The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Remastered and Mina The Hollower have all released on Xbox this year as well, while Xbox still has another massive first-party title dropping tomorrow in the form of World of Warcraft: Forever - albeit only on PC.
