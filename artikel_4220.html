Lego Star Wars 50th Anniversary Collection Sets Revealed For January 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Lego has been killing it with its number of crossover sets lately, releasing dozens of collaborations that don't seem to be slowing down as we get closer to the end of the year. While new crossovers are plenty exciting already, Lego has just revealed a major collection of new Star Wars-themed sets to celebrate the series' 50th anniversary.

With the announcement coming just after revealing that several new Star Wars projects were being announced in January, Lego is starting the party a little early by suddenly adding seven new anniversary sets to its official store, which was first spotted by The Brick Fan.

Listed as 'Coming Soon' on the official Lego store, none of the new releases can be purchased ahead of their January 1, 2027 release date, but all of their details with plenty of high-quality photos are already available for the following seven sets:

Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back™ Poster Tile

Rogue One: A Star Wars Story™ Poster Tile

If you haven't guessed it already, the main theme behind the anniversary set drop revolves around the original concept designs of the series, offering physical versions of classic designs for characters like Leia, Darth Vader, Han Solo, and Luke Skywalker that will likely fetch some absurd prices on the second-hand LEGO market in the coming years.

Aside from the incredibly unique designs, the set also includes several instances of dual-molded legs, which Lego Star Wars fans have been begging to see more often for ages, even with the cheapest set like the Mustafar Duel having it for all three characters included in the set.

While the new sets won't arrive in time for the holiday season, unlike the other November set releases, it's a great way to kick off the new year as a major celebration for the franchise for its half-century mark.

With several potential new game announcements, theme park content, and even more merchandise like Lego's Star Wars drop, it seems like it will be a big year for fans of the beloved sci-fi fantasy franchise if the first announcement for 2027 is anything to go by.
