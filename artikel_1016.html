Turkey head into the final two matchdays of Group A1 under new management, after Vincenzo Montella paid the price for a disastrous World Cup and an awful start to the Nations League campaign.

Former Crescent-Stars midfielder Sergen Yalcin takes over and would immediately endear himself to the fans if he can somehow avoid relegation to League B, with Turkey needing to beat Belgium by four goals, then overcome France on Sunday while hoping tonight's opponents lose to Italy.

Centre back Abdulkerim Badarkci is suspended and Juventus forward Kenan Yildiz remains sidelined through injury, so Arda Guler will once again be responsible for carrying most of Turkey's attacking threat as he aims to carry his good form with Real Madrid onto the international stage.

Belgium start the international window just one point behind second-place Italy, who they face on Sunday, so a victory in Izmir would keep their fate in their own hands when they take on the Azzurri in Brussels.

The Red Devils need to finish in the top two of Group A1 to qualify for the quarter-finals and a pair of wins this week would secure their place in the last eight.

Manager Mark van Bommel has been boosted by the return of Manchester City winger Jeremy Doku, who missed the previous Nations League window through injury. He is expected to start against Turkey, despite lively performances by wide men Mika Godts and Dodi Lukebakio in September and October.
