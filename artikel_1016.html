Harry Kane out for a ‘few months’ as Bayern Munich confirm ankle ligament tear

Harry Kane faces the "next few months" on the sidelines after suffering a torn left ankle ligament during Bayern Munich’s 1-1 draw against Cologne on Friday.

The injury occurred when Cologne defender Joel Schmied intercepted Michael Olise’s low cross at the back post, clattering into the Ballon d’Or winner’s left leg on the follow-through.

Though the England captain got back to his feet after treatment, he was unable to continue and hobbled off the pitch accompanied by two physios.

Following an assessment by the club's medical team, Bayern confirmed Kane had sustained a torn syndesmotic ligament and "will therefore be sidelined for the next few months".

It is a major setback for Kane, who won the Ballon d’Or last month and then became the most-capped England men’s player of all time last week, surpassing the previous record held by Peter Shilton.

Kane scored 73 goals last season to earn the Ballon d’Or and had made a flying start to the current campaign, registering 10 goals and 10 assists in 16 games for Bayern across all competitions.

He also scored eight goals in six Nations League games to help England top their group as he took his overall international tally to 93 goals in 127 caps.

England learned on Friday that they will face Norway in the Nations League quarter-finals, which will be a rematch of the two teams meeting at the same stage of the World Cup back in July.

Thomas Tuchel’s side won that game 2-1 thanks to a brace from Jude Bellingham, and the two-legged quarter-final will be played in late March.

Kane will hope that he is back in time for that tie, but Bayern’s statement indicates that he will not be seen on the pitch for the remainder of 2026 or the early weeks of 2027.

Bayern also suffered another injury blow on Friday night as Michael Olise failed to return for the second half. The club issued an update on Olise’s condition after the game.

The statement read: “Michael Olise has been diagnosed with a contusion of the foot. The French attacking player will receive treatment over the coming days.”
