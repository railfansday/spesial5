Aberdeen sack Robinson

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Aberdeen have parted ways with Robinson after their 4-0 loss to Hibernian; the Dons are second-bottom after just three wins from 12 Premiership games this season; the 51-year-old left St Mirren to take charge at Pittodrie in March; Stuart Duff placed in interim charge

Football Journalist @AlisonGConroy

Aberdeen have sacked Stephen Robinson with the club sitting 11th in the Scottish Premiership.

It follows their 4-0 defeat to Hibernian on Saturday, which came just a week after the Pittodrie club lost their League Cup semi-final to Rangers.

The Dons have lost seven of their 12 Premiership matches this season, including four of their last five.

Fans inside Pittodrie called for his sacking during their defeat to Hibs, with the 51-year-old admitting the performance was not good enough, and ultimately bringing to an end his eight-month stint at the club.

Robinson joined from Scottish Premiership rivals St Mirren in March, signing a deal until the summer of 2029.

He arrived having led the Buddies to three consecutive top-six finishes, plus won the League Cup trophy in December. While he did save the club from the relegation play-offs last season, this season has seen not gone to plan.

Aberdeen say their search for a replacement is underway, with their first fixture following the international break away to Kilmarnock - who are directly below them in the Premiership - on November 21.

A club statement said: "Following a board meeting, Aberdeen Football Club has today taken the decision to part company with head coach Stephen Robinson.

"Stephen leaves the club alongside assistant head coach Brian Kerr and first team coach Stephen Craigan. We thank them for their efforts during their time at Pittodrie and wish them every success for the future.

"Preparatory work to support the appointment of a new manager has been undertaken, and the recruitment process is ongoing. The focus will be on identifying the right candidate to lead the club forward and deliver the progress expected across all areas of the football operation.

"Stuart Duff will assume interim charge of first team training while the club continues its search for a new Head Coach."
