Leny Yoro: Manchester United make Pro Ref decision amid Mauricio Pochettino red card rage

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Manchester United are furious at referees’ chief Howard Webb’s support for the decision to send off Leny Yoro at Liverpool, despite the defender having his one-match suspension removed.

The French centre-back was dismissed after only eight minutes of United’s 4-0 defeat at Anfield on Sunday for fouling Reds striker Alexander Isak as he advanced towards goal, with referee Sam Barrott deciding Yoro had denied an obvious goalscoring opportunity.

United successfully appealed for wrongful dismissal and the suspension has now been removed, the Football Association has confirmed, meaning Yoro is free to play in the Premier League match against Brentford at Old Trafford on Saturday.

Liverpool scored their opening goal directly from the free-kick awarded for Yoro’s challenge.

One question raised was whether Isak had the ball sufficiently under control for this to count as denial of an obvious goalscoring opportunity. The Swede’s touch took the ball into the arms of United goalkeeper Senne Lammens.

Pro Ref’s chief refereeing officer Webb backed the decision to dismiss the 21-year-old, saying it was “certainly not clearly wrong”.

However, United boss Mauricio Pochettino vehemently disagreed and hit out at the lack of VAR intervention.

It is understood that the club are unhappy with Webb’s comments and plan to make their feelings known to Pro Ref.

Speaking at a press conference ahead of his team’s Champions League match against Sporting in Lisbon on Wednesday, the Argentinian said: “Very disappointed. My question always was the same, we can accept the referee can make a mistake because of the emotion of the game, sometimes the view or the position can affect that. But if we have VAR, what were they doing?

“Now, are we going to repeat the game? Are we going to have the possibility to play with similar tools – 11 v 11?

“We have an unbelievable car, but we need to have the level to drive, we need to drive in the best way. We love the technology, yes, and we accept the technology is there, but what are they doing? But it’s not only this mistake, it affects everything.

“No-one is talking about the [Dominik Szoboszlai’s] goal wasn’t legal, they should have disallowed the goal because [Bradley] Barcola was touching our wall. Maybe in the last two years the rules have changed while I was in America. Why didn’t they check it? It must be disallowed.”

Webb discussed the sending-off with former Liverpool and United striker Michael Owen in the latest edition of Match Officials: Mic’d Up, which aired on Tuesday evening but was recorded before the FA confirmed Yoro’s suspension had been removed.

“The reason we’re talking about it is the fourth consideration [in DOGSO decisions], that’s control or likelihood of keeping or gaining control of the ball,” Webb said.

“Isak’s got control of the ball, he’s got possession, he’s got the touch, he knocks it past Yoro. He’s moving at pace at that moment and a crowd comes in and knocks him down.

“There’s a question mark I guess around whether or not he would get onto that ball before Lammens. Now, in the moment, the referee looked at it and felt he would.

“The VAR checks to see whether that decision made on the field was clearly and obviously incorrect, and formed the opinion that it wasn’t.

“I think by and large, most people would see that as a correct decision, with Isak moving quickly on towards that ball. The alternative view is that Lammens might get there first, but I think it’s a little bit debatable. (It’s) certainly not clearly wrong.”

VAR John Brooks was of the same opinion as he said in the audio between him and referee Barrott: “Let’s roll it through firstly to see whether the attacker is going to get on the end of the ball.

“Just roll through real speed. So, at full speed, Isak maintains his momentum and likely gets on the end of it.

“OK, certainly not clearly wrong that he doesn’t. So I think the DOGSO confirmed.”
