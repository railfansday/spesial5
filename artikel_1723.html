Chelsea get new Morgan Rogers penalty review after Leeds handball controversy

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

VAR was correct not to overrule Michael Oliver after he awarded a controversial penalty against Morgan Rogers for handball against Leeds, per Dermot Gallagher.

The match was level at 1-1 when Rogers attempted to block a shot from Jayden Bogle. He turned his back in doing so and the ball appeared to catch him on his upper arm, with Oliver immediately pointing to the spot.

VAR reviewed the incident and allowed the penalty to stand, which Dominic Calvert-Lewin duly buried to put Leeds 2-1 up. The match ended 4-1 in the visitors’ favour.

Chelsea fans were left confused by the decision, with many suspecting the ball had caught Rogers above his sleeve and thus did not warrant handball. Rogers, meanwhile, appeared to protest that the ball had hit him in the face.

Xabi Alonso was similarly unimpressed with the decision, saying post-match: “I don’t agree. I think that it was harsh from Michael Oliver. For me it was not a penalty.”

But, speaking on Sky Sports’ Ref Watch, former Premier League referee Gallagher has opined that VAR was correct to allow Oliver’s decision to stand, pointing out that there was not sufficient evidence to overturn it.

The VAR reviewed a number of different angles of the incident, but none conclusively absolved Rogers.

“You can watch replays, you can watch a multitude of angles, some will say it hit (Rogers) on the elbow and some say the face,” said Gallagher. “There is no conclusive evidence to say the referee is wrong, so he has to give a penalty.

“We are not trying to prove the referee wrong. He gave a penalty. What I am saying is there is no evidence to say the referee is wrong, not that the VAR is gonna say ‘this is the right decision’.

“The referee has given the decision, and the evidence says the referee is not wrong.”

Jay Bothroyd concurred, admitting he felt that the ball might have hit Rogers too high, but that he would not have liked to see the penalty overturned.

“You have got to stick with the referee's decision,” he declared. “It looked like it hit his sleeve but they have a camera to show the elbow. It is difficult to see.

“When you look at that, (everyone has) an opinion), so you have to stick with the referee's decision.
