Villa end goal drought to finally give Emery’s new era lift-off

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Unai Emery is building Aston Villa 2.0 and this was a night when his new kids on the block produced clear signs of lift-off for the next chapter.

Emery always springs into life on European nights and his reconstructed team secured their first win and goals of the season to leave Villa’s 1,700 fans singing in the rain.

Ten summer signings have arrived during a summer of upheaval, which included plenty of high-profile departures after the club’s Europa League triumph in May.

Yet this was an evening that will have further energised Emery on his return to the Champions League.

Nicolas Jackson, Zion Suzuki and João Gomes embody this new Villa team and all stood out here in Bruges.

Record £65m signing Jackson’s fine individual goal proved to be the winner to settle a chaotic, absorbing contest with the Belgian Pro League champions.

Suzuki, the £30m replacement for Emiliano Martínez, has clear presence in goal and he is Villa’s version of former Manchester City goalkeeper Ederson with his wide array of passing.

Gomes was also industrious in midfield to recover from what was a nightmarish debut at Brighton, where he was sent off after 40 minutes.

Despite all the changes to this team, talismanic captain John McGinn remains essential to Villa. He became their all-time leading goalscorer in major European competitions with his 12th goal and led by example again, as he has done so many times.

There were also hints of a strong defensive partnership developing between Pau Torres and Victor Lindelof, following the sale of Ezri Konsa to Arsenal last month.

“Fantastic, fantastic,” purred Emery at one point in the second half from the technical area, and Villa’s season now appears up and running.

Emery said: “This was another step forward and I am so happy and thankful for this. The old players need to keep our standards high and show everyone this is the only way we can achieve the process and reach our targets.

“It’s very important to start the Champions League with three points and hopefully this will give us confidence for the league. We are improving and we must work hard – it is the only way.”

Although Emery has established a reputation as the Europa League king, winning that trophy five times, this competition has proved more difficult to master.

His best run was reaching the semi-finals in the 2021-22 season, when his Villarreal side lost to Liverpool.

It remains a tournament in which he is desperate to make an impression, with Villa returning to Champions League duty here for the first time since their thrilling encounter with Paris St-Germain in April 2025.

Only five of that starting XI from the second leg of the quarter-final remain and one of them, midfielder Amadou Onana, is out until next year after sustaining a serious knee injury at the World Cup with Belgium.

With European heavyweights Barcelona, PSG and Borussia Dortmund to come in the league phase, this always felt like a game Villa probably needed to win.

They had only managed one shot on target so far this season before arriving in this historic city, but scored three goals before half-time.

Their opener came against the run of play in the 11th minute, with McGinn casually finding the corner after a lung-bursting run from Torres. McGinn has overtaken former Villa striker Ollie Watkins at the top of the club’s continental goalscoring charts.

Brugge briefly wobbled but were level eight minutes later when Hugo Vetlesen swept home from 10 yards, capitalising on space in the penalty box.

Good evening and welcome to live coverage of Aston Villa’s Champions League opener against Club Brugge. Unai Emery’s side voyage across the Channel to face the Belgian champions having triumphed in last season’s Europa League and finished fourth in the Premier League. The Villa mothership has since sailed into choppy waters, though, with a raft of big-name sales preceding a run of two defeats and a draw in the league. An opening-weekend thrashing by Brighton and Hove Albion proved quite the storm for Emery’s new-look crew.

Morgan Rogers alighted for Chelsea in a deal worth £117m, a record fee for an English player, and goalkeeper Emiliano Martínez followed soon after, while Youri Tielemans moved to Manchester United. Ezri Konsa and Ollie Watkins completed the exodus of leading men with late-August moves to Arsenal and Al-Hilal respectively. To remedy the outgoings, Villa twice broke their transfer record this summer, pipping Newcastle United to the signing of Johan Manzambi (£59.5m) before landing Nicolas Jackson (£65m, including add-ons), and added Taylor Harwood-Bellis and Ibrahim Mbaye on deadline day. Whether they can hit the ground running remains to be seen, but Emery has a task on his hands aligning his new recruits before the scrutiny intensifies.

Although Villa swept aside Brugge 6-1 across two legs in the Champions League quarter-finals two seasons ago, here Blauw-Zwart (Blue-Black) represent a stern test having made the Jan Breydel Stadium a fortress in last year’s tournament. Their only home defeat in the league phase came against Arsenal, with a 3-3 draw against Barcelona backed up by 4-1 and 3-0 victories over Monaco and Marseille respectively. Belgium’s second-most decorated club have won seven of the last 11 league titles and know how to win matches. In Yann Sommer, Ivan Leko has one of world football’s most experienced goalkeepers, who kept the most clean sheets in the 2024-25 edition of the Champions League (seven). Captain Hans Vanakan, Brandon Mechele and Joaquin Seys were part of Rudi Garcia’s Belgium World Cup squad, and German striker Nicolò Tresoldi already has four goals in six matches this term. English midfielder Freddie Potts could also feature, having settled in nicely in West Flanders having moved from West Ham United during the summer.

The earlier 5.45pm kick-off time, means Villa, Brugge, LASK and AEK Athens have the opportunity to top the 36-team league …at least for a couple of hours.
