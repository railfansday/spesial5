Arsenal star sends Man City warning before blockbuster showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Viktor Gyokeres has warned of the obvious threat that Manchester City still pose to Arsenal ahead of their huge showdown this weekend.

The Premier League title rivals lock horns in north London on Sunday afternoon, with six points and two places separating them in the table as things stand.

Defending champions Arsenal have won nine of their first 11 top-flight games this season to already sit six points clear at the summit following a dramatic 2-2 draw at Newcastle, while they remain unbeaten in the Champions League.

That is in stark contrast to City, who sit third behind surprise package Brighton after Saturday’s disappointing home draw with Fulham, which came after back-to-back surprise losses at the hands of Brighton and Nottingham Forest.

Those struggles continued in the Champions League on Tuesday night, when Enzo Maresca’s side twice had to fight back from a deficit to claim a 2-2 home draw with Napoli - after which star striker Erling Haaland expressed concern over their lack of wins.

Despite that disappointing form, Gyokeres believes that City will still present a significant challenge to Arsenal in their blockbuster clash at the Emirates Stadium this weekend.

“They still have a very good squad and very good players,” the Swedish forward said after scoring the opening goal in Arsenal’s 1-1 draw with Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League on Tuesday.

“Sometimes, you may have a period where you don’t get the results all the time – which is normal for any club.

“It is going to be a difficult game on Sunday. But it is always fun with big games. It is going to be good.”

Gyokeres insists that Arsenal will not spend any time thinking about the potentially significant ramifications of City’s Premier League charges on this season’s title race.

In September, an independent commission found City guilty of all charges related to alleged serious breaches of the Premier League’s financial rules across a nine-season period between 2009/10 and 2017/18 in a seismic verdict.

No sanctions against City have yet been announced, with the club appealing the decision. They deny any wrongdoing.

The latest reports suggest that City’s appeal could be heard in December, with a potential ruling expected early in 2027.

“It is what it is,” Gyokeres said of the situation, per the Press Association. “We are focused on the game on Sunday.

“We can’t control that. It has nothing to do with us. So, we don’t put any energy into that.”
