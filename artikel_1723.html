From Braga to Bristol: The 13 Milan players called up for international duty

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The second international break of the season is upon us, and Milanello will once again be left pretty much empty.

Milan won against Genoa on Sunday at the Stadio Luigi Ferraris to ensure they headed into the fortnight off in the right way, having also snuck back into the top four. After the break comes a big game at home against high-flying Frosinone, but not before flights jet off around the globe.

The official website confirm that a total of 13 Rossoneri players have been called up to represent their countries in November. They will be in UEFA Nations League, CONCACAF Nations League and friendly action, with the full schedule below.

➤ Türkiye vs. Belgium: Thursday, November 12th, 6:00 PM CET, Izmir – UEFA Nations League ➤ Belgium vs. Italy: Sunday, November 15th, 8:45 PM CET, Brussels – UEFA Nations League

➤ Italy vs. France: Thursday, November 12th, 8:45 PM CET, Milan – UEFA Nations League ➤ Belgium vs. Italy: Sunday, November 15th, 8:45 PM CET, Brussels – UEFA Nations League

➤ Italy vs. France: Thursday, November 12th, 8:45 PM CET, Milan – UEFA Nations League ➤ France vs. Türkiye: Sunday, November 15th, 8:45 PM CET, Bordeaux – UEFA Nations League

➤ England vs. Croatia: Thursday, November 12th, 8:45 PM CET, London – UEFA Nations League ➤ Croatia vs. Czechia: Sunday, November 15th, 8:45 PM CET, Osijek – UEFA Nations League

➤ Portugal vs. Denmark: Saturday, November 14th, 7:45 PM CET, Braga – UEFA Nations League ➤ Wales vs. Portugal: Tuesday, November 17th, 7:45 PM CET, Cardiff – UEFA Nations League

➤ Slovenia vs. Switzerland: Friday, November 13th, 8:45 PM CET, Ljubljana – UEFA Nations League ➤ Switzerland vs. Scotland: Monday, November 16th, 8:45 PM CET, St. Gallen – UEFA Nations League

➤ Haiti vs. United States: Saturday, November 14th, 9:30 PM CET, Miami – CONCACAF Nations League ➤ United States vs. Haiti: Tuesday, November 17th, 11:00 PM CET, Cincinnati – CONCACAF Nations League

➤ Costa Rica vs. Ecuador: Friday, November 13th, 1:00 AM CET (+1), Quito – Friendly ➤ Bolivia vs. Ecuador: Tuesday, November 17th, 00:30 AM CET (+1), Guayaquil – Friendly

➤ France vs. Italy: Friday, November 13th, 6:30 PM CET, Laval – Friendly ➤ Italy vs. Belgium: Monday, November 16th, 6:15 PM CET, Livorno – Friendly

➤ France vs. Italy: Friday, November 13th, 6:30 PM CET, Laval – Friendly ➤ England vs. France: Monday, November 16th, 6:30 PM CET, Bristol – Friendly

➤ Italy vs. Portugal: Thursday, November 12th, 5:00 PM CET, Padua – Friendly ➤ Italy vs. Switzerland: Monday, November 16th, 3:00 PM CET, Abano Terme – Friendly
