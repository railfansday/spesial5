Expert: Tottenham blasted for not matching Man City with Will Lankshear deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Tottenham Hotspur have to start inserting buy-back clauses into deals when they sell young starlets in the future.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who cannot understand why Spurs didn't add a return clause as part of their deal to sell Will Lankshear.

The young striker joined Middlesbrough in a £20million package deal in the summer, and has already scored 13 goals from 13 games in the Championship this season.

Wyness believes that Manchester City have set the gold standard when it comes to buy-back clauses, and he cannot fathom why Spurs didn't follow their example.

The Citizens have a clause in James McAtee's contract at Nottingham Forest, which could allow them to buy him back later down the line.

Tottenham failed to get that agreement in place with Lankshear, and it is already proving to be a mistake.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that there is no excuse for Premier League clubs not including buy-back clauses when they sell young players moving forward.

The Scottish businessman pointed out that Lankshear has quickly become "flavour of the month" with his growing reputation, and Spurs now have no control over his future.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how he simply couldn't understand the decision to sell.

"Yeah, I think it is (a mistake). Look, he is the flavour of the month. He's been getting a lot of attention, a lot of headlines and certainly a lot of interest," he said.

"We've talked a lot about City recently, you know, and young talent or players they've sold on. They've always had a buy-back clause.

"For Spurs to not have had a buyback clause with Lankshear, I just don't understand that at all.

"It's something that everybody should be doing at this stage right now."

Wyness previously told Football Insider earlier this week that Lankshear has the potential to develop into a £60m player in the coming years.

Middlesbrough are currently second in the Championship, and there is an expectation that his value will skyrocket is Kim Hellberg's side secure their return to the top flight.

That means Tottenham could effectively end up missing out on £40m from Lankshear long-term, having made the snap decision to sell him in the summer.

The Lilywhites have several promising academy talents coming through, but have not done enough to breed young stars in recent years.
