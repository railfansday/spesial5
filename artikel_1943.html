Scott Parker Reunion Could Be Key to West Ham Defensive Deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

No sooner had Scott Parker been appointed as West Ham’s ‘promotion specialist’ manager than stories surfaced linking him with the recruitment of former players.

Reports linking West Ham with C J Egan-Riley are unlikely to disappear anytime soon, with the former Burnley defender continuing to attract attention following his rapid development this season.

Egan-Riley enjoyed a breakthrough campaign previously under Scott Parker, establishing himself as one of the Championship’s standout young defenders and helping Burnley secure promotion back to the Premier League.

A move to the London Stadium would carry several attractions. Not only does Parker know the player well from their time together at Burnley, but Egan-Riley would also qualify as a home-grown player, an increasingly valuable asset for West Ham who are looking to balance squad registration requirements.

Parker has consistently praised the defender in the past, and stated he was a key player for him in the Championship. He was instrumental in the defender’s development, giving him a prominent role in Burnley’s promotion-winning side. His composure on the ball, ability to play across the back line and potential for further improvement make him the type of profile West Ham are expected to target.

Defensive stalwart for Scott Parker’s West Ham?

The central defender chose Marseille ahead of West Ham in 2025, who secured his signature on a free transfer after the expiry of his Burnley contract, meaning any transfer fee received would represent a substantial profit on their investment.

Having been a bit part player at the French club in his first season, following a squad overhaul in the summer he is now a regular starter and the data shows he has maintained a 93% pass completion in Ligue 1. The 23 year old ranks as one of the leagues most accurate passers in his position.

There is also reason to believe Marseille would be willing sellers. The French giants have made no secret of the fact that they will need to generate funds through player departures during the January transfer window due to ongoing financial issues. West Ham have enjoyed a productive relationship with Marseille over the years, with deals for Dimitri Payet, Emerson and Nayef Aguerd demonstrating that negotiations between the two clubs are far from uncommon.

The structure of any potential deal, be it an initial loan or a straight transfer, is yet to emerge. Either way, if the Hammers can persuade the defender to play a part in their quest for promotion they would be adding a young, home-grown centre-back who appears far from reaching his full potential.
