Leeds’ meeting with Spurs will be their final test before the international break, which could prove timely for the West Yorkshire outfit.

Leeds boss Farke said: “I think this next international break comes at a good time for us because we have the chance right now to get rid of all the problems for Anton [Stach], Noah Okafor and [potentially] Dominic Calvert-Lewin and this will be beneficial.

“But I'm not just looking ahead what will be in three weeks or whatever. It's not the easiest situation at the moment especially offensive-wise, but we want to stay together and fight together and also make sure that in perhaps not the best situation, we still are able to dig in and grind out a result in this important game.”

Here are the key TV and streaming details as Leeds prepare to host Spurs.

Kick-off at Elland Road is scheduled for 12:30pm on Saturday, November 7.

Yes - those without a ticket will be able to watch the action unfold from the comfort of their own homes. TNT Sports 1 and TNT Sports Ultimate will be showing the fixture.

Supporters can stream the action via HBO Max, the streaming home of TNT Sports. It can be streamed via smartphones and laptops and directly to a smart TV. Sport packages can be purchased online. There will also be live audio commentary on BBC Radio Leeds.
